Report overview

Margin Gains Meet an AI Data Center Wildcard

Shinsegae I&C is lifting its operating margin by trimming low-margin businesses, while the role it may play in Shinsegae Group's large-scale AI data center project has emerged as a variable that could reshape its business structure.

02

Business structure

Shinsegae I&C is Shinsegae Group's IT services affiliate, spun off from the group's computing division in 1997, operating across three segments: IT Service, IT Distribution, and IT Information Service.

The IT Service segment covers system integration, system maintenance, and cloud/IDC operations via the Gimpo data center, and generated 30.9 billion won in operating profit in H1 2026, the largest single contributor to the company's earnings.

The IT Distribution segment handles game software and networking equipment distribution including Nintendo and PlayStation titles, with game software sales rising in Q2 2026 on stronger Nintendo Switch 2 sales.

The IT Information Service segment, which includes education and messaging services, has relatively lower profitability and has recently been scaled back.

In smart retail, the company develops AI vision-based self-checkout and unmanned store solutions, expanding supply beyond Shinsegae's own retail network to outside partners.

Its main customers are centered on Shinsegae Group affiliates including E-Mart, and a substantial portion of revenue is generated through intra-group transactions.

Competitively, it is compared to conglomerate-affiliated IT services firms such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS, and Lotte Data Communication, with a considerable revenue-scale gap relative to larger peers.

The company has set AI, cloud, smart retail, and smart infrastructure as its four growth pillars, expanding into AI-based services such as chatbots, demand forecasting, personalized recommendations, image recognition, and robotic process automation (RPA).

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated revenue in 2025 reached 687.2 billion won, up 9.8% from 625.7 billion won in 2024, while operating profit jumped 32.9% to 49.1 billion won, lifting the operating margin from 5.9% to 7.1%.

Net profit attributable to owners, however, fell 16.3% to 28.3 billion won from 33.8 billion won the prior year, so operating profit growth and net profit decline diverged that year.

In 2022, operating profit was only 37.5 billion won yet net profit reached 84.1 billion won, a gap likely driven by one-off items such as intra-group equity transactions.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 net profit was a solid 12.6 billion won, but Q4 2025 saw net profit swing to a loss of 5.4 billion won despite an operating profit of 12.4 billion won, suggesting a non-operating burden that quarter.

In Q1 2026, revenue was 178.6 billion won and operating profit 12.4 billion won, up 3.78% and 16.7% year over year respectively, while net profit rose 9.29% to 10.3 billion won.

Q2 2026 revenue declined 3.5% year over year to 175.0 billion won, yet operating profit rose 13% to 15.2 billion won, again reflecting the effect of trimming low-margin businesses.

For H1 2026 overall, revenue was 353.6 billion won (roughly flat year over year), operating profit was 27.7 billion won (+14.8%), and net profit was 23.1 billion won (+9.3%), with the H1 operating margin rising about one percentage point year over year to 7.8%.

The debt ratio rose somewhat from 23.1% in 2024 to 33.0% in 2025, though equity grew from 408.6 billion won to 433.2 billion won, keeping the financial base intact.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's IT services industry is growing on the back of expanding AI infrastructure investment and digital transformation demand, with domestic IT spending projected to rise 3.3% in 2026 to 42.1 trillion won from 40.85 trillion won the prior year.

Expanding AI infrastructure demand and data center system expansion are cited as the main growth drivers.

Shinsegae I&C competes in this market with conglomerate-affiliated IT services firms such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS, and Lotte Data Communication, but its revenue scale is more than tenfold smaller than Samsung SDS, leaving a substantial gap.

Korea's data center market faces a supply shortfall relative to demand from advanced IT users, with domestic data center supply last year reaching only about half the OECD average.

Against this supply-constrained backdrop, Shinsegae Group's announced 250-megawatt hyperscale AI data center project would be more than double the roughly 100MW facility SK Telecom is building with Amazon Web Services in Ulsan, and could meaningfully affect Korea's data center supply landscape if realized.

Even so, some market observers note that Shinsegae I&C is not widely recognized as a competitor within the industry, leaving its lack of hands-on hyperscale data center design and operation experience as a point of market attention.

06

Outlook

The company has set AI, cloud, smart retail, and smart infrastructure as its four growth pillars and is expanding R&D investment, with H1 2026 R&D spending of 1.62 billion won already exceeding the full-year 2025 figure of 1.57 billion won.

R&D spending as a share of revenue rose from 0.2% to 0.5% year over year, and on an annualized basis is expected to more than double from the prior year.

The AX Center, which oversees R&D, is researching unmanned store payments, LiDAR-based object tracking, autonomous parking control, and image-based inventory monitoring.

On the external partnership front, the company signed an MOU with KT for joint sales of Nutanix-based private/hybrid cloud offerings, seeking to expand in the enterprise cloud market.

The most closely watched variable is Shinsegae Group's announced 250MW AI data center project, formed through a strategic partnership with U.S. startup Reflection AI, with the group reportedly moving into preparation for establishing a joint venture within the year.

Speculation suggests Shinsegae I&C would handle the infrastructure design and operation of the facility, though its role and investment scale have not yet been officially confirmed.

In its semiannual report, the company stated it is placing more weight on medium- to long-term structural transformation than on short-term top-line growth, and is watching whether expanding AI infrastructure demand in the second half translates into results.

Whether the expanded AI and cloud investment can extend beyond internal group demand to external revenue and profit is flagged as the key variable for future earnings.

08

Bull factors

Expected Involvement in the AI Data Center Project

Shinsegae Group has forged a strategic partnership with U.S. startup Reflection AI and announced plans for a 250MW AI data center, the largest of its kind planned in Korea.

Speculation has emerged that Shinsegae I&C would handle the infrastructure design and operation of the facility, and the stock reacted with a sharp short-term rally immediately after the announcement.

With domestic data center supply running at about half the OECD average, realizing a hyperscale facility could open opportunities to expand cloud business using spare power capacity.

Margin Improvement via Low-Margin Business Pruning

The operating margin improved to 7.1% in 2025 from 5.9% in 2024, and rose further to 7.8% in H1 2026, up about one percentage point year over year.

The strategy of scaling back relatively low-margin businesses such as education, messaging services, and EV charging while concentrating on higher-contribution areas like IT distribution and cloud is being reflected in results.

In Q2 2026, operating profit rose 13% even as revenue declined, a pattern interpreted as the result of this structural shift.

Push to Expand External Cloud and Retail Tech Business

Shinsegae I&C signed an MOU with KT for joint sales of Nutanix-based private and hybrid cloud offerings. Its AI-vision-based self-checkout and other smart retail solutions are also being pushed for overseas expansion.

R&D investment already exceeded the prior full year's level in H1 2026 alone, accelerating AI and big data technology development.

09

Bear factors

Stagnant Revenue and Reliance on Affiliate Transactions

H1 2026 revenue was roughly flat year over year, and Q2 revenue actually declined 3.5%. Because a substantial portion of revenue derives from intra-group transactions with Shinsegae Group affiliates, clear evidence of external revenue expansion has yet to materialize.

Whether the expanded AI and cloud investment can extend beyond internal group demand into external revenue and profit remains to be seen.

Lack of Hyperscale Data Center Operating Experience

While Shinsegae I&C operates the Gimpo data center, it has no track record designing or operating a hyperscale facility on the order of 250MW. Some market participants note that the company is not widely recognized as a competitor within the industry.

There are also views that it remains to be seen whether the group has the necessary capacity to execute a project of this scale.

Net Profit Volatility and Divergence from Operating Results

In 2025, operating profit rose 32.9% even as net profit attributable to owners fell 16.3%, and Q4 2025 saw net profit turn negative despite a positive operating profit.

The large 84.1 billion won net profit posted in 2022 is believed to have stemmed from one-off factors, and net profit has not returned to that level since. The frequent divergence between operating results and net profit trends is something to keep in mind when interpreting the company's earnings.

10

Risk factors

Execution Risk

Shinsegae Group's AI data center project remains in the preparation stage for establishing a joint venture, and Shinsegae I&C's specific role, investment size, and ownership structure have not been finalized.

Given the process of translating the Reflection AI partnership into actual construction and operation, delays or changes to the plan cannot be ruled out.

Revenue Structure Risk

Continued pruning of low-margin businesses (education, messaging, EV charging, etc.) could keep revenue flat or cause it to shrink further. Given the high dependence on affiliate-related revenue, a slowdown in group-wide investment or consumption could have a direct impact on earnings.

Competitive and Market Risk

There is a large revenue-scale gap versus major IT services firms such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS, and the hyperscale data center market is also drawing competition from telecom operators and large cloud providers.

If market perceptions of Shinsegae I&C's limited industry presence persist, expanding external contract wins could take longer.

12

Overall view

Shinsegae I&C is continuing a profitability-focused transformation by pruning low-margin businesses, lifting its operating margin, as confirmed in its 2025 and H1 2026 results.

At the same time, growing market attention on the role Shinsegae I&C may play in Shinsegae Group's announced large-scale AI data center project has added a new dimension to the company's business narrative.

That project, however, remains at the joint-venture preparation stage, with the company's role and investment size not yet officially confirmed, and execution risk tied to a lack of hyperscale data center operating experience is also cited.

The structure in which a substantial share of revenue comes from Shinsegae Group affiliates provides a stable base but also makes it harder to gauge the pace of external revenue expansion.

The history of operating profit and net profit frequently diverging is something to factor in when interpreting future quarterly results. The Q3 earnings release and any follow-up news on the AI data center joint venture stand out as the key events to watch for further clarity.
