Report overview

Group-Anchored IT Services Firm Shows Profit Recovery

Shinsegae Information & Communication's operating profit has risen for three consecutive quarters since the first quarter of 2026, while revenue growth has been modest and more than half of sales still come from group-internal demand.

  1. 1

    Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 49.1 billion, up 32.9% from the prior year, and the operating margin of 7.1% was the highest of the 2022–2025 period.

  2. 2

    Operating profit has increased for three consecutive quarters starting from the first quarter of 2026, reaching KRW 16.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

  3. 3

    Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a net loss of KRW 5.41 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to a profit in the first quarter of 2026, rising to KRW 14.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Shinsegae Information & Communication runs three segments: IT services, IT distribution, and IT information services.

Standalone IT services revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 111.6 billion, up 8.9% year on year, as large system integration projects were won and private infrastructure sales to outside customers expanded.

IT distribution revenue was KRW 47.7 billion in the same quarter, up 10.3%, with game sales lifted by stronger Pokémon card sales. IT information services revenue fell as lower-margin messaging business was scaled back, while its operating profit rose.

Group affiliates are the core customer base: in the first half of 2026, affiliates of the largest group retail company accounted for 48.7% of revenue and affiliates of Shinsegae for 18.2%. The company has set AI, cloud, smart retail and smart infrastructure as its four growth pillars.

In retail technology, it applied an AI-vision self-checkout payment management solution at a convenience store chain. In 2026 it widened its distribution lineup by adding Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta AI glasses in Korea. Its competitors include large SI providers such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever. Its differentiation rests on retail-tech capability built on group IT demand.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 596.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 618.9 billion in 2023 (up 3.7%), KRW 625.7 billion in 2024 (up 1.1%) and KRW 687.2 billion in 2025 (up 9.8%), with growth in each year from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 37.5 billion in 2022, KRW 40 billion in 2023 (up 6.5%), KRW 37 billion in 2024 (down 7.5%) and KRW 49.1 billion in 2025 (up 32.9%), making 2025 the largest year of the 2022–2025 period. Operating margin was 6.3%, 6.5%, 5.9% and 7.1% for 2022 through 2025, the highest in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 84.1 billion in 2022, KRW 30.4 billion in 2023 (down 63.8%), KRW 33.8 billion in 2024 (up 11.2%) and KRW 28.3 billion in 2025 (down 16.3%); net profit including non-controlling interests for 2025 was KRW 28.3 billion.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 433.2 billion, total liabilities KRW 143.2 billion and the debt ratio 33.0%. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 18.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 99.2 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, the third quarter of 2025 posted revenue of KRW 163.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 12.6 billion and controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 12.6 billion.

The fourth quarter of 2025 had revenue of KRW 170.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 12.4 billion, but a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 5.41 billion. The first quarter of 2026 had revenue of KRW 178.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 12.4 billion and controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 10.3 billion.

The second quarter of 2026 had revenue of KRW 175 billion, operating profit of KRW 15.2 billion (up 22.4% from the prior quarter) and controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 12.8 billion.

The third quarter of 2026 had revenue of KRW 176.9 billion, operating profit of KRW 16.2 billion and controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 14.1 billion, up 8.2%, 29.0% and 11.8% year on year. The company credited higher revenue and business restructuring for the profit gains.

On a standalone basis, lower-margin business cuts and lower depreciation after the sale of EV charging assets contributed to operating profit growth. In the second quarter, the end of a large SI project reduced revenue, while exiting low-margin businesses and higher game sales lifted operating profit.

Operating profit has recovered more clearly than net profit, which turned profitable only in the first quarter of 2026 after the fourth-quarter 2025 loss; net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 31.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Global and domestic IT spending is presented as being in a growth phase. According to a Gartner outlook cited in press coverage, global IT spending in 2026 is expected to surpass USD 6 trillion for the first time, growing 9.8% from the prior year.

Domestic IT spending is expected to reach KRW 42.1 trillion in 2026, up 3.3% from KRW 40.85 trillion. AI infrastructure demand, data center expansion and digital transformation are cited as main drivers.

Market growth and the company's revenue growth are not the same, and its revenue is strongly affected by group-internal volume. On the cycle, the end of a large SI project reduced SM and SI revenue, and new domestic and external project wins are needed to fill the gap.

IT distribution profit depends on the game console sales cycle. R&D spending was KRW 1.62 billion in the first half of 2026, above the KRW 1.57 billion spent for all of 2025, and rose to 0.5% of revenue from 0.2%. A press estimate puts full-year R&D at around KRW 3.2 billion.

Compared with large SI providers, the company is at an early stage of expanding its external customer revenue. In retail technology, AI checkout and unmanned store demand define the competitive arena.

06

Outlook

The company has said it places more weight on a medium-term business transformation than on short-term top-line growth. It plans to strengthen the core IT services franchise, broaden internal and external work, and run the business with a profitability focus.

In cloud, it signed a business agreement with KT on July 31, 2026 for enterprise private and hybrid cloud services. The subscription private cloud service Sparos One is the centerpiece, with design and technical consulting, while KT is to identify demand through its enterprise customer access.

The two sides plan to extend cooperation to cloud services that support AI operations. In AI glasses distribution, a September 2026 press report said nine Meta AI glasses models would be introduced in Korea. On shareholder returns, the company plans to retire all 835,090 treasury shares it holds by 2027.

A press estimate says R&D spending will rise sharply again in 2026. The company is watching whether stronger second-half AI infrastructure demand will show up in results. Expanded AI use across the group is also mentioned as room for a wider business scope. The next checkpoint is how much cloud and AI glasses distribution contribute to segment revenue.

08

Bull factors

Three Straight Quarters of Operating Profit Growth

Operating profit for the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 16.2 billion, up 29.0% year on year, and it has grown for three consecutive quarters since the first quarter of 2026. The company cites business restructuring, lower-margin business cuts and lower depreciation after the EV charging asset sale. The annual operating margin of 7.1% in 2025 was the highest of 2022–2025.

Cash Generation and Financial Capacity

First-half 2026 operating cash flow was KRW 54.2 billion, up 61.9% year on year. Cash and equivalents increased to KRW 134.1 billion at the end of June 2026 from KRW 98.2 billion at the end of 2025. Total liabilities were KRW 143.2 billion at the end of 2025, with a debt ratio of 33.0%.

Stronger Shareholder Returns

The company plans to retire all 835,090 treasury shares it holds by 2027. It raised the floor of its minimum dividend per share. Its articles were amended so that the dividend record date is set after the dividend amount is confirmed.

The board approved the revised shareholder return policy, which pairs growth investment with returns to shareholders.

09

Bear factors

Revenue Growth Remains Modest

Revenue grew only 1.1% in 2024. Second-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 175 billion, down 2.0% from the prior quarter and down 3.5% year on year. The end of a large SI project reduced SM and SI revenue. Third-quarter revenue rose 8.2% year on year, but quarterly sales have not yet settled into a steady pattern.

Operating Profit and Net Profit Diverge

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 63.8% in 2023, rose 11.2% in 2024 and fell 16.3% in 2025. In the fourth quarter of 2025, operating profit was KRW 12.4 billion, yet the company posted a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 5.41 billion. Operating profit and net profit did not move in the same direction in several periods.

Reliance on Game Sales in Distribution Profit

Improved IT distribution profit owed much to higher game software revenue from Nintendo Switch 2 sales. IT information services revenue fell 20.5% year on year as lower-margin messaging and education business was reduced. Game and device sales tend to swing with product launch cycles.

10

Risk factors

Dependence on Group Demand

Group-internal transactions were about 66.9% of revenue in the first half of 2026. Affiliates of the largest group retail company accounted for 48.7% and Shinsegae affiliates for 18.2%. A change in group IT investment plans would feed directly into revenue. Expanding external customers is still underway, so the concentration remains a risk.

Shrinking Lines During Restructuring

Revenue fell in some lines during the sale of EV charging assets and the reduction of lower-margin business. Third-quarter 2026 IT information services revenue fell 5.9% year on year. The end of a large SI project also reduced SM and SI revenue.

The restructuring effect appears in operating profit, but how fast the revenue base can widen still needs confirmation.

Product Cycles and Consumer Demand

IT distribution profit depends on product cycles, including game consoles and trading cards. In the second quarter of 2026, higher Nintendo Switch 2 sales lifted distribution profit. New device distribution such as AI glasses is at an early stage, where demand and inventory management could drive earnings swings. Profit volatility in this segment could widen if consumer conditions shift.

12

Overall view

Shinsegae Information & Communication's reported results show 2025 revenue of KRW 687.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 49.1 billion.

Operating profit has risen for three consecutive quarters since the first quarter of 2026, and controlling-shareholder net profit recovered from a fourth-quarter 2025 loss to KRW 14.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Revenue has grown for three years since 2023, but controlling-shareholder net profit fell to KRW 28.3 billion in 2025. The high share of group-internal transactions remains a structural feature.

The results of external growth businesses such as cloud, AI glasses distribution and retail technology are not yet clearly visible in reported figures. Shareholder returns were strengthened through treasury share retirement and a higher minimum dividend.

The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual business report, with the operating margin and fourth-quarter controlling-shareholder net profit as the key points.
