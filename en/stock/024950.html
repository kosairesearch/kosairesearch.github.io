Report overview

Electric Bikes Drive Profit Recovery, Q3 2026 Slows

Samchuly Bicycle returned to operating profit from 2024 on higher electric bicycle sales, but third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 77.5% year on year, making the durability of earnings the next question to watch.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Samchuly Bicycle focuses on bicycle manufacturing and distribution, and its consolidated revenue also includes a travel business. A report by Etoday said the travel business accounted for KRW 64.7 billion, or 47.93% of total revenue, as of the third quarter of 2024.

Within the bicycle business, the company says electric bicycles are its largest revenue source. According to the company, electric bicycle segment revenue in 2025 was about 15% higher than in 2020, with average annual growth of roughly 2.5% over the last five years.

The company has not disclosed the exact share of electric bicycles in total revenue. Electric bicycles are a higher-priced product group, so they contribute significantly to both sales and profitability. The company said electric bicycle segment revenue rose 45% year on year in the first quarter of 2026.

General bicycles are recovering on demand for low-cost urban mobility amid high prices and on a lineup with stronger price competitiveness, while competing with low-priced Chinese products. Children's bicycles grew on licensed products such as Catch Teenieping.

On the sales channel side, shared mobility operators have become large buyers, and the company supplied electric bicycles to a shared platform through 2024 and to a public electric bicycle program in Boryeong City in 2025.

Ranking first in the electric bicycle category of the 2025 Korea Quality Satisfaction Index (KS-QEI) and a nationwide integrated after-sales service network are cited as the basis of its quality-focused positioning.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 116.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 106.8 billion in 2023, a decrease of 8.1%, then rose to KRW 161.2 billion in 2024, up 50.9%, and to KRW 175.6 billion in 2025, up 8.9%.

Revenue grew in 2024 and 2025, two consecutive years, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit moved from KRW 1.14 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 6.29 billion in 2023, which turned into an operating profit of KRW 3.01 billion in 2024 and KRW 12.4 billion in 2025, up 312.3%. The operating margin improved from -5.9% in 2023 to 7.1% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a net loss of KRW 99.1 million in 2024 to a net income of KRW 5.87 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 11.6 billion in 2025, and the gap from controlling-shareholder net income reflects non-controlling interests.

The company attributed the 2025 improvement to higher electric bicycle sales and stable demand for general bicycles, and also cited cost structure improvements and efficiency gains.

Operating cash flow improved from negative KRW 5.91 billion in 2022 and negative KRW 2.56 billion in 2023 to KRW 14.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 32 billion in 2025. The quarterly pattern differed from the annual recovery.

After operating profit of KRW 6.91 billion in the third quarter of 2025, the fourth quarter posted an operating loss of KRW 1.9 billion. Operating profit returned to KRW 2.26 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and rose 45.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 3.28 billion in the second quarter.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 39.8 billion, down 2.2% year on year, operating profit was KRW 1.56 billion, down 77.5%, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 380 million, down 87.6%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 2.98 billion.

Revenue rose to KRW 49.9 billion in the second quarter of 2026 before falling to KRW 39.8 billion in the third quarter, a sizable quarter-to-quarter swing.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for bicycles in Korea is moving in two directions. Under high oil prices, the company says electric bicycles have become more useful as a substitute for cars and public transport for short trips. The consumer base spanning commuting and leisure is also widening.

General bicycles, by contrast, have gained interest as low-cost urban mobility amid high prices, while low-priced Chinese products are a variable in price competition.

Some in the industry have worried that shared electric scooters and e-bikes would erode demand for personal bicycles, but there is also an argument that shared service operators have become large buyers and a new customer group for manufacturers.

In competition, Korea's bicycle industry is described as a two-player structure. Etoday reported that Samchuly Bicycle returned to operating profit in 2024 while the other of the top two turned to an operating loss in the same year.

The company says it has lowered entry barriers by splitting its electric bicycle lineup by riding purpose and making batteries, motors, and drivetrains interchangeable. On after-sales, it highlights a system in which trained specialists handle electric bicycles.

Whether the electric bicycle demand cycle continues and how price competition in general bicycles affects profitability will shape the company's position in the industry.

06

Outlook

When it announced 2025 results, the company said it would launch new and competitive product lineups and new models in 2026.

For the second quarter of 2026, the company said the bicycle industry's peak sales season and children's products using the Catch Teenieping and KBO licenses around Family Month in May were selling well. At its first-quarter 2026 results, the company said it expected sales momentum to continue into the second quarter.

Electric bicycles have also drawn attention as gifts for seniors. For general bicycles, the company plans to expand marketing around its more price-competitive lineup. Discussions with shared mobility operators are ongoing, according to a company official.

Because second-quarter 2026 results, with revenue of KRW 49.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.28 billion, have already been filed, the next points to watch are the trends after the third quarter.

The causes of the 77.5% year-on-year drop in third-quarter 2026 operating profit, and fourth-quarter results, will be covered in the 2026 annual report. New product launches in the spring 2027 season and sales in the second-quarter 2027 peak season will show whether the earnings recovery continues.

08

Bull factors

Profitability improvement led by electric bicycles

Operating profit in 2025 rose 312.3% to KRW 12.4 billion, and the operating margin improved from -5.9% in 2023 to 7.1% in 2025. At the first-quarter 2026 results, the company said consolidated operating profit rose 73% year on year. Electric bicycle segment revenue growth of 45% in the same quarter is cited as the driver of this improvement.

Quality and after-sales differentiation

Ranking first in the electric bicycle category of the 2025 Korea Quality Satisfaction Index (KS-QEI) is cited as external recognition of product quality. The company built a nationwide integrated after-sales system to improve customer access, and says it has kept brand trust against low-priced Chinese products.

The company argues that such after-sales support influences purchase decisions for higher-priced electric bicycles.

Improving cash generation

Controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 98.6 billion at the end of 2025, and total liabilities were KRW 126.1 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 76.2% in 2024 to 72.9% in 2025.

Bear factors

Sharp third-quarter profit drop and quarterly volatility

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 1.56 billion, down 77.5% year on year, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 380 million, down 87.6%. The fourth quarter of 2025 posted an operating loss of KRW 1.9 billion, so quarterly losses have also occurred.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 2.98 billion over the last four quarters, a sizable swing across quarters.

Price competition in general bicycles

In general bicycles, the company is responding with a more price-competitive lineup amid a push from low-priced Chinese products. If consumer spending weakens under high prices, both average selling prices and volumes could come under pressure. The expansion of marketing for general bicycles also reflects this competitive pressure.

Gap between consolidated and controlling-shareholder profit

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 11.6 billion in 2025, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.87 billion, with the difference representing non-controlling interests.

Controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 98.6 billion of total equity of KRW 172.9 billion at the end of 2025. To see the profit that actually belongs to controlling shareholders, the controlling-shareholder figure is the relevant base.

Risk factors

Demand and competition risk

Low-priced Chinese products could pressure prices in general bicycles. Some in the industry worry that the spread of shared e-scooters and electric bicycles will erode demand for personal bicycles.

Supplying shared operators has become a new sales channel, but the scale and durability of these bulk purchases remain matters to track through disclosures.

Earnings volatility risk

Quarterly results have swung widely, from an operating loss of KRW 1.9 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to operating profit of KRW 3.28 billion in the second quarter of 2026. Operating profit fell 77.5% year on year in the third quarter of 2026.

Because the second quarter, the industry's peak season, weighs heavily on annual results, a shortfall in peak-season sales could quickly narrow the annual profit.

Financial and ownership structure risk

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 52.7% in 2022 to 76.2% in 2024 before easing to 72.9% in 2025. A large part of consolidated equity belongs to non-controlling interests, so swings in subsidiary results flow into the controlling-shareholder profit.

Because the travel business accounts for a large share of consolidated revenue, changes in that segment also affect consolidated results.

Overall view

Samchuly Bicycle's filed results show a recovery from an operating loss in 2023 to operating profit of KRW 12.4 billion in 2025. Revenue grew in 2024 and 2025, and the company attributes this mainly to higher electric bicycle sales.

In the first quarter of 2026, electric bicycle segment revenue rose 45% year on year, and the company said consolidated operating profit rose 73%.

By contrast, third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 77.5% year on year to KRW 1.56 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 380 million. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 2.98 billion, with wide variation between quarters.

Strengths cited include the electric bicycle-led margin improvement, quality recognition and the integrated after-sales network, and improved operating cash flow. Weaknesses include the sharp third-quarter profit decline, price competition in general bicycles, and the non-controlling interest structure.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter results and the explanation for the third-quarter decline in the 2026 annual report, followed by the first- and second-quarter 2027 results.
