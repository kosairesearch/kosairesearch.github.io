Profit Turn in 2025 Reversed by Three Straight Quarterly Operating Losses

02

Business structure

ContentreeJoongAng runs three segments: Content, Space and Others. The Content segment consists of SLL, which produces and distributes dramas and entertainment shows, and PLUS M, which invests in, produces and distributes films; in the 2025 Q1 quarterly report this segment accounted for 61.8% of revenue.

The Space segment, centered on the Megabox cinema chain and the Playtime indoor kids' play venues, made up 26.0% in the same report. Others, which covers leasing and IT services, accounted for 12.2%. On a full-year basis, the 2024 mix was Content 59.7%, Space 32.5% and Others 7.9%, while Space was 29.9% in 2023.

Within Content, SLL supplies dramas to JTBC, and broadcast episode count and OTT sales mix are the main drivers of earnings swings. In 2026 Q1 the number of JTBC drama episodes aired rose from 16 a year earlier to 30, and the share of exclusive OTT sales widened.

In film, 'The King's Man' (Ahn Jae-hong-style title as released in Korea: 'The Man Who Would Be King'), produced with the participation of SLL label BA Entertainment, reached 16.8 million cumulative admissions.

In Space, Megabox recorded 31.9 million total admissions in 2026 Q1, the highest first-quarter level since COVID-19. However, a company-tracking commentary noted that OTT growth has stalled the recovery in cinema attendance and pressured revenue (as of June 2026).

The competitive set includes Studio Dragon and CJ ENM in drama production and distribution, and CJ CGV among multiplex operators.

04

Earnings analysis

Full-year revenue rose from KRW 852.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 993 billion in 2023 (up 16.5% year on year), fell to KRW 879.5 billion in 2024 (down 11.4%), and reached KRW 1.08 trillion in 2025 (up 23.2%), the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating losses of KRW 71.6 billion in 2022, KRW 68.1 billion in 2023 and KRW 47.4 billion in 2024 gave way to operating profit of KRW 8.84 billion in 2025, a turn to profitability. The operating margin moved from -8.4% in 2022 to -6.9% in 2023 and -5.4% in 2024, before reaching 0.8% in 2025.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 43 billion in 2022, KRW 123.1 billion in 2023, KRW 66.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 90.4 billion in 2025, marking four consecutive years of losses. That loss widened in 2023, narrowed in 2024 and widened again in 2025.

The net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 93.1 billion in 2025, larger than the controlling-shareholder figure.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 50.1 billion in 2022 to negative KRW 84.9 billion in 2023, then negative KRW 27.9 billion in 2024 and negative KRW 261 million in 2025, so the cash outflow narrowed in the last two years.

The debt ratio fell from 380.9% in 2023 to 317.8% in 2025, but total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 1.89 trillion, against total equity of KRW 596.2 billion of which controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 145.4 billion.

By quarter, operating profit was KRW 11.4 billion in 2025 Q3 and KRW 7.91 billion in 2025 Q4 (down 30.5% from the prior quarter). In 2026 Q1 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.56 billion, followed by an operating loss of KRW 59.8 billion in Q2 and KRW 58.2 billion in Q3, with the Q3 loss narrower than Q2.

Revenue in 2026 Q3 was KRW 7.66 billion, down 97.3% year on year, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders that quarter was KRW 236.7 billion. The Q1 2026 turnaround was linked in company and press releases to more JTBC drama episodes, a larger share of exclusive OTT sales and film box office. The drivers of the losses from Q2 onward are items to be confirmed in the periodic report text.

05

Industry analysis

In Korean drama production, earnings depend heavily on broadcast episode count and the share of advance sales to overseas OTT platforms.

The company said its recoupment rate, the share of production costs recovered, rose as the share of exclusive OTT sales widened, and SLL's consolidated operating profit turning positive in 2026 Q1 reflects that trend.

In cinema, a January 2026 brokerage report noted that the domestic box office in 2025 Q4 reached KRW 310.8 billion, up 23% year on year. In financial data the company released in February 2026, average ticket prices in the theater business rose more than 9% from a year earlier.

A company-tracking commentary, however, said OTT growth limits the scope of cinema attendance recovery. It is difficult to pin down where the industry sits in its cycle.

Hits and expanded broadcast slots supported the 2026 Q1 profit, while whether the deterioration from Q2 onward reflects industry conditions or company-specific factors is a matter for the periodic reports to clarify. Studio Dragon and CJ ENM are the comparison points in drama production, and CJ CGV in cinemas.

At group level, SLL said it posted consolidated operating profit of KRW 15.5 billion in 2025, its best result since its launch, while the parent's operating profit for the same year was KRW 8.84 billion. The gap shows that subsidiary results and holding-level profit can move differently.

Risk factors

Broadcast and box office volatility

Content segment revenue and profit depend heavily on drama episode counts and film box office results. The 2026 Q1 turn to profit leaned partly on more broadcast episodes and a film hit, so quarters with fewer slots or without a hit could see profit weaken again. Revenue in 2026 Q3 fell to KRW 7.66 billion, which shows how large quarter-to-quarter swings can be.

Financial leverage

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 317.8%, with total liabilities of KRW 1.89 trillion. Operating cash flow was still negative in 2025, and convertible bond repayment and the delayed SLL listing were cited as financial burdens.

Because most equity is non-controlling interest, the cushion of net assets attributable to controlling shareholders is thin.

Cinema and OTT structural change

Some assessments note that OTT growth is capping cinema revenue as attendance recovery stalls. Business restructuring, including a possible Megabox and Lotte Cinema merger, is seen as an expected driver, but its effect would depend on scope and timing. Competition among multiplex operators such as CJ CGV also weighs on ticket pricing and share.

Overall view

ContentreeJoongAng's results are split between two pillars, Content and Space. Operating profit in 2025 turned to KRW 8.84 billion, but operating losses have run for three quarters since 2026 Q1, and the controlling-shareholder net loss in 2026 Q3 was KRW 236.7 billion.

SLL reported a return to profit in 2026 Q1 and its best result since launch in 2025, and the cinema business showed attendance recovery and higher ticket prices.

On the other side, the stalling of attendance recovery in Space as OTT spreads, a debt ratio of 317.8% with controlling-shareholder equity of KRW 145.4 billion, and four consecutive years of controlling-shareholder net losses from 2022 to 2025 are weak points.

Strengths and weaknesses have coexisted over the same period, and the central question is whether the subsidiary-level recovery has carried through to consolidated results.

The share price sits well below book value per share on controlling-shareholder equity, but with no earnings-based multiple available, comparisons based on multiples are limited.

The next points to confirm are the 2026 Q4 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report, particularly how full-year operating results and the debt structure are presented there.
