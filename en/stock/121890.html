02

Business structure

SD system was founded in April 2001 as an intelligent transportation and toll-collection equipment maker, spun off from Samsung SDS, and listed on KOSDAQ in 2010.

Its core businesses are highway toll-collection systems, on-board units (OBU) for hi-pass, intelligent transportation management systems (ITS), and fire information systems, with a broader range that includes CCTV-based integrated control and parking control systems.

The company developed single-lane and multi-lane hi-pass systems in-house, manufactures toll-collection equipment itself, and supplies installation and maintenance, including system upgrades, as a single package. Segment weights differ widely by source because classifications and reference periods vary.

A Butler compilation based on 2Q 2026 revenue shows fire information systems at 38%, intelligent transportation management at 31%, toll-collection systems at 22%, and hi-pass terminals at 9%.

An Alphasquare compilation instead puts the intelligent transportation system segment at 84.23%, so the reading depends on how the segments are defined. Its main customers are public and quasi-public buyers, including the national expressway operator and private toll-road operators.

Under this structure, revenue moves sharply from quarter to quarter depending on when deliveries and acceptance inspections occur. Revenue in 4Q 2025 was KRW 10.6 billion, more than double the KRW 5.05 billion of 3Q 2025, and in 1Q 2026 it fell to KRW 2.79 billion, down 73.6% from the prior quarter.

As of June 2026 the company had 64 employees, and it runs equipment manufacturing alongside software development and supply.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 22.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 21.3 billion in 2023, a decline of 5.3%, and then dropped a further 15.2% to KRW 18.1 billion in 2024. Revenue rose 59.4% to KRW 28.8 billion in 2025, which was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 730 million in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 1.03 billion in 2023, which was a turn to profit, then to an operating loss of KRW 2.16 billion in 2024, which was a turn to loss, and finally to operating profit of KRW 1.1 billion in 2025, which was a turn back to profit.

Operating margins were -3.2%, 4.8%, -12.0% and 3.8%, so profitability swung more with cost structure than with revenue size.

According to a Digital Today report based on DART filings, the 2025 revenue increase was attributed to higher carried-over sales and a larger order intake in the year, while the operating profit improvement was attributed to better cost ratios and expense cuts.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.64 billion in 2023 and KRW 1.27 billion in 2025, while 2022 and 2024 were net losses. Net profit including non-controlling interests is reported with the same amounts in each year.

Operating cash flow was positive in 2022 to 2024, at KRW 357 million, KRW 1.45 billion and KRW 2.01 billion, but turned to an outflow of KRW 1.93 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, 3Q 2025 revenue was KRW 5.05 billion with operating profit of KRW 24.97 million, while 4Q 2025 revenue was KRW 10.6 billion with operating profit of KRW 958 million, up 3,737.4% from the prior quarter, so most of the year's operating profit landed in the fourth quarter.

In 2026, revenue was KRW 2.79 billion in 1Q with an operating loss of KRW 864 million, KRW 2.22 billion in 2Q with an operating loss of KRW 879 million, and KRW 4.45 billion in 3Q with an operating loss of KRW 315 million, and the losses have been recorded in each of those three quarters. 3Q 2026 revenue rose 100.2% from the prior quarter but fell 11.8% from a year earlier.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 952 million over the last four quarters. The pattern shows losses widening in quarters when revenue fell into the KRW 2 billion range.

05

Industry analysis

The intelligent transportation and hi-pass toll-collection market is structured around public procurement, where order sizes follow the capital spending and maintenance budgets of road operators.

SD system's quarterly revenue swings appear to reflect the concentration of deliveries and acceptance inspections in particular quarters, although project timing for each company has to be checked against individual filings.

The company's explanation that a larger 2025 order intake lifted 2025 revenue shows that orders convert into sales with a time lag. Revenue in 3Q 2026 fell 11.8% from a year earlier, which indicates that gaps in procurement can show up within a single quarter.

In a sector comparison, Alphasquare put SD system's operating margin at 3.82% on an annual basis across 96 companies in the electronic equipment sector, compared with a sector average of -11.74% in the same compilation. The same compilation gives a sector average dividend yield of 2.05%.

The company's strength lies in producing toll-collection equipment in-house and handling installation and maintenance, so equipment and service revenue arise from the same project.

The flip side is that reliance on project-based orders means revenue and fixed costs can quickly fall out of balance in quarters when a large delivery is missing.

The starting point for the outlook is order flow as of the end of 2025. The company attributed 2025 revenue growth to carried-over sales and a larger order intake for the year, which suggests that 2026 revenue depends on how work on the prior year's orders progresses.

Quarterly revenue in 2026 was KRW 2.79 billion in 1Q, KRW 2.22 billion in 2Q and KRW 4.45 billion in 3Q, a pattern of weak first-half revenue followed by a recovery in 3Q. Compared with the concentration of KRW 10.6 billion in 4Q 2025, a year-end seasonality in revenue is likely.

The 3Q 2026 operating loss narrowed to KRW 315 million from KRW 879 million in 2Q, which shows partial progress on margins through cost and expense control.

However, the 3Q 2026 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 337 million is still a loss, so whether operating profit returns in 4Q remains to be verified in filings.

Because the company cited better cost ratios and expense cuts for its 2025 profit improvement, the operating margin in the next periodic report is the key indicator of whether that improvement holds.

Order disclosures show the size and timing of supply contracts with public buyers and are the quickest source for judging revenue trends into 4Q 2026 and 2027.

The 2026 annual preliminary results may be released early in the following year, as the 2025 results were, and the key check at that point will be whether 4Q revenue approaches the 4Q 2025 level.

Bull factors

Rebound in 2025 results

A Digital Today report based on DART filings attributed the revenue growth to carried-over sales and a larger order intake.

Low-borrowing balance sheet

Total liabilities were KRW 8.18 billion and total equity KRW 12 billion at the end of 2025. The debt ratio rose to 68.3% from 21.6% in 2023, while JobKorea's financial compilation shows borrowings at 0.22% of the balance sheet. A small reliance on borrowing is a factor that supports the balance sheet during loss-making periods.

Narrower loss in 3Q 2026

The 3Q 2026 operating loss narrowed to KRW 315 million from KRW 879 million in 2Q 2026, while revenue rose 100.2% from the prior quarter to KRW 4.45 billion. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders also narrowed to KRW 337 million from KRW 821 million. The narrowing loss in a quarter of higher revenue shows that sales and profit can move together.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders also continued over the same period, totaling KRW 952 million over the last four quarters. Operating cash flow turned negative

Operating cash flow swung from a KRW 2.01 billion inflow in 2024 to a KRW 1.93 billion outflow in 2025. Cash left operations even though 2025 operating profit was positive, which signals weak conversion of profit into cash. The filed figures alone do not separate the causes, such as working capital changes.

Quarterly revenue swings and procurement dependence

After KRW 10.6 billion of revenue in 4Q 2025, 1Q 2026 revenue fell 73.6% to KRW 2.79 billion. Because sales are tied to public procurement projects, a timing gap in one quarter can shift the whole year's results. The 2024 operating margin of -12.0% also shows how sharply profit moved in a year with smaller revenue.

Buyer concentration and business structure

The main customers are concentrated among public and quasi-public buyers, including the national expressway operator and private toll-road operators. If procurement budgets or project schedules change, the timing of revenue recognition can shift.

The large weight of fire information systems and intelligent transportation management also makes results sensitive to order flow in those areas.

Financial and cash flow pressure

Overall view

SD system returned to annual profit in 2025, with revenue of KRW 28.8 billion, up 59.4%, and operating profit of KRW 1.1 billion. However, operating losses have continued from 1Q 2026 through 3Q 2026, and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 952 million over the last four quarters.

In 3Q 2026, revenue was KRW 4.45 billion and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 315 million from the prior quarter. Revenue moves sharply from quarter to quarter with the timing of public procurement deliveries, so a single quarter is not enough to judge the direction of results.

Operating cash flow turned to a KRW 1.93 billion outflow in 2025, and the debt ratio rose to 68.3% from 21.6% in 2023. Because no earnings-based PER is calculated, the share price sits in a premium range to book value, and the pace of earnings recovery becomes a major variable.

The next checks are the 2026 annual preliminary results, operating cash flow and 4Q revenue in the 2026 annual report, and whether 1Q 2027 returns to profit.
