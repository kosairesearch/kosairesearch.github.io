InfoBank: Messaging Shifts Toward AI as Quarterly Profit Swings

InfoBank is shifting from enterprise messaging toward an AI communication platform. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was positive from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026, but operating losses have continued for five consecutive quarters since the third quarter of 2025.

  1. 1

    Revenue fell for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, reaching KRW 135.1 billion in 2025. It rebounded to KRW 43.7 billion in the second quarter of 2026, up 28.4% from the prior quarter.

  2. 2

    The 2025 operating loss narrowed to KRW 4.2 billion from the prior year. The operating loss widened again to KRW 672 million in the third quarter of 2026 from the prior quarter.

  3. 3

    Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss in 2024 and 2025. It turned to a profit in the first quarter of 2026, at KRW 1.1 billion in the second quarter and KRW 656 million in the third. The four-quarter total from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 is a net loss of KRW 439 million.

  4. 4

    Operating cash flow was negative from 2023 through 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 38.6% in 2022 to 43.4% in 2025.

  5. 5

02

Business structure

InfoBank was founded in 1995 and is headquartered in Seongnam, Gyeonggi Province. Services accounted for 99% of revenue in the second quarter of 2026, with other businesses at 1%. Its business areas are a communication platform, AI solutions, mobility and smart car, financial SI, and investment and acceleration.

The communication platform handles enterprise business messaging. Messaging began as bulk text sending for companies and has expanded into messenger integration and chatbots. Its AI offering includes the LLM-based chatbot Chatist, which answers questions using company data, along with custom chatbot builds.

The company says it is shifting toward an AI-combined CPaaS model built on decades of messaging technology, and its research lab iLab applies text analysis and data preprocessing automation. Its IN7 platform, launched in October 2025, was selected as a 2026 cloud voucher supplier by NIPA in April 2026.

Earlier reports from around 2023 described its automotive work with Hyundai Motor Group, including in-vehicle infotainment and telematics modules, which are project-based system development contracts with limited margins and few royalty prospects.

A market description from around 2023 listed seven competitors in enterprise messaging, including KT, LG Uplus and Daou Technology, with KT at 44%, LG Uplus at 26% and InfoBank at 10% share. Because that data is from around 2023, current shares may differ.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 140.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 136.9 billion in 2023, was flat in 2024, and declined to KRW 135.1 billion in 2025, down 1.3% year on year. Revenue has fallen for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2022 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

The operating loss widened from KRW 346 million in 2022 to KRW 2.2 billion in 2023 and KRW 5.13 billion in 2024, then narrowed to KRW 4.2 billion in 2025. The company has posted operating losses for four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025.

Operating margin moved from -0.2% in 2022 to -3.8% in 2024 and -3.1% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.19 billion in 2023, up 328.6% year on year, but turned to a net loss of KRW 3.83 billion in 2024 and widened to a net loss of KRW 4.14 billion in 2025.

The positive attributable profit in 2023 despite an operating loss suggests non-operating items played a role.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.29 billion in 2022, KRW 5.95 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 3.74 billion in 2024 and a net loss of KRW 4.69 billion in 2025, a different measure from attributable earnings.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 4.6 billion in 2022 to negative figures in 2023, 2024 and 2025, with the 2025 outflow at KRW 150 million. The debt-to-equity ratio was 38.6% in 2022, 29.4% in 2023, 35.0% in 2024 and 43.4% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 67.5 billion, of which KRW 65.1 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 29.3 billion.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 35.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 35 billion, KRW 34 billion, then KRW 43.7 billion in the second quarter of 2026, before KRW 37.7 billion in the third quarter, up 4.9% year on year.

Operating losses were KRW 321 million, KRW 493 million, KRW 885 million, KRW 314 million and KRW 672 million across the five quarters from the third quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026.

Attributable net income moved from KRW 302 million in the third quarter of 2025 to a net loss of KRW 2.36 billion in the fourth quarter, then to profits of KRW 168 million, KRW 1.1 billion and KRW 656 million in the first three quarters of 2026.

In the second quarter revenue rose and profit recovered, but the operating loss widened again in the third quarter, so the earnings improvement did not move in step with operating performance.

In 2024, the company said it aimed to return to operating profit the following year, but the 2025 operating loss was KRW 4.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Enterprise messaging in Korea has seven competitors, including KT, LG Uplus, InfoBank and Daou Technology, and the top three players hold roughly 80% share, according to a report from around 2023.

The same report projected a 16.9% annual growth rate for the market, a figure that may no longer reflect current conditions. Telecom carriers faced possible limits on low-priced messaging, which was seen as a medium-term benefit for non-carrier enterprise messaging providers like InfoBank.

One securities researcher judged that competition in mobile messaging could ease over the medium term, but that profitability for these companies was unlikely to improve sharply. The AI chatbot build market is highly competitive, with many software firms entering and low-price contracts becoming common.

NAVER and Kakao focus on consumer platforms, while InfoBank concentrates on enterprise messaging infrastructure and AI solutions, so their business structures differ. Daou Technology is cited as a direct messaging rival.

In financial SI, InfoBank competes in a market where large SI firms such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS are also active.

Bull factors

Revenue rebound

Second-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 43.7 billion, up 28.4% from the prior quarter, and third-quarter revenue of KRW 37.7 billion rose 4.9% year on year. After revenue declined through 2025, quarterly sales levels rose in 2026. A report headline cited a 15% growth forecast for 2026. If realized, that would mark an end to the revenue decline.

Attributable profit turns positive

Attributable net income swung from a KRW 2.36 billion loss in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 168 million profit in the first quarter of 2026, followed by KRW 1.1 billion and KRW 656 million. The four-quarter net loss total narrowed to KRW 439 million.

Operating losses persisted, but attributable earnings moved toward recovery, and the quality of that recovery remains to be checked in the annual filing.

Balance sheet and AI groundwork

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 29.3 billion against total equity of KRW 67.5 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio was 43.4%. Price-to-book is well below 1x.

IN7 was selected as a 2026 cloud voucher supplier in April 2026, which could be an early public-sector support case for the strategy of adding an AI platform to messaging.

Bear factors

Operating losses persist

Operating losses have run for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026. The third-quarter 2026 loss of KRW 672 million widened year on year. The first-quarter 2026 loss of KRW 885 million was the largest of the last five quarters. The 2025 annual operating loss remained at KRW 4.2 billion, with an operating margin of -3.1%.

Negative operating cash flow

Operating cash flow went from KRW 4.6 billion in 2022 to outflows of KRW 3.14 billion in 2023, KRW 1.98 billion in 2024 and KRW 150 million in 2025. The 2025 outflow was much smaller, but it is too early to call the business cash generative. Whether cash flow recovers while operating losses continue will need to be checked in the next periodic report.

Competition and low-price bids

In enterprise messaging, KT and LG Uplus hold a combined share well above InfoBank's roughly 10% reported around 2023. The AI chatbot build market has many entrants, and low-price bids have been reported as common.

Price competition may have contributed to the three years of revenue declines from 2023 to 2025, though disclosed figures alone cannot confirm the cause.

10

Risk factors

Gap between net and operating results

In the second quarter of 2026, attributable net income of KRW 1.1 billion came alongside an operating loss of KRW 314 million. If non-operating items drove that profit, it would not reflect improvement in core operations. A similar gap occurred in 2023, so the source of profit needs separate review. Without operating profit, the durability of net profit may be limited.

Business concentration and project revenue

Services generate 99% of revenue, so results are sensitive to messaging volumes and pricing. Mobility work is project-based system development, which can make quarterly revenue lumpy depending on contract timing.

Whether that segment will move to royalty-type revenue is uncertain, and the pace of diversification is not yet visible in disclosed figures.

Competition with larger operators

In messaging, carrier-backed operators such as KT and LG Uplus hold leading positions, and price competition continues with mid-sized players such as Daou Technology. If regulation of carrier low-price sales does not materialize, the advantage for non-carrier providers may remain limited. The direction and timing of any such regulation are hard to confirm from public sources.

Overall view

InfoBank is in a transition from messaging toward an AI communication platform, after revenue declined from 2022.

The annual operating loss narrowed from KRW 5.13 billion in 2024 to KRW 4.2 billion in 2025, yet operating losses have continued for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue rose to KRW 43.7 billion, and attributable net income has been positive since the first quarter of 2026. Whether this reflects operating improvement or non-operating gains will need to be settled in the periodic reports.

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 29.3 billion against total equity of KRW 67.5 billion, and price-to-book sits well below 1x. Supportive factors include the revenue rebound, positive attributable profit and a modest debt load.

Negative factors include continued operating losses, negative operating cash flow and pressure from larger competitors. A report headline by NICE Information Service projected 15% growth for 2026, but third-quarter growth was only 4.9% year on year, showing wide quarterly variation.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual operating results and the direction of operating profit in the first-quarter 2027 report.
