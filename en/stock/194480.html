02

Business structure

Devsisters develops and operates mobile games built on the Cookie Run intellectual property. Its five existing live titles are Cookie Run: Kingdom, Cookie Run: Tower of Adventure, Cookie Run: Witch's Castle, Cookie Run: OvenBreak and Cookie Run: Classic, and the model depends on long-running service.

At its DevNow 2026 showcase in March 2026, the company unveiled Cookie Run: OvenSmash, a real-time battle action game announced for global release on March 26, Cookie Run: Crumble, an idle RPG, and Cookie Run: NewWorld, an open-world title targeted for 2029.

Cookie Run: Crumble launched globally on July 30, 2026, and the company said pre-registrations passed 2 million.

Beyond games, the company runs the Cookie Run: Braverse trading card game, the Roblox-based Cookie Run Card Collection, character merchandise and licensing, and non-game revenue fell in Q3 2026 because few new products or promotions were launched.

In Thailand, Cookie Run: Classic saw new user inflow rise about 1.5 times versus the week before its Thai-language update in October 2026, and the company said Cookie Run: Kingdom's U.S. metrics rose broadly after an animation collaboration.

The company said in March 2026 that the Cookie Run franchise has reached 300 million cumulative users. Competition comes from new-title-driven listed peers such as Krafton, Netmarble and Shift UP, and Devsisters is pursuing differentiation by extending a single IP across genres.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 214.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 161.1 billion in 2023, a 24.8% decline, then rose to KRW 236.2 billion in 2024, up 46.6%, and to KRW 295.6 billion in 2025, up 25.1%, making 2025 the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating losses widened from KRW 19.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 48 billion in 2023, and operating profit of KRW 27.2 billion in 2024 marked a swing into the black. Operating profit then fell 76.6% to KRW 6.35 billion in 2025.

The operating margin moved from -9.3% in 2022 and -29.8% in 2023 to 11.5% in 2024 and 2.1% in 2025, so the margin fell sharply in 2025 even as revenue grew.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 28 billion in 2024 and fell 62.5% to KRW 10.5 billion in 2025, while net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 10.8 billion.

Operating cash flow went from negative in 2022 and 2023 to KRW 45.1 billion in 2024 and then fell to KRW 8.3 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio was 65.8% in 2022, 78.2% in 2023, 68.9% in 2024 and 78.6% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 133.3 billion and total equity of KRW 169.5 billion at end-2025, of which KRW 166.2 billion belonged to controlling shareholders.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 54.9 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 59.6 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 58.5 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 52.7 billion in Q2 2026, before rising to KRW 81.2 billion in Q3 2026.

Operating results were a loss of KRW 729 million in Q3 2025, a loss of KRW 12.4 billion in Q4 2025, a loss of KRW 17.4 billion in Q1 2026 and a loss of KRW 16 billion in Q2 2026, before an operating profit of KRW 7.24 billion in Q3 2026.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was negative from Q4 2025 through Q2 2026, and the Q3 2026 figure of KRW 3.48 billion rose 118.0% year on year.

The company attributed the Q3 improvement to lower branding costs, the disappearance of one-off staff-related costs from the first half, and structural fixed-cost savings, while higher advertising and payment fees tied to new launches partly offset these gains.

05

Industry analysis

The mobile game industry rewards companies that keep large IP in long-running service while releasing new titles in succession.

Devsisters is extending the Cookie Run franchise across genres, including an idle RPG in Cookie Run: Crumble, a real-time battle action game in Cookie Run: OvenSmash and an open-world project in Cookie Run: NewWorld.

Because two new titles drove Q3 2026 growth, how long their popularity lasts is likely to shape the company's standing within the sector. The company cites 300 million cumulative Cookie Run users and is widening into trading cards, offline events and character merchandise.

China requires a publishing license before launch, and the company secured that license in August 2026, which opens a path to revenue there.

Among listed peers such as Krafton, Netmarble, PearlAbyss, Shift UP and Kakao Games, many run several IPs or genres at once, so Devsisters has a relatively concentrated single-IP profile.

Overseas, live-title metrics in Thailand and the United States have risen, while the non-game business is still in the phase of expanding trading card and licensing revenue. Sector-wide supply and demand indicators are not included in this assessment.

Outlook

In its plans for Q4 2026, the company cited expanded language support for its major titles and stronger competitive content and collaborations. For Cookie Run: Crumble, it plans to add new cookies and pets at four-week intervals after launch.

The non-game business aims to pass KRW 20 billion in annual cumulative revenue through new merchandise and a wider licensing business. As of the March 2026 announcement, the Cookie Run Card Collection was planned for global release within 2026.

Cookie Run: Tower of Adventure began its second closed beta in China on October 29, 2026, with about 30,000 local testers, to examine content and the monetization system before a launch planned with a local publisher once the publishing license was in hand. No local launch date has been announced.

Cookie Run: NewWorld is in development as a cross-platform PC, console and mobile title targeting 2029, and Project AR is scheduled to be unveiled in 2027.

The next financial checkpoints are Q4 2026 and full-year results, where the durability of new-title revenue and the level of advertising spend will shape the trend, and whether the KRW 20 billion non-game target is reached.

The 2026 annual report will disclose the full-year operating margin, operating cash flow and debt ratio, which can be compared directly with the 2.1% margin and 78.6% debt ratio of 2025.

08

Bull factors

Early revenue momentum of Cookie Run: Crumble

Cookie Run: Crumble launched globally on July 30, 2026, and the company said it passed KRW 20 billion in cumulative revenue in under a month.

After a recent update, the title returned to the top three of domestic Apple game revenue rankings, and monthly playtime rose 30% from early launch levels, according to the company. The title was cited as a key driver of the 47.9% year-on-year revenue growth in Q3 2026.

Improving metrics for overseas live titles

Cookie Run: Classic added full Thai-language support in October 2026, after which new user inflow in Thailand rose about 1.5 times versus the week before the update.

Cookie Run: Kingdom began a collaboration with an animated series on October 21, 2026, and revenue, new users and active users rose broadly in the United States. The company plans to focus on core regions such as Thailand to raise user activity and profitability.

Effect of the cost-structure improvements

The company listed lower branding costs, the disappearance of one-off staff-related costs from the first half and structural fixed-cost savings as drivers of Q3 profitability. Even with higher advertising and payment fees from new launches, operating profit turned positive at KRW 7.24 billion in Q3 2026. Whether the fixed-cost savings persist once new-title revenue stabilizes is the next point to check.

Bear factors

Sharp profit decline in 2025

Revenue rose 25.1% to KRW 295.6 billion in 2025, but operating profit fell 76.6% to KRW 6.35 billion. The operating margin dropped from 11.5% in 2024 to 2.1% in 2025, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 62.5% to KRW 10.5 billion. The core weakness of the 2025 results is that revenue growth did not translate into profit.

Quarterly losses and accumulated deficit

Operating losses of KRW 12.4 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 17.4 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 16 billion in Q2 2026 followed one another. Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders total KRW 37.7 billion over the last four quarters. The Q3 2026 profit is a single-quarter result, so it offers limited evidence that the loss phase has ended.

Cash flow and financial burden

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 45.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 8.3 billion in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 68.9% in 2024 to 78.6% in 2025, and total liabilities stood at KRW 133.3 billion at end-2025.

Non-game revenue declined in Q3 2026 because few new products or promotions were released, so the contribution from outside games remains small.

Risk factors

Regulation and China launch

Cookie Run: Tower of Adventure secured a Chinese publishing license in August 2026, and its second closed beta began on October 29, 2026. The company plans to review content and monetization with a local publisher before launch, so the timing and monetization structure remain undecided. Results in China could change the overseas revenue mix and the cost burden.

Durability of new-title demand

Cookie Run: Crumble passed 2 million pre-registrations, but it is not yet clear whether early interest will convert into a paying user base. The company plans to add new cookies and pets at four-week intervals after launch, and whether that schedule holds will influence long-term revenue. A large part of the Q3 revenue increase relies on the early effect of new titles.

Large development spending and funding

Cookie Run: NewWorld is in development as a cross-platform title targeting 2029, and Project AR is set to be unveiled in 2027. Running several large projects at once raises development and marketing costs together. Financial capacity will depend on whether internal cash flow or external funding covers these investments.

Overall view

Devsisters operates live Cookie Run titles alongside new releases built on the same IP, and it returned to operating profit of KRW 7.24 billion in Q3 2026.

Early revenue from Cookie Run: Crumble and the Thai performance of Cookie Run: Classic drove that profit, and the company attributed part of the improvement to lower branding costs and fixed-cost savings.

However, the 2025 operating margin fell to 2.1% from 11.5% in 2024, and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders total KRW 37.7 billion over the last four quarters. The debt ratio rose to 78.6% at end-2025 and operating cash flow declined.

Because the profit so far covers a single quarter, continuity has not yet been confirmed. The next checkpoints are post-update revenue trends for Cookie Run: Crumble, the timing of the China launch, and the full-year operating margin and cash flow in the 2026 annual report.
