02

Business structure

Devsisters builds its business around the Cookie Run IP, running both game and non-game operations.

Five live games are currently in service: Cookie Run: Kingdom, Cookie Run: OvenBreak, Cookie Run: Classic, Cookie Run: Tower of Adventures, and Cookie Run: Witch's Castle, and the company treats Cookie Run: Kingdom as its core title.

In 2026 it unveiled several new titles, including the real-time battle action game Cookie Run: OvenSmash, the idle RPG Cookie Run: Crumble, and the open-world Cookie Run: NewWorld. The company said Cookie Run: Crumble passed KRW 20 billion in cumulative revenue less than a month after launch.

Cookie Run: Classic broadened its user base in Thailand, which press reports described as an overseas success. The company said the Cookie Run IP has 300 million cumulative users and aims to grow it into a content platform beyond games.

Non-game operations include TCG card products, the Roblox-based Cookie Run Card Collection, and offline pop-ups and events, and the company targets more than KRW 20 billion in annual non-game revenue for 2026. In Q3, non-game revenue declined because there were no major new product launches or promotions.

Competitively, Devsisters is compared with listed game companies such as Krafton and Netmarble that extend large IPs across genres, and it remains highly dependent on a single major IP.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 214.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 161.1 billion in 2023, a 24.8% decline, then rose to KRW 236.2 billion in 2024 (up 46.6%) and KRW 295.6 billion in 2025 (up 25.1%), growing for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

Of the 2022 to 2025 period, 2025 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest. Operating losses widened from KRW 19.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 48 billion in 2023, before the company returned to an operating profit of KRW 27.2 billion in 2024.

However, 2025 operating profit fell 76.6% to KRW 6.35 billion, and the operating margin dropped from 11.5% in 2024 to 2.1% in 2025. Revenue grew, but less of it converted into profit that year. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 62.5% from KRW 28 billion in 2024 to KRW 10.5 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, which is a separate figure, was KRW 28.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.8 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 26.1 billion in 2023 to KRW 45.1 billion in 2024 and then KRW 8.3 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 169.5 billion, of which KRW 166.2 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 133.3 billion, with the debt ratio rising from 68.9% in 2024 to 78.6% in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating losses of KRW 729 million in Q3 2025 and KRW 12.4 billion in Q4 2025 widened, followed by operating losses of KRW 17.4 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 16 billion in Q2 2026.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 81.2 billion, up 54.1% from the prior quarter and 47.9% year on year, and operating profit turned positive at KRW 7.24 billion. Coverage attributes the revenue gain mainly to the early performance of Cookie Run: Crumble and overseas results from Cookie Run: Classic.

On costs, advertising and payment fees rose, but lower branding spend, the end of one-time personnel costs, and fixed cost reductions offset them. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders in the same quarter was KRW 3.48 billion, up 118.0% year on year.

Over the last four quarters, however, the sum of net losses attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 37.7 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic game industry is reorganizing around large IPs that extend across genres and platforms. Mobile-centered revenue models are increasingly being paired with PC, console, and external platform presence.

Devsisters frames its Cookie Run strategy around genre expansion (idle RPG, real-time battle action, open world) and platform expansion (Roblox, and AR in the future) under the 2026 keywords of 'expansion' and 'evolution'.

The company lists three pillars: fandom expansion, genre and platform expansion, and cultural expansion. It is also validating market potential for its overseas titles together with local publishing partners.

Other listed peers such as Krafton, Netmarble, NC, Pearl Abyss, and Shift UP are also pursuing new titles and IP expansion in parallel, so how a new launch performs relative to peers shapes earnings volatility.

Among KOSDAQ game companies, Devsisters relies heavily on a single IP, so its IP portfolio is narrower than many peers and any one title carries a large weight in quarterly results. In mobile casual launches, playtime and app store ranking are used as early performance indicators. For Cookie Run: Crumble, the company said monthly playtime rose 30% compared with the early launch period.

Cookie Run: OvenSmash was announced for global release on March 26, 2026, and Cookie Run: Crumble, scheduled for the second half, became one of the drivers of Q3 results. The Roblox-based Cookie Run Card Collection is planned for global release within 2026.

The open-world title Cookie Run: NewWorld has reached the stage of having its trailer revealed. Project AR, which uses GPS and AR technology, is slated for 2027. The long-term Project N is being developed as an Unreal Engine-based open-world 3D action RPG.

On non-game operations, the company plans to launch new starter decks, booster packs, and 10th-anniversary character merchandise for Cookie Run: Braverse Card Game in North America in Q4, aiming to pass KRW 20 billion in cumulative annual non-game revenue.

The company says it will keep making strategic investments in new development and IP business expansion. The next checkpoints are whether the new titles' performance continues quarter to quarter and how higher investment affects the operating cost structure. Whether non-game cumulative revenue reaches the KRW 20 billion target in 2026 is also a point to confirm.

Overall view

Devsisters is extending the Cookie Run IP across genres and platforms, and its Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 7.24 billion marked a first sign of earnings recovery.

However, 2025 operating profit fell 76.6% to KRW 6.35 billion with a 2.1% margin, so whether the Q3 turn carries into a full-year profit recovery is a matter for upcoming quarterly results.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters total KRW 37.7 billion, which is why PER is not calculable. On an asset basis, price-to-book is below 1x, though this metric is read together with the pace of earnings recovery.

Q3 results were driven by the early performance of Cookie Run: Crumble and overseas results from Cookie Run: Classic, and cost efficiency contributed to the margin improvement.

Bullish factors include the new-title pipeline and the improved cost structure, while bearish factors include dependence on a single IP, weaker profitability since 2025, and a debt ratio that rose to 78.6% alongside lower operating cash flow.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results and the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
