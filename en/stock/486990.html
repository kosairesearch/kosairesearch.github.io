Nota: Revenue Growing as Quarterly Losses Narrow

Nota's revenue rose for a second straight quarter to KRW 4.59 billion in the third quarter of 2026, but operating losses have continued for four consecutive quarters, so whether sales growth turns into profitability is the key question to watch.

02

Business structure

Nota sells technology that compresses AI models so they can run on constrained hardware such as smartphones, vehicles, robots and servers, and it operates as a software-centric business.

Its core products are the Netspresso platform, which automates the training, compression and deployment of AI models, and NVA (Nota Vision Agent), a video-control solution built on vision-language models.

For the first three quarters of 2026, Netspresso platform revenue was KRW 3.9 billion and Netspresso solution revenue was KRW 8.1 billion, so solutions made up about 67% of sales.

Platform customers are mainly semiconductor companies: Nota completed AI optimization supply contracts for Samsung Electronics' Exynos 2400, 2500 and 2600 processors and supplied platform technology to FuriosaAI's second-generation NPU.

Its solution business centers on transportation, safety and video control, starting with a commercial contract with Kolon Benit and expanding into construction, shipbuilding and healthcare.

Overseas, Nota continues to win intelligent transport system and security orders from the Dubai Roads and Transport Authority and in the United States, Kenya and Abu Dhabi. New customers accounted for 52.3% of revenue in the first three quarters of 2026.

Only some AI Box products are manufactured by external OEM and ODM partners.

In competition, Nota does not try to match large platform and systems-integration companies such as NAVER or Samsung SDS on scale; it positions itself as a specialist in model compression that supplies semiconductor partners on their chip roadmaps.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue rose 55.3% to KRW 13.1 billion in 2025 from KRW 8.44 billion in 2024. Over the same period the operating loss widened from KRW 12 billion to KRW 15.3 billion, so the operating margin moved from -142.4% to -116.6%. Sales grew faster than the loss, which improved the ratio, but the absolute loss became larger.

The company attributed the widening loss mainly to R&D headcount expansion and R&D investment, with investment in AI model compression technology rising sharply.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed from KRW 24.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 16.6 billion in 2025, and the company has posted losses in both years. Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 24.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 16.6 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 10.8 billion in 2024 and -KRW 11.8 billion in 2025. At end-2025, total equity was KRW 27.5 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 14.8 billion.

On a quarterly basis, fourth-quarter 2025 revenue of KRW 9.5 billion was the largest among the disclosed quarters, then fell 62.3% to KRW 3.58 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

Revenue then rose to KRW 3.82 billion in the second quarter (up 6.7% quarter on quarter) and KRW 4.59 billion in the third quarter (up 19.9%).

Operating losses widened from KRW 2.74 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 4.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 6.19 billion in the second quarter, before narrowing to KRW 3.61 billion in the third quarter.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 16.5 billion over the last four disclosed quarters. Operating expenses for January to September 2026 rose 61.1%, with outsourcing costs roughly doubling and ordinary R&D expenses rising about nineteenfold.

Operating cash outflow for the same nine months was about KRW 8.2 billion, similar to a year earlier. The loss pattern looks more like recurring spending on R&D and outsourcing for business expansion than one-time charges.

05

Industry analysis

On-device AI runs models inside devices rather than in the cloud, so competitiveness depends on how far a model can be compressed and how fast it can run under limited compute and power.

Nota calls this inference optimization and says demand is expanding not only for on-device and physical AI but also for data-center AI. Its business is widening from mobile and on-device AI into data centers and physical AI.

In data centers, it signed an additional contract with HyperAccel to supply model compression and compute kernel optimization for the 'Berda' LLM inference chip.

In physical AI, the company said it optimized vision-language-action models on the NPU of the Qualcomm Dragonwing IQ-9075, raising inference speed by up to seven times over the original model. Nota also announced its participation in the AMD Robotics Partner Network at AMD Advancing AI 2026 in July.

In a domestic on-device AI semiconductor humanoid project, LG Electronics leads the overall effort and Mobilint supplies the NPU, while Nota leads the humanoid AI model optimization task and works on compressing vision-language-action models for Mobilint's NPU.

Its partner ecosystem has widened beyond Samsung Electronics and Arm to include FuriosaAI and Mobilint. Compared with listed peers such as NAVER, Kakao and Samsung SDS, whose core businesses are cloud, platforms and systems integration, Nota focuses on model optimization software and semiconductor collaboration.

On the cycle, on-device AI investment is expanding, but monetization is still early, so R&D costs remain heavy relative to revenue. If semiconductor companies strengthen their own optimization tools, the competitive landscape could shift.

06

Outlook

The company said it signed a contract with a global semiconductor company to supply model optimization technology for personal AI, with related revenue to be recognized from the fourth quarter of 2026.

It also signed an additional contract with HyperAccel, following last year's deal, to supply model compression and compute kernel optimization for the 'Berda' LLM inference chip.

Orders rose about 120% year on year to roughly KRW 6.1 billion in the second quarter of 2026, and first-half cumulative orders reached about KRW 17.4 billion including roughly KRW 11.3 billion in the first quarter.

Cumulative orders for the first nine months of 2026 were about KRW 26.3 billion, and the order backlog at end-September was about KRW 15.4 billion.

In the public sector, Nota joined a project of roughly KRW 10.7 billion for an on-device AI urban safety network, where it is responsible for developing a vision-language-model-based multi-hazard detection model and on-device optimization.

In space, it is responsible for on-device AI optimization in a deep-tech challenge project led by Space Lintech, with up to KRW 20 billion over four years. Overseas, the company established Nota AI MEA in the United Arab Emirates in March 2026.

The CEO said growth will continue based on new contracts with global semiconductor companies, follow-up orders from existing customers, and entry into data centers and physical AI. The company's numerical annual guidance was not part of the disclosed materials used here.

The next items to check are whether the contract revenue appears in fourth-quarter 2026 results, how quickly the backlog converts into sales, and how outsourcing costs affect profit and cash flow.

Bull factors

Revenue growth and order backlog

Revenue rose 55.3% in 2025 to KRW 13.1 billion, and sales grew for a second straight quarter in 2026. The order backlog at end-September was about KRW 15.4 billion, roughly 1.28 times cumulative revenue. First-half cumulative orders were about KRW 17.4 billion.

Semiconductor partner ecosystem

Nota holds AI optimization supply contracts for Samsung Electronics' Exynos 2400, 2500 and 2600 chips and partnerships with Arm, NVIDIA and Qualcomm. A personal AI contract with revenue expected from the fourth quarter and an additional HyperAccel contract widen its revenue base.

Signs of improving loss trend

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 6.19 billion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 3.61 billion in the third quarter. The operating margin improved from -142.4% in 2024 to -116.6% in 2025, and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed from KRW 24.9 billion to KRW 16.6 billion.

09

Bear factors

Continued losses and rising costs

Operating losses have continued for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 16.5 billion over the last four quarters.

The cumulative operating loss for the first three quarters of 2026 widened from a year earlier. Outsourcing and R&D costs have risen sharply, keeping cost pressure high.

Limited cash generation

Operating cash flow was negative in both 2024 (-KRW 10.8 billion) and 2025 (-KRW 11.8 billion). Operating cash outflow for January to September 2026 was about KRW 8.2 billion, similar to a year earlier. Because revenue growth has not yet produced positive cash flow, external funding remains an important variable.

Quarterly revenue volatility

Revenue fell 62.3% from KRW 9.5 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 3.58 billion in the first quarter of 2026, showing large quarterly swings. Because orders convert into revenue at different times each quarter, the visibility of quarterly results is limited. Timing and size of semiconductor partner contract revenue can move quarterly results significantly.

10

Risk factors

Competition and technology risk

Nota specializes in model optimization, but if semiconductor companies or large platforms strengthen their own optimization tools, partnership structures could change. Because revenue depends partly on a few partner contracts, changes in contract terms or partner roadmaps would directly affect results.

Financial and funding risk

Total equity was KRW 27.5 billion at end-2025, and operating losses have persisted for four quarters. The Middle East subsidiary raised consolidated subsidiaries to three, adding overseas expansion costs.

If losses continue, additional funding or cost restructuring could become necessary, so equity size and the pace of cash burn should be read together.

Execution risk

Public-safety, humanoid and space projects are expanding at the same time, adding pressure on headcount and outsourcing budgets. Public projects go through demonstration and rollout stages, so monetization may be delayed.

Press coverage notes that the speed at which the backlog converts into revenue and outsourcing costs will determine whether profit and cash flow improve.

12

Overall view

Nota is a software company that supplies on-device AI and inference optimization technology to semiconductor companies and to public and industrial customers. Revenue rose 55.3% to KRW 13.1 billion in 2025, but the operating loss widened to KRW 15.3 billion, so sales growth did not offset the larger loss.

In 2026, revenue grew in both the second and third quarters, and the third-quarter operating loss narrowed to KRW 3.61 billion, a sign of change in the loss pattern.

However, operating losses have persisted for four consecutive quarters, net loss attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 16.5 billion over the last four quarters, and operating cash flow was negative in both 2024 and 2025.

The backlog of about KRW 15.4 billion, the contract revenue expected from the fourth quarter, and the widening semiconductor partner base support the revenue foundation.

On the other side, quarterly revenue swings, higher outsourcing and R&D spending, and reliance on a few partner contracts increase earnings volatility. On valuation, the price-to-earnings ratio cannot be calculated, and the price-to-book ratio sits in a range with a large premium over net assets.

The next items to check are the preliminary 2026 fourth-quarter results, the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and the trend of quarterly losses in the first quarter of 2027.
