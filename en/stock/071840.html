Care services expand, but profit recovery is still the test

02

Business structure

LOTTE Himart is a home appliance retailer operating appliance stores. Its product groups are large appliances, kitchen and living appliances, IT and mobile devices, private-label products, and the Care service for appliance management.

In the third quarter of 2026, large appliance sales fell 3.8% and kitchen and living appliance sales fell 4.1%, while IT and mobile sales rose 11.0%. Total Care service revenue reached KRW 21.2 billion, up 62%, and the nine-month cumulative figure reached KRW 58.6 billion, up 50%, already above full-year 2025 revenue.

The company is shifting from selling appliances to a lifetime care platform that covers repair, management and replacement. Customers who bought appliances together with services exceeded 1 million, and the linked purchase rate was 59%.

On a nine-month cumulative basis, appliance cleaning revenue rose 31%, extended warranty revenue rose 93%, and after-sales service revenue rose 24%. Private-label revenue, including the Flux brand, rose 22.7% in the third quarter.

Used mobile phone purchases reached 6,015 units in the third quarter, up from 1,212 units in the second quarter. Offline remains the core channel: offline total sales were KRW 689.3 billion, down 1.1%, while online total sales were KRW 113 billion, up 13.0%, lifting the online share to 14.1%.

Because higher-margin large and kitchen appliances drive profitability, changes in product mix directly affect operating margin.

Competition comes from offline appliance retailers and online channels, and appliance makers with rental and care services, such as Coway and Cuckoo Homesys, are reference points in the service segment.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 3.34 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.61 trillion in 2023 (down 21.8%), KRW 2.36 trillion in 2024 (down 9.7%) and KRW 2.3 trillion in 2025 (down 2.4%), a three-year decline from 2023 through 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 52 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 8.22 billion in 2023, a swing to profit, then KRW 1.72 billion in 2024 (down 79.1%) and KRW 9.64 billion in 2025 (up 460.2%).

The operating margin went from -1.6% in 2022 to 0.3% in 2023, 0.1% in 2024 and 0.4% in 2025, so margins have stayed slightly positive since 2023 but remain thin.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 527.9 billion in 2022, KRW 35.4 billion in 2023, KRW 305.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.44 billion in 2025, a four-year run of losses in which the loss widened in 2024 and narrowed sharply in 2025.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts in each year. The debt ratio was 90.2% in 2022 and 89.7% in 2023, rose to 103.6% in 2024 and eased to 89.9% in 2025. Quarterly, revenue was KRW 652.5 billion and operating profit KRW 19 billion in the third quarter of 2025.

Fourth-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 524.4 billion, down 19.6% from the prior quarter, and an operating loss of KRW 8.74 billion turned the quarter into a loss.

The first quarter of 2026 posted an operating loss of KRW 14.8 billion, and the second quarter returned to operating profit of KRW 918 million on revenue of KRW 591.1 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 5.51 billion, up 500.2% from the prior quarter but down 70.9% year on year, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.29 billion, a swing to profit. One-off items also mattered.

The second quarter of 2025 included KRW 6.6 billion of value-added tax refund income, which raised that base, and the first quarter of 2026 carried a one-off cost linked to changes in ordinary wage calculation, according to the company.

The nine-month operating loss of KRW 8.4 billion (preliminary, announced November 4, 2026) compares with operating profit of KRW 18.4 billion in the same period of 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Appliance retail is sensitive to the consumption cycle and to life events such as moving and home transactions.

The number of domestic movers fell 10.2% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, and the Bank of Korea's preliminary third-quarter GDP estimate showed private consumption turning negative for the first time in three years and nine months as spending on goods such as appliances weakened.

Large appliance replacement often accompanies moves, so the drop in moving demand weighs directly on large appliance sales. Seasonal appliance sales also slipped as a late heat wave shifted demand.

Still, the decline in kitchen and living appliance sales narrowed from 7.7% in the second quarter to 4.1% in the third quarter.

The company also reported that IT and mobile revenue grew 15.3% in the first quarter and 13.7% in the second quarter of 2026, a result it compared with domestic market growth in the communication devices and computers category. That comparison is based on the company's own disclosure.

Online channels grew 13.0% in the third quarter of 2026, partly offsetting offline weakness, while the service-led model competes for customer touchpoints with rental and care service providers.

06

Outlook

The company plans to grow Care services, private-label products, mobile and online into core categories to change its business structure and restore profitability. Its Care repair subscription, launched in early October 2026, passed 25,000 subscribers within a month.

The company says it will link this service to appliance sales to widen customer touchpoints and encourage repeat purchases. Expanding higher-profit Care services and private-label products is presented as the main lever for margin recovery.

Private-label cumulative revenue and unit sales in the first nine months of 2026 each exceeded 90% of full-year 2025 results. The structure in which operating margin depends on a recovery in large and kitchen appliance sales remains unchanged.

Because the 2025 base included one-off income such as a value-added tax refund, quarterly comparisons should be read with those items excluded. Excluding one-offs, the company said the second-quarter 2026 operating profit decline narrowed to KRW 2.9 billion.

The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, where the full-year operating margin and the large appliance decline will be the next checkpoints. Among quarterly results, the fourth quarter of 2026 is announced first.

Bull factors

Care service growth structure

Third-quarter 2026 total Care service revenue reached KRW 21.2 billion, up 62% year on year, and the nine-month figure reached KRW 58.6 billion, up 50% and already above full-year 2025 revenue. More than 1 million customers bought appliances together with services, and the linked purchase rate was 59%. The company argues that a loop in which service users buy appliances again can broaden revenue per customer.

Joint growth in mobile, private label and online

Mobile sales rose 28% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, extending a run of double-digit growth to three consecutive quarters. Private-label revenue rose 22.7% in the same quarter, and the online share of total sales rose to 14.1%. These higher-frequency categories are partly offsetting weakness in large appliances.

Narrowing losses

Operating profit turned positive in the second quarter of 2026 at KRW 918 million, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive in the third quarter at KRW 2.29 billion.

On an annual basis, operating profit rose to KRW 9.64 billion in 2025, and the controlling-shareholder net loss narrowed from KRW 305.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.44 billion in 2025. The shrinking loss points to a recovery trend.

Bear factors

Weak large and kitchen appliance sales

Third-quarter 2026 large appliance sales fell 3.8% and kitchen and living appliance sales fell 4.1%, and operating profit dropped 70.9% year on year. Gross profit fell 9.2% to KRW 175.9 billion. When higher-margin appliance sales shrink, revenue growth from lower-margin categories does not translate into profit.

Weaker consumer conditions

The number of domestic movers fell 10.2% in the third quarter of 2026, and private consumption turned negative for the first time in three years and nine months. Moving demand is closely tied to large appliance replacement, so external conditions weigh heavily. The company also cited lower moving demand and a late heat wave as reasons for the weak results.

Accumulated losses and earnings capacity

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders occurred in each year from 2022 to 2025, and the four most recent quarters total KRW 36.5 billion in losses. This is consistent with the shift from operating profit in January–September 2025 to an operating loss in the same period of 2026. If earnings capacity does not recover, the pace of rebuilding equity could remain limited.

Risk factors

Demand and seasonal risk

Appliance replacement demand swings sharply from quarter to quarter with moving activity, housing transactions and weather. In the third quarter of 2026, lower moving demand and a late heat wave delayed seasonal and large appliance sales. If consumer weakness persists, growth in Care services and mobile may not fully offset lower appliance sales.

Earnings mix risk

When higher-margin appliance sales shrink, operating margin stays thin even if revenue grows. The first quarter of 2026 included a one-off cost tied to ordinary wage changes, while the second quarter of 2025 had a one-off refund that raised the base. Quarterly results need to be checked with those items stripped out to see underlying earnings power.

Balance sheet risk

The debt ratio rose to 103.6% in 2024 and eased to 89.9% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 942.9 billion and total liabilities were KRW 847.9 billion. A longer period of losses could erode equity and reduce financial flexibility, so the direction of losses and the debt ratio need to be read together.

Overall view

LOTTE Himart's third quarter of 2026 showed mixed results for sales and profitability. Revenue of KRW 654.5 billion rose 0.3% year on year, reversing a decline that had lasted five quarters, with Care services, mobile and private-label products leading growth.

Operating profit, however, fell to KRW 5.51 billion, down 70.9% year on year, as large and kitchen appliance sales declined. Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders ran from 2022 through 2025, and the last four quarters total KRW 36.5 billion.

Operating profit rose to KRW 9.64 billion in 2025, but controlling-shareholder results have not yet reached a sustained recovery. Strengths include fast growth in service revenue, a 59% linked purchase rate and a swing to operating profit in the second and third quarters of 2026.

Weaknesses include weak consumer demand, fewer moving-related purchases and softer sales of higher-margin appliances. On valuation, the share price sits well below net asset value, while an earnings-based multiple cannot be calculated.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results and the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
