02

Business structure

LOTTE Himart started as an appliance retailer and is now shifting its model toward what it calls a lifetime care platform that covers management and repair after the sale. Its core pillar is Care service.

Care service revenue rose 62% year on year in 3Q 2026, and cumulative revenue for the first three quarters rose 50%, surpassing full-year 2025 Care revenue, according to the company. Customers who bought appliances together with services passed 1 million, and the linked purchase rate reached 59%.

The Care repair product launched in early October 2026 passed 25,000 subscribers within a month. IT and mobile sales rose 11% in 3Q 2026, and mobile sales grew 28%, extending double-digit growth for a third consecutive quarter in 2026. Private-label (PB) sales also rose 23% in the same quarter.

By contrast, large appliances and kitchen and living appliances declined on weaker moving demand and a late heatwave, although the decline narrowed from 7.7% in 2Q 2026 to 4.1% in 3Q 2026. Raising the share of Care and PB is a choice to lean on items with relatively higher profitability.

The company plans to widen customer touchpoints in categories with high purchase frequency, such as mobile and online. According to the shareholder table in an IBK Securities report from November 2025, Lotte Shopping and five related parties held 65.46%.

Competition comes from large retailers and online sales channels, and LOTTE Himart is emphasizing services and high-frequency categories to create more reasons for store visits.

04

Earnings analysis

The operating line went from an operating loss of KRW 52 billion in 2022 to an operating profit of KRW 8.22 billion in 2023, a turn to profitability, followed by KRW 1.72 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.64 billion in 2025.

Operating profit in 2025 rose 460.2% from the prior year, but sales of KRW 2.3 trillion fell 2.4%, and the operating margin was only 0.4%. The operating margin moved from -1.6% in 2022 to 0.3% in 2023, 0.1% in 2024 and 0.4% in 2025, never reaching 1%.

Sales fell from KRW 3.34 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.61 trillion in 2023, a 21.8% decline, and to KRW 2.36 trillion in 2024, a 9.7% decline, and they kept falling through 2025, a third straight year of decline.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed from KRW 527.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 35.4 billion in 2023, widened to KRW 305.4 billion in 2024, and narrowed to KRW 2.44 billion in 2025. The company has posted losses for four straight years from 2022 through 2025.

After the widened loss in 2024, total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 942.9 billion and total liabilities were KRW 847.9 billion, with a debt ratio of 89.9%. The 2024 debt ratio of 103.6% was the highest of the four years.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 56.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 262.7 billion in 2023, then recorded KRW 109.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 156.5 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 19 billion in 3Q 2025 gave way to an operating loss of KRW 8.74 billion in 4Q 2025, and after an operating loss of KRW 14.8 billion in 1Q 2026, 2Q 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 918 million.

Operating profit of KRW 5.51 billion in 3Q 2026 rose 500.2% from the prior quarter but fell 70.9% from the year-earlier quarter. Sales bottomed at KRW 524.4 billion in 4Q 2025 and KRW 496.9 billion in 1Q 2026 before recovering to KRW 591.1 billion in 2Q 2026 and KRW 654.5 billion in 3Q 2026.

There were one-off items as well: 2Q 2025 included a KRW 6.6 billion VAT refund, and 1Q 2026 carried a one-time cost tied to a change in regular wage calculations, according to the company.

Cumulative operating loss for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 8.4 billion, compared with operating profit of KRW 18.4 billion a year earlier, per a November 4, 2026 report on preliminary results.

Full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 9.64 billion came close to the KRW 10 billion target the company set in February 2025. Third-quarter profit was affected by lower moving demand and a late heatwave.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic appliance demand remained weak in 2026.

According to Statistics Korea, domestic movers in 3Q 2026 fell 10.2% year on year, and the Bank of Korea's preliminary 3Q GDP estimate showed private consumption turning negative for the first time in three years and nine months, driven by weaker goods spending such as appliances.

Moving demand supports large appliance sales, and as it declined, large appliance sales took a direct hit. In 2Q 2026, LOTTE Himart's total sales growth was -6.2% in April and -0.8% in May before turning to +8.3% in June.

Over the same period, retail sales of communication equipment and computers were down 2.3% in 1Q and up only 6.8% in 2Q, while LOTTE Himart's IT and mobile sales rose 15.3% and 13.7%, outpacing the market, according to the company.

In July 2025, NH Investment & Securities judged that LOTTE Himart's market share was rising even as the domestic appliance market continued to shrink sharply. On the cycle, the timing of a demand recovery remains unclear.

The shift of purchases toward online channels has been cited as a factor reducing store visit frequency at offline appliance retailers. In that competition, LOTTE Himart is trying to differentiate by pairing services and mobile to create reasons to visit stores.

06

Outlook

The company has indicated it will raise the revenue share of Care service and private-label products and drive additional appliance purchases from service users. It also plans to widen customer touchpoints in high-frequency categories such as mobile and online.

At its 2Q 2026 results, the company said it would pair high-margin large and kitchen and living appliance purchases with its services to lift second-half sales and operating profit together.

Reports described the narrowing of the decline in kitchen and living appliances from 7.7% in 2Q 2026 to 4.1% in 3Q 2026 as an early sign of margin improvement. Monthly momentum also turned, with June 2026 total sales up 8.3%.

The Care repair product passed 25,000 subscribers within a month of launch, a fast early response for a new service. However, cumulative sales for the first three quarters of 2026 fell 1.9%, and operating results turned to a loss, so the key question is how much second-half improvement can offset the cumulative loss.

The next checkpoints are the direction of operating profit in preliminary 4Q 2026 results and the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report. 1Q 2027 results will be the first quarter to compare against the KRW 14.8 billion operating loss of 1Q 2026.

Bull factors

Growth in Care service and mobile

Care service revenue rose 62% in 3Q 2026, and cumulative revenue for the first three quarters surpassed full-year 2025 Care revenue. The 59% linked purchase rate and more than 1 million customers suggest contact with customers continues after the appliance sale.

Mobile sales growth of 28% and PB sales growth of 23% show that expansion in high-frequency categories is reaching revenue.

Continuity of the sales rebound

Net sales in 3Q 2026 rose 0.3% year on year to KRW 654.5 billion, ending five quarters of decline. Sales have grown for two consecutive quarters since 2Q 2026, continuing the trend from the 1Q 2026 trough. Total sales growth turning to +8.3% in June 2026 also points to improvement within the quarter.

Early quarterly signs of operating improvement

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 918 million in 2Q 2026 and rose to KRW 5.51 billion in 3Q 2026, far above the prior quarter. The decline in kitchen and living appliance sales narrowed from 7.7% to 4.1% between the two quarters. The shift from a quarterly loss to profit may be read as an early sign of earnings recovery.

Bear factors

Cumulative operating loss

The cumulative operating loss of KRW 8.4 billion for the first three quarters of 2026 reflects a swing from operating profit of KRW 18.4 billion in the same period a year earlier. Operating profit in 3Q 2026 fell 70.9% year on year to KRW 5.51 billion. The KRW 14.8 billion operating loss in 1Q 2026 was the main quarter pulling the cumulative result down.

Declines in large and kitchen and living appliances

Large appliances and kitchen and living appliances saw lower 3Q sales on weaker moving demand and a late heatwave. With the appliance downturn prolonged, the pace of margin improvement could stay limited if high-margin categories do not recover. Given a 2025 operating margin of only 0.4%, the earnings base remains thin.

Financial capacity and accumulated losses

The debt ratio fell from 103.6% in 2024 to 89.9% in 2025, but total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 847.9 billion. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 36.5 billion, so equity is not being built through earnings.

Operating cash flow of KRW 156.5 billion in 2025 shows cash generation, separate from earnings-based capital building.

Risk factors

Policy and demand support

The efficiency-appliance purchase rebate program that NH Investment & Securities pointed to in July 2025 returns 10% of the purchase cost for top-efficiency-grade appliances, a support for large appliance demand. Changes to the program's duration or budget could affect large appliance sales directly. Whether policy support is extended will be a key variable for appliance demand going forward.

Accounting and assets

In July 2025, NH Investment & Securities said that weak absolute profitability means the possibility of goodwill impairment losses has to be considered. This has not yet occurred. If an impairment is recognized, net income would deteriorate further and the impact would flow directly into total equity.

Execution of new services

The Care repair product passed 25,000 subscribers within a month of its early October 2026 launch. Subscriber retention and conversion into additional appliance purchases remain to be confirmed in future results.

Even if Care expansion lifts revenue, it may not translate into operating profit improvement depending on the cost structure of running the service.

Overall view

LOTTE Himart posted net sales of KRW 654.5 billion in 3Q 2026, extending a trend of two consecutive quarters of growth. Care service revenue up 62%, a 59% linked purchase rate, and growth in mobile and PB products show the business-model shift is working on the sales side.

On profitability, operating profit fell from KRW 9.64 billion in 2025 (0.4% margin) to KRW 5.51 billion in 3Q 2026, and the cumulative result for the first three quarters of 2026 turned to an operating loss of KRW 8.4 billion.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was negative for four straight years from 2022 through 2025 and narrowed to KRW 2.44 billion in 2025, but the last four quarters total a net loss of KRW 36.5 billion.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 89.9%, down from 103.6% in 2024, with total equity of KRW 942.9 billion. On valuation, the PER cannot be calculated, and the share price sits low relative to net assets.

The central question is whether the sales rebound carries through to earnings, and the next checkpoints are the preliminary 4Q 2026 results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
