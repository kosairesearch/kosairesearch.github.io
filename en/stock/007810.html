Memory modules grow while quarterly operating profit slips for three straight quarters

02

Business structure

Founded in 1972, Korea Circuit produces and sells circuit boards, including semiconductor substrates. Its main businesses are HDI boards for smartphones and package substrates for memory modules, supplied to leading global manufacturers.

By segment in 2025, PCB manufacturing accounted for 56.0%, FPCB manufacturing for 34.8%, the semiconductor segment for 8.4%, and rental and other for 0.8%.

Its subsidiaries include Interflex, an FPCB specialist, and Signetics, a semiconductor packaging company, and in February 2026 it absorbed the PCB manufacturing division of Teraník.

Within semiconductor PCB sales, the FC family (FC BGA, FC CSP, FC BOC) rose from 68% in 2024 to 74% in 2025, and Daishin Securities estimated in February 2026 that it would reach 80% in 2026. Major customers include Samsung Electronics and SK hynix.

In 2022 Broadcom invested in the company, which built a dedicated production line for FC-BGA for communication components. Demand for server memory modules is broadening as AI and general servers switch to DDR5 RDIMM.

SOCAMM2, a low-power server memory module standard, is cited as being adopted for NVIDIA's next-generation AI platform. Compared with domestic peers, the company has a relatively high share of legacy sales for smartphones and displays, which makes it more exposed to slowing demand in those markets.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 1.6 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.33 trillion in 2023, a 16.6% decline that marked the trough. It then rose 5.6% to KRW 1.41 trillion in 2024 and 7.3% to KRW 1.51 trillion in 2025, two consecutive years of growth, with 2022 the largest year in the period.

Operating profit went from KRW 99.2 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 32.1 billion in 2023, and the loss widened to KRW 33.2 billion in 2024.

In 2025 operating profit was KRW 53.8 billion, a return to profit, and the operating margin moved from 6.2% in 2022 to -2.4% in 2023 and -2.4% in 2024 before reaching 3.6% in 2025.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 125.9 billion in 2024, the largest loss in the period, and the company returned to net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 47.3 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 53.8 billion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder number. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 152.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 39.1 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q4 2025 was the peak, with revenue of KRW 448.1 billion (up 19.3% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 36.1 billion (up 171.9%). Operating profit then fell to KRW 26.4 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 19.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 14.9 billion in Q3.

Analysts attributed about KRW 1 billion of inventory disposal costs and about KRW 6 billion of one-time labor costs to Q4 2025. Q3 2026 net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 9.59 billion, down 17.6% year on year.

05

Industry analysis

On the demand side, analysts say a supply shortage of FC-BGA across the industry is lifting utilization and unit prices. Daishin Securities' November 3, 2026 report expected the company's 2027 semiconductor package revenue to rise 26% year on year to KRW 506.4 billion.

For memory modules, analysts note that higher server memory specifications are enlarging substrate area and layer counts, so both volume and price are rising. For displays, Apple's shift to OLED is expected to lift HDI sales for display applications.

On the cycle, Meritz Securities said in a May 2026 report that memory-module capacity expansion and higher FC-BGA utilization would serve as additional growth drivers. In a September 2026 report, however, the same brokerage said the effect of memory-module expansion was arriving later than expected.

Against domestic peers, the company has a higher share of legacy smartphone and display sales and is therefore more exposed to slowing demand in those end markets.

06

Outlook

The near-term path depends on how quickly SOCAMM mass production scales up. In a May 2026 report, Meritz Securities expected sample revenue from a domestic customer around Q3 2026, with full mass production starting in Q4.

In an October 2026 report, the same brokerage said SOCAMM mass-production sales began in July 2026 and that Q4 2026 supply volume would rise 50% from Q3.

In Meritz's May 2026 estimates, 2026 consolidated revenue was KRW 1.79 trillion and operating profit was KRW 138.5 billion, with 2027 operating profit projected at KRW 215.3 billion.

Because reported operating profit fell in each of the first three quarters of 2026, the full-year estimates depend on a sharp recovery in Q4. Daishin Securities estimated Q4 2026 operating profit at KRW 31.3 billion in its November 3, 2026 report, and expected the decline seen from Q1 to Q3 2026 to end.

SOCAMM2 revenue is estimated to grow from about KRW 26 billion in 2026 to more than KRW 90 billion in 2027. On the other side, delayed memory-module capacity expansion and display shipment delays could limit the Q4 recovery.

08

Bull factors

SOCAMM2 mass production is expanding memory-module revenue

Meritz Securities' October 2026 report said SOCAMM mass-production sales began in July 2026. The same report projected a 50% increase in Q4 supply volume versus Q3. Memory-module demand is linked to replacement demand for server DDR5 RDIMM. If this continues, a higher memory-module share could shift the earnings mix.

Rising FC-family share and tighter FC-BGA supply

Daishin Securities estimated that the FC family's share of semiconductor PCB sales rose from 68% in 2024 to 74% in 2025 and would reach 80% in 2026. Analysts also point to an industry-wide FC-BGA supply shortage lifting utilization and unit prices. A larger high-value share could reduce reliance on legacy products and change the profitability base.

Earnings have been on a recovery path since 2025

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 33.2 billion in 2024 to KRW 53.8 billion in 2025. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 14.9 billion rose 12.2% year on year, and revenue of KRW 456.1 billion rose 21.4%. Revenue growth alongside the earnings recovery is the basis for the bullish case.

09

Bear factors

Quarterly operating profit down for three straight quarters

Operating profit peaked at KRW 36.1 billion in Q4 2025 and then fell to KRW 26.4 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 19.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 14.9 billion in Q3. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 17.6% year on year to KRW 9.59 billion in Q3 2026. Revenue rose in Q3 but profit did not follow. How quickly the decline stops is central to the bearish case.

High share of legacy smartphone and display sales

In a September 2026 report, Meritz Securities said the company's higher share of legacy smartphone and display sales leaves it exposed to slowing demand, unlike some domestic peers. Shipment delays for display products have also increased inventory burden. Without improvement here, growth in high-value products may not fully reach total earnings.

Capacity gains and materials supply are later than expected

Meritz Securities said in its September 2026 report that the effect of memory-module capacity expansion is arriving later than expected. The same report said some raw-materials supply issues delayed shipments and that sales recovery from a major package customer was slower. Continued delays could push back the expected Q4 2026 recovery.

10

Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

Major customers include Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, and FC-BGA for communication parts produced on a dedicated Broadcom line is a sizable part of the business. A change in orders or product transition timing at a single customer could affect total revenue. SOCAMM2 is similarly dependent on mass-production schedules at a small number of customers.

Inventory and one-time costs

Q4 2025 costs included inventory disposal and one-time labor costs. An inventory valuation provision tied to delayed display shipments was also expected to be reflected in Q3 2026 results. If one-time costs recur, the direction of quarterly profit becomes harder to read.

Financial burden and cash generation

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 602 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 84.3% in 2024 to 76.1% in 2025. Lower cash generation during a period of ongoing investment limits financial flexibility.

What to watch next

  1. Q4 2026

    Whether full mass production of SOCAMM products for a domestic customer begins as planned. The timing will determine how fast memory-module revenue expands.

  2. Around March 2027

    The point at which the 2026 annual report and Q4 results are disclosed. It is where the reported figure can be compared with Daishin Securities' estimate of KRW 31.3 billion for Q4 2026 operating profit.

  3. Around May 2027

    The Q1 2027 results release. It is where to check whether the estimated SOCAMM2 revenue path toward more than KRW 90 billion in 2027 and FC-BGA utilization show up in quarterly results.

12

Overall view

Korea Circuit moved from an operating loss in 2024 to KRW 53.8 billion of operating profit in 2025 and is shifting its mix toward SOCAMM2 and FC-family substrates. Revenue and operating profit in Q3 2026 rose year on year, but quarterly operating profit has fallen for three straight quarters since its Q4 2025 peak.

The bullish points are the start of SOCAMM mass-production sales and improving FC-BGA supply, while the bearish points are the legacy product mix, delayed capacity expansion and one-time inventory costs. Broker estimates assume a Q4 2026 operating profit recovery, but these are forecasts, not reported results.

The price-to-book multiple sits well above the book value attributable to controlling shareholders. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 SOCAMM mass-production timing and the Q4 2026 operating profit in the 2026 annual report.
