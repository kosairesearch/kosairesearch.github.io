02

Business structure

Founded in 1998 and listed on KOSDAQ, MDS Tech specializes in embedded software and AI infrastructure solutions.

As of the third quarter of 2025, its revenue mix by segment was embedded system software at 33.7%, embedded software development solutions at 21.1%, industrial automation solutions at 17.5%, and office automation and other at 27.7%.

Since signing an official NVIDIA distribution agreement in 2014, the company has supplied Jetson series products along with DGX and GPU servers in the domestic market.

It also holds the exclusive distribution rights outside Hyundai Motor Group for 'Mobilgene,' the group's proprietary vehicle OS platform developed by Hyundai AutoEver, which is applied across all Hyundai, Kia, and Genesis models.

It supplies the TRACE32 debugging solution needed for robotics and vehicle control development in cooperation with Hyundai AutoEver.

Wholly-owned subsidiary MDS Intelligence's flagship product is RapidAUTO Pro, an automotive software development automation solution built around AUTOSAR, ISO 26262, and A-SPICE standards.

The company has supplied TRACE32 to AI semiconductor startup Primemass since 2023 and recently expanded the relationship through a fund investment, while in July 2026 it formed a strategic partnership with China's SZ Sensing to expand its AI vision solutions business.

Its key customer base includes Hyundai Motor Group and other domestic and global automakers and Tier-1 suppliers, as well as government and corporate customers building AI infrastructure and data centers.

04

Earnings analysis

2025 consolidated revenue rose to KRW 187.7 billion from KRW 165.6 billion in 2024, but operating profit edged down slightly to KRW 5.8 billion from KRW 5.9 billion, pulling the operating margin down from 3.6% to 3.1%. Net income attributable to owners, however, increased to KRW 8.2 billion from KRW 6.9 billion.

In 2023, the company posted operating profit of KRW 8.7 billion and a 5.6% operating margin yet owners' net income of only KRW 1.4 billion, and in 2022 operating profit of KRW 8.2 billion coincided with a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 6.8 billion, reflecting a recurring gap between operating and bottom-line results.

Quarterly volatility has also been pronounced: second-quarter 2025 revenue of KRW 37.9 billion came with a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 3.6 billion, third-quarter revenue of KRW 47.7 billion swung to net income of KRW 7.4 billion, and fourth-quarter revenue of KRW 51.5 billion saw operating profit shrink to roughly KRW 40 million.

Into 2026, the company posted operating profit in the KRW 4-billion-plus range for two consecutive quarters—KRW 4.5 billion on KRW 50.9 billion revenue in the first quarter and KRW 4.2 billion on KRW 57.6 billion revenue in the second—suggesting improved stability.

As a result, first-half 2026 consolidated revenue rose 29.3% year-on-year to KRW 108.5 billion and operating profit reached KRW 8.7 billion, with the company stating that both operating profit and net income for the half already exceeded full-year 2025 figures.

On the cash flow front, 2025 operating cash flow improved sharply to KRW 16.1 billion from KRW 2.9 billion in 2024, and the debt ratio fell from 59.8% in 2024 to 48.0% in 2025.

Meanwhile, non-controlling interests shrank sharply from roughly KRW 22.0 billion at the end of 2024 to KRW 1.9 billion at the end of 2025, suggesting a change in the scope of consolidation or ownership structure.

05

Industry analysis

The embedded software market is projected to grow to USD 30.2 billion by 2030, driven by IoT device proliferation, AI convergence, and rising automotive ADAS demand, though profitability is described as constrained by cost burdens despite revenue growth.

In the automotive sector, the shift toward software-defined vehicles is accelerating demand for development automation solutions that comply with AUTOSAR, ISO 26262, and A-SPICE standards.

On the AI infrastructure front, roughly 250,000 NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs are slated for supply to the Korean government, Hyundai Motor, Samsung Electronics, Naver, and SK Group under the government's sovereign AI strategy, a project described as central to both the sovereign AI initiative and private-sector AI factory buildout.

Amid this backdrop, MDS Tech's NVIDIA-related revenue is reported to have grown more than 300% compared with 2020, reflecting a strong partnership.

On the competitive front, a brokerage research note characterized MDS Tech as the sole domestic company able to offer a turnkey physical AI solution combining Jetson, Mobilgene, and TRACE32.

That said, MDS Tech's specific role within Hyundai Motor Group's robotics business has not been finalized, with a company official stating that while cooperation possibilities remain open depending on how the Hyundai-NVIDIA partnership evolves, there is currently no concrete plan in place.

06

Outlook

Management has said it will strengthen competitiveness in its core embedded solutions business amid an expected upturn in the semiconductor and automotive end-markets, while actively seeking new businesses to drive further earnings momentum.

In July 2026, the company formed a strategic partnership with China's SZ Sensing, an NVIDIA Robot & Vision elite partner, to supply automotive- and industrial-grade AI cameras domestically and expand its AI vision solutions business in conjunction with its Jetson platform operations.

MDS Intelligence has stated it plans to gradually expand the application scope of its RapidAUTO Pro-based automation solutions together with an in-house startup team at Hyundai Motor's future strategy division.

In late January 2026, Aris analyst Lee Jae-mo forecast MDS Tech's full-year 2026 revenue at KRW 195.0 billion (up 9% year-on-year) and operating profit at KRW 10.5 billion (up 10% year-on-year); actual first-half 2026 results of KRW 108.5 billion in revenue and KRW 8.7 billion in operating profit are tracking at a pace approaching that full-year estimate.

At the same time, Aris estimated MDS Intelligence's 2025 revenue at roughly KRW 20.0 billion and operating profit at roughly KRW 2.0 billion. These figures, however, are brokerage estimates from early 2026 and may diverge from subsequently disclosed quarterly and half-year results.

The third-quarter 2026 report is legally required to be filed by November 16, 2026, which should show whether the margin improvement seen in the first half continued into the third quarter.

08

Bull factors

Exclusive Position in AI Infrastructure Distribution

MDS Tech has served as the domestic distributor of NVIDIA Jetson since 2014, with related revenue reported to have grown more than 300% versus 2020.

Combined with its exclusive distribution of Hyundai Motor Group's Mobilgene vehicle OS and supply of the TRACE32 debugging solution, brokerage research has described it as the sole domestic company offering a turnkey physical AI solution.

The government's large-scale Blackwell GPU supply plan under its sovereign AI strategy also intersects with this distribution position.

Simultaneous Revenue and Profitability Improvement in H1 2026

First-half 2026 consolidated revenue rose 29.3% year-on-year to KRW 108.5 billion, a record for a half-year period. Operating profit reached KRW 8.7 billion with an operating margin of 8.0%, up 4.2 percentage points year-on-year according to the company.

Operating profit stayed in the KRW 4-billion-plus range for two consecutive quarters, suggesting improved earnings stability versus prior periods.

Growth Diversification Through New Businesses

Subsidiary MDS Intelligence launched RapidAUTO Pro AI, incorporating generative AI features, in January 2026, strengthening its push into the automotive software development automation market.

The company has also made a fund investment in AI semiconductor startup Primemass, a relationship dating to 2023, expanding its footprint in the CXL market. In July 2026, it partnered with China's SZ Sensing to expand into AI vision solutions.

09

Bear factors

Recurring Gap Between Operating Profit and Net Income

In 2022, operating profit of KRW 8.2 billion coincided with a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 6.8 billion, and in 2023 a 5.6% operating margin translated into owners' net income of only KRW 1.4 billion.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, operating profit shrank to roughly KRW 40 million despite revenue of KRW 51.5 billion, underscoring large swings in quarterly results. This pattern suggests quarterly earnings could remain volatile going forward depending on one-off items.

Slow Conversion of Revenue Growth Into Profit

Some financial data providers have noted, in neutral terms, that the company's revenue growth has not fully translated into improved net income and operating margins. The embedded system software solutions segment has also been described as having limited profitability due to cost burdens despite revenue growth. The decline in operating margin from 3.6% in 2024 to 3.1% in 2025 aligns with this concern.

Reliance on a Limited Set of Partners and Customers

A significant portion of revenue stems from the NVIDIA distribution agreement and the Mobilgene/TRACE32 supply relationships with Hyundai Motor Group. MDS Tech's specific role in Hyundai Motor Group's robotics business remains undetermined, with the company stating there is currently no concrete plan in place. Changes in contract terms or strategy at these specific vendors or customers could directly affect results.

Risk factors

Supply Chain and Partnership Concentration Risk

Conditions in the NVIDIA GPU supply chain, changes to distribution contract terms, or delays in government sovereign AI budget execution could directly affect related revenue. The Mobilgene and TRACE32 supply relationships with Hyundai Motor Group are also exposed to shifts in that customer's strategy.

Commercial results from newer diversification partnerships such as SZ Sensing and Primemass remain to be validated.

Financial Structure and Consolidation Scope Risk

The debt ratio improved from 59.8% in 2024 to 48.0% in 2025, though it has fluctuated over past years. Non-controlling interests fell sharply from roughly KRW 22.0 billion at the end of 2024 to KRW 1.9 billion at the end of 2025, suggesting a change in consolidation scope or ownership structure. The background behind this change warrants further confirmation through future disclosures.

Small-Cap Volatility and Liquidity Risk

As a small-cap stock with a market capitalization of roughly KRW 0.1 trillion, the shares have reportedly moved sharply over short periods in response to physical AI and NVIDIA-related thematic news. If thematic flows reverse, valuation metrics could swing significantly as well. Given the lower liquidity typical of small-cap stocks, price impact from trading can be relatively large.

12

Overall view

MDS Tech is an embedded software company that has leveraged its distribution positions in NVIDIA Jetson and Hyundai Motor's Mobilgene platform to expand into physical AI and software-defined vehicle-related businesses.

In contrast to 2025, when revenue grew but operating margin slipped slightly, first-half 2026 saw both revenue and operating margin improve simultaneously, producing the company's best-ever half-year results.

However, as seen in 2022-2023, the gap between operating profit and net income and quarterly earnings volatility are features that warrant caution when interpreting this company's results.

The launch of AI-enabled products by subsidiary MDS Intelligence and new partnerships with SZ Sensing and Primemass can be viewed as factors diversifying growth drivers, though their commercial impact remains to be validated.

Given its small-cap characteristics, the stock appears to have been influenced by thematic flows, and the resulting volatility in valuation metrics should also be considered.

Before making investment decisions, it would be prudent to continue monitoring the third-quarter 2026 earnings disclosure and whether new partnerships contribute meaningfully to revenue.
