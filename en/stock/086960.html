Embedded Leader Expands via NVIDIA AI Infrastructure

MDS Tech has layered NVIDIA AI infrastructure and physical AI business onto its core embedded software operations, driving three straight years of revenue growth through 2025, even as its operating margin has trended lower.

02

Business structure

Founded in 1994, MDS Tech supplies total embedded-system solutions, providing development tools, consulting and training, along with OS and application software that customers need to build embedded software or systems.

The current revenue mix is composed of embedded system software, including OS bundles, middleware and application software, at 46.3%, embedded software development solutions at 19.9%, industrial automation solutions at 18.1%, and other businesses at 15.7%.

The embedded system software segment is expanding through self-developed solutions such as OS bundles, application software and digital-twin visualization tools.

Core customers span automotive, defense/aerospace and government institutions, and in the automotive sector the company is the domestic distributor of Mobilgene, Hyundai Motor Group's proprietary OS brand developed by Hyundai Autoever.

After forming an official partnership with NVIDIA in 2014, the company entered the embedded AI platform business in earnest and has since broadened into distributing DGX and GPU servers for AI infrastructure.

Key subsidiaries include MDS Intelligence (formerly Hancom Intelligence), while its former stake in Alphanox was divested and removed from the consolidation scope.

The company is extending its portfolio into physical AI areas such as humanoid robots and autonomous driving through talent-development cooperation with the Seoul National University Robotics Institute, an exclusive distribution agreement for AI vision cameras with SZ Sensing Tech, and a technology partnership with Uniqconn on contactless high-speed wireless data transmission. MDS Tech is listed on KOSDAQ, and as of May 2026 its controlling shareholder held a 25.28% stake.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 153.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 155.3 billion in 2023 (up 1.2%), KRW 165.6 billion in 2024 (up 6.6%), and KRW 187.7 billion in 2025 (up 13.4%), marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year and 2022 the smallest in the 2022-2025 span.

Operating profit, by contrast, peaked in 2023 at KRW 8.72 billion after rising from KRW 8.15 billion in 2022 (up 6.9%), then fell to KRW 5.94 billion in 2024 (down 31.8%) and KRW 5.81 billion in 2025 (down 2.2%), a two-year consecutive decline from 2024 through 2025.

The operating margin also slipped from 5.6% in 2023 to 3.6% in 2024 and 3.1% in 2025, meaning profitability failed to keep pace with revenue growth, a pattern consistent with a rising share of relatively lower-margin AI server and GPU distribution revenue.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss of KRW 6.77 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 1.42 billion in 2023, then rose for two consecutive years to KRW 6.87 billion in 2024 (up 384.2%) and KRW 8.23 billion in 2025 (up 19.8%).

On a quarterly basis, operating profit plunged to just KRW 40.13 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 before rebounding sharply to KRW 4.53 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then came in at KRW 4.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (down 7.2% quarter on quarter, up 72.4% year on year), reflecting notable volatility.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 57.6 billion, up 13.3% quarter on quarter and 52.1% year on year, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 6.75 billion, up 207.4% quarter on quarter and swinging back to profit year on year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the trailing four quarters (Q3 2025 through Q2 2026) totaled KRW 19.7 billion. Operating cash flow improved from -KRW 4.11 billion in 2022 to KRW 26.3 billion in 2023, before fluctuating to KRW 2.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 16.1 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The global physical AI market is projected to grow roughly 11.8-fold, from USD 81.6 billion in 2025 to USD 960.4 billion in 2033, and MDS Tech has built a structure supplying both data-center DGX/GPU servers for training and Jetson-based edge platforms for inference.

Within the NVIDIA partner ecosystem, where many firms specialize in either servers/GPUs or vision AI, one independent research house highlighted MDS Tech's hardware portfolio spanning training through edge boards, modules and peripherals as a point of differentiation.

In Korea, expanding public and private GPU investment tied to the government's 'sovereign AI' policy direction has been cited as a favorable policy backdrop for AI infrastructure distributors.

In the automotive electronics segment, demand for embedded development solutions continues amid the shift toward software-defined vehicles, while defense and aerospace markets maintain steady demand for high-reliability embedded systems.

That said, the traditional embedded development tool and OS distribution market appears to have matured, with higher growth concentrated in the newer AI infrastructure and physical AI businesses.

Competitively, a growing number of domestic AI infrastructure distributors and system integrators means the durability of the NVIDIA partnership and the breadth of integrated service capability are cited as key differentiators.

06

Outlook

First-half 2026 consolidated revenue, combining KRW 50.9 billion in the first quarter and KRW 57.6 billion in the second quarter, totaled KRW 108.5 billion, and media reports described this as the first time first-half revenue surpassed KRW 100 billion (media report, August 2026).

The company said it was officially invited to NVIDIA's Jetson Partner Day and plans to strengthen its systems-consulting capability in response to the rollout of the next-generation AI supercomputer platform Vera Rubin (March 2026).

New partnerships, including an exclusive AI vision camera distribution agreement with SZ Sensing Tech (July 2026) and a physical AI MOU with Uniqconn (August 2026), aim to diversify revenue sources toward humanoid robotics and autonomous driving.

The company also said it joined a 'personal AI agent' technology-development project led by the Ministry of Science and ICT and ETRI (an AI-glasses consortium), taking charge of wearable AI computing device development (September 2026).

The potential to cross-sell AI solutions to its roughly 1,500 existing embedded customers has been cited as a growth lever.

However, most of these new partnerships remain at the technology-verification or MOU stage, so the timing and scale of actual revenue recognition will need to be confirmed through future disclosures.

08

Bull factors

Expanding Position Within the NVIDIA Ecosystem

Thirteen years of experience as an NVIDIA Jetson distributor, now supplemented by DGX and GPU server supply, has given the company a hardware portfolio spanning AI training through robotics and autonomous-driving inference.

One independent research house noted this breadth differentiates the company from partners specialized in narrower segments. Continued public and private GPU investment under Korea's sovereign AI policy direction could further highlight its role as a one-stop distribution, design and technical-support provider.

Diversification into New Physical AI Businesses

A series of physical AI partnerships have followed one another, including an exclusive AI vision camera distribution deal with SZ Sensing Tech, a contactless wireless transmission technology collaboration with Uniqconn, and participation in an ETRI-led AI glasses consortium.

These efforts represent attempts to add humanoid robotics, autonomous driving and wearable devices as new revenue sources alongside legacy embedded development solutions. The potential to cross-sell to roughly 1,500 existing embedded customers has also been cited.

Revenue Growth and Earnings Recovery

Revenue has grown for three consecutive years from 2022 through 2025, and continued at a double-digit year-on-year growth rate in the first half of 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders has risen for two consecutive years since turning profitable in 2023, and swung back to profit year on year in the second quarter of 2026. Operating cash flow has also remained positive since 2023.

09

Bear factors

Deteriorating Profitability Trend

Operating profit peaked in 2023 and has declined for two consecutive years through 2025, with the operating margin falling from 5.6% to 3.1%. There is a possibility that the AI server and GPU distribution business driving revenue growth carries a lower margin structure than legacy embedded solutions.

If top-line expansion is not accompanied by profitability improvement, questions about underlying earnings strength could persist.

Quarterly Earnings Volatility

Quarterly results show wide swings, with operating profit plunging to just KRW 40.13 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 before rebounding sharply to KRW 4.53 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders also swung from a loss of KRW 3.59 billion in the second quarter of 2025 to a profit of KRW 7.38 billion in the third quarter.

Such volatility may reflect one-off factors or the timing of large-project revenue recognition in specific quarters, warranting caution in interpreting any single quarter.

Many New Businesses Still in Early Stages

Cooperation with SZ Sensing Tech and Uniqconn, as well as participation in the ETRI consortium, remain mostly at the memorandum-of-understanding stage signed in the second half of 2026, with no specific revenue contribution scale or timing yet disclosed.

Even if the physical AI market shows strong long-term growth prospects, there can be a lag before actual orders and revenue recognition materialize. It cannot be ruled out that new-business investment may fail to offset the profitability slowdown in the legacy embedded business.

10

Risk factors

Business Structure Risk

As the share of AI server and GPU distribution grows, dependence on a single supplier, NVIDIA, could increase. Changes in supply contract terms or shifts in global semiconductor supply-demand conditions could affect results.

Because segment revenue mix can shift quarter to quarter, ongoing monitoring is needed to ensure dependence on any single business line does not become excessive.

Profitability Risk

Given that the operating margin has declined for two consecutive years since 2023, it will be important to monitor whether margins can rebound even if revenue growth continues. If the expanding share of hardware distribution is a structural factor compressing margins, any margin recovery could take time.

New Business Execution Risk

Most new partnerships in physical AI and AI glasses remain at the MOU or agreement stage, with the possibility that they may not progress to technology validation or commercialization.

For the government-led project (the ETRI consortium), the pace of progress could vary with changes in budget allocation or policy scheduling.

Overall view

MDS Tech has built on its core embedded software business by adding NVIDIA AI infrastructure and physical AI operations, producing three consecutive years of revenue growth from 2022 through 2025.

At the same time, the operating margin has declined for two consecutive years since 2023, meaning top-line growth and profitability improvement have not moved together.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders has risen for two consecutive years since turning profitable in 2023 and swung back to profit year on year again in the second quarter of 2026, suggesting the earnings-recovery trend is continuing.

New partnerships with SZ Sensing Tech, Uniqconn and the ETRI consortium remain mostly at an early stage, and their actual revenue contribution in scale and timing will need to be confirmed through future disclosures. The wide swings in quarterly results are another point warranting careful interpretation.

The next regular quarterly report and the extent to which these new partnerships take concrete shape are likely to be key indicators for gauging the company's future earnings trajectory.
