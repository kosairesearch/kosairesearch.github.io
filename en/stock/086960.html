02

Business structure

MDS Tech is an AI and embedded solutions company founded in 1998 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2006.

It supplies embedded system development tools, operating systems and industrial hardware used in automotive, semiconductor equipment and robotics, and the company says it has accumulated more than 30 years of embedded technology.

Based on third-quarter 2026 cumulative sales, embedded system software is the largest segment at 46.0%, followed by embedded software development solutions at 17.3%, industrial automation at 17.9% and other businesses at 18.8%.

The other category includes overseas subsidiary revenue that was reclassified as operating-system revenue during the period. Within embedded system software, the company is expanding self-developed products such as OS bundles, application software and digital-twin visualization solutions.

Recent growth has centered on NVIDIA product lines, with sales of Jetson and DGX products rising as the physical AI market expands. The company names Jetson-based industrial AI and AI infrastructure supply as its two main pillars and targets special markets including defense, aviation and public institutions.

Its customers are reported to be centered on large corporations and public institutions. In October 2026 it signed a contract to supply a Jetson Full Package worth about KRW 4 billion to a humanoid home robot project run by a Korean company referred to as Company A. Self-developed solutions and embedded engineering capability are cited as its main points of differentiation.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 153.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 155.3 billion in 2023 (up 1.2% year on year), KRW 165.6 billion in 2024 (up 6.6%) and KRW 187.7 billion in 2025 (up 13.4%). Sales increased three years in a row from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 span.

Operating profit peaked in 2023 at KRW 8.72 billion (up 6.9%) before falling to KRW 5.94 billion in 2024 (down 31.8%) and KRW 5.81 billion in 2025 (down 2.2%), a decline in two consecutive years.

Operating margin moved from 5.3% in 2022 to 5.6% in 2023, then to 3.6% in 2024 and 3.1% in 2025, so sales growth has not carried over to margins.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss of KRW 6.77 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 1.42 billion in 2023, the turnaround to profit, then rose to KRW 6.87 billion in 2024 (up 384.2%) and KRW 8.23 billion in 2025 (up 19.8%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 6.12 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.19 billion in 2025, below the attributable figure, which reads as non-controlling interests recording losses.

Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 4.11 billion in 2022 to KRW 26.3 billion in 2023, KRW 2.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 16.1 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit fell to KRW 40.13 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, down 98.3% from the prior quarter, then recovered to KRW 4.53 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 11,181.2%), KRW 4.2 billion in the second quarter (down 7.2%) and KRW 5.37 billion in the third quarter (up 28.0% quarter on quarter and 123.4% year on year).

The company attributed the first-quarter 2026 improvement to higher sales of semiconductor and automotive embedded solutions, the effect of the loss-making subsidiary sold in 2025, and better results at a subsidiary.

In the same quarter, standalone net profit fell about 30% year on year because of a derivative valuation loss tied to conversion requests on convertible bonds.

Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 60.3 billion rose 26.3% year on year, but net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.37 billion fell 54.4%, so the rise in operating profit did not carry through to net profit.

05

Industry analysis

The largest demand driver in the end market is physical AI. As applications widen to robots, autonomous driving, humanoids and intelligent manufacturing, demand for edge AI platforms such as NVIDIA Jetson and for embedded software is rising together, a view shared by the company and press coverage.

An industry analysis cites the spread of IoT devices, AI convergence and rising demand for advanced driver-assistance systems as growth drivers for embedded markets. Embedded sales for automotive and semiconductor uses were also cited as drivers of the improvement in the first quarter and third quarter of 2026.

In cycle terms, commentary suggests physical AI is moving from technology development toward system construction and commercial preparation. On competition, MDS Tech seeks to differentiate by combining self-developed products such as operating systems and development tools with engineering services.

It is an official partner of the NVIDIA Enterprise Platform and was invited to NVIDIA's Jetson Partner Day, which the company cites as a foundation for sales. Its share against larger IT services firms in embedded-specialized niches is not supported by the data reviewed.

Because NVIDIA product sales have grown, the business is also exposed to the product policies and product cycles of its supply partner.

In defense and security, a related listed company says it will push software functional safety and security verification work from the fourth quarter of 2026, but that work has not yet appeared in reported results.

06

Outlook

The company says it aims to build physical AI into a new growth engine and has outlined expanding into autonomous robots, humanoids and intelligent manufacturing.

At ROBOTWORLD 2026, held from November 4 to 7, it showed solutions spanning AI computing, vision and sensing, data connectivity and security together with NVIDIA and other partners.

Its Jetson lineup is being widened beyond carrier boards and box PCs to sensor products such as cameras and LiDAR for autonomous driving and humanoids. The company says it will offer integrated solutions tailored to robotics, drones, smart cities and healthcare.

On AI infrastructure, it plans to build consulting capacity for the NVIDIA Vera Rubin platform and is targeting defense, aviation and public institutions. In April 2026 it signed a physical AI talent cooperation agreement with the robotics institute of Seoul National University's engineering college.

A related listed company says it will begin software functional safety and security verification work in defense, aviation and automotive from the fourth quarter of 2026. A company official said favorable conditions in major end markets such as semiconductors and automobiles are expected to continue this year.

Press coverage reported that cumulative sales for the first three quarters of 2026 were a record. However, the timing and scale of these plans have not yet appeared in reported results, and the next checkpoint is the business mix in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Physical AI demand and NVIDIA product sales

Sales of NVIDIA Jetson and DGX products rose as the physical AI market expanded, and semiconductor and automotive embedded solutions also grew, according to the company and press reports.

One outlet reported that cumulative sales for the first three quarters of 2026 were a record, and that third-quarter sales of KRW 60.3 billion were the highest quarterly figure. In October 2026 a Jetson Full Package worth about KRW 4 billion was contracted for a humanoid home robot project.

Recovery in operating profit

Operating profit fell to KRW 40.13 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 but then stayed above KRW 4 billion in each of the three quarters of 2026, at KRW 4.53 billion, KRW 4.2 billion and KRW 5.37 billion. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose 123.4% year on year.

The company cited higher semiconductor and automotive embedded sales and the effect of selling a loss-making subsidiary as the background.

Financial capacity and cash generation

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 48.0% at the end of 2025 from 59.8% in 2024, and equity attributable to controlling shareholders stood at KRW 141.4 billion. Operating cash flow was positive in each of the three years from 2023, reaching KRW 16.1 billion in 2025.

With a steadier capital structure, the company may have more room to absorb non-operating swings such as derivative valuation gains and losses.

09

Bear factors

Declining operating margin trend

Operating margin was 5.3% in 2022, 5.6% in 2023, 3.6% in 2024 and 3.1% in 2025, lower since 2024. In 2025 sales rose 13.4% while operating profit fell 2.2%. Operating profit has recovered in 2026, but whether that reverses the annual margin trend will be visible only in the 2026 annual report.

Quarterly volatility in net profit

Operating profit fell to KRW 40.13 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, down 98.3% from the prior quarter. In the third quarter of 2026 operating profit rose to KRW 5.37 billion, yet net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 54.4% year on year to KRW 3.37 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026, standalone net profit also fell about 30% year on year because of a derivative valuation loss linked to convertible bond conversion requests.

Cost burden and reliance on supplied products

One analysis describes MDS Tech's sales growth as coexisting with cost pressure that keeps profitability limited. Because the 2026 improvement has been led by higher NVIDIA product sales, margins could be affected if supply terms or product mix change. Whether the company can raise the share of its self-developed products also remains an open task.

10

Risk factors

Dependence on a supply partner

The main driver of recent improvement is NVIDIA product sales. If supply terms, pricing or new product timing change, both sales and margins could be affected. The official partner status within the NVIDIA Enterprise Platform could also shift with partner policy changes.

Convertible bonds and derivative valuation effects

If conversion requests on outstanding convertible bonds continue, derivative valuation gains and losses could keep swinging standalone net profit, as seen in the first quarter of 2026. Conversions would also increase the number of shares outstanding and affect per-share metrics.

Project concentration and order volatility

The October 2026 contract is a single order worth about KRW 4 billion. The customer is identified only as Company A, which makes customer concentration hard to assess from outside.

A slowdown in automotive or semiconductor end markets could reduce embedded sales, while the company has only said it expects favorable conditions to continue.

12

Overall view

MDS Tech grew sales from KRW 153.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 187.7 billion in 2025, and both cumulative sales and operating profit rose through the third quarter of 2026. The growth engine is NVIDIA product sales tied to the physical AI theme, along with embedded solutions for automotive and semiconductor uses.

On the other side, operating margin fell from 5.3% in 2022 to 3.1% in 2025, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders moves sharply from quarter to quarter.

In the first quarter of 2026 operating profit improved, but a derivative valuation loss reduced standalone net profit, and in the third quarter operating profit rose while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio is below 1x, and how the multiple should be read depends on how stable earnings prove to be. The 2026 annual report, due around late March 2027, is the nearest checkpoint for whether physical AI orders and security business contribute to sales.
