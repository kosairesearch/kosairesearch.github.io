Margin turnaround built on two customers

Dongwon Parts' earnings have risen sharply on HBM-related equipment replacement demand and a richer product mix, but heavy reliance on two customers remains the central issue.

02

Business structure

Dongwon Parts is a precision parts maker for semiconductor process equipment, founded in 1997. It produces showerheads, chamber parts, heaters and spin chucks used in etching, deposition, cleaning and ion implantation, and supplies both parts for new tools and consumables for maintenance of installed tools.

The consumables share of sales rose from 36.4% in 2022 to 45.1% in 2025, which increased the recurring portion of revenue. The company emphasizes a one-stop production system that handles everything from material sourcing and machining to special bonding, polishing and anodizing in its own facilities.

Its main customers are Lam Research and SEMES, with Applied Materials and Axcelis Technologies also named as key customers.

By region, domestic sales were the largest at KRW 57.7 billion in 2025, followed by KRW 21.4 billion in Asia and KRW 13.0 billion in the Americas, with overseas sales accounting for about 37% of the total.

On competition, NH Investment & Securities analyst Kang Kyung-geun noted that Dongwon Parts is the only Korean supplier among Lam Research's metal showerhead partners.

Passing technical evaluation and field tests by global equipment makers and being listed on their approved vendor list (AVL) is read as meeting the baseline requirement for supplying global toolmakers.

In new businesses, the company is developing aviation MRO and metal 3D printing, and the metal 3D printing unit has signed an MOU with EOS. Semiconductor equipment parts remain the core of the business.

03

Recent trends

According to the DART periodic report, cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 37 billion, up 64.5% year on year. Operating profit for the same period was KRW 8.21 billion, up 2,602.2%, and net income was KRW 4.25 billion, a swing to profit from the prior-year period.

Based on 2025 results, annual revenue was about KRW 92.3 billion, operating profit was KRW 7.8 billion and net income was KRW 3.4 billion, reversing a KRW 5.6 billion net loss in 2024.

Revenue grew from KRW 50.5 billion in 2023 and KRW 59.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 92.3 billion in 2025, while the operating margin moved from about 8.4% in 2025 to 23.0% in the first half of 2026.

First-half 2026 revenue was KRW 63.3 billion and operating profit was KRW 14.5 billion, and the company expects an annual operating margin of about 22%.

Preliminary, unaudited figures cited in media around the listing (nine-month revenue of KRW 100.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 22.4 billion) differ from the DART periodic report figures.

The shares closed at 29,400 won on the first listing day, November 10, above the 26,500 won IPO price, and touched 57,300 won intraday. The stock later fell to 16,270 won, with a 20.25% decline on November 26.

Market capitalization stands at KRW 150.8 billion, and 478,593 shares, about 5.2% of listed shares, were subject to a mandatory holding lock-up that was released on November 15.

04

Outlook

Three variables are likely to determine future earnings: maintaining the product mix, diversifying the customer base and expanding capacity.

On the positive side, the shift of memory makers from general-purpose DRAM tools to new HBM-oriented equipment has reduced the share of legacy showerheads, which underpins profitability. NH Investment & Securities estimated that the legacy share fell from above 70% to around 10%.

The company plans to invest KRW 31.3 billion in building its seventh plant in Namdong Industrial Complex in Incheon and in automation equipment, and the start of operations at that plant will determine its ability to meet volume.

On the negative side, the two customers still accounted for 79.3% of first-half 2026 revenue, and the company itself lists pricing pressure from large customers as a risk factor. Axcelis Technologies rose to 8.1% of first-half 2026 sales from 7.8% in 2025, but diversification remains at an early stage.

Financially, the debt-to-equity ratio was 296.9% at the end of 2025, and total borrowings reached KRW 32.5 billion, more than double the KRW 15.0 billion at the end of 2024. The share price trades below its IPO level, with float changes and supply-demand factors being cited.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 results, the start date of the seventh plant, and the trend in the revenue share of major customers.

05

Bull factors

Legacy decline and margin turnaround

NH Investment & Securities analyst Kang Kyung-geun estimated that legacy products fell from more than 70% to around 10% of showerhead sales, which lowers unit economics. Cost of sales as a share of revenue declined from 84.4% in 2025 to 71.5% in the first half of 2026. The company has said it expects an annual operating margin of about 22%.

Approved supplier status with Lam Research

According to NH Investment & Securities, Dongwon Parts is the only Korean company among Lam Research's metal showerhead partners. Showerhead sales to Lam Research rose from KRW 17.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 33.3 billion in 2025.

Revenue from global toolmakers increased from KRW 18.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 47.3 billion in 2025, and Lam Research accounted for 45.9% of first-half 2026 sales.

Recurring revenue from consumables

The consumables share of sales rose from 36.4% in 2022 to 45.1% in 2025. The company stresses that parts supplied for new tools lead to recurring maintenance consumables revenue.

Because changing components on semiconductor equipment requires lengthy quality qualification, the company argues that repeat revenue is stable.

06

Bear factors

Concentration in two customers

Lam Research (45.9%) and SEMES (33.4%) together accounted for 79.3% of first-half 2026 revenue. In 2025, the two customers that each represented more than 10% of sales totaled KRW 76.8 billion, about 83% of total revenue. The company's securities filing identified pricing pressure from large customers as a risk factor.

High leverage and rising borrowings

The debt-to-equity ratio was 296.9% at the end of 2025, slightly lower than 297.16% a year earlier. Total borrowings reached KRW 32.5 billion, more than double the KRW 15.0 billion at the end of 2024.

Of total equity of KRW 27.5 billion, KRW 12.1 billion consists of asset revaluation gains, which makes the quality of equity a point to watch.

Post-listing supply and a sharp share price fall

Shares available for trading right after listing were 17.52%, lower than the 29.47% initially expected. The mandatory-holding shares subject to release on November 15 totaled 478,593. On November 26, the stock fell 20.25% to 16,270 won, below the 26,500 won IPO price.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Earnings are tied to the AI and HBM-led semiconductor investment cycle, so changes in global chip capital spending could move equipment demand and parts orders together. Exchange rate and raw material price swings are additional variables that can affect profitability. Since listing, the share price has also been exposed to shifts in broader market sentiment.

Industry

If equipment replacement by memory makers winds down after the HBM-focused tool transition, the benefit from the legacy-to-premium shift could narrow. Volume allocation may also change with equipment makers' design revisions or the approval of new suppliers. Because component qualification cycles are long, interruptions in supply can take time to recover from.

Customer and financial

With two customers accounting for most sales, price cuts or order adjustments could cause large swings in revenue. The debt-to-equity ratio of 296.9% and higher borrowings could add funding pressure for capacity investment.

The gap between preliminary figures reported around the listing and the periodic report figures is also a variable in interpreting results.

08

Overall view

Dongwon Parts' DART-based results show a clear rise in profitability, supported by demand for HBM-related equipment replacement and a richer product mix. Cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 8.21 billion, up 2,602.2% year on year.

The bullish factors are the shrinking share of legacy showerheads, a lower cost of sales ratio and a growing base of recurring consumables revenue.

The bearish factors are the structure in which 79.3% of first-half 2026 sales came from two customers, a debt-to-equity ratio of 296.9% at the end of 2025, and the post-listing supply changes and sharp share price decline.

NH Investment & Securities presents the legacy reduction as evidence supporting margin improvement while treating customer concentration as an item to monitor. Results in this report are based on the DART periodic report, which differs from the preliminary figures reported in the media.

The main checkpoints ahead are the fourth-quarter 2026 results, the start of operations at the seventh plant and changes in revenue share by customer. Overall, the margin improvement is visible in the reported figures, while customer mix and capital structure remain the variables that will determine whether it lasts.
