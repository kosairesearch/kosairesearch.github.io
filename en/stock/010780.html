02

Business structure

IS Dongseo operates across construction, concrete, environment and secondary battery segments. In 2025, environment sales were KRW 483.4 billion, or 39.2% of consolidated revenue, while construction sales were KRW 441.7 billion, or 35.8%.

The environment business centers on spent battery recycling, and its operating profit rose from KRW 12.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 20.6 billion in 2025. Secondary battery sales reached KRW 127.7 billion in 2025, up 9.8% from KRW 116.3 billion a year earlier.

The company plans to expand LFP scrap sourcing as the LFP battery recycling market grows. The concrete segment improved profitability on higher volumes and high-value projects such as Pyeongtaek P5. Construction revenue is recognized on handover timing, so quarterly results move sharply with project completions.

First-quarter 2026 segment sales were KRW 214.0 billion for construction, KRW 86.4 billion for environment, KRW 63.4 billion for concrete, KRW 55.0 billion for secondary batteries and KRW 19.1 billion for other. The 2025 order intake included a KRW 341.3 billion housing redevelopment project in Daegu.

Concrete demand is linked to large-diameter piles for semiconductor plants in Yongin and Cheongju. On cost, the 2025 cost-of-sales ratio was 74.6%, below an industry average of about 90% cited by press reports.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 2.28 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.03 trillion in 2023 (down 10.9% year on year), KRW 1.51 trillion in 2024 (down 25.4%) and KRW 1.23 trillion in 2025 (down 18.5%). The operating margin declined from 15.1% in 2022 and 16.8% in 2023 to 11.2% in 2024 and 5.5% in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 operating profit decline mainly to fewer project sites and higher operating costs. Fourth-quarter 2025 also reflected one-off selling and administrative charges, including bad-debt provisions tied to sales of units.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 195.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 161.1 billion in 2023 (down 17.6%). It then swung to a net loss of KRW 148.7 billion in 2024 and narrowed to a net loss of KRW 53.8 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 160.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 71.8 billion in 2025, a different measure. Operating cash flow went from KRW 428.5 billion in 2022 to negative KRW 130.9 billion in 2024 and then recovered to KRW 133.6 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 9.02 billion in the third quarter of 2025 turned into an operating loss of KRW 27 billion in the fourth quarter. The first quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 118.1 billion, with revenue up 46.1% year on year.

The KRW 93.8 billion equity-method gain from the Coentec sale sits below operating profit. Operating profit then fell to KRW 5.1 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.53 billion in the third quarter of 2026. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 280 billion rose 3.3% year on year, but operating profit fell 83.1%.

LS Securities attributed the shortfall against consensus mainly to a construction revenue gap and fair-value losses on some Deokeun commercial and knowledge-industry units.

05

Industry analysis

The construction market is in a slowdown. IS Dongseo said in its first-quarter 2026 results that broad weakness in construction and manufacturing would limit the pace of recovery in some businesses. Across the industry, companies are competing hard for orders as earnings decline.

IS Dongseo's 2025 order intake of KRW 970.3 billion was roughly three times the KRW 323.4 billion of the prior year, according to press reports. LS Securities pointed to higher utilization at concrete and PC operations and rising public-works revenue in waste handling.

However, the construction segment lacks sites beyond those recognized on handover in 2026, raising fixed-cost pressure, according to an analysis. Secondary battery profitability depends on major metal prices. Higher metal prices lifted the segment's operating profit to KRW 8.0 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

On cost, the 2025 cost-of-sales ratio of 74.6% compares with an industry average of about 90%. Construction's share fell to 35.8% while environment rose to 39.2%, a mix that sets the company apart from traditional builders.

06

Outlook

In secondary batteries, the company plans to expand LFP scrap sourcing and operations as the LFP battery recycling market grows. It said it expects to sustain profitability if metal prices hold or rise.

The order backlog at end-2025 was KRW 2.34 trillion, up 28.8% from KRW 1.82 trillion a year earlier, split into KRW 1.48 trillion of building, KRW 818.8 billion of sales-type projects and KRW 43.6 billion of civil works. Press reports translated this backlog into about 1.9 years of 2026 revenue.

In environment, the company cited growth in new orders centered on environmental energy solutions and rising construction revenue as support for steady results. LS Securities said the pace of revenue recognition at the Gyeongsan Pentahills project and any easing of Deokeun-related losses would drive earnings.

The same analysis said that a larger contribution from the company's own housing projects in 2028 could mark a turning point for medium-term profitability. Another report placed the timing of earnings recovery after 2027.

The next checkpoint is how much Deokeun-related fair-value losses and financing costs show up in fourth-quarter 2026 results. Because sales-type projects make up a large share of the backlog, shifts in the housing sales market could affect how fast backlog turns into revenue.

08

Bull factors

Order growth builds the revenue base

Order intake in 2025 reached KRW 970.3 billion, the highest annual level in five years according to press reports, and the backlog at end-2025 was KRW 2.34 trillion. A Daegu housing redevelopment award of KRW 341.3 billion and a construction-cost increase in Ulsan B-14 drove the gain. Press reports converted the backlog into about 1.9 years of 2026 revenue.

Shift toward environment and batteries

Environment sales reached KRW 483.4 billion in 2025, 39.2% of the total and above construction. Environment operating profit rose from KRW 12.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 20.6 billion in 2025.

Secondary batteries earned KRW 8.0 billion in operating profit in the first quarter of 2026, and the company expects profitability to hold if metal prices hold.

Room for construction earnings to recover

First-quarter 2026 construction sales were KRW 214.0 billion, with operating profit of KRW 93.2 billion, reflecting handover-basis revenue from the Goyang Deokeun project. LS Securities said a larger contribution from the company's own housing projects in 2028 could support medium-term profitability. The pace of recovery depends on how quickly the construction revenue gap is filled.

09

Bear factors

Construction revenue gap and volatile quarters

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.53 billion fell 93% short of the KRW 22.3 billion consensus cited by LS Securities. The causes were a construction revenue gap and fair-value losses on some Deokeun commercial and knowledge-industry units. The company said the first-quarter 2026 profit also benefited from a partial reversal of bad-debt provisions.

Financial burden and financing costs

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 2.04 trillion, with a debt ratio of 143.3%. Financing costs on project financing for the Gyeongsan Pentahills project continue to weigh below the operating line, contributing to the third-quarter 2026 net loss. Operating cash flow recovered to KRW 133.6 billion in 2025 after turning negative in 2024.

Shrinking profit and thin margins

The operating margin fell from 15.1% in 2022 to 5.5% in 2025. Operating profit dropped from KRW 345.1 billion to KRW 68.1 billion over the same period. Operating profit of KRW 5.1 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.53 billion in the third quarter of 2026 shows how quickly the first-quarter gain faded.

10

Risk factors

Sales-type projects and valuation losses

Fair-value losses on some Deokeun commercial and knowledge-industry units pulled down third-quarter 2026 operating profit. Bad-debt provisions tied to sales were set aside in the fourth quarter of 2025, leaving room for further losses in sales-type projects.

Sales-type projects account for KRW 818.8 billion of the backlog, so exposure to the housing sales market is substantial.

Battery metal prices and feedstock

Secondary battery profitability depends on major metal prices. Higher metal prices helped lift segment operating profit to KRW 8.0 billion in the first quarter of 2026, but a price decline could weaken the profit trend. The company has named feedstock security and cost reduction as priorities.

Financing costs and construction-manufacturing slowdown

Financing costs on the Gyeongsan Pentahills project weigh on non-operating results. The company said the slowdown in construction and manufacturing could limit the pace of recovery in environment. If both factors persist, quarterly net income could swing more widely.

12

Overall view

IS Dongseo is reshaping its business mix, growing environment and secondary battery units while construction revenue fell from KRW 2.28 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.23 trillion in 2025. On a 2025 basis, environment sales exceeded construction sales.

On profitability, operating profit fell to KRW 68.1 billion in 2025, and losses attributable to controlling shareholders have persisted for two consecutive years, 2024 and 2025.

The first quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 118.1 billion, but the KRW 93.8 billion equity-method gain sits below operating profit and should be considered separately.

Operating profit fell to KRW 1.53 billion in the third quarter of 2026 as construction revenue gaps and Deokeun valuation losses weighed. The backlog of KRW 2.34 trillion and the potential contribution from the company's own housing projects in 2028 are cited as medium-term support.

Meanwhile, the 143.3% debt ratio and project-financing costs remain burdens. The next checkpoint is how fourth-quarter 2026 results and the 2026 annual report reflect valuation losses and financing costs.
