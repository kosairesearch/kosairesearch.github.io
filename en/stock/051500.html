Online Growth and a Margin Test at CJ Freshway

CJ Freshway's online platform Sikbom has passed KRW 100 billion in quarterly transaction value, yet operating margin eased from 3.6% in 2022 to 2.9% in 2025, so whether profitability recovers is the key item to track.

02

Business structure

CJ Freshway operates in three areas: wholesale distribution of food service ingredients and food materials, a food service business, and online ingredient distribution through its Sikbom platform.

According to the company's 2025 annual press release, the distribution segment (food service ingredients and food materials) generated KRW 1.56 trillion of revenue and the food service business KRW 1.89 trillion, so the food service business accounts for more than half of total sales.

In the food service ingredient channel, franchise customers increased their distribution volumes, lifting franchise-channel sales 9.4% year on year in Q3 2026. Food materials sales rose 15.7% in the same quarter, driven by demand for core products such as dairy.

The food service business covers institutional catering for cafeterias and hospitals, its kitchenless model Freshmillon, which serves meals without a dedicated kitchen, and ingredient supply to kids and school channels.

In Q3 2026 food service sales were KRW 509.3 billion, with institutional catering up 6.3% and Freshmillon sales up 52.5%. Online sales, centered on Sikbom, reached KRW 67.7 billion in Q3 2026, up 226.6% year on year.

Sikbom's Q3 transaction value of KRW 113.5 billion was the first quarterly figure above KRW 100 billion, and the number of integrated-delivery products more than doubled while purchases per store rose 14.3%.

In 2025 the company said a merger with its subsidiary Fresh One was aimed at combining product and logistics capabilities.

Among listed peers in the food sector, Hyundai Green Food is often compared with it in catering, while the online business is positioned around its nationwide logistics network linked to an O2O model.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, revenue rose from KRW 2.75 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.07 trillion in 2023 (up 11.9%), KRW 3.22 trillion in 2024 (up 4.9%) and KRW 3.48 trillion in 2025 (up 7.9%), marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 97.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 99.3 billion in 2023 (up 1.4%), KRW 94 billion in 2024 (down 5.3%) and KRW 101.7 billion in 2025 (up 8.1%), and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating margin was 3.6% in 2022, 3.2% in 2023, 2.9% in 2024 and 2.9% in 2025, so margin slipped while sales grew. For 2025, the company credited the merger with its subsidiary Fresh One and operating efficiency gains for the profitability improvement.

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 52.2 billion in 2022, KRW 59.7 billion in 2023 (up 14.3%), KRW 25.9 billion in 2024 (down 56.6%) and KRW 50.1 billion in 2025 (up 93.3%). Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 27.4 billion in 2024, a different figure from controlling-shareholder net income.

The debt ratio, defined as total liabilities divided by total equity, was 267.5% in 2022, 272.1% in 2023, 276.6% in 2024 and 249.1% in 2025. Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 455.2 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 1.13 trillion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 123.6 billion in 2022, KRW 218.4 billion in 2023, KRW 111.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 127.1 billion in 2025. Quarterly, revenue was KRW 901.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 33.6 billion in Q3 2025, then KRW 898 billion and KRW 30 billion in Q4 2025.

Q1 2026 marked the low point, with revenue of KRW 833.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 11 billion.

Q2 2026 (revenue KRW 923.2 billion, operating profit KRW 23.5 billion) and Q3 2026 (revenue KRW 961.4 billion, operating profit KRW 23.8 billion) each recorded two consecutive quarters of increases in both revenue and operating profit.

Even so, Q3 2026 operating profit fell 29.2% year on year, and the company attributed this to investment in the Sikbom platform's customer and seller base alongside a slowdown in dining-out demand.

Controlling-shareholder net income recovered from KRW 3.13 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 11.7 billion in Q2 and KRW 25 billion in Q3, and the four-quarter total from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 is KRW 50.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Food service ingredient distribution and institutional catering move together with dining-out demand and institutional spending. The company said dining-out weakness continued in Q3 2026, yet higher volumes from franchise customers supported its food service ingredient sales.

In Q1 2026 the company said all business segments grew even as domestic consumer sentiment weakened on external uncertainty. The catering side is supported by institutional demand from cafeterias and hospitals as well as kids and school channels, and institutional catering sales rose 6.3% year on year in Q3 2026.

Online ingredient distribution is the fastest-growing area, with Sikbom's quarterly transaction value passing KRW 100 billion. In this area product assortment, delivery logistics and seller commissions shape profitability, and the company has planned a higher online seller commission rate for 2027.

As dining-out demand slows, growth in online and catering is helping maintain overall sales volume. Online platforms, however, carry continuing costs to win customers and sellers, so sales growth has not translated directly into margin gains.

06

Outlook

The company's stated plans for 2027 have two parts: improving product margins in its existing food service ingredient business, and raising the seller commission rate in its online business.

The company said it would expand product margin improvement work from the first half of 2027 to lift profitability across the group. It also plans to keep strengthening the product and logistics competitiveness of Sikbom in the fourth quarter and focus on winning customers and sellers.

The food service business is being restructured around profitability, with continued operational efficiency gains. Freshmillon, the kitchenless model, is positioned as a growth driver after its 52.5% sales increase in Q3 2026.

On profit, the item to check is whether platform investment costs ease from Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 23.8 billion, which fell 29.2% year on year. Q4 2026 results and 2027 full-year outlook have not yet been disclosed.

The 2026 annual report, the next regular filing, will show whether the operating margin moved from 2.9% in 2025. Whether the commission increase and margin work show up in actual profitability is the central item to track in the first half of 2027.

08

Bull factors

Online platform scaling

Sikbom's Q3 2026 transaction value reached KRW 113.5 billion, the first quarter above KRW 100 billion, and online sales of KRW 67.7 billion rose 226.6% year on year. Integrated-delivery products more than doubled and purchases per store rose 14.3%, so customer spending per outlet is growing. The company said it would keep strengthening platform products and logistics in the fourth quarter.

Earnings recovery trend

Controlling-shareholder net income recovered from KRW 25.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 50.1 billion in 2025, and after a low of KRW 3.13 billion in Q1 2026 it rose to KRW 11.7 billion in Q2 and KRW 25 billion in Q3. Operating cash flow was KRW 127.1 billion in 2025, above KRW 111.2 billion in 2024. Operating profit of KRW 101.7 billion in 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Institutional catering base and new model

The food service business generated KRW 1.89 trillion of revenue in 2025, more than half of the company's total. Institutional catering rose 6.3% in Q3 2026 and sales of the kitchenless model Freshmillon rose 52.5%.

Its base in cafeteria and hospital demand is often cited as a strength that makes it less exposed to swings in dining-out demand.

09

Bear factors

Thin operating margin

Operating margin declined from 3.6% in 2022 to 3.2% in 2023 and 2.9% in 2024, and held at 2.9% in 2025. Revenue grew from KRW 2.75 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.48 trillion in 2025, but margin did not follow. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 23.8 billion fell 29.2% year on year.

Platform investment burden

The company attributed the Q3 2026 profit decline to investment in the Sikbom platform's competitiveness and in expanding its customer and seller base. Because these costs flow directly into quarterly profit, the timing at which transaction growth turns into earnings remains open. Whether the commission increase and product margin work lift profit in the first half of 2027 is the key test.

Slowing dining-out demand

The company said dining-out weakness continued in Q3 2026. Sales of food service ingredients and food materials reached KRW 446 billion, up 14.2% year on year, but a slowdown in demand outside franchise channels could add pressure on profit. Operating profit fell 5.3% to KRW 94 billion in 2024, showing how earnings can swing when demand softens.

10

Risk factors

Profit volatility

With operating margin around 2.9%, small changes in costs, labor or investment spending pass through strongly to profit. Q1 2026 operating profit of KRW 11 billion fell 63.2% from the prior quarter, then rose 113.3% in Q2. Quarterly swings this large mean annual results need to be read alongside quarterly ones.

Online competition and commission risk

As Sikbom's transaction value grows, the cost of winning and keeping customers and sellers rises. Because the company plans a higher seller commission rate for 2027, any seller attrition or drop in transactions could slow platform growth. Nationwide logistics costs also scale up as the online business expands.

Balance sheet and dining-out risk

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 1.13 trillion, more than twice total equity of KRW 455.2 billion. The debt ratio fell to 249.1%, but leverage remains high, and rising financing costs or working capital needs could weigh on cash flow. A prolonged dining-out slowdown could weaken food service ingredient sales and reduce financial flexibility.

12

Overall view

CJ Freshway's revenue grew for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and the company described 2025 as its record year. Operating margin, however, slipped from 3.6% in 2022 to 2.9% in 2025, and Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 23.8 billion fell 29.2% year on year.

In the same quarter online sales rose 226.6% and Sikbom's transaction value passed KRW 100 billion for the first time on a quarterly basis, as growth broadens into online. Controlling-shareholder net income rose for two consecutive quarters after its Q1 2026 low, a sign of earnings recovery.

The debt ratio fell to 249.1% in 2025 and total equity stood at KRW 455.2 billion. Strengths include platform growth and a better balance sheet, while weaknesses include a thin operating margin, platform investment costs and softer dining-out demand.

The next items to track are Q4 2026 results, the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and the effect of the commission increase and margin work in the first half of 2027.
