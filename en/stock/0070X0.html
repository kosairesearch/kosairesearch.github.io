From Battery Equipment Toward Solar

Stechm is trying to extend into solar power generation after improving margins in its core battery-equipment business, but the investment size and terms of the new venture are not yet fixed.

02

Business structure

Stechm was founded on February 18, 2020, and is headquartered in Namyang-eup, Hwaseong, Gyeonggi Province. It is a special-purpose machinery maker whose main products are secondary-battery equipment. The company has built manufacturing and engineering capability in automation equipment for secondary-battery production.

The revenue split by product and the list of major customers do not appear in the public material reviewed, so they are described only qualitatively.

According to reports at the time of the listing approval, the largest shareholder group is led by CEO Kim Seom-taek and holds a 44.70% stake. The designated adviser is IBK Investment & Securities. On the same basis, total assets were KRW 18.476 billion and shareholders' equity was KRW 8.063 billion.

The company is classed in the electronics and components sector, yet its actual business is closer to battery-manufacturing equipment. Its products and scale differ entirely from those of sector peers such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek.

The secondary-battery equipment industry depends on the capital-spending schedules of cell makers. In a March 2026 presentation, Samil PwC noted that equipment makers face sales pressure from a short-term freeze in capital spending.

The same material also presented the shift to dry electrode processes and ESS stacking processes as a possible structural opportunity. It is not confirmed which process equipment Stechm supplies, so the relevance of these shifts to the company is hard to judge.

The business purposes added in October 2026 include renewable-energy manufacturing, solar plant development and operation, cosmetics, AI system management and broadcasting and music distribution.

This is an articles-of-incorporation change that leaves the door open to several new businesses beyond the core equipment business.

03

Recent trends

For 2025, the reported figures were revenue of KRW 10.003 billion, net income of KRW 661 million and shareholders' equity of KRW 8.063 billion.

On the basis of a corporate-information service (JobKorea), revenue was KRW 13.56 billion in 2023, KRW 10.21 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.00 billion in 2025, falling two years in a row. On the same service, operating profit rose from KRW 589.93 million in 2024 to KRW 854.21 million in 2025.

The same service calculates the 2025 operating margin at 8.54%, up from 5.78% in 2024. Higher margins on stagnant sales are the defining feature of the 2025 results.

The same service also shows that cash flow from operating activities was negative in both 2024 and 2025. Cash collection may not have kept pace with operating profit, which makes working-capital management an item to monitor.

In a table reproducing NICE D&B audit data, total equity rose from KRW 1.74 billion in 2024 to KRW 8.06 billion in 2025. The material reviewed does not explain the reason for the sharp increase in equity. Quarterly and half-year results for 2026 could not be confirmed from public sources.

Stechm began trading on KONEX on April 27, 2026. At the extraordinary general meeting on October 6, 2026, all six agenda items passed, including the election of four non-executive directors and one inside director. On October 27, 2026, it signed the memorandum of understanding with Century New City Group.

As of 20:43 KST on October 27, 2026, the share price was KRW 11,400, up 2.70% from the previous day. Market capitalization stood at KRW 17.2 billion, and traded value for the day was only KRW 1,101,900.

04

Outlook

The core business sits in a phase where weak electric-vehicle demand and growing ESS demand pull in different directions. Korean credit rating agencies assessed that EV demand weakness and oversupply have become entrenched, with ESS the only source of volume growth.

The same assessment found agreement that ESS cannot be seen as a savior for the industry. An October 12, 2026 report said global ESS installations are rising while US electric-vehicle sales decline.

Whether cell makers' ESS line conversions lead to new equipment orders is a separate question, and Stechm's order status could not be confirmed.

The new business hinges on whether it takes concrete shape. The two companies plan to cooperate on project discovery, information exchange and resource linkage centered on domestic solar power projects. Whether to invest and proceed will be decided after project-by-project feasibility reviews and separate negotiations.

A group official said the counterparty has total assets of about CNY 60 billion, but the amount Stechm would need to fund has not been disclosed. Solar generation projects take time for permits, grid connection and financing, so the timing of any earnings contribution is hard to gauge.

On the market side, the KONEX-to-KOSDAQ transfer environment is a variable. An analysis noted that KONEX companies considering a move to KOSDAQ are increasing as the government pursues a promotion-and-demotion system for KOSDAQ.

A fast-track transfer from KONEX requires one year since listing, a recommendation from the designated adviser and sound management. No transfer plan for Stechm has been confirmed.

The business indicators to watch are battery-equipment orders, whether the operating margin holds, operating cash flow and concrete investment disclosures for the new business.

05

Bull factors

Industry demand shifting toward ESS

An October 12, 2026 report said the growth axis of the secondary-battery industry is moving quickly from electric vehicles to ESS. Battery storage systems are also starting to emerge as core equipment in AI data-center power infrastructure.

Continued line conversions and expansions by cell makers could create order opportunities for equipment suppliers. Whether Stechm's products connect directly to this demand has not been confirmed.

Improved operating margin

On the basis of the corporate-information service, operating profit rose every year, from KRW 397.4 million in 2023 to KRW 589.93 million in 2024 and KRW 854.21 million in 2025. The operating margin climbed from 2.93% in 2023 to 5.78% in 2024 and 8.54% in 2025.

Higher profitability while sales were shrinking suggests that costs or the order mix may have improved. Room for further margin improvement depends on order volume and utilization.

Stronger equity base and a new partner

Shareholders' equity for 2025 was KRW 8.063 billion. In a table reproducing NICE D&B data, that is a large increase from KRW 1.74 billion in 2024. The counterparty, Century New City Group, is a Chinese state-owned company holding an AA+ corporate credit rating.

A broader financial base and outside network could help with funding and project discovery for the new business.

06

Bear factors

Small and shrinking sales

On the corporate-information service, revenue fell from KRW 13.56 billion in 2023 to KRW 10.00 billion in 2025. Sales declined 24.69% in 2024 and 2.08% in 2025 from the prior year. With annual sales around KRW 10 billion, results can swing sharply on one or two large orders. A sales growth driver has not yet been confirmed.

Negative operating cash flow

The corporate-information service shows negative cash flow from operating activities in both 2024 and 2025. Equipment makers tend to carry a heavy working-capital burden because costs are incurred before payments are collected. If cash does not arrive despite reported profit, dependence on outside funding can rise. Additional funding needs could also emerge as the business expands.

Unproven new ventures and diffusion of focus

The agreement sets out a broad direction of cooperation, and the specific investment size and execution terms are not fixed. The added business purposes also include items far from the core business, such as cosmetics, AI system management and broadcasting and music distribution.

A small company keeping several fields open at once can spread its management resources thin. Adding a purpose to the articles of incorporation is a separate matter from actually starting the business.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Korean credit rating agencies diagnosed EV demand weakness and oversupply as structurally entrenched. Expansion plans are being scaled back and start-up timing adjusted, and the agencies see low utilization and profitability pressure likely to persist through 2026.

If cell makers delay capital spending, equipment orders can be delayed as well. Changes in interest rates, exchange rates and the trade environment can also affect customers' investment decisions.

Business

The renewable-energy business requires capabilities different from Stechm's existing equipment manufacturing. Whether to invest and proceed will be decided after project-by-project feasibility reviews and separate negotiations.

With the investment size undecided, funding burden, schedule delays and the agreement falling through cannot be ruled out. The board composition also changed at the extraordinary general meeting, including the new appointment of four non-executive directors. Changes in management direction and shareholder structure should be tracked through disclosures.

Market and liquidity

According to an August 23, 2026 report, average daily traded value on KONEX fell 23% from a year earlier. The same report said that, amid worsening liquidity, the market's role as a growth ladder is seen as having become largely nominal.

Stechm's traded value at the time of writing was only KRW 1,101,900, so even small trades can move the price sharply. Low liquidity can make it hard to trade at the desired time and price.

08

Overall view

Stechm is a small secondary-battery equipment maker with annual sales of around KRW 10 billion, and it listed on KONEX in April 2026. In 2025 sales declined, but operating profit and the operating margin increased.

On the corporate-information service, however, operating cash flow was negative, so the quality of earnings deserves attention. The industry has weak EV demand alongside growing ESS demand, and the spillover to equipment makers depends on the pace of cell makers' capital spending.

In October 2026 the company added nine business purposes and signed a solar-related memorandum of understanding with a Chinese state-owned company, but the investment size and terms are not fixed. The thin trading on KONEX also affects how the share price should be read.

The weighting of bullish and bearish factors is left to the reader, and this report is provided for information only.
