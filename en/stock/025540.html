02

Business structure

Korea Electric Terminal manufactures automotive and electronic connectors, with automotive connectors as the core business. According to a Hana Securities report from January 2026, automotive connectors make up about 90% of total sales and electronic connectors and other products the remaining 10%.

The company also manufactures and sells high-voltage connectors and modules for eco-friendly vehicles, as disclosed in March 2026. Its customers are mainly automakers, and a 2024 press report said roughly 70% of sales came from Hyundai Motor and KIA.

Overseas operations have grown in importance, with a 2024 report noting large ICB product orders won by the U.S. unit from North American automakers and battery makers in the first half of that year. Robot connectors are still under 1% of sales.

Raw materials include copper and resin-based materials, and the raw material ratio to sales was 54% in 2023 and 52% in the first quarter of 2024. Because connector volumes follow customers' model launches and platforms, results track sales of specific vehicle models.

Consolidated sales in 2025 were KRW 1.44 trillion, down 4.4% year on year, showing how directly automaker demand flows into earnings.

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue grew from 1 trillion 1,681 hundred million won in 2022 to 1 trillion 2,969 hundred million won in 2023 (up 11.0% year on year) and 1 trillion 5,098 hundred million won in 2024 (up 16.4% year on year), then came to 1 trillion 4,428 hundred million won in 2025 (down 4.4% year on year).

The largest year among 2022-2025 is 2024. Operating profit rose from 643 hundred million won in 2022 to 1,117 hundred million won in 2023 and 1,713 hundred million won in 2024, then fell to 1,393 hundred million won in 2025 (down 18.7% year on year).

The operating margin was 5.5% in 2022, 8.6% in 2023, 11.3% in 2024 and 9.7% in 2025, declining after peaking in 2024. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose sharply to 1,422 hundred million won in 2024 (up 95.1% year on year) and was 1,060 hundred million won in 2025 (down 25.5% year on year).

Net income including non-controlling interests was 1,062 hundred million won in 2025, a figure separate from net profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

The debt-to-equity ratio at the end of 2025 was 34.1%, down from 48.2% in 2022, and operating cash flow increased from 522 hundred million won in 2022 to 2,192 hundred million won in 2025.

Quarterly revenue bottomed at 3,370 hundred million won in the fourth quarter of 2025 (down 8.4% quarter on quarter), then rose to 3,374 hundred million won in the first quarter of 2026, 3,951 hundred million won in the second quarter (up 17.1% quarter on quarter) and 4,003 hundred million won in the third quarter (up 8.8% year on year).

Over the same period, operating profit declined from 405 hundred million won in the third quarter of 2025 to 331 hundred million won in the fourth quarter, 258 hundred million won in the first quarter of 2026 and 178 hundred million won in the second quarter, and although it edged up to 189 hundred million won in the third quarter, it was down 53.4% year on year.

This was a stretch in which profit fell by more than revenue rose, so margins declined.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was 385 hundred million won in the third quarter of 2025, 229 hundred million won in the fourth quarter, 353 hundred million won in the first quarter of 2026 and 252 hundred million won in the second quarter, and in the third quarter it swung to a loss, with a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of 149 hundred million won.

The sum of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters is 684 hundred million won.

A Hana Securities report in August 2026 summarized the situation to the effect that the numbers were bad but the reasons were understood, and in September of the same year Shinyoung Securities pointed to a phase in which North American revenue is declining.

Industry analysis

Demand for automotive connectors follows automaker production volumes and model mix. A 2026 first-quarter results summary framed the period as EV slowing while HEV and new businesses diversify. Analysts describe a phase where hybrids and electrical components reshape demand as EV growth decelerates.

LS Securities said in November 2025 that a growing share of hybrid vehicles at key customers would keep benefiting the company's wire harness subsidiary. A 2024 analysis pointed to new EV plants in Mexico as an additional opportunity.

On competitiveness, a 2024 press report highlighted that the company's operating and net margins exceeded those of Hyundai Motor and KIA, in an industry where parts makers that depend on automakers often struggle to earn profits.

A 2024 report noted that copper prices rose less than feared and raw material costs stayed in the mid-50 percent range of sales. New businesses are still under 1% of sales, so structural change has a small effect on earnings.

The 2026 picture, with North American sales declining alongside EV slowing, depends on customer production plans and whether the U.S. unit's order flow recovers.

Earlier, the company had said it would use 30% of consolidated net income for shareholder returns over 2024 to 2026. A consensus as of March 2026 projected 2026 sales of KRW 1.46 trillion and operating profit of KRW 157 billion, with 2027 operating profit at KRW 171.4 billion.

On new businesses, the company has said it plans to strengthen its robot connector business.

Items to watch are whether fourth-quarter 2026 operating profit exceeds the third-quarter level and whether controlling-shareholder earnings return to profit. The annual business report should also explain the gap between operating profit and net income.

The 2025 payout ratio was 30.5%, and the dividend amount grew 42.6%. A disclosed return policy is the bullish point on shareholder returns. Bear factors

Falling operating margin

The operating margin slid from 11.3% in 2024 to 9.7% in 2025. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 18.9 billion was down 53.4% year on year. Profit did not rise with sales during the recent quarters, which is the core bearish point. The next check is how far the margin recovers.

Swing to a net loss in the third quarter

The controlling-shareholder result turned to a net loss of KRW 14.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026. Controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 68.4 billion, far below the KRW 142.2 billion posted in 2024. Profit turned negative even as operating profit edged up from the prior quarter, which requires follow-up.

Customer concentration and EV slowdown

A 2024 press report said about 70% of sales came from Hyundai Motor and KIA. A 2026 first-quarter results summary framed EV slowing as the key theme. Shinyoung Securities pointed to a decline in North American sales in September 2026. Because earnings follow customer production plans directly, any cut in those plans would flow straight through.

Risk factors

Earnings estimate risk

Securities-firm consensus projected 2026 operating profit of KRW 157 billion and 2027 operating profit of KRW 171.4 billion, but that outlook dates from after the first-quarter 2026 results.

Operating profit through the first three quarters of 2026 means reaching the full-year estimate requires a sharp fourth-quarter recovery. If fourth-quarter profit falls short, the 2026 and 2027 estimates could be revised down. Such revisions would lower the market's benchmark for how fast earnings can recover.

Raw materials and currency

Raw materials include copper and resin-based materials, with the raw material ratio at 54% of sales in 2023 and 52% in the first quarter of 2024. Higher copper prices would raise cost pressure. As overseas units grow, currency swings have a larger effect on results.

Delay in new-business shift

New businesses such as robot connectors are under 1% of sales. Shifting away from automotive dependence will take time to show up in results. A slower transition could also slow the pace of profit recovery. Business plans and order disclosures are the next items to track.
