High-tech orders lift construction margin; trading profit eases

02

Business structure

Samsung C&T reports four segments: construction, trading and investment, fashion, and resort. In the preliminary release of October 28, construction revenue in the third quarter was KRW 4.465 trillion and operating profit was KRW 277 billion.

Construction revenue was split into building KRW 3.185 trillion, plant KRW 1.121 trillion, and civil engineering KRW 159 billion, with building accounting for the largest share.

Major construction work includes semiconductor fabs such as Pyeongtaek P5 FAB1 and FAB2, data centers and mixed-use buildings, Seoul urban redevelopment projects, and overseas building projects.

Trading and investment posted revenue of KRW 4.2 trillion and operating profit of KRW 99 billion in the same quarter, supported by essential industrial materials trading and the technology business.

Fashion recorded revenue of KRW 498 billion and operating profit of KRW 20 billion, with sales of its flagship brands' signature products rising in an off-season quarter.

Resort posted revenue of KRW 1.115 trillion and operating profit of KRW 85 billion, helped by expansion of the food service business, including institutional catering and food distribution, and operating efficiency.

On a cumulative basis through the third quarter, building accounted for 96.9% of construction orders, or KRW 18.672 trillion, while civil engineering and plant orders were KRW 74 billion and KRW 529 billion.

The company says it has rated the highest credit grade (AA+) among construction firms, and it competes with Hyundai Engineering & Construction, Daewoo Engineering & Construction, and Samsung E&A for urban redevelopment and high-tech orders.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue moved from KRW 43.2 trillion in 2022 to KRW 41.9 trillion in 2023, down 2.9% year on year, then rose to KRW 42.1 trillion in 2024, up 0.5%, and fell to KRW 40.7 trillion in 2025, down 3.2%. Among 2022 through 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2025 was the smallest.

Operating profit rose from KRW 2.53 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.87 trillion in 2023, up 13.5%, to KRW 2.98 trillion in 2024, up 3.9%, and to KRW 3.29 trillion in 2025, up 10.4%, rising for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Over the same period the operating margin climbed from 5.9% to 6.9%, 7.1%, and 8.1%, so profitability improved even in a year when revenue fell. Attributable net income rose from KRW 2.04 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.44 trillion in 2025, with 2025 up 9.4%.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 3.91 trillion in 2025 and should be read separately from attributable net income. At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 29 trillion and the debt-to-equity ratio was 50.5%, down from 84.5% in 2022.

Operating cash flow was KRW 2.62 trillion in 2022 and KRW 3.02 trillion in 2025.

Quarterly results show that operating profit bottomed in the first quarter of 2026 at KRW 720.4 billion, on revenue of KRW 10.5 trillion, then recovered to KRW 1.03 trillion in the second quarter on revenue of KRW 12 trillion, and rose to KRW 1.14 trillion in the third quarter, the second straight quarter of increases.

Attributable net income peaked at KRW 844.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then fell to KRW 595.5 billion in the second quarter and KRW 558 billion in the third quarter, a second straight quarterly decline. The trailing four-quarter attributable net income total is KRW 2.78 trillion.

The third-quarter operating profit gain was driven mainly by construction, where the margin moved from 3.2% in the first quarter to 5.1% in the second and 6.2% in the third.

Trading and investment operating profit, by contrast, fell from KRW 142 billion in the second quarter to KRW 99 billion in the third and weighed on the total.

05

Industry analysis

Construction demand in Korea is concentrated in large projects such as semiconductor plants, data centers, and urban redevelopment.

A November 2026 report said Samsung E&A raised its annual order outlook from KRW 12 trillion to KRW 19 trillion, and the combined annual order targets of four construction companies, including Samsung C&T's construction unit, Samsung E&A, Daewoo Engineering & Construction, and Ipark Hyundai Development Company, rose from KRW 60.0331 trillion to KRW 86.1949 trillion.

The urban redevelopment market was projected at about KRW 75 trillion for 2026, and Samsung C&T raised its housing order target from KRW 7.7 trillion to as much as KRW 13 trillion during its first-quarter 2026 conference call in April.

The company described high-tech construction as a higher-profit business and expected high-tech orders to exceed its original KRW 6.8 trillion target. The same call noted possible repair and revamping orders for energy plants damaged in the Middle East.

Among peers, Daewoo Engineering & Construction reported a cumulative operating margin of 11.5% through the third quarter, up 6.9 percentage points year on year. Samsung C&T's construction margin rose from 3.2% in the first quarter of 2026 to 6.2% in the third quarter, narrowing the gap with some peers on profitability.

Housing sales conditions and interest rates remain variables that could affect the pace of redevelopment orders.

06

Outlook

Samsung C&T set its annual construction order guidance at KRW 30.1 trillion. Cumulative orders through the third quarter reached KRW 19.275 trillion, so reaching the target requires KRW 10.825 trillion of orders in the fourth quarter.

The company said it plans to push revenue and operating profit higher in the fourth quarter through more high-tech construction volume and full-scale work on overseas data center and energy projects.

Urban redevelopment orders reached KRW 12.8449 trillion after the Mok-dong 13th complex award, leaving KRW 155.1 billion to the KRW 13 trillion housing target. The next major bid cited is Mok-dong 9th complex, worth about KRW 2.6 trillion, with bidding closing on January 11, 2027.

Reports on backlog at the end of the third quarter differ, with Money Today citing KRW 37.7430 trillion and Deal Site citing KRW 37.7740 trillion. Building accounted for 76.0% of the backlog, so growth in building orders could raise the share of building in future revenue.

Overseas, the company is reported to take part in construction of large units 3 and 4 at Cernavoda in Romania, with a total EPC scope of USD 15 to 20 billion and a construction start target in the third quarter of 2027, according to a January 2026 report.

The same report described a Romanian SMR project as awaiting final investment decision approval. If these schedules slip, the timing of order recognition and revenue conversion could move back as well.

08

Bull factors

Improving construction profitability

The construction margin rose from 3.2% in the first quarter of 2026 to 5.1% in the second and 6.2% in the third. Progress on Pyeongtaek P5 FAB1 and FAB2 and the start of data center and mixed-use building work supported the improvement. The company has described high-tech construction as a higher-profit business.

Raised order guidance

The annual construction order guidance was raised from KRW 23.5 trillion to KRW 30.1 trillion, an increase of KRW 6.6 trillion. Cumulative orders of KRW 19.275 trillion through the third quarter equal 64.0% of the target.

The third-quarter total of KRW 8.827 trillion included US T1 FAB2 at KRW 2.8 trillion and Daechi Eunma reconstruction at KRW 2.1 trillion.

Urban redevelopment and nuclear pipeline

Cumulative urban redevelopment orders, including Mok-dong 13th complex, reached KRW 12.8449 trillion, close to the KRW 13 trillion annual housing target. Participation in construction of large units 3 and 4 at Cernavoda in Romania was reported, with an EPC scope of USD 15 to 20 billion. The construction start target is the third quarter of 2027.

Bear factors

Lower trading profit

Trading and investment operating profit in the third quarter was KRW 99 billion, down 30.3% from KRW 142 billion in the prior quarter. Attributable net income fell from KRW 844.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 595.5 billion in the second and KRW 558 billion in the third. Consolidated profit growth leans heavily on construction, which is a weak point for the rest of the group.

Concentration in building orders

Building accounted for 96.9% of cumulative construction orders through the third quarter, while civil engineering orders were KRW 74 billion and plant orders were KRW 529 billion. Building also makes up 76.0% of the backlog. Earnings could react sharply to the timing of a few large building projects, which is a bearish factor.

Revenue trend and fourth-quarter execution

Annual revenue fell from KRW 43.2 trillion in 2022 to KRW 40.7 trillion in 2025. Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose from a year earlier, but reaching the annual order target requires KRW 10.825 trillion of orders in the fourth quarter. A shortfall would leave the guidance target unmet.

Risk factors

Redevelopment timing

Redevelopment orders are recognized in the year a builder is selected. The Mok-dong 9th complex bidding closes on January 11, 2027, and a delay could push large order recognition into the following year. The Mok-dong 13th complex also needed selection within the year, which makes schedule management a key variable.

High-tech project profitability

The construction margin improved from 3.2% in the first quarter of 2026 to 6.2% in the third, but it remains in the low single digits. If progress or cost control slips on high-tech projects, the margin could fall back. Quarterly operating profit can move significantly with construction progress.

Overseas nuclear and SMR projects

In January 2026, the Romanian SMR project was reported to be awaiting final investment decision approval. The construction start target for large units 3 and 4 is the third quarter of 2027. Large overseas EPC projects can see recognition timing shift with schedule, financing terms, and country-specific permitting.

Overall view

Samsung C&T disclosed third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 12 trillion and operating profit of KRW 1.14 trillion, and annual operating profit has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

The construction margin improved from 3.2% in the first quarter of 2026 to 6.2% in the third, and the annual order guidance was raised to KRW 30.1 trillion.

Trading and investment operating profit, however, fell from the second quarter, and attributable net income declined for two straight quarters in the second and third quarters of 2026. Orders are heavily weighted toward building, so the timing of redevelopment and high-tech projects can drive the earnings path.

Annual revenue has declined in several of the years since 2022, which makes profitability improvement the central thread of the results. The next checkpoints are fourth-quarter 2026 orders and preliminary results, the outcome of the Mok-dong 9th complex bid, and the annual operating margin in the 2026 business report.
