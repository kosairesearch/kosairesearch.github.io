02

Business structure

LG Innotek is an electronic components maker whose core businesses are camera modules, semiconductor substrates (Package Solutions), and automotive components (Mobility Solutions).

Third-quarter 2026 segment sales were KRW 5.40 trillion for Optical Solutions, KRW 481.8 billion for Package Solutions, and KRW 481.1 billion for Mobility Solutions, based on provisional figures announced on October 27, 2026.

Optical Solutions accounts for about 84.9% of total sales, and within it camera modules make up 87% and 3D sensing modules 13%.

Package Solutions splits into semiconductor substrates at 69% and display solutions at 31%, with steady demand for RF-SiP and FC-CSP and higher volumes of FC-BGA for PC central processing units at global customers.

Mobility Solutions consists of power solutions at 54%, connectivity solutions at 31%, and lighting solutions at 15%, with vehicle communication modules and lighting driving growth while new businesses such as vehicle AP modules and digital keys added to sales.

On the customer side, the company has long been viewed as highly dependent on Apple for mobile camera modules. To reduce that concentration, the company is pursuing a strategy of expanding the share of semiconductor substrates.

Substrate demand has grown with the spread of AI data centers, and Samsung Electro-Mechanics is also widening its production base in Vietnam to target the same high-value market.

According to the business report, the substrate production facility utilization rate in Package Solutions rose from 63.2% in 2023 to 75.6% in 2024 and 80.8% in 2025.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue rising from KRW 19.6 trillion in 2022 to KRW 20.6 trillion in 2023 (up 5.2% year on year), KRW 21.2 trillion in 2024 (up 2.9%), and KRW 21.9 trillion in 2025 (up 3.3%).

Over the same period operating profit fell from KRW 1.27 trillion to KRW 830.8 billion (down 34.7%), KRW 706 billion (down 15.0%), and KRW 665 billion (down 5.8%). Operating margin moved from 6.5% to 4.0%, 3.3%, and 3.0%, so the business grew in size while margins thinned.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders declined from KRW 976.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 341.3 billion in 2025, and net income including non-controlling interests moved from KRW 979.8 billion to KRW 341.3 billion over the same years.

The debt ratio fell from 129.6% in 2022 to 107.0% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 5.76 trillion and total liabilities of KRW 6.17 trillion at the end of 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 1.51 trillion in 2022, KRW 2.1 trillion in 2023, KRW 1.11 trillion in 2024, and KRW 1.33 trillion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue peaked at KRW 7.61 trillion in the fourth quarter of 2025, up 41.7% from the prior quarter, when operating profit was KRW 324.8 billion.

Operating profit then declined for three consecutive quarters from the first quarter of 2026 through the third quarter of 2026, reaching KRW 295.3 billion, KRW 245.8 billion, and KRW 94.8 billion. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 6.37 trillion, up 18.6% year on year, while operating margin dropped to about 1.5%.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 229.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then fell to KRW 169.7 billion and KRW 95.2 billion. Over the most recent four quarters, that profit totaled KRW 630 billion.

A press report citing a quarterly filing said the substrate materials business recorded cumulative third-quarter 2025 revenue of KRW 1.23 trillion (up 14.3%) and operating profit of KRW 80.2 billion (up 65.0%).

05

Industry analysis

The end markets are moving in two directions. Growth in the smartphone market has slowed compared with the past, but analysts say demand for camera modules keeps rising as premium models compete on high-end specifications.

Because camera performance decides competitiveness in premium smartphones, some observers expect camera upgrades to lift average selling prices. In a note dated November 12, 2026, SK Securities said it had not yet detected any change in orders from major customers.

The same note said major customers had planned production increases next year, but that a fall in set demand could not be ruled out. In semiconductor substrates, expanding AI data center investment has kept supply tight, and industry participants see the structural shortage lasting until around 2028.

Samsung Electro-Mechanics is also widening its production base in Vietnam to strengthen its push into high-value FC-BGA. LG Innotek has named its entry into multilayer, large-area FC-BGA for AI servers as a key task, and the speed at which it broadens its customer base in this market is expected to shape its position.

Securities analysts describe camera modules as still providing a stable earnings base, with substrates emerging as a new growth engine. A October 2025 report said automotive parts would grow mainly through high-value products such as vehicle cameras, lighting, and communication modules.

06

Outlook

The company has said it will accelerate the upgrade of its portfolio toward high-margin, high-value products and push physical AI businesses such as autonomous driving and robotics. It has also said it will concentrate on improving profitability by strengthening core business competitiveness.

In substrates, the stated goal is to move FC-BGA from mass production for PC CPUs into high-end, multilayer, large-area products for AI accelerators and servers. The company has said it is pursuing production plant expansion and reviewing additional investment at home and abroad.

A early 2026 press report said it planned to invest KRW 600 billion in Gumi to expand FC-BGA mass production lines alongside high-value camera equipment investment. The same report said substrate utilization was expected to approach its maximum, prompting a review of further capacity expansion.

A March 2026 report said the company aims to lift annual Package Solutions revenue to around KRW 3 trillion by 2030.

In a note dated November 12, 2026, SK Securities said the packaging substrate unit was nearing the end of long-term supply agreement talks with three North American semiconductor companies and was reviewing the size and timing of capacity investment.

The same note expected the cumulative order backlog for new products, including AP modules, to exceed KRW 20 trillion by the end of this year, up from KRW 19.2 trillion last year.

The next points to confirm are whether the long-term supply agreements are signed, the scale of capacity expansion, and whether substrate expansion translates into higher operating margin.

08

Bull factors

Substrate utilization and customer expansion

Package Solutions sales were KRW 481.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 10.1% year on year (provisional). According to the business report, substrate utilization rose from 63.2% in 2023 to 80.8% in 2025.

A March 2026 report said the company aims to lift annual Package Solutions revenue to about KRW 3 trillion by 2030.

Camera pricing and backlog expectations

In a note dated November 12, 2026, SK Securities said it expects camera module price increases and expansion of the packaging substrate business. The same note said the order backlog for new products, including AP modules, could exceed KRW 20 trillion by year-end.

The company reportedly said the average selling price of its main camera for key models in the second half of 2026 rose from the previous model.

Automotive new businesses and production investment

Mobility Solutions sales were KRW 481.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 6.8% year on year (provisional). Vehicle communication modules and lighting grew, and new businesses such as vehicle AP modules and digital keys contributed to sales.

According to an early 2026 report, the company decided to invest KRW 100 billion in its Gwangju plant to add a production line for AP modules for self-driving vehicles.

09

Bear factors

Margin squeezed by the FX timing gap

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 94.8 billion, down 53.4% from a year earlier, with operating margin near 1.5%. The company attributed the decline to a sharp fall in the won-dollar rate between raw material purchases and product shipments.

Operating profit has declined for three straight quarters since the first quarter of 2026, so higher sales have not translated into profit.

Optical concentration and customer dependence

Optical Solutions accounts for about 84.9% of total sales. Dependence on Apple for mobile camera modules has long been viewed as high. The SK Securities note said a decline in set demand from major customers could not be ruled out.

Capacity investment and profitability proof

For Package Solutions, long-term supply agreement talks with North American companies and the scale of capacity expansion have not been finalized. Multilayer, large-area FC-BGA is an area the company itself has named as a task to address.

In substrates, Samsung Electro-Mechanics is also widening its Vietnam base to target the same high-value market.

10

Risk factors

FX swings and cost timing gap

Exchange-rate differences between raw material purchases and shipments can significantly swing quarterly operating profit. The company said this timing gap pushed third-quarter 2026 operating margin down to about 1.5%. Even without a change in direction, mismatched purchase and shipment timing could repeat the same problem.

Customer concentration and set demand

With Optical Solutions at about 84.9% of sales, changes in new-model timing or unit volumes at major customers feed directly into quarterly results. SK Securities pointed to the possibility of lower set demand from major customers. The degree of customer diversification cannot be firmly established from the disclosed results alone.

Payback period of capacity investment

Expansion at the Gumi FC-BGA line and the Gwangju AP module line requires large investments. Substrate output typically takes time to stabilize yield and reach utilization targets. Whether these expansions translate into a recovery in operating margin remains to be confirmed.

12

Overall view

LG Innotek is growing substrates and automotive parts on top of its large camera module base. Revenue rose for three consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, but operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell over the same period, and operating margin declined from 6.5% to 3.0%.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 6.37 trillion, passing KRW 6 trillion for a third quarter for the first time, but operating profit fell to KRW 94.8 billion, and the company cited the FX timing gap.

Bullish factors include higher substrate utilization and customer expansion, expectations for camera price increases, and new automotive businesses.

Bearish factors include customer concentration with Optical Solutions at about 84.9% of sales, margin pressure from currency swings, and the need to prove profitability of substrate capacity investment.

On valuation, reading the price-to-book multiple together with recent earnings scale shows that whether the earnings recovery continues shapes the interpretation.

The next checkpoints are the provisional fourth-quarter 2026 results, the 2026 annual business report, and decisions on long-term supply agreements and capacity investment. How far substrates and automotive parts offset optical weakness remains the central variable for the medium-term assessment.
