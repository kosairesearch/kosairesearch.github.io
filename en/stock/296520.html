02

Business structure

Gaia was founded in August 2010 as a distributor of premium baby products, toys and kidult brands. The company carries brands such as Linkmom and Stokke and operates 70 stores.

It is classified under wholesale and merchandise brokerage, and its model rests on securing distribution rights for overseas brands and supplying them to stores and online channels. On July 28, 2026, the company announced in an IR notice that it had signed an official distributorship agreement for Korea with Hasbro.

Its product categories are baby products, toys and kidult goods, with toys and baby products appearing to be the core lines. Compared with large listed retailers such as Lotte Shopping, Shinsegae and E-Mart, its scale is small, and its model differs from consumer goods makers such as Cuckoo Homesys and Hanssem.

Its competitiveness therefore depends more on brand acquisition and specialty store operations than on a large retail network. The company's largest shareholder is identified in its IR materials as an affiliate of KOSDAQ-listed Dreambee.

A March 4, 2026 disclosure established a co-CEO structure with Na Won-seok and Park Bo-seon to strengthen accountability.

03

Recent trends

Based on 2025 disclosed results, revenue reached KRW 41.97 billion, up 10.4% from the prior year. Over the same period, the company posted an operating loss of KRW 3.54 billion, reversing from operating profit of KRW 743.89 million in 2024.

The net loss came to KRW 3.95 billion, so losses widened below the operating line as well. The operating margin fell from 1.96% in 2024 to -8.42% in 2025. Revenue in 2024 had risen 20.58% to the KRW 38 billion range.

Equity dropped from the KRW 6.9 billion range in 2024 to the KRW 2.9 billion range in 2025, and the debt ratio rose to 321.34%. Operating cash flow remained positive at roughly KRW 530 million in 2025. Share prices recorded KRW 500 on March 4, 2026, KRW 311 on March 31, KRW 580 on April 8 and KRW 218 on October 29.

As of October 29, 2026, the 52-week high was KRW 1,091 and the 52-week low was KRW 256. On April 8, 2026, the company granted stock options on 138,280 common shares to 32 directors, auditors and employees at an exercise price of KRW 500, exercisable from April 9, 2028 through April 9, 2033.

Outlook

The direction of earnings will first appear in the third-quarter 2026 report and the full-year 2026 results disclosure. Two checkpoints stand out: whether revenue growth can sustain the 10.4% pace of 2025, and whether the operating loss narrows.

The timing and scale at which the Hasbro distributorship shows up in toy sales could become a new growth axis. Whether the operating margin moves toward the 1.96% of 2024 or the -8.42% of 2025 is another benchmark for operating improvement.

On the balance sheet, the key items are whether equity in the KRW 2.9 billion range and the 321.34% debt ratio come down, and whether disclosures on capital raising or debt repayment follow.

The KRW 500 exercise price on the stock options sits above the current price of KRW 239, so how the employee incentive plan aligns with the share price will become visible through disclosures.

On the trading side, whether the investment-warning designation and the trading-halt notice are lifted will change the conditions for market participation. Given Gaia's KONEX listing, the frequency of disclosures and the content of IR activity will shape how easily information can be accessed.

Bull factors

Revenue growth continued

The growth rate slowed from 2024, but top-line expansion continued. Revenue also grew 20.58% in 2024, extending the growth run across two consecutive years. The company's IR materials state that its 2025 revenue growth rate places it in the top 10% of its industry. Steady demand for brand distribution provides the basis for this top-line strength.

Hasbro distributorship signed

On July 28, 2026, Gaia announced in an IR notice that it had signed an official distributorship agreement for Korea with Hasbro. The deal offers a path to broaden the global brand lineup in toys, which matters for the business structure. The contribution to revenue, however, has not yet been disclosed. The speed at which the agreement shows up in results will be visible in upcoming quarterly reports.

Profit history and cash generation

Gaia recorded operating profit of KRW 743.89 million in 2024, showing that the business has been profitable before. Operating cash flow remained positive at roughly KRW 530 million in 2025. Cash generation continuing even during the operating loss period helps cushion the financial impact.

The fact that the business has been in profitable territory provides a reference point for gauging room for margin recovery.

Bear factors

Swing to operating loss

Gaia posted a 2025 operating loss of KRW 3.54 billion, reversing from operating profit of KRW 743.89 million in 2024. The net loss of KRW 3.95 billion widened losses below the operating line. Profitability weakened even as revenue grew, which is the central bearish factor.

Shrinking equity and high leverage

Equity fell from the KRW 6.9 billion range in 2024 to the KRW 2.9 billion range in 2025. The debt ratio stood at 321.34%, and the equity ratio was 23.73%. With equity shrinking after accumulated losses, how the company manages its debt structure is the key variable for financial stability. Until capital-raising disclosures appear, financial burden remains a bearish factor.

Trading restrictions and share price

Gaia was designated an investment-warning stock effective June 16, 2026, and a trading-halt notice followed on June 25. As of October 29, 2026, the 52-week high was KRW 1,091 and the low was KRW 256, while the current price stands at KRW 239.

Given the characteristics of a small-cap KONEX stock, trading liquidity may also be limited. Overlapping trading restrictions narrow the range of market participation.

Risk factors

Macro

A shrinking birth rate reduces the child population, a medium- to long-term variable for baby product demand. The company's own profile positions premium growth within a low-birth-rate environment as its business backdrop. If consumer sentiment weakens, purchases of premium products may decline first. Changes in import costs from currency moves could also pressure margins on overseas brands.

Trading-regime

The exchange notice made clear that trading could be halted if the share price rises further while the stock carries the investment-warning designation. An actual halt would reduce liquidity and could limit price discovery.

Exchange measures calling for investor caution amid heightened market volatility also add to the variables. These regulatory constraints change trading conditions independently of the company's operating performance.

Business and financial

Because the model relies on distribution agreements with overseas brands, contract terms and renewals can flow directly into revenue. On March 4, 2026, the company shifted to a co-CEO structure, changing its management framework.

Management changes and contract administration are occurring at the same time that equity has shrunk. If operating losses persist, equity could erode further.

08

Overall view

Gaia is a company where 2025 revenue growth coincided with a swing to operating loss and shrinking equity. Its 2024 profit record and positive operating cash flow in 2025 provide a basis for gauging room for profitability recovery.

On the other hand, the operating margin fell to -8.42% and the debt ratio rose to 321.34%, so financial stability is viewed as a bearish factor. The Hasbro distributorship is a positive for toy lineup expansion, but its revenue contribution has not yet been disclosed.

On the trading side, the June 2026 investment-warning designation and trading-halt notice remain part of the record. The 27.87% stake held by CEO Na Won-seok and the co-CEO structure show that the management accountability framework has changed.

Taken together, this is a period in which growth in sales and weakening finances point in different directions, and the direction of operating results and changes in equity in upcoming quarterly reports are the key checkpoints.
