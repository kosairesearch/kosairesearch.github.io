JT: Q3 2026 Loss Interrupts a Two-Quarter Rebound

JT posted profits in Q1 and Q2 2026, but returned to a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 6.45 billion in Q3 2026.

  1. 1

  2. 2

  4. 4

02

Business structure

JT is a KOSDAQ-listed company focused on final test equipment for back-end semiconductor processes. It was founded in 1998 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2006. Its main products are Burn-In Sorters, Test Handlers and memory module test equipment, supplied to Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and Micron.

Because the customer base is concentrated among memory makers, results move with memory investment cycles and the timing of individual equipment orders. The company itself cited a decline in Handler equipment sales as the main driver of its 2025 earnings swing.

Revenue shares by segment are best checked in the segment note of the periodic reports. JT is also diversifying: it is preparing to supply BLU equipment for AI semiconductors to global OSAT companies, is developing a Wafer Probe Station, and is collaborating on technology for HBM-related equipment.

These points follow the company overview compiled by WISEreport. Other semiconductor-sector listed companies include HANMI Semiconductor, HanaMicron, TSE, PSK and LEENO Industrial. How far their products and customers overlap with JT varies by segment, so item-by-item comparison is needed.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 81.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 47.2 billion in 2023 (down 42.4% year on year), rose 5.0% to KRW 49.5 billion in 2024, and then fell 22.5% to KRW 38.4 billion in 2025. 2022 was the largest year in the period and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit dropped from KRW 15.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.96 billion in 2023 (down 80.4%) and KRW 638 million in 2024 (down 78.5%), and the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 2.2 billion in 2025, a swing into deficit.

The operating margin moved from 18.4% in 2022 to 6.3% in 2023, 1.3% in 2024 and -5.7% in 2025. Controlling-shareholder net income declined from KRW 15 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.49 billion in 2023 and KRW 4.18 billion in 2024, then turned into a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 390 million in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 4.17 billion in 2024, a KRW 10 million gap from the controlling-shareholder figure, and a net loss of KRW 390 million in 2025. Operating cash flow fell sharply to KRW 1.38 billion in 2025 from KRW 16.5 billion in 2024.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 64.3 billion, of which KRW 63.8 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 35.7 billion.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 12.9 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 12.2 billion in Q4 2025 (down 5.9% from the prior quarter), peaked at KRW 23.2 billion in Q1 2026 (up 91.0%), and then fell to KRW 13.9 billion in Q2 2026 (down 40.2%) and KRW 11.6 billion in Q3 2026 (down 16.6% from the prior quarter and down 10.4% year on year).

Operating profit went from KRW 44.28 million in Q3 2025 and KRW 31.49 million in Q4 2025 to KRW 1.69 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 2.34 billion in Q2 2026, before an operating loss of KRW 10.1 million in Q3 2026.

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 3.12 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 5.69 billion in Q2 2026, but Q3 2026 posted a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 6.45 billion.

The gap between the small operating loss and the larger net loss is best traced in the non-operating and tax items disclosed in the Q3 2026 quarterly report notes. According to WISEreport's aggregation, first-half 2026 revenue rose 178.5% year on year and both operating and net income turned positive. Controlling-shareholder net income for the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 3.45 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The main end demand is high-performance memory driven by the spread of AI and data centers. WISEreport's company monitor notes that AI and data center growth is lifting demand for high-performance memory.

The same source says order flow for memory module test equipment and Burn-In Sorters, JT's core products, is tied to rising DRAM demand, so memory investment trends largely determine equipment orders.

Memory makers' capex plans and DRAM pricing directly affect JT's order flow, so quarterly capex announcements from memory makers are useful to track. In back-end test equipment, JT is positioned around memory module testing and Burn-In Sorters.

Other listed semiconductor-sector companies include HANMI Semiconductor, HanaMicron, TSE, PSK and LEENO Industrial. JT's revenue scale is small relative to back-end equipment peers such as HANMI Semiconductor, and in 2025 its results were hit by lower Handler equipment sales.

Competitors' order sizes and market shares fall outside the scope of the disclosed data, so no quantitative comparison is made. The bullish industry factor is a recovery in memory investment, and the bearish factor is the possibility of memory investment cuts. Both can shift the timing of equipment orders and deserve equal weight.

Outlook

The outlook items that can be confirmed are order flow and preparation for new businesses. According to a Daily Union report based on a single-sale supply contract disclosure filed on October 16, 2026, equipment deliveries of about KRW 5.68 billion were identified.

That figure is an estimate based on external supplementary data, so the contract amount and counterparty should be checked against the original DART filing. The report identifies the counterparty as a Micron-affiliated entity in India.

WISEreport's company monitor notes that JT is preparing to supply BLU equipment for AI semiconductors to global OSAT companies and is developing a Wafer Probe Station. Technology collaboration on HBM-related equipment is also under way.

Overseas new factory investment is cited as a long-term growth driver, but no scale or timing has been confirmed in disclosed figures. Revenue timing and scale for the new businesses have not yet appeared in periodic report figures.

Because revenue declined in both Q2 and Q3 2026, whether Q4 revenue recovers in line with contract disclosures is the near-term checkpoint. The 2026 annual results will be set by the year-end closing date of December 31, 2026, and final annual figures will come from the 2026 annual report.

Bull factors

Wider memory test equipment orders

WISEreport's company monitor cites large memory module test equipment orders as a driver of the first-half 2026 improvement. The same source also points to wider Burn-In Sorter orders tied to rising DRAM demand. First-half 2026 revenue was aggregated at a 178.5% year-on-year increase.

If these orders continue to convert into quarterly revenue, memory equipment will remain the main lever for results.

Return to profitability

Q1 2026 revenue peaked at KRW 23.2 billion, with operating profit of KRW 1.69 billion. In Q2, operating profit was KRW 2.34 billion and controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 5.69 billion. Unlike the loss trend through 2025, profits appeared in two consecutive quarters. This recovery is a fact confirmed in the disclosed results.

Diversification efforts

JT is preparing to supply BLU equipment for AI semiconductors to global OSAT companies. Development of a Wafer Probe Station and technology collaboration on HBM-related equipment are running in parallel. WISEreport summarizes these as long-term growth drivers. Revenue contribution from these businesses has not yet appeared in periodic report figures.

Bear factors

Renewed loss in Q3

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 11.6 billion, down 16.6% from the prior quarter and down 10.4% year on year. The company posted an operating loss of KRW 10.1 million, and the controlling-shareholder net loss was KRW 6.45 billion.

The composition of non-operating items needs to be checked in the quarterly report, but the net loss is far larger than the operating loss. The profit recovery lasted only one quarter.

2025 annual loss and sharp cash flow drop

JT swung to an operating loss of KRW 2.2 billion in 2025, with a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 390 million. The company cited lower Handler equipment sales as the main cause. Operating cash flow fell sharply from KRW 16.5 billion to KRW 1.38 billion. The year showed both weaker earnings and weaker cash generation.

Balance sheet strain and customer concentration

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 55.5% at the end of 2025 from 40.9% in 2024, with total liabilities of KRW 35.7 billion. Customers are concentrated among memory makers including Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and Micron. If memory investment slows, orders and revenue could move together.

Risk factors

Order and revenue volatility

Revenue fell from KRW 81.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 38.4 billion in 2025, and after the Q1 2026 peak it declined in both Q2 and Q3. Because quarterly revenue swings with order timing, a single quarter is not enough to judge the trend. Whether Q4 revenue recovers in line with contract disclosures is the nearest checkpoint.

Customer concentration and memory capex cycle

A cut in investment or a change in schedule by memory makers such as Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and Micron can affect equipment orders quickly. The 2025 deterioration linked to lower Handler sales shows dependence on specific product lines. Customer mix and segment revenue shares should be checked in the periodic report notes.

New business execution and financial capacity

The BLU equipment, Wafer Probe Station and HBM collaboration have not yet appeared in periodic report figures. Whether these businesses can fill the gap in existing inspection equipment revenue depends on confirmed revenue contribution.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 55.5% at the end of 2025, and operating cash flow fell to KRW 1.38 billion. Financial capacity will need to be checked in future disclosures.

Overall view

JT's revenue fell from KRW 81.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 38.4 billion in 2025, and the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 2.2 billion in 2025. The operating margin declined from 18.4% in 2022 to -5.7% in 2025.

Bullish factors are wider orders for memory module test equipment and Burn-In Sorters and diversification into BLU equipment for AI semiconductors. Both sets of factors deserve equal weight, and current data alone cannot establish which side dominates. The next checkpoints are the preliminary 2026 annual results, the final operating margin and controlling-shareholder net income in the 2026 annual report, and the size and customer mix of order disclosures.
