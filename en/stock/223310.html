02

Business structure

Satoshi Holdings was founded in 2011 as a maker of electronic parts and listed on KOSDAQ in December 2015, and it now focuses on online commerce for health functional foods and on sales of women's underwear and apparel.

Most of its first-half 2026 sales of KRW 8.1 billion came from these core businesses, according to media reports, while sales tied to new businesses such as space and AI totaled KRW 7 million, all from drones.

In 2025 the company set up a food and beverage subsidiary and acquired or formed subsidiaries for digital-asset finance. Its subsidiary BitPal, a multichain stablecoin payment platform, officially added the Japanese yen stablecoin JPYC on August 20, 2026, with Kaia and Polygon as its priority chains.

Another subsidiary's FIBER platform, which detects optical-network anomalies in AI data centers, was selected as a full member of the NVIDIA Inception program, the company said on May 15, 2026. As of September 30, 2026 the group reported four consolidated subsidiaries.

Shareholders approved a change of the corporate name to Hankook Advanced Solutions on November 10, 2026, and the English name is also changing to Hankook Advanced Solutions.

On August 26, 2026 the company sold its entire stake in a former subsidiary for KRW 10.5 billion, but media reported that no cash came in because the proceeds were offset against convertible bonds held by the buyers.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, sales rose from KRW 20.7 billion in 2022 to a peak of KRW 27.5 billion in 2023 (up 32.8% year on year), then fell to KRW 21.7 billion in 2024 (down 21.0%) and KRW 18.9 billion in 2025 (down 13.1%), two straight years of decline.

Operating profit was KRW 424 million in 2022 and KRW 294 million in 2023 (down 30.7%), turned into a KRW 3.22 billion operating loss in 2024, and widened to a KRW 7.48 billion loss in 2025.

The operating margin moved from 2.0% in 2022 and 1.1% in 2023 to -14.8% in 2024 and -39.6% in 2025, which reads as a cost base that did not adjust as sales shrank.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.27 billion in 2022, KRW 1.32 billion in 2023, KRW 10.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 31.5 billion in 2025, which is four consecutive years of losses.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 15.2% in 2022 and 14.5% in 2023 to 67.1% in 2024 and 300.8% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 17.3 billion, of which KRW 17 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 51.9 billion.

Operating cash flow went from a KRW 2.37 billion inflow in 2022 to outflows of KRW 784 million in 2023, KRW 2.93 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.12 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 4.47 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 4.64 billion, then KRW 6.24 billion in the first quarter of 2026, before falling 69.6% to KRW 1.9 billion in the second quarter; the third quarter of 2026 came in at KRW 2.35 billion, down 47.4% year on year.

Operating losses have been recorded for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, narrowing from KRW 2.22 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.44 billion, KRW 1.18 billion and KRW 977 million in the first three quarters of 2026.

The second quarter of 2026 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 885 million appears to reflect non-operating items larger than the KRW 1.18 billion operating loss, and the third quarter swung back to a net loss of KRW 5.15 billion.

The four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 add up to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 28.6 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Online sales of health functional foods depend heavily on platform exposure and marketing spend, and whether this competitive pressure explains the sales decline since 2024 cannot be determined from disclosures alone.

The two areas the company has pushed in 2026 are AI data-center optical networking and stablecoin payments. In optical networking, the company says its FIBER platform traces how optical-network anomalies affect AI training jobs and recommends when and which equipment to replace.

The company also stresses that the platform can detect failures in next-generation co-packaged optics (CPO), which it describes as hard to catch with existing tools. Selection as a full NVIDIA Inception member is presented by the company as a rare achievement for a Korean optical-infrastructure operator.

In stablecoin payments, BitPal is described as a multichain structure not tied to any single blockchain or issuer, and the company plans to connect won-denominated stablecoins once Korean banks and fintech firms issue them, regardless of issuer.

The issuers and timing of such stablecoins are external variables outside the company's control. Among holding-type listed companies, Satoshi Holdings stands out for frequent subsidiary formation and divestment, which has reshaped its portfolio repeatedly.

Operating cash flow has been negative since 2023, and in 2026 the company turned to convertible bonds and a rights offering for funding. Its position in the market reads as a shift of focus from health-food commerce toward AI infrastructure and digital payments.

The company said it plans to extend FIBER's support to NVIDIA's next-generation CPO Ethernet switch, Spectrum-X Photonics, which is scheduled to ship in the second half of 2026, and to run pilot programs with domestic cloud operators and global AI infrastructure companies.

For BitPal, the company plans to connect won-denominated stablecoins to its global payment service quickly once they are issued by domestic banks and fintech or IT companies.

A third-party rights offering totaling KRW 23.7 billion was announced in September 2026, with a first payment of KRW 7 billion reported as received before September 10, 2026. The company said the proceeds would strengthen financial soundness while expanding new businesses.

Media reports say the largest shareholder changed to an investment partnership holding 35.97% after the offering.

On the audit side, media reported that the first-half 2026 review report carried a disclaimer of opinion for lack of audit evidence, and the company said it is working to obtain an appropriate opinion on a re-audit and the regular audit.

The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, which will be the first place where fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results and the annual operating margin are confirmed.

Because new-business sales were very small in the first half of 2026, whether core sales recover and whether new-business revenue grows will determine how far commercialization has progressed.

Frequent subsidiary transactions and changes in the largest shareholder also raise the possibility of further corrections to disclosures.

Funding and dilution

In March 2026 the company issued convertible bonds with a 4% coupon, and in September 2026 it raised funds through a third-party rights offering. Conversion or offset of such bonds can increase the share count and leave cash flow gaps at the same time, both of which affect the value of existing shareholders' stakes.

Timing of new-business monetization

Revenue from AI optical networking and stablecoin payments was extremely small in the first half of 2026, so time will be needed before it can offset the decline in core sales.

The NVIDIA Inception selection and JPYC support are technology and partnership milestones, and the company has not given a timeline for monetization. Stablecoin payments can also be affected by changes in regulation across countries.

Overall view

Satoshi Holdings' sales have declined since 2024, and the 2025 operating loss of KRW 7.48 billion and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 31.5 billion widened the deficit.

In 2026, however, quarterly operating losses have narrowed for three straight quarters, and the second quarter posted a net profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

On the strength side, the company has improved quarterly results, raised capital through a rights offering totaling KRW 23.7 billion, and reported technology and partnership milestones in new businesses such as the NVIDIA Inception selection and JPYC support.

On the weakness side, new businesses accounted for an extremely small share of first-half 2026 sales of KRW 8.1 billion, the debt-to-equity ratio stands at 300.8%, and media reports of a disclaimer of opinion and convertible-bond offset transactions overlap with funding and governance concerns.

Trailing four-quarter net losses attributable to controlling shareholders total KRW 28.6 billion, no earnings-based multiple can be calculated, and no dividend has been paid.

The next checkpoints are whether Spectrum-X Photonics ships and FIBER's support expands by late December 2026, and the audit opinion and annual operating margin in the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027.
