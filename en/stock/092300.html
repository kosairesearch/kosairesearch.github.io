Auto PCB Maker: Quarterly Profit Recovers, Margins Thin

HYUNWOO INDUSTRIAL CO, whose automotive-electronics PCBs were 86.8% of first-quarter 2026 revenue, extended its quarterly recovery with Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 88.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 6.27 billion, although its annual operating margin stayed in a 3.8%-5.9% range over 2022-2025.

    Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 88.5 billion, up 17.3% from the previous quarter and 37.7% from a year earlier. It is the second straight quarterly increase after the Q1 2026 low.

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

HYUNWOO INDUSTRIAL CO is a KOSDAQ-listed maker of PCBs used in automotive electronics and digital consumer devices such as LCD, LED and OLED products.

In its first-quarter 2026 report, automotive electronics (telematics, BMS and similar) brought in KRW 52.7 billion, IT products (LCD, TV and similar) KRW 5.6 billion (9.3%), and networking equipment and other items KRW 1.2 billion (2.0%). Automotive electronics accounted for 86.8% of that quarter's revenue.

The automotive share has risen steadily. In 2021 it was 50.94%, against 41.02% for display products. On a standalone basis for the first nine months of 2025, automotive electronics were 87.5% of sales and IT products 8.1%.

On customers, a March 2022 technical analysis report said the company supplies PCBs to the vehicle electronics division of LG Electronics. By destination, a FnGuide company summary shows 53.3% domestic and 46.7% export sales.

In new areas, the company is preparing to bid for defense work on the strength of its AS9100 certification and is discussing wider supply of networking equipment centered on the Indian market. The largest shareholder, Byungsun Moon, and two related persons held 32.19%, with treasury shares at 2.01%.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 209.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 237.3 billion in 2023 (up 13.1%), fell to KRW 206.2 billion in 2024 (down 13.1%), and reached KRW 237.4 billion in 2025 (up 15.1%), the largest of the 2022-2025 years.

Operating profit was largest in 2023 at KRW 13.9 billion and smallest in 2024 at KRW 7.75 billion (down 44.3%), then came to KRW 11 billion in 2025 (up 42.5%). The operating margin moved from 4.0% in 2022 to 5.9% in 2023, 3.8% in 2024 and 4.7% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 10.4 billion in 2023 (up 122.9%), then fell to KRW 8.34 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.56 billion in 2025, a decline in each of the two years from 2024 through 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was identical to the attributable figure in the reported numbers. Why 2025 net income fell while operating profit rose is a point to check in the notes to the annual report.

Quarterly, revenue and operating profit slipped from KRW 64.3 billion and KRW 5.15 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 60.8 billion and KRW 2.17 billion in Q4 2025 (operating profit down 57.9% from the previous quarter), then moved to KRW 60.7 billion and KRW 3.36 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 75.4 billion (up 24.2%) and KRW 4.31 billion (up 28.2%) in Q2 2026.

In Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 88.5 billion (up 17.3% from the previous quarter and 37.7% year on year) and operating profit KRW 6.27 billion (up 45.4% and 21.7%), so revenue rose for two straight quarters after the Q1 2026 low and operating profit rose for three straight quarters from Q1 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 944 million in Q4 2025 to KRW 3.99 billion and KRW 6.02 billion in the first two quarters of 2026, then dipped to KRW 5.35 billion in Q3 (down 11.1% from the previous quarter, up 19.6% year on year); the four-quarter total of KRW 16.3 billion is more than double the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 7.56 billion.

A May 14, 2026 article in Etoday reported that results hurt by the EV slowdown and customer inventory adjustments were improving in 2026 as key customers normalized production and automotive volumes recovered.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio fell from 119.6% in 2022 to 75.8% in 2025; at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 114.1 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 86.5 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 762 million in 2022, KRW 20.2 billion in 2023, KRW 18.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 23.8 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for HYUNWOO INDUSTRIAL CO depends on the automotive-electronics market, and especially on the production plans of the large customers that build electronics modules.

The vehicle solutions division of LG Electronics, a customer named in earlier public materials, posted Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 2,826.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 190.3 billion, and LG Electronics said on October 28, 2026 that both figures were the highest ever for a third quarter.

The company attributed this to the conversion of its order backlog into sales and growth in in-vehicle infotainment. LG Electronics also said that it expects only a limited recovery in automaker demand in the fourth quarter but will keep growing sales on its secured backlog.

To cope with the EV slowdown, LG Electronics is diversifying customers and products, and is preparing its Hungarian plant for mass production targeted for February 2027. These figures are LG Electronics' own results and do not map one-to-one onto HYUNWOO INDUSTRIAL CO's revenue.

HYUNWOO INDUSTRIAL CO was affected through 2025 by the EV slowdown and customer inventory adjustments, and volumes have been recovering in 2026. On competition, a 2016 press profile placed the company in the domestic top ten by share behind larger PCB makers, which suggests a smaller niche supplier position.

Outlook

The company has not been found to publish quantitative earnings guidance. What can be confirmed is its investment plan: it plans to invest KRW 9 billion in production facilities and equipment during 2026 and a total of KRW 18 billion in capital spending over the next three years to raise capacity and efficiency.

A company official said on May 14, 2026 that the AS9100 certification obtained at the end of 2025 opened the way to defense-related bids and that it is working with customers.

The same official said defense work is more profitable than ordinary automotive electronics and that entry through aerospace and defense contractors is being prepared.

The medium-term strategy also includes talks on wider supply of networking equipment centered on India and a stronger high-value PCB mix built around automotive, defense and robotics. The defense bids and networking equipment have not yet been disclosed as orders or revenue.

On shareholder returns, the board decided on the 2025 year-end cash dividend on February 27, 2026, with payment scheduled for April 21, 2026.

Upstream, LG Electronics is targeting mass production at its Hungarian plant in February 2027, so the effect of customer production schedules on the company's volumes is the next thing to watch.

The operating margin was 4.0% in 2022, 5.9% in 2023, 3.8% in 2024 and 4.7% in 2025, staying in the low-to-mid single digits. Operating profit swung widely from year to year, including a 44.3% drop to KRW 7.75 billion in 2024. In Q4 2025 it also fell 57.9% from the previous quarter to KRW 2.17 billion. Whether the quarterly recovery is sustainable remains a question given the short run of data.

Automotive electronics were 86.8% of revenue in Q1 2026, so the company is directly exposed to swings in vehicle and EV demand. LG Electronics expects only a limited recovery in automaker demand in Q4 2026. The weakness through 2025 was also tied to customer inventory adjustments. Detailed customer shares need to be checked in the filings.

Net income trend and weak share price

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell in each of the two years from 2024 through 2025 and dropped again by 11.1% from the previous quarter in Q3 2026. Why net income fell during periods of rising operating profit is a point to check in the notes.

Valueline put the six-month share price change at minus 32.58% as of October 23, 2026. The lack of a clear path from profit recovery to the share price could be cited as a weakness.

Risk factors

Industry and demand risk

The EV slowdown and customer inventory adjustments weighed on results through 2025. LG Electronics' view that automaker demand will recover only to a limited extent in Q4 2026 suggests volume growth could slow. Changes in customer production schedules feed straight into utilization and quarterly results.

Cost and currency risk

Exports were 46.7% of sales in the FnGuide company summary, so the company is exposed to currency moves. LG Electronics also named logistics costs and exchange rates as major variables in its Q3 2026 conference call. Raw-material prices and exchange rates can move a margin that has stayed in a 3.8%-5.9% range.

New business and execution risk

The defense and aerospace bids, Indian networking equipment and robotics projects are all in preparation or discussion. Capital spending of KRW 9 billion in 2026 and KRW 18 billion over three years could add depreciation burden if revenue does not follow. The timing and size of new-business orders have not been disclosed.

Overall view

HYUNWOO INDUSTRIAL CO is an automotive-electronics PCB supplier whose quarterly recovery extended to Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 88.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 6.27 billion.

On an annual basis, operating profit of KRW 13.9 billion in 2023 was the largest of the 2022-2025 years, and 2025 recovered to KRW 11 billion. The debt ratio fell to 75.8% in 2025 and operating cash flow rose to KRW 23.8 billion, so the financial base is steadier than before.
