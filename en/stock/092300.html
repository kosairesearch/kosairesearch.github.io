02

Business structure

HYUNWOO INDUSTRIAL CO is a printed circuit board (PCB) manufacturer founded in 1987 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2007. Its core products are multilayer PCBs for automotive electronics, applied in telematics and battery management systems.

Of the KRW 60.7 billion in Q1 2026 sales, automotive electronics products were KRW 52.7 billion, or 86.8%, IT products such as LCD and TV boards were KRW 5.6 billion (9.3%), and other network equipment items were KRW 1.2 billion (2.0%).

Exports in the same quarter were KRW 24.8 billion (40.9%) and domestic sales were KRW 35.9 billion (59.1%). On a cumulative basis through the third quarter of 2025, the automotive electronics share widened from 78.1% a year earlier to 87.5%.

Major customers are LG Group and Hyundai Mobis, and sales in home appliance and display applications move with order volumes from LG Group affiliates. According to a May 2026 report, the company obtained the AS9100 aerospace and defense quality management certification and is strengthening sales to defense customers.

It also participates as a supplier in a robot development project led by an automotive customer, joining PCB specification reviews from the design stage. In network equipment, it is discussing expanded supply with customers in India.

The company's stated strategy is to raise the share of automotive and defense PCBs, which are technically more demanding than general-purpose boards, in order to lift profitability.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 209.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 237.3 billion in 2023, up 13.1%, fell 13.1% to KRW 206.2 billion in 2024, and recovered to KRW 237.4 billion in 2025, up 15.1%. Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year and 2024 was the smallest.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 13.9 billion in 2023, up 67.1%, fell 44.3% to KRW 7.75 billion in 2024, and recovered to KRW 11 billion in 2025, up 42.5%. The operating margin moved from 4.0% in 2022 to 5.9% in 2023, 3.8% in 2024 and 4.7% in 2025, so it has not returned to the 2023 level.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 4.66 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.4 billion in 2023, up 122.9%, then declined 19.7% to KRW 8.34 billion in 2024 and 9.3% to KRW 7.56 billion in 2025, a two-year decline.

Net income including non-controlling interests shows the same annual amounts in each year, so the two measures do not diverge in this period. Operating cash flow improved from KRW 762 million in 2022 to KRW 20.2 billion in 2023, KRW 18.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 23.8 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 114.1 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 86.5 billion.

Quarterly, sales of KRW 64.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.15 billion in Q3 2025 gave way to Q4 2025 sales of KRW 60.8 billion, down 5.3% quarter on quarter, and operating profit of KRW 2.17 billion, down 57.9%.

Q1 2026 sales of KRW 60.7 billion marked the low point, followed by KRW 75.4 billion in Q2 2026, up 24.2%, and KRW 88.5 billion in Q3 2026, up 17.3%, while operating profit rose for three consecutive quarters to KRW 3.36 billion, KRW 4.31 billion and KRW 6.27 billion.

Because sales growth of 37.7% year on year outpaced operating profit growth of 21.7%, the margin in Q3 2026 was lower than a year earlier. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders recovered from KRW 944 million in Q4 2025 to KRW 6.02 billion in Q2 2026 before falling 11.1% to KRW 5.35 billion in Q3 2026. The sum of the last four quarters' net income attributable to controlling shareholders is KRW 16.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Automotive PCBs in Korea are a high-mix, low-volume order-based business in which manufacturers produce to designs supplied by customers, and product life cycles tend to be relatively long. The automotive electronics share of HYUNWOO INDUSTRIAL CO sales rose from 50.94% in 2021 to roughly 86% in 2025.

One news report described the spread of electric vehicles and autonomous driving as a structure that expands demand for high-layer, high-reliability PCBs, and this is that outlet's view rather than a settled fact.

A securities report from April 2024 pointed to the order backlog of LG Electronics' vehicle component division exceeding KRW 100 trillion as a reason to raise expectations for the company's earnings growth. On the demand side, order volumes from LG Group affiliates and automaker production plans largely drive results.

Peers in the PCB sector include Korea Circuit and Isupetasys, and because HYUNWOO INDUSTRIAL CO has a high automotive weighting, its sensitivity to the automaker production cycle is relatively large.

Larger electronic components companies such as LG Innotek and Samsung Electro-Mechanics operate broader product portfolios, so their business structures differ. The 40.9% export share in Q1 2026 indicates that overseas demand adds to a customer base that is weighted toward domestic sales.

Bull factors

Automotive-centered mix moving toward higher margins

Automotive electronics products accounted for 86.8% of Q1 2026 sales, and on a cumulative basis through Q3 2025 the share widened from 78.1% a year earlier to 87.5%. The company states that it aims to lift profitability by increasing the share of technically demanding automotive and defense PCBs. Its AS9100 certification is presented as the starting point for sales to defense customers.

Three consecutive quarters of operating profit gains

Operating profit rose for three consecutive quarters after the Q4 2025 low, reaching KRW 3.36 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 4.31 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 6.27 billion in Q3 2026. Q3 2026 sales were KRW 88.5 billion, up 37.7% year on year. Because operating profit grew 21.7%, slower than sales, the margin improvement was limited.

Lower leverage and stronger operating cash generation

The debt ratio fell from 119.6% in 2022 to 75.8% in 2025. Operating cash flow grew from KRW 762 million in 2022 to KRW 23.8 billion in 2025.

Total equity of KRW 114.1 billion at the end of 2025 is entirely attributable to controlling shareholders, and the balance sheet gives more room for defense certification and equipment investment.

Bear factors

Two consecutive years of lower net income

Sales fell 13.1% in 2024, and operating profit dropped 44.3% to KRW 7.75 billion. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined to KRW 8.34 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.56 billion in 2025. The 2025 operating margin of 4.7% remains below the 5.9% of 2023, so profitability has not fully recovered.

Concentration on a small set of customers

Major customers are LG Group and Hyundai Mobis, and sales in home appliance and display applications move with order volumes from LG Group affiliates. A drop in customer orders or a change in automaker production plans could widen sales swings.

With automotive electronics around 86% of sales, the company is highly exposed to the automotive production cycle.

Sharp Q4 2025 drop and a Q3 2026 pullback in net income

Operating profit in Q4 2025 fell 57.9% quarter on quarter to KRW 2.17 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 78.9% to KRW 944 million. In Q3 2026, net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined 11.1% quarter on quarter to KRW 5.35 billion. Large quarterly swings and a recovery that so far spans only a few quarters are the main bearish points.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration risk

A large share of sales is tied to a small number of customers, including LG Group and Hyundai Mobis. Changes in order volumes or vehicle model strategies at these customers would directly affect sales and capacity utilization.

Whether customer diversification is progressing should be checked in the customer breakdown of the regular reports.

Earnings volatility risk

Operating profit moved from KRW 13.9 billion in 2023 to KRW 7.75 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 11 billion in 2025, a large swing. Quarterly operating profit fell to KRW 2.17 billion in Q4 2025. Because the profit base is small, quarterly results can shift sharply with one-off costs or changes in input costs.

Uncertain commercialization of new businesses

The defense business is at the bidding stage following AS9100 certification, the robot project is at the supplier development stage, and network equipment supply is under discussion with customers in India.

The timing and size of revenue from these businesses have not yet been confirmed through disclosures, leaving uncertainty over when they will contribute to results.

Overall view

HYUNWOO INDUSTRIAL CO is a specialized manufacturer that depends on automotive electronics PCBs for most of its sales, and both annual sales and operating profit recovered in 2025.

In 2026 sales rose for two consecutive quarters and operating profit for three, with Q3 2026 sales of KRW 88.5 billion, up 37.7% year on year. On the other hand, net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined in 2024 and 2025, and the operating margin has not returned to the 2023 level.

The debt ratio fell from 119.6% in 2022 to 75.8% in 2025, and operating cash flow increased. The defense certification and the robot and network projects have not yet reached the results stage, so their scale needs to be confirmed.

Customer concentration and quarterly earnings volatility remain the main bearish factors. The next checkpoints are the segment sales and full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and the outcome of defense bids.
