Report overview

HPA Sales Lift Quarterly Profit; Year-End Delivery in Focus

Yest's high-pressure annealing (HPA) equipment sales began to show up in third-quarter 2026 results, lifting quarterly operating profit to a company record, while annual earnings over 2022–2025 still rest on a thin base.

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Yest is a specialist in semiconductor and display thermal-processing equipment, founded in March 2000. Based on the third-quarter 2026 quarterly report, the semiconductor and display equipment segment, covering chillers, furnaces, chambers and pressure-cure systems, accounted for the largest share of sales at 82.70%.

Semiconductor infrastructure parts such as heating jackets made up 16.09%, and semiconductor process parts such as diamond wheels made up 1.14%. Within equipment, furnaces and NEOCON systems are reported to carry a high share of sales.

NEOCON units control humidity inside the EFEM front-end module, and their use is expanding from foundry customers into memory. High-pressure annealing (HPA) equipment performs heat treatment under high pressure, and supply for foundry and NAND flash customers began in 2026.

The HPA tool processes 125 wafers at a time, versus about 75 for competing tools, which is viewed as a capacity advantage. Reported major customers include Samsung Electronics, Samsung Display and SK hynix.

A NEOCON supply contract with Samsung Electronics signed on March 9, 2026 had a contract value of KRW 8 billion 1,782 million (8.41% of recent revenue). HPSP had long been the de facto monopolist in the HPA market, and in August 2023 it filed a patent infringement suit against Yest.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results started in 2022 with revenue of KRW 76 billion and an operating loss of KRW 16.9 billion. Revenue rose to KRW 79.8 billion in 2023 (up 5.0% year on year), but an operating loss of KRW 411 million persisted, with the loss narrowing.

In 2024, revenue reached KRW 100.1 billion (up 25.4%) and operating profit was KRW 11.3 billion, a swing to profit; 2024 was the largest revenue year in the 2022–2025 period. In 2025, revenue was KRW 87.1 billion (down 13.0%) and operating profit KRW 3.79 billion (down 66.3%).

Operating margins were -22.2% in 2022, -0.5% in 2023, 11.3% in 2024 and 4.4% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 10.4 billion in 2024, a swing to profit, and a net loss of KRW 576 million in 2025, a return to loss.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 11.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 666 million in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder measure.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 136 billion, of which KRW 132 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 90.4 billion; the debt-to-equity ratio rose from 62.5% in 2024 to 66.5% in 2025.

Operating cash flow moved from KRW 8.5 billion in 2024 to -KRW 7.44 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 18.7 billion in the third quarter of 2025 with operating profit of KRW 1.11 billion, and revenue has increased for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

The fourth quarter of 2025 recorded an operating loss of KRW 2.39 billion, and the first quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 3.06 billion. Operating profit was KRW 4.57 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 11 billion in the third quarter, an increase in two consecutive quarters.

Korean business daily Hankyung reported that the third-quarter operating profit was a record for any quarter since the company's founding in 2000, and cited HPA equipment revenue recognition and higher NEOCON orders as drivers.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for that quarter was KRW 7.96 billion, up 350.5% from the prior quarter.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for semiconductor equipment moves sharply with customers' capital spending, and Yest's results follow the same pattern. In 2025, earnings softened as spending by end-market customers was cut, while in 2026 investment in HBM by a major customer was reported to be resuming, supporting equipment demand.

Industry observers note that memory makers run high-volume production of fewer product types, which can favor equipment with high throughput.

As fine-pitch semiconductor processes adopt gate-all-around structures, the need for high-pressure heat treatment could grow, according to an analysis reported by a Korean news agency. In the HPA market, HPSP has long been the dominant player, and Yest is classed as a later entrant.

A Korean brokerage's October 2026 report said Yest's wafer throughput and pressure control give it strengths against competitors, and that it could become a main supplier, while also noting that Yest is a late entrant.

The contrast between foundry, which runs many product types in small volumes, and memory, which runs few types in large volumes, is another variable in equipment adoption. Whether memory-related orders convert into actual contracts will determine where Yest sits in the cycle.

06

Outlook

Hankyung reported that Yest secured NEOCON orders of roughly KRW 40 billion in the first half of 2026 alone. The company expects HPA equipment sales to expand in the memory market in 2027 and is pursuing customer deliveries and market entry for its HPO equipment in 2027.

Other press coverage confirmed that the company has secured clean-room space, materials and staff to handle higher order volumes.

A Korean brokerage's October 2026 report said seven of the nine supply contracts signed in 2026 carry terms ending within 2026, so equipment supply and revenue recognition could continue into the fourth quarter, with high-margin NEOCON equipment making up a large share of those contracts.

The NEOCON contract with Samsung Electronics runs through December 31, 2026, so revenue recognition under that contract needs to be confirmed by that date. The same report named additional HPA orders and new HPO orders as the main factors for confirming medium- to long-term growth.

If contract schedules slip, fourth-quarter revenue could move into later periods, which remains an open risk. The size of the order backlog and the speed at which it converts into revenue are the key variables for the 2027 outlook.

Bull factors

HPA references and high-margin equipment sales

Yest began supplying HPA equipment for foundry and NAND flash customers in 2026, and its 125-wafer tool is reported to have higher throughput than competing 75-wafer units. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 11 billion rose 141.0% from the prior quarter.

A Korean brokerage views HPA as a high-margin product, which could widen profitability gains as its share of sales grows.

NEOCON orders and expanding memory applications

NEOCON units are expanding from foundry into memory applications, and Hankyung reported roughly KRW 40 billion in orders in the first half of 2026 alone. Among 2026 contracts, a large share is reported to be NEOCON equipment, which carries higher profitability. A broader customer base could reduce dependence on any single customer.

Quarterly profit gains and expanded production base

Revenue has risen for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 7.96 billion in the third quarter of 2026. The company said it secured clean-room space, materials and staff to handle higher orders.

Having production capacity in place ahead of volume growth supports the company's ability to convert orders into revenue.

Bear factors

Position as a later supplier

The HPA market has long been effectively controlled by HPSP, and Yest is classed as a later entrant. A Korean brokerage's October 2026 report also called Yest a late entrant while noting it could become a main supplier. Until customer qualification and production track records build up, the pace of share gains may be limited.

Thin annual earnings base

Operating profit fell 66.3% to KRW 3.79 billion in 2025, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung back to a loss of KRW 576 million. Operating cash flow turned to -KRW 7.44 billion in 2025.

With total liabilities at KRW 90.4 billion at year-end 2025, the annual profit record is still too short to call a stable trend.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 38.2% from the prior quarter to KRW 1.77 billion in the second quarter of 2026, even as operating profit rose over the same period.

Quarterly net income swings sharply, and because results depend on equipment delivery timing, more quarters are needed before a trend can be identified.

Risk factors

Patent dispute

HPSP filed a patent infringement suit against Yest in 2023. In April 2025 the Patent Tribunal found that Yest's chamber opening and closing structure differs from the existing patent, but HPSP has filed a further suit based on a separate patent covering a double-wall structure.

Yest has said that patent has prior art and is likely invalid, but no outcome or timing has been confirmed. Business uncertainty remains until further rulings.

Contract timing concentration

Seven of the nine supply contracts signed in 2026 have terms ending by year-end 2026, so revenue could cluster in particular quarters. The NEOCON contract with Samsung Electronics runs through December 31, 2026, and its value equaled 8.41% of recent revenue. If delivery or acceptance schedules slip, revenue recognition could move into later periods.

Chip capex cycle

Earnings softened in 2025 as end-market customers cut investment, and operating profit fell sharply. Equipment demand moves with customers' HBM and other capex plans, so a delay in investment resumption could slow the recovery.

Because memory capex is concentrated among a few customers, changes in their timing could feed directly into order flow.

Overall view

Yest reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 43.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 11 billion, the highest quarterly operating profit since the company's founding, with HPA equipment revenue recognition at the center of the improvement.

Annual results from 2022 to 2025 alternated between losses and profits, and operating profit fell to KRW 3.79 billion in 2025, so the annual earnings base remains thin.

On a quarterly basis, revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and operating profit has increased for two consecutive quarters since the first quarter of 2026.

Strengths include HPA customer references, expanding NEOCON orders and a broader memory application base. Weaknesses include the patent dispute with HPSP, a concentration of contract terms ending in 2026, and the fragility of 2025 annual earnings.

Valuation can be read against the KRW 13.6 billion in net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, but given the earlier loss history, the meaning of the multiple is limited until earnings durability is confirmed.

A Korean brokerage's October 2026 report expected third-quarter results to improve, while naming additional HPA orders and new HPO orders as factors for confirming medium- to long-term growth.

The next checkpoints are the scale of fourth-quarter 2026 HPA revenue, full-year 2026 operating margin in the annual report, and first-quarter 2027 results.
