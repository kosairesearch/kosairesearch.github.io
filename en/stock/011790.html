SKC: Losses Narrowed, Then Widened Again in 3Q26

SKC narrowed its operating loss through 2Q26 but widened it again in 3Q26, leaving its profit recovery dependent on ESS copper foil and semiconductor materials.

  1. 1

    3Q26 revenue rose 26.3% year on year to KRW 638.9 billion, yet the operating loss widened to KRW 49.6 billion from KRW 14.4 billion in 2Q26.

  2. 2

    The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 734.4 billion in 2025, a loss in four consecutive years from 2022, and the debt-to-equity ratio climbed to 232.7% at year-end 2025.

  3. 3

    The semiconductor materials unit posted a 34.5% operating margin in 1Q26, its highest quarterly operating profit on record, according to the company.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

SKC was founded in 1973 and listed on the KOSPI in 1997 as a materials company within the SK Group. Its business spans three units: secondary battery materials, chemicals, and semiconductor materials.

The battery materials unit produces copper foil for batteries and is widening its customer base from electric vehicles to energy storage systems (ESS). The company said ESS accounted for 25% of its battery copper foil sales mix in 4Q25.

Battery materials revenue reached KRW 156.9 billion in 1Q26, up sharply from both the prior quarter and the year-earlier period. The chemicals unit produces PO and PG and reported 1Q26 revenue of KRW 270.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 9.6 billion, returning to profit from the prior quarter.

The semiconductor materials unit supplies test sockets and glass substrates, with 1Q26 revenue of KRW 68.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 23.6 billion. Its profitability was lifted by AI data center demand and higher sales of memory-related products.

Among the three units, chemicals had the largest revenue in 1Q26. North American customers are a growing share of demand, and the company expected North America to account for about 50% of total copper foil sales volume in 2026.

In chemicals, SKC competes with large materials and petrochemical companies such as LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions, Lotte Chemical and Kumho Petro Chemical.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show a revenue rebound alongside widening losses.

Revenue fell from KRW 3.14 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.57 trillion in 2023, a 50.0% decline, then rose 9.6% to KRW 1.72 trillion in 2024 and 6.9% to KRW 1.84 trillion in 2025, two consecutive years of growth from 2024 through 2025. 2022 was the largest revenue year of the four, and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit of KRW 220.3 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 216.3 billion in 2023, which widened to KRW 276.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 305 billion in 2025, three consecutive loss years from 2023 through 2025.

The operating margin moved from 7.0% in 2022 to -13.8% in 2023, -16.1% in 2024 and -16.6% in 2025.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders grew from KRW 68.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 275.5 billion, KRW 443.5 billion and KRW 734.4 billion in the following years, a loss in four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025.

Net loss including non-controlling interests moved from KRW 24.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 719.4 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow was negative in all four years, from KRW 147.2 billion outflow in 2022 to KRW 567.6 billion in 2025, the largest outflow of the period.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 185.2% in 2022 to 232.7% in 2025, when total equity stood at KRW 2.03 trillion, controlling shareholders' equity at KRW 832.1 billion and total liabilities at KRW 4.71 trillion.

Quarterly operating loss widened from KRW 52.8 billion in 3Q25 to KRW 107.6 billion in 4Q25, then narrowed to KRW 28.7 billion in 1Q26 and KRW 14.4 billion in 2Q26. A summary of a public conference call attributed the 4Q25 loss to one-off costs and asset impairment.

Revenue reached KRW 638.9 billion in 3Q26 and the operating loss widened to KRW 49.6 billion from the prior quarter, though it narrowed year on year. Operating losses have run for five consecutive quarters from 3Q25 through 3Q26. The four-quarter total of net losses attributable to controlling shareholders is KRW 842.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The copper foil market is shifting its demand base from electric vehicles toward ESS. The company said its ESS copper foil sales in 1H26 rose 665% from 1H25 and expects the global ESS market to grow from 390 GWh in 2025 to about 920 GWh by 2030.

However, a May 2025 Hana Securities report framed the analysis around an oversupply of copper foil, with long-term supply contracts as the key to securing volume. The same report noted that battery materials volumes were recovering from a trough in 3Q24.

Semiconductor materials are driven mainly by test demand tied to AI data center investment. The test socket business raised its operating margin to about 36%, according to the company, by broadening its non-memory ASIC customer base and increasing sales of high value-added products.

The chemicals unit depends on spreads and selling prices for petrochemical products such as PO and PG.

Sales fell in 4Q25 on lower selling prices and weaker spreads, and the unit returned to profit in 1Q26 on a spillover effect from supply disruptions tied to Middle East geopolitical issues and higher PG sales of high value-added products. In copper foil, how far ESS demand absorbs the oversupply concern will determine where the cycle stands.

06

Outlook

For 2026, the company set targets of at least 20% growth in semiconductor materials sales, about 50% growth in total copper foil sales volume, and 100% growth in ESS sales volume, according to a summary of a March 2026 public conference call.

The plan concentrates production in Malaysia and shifts the Jeongeup plant toward research and development to lower manufacturing costs, with the Jeongeup plant targeting EBITDA breakeven in 2H26. Fixed cost savings were presented at about KRW 40 billion per year.

The company said it is reviewing strategic options for its chemicals business, including a possible sale. In the glass substrate business, sample production for customer reliability evaluation and reviews of new projects with several customers were under way as of 2Q26.

A July 2026 news report citing FnGuide consensus projected an operating profit turnaround in 2027, but that estimate predates the 3Q26 results.

In April 2026 the company said a rights offering to accelerate glass substrates and improve its financial structure was in progress, and the outcome will be visible in later filings.

Glass substrate earnings contribution depends on customer evaluation and production decisions, so stabilizing battery materials profitability is a precondition, according to analysis in the press.

08

Bull factors

Signs the Quarterly Trough Has Passed

EBITDA turned positive in 1Q26 at KRW 10 billion for the first time since 2Q23. North American copper foil volumes rose 95% from the prior quarter, and the operating loss narrowed from KRW 107.6 billion in 4Q25 to KRW 14.4 billion in 2Q26. The company expected the trend to continue into 2Q26.

A High-Margin Semiconductor Materials Unit

The semiconductor materials unit recorded a 34.5% operating margin in 1Q26, its highest quarterly operating profit on record. AI data center demand, sales of memory-related products and a higher share of premium products were cited as drivers. The company set a target of more than 20% sales growth for the unit in 2026.

ESS Copper Foil and Production Footprint Shift

According to the company, ESS copper foil sales in 1H26 rose 665% from 1H25. The Malaysia unit posted quarterly EBITDA profit in 1Q26 on higher productivity. Shifting the Jeongeup plant toward research and development was presented as yielding fixed cost savings of about KRW 40 billion per year.

09

Bear factors

Losses Widened Again in 3Q26

The 3Q26 operating loss of KRW 49.6 billion was larger than in 2Q26, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 128.4 billion from KRW 90.7 billion. The four-quarter total of such net losses is KRW 842.8 billion. Operating cash outflow in 2025 of KRW 567.6 billion was the largest of the four years reported.

Financial Burden and Reliance on Fundraising

The debt-to-equity ratio at year-end 2025 was 232.7%, up from 185.2% in 2022, with total liabilities of KRW 4.71 trillion. Controlling shareholders' equity at that point was KRW 832.1 billion.

The company said it cut 2025 capex to about KRW 240 billion and secured roughly KRW 893.3 billion of liquidity through asset monetization, while a rights offering was under way in April 2026.

Delayed Earnings Contribution from Glass Substrates

The glass substrate business has only started initial reliability evaluations with customers, and analysts say the outcome and pace are hard to gauge. Because earnings contribution will take time, stabilizing battery materials profitability has become more important first. Expectations for new businesses may move the share price, but the earnings base remains limited.

10

Risk factors

Copper foil oversupply and demand volatility

A May 2025 Hana Securities report pointed to long-term supply contracts as the key in an oversupplied copper foil market. Whether ESS demand growth in 2026 absorbs the oversupply concern will shape selling prices and utilization.

In 4Q25, battery materials revenue fell 32.0% from the prior quarter, reflecting inventory adjustments at North American customers.

Financial structure and fundraising

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 232.7% at year-end 2025, and operating cash flow remains negative. Whether the rights offering was completed and whether further fundraising is needed will determine financial capacity.

Because a share issuance changes per-share metrics, the real effect of the capital increase will appear in later filings.

Chemicals spreads and selling prices

In 4Q25, chemicals revenue fell to KRW 247.5 billion on lower selling prices and weaker spreads, according to a summary of the company's explanation. The 1Q26 return to profit was attributed to a spillover effect from supply disruptions tied to Middle East geopolitical issues. If that factor fades, chemicals profitability could deteriorate again.

  3. Around late April 2027 (date unconfirmed)

  4. Timing undetermined

    Disclosure of glass substrate customer reliability evaluation results and confirmed follow-on customer projects would clarify when the new business can contribute to earnings. Any decision on reshaping the portfolio, including a possible chemicals sale, is also a point to watch.

12

Overall view

SKC has recorded net losses attributable to controlling shareholders in four consecutive years from 2022, and 2025 brought the largest losses of the period, with an operating loss of KRW 305 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 734.4 billion.

Operating losses narrowed in 1Q26 and 2Q26 but widened again in 3Q26, to KRW 49.6 billion. Positive factors include the high operating margin of semiconductor materials, rising ESS copper foil sales and cost improvement from the shift to Malaysian production.

Negative factors include the four-quarter net loss total of KRW 842.8 billion, a debt-to-equity ratio of 232.7% and continued operating cash outflows. The glass substrate business is still in customer evaluation, so the timing of its earnings contribution is hard to set.

A July 2026 news report citing an FnGuide consensus projected a 2027 operating profit turnaround, but that estimate predates the 3Q26 results. The next checkpoints are the full-year results in the 2026 annual report and whether operating profit turns positive in the 1Q27 results.
