02

Business structure

Plantynetco, founded in 2000 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2005, is an internet services company. Its core harmful-content filtering unit accounted for 54.3% of 2024 revenue, followed by media content distribution at 31.1% and venture capital at 14.6%, according to a 2025 report by The Bell.

For the first nine months of 2026, consolidated revenue by segment was KRW 16.13 billion for harmful-content filtering, KRW 9.98 billion for media content distribution, KRW 2.93 billion for venture capital and KRW 1.15 billion for leasing, according to the Q3 2026 quarterly report as covered by Vegan News.

Its main customers are KT, SK Telecom and LG Uplus, and the company says it maintains long-term contracts with all three domestic mobile carriers. Line subscribers stood at about 4.62 million as of September 30, 2026.

The company points to more than 25 years of accumulated harmful-content databases and large-scale traffic processing technology as its strengths. Overseas, it supplies digital care solutions to Chunghwa Telecom in Taiwan and runs wired security solutions with VNPT in Vietnam.

Its digital magazine platform Moazine, operated by a media subsidiary, holds more than 2,400 titles and 110,000 items of content. Cumulative R&D spending for January to September 2026 was KRW 1.6 billion, equal to 12.74% of standalone revenue.

Unlike Ahnlab, which focuses on enterprise and endpoint security, Plantynetco provides blocking services inside carrier networks.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 31.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 33.1 billion in 2023 (up 3.6% year on year), KRW 36.7 billion in 2024 (up 10.9%) and KRW 45.6 billion in 2025 (up 24.4%), and it has grown for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.01 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.27 billion in 2023 (up 26.4%), KRW 2.14 billion in 2024 (up 68.3%) and KRW 3.26 billion in 2025 (up 52.4%), also rising for three consecutive years.

Operating margin climbed from 3.1% in 2022 to 3.8% in 2023, 5.8% in 2024 and 7.1% in 2025, so profitability improved alongside sales growth.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss of KRW 1.56 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 1.54 billion in 2023, slipped 0.7% to KRW 1.53 billion in 2024, and recovered to KRW 2.26 billion in 2025 (up 47.5%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 1.71 billion in 2022, then KRW 2.39 billion in 2023, KRW 3.17 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.92 billion in 2025, which fell from 2024. In 2025, controlling-shareholder profit exceeded total net income because non-controlling interests recorded a loss.

The Bell attributed the 2024 improvement partly to exiting weak businesses such as lighting and energy. According to a CMIR report from May 2026, harmful-content filtering revenue rose from KRW 19.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 28.4 billion in 2025, a 42.5% increase that drove most of the group's growth.

Quarterly revenue moved from KRW 10.8 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 9.58 billion in Q4 (down 11.7% quarter on quarter), KRW 10.6 billion in Q1 2026 (up 10.2%), KRW 9.46 billion in Q2 (down 10.3%) and KRW 10.2 billion in Q3 2026 (up 7.6%, but down 6.2% year on year).

Operating results went from an operating loss of KRW 82.03 million in Q3 2025, which the company attributed to temporary marketing for subscriptions to its Moazine platform, to a loss of KRW 150 million in Q4 2025, a profit of KRW 476 million in Q1 2026, KRW 85.11 million in Q2 and KRW 647 million in Q3 2026.

Cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 was KRW 30.2 billion, down 16.2% from the same period a year earlier, according to Vegan News citing the Q3 2026 quarterly report.

05

Industry analysis

Plantynetco's end demand comes from the wired and wireless subscriber bases of domestic mobile carriers.

In its August 2026 first-half results announcement, the company said that AI-driven internet growth is making threats such as deepfakes, smishing and voice phishing more sophisticated, which is increasing demand for wired and wireless security and harmful-content blocking.

Its patented technology for blocking harmful sites based on the QUIC protocol, which is growing in use, is cited as a new business opportunity.

Rising use of generative AI has heightened concern over leaks of personal data and confidential documents, which the company frames as the backdrop for demand for AI prompt security.

From a cycle perspective, the filtering is embedded in carrier services, so the business is sensitive to carriers' contract cycles and subscriber changes. On competition, the company emphasizes its harmful-content database built over 25 years and its large-scale traffic processing technology.

Unlike Ahnlab, which focuses on enterprise and device security, Plantynetco delivers blocking inside the carrier network layer, so its positioning differs. Overseas, Chunghwa Telecom in Taiwan and VNPT in Vietnam are customers, and the carrier partnership model underpins expansion.

The nBlock education-device security solution is cited as a new growth axis tied to the spread of digital classrooms, according to a CMIR report from May 2026.

06

Outlook

For the fourth quarter, the company names the creation of results from new businesses, including encrypted international-traffic blocking and AI prompt security, as its priority.

The AI prompt filtering and personal-data protection project won in Q3 2026 is planned for full commercialization from 2027, aimed at public institutions and corporate customers. The company is also pursuing a security-function certificate for its document security product Officeguard.

Overseas, in Taiwan it is pushing subscriber growth and average revenue per user through anti-voice-phishing and anti-smishing services, while in Vietnam it is revamping the Greennet bundle and launching a wireless security service.

Its digital magazine platform Moazine is set to expand its B2C business on top of the existing B2B model. Shareholder returns are also growing: the company paid its first interim dividend since listing and in June decided to cancel 500,000 treasury shares, with a planned amount of KRW 3.29 billion.

The payout ratio for the 2025 year-end dividend was 62.8%. CMIR, an independent research firm, cited growth in carrier subscribers in Korea, expansion of overseas security operations and growth in Moazine subscribers as 2026 growth variables in its May 2026 report.

The same report said a re-rating would be possible if stable core EBITDA and narrowing platform losses are confirmed together. The company says it will pursue cost efficiency in the second half alongside new business results.

Bull factors

Recovery in core profitability

The company's operating margin rose from 3.1% in 2022 to 7.1% in 2025, and Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 647 million turned positive. Long-term contracts with the three major carriers support about 4.62 million line subscribers as of September 30, 2026.

Quarterly revenue moves up and down, but the core filtering business continues to anchor results, which is cited as a bullish argument.

AI security and overseas wins

The company said it won an AI prompt filtering and personal-data protection project in Q3 2026 and plans full commercialization from 2027. Overseas, it works with Chunghwa Telecom in Taiwan and VNPT in Vietnam on wired security and digital care services.

Rising concern over leaks linked to generative AI is presented as the demand backdrop, which supports the bullish case.

Wider shareholder returns

The company paid its first interim dividend since listing in 2026 and in June decided to cancel 500,000 treasury shares. The return tools now sit alongside year-end dividends paid since 2007.

The payout ratio for the 2025 year-end dividend was 62.8%, and the company said it met the requirements for the separate taxation preference for high dividends.

09

Bear factors

Quarterly earnings volatility

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 927 million in Q4 2025, and Q2 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 85.11 million, down 82.1% from the prior quarter. Cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 was down 16.2% year on year. Whether quarterly profits can stay positive is not yet established, which is the core bearish argument.

Revenue decline and marketing costs

Q3 2026 revenue fell 6.2% year on year. The company attributed weaker second-quarter profitability to marketing spending to secure share in new growth businesses. If costs for new businesses come before revenue, the timing of monetization remains uncertain, which is a bearish factor.

Customer concentration and mature segments

The harmful-content filtering unit depends on contracts with domestic mobile carriers. Media segment revenue fell 17% in 2024 to KRW 11.4 billion, according to The Bell. Until new businesses show up in reported results, growth drivers outside the core unit may be limited, which is a bearish reading.

10

Risk factors

Overseas execution

In Taiwan, the company is pursuing subscriber growth and higher average revenue per user centered on anti-voice-phishing and anti-smishing services, while in Vietnam it is revamping the Greennet bundle and launching a wireless security service.

Because overseas expansion relies on a partner model with local carriers, changes in partners' sales policies or bundle structures could alter the pace of subscriber growth.

In that case, planned revenue gains in Taiwan and Vietnam could come later than expected or yield smaller improvements in average revenue per user.

Certification delay

The document security product Officeguard is pursuing a security-function certificate. If certificate acquisition comes later than planned, new orders and sales expansion for the product could also be delayed.

If the review requires product enhancements, development costs and launch timing could be affected, and this could coincide with the timing for confirming Q4 new-business results.

Technology and policy shifts

AI-driven threats and wider use of the QUIC protocol are changing the technology landscape quickly, so blocking technology must be continually updated. Government policy on harmful sites could shift demand and the regulatory environment. Whether the company's patents and databases keep pace with these changes is a key variable.

Overall view

Plantynetco's results show a structural improvement, with revenue and operating profit rising since 2022, alongside quarterly swings such as the Q4 2025 loss and the Q2 2026 profit decline. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 647 million turned positive, but revenue for the same quarter fell 6.2% year on year.

The core harmful-content filtering business, supported by long-term contracts with the three major carriers and about 4.62 million line subscribers, forms the bullish basis. The timing of monetization for new businesses and customer concentration remain bearish factors.

Price-to-book sits well below 1x and shareholder-return tools have widened, but the small earnings base keeps the multiple sensitive to quarterly results. An outside research firm framed a re-rating as conditional on stable core earnings and narrowing platform losses.

The next confirmation points are Q4 2026 new-business results, the preliminary 2026 full-year results around February 2027 and the 2026 annual report by the end of March 2027.
