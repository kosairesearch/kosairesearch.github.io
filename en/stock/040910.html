ICD: Back in the Black, but the Earnings Base Is Still Thin

ICD returned to annual operating profit in 2025 on an OLED equipment base, and its Q3 2026 revenue rose sharply, yet quarterly net results still swing between profit and loss.

  1. 1

    Full-year 2025 revenue reached KRW 197 billion, the largest of 2022–2025, and operating profit of KRW 4.45 billion marked a turn to profit.

  2. 2

    Q3 2026 revenue rose 144.4% from the prior quarter to KRW 70.9 billion, with operating profit of KRW 4.79 billion.

  3. 3

    In the same quarter, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.45 billion, diverging from the operating profit.

  4. 4

    An FPD equipment supply contract of about KRW 36.6 billion with Hefei Visionox Technology was disclosed on February 24, 2026.

  5. 5

    Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 8.01 billion, turning positive after negative readings in 2022–2024.

02

Business structure

ICD was founded in 2000 to manufacture equipment for LCD, semiconductor and flat-panel display production. Its core products are plasma-based HDP dry etchers and vacuum transfer equipment used in OLED deposition processes.

A breakdown compiled by WiseReport shows AM-OLED front-end equipment at 83.92% and parts and services revenue at 16.08%, though the compilation period is not stated. The same source shows that for the December 2024 period, AM-OLED front-end equipment sales were 55.28% domestic and 44.72% export.

Parts and services revenue was weighted more toward domestic sales, at 79.23%. The company is described as serving display manufacturers worldwide, and the counterparties in the contracts confirmed in our sources are two Chinese display makers.

A supply contract of about KRW 36.6 billion with Hefei Visionox Technology was disclosed on February 24, 2026. A KRW 8.7 billion FPD equipment supply contract with BOE CHUANGYUAN Technology was also confirmed.

R&D spending was about KRW 6.24 billion in 2024, or 4.23% of sales, down from about KRW 8.64 billion (13.88% of sales) in 2023. A display component subsidiary established in 2018 is also consolidated. Other listed equipment makers in the sector include Wonik IPS and Jusung Engineering.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 150.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 62.3 billion in 2023 (down 58.7% year on year), then rose to KRW 147.6 billion in 2024 (up 137.0%) and KRW 197 billion in 2025 (up 33.5%). Revenue has grown for two consecutive years since the 2023 low, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022–2025.

Operating loss widened from KRW 5.05 billion in 2022 to KRW 37.2 billion in 2023, narrowed to KRW 26.7 billion in 2024, and turned into an operating profit of KRW 4.45 billion in 2025; the company attributed the main driver to increased new investment by customers, per a February 13, 2026 disclosure report.

Operating margin was -3.3% in 2022, -59.8% in 2023, -18.1% in 2024 and 2.3% in 2025. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.42 billion, KRW 37.4 billion, KRW 28.3 billion and KRW 1.9 billion from 2022 to 2025, a loss in each of the four years, with the 2025 loss narrowing.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 14.3 billion in 2022, -KRW 1.47 billion in 2023 and -KRW 27.8 billion in 2024, then KRW 8.01 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 32.1% in 2022 to 60.4% in 2023 and 97.0% in 2024, then fell to 46.6% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 98.5 billion (all attributable to controlling shareholders) and total liabilities were KRW 45.9 billion.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 41.9 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 52 billion in Q4 2025 down to KRW 22.4 billion in Q1 2026 (down 56.8% from the prior quarter), then recovered to KRW 29 billion in Q2 and KRW 70.9 billion in Q3 2026.

The Q1 2026 operating loss of KRW 5.03 billion and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.56 billion came in the same quarter as the revenue drop, showing how closely volume drives quarterly results.

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.79 billion turned positive year on year, while the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.45 billion turned negative year on year; the reason for this divergence between the two lines sits below operating profit and should be checked in the filings. Over the last four quarters, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 4.28 billion.

05

Industry analysis

ICD's equipment demand is tied to display panel investment. WiseReport notes that OLED adoption is widening from smartphones into IT, TV and automotive, which has lifted demand for HDP etchers and vacuum transfer equipment.

The same source reports that cumulative revenue for the first nine months of 2025 rose 69.9% year on year, and operating profit turned positive. The climb from the 2023 low through 2024 and 2025 to KRW 70.9 billion in Q3 2026 can be read as customer investment converting into equipment orders.

Display equipment makers tend to see orders cluster around customers' new line investment timing, which produces large swings in annual results. Among disclosed contracts, the largest is the roughly KRW 36.6 billion Hefei Visionox Technology supply agreement disclosed on February 24, 2026.

Overseas customers account for a meaningful share, so results are exposed to the timing of overseas display investment. Other listed equipment makers in the sector include Wonik IPS and Jusung Engineering.

HDP etchers serve LCD and semiconductor uses as well, so demand beyond OLED should be tracked alongside OLED demand.

06

Outlook

The supply agreement of roughly KRW 36.6 billion with Hefei Visionox Technology, disclosed on February 24, 2026, makes the timing of revenue recognition under that contract a point to confirm in the next periodic report.

A KRW 8.7 billion contract with BOE CHUANGYUAN Technology has also been confirmed, so contract sizes range widely. With revenue reaching KRW 70.9 billion in Q3 2026, whether revenue holds near that level in Q4 2026 and beyond is the next checkpoint.

Quarterly revenue moved from KRW 52 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 22.4 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 29 billion in Q2 2026, so continuity across quarters matters. Whether the new customer investment cited for the 2025 turnaround continues in 2026 is the key variable for order flow.

The broadening of OLED adoption into IT, TV and automotive is cited as a medium-term driver of equipment demand. The 2026 annual report will show how the gap between operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders is composed, particularly below the operating line.

Whether the positive operating cash flow of KRW 8.01 billion in 2025 carries into 2026 also needs to be confirmed. With the debt ratio at 46.6% in 2025, working capital shifts from large contract execution are another item to track.

Bull factors

Expanding OLED equipment demand

WiseReport said OLED adoption is spreading from smartphones into IT, TV and automotive, lifting demand for HDP etchers and vacuum transfer equipment. The same source put cumulative revenue for the first nine months of 2025 up 69.9% year on year. On February 24, 2026, a supply contract of about KRW 36.6 billion with Hefei Visionox Technology was disclosed.

Turn to profit and quarterly revenue recovery

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 4.45 billion, a turn from the operating loss of KRW 26.7 billion in 2024. Q3 2026 revenue rose 144.4% from the prior quarter to KRW 70.9 billion, with operating profit of KRW 4.79 billion. Operating cash flow in 2025 also turned positive at KRW 8.01 billion.

Improving balance sheet

The debt ratio fell from 97.0% in 2024 to 46.6% in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 98.5 billion and total liabilities were KRW 45.9 billion. Two of the last four quarters showed positive net income attributable to controlling shareholders, and the four-quarter total was KRW 4.28 billion.

Bear factors

Quarterly revenue volatility

Revenue in 2023 fell 58.7% year on year to KRW 62.3 billion. Q1 2026 revenue dropped 56.8% from the prior quarter to KRW 22.4 billion. The swings in revenue scale with customer investment timing appear in both annual and quarterly figures.

Instability in net income attributable to controlling shareholders

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.56 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 1.45 billion in Q3 2026. Operating profit in Q3 2026 was KRW 4.79 billion. Because the operating profit did not carry through to a positive bottom line, items below operating profit need to be checked.

Concentration in overseas customers and contracts

Export share for AM-OLED front-end equipment was 44.72% in December 2024. The confirmed supply contract counterparties are BOE CHUANGYUAN Technology and Hefei Visionox Technology, both Chinese display makers. Results depend on the investment schedules and contract execution of a small number of customers.

10

Risk factors

Dependence on customer investment cycles

The company attributed the 2025 turnaround mainly to increased new investment by customers. If customer investment slows or is delayed, equipment orders and revenue recognition can shift in the same way. Revenue fell 58.7% in 2023, showing the impact of a downturn in the cycle.

Quarterly earnings swings and possible return to losses

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders ran for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, and even after the turnaround an operating loss of KRW 5.03 billion occurred in Q1 2026. The four-quarter total of KRW 4.28 billion masks large differences between quarters.

Because earnings can change quickly from quarter to quarter, whether profitability persists is the central risk.

Cash flow history and financial burden

Operating cash flow was negative from 2022 to 2024, reaching -KRW 27.8 billion in 2024. It turned to KRW 8.01 billion in 2025, but that is a single year of positive flow.

The debt ratio peaked at 97.0% in 2024 before falling to 46.6% in 2025, and working capital changes during large contract execution could reverse part of that improvement.

12

Overall view

ICD posted operating losses in 2022–2024 before returning to operating profit in 2025, and 2025 revenue of KRW 197 billion was the largest year of 2022–2025. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 70.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.79 billion point to improving recent momentum.

However, the same quarter showed a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.45 billion, and Q1 2026 had both an operating loss and a net loss, so quarterly earnings stability is still being confirmed.

On orders, a contract of about KRW 36.6 billion was disclosed in February 2026, and all confirmed counterparties are Chinese display makers. On the balance sheet, the debt ratio fell from 97.0% in 2024 to 46.6% in 2025, and operating cash flow turned positive in 2025.

Bullish factors include expanding OLED equipment demand and the shift to profit, while bearish factors include quarterly revenue swings and unstable net income attributable to controlling shareholders.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio is below 1x, but the small earnings base means interpretation changes sharply with earnings. The next checkpoints are the preliminary 2026 annual results, the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
