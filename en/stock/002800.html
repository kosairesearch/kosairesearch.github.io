02

Business structure

Sinsin Pharmaceutical was founded in 1959 as a domestic maker of finished pharmaceuticals, and its core business is patch products built on its transdermal drug delivery system (TDDS) technology.

According to a report citing a regulatory filing, patches generated about 57.06% of 2025 sales, and most of the remaining major revenue also comes from over-the-counter (OTC) products.

By dosage form, Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 30.2 billion was led by patches at KRW 16.1 billion, or 53.4%, followed by topical solutions at 16.6%, oral drugs at 11.1%, ointments at 7.5% and aerosols at 4.2%.

By sales channel, pharmacies were the largest at 48.1%, followed by pharmaceutical wholesalers at 22.8% and distribution vendors at 13.3%. Export sales in the same quarter were KRW 4.4 billion, or 14.5% of the total, with Southeast Asia, Asia and Europe named as the main export regions.

The company highlights its direct-trade network with about 13,000 retail pharmacies nationwide. Its flagship Arex brand and related products drove the recent revenue gains. Production is handled mainly at the Sejong plant, which began operating in 2019, and patch lines account for a large share of that plant.

A trade report put the patch line utilization rate at around 98% on a cumulative basis through Q3 2025. Competitively, domestic patches are seen as offering more variety and efficacy than Chinese patches, but domestic OTC market growth potential is viewed as limited.

Related parties of the largest shareholder held 51.50% as of September 10, 2026, concentrating management control.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed basis, annual revenue was KRW 91.9 billion in 2022, KRW 102.6 billion in 2023 (up 11.6% year on year), KRW 106.4 billion in 2024 (up 3.7%) and KRW 113.8 billion in 2025 (up 6.9%).

Revenue rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, with 2025 the largest year and 2022 the smallest in the 2022-2025 span. Operating profit climbed from KRW 5.43 billion in 2022 to KRW 6 billion in 2023 and KRW 6.89 billion in 2024, then reached KRW 10.8 billion in 2025 (up 56.6%).

The operating margin moved from 5.9% in 2022 and 5.9% in 2023 to 6.5% in 2024 and 9.5% in 2025. Revenue grew 6.9% in 2025 while operating profit grew 56.6%, which points to cost-ratio improvement rather than volume alone.

Press reports credited automation and process upgrades at the Sejong plant, operating since 2019, as the main driver of the profit structure change. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 4.41 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.68 billion, KRW 4.89 billion and KRW 9.29 billion in 2025 (up 90.1%).

Reports said non-operating income, including Sejong city employment incentives, contributed to the 2025 net income gain, so whether that recurs will become clearer from the non-operating items in upcoming annual reports.

Quarterly revenue fell from KRW 30.1 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 28.8 billion in Q4 2025, then rose to KRW 30.2 billion, KRW 34 billion and KRW 34.2 billion across Q1 to Q3 2026.

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 3.18 billion in Q1 2026 and then rose to KRW 4.06 billion and KRW 4.28 billion, the latter up 8.1% year on year.

Even so, Q3 2026 produced a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 223 million, so the gap between higher operating profit and a net loss makes non-operating items and income tax the key lines to watch.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 9.82 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 5.46 billion in 2022, KRW 3.93 billion in 2023, KRW 5.94 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.58 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Both the company and the industry expect patch demand to grow as Korea ages. Many observers, however, see limited growth potential in the domestic OTC market, and analysts argue that overseas expansion is needed for top-line growth. A 2024 report said exports had risen more than 43% over the prior two years.

In China, the company agreed with Sinopharm to supply 12 products through Alibaba's online store over five years, for a total of about USD 5.4 million, according to a 2024 report. Press coverage noted that China's sanitary permit process is demanding and raises the entry barrier.

On product competitiveness, domestic patches are viewed as offering more variety and better efficacy than Chinese patches. OTC-focused drugmakers have traditionally been viewed as low-margin businesses, and Sinsin's operating margin in the 9% range has been reported as departing from that pattern.

In microneedle drug delivery, no domestic commercial precedent yet exists, and the company aims to be the first to commercialize one in Korea. Large drugmakers' Q3 2026 results also diverged based on new drug and export performance, so portfolio diversification is a shared industry challenge.

06

Outlook

The core of the new drug pipeline is UIP-620, a transdermal improved new drug for overactive bladder. In a 2024 report, the company said it aimed to enter a domestic Phase 3 trial and commercialize within two to three years, with the trial planned for 500 patients at about ten domestic hospitals.

The same report cited industry expectations of commercialization around 2026 if Phase 3 started, but that timeline depends on trial progress, so the latest stage should be confirmed through company disclosures.

A second candidate, SS-262, is being developed as a transdermal insomnia treatment, and a domestic Phase 1 trial plan had been filed.

The company said it completed non-clinical testing of its microneedle drug in April 2025 and is pursuing rapid approval from the drug regulator through production process setup and equivalence testing. After approval, it plans to review investment in GMP-compliant production equipment.

On the commercial side, an October 2026 report described a supply agreement giving exclusive sales rights to nine products across six OTC brands through 2028, extending the pharmacy network into a wider product range.

Chairman Lee Byeong-gi said in his New Year message that growth would center on patches and be performance-based rather than expansion for its own sake. Overseas, the plan is to widen export countries from China online sales to Central Asia, Indonesia and Latin America.

R&D spending rose from about KRW 1.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.9 billion in 2023 and KRW 2.3 billion in 2024, and whether it translates into new drug results will show up in clinical disclosures and changes in the sales mix.

Risk factors

Product concentration and pricing pressure

Patches generated about 57.06% of 2025 sales, so dependence on a single dosage form is high. Most remaining revenue also comes from OTC products, which exposes the company to shifts in that market. Generic products face pricing pressure, so the pace of product mix change matters.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Q1 2026 operating profit hit a low of KRW 3.18 billion, and Q3 brought a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders. Reports said non-operating income partly contributed to 2025 net income. Quarterly swings are large, so one or two quarters make trend assessment difficult.

Execution risk in overseas and new businesses

Export sales were KRW 4.4 billion in Q1 2026, or 14.5% of revenue, and China entry is at an early stage through online sales. Microneedle approval and new drug trials are long-term tasks where timelines can slip. If new business spending hits costs first, short-term profitability could come under pressure.

Overall view

Sinsin Pharmaceutical raised its operating margin from 5.9% in 2022 to 9.5% in 2025 on a patch and OTC-centered model, and 2025 operating profit of KRW 10.8 billion was up 56.6% year on year.

Revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and the balance sheet improved with a debt ratio of 84.5% and equity of KRW 74.5 billion.

In 2026, revenue rose for three consecutive quarters from KRW 30.2 billion in Q1 to KRW 34.2 billion in Q3, and operating profit increased for two consecutive quarters after Q1.

However, the Q3 2026 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 223 million makes whether operating gains carry through to net profit the central open question. Patches account for more than half of revenue, and limited domestic OTC growth is a structural challenge.

Exports were 14.5% of revenue, and UIP-620 and the microneedle drug remain in clinical or approval stages, so the timing of their payoff is uncertain.

Structural margin gains and quarterly earnings volatility coexist, and the next checkpoints are the operating margin and non-operating items in the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
