Annual turnaround to profit, but quarterly earnings remain volatile

02

Business structure

Macromill Embrain, founded in 1999, is a market research and data company. Although it is classified under IT and software, its revenue centers on online and offline research survey products built on consumer panels.

Its main services include Panel Deep Data (DD.P), and Purchase Deep Data (DD.B), which it launched with ALPS in 2022. Its Trendmonitor content business, AI training data services and LLM training center are also being expanded.

The panel base relies on the network of the global research group Macromill, and the company's own materials cite a panel of 2.7 million people in Northeast Asia and 6.4 million globally.

The advertising and consulting business is positioned as a new growth area; in December 2025 a subsidiary acquired a 51 percent stake in an advertising and consulting firm, broadening the group's scope.

That subsidiary's sales in the fiscal year ended June 2026 rose 28 percent to KRW 6.4 billion from KRW 5.0 billion, but its operating loss widened from KRW 1.0 billion to KRW 1.1 billion. The largest shareholder, MACROMILL, INC., holds 41.35 percent, and chief executive Choi In-su holds 15.02 percent.

In December 2025 the largest shareholder changed to TJ1 Co., Ltd. through an absorption merger of the former largest shareholder, and the name was later changed back to MACROMILL, INC.

04

Earnings analysis

On a fiscal-year basis, sales bottomed at KRW 53.7 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024, down 2.2 percent year on year from KRW 54.9 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023.

Sales then rose 4.6 percent to KRW 56.2 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025 and 4.9 percent to KRW 58.9 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026, the largest year in the period from the fiscal year ended June 2023 to the fiscal year ended June 2026. Profit moved in the opposite direction.

Operating profit fell from KRW 4.71 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to KRW 1.68 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024, a 64.4 percent decline, and the company then posted an operating loss of KRW 1.26 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025.

It returned to operating profit of KRW 2.7 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026. Profit attributable to owners of the parent moved from a loss of KRW 842 million to a profit of KRW 1.76 billion over the same period, while net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.66 billion, a different figure.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 1.53 billion to KRW 4.02 billion. Quarterly results were much more uneven. Sales in the fourth quarter of 2025 reached KRW 21.8 billion, up 60.2 percent from the prior quarter, with operating profit of KRW 2.54 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026 sales fell 54.7 percent to KRW 9.88 billion and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 957 million and a loss attributable to owners of KRW 568 million.

The second quarter of 2026 recovered to sales of KRW 13.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 787 million, but the third quarter fell back to sales of KRW 10.8 billion, operating profit of KRW 244 million and profit attributable to owners of KRW 29.65 million.

Profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 1.56 billion.

The continued losses in advertising and consulting are cited as a background factor for the quarterly swings, and the goodwill impairment is tied to the weak performance of that unit; the quarters in which those amounts were booked can be confirmed in the periodic reports.

05

Industry analysis

In market research, data-driven insight that supports consumer behavior analysis and marketing decisions has become central. According to a company analysis report, advances in big data, AI and machine learning are strengthening real-time consumer trend analysis and predictive analytics.

Rising demand for digital marketing upgrades and consumer experience optimization is pushing integrated research platforms and automation solutions forward as growth drivers. This trend offers Macromill Embrain both an opportunity and a burden.

Extending panel data into AI training data matches the shift in demand, but quarterly sales moving from KRW 9.88 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 13.5 billion in the second and KRW 10.8 billion in the third suggests project volume remains volatile.

Its competitive position starts from its membership in the global research group Macromill, which according to the company analysis runs a network of 20 sites across six countries.

Annual sales have grown two years in a row since the fiscal year ended June 2024 low, which can be read as a recovery phase, but the third-quarter 2026 decline raises the question of whether that recovery will hold.

Compared with large platform and system integration companies in the same IT and software classification, its scale and business structure differ substantially, so comparisons with other research specialists are more meaningful than sector averages.

06

Outlook

The most concrete development after the latest periodic reports is shareholder return. On September 29, 2026 the board approved open-market purchases of 477,238 common shares worth about KRW 801.76 million, with the purchase period running from September 30 through December 29, 2026 via Kiwoom Securities.

The company said the purchased shares will be linked to future cancellation as part of an expanding shareholder value policy. A proposal for a cash dividend was also placed before the annual general meeting, and the company says it will keep expanding a mix of dividends and treasury share purchases and cancellations.

In the business, continued losses in advertising and consulting are the largest variable. According to an IB Tomato report, continued weak performance in the advertising business could lead to additional goodwill impairments and funding burdens.

If loan guarantees expand, credit enhancement pressure could turn into financial burden, so the next periodic report should be read together with the guarantee balance and the goodwill balance.

On the data side, the company highlights strengthening of data services through AI training data offerings and an LLM training center as recent priorities. On sales, third-quarter 2026 revenue fell year on year, so whether next-quarter sales recover is the key checkpoint.

08

Bull factors

Return to profit and two years of sales growth

Sales in the fiscal year ended June 2026 rose 4.9% to KRW 58.9 billion, extending two years of growth from the fiscal year ended June 2025. Operating profit turned positive at KRW 2.7 billion and the operating margin recovered to 4.6% from -2.2%. Operating cash flow also rose to KRW 4.02 billion, improving cash backing for earnings.

Improving balance sheet and new data demand

The debt ratio fell to 61.9 percent in the fiscal year ended June 2026 from 65.9 percent a year earlier, and at the end of June 2026 total liabilities were KRW 17.6 billion against total equity of KRW 28.4 billion.

New demand for AI training data and deep data products could create room for the operating margin to improve. Profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters totals KRW 1.56 billion, showing a move out of loss territory.

Continued shareholder return policy

On September 29, 2026 the board approved open-market purchases of treasury shares worth about KRW 801.76 million, with the purchase period running through December 29, 2026. A cash dividend proposal was also placed before the annual general meeting.

The company stated it will expand shareholder returns by linking dividends with treasury share purchases and cancellations.

09

Bear factors

Third-quarter 2026 sales and profit both declined

Third-quarter 2026 sales fell 20.9 percent year on year to KRW 10.8 billion, operating profit dropped 27.3 percent to KRW 244 million, and profit attributable to owners declined 87.3 percent to KRW 29.65 million.

In the first quarter of 2026 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 957 million and a loss attributable to owners of KRW 568 million. Because quarterly results have not built up steadily, questions remain about whether the annual return to profit can be sustained.

Losses and goodwill impairment in advertising and consulting

The advertising and consulting business remains loss-making, and roughly half of the advertising goodwill was impaired about two years after the acquisition (IB Tomato, September 23, 2026). The subsidiary's operating loss widened from KRW 1.0 billion to KRW 1.1 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026.

Over the same period its net assets fell 61.8 percent from KRW 181.2 million to KRW 69.2 million, per the IB Tomato report.

Expanding guarantees and risk of financial spillover

According to an IB Tomato report, the company has expanded its advertising investment burden through loan guarantees. If weak performance in advertising continues, further goodwill impairments and funding support costs could follow.

With total liabilities at KRW 17.6 billion at the end of June 2026, whether subsidiary burdens pass through to the consolidated balance sheet is a point to monitor.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Sales went from KRW 21.8 billion with operating profit of KRW 2.54 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 9.88 billion with an operating loss of KRW 957 million in the first quarter of 2026. Sales then moved to KRW 13.5 billion in the second quarter and KRW 10.8 billion in the third. The base for the annual return to profit is still thin at the quarterly level.

Subsidiary goodwill and loan guarantees

Goodwill impairment and loan guarantees in the advertising and consulting business could lead to additional expenses and funding burdens. Impairments reduce book value, while guarantees remain contingent obligations that act as variables outside reported earnings. If the subsidiary does not improve, these burdens could pass through to the parent company's finances.

Concentrated ownership and liquidity

The largest shareholder, MACROMILL, INC., holds 41.35 percent and the chief executive holds 15.02 percent, so the two stakes together total 56.37 percent. With ownership concentrated in these holders, the float available for trading is relatively limited, which can increase share price volatility. Changes in major holdings are disclosed through large-shareholding reports.

12

Overall view

Macromill Embrain recorded sales of KRW 58.9 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026, up 4.9 percent, and operating profit of KRW 2.7 billion after a return to profit, so the annual picture shows a recovery. The debt ratio fell to 61.9 percent, indicating an improving balance sheet.

However, third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 10.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 244 million both fell year on year, and the first quarter of 2026 recorded an operating loss, so the stability of quarterly results is not yet established.

Continued losses in advertising and consulting, along with goodwill impairment and expanding guarantees, are the clearest bearish factors. On the other side, treasury share purchases and a cash dividend proposal show shareholder returns expanding.

The next checkpoints are whether fourth-quarter 2026 results recover to third-quarter levels and whether the operating loss and guarantee balance in advertising and consulting shrink. Confirming these would provide a basis for judging whether the annual return to profit can be sustained.
