Report overview

Research Leader, Profitability Still a Work in Progress

Macromill Embrain's revenue rebounded to KRW 56.2 billion in 2025, but the company booked an operating loss of KRW 1.26 billion as profitability deteriorated for a fourth straight year, putting both its expanded shareholder returns and the pace of earnings recovery under watch.

02

Business structure

Macromill Embrain began in 1998 as Korea Panel Research, rebranded as Macromill Embrain following an investment from Japan's Macromill in 2012, and listed on KOSDAQ in 2020 as a leading domestic marketing research firm.

Its core business centers on online and offline survey research, expanded through a panel deep-data service that combines consumer behavior data with survey results.

The company states it operates online research, offline research, and deep-data businesses based on what it describes as the country's largest survey panel of roughly 1.79 million members, while its own website cites a global research panel scale of about 7 million people.

Subsidiaries include Embrain Public, which handles public and opinion polling; MindMagnet, which runs the advertising business; consulting unit Embrain Consulting; and research subsidiary Embrain Research, under which Pulse Research was newly established in 2025.

The largest shareholder is Japan-listed research firm Macromill, Inc., with a governance history in which the controlling shareholder name temporarily changed to TJ1 Co., Ltd. before reverting to Macromill following a merger.

Its client base spans corporate marketing research, public policy surveys, and academic research, and according to past reporting the company has handled more than 1,000 client accounts and thousands of projects annually.

Korea's marketing research market is populated by numerous small and mid-sized firms alongside global research organizations, and with few directly comparable listed peers, Macromill Embrain maintains a relatively distinct market position.

More recently, the company has introduced new data-tech offerings such as an AI-based synthetic survey pilot and the 'CALI' data analytics solution. However, MindMagnet, the advertising subsidiary, continues to post losses, weighing on overall group profitability.

04

Earnings analysis

Macromill Embrain's consolidated revenue rose 7.2% from KRW 51.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 54.9 billion in 2023, fell 2.2% to KRW 53.7 billion in 2024, and then rose 4.6% to KRW 56.2 billion in 2025, the largest figure among the four disclosed years.

Operating income, however, declined every year, from KRW 7.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.71 billion in 2023 (down 36.3% year over year) and KRW 1.68 billion in 2024 (down 64.4%), before turning into an operating loss of KRW 1.26 billion in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders followed the same pattern, shrinking from KRW 5.98 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.76 billion in 2023 (down 37.1%) and KRW 1.41 billion in 2024 (down 62.5%), before swinging to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 842 million in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests also declined from KRW 6.05 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.94 billion in 2023 and KRW 1.89 billion in 2024, before recording a net loss of KRW 1.06 billion in 2025.

The operating margin fell steadily from 14.4% in 2022 to 8.6% in 2023 and 3.1% in 2024, before dropping to -2.2% in 2025, illustrating a widening gap between revenue growth and profitability.

On a quarterly basis, the second quarter of 2025 showed clear strength, with revenue of KRW 21.8 billion and operating income of KRW 2.54 billion, but the third quarter saw revenue plunge 70.7% quarter over quarter to KRW 6.39 billion while the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 2.46 billion.

Fourth-quarter 2025 revenue rose 123.9% quarter over quarter and 16.1% year over year to KRW 14.3 billion, yet the operating loss of KRW 1.67 billion, while narrower than the prior quarter, was wider than the same period a year earlier, marking two consecutive quarters of operating losses in the third and fourth quarters of 2025.

The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the trailing four quarters (first through fourth quarter of 2025) was KRW 842 million, reflecting how pronounced quarterly swings fed directly into full-year profitability.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 54.7% in 2022 to 36.5% in 2023 before rising again to 62.4% in 2024 and 65.9% in 2025, while operating cash flow declined from KRW 9.42 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.53 billion in 2025, indicating weaker cash generation.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's marketing and opinion research industry operates on a project-based model in which order volumes are tied to corporate marketing budgets and public-sector policy survey demand, making it sensitive to the broader economic cycle.

Analysts note that online research, being shorter in duration and lower in cost than offline research, tends to hold up relatively well even during economic slowdowns.

Macromill Embrain is viewed as benefiting from economies of scale thanks to what it describes as the country's largest survey panel infrastructure, though the scarcity of directly comparable listed peers makes industry benchmarking difficult.

More recently, the possibility that generative AI could substitute for real survey respondents through 'synthetic survey' methods has become a major talking point in the research industry, a discussion catalyzed by Nvidia's release of a Korean AI persona dataset.

In a June 2026 webinar, Macromill Embrain disclosed its own synthetic survey pilot results, finding meaningful differences between human and AI responses on brand awareness and actual consumption behavior questions, but relatively small differences in evaluating product concepts and stimuli.

This dynamic illustrates how AI could both partially displace traditional panel-based research and simultaneously offer the company an opportunity to expand into AI-driven data services.

Within the KOSDAQ IT and software classification, which spans large platform companies such as NAVER and Kakao as well as a range of software and data firms, Macromill Embrain occupies a relatively small-cap, specialized data-service niche.

06

Outlook

According to materials approved at the September 29, 2026 annual shareholders' meeting covering the 28th fiscal year (July 2025 to June 2026), consolidated revenue for that period was KRW 58.894 billion, operating income was KRW 2.7 billion, and net income was KRW 1.663 billion, indicating a swing to profit in the period following the most recent quarter covered by regular quarterly filings.

This aligns with reporting that revenue rose 4.9% year over year and operating performance turned from loss to profit over the July 2025-June 2026 period, attributed to improved results across most subsidiaries except MindMagnet.

However, reports indicate that MindMagnet's operating loss offset about 78.6% of the profitability generated by the core research business, suggesting the advertising subsidiary's losses continue to weigh on consolidated results.

On shareholder returns, the board's original cash dividend proposal was reduced at the September 29, 2026 annual meeting following a shareholder motion, with the total dividend amount finalized at KRW 801.76 million.

On the same day, the board resolved to repurchase 477,238 common shares, worth approximately KRW 801.76 million, on the open market through Kiwoom Securities between September 30 and December 29, 2026, with plans to cancel all repurchased shares.

On the business front, the company has expanded its data-tech initiatives, following its June 2026 disclosure of synthetic survey pilot results with the September 2026 launch of the 'CALI' analytics solution combining consumer behavior data.

The upcoming quarterly report covering July-September 2026 should provide a clearer view of the new businesses' revenue contribution and the trajectory of MindMagnet's losses.

08

Bull factors

Strengthened Shareholder Returns

On September 29, 2026, the annual shareholders' meeting and board jointly approved dividends, share buybacks, and share cancellation. The company stated it plans to cancel all repurchased shares, which would meaningfully reduce outstanding share count. However, the amounts involved remain modest relative to market capitalization.

Revenue Recovery in Core Research

Annual revenue in 2025 reached KRW 56.2 billion, up 4.6% year over year and the largest among the four disclosed years. Disclosures covering the most recent fiscal year, released in September 2026, also reported revenue growth and a swing to operating profit.

The turnaround at subsidiary Embrain Public and revenue growth at Embrain Consulting are also understood to have supported the core business recovery.

Expansion into Data-Tech and AI

The company disclosed AI-based synthetic survey pilot results in June 2026 and launched the 'CALI' analytics solution in September 2026, expanding beyond traditional survey research into data technology.

It has also maintained a strategic investment in AI technology firm Post AI, providing room for longer-term business diversification.

09

Bear factors

Four Straight Years of Margin Decline

The operating margin fell steadily from 14.4% in 2022 to 8.6% in 2023 and 3.1% in 2024, before turning negative at -2.2% in 2025. While revenue rebounded in 2025, cost structure improvements did not keep pace, weakening overall income statement strength.

Operating cash flow also declined from KRW 9.42 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.53 billion in 2025, reflecting the margin deterioration in cash generation as well.

Persistent Losses at the Advertising Subsidiary

The advertising business run by subsidiary MindMagnet continues to post losses, and reports indicate this loss offset about 78.6% of the profitability generated by the core research business.

Goodwill impairment, loan guarantees, and borrowings at the subsidiary have also been flagged, keeping subsidiary financial management a variable for consolidated results.

High Quarter-to-Quarter Volatility

In the second quarter of 2025, revenue of KRW 21.8 billion and operating income of KRW 2.54 billion showed clear strength, but the third quarter saw revenue plunge 70.7% quarter over quarter and swing to an operating loss.

Revenue recovered in the fourth quarter, but the operating loss continued, marking two consecutive quarters of losses from the third through fourth quarters. The project-based nature of revenue recognition amplifies quarter-to-quarter volatility, adding uncertainty to the full-year earnings outlook.

10

Risk factors

Economic and Budget Sensitivity

Research order volumes are tied to corporate marketing budgets and public-sector policy survey spending, carrying the risk of reduced project volumes during economic slowdowns. A repeat of sharp single-quarter revenue declines, as seen in the third quarter of 2025, could again pressure full-year profitability.

Subsidiary Financial Risk

Goodwill impairment, loan guarantees, and borrowings associated with advertising subsidiary MindMagnet have been flagged, raising the possibility that further impairment or guarantee-related costs could weigh on consolidated earnings.

With significant profitability divergence across subsidiaries, portfolio management capability remains an important variable.

AI Substitution Risk

There is a risk that the spread of generative AI-based synthetic surveys could erode some demand for traditional panel-based research.

The company itself is openly testing this possibility, and if the pace of its new-business transition fails to keep up with potential erosion of its existing business, it could weigh on the revenue structure.

12

Overall view

Macromill Embrain's annual revenue rebounded to KRW 56.2 billion in 2025, the largest among the four disclosed years, yet the operating margin fell for a fourth consecutive year from 14.4% in 2022 to an operating loss in 2025.

Quarterly results showed high volatility, with strength in the second quarter of 2025 contrasting sharply with a steep downturn in the third quarter, followed by operating losses in both the third and fourth quarters.

At the September 2026 annual shareholders' meeting and board session, a shareholder-return policy combining dividends, share buybacks, and cancellation was approved, marking a notable governance shift, while disclosures for the most recent fiscal year reported revenue growth and a swing to operating profit.

Still, losses at advertising subsidiary MindMagnet, which have offset a substantial portion of the core business's profitability, remain an unresolved challenge.

New initiatives such as the AI-based synthetic survey and new data solutions represent a double-edged factor that could either complement or partially substitute for traditional research demand.

The pace of profitability recovery and the progress of shareholder-return commitments warrant continued monitoring through the next quarterly report and the conclusion of the share buyback program.
