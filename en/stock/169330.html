02

Business structure

Macromill Embrain is a research company founded in 1999 that conducts online and offline surveys, quantitative and qualitative research, and software development. According to the company's profile, it operates a Northeast Asian research panel of 2.7 million people and a global panel of 6.4 million.

Reaching 1.4 million panel members in March 2021 is confirmed in the company's disclosed history. A business analysis on JobKorea, labeled as of March 2024, puts online research above half of research product sales and offline research at roughly 30%.

However, this is an external aggregation with an older reference date, so the latest segment revenue remains unconfirmed. The largest shareholder changed to TJ1 Co., Ltd. in December 2025 following an absorption merger of the former largest shareholder, MACROMILL, INC., and the company was later renamed MACROMILL, INC.

Subsidiaries include Embrain Research, Embrain Public, and Pulse Research, while a business partnership with Post AI began through an equity stake in June 2024. In December 2023, subsidiary Embrain Public acquired a 51% stake in Mind Magnet, becoming its largest shareholder.

The company operates its EZsurvey research solution in-house and, in April 2026, announced a market research support project for startups less than seven years old.

Its scale and structure differ markedly from same-sector listed companies such as NAVER and Samsung SDS, and the revenue of direct competitors was not identified in this review. Results appear to move sharply from quarter to quarter in line with the timing of research project orders.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 54.9 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 53.7 billion in the year ended June 2024, a 2.2% decline, and then rose to KRW 56.2 billion in the year ended June 2025 and KRW 58.9 billion in the year ended June 2026.

Revenue rose for two consecutive fiscal years, and the year ended June 2026 was the largest of the four years, while the year ended June 2024 was the smallest. Profitability weakened sharply in the year ended June 2024.

Operating profit fell from KRW 4.71 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 1.68 billion in the year ended June 2024, a 64.4% decline, and then turned into an operating loss of KRW 1.26 billion in the year ended June 2025.

The year ended June 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 2.7 billion, and the operating margin of 4.6% is a little above half of the 8.6% recorded in the year ended June 2023.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a loss of KRW 842 million in the year ended June 2025 to a profit of KRW 1.76 billion in the year ended June 2026.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.66 billion in the year ended June 2026, below the controlling-shareholder figure, which points to losses within non-controlling interests. Quarterly results are more volatile.

Among the last five quarters, the fourth quarter of 2025 had the largest revenue at KRW 21.8 billion, up 60.2% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 2.54 billion. The first quarter of 2026 revenue fell 54.7% from the prior quarter to KRW 9.88 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 957 million.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 13.5 billion rose 37.1% from the prior quarter but fell 5.3% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 787 million turned profitable compared with the same quarter a year earlier.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.76 billion, the same as the figure for the year ended June 2026. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 1.53 billion in the year ended June 2025 to KRW 4.02 billion in the year ended June 2026. The disclosed results do not separately identify one-off items, so their size could not be confirmed.

Two years of revenue growth and a panel base

Second-quarter 2026 revenue rose 37.1% from the prior quarter but fell 5.3% year on year. Because quarterly revenue scale swings with project orders, the predictability of results is low.

Thin margins and the risk of loss quarters

Because profitable quarters are concentrated in the last four quarters, how long the profit base can be sustained requires confirmation from additional quarterly data.

Cash flow swings and non-controlling losses

Operating cash flow varied widely by year, at KRW 1.97 billion, KRW 4.97 billion, KRW 1.53 billion, and KRW 4.02 billion for the years ended June 2023 through June 2026.

Net income of KRW 1.66 billion in the year ended June 2026 was below the KRW 1.76 billion attributable to controlling shareholders, which points to losses in non-controlling interests. The quality of earnings and the continuity of cash generation are still being tested.

Risk factors

Industry and client budget risk

Research demand is sensitive to corporate advertising and marketing budgets. Because survey orders tend to be adjusted first when budgets shrink, revenue can fluctuate quarter to quarter. The 5.3% year-on-year decline in second-quarter 2026 revenue is one example of this sensitivity showing up in results.

Technology transition risk

As data collection and analysis shift toward AI and automation, pricing and volume in traditional survey outsourcing may be affected. The company emphasizes its in-house solution EZsurvey and AI-based analysis capabilities, but the pace at which this transition affects profitability was not confirmed. Changes in the mix of software revenue and survey outsourcing revenue are the next point to check.

Governance and small-cap risk

The largest shareholder changed to TJ1 Co., Ltd. in December 2025 through an absorption merger, and the company was later renamed MACROMILL, INC. Because the market capitalization is small, share price volatility can be high, and changes in the major shareholder structure can affect the float and trading flow. The details of the executive and major shareholder holding report filed in October 2026 were not confirmed.

Overall view

Macromill Embrain recorded a turnaround to profitability in the year ended June 2026, with revenue of KRW 58.9 billion, operating profit of KRW 2.7 billion, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.76 billion, while the debt-to-equity ratio and operating cash flow also improved.
