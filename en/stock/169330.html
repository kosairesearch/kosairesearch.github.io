02

Business structure

Macromill Embrain was founded in 1998 as Korea Panel Research, received an investment from Japan-listed Macromill in 2012, and merged with Macromill Korea before adopting its current name. The company listed on KOSDAQ in 2020 and has five subsidiaries, including Embrain Public and Mind Magnet.

Its core business is online research, in which it surveys panelists to answer market research demand from corporate clients. According to a financial data aggregator, survey products made up 88% of revenue in the third quarter of 2025, with panel big data at 8% and marketing and consulting at 4%.

Because survey products dominate revenue, results depend heavily on the flow of research demand. The company's own description, as summarized by a securities information site, highlights its large domestic panel infrastructure and system-based research.

The same description says online research takes less time and costs less than offline surveys, which has supported growth even in weak economic conditions. Panel big data is a newer business built on the survey panel; a 2022 technical analysis report recorded its revenue share rising from 0.1% in 2020 to 0.6% in 2021.

Demand typically originates in corporate marketing and product development teams and tends to cluster around advertising campaigns and new product launches. Headcount was 257 as of the end of June 2026. Competition turns on panel size, research quality, and data analysis capability.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was KRW 58.9 billion, up 4.9% from the prior year, extending the growth that began in the year ended June 2025, and it is the largest year among the fiscal years ended June 2023 through June 2026. The year ended June 2024, at KRW 53.7 billion, was the low point.

Operating profit fell 64.4% from KRW 4.71 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 1.68 billion in the year ended June 2024, and the year ended June 2025 posted an operating loss of KRW 1.26 billion.

The year ended June 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 2.7 billion, and the operating margin recovered to 4.6%, still below the 8.6% recorded in the year ended June 2023.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 62.5% to KRW 1.41 billion in the year ended June 2024, moved to a net loss of KRW 842 million in the year ended June 2025, and returned to profit of KRW 1.76 billion in the year ended June 2026.

Total net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.66 billion, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder measure. Operating cash flow rose to KRW 4.02 billion in the year ended June 2026 from KRW 1.53 billion in the year ended June 2025.

The debt ratio eased to 61.9% from 65.9% a year earlier but stays above the 36.5% of the year ended June 2023. Total equity was KRW 28.4 billion at the end of June 2026, of which KRW 26.6 billion belonged to controlling shareholders.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit of KRW 2.54 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 was followed by an operating loss of KRW 957 million in the first quarter of 2026, and the second quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 787 million.

However, second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 13.5 billion was down 5.3% from a year earlier, and quarterly revenue has moved between KRW 13.6 billion and KRW 21.8 billion, so the annual figures alone make the trend hard to read.

Industry analysis

The same description says its large domestic panel infrastructure and system-based research have improved productivity and produced a stable sales portfolio.

Within market research, panel size and data quality are the main points of differentiation, so the more relevant comparison is revenue growth and panel scale within the same research business group.

Improving cash generation

The increase in cash generated from operations over the prior year is the basis for the improvement in cash generation. The debt ratio also fell to 61.9% from 65.9% over the same period. Profit recovery and cash flow improvement moved in the same direction.

Defensive demand for online research

According to the company's description, online research has continued to grow in weak economic conditions because it takes less time and costs less than offline surveys. A large domestic panel infrastructure underpins this advantage.

Survey products accounted for 88% of revenue in the third quarter of 2025, so the business core is clearly defined.

Demand for market research moves with corporate advertising and new product launch budgets. If the economy slows, survey orders may decline and affect revenue directly. The drop to KRW 53.7 billion in revenue for the year ended June 2024 illustrates this linkage.

Quarterly volatility and the possibility of renewed losses

Operating losses occurred in the second quarter of 2025 and the first quarter of 2026, and the year ended June 2025 posted an annual operating loss. If profitability is confined to a quarter or two, sustaining an annual profit becomes difficult. Given the wide gaps between quarters, the durability of profit needs to be confirmed.

Overall view
