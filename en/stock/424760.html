Bellock: Annual Profit Turn Tested by Quarterly Swings

Bellock returned to a full-year operating profit in 2025, but operating losses in each quarter from the first quarter of 2026 through the third quarter make the durability of that recovery the central question.

  1. 1

    Revenue reached KRW 35.4 billion in 2025, up 14.6% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 877 million replaced the KRW 1.96 billion operating loss of 2024.

  2. 2

    Each of the first three quarters of 2026 posted an operating loss, from KRW 1.3 billion in the first quarter to KRW 130 million in the second and KRW 50.83 million in the third.

  3. 3

    Net income attributable to controlling shareholders flipped direction by quarter: positive in the fourth quarter of 2025 and the second quarter of 2026, negative in the first and third quarters of 2026, with a four-quarter total of KRW 1.26 billion.

  4. 4

    The business is widening from security software and services into physical security such as anti-drone systems and AI video analytics, with four core products shown at a March 2026 exhibition.

  5. 5

    The Korea Exchange designated the stock as a caution-level issue in April and again in July 2026 over concentrated trading by a small number of accounts, during a period of high price volatility.

02

Business structure

Bellock is an information security company founded in 2009, headquartered in Ulsan. Its main businesses are security solution supply, construction and maintenance, security services built on those solutions, and security product sales.

A Leading Investment & Securities research note dated November 27, 2023 put the 3Q 2023 revenue mix at security solutions 56.8%, security services about 43.0%, and security products 0.2%. The latest segment mix should be checked in the business section of the periodic report.

The core customers are national infrastructure operators, mainly power generation. The same research said security transactions have accumulated across about 40 power plants, and a May 2024 press report said products have been supplied mainly to power generation subsidiaries of Korea Electric Power Corporation.

The company plans to widen its customer base to public and financial institutions on that reference.

The product lineup consists of the FOCS firewall policy management solution, the B-CMS information security regulation management system, the B-Aegis anti-drone integrated control solution, and the i-object intelligent video analytics solution.

In 2023 it absorbed the AI video analytics company VCI through a merger, adding video analytics to its scope. Press headlines also introduced development of the B-Helios anti-drone edge tracker. Competition differs by segment.

Ahnlab is a reference point in information security solutions, S-1 in physical security, and Samsung SDS and LG CNS in large-scale system integration. Because Bellock relies on power sector references, its revenue scale is far smaller than these large operators.

At the eGISEC 2026 exhibition in March 2026, the company said it was networking with overseas buyers to find export partners.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 27.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 26.4 billion in 2023, a 3.0% decline, then rose 16.9% to KRW 30.9 billion in 2024 and 14.6% to KRW 35.4 billion in 2025, a two-year run of growth from 2024 through 2025.

Operating profit moved from KRW 1.62 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 366 million in 2023, which turned into a loss of KRW 1.96 billion in 2024 before returning to an operating profit of KRW 877 million in 2025. The operating margin was 5.9% in 2022, -1.4% in 2023, -6.4% in 2024 and 2.5% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 210 million in 2022 to a loss of KRW 4.59 billion in 2023, narrowed to a loss of KRW 1.19 billion in 2024, and turned to a profit of KRW 2.13 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed with the same amounts in each year.

The 2023 net loss was much larger than the operating loss, so how non-operating items and taxes were recorded should be checked in the notes to the periodic report.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 4.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 103 million in 2023 and minus KRW 50.17 million in 2024, before rising to KRW 3.31 billion in 2025. Equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 27.8 billion, and the debt ratio fell from 99.9% in 2022 to 30.2% in 2025.

Quarterly, the fourth quarter of 2025 was the largest, with revenue of KRW 15 billion (up 106.4% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 2.18 billion. The first quarter of 2026 revenue dropped 73.8% to KRW 3.92 billion and swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.3 billion.

Revenue recovered to KRW 6.13 billion in the second quarter, up 56.3%, but an operating loss of KRW 130 million remained. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 6.07 billion was down 1.1% from the prior quarter and down 16.4% year on year, and the operating loss of KRW 50.83 million was smaller than a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders showed a loss of KRW 623 million in the third quarter of 2026, a wider loss than the KRW 304 million loss in the same quarter of 2025.

In May 2024 the company raised its revenue target to the KRW 30 billion range and expected operating profit of at least KRW 3 billion, but 2024 actually produced revenue of KRW 30.9 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.96 billion. Details of one-off items should be checked in the notes to the periodic report.

Coverage of the eGISEC 2026 exhibition in March 2026 said that drone threats, AI-driven cyberattacks and supply chain risks have drawn more attention from visitors. This trend overlaps with demand for Bellock's anti-drone solutions.

The company said it is extending from security software into physical security areas such as anti-drone systems and video analytics. Where the industry sits in its cycle will become clearer as quarterly results and contract disclosures accumulate. On competition, the comparison set depends on the segment.

Ahnlab is the typical reference in security software and S-1 in physical security, while Samsung SDS and LG CNS may overlap in power and public system integration buyers. Bellock occupies a niche built on power sector references and is far smaller than the large operators in revenue.

A 2023 research note interpreted the high security requirements for national core infrastructure such as power generation as a source of entry barriers.

However, the revenue pattern points to lumpy results driven by the timing of public and power sector orders, as shown by the gap between KRW 15 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 3.92 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

Bull factors

Annual profit turnaround in 2025

Operating profit of KRW 877 million in 2025 replaced a KRW 1.96 billion operating loss in 2024, and the operating margin rose from -6.4% to 2.5%. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders also turned to a KRW 2.13 billion profit. The debt ratio fell from 99.9% in 2022 to 30.2% in 2025. The sequence points to a recovery in core profitability.

Expansion into integrated physical and cyber products

At the March 2026 exhibition the company showed four core products covering firewall policy management, regulatory compliance, anti-drone systems and video analytics. The B-Aegis anti-drone system was highlighted as drawing concentrated attention.

Rising drone threats and AI-driven attacks may support demand for this portfolio. The company has said it plans to widen cooperation with overseas partners.

Power sector references and an asset base

A 2023 research note cited security transactions accumulated across about 40 power plants. Equity of KRW 27.8 billion at the end of 2025 is entirely attributable to controlling shareholders, and the price-to-book ratio sits below 1 times.

This asset base could serve as a financial cushion as the company expands into new customer groups. The plan to extend into public and financial institutions also builds on the same power sector references.

Bear factors

Operating losses continue through 2026

Operating losses were recorded in each of the first three quarters of 2026, at KRW 1.3 billion, KRW 130 million and KRW 50.83 million. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 6.07 billion fell 16.4% from a year earlier.

The third-quarter net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 623 million was wider than the KRW 304 million loss a year earlier. The annual turnaround has not yet carried into quarterly results, which is the main bearish point.

Revenue concentrated in individual quarters

Revenue of KRW 15 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 fell 73.8% to KRW 3.92 billion in the first quarter of 2026. Such a pattern, with revenue bunching and then dropping sharply, reads as driven by the timing of public and power sector orders.

With a four-quarter total of KRW 1.26 billion in net income attributable to controlling shareholders, a single loss quarter can move the cumulative figure sharply. Low predictability of earnings is a bearish factor.

Trading overheating and market alert history

In April 2026 the stock was designated as a caution-level issue on a single-account net buying criterion, and on July 27, 2026 it was designated again over a high share of buying by a small number of accounts. The two designations can be read as signs that concentrated trading amplified price volatility.

If market alert levels rise, trading restrictions could follow, creating a risk that the share price decouples from business results.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly profit has repeatedly changed direction, with a profit in the fourth quarter of 2025, a net loss in the first quarter of 2026, a profit in the second quarter and a net loss in the third.

In some quarters operating results and net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved in different directions, so the weight of non-operating items should be checked in the periodic report. A single weak quarter could erase the annual turnaround.

Trading controls and share structure

The stock was designated as a caution-level issue twice in 2026, in April and July. A 2-for-1 share consolidation took effect on April 28, 2026, raising the par value to KRW 200, and the new shares were listed on May 20, 2026.

If sharp price increases continue, the stock could move to higher alert stages, leaving a continuing risk of trading restrictions.

Business expansion and competition

New areas such as anti-drone systems and AI video analytics have not yet been proven at large revenue scale. Overseas sales depend on partner cooperation and follow-up talks, and their contribution cannot be sized until contract values and timing are disclosed.

The company competes with established players such as Ahnlab and S-1 across security and physical security, and its smaller revenue scale may limit its bargaining power.

Overall view

Bellock turned from a KRW 1.96 billion operating loss in 2024 to a KRW 877 million operating profit in 2025 on revenue of KRW 35.4 billion, and its debt ratio fell from 99.9% in 2022 to 30.2% in 2025.

However, operating losses have continued for three consecutive quarters from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026, and third-quarter revenue fell 16.4% from a year earlier.

A pattern of revenue bunching into single quarters and then dropping sharply has recurred, so the stability of annual profit is still being tested.

Expansion into anti-drone systems and video analytics, along with overseas export discussions, has a clear direction, but the scale reflected in results should be verified through disclosures.

The two caution-level designations in April and July 2026 remain on record as episodes in which concentrated trading amplified price moves.

Bullish factors include the return to profit, the asset base and power sector references, while bearish factors include quarterly earnings volatility and the trading overheating history. The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and first-quarter 2027 results.
