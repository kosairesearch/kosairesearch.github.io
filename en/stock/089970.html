Report overview

VM: Profit Turnaround Faces a Test of Durability

VM returned to operating profit in 2025, and by the third quarter of 2026 quarterly revenue reached KRW 84.9 billion and operating profit KRW 29.9 billion. However, net income attributable to controlling shareholders has declined for two consecutive quarters since peaking in the first quarter of 2026, so the durability of profit is the key point to track.

  3. 3

02

Business structure

VM is a semiconductor front-end equipment maker whose core product is etching equipment. Some securities analysts describe it as the only domestic etching equipment maker in Korea.

According to a financial data service's aggregation, semiconductor equipment and goods made up 98% of revenue in the second quarter of 2026, with other businesses at 2%. Its main products are poly etch and metal etch equipment, and both product lines are cited as beneficiaries of customer investment expansion.

Its main customer is SK hynix, and the company attributed the increase in 2025 revenue mainly to higher sales to key customers on strong memory demand.

Because equipment purchases follow customers' facility investment, revenue is directly tied to that spending, and analysts explained that results were weak in 2023 and 2024 when a key customer reduced equipment purchases.

A single supply contract worth KRW 78.3 billion was disclosed on January 16, 2026, with an execution period stated as January to May 2026. Same-sector listed peers include Wonik IPS and Jusung Engineering, but the company's specialization in etching equipment is cited as its differentiator. High customer concentration is both a strength and the largest variable.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue fell 81.6% from KRW 141.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 26 billion in 2023, then rose 170.2% to KRW 70.3 billion in 2024 and 105.5% to KRW 144.4 billion in 2025. 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025 for revenue, and 2023 was the smallest.

Revenue has grown for two consecutive years, from 2024 through 2025, after the 2023 low. Operating profit went from KRW 30.9 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 11 billion in 2023, and stayed in loss in 2024 at KRW 8.6 billion with a narrower loss.

In 2025 operating profit was KRW 24.7 billion, a swing back to profit, and operating margin ran at 21.9% in 2022, -42.2% in 2023, -12.2% in 2024 and 17.1% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 27.9 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 6.98 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 3.45 billion in 2024 and KRW 26.1 billion in 2025.

Securities analysts attributed the 2023 to 2024 losses to a key customer reducing equipment purchases, with some costs deferred, and said one-time costs were fully recognized after the second quarter of 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 36.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 9.04 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 50.8 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, up 39.5% from the prior quarter, and peaked at KRW 88.9 billion with operating profit of KRW 30.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

In the second quarter of 2026 revenue fell 18.7% to KRW 72.3 billion and operating profit fell 33.5% to KRW 20.1 billion.

In the third quarter revenue recovered to KRW 84.9 billion, up 17.4% from the prior quarter and 132.9% year on year, and operating profit reached KRW 29.9 billion, up 49.1% from the prior quarter and 230.6% year on year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 25.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then fell 30.0% to KRW 17.9 billion in the second quarter and 0.8% to KRW 17.8 billion in the third, a two-quarter decline.

Over the latest four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 72.4 billion.

Operating cash flow was negative at KRW -8.21 billion in 2022 and KRW -15.6 billion in 2023, then turned positive at KRW 22.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 18.3 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for semiconductor equipment depends heavily on customers' memory investment cycles. The company and securities analysts both cite the resumption of customer investment as the backdrop for the 2025 earnings recovery.

Hana Securities said in a December 2025 report that transition investment and new M15x investment by customers were the main drivers of revenue growth. The same report expected both poly etch and metal etch equipment to benefit.

Shinyoung Securities assessed that equipment revenue recognition proceeded as planned after SK hynix resumed investment.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 36.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 88.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026, but where that places the company in the cycle depends on customer investment plans.

In terms of competitive position, VM is concentrated in etching equipment, so product and customer concentration exist together. Its process area differs from other listed equipment makers, which limits direct substitution.

The single KRW 78.3 billion contract disclosed in January 2026 was cited by securities analysts for its contribution to earnings visibility. The company's explanation that strong memory demand drove 2025 revenue growth is consistent with this picture.

Bull factors

Operating turnaround visible in the numbers

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 24.7 billion in 2025, and third-quarter 2026 operating profit reached KRW 29.9 billion, up 230.6% year on year. Even though second-quarter 2026 revenue declined, operating profit recovered in the third quarter and the profitability direction was maintained.

Quarterly revenue grew from KRW 36.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 84.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Customer investment resumption and product benefits

Hana Securities said in a December 2025 report that both poly and metal etch equipment were expected to benefit.

Financial capacity and improved cash flow

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 177.2 billion and total liabilities were KRW 71.5 billion. Operating cash flow moved from KRW -15.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 22.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 18.3 billion in 2025, staying positive.

The KRW 78.3 billion single supply contract disclosed in January 2026 was cited by securities analysts as a factor raising earnings visibility.

Bear factors

Customer concentration and capex-cycle dependence

Revenue is directly linked to capital spending by its main customer, SK hynix. The operating losses of KRW 11 billion in 2023 and KRW 8.6 billion in 2024 coincided with a period when the customer reduced equipment purchases. If the same structure repeats, earnings could return to losses.

Volatility in quarterly earnings

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders has declined for two straight quarters since the first-quarter 2026 peak, which shows the volatility of quarterly earnings. Revenue fell 18.7% from the prior quarter in the second quarter of 2026, and operating profit fell 33.5%. Quarterly results that swing with single-contract timing and customer orders are a bearish factor.

Rising leverage and limited shareholder payouts

The debt ratio rose from 32.8% in 2022 to 40.4% in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW -15.6 billion in 2023. Cash dividend yield is close to nil.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and capex cycle

Revenue is tied to the timing of a small number of customers' capital spending, so cuts or delays hit earnings directly. The losses of 2023 and 2024 are a case where this risk materialized. Customer investment plans are visible only within what is disclosed, leaving high forecast uncertainty.

Policy and export controls

Semiconductor equipment is a category easily affected by cross-border export controls and trade measures. Equipment order timing can shift with customers' production-site decisions or regulatory changes. This risk tends to appear first in policy announcements rather than in company disclosures.

Earnings volatility and financial burden

Net income has declined since peaking in the first quarter of 2026, so quarterly swings could weigh on annual stability. The debt ratio rose to 40.4% in 2025 from 32.8% in 2022. Securities analysts' view that one-time costs would not recur could also prove wrong and warrants consideration.

Overall view

VM returned to profit in 2025, and quarterly revenue and operating profit grew substantially through the third quarter of 2026, with the resumption of customer facility investment cited as the backdrop. First-quarter 2026 was the peak, with revenue of KRW 88.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 30.1 billion.

After a second-quarter decline, operating profit recovered to KRW 29.9 billion in the third quarter. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders has declined for two consecutive quarters since the KRW 25.6 billion peak in the first quarter of 2026, so the earnings path across quarters is mixed.

Bullish factors include demand for etching equipment, resumed customer investment, and the visibility of the KRW 78.3 billion single contract. Bearish factors include dependence on a few customers led by SK hynix, quarterly earnings volatility, and a rising debt ratio.

The 2023 to 2024 losses coincided with reduced customer investment, so the direction of the investment cycle remains the largest variable for earnings.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report, and the key question is whether quarterly profit holds near the third-quarter 2026 level.
