02

Business structure

Seah Holdings is a holding company whose manufacturing subsidiaries produce special steel. Manufacturing accounted for 99% of revenue in Q2 2026. In January 2026 it completed a share exchange with SeAH Special Steel and made it a wholly owned subsidiary.

Consolidated special steel affiliates include SeAH Besteel Holdings, the Changwon special steel subsidiary and SeAH Besteel. In Q1 2026 the special steel segment posted higher sales and better operating profit, and both operating subsidiaries reported stable results.

Overseas, the group runs special steel sales companies in the United States, Japan, Vietnam, Thailand and India. SeAH Superalloy, a North American alloy company established in May 2024, makes and sells special alloys.

The company says it is focusing on quality management systems to expand sales into semiconductor, aerospace and defense markets. Segment revenue shares and customer mix are items to confirm in the business report.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue in 2025 was KRW 6.61 trillion, up 9.9% from the prior year, and operating profit was KRW 175.5 billion, up 76.9%. 2024 was the smallest year of 2022 to 2025, with revenue of KRW 6.02 trillion and operating profit of KRW 99.2 billion.

Operating margin moved from 2.9% in 2022 to 3.1% in 2023, 1.6% in 2024 and 2.7% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 64.6 billion in 2025, up 94.3%, but still below KRW 123.3 billion in 2022.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 85.9 billion in 2025, recovering from KRW 34.2 billion in 2024. Quarterly operating profit rose to KRW 57.7 billion in Q1 2026 (up 40.5% from the prior quarter) and KRW 98 billion in Q2 2026 (up 70.0%).

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 1.92 trillion, up 15.1% year on year, while operating profit fell to KRW 1.25 billion, down 97.6%. Company commentary cited a 48.4% year-on-year rise in Q1 2026 operating profit.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 49.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 265.2 billion in 2024 and then fell to KRW 106.3 billion in 2025, remaining positive. Whether the Q3 2026 drop reflects one-off costs is a point to confirm in the business report notes and disclosures.

05

Industry analysis

The special steel cycle was in a soft phase through the third quarter of 2025. Nine-month 2025 revenue rose 6.9% while operating profit fell 7.4%, a period in which the company focused on protecting profitability. The company attributed the 2025 recovery in part to higher steel demand.

It said it is securing working capital through local investment and inventory reduction, and improving its quality management system to expand sales into semiconductor, aerospace and defense markets.

SeAH Steel Holdings, a separate listed company in the same group, reported Q3 2026 provisional revenue of KRW 1.221 trillion, up 32.3% year on year, and provisional operating profit of KRW 93.3 billion. These figures belong to SeAH Steel Holdings, not Seah Holdings.

Seah Holdings' competitive position should be read against its special steel subsidiaries and overseas sales network. The key variable for demand is whether sales expand into high-value sectors such as defense and aerospace.

06

Outlook

The company's quantitative targets are shareholder returns and a price-to-book target. In its value-up plan progress disclosure published on March 27, 2026, it set a detailed target of a price-to-book ratio of 0.5x or higher by 2027. It targets KRW 50 billion in share buybacks and cancellations from 2026 to 2028.

Treasury cancellations were carried out on May 15, 2026 (71,000 shares) and September 18, 2026 (187,000 shares). The payout ratio for fiscal 2025 was presented at 25.5%, and the fiscal 2025 dividend amount rose from fiscal 2024.

On the business side, the key variables are whether the Q1 2026 special steel sales growth continues and whether defense and aerospace demand converts into sales. The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, which will contain Q4 and full-year 2026 results. The cause of the Q3 2026 operating profit drop and the Q4 trend are points to verify in that report.

Bull factors

Profit recovery and cash generation in 2025

Expanded shareholder return policy

The company targets KRW 50 billion in share buybacks and cancellations for 2026 to 2028. Treasury cancellations in May and September 2026 reduced shares outstanding. The fiscal 2025 payout ratio was presented at 25.5%, and the dividend amount rose from the prior year.

Special steel sales base and overseas network

In Q1 2026 the special steel segment posted higher sales and better operating profit. The group has special steel sales companies in several countries overseas. The company is improving its quality management system to expand sales into semiconductor, aerospace and defense markets.

09

Bear factors

Sharp drop in Q3 operating profit

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 1.25 billion, down 98.7% from the prior quarter and down 97.6% year on year. Revenue in the same quarter rose 15.1% year on year to KRW 1.92 trillion. Because profit fell while revenue grew, the cause needs to be confirmed.

Financial burden and thin operating margin

The debt-to-equity ratio at end-2025 was 100.4%, above 97.0% in 2022. Total liabilities were KRW 3.1 trillion against total equity of KRW 3.09 trillion. Operating margin stayed low at 1.6% in 2024 and 2.7% in 2025.

Recovery still below earlier levels

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 64.6 billion in 2025 but remains below KRW 123.3 billion in 2022. Quarterly profit dropped sharply after Q2 2026. The four-quarter total of net income attributable to controlling shareholders is KRW 96.7 billion.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly operating profit swung from KRW 98 billion in Q2 2026 to KRW 1.25 billion in Q3. Wide quarterly swings make the annual trend harder to read. Whether the cause is one-off or structural will determine how durable the recovery is.

Financial leverage

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 100.4% at end-2025. Of total equity of KRW 3.09 trillion, the controlling shareholders' stake is KRW 2.22 trillion. Whether the buyback and dividend plans move in step with financial capacity needs to be checked.

Subsidiary structure and industry dependence

Subsidiary structure is changing, including the January 2026 full acquisition of SeAH Special Steel. The company attributed part of the 2025 recovery to higher steel demand. A slowdown in special steel demand could hit subsidiary earnings together.

Overall view

Seah Holdings recovered from its 2024 low, with 2025 operating profit of KRW 175.5 billion, up 76.9%. Operating profit also rose to KRW 57.7 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 98 billion in Q2 2026. However, Q3 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 1.25 billion, down 98.7%, so quarterly volatility remains large.
