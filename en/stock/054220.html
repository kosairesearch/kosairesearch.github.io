02

Business structure

VitzroSys manufactures electric motors and generators as well as electric conversion, supply and control equipment, and has been listed on KOSDAQ since December 2001.

According to the company, its core business is building systems for railways, nuclear power and industrial and public infrastructure, with a long record of railway integrated information system and platform screen door contracts.

In nuclear power, the company announced in early 2026 a contract to supply field control panels for Shin Hanul Units 3 and 4 to Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power.

Overseas, it is carrying out a telecom infrastructure project in Indonesia, and a May 2025 report put the unrecognized backlog for that project at about KRW 13.7 billion.

A consolidated electrical instrumentation subsidiary has expanded its design and construction work for private developers, which the company cited as one driver of first-half 2026 growth.

The company says it intends to grow electrical instrumentation and power automation into a new pillar tied to AI data center investment and rising power demand. Customers are concentrated in public bodies and national infrastructure buyers, which makes quarterly revenue sensitive to project timing.

Against large Korean power equipment makers the company is small, and its differentiation appears to lie in systems integration and control work rather than in manufacturing scale.

04

Earnings analysis

According to periodic reports, annual revenue grew from KRW 8.99 billion in 2022 to KRW 19.6 billion in 2023 (up 117.5% year over year), KRW 25.7 billion in 2024 (up 31.2% year over year), and KRW 44.8 billion in 2025 (up 74.7% year over year), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Among 2022 through 2025, 2025 was the largest year.

Operating profit was an operating loss of KRW 2.36 billion in 2022, an operating loss of KRW 7.06 billion in 2023, and an operating loss of KRW 6.14 billion in 2024, so losses continued, and in 2025 the company turned profitable with an operating profit of KRW 57.73 million.

Operating margin changed from -26.2% in 2022, -36.1% in 2023, and -23.9% in 2024 to 0.1% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a net loss of KRW 2.38 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 6.34 billion in 2023, and a net loss of KRW 10.3 billion in 2024, before turning profitable at KRW 1.52 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 1.52 billion in 2025.

A company official explained the turnaround to profitability in 2025 as the result of eliminating unnecessary costs and reducing selling and administrative expenses.

Cash flow from operating activities turned from -KRW 4.24 billion in 2022, -KRW 1.89 billion in 2023, and -KRW 2.56 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.48 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the company's scale grew from revenue of KRW 10.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 0.31 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to revenue of KRW 14.9 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (up 38.7% quarter over quarter), revenue of KRW 15.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 3.2% quarter over quarter), and revenue of KRW 23.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (up 51.2% quarter over quarter).

Operating profit in the second quarter of 2026 was KRW 0.33 billion, up 333.7% from KRW 75.29 million in the first quarter of 2026.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue declined to KRW 16 billion (down 30.8% quarter over quarter) and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 0.3 billion, with a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 0.84 billion.

The combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the most recent four quarters (the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026) was KRW 2.93 billion.

Industry analysis

The power equipment market is being discussed alongside rising AI data center investment and growing power demand. In its first-half 2026 results release, VitzroSys said it would push electrical instrumentation and power automation work in line with that trend.

Large Korean power equipment makers such as LS ELECTRIC, Hyosung Heavy Industries and HD Hyundai Electric operate at a much larger scale in transformers and switchgear.

VitzroSys's core work is system projects built to client specifications, including railway information systems, nuclear control panels and public infrastructure, so its order and revenue recognition patterns differ from those of the large makers.

In railways, a SRT integrated information system project was cited in a May 2025 report with about KRW 11.9 billion of unrecognized backlog. In nuclear power, the Shin Hanul Units 3 and 4 control panel contract was described in early 2026.

These projects follow specific client schedules, so the timing of revenue recognition can drive quarterly swings. On the cost side, the company has named project-level profitability management and stronger cost competitiveness as priorities.

Beyond the direction of AI-related equipment spending, no verified quantitative industry indicator specific to this company was available.

Planned expansion into AI data center and power infrastructure

Capital restructuring and the reduction schedule

The 66.6% free capital reduction approved on November 17, 2026 is aimed at offsetting accumulated deficits. Media reports from 2018 described total equity of -KRW 99.5 billion and complete capital impairment.

Share consolidation and this free capital reduction together will sharply reduce the number of shares outstanding. Trading will be suspended during the reduction period.

Dependence on public buyers and quarterly volatility

Because revenue recognition depends on project timing, quarterly sales can swing sharply, as the decline in the third quarter of 2026 shows.

Risk factors

Capital structure and listing status

Total liabilities of KRW 34.6 billion exceed total equity of KRW 19.1 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio is 180.7%. The free capital reduction lowers paid-in capital without changing total equity, so financial room depends on a recovery in operating profit.

The company has a history of listing-related issues, so audit opinions and financial indicators remain items to watch.

Project profitability and revenue recognition

Large system projects can see quarterly margins swing on cost changes and schedule delays. The move from a second-quarter profit to a third-quarter loss in 2026 illustrates that pattern.

The company emphasizes project-level profitability management, but the effect should be confirmed in the operating margin of the next annual business report.

Trading volatility after share count reduction

Reports said the share price fell sharply intraday on November 18, 2026, after the free capital reduction was disclosed. A 5-to-1 share consolidation was decided in March 2026 and trading was suspended.

Around the listing of new shares after the reduction, the float and trading conditions will change, which could raise price volatility.
