02

Business structure

SP Systems, founded in 1988, makes gantry robots, factory automation systems and smart factory solutions for industrial customers. Under the business report for 2022, its segments were Robotics at 52%, ICT at 42% and Battery at 6% of sales.

The mix has since shifted: according to a JobKorea company profile, gantry robots and automation peripherals account for 47%, smart factory solutions for 41%, battery parts for 8% and automotive parts for 4%.

The smart factory business reflects sales from MI Cube Solution, a subsidiary acquired in 2021 and booked under the ICT segment. A 2023 media report said the customer base had broadened from automakers Kia and GM to construction, shipbuilding, secondary batteries, food and power generation.

A 2024 report said the company had supplied automation systems to 118 factory lines. A 2023 analyst estimate said battery segment sales could exceed KRW 150 billion within three years on a one-line supply basis, but that outlook depended on mass production timing.

A disclosed supply contract with Samgi covers KRW 8.9 billion of automotive valve body processing automation robot systems, equal to 12.5% of recent sales, with a contract period ending July 31, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 53.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 60.5 billion in 2023 (up 13.8% year on year) and KRW 71 billion in 2024 (up 17.5%), then fell to KRW 61.6 billion in 2025 (down 13.3%). Of the years from 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest for sales.

Operating profit of KRW 3.39 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 304 million in 2023, widened to an operating loss of KRW 2.8 billion in 2024, and narrowed to an operating loss of KRW 764 million in 2025. The operating margin moved from 6.4% in 2022 to -0.5% in 2023, -3.9% in 2024 and -1.2% in 2025.

The largest sales year, 2024, also carried the widest operating loss, so top-line growth did not translate into margin improvement. Attributable net income of KRW 1.75 billion in 2022 became an attributable net loss of KRW 806 million in 2023, KRW 2.12 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.18 billion in 2025.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 5.29 billion in 2025, smaller than the attributable loss because non-controlling interests contributed a positive amount.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 64.9 billion, of which KRW 52 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 34 billion. The debt ratio went from 58.5% in 2022 to 46.3% in 2023 and 52.5% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was negative KRW 4.47 billion in 2023 and turned positive at KRW 4.98 billion in 2025. On a quarterly basis, sales were KRW 15.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 15 billion in the fourth quarter (down 5.6% quarter on quarter).

They bottomed at KRW 10.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 27.9%), then rose to KRW 11.9 billion in the second quarter (up 10.0%) and KRW 13.2 billion in the third quarter (up 11.0%), marking two consecutive quarters of growth.

Operating profit of KRW 1.86 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 turned back into an operating loss of KRW 1.59 billion in the first quarter of 2026. The loss narrowed to KRW 184 million in the second quarter, then widened to KRW 487 million in the third quarter.

Versus the year-earlier quarter, the third-quarter operating loss was smaller. Attributable net income of KRW 251 million in the second quarter of 2026 gave way to an attributable net loss of KRW 841 million in the third quarter. The attributable net loss for the latest four quarters totals KRW 5.48 billion.

05

Industry analysis

SP Systems operates in industrial robots and automation equipment, a market shaped by automaker capital spending and the pace of secondary battery capacity additions. A 2023 media report said the company had moved beyond automakers toward construction, shipbuilding, batteries, food and power generation.

A 2023 analyst report said the automotive share of its robotics business had fallen below 10%. Battery-related orders follow equipment investment cycles, which creates a lag between order disclosures and quarterly sales.

Listed peers in the same sector include collaborative robot makers Doosan Robotics and Rainbow Robotics, and logistics automation company Hyundai Movex. Compared with these companies, SP Systems leans more toward gantry robots and turnkey factory systems.

Because each project is large relative to sales, a single order can move quarterly results considerably, as the KRW 8.9 billion Samgi contract, equal to 12.5% of recent sales, illustrates.

The key variables for the industry outlook are whether customer capacity expansion picks up and whether customer investment schedules slip.

Outlook

The next regular report is the 2026 business report, and the first checkpoint is how full-year 2026 sales and operating results compare with 2025. Sales have recovered since the first quarter of 2026, but third-quarter sales remain below the year-earlier level.

Operating losses narrowed in the second quarter and widened again in the third, so quarterly swings are large. Among disclosed orders, the KRW 8.9 billion Samgi contract had a contract period ending July 31, 2026, so the size of later order disclosures is worth tracking.

In the battery business, a 2023 media report cited medium-term revenue potential, but the recent regular reports do not present battery segment revenue as a separate line item.

Media reports said the share of automakers such as Kia and GM has declined, so how fast new customer orders show up in results is another item to watch. The fourth-quarter 2026 results will first appear in a preliminary release in early 2027 and then be verified in the regular report.

Whether the fourth-quarter operating result improves from the third-quarter level will be a key test of whether annual losses continue.

Bull factors

Two straight quarters of sales growth

Sales bottomed at KRW 10.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then rose to KRW 11.9 billion in the second quarter and KRW 13.2 billion in the third. The recovery is the starting point of the bull case, though third-quarter sales remain 16.9% below the year-earlier quarter.

Attributable profit turned positive in Q2 2026

The operating loss narrowed to KRW 184 million in the second quarter of 2026, and attributable net income turned to KRW 251 million. That quarter shows the direction of narrowing losses, though the profit did not carry into the third quarter.

Diversifying customer base and business scope

A 2023 report described a broadening of the customer base beyond automakers into construction, shipbuilding, batteries, food and power generation. A 2024 report cited automation supply to 118 factory lines, and lower dependence on any single sector can cushion industry swings.

09

Bear factors

Three years of operating losses and renewed widening

Operating losses continued for three years from 2023 to 2025, and the KRW 2.8 billion loss in 2024 was the largest in that period. The KRW 487 million operating loss in the third quarter of 2026 was wider than the second quarter, and the attributable net loss for the latest four quarters totals KRW 5.48 billion.

Sales decline and volatile top line

Annual sales fell 13.3% to KRW 61.6 billion in 2025, and third-quarter 2026 sales were 16.9% below the year-earlier quarter. Although sales have grown for two quarters, they remain below the 2024 level on an annual basis.

Lumpy orders and capital burden

The KRW 8.9 billion Samgi contract was equal to 12.5% of recent sales. Because individual orders are large, a delay in one or two contracts can swing quarterly results, and the attributable net loss widened to KRW 6.18 billion in 2025 against controlling equity of KRW 52 billion.

Risk factors

Profitability risk

The operating margin stayed negative at -0.5% in 2023, -3.9% in 2024 and -1.2% in 2025. If cost and fixed-cost burdens absorb sales growth, a return to profit will be delayed, and large quarterly swings such as the KRW 1.59 billion loss in the first quarter of 2026 make results harder to forecast.

Customer and order concentration risk

Equipment orders from battery and automotive customers depend on when those customers invest, so orders can bunch up or slip.

Because single contracts are large relative to sales, a gap in follow-on orders after a contract ends could leave a hole in revenue, as the Samgi contract period ending July 31, 2026 illustrates.

Financial and liquidity risk

The debt ratio rose to 52.5% in 2025 from 46.3% in 2023. Operating cash flow was negative KRW 4.47 billion in 2023 and turned positive at KRW 4.98 billion in 2025, so continuity still needs to be confirmed. As a small-cap KOSDAQ stock, the shares can be volatile, which may also affect financing conditions.

Overall view

SP Systems reached its sales peak at KRW 71 billion in 2024 before falling to KRW 61.6 billion in 2025, and operating losses continued for three years from 2023 to 2025. In 2026, sales have grown for two quarters since the first-quarter low, and attributable net income turned positive in the second quarter.

However, the operating loss widened again in the third quarter, and an attributable net loss of KRW 841 million means the durability of profit is not yet shown. The business spans gantry robots, smart factory solutions and battery parts, with a customer base broadened beyond automakers.

On valuation, the share price sits below book value attributable to controlling shareholders, but a net loss over the latest four quarters leaves no earnings-based multiple.

Bull factors are the sales recovery and narrowing losses, while bear factors are three years of operating losses, third-quarter 2026 sales down year on year, and reliance on individual large orders.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 business report and the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results due in early 2027.
