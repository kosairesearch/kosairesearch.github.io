Report overview

CJ ENM: Recovery Tested by a Weak TV Ad Market

CJ ENM's operating profit recovered to KRW 132.9 billion in 2025, but revenue has fallen for three straight quarters through Q3 2026 and controlling-shareholder earnings have swung between quarters, so the durability of the recovery is the key question.

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

CJ ENM operates four segments: Media Platform, Film & Drama, Music, and Commerce. Based on the Q1 2026 revenue mix reported by Daishin Securities in May 2026, Film & Drama accounted for 34%, Media Platform 25%, Commerce 28%, and Music 13%.

The Media Platform segment centers on the OTT service TVING and TV advertising from broadcast channels. In Q2 2026, Media Platform revenue rose 19.0% YoY to KRW 380 billion and posted an operating profit of KRW 11 billion after returning to profit.

In the same quarter, TVING recorded revenue of KRW 140.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 6 billion, with 9.7 million monthly active users.

The Film & Drama segment, anchored by subsidiary Studio Dragon and global studio FIFTH SEASON, posted Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 190.2 billion and an operating loss of KRW 10.5 billion. Studio Dragon's Q2 2026 revenue rose 26.9% to KRW 145.3 billion, with operating profit of KRW 15.4 billion.

The Music segment, built on Mnet Plus fan services and artist IP, reported Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 230.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 10.9 billion. Commerce, driven by mobile live commerce, reported Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 402.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 26 billion.

Advertisers are offered integrated ad products built around core IP. In OTT, a TVING and Wavve merger has been cited as a way to strengthen competitiveness, while in music the company competes for artist IP with SM Entertainment, JYP Entertainment, and YG Entertainment.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 4.79 trillion in 2022 to KRW 4.37 trillion in 2023, down 8.8% year on year, then rose to KRW 5.23 trillion in 2024, up 19.8%, making 2024 the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Revenue in 2025 was KRW 5.13 trillion, down 1.9% year on year.

Operating profit was KRW 137.4 billion in 2022, turned into an operating loss of KRW 14.6 billion in 2023, and returned to a profit of KRW 104.5 billion in 2024. Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 132.9 billion, up 27.2% year on year.

The operating margin ran at 2.9% in 2022, -0.3% in 2023, 2.0% in 2024, and 2.6% in 2025. Controlling-shareholder net results were losses of KRW 120.1 billion in 2022, KRW 315.9 billion in 2023, and KRW 503.4 billion in 2024, before turning to net income of KRW 29.5 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, a different measure, was KRW 17 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio at year-end 2025 was 151.5%, up from 137.8% in 2022. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 1.4 trillion in 2024 to KRW 1.17 trillion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q4 2025 operating profit was KRW 86 billion, up 389.5% from the prior quarter, yet the controlling-shareholder result that quarter was a net loss of KRW 80.1 billion.

IBK Securities explained that non-operating expenses of KRW 136.3 billion, including intangible asset impairment, equity-method losses, and financial items, were reflected that quarter.

Q1 2026 operating profit was KRW 1.46 billion, down 98.3% from the prior quarter, followed by KRW 33.4 billion in Q2 and KRW 22.1 billion in Q3. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 1.19 trillion, down 4.4% year on year, and the controlling-shareholder result was a net loss of KRW 17.9 billion.

The controlling-shareholder net loss for the last four quarters totals KRW 68.2 billion. Operating profit recovered in Q2 and Q3, but its direction diverged from controlling-shareholder earnings, so the durability of consolidated profit remains under observation.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic TV advertising market is structurally shrinking. Eugene Investment & Securities noted in a May 2026 report that TV advertising revenue has declined for four consecutive years.

In Q2 2026, TV advertising revenue fell 20.9% YoY, reflecting a weak TV ad market and selective ad spending around the World Cup season, with ad budgets concentrated on mega-events. Digital is growing: TVING advertising revenue rose 52.2% YoY and the service reached 9.7 million MAU in June 2026.

In content production, Studio Dragon reported an operating margin of 10.6% on production-cost efficiency gains. FIFTH SEASON's delivery timing depends on the schedules of global OTT platforms, so quarterly results are volatile.

Music results depend on album sales, with MODYSSEY's debut reaching 300,000 first-week copies and INI reaching 990,000. Commerce growth is led by mobile live commerce.

Competitively, the music business contends with SM Entertainment, JYP Entertainment, and YG Entertainment for artist IP, while the OTT business competes with global players over content costs. The company cited seasonal ad weakness and high content costs as Q3 profitability variables. The industry is in a transition, with TV advertising shrinking while digital and IP businesses grow in weight.

06

Outlook

In its August 2026 earnings release, the company outlined its second-half outlook. It expected TV advertising to improve more than 30% sequentially from the first half, and sponsorship revenue to grow about 26% YoY in the second half.

For Q4, it expected TVING advertising to improve on KBO postseason games and peak-season demand, while noting that TVING's Q3 profitability could be slightly lower than in Q2 due to seasonal ad weakness and high content costs. It expected FIFTH SEASON's delivery gap to persist through the second half.

Q3 results were released on November 5, 2026, with revenue of KRW 1.19 trillion and operating profit of KRW 22.1 billion. The company said it aims to keep improving profitability in the second half by expanding its platform user base, diversifying content and music IP, and broadening global distribution.

External analysis reported a forecast of 2026 operating profit of KRW 120 billion, down 9%, and 2027 operating profit of KRW 210 billion, up 72%. Summing Q1 to Q3 2026 operating profit gives roughly KRW 57 billion, so reaching the KRW 120 billion full-year forecast would require a large contribution from Q4. Whether that forecast is met will become clear with the Q4 results and the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Operating profit recovery

Operating profit rose 27.2% in 2025 to KRW 132.9 billion, and Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 22.1 billion was up 25.5% YoY. Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 33.4 billion rose sharply from the prior quarter. On an operating basis, the improvement over 2024 is visible in the figures. The company cited production-cost control and wider revenue sources as drivers of profitability.

Growth in platforms and IP

TVING advertising revenue rose 52% YoY and Mnet Plus revenue grew 167%. Mobile live commerce transaction volume rose 161.3% YoY. TVING reached 9.7 million MAU in June 2026. Widening revenue through digital advertising and paid fan products is showing up in the reported metrics.

Second-half guidance and external forecasts

The company expected second-half sponsorship revenue to grow about 26% YoY. External analysis forecasts 2027 operating profit of KRW 210 billion, up 72%. The company also projected TV advertising to improve more than 30% sequentially from the first half. Whether these guidance points show up in reported quarterly figures is the next checkpoint.

Bear factors

Structural decline in TV advertising

Eugene Investment & Securities pointed out that TV advertising revenue has declined for four consecutive years. TV advertising revenue fell 20.9% YoY in Q2 2026. Digital growth needs to offset this decline, yet Q3 2026 revenue was still down 4.4% YoY. If the advertising recovery is slow, top-line recovery will be constrained.

Unstable net earnings

In Q3 2026, the company posted an operating profit yet a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 17.9 billion. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 17 billion in 2025, while controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 29.5 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio at year-end 2025 was 151.5%, above 137.8% in 2022.

Film and drama gaps and lower cash flow

FIFTH SEASON's delivery gap was expected to persist through the second half. The decline in operating cash flow, together with the debt burden, is a point to check in the next periodic report. In early 2026, securities firms said the earnings recovery was slower than expected given a difficult operating environment.

10

Risk factors

Content costs and OTT competition

TVING must expand its subscriber and advertising base while content costs rise. The company said Q3 profitability could be slightly lower than in Q2. Intensifying OTT competition could raise original production and rights costs. How the TVING and Wavve merger is reflected in cost efficiency and competitiveness is a key question.

Overseas distribution timing risk

FIFTH SEASON's delivery timing is adjusted to the programming schedules of global OTT platforms. Under this structure, quarterly revenue and operating profit can swing sharply. The Film & Drama segment posted an operating loss of KRW 10.5 billion in Q2 2026.

Whether individual sales to local overseas platforms and brand-pavilion expansion can fill the gap is the point to watch.

Ad market and event concentration

TV advertising revenue fell 20.9% YoY in Q2 2026, and the company cited ad budgets being concentrated on mega-events such as the World Cup. When ad spending clusters in event seasons, quarterly results become more volatile. The music and commerce segments are also sensitive to artist performance and consumer sentiment. If these external factors overlap, the pace of the earnings recovery could slow.

12

Overall view

CJ ENM moved out of the 2022 to 2024 losses, with 2025 operating profit of KRW 132.9 billion, up 27.2%, and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 29.5 billion turning positive.

Operating profit recovered to KRW 33.4 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 22.1 billion in Q3, but revenue declined for three straight quarters, and the Q3 controlling-shareholder result swung to a net loss of KRW 17.9 billion.

TV advertising remains under structural pressure after four consecutive years of decline, so how much digital, TVING, music, and mobile live commerce can offset it is the central question.

The company expects second-half sponsorship growth and a TV ad recovery, while external analysis projects operating profit to fall in 2026 and then rise sharply in 2027. The debt-to-equity ratio of 151.5% and lower operating cash flow are items to monitor for financial capacity.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results and the 2026 annual report, due by March 31, 2027. This report is provided for information only and does not present an investment opinion.
