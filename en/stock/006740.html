02

Business structure

Blue Industrial Development, founded in 1970, makes paper and corrugated products. Its Pyeongtaek plant produces base paper for paper cores and liner board, and a subsidiary processes that base paper into corrugated sheets and boxes for sale, a vertically integrated structure.

The company is classified under textiles, fashion and lifestyle, but its operations are closer to paper and corrugated packaging. In a 2025 year-end business breakdown, flute and liner products accounted for 85% of sales, corrugated sheets and boxes for 12%, and goods, leasing and waste treatment for 3%.

In the second quarter of 2026, paper products made up 84% of revenue, corrugated products 13%, goods and other items 2%, and leasing 1%. Main revenue comes from liner paper sales, with leasing income next. The company has invested in recycling facilities to secure stable raw material supply.

In the first half of 2026, average utilization was 88.0% at Pyeongtaek line No. 3 and 90.2% at the Cheongju corrugated line. Among nine companies in the paper and wood sector, its 2025 annual revenue ranked eighth, so its scale is on the smaller side.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue fell from KRW 105.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 82.6 billion in 2023, a 21.7% decline. It then rose to KRW 88.1 billion in 2024 (up 6.7%) and KRW 93.3 billion in 2025 (up 5.8%), increasing for two consecutive years. 2022 was the largest year in the period and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit of KRW 7.8 billion in 2022 swung to an operating loss of KRW 2.99 billion in 2023, which widened to KRW 13.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 15.8 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 7.4% in 2022 to -3.6% in 2023, -15.7% in 2024 and -17.0% in 2025.

Revenue grew in 2024 and 2025 while the margin fell, so the top-line recovery did not carry through to profit. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.41 billion in 2023, KRW 21.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 24.5 billion in 2025.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 24.5 billion in 2025, at the same level. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 12.6 billion in 2022 and KRW 8.29 billion in 2023 to -KRW 14.6 billion in 2024 and -KRW 6.3 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 49.2% in 2022 to 100.9% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 24.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 24.2 billion in the fourth quarter (down 2.8% from the prior quarter), KRW 24.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 28.2 billion in the second (up 15.3%) and KRW 27.3 billion in the third (down 3.3% from the prior quarter, up 9.5% from a year earlier).

Operating results improved from an operating loss of KRW 3.91 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 5.34 billion in the fourth quarter to an operating loss of KRW 498 million in the first quarter of 2026 and an operating profit of KRW 760 million in the second.

Press coverage dated August 27, 2026 reported that first-half gross margin rose from 2.4% to 15.1%, with higher selling prices and cost structure gains cited as drivers.

However, net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened from KRW 1.23 billion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 1.46 billion in the third.

05

Industry analysis

The paper and corrugated sector is capital-intensive, with heavy fixed costs, so utilization largely determines unit cost. The company's average utilization in the first half of 2026 was 88.0% at Pyeongtaek line No. 3 and 90.2% at the Cheongju corrugated line.

In an industry with high fixed-cost weight, higher utilization lowers fixed cost per unit. Coverage of the first half of 2026 cited selling price recovery and higher utilization together as the reasons gross margin rose from 2.4% to 15.1%.

Vertical integration, from base paper through corrugated processing, also adds value at each stage. Within the nine-company paper and wood sector, the company ranked eighth on 2025 annual revenue and sixth on operating profit.

The sector average operating profit on the same basis was -KRW 3.2 billion, against the company's -KRW 15.8 billion, a wide gap on profitability.

Its corrugated-processing subsidiary's first-half 2026 revenue was reported at KRW 6.9 billion, up about 19% year on year, which points to volume growth in the processing segment.

Bull factors

Operating profit turns positive

Operating profit was KRW 760 million in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 35.11 million in the third quarter, so the operating line stayed in the black. Press coverage dated August 27, 2026 said first-half operating profit was the first half-year profit since 2022. That breaks a run of three straight years of operating losses from 2023 through 2025.

Cost structure and utilization improve

First-half gross margin rose from 2.4% to 15.1%, a gain of 12.7 percentage points, and gross profit was more than seven times the prior-year level. Utilization of 88.0% at Pyeongtaek line No. 3 and 90.2% at the Cheongju corrugated line means more output is spread over fixed costs. Vertical integration from base paper to processing adds value at the sales stage.

Net loss range narrows

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 13.8 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.75 billion in the first quarter of 2026. Across the first three quarters of 2026, quarterly losses stayed between roughly KRW 1.2 billion and KRW 1.8 billion. The reduction from the fourth quarter of 2025 is the main positive signal in the quarterly trend.

Bear factors

Three straight years of operating losses

Operating losses have continued for three straight years from 2023 through 2025, with the 2025 operating loss at KRW 15.8 billion. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 24.5 billion in 2025 from KRW 21.9 billion in 2024.

Over the last four quarters that figure totals KRW 18.2 billion, so the accumulated loss burden remains even after the return to operating profit.

Weaker balance sheet and cash flow

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 49.2% in 2022 to 100.9% in 2025, and total liabilities of KRW 83 billion at year-end 2025 are about equal to shareholders' equity of KRW 82.2 billion. Operating cash flow was -KRW 14.6 billion in 2024 and -KRW 6.3 billion in 2025. Whether cash generation follows the return to operating profit remains a weak point.

Momentum slows in the third quarter

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 27.3 billion, down 3.3% from the prior quarter. Operating profit fell to KRW 35.11 million, down 95.4% from the prior quarter's KRW 760 million.

Net loss widened from KRW 1.23 billion in the second quarter to KRW 1.46 billion in the third, so the narrowing trend paused in a single quarter.

Risk factors

Financial leverage

The debt-to-equity ratio of 100.9% means liabilities are roughly equal to equity. With operating cash flow negative in 2024 and 2025, debt repayment could become a heavier burden. If operating profit does not convert into cash flow, financial flexibility would shrink further.

Raw material supply and utilization

Base paper production depends on recycled raw material supply, and the company has invested in recycling facilities to stabilize it. A drop in utilization would pass fixed-cost burden straight into unit costs. If selling prices weaken again, the gross margin recovered in the first half of 2026 could reverse.

Concentration in paper and scale

Paper products accounted for 84% of revenue in the second quarter of 2026, so exposure to base paper market conditions is high. Among nine companies in the paper and wood sector, the company ranked eighth on 2025 annual revenue, on the smaller side.

The processing subsidiary depends on in-house base paper supply, so changes in base paper prices or supply affect both stages.
