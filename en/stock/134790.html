Earnings analysis

Consolidated revenue fell from KRW 243.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 200.1 billion in 2023, a 17.7% decline, then rose 4.1% to KRW 208.4 billion in 2024 before dropping 6.1% to KRW 195.7 billion in 2025, the smallest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit of KRW 40.89 million in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 1.93 billion in 2023, and the company has posted operating losses for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

The loss widened to KRW 3.36 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.68 billion in 2025, and the operating margin moved from 0.0% in 2022 to -3.4% in 2025. The company attributed the 2025 deterioration mainly to a slowdown in durable goods spending driven by business cycle fluctuations.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 2.54 billion in 2022 to KRW 908 million in 2024, then turned into a net loss of KRW 2.26 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests followed the same direction, with a net loss of KRW 2.26 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow declined from KRW 11.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.66 billion in 2025 but remained positive each year. The debt ratio rose from 34.1% in 2022 to 56.1% in 2025.

In quarterly terms, the operating loss of KRW 1.85 billion in 3Q 2025 narrowed to KRW 619 million in 4Q 2025, and 1Q 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 1.5 billion.

Operating results then turned negative again, with an operating loss of KRW 32.97 million in 2Q 2026 and KRW 903 million in 3Q 2026, two consecutive loss-making quarters. 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 48.5 billion rose 4.2% from a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 3.66 billion.

Separately, Vegan News reported, based on the 3Q 2026 quarterly report, a cumulative operating profit of KRW 570 million for January to September 2026, a provisional media figure rather than a reported annual number.
