Earnings Step-Up and a Refined Sulfuric Acid Expansion

02

Business structure

Miwon Chemicals is a KOSPI-listed company that was spun off from Miwon Commercial in 2011. It makes sulfur-related products, sulfonic acid used as a detergent raw material, and alcohol sulfate at its Ulsan and Tancheon plants.

As of June 2026, surfactants made up about 56% of sales, powdered sulfur and sulfuric acid about 35%, and merchandise and other items about 9%. The same source puts its domestic share of sulfonic acid, a surfactant type, at 20%, and about 70% of 2025 surfactant sales were exports.

According to a press report, first-half 2026 sales of KRW 191.8 billion included KRW 67.2 billion from powdered sulfur and sulfuric acid and KRW 108.1 billion from surfactants. These segment figures come from an October 2026 CNews article and do not appear in the periodic-report table used elsewhere in this report.

The company buys liquid sulfur and alkylbenzene from refiners such as SK Energy, and its refined sulfuric acid is used in PCB etching and plating.

A company official said the powdered sulfur and sulfuric acid markets are limited and hard to grow, while the surfactant unit is working on new applications and overseas expansion. The competitive landscape and customer concentration could not be confirmed through search, so they are described only qualitatively. As of October 14, 2026, the largest shareholder group held 55.46% and treasury shares were 11.85%.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell 1.1% from KRW 235.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 232.6 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 256.1 billion in 2024 (up 10.1%) and KRW 284.5 billion in 2025 (up 11.1%), two consecutive years of increase from 2024 to 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 19 billion in 2022, KRW 24.9 billion in 2023 (up 31.2%), KRW 28.6 billion in 2024 (up 14.9%) and KRW 29.2 billion in 2025 (up 1.8%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, though the pace of growth slowed each year.

The operating margin climbed from 8.1% in 2022 to 11.2% in 2024 before easing to 10.2% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 8.2% from KRW 26.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 24.7 billion in 2025, and net income including non-controlling interests was the same KRW 24.7 billion.

Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 20.7 billion versus KRW 38.2 billion in 2024, and the reported figures alone do not explain the decline.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 72.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 77.3 billion, KRW 86.4 billion, KRW 105.4 billion and KRW 115.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026, rising for four consecutive quarters.

Quarterly operating profit dipped to KRW 5.1 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (down 42.1% from the prior quarter), then rose to KRW 9 billion, KRW 24.3 billion and KRW 22.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, which was down 5.8% from the prior quarter but up 160.6% year on year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 19.7 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 15 billion in the third (down 23.5% from the prior quarter), and the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 through third quarter of 2026) total KRW 48.2 billion, well above the KRW 24.7 billion for full-year 2025.

Cumulative revenue and operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 already exceed the full-year 2025 figures of KRW 284.5 billion and KRW 29.2 billion.

The company's own explanation for the jump and any one-off items could not be confirmed through search, so non-operating items and the notes in the third-quarter 2026 report deserve a closer look.

05

Industry analysis

The sulfuric acid market became more strained in 2026. The price of sulfur shipped from the Middle East to Indonesia rose from USD 514 per ton at the end of February, before the war, to USD 948 on April 23, with the Strait of Hormuz blockade cited as the cause.

Foreign media reports said China, the world's largest sulfuric acid exporter, told its producers to halt exports from May 2026. The same report said spot sulfuric acid rose from below CNY 1,000 per ton in January to about CNY 1,800 at the end of April.

Argus Media forecast that the restriction could last through the end of 2026. On the other hand, sulfuric acid is not on China's official export control list, and a diplomatic source in Beijing described the curbs as informal and likely temporary.

CNews described Miwon Chemicals as a beneficiary of strong PCB conditions and China's sulfuric acid export ban. How much of this showed up in the quarterly results since the second quarter of 2026 has not been confirmed by the company.

Surfactants are a mature market used in detergents and shampoos, and the company's high export share exposes it to the won-dollar exchange rate and overseas demand. The cost burden of higher sulfur prices and the degree of pass-through to selling prices also cannot be confirmed from filings.

Unlike the large diversified chemical companies in the sector, its business is concentrated in sulfur products and surfactants, so market indicators should be tracked mainly for these two markets.

06

Outlook

The largest confirmed business event is the refined sulfuric acid expansion. On October 15, 2026 the board approved expanding sulfuric acid and refined sulfuric acid facilities at the Tancheon plant, with KRW 51.895 billion equal to 29.90% of equity and an investment period through July 31, 2028.

The filing states that the amount may change as the main contract proceeds. Post-expansion capacity in tons, the start-up timing and the financing method were not confirmed in filings or press reports. Full-year earnings guidance from the company also could not be confirmed through search.

A company official has said the powdered sulfur and sulfuric acid markets are limited and hard to grow, so the payoff from the expansion may differ depending on whether the current market strength persists. The surfactant unit is reported to keep pursuing new applications and overseas expansion.

The low debt ratio at the end of 2025 leaves financial room, but the investment equals roughly 30% of total equity, so profit levels and cash flow need to be watched together.

The company also decided on a quarterly cash dividend with a record date of June 30, 2026, so it is worth checking whether the shareholder return policy continues.

08

Bull factors

Higher quarterly profit base

Operating profit of KRW 24.3 billion in the second quarter and KRW 22.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026 came close to the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 29.2 billion within just two quarters. Revenue also rose for four straight quarters to KRW 115.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026. If this level holds, the annual operating margin could move beyond its 2022 to 2025 range.

Refined sulfuric acid expansion

Completed expansion could raise refined sulfuric acid volumes while downstream demand such as PCB remains firm. Press analysis says China's sulfuric acid export halt deepened supply shortages. The investment period runs to July 2028, so market conditions at completion will largely decide the effect.

Low debt and payout record

At the end of 2025 the debt ratio was 12.8% and total equity was KRW 173.6 billion, so financial burden is small. The company has a record of deciding both year-end and quarterly dividends. What remains to be seen is whether financial headroom holds through the expansion spending.

09

Bear factors

Durability of market-driven profit

The profit surge in 2026 coincides with sharp rises in sulfuric acid and sulfur prices and reports of China's export halt. There is a view that China's action may be temporary, as it is not an official control. If conditions normalize, quarterly profit could move back toward 2025 levels.

Limited-growth market and a large investment

A company official said the powdered sulfur and sulfuric acid markets are limited. The expansion amount is 29.90% of equity, which is not small. If demand falls short of expectations, utilization and the operating margin could come under pressure.

Gap between profit and cash flow

Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 20.7 billion versus KRW 38.2 billion in 2024, even though operating profit edged up to KRW 29.2 billion that year. The annual report will need to show whether the high 2026 profit converts into cash.

10

Risk factors

Raw material and market risk

Liquid sulfur, the raw material, is supplied as a refining by-product, and sulfur prices swung sharply with Middle East developments. If cost increases cannot be passed on in selling prices, margins could shrink. The sulfuric acid market is also sensitive to Chinese policy changes.

Currency and export risk

About 70% of surfactant sales are exports, which exposes results to exchange rates and overseas demand. Growth in local production in emerging markets could raise competitive intensity. The breakdown of exports by region was not confirmed.

Execution and safety risk

The investment amount may change as the main contract proceeds, and post-expansion capacity and start-up timing were not confirmed. As a chemical plant handling sulfur compounds, it also faces environmental and safety regulation and accident risk. Delays or cost overruns could reduce investment efficiency.

Overall view

Miwon Chemicals' operating profit rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and in 2026 quarterly operating profit stepped up to KRW 24.3 billion in the second quarter and KRW 22.9 billion in the third.

Over the same period, revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to reach KRW 115.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

In 2025, however, net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 8.2% to KRW 24.7 billion and operating cash flow dropped to KRW 20.7 billion, so the quality of earnings still needs checking.

Press reports say the sulfur price surge and China's sulfuric acid export halt affected the market, but no company statement directly attributing the results to them was found.

The refined sulfuric acid expansion approved in October 2026 equals 29.90% of equity and runs to July 2028, so supply and demand at completion will largely decide its effect.

The bullish side is the higher quarterly profit level and the low debt ratio, and the bearish side is dependence on market conditions and a limited-growth market.

Next points to check are whether China's export restriction continues, the cash flow in the 2026 annual report, and the confirmed terms of the expansion's main contract. This report is for information only and contains no buy or sell opinion.
