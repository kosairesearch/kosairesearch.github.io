02

Business structure

Poongsan Holdings was founded in 1968, listed in 1988, and converted into a holding company in 2008.

The business is split into a holding segment and a manufacturing segment; the holding segment owns subsidiary shares, manages trademarks and provides management consulting, while the manufacturing segment produces non-ferrous metal materials, special steel, packaging materials and machinery equipment.

Of consolidated operating revenue of KRW 416.2 billion for January to September 2026, equity-method investment income was KRW 84.7 billion and product sales were KRW 270.95 billion.

One axis of operating revenue is manufacturing sales and the other is equity-method income, so holding company profit depends heavily on the business conditions of its investees.

On a separate-financials basis, dividend income from subsidiaries was KRW 18.1 billion and trademark income was KRW 11.0 billion for January to September 2026.

According to a company information site as of December 2025, Ryu Jin and seven related parties held 48.76%, the largest stake, and VIP Asset Management held 10.31%. On March 20, 2026, a treasury stock acquisition decision was disclosed as a material matter report.

Demand cited for the manufacturing segment includes defense and high-grade copper alloys for electric vehicles and renewable energy, and the same site noted mass production of high-voltage copper alloys and research on new fields such as strike-type combat drones.

Equity-method income and dividend income reflect the results of the investment portfolio, so the business structure should be read together with manufacturing sales.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 387.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 406.7 billion in 2023 (up 4.9% year on year) and KRW 449.4 billion in 2024 (up 10.5%), then fell to KRW 432.5 billion in 2025 (down 3.8%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 was the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 80.2 billion in 2022, KRW 67.8 billion in 2023 (down 15.4%), KRW 104.7 billion in 2024 (up 54.4%) and KRW 66.8 billion in 2025 (down 36.2%). The operating margin was 20.7% in 2022, 16.7% in 2023, 23.3% in 2024 and 15.4% in 2025, peaking in 2024 before falling in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 decline to lower equity-method investment income.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 73.2 billion in 2022, KRW 83.4 billion in 2023, KRW 100 billion in 2024 and KRW 74.4 billion in 2025, and net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 74.4 billion in 2025.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 1.17 trillion, and the debt-to-equity ratio was 15.5% in 2025, below 18.5% in 2022. Operating cash flow went from KRW 3.47 billion in 2022 to KRW 32.7 billion in 2023 and KRW 17.1 billion in 2024, then turned to an outflow of KRW -5.23 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit in the fourth quarter of 2025 was KRW 2.42 billion, down 86.3% from the prior quarter, marking the low point.

It recovered to KRW 35.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026, a 1,354.5% rise from the prior quarter, then KRW 41.3 billion in the second quarter (up 17.2%) and KRW 34.4 billion in the third quarter (down 16.8% from the prior quarter but up 94.4% year on year).

Equity-method investment income was KRW 30.1 billion of the KRW 121.0 billion operating revenue in the first quarter of 2026, showing that quarterly profit moves with the timing of equity-method income.

Consolidated operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 110.95 billion, up 72.4% year on year, according to a preliminary disclosure reported on November 3, 2026.

Industry analysis

Poongsan Holdings is a holding company, so the business conditions of its subsidiaries and equity-method investees pass through to its results. Equity-method investment income for January to September 2026 rose to KRW 84.7 billion, which points to improved results at the companies it invests in.

On the same disclosure day, Poongsan (103140), a separately listed company with a similar name, reported third-quarter 2026 defense revenue of KRW 331.6 billion on a separate basis, up 46% year on year.

That defense revenue was split into KRW 143.5 billion domestic and KRW 188.1 billion export, supported by strong North American sporting ammunition exports, according to press coverage.

Poongsan's copper and copper alloy segment revenue was KRW 883.7 billion, slightly down from the prior quarter, which was attributed to a vehicle strike and lower production, as well as slower recovery in construction.

Demand for high-value copper materials for AI data centers and semiconductors was cited as supporting that company's results. Partial shipment delays caused by the Middle East conflict were a timing risk for defense exports.

A company information site as of December 2025 cited rising demand from electric vehicles and renewable energy as a driver for mass production of high-value copper alloys. Industry demand for Poongsan Holdings therefore depends on export shipment timing, vehicle production and data center demand at its investees.

Overall view

Poongsan Holdings' operating profit fell to KRW 66.8 billion in 2025 and then recovered, with quarterly operating profit staying above KRW 30 billion in each of the first three quarters of 2026.

Much of the recovery is explained by higher equity-method investment income, which reached KRW 84.7 billion for January to September 2026. As a holding company, its profit depends directly on the business conditions and timing of its subsidiaries and investees, so earnings durability hinges on those factors.
