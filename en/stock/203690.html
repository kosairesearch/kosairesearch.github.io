Wet Wipe OEM Specialist at the Crossroads of Accounting Risk and Revenue Recovery

Amid four consecutive years of operating losses and an ongoing court injunction against a delisting order stemming from accounting violations, ARK Solutions' 2026 Q1 revenue rebounded sharply from recent lows, placing both its path to profitability and its continued exchange listing simultaneously to the test.

02

Business structure

ARK Solutions was established in 2005 for protein research and cosmetics sales, and underwent multiple mergers and name changes (Prostemics → Spear Power → ARK Solutions) before adopting its current name via an extraordinary general meeting in October 2024.

The business is organized into three segments: the Living Health Division (OEM manufacturing and sales of wet wipes and hygiene paper), the Bio Division (R&D on microbiome exosome-based IBD and oncology therapies plus gut health functional foods), and a stem cell cosmetics business.

Over 95% of revenue originates from the Living Health Division; the other two segments contribute negligibly. Revenue consists of approximately 97% product sales (finished and semi-finished goods) and ~3% other revenue.

The wet wipe division differentiates on fully automated smart-factory production and an eleven-step purified water system, claiming the leading domestic wet wipe OEM position by production scale per its own disclosures.

Key customers are wet wipe brand owners and distributors, which structurally hold superior pricing power in contract negotiations with OEM suppliers. The Bio Division holds long-term pipeline potential but remains years away from commercial revenue given ongoing clinical and regulatory requirements.

04

Earnings analysis

ARK Solutions has recorded operating and net losses in every year from 2022 through 2025.

Revenue grew from ~₩26.6 billion in 2022 (operating margin -27.6%) to ~₩37.3 billion in 2023 (operating margin -12.9%), yet the 2023 net loss reached approximately ₩24.2 billion—the largest in the tracked period—reflecting what appears to have been substantial one-time non-cash impairments and write-downs.

Revenue then edged down to ~₩33.2 billion in 2024 (operating margin -18.7%), before collapsing to ~₩16.1 billion in 2025, pushing the operating margin to its worst level at -46.3%.

Operating cash flow has been negative every year: -~₩4.9 billion (2022), -~₩6.8 billion (2023), -~₩1.9 billion (2024), and -~₩10.1 billion (2025), with 2025 seeing the steepest outflow.

The debt-to-equity ratio improved sharply from 300.7% (2022) to 63.5% (2025), primarily reflecting debt repayment funded by a capital raise completed in late December 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue declined progressively from ~₩5.5 billion in Q1 2025 to ~₩3.0 billion in Q4 2025, before rebounding to ~₩7.4 billion in Q1 2026—the highest in the recent six-quarter window—while the operating loss narrowed to ~₩0.95 billion, the smallest tracked quarterly figure, providing the first directional signal of earnings improvement.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic wet wipe market is segmented into baby, adult, and pet care products, with brand owners and distributors structurally holding superior pricing power over OEM manufacturers.

Post-COVID normalization has compressed demand industry-wide from 2023 through 2025, unwinding the pandemic-era surge in hygiene product consumption. Raw material prices (non-woven fabric, purified water, etc.) are a key direct driver of OEM profitability.

Entry barriers in wet wipe OEM are relatively low, intensifying competition among small and mid-size manufacturers, though large-scale automated facilities and water purification systems can provide incremental quality and cost advantages.

The microbiome therapeutics field the Bio Division has entered is a fast-growing global sector with rapidly expanding clinical pipelines, though domestic regulatory frameworks and clinical-stage readiness require substantial time to develop.

While ARK Solutions claims the top domestic wet wipe OEM production scale by its own account, prolonged management-alert status and the ongoing delisting dispute create reputational risk that could impair key customer contract retention and new business development.

06

Outlook

The Q1 2026 revenue rebound and loss improvement represent early directional signals of recovery, but a return to annual profitability depends on demonstrating sustained multi-quarter momentum from Q2 onwards.

The improved balance sheet following the year-end 2025 ownership change and rights issue completion provides the new management team with a more stable platform to rebuild the customer base and optimize the cost structure.

However, the outcome of the delisting injunction lawsuit remains the single most important overhanging event, with the scenario range highly dependent on the court's ruling.

The Bio Division's microbiome exosome IBD pipeline could serve as a medium-to-long-term value re-rating catalyst, but no confirmed clinical entry timelines or licensing milestones are available in publicly disclosed materials.

Renewal and expansion of key accounts in the Living Health Division is the primary leading indicator for the pace of 2026 full-year revenue recovery.

The company's history of repeated capital raises through rights issues and convertible bonds in 2023–2025 means ongoing monitoring of future dilution risk remains necessary.

Bull factors

Q1 2026 Revenue and Loss Simultaneously Improved to Recent Best Levels

Q1 2026 revenue grew ~2.4× versus Q4 2025, reaching the highest level in the recent six-quarter window, while the operating loss narrowed to the smallest quarterly figure tracked—potentially signaling a recovery in Living Health Division order and shipment activity.

If this trend is sustained beyond Q1, a meaningful reduction in the full-year operating loss becomes increasingly plausible.

Substantial Debt Reduction and Improved Balance Sheet Stability

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 300.7% in 2022 to 63.5% in 2025, with total liabilities cut by more than half from ~₩35.1 billion to ~₩16.5 billion.

The completion of the new controlling shareholder's rights issue in late 2025 was the primary driver of this structural improvement, easing near-term debt repayment pressure and providing operational breathing room.

Claims Leading Domestic Wet Wipe OEM Production Infrastructure

The company claims the top domestic wet wipe OEM production scale, backed by fully automated smart-factory lines and an eleven-step purified water system. This infrastructure can support faster customer response times and unit cost competitiveness.

In an environment of tightening hygiene quality standards, this capability could differentiate ARK Solutions from competitors with inferior facilities.

Bear factors

Delisting Decision Under Injunction—Exchange Listing Contingent on Court Stay

The KOSDAQ market committee voted for immediate delisting in July 2025 due to accounting standards violations; the company filed an injunction which, as of the March 2026 annual report, remained pending in court.

The shares' current tradability is contingent on this injunction stay—if dissolved or reversed, actual delisting could materialize, eliminating share tradability entirely.

The stock also remains under management-alert status (designated March 2025), imposing additional exchange regulatory constraints until resolution conditions are met.

Four Consecutive Years of Operating and Net Losses with Persistent Cash Outflow

The company recorded operating and net losses in every year from 2022 through 2025, alongside negative operating cash flow throughout. The 2025 operating margin of -46.3% was the weakest in the four-year period, with operating cash outflow reaching ~₩10.1 billion, highlighting an inability to self-fund operations.

Unless this structural imbalance reverses, continued reliance on external capital to sustain the business remains necessary.

Sharp Revenue Decline and High Single-Segment and Customer Concentration Risk

Full-year 2025 revenue declined approximately 52% year-on-year, with quarterly revenue falling sequentially through each of the four 2025 quarters.

In a structure where over 95% of revenue is concentrated in a single OEM segment, any departure by a key customer or downward pressure on contract prices could substantially delay profitability recovery.

The ongoing delisting dispute and management-alert status are also likely to constrain the company's ability to attract new large-scale customers.

Risk factors

Legal & Regulatory Risk (Highest Priority)

The delisting order resulting from accounting standards violations is the highest-priority risk, with the company's listed status directly dependent on the court's ruling in the injunction case.

A failed injunction bid could trigger wrap-up trading and actual delisting, making this risk a prerequisite consideration ahead of any other fundamental analysis. The separate conditions required to exit management-alert status also warrant independent monitoring.

Liquidity & Dilution Risk

Operating cash flow has been negative for four consecutive years, sustaining the company's dependence on external funding.

The track record of raising over ₩37.6 billion through rights issues, convertible bonds, and redeemable convertible preferred shares between 2023 and 2025 makes future equity dilution a realistic risk ahead of any operational cash flow breakeven. This risk persists until management stabilizes and operating profitability is demonstrated.

Business Concentration & Customer Base Risk

Over 95% of revenue is concentrated in a single OEM business segment, with no meaningful business diversification in practice. Simultaneous customer attrition, raw material price increases, and contract price compression could compound delays in the path to profitability.

The bio pipeline faces a long commercialization horizon and cannot meaningfully offset this structural vulnerability in the near term.

12

Overall view

ARK Solutions generates the vast majority of its revenue from a single wet wipe OEM segment, has posted operating and net losses for four consecutive years, and operates under the fundamental uncertainty of an ongoing court injunction against a delisting order arising from accounting violations.

On the positive side, Q1 2026 revenue rebounded to approximately 2.4× the Q4 2025 level with the operating loss narrowing to a recent-period low, and the debt-to-equity ratio improved dramatically from 300.7% in 2022 to 63.5% in 2025—early signs of financial stabilization.

Nevertheless, multiple structural challenges coexist: the pending delisting lawsuit, management-alert status, four consecutive years of negative operating cash flow, and single-segment concentration risk—of which the legal outcome takes precedence over all others.

The bio division's microbiome exosome IBD pipeline represents a potential long-term re-rating catalyst, though no confirmed clinical or regulatory milestones have been disclosed in publicly available materials.

Analysts and investors should track legal developments and quarterly earnings trends concurrently, treating the exchange-listing uncertainty as the primary lens for all analytical conclusions until that risk is fully and definitively resolved.
