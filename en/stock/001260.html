Report overview

Namkwang: Lower Sales, Higher Margins, Public-Works Backlog

Namkwang's sales fell in 2024 and 2025 while its operating margin rose from 0.8% in 2023 to 3.2% in 2025, and its public-sector-heavy backlog stood at KRW 2.77 trillion at the end of Q1 2026.

  1. 1

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Namkwang is a civil-engineering-focused construction company founded in 1947, handling civil works such as railways, roads, ports and water supply alongside building projects such as public housing and urban redevelopment.

Its backlog stood at KRW 2.77 trillion at the end of Q1 2026, up 33% from a year earlier, and about 77% of it was public-sector work, according to a June 2026 report by Mediapen.

Major backlog items include the Sejong-Anseong Expressway Section 1 (KRW 186.4 billion), the Daegu Industrial Line Railway Section 2 (KRW 160.9 billion) and the Pohang Yeongilman South Breakwater (KRW 131.7 billion).

The building segment has shifted from private housing toward public housing, and the public share of the building backlog rose from 6.3% at end-2023 to 60.6% at the end of the first half of 2026, per Bloter.

Building revenue, however, fell from KRW 202.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 103.0 billion in 2025, and first-half 2026 building sales were KRW 30.3 billion, down 52.9% from KRW 64.3 billion a year earlier.

Redevelopment projects won in 2022 had still not started construction after more than three years, which left a gap in private-sector building revenue. Its clients are concentrated in public agencies, including LH, the national rail authority, K-water and Korea Expressway Corporation.

Public clients accounted for 90.6% of the KRW 502.7 billion in new orders in the first half of 2026. The company competes with large contractors in technical-bid tenders, and its largest shareholder is Seun Construction at 22.37% as of end-June 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales were KRW 450.4 billion in 2022, KRW 547.7 billion in 2023, KRW 504.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 357.4 billion in 2025. 2023 was the largest year in the period, and sales have declined in 2024 (down 7.9%) and 2025 (down 29.1%).

Operating profit fell from KRW 11.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.35 billion in 2023 (down 60.9%), then rose to KRW 7.3 billion in 2024 (up 68.0%) and KRW 11.4 billion in 2025 (up 56.6%), increasing for two consecutive years. The operating margin moved from 2.5% in 2022 to 0.8% in 2023, 1.4% in 2024 and 3.2% in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 sales decline to a slowdown in construction activity and the operating profit increase to improved cost structure, according to its disclosure on changes in sales and profit structure.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 19.3 billion in 2022, KRW 6.01 billion in 2023 (down 68.8%), KRW 6.2 billion in 2024 (up 3.1%) and KRW 5.29 billion in 2025 (down 14.6%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 5.29 billion in 2025, the same as the controlling-shareholder figure, and KRW 5.99 billion in 2023, slightly below the KRW 6.01 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

On a quarterly basis, Q4 2025 operating profit of KRW 6.01 billion was up 2,507.4% from Q3, equal to more than half of the full-year 2025 operating profit. Operating profit then bottomed at KRW 2.25 billion in Q1 2026 and rose for two consecutive quarters, to KRW 2.26 billion in Q2 and KRW 5.06 billion in Q3.

Q3 2026 sales of KRW 98.3 billion were up 22.4% from the prior quarter and 12.2% from a year earlier, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 7.82 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 26.9 billion in 2022, an outflow of KRW 24.2 billion in 2023, KRW 112 million in 2024 and KRW 34.7 billion in 2025. Based on the first-half 2026 semiannual report, operating cash flow was an outflow of KRW 44.0 billion, according to a November 16, 2026 report by Daily Pop.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic construction orders are tilting toward public-sector clients. In an MTN survey of first-half 2026 public-sector orders, as of June 29, Namkwang ranked first with KRW 888.3 billion, and among large contractors Hyundai Engineering & Construction ranked third.

In the same survey, all of Namkwang's orders were classified as civil works. Private housing demand has weakened, and construction starts on private redevelopment projects have slowed.

Analysts note that competition for large redevelopment projects has intensified, which has drawn attention to small and mid-sized projects near transit, according to a March 2026 Hankyung report.

In technical-bid tenders, Namkwang ranked first in the second-stage evaluation for Section 1 of the Gyeyang-Ganghwa Expressway on November 12, 2026, after Taeyoung Engineering & Construction and another contractor were shortlisted for the second stage.

In the first-half civil-works rankings, HJ Shipbuilding & Construction also placed among the top five, so competition in civil works remains significant.

The company attributed its 2025 sales decline to a slowdown in construction activity, while public civil works and public housing have clearer procurement pipelines than private housing, which is more exposed to financing conditions.

Namkwang shows strength in public civil-works orders, but the recovery of private housing sales remains an open issue.

06

Outlook

The Gadeok Industrial Complex (Phase 2) water supply facilities, Section 2, was disclosed on November 17, 2026 as a single sales and supply contract worth about KRW 31.7 billion, equal to 8.86% of 2025 sales.

The work period runs from November 30, 2026 to June 28, 2030, with progress payments, and the company said the period and amount could change during construction.

Section 6 of the Gangdong-Hanam-Namyangju line rail project was selected as the preferred bidder for detailed design, with a construction estimate attributable to the company of about KRW 404.4 billion.

Mediapen noted in June 2026 that the formal contract was the key second-half event and calculated that, if signed, new orders for the year would approach KRW 900 billion including first-half orders.

Section 1 of the Gyeyang-Ganghwa Expressway ranked first in the second-stage evaluation on November 12, 2026, and MTN reported that the contractor is expected to be confirmed after the expressway operator reviews the design proposal.

In housing, 466 of 548 units in the Bucheon Daejang public sale project were allocated to special supply categories, with subscription procedures under way according to a November 17, 2026 report by Financial News.

In private redevelopment, the company secured the construction contract for the 113-unit Garak 7-cha street-housing project in March 2026 and is pursuing projects in central Seoul, according to Hankyung. The company's 80th anniversary falls in 2027.

Existing backlog items such as the Sejong-Anseong Expressway, the Daegu Industrial Line Railway and the Pohang Yeongilman South Breakwater continue as public civil works. The next check point is whether the full-year 2026 operating margin and backlog changes appear in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Backlog and public-sector order base

Backlog reached KRW 2.77 trillion at the end of Q1 2026, up 33% year on year, and about 77% of it is public-sector work. Clearer procurement pipelines and longer work periods support revenue visibility.

Large projects such as Section 6 of the Gangdong-Hanam-Namyangju line and Section 1 of the Gyeyang-Ganghwa Expressway could add to the backlog if formal contracts are signed.

Recovery in operating margin

The operating margin rose from a low of 0.8% in 2023 to 1.4% in 2024 and 3.2% in 2025. The company attributed the 2025 profit increase to an improved cost structure. Operating profit rose for two consecutive years even as sales declined, which can be read as a shift toward profitability over scale. Whether this trend continues will be visible in the next periodic reports.

Quarterly earnings improvement

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 5.06 billion, up 124.1% from the prior quarter, while sales rose 22.4% to KRW 98.3 billion. Operating profit has increased for two consecutive quarters after bottoming at KRW 2.25 billion in Q1 2026.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 7.82 billion. Q3 2026 sales and operating profit both rose from a year earlier.

09

Bear factors

Shrinking sales and a gap in private building revenue

Sales fell from KRW 547.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 357.4 billion in 2025 and declined in both 2024 and 2025. Building revenue dropped from KRW 202.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 103.0 billion in 2025. First-half 2026 building sales were KRW 30.3 billion, down 52.9% year on year.

Private redevelopment construction delays continue, and new public housing work has not yet fully reached revenue.

Cash flow and borrowing burden

Operating cash flow was an outflow of KRW 44.0 billion in the first half of 2026, which differed from positive net income for the same period, according to Daily Pop. The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 234% at the end of Q1 2026 from 228.1% at the end of 2025.

Operating cash flow was negative KRW 24.2 billion in 2023, so annual swings are large. The pace of progress-payment collections on public works is a key variable.

Limited absolute profit base

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 14.6% to KRW 5.29 billion in 2025, compared with KRW 19.3 billion in 2022. Even though the operating margin reached 3.2%, the absolute profit base remains narrow relative to large peers. Because earnings are small, quarterly results can move significantly with changes in cost ratios.

Risk factors

Related-party transactions and governance

Related-party purchases and other expenses in the first half of 2026 totaled KRW 81.5 billion, up 62.0% year on year, according to the semiannual report as reported by Daily Pop. About 61% of the increase came from three unlisted affiliates linked to the chairman's shareholdings.

Receivables from these affiliates rose to KRW 16.3 billion by end-June 2026, an increase of KRW 15.3 billion from year-end 2025. The largest shareholder holds 22.37% as of end-June 2026, so the governance structure and the scale of intra-group dealings are points to monitor.

Litigation and contingent exposure

A counterclaim of KRW 11.5 billion in damages was filed by a trading counterparty at the Seoul Central District Court, according to a disclosure dated August 19, 2026. The claim equals about 9% of total equity at the end of 2025. The company said it would appoint counsel and respond through legal procedures. The claim also carries 12% annual default interest, so the outcome could change the burden on capital and cash.

Project execution and consortium structure

A significant portion of first-half 2026 orders came from consortium projects with shared stakes. The company holds 51% of the Yangju-Hoeche project, 19.5% of Namyangju Wangsuk 2 and about 52.63% of the Gangdong-Hanam rail project. The company has said the work period and contract amount may change during construction.

Completion-guarantee risks also remain, so whether order values convert into sales depends on construction progress.

12

Overall view

Namkwang's sales have declined since their 2023 peak, yet its operating margin rose from 0.8% in 2023 to 3.2% in 2025, and operating profit grew strongly in Q3 2026 from a year earlier.

The backlog reached KRW 2.77 trillion at the end of Q1 2026, mostly public-sector work, and first-half 2026 new orders were also public-led. On the other hand, building revenue fell as private redevelopment projects stalled, and operating cash flow turned into an outflow in the first half of 2026.

The debt-to-equity ratio remained high at 234% at the end of Q1 2026, and the expansion of related-party dealings and the KRW 11.5 billion counterclaim also need monitoring. With a small earnings base, reported profit and valuation multiples can change sharply from quarter to quarter.

The next checkpoints are the formal contract for Section 6 of the Gangdong-Hanam-Namyangju rail line, the Gyeyang-Ganghwa Section 1 contract and the 2026 annual report. This material is provided for information only and does not contain a buy or sell opinion.
