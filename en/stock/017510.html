02

Business structure

SemyungElectricMachinery was founded in 1962 as a trading company and listed on KOSDAQ in 1991, and it makes transmission and substation fittings.

Its core product is fittings for transmission and substation lines supplied to KEPCO, and it also supplies railway overhead line products, sectional beams for textile looms and automotive parts. In 2024 it developed 500kV HVDC fittings and has been building its order book on that technology.

The order backlog grew from KRW 3.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 21.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 34.7 billion in the first nine months of 2025. A company official said orders for 500kV HVDC fittings keep coming in and production volume is rising.

The company says it has ample spare capacity and will raise utilization by adding staff. On inputs, the fittings industry is somewhat affected by labor, processing and aluminum costs, but the company said copper price increases have little effect on it.

In the competitive picture, it is grouped with power equipment names such as LS Electric, Hyosung Heavy Industries and HD Hyundai Electric, and with cable and component makers such as Iljin Electric, Cheryong Electric, Dawon Cable and Gaon Cable. Because its scale and product mix differ, direct comparisons are best drawn against fittings specialists.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue on a disclosed basis went from KRW 14 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.8 billion in 2023 (down 15.3%), then KRW 14.4 billion in 2024 (up 21.9%) and KRW 32.3 billion in 2025 (up 124.0%), rising for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025. 2025 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 span.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.02 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.35 billion in 2023, KRW 3.03 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.8 billion in 2025, rising for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, with 2025 up 389.0%. The operating margin moved from 7.3% in 2022 to 11.4% in 2023, 21.0% in 2024 and 45.8% in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 revenue and profit increase to the KEPCO HVDC 500kV transmission construction order. Operating profit grew much faster than revenue, which points to a large fixed-cost absorption effect.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 2.25 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.31 billion in 2023 (down 42.0%), KRW 4.69 billion in 2024 and KRW 12.8 billion in 2025, rising for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed at the same amounts for each year. Quarterly, revenue rose from KRW 6.46 billion in Q3 2025 with operating profit of KRW 1.87 billion to KRW 9.78 billion and KRW 3.8 billion in Q4 2025.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 9.56 billion, down 2.2% from the prior quarter, while operating profit rose 4.4% to KRW 3.97 billion. In Q2 2026 revenue reached KRW 12.6 billion and operating profit KRW 5.59 billion, with both rising from the prior quarter.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 8.4 billion (down 33.6% quarter on quarter, up 30.1% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 3.3 billion (down 41.0% quarter on quarter, up 76.9% year on year). Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 13 billion.

Operating cash flow turned from deficits of KRW -3.03 billion in 2022 and KRW -5.09 billion in 2023 to positive KRW 15.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 12.2 billion in 2025. The debt ratio rose to 18.5% in 2025 from 12.6% in 2024.

05

Industry analysis

A major driver of power demand is the spread of AI data centers. As hyperscalers expand data centers and power demand rises quickly, expectations for more orders for extra-high-voltage transformers, power cables and transmission and distribution equipment are stirring sentiment in related stocks.

Strong extra-high-voltage transformer exports to North America and Europe belong to the same trend.

In Korea, the normalization of electricity tariffs and the policy shift away from nuclear power have raised nuclear output and activated transmission line construction, which is cited as the background for better fittings sales.

The company official also linked the government's energy highway policy to HVDC construction. In rail, a rail electrification investment plan is lifting demand for overhead line products, according to company commentary.

The cycle position looks like a structure with a lag between construction start and revenue recognition. The company says HVDC work did not start at one point but has been under way for one to two years.

Among competitors, SemyungElectricMachinery is small in scale next to large power equipment makers such as LS Electric, Hyosung Heavy Industries and HD Hyundai Electric. Within fittings, its in-house development of 500kV HVDC products is highlighted as a point of differentiation. Copper and aluminum price swings are cited as a burden for the wire and cable industry.

Quarterly results and information limits

Overall view
