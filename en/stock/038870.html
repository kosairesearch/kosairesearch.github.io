Clean Hydrogen Pivot Amid Persistent Operating Losses

EcoSimplex is building out a biogas-based clean hydrogen business on top of its core landfill-gas power and renewable infrastructure maintenance operations, but the company has posted operating losses for four straight years from 2022 through 2025, and a turn to profitability has yet to be confirmed.

02

Business structure

EcoSimplex, founded in 1997, is an environmental energy company that operated under the name EcoBio until changing to its current name on April 17, 2026.

Its core business is the construction and operation and maintenance (O&M) of renewable energy facilities such as landfill-gas power plants, with the Sudokwon Landfill Site Management Corporation as a key client; in January 2026 the company signed a third-phase O&M services contract for the Sudokwon landfill power facility worth KRW 4.0118 billion.

On top of this, the company is growing a new business that refines biogas from sewage treatment into clean hydrogen for production and supply.

Its 'ECO-Hydrogen Station,' which uses biogas from Seoul's Seonam Water Reclamation Center, held a completion ceremony on June 24, 2026, processing about 4,000 Nm³ of biogas daily to produce 500 kg of clean hydrogen, supplied directly on-site to a nearby hydrogen refueling station.

The project also included an expansion adding compressors and dispensers to serve commercial-vehicle charging demand.

In October 2025 the company signed an MOU with Yeongcheon city in North Gyeongsang Province for an integrated project to produce 1,000 kg per day of clean hydrogen from food-waste biogas, which is billed as the largest such facility nationwide.

The company also runs a bio-sulfur recycling business that processes hydrogen sulfide sludge.

Revenue is centered on the renewable energy infrastructure construction and maintenance segment, while the hydrogen production and refueling segment is still in an early commercial-operation stage, and its exact share of total revenue is not disclosed by segment.

On the competitive front, large construction firms such as Hyundai Engineering & Construction and other participants in local-government-linked biogas hydrogen tenders are increasingly active, intensifying competition for policy-driven project awards.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 16.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.8 billion in 2023 (down 5.9%), rebounded to KRW 18 billion in 2024 (up 14.1%), then declined again to KRW 17.2 billion in 2025 (down 4.3%). Among 2022-2025, 2024 was the largest revenue year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating losses widened from KRW 147 million in 2022 to KRW 2.6 billion in 2023, narrowed to KRW 2.36 billion in 2024, then widened again to KRW 2.66 billion in 2025, marking four consecutive years of operating losses from 2022 through 2025.

The operating margin worsened from -0.9% in 2022 to -16.5% in 2023, improved somewhat to -13.1% in 2024, then slipped again to -15.4% in 2025.

Controlling-interest net income swung from a loss of KRW 4.7 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 2.45 billion in 2023, then reversed to a loss of KRW 3.67 billion in 2024, with the loss narrowing to KRW 2.26 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests moved differently, at -KRW 4.7 billion in 2022, +KRW 2.35 billion in 2023, -KRW 5.46 billion in 2024 and -KRW 7.3 billion in 2025, indicating considerable volatility in non-controlling interest results relative to the controlling-interest figure.

On a quarterly basis, after an operating profit of KRW 251 million in Q2 2025, the company posted operating losses for four consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 through Q2 2026, while revenue over the same stretch fell from KRW 4.7 billion in Q2 2025 to KRW 2.85 billion in Q1 2026 before rebounding to KRW 3.48 billion in Q2 2026, still down 25.9% year on year.

Controlling-interest net income moved in the opposite direction in some quarters: despite operating losses, the company posted large net profits of KRW 1.89 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 1.98 billion in Q2 2026, suggesting non-operating items had a meaningful swing effect on quarterly results, and the cumulative controlling-interest net result over the trailing four quarters (Q3 2025-Q2 2026) was a loss of KRW 411 million.

The Q1 2026 revenue slowdown has been linked to weaker renewable infrastructure construction and maintenance revenue along with a temporary suspension of hydrogen station operations during construction of the Seonam clean hydrogen production facility.

05

Industry analysis

The landfill-gas power generation and renewable infrastructure maintenance business has the character of publicly tendered SOC work, which limits both demand volatility and margin expansion potential.

By contrast, the clean hydrogen production and supply segment is in an early growth phase, supported by the government's hydrogen economy roadmap and carbon-neutrality policy, with annual government tenders for biogas-based clean hydrogen production support shaping project opportunities through policy funding and competitive bidding.

On the competitive front, large construction firms such as Hyundai Engineering & Construction have also moved into water-electrolysis-based hydrogen production facilities, leaving smaller companies like EcoSimplex to focus on the specific technical niche of biogas resource conversion.

Hydrogen production facilities using small-scale, medium-to-large-scale, electrolysis, and carbon-capture methods are being built across the country, dispersing regional supply chains, while the clean hydrogen certification and incentive framework is still being established, often delaying commercial viability.

Converting biogas into hydrogen is positioned as reducing carbon dioxide emissions versus fossil-fuel-based grey hydrogen, but translating this into revenue depends on facility utilization rates and securing sales contracts.

The company's participation in the 2024 government tender, which it describes as an early case of on-site commercial operation, is notable for building a track record in policy-driven project awards.

Outlook

The company rebranded from EcoBio to EcoSimplex on April 17, 2026, positioning the clean hydrogen business as its core growth driver.

Its Magok ECO-Hydrogen Station in Seoul held a completion ceremony on June 24, 2026 and entered commercial operation, with an expansion already completed to serve large-vehicle charging such as route buses and police buses.

In Yeongcheon, North Gyeongsang Province, the company signed an MOU with the city on October 29, 2025 for an integrated biogas-based clean hydrogen production project with a total budget of KRW 26 billion (KRW 13 billion public, KRW 13 billion private), targeting completion by December 2027 with a daily production capacity of 1,000 kg of clean hydrogen.

On the financial side, the board resolved on April 22, 2026 to cancel its own sixth-series convertible bonds (face value KRW 21 billion, book value KRW 3.1815 billion) as a shareholder-value measure.

Going forward, formal quarterly results will be disclosed via the Q3 report due around mid-November 2026 (filing deadline November 16), and the utilization and revenue contribution of the new hydrogen projects in Magok and Yeongcheon are likely to be key variables for the trajectory of the company's results.

However, no specific guidance on the timing or scale of revenue contribution from these new projects has been confirmed, so progress will need to be tracked gradually through disclosed quarterly filings.

Business scope is expanding into Seoul and North Gyeongsang in step with government hydrogen economy policy. However, the utilization rate and profit contribution timing of the new facilities still need to be confirmed through disclosed quarterly results.

Periods of Controlling-Interest Net Profit Turnaround

Risk factors

Policy and Regulatory Dependence Risk

The clean hydrogen production business is heavily dependent on annual government tender selections and subsidy support from ministries such as the Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment.

The clean hydrogen certification and incentive framework is still being established, so project viability can shift with policy changes. The publicly tendered landfill-gas O&M business is similarly affected by local government and public corporation budget policy.

Profitability and Earnings Volatility Risk

Operating income has been negative for four consecutive years, and quarterly controlling-interest net income has at times diverged from the direction of operating income, reducing predictability.

Net income including non-controlling interests also diverges substantially from the controlling-interest figure, suggesting that earnings volatility at subsidiaries has a material effect on consolidated results. It may take time for the new hydrogen businesses to become a stable revenue source.

Trading Supply-Demand and Market Alert Risk

The stock has a history of being designated an investment caution issue in July 2026 due to concentrated single-account trading, and escalation of the market alert system could lead to a trading halt.

With a market capitalization of roughly KRW 30 billion, liquidity is limited, and trading by specific accounts can amplify short-term price volatility. This supply-and-demand-driven volatility can operate independently of underlying fundamentals.

Overall view

EcoSimplex is in a transitional phase, building a new clean hydrogen production and refueling growth axis on top of its existing renewable energy infrastructure business such as landfill-gas power.

Based on disclosed filings, however, the company has posted operating losses for four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025, and the 2025 operating loss widened to KRW 2.66 billion from the prior year, so no numerical confirmation of improved profitability has yet emerged.

Controlling-interest net income has swung sharply in different directions from operating income across quarters; while the cumulative net loss over the trailing four quarters was a relatively contained KRW 411 million, that volatility itself makes interpretation more difficult.

The 2026 name change, the start of commercial operation at the Magok hydrogen station, and the Yeongcheon MOU are confirmed facts of business expansion, but no specific guidance exists yet on the timing or scale of their revenue contribution.

The stock trades at a discount to book value, though earnings-based comparison remains limited given the recent quarterly losses. Investors should continue to track the trend through the Q3 2026 report due in mid-November and the operating performance of the new hydrogen projects.
