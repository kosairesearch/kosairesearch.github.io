Report overview

RaonSecure: Loss Narrowing as the Platform Mix Grows

RaonSecure's disclosed revenue rose for three consecutive years through 2025, but first-half 2026 revenue fell, and operating profit returned to the black in the third quarter of 2026.

  1. 1

    Annual revenue rose from KRW 46.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 63.8 billion in 2025, the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

  2. 2

    Operating profit swung to a KRW 1.65 billion operating loss in 2023 before recovering to KRW 2.78 billion in 2025, with a 4.4% operating margin.

  3. 3

    Net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, moving in the opposite direction to the operating profit recovery.

  4. 4

    First-half 2026 revenue fell 12.9% year on year to KRW 21.6 billion, and the company's stated full-year revenue guidance is about KRW 60 billion (Hana Securities report, October 29, 2026).

  5. 5

    Five AI security products were unveiled on September 30, 2026, and the company is targeting a first contract within 2026 and revenue generation in the second half of 2027.

02

Business structure

RaonSecure is an IT security and authentication platform company that runs both security solutions and services. As of the second quarter of 2025, segment revenue mix was 43.5% solutions, 11.2% whitehat consulting, 16.8% IDaaS authentication and 22.8% blockchain services (BaaS).

In the first half of 2026, solutions accounted for 42% of revenue and services for 58%. Customers are 40% public sector, 30% financial and 30% general corporate.

Core products include a blockchain-based digital ID platform, multi-factor authentication (MFA), role-based access management (EAM), the cloud account management service OmniOne Access, and the subscription penetration testing service Raon PTaaS.

Overseas, the company has run digital ID projects in Indonesia and Costa Rica and supplies subscription-based biometric authentication to a Japanese bank in the SBI Group. Its Japanese MFA platform was reported in February 2026 as approaching 10 million monthly users.

Eugene Investment & Securities said the platform share of sales rose from 30% in 2023 to 50% in 2025 as the mix shifts from on-premise projects to recurring subscriptions. The company competes with domestic security solution and authentication vendors, including listed security firm Ahnlab. As of October 14, 2026, the major shareholder group led by Lee Soon-hyung held 39.99%.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue was KRW 46.8 billion in 2022, KRW 51.8 billion in 2023 (up 10.7% year on year), KRW 62.5 billion in 2024 (up 20.5%) and KRW 63.8 billion in 2025 (up 2.0%). Revenue rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

Eugene Investment & Securities said in a March 2026 report that 2025 sales were the highest since the company's founding.

Operating profit was KRW 4.29 billion in 2022, turned to an operating loss of KRW 1.65 billion in 2023, returned to profit at KRW 1.97 billion in 2024 and reached KRW 2.78 billion in 2025 (up 41.4%). Operating margin was 9.2% in 2022, -3.2% in 2023, 3.1% in 2024 and 4.4% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 6.67 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.16 billion in 2023 (down 37.6%), KRW 4.02 billion in 2024 (down 3.5%) and KRW 3.38 billion in 2025 (down 15.8%), declining for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests went from KRW 8.31 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.55 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 474 million in 2024 and KRW 6.72 billion in 2025.

In quarterly terms, operating profit of KRW 5.09 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 exceeded the full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 2.78 billion, and Hana Securities noted that public and financial project revenue is concentrated in the fourth quarter.

The first quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 9.42 billion (down 59.0% from the prior quarter) and an operating loss of KRW 2.15 billion, and the second-quarter operating loss narrowed to KRW 674 million as revenue rose for two consecutive quarters.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 13 billion fell 18.6% year on year, while operating profit of KRW 961 million returned to profit (down 39.9% year on year) and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 1.21 billion rose 50.4% year on year.

Hana Securities attributed the first-half 2026 revenue decline to the deconsolidation of a subsidiary, which contributed little to profit.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic information security demand is being driven by the government's comprehensive information security measures and the spread of artificial intelligence across the security industry, according to Eugene Investment & Securities and press reports.

Rising security incidents and tighter regulation are expanding demand for internal control and account and access management, which widens the operating environment for authentication and access control vendors.

Hana Securities said in an October 2026 report that RaonSecure's decentralized digital ID (DID) platform has been applied to a national mobile ID.

Overseas, MFA adoption is spreading mainly among Japanese brokerages, and Yuanta Securities said in a 2025 report it expected growth to continue for the company's services to an SBI Group affiliate. The projected KRW 150 trillion global digital ID market by 2030 is the company's own estimate.

In terms of cycle, an industry source noted in a February 2025 ZDNet Korea report that security companies tend to concentrate profits at year-end. With services at 58% of first-half 2026 revenue, the company combines a solution business with a subscription-style service model.

Securities analysts expect policy demand to spread into mobile ID, whitehat consulting, IDaaS authentication and blockchain services. The revenue scale of the AI security products has not yet been disclosed, so their contribution remains to be confirmed in future results.

06

Outlook

According to a Hana Securities report, the company's stated full-year 2026 revenue guidance is about KRW 60 billion, and public and financial project revenue is concentrated in the fourth quarter. The same report expected 2026 full-year operating profit to exceed the 2025 figure of KRW 2.78 billion.

In a March 2026 report, Eugene Investment & Securities projected 2026 revenue of KRW 70 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.8 billion, and the guidance Hana Securities cited (about KRW 60 billion) is lower, so the two estimates differ.

On the AI security front, the company unveiled five AI security products on September 30, 2026, including a product for AI agent and robot identity.

The company targets a first contract for its AI guardrail and penetration testing products within 2026 and has set a target for revenue generation in the second half of 2027.

The subscription business is shifting toward SMB offerings such as OmniOne Access and Raon PTaaS, which Eugene Investment & Securities said would strengthen the recurring revenue base. The user count of its Japanese MFA platform was reported in February 2026 as approaching 10 million monthly users.

On dividends, the company said it will change its procedure to fix the dividend amount before setting the record date.

08

Bull factors

Room for margin improvement from the platform shift

The platform share of sales rose from 30% in 2023 to 50% in 2025, and Eugene Investment & Securities said a larger subscription base would strengthen the platform leverage effect. The operating margin was 9.2% in 2022 and 4.4% in 2025, which leaves room for recovery on this business metric. The company has said it will raise the share of subscription services aimed at SMBs.

Expanding policy and regulatory demand

Government information security measures, rising security incidents and tighter regulation are increasing demand for internal control and account and access management.

Eugene Investment & Securities noted in a March 2026 report that revenue from whitehat consulting rose 47.5%, IDaaS authentication 33.3% and blockchain services 17.2%. Hana Securities said the company's DID platform has been applied to a national mobile ID.

AI security initiatives and overseas expansion

Five AI security products were unveiled on September 30, 2026, and the company targets a first contract for its AI guardrail and penetration testing products within 2026. Its Japanese MFA platform was reported as approaching 10 million monthly users.

The overseas digital ID business is seeking to expand into Latin America and Southeast Asia, building on projects in Indonesia and Costa Rica.

09

Bear factors

Revenue decline in 2026 and a gap in guidance

First-half 2026 revenue was KRW 21.6 billion, down 12.9% year on year, and third-quarter revenue fell 18.6% year on year. Hana Securities cited the deconsolidation of a subsidiary as a factor in the first-half decline.

The company's stated guidance of about KRW 60 billion is below the KRW 70 billion that Eugene Investment & Securities projected in March 2026.

Quarterly concentration and a thin earnings base

Fourth-quarter 2025 operating profit of KRW 5.09 billion exceeded full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 2.78 billion, and the first quarter of 2026 recorded an operating loss of KRW 2.15 billion. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Over the last four quarters, controlling-shareholder net income totaled KRW 4.86 billion, a small earnings base.

Share price volatility and shareholder structure

The three-month share price return to mid-October 2026 was 135.75% according to WiseReport, indicating elevated short-term volatility. The major shareholder group holds 39.99%, and the free float ratio is 54.92%.

Because reported profit changes from period to period, share price moves cannot be assumed to track earnings changes at the same pace.

10

Risk factors

Timing of project revenue recognition

Public and financial project revenue is concentrated in the fourth quarter, so a delay in revenue recognition could cause full-year revenue and operating profit to differ from expectations.

As with the 2025 fourth quarter, when operating profit of KRW 5.09 billion exceeded the full-year figure, quarterly variation is large. Fourth-quarter 2026 results will be confirmed in the annual report due in late March 2027.

Commercialization and competition in new businesses

The AI security products were unveiled in September 2026, but their revenue contribution has not been disclosed, and the revenue target is the second half of 2027. Competition with domestic and overseas security companies may intensify in these new areas. If commercialization is delayed, the benefit of the subscription shift would also be delayed.

Dividend policy and shareholder structure

The company said it will change its dividend procedure by fixing the dividend amount before setting the record date, and it cited a 2025 payout ratio of 63.79%. Market data, however, show an effectively negligible cash dividend yield, so the sources differ. With the major shareholder group holding 39.99%, the free float is 54.92%.

12

Overall view

RaonSecure's disclosed results show revenue expansion alongside a recovery in operating profit. Revenue rose for three consecutive years to KRW 63.8 billion in 2025, and operating profit moved from a loss in 2023 to KRW 2.78 billion in 2025.

However, net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and the 2025 operating margin of 4.4% is below 9.2% in 2022.

In 2026, first-half revenue fell 12.9% to KRW 21.6 billion, and operating profit returned to the black in the third quarter at KRW 961 million. Bullish factors cited include a higher platform and subscription share, policy demand and the AI security business.

Bearish factors include quarterly concentration of results, the revenue decline, a thin earnings base and share price volatility. On valuation, there is a gap between the March 2026 securities analysis and the current market multiples, and earnings-based multiples are sensitive to profit changes.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 close on December 31, the target deadline for the first AI security contract, and the annual report due in late March 2027.
