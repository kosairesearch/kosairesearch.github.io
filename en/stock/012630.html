02

Business structure

HDC is a holding company whose results center on its core subsidiary HDC Hyundai Development Company. The subsidiary focuses on urban redevelopment construction, outsourced housing construction and its own residential development projects.

Its own projects include Seoulwon IPARK, Cheongju Gajeong IPARK and Suwon IPARK City Blocks 10 to 12, and management said revenue recognition from these sites became full-scale in the fourth quarter of 2025.

On the order side, a KRW 924.4 billion redevelopment construction contract for the Yongsan Jeongbi-chang Zone 1 was disclosed in February 2026. Urban redevelopment orders rose 260.1% in 2025 from the prior year, a level reported as fifth-largest in the industry.

The urban redevelopment market was projected at about KRW 80 trillion in 2026, more than 20% larger than the prior year, and major builders are competing for construction rights in large districts such as Apgujeong, Yeouido, Mokdong and Seongsu.

Because the holding company reports subsidiaries on a consolidated basis, cost ratios and completion schedules at HDC Hyundai Development Company flow directly into HDC's results.

Non-construction affiliates include an advanced materials parts maker and a smart home and smart building technology company, which add some exposure beyond construction.

Competition for urban redevelopment contracts with large builders is intense, so bid pricing and construction cost control are seen as key to profitability.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 5.04 trillion in 2022 to KRW 5.91 trillion in 2023, KRW 6.2 trillion in 2024 and KRW 6.58 trillion in 2025. Revenue has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit grew from KRW 158.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 648.9 billion in 2025, up 88.2% in 2025, and it has also risen for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. The operating margin moved from 3.1% in 2022 to 5.3% in 2023, 5.6% in 2024 and 9.9% in 2025, so profit growth far outpaced revenue growth.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a net loss of KRW 5.11 billion in 2022 to KRW 113.1 billion in 2023, a swing to profit, then KRW 146.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 301 billion in 2025, rising for two consecutive years.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 416.5 billion in 2025, and the gap from the controlling-shareholder figure reflects the non-controlling share. Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 1.41 trillion in 2022 to KRW 244.3 billion in 2023, KRW 509 billion in 2024 and KRW 467.3 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue peaked at KRW 1.82 trillion in the third quarter of 2025, fell 24.4% to KRW 1.38 trillion in the fourth quarter, bottomed at KRW 1.26 trillion in the first quarter of 2026, and rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 1.71 trillion in the third quarter of 2026.

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 131.5 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and has increased for three consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, reaching KRW 280 billion.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 202.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026, the largest of the last five quarters, before falling 45.3% to KRW 110.5 billion in the third quarter.

Because quarterly swings are wide, the durability of profit depends on when subsidiary construction revenue is recognized.

05

Industry analysis

Assessments of the construction and development sector remain that conditions are still difficult. A representative of HDC Hyundai Development Company said the company exceeded its annual order target despite uncertainty from high interest rates and rising raw material prices.

The urban redevelopment market was reported in early 2026 to be heading toward about KRW 80 trillion, more than 20% above the prior year. The subsidiary's 2025 new orders were KRW 5.83 trillion, about 124% of its early-year target of KRW 4.70 trillion.

The 2026 new-order target was set in the mid-KRW 6 trillion range, reported as KRW 6.5311 trillion by one outlet and KRW 6.5331 trillion by another, so the exact figure should be checked against filings.

Major builders are competing for contracts in high-value districts such as Apgujeong, Yeouido, Mokdong and Seongsu, which keeps competitive intensity high. Interest rates feed directly into residential sales timing and project financing costs. Even in the same market, margin gaps widen depending on cost management.

As a holding company, HDC's cash flow depends on subsidiary dividends and ownership structure, so sector changes reach its consolidated earnings with a lag.

06

Outlook

HDC Hyundai Development Company's 2026 consolidated revenue target is KRW 4.2336 trillion, slightly above 2025 revenue of KRW 4.147 trillion.

Its 2026 new-order target is in the mid-KRW 6 trillion range, and the company said it aims to reach KRW 6 trillion in orders by linking new bids to ongoing projects such as Yongsan. Reports also described a plan to concentrate resources on major urban redevelopment sites such as Apgujeong to secure construction rights.

Seoulwon IPARK and Cheongju Gajeong IPARK are scheduled for completion in 2027 to 2028, and reports said their earnings will continue to be recognized as construction progresses.

The third-quarter 2026 operating profit improvement at the subsidiary was reported to reflect construction progress at Seoulwon IPARK and lower cost ratios at the Cheonan and Paju projects.

Preliminary fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results are expected in early 2027, when the achievement of the annual order target will also be visible. The 2026 annual report must be filed by the end of March 2027, when full-year results become fixed in a regular filing.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 133.7% at the end of 2025 from 143.2% in 2024, and whether this improvement continues is the next checkpoint.

Risk factors

Interest Rates and Input Costs

High interest rates and rising raw material prices affect construction costs and housing demand at the same time. How much of the cost increase can be passed through to sale prices determines cost ratios.

If subsidiary construction cost ratios worsen, the impact flows directly into the holding company's consolidated operating profit.

Bidding Competition and Completion Timing

If bidding for large urban redevelopment projects overheats, contract pricing could weaken. A delay in the 2027 to 2028 completion schedule would push back the timing of earnings recognition. Whether the 2026 order target is met will need to be checked alongside annual results.

Holding Structure and Quarterly Swings

Holding company results depend on consolidated subsidiary performance. Wide quarterly swings in net profit attributable to controlling shareholders make short-term readings difficult. The gap between total net profit and the controlling-shareholder figure is large, so the size of non-controlling interests also matters.

Overall view

Operating profit rose to KRW 280 billion in the third quarter of 2026 even as revenue fell year on year, so profitability improved before scale expanded. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 301 billion in 2025, and the last four quarters total KRW 477.8 billion.
