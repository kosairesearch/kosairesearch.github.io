02

Business structure

KBI Dongkook IND (001620) is a KOSPI-listed maker of automotive parts. In a disclosure dated October 22, 2026, the company said it would acquire land and buildings in Ulsan to set up a new plant applying a lightweight, premium manufacturing method for crash pads.

Exports accounted for 64.30% of automotive parts sales in 2024, with domestic sales at 35.70% (FnGuide, December 2024). Production sites include the Shinasan plant in Korea and plants in Mexico and Spain, which FnGuide's company profile says were expanded in 2024.

Overseas affiliates include KDK Automotive GmbH in Germany, KDK Automotive Czech in the Czech Republic and KBI JAPAN in Japan. Of the company's total guarantee balance of KRW 53.9 billion as of the September 21, 2026 disclosure, KDK Automotive GmbH accounts for the largest share at KRW 35.6 billion.

Major customers are Hyundai Motor and Kia, and a 2021 press report said the company was the exclusive supplier of a moving center console for Hyundai's Ioniq 5. Because revenue is tied to new-model volumes at automakers, overseas plant utilization and local vehicle sales also feed into results.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 614.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 649.4 billion in 2023 (up 5.7%), KRW 668.4 billion in 2024 (up 2.9%) and KRW 808 billion in 2025 (up 20.9%). Sales have increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 965 million in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 4.5 billion in 2023, back to an operating loss of KRW 6.33 billion in 2024, and to operating profit of KRW 18.6 billion in 2025. The operating margin was -0.2% in 2022, 0.7% in 2023, -0.9% in 2024 and 2.3% in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 return to profit to higher domestic and overseas sales and to the end of a temporary profit reduction caused by a change in the estimated useful life of depreciable assets in the prior year (February 24, 2026 disclosure).

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.19 billion in 2022, KRW 7.61 billion in 2023 (up 81.5%), KRW 25.5 billion in 2024 (up 235.7%) and KRW 31.5 billion in 2025 (up 23.6%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 31.6 billion in 2025, a different figure from profit attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow rose from negative KRW 13.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 57.5 billion in 2023, KRW 64.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 82 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio (total liabilities divided by total equity) was 206.9% in 2022, 217.4% in 2023, 238.5% in 2024 and 221.6% in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit of KRW 14.2 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 was the largest in the period, after which the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.21 billion in the first quarter of 2026, operating profit of KRW 27.01 million in the second quarter and an operating loss of KRW 9.95 billion in the third quarter.

Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 188.5 billion, down 9.5% year on year, with a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 19 billion. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 18.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Revenue at domestic auto parts makers is tied directly to automakers' production and sales volumes, so new-model cycles largely set the direction of results.

KBI Dongkook IND also depends on new-model volumes from Hyundai Motor and Kia, and with exports at 64.30% of 2024 automotive parts sales, it is strongly exposed to overseas vehicle production and local demand.

In the shift toward electrification, lighter vehicle parts and premium interior components have become factors in supplier bidding, which is the context for the company's new plant built on a new process. Local labor costs and the won-translated exchange rate also affect profitability at overseas plants.

Listed auto parts peers such as Hyundai WIA and Sungwoo Hitech share similar exposure to automaker customers and new-model bidding competition.

Where the industry sits in its cycle will become clearer once automaker production trends and the company's quarterly operating margin are available after the fourth quarter of 2026.

Sales growth in 2025 and the swing to a third-quarter 2026 loss came within the same year, so how quickly revenue growth converts into profit remains the open question for the company.

06

Outlook

The largest confirmed item ahead is the Ulsan plant land and building purchase. The acquisition, approved by the board on October 21, 2026, has a scheduled acquisition date of January 15, 2027, and the disclosure says it is meant to build a new production line using new technology and processes for new vehicle orders.

At 3.15% of total assets, the KRW 27.5 billion purchase is not a large single investment, but with total liabilities of KRW 593.9 billion at end-2025, how the purchase is funded is a point to check in later disclosures.

Overseas, the guarantee for KDK Automotive GmbH was extended by one year to run from September 25, 2026 to September 25, 2027, and the disclosure notes that a correction filing is planned if the terms change at actual contract signing.

Whether the Mexico and Spain plants, expanded in 2024, translate into more overseas volume will be a key point for 2027 results. Because an operating loss occurred in the third quarter of 2026, whether the operating margin returns to positive territory in later quarters is the central item to confirm.

The timing and size of revenue from the new vehicle orders have not been disclosed, so whether production start dates and order volumes are made public is the next point to watch.

08

Bull factors

Return to operating profit and sales growth

Sales in 2025 rose 20.9% year on year to KRW 808 billion, and operating profit turned to KRW 18.6 billion. The company attributed the improvement to higher domestic and overseas sales and to the end of a depreciation-related profit reduction in the prior year. Sales have risen for three consecutive years from 2023, which points to steady top-line growth.

Ulsan plant for new vehicle orders

The KRW 27.5 billion Ulsan land and building purchase, disclosed on October 22, 2026, is intended to set up a production line with new technology and processes for new model orders. The acquisition amount is stated as 3.15% of total assets. FnGuide reported that the Mexico and Spain plants were expanded in 2024 to increase overseas capacity.

Improved cash generation

Operating cash flow improved from negative KRW 13.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 82 billion in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders also rose from KRW 4.19 billion in 2022 to KRW 31.5 billion in 2025. Profit and cash flow growing together is a fact to note when assessing financial capacity.

Bear factors

Third-quarter 2026 loss

The operating loss in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 9.95 billion and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 19 billion, the largest loss in the observed quarters.

An operating loss of KRW 1.21 billion also appeared in the first quarter of 2026, and second-quarter operating profit was only KRW 27.01 million. Compared with KRW 14.2 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, the swing in quarterly results is a weakness.

High debt burden

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 593.9 billion, well above total equity of KRW 268 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 238.5% in 2024 to 221.6% in 2025 but remains above 200%. Because the industry requires continuing capital spending, borrowing costs and debt could weigh more heavily if results weaken.

Expanding guarantees for affiliates

A disclosure dated September 21, 2026 approved a KRW 17.2 billion guarantee for affiliate KDK Automotive GmbH, bringing the company's total guarantee balance to KRW 53.9 billion. KDK Automotive GmbH accounts for the largest part at KRW 35.6 billion. The affiliate reported 2025 net profit of KRW 3.47 billion.

Risk factors

Customer concentration and automaker sales swings

Revenue depends on new-model volumes at Hyundai Motor, Kia and other automakers. The 2025 sales increase was attributed to higher domestic and overseas sales, but a drop in automaker production or sales would quickly reduce volumes. Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 188.5 billion, down 9.5% year on year.

Financial leverage and large capital spending

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 593.9 billion, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 221.6%. Final payment of KRW 27.5 billion for the Ulsan site is scheduled for January 15, 2027. If operating losses continue, the burden of funding the investment could increase.

Affiliate guarantees and overseas production

Most of the company's KRW 53.9 billion total guarantee balance relates to borrowings of overseas affiliates. Operating results at the German, Czech and Japanese affiliates and at the Mexico and Spain plants affect consolidated results. Exchange rates and local production costs are variables that determine profitability overseas.

Overall view

KBI Dongkook IND showed a return to annual operating profit in 2025, with sales of KRW 808 billion and operating profit of KRW 18.6 billion, alongside sales growth. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders also rose from KRW 4.19 billion in 2022 to KRW 31.5 billion in 2025.

However, the third quarter of 2026 brought an operating loss of KRW 9.95 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 19 billion, raising quarterly volatility again.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 221.6% at end-2025 from 238.5% in 2024 but remains high, and the total guarantee balance stands at KRW 53.9 billion. The Ulsan plant purchase of KRW 27.5 billion, disclosed on October 22, 2026, aims to serve new vehicle orders, with final payment scheduled for January 15, 2027.

Strengths include the return to profit, improved cash flow and investment for new models, while weaknesses include quarterly losses, high debt and dependence on automakers.

The next checkpoints are the provisional 2026 annual results expected around late February 2027 and the 2026 annual report expected around mid-March 2027, which will show fourth-quarter operating profit and the debt-to-equity ratio.
