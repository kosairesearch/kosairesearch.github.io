Crimson Desert Fades, Black Desert Holds Up Earnings

Crimson Desert's quarterly sales have declined since its first-quarter peak, and a rebound at Black Desert and an 86% overseas revenue mix carried PearlAbyss's third quarter of 2026.

  1. 1

  3. 3

02

Business structure

PearlAbyss develops PC and console packaged games and long-running online games. Its core franchises are Black Desert, an online MMORPG, and Crimson Desert, an open-world action game launched globally on March 20, 2026.

In the third quarter of 2026, Black Desert generated KRW 61.0 billion and Crimson Desert generated KRW 40.6 billion, so Crimson Desert accounted for about 40% of total revenue. Black Desert runs on continuous paid user spending, which helps support quarterly revenue even as new-release sales decline.

In the third quarter Black Desert rose 10.9% from the prior quarter, helped by the new region Edania Part 2 and the new Agent class.

Crimson Desert's sales concentrated at launch as is typical for packaged games; third-quarter unit sales reached about 680,000 copies, but the average selling price fell from KRW 63,000 to KRW 59,000. By platform, PC accounted for 74% of third-quarter revenue, consoles 19% and mobile 7%.

By region, the Americas and Europe made up 68%, Asia 18% and Korea 14%. With overseas revenue at 86%, the business is highly exposed to the translation of foreign-currency income into won. The next major title, Dokkaebi, is in development, and the company targets a full marketing push in the second half of 2027. Crimson Desert was also reported to have won the Golden Joystick Award for PC Game of the Year.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue was KRW 385.7 billion in 2022, the largest year of the 2022–2025 window. It then fell to KRW 333.5 billion in 2023, down 13.5% year on year, before rising 2.7% to KRW 342.4 billion in 2024 and 6.8% to KRW 365.6 billion in 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 16.4 billion in 2022, turned into an operating loss of KRW 16.4 billion in 2023, and remained in the red in 2024 with a narrower operating loss of KRW 12.3 billion, then widened again to an operating loss of KRW 14.8 billion in 2025.

The operating margin moved from 4.2% in 2022 to -4.9% in 2023, -3.6% in 2024 and -4.1% in 2025.

Attributable net income swung from a net loss of KRW 43 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 15.2 billion in 2023, rose to KRW 60.3 billion in 2024, which was up 296.8%, and then returned to a net loss of KRW 8.41 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests followed the same path across all four years. In 2024 the company posted an operating loss while attributable net income was positive, so non-operating items and taxes need to be read alongside operating results.

Operating cash flow was KRW 47.6 billion in 2022 and KRW 32.4 billion in 2023, turned negative at -KRW 5.39 billion in 2024, and recovered to KRW 23.9 billion in 2025.

Quarterly results moved from revenue of KRW 106.8 billion, operating profit of KRW 10.6 billion and attributable net income of KRW 29 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 8.41 billion and an attributable net loss of KRW 15.2 billion in the fourth quarter, when revenue fell 10.6% quarter on quarter to KRW 95.5 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue reached KRW 328.5 billion, operating profit KRW 212.1 billion and attributable net income KRW 158 billion, driven by the Crimson Desert launch.

In the second quarter revenue was KRW 192.6 billion, down 41.4% quarter on quarter, operating profit was KRW 67.6 billion, down 68.1%, and attributable net income was KRW 99.5 billion, down 37.0%.

In the third quarter revenue was KRW 101.5 billion, operating profit KRW 20.7 billion and attributable net income KRW 10.8 billion. Third-quarter operating expenses were KRW 80.8 billion, down 35.4% from the prior quarter but up 27.6% from a year earlier.

The company said personnel costs fell 50.9% quarter on quarter after one-off bonuses in the second quarter did not recur, while advertising spending rose 22% on the Haidel festival and Crimson Desert Enhanced marketing.

Press reports described third-quarter revenue as up 18% and operating profit as down 8.8% year on year, but because the company restated its 2026 results on a continuing-operations basis after selling its Pearl Abyss stake, those comparisons differ from the year-on-year figures in the disclosure table.

05

Industry analysis

In packaged games, Crimson Desert's sales curve follows the typical pattern of demand concentrated shortly after launch.

Its revenue fell from KRW 266.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 136.1 billion in the second and KRW 40.6 billion in the third, and the company attributed this to packaged-game demand clustering around launch.

To extend sales life, the company is relying on its first DLC, Journey into the Unknown, released worldwide on October 30, 2026, along with discount sales and story-enhancement updates.

On the demand side, 68% of third-quarter revenue came from the Americas and Europe, so performance in Western console and PC markets is the key variable. On currency, the won-dollar rate fell about 10% from the level assumed when the business plan was set, and a high overseas revenue mix magnifies earnings swings.

In online gaming, Black Desert has steady paid spending, but the company said competition in mobile had intensified and weakened that version somewhat.

Black Desert's new content and the Hyper Boost system for returning players were followed by improvements in Steam rankings, daily active users and streaming viewership.

In terms of cycle position, Crimson Desert is in the sales-tail stage after launch, while Black Desert is a live service whose revenue moves with content updates. Running two IPs at once means the online service revenue partly offsets slower sales from the packaged title.

Outlook

The company guided 2026 revenue of KRW 709.8 billion to KRW 743.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 315.5 billion to KRW 345.2 billion.

In its November 12, 2026 results materials, the company projected fourth-quarter operating revenue of KRW 87.2 billion to KRW 121.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 15.1 billion to KRW 44.8 billion. The same materials said a stronger won could leave fourth-quarter results slightly below guidance.

The company said Crimson Desert's full-game sales have risen since the DLC launch, and it is combining discounts with marketing to extend sales life. The Nintendo Switch 2 version is being prepared with a target release in the first half of 2027, and the company said specific timing will be announced later.

Black Desert will hold its Kalpeon Festival in December 2026 and plans to unveil 2027 content and live-service plans. For Dokkaebi, the company plans to reveal development status within 2026 and targets a full marketing push in the second half of 2027, while keeping a 2–3 year gap between releases.

Press reports have put Dokkaebi's launch in the second half of 2028. The company said it is studying the possibility of multiplayer for Crimson Desert but gave no timeline. On shareholder returns, the company plans its first dividend based on 2026 results and continues to pursue share buybacks and cancellations.

Bull factors

Black Desert revenue rebound

Black Desert revenue reached KRW 61.0 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 10.9% from the prior quarter. The company said the new region Edania Part 2, the new Agent class and the Hyper Boost system for new and returning players improved Steam rankings and daily active users. Because paid spending continues, the franchise supports the earnings base even when new-release sales decline.

Extended sales life for Crimson Desert

Crimson Desert sold about 680,000 copies in the third quarter of 2026, and its first DLC, Journey into the Unknown, launched on October 30. The company said base-game sales have also increased since the DLC release.

A Nintendo Switch 2 version, with a target of the first half of 2027, is being prepared to reach a new player base.

Improved balance sheet and shareholder returns

The debt ratio fell to 43.8% at the end of 2025 from 74.8% in 2022, with total liabilities of KRW 350 billion and total equity of KRW 799.1 billion. Operating cash flow recovered to KRW 23.9 billion in 2025 from a negative figure in 2024.

The company said it plans a first dividend based on 2026 results and continues to pursue share buybacks and cancellations.

Bear factors

Sharp decline in Crimson Desert sales

Crimson Desert revenue fell each quarter in 2026, from KRW 266.5 billion in the first quarter to KRW 136.1 billion in the second and KRW 40.6 billion in the third. The average selling price declined from KRW 63,000 to KRW 59,000, which the company attributed to the stronger won and discount sales. If the sales curve does not flatten, meeting annual guidance could become harder.

Stronger won and high overseas mix

Overseas revenue accounted for 86% of third-quarter 2026 revenue, so currency moves feed directly into reported results. The company said the won-dollar rate fell about 10% from the level assumed in its business plan and cited the stronger won as the reason net income declined. The company also said fourth-quarter results could slightly miss guidance.

Rising costs and loss history

Third-quarter 2026 operating expenses were KRW 80.8 billion, up 27.6% from a year earlier, with higher personnel, fee and advertising costs cited as drivers. The company posted operating losses from 2023 through 2025, and the 2025 operating margin was -4.1%. The company also said the mobile version of Black Desert weakened amid intensifying competition.

Risk factors

Currency

With overseas sales accounting for 86% of revenue, fluctuations in the KRW/USD exchange rate affect both KRW-translated revenue and net income. The company said that a stronger won could cause it to come in slightly below its fourth-quarter guidance.

If the direction of the exchange rate does not change, it is uncertain whether the company will meet its full-year guidance.

New titles and release timing

Dokkaebi has a target of revealing development status within 2026 and a full marketing push in the second half of 2027, while press reports put its launch in the second half of 2028. The Switch 2 version is targeted for the first half of 2027, so any delay would postpone the benefit of extended sales life. Changes to development timelines are confirmed through the company's official announcements.

Earnings volatility

There were years between 2023 and 2025 in which the company posted an operating loss, and its first-quarter 2026 results relied heavily on the launch of Crimson Desert.

A structure that depends on the success of a single IP leads to large quarterly swings, and net income attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters was also concentrated in the first quarter. The key question is how much the stable revenue from Black Desert can offset these swings.

Overall view

PearlAbyss's 2026 results have moved on two axes: the launch effect of Crimson Desert and the rebound of Black Desert.

Crimson Desert revenue has declined every quarter, from KRW 266.5 billion in the first quarter to KRW 40.6 billion in the third, while Black Desert rose to KRW 61.0 billion in the third quarter and represents about 60% of total revenue.

On the annual disclosure basis, the company posted operating losses from 2023 through 2025 with a negative operating margin, while attributable net income was positive in 2024 and turned into a net loss in 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 74.8% in 2022 to 43.8% in 2025, and total equity was KRW 799.1 billion at year-end 2025.

The 2026 guidance is KRW 709.8 billion to KRW 743.8 billion in revenue and KRW 315.5 billion to KRW 345.2 billion in operating profit, and fourth-quarter results may come in slightly below that path because of the stronger won.

Supportive factors include improving user metrics at Black Desert, efforts to extend Crimson Desert's sales life through DLC and the Switch 2 version, and shareholder return plans. Headwinds include the Crimson Desert sales curve, currency exposure from an 86% overseas mix, and rising operating costs.

The next items to track are fourth-quarter 2026 results, the confirmed figures in the 2026 annual report, and whether the Switch 2 version launches in the first half of 2027.
