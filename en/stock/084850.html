02

Business structure

ITM Semiconductor focuses on secondary battery protection circuits and also runs battery packs, sensors, e-cigarette and automotive electronics businesses. Its main products are the Protection Module Package (PMP) and POC, and PMP accounted for KRW 397.3 billion in 2025, or 65.9% of consolidated revenue.

E-cigarette sales were KRW 135.9 billion, or 22.5% of revenue, making it the second-largest segment. In e-cigarettes the company supplies devices and cartridges for KT&G's heated tobacco platform, and Hana Securities cited an ODM cartridge share of about 85%.

On the customer side, the protection circuit business depended heavily on Apple, whose 2025 sales were KRW 345.8 billion, or 57.3% of consolidated revenue, and the company disclosed the termination of that business on April 13, 2026.

The company plans to fill the gap with Samsung flagship smartphone protection circuits and wearable device applications. Samsung-related protection circuit sales were KRW 15.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026, up 68% year on year.

New businesses include defense battery packs, marine energy storage systems, and motor controllers and battery management systems for robots, and the company already supplies motor controllers to a global four-legged robot maker.

The largest shareholder is NICE Holdings and related parties with a 63.23% stake, and treasury shares make up 8.79%, as of April 24, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 633.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 580.5 billion in 2023 (down 8.4% year on year), then rose to KRW 647.9 billion in 2024 (up 11.6%), the largest year in the 2022–2025 period, before declining to KRW 603.1 billion in 2025 (down 6.9%).

Operating losses of KRW 16.2 billion in 2022 and KRW 14.9 billion in 2023 narrowed in 2023, and the company returned to an operating profit of KRW 2.4 billion in 2024. The 2025 operating loss of KRW 7 billion turned the year back into deficit, and the operating margin moved from 0.4% in 2024 to -1.2% in 2025.

The net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from losses of KRW 21.3 billion in 2022 and KRW 33 billion in 2023 to a profit of KRW 1.22 billion in 2024, then to a loss of KRW 93.6 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests followed the same pattern, with a loss of KRW 21.3 billion in 2022, a loss of KRW 33 billion in 2023, a profit of KRW 1.22 billion in 2024, and a loss of KRW 93.6 billion in 2025.

Hana Securities cited equipment impairment of KRW 59.6 billion reflected in 2025 as the factor that set the earnings trough. Financial structure weakened in 2025, with the debt-to-equity ratio rising from 206.2% in 2024 to 417.9%, year-end total equity of KRW 94.6 billion and total liabilities of KRW 395.3 billion.

Operating cash flow stayed positive every year, at KRW 21.2 billion in 2022, KRW 53.2 billion in 2023, KRW 29.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 23.7 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 508 million in the third quarter of 2025 turned into a loss of KRW 69.1 billion in the fourth quarter.

First-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 132.2 billion, down 20.7% from the prior quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 4.93 billion, and second-quarter revenue fell to KRW 3.41 billion, down 97.4%, a period that overlaps with the Apple termination on April 13, 2026.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 80.2 billion with an operating loss of KRW 3.89 billion, a loss that narrowed from the second quarter but turned the year-on-year comparison into a swing to loss.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for ITM Semiconductor comes mainly from smartphones, wearable devices and e-cigarettes.

In protection circuits, one analysis says the PMP package, which replaces conventional PCMs, is gaining share as wearables and IoT devices spread, although the same analysis notes that profitability weakens in off-season periods.

Smartphone protection circuits are going through a change in customer mix in 2026, as the Apple business ends and supply for Samsung flagship models and wearable applications grows. The e-cigarette business operates as an ODM with a cartridge share of about 85% at KT&G, so it moves with the customer's product cycle.

In a March 2026 report, Hana Securities projected e-cigarette revenue of KRW 204.1 billion for 2026, up 50.1%, and a share of about 49% of total revenue. Defense and robotics remain at an early stage.

Hana Securities said the defense battery pack for next-generation submarines and unmanned underwater vehicles could move to mass production after prototype deliveries, and the robotics unit is widening its partnerships around drive components. The revenue scale of defense and robotics has not been disclosed.

Relative to its peers, the company depends heavily on a small number of large customers, so its results are shaped closely by those customers' product cycles.

06

Outlook

The company has set a target of returning to profit in the second half of 2026. On costs, lower depreciation following the 2025 equipment impairment is expected to contribute, and Hana Securities estimated the 2026 depreciation reduction at about KRW 20 billion.

On sales, the company targets more than doubling protection circuit sales and growing e-cigarette sales by more than 50% in 2026 relative to 2025. The company said Samsung-related protection circuit sales will keep rising through year-end as new models and supply volumes expand.

Hana Securities said mass production of a new e-cigarette device is scheduled for the second half of 2026 on the back of a sole-supplier award. New businesses include defense battery packs, marine energy storage systems, and motor controllers and battery management systems for humanoid robots.

However, Hana Securities projected a 2026 operating profit of KRW 10.4 billion in its March 2026 report, while operating losses have been disclosed for each quarter from the first through the third quarter of 2026.

The third-quarter 2026 operating loss narrowed to KRW 3.89 billion, but whether the company returns to profit will be confirmed in the fourth-quarter 2026 results and the annual business report.

The company has said it will clear the remaining costs of the Apple business by year-end, so the fourth-quarter cost structure is the main point to monitor.

Bear factors

Apple-related sales were KRW 345.8 billion in 2025, or 57.3% of consolidated revenue. The company disclosed the termination on April 13, 2026, and second-quarter 2026 revenue fell 97.4% from the prior quarter to KRW 3.41 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 80.2 billion was down 51.0% year on year, so the gap has not yet been fully filled.

Consecutive losses and financial burden

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 417.9% at the end of 2025 from 206.2% in 2024. Total equity was KRW 94.6 billion against total liabilities of KRW 395.3 billion.

Gap between projections and reported results

In a March 2026 report, Hana Securities projected a KRW 10.4 billion operating profit for 2026. Operating losses have nonetheless been disclosed in each quarter from the first through the third quarter of 2026.

The timing of mass production in defense and robotics has not been confirmed, leaving uncertainty in the outlook.

Risk factors

Customer concentration risk

A large share of revenue depends on a small number of customers. The Apple business has already ended, and reliance on Samsung Electronics and KT&G has grown. Changes in those customers' product cycles or order volumes flow directly into the company's results.

E-cigarette mass-production timing risk

Hana Securities said a new e-cigarette device is scheduled for mass production in the second half of 2026 on the back of a sole-supplier award. The company's target of growing e-cigarette sales by more than 50% depends on this timing, so if mass production starts after year-end, the path to that target could narrow.

Because the business operates as an ODM, the timing of mass production is set by the customer's product schedule. Whether production starts will show in the year-end 2026 results.

Residual Apple exit cost risk

The company has said it will clear the remaining costs of exiting the Apple business by year-end. If those costs are not resolved by then, they could remain a burden on the fourth-quarter cost structure and push back the timing of the return to profit.

With operating losses already narrowing in the third quarter, the scale of the remaining costs is not yet clear, making it difficult to judge whether the narrowing trend will continue into the fourth quarter.

How these costs are handled may be confirmed in the fourth-quarter operating results in the 2026 annual business report.

Overall view

ITM Semiconductor returned to an operating profit in 2024 after prior losses, then posted a loss again in 2025, and losses have been disclosed for four consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026.

As the Apple business of KRW 345.8 billion in 2025 falls away, revenue is shifting toward Samsung-related protection circuits and e-cigarettes.

Samsung-related protection circuit sales rose 68% year on year in the first quarter of 2026, and the e-cigarette business holds a cartridge share of about 85% while preparing a new device for mass production. Against that, third-quarter 2026 revenue fell 51.0% year on year and operating losses continued.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 417.9% at the end of 2025, with total equity of KRW 94.6 billion. Defense and robotics have been set as new directions, but their revenue scale has not yet been disclosed.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 results and annual business report, followed by operating results in the first-quarter 2027 report.
