02

Business structure

MKElectron is a back-end materials company whose main products are bonding wire and solder balls used in semiconductor packaging. Its product lineup includes gold wire and other bonding wire, gold evaporation material, gold sputtering targets and solder balls, with bonding wire accounting for most of its revenue.

New businesses include palladium alloy for pogo pins, solder paste and materials for semiconductor test sockets. The business is anchored by the Korean head office and the Chinese subsidiary; the Chinese unit's Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 98.2 billion (up 56% year on year, preliminary figure announced May 15, 2026).

Customers include integrated device manufacturers, system semiconductor companies and global outsourced assembly and test (OSAT) providers, with applications spanning automotive, industrial, mobile and power semiconductors.

The Chinese unit's volume growth is concentrated in bonding wire supplied to Chinese customers among the top global OSATs. On a consolidated basis the group also includes a real-estate trust affiliate and an environmental energy affiliate, so its non-semiconductor results can move differently from the core business.

A new business in high-capacity silicon-alloy anode materials for secondary batteries is also under development. On competition, a brokerage analysis notes that global top-tier customers keep the company's market share high despite local Chinese competitors.

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated revenue rose from KRW 1.02 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.12 trillion in 2023 (up 9.2%), KRW 1.17 trillion in 2024 (up 4.8%) and KRW 1.4 trillion in 2025 (up 19.9%), growing for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year in the 2022–2025 span.

Operating profit was largest in 2022 at KRW 80.3 billion, then fell to KRW 46.5 billion in 2023 (down 42.1%), rose to KRW 56.1 billion in 2024 (up 20.7%), and dropped to KRW 14.2 billion in 2025 (down 74.7%), the smallest year in the span.

Revenue growth came with a shrinking margin, from 7.8% in 2022 to 4.2% in 2023, 4.8% in 2024 and 1.0% in 2025. Attributable net losses of KRW 11.6 billion in 2022, KRW 35.2 billion in 2023 and KRW 26.9 billion in 2024 gave way to attributable net income of KRW 1.33 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests moved from losses of KRW 39.1 billion in 2023 and KRW 37.7 billion in 2024 to a KRW 13.8 billion profit in 2025.

Operating cash flow swung from KRW 80.2 billion in 2022 to negative KRW 325.7 billion in 2023, KRW 166.5 billion in 2024 and negative KRW 159.3 billion in 2025; working-capital needs from faster revenue growth may have contributed.

Quarterly operating profit moved from a KRW 6.43 billion loss in Q3 2025 to KRW 10 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 15.1 billion in Q1 2026 (up 51.5% quarter on quarter), KRW 26.3 billion in Q2 2026 (up 73.6%) and KRW 12.1 billion in Q3 2026 (down 54.1%).

A brokerage noted that first-quarter consolidated results fell short of its estimates because of weak results at the real-estate trust affiliate and seasonality at the environmental energy affiliate. Second-quarter profitability was attributed to gold prices and exchange rates from March onward. Attributable net income for the last four quarters totals KRW 32.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

A brokerage analysis argues that the shift in AI server memory from DDR-centric to mixed LPDDR architectures is driving a rapid rise in bonding-wire demand. SOCAMM2, an LPDDR-based server memory module, is seen as greatly expanding bonding-wire demand because of its multi-layer stacking structure.

The same brokerage viewed the current phase as the early stage of a shift from a down-cycle to an up-cycle. The company said Chinese and Korean shipments rose together in Q1 2026 as the semiconductor market improved.

Gold wire sales are sensitive to gold prices and exchange rates, so price swings pass directly into margins. In China, localization of semiconductor supply chains favors materials suppliers with domestic customer bases.

Copper-based products carry higher margins than gold-based products, which analysts expect to lift contribution from the Chinese unit. On competition, local Chinese rivals exist, but global top-tier customers keep high share, which analysts cite as evidence of technical capability and entry barriers. Whether SOCAMM volume appears meaningfully in reported results remains to be verified in later filings.

06

Outlook

In its corporate value enhancement plan disclosed on April 1, 2026, the company listed securing working capital for its bonding-wire business and reviewing capacity expansion as key tasks. The plan also includes lowering raw material supply costs using infrastructure at its Eumseong recycling center in Chungbuk.

The company plans to spend on capital expenditure to expand production of solder paste and palladium alloy materials for semiconductor testing. It also said it would secure funds for dividends through higher sales and better profitability. The company said in May 2026 that it aims for record annual results this year.

It said funds for higher second-half volumes had already been secured, mainly through P-CBO financing, and it stated that it avoids large-scale investment in favor of steady growth.

A brokerage forecast from May 2026 projected 2026 consolidated revenue up 38% and operating profit up 583%; that forecast predates later disclosed quarters and should be checked against them. One analysis expects palladium alloy sales to grow substantially in 2027 from 2026.

The full effect of SOCAMM2 volume was expected from late Q2 or Q3 onward, which will show in later quarterly revenue mix.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly operating profit has stayed positive

After operating profit turned positive in Q4 2025, quarterly operating profit stayed in the black: KRW 15.1 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 26.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 12.1 billion in Q3. Q3 2026 operating profit also turned positive from a year earlier. Attributable net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 32.9 billion.

AI memory architecture and wire-bonding demand

Wider LPDDR adoption in server memory and high-layer stacking are cited as core drivers of bonding demand inside packages. SOCAMM2, an LPDDR-based server memory module, is seen as greatly expanding wire-bonding demand because of its multi-layer stacking. These drivers align with the company's bonding-wire-centered structure.

Chinese subsidiary growth and new businesses

Per the preliminary figures announced May 15, 2026, the Chinese unit's Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 98.2 billion, up 56% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 7.3 billion, up 204%. New businesses include domestically localized palladium alloy for higher-value materials, solder paste and test-socket materials. If these businesses reach scale, the revenue mix could change.

Bear factors

Record of weaker profitability

Annual operating profit in 2025 was KRW 14.2 billion, down 74.7%, and the operating margin fell to 1.0%. The margin had slipped from 7.8% in 2022 starting in 2023, and the 2026 quarterly recovery has not yet been confirmed at the annual level. Operating cash flow was also negative in 2023 and 2025.

Margin swings from gold prices and exchange rates

Analysts note that profitability improved from March onward because of gold prices and exchange rates. The same mechanism means margins could weaken if gold or the currency moves against the company. Higher bonding-wire volumes would not fully offset that if cost pass-through lags. This structure widens quarter-to-quarter swings in operating profit.

Affiliate results and financial load

Consolidated results fell short of estimates in Q1 2026 because of weak results at the real-estate trust affiliate and seasonality at the environmental energy affiliate. At the end of 2025 the debt ratio was 153.0%, with total liabilities of KRW 1.53 trillion. Measured against attributable equity of KRW 377.8 billion, financial leverage is high.

10

Risk factors

Raw material prices and FX

Gold-wire-centered products are highly exposed to gold prices and exchange rates. Shifts in the mix between gold and copper wire change both cost structure and margins. Part of the 2026 quarterly recovery came from these factors, making price swings the most direct risk.

Financial structure and funding

The debt ratio was 153.0% at the end of 2025, and operating cash flow was negative in 2023 and 2025. The company said it secured second-half funding mainly through P-CBO financing. Such funding can bring potential changes in share count, which remains a risk.

Customer concentration and governance

The Chinese unit's volume growth is concentrated among Chinese customers that are among the top global OSATs. The largest shareholder group holds more than 30%, reported between 31.83% and 37.21% depending on the source. Changes in major customers or in ownership structure could affect both the float and results.

  2. March 2027 annual general meeting (held March 31 in 2026)

    Progress on the corporate value enhancement plan and the dividend decision are the checkpoints. Progress on capacity expansion review and capex for solder paste and palladium alloy also falls under this check.

  3. Around May 15, 2027 (statutory deadline for the Q1 2027 quarterly report)

    The check is whether SOCAMM2-related wire-bonding volume shows up in quarterly revenue and operating profit. It also covers how Chinese unit growth and gold-price and FX effects appear in quarterly margins.

  4. During 2027 (timing not yet set)

    The check is whether the bonding-wire capacity expansion review and capex for solder paste and palladium alloy are decided, and at what scale. Once capex is set, it will affect the financing structure and the financial load.

12

Overall view

MKElectron has moved through a 2025 low, with a 1.0% operating margin and a turn to attributable net profit, and quarterly operating profit has recovered through 2026.

In the disclosed results, Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 26.3 billion was the largest of the last five quarters, and attributable net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 32.9 billion.

Growth drivers include bonding-wire demand from AI server memory, volume growth at the Chinese unit and new businesses such as palladium alloy. On the other side, margins are exposed to gold prices and exchange rates, operating cash flow was negative in 2023 and 2025, and the debt ratio of 153.0% is a financial burden.

Q3 2026 operating profit fell from Q2, so the continuity of quarterly results is still to be confirmed. Shareholder concentration above 30% and swings at affiliates also shape consolidated results.

Brokerage analysis is positive on the structural shift in wire-bonding demand, but how SOCAMM volume appears in reported results is the central test. The next checkpoint is the annual operating margin and operating cash flow in the 2026 annual report.
