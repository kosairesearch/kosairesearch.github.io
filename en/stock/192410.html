Antenna Sales Grow, Earnings Power Still a Work in Progress

02

Business structure

Oneul E&M was founded in 1997 to manufacture and service electronics and telecom equipment and listed on KOSDAQ in 2014.

Its core business supplies base station antennas and 5G small cell antennas to carriers in Korea and abroad, and the antenna segment accounted for 99% of sales in Q2 2026 while the travel segment accounted for 1%. Media reports put overseas sales at 33.6% of revenue in 2024, rising to about 68% in 2025.

The largest overseas customer is US carrier AT&T, and the company said it held a 44% supplier share in AT&T's 5G network modernization project as of September 2025.

Small cell antenna supply to AT&T through its US subsidiary and its partnership with Crown Castle are presented as the entry point into North American stadium and venue antenna markets. In Europe the company supplies specialized antennas for metro telecom networks in Paris and Rome.

In Korea it maintains antenna supply through cooperation with the three national mobile carriers.

In new businesses, it has completed first-stage development of an active satellite-tracking antenna combining its robotics technology and has set up a dedicated R&D team for 6G and low-earth-orbit (LEO) satellite communication antennas.

New businesses remain centered on development-stage technology projects, so they are too early to treat as a replacement for antenna revenue. Within the sector, KMW, which makes antennas and RF components, is a comparison point, but differences in scale and customer mix limit direct comparisons.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 21.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.4 billion in 2023 (down 17.3% year on year) and KRW 13 billion in 2024 (down 25.4%), then recovered to KRW 16.7 billion in 2025 (up 28.2%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

The operating loss went from KRW 16 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.49 billion in 2023, KRW 6.49 billion in 2024, and KRW 1.85 billion in 2025, and the operating margin improved from -76.0% in 2022 to -11.1% in 2025.

Media reports attribute the 2025 improvement mainly to next-generation 5G telecom businesses and cite a telecom-only operating profit of about KRW 2.9 billion, reported on February 26, 2026.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 19.3 billion in 2022, KRW 13.9 billion in 2023, KRW 17.2 billion in 2024, and KRW 78.3 billion in 2025, the largest loss in the period, and the net loss including non-controlling interests was also KRW 78.3 billion in 2025.

The operating loss narrowed while the net loss widened sharply in 2025, which suggests non-operating items had a large effect and should be checked in the notes to the periodic report.

Operating cash flow was KRW -11.5 billion in 2022, KRW 10.6 billion in 2023, KRW -3.25 billion in 2024, and KRW -5.19 billion in 2025, positive only in 2023.

Quarterly revenue peaked at KRW 5.62 billion in Q3 2025, then came in at KRW 3.89 billion in Q4 2025 (down 30.7% quarter on quarter), KRW 5.12 billion in Q1 2026 (up 31.5%), KRW 4.52 billion in Q2 2026 (down 11.7%), and KRW 3.22 billion in Q3 2026 (down 28.7% quarter on quarter and down 42.7% year on year).

Operating profit was KRW 595 million in Q3 2025, followed by an operating loss of KRW 787 million in Q4 2025, KRW 386 million in Q1 2026, KRW 1.38 billion in Q2 2026, and KRW 3.44 billion in Q3 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to KRW 33.1 billion in Q3 2026, yet the operating loss in the same quarter was KRW 3.44 billion, a very large gap that appears to come from non-operating items; whether it is one-off should be checked in the Q3 2026 quarterly report's non-operating income detail.

The sum for the last four quarters is KRW 16.2 billion, and that total depends on this single profitable quarter.

05

Industry analysis

The company's end demand comes from carriers' 5G infrastructure spending, and management expects the global expansion of 5G investment to add to antenna growth. In the US, AT&T's expanded 5G and telecom infrastructure investment is driving higher order intake, according to the company and media coverage.

The company also cites intensifying competition to secure 6G technology as a medium-term demand driver. Demand for antennas at North American stadiums and venues ahead of the 2026 World Cup and the 2028 Olympics is another growth factor the company highlights.

With overseas sales at about 68% of 2025 revenue, the domestic share is roughly 32%, so the effect of domestic carrier investment on total sales is relatively limited. In cycle terms, the business appears to be transitioning from 5G infrastructure buildout toward 6G preparation.

On competition, the 44% supplier share in the AT&T 5G project is a company-reported figure, not an independent tally.

KMW, an antenna and RF component maker in the same sector, is a comparison point, but Samsung Electro-Mechanics is a large component maker with different scale and product mix, so direct comparison is difficult.

Reliance on project awards from a few overseas customers can be read as a factor that amplifies quarterly revenue swings.

06

Outlook

When it released its half-year report, the company presented the KT supply contract as an additional driver of second-half earnings improvement. Management said order intake at its US subsidiary and its domestic antenna business has been rising steadily.

The company stated it aims for a full turnaround in the second half and expects the pace of improvement to widen toward year-end.

However, the disclosed Q3 2026 results showed revenue of KRW 3.22 billion (down 28.7% quarter on quarter) and an operating loss of KRW 3.44 billion, which points in a different direction from that target.

On capital, the company is pursuing the sale of remaining convertible bonds and said it can fully clear capital impairment within this year.

The Seoul Administrative Court granted an injunction suspending enforcement of a KRW 29 billion value-added tax assessment issued by the Namdaemun tax office in August 2026, so enforcement is halted until the main judgment.

The timing of capital impairment clearance and the capital structure could change with the main judgment, which is the next checkpoint.

In new businesses, the company set up a dedicated R&D team for 6G and LEO satellite communication antennas and completed first-stage development of a pan-tilt active satellite-tracking antenna.

Whether these projects produce revenue large enough to offset the core business losses has not yet appeared in disclosed figures, so segment information in the 2026 annual report is the next checkpoint.

Whether the revenue timing of announced orders and the KT contract lines up with the operating loss trend is the key point to watch.

Valuation for this company can only be read alongside its small earnings base and the concentration of profit in a single quarter.

The last four quarters of net income attributable to controlling shareholders, KRW 16.2 billion, which underpin the PER calculation, include the KRW 33.1 billion profit in Q3 2026, and the other three quarters are losses in aggregate.

As a result, PER is driven heavily by one profitable quarter, and with operating losses continuing quarter after quarter, earnings strength is hard to judge from PER alone. Price-to-book is sensitive to the size of equity.

Controlling shareholders' equity was KRW 3.19 billion at end-2025, so changes in equity move the multiple considerably. Equity after Q3 2026 depends on how the convertible bond conversions and the Q3 profit are reflected, so it should be checked in the quarterly balance sheet.

Dividend-based valuation comparisons do not apply at present. Comparing multiples with listed peers has limited value given large differences in scale and earnings structure.

08

Bull factors

Structural rise in overseas sales share

The company says it holds a 44% supplier share in the AT&T 5G project, and North American sales growth is presented as the main driver of earnings improvement. If overseas orders keep rising, the revenue mix of the core business could shift.

Quarterly sales recovery and narrower losses

According to the company, Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 5.12 billion, up 69% year on year, and the operating loss was KRW 390 million, down 64% from a year earlier. The company announced standalone antenna division revenue of about KRW 4.19 billion for the quarter (announced May 18, 2026). The narrowing of losses is visible on a quarterly basis.

Improving financial structure

The company said KRW 30.1 billion of the KRW 40 billion fifth convertible bond has been converted into equity. The stock was also removed from the investment caution designation. The Seoul Administrative Court granted an injunction suspending enforcement of a KRW 29 billion tax assessment.

09

Bear factors

Continuing and widening quarterly operating losses

Operating losses have continued for four straight quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026. The Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 3.44 billion marks a swing into loss from a year earlier, and revenue has declined for two straight quarters. A widening loss alongside falling sales is at odds with the second-half turnaround the company has described.

Composition and durability of the profit swing

The KRW 33.1 billion Q3 2026 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders is far larger than the KRW 3.44 billion operating loss, pointing to a large non-operating contribution. The four-quarter total of KRW 16.2 billion also rests heavily on this single quarter. If the item is one-off, earnings could return to losses in later quarters.

Thin equity base and customer concentration

The debt-to-equity ratio was 767.4% at end-2025, and controlling shareholders' equity was only KRW 3.19 billion. North America accounted for 84% of overseas sales in Q1 2026, and overseas sales are concentrated in the AT&T project, so changes in a single customer's orders pass directly into revenue.

10

Risk factors

Tax and capital structure

The KRW 29 billion tax assessment is suspended, but uncertainty remains until the main judgment. The plan to clear capital impairment depends on selling the remaining convertible bonds and completing conversions, so delays would push back the capital benefit. An adverse main judgment would directly affect equity and cash outflows.

Customer and regional concentration

Overseas sales are about 68% of revenue, and North America accounted for 84% of overseas sales in Q1 2026. A delay in one carrier project's order timing could swing quarterly revenue sharply. Supply relationships with the three domestic carriers continue, but their share of sales is comparatively smaller.

Cost burden from new businesses

New businesses such as 6G, LEO satellite communications, and robotics-based antennas are still in development, so R&D spending could widen operating losses while the core business remains loss-making. Their revenue contribution has not yet appeared in disclosed figures. If results are delayed, financial capacity could be used up before the core business recovers.

What to watch next

  1. End of December 2026

    The company said it can fully clear capital impairment within the year, so changes in total equity and controlling shareholders' equity on the year-end balance sheet are the key checks. Progress on the sale of the remaining convertible bonds should be watched as well.

  2. End of December 2026 (timing of KT contract revenue)

    Whether revenue from the KT supply contract, which the company cited as a second-half earnings driver, is recognized and at what scale. Whether year-end sales recover after the Q3 decline will determine whether the stated target is met.

  3. After December 2026 (main judgment date not yet set)

    When the main judgment on the KRW 29 billion tax assessment is issued after the injunction, the capital structure and possible cash outflows will change. The outcome could shift the timing of clearing capital impairment.

  4. By March 31, 2027

    This is the filing deadline for the 2026 annual report, which will disclose Q4 and full-year 2026 results and the non-operating income breakdown. The composition of the KRW 33.1 billion Q3 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders and the operating loss trend can be checked together.

12

Overall view

Oneul E&M's overseas antenna sales expansion lifted 2025 revenue and narrowed its operating loss, but in Q3 2026 revenue fell for a second straight quarter and the operating loss widened to KRW 3.44 billion.

The swing to KRW 33.1 billion in net income attributable to controlling shareholders appears to be driven heavily by non-operating items, and the four-quarter total of KRW 16.2 billion also leans on that one quarter.

On the financial side, the convertible bond conversions and the suspension of the tax enforcement have eased capital concerns, but the debt-to-equity ratio of 767.4% and controlling shareholders' equity of KRW 3.19 billion at end-2025 show a still-thin equity base.

Bullish factors include AT&T-led overseas orders, growth in North American sales, and the plan to clear capital impairment within the year. Bearish factors include four straight quarters of operating losses, customer and regional concentration, the main tax lawsuit, and new-business costs.

The second-half turnaround the company described and the disclosed Q3 operating results currently point in different directions.

The next checkpoints are the capital structure at end-December 2026, the revenue timing of the KT contract, the main tax judgment, and the Q4 results and non-operating income detail in the 2026 annual report due by March 31, 2027.
