Specialty Gas Margins Recover, Quarterly Swings Remain the Test

02

Business structure

TEMC is a materials company whose core business is specialty gases used in semiconductor and display processes. WiseReport's company monitor describes it as a maker founded in 2015 that listed on KOSDAQ in 2023.

According to the innovation-data platform Innoforest, the company developed its own purification technology for xenon, krypton, CF-series gases and carbon monoxide, localizing rare gases that had previously been fully imported.

Beyond semiconductor specialty gases, its business spans semiconductor equipment, secondary-battery equipment and industrial gases. In semiconductor equipment, deliveries of chemical supply systems are the key item.

On March 30, 2026, Edaily reported that an independent research house put chemical supply-system deliveries to SK hynix at KRW 48.4 billion, expected to start in the second quarter, and also flagged equipment revenue from the first Yongin semiconductor cluster order.

NH Investment & Securities cited a stable revenue base built on domestic integrated device manufacturers and noted that supply to a U.S. semiconductor fab had begun. On product mix, the expansion of high-value products such as diborane is cited as a driver of profitability.

Foosung, a listed peer in the same chemical sector that handles fluorine-based materials, can serve as a comparison point within the industry. The revenue split by business division can be checked in the segment information of the annual report.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 351.7 billion in 2022, KRW 200.8 billion in 2023 (down 42.9% year on year), KRW 310.2 billion in 2024 (up 54.5%) and KRW 279.7 billion in 2025 (down 9.8%), making 2022 the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 53.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 21.1 billion in 2023 (down 60.4%) and KRW 19.7 billion in 2024 (down 6.7%), then rose to KRW 24.8 billion in 2025 (up 26.1%), with 2024 the smallest year in that span.

The operating margin fell from 15.1% in 2022 to 6.4% in 2024 before recovering to 8.9% in 2025, and NH Investment & Securities cited a shift in product mix as a factor behind the margin improvement.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 41.8 billion in 2022, KRW 22.7 billion in 2023 (down 45.7%), KRW 12.9 billion in 2024 (down 43.3%) and KRW 16 billion in 2025 (up 23.9%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 17.7 billion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder line. Operating cash flow was KRW 22.1 billion in 2022, KRW 63 billion in 2023, KRW 39.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 21.6 billion in 2025.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 290.7 billion, of which KRW 197.3 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 131.1 billion, and the debt ratio fell from 101.3% in 2022 to 45.1% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 82.4 billion in Q4 2025 (up 42.1% quarter on quarter) to KRW 49.5 billion in Q1 2026 (down 39.9%), then KRW 86.5 billion in Q2 and KRW 74.4 billion in Q3 2026 (down 13.9% quarter on quarter, up 28.4% year on year).

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 4.99 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 6.2 billion in Q4 2025, dropped to KRW 142 million in Q1 2026 (down 97.7%), and recovered to KRW 5.86 billion in Q2 and KRW 5.78 billion in Q3 2026 (up 15.8% year on year).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.58 billion in Q3 2026, down 58.7% year on year, moving in the opposite direction to operating profit over the same quarter. Over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, that line totaled KRW 16.1 billion.

WiseReport's first-half 2026 summary, as of September 7, 2026, showed consolidated revenue down 2.4%, operating profit down 56.0% and net income up 43.2% year on year.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from downstream customers depends heavily on the memory chip cycle. In a March 30, 2026 Edaily report, an independent research house identified the recovery in customers' NAND utilization, driven by higher memory prices, as a key variable for 2026 results.

The same report said that supply disruptions in global helium linked to the Middle East conflict could make high-purity helium production a new growth driver. Expansion of the market for dopants used in DRAM and logic chips, and structural changes in 3D DRAM, were also cited as tailwinds.

WiseReport noted that the semiconductor specialty gas segment is directly affected by chip capex and weakens when the downturn deepens.

The industry therefore moves on two axes, capex flows and memory utilization, and the bullish case (utilization recovery) and the bearish case (exposure to capex cuts) rest on the same variables.

On competition, an equity research platform's 2025 full-year comparison showed TEMC's operating margin above the chemical sector average and its debt ratio below it. Foosung, a listed chemical peer that handles fluorine-based materials, is a comparison point in specialty gases.

In semiconductor equipment, the chemical supply-system deliveries to SK hynix and the Yongin cluster orders determine how much downstream demand reaches reported results.

06

Outlook

In an early-April 2026 report covered by Daily Invest, NH Investment & Securities projected 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 309.4 billion (up 10.6% year on year) and operating profit of KRW 30.3 billion (up 22.1%).

The same analysis named profitability improvement, rather than revenue growth, as the key point for 2026, and expected operating profit to rise faster than revenue as high-value products such as diborane gained share.

Reported operating profit for Q1 through Q3 2026 was KRW 142 million, KRW 5.86 billion and KRW 5.78 billion, so the spread between quarters is wide, and reaching the full-year projection would require a sharp rise in Q4 operating profit.

In a March 2026 report, the independent research house said chemical supply-system deliveries to SK hynix worth KRW 48.4 billion would start in the second quarter, and that further full-year growth would be possible once equipment revenue from the first Yongin cluster order is recognized.

The start of supply to a U.S. semiconductor fab was also cited as widening the revenue base. High-purity helium production was described in the same period as planned for the first half of 2026, linked to a neon manufacturing process.

On the industry side, the recovery in NAND utilization and the capex trend will determine whether this outlook materializes. The next checkpoint is the annual report covering 2026, where the share of high-value products and helium in revenue and the U.S. supply results can be checked.

Bull factors

Memory utilization recovery and large customer deliveries

In a March 2026 report, an independent research house viewed the recovery in NAND utilization driven by higher memory prices positively. The same analysis put forward KRW 48.4 billion of chemical supply-system deliveries to SK hynix, starting in the second quarter of 2026. It also said equipment revenue from the first Yongin cluster order could enable further full-year growth.

Improved financial structure

The debt ratio fell from 101.3% in 2022 to 45.1% in 2025, and total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 290.7 billion. The operating margin rose from 6.4% in 2024 to 8.9% in 2025, and operating cash flow was KRW 21.6 billion in 2025. With a thicker capital base, the financial burden is smaller than before even when quarterly profit fluctuates.

High-value products and localized rare gases

NH Investment & Securities argued that a higher share of high-value products such as diborane would lift operating profit faster than revenue. High-purity helium production was described as planned for the first half of 2026, and localization of the neon manufacturing process was already cited as an operating base. If these lines gain traction in results, there is room for the operating margin to improve.

Bear factors

Large swings in quarterly profit

Operating profit fell 97.7% quarter on quarter to KRW 142 million in Q1 2026. It recovered to KRW 5.86 billion in Q2 and KRW 5.78 billion in Q3, but the spread between quarters remains wide. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 58.7% year on year to KRW 1.58 billion in Q3 2026.

Exposure to semiconductor capex

When chip capex shrinks, the specialty gas business is directly affected. The same summary pointed to this structure in connection with the first-half 2026 operating profit decline. If memory utilization turns down again, the earnings recovery could be delayed.

Gap versus full-year projections

NH Investment & Securities projected 2026 operating profit of KRW 30.3 billion, while quarterly operating profit for Q1 through Q3 2026 was KRW 142 million, KRW 5.86 billion and KRW 5.78 billion. Meeting the full-year projection would require a large increase in Q4 operating profit.

Whether profitability improvement, rather than revenue growth, shows up in reported results will become clear in the annual report.

10

Risk factors

Industry cycle and customer concentration

Earnings are closely tied to memory chip capex and utilization, and a revenue base built on domestic IDM customers means a single customer's investment cut can hit sales directly. Revenue fell 9.8% in 2025, so the revenue base is sensitive to the external environment.

How far new U.S. supply offsets changes at existing customers will need to be checked in the quarterly revenue mix.

Execution of new businesses and overseas expansion

High-purity helium production, high-value products such as diborane and supply to U.S. fabs are at different stages of plan versus execution. Depending on how fast production converts, the timing of profitability gains could slip. These items can be tracked in the business descriptions and revenue mix of the quarterly reports.

Divergence across profit lines and share price volatility

With quarterly net income attributable to controlling shareholders moving between KRW 1.58 billion and KRW 6.69 billion, earnings-based metrics can change sharply from quarter to quarter.

Operating profit rose in Q3 2026 while controlling-shareholder net income fell, which points to the need to check non-operating items and taxes. The share price can also be affected by semiconductor sector theme flows.

Overall view

TEMC is a company whose core business is specialty gases for semiconductor and display processes. Its 2025 annual revenue fell, but operating profit and operating margin recovered. The debt ratio fell from 101.3% in 2022 to 45.1% in 2025, leaving a lighter financial structure.

Quarterly results in 2026 showed wide swings: operating profit dropped to KRW 142 million in Q1 and recovered to KRW 5.86 billion in Q2 and KRW 5.78 billion in Q3. Q3 revenue rose 28.4% year on year, but net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 58.7%.

The bullish factors are the recovery in memory utilization, chemical supply-system deliveries to SK hynix and a shift toward high-value products, while the bearish factors are exposure to the chip capex cycle and large quarter-to-quarter swings in profit.

An NH Investment & Securities analysis projected higher operating profit in 2026, but reported Q1 to Q3 results show that reaching that projection depends on Q4. The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report, due by March 31, 2027, and the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
