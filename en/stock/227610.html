Profit in the First Half, Loss Again in Q3

02

Business structure

Outin Futures was founded in December 2000 and listed on KOSDAQ in July 2017. It runs two businesses: an ODM (original development manufacturing) operation covering product planning through production, and its own NEOGEN brand.

According to a report analyzing its 2024 annual filing, the cosmetics segment generated KRW 34.2 billion of the KRW 34.7 billion total, while the interior segment fell 70% to KRW 500 million.

In the same year the new-business segment produced almost no revenue while still posting operating losses, and overseas sales reached KRW 15.9 billion, or 45.8% of the total.

The company said first-quarter 2026 ODM revenue grew about 16% year on year, while brand revenue declined as preparations for overseas distribution were delayed.

NEOGEN sunscreen sold out after entering Costco's US online channel and drew repeat orders, and the company confirmed placement in 250 Rive Gauche stores in Russia as of May 2026. A Boutiqaat listing agreement in the Middle East was described as due for completion at that time.

Domestic channels include Olive Young and Kakao Gift, while overseas sales run through Amazon, Ulta and Vietnamese distributors. In China the company signed a supply agreement with a major cosmetics distributor and obtained a hygiene permit, and it is extending into disinfection masks and makeup and hair products.

Competitively, its ODM business is compared with large manufacturers such as Cosmax and Kolmar Korea, while its own brand competes with other K-beauty labels abroad.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 35 billion in 2022 to KRW 32.7 billion in 2023, a 6.7% decline, then rose to KRW 34.7 billion in 2024 (up 6.1%) and KRW 42.4 billion in 2025 (up 22.2%). Revenue grew in both 2024 and 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022–2025 period.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 9.69 billion in 2022 to an operating profit of KRW 163 million in 2023, back to an operating loss of KRW 3.37 billion in 2024, and to a narrower operating loss of KRW 1.81 billion in 2025. Operating margins were -27.7%, 0.5%, -9.7% and -4.3% for 2022 through 2025.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 17.2 billion in 2022, KRW 247 million in 2023, KRW 6.77 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.52 billion in 2025, a loss in each of the four years.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 17.3 billion, KRW 328 million, KRW 6.89 billion and KRW 3.64 billion in the same years. Operating cash flow was negative in 2022 to 2024, at -KRW 4.66 billion, -KRW 6.01 billion and -KRW 4.1 billion, before turning positive at KRW 322 million in 2025.

Quarterly results show the first quarter of 2026 at revenue of KRW 9.64 billion and an operating loss of KRW 294 million, the second quarter at KRW 12.4 billion and an operating profit of KRW 1.02 billion, and the third quarter at KRW 8.23 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.34 billion, which was a 33.4% drop from the prior quarter.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totals KRW 3.27 billion.

The company's preliminary first-half 2026 results, announced on August 14, 2026, show revenue of KRW 22 billion and operating profit of KRW 700 million, which it attributed to stable ODM sales, overseas distribution expansion and lower selling and administrative costs.

The company also said the second-quarter operating margin improved to 8.25% from -3.05% in the first quarter. The third quarter, however, combined a sales decline with a renewed operating loss, so quarter-to-quarter swings remain large.

05

Industry analysis

In cosmetics, the ODM model means manufacturing and development are performed for brand owners, so the pace at which those brands expand overseas flows directly into volume and revenue.

Reporting from late July 2026 said interest in K-beauty has made companies that run both their own brands and ODM operations attractive partners for foreign firms. The company said it is running pop-up stores and local partnerships in key markets such as the United States and Japan.

In domestic ODM competition, large manufacturers such as Cosmax and Kolmar Korea lead on production scale and customer base, placing Outin Futures among mid-sized ODM players. Cosmetics accounted for 99% of revenue in 2024, so the company is directly exposed to sector trends.

By contrast, the interior and new-business segments have posted operating losses since 2024, leaving portfolio clean-up as an open task. In China, the company is extending its range into disinfection masks and makeup and hair products on the back of a distributor supply agreement and a hygiene permit.

As overseas channels grow, channel inventory management and promotion spending can rise too, so the speed of margin recovery depends on cost control. Companies with a high overseas revenue share are also exposed to exchange rates and local distribution rules, which is a factor to weigh alongside the sector picture.

06

Outlook

The company said it will keep cost and expense controls in place in the second half of 2026 and focus on expanding overseas distribution in the United States, Russia, the Middle East, Vietnam and India.

In the US it plans to strengthen retail, social commerce and branding channels at the same time, including TikTok Shop and a pop-up store in SoHo, New York, alongside Costco. As of May 2026 the company said it was negotiating an in-store Costco placement, but whether and when that happens has not been announced.

The company confirmed placement in 250 Rive Gauche stores in Russia, and a listing agreement with Boutiqaat in the Middle East was described as due for completion. Talks with a Vietnamese distributor on large brand events and promotions have also been reported.

With Aymo Beauty in India, discussions covered developing a first flagship product for a new premium brand, with cooperation expected to extend to skincare and makeup. Reports also pointed to talks on a NEOGEN product line aimed at Walmart's market.

The company had expected visible results from overseas channels starting in the second quarter, and second-quarter operating profit did turn positive.

However, third-quarter revenue fell 33.4% from the prior quarter and the operating loss widened to KRW 1.34 billion, so how quickly overseas channel growth shows up in quarterly profit is the next checkpoint.

Whether the overseas revenue share and the reduction in selling and administrative costs hold up is another item to track.

Bull factors

First-half 2026 profit turn

The second-quarter operating margin rose to 8.25% from -3.05% in the first quarter. The company credits stable ODM sales, overseas distribution growth and lower selling and administrative costs. The first-half profit is consistent with the quarterly figures in the company's filings.

Wider overseas distribution

NEOGEN sunscreen sold out after entering Costco's US online channel and received repeat orders. Placement in 250 Rive Gauche stores in Russia has been confirmed, and a Boutiqaat agreement in the Middle East was announced as due for completion. As channels grow, the overseas sales base for the company's own brand is widening, which is a bullish factor.

ODM growth and new partnerships

The company reported that first-quarter 2026 ODM revenue rose about 16% year on year, based on its quarterly report. It has discussed a first flagship product for a new premium brand with Aymo Beauty in India, and it is also in talks with partners in Thailand and Vietnam. More client brands could support steadier ODM volumes, which is a bullish factor.

09

Bear factors

Loss returns in Q3

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 8.23 billion, down 23.3% year on year, and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.34 billion. The second-quarter profit was erased within a single quarter.

Revenue decline and the return to losses came in the same quarter, so whether the first-half profit is sustainable now needs confirmation.

Accumulated losses and balance sheet strain

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders continued in each year from 2022 to 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 39.5 billion, and the debt ratio was 214.8%. Liabilities are large relative to total equity of KRW 18.4 billion, so a prolonged loss period would reduce financial flexibility.

Brand weakness and non-core segments

Brand revenue fell year on year in the first quarter of 2026 as preparations for overseas distribution were delayed. The interior and new-business segments have posted operating losses since 2024, which constrains profitability. The later brand revenue recovers, the heavier the burden on the ODM-centered structure becomes.

Risk factors

Share price volatility and market alerts

On July 22, 2026, Korea Exchange designated the stock as an investment-caution issue after it traded on five or more of the previous 15 days under that status and rose more than 75%.

On October 22, 2026, it was designated for one day after a 17.46% gain over three days and a 5.20% buy-side involvement by a single account. Repeated alerts of this kind can bring trading restrictions and larger price swings.

Risk of renewed SG&A growth

The company said it will keep reducing selling and administrative costs in the second half of 2026. However, planned TikTok Shop activity, a SoHo pop-up in New York and large brand events with a Vietnamese distributor could push up marketing and promotion spending.

If the cost cuts cited behind the first-half profit turn are reversed by overseas channel investment, it may be hard to sustain the second-quarter level of operating margin.

China product expansion execution

The company has signed a supply agreement with a major Chinese cosmetics distributor and obtained a hygiene permit, and is widening its range to disinfection masks as well as makeup and hair products. Adding product lines can increase development, certification and channel inventory burdens.

If products fall outside the scope of the hygiene permit or local distribution rules change, planned sales could be delayed. How quickly this expansion shows up in quarterly profit has yet to be confirmed.

Overall view

Outin Futures recorded net losses attributable to controlling shareholders in every year from 2022 to 2025. Revenue in 2025 reached KRW 42.4 billion, the largest of the four years, but an operating loss of KRW 1.81 billion remained.

The first half of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 700 million on a preliminary basis announced on August 14, 2026, but the third quarter swung back to an operating loss of KRW 1.34 billion.

Bullish factors include stable ODM revenue, wider overseas distribution for NEOGEN and lower selling and administrative costs. Bearish factors include quarterly volatility, a large liability base, losses in the interior and new-business segments and price swings tied to investment-caution designations.

On valuation, the price-to-earnings ratio cannot be calculated because of the last four quarters of net losses, and the price-to-book ratio sits below book value per share.

The next checkpoints are the full-year 2026 operating margin in the annual report, whether overseas contracts and placements show up in revenue, and quarterly profit in the first quarter of 2027. This report is for information only and contains no buy or sell opinion.
