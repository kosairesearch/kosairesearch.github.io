02

Business structure

Kyungin Electronics' core business is remote controls used in consumer electronics such as TVs. According to WiseReport's company monitor, the company has grown sales as a major vendor to a U.S. TV maker, supported by rapid new-technology development and advance technology work since the pandemic.

Because its main customer is an overseas TV maker, revenue appears sensitive to overseas set production volumes and delivery schedules. The company holds core smart-device technologies including BLE communication, voice recognition and motion control.

The same source says R&D is being stepped up to mass-produce next-generation technologies such as touch sensors and Zigbee modules. R&D spending in 2025 was KRW 545 million, equal to 1.51% of revenue.

At the end of 2025, total assets were KRW 87.3 billion, total liabilities KRW 6.89 billion and total equity KRW 80.4 billion, a capital-heavy balance sheet. How concentrated the business is in remote controls alone can be checked against segment disclosures.

Competition turns on TV makers' vendor selection and the speed of technology response. LG Innotek and Samsung Electro-Mechanics share the electronics components sector but differ in product and customer structure, so they are not direct comparables.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue rising steadily while profit moved up and down. Revenue went from KRW 20.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 21.1 billion in 2023 (up 5.4% year on year), KRW 22.9 billion in 2024 (up 8.5%) and KRW 36.1 billion in 2025 (up 57.2%), rising three years in a row from 2023 through 2025.

The largest year in 2022 to 2025 was 2025, and the smallest was 2022. Operating profit rose from KRW 110 million in 2022 to KRW 216 million in 2023 (up 95.3%), then turned to an operating loss of KRW 1.27 billion in 2024, a swing into deficit. In 2025 operating profit of KRW 1.02 billion marked a return to profit.

The operating margin was 0.6% in 2022, 1.0% in 2023, -5.5% in 2024 and 2.8% in 2025. Revenue grew 57.2% in 2025, yet the margin stayed at 2.8%, so volume expansion did not translate directly into margin improvement.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.71 billion in 2022, KRW 5.78 billion in 2023 (up 113.7%), KRW 2.56 billion in 2024 (down 55.7%) and KRW 3.83 billion in 2025 (up 49.5%). The largest year was 2023 and the smallest was 2024.

In the disclosed figures, 2025 net income including non-controlling interests is also KRW 3.83 billion. Net income stayed positive in 2024 despite an operating loss, which suggests non-operating items supported the bottom line; the detail should be checked against the 2024 annual report notes.

Operating cash flow was KRW 5.67 billion in 2022, -KRW 120 million in 2023, KRW 8.53 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.73 billion in 2025, with large swings.

Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 548 million on revenue of KRW 10.4 billion in Q3 2025 fell to KRW 125 million on revenue of KRW 9.13 billion in Q4 2025 (revenue down 11.8% and operating profit down 77.1% quarter on quarter).

It recovered to KRW 619 million in Q1 2026 on revenue of KRW 11.1 billion and KRW 729 million in Q2 2026 on revenue of KRW 11.7 billion. Q3 2026 revenue fell 10.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 10.5 billion, and the quarter posted an operating loss of KRW 372 million.

The Q3 2026 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.82 billion compares with KRW 2.98 billion of profit in Q2 2026. A net loss far larger than the operating loss points to non-operating items and taxes as factors outside core operations. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 1.65 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The remote-control business follows order flow driven by set production volumes at U.S. TV makers, so TV shipment trends are the most direct upstream variable. According to WiseReport's company monitor, revenue for the first half of 2026 rose 37.2% year on year and operating profit rose 284.0%.

That growth rate is consistent with combining the disclosed Q1 and Q2 2026 results, so the recovery appears concentrated in the first half of 2026. However, the same trend was followed by an operating loss in Q3 2026, so whether the sector recovery holds must be checked quarter by quarter.

On the technology side, the shift in remote controls from simple infrared designs toward BLE, voice and motion control matters. R&D allocated to these technologies can be read as preparation for customers' replacement demand.

On competition, specialized remote-control suppliers depend on long-term vendor relationships with TV makers and on advance technology development.

LG Innotek and Samsung Electro-Mechanics mainly run larger electronic components businesses, so they are reference names within the sector classification rather than direct comparables.

Outlook

The most direct confirmed item is the share buyback resolved by the board on November 12, 2026. The amount is KRW 2.0 billion, with an expected acquisition period from November 13, 2026 through February 12, 2027.

Shinhan Investment Corp. is named as the brokerage, and the company stated the purpose as stabilizing the share price and enhancing shareholder value.

The same disclosure says that of the 258,665 treasury shares held as of January 1, 2026, 2,000 were disposed of and 256,665 were cancelled, leaving treasury shares at zero as recorded on November 12, 2026.

On the business side, mass production of BLE communication, voice recognition, motion control, touch sensors and Zigbee modules is presented as the key task for the next growth stage.

Because aggregated data show sharp first-half 2026 year-on-year gains, whether profit momentum continues after the Q3 loss is the central question for the outlook. Whether Q4 2026 returns to the Q1 and Q2 level or stays in loss will determine the full-year result.

The next periodic report, the 2026 annual report, will show whether the full-year operating margin exceeds 2.8% for 2025. The size of the non-operating items behind the Q3 net loss can be checked in the same report.

Shareholder returns alongside technology investment

Bear factors

Q3 2026 swing to loss and a wider net loss

Q3 2026 recorded an operating loss of KRW 372 million, a swing from profit a year earlier, and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.82 billion. Revenue fell 10.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 10.5 billion. The net loss far exceeds the operating loss, showing that results can swing sharply on non-operating items.

A thin operating margin

The operating margin was -5.5% in 2024 and 2.8% in 2025, staying in the low single digits. Even with revenue up 57.2% in 2025, the margin was 2.8%. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 1.65 billion, a small profit relative to revenue.

Reliance on one customer and quarterly volatility

With the main customer identified as a U.S. TV maker, changes in its set production plans can flow directly into revenue. In Q4 2025, revenue fell 11.8% and operating profit fell 77.1% quarter on quarter. Swings between quarters are large, so annual figures alone make the trend hard to read.

10

Risk factors

Technology transition cost

As remote-control functions shift toward wireless and voice, R&D costs may rise, and slower mass-production results could weigh on profit. R&D spending in 2025 was KRW 545 million, or 1.51% of revenue. It should be read against 2024 R&D of KRW 616 million, or 2.68% of revenue, to see how the ratio has changed.

Cash flow volatility

Operating cash flow was negative at KRW 120 million in 2023, then KRW 8.53 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.73 billion in 2025, a large year-to-year swing. Debt is small at the end of 2025, but if operating cash generation is not stable, the capacity for shareholder returns such as the buyback needs careful reading.

Limited free float

According to WiseReport's company monitor, the free float ratio is 32.66%. With limited tradable shares, buybacks or trades by a small number of holders can widen price swings. The foreign ownership ratio is recorded at 8.90%.

Overall view

In 2025 the company recorded revenue of KRW 36.1 billion, up 57.2%, and operating profit of KRW 1.02 billion, turning the 2024 operating loss into a profit.

Aggregated data show first-half 2026 revenue and operating profit rising sharply year on year, but Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 10.5 billion produced an operating loss of KRW 372 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.82 billion.

With the 2025 operating margin at a thin 2.8%, whether revenue recovery translates into margin improvement will be confirmed through the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report.

On the financial side, total equity of KRW 80.4 billion at the end of 2025 and a debt ratio of 8.6% indicate a light debt burden, and market capitalization sits below total equity.

Strengths include revenue growth, financial capacity and the share buyback, while weaknesses include the quarterly loss, the thin margin and customer concentration. The weight of these factors will depend on whether quarterly results return to a profit trend.

The next checkpoints are the end of the buyback period on February 12, 2027, the 2026 annual report due March 31, 2027, and the Q1 2027 quarterly report due May 15, 2027.
