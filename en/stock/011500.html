02

Business structure

Hannong Chemicals is a specialty chemicals manufacturer that listed on the KOSPI market in January 2003. As of the second quarter of 2026, product sales accounted for 95% of revenue and merchandise sales for 5%, so self-manufactured products make up most of the top line.

The company says it has built its reputation in advanced specialty chemicals through continued R&D and production innovation.

Specialty chemicals are smaller in scale than the large petrochemical producers that make bulk base materials, so sales and profit move with the business cycles of end-user industries and export prices.

New initiatives include the development of colloid materials and entry into new markets, with polymer electrolyte materials for all-solid-state batteries at the center of R&D.

All-solid-state batteries draw attention for their wider range of applications than conventional secondary batteries, but the timing of commercialization remains uncertain.

The government's next-generation secondary battery program for eco-friendly mobility runs from 2024 through 2028 with a total project cost of KRW 117.23 billion. The largest shareholder group, led by Kim Eung-sang and three others, held 46.03% as of October 2025, and treasury shares stood at 2.19%.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 239.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 212.4 billion in 2023, a decline of 11.3%, before recovering to KRW 247.5 billion in 2024, a rise of 16.5%. Sales then fell 13.3% to KRW 214.5 billion in 2025. The largest year of 2022 to 2025 was 2024 and the smallest was 2023.

Operating profit went from KRW 4.54 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 2.43 billion in 2023, a swing into deficit, and returned to a profit of KRW 6.09 billion in 2024.

Operating profit then fell 41.2% to KRW 3.58 billion in 2025, and the company attributed the decline mainly to a worsening business environment that reduced both sales and operating profit. Operating margin was 1.9% in 2022, -1.1% in 2023, 2.5% in 2024 and 1.7% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 12.4 billion in 2023, up 44.7%, then fell 63.8% to KRW 4.49 billion in 2024 and 13.9% to KRW 3.87 billion in 2025, a two-year decline from 2024 through 2025.

Profit rose in 2023 despite an operating loss, which points to a material role for non-operating items, though the breakdown is needed before drawing conclusions on the cause.

Quarterly sales rose from KRW 51.2 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 47.9 billion in the fourth quarter to KRW 59.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 72.4 billion in the second.

Operating profit moved from KRW 169 million in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 1.16 billion in the fourth to KRW 3.34 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 7.78 billion in the second.

Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 54.1 billion rose 5.7% year on year, but operating profit of KRW 341 million fell 95.6% from the prior quarter, and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 107 million, a swing into deficit.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 8.87 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 17.5 billion in 2022, KRW 28.3 billion in 2023, KRW 16.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 18.9 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The biggest variable in the specialty chemicals market is oversupply from China. Chinese petrochemical capacity expansion has pushed down export prices, and protectionist trade policy, a strong-dollar environment and trade friction have squeezed the profitability of export-dependent chemical companies.

In the first half of 2025, a slowdown in the economy, weak consumption and investment, softer private spending and delayed capital expenditure together weighed on results across major industrial segments.

In this setting, Hannong Chemicals' earnings have responded closely to end-market industrial activity and export prices.

The sharp recovery in operating profit in the first and second quarters of 2026 reads as a sign of recovery, but the slowdown in the third quarter makes it premature to place the company firmly within the cycle.

Among peers, large petrochemical and materials companies such as LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions, Lotte Chemical and Kumho Petro Chemical are far larger in scale and product range. Smaller listed peers such as SK Chemicals, LOTTE Fine Chemical and Foosung share Hannong's specialty chemicals focus.

Solid-state battery materials are tied to the technology path of the secondary battery industry, and the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy program runs from 2024 through 2028 with the aim of securing next-generation battery technology.

Commercialization speed, however, depends on whether cell makers and automakers adopt the technology, so many external variables remain.

Outlook

Quarterly volatility in 2026 has been large. Operating profit was KRW 3.34 billion in the first quarter, KRW 7.78 billion in the second and KRW 341 million in the third, reversing direction each quarter.

Fourth-quarter 2026 results will settle full-year operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders, and with the last four quarters totaling KRW 8.87 billion, one or two quarters can shift the annual figures considerably.

For polymer electrolyte materials for all-solid-state batteries, the key question is whether research yields results during the national program running from 2024 through 2028. Because commercialization timing is uncertain, the contribution to near-term earnings may be limited.

Colloid materials and new market entry are R&D-driven growth projects that will take time to change the revenue mix. Product sales made up 95% of revenue in the second quarter of 2026, so profitability in existing specialty chemicals will remain central to results for now.

Whether the Chinese oversupply and export price trend turn is the external factor that determines margins in the existing business. The metric to watch is operating margin.

The margin was 1.7% in 2025, and the level at which quarterly operating margin holds after the first quarter of 2026 will serve as a gauge for the direction of 2027 earnings.

Risk factors

Chinese oversupply and export prices

If Chinese chemical capacity keeps expanding, pressure on export prices may persist. If the weak business environment that the company cited as the main cause of the 2025 profit decline recurs, quarterly recovery could be constrained. Uncertain commercialization of solid-state battery materials

If results do not lead to commercial business by 2028, when the national program ends, expectations may not show up in earnings. Small earnings base and quarterly volatility
