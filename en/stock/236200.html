Biometric Security Leader Faces Quarterly Earnings Volatility

02

Business structure

Suprema is a security company that builds access control, time attendance and video security solutions on biometric technology such as fingerprint and face recognition. Founded in 2000, it supplies both terminal hardware and integrated security platform software.

Its flagship products are the BioStation 3 access terminal and the BioStar X integrated management platform, with the cloud-based BioStar Air also offered alongside on-premise deployments.

It has added the ViOnyx AI video analytics camera, extending its scope from access authentication to detecting abnormal situations inside facilities.

Smartphone fingerprint solutions account for the largest share of revenue, and because they are delivered through an intermediate customer, quarterly recognition varies widely, according to a Financial Consumer News report. Physical security devices serve steady annual demand from companies and public institutions.

Data centers are another key customer channel: data center sales were KRW 3.1 billion in 2025 and rose to KRW 4.7 billion in the first half of 2026. The company exports to more than 140 countries and, per its company profile, operates seven overseas branches.

In competition, an Omdia analysis from 2021 ranked Suprema first in the global biometric access control device market excluding China. It competes with many global security companies while pursuing a premium price strategy.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue went from KRW 89.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 94.6 billion in 2023 (up 5.9%), KRW 108.2 billion in 2024 (up 14.4%) and KRW 137.3 billion in 2025 (up 26.9%). Revenue has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022–2025 span.

Operating profit fell from KRW 17.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.7 billion in 2023 (down 6.7%), then rose two years in a row to KRW 23.3 billion in 2024 (up 39.8%) and KRW 32.7 billion in 2025 (up 40.6%).

Operating margin dipped from 20.0% in 2022 to 17.6% in 2023 before recovering to 21.5% in 2024 and 23.8% in 2025, so 2023 stands out as a year when revenue grew but margin fell.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 17.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 23.0 billion in 2023 (up 28.5%) and KRW 32.5 billion in 2024 (up 41.5%), then slipped 0.3% to KRW 32.4 billion in 2025.

The narrow gap between 2025 operating profit of KRW 32.7 billion and attributable net income of KRW 32.4 billion suggests non-operating items were not a large drag that year. Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed at the same amounts as attributable net income in each year.

At year-end 2025, total liabilities were KRW 28.8 billion against total equity of KRW 271.1 billion, a debt ratio of 10.6%, and operating cash flow rose from KRW 18.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 37.3 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 30.3 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 45.6 billion in Q4 2025 (up 50.3% from the prior quarter), KRW 31.1 billion in Q1 2026 (down 31.8%), KRW 40.3 billion in Q2 2026 (up 29.6%) and KRW 32.8 billion in Q3 2026 (down 18.6%).

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 6.52 billion, KRW 13 billion, KRW 5.39 billion, KRW 10.2 billion and KRW 5.67 billion over the same period, and the Q3 2026 operating margin was reported at about 17.3%. Q3 2026 turned the net result into a loss of KRW 12.6 billion from net income of KRW 10.6 billion a year earlier.

A Financial Consumer News report citing Korea Investment & Securities pointed to early raw material purchases that rose to KRW 15.7 billion in Q2 2026 from KRW 10.8 billion in Q1, a weaker won that cut revenue, and widening losses on financial assets as the main drivers. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 29.2 billion.

Industry analysis

A Financial Consumer News report citing Korea Investment & Securities says annual demand for physical security continues.

In biometric access control devices, Suprema ranked first globally excluding China with roughly 13% share in an Omdia analysis from 2021, and the company says its share is especially strong in Europe, the Middle East and Africa.

The industry cycle should be read as the interaction of steady annual demand with currency and raw material conditions. The won averaged around 1,500 per dollar in Q2 2026 and fell to the 1,400 range in Q3, which the same report cited as a factor shrinking revenue.

Lost sales in the Middle East were also named as a Q3 headwind. In competition, biometric access control faces many global security companies, and in Korea listed security peers such as S-1 share the same sector classification.

Data center security demand has emerged as a growth axis, with first-half 2026 sales of KRW 4.7 billion. The expansion into AI video security, integrated monitoring and robot security also reshapes the business map.

A Small Insight Research report from early November 2026 noted that the revenue contribution of these new businesses will only become clear once actual deployment sites and supply volumes take shape.

On the product side, it unveiled two devices slated for official launch in the first half of 2026 at Intersec 2026.

Among them, the BioStation 3 Max is an AI-based biometric authentication terminal aimed at facilities with high security needs and enterprise lobby environments. The company plans to accelerate expansion into the Middle East, Africa, the Commonwealth of Independent States, India and Asia.

Data center expansion became visible with first-half 2026 sales of KRW 4.7 billion, and how much of this feeds into full-year 2026 results is the key question. The contribution of the AI video security platform ViOnyx and BioStar X-linked projects depends on the emergence of actual deployment sites and supply volumes.

Because Q4 2026 results have not yet been disclosed in a periodic report, the next checkpoint is whether full-year revenue holds above the KRW 137.3 billion recorded in 2025. Whether Middle East sales recover and whether currency and raw material cost pressure ease are also points to track in upcoming results.

In the 2026 annual report, the next periodic filing, the key metric is whether full-year operating margin holds near the 23.8% recorded in 2025.

Bull factors

Margin recovery alongside annual growth

Operating margin moved from 20.0% in 2022 to 17.6% in 2023 and then climbed to 23.8% in 2025. Over the same span revenue grew from KRW 89.4 billion to KRW 137.3 billion, and 2025 operating profit of KRW 32.7 billion was the highest of the four years. The margin recovery arriving alongside revenue growth is cited as evidence of an improving profit structure.

Data center and AI video security expansion

Data center sales rose from KRW 2.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.1 billion in 2025 to KRW 4.7 billion in the first half of 2026, already exceeding the prior full-year figure.

The company pairs BioStar X, which combines access control and video management, with the ViOnyx AI camera to extend into person tracking and abnormal event detection. Small Insight Research, in an early November 2026 report, characterized this as an expansion built on a high-margin biometric business.

Balance sheet capacity and shareholder return targets

At year-end 2025, total liabilities were KRW 28.8 billion against total equity of KRW 271.1 billion, a debt ratio of 10.6%. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 18.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 37.3 billion in 2025.

The company has set a minimum per-share dividend target and a three-year average total shareholder return ratio of 40% or more for 2026–2028.

Swing to a loss in Q3 2026

Q3 2026 revenue rose 8.2% year on year to KRW 32.8 billion, but operating profit fell 13.1% to KRW 5.67 billion. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 10.6 billion a year earlier to a net loss of KRW 12.6 billion. The four-quarter total of KRW 29.2 billion sits below the KRW 32.4 billion recorded for full-year 2025.

Large swings in quarterly revenue

Revenue was KRW 31.1 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 40.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 32.8 billion in Q3, a pattern of sharp quarterly movement. A Financial Consumer News report noted that smartphone fingerprint sales are delivered through an intermediate customer, which produces large quarter-to-quarter variation.

Q3 revenue fell 18.6% from the prior quarter, and the same report estimated weak biometric solution sales as a cause.

Non-operating swings and currency and raw material pressure

In Q3 2026, pre-tax income turned negative even though operating profit remained positive. A brokerage analysis reported by Financial Consumer News pointed to widening losses on financial assets and weak Q3 returns on investment holdings as the main causes. A weaker won and early raw material purchases were also cited as factors that squeezed profitability.

Risk factors

Customer concentration

Smartphone fingerprint solutions account for the largest revenue share and are delivered through an intermediate customer. In this structure, a drop in volume from a single relationship can move quarterly revenue and profit sharply. Weak biometric solution sales were estimated as a cause of the Q3 2026 revenue decline.

Currency and raw material costs

A Korea Investment & Securities analysis reported by Financial Consumer News showed Q2 2026 raw material purchases rising to KRW 15.7 billion from KRW 10.8 billion in Q1. Materials secured in a high-exchange-rate environment met a falling won, pressuring revenue and profitability at the same time. Component prices for items such as ICs and PCBs, together with the exchange rate, will shape upcoming margins.

Non-operating valuation swings and new business execution

Valuation gains and losses on investment holdings are a non-operating variable that can move quarterly profit sharply. Financial Consumer News reported a KRW 6.8 billion valuation gain in the first half of 2026, followed by weak investment returns in Q3.

The data center and AI video security businesses only translate into revenue once actual deployment sites and supply volumes take shape.

Overall view

Suprema is a company that an Omdia analysis from 2021 ranked first in global biometric access control devices excluding China. On a disclosed basis, 2025 revenue of KRW 137.3 billion was above 2022, and operating margin recovered from 17.6% in 2023 to 23.8% in 2025.

Yet in Q3 2026 revenue rose year on year while operating profit fell 13.1% and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 12.6 billion.

Quarterly revenue has moved sharply, reflecting the delivery structure of smartphone fingerprint sales, currency moves and early raw material purchases. Strengths include data center sales rising to KRW 4.7 billion in the first half of 2026, a low debt ratio of 10.6%, and the 2026–2028 shareholder return targets.

Weaknesses include volatility from non-operating financial asset valuations, lost Middle East sales, and new businesses whose supply scale has yet to take shape. Valuation sits at a premium to net assets, so the pace of profit recovery is the key variable for interpreting it.

The next checkpoint is whether full-year operating margin and revenue growth continue in the 2026 annual report, the next periodic filing.
