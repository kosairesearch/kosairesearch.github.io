02

Business structure

Knowmerce is an entertainment-technology company that monetizes artist intellectual property through a fan platform, concerts, merchandise and content. It was founded in 2019, listed on KOSDAQ in November 2024, and has subsidiaries including Knowmerce Entertainment and Knowmerce Japan.

Its main business has three parts. The fan platform fromm provides the subscription-based revenue base, concerts cover overseas K-pop artist tours, and MD (merchandise) covers goods production and sales.

According to a Hana Securities report, the cumulative revenue mix in the first nine months of 2025 was concerts 44.1%, MD 31.0% and platform 24.8%. The same report ranks gross profit margins as platform, then concerts, then MD.

In practice the largest segment, concerts, drives revenue scale, while the platform lifts margins. Media reports in the first half of 2026 said the company added advertising agency services to its business purpose to connect artist IP to advertising and marketing revenue.

WiseReport's company monitor puts Knowmerce third in the fan platform market and describes its profitability as limited because competition is intensifying. The same source said the company expanded its partner agencies and reached a 91% continuous collaboration ratio. Concert earnings move with artist schedules, negotiating power with overseas venues and demand forecasting.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 42.3 billion in 2023 to KRW 68.9 billion in 2024 (up 62.9% year on year) and KRW 75.7 billion in 2025 (up 9.9%), and grew for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

Of 2023 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year, while growth slowed sharply from 62.9% in 2024 to 9.9% in 2025. Profit has been recovering from losses. Operating profit went from an operating loss of KRW 204 million in 2023 to KRW 8.18 billion in 2024, a turn to profit, and then to KRW 12.3 billion in 2025 (up 50.8%).

The operating margin moved from -0.5% in 2023 to 11.9% in 2024 and 16.3% in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from a net loss of KRW 31 billion in 2023 to KRW 9.88 billion in 2024, a turn to profit, and then to KRW 14.8 billion in 2025 (up 50.3%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 31.3 billion in 2023, KRW 9.88 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.8 billion in 2025, so it differs from the controlling-shareholder figure.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 15.9 billion in 2023 and -KRW 32.2 billion in 2024, then turned positive at KRW 763 million in 2025. Quarterly results are more volatile.

After Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 27.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.43 billion, Q4 2025 revenue fell to KRW 12.9 billion (down 53.3% quarter on quarter) and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.91 billion, a swing to loss.

In Q1 2026, revenue was KRW 18.6 billion (up 44.0% quarter on quarter) and operating profit KRW 4 billion, a turn to profit; the company attributed this to cost efficiency and a stronger recurring revenue structure on its platform.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 16.1 billion (down 13.1%) with operating profit of KRW 3.55 billion (down 11.3%). Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 22 billion (up 36.0% quarter on quarter but down 20.6% year on year) and operating profit KRW 5.01 billion (down 7.8% year on year).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 1.03 billion in Q3 2026, after KRW 4.59 billion of profit in Q2. Operating profit was positive while the controlling-shareholder result was a loss, so the breakdown of non-operating items is a point to check in the 2026 annual report.

In August 2026 the company said the first half included one-time items, including costs tied to a specific legal dispute.

Equity at end-2025 was KRW 103.7 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, total liabilities were KRW 11.7 billion, and the debt ratio fell from 19.4% in 2023 to 14.7% in 2024 and 11.3% in 2025.

Industry analysis

In Korea's entertainment sector, concerts have traditionally been seen as a less favorable business environment, yet Hana Securities said in a November 2025 report that Knowmerce was preparing for high growth despite this.

The same report said that because market expectations for the sector were high in 2025, the shortfall against those expectations weighed on results. Revenue growth slowed from 63% in 2024 to 10% in 2025, and one media outlet described the growth curve as bending sharply downward.

Q1 2026 revenue fell year on year while selling and administrative expenses rose, so the profit decline was larger than the revenue decline. The company said in 2025 that it was preparing infrastructure and strategy for China entry in step with a warmer policy climate.

On August 14, 2026, the company said China business growth, new IP and global tours were being reflected in results in sequence from Q3. Actual results after the listing fell well short of the pre-listing estimates.

A July 2026 report said that based on the 2025 annual report, revenue was 27.59% and operating profit 59.93% below the estimates. Structurally, Knowmerce differs from large agency-and-album-centered companies such as Hybe and SM Entertainment, since it weights toward an IP platform and concerts.

Overall view

Revenue rose to KRW 68.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 75.7 billion in 2025, but growth slowed from 62.9% to 9.9%. Quarterly results have changed direction often, with an operating loss in Q4 2025, a return to profit in Q1 2026 and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026.
