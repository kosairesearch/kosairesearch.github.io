Display Equipment Maker: Profit Surge in 2025, Volatility Since

02

Business structure

Dongaeltekco was founded in April 1999 and mainly manufactures and sells back-end inspection equipment for LCD and OLED panels.

Based on 2023 data, display inspection equipment accounted for 60.21% of sales, OLED manufacturing equipment for 36.61%, and new technology financing for 3.18%, with exports at 76.29% of the total.

The OLED manufacturing equipment business sits with its subsidiary Sunic System, which according to a ThinkPool report accounted for about 88% of consolidated revenue based on 2025 results.

Core inspection products include aging systems that screen out early panel defects, vision inspection systems that detect pixel defects, and Demura systems that correct brightness uniformity. Its customers include Samsung Display and LG Display, and BOE of China is also cited in press coverage as a major supplier.

The company describes itself as the leader in small OLED deposition equipment, and ThinkPool reports it also leads in the OLEDoS deposition equipment market.

Sunic System was reported to have developed 8.6-generation OLED deposition equipment first in the industry and to have won a mass-production deposition line from BOE.

The new technology financing segment runs venture capital investments, and press reports say listings of portfolio companies helped the company return to profitability in 2025. Combining inspection and deposition equipment under one group is cited as a competitive strength across OLED front-end and back-end processes.

However, a large share of consolidated revenue depends on one subsidiary, so quarterly results can swing sharply with the timing of large panel makers' capital spending.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 213.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 165.1 billion in 2023 (down 22.8% year on year), then rose to KRW 178.4 billion in 2024 (up 8.1%) and KRW 582.4 billion in 2025 (up 226.4%).

Operating profit dropped from KRW 13.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.24 billion in 2023 (down 83.0%), then recovered to KRW 14.7 billion in 2024 (up 557.0%) and KRW 113.2 billion in 2025 (up 668.7%). The operating margin moved from 6.2% in 2022 to 1.4% in 2023, 8.3% in 2024 and 19.4% in 2025.

In an early 2024 report, Eugene Investment & Securities attributed the 2023 weakness to conservative investment by LG Display, a key customer of the parent, which reduced related sales.

ThinkPool reported that 2025 growth reflected large equipment supplies by subsidiary Sunic System to BOE's 8.6-generation OLED line in Chengdu.

Net income for 2025 turned positive at KRW 96.3 billion, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 49.2 billion; the difference reflects non-controlling interests.

The debt ratio (total liabilities to total equity) rose from 44.9% in 2022 to 196.7% in 2024 before easing to 107.8% in 2025, and operating cash flow moved from -KRW 23.3 billion in 2023 and -KRW 20.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 93.9 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, revenue peaked at KRW 239 billion in Q4 2025 with operating profit of KRW 54.9 billion, then fell to KRW 40.9 billion with operating profit of KRW 4.02 billion in Q1 2026.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 55.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 991 million, and Q3 2026 revenue rose to KRW 92.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 15.4 billion, down 26.8% from a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a KRW 7.62 billion loss in Q1 2026 and then returned to profit of KRW 6.96 billion in Q2 and KRW 4.78 billion in Q3; the last four quarters total KRW 21.9 billion. Quarterly margin swings are the defining feature of the 2026 results.

05

Industry analysis

Dongaeltekco's orders are directly linked to the capital spending cycle of large panel makers. Just as the 2023 sales decline was attributed to conservative investment by LG Display, a cut in customer spending translates quickly into lower revenue. The current industry focus is 8.6-generation IT OLED investment.

A February 2026 article pointed to large 8.6-generation investment by Samsung Display and China's BOE, along with demand for high-performance panels from the spread of on-device AI devices.

That trend ties into demand for Sunic System's deposition equipment and was reported to work in the same direction for Dongaeltekco's inspection equipment orders.

On competition, the press emphasizes that Sunic System has established a position in an OLED deposition equipment market long dominated by Japanese companies. This is press interpretation, and the share claims rest largely on company descriptions rather than audited figures.

Expansion of inspection markets for metaverse OLEDoS and micro LED is also cited in coverage as a growth area. Quarterly revenue peaked in Q4 2025 and bottomed in Q1 2026. Whether the industry cycle translates into order recovery should be checked through the quarterly revenue trend.

06

Outlook

The company attributed its 2025 profit improvement to higher orders tied to investment expansion in downstream industries and to better production efficiency, as ThinkPool reported in late January 2026. A ThinkPool report from April 2026 cited an order backlog above KRW 300 billion.

How quickly that backlog converts into quarterly revenue is the starting point for judging results after Q4 2026. Q3 2026 revenue rose from the prior quarter but operating profit fell from a year earlier, so the profitability of the incremental revenue should be checked alongside the top line.

On products, R&D on deposition equipment for electroplated metal masks and next-generation OLED deposition equipment were presented as portfolio expansion paths. In inspection equipment, the OLEDoS and micro LED inspection markets are cited as growth areas.

On shareholder returns, a cash dividend decision was disclosed on January 30, 2026, and in 2024 the company resolved to cancel treasury shares equal to about 18% of issued shares. The next check point is how far the 2025 operating margin of 19.4% is sustained in the 2026 annual business report.

Bull factors

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 113.2 billion, up 668.7% year on year, and the operating margin rose from 8.3% in 2024 to 19.4%. Operating profit has grown for two consecutive years since the 2023 trough. Operating cash flow turned to KRW 93.9 billion in 2025, which supports the earnings with cash.

Order Backlog and 8.6-Generation Investment

ThinkPool cited an order backlog above KRW 300 billion in an April 2026 report. Sunic System's supply to BOE's 8.6-generation OLED line was reflected in 2025 results, and expanded 8.6-generation IT OLED investment by Samsung Display and BOE was cited in a February 2026 article. How far this demand converts into quarterly revenue is the point that will test the bullish case.

Balance Sheet Repair and Shareholder Returns

The debt ratio fell from 196.7% in 2024 to 107.8% in 2025. In 2024 the company resolved to cancel treasury shares equal to about 18% of issued shares, and on January 30, 2026 it decided a cash dividend. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 241.9 billion.

Bear factors

Sharp Swings in Quarterly Profit

Operating profit fell from KRW 54.9 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 4.02 billion in Q1 2026 and dropped to KRW 991 million in Q2. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 15.4 billion was 26.8% below the year-earlier level.

The last four quarters total KRW 21.9 billion in net income attributable to controlling shareholders, which shows how unevenly profit has been contributed across quarters.

Customer and Regional Concentration

The 2023 sales decline was attributed to conservative investment by LG Display. Exports were 76.29% of sales in 2023, so a cut in a major customer's spending feeds directly into lower revenue.

Press coverage says 2025 results reflected supply to BOE's line in China, which points to high customer and regional concentration.

Past Losses and Debt Burden

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 6.54 billion in 2023 and KRW 29.8 billion in 2024 were accompanied by negative operating cash flow. The debt ratio stood at 196.7% in 2024, and total liabilities of KRW 260.7 billion at the end of 2025 exceed total equity of KRW 241.9 billion. Whether 2025 profitability continues will be confirmed in upcoming periodic reports.

Risk factors

Trading and Market Risk

The Korea Exchange designated Dongaeltekco as an investment caution stock on November 16, 2026, citing concentrated trading by a small number of accounts. The filing noted a 17.14% share price gain over three days and a 49.77% buy-side participation rate for the top ten accounts. The designation reflects trading concentration and signals an environment where price swings can widen.

Customer Capex Cycle Risk

Much of revenue is tied to large panel makers' capital spending, so a pause in investment can sharply reduce quarterly results. Q1 2026 revenue falling 82.9% from the prior quarter illustrates how this structure plays out.

When revenue recognition timing differs from quarter to quarter, the range of possible results widens even as order disclosures increase.

Financial and Business Concentration Risk

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 of KRW 260.7 billion exceed total equity of KRW 241.9 billion. A large share of consolidated revenue depends on subsidiary Sunic System, so delays or adjustments in its orders flow straight into consolidated results. This concentration adds to earnings volatility.

Overall view

Dongaeltekco is a company whose earnings profile changed sharply after its 2023 trough of KRW 2.24 billion in operating profit. Revenue grew to KRW 582.4 billion and operating profit to KRW 113.2 billion in 2025, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 49.2 billion.

However, after peaking in Q4 2025, operating profit fell to KRW 4.02 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 991 million in Q2, and recovered to KRW 15.4 billion in Q3, which was still 26.8% below a year earlier.

Bullish factors include the 2025 earnings scale, an order backlog above KRW 300 billion, expanding 8.6-generation OLED investment, a lower debt ratio and shareholder returns.

Bearish factors include large quarterly swings, concentration on large panel makers and BOE, the loss and negative cash flow history of 2023–2024, and the investment caution designation on November 16, 2026.

Valuation sits low relative to net assets, but multiple interpretations diverge by earnings basis until the durability of profit is confirmed. The next checkpoints are the 2026 preliminary annual results, the operating margin in the 2026 annual business report, and quarterly profit in Q1 2027.
