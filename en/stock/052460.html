02

Business structure

iCRAFTCo was founded in 2000 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2005 as an IT and software company. It began with internet core network construction and now also provides AI infrastructure build-outs, network solutions and cloud services.

According to the company profile, its main areas are design and construction of AI data centers on GPU computing platforms, construction of internet core and access networks for telecom carriers, and cloud transition services.

The business appears to split into network construction for telecom operators and equipment supply and build-out for customers' AI infrastructure. The company attributed its 2025 improvement to higher revenue from customers' expanding AI infrastructure investment and to cost reduction.

The 2026 supply contracts cover NVIDIA Spectrum-X and Infiniband switch lines, and the July 2026 contract with Ceylab and the November 2026 contract with NAVER Cloud were both recorded as outsourced production.

The structure therefore reads as a combination of procurement, design and construction capability rather than in-house manufacturing. The counterparties disclosed in 2026 are NAVER Cloud and two other domestic companies, so revenue depends heavily on a few large customers.

Compared with large SI firms in the same IT and software sector, such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS, the scope of work is narrower and project-based revenue weighs more. As of July 31, 2026, the largest shareholder and related parties held 21.10%.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 112.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 138.7 billion in 2023, up 22.8%, fell to KRW 100.5 billion in 2024, down 27.5%, and then reached KRW 206.5 billion in 2025, up 105.5%, making 2025 the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 247 million in 2022, KRW 5.15 billion in 2023 (up 1,983.2%), KRW 1.6 billion in 2024 (down 69.0%) and KRW 8.01 billion in 2025 (up 401.4%).

The operating margin was 0.2% in 2022, 3.7% in 2023, 1.6% in 2024 and 3.9% in 2025, so revenue more than doubled in 2025 while margins stayed in the mid-to-high single digits.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 505 million in 2022, KRW 4.56 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 926 million in 2024 that turned the year into a loss, and KRW 10.1 billion in 2025, a swing back to profit.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 10.1 billion in 2025, similar to the controlling-shareholder figure.

The quarterly pattern shows that Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 109.1 billion, up 293.4% from the prior quarter, exceeded half of full-year revenue, and Q4 2025 operating profit of KRW 11.4 billion was larger than the full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 8.01 billion.

Against Q3 2025, when revenue was KRW 27.7 billion and the operating loss was KRW 1.05 billion, the annual result was concentrated in the fourth quarter. Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 16.3 billion, down 85.0% from the prior quarter, and the operating loss was KRW 2.5 billion, a swing back to loss.

Q2 2026 revenue recovered to KRW 40 billion, up 145.0%, but the operating loss of KRW 291 million and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.73 billion continued.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 36.6 billion, up 32.0% year on year, the operating loss narrowed to KRW 695 million, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to KRW 2.6 billion.

Over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 7.12 billion, and KRW 8.19 billion of that came from Q4 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 improvement to rising AI infrastructure investment by customers and cost reduction, while one analysis tied the weak first half of 2026 to telecom operators concentrating network investment in the second half.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 168.4% in 2022 to 80.4% in 2024 and then rose to 104.8% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 46.8 billion, of which KRW 46.5 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 49 billion.

Industry analysis

Domestic AI infrastructure investment is lifting demand for network equipment and build-outs at the same time as data center expansion and securing of GPU computing resources proceed.

A company overview comment on the Kiwoom-style monitoring site, Company Monitor, said AI infrastructure investment in 2026 would increase data center demand and that AI, cloud and digital transformation would drive growth in the IT services market.

This is an industry outlook and does not guarantee the order size of any single company. In cycle terms, the company's revenue flattened in 2026 after customer investment concentrated in Q4 2025.

Cumulative revenue for the first nine months of 2026 was KRW 92.9 billion, down 4.5% year on year, based on preliminary results disclosed in 2026. Telecom operators tend to concentrate network investment in the second half, so quarterly revenue can be structurally uneven.

Compared with large SI companies in the same IT and software sector, the company is more specialized in internet core networks and AI data center equipment build-outs.

However, because the contracts involve NVIDIA-line switch supply, unit prices and margins may depend on the original manufacturer's pricing policy and procurement terms.

Combining overseas equipment supply with domestic construction also means revenue timing can shift with customer investment schedules and equipment arrival dates.

Even in a strong market, the company relies on project-based awards, so bidding competition and the pace of client budget execution are key variables for quarterly results.

Outlook

The contract signed on July 20, 2026 is a NIPA NVIDIA Spectrum-X supply deal worth KRW 32.35 billion with Ceylab, running from July 20, 2026 to December 31, 2031, under outsourced production. Its payment terms specify a 50% advance, 30% interim payment and 20% balance.

The contract with NAVER Cloud disclosed on November 16, 2026 covers AI Factory Spectrum-X and Infiniband switch supply, carries a contract value of KRW 209.1 billion and equals 101.26% of 2025 consolidated revenue.

It runs from November 13, 2026 to December 31, 2027, and payment is made in five installments after inspection within one month of each delivery. This contract has no down payment or advance, so the cash burden before delivery is comparatively small.

The contract may be transferred to a company designated by the counterparty, and the company said it would file a correction disclosure once a succession contract is confirmed.

Because the three contracts together exceed 2025 revenue, how their revenue is recognized in quarterly results from Q4 2026 onward is the central checkpoint. Cancelling 1 million treasury shares reduces total shares issued to 13,607,936.

The 2026 annual report must be filed by the end of March 2027, within 90 days of fiscal year-end. The operating margin and the scale of large-contract revenue recognized in that report are the next points to verify.

Overall view

iCRAFTCo began with internet core network construction and is shifting its center of gravity toward AI data center equipment supply and build-outs.

In 2025 it reported revenue of KRW 206.5 billion, operating profit of KRW 8.01 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 10.1 billion, a swing to profit for the year. Profit was concentrated in Q4 2025, however, and operating losses continued from Q1 through Q3 2026.

The three supply contracts disclosed since July 2026, and especially the KRW 209.1 billion contract disclosed on November 16, 2026, widen the revenue base.

On the other side, the counterparties are concentrated among a few customers, the contracting entity could change, and the outsourced structure may add working capital burden. Operating cash flow changed sign from year to year, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 104.8% in 2025, and both points deserve equal weight.

The share price has risen sharply in 2026, and a caution designation was announced. The next checkpoints are the preliminary 2026 full-year results in early February 2027 and the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027.

If those reports show how much large-contract revenue is recognized and what the operating margin is, the substance of the business shift will become clearer.
