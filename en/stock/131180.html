Report overview

Operating profit recovers, but Q3 2026 turns to net loss

Dilli Illustrate is a KOSDAQ-listed maker of industrial digital inkjet printers that sells mostly overseas. Its operating profit rose sharply through the first half of 2026, but the third quarter of 2026 turned to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 80.55 million.

02

Business structure

Dilli Illustrate was listed on KOSDAQ on January 31, 2011 as a computer and peripheral device maker, and it develops, manufactures and sells industrial digital inkjet printers.

A company profile describes its digital UV inkjet printers as top-tier inkjet technology and presents its proprietary inkjet core technology and development experience as the basis of the business. Its flagship machine is the NEO GALAXY, a high-speed, eco-friendly water-based digital perfecting press.

The lineup also includes the NEO PICASSO PLUS, a seven-color UV inkjet label press, an automatic bookbinding unit (ABM), and a hybrid UV printer called NEO EUS. A 3.2-meter hybrid UV printer combined with an automatic board output system forms an automated sheet printing line.

Target applications include labels, flexible packaging and packaging, with the emphasis on machines that make complex and hazardous print processes easier and safer. Most revenue comes from overseas. Exports made up about 88% of KRW 38.2 billion in 2024 revenue, and the main markets are the United States and Europe.

The company says it is pursuing a customized in-house sales channel with European partners. Its machines hold UL (United States), TUV (Germany) and EU RoHS certifications, which the company cites as a design-stage differentiator.

Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek, which sit in the same electronics and components sector classification, are component suppliers and have a different business structure from industrial inkjet printing.

The largest shareholder and related parties hold 37.49% according to the latest filing, with the last change recorded on June 10, 2024.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell 30.9% from KRW 37.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 25.7 billion in 2023, rose 48.7% to KRW 38.2 billion in 2024, and then fell 15.5% to KRW 32.3 billion in 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit moved from KRW 2.28 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 891 million in 2023, which marked a swing into loss, and then returned to a profit of KRW 2.31 billion in 2024. Operating profit fell 65.2% to KRW 803 million in 2025.

The operating margin was 6.1% in 2022, -3.5% in 2023, 6.0% in 2024 and 2.5% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.62 billion in 2022, KRW 684 million in 2023 (down 73.9%), KRW 4.81 billion in 2024 (up 603.0%) and KRW 1.85 billion in 2025 (down 61.6%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 688 million in 2023, KRW 4.81 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.85 billion in 2025, a slightly different figure from the controlling-shareholder line.

A company profile summary attributed the 2024 third-quarter swing to profit to strong sales of new products, and summarized the first nine months of 2025 as revenue down 19.6% and operating profit down 68.2%, citing global uncertainty.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 7.08 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 10.1 billion in Q4 2025 (up 42.3% quarter on quarter), KRW 8.3 billion in Q1 2026 (down 17.5%), KRW 11.1 billion in Q2 2026 (up 33.3%) and KRW 10.5 billion in Q3 2026 (down 5.5%).

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 176 million in Q3 2025 and KRW 118 million in Q4 2025 (down 33.1%) to KRW 442 million in Q1 2026 and KRW 1.2 billion in Q2 2026, before KRW 979 million in Q3 2026 (down 18.2%).

Quarterly net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 416 million in Q3 2025 and KRW 826 million in Q4 2025 to KRW 1.18 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 1.35 billion in Q2 2026, then turned to a net loss of KRW 80.55 million in Q3 2026.

Because operating profit and net profit moved in opposite directions in Q3 2026, how non-operating items and income tax expense were recorded is a point to confirm in the 2026 annual report.

On the cash side, operating cash flow was KRW 1.29 billion in 2022, KRW 2.32 billion in 2023 and KRW 1.79 billion in 2024, then turned to -KRW 3.69 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

In the industrial digital printing market where Dilli Illustrate operates, demand for label, flexible packaging and packaging printing forms the core. The company says it is strengthening development of eco-friendly water-based ink solutions in response to tightening environmental regulation and ESG trends.

At K-PRINT 2026, the company emphasized machines that maintain productivity regardless of operator skill, and machines that automate the process from printing through drying and cutting. The company explained that this fits demand for reducing dependence on manual labor on the shop floor.

The company also sees rising demand for eco-friendly, high-precision productive equipment in the European print market. On competition, Dilli Illustrate presents its proprietary inkjet head technology and international certifications such as UL and TUV as differentiators.

The company said brand trust and compliance with technical standards are central to purchasing decisions in North America and Europe.

Samsung Electro-Mechanics, LG Innotek and LG Display, which share the same electronics and components sector classification, are component and display suppliers, so they are sector neighbors rather than direct competitors.

Revenue fell from KRW 37.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 25.7 billion in 2023, recovered to KRW 38.2 billion in 2024, and fell again to KRW 32.3 billion in 2025, so swings in export equipment demand show up directly in the revenue trend.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 10.5 billion, up year on year, could be read as a sign of demand recovery, but one quarter of growth is not enough to call the direction of the industry cycle.

Export base and product lineup

Exports made up about 88% of 2024 revenue, with the United States and Europe as the main markets. The company showed label and water-based printing machines, including the NEO GALAXY and NEO PICASSO PLUS, at K-PRINT 2026 in August 2026. The European partner channel the company is pursuing could widen its sales routes.

Balance sheet headroom

Bear factors

Q3 2026 swing to net loss

Operating profit in Q3 2026 was KRW 979 million, but net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 80.55 million. Operating profit in the same quarter fell 18.2% from the prior quarter.

Because operating profit and net profit moved in opposite directions, what happened below the operating line is the key point of the bearish case.

Weaker 2025 earnings power and cash flow

Full-year 2025 operating profit fell 65.2% to KRW 803 million, and revenue fell 15.5%. Operating cash flow moved from KRW 1.79 billion in 2024 to -KRW 3.69 billion in 2025. On an annual basis, the quarterly recovery seen in 2026 is not yet reflected.

Export dependence and revenue swings

Revenue fell 30.9% to KRW 25.7 billion in 2023, recovered to KRW 38.2 billion in 2024, and fell again to KRW 32.3 billion in 2025. With about 88% of revenue from exports, the company is directly exposed to overseas demand. Concentration in the United States and Europe can amplify revenue swings if demand slows in either region.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

The operating margin moved from -3.5% in 2023 to 6.0% in 2024 and 2.5% in 2025. Quarterly net income also turned to a loss in Q3 2026. With earnings swinging this widely, a trend cannot be judged from one or two quarters.

Reliance on overseas sales channels

Building a sales channel around European partners ties revenue to overseas partners. Whether that channel translates into revenue is a question for future periodic reports. If partner sales lag in the United States or Europe, the pace of revenue recovery could slow.

Share structure and trading conditions

Trading in ordinary shares was suspended from April 24, 2026 and resumed on May 18, 2026 after the share consolidation. The stake held by the largest shareholder and related parties is a factor that reduces the shares available for trading. With a large holder share, the tradable float is limited and trading can be more volatile.

Overall view

Dilli Illustrate is a KOSDAQ-listed company that mainly sells industrial digital inkjet printers overseas. Annual revenue moved between KRW 37.2 billion in 2022 and KRW 32.3 billion in 2025, and operating profit fell to KRW 803 million in 2025, weakening earnings power.

Quarterly operating profit recovered in 2026 to KRW 442 million in Q1, KRW 1.2 billion in Q2 and KRW 979 million in Q3. However, Q3 2026 swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 80.55 million.
