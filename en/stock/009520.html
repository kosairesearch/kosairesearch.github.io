02

Business structure

POSCO M-Tech is a steel and metals company whose core businesses are steel product packaging and steel raw materials. By segment, 2025 sales were steel product packaging 51.2%, steel raw materials 37.4%, consignment operations 9.9% and engineering 1.5%.

The packaging business runs packaging lines at the Pohang and Gwangyang steelworks, and the company's 2022 sustainability report cites 46 packaging lines in total. Packaging also covers packaging equipment sales and packaging automation technology.

The raw materials business centers on aluminum deoxidizers and supplies raw products such as aluminum ingots and pellets to steelmakers. Its sales move with LME aluminum prices, exchange rates and the premium rate negotiated with POSCO.

Since 2012 the company has also operated POSCO's ferromanganese plant on consignment. Because the customer base leans heavily on POSCO Group steelmakers, packaging sales depend on POSCO's sales flow and orders.

One analysis says the packaging business is growing its volume by adding some competitive bid orders to existing POSCO supply. The raw materials share was 37.7% in 2024 and 37.4% in 2025, staying near 40%.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on disclosed results, annual sales were KRW 342.2 billion in 2022, KRW 341.4 billion in 2023 (down 0.2% year on year), KRW 346.6 billion in 2024 (up 1.5%) and KRW 357.4 billion in 2025 (up 3.1%). Sales rose for two consecutive years from the 2023 low, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit moved the other way, falling from KRW 6.36 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.49 billion in 2023 (down 29.4%), KRW 1.42 billion in 2024 (down 68.3%) and KRW 1.03 billion in 2025 (down 27.3%). It has declined for three consecutive years since 2023.

The operating margin slid from 1.9% in 2022 to 1.3%, 0.4% and 0.3%, so sales grew on a thinner margin. Net profit attributable to owners was KRW 4.83 billion in 2022, KRW 4.87 billion in 2023 (up 0.9%), KRW 568 million in 2024 (down 88.3%) and KRW 1.41 billion in 2025 (up 148.7%).

Total net profit including non-controlling interests was the same as the owners' figure in each year. A cumulative review of the first three quarters of 2025 cited weaker steel demand, US tariff policy, a construction slump, lower deoxidizer shipments and higher raw material costs as pressures.

Quarterly sales were KRW 82.9 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 98.9 billion in Q4 2025 (up 19.3% quarter on quarter), KRW 103.3 billion in Q1 2026 (up 4.5%), KRW 107.2 billion in Q2 2026 (up 3.7%) and KRW 107.8 billion in Q3 2026 (up 0.6% quarter on quarter and 30.1% year on year). Sales have risen for four straight quarters.

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 1.74 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 3.34 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 2.48 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 9.67 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 2.52 billion in Q3 2026. The Q2 2026 jump was large, so whether it was one-off will be checked against the notes in the next periodic report.

Net profit attributable to owners peaked at KRW 7.73 billion in Q2 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 18.4 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 1.56 billion in 2025, turning positive from KRW -7.69 billion in 2024, after KRW 17.2 billion in 2023.

The debt ratio moved modestly from 39.4% in 2022 to 35.8% in 2025, with equity of KRW 114.3 billion and total liabilities of KRW 40.9 billion at end-2025.

05

Industry analysis

The packaging business is tied to steel output and orders from the POSCO Group. A 2025 report from the Korea Investor Relations Council's research center judged that product packaging sales had stalled, staying near KRW 180 billion for three years.

Packaging equipment sales peaked at KRW 28.4 billion in 2023 but dropped sharply in 2024 as steel market weakness left a gap in orders. The same report saw further POSCO Group orders as still a valid expectation.

The raw materials business centers on aluminum deoxidizers, and the report said LME aluminum prices and a persistently strong exchange rate were lifting sales. LME aluminum averaged about USD 2,438 in 2024.

Higher raw material prices lift sales but can also raise cost pressure, so the margin direction needs separate attention. The POSCO Group context also matters. Its 2025 consolidated operating profit was KRW 1.827 trillion, down 15.7%, as weak steel and battery materials markets hit earnings.

The group has said it will raise KRW 2.8 trillion through asset sales by 2028. Unlike Hyundai Steel Company and Dongkuk Steel Mill, which are centered on steelmaking capacity, this company focuses on packaging and raw material supply services.

Its results are therefore more sensitive to group orders and raw material prices than to the steel production cycle alone.

06

Outlook

Recent momentum is positive at the quarterly level. Sales have risen for four straight quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, and operating profit was positive in each quarter of 2026 so far. Because Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 9.67 billion was strong, whether that level can recur is the key check.

Q3 2026 sales rose 30.1% year on year but only 0.6% quarter on quarter, so growth slowed. In raw materials, LME aluminum prices, the exchange rate and the outcome of fee negotiations with POSCO determine results. In packaging, a recovery in POSCO Group orders and competitive bid wins set the volume.

Group-level recovery is another variable. POSCO Group has emphasized a recovery in steel and battery materials and has said asset sales will ease its financial burden.

A revival in group demand could lift packaging and raw material volumes together, but a change in group ordering structure could also shift how volumes are allocated. The company reported exports of KRW 10.4 billion in 2025, and that share remains small.

To move beyond the 0.3% operating margin of 2025, quarterly profitability must persist across several years. The next annual report will show how far the 2026 operating margin exceeded 2025's 0.3%. Whether the Q2 2026 profit was one-off and whether Q3 profit holds in Q4 will show how durable the profit run is.

The annual business report is due by the end of March 2027, and the first-half report is due in mid-August 2027.

Bull factors

Continuity in quarterly results

Raw materials and cost conditions

The raw materials share stayed at 37.7% in 2024 and 37.4% in 2025. A Korea Investor Relations Council report saw LME aluminum prices and a strong exchange rate as factors lifting raw material sales. Operating cash flow turning positive to KRW 1.56 billion in 2025 can be read as linked to the raw materials recovery.

Group turnaround and order expectations

POSCO Group's 2025 consolidated operating profit fell 15.7% to KRW 1.827 trillion, but the group is emphasizing a turnaround in 2026. It has also set out a plan to raise KRW 2.8 trillion through asset sales by 2028. The Korea Investor Relations Council report saw further POSCO Group orders as a still-valid expectation.

Bear factors

Falling annual operating margin

Operating profit has declined for three consecutive years since 2023 to KRW 1.03 billion in 2025, and the operating margin fell from 1.9% in 2022 to 0.3% in 2025. Sales rose in 2024 and 2025, yet margins kept thinning. Net profit attributable to owners fell 88.3% to KRW 568 million in 2024.

Volatile quarterly earnings

Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 9.67 billion was far higher than KRW 2.48 billion in Q1 and KRW 2.52 billion in Q3. Whether that surge was one-off or recurring has yet to be confirmed.

With large quarterly swings, the annual trend is hard to read, and the KRW 18.4 billion owners' net profit over the last four quarters leans heavily on a single quarter.

Stalled packaging business

One analysis says product packaging sales have stalled, staying near KRW 180 billion for three years. Packaging equipment sales hit a record KRW 28.4 billion in 2023 but fell sharply in 2024 on an order gap.

On a cumulative basis through Q3 2025, lower deoxidizer shipments and the construction slump weighed on profitability.

Risk factors

Customer concentration and fee negotiations

Both the packaging and raw materials businesses depend heavily on transactions with the POSCO Group. Raw material results move with the premium rate set through negotiation with POSCO. The 2024 drop in packaging sales shows how weaker group sales can cut packaging volume.

Raw material prices and exchange rates

Raw material sales are sensitive to LME aluminum prices and exchange rates. Higher prices and a stronger exchange rate lift sales but can also raise cost pressure. LME aluminum averaged about USD 2,438 in 2024, and larger price swings widen quarterly profit volatility.

Steel demand and trade conditions

Cumulative results through Q3 2025 cited US tariff policy and the construction slump as weighing on steel demand. Lower steel demand reduces deoxidizer and packaging volumes together. As POSCO Group pursues asset sales and restructuring, its ordering structure could also change.

Overall view

POSCO M-Tech's sales have risen for four straight quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, and operating profit was positive in each quarter of 2026 so far, so the quarterly recovery is clear.

However, operating profit has declined for three consecutive years since 2023 to KRW 1.03 billion in 2025, with a 0.3% operating margin, so annual earnings power is still thin. LME aluminum prices, the exchange rate and fee negotiations with POSCO are the major variables for raw materials.

Packaging equipment sales hit a record in 2023 and fell sharply in 2024, showing sensitivity to group orders. Whether the Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 9.67 billion was one-off or recurring is the most important check.

Valuation reads differently depending on the durability of earnings, viewed together with the premium over net assets and the KRW 18.4 billion owners' net profit over the last four quarters.

The strengths of continued quarterly profit and group turnaround and the weaknesses of thinning annual margins and customer concentration carry equal weight. The next checks are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and Q1 2027 results.
