Cosmetic Glass ODM: Quarterly Profit Growth Continues

02

Business structure

SMCG, founded in 1998, is a cosmetic glass-container ODM maker that listed on KOSDAQ in March 2025 through a SPAC merger. Its offering runs from container design and post-processing, such as coating and printing, to auxiliary parts like pumps and caps.

Products fall into colored glass containers, clear glass containers and auxiliary parts, which accounted for 63.4%, 12.4% and 24.2% of 2024 revenue, respectively.

In the first half of 2026, colored containers generated KRW 17.6 billion, auxiliary parts KRW 9.3 billion and clear containers KRW 5.5 billion, so colored containers remain the largest line.

Based on 2025 data, colored glass containers were 82.30% domestic and 17.70% exported, clear containers 93.51% domestic and 6.49% exported, and auxiliary parts 76.52% domestic and 23.48% exported.

Customers include L'Oréal, Amorepacific and Johnson & Johnson as well as several K-beauty and indie brands, with about 300 major and indie brands cited in total. In 2024 the top five customers by revenue included several K-beauty brands alongside Amorepacific, L'Oréal and Johnson & Johnson.

The company says it replaces about 70% of its raw material with recycled glass and holds GRS certification, and it operates automated production lines with roughly 800 molds. One press report describes its 50-ton electric melting furnace as the only one of its size in the industry. A securities firm estimated that fixed costs were about 80% of the cost base in 2024.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 37.4 billion in 2023, KRW 54.6 billion in 2024 (up 46.0%) and KRW 55.8 billion in 2025 (up 2.2%). Operating profit was KRW 2.75 billion in 2023, KRW 4.45 billion in 2024 (up 61.7%) and KRW 4.94 billion in 2025 (up 11.2%), rising two years in a row from 2024 through 2025.

The operating margin moved gradually higher, from 7.4% in 2023 to 8.1% in 2024 and 8.9% in 2025. A Korea Investment & Securities report on second-quarter 2025 results attributed the sharp slowdown in 2025 growth partly to two top customers that were redesigning their products, which left their contribution minimal.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 371 million in 2023 to a net loss of KRW 2.06 billion in 2024 and a profit of KRW 1.33 billion in 2025, while net profit including non-controlling interests showed the same values for each year.

The gap between higher operating profit and the 2024 net loss can be traced to items below operating profit, such as non-operating gains and losses and income tax, which the full statements should be checked against.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 12.7 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 13.8 billion in Q4 2025 (up 8.3% quarter on quarter), KRW 15 billion in Q1 2026 (up 9.2%), KRW 17.3 billion in Q2 2026 (up 15.2%) and KRW 17.4 billion in Q3 2026 (up 0.3%), four straight quarters of growth.

Operating profit rose to KRW 1.66 billion in Q2 2026, a 22.3% quarter-on-quarter gain that was the largest among the recent quarters, before reaching KRW 1.72 billion in Q3 2026 (up 3.7%).

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 1.49 billion in Q3 2026, up 120.4% year on year, and totaled KRW 4.74 billion over the last four quarters.

A News Pim report described first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 32.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.0 billion as the best half-year results on record, which the company attributed to higher-value products and production efficiency.

Operating cash flow was negative KRW 1.92 billion in 2023, positive KRW 7.68 billion in 2024 and negative KRW 1.68 billion in 2025, so whether profit gains convert into cash is a point to check in the next annual report.

Debt-to-equity fell from 743.3% in 2023 to 70.9% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 29.5 billion against equity of KRW 41.7 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders.

05

Industry analysis

Under the market classification, SMCG sits in the KOSDAQ non-metallic materials group, but its operations are cosmetic glass-container ODM work within the auxiliary-materials segment of the cosmetics value chain. Several analyses point to K-beauty growth and an eco-friendly trend as the main sources of demand.

An investment information site summarized expected global cosmetics market growth at 6.1% a year. In Korea, a leading glass-container maker was sold to a private equity fund in 2022 and its business weakened, and securities analysts argued that SMCG absorbed the resulting gap in supply.

According to the company, its global revenue share rose from 21.8% in Q1 2025 to 32.6% in Q1 2026, which it attributes to deeper ties with major customers and wider reach among European and North American indie brands.

In domestic competition, the company emphasizes large-scale production on its electric melting furnaces and its pre-mold product range. A high recycled-glass share is also cited as lowering carbon-allowance burden.

On the other side, glass manufacturing is capital and fixed-cost heavy, so profits are sensitive to utilization and order flow. Expansion into heavy-weight perfume bottles and color cosmetic containers, markets with higher entry barriers, is shifting the product mix within that structure.

06

Outlook

The company said it will invest in production-innovation equipment and expand global partnerships in the second half of 2026 to strengthen cost competitiveness and its overseas position.

Reports said a multi-production system and vision inspection equipment were installed on its fifth line in the first half of 2026 and are in operation.

Press coverage also said that once facility investments scheduled for this year are completed, growth should continue in higher-value lines such as heavy perfume bottles and color cosmetic containers. The company says expansion into these categories is diversifying its customer base.

It also plans to use the North American distribution network of the PKG Group to win new customers and overseas business. A consensus of securities-firm estimates shows 2026 earnings rising substantially from the 2025 level.

Over the longer term, patent applications for parting-line sculpted glass containers in China, Europe and the United States are part of its global expansion plan. Because Q3 2026 revenue rose only 0.3% quarter on quarter, the question is whether volume recovers after the second-half equipment investment.

The annual report for 2026 will show full-year revenue and the fourth-quarter operating margin, the next checkpoints for the outlook.

Bull factors

Consecutive quarterly improvement

Revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders each rose for four straight quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026. Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 1.72 billion, up 57.0% year on year, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.49 billion, up 120.4%. The 2025 figure of KRW 1.33 billion moved the company from a 2024 net loss back into profit.

Wider overseas customer base

According to the company, its global revenue share rose from 21.8% in Q1 2025 to 32.6% in Q1 2026. Deeper ties with major customers such as L'Oréal and Johnson & Johnson, along with wider supply to European and North American indie brands, were cited as the drivers. Based on 2025 data, exports were 23.48% of auxiliary parts sales and 17.70% of colored container sales.

Fixed-cost leverage and equipment

The electric melting furnace system carries high fixed costs, so profit improves sharply as revenue rises. The company installed a multi-production system and vision inspection equipment on its fifth line in the first half and plans further production-innovation investment in the second half.

First-half 2026 revenue of KRW 32.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.0 billion were reported as the best half-year results on record.

Bear factors

Customer concentration and redesign risk

A Korea Investment & Securities report noted that the top two customers accounted for more than 40% of sales in 2024. In 2025 both were redesigning products, which limited their contribution to sales. Quarterly results can swing widely with the order flow of a few large customers.

Slower growth and cash flow

Revenue growth slowed to 2.2% in 2025 from 46.0% in 2024. Operating cash flow was negative KRW 1.68 billion in 2025, diverging from the operating profit. Q3 2026 revenue rose only 0.3% quarter on quarter, narrowing the pace of quarterly growth.

Ownership concentration and price volatility

Major shareholders held 47.57% as of October 1, 2026, and the free float ratio was 50.17%. The exchange designated the stock as an investment-caution issue based on its July 10, 2026 closing price and trading volume. The stock fell sharply over the past year.

Risk factors

Customer concentration

Orders can shift when top customers redesign products or change brand cycles. Several K-beauty brands are among the largest customers, so differences in brand growth feed directly into results. A change in one customer's orders can quickly alter quarterly revenue growth.

Fixed costs and capex burden

If utilization falls, a revenue decline can translate into a larger profit decline. Cash flow pressure may continue while second-half production investment is executed, and operating cash flow was negative in 2025.

Governance, liquidity and volatility

Major shareholders hold 47.57%, and the free float ratio is 50.17%. Under this structure, the closing price can move sharply even on low-volume days. The July 2026 investment-caution designation is an official record of this volatility.

12

Overall view

SMCG moved from a net loss in 2024 to a profit in 2025 on the back of its cosmetic glass-container ODM business, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders, revenue and operating profit each rose for four straight quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026.

Its global revenue share gains, electric-furnace fixed-cost structure and expansion into heavy perfume bottles and color cosmetic containers are cited as strengths. On the other side, 2025 revenue growth was only 2.2%, and Q3 2026 revenue rose just 0.3% quarter on quarter.

Shifts in top-customer redesigns and orders could move quarterly results, and operating cash flow was negative in 2025. High ownership concentration, a limited free float and the July 2026 investment-caution designation remain sources of price volatility.

Valuation looks different on the last four quarters of earnings than on consensus estimates, so the reading depends on whether earnings growth continues.

Next checkpoints are the results of equipment investment at the end of December 2026, the operating margin and operating cash flow in the 2026 annual report, and revenue trends in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
