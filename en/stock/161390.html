02

Business structure

Hankook Tire & Technology is part of the Hankook & Company group and runs two businesses, tires and thermal management.

In Q3 2026, according to preliminary results announced on November 6, 2026, tire segment sales were KRW 2.81 trillion, about 51% of consolidated sales of KRW 2.66 trillion from Hanon Systems included in consolidated sales of KRW 5.47 trillion.

Hanon Systems, the thermal management arm, recorded Q3 2026 sales of KRW 2.66 trillion. In tires, 18-inch and larger models made up 49.4% of passenger and light truck tire sales in the first three quarters of 2026. EV-only tires accounted for 32.3% of original equipment tire sales over the same period.

The company supplies OE tires to premium models including the Porsche 911 Carrera, Panamera and Taycan and the Ford Mustang Mach-E. Its iON brand, a full-line EV tire lineup, is positioned as a way to strengthen the replacement market.

Hanon Systems provides vehicle heating and cooling and battery thermal management, and it has been consolidated since the first quarter of 2025. Production is being expanded at plants in Tennessee in the United States and Hungary to widen the supply base in North America and Europe.

Industry commentary holds that makers with high-inch and EV-dedicated lineups ahead of demand are better positioned in a market that increasingly rewards technology.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed basis, revenue rose from KRW 8.39 trillion in 2022 to KRW 8.94 trillion in 2023 (up 6.5%) and KRW 9.41 trillion in 2024 (up 5.3%), then jumped to KRW 21.2 trillion in 2025 (up 125.3%).

The 2025 jump reflects Hanon Systems being consolidated from the first quarter of 2025, and revenue has grown for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 705.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.33 trillion in 2023 (up 88.1%) and KRW 1.76 trillion in 2024 (up 32.7%), then KRW 1.84 trillion in 2025 (up 4.5%). Operating margin fell from 18.7% in 2024 to 8.7% in 2025, which reads as lower-margin thermal management sales entering the consolidated base.

Press reports put the Q2 2026 tire margin at 17.2% and Hanon Systems at 3.6%. Another report noted that purchase price allocation amortization began to be recognized from the second quarter of 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 690.2 billion in 2022, KRW 720.2 billion in 2023 (up 4.3%), KRW 1.11 trillion in 2024 (up 54.8%), and KRW 1.09 trillion in 2025 (down 2.3%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 971.6 billion in 2025, below the controlling-shareholder figure, which means non-controlling interests absorbed losses. Quarterly revenue went from KRW 5.31 trillion in Q1 2026 to KRW 5.68 trillion in Q2 and KRW 5.47 trillion in Q3.

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 507 billion in Q1 2026 and rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 598.8 billion in Q3. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 346.2 billion in Q1 2026 and has declined for two consecutive quarters to KRW 224.2 billion in Q3.

Tire segment operating profit fell 2.6% to KRW 505.9 billion in Q3 2026 as Middle East-related raw material and logistics costs rose, according to the company. The last four quarters of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders total KRW 1.05 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

Industry commentary describes the tire market as being reshaped around two axes, premium high-inch products and electrification. Models of 18 inches and above sell at higher unit prices than standard sizes, so they add more profit per unit sold.

EVs are heavier and deliver stronger instant torque than combustion cars, which raises the bar on durability, noise and efficiency, and makers that secured EV-dedicated lines early are seen as better placed.

On the other side, a 2025 report noted that the planned end of US EV subsidies is slowing EV market growth and could delay a recovery in demand for thermal management parts. Raw material and logistics costs in Q3 2026 were affected by Middle East geopolitical tensions, according to the company.

Auto demand softening and trade changes have made it hard for auto parts makers broadly to defend profitability, yet press reports said Hankook held a double-digit operating margin. Hanon Systems' operating profit fell 66% in 2024 according to a 2025 report, and it rose 26.6% in Q3 2026.

Commentary cites a margin of around 5% as the level for auto parts makers on average, and a return to at least that level is seen as the benchmark for Hanon Systems.

06

Outlook

The company said it is expanding production at its Tennessee and Hungary plants to widen its global manufacturing and supply base (as announced November 6, 2026). A 2024 report noted that large capital expenditure for phase two of the Tennessee plant and the Hungary expansion had been planned through 2026.

In the same report, a credit rating agency judged that strong tire profitability and operating cash generation could keep financial burden within a manageable range after consolidation.

On OE supply, the company began supplying the Porsche 911 Carrera and has widened its lineup to models including the Panamera and the Volkswagen Atlas.

The company said it plans to respond steadily to demand shifts in major markets such as North America and Europe while expanding OE supply cooperation with global automakers.

The entry of the iON EV brand into the replacement market and development of the i-Flex airless tire are presented as medium- to long-term projects. In thermal management, reports describe Hanon Systems' return to normal margins as the key issue.

A report also said exercising the put option early next year under the acquisition contract would require about KRW 300 billion in additional funds. The 2026 annual report, the next periodic filing, has a statutory deadline at the end of March 2027.

08

Bull factors

Premium tire mix strengthens

Tire segment sales reached KRW 2.81 trillion in Q3 2026, a quarterly record (preliminary, announced November 6, 2026). Models of 18 inches and above made up 49.4% of passenger and light truck tire sales in the first three quarters of 2026, and EV-only tires accounted for 32.3% of OE tire sales. Higher unit prices on these models add profit per unit, according to industry explanations.

Operating profit up two straight quarters

Consolidated operating profit rose from a Q1 2026 low of KRW 507 billion to KRW 559.1 billion in Q2 and KRW 598.8 billion in Q3, a two-quarter run of gains. Q3 operating profit was up 2.2% year on year and 7.1% from the prior quarter. It rose even as revenue fell 3.8% from Q2.

Thermal unit profit improves; capacity added

Hanon Systems' Q3 2026 operating profit rose 26.6% to KRW 120.6 billion, and its operating margin reached 4.5%, up 1.0 percentage point from a year earlier (preliminary). Commentary sees the consolidated thermal business, alongside tires, as a portfolio for electrification. Expanded capacity in Tennessee and Hungary is cited as widening production and supply.

Bear factors

Higher financial leverage

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 41.6% in 2024 to 87.6% in 2025, and total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 12.4 trillion. A 2025 report said a credit rating agency flagged a possible downgrade if the consolidated borrowing dependence ratio exceeds 30%.

A report also said exercising the put option in early 2027, under the acquisition contract, would require about KRW 300 billion more.

Slower earnings momentum

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 2.3% to KRW 1.09 trillion in 2025. It has declined for two consecutive quarters from Q2 to Q3 2026, and Q3 2026 came in at KRW 224.2 billion, down 42.6% year on year. Operating margin in 2025 was 8.7%, below 18.7% in 2024.

Cost and demand variables

Tire segment operating profit fell 2.6% to KRW 505.9 billion in Q3 2026, and the company cited higher raw material and logistics costs linked to prolonged Middle East tensions. A 2025 report said the planned end of US EV subsidies raised concerns about slower EV demand. Auto demand softening and trade changes are described as making profit defense harder across auto parts makers.

10

Risk factors

Financial and credit

Consolidating Hanon Systems sharply raised consolidated total borrowings and financial leverage. Borrowing dependence remains a key condition under credit rating criteria.

Reports note that if Hanon Systems' capital spending and rights issue participation lead to more borrowing, financial stability metrics could weaken again.

Thermal business profitability

Hanon Systems' operating margin was 4.5% in Q3 2026, below the roughly 5% level cited as the auto parts industry average. If EV demand recovers slowly, demand for thermal parts could lag as well.

Purchase price allocation amortization recognized from Q2 2025 was reported as another factor that could slow the margin recovery.

Costs, trade and governance

Higher raw material and logistics costs tied to Middle East instability are pressing tire segment margins. A report said US auto parts tariffs weighed on Hanon Systems' profitability. A 2025 report said the legal detention of the holding company's chairman could delay group decision-making.

What to watch next

  1. Late March 2027 (statutory deadline)

    The 2026 annual report. The annual operating margin (8.7% in 2025) and debt-to-equity ratio (87.6% in 2025) will show how the Hanon Systems effect is reflected in results.

  2. Early 2027

    Whether the put option is exercised. Exercise would require about KRW 300 billion in additional funds, and how that is financed will shape the debt structure.

  3. Around late April 2027 (expected)

    Q1 2027 preliminary results. Whether tire segment operating margin holds near 18%, and whether net profit attributable to controlling shareholders breaks its decline since Q3 2026, are the quarterly checkpoints.

  4. End of 2027

    Whether Hanon Systems' operating margin returns to at least 5%. This is the timeline cited in press commentary and can be checked against filed results.

12

Overall view

Hankook Tire & Technology lifted its tire mix toward high-inch and EV-dedicated models and posted a record quarterly tire sales figure. Operating profit reached KRW 598.8 billion in Q3 2026 after two consecutive quarterly gains.

However, revenue jumped to KRW 21.2 trillion in 2025 after consolidating Hanon Systems, while operating margin fell to 8.7%. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 1.09 trillion in 2025 and has declined for two consecutive quarters since Q2 2026.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 87.6% in 2025, making financial leverage a central variable. Strengths lie in tire margins and the EV and high-inch lineup, while weaknesses lie in the low-margin thermal business and debt burden.

Middle East-linked raw material and logistics costs and concerns about slower EV demand are variables that cut both ways. The next checkpoint is the annual operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report.
