Report overview

Health food slowdown, AI acquisition reshapes CXI

As revenue and profit at its China health food business shrink, CXI Healthcare Technology Group decided on October 13, 2026 to acquire a 55% stake in AIDT Technologies for about KRW 60.6 billion, moving to reshape its business mix.

  1. 1

    Annual revenue fell from KRW 108.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 58.9 billion in 2025 (converted to KRW), and operating profit dropped 51.0% from KRW 20.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 9.95 billion in 2025.

  2. 2

    After an operating loss of KRW 3.68 billion in the second quarter of 2025, the company returned to profit in the third quarter, and fourth-quarter operating profit recovered to KRW 7.74 billion, still 51.3% below the year-earlier quarter.

  3. 3

    At the end of 2025 the debt ratio was 0.5%, with total equity of KRW 515.4 billion against total liabilities of KRW 2.66 billion, and 2025 operating cash flow was KRW 42.2 billion.

  4. 4

    In preliminary results disclosed on October 8, 2026 (internal pre-audit accounts), consolidated operating profit for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was about KRW 2.99 billion, down 69.3%, and revenue was about KRW 49.5 billion, down 14.2%.

  5. 5

    In a corrected filing on October 22, 2026, the company said USD 6.6 million of the USD 45.375 million AIDT purchase price would be borrowed from a subsidiary and the remaining USD 38.775 million funded from its own cash.

02

Business structure

CXI Healthcare Technology Group was founded in February 2009 and produces and sells health food in China. It is a Cayman Islands holding company listed on KOSDAQ that controls nine affiliates. It was formerly called CKH Food & Health and adopted its current name in October 2023.

Its core business makes traditional-medicine-based supplements in capsule, tablet, granule and tea forms under the Yongsheng Huoli brand. It runs raw-material distribution for ingredients such as cordyceps and tea leaves in-house to support quality and cost control.

Sales are centered on online platforms including JD.com, Tmall Global and Douyin. In cosmetics it sells the TUNEMAKERS skincare brand and, in October 2025, signed a distribution contract to sell 15 products of the Korean brand Answer Garden in China.

In July 2026 a subsidiary signed a hospital AI diagnostic-system technical support contract worth CNY 33.79 million (about KRW 7.3 billion).

Segment revenue shares could not be verified, so no figures are given, and although disclosure portals classify the company as other financial services, its actual operations are centered on health food.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 108.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 74.8 billion in 2023 (down 30.8%), KRW 74.7 billion in 2024 (unchanged year on year) and KRW 58.9 billion in 2025 (down 21.2%), a decline in each year from 2023 through 2025 (all converted to KRW).

Operating profit was KRW 6.71 billion in 2022, KRW 7.45 billion in 2023, KRW 20.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.95 billion in 2025; 2024 was the largest year of 2022-2025, and 2025 was down 51.0% year on year.

The operating margin moved from 6.2% to 10.0%, 27.2% and 16.9%; in 2024 revenue was flat yet operating profit rose 172.7%, so margin drove the gain, while in 2025 the margin narrowed alongside lower revenue.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.38 billion, KRW 7.86 billion, KRW 31.6 billion and KRW 13 billion, with 2025 down 58.8%. In both 2024 and 2025 that figure exceeded operating profit, and the composition of the gap cannot be determined from the reported figures alone.

By quarter, revenue was KRW 27.7 billion and operating profit KRW 15.9 billion in the fourth quarter of 2024, falling to KRW 16.7 billion and KRW 4.94 billion in the first quarter of 2025.

The second quarter brought revenue of KRW 7.35 billion, an operating loss of KRW 3.68 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.76 billion.

The third quarter returned to profit with revenue of KRW 15.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 0.95 billion, and the fourth quarter posted revenue of KRW 19.7 billion, operating profit of KRW 7.74 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 10.5 billion, so revenue rose in two consecutive quarters from the third quarter, though fourth-quarter revenue and operating profit were still 28.7% and 51.3% below the year-earlier quarter.

Operating cash flow was KRW 37 billion in 2022, KRW 34.6 billion in 2023, KRW 45.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 42.2 billion in 2025, well above 2025 net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 13 billion.

Preliminary figures are separate: in internal accounts disclosed on October 8, 2026, consolidated results for July 2025 through June 2026 showed operating profit of about KRW 2.99 billion (down 69.3%), revenue of about KRW 49.5 billion (down 14.2%) and net income of about KRW 8.07 billion (down 36.7%), with lower revenue and profit at the main subsidiary Yongsheng Huoli cited as the main cause.

A shareholder-meeting disclosure on October 28, 2026 reported an unqualified audit opinion on the consolidated statements. These figures are not from a periodic report, and because the currency conversion basis differs from the annual figures above, they are not compared directly.

Industry analysis

Size and growth statistics for China's health supplement market could not be verified from reliable sources, so none are given. The company's sales structure relies heavily on online platforms such as JD.com, Tmall Global and Douyin, which leaves it sensitive to platform competition and consumer trends.

For the fiscal year ended June 2026, lower revenue and profit at the main subsidiary Yongsheng Huoli were cited as the main cause of the earnings change, which suggests the core health food business weighed on results.

The company is running research and development to diversify into general food formats, and in cosmetics it is widening its lineup by bringing Korean indie brands into China.

Comparable peer data is limited, and listed companies in the same financial classification, such as KB Financial Group, differ in business nature and are not direct comparisons.

In its new AI area, AIDT Technologies offers vertical AI solutions built on a Data-to-AI value chain that links data collection and processing, model training and industry-specific applications.

An external valuation firm estimated that AIDT consolidated revenue would rise to about USD 52.07 million in 2027 and keep growing at roughly 40% a year through 2029, but this is an outside estimate, not a confirmed result.

06

Outlook

No quantitative company guidance was found, so the outlook rests on disclosed business events. On October 13, 2026 the company decided to buy 27,500 shares of AIDT Technologies (a 55% stake) for about KRW 60.6 billion, with a planned acquisition date of October 19, 2026.

A later corrected filing changed the funding from entirely its own cash to a mix of a subsidiary loan and its own cash, so completion and the timing of consolidation need to be confirmed in follow-up filings.

According to the company, AIDT posted first-half 2026 revenue of USD 36.97 million (about KRW 53.6 billion) and operating profit of USD 7.24 million (about KRW 10.5 billion).

In the external valuation, the discounted cash flow estimate per share was USD 1,618 to USD 1,950, and the transaction price of USD 1,650 per share fell within that range.

On capital policy, the company decided to cut par value from USD 0.5 to USD 0.1 citing balance sheet improvement and a base for shareholder returns, and it also carried out a 5-for-1 reverse share consolidation.

A filing registered on October 2, 2026 reported that the trading halt was lifted following the listing change for the consolidated shares. It also decided on a roughly KRW 7.2 billion third-party rights offering directed at its largest shareholder, Lin Jinsheng.

At the regular shareholder meeting on October 28, 2026, all 11 agenda items, including a subsidiary liquidation, were approved.

08

Bull factors

Near debt-free balance sheet

The debt ratio fell from 2.2% in 2022 to 0.5% in 2025, and total liabilities of KRW 2.66 billion at the end of 2025 are minimal against total equity of KRW 515.4 billion. Operating cash flow stayed between KRW 34.6 billion and KRW 45.5 billion throughout 2022-2025. Even as profit declined, financial burden has remained small.

Quarterly recovery trend

Operating loss of KRW 3.68 billion in the second quarter of 2025 gave way to a return to profit in the third quarter (KRW 0.95 billion) and operating profit of KRW 7.74 billion in the fourth. Revenue also rose in two consecutive quarters, reaching KRW 19.7 billion in the fourth quarter.

Fourth-quarter net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 10.5 billion makes up most of the KRW 13 billion total for the last four quarters.

Adding a profitable AI business

The company stressed that it is taking control of an AI business that already generates revenue and profit, unlike an early-stage venture. AIDT's first-half 2026 revenue and operating profit rose 93.5% and 144.0% from a year earlier, and its operating margin was 19.6%. If consolidated, it could offset part of the weakness in the health food business.

09

Bear factors

Persistent revenue decline and margin compression

Annual revenue declined from KRW 108.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 58.9 billion in 2025, and the operating margin fell from 27.2% in 2024 to 16.9% in 2025. Preliminary results show operating profit down 69.3% in the fiscal year ended June 2026, so the downtrend continued.

Fourth-quarter 2025 revenue and operating profit were down 28.7% and 51.3% from the year-earlier quarter, which shows the recovery is limited in scale.

Capital allocation burden from a large acquisition

The purchase price equals 12.33% of the equity stated in the filing. It is a large outlay relative to the earnings of the existing health food business, and part of the funding was changed to a subsidiary loan through a corrected plan. If post-acquisition performance falls short of external estimates, the effect on net assets could be larger.

Earnings volatility and reliance on non-operating items

In the second quarter of 2025 the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 3.68 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.76 billion, so quarterly profit swung widely within a single year.

In both 2024 and 2025 net income attributable to controlling shareholders exceeded operating profit, which suggests non-operating items may have affected net income. For the fiscal year ended June 2026, pre-tax income from continuing operations of about KRW 8.34 billion was also larger than operating profit.

10

Risk factors

Acquisition execution and integration

AIDT's growth outlook rests on an external valuation firm's estimates and the company's own description, not on results confirmed after consolidation. Corrected filings followed the planned acquisition date, so the completion timing and funding structure must be confirmed in follow-up filings. Health food and AI differ in nature, which leaves uncertainty in resource allocation and integration.

Governance and disclosure

As a foreign holding company, CXI prepares only consolidated financial statements under international accounting standards and no separate statements. Net assets and profit are concentrated in China subsidiaries, so readers need to check fund transfers and the local regulatory environment separately.

A rights offering directed at the largest shareholder and corrections to the acquisition funding also raise the need to track disclosure changes.

Listing maintenance and share price volatility

The company said it planned to complete the capital reduction and share consolidation by the end of June 2026 to satisfy in advance the new listing maintenance requirements due to take effect in July.

Trading was halted during the consolidation and listing change and then resumed, so share price moves reflect the effects of capital policy. A relatively small tradable float can amplify price volatility.

Overall view

CXI Healthcare Technology Group is a China health food holding company with almost no debt and large total equity, but its revenue declined in each year from 2023 through 2025 based on reported results.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 20.3 billion in 2024 and fell to KRW 9.95 billion in 2025; on a quarterly basis it recovered to KRW 7.74 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 after an operating loss in the second quarter, but remained below the year-earlier level.

Preliminary results for the fiscal year ended June 2026 (disclosed October 8, 2026) showed operating profit of about KRW 2.99 billion, down 69.3%.

On the bullish side the issues are financial stability and adding a profitable AI business, and on the bearish side weakness in the core business and the capital allocation burden of a large acquisition. Completion of the deal, the funding structure and post-consolidation results are not yet settled.

The next quarterly report, follow-up acquisition filings and the annual report are the points that will test this picture. This report is for information only and contains no buy or sell opinion or target price.
