UST: Navigating an Earnings Recovery After a Multi-Year Downturn

After three straight years of declining revenue and operating profit since 2022, UST has posted two consecutive quarters of improvement in early 2026 following a trough in the fourth quarter of 2025.

02

Business structure

UST is a stainless steel pipe (STS pipe) manufacturer headquartered in Pohang, North Gyeongsang Province. The company was spun off from Mijoo Steel in April 2009 under the name Mijoo STS, renamed UST in 2017, and listed on KOSDAQ the same year.

Based on the second quarter of 2026, stainless steel pipe accounted for roughly 96% of sales, with high-purity clean pipe at about 2%, processing/toll manufacturing at 1%, and other items making up the remainder.

UST's clean pipes and tubes are supplied to domestic semiconductor, display, and secondary battery makers, and are exported to the United States, Europe, Japan, and Southeast Asia. The company had 76 employees as of the end of 2025, reflecting its scale as a small specialized manufacturer.

Governance is centered on its controlling shareholder, Hwanggeum STS. Within the broader steel pipe industry, UST is a small-cap name relative to larger players such as SeAH Steel, Nexteel, and Kiswire, and it has carved out a niche in small-diameter clean pipes for the semiconductor and battery sectors.

In 2022, the company disclosed development of a hybrid separator for electric-vehicle batteries, though its contribution to current sales has not been separately confirmed.

04

Earnings analysis

UST's annual revenue fell for three straight years, from KRW 99.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 99.1 billion in 2023 (down 0.7% year-on-year), KRW 74.8 billion in 2024 (down 24.6%), and KRW 58 billion in 2025 (down 22.5%).

Operating profit followed the same trajectory, declining from KRW 16.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 14.1 billion in 2023 (down 13.2%), KRW 6.9 billion in 2024 (down 50.9%), and KRW 2.33 billion in 2025 (down 66.2%), with the operating margin dropping from 16.2% in 2022 to 4.0% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders also contracted from KRW 12.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.52 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.44 billion in 2025 (down 55.7% year-on-year).

On a quarterly basis, however, after bottoming in the fourth quarter of 2025 with revenue of KRW 14.5 billion and an operating loss of KRW 63.63 million, UST posted two consecutive quarters of improvement: revenue of KRW 16 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.05 billion (swing to profit) in the first quarter of 2026, followed by revenue of KRW 18.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.77 billion in the second quarter of 2026.

Second-quarter 2026 operating profit rose 68.7% quarter-on-quarter and 634.3% year-on-year, while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 1.66 billion, up 74.7% quarter-on-quarter and 285.9% year-on-year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the trailing four quarters (Q3 2025 through Q2 2026) totaled KRW 3.77 billion.

Separately from the earnings contraction, the balance sheet steadily strengthened, with the debt-to-equity ratio falling from 26.6% in 2022 to 5.9% in 2025; total equity stood at KRW 82.8 billion (entirely attributable to controlling shareholders) against total liabilities of just KRW 4.88 billion at the end of 2025.

Operating cash flow peaked at KRW 17.6 billion in 2023 before settling at KRW 4.56 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.56 billion in 2025, remaining relatively stable relative to the scale of profit.

05

Industry analysis

Stainless steel pipe demand is closely tied to capital expenditure cycles in downstream industries such as semiconductors, displays, secondary batteries, plants, and construction.

The global stainless steel market is expected to grow moderately, with one market research firm projecting a 6.4% compound annual growth rate from 2026 to 2034, and another forecasting 4.72% annual growth from 2026 to 2031.

At a domestic steel pipe industry seminar, expanded pipe exports tied to US oil country tubular goods (OCTG) and domestic semiconductor cluster construction were cited as factors that could support a market recovery, though the same analysis noted that despite a surge in pipe exports between January and July 2026, domestic demand remained weak even amid semiconductor cluster construction.

The same forum projected roughly 1% improvement in domestic demand in 2027 on the back of expanded large-scale construction projects.

Conversely, another analysis found that short-term recovery prospects for the steel and secondary battery industries remain limited given weak domestic demand, global oversupply, and shifting policy.

Amid this industry volatility, UST focuses on a narrow segment of small-diameter clean pipes for semiconductor and battery applications rather than competing broadly like larger pipe makers, making its results more sensitive to the capex timing of specific downstream industries than to overall sector supply and demand.

Competitively, the company appears to have carved out a position in a specialized niche requiring high-purity, precision processing rather than competing head-on with larger pipe makers such as SeAH Steel and Nexteel.

06

Outlook

In its value-up plan decided on March 31, 2026, UST set goals of stable earnings generation, maintaining financial soundness, and keeping dividends at an appropriate level to enhance shareholder value.

Specifically, the plan calls for profit expansion through stronger cost competitiveness and profitability-focused management, along with securing stable dividend resources based on continued profitability.

According to the disclosure, the 2025 dividend payout ratio was presented at 57.7%, with dividend payments for both 2024 and 2025 set at an identical KRW 141,075,954,0 each year. This suggests a policy of maintaining total dividend payouts even as profit shrank.

On the earnings front, revenue and operating profit have improved for two consecutive quarters in the first and second quarters of 2026 after bottoming in the fourth quarter of 2025, and whether this trend continues through the second half of 2026 is the next point to watch.

It also remains to be seen whether the roughly 1% improvement in domestic pipe demand projected for 2027 and the trend of expanding pipe exports to the United States, as cited at an industry seminar, translate into actual revenue for UST.

However, these are industry-wide analyses rather than company-specific order backlogs or guidance, so it would be premature to assume they apply directly to UST's individual sales.

Bull factors

Two Consecutive Quarters of Profit Recovery

After bottoming with an operating loss of KRW 63.63 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, UST posted operating profit of KRW 1.05 billion (a swing to profit) in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 1.77 billion in the second quarter of 2026, up 68.7% quarter-on-quarter and 634.3% year-on-year.

Revenue also rose for two straight quarters, to KRW 16 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 18.3 billion in Q2 2026. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 1.66 billion in the second quarter of 2026, up 285.9% year-on-year. Whether this trend continues will be confirmed in the next quarterly report.

Very Low Leverage

The debt-to-equity ratio fell sharply to 5.9% at the end of 2025 from 26.6% in 2022. Total equity stood at KRW 82.8 billion, entirely attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of only KRW 4.88 billion.

The balance sheet has steadily strengthened even as profit shrank, leaving the company with low reliance on external financing.

Niche Product Exposure to Advanced Industries

High-purity clean pipes are supplied to domestic semiconductor, display, and secondary battery makers, and are also exported to the United States, Europe, Japan, and Southeast Asia.

In 2022, UST disclosed the development of a hybrid separator for electric-vehicle batteries, and in April 2026 market commentary cited expectations for expanded battery applications. However, the actual revenue contribution of these newer products has not been separately confirmed.

Bear factors

Three Straight Years of Declining Revenue and Profit

Revenue fell for three consecutive years from KRW 99.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 58 billion in 2025, while operating profit declined from KRW 16.2 billion to KRW 2.33 billion over the same period. The operating margin also dropped sharply, from 16.2% in 2022 to 4.0% in 2025.

While quarterly results have improved in 2026, this has not yet been confirmed as a break from the multi-year annual decline.

Quarterly Volatility and Risk of Renewed Losses

Just after posting operating profit of KRW 1.33 billion in the third quarter of 2025, UST swung to an operating loss of KRW 63.63 million in the fourth quarter. Given the wide swings between quarters, a recurrence of a single-quarter loss cannot be ruled out.

As a small-cap name with a limited revenue base, a shift in order timing from a single customer can meaningfully swing quarterly results.

Mixed Industry Outlook

At one industry seminar, expanded pipe exports to the United States and semiconductor cluster construction were cited as factors that could support a recovery, but the same forum also noted that domestic demand remained weak from January through July 2026.

Other analysis suggested that short-term recovery prospects for the steel and secondary battery industries remain limited. With industry views diverging, it would be premature to assume these trends translate directly into company-specific results.

Risk factors

Downstream Demand Volatility

Stainless steel pipe and high-purity clean pipe, which make up most of UST's revenue, depend on the capex schedules of advanced industries such as semiconductors, displays, and secondary batteries. Delays or cuts in capital spending by these sectors could directly affect orders and revenue.

While the global stainless steel market overall is expected to grow moderately, regional and sector-level demand can vary significantly.

Raw Material and Foreign Exchange Volatility

Fluctuations in raw material prices, such as nickel used in stainless steel production, directly affect costs. Given UST's export exposure, exchange rate movements can also affect profitability. Both raw material prices and exchange rates are external variables largely outside the company's control.

Ownership Structure and Liquidity

Management operates under a governance structure centered on controlling shareholder Hwanggeum STS, which may limit the proportion of freely traded shares. As a small-cap KOSDAQ-listed stock, trading volume and share price volatility can be relatively high.

Investors should continue to monitor disclosures regarding ownership structure and transactions by related parties.

Overall view

UST is a small-cap stainless steel pipe maker whose revenue and operating profit declined for three consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, with the operating margin falling from 16.2% to 4.0%.

However, after bottoming with an operating loss of KRW 63.63 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, the company posted two consecutive quarters of improvement in revenue and operating profit in the first and second quarters of 2026, an early sign of earnings recovery.

Its balance sheet steadily strengthened even during the profit contraction, with the debt-to-equity ratio falling to 5.9% at the end of 2025, and the company's 2026 value-up plan sets stable earnings and dividend maintenance as goals.

On the industry side, views diverge between expectations for expanded US pipe exports and semiconductor cluster construction on one hand, and persistent domestic demand weakness and a slow recovery outlook for the steel and secondary battery sectors on the other.

Given the wide swings in quarterly results, the possibility of another single-quarter loss cannot be ruled out. Investors should watch the upcoming third-quarter report and industry demand indicators before forming a view.
