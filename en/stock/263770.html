Profit recovery on semiconductor piping demand; sales growth still the test

02

Business structure

UST was spun off from Miju Steel in 2009 as a stainless steel (STS) pipe maker and took its current name in 2017. Its headquarters are in Pohang, North Gyeongsang Province, and it belongs to the Hwang Kum Steel & Technology group.

According to Butler company data, the 2026 Q2 revenue mix was 96% stainless steel pipe, 2% high-purity clean tube, 2% other and 1% processing services. Stainless pipes serve construction, shipbuilding, offshore plant, petrochemical, paper, auto parts, food and beverage, semiconductor and bio customers.

Clean pipes and tubes are supplied to domestic semiconductor, display and secondary battery makers and exported to the United States, Europe, Japan and Southeast Asia.

Steel & Metal News, citing the Q3 2026 quarterly report, said exports of high-purity STS clean tubes to the United States and Japan grew alongside expanding AI-driven semiconductor capital spending.

The same report put the Q3 2026 STS pipe selling price at KRW 4,366 per kilogram, 16.7% above the 2025 average of KRW 3,741. Peers in the same steel and metal sector include SeAH Steel, Dongkuk Steel Mill and KG Dongbusteel.

Stainless pipe accounts for 96% of revenue, so changes in clean-tube demand have a limited direct effect on total results.

04

Earnings analysis

Reported annual sales were KRW 99.9 billion in 2022, KRW 99.1 billion in 2023 (down 0.7% year on year), KRW 74.8 billion in 2024 (down 24.6%) and KRW 58 billion in 2025 (down 22.5%). Sales fell for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025; 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest within 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 16.2 billion in 2022, KRW 14.1 billion in 2023 (down 13.2%), KRW 6.9 billion in 2024 (down 50.9%) and KRW 2.33 billion in 2025 (down 66.2%), also declining for three consecutive years from 2023.

The operating margin fell every year, from 16.2% in 2022 to 14.2% in 2023, 9.2% in 2024 and 4.0% in 2025. Owner-attributable net income declined from KRW 12.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.52 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.44 billion in 2025 (down 55.7%).

Net income including non-controlling interests is reported at the same amounts for 2022, 2024 and 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 7.97 billion in 2022, KRW 17.6 billion in 2023, KRW 4.56 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.56 billion in 2025, with wide swings between years.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 82.8 billion and total liabilities KRW 4.88 billion; the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 26.6% in 2022 to 5.9% in 2025.

Quarterly, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 15.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.33 billion; Q4 2025 revenue dropped to KRW 14.5 billion (down 5.5% quarter on quarter) and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 63.63 million.

Q1 2026 revenue rose to KRW 16 billion (up 10.0%) with operating profit of KRW 1.05 billion, a return to profit. Q2 2026 operating profit reached KRW 1.77 billion (up 68.7% quarter on quarter), and Q3 2026 operating profit reached KRW 3.32 billion (up 88.1% quarter on quarter and up 150.7% year on year).

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 18.3 billion, down 0.2% quarter on quarter but up 18.9% year on year. Steel & Metal News reported that Q3 cost of sales as a share of revenue was 77.1%, down from 85.3% a year earlier, and cited higher selling prices and lower selling expenses as drivers.

Vegan News, citing the Q3 2026 quarterly report, reported that revenue for January to September 2026 rose 21.0% year on year to KRW 52.6 billion. Owner-attributable net income for the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 5.33 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for stainless steel pipe comes from semiconductor, display and secondary battery equipment as well as construction, shipbuilding and petrochemical plants. UST clean tubes are tied to demand from semiconductor and display equipment, so capital spending cycles feed into quarterly results.

Steel & Metal News, reporting on Q3 2026, cited expanding AI-driven semiconductor capital spending as the background to higher exports of high-purity STS clean tubes to the United States and Japan. The same report linked the higher selling price to export growth.

In cycle terms, revenue peaked at KRW 99.9 billion in 2022, fell through 2025, and recovered to a quarterly range of KRW 16 billion to KRW 18.3 billion in 2026. Within the steel and metal sector, UST is compared with listed peers such as SeAH Steel, Dongkuk Steel Mill and KG Dongbusteel.

General-purpose pipe and high-purity clean tube serve different customers and quality requirements, so the business profile differs even within the sector. Stainless raw material prices and exchange rates affect costs and export margins. The key question for the industry read is whether 2026 sales growth came from volume or from unit price.

No specific schedule for new orders, capacity expansion or product launches has been announced.

Bull factors

Profit recovery trend

After an operating loss of KRW 63.63 million in Q4 2025, UST returned to profit in Q1 2026 with operating profit of KRW 1.05 billion, and operating profit rose in both Q2 and Q3. Steel & Metal News reported the Q3 2026 operating margin at 18.2%, up from 8.4% a year earlier. The same report put the net margin up from 7.3% to 14.8%.

Selling price and cost ratio

The Q3 2026 STS pipe selling price was KRW 4,366 per kilogram, 16.7% above the 2025 average of KRW 3,741. Cost of sales as a share of revenue fell to 77.1% from 85.3% a year earlier, and lower selling expenses were also cited as a contributor. The lower cost burden is presented as the basis for the margin recovery.

Financial capacity

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 4.88 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell to 5.9% from 26.6% in 2022. With total equity of KRW 82.8 billion and a small debt load, the balance sheet could serve as a buffer if the industry slows.

Bear factors

Multi-year sales decline

Sales fell for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, from KRW 99.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 58 billion in 2025. The operating margin also fell from 16.2% in 2022 to 4.0% in 2025.

Because sales declines and margin compression persisted together for several years, whether the recovery is temporary or structural remains open.

Quarterly profit volatility

In Q4 2025 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 63.63 million and owner-attributable net income of KRW 862,000. Since returning to profit in Q1 2026, quarterly profit has been positive for only two quarters so far.

With a small earnings base, quarterly swings are large, which makes trend conclusions from one or two quarters difficult.

Flat quarterly revenue

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 18.3 billion was down 0.2% quarter on quarter and stayed at the Q2 level. If the profit recovery rests mainly on higher selling prices and a lower cost ratio rather than on sales volume, volume-driven growth remains an open question.

10

Risk factors

Demand concentration

Results are linked to the capital spending cycle in semiconductor, display and secondary battery equipment. If that spending slows, demand for clean tubes and export volumes could fall together. Exports were reported to grow mainly toward the United States and Japan, so demand shifts in those markets also matter.

Raw materials and FX

Stainless raw material prices and exchange rates affect both costs and export profitability. Whether the higher selling price can absorb cost increases will determine if the 2026 margin holds. If cost increases are not passed through to prices, the operating margin could come under renewed pressure.

Small-cap profile and ownership

UST is a small-cap company with a relatively high share held by the controlling shareholder side, which may limit trading liquidity. Small caps often show larger price swings, and the stock could move sharply around earnings releases.

Overall view

UST is a small-cap maker of stainless steel pipe whose reported sales and operating profit both declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. In 2025, revenue was KRW 58 billion, operating profit KRW 2.33 billion and the operating margin 4.0%.

Profit trends changed after the Q4 2025 operating loss of KRW 63.63 million, with a return to profit in Q1 2026 and operating profit rising in Q2 and Q3.
