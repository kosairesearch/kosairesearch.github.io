Oricom: Quarterly Profit Recovery Amid Lower Annual Margins

Oricom's operating profit rose in both Q2 and Q3 2026, but its annual operating margin fell to 3.9% in 2025 from 7.5% in 2022.

  1. 1

    Annual sales rose 6.0% year on year to KRW 227.7 billion in 2025, while operating profit fell 25.7% to KRW 8.92 billion.

  2. 2

    Q3 2026 operating profit reached KRW 3.29 billion, up 447.8% year on year, and rose in both Q2 and Q3 2026.

  3. 3

    The operating margin declined from 7.5% in 2022 to 3.9% in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose from 80.1% in 2024 to 87.1% in 2025.

  4. 4

    Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 11.1 billion over the last four quarters, compared with KRW 7.76 billion for full-year 2025.

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Oricom is an advertising agency founded in February 1975 that runs advertising production and agency services alongside magazine publishing.

Saramin's company profile lists transit advertising on subway and city bus exteriors, outdoor advertising production and agency work, and magazine publishing as its main businesses.

According to JobKorea, as of March 2026 the revenue mix was 39% magazine publishing income, 18% BTL business income, 16% other income, 8% advertising production income, and 18% other. Because magazine publishing is the largest share, changes in demand for print advertising weigh heavily on the business as a whole.

Saramin's profile names Vogue, GQ, Vogue Girl, Allure and W among the titles it publishes.

Production awards include a Consumers' Choice Good Advertising Award grand prize in January 2026 and a finalist placement at the Seoul Video Advertising Festival TVCF AWARDS in March 2026 in the AI Creative category, both for a digital bank client's campaigns.

Large listed agencies such as Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide compete in the same industry, and the gap in revenue scale is wide. The company is classified as a Doosan Group affiliate, which makes the share of related-party transactions a variable to watch when reading its revenue structure.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on DART periodic reports, annual sales rose from KRW 190.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 218.6 billion in 2023 (up 15.0% year on year), dipped to KRW 214.9 billion in 2024 (down 1.7%), and reached KRW 227.7 billion in 2025 (up 6.0%), the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating profit over the same period was KRW 14.2 billion, KRW 12.2 billion (down 14.1%), KRW 12 billion (down 1.4%) and KRW 8.92 billion (down 25.7%), a decline for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit fell in 2025 even as sales grew, so the operating margin slid from 7.5% in 2022 to 5.6% in 2023, 5.6% in 2024 and 3.9% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 12.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.83 billion in 2023 (down 20.0%), KRW 9.82 billion in 2024 (down 0.1%) and KRW 7.76 billion in 2025 (down 21.0%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported as KRW 12.3 billion, KRW 9.83 billion, KRW 9.82 billion and KRW 7.76 billion for the same years. The debt-to-equity ratio was 101.2% in 2022 and 101.4% in 2023, fell to 80.1% in 2024 and rose to 87.1% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 130.2 billion, of which controlling shareholders' equity was KRW 130.2 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 113.4 billion.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 19.2 billion in 2022 and KRW 16.1 billion in 2023 to -KRW 3.29 billion in 2024, before recovering to KRW 10.7 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 55.8 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 62.5 billion in Q4 2025 (up 11.9% quarter on quarter), fell to KRW 50.3 billion in Q1 2026 (down 19.5%), and then rose to KRW 57.3 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 65 billion in Q3 2026 (up 16.3% year on year).

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 600 million in Q3 2025 to KRW 4.82 billion in Q4 2025, fell to KRW 1.5 billion in Q1 2026, and then rose to KRW 3.04 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 3.29 billion in Q3 2026, with increases in two consecutive quarters after the Q1 2026 low.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 11.1 billion. Reporting on the Q4 2025 concentration of profit points to advertising peak-season effects, but the cause should be confirmed in the business report notes and explanations.

05

Industry analysis

Advertising is a business sensitive to corporate marketing budgets, the economic cycle and the pace at which media shifts to digital. Because Oricom has a large share of magazine publishing, it is directly exposed to advertising spend moving from print to digital media.

Quarterly sales show this sensitivity, falling from KRW 62.5 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 50.3 billion in Q1 2026 before recovering to KRW 65 billion in Q3 2026.

Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide, the listed agencies in the same industry, compete by building digital production and data capabilities, while Oricom responds with a portfolio combining magazines, outdoor and BTL. The gap in revenue scale with the large agencies is wide.

On November 17, 2026, the share price rose 19.01% and trading volume exceeded 8.05 million shares. The same article noted there is no official basis to attribute that surge to a specific business result.

Where the advertising cycle stands should be judged with industry indicators, since the company's quarterly results alone cannot settle the question. On the client side, the marketing budget allocation of consumer goods, financial and platform companies translates directly into order flow.

Dividends go through board decisions and shareholder meeting approval, so the 2026 fiscal year dividend decision is the next checkpoint.

Share price low relative to book value

The price-to-book ratio is below 1x, so the share price is low relative to book asset value. The debt-to-equity ratio declined from 101.2% in 2022 to 87.1% in 2025. With total equity of KRW 130.2 billion at the end of 2025, a financial cushion can be read from the balance sheet.

Overall view
