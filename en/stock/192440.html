02

Business structure

Spigen Korea was founded in 2009 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2014 as a mobile accessories company, and it holds six unlisted consolidated subsidiaries. Its core products are smartphone cases and screen protectors, sold under the Spigen brand mainly through online marketplaces centered on Amazon.

Sales began in North America and expanded to Europe, India, Japan and more than 52 countries. In 2020, protective film sales were KRW 45.3 billion, and annual revenue first exceeded KRW 400 billion in the same year.

The company emphasizes premium materials and in-house mold development, and is widening its lineup to foldable cases and wearable accessories. Because sales are concentrated on online marketplaces, changes in platform policies flow directly into sales.

On the competitive side, low- to mid-priced brands have continued to enter the market, and in 2024 IBK Investment & Securities pointed to rising penetration of lower-priced Chinese products as a cause of weak industry conditions. The latest segment revenue mix is best checked in the business segment table of the annual report.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 445.9 billion in 2022, KRW 447.4 billion in 2023 (up 0.3%), KRW 465.3 billion in 2024 (up 4.0%) and KRW 463.7 billion in 2025 (down 0.3%), moving within a range of roughly KRW 445 billion to KRW 465 billion. 2024 was the largest year in the period and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 52 billion in 2022 to KRW 35.3 billion in 2023 (down 32.1%) and KRW 21.7 billion in 2024 (down 38.4%), then recovered to KRW 33.2 billion in 2025 (up 52.7%). The operating margin moved from 11.7% in 2022 to 7.9%, 4.7% and 7.2% in 2025.

Because revenue did not fall sharply while margins did, the cause looks more like cost structure than sales decline. IBK Investment & Securities noted that selling and administrative expenses rose from 32.4% of sales at listing to 58.9% in the first half of 2023.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 33 billion in 2022, KRW 42.6 billion in 2023 (up 29.2%), KRW 29 billion in 2024 (down 32.0%) and KRW 29 billion in 2025 (up 0.1%), with 2023 the largest year.

Brokerage commentary at the time credited strong non-operating financial income for 2023 profit exceeding operating profit.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 32.8 billion, KRW 41.9 billion, KRW 27.9 billion and KRW 29 billion for 2022 to 2025, a different figure from profit attributable to owners of the parent.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 530.3 billion, of which KRW 529.1 billion belonged to owners of the parent, and total liabilities were KRW 43.8 billion. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 20.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 43.1 billion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 9.19 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 13.8 billion in the fourth quarter, then bottomed at KRW 11.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 before rising to KRW 12.6 billion in the second quarter and KRW 18.6 billion in the third quarter.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 138.3 billion was up 6.4% from a year earlier.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent bottomed at KRW 9.82 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and rose for three straight quarters to KRW 16.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 56.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for mobile accessories depends heavily on smartphone shipments and replacement cycles. In a 2024 report, IBK Investment & Securities cited weak global smartphone sales and rising penetration of lower-priced Chinese products as reasons for the slump in IT peripherals.

The same report linked the company's margin decline to these industry factors. By contrast, a company monitor commentary dated September 2026 described 5G and foldable smartphone launches and rising smartphone penetration in emerging markets as growth opportunities for the category.

Competition is intense because many low- to mid-priced brands compete on price, and the company positions in-house materials and mold development as its differentiator.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose from a year earlier and operating profit more than doubled, which suggests that both demand recovery and cost efficiency may have contributed.

The split between the two cannot be separated from disclosures alone, and changes in selling and administrative expense lines are the clue to watch. With sales spread across more than 52 countries, the company is exposed to currency movements and to changes in platform fee structures in each market.

06

Outlook

The growth lines confirmed so far are the expansion of foldable case and wearable accessory lineups. Launches of 5G and foldable smartphones are cited as a supporting factor for demand. Cost control through contract manufacturing is also identified as a contributor to the profit recovery.

Quarterly profit has risen in both the second and third quarters of 2026 after bottoming in the first quarter.

The next checkpoint is the fourth-quarter 2026 and full-year preliminary results, and the key question is whether the annual operating margin exceeds the 7.2% of 2025 and whether the third-quarter improvement holds through year-end.

Once the 2026 annual report is filed, the confirmed selling and administrative cost structure and the segment breakdown will become available. First-quarter 2027 results will show whether the impact of low- to mid-priced competition is easing and whether the selling expense ratio has come down.

On shareholder returns, dividend decisions and treasury share policy are expected around the regular shareholder meeting. Whether the lack of shareholder-friendly policy flagged by brokers in 2024 has been addressed is also worth watching.

08

Bull factors

Turn in quarterly profit

Quarterly operating profit began rising in the second quarter of 2026 and kept rising in the third quarter, reaching KRW 18.6 billion. Profit attributable to owners of the parent has also risen for three straight quarters since the fourth-quarter 2025 low. This matches the annual margin recovery from 4.7% in 2024 to 7.2% in 2025.

Financial headroom

Total liabilities were KRW 43.8 billion and total equity KRW 530.3 billion at the end of 2025. The debt ratio fell from 11.6% in 2022 to 8.2% in 2025, and operating cash flow rose from KRW 20.7 billion to KRW 43.1 billion over the same period. This gives room to support the profit recovery.

Wider product categories

Based on premium materials and in-house mold development, the company is expanding foldable case and wearable accessory lines. 5G and foldable smartphone launches and rising smartphone penetration in emerging markets are cited as growth opportunities.

The revenue contribution of new products is not separated in disclosures, so the annual report's explanation is the item to check.

09

Bear factors

Track record of margin decline

The operating margin fell from 11.7% in 2022 to 7.9% in 2023 and 4.7% in 2024, and recovered to 7.2% in 2025 but remains below the 2022 level. IBK Investment & Securities pointed out that selling and administrative expenses rose from 32.4% of sales at listing to 58.9% in the first half of 2023. Without a change in cost structure, recovering margins on flat revenue will be difficult.

Intensifying competition

A 2024 brokerage report said the company, lacking its own distribution channels, has struggled to defend margins as low- to mid-priced brands keep entering the market. Rising penetration of lower-priced Chinese products was also cited as a cause of weak industry conditions. If price competition continues, higher unit volumes may not translate into higher margins.

Growth drivers and shareholder return questions

In 2024 IBK Investment & Securities noted that the company had lacked clear new growth drivers and shareholder-friendly policies for an extended period. The same brokerage judged that a real estate purchase was unlikely to strengthen the core business.

How shareholder returns and new business directions are designed after the profit recovery is the next item to check.

10

Risk factors

Platform dependence

Sales are centered on online marketplaces such as Amazon, so changes in platform fees or listing policies flow directly into revenue and margins. With sales spread across more than 52 countries, exposure to country-specific regulation, currency movements and tax burdens also grows. The pace of channel diversification will show up in the revenue mix of the annual report.

Smartphone shipment cycle

Revenue tends to track smartphone unit sales and the timing of new model launches. In 2024, weak global smartphone sales were cited as a cause of industry deterioration. In years when shipments decline, both sales volume and margin defense can come under pressure.

Liquidity and ownership structure

Major shareholders hold 45.06% as of October 13, 2026, and the free-float ratio is 48.83%. Limited tradable supply can increase trading volatility. For minority shareholders, profit distribution policy and any change in ownership structure become important variables.

12

Overall view

Spigen Korea's disclosed results show revenue stuck within roughly KRW 445 billion to KRW 465 billion since 2022, with the operating margin falling from 11.7% to 4.7% before recovering to 7.2% in 2025.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 18.6 billion and profit attributable to owners of the parent of KRW 16.5 billion show that the quarterly recovery has continued.

However, this recovery has not restored the 2022 margin level, and selling and administrative costs and low- to mid-priced competition have been cited as factors weighing on margins. A debt ratio of 8.2% and operating cash flow of KRW 43.1 billion in 2025 act as financial buffers.

On the other hand, the Amazon-centered sales structure and the concentration of major shareholder ownership are risks to weigh together. The share price sits in a range where the discount to net asset value is wide, and market commentary attributes part of this to the absence of clear shareholder return policies.
