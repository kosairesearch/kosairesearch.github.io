Back in the Black in 2025, Q3 2026 Loss Tests the Recovery

02

Business structure

DSR Wire was founded in 1971 to make and sell wire rope and hard steel wire, and it runs a Korean business segment and a Vietnamese business segment.

Its main products are wire rope, hard steel wire, A.C.S.R. and PC strand, and it also runs export and import trading, services, real estate leasing and solar power generation. In March 2025 it acquired a stake in the Vietnamese unit DSR CABLE VINA Co., Ltd. to extend its overseas footprint.

Hard steel wire serves as an intermediate material for automobiles, mining, fishing, machinery and other industries. The company has placed new product development and a higher share of high-value products at the center of its sales strategy. Among domestic wire makers, Kiswire is cited as the market share leader.

In 2024, exports grew despite weakness in key domestic industries and lifted sales. The company's controlling shareholder is described as the chief executive, who held a 23.59% stake as of February 2026. Demand spans domestic industrial users and export markets, so the drivers behind earnings swings are varied.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 261.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 213 billion in 2023 (down 18.6% year on year), then rose to KRW 215.6 billion in 2024 (up 1.2%) and KRW 226.6 billion in 2025 (up 5.1%), growing two years in a row from 2024 through 2025. 2022 was the largest sales year in the period and 2023 the smallest.

The Steel Times reported in 2023 that 2022 was the company's best result since its founding.

Operating profit went from KRW 26.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 23.5 billion in 2023 (down 10.9%) and KRW 19.5 billion in 2024 (down 17.0%), then recovered to KRW 28 billion in 2025 (up 43.7%). 2025 was the largest operating profit year in the period and 2024 the smallest.

In 2024, exports lifted sales, but higher selling and administrative expenses cut operating profit, and a larger equity-method loss turned the net result into a loss, with the company citing a employee welfare fund contribution as a factor in the expense increase.

In 2025, net income attributable to controlling shareholders returned to a KRW 24.7 billion profit, and the company cited lower employee welfare fund contributions and smaller impairment losses on equity-method investments as the main reasons.

The operating margin moved from 10.1% in 2022 to 11.0% in 2023 and 9.1% in 2024, then reached 12.4% in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 20.6 billion in 2022, KRW 41.7 billion in 2023, KRW 6.22 billion in 2024 and KRW 16.6 billion in 2025, with wide swings.

Quarterly, sales of KRW 55.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 8.39 billion in Q3 2025 gave way to Q4 2025 operating profit of KRW 5.87 billion (down 30.1% from the prior quarter).

Q1 2026 operating profit was KRW 6.57 billion, and Q2 2026 recovered to sales of KRW 64.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 7.72 billion.

In Q3 2026, sales reached KRW 61 billion (up 10.3% year on year), but operating profit fell to KRW 6.78 billion (down 19.2% year on year) and results attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a KRW 3.2 billion net loss. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 24.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The wire segment is sensitive to construction and industrial demand, so its earnings cycle is wide.

In the first half of 2023, operating profit at six domestic wire makers fell 35.2% in aggregate, and The Steel Times reported that DSR Wire's sales fell 16.2% over the same period while lower cost of sales kept its operating profit intact.

Export markets remain under pressure from low-priced producers in emerging economies, which has left demand flat. Results across the steel sector diverged in Q3 2026. SeAH Steel posted an operating profit of KRW 20.2 billion, turning positive from a year earlier, in a preliminary disclosure.

Dongkuk Steel Mill's sales rose 21.2% year on year to KRW 932.3 billion, but its net income fell 38.0%, and the company said it expanded long-product sales to meet demand from semiconductor plants and AI data centers.

Daehan Steel recorded an operating loss of KRW 1.42 billion in Q3 2026, turning into a loss from a year earlier. Results differ by customer base and product even within the same industry.

DSR Wire's focus on wire rope and hard steel wire suggests it is more directly exposed to construction and industrial intermediate demand and export price competition than to large project demand.

06

Outlook

The company first disclosed its corporate value-up plan on March 27, 2026, and after a board resolution on October 21, 2026 it re-disclosed a version with more detailed targets and action plans on October 22. Short-term targets are set for fiscal 2027 and medium-term targets for fiscal 2029.

The execution plan centers on robotics and AI-driven unmanned operation and automation to cut manufacturing costs, plus a broader mix of high-value products to lift the operating margin. Expanding customers and developing new items are also presented as growth measures.

The plan includes process automation aimed at lowering defect rates. On shareholder returns, the company said it will check whether it meets the separate-taxation requirements for high-dividend companies and is considering a longer-term increase in dividend size.

The 2025 payout ratio was 25.1%, and the total dividend amount rose from KRW 1.44 billion for 2024 to KRW 6.192 billion for 2025. Annual evaluation of target achievement is under review for implementation from 2027, so the monitoring framework is still at the review stage.

The compliance rate on key indicators tied to the PBR improvement plan is recorded at 40%. Overseas expansion through the Vietnamese subsidiary is a parallel task. Whether the plan translates into a higher operating margin will be visible in the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, which is the next checkpoint.

08

Bull factors

Profit recovery in 2025

Operating profit rose 43.7% to KRW 28 billion in 2025, and the operating margin of 12.4% exceeded the 10.1% in 2022 and 11.0% in 2023. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a 2024 loss to a KRW 24.7 billion profit.

The company cited lower employee welfare fund contributions and smaller impairment losses on equity-method investments as reasons for the improvement.

Cost-reduction plan and shift to high-value products

The value-up plan sets goals for lowering manufacturing costs and defect rates through robotics, AI-driven unmanned operation and process automation. It also presents a portfolio shift centered on high-value specialty product lines. If executed, room for improvement in the operating margin could show up in operating metrics.

Stated plan to expand shareholder returns

The company stated that it qualifies as a high-dividend company, and its 2025 payout ratio was 25.1%. Total dividends rose from KRW 1.44 billion for 2024 to KRW 6.192 billion for 2025. The disclosure states that dividend size will be increased on the back of business performance growth.

09

Bear factors

Q3 2026 swing to a loss

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 6.78 billion, down 19.2% year on year, and results attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a profit a year earlier to a KRW 3.2 billion net loss. Sales rose 10.3% to KRW 61 billion, but profit did not follow. The four-quarter total of KRW 24.4 billion includes this loss.

Slow recovery in sales scale

Sales of KRW 226.6 billion in 2025 remained below KRW 261.7 billion in 2022. After falling to KRW 213 billion in 2023, sales grew in both 2024 and 2025, but the recovery has been modest. Operating cash flow also dropped to KRW 6.22 billion in 2024.

Export price competition and mixed sector results

Export demand is described as stagnating under low-priced competition from emerging-economy producers. In Q3 2026, Dongkuk Steel Mill's net income fell 38.0% year on year, and Daehan Steel swung to an operating loss. Signals across the sector point to a difficult operating backdrop.

Risk factors

Plan execution and target achievement risk

The value-up plan sets direction, but whether and how much dividends will rise has not been decided. Annual evaluation of target achievement is still under review from 2027, so an external check on results is not yet in place. Savings from automation may take time to appear in unit costs.

Earnings volatility risk

Controlling-shareholder results posted a KRW 28.3 billion net loss in 2024, and operating profit fell 30.1% from the prior quarter in Q4 2025. Q3 2026 then turned to a KRW 3.2 billion net loss. Wide quarter-to-quarter swings leave earnings continuity weak.

Export pricing and ownership concentration risk

If low-priced competition from emerging economies keeps pushing export prices down, the premise of the profitability plan could weaken. The chief executive's 23.59% stake signals high ownership concentration, which limits the influence of minority shareholders. How the Vietnamese expansion is reflected in consolidated results also needs confirmation.

Overall view

DSR Wire showed a profit recovery in 2025, with operating profit of KRW 28 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 24.7 billion, and the operating margin rose to 12.4%.

However, in Q3 2026 sales grew to KRW 61 billion while operating profit fell to KRW 6.78 billion and controlling-shareholder results posted a KRW 3.2 billion net loss. Viewed alongside the 2024 net loss, the continuity of earnings is still at an early stage of verification.

The value-up plan covers robotics and AI automation, a larger share of high-value products and a dividend increase, but whether and how much dividends rise has not been fixed. A chief executive stake of 23.59% also marks a concentrated ownership structure.

Within the same sector, Q3 2026 results split between SeAH Steel, which turned profitable, and Daehan Steel, which swung to a loss, so the direction of the cycle is not uniform.

The next checkpoints are preliminary fiscal 2026 results, the quarterly profit explanations in the 2026 annual report, and the 2027 dividend decision and Q1 2027 results, with the operating margin in the next periodic report the most direct test.
