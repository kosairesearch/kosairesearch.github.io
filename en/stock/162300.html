Shin Steel: Sales Rebound, Losses Return

Shin Steel's sales recovered through the second quarter of 2026, but the third quarter swung to an operating loss, and the operating margin has trended lower since 2022.

02

Business structure

Shin Steel processes and distributes steel sheet to customer specifications, and supplying steel for home appliances is the core of the business.

Based on 2024 figures, product sales mix was color-coated steel 52.9%, galvanized steel 16.5%, cold-rolled steel 3.8%, off-grade products 10%, heavy plate 1.9% and other 14.9%.

Per a Hana Securities report from December 2024, segment sales mix was home appliances 71.8%, steel distribution 9.2%, construction materials 9.2%, shipbuilding 2.4%, automotive 1.0% and other 6.4%.

Its main customers are home appliance makers that select the company as a first-tier vendor, and a securities report from early 2024 described appliance customers as about 70% of total sales.

The company has traded with LG Electronics' overseas units since 2007, and overseas sales were about 78% of the total in the first quarter of 2024. Hana Securities expected the 2025 country mix in a December 2025 report to be Thailand 30%, Korea 24%, United States 11%, Mexico 5%, Turkey 5%, India 4% and other 21%.

Overseas production expanded with the completion of a second plant in Thailand and the completion of a Mexico plant, and the Mexico unit's capacity-based sales scale was presented as roughly KRW 110 billion to KRW 120 billion.

Competition depends on the overseas production footprint and local processing capability of appliance customers, and the company is more concentrated in a few end-markets than large steelmakers that serve automotive and construction.

Under this structure, appliance market conditions and utilization at overseas units are the key variables for sales and profit.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales were KRW 381 billion in 2022, KRW 323.8 billion in 2023, KRW 386.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 352.2 billion in 2025, with 2024 the largest year and 2023 the smallest in the 2022 to 2025 period. Sales rose 19.4% in 2024 and then fell 8.9% in 2025.

Operating profit dropped from KRW 15.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.05 billion in 2025 and declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, with declines of 17.8% in 2023, 19.8% in 2024 and 70.9% in 2025.

The operating margin fell from 4.2% in 2022 to 4.0% in 2023, 2.7% in 2024 and 0.9% in 2025, and a 2024 press report pointed to cost-of-sales pressure as a factor in lower profitability.

Controlling-shareholder net income rose from KRW 5.89 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.42 billion in 2023 and KRW 6.51 billion in 2024, then fell sharply to KRW 1.34 billion in 2025, down 79.5%.

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 115.3 billion and total equity was KRW 85.1 billion, and the debt ratio fell from 183.3% in 2024 to 135.5% in 2025. Operating cash flow improved from negative KRW 12.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.26 billion in 2023, KRW 3.03 billion in 2024 and KRW 15.9 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, operating profit recovered to KRW 877 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 739 million in the second quarter of 2026, after KRW 476 million in the first quarter of 2026.

In the third quarter of 2026, sales were KRW 78.2 billion, down 22.6% from the prior quarter and 8.4% from a year earlier, the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.23 billion, and the controlling-shareholder net loss widened to KRW 5.18 billion from KRW 421 million in the second quarter.

Because the loss widened as sales fell, fixed-cost burden looks like the main variable behind earnings swings. The controlling-shareholder net loss for the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 4.56 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Shin Steel's end market is home appliance demand, and one analysis said appliance demand in 2023 was somewhat weaker than in 2022. In 2024, demand for certain appliances such as air conditioners rose and sales increased, and the company said global demand for convenience-oriented appliances would be positive for sales.

Securities analysts at the end of 2024 pointed to lower steel prices and weaker appliance demand, which cut volumes, as the causes of the 2025 sales decline.

The company has said general appliance markets are strongly affected by business cycles and that its high export share makes it more exposed to global economic conditions. US steel markets have seen rising prices.

Nippon Steel raised its fiscal-year net profit forecast on November 5, 2026, citing better results at its US subsidiary, with higher US steel prices mentioned as a backdrop. This trend links to Shin Steel's US sales share, which was expected at 11% in 2025.

Unlike large domestic steelmakers that serve automotive and construction demand at scale, Shin Steel is a mid-sized processor and distributor of appliance steel, so its cycle is best read through appliance market conditions.

Its cycle position can be gauged only by looking at the pace of appliance demand recovery together with US steel price trends.

06

Outlook

In December 2025, the Korea IR Council forecast 2026 annual sales of KRW 418.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 10.3 billion, citing full operation of the Mexico plant as the basis for growth. In the same period, Hana Securities expected the Mexico plant to generate initial 2026 sales of around KRW 40 billion.

Hana Securities said a US RULE8 approval in the first half of 2026 and expanded North American sales by domestic appliance customers were conditions for a rebound, and it also cited higher volumes from the abolition of the US steel quota system as an expected driver.

However, reported 2026 results show sales recovering to KRW 94.9 billion in the first quarter and KRW 100.9 billion in the second, while the third quarter turned to an operating loss of KRW 1.23 billion. The gap between those forecasts and reported results is therefore wide.

Whether the operating loss continues in the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results is the first checkpoint. In the 2026 annual report, the key items are how the operating margin moved from 0.9% in 2025 and whether the Mexico unit's sales contribution is disclosed separately.

The status of the US RULE8 approval and North American sales performance can be checked in the business overview section of the annual report.

Bull factors

Wider overseas production base

Completion of a second plant in Thailand and the Mexico plant broadened the overseas production base. Hana Securities expected the Mexico plant to generate initial 2026 sales of around KRW 40 billion in a December 2025 report.

The Mexico unit's capacity-based sales scale was presented as roughly KRW 110 billion to KRW 120 billion, a potential level once fully operating.

Sales recovery in Q2 2026

Sales in the second quarter of 2026 were KRW 100.9 billion, the largest of the last five quarters, following KRW 94.9 billion in the first quarter, so both quarters exceeded KRW 90 billion. Operating profit in the second quarter was KRW 739 million, up from KRW 476 million in the first quarter.

The bull case holds that if appliance demand recovers alongside overseas operations, sales growth could translate into earnings recovery.

Cash dividend and balance-sheet improvement

At the shareholder meeting on March 26, 2026, the 18th fiscal term (2025) consolidated and separate financial statements were approved and a cash dividend per share was confirmed.

The debt ratio fell to 135.5% at the end of 2025 from 183.3% in 2024, and operating cash flow rose to KRW 15.9 billion from KRW 3.03 billion. If dividend decisions continue during loss-making periods, they could be read as evidence of a commitment to shareholder returns.

Bear factors

Operating loss in Q3 2026

The third quarter of 2026 recorded an operating loss of KRW 1.23 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 5.18 billion, the second consecutive loss-making quarter. The controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters totaled KRW 4.56 billion.

Losses widened in a quarter when sales fell 22.6% from the prior quarter, which the bear case cites as evidence that the sales recovery did not carry through to profit.

Long-term decline in profitability

Controlling-shareholder net income for 2025 was KRW 1.34 billion, down 79.5% from the prior year. A 2024 press report cited cost-of-sales pressure as a factor in lower profitability, and if that cost structure persists, a margin recovery could be limited.

Concentration in appliances and overseas markets

Appliance customers account for about 70% of sales, which makes results sensitive to appliance market swings. Overseas sales were about 78% of the total in the first quarter of 2024, and the company has said its high export share makes it more exposed to global economic conditions. Earnings volatility could increase if currency and trade variables become larger.

Risk factors

Trade and tariffs

The US share of sales was expected at 11% for 2025. Hana Securities cited easing tariff burdens and the abolition of the US steel quota system as expected earnings drivers in a December 2025 report. If trade policy moves differently from those expectations, the timing of higher North American volumes could be delayed.

Raw materials and selling prices

Margins depend on the gap between steel selling prices and raw material costs, so when steel prices fall, profit in a processing-focused business can shrink quickly.

Securities analysts at the end of 2024 cited lower steel prices and weaker appliance demand, which reduced volumes, as the background to the 2025 sales decline. The fall in the operating margin to 0.9% in reported results can be read as a reflection of this pressure.

Financial burden

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 115.3 billion, more than total equity of KRW 85.1 billion. Operating cash flow improved to KRW 15.9 billion in 2025, but if quarterly losses continue, borrowing costs and cash generation could come under pressure again. As a small-cap company, share price swings around earnings releases could also be large.

Overall view

Shin Steel processes and distributes steel sheet for home appliances and has been expanding its overseas production base. Its 2025 annual results were KRW 352.2 billion in sales and KRW 3.05 billion in operating profit.

The operating margin fell from 4.2% in 2022 to 0.9% in 2025, and the third quarter of 2026 brought an operating loss of KRW 1.23 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 5.18 billion, the second straight loss-making quarter.

Positive factors include the possible full operation of the Mexico plant, a sales recovery to KRW 100.9 billion in the second quarter of 2026, a cash dividend approved at the shareholder meeting, and a debt ratio that improved to 135.5% at the end of 2025.

Negative factors include the swing to a third-quarter loss, a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 4.56 billion over the last four quarters, concentration in appliance customers at about 70% of sales, and sensitivity to exchange rates and global conditions through overseas sales.

The price-to-book ratio is below 1x, while the price-to-earnings ratio cannot be calculated because of recent net losses, so the valuation reading depends on whether earnings recover.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results, the operating margin and Mexico unit contribution in the 2026 annual report, and first-quarter 2027 results. Together these disclosures will show whether the sales recovery turns into profit.
