02

Business structure

Wonik IPS supplies deposition and thermal-processing equipment for semiconductor and display manufacturing. The company listed on KOSDAQ in May 2016 and is classified under special-purpose machinery manufacturing.

Semiconductors are the core of revenue, and Meritz Securities put the semiconductor share of Q1 2026 revenue at 88%. In the same analysis, semiconductor parts were estimated at about 21% of semiconductor sales, and a rising parts share was cited as a profitability driver.

The display segment swings widely by quarter, and its Q1 2026 revenue fell from a year earlier. On the customer side, the relationship with Samsung Electronics stands out.

As of October 1, 2026 shareholder records, the largest shareholder Wonik Holdings and related parties held 32.95%, and Samsung Electronics held 7.54%. In competition, Jusung Engineering and PSK are among the names cited as beneficiaries of the same memory capex cycle.

One brokerage view credits the company's deposition tools with high productivity on major customers' mass-production lines. The business centers on front-end semiconductor equipment, so it is sensitive to memory investment cycles.

04

Earnings analysis

In the annual figures filed with DART, revenue fell from KRW 1.01 trillion in 2022 to KRW 690.3 billion in 2023, a 31.8% decline, then rose 8.4% to KRW 748.2 billion in 2024 and 21.6% to KRW 909.8 billion in 2025. 2023 was the low point, revenue rose for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025, and 2022 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit went from KRW 97.6 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 18.1 billion in 2023, turned to a profit of KRW 10.6 billion in 2024, and reached KRW 73.8 billion in 2025, up 593.6%. Operating margin was 9.6% in 2022, -2.6% in 2023, 1.4% in 2024, and 8.1% in 2025.

Net income attributable to owners was KRW 89.4 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 13.5 billion in 2023, KRW 20.7 billion in 2024, and KRW 84 billion in 2025, up 305.0%. Net income matched net income attributable to owners in each year, so no non-controlling interest effect appears on an annual basis.

Operating cash flow rose to KRW 156.6 billion in 2025 from KRW 78.3 billion in 2024. On a quarterly basis, operating profit fell from KRW 36.5 billion in Q2 2025 to KRW 27.5 billion in Q3 and KRW 17.2 billion in Q4, and dropped to KRW 10.7 billion in Q1 2026.

Meritz Securities said the Q1 2026 operating profit was helped by a reversal of inventory valuation allowances. Q2 2026 operating profit was KRW 18.4 billion, up 71.3% from the prior quarter but down 49.6% from a year earlier.

Q2 2026 net income attributable to owners of KRW 89.9 billion far exceeded operating profit, so whether non-operating items or tax effects were one-off should be checked in the regular report.

Preliminary Q3 2026 results, disclosed November 9, 2026, showed revenue of KRW 337.79 billion, up 56.04% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 66.65 billion, up 262.58% from the prior quarter. Net income fell to KRW 3.33 billion, down 96.30% from the prior quarter. Operating profit beat the market consensus of KRW 55.1 billion compiled by Yonhap Infomax by about 21%.

05

Industry analysis

Memory capex sits at the center of equipment demand. In a May 2026 report, Meritz Securities said D-RAM capex rose 42% to USD 47.4 billion in 2025 and is expected to rise another 21% in 2026.

The same report said D-RAM fab revenue recognition would continue through the year, with NAND foundry Taylor fab and Chinese display equipment shipments concentrated in the second half. In November 2025, NH Investment & Securities said memory supply tightness was more likely to persist through 2027.

Front-end equipment makers face a lag between order intake and revenue recognition, so order gains in the first half feed into second-half sales.

In August 2026, SK Securities said revenue recognition from overseas shipments, including to the United States and China, was becoming substantial, and cited expanded supply to CXMT in China as another growth factor. Meritz Securities also expects a NAND and eSSD investment cycle from 2027.

Jusung Engineering and PSK are among the domestic names cited as memory capex beneficiaries. Wonik IPS is classified as a company with a large share of front-end deposition equipment.

Outlook

Brokerage estimates point to a sharp rise in 2026 results. In a May 2026 report, Meritz Securities projected 2026 revenue of KRW 1.3189 trillion and operating profit of KRW 220.4 billion. In an August 2026 report, SK Securities estimated 2026 revenue of KRW 1.274 trillion and operating profit of KRW 189 billion.

The two estimates differ because they come from different reports with different assumptions. Preliminary cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 was KRW 719.16 billion, so reaching the full-year estimates would require a large jump in Q4 revenue.

Preliminary cumulative operating profit for the same period was KRW 95.77 billion. Meritz Securities expects D-RAM and HBM-related investment to drive 2026 growth, with a NAND and eSSD investment cycle adding growth from 2027.

Display equipment shipments to Chinese customers are expected to concentrate in the second half. The investment timing of Samsung Electronics' Pyeongtaek P4 fab and SK hynix's M15X will determine the timing of equipment orders and shipments. The next checkpoint is the order backlog and Q4 revenue recognition in the Q3 2026 regular report.

Bull factors

Memory capex expansion

Analysts say Wonik IPS could benefit as Samsung Electronics' Pyeongtaek P4 and SK hynix's M15X investments gain pace. Meritz Securities sees D-RAM fab revenue recognition continuing through 2026, with a NAND and eSSD cycle adding growth from 2027. The firm expects a stepwise rise in results as equipment shipments concentrate in the second half.

Recovery in earnings power

Operating profit reached KRW 73.8 billion in 2025, up 593.6%, marking a recovery after the 2023 operating loss. Preliminary cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 95.77 billion, up 69.18% from a year earlier. Operating margin improved from -2.6% in 2023 to 8.1% in 2025.

Order intake and overseas shipments

SK Securities cited a rise in order backlog as a basis for second-half earnings improvement in an August 2026 report. Revenue recognition from overseas shipments, including to the United States and China, was also highlighted. Preliminary Q3 2026 revenue rose 25.83% year on year, confirming growth on the top line.

Bear factors

Sharp drop in quarterly net income

Preliminary Q3 2026 net income and net income attributable to owners were each KRW 3.33 billion, down 88.22% from a year earlier. Against KRW 89.9 billion in Q2 2026 net income attributable to owners, the decline is 96.30% from the prior quarter.

Operating profit rose while net income moved the other way, so non-operating items and earnings persistence need to be verified.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Operating profit fell from KRW 36.5 billion in Q2 2025 to KRW 10.7 billion in Q1 2026, and Q1 2026 revenue dropped 40.0% from the prior quarter. Q2 2026 operating profit was down 49.6% from a year earlier.

Because quarterly revenue moves sharply with equipment shipment timing, the quarterly trend has not moved in one direction.

Burden of reaching estimates

Meritz Securities' 2026 revenue estimate of KRW 1.3189 trillion sits well above cumulative preliminary revenue of KRW 719.16 billion for January to September 2026. Reaching it would require a large jump in Q4 revenue.

In August 2026, SK Securities forecast Q3 revenue of KRW 401.1 billion, while the preliminary figure was KRW 337.79 billion.

Risk factors

Customer capex timing

Equipment revenue depends on when customers place orders, build, and accept deliveries. Because Meritz Securities' stepwise growth view assumes shipments concentrate in the second half, a delay in customer investment could push back revenue recognition. Samsung Electronics is also a major shareholder with 7.54% as of October 1, 2026.

Preliminary figures and one-off items

Q3 2026 results are preliminary and unaudited, so they may change in the regular report. Q1 operating profit reflects a reversal of inventory valuation allowances, so the operating profit trend excluding that reversal is the next point to check. Because Q2 2026 net income far exceeded operating profit, whether one-off items played a role matters.

Ownership structure and free float

As of October 1, 2026, the largest shareholder Wonik Holdings and related parties held 32.95%. With a large related-party stake, the number of shares available for trading is limited, which can amplify price moves.

This ownership structure is not a direct earnings variable but is worth considering when reading share price movements.

Overall view

Wonik IPS's preliminary Q3 2026 results show revenue and operating profit rising sharply from a year earlier, indicating that the memory capex benefit is visible at the operating level. In the same preliminary release, however, net income fell 88.22% to KRW 3.33 billion, so results diverge across profit lines.

Annual figures filed with DART show a path from an operating loss in 2023 to a profit in 2024 and a recovery to KRW 73.8 billion in operating profit in 2025. Quarterly operating profit fell from Q2 2025 to KRW 10.7 billion in Q1 2026 before rising again in the Q3 preliminary figures.

Brokerage estimates put 2026 revenue between KRW 1.274 trillion and KRW 1.3189 trillion, which implies a large Q4 revenue step-up from cumulative preliminary levels.

Bullish factors are concentrated memory capex, recovering operating profit, and expanding overseas shipments, while bearish factors are the net income drop, quarterly volatility, and the burden of reaching estimates. With the price-to-book ratio well above 1 times, the durability of earnings is central to valuation.

The next checkpoints are the Q3 2026 quarterly report, due November 14, 2026, and the 2026 annual report, due March 31, 2027.
