Full Utilization in a Tight 8-inch Market; FX and Subsidiaries Are Swing Factors

Full utilization and price increases in 8-inch foundry lifted profit in the second quarter of 2026, while a weaker won and subsidiary results became the key swing factors in the third quarter.

02

Business structure

DB HiTek is a specialist 8-inch (200mm) system semiconductor foundry. Samsung Securities assesses its capacity as second in Korea and within the global top ten. Its core product is BCD power semiconductors, including PMICs, and the product range extends to DDI, image sensors and third-generation SJ MOSFETs.

BCD products accounted for 69% of second-quarter 2026 revenue, up 4 percentage points from a year earlier. Over the same period, industrial products rose from 11% to 19% of revenue while automotive products held at 12%.

Demand is shifting from automotive and industrial uses toward AI data centers, robots and electric vehicles. According to preliminary results released on 12 November 2026, third-quarter foundry segment revenue was KRW 345.3 billion and operating profit was KRW 98 billion.

China accounts for 62% of total revenue and is the largest market. In competition, TSMC and Samsung Electronics are reducing 8-inch capacity as they move to 12-inch advanced nodes, while SMIC and Hua Hong Group (HHG) are concentrating new investment on 12-inch lines.

Additional 8-inch capacity is limited, so utilization and pricing at existing lines drive profitability. Consolidated results also include a fabless subsidiary and another subsidiary whose alloy business was halted in May 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 1.68 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.15 trillion in 2023 (down 31.1% year on year), then KRW 1.13 trillion in 2024 (down 2.0%), before recovering to KRW 1.4 trillion in 2025 (up 23.5%). Of 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 768.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 265.4 billion in 2023 (down 65.5%) and KRW 190.8 billion in 2024 (down 28.1%), then rose to KRW 277.3 billion in 2025 (up 45.3%). The operating margin declined from 45.9% in 2022 to 23.0% in 2023 and 16.9% in 2024, then rose to 19.8% in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent fell from KRW 558.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 264.1 billion in 2023 (down 52.7%) and KRW 229.5 billion in 2024 (down 13.1%), then rose to KRW 256.1 billion in 2025 (up 11.6%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 252.6 billion in 2025, a different figure from profit attributable to owners of the parent. The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 33.4% at the end of 2025 from 19.8% in 2024, while operating cash flow fell from KRW 730.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 357 billion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 80.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 70.4 billion in the fourth quarter (down 12.7% from the prior quarter), then bottomed at KRW 63.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 9.5%) before jumping to KRW 108.4 billion in the second quarter (up 70.2%).

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 90 billion fell 17.0% from the prior quarter but rose 11.7% from a year earlier. DS Investment noted in an April 2026 report that a KRW 22.1 billion one-time welfare fund expense would be booked in the first quarter.

Third-quarter profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 28.5 billion, down 73.6% from the prior quarter and 68.4% from a year earlier, a far steeper decline than the 17.0% drop in operating profit. Profit attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters totaled KRW 284 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The 8-inch foundry segment depends on processes that power management ICs and power semiconductors still rely on, so demand is shifting toward AI servers and electric vehicles. KB Securities analyst Lee Chang-min said AI server power management IC demand is steeply raising 8-inch demand.

On the supply side, TSMC and Samsung Electronics are cutting 8-inch capacity as they move to 12-inch advanced nodes. TrendForce estimated that 8-inch foundry prices rose 5% to 15% in the first quarter of 2026 and could have risen by up to a further 10% in the second quarter.

SMIC and Nexchip each raised 8-inch-centered prices in January and March 2026. China's five-year plan for BCD self-reliance is also cited as a driver of domestic substitution demand. Because Chinese players are concentrating new investment on 12-inch lines, the pace of 8-inch supply expansion is seen as limited.

Samsung Securities maintained its view that the 8-inch market is in a structural upcycle with a prolonged profit cycle. Among mid-sized and smaller 8-inch foundries, DB HiTek's capacity scale and power semiconductor specialization are cited as strengths.

However, an 8-inch wafer yields fewer chips than a 12-inch wafer, so the shift toward higher-value product mix is central to profitability.

06

Outlook

The company is pursuing further price increases in the second half of 2026 and plans to extend them from BCD products to DDI (display driver ICs). It expects 2026 annual utilization of 98% and says it is hard to allocate all the volume customers request.

Capacity will be expanded in stages, taking into account customer wins, technology development and investment capacity. It plans to add high-voltage BCD and MOSFET capacity, broaden applications to data centers, robots and drones, and expand new processes such as silicon capacitors and GaN.

In 8-inch silicon carbide, the company said on 9 September 2026 that reliability verification of its 1200V SiC MOSFET process is complete, with mass production targeted for 2027. It will first offer the process to existing partner customers and extend foundry service to all customers from the second quarter of 2027.

The company targets raising the share of high-value revenue from AI data centers, automotive and industrial applications from 24% in 2025 to 60% by 2030, with total revenue of KRW 1.8 trillion.

Samsung Securities estimated fourth-quarter 2026 revenue at KRW 421 billion and operating profit at KRW 111.3 billion; these are broker estimates, not reported results. The won-dollar rate was in the low 1300s as of November 2026, down from 1,420 in the third quarter.

Samsung Securities said this decline would offset the fourth-quarter price-increase effect and expects one to two more industry-wide price increases next year.

08

Bull factors

Shrinking 8-inch Supply and Price Increases

Reduced 8-inch capacity at major players and rising demand for AI server power management ICs have combined to keep supply tight. DB HiTek raised prices twice, in March and July 2026, and is pursuing further increases in the second half. Third-quarter utilization was effectively 100%, a fully loaded fab. The company sees Chinese fabless customers' chip localization as adding BCD and MOSFET orders.

Rising Share of Higher-Value Products

BCD products reached 69% of second-quarter 2026 revenue, up from a year earlier, and industrial products rose from 11% to 19%. The company has set a target of 60% high-value revenue share by 2030. A shift to a higher-priced product mix widens room for operating margin improvement. The operating margin recovered to 19.8% in 2025 from 16.9% in 2024.

A New Growth Axis in SiC Foundry

The company said on 9 September 2026 that it has completed reliability verification of an 8-inch 1200V SiC MOSFET process, which it describes as a world first. Mass production is targeted for 2027, with service expanding to all customers from the second quarter of 2027.

The company says design kits can cut customers' development time by more than a year. Whether this becomes a separate growth axis from the silicon foundry business depends on mass production and customer wins.

09

Bear factors

A Weaker Won Weighed on Third-Quarter Profit

Samsung Securities said third-quarter operating profit of KRW 90 billion fell short of market expectations, as cost increases from the weaker won outweighed the effect of price increases. Shares fell more than 16% in early trading right after the results were released on 12 November 2026. Samsung Securities expects the won's decline to offset price gains again in the fourth quarter.

China Concentration and Policy Variables

China accounts for 62% of revenue, so regional concentration is high. China's BCD self-reliance policy and local fabless chip localization add orders, but if policy or customer structure shifts, volume allocation could change as well. How far price increases are accepted by Chinese customers will be central to future margins.

Subsidiary Results and Rising Debt

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 33.4% at the end of 2025 from 19.8% in 2024, with total liabilities of KRW 748.6 billion. On a consolidated basis, the fabless subsidiary may see lower profitability, and the halted alloy business reduces consolidated revenue.

Third-quarter profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 28.5 billion, a wide gap from operating profit of KRW 90 billion.

10

Risk factors

Pricing and Cycle Risk

If price increases dampen demand, or if Chinese players' equipment conversion adds supply, the current market balance could shift. Broker expectations for further increases rest on the assumption that the upcycle continues, so a change in that assumption could force revisions to pricing and utilization estimates.

Execution Risk on New Businesses

Mass production of 8-inch SiC is targeted for 2027, and the 2030 goals of 60% high-value revenue share and KRW 1.8 trillion in revenue are long-term plans. Because capacity expansion will proceed in stages depending on customer wins and technology progress, timing could slip.

FX and Financial Risk

Won-dollar fluctuations can offset the effect of price increases, as already seen in the third quarter. With the debt-to-equity ratio rising to 33.4% at the end of 2025, changes in financial capacity can be tracked through regular report figures. Subsidiary earnings swings that move consolidated profit are another factor to weigh.

12

Overall view

DB HiTek is a company whose operating profit rose to KRW 108.4 billion in the second quarter of 2026 as 8-inch supply tightness, higher utilization and price increases came together.

Based on reported results, operating profit recovered in 2025 from the 2024 low to KRW 277.3 billion, but it remains below KRW 768.7 billion in 2022.

Third-quarter operating profit was KRW 90 billion, but profit attributable to owners of the parent fell to KRW 28.5 billion amid the weaker won and subsidiary factors, so the trend was mixed.

Positive factors are supply tightness, the durability of price increases, a growing share of higher-value products and the new SiC business. Negative factors are sensitivity to the won, concentration in China, and subsidiary results alongside a rising debt-to-equity ratio.

Broker target prices have been cut, with Samsung Securities presenting KRW 130,000 (13 November 2026) and KB Securities KRW 160,000 (11 November 2026), and both cited the weaker won for third-quarter results falling short. This report offers no investment opinion.

The next checkpoints are preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results, the 2026 annual report and the expansion of SiC service in the second quarter of 2027.
