02

Business structure

MakinaRocks is a physical AI company whose core product is Runway, an AI operating system that runs AI inside closed-network environments such as factories, power facilities and defense systems.

Founded in 2017, the company focuses on vertical AI solutions tailored to industrial sites and is shifting its model toward a platform built around Runway. Revenue to date has mainly come from project-based AI solution deliveries and services.

Korea Investment & Securities expects platform product revenue to eventually exceed service revenue, and argues that product revenue carries lower fixed-cost burden, which could widen margin improvement.

By sector, first-half 2026 new orders were reported as 27% defense and aerospace, 23% heavy industry, 22% advanced manufacturing and 21% general manufacturing. Named major customers include Samsung Electronics and Hyundai Motor.

Reference projects include an generative AI rollout at K-water smart water treatment plants and AI automation of vehicle electronics testing at Denso Korea.

According to an IR note reported by securities media, the company cited 25TB of process data, 6,000 real-operation AI cases and a 97% re-contract rate as evidence of a customer lock-in structure.

In industrial AI, global big tech firms and domestic IT services and software companies are rivals, and MakinaRocks positions closed-network operation and field-verified references as its differentiators. How quickly that differentiation turns into recognized revenue depends on inspection and billing schedules.

03

Recent trends

Disclosed Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 7.23 billion, up 213.8% from the same period a year earlier. Operating loss for the same period was KRW 4.39 billion, with losses widening year on year, while net loss was KRW 4.33 billion, narrower than the prior year.

Recent media reports put cumulative January–September revenue at KRW 14.3 billion, above the 2025 full-year revenue of KRW 11.5 billion, and Q3 standalone revenue at KRW 7.2 billion.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 3.0 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 4.0 billion in Q2 and KRW 7.2 billion in Q3, with the growth step-up concentrated in Q3.

First-half 2026 consolidated revenue was KRW 7.0 billion, up 168% from KRW 2.6 billion a year earlier, and the operating loss narrowed about 28% to KRW 4.2 billion from KRW 5.9 billion. Reports also indicate that cumulative operating expenses rose 70% against 190% revenue growth.

Based on 2025 figures, full-year revenue was KRW 11.5 billion and operating loss was KRW 8.0 billion, an operating margin of -70.2%.

On the financing side, the May 2026 KOSDAQ listing raised KRW 38.4 billion, cash and equivalents at end-H1 stood at KRW 44.1 billion, and the debt ratio fell from 53% before listing to 12%.

As of 11:52 KST on November 13, 2026, the share price was KRW 13,310 (down 2.42% from the previous close) with a market capitalization of KRW 236.8 billion.

04

Outlook

The company has described a structure in which final inspections and billing concentrate in the second half after first-half deployment, so revenue recognition is also weighted toward H2. Media reports indicate the full-year guidance is maintained on that premise.

Korea Investment & Securities expects strong revenue growth backed by the order backlog and margin improvement from the shift to a product-based model, and it presented 2027 as the target year for turning profitable.

The same brokerage noted that the high ratio of shares released from lock-up could keep share price volatility elevated for some time. Because the company has not disclosed the detailed first-half order amount, the rate at which backlog converts into revenue cannot be quantified from current disclosures.

The share of Runway-based product revenue in total sales is also limited in public figures, so it remains an item to confirm in the revenue breakdown of periodic reports. Defense budget execution timing and manufacturing capital spending trends are external variables that could affect when revenue is recognized. Whether the pace of loss reduction and revenue growth continues in parallel is the core path toward breakeven.

05

Bull factors

Accelerating revenue growth

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 3.0 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 7.2 billion in Q3, with growth concentrated in the third quarter. This can be read as first-half order build-up flowing into Q3 revenue, and the pace at which orders convert to revenue in later quarters is the next point to check.

Wider financial capacity

The May 2026 KOSDAQ listing raised KRW 38.4 billion, and cash and equivalents stood at KRW 44.1 billion at the end of H1 2026. The debt ratio fell from 53% before listing to 12%, and receivables declined from KRW 4.2 billion at end-2025 to KRW 1.3 billion. The company said this balance sheet supports large, long-term project execution.

Platform shift and customer lock-in

Korea Investment & Securities expects Runway-centered product revenue to eventually exceed service revenue. The brokerage argues product revenue carries lower fixed-cost burden, which could widen margin gains. The company cited a 97% re-contract rate and 25TB of process data as evidence of customer lock-in.

06

Bear factors

Losses still structural

Operating loss in Q3 2026 was KRW 4.39 billion and net loss was KRW 4.33 billion, so losses persist. The 2025 operating loss was KRW 8.0 billion, an operating margin of -70.2%. Loss ratios improved because operating cost growth trailed revenue growth, but the breakeven timing is only presented as a brokerage estimate.

Dependence on inspection and billing timing

The company has said final inspections and billing for defense and manufacturing projects concentrate in H2. Because revenue recognition depends on inspection completion, schedule slippage could make quarterly revenue more volatile.

The detailed first-half order amount has not been disclosed, which makes the conversion speed from orders to revenue hard to gauge from outside.

Lock-up expiries and share price volatility

Korea Investment & Securities noted that the high ratio of shares released from lock-up could keep share price volatility elevated for a period. As of 11:52 KST on November 13, 2026, the share price was KRW 13,310, down 2.42% from the prior close.

At a market capitalization of KRW 236.8 billion, changes in tradable supply can feed directly into price movements.

07

Risk factors

Macro

The pace of defense and public budget execution and manufacturing capital spending can affect when projects pass inspection. If interest rates and slowing growth delay private manufacturers' AI adoption, conversion of the order backlog into revenue could also slip.

Because revenue is concentrated in a few sectors, the company is exposed to changes in the external environment.

Competition and technology

In industrial AI, global big tech companies and domestic IT services and software firms compete for the same customers. Rapid AI technology change requires continued investment to upgrade the platform.

The company said it will direct proceeds toward core product upgrades and new market expansion, and whether that spending converts into revenue remains to be seen.

Supply and liquidity

Trading value on November 13, 2026 was KRW 1.6 billion, which is modest in scale. Price swings could widen if selling supply rises after lock-up expiries. The mandatory holding structure for institutionally allocated shares after listing determines the supply pressure at release dates.

08

Overall view

MakinaRocks' disclosed Q3 2026 revenue rose 213.8% year on year, and its order intake and financial capacity have expanded since the listing. However, an operating loss of KRW 4.39 billion and a net loss of KRW 4.33 billion show that the loss structure persists.

Bullish factors center on the pace of revenue growth, first-half order volume, and KRW 44.1 billion in cash with a lower debt ratio. Bearish factors center on continued losses before breakeven, revenue recognition concentrated around inspection and billing timing, and share price volatility from lock-up expiries.

Brokerage views are split between those who expect growth and margin improvement to take hold and those who point to 2027 as the breakeven year. Korea Investment & Securities has presented 2027 as the target year for turning profitable.

This summary is informational, based on disclosed figures and business indicators, and does not express a view on share price direction.

Going forward, the scale of second-half inspection and billing completion, the revenue mix of Runway, and the trend in operating loss will be the main variables that shift the balance between these bullish and bearish factors.
