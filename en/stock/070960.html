02

Business structure

Mona Yongpyong runs a four-season resort based on Mt. Bawang (1,458m) in Daegwallyeong, Pyeongchang, with hotels, condominiums, skiing, golf, a sightseeing cable car, a water park and an MTB park. Its business has two core pillars: resort operations and premium condominium sales.

In 2024 the company changed its name from its former one to Mona Yongpyong and positioned itself as a four-season wellness village. Resort profitability is being pursued through room and product pricing discipline, channel-level profit monitoring and operating efficiency.

In Q1 2026, with almost no condo sales, Edaily estimated that KRW 47.3 billion of the KRW 47.7 billion in revenue came from resort operations. Winter demand centers on the ski slopes and lift passes, while summer and autumn demand is filled by MTB events, the Mt.

Bawang cable car, the thousand-year yew forest trail and seasonal programs. In July 2026 the wellness center reopened after renovation with expanded indoor and outdoor programs.

Overseas sales were concentrated in Taiwan and China, and the company plans to expand into Southeast Asia, Australia and New Zealand, and the Americas. Condominium sales are the core of the development business, and new condo revenue recognition was limited in the first half of 2026. A May 2024 report presented a plan of KRW 1.5 trillion in condominium and resort sales through 2030.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue on a disclosed basis rose from KRW 143.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 211.6 billion in 2023 (up 47.7% year on year) and KRW 267.8 billion in 2024 (up 26.5%), before falling to KRW 250.1 billion in 2025 (down 6.6%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.55 billion in 2022 to KRW 21.4 billion in 2023 (up 1,278.9%), then KRW 27 billion in 2024 (up 26.0%) and KRW 23.3 billion in 2025 (down 13.7%). The operating margin was 1.1% in 2022 and 10.1% in 2023, 10.1% in 2024 and 9.3% in 2025, staying near 10% after 2023.

Attributable net income turned from a net loss of KRW 12.1 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 10 billion in 2023, fell to KRW 8 billion in 2024 (down 20.0%), and swung to a net loss of KRW 30.6 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests is a different figure and was KRW 7.95 billion in 2024.

The debt ratio was 183.4% at end-2025, up from 129.9% in 2022, with total liabilities of KRW 665.1 billion and total equity of KRW 362.7 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 131.1 billion in 2023 but outflows followed, at -KRW 104 billion in 2024 and -KRW 20.8 billion in 2025.

In quarterly terms, Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 48.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.52 billion gave way to Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 42.1 billion (down 12.4% quarter on quarter) and an operating loss of KRW 487 million.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 47.7 billion (up 13.3%) with operating profit of KRW 9.37 billion, a return to profit, and Edaily analyzed the 19.6% margin as a likely one-off effect of the pause in upfront condo costs.

Q2 2026 revenue fell to KRW 34 billion (down 28.7%), with an operating loss of KRW 8.19 billion and an attributable net loss of KRW 9.23 billion, and the company attributed this to property taxes and other fixed costs concentrated in the quarter.

Q3 2026 revenue rose to KRW 51.5 billion (up 7.2% year on year) and operating profit to KRW 7.36 billion (up 62.8%), and the company cited the recognition of sales from remaining condo units left over from the off-season along with cost cuts. Attributable net losses over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 43.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic leisure and tourism is shifting from expansion toward defending profitability as high prices and weaker consumer spending persist. In Q3 2026, Mona Yongpyong's operating profit growth exceeded its revenue growth even without new condo sales, which reads as a resort-operations response to this environment.

The condo market faces lingering risk of tighter project financing for real estate developers as high interest rates persist, and rising construction materials and labor costs pressure condo margins.

Shifting revenue from a winter-concentrated ski profile toward four-season earnings is the direction of the restructuring the company has been pursuing. The company combines the natural resources of Mt.

Bawang with hotels, condominiums, golf and a cable car, and in August 2026 it won the resort category of a Korean consumer trust brand award. The UCI MTB World Series held near Mt. Bawang in early May 2026 reportedly drew teams from more than 40 countries and about 13,000 spectators.

Its earnings structure differs from casino-centered listed peers such as Kangwon Land and Grand Korea Leisure, so comparison benchmarks within the sector diverge.

06

Outlook

The 2026/27 ski season targets a late-November opening, and the first snowmaking began on October 22, 2026. For the winter operation, the company plans to restructure lift passes into 4-, 6- and 8-hour options, move the slope opening earlier to 8:30 a.m., and shorten the groomed-run time to 1 hour 30 minutes.

The company said it will strengthen winter peak-season operations and attract overseas visitors. For the second half, it plans to improve summer and autumn operating results, expand four-season content around Mt. Bawang and tighten room and product pricing.

Overseas sales are to extend to Southeast Asia, Australia and New Zealand, and the Americas, alongside partnerships with travel agencies by country and global online travel platforms.

On condos, the company is preparing to push ahead with the Olympic Village Phase 2 project at year-end, with procedures moving in sequence. One brokerage official viewed early subscription rates for year-end premium condo sales and control of rising construction costs as the watershed for long-term growth.

As of a May 2024 report, the Goryeong Comprehensive Theme Park was introduced as a project nearing construction start. The next checkpoint is the annual operating margin and the timing of condo sales recognition in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Improving resort profitability

The Q3 2026 operating margin rose 4.9 percentage points to 14.3% from 9.4% a year earlier. The company attributed this to room and product pricing, channel cost management and expense cuts. Operating profit stayed in the black even without new condo revenue, driven by resort operations.

Four-season brand and event draw

On August 27, 2026 the company won the resort category of a Korean consumer trust brand award. The push to broaden demand beyond skiing matches its shift toward a four-season resort model.

Condo pipeline restart

The company is sequentially advancing procedures for the Olympic Village Phase 2 project, targeted for year-end 2026. It aims to fill the gap in new condo sales and lift medium-term earnings capacity. A May 2024 report presented a condo development scale of KRW 1.5 trillion through 2030.

Bear factors

Condo gap and shrinking revenue

Edaily reported that Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 47.7 billion fell about 18.2% from KRW 58.3 billion a year earlier. Preliminary first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 81.7 billion was affected by limited recognition of new condo sales. Annual revenue also fell 6.6% in 2025.

Persistent losses and balance-sheet strain

Attributable net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 43.9 billion. The debt ratio was 183.4% at end-2025, and operating cash flow was -KRW 20.8 billion in 2025. Q2 2026 swung back to an attributable net loss of KRW 9.23 billion.

Questions over the durability of margins

Edaily analyzed the 19.6% Q1 2026 operating margin as likely a one-off effect of the pause in upfront condo costs. The company said the KRW 8.19 billion Q2 2026 operating loss reflected seasonal concentration of fixed costs.

Part of the Q3 2026 improvement came from recognizing remaining condo units, so the share of non-recurring factors deserves attention.

Risk factors

Condo development risk

Prolonged high interest rates keep concerns over project financing for real estate developers alive. Rising construction materials and labor costs directly pressure condo margins. If early subscription rates for year-end sales fall short, the benefit of the condo restart could be delayed.

Seasonality and weather risk

Resort earnings depend heavily on winter peak-season visitors and weather. The company said Q2 2026 profitability temporarily slowed as property taxes and other fixed costs concentrated in the quarter. The late-November opening target may also shift with temperature, humidity and other conditions.

Balance-sheet risk

Total liabilities of KRW 665.1 billion at end-2025 exceed total equity of KRW 362.7 billion. Operating cash flow was -KRW 104 billion in 2024 and -KRW 20.8 billion in 2025. Until condo sales restart, how much operating cash generation can ease the debt burden is the key question.

12

Overall view

Mona Yongpyong is a company built on two pillars: four-season resort operations centered on Mt. Bawang and premium condominium sales. In Q3 2026, operating profit rose 62.8% to KRW 7.36 billion and the operating margin reached 14.3%, confirming improved resort profitability in the figures.

The company stated that Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 51.5 billion included recognition of remaining condo units left over from the off-season. Preliminary first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 81.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.2 billion reflected the gap in condo sales that reduced the top line.

Attributable net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 43.9 billion, and the attributable net loss for 2025 was KRW 30.6 billion. The debt ratio rose to 183.4% at end-2025 from 129.9% in 2022, and operating cash flow was negative in both 2024 and 2025.

Strengths include recovering resort profitability, four-season content and brand recognition, and the Olympic Village Phase 2 project, while weaknesses include the condo sales gap, continuing net losses and a high debt ratio.

Against this balance, the next points to track are results from the 2026/27 winter season, whether year-end condo sales proceed, and full-year results in the 2026 annual report.
