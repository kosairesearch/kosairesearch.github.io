02

Business structure

Bitmax is a KOSDAQ-listed company (stock code 377030) built around augmented reality (AR) software, and its former name was Maxt. Its AR development platform AR SDK, commercialized in 2012, is described in a Valueline company overview as used by more than 30,000 developers in over 50 countries.

The platform business centers on an industrial AR solution called MAXWORK and a digital-twin construction platform applied across real estate, construction and manufacturing.

In July 2025 the company set up an SI division after absorbing its subsidiary IL4U, and this unit provides MES, PLM and MIS system construction and operation services to large electronics manufacturers.

The company says it applies AR SDK v6.0 to manufacturing and logistics systems and expects a data-analysis technology that predicts supply-chain risk to spread through its existing customer network.

The SI business rests on IT services that build and run production, development and management information systems for manufacturing clients. Because the company includes holding digital assets such as bitcoin in its business scope, price swings in those assets can affect its financial indicators.

Media reports based on electronic disclosure data said that on May 30, 2025 the company bought 35.63 more bitcoins, bringing its total to 230.26.

Competition runs along two lines: in AR platforms, the size of the developer-tool ecosystem matters, while in manufacturing IT construction, bidding contests with large SI firms such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and POSCO DX Company are the key variable.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 2.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.77 billion in 2023 (down 39.0% year on year) and then jumped to KRW 37.2 billion in 2024 (up 2,005.4% year on year). 2024 was the largest year of the 2022–2025 period, and 2025 revenue of KRW 27.2 billion fell 26.8% year on year.

Operating losses were KRW 10.8 billion in 2022, KRW 16.5 billion in 2023 (losses continued and widened), KRW 18.5 billion in 2024 (losses continued and widened) and KRW 10.9 billion in 2025 (losses continued but narrowed), making 2022 through 2025 four consecutive years of operating losses.

The operating margin moved from -371.9% in 2022 and -935.9% in 2023 to -49.9% in 2024 and -39.9% in 2025, so the period of larger revenue coincided with a smaller loss ratio.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened from KRW 14.9 billion in 2022 and KRW 13.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 26.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 96.2 billion in 2025, while the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 96.4 billion in 2025.

A KRW 10.9 billion operating loss alongside a KRW 96.2 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders means non-operating items were large, and their source is a point to verify in the notes to the annual report.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 6.78 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 6.54 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 6.14 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 4.53 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 4.05 billion in Q3 2026, declining for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026.

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 2.57 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 681 million in Q3 2026, with losses in five consecutive quarters.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 2.04 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 1.66 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 905 million in Q2 2026, and turned into net income of KRW 1.03 billion attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026.

The sum of net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 3.56 billion. Operating cash flow was negative in every year, at KRW -83.6 million... in 2022 at -KRW 8.36 billion, -KRW 21 billion in 2023, -KRW 16.8 billion in 2024 and -KRW 10.5 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Bitmax's revenue demand is tied to investment cycles in high-technology industries such as manufacturing and electronics. A company business summary says its IT services segment is affected by volatility in these industries, and that clients are concentrating spending on improving system operating efficiency.

The decline in revenue since Q4 2025 can be read as overlapping with this demand volatility. The AR platform's downstream markets include spatial data and digital-twin demand from real estate, construction and manufacturing sites, and the size of the developer ecosystem serves as a key gauge of competitiveness.

The manufacturing IT construction market includes large listed SI companies such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and POSCO DX Company, and the SI division created through the July 2025 merger faces the task of building a track record.

The company has also pushed into AI and security, including the launch of an integrated AI and post-quantum security platform called PatchGuard, but whether this new business feeds into revenue is not yet separable in the reported results.

On the digital-asset side, the bitcoin price cycle drives valuation gains and losses. Media reported that purchases were made during a phase of bitcoin price correction. Within its sector, Bitmax is a small-cap company with a large gap in both market value and revenue scale compared with larger peers.

As a result, the sector cycle and digital-asset prices both act as variables for reported earnings at the same time.

Outlook

On November 3, 2026, the board approved a third-party allotment capital increase in kind, in which new common shares totaling 13,961,520 would be issued against a contributed asset valued at KRW 21.56 billion.

The recipient is an affiliated optical-communication component maker (KOSDAQ code 062970), and the company stated that the aim is to strengthen capital and improve its financial structure and stability. Payment is scheduled for December 29, 2026, and the new shares are scheduled to list on January 18, 2027.

The contributed-asset value, the number of shares allotted and the payment date may change depending on the court's review. If completed as planned, that affiliate would become the largest shareholder, with a stake of 49.42% after the acquisition recorded in the filing.

A separate disclosure is planned once the change in largest shareholder is confirmed. The largest-shareholder information differs across filings: one cites an investment partnership at 7.76%, while another cites Meta Platform Investment Partnership at 11.83%, reflecting different reference dates.

A third-party allotment approved on April 28, 2026 covered 2,126,754 common shares and raised KRW 4.99 billion for acquiring securities of another company. A 4-for-1 free capital reduction took effect on April 9, 2026, which also changed the capital structure.

The company has presented applying AR SDK v6.0 and launching a data-analysis-based AX solution as axes of business expansion.

Bull factors

Operating losses narrowing on cost control

Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 681 million was smaller than the KRW 2.57 billion in Q3 2025, and the 2025 annual operating loss of KRW 10.9 billion was narrower than KRW 18.5 billion in 2024. A company business summary attributes the smaller loss to cost reduction and operating efficiency even as revenue fell. The operating margin improved from -49.9% in 2024 to -39.9% in 2025.

Capital strengthening through an in-kind contribution

On November 3, 2026, the board approved a capital increase in kind worth KRW 21.56 billion. The company stated that the purpose is to strengthen capital and improve its financial structure.

Since total equity had fallen to KRW 10.6 billion at the end of 2025, the change in equity after completion will serve as a measure of how much financial strain eases.

Expansion into data analysis on AR technology

The company says it applies AR SDK v6.0 to manufacturing and logistics systems and plans to introduce an AX solution based on data analysis. News of the launch of an integrated AI and post-quantum security platform called PatchGuard was reported in May 2026. Broadening the technology base from AR into data and security is a positive factor.

Bear factors

Revenue down for four consecutive quarters

Revenue has declined for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, and Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 4.05 billion was down 40.2% year on year. Annual revenue also fell from KRW 37.2 billion in 2024 to KRW 27.2 billion in 2025. With the revenue base shrinking while losses narrow, whether revenue recovers is the central point to watch.

Heavy financial structure

At the end of 2025, the debt-to-equity ratio was 909.8%, with total equity of KRW 10.6 billion and total liabilities of KRW 96.5 billion. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 96.2 billion in 2025.

One analysis piece described the financial position as very fragile given continued operating and net losses, and said convertible bond issuance adds financial burden.

Dilution and uncertain control

A third-party allotment in April 2026 issued 2,126,754 shares, and the in-kind capital increase resolved in November would add 13,961,520 shares. The new shares approach the 14,291,702 common shares outstanding before the increase.

The contributed-asset value and share count may change after the court's review, and the largest-shareholder information differs across filings, so the timing of the final control structure is uncertain.

Risk factors

Sensitivity to industry cycles

The IT services segment is affected by volatility in high-technology industries, and clients are concentrating spending on operating efficiency. Because the revenue base is tied to this demand, a prolonged cut in investment could extend revenue declines. Bidding competition with large SI companies is an added burden.

Financing and shareholder dilution

A third-party allotment in April 2026 and an in-kind capital increase in November 2026 followed one another, and convertible bond issuance has also been reported. Repeated fundraising can lead to shareholder dilution and added financial burden.

All of the new shares in the November plan are scheduled for a one-year lock-up, so tradable supply would increase with a time lag.

Digital-asset prices and earnings volatility

The 230.26 bitcoins held are exposed to price swings, and purchases were made during a price correction. If valuation gains and losses on digital assets flow into non-operating results, net income volatility increases. Any analysis of the Q3 2026 turn to net income should also examine this item.

Overall view

Bitmax combines an AR platform, a manufacturing SI business and bitcoin holdings. Its 2025 operating loss of KRW 10.9 billion was narrower than KRW 18.5 billion in 2024, and the Q3 2026 operating loss narrowed to KRW 681 million.

Revenue, however, has declined for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, with Q3 2026 revenue down 40.2% year on year.

The Q3 2026 net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 10.3 billion marks a turn to profit, but it came while an operating loss remained, so its source needs verification. The debt-to-equity ratio of 909.8% and total equity of KRW 10.6 billion at the end of 2025 leave the financial burden heavy.

Strengths include narrower losses, the capital increase under way and broader technology applications, while weaknesses include falling revenue, high leverage, repeated share issuance and dependence on digital-asset prices.

The next checkpoints are the payment of the in-kind capital increase and confirmation of the change in largest shareholder on December 29, 2026, the listing of new shares on January 18, 2027, and the 2026 annual business report due by March 31, 2027.
