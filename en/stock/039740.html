02

Business structure

Korea Information Engineering Services was founded in 1990 and is an IT company that generates most of its business from IT hardware distribution and software development. By business segment, IT accounted for 96%, education services for 2%, and e-commerce solutions and e-commerce for 1% of revenue in Q2 2026.

The distribution-led structure is long-standing, as IT hardware distribution represented 94.02% of consolidated business in 2020. The company operates as a distributor for several vendors, including HPE, HPI, Lenovo, and Microsoft Surface Hub, and that supply relationship is central to the business.

Its customers include public institutions and private companies, and large national projects drive results. The company signed an equipment supply contract in 2025 worth KRW 134.5 billion, equal to 69.22% of that year's revenue.

In 2026 it also won a project worth above KRW 10 billion from the Korea Institute of Ocean Science & Technology (KIOST). On the software side, it offers enterprise database and data infrastructure products, including the Tibero DBMS, and is developing a private enterprise AI chatbot and an AI appliance.

Because large contracts depend on vendor cooperation, the company's relationship with HPE matters for delivery, and the company has described a close collaboration with HPE that delivered large volumes and completed final inspection.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 105.4 billion in 2022, KRW 115.6 billion in 2023 (up 9.7% year on year), KRW 194.1 billion in 2024 (up 68.0% year on year), and KRW 146.3 billion in 2025 (down 24.6% year on year), making 2024 the largest year in the period.

According to the company, annual revenue of KRW 194.0 billion in 2024 was the highest annual result since its founding.

Operating profit fell from KRW 1.84 billion in 2022 to KRW 60.32 million in 2023 (down 96.7% year on year), then went through KRW 620 million in 2024 (up 923.6% year on year) to reach KRW 420 million in 2025 (down 32.0% year on year). The operating margin was 1.7% in 2022, 0.1% in 2023, 0.3% in 2024, and 0.3% in 2025.

Even though revenue rose sharply in 2024, the margin stayed at around 0.3%, so top-line growth did not translate into profitability.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.35 billion in 2022, KRW 2.0 billion in 2023 (down 40.3% year on year), KRW 3.6 billion in 2024 (up 80.2% year on year), and KRW 2.15 billion in 2025 (down 40.3% year on year).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.75 billion in 2025, which should be viewed separately from net income attributable to controlling shareholders.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 52.9 billion, of which KRW 52.0 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 58.5 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 80.2% in 2022 to 110.7% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was highest in 2024 at KRW 9.89 billion and was KRW 3.25 billion in 2025. In the quarterly trend, revenue of KRW 38.2 billion in Q3 2025 fell to KRW 25.0 billion in Q4 2025 (down 34.7% quarter on quarter), then jumped to KRW 170.7 billion in Q1 2026 (up 584.2% quarter on quarter).

Revenue in Q2 2026 was KRW 62.9 billion (down 63.2% quarter on quarter), and Q3 was KRW 71.3 billion (up 13.3% quarter on quarter and up 86.5% year on year). This sharp swing appears to be the result of contract volumes from 2025 being recognized in a concentrated way in Q1.

Operating profit of KRW 36.18 million in Q3 2025 turned into an operating loss of KRW 180 million in Q4 2025 (a swing to a loss from the previous quarter), and then was KRW 1.07 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 450 million in Q2, and KRW 2.22 billion in Q3.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a net loss of KRW 38.26 million in Q3 2025, KRW 2.5 billion in Q4 (a swing to profit from the previous quarter), KRW 730 million in Q1 2026, KRW 1.18 billion in Q2, and KRW 2.04 billion in Q3.

The fact that net income attributable to controlling shareholders came to KRW 2.5 billion in Q4 2025 despite an operating loss is a point where the influence of non-operating items, such as non-operating income and expenses, should also be examined.

On a company-reported basis, cumulative revenue for the first half of 2026 was KRW 233.6 billion and operating profit was KRW 1.5 billion.

Industry analysis

Downstream demand is supported by expanding hardware investment, such as servers and storage, in line with the digital transformation trend. That hardware demand is rising alongside the acceleration of digital transformation is the assessment of a technical analysis report from February 2022.

The company recently declared that it would transform itself into an AI infrastructure specialist, going beyond its existing IT distribution and infrastructure business.

In terms of its standing within its sector, it ranks in the lower middle range by operating margin among the 95 stocks in the electronic equipment/instruments sector. Its market capitalization and revenue size are also in the lower-middle range within the sector.

In the competitive landscape, the company's differentiator is that it combines a vendor master-distributor structure with its own software lineup. However, because revenue scale varies with large government-led projects, the stability of the order pipeline is the key factor when compared with peer distributors.

There are expectations that, as references from large projects accumulate, securing references through these results will help drive the restructuring of its business.

Conversely, project-based revenue swings widely from quarter to quarter, so the timing of individual contracts, rather than the industry cycle, determines quarterly results.

Outlook

The company has indicated its direction of maintaining a growth trend in the second half of 2026 as well, continuing to enhance shareholder value and set new earnings records. A direction has been presented, but the specific numerical targets that can be confirmed in public materials are limited.

Because revenue in Q2 2026 fell from the previous quarter, revenue in Q4 2026 and in 2027 depends on the size and recognition timing of new orders.

An order disclosure has already been made on May 26, 2026, in the form of a Single Sales/Supply Contract Execution filing, so the frequency of disclosures of large contracts serves as an indicator of the earnings trend.

On the business side, there is a plan to secure a new revenue model through its own software, such as an enterprise private AI chatbot and an AI appliance. The share of revenue and profit that this new business accounts for is not yet sufficiently visible in the disclosed results.

Given the precedent that the Q4 2025 results disclosure came out on March 19, 2026, the annual report containing full-year 2026 results is likely to be filed in March 2027. At that point, the financial points to check are how operating cash flow and the debt-to-equity ratio have changed from their 2025 levels.

It is also necessary to look at whether the operating margin stays at around 0.3% and whether quarterly profits carry through to the full year. How quickly revenue is backfilled by other orders after the large projects is the key variable for the 2027 trend.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly earnings recovery

Attributable net income rose for two consecutive quarters in Q2 and Q3 2026, reaching KRW 2.04 billion in Q3. The company reported first-half 2026 results as its largest since its founding. The last four quarters total KRW 6.44 billion, a change from the loss-making stretch in 2025.

AI infrastructure shift and in-house software

The company has declared a shift toward an AI infrastructure business and offers in-house solutions, including the Tibero enterprise DBMS. A private enterprise AI chatbot and an AI appliance are presented as new revenue models. If software accounts for a larger share of revenue, the margin structure could change.

Accumulating large public-sector references

The company delivered and completed final inspection of a KRW 134.5 billion equipment contract signed in 2025, working with its vendor. Such references may matter in future public-sector bids.

09

Bear factors

Revenue volatility from contract concentration

Revenue fell from KRW 170.7 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 62.9 billion in Q2, down 63.2% quarter on quarter. Revenue also dropped to KRW 25 billion in Q4 2025. Because revenue follows the timing of large contracts, quarterly results are hard to forecast.

Thin operating margin and profit declines

The operating margin was 0.3% in both 2024 and 2025. Operating profit fell 32.0% to KRW 420 million in 2025, and attributable net income fell 40.3% to KRW 2.15 billion. Without a margin gain, revenue growth adds limited value to shareholders.

Rising financial leverage

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 76.6% in 2024 to 110.7% in 2025. At year-end 2025, total liabilities of KRW 58.5 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 52.9 billion. Operating cash flow also fell from KRW 9.89 billion in 2024 to KRW 3.25 billion in 2025.

10

Risk factors

Contract concentration risk

Much of revenue comes from large public and national projects, so a delay or cancellation of one contract can swing quarterly results sharply. The 2025 contract lifted Q1 2026 revenue and was followed by a sharp drop in Q2, illustrating this pattern. Continuity of new orders is the key issue.

Vendor dependence risk

The distribution business depends on distributor status with overseas vendors such as HPE. Changes in vendor product policy or supply terms directly affect the revenue structure. Delivering large volumes with vendor cooperation is a strength but also raises dependence.

Business transition and financial burden

The shift to an AI infrastructure business has not yet shown up clearly in reported revenue from new software. With the debt-to-equity ratio at 110.7%, more transition spending could add financial strain. The small market capitalization also means share price and liquidity can be volatile.

What to watch next

  1. Around mid-March 2027

    Publication of the 2026 annual report with full-year results. Given that the Q4 2025 results were released on March 19, 2026, this timing is the next regular checkpoint. Watch whether the operating margin moves beyond 0.3% and whether the 110.7% debt-to-equity ratio declines.

  2. Around mid-May 2027

    The Q1 2027 quarterly report. It will show whether revenue again concentrates as it did in Q1 2026 or holds at a steadier level.

  3. Ongoing from November 2026

    Supply contract disclosures, as with the May 26, 2026 filing. Contract size and revenue recognition timing indicate the direction of upcoming quarters. If new contracts are smaller than the 2025 contract, the risk of lower revenue should be weighed.

12

Overall view

Korea Information Engineering Services is centered on IT hardware distribution and software development, and its results move sharply with the award of large national and public projects.

The recovery in Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 170.7 billion and in first-half 2026 results appears to reflect the recognition of the 2025 contract. Attributable net income rose for two consecutive quarters in Q2 and Q3 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 6.44 billion.

However, the operating margin stayed at 0.3% in both 2024 and 2025, and operating profit fell 32.0% in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 80.2% in 2022 to 110.7% in 2025, indicating higher leverage.

The shift toward an AI infrastructure business has been declared, but the scale of new software revenue is not yet visible in reported results. The next checkpoints are the operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report, followed by whether Q1 2027 revenue replaces the gap left by the completed contract.
