Report overview

Refine: Earnings Concentrated in One Rights-Check Service Line

Refine depends heavily on rights checks tied to jeonse and monthly-rent deposit loans, and after peaking in Q1 2026, its quarterly operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders have declined for two straight quarters, making profit trends and new-business progress the key items to track.

02

Business structure

Refine is a specialized real estate rights-check company, established to provide real estate, finance and risk management services.

Its revenue is concentrated in lending-related services: in 2023, jeonse-deposit loan services accounted for 91.9% of revenue, mortgage loan services for 3.8% and jeonse-deposit return guarantee services for 4.3%.

Jeonse-deposit loan revenue rose from KRW 50.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 61.1 billion in 2023, and mortgage-service revenue grew 24.0% to KRW 2.5 billion. In the bank loan screening process, insurers and guarantee companies that are its clients order rights checks.

A March 2023 brokerage report described Refine as the domestic leader in real estate rights checks with a market share above 90%, and cited its patents as supporting a dominant position. Its new initiatives include a B2C platform and a real estate data business, supported by a planned big-data center.

Standalone revenue fell 13.3% in Q1 2025. Its affiliate is the unlisted K&J Insurance Brokerage, and it has no consolidated subsidiaries.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 55.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 66.5 billion in 2023 (up 20.7% year on year), reached KRW 67.8 billion in 2024 (up 2.1%), and fell to KRW 61.6 billion in 2025 (down 9.3%).

The 2023 increase was led by jeonse-deposit loan services, while in 2022 revenue had declined 6.3% on lower monthly-rent deposit loan services.

Operating profit was KRW 17.9 billion in 2022, KRW 22.6 billion in 2023 (up 25.7%), KRW 20.5 billion in 2024 (down 9.1%) and KRW 18.9 billion in 2025 (down 7.6%). 2023 was the largest year in the period, and operating profit has fallen in each of 2024 and 2025.

In 2024, revenue grew 2.1% while operating profit fell 9.1%, so operating margin dropped from 33.9% to 30.2%. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 21.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 20.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.06 billion in 2025, while total net income in 2025 was the same KRW 9.06 billion.

The 2025 payout ratio was 49.72%, with a dividend amount of KRW 4.5 billion. At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 223 billion, total liabilities KRW 11.9 billion and the debt ratio 5.3%.

In Q3 2025, revenue was KRW 15.2 billion, operating profit KRW 4.82 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 6.18 billion.

In Q4 2025, revenue fell 9.7% quarter on quarter to KRW 13.8 billion, operating profit was KRW 3.26 billion, and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 6.91 billion, turning from profit to loss.

In Q1 2026, revenue was KRW 15 billion, operating profit KRW 5.55 billion (up 70.5% quarter on quarter) and net income attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 5.71 billion, a return to profit.

In Q3 2026, revenue was KRW 12.2 billion (down 19.6% year on year), operating profit KRW 2.76 billion (down 42.6%) and net income attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 3.34 billion (down 46.0%). The last four quarters sum to KRW 6.97 billion of net income attributable to controlling shareholders.

Operating profit has fallen faster than revenue, so cost structure is now moving profit more than sales. The drivers of the Q4 2025 loss are a checkpoint for the next annual report.

05

Industry analysis

Rights checks for jeonse and monthly-rent deposit loans track rental transactions and loan demand. Total jeonse and monthly-rent transactions fell 4.1% in 2023, yet Refine's rights-check volume for these loans rose 16.2%, indicating share gains even as the market shrank. The per-case rights-check fee also rose 3.1%.

Strong jeonse markets were cited as a growth driver in 2024. The cycle position can be read from quarterly results: revenue and operating profit have both declined for two straight quarters after the Q1 2026 peak, and operating profit has fallen faster than revenue.

A 2023 brokerage report described Refine as the domestic leader with a market share above 90%, and its patent-based position was cited as a barrier to entry. That view dates from 2023, so today's share cannot be read directly from it.

Because the company sits inside bank and guarantor loan screening, changes in lending rates and loan limits flow through to rights-check volume. Shifts in rental-market regulation and financial policy can also change demand paths.

Whether the real estate data and B2C platform businesses broaden demand beyond the core service is not yet separable in the reported figures.

06

Outlook

In its March 31, 2026 corporate value-up plan, Refine stated that it aims to create stable earnings through stronger business competitiveness and to raise medium- to long-term corporate value.

It also said it would look for a shareholder-return policy that balances growth investment with financial soundness, and it disclosed that it qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act.

At the November 17, 2026 extraordinary meeting, the shareholder-proposed capital reserve reduction and retained earnings transfer passed, while an amendment to part of the articles of incorporation was rejected.

The new business agenda centers on a B2C platform, a real estate data business and a planned big-data center. When and how much these businesses will show up in reported results should become clearer in the next annual report's business section.

On the quarterly track, Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.76 billion is the reference point for the next quarter. Whether profit recovers from the Q1 2026 peak is the near-term item to watch. Execution of the shareholder-return plan and the capital reserve procedure is also a follow-up item.

Bull factors

Low Debt Burden and Operating Margins Near 30%

At year-end 2025 the debt ratio was 5.3%, with total liabilities of KRW 11.9 billion against total equity of KRW 223 billion. The debt ratio fell from 9.2% in 2022 to 5.3% in 2025. Operating margin stayed in the 30% range from 2022 through 2025. This balance sheet gives a buffer during periods of falling earnings.

High-Dividend Status and Shareholder-Return Steps

The 2025 fiscal-year payout ratio was 49.72%, with a dividend amount of KRW 4.5 billion. The company stated that it qualifies as a high-dividend company. At the November 17, 2026 extraordinary meeting, the capital reserve reduction and retained earnings transfer passed with 99.9% approval of shares present.

Market Position in Rights Checks and Data Expansion

A March 2023 brokerage report described Refine as the domestic leader in real estate rights checks with a market share above 90%, and cited a patent-based dominant position. The company is pursuing a B2C platform and a real estate data business, with a planned big-data center as a hub for its data ecosystem.

09

Bear factors

Revenue Concentrated in Loan-Linked Services

In 2023, jeonse-deposit loan services accounted for 91.9% of revenue. Because sales are concentrated in this service, changes in jeonse and loan demand pass through quickly to results. Revenue fell 9.3% to KRW 61.6 billion in 2025, and in 2022 a decline in the same service had cut total revenue by 6.3%.

Falling Profit and a Recent Swing to Loss

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 56.1% to KRW 9.06 billion in 2025. In Q4 2025 it swung to a net loss of KRW 6.91 billion. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.76 billion was down 42.6% from a year earlier, a steeper decline than the 19.6% fall in revenue.

Uncertain Payoff From New Businesses

The B2C platform and real estate data businesses are presented as initiatives under way. Their revenue scale cannot be separated from the reported figures. Standalone revenue fell 13.3% and operating profit fell 29.4% in Q1 2025, the same period the new businesses were being expanded.

Risk factors

Policy and Regulatory Risk

Changes in financial policy on jeonse loans and guarantees, or in rental-market regulation, could move both rights-check volume and per-case fees. The per-case fee rose 3.1% in 2023, but there is no assurance that increase continues. Because revenue is concentrated, a drop in loan demand could affect results quickly.

Governance and Shareholder Structure

At the November 17, 2026 extraordinary meeting, an amendment to part of the articles of incorporation was rejected. As of June 2025, the largest shareholder and related parties held 47.96%. With ownership concentrated, the direction and process of governance decisions merit attention.

Earnings Volatility

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 12.2 billion was down 13.2% quarter on quarter. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 6.97 billion, a small base. Having swung to a loss in Q4 2025, quarterly profit has shown wide swings.

Overall view

Refine is a specialized company centered on rights checks for jeonse and monthly-rent deposit loans, and in 2023 jeonse-deposit loan services accounted for 91.9% of revenue. Operating margin stayed in the 30% range from 2022 through 2025, and the debt ratio was a low 5.3% at year-end 2025.

On the other hand, net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 21.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 9.06 billion in 2025, and Q4 2025 swung to a loss. Two consecutive quarterly declines since the Q1 2026 peak should be read alongside these results.

In valuation terms, current multiples sit above the 7 to 8 times PER range of the past, and the earnings decline is behind that gap. On shareholder returns, the 49.72% payout ratio for fiscal 2025 and the high-dividend disclosure are the reference points.

The new businesses, a B2C platform and a real estate data business, cannot yet be separated from reported figures, so their results remain to be confirmed. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results and the 2026 annual report.
