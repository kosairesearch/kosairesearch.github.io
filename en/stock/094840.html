02

Business structure

Suprema HQ runs a biometric security product ODM (original design manufacturing) business and serves as the business holding company of the Suprema group, which handles management services for affiliates.

Its ODM business supplies systems and products for access security and time-attendance management that require biometric technology.

Of Q1 2026 sales of KRW 7.33 billion, ODM sales for access security and time-attendance systems came to KRW 6.154 billion, or 83.82% of total sales, and service and other sales were KRW 1.188 billion (16.18%). On a first-half 2026 basis, the ODM business accounted for 78.47% of total sales.

Sales to key customer A accounted for 82.09%, a high degree of customer concentration. A March 2023 Korea IR Council report said it held a 60-70% share of the domestic institutional market for access control and overtime-check devices.

Supplying biometric systems to security companies was described as the basis of its stable sales. R&D expenses in Q1 2026 were KRW 260 million, or 3.55% of sales. Its main consolidated subsidiary is Suprema ID.

An August 2026 amendment added eight business purposes, including crime and disaster prevention, disaster response, video monitoring, location-based services, and CVC and accelerator investment.

Having entered the market at its founding and built more than 20 years of operating history was seen as an advantage over later entrants.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales moved from KRW 32.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 20.4 billion in 2023 (down 37.1% year on year), KRW 20.1 billion in 2024 (down 1.4%), and KRW 21.2 billion in 2025 (up 5.6%). Among 2022-2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit swung from KRW 6.61 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 2.68 billion in 2023, then returned to an operating profit of KRW 2.46 billion in 2024 and reached KRW 3.86 billion in 2025, up 56.6%. The operating margin was 20.4% in 2022, -13.1% in 2023, 12.3% in 2024 and 18.2% in 2025.

Attributable net income peaked at KRW 12.6 billion in 2022... more precisely: KRW 37.3 billion in 2023 (up 196.6%), after KRW 12.6 billion in 2022, then fell 59.2% to KRW 15.2 billion in 2024 and 12.0% to KRW 13.4 billion in 2025, a two-year decline.

In 2023, attributable net income of KRW 37.3 billion came despite an operating loss, which suggests non-operating items contributed heavily. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 13.7 billion in 2022, KRW 37.5 billion in 2023, KRW 15.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 13.4 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 operating profit increase mainly to higher management consulting fee sales and lower selling and administrative expenses such as salaries. By quarter, sales peaked in Q1 2026 at KRW 7.33 billion and then fell for two consecutive quarters through Q3 2026.

Q2 2026 operating profit was KRW 136 million (down 86.3% from the prior quarter), and Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 337 million (down 51.3% year on year). Q3 2026 attributable net loss of KRW 3.71 billion marked a swing to loss from a profit a year earlier. Attributable net income over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 655 million.

05

Industry analysis

Growth in unmanned security demand, continued R&D and new product launches are cited as the drivers of improved results, along with rising safety and security needs that expand demand for access control and time-attendance systems.

The 60-70% share figure is from a March 2023 report, so it cannot be compared directly with current market structure. Sales in 2025 (KRW 21.2 billion) were about 65% of the 2022 level (KRW 32.4 billion), so whether demand recovery has translated into scale is an open question.

With key customer concentration above 80%, order changes from a single customer flow directly into results. An IR council analysis argued that reducing reliance on specific customers and widening the client base could reduce earnings volatility from external changes.

In competition, the company's operating history since its founding is cited as a barrier for later entrants. Its holding-company structure and affiliate investment results also separate its earnings from the core business.

The report said whether efforts to strengthen core competitiveness, affiliate investment results and shareholder value translate into concrete outcomes is the key question.

06

Outlook

The company said it has grown recent sales through continued R&D and new product launches in response to rising demand for unmanned security. The August 2026 amendment added business purposes such as crime and disaster prevention, disaster response and video monitoring, pointing to possible expansion beyond the core.

The IR council report projected 2026 sales at KRW 22.2 billion, up 4.7% from the prior year, and operating profit at KRW 3.7 billion, down 3.7%.

Sales in Q1 through Q3 2026 were KRW 7.33 billion, KRW 4.24 billion and KRW 4.07 billion, a combined total of about KRW 15.6 billion, so reaching the KRW 22.2 billion projection would require roughly KRW 6.6 billion in Q4.

Operating profit for the same three quarters totaled about KRW 1.47 billion, so the KRW 3.7 billion projection would require about KRW 2.2 billion in Q4, compared with KRW 1.05 billion in Q4 2025. The 2026 equity-method income estimate is KRW 10.5 billion, far larger than the operating profit estimate.

On shareholder returns, the company decided in January 2026 to gratuitously contribute 523,591 treasury shares to a foundation, which it said serves ESG and social contribution purposes. The next checkpoint is Q4 results and the size of equity-method gains in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in operating margin

The operating margin fell from 20.4% in 2022 to -13.1% in 2023, then recovered to 12.3% in 2024 and 18.2% in 2025. Operating profit reached KRW 3.86 billion in 2025, up 56.6%. The debt ratio also declined from 4.8% in 2022 to 3.5% in 2025.

Equity-method income as an additional earnings source

Equity-method income is expected to account for a significant share of results, with a 2026 estimate of KRW 10.5 billion. Compared with the KRW 3.7 billion operating profit estimate in the same report, its impact on total earnings is large. If affiliate investments keep performing, this becomes an earnings source outside the core business.

Financial capacity and cash flow turnaround

Total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 241.8 billion and total liabilities were KRW 8.47 billion. Operating cash flow turned from -KRW 4.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 4.39 billion in 2025. The company also has room in capital management, including the treasury share contribution to a foundation.

Bear factors

Q3 net loss and two quarters of falling sales

Sales fell to KRW 4.24 billion in Q2 2026, down 42.1% from the prior quarter, and were KRW 4.07 billion in Q3 2026. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 337 million was down 51.3% year on year. Attributable net loss of KRW 3.71 billion marked a swing from profit a year earlier.

Dependence on a single customer

Sales to key customer A accounted for 82.09%, a high degree of customer concentration. A reduction in orders or a change in terms from this customer would flow directly into sales and profit. Analysts also cite customer diversification as a medium-term task.

Q4 burden relative to estimates

Operating profit for Q1-Q3 2026 totaled about KRW 1.47 billion, so reaching the KRW 3.7 billion estimate requires roughly KRW 2.2 billion in Q4. Q4 2025 operating profit was KRW 1.05 billion. Sales would also need about KRW 6.6 billion in Q4 to reach the KRW 22.2 billion projection.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration risk

With ODM sales at 83.82% of total and key customer share above 80%, a reduction in orders or a change in terms from a single customer would cause large sales swings. Progress in diversification can be checked through the customer mix in upcoming periodic reports.

Volatility of non-operating income

In 2023, attributable net income reached KRW 37.3 billion despite an operating loss, showing a wide gap between net and operating results. In Q3 2026, an operating profit of KRW 337 million coexisted with an attributable net loss of KRW 3.71 billion. Earnings trends are hard to judge from operating profit alone.

Execution of new businesses and demand shifts

The August 2026 amendment added business purposes such as disaster prevention and video monitoring, but sales in these areas are not separately disclosed. R&D spending was 3.55% of Q1 2026 sales. If demand for unmanned security and access control does not continue as expected, recovery in core sales could be delayed.

Overall view

Suprema HQ is a business holding company that combines an ODM business with management services for affiliates. Operating profit rose 56.6% to KRW 3.86 billion in 2025, and the operating margin recovered to 18.2%.

However, attributable net income peaked at KRW 37.3 billion in 2023 and then fell for two consecutive years, and Q3 2026 turned to an attributable net loss of KRW 3.71 billion. Sales peaked in Q1 2026 and then fell in Q2 and Q3, and sales to key customer A accounted for 82.09%.

Based on an IR council estimate, 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.7 billion and equity-method income of KRW 10.5 billion form the core of the earnings outlook, while cumulative operating profit through Q3 implies a sharp Q4 recovery would be needed.

Strengths include the margin recovery, equity-method income and financial capacity, while weaknesses include the Q3 loss, customer concentration and volatility in non-operating income. The next checkpoint is Q4 results and equity-method income in the 2026 annual report.
