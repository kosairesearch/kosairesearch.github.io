Robots Lift Revenue Mix as Quarterly Losses Narrow

Robotics is becoming the core of consolidated revenue, but annual results turned to losses in 2025 and attributable earnings remain negative over the last four quarters.

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Cowinrobotics, formerly Cowintech, renamed itself on September 1, 2026 and relaunched as a robot-centered company. It positions itself as a full-stack robot maker spanning robot design and hardware manufacturing, autonomous driving, control software and system integration.

Its main products include industrial autonomous mobile robots (AMRs), an ultra-heavy-duty AMR for moving tens-of-tons-class ESS finished products, and High-Pick AMRs that connect to six-axis articulated robots.

Robot sales for January to September 2026 were KRW 72.191 billion, or 55.91% of the robotics and automation segment.

The secondary-battery materials and engineering business generated KRW 21.452 billion, or 13.58% of consolidated sales, and is run by subsidiary TOP MATERIAL, according to a TipRanks summary of the quarterly report. The robot share of standalone sales rose from 52.1% in the first half of 2026 to 60.1% in Q3.

NH Investment & Securities said in a September 2026 report that first-half 2026 new orders were 43% EV batteries, 40% ESS, 12% automotive and 5% semiconductors and other sectors. Customers started in batteries and ESS and are widening into electronic components such as MLCCs, semiconductors and displays.

The company says ESS production lines cannot be served by general-purpose robots alone, so process-specific design capability is its edge. The company also said its robot business grew at an average annual rate of 42.2% from 2020 to 2025.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 201.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 336 billion in 2023, up 67.0% year on year, and then fell 27.3% to KRW 244.4 billion in 2024 and 36.7% to KRW 154.7 billion in 2025, declining for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit grew from KRW 14.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 22.8 billion in 2023, up 54.4%, then dropped 84.2% to KRW 3.6 billion in 2024, and in 2025 the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 25.4 billion. The operating margin moved from 7.3% in 2022 and 6.8% in 2023 to 1.5% in 2024 and -16.4% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 16.2 billion in 2024, the largest year of 2022 to 2025, and fell to KRW 1.7 billion in 2025, down 89.5%. Net income including non-controlling interests turned to a net loss of KRW 17.6 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 256.1 billion, of which KRW 171.9 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 172.1 billion. The debt ratio rose from 56.1% in 2024 to 67.2% in 2025.

Operating cash flow swung from negative KRW 34.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 20.6 billion in 2025. Quarterly revenue went from KRW 39.3 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 40.9 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 39.8 billion in Q1 2026, then rose to KRW 58.4 billion in Q2 and KRW 59.8 billion in Q3, two consecutive quarters of growth.

The operating loss was largest in Q4 2025 at KRW 20.3 billion among the quarters in the disclosed record, then narrowed from KRW 1.04 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 7.41 billion in Q2 to KRW 1.66 billion in Q3. The company said the Q2 consolidated loss was driven largely by volatility in its battery subsidiary.

Attributable net loss was KRW 11.9 billion in Q3 2026, a swing from a year-earlier profit, and the attributable net loss over the last four quarters totaled KRW 9.47 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The core end market is still secondary batteries, but demand weight is shifting toward ESS. The company says rising power demand from AI data center expansion has pushed the global ESS market into a structural growth phase.

It expects the wave of production-site investment by battery and ESS makers, centered in North America, to translate into automation demand. Battery market conditions have been volatile since 2025, and the company said in Q3 2026 that most of the effect of the battery cycle is reflected in this year's results.

On the robot side, the company points to robot transformation (RX) demand across manufacturing sites. It positions process-specific design and field references, rather than general-purpose robots, as its differentiator.

Battery materials peers such as Ecopro BM and L&F are exposed to the same cell-maker investment cycle, and the earnings of subsidiary TOP MATERIAL are also affected by that cycle. The pace of recovery in the battery materials business will be confirmed through results from Q4 2026 onward.

06

Outlook

The company plans to expand its robot training and learning center by the end of 2026 and to broaden its business into mobile robots and humanoid robots.

In its corporate value-up plan announced in March 2026, it said it would restructure around robots and keep a high payout ratio through a step-by-step increase in dividends.

NH Investment & Securities said in a September 2026 report that AMR revenue, about KRW 30 billion in 2025, could roughly double to about KRW 60 billion in 2026. The same report said 2026 would show improvement in orders and 2027 in earnings, as the ESS, automotive and AMR orders added this year convert into revenue.

The company said it is in talks to supply additional robot systems for other gigafactory expansions in North America with the same ESS customer. Because most contract terms are undisclosed, the revenue contribution of individual orders will be gauged through quarterly results and the annual report. On profitability, the company said in its Q3 2026 statement that it will focus on improving margins.

08

Bull factors

Robots Become the Core Business

Robot sales for January to September 2026 reached KRW 72.191 billion, or 55.91% of the robotics and automation segment. The robot share of standalone sales rose from 52.1% in the first half to 60.1% in Q3.

NH Investment & Securities projected in a September 2026 report that AMR revenue could double from about KRW 30 billion in 2025 to about KRW 60 billion in 2026.

Diversifying New Orders

NH Investment & Securities put first-half 2026 standalone new orders at about KRW 105 billion. The earlier heavy reliance on EV orders has declined sharply, with ESS at 40%, automotive at 12% and semiconductors and other sectors at 5%. The company announced a new robot integration order from a global ESS company on November 10, 2026.

Narrowing Quarterly Losses and Dividend Policy

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 7.41 billion in Q2 2026 to KRW 1.66 billion in Q3, and Q3 revenue of KRW 59.8 billion marked a second consecutive quarter of growth. In its March 2026 value-up plan, the company said it would keep a high payout ratio through a step-by-step increase in dividends.

Bear factors

Annual Swing to Losses

The 2025 operating loss of KRW 25.4 billion marked a swing to losses, with an operating margin of -16.4%. Attributable net income fell to KRW 1.7 billion in 2025, and net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 17.6 billion.

The attributable net loss in Q3 2026 was KRW 11.9 billion, a swing from a year-earlier profit, and the four-quarter total was KRW 9.47 billion.

Earnings Volatility at the Battery Subsidiary

The company said the Q2 2026 consolidated operating loss was driven largely by volatility in its battery subsidiary. Battery materials and engineering sales for January to September 2026 were KRW 21.452 billion, or 13.58% of consolidated sales, and TOP MATERIAL's results flow directly into consolidated earnings. Even as robot profits grow, swings at the subsidiary can move consolidated results.

Delayed Conversion of Orders into Earnings

NH Investment & Securities expects the 2026 order improvement to be reflected fully in 2027 earnings. In other words, the conversion of this year's orders into revenue is pushed into 2027.

Because counterparties and amounts are largely undisclosed, outsiders have difficulty gauging how much individual orders will contribute to results.

10

Risk factors

End-Market Investment and Policy

Investment in batteries and ESS depends on North American production-site expansion and policy conditions. The company cited rising North American ESS demand as a reason for battery market improvement, and robot orders could be affected if end-market investment slows. Earnings volatility at the battery subsidiary also flows straight into consolidated results.

Customer Concentration and Undisclosed Contracts

Battery-related customers still dominate new orders, with EV at 43% and ESS at 40% in first-half 2026. The company does not disclose counterparties or contract amounts, citing business confidentiality. This makes it hard for outside observers to gauge customer dependence and the real impact of order size.

Financial Capacity and Cash Flow

Operating cash flow swung from negative KRW 34.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 20.6 billion in 2025, though the 2024 outflow remains part of the record. The debt ratio rose from 56.1% in 2024 to 67.2% in 2025. If losses persist, the question is whether capital and dividend capacity will erode.

12

Overall view

Cowinrobotics is in the middle of shifting from a battery automation equipment maker toward a robot-centered business. Robot sales for January to September 2026 were KRW 72.191 billion, or 55.91% of the robotics and automation segment, and the robot share of standalone sales reached 60.1% in Q3.

New orders are also diversifying into ESS, automotive and semiconductors as EV dependence declines. On the other hand, the 2025 operating loss of KRW 25.4 billion and the Q3 2026 attributable net loss of KRW 11.9 billion show that profitability has not yet recovered.

Quarterly operating losses have narrowed since Q2 2026, while attributable net losses widened. The bullish factors are robot growth and order diversification, while the bearish factors are continued losses, earnings volatility at the battery subsidiary and the delay in converting orders into earnings. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
