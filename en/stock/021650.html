02

Business structure

Cubic Korea is a specialist in curved-surface printing that has been listed on the KOSDAQ since January 2003. Based on second-quarter 2026 sales, automotive exterior parts accounted for 89% and automotive interior parts for 11%, so the exterior segment is the core.

Exterior products include radiator grilles, emblems and moldings, while the interior line includes the Curl-Fit product family.

Its production sites are the Ansan and Busan plants, and the Ansan plant's 2025 sales of KRW 19.3 billion equaled about 67% of the KRW 28.8 billion recent sales base on a 2025 standalone basis.

On September 30, 2026 the board approved halting commercial operation at Ansan, with the production stop date recorded as January 1, 2027. The company plans to consolidate production at Busan to cut fixed and operating costs and to secure liquidity through the sale of idle assets.

Major customers are tied to mass-produced vehicle models, and the company attributed its 2025 decline to lower output of those models. Within the auto parts industry, its 2025 sales ranked 84th among 140 listed companies and its market capitalization ranked 114th.

Competition centers on program-based supply awards and production efficiency, and the company is responding to its exterior-heavy structure by consolidating plants. The key variables for the business are therefore exterior-parts concentration and whether the Ansan-to-Busan transfer is executed.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 210 billion in 2022 to KRW 252.1 billion in 2023, a 20.0% increase year on year, and were unchanged at KRW 252.1 billion in 2024. Sales fell 9.9% to KRW 227.2 billion in 2025, with 2024 the largest year and 2022 the smallest year of the 2022 to 2025 span.

The company attributed the 2025 decline to lower output of mass-produced vehicle models, and operating profit fell 30.6% from KRW 11.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 7.93 billion in 2025.

The operating margin moved from 4.3% in 2022, 4.2% in 2023 and 4.5% in 2024 to 3.5% in 2025, and 2025 was the smallest operating profit year of the span.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 52.4% from KRW 9.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 4.62 billion in 2025, and the company said lower foreign exchange gains and higher foreign exchange losses weighed on net income.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 9.39 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.1 billion in 2024 before falling to KRW 9.71 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio declined from 98.3% in 2022 and 100.3% in 2023 to 80.9% in 2024 and 65.6% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 98.5 billion and total liabilities of KRW 64.6 billion at the end of 2025.

Quarterly operating profit of KRW 2.01 billion in the third quarter of 2025 turned into an operating loss of KRW 719 million in the fourth quarter of 2025.

The first quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 1.72 billion, but the second quarter fell to KRW 993 million and the third quarter reached KRW 1.28 billion, down 36.7% year on year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 1.14 billion in the third quarter of 2026, so sales recovered in 2026 while profitability did not recover in step.

05

Industry analysis

Cubic Korea belongs to the auto parts industry, where demand depends on output of mass-produced vehicle models at automakers. The 2025 sales decline was attributed to lower output of those models, which confirms that automaker production cycles drive results.

In its disclosure the company pointed to rapidly changing domestic and overseas auto parts and surface treatment market conditions as the backdrop for consolidating production.

On a 2025 annual basis, the company's operating margin of 3.49% was above the industry average of -0.55% in one data set, and its debt ratio was well below the industry average.

In the same data set its sales ranked 84th and its market capitalization ranked 114th among the industry's listed companies, so its scale is small even as its profitability metrics compare favorably. Competition in the industry turns on program-based supply and production efficiency.

With exterior parts at 89% of sales, changes in vehicle program cycles can move revenue considerably. Currency swings, which affected 2025 net income through foreign exchange gains and losses, are a non-operating factor visible in the 2025 results.

06

Outlook

The nearest scheduled step the company has disclosed is the sequential transfer of production processes from Ansan to Busan and trial operation during the fourth quarter of 2026. The company said it aims to minimize the short-term drop in sales and production volume during this transfer.

The Ansan production stop date is recorded as January 1, 2027, and the company left open the possibility of corrective disclosures. Over the medium to long term, it expects profitability to improve through higher production efficiency and lower duplicated infrastructure costs.

It said it will re-disclose once specifics such as the sale of the Ansan site are settled, so the size and timing of idle-asset sales are the next checkpoint.

In its corporate value enhancement plan published on March 24, 2026, the company set out stronger quality competitiveness, new market development and new technology development as its directions.

In the same disclosure it said it qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act, citing a 2025 payout ratio of 35.4% and a 2025 dividend amount of KRW 1.64 billion. It reported the 2025 dividend amount rose 18.3% year on year.

Because a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders emerged in the third quarter of 2026, dividend capacity should be read alongside whether earnings recover.

08

Bull factors

Sales recovery

Sales recovered from KRW 48.3 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 54.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 57.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026. The third-quarter figure is the highest of the last five quarters. Year-on-year growth in the third quarter was only 1.4%, but the sales base has widened again.

Plant consolidation expected to cut costs

The company said consolidating production at Busan would lower fixed costs and raise operating efficiency. It also targets lower duplicated infrastructure costs and savings in utilities and administration. A plan to sell idle assets to secure liquidity and financial soundness was disclosed alongside.

High-dividend status

The company said it qualifies as a high-dividend company, with a 2025 payout ratio of 35.4%. The 2025 dividend amount was guided to have risen 18.3% year on year. Presenting dividend metrics in its 2026 corporate value enhancement plan also shows its shareholder return stance.

Bear factors

Weak third-quarter 2026 earnings

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.28 billion fell 36.7% year on year. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 1.14 billion. Over the last four quarters that line totals only KRW 871 million, leaving a thin earnings base.

Concentration in vehicle models and exterior parts

Automotive exterior parts were 89% of sales in the second quarter of 2026, a high share. The company attributed the 2025 decline in sales and operating profit mainly to lower output of mass-produced models. The 3.5% operating margin in 2025 was the lowest of the 2022 to 2025 span.

Execution risk in the production transfer

The Ansan plant's 2025 sales equal about 67% of recent standalone sales. Production volume could be interrupted while processes move to Busan and undergo trial operation in the fourth quarter of 2026. The company aims to minimize declines, but the actual execution outcome has not yet been disclosed.

10

Risk factors

Execution of plant transfer

Moving production from Ansan to Busan may not follow the planned trial operation schedule or volume path. Utilization and quality stability at Busan must be confirmed after the January 1, 2027 stop. Any delay in the transfer would directly affect sales in the fourth quarter of 2026 and early 2027.

Concentration on vehicle models

A high exterior-parts share means changes in output of specific vehicle programs flow directly into sales. In 2025, lower output of mass-produced models pulled down both sales and operating profit. Model replacement cycles and the ability to secure new programs will shape the revenue structure going forward.

Currency and non-operating swings

The company cited lower foreign exchange gains and higher foreign exchange losses as reasons for the 2025 decline in net income. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders can move with currency-related items even when operating profit is positive. As a result, net income can swing more widely than operating profit.

Overall view

Cubic Korea recorded third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 57.8 billion, the highest of the last five quarters, but operating profit of KRW 1.28 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.14 billion show earnings remain weak.

Full-year 2025 results saw sales, operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders all decline, with the operating margin at 3.5%, as lower output of mass-produced vehicle models weighed.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 98.3% in 2022 to 65.6% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 98.5 billion at the end of 2025.

The Ansan halt and Busan transfer run on a schedule of trial operation in the fourth quarter of 2026 and a stop on January 1, 2027, and whether fixed-cost savings and better profitability show up in the numbers is the central question.

Strengths include the sales recovery, the plant consolidation plan and high-dividend status, while weaknesses include the weaker third-quarter 2026 earnings, concentration in exterior parts and vehicle models, and the possibility of volume gaps during the transfer.

The next checkpoints are the Busan trial-operation results, preliminary results in early 2027 and the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027. This report is provided for information only and does not state an investment opinion or target price.
