Avatec: Display Etching Base, Quarterly Profit Slowing

Avatec turned profitable in 2023 and grew annual operating profit through 2025, but quarterly sales have declined for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, and quarterly operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders have fallen.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Avatec is an electronic components company whose core business is display panel slimming and ITO coating for etching processes, and it also runs a multilayer ceramic capacitor (MLCC) business.

A 2023 October research report said that domestic sales accounted for 93% of revenue in 2022, and that production sites are located in Ochang and Gumi 1 and 2 plants in Korea.

Meritz Securities' February 2026 report put 2025 preliminary display segment revenue at KRW 72.4 billion and MLCC segment revenue at KRW 8.6 billion, a combined total close to the disclosed annual revenue of KRW 80.9 billion.

Display customers are domestic panel makers, and demand is influenced by the spread of OLED adoption. A WISEreport company note said OLED is spreading quickly across mobile, TV, tablet and automotive uses, with OLED reaching a record share of the display market. MLCC grew during 2025.

According to the Meritz report, Q4 2025 MLCC revenue was KRW 5.3 billion, about 2.5 times the prior quarter, helped by a rebound in sales to solar inverter customers and larger volumes for domestic display customers. Compared with large component makers such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics, Avatec's MLCC scale is small.

A 2023 report expected profitability to improve in both the display and MLCC divisions. Shareholder return actions have also continued: the March 2026 annual general meeting approved financial statements and a cash dividend, and in September 2026 a share buyback contract was disclosed.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 73.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 75.8 billion in 2023 (up 3.5% year on year) and KRW 84.5 billion in 2024 (up 11.6%), then fell to KRW 80.9 billion in 2025 (down 4.3%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit turned from an operating loss of KRW 5.35 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 2.2 billion in 2023, then reached KRW 5.8 billion in 2024 (up 163.5%) and KRW 6.15 billion in 2025 (up 6.0%), increasing for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

The operating margin moved from -7.3% in 2022 to 2.9% in 2023, 6.9% in 2024 and 7.6% in 2025. In 2025 the margin rose even though revenue declined, which is the notable change in the earnings structure.

In its February 2026 report, Meritz Securities raised its 2026 profit forecast, citing cost control, higher utilization from the MLCC sales rebound, and lower fixed-cost burden.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from a net loss of KRW 4.32 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.8 billion in 2023 (a turn to profit), KRW 8.9 billion in 2024 (up 85.3%) and KRW 6.92 billion in 2025 (down 22.2%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 6.92 billion in 2025, so the key feature of 2025 is that operating profit rose while net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell.

Operating cash flow moved from -KRW 1.98 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.44 billion in 2023, KRW 16.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.87 billion in 2025, staying positive since 2023.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 22.8 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 21.7 billion in Q4 2025 (down 4.7% from the prior quarter), KRW 17 billion in Q1 2026 (down 21.9%), KRW 16.9 billion in Q2 2026 (down 0.4%) and KRW 15.3 billion in Q3 2026 (down 9.6% quarter on quarter and down 33.0% year on year).

Revenue has declined for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026.

Operating profit fell from KRW 3.3 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 2.69 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 767 million in Q1 2026 and KRW 336 million in Q2 2026; Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 722 million rose 114.9% from the prior quarter but fell 78.2% from a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.05 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 2.74 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 920 million in Q1 2026, KRW 1.31 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 225 million in Q3 2026. The sum over the latest four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) is KRW 5.19 billion.

Whether MLCC or display revenue drove the quarterly decline is a point to check in the segment disclosures of the periodic reports.

05

Industry analysis

Avatec's market is structured so that etching orders shift with display panel capital spending and OLED production volumes. A WISEreport company note said OLED is being adopted quickly across mobile, TV, tablet and automotive uses, with OLED reaching a record share of the display market.

A October 2023 report projected both a rise in OLED etching revenue and a recovery in LCD volumes, the two pillars of display sales that were discussed together from that period. The MLCC side depends on industrial demand.

In a November 2025 report, Meritz Securities cited the resumption of supply to solar inverter customers and larger volumes for domestic display customers as reasons for the MLCC sales jump.

Its February 2026 report noted that additional supply to Tier-1 automotive suppliers was possible and classified it as a potential factor not yet included in forecasts.

In MLCC, Avatec is much smaller in scale than large multilayer ceramic capacitor makers such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics, so its MLCC results move sharply from quarter to quarter depending on volumes for specific customers.

Competition in display etching is shaped by customer selection and process technology, which makes direct comparison by market share difficult. On cycle position, the display business is in an OLED expansion phase, while MLCC appears to be in a recovery phase since the second half of 2025.

However, revenue has declined for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, which indicates that the recovery has not yet clearly shown up in quarterly results in either business.

Segment quarterly disclosures should show whether display volume adjustments or the timing of MLCC volumes drove the quarterly decline.

Outlook

Company-level annual guidance is not the main reference point here, so brokerage forecasts and disclosure flows serve as the basis for assessment.

In an April 2026 report, Meritz Securities projected 2026 standalone revenue of KRW 86.0 billion and operating profit of KRW 10.5 billion, with MLCC revenue of KRW 22.8 billion (up 166.0% year on year).

However, quarterly revenue in Q1 to Q3 2026 came in at KRW 17 billion, KRW 16.9 billion and KRW 15.3 billion, which widened the gap to that forecast.

A February 2026 report flagged possible additional supply to Tier-1 automotive suppliers as a potential upside to medium-term earnings, and whether that supply has started is a point to verify in upcoming disclosures.

On shareholder returns, the March 2026 annual general meeting approved a cash dividend, and a share buyback contract was disclosed in September 2026. In display, the question is whether the Q3 2025 pattern of an OLED etching rebound from seasonal demand repeats in the next peak season.

For MLCC, the benchmark is how far Q4 2026 revenue rises from KRW 5.3 billion in Q4 2025. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual business report.

In that report, 2026 full-year revenue and operating profit should be compared with KRW 80.9 billion and KRW 6.15 billion in 2025, and the mix between display and MLCC should be checked. If the four-quarter revenue decline extends into Q1 2027, it would become harder to hold annual earnings at the 2025 level.

08

Bull factors

Continuity of operating profit recovery

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 5.35 billion in 2022 to a turn to profit in 2023, then increased for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025. The operating margin rose from -7.3% in 2022 to 7.6% in 2025. In 2025 operating profit grew 6.0% even though revenue fell 4.3%.

Expansion trend in the MLCC business

MLCC revenue rose to KRW 5.3 billion in Q4 2025, about 2.5 times the prior quarter, according to Meritz Securities, with the resumption of solar inverter supply and larger volumes for domestic display customers as drivers. An April 2026 report projected 2026 MLCC revenue of KRW 22.8 billion.

Financial capacity and shareholder returns

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 152 billion, total liabilities were KRW 11.3 billion and the debt ratio was 7.4%. Operating cash flow stayed positive at KRW 9.87 billion in 2025. A cash dividend was approved at the March 2026 annual general meeting, and a share buyback contract was disclosed in September 2026.

09

Bear factors

Consecutive quarterly revenue declines

Revenue has declined for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 15.3 billion fell 33.0% year on year and 9.6% from the prior quarter. Which segment drove the decline must be checked in the segment disclosures.

Steep slowdown in earnings

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 722 million fell 78.2% year on year. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 225 million, down 92.6%. The sum over the latest four quarters, KRW 5.19 billion, is below the 2025 full-year figure of KRW 6.92 billion.

Display dependence and a gap to forecasts

On 2025 preliminary figures, display revenue of KRW 72.4 billion was far larger than MLCC revenue of KRW 8.6 billion. The KRW 86.0 billion 2026 revenue forecast presented by Meritz Securities in April 2026 has widened its gap as quarterly revenue declined to KRW 17 billion, KRW 16.9 billion and KRW 15.3 billion.

The additional Tier-1 automotive supply noted in the February 2026 report remains a potential factor not yet reflected in reported results.

10

Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

Display etching revenue is concentrated among domestic panel customers and accounts for most of 2025 revenue. A change in a customer's capital spending or volume adjustments can quickly reduce quarterly revenue.

Whether the 2026 quarterly decline is linked to this concentration should be checked in the next segment disclosures.

MLCC profitability and competition

MLCC revenue rose to KRW 5.3 billion in Q4 2025, but it depends on the resumed solar inverter supply and volumes tied to specific customers. Scale is much smaller than large players such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics, which may limit pricing and volume negotiating power. If MLCC utilization falls, the fixed-cost burden could rise again.

Earnings volatility and small-cap liquidity

Quarterly operating profit swung widely, from KRW 3.3 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 336 million in Q2 2026. As a small-cap KOSDAQ stock, the share price has tended to move sharply within the past year. In periods of large earnings swings, market interpretation can shift quickly with each quarterly disclosure.

12

Overall view

Avatec combines display etching with MLCC, and it has built up profitability from an operating loss in 2022 through a turn to profit in 2023 to a 7.6% operating margin in 2025. The 2025 annual results are summarized by revenue falling 4.3% while operating profit rose 6.0%, extending a two-year increase from 2024.

On the other side, revenue has declined for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, and Q3 2026 operating profit fell 78.2% year on year.

MLCC revenue rose to KRW 5.3 billion in Q4 2025 and a growth forecast was presented for 2026, but the gap between that outlook and reported quarterly revenue is a point to verify in the next periodic report.

On finances, total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 152 billion and the debt ratio was 7.4%, confirming financial stability. The bullish factors of continued earnings recovery and MLCC expansion and the bearish factors of the sharp quarterly profit decline and dependence on display should be weighed equally.
