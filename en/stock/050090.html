02

Business structure

BK Holdings was founded in June 2000 and listed on KOSDAQ in June 2004, and it is an affiliate of a larger Korean business group. Its predecessor, listed as Phoenix Materials, produced solder balls and metal pastes used in semiconductor packaging.

After changing its name in March 2022, the semiconductor materials business was wound down following an operating suspension in 2023, and advertising production and media agency work is now the core.

The production unit makes TV, radio, newspaper and magazine advertisements by medium, and the media agency unit buys and places ads across multiple media. Based on Q2 2026 revenue, the advertising agency business accounted for 100% of sales according to a third-party data aggregator.

Its head office is in Gumi, North Gyeongsang Province, with a Seoul branch. On 27 March 2026, one co-CEO stepped down and Hong Jeong-hwa was appointed, so Hong Seok-gyu and Hong Jeong-hwa now serve as co-CEOs.

As of 2 October 2026, the largest shareholder group, led by its largest shareholder and four related parties, held 44.94%. A change in the largest shareholder was disclosed on 8 October 2026, and 1.44 million shares acquired in the third-party allotment are subject to a one-year lock-up that has been completed.

In the media market, the four major media ad markets have stagnated while digital ads on online video and platforms are growing.

At the 18 September 2026 extraordinary shareholders meeting, 26 new business purposes covering displays, AI infrastructure, data centers and rare earth extraction were added to the articles of incorporation, but no concrete revenue plan for these businesses has been disclosed.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue went from KRW 17.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.3 billion in 2023 (down 34.4% year on year), KRW 12.7 billion in 2024 (up 12.1%), and KRW 7.45 billion in 2025 (down 41.4%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest revenue year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating losses were KRW 2 billion in 2022, KRW 685 million in 2023, KRW 2.03 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.57 billion in 2025, which makes four consecutive years of losses from 2022 through 2025. The loss narrowed in 2023 and widened in 2024 and again in 2025.

Operating margins were -11.6% in 2022, -6.0% in 2023, -16.0% in 2024 and -48.0% in 2025, so in 2025 the loss widened as revenue fell, indicating the cost base did not adjust to the lower sales.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.06 billion in 2022, KRW 4.42 billion in 2023, KRW 2.01 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.84 billion in 2025, and the net loss including non-controlling interests is reported at the same amounts.

The debt-to-equity ratio was 30.2% in 2022, 26.8% in 2023, 25.6% in 2024 and 29.9% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 11.1 billion with total liabilities of KRW 3.33 billion.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 1.84 billion in 2022, -KRW 2.42 billion in 2023, KRW 346 million in 2024 and -KRW 2.74 billion in 2025, so 2024 was the only positive year.

On a quarterly basis, revenue moved from KRW 2.69 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 2.4 billion in Q4 2025 (down 10.8% quarter on quarter) and KRW 1.05 billion in Q1 2026 (down 56.3%), then rose to KRW 1.42 billion in Q2 2026 (up 36.1%) and KRW 2.6 billion in Q3 2026 (up 82.5% quarter on quarter, but down 3.2% year on year), marking two straight quarters of growth in Q2 and Q3 2026.

Operating losses widened from KRW 516 million in Q3 2025 to KRW 820 million in Q4 2025 and KRW 1.06 billion in Q1 2026, then narrowed to KRW 727 million in Q2 and KRW 440 million in Q3 2026.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed to KRW 602 million in Q3 2026 from the prior quarter, but widened from KRW 460 million a year earlier. Reported figures alone do not separate the size of one-off items from recurring results.

Industry analysis

The advertising market that drives the company is cyclical and tied to advertisers' marketing budgets, so budget changes tend to show up in results quickly.

A FnGuide commentary on the first nine months of 2025 said marketing budgets shrank with the economic cycle, reducing advertising production and media agency sales, while the four major media ad markets stagnated.

Wisereport's comment as of September 2026 described the four major media ad markets as flat while digital ads on online video and platforms keep growing.

The same source said that in the first half of 2026, advertising production and media agency sales rose slightly on a standalone basis, and that costs fell as the company focused on advertising after halting its semiconductor materials unit, which it described as an improving profitability trend.

Because the company now depends on a single advertising business after exiting semiconductor materials, it is directly exposed to swings in ad demand. In competition, the company is a smaller player than large full-service advertising agencies, with 2025 revenue of KRW 7.45 billion.

Growth in digital media could change the revenue mix of media agency work, but no disclosure shows which digital media segments the company is expanding into. The available data are not sufficient to place the industry at a specific point in its cycle using quantitative indicators.

Overall view

BK Holdings exited semiconductor materials and made advertising production and media agency work its core, but reported operating losses ran for four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025, and 2025 revenue fell to KRW 7.45 billion.

In 2026, operating losses narrowed from KRW 1.06 billion in Q1 to KRW 440 million in Q3, and Q3 revenue rose from the prior quarter to KRW 2.6 billion. The improvement reflects both cost adjustments and a revenue rebound, but the distance to annual profitability has not yet appeared in reported results.

On the financial side, the debt-to-equity ratio was 29.9% at the end of 2025, so leverage is modest, but the share count has grown through rights and allotment issues.

On listing status, a disclosure dated 16 June 2026 flagged the risk of managed-stock designation, and whether market capitalization meets the threshold remains an open variable. Adding 26 business purposes widened the range of options, but no concrete execution plan has been disclosed. The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly results.
