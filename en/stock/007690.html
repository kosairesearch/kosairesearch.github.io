02

Business structure

Kukdo Chemical makes epoxy resins, curing agents and polyol resins. Based on the first nine months of 2023, epoxy resins accounted for 95% of segment revenue and polyol resins for 5%, making epoxy the core business.

On a domestic headquarters basis at the same time, epoxy made up 69% of product revenue, curing agents 18%, composite-material epoxy 9%, and other products 4%. Stated annual capacity is 750,000 tons of epoxy and 120,000 tons of polyol, and the company is reported to hold about 65% of the domestic epoxy resin market.

Its global epoxy share was 18% in 2023, which made it the world's largest. By production region for the first nine months of 2023, headquarters accounted for 77% of revenue, China 18%, India 2%, and other domestic subsidiaries 3%. For 2023, domestic sales were about 21% and overseas sales about 79%.

Key demand comes from paint, composite materials and electronics customers, and the downstream industries are shipbuilding, construction and electrical electronics. High-value epoxy resins used in items such as wind turbine blades are reported to carry a margin more than twice that of general epoxy.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed figures, revenue fell from KRW 1.6 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.31 trillion in 2023, down 18.1% year on year, then edged down 0.5% to KRW 1.31 trillion in 2024 before rising 6.9% to KRW 1.4 trillion in 2025. Of 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year by revenue and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit dropped from KRW 96.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 19.2 billion in 2023, down 80.2%. It then rose 32.3% to KRW 25.3 billion in 2024 and 109.9% to KRW 53.2 billion in 2025, two consecutive years of growth. The operating margin was 6.1% in 2022, 1.5% in 2023, 1.9% in 2024 and 3.8% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 74.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.7 billion in 2023, down 89.6%, then rose 14.8% to KRW 8.84 billion in 2024 and 146.6% to KRW 21.8 billion in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 21.9 billion in 2025, close to the controlling-shareholder figure.

In the quarterly series, Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 534.3 billion, up 29.1% quarter on quarter, with operating profit of KRW 48 billion, up 365.5%, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 43.6 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 439.9 billion was up 24.1% year on year, but operating profit fell to KRW 4.82 billion, down 55.3% year on year, and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.22 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 9.04 billion in 2025, down from KRW 113.5 billion in 2022, and it fell even in 2024 and 2025 when operating profit rose. This gap between profit and cash generation is a point to track.

The disclosed numbers alone do not separate the causes of the Q2 spike and the Q3 drop, or show how much of either was one-off.

05

Industry analysis

Epoxy resins are an intermediate used in paints, electrical and electronics, construction, and adhesives. According to the company's first-half 2026 performance commentary, a shipbuilding boom kept demand for marine coatings firm, while expanded AI infrastructure investment raised demand in electrical and electronics.

Overseas, the company is widening its share through its Indian manufacturing subsidiary, and the closing of an EU anti-dumping investigation is described as having secured its overseas market base. The recovery in profit since 2024, after the 2023 low, coincides with this demand improvement.

In competitive terms, Kukdo Chemical holds the world's top global epoxy share and about 65% of the domestic market. On Alphasquare's annual data for 121 chemical companies as of October 16, 2026, its operating margin of 3.81% was above the industry average of 1.00%.

Still, downstream demand is concentrated in shipbuilding and electrical electronics, so a downturn in either cycle could sharply move earnings. The spread between raw material and product prices also drives profitability, which matters when reading the industry cycle.

Outlook

India is the first growth lever cited. Hana Securities said in a July 2026 report that the Indian market offers strong growth potential, while IBK Securities viewed the Indian epoxy and curing agent market as likely to grow faster than the global market and as the region to watch most closely for medium-term earnings.

In Europe, the closing of an anti-dumping investigation is seen as having clarified export conditions. On product mix, a securities research note dated February 2024 projected that high-value epoxy would rise from 17% of sales in 2023 to 30% in 2024.

That projection was made in 2023, so the actual share should be read against later filings. Since high-value products carry higher margins than general products, how far this share feeds into operating margin will shape the earnings direction.

Hana Securities set a target price of KRW 62,000 in its July 27, 2026 report, a view from that broker at that date. The Q3 2026 drop in operating profit to KRW 4.82 billion is the next thing to watch in whether it carries into Q4. The 2026 Q4 provisional results and the 2026 annual report will show how this trend is playing out.

08

Bull factors

Operating profit recovery and margin gains

Operating profit reached KRW 53.2 billion in 2025, up 109.9%, and the operating margin rose from 1.5% in 2023 to 3.8% in 2025. In Q2 2026 operating profit reached KRW 48 billion, up 365.5% quarter on quarter, a sharp quarterly recovery. A February 2024 securities note expected high-value epoxy to reach 30% of sales in 2024.

Expansion in India and overseas

Hana Securities noted that its Indian subsidiary's revenue grew at an average annual rate of 77% over 2023 to 2025. IBK Securities named the Indian epoxy and curing agent market as the region to watch most closely for medium-term earnings. The closing of an EU anti-dumping investigation is also cited as a positive for the overseas market base.

Shipbuilding and AI infrastructure demand

The first-half 2026 performance commentary described shipbuilding-driven demand for marine coatings as firm. It also cited growing electrical and electronics demand tied to AI infrastructure investment. Improvement in both end markets at once is cited as widening the epoxy business's sales base.

09

Bear factors

Q3 swing to loss and earnings volatility

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7.22 billion in Q3 2026, a swing into loss. Revenue of KRW 439.9 billion rose 24.1% year on year over the same period, but operating profit fell 55.3% to KRW 4.82 billion. Compared with KRW 48 billion in Q2, quarterly profit swung sharply.

Thin margins and weaker cash flow

The operating margin was 1.5% in 2023 and 1.9% in 2024, and reached only 3.8% in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 74.7% in 2022 to 99.6% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 873.2 billion at year-end 2025.

Even as operating profit rose in 2024 and 2025, operating cash flow fell to KRW 54.6 billion and then KRW 9.04 billion, a gap between profit and cash.

Cyclical dependence and concentration

The epoxy business depends on shipbuilding and electrical electronics demand, so results can swing with those cycles. Epoxy accounts for most revenue, leaving product diversification narrow. With overseas sales at about 79% in 2023, exposure to foreign demand and trade conditions is large.

10

Risk factors

Raw material spreads

Key raw materials for epoxy include EC and BPA, and the gap between raw material and product prices drives profitability. The 2023 profit drop, when operating profit fell 80.2%, was linked to narrowing epoxy spreads. Earnings direction depends on the spread level at any given time.

High-value product mix

Expanding the share of high-value epoxy has been presented as the main route to lifting the operating margin. A February 2024 securities note projected that high-value sales would rise from 17% in 2023 to 30% in 2024, but that projection was made in 2023.

If the actual share falls short of this plan, the higher margins of high-value products may not be fully reflected in earnings. This share needs to be verified in the periodic filings.

India expansion execution

Expanding market share through the Indian local manufacturing subsidiary is cited as a medium-term growth lever. IBK Securities viewed the Indian epoxy and curing agent market as the region to watch most closely for medium-term earnings.

If local share gains do not follow the plan, or growth in the Indian market slows, expectations for overseas growth could fall. Whether this growth appears in the 2026 annual report is the key point.

Overall view

Kukdo Chemical is an epoxy-centered specialty chemicals company whose operating profit recovered to KRW 53.2 billion in 2025, up 109.9%.

After KRW 48 billion in operating profit in Q2 2026, Q3 fell to KRW 4.82 billion and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.22 billion, so earnings durability is still being tested.

Strengths include the profit recovery, expansion in India and overseas markets, the plan to raise high-value product share, and improving shipbuilding and electrical electronics demand.

Weaknesses include the Q3 swing to loss, lower operating cash flow, a debt ratio that rose to 99.6%, and a business structure sensitive to cycles. Price-to-book sits below the sector average, but volatile earnings can change how multiples are read.

Hana Securities' target price of KRW 62,000 in its July 27, 2026 report is that broker's view. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 provisional results and the 2026 annual report's operating margin and cash flow.
