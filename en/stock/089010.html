02

Business structure

Chemtronicsco was founded in 1983 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2007. It built its base in chemical materials and expanded into TV and appliance electronic parts and modules, automotive electronics, and OLED glass etching.

In January 2025 it acquired wafer reclaim specialist J3, widening its footprint in semiconductor materials. Based on second-quarter 2026 sales, the electronics business accounted for 51%, distribution and other businesses including solar projects for 28%, semiconductors for 15% and automotive electronics for 7%.

A brokerage put chemical distribution at about 24% of consolidated sales, so the segment mix depends on how the businesses are counted. In electronics, wireless charging parts and sets hold a patent-based No. 1 share, and WISEREPORT's company monitor estimates the company holds about 70% of the wearables market.

The semiconductor unit has started full-scale production of ultra-high-purity PGMEA, and EUV lithography PGMEA sales are expected to grow from 2026. The automotive unit is expanding EV charger supply and pursuing entry as a HKMC Tier1 supplier.

In electronics, a new Hybrid OLED facility is still in customer qualification, so its sales contribution has been limited. The model mixes materials manufacturing, electronic components and distribution, so profitability differs across units and feeds directly into earnings swings.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales moved from KRW 621 billion in 2022 to KRW 542.3 billion in 2023, a 12.7% decline, before rising to KRW 575.2 billion in 2024 (up 6.1%) and KRW 637.5 billion in 2025 (up 10.8%). Sales have grown two years in a row, from 2024 through 2025. 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025, and 2023 was the smallest.

Operating profit hit its low in 2023 at KRW 19.1 billion, a 14.9% decline, then reached the period high of KRW 37.3 billion in 2024, a 95.2% increase, before falling 37.8% to KRW 23.2 billion in 2025.

The operating margin was 3.6% in 2022, 3.5% in 2023, 6.5% in 2024 and 3.6% in 2025, peaking in 2024 and falling back in 2025. The company attributed the 2025 sales increase partly to a business transfer effect, but said diversification and early-stage quality costs reduced profitability.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a KRW 9.11 billion loss in 2023, a shift to deficit, to KRW 20.6 billion in 2024, a return to profit, and then fell 50.2% to KRW 10.3 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 13.5 billion in 2025, a different figure from profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

By quarter, sales reached KRW 178.9 billion and operating profit KRW 8.06 billion in the third quarter of 2025, then the fourth quarter hit the low point with sales of KRW 161.6 billion (down 9.7% from the prior quarter), operating profit of KRW 1.9 billion (down 76.5%) and a controlling-interest net loss of KRW 5.18 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026 sales were KRW 174.5 billion, operating profit KRW 7.42 billion and controlling-interest net profit KRW 3.22 billion. The second quarter showed sales of KRW 175.3 billion, operating profit of KRW 10 billion and controlling-interest net profit of KRW 7.18 billion.

Third-quarter sales were KRW 177.3 billion, up 1.1% from the prior quarter but down 0.9% from a year earlier, while operating profit was KRW 9.71 billion, up 20.4% year on year.

A brokerage described first-quarter growth as weaker than expected, with a 4.3% operating margin, and linked it to weaker OLED panel production that reduced etching sales and a rising share of lower-margin appliance business. Controlling-interest net profit for the last four quarters combined totals KRW 12.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for semiconductor materials is rising on the back of AI chips and advanced manufacturing, according to a May 2026 brokerage report from Shinhan Investment Corp. The same report described semiconductor materials sales as entering a full-scale phase in a favorable industry backdrop.

The company is reviewing an expansion of its core chemical materials capacity from 25,000 tons to 58,000 tons a year. On the display side, weaker smartphone OLED panel production has weighed on demand, reducing sales of the etching process that carves circuits during OLED manufacturing.

Chemical product prices have risen since recent Middle East tensions and the aftermath of the Iran war, lifting distribution margins, the same report said.

In TGV glass substrates, the company aims to complete an integrated production system to bring the process in-house, and it plans to strengthen R&D in application technologies, including CPO.

Among its businesses, wireless charging and wearables parts rely on patent-based share, while semiconductors accounted for about 15% of second-quarter 2026 sales and remain a smaller unit. Among listed semiconductor materials companies in the same sector, ENF Technology is one reference point for comparison. How far the company scales its materials production as demand recovers will determine its competitive position.

06

Outlook

In a corporate value-up plan disclosed on March 26, 2026, the company said it would keep expanding R&D spending and set a target of 100% growth in semiconductor sales from the prior year, including ultra-high-purity PGMEA.

The same plan included completing an integrated production system for in-house TGV processing, expanding TGV application technologies including CPO, and a full-scale expansion of IT device etching sales. In semiconductors, PGMEA sales for EUV lithography are expected to grow from 2026.

The new Hybrid OLED facility in electronics is still in customer qualification, so the timing of its sales contribution is not set. Shinhan Investment Corp. raised its target price to KRW 49,000 in a May 19, 2026 report, according to press coverage.

That report based its view on semiconductor materials sales ramping from the second quarter and on stepwise growth supported by better margins in chemical distribution.

Using the idle land at the J3 Cheonan site and a permit for hydrofluoric acid-related business remain at the discussion stage, with no disclosed schedule.

Sales rose for three straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026 but were still down 0.9% from a year earlier, so the sustainability of top-line growth will show in the coming quarters. The 2026 annual report, the next regular filing, will show whether the semiconductor sales target was met.

Bull factors

Semiconductor sales target

The company disclosed a target of 100% growth in semiconductor sales from the prior year in 2026. With ultra-high-purity PGMEA production running, EUV lithography materials sales are expected to rise from 2026. Semiconductors were about 15% of second-quarter 2026 sales, leaving room for the business to expand.

Quarterly profit recovery

Sales have risen for three straight quarters from the first quarter of 2026. Operating profit moved to KRW 7.42 billion, KRW 10 billion and KRW 9.71 billion across the first three quarters, recovering from the KRW 1.9 billion low in the fourth quarter of 2025. Third-quarter operating profit was up 20.4% from a year earlier.

Capacity review and distribution margins

Shinhan Investment Corp. reported that the company is reviewing an expansion of core chemical materials capacity from 25,000 to 58,000 tons a year. The same report said higher chemical product prices are lifting distribution margins. The distribution business was cited as about 24% of consolidated sales.

Bear factors

Profitability fell in 2025

Operating profit fell 37.8% to KRW 23.2 billion in 2025, and the operating margin dropped from 6.5% in 2024 to 3.6%. The company cited diversification and early-stage quality costs as reasons for the lower profit. Controlling-interest net profit also fell 50.2% to KRW 10.3 billion.

Financial burden and weaker cash flow

Total liabilities were KRW 519.5 billion and total equity KRW 262.8 billion at end-2025, a debt ratio of 197.7%. The ratio moved from 170.7% in 2023 to 173.0% in 2024 and then 197.7% in 2025. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 647 million in 2025 from KRW 69.9 billion in 2024.

Slowdown in electronics and OLED

A brokerage attributed the weaker results to lower etching sales as smartphone OLED panel output slowed, and to a larger share of lower-margin appliance business. Electronics accounted for 51% of second-quarter 2026 sales, so a slowdown there has a large effect on total results. The new Hybrid OLED facility is still in customer qualification and has contributed little to sales.

10

Risk factors

Customer and industry concentration

Electronics made up 51% of second-quarter 2026 sales, so results are tied to smartphone OLED panel output. The first quarter of 2026, when etching sales declined, illustrates this exposure. If this weakness outpaces the growth of semiconductor materials, the overall profit recovery could slow.

Financial leverage

Total liabilities of KRW 519.5 billion and a 197.7% debt ratio reduce the room to absorb changes in borrowing conditions. With operating cash flow at KRW 647 million in 2025, funding capacity investment and debt repayment together is a burden. Of total equity of KRW 262.8 billion at end-2025, controlling-interest equity is KRW 235 billion.

Execution of business transition

The plan to expand capacity from 25,000 to 58,000 tons a year is still under review, with no fixed timing. Use of the idle land at the Cheonan site and a hydrofluoric acid-related permit are also at the discussion stage.

The company cited diversification and early-stage quality costs as weighing on 2025 profit, so transition costs could recur.

12

Overall view

Chemtronicsco's 2025 sales rose to KRW 637.5 billion, but operating profit fell to KRW 23.2 billion, so growth and profitability diverged that year. Since the first quarter of 2026, sales have risen for three straight quarters and operating profit has recovered to KRW 7.42 billion, KRW 10 billion and KRW 9.71 billion.

Brokerage reports point to semiconductor materials sales growth and better chemical distribution margins behind this trend. On the other side, lower etching sales tied to weaker OLED panel output and a larger appliance share remain constraints on profitability.

Financially, the debt ratio was 197.7% at end-2025 and operating cash flow was only KRW 647 million, so the investment burden is heavy. Controlling-interest net profit for the last four quarters totals KRW 12.3 billion, so the recovery is under way but the absolute level remains small.

The next checkpoints are whether the semiconductor sales target was met and the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and whether the capacity review becomes actual investment. Strengths in semiconductor materials coexist with weakness in electronics and the financial burden.
