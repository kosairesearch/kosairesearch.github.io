Moda-InnoChips: Profit Swings Test Its Electronics Margins

02

Business structure

Moda-InnoChips was founded in 2000 as a ceramic passive component maker and listed on KOSDAQ in December 2005.

The company focuses on components that block electrostatic discharge (ESD) and electromagnetic interference (EMI), developing and producing chip varistors, ESD filters, power inductors and piezoelectric actuators.

Its complex parts combine ESD protection and EMI noise filtering in one chip, which the company says can replace several discrete components, reducing board footprint and cost.

Its main customers are domestic and overseas mobile phone makers, and its applications include smartphones, wearables, computers, automotive, displays and IoT devices.

The business has two pillars, electronic components and fashion and goods distribution, and one corporate database shows the two segments at nearly equal weight. The distribution unit mainly sells clothing and goods, and in 2025 the company cited weak conditions in that unit as a cause of lower sales.

The company was called Innochips Technology until it changed its name in 2016. On March 9, 2026, a decision was disclosed for Rozen to absorb Moda-InnoChips through a merger, with Rozen surviving and Moda-InnoChips dissolving.

The largest shareholder of the dissolving company is Daemyung Chemical, with a 75.25% stake, and the filing states that the largest shareholder would not change if the merger is completed.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual sales declined from KRW 411.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 390 billion in 2023 (down 5.2% year on year), KRW 388.9 billion in 2024 (down 0.3%) and KRW 358.1 billion in 2025 (down 7.9%), marking three consecutive annual declines from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 23.2 billion in 2022, KRW 16.2 billion in 2023 (down 29.9%), KRW 38.6 billion in 2024 (up 138.0%) and KRW 27.9 billion in 2025 (down 27.8%). The operating margin was 5.6% in 2022, 4.2% in 2023, 9.9% in 2024 and 7.8% in 2025, with 2024 the highest year in the period.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 61.34 million in 2022 and a loss of KRW 7.36 billion in 2023, turned to a profit of KRW 12.3 billion in 2024, and returned to a loss of KRW 4.64 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 12.7 billion in 2024 and a loss of KRW 6.23 billion in 2025.

For 2025, the company cited higher cost ratios in its electronics segment, which hurt profitability, and weaker conditions in its distribution segment, which reduced sales; it also pointed to lower foreign currency translation gains in electronics and higher other bad-debt provisions in distribution.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 73.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 29.5 billion in 2023 and KRW 10.1 billion in 2024, then rose to KRW 27 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 had sales of KRW 76.8 billion and an operating loss of KRW 3.69 billion, followed by a fourth-quarter operating profit of KRW 11.4 billion on sales of KRW 94 billion, up 22.4% from the prior quarter.

The first quarter of 2026 recorded sales of KRW 87.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.06 billion, and the second quarter recorded sales of KRW 93.7 billion, operating profit of KRW 6.4 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.9 billion.

The third quarter of 2026 recorded sales of KRW 80.6 billion, up 4.9% year on year, but an operating loss of KRW 2.87 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 12.3 billion, and the last four quarters total a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 16.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

ESD and EMI protection components are passive parts whose demand grows as circuits in smartphones, wearables and automobiles become more complex. The company presents its products as supplying advanced fields such as smartphones, automotive, displays and IoT devices.

Corporate information sources describe ceramic passive components as a business with high entry barriers because materials, process and design technology are critical. Listed companies classified in the same electronics and components sector include Samsung Electro-Mechanics, LG Innotek, Isupetasys and Korea Circuit.

The 2025 margin decline was attributed directly to higher cost ratios in the electronics segment, so the cost structure of the components business is a key variable for margins. The distribution segment is exposed to consumer conditions, and weakness there was one driver of lower 2025 sales.

Operating profit rose 109.2% quarter on quarter in the second quarter of 2026 before the third quarter returned to an operating loss, which shows wide swings in quarterly profitability.

How order flow from mobile handset customers and product mix feed into quarterly results can be followed through the disclosed quarterly figures.

The schedule listed a merger contract date of March 9, 2026, a shareholder record date of March 24, 2026, a shareholder meeting planned for May 2026, and a merger effective date of July 1, 2026.

If the merger is completed, the listing status of the dissolving company changes, so later DART filings should be checked for whether the stock remains listed and how the ownership structure looks.

On the earnings side, third-quarter 2026 sales rose 4.9% year on year and the operating loss narrowed from KRW 3.69 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.87 billion, but the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened from KRW 9.02 billion to KRW 12.3 billion.

The gap between the operating line and the net line is therefore the next point to track. Because lower foreign currency translation gains and higher bad-debt provisions weighed on net income in 2025, exchange rates and collections in the distribution segment remain variables for net results. Bull factors

Return to profit in Q2 2026

Operating profit in the second quarter of 2026 was KRW 6.4 billion, up 109.2% from the prior quarter, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a profit of KRW 1.9 billion. In the fourth quarter of 2025, operating profit of KRW 11.4 billion followed an operating loss in the third quarter.

Quarterly profit has therefore rebounded in two separate periods. Whether the profit continues over consecutive quarters will be shown by later quarterly results.

Share price low against book net assets

Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 373.5 billion, of which KRW 354.9 billion belonged to controlling shareholders. Current market capitalization is below that controlling-shareholder equity, and the price-to-book ratio is well under 1x.

Whether book assets cushion earnings swings can be checked through the trend in equity. The point to watch is whether equity erodes quickly while losses continue.

Complex parts and product expansion

The company says its complex parts, which combine ESD protection and EMI filtering in one chip, can replace several discrete components and reduce board space and cost. It describes a steady pipeline of new products including ESD filters, power inductors and CM(E)F parts.

Applications extend beyond handsets to automotive and IoT devices. Whether these product strengths translate into profit depends on the trend in cost ratios.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 12.3 billion in the same quarter. The last four quarters total a net loss of KRW 16.1 billion attributable to controlling shareholders. Net results deteriorated even though sales rose 4.9% year on year, which is the main bearish point.

High debt and weaker cash generation

At end-2025, total liabilities of KRW 577.8 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 373.5 billion, and the debt ratio was 154.7%. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 73.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.1 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 27 billion in 2025, still below the 2022 level.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 12.3 billion in 2024, yet operating cash flow that year was only KRW 10.1 billion. The gap between earnings and cash adds to financial pressure.

Distribution weakness and bad-debt costs

The company cited weak conditions in its distribution segment as a reason for lower 2025 sales. Higher other bad-debt provisions in distribution were also named as a factor that reduced net income that year. Lower foreign currency translation gains in electronics added to the decline in net income. If distribution results do not recover, volatility in company-wide net income will persist.

Risk factors

Cost ratio and currency variables

The 2025 profit decline was directly attributed to higher cost ratios in the electronics segment. Lower foreign currency translation gains also weighed on net income. If cost pressure rises again or exchange rates move unfavorably, quarterly results could swing between profit and loss. The operating margin fell from 9.9% in 2024 to 7.8% in 2025, which underpins this risk.

Distribution demand and bad debts

The distribution segment's sales fell with weak business conditions, and other bad-debt provisions increased. If consumer conditions stay weak for long, sales declines and receivable losses could appear together. Operating profit in 2025 fell 27.8% year on year, and this segment's effects were part of that decline. The pace of distribution recovery is a variable for the stability of company-wide operating profit.

Merger timing and governance

The merger decision disclosed on March 9, 2026 scheduled completion for July 1, 2026. If the merger is completed, the listing status of the dissolving company's shares changes. The filing stated that no significant change in the largest shareholder's control was expected. The progress and final structure of the merger must be checked through later DART filings.
