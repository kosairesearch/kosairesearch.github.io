02

Business structure

SUNGDO Engineering & Construction, founded in 1988, is a general construction and engineering company that provides turnkey cleanroom facility services for semiconductor, display and secondary battery plants, covering design, procurement, construction, commissioning and maintenance.

Its reporting segments are High-tech Industrial Facilities, Gas & Chemical, Plant, General Construction, Real Estate Development and Others.

In the first quarter of 2026, according to the quarterly report, High-tech Industrial Facilities and General Construction each accounted for 48% of revenue, Plant for 2%, Gas & Chemical for 1% and Real Estate Development for 1%.

The High-tech segment covers cleanroom construction for IT, bio and environmental technology industries, while General Construction centers on logistics and industrial plant works.

Gas & Chemical, classified as a new business from 2023, handles industrial gas plants and accounts for roughly 10% of total revenue, according to a March 2026 report by The Bell.

Domestic sales made up 81% of first-quarter 2026 revenue and exports 19%, with overseas operations run through subsidiaries in the United States, Vietnam, Hungary and Poland.

Its customers center on large domestic semiconductor and display manufacturers, including Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and LG Display, and the company has recently extended into secondary battery and bio plants.

Real estate development is a new-town project in Daqing, Heilongjiang Province, China, which the company has run since 2007.

Its 2025 mechanical and gas construction capacity rating stood at KRW 270.7 billion, ranking 14th among licensed firms, and in January 2026 it swapped treasury shares with a domestic mechanical-facility peer, with each becoming a shareholder of the other.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 705.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 678.6 billion in 2023 (down 3.9% year over year), then rose sharply to KRW 999.6 billion in 2024 (up 47.3% year over year).

In 2025 it declined to KRW 743.4 billion (down 25.6% year over year), so across 2022–2025 the largest year was 2024 and the smallest was 2023. The press reported the 2024 revenue as an all-time record, and there are reports that U.S. revenue that year was KRW 157.3 billion, up sharply from KRW 4.9 billion in 2022.

Operating profit turned positive, moving from an operating loss of KRW 4.99 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.3 billion in 2023, and then rose to KRW 27.3 billion in 2024 (up 121.7% year over year) and KRW 57.7 billion in 2025 (up 111.4% year over year), increasing for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

The operating margin improved from -0.7% in 2022 to 1.8% in 2023, 2.7% in 2024 and 7.8% in 2025.

In 2025, operating profit rose sharply even though revenue declined, which improved the margin; in its preliminary earnings disclosure of February 2026, the company explained that the domestic economic slump weakened the order environment for its general construction and high-tech businesses, while overseas subsidiaries such as those in the U.S. and Vietnam posted relatively solid results.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.11 billion in 2022, KRW 3.99 billion in 2023 (up 258.3% year over year), KRW 15.3 billion in 2024 (up 283.9% year over year) and KRW 28.2 billion in 2025 (up 83.9% year over year), increasing for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Net income for 2025 including non-controlling interests was also KRW 28.2 billion, the same as net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for that year.

On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 had revenue of KRW 176.1 billion, operating profit of KRW 14.7 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 12.6 billion, but this changed to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.52 billion in the fourth quarter.

In the first quarter of 2026 it returned to profit, with net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 9.99 billion, but it then declined for two consecutive quarters, to KRW 5.67 billion in the second quarter and KRW 2.8 billion in the third quarter.

The sum of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the most recent four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) is KRW 9.94 billion.

The operating margin in the third quarter of 2026 was lower than in the same period a year earlier, and the debt ratio rose from 95.8% in 2022 to 174.8% in 2024 before falling to 119.6% in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Demand in the company's core markets depends on a recovery in semiconductor investment.

In a March 2026 report, The Bell said that renewed chip-industry investment is raising demand for mechanical facilities that go into fabs, while the same report said a slow recovery in construction activity has constrained growth in the general construction segment.

The company also said in its 2025 earnings disclosure that order conditions were weak amid a domestic economic slowdown, so the pace of domestic construction demand is a key variable for the general construction business.

To strengthen its high-tech business, the company bought land in Anseong, Gyeonggi Province, in 2022 and built a research center and factories 1 and 2 there.

In the competitive landscape, SUNGDO ranked 14th among licensed holders of mechanical and gas construction capacity in 2025, while the domestic number two in that license category, a mechanical-facility peer, became a cross-shareholder through a treasury share swap in January 2026.

In civil and building construction, it ranked 49th in 2025. The general construction business competes with large construction firms affiliated with conglomerates, and the CEO emphasized accumulated experience and new technology in a 2023 interview.

In cleanroom construction, media have reported work for Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and LG Display. Customer concentration also means results are sensitive to the investment cycles of a few large clients.

Expansion into secondary battery and bio plants can be read as an effort to reduce dependence on the semiconductor business.

06

Outlook

The company has not published an annual earnings target that can be verified, so the outlook here rests on disclosed contracts and completion dates.

The Shinsung Osong plant expansion contract disclosed on October 7, 2026 is worth KRW 76.3 billion, runs from October 7, 2026 to July 31, 2027, excludes value-added tax and may be adjusted for design changes.

Payment terms call for an advance of KRW 5 billion after signing and an interim payment of KRW 5 billion in January 2027.

The B1 expansion for a domestic PCB maker, disclosed as a change contract dated October 30, 2026, has a total contract value of KRW 170 billion, of which the company's 65% obligation share is KRW 110.5 billion, with completion due by June 15, 2027. Both contracts are large relative to 2025 revenue of KRW 743.4 billion.

However, the pace at which orders convert into revenue depends on percentage of completion.

As of the first-quarter 2026 quarterly report, the Yongin piping project was 37% complete with a deadline of June 30, 2027, the BMW expansion project was 5% complete with a deadline of December 31, 2027, and the MSC new building project was 1% complete with a deadline of April 30, 2028.

As of a March 2026 report, the China real estate business was at a stage where construction and sales timing were being reviewed against market conditions.

The points to confirm are the revenue mix and operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and how quickly post-October 2026 orders convert to revenue in the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report.

Bull factors

Large expansion contracts disclosed in succession

The Shinsung Osong plant expansion disclosed in October 2026 is worth KRW 76.3 billion, while the Daeduck B1 expansion totals KRW 170 billion in contract value, of which the 65% share is KRW 110.5 billion.

A new-build contract for the same PCB customer was also disclosed in January 2026, showing a continuing order flow in the high-tech segment. The speed at which these orders turn into revenue will be a key variable for upcoming results.

Improving operating profit and margin

Operating profit turned positive in 2023 and rose to KRW 27.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 57.7 billion in 2025, a second straight year of growth. The operating margin improved from -0.7% in 2022 to 7.8% in 2025. Profit rose in 2025 even as revenue fell 25.6%, which points to a shift in the profitability structure. The company attributed the improvement partly to overseas units in the United States and Vietnam.

Overseas units and gas and chemical business base

Media reports say US revenue rose from KRW 4.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 157.3 billion in 2024, and Hungary revenue reached KRW 87.0 billion in 2024. The Gas & Chemical business, classified as a new segment from 2023, has settled at about 10% of total revenue. These two areas help spread revenue sources while domestic construction demand is slow.

Bear factors

Slowing quarterly profit

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 8.72 billion fell 40.7% from a year earlier, and attributable net income of KRW 2.8 billion fell 77.8%. Attributable net income peaked at KRW 9.99 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then declined for two consecutive quarters.

The four-quarter total of KRW 9.94 billion sits well below the KRW 28.2 billion recorded for 2025 as a full year.

Revenue contraction and weak domestic orders

Revenue fell 25.6% to KRW 743.4 billion in 2025 and dropped 12.7% quarter on quarter to KRW 144.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026. The company attributed the weakness to a domestic economic slowdown that hit order conditions in general construction and high-tech work. The Bell cited a slow recovery in construction activity as a factor.

Balance sheet burden and contingent liabilities

The debt ratio fell from 174.8% in 2024 to 119.6% in 2025, but total liabilities of KRW 327.8 billion still exceed total equity of KRW 274 billion.

The subrogation claim lawsuit ended after the Supreme Court dismissed all appeals in a May 2026 ruling, but its financial impact needs to be confirmed in follow-up disclosures. The timing of construction and sales in the China real estate business is also being reviewed against market conditions.

10

Risk factors

Customer capex cycle

Results are tied to capital spending by large semiconductor, display and PCB customers. As the company noted for 2025, if customer investment schedules slip, construction progress and revenue recognition can slip with them.

Even with the large contracts disclosed in October 2026, completion deadlines run into mid-2027 and beyond, so revenue will be recognized across several quarters.

Litigation and balance sheet

A May 15, 2026 disclosure showed that the Supreme Court dismissed all appeals in the subrogation claim lawsuit, with appeal costs assigned to the company. Even with the litigation concluded, total liabilities still exceed total equity. Changes in interest rates and borrowing conditions could affect how financing costs weigh on profit.

Overseas units and China real estate

Overseas subsidiaries in the United States, Vietnam, Hungary and Poland have grown in importance, but media reported that overseas results fell sharply in 2025 excluding Vietnam. The China real estate project in Daqing, Heilongjiang, is being timed against market conditions for construction and sales. Local economic conditions abroad and changes in China's property market remain sources of earnings volatility.

12

Overall view

SUNGDO Engineering & Construction recovered from an operating loss in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 57.7 billion in 2025, with the operating margin moving from -0.7% to 7.8%.

However, 2025 revenue of KRW 743.4 billion fell 25.6% year on year, and in the third quarter of 2026 revenue rose while operating profit and attributable net income declined from a year earlier.

Expansion contracts disclosed in October 2026, including KRW 76.3 billion for Shinsung and KRW 110.5 billion for the 65% share of the Daeduck B1 project, have completion dates running from mid-2027 to April 2028.

The debt ratio fell from 174.8% in 2024 to 119.6% in 2025, but total liabilities of KRW 327.8 billion still exceed total equity of KRW 274 billion. Strengths include the order recovery, the improving operating margin, and the base built by overseas units and the gas and chemical business.

Weaknesses include the slowdown in quarterly profit, slow recovery in domestic construction demand, and volatility from overseas results and the China real estate project. Valuation reflects a price-to-book level at a discount to net assets alongside volatile earnings.

The next points to confirm are the operating margin and order conversion pace in the 2026 annual report, and the attributable net income trend in the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report.
