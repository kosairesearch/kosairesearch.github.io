02

Business structure

Dobu is a respiratory-protection manufacturer headquartered in Gwangju, Gyeonggi Province, and it is listed on the Konex market. Its registered industry code is other textile products manufacturing.

Its products include industrial dust and gas masks, emergency escape hoods for fire and toxic-gas situations, and relief masks. The company is reported to hold products certified under occupational safety and health standards and to supply parts of Asia through export channels.

A revenue breakdown by product line could not be confirmed from available disclosure summaries, so it is described only qualitatively.

The COVID-19 boom explains much of the earnings swing. Per FnGuide, revenue in 2020 was KRW 124.1 billion with operating profit of KRW 34.1 billion, but in 2021 revenue fell to KRW 5.2 billion with an operating loss of KRW 12.1 billion.

FnGuide says results deteriorated as the health-mask market shrank, while the company has kept stable sales and market share in civilian gas masks through negotiated contracts with local governments. Given this structure, gas-mask demand is exposed to municipal budgets and procurement timing.

A report dated March 23, 2026 had a headline describing Dobu as an emerging player in the civilian gas-mask market. The sector label is textile/fashion/lifestyle, but peers such as Youngone, F&F and Handsome differ in products, customers and demand drivers, so direct comparison is difficult. Market-share figures by competitor could not be verified from reliable statistics and are not presented.

03

Recent trends

For 2025 (the 19th fiscal term), separate-basis revenue was KRW 16,451 million, with an operating loss of KRW 148 million and a net loss of KRW 220 million. On a consolidated basis, revenue was KRW 16,451 million, the operating loss was KRW 157 million and the net loss was KRW 229 million.

The auditor's opinion was unqualified for both the separate and consolidated statements. Per FnGuide, separate-basis 2024 revenue was KRW 16.6 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 10.5 billion and a net loss of KRW 16.1 billion.

The specific reason the loss narrowed so much in 2025 is not documented in the sources reviewed, so no conclusion is drawn. At the end of 2025, separate total assets were KRW 11,750 million, total liabilities KRW 11,127 million and total equity KRW 623 million. Paid-in capital on the same basis was KRW 19,022 million. Equity that is small relative to paid-in capital points to a large accumulated deficit.

The share price is highly volatile. Pinpoint News reported that Dobu fell 14.35% to close at KRW 561 on October 16. On October 27 the stock closed at KRW 800, down 11.01% from the previous close, for a market capitalization of KRW 30.4 billion.

According to Investing.com, the 52-week range runs from KRW 478 to KRW 1,219. The same source shows three-month average volume of only 432 shares, which indicates very low liquidity. Trading value on that day was just KRW 589,956.

At the annual general meeting on March 27, 2026, shareholders approved all agenda items, including the new appointment of Park Hyung-min as an inside director.

Outlook

The path ahead depends on whether civilian gas-mask demand persists, whether sales of industrial and disaster-response products expand, and whether the balance sheet stabilizes.

Dobu took part in the joint investor relations event for Konex-listed companies held at the Korea Exchange on November 7, 2025, where it presented its product portfolio. The company said at the event that it would continue discussions on investment cooperation and overseas sales expansion.

A disclosure listing shows an investor relations results filing on May 22, 2026, a change to the designated advisor contract on June 22, and an investor relations event filing on September 17. The details of these filings and the size of any new contracts could not be confirmed.

FnGuide carries no broker estimates for 2026, so external forecasts offer little guide to earnings. On business metrics, the operating loss has narrowed to a small amount on flat sales, so there is room for the operating margin to improve through higher sales and cost control.

Given the thin equity base, whether concrete balance-sheet repair measures appear in disclosures is a key item to watch.

05

Bull factors

Steady civilian gas-mask demand base

In civilian gas masks, the company has kept stable sales and market share through negotiated contracts with local governments. Because demand is tied to disaster preparedness and public procurement, its sensitivity to the economic cycle may be lower than that of apparel businesses.

A report on March 23 also covered the company's presence in the civilian gas-mask market. This speaks to revenue stability, however, and does not guarantee profit levels.

Sharply narrower operating loss

Per FnGuide, the separate operating loss in 2024 was KRW 10.5 billion. The separate operating loss for 2025 was disclosed as KRW 148 million. The much smaller loss can be read as a move closer to break-even. Whether the cause is a one-off factor or a structural improvement has not been confirmed.

Certified product line and overseas channels

The range is broad, from industrial dust and gas masks to escape hoods and relief masks. The company holds products certified under occupational safety standards and is reported to supply parts of Asia.

At the investor relations event, investors asked about performance comparisons of industrial and chemical-biological-radiological masks and their applicability by field. Overseas expansion could reduce dependence on domestic procurement, but concrete results have not yet been confirmed in disclosures.

06

Bear factors

Thin equity and heavy liabilities

At the end of 2025, separate total liabilities were KRW 11,127 million against total assets of KRW 11,750 million. Total equity was only KRW 623 million. With paid-in capital at KRW 19,022 million, the accumulated deficit appears to have consumed most of the capital. Even a small additional loss could strain financial stability.

Stagnant sales and continued losses

Even with separate revenue of KRW 16,451 million in 2025, the company posted an operating loss of KRW 148 million and a net loss of KRW 220 million. Compared with the KRW 124.1 billion of revenue in 2020 per FnGuide, the current scale is far smaller.

No clear new growth driver has been confirmed since the COVID-19 boom faded. Continued losses, even small ones, would further erode the equity cushion.

Extremely low liquidity and sharp swings

Three-month average volume is about 432 shares. Trading value on October 27 was only KRW 589,956, and on October 16 the stock fell 14.35% in a day to close at KRW 561. Small orders can move the price sharply, so it is hard to judge whether the market price reflects company value in a stable way. Order execution and the ability to exit positions are constrained.

07

Risk factors

Policy and demand

Civilian gas-mask sales rely on negotiated contracts with local governments, so they are exposed to local budgets and changes in procurement methods. Lower unit prices or volumes could shake revenue quickly. The health-mask market shrank after COVID-19 ended, which shows that hygiene-driven demand is hard to sustain. The timing of any demand increase tied to security or disaster concerns is also hard to predict.

Financial and listing

Equity is very low relative to paid-in capital, so any further loss would reduce the cushion more. Liabilities are close to total assets, which makes debt maturities and funding conditions important. No capital increase or other balance-sheet repair plan could be confirmed.

Specific requirements under the Konex listing rules should be checked separately against exchange disclosures and rule amendments.

Market and liquidity

The 52-week range is wide, from KRW 478 to KRW 1,219. With so little volume, theme-driven flows or small sell orders can move the price sharply. Price changes may occur regardless of company results. In stretches with wide gaps between quotes, executing at a desired price can be difficult.

08

Overall view

Dobu is a small manufacturer specializing in civilian gas masks and industrial respiratory protection, and its business differs in nature from apparel and fashion companies despite its sector label.

Per its disclosed results, separate-basis revenue for 2025 was KRW 16,451 million and the operating loss was KRW 148 million. Compared with the KRW 10.5 billion operating loss for 2024 reported by FnGuide, the loss has narrowed sharply, though the reason has not been confirmed.

On the bullish side are steady demand from municipal negotiated contracts, the narrower loss, a certified product line and overseas channels. On the bearish side are equity that is very low relative to paid-in capital, liabilities close to total assets, stagnant sales and extremely thin trading.

Key items to track are gas-mask procurement trends, results from overseas sales, disclosures on balance-sheet repair and changes in the operating margin. This report is for information only, and investment decisions rest with the reader.
