Battery foil price hikes are the key to breakeven

02

Business structure

Solus Advanced Materials runs two core businesses: battery foil (copper foil for lithium-ion batteries) and OLED materials. Battery foil production centers on the Tatabánya plant in Hungary, with annual capacity of 38,000 tons, and the company has described it as the only battery foil production base in Europe.

A Quebec plant in Canada with 25,000 tons of planned capacity was reported in April 2026 as targeted for completion this year and operation next year. In March 2026 the company said it counts eight global battery and automaker customers, including CATL.

In its 2025 results, the copper foil division, which includes HVLP foil for AI accelerator substrates, posted sales of KRW 306.5 billion, up 56.2% from a year earlier, while battery foil sales fell 26% on weaker global EV demand.

The OLED business supplies R-Prime hole transport materials for mobile displays and IP-based hole blocking layer materials, and press reports said its Iksan Hamyeol plant was completed in March 2026, doubling capacity.

The sale of the Luxembourg copper foil unit, Circuit Foil Luxembourg (CFL), was disclosed in January 2026 under a share purchase agreement of about KRW 301.4 billion. The business is being refocused on battery foil, and the completion and timing of the copper foil asset sale should be checked in the periodic filings.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 461.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 429.4 billion in 2023 (down 6.9% year on year), then rose for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025, to KRW 570.9 billion in 2024 (up 33.0% year on year) and KRW 616.1 billion in 2025 (up 7.9% year on year). 2025 is the largest year of the 2022-2025 period.

The company posted operating losses of KRW 45.2 billion in 2022, KRW 73.2 billion in 2023, KRW 54.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 73.3 billion in 2025, four straight years in the red, and its operating margin was at its worst in 2023 at -17.0%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned from a net loss of KRW 11.3 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 187.5 billion in 2023, and the sale of its subsidiary Solus Biotech that year (securing KRW 350.0 billion) may have affected the earnings structure.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders in 2024 was KRW 4.64 billion, down 97.5%, and in 2025 it turned back to a loss, with a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 66.9 billion.

Including non-controlling interests, the net loss for 2025 was KRW 138.4 billion, larger than on a controlling-shareholder basis. Operating cash flow was -KRW 190.0 billion in 2024 and -KRW 18.1 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 145.1 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 169.5 billion in Q4 2025 (up 16.8% quarter on quarter) and KRW 192.6 billion in Q1 2026 (up 13.6% quarter on quarter), but fell to KRW 125.5 billion in Q3 2026, down 13.5% year on year.

Operating losses continued every quarter from KRW 21.2 billion in Q3 2025 through KRW 30.1 billion in Q3 2026, with the largest being KRW 31.6 billion in Q2 2026.

The Q2 2026 net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 43.3 billion was a profit despite an operating loss, so non-operating items and other factors are suspected to have contributed, and the breakdown must be confirmed in the periodic report.

In Q3 2026, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 57.6 billion, a return to a loss. The company cited higher electricity costs in Hungary and increased depreciation expenses as factors behind the Q3 earnings deterioration.

05

Industry analysis

Battery foil demand follows EV and ESS battery demand. In its 2025 results the company said battery foil sales fell 26% on weaker global EV demand, and in 3Q 2026 it attributed higher shipments to expanding demand from global battery and EV customers.

Eugene Investment & Securities said in a November 2026 report that some rivals have converted battery foil lines to circuit foil, which is improving supply and demand for battery foil. The same report described demand for battery foil in European EVs and US ESS as firm.

Rising demand from US and European customers for non-Chinese battery foil was read as strengthening suppliers' pricing power. The same report noted that Solus Advanced Materials is the only company producing battery foil in Europe.

Press coverage in November 2026 pointed to the EU's Made in Europe policy direction as favorable to local production capacity.

In copper foil, demand for high-value products for AI accelerator substrates is rising, and in April 2026 a securities report described the company as a main vendor of substrate circuit foil to a global AI company. The company also says it has the largest number of customers among domestic copper foil makers.

06

Outlook

At its 3Q 2026 results, the company said it will complete price negotiations with battery foil customers and continue to raise prices in stages in 2027. Negotiations with some key customers are already done, and the higher prices will be fully reflected from 4Q 2026 results.

Eugene Investment & Securities said in its November 2026 report that processing fees for major customers had risen 50% and that remaining customers would be finished within the year. The same report said Hungarian plant 2's restart timing could weigh on 4Q results but would have limited effect on 2027.

The company said in October 2026 that it expects EBITDA break-even in early 2027 and quarterly operating profit break-even later in 2027. Eugene Investment & Securities expects a return to profit from the second half of 2027.

The 2026 annual revenue target the company gave in its 2025 results is KRW 533.0 billion, including KRW 394.0 billion from battery foil and KRW 139.0 billion from OLED.

The Canadian Quebec plant is targeted for completion in 2026 and operation in 2027, with capacity of 25,000 tons, and the company said in April 2026 that the site could expand to as much as 63,000 tons.

The points to check are how much of the 4Q price increases reaches the P&L, whether EBITDA turns positive in early 2027, and whether the Canadian plant starts on schedule.

08

Bull factors

Execution of battery foil price increases

The company says it has concluded price talks with key customers, with higher prices reflected from 4Q 2026 results. Eugene Investment & Securities reported in November 2026 that processing fees for major customers rose 50%. The bull case is that realized price increases could offset part of the power and depreciation burden.

The only battery foil base in Europe

The Hungarian plant, with 38,000 tons of annual capacity, is described as the only battery foil production base in Europe. Once the Canadian plant runs, the company would have production in both North America and Europe. Analysts say rising demand for non-Chinese battery foil has strengthened supplier bargaining power.

Growth in AI circuit foil and copper foil

The company said copper foil division sales reached KRW 306.5 billion in 2025, up 56.2% from a year earlier. High-value HVLP foil grew with demand for AI accelerator substrates. In April 2026 a securities report cited its position as a main vendor of substrate circuit foil to a global AI company as a strength.

09

Bear factors

Five straight quarters of losses

Operating losses have continued for five straight quarters from 3Q 2025 to 3Q 2026, and the 3Q 2026 operating loss was KRW 30.1 billion. Controlling-shareholder net loss returned to -KRW 57.6 billion in 3Q 2026. Since the price effect appears only from 4Q onward, the timing of the return to profit could slip.

Power costs and depreciation burden

Hungarian wholesale power prices rose about 38%, from EUR 109.2 to EUR 151.1 per MWh between 2Q and 3Q 2026. Higher depreciation from new equipment also hit the P&L. If power prices rise again or utilization stays low, the fixed-cost burden could persist.

Balance sheet strain and weak earnings base

The debt ratio rose from 59.1% in 2022 to 128.5% in 2025, and total liabilities were KRW 1.21 trillion at end-2025. Operating cash flow was -KRW 190 billion in 2024 and -KRW 18.1 billion in 2025. Financial room may depend on the timing and size of proceeds from the copper foil asset sale.

10

Risk factors

Failure to pass on prices

Price increases have been agreed with some customers, while talks with the rest and further increases for 2027 remain open. If customers do not accept the increases, the margin recovery will be limited. The outcome will show up in quarterly results and changes in the customer mix.

Energy and facility costs

Higher Hungarian power prices and depreciation on new equipment weighed on 3Q 2026 results. The fixed-cost burden could change depending on when the Canadian plant starts and when Hungarian plant 2 restarts. If investment continues, the depreciation burden could last longer.

Balance sheet and asset sales

The debt ratio of 128.5% and operating cash outflows persist. The timing and size of proceeds from the CFL sale will determine the financial cushion. If the sale is delayed, the funding burden could last longer.

  4. Around April 2027 (1Q 2027 results)

    The core question is whether quarterly EBITDA turned positive in 1Q 2027, which would indicate whether price increases are translating into earnings improvement.

  5. During 2027 (company target timing)

12

Overall view

Solus Advanced Materials has the strengths of battery foil price increases and local production in Europe, but it posted an operating loss of KRW 30.1 billion in 3Q 2026, extending losses to five straight quarters.

Bull factors include its position as the only European battery foil base, eight customers, AI circuit foil demand and price increases reflected from 4Q.

Bear factors include higher Hungarian power costs and depreciation, a 128.5% debt ratio, negative operating cash flow and a controlling-shareholder net loss of -KRW 66.9 billion in 2025.

The company targets quarterly EBITDA break-even in early 2027 and quarterly operating break-even later in 2027, and Eugene Investment & Securities sees a possible return to profit from the second half of 2027. Both outlooks depend on executing the price increases and stabilizing costs.

The size and timing of proceeds from the copper foil sale could change the balance sheet. The P/B ratio sits below 1x, but the thin earnings base limits comparisons on earnings.

Next checkpoints are the 4Q 2026 preliminary results, the operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report, and whether EBITDA turns positive in 1Q 2027.
