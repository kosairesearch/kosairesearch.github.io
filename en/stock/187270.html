02

Business structure

Shin Hwa Contech is a connector and automotive electronics specialist founded in 2002, and it currently holds six unlisted subsidiaries. Its base has been built on mobile and home-appliance connectors, and it is expanding its product portfolio into automotive electronic components.

Main products include connectors and components for electronics, mobile phones, TVs and automobiles. The business started from precision connectors for data communication and is extending into automotive electronics, robotics and AI voice recognition.

In automotive, the company holds Tier 1 supplier status with Hyundai Motor and Kia and has Wiring SQ certification. Its key new product is the in-car AI array microphone, which the company says drove Q3 2026 revenue growth as mass production began.

The voice-recognition microphone module is reported to be secured for about 7 million units over six years on Hyundai-Kia platforms. Production bases are in China's Qingdao and Vietnam's Vina and Hung Yen units. Customers include automakers as well as electronics makers such as Samsung Electronics.

Segment revenue shares are hard to quantify, so the relative weight of the mobile and home-appliance core versus automotive electronics is the main point to watch.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 44.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 51.9 billion in 2023 (up 15.6% year on year), held at KRW 52 billion in 2024 (no change year on year), and reached KRW 53.4 billion in 2025 (up 2.8%), making 2025 the largest year in the 2022-2025 span.

Operating profit grew to KRW 4.91 billion in 2023 (up 24.4%) and KRW 6.96 billion in 2024 (up 41.7%), which was the peak year, before falling 31.1% to KRW 4.8 billion in 2025.

The operating margin went from 8.8% in 2022 to 9.5% in 2023 and 13.4% in 2024, then slipped back to 9.0% in 2025, so the margin did not recover to the 2024 level even as revenue grew.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 7.58 billion in 2024 (up 91.8%) and dropped to KRW 3.1 billion in 2025 (down 59.2%), while 2025 net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 2.9 billion.

Quarterly, Q4 2025 operating profit was KRW 14.51 million and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 40.97 million, a swing into loss, before Q1 2026 returned to profit with operating profit of KRW 1.38 billion and controlling net income of KRW 1.64 billion.

Q2 2026 revenue rose 21.9% quarter on quarter to KRW 16.4 billion, but operating profit fell 50.4% to KRW 684 million. Q3 2026 operating margin was 12.7%, up 9.2 percentage points from 3.5% a year earlier.

For the first half, the company said investment costs rose as it focused resources on building mass-production capacity for its in-car AI microphone business, and for the nine-month period it attributed the profit pressure to costs from new product development and mass-production setup.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 4.47 billion. On the balance sheet, total equity was KRW 70.9 billion and total liabilities KRW 24.2 billion at the end of 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 61.6% in 2022 to 34.1% in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 6.54 billion in 2025, below KRW 12.5 billion in 2023.

05

Industry analysis

Connector and automotive electronics demand is tied to both consumer electronics and automobiles. On the auto side, the company is strengthening growth momentum by doubling supply of in-car digital microphones, a core component for the autonomous-driving era.

Mobile demand moves with smartphone production: in the 2025 first half, connector demand fell on weaker smartphone production at Samsung Electronics, but revenue held up because the C Type connector content per device doubled to two. A rugged integrated C Plug approval was cited as a potential price uplift.

In cycle terms, the mobile and home-appliance core looks mature, while automotive electronics is in an early volume-ramp phase.

On competition, Shin Hwa Contech's Tier 1 status with Hyundai and Kia is a barrier to entry, but verified market-share data for competitors on the same voice-recognition platforms was not found. The Vietnam and China production bases may help cost and supply responsiveness. Reading the industry outlook requires tracking automobile output and smartphone shipments together.

06

Outlook

The company expects related revenue to rise as the number of vehicle models adopting its AI array microphone expands in 2027. In the 2026 first-half filing, it said related revenue would increase sequentially in the second half as mass-production volumes grow.

In 2024, the CEO projected that total revenue would more than double from 2023 once mass production began in the second quarter of 2026. Cumulative revenue through Q3 2026 was KRW 45.6 billion, up 16.1% year on year, so the growth pace confirmed so far falls short of that doubling projection.

On supply, the 14 million-unit phased delivery schedule runs from the second half of 2026 through 2032, so how quickly volume reaches quarterly revenue is the next checkpoint. On profitability, the key test is whether the operating margin moves back toward the 13.4% seen in 2024 as investment costs ease.

The 2026 annual report, the next regular filing, will show full-year operating margin and the automotive revenue trend.

08

Bull factors

Expanding in-car AI microphone supply

The company plans to supply 14 million units in phases from the second half of 2026 through 2032. It attributes its revenue growth to the ramp of its AI array microphone. The long delivery window could build a multi-year revenue base.

Early signs of margin recovery

The Q3 2026 operating margin was 12.7%, versus 3.5% a year earlier. Controlling net income returned to profit in Q1 2026 and reached KRW 2.17 billion in Q3. The company says its additional large-scale investment burden is limited now that mass-production capacity is in place.

Stronger balance sheet

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 61.6% in 2022 to 34.1% in 2025, passing through 55.4% in 2023 and 45.1% in 2024. Total equity was KRW 70.9 billion and total liabilities KRW 24.2 billion at end-2025. With lower leverage, room for investment appears broader.

09

Bear factors

Profit decline in 2025

Full-year 2025 operating profit fell 31.1% to KRW 4.8 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders dropped 59.2% to KRW 3.1 billion. The operating margin fell from 13.4% to 9.0%. Revenue grew to KRW 53.4 billion, but profit did not follow.

Investment costs and quarterly volatility

Q2 2026 operating profit was KRW 684 million, down 50.4% from the prior quarter. First-half operating profit fell 51.2% to KRW 2.1 billion. The company describes the decline as a temporary effect of upfront investment, but quarterly profit has not yet settled into a stable pattern.

Weak legacy mobile demand

Connector demand declined as Samsung Electronics' smartphone production weakened. While the mobile and home-appliance core softens, automotive electronics must fill the profit gap. Controlling net income swung into a KRW 40.97 million loss in Q4 2025, showing how core-business swings can hit profit directly.

10

Risk factors

Customer and platform concentration

Revenue from the in-car AI microphone depends on mass production of specific models on Hyundai and Kia platforms. If model expansion slows, the revenue growth the company expects for 2027 could be delayed. The supply plan runs through 2032, but actual delivery pace depends on automakers' production schedules.

Profit volatility

The operating margin fell to 9.0% in 2025 from 13.4% in 2024, and quarterly operating profit ranged from KRW 14.51 million to KRW 2 billion in the last five quarters. New product development and mass-production setup costs have driven quarterly results. If cost burdens do not ease, the margin may not recover even as revenue grows.

Core demand and currency exposure

Mobile and home-appliance connector demand moves with finished-goods shipments such as smartphones. With several overseas production subsidiaries, currency moves and local production conditions can also affect costs. The detailed effect should be checked in the currency-related notes of the periodic reports.

12

Overall view

Shin Hwa Contech pairs a core connector business with a new growth engine in in-car AI voice-recognition microphones. Q3 2026 results improved clearly, with revenue of KRW 15.8 billion, operating profit of KRW 2 billion and an operating margin of 12.7%, above the prior-year quarter.

However, full-year 2025 operating profit fell 31.1% to KRW 4.8 billion, and first-half 2026 profit declined on mass-production investment costs. The supply plan runs to 14 million units over 2026 through 2032, which gives the revenue base a long horizon.

Against that, mobile and home-appliance demand, customer concentration and quarterly profit volatility remain. The share price trades below book value per share and earnings multiples are highly sensitive to profit swings, so interpretation depends on how stable profit direction becomes.

The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, due by late March 2027, which will show full-year operating margin and the automotive revenue trend.
