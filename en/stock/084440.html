Ubion: AI Platform Pivot Meets Volatile Quarterly Earnings

Ubion returned to operating profit in the fiscal year ended September 2025 but posted an operating loss in Q2 2026, so whether its AI platform pivot translates into steady quarterly profit is the key question.

02

Business structure

Ubion, founded in 2000, develops and sells online education platforms and learning management systems (LMS). Its business is split between edtech and education services, and one data aggregator put the split at 61% edtech and 39% education services based on Q3 2025 revenue.

That figure is an external estimate rather than a company disclosure, and official segment revenue could not be confirmed in this review. Its flagship edtech product for universities is the learning experience platform Cosmos LXP.

In a May 2026 press release, the company said this product had captured university demand for converting legacy LMS systems into AI-native platforms. The education services side includes Wowpass, the Ubion remote lifelong education institute, and corporate training.

On the enterprise side, the company cites deployments for the sales organization of a global electronics company and a learning platform for a semiconductor division. Its company history shows selection as an operator of a digital remote-training archive project in April 2025.

A DScovery system maintenance and operation contract with Samsung Electronics, worth KRW 1.85 billion, was disclosed on October 6, 2026, running from September 29, 2026 to September 28, 2027, equal to 5.49% of revenue for the fiscal year ended September 2025.

Competition overlaps with existing education platform vendors in university LMS and corporate training, and it is also compared with generative-AI learning services. Compared with large same-sector companies such as NAVER and Samsung SDS, its revenue scale and business breadth are much smaller.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue was KRW 31.8 billion for the fiscal year ended September 2023, KRW 33.3 billion for the fiscal year ended September 2024 (up 4.6% year on year), and KRW 33.7 billion for the fiscal year ended September 2025 (up 1.2% year on year).

Revenue has risen for two consecutive years from the fiscal year ended September 2024 through the fiscal year ended September 2025. The fiscal year ended September 2025 is the largest of the three years shown, and the fiscal year ended September 2023 is the smallest.

Revenue growth slowed to 1.2% in the fiscal year ended September 2025.

Operating profit moved from KRW 385 million in the fiscal year ended September 2023 to an operating loss of KRW 2.02 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2024, a swing into deficit, and then turned to an operating profit of KRW 1.41 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2025.

The operating margin moved from 1.2% to -6.1% and then to 4.2%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 601 million in the fiscal year ended September 2023, a wider loss of KRW 2.8 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2024, and a profit of KRW 1.31 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2025, a turn to profit.

Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 1.31 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2025.

The debt ratio, measured as total liabilities over total equity, was 148.3% in the fiscal year ended September 2023, 263.6% in the fiscal year ended September 2024, and 194.6% in the fiscal year ended September 2025.

At the end of September 2025, total equity was KRW 7.7 billion, of which KRW 7.6 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 15 billion.

Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 477 million in the fiscal year ended September 2023 and negative KRW 1.61 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2024 to KRW 4.74 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q2 2025 revenue was KRW 9.28 billion with an operating loss of KRW 523 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 394 million. Q3 2025 revenue fell to KRW 4.43 billion, down 52.2% from the prior quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 63.38 million.

Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 8.14 billion, up 83.5% from the prior quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 123 million. Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 12.7 billion, up 56.5% from the prior quarter, with operating profit of KRW 1.12 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 799 million.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 8.26 billion, down 35.1% from the prior quarter and down 11.0% from a year earlier, with an operating loss of KRW 1.51 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.25 billion.

Over the last four quarters, the sum of net losses attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 380 million. Quarterly revenue swings are large, so whether one-off items such as the timing of large project recognition are involved should be checked against footnotes in the quarterly reports and segment revenue.

A May 2026 company press release described first-half revenue of KRW 20.7 billion as the largest half-year result since its founding, but the release timing and period basis do not match the quarterly split in the disclosures, so this report does not use that figure.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic education platform demand is centered on two drivers: universities moving to next-generation LMS and corporate training adopting AI. The company says Cosmos LXP has captured demand from universities converting to AI-based learning platforms, but no independent market share data was found in this review.

On the enterprise side, it cites references from the sales organization of a global electronics company and a learning platform for a semiconductor division.

The spread of AI learning services is both an opportunity and a source of competition, since large IT companies and startups built around generative AI are chasing the same learning demand. Education policy shapes the direction of demand.

If digital education policy changes, purchase timing and budget allocation at schools and universities could shift, so policy schedules need to be monitored. Among listed companies in the same sector, NAVER, Samsung SDS, and Kakao are far larger in revenue and business scope, which limits direct comparison.

Even against the average of the KOSDAQ IT services sector, Ubion's revenue scale is on the small side. Its position against competitors depends on the strength of its university LMS focus and the pace at which enterprise references expand, and neither factor is yet fully visible in quarterly results.

Outlook

In a May 2026 announcement, the company said it would focus in the second half on expanding Cosmos LXP's share and winning large projects. Plans to file patents on core AI technology and to expand its role as an AI technology partner in the enterprise market were also presented.

The confirmed contract so far is the DScovery system maintenance and operation agreement with Samsung Electronics, worth KRW 1.85 billion, running from September 29, 2026 to September 28, 2027, and disclosed on October 6, 2026.

The contract is a single deal with modest revenue scale, but it matters as an enterprise reference. Because maintenance revenue is recognized over the contract period, how the quarterly contribution is booked should be checked in the quarterly reports.

The consensus estimate for the fiscal year ending September 2026 was not confirmed, as the forecast fields on the fnguide page were blank. No numeric annual guidance from the company was found in this review.

Whether the annual report separately discloses AI platform segment revenue is the first point for confirming the business transition in numbers.

The next checkpoint is whether October to December 2026 results, reported by the mid-February 2027 quarterly filing deadline, carry forward the recovery that followed the Q2 2026 loss.

Operating cash flow also moved from negative KRW 1.61 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2024 to KRW 4.74 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2025, so the earnings recovery showed up in cash generation. The debt ratio also fell from 263.6% to 194.6%.

AI platform pivot and university market position

In a May 2026 press release, the company said Cosmos LXP captured university demand for converting legacy LMS systems into AI-based platforms. It also said revenue rose across both university and enterprise businesses. These are company statements, and independent market share data has not been confirmed.

Large enterprise contract

The DScovery system maintenance and operation contract with Samsung Electronics, disclosed on October 6, 2026, is worth KRW 1.85 billion, equal to 5.49% of revenue for the fiscal year ended September 2025. It serves as a reference for an electronics and semiconductor learning platform. The contract runs through September 28, 2027.

The sum of KRW 380 million in net losses over the last four quarters indicates that the quarterly earnings base is not yet stable.

Financial burden and R&D spending

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly revenue moved sharply, from KRW 4.43 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 12.7 billion in Q1 2026. Quarterly profit can swing depending on when project revenue is recognized and on the seasonal structure of education demand. The weight of one-off items must be checked in the footnotes of quarterly reports.

Policy and demand

Education policy and university and corporate training budgets shape demand. A change in digital education policy could delay purchase timing. Whether the AI platform pivot can offset such policy effects has not yet been confirmed by disclosures.

Balance sheet and funding

Total liabilities of KRW 15 billion exceed total equity of KRW 7.7 billion, leaving a meaningful debt burden. Operating cash flow was positive in the fiscal year ended September 2025 but negative in the fiscal years ended September 2023 and 2024. Repayment schedules should be checked in the borrowings schedule of the annual report.

Overall view

In the fiscal year ended September 2025, Ubion moved from loss to profit, with revenue of KRW 33.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.41 billion, and operating cash flow turned to KRW 4.74 billion.

The debt ratio fell from 263.6% in the fiscal year ended September 2024 to 194.6% in the fiscal year ended September 2025.

However, after a Q1 2026 profit, the company returned to an operating loss of KRW 1.51 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.25 billion in Q2 2026, and the sum of net losses over the last four quarters is KRW 380 million.

The company emphasizes its AI platform pivot centered on Cosmos LXP and a wider set of large enterprise contracts, including one with Samsung Electronics, and whether this shift shows up in quarterly profit is the central question.

Bull-side factors are the return to profit and the university and enterprise references, while bear-side factors are quarterly earnings volatility and financial burden.

On valuation, the price-to-earnings ratio cannot be calculated because of recent net losses, and the price-to-book multiple sits in a premium range relative to net assets.

The next checkpoints are the annual report for the fiscal year ending September 2026, due in late December 2026, and the first-quarter report for the fiscal year ending September 2027, due February 14, 2027.
