04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, falling from KRW 152.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 117.9 billion in 2025. Operating profit fell from KRW 33.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.3 billion in 2025, and 2025 was the smallest year in the 2022 to 2025 range.

The operating margin declined from 21.7% in 2022 to 10.4% in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 6.45 billion in 2024 before returning to a profit of KRW 6.74 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Advertisers in the domestic digital ad market are allocating budgets selectively, and DB Securities cited a weak ad market in its June 2026 report. Demand for OTT advertising remains strong, with OTT ad volume up more than 40% year on year in the third quarter of 2026 on a preliminary basis.

Cuts by appliance, shopping and commerce advertisers weighed on both segments. Growth in subway and digital out-of-home media supported the platform segment, and the company said related revenue rose more than 90% year on year in the first quarter of 2026.

Outlook

The company plans to expand its business in the fourth quarter of 2026 with The Square Gangnam and the NAP DSP Plus integrated video ad platform. DB Securities projected fourth-quarter operating profit of KRW 8.4 billion in its August 21, 2026 report, citing the peak ad season and higher commerce CPS volume.

NH Investment & Securities projected second-half operating profit of KRW 13.4 billion in its September 7, 2026 report. Those forecasts predate the third-quarter preliminary release, so the gap with the actual figures can be checked in the periodic report.

Bull factors

Preliminary third-quarter operating profit was KRW 5.662 billion, higher than a year earlier. The company attributed the gains to lower bad-debt expenses and platform cost efficiency.

OTT and out-of-home media growth

Platform advertising revenue grew 30% year on year in the first quarter of 2026, led by subway advertising and digital out-of-home sales. The October 2026 launch of The Square Gangnam widened the outdoor media portfolio.

Analyst target prices raised

DB Securities said in an August 21, 2026 report that it kept its Buy rating and raised its target price to KRW 16,500. NH Investment & Securities said in a September 7, 2026 report that it kept its Buy rating and raised its target price from KRW 14,000 to KRW 16,000. Both firms cited raised earnings estimates and shareholder returns.

Risk factors

Sale and ownership change

Media reports say KT sent requests for proposals for a possible sale of kt nasmedia. The company declined to confirm the matter. A completed sale could change the business structure and shareholder base, and KT held about 43% of the shares as reported in September 2026.

New media execution

The company has set the operation of The Square Gangnam and the expansion of NAP DSP Plus as its business expansion plans for the fourth quarter of 2026. These two businesses must convert into transaction volume for fourth-quarter operating profit to approach the KRW 8.4 billion projected by DB Securities.

If sales of the new outdoor media to advertisers or the pace of DSP expansion fall short of plans, the gap between analyst forecasts and actual results could widen. That gap can be checked in the fourth-quarter preliminary results and the annual business report.

Cost and bad-debt burden

A company official said bad-debt expenses tied to advertising agencies weighed on 2025 results. Costs from expanding outdoor media and higher labor costs add pressure. In the third quarter of 2026, preparation costs for The Square Gangnam were recognized in advance.

Overall view

kt nasmedia's revenue and operating profit declined from 2022 through 2025, with full-year 2025 operating profit falling to KRW 12.3 billion, while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss in 2024 to a profit in 2025.

In 2026, operating profit of KRW 3.32 billion in the first quarter and KRW 7.82 billion in the second quarter was followed by preliminary third-quarter operating profit of KRW 5.662 billion, all pointing to a profit recovery.

On the other side, revenue fell 5.4% in the preliminary third quarter, and cuts by appliance, shopping and commerce advertisers constrain the rebound. OTT and subway and outdoor media are growth drivers, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 118.3% in 2025.

The next checkpoints are the confirmed third-quarter figures, fourth-quarter results in the peak season, and any disclosure on the reported sale.
