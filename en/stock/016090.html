02

Business structure

Daehyun manufactures and sells women's apparel under brands including CC Collect, Zooc, Mojo S Phine and Dewl. As of the end of 2025, Zooc accounted for 26.58% of sales, Mojo S Phine for 26.41%, Dewl for 22.65% and CC Collect for 22.01%, while other items made up 2.36%.

In 2022 Mojo S Phine was the largest at 27.14% and CC Collect stood at 17.60%, but CC Collect's share rose every year through 2025, narrowing the gap among the four core brands.

According to FnGuide's company profile, products reach consumers through 371 distribution points nationwide, its own online malls and open markets. Exports were 0.37% of 2025 sales, so nearly all revenue comes from Korea.

In 2025 the company launched LUVM in January, renewed the MOJO brand in February, and in August expanded its overseas online select shop ARCHIVE 2253 into the online and offline select shop BOUNDARY.

FnGuide's summary points to large fashion groups expanding imports of overseas luxury brands and online designer brands moving into physical stores as eroding the domestic market.

In response, the company is using major department store and outlet channels to raise profitability while widening reach through a select shop that curates global and rising domestic brands. Shin Yun-hwang was appointed chief executive in April 2024. Listed companies in the same sector include Handsome, LF, F&F and Shinsegae International.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales moved from KRW 319.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 295.3 billion in 2023 (down 7.5% year on year), KRW 259.3 billion in 2024 (down 12.2%) and KRW 240.5 billion in 2025 (down 7.2%), a three-year consecutive decline from 2023 through 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 30.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 21.3 billion in 2023 (down 29.4%), KRW 14.4 billion in 2024 (down 32.6%) and KRW 7.08 billion in 2025 (down 50.8%), also declining three years in a row.

The operating margin fell from 9.5% in 2022 to 7.2%, 5.5% and 2.9% in 2025, so profit shrank far faster than sales. Profit attributable to owners of the parent went from KRW 25.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 11 billion in 2025, with the 2025 figure down 30.7% year on year.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts as profit attributable to owners across 2022 to 2025. According to FnGuide, the cost of sales ratio rose from 41.60% in 2022 to 45.89% in 2025, which suggests cost pressure grew as revenue contracted.

Operating cash flow was KRW 21.8 billion in 2022, KRW 35.9 billion in 2023, KRW 15.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 26.1 billion in 2025, and it did not move in step with profit, rising in 2023 even as earnings fell.

On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 showed sales of KRW 46.8 billion, an operating loss of KRW 1.99 billion and a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 455 million.

The fourth quarter of 2025 then posted sales of KRW 77.7 billion (up 66.0% from the prior quarter), operating profit of KRW 6.94 billion and profit attributable to owners of KRW 7.07 billion.

Sales then declined in each of the first three quarters of 2026, to KRW 65.8 billion, KRW 54.1 billion and KRW 47.3 billion, and operating profit fell to KRW 3.57 billion, KRW 1.83 billion and KRW 84.06 million.

Even so, third-quarter 2026 sales were up 1.1% year on year, operating profit moved back into the black from a loss a year earlier, and profit attributable to owners of KRW 1.39 billion turned positive year on year.

Over the last four quarters, profit attributable to owners totaled KRW 14.9 billion, and the fourth quarter of 2025 alone accounted for close to half of that, so earnings were concentrated in one quarter.

05

Industry analysis

Competition in the domestic women's apparel market has intensified as large fashion groups expand imports of overseas luxury brands and online designer brands move into physical stores.

Against that backdrop, Daehyun's sales fell from KRW 319.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 240.5 billion in 2025, and its operating margin declined from 9.5% to 2.9%. The cost of sales ratio rose from 41.60% in 2022 to 45.89% in 2025, and with revenue shrinking, fixed-cost pressure may have grown relative to sales.

The company is responding by widening its offline reach through the BOUNDARY select shop and by carrying both overseas and rising domestic brands. In cycle terms, the sales decline that has run since 2022 looks more like an adjustment phase than a recovery.

Third-quarter 2026 sales up 1.1% year on year is a point to watch for whether the pace of decline is slowing. Whether the post-peak drop after the fourth quarter of 2025 reflects seasonal inventory or channel adjustment is a question the segment discussion in the annual report should answer.

Among peers, Daehyun is a women's apparel-focused company with four core brands of fairly even weight, and with exports at 0.37% its growth from overseas markets is limited. Handsome, LF, F&F and Shinsegae International differ from it in business structure and distribution approach.

Bull factors

Balance sheet headroom

Total liabilities were KRW 37.5 billion at the end of 2025, with a debt ratio of 13.5% against total equity of KRW 277.4 billion. The ratio has fallen from 22.7% in 2022, which points to a light borrowing burden.

Operating cash flow of KRW 26.1 billion in 2025 shows cash generation continued even in a year when earnings fell.

Shift from loss back to profit

Operating profit moved from a loss of KRW 1.99 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to a profit of KRW 6.94 billion in the fourth quarter. Operating profit remained positive at KRW 84.06 million in the third quarter of 2026. Third-quarter 2026 sales were up 1.1% year on year, so the top line held at the prior-year level.

Diversified brand portfolio

As of the end of 2025, Zooc, Mojo S Phine, Dewl and CC Collect each held a share above 22%, which keeps the portfolio spread evenly. A slump in any single brand therefore has a comparatively limited effect on total sales. The LUVM launch and the BOUNDARY expansion in 2025 show the company testing new brands and channels.

Bear factors

Three years of shrinking sales and profit

Sales declined from KRW 319.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 240.5 billion in 2025, and operating profit fell from KRW 30.2 billion to KRW 7.08 billion. The operating margin dropped from 9.5% to 2.9%. Profit attributable to owners also fell from KRW 25.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 11 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales and operating profit falling together

Following the KRW 77.7 billion peak in the fourth quarter of 2025, sales fell for three consecutive quarters to KRW 47.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026. Operating profit of KRW 84.06 million was down 95.4% from the prior quarter.

Close to half of the KRW 14.9 billion in profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters came in the fourth quarter of 2025, so earnings look less durable.

Rising cost ratio and domestic competition

The cost of sales ratio rose from 41.60% in 2022 to 45.89% in 2025. FnGuide's summary points to imports of overseas luxury brands by large fashion groups and online designer brands moving offline as eroding the domestic market. With exports at 0.37%, the company has limited means to offset domestic weakness overseas.

Risk factors

Demand and competition risk

If domestic demand for women's apparel shifts toward overseas luxury and online designer brands, sales of existing brands could fall further. A slowdown in any of the four core brands could widen the total sales decline.

Whether the select shop expansion can grow enough to replace department store and outlet sales is not yet visible in the numbers.

Profitability risk

If sales keep shrinking while the cost of sales ratio rises, the operating margin could fall further. The operating margin was 2.9% in 2025, and third-quarter 2026 operating profit was only KRW 84.06 million. With profit this thin, another drop in sales could make it harder to stay in the black.

Small-cap volatility risk

As a small-cap stock, its trading volume and price volatility can run higher than large-cap peers. Prices can move sharply around earnings releases. Metrics such as dividend yield and multiples also shift with the share price, so a single indicator is hard to rely on.

Overall view

Daehyun's sales have fallen for three consecutive years since 2022, and its operating margin declined to 2.9% in 2025. Operating profit has stayed positive from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, but sales have declined for three consecutive quarters over the same span.

The balance sheet is relatively light, with a debt ratio of 13.5% and total equity of KRW 277.4 billion at the end of 2025. The even spread across four core brands also limits the impact of weakness in any one of them.

On the other side, the cost of sales ratio rose from 41.60% in 2022 to 45.89% in 2025, and domestic competition has intensified. Profit attributable to owners of KRW 14.9 billion over the last four quarters was concentrated in one quarter, so earnings durability is limited.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report, with the key questions being whether the pace of sales decline slows and whether operating profit stays positive.
