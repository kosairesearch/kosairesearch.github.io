02

Business structure

S&W started in 1967 with industrial bolts and expanded into precision machining for engine parts and forged products. The company now reports three business segments: marine engine parts, industrial parts, and forged products.

According to a figure posted on a securities information site, product sales mix is 64.42% marine (diesel) engine parts, 18.89% industrial parts, 5.38% forged products, and 11.31% other. Because marine engine parts account for most sales, vessel orders and engine makers' production plans drive results.

Key marine engine products include cams and camshafts, engine bolts, and valve seat rings. Industrial parts are specialty bolts and nuts used in plants and power facilities, and forged products link to construction demand.

Based on 2024 data, the domestic share was 99.23% for engine bolts, 75.39% for cams and camshafts, 77.47% for industrial parts, and 100.00% for forged products, so domestic sales are the core.

A 2010 securities research report described the company as supplying domestic engine makers, large shipbuilders, and plant contractors. By industry classification, the company sits closer to machinery and marine equipment components than to automobiles.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on full-year figures in the DART periodic report, sales rose from KRW 36 billion in 2022 to KRW 43.6 billion in 2023 (up 21.2% year on year), KRW 47.1 billion in 2024 (up 8.0%), and KRW 48.8 billion in 2025 (up 3.6%), marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 to 2025.

Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest sales year and 2022 the smallest. Operating profit moved from KRW 2.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.23 billion in 2023 and KRW 3.46 billion in 2024, then fell to KRW 901 million in 2025, down 73.9% year on year.

Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest operating profit year and 2025 the smallest. The operating margin was 6.9% in 2022, 7.4% in 2023, and 7.3% in 2024, before dropping to 1.8% in 2025.

Sales rose slightly in 2025, but the company attributed the operating profit decline to higher raw material costs, manufacturing expenses, and wage-related costs.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.33 billion in 2022, KRW 3.47 billion in 2023 (up 49.1%), and KRW 4.76 billion in 2024 (up 37.4%), then KRW 3.63 billion in 2025, down 23.8%. Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest for this measure.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts in each year. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 45.9% in 2022, 47.0% in 2023, and 43.1% in 2024 to 59.5% in 2025.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 53.5 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 31.8 billion. Operating cash flow went from KRW 3.69 billion in 2022, KRW 4.21 billion in 2023, and KRW 4.46 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.8 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, sales were KRW 11.6 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 12.6 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 11.5 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 12.8 billion in Q2 2026, and KRW 12.8 billion in Q3 2026.

Operating profit of KRW 370 million in Q3 2025 turned into an operating loss of KRW 102 million in Q4 2025, then returned to a profit of KRW 309 million in Q1 2026, rose to KRW 415 million in Q2 2026, and fell to KRW 207 million in Q3 2026.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 919 million in Q3 2025, a net loss of KRW 216 million in Q4 2025, KRW 266 million in Q1 2026, KRW 635 million in Q2 2026, the highest of the last four quarters, and KRW 411 million in Q3 2026. The total for the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) was KRW 1.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand in the company's main markets depends heavily on shipbuilding orders.

A securities information site summarized that in the first half of 2026, operating profit in the marine engine parts and industrial parts segments improved, driven by higher orders for high-value vessel types such as LNG carriers and very large container ships, along with expanded petrochemical plant investment in the Middle East.

The same source said that replacement demand for aging vessels under environmental regulations and new demand are keeping shipbuilding order volumes on an upward trend. By contrast, the forged products segment's total sales declined slightly due to weak construction activity.

Shipbuilding is an order-driven industry, so order backlog and newbuilding price indices are the gauges of the upstream flow. How long the ordering cycle lasts depends on the trend in order backlog. Public figures that support this company's market share or a comparison of its results with peers are limited.

Unlike listed automobile parts makers, the company's main demand is tied to vessel and plant orders, so shipbuilding and plant ordering trends matter more than automobile market supply and demand.

Its supply history to domestic engine makers dating back to around 2010 is a stabilizing factor for demand, but because sales are concentrated in engine parts, it is sensitive to order changes from specific customers.

06

Outlook

The company has not published quantitative guidance in the materials currently available. Instead, the confirmed facts fall into three areas: the order environment, governance, and listing requirements.

A reported increase in 2026 first-half orders for high-value vessel types such as LNG carriers is a favorable factor for operations. Because Q3 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 207 million, whether orders translate into sales and margins is the next checkpoint.

On governance, Jung Jae-woo was elected as an inside director at the extraordinary shareholders' meeting on October 30, 2026, and the board appointed him as a co-CEO the same day. The stated reason was improving management efficiency, and Jung Woo-jin remains CEO, so the company now has a two-person CEO structure.

On listing requirements, the stock was designated as an administrative issue on August 13, 2026 due to a market cap shortfall. The market cap threshold is KRW 200 billion through December 31, 2026, rising to KRW 300 billion from January 1, 2027.

According to a Korea Exchange notice, the designation can be lifted if the threshold is met for 45 trading days within 90 trading days after designation.

On October 2, 2026, the Seoul Southern District Court granted an injunction against the delisting decision, and the exchange has since postponed the delisting procedures that followed the designation. The administrative designation itself has not been lifted.

The KRW 635 million in Q2 2026 is the highest of the last four quarters.

Whether the operating margin can return to the 6.9% to 7.4% range seen from 2022 to 2024 is the room for improvement.

Stable Sales Base

Sales were KRW 12.8 billion in both Q2 and Q3 2026, and Q3 2026 sales rose 9.8% year on year. Sales in 2025 were KRW 48.8 billion, the largest of the 2022 to 2025 period. A stable sales base is the starting point for any profit recovery.

Administrative Designation and Listing Requirements

Overall view

S&W is a small marine engine parts maker whose sales grew for three consecutive years from KRW 36 billion in 2022 to KRW 48.8 billion in 2025.

However, operating profit fell to KRW 901 million in 2025, cutting the operating margin to 1.8%, and the company cited higher raw material, manufacturing, and wage-related costs. The 2026 quarterly trend is mixed.

Sales held at KRW 12.8 billion in both Q2 and Q3 2026, but operating profit declined from KRW 415 million in Q2 to KRW 207 million in Q3. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 635 million in Q2 2026, the highest of the last four quarters, and KRW 411 million in Q3.

Higher orders for high-value vessel types are a favorable industry factor, while the administrative designation for market cap shortfall and the threshold rising from January 1, 2027 are institutional variables for continued listing.

The two-person CEO structure, the concentrated shareholder base, and the one-day investment caution designation are also part of the record.

The next checkpoints are the market cap threshold change on December 31, 2026, preliminary full-year results in early 2027, and the 2026 annual business report at the end of March 2027.
