Korea Steel: Operating Turn Positive, Net Losses Persist

02

Business structure

Korea Steel is a manufacturer of section steel and semi-finished steel founded in 1971 and listed on the KOSPI market since November 1989. Its main products include billets, equal angles, round bars, flat bars, grating and rebar, which serve as base materials for construction, shipbuilding and automotive industries.

Following the start of a new rolling mill in the third quarter of 2022, the company entered new markets and is selectively expanding into higher-value steel grades and eco-friendly steel. As of year-end 2025, domestic sales accounted for 95.9% of revenue and exports for 4.1%.

By product, rebar was sold entirely domestically (100%), while section steel was 89% domestic and 11% export. Because earnings are tied to domestic construction and manufacturing demand, expanding export channels is the central task for changing the business structure.

In its value-up plan disclosed on April 1, 2026, the company set out a policy of raising cost competitiveness and quality and of using its overseas sales network to find new key customers.

In the competitive landscape, a 2025 media report noted that Korea Steel was the only domestic rebar maker whose sales rose in the first half of 2025, while most peers saw earnings decline.

Over the same period, receivables growth ran at roughly twice the pace of sales growth, which drew debate over the quality of sales driven by credit terms. Based on the September 2025 quarter, the company's operating margin was reported as low relative to peers.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed basis, sales rose from KRW 795.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 830.4 billion in 2023 (up 4.3% year on year), the peak year, and then fell to KRW 768.8 billion in 2024 (down 7.4%) and KRW 767.1 billion in 2025 (down 0.2%), a two-year decline.

Operating profit was largest in 2023 at KRW 47.3 billion (up 54.4%) and smallest in 2024 at KRW 10.6 billion (down 77.6%). It recovered to KRW 11.7 billion in 2025 (up 10.6%).

The operating margin narrowed from 3.8% in 2022 and 5.7% in 2023 to 1.4% in 2024 and 1.5% in 2025, so margin compression amplified earnings swings more than sales movements did. According to a financial data aggregator, the cost of sales ratio rose from 90.93% in 2023 to 95.35% in 2024 and 95.24% in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 40.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 28.1 billion in 2023 (down 30.8%), turned into a net loss of KRW 2.92 billion in 2024, and returned to a profit of KRW 2.99 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was filed as KRW 40.6 billion in 2022, KRW 28.1 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 2.92 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.99 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity stood at KRW 214.1 billion and total liabilities at KRW 339.3 billion, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 158.5%.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 17.8 billion in 2022 and KRW 20.8 billion in 2023 to KRW 47.9 billion in 2024, then fell to KRW 9.18 billion in 2025, which one media report put at an 80.8% decline.

Quarterly results moved from operating profit of KRW 387 million in Q3 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 724 million in Q4, and the loss widened to KRW 3.61 billion in Q1 2026. Company commentary cited higher raw material procurement costs and Chinese oversupply as cost burdens.

Operating profit returned to KRW 473 million in Q2 2026 and rose to KRW 944 million in Q3, up 99.4% from the prior quarter. However, Q3 2026 sales of KRW 187.8 billion fell 26.4% from the prior quarter and were down 0.3% year on year.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters total KRW 3.28 billion, meaning operating improvement has not yet fully carried through to net income. Quarter-to-quarter swings are large, so the period is still too short to read as a trend.

Industry analysis

In steel and metals, the key variable for demand in 2026 is slowing global steel demand. The company's own earnings commentary pointed to higher raw material costs from a strong-dollar environment and Chinese oversupply as the main external uncertainties.

A media report said most domestic rebar producers saw earnings decline in the first half of 2025, while Korea Steel's sales rose over the same period. However, receivables growth outpaced sales growth in that period, which raised questions about the quality of sales driven by credit terms.

Operating cash flow also fell about 5% year on year over the same period, according to the same report. On competitive position, the operating margin for the September 2025 quarter was reported as lower than peers, so the company's sales defense has not translated into margin strength.

Quarterly results in 2026 showed a Q1 loss widening and a Q2 return to profit, which makes the period too short to judge the strength of any industry recovery. If Chinese oversupply persists, pressure on domestic rebar prices and profitability could continue, which is a structural weakness.

On the other hand, a domestic share of 95.9% at year-end 2025 limits exposure to price competition in export markets. Whether the industry has bottomed can be checked against Q4 2026 preliminary results and margin trends in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.

06

Outlook

In its value-up plan disclosed on April 1, 2026, the company said it would cut manufacturing costs through production process improvements and strategic raw material purchasing, and reduce defect rates through stricter quality control.

On overseas sales, it plans market-specific strategies using its existing overseas sales network and aims to find new key customers to raise the share of export revenue.

On shareholder returns, it is reviewing a procedure that sets the record date after the dividend amount is decided, and it stated that it will keep a sustainable dividend policy within distributable profit.

The plan is written in directional terms without quantitative targets, so progress will need to be checked against cost ratios and export mix in periodic reports. The company disclosed a 2025 payout ratio of 383% and said it falls under the high-dividend company designation of the Restriction of Special Taxation Act.

Following this, a media report in October 2026 said an interim dividend for 2026 is expected not to be paid. Total equity fell from KRW 222.0 billion at year-end 2024 to KRW 214.1 billion at year-end 2025, and a media report put it at KRW 205.7 billion at mid-2026.

The same report said retained earnings declined from KRW 107.1 billion to KRW 92.7 billion. A March 31, 2026 disclosure announced a dual representative director structure with Han Gil-gu and Park Sung-woo. The share consolidation took effect on April 17, 2026.

A September 2026 filing put the largest shareholder group's stake at 52.73%. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results and the cost ratio, export mix and dividend decisions in the 2026 annual report.

If the company's cost reduction and defect-rate targets translate into margins, there is room for the operating margin to improve further.

Share price relative to net assets

The price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x, so the share price is low relative to asset value. Total equity of KRW 214.1 billion at year-end 2025 means the net asset base remains large. This is cited as a possible floor reference if earnings recover.

Policy commitments on shareholder returns and overseas expansion

The value-up plan disclosed on April 1, 2026 covers profitability-focused management, overseas channel expansion and a review of the dividend procedure. The company said it will maintain a sustainable dividend policy within distributable profit, considering operating results, investment plans and future cash flows. If implemented, shareholder returns and customer diversification could advance together.

Thinner capital buffer and weaker cash generation

Risk factors

External supply, demand and currency

If Chinese oversupply persists, pressure could continue on domestic rebar and section steel prices and margins. A strong-dollar environment raises raw material costs and could push the operating margin lower again. With domestic sales at 95.9%, a slowdown in domestic construction would feed directly into revenue.

Receivables and cash collection

A 2025 media report said receivables grew at roughly twice the pace of sales, and the receivables turnover ratio fell from the prior-year period. Operating cash flow also declined over that period, suggesting that expanded credit terms may be slowing cash collection. This should be read together with the drop in 2025 operating cash flow to KRW 9.18 billion.

Governance and shareholder return policy

A March 31, 2026 disclosure changed the representative director structure to a dual-director arrangement. As of a September 2026 filing, the largest shareholder group held 52.73%, which limits the free float.

Combined with the 383% 2025 payout ratio and reports of no interim dividend for 2026, the dividend policy could change in the future.

Overall view

Korea Steel recovered operating profit to KRW 11.7 billion in 2025 from its 2024 low, and posted operating profit in Q2 and Q3 2026.

However, sales declined for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025, and the KRW 3.61 billion operating loss in Q1 2026 together with cumulative net losses of KRW 3.28 billion over the last four quarters means earnings durability is not yet proven.

On the financial side, a debt-to-equity ratio of 158.5% at year-end 2025, operating cash flow of KRW 9.18 billion in 2025 and a 383% payout ratio for 2025 put the balance between capital capacity and dividend policy at the center of the debate.

Strengths include the value-up plan covering cost reduction and overseas expansion, defended domestic rebar sales, and a share price low relative to net assets.

Weaknesses include cost pressure from Chinese oversupply and a strong currency, questions over credit-driven sales, and reports that no interim dividend is expected for 2026.

Because both sides coexist, the next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual report and the operating margin and net profit trend in the Q1 2027 quarterly report. This picture will be updated as confirmed figures are filed.
