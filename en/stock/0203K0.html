Report overview

Deep-Learning Traffic Analytics Enters KONEX

Songwoo Infotech, a deep-learning video analytics specialist for smart traffic management since 2010, made its debut on the KONEX market in July 2026, testing the viability of vertical expansion into smart-city and AI-based disaster monitoring segments.

02

Business structure

Songwoo Infotech was founded in August 2010 by CEO Seo Young-jin and is headquartered in Gajwa-dong, Jinju, Gyeongnam, where its R&D facilities are also located.

According to Boannews and Newspim, the business is structured around three segments: ① traffic control systems, ② video surveillance devices, and ③ license plate recognition (LPR), with proprietary deep-learning video analytics technology serving as the common backbone across all three.

In the traffic control segment, the company supplies its smart intersection solution incorporating the DeepSORT algorithm, integrating safety features such as automated pedestrian signal extension, pedestrian-responsive signals, right-turn alerts, and speed notifications.

The video surveillance segment offers an active surveillance system capable of generating metadata on vehicle types, pedestrians, and animals from CCTV and IoT sensors, enabling precise analysis of traffic flow, dwell time, and congestion zones.

The LPR segment delivers a vehicle number recognition system that accumulates and classifies 22 datasets—including manufacturer and model—to map movement routes and traffic conditions in designated areas.

The primary customer base is understood to be public-sector entities such as local governments and road management authorities, resulting in irregular revenue timing typical of project-based procurement businesses.

Key competitors include KOSDAQ-listed Rexgen (98%+ plate recognition accuracy, 120+ patents) and AI video analytics specialist Witkon; Songwoo Infotech differentiates through regional project execution capability and its in-house DeepSORT algorithm.

The company operates a proprietary road simulation facility within its R&D wing to simultaneously advance product development and accumulate real-world data.

CEO Seo has articulated a strategic vision of evolving into a smart-city solutions provider, with data and platform competitiveness as the stated competitive moat.

03

Recent trends

The most recent confirmed financials are FY2025 consolidated figures disclosed at the time of the KONEX listing application, as reported by Newspim: revenue of KRW 9.787 billion, equity of KRW 14.049 billion, and net income of KRW 2.145 billion.

This implies a net margin of approximately 22%, which is elevated relative to hardware-heavy peers and consistent with a software-centric revenue mix; however, as the original DART filing has not been independently verified by this report, these figures should be treated with appropriate caution.

On the product and technology front, the company publicly demonstrated its DeepSORT-based traffic control system at SECON 2025 (Ilsan KINTEX, March 2025), and followed up with a smart intersection showcase at SECON 2026 (February 2026), raising its industry profile.

In 2025, Songwoo Infotech was selected through a Ministry of Science and ICT open competition to deploy an AI wildfire monitoring system in the Sancheong and Hadong areas of South Gyeongnam, marking its first revenue-generating expansion beyond traffic management into AI-based disaster prevention.

The company submitted its KONEX listing application with IBK Investment & Securities as designated advisor on June 29, 2026, completing the approximately two-week listing review process to begin trading on KONEX in July 2026.

As of July 22, 2026, the share price stands at KRW 4,700 with no intraday change, and daily trading volume is zero, meaning the stock's market price discovery function is non-operational.

Market capitalization rounds to 0.0 trillion won, placing the company firmly in the nano-cap tier even within the KONEX universe, with no meaningful institutional or foreign investor participation observed.

The equity base of approximately KRW 14 billion suggests a sound capital foundation, but consistent quarterly disclosures will be necessary to verify revenue and earnings stability over time.

04

Outlook

Songwoo Infotech has articulated a medium-to-long-term vision of leveraging its AI video analytics technology and data assets—accumulated in the traffic safety domain—to expand into broader smart-city solutions, with a stated plan to invest in higher-performance NPU hardware to improve inference speed and processing precision.

From a market structure perspective, the global intelligent traffic camera market is projected to grow from USD 16.06 billion in 2025 to USD 32.03 billion by 2034 at a 9.01% CAGR (Industry Today, May 2026), while South Korea's smart transportation market is expected to expand at approximately 8.9% CAGR over 2025–2033 (IMARC Group), providing structural support for the company's addressable market.

Domestically, public agencies including the Korea Expressway Corporation are accelerating deployment of AI-based next-generation ITS (C-ITS), suggesting a potentially expanding pipeline of public-sector contracts.

The concurrent trends of Edge AI adoption and cloud-based surveillance service proliferation represent both new business opportunities and technology transition investment burdens, creating a dual-faceted outlook for the company's product roadmap.

The company's track record from the Ministry of Science and ICT AI wildfire monitoring project opens pathways into disaster prevention and environmental monitoring verticals, although the timing and scale of such contract wins remain uncertain given the lumpy nature of public procurement.

Should the current state of near-zero KONEX trading volume persist, securing a path to higher-tier listing—such as a transfer to KOSDAQ—may emerge as a key medium-term agenda for capital-raising capacity.

05

Bull factors

Deep-learning LPR proprietary IP as a meaningful moat

Songwoo Infotech holds a proprietary algorithm (DeepSORT-based) that accumulates, classifies, and analyzes 22 vehicle datasets—including manufacturer and model—in its LPR segment.

AI training datasets of this nature are difficult to replicate in the short term and exhibit a self-reinforcing dynamic, as performance improves continuously with real-world deployment.

Consecutive appearances at SECON 2025 and SECON 2026 have provided a public technical validation process while broadening the company's prospective customer touchpoints. The in-house road simulation facility also provides infrastructure capable of shortening new-product development cycles and improving dataset quality.

Vertical expansion potential into AI disaster prevention and smart-city verticals

Having been selected for and executed a Ministry of Science and ICT AI wildfire monitoring project, Songwoo Infotech has demonstrated that its business portfolio can expand beyond traffic control into disaster prevention and environmental monitoring.

The company's core competencies in real-time video analytics, object detection, and big data platform technology are transferable to adjacent verticals including smart factories, smart buildings, and forest monitoring.

This constitutes a tangible diversification pathway away from the current concentration in public traffic procurement contracts. Continued government infrastructure spending on smart-city initiatives could widen the scope of addressable projects over the medium term.

Profitable operations backed by a solid equity base

According to Newspim's report on the KONEX listing application, FY2025 consolidated net income of KRW 2.145 billion against equity of KRW 14.049 billion reflects a relatively high profitability profile consistent with a software-driven business model.

The equity base of KRW 14 billion provides a foundation that reduces the near-term dependence on external debt or capital raises for a company of this scale. The KONEX listing has opened a capital-market access pathway, creating more favorable conditions for funding R&D and planned NPU hardware investment.

A continuation of the current profitable run rate would also improve the financial eligibility profile for a potential higher-tier listing.

Bear factors

KONEX-specific extreme illiquidity risk

A zero-won daily trading volume on the reference date (July 22, 2026) effectively signals the absence of active market participants, meaning the stock price may persistently fail to reflect fair value.

The KONEX market's structural constraints—including institutional-only trading designation and a minimum KRW 30 million deposit requirement—severely limit retail investor participation.

A prolonged liquidity vacuum blocks public price discovery and dilutes the effectiveness of stock option programs as a tool for talent attraction and retention. The risk of a deepening small-cap discount is a persistent concern and could raise dilution costs in any future capital raise.

Revenue instability from heavy reliance on public-sector procurement

The primary customer base is presumed to consist of local governments and public road management authorities, meaning revenue is subject to seasonal concentration and quarter-to-quarter lumpiness inherent in project-based procurement.

Revenue can shift sharply depending on government budget execution cycles and changes in project priorities at the procuring entity, making annual earnings visibility difficult to sustain.

The domestic ITS market is subject to competitive bidding involving established players such as Rexgen and Witkon, as well as large-cap SI affiliates, exposing Songwoo Infotech to procurement pricing and margin pressure given its smaller scale.

If reported profitability is concentrated in a small number of contracts, the possibility of one-time gains embedded in the earnings figure cannot be fully ruled out without additional disclosure.

Scale disadvantage and R&D resource constraints

Competitor Rexgen holds 120+ patents and has demonstrated export capabilities in Central Asia and Southeast Asia, while Songwoo Infotech has grown primarily through regional domestic projects with no confirmed international market track record.

Revenue of KRW 9.787 billion is small in absolute terms relative to ITS peers, potentially limiting the company's capacity to independently meet the resource and certification requirements for large-scale national infrastructure projects.

The AI video analytics field evolves rapidly and faces potential indirect entry by major cloud and AI platform providers, making sustained R&D investment essential—yet the absolute R&D budget of a company at this revenue scale is inherently constrained.

The planned expansion of hardware investment, including NPU adoption, may also exert temporary pressure on profitability margins.

07

Risk factors

Macro / Policy Risk

Domestic ITS and smart transportation investment is heavily dependent on budget allocations by central and local government authorities, with procurement volumes at risk of contraction during fiscal tightening cycles.

While the current policy environment of digital transformation and AI investment is broadly supportive, the risk of policy discontinuity arising from administration changes or shifts in budget priorities remains.

The ongoing legislative movement toward an AI Basic Law—with heightened requirements around AI safety, transparency, and personal data protection—could impose additional compliance costs and extend product certification timelines.

Competitive / Technology Risk

Deep-learning AI video analytics is a domain in which technological differentiation can erode rapidly as open-source model proliferation and cloud AI platform advances lower barriers to entry.

Indirect entry by large domestic or international IT and security conglomerates into the traffic control software market could sharpen competitive intensity, and competition from large-cap SI affiliates offering integrated smart-city platforms may translate into procurement pricing pressure.

Tightening TTA certification and GS certification requirements for LPR and AI traffic management products could also generate incremental compliance and re-certification costs.

Liquidity / Financial Risk

The structural liquidity constraints of the KONEX market make it difficult for investors to exit positions at desired prices and timing, raising the effective opportunity cost of capital commitment.

In a project-based business model, delays or cancellations in a specific quarter's contract wins can cause a sharp deterioration in cash flow, which may in turn delay planned capital expenditures such as the NPU hardware investment.

Furthermore, as FY2025 financial figures have been sourced from media reporting on the listing application rather than from independently verified DART filings, meaningful uncertainty around the company's precise financial condition persists until regular periodic disclosures—such as quarterly reports—provide formal confirmation.

08

Overall view

Songwoo Infotech is a small specialist firm that has accumulated proprietary deep-learning algorithms and real-world datasets in the LPR and smart traffic control domains since its founding in 2010, making its formal capital-market debut with a KONEX listing in July 2026.

The profitable FY2025 profile (consolidated revenue KRW 9.787 billion, net income KRW 2.145 billion, equity KRW 14.049 billion, per Newspim) represents a solid financial foundation for a company of this scale, though revenue variability stemming from public-sector procurement concentration and an absolute size disadvantage relative to larger peers constrain the pace of business expansion.

The global ITS market's projected 10%+ annual growth, deepening domestic C-ITS investment, and the company's maiden foray into AI-driven disaster prevention collectively suggest potential diversification pathways, but the dual uncertainties of public budget cycles and intensifying AI competition remain material.

The near-zero trading volume environment on the reference date effectively suspends reliable price discovery, and granular financial verification awaits the accumulation of periodic DART disclosures.

In summary, Songwoo Infotech presents a profile in which niche proprietary AI traffic management expertise coexists with structural constraints of small scale, illiquidity, and public-sector revenue dependency—a combination that warrants balanced consideration of both dimensions.
