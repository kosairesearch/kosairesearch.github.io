Subsidiary Earnings Recovery Flows Into SK's Holding Results

02

Business structure

SK is a holding company that owns stakes in subsidiaries and earns income from dividends, brand royalties and IT service revenue.

Its major subsidiaries include SK hynix, SK Square, SK Innovation, SK Telecom, SK ecoplant and SK Biopharm, and the unlisted subsidiaries SK Siltron and SK Failure Analysis are included in Hanwha Investment's valuation.

According to Hanwha Investment, stand-alone revenue in 3Q26 was KRW 1.1 trillion and stand-alone operating profit was KRW 228.5 billion. Brand royalty revenue rose 18% year on year on subsidiary growth, and IT service revenue rose 21% on wider demand for AX and DT.

Stand-alone figures must be read separately from consolidated results, where 3Q26 revenue was KRW 41.3 trillion and operating profit was KRW 5.02 trillion.

SK ecoplant posted 3Q26 revenue of KRW 5.9 trillion and operating profit of KRW 795 billion, and its Asset Lifecycle segment, which Hanwha Investment attributes to higher D-RAM and NAND module prices, reported revenue of KRW 2.3 trillion and operating profit of KRW 539 billion.

Its Hi-tech segment reported revenue of KRW 2.1 trillion on progress at the Yongin semiconductor cluster project. In the holding structure, subsidiary cash flows reach the parent through dividends and royalties, and the pace depends on each subsidiary's dividend policy.

Fellow listed holding company SK Square reported 3Q26 consolidated operating profit of KRW 7.9 trillion, also driven by SK hynix.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART filing basis, revenue fell from KRW 134.6 trillion in 2022 to KRW 131.2 trillion in 2023 (down 2.5%), KRW 124.7 trillion in 2024 (down 5.0%) and KRW 122.7 trillion in 2025 (down 1.6%), a decline for three straight years. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for revenue.

Operating profit fell from KRW 8 trillion in 2022 to KRW 5.06 trillion in 2023 (down 36.8%), KRW 2.36 trillion in 2024 (down 53.4%) and KRW 1.82 trillion in 2025 (down 22.8%), and operating margin declined from 5.9% to 1.5%.

Parent-attributable net profit of KRW 3.97 trillion in 2022 became a net loss of KRW 776.8 billion in 2023, the loss widened to KRW 1.29 trillion in 2024, and the company returned to a KRW 1.6 trillion surplus in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 406.4 billion in 2023, a net profit of KRW 528.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.56 trillion in 2025, a gap that reflects the non-controlling share.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 85.7 trillion, of which parent shareholders' equity was KRW 25.2 trillion, and the debt ratio fell from 170.9% in 2022 to 149.2% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 7.68 trillion in 2022, KRW 11.4 trillion in 2023, KRW 8.11 trillion in 2024 and KRW 6.1 trillion in 2025, declining after 2023. Quarterly, 4Q25 was the low point.

Revenue rose from KRW 30.3 trillion in 4Q25 to KRW 36.8 trillion in 1Q26 and KRW 42.2 trillion in 2Q26, then edged down 2.1% to KRW 41.3 trillion in 3Q26, still up 33.2% year on year.

Operating profit rose from KRW 391.7 billion in 4Q25 to KRW 3.67 trillion in 1Q26, KRW 4.84 trillion in 2Q26 and KRW 5.02 trillion in 3Q26, three consecutive quarterly increases.

Parent-attributable net profit moved from a KRW 2.25 trillion net loss in 4Q25 to surpluses of KRW 3.38 trillion, KRW 5.96 trillion and KRW 5.63 trillion in 1Q26 to 3Q26.

The gap between 4Q25 operating profit and the parent-level net loss was large, so the contribution of non-operating items should be examined quarter by quarter. Parent-attributable net profit for the last four quarters totals KRW 12.7 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

The memory cycle links SK's equity value and cash flows directly to SK hynix's results. SK hynix reported 3Q26 revenue of KRW 104.6 trillion and operating profit of KRW 83.3 trillion, an 80% operating margin, and expects memory supply to stay short through 2030.

In the same release it said long-term supply agreements cover more than 50% of its 2027 and 2028 capacity.

By contrast, BNK Investment & Securities kept a Hold rating in a July 2026 report with a KRW 1.85 million target price for SK hynix, acknowledging that AI server DRAM and eSSD remain in short supply while pointing to the possibility that hyperscalers slow their infrastructure spending.

The same report said earnings momentum could weaken after year-end. The brokerage views on where the cycle stands therefore diverge. Fellow listed holding company SK Square reported 3Q26 consolidated operating profit of KRW 7.9 trillion and cited SK hynix's results as a driver.

Subsidiary SK Telecom reported 3Q26 operating profit of KRW 552.3 billion, above KRW 500 billion for a third consecutive quarter, according to its results coverage on November 4, 2026. SK's exposure to the memory cycle is the largest among its holdings, while telecom and other subsidiaries contribute a smaller share.

06

Outlook

In a November 17, 2026 report, Hanwha Investment raised its 2026 operating profit estimate for SK by 34.7% to KRW 16.7 trillion from KRW 12.4 trillion, and lifted its parent-attributable net profit estimate by 37.1% to KRW 16.9 trillion from KRW 12.3 trillion.

The same report cut the target price on lower listed-subsidiary values while raising the enterprise value of SK ecoplant to KRW 5.7 trillion.

Hanwha Investment said it expects a new shareholder return policy to be announced within the year, and it flagged how much subsidiary dividend income is linked to shareholder returns as the key question.

SK hynix plans shareholder returns of about KRW 100 trillion for its 2026 fiscal year, including at least KRW 30 trillion in cash dividends and buybacks and cancellations. Its board approved a KRW 14 trillion third-quarter cash dividend and a KRW 20 trillion share buyback in 3Q26.

SK hynix expects HBM4 to become its mainstream product from the fourth quarter and says its D-RAM and HBM volumes for the next three years are effectively sold out through 2029. For SK, the confirmation points are the size and timing of subsidiary dividends and the specifics of the shareholder return policy.

The 4Q26 results have not yet been filed, so whether operating profit extends its three-quarter growth run is the next earnings checkpoint.

Bull factors

Subsidiary profit growth feeding into stake values

SK hynix reported 3Q26 revenue of KRW 104.6 trillion and operating profit of KRW 83.3 trillion, which forms the earnings base behind SK's stakes. Hanwha Investment highlighted that cash flows from SK hynix and SK ecoplant reach the holding company. The same report raised its 2026 operating profit estimate for SK to KRW 16.7 trillion.

Shareholder return policy and dividend linkage

Hanwha Investment expects a new shareholder return policy to be announced within 2026. It said how far subsidiary dividend income is linked to shareholder returns is the key question.

Parent-level profit staying in surplus

Parent-attributable net profit stayed in surplus for three quarters after the 2025 turnaround, at KRW 3.38 trillion in 1Q26, KRW 5.96 trillion in 2Q26 and KRW 5.63 trillion in 3Q26. The last four quarters total KRW 12.7 trillion.

According to Hanwha Investment, SK ecoplant's 3Q26 revenue rose 82% and operating profit rose 405% year on year.

09

Bear factors

Lower listed-subsidiary values and holding discount

Hanwha Investment cut its target price to reflect lower values for listed subsidiaries. Its valuation applied a 50% discount to listed-company values. Because stake values follow subsidiary share prices, the result shifts sharply with the discount assumption.

Divergent broker views on the memory cycle

In a July 2026 report, BNK Investment & Securities set a KRW 1.85 million target price for SK hynix and assigned a Hold rating. It cited the possibility that hyperscalers slow infrastructure spending and said earnings momentum could weaken after year-end. This is one brokerage's view, and other brokers raised their targets over the same period.

Thin core profitability and balance sheet load

Operating margin fell from 5.9% in 2022 to 1.5% in 2025. Operating cash flow declined from KRW 11.4 trillion in 2023 to KRW 6.1 trillion in 2025. The debt ratio stood at 149.2% at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 127.8 trillion.

10

Risk factors

Concentration on subsidiary results

The holding company's results depend heavily on a few subsidiaries, notably SK hynix and SK ecoplant. SK hynix's earnings track D-RAM and NAND prices, so a price downturn could reduce dividends and equity-linked income at the same time. The sharp rise in 3Q26 operating profit also rests on subsidiary results.

Yongin cluster project execution

Hi-tech revenue at SK ecoplant depends on construction progress at the Yongin semiconductor cluster project.

If the process falls behind schedule, revenue recognition in that segment could be delayed, and the timing at which subsidiary profits reach the holding company's stake value and dividend income could also shift.

Because the Asset Lifecycle segment is driving profit growth at SK ecoplant, a setback in the Hi-tech segment could affect the group's earnings mix.

HBM4 product transition

SK hynix expects HBM4 to become its mainstream product from the fourth quarter. If the transition runs behind plan, the share of high-value products in sales could shrink, affecting subsidiary operating profit and the dividend amount reaching the holding company.

Because D-RAM and HBM volumes are described as effectively sold out through 2029, a delay would also bear on the timing of deliveries under long-term supply agreements.

12

Overall view

SK's DART-filed results show revenue and operating profit declining from 2022 through 2025, with operating margin falling from 5.9% to 1.5%. Parent-attributable net profit posted losses in 2023 and 2024 before turning to a surplus in 2025.

The quarterly picture changed in 2026, with operating profit rising to KRW 5.02 trillion in 3Q26 for a third consecutive quarterly increase. The improvement is tied to the earnings recovery at subsidiaries such as SK hynix.

Hanwha Investment cut its target price on lower listed-subsidiary values while maintaining its Buy rating. Positive factors include the possibility of higher subsidiary dividends and a shareholder return policy announcement.

Negative factors include lower listed-subsidiary values, the discount assumption and divergent broker views on the memory cycle.

Given an operating margin of 1.5% in 2025 and a debt ratio of 149.2% at year-end, the quality of holding-level earnings depends on how far subsidiary dividends convert into holding-company cash. The next confirmation points are the year-end shareholder return policy, the 4Q26 results and the 2026 annual report.
