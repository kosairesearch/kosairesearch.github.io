02

Business structure

TIME Technical Support develops integrated logistics support (ILS/IPS) elements for weapon systems, along with electronic technical manuals such as IETM and CBT, AR/VR/MR-based virtual training content, military technical translation, publishing and printing, and support equipment.

Founded in 2004 and based in Changwon, the company is a small enterprise organized into an IPS division, an AR/VR/MR digital content division, a technical translation division, a publishing and printing division and a support equipment division.

Its 2025 revenue of 5,552,192,871 KRW is reported entirely as service revenue, which points to a structure that depends on development and service contracts rather than product sales.

Defense orders typically reach the company through prime contractors that develop complete weapon systems, with TIME Technical Support working as a supplier. On exports, a 2008 project to translate a technical manual for a Turkey-bound tank was recorded as the company's first export project.

On the technical side, it has operated and developed S1000D-based electronic technical manuals with a domestic aerospace prime over four years of collaboration. The annual report also records the first domestic generation of an LSA database under an international standard.

Its 2007 Defense Ministry security clearance forms a barrier to entry in translating classified military documents. Competitively, the company is concentrated in a narrow niche of defense IPS and military translation, so its revenue scale and customer mix differ from those of broader listed IT service companies.

A generative AI-based IPS enhancement platform is under research at a defense AI specialized center, aiming to learn from IPS deliverables.

03

Recent trends

According to the 2025 annual report (filed March 23, 2026), revenue reached 5,552,192,871 KRW, up from 5,172,575,079 KRW in 2024, a growth rate of 7.34%. Operating profit was 297,730,437 KRW, reversing an operating loss of 1,340,888,709 KRW in 2024. The operating margin improved to 5.36% from -25.92% in 2024.

Pre-tax profit was 145,342,784 KRW, but after an income tax benefit of 859,147,656 KRW, net profit was reported at 1,004,490,440 KRW, compared with a net loss of 1,502,120,696 KRW in 2024. Basic earnings per share swung from a loss in 2024 to a profit in 2025.

Operating cash flow was an outflow of 343,147,826 KRW, mainly reflecting a 653,073,509 KRW increase in trade receivables, and advances received from customers fell by 429,965,758 KRW.

At the end of 2024, total assets and total liabilities were each about 53 billion KRW, and rounded equity stood at zero, indicating near-complete erosion of capital.

According to an investment data site, the share price was shown at 3,900 KRW on October 5, 2026, while the current price at the time of writing is 1,599 KRW with a market capitalization of 3.4 billion KRW. Trading value at the time of writing was 2,397,179 KRW, reflecting thin liquidity typical of KONEX trading. The largest shareholder held 78.25% as of December 2024.

04

Outlook

On the sector side, defense improvement spending is projected to grow 11.3% annually under the mid-term defense plan, and demand for S1000D-based technical manuals and GEIA-based KSP application is rising in integrated logistics support.

A securities research report by Seoul Credit Rating & Information (published December 2025) cites expanding domestic defense exports as another driver of IPS and technical translation demand.

On operating metrics, the recovery of the operating margin to 5.36% in 2025 from -25.92% in 2024 leaves room for improvement if revenue keeps growing and fixed costs are absorbed.

However, because most of 2025 net profit came from an income tax benefit, whether operating profit turns positive again will define the quality of earnings.

The generative AI-based IPS platform is described as being in research, so its commercialization timing and revenue contribution cannot be gauged from current filings.

At the 2026 annual report filing, the continuity of operating profit, the size of any tax benefit and the direction of operating cash flow will be the main checkpoints. As long as the largest shareholder's concentrated stake persists, the tradable float will remain limited.

Whether sector demand translates into order gains for the company depends on the procurement schedules of major defense prime contractors.

05

Bull factors

Defense improvement spending and IPS demand

According to the mid-term defense plan, defense improvement spending is projected to grow 11.3% a year. Demand for S1000D-based technical manuals is growing within integrated logistics support.

According to the company's own profile, TIME Technical Support takes part in developing and operating major ROK weapon systems such as the T-50, FA-50 and K21 armored vehicle, placing it within the direct scope of this demand.

If demand for electronic manuals and maintenance systems rises together, IPS element development volume could expand in parallel.

Return to operating profit

Revenue in 2025 rose to 5,552,192,871 KRW, and operating profit reached 297,730,437 KRW. The company moved from a 1,340,888,709 KRW operating loss in 2024 back into profit. The 5.36% operating margin contrasts with -25.92% in 2024.

For a company of this scale, the move into a range where fixed costs are being absorbed is read as a sign of recovering earnings capacity.

Security clearance and generative AI platform capability

TIME Technical Support received a Defense Ministry security clearance in 2007 and operates as a technical translation specialist that meets entry requirements for classified military documents. A defense AI specialized center is researching a generative AI platform that learns from IPS deliverables.

The accumulated technical manual data could serve as training assets for the AI platform, a point cited as a differentiator.

06

Bear factors

2024 losses and eroded equity

The 2024 net loss was 1,502,120,696 KRW and the operating loss was 1,340,888,709 KRW. At the end of 2024, assets and liabilities were each about 53 billion KRW, and equity was shown as zero on a rounded basis.

The 2025 turnaround reduced the deficit, but with thin equity, the debt burden remains a weakness in the capital structure. The size of accumulated losses relative to the company's scale also deserves attention.

Tax-benefit dependence and cash flow reversal

The 2025 net profit of 1,004,490,440 KRW reflects an income tax benefit of 859,147,656 KRW, while pre-tax profit was only 145,342,784 KRW. Operating cash flow was an outflow of 343,147,826 KRW. A 653,073,509 KRW increase in trade receivables is cited as the main factor behind slower cash collection. Narrowing the gap between booked profit and cash generation is the task ahead.

Largest-shareholder concentration and KONEX liquidity

As of December 2024, the largest shareholder, CEO Ju Yang-hyo, held 78.25%, and including related parties the stake reached 83.73%. Under this structure, the tradable float is limited. Trading value at the time of writing was only 2,397,179 KRW.

According to an investment data site, the share price was 3,900 KRW on October 5, 2026, and is shown at 1,599 KRW at the time of writing.

07

Risk factors

Macro

The projected rise in defense improvement spending depends on fiscal conditions and budget allocation. If budgets are executed later than planned, IPS orders could also be delayed. Export contract conditions and changes in the international defense market can indirectly affect domestic subcontracting volumes. These external factors are largely outside the company's control.

Business structure

TIME Technical Support receives orders through prime contractors that develop complete weapon systems. If the schedule or cooperation structure of a specific program changes, earnings volatility could widen.

Because the business is concentrated in IPS and military translation, a gap in one program has a large effect on total revenue. Broadening the revenue base remains a task.

Liquidity and governance

With the largest shareholder holding 78.25%, the tradable float is small. KONEX trading volume is thin, so price moves can be large even on small trades. Trading value at the time of writing was around 2,397,179 KRW.

The scope of disclosure and information flow is also more limited than for large-cap stocks, which is a factor to consider.

08

Overall view

Based on the 2025 annual report, TIME Technical Support is a defense IPS company that showed both revenue growth and a return to operating profit. The sector backdrop of rising defense improvement spending and IPS demand is cited as an operating opportunity.

On the other side, net profit relied heavily on an income tax benefit, operating cash flow was negative, and equity stood at roughly zero on a rounded basis at the end of 2024. The 78.25% largest-shareholder stake and KONEX trading volume are structural factors that make share price movements hard to interpret.

According to an investment data site, the share price moved from 3,900 KRW on October 5, 2026 to 1,599 KRW at the time of writing, and filings alone cannot explain how that move relates to earnings changes over the same period.

Strengths lie in sector demand and the operating turnaround, while weaknesses lie in cash flow, capital structure, governance and liquidity. The next checkpoints are the continuity of operating profit and the direction of operating cash flow in the 2026 quarterly disclosures and the annual report.
