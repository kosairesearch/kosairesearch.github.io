Report overview

Back to Profit, Thin Balance Sheet

TIME Technical Support is a KONEX-listed defense logistics-support software and technical translation company. It returned to an operating profit in 2025, but its financial cushion remains thin.

02

Business structure

TIME Technical Support began as a technical translation business in August 2004. It converted to a corporation in October 2018 and listed on the KONEX market in June 2021. It specializes in integrated product support (IPS) and technical translation covering the full life cycle of defense weapon systems.

The IPS business develops interactive electronic technical manuals (IETM) and electronic training materials (CBT) that support weapon system maintenance and operation. The company says its IPS portfolio spans programs from the T-50 to the KF-X (KF-21).

Its technical translation unit holds the security clearance needed for weapon system import and export work, which the company describes as unique in the industry. It also runs a separate business developing training and maintenance platforms for weapon systems using virtual and augmented reality.

Based on a financial data provider's compilation, revenue is split 57% IPS, 40% technical translation and 3% software. On customers, the annual report's order list shows 19 contracts: 12 from KAI, 4 from a defense supplier, 2 from Hyundai Rotem and 1 from Hanwha Aerospace. Customers are concentrated in large aerospace and defense groups, so order timing has an outsized effect.

In electronic technical manual software, KAIS for the army and navy and an air force tool called eXPIS developed by another company have been used. TIME Technical Support plans to use its S-Series standard-based expertise to meet overseas demand.

The company says it supplies Raytheon's EAGLE logistics toolset in Korea and is its only official reseller there. Software accounts for a small share of sales, so direct comparison with large system integrators is difficult.

Ownership is concentrated. As of December 2024, CEO Ju Yang-hyo held 78.25%, and he and eight related parties together held 83.73%.

03

Recent trends

On a financial data provider's compilation, 2025 revenue was 5.552 billion won, up 7.34% from 2024. Operating profit was 297.73 million won, an operating margin of 5.36%. In 2023, operating profit was 599.37 million won and the margin was 10.27%.

Results deteriorated sharply in 2024. On a separate basis, revenue fell 11.4%, and operating profit and net income both turned to losses. Revenue was 5.173 billion won, the operating loss was 1.341 billion won and the net loss was 1.502 billion won. Gross profit was also negative, with a gross loss of 744.48 million won.

2025 net income was 1.004 billion won, far above operating profit. Pre-tax income was 145.34 million won, while income tax expense was negative 859.15 million won. Tax items, more than operating improvement, drove the higher net income.

The balance sheet improved, but pressure remains. Total equity rose from roughly 2 million won in 2024 to 1.066 billion won in 2025. Total liabilities were 4.533 billion won, the debt ratio was 425.08% and borrowing dependence was 60.99%. Operating cash flow in 2025 was a net outflow of 352.79 million won.

Business news continued through 2026. On January 29, it signed a technical translation contract with STX Engine for naval vessel propulsion manuals. On April 22, it won KAI's LAH second-mass-production PBL integrated test equipment logistics support development project.

On May 12, media reported its win of KAI's KF-21 operator training material development support project. On June 16, it announced it was starting a generative AI platform project for logistics support with Hanwha Aerospace. Quarterly or half-year results after 2025 could not be confirmed in this research.

The share price is volatile. The October 21, 2026 close was 3,900 won on volume of only 53 shares. On October 27, 2026, the price was 2,790 won, down 11.15% from the prior session, implying a prior close of about 3,140 won. The 52-week range runs from 1,702 won to 7,620 won.

The 12-month change as of October 21 was 107.42%. Trading value on October 27 was only 13,045,755 won, so even small trades can move the price.

04

Outlook

Defense budgets are the main external variable. FnGuide's company summary says defense capability improvement spending is expected to grow 11.3% a year on average under the mid-term defense plan. This growth is cited as background for IPS and technical manual development demand.

However, no broker consensus could be found, so earnings expectations must rest on company filings and order records.

Some long-dated contracts support the order book. The annual report's order list includes KAI contracts running to 2028 and 2030. Long contract terms improve revenue visibility, but low-progress orders delay revenue recognition. Two contracts signed in December 2025 are only 1-2% complete.

AI is the core of the expansion effort. The Hanwha Aerospace platform structures and vectorizes technical manual data, combines retrieval-augmented generation with a lightweight language model, and is designed to run on closed networks.

The company plans to extend the scope to predictive maintenance, digital twins and maintenance optimization. Media have also reported that Hanwha Aerospace is expanding its digital MRO solutions. The size and timing of any revenue and profit contribution from this business have not been disclosed.

On profitability, the operating margin has room to improve. The 2025 operating margin of 5.36% is below the 10.27% of 2023. Project cost control and staff utilization can change the margin widely. If costs exceed contract prices, as in the 2024 gross loss, losses could recur.

On the financial side, borrowing and cash flow are key. Operating cash flow was a net outflow in 2025, so working capital funding matters. Because 2025 net income was affected by tax items, earnings quality from 2026 should be checked through operating profit and cash flow.

05

Bull factors

Return to operating profit and long-dated orders

Operating profit of 297.73 million won in 2025 reversed the 2024 operating loss. Revenue also rose 7.34% in 2025, returning to growth after a decline in 2024. The order list includes contracts running to 2028 and 2030, which provides a revenue base for a period. Order news centered on KAI continued in 2026.

Defense digitalization and AI expansion

A project to develop a generative AI platform for logistics support with Hanwha Aerospace has started. The April 7, 2026 IPS forum covered S1000D-based digital transformation and AI-based predictive maintenance, among other topics.

The company says its technical manual data and experience in secure environments underpin the AI business. Large defense groups expanding AI maintenance forms part of the demand backdrop.

Security clearance and overseas solution rights

TIME Technical Support was the first specialist technical translation firm to obtain Ministry of National Defense security clearance, in 2007. Clearance acts as an entry requirement for work on military documents. The company says it is the only official domestic reseller of Raytheon's EAGLE. Capability based on international standards is a prerequisite for overseas expansion.

06

Bear factors

Small profit base and high volatility

Operating profit of 297.73 million won in 2025 was only 5.36% of 5.552 billion won in revenue. In 2024, the company recorded a gross loss of 744.48 million won and an operating loss of 1.341 billion won. This shows that results can swing widely depending on project pricing and staffing.

The 2025 net income of 1.004 billion won owes much to a tax benefit, so it is not a clean measure of operating performance.

High leverage and cash outflow

At end-2025 the debt ratio was 425.08% and borrowing dependence was 60.99%. The quick ratio was 63.57%, below 100%. With operating cash flow a net outflow of 352.79 million won, working capital must rely on outside funding. Total equity had shrunk to roughly 2 million won at end-2024, so the capital cushion is thin.

Customer concentration and low liquidity

Of the 19 contracts on the annual report's order list, 12 come from KAI. Results therefore depend on a single customer's budget and schedule. Related-party holdings of 83.73% leave few shares in circulation, and volume on October 21 was only 53 shares. In such conditions, wide bid-ask gaps can cause execution prices to differ sharply.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Defense capability spending and program schedules depend on national budgets and the security environment. Program delays or budget adjustments directly affect the timing of the company's revenue recognition. Borrowing dependence is 60.99%, so interest burden can change with interest rate levels. The 2025 interest coverage ratio was a modest 2.57 times.

Business

A large share of orders is concentrated in KAI, so customer schedule and budget changes matter greatly. Under percentage-of-completion revenue recognition, cost overruns can be recognized all at once. The annual report lists total unbilled construction receivables of 699,806 thousand won in its table.

The AI business's revenue contribution has not been disclosed, and competition with larger firms is unavoidable.

Financial and market

The KONEX market has limited participation, and trading is sparse. The three-month average volume is reported at 233 shares. The 6.0 billion won market capitalization is more than five times the 1.066 billion won total equity at end-2025. Events such as capital raising or changes in the major shareholder's stake could widen price volatility.

08

Overall view

TIME Technical Support is a small KONEX company with a stable customer base in defense logistics support, built on IPS and technical translation. In 2025, revenue growth and a return to operating profit were confirmed, and in 2026 KAI orders and the Hanwha Aerospace AI platform project followed.

At the same time, the operating margin is low at 5.36%, and the company posted a large loss in 2024. 2025 net income was influenced by a tax benefit, and the 425.08% debt ratio and operating cash outflow remain financial burdens.

With a 6.0 billion won market capitalization and very thin trading, the price can move sharply on small volumes, and it fell 11.15% from the prior session on October 27, 2026.

This report is a qualitative summary for information only, and results and financial figures after the first half of 2026 should be checked directly in DART filings.
