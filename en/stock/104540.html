02

Business structure

Corentec is an orthopedic implant specialist founded in 2000 and based in Chungcheongnam-do, South Korea, designing, manufacturing and selling hip, knee and spinal fixation devices.

The company announced on November 9, 2026 that it had restructured its portfolio around artificial joints after exiting non-core pharmaceutical operations. In the first half of 2023, artificial hip and knee sales were KRW 12.5 billion (38.8%) and KRW 15.1 billion (46.7%), together 85.5% of total sales.

The company says its clinical track record made it the number one domestic artificial hip joint supplier in 2025, and it is upgrading its knee lineup on that base. Overseas, US operations account for about 20% of sales, and the company stated in July 2026 that its US market share is about 0.2%.

The United States, which accounts for 64% of the global joint replacement market, is the target of a pipeline that includes cementless knee implants. Sales are also expanding into Latin America and Southeast Asia, but country-level revenue mix for 2025 was not confirmed.

Its dental push began in 2022 with a partnership with US-based RODO Medical on cementless implant prosthetics, but 2026 reports describe the business as refocused on artificial joints, and the current scale of dental operations was not confirmed.

Against competitors, the gap between its domestic hip leadership and its US share is read as room for growth, but competitor share comparisons were not confirmed in this review.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 63.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 92 billion in 2023 (up 44.0% year on year), KRW 93.7 billion in 2024 (up 1.8%) and KRW 100.4 billion in 2025 (up 7.2%), marking three straight years of growth from 2023 to 2025.

Of the 2022 to 2025 period, 2025 was the largest year, and growth accelerated from 1.8% in 2024 to 7.2% in 2025. Operating profit was KRW 5.85 billion in 2022 and KRW 7.05 billion in 2023 (up 20.7%), fell to KRW 3.89 billion in 2024 (down 44.8%), and recovered to KRW 9.12 billion in 2025 (up 134.3%).

The operating margin declined from 9.1% in 2022 to 7.7% in 2023 and 4.2% in 2024 before returning to 9.1% in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent swung to a net loss of KRW 1.32 billion in 2024 and back to a net profit of KRW 6.97 billion in 2025, and the filing summary does not explain why 2024 showed a net loss despite positive operating profit.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 10.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.63 billion in 2023, to -KRW 6.61 billion in 2024, and recovered to KRW 26.5 billion in 2025.

At end-2025 total equity was KRW 89.2 billion, of which KRW 78.4 billion belonged to owners of the parent, and total liabilities were KRW 82.8 billion, giving a debt ratio of 92.8% against 128.3% in 2022.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 25.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 28.6 billion in the fourth quarter (up 11.0% from the prior quarter), fell to KRW 24.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 14.1%), and then held near KRW 25.8 billion and KRW 25.6 billion in the second and third quarters.

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 3.62 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 3.09 billion in the fourth quarter to a trough of KRW 886 million in the first quarter of 2026, then rose to KRW 1.45 billion and KRW 1.8 billion in the second and third quarters, two straight quarters of gains, though third-quarter operating profit was still 50.3% below a year earlier.

The company said third-quarter operating profit included upfront R&D spending for entry into large overseas markets, and attributed the first-quarter profit decline to the performance of a consolidated subsidiary.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent went from KRW 1.44 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 3.87 billion in the fourth quarter to KRW 1.28 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 1.1 billion in the second, then turned to a net loss of KRW 707 million in the third quarter.

The sources do not identify the cause of that loss despite positive third-quarter operating profit.

The four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 total KRW 5.54 billion in profit attributable to owners of the parent, and nine-month 2026 cumulative revenue was KRW 76.0 billion with operating profit of KRW 4.14 billion.

Industry analysis

In artificial joints, the United States is the largest market, accounting for 64% of the global joint replacement market, and the company announced in November 2026 that it is preparing a pipeline aimed at that market. Domestically, steady demand for artificial joints is cited as the base of the company's earnings.

On the cycle, the company notes that orthopedic surgery and product shipments are relatively more active in the fourth quarter, so seasonality is pronounced.

On competition, the company reports it was the number one domestic artificial hip joint supplier by market share in 2025, but comparisons with major global players were not confirmed in this review.

With a US share of about 0.2%, the room to enter is large, but local distribution and regulatory approval are industry-wide requirements, and company-specific detail was not confirmed.

In dental prosthetics, a 2022 report citing statistics from the Korea Research Institute of Science and Technology projected a market of about KRW 710 billion in 2023, but more recent market data were not confirmed.

In 2026, the business the company emphasizes is artificial joints, and dental operations do not feature prominently in its recent strategy descriptions.

By sector classification the company falls under biopharma, but its operating business is orthopedic medical devices, so direct comparisons of business structure with listed biopharma peers are limited.

The exit from non-core pharmaceutical operations narrowed the business to one artificial joint axis, which highlights the gap between the sector label and the actual business.

06

Outlook

The company says it views the fourth quarter of 2026 as a seasonal peak, when orthopedic surgeries and product shipments are relatively active, and expects results to improve.

It states that it is responding to rising domestic and overseas orders by improving cost ratios and shortening delivery lead times, and is streamlining fixed costs so that new product launches and sales growth translate into higher operating profit.

To that end, it hired a supply chain management specialist with more than 26 years of experience at companies including Alcon and GE Healthcare to streamline manufacturing and logistics.

In the United States, it is preparing new product launches and diversifying vendors, but the specific launch timing was not confirmed in this review. The knee joint is the next growth area the company says it will focus on after hips, and development of cementless knee implants is described as ongoing.

The company expects structural operating profit improvement to appear gradually as R&D results become visible and cost and process adjustments take hold, but it has not provided numerical guidance.

On capital structure, the company announced a debt ratio of 77.0% at the end of the third quarter of 2026, and all 621,670 redeemable convertible preferred shares converted into common shares on May 22, 2026, bringing common shares outstanding to 13,407,410.

The next checkpoints are whether fourth-quarter results show the seasonal peak effect and whether the operating margin, after its first-quarter trough, returns to the 9.1% level of 2025.

Bull factors

Three Years of Revenue Growth Continues Into 2026

Revenue rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, reaching KRW 100.4 billion in 2025. Nine-month 2026 cumulative revenue was KRW 76.0 billion, and the company said domestic artificial joint demand and US sales supported results.

First-quarter 2026 revenue rose 8.6% year on year to KRW 24.6 billion, showing that the core business's growth held at the quarterly level.

Balance Sheet Repair and a Simpler Capital Structure

The company announced that its debt ratio fell from 92.8% at end-2025 to 77.0% at end-third quarter 2026. The reduction came from repaying borrowings with proceeds from the sale of a stake in a non-core pharmaceutical business.

On May 22, 2026, all 621,670 redeemable convertible preferred shares converted into common stock, which simplified the capital structure.

Room to Expand in the US and Knee Joints

The United States accounts for 64% of the global joint replacement market, yet the company's US share is only about 0.2%. US operations already make up roughly 20% of total sales. The company is pursuing a pipeline that includes cementless knee implants to narrow that gap.

Bear factors

Shareholder Net Loss in the Third Quarter

Profit attributable to owners of the parent turned to a net loss of KRW 707 million in the third quarter of 2026, after a profit a year earlier. Operating profit in the same quarter was KRW 1.8 billion, down 50.3% from a year earlier.

The company attributed part of the operating result to upfront R&D spending, but the cause of the net loss was not identified in the sources.

Unconfirmed Durability of Margin Recovery

The operating margin recovered from 4.2% in 2024 to 9.1% in 2025, but first-quarter 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 886 million. The company attributed the first-quarter decline to the performance of a consolidated subsidiary.

Cost ratio improvement and fixed-cost efficiency are under way, but their effect has not yet appeared in quarterly results.

A Low Overseas Starting Point and Subsidiary Exposure

A US share of about 0.2% signals room to grow, but it also means local competition and approval requirements are a significant hurdle. In the first quarter of 2026, the performance of a consolidated subsidiary pulled quarterly operating profit down.

The current scale of dental operations was not confirmed in public materials, so it has not yet emerged as a new growth axis.

Risk factors

Quarterly Earnings Volatility

Revenue is seasonal, peaking in the fourth quarter and softening in the first. After KRW 28.6 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, revenue fell to KRW 24.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026, and operating profit dropped to KRW 886 million. Large swings in quarterly profit require caution when interpreting annual figures.

Financial and Cash Flow

Operating cash flow was -KRW 6.61 billion in 2024, and total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 82.8 billion. Repayment of borrowings lowered the debt ratio, but the stability of cash flow needs confirmation over a longer period.

The 77.0% debt ratio at end-third quarter 2026 is a company-announced figure and should be rechecked against periodic filings.

Business Concentration and Regulation

With non-core pharmaceutical operations exited, the business now rests mainly on one artificial joint axis. US expansion and new products depend on local regulatory approval and distribution, and launch timing has not been disclosed. The lack of confirmed progress in other areas such as dental adds to the concentration risk.
