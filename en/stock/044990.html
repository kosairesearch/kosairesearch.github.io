02

Business structure

H&S High Tech is an electronic components company whose core products are ACF (anisotropic conductive film) and crystal oscillators. ACF is described as an ultra-precise bonding material that connects fine electronic parts such as displays and smartphone camera modules.

The business is split into a materials division and an electronics division, and in 2024 the materials division generated KRW 57.8 billion of sales and the electronics division KRW 18.3 billion, according to a July 2025 company report from Daishin Securities.

The company said it holds more than 80% of the domestic ACF market for LCD and PCB use, as reported in August 2026. Press coverage from November 2025, citing the business report, said its global share in large display PCB and COF bonding products exceeds 70%.

Its customers center on global mobile device makers, and sales of premium products tend to concentrate from September through year-end. The competitive landscape is described as a three-player structure alongside Japan's Dexerials and Resonac.

A 2025 securities report characterized the crystal oscillator business as having declined for two consecutive years. One brokerage analysis put the share of new businesses at 1.9% of sales in 2024 and 4.1% in 2025, and the CEO said the company has invested more than KRW 25 billion in R&D over the past five years.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales moved from KRW 97.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 80.6 billion in 2023, a 17.1% decline, then to KRW 76.2 billion in 2024, down 5.5%, before recovering to KRW 81.9 billion in 2025, up 7.5%. Across 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 21.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 14 billion in 2023, KRW 13.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.66 billion in 2025, declining for three consecutive years from 2023.

In 2025 operating profit fell 27.7% even though sales grew, so the failure of higher sales to reach profit is the key sign of weaker profitability. The operating margin was 22.2%, 17.3%, 17.6% and 11.8% across the four years, lowest in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 19.3 billion in 2022, peaked at KRW 30.1 billion in 2023 (up 55.9%), then fell to KRW 14.7 billion in 2024 (down 51.3%) and KRW 8.89 billion in 2025 (down 39.4%).

In 2023 that profit exceeded operating profit, suggesting non-operating items may have played a large role. Operating cash flow declined every year, from KRW 35.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 25.1 billion, KRW 18 billion, and KRW 10.7 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, sales went from KRW 23.5 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 21.6 billion in Q4 2025, then rose to KRW 22.2 billion, KRW 22.8 billion and KRW 27 billion across the first three quarters of 2026.

Operating profit was KRW 3.07 billion, KRW 1.97 billion and KRW 3.97 billion in those three quarters, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 597 million in Q3 2026 after peaking at KRW 3.64 billion in Q1 2026, declining for two consecutive quarters. The sum of profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 8.66 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The company says the ACF market is being reshaped around fine-pitch alignment-type ACF as high-resolution displays spread (reported in November 2025). The market is described as a three-player structure involving Dexerials and Resonac alongside H&S High Tech.

The company has claimed that Resonac is failing to keep pace in alignment-type technology, a view that comes from the company itself. Demand from the front end depends heavily on the timing of new smartphone launches by global mobile makers.

The company said premium product supply concentrates from September through year-end (reported in August 2026). In the first half of 2026, a high exchange rate was cited as a factor lifting won-denominated sales.

The materials division is led by ACF for mobile camera modules, while the electronics division is supported by crystal oscillators. A 2025 securities report suggested the oscillator business could recover from its decline, but 2026 results have not yet confirmed that.

On the display side, a 2025 brokerage analysis said supply is widening from LCD-focused products to ACF for mass-produced OLED. Overall, the company holds a high share of its core market, but quarterly results move with customer set shipments and the exchange rate.

06

Outlook

When it announced first-half 2026 results, the company said ACF supply should rise further in the second half (reported in August 2026). It attributed the expected increase to new smartphone launches in volume.

The company said that if the momentum continued in the second half, annual sales could exceed KRW 100 billion for the first time since its founding. That is a company projection, and nine-month 2026 sales were about KRW 72 billion according to press reports on the quarterly report.

Q4 2026 results have not yet been published in a periodic report, so whether the annual target is reached will be seen in the 2026 annual business report.

On the new-business side, a 2025 brokerage analysis said development is under way for OLED mass-production ACF, automotive electronics materials and NCF for semiconductor bonding.

On the technology side, whether more than KRW 25 billion of R&D over five years turns into advanced products such as alignment-type ACF is the key question. On the production side, a new line for high-spec alignment-type ACF and low-temperature fast-curing ACF was planned using funds from the IPO. Whether that capacity starts operating and feeds into sales and margins is the next point to confirm.

Bull factors

High share in alignment-type ACF

Press coverage from November 2025 citing the business report put its global share in large display PCB and COF bonding above 70%. Its alignment-type technology is presented as the point of differentiation in a three-player field with Dexerials and Resonac.

Sales recovery and consecutive growth

Sales rose for three consecutive quarters, from KRW 21.6 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 22.2 billion, KRW 22.8 billion and KRW 27 billion in the first three quarters of 2026. Q3 2026 sales were up 14.7% year on year. Press reports based on the quarterly report put nine-month sales at KRW 72 billion, up 19.4%.

Financial room and new-business expansion

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 12.5%, with total liabilities of KRW 12.9 billion. Total equity of KRW 102.9 billion is entirely attributable to controlling shareholders.

A brokerage analysis said the share of new businesses rose from 1.9% of sales in 2024 to 4.1% in 2025, with expanded ACF supply for OLED mass production cited.

09

Bear factors

Lower profitability and falling net profit

The operating margin fell from 22.2% in 2022 to 11.8% in 2025, and operating profit has declined for three consecutive years from 2023. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 597 million in Q3 2026, down 86.3% year on year, and the gap with operating profit of KRW 3.97 billion widened sharply. Operating cash flow also fell every year, to KRW 10.7 billion in 2025.

Dependence on mobile set cycles and FX

Sales concentrate from September through year-end around the premium product launches of global mobile makers. In the first half of 2026, a high exchange rate was cited as lifting won-denominated sales. If customer set volumes fall or the exchange rate shifts, quarterly results could swing more widely.

Rising competition and a weak oscillator business

The ACF market is described as a three-player contest with Dexerials and Resonac, and competition is intense. A 2025 securities report said the oscillator business had posted two consecutive years of decline.

Whether that segment, part of KRW 18.3 billion in 2024 electronics sales, recovers will need to be checked in the 2026 annual business report.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and set shipments

A large part of sales is tied to smartphone shipments by global mobile makers. If a key customer's premium-model volumes fall or launches slip, quarterly sales and operating profit can both move sharply. In Q3 2026 sales rose while operating profit fell 6.0% year on year, which illustrates this sensitivity.

Exchange rate volatility

The company said a high exchange rate lifted won-denominated sales in the first half of 2026. If the exchange rate stabilizes or the won strengthens, the won value of overseas sales would shrink. How much of the sales growth came from currency effects needs to be checked against quarterly data.

Capacity and R&D spending burden

More than KRW 25 billion has gone into R&D over the past five years, and a new line for high-spec alignment-type ACF and low-temperature fast-curing ACF is being built. Operating margins could stay low until these costs are recovered through sales. The start-up timing and yields of the new line will need to be checked in reports from 2027 onward.

12

Overall view

H&S High Tech and press reports describe the company as holding more than 80% of the domestic ACF market for LCD and PCB use, and more than 70% of global share in large display PCB and COF bonding products. Sales rose for three consecutive quarters through Q3 2026, and nine-month sales reached KRW 72 billion, up 19.4%.

On the other side, annual operating profit has declined for three straight years from 2023, and the operating margin fell to 11.8% in 2025. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 597 million in Q3 2026, down 86.3% year on year, so whether the operating recovery reaches net profit is the central issue.

On the financial side, the debt ratio at the end of 2025 was a low 12.5%. Strengths are market share and the sales recovery, while weaknesses are the margin decline and reliance on customers and the exchange rate.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results and the 2026 annual business report, and whether the KRW 100 billion sales target is met and the operating margin recovers will be the benchmarks.
