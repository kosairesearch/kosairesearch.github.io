02

Business structure

YC was founded in October 2015 and listed on KOSDAQ in December 2015 as a semiconductor test equipment maker. Its core product is the memory wafer tester, which is used in the EDS process to separate good and defective dies at the wafer stage.

A securities report from late 2025 estimated that the DRAM and HBM wafer tester MT8311 accounts for about 80% of company revenue. Based on the third quarter of 2025, semiconductor and electronics made up 98.6% of revenue and other businesses 1.4%.

A consolidated subsidiary also sells semiconductor manufacturing equipment parts. Customer concentration is high: a Korea IR Council report put Samsung Electronics-related revenue at about 95% of the total, as reported in November 2025.

Samsung Electronics invested KRW 47.3 billion in 2020 to acquire a stake that was later diluted to about 11.70%, as reported by the press. YC developed its HBM tester independently rather than through joint development with Samsung, which reportedly secured its sales rights.

On the competitive side, Advantest was described as the only company able to produce high-speed-inspection HBM wafer testers, and a Hyundai Motor Securities analyst said in a June 2024 report that YC could make such a tester domestically.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 285.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 255.2 billion in 2023 (down 10.5% year on year) and KRW 211.2 billion in 2024 (down 17.2%), then rose to KRW 272.6 billion in 2025 (up 29.1%). The largest year in 2022–2025 was 2022 and the smallest was 2024.

Operating profit fell from KRW 36.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.57 billion in 2023 (down 76.5%), the low point, and then rose to KRW 10.6 billion in 2024 (up 23.7%) and KRW 17 billion in 2025 (up 60.3%), increasing for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

Operating margin was 12.8% in 2022, 3.4% in 2023, 5.0% in 2024 and 6.2% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 26.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.5 billion in 2023 (down 48.2%) and KRW 11 billion in 2024 (down 18.5%), recovering to KRW 19.3 billion in 2025 (up 74.9%) but still below the 2022 level.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 29 billion in 2025, above the controlling-shareholder figure. Operating cash flow was negative KRW 23.6 billion in 2024 before turning to KRW 41.2 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio declined from 46.4% in 2022 to 27.5% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 476.2 billion at the end of 2025, of which KRW 368 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities of KRW 131 billion.

Quarterly, revenue rose from KRW 66.6 billion with operating profit of KRW 4.89 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 86.7 billion and KRW 9.24 billion in the fourth quarter.

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue fell 61.6% quarter on quarter to KRW 33.3 billion and operating profit fell 56.8% to KRW 3.99 billion, then the second quarter jumped to KRW 105 billion in revenue (up 215.2%) and KRW 15.1 billion in operating profit (up 276.8%).

The second quarter of 2026 overlaps with the delivery period of the KRW 40.3 billion Samsung contract, which ran from March 3 to June 30, 2026.

Third-quarter revenue of KRW 92.7 billion was down 11.7% from the prior quarter but up 39.2% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 12.4 billion was down 17.9% quarter on quarter but up 152.7% year on year.

Controlling-shareholder net profit was KRW 3.18 billion in the third quarter, down 69.8% from the prior quarter. Over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, controlling-shareholder net profit totaled KRW 31.8 billion, above the KRW 19.3 billion recorded for full-year 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Securities analysts have argued that the importance of test processes that screen defective stacked chips is growing as HBM output rises. High-speed-inspection HBM testers were described as producible only by Advantest, and YC was named as a domestic company able to make such a tester.

In an August 2024 report, a KB Securities analyst said intensifying HBM competition would be an opportunity for Korean equipment makers and named PSK and HANMI Semiconductor as relevant companies.

The company's earnings also show exposure to memory cycles: it attributed the revenue declines in 2023 and 2024 to a slowdown in part of the semiconductor industry.

A late-2025 report expected that, after Samsung Electronics finishes HBM4 customer mass-production tests, large burn-in tester orders could follow, centered on the new P4 line. Retooling demand from converting DRAM equipment to NAND was also cited as a revenue driver.

Because orders concentrate on a few suppliers with specialized processes, quarterly revenue can swing with customers' capital spending schedules. YC is a mid-sized memory wafer tester specialist, and its business structure differs from that of large diversified equipment makers.

How closely demand for memory test equipment tracks HBM investment is the most important variable for this sector.

Outlook

The contracts confirmed so far are three Samsung Electronics agreements.

A KRW 38.75 billion contract disclosed in December 2025 ran through March 30, 2026, a KRW 40.3 billion contract signed on March 3, 2026 ran through June 30, 2026, and a KRW 93 billion contract starting in late April 2026 runs through November 30, 2026, which was reported as 34.11% of 2025 consolidated revenue.

November 30, 2026 is therefore the first point to check for whether follow-on orders continue.

Coverage in November 2025 reported that YC was in qualification testing for HBM burn-in testers at SK hynix, and a company official said that if YC secured orders in that competition, a substantial share of revenue would come from SK hynix.

The same coverage said YC was preparing to supply HBM4 burn-in testers to both Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. A late-2025 report expected large orders centered on Samsung's new P4 line after its HBM4 mass-production tests conclude, with NAND-conversion retooling revenue possibly adding to that.

In a July 2024 report, the company said it planned to develop additional high-end inspection equipment for the market after HBM5. These outlooks come from analyst reports at the time, so actual order sizes should be confirmed in the periodic reports' revenue breakdown.

The 2026 annual business report, the next periodic filing, will show fourth-quarter 2026 results and whether SK hynix supply materialized.

08

Bull factors

Continuity of Samsung contracts

Supply contracts with Samsung Electronics continued, from KRW 38.75 billion in December 2025 and KRW 40.3 billion in March 2026 to KRW 93 billion from late April 2026, which was reported as 34.11% of 2025 consolidated revenue. The sequence of contracts points to continuing demand for memory test equipment.

Profit recovery trend

Operating profit rose from the KRW 8.57 billion low in 2023 to KRW 10.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 17 billion in 2025, increasing for two consecutive years.

Operating profit of KRW 15.1 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 12.4 billion in the third quarter, the latter up 152.7% year on year, shows the recovery continuing in the most recent quarters.

Independent development and high-speed test capability

YC reportedly developed its HBM tester independently rather than jointly with Samsung, which allowed it to pursue sales beyond one customer. A June 2024 analyst report said YC could make a high-speed-inspection HBM tester domestically, a capability previously described as limited to Advantest. Technology differentiation could support customer expansion.

Bear factors

Heavy reliance on Samsung Electronics

Company results are directly affected by Samsung's earnings and industry conditions, so unless the customer base diversifies, one customer's investment timing could determine quarterly results.

Large quarterly swings

First-quarter 2026 revenue fell 61.6% quarter on quarter to KRW 33.3 billion, and operating profit fell 56.8% to KRW 3.99 billion. Revenue then rebounded to KRW 105 billion in the second quarter.

Controlling-shareholder net profit fell 69.8% in the third quarter to KRW 3.18 billion, so quarterly earnings have not been stable.

Low profitability and cycle sensitivity

Operating margin fell from 12.8% in 2022 to 3.4% in 2023 and was still 6.2% in 2025. Controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 19.3 billion in 2025 remained below the KRW 26.1 billion of 2022.

The company attributed its 2023 and 2024 revenue declines to a slowdown in part of the semiconductor industry, which suggests margins are sensitive to the cycle.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration risk

With about 95% of revenue tied to Samsung Electronics, any halt or reduction in its orders would be strongly reflected in results. The KRW 93 billion contract ends on November 30, 2026, so whether follow-on orders arrive without a gap is the near-term issue. Greater in-house equipment or other supplier adoption by the customer could amplify the effect.

Technology competition and product transition risk

In HBM testing, Advantest holds an advantage in high-speed inspection. YC has been credited with comparable capability, but each HBM generation changes specifications and requires continued development spending. Failure to pass customer qualification for new products could create a revenue gap.

Delayed profitability recovery risk

Operating margin of 6.2% in 2025 remains about half the 12.8% recorded in 2022. Quarterly profit rose sharply in the second quarter of 2026 and then declined in the third. If revenue keeps swinging, a high fixed-cost base could slow the recovery in margins.

Overall view

YC is a semiconductor test equipment maker whose core product is the memory wafer tester, with Samsung Electronics accounting for about 95% of revenue.

On an annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 285.3 billion and operating profit from KRW 36.4 billion in 2022 to a low in 2023, then recovered to KRW 272.6 billion and KRW 17 billion in 2025. Operating margin of 6.2% in 2025 remained below the 12.8% of 2022.

Quarterly results in 2026 were volatile, with operating profit of KRW 15.1 billion in the second quarter and KRW 12.4 billion in the third after a sharp first-quarter drop.

Supply contracts with Samsung Electronics were disclosed continuously from December 2025 through November 2026, while whether SK hynix supply materializes remains an open question.

Bullish factors include contract continuity, profit recovery and high-speed inspection technology, while bearish factors include customer concentration, quarterly volatility and low profitability.

The next checkpoints are the end of the KRW 93 billion contract period on November 30, 2026, the 2026 annual business report and first-quarter 2027 results.
