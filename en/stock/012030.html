DB: Holding-company earnings swing as Q3 2026 profit falls

02

Business structure

DB was founded in 1977 and listed on the KOSPI in 1993. According to its company profile, its businesses are IT services centered on SI and ITO, a trading business in chemicals and steel products, and a brand business that manages the DB trademark and group branding and advertising.

The IT business is expanding into service-type offerings such as SaaS and IaaS by using group affiliates' infrastructure, while adding financial-sector clients.

The core semiconductor affiliate, DB HiTek, is an 8-inch foundry; at end-2021 DB held 12.4% of it, and its carrying value equaled 65.7% of DB's standalone total assets.

The financial affiliates include non-life insurance, securities, life insurance and capital businesses, so insurance and brokerage earnings carry significant weight. A 2025 press report said financial businesses were supporting DB's earnings.

DB was excluded from holding-company regulation as of January 1, 2025 because it did not meet the Fair Trade Act requirements. As of October 1, 2025, the largest shareholder and related parties held 45.11% and treasury shares were 5.04%.

SK Square is often compared with DB because both hold IT and chip affiliates, but DB's larger financial-affiliate exposure means its earnings drivers differ. The breakdown of revenue by segment is most precisely confirmed in the segment note of the annual business report.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue on disclosed annual figures rose from KRW 401.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 458.6 billion in 2023 (up 14.3%), KRW 587.4 billion in 2024 (up 28.1%) and KRW 643.5 billion in 2025 (up 9.5%), with three consecutive years of growth from 2023 to 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year.

Operating profit rose from KRW 23.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 37 billion in 2023 (up 56.8%), fell to KRW 29.6 billion in 2024 (down 20.0%) and recovered to KRW 41.6 billion in 2025 (up 40.4%). Operating margin was 5.9% in 2022, 8.1% in 2023, 5.0% in 2024 and 6.5% in 2025.

Revenue grew 28.1% in 2024, yet the operating margin fell to 5.0%, so top-line expansion did not translate into margin gains.

Attributable net income rose from KRW 5.71 billion in 2022 to KRW 20.3 billion in 2023 (up 255.4%) and KRW 92.3 billion in 2024 (up 354.6%), then fell to KRW 49.2 billion in 2025 (down 46.7%). 2024 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 range.

Attributable net income in 2024 far exceeded operating profit of KRW 29.6 billion, which points to a large contribution from items outside operating profit.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 92.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 49.4 billion in 2025, so it should be read separately from attributable net income.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 27.1 billion in 2022 and KRW 52.4 billion in 2023 to KRW 2.26 billion in 2024, then KRW 15.2 billion in 2025. 2024 combined the largest attributable net income with the smallest operating cash flow, so earnings and cash moved apart.

The debt ratio jumped from 45.4% in 2022 to 122.5% in 2023, then held near 120% with 120.7% in 2024 and 119.8% in 2025. At end-2025, total equity was KRW 450.2 billion, attributable equity was KRW 449.2 billion and total liabilities were KRW 539.2 billion.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 159.3 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 192.1 billion in Q4 2025 (up 20.6% quarter on quarter), dipped to KRW 166.8 billion in Q1 2026 (down 13.2%), then reached KRW 230 billion in Q2 2026 (up 37.9%). Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 210.1 billion, down 8.7% quarter on quarter but up 31.9% year on year.

Attributable net income went from KRW 26.6 billion in Q3 2025 to a net loss of KRW 13.6 billion in Q4 2025, a swing from profit to loss. It returned to profit at KRW 29.8 billion in Q1 2026, then fell to KRW 25.2 billion in Q2 and KRW 10.3 billion in Q3, two consecutive quarters of decline.

Q4 2025 operating profit was only KRW 2.36 billion, while the attributable net loss reached KRW 13.6 billion, which suggests non-operating items weighed heavily. Attributable net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 51.6 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Holding-company shares often trade at a discount to the combined value of their affiliates, so affiliate industry conditions and changes in the holding structure both act as share-price drivers.

The 8-inch foundry affiliate is sensitive to demand for analog and power semiconductors, utilization and average selling prices, so where the chip cycle stands largely determines its results. Signals from the financial affiliates are already visible.

DB Financial Investment, a securities affiliate in the DB group, said in a disclosure on November 16, 2026 that lower stock market activity reduced brokerage and asset-management income. The same period saw client assets rise from the end of the previous year. Trading income is weak while the asset base is widening.

The IT services business is linked to group IT investment cycles and is expanding into service-type offerings. Insurance affiliates' earnings move with loss ratios and interest-rate conditions.

Among listed holding companies, SK Square is often compared with DB because both hold IT and chip affiliates, but DB's heavier financial exposure means its industry sensitivity differs. Position within the sector therefore needs to be read by affiliate cycle.

06

Outlook

Based on disclosed figures, revenue rose from KRW 166.8 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 230 billion in Q2 and reached KRW 210.1 billion in Q3, while profit has declined since its Q1 peak, so revenue and profit are moving in different directions.

On the holding-company side, DB has remained outside holding-company regulation since January 1, 2025.

A 2025 press report said DB received holding-company conversion notices from the Fair Trade Commission for four consecutive years but did not meet the requirement after the share price of its listed foundry affiliate fell.

Under the Fair Trade Act, a holding company is required to convert when total assets exceed KRW 500 billion and the value of its affiliate shares exceeds 50% of total assets. On the affiliate side, client assets at the group brokerage affiliate DB Financial Investment rose year on year.

The key checkpoint for the outlook is how the annual operating margin and operating cash flow turn out in the 2026 annual business report, the next periodic filing. The IT business has set out a direction toward SaaS and IaaS expansion, but scale and profitability must be confirmed in periodic filings.

Operating cash flow fell to KRW 2.26 billion in 2024, so whether 2026 cash generation keeps pace with earnings is another point to check. Q4 2026 results have not yet been disclosed, so whether the profit recovery holds will be checked through the full-year preliminary results and the annual business report.

Valuation for a holding company has to be read by considering both the value of its affiliate stakes and its book equity. The price-to-book ratio sits below 1x, so the discount to book equity and the discount to the sum of affiliate market values are both part of the discussion.

On earnings, attributable net income swings widely, so the same share price can look very different against profit depending on the year. Attributable net income fell from KRW 92.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 49.2 billion in 2025, and totals KRW 51.6 billion over the last four quarters.

The sum-of-the-parts approach, which adds listed affiliates at market value and then looks at the holding discount, changes with those affiliates' share prices. Comparing the current position with a past multiple band requires matching the earnings basis for the same period.

The dividend yield is low, so shareholder returns should be read together with treasury share holdings and affiliate dividend flows. Comparisons of multiples with other listed holding companies also depend on each company's affiliate mix.

08

Bull factors

Affiliate earnings flow into holding-level profit

DB's earnings combine affiliate results and stake values at the holding level. Better affiliate profits could flow through to holding-level income in the same direction. Operating profit rose 40.4% to KRW 41.6 billion in 2025 from KRW 29.6 billion in 2024, which points to recovering earnings capacity.

Revenue scale expanded in Q2 2026

Q2 2026 revenue reached KRW 230 billion, up 37.9% quarter on quarter, the largest quarterly revenue in the disclosed period. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 210.1 billion was up 31.9% year on year, so top-line growth continued.

Whether this turns into higher profit should be read alongside operating margin and attributable net income trends.

Group IT base and service-type expansion

The IT business is expanding into service-type offerings such as SaaS and IaaS by using group affiliates' infrastructure. Because group affiliates are major clients, a rise in group IT spending can feed directly into parent-level revenue.

Attributable net income of KRW 29.8 billion in Q1 2026 marked a return to profit, and growing cash generation from the core business is the next task.

09

Bear factors

Rapid slowdown in quarterly profit

Attributable net income fell from KRW 29.8 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 25.2 billion in Q2 (down 15.5% quarter on quarter) and KRW 10.3 billion in Q3 (down 59.2% quarter on quarter), two consecutive quarters of decline. Q3 was down 61.4% year on year, so revenue growth did not carry through to profit. Q4 2025 recorded an attributable net loss of KRW 13.6 billion, so quarterly earnings have been volatile.

Holding-company status and regulatory pressure

As of January 1, 2025, DB did not meet the holding-company requirements and was excluded from holding-company regulation. Under the Fair Trade Act, conversion is required when total assets exceed KRW 500 billion and affiliate share value exceeds 50% of total assets. A 2025 report said the requirement was not met after the share price of the listed foundry affiliate fell.

High leverage and thin operating margins

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 539.2 billion, above total equity of KRW 450.2 billion. The debt ratio has stayed near 120% since 2023. Operating margin was 5.0% in 2024 and 6.5% in 2025, staying near the mid-single digits. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 2.26 billion in 2024, showing a gap between earnings and cash generation.

Risk factors

Financial affiliate conditions

Financial earnings are sensitive to market volatility, which is a risk to holding-level profit. Insurance affiliates' results also depend on loss ratios and interest rates, so changes in these conditions can pass through directly given the weight of financial businesses.

Semiconductor affiliate cycle

The 8-inch foundry affiliate's results depend on utilization, average selling prices and demand for non-memory chips, and the holding company feels this indirectly through its stake. DB held 12.4% of this affiliate at end-2021, so how that stake has changed since is also a point to confirm.

Governance and shareholding changes

Following the January 1, 2025 exclusion from holding-company regulation, whether the Fair Trade Act requirements are met could become an issue again. The largest shareholder and related parties' stake rose from 43.81% on January 1, 2025 to 45.11% on October 1, 2025. Treasury shares of 5.04% are an item to consider alongside shareholder-return policy.

Overall view

DB's revenue rose from KRW 401.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 643.5 billion in 2025, while attributable net income peaked at KRW 92.3 billion in 2024 and fell to KRW 49.2 billion in 2025.

Revenue moved to KRW 166.8 billion, KRW 230 billion and KRW 210.1 billion across Q1 to Q3 2026, but attributable net income moved to KRW 29.8 billion, KRW 25.2 billion and KRW 10.3 billion, two consecutive quarters of decline.

The price-to-book ratio is below 1x, so discussion of a discount to affiliate value naturally follows. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 and full-year preliminary results (typically January to February 2027) and the 2026 annual business report (due by end-March 2027).
