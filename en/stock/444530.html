02

Business structure

Simplatform is an industrial AI company that collects and analyzes equipment and process data from manufacturing sites, builds AI models, and applies them to production processes.

Its main product is the in-house platform NUBISON AX, an integrated solution that covers data collection and analysis, AI model development, and operation and management.

Its core data-collection technology, ThingDriver, reads the communication protocols of existing equipment directly, so no additional sensors are needed.

Applications include equipment anomaly detection, quality prediction, and process optimization, and the company is extending references into healthcare and public-sector work beyond manufacturing.

Shinhan Investment Corp. said in a May 2026 report that the company operates 13 industrial AI packages on a single algorithm core.

The company's disclosures did not confirm a revenue breakdown by business segment; an external results aggregator shows the NUBISON AIoT line accounting for 100% of second-quarter 2026 revenue.

On the customer side, the platform has been extended to a DB HiTek semiconductor fab, and a proof of concept with Infineon on GaN MOCVD process anomaly detection and predictive maintenance is in progress.

Government projects include selection as a participant in a small and mid-sized manufacturing AI transition program run by the Human Resources Development Service of Korea under the Ministry of Employment and Labor, and in October 2026 the company disclosed participation in a Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy national project, the Chungbuk-Cheongju advanced semiconductor packaging AX demonstration complex.

Compared with large IT services firms such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, Simplatform is a smaller company specializing in manufacturing sites.

Revenue is recognized mainly on a project-completion basis for build-type contracts, so quarterly revenue depends heavily on whether specific projects are completed in a given quarter.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 4.39 billion in 2023, KRW 7.23 billion in 2024 (up 64.6% year on year), and KRW 10.4 billion in 2025 (up 44.0% year on year), rising two years in a row from 2024 to 2025. The largest year in 2023–2025 was 2025, and the smallest was 2023.

Operating losses were KRW 1.54 billion in 2023, KRW 143 million in 2024 (losses narrowed), and KRW 2.66 billion in 2025 (losses widened), a loss in each of the three years. The operating margin was -35.1% in 2023, -2.0% in 2024, and -25.6% in 2025, so the margin improved sharply in 2024 and then deteriorated in 2025.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.19 billion in 2023, KRW 8.02 billion in 2024, and KRW 2.53 billion in 2025, a loss in each of the three years, while the net loss including non-controlling interests followed the same figures.

Operating cash flow was negative in all three years: KRW -1.06 billion in 2023, KRW -1.99 billion in 2024, and KRW -761 million in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 had revenue of KRW 1.87 billion and an operating loss of KRW 3.06 billion; the fourth quarter of 2025 then posted revenue of KRW 7.52 billion (up 302.4% from the prior quarter) and an operating profit of KRW 2.51 billion, a turn to profit, with net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.56 billion.

Revenue then fell to KRW 472 million in the first quarter of 2026 (down 93.7% from the prior quarter), and the operating loss of KRW 2.37 billion marked a return to losses.

In the second quarter of 2026 revenue was KRW 2.25 billion with an operating loss of KRW 1.44 billion, and in the third quarter revenue was KRW 2.46 billion with an operating loss of KRW 1.73 billion; revenue rose for two consecutive quarters and losses narrowed year on year.

The sum of net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters was KRW 2.82 billion. According to news reports dated November 16, 2026, the company attributed the third-quarter revenue to a higher number of completed projects and lower operating costs.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic demand for industrial AI is supported by government budgets. In a May 2026 report, Shinhan Investment Corp. noted that the 2026 Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy budget for manufacturing and industrial AX doubled from the prior year to KRW 1.1 trillion.

The same report expected adoption led by mid-sized and small manufacturers to accelerate as AI factory flagship projects and industrial-complex AX conversion programs expand.

Globally, the same brokerage said the industrial AI market is growing faster than the AI software market as a whole, driven by manufacturing automation and rising physical AI demand.

In semiconductors, Shinhan said wafer defect analysis and CVD equipment predictive maintenance solutions supplied to a domestic chipmaker delivered a 1% yield improvement, which it converted into an annual profit benefit of about KRW 16 billion.

That is the brokerage's own estimate, not a figure from company disclosures. Against large IT services firms with far larger revenue and customer bases, Simplatform's single contracts represent a much larger share of its revenue.

Its differentiation rests on handling everything from manufacturing equipment data collection to AI model operation on one in-house platform. Whether proof-of-concept projects convert into full contracts is the key factor for expansion.

06

Outlook

The nearest formal checkpoint is the 2026 annual report. Disclosed results through the third quarter show operating losses continuing each quarter, so whether the full year reaches an operating profit depends on the size of fourth-quarter 2026 revenue.

In a May 28, 2026 report, Shinhan Investment Corp. projected 2026 revenue of KRW 14.8 billion and an operating profit of KRW 500 million, and said a turn to profit was possible, conditional on proof-of-concept projects converting into full contracts and manufacturing automation investment continuing.

The Chungbuk-Cheongju semiconductor packaging AX demonstration complex project runs for 39 months from October 1, 2026 through December 31, 2029, with the first contract year ending December 31, 2026 and the second contract year covering 2027.

The disclosed total project budget is KRW 30.1 billion, of which Simplatform's government contribution is about KRW 1.93 billion. The Infineon proof of concept is described as transitioning into a long-term technical support structure through April 2028 after the PoC is completed.

In November 2026 the company said it is pursuing entry into Malaysia's semiconductor cluster, and it said it shipped servers carrying its AX platform for export, which it plans to treat as the start of overseas revenue.

The 2026 designation as an excellent specialized research business, announced by the Ministry of Science and ICT on September 2, 2026, is a technology credential. How these projects and overseas efforts appear in quarterly revenue will be visible in the 2027 quarterly results.

Bull factors

Government AX Budget and National Project Participation

Shinhan Investment Corp. said the 2026 Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy budget for manufacturing and industrial AX doubled from the prior year to KRW 1.1 trillion. The company was selected as an implementer of the small and mid-sized manufacturing AI transition program.

The Chungbuk-Cheongju semiconductor packaging project, disclosed in October 2026, runs for 39 months through December 31, 2029.

Profit in Q4 2025 and Revenue Growth in Q3 2026

Revenue in 2025 rose 44.0% year on year. The fourth quarter of 2025 turned to an operating profit of KRW 2.51 billion, the only operating profit among the last five disclosed quarters. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 2.46 billion was up 31.3% year on year, and the operating loss narrowed from a year earlier.

Efforts to Expand Global Semiconductor References

A proof of concept with Infineon on GaN MOCVD process anomaly detection and predictive maintenance is in progress, and it is described as transitioning into a long-term technical support structure through April 2028. Shinhan presented its own estimate of the yield-improvement benefit at a domestic chipmaker customer. The company said it is pursuing entry into Malaysia's semiconductor cluster.

09

Bear factors

Large Swings in Quarterly Revenue

After KRW 7.52 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, revenue fell to KRW 472 million in the first quarter of 2026, down 93.7% from the prior quarter. Revenue is recognized on project completion, so it tends to cluster in particular quarters. If new projects start or finish later than planned, quarterly results could fall short of expectations.

Three Years of Operating Losses and Continued Losses in 2026

The 2025 operating loss was KRW 2.66 billion, with an operating margin of -25.6%. Operating losses continued through the first three quarters of 2026, at KRW 2.37 billion, KRW 1.44 billion and KRW 1.73 billion.

The sum of net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters was KRW 2.82 billion, so the durability of the profit turn has not yet been confirmed.

Uncertainty over Conversion of PoCs into Full Contracts

The company's outlook depends on how quickly many proof-of-concept projects convert into full contracts. Shinhan's profit-turn projection was also conditional on PoCs converting. Even with a larger government budget, revenue recognition would be delayed if individual projects do not move to full contracts.

10

Risk factors

Earnings Volatility and Seasonality

Under a project-based revenue structure, a single quarter can drive a large share of annual results. The drop from KRW 7.52 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 472 million in the first quarter of 2026 illustrates this. The trend is hard to judge from quarterly figures alone.

Dependence on Policy Budgets and Project Schedules

The disclosure states that the project period and budget may change through government policy and agreement between the parties. It remains to be confirmed whether the expanded 2026 AX budget translates into actual orders. If project schedules slip, the timing of revenue recognition could also move later.

Small Market Capitalization and Potential Share Sales

With a market capitalization of about KRW 19.9 billion, the company is small even within KOSDAQ. The voluntary lock-up of co-CEOs Lim Dae-geun and Kang Tae-shin is scheduled to expire in March 2028, so the possibility of share sales after that date needs to be monitored. Small-cap stocks can be sensitive to trading swings.

12

Overall view

Simplatform runs a manufacturing-data-based business centered on its industrial AI platform NUBISON AX, and revenue rose for two straight years to KRW 10.4 billion in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 14.8 billion, and the debt ratio improved from -458.6% in 2023 to 32.0% in 2025.

On the other side, operating losses persisted for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and they have continued through the first three quarters of 2026.

The fourth-quarter 2025 profit turn and the 31.3% year-on-year revenue growth in the third quarter of 2026 are positive signs, but the large quarterly swings make the trend difficult to judge.

On policy, the expanded 2026 AX budget and national project participation form the demand base, but revenue recognition depends on proof-of-concept projects converting into full contracts. Shinhan Investment Corp. presented the possibility of a 2026 profit turn, but that projection assumed PoC conversion.

The checkpoints are the operating loss in the 2026 annual report, whether quarterly revenue recovers in the first quarter of 2027, and the expiry of the lock-up in March 2028.
