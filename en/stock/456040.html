Chip Materials Support Profit; Net Income Mixed

OCI Company posted operating profit in each quarter from Q1 2026 through Q3 2026, but swung to a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 6.9 billion in Q3 2026, after full-year 2025 operating profit had fallen to KRW 440 million.

02

Business structure

OCI Company makes semiconductor materials alongside carbon chemical products, and its operations are reported in two divisions: Basic Chemicals and Carbon Chemicals.

In Q3 2026, according to company-announced results reported in the press, the Basic Chemicals division (including semiconductor materials) posted revenue of KRW 212.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 9.7 billion.

In the same quarter, the Carbon Chemicals division recorded revenue of KRW 397.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 29 billion, making it the larger contributor.

Within Basic Chemicals, lower chlor-alkali selling prices reduced basic materials results, while semiconductor materials grew in both revenue and operating profit.

The flagship product is high-purity polysilicon for semiconductors, and the line also includes hydrogen peroxide, phosphoric acid and advanced etchant development.

A February 2026 press report said the company is the only domestic supplier of semiconductor polysilicon and named Samsung Electronics and SK hynix as major customers. In Carbon Chemicals, higher carbon black selling prices and greater pitch volumes supported results.

The company is reshaping its portfolio toward semiconductors and higher-value materials, and it has discontinued its loss-making TDI business. Solar-grade polysilicon is reported to be produced by OCI TerraSus, a Malaysian subsidiary of holding company OCI Holdings, so it sits outside this division structure.

The company points to its position as the sole domestic supplier in two semiconductor materials categories as its main competitive edge.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 1.29 trillion in 2023 to KRW 2.22 trillion in 2024, up 71.9% year on year, and then fell to KRW 2.01 trillion in 2025, down 9.3%. Among 2023 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year for revenue and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 57.5 billion in 2023 to KRW 110.5 billion in 2024, up 92.3%, before dropping to KRW 440 million in 2025, down 99.6%. Operating margin was 4.5% in 2023, 5.0% in 2024 and 0.0% in 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net income reached KRW 96 billion in 2024, up 84.0%, but turned into a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 62.6 billion in 2025. Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 68.5 billion in 2025, which is reported separately from the controlling-shareholder figure.

The debt-to-equity ratio was 102.4% in 2023 and 83.9% in 2024, then rose to 95.4% in 2025, while operating cash flow fell from KRW 163.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 56.1 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the operating loss of KRW 10.3 billion in Q3 2025 marked the low point, and Q4 2025 returned to operating profit of KRW 2.81 billion.

Operating profit recovered to KRW 27.8 billion in Q1 2026, up 889.1% from the prior quarter, and KRW 43.3 billion in Q2 2026, up 55.5%, before falling to KRW 30.3 billion in Q3 2026, down 30.0%. The company attributed the Q3 decline to a weaker won and narrower BTX and TDI product spreads.

Revenue rose sequentially in each of the three quarters from Q1 2026 to Q3 2026, reaching KRW 599.1 billion in Q3 2026, up 25.8% year on year. Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 24.8 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 30 billion in Q2 2026, then Q3 2026 posted a KRW 6.9 billion loss.

Because the operating profit stayed positive while controlling-shareholder results turned negative, non-operating items and income tax lines are the points to watch in the next periodic report.

05

Industry analysis

Semiconductor materials are tied to the chip upcycle driven by surging AI demand. The company expects growth in key semiconductor materials, including semiconductor-grade polysilicon, to continue into Q4 2026 as downstream chip demand rises.

In Carbon Chemicals, higher carbon black selling prices reflecting oil price increases and greater pitch volumes supported results. Conversely, narrower spreads on BTX and TDI products weighed on operating profit. In basic materials, lower chlor-alkali selling prices reduced earnings.

Earnings sensitivity to commodity chemical spreads is common to peers such as LG Chem, Lotte Chemical and Kumho Petro Chemical. In semiconductor process materials, the narrow domestic supply base is cited as an advantage, but it also means earnings are linked to the semiconductor investment cycle.

Section 232 of the U.S. Trade Expansion Act is described as targeting Chinese solar-grade polysilicon, so its policy exposure does not sit on the same footing as the company's semiconductor business.

The industry picture for this company therefore reads as two separate drivers: chip investment flows on one side and carbon and basic chemical spreads on the other. Whether the chip boom sustains is the largest external variable for the materials business.

06

Outlook

For Q4 2026, the company expects growth in its semiconductor materials to continue while Carbon Chemicals results decline slightly because of higher costs tied to scheduled maintenance.

Maintenance is planned at both basic chemicals and carbon chemicals facilities, so Q4 revenue and operating profit are expected to fall slightly from Q3. The company is pursuing higher utilization of semiconductor-grade hydrogen peroxide, expanded phosphoric acid capacity and development of higher-value etchants.

A February 2026 press report said the company plans to grow semiconductor-grade polysilicon revenue from KRW 219 billion in 2022 to roughly KRW 800 billion in 2027. The wafer reclamation business targets facility completion in 2027 and commercialization in 2028.

The TDI business was discontinued after persistent losses, and the portfolio reshaping aims to shrink low-return businesses in favor of semiconductor and higher-value materials.

Whether revenue growth from Q1 2026 through Q3 2026 carries into Q4 depends on maintenance volumes and the pace of semiconductor materials sales. Reaching the 2027 plan will require new capacity to start on schedule alongside customer contracts of matching size.

The 2026 annual report, the next periodic filing, will confirm full-year and fourth-quarter figures along with segment profit.

Risk factors

Policy and trade risk

Detailed guidance under U.S. Section 232 is expected in early December 2026, and reports say the measures take effect on December 4. The measures cover polysilicon and derivative products broadly, and customers are reported to be waiting for implementation details before buying. Whether they directly affect the company's semiconductor polysilicon sales will depend on the detailed rules.

Feedstock, spreads and currency

Spreads on BTX, TDI and carbon black move with oil prices and exchange rates, so operating profit can swing sharply from quarter to quarter. In Q3 2026, a stronger won and narrower spreads worked together. Lower chlor-alkali selling prices are another drag on basic materials results. Higher oil prices support carbon black pricing but can add pressure on raw material costs.

Leverage and cash flow

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 95.4% at the end of 2025 from 83.9% in 2024. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 163.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 56.1 billion in 2025. If capital spending and borrowing rise together against total equity of KRW 1.16 trillion, financial burden could increase. The pace at which cash generation recovers after a loss-making year will matter.

Overall view

OCI Company's earnings have moved past a low point, with full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 440 million, and operating profit has stayed positive in each quarter from Q1 2026 through Q3 2026.

Revenue reached KRW 599.1 billion in Q3 2026 after three consecutive quarterly increases, and the semiconductor materials line is reported as the main driver of both revenue and operating profit.

However, Q3 2026 brought a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 6.9 billion, and full-year 2025 recorded a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 62.6 billion and a net loss of KRW 68.5 billion including non-controlling interests.

For Q4 2026, the company expects Carbon Chemicals-led results to decline slightly because of scheduled maintenance. On the strength side, semiconductor materials growth, the wafer reclamation plan and a price-to-book ratio well below 1x are cited.

On the weakness side, spread volatility, the return to a net loss and a higher debt-to-equity ratio are noted. These factors carry similar weight, and the variables that will decide the direction of quarterly results are the detailed U.S. Section 232 guidance and carbon and basic chemical spreads.

The next checkpoints are preliminary Q4 2026 results, confirmed figures in the 2026 annual report and the 2027 facility completion schedule. This report is for information only and does not include a buy or sell opinion or a target price.
