02

Business structure

Yura Tech produces automotive ignition parts and has been listed on KOSDAQ since January 11, 2001. Its main customers are domestic automakers including Hyundai Motor and KIA.

The company also supplies electric-vehicle-related components, such as PCB blocks, an inlet temperature sensor for EV charging terminals, and an emergency release cable, to a related affiliate in the same group.

Related-party sales accounted for 18.4% of separate-basis revenue in 2025, and the affiliate remains a large share of sales in the first half of 2026. A past press report noted that most of the company's core sales come from parts used only in internal-combustion vehicles. Ownership is concentrated in the founding family, and management is led by the family members.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 190.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 209.7 billion in 2023, a 10.3% increase, making 2023 the largest year of the 2022-2025 period. Revenue then fell 4.0% to KRW 201.4 billion in 2024 and 2.0% to KRW 197.3 billion in 2025, two consecutive years of decline.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 439 million in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 6.03 billion in 2023, then KRW 10.5 billion in 2024, before dropping to KRW 1.81 billion in 2025, down 82.7%. The company attributed the 2025 decline to lower sales and higher costs from currency movements.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 866 million in 2022, KRW 17.3 billion in 2023, KRW 13.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 11 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests followed the same path in those years.

The operating margin was -0.2% in 2022, 2.9% in 2023, 5.2% in 2024 and 0.9% in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 16.4 billion in 2025, up from KRW 13.2 billion in 2023.

Quarterly results show an operating loss of KRW 180 million in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 523 million in the fourth quarter, followed by an operating loss of KRW 1.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026. The second quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 1.11 billion on revenue of KRW 54.2 billion.

The third quarter of 2026 posted an operating loss of KRW 648 million on revenue of KRW 49 billion, down 2.5% year on year. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 5.27 billion over the last four quarters.

Industry analysis

Demand for ignition parts is tied to internal-combustion vehicle production and the installed base. A 2025 third-quarter summary attributed the profit decline to lower ignition-parts sales and higher cost burdens, along with weak domestic demand.

The same summary expected export growth from competitive quality and pricing of major automaker models. Press coverage in 2025 noted that demand for EV-related parts could grow as the electric vehicle market expands.

Past reporting also highlighted that the core sales mix is concentrated in internal-combustion parts, so the pace of electrification directly affects the revenue structure. Compared with broader auto-parts makers such as Hyundai Mobis and HL Mando, Yura Tech's product scope is narrower and focused on ignition systems.

Its domestic automaker customer base provides a stable sales foundation, but volumes depend on customers' model mix and production plans. Whether the 2026 swing between profit and loss reflects industry conditions or cost factors should be checked against future filings.
