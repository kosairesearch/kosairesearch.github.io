02

Business structure

Seoul Pharma was founded in 1985, manages pharmaceutical manufacturing and sales as its core business, and listed on KOSDAQ in 2000. The company is known as a specialist in orally disintegrating film (ODF) formulations.

In 2024, non-tablet products made up 74.32% of sales, followed by hard capsules at 13.42%, liquid formulations at 5.59%, and soft capsules at 3.19%. Non-tablet merchandise accounted for 2.79%, ointments and creams for 1.28%, and other sales for 0.76% in the same year.

Domestic sales were 97.7% of the 2024 total, with exports at 2.3%, so the business is centered on Korea. Media reports said seven key OTC products have recorded modest annual sales of roughly KRW 300 million to KRW 400 million over the past five years, limiting their contribution to total revenue.

The head office and production base is in Osong, Cheongju, Chungbuk. Compared with large biopharma names, the portfolio is built around formulation-based products rather than biologics. Segment-level revenue and product-level prescription trends are shown only in the 2024 composition.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue rose from KRW 50.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 52.5 billion in 2023 (up 4.8% year on year) and KRW 53.6 billion in 2024 (up 2.1%), then fell to KRW 52.1 billion in 2025 (down 2.7%). Over 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.74 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.21 billion in 2023 (down 30.2%) and KRW 1.53 billion in 2024 (up 26.0%), and then turned into an operating loss of KRW 1.28 billion in 2025, a swing into the red. The operating margin was 3.5% in 2022, 2.3% in 2023, and 2.9% in 2024, and then -2.5% in 2025.

The company attributed the main cause of the 2025 swing to a temporary rise in management rationalization costs tied to a business restructuring.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 855 million in 2022 to KRW 3.52 billion in 2023 (up 311.3%), the peak, and then fell to KRW 1.7 billion in 2024 (down 51.7%) and KRW 754 million in 2025 (down 55.6%), declining for two consecutive years.

In 2023, operating profit fell while controlling-shareholder net income rose sharply, which suggests that non-operating items had a large effect. Net income in this period was disclosed at the same amounts as controlling-shareholder net income, so no gap between the two appears.

Quarterly revenue has declined for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, moving from KRW 13.6 billion to KRW 12.1 billion, KRW 11.8 billion, and KRW 10.5 billion, and Q3 2026 was down 22.9% year on year.

Operating losses ran from KRW 33.6 million in Q3 2025 to KRW 217 million in Q4 2025, KRW 186 million in Q1 2026, KRW 343 million in Q2 2026, and KRW 973 million in Q3 2026, five consecutive quarters in the red, with the Q3 2026 loss the largest of the period.

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 2.62 billion in Q3 2025, then turned to a net loss of KRW 376 million in Q4 2025 and continued in the red through Q3 2026, with a net loss of KRW 1.32 billion in that quarter, four consecutive quarters of losses totaling KRW 2.21 billion.

The 2024 cost of sales ratio was 38.5% and the SG&A ratio was 58.6%, and a securities information service attributed weaker profitability in the first half of 2025 to higher cost burdens.
