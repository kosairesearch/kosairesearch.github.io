02

Business structure

F&F's flagship brand is MLB, and Discovery forms the second pillar of the business. MLB's domestic sales are reported across adult, kids and duty-free channels.

As foreign tourist numbers rose, MLB flagship stores in major districts such as Garosu-gil, Hannam, Myeongdong and Seongsu were reported to have rebounded sharply. Daishin Securities projected in an August 2026 report that domestic non-duty-free channel sales would grow 16.9%.

Overseas, China and Hong Kong are the core markets, and China MLB is described as a business that has shown both growth and profitability. SK Securities cited the lower sales share of the China business, which carries higher profitability, as the main reason for the third-quarter margin decline.

Discovery sales fell from KRW 490 billion in 2022 to KRW 347 billion in 2025, according to a Korean securities news report. Since the average operating margin of the apparel industry is cited at 5 to 10 percent, the margin of the MLB-centered structure is relatively high.

The company committed about KRW 553.7 billion to a TaylorMade-related fund, and if the acquisition goes through, the business could shift from a licensing-based structure to one centered on owned IP, according to securities market analysis. Annual revenue for 2025 was KRW 1.93 trillion and operating profit was KRW 468.6 billion.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 1.81 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.98 trillion in 2023, up 9.4 percent, fell 4.2 percent to KRW 1.9 trillion in 2024, and recovered 2.0 percent to KRW 1.93 trillion in 2025. 2023 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest among 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit fell 18.3 percent from KRW 551.8 billion in 2023 to KRW 450.7 billion in 2024, then rose 4.0 percent to KRW 468.6 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 29.0 percent in 2022 to 27.9 percent in 2023, 23.8 percent in 2024 and 24.2 percent in 2025.

The decline in Discovery sales since 2022 is cited as a factor pressing on margins.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 441.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 425.1 billion in 2023 (down 3.8 percent) and KRW 360.4 billion in 2024 (down 15.2 percent), then recovered to KRW 398.5 billion in 2025 (up 10.6 percent).

Net income including non-controlling interests for 2025 was KRW 400.7 billion, a different figure. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 1.88 trillion and total liabilities were KRW 772.8 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 66.5 percent in 2022 to 41.1 percent in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit rose from KRW 128 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 132.9 billion in the fourth quarter and KRW 153.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

Second-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 86.5 billion, down 43.6 percent from the prior quarter, and Daishin Securities attributed this to slower China shipments to keep inventory at healthy levels.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 108.4 billion, up 25.4 percent from the prior quarter but down 15.3 percent year on year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 197.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 73 billion in the second quarter and KRW 65.9 billion in the third, declining for two consecutive quarters.

In the first quarter, this net income exceeded operating profit, which suggests non-operating items may have contributed meaningfully. Third-quarter reported figures fell short of the SK Securities estimates of KRW 484.6 billion in revenue and KRW 127 billion in operating profit.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totals KRW 491 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic apparel market is in a setting where brands such as MLB are growing despite weak consumption. Daishin Securities analyzed that MLB's domestic regular-channel sales grew 15 percent in the first quarter of 2026. A domestic consumption recovery tied to a strong stock market is also cited as a growth driver.

The rise in foreign tourists visiting Korea, which lifted sales in districts such as Myeongdong and Seongsu, is cited as a differentiator against the broader industry. China's retail apparel market is viewed as recovering gradually.

SK Securities expected China's inventory adjustment to wrap up in the third quarter of 2026 and shipments to normalize from the fourth quarter. Exchange rates are also a variable.

SK Securities reflected the stronger won in the third quarter as a factor reducing won-converted China sales, while another analysis saw a stronger renminbi as favorable for won-based results. In the same report, SK Securities said Discovery's domestic business was weaker than expected.

The average operating margin of the apparel industry is cited at 5 to 10 percent, and F&F maintains a high-margin structure centered on MLB. Because brand mix and channel composition differ from peer listed companies, reading channel structure differences is more appropriate than direct numerical comparison.

The cycle position depends on whether the China inventory adjustment has ended and whether inbound demand in Korea continues.

06

Outlook

Reports said the company guided 4 percent revenue growth for 2026 and 6 percent for the first quarter. First-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 560.9 billion rose 10.9 percent year on year, while third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 452.1 billion fell 4.7 percent from a year earlier.

SK Securities estimated 2026 annual revenue of KRW 2.03 trillion and operating profit of KRW 506 billion in an October 2026 report. Reaching that annual estimate would require fourth-quarter operating profit to rise considerably above the third quarter.

Whether China shipments return to normal levels in the fourth quarter is the checkpoint that will shape the annual trend. The TaylorMade-related investment is reported to include a fund commitment of about KRW 553.7 billion and a KRW 4 trillion underwriting letter for acquisition financing.

The company is reportedly at a stage where it must decide whether to exercise a preemptive purchase right, though a direct acquisition would require several trillion won of funds, according to analysis.

If the acquisition is completed, the company could move away from a licensing-based structure and grow an IP-centered business, which is viewed as a key variable for the medium-term strategy. Reports said the company continues to expand its shareholder return policy. The 2026 annual report will show Discovery's full-year results and the full-year operating margin.

08

Bull factors

Domestic MLB growth and inbound demand

Daishin Securities analyzed that MLB's domestic regular-channel sales grew 15 percent in the first quarter of 2026, and rising foreign tourist numbers lifted sales at flagship stores in major districts.

Domestic consumption recovery and inbound demand are cited as bullish factors that are broadening MLB's earnings base.

Expectation of China shipment normalization

SK Securities said in an October 2026 report that it expected China's inventory adjustment to end in the third quarter and shipments to normalize from the fourth quarter. The same report said that once China inventory returns to normal levels, the focus would shift to the pace of normalization in the China business.

Because the China business carries relatively high profitability, a shipment recovery could lead to a margin recovery.

Shareholder returns and the TaylorMade option

Reports said an expanding shareholder return policy is confirmed. The company committed about KRW 553.7 billion to a TaylorMade-related fund and maintains a KRW 4 trillion underwriting letter for acquisition financing.

Securities market analysis says a completed acquisition could move the business from a licensing-based model to one centered on owned IP.

09

Bear factors

Prolonged weakness at Discovery

Coverage framed the question of how long Discovery's weakness will last, and DB Financial Investment lowered its target price in August 2026. SK Securities also said Discovery's domestic business was weaker than expected in the third quarter of 2026. Until a Discovery recovery is confirmed, the contribution from businesses outside MLB remains limited.

Earnings declines since the second quarter

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 73 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 65.9 billion in the third, a two-quarter decline. Third-quarter operating profit of KRW 108.4 billion fell 15.3 percent year on year.

Third-quarter reported figures were below both the revenue and operating profit estimates of SK Securities. The downward flow of estimates is cited as a bearish factor.

China inventory adjustment and exchange rates

China shipments were slowed in the second quarter to keep inventory healthy, and the stronger won in the third quarter reduced won-converted sales. SK Securities cut its third-quarter estimates to reflect the China inventory adjustment and the stronger won.

Because a lower China sales share also reduces profitability, whether shipments normalize in the fourth quarter is key to a earnings recovery.

10

Risk factors

Reliance on domestic districts and duty-free

Domestic MLB growth is concentrated in flagship stores in major districts such as Garosu-gil, Hannam, Myeongdong and Seongsu, as well as in the duty-free channel. Because these sales move with the inflow of foreign tourists, a drop in visits or a shift in foot traffic across districts could slow domestic sales growth.

If domestic consumption recovery fails to offset this, growth in the domestic channel may be hard to sustain at its current level.

Non-operating items and net income swings

In the first quarter of 2026, net income attributable to controlling shareholders exceeded operating profit, suggesting that non-operating items made a substantial contribution. Such items are not directly tied to business performance, so it is difficult to predict whether they will recur in later quarters.

If non-operating items shrink, net income attributable to controlling shareholders could fall even if operating profit holds up. The earnings reading based on the combined total of the last four quarters could also change depending on whether these items persist.

Financial burden of the TaylorMade investment

The company committed about KRW 553.7 billion to a TaylorMade-related fund and maintains a KRW 4 trillion underwriting letter for acquisition financing. Analysis indicates a direct acquisition would require several trillion won, so deal terms and funding methods could change the financial structure. The timing and scale of how the investment will be reflected in earnings remain uncertain.

Overall view

F&F's revenue has moved between roughly KRW 1.8 trillion and KRW 1.9 trillion since 2022, and in 2025 operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders recovered, turning the earnings direction.

However, in the second and third quarters of 2026, China MLB shipment adjustments and weaker Discovery sales coincided, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined for two consecutive quarters.

The bullish case cites domestic MLB inbound demand, normalization of China shipments and an expanding shareholder return policy. The bearish case cites a prolonged Discovery slump, third-quarter reported figures falling below broker estimates, and exchange rate swings.

Broker views are also mixed: DB Financial Investment lowered its target in August 2026, while SK Securities kept its buy rating and KRW 94,000 target in October 2026.

The TaylorMade investment is discussed both as a growth option and a financial burden, and whether and on what terms the deal proceeds is a variable that could change the medium-term business structure.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 results, whether China shipments normalize, and Discovery's results in the 2026 annual report.
