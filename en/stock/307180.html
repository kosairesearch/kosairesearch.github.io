02

Business structure

IL builds lighting and automotive lamp components on its LED silicone lens technology.

AlphaSquare's breakdown of sales shows B2B lighting, systems and street lighting at 41.6 percent, B2G public-sector business at 29.4 percent, and B2C products such as hair care and hi-pass terminals at 29.0 percent, though the page does not state the reference period.

The core is automotive electronics parts and intelligent transport systems (ITS). According to WiseReport, the 2024 acquisition of IL Mobility and the investment in IL Celion brought the lamp process under vertical integration.

On top of this base, the company is building the ILBOT humanoid robot and a physical AI operations data platform as growth businesses. Robot-related sales of about KRW 2.7 billion first appeared in the third-quarter 2026 results.

The company plans to extend robot supply beyond manufacturing into logistics, distribution and entertainment. On the construction side, IL said it signed a unit-price lighting contract worth about KRW 3.6 billion with POSCO E&C to support cash generation in its existing business.

Robotics peers in the same machinery and equipment sector include Rainbow Robotics, Robotis and Doosan Robotics, though business models differ widely and direct comparisons are limited.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue fell from KRW 49.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 39.1 billion in 2023 (down 20.5 percent year on year), then rose to KRW 83.5 billion in 2024 (up 113.7 percent) and KRW 102.9 billion in 2025 (up 23.2 percent).

Revenue rose for two consecutive years, 2024 and 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period. Operating loss was KRW 744 million in 2022 and widened to KRW 16.7 billion in 2023, a continued deficit with a larger loss.

The company returned to operating profit of KRW 5.04 billion in 2024, a swing into the black, but posted an operating loss of KRW 8.5 billion in 2025, a turn back into deficit.

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 1.94 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 18.6 billion in 2023, net income of KRW 432 million in 2024 and a net loss of KRW 11.1 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, a different measure, was KRW 2.09 billion, a net loss of KRW 18.1 billion, KRW 899 million and a net loss of KRW 10.8 billion over the same years. The operating margin was -1.5 percent in 2022, -42.8 percent in 2023, 6.0 percent in 2024 and -8.3 percent in 2025.

For the 2025 deficit, WiseReport attributed it to acquisition-led vertical integration that lifted revenue but raised cost burdens and one-off expenses. Dealsite reported an accounting-industry view that a Big Four auditor change in its first year pulled large charges into the period, a so-called big bath.

Quarterly, revenue was KRW 19.3 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, down 33.8 percent from the prior quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 9.31 billion. In the first quarter of 2026 revenue recovered to KRW 25.2 billion and operating profit was KRW 154 million, a turn to profit.

Second-quarter revenue was KRW 24.9 billion with operating profit of KRW 2.76 billion, up 1,688.9 percent from the prior quarter. Third-quarter revenue was KRW 33.4 billion, up 34.2 percent quarter on quarter and 14.4 percent year on year, with operating profit of KRW 2.14 billion, up 83.0 percent year on year.

Controlling-shareholder net loss was KRW 592 million in the first quarter of 2026, and the combined controlling-shareholder net loss for the last four quarters was KRW 10.9 billion.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 23.2 billion, of which KRW 19.8 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 131.5 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio moved from 213.0 percent in 2022 to 449.7 percent in 2023, 677.9 percent in 2024 and 567.1 percent in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 1.56 billion in 2022, then negative at KRW 5.21 billion in 2023, KRW 3.98 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.12 billion in 2025.

Industry analysis

The physical AI robot business entered actual supply and revenue generation in 2026. The company said it is widening humanoid robot supply beyond manufacturing into logistics, distribution and entertainment.

Hana Securities reported that across roughly 3,300 hours of operation from February to July 2026, key metrics such as average work time, work variance and process waiting time improved 30 to 97 percent versus prior levels.

Those results come from specific operating sites and may not represent the performance of every customer. The company said its existing automotive electronics and ITS businesses continue to support earnings through better profitability.

The lamp business is noted for vertical integration spanning from automotive lamp PCBs to silicone lenses and plastic cover injection molding. Robotics peers in the same machinery and equipment sector include Rainbow Robotics, Robotis and Doosan Robotics.

Independent indicators of where the robot demand cycle stands are still limited, so quarterly results are the main observable gauge for now. The company has outlined a strategy aimed at high-function robot markets such as humanoids, drones and aerospace, built on its solid-state battery technology.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in quarterly revenue and operating profit

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 33.4 billion rose 14.4 percent year on year, which Seoul Economic Daily and Financial News reported as the largest quarterly revenue since the company's founding. Operating profit reached KRW 2.14 billion, up 83.0 percent year on year.

Operating profit stayed positive in all three quarters of 2026. The reversal in profit direction after the fourth-quarter 2025 operating loss is cited as a bullish factor.

First booking of humanoid revenue

Financial News reported that robot-related sales of about KRW 2.7 billion, including the ILBOT humanoid, were included in third-quarter results. Reports differ on whether the figure refers to the quarter or the year to date, but both agree that new-business revenue has started to show up.

Hana Securities analyzed that key operating metrics improved 30 to 97 percent versus prior levels over February to July 2026. Accumulating operating data could serve as evidence of execution in the robot business.

Better profitability and orders in existing businesses

The company said better profitability in its automotive electronics and ITS businesses lifted year-to-date results through the third quarter. In July 2026 it signed a unit-price lighting contract worth about KRW 3.6 billion with POSCO E&C, running through May 2027.

The company's plan is for cash generated by the existing business to fund new-business investment. Whether this translates into actual cash flow can be checked in upcoming periodic reports.

09

Bear factors

Annual losses and a sharp fourth-quarter drop

The 2025 annual operating loss was KRW 8.5 billion and the controlling-shareholder net loss was KRW 11.1 billion, a turn into deficit. The fourth-quarter operating loss of KRW 9.31 billion accounted for a large part of that.

Combined controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four quarters were KRW 10.9 billion, so the period of profit-based stability remains short. Operating losses widened in 2023 and the company returned to deficit in 2025, which makes earnings continuity a weakness.

High leverage and cash flow strain

The debt-to-equity ratio was 567.1 percent at the end of 2025, lower than 677.9 percent in 2024 but still high. Total liabilities of KRW 131.5 billion far exceed total equity of KRW 23.2 billion.

Operating cash flow was negative in each year, at KRW -5.21 billion in 2023, KRW -3.98 billion in 2024 and KRW -1.12 billion in 2025. Continued losses could raise reliance on external funding, which is cited as a bearish factor.

Scale of new businesses and accounting uncertainty

Robot-related sales of about KRW 2.7 billion remain small relative to third-quarter revenue of KRW 33.4 billion, and humanoid profitability has not yet been proven in reported results. Dealsite framed the large 2025 loss, booked in the first year after an auditor change, as a big bath.

The same report pointed to listing-maintenance risk and possible trading suspension tied to the size of the pre-tax loss. Unresolved accounting questions add uncertainty to new-business investment.

Risk factors

Financial structure

The gap between total liabilities of KRW 131.5 billion and total equity of KRW 23.2 billion could make results sensitive to interest costs and borrowing terms. Operating cash flow remained negative through 2025, which raises the need for working-capital funding.

Although the debt ratio has fallen, it is still high, and any further fundraising could bring dilution or higher financing costs. Whether balance-sheet repair keeps pace with earnings recovery is the main point to watch.

Accounting and listing maintenance

Dealsite reported in March 2026 that the large 2025 loss from the first year under a new auditor raised listing-maintenance and trading-suspension concerns. That report predates later audit procedures and any asset revaluation outcomes.

The audit opinion and any asset revaluation results can be checked in the 2026 annual report. Changes in accounting judgments could affect market conditions for the stock.

Execution in new businesses

Humanoid robot revenue first appeared in third-quarter 2026 results, and expanding customers and accumulating operating data are still at an early stage. As the robot business widens into manufacturing, logistics and entertainment, customer-specific requirements and cost burdens could grow.

The business also still relies on the profitability of automotive electronics and ITS. Whether new-business revenue keeps appearing each quarter is the measure of execution risk.

Overall view

IL's operating profit was in the black in each of the first three quarters of 2026, and third-quarter revenue of KRW 33.4 billion was reported as the largest quarterly figure since the company's founding.

However, the 2025 annual operating loss of KRW 8.5 billion, controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 11.1 billion and combined four-quarter controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 10.9 billion show that the track record of profitability is still short.

A debt-to-equity ratio of 567.1 percent and total liabilities of KRW 131.5 billion remain a financial burden, and operating cash flow was also negative through 2025.

Robot-related sales of about KRW 2.7 billion and the POSCO E&C unit-price contract are evidence that new and existing businesses have both begun contributing to results. The company said nine executives bought shares on the open market and framed this as a commitment to responsible management.

The next checkpoints are controlling-shareholder net income and the audit opinion in the 2026 annual report, and whether operating profit stays positive in the first quarter of 2027.
