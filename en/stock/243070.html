02

Business structure

Huons is a pharmaceutical company built around prescription drugs, beauty and wellbeing products, and contract manufacturing (CMO). In the third quarter of 2025, prescription drug revenue was KRW 70 billion, up 5.1% year on year, and revenue from its flagship anesthetic rose 44% year on year.

CMO revenue reached KRW 19.9 billion, up 28.0%, supported by higher utilization of the eye drop line at Plant 2. Beauty and wellbeing revenue fell 27.6% to KRW 35.3 billion because the health functional food unit was transferred to subsidiary Huons N.

Excluding the transferred business, revenue was KRW 34.7 billion, up 0.1%. Subsidiary Huons N posted third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 21.8 billion, up 12.5% year on year.

Botulinum toxin is handled by subsidiary Huons BioPharma, whose third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 32.8% from the prior quarter to KRW 10.4 billion on higher Chinese toxin exports. The company began its vaccine distribution business in April 2026, and its continuous glucose monitor business ended.

In an August 2024 report, prescription drugs accounted for 44% of first-half sales and beauty and wellbeing products for 33%. Competitors in botulinum toxin include Hugel and PharmaResearch.

04

Earnings analysis

Huons' reported results peaked in 2023, dipped, and recovered in 2025. Revenue rose from KRW 492.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 552 billion in 2023, up 12.1%, then to KRW 590.2 billion in 2024, up 6.9%, and to KRW 620.8 billion in 2025, up 5.2%, marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 56 billion in 2023, the largest year of 2022 to 2025, and KRW 39.7 billion in 2024, down 29.1%, the smallest year. Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 45.6 billion, up 14.9%, with an operating margin of 7.3%, above 6.7% in 2024 but below 10.1% in 2023.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 50.5 billion in 2023, the largest year, and recovered to KRW 42.7 billion in 2025, up 44.8%. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 42.2 billion in 2025 and is a different figure from controlling-shareholder net income.

Operating cash flow was KRW 45 billion in 2025, below KRW 52.6 billion in 2024. Quarterly, the fourth quarter of 2025 posted revenue of KRW 165.2 billion, up 7.5% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 9.82 billion, before the first quarter of 2026 swung to an operating loss of KRW 649 million.

The second quarter returned to operating profit of KRW 2.93 billion, but the third quarter posted revenue of KRW 144 billion, down 6.3% year on year, and an operating loss of KRW 4.47 billion.

The company attributed the sales decline to the end of its continuous glucose monitor business and the absence of US exports, and the profit squeeze to higher cost of sales, R&D spending, and added recall costs on a US export product.

Third-quarter R&D rose 13.9% year on year and 21% from the prior quarter, and cumulative R&D for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 7.9 billion, up 24.8%, about 4.9% of sales.

Third-quarter net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 16.1 billion, up 103.8% year on year, moving in the opposite direction from the operating loss; how non-operating items contributed will be checked in the regular report line items. Over the last four quarters, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 2.71 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Among Korean pharmaceutical companies, Huons combines a prescription drug core with eye drop and injectable contract manufacturing. The recovery in US injectable exports and higher eye drop CMO volumes lifted results in the third quarter of 2025.

In the third quarter of 2026, however, there were no US export sales, which made overseas shipments a source of quarterly swings. Botulinum toxin exports to China have emerged as a new growth lever. In domestic botulinum toxin, the company competes with Hugel and PharmaResearch.

Vaccine and domestic drug sales kept growing in the third quarter of 2026. CMO revenue depends on line utilization, which lifted sales in the third quarter of 2025. Vaccine distribution is a new business started in April 2026 with a distribution model different from its prescription drug business.

Assessing where Huons sits in the industry cycle requires comparing peers such as SK Bioscience and Samsung Biologics on quarterly results and order flow.

06

Outlook

In a corrected filing dated November 12, 2026, the company lowered its full-year revenue outlook from KRW 646.2 billion to KRW 584.6 billion, a cut of about 9.5% from the earlier forecast.

The company plans to expand its vaccine distribution business to restore sales and to focus on margin improvement through cost discipline. In the R&D pipeline, a new drug trial began enrolling its first patient in July 2026.

Raising utilization at the second plant was cited as a medium-term priority in the third quarter of 2025. The company decided a cash dividend for the third quarter of 2026, which it described as a reduced dividend funded by transferring capital reserves to retained earnings.

The record date is December 4 and the scheduled payment date is December 22. The next regular report, the 2026 annual report, will show progress against the revenue outlook and fourth-quarter results.

Because the outlook of KRW 584.6 billion sits below the 2025 reported revenue of KRW 620.8 billion, the guidance implies lower revenue in 2026 than in 2025. The contribution of vaccine distribution and toxin exports to recovering that outlook is the next point to check.

Overall view

Huons' reported 2025 results show three consecutive years of revenue growth, operating profit of KRW 45.6 billion, and a debt ratio of 64.0%, pointing to recovery and a stronger balance sheet.

However, the third-quarter 2026 operating loss of KRW 4.47 billion and the lowered revenue outlook from KRW 646.2 billion to KRW 584.6 billion show near-term pressure on profitability and top line. Higher R&D, the absence of US exports, recall costs, and the end of the glucose monitor business acted together.

Strengths include vaccine distribution expansion, toxin exports to China, CMO growth, and the return to operating profit in the second quarter of 2026. Weaknesses include the renewed third-quarter loss, the lowered outlook, the funding structure of the reduced dividend, and uncertainty in the new drug trial.

The next points to check are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results, the margin and outlook progress in the 2026 annual report, vaccine distribution revenue scale, and whether US exports resume. How the share price is read depends on whether profit recovery continues quarter to quarter.
