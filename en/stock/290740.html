Actro Widens From Smartphone Parts Into Equipment and New Businesses

Actro is widening beyond smartphone camera actuators into inspection equipment and automotive and robot parts, but third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 118 million, so whether profitability recovers on a sustained basis is the next item to watch.

  2. 2

    Revenue rose for three straight years from 2023 to 2025 to KRW 244.9 billion, the largest year of 2022–2025, but third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 50.2 billion fell 29.5% from a year earlier.

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Actro was founded in 2012 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2018 as a maker of actuators for mobile devices and automation equipment. Its core products are AF, OIS and folded-zoom actuators for smartphone camera modules, which move the lens up and down to correct focus and shake.

Production runs through its Vietnam subsidiaries, ActRO VINA and ARP VINA, and ActRO VINA is wholly owned by Actro. Customers are reported to include Samsung, Google and China-based handset makers.

The second pillar is equipment: the company has begun selling its in-house inspection equipment to outside customers, and said cumulative equipment sales for the first three quarters of 2025 reached the KRW 18 billion range.

Meritz Securities forecast 2025 equipment revenue of KRW 21.1 billion, which is an analyst estimate. The third pillar is new businesses.

Terahertz-based non-destructive inspection equipment is reported to be at the mass-production stage for cathode and separator inspection in secondary batteries, and quality tests are under way for semiconductor back-end and glass-substrate inspection.

Heating parts for autonomous-driving cameras are expected to expand supply, starting with a North American EV maker. The largest shareholder, Ha Dong-gil, and related parties held 38.4% as of April 23, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 133 billion in 2022 to KRW 156.8 billion in 2023 (up 17.9%), KRW 203.1 billion in 2024 (up 29.5%) and KRW 244.9 billion in 2025 (up 20.6%), growing three years in a row from 2023 to 2025, with 2025 the largest year of 2022–2025.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 5.77 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.69 billion in 2023, a turnaround to profit, fell 48.5% to KRW 2.42 billion in 2024, and rose 172.2% to KRW 6.58 billion in 2025.

The operating margin was -4.3% in 2022, 3.0% in 2023, 1.2% in 2024 and 2.7% in 2025, so 2025 improved on 2024 but stayed below 2023.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from a net loss of KRW 9.33 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.75 billion in 2023, KRW 1.49 billion in 2024 (down 74.2%) and KRW 7.38 billion in 2025 (up 396.7%); total net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 7.32 billion in 2025, a different figure.

The company attributed the 2025 improvement to higher sales of folded-zoom and slim actuators for new flagship models, plus profit contribution from external sales of automation equipment and six-axis inspection machines.

Quarterly, third-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 71.3 billion with operating profit of KRW 2.65 billion; fourth-quarter revenue fell 12.5% quarter on quarter to KRW 62.3 billion and operating profit fell 21.0% to KRW 2.09 billion.

First-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 48.7 billion, down 21.9% from the prior quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 487 million; the company cited the seasonal low and the prolonged Middle East conflict as reducing actuator shipments.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue rose 8.0% to KRW 52.6 billion with an operating loss of KRW 404 million, a narrower loss. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 50.2 billion, down 29.5% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 118 million, a swing to profit that was down 95.5% from a year earlier.

However, the third-quarter controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 3.49 billion means the disclosed figures alone do not pin down the non-operating items that separate operating profit from the bottom line.

Controlling-shareholder net income for the four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 totaled KRW 4.07 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 3.37 billion in 2022, KRW 24.9 billion in 2023, -KRW 337 million in 2024 and KRW 19.9 billion in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio was 69.0% in 2025 versus 44.6% in 2022.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for camera-module actuators follows the spread of OIS and folded-zoom designs as smartphone camera performance competition intensifies. A December 2025 company-commentary summary said OIS adoption is extending to mid- and low-priced smartphones, which is raising Actro's market share.

Meritz Securities, in a December 2025 report, pointed to slowing growth in the smartphone market, while SK Securities, in an August 2025 report, credited the company with a profit turnaround despite a weak global smartphone market.

In a phase where set shipments are flat, higher-value products such as folded-zoom actuators carry the earnings. The spread of autonomous driving and robotics, which increases the number of cameras per vehicle, is presented as a backdrop for the business expansion.

In secondary-battery and semiconductor inspection equipment, the independent research firm JLC Research said in a November 2025 report that the business could expand into semiconductor back-end, glass substrate and battery applications.

On equipment profitability, SK Securities said in its August 2025 report that inspection equipment earns more than five times the margin of actuator parts. Meritz Securities said in-house automation lines improved yields and expanded market share.

Eugene Investment & Securities grouped Samsung Electro-Mechanics and other peers together when comparing valuation in its May 2026 report.

06

Outlook

In its first-quarter 2026 results announcement in May 2026, the company said supply of folded-zoom products to global customers and mass production of automotive parts for North American carmakers would begin in earnest from the second quarter, lifting results.

It said orders would concentrate in the second half, with external equipment sales and robot-part mass production starting, and it expects higher sales of general-purpose terahertz inspection equipment.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue rose 8.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 52.6 billion, but an operating loss of KRW 404 million continued, and third-quarter operating profit of KRW 118 million marked a swing to profit.

The revenue recovery appeared from the second quarter, but the operating profit margin of that swing remains thin.

Eugene Investment & Securities said in its May 2026 report that the company is diversifying into automotive and robot parts beyond equipment, and expects new-business revenue to become visible in the second half.

In an April 2026 news report, the company said it aims to begin mass production of physical-AI parts at its second Vietnam subsidiary sequentially in the second and third quarters.

Investment in the Vietnam subsidiaries was disclosed in March 2026, at about KRW 10.3 billion for ARP VINA and KRW 5.13555 billion for ActRO VINA. A share cancellation led to a listing change on November 2, 2026, bringing common shares listed to 9,571,470.

08

Bull factors

Profit contribution from the equipment business

The company is expanding external sales of in-house inspection equipment and automation lines, and the 2025 operating margin improvement to 2.7% from 1.2% in 2024 is attributed partly to this business.

SK Securities said in its August 2025 report that inspection equipment earns more than five times the margin of actuator parts. The core argument is that a larger equipment share widens room for overall profitability to improve.

Earnings recovery and cash generation in 2025

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 6.58 billion, up 172.2%, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7.38 billion, up 396.7%. Operating cash flow also turned from -KRW 337 million in 2024 to KRW 19.9 billion in 2025.

Total equity rose from KRW 69.1 billion at the end of 2024 to KRW 73.2 billion at the end of 2025, strengthening the financial base within a year.

New-business pipeline

Mass production of automotive parts for North American carmakers and of humanoid and autonomous-driving parts was presented as a second-half 2026 plan. Terahertz inspection equipment has been described as being at the mass-production application stage for cathode and separator inspection in secondary batteries.

The disclosure said proceeds from a KRW 11.7 billion treasury-share disposal would fund automation equipment for autonomous-driving and humanoid parts.

Bear factors

Volatility in quarterly results

After an operating loss of KRW 487 million in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 404 million in the second, third-quarter operating profit of KRW 118 million was down 95.5% from a year earlier. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 50.2 billion fell 29.5% year on year. Whether the swing to profit is a one-off will show up in full-year 2026 results.

Dependence on the smartphone market

Actuator revenue is tied to smartphone shipments, and the company attributed the first-quarter 2026 shipment decline to the seasonal low and the prolonged Middle East conflict. Meritz Securities pointed to slowing smartphone market growth in its December 2025 report. The contribution from new businesses is not yet clearly visible in the disclosed results.

A thin earnings base

The third quarter of 2026 posted a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 3.49 billion, a swing into loss from the prior quarter. Controlling-shareholder net income for the four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 totaled only KRW 4.07 billion.

The company moved from a loss in 2022 to profit in 2023, but net income fell to KRW 1.49 billion in 2024, so earnings have not built up steadily.

Risk factors

Customer and industry concentration

Key customers include Samsung and Google as well as China-based handset makers. Prolonged slowing in smartphone market growth could reduce actuator shipments. The company itself cited the seasonal low and the prolonged Middle East conflict for the first-quarter 2026 shipment decline.

Share-price volatility and trading flows

On January 9, 2026, the stock was designated a one-day investment-caution stock after rising 75.08% over 15 days, with buying concentrated in a small number of accounts. The top 20 accounts accounted for 52.39% of buying, which was cited as a basis for the designation. With thin trading volume, the share price can move sharply on changes in flows.

Investment burden and financial position

In March 2026 the company disclosed investments of about KRW 10.3 billion in ARP VINA and KRW 5.13555 billion in ActRO VINA. The debt-to-equity ratio was 69.0% at the end of 2025, up from 44.6% in 2022, and operating cash flow was -KRW 337 million in 2024. If equipment investment does not translate into earnings, the financial burden could grow.

Overall view

Actro began with smartphone camera actuators and is widening into in-house inspection equipment, automation lines, and automotive and robot parts.

On an annual basis, operating results recovered from an operating loss of KRW 5.77 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 6.58 billion in 2025, and revenue grew three years in a row from 2023 to 2025. The 2026 quarterly trend has been less steady.

The company posted operating losses in the first and second quarters, third-quarter revenue of KRW 50.2 billion fell 29.5% year on year, and the third-quarter swing to KRW 118 million operating profit came alongside a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 3.49 billion.

Strengths include profitability of the equipment business, the earnings recovery in 2025, and a pipeline of new businesses in North American automotive and robot parts.

Weaknesses include dependence on smartphones, quarterly volatility, the fact that new-business contributions are not yet clearly visible in disclosed results, and share-price volatility shown by the January 2026 surge and investment-caution designation.

Securities reports that were published either left the rating blank or focused on valuation and visibility of new businesses, and valuation readings shift with the earnings recovery. The next checkpoints are full-year 2026 preliminary results, expected around February 2027, and the 2026 annual business report.
