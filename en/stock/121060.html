02

Business structure

Unipoint was founded in 1996, starting with middleware and database construction, and today supplies enterprise IT infrastructure solutions, information security solutions and implementation services, according to its website.

It listed on the Konex market in October 2024's predecessor year of 2014, specifically in October 2014, and operates in computer programming, systems integration and management. In fiscal 2024, IT infrastructure accounted for 58.50% of sales and security for 20.37%, with services and other items making up the rest.

The business leans toward distribution and maintenance of products from overseas software and hardware vendors, which keeps gross margins thin. The fiscal 2025 cost of sales ratio was 86.85%, and separate-basis gross profit was KRW 7.7 billion.

Customers cited publicly include Samsung, Hyundai Motor, KB Kookmin Bank and the Ministry of National Defense. The company has said that after its IBM distribution agreement ended in the past, it pursued diversification into RPA, cloud migration tools and a content management partnership.

Large systems integrators in the sector, such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS, operate at a very different scale, and Unipoint is a small solutions supplier by comparison.

03

Recent trends

Per FnGuide, revenue fell from KRW 81.5 billion in 2021 to KRW 58.6 billion in 2022 and KRW 41.3 billion in 2023, then recovered to KRW 54.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 58.3 billion in 2025. In 2023 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 14 billion and a net loss of KRW 17 billion.

It returned to profit in 2024, with operating profit of KRW 2 billion and net profit of KRW 3 billion.

The 29th-term separate financial statements approved at the March 24, 2026 shareholders' meeting show revenue of KRW 58.346 billion, operating profit of KRW 477 million and net profit of KRW 457 million, with an unqualified audit opinion.

Total assets were KRW 30.658 billion, total liabilities KRW 22.187 billion and total equity KRW 8.471 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio was 261.92%. Trade and other current receivables rose from KRW 15.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 18.6 billion in 2025, and operating cash flow remained negative in 2025.

The meeting also approved a cash dividend of KRW 30 per share. The share price fell 14.38% to KRW 524 on March 6, 2026, the day the capital reduction was announced, and rose 14.52% to KRW 639 on March 24.

Hankyung Korea Market showed KRW 776 with zero volume on October 13, and at the time of writing the price is KRW 701, market capitalization KRW 3.6 billion and trading value zero. Results for the first half of 2026 and later periods could not be confirmed in our research.

04

Outlook

FnGuide shows no published consensus estimates for 2026, so future results can only be verified through the company's own filings. With a fiscal 2025 operating margin of just 0.82%, room for improvement in business metrics depends on managing gross margin and controlling selling and administrative expenses.

Enterprise IT demand for security, cloud migration and process automation is a growth area the company has emphasized, but the size of related orders was not confirmed. On capital policy, the May 2026 treasury share cancellation reduced the share count, and the KRW 30 per share dividend continued.

Ownership by the largest shareholder group has increased, concentrating governance further, and an amendment to the articles added real estate sales and leasing to the business purposes, although no specific plan was confirmed.

Rising receivables and negative operating cash flow make the conversion of profit into cash a point to watch. Sales direction, operating margin and cash flow in the next regular filings are the key items to check.

05

Bull factors

Two Straight Years of Operating Profit

The company moved from an operating loss of KRW 14 billion in 2023 to operating profit of KRW 2 billion in 2024 and KRW 477 million in 2025. Revenue also recovered from KRW 41.3 billion in 2023 to KRW 58.3 billion in 2025. The audit opinion was unqualified. The amounts are small, but exiting the loss-making phase is a positive shift in business metrics.

Shareholder Returns and Capital Policy

On May 8, 2026 the company completed a bonus capital reduction by cancelling 787,887 treasury shares. It described the purpose as improving capital structure and enhancing shareholder value. A cash dividend of KRW 30 per share was also approved for fiscal 2025. The reduction cut shares outstanding to 5,193,783.

Rising Insider and Major Shareholder Stakes

On January 30, 2026 a corporate major shareholder bought 615,860 shares off-market, lifting its stake from 27.67% to 37.97%. An inside director's stake reached 17.16% per a March 30, 2026 filing after market purchases, with a reported purchase price of KRW 811.

These filings show management and major holders increasing their holdings. This does not indicate the direction of the share price and can also be read as a consolidation of control.

06

Bear factors

Very Low Profitability

The fiscal 2025 operating margin was 0.82% and the net margin 0.78%. With a cost of sales ratio of 86.85%, even small cost changes could swing results between profit and loss, and the company did post an operating loss in 2023. Revenue growth has so far contributed only limited profit.

Cash Flow and Receivables

Trade and other current receivables increased from KRW 15.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 18.6 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow was negative in 2025 and also in 2024. The debt-to-equity ratio is high at 261.92%, although the borrowing dependence ratio was reported at a low 3.13%.

Thin Trading and Large Price Swings

As a Konex stock, Unipoint has days with zero trading value, and its three-month average volume was about 3,370 shares per Investing.com. Single-day moves have been large, including a 14.99% fall on January 12, 2026, a 14.38% fall on March 6 and a 14.52% rise on March 24.

With a market capitalization of only KRW 3.6 billion, small trades can move the price sharply. Executing trades at a desired time may be difficult.

07

Risk factors

Supply Contract

A large share of the business is distribution of products from overseas software and hardware vendors, so changes in distribution agreements matter. The company has said it pursued diversification after its IBM distribution partnership ended in the past.

If dependence on a specific vendor rises, contract renewals can directly affect revenue and profit. The current contract status was not confirmed in our research.

Governance and Capital Policy

The corporate major shareholder's stake rose to 37.97%, concentrating governance. Capital reductions and treasury share cancellations affect minority holders' ownership ratios. The articles were amended to add real estate sales and leasing as a business purpose, but no concrete plan was identified in the filings. Any change in business direction should be checked through disclosures.

Liquidity

The Konex market has limited participants and order books can have wide gaps. Investing.com showed a wide bid-ask spread of KRW 656 to KRW 875. On days with no trades, the quoted price simply carries over from the previous day. As a result, the displayed price may differ from the price at which a trade can actually be executed.

08

Overall view

Unipoint is a small IT infrastructure and security solutions supplier founded in 1996, which returned to operating profit in 2024 and 2025 after a loss in 2023. With a fiscal 2025 operating margin of 0.82%, however, the stability of that profit is still being tested.

The May 2026 treasury share cancellation, the KRW 30 per share dividend and the increased stakes held by a major shareholder and an inside director are facts on capital policy and governance.

On the other side, rising receivables, negative operating cash flow, a high debt-to-equity ratio and thin Konex trading are burdens. FnGuide shows a price-to-book ratio of 0.38 on a fiscal 2025 basis, but low trading volume limits how far that figure can be interpreted.

Results for the first half of 2026 and later periods were not confirmed in our research and should be checked in future filings. This report is for information only, and investment decisions rest with the reader.
