02

Business structure

Unipoint supplies IT infrastructure and security solutions, combining system integration (SI), maintenance and software distribution. By segment, the IT infrastructure business accounted for 58.50% of 2024 sales, the security business 20.37%, SI maintenance 16.36%, and security maintenance 4.77%.

The security business resells external products as a distributor, and the company signed a partnership agreement with Ahnlab in March 2024 and a distribution agreement in March 2025.

In December 2023 it signed a public-sector distribution agreement with TmaxTibero, widening its distribution channel into government and public clients. Product sales made up 74.21% of revenue in 2022 and 72.15% in 2023, higher than the service share.

Most revenue comes from Korea, with domestic sales at 99.90% in 2024. The cost burden is heavy, with a cost of sales ratio of 86.85% and a selling and administrative expense ratio of 12.33% in 2025.

Larger SI operators in the same sector, including Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, have far greater capital and headcount. The company was founded in 1996, listed on KONEX on October 24, 2014, and had 101 employees at the end of 2024.

Because the security business distributes third-party brands, revenue structure can shift with supplier contract terms. Unipoint's model leans toward distribution and maintenance at a small-to-mid scale rather than large project bidding.

Recent trends

Revenue for 2025 was KRW 58,346.05 million, up 7.4% from KRW 54,316.70 million in 2024. Operating profit rose 126.4% to KRW 477.15 million from KRW 210.76 million, leaving an operating margin of 0.8%. Net income for 2025 was KRW 456.87 million, compared with KRW 295.56 million in 2024.

In 2023 revenue was KRW 41,319.18 million, with an operating loss of KRW 1,406.49 million and a net loss of KRW 1,711.42 million. Revenue in 2022 was KRW 58,593.00 million, with an operating loss of KRW 804.20 million.

Over the same period, cash flow from operating activities moved from an inflow of KRW 1,147.62 million in 2023 to outflows of KRW 1,979.21 million in 2024 and KRW 1,456.39 million in 2025. At the end of 2025, the debt ratio was 261.92%, the equity ratio 27.63%, and the borrowing dependence 3.13%.

At the shareholders meeting on March 24, 2026, the financial statements for the 29th term were approved and a cash dividend of KRW 30 per share was resolved. On May 8, 2026, a capital reduction through cancellation of 787,887 treasury shares was completed, reducing shares outstanding from 5,981,670 to 5,193,783.

The share price was KRW 524 on March 6, KRW 789 on April 24 and KRW 701 on May 8, 2026, and it fell 13.14% to KRW 701 on November 11, 2026.

Outlook

The treasury share cancellation completed on May 8, 2026 reduced shares outstanding to 5,193,783, with a par value of KRW 500 per share. A smaller share count changes the denominator of per-share metrics, and the company cited improved capital structure efficiency and shareholder value as the purpose of the reduction.

The price-to-book ratio shown by FnGuide was 0.49x, based on book value per share as of December 2025. Operating profit has stayed positive for a second year, but the operating margin has not exceeded 1%, so the pace of profit improvement depends on sales growth and changes in the cost ratio.

The cash outflows from operating activities in 2024 and 2025 remain the key indicator of whether profit gains convert into cash generation. The 2026 annual operating results and cash flows will be disclosed through the annual business report due in March 2027.

As a KONEX-listed company, it has gone through capital structure change procedures, including a trading suspension from April 29, 2026, and trading conditions may change with the disclosure flow.

Bull factors

Two consecutive years of operating profit and sales growth

Operating profit was KRW 210.76 million in 2024 and KRW 477.15 million in 2025, keeping the company profitable for a second year. Revenue rose 7.4% in 2025 to KRW 58,346.05 million, recovering after the 2023 decline.

Compared with the operating loss of KRW 1,406.49 million in 2023, the improvement in earnings capacity is visible in the disclosed figures.

Capital structure adjustment and shareholder returns

On May 8, 2026, a capital reduction through cancellation of 787,887 treasury shares was completed, reducing shares outstanding to 5,193,783. The shareholders meeting in March 2026 approved a year-end cash dividend of KRW 30 per common share, with a dividend yield of 4.9%. The company stated that capital structure efficiency and shareholder value were the purpose of the reduction.

Distribution channels built with Ahnlab and TmaxTibero

The company signed a partnership agreement with Ahnlab in March 2024 and a distribution agreement in March 2025. The TmaxTibero agreement predates the Ahnlab agreements and serves as the company's public-sector channel. The security business accounted for 20.37% of 2024 sales.

Bear factors

Thin margins and a high cost ratio

The operating margin was 0.8% in 2025 and 0.39% in 2024, so profit is small relative to revenue of KRW 58,346.05 million. The cost of sales ratio was 86.85% in 2025, meaning most revenue flows out as cost. With a selling and administrative expense ratio of 12.33%, little remains from gross profit.

Persistent cash flow deficits

Cash flow from operating activities was negative in both 2024 and 2025, with an outflow of KRW 1,456.39 million in 2025. Cash operating income for 2025 was a deficit of KRW 1,007.91 million. The gap between net income of KRW 456.87 million and operating cash flow is cited as a weakness.

Financial leverage and shifts in ownership

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 261.92% and the equity ratio was 27.63%, indicating a relatively high reliance on debt. On January 30, 2026, Jung Woo-cheon, a major shareholder with more than 10%, sold his entire 615,860-share stake over the counter, a holding that stood at 10.3% as of December 20, 2024.

As a small KONEX-listed stock with a market capitalization of KRW 3.6 billion, the share price moved sharply, falling 13.14% in one session on November 11, 2026.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Corporate IT spending is affected by interest rates and the economic cycle. With domestic sales at 99.90% in 2024, Unipoint is directly exposed to order changes from domestic corporate and public clients.

Product sales accounted for 70%-plus of revenue in 2022 and 2023, so changes in supplier product policies also add to revenue volatility.

Industry and competition

Larger SI operators in the sector have greater capital and headcount, giving them an edge in large project competition. The security business depends on distribution contracts with external brands, so changes in contract terms or distribution rights can directly affect sales. With a cost of sales ratio of 86.85%, price competition could further compress margins.

Liquidity and share price

KONEX-listed companies often have small trading volumes, which can amplify price moves. Trading in common shares was suspended from April 29, 2026 due to electronic registration changes linked to share consolidation and division procedures.

The share price fell 13.14% in a single session on November 11, 2026, and although the share count has fallen, the price still depends on market conditions and supply and demand.

08

Overall view

Unipoint has stayed profitable at the operating level in 2024 and 2025 and has moved out of its earlier losses. Revenue for 2025 was KRW 58,346.05 million, up 7.4%, and net income was KRW 456.87 million. However, the 0.8% operating margin and the 86.85% cost of sales ratio mean profit remains thin relative to revenue.

Cash flow from operating activities was negative in both 2024 and 2025, so whether accounting profit converts into cash is the main open question.

Strengths cited include two consecutive years of operating profit, the treasury share cancellation and the KRW 30 dividend, and distribution channels with Ahnlab and TmaxTibero.

Weaknesses cited include the low margin, operating cash outflows, a debt ratio of 261.92%, the full sale of a major shareholder's stake, and the share price volatility typical of a small KONEX stock. This summary is intended to lay out facts and the balance of strengths and weaknesses.
