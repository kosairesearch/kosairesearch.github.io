02

Business structure

Parataxis Ethereum is a data security solutions company founded in 2005 that listed on KOSDAQ through a 2023 merger. It changed its name from Sinsiway in January 2026 and became an affiliate of Parataxis Holdings LLC, a U.S. digital asset investment firm.

The business runs on two pillars: data security and a digital asset treasury (DAT) strategy. The data security business centers on DB access control, encryption, cloud security solutions and maintenance, and the company said its 2025 revenue from this business was about KRW 14 billion.

In first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 6.93915 billion, the DB encryption product family (including PETRA CIPHER) contributed KRW 1.98365 billion, about 28.6%, and the DB access control family (including PETRA) contributed KRW 1.7065 billion, about 24.6%.

Cloud-related revenue was KRW 1.59334 billion and service and maintenance revenue was KRW 1.63637 billion in the same period. These segment figures come from media reports of the company's August 18, 2026 announcement and are not filed regular-report values.

The DAT business began when the company adopted ETH as a strategic financial asset in January 2026, with a plan to hold ETH long term and earn staking rewards.

Its customers are enterprises and public institutions, and according to a corporate information service, the company obtained the first CC certification for DB encryption and holds 16 patents. In security software it competes for enterprise and public customers with peers in the same sector such as Ahnlab.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 9.72 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.5 billion in 2023 (up 7.6% year on year), KRW 11.8 billion in 2024 (up 13.1%) and KRW 14 billion in 2025 (up 18.4%), which is three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 2.13 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.53 billion in 2023 (down 27.9%), then rose to KRW 2.38 billion in 2024 (up 55.2%) and KRW 3.63 billion in 2025 (up 52.5%), two consecutive years of growth.

The operating margin went from 21.9% in 2022 to 14.7% in 2023, 20.1% in 2024 and 25.9% in 2025, so the 2025 level sits above 2022.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from KRW 1.8 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 19.17 million in 2023, a turn to loss, before returning to profit of KRW 2.51 billion in 2024 and reaching KRW 3.34 billion in 2025 (up 33.1%).

Consolidated net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same yearly levels. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 43.8% in 2022 to 20.3% in 2023 and 12.5% in 2024, then rose to 28.9% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 26.3 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 7.58 billion. Operating cash flow rose every year, from KRW 2.02 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.71 billion in 2023, KRW 3.54 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.81 billion in 2025.

On a three-month basis, revenue climbed from KRW 2.97 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 5.87 billion in the fourth quarter (up 97.7% quarter on quarter), then KRW 2.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 54.0%), KRW 4.24 billion in the second quarter (up 57.1%) and KRW 3.35 billion in the third quarter (down 21.1% quarter on quarter, up 12.7% year on year).

Operating profit of KRW 824 million in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 2.4 billion in the fourth quarter gave way to an operating loss of KRW 308 million in the first quarter of 2026, a turn back to profit at KRW 1.13 billion in the second quarter, and KRW 573 million in the third quarter (down 30.5% year on year).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.17 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, a net loss of KRW 362 million in the first quarter of 2026, a deeper net loss of KRW 2.69 billion in the second quarter, and KRW 4.25 billion in the third quarter (up 462.4% year on year).

In the second quarter, operating profit of KRW 1.13 billion coexisted with a net loss of KRW 2.69 billion, and in the third quarter operating profit of KRW 573 million coexisted with net income of KRW 4.25 billion, so both quarters show a wide gap between operating results and bottom-line results.

Because the company holds ETH as a financial asset, valuation and disposal gains or losses may flow through non-operating items, and the detailed line items will be visible in the 2026 annual report.

The sum of net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, is KRW 3.37 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In security software, the company's growth is tied to rising security demand from the shift to cloud, according to a corporate information service, which reported first-half 2026 standalone revenue up 34.6%. The company attributes part of that to a wider customer base for its flagship PETRA DB security products.

It competes for enterprise and public customers with sector peers such as Ahnlab, and it points to its DB encryption certification and patents as differentiators. On the digital asset side, the notable feature is that it became the first Korean-listed company to adopt an Ethereum-based DAT strategy.

Its claim to hold the largest Ethereum position among Asian DAT companies comes from the company and media reports, and per CoinGecko data cited on October 16, 2026, it ranked 11th among listed companies worldwide.

In the United States, the CLARITY Act cleared a Senate Banking Committee markup in May 2026 and was close to completing legislation, and if enacted, Ethereum would be classified as a commodity under CFTC oversight. The company views this legislation as a catalyst for institutional inflows.

It also says domestic institutions face limits on direct crypto investment and could gain Ethereum exposure through a listed company. However, linking the core business with the DAT strategy is still at an early stage, and no staking share had been specified as of October 2026.

06

Outlook

The company names staking of its Ethereum holdings as the top priority for the second half of 2026. In an October 2026 interview, its CEO said legal and accounting requirements were under review and set a goal of running a test-scale staking program through an external service within the year.

No staking share has been specified, and the company says it will set one after meeting legal and audit standards as a listed firm.

It is weighing building its own infrastructure against partnering with outside providers, and it plans to flesh out its operating strategy for held assets, including staking, by the first half of 2027. It has also said it is studying custody services for domestic institutions.

Core business revenue and operating profit rose 34.6% and 101.4% in the first half of 2026, but third-quarter revenue fell 21.1% from the prior quarter, so whether the momentum holds is the open question.

The merger with Parataxis Korea was decided on April 7, 2026 and withdrawn on October 6, 2026, and the extraordinary shareholder meeting scheduled for November 27 was also withdrawn.

The stated reasons for the withdrawal included Parataxis Korea's delisting decision and the legal process around it, and the possibility of large-scale exercise of appraisal rights. The exchange's deadline for deciding whether to designate the company as an unfaithful disclosure company is November 23, 2026.

Bull factors

Core business profit recovery

Operating profit rose 52.5% to KRW 3.63 billion in 2025, and the operating margin climbed to 25.9%. The company said on August 18, 2026 that first-half revenue of KRW 6.93915 billion and operating profit of KRW 818.35 million rose 34.6% and 101.4% year on year. The second-quarter operating margin of 26.6% was above the 22.9% of a year earlier.

Core cash flow and a moderate debt load

Operating cash flow grew from KRW 2.02 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.81 billion in 2025. Total liabilities were KRW 7.58 billion at the end of 2025 against equity of KRW 26.3 billion, and the debt ratio rose from 12.5% in 2024 to 28.9% in 2025 but remains moderate relative to equity. The company says core-business cash flow provides the base for a long-term Ethereum treasury strategy.

Ethereum holding scale and the legislative backdrop

Ethereum holdings rose to about 11,729 ETH in reports dated October 15, 2026. The company described its position as the largest among Asian listed holders in May 2026, and CoinGecko data cited on October 16, 2026 placed it 11th among listed companies worldwide.

Reports on the CLARITY Act clearing a Senate Banking Committee markup in May 2026 accompany the company's view that institutional inflows could follow if the framework takes effect.

Bear factors

Gap between quarterly net income and operating results

Second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.13 billion came with a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.69 billion, which widened the loss. In the third quarter operating profit fell to KRW 573 million while net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 4.25 billion.

Over the last four quarters that net income totals KRW 3.37 billion, and its direction has diverged from operating results. Whether the gap comes from non-operating items has to be confirmed in the detailed regular-report line items.

Volatile quarterly revenue

Revenue peaked at KRW 5.87 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and fell 54.0% to KRW 2.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 3.35 billion was down 21.1% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 573 million was down 30.5% year on year.

Quarterly swings are large enough that the first-half growth trend cannot be assumed to have carried into the third quarter.

Merger withdrawal and disclosure issues

The company decided on April 7, 2026 to absorb Parataxis Korea through a merger, then withdrew that decision on October 6, 2026. Because the withdrawal was classified as a reversal of disclosure, the exchange announced on October 29, 2026 a planned designation as an unfaithful disclosure company. The governance restructuring and financial stabilization that the merger was meant to deliver are on hold.

10

Risk factors

Crypto price volatility

Held Ethereum is valued at market prices, so a decline would move non-operating results and total equity together. The gap between operating results and net income in 2026 may be linked to this effect. The company keeps buying ETH, so exposure to price moves grows with holdings.

Staking and custody operations

Basic staking had not started as of October 2026, and its share and method are undecided. The company is weighing its own infrastructure alongside outside partners, which adds technology and security risk in custody and operations. If legal and accounting requirements take longer to meet, the planned staking income will slip as well.

Disclosure and governance

After the merger withdrawal, the company was given notice of a planned designation as an unfaithful disclosure company, with a decision deadline of November 23, 2026. Parataxis Korea has sought an injunction against its delisting decision, and the court's ruling could change how the affiliated companies are arranged.

Because the largest shareholder sits within the Parataxis group, transparency in intra-group decisions is a key issue.

Overall view

Parataxis Ethereum is a KOSDAQ company that combines a data security core business with an Ethereum treasury strategy.

The core business posted revenue of KRW 14 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.63 billion in 2025, the largest year of 2022–2025, and the company said first-half 2026 operating profit rose 101.4% year on year.

Its balance sheet at the end of 2025 showed total liabilities of KRW 7.58 billion against equity of KRW 26.3 billion, a moderate debt burden relative to equity. Positive factors are the recovery in core profit, steadily rising operating cash flow, the scale of Ethereum holdings and the legislative backdrop.

Negative factors are large swings in quarterly net income, volatile quarterly revenue, and disclosure and governance issues including the merger withdrawal and the notice of a planned unfaithful-disclosure designation.

Third-quarter 2026 net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to profit at KRW 4.25 billion, but operating profit in the same quarter was only KRW 573 million, so the source of earnings needs to be separated.

The next checkpoints are the exchange's decision on the unfaithful-disclosure designation by November 23, 2026, whether the staking test runs by the end of 2026, and the fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results and non-operating line items in the annual report expected around late March 2027.
