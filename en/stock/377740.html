02

Business structure

BioNote runs two businesses: an animal diagnostics unit that sells veterinary test products, and a life science (bio contents) unit that supplies intermediate products and raw materials based on its recombinant antigen and antibody manufacturing technology.

Through the first nine months of 2023, animal diagnostics accounted for 71% of revenue and bio contents for 29%, according to a February 2024 equity research report.

The core animal diagnostics lineup comprises the Rapid veterinary test kits, the Vcheck F fluorescence immunoassay analyzer, the Vcheck M point-of-care molecular analyzer, the Vcheck C biochemistry analyzer and the Vcheck H hematology analyzer.

In its preliminary release of May 13, 2026, the company said cumulative global sales of Vcheck F were approaching 25,000 units.

The life science unit's sales rose through intermediate products and raw materials supplied to affiliated companies, and the company said profitability improved through cost cuts and operating efficiency.

Its main customers are distributors and affiliated companies in each country, and the same research report recorded a supply agreement with a large distributor serving animal hospitals in the United States and globally.

The company says it is widening its range from immunoassays into biochemistry, hematology, urine and molecular testing. In its preliminary first-half release of August 6, 2026, it cited entry into the automated CLIA market and an AI-based diagnostics platform as portfolio diversification steps.

Because the revenue base is weighted toward animal diagnostics, demand for that product family largely sets the direction of total sales.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 479.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 90.1 billion in 2023, a decline of 81.2%, and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 47 billion in 2023. Revenue recovered to KRW 102.8 billion in 2024, up 14.1%, and operating profit turned to KRW 12.2 billion.

In 2025 revenue reached KRW 118.3 billion, up 15.1%, and operating profit rose to KRW 16.7 billion, up 36.8%, extending the increase to two consecutive years. The operating margin moved from 61.6% in 2022 to -52.2% in 2023, then recovered to 11.9% in 2024 and 14.1% in 2025.

The 2022 result was driven largely by the bio contents unit, which had COVID diagnostics-related revenue, according to a February 2024 research report. The company attributed the 2023 revenue decline to weak results after the COVID endemic transition.

The company said 2024 revenue, excluding the pandemic period, exceeded KRW 100 billion for the first time since its founding. It attributed the 2025 operating profit increase to new product launches, higher sales of existing products and better cost of sales.

Attributable net loss and net loss were both KRW 87.5 billion in 2025, and the company cited losses from equity-method affiliates among the factors behind the swing into loss.

Quarterly figures show a wide gap between operating profit and net income: in Q1 2026 operating profit was KRW 6.35 billion while attributable net income was KRW 41.3 billion, in Q2 operating profit was KRW 8.46 billion against attributable net income of KRW 9.07 billion, and in Q3 operating profit was KRW 7.99 billion against an attributable net loss of KRW 65.3 billion.

Pre-tax loss in Q3 2026 was KRW 75.39 billion, which means non-operating losses far exceeded operating profit. Cumulative January to September 2026 revenue was KRW 101.0 billion, up 11.8%, and operating profit was KRW 22.79 billion, up 43.6%, according to press coverage of the third-quarter report. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 57.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 14.4 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The animal diagnostics market is growing on the back of rising pet households and the broader humanization of pets, according to a corporate commentary on a securities information service. Livestock in vitro diagnostics is described as maintaining steady growth supported by rising national disease-control budgets.

BioNote's position in the cycle can be seen in three phases: the COVID-driven peak, the contraction and the recovery. Revenue was KRW 479.7 billion in 2022, when COVID diagnostics sales were significant, and fell to KRW 90.1 billion in 2023 after the endemic transition.

Sales recovered from 2024 as product demand returned, reaching KRW 118.3 billion in 2025, which is still well below the 2022 level. On competition, the company positions itself on a broad diagnostic solution spanning immunoassays, biochemistry, hematology, urine and molecular testing.

Overseas sales run through distributors and overseas subsidiaries, so the company says the reach of its distribution network determines its market position. The life science unit is moving toward higher profitability through cost reduction and operating efficiency.

Entry into the animal antibody therapeutics market is a new growth driver the company has cited, but its contribution to earnings has not yet appeared in reported figures.

06

Outlook

The company said in its May 2026 preliminary first-quarter release that it intends to keep expanding its share of the global animal diagnostics market.

In its August 6, 2026 preliminary first-half release, it cited entry into the automated CLIA market and an AI-based diagnostics platform as steps in portfolio diversification.

It plans to combine the independent capabilities of its overseas subsidiaries with a diversified global distribution network to extend its reach.

In a February 2026 report, the company said it would strengthen its partnership with large corporate veterinary hospital groups, which it described as central to the US veterinary market. It also said it would enter the animal antibody therapeutics market to secure a new growth engine.

On shareholder returns, it decided in February 2026 to cancel 650,000 treasury shares, with the cancellation scheduled for March 6, 2026, and it also decided on a year-end cash dividend.

Quarterly revenue in 2026 was KRW 32.6 billion, KRW 36.4 billion and KRW 32.1 billion for Q1 to Q3, peaking in Q2 before falling 11.8% quarter on quarter in Q3. Operating profit in Q3 was KRW 7.99 billion, up year on year, but net income continues to depend on non-operating items.

The next checkpoint is therefore whether overseas partnerships and new products lift the operating margin. Results from the new businesses can be checked numerically in regular reports from 2027 onward.

Revenue base shrunk from the pandemic peak

Risk factors

Affiliate and non-operating earnings volatility

The company cited equity-method losses among the factors behind the KRW 87.5 billion net loss in 2025. Q3 2026 pre-tax loss of KRW 75.39 billion far exceeded operating profit of KRW 7.99 billion.

Because operating results and net income move separately, operating profit gains alone do not indicate the direction of net income.

Product concentration and post-pandemic normalization

Animal diagnostics made up 71% of revenue in 2023, with Vcheck F and the Rapid kit line as core products. The company experienced a sharp revenue decline after the COVID-related sales period of 2022 ended. Demand shifts in a single product family flow directly into total results.

Overseas distribution and new business execution

Customers are mainly overseas distributors and affiliates, so results depend on partner performance and contract terms. Automated CLIA, the AI-based platform and animal antibody therapeutics are new businesses whose contribution is not yet visible in reported results. Delays in executing them could slow growth in the current product lines.

12

Overall view

BioNote has increased operating profit since swinging to profit in 2024, driven by its animal diagnostics product lines, and operating profit for Q1 to Q3 2026 was each up year on year.

Attributable net income, however, has alternated into losses, with a 2025 net loss of KRW 87.5 billion and a Q3 2026 loss of KRW 65.3 billion, and the four-quarter attributable net loss totals KRW 209 billion.

Strengths include the recovery in operating profit, a light financial burden with a 3.0% debt ratio, and shareholder returns through treasury share cancellation and dividends.

Weaknesses include the volatility of equity-method and non-operating results, revenue that has shrunk relative to 2022, and lower operating cash flow. On valuation, the price-to-earnings ratio cannot be calculated because of losses, and the price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x.

The stock is currently in a phase where operating recovery and net income volatility coexist. The next checkpoints are the gap between operating profit and attributable net income in the FY2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 results.
