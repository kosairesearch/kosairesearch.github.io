S&D: Profit Growth Amid Quarterly Swings

02

Business structure

S&D is a functional food ingredient specialist founded in 1998, listed on KONEX in 2016 and moved to KOSDAQ in 2021. As of Q2 2026, general-function food ingredients accounted for about 98% of revenue and contract-manufactured products for about 2%.

Its core products are liquid and powder sauce materials that serve as the main ingredients for sauces, and its main customer is Samyang Foods. A securities report from May 2025 identified the production of liquid sauce used in Samyang Foods' Buldak products as the main driver of growth.

In the first quarter of 2025, health functional food ingredients accounted for 7.5% of revenue. The company has broadened its business into natural ingredients for household goods and cosmetics and holds 31 patents.

In December 2024 it disclosed an investment of KRW 16 billion in a factory expansion and production equipment, and in July 2025 its Food Science Center 2nd plant was completed.

Because revenue is tied to the production and export volumes of a major customer, changes in that customer's overseas sales flow directly into S&D's orders. In competition, the long-term questions are whether customers bring sauce materials in-house and whether substitute ingredients emerge.

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 73.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 88.3 billion in 2023 (up 20.4% year on year), KRW 116.1 billion in 2024 (up 31.6%) and KRW 150.9 billion in 2025 (up 30.0%).

Revenue increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year and 2022 the smallest in the 2022 to 2025 period. Operating profit grew from KRW 10.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 23 billion in 2025, and the fastest growth came in 2024 at 56.2%.

Operating margin moved through 14.9%, 14.6%, 17.3% and 15.2% over the same years. A securities report from the first quarter of 2025 said higher utilization led to better profitability, but the drivers of the annual margin change cannot be separated from the disclosed figures alone.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders grew from KRW 9.85 billion in 2022 to KRW 20.6 billion in 2025, with 2024 growth at 43.4%. Net profit including non-controlling interests was disclosed at the same values in each year, so it follows the same path.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 8.28 billion in 2022 to KRW 22.8 billion in 2025, which strengthened cash generation. The debt ratio went from 19.5% in 2022 and 19.7% in 2023 up to 36.7% in 2024 and down to 26.3% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 86.2 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 22.7 billion.

Quarterly revenue ran at KRW 39 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 38.1 billion in Q4 2025 (down 2.5% from the prior quarter), KRW 45.5 billion in Q1 2026 (up 19.4%), KRW 49.9 billion in Q2 2026 (up 9.8%) and KRW 44 billion in Q3 2026 (down 11.8%).

Operating profit rose from KRW 6.1 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 8.3 billion in Q2 2026 before falling to KRW 6.22 billion in Q3 2026, down 25.1% from the prior quarter. Q3 2026 operating profit grew only 2.0% from a year earlier, well below the 12.8% revenue growth.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 6.58 billion in Q2 2026, the highest of the last five quarters, before falling to KRW 4.97 billion in Q3 2026. The sum over the last four quarters was KRW 24.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

S&D's revenue growth has tracked the overseas sales of Samyang Foods' Buldak brand. A December 2024 securities report expected that the entry of Buldak noodles into large U.S. retail chains, together with the customer's factory expansion, would lead to revenue growth.

A May 2025 securities report noted that export demand for Buldak sauce had outrun supply capacity and saw the brand-building of sauces and the customer's North American localization strategy supporting growth.

In the food ingredient market, the company supplies raw materials rather than branded finished goods, so the customer's sales direction and production plans effectively set the size of demand.

On competition, its differentiators are long-term supply relationships with a specific customer and the ability to raise utilization. Conversely, if the customer expands in-house production or secures other suppliers, volumes could decline.

Raw material price swings are a common variable for ingredient makers and affect margins as well. How far the natural-ingredient expansion into household goods and cosmetics adds to revenue will become visible through segment disclosures in future periodic reports. For now, the center of growth remains the single sauce-material line.

Outlook

The confirmed capital investment is the December 2024 decision to spend KRW 16 billion on a factory expansion and production equipment. A May 2025 securities report expected new production lines to start operating from the third quarter of 2025 and to allow about a 30% increase in volume over the previous capacity.

In July 2025 the Food Science Center 2nd plant was completed, widening the production base. How much of the added capacity reached revenue should be read alongside the 2026 quarterly pattern, in which revenue rose 19.4% from the prior quarter in Q1, rose 9.8% in Q2 and fell 11.8% in Q3.

Because operating profit fell from the prior quarter in Q3 2026, whether the capacity gains translate into margin is the key point to check. The next periodic report, the 2026 annual report, will disclose the full-year revenue mix and facility investment status.

As the customer localizes in North America, the regional flow of supplied volumes may shift, so tracking this in disclosures will be necessary. Continuity of growth depends on whether customer demand and utilization of the expanded capacity line up.

08

Bull factors

Revenue expansion tied to customer growth

Revenue rose from KRW 73.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 150.9 billion in 2025 and increased for three consecutive years from 2023. Growth was 31.6% in 2024 and 30.0% in 2025, both above 30%.

The expansion coincided with the overseas sales rise of a major customer, which is the basis for the bullish case that growth depends on customer demand.

Capacity expansion and supply headroom

In December 2024 the company disclosed a KRW 16 billion investment in a factory expansion and production equipment. A May 2025 securities report expected new lines to start in the third quarter and allow about a 30% volume increase over the previous capacity.

The same report said export demand for Buldak sauce exceeded supply capacity, so if the added capacity turns into shipments, room for further growth exists.

Cash generation and financial headroom

Operating cash flow grew from KRW 8.28 billion in 2022 to KRW 22.8 billion in 2025. At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 22.7 billion and total equity was KRW 86.2 billion, with a debt ratio of 26.3%. The ratio fell from 36.7% in 2024, indicating a lighter financial burden.

Bear factors

Slowing quarterly earnings

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 6.22 billion, down 25.1% from the prior quarter, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.97 billion, down 24.5%. Revenue grew 12.8% from a year earlier, but operating profit grew only 2.0%. The drop in both measures from Q2 2026 is cited as the bearish point.

Margin pressure

Operating margin fell from 17.3% in 2024 to 15.2% in 2025. Compared with 14.9% in 2022 and 14.6% in 2023, the 2024 level was relatively high, so whether margins hold that range after 2025 is an open question. A margin decline during a period of revenue growth raises questions about the cost structure and pricing power.

Customer and product concentration

About 98% of revenue comes from general-function food ingredients, and the main customer is Samyang Foods. A change in the customer's sales or sourcing strategy could affect volumes and pricing power.

Diversification is under way, but the revenue mix remains concentrated in one ingredient line, which is cited as a bearish point.

Risk factors

Customer dependence

The main customer's overseas sales and production plans directly shape revenue. If the customer expands in-house production or adds suppliers, volumes could fall. As the customer localizes in North America, the region and method of supply could also change.

Capacity investment and utilization

The KRW 16 billion expansion disclosed in December 2024 and the second plant completed in July 2025 add to fixed costs. If demand slows below expectations, lower utilization could pressure margins. Whether the added volume is translating into earnings, given the Q3 2026 decline, is the point to monitor.

Quarterly volatility and share price movement

Quarterly revenue and profit have swung sharply from quarter to quarter in 2026, which makes the direction of annual earnings harder to read.

The share price can move with supply and demand and market flows separately from earnings, so movements around results announcements should be read apart from the disclosed figures.

Overall view

S&D grew revenue, operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in every year from 2022 through 2025, with 2025 revenue of KRW 150.9 billion, up 30.0% year on year.

The growth engine is sauce-material supply for Samyang Foods' Buldak products, supported by capacity expansion and new production lines. On the other side, operating margin eased from 17.3% in 2024 to 15.2% in 2025, and in Q3 2026 operating profit grew only 2.0% despite revenue growth of 12.8%.

Operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders both fell from the prior quarter in Q3 2026, and the four-quarter total stood at KRW 24.3 billion. On financial structure, the debt ratio fell to 26.3% in 2025 and operating cash flow rose to KRW 22.8 billion, which points to improving stability.

The share price sits at a premium to book value, and because earnings have grown, the earnings multiple is lower at an unchanged price. Bullish factors are customer growth and capacity additions, while bearish factors are slowing quarterly profit and concentration in customer and product. The next points to check are the preliminary Q4 2026 results and the 2026 annual report.
