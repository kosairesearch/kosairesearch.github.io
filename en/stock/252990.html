02

Business structure

Semcns makes ceramic STF (Space Transformer) parts, the core component of probe cards used in wafer testing.

The company was spun out in 2016 after acquiring the ceramic substrate division of Samsung Electro-Mechanics and uses low-temperature co-fired ceramic (LTCC) technology to produce large-area, non-shrinking substrates. Its sales lean heavily toward memory.

On a Q3 2025 basis, NAND probe-card ceramic STF sales were KRW 40 billion, or 72% of total sales, while DRAM sales were KRW 14.3 billion. The DRAM share of sales was reported at about 23% in 2024, and the company is widening its mix from NAND toward DRAM and non-memory products.

Customers listed in its IR materials include Samsung Electronics, SK hynix, Intel, Micron, Kioxia, TSMC and Western Digital. Probe-card and test-socket makers FormFactor, Technoprobe and TSE are also cited as customers. Ceramic substrate technology is considered to be held by only a few companies worldwide.

Production is based at the new Osong plant in Chungbuk, which the company said raised capacity more than threefold after completion in February 2024. On a Q3 2025 basis, capacity was about 4,680 sheets with an average utilization rate of 54.1%, according to press reports. Ceramic STF for CIS probe cards is being developed as a new market but remains a small share of sales.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 50.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 30.9 billion in 2023, a 38.3% decline, then rose to KRW 53.3 billion in 2024 (up 72.3%) and KRW 78 billion in 2025 (up 46.3%), so sales have risen for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025. 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit went from KRW 14.9 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 2.91 billion in 2023, turned to a profit of KRW 5.2 billion in 2024, and rose 182.0% to KRW 14.7 billion in 2025. Operating margin was 29.7% in 2022, -9.4% in 2023, 9.8% in 2024 and 18.8% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 15.1 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 1.31 billion in 2023, KRW 3.32 billion in 2024, and KRW 15.1 billion in 2025, up 354.0%. Net income including non-controlling interests also moved from a loss in 2023 to a profit in 2024.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 17 billion in 2022 to KRW 293 million in 2023, then recovered to KRW 5.62 billion in 2024 and KRW 19.2 billion in 2025. The 2023 drop coincided with the year's sales decline. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 78.9% in 2022 and 87.9% in 2023 to 54.3% in 2024 and 52.4% in 2025.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 166.5 billion and total liabilities were KRW 87.2 billion.

On a quarterly basis, revenue rose from KRW 20.8 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 22.7 billion in Q4 2025 (up 8.9% quarter on quarter), KRW 27.2 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 31.3 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 32.2 billion in Q3 2026, which is four consecutive quarters of growth from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026.

Quarter-on-quarter growth slowed to 3.0% in Q3 2026. Operating profit fell to KRW 4.35 billion in Q4 2025 (down 19.8% quarter on quarter), then rose to KRW 7.68 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 9.53 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 11.2 billion in Q3 2026, three consecutive quarters of growth, with Q3 2026 up 105.9% year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 7.7% quarter on quarter in Q3 2026, to KRW 10.4 billion from KRW 11.3 billion. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 34.8 billion.

Press reports attributed the margin improvement to higher-value product sales and the stronger profitability of DRAM products, while depreciation from capacity investment was cited as a burden.

05

Industry analysis

Ceramic STF is a consumable part inside probe cards that supports MEMS pins and passes signals to test equipment, and it must be replaced during wafer testing. Because demand rises with chip output, analysts say operating leverage grows when the industry recovers.

In the memory cycle, Meritz Securities said in a November 2025 report that third-quarter 2025 DRAM sales hit a quarterly record. The same report said NAND volumes for Kioxia in Japan and YMTC in China kept expanding.

In HBM, the company says rising test-pin counts and larger chip sizes increase demand for multilayer, large-area substrates. Ceramic is also seen as suited to HBM processes because of its durability and heat resistance.

One brokerage report said some leading global probe-card makers have not adopted Semcns ceramic STF, while Technoprobe of Italy plans to apply the product to its HBM probe cards. The high barrier to entry in ceramic substrate technology is cited as a strength. TSE, a listed peer in the test process, is also cited as a Semcns customer.

Outlook

In a 2026 press report, the company set a goal of becoming a global advanced semiconductor materials and parts company growing at an average annual rate above 40% through 2027.

To pursue it, the company is raising utilization at its new Osong plant and expanding toward a broader semiconductor materials and parts platform.

On April 13, 2026, the Korea Development Bank visited the Osong plant, which was selected for support under the advanced strategic industry fund, to check the progress of facility investment. The company plans to use these funds to expand the plant to match global chip demand.

A material-matter report on the acquisition of tangible assets was disclosed on October 15, 2026. On HBM, press reports from November 2025 said Samsung Electronics and the company are jointly developing customized HBM ceramic substrates.

Kiwoom Securities said in a May 8, 2026 report that the company could be a major beneficiary of the HBM era. On overseas customers, a November 2025 report said the company would supply STF for logic chip testing to Eahrtest Systems in the United States.

Meritz Securities said in a November 2025 report that DRAM sales growth and a NAND recovery would coincide in 2026. These outlooks rest on broker estimates and press reports rather than official company guidance.

The next points to confirm are the fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 preliminary results and the segment sales breakdown in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Expansion into DRAM and HBM

On a Q3 2025 basis, DRAM STF sales were KRW 14.3 billion, and the DRAM share of sales was about 23% in 2024. Meritz Securities said in a November 2025 report that the company could benefit from wider HBM probe-card supply. Kiwoom Securities also highlighted the company's potential to benefit in the HBM era in a May 8, 2026 report.

Profit recovery for three straight quarters

Operating profit rose for three consecutive quarters after the Q4 2025 low, reaching KRW 7.68 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 9.53 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 11.2 billion in Q3 2026. Q3 2026 operating profit was up 105.9% year on year. The operating margin was 18.8% in 2025, recovered from the 2023 loss. Press reports also said DRAM products are more profitable than NAND products.

Global customer references

According to the company's IR materials, it deals directly with Samsung Electronics, SK hynix, Intel, Micron and TSMC. A November 2025 press report said it is jointly developing customized HBM ceramic substrates with Samsung Electronics. Another November 2025 report said it will supply STF for logic chip testing to a US customer.

NAND concentration

NAND ceramic STF accounted for 72% of sales on a Q3 2025 basis, leaving the company sensitive to the NAND cycle and customer volumes. NAND volume growth has relied heavily on Kioxia in Japan and YMTC in China. DRAM's share is rising, but it has not yet reached a scale that replaces NAND.

09

Bear factors

Earnings volatility and uneven profit flow-through

Sales fell 38.3% in 2023, producing an operating loss of KRW 2.91 billion and a net loss of KRW 1.31 billion. In Q3 2026, operating profit rose 17.2% quarter on quarter, but net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 7.7%. The cause of this gap will be clarified in the income statement details of the next annual report.

Capacity and depreciation burden

Assets related to the Osong plant total KRW 117.7 billion, and press reports said they are pledged as collateral for long-term borrowings from the Korea Development Bank of about KRW 54.6 billion. Average utilization was reported at 54.1% through Q3 2025, and depreciation was cited as a burden. If utilization does not rise, fixed costs could weigh on profit.

HBM qualification risk

Press analysis suggests that expanded HBM probe-card supply depends on customer qualification. If qualification is delayed, the timing of higher supply volumes could also slip. Expectations for HBM rest partly on broker estimates and remain volatile until they show up in reported sales.

10

Risk factors

Customer qualification and HBM supply

Analysts see customer qualification at major global memory makers as a key variable for medium-term growth. If HBM probe cards fail to pass qualification, the potential for higher supply volumes would be limited. Changes in orders from probe-card makers could also affect the revenue mix.

Industry cycle and utilization

Test parts follow chip output, so a slowdown in the memory cycle could lead to lower orders. With average utilization reported at 54.1% through Q3 2025, a demand slowdown would increase the fixed-cost burden. Because capacity investment has grown, depreciation could also weigh for a long period.

Governance and financial structure

The largest shareholder is WYC, whose name changed from WYK in March 2024 after a shareholder meeting. Vice Chairman Choi Yu-jin has served as CEO since 2020. Because Osong plant assets are pledged against borrowings, financing conditions are also a point to watch.

Overall view

Semcns makes ceramic STF parts for probe cards. After an operating loss in 2023, it posted an operating profit of KRW 14.7 billion in 2025, and operating profit has risen for three consecutive quarters through Q3 2026.

The revenue mix still leans toward NAND, but the DRAM share has grown from about 23% in 2024 to KRW 14.3 billion on a Q3 2025 basis. Strengths include wider DRAM and HBM demand, global customer references and a lower debt-to-equity ratio of 52.4% in 2025.

Weaknesses include NAND concentration, the decline in net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026, and depreciation at the Osong plant. Total liabilities were KRW 87.2 billion at the end of 2025 and operating cash flow was KRW 19.2 billion in 2025.

Whether the recovery lasts will be clarified by the Q4 and full-year 2026 preliminary results and the segment sales in the 2026 annual report.
