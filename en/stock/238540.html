02

Business structure

NanoSolution is a materials company that develops and mass-produces carbon nanotubes (CNT), and it was founded in 2006. Its main products are single-walled carbon nanotubes (SWCNT), nanomaterial dispersions, and functional coating solutions that use them.

Press coverage described the company as holding technology to synthesize SWCNT through arc discharge. According to the company, its capabilities extend from dispersion, which turns powder into solutions, to coating solution manufacturing.

CEO Kim Hyung-yeol has said that NanoSolution is the only company able to carry out this entire process. Its clearest application is antistatic coating solutions for automotive displays, which have been supplied exclusively to LG Display since 2017.

In batteries, the company is preparing to use CNT as a conductive agent for silicon anodes and is at the stage of application testing with battery makers.

On the production side, the Iksan plant sits on a 24,394-square-meter site in Iksan's third industrial complex, with KRW 17.7 billion invested in CNT and dispersion capacity for display and battery electrode uses.

The Iksan plant held its completion ceremony in May 2026, and the company expects 27 new jobs from the facility. On the customer side, LG Display is the representative counterparty.

03

Recent trends

Sales for 2025 were KRW 6,244.4 million, down 38.7% from the prior year. Sales for 2024 were KRW 10,183.5 million. Operating profit was KRW 626.2 million in 2025, down 80.0% year on year, and the operating margin fell to 10.03% from 30.73% in 2024. Net income was KRW 1,340.1 million, down 54.7% from the prior year.

Pre-tax profit declined by less than operating profit because non-operating income rose. Based on figures compiled by JobKorea, cash flow from operating activities in 2025 was negative KRW 1,718.0 million, and investing cash flow was negative KRW 12,553.7 million.

Total assets rose to KRW 34,280.3 million in the same year, and the borrowing dependence ratio rose to 20.46% from 6.12% in 2024. Equity stood at KRW 25,611.1 million, with an equity ratio of 74.71%. The company raised KRW 16.0 billion in a Series A round in May 2025.

It filed its KONEX listing application on July 6, 2026. As of November 11, 2026, the share price was KRW 103,800, unchanged from the previous close, with zero trading value.

04

Outlook

The Iksan plant expands the company's capacity for CNT and dispersion production. In an August 2025 interview, the CEO said that mass production of silicon anode materials after the plant's completion could more than double current sales.

That outlook depends on whether battery application tests convert into volume production. The company has said the KRW 16.0 billion raised in its Series A round will go to facility expansion at Iksan and a second Jeonju plant. According to August 2025 reporting, the company has targeted a KOSDAQ listing before 2028.

With 2025 sales down 38.7% and operating profit down 80.0%, the speed at which the expansion translates into sales and profit is the key variable. In displays, whether LG Display's exclusive supply continues is the reference point for revenue stability.

KONEX saw 50 companies list in 2016 but fewer than 20 a year since 2019, a backdrop that may keep trading liquidity constrained. The next checkpoint is whether the 2026 quarterly disclosures show a sales recovery and revenue from anode materials.

05

Bull factors

Expanded production base

The Iksan plant is a production base completed on a 24,394-square-meter site in Iksan's third industrial complex, with KRW 17.7 billion invested.

It produces CNT and dispersions for display and battery electrode materials together, and it has been introduced as a hub serving diverse demand, including large corporate customers.

If customer demand grows, the production assets provide the physical foundation for sales expansion, which makes this a strength on the asset side.

Exclusive supply relationship

Press coverage in August 2025 reported that the company has supplied automotive antistatic coating solutions exclusively to LG Display since 2017. The coating uses SWCNT as a core material and is applied in automotive display manufacturing that requires static prevention. If this supply relationship continues over the long term, it adds predictability to the revenue base.

Funding and technical recognition

A KRW 16.0 billion Series A round in May 2025 secured funding for equipment investment.

In December 2025, the company was designated an advanced technology firm by the Ministry of Science and ICT, and in July 2025 it received a Prime Minister's Award in the industrial technology category at an international nanotechnology conference.

A series of external technical certifications provides third-party evidence of R&D capability, which is a basis for the bull case.

06

Bear factors

Simultaneous decline in scale and profit

Operating profit fell 80.0% to KRW 626.2 million in 2025, a decline much steeper than the drop in sales. The operating margin fell to 10.03% from 30.73% in 2024. Because the sales decline passed through heavily into profit, the path to restoring profitability is narrow without a sales recovery.

Weak cash generation and borrowing reliance

Cash flow from operating activities was negative KRW 1,718.0 million in 2025, and investing cash flow was negative KRW 12,553.7 million. Non-current liabilities rose to KRW 6,833.1 million, and the borrowing dependence ratio increased from 6.12% in 2024 to 20.46%. Expansion spending has been funded by outside capital and debt rather than operating cash, which is a weakness.

Battery business not yet proven

The battery conductive agent business has been reported as being in application testing with battery makers. The CEO has said that mass production of anode materials could more than double current sales, but this outlook has not yet been demonstrated through volume production.

If the gap between passing tests and converting to volume production lengthens, the recovery in utilization of the new capacity could be delayed.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Demand for display and battery materials depends heavily on set makers' capital spending and downstream industry cycles. If investment by large display or battery customers declines, orders for CNT coating solutions and conductive agents could fall as well.

Raw material price swings and pressure from customers for lower prices also represent paths that could weigh on profitability.

Liquidity

NanoSolution is listed on KONEX, and trading value on the reference date was zero. The number of new KONEX listings fell from 50 in 2016 to fewer than 20 per year after 2019, leaving the market less active. With a market capitalization of KRW 116.0 billion, thin trading could constrain price formation.

Execution

Whether the Iksan plant ramps up utilization and whether anode material moves into volume production as planned are the key issues. If customer qualification is delayed or volume pricing terms do not work, the payback period of the expansion investment could change. The target of a KOSDAQ transfer listing before 2028 is also tied to the pace of earnings recovery.

08

Overall view

NanoSolution is a materials company with CNT dispersion and coating technology, an Iksan production base, and an exclusive supply relationship with LG Display. Sales fell 38.7% in 2025 and operating profit fell 80.0%, so both scale and profitability weakened at the same time.

The equity ratio of 74.71% and the KRW 16.0 billion Series A round serve as financial buffers. On the other hand, negative operating cash flow and a higher borrowing dependence ratio show that turning expansion into cash generation remains an open task.

Anode conductive agents are still at the testing stage, so the timing of their contribution to results is uncertain. A trading value of zero and a market capitalization of KRW 116.0 billion point to liquidity constraints on the KONEX market.

Strengths lie in production capacity and technical certifications, while weaknesses lie in earnings power and cash flow. The core indicator from here is whether the 2026 quarterly disclosures show a sales recovery and improving cash flow after the expansion.
