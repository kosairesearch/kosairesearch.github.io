02

Business structure

NanoSolution is a CNT materials company founded on December 22, 2006, with 55 employees as of 2026. It is headquartered in Deokjin-gu, Jeonju, in North Jeolla Province. Its website says it has vertically integrated the whole CNT value chain, from powder synthesis through dispersion and coating.

In news coverage of its exchange filing, it is classified as an other-chemical-products maker whose main products include CNT coating solutions. Its core application is an anti-static coating for automotive displays.

According to ETNews (August 26, 2025), the SWCNT-based coating has been supplied exclusively to LG Display since 2017. The second pillar is secondary-battery materials, where it promotes a water-based SWCNT dispersion and a primer coating for anodes.

As described in the August 2025 report, it had secured anode conductive-additive dispersion technology and was running application tests with battery makers. No confirmed public figures on revenue by segment were found, so the mix is discussed only qualitatively.

On the competitive front, Advanced Nano Products, a separate listed company focused on CNT conductive additives for batteries, is the natural comparison. Shinhan Investment Corp. data show Advanced Nano Products' preliminary 2022 revenue at KRW 79.9 billion.

NanoSolution's 2025 revenue is far smaller, so the scale gap is wide. NanoSolution does focus on automotive display coatings and SWCNT dispersion, so the two companies' businesses do not overlap completely.

03

Recent trends

The Korea Exchange received NanoSolution's KONEX new-listing application on July 6, 2026, with Korea Investment & Securities as designated adviser. Financial News (August 23, 2026) reported that only three companies listed newly on KONEX in 2026: S-TechM, Songwoo Infotech and NanoSolution.

On 2025 financials, equity was KRW 25.611 billion, revenue was KRW 6.244 billion and net income was KRW 1.34 billion. Company-data aggregators show 2025 revenue down 38.7% year on year and operating profit down 80.0% to KRW 626.2 million.

In the same data, the operating margin fell from 30.73% in 2024 to 10.03% in 2025. Net income stayed above KRW 1.3 billion despite the fall in operating profit because non-operating income was larger than operating profit. Non-operating income was recorded at about KRW 1.31 billion.

The same data show negative operating cash flow and a large cash outflow from investing activities, while total assets more than doubled.

In market data, the share price is KRW 103,800, unchanged from the prior day, with zero trading value. Market capitalization is KRW 116.0 billion. A simple calculation against 2025 equity and net income puts market capitalization at roughly 4.5 times equity and roughly 87 times net income.

This ratio does not reflect results for recent quarters. An August 2026 report said average daily trading value across KONEX fell 23% year on year. With trading this thin, closing prices may carry limited price-discovery value.

04

Outlook

The company raised a KRW 16 billion Series A round, and the funds are directed to facility expansion including the Iksan plant and a second Jeonju plant.

CEO Kim Hyung-yeol said that once the Iksan plant is completed and mass-produces anode materials for secondary batteries, revenue could more than double from its level at the time. This is a management statement from August 2025, and the actual start-up timing and customer qualification need separate confirmation.

The company also targets a KOSDAQ listing before 2028. The KONEX environment, however, is not supportive. Since KONEX opened in 2013, an average of 8.5 companies a year have moved up to KOSDAQ, but as of August 23, 2026 there had been none in 2026.

Shinhan Investment Corp. wrote in a 2023 report that SWCNT demand is expected to move in step with the silicon-anode market. For that view to apply to NanoSolution, its anode dispersion would need to be adopted on customers' mass-production lines.

Items to watch are results for 2026 periods, progress on the capacity build-out and the stage of application at battery customers.

05

Bull factors

Integrated process and an established customer

The company says it covers the whole chain from CNT powder synthesis to dispersion and coating. The CEO claims NanoSolution is the only company able to carry out the entire process. It also has a record of supplying an automotive anti-static coating exclusively to LG Display since 2017. A long supply record can be read as evidence of having passed customer qualification.

Expansion funding and a sound balance sheet

The KRW 16 billion Series A proceeds go to expanding the Iksan plant and a second Jeonju plant. Aggregator data show a 2025 equity ratio of 74.71% and a debt ratio of 33.85%. The low financial burden gives some cushion during the build-out. If battery materials begin to contribute to revenue, the business mix could broaden.

Potential SWCNT demand growth in batteries

Using CNTs as a battery conductive additive helps buffer silicon expansion and supports higher energy density. Shinhan Investment Corp. expects SWCNT to move in step with growth in the silicon-anode market. Aggregator data show the 2025 operating margin fell to 10.03%, but profitability remained positive. If the customer base widens, plant utilization and margins have room to improve.

06

Bear factors

2025 revenue and operating profit both fell

In 2025, revenue fell 38.7% and operating profit fell 80.0% year on year. Net income was supported by non-operating income, which makes it hard to gauge core earning power. No official company explanation for the decline was found. Whether results recovered in 2026 has not yet been confirmed.

Customer concentration and early-stage battery business

The automotive coating is supplied exclusively to LG Display, so dependence on a single customer may be high. The battery conductive additive was at the application-testing stage with battery makers as of the August 2025 report. Whether and when it will be adopted in mass production is uncertain. This field also has listed competitors with much larger revenue.

Thin liquidity and market-cap burden

Trading value is currently zero, so it is hard to judge whether the quoted price fully reflects market assessment. A market capitalization of KRW 116.0 billion equals about 4.5 times 2025 equity and about 87 times 2025 net income on a simple calculation.

Some may argue that sustaining this level requires the capacity expansion to show up in earnings. With little trading, small orders can move the price sharply.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Automotive displays and secondary batteries are both exposed to the auto and EV demand cycle. If demand slows in end markets, customer orders and plant utilization could decline. It has not been confirmed whether demand factors lay behind the 2025 revenue decline. Exchange-rate and raw-material price moves could also affect margins.

Business and execution

The expansion is concentrated in the Iksan plant and a second Jeonju plant. If completion is delayed or mass-production yields do not stabilize, the investment burden could rise before any revenue benefit. Customer qualification and the timing of new orders are hard for the company to control. In 2025, investing cash outflow was large and operating cash flow was negative.

Market and structural

Average daily trading value on KONEX is falling, and as of August 23, 2026 there had been no transfer to KOSDAQ. Even with a KOSDAQ target, the schedule and review outcome are uncertain. Low liquidity makes it hard for investors to trade at the time and price they want. Changes to KONEX-specific rules could also affect the investment environment.

08

Overall view

NanoSolution is a small materials company with an in-house process from SWCNT synthesis to coating and a long supply record in automotive display coatings. In 2025, revenue and operating profit fell sharply, and net income relied heavily on non-operating income.

Bulls point to expansion funding, a low debt burden and the potential growth of SWCNT demand in batteries. Bears point to the earnings decline, customer concentration, the early stage of the battery business and thin KONEX liquidity.

With zero trading value, the quoted price is hard to treat as a consensus market assessment. Items to confirm going forward are 2026 results, the start-up timing of the Iksan plant and adoption by battery customers. This report is for information only and leaves any judgment on direction to the reader.
