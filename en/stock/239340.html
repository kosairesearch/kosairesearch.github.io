Report overview

AI portal pivot amid losses: earnings recovery is the test

ESTaid has posted losses for four straight years from 2022 to 2025 and for five consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, so whether the ZUM AI portal pivot translates into earnings is the central question.

  1. 1

    Annual revenue fell from KRW 22.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.6 billion in 2025, and the 2025 operating loss was KRW 5.58 billion.

  2. 2

    Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 3.63 billion, down 5.0% year on year, and the operating loss widened to KRW 1.59 billion from a year earlier.

  3. 3

    The debt ratio rose from 43.1% in 2022 to 121.6% in 2025, and total liabilities of KRW 18.4 billion at the end of 2025 exceed total equity of KRW 15.1 billion.

  4. 4

    A large share of revenue comes from an asset management subsidiary, while advertising revenue at the portal business has been cited as a drag on results.

  5. 5

    The November 2026 extraordinary shareholders' meeting and the planned January 2027 increase in the KOSDAQ market-capitalization threshold are the next checkpoints.

02

Business structure

ESTaid's parent ESTsoft develops the AI technology, while ESTaid operates portal, content and advertising platforms built on it, according to Blotter. The company changed its name from Zum Internet in March 2024 to signal its shift toward AI, and moved to single-CEO management the same year.

Revenue is concentrated in subsidiaries: Blotter reported that 77% of consolidated revenue in the first quarter of 2026 came from an asset management subsidiary, while ESTaid's standalone revenue was KRW 900 million. FnGuide's company profile puts the asset management segment at 67.77% of revenue, the largest share.

In the portal business, ZUM was positioned in July 2025 as the first AI search portal in Korea, followed by an AI tarot chat launch on vonvon in September 2025 and mevu's launch in North America and Japan in November 2025. FnGuide reported that advertising revenue at the portal business declined.

In 2026 the company is participating in an independent AI foundation model project within the LG AI Research consortium. In portal and AI search it competes in the same market as large platforms such as NAVER and Kakao.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue declining while losses persisted. Revenue fell from KRW 22.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.8 billion in 2023, a 39.6% decrease, recovered to KRW 16.4 billion in 2024 (up 18.5%), and declined to KRW 15.6 billion in 2025 (down 5.0%).

Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for revenue and 2023 the smallest. The operating loss widened from KRW 726 million in 2022 to KRW 6.18 billion in 2023, narrowed to KRW 4.81 billion in 2024, and widened again to KRW 5.58 billion in 2025.

The operating margin in 2023, at -44.6%, was the lowest of the four years. In 2024 revenue rose and the operating loss narrowed, whereas in 2025 revenue fell and the operating loss widened.

Operating cash flow moved from KRW 2.22 billion in 2022 and KRW 1.1 billion in 2023 to -KRW 907 million in 2024 and -KRW 9.39 billion in 2025. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.47 billion in 2022, KRW 7.26 billion in 2023, KRW 5.03 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.39 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 3.82 billion in the third quarter of 2025 with an operating loss of KRW 1.06 billion, fell to KRW 2.66 billion in the fourth quarter, and rose to KRW 4.11 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 54.4% quarter on quarter), while the operating loss widened to KRW 1.92 billion.

The operating loss of KRW 556 million in the second quarter of 2026 was the smallest of the last five quarters, but it widened to KRW 1.59 billion in the third quarter. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 6.44 billion.

Blotter reported first-quarter 2026 consolidated revenue down 14.6% year on year, and FnGuide reported that cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2025 fell 3.3% while the operating loss rose 68.1%.

05

Industry analysis

ESTaid's business relies heavily on advertising revenue built on portal traffic. FnGuide attributed the deterioration in results to declining advertising revenue and higher costs at the portal business. The company is pursuing a model that embeds advertising naturally into AI conversational search results.

On the technology side, the parent handles AI models and software while ESTaid runs the platforms. In Korea's portal market it competes with large platforms such as NAVER and Kakao, and because its asset management subsidiary accounts for a large share of revenue, its portal results are difficult to isolate.

Government-led AI projects are cited as growth opportunities at the group level, but project scale and timing of revenue contribution have not been disclosed.

On the regulatory side, listing requirements for stocks trading below KRW 1,000 were tightened, and the market-capitalization threshold for KOSDAQ companies was raised from KRW 15 billion to KRW 20 billion, with a further increase to KRW 30 billion reported for January 2027. These thresholds apply to small caps broadly and should be considered separately from company-specific factors.

06

Outlook

The company says it will upgrade ZUM into an open AI portal and strengthen revenue models for AI content and advertising. It is pursuing business expansion through external partnerships, alongside AI-based organizational efficiency and cost reduction.

According to Blotter, this direction is presented as central to recovering the core business. At the group level, ESTaid plans to stay in the LG AI Research consortium's independent AI foundation model project and to pursue additional government AI projects.

The company says it is reviewing various measures to enhance corporate value but has not reached the stage of announcing specific policies.

At the regular shareholders' meeting in March 2026, digital content production, character licensing, electronic finance and education-related businesses were added to the stated business purposes.

At the extraordinary meeting in October 2026, an amendment to the purpose clause was voted down, while provisions on convertible and bond-with-warrant issuance passed. The extraordinary meeting scheduled for November 27, 2026 was called without stated agenda items, and the company said it would disclose them later.

Since the operating loss narrowed to KRW 556 million in the second quarter of 2026, whether quarterly losses narrow again is the next point to watch.

08

Bull factors

Group AI technology base behind the portal pivot

Parent ESTsoft handles AI models and software, while ESTaid runs the portal and content platforms. The company says it is upgrading ZUM into an open AI portal. Its participation in the LG AI Research consortium's independent AI foundation model project is cited as a route into government-led AI work.

Quarters with narrower operating losses

Operating loss in the second quarter of 2026 was KRW 556 million, the smallest of the last five quarters. In 2024 revenue rose 18.5% and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 4.81 billion. Cost efficiency and AI-based organizational work are cited as parallel efforts that could support further narrowing.

Expanded business purpose and partnership plans

At the March 2026 regular shareholders' meeting, digital content production, character development and licensing, electronic finance and education-related businesses were added to the stated purposes. The company says it will pursue business expansion through external partnerships. The expanded purpose clause is cited as a basis for identifying new revenue sources.

09

Bear factors

Four straight years of losses and weaker cash flow

The company recorded losses for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, and operating cash flow shifted from KRW 2.22 billion in 2022 to -KRW 9.39 billion in 2025. Losses have continued for five consecutive quarters, and the operating loss widened to KRW 1.59 billion in the third quarter of 2026 from a year earlier. The lack of sustained quarterly improvement is the main bearish factor.

Revenue decline and dependence on a subsidiary

Revenue fell from KRW 22.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.6 billion in 2025, and third-quarter 2026 revenue was down 5.0% year on year. Blotter reported that 77% of first-quarter 2026 consolidated revenue came from an asset management subsidiary, with standalone revenue of KRW 900 million. The bearish case is that results depend heavily on a subsidiary while the core portal base is weaker.

Rising liabilities and shrinking equity cushion

The debt ratio rose from 43.1% in 2022 to 72.5% in 2024 and 121.6% in 2025. Total liabilities of KRW 18.4 billion at the end of 2025 exceed total equity of KRW 15.1 billion. With losses continuing, rising liabilities reduce financial flexibility, which is the bearish point.

10

Risk factors

Market alerts and share price volatility

The stock was designated a caution stock on June 5, 2026 for concentrated trading in a small number of accounts, and again on August 7, 2026 after a rise of more than 60% over five trading days. Under the exchange's alert system, designations can escalate, and trading may be suspended at the warning stage. Pronounced price volatility is itself a risk.

Governance and financing structure

At the October 2026 extraordinary meeting, the amendment to the purpose clause was voted down, and the independent and inside director appointments were withdrawn after the candidates stepped down. Amendments on convertible bond and bond-with-warrant issuance passed at the same meeting.

These provisions leave open the possibility of share count increases depending on future financing, so their use needs to be monitored.

Dependence on government AI projects and the parent

The parent ESTsoft develops the AI technology while ESTaid operates the platforms that use it. Participation in the LG AI Research consortium's independent AI foundation model project is cited as an opportunity, but its scale and revenue timing have not been disclosed.

Earnings outlooks could shift considerably depending on group technology and the outcome of government projects.

12

Overall view

ESTaid is reorganizing around its AI portal pivot with its parent, but results remain loss-making. Annual revenue fell from KRW 22.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.6 billion in 2025, the 2025 operating loss was KRW 5.58 billion, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.39 billion.

Losses have persisted for five consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 6.44 billion.

On the constructive side are the group's AI technology base, participation in government AI projects, the narrower operating loss in the second quarter of 2026, and the expanded business purpose.

On the cautious side are the rising debt ratio, dependence on an asset management subsidiary, declining advertising revenue, caution-stock designations, and governance items. The next checkpoints are the November 27, 2026 extraordinary meeting, the January 2027 threshold increase and the 2026 annual business report.

Whether the operating margin and operating cash flow improve quarter by quarter is the most direct measure of the AI portal pivot's progress.
