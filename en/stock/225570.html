NEXON Games: Live Revenue Slide Meets a Pipeline Gap

Revenue still rests on live titles such as Blue Archive, Sudden Attack and The First Descendant, but mobile declines and a wider Q3 2026 operating loss have weakened the earnings trend. The next checkpoints are new-title timing and 4Q 2026 profitability.

02

Business structure

NEXON Games develops games for PC, mobile and console. Of the KRW 38.6 billion in Q3 2026 revenue, PC and console contributed KRW 26.1 billion, or about 67.5%.

Mobile revenue in the same quarter was KRW 10.9 billion, down 51.3% year on year, which the company attributed to lower revenue from major titles such as Blue Archive. By region, domestic revenue was KRW 29.66 billion and overseas revenue was KRW 8.99 billion.

Its main live titles are the mobile subculture RPG Blue Archive, the FPS Sudden Attack, and the PC and console shooter The First Descendant. Blue Archive held a global 5th anniversary festival on November 7 and 8, 2026.

In development are Dungeon Fighter Idle (a mobile idle RPG), Dungeon Fighter Arad (an open-world action RPG), Project RX (a PC and mobile subculture title), Uchi: The Wayfarer (a PC and console single-player action adventure based on a Korean classic), and Project DX, built on the Durango IP.

The company said it is preparing Dungeon Fighter Idle for a first-half 2027 launch, and no release timing has been disclosed for the other new titles. The Dungeon Fighter IP is a flagship IP of parent NEXON Co., Ltd., and NEXON Games develops under an IP use agreement with Neople.

On competition, it faces listed peers such as Netmarble, Pearl Abyss and Krafton, with live-IP operating capability as the common battleground.

04

Earnings analysis

In the annual DART-filed results, revenue rose from KRW 132.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 193.3 billion in 2023 (up 46.0%) and KRW 256.1 billion in 2024 (up 32.5%), making 2024 the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Revenue fell 30.0% to KRW 179.3 billion in 2025.

Operating profit grew from KRW 5.16 billion in 2022 to KRW 12 billion in 2023 (up 133.0%) and KRW 38.7 billion in 2024 (up 222.4%), then turned into an operating loss of KRW 60.2 billion in 2025. The operating margin fell from 15.1% in 2024 to -33.6% in 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 31.4 billion in 2024, and in 2025 it turned into a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 61.8 billion. Net income including non-controlling interests was also a net loss of KRW 61.8 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 66.5 billion in 2024 and -KRW 42.7 billion in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 37.3% in 2022 to 58.7% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 254.9 billion, of which controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 254.8 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 149.6 billion.

On a quarterly basis, revenue went from KRW 50.2 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 39.3 billion in Q4 2025 (down 21.7% quarter on quarter), then recovered to KRW 41.5 billion in Q1 2026.

It then fell to KRW 41.5 billion in Q2 2026 (down 0.1% quarter on quarter) and KRW 38.6 billion in Q3 2026 (down 6.8% quarter on quarter and down 22.9% year on year).

Operating results went from a KRW 10.5 billion loss in Q3 2025 and a KRW 23.1 billion loss in Q4 2025 to a KRW 21.1 billion loss in Q1 2026, then a KRW 10.7 billion operating profit in Q2 2026, which turned positive.

Q3 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 22.1 billion, wider than the KRW 10.5 billion loss a year earlier. Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 8.65 billion in Q2 2026, but Q3 2026 showed a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 28.8 billion.

The controlling-shareholder net loss over the latest four quarters totals KRW 69.1 billion.

Cumulative revenue for the first nine months of 2026 was KRW 121.6 billion, down 13.1% year on year, and the cumulative operating loss narrowed to KRW 32.5 billion from KRW 37.2 billion a year earlier, according to press coverage.

Q3 2026 operating expenses were KRW 60.7 billion, roughly flat year on year, and personnel costs were KRW 38.3 billion. The company explained that the 97.1% rise in expenses from the prior quarter reflects a base effect from a one-time cost reversal in the earlier quarter.

05

Industry analysis

Within the game sector, Q3 2026 results diverged by company. Parent NEXON Co., Ltd., listed in Tokyo, announced Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 1.23 trillion and operating profit of KRW 371.8 billion, up 17% and 11% year on year, with the Maple franchise driving growth.

NEXON Games, by contrast, saw revenue fall 22.9% year on year in the same quarter. Its PC and console revenue rose only 1.8% year on year, and the decline in mobile revenue drove the overall drop. The contrast shows how reliance on a few IPs can increase quarterly earnings volatility.

Across the sector, coverage points to a second half of 2026 filled with new titles across MMORPG, sports, cooperative action and life simulation genres. When compared with listed peers such as Netmarble, Pearl Abyss and Krafton, the share of live IP revenue and the timing of new releases are the core comparison points.

Earlier in 2026, brokerage commentary cited the lack of new titles and falling revenue from existing games as reasons for the share price weakness.

Outlook

The nearest schedule item the company has indicated is the preparation of Dungeon Fighter Idle for a first-half 2027 launch. The game moves the Dungeon Fighter IP into the idle mobile genre, and whether it can serve as a new revenue source while mobile revenue is declining is the key question.

NEXON Games said it is focusing on raising the completeness of its new titles and plans to disclose information on them in sequence.

Uchi: The Wayfarer was reported in September 2026 to be drawing on Korean cultural heritage material through a tie-up with the Korea Tourism Organization, but no release date has been disclosed.

Project RX is a subculture title drawing on the know-how of the Blue Archive development team, and no specific schedule has been announced.

On the live side, Blue Archive held its 5th anniversary event in early November 2026, and The First Descendant received a large update on October 15, 2026 with reworked key successors and new weapons. How these two titles' operating performance feeds into revenue after Q4 is the next checkpoint.

The Q4 2026 results and the 2026 annual report have not yet been filed. Whether Q4 revenue recovers from the KRW 38.6 billion level of Q3 and whether the operating loss pattern continues will be the key facts for reading full-year results.

08

Bull factors

A live-IP revenue base

Live titles including Blue Archive, Sudden Attack and The First Descendant remain the revenue base. Q3 2026 PC and console revenue rose 6.5% quarter on quarter to KRW 26.1 billion, which the company attributed to updates in Sudden Attack and The First Descendant.

Blue Archive held its global 5th anniversary event in November 2026 as it continues long-term operations. The quarter-on-quarter increase in PC and console revenue shows that live operations are holding a certain level of revenue on a quarterly basis.

The Q2 2026 return to profit

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 10.7 billion in Q2 2026, and controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 8.65 billion. Revenue in the same quarter held at KRW 41.5 billion, the level of Q1.

Q3 returned to an operating loss, but the fact that the quarter moved back into profit shows some room to adjust the cost structure. Q3 operating expenses rose only 0.2% year on year, which indicates that cost control was maintained.

A diversified new-title pipeline

Dungeon Fighter Idle is being prepared for a first-half 2027 launch, and several new titles across genres are in development, including Dungeon Fighter Arad, Project RX, Uchi: The Wayfarer and Project DX. Uchi: The Wayfarer is the company's first single-player game and targets a global PC and console release.

If these titles launch, the mix could shift toward PC and console alongside mobile. However, only a limited number of titles have confirmed release timing.

Bear factors

Continued revenue decline

Revenue fell 30.0% to KRW 179.3 billion in 2025, and Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 38.6 billion was down 22.9% year on year. Revenue declined quarter on quarter in both Q2 and Q3 2026. Mobile revenue fell 51.3% year on year in Q3, so the drop in major titles drove the overall decline. Whether the decline continues after Q4 2026 is the central checkpoint for the bearish case.

A return to a loss-making structure

The company swung to an operating loss of KRW 60.2 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 61.8 billion in 2025, and the Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 22.1 billion was wider than the KRW 10.5 billion loss a year earlier.

The controlling-shareholder net loss over the latest four quarters totals KRW 69.1 billion. Operating cash flow was negative at -KRW 42.7 billion in 2025. The Q2 2026 return to profit lasted only one quarter, so the earnings structure cannot yet be described as stable.

A new-title gap and timing uncertainty

Uchi: The Wayfarer, Project RX and Project DX have no announced release dates. Commentary also said The First Descendant had fallen short of expectations. The company has said it will disclose new-title information in sequence, but the timing of disclosures and commercial results remain uncertain.

Risk factors

Concentration on live IPs and mobile decline

Revenue is concentrated in a few live titles, including Blue Archive, Sudden Attack and The First Descendant. Q3 2026 mobile revenue of KRW 10.9 billion fell 51.3% year on year. User attrition or changes in operating policy at a single title can directly affect quarterly results. If new titles do not launch on time, this concentration will keep earnings volatile.

Changes in the financial structure

The debt ratio rose from 37.3% in 2022 to 58.7% in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 254.9 billion and total liabilities were KRW 149.6 billion. Operating cash flow turned negative at -KRW 42.7 billion in 2025. If losses continue, the company's capital capacity and cash generation will need to be watched together.

IP agreements and dependence on the parent

Changes to the terms of the IP use agreement with Neople or to the parent's IP strategy could affect new-title schedules and revenue sharing. The parent's Q3 2026 results grew on the same day that the subsidiary's revenue declined, so the two companies' earnings are moving in different directions.

Overall view

NEXON Games' Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 38.6 billion fell 22.9% year on year, and its operating loss of KRW 22.1 billion widened.

The 2025 annual results filed with DART also show a swing to losses, with revenue of KRW 179.3 billion, an operating loss of KRW 60.2 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 61.8 billion.

The fall in operating margin from 15.1% in 2024 to -33.6% in 2025 marks a change in the direction of the earnings structure. On the other side, Q2 2026 included a quarter with an operating profit of KRW 10.7 billion, and Q3 PC and console revenue rose from the prior quarter.

The bullish case rests on the live-IP base, the new-title pipeline including Dungeon Fighter Idle, and cost control. The bearish case rests on the continued revenue decline, a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 69.1 billion over the latest four quarters, and new titles with uncertain release schedules.

Because no earnings-based multiple can be calculated, comparison on a net-asset basis is central to valuation. The next checkpoints are the Q4 results that will appear in the 2026 annual report and the first-half 2027 launch of Dungeon Fighter Idle.

The divergence between parent NEXON's growth and the subsidiary's results shows the difference in the two companies' business structures.
