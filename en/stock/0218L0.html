02

Business structure

Neovue was created through a spin-off of the automotive display unit from its parent TOBIS, with July 1, 2026 as the spin-off date and KOSDAQ re-listing on July 27, 2026. Its core products are in-vehicle dual and single displays, HVAC temperature-control displays and digital touch keys for automotive electronics.

The business extends across digital cockpits, instrument clusters and infotainment, and the company frames the shift from physical buttons to touch displays as its growth backdrop (securities registration statement).

Within the parent, this unit's share of revenue rose from 39.2% in 2023 to 51.9% in 2024 and 55.2% in 2025 (securities registration statement).

Press reports put the unit's 2025 revenue at KRW 353.5 billion, above the parent's remaining unit at KRW 286.7 billion, and its share reached 56.6% of total revenue in the first quarter of 2026. Production capacity of about KRW 600 billion a year has been built in Seosan, South Chungcheong, and Dalian, China.

The customer base has widened from LG Display to Hyundai Mobis and DENSO Korea, and Continental, a Tier-1 supplier, has recently been added. LG Display and DENSO Korea still account for about 80% of sales, which makes customer concentration high.

Among listed sector peers, LG Display, LG Innotek and Samsung Electro-Mechanics operate in adjacent areas, but their business mix differs, so direct comparison is limited.

03

Recent trends

Based on the cumulative third-quarter 2026 results in the DART regular report, revenue was KRW 109.8 billion, operating profit KRW 4.8 billion and net income KRW 1.09 billion. The nine-month operating margin works out to the low-4% range, below the 5.4% first-quarter 2026 margin reported in the press.

Financial costs are cited as one reason net income stayed small relative to operating profit, and the half-year financial data in the securities registration statement also showed the spin-off entity's financial costs exceeding its financial income.

Press coverage put the debt-to-equity ratio at 158% and the borrowing dependence at 25.8%, with the capital expenditure burden attributed to the new entity.

Listing-related coverage cited 2024 standalone revenue of KRW 268.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 97.7 billion, but the period and basis differ from the nine-month disclosure, so direct comparison is difficult.

The share price opened at KRW 25,600 on July 27, 2026, fell to KRW 17,950 and closed limit-down, according to press reports. The price was KRW 10,110 at 14:23 KST on November 18, 2026, about 60% below the opening price, with trading value of KRW 100 million at the time of writing.

Major shareholders, including CEO Kim Yong-beom, agreed to joint exercise of voting rights for three years and mutual preemptive purchase rights, and a 17.85% stake was placed under voluntary lock-up, according to press reports.

04

Outlook

The operating indicators to track are the plant utilization rate and how quickly the order backlog converts into revenue. Press reports put first-quarter 2026 utilization at 79%, and absorbing fixed costs such as depreciation on KRW 145.0 billion of property, plant and equipment requires utilization to rise further.

The order backlog was reported at about KRW 500 billion, and fixed-cost absorption improves as utilization rises. If volumes from new customers such as Continental expand as planned, press coverage expects the customer base to become less concentrated around LG Display and DENSO Korea.

Before the spin-off, cash generated by the casino business helped fund automotive capital expenditure, but as an independent company Neovue must cover investment and borrowing needs from its own cash flow. Operating cash flow and the pace of debt repayment therefore serve as key gauges of the balance sheet.

Demand for digital cockpits, driven by the shift toward software-defined vehicles, is cited as a medium-term growth factor, while near-term results depend on vehicle sales and customer production schedules.

The next checkpoints are fourth-quarter 2026 results and the customer-level revenue breakdown in the 2026 annual business report.

05

Bull factors

Rising focus on automotive displays

The unit's share of parent revenue climbed from 39.2% in 2023 to 55.2% in 2025, and reached 56.6% of total revenue in the first quarter of 2026. Press reports put its 2025 revenue at KRW 353.5 billion, above the parent's remaining unit at KRW 286.7 billion.

The shift toward automotive gives the company a clearer identity as an independent business, which is a bullish factor.

Order backlog and capacity base

Press reports cite an order backlog of about KRW 500 billion. Production capacity of about KRW 600 billion a year has been built in Seosan and Dalian. If mass-production transitions continue against this backlog, revenue visibility could be a supportive factor.

Digital cockpit shift and customer expansion

The securities registration statement describes an accelerating digital cockpit trend, in which physical buttons are replaced by touch displays as vehicles shift to software-defined architectures. The customer base has expanded from LG Display to Hyundai Mobis, DENSO Korea and Continental. Press coverage expects a broader customer portfolio if volumes from new customers grow as planned.

06

Bear factors

Concentration on LG Display and DENSO Korea

Press reports say LG Display and DENSO Korea together account for about 80% of sales. Customer diversification is cited as a challenge, and because of the automotive supply chain, results can fluctuate with customers' production schedules and vehicle sales. The size of any shift in the customer mix will become visible in later customer-level revenue disclosures.

Thin margins and fixed-cost burden

Press reports say the first-quarter 2026 operating margin of 5.4% was below that of the parent TOBIS. Cumulative third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 4.8 billion and net income KRW 1.09 billion.

With utilization at 79%, the fixed-cost burden from depreciation on about KRW 145.0 billion of assets remains a weakness.

Borrowing burden and standalone balance sheet

Press reports cite a debt-to-equity ratio of 158% and borrowing dependence of 25.8%. After the spin-off, capital expenditure and debt service must be funded from the company's own cash flow without support from the casino business.

Financial costs exceeding financial income in the half-year data of the securities registration statement also count as a weakness.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Vehicle sales and automaker production schedules drive demand for automotive components. A slowdown in vehicle sales or production adjustments by customers could translate into earnings volatility. Macro demand changes flow directly into revenue through customer orders, which is the core exposure.

Customer concentration

LG Display and DENSO Korea are reported to account for about 80% of sales. A change in orders from a single customer can have a large effect on total revenue. The timing and scale of mass-production volumes from new customers such as Continental remain key variables.

Supply, demand and liquidity

Trading value at the time of writing was KRW 100 million, indicating a thin market. The share price has remained well below its opening level since the limit-down on the first listing day. The timing and volume of the release of the 17.85% stake under voluntary lock-up remain a supply variable.

08

Overall view

Neovue is a newly spun-off company focused on automotive displays, and its rising business share, production capacity and order backlog show the foundation for growth.

The DART figures for cumulative third-quarter 2026 (revenue of KRW 109.8 billion, operating profit of KRW 4.8 billion and net income of KRW 1.09 billion) highlight the gap between scale and profitability.

Bullish factors are the higher automotive share, the order backlog and efforts to diversify customers, while bearish factors are the concentration on LG Display and DENSO Korea, the fixed-cost burden at 79% utilization, and the balance sheet reported at a 158% debt-to-equity ratio.

Since listing, the share price has traded well below its opening level, and trading value of KRW 100 million adds a supply-side factor. The next checkpoints are improvement in utilization, the revenue contribution from new customer volumes and the trend in operating cash flow. This is a qualitative summary provided for information purposes.
