02

Business structure

Neovue was established when Tovis, a monitor and display maker, spun off its automotive display division. The Korea Exchange approved the relisting, and trading began on July 27, 2026. Its main products are dual and single car displays, climate-control displays and digital touch keys.

Its scope covers automotive displays broadly, including digital cockpits, clusters and infotainment. According to company IR material, it entered the automotive display business in 2018, building on its TFT-LCD know-how from mobile phone displays.

Before the split, this unit's share of Tovis's revenue rose steadily. It was 39.2% in 2023, 51.9% in 2024 and 55.2% in 2025. The customer mix is concentrated. LG Display and Denso Korea account for roughly 80% of sales.

The surviving company Tovis is a separate entity, and it supplies casino monitors and button decks to global slot machine makers such as IGT, Light & Wonder and Aristocrat. Neovue handles only the automotive business, not the casino and industrial lines.

No verified data on market share or peer-by-peer comparison was available, so competition is described qualitatively. Because its customers are large panel and parts makers, pricing leverage may be limited, which is a structural point to note for the industry.

03

Recent trends

On the spun-off unit's basis in the registration statement, revenue in 2023 was about KRW 134.7 billion, with an operating loss of about KRW 12.0 billion. In 2024, revenue was about KRW 268.3 billion and operating profit turned positive at about KRW 9.8 billion.

In 2025, revenue was about KRW 306.8 billion, operating profit about KRW 16.6 billion and net income about KRW 14.1 billion. Some press reports gave 2024 operating profit as KRW 97.7 billion, which differs in magnitude from the filing table, so this report follows the filing.

The first-quarter 2026 operating margin was 5.4%, lower than that of the surviving Tovis. Plant utilization was around 79% at that time, and the order backlog was reported at about KRW 500 billion. Capital expenditure was assigned to Neovue, leaving a debt-to-equity ratio of 158% and borrowings dependence of 25.8%. On the split date, consolidated assets were KRW 216.3 billion and equity was KRW 84.8 billion.

On the share price, the opening price on the relisting day was KRW 25,600 and the stock then fell sharply, sliding to the daily lower limit. Media interpreted this as reflecting both supply overhang and concern over standalone management.

On October 27, 2026, the stock stood at KRW 11,700 (-1.27% day on day), with a market capitalization of KRW 62.4 billion and trading value of KRW 400 million. The current price is under half the first-day opening price, and trading value is modest.

04

Outlook

The key item to watch is how fast the order backlog converts into revenue. Results in the automotive sector can fluctuate with customers' production schedules and vehicle sales. On operating metrics, utilization offers room for improvement.

With utilization around 79% in the first quarter of 2026, a rise could lower the fixed-cost burden and lift the operating margin. Reports note that utilization must rise further to absorb depreciation on KRW 145 billion of tangible assets added through capacity expansion.

An investment bank official assessed that the spin-off itself does not guarantee a rise in corporate value, and Neovue must prove order growth and better profitability. On governance, major shareholders agreed to exercise votes jointly and hold mutual rights of first refusal for three years after relisting.

This arrangement supports control stability, but it may separately affect free float and trading activity. The first post-split quarterly results and the trend in borrowings will indicate the financial stability of the standalone structure.

Progress on customer diversification can be checked through new order disclosures and changes in sales by customer. This report does not present earnings estimates that could not be verified.

Bull factors

Fast-growing scale

Per the registration statement, the spun-off unit's revenue grew from about KRW 134.7 billion in 2023 to about KRW 306.8 billion in 2025. It moved from an operating loss to an operating profit over the period. Its share of pre-split Tovis revenue also rose from 39.2% in 2023 to 55.2% in 2025.

Backlog and utilization headroom

Neovue was reported to hold an order backlog of about KRW 500 billion. With utilization around 79% in the first quarter of 2026, there is room to raise plant usage. If orders convert into revenue, the lower fixed-cost burden could improve the operating margin.

Business focus and governance safeguards

The split was pursued to remove the conglomerate discount and let each business be valued independently. Major shareholders agreed to joint voting for three years and placed voluntary lock-ups on part of their shares. The simpler business and control structure makes it easier for the market to assess operating results directly.

Bear factors

Thin operating margin

The first-quarter 2026 operating margin was 5.4%, below that of the surviving Tovis. Per the registration statement, the unit's 2025 operating profit of about KRW 16.6 billion is small relative to revenue. Fixed costs such as depreciation are heavy, so margin improvement depends on utilization.

Customer concentration

LG Display and Denso Korea were reported to account for roughly 80% of sales. If either customer adjusts orders or production plans, the earnings impact could be large. No verified figures yet show progress in winning new customers.

Balance sheet and supply overhang

The debt-to-equity ratio was reported at 158% and borrowings dependence at 25.8%. The stock fell sharply right after relisting, and supply overhang was cited as a factor at the time. A market capitalization of KRW 62.4 billion and trading value of KRW 400 million also point to thin trading.

07

Risk factors

Industry

Demand for automotive displays moves with carmakers' production schedules and sales volumes. Even with a backlog, results can fluctuate depending on customers' circumstances. Slower electric vehicle demand or changes in vehicle option mix could shift the timing at which orders turn into revenue.

Financial

Capital expenditure from the automotive expansion was assigned to Neovue, which raised its financial burden. If utilization cannot absorb fixed costs such as depreciation and interest, earnings could shrink.

The registration statement itself lists weaker financial stability and profitability from the spin-off as a key investment risk.

Market and supply

As a relisted stock, its first price was set through a call auction without a reference price, and early volatility was large. Low trading value means even small orders can move the price sharply. Market views may stay divided until standalone results are confirmed after the split.

08

Overall view

Per the registration statement, Neovue is an automotive display maker whose scale has grown quickly, and the backlog reported at about KRW 500 billion supports business visibility.

On the other hand, the 5.4% operating margin in the first quarter of 2026, the 158% debt-to-equity ratio and the roughly 80% share of its top two customers weigh on the early standalone period.

The gap between the KRW 25,600 opening price on the first day and the KRW 11,700 price on October 27, 2026 can be read only as a fact about how the market has priced these factors. Utilization, operating margin, the borrowing trend and customer diversification are the items to monitor. This report is for information only, and any view on the share price is left to the reader.
