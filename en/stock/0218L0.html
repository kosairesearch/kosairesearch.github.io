Spin-off auto display maker faces its earnings test

Neovue, a newly independent automotive display maker, still needs to convert its backlog and capacity into earnings.

  1. 1

    TOVIS Co., Ltd.'s automotive display business was split off and relisted on KOSDAQ as Neovue on July 27, 2026.

02

Business structure

Neovue was established through the spin-off of the automotive display business division of TOVIS Co., Ltd. Its main products are automotive in-vehicle displays, including dual and single car displays, HVAC (heating, ventilation and air conditioning) displays and digital touch keys.

According to 2025 figures, the business division's sales were 353.5 billion won, above the 286.7 billion won of the surviving TOVIS division. Neovue's sales still accounted for 56.6% of the total in the first quarter of 2026, making automotive the central axis of the company since the split.

The surviving TOVIS keeps its casino and industrial display business, and the cash generation and financial metrics of the two businesses are now separated. The automotive display market is growing as the spread of software-defined vehicles and digital cockpits raises the number of displays installed per car.

Customer concentration is high, with LG Display and DENSO Korea together accounting for about 80% of sales. Continental AG was recently added as a customer. Production sites are in Seocheon, South Chungcheong Province, and Dalian, China, with combined annual capacity of 600 billion won.

The subsidiary Dalian Hyundai LCD, which is 100% owned, manufactures and sells display modules for mobile phones and automotive displays.

Neovue has secured a backlog of 500 billion won, but because of the nature of the automotive industry, its earnings may fluctuate with customers' production schedules and vehicle sales.

03

Recent trends

Neovue relisted on KOSDAQ on July 27, 2026. On its first trading day the stock opened at 25,600 won, fell sharply during the session and closed at 17,950 won, which is 29.88% below the opening price.

According to the Korea Exchange, as of July 1, the spin-off date, consolidated assets were 216.3 billion won and equity was 84.8 billion won. Standalone sales for 2024 were reported at 268.3 billion won and operating profit at 97.7 billion won.

Reports differ on 2025 sales because of differing scopes: one newspaper reported 537.57 billion won in sales and 50.1 billion won in net income, while the business division basis was reported at 353.5 billion won.

In the first quarter of 2026, Neovue's operating margin was 5.4%, and its factory utilization was reported at about 79%. Over the same period, its debt-to-equity ratio was 158% and its borrowing dependence was 25.8%, which was explained by capital expenditure being attributed to Neovue after the split.

On September 10, 2026, the board approved a loan guarantee of 6.98775 billion won for its subsidiary Dalian Hyundai LCD, and the total outstanding guarantees stand at 21.56220 billion won.

Dalian Hyundai LCD reported 2025 sales of 111.0002 billion won and net income of 6.49 billion won, while its total equity was reported as negative 26.158 billion won. The current price is 9,950 won, the market capitalization is 52.5 billion won, and trading value is about 200 million won.

04

Outlook

The next checkpoint is how quickly the 500 billion won backlog converts into actual sales.

Against annual capacity of 600 billion won, utilization was about 79% in the first quarter of 2026, and higher utilization is needed to absorb fixed costs such as depreciation on about 145 billion won of property, plant and equipment.

Coverage reports argue that if new customer volumes, including Continental AG, expand as planned, the sales mix tilted toward LG Display and DENSO Korea would ease. Lifting the operating margin, which is currently below that of the surviving TOVIS, is another key metric.

Since the split, capital expenditure and borrowing burdens must be funded from the company's own cash flow, so the path toward lowering the 158% debt-to-equity ratio and 25.8% borrowing dependence is the main item to track.

Because the subsidiary Dalian Hyundai LCD has negative equity and total guarantees of 21.56220 billion won, the performance of the Chinese unit and the status of guarantee obligations are also matters to confirm.

Voluntary lock-up of the major shareholders' 17.85% stake and a three-year joint voting agreement are described as steps to reduce overhang and management uncertainty raised during the split.

The timing of the lock-up release and the change in tradable shares afterward are items to watch from a supply-and-demand perspective. The guarantee period approved on September 10, 2026 runs through June 23, 2028.

05

Bull factors

Demand structure in automotive displays

Growth in the automotive display market is drawing attention as the spread of software-defined vehicles and digital cockpits increases the number of displays per vehicle. Neovue mainly produces dual and single in-vehicle displays, HVAC displays and digital touch keys.

On a 2025 basis, Neovue's business division sales exceeded those of the surviving TOVIS division, and it still made up 56.6% of total sales in the first quarter of 2026. Some market observers have suggested this structure could earn a higher corporate value than the traditional casino display business.

Backlog and a widening customer base

Neovue has reportedly secured a backlog of 500 billion won. Continental AG, a global Tier-1 supplier, was added to a customer portfolio that had centered on LG Display. The company built annual production capacity of 600 billion won in Seocheon and Dalian, broadening its base for accepting orders. Coverage indicates that planned expansion of volumes from new customers could help ease customer concentration.

Major shareholder commitments after the split

CEO Kim Yong-beom holds 11.00% and co-CEO Ha Hee-jo holds 4.49%, and major shareholders agreed to a three-year joint exercise of voting rights and mutual preemptive purchase rights after relisting. An additional 17.85% stake has been placed under voluntary lock-up. The stake held by Mr.

Kim and related parties rose from 16.93% at the end of the first quarter to 17.08% just before the split. The company combined treasury share cancellation with share purchases by some executives, a step described as reducing overhang and management concerns raised during the split.

06

Bear factors

Low operating margin and fixed-cost burden

Neovue's operating margin in the first quarter of 2026 was 5.4%, below the 14% of the surviving TOVIS. Factory utilization was about 79%, and higher utilization is required to absorb fixed costs such as depreciation on about 145 billion won of property, plant and equipment.

A simple comparison with the 2024 standalone operating profit of 97.7 billion won is limited because the basis and period differ. The pace of margin improvement is a variable that determines when earnings recover after capacity expansion.

Sales concentrated in two customers

LG Display and DENSO Korea together account for about 80% of sales. Because of the nature of the automotive industry, earnings can fluctuate with customers' production schedules and vehicle sales. This concentration would ease only if volumes from new customers such as Continental AG expand.

The speed at which new customers move into mass production is seen as the variable that determines the degree of concentration.

Financial burden and the challenge of independence

Because capital expenditure from the automotive expansion was attributed to Neovue, its debt-to-equity ratio reached 158% and borrowing dependence reached 25.8%. Before the split, cash from the casino business could fund its capital spending, but after independence it must rely on its own cash flow.

Edaily interpreted the sharp first-day decline as reflecting supply-and-demand pressure and concern about independent management. The balance between cash generation and borrowing burden remains the central challenge of independent operation.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Demand for automotive displays depends on vehicle production and sales. A change in customers' production schedules can shift the timing of revenue recognition. If weakness in vehicle demand lasts longer, the timing of any improvement in utilization, currently around 79%, could also be delayed.

China subsidiary and currency

The subsidiary Dalian Hyundai LCD reported negative total equity of 26.158 billion won, although its 2025 net income was 6.49 billion won. The latest guarantee equals 8.24% of equity, and total outstanding guarantees amount to 21.56220 billion won. Because the borrowings are denominated in yuan, their won-equivalent values change with the exchange rate.

Liquidity and supply-demand

The stock plunged close to its daily limit on its first trading day, which created large supply-and-demand swings right after listing. Trading value of only about 200 million won means that small trades can move the price significantly.

The timing and size of the lock-up release on major shareholders' shares are variables that could affect supply and demand.

08

Overall view

Neovue is a newly independent company split from TOVIS's automotive display business, and it has strengths in business scale, customer base and production capacity.

On a 2025 basis, its business division's sales exceeded those of the surviving division, and reports cite a backlog of 500 billion won and annual capacity of 600 billion won.

On the other hand, the first-quarter 2026 operating margin of 5.4%, utilization of about 79% and debt-to-equity ratio of 158% show that profitability and financial burden remain open challenges. Customer concentration of about 80% and the subsidiary's negative equity and guarantees also act as weaknesses.

The sharp decline in the share price from the opening level after listing reflects that verification of independent performance is still under way. The lock-up and joint voting agreement by major shareholders are described as measures that reduce uncertainty over supply-demand and management control.

Progress on this will be confirmed through quarterly results disclosed going forward and the trend in customer volumes.
