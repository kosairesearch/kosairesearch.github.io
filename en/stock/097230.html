02

Business structure

HJ Shipbuilding & Construction runs two business lines, shipbuilding and construction. The shipbuilding line covers commercial vessels such as container ships and oil tankers, along with defense vessels including large transport ships and high-speed landing craft.

A company profile describes the group as holding a monopoly-type position in high-speed landing craft.

IB Tomato reported in January 2026 that 2025 wins included four container ships worth KRW 640.8 billion, four follow-on Geomdokburi-class vessels worth KRW 312.6 billion, and a multipurpose chemical-dispersal vessel worth KRW 68.9 billion.

A disclosure dated April 27, 2026 showed a contract with a European shipowner for two container ships worth KRW 357.2 billion, running through October 31, 2028. The company announced on October 15, 2026 that it had completed maintenance, repair and overhaul work on the U.S.

Navy support ship USNS Amelia Earhart, which it described as the first such job by a mid-sized Korean shipbuilder. In construction, the company has focused on public infrastructure.

Its 2025 wins included the third exhibition hall at KINTEX worth KRW 55.9 billion, the Dongul substation project worth KRW 73.4 billion, and the Busan public fish market project worth KRW 74.9 billion.

On June 18, 2026 the company announced it had been selected as the winning bidder for Section 3 of the Jecheon–Yeongwol expressway, valued at KRW 212.6 billion excluding VAT, in which it holds a 70% stake as lead member. Twenty-five domestic construction firms took part in the bidding.

The company also says it is expanding into privately funded urban redevelopment projects. Its main customers are European shipowners, the U.S. Navy and public project owners, and its construction bids face competition from large builders.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 1.79 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.16 trillion in 2023, an increase of 20.9% year on year, making 2023 the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Revenue fell 12.8% to KRW 1.89 trillion in 2024 and rose 6.0% to KRW 2 trillion in 2025.

Operating profit moved from KRW 6.63 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 108.8 billion in 2023, which marked a swing into loss, and then to KRW 7.25 billion in 2024, a swing back into profit. Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 67.1 billion, up 824.9% year on year.

The operating margin moved from -5.0% in 2023 to 3.4% in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 114 billion in 2023, when the loss widened, then turned to a profit of KRW 5.34 billion in 2024 and reached KRW 51.4 billion in 2025, up 862.6%.

Net profit including non-controlling interests for 2025 was KRW 51.4 billion, a separate line from the controlling-shareholder figure. Operating cash flow was -KRW 86.4 billion in 2024, a net outflow, and recovered to KRW 6.33 billion in 2025.

IB Tomato reported in January 2026 that the company cut its provision for construction losses by about two-thirds within a year and that cumulative construction profit turned positive.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit rose from KRW 5.79 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 50.5 billion in the fourth quarter, then fell to KRW 24.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026, a 51.3% drop, on revenue of KRW 541.4 billion, down 14.0%.

It recovered to KRW 64.9 billion in the second quarter on revenue of KRW 729.9 billion and reached KRW 66 billion in the third quarter on revenue of KRW 733.2 billion. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter was KRW 42.6 billion, down 31.9% from KRW 62.5 billion in the second quarter.

Operating profit rose 1.8% in that quarter while controlling-shareholder profit fell, which points to a wider gap between operating and final profit lines, and the disclosed figures alone do not pin down the specific cause.

Over the four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 179.2 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 567.0% in 2022 and 747.9% in 2023 to 266.4% in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 669.6 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 1.78 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

The improvement in shipbuilding profit has been led by new-build vessels, according to an IBK Investment & Securities report from March 2026.

The same report said news of high-speed landing craft exports, possibly to the UAE, could emerge as early as 2026, with further potential in the Middle East, South America and Southeast Asia.

While large shipbuilders concentrate on large vessels, a company profile lists a monopoly-type position in high-speed landing craft as part of its defense-vessel strength.

In February 2026 the company announced a contract to build its first container ships of 10,100 TEU class at its Yeongdo shipyard in Busan, a sign that the range of vessels it can build is widening.

In March 2026, the largest shareholder, EcoPrime Marine Pacific, signed a memorandum of agreement with HD Hyundai Heavy Industries on the transfer of assets at the Gunsan shipyard. At that time, an IBK analyst said it had not been decided whether HJ Shipbuilding & Construction would take over the assets directly.

Public construction bidding is competitive. Twenty-five domestic builders took part in the Jecheon–Yeongwol expressway tender, and the company won by relying on its highway construction experience. A company official said the result showed that its public-works capabilities were recognized against large builders.

In portfolio terms, shipbuilding combines commercial and defense vessels, while construction is diversifying around infrastructure and urban redevelopment. The fact that both segments now contribute profit is the main sign of its position within the sector.

Outlook

The company disclosed that the contract value for the two container ships with a European shipowner may change depending on construction progress. The contract runs through October 31, 2028, so construction and revenue recognition are expected to continue into the autumn of 2028. After completing the U.S.

Navy MRO work, the company said it would pursue follow-on orders. A June 2026 report said the final award for Section 4 of the Namyangju–Chuncheon national road was approaching. The Gunsan shipyard matter depends on who takes over the assets, which will determine whether the business is consolidated.

In March 2026 IBK Investment & Securities said that if cooperation with a U.S. shipyard takes shape and UAE exports of high-speed landing craft become concrete, it would raise both earnings and the multiple.

A company official stressed that order intake had set record levels in recent years, according to IB Tomato in January 2026. The company also presented a plan to combine its traditional public infrastructure strength with privately funded urban redevelopment.

How quickly order intake turns into reported earnings, however, depends on construction schedules and payment terms. The next points to track are the revenue trends and order backlog for shipbuilding and construction in the annual report.

Bull factors

Operating profit recovery

Operating profit reached KRW 66 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 1.8% from the prior quarter and a second straight quarterly increase. It followed a first-quarter 2026 low of KRW 24.6 billion and a recovery to KRW 64.9 billion in the second quarter. Full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 67.1 billion was up 824.9%.

Expanding order intake

The two container ships contracted with a European shipowner, disclosed on April 27, 2026, have a combined value of KRW 357.2 billion. In June 2026 the company was selected for Section 3 of the Jecheon–Yeongwol expressway, worth KRW 212.6 billion.

Reports said it also won four container ships worth KRW 640.8 billion and four follow-on Geomdokburi-class vessels worth KRW 312.6 billion in 2025. Order flow across both shipbuilding and public construction can be viewed as a supporting factor.

Stronger balance sheet

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 567.0% in 2022 to 266.4% in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 669.6 billion, equal to the controlling-shareholder portion. A company official said in January 2026 that key financial indicators, including the debt ratio, improved after the 2025 rights offering.

09

Bear factors

Quarterly earnings volatility

Revenue in the first quarter of 2026 fell 14.0% from the prior quarter, and operating profit fell 51.3%. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter was KRW 42.6 billion, down 31.9% from the second quarter. Annual revenue also fell 12.8% in 2024, so the earnings trend is uneven across both quarters and years.

Weak cash generation

Operating cash flow was a net outflow of KRW 86.4 billion in 2024 and reached only KRW 6.33 billion in 2025. Compared with 2025 operating profit of KRW 67.1 billion, cash generation has lagged earnings in some periods.

Whether larger order books raise working-capital needs can be gauged from operating cash flow in the next annual report.

Short profit record and open items

Apart from the 2023 operating loss of KRW 108.8 billion, the company has posted operating profit only in 2024 and 2025 within the disclosed period.

A March 2026 memorandum on the Gunsan shipyard asset transfer was signed, but reports at the time said the acquiring party and HJ Shipbuilding & Construction's role had not been settled. Until that matter is resolved, earnings depend on existing shipbuilding and construction orders.

10

Risk factors

Residual project-loss risk

IB Tomato reported on the wind-down of low-margin projects, including the Busan Opera House project. A view also holds that the shipbuilding segment's long-term earnings outlook was adjusted slightly conservatively to reflect delivery timing. Losses from past projects could still affect results if similar issues recur.

Leverage risk

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 1.78 trillion, well above total equity of KRW 669.6 billion. The debt ratio improved from 567.0% in 2022, but the absolute liability level remains large. Higher financing costs or bid and performance guarantee burdens could slow the pace of earnings recovery.

Concentrated ownership risk

The largest shareholder, EcoPrime Marine Pacific, holds about 48.9% according to FnGuide ownership data as of the last change on October 28, 2025. With the acquiring entity and method for the Gunsan shipyard assets still unsettled, the ownership structure or transaction terms could change. How the related-party transaction structure is finalized is a key variable.

Overall view

HJ Shipbuilding & Construction has restored earnings capacity, moving from an operating loss of KRW 108.8 billion in 2023 to operating profit of KRW 67.1 billion in 2025. In 2026, operating profit rose for two consecutive quarters after the first-quarter low.

Orders continued, with a KRW 357.2 billion container ship contract disclosed in April 2026 and the KRW 212.6 billion Jecheon–Yeongwol expressway award announced in June.

On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 567.0% in 2022 to 266.4% in 2025, yet total liabilities of KRW 1.78 trillion remain larger than total equity of KRW 669.6 billion.

Quarterly results are volatile: revenue and operating profit fell in the first quarter of 2026, and third-quarter controlling-shareholder profit fell 31.9% from the second quarter.

Operating cash flow was a net outflow in 2024 and only KRW 6.33 billion in 2025, so the pace at which profit converts to cash remains a point to track. Consolidation of the Gunsan shipyard and overseas exports are still unsettled, and current earnings rest mainly on existing shipbuilding and construction orders.
