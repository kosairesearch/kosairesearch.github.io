Business structure

Samil Enterprise is a building construction company listed on KOSDAQ on November 30, 1992. A corporate information aggregator that tallies revenue by segment for the second quarter of 2026 shows building construction at 100%, and no construction materials manufacturing revenue appears in the disclosed breakdown.

Its clients span public, private and healthcare sectors.

The company website lists sites including an outpatient center and parking structure expansion at a university hospital, an administration building in Pyeongtaek, a GMP plant for a chemical company in Hwaseong, and a new building for the Korea Expressway Corporation's Hapcheon branch.

The KRW 61.6 billion new church sanctuary contract signed on October 22, 2026 was awarded by a Presbyterian church in Changdong, Seoul.

The KRW 53.1 billion flagship store contract with Prada Korea in Gangnam, Seoul had its end date extended from November 30, 2026 to March 11, 2027 through a corrected filing submitted on November 12, 2026.

The company says it avoids low-price bids and selects projects based on construction quality, and it secures public-works competitiveness through joint ventures with firms that have stronger financial structures and technology.

The backlog of KRW 198.3 billion at end-2025 raised the share of medium- to long-term projects that run into 2026 and beyond. Because the company is smaller than large builders, a single site carries a large revenue share, and the Changdong contract alone equals 72.34% of 2025 revenue.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 63.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 78.6 billion in 2023 (up 23.6% year on year) and KRW 112.7 billion in 2024 (up 43.2%), then fell to KRW 85.2 billion in 2025 (down 24.4%). 2024 was the largest revenue year in the 2022 to 2025 span, and 2022 was the smallest.

The company attributed the 2025 revenue decline to fewer construction orders. Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 7.39 billion, up 30.8% year on year, and the company cited cost improvements as the driver.

Operating profit went from KRW 2.17 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.1 billion in 2023, KRW 5.65 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.39 billion in 2025, and it has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

The operating margin climbed from 3.4% in 2022 to 3.9% in 2023, 5.0% in 2024 and 8.7% in 2025, and the largest margin gain came in the year revenue shrank. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders grew from KRW 2.49 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.84 billion in 2025, with the 2025 increase at 17.0%.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 11.1 billion in 2022 to negative KRW 4.6 billion in 2023, then recovered to KRW 7.01 billion in 2024 and KRW 15.8 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 17.4% in 2022 to 33.5% in 2025, though total liabilities of KRW 24.5 billion at end-2025 remained below total equity of KRW 73.3 billion.

Quarterly revenue fell from KRW 26.2 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 20.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, rose to KRW 38.3 billion in the second quarter, and reached KRW 34.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 95.5% year on year.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.37 billion is the largest of the last five quarters, and whether it reflects a one-off or a change in the operating base should be checked against the line items in the third-quarter 2026 quarterly report.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's construction market moves with both public and private orders, and when orders shrink, the backlogs of mid-sized builders are the first to be affected. Samil Enterprise's 2025 revenue decline was disclosed as caused by fewer construction orders, which fits this pattern.

Revenue peaked in 2024, fell in 2025, and the quarterly figures in 2026 point to a recovery. A financial news report projected that new contracts concentrated in the second half of 2025 would feed into results after 2026.

Large builders such as Samsung C&T, Hyundai Engineering & Construction, Daewoo Engineering & Construction, GS Engineering & Construction and DL E&C hold advantages in scale and credit.

Samil Enterprise competes at a mid-sized scale, combining selective bidding with joint ventures to take part in public and private projects. Because the 2025 profit gain came from cost improvements, swings in material and labor costs could reverse that gain. Interest rates and private development demand also remain external factors that shape order volumes.
