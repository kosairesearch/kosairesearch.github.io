Report overview

Energy and palm lift profit; next checkpoints

POSCO International's 3Q 2026 operating profit rose year on year, driven mainly by its energy and Indonesian palm businesses, and the next checkpoints are the closing of a U.S. gas asset acquisition and the 2026 annual report.

02

Business structure

POSCO International is a diversified trading company with three core areas: steel product trading, energy (gas field development, LNG terminals and power generation), and materials and bio (including palm operations in Indonesia).

According to the company's February 2026 earnings call summary of 2025 segments, the gas E&P business posted sales of KRW 650.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 356.1 billion. The LNG terminal and power business recorded sales of KRW 2.89 trillion and operating profit of KRW 172.1 billion.

Subsidiaries including Senex had sales of KRW 450.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 98.4 billion, and total energy operating profit of KRW 626.6 billion equaled 54% of group operating profit.

In steel, sales were KRW 14.55 trillion with operating profit of KRW 237.4 billion, while the materials and bio segment booked sales of KRW 8.83 trillion and operating profit of KRW 69.5 billion.

The Indonesian palm business earned KRW 101 billion in 2025 operating profit, up 23% year on year, and the company said the PT.PAR farm acquisition, consolidated in November 2025, completed a value chain from seeds to crushing and refining.

Peers in general trading include LX International and the trading arm of Samsung C&T; press coverage of 3Q 2026 reported that all three grew sales and operating profit year on year, with POSCO International the largest by both measures. Its steel trading remains exposed to steel market conditions and currency moves.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 38 trillion in 2022 to KRW 33.1 trillion in 2023 (down 12.8% year on year) and to KRW 32.3 trillion in 2024 (down 2.4%). Revenue recovered slightly to KRW 32.4 trillion in 2025, up 0.1%; within 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for sales and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 902.5 billion, KRW 1.16 trillion, KRW 1.12 trillion and KRW 1.17 trillion for 2022 through 2025, staying above KRW 1 trillion since 2023, with 2025 the largest year. The operating margin rose from 2.4% in 2022 to 3.6% in 2025, so profitability improved while sales shrank.

Owner net income was KRW 589.7 billion in 2022, KRW 673.9 billion in 2023 (up 14.3%), KRW 514.6 billion in 2024 (down 23.6%) and KRW 614.1 billion in 2025 (up 19.3%), with 2023 the largest year.

Years in which operating profit and owner net income moved in different directions show that non-operating items, taxes and non-controlling interests affect the final bottom line.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 604.9 billion, KRW 680.4 billion, KRW 503.4 billion and KRW 636.8 billion for 2022 to 2025.

Quarterly operating profit moved from KRW 315.9 billion in 3Q 2025 to KRW 265.6 billion in 4Q 2025 (down 15.9% quarter on quarter), then rose to KRW 357.5 billion in 1Q 2026 and KRW 429 billion in 2Q 2026. 3Q 2026 operating profit was KRW 400.7 billion, down 6.6% from the prior quarter but up 26.9% year on year, on revenue of KRW 9.03 trillion.

Press coverage of the release credited energy operating profit of KRW 239.1 billion (up 19.6%) and Indonesian palm operating profit of KRW 83.7 billion (up 454.3%). Steel operating profit fell 23.8% to KRW 51.3 billion as a weaker exchange rate and foreign-currency translation losses weighed on results.

A financial industry analysis reported in early November 2026 expected a temporary profit decline in 4Q from palm seasonality and lower system marginal price (SMP). Operating cash flow rose from KRW 876.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.94 trillion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

POSCO International's earnings are highly sensitive to commodity prices. Samsung Securities said in an October 2026 report that oil prices, which rose after the Middle East conflict, feed into gas field selling prices with a lag.

The power business was helped by higher system marginal price (SMP) on the back of higher oil prices; utilization fell to 27% in 3Q 2026, yet operating profit rose 21.9% to KRW 85.2 billion.

In LNG, the first shipments under a 20-year contract for roughly 400,000 tons per year with Cheniere of the United States began in October 2026. The palm business benefited from higher crude palm oil (CPO) prices and the Indonesian farm acquisition.

A credit rating report cited a continued slump in steel market conditions and supply chain uncertainty from the closure of the Strait of Hormuz as variables for trading results.

Among general trading peers, all three companies (POSCO International, LX International and the trading arm of Samsung C&T) grew sales and profit in 3Q 2026, but the sources of profit differ: LX International's logistics business led, while POSCO International's energy and palm businesses did.

Samsung C&T's trading arm growth was described as centered on industrial materials. Yuanta Securities said the profit expansion at Senex and the Indonesian palm business is easing volatility in earnings from other businesses.

By contrast, steel profit declined on the weaker exchange rate, showing that profits in the materials segment depend on currency movements.

06

Outlook

In February 2026, the company said it targets palm business operating profit of roughly USD 170 million for 2026 during its earnings call. At the 3Q 2026 release, an executive said third-quarter results substantially beat plan and the company expects to meet its full-year target.

A Hana Securities analyst expects Senex's production growth and a sales mix improvement from higher oil prices to continue through the year. The company is aiming to close the acquisition of a U.S. gas asset by the end of November 2026.

At the Gwangyang second LNG terminal, storage capacity is set to rise from 930,000 kl to 1.33 million kl, and press reports said full completion was being prepared for November 2026.

The company expects gas sales and additional drilling to generate average operating profit of about KRW 100 billion per year through 2040. Planned investment for 2027 is about KRW 2 trillion, with half for energy and the rest for palm, food and rare earths.

An analysis cited in early November 2026 expects a temporary 4Q profit decline from palm seasonality and lower SMP, while forecasts also point to strengthening structural growth drivers. The 2026 annual report, the next regular filing, will confirm whether the palm target was met and show the full-year energy mix.

08

Bull factors

Energy as the profit core

Energy operating profit was KRW 626.6 billion in 2025, or 54% of group operating profit. In 3Q 2026 the energy segment earned KRW 239.1 billion, and Senex's operating profit reached KRW 55.1 billion, up 106.4% year on year.

The company expects gas sales and additional drilling to generate about KRW 100 billion in average annual operating profit through 2040.

Palm business metrics improving

After the PT.PAR farm acquisition was consolidated in November 2025, the Indonesian palm business earned KRW 83.7 billion in operating profit in 3Q 2026, up 454.3% year on year. The company set a 2026 palm operating profit target of roughly USD 170 million.

Higher palm prices and better production management both contributed, and the operating metrics show measurable improvement.

Easing financial pressure

Net debt to equity stood at 67.3% at the end of 3Q 2026, down from 72.5% in the prior quarter. EBITDA for the quarter was KRW 562.1 billion, up 28.2% year on year. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 876.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.94 trillion in 2025, indicating better conversion of earnings into cash.

09

Bear factors

Currency sensitivity in steel and materials

Steel operating profit in 3Q 2026 fell 23.8% year on year to KRW 51.3 billion. Press coverage linked the decline to a lower exchange rate and foreign-currency translation losses. The materials and bio segment also saw its profitability stall despite higher sales, again because of currency effects.

Quarterly seasonality and volatility

Operating profit in 3Q 2026 fell 6.6% from the prior quarter. In 4Q 2025 it dropped 15.9% quarter on quarter to KRW 265.6 billion. A financial industry analysis reported in November 2026 expected a temporary 4Q profit decline from palm seasonality and lower SMP.

Borrowings and expanding investment

The debt ratio rose to 140.0% at the end of 2025 from 135.9% in 2024. Borrowings at the end of 3Q 2026 were KRW 6.20 trillion, up 16.1% year on year. Planned investment of about KRW 2 trillion for 2027, half in energy, could keep funding needs elevated.

10

Risk factors

Commodity and geopolitical risk

Gas and LNG selling prices follow oil with a lag, so sharp oil price swings can disrupt earnings. A credit rating report named supply chain uncertainty from the closure of the Strait of Hormuz as a source of trading volatility. The power business depends on SMP and plant utilization, which fell to 27% in 3Q 2026.

Exchange rates and steel market conditions

A weaker exchange rate lowered profit in steel and materials and bio, and foreign-currency translation losses added to the pressure. A credit rating report also flagged a continued slump in steel market conditions. Both businesses can see sales rise without a matching improvement in profitability.

Execution of acquisitions and investment

If the U.S. gas asset acquisition does not close on schedule, or production does not ramp as expected, payback could take longer. Borrowings at the end of 3Q 2026 were KRW 6.20 trillion and net debt to equity was 67.3%. Execution of the roughly KRW 2 trillion 2027 investment plan could further narrow financial headroom.

12

Overall view

POSCO International is shifting from a steel-centered trading company toward a structure where energy and palm generate a larger share of profit. Energy operating profit accounted for 54% of group operating profit in 2025.

Full-year 2025 operating profit was KRW 1.17 trillion, staying above KRW 1 trillion since 2023, and press reports put cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 at KRW 1.19 trillion.

Owner net income, however, was KRW 614.1 billion in 2025, below the KRW 673.9 billion of 2023, so operating gains did not translate fully into bottom-line profit. Owner net income over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 1.02 trillion.

Bullish factors include better operating metrics from gas sales prices, the long-term LNG contract and the palm acquisition. Bearish factors include currency sensitivity in steel, quarterly seasonality, a higher debt ratio and the 2027 investment burden.

The next checkpoints are the closing of the U.S. gas asset acquisition by late November 2026, the 4Q 2026 preliminary results in early 2027, and the 2026 annual report by the end of March 2027.
