Report overview

Spirits-Based Profit Recovery Meets a 2026 Slowdown

JinroDistillers is a spirits-feedstock maker whose operating profit and profit attributable to owners rose for three straight years through 2025, but both sales and profit fell in the third quarter of 2026, making the durability of that recovery the key question.

02

Business structure

JinroDistillers was founded in 1984 as Woosin Fermentation and took its current name after leaving the Jinro group of companies in 1992. Its shares were registered on the over-the-counter market in 1993 and now trade on KOSDAQ.

Its core products are fermented spirits and rectified spirits, which serve as alcohol feedstock for shochu; in the first half of 2026, fermented spirits made up 48.4% of sales and rectified spirits 36.2%, according to Pinpoint News.

Over the same period, the alcohol disinfectant Crean Call contributed 12.4% and Andong soju 3.0%, with some revenue from biogas power generation. In FnGuide's breakdown for December 2024, fermented spirits were 50.0% of sales, rectified spirits 36.78%, alcohol disinfectant 10.25%, and Andong soju 2.76%.

The company supplies the domestic liquor market, and demand for spirits moves with demand for shochu and other alcoholic beverages. In FnGuide's December 2024 data, JinroDistillers held a 16.45% share of the spirits market, ahead of Seoyoung Spirits at 10.26% and MH Ethanol at 9.45%.

A 2024 NewsWorker report described the company as having the second-largest production capacity among nine domestic spirits producers. The company holds a 49.64% stake in Andong Soju Ilpum, according to Pinpoint News' report basis.

Crean Call's share of sales fell from 14% in 2021 and 13% in 2022 to 8.6% in 2023, then rose to 12.4% in the first half of 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales rose from KRW 99.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 100.1 billion in 2023 (up 0.5%) and KRW 101.5 billion in 2024 (up 1.4%), then fell to KRW 99.8 billion in 2025 (down 1.6%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year for sales and 2022 the smallest. Profit moved far more than sales.

Operating profit went from KRW 3.79 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.06 billion in 2023 (up 112.6%), KRW 11.5 billion in 2024 (up 43.2%) and KRW 14 billion in 2025 (up 21.5%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

The operating margin climbed from 3.8% in 2022 to 8.1%, 11.4% and 14.0% in 2025, so profitability improved even as 2025 sales declined.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent went from KRW 3.27 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.11 billion in 2023 (up 86.7%), KRW 10.2 billion in 2024 (up 67.9%) and KRW 12.1 billion in 2025 (up 18.5%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 12.5 billion in 2025 and should be read separately from profit attributable to owners. Operating cash flow moved from negative KRW 2.18 billion in 2022 and negative KRW 594 million in 2023 to KRW 20 billion in 2024 and KRW 17.9 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales were KRW 23.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 24.7 billion in the fourth quarter (up 6.1% quarter on quarter), KRW 21.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 12.0%), KRW 28.8 billion in the second quarter (up 32.4%) and KRW 20.8 billion in the third quarter (down 27.7%, or down 10.6% year on year).

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 1.99 billion, KRW 2.06 billion, KRW 3.07 billion, KRW 4.69 billion and KRW 697 million, respectively, with the third quarter of 2026 down 65.1% from a year earlier. Profit rose with sales in the second quarter but fell sharply when sales dropped in the third quarter.

WiseReport attributed the first-half 2026 decline to lower spirits sales. Profit attributable to owners for the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 8.69 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for the spirits business comes from shochu and other alcoholic beverages. Hite Jinro reported that its third-quarter 2026 shochu sales fell 3.5% year on year to KRW 367.5 billion, while operating profit in the segment rose 7%, according to SBS Biz.

Hite Jinro described the result as cost efficiency offsetting a shrinking liquor market. A 2024 NewsWorker report pointed to a stalled shochu market and weaker consumer sentiment as uncertainties for JinroDistillers.

In FnGuide's data for December 2024, the company's 16.45% share of the spirits market was ahead of Seoyoung Spirits at 10.26% and MH Ethanol at 9.45%. The same NewsWorker report, however, described the company as ranked second in the industry with a 16.55% share, so sources differ on its ranking.

Rising raw material prices were flagged as a burden on growth in a 2024 report. WiseReport's company commentary states that the company maintains its cost advantage through energy-saving equipment investment. In this structure, competitiveness for spirits producers depends on plant scale and unit cost.

The operating margin rising to 14.0% in 2025 shows that cost gains translated into profit even while the shochu market was flat.

06

Outlook

WiseReport's first-half 2026 figures, as of September 2, 2026, show sales down 2.5%, operating profit down 22.1% and net income down 26.7%. WiseReport attributed the first-half sales decline to lower spirits sales.

Quarterly sales rose 32.4% in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 28.8 billion, then fell to KRW 20.8 billion in the third quarter. Because quarterly profit has swung widely, whether sales hold at the current level in the fourth quarter and beyond is the central variable.

Crean Call's share of sales was 12.4% in the first half of 2026, higher than the 8.6% recorded in 2023, indicating that non-spirits revenue has grown somewhat. On costs, WiseReport cites energy-saving equipment investment as supporting margins.

The 2025 year-end dividend was confirmed at the March 2026 annual general meeting, with total dividends of roughly KRW 8.3 billion. Whether the liquor market contraction continues and how spirits demand tracks shochu production will shape future results.

The next checkpoint is whether the annual operating margin in the 2026 annual report holds near the 14.0% recorded in 2025.

Bull factors

Spirits-centered demand and market share

Fermented and rectified spirits made up 84.6% of first-half 2026 sales, giving a clear base tied to liquor producers' demand. FnGuide data put the company's December 2024 spirits market share at 16.45%, ahead of Seoyoung Spirits and MH Ethanol.

A 2024 NewsWorker report described its production capacity as the second largest among nine domestic spirits producers.

Margin expansion and three years of growth

The operating margin rose from 3.8% in 2022 to 14.0% in 2025. Operating profit grew for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, reaching KRW 14 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow turned positive from 2024, so profit gains also showed up in cash generation.

Financial headroom and dividend continuity

Total liabilities were KRW 8.2 billion at the end of 2025 and the debt-to-equity ratio was 8.7%. The 2025 year-end dividend was confirmed at the March 2026 annual general meeting, with total payouts in the KRW 8 billion range. The debt ratio fell from 22.8% in 2022, which shows the balance sheet strengthening in the disclosed figures.

Bear factors

Profit slowdown since the first half of 2026

According to WiseReport, first-half 2026 sales fell 2.5%, operating profit fell 22.1% and net income fell 26.7%. Third-quarter sales were KRW 20.8 billion, down 10.6% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 697 million, down 65.1%. WiseReport cited lower spirits sales as the reason for the first-half sales decline.

Shrinking shochu market and demand linkage

Hite Jinro reported that its third-quarter 2026 shochu sales fell 3.5% from a year earlier (SBS Biz). With the liquor market shrinking across the industry, spirits demand could move in the same direction. A 2024 NewsWorker report flagged a stalled shochu market and weaker consumer sentiment as key uncertainties.

Quarterly profit volatility and thin liquidity

Operating profit fell from KRW 4.69 billion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 697 million in the third quarter. WiseReport's September 2026 data show a free float ratio of 34.36% and foreign ownership of 0.54%. Wide quarterly profit swings combined with thin liquidity could make trading sensitive to results.

Risk factors

Concentration risk

Spirits account for more than 80% of sales, concentrating revenue in one product group (Pinpoint News). A drop in liquor demand or production cuts for shochu would flow directly into results. A 2024 NewsWorker report noted that contributions from newer businesses outside spirits were small.

Raw material and energy costs

A 2024 NewsWorker report flagged rising raw material prices as a burden on growth. WiseReport's company commentary says energy-saving equipment supports cost competitiveness. Because cost changes flow directly into the operating margin, cost trends are a key variable.

Affiliate exposure and trading conditions

The company holds a 49.64% stake in Andong Soju Ilpum, so affiliate performance feeds into consolidated results (Pinpoint News, based on the report at the time). With a free float ratio of 34.36%, trading conditions can make the share price move sharply. In a small capital structure, even small changes in results can be reflected sensitively in the share price.

Overall view

JinroDistillers is a spirits-focused producer whose fermented and rectified spirits accounted for 84.6% of first-half 2026 sales.

Annual operating profit rose from KRW 3.79 billion in 2022 to KRW 14 billion in 2025 and the operating margin climbed from 3.8% to 14.0%, so the direction of recovery is confirmed in the disclosures.

Sales fell 1.6% in 2025 to KRW 99.8 billion, so it is worth checking whether the profit gains came from costs and profitability structure rather than volume. Cumulative sales for the first three quarters of 2026 fell 5.0% to KRW 71.3 billion, and cumulative operating profit fell 29.2% to KRW 8.46 billion.

Strengths include spirits market share, a low debt ratio, dividend continuity and energy-saving equipment investment, while weaknesses include the shrinking liquor market, concentration in spirits, raw material pressure and a low free float ratio.

On valuation, the stock trades with a PER below the sector level and a modest premium to book value. The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and whether quarterly profit recovers in the first quarter of 2027.
