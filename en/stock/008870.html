Provisional Net Loss Puts Core Earnings Recovery in Focus

Kumbi swung to a provisional net loss for the fiscal year ended September 2026, with operating profit falling to KRW 122.05 million, making core business recovery the next checkpoint.

  2. 2

02

Business structure

Kumbi operates a Glass Division (glass bottle manufacturing), a CAP Division (bottle cap manufacturing) and a cosmetics business. Segment revenue shares could not be confirmed in this review, so no segment figures are stated.

According to a corporate profile service (comments as of June 2026), the CAP Division's results weakened as high interest rates and low growth reduced consumption, the Glass Division was affected by changes in alcohol consumption, and the plastic container business slowed amid uncertainty in China.

End-demand comes mainly from food and beverage customers, and the company stated in its September 2026 fiscal-year disclosure that weakness in these downstream industries shrank the market. The company cited intensifying competition among suppliers as a major cause of the earnings decline.

It is pursuing export growth in the United States, Japan and Southeast Asia and the resumption of China exports, and has pursued European and Japanese market development and cost improvement since 2024, according to a financial information service.

R&D spending for the fiscal year ended September 2025 was KRW 1.63 billion, or 0.65% of revenue. Product-level market share data was not confirmed, so competitive position is described qualitatively.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 251 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2023 to KRW 259.5 billion in fiscal 2024 (up 3.4% year on year), then fell to KRW 252.4 billion in fiscal 2025 (down 2.7%). Fiscal 2024 was the largest year in the three-year span for revenue.

Operating profit went from KRW 5.89 billion to KRW 12.4 billion (up 109.9%) and then to KRW 3.37 billion in fiscal 2025 (down 72.7%), the smallest year of the span. Operating margin moved from 2.3% to 4.8% and then to 1.3%.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss of KRW 6.78 billion in fiscal 2023 to a profit of KRW 3.59 billion in fiscal 2024, then declined to KRW 2.16 billion in fiscal 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 8.47 billion in fiscal 2023, a profit of KRW 5.97 billion in fiscal 2024 and KRW 3.83 billion in fiscal 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 12.5 billion, KRW 26.8 billion and KRW 13.7 billion across the same years.

Quarterly revenue fell from KRW 64.9 billion in the second quarter of 2025 to KRW 61.3 billion and KRW 58 billion in the third and fourth quarters, then recovered to KRW 58.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 65.5 billion in the second quarter.

Operating profit was KRW 2.74 billion in the second quarter of 2025, then an operating loss of KRW 232 million in the third quarter and KRW 111 million in the fourth quarter. It returned to KRW 640 million in the first quarter of 2026 but fell to KRW 40.55 million in the second quarter.

A provisional fiscal 2026 result disclosed on November 9, 2026 (not yet audited) shows revenue of KRW 247.03 billion (down 2.1%), operating profit of KRW 122.05 million and a net loss of KRW 11.10 billion, with a pre-tax loss of KRW 13.04 billion. Because these are provisional, the audited figures may differ.

The 63.8% year-on-year operating profit decline cited in one news report does not reconcile with the prior-year figure in the same filing, so it should be rechecked against the annual report.

The CAP Division weakened as high interest rates and low growth reduced consumption, and the Glass Division is exposed to shifts in alcohol consumption. Uncertainty in China was cited as a drag on the plastic container business.

On the supply side, the company identified intensifying competition among suppliers as a major cause of the earnings decline. Shipment volume or price indices needed to judge the cycle position were not confirmed in this review, so no cyclical conclusion is drawn.

Revenue declined in both the fiscal year ended September 2025 and the provisional fiscal 2026 result, so the revenue base has not widened. Larger listed chemical companies in the same sector have petrochemical-centered business structures, which limits direct metric comparisons.

08

Bull factors

Revenue Recovery

On a disclosed basis, revenue rose in both the first and second quarters of 2026, with the second quarter reaching KRW 65.5 billion, up 11.9% from the prior quarter. Revenue has moved up from the fourth-quarter 2025 low. Year-on-year growth in the second quarter was only 0.9%, so the recovery is modest.

Export Diversification

The company is expanding beyond Southeast Asia into the United States and Japan and is working to normalize China exports. Since 2024, it has pursued European and Japanese market development alongside cost improvement. This could partly reduce dependence on weak domestic food and beverage demand. Confirmed export share or performance figures were not found in this review.

Improving Leverage Trend

The debt ratio fell from 141.3% in the fiscal year ended September 2023 to 126.4% in 2024 and 124.5% in 2025. Operating cash flow stayed positive in all three years, at KRW 12.5 billion, KRW 26.8 billion and KRW 13.7 billion. Profit is small, but cash generation has been maintained.

Bear factors

Sharp Drop in Operating Profit and Swing to Loss

The provisional operating profit for the fiscal year ended September 2026 was KRW 122.05 million, and the net loss reached KRW 11.10 billion. That is far below the KRW 3.37 billion operating profit of the fiscal year ended September 2025.

Second-quarter 2026 operating profit was only KRW 40.55 million, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 665 million.

Weak Downstream Demand and Intensifying Competition

The shrinking market in downstream industries such as food and beverage is one background factor behind the earnings decline. It also cited intensifying competition among suppliers as a major cause of lower earnings.

The CAP Division slowed as consumption fell amid high rates and low growth, and the plastic container business slowed on China uncertainty.

Equity Shrinkage

Equity attributable to the parent excluding non-controlling interests was reported at KRW 76.0 billion. Compared with KRW 85.6 billion of controlling shareholders' equity at the end of September 2025, the equity base has narrowed. These are provisional figures and may change in the audited annual report.

Risk factors

Demand Risk

If downstream food and beverage demand does not recover, glass bottle and cap volumes could continue to decline. If high rates and low growth keep suppressing consumption, the CAP Division's weakness could persist. Structural shifts in alcohol consumption affect the Glass Division over the longer term.

Profitability and Competition Risk

Operating margin fell from 1.3% in the fiscal year ended September 2025 to a lower level in the provisional fiscal 2026 result. Heightened competition could increase pressure on pricing and costs.

The collapse of second-quarter 2026 operating profit to KRW 40.55 million suggests such pressure is already visible in the numbers.

Disclosure and Financial Verification Risk

The fiscal 2026 figures are provisional and may differ from the audited annual report. The year-on-year decline rate in the disclosure text does not reconcile with the prior-year figure in the same filing, so it needs rechecking.

The effect of lower equity on dividends and investment capacity can only be assessed after the audited disclosure.

Overall view

Annual operating profit declined from KRW 12.4 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2024 to KRW 3.37 billion in 2025, and to KRW 122.05 million on the provisional basis.

Quarterly data showed a return to operating profit in the first quarter of 2026 and a revenue rebound in the second quarter, but second-quarter operating profit was only KRW 40.55 million. The debt ratio fell from 141.3% to 124.5% on a disclosed basis, though provisional total equity declined.
