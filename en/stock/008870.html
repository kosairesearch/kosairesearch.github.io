02

Business structure

Kumbi operates a glass business, a CAP business (bottle caps) and a cosmetics business.

In its Q1 2026 commentary, the CAP business was described as weak on high interest rates and low growth that reduced consumption, while the glass business was affected by changes in liquor consumption and the plastic container business slowed on China-related uncertainty.

Demand for glass containers and caps is tied to liquor and food and beverage consumption, so downstream consumer trends directly shape performance.

On exports, the company is pursuing expansion into the United States and Japan beyond Southeast Asia and a normalization of China exports, and since 2024 it has paired Europe and Japan market development with cost improvement. R&D spending in fiscal 2025 was 1,631 million won, or 0.65% of sales.

Operating margin moved from 2.3% in fiscal 2023 to 4.8% in fiscal 2024 and 1.3% in fiscal 2025, showing that profit swings widely with cost and pricing conditions even when sales are similar.

At the end of September 2025, total equity was KRW 132.8 billion, of which KRW 85.6 billion was controlling-shareholder equity, and total liabilities were KRW 165.4 billion. The debt ratio fell from 141.3% in fiscal 2023 to 124.5% in fiscal 2025 but remains above 100%.

The customer base appears concentrated in liquor and food and beverage makers, so a slowdown in downstream consumption feeds quickly into volumes.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 251 billion in fiscal 2023 to KRW 259.5 billion in fiscal 2024 (up 3.4%), then fell to KRW 252.4 billion in fiscal 2025 (down 2.7%). Fiscal 2024 was the largest year in the period and fiscal 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 5.89 billion in fiscal 2023, KRW 12.4 billion in fiscal 2024 (up 109.9%) and KRW 3.37 billion in fiscal 2025 (down 72.7%), and operating margin followed the same pattern at 2.3%, 4.8% and 1.3%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a net loss of KRW 6.78 billion in fiscal 2023 to KRW 3.59 billion in fiscal 2024, then fell to KRW 2.16 billion in fiscal 2025 (down 39.9%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 8.47 billion, KRW 5.97 billion and KRW 3.83 billion across fiscal 2023 to 2025; it differs from the controlling-shareholder figure because of the non-controlling share.

The preliminary fiscal 2026 result shows sales of KRW 247.03 billion, down 2.1%, a sharp drop in operating profit, and a net loss of KRW 11.10 billion, announced November 9, 2026.

The company attributed the decline to intensifying competition among rivals and weak downstream food and beverage demand that shrank the market.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 64.9 billion in Q2 2025 to KRW 61.3 billion in Q3 (down 5.6%) and KRW 58 billion in Q4 (down 5.3%), then rose to KRW 58.5 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 65.5 billion in Q2 2026, two straight quarterly increases. Q2 2026 sales were up only 0.9% year on year.

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 2.74 billion in Q2 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 232 million in Q3, an operating loss of KRW 111 million in Q4 (a continued loss with a smaller deficit), and KRW 640 million in Q1 2026, a return to profit.

Q2 2026 operating profit was KRW 40.55 million, down 93.7% from the prior quarter and 98.5% year on year. Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 1.33 billion in Q3 2025, then a net loss of KRW 1.08 billion in Q4, KRW 349 million of profit in Q1 2026, and a net loss of KRW 665 million in Q2 2026.

The four quarters from Q3 2025 to Q2 2026 total a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 62.78 million. The Q3 2025 profit reflected a tax effect, with pre-tax results negative while income tax expense was booked as a credit.

Q2 2026 cost of sales rose sharply from the prior quarter, so higher sales did not translate into better operating profit. Operating cash flow was KRW 12.5 billion, KRW 26.8 billion and KRW 13.7 billion across fiscal 2023 to 2025, and the debt ratio declined from 141.3% to 124.5%.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for Kumbi's glass and caps rests on liquor and food and beverage consumption, and the recent trend is not favorable. The decline in CAP business volumes has been linked to high interest rates and low growth, so a recovery in cap demand is tied to the rate environment.

The China-related uncertainty in the plastic container business shows how overseas demand reaches domestic results. Efforts to widen export markets to the United States, Japan and Europe read as an attempt to offset weaker domestic consumption.

Operating profit peaked in fiscal 2024 and fell in fiscal 2025, which suggests an industry adjustment is showing up in earnings first. Sales rose for two straight quarters into Q2 2026 while operating profit declined, which suggests difficulty passing on prices or absorbing costs.

With competition intensifying, utilization and fixed-cost absorption drive margins, so profit swings are large even when sales are flat. It is too early to call the cycle position, but the move in operating margin from 2.3% to 4.8% and then to 1.3% indicates the strength of the change.

Risk factors

Overall view

Kumbi's sales have stayed within the KRW 250 billion range since fiscal 2023, and the preliminary fiscal 2026 sales of KRW 247.03 billion kept a similar scale. Operating profit, however, fell from its fiscal 2024 peak of KRW 12.4 billion, and the preliminary fiscal 2026 result shows a swing to a net loss.
