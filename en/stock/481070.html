02

Business structure

AU Brandz operates the footwear and apparel brand Rockfish Weatherwear. The company listed on KOSDAQ in April 2025 and acquired shares in the UK parent of the brand, securing its brand intellectual property.

The brand first became known for rain boots, but its sales mix has shifted toward all-season products such as Mary Jane shoes and sneakers. All-season products generated KRW 22.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026, or 91% of sales, according to press coverage of the company's results.

Sneakers and apparel brought in KRW 14.6 billion and Mary Jane shoes KRW 7.5 billion in the same quarter. Retail is centered on offline stores, with 52 locations at the end of September 2026, including 8 in Korea and 44 overseas. China accounts for the largest share of overseas stores at 31.

In Japan, the joint venture AU BRANDZ JAPAN launched in August 2026 and opened a store in Shinjuku Lumine 2, while a store also opened at ION Orchard in Singapore. Overseas and foreign-customer sales, including foreign shoppers in Korean stores, reached 76.3% of sales in the third quarter of 2026.

Chinese selling prices are two to three times the local popular price range, but local production is said to lower costs, so margins should improve as China's sales share rises, according to an analyst view reported in the press.

04

Earnings analysis

AU Brandz's annual results show sales and profit moving in opposite directions. Sales rose from KRW 41.9 billion in 2023 to KRW 44.6 billion in 2024 (up 6.5%) and KRW 60 billion in 2025 (up 34.5%), with 2025 the largest year of 2023 to 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 16.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 12.4 billion in 2024 (down 23.4%) and KRW 10.1 billion in 2025 (down 18.9%), declining for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025, with 2023 the largest year. The operating margin fell from 38.7% in 2023 to 27.9% in 2024 and 16.8% in 2025.

Press coverage attributed 2025 sales growth to wider domestic offline retail and the entry into China and other Asian markets. A rise in fixed costs from store expansion is cited as a factor behind the profit decline.

Hana Securities estimated in a March 2026 report that flagship stores rose from 3 in 2024 to 13 in 2025 and that fixed costs increased by about KRW 4.5 billion. An SK Securities researcher said higher personnel and depreciation costs were largely capital expenditure needed for the China expansion.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 12.9 billion in 2023, KRW 10.1 billion in 2024 (down 22.0%) and KRW 8.46 billion in 2025 (down 16.1%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 13.2 billion in 2023, KRW 10.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.76 billion in 2025, and the gap between the two measures is the non-controlling share.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 15.9 billion in 2023 to KRW 8.18 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.36 billion in 2025, moving in the same direction as profit. Quarterly results were more volatile. Fourth-quarter 2025 sales were KRW 26.4 billion, up 121.7% from the prior quarter, with operating profit of KRW 5.89 billion.

First-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 18.5 billion (down 30.0% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 2.02 billion (down 65.7%) were reported to reflect initial costs of overseas expansion. Second-quarter 2026 sales recovered to KRW 30.3 billion with operating profit of KRW 6.27 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 24.4 billion (down 19.4% quarter on quarter) with operating profit of KRW 5.08 billion (down 19.1%). Press reports put the operating margin at 11% in the first quarter, 20.7% in the second and 20.8% in the third.

Operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 totals KRW 13.37 billion, above the 2025 full-year figure of KRW 10.1 billion. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totals KRW 14.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

China is the central axis of AU Brandz's growth. Press reports say that, as Chinese consumers favor girlish apparel over logo-driven brands, demand for Mary Jane shoes and ballet-core footwear is rising.

The company said its flagship store ranked first by paid sales in men's and women's footwear among potential sellers during the Tmall 618 event in May 2026, which it announced on June 9, 2026. Second-quarter 2026 Mary Jane sales rose 398% year on year.

Hana Securities said in a March 2026 report that monthly online sales in China had expanded to around KRW 500 million. Export share was reported to have risen from about 15% in early 2025 to 30% by year-end, according to the same report.

Concentration is high: more than half of second-quarter 2026 sales came from Greater China, and the visa-free policy for Chinese travelers is set to end in late 2026, so policy changes bear directly on results.

The company is responding by negotiating with large retail groups in Japan, Southeast Asia and the Middle East. SK Securities said the brand, product and overseas demand had already been validated.

Public share data against listed peers is limited, so the company's position is best read through its all-season line-up and its China operating experience, as the company itself describes it.

In fashion, product development cycles and inventory management tend to decide how well a brand converts trend shifts into sales.

06

Outlook

The company's 2026 consolidated sales guidance, disclosed through a fair disclosure filing, is KRW 120 billion, roughly twice 2025 sales. Cumulative sales for the first three quarters of 2026 total KRW 73.2 billion, so reaching the target would require about KRW 46.8 billion in the fourth quarter.

The company expects the seasonal peak and new overseas store openings to strengthen growth momentum in the fourth quarter. In China, the company operated 31 stores at the end of September 2026 and targets 40 by year-end. Press reports say the plan extends to 80 stores in 2027.

The Japan joint venture AU BRANDZ JAPAN launched in August 2026 and began sales at Shinjuku Lumine 2.

The company lists growth of the Japan venture, expansion into Singapore, Malaysia and Australia, and an integrated operating system centered on its Shanghai R&D center as execution items for the KRW 120 billion roadmap.

It also plans to cut product development time from about eight months to about three months by linking planning, production and reorders. The company expects roughly half of 2026 sales to come from China. The next thing to check is whether the annual target shows up in quarterly results.

Bull factors

Sales growth pace

Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 24.4 billion, up 104.7% year on year. Cumulative sales for the first three quarters of 2026 reached KRW 73.2 billion, already above full-year 2025 sales of KRW 60 billion. The company set its 2026 sales guidance at KRW 120 billion.

Overseas store network

At the end of September 2026 the company ran 52 stores, including 44 overseas. It targets 40 stores in China by year-end, and its Japan joint venture launched in August 2026. It has also opened a store at ION Orchard in Singapore, widening its presence in major Asian retail districts.

Signs of margin recovery

Press reports put the operating margin in the second and third quarters of 2026 in the 20% range, above the 16.8% annual margin for 2025. Third-quarter operating profit rose 524.2% year on year. Analysts note that local production in China should improve margins as the China share of sales grows.

Bear factors

Concentration in Greater China

Analysts note that more than half of second-quarter 2026 sales came from Greater China. If the visa-free policy for Chinese travelers ends in late 2026 as scheduled, results would be affected, according to that analysis.

Expansion in Japan and Southeast Asia is underway, but its effect on diversifying sales is still at an early stage in quarterly results.

Multi-year profit decline

Operating profit fell from KRW 16.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 10.1 billion in 2025, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 12.9 billion to KRW 8.46 billion over the same period. Operating cash flow dropped from KRW 15.9 billion in 2023 to KRW 7.36 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 24.4% in 2023 to 41.3% in 2025.

Trading supply and share price volatility

Reports note that free float is only about 19%, which limits institutional trading. The stock was designated a short-term investment-caution stock on June 15, 2026 and again on July 13, 2026. The largest shareholder and related parties held 60.65% at the end of the first quarter of 2026.

Risk factors

Policy and regional concentration

The company depends heavily on Greater China, and results are sensitive to changes in China policy. The end of the visa-free policy is scheduled for late 2026. As overseas store counts grow, exposure to local regulation and lease terms also increases.

Costs and quarterly volatility

Fixed costs and initial overseas investment have risen with store expansion. First-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 65.7% from the prior quarter. Because quarterly sales swing with seasonality and store opening timing, trends are hard to read from single quarters.

Pressure to meet guidance

Reaching the KRW 120 billion annual guidance requires about KRW 46.8 billion more in the fourth quarter of 2026. A delay in overseas openings or a slowdown in consumer demand could widen the gap to target. Any change to the guidance would appear in company disclosures.

Overall view

AU Brandz's quarterly sales have grown quickly in 2026. Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 24.4 billion, up 104.7% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 5.08 billion, up 524.2%.

However, annual operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders declined for two consecutive years after 2023, and the operating margin fell from 38.7% in 2023 to 16.8% in 2025.

Cumulative sales for the first three quarters of 2026 of KRW 73.2 billion are more than half of the KRW 120 billion annual guidance, so sales in the remaining quarters are the key variable.

Store expansion and entry into Japan and Southeast Asia broaden the revenue base, while concentration in Greater China and the end of the visa-free policy in late 2026 remain open risks.

Market capitalization sits in a premium range over the equity attributable to controlling shareholders, so whether earnings recovery can support that premium is the central question. The next checkpoints are the 40-store China target by year-end 2026 and full-year 2026 preliminary results in early 2027.
