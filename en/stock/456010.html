02

Business structure

ICTK is a fabless company that designs and supplies security chips and security modules based on PUF technology. Daily Invest reported that security chips accounted for 51.47%, security modules and devices for 12.56%, and solution development services for 35.96% of cumulative revenue through Q3 2025.

A quantum-secure communication infrastructure project ran from 2020 to 2023, with LG Uplus, HYBE, Kakao Mobility and the Jeollanam-do Provincial Government among its participants, according to a Hana Securities company analysis dated February 2025.

In 2024 the company operated an open quantum test bed, developed PQC TLS and CA servers, and localized a PUF-based PCIe HSM that carries a quantum-resistant algorithm. The revenue mix combines product sales with development services, and project revenue tends to be recognized over several years.

According to the H1 2026 company summary, security chip supply to telecom operators and global big tech firms grew, but continued R&D spending produced an operating loss. A July 2026 research report noted that development of security chips with BTQ Technology was completed and mass production preparations have begun.

The key variable in the competitive landscape is the transition to post-quantum cryptography as quantum computing emerges. FnGuide's company summary attributed the 2025 deterioration to lower solution development revenue and weaker security chip sales.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 2.57 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.19 billion in 2023, up 141.1% year on year, and reached KRW 6.68 billion in 2024, up 8.0%, making 2024 the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period. Revenue fell 39.9% to KRW 4.02 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the decline to the end of an existing project in 2024 and new project awards secured at the end of 2025, and said revenue from the new project will be recognized in sequence through 2029.

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 3.33 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.36 billion in 2023, then widened to KRW 6.69 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.86 billion in 2025. The operating margin was -38.2% in 2023, the smallest loss margin in the period, and deteriorated to -220.5% in 2025.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 10.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.03 billion in 2023 and KRW 5.82 billion in 2024, then rose to KRW 6.68 billion in 2025. Net loss including non-controlling interests was also KRW 6.68 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 43.9 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 29.3 billion. The debt ratio climbed from 6.5% in 2024 to 66.6% in 2025. Operating cash flow was negative in every year from 2022 to 2025, at -KRW 2.94 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 399 million with an operating loss of KRW 2.89 billion. Q4 2025 revenue rose to KRW 2.38 billion, up 496.3% quarter on quarter, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 766 million.

Q1 2026 revenue fell to KRW 605 million, down 74.6% quarter on quarter, then rose to KRW 1.72 billion in Q2 and KRW 3.93 billion in Q3, two consecutive quarterly increases. Q3 2026 posted an operating profit of KRW 106 million, a swing to profit.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.22 billion in Q4 2025, then a loss of KRW 1.15 billion in Q1 2026 and a loss of KRW 12.8 billion in Q2 2026, before narrowing to a loss of KRW 4.66 billion in Q3 2026.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 16.4 billion. For the cumulative nine months of 2025, FnGuide cited one-off costs related to the listing and higher R&D spending as drivers of the operating loss.

05

Industry analysis

ICTK operates in the security chip and cryptography segment of the semiconductor industry. FnGuide's company summary projects that the embedded security market will grow at a 5.7% annual rate and reach roughly KRW 13 trillion by 2028.

The same summary notes that the spread of generative AI has increased demand for PUF-based authentication solutions to counter deepfake threats.

One demand driver is the transition to post-quantum cryptography as quantum computers emerge, and FnGuide described this transition as a transitional period in which the company's market response was limited in 2025.

At the end of September 2026, press reports by Daily Invest and Wide Economy said the shares surged on expectations of benefits from a government mandate for PQC adoption. The timing and scope of any mandate are not confirmed by these reports and should be checked against future policy announcements.

Project-based awards move quarterly revenue sharply, as shown by the move from KRW 399 million in Q3 2025 to KRW 2.38 billion in Q4 2025 and then to KRW 605 million in Q1 2026. The company's competitive position combines product sales with development services.

Within the semiconductor sector, security chip demand appears to follow telecom and platform operators' security investment schedules and policy timelines, and whether those schedules convert into actual orders is the item to watch.

Bear factors

Four years of operating losses and deep margin deficits

Operating losses have recurred from 2022 through 2025, and the KRW 8.86 billion loss in 2025 exceeded the KRW 6.69 billion loss in 2024. The 2025 operating margin of -220.5% is the lowest in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Revenue shrank to KRW 4.02 billion while the loss widened, which is the core bearish point on the cost structure.

Volatility in quarterly net losses

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued for three consecutive quarters from Q1 through Q3 2026. The KRW 12.8 billion loss in Q2 2026 was the largest among the disclosed quarters.

Revenue gaps and balance-sheet strain

Revenue fell 39.9% to KRW 4.02 billion in 2025, which the company linked to the end of an existing project in 2024. Total liabilities reached KRW 29.3 billion at the end of 2025, lifting the debt ratio to 66.6%. FnGuide assessed that the company's market response was limited during the PQC transition in 2025.

Risk factors

Dependence on project awards

Revenue moves sharply by quarter depending on when projects are awarded. It fell from KRW 2.38 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 605 million in Q1 2026. Even though new project revenue is spread through 2029, a gap in awards could make quarterly swings larger again.

Balance sheet and cash flow

Total liabilities were KRW 29.3 billion against total equity of KRW 43.9 billion at the end of 2025. The effect of the KRW 12.8 billion Q2 2026 net loss on equity and funding is an item to confirm in upcoming filings.

Technology transition and policy timing

The pace of the PQC transition and the timing of any government mandate will shape revenue opportunities. Late-September 2026 reports showed expectations being priced into the shares before any mandate was confirmed. The revenue contribution timing of the BTQ-linked security chip is an item to confirm in company disclosures.

Overall view

ICTK recorded operating losses from 2022 through 2025 but swung to an operating profit of KRW 106 million in Q3 2026. Revenue rose for two consecutive quarters from the Q1 2026 low of KRW 605 million to KRW 3.93 billion.

However, net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters total KRW 16.4 billion, and the KRW 12.8 billion loss in Q2 2026 was the largest among the disclosed quarters. On an annual basis, revenue fell 39.9% to KRW 4.02 billion in 2025, reflecting the end of an existing project in 2024.

On the business side, security chip supply expansion and the PQC transition are the bullish factors, while mandate timing uncertainty and project award gaps are the bearish factors. The outlook for the embedded security market and revenue from a new project recognized through 2029 provide medium-term visibility.

On valuation, an earnings-based multiple cannot be calculated because of the recent net losses, and market capitalization is well above controlling-shareholder equity. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results in the annual report and the revenue trend in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
