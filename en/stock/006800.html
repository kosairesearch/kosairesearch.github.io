Mirae Asset Securities: Core Recovery Versus Investment-Asset Swings

Mirae Asset Securities swung to a third-quarter 2026 loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 394.1 billion, yet its 2025 profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.57 trillion was the largest of the 2022–2025 period.

02

Business structure

Mirae Asset Securities earns revenue from brokerage, trading including principal investments, wealth management (WM), investment banking (IB) and overseas subsidiaries.

According to Korea Financial News, standalone net operating revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 600.8 billion, split roughly 49% brokerage, 30% trading, 18% WM and 4% IB. Brokerage commissions were KRW 330.6 billion in the same quarter, which shows how closely results track domestic trading volume.

Per company figures, client assets stood at KRW 625.5 trillion and pension assets at KRW 76.2 trillion at the end of the third quarter, a base that limited the decline during the market correction. Trading and investment-asset income, by contrast, passes through fair-value swings in listed and unlisted holdings.

A May 2026 Hana Securities report split investment-purpose assets into KRW 6 trillion of innovation-company stakes, KRW 2 trillion of alternative investments and KRW 4 trillion of IB and operating-related assets.

Overseas subsidiaries posted cumulative pre-tax profit of KRW 713 billion in the first nine months of 2026, up 138% year on year, which the company said was a record for that period. Management outlined expansion into Japan, Singapore, the United States and Australia, alongside digital-asset businesses.

In an October 2026 report, NH Investment & Securities pointed to the planned acquisition of Sanko Securities as a route into Japan's retail business.

In domestic brokerage, NH Investment & Securities said in April 2025 that the company's early rise among alternative trading system share leaders reflected its retail and institutional sales strength.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 19.2 trillion in 2022 to KRW 29.3 trillion in 2025, a 31.7% gain that makes 2025 the largest year of 2022–2025. Revenue has grown for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit fell 37.6% to KRW 521 billion in 2023, the low point, and then rose to KRW 1.19 trillion in 2024 and KRW 1.92 trillion in 2025, two years of consecutive growth.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders followed the same path, dropping to KRW 327 billion in 2023 before recovering to KRW 921.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.57 trillion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 4.4% in 2022 to 2.6% in 2023, 5.3% in 2024 and 6.5% in 2025, the highest of the four years.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.58 trillion in 2025, close to the profit attributable to controlling shareholders. At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 136.8 trillion against total equity of KRW 13.5 trillion, and the debt ratio rose from 863.6% in 2022 to 1,015.0% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was negative in each year from 2023 to 2025, at KRW -4.08 trillion, KRW -10.3 trillion and KRW -6.86 trillion. Because brokers' cash flows move with financial-asset trading, a single year is a weak gauge of core cash generation.

Quarterly operating profit climbed from KRW 222.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 845.7 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 1.38 trillion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 2.49 trillion in the second quarter.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 7.5 trillion, down 65.3% from the prior quarter, and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 502.3 billion and a loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 394.1 billion.

The company said that excluding the SpaceX-related valuation loss of KRW 514 billion and private-equity losses, third-quarter pre-tax profit would have been about KRW 380 billion. Overseas subsidiaries recorded a pre-tax loss of KRW 139.3 billion in the same quarter.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, totals KRW 3.07 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic stock trading value peaked in the second quarter of 2026 and then fell 42% on a daily-average basis in the third quarter, and that decline flowed straight into brokerage revenue.

Third-quarter domestic equity brokerage commissions were KRW 215.9 billion, down 52% from the prior quarter but up 42% from a year earlier. Overseas equity brokerage commissions were KRW 114.7 billion, down 36% from the prior quarter, and IB fees were KRW 28.5 billion, down 34%.

Securities earnings move sharply quarter to quarter with market prices, valuation gains, trading volume, interest rates and the value of overseas investments. The company said brokerage commissions stayed at the industry's top level despite the sharp drop in trading value.

In July 2026, Sangsangin Securities expected a rise in domestic stock and ETF trading value to lift brokerage earnings. On digital assets, a token-securities framework is scheduled to take effect in February 2027, but Hana Securities expects the market to form gradually rather than quickly.

Among domestic brokers, Mirae Asset Securities stands out for expanding overseas subsidiaries and wealth-management assets together.

06

Outlook

The core of the 2027–2029 shareholder-return policy is a minimum return of 35% of consolidated adjusted net profit through dividends and share buybacks combined.

About 65 million of the 108.4 million treasury shares held, roughly 60%, are planned for cancellation right after the 2027 annual general meeting, a stake worth about KRW 1.4 trillion at the November 11, 2026 close.

The company said it is considering further cancellation or use of the remaining treasury shares as return funding. Since 2021, dividends and cancellations have returned more than KRW 2.2 trillion to shareholders, and the return ratio over the last two years was about 40%.

On overseas profits, the company plans to channel earnings from foreign subsidiaries back to Korea as funding for shareholder returns.

On digital assets, Hana Securities saw an advantage in the combined securities, asset-management and crypto-exchange structure if token-securities markets expand, but it expected limited near-term earnings impact from the February 2027 start.

The same report noted that macro swings in investment-purpose assets can raise earnings volatility.

The planned Sanko Securities acquisition, highlighted by NH Investment & Securities as a step toward overseas retail expansion, will need to be tracked through future disclosures for its closing timing and integration results.

08

Bull factors

Earnings recovery in 2025 and overseas growth

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 70.3% in 2025 to KRW 1.57 trillion, and the operating margin of 6.5% was the highest since 2022. Company figures show nine-month 2026 cumulative pre-tax profit and net profit up 156% and 148% year on year.

Overseas subsidiaries' cumulative pre-tax profit of KRW 713 billion is the highest on record for the first nine months.

A defined shareholder-return framework

The 2027–2029 policy combines dividends and buybacks with a floor of 35% of consolidated adjusted net profit. About 65 million treasury shares are planned for cancellation after the 2027 annual general meeting. The company says it has returned more than KRW 2.2 trillion since 2021 through dividends and cancellations.

A fee base in wealth management

Per company figures, domestic client assets were KRW 625.5 trillion and pension assets KRW 76.2 trillion at the end of the third quarter of 2026. WM fee income in the quarter was KRW 123.6 billion. In July 2026, Sangsangin Securities expected growth in financial products and pension assets to support WM earnings.

09

Bear factors

Volatility from investment-asset valuation losses

The SpaceX-related valuation loss in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 514 billion on a consolidated basis, with a pre-tax loss of KRW 520.6 billion and a net loss of KRW 405.6 billion.

Management said core earnings stayed profitable excluding valuation items, but reported quarterly results depended heavily on the price of a single holding. In July 2026, Sangsangin Securities had flagged that much of the second-quarter valuation gain could reverse in the third quarter.

Slower trading value and lower commissions

Average daily domestic stock trading value fell 42% from the second quarter, and brokerage commissions of KRW 330.6 billion dropped 47% from the prior quarter. IB fees of KRW 28.5 billion fell 34% from the prior quarter. A prolonged market slowdown could slow the recovery of fee-driven earnings.

Leverage and cash-flow pressure

The debt ratio stood at 1,015.0% at the end of 2025. Operating cash flow was negative in each year from 2023 through 2025. Overseas subsidiaries also booked a pre-tax loss of KRW 139.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026, adding to quarterly swings.

10

Risk factors

Fair-value risk in investment assets

Unlisted and overseas innovation holdings are marked to fair value, so price moves hit quarterly earnings directly. In a May 2026 Hana Securities report, innovation-company stakes were KRW 6 trillion of roughly KRW 12 trillion in investment-purpose assets, the largest share. A sharp move in one holding can push a quarter into loss even when core business remains profitable.

Market-volume and regulatory risk

Domestic trading value shifts quickly with rates, indices and policy, as the 42% third-quarter drop from a second-quarter peak shows. A token-securities framework is slated for February 2027, but Hana Securities expects limited near-term earnings impact because the market should build gradually.

If the regulatory timeline and market development diverge, the contribution from digital-asset businesses could also be delayed.

Overseas expansion and acquisition risk

Overseas subsidiaries set a cumulative record through nine months but posted a third-quarter pre-tax loss of KRW 139.3 billion. NH Investment & Securities highlighted the planned Sanko Securities acquisition for Japanese retail, and new-market entry carries upfront cost and delayed-payoff risk. Closing timing and integration results remain items for future disclosures.

12

Overall view

2025 marked a year of earnings recovery, with the operating margin reaching 6.5%, the highest since 2022, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders rising to KRW 1.57 trillion.

In the third quarter of 2026, lower domestic trading value and SpaceX-related valuation losses combined to produce an operating loss of KRW 502.3 billion and a loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 394.1 billion.

The company said third-quarter pre-tax profit excluding valuation items would have been about KRW 380 billion, which shows how heavily quarterly results depend on items outside core business. Cumulative nine-month results are, per company statements, the highest for that period.

The shareholder-return framework for 2027–2029 sets a 35% floor and a planned cancellation of about 65 million shares. Broker targets diverge widely, and the spread reflects different assumptions. The debt ratio of 1,015.0% and three straight years of negative operating cash flow remain financial items to track.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results, the February 2027 start of the token-securities framework, the 2026 annual report and the execution of the share cancellation.
