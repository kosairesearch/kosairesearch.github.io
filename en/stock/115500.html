Korea Computer & Systems: Margins Up as Sales Fall

Korea Computer & Systems' sales have declined for two straight years, yet its operating margin rose from 1.3% in 2022 to 8.3% in 2025, while third-quarter 2026 operating profit grew and net profit attributable to owners fell.

  1. 1

    Sales peaked at KRW 47.7 billion in 2023, then fell to KRW 29.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 24.9 billion in 2025, the smallest year in the 2022–2025 span.

  2. 2

    Operating margin rose from 1.3% in 2022 to 8.3% in 2025, and operating profit reached KRW 2.06 billion in 2025, up 4.0% year on year.

  3. 3

    Operating profit bottomed at KRW 239 million in the first quarter of 2026, then rose for two straight quarters to KRW 567 million in the third quarter, up 97.9% year on year.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Korea Computer & Systems, founded in 1974, runs NonStop SI, Enterprise Solution, transportation infrastructure and KIOSK terminal businesses, built on 24-hour, non-stop real-time system technology.

Its main products include NS16000 Series hardware and Enterprise Solution software, and it is pushing CDC, BI, application virtualization and security solutions. A WiseReport company monitor summary puts maintenance and services at about 73% of revenue and products at about 26%.

Butler, a financial data site, instead splits second-quarter 2026 revenue into services at 64% and financial solutions at 36%, so the mix depends on the source and period. A KB Securities report from February 2025 said the services share was in the high-50s percent range for 2022 and 2023.

Product sales swing with customer investment decisions, and the same report traced the 2024 revenue drop to weaker product sales as customers cut spending. On quantum, the company offers the KEV7 communications encryption chip and quantum random number generator technology.

WiseReport described its terminal encryption for power distribution intelligence systems as a KRW 20 billion scale business, and put 2025 R&D spending at KRW 2.74 billion, or 11.0% of revenue.

Compared with large SI peers such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, the company is much smaller and concentrated in specialized terminal and security niches.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales rose from KRW 41.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 47.7 billion in 2023, up 14.5%, before falling to KRW 29.5 billion in 2024, down 38.1%, and KRW 24.9 billion in 2025, down 15.8%. The largest year in the 2022–2025 span was 2023 and the smallest was 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 545 million in 2022 to KRW 3.15 billion in 2023, up 478.9%, then KRW 1.98 billion in 2024, down 37.1%, and recovered to KRW 2.06 billion in 2025, up 4.0%. Operating margin held at 6.7% in 2024 and 8.3% in 2025, above the 6.6% of 2023 even as sales shrank.

WiseReport's commentary on 2025 said costs were cut and the cost structure of the solutions business improved, lifting profitability while sales fell.

Net profit attributable to owners went from KRW 829 million in 2022 to KRW 2.77 billion in 2023, up 234.0%, KRW 2 billion in 2024, down 27.8%, and KRW 2.53 billion in 2025, up 26.4%, which exceeded operating profit that year.

Cash generation weakened, with operating cash flow falling from KRW 3.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 62.79 million in 2025.

Quarterly sales jumped from KRW 4.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 11.9 billion in the fourth quarter, up 176.0% on the prior quarter, then dropped to KRW 5.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, down 57.0%, before reaching KRW 5.98 billion in the second quarter and KRW 5.23 billion in the third, up 21.8% year on year.

Operating profit fell from KRW 866 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 239 million in the first quarter of 2026, down 72.4%, then rose to KRW 263 million and KRW 567 million.

Net profit attributable to owners, however, slid from KRW 1.12 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 231 million in the third quarter of 2026, moving against operating profit. The last four quarters total KRW 2.11 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Downstream demand feeds directly into results. KB Securities' February 2025 report attributed the 2024 slump to a weaker downstream market and reduced customer spending on products.

A September 2023 report said Korea's server market was expected to grow on planned data center expansions by large digital service providers, but that delayed GPU adoption had raised uncertainty. That report is dated and does not describe current demand.

On policy, a 2025 press report said barrier-free system mandates for public facilities and private sites were being discussed, which could lift replacement demand for kiosks and terminals.

An October 2026 report said unmanned service terminals are spreading across retail, restaurants, finance and public institutions as labor-saving demand grows, while analysts in the same report urged a focus on actual supply contracts and operating profit gains rather than short-lived theme trading.

In quantum, a April 2026 press report said security and IT stocks surged on hopes for quantum computing, and KB Securities named the timing of quantum communications commercialization as a key risk.

Against large SI peers such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, the company is a small player focused on specialized terminal and security niches.

Outlook

At the March 26, 2026 annual general meeting, shareholders approved the 24th-term financial statements and confirmed a cash dividend.

The dividend total was disclosed as KRW 2.16 billion, and Kim Kwang-muk, Lee Ho-sung, Jung Byung-chan and Park Beom-seop were re-elected as representative director, inside director, outside director and auditor, respectively. That approval fixed the disclosed basis for 2025 annual results.

On business development, a 2025 press report said the company is developing an integrated unmanned counter solution that combines AI-based consultation and guidance, with cooperation talks with financial firms reported.

Expansion of barrier-free system mandates to public facilities and private sites would, by the same logic, lift kiosk and terminal replacement demand. In quantum, an August 2024 report said a KRW 3 billion scale quantum science R&D project would proceed after exemption from preliminary feasibility review.

The next checkpoint is whether the third-quarter 2026 operating profit gains carry into the fourth quarter of 2026 and the first quarter of 2027. Whether sales again cluster at year-end, as in the fourth quarter of 2025 at KRW 11.9 billion, will be visible in the 2026 annual report. Whether net profit attributable to owners follows operating profit is the other point to watch.

08

Bull factors

Structural margin improvement

Operating margin rose from 1.3% in 2022 to 6.6% in 2023 and 8.3% in 2025. Profitability improved even as sales fell in 2024 and 2025, which WiseReport tied to cost cuts and a better solutions cost structure.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 567 million, up 97.9% year on year, shows the improvement continuing on a quarterly basis.

Links to quantum and unmanned terminal markets

On quantum communications, the company has its own KEV7 encryption chip and quantum random number generator technology. In unmanned terminals, it has delivered barrier-free terminals and is developing an AI-based unmanned counter solution.

Press reports in April and October 2026 described strong share price moves on quantum and kiosk expectations, and whether demand converts into actual supply contracts is the key test.

Service-led revenue base

A high share of maintenance and services offers a base that partly absorbs swings in product sales. KB Securities put the services share in the high-50s percent range for 2022 and 2023, and WiseReport cited the stable revenue base from maintenance and services as a support for 2025 results. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 5.23 billion rose 21.8% year on year.

Bear factors

Two years of sales decline

Sales fell from KRW 47.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 29.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 24.9 billion in 2025, two straight years of decline.

First-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 5.1 billion was down 57.0% from the prior quarter, and the second and third quarters recovered but remain below the KRW 11.9 billion of the fourth quarter of 2025.

Operating profit and net profit diverge

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose 97.9% year on year, but net profit attributable to owners fell 34.8% to KRW 231 million. Until the link from operating gains to net profit is established, earnings momentum reads as mixed.

Theme-driven share price volatility

Press reports described limit-up moves in April 2026 on quantum expectations and a strong rally in October on kiosk themes. Analysts cautioned that such spikes should be checked against actual supply contracts and operating profit gains. Theme-driven flows can move the share price independently of reported results.

10

Risk factors

Customer capex cycle and product sales swings

Product sales are sensitive to customer investment cuts, which explain part of the 2024 revenue decline. A prolonged downstream slowdown could make it hard for service revenue alone to offset lower product sales. Quarterly results are also volatile, as the fourth quarter of 2025 showed with sales concentrated at year-end.

Timing of quantum commercialization

KB Securities named the commercialization of quantum communications as a key risk factor. If commercialization slips, the point at which the technology converts into revenue could also be delayed. The pace of the large quantum R&D project reported in August 2024 is another variable for timing.

Changes in financial structure and cash flow

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 26.5% in 2024 to 40.8% in 2025. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 3.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.24 billion in 2023, KRW 2.36 billion in 2024 and KRW 62.79 million in 2025.

All KRW 19.7 billion of total equity is attributable to owners of the parent, but the cash generation trend needs separate tracking.

12

Overall view

Korea Computer & Systems' sales have fallen in 2024 and 2025 after peaking at KRW 47.7 billion in 2023, yet its operating margin climbed from 1.3% in 2022 to 8.3% in 2025, which is the core message of the disclosed results.

In 2026, operating profit rose for two straight quarters after the first-quarter trough, and third-quarter operating profit was up 97.9% year on year. In the same quarter, net profit attributable to owners fell 34.8%, and the last four quarters total KRW 2.11 billion, below the KRW 2.53 billion of 2025.

Bullish points are the margin improvement, a service-led revenue base and links to quantum and unmanned terminal markets. Bearish points are the two years of sales decline, mixed earnings, theme-driven share price swings, and the higher debt ratio alongside weaker operating cash flow.

Theme-based expectations need to be tested against disclosed supply contracts, and quantum commercialization timing and the customer capex cycle remain open variables. Valuation reads as a premium to book value and as a PER above the industry average in WiseReport's April 2026 data.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report, due by late March 2027, and the first-quarter 2027 report, due May 15, 2027.
