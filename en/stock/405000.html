02

Business structure

Plasmapp is a medical device company that sells low-temperature plasma sterilizers, implant surface treatment equipment and consumables.

Its main businesses are the STERLINK low-temperature plasma sterilization solution, the ACTILINK implant surface regeneration and activation solution, and consumables such as sterilization pouches and sterilants.

In the first quarter of 2026, equipment accounted for 60.0% of revenue and consumables for 37.5%, and exports made up 41.5%, according to an independent research report dated July 2026. The same report described the model as one in which equipment sales lead to recurring consumables revenue.

The customer base was centered on dental clinics, but the company said the larger STERI 700 sterilizer, which has MFDS approval, would open ophthalmology and orthopedic practices that handle large instruments. The STERI 700 chamber is about four times wider than the company's existing 7L and 14L small sterilizers.

In ophthalmology, the company is testing products with Alcon. Overseas, Plasmapp has signed a supply contract with QMED Master Distribution in Florida, and it has set up US and European subsidiaries and signed agreements with partners in 12 countries.

Competition comes from several equipment makers in the low-temperature sterilization and implant surface treatment markets for dental and eye care. Because equipment placements and consumables move together, the pace of installed-base growth largely determines the size of consumables revenue.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue on a filed basis moved from KRW 13.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.9 billion in 2023 (down 2.9% year on year), KRW 10.1 billion in 2024 (down 21.5%) and KRW 8.48 billion in 2025 (down 16.3%).

Revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest in the 2022–2025 period. A Korean trade report said revenue had risen from KRW 6.4 billion in 2021 to KRW 13.3 billion in 2022 before the 2023 decline.

The operating loss widened from KRW 17.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 20.6 billion in 2023, then narrowed to KRW 17.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.6 billion in 2025, and the operating margin improved from -174.2% in 2024 to -89.6% in 2025.

The company attributed the cost reduction to a slimmer fixed-cost structure, better outsourcing and purchasing terms, and workforce efficiency, and said the operating loss fell by more than KRW 10 billion.

The loss attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 17.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 20.8 billion in 2024 and then KRW 6.59 billion in 2025, while the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 6.59 billion in 2025.

Capital impairment that was near 60% at the end of 2024 was fully resolved during 2025, and the debt ratio fell from 463.6% to 259.3%, with total liabilities of KRW 14.8 billion. Operating cash outflow narrowed from -KRW 16 billion in 2022 and -KRW 22 billion in 2023 to -KRW 4.52 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue of KRW 4.19 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 rose 94.0% from the prior quarter and was the highest quarter in 2025.

Revenue then fell to KRW 2.82 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 2.84 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.64 billion in the third quarter, which was down 42.4% from the prior quarter and 24.1% from a year earlier.

The operating loss has been reported in each of the five quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

The loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 2.84 billion in the third quarter of 2026, the largest of the five quarters, and totaled KRW 8.77 billion over the last four quarters. In all five quarters, the loss attributable to controlling shareholders was larger than the operating loss.

05

Industry analysis

In low-temperature plasma sterilization, the main barrier to overseas sales is certification. The company said it is working through country-specific regulatory steps with the aim of obtaining FDA 510(k) clearance and CE marking in the first half of 2027.

Because certification timing sets when overseas sales can begin, the pace of North American and European revenue growth depends on the regulatory schedule. Domestically, the customer base is moving from dental clinics toward ophthalmology and orthopedic practices that need to sterilize large instruments.

The company said it holds 174 patents and 45 certifications including FDA clearance and sells in about 55 countries, but this was its own description at the time of its 2022 listing and cannot be verified as a current market share figure.

Equipment revenue can swing from quarter to quarter depending on contract and installation timing, while consumables are generally described as the steadier layer that builds on the installed base.

Revenue peaked in the fourth quarter of 2025 and then moved in mixed directions, down in the first quarter of 2026, slightly up in the second and down in the third.

Overseas, the US distributor QMED holds a two-year priority negotiation right for the Mexican ophthalmology market, which gives Plasmapp a base for expansion into Latin America. Ophthalmology demand is the common growth thread in both domestic and overseas plans, which defines the company's position in the sector.

Outlook

The company said it plans to begin domestic sales of the STERI 700 after receiving MFDS approval. In ophthalmology, it is testing products with Alcon and pursuing new customers.

An independent research report from July 2026 said the company plans to launch the large-capacity STERI 700 sterilizer and the Plasma Endo root canal treatment device. The company said in June 2026 that PLASMA ENDO was in the approval process with a target launch in the third quarter of 2026.

That target date has passed, so the launch status and when it will show up in revenue are the next items to check. The QMED agreement covers a minimum purchase of 390 units, including 300 STERLINK U510 units and 90 STERI 700 units, worth about KRW 3.5 billion, with deliveries on a quarterly schedule.

The company said its annual active trading customers more than doubled year on year and that repurchase rates in key customer groups recovered to above 70%.

The independent research report said that if 2026 revenue of KRW 18 billion and breakeven were achieved, the company could ease concerns about its listing status and could be revalued as a plasma medtech platform company.

However, revenue in the first three quarters of 2026 stayed below the fourth-quarter 2025 level of KRW 4.19 billion, and that scenario would require fourth-quarter revenue to rise sharply above each quarter of 2026 so far. The FDA and CE outcomes in the first half of 2027 will mark a key point for when overseas sales can expand.

Bull factors

Cost restructuring and a rebuilt equity base

The 2025 operating loss narrowed to KRW 7.6 billion from KRW 17.7 billion in 2024, and the operating margin improved to -89.6% from -174.2%. The company cited a slimmer fixed-cost base, better outsourcing and purchasing terms, and workforce efficiency as the drivers. Capital impairment was fully resolved during 2025, which left total equity positive at the end of the year.

New products and certification timeline

The large-capacity STERI 700 has MFDS approval and is set for domestic sales, with the customer base widening toward ophthalmology and orthopedics. PLASMA ENDO had a target launch in the third quarter of 2026.

If FDA 510(k) clearance and CE certification are obtained on the targeted schedule in the first half of 2027, overseas sales become possible, so whether these milestones reach revenue is the key test for the bull case.

Overseas contracts and export mix

The QMED agreement commits a minimum purchase of 390 units, including 300 STERLINK U510 and 90 STERI 700 units, worth about KRW 3.5 billion. It also includes a priority negotiation right for the Mexican ophthalmology market and a North American priority purchase right for the STERI 700.

A KRW 2.5 billion contract with a dental implant company in March 2026 also carried exclusive sales and priority purchase rights. Exports made up 41.5% of revenue in the first quarter of 2026.

Revenue has not sustained the recovery seen after the fourth quarter of 2025 peak, and filed results do not yet show how much new-product revenue is contributing.

Wider losses to controlling shareholders and a thin equity cushion

Frequent financing and share dilution

Overall view

Plasmapp narrowed its 2025 operating loss to KRW 7.6 billion, resolved capital impairment and reshaped its cost base.

However, revenue in the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 2.82 billion, KRW 2.84 billion and KRW 1.64 billion, below the KRW 4.19 billion peak of the fourth quarter of 2025, and the operating loss and the loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened across five consecutive loss-making quarters.

On the positive side are the STERI 700 approval, the PLASMA ENDO development and contracts with QMED and a dental implant company that broaden the revenue base.

On the negative side are the KRW 8.77 billion loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, a thin equity base and reliance on outside financing, including the August 2026 share issuance.

On valuation, no earnings-based multiple is available, so the comparison runs through equity and revenue. The 2026 revenue scenario of KRW 18 billion would require fourth-quarter revenue to rise sharply.

The next items to check are the annual figures in the 2026 annual report, the FDA and CE outcomes in the first half of 2027, and quarterly revenue in the first quarter of 2027.
