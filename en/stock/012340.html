Nuin TEK: Profit Turn Meets Balance-Sheet Risk

02

Business structure

Nuin TEK was founded in 1977 and listed on KOSDAQ in 1997 as a manufacturer of capacitors and metallized film. By product, film capacitors (PECAP) account for the largest share at 55.73%, followed by metallized film, a capacitor raw material, at 28.51%, AC capacitors at 11.01%, and other products at 4.75%.

The automotive capacitor business centers on capacitors used in EV and hybrid vehicle inverters, with a supply history to Hyundai Motor's Ioniq EV and plug-in hybrid models.

Power capacitors are supplied as low-, high-, and extra-high-voltage power-factor correction products for power facilities, factories, transformers, transmission lines, and energy storage systems (ESS). The company operates subsidiaries in China, Romania, Thailand, and Mexico.

Orders and supply volumes for automotive capacitors to European automakers have recently been increasing.

In capacitors and metallized film, the company competes with domestic passive-component makers such as Samwha Capacitor, and its bargaining power with automakers over supply prices is assessed as relatively limited. Overall, its revenue structure is closely tied to demand cycles in the broader electronic components industry.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose for three consecutive years from KRW 75.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 79.1 billion in 2023 (up 5.0%), KRW 79.5 billion in 2024 (up 0.5%), and KRW 82.8 billion in 2025 (up 4.1%).

Over the same period, the operating line stayed in the red for four straight years, with an operating loss of KRW 8.22 billion in 2022, KRW 11.1 billion in 2023 (loss widened), KRW 5.86 billion in 2024 (loss narrowed), and KRW 7.23 billion in 2025 (loss widened again).

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders also posted losses for four consecutive years, from a loss of KRW 9.63 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.8 billion in 2023 (wider), KRW 6.57 billion in 2024 (narrower), and KRW 12.7 billion in 2025 (wider).

The wider 2025 net loss was affected by other expenses surging from KRW 400 million to KRW 4.2 billion. The operating margin moved from -10.9% in 2022 to -14.0% in 2023, improved to -7.4% in 2024, and slipped again to -8.7% in 2025.

By quarter, revenue bottomed at KRW 20.2 billion in Q3 2025 (down 6.2% quarter-on-quarter) before rising for three straight quarters to KRW 20.4 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 23.1 billion in Q1 2026, and KRW 26.5 billion in Q2 2026.

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 24.48 million in Q1 2026 and reached KRW 233 million in Q2 2026 (up 851.4% quarter-on-quarter, turning positive year-on-year).

Controlling-shareholder net profit also turned positive at KRW 303 million in Q2 2026 versus both the prior quarter and the year-earlier period, though the trailing four quarters (Q3 2025 through Q2 2026) still show a cumulative net loss of KRW 9.1 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

Operating cash flow was negative from 2022 to 2024 (-KRW 3.09 billion, -KRW 4.06 billion, -KRW 1.23 billion) before turning positive at KRW 5.4 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The EV and hybrid vehicle market, the main downstream industry, is in a phase where demand for inverter and power-conversion capacitors is steadily rising as automakers shift their vehicle mix toward eco-friendly models. In Europe, orders and supply volumes of automotive capacitors to OEM customers have been expanding.

In response, the company is first pursuing efficiency gains and process optimization on existing production lines, with capacity expansion and additional equipment investment to be considered in stages if order growth continues.

The power capacitor segment is seeing its application scope broaden amid the spread of renewable energy infrastructure such as solar, wind, and ESS.

In the domestic passive-components industry, the company competes with firms such as Samwha Capacitor, and structural constraints have been noted in its ability to pass rising costs through to automaker customers in pricing.

An industry source noted that whether rising orders translate into actual production volume and higher utilization will be the key variable for future performance.

Despite structural growth in the downstream industry, Nuin TEK's profitability has remained in the red for years, a gap between the industry cycle and the company's own results that has drawn attention.

06

Outlook

The company has set a consolidated revenue target of KRW 100 billion for 2026. For the second half, it plans to diversify its revenue base by continuing growth in automotive capacitors and metallized film while expanding power capacitor sales.

Some new projects with existing customers are already generating revenue, and the company expects additional new projects to be reflected in results progressively from the fourth quarter of 2026.

In response to rising orders and supply volumes to European OEM customers, it plans to first pursue production efficiency gains and then adjust capacity in stages according to order flow.

On the financial side, the company cleared accumulated deficits and recapitalized through a 5-for-1 capital reduction and a KRW 12.8 billion rights offering in April 2026. A separate KRW 3 billion third-party allotment, in which controlling shareholder and CEO Jang Gi-su participated, was also completed.

Management has stated it will accelerate efforts to enhance corporate and shareholder value based on this balance-sheet restructuring and the operating profit turnaround in its core businesses.

Bull factors

Continuity of the Quarterly Profit Turnaround

Operating profit was positive in both the first and second quarters of 2026. Controlling-shareholder net profit also turned positive at KRW 303 million in Q2 2026. Rising sales of automotive capacitors and metallized film were the main drivers.

Expanding Orders from European Automakers

Orders and supply volumes of automotive capacitors to European OEM customers have been increasing. The company is first improving existing production line efficiency and reviewing phased capacity expansion in line with order flow. This could also help reduce dependence on any single automaker customer.

Expansion into Power Capacitors and Renewable Energy

The company has stated plans to diversify its revenue base by adding power capacitor sales in the second half. Power capacitors are supplied to power facilities, transformers, transmission lines, and ESS. The 2026 revenue target of KRW 100 billion is also based on this diversification strategy.

09

Bear factors

Repeated External Fundraising and Shareholder Dilution

Nuin TEK conducted a KRW 26.1 billion rights offering in 2023, followed by another worth KRW 12.8 billion in 2026. Shareholders who participated in the 2023 offering reportedly suffered a loss rate of 77%.

New shares issued without a lock-up period can raise concerns about overhang selling pressure immediately after listing.

Management-Issue Designation and Capital Impairment History

On March 19, 2026, Nuin TEK was designated a management-issue stock due to five consecutive years of operating losses. Earlier, its capital impairment ratio had expanded to 43.48%, leading to an investment-alert designation as well.

Although the capital reduction and rights offering restructured the balance sheet, the possibility of re-designation cannot be fully ruled out.

Structural Cost Pressure and Weak Bargaining Power with Automakers

The company's relatively weak bargaining power with automakers has resulted in a persistent inability to fully pass rising costs through to selling prices. This has been cited as the core reason for years of operating losses. If raw material prices or exchange rates worsen again, the recent profitability improvement could reverse.

Risk factors

Listing-Maintenance Requirement Risk

The exchange raised the market-cap threshold for delisting review to KRW 30 billion in 2026. Even after the capital reduction and rights offering, Nuin TEK's market capitalization has remained near or below this threshold, leaving it exposed to renewed delisting risk. Whether share price and market-cap requirements are met needs ongoing monitoring.

Share Price Volatility Risk

In October 2026, Nuin TEK's share price repeatedly hit the daily upper limit across multiple trading sessions. On October 19, it closed up 29.88% at KRW 878, the daily price limit. As a low-priced stock, it can react sensitively to short-term supply-and-demand shifts given large price swings relative to trading value.

Governance and Related-Party Transaction Risk

There have been reports that revenue has flowed to a company related to the owner's family. In some cases, Nuin TEK posted losses while the related company reportedly turned a profit. The fairness and continuation of such internal transactions warrant scrutiny.

Overall view

Nuin TEK posted operating profit for two consecutive quarters in the first and second quarters of 2026, showing signs of emerging from four straight years of losses. Rising automotive capacitor and metallized film sales, along with expanding European OEM orders, are cited as the main background to this improvement.

However, the trailing four quarters (Q3 2025 through Q2 2026) still show a cumulative net loss of KRW 9.1 billion attributable to controlling shareholders, so whether the quarterly profit translates into a full-year turnaround requires further confirmation.

On the financial side, the 2026 management-issue designation, a 5-for-1 capital reduction, and a KRW 12.8 billion rights offering were all carried out around the same time. Market capitalization remains near the exchange's raised listing-maintenance threshold, so renewed delisting risk cannot be ruled out.

Ultimately, this is a point where both the durability of the profit turnaround and the pace of balance-sheet stabilization need to be confirmed together.
