Nuin TEK: Back in Operating Profit in H1 2026, Balance Sheet Still the Test

02

Business structure

Nuin TEK manufactures and sells capacitors and metallized films and is listed on KOSDAQ. The company supplies capacitors for home appliances and renewable energy and metallized films to domestic and overseas markets, with overseas affiliates in China, Romania, Thailand and Mexico.

Its main consolidated subsidiaries are a Chinese capacitor maker and NUINTEK EUROPE SRL, which makes metallized film.

A product mix shown on Alphasquare lists DC-Link capacitors for renewable energy at 51.03%, metallized film at 32.26%, AC capacitors at 12.04% and raw materials and other items at 4.67%, although the reference year for that split is not stated on the page.

According to a performance comment on Wisereport, the company exclusively supplies DC-Link capacitors for the Ioniq 5 and Carnival HEV, and it plans supply for new models including the Sorento and Avante. The company said automotive capacitor sales rose 30% and metallized film sales rose 11% in H1 2026.

Wisereport also notes the company is widening supply to global automakers through inverter manufacturers. Peers in the same electronic components sector include Samsung Electro-Mechanics, LG Innotek and Isupetasys, but Nuin TEK is a smaller company focused on capacitors and films, so direct comparison is limited. Raw material prices and labor costs drive its cost of sales.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on filed figures, consolidated revenue rose from KRW 75.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 79.1 billion in 2023 (up 5.0% year on year), KRW 79.5 billion in 2024 (up 0.5%) and KRW 82.8 billion in 2025 (up 4.1%), with 2025 the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating losses were KRW 8.22 billion in 2022, KRW 11.1 billion in 2023, KRW 5.86 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.23 billion in 2025, and operating margins were -10.9%, -14.0%, -7.4% and -8.7%, marking four consecutive years of losses.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 9.63 billion in 2022, KRW 12.8 billion in 2023, KRW 6.57 billion in 2024 and KRW 12.7 billion in 2025, while net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same loss levels for each year.

The company attributed the 2025 deterioration to higher raw material and auxiliary material costs for eco-friendly automotive capacitors, which raised cost of sales. It also cited impairment losses on tangible and intangible assets, which increased other non-operating expenses, and higher labor costs.

The debt-to-equity ratio was 342.9% in 2022, 139.6% in 2023, 194.9% in 2024 and 383.1% in 2025, and total liabilities were KRW 58 billion against total equity of KRW 15.1 billion at end-2025.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 3.09 billion in 2022, -KRW 4.06 billion in 2023 and -KRW 1.23 billion in 2024, then turned positive at KRW 5.4 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 20.2 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 20.4 billion in Q4 2025 (up 1.2% quarter on quarter), KRW 23.1 billion in Q1 2026 (up 13.0%), KRW 26.5 billion in Q2 2026 (up 14.8%) and KRW 27 billion in Q3 2026 (up 1.7% quarter on quarter and up 33.5% year on year), rising for four consecutive quarters.

Operating losses were KRW 1.2 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 3.81 billion in Q4 2025. Operating profit was KRW 24.48 million in Q1 2026 and KRW 233 million in Q2 2026, which was up 851.4% quarter on quarter, before an operating loss of KRW 155 million in Q3 2026, which was smaller than a year earlier.

Among the last five quarters, the Q4 2025 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.71 billion was the largest. Net income turned positive at KRW 303 million in Q2 2026 before a net loss of KRW 1.76 billion in Q3 2026.

The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 9.4 billion. Revenue has grown steadily, but quarterly profit remains volatile, and the Q3 2026 operating loss came when revenue growth slowed to 1.7% quarter on quarter.

05

Industry analysis

Nuin TEK's core demand comes from DC-Link capacitors for electric and hybrid vehicles. Wisereport notes that sales volumes expanded on higher demand for electric and hybrid vehicles. Press reports also cite eco-friendly vehicle and solar capacitors as its main products.

Automotive component revenue moves with model adoption and mass-production timing, so the start of production for new models is a key variable for revenue. The planned supply for new models such as the Sorento and Avante reflects this structure.

On the industry cycle, higher electric vehicle demand is a favorable factor. Higher raw material costs, however, pressure margins, as shown by the higher cost of sales in 2025 from rising raw and auxiliary material costs.

In competition, Nuin TEK is a smaller specialist focused on capacitors and metallized films, unlike large electronic component makers. In an Alphasquare comparison of 81 stocks in the electrical equipment sector, its market capitalization ranks near the bottom, and the date of that comparison is not stated on the page.

Its scale and product range differ greatly from large component makers such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek. Its industry position therefore appears to depend on mass-production results for specific vehicle models and on cost control.

Outlook

The company said it will extend growth in metallized film and automotive capacitors in the second half of 2026 and expand power capacitor sales to build a more stable earnings base, according to a report dated 5 August 2026.

Wisereport notes that exclusive DC-Link supply for new models such as the Sorento and Avante is scheduled, and that the company intends to widen supply to global automakers through inverter manufacturers.

On capital, the board decided on 11 March 2026 a rights offering of about KRW 12.8 billion, with unsold shares offered to the public, alongside a reverse stock split that consolidated five common shares into one. The reverse split was completed on 15 April 2026.

According to the securities registration statement, the largest shareholder, CEO Jang Ki-su, planned to subscribe for roughly KRW 2.0 to 2.5 billion. In August 2026 the company said payment for a KRW 3 billion third-party allotment to its largest shareholder had been completed.

The planned KRW 12.8 billion offering and the KRW 3 billion third-party allotment are separate procedures, so the final amount raised and changes in share count will be confirmed in later filings.

On the trading side, an August 2026 disclosure flagged a possible designation as a managed issue because the share price is below KRW 1,000.

The next checkpoints are whether new-model mass-production supply shows up in 2026 results, whether quarterly operating profit stays positive, and the debt structure when full-year 2026 results are filed.

Bull factors

Expanding DC-Link supply for new models

Wisereport notes exclusive DC-Link supply for the Ioniq 5 and Carnival HEV. Exclusive supply for new models including the Sorento and Avante is planned. The company is also pursuing wider supply to global automakers through inverter makers.

Capital raising and largest-shareholder participation

Following the reverse split, a rights offering process was carried out, and in August 2026 payment for a KRW 3 billion third-party allotment to the largest shareholder was completed. The company framed this as a step toward a stronger earnings base. Metallized film sales growth of 11% is also cited as a second pillar.

Bear factors

High debt relative to equity

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 383.1% at end-2025 from 194.9% in 2024. Total liabilities were KRW 58 billion against total equity of KRW 15.1 billion. Operating cash flow turned positive at KRW 5.4 billion in 2025, after three years of negative readings.

Volatile quarterly profit

Operating loss returned in Q3 2026 at KRW 155 million. Revenue growth slowed to 1.7% quarter on quarter in Q3 2026. The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 9.4 billion, and the Q4 2025 net loss was KRW 7.71 billion.

Listing maintenance and dilution pressure

An August 2026 disclosure flagged a possible managed-issue designation because the share price is below KRW 1,000. Press coverage raised overhang concerns tied to possible underwriter absorption of unsubscribed shares and the absence of lock-up conditions. Reports also say the exchange raised its market-exit market-cap threshold to KRW 30 billion.

Risk factors

Financial soundness

A debt-to-equity ratio of 383.1% and total equity of KRW 15.1 billion leave a thin buffer. Operating cash flow turned positive in 2025, but that is a single year, so sustainability is not yet tested. Further capital raising would bring dilution and financial burden together.

Listing rules and trading conditions

The August 2026 disclosure flagged a possible managed-issue designation, and a share price below KRW 1,000 could lead to a designation decision. The raised market-cap threshold narrows the conditions for remaining listed. These rules and the share price are linked, so both need to be tracked together.

Cost inflation and customer concentration

The company attributed the 2025 rise in cost of sales mainly to higher raw and auxiliary material costs for eco-friendly automotive capacitors. Its main products are concentrated in DC-Link capacitors for electric and hybrid vehicles, so results are sensitive to automaker production schedules and model adoption. Higher labor costs also raise manufacturing costs.
