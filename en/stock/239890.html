02

Business structure

P&H Tech develops and produces organic materials used in OLED display panels. Its main products are mass-produced materials such as high-refractive-index CPL and hole transport layer (HTL) materials, and it also supplies Pd catalysts and raw material intermediates used in OLED material synthesis.

In the third quarter of 2025, the revenue mix was 96.76% OLED materials, 2.48% OLED raw materials, 0.72% merchandise and 0.04% other. Its customers are major display panel makers in Korea and abroad.

Patented materials are supplied directly to panel makers, while emitting-layer materials are delivered indirectly through material customers, so the two product lines carry different earnings volatility, according to analyst commentary.

In Q1 2025, weak tablet-related material sales and lower demand for emitting-layer materials from a primary customer sharply hurt results. Company profile information describes development work on blue phosphorescent materials and materials for tandem OLED structures.

The Jincheon P2 plant was completed and began operating in June 2026, and production capacity expansion and R&D infrastructure investment followed in August 2026. The company had 71 employees as of end-June 2026.

The sector tag is secondary batteries, but most revenue comes from OLED materials, so the business should be read alongside OLED panel demand.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 35 billion in 2022 to KRW 40.2 billion in 2023 (up 14.7% year on year), its peak, then fell to KRW 33.6 billion in 2024 (down 16.4%) and KRW 30.1 billion in 2025 (down 10.3%).

Operating profit went from KRW 6.51 billion in 2023 (up 35.5%) to KRW 2.54 billion in 2024 (down 61.0%) and KRW 2.19 billion in 2025 (down 13.5%), declining for two consecutive years, and the operating margin fell from 16.2% in 2023 to 7.3% in 2025.

Securities analysts pointed to slower-than-expected expansion of patented products, lower demand for emitting-layer materials from a primary customer, and a stagnant industry as reasons for the 2024 weakness.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 7.56 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.62 billion in 2023, KRW 3.39 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.87 billion in 2025, a three-year decline. Net profit for all four years was reported at the same amounts as profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 55.6 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 11.6 billion, with the debt ratio falling from 119.9% in 2022 to 20.9% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 9.39 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.28 billion in 2025, well above KRW 2.08 billion in 2022 and KRW 2.99 billion in 2023, even as profit declined.

Quarterly revenue peaked at KRW 7.32 billion in Q4 2025 (up 7.0% from the prior quarter), the highest of the last five quarters, then fell to KRW 5.56 billion in Q1 2026 (down 24.1%) before recovering to KRW 6.96 billion in Q2 and KRW 6.05 billion in Q3 2026 (down 11.7% year on year).

Operating profit fell from KRW 969 million in Q3 2025 to KRW 338 million in Q4 (down 65.1%) and KRW 87.57 million in Q1 2026 (down 74.1%), then rose to KRW 157 million in Q2 and KRW 535 million in Q3 2026 (up 241.7% from the prior quarter), growing for two consecutive quarters.

Still, Q3 2026 operating profit was down 44.8% year on year. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders jumped to KRW 1.27 billion in Q2 2026 (up 686.8%) and was KRW 1.21 billion in Q3 (down 4.3% from the prior quarter, up 32.6% year on year), with a four-quarter total of KRW 3.21 billion.

Operating profit fell year on year in Q3 while controlling-shareholder net profit rose, so differences in non-operating items should be checked in the quarterly report text.

05

Industry analysis

The sector tag is secondary batteries, but most revenue comes from OLED materials, so the industry analysis should center on OLED panel demand. The company's position in the OLED panel market depends on the mix of patented materials and emitting-layer materials.

Securities analysts expect tandem OLED structures, which offer long life and high efficiency, to spread quickly into mid-sized panels such as tablets and automotive displays.

Some analysts expect patented materials, delivered directly to panel makers after customer qualification, to grow as a share of sales, while others see emitting-layer materials, delivered indirectly, as a source of high earnings volatility.

Weakness in 2024 and early 2025 was attributed to a combination of lower tablet-related material sales and lower emitting-layer demand. A company information site cited the expected industry benefit from a U.S. import ban on BOE panels from China.

Some commentary noted that material sales did not grow as fast as the smartphone market share gains of a panel customer. Market share figures are not part of the disclosed results, so the position in the cycle has to be read from the quarterly revenue trend.

Quarterly revenue in 2026 has stayed between KRW 5.56 billion and KRW 6.96 billion, below the KRW 7.32 billion of Q4 2025, so the pace at which an industry recovery shows up in revenue still looks gradual.

Balance sheet capacity

Operating cash flow stayed at KRW 9.39 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.28 billion in 2025 even as profit declined. This financial room can cushion both facility investment and swings in customer demand.

Capacity expansion and patented materials

Bear factors

Two years of falling revenue

Annual revenue fell from KRW 40.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 33.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 30.1 billion in 2025. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 6.05 billion was down 11.7% year on year. Quarterly revenue has not yet returned above the KRW 7.32 billion recorded in Q4 2025, the highest of the last five quarters.

Margin compression and quarterly volatility

The operating margin fell from 16.2% in 2023 to 7.3% in 2025. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 535 million was down 44.8% year on year. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals only KRW 3.21 billion, so the earnings trend swings sharply with each quarter.

Customer and product concentration

Emitting-layer materials reach panel makers through material customers, so results swing widely with demand from a primary customer. In 2024, that demand declined and tablet-related sales weakened. Securities analysts said weak tablet-related material sales sharply hurt results in Q1 2025.

Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

Emitting-layer materials are delivered through material customers, so a drop in demand from a primary customer hits revenue directly. Changes in tablet OLED demand have already affected material sales in the past. If patented materials do not grow their share quickly, improvement in product mix could be delayed.

Expansion spending and fixed costs

If the Jincheon P2 plant and the August 2026 expansion do not translate into sales, fixed costs could weigh on earnings. With the operating margin in the 7% range in 2024 and 2025, the payback period for the investment could lengthen. How much of the expansion reaches quarterly revenue will only become clear over time.

Share price volatility and sector mismatch

Because market capitalization is small, the share price can move sharply on trading flows unrelated to earnings. Expectations for an OLED panel recovery partly depend on policy variables such as import restrictions on Chinese panels.

Because the sector tag is secondary batteries while the business is OLED materials, sector index moves and company results may diverge.

Overall view

P&H Tech has a business structure centered on OLED materials. Revenue and profit declined from a 2023 peak, and quarterly profit has been recovering during 2026. The lower operating margin since 2022 reads as the combined result of falling sales and volatility in demand for patented and emitting-layer materials.

On the financial side, the debt ratio fell to 20.9% at the end of 2025, which points to a steady capital structure.

Strengths include the quarterly profit recovery, low debt and capacity investment, while weaknesses include two straight years of lower annual revenue, the year-on-year decline in Q3 2026 operating profit and volatility from customer and product concentration.

Both sides deserve equal weight, and whether the new capacity converts into revenue is the key variable. Valuation sits in a discount to net asset value, but the small earnings base makes the multiple sensitive to profit swings.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and whether the expansion effect appears in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
