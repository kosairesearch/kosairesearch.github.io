SUN Kwang: Profit Recovery Slows in Q3 2026, Port Lease Probe in Focus

SUN Kwang posted its highest disclosed operating profit of KRW 41.4 billion in 2025, but operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders both fell from a year earlier in Q3 2026, while an investigation into alleged illegal subletting of Incheon port land has emerged as a new variable.

02

Business structure

Founded in 1961 and listed on KOSDAQ, SUN Kwang is a comprehensive logistics company that provides port handling, grain storage, transportation and warehousing services centered on Incheon and Gunsan ports. Its business is divided into grain silo handling, container handling, general cargo handling and leasing.

Based on the Q3 2023 mix, container handling accounted for 42.66% of revenue, silo handling for 34.90%, general cargo handling for 20.30% and leasing for 2.13%. Silo handling covers receiving, storing and releasing imported grain such as corn and wheat at dedicated grain terminals in Incheon and Gunsan.

Reported grain storage capacity differs between sources, at 790,000 tons and 940,000 tons, so a single figure should be treated with caution. Container handling is carried out mainly by its subsidiary SNCT at a 480,000-square-meter dedicated terminal in Incheon New Port, which has seven STS cranes and 28 ARMG units.

The Hyundai Glovis-contracted car terminal at Pyeongtaek-Dangjin port handles, according to company materials, about 400,000 vehicles a year in imports and exports, with major customers including Hyundai, Kia and BMW. Leasing is a small share of revenue but rests on port land in Incheon.

Competition depends on securing berths and port-adjacent land. Large peers such as CJ Logistics, HANJIN Logistics and Hyundai Glovis have business mixes built more around vehicles, parcels and bulk cargo.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue on a disclosed annual basis rose from KRW 172.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 185.3 billion in 2023 (up 7.6% year on year), KRW 186.8 billion in 2024 (up 0.8%) and KRW 190.4 billion in 2025 (up 2.0%), growing three years in a row from 2023 through 2025. 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025 and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit jumped from KRW 26.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 39.1 billion in 2023 (up 46.1%), fell to KRW 36.4 billion in 2024 (down 6.9%) and recovered to KRW 41.4 billion in 2025 (up 13.8%).

The company attributed the 2023 gain to higher silo and container revenue, lower selling and administrative commission expenses and lower income tax expense. For 2025, higher general cargo revenue, lower commission expenses and lower income tax expense were cited as the main drivers.

The operating margin moved from 15.5% in 2022 to 21.1% in 2023, 19.5% in 2024 and 21.7% in 2025, staying around 20% since 2023.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 14.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 29.8 billion in 2023 (up 105.7%), KRW 28.5 billion in 2024 (down 4.5%) and KRW 39.6 billion in 2025 (up 38.9%).

Total net profit, which includes non-controlling interests, was KRW 28.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 39.5 billion in 2025, a slightly different figure from the controlling-shareholder line.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 48 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 49.2 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 44.3 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 51.6 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 48.4 billion in Q3 2026. Operating profit over the same quarters was KRW 12.5 billion, KRW 11.5 billion, KRW 10.6 billion, KRW 12.3 billion and KRW 10.4 billion.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 7.08 billion, down 35.4% year on year, and the four quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 total KRW 41.9 billion. At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 257.1 billion and the controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 432.7 billion.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 50.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 71.1 billion in 2024 before easing to KRW 67.7 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

SUN Kwang's results depend on export-import volumes, grain import flows and ocean freight rates. The company has said that container and silo grain imports such as corn and wheat have stayed at a similar level each year, which suggests a relatively steady volume base in grain.

A 2024 report, however, noted that a sharp rise in international freight rates from the Red Sea disruption could reduce logistics volumes and weigh on the company's results.

Because its 2023 recovery was described as a normalization after the pandemic, the business has become more sensitive to global trade and freight conditions. Port land costs in Incheon are also a point of debate.

The Incheon Port Authority, referred to as IPA, reportedly reduces rents to 24% below the standard level for port operators, a benefit reaching about 250 companies a year, with roughly KRW 25 billion a year at stake.

The policy is meant to strengthen port competitiveness, but some industry voices argue that it has benefited companies involved in illegal subletting and weakened its effectiveness. Against peers, SUN Kwang is a port specialist concentrated on grain silo and container handling.

It has a different revenue mix from large logistics groups such as CJ Logistics and HANJIN Logistics, which also run parcel and land transport, and it connects to Hyundai Glovis through the car terminal contract.

In Q3 2026 revenue rose slightly year on year while profit fell, which can be read as a sign that cost pressure increased.

06

Outlook

The company's medium-term growth drivers come down to two projects: the Incheon New Port container terminal and the Dangjin grain terminal.

In December 2025 it signed a shareholder agreement for the Incheon New Port Phase 1-2 container terminal operation, and it has since been reported to take part in operating an automated berth. The start date of operations and the scale of earnings contribution are not yet laid out in publicly available figures.

A private development MOU for the Dangjin grain terminal was signed in 2025, and the company said the investment aims to secure grain handling and eco-friendly storage in the Dangjin area to attract new volumes from grain processing plants in the capital region. This would broaden the volume base of the silo business.

The car terminal business continues under the HPIT contract, so vehicle export and import flows feed directly into revenue.

On the Incheon port land, reports said the Board of Audit and police are investigating, and the outcome could change lease terms and whether rent reductions continue, affecting the leasing business and port-land-based operations more broadly.

Full-year 2026 results have not yet been disclosed and will be fixed in the 2026 annual report, where growth rates and margin changes can be checked against 2025 revenue of KRW 190.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 41.4 billion.

In the quarterly series, the key point is whether the Q3 2026 margin slowdown continues into the next quarters. Few disclosures give order or new contract sizes, so volume trends will need to be read alongside the business-segment commentary in the periodic reports.

Bull factors

Improved earnings base

Operating profit rose from KRW 26.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 41.4 billion in 2025, and the operating margin moved from 15.5% to 21.7% over the same period. Holding around 20% since 2023 is cited as evidence of a more stable earnings structure.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 39.6 billion in 2025, up 38.9% from a year earlier.

Stronger balance sheet

The debt-to-equity ratio declined from 85.8% in 2022 to 59.2% in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 434.2 billion, of which KRW 432.7 billion belongs to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow has stayed above KRW 50 billion in each year since 2022.

Infrastructure pipeline

In December 2025 a shareholder agreement for the Incheon New Port Phase 1-2 container terminal operation was signed, and a private development MOU for the Dangjin grain terminal was also agreed. The company said the Dangjin investment is aimed at attracting grain processing volumes from the capital region. Together with the car terminal contract, this could broaden the volume base.

Bear factors

Slowdown in recent quarterly profit

Revenue in the same quarter rose only 0.9% to KRW 48.4 billion, so the sales gain did not translate into higher profit. The four quarters through Q3 2026 total KRW 41.9 billion for controlling-shareholder net profit.

Alleged illegal subletting of Incheon port land

Reports from November 2026 said SUN Kwang faces allegations that a used-car exporter was allowed to directly manage and operate part of its Namhang berth and yard. The Board of Audit and police are investigating, and SUN Kwang is among the companies that have received IPA rent reductions. Lease terms and the reduction benefit could change depending on the outcome.

Sensitivity to freight rates and trade volumes

Container handling was the largest segment at 42.66% of revenue in Q3 2023. A 2024 report said a sharp rise in international freight rates could reduce logistics volumes and weigh on results. Because handling revenue responds directly to export and import volumes, a drop in trade would feed through quickly.

Risk factors

Regulatory and legal

Alleged illegal subletting of Incheon port land is under review by the Board of Audit and the police. If lease contracts or rent reductions are adjusted, cost structures in leasing and port handling could both shift. The scope and outcome of the probe have not been settled.

Volume and freight-rate exposure

Container and grain volumes depend on global trade and ocean freight rates. A 2024 report pointed out that sharp freight increases could reduce logistics volumes. If costs rise while quarterly revenue stays flat, the operating margin could fall further.

Investment and execution

The start of operations and the timing of earnings contribution for the Incheon New Port Phase 1-2 terminal and the Dangjin grain terminal have not been disclosed. If large port investments do not translate into actual volumes, financial burdens could remain.

The debt ratio has fallen, but the direction of the balance sheet could change depending on the scale of new investment.

Overall view

SUN Kwang expanded both revenue and operating profit from 2022 to 2025 and reached an operating margin of 21.7% in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 59.2% at the end of 2025, with total equity of KRW 434.2 billion.

In Q3 2026, however, revenue rose only 0.9% while operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders both declined year on year, and the four quarters through Q3 2026 total KRW 41.9 billion for controlling-shareholder net profit. The share price trades well below one times book value.

The probe into alleged illegal subletting of Incheon port land adds questions about the stability of the leasing business. Over the medium term, the key question is whether the Incheon New Port container terminal and the Dangjin grain terminal broaden the volume base. The next checkpoints are the preliminary Q4 2026 results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
