02

Business structure

TEMC CNS was founded in 2012 under its former name, listed on KOSDAQ in December 2016, and adopted its current name in March 2024. Its largest shareholder is TEMC, which held 33.40% as of March 31, 2026, and treasury shares stood at 10.33% as of October 30, 2026. The business has two segments.

The chemicals segment makes HCDS and TiCl4 precursors and specialty gases, while the equipment segment makes central chemical supply systems and secondary-battery electrolyte supply equipment. Subsidiaries include a secondary-battery equipment maker (99.2% stake) and a semiconductor equipment maker (54.4% stake).

By product, the 2023 revenue mix was 46.9% secondary-battery equipment, 27.8% semiconductor equipment and 25.3% semiconductor materials. Recent disclosures and news focus on chip-related supply, so the semiconductor side now drives the business.

Customer concentration is high at SK hynix: a KRW 14.52 billion supply contract disclosed on December 29, 2025 equaled 7.50% of prior-year sales, and a KRW 25.2 billion chemical supply-equipment contract disclosed in June 2026 equaled 15.03% of prior-year sales.

A fire at the Anseong plant in 2025 interrupted two materials supplied to SK hynix, estimated at roughly KRW 13 billion in annual sales, which was less than 7% of 2024 total revenue.

Peers in the same KOSDAQ machinery and equipment sector include People & Technology and Sunic System, though product mix and customer structure differ, which limits direct comparison.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 165 billion in 2022 to KRW 140.3 billion in 2023, down 14.9% year on year, then rose to KRW 193.5 billion in 2024, up 37.9% and the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Revenue fell 13.2% to KRW 168 billion in 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 22.8 billion in 2022, the largest of the four years, and KRW 5.92 billion in 2024, the smallest, down 55.5%. It recovered to KRW 9 billion in 2025, up 51.9%, but the operating margin of 5.4% remained below the 13.8% of 2022.

The margin path ran 13.8% in 2022, 9.5% in 2023, 3.1% in 2024 and 5.4% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 14.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 13 billion in 2023, KRW 9.47 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.2 billion in 2025, a decline for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 4.26 billion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder number. Operating cash flow was KRW 37.2 billion in 2022, turned negative at -KRW 8.34 billion in 2023, and returned to KRW 9.48 billion in 2024 and KRW 17.4 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose to 76.6% in 2023 and was 45.0% in 2025, when equity totaled KRW 115.9 billion, of which KRW 111.6 billion belonged to controlling shareholders. Quarterly results swung in both directions.

Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 31.1 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.12 billion, and Q4 2025 revenue rose 66.7% quarter on quarter to KRW 51.8 billion with operating profit of KRW 848 million. Q1 2026 revenue fell 55.0% to KRW 23.3 billion and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 3.61 billion.

Q2 2026 revenue rose 140.1% to KRW 55.9 billion and operating profit turned positive at KRW 96.74 million. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 41 billion, up 31.8% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 1.31 billion, up 17.3% year on year.

Yet the controlling-shareholder result in Q3 2026 was a loss of KRW 927 million, a swing to a loss from a year-earlier profit. Operating profit rising while the controlling-shareholder result stays negative points to non-operating and tax items, which the quarterly reports will need to explain.

The controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, totals KRW 627 million.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from the front end depends on SK hynix capital spending. The equipment segment's results are affected by the capex trend of semiconductor device makers, while the chemicals segment tracks chip production volumes and is less sensitive to economic swings.

In 2026, the recovery in the chip market and the expansion of AI memory investment have been cited as strengthening the relationship with SK hynix.

The equipment backlog stood at KRW 161.2 billion at the end of Q1 2026 on a DART quarterly-report basis, including KRW 31.9 billion in new orders and KRW 13.9 billion delivered during the period. Press reports put the equipment backlog at KRW 147.5 billion at the end of the first half of 2026 on a standalone basis.

Equipment has already been delivered to the Yongin fab 1, and a large part of the related revenue is expected to be recognized in Q1 2027 in line with the customer's installation and commissioning schedule.

On the competitive side, the company is pursuing localization of materials and a larger share of the supply-equipment market. Secondary-battery equipment accounted for 46.9% of revenue in 2023, but in 2026 the disclosed orders stand out on the semiconductor side.

In cycle terms, rising memory capex is a favorable backdrop, but equipment businesses are strongly exposed to the capex cycle.

The KRW 25.2 billion supply contract disclosed in June 2026 runs through November 30, 2026, so follow-on volumes and delivery progress after that contract are the next checkpoint. Revenue tied to Yongin fab 1 expected in Q1 2027 depends on the customer's installation and commissioning schedule.

Q3 2026 showed operating profit in the black while the controlling-shareholder result stayed negative, so whether operating gains carry through to net income is another point to watch. The key item for the outlook is whether the operating margin in the 2026 annual report rises above the 5.4% of 2025.

Risk factors

Customer concentration

The KRW 25.2 billion contract disclosed in June 2026 runs through November 30, 2026. If follow-on orders do not come through, revenue could have a gap. Concentration on one main customer means changes in its investment schedule feed directly into results.

Plant incident and production continuity

A 2025 fire at the Anseong plant affected two materials supplied to SK hynix, estimated at roughly KRW 13 billion in annual sales. The company said the production-halt disclosure duty for KOSDAQ companies applies only when the halt equals 10% or more of prior-year sales. That leaves a gap in disclosure for smaller interruptions.

Timing of revenue recognition and volatility

Revenue tied to Yongin fab 1 is expected in Q1 2027 but depends on the customer's installation and commissioning schedule. Annual revenue moved sharply in 2023 to 2025, including declines of 14.9% and 13.2% and a rise of 37.9%. Quarterly results also alternated between operating losses and profits in 2026, so volatility is high.

Overall view

After an operating loss of KRW 3.61 billion in Q1 and a net loss, operating profit turned positive in Q2, and Q3 operating profit of KRW 1.31 billion came with a controlling-shareholder loss of KRW 927 million, extending the losses to three quarters.
