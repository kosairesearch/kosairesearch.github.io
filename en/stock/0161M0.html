Usage Surges, Losses Persist

Typecast API usage has risen sharply, but cumulative 2026 third-quarter filings show flat revenue and wider losses.

02

Business structure

Founded in 2017, Neosapience is an AI voice generation company that runs the Typecast speech synthesis service on its proprietary SSFM foundation model. Typecast supports more than 700 AI voice characters and 37 languages, and cumulative subscribers have reached 3.2 million. The business has three pillars.

The first is a subscription service used mainly by individual creators, which the company is using as a base to move into enterprise markets.

The second is the Typecast API, where paid subscribers rose 152 times year on year as of late September 2026, driven by creators who automate video production and by API integration through coding tools.

The third is Neona Agents, a B2B conversational AI agent launched on September 1, 2026 that combines speech recognition, response generation and speech synthesis in-house. Revenue shares by segment are not separately disclosed, so the relative scale of each business is hard to gauge.

Overseas sales made up 7.02% in the first half of 2026, leaving domestic revenue as the bulk, and reports put the share of the top five B2B API customers at 67%. In the public sector, Typecast API was connected to AiSAC, the advertising creation service of KOBACO, in November 2026.

The company competes with several AI voice and content creation firms and points to emotional expression and real-time response speed as its differentiators.

03

Recent trends

Based on the cumulative 2026 third-quarter DART filing, revenue was KRW 2.91 billion, down 0.4% year on year. The operating loss reached KRW 1.8 billion and the net loss KRW 1.57 billion, and both losses widened from the same period a year earlier.

Typecast API usage grew sharply, yet reported revenue stayed roughly flat. The company has said API revenue is recognized when customers actually use their credits, so the growth should appear in reported results later.

In consolidated 2025 figures reported by the press, revenue was about KRW 10.65 billion, up more than 66% year on year, and the operating loss narrowed from about KRW 7.9 billion to about KRW 2.7 billion.

The 2025 net loss of about KRW 6.99 billion included non-cash items such as amortization of convertible discounts and derivative valuation losses.

In the first quarter of 2026, redeemable convertible preferred shares were fully converted into common shares, turning total equity from negative to about KRW 16.7 billion and lifting the current ratio to 287.18%. The shares debuted on September 21, 2026 and touched KRW 33,500 intraday on the first day.

The stock closed at KRW 5,340 on November 6, 2026, down 4.64% that day. As of 14:23 on November 18, 2026, it traded at KRW 4,830, down 4.17% on the day, with trading value of about KRW 400 million.

04

Outlook

The key question is how much of the sharp rise in API usage converts into reported revenue. The company says API revenue is recognized as customers consume credits, so growth should show more clearly in reported results over time, although absolute API revenue has not been disclosed.

Pre-listing coverage cited the company's 2026 estimate of KRW 14.1 billion in revenue and an operating loss of KRW 2.4 billion.

Set against nine-month revenue of KRW 2.91 billion, reaching that estimate would require a heavy concentration of revenue in the fourth quarter, which makes the 2026 fourth-quarter and full-year results the next checkpoint.

The same estimates project revenue of KRW 25.4 billion and an operating profit of KRW 3.3 billion in 2027, a turn to profitability that depends on the growth assumptions holding.

Planned use of proceeds includes KRW 15.1 billion for research and development over four years, KRW 6.3 billion for GPU infrastructure and KRW 5.9 billion for new and overseas businesses, based on the lower end of the offering price.

On the product side, Neona Agents is expanding from education into customer service and sales, and the company targets cutting response latency from about 0.6 seconds to about 0.3 seconds.

On the equity side, only 28.66% of listed shares were initially tradable, and four insiders including the CEO agreed to hold 37.68% of their stake for three years, so that supply cannot reach the market until the lock-up ends. Trading flow and disclosed results are therefore variables to track together.

05

Bull factors

Surge in paid API subscribers

Paid Typecast API subscribers rose 152 times year on year as of late September 2026, while monthly usage grew about 160 times and total users about 63 times. Paid subscribers in the third quarter grew 5.7 times from the prior quarter, a faster pace than earlier quarters.

The company attributes the increase mainly to AI-driven video production automation and API integration through coding tools. These figures are company-reported, and absolute API revenue has not been disclosed.

Wider public and broadcast references

On November 12, 2026, Typecast AI voices were supplied to KOBACO's AiSAC advertising creation service. The company says thousands of corporate clients in home shopping, broadcasting and education already use Typecast.

The supply is seen by some observers as an example of proven technology extending into public services. Contract size and the share of recurring revenue have not been disclosed.

Balance sheet normalization

In the first quarter of 2026, all redeemable convertible preferred shares were converted into common shares, lifting total equity to about KRW 16.7 billion and raising the current ratio from 36.96% to 287.18%.

The company said it is working to offset accumulated deficits to lay the groundwork for distributable profits after listing. Allocations of proceeds include KRW 15.1 billion for research and development and KRW 6.3 billion for GPU infrastructure, which expands room for AI agent investment.

06

Bear factors

Persistent losses and flat revenue

Cumulative 2026 third-quarter revenue was KRW 2.91 billion, down 0.4% year on year, while the operating loss of KRW 1.8 billion and the net loss of KRW 1.57 billion both widened from the prior year.

Consolidated 2025 results also showed an operating loss of about KRW 2.7 billion and a net loss of about KRW 6.99 billion. The main weakness is that the sharp rise in usage metrics has not yet translated into reported revenue growth.

Low revenue base against annual estimates

Pre-listing coverage cited the company's 2026 estimates of KRW 14.1 billion in revenue and a KRW 2.4 billion operating loss. Nine-month revenue of KRW 2.91 billion would require a very strong fourth quarter to reach that level.

The projected 2027 turn to profitability also assumes revenue of KRW 25.4 billion, so whether those growth assumptions hold is the critical test.

Thin float and share price volatility

Only 349 million... specifically 3,495,095 shares, or 28.66% of listed shares, were initially tradable after listing. The CEO and three other insiders agreed to hold 37.68% of their stake for three years.

The share price, which touched KRW 33,500 intraday on the first day, closed at KRW 5,340 on November 6, 2026 and traded at KRW 4,830 as of the reference time, with trading value of about KRW 400 million.

07

Risk factors

Macro

A shift in interest rates or in investor appetite for KOSDAQ growth stocks could amplify volatility in a recently listed share price. The company plans to spend heavily on R&D and GPU infrastructure, so capital market conditions could affect the pace of investment. Newly listed companies tend to be sensitive to broader market risk appetite.

New business execution

Neona Agents plans to extend from education into enterprise work such as customer service and sales. The company targets cutting response latency from about 0.6 seconds to about 0.3 seconds, and whether that performance holds in real enterprise settings is a precondition for expansion.

The plan commits KRW 15.1 billion to research and development, KRW 6.3 billion to GPU infrastructure and KRW 5.9 billion to new and overseas businesses over four years, so slower spending could weigh on the projected return to profitability.

Technology and regulation

Voice synthesis carries misuse concerns such as unauthorized voice cloning, which could bring tighter regulation. If large platforms and generative AI companies integrate voice features into their own products, pricing and share competition could intensify.

The company says it runs speech recognition through speech synthesis in-house, but whether that technical gap persists remains an open variable.

08

Overall view

Neosapience's current picture combines fast growth in operating metrics with persistent losses in reported financials. On the strength side, Typecast API paid subscribers and usage have risen sharply, and public-sector supply and the launch of B2B agents are widening the customer base.

The resolution of capital impairment and the improved current ratio are positive factors for financial stability. On the weakness side, cumulative 2026 third-quarter revenue fell 0.4% year on year, and both the operating loss and the net loss widened.

Whether usage metrics convert into reported revenue will become clearer in the 2026 fourth-quarter and full-year results. The share price sits below the offering price, and with a small tradable float, trading may remain volatile. This is a qualitative summary provided for information purposes only.
