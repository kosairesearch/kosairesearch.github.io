02

Business structure

NBT operates a mobile point platform that offers performance advertising channels to advertisers and point rewards to users. In the product mix tracked by CompanyGuide, the B2B offerwall Adison Offerwall accounts for 86.01% of revenue and the B2C app-tech app CashSlide for 8.83%.

Valueline notes that the advertising segment makes up more than 97% of total revenue. Because advertisers pay according to performance when users complete actions linked to points, revenue moves with the scale of advertising budgets. The company says CashSlide has more than 25 million cumulative sign-ups.

Overseas operations began in the second half of 2023 with North America, Taiwan, Thailand and Indonesia, and in June 2025 France, Canada and Mexico were added. In September 2025 NBT signed a stablecoin cooperation MOU with Openasset, and in November 2025 it launched the LinkCommerce affiliate shopping platform.

R&D spending in 2025 was about KRW 1.8 billion, roughly 2% of revenue, and headcount at year-end 2025 was 87. The company calls itself the leading domestic point platform operator, but public comparative market-share data against other online ad media is limited.

04

Earnings analysis

In the annual results disclosed in DART periodic reports, revenue went from KRW 108.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 106.7 billion in 2023 (down 1.4%) and KRW 105.1 billion in 2024 (down 1.4%), before falling to KRW 92.3 billion in 2025 (down 12.2%).

Revenue has declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2022 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 window. Operating profit of KRW 2.28 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 2.77 billion in 2023, and the loss widened to KRW 3.34 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.5 billion in 2025.

The operating margin slid from 2.1% in 2022 to -7.0% in 2025. The company attributed the 2025 loss to early investment in seven new overseas markets, the launch of its commerce business and upgrades to its B2C app-tech service, which temporarily raised selling and administrative costs.

The controlling shareholders' net loss narrowed from KRW 6.61 billion in 2024 to KRW 4.54 billion in 2025, and the company said a gain on disposal of investment assets from selling held property lifted non-operating income. The narrower 2025 loss therefore reflects non-operating gains more than operating improvement.

Operating cash flow moved from KRW 1.85 billion in 2022 to -KRW 2.01 billion in 2023, -KRW 3.1 billion in 2024 and -KRW 9.96 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 173.3% in 2024 to 71.9% in 2025.

Quarterly, revenue was KRW 23 billion in Q3 2025 with an operating loss of KRW 1.7 billion, and improved to KRW 25.7 billion in Q4 2025 with an operating loss of KRW 733 million.

Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 22.1 billion marked the low point, and the company attributed it to the seasonal off-season and political uncertainty that pulled marketing budgets across the industry lower.

Q2 2026 revenue rose to KRW 25.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 486 million, and Q3 2026 revenue reached KRW 28.6 billion (up 12.1% quarter on quarter and 24.4% year on year), with operating profit of KRW 1.92 billion and controlling net income of KRW 1.88 billion. Controlling net income for the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, totaled KRW 2.71 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's online advertising market grew in 2024, and the offerwall market was described as stable, supported by wider adoption by large platforms and expanding overseas entry.

Even so, NBT's revenue fell over the same period, and the company attributed the decline to lower ad demand for Adison Offerwall and weaker user activity on CashSlide. Online advertising is sensitive to economic cycles, and the first quarter is seasonally weak, so quarterly revenue depends heavily on the ad cycle.

In its Q1 2026 results release, the company blamed a broad pullback in industry marketing budgets and said recovery had appeared across the business from April. The 24.4% year-on-year revenue increase in Q3 2026 appears to coincide with that recovery.

In competition, NBT calls itself the leading domestic point platform, but its scale and ad inventory are far smaller than those of large platforms such as NAVER and Kakao. The company says its edge is combining a specialized B2B offerwall channel with a B2C app-tech user network.

Overseas, it has added countries since the second half of 2023, and it counted seven newly entered markets at the end of 2025. Faster recovery is possible if the ad cycle and overseas expansion align, but public data does not break that effect down into separate figures.

06

Outlook

In a June 2, 2026 release, the company said recovery in core revenue, expansion of its commerce business and cost efficiency were advancing together, raising the likelihood of a full-year return to profit. Cost measures cited include relocating offices and expanding direct sales.

The company expects the benefits of improvement to take hold from Q2, with peak-season effects and commerce results adding to the second half. Reported results have followed that direction, with operating profit turning positive in Q2 and reaching KRW 1.92 billion in Q3.

Its commerce business centers on LinkCommerce, launched in November 2025, layered on top of an advertising-centered revenue base. Overseas, the seven markets entered by the end of 2025 are still feeding into revenue.

The stablecoin cooperation is at the MOU stage, so the timing of any revenue contribution will need to be confirmed through future disclosures. The full-year profit target is the company's own goal, not a confirmed forecast.

The key test is how much fourth-quarter peak-season strength lifts operating profit, and whether the seasonal first quarter of 2027 holds near the level of Q1 2026, which would indicate the strength of the ad cycle.

08

Bull factors

Q3 2026 rebound

Revenue rose in both Q2 and Q3 2026 after the Q1 2026 low. Controlling net income of KRW 1.88 billion in Q3 2026 shows earnings capacity moving back toward profit.

Lighter balance sheet

Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 15 billion against total equity of KRW 20.8 billion. Part of the narrower 2025 loss came from a property sale gain, so whether the balance-sheet improvement holds alongside operating performance can be confirmed in later results.

New businesses and overseas reach

LinkCommerce launched in November 2025, and the company expects its commerce business to help second-half results. Adison Offerwall added six countries in June 2025, including France, Canada and Mexico, widening its overseas channel.

A stablecoin cooperation MOU is also in place, but the timing of any revenue contribution has not been disclosed.

09

Bear factors

Accumulated annual operating losses

Operating losses ran for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and the 2025 operating loss of KRW 6.5 billion was larger than the KRW 3.34 billion loss in 2024. The operating margin fell from 2.1% in 2022 to -7.0% in 2025.

The company has set a full-year return to profit as a goal, but the sign of full-year 2026 operating profit will have to be confirmed in the annual report.

Weakening cash generation

Operating cash flow moved from a KRW 1.85 billion surplus in 2022 to -KRW 2.01 billion in 2023, -KRW 3.1 billion in 2024 and -KRW 9.96 billion in 2025, a widening deficit. If cash outflows continue even as book earnings improve, working capital and investment capacity could come under pressure. Whether the recent four-quarter profit translates into better cash flow remains to be seen.

Nature of the narrower loss

The controlling shareholders' net loss narrowed to KRW 4.54 billion in 2025 from KRW 6.61 billion in 2024, but the company said a gain on disposal of investment assets from a property sale lifted non-operating income. Over the same year the operating loss widened to KRW 6.5 billion.

Revenue has also declined for three consecutive years from 2023, so top-line recovery has not yet appeared on an annual basis.

10

Risk factors

Ad cycle and seasonality

Revenue at Adison Offerwall and CashSlide is directly linked to advertiser budgets. The company attributed the KRW 22.1 billion revenue in Q1 2026 to the seasonal off-season and political uncertainty that squeezed industry marketing budgets. If the ad cycle slows again, the recovery seen in Q3 2026 could reverse.

Cost of new-business investment

The company said investment in seven overseas markets, its commerce business and upgrades to the B2C service temporarily raised operating costs, including selling and administrative expenses, in 2025. Fixed-cost pressure could persist until the new businesses turn into revenue. LinkCommerce and the stablecoin cooperation appear to be at an early stage.

One-off gains and cash flow

The narrower 2025 net loss relied partly on a property sale gain. The operating cash outflow widened to -KRW 9.96 billion in 2025. If earnings metrics improve without matching cash inflows, judging financial capacity becomes harder.

12

Overall view

NBT is a point-platform operator whose advertising revenue makes up more than 97% of the total. Revenue and operating profit weakened together from 2023 to 2025, and the 2025 operating loss widened to KRW 6.5 billion while the operating cash outflow grew to -KRW 9.96 billion.

The narrower 2025 net loss reflected a property sale gain, so it is hard to read as an improvement in the core business. In 2026 the quarterly trend turned, with Q3 revenue of KRW 28.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.92 billion, a swing to profit.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 71.9% in 2025, and market capitalization sits slightly above total equity. The company targets a full-year return to profit through its commerce business and cost efficiency, and that is the company's stated goal.

Strengths are the Q3 operating recovery and a lighter balance sheet, while weaknesses are the accumulated annual operating losses and the deteriorating cash flow. The next checkpoint is the sign of full-year 2026 operating profit and operating cash flow in the 2026 annual report.
