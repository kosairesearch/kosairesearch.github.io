02

Business structure

Pungguk Ethanol was founded in 1954 to produce ethanol used as a raw material for soju and listed on KOSDAQ in 1994. The business is split into the parent's ethanol operations, the industrial gas operations of a 50%-owned subsidiary, and the hydrogen operations of a 56.4%-owned subsidiary.

On a consolidated basis after adjusting for intercompany sales as of the third quarter of 2025, hydrogen gas made up 41% of revenue, ethanol 34% and industrial gases 28%. Ethanol was the largest revenue source through 2024, but the mix has recently shifted toward hydrogen.

In 2023, ethanol and ethanol-related products accounted for 35% of revenue, hydrogen and acetylene for 35%, and industrial gases for 30%, according to an April 2024 report.

The hydrogen business recovers by-product gas from refining and petrochemical processes in Ulsan's petrochemical complex and supplies it to nearby large industrial customers through pipelines.

After signing a feedstock gas acquisition agreement in 2004, the company completed a hydrogen plant the following year and went on to sign supply contracts with several SK group affiliates.

An information service has noted that industrial gas results improved on medical gas GMP certification and expanding demand for eco-friendly fumigants.

Among domestic ethanol producers, the company is ranked third behind two larger rivals, and its ethanol business held a 9.60% market share as of June 2026 by one data provider's count.

In an April 2024 report, the company said it would focus on ultra-high-purity ethanol used in semiconductor cleaning, wafer drying, EV battery materials and bio-pharmaceutical raw materials.

04

Earnings analysis

On the filed figures, annual revenue rose from KRW 154 billion in 2022 to KRW 168.5 billion in 2023, up 9.4% year on year, then fell 7.2% to KRW 156.3 billion in 2024 and recovered 4.6% to KRW 163.5 billion in 2025. The largest revenue year in the 2022 to 2025 period was 2023.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 3.77 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.7 billion in 2023, up 209.8%, then rose 7.1% to KRW 12.5 billion in 2024 and 34.0% to KRW 16.8 billion in 2025. Operating profit increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the period.

The operating margin moved from 2.4% in 2022 to 6.9% in 2023, 8.0% in 2024 and 10.2% in 2025, so margin improvement rather than sales expansion drove most of the profit gain. The company attributed the main cause of 2025 results to higher sales at its consolidated hydrogen subsidiary.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.44 billion in 2022, KRW 8.47 billion in 2023 (up 146.0%), KRW 8.05 billion in 2024 (down 4.9%) and KRW 10.2 billion in 2025 (up 27.1%).

In 2024 operating profit rose while controlling-shareholder net profit fell, but net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 10.5 billion, not far from KRW 10.8 billion in 2023.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit was KRW 5.73 billion in the third quarter of 2025 on revenue of KRW 43.4 billion, then fell to KRW 3.86 billion in the fourth quarter, down 32.6% from the prior quarter.

The first quarter of 2026 marked the low, with operating profit of KRW 2.96 billion, down 23.4% from the prior quarter, before recovering to KRW 4.46 billion in the second quarter, up 50.8%.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 5.0% from the prior quarter to KRW 44 billion, while operating profit fell 19.4% to KRW 3.6 billion, down 37.2% year on year. Controlling-shareholder net profit for the latest four quarters totaled KRW 9.32 billion.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 5.26 billion in 2022 to KRW 20.2 billion in 2023, KRW 26 billion in 2024 and KRW 23.4 billion in 2025, exceeding 2025 net profit of KRW 13.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for ethanol is closely tied to soju production. In a 2024 report, the company said it was expanding its ultra-high-purity ethanol business to respond to declining soju consumption.

An information service summarized that demand for non-alcoholic-beverage ethanol has been steadily rising as interest in safe food grows. Among domestic ethanol producers, the company ranks third, according to a February 2026 Blother report.

In hydrogen, demand rose as factory utilization recovered in Ulsan's industrial complex, and volumes concentrated at the subsidiary that already has a pipeline-based supply network.

Hydrogen production capacity was about 149.97 million Nm³ as of the third quarter of 2025, below roughly 166.44 million Nm³ in 2023, the year of the highest results. The company said it is not yet at full capacity and has room to absorb additional demand.

In industrial gases, medical gas GMP certification and wider adoption of eco-friendly fumigants were cited as drivers of improved results. Ultra-high-purity ethanol is seen as a potential demand area linked to efforts to localize semiconductor materials.

Unlike many large listed food and beverage companies that sell finished consumer products, the company is closer to a business-to-business supplier of gases and ethanol to industrial customers, with hydrogen and industrial gases making up a large share of revenue.

06

Outlook

In an April 2024 report, the company described its 2024 goal as a challenge to reach revenue of KRW 200 billion and operating profit of KRW 15 billion. Filed 2024 results were revenue of KRW 156.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 12.5 billion, short of that goal.

In 2025 revenue grew 4.6% and operating profit grew 34.0%, extending the profit recovery. Quarterly operating profit in 2026 moved from KRW 2.96 billion in the first quarter to KRW 4.46 billion in the second, then fell back to KRW 3.6 billion in the third.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose year on year, but operating profit declined, indicating margin pressure. An information service estimated that consolidated operating profit for the first half of 2026 rose 3.5% year on year.

In hydrogen, the company said that recovered factory utilization in Ulsan and its pipeline-based supply network leave room to absorb additional demand. For ultra-high-purity ethanol, the next question is how much revenue contribution appears in periodic filings after the 2024 announcement.

A new chief executive was appointed in March 2026, and the company is currently listed with co-representative directors Lee Han-yong and Kim Gyu-ho.

08

Bull factors

Three Years of Profit Growth and Margin Gains

Operating profit rose for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and the operating margin climbed from 2.4% in 2022 to 10.2% in 2025. The 34.0% rise in 2025 operating profit far outpaced the 4.6% revenue growth, pointing to efficiency gains at the center of the improvement. Operating cash flow of KRW 23.4 billion in 2025 also exceeded net profit.

Spare Capacity in the Hydrogen Business

Hydrogen gas made up the largest share of consolidated revenue at 41% as of the third quarter of 2025. Recovered utilization at Ulsan petrochemical plants and a pipeline-based supply network are absorbing volume, and the company says it is not yet at full capacity.

Hydrogen production capacity of about 149.97 million Nm³ in the third quarter of 2025 was below its 2023 peak level, leaving room for additional demand.

Low Debt and a Thick Equity Base

Total equity stood at KRW 198.2 billion, of which KRW 171.8 billion belongs to controlling shareholders. The balance sheet gives the company a base to fund facility investment or new businesses.

09

Bear factors

Profit Declined in the Third Quarter of 2026

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 3.6 billion, down 37.2% from a year earlier. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.65 billion, down 31.2% year on year.

Controlling-shareholder net profit for the latest four quarters totaled KRW 9.32 billion, showing that the peak quarterly level of 2025 has not been sustained.

Shrinking Share of the Core Ethanol Business

The ethanol share of revenue fell from 38% in 2024 to 34% as of the third quarter of 2025. Continued declines in soju consumption could weigh on raw material demand, and the company has said it is responding by expanding ultra-high-purity ethanol. Until those new businesses scale, demand swings in the core business remain a source of earnings volatility.

Limited Top-Line Growth Against Targets

The 2024 goal of revenue of KRW 200 billion and operating profit of KRW 15 billion cited in an April 2024 report was well above the filed 2024 results of KRW 156.3 billion and KRW 12.5 billion. Revenue of KRW 163.5 billion in 2025 also remained below the 2023 high of KRW 168.5 billion. Profit growth has depended more on margins than on top-line expansion.

10

Risk factors

Customer Concentration and Utilization Dependence

The hydrogen business supplies large customers in Ulsan's petrochemical complex, so changes in those customers' plant utilization feed directly into sales. If factory utilization in the Ulsan industrial complex falls again, hydrogen volumes and revenue could decline together. Revenue by individual customer is not disclosed in the filings referenced here.

Soju Consumption and Ethanol Demand

The ethanol business is tied to soju production, so long-term declines in soju consumption could weigh on raw material demand. Growth in demand for non-alcoholic-beverage ethanol is cited as a partial offset, but its scale relative to any core-business decline is limited in the available disclosures. The ethanol share of revenue has already been falling.

Governance and a Limited Free Float

Major shareholders and related parties hold 67.78%, which limits the number of shares available for trading. A 2024 report noted that daily trading volume fell below 30,000 shares in some periods.

A new chief executive was appointed in March 2026, and strengthening shareholder return policies has been cited as a remaining task.

Overall view

Pungguk Ethanol began from operating profit of KRW 3.77 billion in 2022 and posted three consecutive years of growth through 2025, with the operating margin reaching 10.2% in 2025. The improvement was driven more by margins than by sales expansion, which points to a change in the earnings structure.

The revenue mix is moving toward hydrogen gas, and the pipeline-based supply in Ulsan is absorbing volume. On the other hand, third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.6 billion was down 37.2% year on year, so quarterly earnings have not been steady.

The company fell short of the challenge goal it set for 2024, and 2025 revenue did not surpass the 2023 high. The debt ratio of 12.9% is low, but major shareholders and related parties hold 67.78%, which limits the free float.

The next points to confirm are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, the hydrogen and ethanol revenue mix in the first-quarter 2027 report, and whether ultra-high-purity ethanol begins to contribute meaningful revenue.
