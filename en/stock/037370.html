02

Business structure

EG produces high-purity iron oxide used as a raw material for magnet components in electric vehicle and home appliance motors.

According to a company profile, its high-grade iron oxide ranks about 12th to 15th in global market share, and sales of SKM-2T, a motor-grade product that only EG produces among motor materials, have kept growing.

Its core iron oxide segment processes iron feedstock into iron oxide and ferrite powder for ferrite cores, and it makes up the center of revenue. That business is sensitive to the ferrite core cycle in East Asia and to downstream inventory and utilization rates.

The Energy and Environment segment covers plant and materials businesses in the energy and environment field. Among consolidated subsidiaries, one is engaged in metal trading and leasing.

A 2024 company commentary said sales fell on lower iron oxide prices and that its waste treatment business, a former new venture, was struggling. The same commentary said EG had started developing lithium compound businesses and had long-term plans that include spent battery recycling. Raw material prices, exchange rates and the ferrite core cycle are therefore the main variables for margins.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 70.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 61.8 billion in 2023, a 12.0% decrease year on year, rose to KRW 65.2 billion in 2024, up 5.5%, and then declined to KRW 62.8 billion in 2025, down 3.8%. Of 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating loss widened from KRW 8.56 billion in 2022 to KRW 14.4 billion in 2023, with losses expanding year on year, and then narrowed to KRW 5.47 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.17 billion in 2025.

The company recorded operating losses for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, and its operating margin improved from -23.2% in 2023 to -12.2%, -8.4% and -3.5% in the following years, as shown in the filings.

Based on FnGuide's aggregated figures, the cost of sales ratio fell from 96.55% in 2023 to 81.27% in 2025, and gross profit rose from KRW 2.1 billion to KRW 11.8 billion, so the margin recovery came more from lower cost of sales than from sales growth.

Net loss attributable to shareholders was KRW 2.41 billion in 2022, KRW 7.97 billion in 2023, KRW 2.76 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.19 billion in 2025.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 11.3 billion, KRW 20.6 billion, KRW 6.75 billion and KRW 7.68 billion over the same years, and the gap reflects the share attributable to non-controlling interests.

On a quarterly basis, operating loss of KRW 780 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 was followed by KRW 721 million in the first quarter of 2026, then operating profit of KRW 1.04 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.22 billion in the third quarter.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 18.5 billion rose 17.2% from a year earlier. Net loss attributable to shareholders was KRW 1.13 billion, KRW 922 million and KRW 373 million in the first three quarters of 2026, three consecutive loss quarters.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, net profit attributable to shareholders of KRW 663 million came despite an operating loss, which appears to reflect non-operating items, and the one-off nature can be checked in that quarter's report. Operating cash flow was negative in each year from 2022 to 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The core downstream market for iron oxide is the ferrite core market. FnGuide notes that the market for ferrite cores used in electric vehicle converters and charging equipment is growing. The same source says China's rare earth export controls have spurred research into ferrite as a substitute.

Substitution away from rare earth magnets could widen ferrite demand, but the risk that demand moves to other materials should also be weighed. On pricing, a fall in iron oxide prices was cited as the cause of lower sales in 2024, and a lower cost of sales ratio then supported results as raw material prices eased.

EG's market position is about 12th to 15th in global high-grade iron oxide share, and it is described as the only producer of the SKM-2T motor-grade product. Within Korea's chemicals sector, EG is a small magnetic materials supplier rather than a large petrochemical company.

Because ferrite demand is tied to electric vehicle and electronic component production, utilization and shipment volumes can vary with the direction of downstream conditions. Its positioning therefore depends on the share of its niche products and on how well it manages raw material costs.

06

Outlook

The most recent trend is the return to operating profit, with operating profit recorded in both the second and third quarters of 2026 and third-quarter revenue of KRW 18.5 billion, up 17.2% from a year earlier.

The next checkpoint is whether the profitable run continues into the fourth quarter, which will be reflected in the annual figures of the 2026 annual report.

Net loss attributable to shareholders narrowed from KRW 1.13 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 922 million in the second quarter and KRW 373 million in the third, so whether that trend continues is a key question.

Total equity was KRW 23.2 billion and total liabilities were KRW 128.4 billion at the end of 2025, so the pace of reducing leverage matters. Operating cash flow was negative from 2022 to 2025, so whether operating profit converts into cash is also worth watching.

Ferrite core demand depends on growth in electric vehicle charging and converter markets and on substitution research tied to rare earth controls, which will shape iron oxide volumes.

The Energy and Environment segment was flagged for weaker profitability in a 2025 filing summary, so its recovery is another variable for total earnings. New businesses such as lithium compounds are at a development stage, and no commercialization timeline is confirmed.

Bull factors

Quarterly operating profit turns positive

Operating profit of KRW 1.04 billion in the second quarter of 2026 turned positive, and KRW 1.22 billion in the third quarter turned positive from a year earlier. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 18.5 billion rose 17.2% year on year.

The 2025 operating loss of KRW 2.17 billion was far smaller than the KRW 14.4 billion loss in 2023, and the operating margin improved to -3.5%.

Gross profit gains from a lower cost ratio

Based on FnGuide's aggregated figures, the cost of sales ratio fell from 96.55% in 2023 to 81.27% in 2025. Gross profit over the same period rose from KRW 2.1 billion to KRW 11.8 billion. The improvement came from cost structure even though revenue did not grow sharply.

Possible expansion of ferrite demand

FnGuide notes that the market for ferrite cores in electric vehicle converters and charging equipment is growing, and that China's rare earth export controls have spurred ferrite substitution research.

EG is described as ranking about 12th to 15th in global high-grade iron oxide share and as the only producer of its motor-grade product. Both factors could support iron oxide volumes.

Bear factors

High leverage and capital structure burden

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 204.6% in 2022 to 554.4% in 2025. Total liabilities were KRW 128.4 billion and total equity KRW 23.2 billion at the end of 2025.

Total equity is smaller than the KRW 60.3 billion attributable to shareholders, which reflects a large non-controlling interest burden, and the pace of balance sheet repair is the issue.

Continued shareholder net losses and negative cash flow

Net loss attributable to shareholders was KRW 1.13 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 922 million in the second and KRW 373 million in the third, three straight loss quarters. The cumulative net loss attributable to shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 1.76 billion. Operating cash flow was still negative at KRW -3.67 billion in 2025.

Iron oxide prices and new business uncertainty

A 2024 company commentary said sales fell on lower iron oxide prices and that the waste treatment business was struggling. New businesses such as lithium compounds are at a development stage, so the timing of their earnings contribution is unclear. The Energy and Environment segment was flagged for weaker profitability in a 2025 filing summary.

10

Risk factors

Financial risk

A debt-to-equity ratio of 554.4% and total liabilities of KRW 128.4 billion could translate into interest and refinancing burdens. With total equity at KRW 23.2 billion, continued losses would further reduce financial room. Even with operating profit turning positive, negative operating cash flow could constrain funds for repayment.

Raw material and price risk

Swings in iron oxide prices, iron scrap prices and exchange rates can move margins. In 2024, lower iron oxide prices led to lower sales. Even after the cost of sales ratio fell to 81.27%, a rebound in raw material prices could reverse part of the improvement.

Concentration and customer demand risk

The iron oxide business depends on the ferrite core cycle and is sensitive to downstream inventory and utilization changes. If substitution research moves demand to other materials, volumes could fall.

Because the portfolio is concentrated in a few segments, changes in specific demand sources can have a large effect on total results.
