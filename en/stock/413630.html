02

Business structure

CP System, founded in 1993, is a specialist in industrial cable protection products, making cable chains, flexible tubes and connectors and exporting to more than 30 countries. It listed on KOSDAQ in June 2024 through a merger with a special purpose acquisition company.

Its core cable chains protect cables and hoses in moving machinery, and their applications have widened from general industry to semiconductors, displays, secondary batteries and robots.

G-Clean Chain, a cable chain for semiconductor and battery cleanrooms, is the product the company credited for sales growth in 3Q 2026. Roboway and Robokit are cable protection solutions for robotic automation equipment, and sales have grown as they gained recognition in the automotive and battery industries.

In shipbuilding, the company works with major domestic shipbuilders, and one report said it agreed to supply specialty cable chains for six large car carriers under the MASGA project. Eugene Investment & Securities estimated steady growth in China sales and rapid growth in India-bound sales.

In September 2026 the company said it had been registered as an official vendor of a global secondary battery cell maker. Its customers now span semiconductor equipment makers, industrial automation firms, shipbuilders and medical equipment companies.

Industrial cable protection is also contested by overseas component makers, but one report said the company has proven de facto proprietary technology in the domestic shipbuilding supply chain through customer quality certifications.

The sector classification is chemicals, but the product structure is closer to industrial components, so its sales mix differs from large petrochemical listings such as LG Chem and Lotte Chemical.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results moved in different directions between 2024 and 2025. Sales were KRW 21.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 19.1 billion in 2025, down 10.5% year on year.

Operating profit turned to KRW 3.32 billion in 2024 from a loss, then fell to KRW 2.14 billion in 2025, down 35.5%, and the operating margin declined from 15.6% to 11.2%. The company attributed the 2025 operating profit decline to temporary costs from setting up an overseas branch.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 57.68 million in 2022 to KRW 209 million in 2023, up 262.5%, then swung to a net loss of KRW 12.1 billion in 2024, and returned to KRW 2 billion in profit in 2025.

Net profit and controlling-shareholder net profit were reported as the same amounts for 2022 to 2025, so no non-controlling interest gap appears on an annual basis. Operating cash flow moved from -KRW 37.15 million in 2022 to KRW 148 million in 2023, KRW 4.46 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.99 billion in 2025.

At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 43.5 billion and total liabilities were KRW 2.9 billion, a debt ratio of 6.7%. On a quarterly basis, operating profit bottomed in 4Q 2025 at KRW 405 million, down 26.9% from the prior quarter, after KRW 554 million in 3Q 2025.

It recovered to KRW 534 million in 1Q 2026, up 31.7%, and the company said raw material pre-purchases for additional orders weighed on that quarter. Sales rose to KRW 6.85 billion in 2Q 2026, up 31.5%, and operating profit to KRW 1.87 billion, up 250.9%.

Operating profit reached KRW 2.1 billion in 3Q 2026, up 12.1% from the prior quarter, and grew for three consecutive quarters from 1Q to 3Q 2026. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 710 million in 3Q 2026, down 60.3% from the prior quarter, though it rose 2.3% year on year.

Over the most recent four quarters, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 4.01 billion.

05

Industry analysis

CP System's demand is tied to capital spending cycles in semiconductor and display equipment, secondary batteries, robotic automation, shipbuilding and medical equipment.

In a late-2025 report, Eugene Investment & Securities noted that large automotive and battery projects were largely completed by the first half of 2025 and that new investment had been held back by weak electric vehicle sales.

By contrast, the company said in May 2026 that it was responding to expanding investment in semiconductor and robotic automation equipment, supported by rising machine tool orders and wider overseas orders from major equipment makers.

In shipbuilding, some revenue was pushed into 1Q 2026 by customer circumstances at the end of 2025, and the company said shipbuilding projects were ongoing.

Cycle positions differ by sector: semiconductor and display equipment and robotic automation have seen demand growth according to company commentary, while automotive and battery investment depends on electric vehicle demand. In overseas sales, China is growing steadily and India is cited as the growth axis.

Eugene estimated that India-bound sales were about 17% of overseas sales in 2024, roughly KRW 880 million. Within the chemicals sector, large listings such as LG Chem and Lotte Chemical are primarily petrochemical businesses and are not direct comparables.

Cable protection products compete with domestic and overseas specialists in the industrial automation components market. The claim of de facto proprietary technology in shipbuilding supply cannot be sized with reliable market share data.

High customer concentration by industry means that a cut in one sector's capital spending passes quickly into sales.

06

Outlook

The company says it aims to strengthen production capacity through higher value-added products centered on G-Clean Chain and the second plant, and to expand supply to global customers.

The second plant is being built on land and buildings acquired in Gijang-gun, Busan, with completion targeted for the first half of 2027. A report says production capacity will triple.

Reported investment amounts differ: an August 2026 report cited KRW 7.1 billion, while a report dated November 11, 2026 cited KRW 10 billion. In a June 2026 report, Eugene Investment & Securities estimated full-year 2026 sales of KRW 23.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 4 billion, with an operating margin of 17.0%.

These are broker estimates, not disclosed results. Cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 4.5 billion, already above that full-year estimate, and 4Q 2026 results remain to be reported. The 2026 annual report will confirm the full-year figures.

In September 2026 the company said its vendor registration with a global secondary battery cell maker established a base for cleanroom cable protection supply.

In shipbuilding, the company plans to take part in orders for higher value-added vessels such as naval ships and LNG carriers as the MASGA project expands, and an official said it is preparing production lines and raw material supply for expected growth in specialty cable chain orders.

A separate announcement said Roboway supply to global automotive production lines is expanding. The next points to check are the 4Q 2026 earnings trend and the production effect after the second plant opens in the first half of 2027.

Bull factors

Consecutive Quarterly Operating Recovery

Operating profit rose from a KRW 410 million low in 4Q 2025 to KRW 534 million in 1Q 2026, KRW 1.87 billion in 2Q and KRW 2.1 billion in 3Q 2026. Cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 4.5 billion, up 160.0% year on year. Sales and profit growing together from 2Q 2026 separates this period from the weakness of late 2025.

Advanced Industry Capex Demand and New Products

The company said G-Clean Chain sales grew in 3Q 2026, with sales expanding mainly across semiconductor, display and automotive industries. Roboway is gaining supply as it earns recognition in automotive and battery manufacturing.

Registration as an official vendor for a global secondary battery cell maker in September 2026 widened the base for cleanroom cable protection.

Shipbuilding, Overseas Sales and Capacity Expansion

The company expects specialty cable chain orders under the MASGA project to keep growing. Eugene Investment & Securities estimated a sharp rise in India-bound sales in 2026. The second plant targets completion in the first half of 2027 and is planned to triple production capacity.

09

Bear factors

Weaker Full-Year 2025 Results

Sales fell 10.5% to KRW 19.1 billion in 2025, and operating profit declined 35.5% to KRW 2.14 billion. The operating margin dropped from 15.6% in 2024 to 11.2% in 2025. A net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 12.1 billion in 2024 means the profit track record is still short.

Quarterly Profit Volatility

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 710 million in 3Q 2026, down 60.3% from KRW 1.79 billion in 2Q. The swing between quarters is large. Until the notes explaining the difference are reviewed, the durability of profit remains too early to judge.

Project Timing and Deferred Capex

A late-2025 report cited delayed investment in major industrial projects and the shift of shipbuilding revenue into 1Q 2026 as burdens on results. Automotive and battery investment was described as held back by weak electric vehicle sales. Quarterly sales are exposed to the timing of investment by a few customer sectors.

10

Risk factors

Customer Investment Timing

When large shipbuilding or automotive projects slip because of customer circumstances, quarterly sales are pushed back. The 4Q 2025 earnings gap is one such case. How far the sales recovery depends on a few projects can be gauged from upcoming quarterly disclosures.

Currency and Raw Materials

Foreign currency translation gains from export growth and financial income from a weaker won lifted 1Q 2026 net profit. A reversal in the exchange rate would reduce that contribution. Raw material pre-purchases for additional orders can weigh on operating profit.

Expansion Execution

Reported investment for the second plant differs between KRW 7.1 billion and KRW 10 billion across reports. If the first-half 2027 completion target slips, the benefit of added capacity is also delayed. Whether utilization matches order flow after the plant opens is the next point to check.

Overall view

CP System's operating profit rose for three consecutive quarters from 1Q to 3Q 2026 after bottoming in 4Q 2025, and cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 grew sharply year on year.

However, full-year 2025 sales and operating profit declined, and the company posted a KRW 12.1 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in 2024, so the profit recovery period is still only three quarters.

Positive factors include sales growth of G-Clean Chain and Roboway aimed at semiconductor, secondary battery and robotics demand, participation in the MASGA shipbuilding project, vendor registration with a secondary battery cell maker, and the second plant expansion.

Negative factors include quarterly sales swings from delayed large projects, the 60.3% quarterly decline in 3Q 2026 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders, and earnings volatility from raw material pre-purchases and exchange rates.

On valuation, market value is large relative to KRW 4.01 billion of controlling-shareholder net profit over the last four quarters, and Eugene Investment & Securities judged in a June 2026 report that the stock traded at a discount to domestic peers.

That report's full-year 2026 operating profit estimate of KRW 4 billion has already been exceeded by the KRW 4.5 billion cumulative figure for the first three quarters. The next checkpoints are the preliminary full-year 2026 results, the 2026 annual report, 1Q 2027 results and completion of the second plant.
