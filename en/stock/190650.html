02

Business structure

Korea Asset Investment Securities was established in January 2000 and lists IB advisory, bond underwriting, trading, brokerage and fund management as its main businesses.

The company centers on financing for small and venture businesses, and its cumulative small and venture business financing support passed KRW 2.7 trillion as of March 2026. In June 2024 it was re-designated for a fifth term as a financial investment company specializing in small and medium-sized enterprises.

Income comes from commission fees and from valuation and disposal gains on securities and derivatives. A 2024 news report covering April 2023 to March 2024 cited net operating revenue of KRW 55.3 billion, of which fee income was KRW 38.1 billion and disposal and valuation gains were KRW 17.1 billion.

That report's revenue basis differs from the DART sales figures used in this report. The company attributed softer fee income to a real estate downturn that reduced financial advisory fees. Collective investment AUM passed KRW 900 billion in September 2025.

Listed financial peers include Mirae Asset Securities, Samsung Securities, NH Investment & Securities, KIWOOM Securities and Daishin Securities. The largest shareholder, a Korean partnership entity, holds 45.68% of common shares according to NICE Investors data, and the company had 226 employees as of March 31, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On the DART periodic reports, sales moved from KRW 223 billion in the year to March 2023 to KRW 217.3 billion in the year to March 2024 (down 2.6%).

They then rose to KRW 268.5 billion in the year to March 2025 (up 23.6%), the largest year in the period, and reached KRW 261.9 billion in the year to March 2026 (down 2.5%).

Operating results swung from an operating loss of KRW 2.08 billion in the year to March 2023 to operating profit of KRW 7.87 billion in the year to March 2024, a turnaround to profit.

Operating profit then rose to KRW 10.3 billion in the year to March 2025 (up 30.2%) and fell to KRW 9.15 billion in the year to March 2026 (down 10.8%).

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders followed the same path: a net loss of KRW 2.77 billion in the year to March 2023, a profit of KRW 5.81 billion in the year to March 2024, KRW 7.73 billion in the year to March 2025 (up 33.1%) and KRW 5.86 billion in the year to March 2026 (down 24.1%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same values in each year.

The operating margin was -0.9% in the year to March 2023, 3.6% in the year to March 2024, 3.8% in the year to March 2025 and 3.5% in the year to March 2026, so the margin held in the mid-3% range even in a year when sales declined.

Operating cash flow was an outflow of KRW 36.7 billion, an outflow of KRW 33.3 billion, an inflow of KRW 51.7 billion and an outflow of KRW 23.5 billion across the four fiscal years, with wide swings.

Quarterly, operating profit was KRW 4.72 billion in 3Q 2025, an operating loss of KRW 973 million in 4Q 2025 (a turn to loss), operating profit of KRW 4.56 billion in 1Q 2026 (a turn to profit), KRW 6.22 billion in 2Q 2026 (up 36.5% from the prior quarter) and KRW 647 million in 3Q 2026.

Revenue in 3Q 2026 of KRW 52.9 billion was down 25.2% from the prior quarter. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the four quarters from 4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026 totaled KRW 7.87 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Korean securities firms like Korea Asset Investment Securities have earnings closely tied to stock market trading and proprietary trading gains. The company's results show the same pattern.

A news report covering April 2023 to March 2024 said valuation and disposal gains on securities and derivatives more than doubled from the prior year. Over the same period fee income declined slightly as the real estate downturn reduced advisory demand.

Revenue therefore spans two drivers: market price movements and real estate-related advisory demand. To interpret the fall in operating profit to KRW 647 million in 3Q 2026, the mix between fee income and disposal gains should be checked in the next periodic report.

On competitive positioning, the company's distinguishing feature is its specialization in financing for small and mid-sized businesses, which it has held through several re-designations as a specialized investment firm.

Listed peers in the same industry include Mirae Asset Securities, Samsung Securities, NH Investment & Securities, KIWOOM Securities and Daishin Securities. A practical starting point is how the weight of proprietary trading gains changes in the coming quarterly reports.
