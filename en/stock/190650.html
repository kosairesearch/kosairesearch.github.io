Revenue Up Three Straight Quarters, Profit Still Volatile

Korea Asset Investment Securities posted revenue growth for three straight quarters through Q2 2026 and operating profit in both Q1 and Q2 2026. Annual operating profit and controlling-shareholder net profit for the fiscal year ended March 2026 declined from the prior year, and Korean trading volume contracted in Q3 2026.

02

Business structure

Korea Asset Investment Securities was founded in January 2000 and runs the traditional securities businesses of brokerage, proprietary trading, investment banking (IB) and asset management. Revenue shares by segment were not confirmed by filings or press reports as of this report, so no segment figures are cited.

According to a press report on the fiscal year ended March 2024, fee and commission income declined slightly from the prior year to KRW 38.1 billion, and the company attributed the drop in financial advisory fees to a downturn in the property market.

Gains and losses on valuation and disposal of securities and derivatives rose to KRW 17.1 billion, more than double the prior year, which suggests a larger role for proprietary investment results. The company was also reported to have built specialist strength in renewable energy investment.

It has been designated a specialized financial investment company for small and mid-sized enterprises five consecutive times, pointing to corporate finance for SMEs and mid-sized firms as a core client base; this designation was reported in 2024.

The capital gap with larger firms is wide: Korea Investment & Securities reported standalone equity of KRW 13.55 trillion as of end-September 2026, compared with total equity of KRW 99.9 billion for this company at end-March 2026.

The company is therefore better understood as competing in small and mid-sized corporate finance rather than head-to-head with large brokers. A securities information site summary cited deterioration in proprietary trading and IB profitability, but because its basis differs, those figures are not used here.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue moved from KRW 223 billion for the fiscal year ended March 2023 to KRW 217.3 billion for FY ended March 2024, down 2.6%, then rose to KRW 268.5 billion for FY ended March 2025, up 23.6%, and fell to KRW 261.9 billion for FY ended March 2026, down 2.5%.

FY ended March 2025 was the largest year in the FY2023 to FY2026 span, and FY ended March 2024 was the smallest.

Operating profit swung from an operating loss of KRW 2.08 billion in FY ended March 2023 to operating profit of KRW 7.87 billion in FY ended March 2024, rose to KRW 10.3 billion in FY ended March 2025 (up 30.2%), and fell to KRW 9.15 billion in FY ended March 2026 (down 10.8%).

The operating margin was 3.6%, 3.8% and 3.5% in the last three fiscal years, all clustered between 3.5% and 3.8%.

Controlling-shareholder net profit moved from a loss of KRW 2.77 billion in FY ended March 2023 to KRW 5.81 billion in FY ended March 2024, rose to KRW 7.73 billion in FY ended March 2025 (up 33.1%), and fell to KRW 5.86 billion in FY ended March 2026 (down 24.1%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts in each year. Job Korea financial data shows non-operating income fell sharply in FY ended March 2026 compared with FY ended March 2025, which appears to have widened the decline in pre-tax profit.

Operating cash flow was negative KRW 36.7 billion, negative KRW 33.3 billion, positive KRW 51.7 billion and negative KRW 23.5 billion across the four fiscal years, with large swings.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 72.4 billion in Q2 2025, KRW 59.6 billion in Q3 2025 (down 17.7% quarter on quarter), KRW 63.6 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 66.3 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 70.8 billion in Q2 2026, rising for three consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q2 2026.

Operating profit was KRW 847 million in Q2 2025, KRW 4.72 billion in Q3 2025, an operating loss of KRW 973 million in Q4 2025, KRW 4.56 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 6.22 billion in Q2 2026. The 634.0% year-on-year gain in Q2 2026 operating profit largely reflects a low base in Q2 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net profit recovered to KRW 3.09 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 3.59 billion in Q2 2026 after a KRW 941 million loss in Q4 2025. The factors behind the Q4 2025 loss are not confirmed by the disclosures reviewed, so they are not described here.

05

Industry analysis

Korean stock trading volume, the core indicator for brokers, fell 36.24% in Q3 2026 from Q2, with average daily value dropping from KRW 118.1 trillion to KRW 75.3 trillion on the combined KRX and NXT basis.

The contraction reduced brokerage income, and rising market interest rates added bond valuation losses and higher funding costs. Among the five largest Korean brokers, the three that had reported Q3 2026 results posted an average 52.3% drop in consolidated net profit from the prior quarter.

The decline was attributed to a high base in the first half, weaker stock markets in the second half and rising rates.

Within the same market, the subsidiary Korea Investment & Securities saw brokerage commission income fall 36.5% from the prior quarter, while its investment banking revenue rose 12.0% to KRW 216.4 billion, showing that IB income can partly offset weaker brokerage.

The segment mix of Korea Asset Investment Securities is not confirmed, so the direct effect of this market on the company is not quantified. On competitive position, large brokers hold far larger capital bases and funding tools such as bills and IMA balances, but whether this company uses such tools is not confirmed.

Because interest rates and trading volume have moved together in the sector, whether they continue to do so will shape the direction of industry earnings.

Bull factors

Quarterly Profit Recovery on a Low Base

Operating profit was positive in both Q1 and Q2 2026, and Q2 operating profit of KRW 6.22 billion rose 634.0% from a year earlier. A low base of KRW 847 million in Q2 2025 magnified the growth rate. Revenue rose for three consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q2 2026. Given the base effect, the growth rates alone do not establish a durable improvement in earnings quality.

Gradual Improvement in Capital Structure

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 313.9% for FY ended March 2025 to 276.3% for FY ended March 2026. Total equity at end-March 2026 was KRW 99.9 billion, equal to controlling-shareholder equity, which indicates no non-controlling interests.

Total liabilities of KRW 276 billion remain large, but the build-up of equity is a positive factor for financial capacity.

SME Finance and Renewable Energy Investment Track Record

Designation as a specialized financial investment company for small and mid-sized enterprises for five consecutive times shows corporate finance capability for SMEs and mid-sized firms.

Gains on valuation and disposal of securities and derivatives reached KRW 17.1 billion in FY ended March 2024, more than double the prior year. Such trading gains and corporate finance experience could buffer earnings in weak brokerage periods. The designation was reported in 2024, so the current status requires separate confirmation.
