02

Business structure

Withtech makes monitoring equipment that measures airborne molecular contamination (AMC) in semiconductor cleanrooms.

According to a 2021 technical analysis report by NICE D&B, its main product is a wet-method AMC monitoring system, and it also supplies TMS equipment that continuously monitors emissions from industrial facilities.

Alphasquare's breakdown as of December 2025 shows own products at 55.44%, services at 44.13% and other items at 0.43%. The same report listed Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and LG Display as customers, and news coverage says the company has carried out similar SK hynix contracts over the past three years.

A services share near 44% means maintenance and service contracts form a large part of revenue alongside equipment delivery. The Samsung Electronics TMS maintenance contract disclosed in October 2026, worth KRW 13.04 billion, is an example of such a service contract.

On competition, the 2021 report noted that wet-method units cost more and take longer to analyze than optical-method rivals. The same report described a mobile radiochemical laboratory for radioactive waste nuclide analysis as a planned growth product.

The exchange classifies the company under machinery and equipment, while a data provider classifies it under semiconductor equipment, so the peer group changes with the benchmark used. Quarterly revenue is strongly driven by when chipmakers spend on equipment and when deliveries take place.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results fell from 2022 through 2024 and recovered in 2025. Revenue was highest in 2022 at KRW 72.6 billion, then dropped to KRW 49.6 billion in 2023 (down 31.7%) and KRW 43.6 billion in 2024 (down 12.0%) before rising to KRW 57.8 billion in 2025 (up 32.5%).

Operating profit fell from KRW 14.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.42 billion in 2023 (down 83.2%) and KRW 966 million in 2024 (down 60.2%), and the operating margin slid from 19.9% to 2.2%.

In 2025 operating profit recovered to KRW 5.13 billion (up 430.9%) with an 8.9% margin, and the company said higher AMC equipment sales lifted both revenue and profit.

Parent-shareholder net profit fell from KRW 12.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.33 billion in 2023 (down 48.3%), then rose to KRW 7.07 billion in 2024 (up 11.6%) and eased to KRW 6.44 billion in 2025 (down 8.8%).

In 2024 net profit rose even though operating profit fell 60.2%, so the role of non-operating items and taxes is worth checking in the annual report notes.

Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 2.3 billion in 2023 to KRW 2.53 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.5 billion in 2025, while the debt ratio rose from 16.4% in 2024 to 22.6% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue peaked at KRW 22.8 billion in Q4 2025, bottomed at KRW 7.29 billion in Q1 2026 (down 68.0% from the prior quarter), and then grew for two straight quarters from Q2 to Q3 2026, reaching KRW 18.3 billion and KRW 18.6 billion.

Operating results swung from an operating loss of KRW 1.91 billion in Q1 2026 to operating profit of KRW 2.65 billion in Q2 and KRW 3.9 billion in Q3 (up 96.2% year on year), the highest of the five disclosed quarters.

Net profit moved differently: parent-shareholder net profit was KRW 2.31 billion in Q1 2026 despite the operating loss, and fell to KRW 960 million in Q3 2026 (down 74.5% year on year) even as operating profit rose. Parent-shareholder net profit for the latest four quarters totaled KRW 11.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Withtech's end market is the cleanroom contamination control demand created by semiconductor and display fabs. A 2021 NICE D&B report said that rising chipmaker capex supports steady revenue in a quality-driven, negotiated-contract environment, and it cited high entry barriers as a strength.

The same report described a shift in contamination monitoring from particle-based to molecular-level AMC monitoring. On competition, optical-method AMC monitors are the alternative, and wet-method units differ in unit cost and analysis time.

Recent supply has come in repeated contracts with SK hynix, with two disclosed in September and October 2026. The rebound in quarterly revenue from Q1 to Q2 2026 after the Q1 decline shows results moving with fab investment timing.

In sector comparisons, People & Technology, SPG and Koh Young Technology are grouped in the same machinery and equipment category. Withtech is closer to process-specific semiconductor equipment, so its demand drivers differ from robotics or construction machinery peers in the same classification.

How these contracts flow into quarterly revenue in 2027 is the next point to confirm. At the March 23, 2026 shareholders meeting, the 23rd-term financial statements were approved with clean audit opinions on both separate and consolidated bases, and a cash dividend on common shares was set.

The 2026 annual business report will allow comparison of annual revenue and operating margin with 2025, when revenue was KRW 57.8 billion and the margin 8.9%. Because the 2025 preliminary annual figures were disclosed on February 9, 2026, the 2026 preliminary figures are likely in early 2027.

Quarterly direction will depend on whether TMS maintenance revenue keeps coming in each quarter and on delivery timing of semiconductor equipment.

Repeat contracts with chipmakers

Bear factors

Large swings in quarterly results

Q1 2026 revenue fell 68.0% from the prior quarter to KRW 7.29 billion, and operating results swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.91 billion. Operating profit fell from KRW 14.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 966 million in 2024, another sign of volatility.

Revenue that can swing sharply within a few quarters with customer investment timing is the main bearish factor.

Operating profit and net profit diverge

Parent-shareholder net profit in Q3 2026 was KRW 960 million, down 71.6% from the prior quarter and 74.5% from a year earlier. Operating profit in the same quarter rose to KRW 3.9 billion, so the factors that held down net profit need to be identified.

In 2024 operating profit fell while parent-shareholder net profit rose, so the two measures have not moved together.

Customer concentration and product limits

Services made up 44.13% of revenue, so the income structure relies heavily on service contracts alongside equipment sales. Results depend on a small group of chipmaker customers, including SK hynix and Samsung Electronics.

The 2021 report pointed to higher unit cost and longer analysis time as drawbacks of wet-method AMC equipment compared with optical rivals. The company also attributed the 2025 revenue increase to AMC equipment sales.

Risk factors

Customer concentration and capex cycle

With key customers concentrated among chipmakers, a pullback in capital spending could hit equipment and maintenance revenue at the same time. The 68.0% quarter-on-quarter revenue drop in Q1 2026 is an example of this risk showing up in results.

Whether individual contracts, such as the SK hynix contract ending April 30, 2027, are renewed is the item to track.

Earnings structure shifts and net profit gap

Parent-shareholder net profit fell in Q3 2026 even though operating profit rose, so drivers such as non-operating items and taxes need to be identified. Parent-shareholder net profit for the latest four quarters totals KRW 11.4 billion. Because quarterly results have moved between profit and loss, earnings stability still needs more evidence.

New business execution and peer benchmark risk

The radioactive nuclide analysis laboratory and nuclear decommissioning-related business were in the productization stage as of the 2021 report. When and how much they contribute to results will become visible in upcoming periodic reports. Investment in new areas could also weigh on core profitability.

Because the company is classed under machinery and equipment by the exchange but under semiconductor equipment by a data provider, valuation comparisons can change with the benchmark chosen.

Overall view

Withtech makes AMC monitoring equipment for semiconductor cleanrooms and TMS emissions monitoring systems. Its 2025 results recovered, with revenue of KRW 57.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.13 billion.

Quarterly results in 2026 improved from an operating loss in Q1 to operating profit in Q2 and KRW 3.9 billion in Q3. However, parent-shareholder net profit fell to KRW 960 million in the same quarter, so whether the operating recovery carries through to net profit is a task for the next periodic report.

On orders, contracts with SK hynix and Samsung Electronics were disclosed one after another between September and October 2026, showing continuing customer relationships. Bullish factors include a 22.6% debt ratio and operating cash flow of KRW 10.5 billion in 2025.

Bearish factors include concentration among a few chipmaker customers, large quarterly revenue swings, and the earlier decline in operating profit from KRW 14.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 966 million in 2024.

On valuation, the multiple is low against the industry average, but the reading changes with the profit measure used, so it should be read alongside the earnings trend.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary 2026 annual results, expected in early 2027, and the 2026 annual business report, where operating margin and parent-shareholder net profit will be the key measures.
