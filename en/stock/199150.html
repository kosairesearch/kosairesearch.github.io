AI Data Demand Meets a Revenue Setback

02

Business structure

Data Streams is a data solutions company founded in September 2001 and listed on the KONEX market in June 2014. Its core products are TeraStream, MetaStream and TeraONE, and TeraStream is a data integration solution that extracts, transforms and loads large volumes of data quickly.

The company says it packages data integration, standardization, quality management, lineage, cataloging and virtualization into a single platform, TeraONE. It also stresses that its 14 products are modular and can be supplied individually.

A Research Alum report described the company as the domestic leader in data integration, with a share of about 90% in the financial sector. That report is an older document hosted on the company's website, so current market share may differ.

According to an IT Daily report, Gartner assessed the company as a leader in data fabric, the next-generation big data platform trend. The company's newsletter cites a data governance portal built for KB Kookmin Bank and data lake consulting performed for SK Group.

It is applying generative AI to automate metadata extraction and also operates AI-Stream, a generative AI solution for government and public institutions.

No public source confirms revenue weights by product or service, so they are described qualitatively only, and the competitive set is global data integration vendors and large system integrators.

03

Recent trends

Revenue for 2025 was KRW 20.54 billion, down 22.1% from the prior year. Operating profit for the same year was KRW 264.28 million, down 61.4%. Per Saramin's financial data, the 2025 operating margin was 1.3% and net income was KRW 254.23 million.

These figures appear to be on a standalone basis, given how they line up with the 2024 standalone numbers, and consolidated figures should be checked in the annual report. In 2024, revenue rose 4.2% to KRW 26.4 billion and the company returned to a net profit of KRW 700 million.

The company attributed the improvement to a reorganized sales force, lower outsourcing and overhead costs from using in-house staff, and a revaluation of tangible assets.

In 2023, consolidated revenue was KRW 25.5 billion with an operating loss of KRW 4.9 billion and a net loss of KRW 4.9 billion, and equity was impaired. The company had guided to 2025 revenue of KRW 32 billion, and actual revenue fell well short of that outlook.

As of 17:02 KST on 2026-10-27, the share price is KRW 1,689 (-0.47% day on day), market capitalization is KRW 7.2 billion and traded value is KRW 0. Investing.com shows a 2026-09-23 close of KRW 1,699, a 52-week range of KRW 1,405 to 3,700 and a one-year change of -41.4%.

04

Outlook

The Pakistan post office automation project is backed by the Economic Development Cooperation Fund (EDCF), the company completed final contracting on 2026-08-07, and its share is about KRW 4 billion of the KRW 28.2 billion total.

The company will supply three products to the project: MetaStream, TeraStream and TeraSort. Lead member Bankware Global has stated that the project runs through March 2028.

The timing of revenue recognition for the company's share is not confirmed in public sources, so its earnings impact should be checked against later filings.

Between 2026-03-31 and 2026-06-11, the company issued a series of releases on its AI-Ready Data strategy and partnerships, and on 2026-02-27 it presented an AI governance strategy based on data lineage. Its medium-term targets are revenue of KRW 100 billion and operating profit of KRW 26 billion by 2029.

A 2023 report cited a goal of exceeding KRW 100 billion in annual revenue in 2026. With 2025 revenue at KRW 20.54 billion, the gap between targets and results is a variable that needs verification.

The KOSDAQ transfer listing was postponed to 2024 or later in a 2023 report, and no confirmed material describes its later progress.

05

Bull factors

Integration and governance suite with financial and public references

The company offers integration, standardization, quality, lineage, cataloging and virtualization solutions needed for data fabric and supplies 14 products as modules. Large customer cases such as KB Kookmin Bank and SK Group are cited in its newsletter. It also won the grand prize at the 12th Korea KONEX Awards. This product breadth and reference base can be read as supporting its credibility in competitive bids.

A first overseas public-sector reference

The Pakistan post office project builds a digital transformation framework integrating logistics, financial and public services across about 2,700 post offices. The company's three products are formally included in the software supply scope of the final contract.

The company says it intends to use the project as a base to pursue further opportunities in overseas public-sector and financial markets. Having an overseas case for a business centered on the domestic market can be seen as an early step toward diversification.

AI-driven data demand and cost restructuring

Through 2026 the company has repeatedly put out materials placing AI-Ready Data and AI governance at the center of its message. In 2024 it returned to profit through structural changes that cut outsourcing and overhead costs.

With a 2025 operating margin of only 1.3%, room remains for margin improvement through revenue scale and cost control. That statement concerns business metrics only and is not a view on the share price.

06

Bear factors

2025 decline and the gap to targets

Revenue fell 22.1% and operating profit fell 61.4% in 2025. Compared with the company's own KRW 32 billion revenue outlook for that year, the gap between guidance and results is large. The 2023-era goal of KRW 100 billion in annual revenue for 2026 is also far from the current revenue scale. Growth targets that are revised repeatedly weigh on how management plans are assessed.

A fragile balance sheet

Equity was impaired in 2023, and in 2024 consolidated total equity was KRW 1.7 billion with a debt ratio of 885.69%. Consolidated total assets in 2024 were KRW 16.5 billion and total liabilities were KRW 14.8 billion.

With 2025 operating profit at KRW 264.28 million, the pace at which earnings can rebuild equity is limited. The earnings base is thin relative to the debt load, which leaves little room to absorb external shocks.

Thin KONEX liquidity and a falling share price

As of 2026-10-27, market capitalization is KRW 7.2 billion and traded value is KRW 0. Investing.com shows three-month average volume of about 720 shares and a one-year change of -41.4%. Thin trading means small orders can move the price sharply and trades may be hard to execute when desired. The absence of a confirmed KOSDAQ transfer date is a further constraint on market access.

07

Risk factors

Overseas project

The Pakistan project is run by a joint venture led by Bankware Global and runs through March 2028. In a joint venture structure, schedule management by the lead member and other partners can affect the company's own revenue recognition.

Overseas public projects can be affected by the counterparty's fiscal conditions, exchange rates and policy changes. With the company's share at about KRW 4 billion, delays or scope changes could shift the timing at which results are reflected.

Security and regulation

The company said it blocked the abnormal external access, found during its investigation that some personal data had been leaked, applied security measures and reported the matter to the relevant authorities. The notice does not confirm the scale of the leak.

The amended Personal Information Protection Act, in force since 2026-09-11, allows fines of up to 10% of revenue for repeated or serious breaches, covering cases such as a repeat violation within three years, 10 million or more victims, or a leak after a corrective order was ignored.

It has not been confirmed whether this incident meets those conditions. For a company that promotes data governance, reputational burden cannot be ruled out.

Financial and ownership

A 2024 debt ratio of 885.69% leaves the company sensitive to changes in interest rates and funding conditions. The holding of the largest shareholder and 17 related parties was 62.40% in the report dated 2026-02-20, down from 65.06% in the prior report.

The change reflects grants and exercises of stock options and changes in related parties' holdings. This is reported only as a factual change in ownership, and interpretation is left to the reader. Still, cumulative stock option exercises in a thinly traded stock can affect supply and demand.

08

Overall view

Data Streams is a KONEX-listed company with a long track record in data integration and governance and references in the financial and public sectors. In 2025, revenue fell 22.1% to KRW 20.54 billion and operating profit fell 61.4% to KRW 264.28 million.

The Pakistan post office automation project and the AI-Ready Data strategy are presented as grounds for expansion, but the timing and size of their revenue contribution need to be confirmed through filings. A 2024 debt ratio of 885.69% and the 2023 capital impairment are weak points in financial stability.

The personal data breach notice of 2026-09-18 and the entry into force of the amended Personal Information Protection Act are additional security and regulatory items to monitor. Market conditions of KRW 7.2 billion in market capitalization and KRW 0 in traded value point to uncertainty in price formation.

Because bull and bear factors coexist, the judgment is best left to readers after they check the figures in forthcoming annual and quarterly filings.
