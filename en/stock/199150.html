02

Business structure

Data Streams, founded in 2001, develops data integration and big data analytics platforms as well as data governance solutions. The company says it builds in-house the core solutions needed across the full data lifecycle, from collection to analysis and use.

Its product lines are grouped into data integration, big data platforms, data governance and data virtualization, which the company presents together as a data fabric platform.

A flagship product is mentioned as the centerpiece of its lineup, and a 2023 report expected completion of its V4.0 development to narrow losses. Its customers span finance, the public sector, manufacturing, services, education and healthcare, so its results are not tied to the cycle of a single industry.

According to the FnGuide company summary, the 2024 improvement was led by higher sales of data integration and governance solutions, while profitability was described as limited by cost burdens. The company has set out plans to raise the share of solution revenue and to deepen overseas sales.

In competition, large IT services players such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever operate in the same data platform areas. Because its revenue base is far smaller than these groups, its room for staffing and large project bids is structurally narrower. On the other hand, its focus on a defined set of products can be seen as a point of differentiation.

03

Recent trends

Based on financial data compiled for 2025, revenue was KRW 20.54 billion, down 22.1% from the prior year. Operating profit over the same period was KRW 264 million, down 61.4%, but the company remained in the black.

For 2024, revenue of KRW 26.4 billion and net profit of KRW 700 million were reported, moving the company from a 2023 operating loss of KRW 4.99 billion to a profit, according to an April 2025 press report. The same report said the turnaround also cleared its capital impairment.

Total equity in the compiled data was positive at roughly KRW 2.4 billion at the end of 2025. However, cash flow from operating activities was a loss of KRW 188 million in 2025, and investing cash flow was a loss of KRW 3.41 billion.

The leverage ratio stood at 544.57%, the equity ratio at 15.51%, and interest coverage at about 1.05. These figures suggest the interest and debt burden remains large relative to operating profit.

The share price is KRW 1,700, with a market capitalization of KRW 7.3 billion and zero trading value, and according to an investment information site it sits in the lower part of a 52-week range of KRW 1,405 to KRW 3,700.

The recovery in 2024 has not yet translated into a clear improvement in either cash generation or the financial structure.

04

Outlook

The outlook rests on two axes: a recovery in revenue and an improvement in the financial structure. On the positive side, the 2024 turnaround and the operating profit kept in 2025 show that the business base has not broken down.

Demand for data integration and governance is drawing attention alongside wider AI adoption, so product competitiveness could translate into project wins with corporate clients.

Whether the company's plans to raise the share of solution revenue and deepen overseas sales turn into actual revenue growth is the key variable. On the negative side, revenue fell to the KRW 20 billion range in 2025, a wide gap from the KRW 100 billion annual revenue target for 2026 set in a 2023 report.

The same 2023 report said the planned KOSDAQ transfer listing had been pushed back beyond the following year. Negative operating cash flow in 2025 and a high leverage ratio could limit room for further investment.

The next checkpoints are whether the revenue decline stops in the full-year 2026 results, whether operating cash flow turns positive, and whether reliance on borrowings falls. If all three improve together, the small-cap capital structure could ease.

05

Bull factors

Return to profitability

A 2025 press report said the company swung to profit in 2024 with revenue of KRW 26.4 billion and net profit of KRW 700 million. That turnaround cleared capital impairment and laid a foundation for financial soundness. Operating profit staying positive in 2025 indicates the business structure has not collapsed.

Data fabric product positioning

The company positions itself around a data fabric platform that spans integration, big data platforms, governance and virtualization. Data management is often cited as an area where corporate demand grows alongside AI adoption.

A 2023 report expected completion of V4.0 development of its flagship product to narrow losses, which points to product-side progress.

Diversified customer base

Customers span finance, the public sector, manufacturing, services, education and healthcare. A spread across industries means results are not tied to one sector's cycle, which can be viewed as a source of stability. The plan to deepen overseas sales could also widen the growth path.

06

Bear factors

Revenue decline and margin squeeze

Operating profit dropped 61.4% to KRW 264 million, so the 2024 profit scale was not sustained. A growth rebound that reversed within a year leaves open how durable the 2024 recovery is.

Cash flow and debt burden

Operating cash flow was a loss of KRW 188 million in 2025. Investing cash flow was a loss of KRW 3.41 billion in the same year. A leverage ratio of 544.57% and borrowings dependence of 31.01% indicate the financial burden remains heavy.

Small-cap liquidity and scale gap

Market capitalization is KRW 7.3 billion and trading value is zero, which points to thin liquidity. Even after clearing capital impairment, the equity ratio was only 15.51% in 2025. Compared with large IT services firms such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS, the gap in revenue scale and staffing base is clear.

07

Risk factors

Macro

If a slowdown weakens corporate IT spending, orders for data solutions could be delayed. Interest rates held at high levels would raise financing costs for a company that depends on borrowings. Its capacity to absorb external shocks is weaker than that of large IT groups with bigger investment budgets.

Competition

Data platform markets are contested by large SI firms and global data software vendors. If customers prefer integrated packages from large providers, smaller solution vendors could lose bids. A focus on specific products can, however, serve narrower demand segments.

Financial and liquidity

Negative operating cash flow in 2025 and a 544.57% leverage ratio raise the need for external funding. With a market capitalization of KRW 7.3 billion and zero trading value, financing terms could turn out unfavorable.

The earlier postponement of the planned KOSDAQ transfer listing also leaves uncertainty over capital-raising routes.

08

Overall view

Data Streams moved from losses to profit in 2024, cleared capital impairment, and kept an operating profit in 2025. Yet revenue fell 22.1% and operating profit fell 61.4% in 2025, which weakens the growth trend. On the financial structure side, a 544.57% leverage ratio and 31.01% borrowings dependence remain high.

The operating cash flow loss in 2025 shows a gap between reported profit and cash generation. Market indicators of KRW 7.3 billion in market capitalization and zero trading value point to limited liquidity and price discovery.

Strengths include the profit base, the data fabric product line and a diversified customer base, while weaknesses include revenue contraction, cash flow losses and thin liquidity. The turning points ahead are whether the revenue decline stops and whether cash flow improves in the full-year 2026 results.
