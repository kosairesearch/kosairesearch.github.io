02

Business structure

Sung Kwang Bend is a manufacturer of pipe fittings used to join piping systems. Its products go into industrial piping for shipbuilding and offshore plants, petrochemicals and power generation. Volumes depend on plant construction orders, so annual revenue is sensitive to the timing of project awards.

One analysis puts export exposure at about 70 percent of sales, so won-dollar moves feed directly into profit and loss. North American LNG export terminals are the main demand driver recently. IBK Securities estimates that more than 60 percent of the KRW 72.4 billion in new orders in Q1 2026 were for US LNG terminals.

Projects cited include Rio Grande, Port Arthur, Commonwealth and CP2. Hyundai Motor Securities noted that an LNG order from Qatar has also started to be recognized as revenue.

A stainless steel butt welding fittings supply contract for Canada's LNGC Phase 2, worth KRW 25.49 billion, was signed on August 20, 2026 with JGC Fluor BC LNG II JV. In competition, the company is grouped with Taekwang as one of the two leaders in domestic fittings.

On the production side, Korea Investment & Securities pointed to a bottleneck in the production process caused by a sharp rise in order volume.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 244 billion in 2022 to KRW 254.7 billion in 2023 (up 4.4%), then fell to KRW 227.7 billion in 2024 (down 10.6%) before recovering to KRW 245.7 billion in 2025 (up 7.9%).

Operating profit climbed from KRW 26.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 44.8 billion in 2023 (up 67.5%), then declined to KRW 42 billion in 2024 (down 6.3%) and KRW 42 billion in 2025 (down 0.1%), falling two years in a row from 2024 through 2025.

The operating margin rose from 11.0 percent in 2022 to 17.6 percent in 2023 and 18.4 percent in 2024, before easing to 17.1 percent in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was highest in 2024 at KRW 41 billion and lowest in 2025 at KRW 34.4 billion (down 16.0%).

Net income including non-controlling interests is a separate line, and in the filed figures it matched the controlling-shareholder figure in each year from 2022 to 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 549.5 billion and total liabilities were KRW 40.8 billion, and the debt ratio fell from 11.7 percent in 2022 to 7.4 percent in 2025. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 28.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 37.9 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 64.6 billion with operating profit of KRW 11.7 billion, Q4 2025 revenue fell 8.1 percent quarter on quarter to KRW 59.3 billion, Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 59.1 billion, and Q2 2026 revenue rose 8.4 percent to KRW 64.1 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue fell to KRW 42.8 billion, down 33.6 percent year on year, and the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 1.17 billion. One analyst at Umbrella Research estimated the break-even quarterly revenue at KRW 45.1 billion and said fixed costs of about KRW 23.7 billion were not recovered.

The Q3 net loss of KRW 6.09 billion came after non-operating losses of KRW 7.01 billion. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 19.9 billion, below the KRW 34.4 billion recorded for full-year 2025.

Industry analysis

Investment in North American LNG export terminals has passed final investment decisions (FID) since the second half of 2025, and that is feeding into fitting orders.

Korea Investment & Securities expected Q2 2026 new orders to exceed KRW 70 billion and, because fitting lead times are typically three to six months, placed the point where US demand would show up in revenue in Q3 2026. Q3 2026 revenue nonetheless fell 33.6 percent year on year to KRW 42.8 billion.

Korea Investment & Securities cited a delay in recognizing about KRW 20 billion of carbon steel fitting revenue as the main cause. IBK Securities attributed the slower progress of Middle East projects to the Iran war.

Order intake of KRW 50.8 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 52.5 billion in Q4 2025 was weak, which the same broker linked to a thin revenue base in early 2026. New orders in 2025 totaled KRW 184.9 billion, down 15.6 percent, which marks where the cycle stood.

Shinyoung Securities named Taekwang and Sung Kwang Bend as beneficiaries of expanding North American natural gas demand. A hypothesis circulating in the market links delays or cancellations at 47 large US data center projects to longer lead times for water-cooled cooling piping. The stronger won is also reducing the won value of export sales.

Bear factors

Utilization and fixed-cost burden

FX and non-operating losses

With exports at about 70 percent of sales, won appreciation fed through to valuation losses on foreign-currency assets and lower won-translated export sales. Data Hero's Kim In-jung characterized most of the net loss as book valuation losses.

Shrinking earnings base

Korea Investment & Securities put the delayed revenue recognition at about KRW 20 billion of carbon steel fittings, and until that is recognized the earnings base remains thin.

Production bottleneck and data center demand

Production capacity and delivery management remain constraints on revenue recovery.

Overall view

Sung Kwang Bend's Q3 2026 showed a clear weakening in results, with revenue of KRW 42.8 billion, down 33.6 percent year on year, and an operating loss of KRW 1.17 billion. Lower shipments that left fixed costs uncovered and valuation losses from a stronger won are cited as the causes.

On an annual basis, operating profit has fallen two years in a row since its 2023 peak, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders dropped to KRW 34.4 billion in 2025. On the other side, the debt ratio at the end of 2025 was a low 7.4 percent and operating cash flow was KRW 37.9 billion.

On orders, new orders are estimated to recover from KRW 184.9 billion in 2025 to about KRW 274.1 billion in 2026, and a Canadian LNG contract has been signed. Still, the lag between orders and revenue, production bottlenecks, and tariff and currency variables remain.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results and whether operating margin and revenue recover in the FY2026 annual report.
