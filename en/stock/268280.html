02

Business structure

Miwon Specialty Chemical was spun off from Miwon Holdings in 2017 and later listed on the KOSPI. The company produces monomers, oligomers and related polymers, core materials for energy curing, and supplies them worldwide. These materials are widely used in coatings, inks, adhesives and electronic materials.

As of Q2 2026 sales, energy-curable resins and other products accounted for 99% of revenue, with services at 1%, so the business is concentrated in a single material family. Domestic market share was 50% and overseas market share was 15% as of September 2025.

The company describes itself as having the longest history among domestic chemical manufacturers and operates production and sales subsidiaries in the United States and Germany, along with research facilities. Exports made up 82.7% of consolidated sales in December 2025, against 17.3% domestic.

The only consolidated subsidiary is Miwon Specialty Chemical USA, Inc., established in October 2018. R&D spending in 2025 was KRW 6.08 billion, or 1.14% of sales.

Competition overlaps with larger listed peers such as LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions, SKC and Kumho Petro Chemical, but those groups have broader portfolios, so scale and structure are not directly comparable with a company centered on one product family.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 611.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 437.7 billion in 2023, a 28.4% decline, then rose 16.4% to KRW 509.3 billion in 2024 and 4.5% to KRW 532.1 billion in 2025. Revenue increased for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025, following the 2023 low.

The largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period was 2022, and the smallest was 2023. Operating profit went from KRW 80.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 22.5 billion in 2023, a 72.1% decline, then rose 160.9% to KRW 58.8 billion in 2024 and 11.4% to KRW 65.5 billion in 2025, increasing for two consecutive years from the 2023 low.

The operating margin was 13.2% in 2022, 5.1% in 2023, 11.5% in 2024 and 12.3% in 2025. In the 2023 downturn, the consolidated cost of sales ratio rose from 78.44% to 87.64%, adding cost pressure; it then eased to 79.77% in 2024 and 78.76% in 2025 as margins recovered.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 64.8 billion in 2022, KRW 18.6 billion in 2023 (down 71.3%), KRW 54.6 billion in 2024 (up 193.3%) and KRW 61.3 billion in 2025 (up 12.3%). Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same yearly values.

Operating cash flow was KRW 39.4 billion in 2022, KRW 109.2 billion in 2023, KRW 54 billion in 2024 and KRW 76.5 billion in 2025, so cash generation was strongest in 2023, the year of the profit trough.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 133.3 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 126.1 billion in Q4 2025 (down 5.4% from the prior quarter), KRW 141.3 billion in Q1 2026 (up 12.0%), KRW 162.8 billion in Q2 2026 (up 15.3%) and KRW 161.9 billion in Q3 2026 (down 0.6% from the prior quarter, up 21.4% year on year).

Operating profit was KRW 18.2 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 10.1 billion in Q4 2025 (down 44.5%), KRW 19.3 billion in Q1 2026 (up 90.9%), KRW 37.1 billion in Q2 2026 (up 92.5%) and KRW 16.7 billion in Q3 2026 (down 55.0% from the prior quarter, down 8.2% year on year).

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 17.8 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 15.2 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 21.5 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 33 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 96.09 million in Q3 2026, and the four quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 total KRW 69.8 billion.

Q3 2026 operating profit was less than half of Q2 2026, and the gap between operating profit and controlling-shareholder net profit widened; the drivers, such as non-operating items and income tax, need to be confirmed from disclosure line items.

Industry analysis

Energy-curable resins, also called UV and EB curable resins, harden within a short time and serve as process materials across coatings, inks, adhesives and electronic materials. Domestic market share was 50% as of September 2025, so the company holds a large share of domestic demand.

Overseas market share is 15%, leaving room for expansion abroad. With exports at 82.7% of consolidated sales, overseas demand and exchange rates have a direct effect on revenue and margins.

Industry summaries from around 2025 cited rising raw material prices and supply chain issues as challenges across the sector. 2023 was the year when revenue fell 28.4% and the cost of sales ratio rose to 87.64%, making it the period of largest earnings swings.

In 2024 and 2025 the cost ratio returned to the 78% range and margins recovered. On competition, LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions, SKC and Kumho Petro Chemical are large chemical groups with broader portfolios, so their market position is hard to compare directly with a company focused on one product family.

The company emphasizes its long domestic history and its network of overseas subsidiaries as sources of customer reach. Revenue held in the KRW 160 billion range in Q2 and Q3 2026, which points to demand stability, while operating margins moved sharply between quarters.

Outlook

The amendment added real estate leasing, holding-company business through ownership of subsidiary shares, joint product development and sales with subsidiaries, and administrative support services to the business purpose.

The same meeting approved a cash dividend, and the financial statements, both consolidated and standalone, received a clean audit opinion. Consolidated total assets for 2025 were reported at KRW 549.2 billion and total equity at KRW 448.5 billion.

R&D spending rose from KRW 4.85 billion in 2024, or 0.95% of sales, to KRW 6.08 billion in 2025, or 1.14% of sales. Overseas, the company describes using its production and sales subsidiaries and research facilities in the United States and Germany to widen customer reach.

Risk factors

Concentration risk

Energy-curable resins and related products account for 99% of revenue. If demand across coatings, inks, adhesives and electronic materials moves together, results could swing sharply at once. Whether the portfolio broadens will show up in future disclosures of business mix.

Export and currency exposure

With exports at 82.7% of sales, currency movements affect translated revenue and profitability. Overseas bases in the United States and Germany also expose the company to local demand and pricing competition. If raw material prices rise at the same time, margins could come under pressure.

Governance and float

Miwon Holdings and related parties held 73.58% as of March 23, 2026. With a limited free float, the range of share supply and demand may be narrow.

The March 2026 amendment added holding-company and real estate leasing activities to the business purpose, so any shift in strategy needs to be confirmed through follow-up filings.
