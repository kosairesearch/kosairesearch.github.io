02

Business structure

Rznomics develops RNA-based gene therapies, and its core technology is an RNA trans-splicing platform. The technology is described as removing disease-causing RNA and expressing RNA that carries a therapeutic payload.

Existing commercial RNA technologies such as siRNA and ASO mainly suppress disease gene expression, whereas RNA editing is distinguished by directly replacing it with a therapeutic sequence.

The lead internal program is RZ-001, an anticancer gene therapy that targets telomerase mRNA expressed in more than 80% of cancer types.

RZ-001 has been given in combination with the immuno-oncology drugs atezolizumab and bevacizumab to patients with liver cancer who had no prior systemic therapy, and a Phase 1/2a study in recurrent glioblastoma is also under way.

RZ-003, an Alzheimer's candidate based on APOE4 gene editing, is preclinical and has a material transfer agreement with a global big pharma. RZ-004, for inherited retinitis pigmentosa, is being prepared for clinical development in Australia.

Circular RNA is the platform extension axis, and in March 2026 a joint academic study on delivering circular RNA by intravenous injection alone was announced.

The model has the company handle early candidate research and early clinical stages, while late-stage development and commercialization go to large global pharma partners. The clearest example is the May 2025 agreement with Eli Lilly. The business is best viewed through pipeline stage and partnerships.

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed figures, revenue was KRW 144 million in 2023 and KRW 7.92 billion in 2025, with no revenue data for 2022 and 2024. Operating loss narrowed from KRW 19.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.5 billion in 2023 and KRW 12.9 billion in 2024, then widened to KRW 14.6 billion in 2025.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 31.5 billion in 2022, KRW 13.6 billion in 2023, KRW 18.9 billion in 2024, and KRW 103.8 billion in 2025, when the loss widened sharply from the prior year.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was also KRW 103.8 billion in 2025, so the total loss and the controlling-shareholder loss matched. The disclosures show losses in each year from 2022 through 2025, four consecutive years.

The operating margin improved from -10,808.7% in 2023 to -184.3% in 2025, but this reflects a larger revenue base rather than a sharp cut in losses.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 14.5 billion in 2022, -KRW 14.4 billion in 2023, -KRW 11.2 billion in 2024, and -KRW 10.9 billion in 2025, so cash outflow stayed above KRW 10 billion a year.

At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 58.9 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, with total liabilities of KRW 3.96 billion and a debt ratio of 6.7%.

Debt ratios shown as negative for 2022 through 2024 reflect periods of negative equity, and the year-end 2025 equity level coincided with the KOSDAQ listing in December 2025.

Quarterly revenue had no data for the third quarter of 2025, then KRW 830 million in the fourth quarter, KRW 366 million in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 808 million in the second quarter, and KRW 449 million in the third quarter, so quarter-to-quarter swings are large.

Operating loss went from KRW 8.08 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 5.44 billion in the fourth quarter, then KRW 8.06 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 6.85 billion in the second quarter, and KRW 6.91 billion in the third quarter. The third quarter of 2026 loss was smaller than a year earlier.

The disclosures show five consecutive quarters of losses from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 25.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The RNA therapeutics market has seen siRNA and ASO commercialized first to suppress disease gene expression, and RNA editing is viewed as the next-generation step.

On March 9, 2026, NH Investment & Securities named Rznomics a top small-to-mid cap KOSDAQ biotech pick for the year, citing its competitiveness among the global leaders in next-generation RNA editing.

The same brokerage pointed to the possibility of technology transfer for an RNA-based Alzheimer's therapy and validation of the platform technology as key investment points.

In vivo CAR-T is an area where large global pharma companies are investing heavily, and circular RNA approaches are cited as a basis for expansion into it.

Smallinsight Research, an independent research house, analyzed that first human data for an RNA correction anticancer therapy could support a platform-level revaluation, citing earlier approaches such as CAR-T and CRISPR that received a platform premium after first-in-human proof of concept.

In peer terms, OliX Pharmaceuticals relies on siRNA to suppress target gene expression, so even within RNA therapeutics the mode of action differs between suppression and replacement.

A foreign RNA editing candidate's share price surged after it released first efficacy data, which some see as evidence of high sector expectations.

The competitive yardstick is moving from mode of action to clinical and manufacturing capability, so clinical data and production capacity are likely to be the differentiators.

Regulatory benefits tied to orphan drug designation, including priority review, tax relief, waived approval fees, and up to seven years of market exclusivity, shape the development environment for RZ-001.

Bull factors

A September 2026 report said talks on indication expansion and new licensing were under way with Lilly. Additional agreements would build a record of platform-level deals.

Early clinical data for RZ-001

RZ-001 holds FDA orphan drug and fast track designations. The June 2026 ASNO 2026 update reported 20 patients screened, 10 treated, and no new safety concern identified. The company said some patients showed tumor recurrence control lasting more than six months.

Pipeline breadth

RZ-003 for Alzheimer's has a material transfer agreement with a global big pharma and is planned for licensing at the preclinical stage. RZ-004 for retinal disease is being prepared for clinical work in Australia.

Circular RNA-based in vivo CAR-T is an area attracting large pharma investment, and expansion potential is cited.

Risk factors

Clinical risk

RZ-001 data are interim results centered on a small number of patients in liver cancer and glioblastoma. Response rates and safety could change in later enrollment and long-term follow-up. Platform value depends heavily on the outcome of human validation, so interim results leave wide room for interpretation.

Funding and dilution risk

Operating cash outflow was KRW 10.9 billion in 2025, against year-end 2025 total equity of KRW 58.9 billion. If losses continue and equity shrinks, additional funding may be needed. The 100% bonus share issue decided in June 2026 has already increased the number of shares.

Licensing uncertainty

Apart from the Eli Lilly agreement, few platform or pipeline deals have been finalized. If negotiations stall or terms come in lower, expectations for company value could be reset. Negotiation progress can only be tracked through disclosures and media reports.

Overall view

Rznomics is built on an RNA trans-splicing platform and holds clinical pipeline assets including RZ-001, along with licensing experience with Eli Lilly.

The disclosed results show small revenue, a wider loss at the operating and controlling-shareholder levels in 2025, four consecutive years of losses from 2022 to 2025, and five consecutive quarters of losses.

The controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters totaled KRW 25.9 billion, and operating cash outflow has exceeded KRW 10 billion each year. Positive factors include the Eli Lilly agreement, human data for RZ-001, and pipeline breadth across RZ-003 and RZ-004.

Negative factors include interim results covering 10 treated patients, accumulated losses and cash outflow, a larger share count after the bonus issue, and a large premium to net assets on P/B. These factors carry comparable weight, and the current data do not settle which side prevails.

The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, and further Eli Lilly agreements and the RZ-003 licensing outcome will show the direction of platform value around that time.
