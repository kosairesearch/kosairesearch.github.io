02

Business structure

Revu is an influencer marketing company founded in 2014. It operates the Revu, REVU Select and REVU Global matching platforms, with a pool of 1.58 million members and 78,000 cumulative client companies.

As of 2023, the revenue mix was Revu service 71.7%, REVU Select 1.9%, its China-focused subsidiary 23.0%, and other 3.4%. That subsidiary's 51% stake was sold for a total of about KRW 2 billion. In August 2024 Revu acquired 54% of short-form agency Shortt for about KRW 8.1 billion.

Shortt posted first-half 2025 revenue of KRW 5.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.3 billion. Core Revu clients are mainly small business advertisers. Global Shortt revenue comes from cosmetics and food clients across TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

The U.S. accounts for more than 40% of global business, according to a Chosun Biz report, which also cited a 66% domestic share. Competition overlaps between influencer platforms and short-form agencies.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue was KRW 40.3 billion in 2022, KRW 51.6 billion in 2023 (up 28.1%), KRW 48.9 billion in 2024 (down 5.2%) and KRW 56.5 billion in 2025 (up 15.4%).

Operating profit fell from KRW 4.55 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.79 billion in 2023 (down 38.7%), then rose to KRW 7.47 billion in 2024 (up 168.2%) and KRW 8.88 billion in 2025 (up 18.8%), growing two years in a row from 2024. Operating margin was 11.3%, 5.4%, 15.3% and 15.7% from 2022 to 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.95 billion, KRW 4.26 billion, KRW 6.51 billion and KRW 5.32 billion, while net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 6.41 billion in 2025.

Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 51.6 billion, of which KRW 48.3 billion attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 37.8 billion. The debt ratio went from 45.2% in 2023 to 77.9% in 2024 and 73.2% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 8.39 billion, KRW 7.67 billion, KRW 13.3 billion and KRW 12.7 billion from 2022 to 2025. Quarterly, 3Q 2025 operating profit was KRW 2.92 billion, 4Q 2025 KRW 2.48 billion, 1Q 2026 KRW 1.28 billion, 2Q 2026 KRW 1.99 billion, and 3Q 2026 KRW 1.48 billion. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 4.56 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Influencer and creator marketing is tied to advertising budgets shifting toward content-led media. Shortt's acquisition is cited as a way to secure top-tier clients in a growing short-form ad market. A 2024 brokerage note described the influencer marketing market as expanding at home and abroad.

Advertising agencies such as Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide run full-service models, while HYBE, SM Entertainment and JYP Ent. are content-production led, so Revu's business model differs from both. Revu functions more as a matching platform linking advertisers with creators.

The 66% domestic share is a figure from a Chosun Biz report in 3Q 2025. Ad-market cycles directly affect the budgets of small business advertisers, and short-form exposure depends on platform algorithms and policy.

Revu's strengths are platform share and short-form capability, while its U.S. expansion remains at an early stage.

06

Outlook

The next scheduled audited results are in the 2026 annual report. A February 2026 brokerage note said Revu and Shortt are growing together and that earnings growth continues. The same note said the largest shareholder is expected to change to Cadence Capital on March 24 upon closing.

Growth plans could be expanded after the strategy is reorganized under the new owner. Global expansion centers on the U.S. and Europe with K-beauty and K-entertainment content. Because Shortt's global revenue relies on cosmetics and food clients, client expansion is a checkpoint. Whether operating margin holds near 15.7% in the next annual report is the key item.

Bull factors

Short-form acquisition widens the business

Revu bought 54% of Shortt in August 2024, adding short-form production and media buying. Shortt posted first-half 2025 revenue of KRW 5.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.3 billion. Global channels span TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

Domestic platform position and member base

Per a Chosun Biz report, Revu holds a 66% domestic share with a 1.58 million member pool. A February 2026 brokerage note pointed to joint growth of Revu and Shortt. Data network effects are cited as a strength.

Financial capacity and cash generation

Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 51.6 billion with a 73.2% debt ratio. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 8.39 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 12.7 billion in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 4.56 billion.

09

Bear factors

Large swings in quarterly profit

Operating profit was KRW 1.28 billion in 1Q 2026, down 48.2% from the prior quarter, and KRW 1.48 billion in 3Q 2026, down 49.1% year on year. The 2Q rebound to KRW 1.99 billion did not carry into 3Q. Quarterly profit has not settled at a stable level.

Flat revenue and a narrower group perimeter

3Q 2026 revenue fell 0.9% year on year, and 2024 revenue fell 5.2% to KRW 48.9 billion. The China-focused subsidiary accounted for about 22.95% of 2023 revenue, so its sale changed the revenue base.

Lower profit attributable to controlling shareholders

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 18.3% to KRW 5.32 billion in 2025, and 4Q 2025 fell 67.4% from the prior quarter to KRW 759 million. 3Q 2026 was down 38.5% year on year, so the profit recovery is not yet clear.

10

Risk factors

Ownership change

A February 2026 brokerage note said the largest shareholder is expected to change to Cadence Capital on closing. Business direction and investment plans under the new owner will need confirming as they are disclosed. Whether the ownership change leads to a strategy reset remains open.

Client concentration in global business

Shortt's global revenue depends on cosmetics and food clients. If advertising from these sectors slows or major clients reduce contracts, growth in global revenue could be limited. Whether the client base broadens beyond cosmetics and food will determine the stability of earnings. Changes in the client mix should be tracked as they emerge.

Reliance on external platforms

Global revenue relies on external platforms such as TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. Changes in platform algorithms or policy could affect Shortt's creator supply and ad delivery. With the U.S. above 40% of global business, U.S. regulation and economic conditions are also exposures.

Overall view

Revu's audited results show revenue and profit both expanding since 2022. Revenue was KRW 56.5 billion in 2025 and operating profit KRW 8.88 billion, the largest year of 2022–2025, with a 15.7% operating margin.

However, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 5.32 billion in 2025, and 3Q 2026 operating profit fell 49.1% year on year. The Shortt acquisition added a short-form business, and the domestic platform share is 66% per a Chosun Biz report.

Against that, the subsidiary sale and flat 3Q 2026 revenue raise questions about sustained top-line growth. The ownership change and strategy reset are medium-term variables. The next check is the 2026 annual report, and whether operating margin holds near 15.7% is the key item.
