02

Business structure

Bucket Studio was founded in 1999 and listed on KOSDAQ in December 2003. The company describes its main business purposes as online content provision, including films, and mobile services such as bulk text messaging.

Its 1Q 2025 quarterly report shows the Digital Biz segment's text messaging services at 44.0% of sales, the largest share, followed by kiosk leasing revenue at 38.3%.

Content distribution accounted for 5.1% through standard video packages and 3.9% through online sales, while theater operations, covering film screening and concession goods, came to 4.4%. F&B, which covers food and beverages, made up 4.0%. The theater revenue is recorded under the Segye Megabox brand.

FnGuide's tally put domestic sales at 99.5% for 2024. Entertainment peers such as HYBE, SM Entertainment and CJ ENM center their revenue on artists or content IP, whereas Bucket Studio's revenue rests mainly on services and distribution.

Apart from the business mix, its shareholder structure and its position at the top of the ownership chain linked to the crypto exchange Bithumb have been cited as major variables in valuing the company.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 44.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 18.9 billion in 2023 (down 57.7% year on year), KRW 17.5 billion in 2024 (down 7.6%) and KRW 17.3 billion in 2025 (down 1.1%).

Revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating losses were KRW 7.32 billion in 2022, KRW 6.52 billion in 2023, KRW 8.26 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.18 billion in 2025, which is four consecutive years of losses from 2022 to 2025. In 2024 the operating loss widened while revenue fell, and in 2025 it narrowed again.

Operating margins were -16.4% in 2022, -34.5% in 2023, -47.3% in 2024 and -35.8% in 2025. The 2022 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, KRW 188.7 billion, was far larger than the operating loss, which points to a large non-operating drag.

The company swung to a profit of KRW 3.82 billion attributable to controlling shareholders in 2023, returned to a loss of KRW 1.81 billion in 2024, and the loss widened to KRW 38.3 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts as the controlling-shareholder figures in each year. By quarter, the operating loss widened to KRW 1.93 billion in Q4 2025 before narrowing to KRW 1.08 billion in Q3 2026.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, however, rose from KRW 1.82 billion in Q2 2026 to KRW 5.61 billion in Q3 2026, which signals that non-operating items moved sharply. That Q3 2026 loss was still smaller than the year-earlier quarter.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders for the most recent four quarters totals KRW 46.8 billion. Quarterly revenue rose to KRW 5.24 billion in Q2 2026, up 17.4% from the prior quarter, then fell to KRW 4.05 billion in Q3 2026, down 22.7% from the prior quarter and down 20.6% from a year earlier.

Operating cash flow was negative in every year from 2022 to 2025, at KRW -15.5 billion, KRW -7.31 billion, KRW -5.63 billion and KRW -6.29 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Bucket Studio spans three markets with different characteristics. Text messaging services generate revenue that moves with corporate clients' messaging demand, and this segment accounted for 44.0% of sales in 1Q 2025.

Kiosk leasing depends on the installation and leasing volume of self-ordering equipment and made up 38.3% in the same period. Video content distribution is sensitive to domestic demand and content lineups, and the company's standard video packages and online sales together stayed near 9%.

Theater operations earn screening and concession revenue from cinemas under the Segye Megabox brand and account for about 4.4%. Annual revenue has moved within the KRW 17 billion to KRW 19 billion range since 2023, which looks more like contraction followed by a plateau than growth.

Compared with HYBE or SM Entertainment, whose revenue rests on artist IP, music and live events, the company's model is different. Against CJ CGV, the company's theater exposure is small, so cinema industry conditions have limited influence.

Text messaging and kiosk leasing are both enterprise-facing services and share exposure to economic cycles and client spending. Trading has been suspended since March 2023, which limits the availability of market-based supply and demand indicators.

06

Outlook

A share purchase agreement involving a change of the largest shareholder was disclosed on December 26, 2025, with a total purchase price reported at about KRW 240 billion.

The buyer paid a KRW 24 billion deposit but did not pay a KRW 50 billion interim payment in April 2026, and the company notified a breach of contract and demanded a cure. When no cure followed, the company disclosed on April 20, 2026 that it had exercised its right to cancel the contract.

A company official said a second open sale could come at a lower valuation but that completing a sale matters more.

In June 2026 the company was designated an unfaithful disclosure company, with 8.5 penalty points and a KRW 34 million fine, and its cumulative penalty points over the past year reached 16, triggering a listing-qualification review.

At the March 31, 2026 general shareholders meeting, the financial statements and charter amendments passed, but the election of most directors and the auditor was rejected, leaving a three-member board with one outside director.

A July 30, 2026 report said NMSI, a US mortgage lender, was selected as the new preferred bidder and will proceed with due diligence and other follow-up steps. Industry observers reportedly view a failed sale this time as making listing maintenance practically difficult.

The next confirmation points are whether the change-of-control agreement is signed, the outcome of due diligence, and the exchange's listing decision.

Risk factors

Ownership sale and financing

A KRW 50 billion interim payment went unpaid in April 2026, triggering a breach notice, and the first sale ultimately collapsed after the contract was cancelled. The terms and financing structure of the second bidder, NMSI, must be confirmed through later disclosures.

With a total price of about KRW 240 billion, any renewed delay in financing could push back a solution to the ownership problem.

Listing qualification and disclosure rules

The unfaithful disclosure designation carried 8.5 penalty points and a KRW 34 million fine, bringing cumulative points to 16 over the past year. A disclaimer of opinion on the 2023 financial statements has also created a delisting trigger in the past.

Internal-control concerns and regulatory sanctions surrounding the affiliated crypto exchange Bithumb are being cited as reasons the deal valuation may be reassessed.

Concentration and flat top line

Content and theater segments are small, so a shortfall in either core line would weigh heavily on total sales. Quarterly swings are also large, with Q3 2026 revenue falling 22.7% from the prior quarter.
