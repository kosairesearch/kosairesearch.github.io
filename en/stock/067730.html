Logisys: Three Years of Growth, Then Net Income Slows in 2026

Logisys recorded three straight years of growth in revenue, operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders over 2023–2025. In 2026, revenue has risen for two straight quarters, while attributable net income has fallen for two straight quarters.

  1. 1

    On a DART-filed basis, 2025 revenue was KRW 48.5 billion and operating profit was KRW 2.88 billion, with the operating margin rising to 5.9% from 0.4% in 2022.

  2. 2

    Attributable net income for the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 478 million, down 33.7% year on year, while operating profit for the same quarter was KRW 879 million, up 2.2%.

  3. 3

    Total equity stood at KRW 21 billion at the end of 2025, and the debt ratio fell to 42.1% from 54.7% in 2024.

  4. 4

    A KRW 500 million treasury share trust contract disclosed on November 9, 2026 runs until May 10, 2027.

  5. 5

    Fourth-quarter 2026 results have not yet been filed in a periodic report, and the next periodic report is the 2026 annual report.

02

Business structure

Logisys was founded in 1996 as a spin-off from the technology division of a larger group, and it provides IT maintenance and ATM agency management for financial institutions. Its business rests on two pillars: integrated maintenance of IT equipment for financial and public institutions, and VAN agency services.

The equipment maintenance unit relies on an integrated maintenance system developed in 2006 and a call center to track failure history and respond quickly. The VAN unit handles agency management for automated teller machines (ATMs) and agency services for cash logistics. Segment revenue mix differs across sources.

Alphasquare's snapshot shows IT equipment maintenance services at 56.20%, VAN agency services at 42.30% and PC and peripheral supply at 1.50%. Butler instead reports IT equipment maintenance at 100% based on second-quarter 2026 revenue, reflecting a different basis and classification.

Customers are centered on financial institutions, the company has expanded into the public sector, and it has partnerships with large SI companies. The company's cost competitiveness is attributed to a self-sourced order structure built through integrated maintenance work.

Its 20-plus years of operating know-how and nationwide service network are cited as advantages in the bulk outsourcing market for financial institutions. New initiatives include unmanned security services.

On an annual basis, Alphasquare ranks Logisys 97th by market capitalization among 100 companies in the IT services industry, a wide scale gap with larger listed peers in the same sector.

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed basis, revenue rose from KRW 32.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 36.5 billion in 2023 (up 11.1% year on year), KRW 42.9 billion in 2024 (up 17.6%) and KRW 48.5 billion in 2025 (up 13.3%). Revenue has grown for three straight years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 is the largest year of the 2022–2025 period.

Operating profit went from KRW 137 million in 2022 to KRW 545 million in 2023 (up 298.8%), KRW 2.39 billion in 2024 (up 338.4%) and KRW 2.88 billion in 2025 (up 20.6%). The operating margin rose to 1.5%, 5.6% and 5.9% in successive years from 0.4%, so profitability improved faster than sales.

In 2024, operating profit grew far faster than revenue, which points to a sharp improvement in the cost structure. One company earnings commentary attributes the gross profit improvement to cost competitiveness built through integrated maintenance work.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 204 million in 2022, KRW 516 million in 2023, KRW 2.41 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.3 billion in 2025. In 2025, net income exceeded operating profit, meaning non-operating items added to earnings, and the breakdown is a point to check in the annual report notes.

Operating cash flow moved from -KRW 281 million in 2022 to KRW 2.87 billion in 2023, KRW 5.19 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.86 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue fell from KRW 12.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 11.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then rose to KRW 12.1 billion and KRW 12.6 billion in the second and third quarters, two straight quarterly gains.

Operating profit dropped to KRW 103 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, recovered to KRW 1.06 billion in the first quarter of 2026, and then came in at KRW 784 million and KRW 879 million.

Attributable net income peaked at KRW 1.04 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then fell to KRW 1.01 billion and KRW 478 million, two straight quarterly declines. In the third quarter of 2026, operating profit rose 12.1% from the prior quarter while attributable net income fell 52.5%. The sum of attributable net income over the last four quarters is KRW 3.45 billion.

Industry analysis

Logisys's end market is equipment and automated machine maintenance for financial and public institutions. As the bulk outsourcing market for financial institutions expands, VAN opportunities are growing, and the scope is widening into ATM agency management and bulk outsourcing of automated machines.

A company profile describes IT equipment investment as recovering, and new initiatives such as unmanned security are under way.

On the other side, as the maintenance market shifts toward integrated maintenance services, demand for security problem resolution and lower management costs has grown, yet the same record notes that total revenue declined.

Separate-basis revenue in the first quarter of 2026 fell year on year and operating profit dipped slightly, while net income rose, according to a financial data site.

Revenue has risen for two straight quarters since its first-quarter 2026 low, and whether this continues after the fourth quarter of 2026 is the starting point for checking the industry trend. In competitive terms, partnerships with large SI companies serve as an order channel.

On an annual basis, Alphasquare ranks the company 97th by market capitalization among 100 IT services companies, placing it among the smaller players in its industry.

Outlook

The most concrete scheduled item is the treasury share trust contract. Logisys decided to enter into a KRW 500 million treasury share acquisition trust contract with Daishin Securities and disclosed it on November 9, 2026.

The contract runs from November 9, 2026 to May 10, 2027, and its stated purposes are price stabilization and employee performance compensation. The planned acquisition covers 253,807 common shares, with the reference price set at the closing price on the business day before the board resolution.

The disclosure states that actual quantity and price may vary with share price movements, and that the decision itself does not mean the acquisition is complete. The company's profile lists unmanned security services among its new initiatives.

The fourth quarter of 2026 has not yet been reported in a periodic filing, and the next periodic report is the 2026 annual report. That report will fix full-year 2026 revenue, operating profit and attributable net income.

It will also show how the gap between operating profit and attributable net income seen in the third quarter of 2026 is reflected on a full-year basis. Whether the third-quarter decline in attributable net income is temporary or persistent is a matter to check in the cost items of later quarterly reports.

Bull factors

Three years of growth and a higher operating margin

On a DART-filed basis, revenue, operating profit and attributable net income each rose for three straight years from 2023 through 2025. The operating margin increased from 0.4% in 2022 to 5.9% in 2025. Operating cash flow stayed positive over the same period, reaching KRW 4.86 billion in 2025. Earnings strength improved quickly relative to the company's size, which is the core bullish point.

A steadier balance sheet

Total equity was KRW 21 billion at the end of 2025, and the debt ratio fell to 42.1% from 54.7% in 2024. Total liabilities were KRW 8.85 billion, so the debt burden relative to equity has eased. Shareholder return measures such as the treasury share trust are being carried out on this base.

Bulk outsourcing demand and the 2026 revenue recovery

Bulk outsourcing demand in the financial sector is expanding, creating more VAN opportunities, and the business is widening into ATM agency management and automated machine bulk outsourcing. Revenue rose in the second and third quarters of 2026, two straight quarterly gains. New initiatives such as unmanned security are also cited as growth options.

Bear factors

Net income slowdown in 2026

Pressure on service revenue

A company earnings record notes that total revenue declined as the maintenance market shifted toward integrated maintenance services. Separate-basis revenue in the first quarter of 2026 fell year on year.

Gross profit improved on cost competitiveness, but if top-line growth slows, the pace of profit expansion could be constrained.

Limits of scale and partner dependence

On an annual basis, Alphasquare ranks the company 97th by market capitalization among 100 IT services companies, indicating a small scale. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 12.6 billion, up only 0.6% year on year.

Because partnerships with large SI companies form an order channel, contract terms and the degree of business independence are factors to check.

Risk factors

Quarterly earnings volatility

Operating profit fell to KRW 103 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 and recovered to KRW 1.06 billion in the first quarter of 2026, so quarterly swings are large.

Attributable net income and operating profit have moved in different directions from quarter to quarter, making it hard to judge the trend from a single quarter. Concentration in financial clients and business lines

Revenue is concentrated in IT equipment maintenance and VAN agency services for financial and public institutions. Changes in financial institutions' IT budgets or automated machine operating policies could affect orders and service pricing.

The earnings contribution of the new unmanned security business is difficult to isolate from the reported figures.

Small-cap liquidity and share price volatility

Logisys is a small-cap stock with a small market capitalization, and its free float ratio is reported at about 44.99%. Small caps can see wide share price swings depending on trading volume.
