02

Business structure

Amogreentech was founded in 2004 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2019 as a advanced materials and components company.

Its business spans an advanced materials segment, a functional components segment including flexible batteries, and an energy and environment systems segment covering ESS and AMB modules for SiC power semiconductors as well as water treatment filters.

According to SK Securities' April 2026 report, product revenue shares in 2025 were 35.5% for ESS finished systems, 35.0% for magnetic materials, 22.3% for nano-membranes, and 7.2% for others.

High-efficiency magnetic materials are components that reduce energy loss during power conversion, and the company says it was the first in Korea and the third in the world to mass-produce them.

ESS finished products are LFP battery-based systems with proprietary BMS technology, mainly small standby-power ESS for telecom, renewable energy and electric vehicle applications. Nano-membranes are nanofiber-based vent waterproofing parts, and reports cite smartphone customers as the main revenue source.

Key customers include Tesla, Rivian, Samsung Electronics, SoftBank and American Tower. According to the company, high-efficiency magnetic materials and ESS together generated KRW 50.5 billion in first-half 2026 revenue, or 75.9% of the total.

Affiliates include listed companies Amotech and Amosense, with eight companies in the group in total. The largest shareholder, Kim Byung-kyu and nine related parties, held 45.98% as of August 31, 2026.

Because its product mix differs from peers in electronic components such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek, direct comparisons of earnings and valuation are limited.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 142.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 147.6 billion in 2023, up 3.8%, making 2023 the largest year of 2022 to 2025. It fell to KRW 128.6 billion in 2024, down 12.9%, the smallest year in the period, and recovered slightly to KRW 130.6 billion in 2025, up 1.6%.

Operating profit moved from KRW 12.3 billion in 2023, up 39.1%, to KRW 4.47 billion in 2024, down 63.6%, before rising to KRW 9.38 billion in 2025, up 109.9%. Operating margin was 6.2% in 2022, 8.3% in 2023, 3.5% in 2024 and 7.2% in 2025, with 2024 the lowest and 2025 back above 7%.

Profit attributable to shareholders went from KRW 13 billion in 2023 to KRW 2.12 billion in 2024, down 83.7%, and then recovered to KRW 5.9 billion in 2025, up 178.1%. Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts as shareholder net income in each year.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 156.1% in 2022 to 133.9% in 2025, and year-end 2025 total equity of KRW 81.1 billion was entirely attributable to shareholders. Operating cash flow went from KRW 8.63 billion in 2024 to KRW 21.7 billion in 2025.

During the 2025 recovery, ESS finished products were the largest product category at 35.5% of revenue. Quarterly, Q4 2025 was the low point with revenue of KRW 29.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.51 billion, and by Q3 2026 revenue had reached KRW 37.1 billion and operating profit KRW 2.98 billion.

In the same quarter, profit attributable to shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 1 billion, a move into the red from a year earlier. Net profit attributable to shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 2.24 billion, a small figure compared with KRW 13 billion for 2023 alone.

In first-half 2026, sales outside ESS declined, and a securities firm report cited a 5.2% drop in revenue and a 39.3% drop in operating profit for the half.

05

Industry analysis

End-market demand spans electric vehicles, ESS, robotics and LNG power generation.

In April 2026, SK Securities expected revenue growth to gather pace from the second half of 2026, citing a rebound in EV shipments from a value model launch, higher ESS shipments tied to AI data center demand, and a larger share at Tesla.

In the ESS market, the replacement of lead-acid batteries with LFP ESS was described as only just beginning. On the other hand, a Q1 2026 company summary noted that high-efficiency magnetic materials revenue fell on demand swings in electric vehicles and renewable energy.

ESS was described as safe but still affected by market conditions. In robotics, a securities report raised the possibility of entry into the Tesla Optimus charger value chain; this is the broker's view rather than a company announcement.

In LNG power generation, the company supplies metallic converters, which are exhaust gas purification parts, to a global gas turbine maker.

Within the same group, Amotech cited expanded supply of AI and automotive MLCCs as a driver of its third-quarter 2026 results, suggesting automotive and AI demand is a shared theme. The company says its high-efficiency magnetic materials were the first mass-produced in Korea and third in the world.

No public market share comparison against competitors was confirmed, so competitive positioning is best read through technology and customer mix. Companies such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek operate with different product mixes, which limits direct comparison.

06

Outlook

In its Q3 2026 announcement, the company attributed results to expanding high-efficiency magnetic component sales to global ESS and electric vehicle makers.

In April 2026, SK Securities said the company was reportedly selected as the main vendor for magnetic materials used in Tesla's Optimus charger, expecting supply to start in the second half of 2026 and initial revenue of roughly KRW 7 billion on a full-year basis in 2027.

The same report said Japan's BCP regulation, which extends telecom base station backup power from two hours to 72 hours, should lead to ESS supply to SoftBank and KDDI beginning in the second half of 2026.

It also pointed to possible supply expansion of metallic converters as LNG combined-cycle plants expand to meet AI data center power demand.

A Yuanta Securities report published in late September 2026 noted weak first-half results but expected revenue growth to resume in 2027 as ongoing projects and new demand coincide. The same report warned that if sales outside ESS do not recover, overall growth could remain limited.

Magnetic materials growth tied to expanded Tesla EV and ESS production was presented as a broker scenario, not a company-reported result. The company's announcements have focused on business direction, and no formal annual numerical target has been confirmed.

The next checkpoints are whether the Optimus charger and Japanese telecom ESS supply begin in the second half of 2026, and how the product revenue mix changes in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

ESS and magnetic materials as the core

ESS finished products were the largest revenue category in 2025 at 35.5%, and including ESS-bound magnetic materials, ESS-related revenue was estimated at about half of total sales. In first-half 2026, the two businesses together made up 75.9% of revenue.

If Japanese telecom ESS supply and standby-power replacement demand continue, this core could support overall sales.

Attempts to enter EV and robotics value chains

According to the company, Q3 2026 high-efficiency magnetic component revenue was KRW 15.7 billion, up 20.6% from the previous quarter. SK Securities said the company is reportedly the main magnetic materials vendor for the Optimus charger, citing a relationship with Tesla spanning about nine years. The same report projected supply starting in the second half of 2026 and initial revenue in 2027.

Operating profit recovery and balance sheet improvement

Operating profit reached KRW 9.38 billion in 2025, up 109.9%, and rose for three consecutive quarters from Q1 2026 through Q3 2026. Operating margin improved from 3.5% in 2024 to 7.2% in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 156.1% in 2022 to 133.9% in 2025, easing financial burden.

09

Bear factors

Weak first half 2026 outside ESS

Citing a Yuanta Securities report, press coverage said first-half 2026 revenue declined from a year earlier and operating profit fell by a larger margin. Most business units outside ESS saw revenue declines at the same time. The same analysis warned that simultaneous declines across several units could slow the overall recovery.

Q3 net income swung into the red

Q3 2026 operating profit rose 37.4% year on year to KRW 2.98 billion, but profit attributable to shareholders turned into a KRW 1 billion net loss. Because operating profit and net profit moved in opposite directions in the same quarter, the gap in the income structure needs to be checked in the annual report.

Net profit attributable to shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 2.24 billion, far below the KRW 13 billion recorded in 2023.

Sensitivity to swings in EV demand

The main customers for high-efficiency magnetic materials are reported to be EV makers such as Tesla and Rivian, so results are directly exposed to EV sales trends. A Q1 2026 company summary attributed a drop in magnetic materials results to demand swings in electric vehicles and renewable energy. If an EV shipment rebound is delayed, the timing of recovery in magnetic materials revenue could also slip.

10

Risk factors

Timing of new supply

Supply to the Optimus charger chain and Japanese telecom ESS are scheduled to begin in the second half of 2026 based on broker forecasts. If supply starts later, the 2027 revenue growth scenario could also be pushed back. Because these are forecasts rather than reported results, schedule risk needs to be considered separately.

Raw material price swings

A 2024 Daishin Securities report estimated that lithium-ion batteries account for 60% of ESS cost, and lithium for about 45% of LFP battery cost. The broker noted high sensitivity to lithium prices. With ESS finished products at 35.5% of 2025 revenue, raw material swings could affect margins.

Governance and shareholder structure

The largest shareholder, Kim Byung-kyu and nine related parties, held 45.98% as of August 31, 2026. Press reports said a proposal on the director compensation limit was voted down at the March 2026 annual shareholder meeting. The free float ratio is listed at 54.02%, so governance and share structure should be considered together.

Overall view

Amogreentech's full-year 2025 operating profit rose 109.9% to KRW 9.38 billion, moving away from the 2024 low, and revenue has risen for three straight quarters through Q3 2026. ESS and high-efficiency magnetic materials made up 75.9% of first-half 2026 revenue, narrowing the sales base to these two businesses.

However, Q3 2026 brought a KRW 1 billion net loss attributable to shareholders, a swing from a year earlier, so the operating profit gains did not carry through to net income. Sales outside ESS declined in the first half of 2026, leaving recovery uneven across business units.

Securities firms expect Optimus charger and Japanese telecom ESS supply to begin in the second half of 2026 and earnings to recover in 2027, but these are third-party estimates and should be read separately from reported results. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 156.1% in 2022 to 133.9% in 2025.

The largest shareholder holds 45.98%, leaving a free float ratio of 54.02%. The next checkpoints are the start timing of new supply, the income structure in the 2026 annual report, and the revenue trend in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
