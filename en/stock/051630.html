Narrower Losses Under a Investment Warning, Earnings Proof Is the Test

02

Business structure

Chinyang Chemical was established on January 3, 2001 through a spin-off of the synthetic resin business and listed on the KOSPI market on January 29, 2001. Its core products are PVC flooring and synthetic leather, and its product lineup also includes automotive fabrics and tarpaulin.

The synthetic leather is based on TPO film, and the company's annual report describes a calendar-based mass production method that allows quick changes in thickness and specifications, supporting small-lot, multi-variety production.

The flooring line carries KS quality certification and links to construction and interior demand. Customers include automotive material-related partners, an overseas affiliate, and building-material distributors, so automotive interior demand and construction demand both support sales.

Profitability depends on raw material prices and selling price adjustments, and construction and interior market conditions directly affect results. The largest shareholder is the holding company Chinyang Holdings, which the annual report lists at a 68.20% stake.

Chinyang Holdings, Chin Yang Industry and Chinyang Poly Urethane are separate listed companies, but the press has at times grouped them together as a political-theme trade.

Compared with large petrochemical groups such as LG Chem and Lotte Chemical, the company is a smaller, processing-focused business, so changes in raw materials and prices tend to show up in results quickly.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show a combination of a shrinking top line and a smaller operating loss.

Revenue fell from KRW 29.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 28.3 billion in 2023 (down 2.8% year on year), KRW 24.5 billion in 2024 (down 13.3%), and KRW 18.6 billion in 2025 (down 24.3%), a decline in each of the three years from 2023 to 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

The operating loss widened from KRW 1.06 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.15 billion in 2023 and KRW 4.14 billion in 2024, then narrowed to KRW 743 million in 2025. The operating margin deteriorated from -3.6% in 2022 to -7.6% in 2023 and -16.9% in 2024, before improving to -4.0% in 2025.

The shareholder-attributable net loss of KRW 4.49 billion in 2024 became a shareholder-attributable net income of KRW 896 million in 2025, and net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 896 million.

Because the company posted an operating loss while net income turned positive, the items that created this gap should be checked in the 2026 annual report.

Quarterly, the operating loss narrowed from KRW 312 million in Q3 2025 to KRW 152 million in Q4 2025, and turned into an operating profit of KRW 117 million in Q1 2026. Operating losses returned in Q2 2026 at KRW 190 million, and Q3 2026 posted a loss of KRW 24.18 million, smaller than a year earlier.

Revenue rose from KRW 3.45 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 4.6 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 5.62 billion in Q1 2026, then held at KRW 5.31 billion in Q2 and KRW 5.66 billion in Q3 2026.

Shareholder-attributable net income fell 96.6% to KRW 34.45 million in Q1 2026, followed by a shareholder-attributable net loss of KRW 246 million in Q2 and net income of KRW 4.81 million in Q3.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 28.3% in 2022 to 111.6% in 2024 and 107.1% in 2025, and operating cash flow was negative in every year from 2022 to 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The company's main demand comes from building interior materials and automotive interior materials. On the building side, PVC flooring is sensitive to construction and interior market conditions, while on the automotive side, synthetic leather and fabrics move with vehicle interior demand.

Chinyang Holdings noted in a corporate monitor comment on the first quarter of 2026 that its subsidiary Chinyang Chemical is expanding its PVC synthetic leather into automotive interiors on rising vegan leather demand and the use of bio-based materials.

A fnguide comment dated June 2024 also expected vegan synthetic leather demand in automotive materials to rise gradually as concern grows over animal leather. This trend is cited as a margin driver, but the quarters in which higher revenue translated into an operating profit have been limited to Q1 2026.

Raw materials are petrochemical-based, so price swings flow into product costs quickly, and margins narrow when selling price adjustments lag.

In the sector, large petrochemical groups such as LG Chem, Lotte Chemical, Hanwha Solutions and Kumho Petro Chemical operate from the feedstock stage, while Chinyang Chemical is a small to mid-sized processor that converts synthetic resins into materials.

Its scale is smaller than these groups, so its differentiation lies more in small-lot, multi-variety production and application-specific materials than in mass-production cost competitiveness.

A recovery in both construction and automotive production cycles could speed up revenue recovery, but the company-level operating indicators that would confirm this remain limited in the disclosed results.

06

Outlook

The company has not disclosed annual earnings targets within the scope of this report. Facts visible in recent quarters are that revenue has held in a range of roughly KRW 5.3 billion to KRW 5.7 billion per quarter since Q1 2026.

Q3 2026 revenue rose 63.7% from a year earlier and the operating loss narrowed from the prior year, but the quarter still recorded an operating loss. The Q1 2026 operating profit lasted only one quarter, and the return to an operating loss in Q2 reversed that trend.

On the synthetic leather side, the annual report presents TPO film-based calendar production as an advantage for small-lot, multi-variety orders. Whether demand for vegan materials in automotive interiors turns into actual orders is a key variable for sustained revenue.

On the building side, construction and interior conditions and raw material prices continue to determine flooring profitability. On governance, the high stake held by the largest shareholder, Chinyang Holdings, lowers the free float, which links to share-price volatility.

The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, due within 90 days of fiscal year-end, around late March 2027, which will finalize Q4 2026 results, full-year operating margin and operating cash flow. After that, the Q1 2027 quarterly report will show whether quarterly operating losses continue after Q2 2026.

Growing demand for vegan synthetic leather

A Chinyang Holdings corporate monitor comment on Q1 2026 cited rising vegan leather demand and the use of bio-based materials as the reasons its subsidiary's PVC synthetic leather is widening its reach into automotive interiors.

A fnguide comment also expected vegan leather demand in automotive materials to rise gradually. Whether this turns into actual orders and margins will be visible in upcoming periodic reports.

Bear factors

Financial burden and cash generation

Investment Warning and theme-driven volatility

The Korea Exchange designated Chinyang Chemical an Investment Warning issue from November 5, 2026, which requires a 100% margin deposit and bars margin purchases. A notice warned that further sharp gains could trigger a one-day trading halt.

Press reports have grouped the stock with political-theme shares, so supply-demand swings unrelated to operating results are possible.

Risk factors

Demand and raw materials

Revenue depends on construction, interior and automotive production, and petrochemical raw material price swings flow into product costs quickly. Delayed selling price adjustments could push the operating margin lower again. The operating losses in Q2 and Q3 2026 show how this structure has affected results.

Ownership structure and float

The annual report lists the largest shareholder, Chinyang Holdings, at a 68.20% stake. A fnguide figure as of September 2024 put the free-float ratio at 31.80%, so the tradable share count is limited.

This structure tends to amplify price moves when trading volume rises and makes the stock more sensitive to theme-driven flows.

Balance sheet and cash flow

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 of KRW 42.5 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 39.7 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose from 28.3% in 2022 to 107.1%. With operating cash flow negative every year, higher reliance on borrowing could increase funding pressure. The borrowings and cash flow items in the 2026 annual report will show this more clearly.

Overall view

Revenue declined for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, while the operating loss narrowed to KRW 743 million in 2025 and shareholder-attributable net income turned positive at KRW 896 million.

Quarterly revenue held in the mid-KRW 5 billion range in 2026, and Q3 2026 revenue rose 63.7% from a year earlier, but operating losses appeared in both Q2 and Q3. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 28.3% in 2022 to 107.1% in 2025, and operating cash flow was negative every year.
