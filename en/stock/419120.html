Report overview

Subscription font platform cutting production time with AI

Sandoll, a leading Korean font platform, is pairing subscription cash flow with an AI-driven production shift, and its profits have recovered from the 2023 low but still swing sharply from quarter to quarter.

  1. 1

    Revenue reached KRW 20 billion in 2025, the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span, and operating profit rose for two straight years from 2024 to 2025 to KRW 4.45 billion.

  2. 2

    Profit attributable to owners of the parent fell 31.9% to KRW 3.58 billion in 2025, and the fourth quarter of 2025 posted a KRW 302 million net loss even though operating profit was positive.

  3. 3

    Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 1.28 billion, up 281.0% from a year earlier, and profit attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters totals KRW 2.44 billion.

  4. 4

    According to the company, its in-house AI generates the remaining glyphs from about 2,500 input characters, and a July 2026 release said the first AI-produced mobile font had already launched.

  5. 5

    Separate-basis figures announced for the first half of 2026 show revenue of KRW 8 billion, up 10% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 3.2 billion, up 31%, though the basis differs from the consolidated filings.

02

Business structure

Sandoll is built around Korean font development and the operation of a subscription font platform, and it is described as the number one player in the Korean font market by share.

Its core asset is the cloud-based platform Sandoll Guurum, which was reported in February 2026 to have 2 million subscribers and 97 member companies. The company says its customer retention rate is 95%, and it explains that users of its fonts must keep subscribing to maintain the works they created.

In its custom font business, the company said in its third-quarter 2025 results that rising overseas project wins expanded global revenue. A price increase earlier in 2025 and a larger base of paying users were cited as drivers of revenue growth.

Its subsidiary Yoon Design co-developed products with Sandoll that contributed to sales, and Bibitree, which has focused on corporate clients since adopting AI, improved its profitability.

The investment portfolio includes a 20% stake in music content company Most Contents and a 19% stake in Beluga, an AI company for automatic webtoon translation.

On the competitive side, the growing supply of free fonts that can be used commercially is cited as a pressure point, and Sandoll positions itself on AI glyph generation and the value of its subscription platform.

LG CNS, Eastsoft and NC AI are among the AI-related companies that have joined or are preparing to join the platform. In August 2026 the company signed an MOU with content IP company Gemstone E&M to pursue fandom fonts built on artist IP.

04

Earnings analysis

On a consolidated basis, revenue fell from KRW 18.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 14.2 billion in 2023, a 22.7% decline, then rose to KRW 15.7 billion in 2024 (up 11.0%) and KRW 20 billion in 2025 (up 26.8%), making 2025 the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating profit dropped from KRW 8.06 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.78 billion in 2023, a 65.5% decline, and then rose to KRW 2.86 billion in 2024 (up 2.9%) and KRW 4.45 billion in 2025 (up 55.7%), marking two straight years of growth. 2022 remains the largest year for operating profit in the period.

The operating margin fell from 43.9% in 2022 to 19.6% in 2023 and then edged up to 18.2% in 2024 and 22.3% in 2025. The sharp margin drop in 2023 came alongside lower revenue, and the margin recovery since 2024 has been smaller than the revenue recovery.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent went from KRW 9.44 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.21 billion in 2023 (down 66.0%), KRW 5.26 billion in 2024 (up 63.6%) and KRW 3.58 billion in 2025 (down 31.9%).

In 2025 operating profit rose while profit attributable to owners of the parent fell, and the company disclosed in an electronic filing in February 2026 that losses from subsidiaries and financial investment valuations had been reflected.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 3.64 billion in 2025, a small gap from the parent-owner figure. At the end of 2025, total equity stood at KRW 59.9 billion, of which KRW 59 billion belonged to owners of the parent, and total liabilities were KRW 15.7 billion.

The debt ratio rose from 19.6% in 2022 to 30.4% in 2024 before easing to 26.3% in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 7.65 billion in 2022, KRW 3.6 billion in 2023, KRW 1.25 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.03 billion in 2025, a recovery in 2025.

Quarterly revenue moved within a range of about KRW 4.8 billion to KRW 5.3 billion from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 4.0% from a year earlier.

Operating profit went from KRW 335 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.23 billion in the fourth quarter (up 266.3%), peaked at KRW 1.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then fell to KRW 1.18 billion in the second quarter (down 30.8%) before reaching KRW 1.28 billion in the third quarter (up 8.3%, and up 281.0% from a year earlier).

Profit attributable to owners of the parent moved from a KRW 302 million net loss in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 765 million in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 629 million in the second quarter and KRW 1.35 billion in the third quarter.

Because the fourth quarter of 2025 posted positive operating profit alongside a net loss, non-operating items have had a large effect in some quarters.

05

Industry analysis

Two opposing forces are at work in the font industry. On one side, the growing supply of free fonts that can be used commercially and advancing AI glyph generation could weaken the case for paid fonts.

On the other, a Sandoll representative has said that as generative AI expands content production, fonts that raise polish and differentiation become more important. The company attributed greater industry-wide interest in fonts to the spread of K-content in its 2025 results.

As the leading font platform in Korea, Sandoll grows subscribers and member companies together, and generative AI design tools and free font distribution channels overlap with it as competitors.

In overseas expansion, the company launched a mobile service in Thailand in 2025, and its CEO said AI has made expansion into Latin scripts and Thai easier.

Korean-script fonts were seen as difficult for AI because they require consistent shapes across thousands of characters, but the company says it now applies its in-house AI to actual production.

On the content IP side, mobile fonts based on webtoon and web novel IP were unveiled in June 2026, and an August 2026 agreement launched a project for fandom fonts built on artist IP. The platform is expanding toward an AI studio that bundles image, presentation and audio assets.

Within the competitive landscape, platform membership and subscriber retention are presented as points of differentiation rather than standalone font sales.

06

Outlook

The 2026 task the company has set out is commercializing its AI font production solution. In its March 2026 corporate value plan, the company paired expanded subscription platform revenue with a stronger share of recurring revenue.

A July 2026 release said automating glyph generation cut production time by about 60%, and that the company had started developing AI for multilingual fonts. Its Font Playground service, which lets users generate only the characters they need, has been described as combining free features with a premium paid version.

Together with LG CNS, the company is developing technology that inserts chosen fonts into images created by generative AI, and it says a prototype has already launched.

Under the agreement with Gemstone E&M, plans include artist font sales, fan club and concert-exclusive fonts, brand collaborations, and a global font platform with a creator marketplace.

On shareholder returns, the company disclosed in March 2026 a target of a total shareholder return ratio of at least 40%, combining dividends with share cancellations. It also said it is reviewing new businesses, strategic investments and M&A.

First-half 2026 separate-basis results were revenue of KRW 8 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.2 billion. How much the AI production shift contributes to revenue and margins has not yet appeared in annual results, and the first annual check point is the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.

Against its past trading range, the useful starting point is how the multiple moved during the earlier profit recovery. 08

Bull factors

Room for cost-structure gains from AI production

The company says its in-house AI generates the remaining 11,172 characters from roughly 2,500 input characters. The company describes production that once took at least four months as shrinking to a few days. A July 2026 release cited about 60% shorter production time and extended the technology to PC retail fonts. The bull case rests on lower production costs widening room for operating margin gains.

Subscription cash flow and high retention

The company reports a customer retention rate of 95% for its subscription platform, and its CEO said paying members grew 19% in 2025 while supplier count rose 30%. Operating cash flow recovered to KRW 6.03 billion in 2025 from KRW 1.25 billion in 2024. The subscription structure is the basis for the bull view that cash inflows are stable.

Wider shareholder returns and a solid capital base

The company set a target of a total shareholder return ratio of at least 40% and reported return ratios of 46% in 2023 and 33% in 2024. It cancelled shares worth KRW 4.4 billion in January 2026 and said it is reviewing a Korean-style majority-of-minority mechanism to protect minority shareholders. Equity attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 59 billion at the end of 2025, supporting the capital base.

09

Bear factors

Earnings volatility and lower margins

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 9.44 billion in 2022, fell 66.0% to KRW 3.21 billion in 2023, and fell 31.9% to KRW 3.58 billion in 2025. The operating margin went from 43.9% in 2022 to 22.3% in 2025, roughly half the earlier level.

In the fourth quarter of 2025 operating profit was KRW 1.23 billion, yet the parent-owner result was a KRW 302 million net loss.

Valuation risk from a wider investment push

The company holds a 20% stake in a music content company and a 19% stake in a webtoon translation AI firm, and it plans to expand investments further in 2026. The investable capital was described as about KRW 30 billion.

A February 2026 electronic filing said losses from subsidiaries and financial investments had been reflected. Investment gains and losses could obscure core business earnings.

Competition from free fonts and AI tools

With free fonts usable commercially spreading and AI glyph tools improving, proving the value of paid subscriptions is cited as a challenge. Second-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 4.92 billion, down 6.9% from the prior quarter.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 5.01 billion rose 4.0% from a year earlier, a moderate pace of top-line growth.

10

Risk factors

Governance and controlling-shareholder influence

After the founder's death in May 2024, KCGI came in as a major shareholder to help resolve inheritance tax issues. As of a February 2026 report, KCGI and Sandoll Communication jointly held 17.92%. The March 2026 disclosure showed a five-member board with two outside directors and one non-standing auditor.

The risk is that asset reallocation led by a major shareholder could diverge from minority shareholder interests.

Execution of new businesses versus core earnings

AI font production, Font Playground, multilingual fonts and fandom IP projects are moving forward at the same time. The company has also said it is reviewing new businesses and M&A. Because new businesses take time and cost money before generating revenue, core earnings could be affected in the interim.

Volatility typical of a small-cap stock

As a small-cap KOSDAQ stock, trading volume can be limited. Quarterly profit is also small, so a single valuation gain or loss can move profit attributable to owners of the parent sharply. The fourth quarter of 2025, which posted positive operating profit but a net loss, illustrates this.

12

Overall view

Sandoll combines subscription cash flow from its font platform with AI font production and an investment business. Revenue of KRW 20 billion in 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 span, and operating profit of KRW 4.45 billion rose for a second straight year after the 2023 low.

However, the operating margin has fallen from 43.9% in 2022 to 22.3% in 2025, and profit attributable to owners of the parent dropped 31.9% to KRW 3.58 billion in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was positive in each quarter from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026, and the KRW 1.35 billion in the third quarter reflects a recovery from the fourth-quarter 2025 net loss.

The bull points are AI production cost savings, a subscription base with 95% retention, and a target of at least 40% total shareholder return; the bear points are earnings volatility, valuation gains and losses on investments, and competition from free fonts and AI tools.

The share price trades below book value per share, but that fact alone does not settle a judgment on value. The answer depends on whether earnings persist and whether the AI production shift shows up in revenue and operating margin.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report, due by March 31, 2027, and the first-quarter 2027 report. This report is for information only and does not include a buy or sell opinion.
