02

Business structure

Samyoung Electronics was founded in 1968 and manufactures aluminum electrolytic capacitors, with demand from home appliances and large TV displays as its main end markets. In the full-year 2024 breakdown, capacitor products accounted for 84.27% of sales and capacitor materials for 15.73%.

In the cumulative period through the third quarter of 2023, electrolytic capacitors made up 82% and capacitor materials 18%, while Korea accounted for 81% of sales and China for 19%. Samsung Electronics represented 26.9% of 2022 sales and LG Electronics 21.6%, according to a 2023 research report.

That report projected automotive electronics would rise from 12% of Korean head-office sales in 2022 to 25% in 2025. Overseas subsidiaries include Qingdao Samyoung Electronics in China and Samyoung Hong Kong.

Nippon Chemi-Con is the largest shareholder, holding 53.06% as of August 7, 2026 per WiseReport data, with treasury shares at 4.95%. The company is localizing hybrid capacitors that were previously imported from Japan, with a planned investment of KRW 30–50 billion.

Through a joint venture with Abico Electronics and Nippon Chemi-Con, the SNA Electronics plant in Vietnam produces 200,000 inverter line filters per month.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on the filed figures, annual sales fell from KRW 227.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 177.3 billion in 2023 (down 22.2%), KRW 163 billion in 2024 (down 8.1%), and KRW 149.7 billion in 2025 (down 8.1%), declining for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Operating profit dropped from KRW 16.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.46 billion in 2023 (down 60.3%), the low point, before rising 13.1% to KRW 7.3 billion in 2024 and 26.6% to KRW 9.24 billion in 2025.

Operating margin moved from 7.1% in 2022 to 3.6% in 2023, 4.5% in 2024, and 6.2% in 2025, so margins recovered from the trough even as sales contracted.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 18.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.8 billion, KRW 11.1 billion, and KRW 5.12 billion in 2025, a 53.9% drop in the latest year.

A news report on the electronic disclosure said the company attributed the 2025 profit decline to an asset value adjustment (impairment loss). Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 5.15 billion in 2025, a gap of only KRW 0.03 billion from the controlling-shareholder figure.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 6.21 billion in 2022 to KRW 30.9 billion in 2023, KRW 39.8 billion in 2024, and KRW 30.1 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales in the fourth quarter of 2025 were KRW 34.5 billion, down 2.5% from the prior quarter, and that quarter posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.36 billion.

Sales then rose for three consecutive quarters, to KRW 39.2 billion, KRW 41.7 billion, and KRW 46.3 billion, with operating profit at KRW 2.14 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 3.11 billion in both the second and third quarters.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.04 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 44.6% from KRW 5.49 billion in the second quarter. The last four quarters total KRW 4.66 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Capacitor demand is closely tied to home appliances and large TV displays, so the business follows those end-market cycles. A first-quarter 2026 earnings commentary cited slowing global growth and delayed recovery in IT demand, which limit growth in commodity products.

The same commentary pointed to price competition in capacitors and raw material and currency swings as pressures on manufacturing costs. By contrast, automotive electronics, AI servers, data centers, and renewable energy are described as sources of medium-term growth and rising demand for higher-value products.

The company is expanding production of inverter line filters for electric and hybrid vehicles and hybrid capacitors to raise its automotive share. Samwha Capacitor is the main capacitor-sector peer for comparison, while Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek are listed among component-sector peers.

Their scale and product mix differ considerably, which limits direct comparison. Whether the diversification of markets and customers outlined in the value-up plan reduces dependence on home appliances will be central to judging the company's position against its end markets.

06

Outlook

On March 27, 2026, the company filed a value-up plan that set market and customer diversification, governance improvement, and stable profitability as its goals. To pursue them, it cited facility expansion to raise production efficiency and sales, and expansion into new businesses.

The same plan showed a payout ratio of 111% for 2025, the most recent fiscal year, with 2025 dividends of about KRW 5.7 billion. On the business side, the Vietnam joint venture with SNA Electronics is described as producing 200,000 inverter line filters per month, and supply to a European automaker has begun.

The company is investing KRW 30–50 billion to localize hybrid capacitors and is pursuing an 80% share of the domestic hybrid capacitor market, according to a corporate analysis summary.

It is also pushing to expand electrolytic capacitor supply for electric vehicle parts, including charging modules for the Hyundai IONIQ 6. Its long-term target cited in the same summary is KRW 100 billion in annual inverter line filter sales by 2030.

These targets come from a corporate analysis summary rather than from a numerical guidance issued by the company. How much of them shows up in reported results should be checked against the segment disclosures in the periodic reports.

Overall view

Samyoung Electronics saw sales fall from KRW 227.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 149.7 billion in 2025, yet operating profit rose in both 2024 and 2025, and the 2025 operating margin of 6.2% was above the 3.6% of 2023.

However, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders declined to KRW 5.12 billion in 2025, and the company attributed this to an asset value adjustment (impairment loss).

Quarterly sales rose for three consecutive quarters after the fourth-quarter 2025 low, and third-quarter 2026 sales were up 30.9% year on year.

On the balance sheet, equity stood at KRW 540 billion and the debt ratio at 4.4% at the end of 2025, a large asset base relative to earnings.

Automotive components, hybrid capacitors, and the Vietnam joint venture are presented as ways to reduce dependence on home appliances, but their sales contribution is not yet visible by segment in the periodic reports.

Strengths in financial capacity and the quarterly recovery coexist with weaknesses in shrinking sales and earnings volatility, with roughly equal weight. The next checkpoints are the annual results in the 2026 annual report, the dividend decision at the 2027 annual general meeting, and first-quarter 2027 results.
