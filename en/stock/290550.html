02

Business structure

DK Tech began as a maker of FPCA modules for smartphones and smartwatches using surface-mount technology (SMT). It has since broadened into vehicle wireless charging modules and battery management system (BMS) modules, with production centered on its Vietnam subsidiary.

In 2023, OLED FPCA accounted for 80.6% of revenue, battery FPCA for 17.2% and vehicle smartphone wireless chargers for 2.2%, and smartphones made up 90.6% of revenue by application. By 3Q 2025 the revenue mix was OLED 56.8%, automotive 29.8% and battery 13.4%, a larger weighting toward automotive.

Exports were 98% of 2023 results. The company has set up a North American corporate entity to address demand in automotive and energy solutions.

Its vehicle wireless-charging business runs through BH EVS, which as of end-2023 was 59% owned by its parent company and 41% owned by DK Tech, and DK Tech has been taking over part of an existing supplier's volume.

ESS BMS modules, previously supplied to one battery maker, were extended under supply contracts signed at the end of 2025 with three major domestic battery makers. Domestic SMT and FPCA peers include domestic SMT specialists.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results bottomed in 2023 and grew in both 2024 and 2025. Revenue fell from KRW 363.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 280.2 billion in 2023, a 22.9% decline, then rose to KRW 403.3 billion in 2024, up 43.9%, and KRW 425.8 billion in 2025, up 5.6%. 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025 for revenue.

Operating profit fell from KRW 21.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 14.7 billion in 2023, a 30.7% decline, recovered to KRW 23.1 billion in 2024, up 57.2%, and then slipped to KRW 21.6 billion in 2025, down 6.5%. 2024 was the largest year for operating profit.

The operating margin was 5.8% in 2022, 5.2% in 2023, 5.7% in 2024 and 5.1% in 2025, so the margin declined in 2025 even as revenue grew. Net profit attributable to owners fell to KRW 4.37 billion in 2023, then jumped to KRW 26.9 billion in 2024, up 514.4%, before declining 13.8% to KRW 23.2 billion in 2025.

The 2024 jump in owner net profit far outpaced the 57.2% rise in operating profit, so non-operating items should be checked in the periodic reports. Net profit including non-controlling interests is shown as KRW 23.2 billion for 2025.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 25.2 billion in 2022 and KRW 11.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 28.5 billion in 2024, then fell sharply to KRW 4.51 billion in 2025. The debt ratio fell from 98.5% in 2022 to 57.2% in 2024 and was 57.6% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose for four consecutive quarters, from KRW 114.8 billion in 4Q 2025, up 8.5% from the prior quarter, to KRW 149.6 billion in 3Q 2026. 2Q 2026 revenue of KRW 148 billion, up 28.0% from the prior quarter, lifted quarterly sales to a higher level.

Operating profit also rose for four consecutive quarters, from KRW 6.07 billion in 4Q 2025 to KRW 12.9 billion in 3Q 2026, and 3Q 2026 was up 168.0% year on year.

Owner net profit peaked at KRW 13.9 billion in 1Q 2026, up 73.5% from the prior quarter, then fell to KRW 10.5 billion in 2Q 2026, down 24.4%, and to KRW 8.58 billion in 3Q 2026, down 18.2%, marking two straight quarterly declines.

Owner net profit for the last four quarters, from 4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026, totaled KRW 40.9 billion. The reasons operating profit and owner net profit moved in different directions in some quarters should be confirmed in the periodic reports' non-operating and tax items.

05

Industry analysis

DK Tech's core demand still comes from smartphone set makers. In 2023, smartphones accounted for 90.6% of revenue by application, and analysts have argued that growth has been constrained as the smartphone industry entered a mature phase.

In 2026, reports describe demand broadening toward automotive and new businesses such as ESS and robotics.

On the automotive side, growth in the automotive display market is cited as the basis, with a market research estimate projecting the global automotive display market to reach USD 44 billion by 2030 and automotive OLED to grow at an annual rate of 26% over the same period.

On the ESS side, reports say rising demand from AI data centers and power infrastructure in North America is pushing ESS adoption forward. In robotics, the humanoid robot market is still at an early stage, with the timing of commercialization and the pace of demand expansion cited as key variables.

On competition, DK Tech competes with domestic SMT and FPCA makers, but the market has noted technology that can be applied across industries beyond simple component supply. A 2024 securities report assessed that DK Tech has the largest Vietnam production capacity among domestic peers.

Listed companies in the same electronic components sector include Samsung Electro-Mechanics, LG Innotek and Isupetasys. On the battery side, expanding BMS customers and securing North American production bases remain open tasks.

Outlook

Three threads are reported in company and media sources. First, in automotive, a new order for vehicle display modules from a North American EV maker was reported in February 2026, with mass production targeted for November after prototype testing.

Around the same time a company official said mass production of vehicle display modules for global customers was scheduled from 1Q 2026, so the timing of mass production is read differently across reports.

Second, in robotics, a supply of BMS FPCAs for Boston Dynamics robot batteries was reported in January 2026, with supply reportedly going through a US affiliate formed in a joint venture with the parent company. A charger module for humanoid robots was reported to be approaching a proof-of-concept stage.

Third, in ESS, supply contracts with three major battery makers underpin expectations for BMS revenue growth, and a March 2026 report discussed a sharp increase in 2026. Hana Securities raised its annual outlook in June 2026, citing solid 1Q results and new businesses entering full operation.

Hanyang Securities projected in a November 2026 report that 2027 operating profit would rise 62%, and that ESS and vehicle new-business revenue growth of KRW 150 billion would account for 77% of total growth.

According to the report, ESS BMS carries an operating margin of about 12.5% because DK Tech takes part in development, design and raw material sourcing. On production, a dedicated ESS line in Vietnam has raised cost competitiveness, and a Georgia factory setup for North American demand was reported to start in 2026.

Most of these outlooks rest on reports and media coverage, so whether they show up as actual revenue in the next periodic reports is the key test.

08

Bull factors

Four Straight Quarters of Growth

Quarterly revenue and operating profit each rose for four consecutive quarters, from 4Q 2025 through 3Q 2026. 3Q 2026 revenue grew 41.5% year on year while operating profit grew 168.0%, so earnings rose faster than sales. 2Q 2026 revenue of KRW 148 billion, up 28.0% from the prior quarter, lifted the quarterly sales level.

Broader Customers and Product Mix

ESS BMS supply contracts with three major domestic battery makers have expanded the customer base to three. In automotive, a display module order from a North American EV maker was reported, and BMS FPCA supply for robot batteries has also been reported. Hanyang Securities projected a 62% increase in 2027 operating profit.

Higher-Margin BMS Business

DK Tech's participation in development, design and raw material sourcing is cited as a factor supporting the ESS BMS margin. A dedicated ESS line in Vietnam has also been credited with improving cost competitiveness. If new-business revenue grows, the room for operating margin improvement widens.

09

Bear factors

Slowing Earnings Momentum

Owner net profit has declined for two straight quarters since its 1Q 2026 peak. Annual operating profit fell 6.5% in 2025, and operating cash flow dropped to KRW 4.51 billion from KRW 28.5 billion in 2024. Whether revenue growth continues to translate into profit growth needs to be confirmed.

Dependence on Smartphones

Smartphones accounted for 90.6% of revenue by application in 2023, and analysts have said growth is limited when the smartphone industry is mature. OLED's share fell to 56.8% in 3Q 2025 but remains a majority. Until new-business revenue grows larger, set-demand swings will still affect results.

Early Stage of New Businesses

The humanoid robot market is still at an early stage, and the timing of commercialization and the pace of demand expansion are cited as key variables. If technology validation and customer acquisition run behind schedule, the near-term earnings effect could be limited. Reports also differ on the mass production timing for automotive OLED, which needs confirmation.

10

Risk factors

Customer and Demand Concentration

A large part of revenue is tied to smartphone demand from domestic set makers. In 2023, weaker shipments by domestic set makers reduced FPCA orders, which in turn shrank total revenue. A renewed decline in smartphone shipments could move results through the same channel.

Commercialization and Order Execution

Mass production timing and volumes for ESS customers, automotive modules and robot BMS supply differ across reports. A delay in mass production would push back revenue recognition, and upward estimate revisions may not show up in reported results. Expectations of lifting the new-business share to around 40% depend on conversion into actual revenue.

Cash Flow and Working Capital

Operating cash flow fell sharply to KRW 4.51 billion in 2025 from KRW 28.5 billion in 2024. If working capital needs grow, earnings gains may not translate into cash generation. The decline in owner net profit since 2Q 2026 is another item to monitor alongside cash flow.

12

Overall view

DK Tech's revenue and operating profit recovered from the 2023 low, and both have risen for four consecutive quarters from 4Q 2025 through 3Q 2026. 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 149.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 12.9 billion are the highest of the last five quarters.

However, owner net profit peaked at KRW 13.9 billion in 1Q 2026 and has declined for two straight quarters, and operating cash flow fell to KRW 4.51 billion in 2025. The revenue base is moving from smartphone OLED toward automotive, ESS and robotics, and orders and reports supporting this shift are accumulating.

The bullish points are customer diversification and steadily rising operating profit, while the bearish points are smartphone dependence, uncertain commercialization timing for new businesses, and weaker cash flow. The weight of these two sides is not yet settled in reported results.

The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, which will confirm full-year revenue and operating profit, 4Q results and operating cash flow. How new-business revenue feeds into results after 2027 is the central question for the medium-term trajectory.
