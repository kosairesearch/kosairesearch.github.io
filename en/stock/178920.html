02

Business structure

PI Advanced Materials makes polyimide (PI) film, varnish, and powder and molded products, and as of the end of 1Q 2026 this business accounted for 99.9% of total sales.

Polyimide film offers high heat resistance and insulation, so it is used in flexible printed circuit boards (FPCB), heat-dissipation sheets, industrial processes and battery insulation. Press coverage in 2025 reported a 30.5% global market share in polyimide film, ranking the company first.

Revenue is split into FPCB, heat-dissipation sheet and advanced-industry applications. For full-year 2024, revenue by application was KRW 115.2 billion for FPCB, KRW 73.1 billion for heat-dissipation sheets and KRW 63.0 billion for advanced industries.

In 3Q 2026, FPCB revenue was KRW 26.8 billion (down 6.6% year on year), heat-dissipation sheet revenue KRW 8.6 billion (down 64.4%) and advanced-industry revenue KRW 19.5 billion (up 28.6%).

Advanced-industry revenue of KRW 14.1 billion in 1Q 2026 broke down into EV and batteries 32%, industrial processes 25% and displays 11%. Of the KRW 16.9 billion in heat-dissipation sheet revenue in the same quarter, 78% came under long-term supply contracts.

The company attributed the 3Q 2026 decline in heat-dissipation sheet sales to deepening weakness in demand across its major customers. It has said it will diversify into aerospace, semiconductor processes and robotics.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, sales fell from KRW 276.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 217.6 billion in 2023 (down 21.3% year on year) and then rose to KRW 251.3 billion in 2024 (up 15.5%) and KRW 263.4 billion in 2025 (up 4.8%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 52.1 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 3.94 billion in 2023, which turned it back to a profit of KRW 34.9 billion in 2024, and then rose to KRW 43 billion in 2025 (up 23.2%). Operating margin was 18.9% in 2022, -1.8% in 2023, 13.9% in 2024 and 16.3% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 45.7 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 1.8 billion in 2023 (a swing into loss), KRW 23.4 billion in 2024 (a swing back to profit) and KRW 30.4 billion in 2025 (up 30.0%).

Net income including non-controlling interests matches the controlling-shareholder figure in each of these years as disclosed.

Operating cash flow was KRW 17.4 billion in 2022, KRW 60 billion in 2023, KRW 55.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 78.3 billion in 2025, so 2023 still produced KRW 60 billion of operating cash flow despite the operating loss.

The gap between earnings and cash flow will be clarified by the cash flow statement in the annual business report. Quarterly swings are large.

From 3Q 2025 operating profit of KRW 11.6 billion and sales of KRW 68.4 billion, 4Q 2025 sales fell to KRW 58.4 billion (down 14.6% quarter on quarter) and operating profit to KRW 6.63 billion (down 42.9%). In 1Q 2026, sales were KRW 64.1 billion and operating profit KRW 14 billion (up 111.8% quarter on quarter).

In 2Q 2026, sales reached KRW 77.5 billion and operating profit KRW 23 billion (up 63.6%), the highest of the last five quarters. The company cited higher average selling prices and currency effects for the 2Q improvement.

In 3Q 2026, sales were KRW 55.5 billion (down 28.3% quarter on quarter and 18.8% year on year), operating profit KRW 12.5 billion (down 45.5% quarter on quarter, up 7.9% year on year) and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 9.3 billion (up 10.3% year on year).

The company blamed the sales drop on softer mobile end-market demand tied to a surge in memory chip prices, and said its operating margin rose 5.6 percentage points year on year.

Cumulative sales for January to September 2026 were KRW 197.09 billion (down 3.8%) and operating profit KRW 49.56 billion (up 36.1%); these are preliminary figures announced on October 28, 2026 and not yet audited.

05

Industry analysis

Smartphones remain the core application. In 3Q 2025, press reports said weaker Chinese smartphone demand and slow sales of slim models pushed sales down across all three core product groups.

In 3Q 2026, the company again cited softer mobile end-market demand after a surge in memory chip prices as the reason for lower sales. The company said the shift toward higher-value products continued in the same quarter.

Advanced-industry sales were KRW 19.5 billion in 3Q 2026, up 28.6% year on year, which shows that non-mobile applications are gaining weight. In 2024, EV and battery demand stagnated, but demand for semiconductor-use film grew, and advanced-industry sales rose 6.9% to KRW 63.0 billion.

The heat-dissipation sheet market saw intensifying competition and weaker demand in 2Q 2026, and sales declined. On the cycle, a 2025 report said sales peaked at KRW 301.9 billion in 2021 before falling to KRW 217.6 billion in 2023, followed by recovery in 2024 and 2025.

In 3Q 2026, demand adjustment in mobile has slowed the business again. The company holds the number one position in polyimide film with a reported 30.5% global share.

06

Outlook

The 2026 targets summarized in a February 2026 securities report were sales of KRW 300 billion, operating profit of KRW 70 billion and an operating margin of 20%.

Against these, cumulative sales for January to September 2026 were KRW 197.09 billion and operating profit KRW 49.56 billion (both preliminary), which means the annual targets have not yet been reached.

When the CEO took office in March 2023, a goal of raising 2026 sales to the KRW 600 billion range was reported, and the current year-to-date sales trend is far from it. For 3Q 2026 FPCB sales, the company said demand for new products fell short of expectations.

Heat-dissipation sheets had a 78% long-term contract share in 1Q 2026, but 3Q 2026 sales dropped to KRW 8.6 billion. The company repaid more debt in 3Q 2026, lowering the debt ratio from 39% a year earlier to 22%, and plans further repayments in 4Q to reduce interest costs.

It said it will respond flexibly to changes in smartphone demand amid continuing market uncertainty, including year-end inventory adjustments, and upgrade its raw material purchasing strategy. Diversification into aerospace, semiconductor processes and robotics is ongoing.

The next checkpoints are the 4Q 2026 preliminary results and annual sales and operating profit in the 2026 business report, along with whether heat-dissipation sheet sales recover from the 3Q 2026 level.

08

Bull factors

Operating profit recovery

Operating profit reached KRW 43 billion in 2025, up 23.2%. Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 49.56 billion (preliminary, announced October 28, 2026), up 36.1% year on year.

The company said its 3Q 2026 operating margin rose 5.6 percentage points year on year and credited cost and expense management.

Stronger balance sheet

The debt ratio fell from 46.1% in 2022 to 30.9% at year-end 2025, and total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 109.6 billion. The company said it made further debt repayments in 3Q 2026, lowering the debt ratio from 39% a year earlier to 22%. Operating cash flow rose to KRW 78.3 billion in 2025 from KRW 17.4 billion in 2022.

Growth in non-mobile applications

Advanced-industry sales were KRW 19.5 billion in 3Q 2026, up 28.6% year on year. In 1Q 2026, advanced-industry sales were split across EVs and batteries (32%), industrial processes (25%) and displays (11%).

Long-term contracts made up 78% of heat-dissipation sheet sales in 1Q 2026, which points to a contract-backed revenue base.

09

Bear factors

Mobile demand slowdown

3Q 2026 sales were KRW 55.5 billion, down 18.8% year on year. The company pointed to softer mobile end-market demand after a surge in memory chip prices. FPCB sales also fell 6.6% to KRW 26.8 billion, so dependence on mobile remains high, which is the main bearish factor.

Weak heat-dissipation sheet demand

Heat-dissipation sheet sales fell from KRW 21.3 billion in 2Q 2026 to KRW 8.6 billion in 3Q 2026, down 64.4% year on year. The company attributed this to deepening weakness in demand from key customers.

Sales had already dropped 20.3% year on year in 2Q 2026 on intensifying competition and softer demand, so the weakness has now lasted two quarters.

Volatile quarterly profit

Operating profit fell 45.5% quarter on quarter from KRW 23 billion in 2Q 2026 to KRW 12.5 billion in 3Q 2026, showing wide quarterly swings. Operating profit was KRW 6.63 billion in 4Q 2025, down 42.9% from the prior quarter.

Cumulative sales for January to September 2026 were KRW 197.09 billion, down 3.8% year on year, so top-line growth has not yet appeared.

10

Risk factors

Concentration in mobile end-markets

FPCB was the largest application in 2024 at KRW 115.2 billion, so earnings are sensitive to smartphone demand. The company has said market uncertainty continues, including year-end inventory adjustments. Weaker-than-expected demand for new products is part of the same exposure.

Dependence on selling prices and FX

The company cited higher average selling prices and currency effects for the 2Q 2026 improvement. If selling prices or exchange rates change, quarterly profit could move accordingly. The company has said it will upgrade its raw material purchasing strategy to manage cost pressure.

Possible revisions to preliminary figures

The 3Q 2026 results are preliminary figures announced on October 28, 2026, based on unaudited accounts that the company said may change. Figures could still move until the periodic report is finalized. The cumulative results are subject to the same condition.

Overall view

PI Advanced Materials is reported to hold the number one global share in polyimide film, and operating profit rose to KRW 43 billion in 2025 after the return to profit in 2024.

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 49.56 billion (preliminary), up year on year, while cumulative sales of KRW 197.09 billion were down 3.8%. In 3Q 2026, sales fell 18.8% year on year on softer mobile demand, and heat-dissipation sheet sales dropped sharply.

Bullish factors are the profit recovery, the lower debt ratio and growth in advanced-industry sales. Bearish factors are reliance on mobile, weakness in heat-dissipation sheets and volatile quarterly profit. Year-to-date sales and operating profit have not yet reached the 2026 targets.

The next checkpoints are the 4Q 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 business report, along with whether heat-dissipation sheet sales recover.
