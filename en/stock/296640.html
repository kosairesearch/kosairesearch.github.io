Report overview

AX revenue grows while margins are still in transition

02

Business structure

Innoax supplies rules-based automation software and services mainly to financial clients, and at its March 26, 2026 shareholder meeting it changed its corporate name and restructured itself as an AI transformation (AX) partner.

The company says it has held the number one share of the Korean BRMS market for financial institutions, including insurers, for more than 20 years. Its flagship product lines accounted for 91% of consolidated revenue in 2025, a share that fell to 70.5% in Q1 2026.

The gap is being filled by InnoPAS, an AI-based insurance premium-calculation automation solution, and by AX services. The company says 75% of Korean card companies and 72% of insurers have adopted its solutions, with repurchase rates in the 80% range.

In Q1 2026, JS Link, an enterprise software firm, became a consolidated subsidiary. Large system integrators are another major channel: a June 2026 supply contract with Samsung SDS for an upgrade of Samsung Life Insurance's product management system is worth KRW 3.48 billion, equal to 15.18% of 2025 revenue.

A contract with LG CNS to build a core system for a national federation of community credit cooperatives is worth KRW 4.25 billion excluding VAT, equal to 18.6% of consolidated revenue at year-end 2025.

In competition, the company points to more than two decades of financial-sector domain expertise as its differentiator.

04

Earnings analysis

On a fiscal basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 16.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.5 billion in 2023 (down 4.4% year on year), rose to KRW 23.7 billion in 2024 (up 52.8%), and then eased to KRW 22.9 billion in 2025 (down 3.3%). Of the 2022–2025 period, 2024 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 1.29 billion in 2022, turned into an operating loss of KRW 1.24 billion in 2023, returned to an operating profit of KRW 2.4 billion in 2024, and fell to KRW 922 million in 2025, down 61.5%. Operating margins were 7.9% in 2022, -8.0% in 2023, 10.1% in 2024 and 4.0% in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 2.15 billion in 2022, KRW 34.78 million in 2023 (down 98.4%), KRW 3.07 billion in 2024 (up 8,731.3%) and KRW 2.58 billion in 2025 (down 15.9%). Net income including non-controlling interests matched those figures in every year, so no gap appears between the two lines.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 3.42 billion in 2022 and KRW 366 million in 2023 to KRW 4.1 billion in 2024, then to -KRW 1.57 billion in 2025.

At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 34.2 billion, all of it attributable to owners of the parent, against total liabilities of KRW 6.39 billion; the debt ratio fell from 43.4% in 2024 to 18.7% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from a low of KRW 5.93 billion in Q4 2025 (down 0.7% quarter on quarter) to KRW 8.09 billion in Q3 2026, with three consecutive quarterly gains.

Operating profit fell from KRW 62.48 million in Q4 2025 (down 80.3%) to a peak of KRW 4.6 billion in Q1 2026 and then declined to KRW 2.9 billion in Q2 and KRW 1.9 billion in Q3.

Over the same period, profit attributable to owners of the parent rose to KRW 624 million in Q1 2026, KRW 2.9 billion in Q2 and KRW 4.38 billion in Q3, so the two profit lines moved in opposite directions.

The company said cumulative Q3 2026 net income was lifted by a one-time gain from selling part of an investment stake, and it attributed the first-half increase mainly to fair-value gains on its stake in an AI firm.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from the sector is centered on financial-industry business automation and AI transformation. The company says its long-standing financial clients, including card companies and insurers, put it in a favorable position to turn their AX demand into new revenue.

It is developing an AI agent platform that supports insurance operations across the business and has a roadmap to extend from finance into other industries. According to company figures, the license share of revenue rose from 20.7% in 2023 to 26.2% in 2024, and the company describes license revenue as higher-margin.

Large system integrators LG CNS and Samsung SDS appear as both clients and contract counterparties, so the company supplies solutions and development staff into large integrators' financial system projects. The number one market share claim rests on company statements.

On AI regulation, the company is preparing an AI governance platform that applies policy to AI decisions and records the basis for those decisions. A financial-client base gives demand stability, but results may also be sensitive to IT investment cycles within those industries.

06

Outlook

The company expects AX revenue growth to accelerate and profitability to improve from the second half of 2026.

In the first half of 2026, profitability was temporarily reduced by a larger share of services-heavy AX projects and up-front hiring of AI specialists, and the company says revenue growth and margin improvement should appear together once AX product development and organizational setup are complete.

Backlog stood at KRW 17.9 billion at the end of Q2 2026, up from KRW 16.08 billion at the end of Q1 2026. Press reports attribute the backlog increase mainly to large projects with financial institutions and airline and insurance companies.

On products, the company said in April 2026 that it plans to launch a rule copilot that converts natural-language documents into executable BRMS rules, and an AI governance platform, during 2026.

On shareholder returns, a November 17, 2026 extraordinary shareholder meeting will consider transferring capital reserves to retained earnings, and the company said that if approved it could pursue an additional reduction-of-capital dividend in 2027.

The company's target is a 43% payout ratio for 2027, rising in steps to 44% in 2028 and 45% in 2029, according to press reports. Four executives bought 42,731 shares in the open market in August 2026, investing KRW 280 million.

For the LG CNS contract, payment terms were changed from final-inspection payment to staged interim payments after stage inspections, and the contract runs from October 15, 2026 to November 30, 2028.

08

Bull factors

Pace of AX revenue growth

Standalone AX revenue rose from KRW 260 million in 2025 to KRW 2.06 billion in the first half of 2026, roughly eight times the full-year 2025 level. The AX share of consolidated revenue rose from 12% in Q1 2026 to 15% in Q2. The company expects AX growth to speed up from the second half.

Installed base among financial clients

The company reports solution adoption of 75% among card companies and 72% among insurers, with repurchase rates in the 80% range. The position is viewed as favorable for converting existing financial clients' AX demand into new revenue. Backlog rose from KRW 16.08 billion at end-Q1 2026 to KRW 17.9 billion at end-Q2.

Expanding shareholder returns

The company carried out its first quarterly dividend in its corporate history and set a 2027 payout target of 43%. In August 2026, four executives bought 42,731 shares in the open market for KRW 280 million. Press reports describe a plan to lift the payout ratio to 44% in 2028 and 45% in 2029.

09

Bear factors

Slowing operating profit

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 190 million was down 34.1% quarter on quarter and down 40.1% year on year. The 2025 operating margin of 4.0% is below the 10.1% recorded in 2024. The company attributes the pressure to AX hiring and R&D costs that were booked ahead of revenue.

Reliance on one-time items for net profit

The company said the first-half 2026 net profit increase was driven largely by fair-value gains on a stake in an AI firm. Cumulative Q3 2026 net income includes a one-time gain from selling part of an investment stake. Operating profit fell while net profit rose, so the two measures diverged.

Project concentration and payment terms

The Samsung Life supply contract of KRW 3.48 billion equals 15.18% of 2025 revenue, and the LG CNS contract of KRW 4.25 billion equals 18.6% of consolidated revenue at year-end 2025, so individual projects are large relative to the base.

A corrective filing changed the LG CNS contract from final-inspection payment to staged interim payments. The filing does not state whether stage inspections are complete or whether interim payments have been received.

10

Risk factors

Project dependence and contract terms

Revenue recognition and cash collection on large contracts depend on inspection schedules. The LG CNS contract runs from October 15, 2026 to November 30, 2028, and its payment terms were changed by a corrective filing.

Stage inspections and interim payment receipts are not stated in the filing and will need to be confirmed through later disclosures.

Up-front spending and profitability

Hiring AI specialists for the AX business has raised personnel and outsourcing costs ahead of revenue. Headcount reached 165 at the end of Q1 2026, up 12 from a year earlier. The company says project-level cost control and license revenue growth should improve profitability.

Share price volatility and market alerts

The stock was designated a one-day short-term investment-caution stock on August 21, 2026, citing a 80.31% rise over 15 days and concentrated same-day buying by a small number of accounts. Under the exchange's market alert system, such designations can be escalated to investment warning and investment risk stages. Large price swings remain a key risk factor for the stock.

12

Overall view

Innoax is adding an AX business to its financial-sector BRMS franchise, and its profile is changing as a result. Revenue has risen for three straight quarters since its Q4 2025 low through Q3 2026, and standalone AX revenue expanded from KRW 260 million in 2025 to KRW 2.06 billion in the first half of 2026.

Operating profit, however, fell to KRW 922 million in 2025 and has declined for two quarters since its Q1 2026 peak. Part of the net profit increase reflects fair-value gains and one-time items, so the operating margin is the better gauge of whether operations are recovering.

Shareholder returns are strengthening through the quarterly dividend, a 43% payout target for 2027 and executive share purchases. The stock was volatile enough to be designated a short-term investment-caution stock in August 2026, and the price-to-book ratio is below 1x.

The next checkpoints are the November 17, 2026 shareholder meeting, product launches by end-2026, and the operating margin in the 2026 annual business report.
