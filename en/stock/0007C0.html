AI Infra Orders Expand, Losses Persist

02

Business structure

Founded in 2011, Acryl is an AI infrastructure company that newly listed on KOSDAQ on December 16, 2025.

The company provides an MLOps solution called Jonathan that supports the full cycle of AI model development, deployment and operation, and describes itself as a 'software Nvidia' pursuing an AX (AI-Experience) infrastructure ecosystem.

In healthcare, it operates a separate specialized platform called NADIA, which processes medical data into AI-usable formats for hospitals and public institutions.

According to the 2025 annual report filed with DART, product revenue mix was led by the Jonathan solution at 52.27%, followed by government subsidy income at 28.32%, service revenue at 13.09%, and the NADIA medical solution at 6.33%.

Media coverage at the time of listing reported roughly 80 major client companies including Samsung E&A, Samsung Welstory and Seegene, reflecting a recurring, contract-based B2B revenue structure.

More recently the company added GPUBASE, a GPU monitoring, virtualization and orchestration tool designed to raise GPU utilization at data centers, as a new growth pillar.

In healthcare, Acryl is pursuing both domestic and overseas markets through NADIA-ANE, an AI-native electronic medical record system, and ALLM.H, a medical-specialized foundation model.

Jonathan competes in the general-purpose AI infrastructure operation market while GPUBASE positions itself in the GPU cluster efficiency software segment, broadening the company's overall footprint.

04

Earnings analysis

Acryl's consolidated revenue declined 0.8% from KRW 13.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 13.3 billion in 2025.

Over the same period the operating loss narrowed from KRW 2.39 billion to KRW 2.25 billion, and the controlling-interest net loss narrowed from KRW 5.47 billion to KRW 2.81 billion, with losses continuing for two straight years from 2024 through 2025 while the loss magnitude shrank.

The 2025 operating margin improved to -16.9% from -17.8% a year earlier, and the debt-to-equity ratio shifted sharply from -165.7% in 2024 to 16.4% in 2025, a change attributable in part to the roughly KRW 42.1 billion raised through the December 2025 listing.

Total equity at the end of 2025 stood at KRW 43.7 billion (entirely attributable to controlling shareholders), while total liabilities were KRW 7.19 billion. Operating cash flow, however, worsened from -KRW 1.4 billion in 2024 to -KRW 4.71 billion in 2025.

In the first quarterly results disclosed after listing, Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 6.57 billion, but it fell 16.7% quarter on quarter to KRW 5.47 billion in Q2.

The operating loss widened from KRW 786 million in Q1 to KRW 1.5 billion in Q2, and the controlling-interest net loss widened from KRW 695 million to KRW 1.53 billion, marking two consecutive quarters of widening losses from Q1 through Q2 2026.

The company's own 2026 estimate disclosed in its annual report called for revenue of KRW 27.74 billion and a swing to an operating profit of KRW 3.28 billion, leaving a notable gap to close in the second half relative to the revenue and loss trajectory seen through the first half.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic corporations and public institutions are increasing large-scale GPU infrastructure investment in line with the spread of generative AI, and Shinhan Investment Corp. estimated in a March 2026 report that Nvidia plans to supply roughly 260,000 GPUs domestically.

Shinhan Investment Corp. noted that solutions like GPUBASE are typically priced at 8-10% of GPU hardware cost, suggesting that expanded adoption of high-end GPUs could generate more than KRW 1 trillion in order opportunities for Acryl.

Korea IR Service assessed that the center of gravity in AI infrastructure networking is shifting from the traditional dedicated InfiniBand standard toward lower-cost, Ethernet-based RoCEv2.

This shift, combined with demand to reduce dependence on specific overseas equipment vendors, could widen the business opportunity for software-based solutions such as GPUBASE.

In medical AI, Acryl has entered Central Asian markets starting with a digital healthcare project in Uzbekistan, and has strengthened its digital medical device business by making Fine Healthcare a wholly owned subsidiary.

Domestically, the company is expanding its footprint through greater participation in local government and public R&D projects.

The AI infrastructure operation software space also carries the risk that global big tech firms could internalize their own solutions, which could become a competitive pressure on outside software suppliers such as Acryl.

06

Outlook

The company cites expanded GPUBASE commercialization, entry into the U.S. market, and strengthened medical/bio operations as key drivers for second-half improvement.

The order backlog stood at KRW 21.2 billion at the end of Q2 2026, up 49% from the end of Q1, and the company said in an August 2026 preliminary announcement that this supports expectations of faster profit and loss improvement in the second half.

GPUBASE's cumulative supply to Hancom's domestic modular data center (MDC) project grew to KRW 5.5 billion after a premium-tier contract was added on July 29, 2026, on top of the initial basic-tier agreement.

This project, with a total budget of roughly KRW 6.7 billion including KRW 5.5 billion in government support, is being carried out jointly with Yonsei University, Sungkyunkwan University and Ajou University through December 2028, aiming to replace Nvidia's proprietary InfiniBand networking with open Ethernet.

Acryl said it presented GPUBASE's performance validation results across three major global cloud environments at the UKC 2026 event in Orlando, and plans to pursue customers among U.S. universities, research institutes and data centers.

Korea IR Service forecast in a July 2026 report that Acryl's 2026 revenue would grow 57% year on year to KRW 20.8 billion, with the operating loss narrowing to KRW 1.7 billion.

However, a gap remains between the company's own 2026 estimate disclosed in its annual report-revenue of KRW 27.74 billion and a swing to an operating profit of KRW 3.28 billion-and the trajectory seen through the first half, making the degree of second-half catch-up a key point to monitor.

08

Bull factors

Expanding GPU Infrastructure Demand

Shinhan Investment Corp. forecast in a March 2026 report that Nvidia's planned supply of roughly 260,000 GPUs domestically could create order opportunities exceeding KRW 1 trillion for Acryl. GPUBASE can be applied without replacing costly network equipment, allowing the company to emphasize cost efficiency.

The 49% quarter-on-quarter rise in order backlog to KRW 21.2 billion at the end of Q2 2026 has also been presented as a signal of growing demand.

Renewed Revenue Growth

Q1 2026 revenue grew 123% year on year to KRW 6.57 billion, and cumulative first-half revenue rose 150% year on year to KRW 12 billion, equivalent to about 90% of full-year 2025 revenue.

The expansion of Jonathan's client base and industries served, along with greater participation in government-led AX projects, is cited as the growth driver. Still, Q2 revenue fell 16.7% from Q1, indicating quarter-to-quarter volatility remains.

U.S. Market and Medical AI Expansion

Acryl presented GPUBASE's performance validation results across global cloud environments at UKC 2026 and is pursuing customers among U.S. universities, research institutes and data centers.

In healthcare, the company entered Central Asia starting with a digital healthcare project in Uzbekistan, and strengthened its digital medical device business by making Fine Healthcare a wholly owned subsidiary. Both initiatives remain at an early stage but have the potential to become new revenue sources.

09

Bear factors

Delayed Profit Improvement

The operating loss widened from KRW 786 million in Q1 2026 to KRW 1.5 billion in Q2, and the controlling-interest net loss widened from KRW 695 million to KRW 1.53 billion, marking two consecutive quarters of expanding losses.

Revenue also fell 16.7% from KRW 6.57 billion in Q1 to KRW 5.47 billion in Q2, showing unstable quarter-to-quarter trends. There remains a sizable gap between the company's own 2026 estimate of a swing to an operating profit of KRW 3.28 billion and the actual trajectory seen through the first half.

History of Stagnant Revenue

Consolidated revenue actually declined 0.8% from KRW 13.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 13.3 billion in 2025. Korea IR Service pointed to this two-year stagnation in revenue as a reason why the company's price-to-book ratio is hard to justify relative to peers.

It remains to be confirmed whether recent quarterly growth reflects sustained demand rather than one-off project recognition.

Continued Cash Outflow

Operating cash flow deteriorated from -KRW 1.4 billion in 2024 to -KRW 4.71 billion in 2025. If the conversion of order backlog into revenue slows, funding needs for R&D and overseas expansion could become a financial burden. Although the IPO bolstered the capital base, continued losses could gradually erode that capital cushion.

10

Risk factors

Earnings Volatility

Quarterly revenue swings are large, meaning a decline such as the one seen in Q2 2026 relative to the prior quarter could recur. The fact that operating and net losses widened for two consecutive quarters from Q1 through Q2 2026 suggests cost control warrants closer scrutiny. Failure to meet the company's own annual profitability target could weigh on investor sentiment.

Order and Commercialization Uncertainty

GPUBASE's entry into North America and the Hancom MDC project remain at an early stage, and the credibility of the growth narrative could weaken if follow-on contracts do not materialize.

Korea IR Service has also noted the need to confirm actual follow-on orders for NADIA in Central Asia and for GPUBASE-based infrastructure projects. Because government subsidy income makes up a significant portion of revenue, changes in subsidy policy could affect the revenue mix.

Competitive and Technology Shifts

The AI infrastructure operation software market is in a transitional period between networking standards (InfiniBand versus Ethernet), and a shift in technology direction could affect product competitiveness.

If global big tech firms internalize their own GPU operation software, competition with outside suppliers could intensify. Given its relatively small scale, constraints on personnel and R&D resources could affect the pace of new product development.

12

Overall view

Acryl began disclosing quarterly results for the first time after its December 2025 KOSDAQ listing, and while both Q1 and Q2 2026 revenue rose year on year, the operating and net losses actually widened in Q2.

On an annual basis, the company posted losses for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025, with the loss magnitude narrowing in 2025.

The company points to GPUBASE commercialization, U.S. market entry, and medical AI expansion as key drivers for second-half growth and profit improvement, citing the 49% quarter-on-quarter rise in order backlog at the end of Q2 as one piece of supporting evidence.

However, Korea IR Service has flagged two years of stagnant revenue and said additional confirmed orders are needed, and a gap remains between the company's own 2026 profitability estimate disclosed in its annual report and the results seen through the first half.

On balance, how Acryl is assessed going forward depends on how quickly its order backlog converts into revenue and whether profit and loss trends improve in upcoming quarterly results.
