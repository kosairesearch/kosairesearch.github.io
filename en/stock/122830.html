Report overview

From Captive Telecom Niche to AI Equipment

ONE4U is a KONEX-listed company that earned high margins from captive military and justice-sector telecom, and it is now widening its base toward AI industrial solutions through a merger and a manufacturing acquisition.

  1. 1

    FnGuide's business summary credits the company with a share above 90% in the Ministry of National Defense business.

  2. 2

    On a standalone basis for fiscal 2025 (28th term), revenue was KRW 9,049 million, operating profit KRW 2,298 million and net profit KRW 2,428 million.

  3. 3

    The absorption merger with an unlisted company has closed and the merger completion report has been filed. The new shares are scheduled to list on KONEX on November 5, 2026.

  4. 4

    On September 1, 2026, the board resolved to acquire a 65% stake in a precision inspection-equipment maker for KRW 5.9 billion.

  5. 5

    On October 21, 2026, the company decided on a KRW 300 million treasury-share trust contract with Daishin Securities.

02

Business structure

ONE4U, founded in 1998, is a software developer and supplier that listed on KONEX on October 23, 2020. The company has pursued a focus strategy on special institutions where mobile phone use is restricted, such as the defense and justice ministries, the police, hospitals and boarding schools.

Its flagship offerings are smart payphone-based non-face-to-face communication services for military units, a smart visitation service for the justice ministry, and a service for nursing hospitals.

FnGuide describes it as providing the defense and justice ministries with an integrated service that combines AI face authentication, speech-to-text and LLM technology. The same source states that its share of the defense business is above 90%, protected by a self-developed system that acts as an entry barrier.

According to company material, large carriers such as KT and LG U+ also struggled to enter this niche. Segment-level revenue weights could not be confirmed, so the mix is described only qualitatively. The scope of business widened during 2026.

At the March annual general meeting, information and communication equipment wholesale and retail and renewable energy were added to the business purposes.

In August the company formally launched an AI process anomaly-detection and predictive-maintenance solution aimed at semiconductor, battery and handset-component manufacturing sites.

The company said it intends to shift from an AI software firm to an AI materials-parts-equipment company that produces inspection equipment directly.

03

Recent trends

On a standalone basis for 2025, revenue was KRW 9,049 million, operating profit KRW 2,298 million, net profit KRW 2,428 million, and earnings per share KRW 74. Per FnGuide, 2024 revenue was KRW 10.0 billion, operating profit KRW 2.8 billion and net profit KRW 2.5 billion.

A simple comparison shows lower revenue and operating profit in 2025, with net profit larger than operating profit. In the first quarter of 2025, standalone revenue fell 8.2% year on year, while operating profit rose 8.7% and net profit rose 5.4%.

NICE Biz Info scored its profitability at 93 points (top tier) and its size at 96 points within its industry as of 2025. The same source put growth at 30 points (lower tier) and activity at 14 points (bottom tier). Quarterly and half-year figures for 2026 could not be confirmed and are therefore not discussed.

On the share price, Hankyung Korea Market showed KRW 1,290 intraday on October 1, 2026, a 52-week range of KRW 847 to KRW 2,550, a three-month change of +30.92% and a twelve-month change of -22.03%.

As of October 27, 2026, the price is KRW 1,299, down 4.27% from the previous session, with a market capitalization of KRW 42.5 billion and trading value of KRW 10,318,637.

04

Outlook

The items to watch are the reflection of merger effects, the completion of the acquisition, and the first orders from the new businesses.

Before the merger, year-end 2025 assets were KRW 44.75 billion and total equity KRW 22.76 billion; after the merger, assets are stated at KRW 52.01 billion and total equity at KRW 25.54 billion.

This is a summary that combines balance sheets as of December 31, 2025, and it should be distinguished from actual post-merger results. The merger shares are scheduled to list on the KONEX market on November 5, 2026.

At the time of the acquisition agreement, the company described a plan to develop integrated products with AI built in from the equipment design stage and said it would expand overseas after securing domestic supply records.

Reports indicated that payment was planned for completion in September 2026, so completion should be confirmed through a separate disclosure. On the sector side, media reported buying spreading across electronic equipment and device stocks on expectations of expanding AI infrastructure investment.

The same article cautioned that the actual degree of benefit may vary with each company's product competitiveness and pace of commercialization. Whether the legacy captive-telecom margins hold and how much the new businesses contribute to revenue are the points to check in future results.

05

Bull factors

Niche position and high profitability

FnGuide states that the company holds a share above 90% in the defense business. Standalone operating profit stayed positive at KRW 2,298 million in 2025. NICE Biz Info scored its profitability in the top tier at 93 points.

The fact that large carriers struggled to enter the market can be read as a factor that reduces competitive pressure.

Expansion into AI industrial solutions

The company formally launched an AI process anomaly-detection and predictive-maintenance solution in August 2026 and decided in September to acquire an inspection-equipment maker. Its logic is that combining software with equipment manufacturing could reduce dependence on a single market.

Semiconductor, battery and handset-component lines were named as first targets. The addition of renewable energy to the business purposes also signals an intent to expand.

Larger capital base and incentive funding

Total equity after the merger is stated at KRW 25.54 billion, larger than before the merger. On October 21, 2026, the company decided on a KRW 300 million treasury-share trust contract. It gave securing funds for employee performance rewards as the purpose.

The treasury-share acquisition limit was disclosed as KRW 12.88 billion based on the end of the prior fiscal year.

06

Bear factors

Flat revenue and weak growth score

Standalone revenue for 2025 was KRW 9,049 million, below the KRW 10.0 billion that FnGuide reports for 2024. Standalone revenue also declined year on year in the first quarter of 2025. NICE Biz Info scored growth at 30 points (lower tier) and activity at 14 points (bottom tier). With a limited customer base, the captive-institution market may face structural limits on revenue expansion.

Execution burden of new businesses

Moving from a software-centered company into equipment manufacturing requires production, sales and quality-control experience. The KRW 5.9 billion purchase price is not small against total equity of KRW 22.76 billion at the end of 2025 before the merger.

Specific orders or revenue from the new solutions could not be confirmed. Overseas expansion is presented as a task to follow once domestic supply records are secured.

Thin liquidity and ownership dilution

Trading value on October 27, 2026 was only KRW 10,318,637, which shows the liquidity constraint of a small KONEX stock. The merger issued 2,610,584 new shares, and the combined stake of the largest shareholder and related parties changes from 49.87% to 46.19%.

The merger shares are scheduled to list on November 5, 2026, which could affect supply and demand. Because it was a small-scale merger, existing shareholders were not given appraisal rights.

07

Risk factors

Macro

The semiconductor, battery and handset-component industries named as demand sources for the new business are sensitive to capital-spending cycles. If manufacturing investment slows, adoption of inspection equipment and predictive-maintenance solutions could be delayed.

Small KONEX stocks can move sharply with shifts in market sentiment. The wide 52-week range of KRW 847 to KRW 2,550 illustrates this.

Business

Core earnings are concentrated in contracts with a small number of public bodies such as the defense and justice ministries. A change in contract terms or policy could directly affect results.

It has not been confirmed whether integration of the acquired equipment maker and development of AI-integrated products will proceed as planned. The share above 90% in the defense business comes from FnGuide material and is not a figure independently verified through filings.

Liquidity and governance

Low daily trading value could produce a large price impact on sizable trades. The total number of shares issued rose to 35,362,569 after the merger. The combined stake of the largest shareholder side fell to 46.19%, although the company said there is no change in control.

A disclosure showed an executive director's holdings rising through merger share allocation, which is neutrally read as a change in ownership structure.

08

Overall view

ONE4U is a KONEX company that, on the strength of its position in captive telecom for defense and justice institutions, remained profitable at both the operating and net levels in 2025.

At the same time, 2025 standalone revenue was below the 2024 figure reported by FnGuide and an external assessment placed growth in the lower tier, so one view is that the legacy business alone offers limited room to expand.

The company is widening its base through an absorption merger, the launch of an AI process anomaly-detection solution and the acquisition of a precision inspection-equipment maker. Whether this shift translates into results has not yet been confirmed in disclosed figures.

The listing of merger shares on November 5, 2026, completion of the acquisition and progress of the treasury-share purchase are facts to verify later. The bullish factors are market position, profitability and a larger capital base, while the bearish factors are flat revenue, execution burden and thin liquidity. This report is for information only, and any judgment is left to the reader.
