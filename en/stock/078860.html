02

Business structure

Iokenm was founded in April 2000 as a talent management company and listed on KOSDAQ in 2006. Shareholders approved a change of the corporate name to IOKENM Co., Ltd. at the general meeting on 27 March 2026, citing a goal of improving corporate image.

According to Alphasquare's breakdown, management accounts for 87.57% of revenue, records and music for 7.54%, and other businesses for 4.89%.

Long-term re-contracts with artists including Ko Hyun-jung, Kim Kang-woo, Yoon Sang-hyun and Koo Hye-sun were reported, so the management core depends on keeping these contracts in place.

In concerts, the company said on 10 August 2026 that its single 'Park Ji-hoon Asia Fancon Tour' project in the first half generated KRW 2 billion in revenue and KRW 370 million in operating profit. These are company-announced figures, not periodic-report results.

In content IP, the company is spending about KRW 5 billion on a 'Goguryeo' comic planned at 70 volumes, based on Kim Jin-myeong's novel series. Among 49 companies in the entertainment sector, Alphasquare ranks Iokenm 45th by annual revenue, a wide scale gap versus large agencies.

Former affiliates of the Ssangbangwool group, including DMOA, are pursuing sales or ownership restructuring, so governance changes are a variable alongside the business mix.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue was KRW 19 billion in 2022 with an operating loss of KRW 10.8 billion, and 2024 revenue of KRW 25.1 billion was the largest year in 2022–2025. Revenue fell 51.3% year on year to KRW 12.2 billion in 2025, the smallest year in the period.

Operating loss narrowed to KRW 7.67 billion in 2023 and KRW 7.28 billion in 2024 before widening to KRW 8.02 billion in 2025. Operating margin moved from -57.0% in 2022 to -34.3% in 2023 and -29.0% in 2024, then fell to -65.6% in 2025 as revenue nearly halved.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders shrank from KRW 58.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 21.6 billion in 2023, KRW 21.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 17.1 billion in 2025, and losses ran for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 17.4 billion in 2025, a different figure from the KRW 17.1 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

Operating cash flow was negative each year, at -KRW 3.73 billion in 2022, -KRW 263 million in 2023, -KRW 7.42 billion in 2024 and -KRW 10.2 billion in 2025, the largest outflow of the four years.

The debt ratio fell from 46.1% in 2023 to 23.7% in 2024 and 23.6% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 68.6 billion, of which KRW 68.2 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 16.2 billion.

Quarterly, revenue was KRW 3.15 billion in the third quarter of 2025 with an operating loss of KRW 1.15 billion. In the fourth quarter revenue was KRW 2.77 billion, the operating loss widened to KRW 2.91 billion and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 11 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026 revenue fell to KRW 981 million and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 5.09 billion turned positive, while the operating loss was KRW 1.38 billion.

Revenue recovered to KRW 2.76 billion in the second quarter with an operating loss of KRW 746 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.53 billion.

In the third quarter revenue was KRW 1.56 billion, the operating loss was KRW 42.9 million and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.4 billion.

Operating losses have run for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totals KRW 11.8 billion.

Outlook

The funding secured is a third-party rights issue disclosed on 25 June 2026 that raised KRW 15 billion, made up of KRW 8 billion for operating funds and KRW 7 billion for other uses.

The allottee is Contra 31 Fund, and the company describes the raise as a basis for independent management and expansion into new businesses. Among the planned uses, the company said about KRW 5 billion goes to producing the Goguryeo IP, and the comic is planned at 70 volumes with publication under way.

In concerts, the company says the Park Ji-hoon Asia Fancon Tour showed an ability to earn revenue directly from concert planning and production. Re-contracts with Ko Hyun-jung, Kim Kang-woo, Yoon Sang-hyun and Koo Hye-sun have been reported in management.

CEO Choi Seok-woo said the company will grow high-value new businesses in concerts and IP, built on artist trust and core competitiveness.

However, how much concerts and IP add to annual revenue has not yet appeared in periodic-report figures, and quarterly revenue in 2026 has ranged from KRW 981 million to KRW 2.76 billion.

The next checkpoint is how much the concert and IP businesses are reflected in the annual revenue structure in the 2026 annual report, with artist contracts and shareholding changes surfacing through interim disclosures before then.

The scale of spending on the Goguryeo IP and the progress of its publication are also points to confirm on fund use.
