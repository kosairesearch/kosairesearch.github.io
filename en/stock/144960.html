02

Business structure

New Power Plasma makes plasma components used in semiconductor and display manufacturing. Its core products are cleaning systems that remove residue from process chambers, remote plasma sources (RPS), RF power supplies and matchers.

Its subsidiaries include Dooinsys, which makes UTG (ultra-thin glass), Space Pro, which handles defense composite materials, and Korea Fiber, which is developing carbon-fiber power transmission cable.

Based on Q1 2026 product mix, cleaning systems accounted for 23.3% of revenue, RF systems 1.2%, defense and other segments 52%, UTG and related 18.1%, and other 5.4%. With defense and other segments above half of revenue, semiconductor equipment cycles alone cannot explain the earnings.

On the semiconductor side, the company says demand for plasma-based equipment has risen with finer process nodes. Customers are mainly global equipment makers; a Hana Securities analysis reported in October 2026 put Applied Materials at about 40% of the mix and expected supply to Lam Research to expand.

Competitors include the US companies MKS and Advanced Energy. Estimates of global RPS market share differ across reports: one report placed the company second to MKS at about 30%, while an IBK Securities report from July 2026 described the plasma component market as an oligopoly in which the company holds about 10%. The two figures use different bases, so neither can be taken as definitive.

04

Earnings analysis

On the annual disclosed figures, revenue fell from KRW 352.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 350.7 billion in 2023 (down 0.6% year on year), then rose to KRW 519.8 billion in 2024 (up 48.2%) and KRW 578 billion in 2025 (up 11.2%), growing for two consecutive years.

Of the 2022 to 2025 period, 2025 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest. Operating profit went from KRW 14.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.1 billion in 2023 (up 13.9%), KRW 26.1 billion in 2024 (up 62.4%) and KRW 28.7 billion in 2025 (up 9.7%), growing for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Over the same period the operating margin moved from 4.0% in 2022 to 4.6%, 5.0% in 2024 and 5.0% in 2025, so in 2025 the margin stayed flat even as revenue rose 11.2%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 22.3 billion in 2024 (up 37.0%), the largest year in the period, and fell to KRW 8.52 billion in 2025 (down 61.8%), the smallest.

Total net income in 2025 was KRW 7.6 billion, below the controlling-shareholder figure, which means the non-controlling interests absorbed a loss. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 63.2 billion in 2024 to KRW 9.84 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 151.4 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 155.1 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 148.5 billion in Q1 2026 (down 4.3% quarter on quarter), then rose to KRW 208.8 billion in Q2 2026 (up 40.6%) and KRW 218 billion in Q3 2026 (up 44.0% year on year), growing for two consecutive quarters from Q2 to Q3 2026.

Operating profit rose for four consecutive quarters from KRW 6.97 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 36.7 billion in Q3 2026, and Q3 2026 was up 506.7% year on year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 11.9 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 21.5 billion in Q2 (up 80.1% quarter on quarter) and KRW 9.59 billion in Q3 (down 55.4%), so it turned lower in Q3.

Its swings are larger than those in operating profit, and the quarterly drivers should be checked in the quarterly report notes. The sum of controlling-shareholder net income for the last four quarters is KRW 44.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The core demand driver is growing use of plasma-based equipment as chip manufacturing moves to finer process nodes, which the company cited in its Q1 2025 results.

Supply-chain realignment is also a factor: a Meritz Securities report from July 2024 said RPS demand from Chinese local equipment makers rose after the US-China dispute, lifting the overseas revenue share from 39.0% in 2022 to 58.4% in 2023.

By contrast, Leading Investment & Securities analysed that domestic revenue fell sharply in 2023 as major Korean chipmakers cut investment. Expanded global investment tied to AI chips was later cited as a growth driver.

An October 2026 news report noted expectations that planned large-scale US chip investment and dual sourcing demand from Chinese chipmakers amid US-China technology rivalry could support earnings growth through 2028.

On competition, Hana Securities views the invalidation of a patent ruling against MKS as positive for market share. Applied Materials has diversified away from an MKS-centred supply structure, and the company's share of its supply has reportedly grown.

The defense segment moves on a different cycle from semiconductors, while UTG supply at Dooinsys is centred on Samsung Display for foldable products.

06

Outlook

Broker forecasts diverge. IBK Securities, in a July 2026 report, projected 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 704.9 billion (up 22.0%) and operating profit of KRW 72.9 billion (up 154.3%), and presented a target price of KRW 15,000.

A broker estimate cited in an October 2026 news report put consolidated revenue at KRW 723.5 billion and operating profit at KRW 64.9 billion, a lower operating profit figure than IBK's. The same report cited an IB industry view of revenue near KRW 700 billion and operating profit around KRW 70 billion.

Growth drivers fall into two groups: the semiconductor side rests on expanded supply to Applied Materials and expected supply growth to Lam Research, while the defense and UTG side depends on subsidiary results.

The company's IR disclosures said Dooinsys passed the KOSDAQ listing preliminary review in April 2025, but recent disclosures on the listing timeline and use of proceeds are limited.

Meritz Securities mentioned that funds from a potential listing could go to a Vietnam line expansion in 2024, so capacity plans should be checked against the latest filings.

The next regular report, the 2026 annual report, will show whether the full-year operating margin exceeds 5.0% and whether the quarterly profit gains carried through to the annual figures.

Bull factors

Consecutive quarterly operating profit gains

Operating profit has risen for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026. Q3 2026 operating profit reached KRW 36.7 billion, up 506.7% year on year, on revenue of KRW 218 billion, up 4.4% from Q2. Operating profit growth has far outpaced revenue growth, showing up in profitability.

Wider supply to global equipment makers

A Hana Securities analysis puts Applied Materials at about 40% of the mix and expects supply to Lam Research to expand. The invalidation of a patent ruling against MKS is viewed as positive for market share. Reports also describe a rising adoption share as Applied Materials diversifies its supply base.

Broker estimates pointing higher

In a July 2026 report, IBK Securities projected 2026 consolidated operating profit of KRW 72.9 billion and presented a target price of KRW 15,000. The report cited the oligopolistic plasma component market and a diversified customer base. Broker estimates depend on the valuation methods and assumptions used.

09

Bear factors

Volatility in net income attributable to controlling shareholders

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 22.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 8.52 billion in 2025, down 61.8%. In Q3 2026 it was KRW 9.59 billion, down 55.4% from Q2, so operating profit gains did not carry through to this line in every quarter.

Flat margin and weaker cash flow

The operating margin stayed at 5.0% in both 2024 and 2025. Unlike the margin, operating cash flow declined over the same period, so cash generation weakened. The debt ratio eased to 130.2% at end-2025 from 148.1% in 2024, but total liabilities of KRW 560.6 billion still exceed total equity.

Business concentration and competition

Defense and other segments made up 52% of Q1 2026 revenue, so results depend heavily on sectors outside semiconductor equipment. On the semiconductor side, Applied Materials at about 40% means customer concentration is high. Competition with MKS and Advanced Energy continues, and market share figures differ across reports.

10

Risk factors

Customer and supply-chain concentration

Semiconductor revenue is concentrated, with Applied Materials at about 40%, so cuts or order changes by that customer would feed directly into results. Demand from Chinese local equipment makers has grown with supply-chain realignment, so changes in regulation or trade conditions could shift the overseas revenue share.

Dependence on subsidiaries and segments

Defense and UTG make up a large part of revenue, so subsidiary results pass strongly into consolidated figures. UTG supply is centred on Samsung Display's foldable products and is affected by that customer's product cycle. The KOSDAQ listing process and use of proceeds at Dooinsys should be checked against the latest filings.

Financial structure and cash flow

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 560.6 billion, larger than total equity of KRW 430.5 billion. Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 9.84 billion, far below KRW 63.2 billion in 2024.

Even as quarterly operating profit rises, working capital and investment outlays can change cash generation, so quarterly cash flow disclosures should be read alongside earnings.

Overall view

New Power Plasma's annual revenue has grown for two consecutive years from 2024, and operating profit for three consecutive years from 2023, so revenue and profit have expanded together.

In 2026, quarterly operating profit has risen for four consecutive quarters, which the disclosed figures show as a fast recovery in earnings power.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders, however, fell to KRW 8.52 billion in 2025 and declined again from Q2 to Q3 2026, so whether operating profit gains carry through to net income is still being confirmed.

Defense and other segments, which account for about half of revenue, together with the UTG subsidiary, add volatility from outside the semiconductor equipment cycle.

On the semiconductor side, Applied Materials at about 40% signals high customer concentration, and reported global RPS market shares differ, so the basis should be checked.

Broker estimates range from IBK Securities' KRW 72.9 billion operating profit for 2026 to KRW 64.9 billion in an October 2026 report, and target prices rest on assumptions that differ by report.

The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, which will show the full-year operating margin and whether net income attributable to controlling shareholders recovers.
