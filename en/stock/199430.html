02

Business structure

KNR Systems was founded in 2000 as a maker of test equipment built on hydraulic and electric precision control technology, and listed on KOSDAQ on March 7, 2024. Its core line is simulator-grade test equipment that reproduces real operating conditions for product development and verification.

A 2024 IPO research report put 2022 sales mix at 90.7% test equipment, 7.0% test and evaluation services, 0.9% hydraulic robot systems and 1.3% other items, so robotics is the segment the company is working to grow.

Test equipment is project-based with build times of three to six months, so timing gaps between contract and revenue recognition move quarterly results sharply. In robotics the company supplies hydraulic actuators and robot arms and hands.

A KB Securities analyst described it in January 2026 as having a full line of hydraulic robot core components in Korea and noted that it supplies hydraulic actuators to Boston Dynamics. The company said it completed in 2025 a hybrid actuator line combining an electric motor with a hydraulic actuator.

It signed a main contract for a cutting platform for reactor structures in nuclear decommissioning, entering that market. It has a technology cooperation with Amazon Robotics and is taking part in a humanoid production-line project at a large domestic company.

Within the electrical equipment sector, LS ELECTRIC and Hyosung Heavy Industries are large power-equipment makers with different product mixes and order structures, so they serve more as sector reference points than direct comparables.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed basis, revenue fell from KRW 51.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 21.4 billion in 2023, a 58.2% decline, then to KRW 18.5 billion in 2024 (down 13.4%) and KRW 15.9 billion in 2025 (down 14.1%), so revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Of the 2022 to 2025 period, 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest. Operating profit of KRW 2.98 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 4.34 billion in 2023, followed by losses of KRW 7.72 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.54 billion in 2025, three straight years of operating losses.

The 2025 loss was slightly smaller than in 2024, but with revenue falling, the operating margin dropped from -41.7% in 2024 to -47.4% in 2025.

The company attributes this to project revenue slipping into the following year; the CEO said roughly half of the expected volume moved into the first half of 2026 (MoneyToday/The Bell, March 2026).

Net profit attributable to owners of the parent went from KRW 1.52 billion in 2022 to losses of KRW 3.79 billion in 2023, KRW 7.86 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.46 billion in 2025, three straight years of losses.

Total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 22.9 billion and total liabilities KRW 24.1 billion, with the debt ratio rising from 65.1% in 2024 to 105.1% in 2025. Operating cash flow went from KRW 2.53 billion in 2022 to -KRW 11.6 billion in 2024 and -KRW 2.02 billion in 2025, a smaller outflow.

Quarterly, revenue of KRW 9.59 billion in Q4 2025 (up 331.9% quarter on quarter) carried the year, followed by KRW 1.85 billion in Q1 2026 (down 80.7%), then KRW 4.51 billion and KRW 6.97 billion in Q2 and Q3, two straight quarters of growth.

Still, the Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 2.87 billion widened from the prior quarter and from a year earlier. The last four quarters together show an attributable net loss of KRW 9.35 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Robot and physical AI themes lifted both the stock and the business narrative. On January 14, 2026, shares rose nearly 20% in early trading as brokerage research pointed to the company's role as a maker of hydraulic robot core components that could benefit from the physical AI theme.

Hydraulic actuators are used where high output is needed, such as handling heavy loads or working in harsh environments, so the pace of robot commercialization shapes demand.

On test equipment, the KB Securities report argued that specialist test equipment is essential in developing a wide range of products and expected the company's growth to continue. That is an analyst's view, not a market-size or demand statistic.

Competitively, the company is described as the only domestic maker of simulator-grade hydraulic test equipment. In robot actuators it may compete with electric drive technologies, and the company positions its hybrid of hydraulic and electric designs as a differentiator.

Entry into nuclear decommissioning shows its test and fabrication capabilities extending into other industries.

Large power-equipment makers in the same sector follow power-infrastructure demand cycles that differ from its robotics and test-equipment exposure, so sector classification alone does not allow a direct comparison of cycle position.

06

Outlook

The company aims to unveil the super humanoid robot it has been developing since 2025 at the end of 2026. The bipedal, high-payload robot is designed at 2.5 meters tall and 1.5 meters wide, with a target payload of 400 kg to 600 kg.

In July 2026 the company said it had finished a robot hand with a maximum single-hand grip of 300 kg, and dedicated production equipment for the hand has been installed at a Vietnam factory of its production partner Seojin Systems.

KNR Systems handles sales and technology development, while the partner handles production and sales. The company said it plans to complete full system integration within the year.

On the financial side, the CEO projected 2026 revenue at more than double 2025, starting from about KRW 15 billion of carried-over project revenue that is fully contracted and already in production.

However, filed quarterly revenue for Q1 to Q3 2026 was KRW 1.85 billion, KRW 4.51 billion and KRW 6.97 billion, so the double-revenue outlook would require a large recognized amount in Q4. The break-even marker the CEO cited, a sales level of around KRW 30 billion, is roughly twice the 2025 revenue of KRW 15.9 billion. The next checkpoints are new robotics orders and the timing of project revenue recognition.

08

Bull factors

Position in hydraulic robot core parts

In a January 2026 report, a KB Securities analyst described the company as having the only full domestic line of hydraulic robot core components. The same report cited hydraulic actuator supply to Boston Dynamics as evidence of technical recognition.

Its hybrid rotary and linear actuator line was cited as a basis for product expansion. This is a brokerage assessment, not a direct indicator of order volume.

Carried-over revenue and quarterly recovery

The CEO said that project volume deferred from the second half of 2025 to 2026 is worth about KRW 15.0 billion, and that all of it has been contracted and is now being manufactured (Money Today The Bell, March 2026).

Reported quarterly revenue rose for two consecutive quarters, from a low of KRW 1.85 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 4.51 billion in Q2 and KRW 6.97 billion in Q3. Q3 revenue increased 214.2% from a year earlier. The next checkpoint is whether this trend continues into Q4 and 2027.

Expansion into humanoid and nuclear applications

In July 2026, the company announced that it had successfully built a heavy-load robot hand with a maximum one-hand grip strength of 300 kg. It said that a dedicated robot-hand production facility has been set up at the Vietnam plant of Seojin System.

It also signed a main contract to build a cutting platform for activated structures in nuclear heavy-water reactors, entering the nuclear decommissioning market. Because the same core components are used across several applications, there is room for product sales channels to expand.

09

Bear factors

Continued operating losses and wider losses

Based on reported results, the company has posted an operating loss for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and its operating margin in 2025 was -47.4%. In Q3 2026, the operating loss was KRW 2.87 billion, a wider loss than both the previous quarter and the same period a year earlier. Losses continue to accumulate faster than revenue is recovering.

A high bar for profitability

The CEO explained that annual fixed costs remain in the KRW 7 billion range, so the break-even point is around KRW 30 billion in revenue (Money Today The Bell, March 2026). Revenue in 2025 was KRW 15.9 billion, and quarterly revenue in Q1–Q3 2026 ranged between KRW 1.85 billion and KRW 6.97 billion. Quarterly revenue is highly volatile, which makes it hard to predict when the company will turn profitable.

Rising leverage and cash-flow pressure

The debt ratio rose from 65.1% in 2024 to 105.1% in 2025. Operating cash flow narrowed to -KRW 2.02 billion in 2025 after a large outflow of -KRW 11.6 billion in 2024, but it is still negative. In February 2026, the company secured funds by issuing KRW 15.8 billion of privately placed convertible bonds. The checkpoint is whether this reliance on external financing continues.

10

Risk factors

Project revenue timing

Test equipment takes 3 to 6 months to manufacture, so revenue shifts between quarters depending on the gap between contract signing and delivery. A typical example is the plunge to KRW 1.85 billion in Q1 2026 after KRW 9.59 billion in Q4 2025.

The CEO explained that this was not because projects were canceled but because they were pushed into the following year. The full-year outlook therefore depends on the timing of quarterly revenue recognition.

Funding and dilution

The KRW 15.8 billion privately placed convertible bonds issued in February 2026 have a coupon rate of 0%, a yield to maturity of 1%, and a maturity date of February 26, 2031. It was disclosed that there is no refixing provision.

If the bonds are converted, the number of shares will increase, which could affect per-share metrics. The checkpoint is how the proceeds are used to commercialize the robot technology.

Share-price volatility and market alerts

The company was designated an investment warning stock on July 29, 2026, and the designation was lifted on August 12, 2026. The exchange disclosed that the stock could be designated again if its price rises further after the designation is lifted.

In January 2026, an executive sold shares on the market, and in February 2026, another executive exercised a stock purchase right and increased the number of shares held. The risk is that the stock's price is highly volatile.

12

Overall view

KNR Systems combines two areas, hydraulic robot core components and test equipment, and its supply to Boston Dynamics and its main contract in nuclear decommissioning are cited as evidence of technical credibility.

Filed results, however, show falling revenue since 2023 and operating losses that continue through the third quarter of 2026. The CEO's projection of more than double the 2026 revenue depends on how much revenue is recognized in Q4.

The share price is not explained by earnings-based metrics and rests on net assets and robot-business expectations. Whether those expectations translate into profit is the central question. The next checkpoints are the super humanoid unveiling at the end of 2026 and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.

The February 2026 convertible bond issue and the August 2026 release from investment-warning designation show a stock with high price volatility. This report is for information only and does not include a buy or sell opinion.
