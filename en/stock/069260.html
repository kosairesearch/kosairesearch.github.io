Revenue Recovers, but a Net Loss Keeps Margins in Focus

02

Business structure

TKG Huchems produces nitric acid, DNT (dinitrotoluene), MNB (mononitrobenzene) and other fine chemicals. As of the third quarter of 2025, the fine chemicals segment accounted for 95.9% of total revenue, with the electronic materials segment making up the rest, according to Business Post.

Products fall into the NT group (DNT, MNB) and the NA group (nitric acid, starter acid). IB Tomato reported in 2025 that the NT group made up 66% of revenue and the NA group 31%, while the NA group contributed 84% of operating profit.

The same report put the company's share of the domestic nitric acid market at about 90%. The company runs 14 production plants in the Yeosu industrial complex, producing about 3 million tons a year, with nitric acid capacity of 1.73 million tons a year, a September 2026 report said.

Major customers include Hanwha Solutions, OCI, BASF Korea and Kumho Mitsui Chemicals, under long-term supply contracts ranging from 15 to 30 years.

A nitric acid supply contract with an Indonesian company, disclosed on September 28, 2026, is worth KRW 337.8 billion, about 30% of recent revenue, and runs from March 31, 2029 to March 30, 2039. Yuanta Securities analyzed that the company's profit rises when ammonia, its key raw material, gets cheaper.

Shipments also move sharply with customers' scheduled maintenance shutdowns. Unlike commodity petrochemical companies, its focus on intermediates such as nitric acid and DNT limits direct comparisons with large petrochemical groups.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 1.24 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.05 trillion in 2023, a 14.8% decline and the smallest year of the 2022 to 2025 span, before rising 12.9% to KRW 1.19 trillion in 2024 and falling 5.1% to KRW 1.13 trillion in 2025.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 121.2 billion in 2023, up 3.5% from the prior year, and then declined 33.3% to KRW 80.8 billion in 2024 and 18.8% to KRW 65.6 billion in 2025, two consecutive years of decline.

The operating margin moved in the same direction, from 9.5% in 2022 and 11.5% in 2023 to 6.8% in 2024 and 5.8% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose 64.7% to KRW 134.8 billion in 2023, then fell to KRW 77.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 59.9 billion in 2025, also two consecutive years of decline.

Net income including non-controlling interests, at KRW 59.2 billion in 2025, is a different figure from controlling shareholders' net income. Operating cash flow went from KRW 221.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 79.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 102.4 billion in 2025.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, DNT shipments fell because a TDI customer carried out scheduled maintenance.

Quarterly, revenue went from KRW 291.7 billion in the third quarter of 2025 with operating profit of KRW 26.4 billion and controlling shareholders' net income of KRW 28.5 billion, to KRW 276.5 billion in the fourth quarter (down 5.2% from the prior quarter), with operating profit of KRW 4.61 billion (down 82.5%) and a controlling shareholders' net loss of KRW 1.33 billion.

The first quarter of 2026 recovered to revenue of KRW 294.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 15.2 billion (up 229.8%), and the second quarter reached revenue of KRW 397.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 32.8 billion (up 116.0%).

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue of KRW 411.2 billion (up 40.9% year on year) contrasted with operating profit of KRW 21.1 billion (down 20.0%), and the controlling shareholders' net loss of KRW 133.1 billion turned a year-earlier profit into a loss.

The gap between positive operating profit and a larger controlling shareholders' net loss appears to come from non-operating items and income tax, and the detailed breakdown is a point to check in the 2026 annual report.

05

Industry analysis

Urethane demand depends on raw materials such as TDI and MDI, so the supply situation in those downstream markets connects directly to TKG Huchems' volumes and revenue. Brokerage research has argued that damage to urethane facilities in the Middle East after the 2026 Iran conflict tightened supply.

Yuanta Securities expected the effect to become prominent in 2027. On the raw material side, Yuanta reported in a May 2026 report that ammonia prices rose from USD 379 in 2025 to USD 650 in 2026, and estimated that a drop to USD 350 to 450 in 2027 would add KRW 12 billion to 18 billion in operating profit.

Carbon credit prices rose from KRW 9,600 in 2025 to above KRW 20,000 in the second quarter of 2026, according to Yuanta's June 2026 report, which put the company's holdings at 4.45 million tons.

The company's high share of the domestic nitric acid market and its long-term supply contracts can be read as signs of its market position.

On the other hand, a resolution of the Middle East supply disruption could weaken the urethane tightness benefit, and because the portfolio centers on nitric acid and DNT, the company is highly exposed to changes in the downstream industry.

Any view on where the company sits in the cycle rests on brokerage forecasts and will need to be tested against 2027 results.

Risk factors

Raw Material Prices

Ammonia and other raw material prices can move margins depending on when price increases pass through. Yuanta Securities analyzed that profit rises when raw material prices fall, so rising inputs act as a burden. How much of the cost increase after March 2026 was absorbed by price hikes is a point to verify.

Customer Concentration and Demand Swings

Major customers include Hanwha Solutions, OCI and BASF Korea, and shipments shift with their maintenance schedules. If the Middle East urethane supply disruption eases, the current supply tightness benefit could weaken.

Because revenue is concentrated in a few product groups, a demand change in one product has a large effect on overall results.

Persistent Net Losses and Carbon Credit Variability

The controlling shareholders' net loss over the last four quarters accumulated to KRW 97.1 billion, and the third-quarter 2026 net loss widened to KRW 133.1 billion. Changes in carbon credit prices affect other revenue and profit.

Whether the net loss persists remains open until non-operating items and income tax effects appear in the periodic reports.

Overall view

The third quarter of 2026 combined revenue of KRW 411.2 billion, up 40.9% year on year, with operating profit of KRW 21.1 billion, so top-line recovery and a slowdown in profitability appeared together.

The controlling shareholders' net loss of KRW 133.1 billion turned a year-earlier profit into a loss, and the cumulative net loss over the last four quarters is KRW 97.1 billion. On an annual basis, operating profit fell for two consecutive years after the 2023 peak, leaving the 2025 operating margin at 5.8%.

Positive factors include revenue growth for three straight quarters, price increases, expected 2027 benefits from the Middle East urethane supply disruption, higher carbon credit prices and a debt-to-equity ratio of 28.6% at the end of 2025.

Negative factors include the possibility that net losses persist, revenue concentration in nitric acid and MNB, rising ammonia prices and shipment swings from customer maintenance.

Brokerage estimates assume an operating profit recovery in 2026, so assessments may diverge until profit trends are confirmed in fourth-quarter results and the 2026 annual report.

The points to check are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results expected in early 2027, the composition of non-operating items in the 2026 annual report, and the ammonia price trend in 2027, which together will indicate whether the company moves out of the net loss zone.
