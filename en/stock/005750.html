Remodeling Demand Lifts Profits; Quarterly Swings and Governance Remain Watchpoints

Daelim Bath, a leading domestic sanitaryware maker, has lifted operating profit for three consecutive years through 2025 on premium and remodeling demand, but operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders both fell year on year in Q3 2026, and governance issues remain open.

02

Business structure

Daelim Bath is a bath specialist whose core products are sanitaryware and faucets and fittings.

Its operations are split into the Bath segment and the Furniture segment, and a press report based on the annual report filed in March 2026 put Bath segment 2025 revenue at KRW 195.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 16.8 billion.

The Bath segment therefore accounts for most of consolidated operating profit of KRW 18.6 billion. The Furniture segment was reported to have an operating margin below 0.5%, so its contribution to profit is small.

Within the Bath segment, toilets and faucets are the core products, and the company said sales of both rose in 2025, with toilet sales growing especially fast.

Customers fall into two groups: B2B buyers supplying sanitaryware and faucets to residential complexes, and B2C buyers who visit the online mall Daelim Mall or its showrooms. Daelim Mall sales rose 82% in 2025, and the company said roughly 30% of showroom visitors go on to buy.

The company is expanding its high-end FÜLEN brand into premium residential and landmark projects, and large Gangnam redevelopment supply contributed to results. Distribution is widening through Coupang Rocket Installation, where 14 products were on sale, and fashion and lifestyle platforms such as Ably and 29CM.

In domestic sanitaryware, the company holds the leading position by a wide margin over the No. 2 player. Its BK segment handles bath and kitchen interior construction and its Care segment runs rental services, and reports say both helped first-half 2026 results.

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated revenue rose from KRW 253.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 266.3 billion in 2023 (up 5.0%), KRW 281.1 billion in 2024 (up 5.5%) and KRW 300.6 billion in 2025 (up 6.9%), recording three consecutive years of increases from 2023 to 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 1.45 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.06 billion in 2023, KRW 10.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 18.6 billion in 2025, also three consecutive years of increases from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

The operating margin climbed from 0.6% in 2022 to 1.5% in 2023, 3.6% in 2024 and 6.2% in 2025. Because operating profit grew 84.4% in 2025 against 6.9% revenue growth, margin improvement drove more of the profit gain than sales expansion did. The company attributed the improvement to higher sales and cost reductions.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 809 million in 2022 and a loss of KRW 4.65 billion in 2023, turned to a profit of KRW 5.36 billion in 2024, and reached KRW 13.4 billion in 2025, up 149.5%.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 13.5 billion in 2025, a different figure that should be read separately.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 7.29 billion in 2022 to KRW 18.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 20.7 billion in 2024, then fell to KRW 13.5 billion in 2025, so cash generation lagged profit growth that year.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 113.7% in 2022 to 93.0% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 175.4 billion, of which KRW 167.8 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 163.1 billion.

Quarterly operating profit fell from KRW 7.53 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 2.63 billion in Q4 2025, down 65.1% from the prior quarter, then rose to KRW 5.58 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 6.5 billion in Q2 2026 before reaching KRW 6.4 billion in Q3 2026, down 1.6% from Q2.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 77.6 billion was up 4.5% year on year, but operating profit fell 15.0%, so sales growth did not carry through to profit that quarter. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, totaled KRW 14.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In domestic sanitaryware, Daelim Bath held a 61.8% share in 2025, 36.5 percentage points ahead of the No. 2 player, according to annual shipment data published by the ceramic and tile cooperative.

On the demand side, the new-build apartment market has weakened while packaged replacement demand that covers the whole bathroom has grown. The company expects domestic remodeling growth to continue, citing aging housing stock and demand for better living conditions.

Analysis of 2025 results also indicated that replacement and upgrade demand mattered more than a rebound in new construction. In cycle terms, the business is shifting its weight toward replacement demand rather than new-build volumes, and if new-build recovery is slow, volume growth may stay limited.

On competition, the company pairs premium high-value products with online and showroom channels to differentiate itself. Past press reports pointed out that R&D spending as a share of sales lagged the industry average, raising concern about product speed relative to rivals.

Overseas, the company listed its filter shower head on Amazon in the United States in August 2025 and said it is reviewing possible North American expansion. Given its high domestic share, results from overseas expansion will be a useful gauge of diversification.

06

Outlook

Three plans have been publicly stated by the company. First, it plans to widen its Coupang Rocket Installation range in 2026 with items such as rain shower sets and urinals. Second, after listing on Ably and 29CM, it intends to keep expanding its distribution through vertical commerce and specialist platforms.

Third, it plans to scale up overseas market entry in stages. Daelim Mall sales rose 72% year on year in the first half of 2026, with bidet sales up 110%, the company said on July 2, 2026.

According to data compiled by WiseReport, consolidated revenue in the first half of 2026 rose 3.1%, operating profit rose 42.9% and net profit rose 49.9%. Drivers of the first-half improvement included wider supply to premium residential complexes and hotels and large Gangnam redevelopment orders.

However, Q3 2026 operating profit fell despite higher revenue, so whether sales growth converts into profit is the next point to check. The FY2026 annual report, expected around mid-March 2027, will show whether the annual operating margin holds at the 2025 level of 6.2%.

The Q1 2027 quarterly report, expected in mid-May 2027, will show how operating profit moves against the KRW 6.5 billion and KRW 6.4 billion of Q2 and Q3 2026. Overseas entry remains at the review stage for North America, and its revenue contribution has not yet been confirmed in filings.

08

Bull factors

Premium and remodeling demand improved the profit structure

The operating margin rose from 0.6% in 2022 to 6.2% in 2025, while the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 113.7% to 93.0% over the same period. The company said premium products and packaged bathroom replacement demand supported results.

Operating cash flow was KRW 13.5 billion in 2025, below KRW 20.7 billion in 2024 but well above KRW 7.29 billion in 2022.

Leading position in domestic sanitaryware

Domestic sanitaryware shipment data put the company's 2025 market share at 61.8%, with a 36.5 percentage point gap to the No. 2 player. The leading position rests on its B2B supply network and production base, which can absorb volume when replacement demand rises. Diversification opportunities, including overseas listings of filter shower heads, also remain open.

Fast growth in online and B2C channels

Daelim Mall sales grew 82% in 2025 and 72% year on year in the first half of 2026. Bidet sales rose 110% in the first half of 2026 and led that growth. Wider consumer touchpoints through Coupang Rocket Installation and fashion platform listings are cited as a basis for results.

09

Bear factors

Profit softening in Q3 2026

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 6.4 billion, down 15.0% year on year, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.84 billion, down 33.0%. Revenue rose 4.5% over the same period, but the operating margin was lower than a year earlier.

Q4 2025 operating profit of KRW 2.63 billion, down 65.1% from the prior quarter, shows how widely quarterly profit can swing.

Governance and disclosure issues

The auditor resigned mid-term on January 15, 2026, before completing the term. A press report put total pay for five registered directors and auditors in the 2025 business year at KRW 10.35 billion, well above the KRW 2 billion pay ceiling approved at the March 2025 shareholders' meeting. An April 2026 report referred to a BBB- credit rating and said the company did not respond to press questions.

Weak new-build market and a low-margin segment

With the new-build apartment market weak, the company depends more on replacement demand. The Furniture segment was reported to have an operating margin below 0.5%. Earlier press coverage also flagged that R&D spending lagged the industry average, which could weigh on new-product competition.

10

Risk factors

Quarterly earnings volatility

Quarterly operating profit fell from KRW 7.53 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 2.63 billion in Q4, then recovered to KRW 5.58 billion in Q1 2026. Even as annual profit rises, wide quarter-to-quarter swings make the trend harder to read. Q3 2026, when revenue rose while profit fell, is a clear example.

Governance and audit structure

The mid-term resignation of the auditor has raised concern about a gap in audit oversight. Press reports on total director pay relative to the approved ceiling could lead to debate over how profits are distributed.

Reports on the credit rating and the lack of responses to press questions are factors that could affect external assessment and financing conditions.

Construction cycle and channel execution

If the new-build downturn persists, B2B supply volumes could shrink. Growth in online and showroom channels is concentrated in Daelim Mall, so a slowdown there could change the earnings trend.

Overseas entry and product range expansion are still at the plan stage, so their execution pace and profitability remain uncertain.

12

Overall view

Daelim Bath used its 61.8% share of domestic sanitaryware in 2025 to capture premium and remodeling demand, lifting its operating margin from 0.6% in 2022 to 6.2% in 2025.

Revenue of KRW 300.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 18.6 billion in 2025 both rose from the prior year, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell to 93.0%. However, in Q3 2026 revenue rose 4.5% while operating profit fell 15.0%, so whether the earnings recovery holds quarter to quarter is still an open question.

On governance, the auditor's mid-term resignation in January 2026 and press reports on director pay are matters to review separately from results.

Strengths include market leadership, online channel growth and margin improvement, while weaknesses include quarterly profit swings, a weak new-build market and governance concerns. Multiples sit below the industry average, which may partly reflect how the market is weighing profit volatility and governance issues.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the FY2026 annual report and quarterly operating profit in the Q1 2027 report.
