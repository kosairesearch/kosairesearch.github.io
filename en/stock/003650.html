02

Business structure

Michang OIL IND is a specialty oil maker centered on lubricants, rubber compounding oils and electrical insulating oils. By product mix, lubricants account for about 72% of sales, rubber compounding oils about 21% and electrical insulating oils about 3%, and a separate data aggregator puts lubricants at 73%.

By export mix at December 2025, lubricants were 53.18% exports and 46.82% domestic, rubber compounding oils were 90.11% exports, and electrical insulating oils were 37.11% exports, so rubber compounding oils depend heavily on overseas buyers.

Production runs through the Busan and Ulsan plants, which are also being used for coolant production. The new business is a non-conductive immersion-cooling coolant aimed at heat management demand from AI data centers.

The company is expanding development and production cooperation with ENEOS and has invested KRW 1.5 billion in a data-center immersion-cooling solutions firm to formalize coolant supply cooperation. The coolant's revenue contribution is not yet visible in reported figures.

Among listed peers, SK Innovation and S-Oil run large refining-centered businesses, while Hankook Shell OIL, Kukdong OIL & Chemicals and Daewon operate in the same sector. Michang OIL IND is differentiated by its specialty-oil product mix rather than by refining scale.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales were KRW 418.8 billion in 2022, KRW 409.2 billion in 2023, KRW 431.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 405.5 billion in 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year for sales and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 48 billion in 2023, then fell 4.9% to KRW 45.6 billion in 2024 and 24.3% to KRW 34.6 billion in 2025, a second straight annual decline. Operating margin moved from 11.7% in 2023 to 8.5% in 2025.

Aggregator data shows gross profit falling from about KRW 59.6 billion in 2024 to about KRW 48.1 billion in 2025, pointing to both lower sales and higher cost pressure.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 47.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 52.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 68.9 billion in 2025, a third straight annual increase. Net income in 2025 equaled profit attributable to controlling shareholders at KRW 68.9 billion.

The gap between falling operating profit and rising net income points to non-operating items, which aggregator data shows rising to about KRW 63.2 billion in 2025 from about KRW 44.2 billion in 2024.

A KRW 30.3 billion private equity fund redemption was also reported in November 2025, and how much of such one-off items is in the figures should be checked in the periodic report notes.

Quarterly sales bottomed at KRW 94.3 billion in Q4 2025, down 11.1% from the prior quarter, and then rose to KRW 101.1 billion, KRW 135.9 billion and KRW 142.6 billion in Q1, Q2 and Q3 2026, three straight quarterly gains.

Operating profit moved to KRW 13.7 billion, KRW 8.16 billion and KRW 12.9 billion over the same three quarters. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 79.4 billion in Q2 2026, far above that quarter's operating profit, and turned into a KRW 28.5 billion net loss in Q3 2026. The sum of profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters is KRW 87.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for Michang OIL IND's core products comes from automotive, marine and industrial equipment users for lubricants, and from electrical and rubber processing users for specialty oils.

The company's earnings commentary says that falling sales volumes from weaker domestic and overseas economies were offset by a focus on specialty oil production, product upgrades and new product development. The same commentary notes that crude oil prices and exchange rates make earnings volatile.

The periodic report shows a domestic market share of 8.4% in 2023, 8.5% in 2024 and 8.4% in 2025, a largely stable position. Among listed peers, SK Innovation and S-Oil are far larger refining-centered businesses, while Hankook Shell OIL, Kukdong OIL & Chemicals and Daewon are compared on product mix.

A newer demand source is immersion-cooling coolant for AI data centers. The cycle position of refining and lubricant markets depends on the direction of crude prices, exchange rates and industrial output indicators.

A specialty-oil-centered structure makes product fit for each customer more important than competing on commodity refined volume.

Outlook

According to company commentary, the focus remains on specialty oil production, and sales of electrical insulating oils and rubber compounding oils are expected to grow on an established market base. Product upgrades and new product development are cited as growth drivers.

The coolant business is building a production setup using the Busan and Ulsan plants. Cooperation with ENEOS is being expanded, and a smart-box unit has been introduced for R&D use at a data-center partner to verify the technology. Commercial timing and sales scale for the coolant have not been disclosed in figures.

The first point to watch is whether the three straight quarters of sales growth continue. The second is whether operating margin recovers from 8.5% in 2025.

Operating profit rose to KRW 12.9 billion in Q3 2026, but a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders appeared, so whether non-operating items normalize will largely decide the earnings path. Crude oil prices and exchange rates remain variables to track.

The nearest formal checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, which will show full-year operating margin and the makeup of non-operating items.

Bear factors

Operating profit fell 4.9% to KRW 45.6 billion in 2024 and then 24.3% to KRW 34.6 billion in 2025. Sales fell 6.1% in 2025, and operating margin dropped to 8.5% from 11.7% in 2023. Lower sales and higher cost pressure appeared together.

Q3 2026 Net Loss Attributable to Controlling Shareholders

The company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 28.5 billion in Q3 2026. Operating profit rose to KRW 12.9 billion in the same quarter, but Q2 2026 profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 79.4 billion far exceeded that quarter's KRW 8.16 billion operating profit. Swings in non-operating items make the quarterly earnings path unstable.

Lower Volumes and Crude and FX Swings

Company commentary says weaker domestic and overseas economies reduced lubricant volumes, and crude prices and exchange rates keep earnings volatile. Rubber compounding oils were 90.11% exports as of December 2025, showing high dependence on overseas demand.

10

Risk factors

Feedstock and FX Risk

Crude oil prices and exchange rates affect both feedstock costs and selling prices. The company itself cites them as sources of earnings volatility. In periods when price pass-through is difficult, gross profit can decline as it did in 2025.

Reliance on Non-Operating Income

A large part of the 2025 net income increase appears linked to non-operating items. A KRW 30.3 billion private equity fund redemption was also reported in November 2025. Whether such items recur is uncertain, and they should be separated from core operating strength.

Coolant Commercialization and Export Concentration

Commercial timing and revenue scale for the coolant have not yet been shown in figures. If the new business does not grow as expected, it may not offset slower profitability in the core business. High overseas sales in rubber compounding oils mean demand shifts in specific export markets feed directly into results.

Overall view
