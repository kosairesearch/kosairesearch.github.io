Report overview

Materials shift lifts profit; parts mix is the next test

FNS Tech is shifting its mix from display equipment toward semiconductor materials and parts, and its operating profit rose for three straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026, reaching KRW 6.2 billion.

02

Business structure

FNS Tech was founded in March 2002 and supplies semiconductor and display equipment as well as parts and materials. The company reports as a single segment, but its operations split into equipment manufacturing and parts and materials.

In equipment, it supplies wet tools such as etchers, strippers and cleaners used on OLED production lines, and it has kept supplying OLED lines since 2009. The core of parts and materials is the CMP pad, a consumable that planarizes wafer surfaces, and the company localized this product in 2015.

A reusable pad developed jointly with Samsung Electronics, for which a joint patent was filed in September 2019, was later delivered to Samsung Electronics' Hwaseong Line 17.

The UV lamps used in ultrapure water (UPW) processes are made through a Taiwan subsidiary, and company officials have stressed profit improvement centered on semiconductor parts and materials. Materials and parts sales reached KRW 19.5 billion in the first half of 2026, or 72.3% of total sales.

Company officials said in March 2026 that its customer base has diversified from a Samsung Display focus to include Samsung Electronics, Applied Materials, TSMC and Micron.

Listed semiconductor peers include equipment makers Wonik IPS, PSK and Jusung Engineering and materials makers Soulbrain and Tokai Carbon Korea, which serve as comparison points.

Equipment sales move with the timing of display customers' line investments, while parts and materials see recurring demand as long as production continues, according to the company's own explanation.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales moved from KRW 67.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 38.9 billion in 2023, then KRW 88.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 73.7 billion in 2025. Sales in 2023 fell 42.5% year on year and were the smallest year of 2022 to 2025, while 2024, up 127.6% to KRW 88.5 billion, was the largest.

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 2.28 billion in 2023, down 60.8%, and then rose to KRW 8.81 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.3 billion in 2025, increasing for two consecutive years. The operating margin went from 8.6% in 2022 and 5.9% in 2023 to 10.0% in 2024 and 14.1% in 2025, the highest of the period.

Profit attributable to owners was largest in 2024 at KRW 14 billion, up 696.4%, but fell 37.9% to KRW 8.69 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 9.16 billion in 2025, which differs from profit attributable to owners, so the two measures should be read separately.

In 2025 operating profit rose while profit attributable to owners fell, so the effects of non-operating items and non-controlling interests should be checked in the notes to the annual business report. Operating cash flow declined from KRW 14.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.15 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 44.4% in 2022 to 66.4% in 2024 before falling to 39.3% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 87.5 billion, equity attributable to owners was KRW 83.8 billion and total liabilities were KRW 34.4 billion.

Quarterly operating profit bottomed at KRW 1.48 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, down 53.2% from the prior quarter, and has risen for three consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026.

Sales bottomed at KRW 12.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 14 billion and then KRW 21.2 billion in the third quarter. Profit attributable to owners in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 6.47 billion, up 120.0% from the prior quarter and 119.5% year on year.

The KRW 6.2 billion operating profit for the same quarter exceeded the KRW 5.2 billion estimate SK Securities published in its October 31, 2026 report.

05

Industry analysis

Industry coverage in the press and by brokers shares a common view that semiconductor demand is being driven by high-bandwidth memory (HBM), fueled by AI accelerators and data centers. Rising HBM output is said to lift demand for UV lamps used in UPW processes and for CMP pads.

To meet this shift, the company is developing HBM pads and glass-substrate polishing pads, and analysts note the distinction between development and mass-production adoption at customers.

The display equipment business is exposed to customers' line investment timing, and the company's results swung sharply during past display investment gaps. A past brokerage analysis showed sales falling from KRW 69.4 billion in 2017 to KRW 31.4 billion in 2019.

This is why equipment and parts businesses are described as offsetting different industry cycles. Export share reached 29.9% in the first half of 2026, up from 0.4% a year earlier. The CMP pad is cited as a differentiator because it localized a material previously imported.

Listed peers in the same sector, including Wonik IPS, PSK, Jusung Engineering and HANMI Semiconductor, along with materials makers Soulbrain and Tokai Carbon Korea, serve as reference points in the value chain.

The current position sits where recovery in display capital spending overlaps with a rising share of semiconductor materials.

06

Outlook

In March 2026, company officials said parts and materials sales, about KRW 24 billion in 2025, are expected to reach KRW 35 billion this year and KRW 55 billion later. First-half 2026 parts and materials sales of KRW 19.5 billion are more than half of that KRW 35 billion target.

The same officials said full-year consolidation of the Taiwan subsidiary in 2026 will bring growth effects into full view. Expansion of the SK hynix lamp business was also cited as a growth factor.

In an October 31, 2026 report, SK Securities expected the third-quarter operating margin to rise to about 27.4% as the materials and parts mix grows. That estimate predates the disclosure, so it serves as a reference to compare against reported figures.

HBM pads and glass-substrate polishing pads are currently described as being in development, so whether they move into customer mass-production lines after qualification is the next checkpoint.

The 2026 annual business report, the next regular filing, will show whether parts and materials sales and the operating margin meet company expectations. Given that the 2025 full-year provisional results were announced on February 4, 2026, the 2026 provisional results are expected in early February 2027. Changes in equipment orders and their effect on the full-year total also need to be tracked.

Bull factors

Materials and parts at 72.3% of sales

Materials and parts sales reached KRW 19.5 billion in the first half of 2026, or 72.3% of sales, versus 31.4% a year earlier. Press coverage describes this structural change as reducing the volatility of equipment sales.

SK Securities projected that a larger materials and parts share would lift the third-quarter 2026 operating margin.

Third-quarter operating profit beat estimate

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 6.2 billion rose 64.8% from the prior quarter and has increased for three consecutive quarters. It exceeded the KRW 5.2 billion estimate in SK Securities' October 31, 2026 report. Profit attributable to owners of KRW 6.47 billion was up 119.5% year on year.

Customer diversification and new pads

Company officials said in March 2026 that customers now extend beyond Samsung Electronics to Applied Materials, TSMC and Micron. Development of HBM pads and glass-substrate polishing pads is viewed as applying CMP pad technology to new markets. Full-year consolidation of the Taiwan subsidiary in 2026 was also cited as a growth driver.

Bear factors

Large swings in quarterly profit

Fourth-quarter 2025 operating profit of KRW 1.48 billion fell 53.2% from the prior quarter, and profit attributable to owners of KRW 753 million fell 74.5%. Annual profit attributable to owners fell 37.9% to KRW 8.69 billion in 2025. Profit recovery has continued across three quarters of 2026, but quarterly swings remain large.

Dependence on display capex cycles

The equipment business has seen sales swing sharply with the investment timing of large customers such as Samsung Display. Sales fell from KRW 69.4 billion in 2017 to KRW 31.4 billion in 2019 in a past episode. If equipment orders pause, the question is whether parts and materials growth can offset the gap.

Lower sales and uncertainty for new products

Press tallies indicate first-half 2026 sales fell 37.1% year on year. HBM and glass-substrate pads remain in development, and it is too early to judge whether they will reach customer mass production. Analysts point to the revenue contribution of new products as the key metric for future results.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration

The main CMP pad customer is reported to be Samsung Electronics, and equipment sales have depended on Samsung Display investment. A delay in one customer's capital spending can move quarterly sales directly. Diversification is under way, but customer-level revenue shares must be checked in regulatory filings.

Qualification timing for new products

Analysts note that semiconductor materials can face long quality and reliability qualification periods. Development completion and mass-production adoption for HBM and glass-substrate pads are separate stages, so revenue contribution may come later. The gap between product development and customer qualification could push back when revenue appears.

Overseas subsidiary and currency

The Taiwan subsidiary acquisition has increased overseas sales, and the export share reached 29.9% in the first half of 2026. Overseas subsidiary results and currency translation affect parent-company profit and loss. Industry observers note that wider subsidiary profitability could strengthen the balance sheet.

12

Overall view

FNS Tech is shifting from a display-equipment base toward semiconductor materials and parts, and the change is showing up first in profitability metrics.

The materials and parts share reached 72.3% of sales in the first half of 2026, up from 31.4% a year earlier, and operating profit of KRW 6.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026 marked a third straight quarterly increase.

However, profit attributable to owners fell from KRW 14 billion in 2024 to KRW 8.69 billion in 2025, and the fourth quarter of 2025 marked a quarterly low, so earnings volatility is large.

The equipment business has a history of swings tied to Samsung Display's investment cycle, so the key question is how far the growth of parts and materials can offset weakness in equipment.

HBM and glass-substrate polishing pads are new growth drivers, but they are reported to be in development, and their revenue contribution is not yet visible. With the price-to-book ratio below 1x, interpretations of the multiple based on recent earnings point in different directions.

The next checkpoints are the early-February 2027 provisional full-year 2026 results and the parts and materials share and operating margin in the 2026 annual business report. This report is for information only and does not include a buy or sell opinion or a target price.
