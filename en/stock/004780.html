02

Business structure

DaeryukCan makes and sells metal packaging containers and gas products, and it is a mid-sized company founded in 1966. Its product lines include containers for lubricant oil, edible oil, paint, aerosols and butane gas, along with butane gas itself.

The headquarters is in Yeoksam-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, and the main plant is in Yeongin-myeon, Asan, South Chungcheong Province. One media report put the 2024 sales mix at aerosol containers 62%, general containers 34% and portable butane gas 4%.

The company has produced and sold portable butane fuel under its own brand since 1994.

Its CRV explosion-prevention patent technology has been described as the world's first of its kind that the company developed in-house, and the company filed patents in Korea and abroad after developing it in 2009, also receiving certification from UL, a US safety testing body.

In butane can production, major makers including other domestic producers lead the market, while output changes differ by company. The company had 437 employees as of June 30, 2026.

The customer base appears centered on demand from general and aerosol container users, and because the two segments have different raw material and demand drivers, they need to be tracked separately.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue went from KRW 276 billion in 2022 to KRW 271.1 billion in 2023 (down 1.8% year on year), KRW 274.7 billion in 2024 (up 1.3%) and KRW 266.6 billion in 2025 (down 2.9%). The largest year in the 2022-2025 period was 2022 and the smallest was 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 9.68 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.6 billion in 2023 (up 61.5%) and then to KRW 15.8 billion in 2024 (up 1.2%), before falling to KRW 10.3 billion in 2025 (down 34.9%).

The operating margin was 3.5% in 2022, 5.8% in 2023, 5.8% in 2024 and 3.9% in 2025, so the 2024 level was not regained in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached its high in 2023 at KRW 13.1 billion, then fell to KRW 11.5 billion in 2024 (down 11.9%) and KRW 9.18 billion in 2025 (down 20.3%), a two-year decline in a row. Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same levels in each year.

According to one media report, selling and administrative expenses rose 9.1% from KRW 16.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 18 billion in 2024, pressuring margins. The same report said cost of sales fell in 2023 and rose again in 2024, suggesting the cost savings were one-off.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 64.2 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 64.9 billion in the fourth quarter to KRW 69.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 78.2 billion in the second quarter, which was the peak (up 13.0% from the prior quarter).

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 57.4 billion, down 26.6% from the prior quarter and down 10.6% year on year.

Quarterly operating profit peaked at KRW 3.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then fell to KRW 2.34 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.37 billion in the third quarter, a two-quarter decline in a row. The third-quarter 2026 operating profit was down 58.5% year on year.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 388 million in the third quarter of 2026, down 79.6% from the prior quarter and down 87.5% year on year. The four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026 total KRW 8.28 billion in profit attributable to controlling shareholders. In the third quarter of 2026, revenue and profit fell at the same time.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for butane cans appears to move with LPG prices. One report noted that first-half 2026 butane can output rose temporarily even as LPG prices kept climbing, while some producers saw output move in different directions.

A media analysis said the aerosol and general container markets have entered maturity and that attempts to open new markets have failed, which is reflected in earnings. The same analysis put the three-year average growth rate at minus 0.2%, which it described as effectively stagnant.

Since 2024, poor results have been attributed to weak cost control and market saturation. In terms of competitive position, DaeryukCan ranks among the major butane can producers in Korea, while its main point of differentiation is technology.

The CRV explosion-prevention patent could serve as a safety-based differentiator. However, one report said R&D spending is below the industry average, which limits differentiation through technical innovation.

With the core market in a mature phase, cost control and selling and administrative efficiency are the main drivers of margins. Given limited market growth, the results of product diversification and new customer acquisition are the key variables.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Revenue and profit both fell in the third quarter of 2026 after the second-quarter revenue peak. When selling and administrative expenses are rising, falling revenue makes it harder to defend margins. The operating margin was 3.9% in 2025, below 5.8% in 2024, which may signal a narrower cost cushion.

Governance and disclosure discipline

The notice concerned a possible designation as an unfaithful disclosure company, which relates to delayed disclosure. Under exchange rules, accumulated penalty points above certain thresholds can lead to trading suspension or a listing review.

The company's accumulated penalty points over the past year were reported as zero, but whether penalty points are assessed for this case needs to be checked.

Raw materials and demand flow

Butane can output has been reported to move with LPG price trends. In the first half of 2026, output rose at some producers despite the LPG uptrend, while changes differed by company. If cost increases cannot be passed through to selling prices, margins could come under further pressure.
