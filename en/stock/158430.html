02

Business structure

Aton is a fintech security company founded in 1999, focusing on financial transaction authentication, private certificates, the PASS simple-authentication platform, smart finance system construction, and T-money IC chip sales.

By segment, a 2022 brokerage report put the fintech security solution unit at 38.5% of Q1 2022 revenue, the fintech platform unit at 19.3%, smart finance at 20.3%, T-money solutions at 0.8%, and others at 21.1%.

Data aggregator Butler classified Q2 2026 revenue as 47% services, 23% solutions, 15% manufacturing, 8% information and communication, and 6% used cars, but the classification differs from the 2022 scheme, so the two cannot be compared directly.

Major customers are large domestic banks, securities firms, and the three major telecom operators, according to a 2025 brokerage report. Revenue follows a sequence of system construction, licensing, and maintenance, per a 2024 brokerage report.

According to the FnGuide financial information site, the company has completed a next-generation authentication solution using the quantum-resistant cryptography standard certified by the US NIST, and it is preparing for cloud migration in the financial sector.

A July 2025 brokerage report described Aton as a partner to a Korean bank in a CBDC pilot covering 100,000 users. Because of its structure, revenue depends on financial-sector IT budgets and competition with large system integrators.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 44.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 55 billion in 2023 (up 23.0% year on year), KRW 65.4 billion in 2024 (up 18.9%), and KRW 67.6 billion in 2025 (up 3.3%), increasing three years in a row from 2023 through 2025. 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating profit rose from KRW 8.05 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.4 billion in 2023 (up 41.8%) and KRW 14.4 billion in 2024 (up 26.5%), then fell 28.3% to KRW 10.4 billion in 2025, and operating margin dropped from 22.1% in 2024 to 15.3% in 2025.

The company cited higher selling, general and administrative expenses tied to expanded business activity as the main factor behind 2025 earnings changes.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 17 billion in 2022, the largest year in the span, and that figure exceeded operating profit of KRW 8.05 billion, suggesting non-operating items contributed heavily.

It then moved to KRW 9.19 billion in 2023 (down 45.9%), KRW 10.5 billion in 2024 (up 14.8%), and KRW 1.55 billion in 2025 (down 85.3%). Quarterly, revenue reached KRW 16.3 billion in Q4 2025 but the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.23 billion, a swing from profit.

Q1 2026 revenue fell 8.7% to KRW 14.8 billion with operating profit of KRW 432 million, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 1.12 billion.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 17.4 billion, operating profit KRW 3.15 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 3.65 billion, but Q3 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 732 million and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.94 billion.

Over the latest four quarters the cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders totals KRW 398 million. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 68.2% in 2022 to 39.3% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 104.4 billion at end-2025, of which KRW 99.3 billion belongs to controlling shareholders.

Operating cash flow, however, dropped from KRW 14.2 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.56 billion in 2025. Per a Q3 2026 filing summary, the profit decline reflects cost growth alongside revenue growth.

05

Industry analysis

Aton operates in fintech security, centered on financial transaction authentication and electronic signatures. After the mandatory use of public certificates ended in 2016, financial institutions could issue their own certificates, and demand for private authentication grew, according to a 2019 research note.

A 2022 brokerage report also cited steady demand growth for private certificates as a driver of the fintech platform unit.

For Q1 2025, preparation for financial cloud environment solutions and expansion of the corporate private certificate market were cited as revenue drivers, while a limited profitability view was noted due to rising costs.

On digital assets, one brokerage argued that system-building demand could rise private-sector if stablecoin rules are institutionalized, and the company pointed to its CBDC system reference as a basis. That scenario depends on the content and timing of the institutionalization legislation.

In the same KOSDAQ security space, companies such as Ahnlab (Ahnlab) compete, but Aton's business is concentrated on financial-sector authentication and its simple-authentication platform.

Because its customers are mainly large banks, securities firms, and telecom operators, it is exposed to the investment cycle in financial-sector IT.

06

Outlook

The business directions Aton has confirmed fall into three areas. First, it has completed a next-generation authentication solution applying the quantum-resistant cryptography standard. Second, it is preparing for cloud migration demand in the financial sector, according to the FnGuide company profile.

Third, a brokerage report noted the company's plan to extend its CBDC pilot experience into digital asset authentication, including stablecoins. When and how much this translates into revenue should be confirmed through segment revenue disclosures in periodic reports.

In recent quarters, Q3 2026 revenue rose to KRW 17.7 billion, but operating profit was only KRW 732 million, so whether revenue growth is absorbed by the cost structure is the key question.

It needs to be determined through upcoming disclosures whether the KRW 3.15 billion operating profit in Q2 2026 was a temporary recovery or part of a structure with large quarterly swings.

On an annual basis, operating margin fell to 15.3% in 2025 from 22.1% in 2024, so the operating margin in the 2026 annual report is the first checkpoint. The second is the ratio of selling, general and administrative expenses, and the third is whether operating cash flow recovers from KRW 1.56 billion in 2025.

Volatility in equity-method and valuation gains and losses is also a variable that will shape the direction of net income attributable to controlling shareholders.

Bull factors

Authentication technology development completed

According to the FnGuide company profile, Aton has completed a next-generation authentication solution applying the NIST-certified quantum-resistant cryptography standard. With cloud migration preparation under way in the financial sector, there is room for upgrades to products sold to existing financial clients. Its revenue contribution still needs to be confirmed through segment disclosures.

Earnings recovery in Q2 2026

Q2 2026 operating profit rose 628.6% from the prior quarter to KRW 3.15 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose 225.2% to KRW 3.65 billion. Revenue in the same quarter increased 17.5% from Q1 to KRW 17.4 billion. Q3 2026 revenue held at KRW 17.7 billion, so the revenue base has been maintained.

Easing financial leverage

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 39.3% at end-2025 from 68.2% in 2022, with total liabilities of KRW 41.1 billion and total equity of KRW 104.4 billion. Equity attributable to controlling shareholders accounts for KRW 99.3 billion of that total. Lower debt can serve as a financial cushion during periods of earnings volatility.

Bear factors

Weaker earnings base

Operating profit fell 28.3% to KRW 10.4 billion in 2025, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 85.3% to KRW 1.55 billion. Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 732 million, down 78.0% year on year.

The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totals KRW 398 million.

Cost and non-operating pressure

The company cited higher selling, general and administrative expenses as a main factor in 2025 earnings changes. Coverage of the same disclosure also noted rising equity-method losses and valuation losses. In Q4 2025 the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.23 billion.

Slower cash generation

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 11 billion in 2022 and KRW 14.2 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.56 billion in 2025. If revenue growth does not convert into cash, working capital and investment burdens could grow. Large quarterly swings in operating profit have made the earnings direction hard to gauge.

10

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly profit swings sharply. Operating profit went from KRW 432 million in Q1 2026 to KRW 3.15 billion in Q2 and back down to KRW 732 million in Q3. If profit does not keep pace with revenue, the annual earnings trend could weaken.

Regulatory timing

Demand from digital asset applications depends on the content and timeline of stablecoin legislation. One brokerage report also listed this as a key risk factor. If institutionalization is delayed, the timing of converting the CBDC reference into revenue could also slip.

Customer concentration and competition

Major customers are concentrated among large banks, securities firms, and telecom operators, so results are sensitive to changes in financial-sector IT budgets. Competition with large system integrators could also constrain new contract pricing and revenue growth. Overlap with other KOSDAQ security companies adds competitive pressure.

12

Overall view

Aton is a company focused on financial transaction authentication and fintech security solutions, and its revenue rose from KRW 44.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 67.6 billion in 2025.

Earnings were volatile over the same period, however, with 2025 operating profit falling 28.3% to KRW 10.4 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders falling 85.3% to KRW 1.55 billion.

In 2026, revenue rose in Q2 and Q3 after a Q1 low, but Q3 operating profit fell 78.0% year on year to KRW 732 million and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.94 billion.

Bullish factors include completion of a next-generation authentication solution and a debt-to-equity ratio that fell to 39.3%, while bearish factors include consecutive earnings declines and shrinking operating cash flow. Demand from digital assets such as stablecoins has a direction but an uncertain timeline.

The most important observation points are whether the Q2 2026 operating profit recovery continues and whether the cost structure absorbs revenue growth. The next checkpoint is the operating margin and operating cash flow in the 2026 annual report. This report contains no buy or sell opinion and no target price and is provided for information only.
