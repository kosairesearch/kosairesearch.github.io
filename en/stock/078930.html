Report overview

Refining Recovery and AI Data Center Plans in Focus

GS reported quarterly operating profit above KRW 1.26 trillion, KRW 1.72 trillion and KRW 1.27 trillion in the first three quarters of 2026, yet annual operating profit declined from 2022 through 2025. Whether the recovery holds and whether the AI data center plan becomes an operating business are the key questions.

02

Business structure

GS is a holding company under the Monopoly Regulation and Fair Trade Act, operating under a structure that began when it separated from LG Group in 2004.

The holding company holds controlling stakes in major subsidiaries including GS Energy (100%), GS Retail (58.62%), GS Global (50.78%), GS E&R (89.67%) and GS EPS (70%). GS Energy, the center of the energy business, owns 50% of GS Caltex and 51% of GS Power, while Chevron holds the remaining GS Caltex shares.

According to press reports, GS Caltex generated revenue of roughly KRW 42 trillion to KRW 45 trillion a year over the last three years, with refining and lubricants at the center of its earnings.

GS Retail, which runs convenience stores, was reported in April 2026 to maintain revenue of about KRW 12 trillion and operating profit of about KRW 300 billion. Holding company earnings depend on subsidiary results and dividend inflows, so refining and power subsidiaries drive the parent's results.

The group's official profile groups the businesses into energy and power, retail and services, and construction and infrastructure, but several listed affiliates have no equity link to the holding company, which adds to governance uncertainty.

The largest shareholder and related parties hold a majority of the holding company's shares. Within power, subsidiaries such as GS EPS and GS E&R are sensitive to wholesale electricity prices.

Peers include other large holding companies with refining, chemicals and power affiliates, and differences in subsidiary portfolios shape valuation comparisons.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 28.8 trillion in 2022 to KRW 26 trillion in 2023 (down 9.7% year on year), KRW 25.3 trillion in 2024 (down 2.6%) and KRW 25.2 trillion in 2025 (down 0.4%).

Revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit fell from KRW 5.01 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.72 trillion in 2023 (down 25.6%), KRW 3.06 trillion in 2024 (down 17.8%) and KRW 2.94 trillion in 2025 (down 4.1%).

Operating profit fell faster than revenue, so the operating margin narrowed from 17.4% in 2022 to 14.3%, 12.1% and 11.7% in the following years.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 2.14 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.3 trillion in 2023 (down 39.3%) and KRW 567 billion in 2024 (down 56.4%), then rose to KRW 798.7 billion in 2025 (up 40.9%).

Consolidated net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.04 trillion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder number.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 105.7% in 2022 to 86.0% at the end of 2025, when total equity was KRW 19.2 trillion, of which KRW 14.7 trillion was attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 16.5 trillion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 2.74 trillion in 2022, KRW 3.02 trillion in 2023, KRW 2.43 trillion in 2024 and KRW 2.19 trillion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue and operating profit rose from KRW 6.53 trillion and KRW 873.3 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 6.84 trillion and KRW 1.26 trillion in Q1 2026, a 62.1% quarter-on-quarter rise in operating profit.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 7.41 trillion with operating profit of KRW 1.72 trillion, up 36.5% from the prior quarter, the largest operating profit among the last five quarters. Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 1.27 trillion, down 25.8% from Q2 but up 46.0% year on year.

Heungkuk Securities had estimated Q3 operating profit of KRW 1.5 trillion in a September 1, 2026 report, and the disclosed figure came in below that. Refining profits move sharply with the industry cycle and inventory valuation, which widens quarter-to-quarter swings.

05

Industry analysis

Refining and lubricants are cited as the main driver of the 2026 earnings recovery. In a July 2026 report, BNK Investment & Securities expected second-quarter operating profit to beat consensus on strong refining and lubricant results.

The same report said GS's stable earnings from refining and power subsidiaries and its dividend appeal had been investment points, while weak growth had served as a reason for a discount on price-to-book. In the power market, weak system marginal prices (SMP) are weighing on power subsidiaries.

In a September 2026 report, Heungkuk Securities pointed to weak results at GS EPS and GS E&R, while stressing solid operating profit at GS Energy and GS Caltex. In retail, the GS Retail chief said at the March 2026 shareholder meeting that investment in AI and digital tools would keep expanding.

Refining margins move with crude prices and demand, so the position of the refining cycle is difficult to pin down. Still, operating profit above KRW 1 trillion in each quarter from Q1 to Q3 2026 marks a change in earnings capacity compared with 2025 quarters.

Among holding companies, those with large refining exposure and those with large power and new-business exposure have different earnings profiles, which frames sector comparisons. The industry read depends on the direction of two variables, refining margins and wholesale power prices.

Outlook

In a September 1, 2026 report, Heungkuk Securities estimated 2026 consolidated revenue at KRW 29.7 trillion and operating profit at KRW 5.7 trillion. The estimate rested mainly on strong results at GS Energy.

In a November 6, 2026 report, the same firm also expected GS Energy's strength to lift 2026 consolidated operating profit substantially.

Heungkuk Securities described the company as concentrating its business capacity on AI and cited a plan for a 2.4GW-class AI data center in Gangwon Province in its November 6, 2026 report.

BNK Investment & Securities said in its July 2026 report that GS was pursuing data center construction and operation, but noted that no confirmed or concrete details had yet emerged. The retail arm plans to keep expanding AI and digital investment to raise operating competitiveness.

The next checkpoint is whether investment decisions and financing plans for the AI data center become concrete in company disclosures. Whether full-year 2026 operating profit approaches the Heungkuk estimate can be checked in the 2026 annual business report.

Whether quarterly operating profit stays above KRW 1 trillion can also be checked against the fourth-quarter 2026 results. The duration of wholesale power price effects on GS EPS and GS E&R is another item to track.

Bull factors

Recovery in quarterly earnings

Operating profit was KRW 1.26 trillion in Q1, KRW 1.72 trillion in Q2 and KRW 1.27 trillion in Q3 2026, each above KRW 1 trillion. Q3 operating profit rose 46.0% year on year, and Q2 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was the largest among the last five quarters. Heungkuk Securities expects GS Energy's strength to lift 2026 consolidated operating profit substantially.

Dividend base and subsidiary value

BNK Investment & Securities noted that stable earnings from refining and power subsidiaries and dividend appeal have been GS's investment points. In a September 1, 2026 report, Heungkuk Securities raised its target to KRW 170,000, citing solid operating results and higher value of subsidiary stakes. BNK said it reflected higher book value in its target price.

AI data centers as a growth option

Heungkuk Securities cited a 2.4GW-class AI data center in Gangwon Province as central to GS's AI business in its November 6, 2026 report. BNK Investment & Securities also said in July 2026 that GS was pursuing data center construction and operation.

The bull case holds that if the new business becomes operating, the growth assessment of the holding company could change.

Bear factors

Earnings volatility and margin decline

Operating profit declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and the operating margin narrowed from 17.4% in 2022 to 11.7% in 2025. Q3 2026 operating profit fell 25.8% from the prior quarter. The bear case starts from quarterly profits swinging sharply with the refining cycle.

Gap in concrete AI data center details

BNK Investment & Securities said in its July 2026 report that no confirmed or concrete details on data centers had emerged. The 2.4GW plan has been cited, but whether investment decisions and financing plans follow in company disclosures remains to be verified. If the plan stays at the concept stage, its value as a growth option may be read as limited.

Subsidiary weakness and growth discount

In a September 2026 report, Heungkuk Securities said weak wholesale power prices were weighing on some subsidiaries, including GS EPS and GS E&R. BNK Investment & Securities noted that weak growth has been a reason for a discount on price-to-book. Heungkuk Securities presented KRW 115,000 on November 6, 2026, below its earlier target.

10

Risk factors

Achievement of estimates

Heungkuk Securities estimated 2026 consolidated revenue at KRW 29.7 trillion and operating profit at KRW 5.7 trillion, resting mainly on strong results at GS Energy. Its September estimate of KRW 1.5 trillion for Q3 operating profit was above the disclosed figure.

If quarterly operating profit does not hold above KRW 1 trillion in the fourth quarter, full-year results could fall short of that estimate. A wider gap between estimate and actual results could prompt a reassessment of the recovery view centered on GS Energy.

Retail digital investment

GS Retail, centered on convenience stores, is reported to maintain revenue of about KRW 12 trillion and operating profit of about KRW 300 billion. Its chief said at the March 2026 shareholder meeting that investment in AI and digital tools would keep expanding.

If this spending is recognized as cost first, convenience store margins could come under pressure and affect dividend inflows to the holding company. If the investment does not translate into revenue, the retail segment's contribution to profit may remain limited.

Governance uncertainty

Several affiliates sit outside the group, and some have no equity link to the holding company. Because holding company earnings depend on subsidiary results and dividend inflows, this gap in ownership links could affect how subsidiary profits and dividends flow up to the parent.

Under a structure in which the largest shareholder and related parties hold a majority of the holding company's shares, any change in that ownership base could alter the foundation of the holding company structure.

Overall view

GS's annual revenue and operating profit declined after 2022, with 2025 operating profit at KRW 2.94 trillion and the operating margin at 11.7%. In 2026, quarterly operating profit above KRW 1 trillion marked a recovery, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 40.9% in 2025.

The recovery leans heavily on refining and lubricants and on GS Energy's results. Quarterly profit remains volatile with the industry cycle, and operating profit fell 25.8% quarter on quarter in Q3 2026.

Heungkuk Securities cited a 2.4GW-class AI data center plan as a growth axis, while BNK Investment & Securities said in July 2026 that no concrete details had emerged.

Target prices differ widely by firm and date, such as KRW 100,000 from BNK Investment & Securities on July 3, 2026 and KRW 115,000 from Heungkuk Securities on November 6, 2026.

The earnings recovery argument and the holding company discount argument stand side by side, and the next checkpoints are fourth-quarter 2026 results, the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual business report, and AI data center investment decisions.
