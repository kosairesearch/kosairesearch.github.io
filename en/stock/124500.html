Report overview

Gold trading lifts revenue; profit base still being tested

Gold-linked Web3 operations account for most of Itcenglobal's revenue, but the company posted an operating loss in the third quarter of 2026, making the durability of profit the next key test.

02

Business structure

Itcenglobal is a holding-type IT company whose operations split into a Web3 segment and an IT services segment.

The core of the Web3 segment is the subsidiary Korea Gold Exchange, which handles the import, refining, processing, wholesale and retail, and export of physical gold and silver, alongside a gold trading platform and digital asset businesses such as RWA and STO.

Web3 segment revenue reached KRW 5.50 trillion in the first half of 2026, up 122.6% year on year, and its share of segment revenue rose to 90.96%, according to press coverage of regulatory filings.

However, 91.8% of that Web3 revenue came from Korea Gold Exchange alone, and the combined first-half 2026 revenue of the two digital asset affiliates was KRW 1.6 billion, about 0.03% of consolidated revenue. The Web3 segment figures include transactions between group companies.

The IT services segment consists of affiliates including ITCTS, Itcen NTEK, Itcen Cloit and Itcen PNS.

Based on company statements, ITCTS posted 2025 consolidated revenue of KRW 1.10 trillion and operating income of KRW 23.6 billion, while the NTEK affiliate posted revenue of KRW 572.0 billion and operating income of KRW 20.9 billion.

In the first half of 2026, IT services revenue rose 8.2% to KRW 546.0 billion and operating income reached KRW 11.1 billion, compared with an operating loss of KRW 3.1 billion a year earlier.

The segment mainly runs public and financial system projects and maintenance-oriented work, and it uses the in-house AI tools Intellicen Code and AgentGo in development.

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated revenue rose from KRW 2.62 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.82 trillion in 2023 (up 7.4%), KRW 4.96 trillion in 2024 (up 76.3%), and KRW 8.87 trillion in 2025 (up 78.8%), growing for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Over the same period operating profit went from KRW 30.4 billion to KRW 30.5 billion and KRW 58.6 billion before reaching KRW 279.9 billion in 2025 (up 378.0%), yet the operating margin only moved from 1.2% in 2022, 1.1% in 2023 and 1.2% in 2024 to 3.2% in 2025.

Revenue grew more than threefold while margins stayed thin, which reflects expansion centered on the physical gold trading business in the Web3 segment.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 1.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 650 million in 2023 (down 61.7%), then rose to KRW 4.25 billion in 2024 and KRW 46.6 billion in 2025, increasing for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests swung to a net loss of KRW 6.2 billion in 2023 before reaching KRW 198.3 billion in 2025, and the wide gap between that figure and the controlling-shareholder share indicates a large portion attributable to non-controlling interests.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 511.8 billion, of which the controlling shareholders held KRW 125.4 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 1.09 trillion, with the debt ratio rising from 202.6% in 2022 to 212.5% in 2025.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 123.3 billion in 2022 to a negative KRW 29.3 billion in 2023, then KRW 26.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 238.2 billion in 2025. The quarterly pattern is more volatile.

Revenue peaked at KRW 4.24 trillion in the fourth quarter of 2025, up 145.5% from the prior quarter, and then declined for three consecutive quarters to KRW 3.29 trillion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 2.29 trillion in the second and KRW 1.53 trillion in the third, which was down 11.4% from a year earlier.

Operating profit fell from KRW 160.1 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (up 148.3% from the prior quarter) to KRW 99.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 8.29 billion in the second (down 91.6%), and then an operating loss of KRW 6.21 billion in the third quarter, a shift into loss.

Fourth-quarter revenue equaled nearly half of full-year 2025 revenue, which shows how concentrated the year-end earnings are.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 65.7 billion, above the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 46.6 billion, but that total leans heavily on the KRW 26.2 billion posted in the fourth quarter of 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The direction of the company's earnings is closely tied to gold market volume and prices.

According to an analysis by Samsung Securities reported from an April 2026 report, the monthly average trading volume on the KRX gold market in the first quarter of 2026 was 14.4 tonnes, up 201% year on year, and trading value stayed near KRW 11.07 trillion, close to the fourth quarter of 2025.

The same analysis said the company's gold-related revenue shows a high correlation with KRX gold trading value. In March 2026 the company said that safe-haven demand for gold was rising amid geopolitical risk and that it would expand into real-asset-linked and tokenized securities markets.

Physical gold trading carries thin margins, so even as volume grows, operating margin does not rise as quickly as Web3 segment revenue. The information gathered does not allow a numerical comparison of the company's position in the gold trading market against competitors.

In IT services, demand for AI infrastructure is expanding, with high-performance computing, AI data centers and GPU servers cited as growth drivers for affiliates (ZDNet Korea report).

The IT services business competes in an area shared by large listed names such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, but most of the consolidated revenue comes from the gold-related Web3 segment rather than from IT services.

Samsung Securities also noted that IT services revenue is seasonal, concentrated in the second half and especially the fourth quarter, which makes first-half results comparatively weaker.

06

Outlook

In its announcement of third-quarter 2026 results, the company said both the Web3 and IT services businesses concentrate revenue and profit in the fourth quarter, and it expects a high full-year result (press coverage of a November 13, 2026 filing).

In the same statement, the company said some procedures related to the listing of the Web3 business are being delayed through discussions with relevant authorities, but that the overall schedule is not significantly disrupted.

In its first-half 2026 announcement, the company said it plans to pursue an investment brokerage license for beneficiary certificates and STO preliminary approval in the second half, and to expand STO-related business in stages (press coverage from August 2026).

In its March 2026 statement on 2025 results, the company set a goal of building a digital gold ecosystem worth KRW 10 trillion within five years on a digital gold and stablecoin platform.

However, most Web3 revenue still comes from physical gold operations, and the contribution from digital asset affiliates remains very small.

Filings also combine the Web3 and management and investment segments for operating profit, which makes it difficult to see from outside how much profit the digital asset businesses generate. Within IT services, the profit structure is improving.

One affiliate said that although its 2026 revenue growth was 2.6%, it lifted its gross margin from 5.0% to 9.7% and cut selling and administrative expenses by 23.6% (Digital Daily report). Another affiliate reported first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 274.7 billion and operating income of KRW 15.3 billion.

The next checkpoint is how fourth-quarter 2026 revenue and operating profit compare with the fourth quarter of 2025, and whether the third-quarter operating loss proves temporary.

08

Bull factors

Revenue growth tied to gold trading

First-half 2026 Web3 segment revenue reached KRW 5.50 trillion, up 122.6% year on year. Press coverage reported that KRX gold market trading volume rose 201% year on year in the first quarter of 2026. Samsung Securities said the company's gold-related revenue is highly correlated with KRX gold trading value.

Recovery in IT services profitability

The IT services segment posted first-half 2026 operating income of KRW 11.1 billion, turning from an operating loss of KRW 3.1 billion a year earlier. The company said IT services operating income rose more than 480% in the cumulative third quarter of 2026. Reports said one affiliate lifted its gross margin from 5.0% to 9.7%, with in-house AI tools cited as a driver.

Digital asset licensing in progress

The company is pursuing an investment brokerage license for beneficiary certificates and STO preliminary approval, and plans to expand STO-related business in stages. In its statement on 2025 results it set a goal of a KRW 10 trillion digital gold ecosystem within five years.

If approvals are finalized, it will become possible to see whether digital asset businesses can build a revenue base separate from physical gold.

09

Bear factors

Operating loss in the third quarter

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit turned into an operating loss of KRW 6.21 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 95.0% from the prior quarter to KRW 1.01 billion.

Revenue has declined for three consecutive quarters from the fourth-quarter 2025 peak of KRW 4.24 trillion to KRW 1.53 trillion in the third quarter of 2026. Compared with KRW 160.1 billion of operating profit in the fourth quarter of 2025, the direction of earnings has changed sharply.

Concentration of Web3 revenue

Of the KRW 5.50 trillion in Web3 segment revenue, 91.8% came from Korea Gold Exchange alone. The combined first-half 2026 revenue of the digital asset affiliates was KRW 1.6 billion, about 0.03% of consolidated revenue. The structure shows revenue that can swing sharply with gold prices and trading volume.

Thin margins and high leverage

The 2025 operating margin of 3.2% is higher than the 1.2% of 2022 but remains low. Total liabilities were KRW 1.09 trillion at the end of 2025, and the debt ratio rose to 212.5% from 202.6% in 2022.

Controlling shareholders' equity was KRW 125.4 billion of total equity of KRW 511.8 billion, leaving limited financial cushion.

10

Risk factors

Dependence on gold prices and volume

Analysis indicates Web3 segment revenue is linked to gold prices and KRX gold trading value. A drop in volume or a sharp price swing could move revenue and profit significantly. The third-quarter 2026 operating loss shows this volatility already feeding into results.

Financial structure and cash flow

The debt ratio was 212.5% in 2025, which is high. Operating cash flow was negative KRW 29.3 billion in 2023 and rose to KRW 238.2 billion in 2025, but it has varied widely between years.

Of 2025 net income of KRW 198.3 billion, KRW 46.6 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders, so the earnings available to shareholders depend heavily on the non-controlling share.

Regulatory and listing timing, plus seasonality

The company said some listing-related procedures for the Web3 business are delayed through discussions with authorities. The pace of digital asset expansion could depend on the outcome of the investment brokerage and STO preliminary approvals.

IT services revenue is seasonal and concentrated in the second half, especially the fourth quarter, so quarterly results need careful reading.

12

Overall view

Itcenglobal grew revenue from KRW 2.62 trillion in 2022 to KRW 8.87 trillion in 2025, and operating profit rose 378.0% to KRW 279.9 billion in 2025. Growth centers on the Web3 segment, including Korea Gold Exchange, which accounted for 91.8% of the KRW 5.50 trillion Web3 revenue in the first half of 2026.

The IT services segment returned to profit with first-half 2026 operating income of KRW 11.1 billion, which press coverage presented as a positive sign of a more diversified profit base.

On the other hand, the 2025 operating margin stayed at 3.2%, and the third quarter of 2026 brought an operating loss of KRW 6.21 billion, reversing the direction of quarterly profit. The debt ratio was 212.5% in 2025, and controlling shareholders' equity is roughly a quarter of total equity.

Digital asset businesses are at the licensing stage with a still very small revenue contribution.

Bullish factors include gold trading growth, the recovery in IT services profitability and progress on digital asset licensing, while bearish factors include the third-quarter loss, concentration of Web3 revenue, and thin margins with high leverage.

The next checkpoints are the Web3 listing procedures and licensing outcomes, followed by full-year 2026 preliminary results and the annual business report.
