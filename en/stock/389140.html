Report overview

Losses narrowing, but quarterly losses persist at 4by4

4by4 cut its 2025 operating loss to KRW 7.4 billion, but it has posted operating losses for five consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, so when the losses close is the next checkpoint.

02

Business structure

4by4 is an AI content solutions company founded in 2017 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2022. Its operations span content production, exhibition projects, AI solutions and an esports subsidiary business.

The content production division returned to profit in the first half of 2026 on CES-related project wins, according to the company. The exhibition division grew sales from its carried-over order backlog, and the completion of a children's multicultural space content project contributed to first-quarter 2026 revenue.

Its flagship product AI PIXELL is video-enhancement and upscaling software sold as an installed PRO version, a real-time HD-to-4K LIVE solution, a VOD restoration service and editing-tool plug-ins.

Customers include broadcasters, OTT platforms and video producers, and the company cites restoration projects for KBS and SPOTV. The esports business centers on the BNK FearX team acquired through the 2023 SBXG deal and the subsidiary LoLQ.

The company said FearX posted first-half 2026 sales of KRW 4.3 billion with an operating loss of KRW 25 million, and LoLQ returned to profit with sales up 41%. At the March 2026 shareholder meeting, the business purpose was expanded to include management consulting for affiliates and real estate leasing.

Competition runs across AI video-enhancement software and the esports IP and cultural content markets, and the company said intensifying competition in the latter pulled total sales down slightly.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show diverging trends in sales and losses. Revenue rose from KRW 16.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 33.8 billion in 2023, an increase of 108.4% that made 2023 the largest year in the period, before slipping to KRW 33.3 billion in 2024 (down 1.4%) and recovering to KRW 33.7 billion in 2025 (up 1.3%).

In 2023, sales more than doubled yet the operating loss widened to KRW 16.3 billion, the same year the company secured esports team operating rights.

The operating loss narrowed to KRW 13.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.4 billion in 2025, and the operating margin improved from -64.8% in 2022 to -48.2%, -41.7% and then -21.9% in 2025. The company attributes the improvement to management and project-operation efficiency.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 8.77 billion in 2022, KRW 19.4 billion in 2023, KRW 11.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.4 billion in 2025, while the total net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 8.78 billion, KRW 20.6 billion, KRW 12.3 billion and KRW 11 billion in the same years.

Operating cash flow swung from an inflow of KRW 121 million in 2022 to an outflow of KRW 10.2 billion in 2023, then narrowed to outflows of KRW 2.63 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.62 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue has been volatile: KRW 8.47 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 9.33 billion in the fourth quarter (up 10.1% from the prior quarter), KRW 7.33 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 21.4%), KRW 11.1 billion in the second quarter (up 51.7%) and KRW 5.87 billion in the third quarter (down 47.2%).

Operating losses were KRW 2.05 billion, KRW 2.65 billion, KRW 2.5 billion, KRW 603 million and KRW 2.24 billion across the same five quarters, so the second quarter was the smallest loss before the third quarter widened again.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 10.2 billion. Revenue appears to depend heavily on when projects are recognized, so quarter-to-quarter swings are large.

05

Industry analysis

The AI video-enhancement market is broadening on demand from broadcasters, OTT platforms, video producers and archive remastering.

A newer source of demand is post-production work that brings AI-generated footage up to a deliverable standard. 4by4 is expanding locally processed installed software and editing-tool plug-ins to address this shift.

Performance and pricing claims come from the company and have not been independently verified, so its technical edge over rival solutions is hard to judge from outside sources. The exhibition business runs on project orders, so quarterly sales move with the timing and size of awards.

In content production, one-off contracts such as CES-related projects and children's space builds drive results. In esports, BNK FearX finished the group stage of the 2026 LCK Cup at 3 wins and 2 losses, which industry observers read as an early sign of the rebuilt roster's competitiveness.

Large entertainment companies such as Hybe and YG Entertainment are built around artist IP, so their business models differ from 4by4's. Within the media and entertainment group, few listed peers combine technology solutions with project-based revenue, which limits direct comparison.

06

Outlook

The company's stated direction for this year is profit-focused operations and building the base for a return to profitability through commercialization of AI PIXELL. It plans to widen new revenue sources in the second half around the AI Pixell product released on June 29, 2026.

The company announced on August 14, 2026 that first-half 2026 standalone sales rose 50.8% year on year to KRW 9.17 billion, and consolidated sales were KRW 18.46 billion, up 16.1%. Consolidated operating loss for the first half was KRW 3.11 billion, so the business remains loss-making.

The nearest checkpoint is Inter BEE 2026 in Makuhari Messe, Japan, from November 18 to 20, 2026, where the macOS version of AI PIXELL PRO will be shown with a KRW 220,000 perpetual license, along with DaVinci Resolve and Nuke plug-ins.

The company said it will expand cooperation with Japanese broadcasters, video producers, post-production firms and distribution partners.

On overseas commercialization, the company said proof-of-concept work with major broadcasting and OTT companies in Japan and the United States is leading to more concrete commercial discussions.

In esports, the company aims to strengthen the BNK FearX roster for the 2026 integrated season and expand its fan base through combined content and AI capabilities.

Because the third-quarter 2026 operating loss widened to KRW 2.24 billion from the second quarter, the size of the fourth-quarter loss and the full-year 2026 operating margin are the next checkpoints. The company has not yet set a numerical profitability target.

Bull factors

Multi-year narrowing of losses

The operating margin improved from -64.8% in 2022 to -21.9% in 2025, and the operating loss fell from KRW 16.3 billion in 2023 to KRW 7.4 billion in 2025. The company attributes the gains to management and project-operation efficiency. Bulls point to this trend as evidence that a base for returning to profit is forming.

Expanding AI PIXELL lineup and overseas proofs of concept

The AI PIXELL lineup now spans the installed PRO version, real-time LIVE, VOD remastering and editing-tool plug-ins. The company said proofs of concept with major broadcasting and OTT companies in Japan and the United States are leading to more concrete commercialization talks. The macOS release at Inter BEE 2026 in November 2026 widens the usable environments.

Low-borrowing balance sheet

The company emphasizes a balance sheet without borrowing burden and said its standalone debt-to-equity ratio was 15.3% and equity ratio 86.7% in the first half of 2026. Total equity was KRW 40.2 billion at year-end 2025, with KRW 39.9 billion attributable to controlling shareholders. Bulls cite this financial room as a buffer while losses continue.

Bear factors

Five consecutive quarters of operating losses

The company has posted operating losses for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. The operating loss narrowed to KRW 603 million in the second quarter but widened to KRW 2.24 billion in the third.

Large swings in quarterly revenue

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 5.87 billion, down 47.2% from the prior quarter and 30.7% from a year earlier. Revenue had jumped 51.7% to KRW 11.1 billion in the second quarter, so results shift sharply with when projects are recognized.

Exhibition and content work depend on project-by-project awards, which makes quarterly results hard to forecast.

Operating cash outflows and cumulative losses

Operating cash flow fell to an outflow of KRW 10.2 billion in 2023 and remained negative at KRW 2.63 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.62 billion in 2025. The company posted losses in each year from 2022 through 2025. Bears view whether cash outflows stop as the key unresolved weakness while losses persist.

10

Risk factors

Dependence on project awards

Exhibition and content production work on a contract-by-contract basis, so a gap in awards can sharply reduce quarterly revenue. Third-quarter 2026 revenue falling 30.7% from a year earlier is an example.

Because the timing of project revenue recognition feeds directly into results, order backlog and recognition timing both need to be tracked.

Technology and competition risk

Performance and pricing for AI PIXELL are company claims and have not been independently verified. If generative AI tools build similar enhancement features in, demand for standalone software could shrink. Whether overseas proofs of concept convert into commercial contracts with local broadcasters remains uncertain.

Portfolio mix and esports volatility

The FearX esports subsidiary posted first-half 2026 sales of KRW 4.3 billion and an operating loss of KRW 25 million, near break-even, but its results can swing with team performance and sponsorship conditions. The mix of exhibition, content, solutions and esports makes segment profit hard to separate from outside. Adding affiliate consulting and real estate leasing to the business purpose could also spread management focus.

Overall view

4by4 cut its operating margin from -64.8% in 2022 to -21.9% in 2025, and revenue recovered to KRW 33.7 billion in 2025.

However, the company has posted operating losses for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and the third-quarter 2026 operating loss of KRW 2.24 billion was larger than in the second quarter.

Bull-side arguments rest on efficiency gains, the expanding AI PIXELL lineup, overseas proofs of concept and a balance sheet with low borrowing. Bear-side points include sharp quarterly revenue swings, operating cash outflows and reliance on project awards.

The next checkpoints are the AI PIXELL product debut at Inter BEE 2026, the size of the fourth-quarter 2026 loss and the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual business report.
