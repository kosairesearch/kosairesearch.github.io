02

Business structure

SK Innovation is an energy company that combines refining and petrochemicals, lubricants, gas and power (E&S), batteries, and oil and gas exploration.

In preliminary third-quarter 2026 segment figures released on October 29, 2026, SK Energy, which runs the refining business, posted operating profit of KRW 1.21 trillion. Lubricant subsidiary SK Enmove posted KRW 1.09 trillion, a figure the company described as a record for a single quarter.

SK Innovation E&S, which handles gas and power, reported operating profit of KRW 7.7 billion-equivalent scale at KRW 774.4 billion, reflecting higher wholesale power prices and long-term contracted LNG supply.

The chemicals subsidiary SK Geocentric posted KRW 86.7 billion, and SK Incheon Petrochemical posted KRW 270.3 billion. The exploration subsidiary SK Earthon began first crude production at a Vietnamese block and booked KRW 31.4 billion.

Battery unit SK On recorded revenue of KRW 1.53 trillion and an operating loss of KRW 80.5 billion in the same quarter. SK On signed a supply contract with US-based Neovolta for roughly 9 GWh of LFP pouch cells for ESS, and it secured LFP cathode supply for ESS with L&F and POSCO Future M to broaden its materials base.

Refining output goes to domestic demand and overseas export markets, and export profitability depends on international product prices and refining margins. In refining, the main domestic peer is S-Oil, but SK Innovation also holds lubricants and LNG power, so refining cycles alone do not determine its results. The battery unit is moving from electric-vehicle automaker customers toward North American ESS customers.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue went from KRW 78.1 trillion in 2022 to KRW 77.3 trillion in 2023 (down 1.0% year on year) and KRW 74.7 trillion in 2024 (down 3.3%), before rising to KRW 80.3 trillion in 2025 (up 7.5%), the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 3.92 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.9 trillion in 2023 (down 51.4%) and KRW 315.5 billion in 2024 (down 83.4%), the smallest year in the span. Operating profit recovered to KRW 448.7 billion in 2025 (up 42.2%), but the operating margin was only 0.6%.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 256.3 billion in 2023 to a loss of KRW 2.26 trillion in 2024, a swing into losses, and widened to a loss of KRW 3.35 trillion in 2025, the second straight loss-making year.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 2.37 trillion in 2024 and a loss of KRW 5.44 trillion in 2025, a larger loss than the figure for controlling shareholders.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 36.4 trillion, of which controlling shareholders' equity was KRW 21.8 trillion, and total liabilities were KRW 69.2 trillion, a debt-to-equity ratio of 190.2%.

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 573.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 337.4 billion in the fourth quarter to KRW 2.16 trillion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 3.49 trillion in the second, and KRW 3.7 trillion in the third, which is three straight quarters of increases after the fourth-quarter 2025 low.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 28.8 trillion was down 1.6% from the prior quarter.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss of KRW 2.24 trillion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to a profit of KRW 964.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then KRW 215 billion in the second and KRW 2.59 trillion in the third.

Still, the second quarter included an inventory-related gain of about KRW 560 billion at SK Energy and one-off items at SK On, both reported by the press, so the KRW 1.53 trillion total for the last four quarters should not be read as fully repeatable.

05

Industry analysis

Supply disruption is the central variable in refining. The company said that in the third quarter of 2026, lower utilization at Middle Eastern refineries and Russia's diesel export restrictions tightened global product supply. Summer transport fuel demand overlapped, and refining margins were strong.

Attacks on Russian refineries by Ukraine were also cited as a factor. In base oil, spreads stayed high amid global supply disruption.

On the second-quarter 2026 conference call, SK Enmove called the Group III base oil market very favorable, while saying its duration depends on geopolitical variables, market supply and demand, and whether competitors normalize operations.

In refining, the main domestic peer is S-Oil, but SK Innovation's lubricants and LNG power businesses mean refining conditions alone do not set its results.

For batteries, the company said electric vehicle demand recovery has been slower than expected, while it expects ESS demand to grow with AI data centers and clean energy. The battery unit competes for ESS orders with domestic and foreign battery makers.

Press reports said SK On's average utilization across its domestic and overseas plants was 36.5% in the first quarter of 2026, well below the roughly 70% level usually associated with profitability. Refining margins and base oil spreads move sharply with geopolitical news, so the sector read carries high volatility.

06

Outlook

In its third-quarter 2026 release, the company said energy market uncertainty and price volatility will continue amid global geopolitical tension. For the fourth quarter, it expects the currently high base oil spreads to narrow gradually if supply disruptions ease.

In refining, higher run rates after the second-quarter turnaround were reflected in the third quarter. SK On plans to expand North American ESS orders while using its Hungarian plant to address European electric vehicle demand.

With the restructuring of the Ford joint venture completed, the company expects to cut roughly KRW 300 billion in annual depreciation and KRW 200 billion in annual interest expense. It is also implementing a plan to convert some electric vehicle battery lines to ESS lines.

At its March 2026 annual shareholder meeting, the CEO said SK On's sale is not under review. The shareholder return policy is under internal review, and the company describes debt reduction as the path to a stronger balance sheet.

The Namyangju cogeneration project began construction in the third quarter of 2026 after its subsidiary was established, and the Yongin project's heat source facilities and LNG piping work is in progress.

The next checkpoints are how much spreads narrow in the fourth quarter and whether SK On's operating result recovers from its third-quarter loss.

08

Bull factors

Refining and lubricant margins strong amid supply disruption

Export margins for refined products and base oil spreads stayed high in the third quarter of 2026, and refining and lubricant profits supported results. The company said SK Enmove's KRW 1.09 trillion operating profit in the quarter exceeded the 2022 full-year record of KRW 1.07 trillion in a single quarter. This profit structure can persist as long as supply disruption continues, which is the core of the bull case.

Battery losses narrowing with a shift toward ESS

Press reports put SK On's second-quarter 2026 operating profit at KRW 821.8 billion, taking the unit out of the red. It posted a third-quarter operating loss of KRW 80.5 billion, but the company said underlying profitability excluding one-offs kept improving.

Converting some electric vehicle lines to ESS and signing a roughly 9 GWh ESS supply contract with Neovolta are cited as grounds for the expected improvement.

Balance sheet repair and shareholder return review

Whether the decline in net borrowings continues in the following quarters is the point to check for shareholder return capacity. It said that as financial strength recovers it aims to create room for shareholder returns over the medium to long term, and that its return policy is under internal review. At the March 2026 annual meeting, it said it is not considering a sale of SK On.

09

Bear factors

Reliance on external market conditions and one-off gains

Press reports said SK Energy booked an inventory-related gain of about KRW 560 billion in the second quarter, and SK On's second-quarter profit included one-off items such as higher customer compensation and tax credits.

Financial industry analysts had expected SK On to return to a loss in the third quarter, and it did, with an operating loss of KRW 80.5 billion. Much of the improvement rests on external market conditions and one-off items, so sustainability is uncertain.

Low battery utilization and slow electric vehicle demand

Press reports said SK On's average utilization across domestic and overseas plants was 36.5% in the first quarter of 2026, far below the roughly 70% level usually associated with profitability.

The company said electric vehicle battery demand recovery is slower than expected and that it is prioritizing cost reduction and line conversion. If utilization stays low, fixed costs could continue to weigh on profit.

High leverage and traces of cumulative losses

The debt-to-equity ratio at the end of 2025 was 190.2%, above 189.2% in 2022. Total liabilities were KRW 69.2 trillion against total equity of KRW 36.4 trillion. Net losses including non-controlling interests were KRW 2.37 trillion in 2024 and KRW 5.44 trillion in 2025, which weakened capital headroom. Net borrowings are falling, but the size of the debt load could limit the pace of profit recovery.

10

Risk factors

Geopolitics and oil price swings

If Middle East tensions or attacks on Russian refineries ease and supply recovers, refining margins and base oil spreads could fall quickly. The company expects high spreads to narrow gradually as disruptions ease.

Inventory valuation gains can also reverse when crude prices fall, which could make quarterly profit more volatile.

Battery demand and execution of the ESS conversion

If electric vehicle demand recovery stays slow, SK On's plant utilization and profit could remain under pressure. The ESS shift depends on AI data center demand, and whether order volumes and delivery schedules convert into revenue is still being tested. Whether the roughly 9 GWh contract and materials supply agreements turn into actual sales is the key check.

Financial burden and timing of shareholder returns

With a debt-to-equity ratio of 190.2%, the room for shareholder returns depends on how fast net borrowings fall. The company has only said its return policy is under internal review, and specific criteria and timing have not been set. If battery losses continue, financial capacity could be absorbed by losses rather than expanded returns.

12

Overall view

SK Innovation's operating profit has risen for three straight quarters from the first to the third quarter of 2026, and the KRW 3.7 trillion third-quarter figure is one the company described as a record quarter.

The improvement was led by refining margins lifted by Middle Eastern and Russian supply disruptions, base oil spreads, and higher power prices at its E&S business.

By contrast, battery unit SK On moved back to an operating loss of KRW 80.5 billion in the third quarter after a second-quarter profit, so whether profit can last has not been confirmed.

The company posted net losses attributable to controlling shareholders in both 2024 and 2025, and its debt-to-equity ratio stood at 190.2% at the end of 2025. Bull factors are refining and lubricant profit while supply disruption lasts, the ESS shift with cost reduction, and a KRW 3 trillion cut in net borrowings.

Bear factors are dependence on external market conditions, one-off gains such as second-quarter inventory gains and customer compensation, low battery utilization, and weak electric vehicle demand.

The next checkpoints are how much spreads narrow in the fourth quarter and whether SK On recovers from its third-quarter loss.
