02

Business structure

Genotech was founded in July 1997 and is listed on the KONEX market. Its industry code is other chemical products manufacturing, but the company describes its business as R&D, manufacturing and sales of basic bio-materials, diagnostic bio-materials and biopharmaceutical materials.

Synthetic genes made up about 72% of 2024 revenue and genetic analysis services about 28%. Its product list also includes active pharmaceutical ingredients, reagents for in-vitro diagnostic analyzers and anticancer-related biopharmaceutical materials.

The company is pursuing precision diagnosis of genetic variants as its future strategic business. At an earlier KONEX investor briefing, it said it began developing diagnostic kits in 2013 and presented its in-house gene amplification technology, FenDEL.

It also described an exploratory clinical study at Ajou University Hospital of a kit that detects EGFR mutations in the blood of lung cancer patients. Intensifying competition in gene synthesis and analysis is cited as one reason behind the recent earnings weakness.

Genomic analysis firms such as Macrogen and Syntekabio are listed as comparable companies, while the business differs fundamentally from large chemical groups such as LG Chem and Lotte Chemical. Headcount was 31 as of August 2026.

03

Recent trends

Based on the FY2025 (29th term) standalone statements approved at the March 23, 2026 shareholders' meeting, revenue was KRW 2,563 million, the operating loss was KRW 870 million and the net loss was KRW 1,146 million. Revenue rose 4.4% from KRW 2,450 million a year earlier, a modest rebound.

In 2024, revenue had fallen 10.8% to KRW 2,450 million, with an operating loss of roughly KRW 960 million. In 2023, revenue dropped to KRW 2,750 million from KRW 5,210 million the year before, and the operating loss was KRW 510 million.

Against 2021 revenue of KRW 6.6 billion with operating profit of KRW 3.2 billion, and 2022 revenue of KRW 5.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.7 billion, both scale and profitability have receded sharply.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 9,549 million, down steadily from KRW 11.45 billion at the end of 2023. The 2025 debt ratio was 10.01% and the operating margin was -33.96%. The 52-week price range runs from KRW 805 to KRW 1,600.

As of October 27, 2026, the share price is KRW 1,199, unchanged from the prior day, and the market capitalization of KRW 6.8 billion is below FY2025 year-end equity. Results for the first half of 2026 and later disclosures could not be confirmed in our research.

04

Outlook

In the near term, the key questions are whether synthetic-gene and analysis revenue stabilizes at around KRW 2.5 billion and how well research spending is controlled.

A business summary cited by FnGuide says the molecular diagnostics market is expected to grow at an 11.4% compound annual rate through 2028, with the oncology segment growing at 26.9%.

For that market growth to reach Genotech's revenue, clinical results, regulatory approval and sales channels for its diagnostic kits would need to be in place first. The company previously said it was also developing kits to detect BRAF and ApoE genes in addition to EGFR.

However, our research could not confirm commercialization timelines or revenue targets for these products. A current ratio of 12.19 suggests ample short-term payment capacity, so continued losses do not imply an immediate need for external funding.

Even so, equity has declined since 2023, which makes the size of losses and the pace of equity erosion indicators to monitor in upcoming filings. Segment revenue mix and R&D expense trends in the next periodic reports are the natural starting point for assessment.

05

Bull factors

Balance sheet with little debt

The debt ratio was a low 10.01% in 2025. Reliance on borrowings was 0% as of 2024. The current ratio of 12.19 also points to ample short-term liquidity. In a period of continued losses, the ability to keep funding R&D without interest burden is a structural strength.

Slowing revenue decline

Revenue in 2025 was KRW 2.56 billion, up 4.4% year on year. After KRW 2.8 billion in 2023 and KRW 2.5 billion in 2024, sales appear to be settling in the mid-KRW 2 billion range. The operating loss also narrowed to KRW 870 million in 2025 from roughly KRW 960 million in 2024. Room for improvement in operating metrics would come from a revenue recovery and cost control.

Diagnostics aimed at a growing market

The molecular diagnostics market is forecast to grow at 11.4% a year through 2028, with the oncology segment growing at 26.9%. The company began developing diagnostic kits in 2013 based on gene analysis and engineering technology and owns an in-house amplification technology.

The strategy extends core synthetic-gene know-how into diagnostics. If results materialize, the revenue mix could become more diversified.

06

Bear factors

Three consecutive operating losses and a smaller revenue base

Operating losses were KRW 0.5 billion in 2023, KRW 1.0 billion in 2024 and KRW 0.9 billion in 2025, three years in a row. Revenue fell sharply from KRW 6.6 billion in 2021 to KRW 2.6 billion in 2025. The 2025 operating margin of -33.96% means losses equal about one-third of sales. The failure to regain a revenue scale that covers fixed costs is a burden.

Equity eroding steadily

Equity declined from KRW 11.45 billion in 2023 to KRW 10.69 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.55 billion in 2025. ROE in 2025 was -11.32%. The 2025 loss per share was KRW 202. If losses continue, the net asset base weakens gradually.

Uncertain payoff from new business

In 2024, higher R&D spending and intensifying competition lifted the operating loss by 89.6% from a year earlier. Diagnostic kits must pass clinical trials and approval before generating sales, and our research could not confirm commercialization timing or revenue contribution.

Larger genomic analysis companies such as Macrogen operate in the same market. Investment costs may appear before results do.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Demand for synthetic genes and analysis services is tied to R&D spending by research institutions and pharma and biotech companies. A cooling of investor sentiment in the biotech sector could reduce orders. Because the revenue base is small, order changes from a few customers can swing results significantly. Interest rates and the funding environment also affect capacity for new-business investment.

Industry

Competition in gene synthesis and analysis has intensified. For in-vitro diagnostic kits, the commercialization date depends heavily on clinical results and regulatory approval.

The company disclosed an exploratory study of its EGFR mutation detection kit, but later-stage progress could not be confirmed in our research. Price and technology competition from larger genomics firms is also a burden.

Company-specific

KONEX stocks trade infrequently, and the stock shows zero trading value even at KRW 1,199. Even when quotes exist, executing a desired quantity at a desired price may be difficult. The largest shareholder and related parties hold a concentrated 48.36%, limiting the free float.

If losses continue, equity will keep shrinking, and depending on how funding is raised, shareholder dilution could occur.

08

Overall view

Genotech is a small KONEX-listed bio-materials company engaged in synthetic genes and genetic analysis services, although its industry code is chemicals. On a standalone basis for FY2025, revenue was KRW 2,563 million and the operating loss was KRW 870 million.

Operating losses have persisted for three years since 2023, and revenue is far below its 2021 level. On the other hand, the low debt ratio and absence of borrowings indicate a financial cushion.

The diagnostics business is supported by market growth forecasts, but commercialization timing and revenue contribution have not been confirmed. The low liquidity of the KONEX market and the concentrated holdings of the largest shareholder are trading constraints.

This report is for information only, and upcoming periodic reports and diagnostic-kit disclosures should be checked for changes in the size of losses and progress in the new business.
