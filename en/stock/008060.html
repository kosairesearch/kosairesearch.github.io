02

Business structure

Daeduck is a business holding company that holds stakes in its subsidiaries and earns dividends rather than running production lines itself. Its PCB business was split off into Daeduck Electronics in a 2020 spin-off, leaving the parent to operate as a holding company.

FnGuide reports three domestic subsidiaries and divides reported segments into PCB, RF, and other (non-holding) businesses. FnGuide lists 2025 segment revenue shares of 70% PCB, 23% RF, and 7% other. Alphasquare counts 66.98% PCB, 27.23% RF, and 5.79% other, so the split differs by source.

The PCB core is Daeduck Electronics, which makes semiconductor package substrates and multi-layer boards for chip, server, and telecom equipment makers. A February 2026 Korean brokerage report projected a recovery in FC-BGA sales and growth in AI-related MLB sales at that subsidiary.

The RF arm, run by a subsidiary, mass-produces SAW filters and RF modules and has entered automotive electronics parts. FnGuide flagged weak RF performance alongside expansion into new markets. A new-business subsidiary designs and develops 5G communication modules.

Per FnGuide, the largest shareholder group holds 42.75% and treasury shares account for 7.22%. Because the parent mainly relies on subsidiary dividends and subsidiary results, weakness in subsidiary markets passes straight into parent earnings.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed annual basis, 2022 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025, with revenue of KRW 1.66 trillion and operating profit of KRW 196.9 billion. Revenue fell 23.0% to KRW 1.28 trillion in 2023, and operating profit dropped 84.1% to KRW 31.3 billion.

In 2024, revenue was KRW 1.27 trillion, down 0.9%, and operating profit was KRW 8.42 billion, down 73.1%, the smallest year in the period. Operating margin declined from 11.8% in 2022 to 2.4% in 2023 and 0.7% in 2024, and stood at 0.8% in 2025.

In 2025, revenue rose 9.3% to KRW 1.38 trillion and operating profit rose 34.4% to KRW 11.3 billion, but margin remained low. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was largest in 2024 at KRW 22.6 billion and fell 84.3% to KRW 3.54 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 6.23 billion in 2025, a different measure that should not be read as the same figure. Operating cash flow decreased from KRW 253.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 148.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 88.8 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit moved from KRW 21 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 27.7 billion in Q4 2025, and then rose each quarter through Q3 2026 to KRW 94.2 billion, with operating profit growing far faster than revenue.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 791 million in Q4 2025, returned to KRW 15.4 billion in Q1 2026, and stood at KRW 16.1 billion in both Q2 and Q3 2026. The last four quarters total KRW 46.8 billion on that measure.

The debt ratio rose to 26.8% at end-2025 from 19.8% in 2024. The size of one-off gains and losses is a point to check in the notes of the annual report.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from the downstream market is running strongly in AI server and semiconductor package substrates. Subsidiary Daeduck Electronics filed preliminary consolidated Q3 2026 results on October 30, 2026, showing revenue of KRW 418.1 billion, up 46.1% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 87.9 billion, up 259.4%.

Cumulative January to September revenue reached KRW 1.17 trillion and operating profit KRW 209.4 billion, up 55.9% and 941.4%. Net income in the same quarter fell 5.7% from the prior quarter, so quarterly profit did not rise in a straight line.

In a February 2026 report, a Korean brokerage pointed to Samsung Electronics' Tesla autonomous-driving chip orders as a source of added revenue for Daeduck Electronics. Substrate demand is sensitive to capital spending cycles and utilization, so pricing and yields are a structural variable.

The RF business is tied to smartphone component demand and 5G adoption, so the pace of recovery in the weak RF segment flagged by FnGuide is key. Among holding companies such as SK, LG, and Hanjinkal, the shared structure is dependence on subsidiary dividends and a holding-company discount.

Daeduck is directly exposed to the cycles of its two operating subsidiaries and has a more concentrated portfolio.

Daeduck Electronics disclosed preliminary Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 87.9 billion, up 259.4%, on October 30, 2026. Cumulative January to September operating profit reached KRW 209.4 billion, up 941.4%. Substrate demand is visibly reaching parent-level earnings through the subsidiary.

Discount to book value and shareholder structure

Bear factors

Long-term shrinkage in profit scale

Operating profit fell from KRW 196.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.3 billion in 2025, and operating margin dropped from 11.8% to 0.8%. The 2024 figure of KRW 8.42 billion was the smallest in the period. How far quarterly recovery can restore annual earnings power is not yet settled.

Dependence on subsidiary cycles

Parent income depends largely on subsidiary dividends, so a downturn in substrate demand would hit holding-company earnings too. Substrates are sensitive to capital spending cycles and utilization. FnGuide flagged weakness in the RF business, which is tied to smartphone component demand.

Weaker attributable profit and cash flow

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 84.3% to KRW 3.54 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow declined from KRW 253.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 88.8 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 19.8% in 2024 to 26.8% in 2025, and Q4 2025 posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 791 million.

Risk factors

Subsidiary cycle

Substrate and RF businesses are highly cyclical. Even after the subsidiary's profit improved, pricing pressure or lower utilization would quickly affect parent earnings. Parent earnings swings should be read together with the subsidiary's quarterly results.

Holding discount and governance

Holding companies often trade at a discount to the sum of their subsidiaries, a dynamic that is frequently discussed for Daeduck. The largest shareholder group holds 42.75% and treasury shares 7.22%, so governance events could affect the share price.

Changes in subsidiary stake sales or treasury share policy are variables that can be confirmed through disclosures.

Quarterly volatility and preliminary figures

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved to a net loss of KRW 791 million in Q4 2025 before turning positive in Q1 2026. Quarterly results can swing sharply on one-off items, so their size needs checking. Daeduck Electronics' Q3 2026 results are preliminary and may change during external audit.

Overall view

Daeduck is a holding company with PCB and RF subsidiaries. On a filed basis, Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 94.2 billion was up 349.2% year on year, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders has stayed positive since Q1 2026.

However, 2025 annual operating profit of KRW 11.3 billion remains far below 2022's KRW 196.9 billion, and operating margin stayed low at 0.7% in 2024 and 0.8% in 2025.

Strengths include subsidiary Daeduck Electronics' preliminary Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 87.9 billion, up 259.4%, four consecutive quarters of rising operating profit, and a price-to-book ratio below 1x.

Weaknesses include exposure to subsidiary and substrate cycles, weakness in the RF business, an 84.3% drop in 2025 net income attributable to controlling shareholders, and a declining trend in operating cash flow. Because subsidiary results flow directly into parent earnings, both should be tracked together.

The next checkpoints are preliminary Q4 2026 results, the operating margin and one-off items in the 2026 annual report, and subsidiary dividend policy. This report does not include a buy or sell opinion.
