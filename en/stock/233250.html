Business structure

Median Diagnostics is an animal disease diagnostics company that listed on the KONEX market in 2017, with immunodiagnostic, molecular diagnostic and point-of-care technologies. The company describes itself as the first domestic firm licensed to manufacture animal diagnostic pharmaceuticals, in 2001.

Its core focus is diagnostics for high-consequence livestock diseases such as ASF, FMD, HPAI, PED and BVD. The product range spans PCR, rapid kits, ELISA, standard reagents and raw materials such as monoclonal antibodies and antigens. It also develops and supplies point-of-care systems for companion-animal diagnostics.

A revenue breakdown by product was not confirmed, so the mix is described only qualitatively: livestock disease control demand and companion-animal diagnostics appear to make up sales together.

In a filing dated November 4, 2024, the two STIC private equity funds transferred 1,252,894 common shares to NanoHelix. The stake was 71.74% in total, and the transfer price was about KRW 21.0 billion.

NanoHelix, founded in December 2008, makes nucleic acid amplification, extraction and molecular diagnostic products and emphasizes localized PCR enzyme raw materials.

Pairing a parent with molecular diagnostic raw-material expertise and an animal diagnostic kit maker creates room for collaboration in molecular products. The company has launched Rapid Kit and POCT PCR equipment to address growth in the rapid diagnostic kit market. As of October 5, 2026, the company had 69 employees.

The field is contested by large overseas players and smaller domestic firms, and market-share data by competitor could not be confirmed.
