HD Hyundai: Profit Recovery on Shipbuilding and Engines, with Wider Quarterly Swings

HD Hyundai's operating profit recovered from its 2023 low to KRW 6.1 trillion in 2025, and operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 rose 71.5% year on year to KRW 2.92 trillion but fell 29.2% from the prior quarter.

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

HD Hyundai is a holding company whose core is shipbuilding and offshore, with construction equipment, energy and power equipment affiliates.

Of third-quarter 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 22.1 trillion, the refining affiliate HD Hyundai Oilbank contributed KRW 10.0 trillion and the shipbuilding affiliate contributed KRW 8.3 trillion. Goodkyung News reported that HD Hyundai Heavy Industries and HD Hyundai Oilbank together account for about 80% of revenue.

In shipbuilding, higher productivity and higher ship prices lifted revenue, and engine machinery and offshore plant work supported profit.

The construction equipment affiliate, HD Hyundai Site Solutions, posted revenue of KRW 2.2 trillion and operating profit of KRW 200.2 billion in the quarter, helped by demand for industrial and power generation engines.

HD Hyundai Electric, the power equipment affiliate, posted revenue of KRW 1.2 trillion and operating profit of KRW 284.5 billion, an operating margin of 24.1%.

HD Hyundai Marine Solution, focused on ship maintenance, reported revenue of KRW 650.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 95.7 billion, with one-off costs from the Goltens acquisition limiting profit growth.

The marine engine affiliate, HD Hyundai Marine Engine, announced preliminary third-quarter results on October 28, 2026 with revenue of KRW 211.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 60.3 billion. Because a holding company consolidates affiliate results, diverging sector cycles also widen swings in group earnings.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 60.8 trillion in 2022 to KRW 61.3 trillion in 2023 (up 0.8%), KRW 67.8 trillion in 2024 (up 10.5%) and KRW 71.3 trillion in 2025 (up 5.2%), growing for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 3.39 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.03 trillion in 2023 (down 40.0%), the low point, then rose to KRW 2.98 trillion in 2024 (up 46.8%) and KRW 6.1 trillion in 2025 (up 104.5%), growing for two consecutive years.

The operating margin was 5.6% in 2022, 3.3% in 2023, 4.4% in 2024 and 8.6% in 2025, so in 2025 operating profit rose far faster than revenue.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent fell from KRW 1.41 trillion in 2022 to KRW 264.5 billion in 2023 (down 81.2%) and then rose to KRW 509 billion in 2024 and KRW 962.7 billion in 2025, growing for two consecutive years.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 2.23 trillion in 2022, KRW 785.8 billion in 2023, KRW 1.93 trillion in 2024 and KRW 3.68 trillion in 2025; the gap from owner-attributable profit reflects minority holders' share.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 30.3 trillion, of which equity attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 10.1 trillion. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 2.13 trillion in 2022 to KRW 7.51 trillion in 2024 and KRW 7.38 trillion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit climbed from KRW 1.7 trillion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 4.12 trillion in the second quarter of 2026, then fell to KRW 2.92 trillion in the third quarter (down 29.2% from the prior quarter). The company cited seasonal working-day declines and currency movements.

Owner-attributable profit fell from KRW 1.33 trillion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 556.4 billion in the third quarter (down 58.2%), and the four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 total KRW 3.12 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

Shipbuilding is in a phase where higher-value vessels make up a larger share and higher ship prices flow into revenue. A shipbuilding affiliate posted third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 479.0 billion with an operating margin of about 20.2%, helped by a larger share of LNG carriers.

In engines, the marine engine affiliate raised the revenue share of dual-fuel engines and posted an operating margin of about 28.5% in the third quarter of 2026.

The refining unit, HD Hyundai Oilbank, recorded revenue of KRW 10.0 trillion and operating profit of KRW 900.9 billion as crude prices and refined product prices fell and refining margins narrowed. Its petrochemical business turned to a loss due to global oversupply and narrower product spreads.

In power equipment, AI data center and North American distribution equipment demand are the growth drivers. HD Hyundai Electric's third-quarter orders were USD 980 million, down 19.1% year on year, while its order backlog reached USD 8.84 billion, up 4.2% from the prior quarter.

In construction equipment, a global demand recovery and power generation engine demand are the main drivers. Competitively, shipbuilding profitability varies widely by vessel type, while power equipment is responding through capacity expansion and selective orders focused on profitability.

Each business sits at a different point in its cycle, so a boom in one unit and a slowdown in another can occur together.

08

Bull factors

Higher-value shipbuilding lifts profitability

The shipbuilding affiliate posted third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 8.29 trillion and operating profit of KRW 1.45 trillion, with productivity gains and higher ship prices lifting revenue. A larger share of dual-fuel engines and LNG carriers was cited as a profitability driver. Shipbuilding profit is the central axis of the group's operating profit recovery.

Power equipment margins and a growing backlog

HD Hyundai Electric recorded third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 1.18 trillion, operating profit of KRW 284.5 billion and an operating margin of 24.1%. Its order backlog at the end of the quarter was USD 8.84 billion, up 4.2% from the prior quarter. AI data center and North American distribution demand are cited as growth grounds.

Earnings recovery since the 2023 low

Operating profit grew for two consecutive years from the 2023 low of KRW 203.2 billion to KRW 2.98 trillion in 2024 and KRW 6.1 trillion in 2025. The operating margin rose from 3.3% in 2023 to 8.6% in 2025.

Owner-attributable profit was KRW 781.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 1.33 trillion in the second quarter.

Bear factors

Quarterly profit declined in the third quarter

Operating profit fell 29.2% from KRW 4.12 trillion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 2.92 trillion in the third quarter, and owner-attributable profit fell 58.2% to KRW 556.4 billion. The company cited fewer working days and currency movements. Wide quarterly swings can make the annual trend and the quarterly pattern read differently.

Weak refining and petrochemical conditions

HD Hyundai Oilbank's third-quarter operating profit was KRW 900.9 billion as narrower refining margins followed lower crude and product prices. Its petrochemical business turned to a loss on global oversupply and narrower product spreads. Continuing geopolitical uncertainty in the Middle East adds to the volatility.

Lower power equipment orders

HD Hyundai Electric's third-quarter orders were USD 980 million, down 19.1% year on year. The company attributed the decline to a base effect from large orders in the first half. A slower order flow could raise questions about the durability of power equipment revenue growth.

10

Risk factors

Capacity expansion execution

HD Hyundai Electric is investing KRW 198.0 billion in a distribution equipment campus in Cheongju to meet AI data center and North American distribution demand. HD Hyundai Site Solutions is also expanding power generation engine capacity through a new engine plant in Gunsan.

If these investments are not completed on schedule or utilization does not hold up, fixed costs could weigh on operating margins. A change in demand outlook could also lengthen the payback period for the new capacity.

Acquisition costs and one-off items

HD Hyundai Marine Solution's operating profit grew less than its revenue because of one-off costs from the Goltens acquisition. Costs may continue until the deal's benefits fully materialize. The operating margin fell to 3.3% in 2023, showing that margins can shift sharply within a short period.

Financial leverage and debt scale

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 181.9% in 2022 to 192.6% in 2023 before falling to 159.4% in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 48.4 trillion, larger than total equity of KRW 30.3 trillion. Larger affiliate investment or acquisition financing could raise financial burden again.

Overall view

HD Hyundai is a holding company spanning shipbuilding and offshore, construction equipment, energy and power equipment, with annual revenue rising from KRW 60.8 trillion in 2022 to KRW 71.3 trillion in 2025.

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 2.03 trillion in 2023 and recovered to KRW 6.1 trillion in 2025, while the operating margin rose from 3.3% to 8.6% over the same period. In the third quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 22.1 trillion and operating profit was KRW 2.92 trillion, up 21.2% and 71.5% year on year.

However, operating profit fell 29.2% from the prior quarter and owner-attributable profit fell 58.2%, making quarterly volatility clear. Strengths include higher-value shipbuilding and engine profitability and a growing power equipment backlog.

Weaknesses include the refining and petrochemical downturn, lower power equipment orders and one-off acquisition costs. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 181.9% in 2022 to 159.4% in 2025, but total liabilities of KRW 48.4 trillion keep financial burden under review.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio sits below 1x, and owner-attributable equity is only part of total equity, so the comparison reads differently depending on the base.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report, where the quarterly operating margin and refining and power equipment trends will be the key measures.
