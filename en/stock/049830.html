Seung IL: Thin Margins and Swinging Quarterly Results

02

Business structure

Seung IL makes metal packaging in two main lines: aerosol cans and general cans. Its aerosol cans for cosmetics and insecticides are supplied on an OEM basis, while its general cans, including food and industrial cans, go to customers such as CJ and GS Caltex.

In its Q1 2026 earnings commentary, the company said it held a 66% domestic aerosol share and remained first in that segment. The same commentary said low utilization and lower prices for can and valve products weighed on profitability in the quarter.

A food repackaging and sales business started in December 2024, and export reach has expanded to Southeast Asia and other continents. Plants in Cheonan and Eumseong are reported to be under construction. Metal can production requires equipment investment and customer qualification, which creates entry barriers.

Some data providers classify the stock under the KOSDAQ metals sector rather than chemicals, and its cost structure differs from large chemical makers linked to petrochemical feedstock prices. Revenue shares by aerosol and general can lines should be checked in the business segment disclosures of the annual report.

Earnings analysis

On the audited annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 158.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 141.9 billion in 2023, a 10.7% decline, rose 1.8% to KRW 144.4 billion in 2024, and then fell 2.0% to KRW 141.5 billion in 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 1.29 billion in 2022, KRW 598 million in 2023 after a 53.5% drop, KRW 2.29 billion in 2024 after a 282.3% gain, and KRW 2.28 billion in 2025, down 0.4%. Operating margin was 0.8%, 0.4%, 1.6% and 1.6% over 2022 to 2025, so profit is thin relative to sales.

For 2024, the company attributed the operating profit increase to higher sales from a strong dollar and lower manufacturing costs from reduced raw material expenses. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 3.66 billion in 2024 and fell 31.3% to KRW 2.52 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 2.52 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 2.29 billion in 2022, KRW 8.25 billion in 2023, KRW 15.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.2 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 36.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 464 million and controlling net income of KRW 674 million.

Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 32.4 billion, down 11.5% from the prior quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 644 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 546 million.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 35.3 billion with operating profit of KRW 425 million and controlling net income of KRW 1.2 billion, a return to profit. Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 41.4 billion, operating profit was KRW 1.95 billion, up 359.2% from the prior quarter, and controlling net income was KRW 1.86 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 35.9 billion, down 13.3% from the prior quarter and 2.1% from a year earlier, with operating profit of KRW 663 million, down 66.0% from the prior quarter and up 42.9% from a year earlier.

The company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.7 billion in Q3 2026, a swing to loss from a year earlier. The gap between positive operating profit and a controlling-shareholder loss in Q3 2026 is a point to check against non-operating items in the quarterly report.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for Seung IL comes from aerosol containers for cosmetics and insecticides and from food and industrial general cans. Aerosol volumes depend on customers' production plans and cosmetic OEM orders, so utilization moves with those schedules.

In its Q1 2026 commentary, the company cited low utilization as a cause of weaker profitability. In practice, filling production capacity matters as much as the underlying can demand. General cans depend on food consumption and industrial output.

Raw materials such as steel and aluminum, along with valve components, feed into cost. In Q1 2026, lower can and valve prices reduced both revenue and operating profit, according to the company. The company claims a leading share of the domestic aerosol can market, which gives it a solid position.

In metal packaging, competitiveness rests on plant scale, customer qualification and delivery capability. With plants being built in Cheonan and Eumseong, the ability to fill new capacity will be a key variable for its position within the industry.

Export expansion toward Southeast Asia and other continents is the stated direction, and its results should be tracked in future revenue mix.

Revenue, however, fell from KRW 158.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 141.5 billion in 2025, and operating margin has stayed between 0.4% and 1.6%, leaving a thin profit cushion.
