02

Business structure

Flitto is an AI data and solutions company with two pillars: sales of AI training data and AI translation solutions. Its data business started with text and has expanded into multimodal data covering audio, images and video, as well as physical AI data.

The data marketplace opened in June 2026 and shifts the company from direct sales toward distribution and brokerage, with Flitto verifying copyright and personal data to guarantee transactions. Reports say more than 500 datasets are listed on the marketplace.

The core of the solutions business is Vogl, a real-time AI translation brand that combined the existing Live Translation and Chat Translation services into one brand in September 2026. Vogl passed 80,000 subscribers in Q3 2026.

Its customers now span global corporate events including AWS and AMD, retail sites such as Shinsegae Department Store and Olive Young, and subscription clients including Korean Air, CJ CheilJedang, Megazone Cloud and LG Electronics.

The on-premise option, which installs the translation model on a company's own servers, targets large enterprises and public agencies that prioritize security. Reports put first-half 2026 solutions revenue at about KRW 2.4 billion, up 175 percent year on year.

The company has not separately disclosed revenue by business line, so segment amounts are limited to what has been confirmed.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 13.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.8 billion in 2023 (up 30.2% year on year), KRW 20.3 billion in 2024 (up 14.3%) and KRW 36 billion in 2025 (up 77.2%). Revenue has grown for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating results moved from losses of KRW 6.6 billion in 2022, KRW 5.09 billion in 2023 and KRW 396 million in 2024 to operating profit of KRW 6.15 billion in 2025, a swing from losses to profit.

The operating margin improved from -48.4% in 2022 and -28.7% in 2023 to -2.0% in 2024 and 17.1% in 2025, as the losses narrowed.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from a loss of KRW 6.79 billion in 2023, when losses widened, to a profit of KRW 809 million in 2024, and rose to KRW 6.45 billion in 2025, up 696.7 percent year on year.

Net income including non-controlling interests is a separate figure, and the 2025 value is also KRW 6.45 billion. Operating cash flow went from outflows of KRW 5.44 billion in 2022 and KRW 4.45 billion in 2023 to inflows of KRW 1.39 billion in 2024 and KRW 10 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 260.0% in 2023 to 50.1% in 2024 and 42.8% in 2025.

Quarterly results show that Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 10.2 billion (down 13.3 percent from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 721 million (down 74.5 percent) slowed, and then Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 5.21 billion (down 48.9 percent) and an operating loss of KRW 64.1 million marked a return to losses.

The company said it raised R&D spending to 19 percent of standalone revenue in Q1 to strengthen its solutions business, and CFO Yoon Min-yong described the Q1 operating loss as deliberate cost spending.

Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 8.11 billion (up 55.5 percent quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 1.4 billion marked a return to profit.

Q3 2026 revenue reached KRW 17.8 billion (up 119.4 percent quarter on quarter and 51.0 percent year on year) and operating profit reached KRW 8.97 billion (up 217.1 percent year on year). Based on the company's preliminary release of October 20, 2026, the Q3 operating margin was 50.4 percent.

Digital Daily reported that about 87 percent of cumulative operating profit was concentrated in Q3, which makes the durability of profitability the key question.

Q3 2026 net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 8.35 billion, up 436.5 percent from Q2 2026, and the sum over the latest four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) was KRW 11 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Flitto operates in two markets, data and translation. In data, demand for high-quality AI training data is spreading from software into physical sites such as robotics, manufacturing and industrial automation.

In May 2026 the company said it would upgrade a platform that collects physical AI training data through crowdsourcing, arguing that the earlier suit-based motion capture approach was costly and hard to scale.

In translation, demand is driven by the need for multilingual communication at companies and rising numbers of foreign visitors. The number of events using Vogl Stage rose from 150 in 2024 to 442 in 2025, and the company plans to serve more than 600 global events in 2026.

On competition, Vogl Stage is priced 20 to 30 percent above rival solutions, and the company argues that its large custom datasets allow accurate handling of proper nouns and technical terms. The company also raised R&D to 19 percent of standalone revenue, above the 15 to 16 percent it cited as the industry average.

Whether the company can keep both its price premium and its high R&D ratio will test its competitiveness, particularly as large corporations increasingly enter the AI translation market. Flitto is positioning on-premise deployment and data-driven personalization as its points of difference.

06

Outlook

The company said it expects continued high growth and profitability in Q4 2026, according to CFO Yoon Min-yong in the October 20, 2026 release. In Q4 it plans to deliver a large volume of data to global big tech companies.

A KRW 3.9 billion solutions contract with a global semiconductor company, signed on October 12, 2026, was executed after the Q3 measurement period and will be reflected in future results.

Two data supply contracts with a global IT company, which were corrected in a June 2026 filing, expanded from about KRW 14 billion to about KRW 31.5 billion combined.

In the public sector, the company said it won data construction projects from bodies including the National Institute of Korean Language, the Institute for Information and Communications Technology Planning and Evaluation, and the Small Enterprise and Market Service.

For overseas expansion, the company plans to take its data and marketplace business global from 2027 and to deploy on-premise solutions in overseas markets. Reports said the US subsidiary, which lagged its Japanese and Chinese counterparts, is expected to start full local operations in 2027.

Portable interpretation devices and classroom AI devices carrying the Vogl brand may also launch from 2027. These plans are company statements, and their timing and scale will become visible in results from 2027 onward.

08

Bull factors

Subscription-based growth in the Vogl solutions business

Vogl subscribers passed 80,000 in Q3 2026, about 14 times the Q4 2025 level at launch. The company said Korean Air, CJ CheilJedang, Megazone Cloud and LG Electronics have adopted it on a subscription basis. The next checkpoint is how the subscriber growth shows up in second-half solutions revenue. As subscription revenue accumulates, the recurring share of earnings could rise.

Expanding data contracts with global companies

Two data supply contracts with a global IT company, corrected in a June 2026 filing, expanded from about KRW 14 billion to about KRW 31.5 billion combined. On October 12, 2026 the company signed a KRW 3.9 billion solutions contract with a global semiconductor company.

The company said it will deliver large data volumes to global big tech companies through Q4. Reports say more than 500 datasets are listed on the data marketplace.

Structural improvement in operating margin

The operating margin improved from -48.4% in 2022 to 17.1% in 2025, and the Q3 2026 margin reached 50.4% on the company's preliminary figures. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 260.0% in 2023 to 42.8% in 2025. Operating cash flow turned to an inflow of KRW 10 billion in 2025, a sign of stronger cash generation.

09

Bear factors

High volatility in quarterly results

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 5.21 billion with an operating loss of KRW 64.1 million, and Q4 2025 operating profit fell 74.5 percent from the prior quarter. Digital Daily reported that about 87 percent of cumulative operating profit was concentrated in Q3. When profitability clusters in a single quarter, the predictability of results in later quarters falls.

Doubts over the durability of data orders

Large data supply deals tend to make quarterly revenue depend heavily on delivery timing. The company said it is discussing additional solutions deployments with several global companies, but the size and timing have not been disclosed. A customer base centered on big tech also adds concentration risk.

Price premium and cost burden

Vogl Stage is priced 20 to 30 percent above rival solutions. The company raised Q1 R&D spending to 19 percent of standalone revenue, against an industry average it put at 15 to 16 percent. Keeping a price premium and a high R&D ratio while funding overseas expansion is the central question. The US subsidiary's slower progress compared with Japan and China also remains a burden.

10

Risk factors

Earnings volatility and profit concentration

Quarterly revenue has moved sharply with the timing of data deliveries. The Q1 2026 operating loss coincided with R&D investment, and the high concentration of profit in Q3 makes it hard to read a single trend. Q4 results will depend on the mix of large deliveries and solutions revenue.

Data transaction guarantees and regulation

On the data marketplace, Flitto verifies copyright and personal data to guarantee transactions. As the scope of that guarantee widens, liability and dispute exposure related to copyright and personal data could grow.

Changes in domestic and overseas regulation of AI training data could also affect transaction terms and pricing.

Overseas expansion and hardware execution

The US subsidiary plans to start full operations from 2027, and portable and classroom devices under the Vogl brand may launch from 2027. A transparent-display translation terminal is in a pilot stage at a pop-up store in Seongsu-dong, Seoul. Hardware and overseas expansion carry investment burdens and execution delay risk.

  2. Results after Q4 2026

    Whether the KRW 3.9 billion contract signed in October 2026 is reflected in results, and whether talks with several global companies turn into new contracts.

  3. Around February 2027

  4. End of March 2027

    The filing of the 2026 annual business report, when annual results and notes are confirmed as regulatory filing figures.

  5. During 2027

    The start of full US subsidiary operations, global expansion of the data marketplace, and the launch of Vogl-branded devices will determine the growth drivers for 2027.

12

Overall view

Flitto moved from an operating loss of KRW 6.6 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 6.15 billion in 2025, and Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 17.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 8.97 billion were the highest quarterly figures on record.

Growth rests on subscription expansion for the real-time translation service Vogl and on AI data supply to global companies. Vogl passed 80,000 subscribers in Q3 2026, and the combined value of two data supply contracts expanded to about KRW 31.5 billion in a June 2026 corrected filing.

At the same time, the Q1 2026 operating loss, the decline in Q4 2025 operating profit and the concentration of profit in Q3 show that quarterly results can swing sharply. Whether new orders follow the large data deliveries, and whether Vogl keeps its price premium, are the points on which the outlook divides.

Market capitalization sits well above book equity, a large premium over net asset value, and earnings-based multiples are sensitive to results.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 data deliveries, the annual preliminary release in early 2027, the 2026 annual business report by the end of March 2027, and the start of US subsidiary operations in 2027. This material is for information only and does not include a buy or sell opinion.
