Report overview

Record quarterly sales, but profit slips in 3Q 2026

Overseas merchant volume lifted NHN KCP's 3Q 2026 revenue to a record quarterly level, while operating profit and profit attributable to owners both fell year on year.

  2. 2

02

Business structure

NHN KCP is a comprehensive payment company founded in 1994, and its business is organized into online payments, offline payments and project divisions. The online payments division provides PG (payment gateway) and VAN services, and the company holds four unlisted subsidiaries that are consolidated.

In November 2024 Mirae Asset Securities described the company as the number one PG operator in Korea by market share, and in February 2026 NH Investment & Securities again highlighted its number one position in card payment PG share.

The revenue model charges fees on the transaction value processed for merchants, so results move with total payment volume and merchant mix. Total transaction value in 3Q 2026 reached KRW 17.349 trillion, up 31% year on year and the largest quarterly level on record.

Growth has been led by overseas merchants, where payments for vehicle sales at global electric and imported car merchants lifted overseas transaction value 75% year on year.

Domestic merchant transaction value also rose 20% on the steady growth of existing merchants and inflows of new ones, which the company said outpaced the growth of Korea's online shopping market.

In offline payments, the number of merchants using the cloud POS service KCP POS+ passed 20,000 at the time of the 1Q 2026 results.

Key competitive variables are merchant acquisition and negotiating power with card issuers, and in February 2025 Kiwoom Securities flagged card issuers' bargaining power and direct card approval issues as margin pressures.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results showed continued top-line growth, while the operating margin fell from 2022 and then recovered in 2025.

Revenue rose from KRW 822.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 972 billion in 2023 (up 18.1% year on year), KRW 1.11 trillion in 2024 (up 13.7%) and KRW 1.23 trillion in 2025 (up 11.7%), with three consecutive years of increases from 2023 to 2025 and 2025 as the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 44.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 42 billion in 2023 (down 5.1%), then KRW 43.8 billion in 2024 (up 4.3%) and KRW 54.7 billion in 2025 (up 24.9%), with two consecutive years of increases from 2024 to 2025.

The operating margin declined from 5.4% in 2022 to 4.3% in 2023 and 4.0% in 2024 before rising to 4.4% in 2025. Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 34.9 billion in 2022, KRW 35.3 billion in 2023 (up 1.0%), KRW 45.2 billion in 2024 (up 28.1%) and KRW 45.3 billion in 2025 (up 0.2%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 45.8 billion in 2025 and should be read separately from profit attributable to owners. Operating cash flow was KRW 41.4 billion in 2022 and KRW 63.4 billion in 2023, turned to -KRW 61.2 billion in 2024 and reached KRW 200.3 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from KRW 335.6 billion in 4Q 2025 (up 5.7% quarter on quarter) to KRW 386.7 billion in 3Q 2026.

Operating profit was KRW 13.8 billion in 1Q 2026, KRW 15.4 billion in 2Q and KRW 14 billion in 3Q, and profit attributable to owners peaked at KRW 16.1 billion in 1Q 2026 before declining in 2Q and 3Q to KRW 10.1 billion.

In November 2025 NH Investment & Securities explained that completed negotiations on card issuer and PG interchange-related fees in 3Q 2025 supported margin improvement. Profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters (4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026) totals KRW 51.5 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In Korea's payment market, the company has grown from its position as the number one PG operator by market share. Domestic merchant transaction value rose 20% in 3Q 2026, which the company said clearly outpaced the growth of Korea's online shopping market.

Overseas, payments tied to electric and imported car sales have become the main growth driver. In a June 2026 report, Kiwoom Securities said strong Tesla sales and new Mercedes-Benz merchants would lead overseas growth.

On margins, negotiating power between card issuers and merchants and the issue of direct card approval are recurring variables. In February 2025 Kiwoom Securities analyzed that these factors could limit valuation over the medium term.

On new businesses, NH Investment & Securities said in a February 2026 report that payment company shares are more sensitive to the progress of the won stablecoin bill than to earnings.

In agentic commerce, Kyobo Securities noted in an April 2026 report that the company was adopted as a partner for Google's agent payment standard AP2. Its number one PG position and loyal merchant base are cited as competitive strengths.

06

Outlook

The company stated on 10 November 2026 that it will upgrade AI agent payment infrastructure and link it to new payment methods such as stablecoins.

It has completed building a payment-specialized mainnet with Avalanche and showed integrated infrastructure linking sub-second stablecoin payments to settlement at events including KBW2026. The company said its AP2-based infrastructure will undergo technical verification for real-world deployment.

At the 1Q 2026 results it also said it would solidify its payment value chain through stablecoin payment infrastructure and further overseas expansion.

In a June 2026 report, Kiwoom Securities projected domestic gross merchandise value (GMV) of KRW 48.4 trillion for 2026, up 13% year on year, and overseas GMV of KRW 12.2 trillion, up 41%.

The same report expected the cost of sales ratio to rise 0.4 percentage points to 90.7% while maintaining an operating margin of 4.4%. The company has not disclosed the revenue contribution of its new payment infrastructure in figures. The 2026 annual report will show the full-year trend of overseas volume share and operating margin.

08

Bull factors

Rapid overseas merchant growth

Overseas merchant transaction value rose 75% year on year in 3Q 2026 and passed KRW 10 trillion for the first three quarters. Payments for vehicle sales at global electric and imported car merchants led the increase. Kiwoom Securities expected overseas GMV to grow 41% in 2026 in a June 2026 report.

Early moves into new payment infrastructure

The company completed a payment-specialized mainnet with Avalanche and is preparing AI payment infrastructure based on AP2. Kyobo Securities noted in an April 2026 report that the company was adopted as an AP2 partner. Commercial results from this infrastructure have not yet appeared in reported figures.

Expanding domestic merchant base

Domestic merchant transaction value rose 20% year on year in 3Q 2026, supported by steady growth at existing merchants and new merchant inflows. The company said this growth rate exceeded the growth of Korea's online shopping market. Merchants using its offline POS service also passed 20,000 at the time of the 1Q 2026 results.

09

Bear factors

Profit moving against sales

Revenue in 3Q 2026 rose 21.8% year on year, but operating profit fell 11.2% to KRW 14 billion. Profit attributable to owners fell 25.8% to KRW 10.1 billion. Profit attributable to owners declined in both 2Q and 3Q 2026 after peaking at KRW 16.1 billion in 1Q 2026.

Fee negotiations and margin pressure

The operating margin fell from 5.4% in 2022 to 4.0% in 2024 and reached only 4.4% in 2025. In February 2025 Kiwoom Securities cited negotiating power between card issuers and merchants and the issue of direct card approval as margin pressures. The firm analyzed that these factors could limit profit improvement over the medium term.

Rising cost ratio and volatile cash flow

Kiwoom Securities expected the 2026 cost of sales ratio to rise 0.4 percentage points to 90.7%. Operating cash flow was volatile, moving from -KRW 61.2 billion in 2024 to KRW 200.3 billion in 2025. Revenue growth has not moved in step with profit and cash flow across recent periods.

10

Risk factors

Policy and regulation

NH Investment & Securities said in a February 2026 report that payment company shares are more sensitive to the progress of the won stablecoin bill than to earnings. A delay in the legislative timetable could push back expectations for new businesses.

The speed at which the company's stablecoin infrastructure can be commercialized depends on the regulatory framework.

Fee structure and card issuer relations

The outcome of interchange-related fee negotiations with card issuers directly affects the company's margin. NH Investment & Securities attributed margin improvement to the completion of negotiations in 3Q 2025, but expanding direct card approval could change the fee structure. Shifts in negotiating power between merchants and card issuers remain a margin variable.

Concentration in overseas volume

Overseas merchant transaction value growth in 3Q 2026 depends heavily on vehicle sales at global electric and imported car merchants. Growth concentrated in one sector and a specific merchant group can slow if sales in that sector weaken. Balancing domestic and overseas growth is an important task for the company.

12

Overall view

NHN KCP's 3Q 2026 showed sales and profit moving in different directions. Revenue of KRW 386.7 billion and transaction value of KRW 17.349 trillion were record quarterly levels, led by a 75% increase in overseas merchant transaction value.

Operating profit of KRW 14 billion and profit attributable to owners of KRW 10.1 billion fell 11.2% and 25.8% year on year.

On an annual basis, revenue has risen for three consecutive years since 2023 and operating profit peaked at KRW 54.7 billion in 2025, yet the 2025 operating margin of 4.4% remained below the 5.4% of 2022.

The next point to confirm is whether overseas volume growth translates into margin improvement, which the 2026 annual report will show. AI agent payment and stablecoin infrastructure has moved through mainnet construction and demonstrations, and its revenue contribution has not yet been disclosed in figures.

Broker views coexist, from Kiwoom Securities' June 2026 target price of KRW 22,000 to earlier comments on margin pressure. Strengths and weaknesses carry equal weight here, and the factual tracking point is how transaction growth and operating profit align in upcoming disclosures.
