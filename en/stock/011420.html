02

Business structure

Galaxia SM was founded in 1975, listed in 1989, and became a Hyosung Group affiliate in 2011. Its core business is distributing fitness equipment and supplying solutions under an exclusive domestic distribution right for the Italian premium fitness brand Technogym.

The company is extending supply beyond fitness centers to corporations, hotels, hospitals and sports facilities, offering customized wellness and performance solutions. Sports marketing, player management and an advertising service specialized for apartment sales are added on top of that structure.

Sources differ on the revenue mix. The job site Jobkorea put health equipment at 76% and marketing and others at 24% as of March 2026.

A press report based on cumulative figures through Q3 2024 said product sales from distribution overwhelmed service sales from marketing, while a financial information site described sports marketing as the main revenue line with equipment sales following. Under either view, distribution makes up a large share.

Given the nature of distribution, margins are low and inventory management costs arise, a point raised in the same reporting. Since 2024 the company has pushed direct sales through offline showrooms and online platforms to strengthen marketing to B2C and premium customers.

Unlike advertising agencies such as Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide, its structure carries a high share of hardware distribution.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales moved from KRW 30.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 33 billion in 2023, up 9.5%, then fell 1.4% to KRW 32.5 billion in 2024 before rising 24.2% to KRW 40.3 billion in 2025, the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating profit was KRW 4.17 billion in 2022 and KRW 4.58 billion in 2023, up 9.8%, making 2023 the largest year in the span, while 2024 at KRW 3.23 billion, down 29.6%, was the smallest. The KRW 3.99 billion operating profit in 2025, up 23.6%, recovered ground but remained below the 2023 level.

The operating margin fell from 13.9% in both 2022 and 2023 to 9.9% in both 2024 and 2025. Even with sales growth in 2025, the margin stayed at the 2024 level of 9.9%, so top-line recovery did not translate into margin recovery.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 4.61 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.67 billion in 2023, down 42.0%, and then rose for two consecutive years, in 2024 and 2025, reaching KRW 3.72 billion in 2025, up 28.4%, which is still below 2022.

The debt ratio went from 73.6% in 2022 to 20.5% in 2023, 10.9% in 2024 and 14.6% in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 872 million in 2022, KRW 6.93 billion in 2023, KRW 1.45 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.77 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q4 2025 sales of KRW 14.4 billion, up 39.0% from the prior quarter, were the highest of the last five quarters. Q1 2026 sales of KRW 6.49 billion, down 54.9% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 71.4 million, down 95.1%, marked a sharp drop.

Q2 2026 recovered to sales of KRW 11 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.75 billion, but Q3 2026 sales fell to KRW 5.99 billion, down 42.1% year on year, with an operating loss of KRW 771 million. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 2.33 billion.

A press report cited new distribution rights and the timing of marketing agency contracts as factors behind the quarterly volatility.

05

Industry analysis

Demand in South Korea's healthcare and fitness market is being driven by population aging and greater health awareness, and one analysis points to rising demand for home fitness and digital training solutions.

B2B demand is being reshaped around digital content, high-performance equipment and customized space design, according to one assessment. The wellness solution that combines Technogym hardware with content is the high-value ecosystem the company is promoting.

The company said it exhibited at KIMES in 2026, an unusual move for a fitness equipment maker, showing AI-based healthcare solutions and rehabilitation and exercise solutions.

Among 49 companies in the entertainment sector on an annual basis, the company ranked 8th on operating margin, 29th on market capitalization and 33rd on sales, according to a financial information site as of early October 2026.

Unlike advertising agencies such as Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide, its distribution-centered structure tends to show lower margins.

In competition, the status of its exclusive Technogym distribution rights is central, and a legal dispute with a former importer over trade secret misappropriation has been reported.

Entry into the medical and rehabilitation market targets hospital and public health facility customers, a different base from its traditional fitness equipment buyers.

06

Outlook

In its August 14, 2026 first-half announcement, the company emphasized steady growth in its core Technogym business and the effect of operating efficiency measures.

First-half consolidated sales were KRW 17.5 billion, up 12% year on year, operating profit was KRW 1.8 billion, up 26%, and the operating margin rose 1.2 percentage points to 10.4% from 9.2% a year earlier.

The company said it has spent the last three years restructuring weak businesses and shifting investment toward higher-growth, higher-margin areas. In 2026 it pursued new investments, including the acquisition of Bizbusiness, an online education operator, as reported by the press.

In the medical and rehabilitation market, it has started offering customized solutions to hospitals, rehabilitation centers and public health facilities, according to reporting tied to its 2026 annual shareholders meeting.

The company has not presented quantitative targets and has only stated a direction of finding new growth opportunities and raising corporate value. Q3 2026 showed a sales decline and an operating loss, so whether the first-half growth trend carried into the second half remains an open question.

Q4 2026 results have not yet been disclosed in a periodic report. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 sales and operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and the share of revenue from the medical and rehabilitation business.

Profitability of newly acquired businesses can only be confirmed through results after the acquisition, which leaves the outlook highly uncertain.

08

Bull factors

Sales growth led by Technogym

First-half 2026 sales reached KRW 17.5 billion, up 12% year on year. Annual sales in 2025 rose 24.2% to KRW 40.3 billion, the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span. The company attributes the top-line growth to steady expansion of its core Technogym business.

Signs of improved profitability

The operating margin rose from 9.9% in 2024 and 2025 to 10.4% in the first half of 2026. The company cites the exit of weak businesses and efficiency measures as drivers. Operating profit growth outpacing sales growth is also presented as evidence of this shift.

Expansion into medical and rehabilitation

The company has begun proposing customized solutions to hospitals, rehabilitation centers and public health facilities. It showed AI-based healthcare solutions at KIMES in 2026. It is also pursuing a broader portfolio, including the reported acquisition of the online education operator Bizbusiness.

09

Bear factors

Swing to loss in Q3 2026

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 5.99 billion, down 42.1% year on year. The quarter posted an operating loss of KRW 771 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 447 million. Together with Q1 2026 operating profit falling to KRW 71.4 million, this points to large quarterly swings.

Distribution-heavy model with thin margins

A press analysis notes that much of revenue comes from product distribution, which carries thin margins and inventory costs. The operating margin fell from 13.9% in 2022 and 2023 to 9.9% in 2024 and 2025. Operating cash flow also dropped to KRW 1.45 billion in 2024 from KRW 6.93 billion in 2023.

Distribution-right dispute and governance concerns

A press report described a lawsuit from a former importer alleging trade secret misappropriation during the process of securing the exclusive domestic Technogym rights. As a Hyosung Group affiliate, the company has also drawn governance-related concerns in one analysis. Any change in the distribution arrangement could narrow its core revenue base.

Risk factors

Quarterly earnings volatility

Over the last five quarters, sales swung between a 54.9% drop and a 69.8% rise from the prior quarter. Whether the Q3 2026 loss is one-off or structural will have to be settled by the periodic reports. A press report also links cash flow swings to the timing of new distribution rights and marketing agency contracts.

Distribution rights and legal disputes

Because the core business depends on the Technogym domestic distribution right, changes in contract terms or rights directly affect results. Reports describe a legal dispute with a former importer. A change in the distribution structure could narrow the revenue base.

Governance and small-scale market position

One analysis says governance and related-party matters need scrutiny because the company is a Hyosung Group affiliate. It ranks 29th by market capitalization and 33rd by sales within its sector, in the lower-middle tier. Its small scale may make it more sensitive to external shocks to trading and the share price.

Overall view

Galaxia SM is built around the Technogym domestic distribution business, and in 2025 both sales of KRW 40.3 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.72 billion increased.

The operating margin fell from 13.9% in 2022 and 2023 to 9.9% in 2024 and 2025 and then rose to 10.4% in the first half of 2026, according to the company. By contrast, Q3 2026 sales fell 42.1% year on year and the quarter posted an operating loss of KRW 771 million.

Strengths include growth centered on Technogym, margin gains from efficiency measures, and expansion into medical and rehabilitation, while weaknesses include quarterly volatility, thin margins from a distribution-heavy model, and concerns over distribution rights disputes and governance.

On the financial side, total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 72.3 billion and total liabilities were KRW 10.6 billion, a debt ratio of 14.6%. On valuation, the price-to-book ratio is below 1 times and the dividend yield is below the sector average.

On an earnings basis, profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 2.33 billion and includes the Q3 2026 net loss.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results in the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report, and the key question is whether the quarterly loss is one-off or structural.
