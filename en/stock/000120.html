02

Business structure

CJ Logistics runs three segments: O-NE parcel delivery, Contract Logistics (CL), and Global. In parcel delivery, a seven-day delivery service and an individual parcel service are driving volume growth. Total parcel volume rose 4.6% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, outpacing market growth.

In preliminary results announced on November 10, 2026, O-NE revenue was KRW 976.5 billion, up 1.2%, while operating profit fell 39.5% to KRW 38.0 billion.

CL revenue was KRW 933.0 billion, up 8.3%, and operating profit was KRW 49.2 billion, down 11%, with warehousing and delivery (W&D), fulfillment and middle-mile transport leading growth.

Global revenue rose 13.1% to KRW 1.17 trillion, supported by strategic markets such as the United States, India and Vietnam and by cross-border e-commerce (CBE) volumes, but operating profit was only KRW 11.8 billion.

Large e-commerce shippers are a key customer base, and the W&D business expanded after acquiring logistics assets of large retailers such as E-Mart in 2025. Coupang is the most direct rival in parcel delivery, and a Korea Investment & Securities analyst described it as the company's original competitor. Technologically, the company began co-developing a logistics humanoid robot with Rainbow Robotics last year.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 12.1 trillion in 2022, KRW 11.8 trillion in 2023 (down 3.0% year on year), KRW 12.1 trillion in 2024 (up 3.0%) and KRW 12.3 trillion in 2025 (up 1.4%). Revenue has risen for two consecutive years, from 2024 through 2025, and 2025 is the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

Operating profit rose from KRW 411.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 480.2 billion in 2023 (up 16.6%) and KRW 530.7 billion in 2024 (up 10.5%), making 2024 the largest year, before falling to KRW 508.1 billion in 2025 (down 4.3%).

The operating margin climbed from 3.4% in 2022 to 4.4% in 2024 and eased to 4.1% in 2025, so margins did not widen at the same pace as sales.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 248.5 billion in 2024 and was KRW 242.1 billion in 2025 (down 2.6%), while net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 258.7 billion in 2025, a different figure.

Quarterly operating profit moved from KRW 147.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 159.6 billion in the fourth quarter (up 7.9% quarter on quarter), then hit a low of KRW 92.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 42.3%).

It then rose for two consecutive quarters, to KRW 101.6 billion in the second quarter (up 10.3%) and KRW 110.5 billion in the third (up 8.8%), yet the third-quarter figure was 25.3% lower than a year earlier.

Chuseok moved from the fourth quarter of 2025 into the third quarter of 2026, cutting O-NE operating days, and service investment and higher operating costs from oil and price increases added to the pressure.

Cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 was KRW 9.95 trillion (up 9.3%), and cumulative operating profit was KRW 304.2 billion (down 12.7%). At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 4.44 trillion and total liabilities were KRW 5.9 trillion, with the debt ratio rising to 132.9% from 130.6% in 2024. Operating cash flow was KRW 902.4 billion in 2025, up from KRW 606.2 billion in 2024.

05

Industry analysis

Parcel volume momentum is firm. Overnight and same-day delivery volume rose 8% and e-commerce fulfillment volume rose 16% in the third quarter of 2026.

Coupang remains the central competitive reference, and a January 2026 analysis argued that premium services such as seven-day delivery had been hard to expand while domestic logistics demand was weak.

Unit pricing is under pressure. iM Securities said in August 2026 that parcel unit prices fell 3.9% and extended a decline for ten consecutive quarters despite volume growth of 11%. Sangsangin Securities cited lower average selling prices from parcel promotions as the main cause of profit weakness in April 2026.

In contract logistics, Hana Securities said in April 2026 that stabilization of W&D centers acquired from large retailers was slower than expected. Oil and price increases remain an external cost headwind.

Expectations for market share reshuffling tied to a revised large-retailer distribution law were also delayed, according to an April 2026 analysis. The cycle picture is mixed, with volume growing while unit prices and costs press on margins.

In global logistics, the company is linking freight forwarding with local contract logistics to build end-to-end capability. Domestic parcel peers such as Hanjin and Logen compete in the same market.

06

Outlook

Management has framed the next phase as moving from volume growth to higher-quality growth. CEO Shin Young-soo said at a second-half town hall in August 2026 that the company must convert revenue growth into profit, since operating profit fell on external factors.

In Korea the plan is to strengthen market position and profitability, while abroad the company aims to widen its business scope and customer base. Overseas, it is building networks and customer bases in strategic markets such as the United States and India.

The company is exiting non-core shipping in Vietnam to focus on contract logistics. Operationally it is pursuing AI and automation to improve operations and expand new contracts at home and abroad.

Estimates vary. iM Securities lowered its 2026 and 2027 operating profit estimates by 4% and 5% and projected 2026 operating profit of KRW 462.0 billion, below the KRW 508.1 billion recorded in 2025.

Korea Investment & Securities, by contrast, said in July 2026 that operating profit should rebound from the first quarter. The next checkpoint is the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary result, where the Chuseok calendar shift will show up in the year-on-year comparison. The next regular filing is the 2026 annual report, which will disclose the full-year operating margin.

08

Bull factors

Parcel volume outpaces the market

The seven-day delivery and individual parcel services led the gains, with overnight and same-day volume up 8% and fulfillment volume up 16%. A January 2026 analysis said the company emerged as an alternative after Coupang-related issues.

Contract logistics and global revenue expand

Contract logistics revenue reached KRW 933.0 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 8.3%, and W&D revenue grew 11.8%. Global revenue rose 13.1%, and CBE volume grew at a double-digit rate. The company said new contracts and volume expanded across all business units.

Quarterly operating profit recovery

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 92.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then rose to KRW 101.6 billion and KRW 110.5 billion over the next two quarters. Korea Investment & Securities said in its July 2026 report that operating profit should rebound from the first-quarter low.

Part of the third-quarter decline stemmed from the Chuseok shift, which is a factor to check in the fourth-quarter comparison.

09

Bear factors

Parcel unit prices offset volume gains

iM Securities said parcel volume rose 11% while unit prices fell 3.9%, extending a decline for ten consecutive quarters. Sangsangin Securities cited lower average selling prices from ongoing promotions as the main cause in April 2026. Whether volume growth can offset unit price declines is the central issue for earnings recovery.

Operating cost and investment burden

Service upgrade investment and higher operating costs were cited as reasons for the third-quarter profit decline. Hana Securities noted in a May 2026 report that parcel hub investment would be spent over several quarters. Higher oil and material prices also add cost pressure in contract logistics.

Global profitability and lower full-year estimates

Global operating profit fell to KRW 11.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026 from a year earlier. iM Securities cut its target while saying the timing of the profit rebound is uncertain. Its 2026 operating profit estimate of KRW 462.0 billion is below 2025. Exiting Vietnam shipping improves the portfolio, but the timing of a profitability turn has not been confirmed.

10

Risk factors

Competition and policy

If Coupang-related issues persist, parcel volume allocation could shift. Stronger competition could push unit prices lower. Delays in the expected retail distribution law change and related market share reshuffling are also variables.

Costs and external environment

Higher oil and prices, along with Middle East related uncertainty, pushed up operating costs. In the third quarter of 2026, the global segment's operating profit declined because of prolonged external uncertainty and costs from expanding logistics hubs. The pace at which cost increases can be passed on to customers is the key question.

New assets and investment execution

Analysts said stabilizing the logistics centers acquired from large retailers in 2025 has taken longer than expected. If hub and service investment costs persist, the pace of operating margin recovery could be limited. Early costs of new contracts may keep weighing on profit for some time.

12

Overall view

CJ Logistics has grown revenue for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025, and third-quarter 2026 parcel volume outpaced the market, so top-line growth is clear.

However, operating profit peaked at KRW 530.7 billion in 2024, fell to KRW 508.1 billion in 2025, and dropped 25.3% year on year in the third quarter of 2026. Operating profit rising for two consecutive quarters after the first-quarter 2026 trough can be read as a sign of recovery.

Still, lower parcel unit prices, higher operating costs from oil and prices, and weak global profitability are cited as factors slowing the earnings recovery.

Broker targets include Korea Investment & Securities at KRW 125,000 in July 2026 and iM Securities at KRW 110,000 in August 2026, with both maintaining a Buy rating. iM Securities' 2026 operating profit estimate of KRW 462.0 billion is below 2025.

With strengths and weaknesses both present, the next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report. This report is for information only and does not include an investment opinion.
