Q3 2026 net loss swing; core profit recovery is the test

02

Business structure

SJM is an automotive parts company that was spun off through an instrument-based physical split from SJM Holdings in 2010. The business is divided into an automotive division and a plant division.

The automotive division makes parts that absorb noise and vibration in vehicle exhaust systems, while the plant division makes piping fittings for construction and plant use. Exhaust vibration absorbing parts are a product family that dampens vibration between the engine and the exhaust system (general description).

The automotive division is the core of the company. The company has been praised for maintaining sales through its high-value commercial vehicle business.

Its new business areas include development of the ZLTube EV cooling system component and localization of the Gimbal Joint for the KF-21 fighter jet, which expand its reach into electrified vehicles and aviation parts. The company also manufactures industrial products.

Its R&D spending in 2025 was KRW 4,584 million, equal to 2.28% of revenue. Because automotive revenue depends on the production volume and model mix of vehicle makers, the shift toward electrification directly affects the product demand mix. Its business mix differs from other listed automotive suppliers, which limits simple comparisons.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 180.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 198.9 billion in 2023 (up 10.3% YoY) and KRW 201.9 billion in 2024 (up 1.5%), then edged down to KRW 201.4 billion in 2025 (down 0.2%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 7.09 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.2 billion in 2023 (up 86.7%) and KRW 14.2 billion in 2024 (up 7.6%), then fell to KRW 11.3 billion in 2025 (down 20.5%). The operating margin rose from 3.9% in 2022 to 7.1% in 2024 before falling to 5.6% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders climbed from KRW 4.92 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.6 billion in 2023 (up 257.4%) and KRW 20.8 billion in 2024 (up 18.2%), then dropped to KRW 12.1 billion in 2025 (down 41.7%).

Total net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 10.7 billion in 2025, which differs from the controlling-shareholder figure because non-controlling interests recorded a loss.

Reports attributed the 2025 decline to weaker profitability across the business and deteriorating foreign-currency valuation gains and losses. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 9.34 billion in 2022 to KRW 34.4 billion in 2025, but the filed figures do not break down the drivers.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 240.2 billion, of which KRW 231.8 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 45.7 billion.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 50 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 49.5 billion in Q4 2025 (down 0.9% QoQ), and KRW 53.1 billion in Q1 2026 (up 7.2% QoQ), the peak of the period.

It then declined for two consecutive quarters from Q2 2026 to Q3 2026, reaching KRW 52.5 billion and KRW 49.7 billion (down 5.4% QoQ and down 0.5% YoY in Q3).

Operating profit was KRW 3.09 billion in Q3 2025, swung to an operating loss of KRW 164 million in Q4 2025, returned to KRW 3.14 billion in Q1 2026, rose to KRW 4.69 billion in Q2 2026 (up 49.2% QoQ), and fell to KRW 2.36 billion in Q3 2026 (down 49.7% QoQ and down 23.7% YoY).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.23 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 118 million in Q4 2025, KRW 6.56 billion in Q1 2026, and KRW 6.08 billion in Q2 2026, before a net loss of KRW 7.43 billion in Q3 2026. The Sihwa plant's production was halted from July 27 to September 26, 2026 under a lockout.

The filings do not separate the plant halt's effect on Q3 profit, so the post-resumption effect must be checked in later results.

05

Industry analysis

Vehicle makers have been reducing the share of internal-combustion vehicles and pushing toward hybrids and EVs amid a slowing economy.

Exhaust noise and vibration parts are used mainly in internal-combustion and hybrid vehicles, so the pace of electrification is a variable that reshapes the demand mix for the company's products (analysis). Pure EVs do not use exhaust system parts, so a larger EV share reads as a structural pressure.

By contrast, hybrids still carry demand for these parts, which partly cushions the transition period. The automotive division has been credited with holding sales through its high-value commercial vehicle business, but it is hard to gauge how far this offsets passenger-vehicle declines without segment figures.

In aviation, the company is widening its position in defense and aerospace parts through localization of KF-21 fighter components. The filings do not split out new-business revenue, so the speed of this shift cannot be measured in numbers.

R&D spending has stayed between about 1.8% and 2.3% of revenue since 2021, reaching 2.28% in 2025. Hyundai Mobis, Hanon Systems and Sungwoo Hitech are listed automotive peers, but their product mixes differ, so direct comparisons have limits.

Industry conditions depend on vehicle production plans and model mix, so quarterly revenue trends should be read alongside them.

After Q3 revenue declined from the prior quarter, whether quarterly revenue recovers after the resumption is the next checkpoint. The filings do not specify when the ZLTube and KF-21 Gimbal Joint localization efforts will translate into revenue.

The operating margin fell to 5.6% in 2025, so the 2026 annual report's full-year operating margin will be a key indicator of whether the stated profitability goal is progressing.

With net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaling KRW 5.33 billion over the last four quarters, the Q4 2026 result will shape the annual picture.

Bull factors

Financial capacity and low leverage

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 240.2 billion, equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 231.8 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 45.7 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 30.0% in 2022 to 19.0% in 2025.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 9.34 billion in 2022 to KRW 34.4 billion in 2025, so cash generation grew even in 2025 when profit declined.

Value-up plan and dividend returns

The company set profitability improvement and shareholder value enhancement as goals in the value-up plan its board approved in October 2026. The 2025 dividend payout ratio was 26.4%, and total dividends were KRW 3.199 billion, up 17.1% from two years earlier. The company reported that it qualifies as a high-dividend company.

Expansion into EV and aviation parts

The development of the ZLTube EV cooling system component and localization of the Gimbal Joint for the KF-21 fighter are presented as new business pillars. Success in aviation parts localization keeps open the possibility of widening the business into defense and aerospace demand. R&D spending in 2025 equaled 2.28% of revenue, so development costs continue.

Bear factors

2025 profit decline and Q3 2026 loss

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 11.3 billion, down 20.5% YoY, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 12.1 billion, down 41.7%. Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 2.36 billion, down 23.7% YoY, and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.43 billion. Over the last four quarters, that net income totaled only KRW 5.33 billion.

EV transition pressure on exhaust parts demand

Vehicle makers are pushing toward hybrids and EVs, which could reduce demand for internal-combustion-focused exhaust parts. Pure EVs do not use exhaust system parts, which creates a structural limit. Because the automotive division is the core, the pace of this shift feeds directly into results.

Production halt and quarterly volatility

Q4 2025 swung to an operating loss of KRW 164 million. The Sihwa plant halted production from July 27 to September 26, 2026 under a lockout before resuming. Revenue has also declined for two consecutive quarters from Q2 2026 to Q3 2026, which adds to the pressure.

The Sihwa plant stopped production from July 27 to September 26, 2026 and then resumed. The pace at which utilization recovers after the restart, and whether any further interruption occurs, will affect results. The filings do not separate the profit impact of the Q3 halt.

Value-up execution and price reflection

Overall view

SJM's disclosed results show revenue, operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders rising from 2022 through 2024 before profit declined in 2025.

Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 53.1 billion was the peak, and Q3 2026 showed revenue of KRW 49.7 billion, operating profit of KRW 2.36 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.43 billion.

On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was 19.0% and total equity was KRW 240.2 billion at the end of 2025, a lower leverage profile than in 2022. The value-up plan disclosed in October 2026 identifies the price-to-book level and market communication as challenges and sets profitability improvement as a goal.

On the industry side, electrification reduces demand for exhaust parts, while hybrid demand and new business expansion act as partial buffers. The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, the effect of the Sihwa plant's normalization in Q1 2027 results, and execution of the value-up plan.
