Profitable Trend Built on eSIM and Smart Learning, Quarterly Profit Volatile

02

Business structure

Uangel is a software company founded in 1999 that supplies core network and IoT solutions to fixed and mobile telecom operators in Korea and abroad. Its business spans four segments: Core Network, Smart Learning, Service, and Global Data.

Global Data, which includes eSIM services, accounted for 40.23% of 2025 revenue of KRW 61.6 billion. Its main product is the Roaming Dokkaebi eSIM service, which provides LTE and 5G data in more than 150 countries, and media reports said cumulative users passed 3 million.

Smart Learning runs B2B services for early childhood education institutions and B2C content for parents and children, and the company says it holds the number one share of Korea's early childhood education market.

The Core Network segment is described by the company as having contributed to profit improvement through core network solution supply. A single contract for 2026 maintenance phase two intelligent network equipment technical support with SK Telecom has been disclosed.

Customers fall into telecom operators and early childhood education buyers, so demand shifts in one segment do not automatically support results in another.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue rose from KRW 30 billion in 2022 to KRW 36.6 billion in 2023 (up 21.9% year on year), KRW 53 billion in 2024 (up 44.8%), and KRW 61.6 billion in 2025 (up 16.1%). Revenue grew for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit moved from KRW 846 million in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 1.78 billion in 2023, a turnaround to KRW 789 million in 2024, and KRW 3.42 billion in 2025 (up 333.6%). The operating margin was 2.8% in 2022, -4.9% in 2023, 1.5% in 2024, and 5.6% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 1.44 billion in 2023 (down 34.8%) from KRW 2.21 billion in 2022, then rose to KRW 3.66 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.36 billion in 2025, increasing for two consecutive years.

Net income including non-controlling interests is reported separately, at KRW 4.37 billion for 2025. Operating cash flow swung from -KRW 1.86 billion in 2024 to KRW 11.9 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 14.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.55 billion, and Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 15 billion with operating profit of KRW 339 million, down 78.1% from the prior quarter.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 17.7 billion, up 18.3% quarter on quarter, but operating profit was only KRW 202 million, down 40.5%, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.72 billion.

Q2 2026 revenue fell 14.3% to KRW 15.2 billion, while operating profit rose 511.9% to KRW 1.23 billion; an independent research report published in August 2026 attributed the margin gain mainly to product mix improvement.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 16.5 billion (up 14.2% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 2.62 billion (up 69.0% year on year). Net income attributable to controlling shareholders in the same quarter was KRW 72.35 million, down 96.2% from the prior quarter.

The sum of net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 4.11 billion. The non-operating items that explain the gap between operating profit and controlling shareholder net income are a point to check in the next regular report.

05

Industry analysis

The eSIM-based global data market is described as having grown mainly among people in their twenties and thirties as travel demand recovered after the pandemic. After a 2025 SK Telecom USIM hacking incident raised concern about physical USIM cards, media reported heightened interest in eSIM demand.

Because the segment relies on travel and substitution demand, travel conditions and pricing by competing services become variables for Global Data results. Smart Learning is cited as a stable revenue base because demand for early childhood education content has held up despite a low birth rate.

The Core Network segment is pursuing research on virtualization, service-based architecture, and new mobile protocols aimed at leadership in the 5G market, and demand for equipment and services moves with operators' network upgrade cycles.

Among IT services peers, large firms such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS have a higher share of enterprise IT services, so their business mix differs. Uangel combines core network solutions, B2B and B2C content for young children, and overseas data roaming, which gives it a less common business mix.

Its capital structure, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 22.7% and reported absence of borrowings, shows a low debt burden compared with the sector. Because the company is small, one contract or a shift in product mix can have a large effect on quarterly results.

06

Outlook

The corporate value enhancement plan the company disclosed targets higher eSIM roaming revenue and securing 6G references. Specific goals include expanding B2B market share in roaming data and taking part in national 5G and 6G research projects.

To support this, the company said it will step up marketing and investment in its Roaming Dokkaebi Biz brand. The company said it will retire 200,000 treasury shares to raise per-share value, keep its payout ratio at 25% or higher, and change the dividend process so the dividend amount is set before the record date.

The dividend paid for 2025 was KRW 123.6 million, equal to a 28.3% payout ratio. The company also said it will keep advancing AI-based learning services and expanding global roaming markets in the second half of 2026. The publicly stated goals are mainly qualitative, and no quantitative target has been confirmed.

The timing of the treasury share retirement and the outcome of the 6G research project participation should be confirmed through disclosures. Whether the year-on-year operating profit gains seen in Q3 2026 continue can be judged from operating margin trends in the next regular report.

Whether the sharp decline in quarterly net income attributable to controlling shareholders reflects a temporary factor can be checked in the non-operating line items of the next regular report.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in operating profit

Operating profit recovered from an operating loss of KRW 1.78 billion in 2023 to KRW 3.42 billion in 2025, with a 5.6% operating margin in 2025. Q3 2026 operating profit reached KRW 2.62 billion, up 69.0% year on year.

Q2 2026 operating profit rose 511.9% from the prior quarter, which an independent research report linked to product mix improvement. Whether this recovery direction holds is the core of the business-side bull case.

Debt-light balance sheet and shareholder returns

Total liabilities were KRW 14.4 billion at the end of 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio was 22.7%. Media coverage highlighted the company's operating without borrowings as a strength.

The company disclosed a plan to retire 200,000 treasury shares and keep its payout ratio at 25% or higher, and the 2025 payout ratio was 28.3%. Financial capacity alongside a return plan is cited as a bullish factor.

eSIM roaming and 6G projects

Global Data accounted for 40.23% of 2025 revenue, and Roaming Dokkaebi is reported to serve more than 150 countries with cumulative users above 3 million. The company set targets of expanding B2B roaming share and joining national 5G and 6G research projects. If travel and overseas data demand continue, this segment could lead revenue growth.

09

Bear factors

Large swings in quarterly profit

Operating profit fell 78.1% quarter on quarter to KRW 339 million in Q4 2025, and fell 40.5% to KRW 202 million in Q1 2026. Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 2.62 billion, yet net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 72.35 million, down 96.2% from the prior quarter. Large quarterly swings between operating profit and net income are cited as a bearish factor.

Slowing revenue growth

Revenue growth slowed from 44.8% in 2024 to 16.1% in 2025. Q2 2026 revenue fell 14.3% quarter on quarter to KRW 15.2 billion, and Q3 2026 revenue rose only 14.2% year on year. The fact that the KRW 17.7 billion peak of Q1 2026 was not sustained is cited as a bearish point.

Governance uncertainty

In a November 2025 disclosure, the company said it had become aware that a third-party company had petitioned the court for permission to convene a shareholder meeting. Details of the petition were not disclosed. The largest shareholder and related parties are recorded as holding 17.53%. Uncertainty around the shareholding structure is cited as a shareholder-related variable.

10

Risk factors

Dependence on travel and overseas data demand

Global Data accounted for 40.23% of 2025 revenue, and eSIM roaming is closely tied to travel demand. If travel demand weakens or competing services cut prices, growth in this segment could slow. Whether user growth for Roaming Dokkaebi holds is a point to confirm.

Low birth rate and smart learning demand

Smart Learning is a content business aimed at young children, so it is exposed to the decline in births. Media reported that demand has held up despite the low birth rate, but the structural pressure from a shrinking target population remains over the long term.

Whether the company keeps its number one share of Korea's early childhood education market is a point to confirm.

Governance and shareholder dispute risk

The petition to convene a shareholder meeting is a matter the company said it became aware of. The largest shareholder holds 17.53%, and related-party holdings are separately recorded, so changes in the shareholder structure could affect decisions. Confirming the treasury share retirement and shareholder return plan through disclosures is necessary.

Overall view

Uangel's disclosed results from 2022 to 2025 show revenue growth and a recovery in operating profit. Revenue was largest in 2025 at KRW 61.6 billion, and operating profit recovered from an operating loss of KRW 1.78 billion in 2023 to KRW 3.42 billion in 2025.

The 2025 operating margin was 5.6%, and the debt-to-equity ratio of 22.7% points to a light debt burden. However, Q3 2026 operating profit rose to KRW 2.62 billion while net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 72.35 million, so quarterly earnings volatility is pronounced.

The sum of such net income over the last four quarters is KRW 4.11 billion. On the bullish side, the discussion centers on eSIM roaming growth, the target of joining 6G projects, and the treasury share retirement with a payout ratio of at least 25%.

On the bearish side, the discussion centers on slowing revenue growth, concern over smart learning demand amid a low birth rate, and governance issues such as the petition to convene a shareholder meeting.

The non-operating items behind the gap between operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders are the key point to check in the 2026 annual report.
