LiComm: Order Growth Coexists with Quarterly Losses

LiComm turned profitable on an annual basis in 2025, but its operating loss widened to KRW 906 million in the third quarter of 2026, and order flow for AI data center amplifiers and defense fiber lasers is the main variable for future earnings.

  1. 1

    Annual operating profit for 2025 was KRW 582 million (3.3% operating margin), a swing from the operating loss of KRW 5.34 billion in 2024, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 261 million.

  2. 2

    Quarterly profitability swung sharply in 2026, with an operating loss of KRW 880 million in the first quarter, operating profit of KRW 133 million in the second, and an operating loss of KRW 906 million in the third.

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

LiComm, founded in 1999 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2023, develops and sells optical communication modules, including optical amplifiers, analog optical transceivers and repeaters, and is pushing into fiber lasers and sensing.

By segment, optical amplifiers accounted for 72%, optical repeaters 24%, optical transceivers 3% and lasers 2% of second-quarter 2026 revenue, according to the Butler company data aggregator. Optical amplifiers are used for data center interconnect (DCI), the high-capacity transmission between and within data centers.

SK Securities explained that the products go to global optical transmission equipment makers such as Fujitsu and NEC and are ultimately used on optical networks of AT&T, Verizon and NTT and in traffic handling for big tech data centers. Per SK Securities, amplifier revenue was KRW 9.9 billion in 2025.

Fiber lasers are expanding into defense and industrial uses, and SK Securities noted first defense revenue of KRW 4.3 billion in 2025 following a KRW 5.3 billion order from LIG Nex1 for a laser counter-drone weapon localization project.

The company was also selected as a participating institution in a KRW 105 billion aerospace semiconductor strategy research group and is developing amplifiers for low-earth-orbit satellite communications. Its March 2026 shareholder meeting added industrial fiber laser rental and online sales to its business purposes.

Customers fall into two groups, overseas optical transmission equipment makers and domestic defense system contractors, so quarterly revenue can move sharply with the timing of orders from a few clients.

Media coverage groups LiComm with TAIHAN Fiberoptics as optical communication stocks, and the company's early defense laser supply record is cited as a point of difference.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 19.5 billion in 2023, KRW 13.1 billion in 2024 (down 32.5% year on year) and KRW 17.7 billion in 2025 (up 34.6%). Operating losses widened from KRW 2.12 billion in 2023 to KRW 5.34 billion in 2024 before the company posted operating profit of KRW 582 million in 2025, a turnaround to profit.

Operating margin moved from -10.9% in 2023 and -40.7% in 2024 to 3.3% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from a loss of KRW 5.8 billion in 2023 and a loss of KRW 3.75 billion in 2024 to a profit of KRW 261 million in 2025.

The company credited its 2025 result to the first commercialization in defense, higher supply of key products for AI data centers and new deliveries of industrial laser equipment. Quarterly results are volatile.

In the third quarter of 2025 revenue was KRW 3.26 billion with an operating loss of KRW 69.92 million, and in the fourth quarter revenue rose to KRW 8.13 billion (up 149.6% from the prior quarter) with operating profit of KRW 2.18 billion, a swing from the prior quarter.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 1.5 billion in that fourth quarter. In the first quarter of 2026 revenue fell 66.8% quarter on quarter to KRW 2.7 billion and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 880 million.

In the second quarter revenue recovered to KRW 4.53 billion (up 67.9%) with operating profit of KRW 133 million, and third-quarter revenue of KRW 4.59 billion rose 1.3% sequentially and 41.0% year on year, the second consecutive quarterly increase.

Yet the third-quarter operating loss of KRW 906 million was a swing back to a loss from the prior quarter and widened from a year earlier.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders were KRW 716 million in the first quarter and KRW 1.18 billion in the third, and the last four quarters total a net loss of KRW 286 million. Operating cash flow was negative at -KRW 1.37 billion in 2024 and -KRW 951 million in 2025.

Revenue growth has not yet translated into better operating profit, which is the central task for 2026 quarterly results.

05

Industry analysis

Securities analysts broadly describe optical communications as a market recovering from a downturn. IBK Securities, in a December 2025 report, said improving demand in the optical communication market and revenue recognition for fiber lasers were coinciding.

On the demand side, SK Securities expects growing data center traffic tied to AI infrastructure expansion to lift demand for optical amplifiers. On the defense side, demand for laser weapon systems against drone threats is cited as expanding.

SK Securities described LiComm as a key laser light source supplier in a domestic laser counter-drone weapon localization project. IBK Securities said the company had moved toward fiber lasers to get ahead of possible end-of-life (EOL) for its main optical transceiver products.

Transceivers made up only 3% of second-quarter 2026 revenue, so the business mix has already shifted toward amplifiers and lasers. On the cycle, one view holds that optical component demand is only at the early stage of recovery, while another sees 2026 as the year North American AI data center orders begin in earnest.

Competitively, LiComm is a supply partner to global optical transmission equipment makers and has secured early references in defense lasers, a field with high entry barriers. A market-share comparison with TAIHAN Fiberoptics or other peers is not drawn here, as the sources do not support one.

06

Outlook

SK Securities projects 2026 revenue of KRW 22.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 800 million, up 27.5% and 43.9% respectively, with an operating margin of 3.7%.

The same report sees amplifier revenue rising to KRW 13.8 billion in 2026 and KRW 21.8 billion in 2027, and defense revenue expanding from KRW 4.9 billion in 2026 to KRW 15.8 billion in 2027.

For 2027 it forecasts revenue of KRW 43.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 10.1 billion, a 23.3% operating margin, as earnings leverage begins. These forecasts date from June 5, 2026, so the gap between them and actual results should be checked against each new disclosure.

Because operating losses already occurred in the first and third quarters of 2026, reaching the KRW 800 million forecast would require a fourth-quarter recovery.

On orders, the supply contract with 1Finity has a confirmed value of KRW 3.87 billion, equal to 21.87% of recent revenue, and runs from June 2, 2026 to December 6, 2027.

A supplementary laser generator contract with LIG Nex1 for KRW 790 million was signed on March 27, 2026 and runs through December 20, 2026, so whether follow-on volumes arrive is a key check on the defense revenue path. The company says it has achieved a laser module output more than twice its previous level.

In aerospace, amplifiers for low-earth-orbit satellite communications are still in development, and no commercialization date has been confirmed in the disclosures reviewed.

Share price level and ownership and dilution factors

Risk factors

Customer and order concentration

Optical amplifier revenue depends on a small number of global optical transmission equipment makers such as Fujitsu and NEC. The main transceiver line faces possible end-of-life.

Defense supply contracts are tied to individual terms, such as the LIG Nex1 contract through December 20, 2026, so follow-on orders determine revenue visibility.

Defense program timing and policy risk

SK Securities' projection of defense revenue reaching KRW 15.8 billion in 2027 depends on the timing of mass production in the domestic laser counter-drone program. A delay could push back the timing of the expected 2027 earnings leverage. Defense revenue began only in 2025, so quarterly swings may be large.

Dilution, liquidity and cash flow risk

A convertible bond issuance decision was disclosed on July 8, 2026, and conversion could increase the share count. Operating cash flow was negative in both 2024 and 2025. A high major shareholder stake and a low free float ratio may amplify share price volatility.

Overall view

LiComm turned to an annual operating profit of KRW 582 million in 2025, and fourth-quarter 2025 revenue and operating profit rose sharply, but quarterly profitability in 2026 has been unstable, with operating losses of KRW 880 million in the first quarter and KRW 906 million in the third.

Revenue rose for two consecutive quarters in the second and third quarters of 2026, yet the third-quarter operating loss widened from a year earlier.

On the business side, AI data center amplifier orders and defense fiber laser supply are confirmed growth lines, and SK Securities forecasts 2027 operating profit of KRW 10.1 billion.

Against that, net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters total KRW 286 million, and operating cash flow was negative in both 2024 and 2025. The share price sits in a high range relative to book value, so whether the earnings turnaround holds is central to interpreting valuation.

Neither SK Securities nor IBK Securities published a target price or investment rating. The next checkpoints are the expiry of the LIG Nex1 contract period on December 20, 2026 and the annual 2026 results disclosure expected in early February 2027.
