02

Business structure

Ubiquoss manufactures and sells wireline data transmission equipment for internet networks.

Based on the Q1 2026 quarterly report, sales consisted of network equipment such as switches at KRW 13.64 billion (59.5%), FTTH (fiber-to-the-home) equipment at KRW 7.40 billion (32.3%), and maintenance and other revenue at KRW 1.88 billion (8.2%). Production in the same quarter was 14,894 switches and 462 FTTH units.

Domestic sales accounted for 98.9% of revenue, at KRW 22.66 billion, while exports were only KRW 262 million. Its main customers are telecom operators including KT and LG Uplus, so the investment scale of these operators largely determines sales.

An August 2024 press report said KT and LG Uplus reduced equipment investment as 5G infrastructure buildout neared completion. Raw material purchases in Q1 2026 totaled KRW 20.60 billion, of which integrated circuits (ICs) made up KRW 6.04 billion (29.3%) and optical modules KRW 2.97 billion (14.4%).

In a January 2026 report, Hana Securities described Ubiquoss as the largest domestic wireline network equipment maker. Switches and FTTH together accounted for more than 90% of Q1 2026 sales, indicating high product concentration. At end-2025, the debt ratio was 14.1% and total liabilities were KRW 27.6 billion.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed annual basis, sales rose from KRW 133.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 150.3 billion in 2023 (up 12.4% year on year), fell to KRW 103.8 billion in 2024 (down 30.9%), and recovered to KRW 116.4 billion in 2025 (up 12.2%).

Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year for sales, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders, and 2024 was the smallest. Operating profit was KRW 24.5 billion in 2022, KRW 29 billion in 2023 (up 18.3%), KRW 12.4 billion in 2024 (down 57.4%) and KRW 23.1 billion in 2025 (up 86.8%).

The operating margin was 18.4% in 2022, 19.3% in 2023, 11.9% in 2024 and 19.8% in 2025, so the margin was lowest in 2024 when sales fell and rebounded as sales grew in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 21.5 billion in 2022, KRW 25.9 billion in 2023 (up 20.5%), KRW 21.3 billion in 2024 (down 17.8%) and KRW 24.1 billion in 2025 (up 13.0%). Net profit including non-controlling interests was filed at the same levels for each year.

Operating cash flow was KRW 1.92 billion in 2022, KRW 30.5 billion in 2023, KRW 38.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 24.5 billion in 2025, with 2024 the highest. At end-2025, total equity was KRW 195.8 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 27.6 billion.

The debt ratio fell from 27.1% in 2022 to 14.1% in 2025. On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 sales were KRW 28.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 5.57 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 5.96 billion.

Q4 2025 sales rose to KRW 35 billion (up 24.0% quarter on quarter), with operating profit of KRW 9.1 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 9.33 billion.

Q1 2026 sales fell to KRW 22.9 billion (down 34.5% quarter on quarter), with operating profit of KRW 3.66 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.25 billion; a ThinkPool earnings flash put Q1 sales 9.8% below market estimates (reported May 15, 2026).

Q2 2026 sales were KRW 35.7 billion (up 55.7% quarter on quarter), with operating profit of KRW 9.01 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 10 billion.

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 27.8 billion (down 22.3% quarter on quarter and down 1.6% year on year), operating profit KRW 6.28 billion (up 12.7% year on year), and profit attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 1.1 billion (down 81.5% year on year).

The gap between Q3 operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders widened sharply, and the items behind that gap cannot be identified from the summary figures alone. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 27.7 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for wireline telecom equipment moves sharply with operators' network buildout cycles. An August 2024 press report said KT and LG Uplus cut equipment investment as 5G infrastructure buildout neared completion.

A February 2026 report described Ubiquoss's main subsidiary as entering a maturity phase after 5G commercialization, with growth slowing. Against that backdrop, Hana Securities cited the Ministry of Science and ICT's fiber coverage expansion policy as a driver of demand recovery in its January 2026 report.

The policy targets raising fiber coverage from 91% to 98% by 2030 and expanding backbone capacity more than fourfold. The same report argued that physical AI could increase backbone traffic by up to 30 times, supporting switch replacement demand over several years.

Ubiquoss's position in the cycle reads as a stage after the 5G equipment investment adjustment, waiting for policy-driven demand. It is regarded as a leading domestic wireline equipment maker, while a ThinkPool analysis said its sales and operating profit growth in Q1 2026 were both lower than peers.

06

Outlook

The most recent filed quarter is Q3 2026, and the next periodic report will be the 2026 annual business report. Q3 sales of KRW 27.8 billion fell from the prior quarter but remained well below the KRW 35.7 billion of Q2, showing large quarter-to-quarter swings.

In its January 2026 report, Hana Securities expected FTTH sales to recover under the fiber coverage policy and lead a broader sales recovery. FTTH made up 32.3% of Q1 2026 sales, so the size of this share will show how much the policy is contributing.

Switches were the largest category at 59.5% of Q1 sales, and the key question is whether rising backbone traffic turns into actual replacement orders. The 98% fiber coverage target runs to 2030 and is not directly linked to near-term earnings.

The same report raised the possibility of M&A using the company's cash resources, but that was the brokerage's suggestion rather than a company announcement. Order volumes are likely to become visible when operators disclose annual capex plans. The next checkpoint is the sales mix and operating margin in the 2026 annual business report.

08

Bull factors

Policy-driven demand

In its January 2026 report, Hana Securities argued that fiber coverage expansion and backbone capacity plans would support FTTH and switch demand. The same report noted that FTTH accounts for up to two-thirds of Ubiquoss sales. The core of the analysis is that FTTH sales could recover if the policy is implemented.

Earnings recovery

Operating profit rose 86.8% to KRW 23.1 billion in 2025, and the operating margin recovered to 19.8% from 11.9% in 2024. Q2 2026 sales of KRW 35.7 billion were the highest among the filed quarters. According to ThinkPool, Q1 2026 operating profit rose 27% year on year.

Balance sheet and shareholder returns

The debt ratio fell to 14.1% at end-2025 from 27.1% in 2022, and total liabilities were KRW 27.6 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 24.5 billion in 2025. Hana Securities pointed to the company raising its shareholder return ratio toward 40%.

09

Bear factors

Dependence on telecom capex cycles

A 2024 press report said KT and LG Uplus cut equipment investment after 5G buildout wound down. Under this structure, sales swing with operator orders. Sales fell 30.9% to KRW 103.8 billion in 2024.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Q3 2026 sales of KRW 27.8 billion fell 22.3% from the prior quarter, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.1 billion fell 81.5% year on year. Q1 2026 sales were 9.8% below market estimates according to ThinkPool. Quarterly swings are large, which makes the annual trend harder to read.

Product and customer concentration

Switches and FTTH equipment together accounted for more than 90% of Q1 2026 sales, and domestic sales were 98.9%. With the customer base concentrated in a few operators such as KT and LG Uplus, the business is sensitive to their ordering schedules. ThinkPool said both sales and operating profit growth were lower than peers.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and capex cycles

A large share of sales depends on orders from operators such as KT and LG Uplus. When operators reduce capex, sales swings widen. The 30.9% sales decline in 2024 is an example of this structure.

Key component sourcing

Of the KRW 20.60 billion in Q1 2026 raw material purchases, integrated circuits accounted for 29.3% and optical modules for 14.4%. The price and availability of these two components can affect margin trends. The product mix relies heavily on these parts.

Policy pace and earnings swings

The 98% fiber coverage target and backbone expansion are medium-term plans running to 2030. If policy execution slows, the timing of an FTTH recovery also shifts. Short-term earnings volatility is also high, as Q3 2026 profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 1.1 billion.

12

Overall view

On filed figures, Ubiquoss moved past its 2024 earnings low and recovered in 2025 with sales of KRW 116.4 billion, operating profit of KRW 23.1 billion and an operating margin of 19.8%. The business depends heavily on operator orders, and switches and FTTH equipment made up most of Q1 2026 sales.

Quarterly sales in 2026 were KRW 22.9 billion in Q1, KRW 35.7 billion in Q2 and KRW 27.8 billion in Q3, a wide range, and Q3 profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 1.1 billion. On the balance sheet, the debt ratio was 14.1% and total liabilities KRW 27.6 billion at end-2025, a low burden.

Bullish factors include fiber coverage and backbone policies and traffic growth expected from physical AI. Bearish factors include the precedent of operator capex cuts, product and customer concentration, and volatile quarterly profit.

For valuation, the reference point is the past high price-to-book level that Hana Securities cited in January 2026. The next checkpoints are the FTTH share and operating margin in the 2026 annual business report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
