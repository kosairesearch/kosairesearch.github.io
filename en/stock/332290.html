02

Business structure

Nousbo was established in 2007 to develop, manufacture and sell fertilizers and organic farming materials, and operates golf course landscaping as a core business; it listed on KOSDAQ in 2022 through a SPAC merger.

Its main products are compound fertilizers and controlled-release fertilizers (CRF), a coating technology that reduces the frequency of fertilizer application and labor requirements.

Domestically, the company signed a 2026 inorganic fertilizer supply contract with the NongHyup Economic Business Group worth KRW 40.26 billion, equivalent to 40.66% of its most recently disclosed annual revenue.

Overseas, in March 2025 Nousbo signed a supply agreement with ITOCHU, one of Japan's largest trading companies, worth up to KRW 280 billion over five years for CRF coated fertilizer, expanding exports into the Japanese market.

It has since entered a two-year supply agreement with Wilbur-Ellis, a North American agri-input distributor with roughly KRW 4 trillion in revenue, to supply seedling and turf CRF products in North America.

In Vietnam, Nousbo signed a memorandum of understanding with SK leviO to supply biodegradable CRF products for a low-carbon rice production project in the Mekong Delta region. In 2026, Nousbo exported biodegradable coated fertilizer to Ukraine, marking its first entry into the European market.

While domestic fertilizer and golf course landscaping remain the operational base, growing export volume has become a key pillar of the company's growth strategy.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 73 billion in 2022 to KRW 82.2 billion in 2023 (up 12.5% year-on-year), KRW 99 billion in 2024 (up 20.5%), and KRW 104.2 billion in 2025 (up 5.2%), marking three consecutive years of growth since 2023, with 2025 the highest year on record among the disclosed figures.

On profitability, the company posted an operating loss of KRW 3.31 billion in 2022 and KRW 2.28 billion in 2023, continuing in the red but with a narrower loss, before swinging to an operating profit of KRW 3.67 billion in 2024 and growing it 12.2% to KRW 4.12 billion in 2025.

The operating margin improved from -4.5% in 2022 and -2.8% in 2023 to 3.7% in 2024 and 4.0% in 2025. However, net profit attributable to owners, which turned positive at KRW 3.03 billion in 2024, fell 66.2% to KRW 1.03 billion in 2025, diverging from the rise in operating profit.

On a quarterly basis, revenue of KRW 35.4 billion in Q2 2025 declined to KRW 25.1 billion in Q3 (down 29.1% quarter-on-quarter) and KRW 23.6 billion in Q4 (down 6.0%), before rebounding to KRW 31.6 billion in Q1 2026 (up 34.2%) and KRW 51.7 billion in Q2 2026 (up 63.5% quarter-on-quarter and 46.1% year-on-year), two consecutive quarters of growth.

Operating profit also recovered from an operating loss of KRW 1.03 billion in Q4 2025 to a profit of KRW 1.38 billion in Q1 2026, then KRW 3.42 billion in Q2 2026 (up 147.0% quarter-on-quarter and 2.8% year-on-year).

Net profit attributable to owners turned positive at KRW 1.44 billion in Q1 2026 and jumped to KRW 3.5 billion in Q2 2026 (up 142.9% quarter-on-quarter and 357.2% year-on-year), bringing the trailing four-quarter sum (Q3 2025 through Q2 2026) to KRW 5.54 billion.

Operating cash flow swung from outflows of KRW 13.5 billion in 2022 and KRW 3.42 billion in 2023 to an inflow of KRW 8.46 billion in 2024, then back to an outflow of KRW 1.98 billion in 2025, highlighting a gap between reported earnings and cash generation.

05

Industry analysis

Global demand for controlled-release fertilizer (CRF) is expanding on the back of labor-saving and reduced environmental burden, and Nousbo is one of the few Korean fertilizer makers to have steadily grown its export share.

The European Union is advancing phased regulation of non-biodegradable coated fertilizers that generate microplastics along with new biodegradable coating standards, putting pressure on the industry to shift toward eco-friendly materials.

Nousbo completed a Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT) international filing in August 2026 for its biodegradable-resin coated fertilizer manufacturing method, positioning itself ahead of this regulatory shift.

Most of its potassium chloride raw material is sourced from Southeast Asian countries, which provides some insulation from Middle East-driven global raw material supply risks.

Domestically, Nousbo's 'Hi-Coat' product was designated as the first premium fertilizer under a newly introduced certification system, a point of quality differentiation cited by the company.

That said, the company's business differs in character from the large petrochemical and specialty chemical firms within the KOSDAQ chemical sector classification, so its position is better assessed within the niche of specialized agricultural inputs rather than direct comparison with bulk chemical producers.

The strategy of broadening export destinations across Japan, North America, Southeast Asia and Europe reduces dependence on any single market's demand swings, but each market requires meeting its own certification and regulatory requirements before volumes can scale.

06

Outlook

Building on its supply agreement with ITOCHU, the company is conducting CRF coated fertilizer application tests on large-scale plantation crops in Southeast Asia and has said supply expansion plans will be discussed based on the results.

In North America, Nousbo has stated it plans to begin supplying initial products to Wilbur-Ellis by the end of 2026, making actual delivery a key milestone for recognizing North American sales.

In Vietnam, the company is working with SK leviO to supply biodegradable CRF for a low-carbon, high-quality rice production project in the Mekong Delta, aiming to use it as a global reference case for further market entry.

In Europe, following its 2026 export of biodegradable coated fertilizer to Ukraine, the company is exploring repeat export opportunities tied to reconstruction and landscaping projects.

Domestically, the 2026 inorganic fertilizer supply contract with the NongHyup Economic Business Group accounts for a substantial share of annual revenue, meaning the final confirmed procurement volume will directly affect full-year results.

The company has said it plans to further expand its export footprint into EU member states and North American and Asian countries facing tightening chemical regulations, leveraging the biodegradable coating technology secured through its PCT filing.

Most of these overseas agreements remain at the testing or initial-volume stage, so additional confirmation of results will be needed before supply scales up meaningfully.

08

Bull factors

Diversified Export Channels

By expanding supply and partnership targets to ITOCHU in Japan, Wilbur-Ellis in North America, SK leviO in Vietnam, and Ukraine in Europe, the company is building a structure that reduces dependence on any single market.

While each agreement remains at an early or testing stage, securing multiple large distribution networks is meaningful for long-term sales diversification. The common thread behind these new overseas contracts is the company's capability to develop customized products based on its CRF technology.

Improved Earnings Structure Since 2024

Following consecutive losses in 2022-2023, the operating margin improved to 3.7% in 2024 and 4.0% in 2025 after the swing to profit. In Q1 and Q2 2026, both revenue and operating profit rose for two consecutive quarters, extending the post-turnaround recovery.

Rising demand for controlled-release fertilizer and expansion of functional products are cited as drivers of the improved profitability.

Technology Positioned for Environmental Regulation

By completing a PCT international filing in August 2026 for its biodegradable-resin coated fertilizer manufacturing method, the company has laid a technical foundation to respond to tightening microplastic and chemical regulations in markets such as the EU.

Its domestic 'Hi-Coat' product was designated as the first premium fertilizer under a newly introduced certification system, supporting quality credibility.

The export of biodegradable coated fertilizer to Ukraine marks an early entry point into the European market that could be read as positioning ahead of regulatory change.

Bear factors

Declining and Volatile Net Profit

While operating profit rose year-on-year in 2025, net profit attributable to owners fell 66.2%, with operating profit and net profit moving in opposite directions. On a quarterly basis, the company posted an operating loss and net loss in Q4 2025 before recovering in 2026, showing substantial quarter-to-quarter swings. Such volatility suggests non-operating items or one-off factors may have played a role.

Pronounced Seasonality

Revenue declined from KRW 35.4 billion in Q2 2025 through Q3 and Q4 before rising again in Q1 and Q2 2026, reflecting the pronounced seasonal demand pattern typical of agricultural fertilizers. This makes it difficult to extrapolate annual results from any single quarter.

Even as the export mix grows, differing planting and fertilization seasons across countries may limit how much this seasonality can be smoothed out.

Cash Flow Diverging From Earnings

Operating cash flow swung to an inflow of KRW 8.46 billion in 2024 but reverted to an outflow of KRW 1.98 billion in 2025. Despite net profit attributable to owners remaining positive over the same period, the negative cash flow suggests working capital items such as receivables or inventory may have had an impact. If expanding overseas exports lengthen receivable collection periods, this gap could recur.

Risk factors

Raw Material Supply

Potassium chloride, a core raw material, is sourced mostly from Southeast Asian countries, directly exposing the company to regional supply disruptions or price swings. If Middle East geopolitical tensions affect global fertilizer prices broadly, it could add to cost pressure.

While the company emphasizes stable supply chain management, risk remains given the concentration of its sourcing.

Public Procurement Dependence

The 2026 fertilizer supply contract with the NongHyup Economic Business Group accounts for 40.66% of the company's most recently disclosed revenue, representing a high degree of customer concentration.

Under the contract terms, a correction disclosure may be required if the final procurement volume differs from the contract amount by 10% or more, meaning volume changes feed directly into results. Shifts in public procurement policy or budgets could increase revenue volatility.

FX and Overseas Regulation

As the export share grows, foreign exchange movements could have a greater impact on revenue and profitability. As microplastic and chemical regulations led by the European Union become more concrete, certification costs or product conversion burdens may arise.

Delays in country-specific fertilizer registration or certification procedures could push back the actual implementation timing of new export contracts.

Overall view

Nousbo is a company built on domestic fertilizer and golf course landscaping businesses that has leveraged its controlled-release fertilizer (CRF) technology to expand export destinations into Japan, North America, Southeast Asia, and Europe.

Following consecutive losses in 2022-2023, the company turned to operating profit in 2024 and grew it further through 2025, while net profit attributable to owners fell sharply in 2025 and quarterly volatility remained pronounced.

Revenue and operating profit rose for two consecutive quarters in Q1 and Q2 2026, signaling a recovery, but the return to negative operating cash flow in 2025 shows that improved earnings did not directly translate into cash generation.

Overseas agreements with ITOCHU, Wilbur-Ellis, SK leviO, and the Ukraine export remain mostly at early or testing stages, leaving actual supply expansion as a key variable for future results.

The structure in which the public-sector contract with the NongHyup Economic Business Group accounts for a substantial share of revenue also warrants continued monitoring.

No investment opinion is expressed here, and the trend should be reassessed through upcoming quarterly reports and the progress of overseas contract execution.
