02

Business structure

Nousbo is an agricultural materials company whose core business is controlled-release coated fertilizer (CRF).

The company says it holds a foaming, spreading fertilizer manufacturing technology that dissolves quickly in water, which it presents as a product advantage because it does not require even application across paddy fields.

Its flagship products are Nousbo All Coating 31 for paddy rice and the Napjakgi topdressing series.

According to a NICE D&B summary based on the 2022 annual report, the business is divided into a fertilizer segment covering chemical, horticultural and controlled-release fertilizers, agricultural materials, and golf course management.

In golf course services, expanded consulting and technical services were cited as a driver of 2025 sales improvement. The customer base rests on two pillars: domestic Nonghyup cooperatives and large overseas trading houses.

Nousbo signed a supply contract with Nonghyup Economic Holdings for fertilizer purchases nationwide from January 1 to December 31, 2026, worth KRW 40.2 billion, according to a February 2026 disclosure.

Overseas, it signed a five-year supply agreement with Itochu Corporation in March 2025 for controlled-release coated fertilizer worth up to KRW 280 billion. Export value rose from KRW 8.78 billion in 2020 to KRW 13.22 billion in 2022, and fertilizer exports were put at KRW 17 billion in 2024.

The company also runs a green tea and matcha export business and took part in a food ingredients trade show in Las Vegas on October 28 to 30, 2026, under its SEEIN matcha brand.

Compared with large petrochemical-focused listed chemical companies, Nousbo operates in a narrow niche of agricultural coated fertilizer, and its differentiation rests on technology and local distribution networks.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 73 billion in 2022 to KRW 82.2 billion in 2023 (up 12.5% year on year), KRW 99 billion in 2024 (up 20.5%), and KRW 104.2 billion in 2025 (up 5.2%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, with 2025 the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 3.31 billion in 2022 and KRW 2.28 billion in 2023 to an operating profit of KRW 3.67 billion in 2024, and then to KRW 4.12 billion in 2025 (up 12.2%). The operating margin moved from -4.5% in 2022 and -2.8% in 2023 to 3.7% in 2024 and 4.0% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to KRW 3.03 billion in 2024 but fell 66.2% to KRW 1.03 billion in 2025, while net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 1.03 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 change partly to the early recognition of performance-related payments alongside growth in its core product sales, according to a February 2026 report.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 190.0% in 2022 and 258.1% in 2023 to 144.6% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 34.7 billion and total liabilities were KRW 50.2 billion.

Operating cash flow was negative at KRW -13.5 billion in 2022, KRW -3.42 billion in 2023 and KRW -1.98 billion in 2025, and positive only in 2024 at KRW 8.46 billion.

Quarterly, an operating profit of KRW 1.66 billion in the third quarter of 2025 on sales of KRW 25.1 billion gave way to an operating loss of KRW 1.03 billion in the fourth quarter, which turned the quarter loss-making, before returning to operating profit of KRW 1.38 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

Second-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 51.7 billion (up 63.5% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 3.42 billion (up 147.0%) were the largest of the last five quarters, while aggregator data show cost of sales rising sharply in the same quarter.

Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 38.3 billion (up 52.9% year on year), operating profit KRW 1.93 billion (up 16.1% year on year) and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 1.19 billion, showing that quarterly margins swung even as sales rose.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (fourth quarter 2025 to third quarter 2026) totaled KRW 5.56 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Nousbo's end markets are domestic agricultural fertilizer buyers and overseas agricultural materials markets. Controlled-release fertilizers release nutrients gradually after a single application, which aligns with farmers' demand to cut application frequency and labor.

The company's performance commentary cited rising demand for slow-release fertilizers as a driver of 2025 sales improvement, and also pointed to a wider range of products using functional materials.

In Korea, annual supply contracts with large buyers such as Nonghyup Economic Holdings account for a meaningful share of business, so contract renewals shape how predictable sales are.

Overseas, coated fertilizer applications have widened in Indonesia's palm oil farming market, and the company said network-building at local trade shows led to 2026 sales growth. Product diversification is also under way, with Micro CRF for North American turf and CRF for tropical crops in development.

Fertilizer prices are generally understood to be influenced by grain and raw material prices, so input cost swings can affect margins. Among listed chemical peers such as LG Chem and Lotte Chemical, whose focus lies in petrochemicals and battery materials, product lines and customer bases do not overlap much.

Nousbo's position reads as an expansion of overseas channels within the narrow niche of agricultural controlled-release fertilizer.

Expanding overseas export channels

The five-year Itochu supply agreement signed in March 2025, worth up to KRW 280 billion, adds visibility to overseas sales. Fertilizer exports were put at KRW 17 billion in 2024, showing the export base has grown over recent years.

Sales growth and a return to profit

Sales rose from KRW 73 billion in 2022 to KRW 104.2 billion in 2025, and cumulative sales for the first three quarters of 2026 reached KRW 121.6 billion, above the full-year 2025 total. The operating margin has been positive since 2024 and stood at 4.0% in 2025. Second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.42 billion was the largest of the last five quarters.

Product and business diversification

The company is widening its product line with coated fertilizer using biodegradable resin, CRF for tropical crops, and Micro CRF for North American turf. It is pursuing OMRI registration and Halal certification to prepare for entry into the Middle East and Latin America. Its matcha and green tea exports add export items outside agriculture.

Capital and ownership structure

Paid-in capital rose from KRW 3.3 billion to KRW 3.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025, with the number of shares rising alongside. Related parties of the largest shareholder hold 49.37%, which limits the free float.

The increase in share count and the ownership structure can affect per-share value interpretation and trading flows.

Overseas operations and currency

As overseas sales grow, the company becomes more exposed to local demand changes, currency movements and local certification timelines. Certification for the Middle East and Latin America is still being pursued and North American products are in development, so commercialization timing has not been set. Even if export growth continues, the degree of concentration in particular markets should be watched.

Overall view

Nousbo is an agricultural materials company centered on controlled-release coated fertilizer. Sales rose from KRW 73 billion in 2022 to KRW 104.2 billion in 2025, and operating profit moved into the black in 2024 and reached KRW 4.12 billion in 2025.

However, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 66.2% to KRW 1.03 billion in 2025, and the fourth quarter of 2025 showed both an operating loss and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, so quarterly earnings are volatile.

In 2026, profit continued in the first and second quarters, and cumulative sales of KRW 121.6 billion through the third quarter exceeded full-year 2025 sales, marking a clear expansion in the top line.

Growth rests on overseas exports including Indonesia, the Nonghyup domestic supply contract and the Itochu supply agreement. On the other hand, the domestic supply contract ends on December 31, 2026 and a follow-on is needed, while overseas sales are exposed to currency and local demand shifts.

On the financial side, the debt-to-equity ratio fell to 144.6% in 2025, but total liabilities of KRW 50.2 billion still exceed total equity of KRW 34.7 billion.

The share price sits in a range below net asset value while earnings remain volatile, and the next checkpoints are the terms of the Nonghyup follow-on contract, the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027, and the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report.
