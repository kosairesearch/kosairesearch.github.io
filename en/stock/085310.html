NK Moves Past Losses; Sustaining Profit Is the Test

NK has posted quarterly operating profit in every quarter since the fourth quarter of 2025, yet its 2025 full-year operating loss remains and the third quarter of 2026 returned to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders.

    Revenue reached KRW 130.1 billion in 2025, the largest year in 2022–2025, and has risen for three consecutive years since 2023.

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

NK was founded in 1980 under its former name, became a corporation in 1984, and listed on the KOSPI in 2008. Its main products are fixed fire-extinguishing systems, high-pressure gas cylinders, ballast water treatment systems, offshore plant equipment and special equipment, supported by six consolidated subsidiaries.

In the consolidated sales mix as of December 2024, fire-extinguishing systems accounted for 61.83%, high-pressure gas cylinders for 22.71%, special equipment for 12.88% and ballast water treatment for 1.88%.

Because fire-extinguishing systems exceed 60% of sales, demand for that product line sets the baseline for earnings. The same table shows exports at 60% and domestic sales at 40% of the mix.

Among the subsidiaries, one makes natural gas and hydrogen storage vessels, META ENE sells and trades high-pressure gas cylinders, NUTECH O3 and NK USA sell ballast water treatment systems, and NK Ederment, established in May 2025, makes military equipment and parts.

In its first-quarter 2026 results release, the company attributed the improvement to steady sales of fixed fire-extinguishing systems and a larger share of defense and special equipment.

Hydraulic supply units delivered for construction of naval submarines and surface ships were cited as a driver of special-equipment demand. Compared with larger plant and robotics companies in the same machinery sector, NK's sales are concentrated in a narrow set of fire-extinguishing systems and high-pressure vessels.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 76.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 91.5 billion in 2023 (up 19.4% year on year), KRW 110.9 billion in 2024 (up 21.2% year on year), and KRW 130.1 billion in 2025 (up 17.3% year on year).

Revenue has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 is the largest year in the 2022-2025 period.

Operating profit was KRW 0.87 billion in 2022, then swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.1 billion in 2023, widened to an operating loss of KRW 4.82 billion in 2024, and narrowed to an operating loss of KRW 0.65 billion in 2025.

The operating margin moved from 1.1% in 2022 to -1.2% in 2023, -4.3% in 2024, and -0.5% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose sharply to KRW 59.4 billion in 2023 (up 454.9% year on year), but because the company posted an operating loss that same year, the contribution of non-operating items needs to be verified in the notes to the 2023 annual report.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 0.87 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.14 billion in 2025, meaning two consecutive years of losses, with the loss widening in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 2.24 billion in 2024 and a net loss of KRW 3.69 billion in 2025, which differs from the figures attributable to controlling shareholders.

On a quarterly basis, Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 49.0 billion and operating profit was KRW 5.0 billion, a return to profitability; Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 31.9 billion (down 34.9% quarter on quarter), Q2 was KRW 36.8 billion, and Q3 was KRW 31.5 billion (up 10.3% year on year).

Operating profit was KRW 0.53 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 3.14 billion in Q2, and KRW 3.44 billion in Q3, but net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in the same Q3 was KRW 1.95 billion, a return to a loss.

Operating cash flow was KRW 12.0 billion in 2023, then -KRW 8.68 billion in 2024 and -KRW 5.88 billion in 2025, negative in both 2024 and 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for NK's core fire-extinguishing systems is tied to the shipbuilding market and environmental regulation. The company's 2025 third-quarter cumulative earnings commentary pointed to a shipbuilding recovery and tighter environmental rules as demand drivers for fire-extinguishing systems.

Ballast water treatment systems are installed in line with ship ballast regulation, but they accounted for only 1.88% of the sales mix as of December 2024. Whether recovery in offshore demand carries through both the fire-extinguishing and ballast lines is the starting point for judging the industry cycle.

Special equipment supplies hydraulic units for naval submarines and surface ships, so whether defense demand becomes a pillar of earnings is the item to watch in coming quarters. Demand growth from autonomous vehicles and data centers is cited as a driver for the semiconductor special gas business.

Hydrogen storage vessels fall under the subsidiary that makes natural gas and hydrogen containers and are tied to hydrogen vehicle and charging station demand over the medium to long term.

In competition, NK operates as a specialist maker of high-pressure vessels and fire-extinguishing systems, and its narrow scope means industry shifts feed directly into results. Its position in the cycle depends on whether the shipbuilding recovery continues.

06

Outlook

In its first-quarter 2026 results release, the company said it aims to maintain sales of its core fire-extinguishing systems while widening the share of defense and special equipment. In the same release, it said improved results and a strengthened shareholder return policy would help restore market confidence.

In March 2026 it decided on a 10-to-1 share consolidation and presented maintaining an appropriate free float as the purpose. The new shares were scheduled to list on May 22, 2026, but the trading resumption date was later changed to June 1, 2026.

In April 2026 the company cancelled about 4.7 million treasury shares, roughly 6% of issued shares. NK Ederment, a military equipment and parts subsidiary established in May 2025, provides a corporate base for defense expansion.

Hydrogen storage vessels and semiconductor special gases are cited as medium- to long-term growth areas. Revenue contribution from these businesses should be checked in the segment sales items of the periodic reports.

The most direct point to watch is how the share of special equipment and high-pressure gas cylinders changes in the 2026 annual report compared with December 2024.

08

Bull factors

Operating profit has turned positive

Operating profit turned positive with KRW 5 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and stayed positive in each quarter of 2026, at KRW 534 million, KRW 3.14 billion and KRW 3.44 billion. The 2025 full-year operating loss narrowed to KRW 648 million from KRW 4.82 billion in 2024. The company described the first-quarter results as structural improvement rather than a one-off rebound.

Wider share of defense and special equipment

The company cited a larger share of defense and special equipment as a driver of first-quarter 2026 results. As of December 2024, special equipment accounted for 12.88% of sales, and hydraulic supply deliveries for naval submarines and surface ships have been described as contributing to results. How the product mix shift shows up in profitability is the point to track in this business.

Shareholder returns and balance sheet capacity

In April 2026 the company cancelled about 4.7 million treasury shares, and in March 2026 it decided on a 10-to-1 share consolidation to adjust the free float. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 166.2 billion and total liabilities were KRW 64.3 billion. The company presented a stronger shareholder return policy alongside its earnings improvement.

Bear factors

Quarterly losses returned

The third-quarter 2026 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.95 billion followed net income of KRW 3.59 billion in the second quarter. The 2025 full-year net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 4.14 billion.

Operating profit not converting into controlling-shareholder net income in some quarters is cited as a weakness.

Cost pressure and cash outflow

The cost of sales ratio rose from 83.40% in 2021 to 93.46% in 2024. Commentary on the 2025 third-quarter cumulative results pointed to higher raw material prices and one-off costs weighing on profitability. Operating cash flow was negative at KRW -8.68 billion in 2024 and KRW -5.88 billion in 2025.

Concentration in fire-extinguishing systems

As of December 2024, fire-extinguishing systems accounted for 61.83% of sales and exports were 60% of the mix. Volumes for the core products depend on the shipbuilding cycle and overseas orders. Defense and special equipment made up 12.88% of sales as of December 2024, still a small share.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly revenue swung from KRW 49 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 31.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026, a 34.9% decline. Operating profit was positive in the third quarter of 2026 while net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned negative. Whether quarterly profits carry into annual results is the most direct risk.

Raw materials, FX and overseas demand

With exports at 60% of the mix, overseas demand and currency movements affect both sales and profitability. Higher raw material prices were cited as a profit pressure in the 2025 third-quarter commentary. With the cost of sales ratio reaching 93.46% in 2024, the ability to pass costs through to prices is important.

Execution of expansion and cash flow

Hydrogen storage vessels, semiconductor special gases and defense are cited as medium- to long-term drivers, but their contribution to sales is still small relative to the total. Negative operating cash flow in 2024 and 2025 may signal pressure from investment and working capital. The debt ratio rose to 38.7% in 2025 from 24.1% in 2024.

Overall view

NK's revenue rose through 2025 after 2022, and its operating loss narrowed in 2025 after losses in 2023 and 2024. Quarterly operating profit stayed positive from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and the company credits a larger share of defense and special equipment for the improvement.

On the other side, the third-quarter 2026 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.95 billion turned the quarter negative again, and the 2025 full-year net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.14 billion.

Higher cost of sales, negative operating cash flow and concentration in fire-extinguishing systems remain weaknesses. The share price sits low relative to net asset value, and whether the company posts an annual profit is the reference for reading valuation.

The 2026 fourth-quarter and full-year results due in early 2027 will show whether operating profit carries through to net income attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating improvement is confirmed, while the durability of earnings and the cash flow recovery are still being tested.
