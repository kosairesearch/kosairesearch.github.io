02

Business structure

The Contents On was founded in December 2012 and listed on KONEX in 2018 as a film and drama producer and distributor. The company used off-theater rights distribution to build cash flow, then moved toward a multi-format model that combines film and drama investment and production.

Its film segment consists of theater share income and content income, and content income fell from KRW 128.1 million in 2022 to KRW 55.5 million in 2024 (in 100 million won units, reported as 128.1 and 55.5).

Drama production service revenue was absent in 2022 and 2023, appeared at KRW 4.29 billion in 2024, and reached KRW 4.26 billion in the first half of 2025. In 2024 film accounted for 61.4% of sales, with drama and other segments making up the rest.

The company distributes roughly 200 titles a year, mainly to OTT and IPTV platforms, and reports a cumulative library of about 2,600 titles. Another source describes the library as about 2,500 titles, reflecting differences in counting basis.

Its market share stands at 3.30% by TV VOD sales scale and 0.30% by theatrical distribution audience. It competes in drama production with listed peers such as Studio Dragon, but with 13 employees its capacity in capital and staffing is limited. As of December 2025, domestic accounted for 95.33% of content income, with exports at 4.67%.

03

Recent trends

2025 sales were KRW 8,903.61 million, down 24.0% year on year. Over the same period, the operating loss was KRW 1,058.88 million. On a separate basis, the net loss was KRW 6,575 million and total equity was negative KRW 1,192 million.

Sales moved from KRW 21.47 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.62 billion in 2023 and KRW 11.71 billion in 2024. At the March 31, 2026 annual general meeting, the 14th-term financial statements were approved with 62.2% support of total issued shares.

At the same meeting, a film division manager was newly elected as an inside director. In February 2026, one outside director left the board mid-term. The abnormal-signal report lists financial and audit-opinion emphasis matters as the latest entry as of June 30, 2026.

The share price was KRW 3,300 at 4:10 p.m. on March 31, 2026. It hit an all-time low of KRW 320 on August 14, 2026, and by late October its one-month change was -45% and six-month change was -63%.

04

Outlook

The 2026 fiscal-year settlement results have not yet been disclosed, while 2025 showed a sales decline, an operating loss, and negative total equity together. In drama, the co-produced drama Twelve was released on KBS 2TV and Disney+ on August 23, 2025.

The company has dramas in planning and writing stages, including Welcome to Q University Admissions, The Butler of the Shin Family, and Merry Christmas House. Among these, Welcome to Q University Admissions had a drama adaptation rights agreement signed in 2024.

On the film and video side, the IPTV and VOD service for Cyber Cartel started on October 8, 2026. The same homepage notices also cover the documentary Concrete Green Island (October 7, 2026) and World Breaker (September 30, 2026).

The company held an investor briefing on September 4, 2026 and disclosed the results on September 17. The main financial checkpoints are whether the negative equity position is resolved and what the audit opinion on the 2026 settlement says.

On the revenue side, the continuity of drama production service revenue and the recovery of film content income are the points to watch.

05

Bull factors

A cumulative library of about 2,600 titles

The company distributes about 200 titles a year, mainly to OTT and IPTV platforms, and has built a cumulative library of about 2,600 titles. This rights base is described as the foundation for cash flow from off-theater channels.

In 2024 it recorded digital rights distribution of about 2,300 titles in its company history.

The shift in market benchmarks from theater box office to platform viewing and subscription metrics aligns with a rights-based revenue model (an analysis noting that the market benchmark moved to platform viewing and subscription indicators after the pandemic).

Drama production revenue added

Drama production service revenue was KRW 4.29 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.26 billion in the first half of 2025. Over the same period, drama accounted for a large share of total sales, adding a new axis to a revenue structure that had been film-centered.

The co-produced drama Twelve, released on KBS 2TV and Disney+, added a production track record. Production service revenue is recognized according to production schedules and delivery timing rather than rights-licensing cycles.

Second-half 2026 releases and investor relations

Notices on the company homepage ran from September 30 to October 8, 2026 and covered new releases including World Breaker, Concrete Green Island, and Cyber Cartel. In September 2026 the company held an investor briefing and disclosed the outcome, continuing its investor relations activity. Its issued shares consist of 4,552,359 common shares and 222,222 preferred shares.

06

Bear factors

Sales decline and continued operating losses

2025 sales were KRW 8,903.61 million, down 24.0% year on year. The operating loss was KRW 1,058.88 million. On a separate basis, the net loss of KRW 6,575 million was larger than the operating loss. Sales rose to KRW 11.71 billion in 2024 before falling again in 2025.

Negative equity and audit emphasis

On a separate basis, total assets were KRW 18,339 million and total liabilities were KRW 19,531 million, so liabilities exceeded assets. As a result, total equity was negative KRW 1,192 million, indicating capital impairment.

The abnormal-signal report recorded financial and audit-opinion emphasis matters as the latest item as of June 30, 2026.

Concentration in drama and a small organization

In the first half of 2025, drama production service revenue of KRW 4.26 billion made up most of KRW 5.87 billion in total sales. Exports account for only 4.67% of content income, leaving high dependence on domestic revenue. Headcount was 13 as of December 2025. The 52-week share price range has been between KRW 320 and KRW 4,140.

07

Risk factors

Macro

After the pandemic, the market benchmark shifted from theatrical box office to platform viewing and subscription metrics. This shift can change how rights revenue is valued. A theatrical distribution share of 0.30% means the company has limited reliance on theatrical windows.

Because OTT platform scheduling and rights purchase decisions determine the timing of revenue, dependence on outside decisions is high.

Financial

If negative equity persists, it could weigh on financing capacity and on the confidence of counterparties. Financial and audit-opinion emphasis matters were recorded as of June 30, 2026. With both operating and net losses in 2025, whether the loss-making structure can be resolved remains the central issue.

Market and governance

The share price fell to KRW 320 on August 14, 2026, and by late October it was around KRW 1,100, showing high volatility. The approval of the financial statements at the March 2026 annual meeting drew 62.2% support of total issued shares. One outside director left the board in February 2026. As a KONEX-listed company, trading liquidity may be limited.

08

Overall view

The Contents On is in a transition phase, adding drama production revenue to a rights-distribution base. Sales fell to KRW 8,903.61 million in 2025, operating and net losses continued, and total equity was negative.

On the strength side, the cumulative library of about 2,600 titles, the addition of drama production service revenue, and second-half 2026 releases are the cited factors.

On the weakness side, continued losses, capital impairment, audit-opinion emphasis, drama concentration, and a small organization are the main factors. Its market share of 3.30% in TV VOD and 0.30% in theatrical distribution places it below larger operators in scale.

When the 2026 fiscal-year results are disclosed, the first confirmable points will be whether sales recover and whether the capital structure changes. The share price moved widely in 2026, from KRW 320 to about KRW 1,100.
