Revenue Up Three Straight Years; Losses Persist

02

Business structure

SCL Science builds three business lines on its protein-based platform technology: medical sealants, biologics logistics and medical diagnostics. The logistics unit runs an IoT-based cold chain that follows domestic and international regulatory standards and moves human-derived materials and drugs.

In 2025 it added regional business rights from a Seoul-based research institute to widen its nationwide network. The company said roughly half of the first-half 2026 increase in standalone revenue came from its Yeongnam and Honam cold chain logistics business.

The hemostatic product InnoSEAL Plus DL is supported by a multicenter clinical study published in the journal Biomedicines. The company said supply-contract revenue from this product would be reflected in the second half of 2026.

Its subsidiary SCL Healthcare joined the group in March 2025, and in the first half of 2026 it recorded revenue of KRW 5.05 billion and an operating profit of KRW 57 million after turning profitable.

SCL Healthcare's core activities are CRO-based central lab services (C-LAB) and genetic-analysis precision medicine tests, with cancer gene, adult disease and gut metabolite (TMAO) test volumes rising. The company has said it is pursuing a merger with this subsidiary.

Its AI-focused cancer vaccine subsidiary has secured a patent for predicting response to immuno-oncology therapy and is expanding its R&D pipeline. Because logistics and testing earnings sit alongside an R&D pipeline under one roof, profit structures differ markedly across the units.

04

Earnings analysis

In the annual DART-filed results, revenue rose from KRW 370 million in 2022 to KRW 1.32 billion in 2023 (up 257.5% year on year), KRW 4.74 billion in 2024 (up 258.3%), and KRW 22 billion in 2025 (up 364.6%).

Revenue has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating losses shrank each year, from KRW 4.61 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.02 billion in 2023, KRW 3.66 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.4 billion in 2025, and the company recorded losses for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025. The operating margin improved from -1,245.4% in 2022 to -15.4% in 2025.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 3.93 billion in 2025 from KRW 3.73 billion in 2024, and the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 4.12 billion in 2025.

At end-2025, total liabilities were KRW 56.4 billion and total equity was KRW 23.5 billion, so the debt-to-equity ratio reached 240.1%, up from 55.0% in 2024. Operating cash flow was negative every year: -KRW 4.1 billion in 2022, -KRW 2.82 billion in 2023, -KRW 5.96 billion in 2024 and -KRW 2.17 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 169 million in Q3 2025 turned into an operating loss of KRW 848 million in Q4 2025, followed by a KRW 648 million operating loss in Q1 2026, an operating profit of KRW 406 million in Q2 2026, and an operating loss of KRW 190 million in Q3 2026.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 7.85 billion, up 3.4% from the prior quarter and 12.8% from a year earlier. The company's disclosed cumulative consolidated revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 23.1 billion, up 68% year on year.

The company attributed the consolidated operating loss to higher R&D and investment costs at its cancer vaccine subsidiary and said the core business's profitability had not declined.

In Q1 2026, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 850 million exceeded the operating loss of KRW 648 million, so non-operating items had a larger effect. The company said first-half net income included convertible bond valuation gains and losses and interest expense, which are non-cash items.

05

Industry analysis

SCL Science sits within the biopharma sector but puts its weight on service areas such as logistics and testing rather than new drug development or large-scale contract manufacturing.

Compared with peers such as Samsung Biologics and Celltrion, which build their businesses around large production capacity and product pipelines, its business model and scale differ sharply.

As development and commercialization of biologic drugs grow, demand for safely transporting and testing biologics can rise alongside, which forms the backdrop for its logistics business.

Still, annual revenue was about KRW 22 billion in 2025, and quarterly revenue fell 7.6% in Q1 2026 and 0.9% in Q2 before rising 3.4% in Q3, a pattern that suggests logistics and testing demand may move with client schedules and contract timing.

Cold chain transport requires temperature control and regulatory compliance capabilities, and the company positions itself on specialized transport that meets domestic and international rules.

In medical diagnostics, growth depends on expanding genetic test menus and test volumes, and competitive intensity varies by player, so company-by-company comparison is needed.

The hemostatic product InnoSEAL Plus DL is presented as a new revenue source, but how large its sales become will only be visible once the second-half 2026 supply-contract revenue is recognized. The cancer vaccine business is R&D-driven, and its value depends heavily on whether it enters clinical stages.

For that reason, comparing the business with large biopharma growth rates is less informative than comparing margins in logistics and testing against R&D cost burden.

Outlook

The company said it expects supply-contract revenue from InnoSEAL Plus DL to be recognized in the second half of 2026, while SCL Healthcare's growth and R&D at its cancer vaccine subsidiary continue.

For 2026 it has pointed to strengthening biologics logistics and expanding medical device sales, with a goal of pairing revenue growth with profitability. The mass spectrometry facility at SCL Healthcare was described as entering full operation from Q2 2026, with improved testing-business results expected.

After the planned merger, the company said it would combine the profit base of its core business with data capabilities and keep investing in precision medicine including cancer vaccines. It presents building a medical data platform and developing cancer vaccines as its core tasks for data-driven precision medicine.

The variable to watch is how quickly R&D spending converts into revenue, since costs from clinical entry were reflected in Q3 2026 operating results, and the point at which the operating loss narrows again is the next checkpoint.

Whether the InnoSEAL Plus DL contract revenue shows up in second-half 2026 results will determine how much the revenue mix changes. Merger terms and timing still need to be set in filings before the change in the consolidated business mix can be gauged.

The 2026 annual business report, the next periodic filing, will fix the fourth-quarter 2026 results and the full-year operating margin.
