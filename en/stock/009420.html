02

Business structure

Hanall Biopharma was founded in 1973 and listed on KOSPI in 1989. It produces and sells more than 160 medicines in categories such as endocrine and cardiovascular, with research, manufacturing and sales of synthetic and biopharmaceutical drugs as its core business.

Overseas clinical and licensing work runs through its U.S. unit HPI. Its flagship pharmaceutical product is a probiotic drug, with 2025 annual sales of KRW 23.8 billion according to company announcements. Nine-month 2026 sales of this product rose 31% year on year to KRW 22.3 billion.

The company reported 2025 pharmaceutical operating sales of KRW 133.8 billion, up 13% year on year, and plans to launch eight new products in 2026.

Its R&D centers on autoimmune therapies, including IMVT-1402 developed with a partner and batoclimab, and the company holds rights to batoclimab outside China, the U.S. and Europe. As of October 2, 2026, Daewoong Pharmaceutical and three related parties held 31.28%, and the National Pension Service held 10.84%.

FcRn-based autoimmune drugs are the key competitive set, and Johnson & Johnson's nipocalimab is cited as a clinical benchmark.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 110 billion in 2022 to KRW 134.9 billion in 2023 (up 22.7% year on year), KRW 138.9 billion in 2024 (up 3.0%), and KRW 155.2 billion in 2025 (up 11.7%), growing for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. 2025 was the largest revenue year of 2022 through 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 1.51 billion in 2022 and KRW 2.21 billion in 2023 (up 46.9%), then fell to KRW 230 million in 2024 (down 89.6%) before turning into an operating loss of KRW 916 million in 2025. The operating margin moved from 1.4% in 2022 to 1.6% in 2023, 0.2% in 2024 and -0.6% in 2025.

Selling and administrative expenses are cited as a main reason for the 2025 swing to loss, with reports saying they rose 18% to KRW 62.5 billion, driven by international arbitration legal costs with a Chinese partner and a weaker won.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 251 million in 2022 and KRW 3.51 billion in 2023, then turned to a net loss of KRW 1.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.56 billion in 2025, two consecutive years of losses. Net profit including non-controlling interests followed the same direction in 2024 and 2025.

At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 161.7 billion and total liabilities were KRW 63.5 billion, and the debt ratio rose from 26.5% in 2024 to 39.3% in 2025.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 25.2 billion in 2022 and KRW 29.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 9.36 billion in 2024, then turned negative at -KRW 2.34 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, operating profit went from KRW 349 million in Q3 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 2.02 billion in Q4 2025, then returned to a profit of KRW 839 million in Q1 2026.

Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 5.6 billion was attributed in a company-linked earnings comment to deferred revenue recognized after the end of a batoclimab trial, while the controlling-shareholder net loss for that quarter was KRW 19.2 billion.

In Q3 2026, revenue was KRW 39.9 billion, operating profit KRW 1.27 billion and controlling-shareholder net profit KRW 3.06 billion, and the four-quarter total of controlling-shareholder net losses from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 is KRW 22.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The core market is autoimmune therapies that use FcRn inhibition. Eugene Investment & Securities described the FcRn inhibitor market as a fast-growing market in an April 2026 report.

Shinhan Investment Corp. said in a May 29, 2026 report that IMVT-1402 had shown a possible first-in-class success in refractory rheumatoid arthritis through an anti-FcRn mechanism. The same report pointed to a 72.7% ACR20 response at week 16, above 45.5% for Johnson & Johnson's nipocalimab.

This comparison is the brokerage's reading of an interim analysis released by the partner. On September 23, 2026 (local time), the partner announced proof-of-concept Phase 2 results in cutaneous lupus and decided to stop development in that indication.

Daol Investment & Securities said the value loss from that stop was limited, but that the chance of expansion into indications driven less by autoantibodies had fallen.

Batoclimab received orphan drug designation in Japan for thyroid eye disease in February 2025, and the company has named Japan as its fastest commercial path.

Overall, the company combines its own pharmaceutical sales with pipeline licensing and royalty income, so partner clinical timelines and rights negotiations shape its earnings more than the cycle of any downstream market.

06

Outlook

The company plans to launch eight new products in 2026 and said five global clinical readouts from three core assets, batoclimab, IMVT-1402 and tanfanercept, would be released in sequence during 2026.

Of the two batoclimab Phase 3 trials in thyroid eye disease, one was completed at the end of 2025, and the company planned a first topline readout in the first quarter of 2026 and a combined release of the second trial in the first half, according to January 2026 reports.

The company also said that if batoclimab were approved in China within 2026, milestone income could follow. Preparations for a Japanese new-drug application for batoclimab are under way.

Shinhan Investment Corp. said in May 2026 that it was focused on additional IMVT-1402 clinical data expected in 2027, and estimated IMVT-1402 sales excluding refractory rheumatoid arthritis at more than USD 5.1 billion in 2032.

Consensus as of early September 2026 showed an operating profit for 2026 but a net loss attributable to shareholders, and estimates varied widely across brokers.

Shinhan Investment Corp. presented a target price of KRW 130,000 in a May 29, 2026 report, and Hyundai Motor Securities presented KRW 93,000 in a June 1, 2026 report. The next checkpoint is how a 2026 China approval and further IMVT-1402 data feed into reported results.

08

Bull factors

Relative strength of IMVT-1402 clinical data

Interim week-16 data for IMVT-1402 in refractory rheumatoid arthritis, released by the partner, showed a 72.7% ACR20 response. Shinhan Investment Corp. compared this with 45.5% for Johnson & Johnson's nipocalimab and assessed a possible advantage of the anti-FcRn mechanism. If that reading holds, the scope for royalty and milestone income could widen.

Return to quarterly operating profit in 2026

Operating profit turned positive in the first quarter of 2026 at KRW 839 million, then reached KRW 5.6 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.27 billion in the third. Press reports said ordinary R&D expenses fell 31% year on year in the first quarter.

The company has said the end of international arbitration should ease legal cost burdens. Whether operating profit stays positive quarter after quarter is the key test.

Growth in the pharmaceutical business

According to company announcements, the flagship probiotic product grew from KRW 12.5 billion in 2023 to KRW 23.8 billion in 2025. Nine-month 2026 sales rose 31% year on year to KRW 22.3 billion. The company expects sales of this product to reach around KRW 30 billion in 2026.

Bear factors

Gap between recent operating profit and net losses

The controlling-shareholder net loss totals KRW 22.1 billion over the last four quarters, and in Q2 2026 a net loss of KRW 19.2 billion came despite operating profit of KRW 5.6 billion. Consensus in early September 2026 still showed a net loss attributable to shareholders for 2026. Whether operating profit gains start to carry through to net income is an open question.

Pipeline concentration and indication-stop risk

On September 23, 2026 (local time), the partner announced proof-of-concept Phase 2 results in cutaneous lupus and decided to stop development in that indication. Press reports said sentiment weakened after the announcement.

An April 2026 report by Eugene Investment & Securities noted that arbitration over rights reversion linked to development delays was under way.

Cost burden and narrower financial room

Selling and administrative expenses rose 18% to KRW 62.5 billion in 2025, and operating cash flow turned negative at -KRW 2.34 billion. The debt ratio rose from 26.5% in 2024 to 39.3% in 2025.

With total equity of KRW 161.7 billion and total liabilities of KRW 63.5 billion, continued clinical and legal spending could narrow financial room.

10

Risk factors

Clinical and regulatory risk

Clinical readouts for core assets could fall short of expectations or slip. Batoclimab still has thyroid eye disease Phase 3 data and a Japanese filing to prepare, and additional IMVT-1402 data is awaited in 2027. Given a recent indication stop, the value swing from individual results could be large.

Partner and legal risk

The company has said international arbitration with its Chinese partner should end during 2026, but the outcome and timing could change costs and rights arrangements. The timing of Chinese approval determines when milestone income arrives. A brokerage has also pointed to arbitration over rights reversion.

Earnings volatility risk

Own-product sales are growing, but quarterly results swing widely with the timing of partner revenue recognition and selling expense burdens. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.27 billion fell 77.2% from the prior quarter.

Frequent divergence between operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income also complicates interpretation.

Overall view

Hanall Biopharma's revenue rose from KRW 110 billion in 2022 to KRW 155.2 billion in 2025, yet its operating margin slid from 1.4% to -0.6%, and the company swung to an operating loss and a controlling-shareholder net loss in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit in 2026 turned positive in the first quarter and then reached KRW 5.6 billion in the second and KRW 1.27 billion in the third, but controlling-shareholder net losses persisted over the same span, leaving a wide gap between operating and net results.

Own-product growth is visible, especially in the probiotic product, while much of the corporate value depends on the clinical and approval timelines of partner-linked IMVT-1402 and batoclimab. On the strength side are brokerage readings of IMVT-1402 interim data and the return to operating profit.

On the weakness side are four-quarter controlling-shareholder net losses of KRW 22.1 billion, an indication stop, arbitration costs and a higher debt ratio. The balance between these depends on clinical outcomes and the timing of legal cost resolution.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report deadline of March 31, 2027 and the first-quarter 2027 report, where the sustainability of operating profit and the turn in controlling-shareholder results will be tested.

Brokerage target prices reflect the dates they were issued, May and June 2026, and do not capture later changes.
