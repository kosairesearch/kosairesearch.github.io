Kctech: Checking Order Flow After the Quarterly Profit Peak

Kctech's operating profit peaked in the first quarter of 2026 and fell in each of the two following quarters, yet the nine-month cumulative figure already exceeds full-year 2025 operating profit.

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Kctech was established in 2017 through a spin-off of its semiconductor and display equipment and materials business, and it focuses on front-end equipment and consumable materials.

Its main products are CMP (chemical mechanical polishing) equipment, wet cleaning systems and CMP slurry, and it operates an overseas unit, KCTech America.

In the first quarter of 2026, the semiconductor segment generated KRW 147.2 billion, or 94.3% of revenue, while the display segment contributed KRW 8.9 billion, or 5.7%. The display segment's net loss narrowed from KRW 1.32 billion to KRW 130 million in the same quarter.

By region, domestic sales reached KRW 139.3 billion (89.2%), followed by China at KRW 13.9 billion (8.9%) and the United States at KRW 2.9 billion (1.9%). Growth in CMP equipment, CMP slurry and cleaning equipment supply drove the first-quarter result.

Samsung Electronics and SK hynix are the main disclosed counterparties, and a single supply contract with SK hynix was disclosed in March 2026. On August 12, 2026, a KRW 40.16 billion semiconductor manufacturing equipment contract with Samsung Electronics was disclosed, equal to 10.49% of revenue.

The company is also pursuing CMP technology for build-up film (BuF) substrates. In competition, localization of cleaning and CMP equipment is cited as a factor behind semiconductor segment gains, and sector peers listed include Wonik IPS, PSK and Soulbrain.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 378.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 286.9 billion in 2023 (down 24.1% year on year), rebounded to KRW 385.4 billion in 2024 (up 34.3%), and dipped to KRW 382.9 billion in 2025 (down 0.7%).

Operating profit went from KRW 60.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 32.7 billion in 2023 (down 45.8%), the lowest year of the period, then rose to KRW 49.8 billion in 2024 (up 52.2%) and KRW 60 billion in 2025 (up 20.5%). Operating profit was largest in 2022 across the four years.

Operating margin moved from 16.0% in 2022 to 11.4% in 2023, 12.9% in 2024 and 15.7% in 2025, so the 2025 margin recovered to near the 2022 level.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 55.4 billion in 2022, KRW 31.7 billion in 2023 (down 42.8%), KRW 52.7 billion in 2024 (up 66.2%) and KRW 53.4 billion in 2025 (up 1.3%); total net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts each year.

Operating cash flow was KRW 55.5 billion, KRW 26.6 billion, KRW 99.9 billion and KRW 60.2 billion for 2022 through 2025, peaking in 2024. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 530.2 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 70.2 billion, a debt ratio of 13.2%.

Quarterly revenue moved from KRW 96 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 111.7 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 156.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 112.7 billion in the second quarter and KRW 114.2 billion in the third quarter.

Operating profit ran KRW 14.9 billion, KRW 21.7 billion, KRW 34.8 billion, KRW 23.5 billion and KRW 20.2 billion over the same five quarters, falling in two consecutive quarters after the first-quarter peak.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 31.6 billion in the first quarter and KRW 21.1 billion in the second, then rose 31.7% to KRW 27.8 billion in the third quarter, moving against operating profit.

Cumulative figures for January to September 2026, reported by Digital Today on the third-quarter filing, show revenue of KRW 382.99 billion, operating profit of KRW 78.5 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 80.5 billion. Over the last four quarters, controlling-shareholder net income totaled KRW 100.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Kctech's demand outlook rests on memory chip capital spending. In a December 2025 report, DB Investment & Securities said Samsung Electronics and SK hynix were moving to 1cnm DRAM processes and adding new capacity during a DRAM upcycle.

The same report projected 2026 operating profit of KRW 75.8 billion (up 36% year on year), an estimate made as of December 2025. Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 of KRW 78.5 billion already exceeds that projection.

Bullish drivers include wider adoption of AI, big data and autonomous driving technologies and a rising localization rate for chip equipment. On the bearish side, in early 2025 DB Investment & Securities cut its 2025 estimates to reflect conservative capital spending by a key customer.

The cycle position cannot be pinned down with confidence, so memory makers' capacity timing and the pace of capital spending remain the main variables. In competition, the company supplies CMP equipment, cleaning equipment and slurry together, which is described as a portfolio spanning both equipment and materials.

On the display side, it is responding to new equipment demand tied to the shift toward OLED and next-generation displays. Listed sector peers such as Wonik IPS, PSK and Jusung Engineering face the same equipment cycle.

Supercritical cleaning equipment is in customer testing, and DS Investment & Securities said in November 2025 that revenue from it would act as a re-rating factor once booked.

Outlook

The major disclosed orders are a single supply contract with SK hynix for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, disclosed in March 2026, and the KRW 40.16 billion contract with Samsung Electronics disclosed on August 12, 2026.

The Samsung contract's filing did not include product details or a delivery schedule, and a news report judged that its revenue would likely be recognized after the current quarter.

The second-quarter 2026 preliminary results, disclosed under fair disclosure rules on August 5, showed revenue of KRW 112.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 23.5 billion. Preliminary fourth-quarter results are expected around the turn of the year, following the same pattern.

Cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 of KRW 382.99 billion is almost equal to full-year 2025 revenue of KRW 382.9 billion, so the full-year 2026 comparison depends on fourth-quarter results.

The 2026 annual report is due by March 31, 2027, and full-year 2026 revenue and operating profit will be compared directly with KRW 382.9 billion and KRW 60 billion for 2025.

First-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 34.8 billion is the quarterly peak, making the first quarter of 2027 a comparison point against that level. Supercritical cleaning equipment is in customer testing, and no date for first revenue has been set.

The company's full-year guidance is not part of the material reviewed here. The next checkpoints are fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report, specifically operating margin and the booking of the Samsung contract revenue.

Bull factors

Earnings power recovering

Operating margin recovered from 11.4% in 2023 to 15.7% in 2025, and first-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 34.8 billion was the largest quarterly figure in the disclosed record.

According to Digital Today, cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 of KRW 78.5 billion exceeded full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 60 billion. The margin rise to the 22% range was attributed to higher supply of CMP equipment, slurry and cleaning equipment together.

Large customer orders disclosed

A single supply contract with SK hynix for semiconductor manufacturing equipment was disclosed in March 2026, and a KRW 40.16 billion contract with Samsung Electronics followed on August 12, 2026. The Samsung contract equals 10.49% of revenue as a single order.

Both customers are tied to domestic memory investment, so whether memory capacity additions translate into orders is the point to watch.

Localization and product expansion

The semiconductor segment generated KRW 147.2 billion in first-quarter 2026 revenue, or 94.3% of the total. Rising domestic localization of chip equipment has been cited as a driver of the segment's gains.

The company is developing CMP technology for build-up film, and supercritical cleaning equipment is in customer testing.

Bear factors

Slowdown after the quarterly peak

Operating profit fell from KRW 34.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 23.5 billion in the second and KRW 20.2 billion in the third. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 114.2 billion rose only 1.3% from the prior quarter, a sign that top-line growth has stalled.

Third-quarter operating profit was still up 35.8% from a year earlier, so the reading depends on the comparison period.

Earnings volatility and domestic concentration

In 2023, revenue fell 24.1% and operating profit fell 45.8%, while controlling-shareholder net income dropped 42.8%. Revenue slipped 0.7% in 2025. Domestic sales made up 89.2% in the first quarter of 2026, so earnings lean heavily on the domestic chip investment cycle.

Display losses and order timing

The display segment generated KRW 8.9 billion in first-quarter 2026 revenue, or 5.7% of the total, and still posted a net loss of KRW 130 million. A news report judged that revenue from large contracts would likely be recognized after the current quarter, creating a lag between orders and quarterly results.

Risk factors

Customer capital spending swings

If the pace of capital spending at major customers shifts, orders and revenue move with it. In early 2025, DB Investment & Securities cut its 2025 revenue and operating profit estimates by 10% and 13%, reflecting conservative spending by a key customer. A delay in memory makers' investment execution could also push back equipment order timing.

Revenue recognition timing

The timing of revenue recognition on large contracts drives quarterly results. The KRW 40.16 billion Samsung contract disclosed in August 2026 lacks product and delivery details, and a news report said its revenue would likely be booked in later quarters. A delay in recognition would widen the gap between order announcements and quarterly results.

Uncertain commercialization of new equipment

Supercritical cleaning equipment is in customer testing, and no disclosed date has been set for first revenue. DS Investment & Securities said in November 2025 that related sales would act as a re-rating factor, but commercialization timing depends on test results. The new products have not yet appeared as a contributor in reported earnings.

Overall view

Kctech's annual results from 2022 to 2025 show a recovery path, with operating profit rising in 2024 and 2025 after a 2023 low, and a 2025 operating margin of 15.7%.

In 2026, operating profit fell in the second and third quarters after a first-quarter peak of KRW 156.1 billion in revenue and KRW 34.8 billion in operating profit, while third-quarter controlling-shareholder net income rose from the prior quarter, moving in a different direction.

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 of KRW 78.5 billion exceeds full-year 2025 operating profit.

Bullish factors include order growth centered on the semiconductor segment, including the KRW 40.16 billion Samsung contract, a recovering operating margin, wider localization and testing of new cleaning equipment.

Bearish factors include slowing growth after the quarterly peak, customer concentration with domestic sales at 89.2%, the display segment's continued net loss and earnings volatility tied to customer capital spending.

Because the timing of order revenue recognition varies by quarter, the direction of earnings is hard to read from a single period.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual report and first-quarter 2027 results, and the key question is whether the operating margin and revenue recognition scale hold.
