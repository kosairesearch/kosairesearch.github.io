02

Business structure

d'Alba Global is a skincare-centered cosmetics manufacturer and marketer. In the first quarter of 2026, the cosmetics segment accounted for KRW 168.4 billion, or 98.3% of consolidated revenue, while the inner beauty segment (KRW 0.58 billion) and the home beauty device segment (KRW 2.06 billion) were small.

Within cosmetics, mist products were the largest line at KRW 74.1 billion (43.4%), followed by sun care at KRW 41.5 billion (24.3%) and creams at KRW 17.3 billion (10.1%).

In the same quarter, exports were KRW 117.6 billion (68.7%) and domestic sales were KRW 53.5 billion (31.3%), and within exports, B2C online exports of KRW 79.4 billion (46.4%) were larger than B2B vendor exports of KRW 38.2 billion (22.3%).

In the third quarter of 2026, overseas sales of KRW 116.6 billion rose 59% year on year and made up 75% of revenue, while domestic sales fell 4% to KRW 39 billion.

Overseas customer access is broadening across online channels such as Amazon, TikTok Shop and Shopee and offline retail such as Costco, Sephora, Boots and Watsons. At the end of the third quarter, overseas offline doors totaled about 10,000, more than 1,000 added from the prior quarter, and US Costco doors reached 400.

By region, Europe grew 162% and Greater China 134% year on year, while cumulative third-quarter sales across India, the Middle East and Latin America were about KRW 8.3 billion. Product lines are widening beyond the flagship First Spray Serum into multi-balms, tone-up sunscreens and eye patches.

The company listed on KOSPI in May 2025 and competes with other listed cosmetics companies for overseas retail placement and marketing spend.

04

Earnings analysis

The annual figures on record show revenue and profit rising together. Revenue went from KRW 200.8 billion in 2023 to KRW 309.1 billion in 2024 (up 53.9%) and KRW 519.7 billion in 2025 (up 68.2%).

Operating profit went from KRW 32.4 billion in 2023 to KRW 59.8 billion in 2024 (up 84.4%) and KRW 101.5 billion in 2025 (up 69.6%), and 2025 was the largest year of the 2023 to 2025 period.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent went from KRW 13.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 15.4 billion in 2024 (up 13.3%) and KRW 79.1 billion in 2025 (up 413.0%), with the 2025 jump starting from a low 2024 base. Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts in each year.

The operating margin was 16.2% in 2023, 19.4% in 2024 and 19.5% in 2025, so margins were essentially held in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 402.8% in 2023 to 33.3% in 2024 and 28.6% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 198.6 billion and total liabilities of KRW 56.8 billion at the end of 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 24.3 billion in 2023, KRW 15.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 68.5 billion in 2025. Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 114.2 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 163.4 billion in the fourth quarter (up 43.2% sequentially), a quarter the company describes as the peak season of the year.

First-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 171.2 billion (up 4.8% sequentially) with operating profit of KRW 45.1 billion (up 76.6%). Second-quarter revenue was KRW 186.9 billion (up 9.1%) with operating profit of KRW 47.2 billion (up 4.8%).

Third-quarter revenue of KRW 155.6 billion fell 16.7% sequentially, operating profit of KRW 33.4 billion fell 29.2%, and the operating margin was 21.5%.

The company attributed the third-quarter dip to sell-through of existing product stock during a new product launch and a shortage of sunscreen tube containers, both of which it said normalized during the quarter. Profit attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters totaled KRW 109.7 billion.

05

Industry analysis

d'Alba Global sits in an industry setting where overseas online and offline retail are growing at the same time. Third-quarter 2026 overseas growth appeared across every region: Europe up 162% year on year, Greater China up 134%, ASEAN up 55%, Japan up 34% and North America up 31%.

In Greater China, sales on Douyin and Tmall rose 203% and 98%, respectively, while in ASEAN, Shopee and TikTok Shop led growth, centered on Vietnam. In the second quarter, Europe rose 242% and North America 174%, showing faster expansion in Western markets.

This expansion is taking place alongside cost pressures from won strength and higher global freight rates. The company said it moved part of its logistics to sea freight to ease shipping costs.

In cycle terms, the overseas offline rollout looks to be in an early-to-middle stage, as shown by the addition of more than 1,000 overseas doors in a single quarter to about 10,000.

Competitively, d'Alba Global contests online platform visibility and retail placement with listed peers such as APR, Amorepacific and LG H&H. In product terms, the company is moving from a skincare focus toward hair, body and beauty devices.

06

Outlook

The short-term direction the company has stated is continued growth in the fourth quarter. The company describes the fourth quarter as the peak season of the year and said it would pair large online promotions with wider global retail channel expansion.

On the product calendar, the nearest item is a new beauty device model that the company plans to unveil and launch in the fourth quarter of 2026. Some reports, however, place the beauty device launch in the fourth quarter of 2027, so the timing should be confirmed against official company announcements.

A body-care brand is planned for the first half of 2027, following the d'Alba Professional hair line. For distribution, the company plans to expand to all 625 US Costco stores in 2027 and to add retailers such as Rossmann in Germany and the Czech Republic.

US Costco doors were 400 at the end of the third quarter, so the speed at which store additions show up in revenue is a core checkpoint for the durability of overseas growth.

India and the Middle East grew 374% and 568% year on year in the third quarter, so their contribution to new-market growth also warrants tracking. The company expects the premium fragrance brand in which it took a stake in July to generate more than KRW 10 billion in 2026 revenue.

How much currency translation affects quarterly results if the won strengthens further is also a point to track.

Bull factors

Sustained overseas sales growth

Overseas sales in the second quarter of 2026 rose 74% year on year to about 76% of revenue, and in the third quarter overseas sales grew 59% to 75% of revenue. Europe grew 242% and North America 174% in the second quarter, so Western market expansion is lifting results. Product counts on Amazon and TikTok Shop have also widened.

Structural margin improvement

The operating margin went from 16.2% in 2023 to 19.5% in 2025, and reached 25.3% in the second quarter of 2026 and 21.5% in the third quarter. The company said marketing spend fell about 8 percentage points as a share of sales and that a shift to sea freight reduced logistics costs.

The third-quarter SG&A ratio fell 5.8 percentage points to 54.7%, which the company presented as a result of cost efficiency.

Wider distribution and product lines

Overseas offline doors reached about 10,000 at the end of the third quarter, more than 1,000 added in one quarter. The company plans to take its US Costco footprint from 400 doors to all 625 stores in 2027.

Following the hair brand, a body brand in the first half of 2027 and a new beauty device are widening the product lineup.

09

Bear factors

Won strength and freight costs

By the company's estimate, applying end-of-second-quarter exchange rates to third-quarter overseas sales would have produced about KRW 170.5 billion in revenue, implying a roughly 9% sales effect from won strength. Higher global freight rates also remain a cost variable.

The company offset these through marketing and logistics efficiency, but if currency headwinds persist, room to defend margins could narrow.

Slowing sequential revenue

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 155.6 billion fell 16.7% sequentially, and operating profit of KRW 33.4 billion fell 29.2%. Domestic sales dropped 4% year on year, and sell-through during a product transition created a revenue gap.

The company said both issues normalized within the quarter, but quarterly momentum is below the second-quarter peak.

Cash conversion and valuation load

Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 68.5 billion, below profit attributable to owners of the parent of KRW 79.1 billion. Part of the 2025 profit jump reflects a low 2024 base. With the price-to-book ratio in a range with a large premium over net assets, the earnings trend needs to keep supporting that premium.

10

Risk factors

FX and logistics

Won strength reduces the won value of overseas sales, with the third-quarter effect estimated at about 9%. Higher global freight rates were partly absorbed by the shift to sea freight, but a change in freight conditions could bring cost pressure back. Both factors are external variables that the company has limited control over.

Channel concentration

Sales are concentrated in specific online platforms such as Amazon, TikTok Shop, Douyin and Tmall, and in large retailers such as Costco. Platform policy changes or shifts in retailer inventory could move quarterly revenue. Whether store expansion speed and inventory and reorder management stay aligned is a key item to verify.

Product transitions and supply

Sell-through of existing stock during a new product launch created a third-quarter revenue gap, and sunscreen tube container supply also fell short. Widening into hair, body, beauty devices and fragrance carries execution costs and uncertainty over how new brands perform. The fragrance brand's 2026 revenue target of more than KRW 10 billion is a stated company goal.

Overall view

d'Alba Global is a cosmetics company whose revenue and operating profit have grown together since 2023, with operating profit of KRW 101.5 billion in 2025. That first-nine-month 2026 operating profit exceeded the full-year 2025 figure is based on the preliminary disclosure of November 12, 2026.

Overseas sales are the growth engine, at 75% of third-quarter 2026 revenue. Profitability also improved, with the operating margin rising from 16.2% in 2023 to 19.5% in 2025 and reaching 21.5% in the third quarter of 2026.

On the other side, the company estimates won strength reduced sales by about 9%, third-quarter revenue fell 16.7% sequentially, and domestic sales declined 4%, all of which mark weak points in the quarterly trend.

Operating cash flow in 2025 falling below profit attributable to owners of the parent is another item to check on earnings quality. The valuation sits in a range with a large premium over net assets, so it should be read alongside the earnings trend.

Strengths and weaknesses coexist, and the next checkpoints are fourth-quarter 2026 results and new product timing, the 2026 annual business report, and the pace of Costco expansion in 2027. This report is for information only and does not include a buy or sell opinion.
