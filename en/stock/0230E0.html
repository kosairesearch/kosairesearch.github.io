02

Business structure

Eastend is a multi-brand D2C company that plans and develops its own brands. It operates the women's urban casual brand CITYBREEZE, the contemporary brand ARTID, and the basic cosmetics brand B'note. Two apparel brands form the core, and B'note is a cosmetics line launched in 2025.

The business began on online sales channels. In 2025 the company cut its online sellers from 12 to 3 and clearance-sold long-aged inventory, which shrank sales. The company said it normalized its online sales channels in January 2026.

It has since expanded offline, operating 22 department store locations in Korea and one flagship store in Hannam-dong as of the date of its listing eligibility report. The model is therefore moving from online-grown brands into department store distribution.

In competition, the online-centric women's apparel market has many brands, so channel concentration, inventory management and product sustainability shape competitiveness. The company says it is focusing on product, content and IT service capabilities to launch multiple commerce brands. As of October 2026 the company had 79 employees, and CEO Kim Dong-jin is its largest shareholder.

03

Recent trends

Sales in 2025 were KRW 26.826 billion, down 10.67% from the prior year. The company attributed the decline to a reorganization of its sales channels and clearance sales of long-aged inventory. The 2025 operating loss was KRW 8.922 billion, about 33.3% of sales, and the net loss was KRW 9.267 billion.

At the end of 2025, total assets were KRW 21.4 billion, total liabilities were KRW 16.799 billion and equity was KRW 4.601 billion, with a debt ratio of 365%. Cash flow from operating activities was a outflow of KRW 4.04 billion, and year-end cash was KRW 130 million.

First-half 2026 sales reached KRW 17.82 billion, up 43.24% year on year. Gross profit for the same period was KRW 10.3 billion, and gross margin was 57.92%, which is 9.27 percentage points above 48.65% in the first half of 2025. First-half 2026 sales equaled 66.4% of full-year 2025 sales.

Reports still describe a return to operating profit as an outstanding task. Shares did not trade during regular hours on the first day of KONEX listing on October 26, 2026, and no volume was recorded on the second day afternoon. Paid-in capital as of October 1, 2026 was KRW 2.402 billion.

04

Outlook

The company says the KONEX listing will raise transparency and external credibility and build a track record in capital markets and disclosure capability. Over the medium to long term it plans to move its listing to KOSDAQ.

The key variable for results is whether the sales recovery seen in the first half of 2026 carries through the second half and into the full year. It is equally important whether the gross margin gain can absorb selling and administrative expenses and turn operations profitable.

Since the 2025 operating loss equaled about 33.3% of sales, reports argue that cost restructuring must run alongside revenue growth. Expanding offline stores widens the sales base but can also add fixed costs.

On the financial side, the debt ratio was 365% at the end of 2025 and equity was about KRW 4.6 billion, so funding capacity is an important variable. On the share side, no trades took place immediately after listing, so price formation is at an early stage.

Large holdings, including CEO Kim Dong-jin's 38.5% and Capstone Partners' 16.62% as a 10% holder, limit the number of tradable shares. Full-year 2026 results are scheduled for disclosure in early 2027.

05

Bull factors

First-half sales recovery and channel normalization

First-half 2026 sales reached KRW 17.82 billion, up 43.24% year on year. The company says the recovery followed the normalization of its online sales channels in January 2026. The trend marks a move out of the contraction that followed the 2025 reduction in sellers. First-half sales equal 66.4% of full-year 2025 sales.

Gross margin improvement

Gross margin rose 9.27 percentage points, from 48.65% in the first half of 2025 to 57.92% in the first half of 2026. The company attributes the gain to a lower share of cost of sales in revenue. Reports say first-half gross margin moved close to the 2024 full-year level of about 61.5%.

KONEX listing and disclosure track record

The company plans to use the KONEX listing to raise transparency and credibility and to build disclosure capability. It has set a medium- to long-term goal of moving to KOSDAQ. Kyobo Securities served as designated adviser and listing sponsor.

06

Bear factors

Persistent operating loss and the turn to profit

The operating loss came to roughly one-third of 2025 sales. The net loss of KRW 9.267 billion was larger than the operating loss. Despite the first-half recovery, reporting describes the return to operating profit as an open task.

High debt ratio and cash outflow

At the end of 2025, equity was KRW 4.601 billion and total liabilities were KRW 16.799 billion, a debt ratio of 365%. Operating cash flow was an outflow of KRW 4.04 billion, and year-end cash was only KRW 130 million. These figures can be read as indicating limited financial capacity.

Channel dependence and sales volatility

Sales fell 10.67% in 2025 after the company cut online sellers from 12 to 3 and clearance-sold aged inventory. Revenue is therefore sensitive to channel mix and inventory handling. The 2026 recovery covers only one half-year since channel normalization, so its durability has a short track record.

07

Risk factors

Macro

A slowdown in domestic consumption could reduce demand for mid-priced women's apparel and cosmetics at the same time. Changes in raw material and logistics costs could affect the cost of sales ratio. Interest rate levels feed directly into financing costs for a company carrying debt.

Industry

Fee policies and exposure rules on online platforms directly affect the mix of sales channels. The 2025 channel reduction and clearance sales that cut revenue show how channel dependence turns into earnings volatility. Competition among fashion brands is intense, so the results of new brand launches are uncertain.

Liquidity

No trades were executed on the first or second day after listing, and trading value was recorded at zero. Large holdings, including CEO Kim Dong-jin's 38.5% and Capstone Partners' 16.62%, limit the number of tradable shares. KONEX trading volumes are small, so price moves can be large.

08

Overall view

Eastend listed on KONEX on October 26, 2026, carrying the financial burden of lower 2025 sales and an operating loss. In the first half of 2026, its channel normalization supported a 43.24% sales increase and a gross margin of 57.92%, which provides grounds for the recovery.

However, the return to operating profit has not yet happened, and the 365% debt ratio and operating cash outflow are cited as weaknesses in financial capacity. No trades executed immediately after listing, so price formation is at an early stage.

The strength side is the turn in sales and profitability indicators, while the weakness side is that cost structure and financial burden remain unresolved.

The relative weight of the two factors will show in the second-half 2026 results and the full-year 2026 disclosure through the durability of the sales recovery and the direction of operating profit or loss.
