Report overview

Channel Reset Fuels Revenue Recovery; Profitability the Defining Challenge

Eastend, which achieved revenues exceeding KRW 30 billion in FY2024 via a D2C multi-brand strategy, posted an operating loss of KRW 8.9 billion in FY2025 during a sharp channel restructuring, but has entered a recovery phase as H1 2026 revenues rebounded 43% following online channel normalization—yet operating profitability has yet to be restored.

02

Business structure

Eastend has developed since its 2016 founding into a multi-brand D2C fashion and beauty company by combining data-driven product planning with online distribution capabilities.

Its flagship brand, CITYBREEZE, serves the women's urban casual segment targeting the 20–30 age cohort; the signature "Wrinkle-Free" line has accumulated sales of more than 400,000 units, establishing it as the company's defining hit product.

The second brand, ARTID, is positioned as a young contemporary women's label for the 30s demographic and is being cultivated as Eastend's second growth pillar after CITYBREEZE.

B'note, a skincare line launched in 2025 using domestically sourced ingredients, demonstrated rapid market traction by ranking first across Musinsa's entire beauty category just eight days after its September 2026 product launch.

The distribution network spans major online fashion platforms—Musinsa, W Concept, and 29CM—as well as 22 department store doors nationwide and a flagship store in Seoul's Hannam neighborhood, reflecting a strategy of migrating online-proven brands into brick-and-mortar retail.

A data-responsive production model is designed to keep inventory turnover efficient relative to offline-centric peers. The company completed a Series B financing totaling KRW 13.4 billion across 2023 and 2024, following earlier Seed (KRW 1 billion) and Series A (KRW 5.6 billion) rounds.

Overseas efforts focus on building B2B revenue streams in Japan, China, and the United States; B'note completed initial shipments to Japan in H1 2026 and is reportedly negotiating with partners in the Middle East and India.

Domestic D2C competitors include PPB Studios and LapoLabs, while Eastend differentiates itself by targeting an evolution from a single-category fashion player to a diversified consumer goods platform.

03

Recent trends

FY2025 revenue totaled KRW 26.8 billion, declining 10.67% from KRW 30.0 billion in FY2024. The contraction was driven by a deliberate distribution overhaul in which active online sales channels were cut from twelve to three, paired with clearance sales of long-aged inventory.

In the process, the FY2025 gross margin fell to approximately 48.7%, and selling, general, and administrative expenses materially exceeded gross profit, producing an operating loss of KRW 8.9 billion.

The reversal from the KRW 771 million operating profit in FY2024 is understood to reflect the concentration of one-time restructuring-related costs within FY2025. The net loss widened to KRW 9.3 billion, contrasting sharply with net income of KRW 280 million in the prior year.

By year-end 2025, total equity had contracted to approximately KRW 4.6 billion, the current ratio fell from 229.7% to 109%, the debt-to-equity ratio rose to approximately 365%, and the debt dependence ratio stood at roughly 57%, indicating broad-based balance sheet deterioration.

Online channel normalization from January 2026 has since driven a sharp sales recovery: H1 2026 revenue reached KRW 17.8 billion, up 43.24% year-on-year and equivalent to 66.4% of the full-year FY2025 figure.

The H1 2026 gross margin of 57.92% improved 9.27 percentage points from the year-earlier period, approaching the FY2024 annual level of approximately 61.5%, yet operating losses persisted through H1 2026, leaving the achievement of full-year operating profitability as an outstanding challenge.

04

Outlook

If the revenue rebound pace and gross margin improvement seen in H1 2026 are sustained through the second half, full-year 2026 revenues appear likely to expand materially relative to FY2025.

The pivotal question is whether improvements in gross margin can be converted into operating-level profitability through tighter control of fixed costs, as this is the prerequisite for halting equity erosion and beginning balance sheet repair.

B'note aims to consolidate its domestic skincare position following the Musinsa Beauty top ranking, while the completion of initial Japan shipments and ongoing negotiations with Middle Eastern and Indian partners make the pace of B2B revenue materialization a key metric to watch.

CITYBREEZE broadened its offline footprint by entering Hyundai Department Store Daegu in August 2026, and has shifted to a B2B-centric revenue model in Japan to improve overseas margin quality.

Designation under the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy's "2nd Innovative Premier 1000" program in July 2026 expands access to government export financing and business support, which can be practically meaningful for a small-cap company operating with constrained liquidity.

Over the medium term, the company is pursuing KOSDAQ uplisting, treating the KONEX listing as a stepping stone for accumulating disclosure expertise and management transparency.

However, if the return to operating profitability is delayed, the current leverage and liquidity metrics suggest the potential need for additional financing, making cost discipline and the progress of overseas orders the primary variables to track.

05

Bull factors

Swift Revenue and Margin Recovery Post Channel Normalization

Following online channel normalization in January 2026, H1 revenue surged 43.24% year-on-year, suggesting that the FY2025 contraction reflected a deliberate, temporary adjustment rather than structural brand erosion.

The H1 2026 gross margin of 57.92% surpassed the FY2025 annual figure of 48.65% by 9.27 percentage points, and recovery toward the FY2024 annual level of approximately 61.5% appears underway.

With H1 2026 revenue at 66.4% of full-year FY2025 and H1 gross profit at 79.1% of the FY2025 annual total, the contribution of historically stronger second-half seasonality could support meaningful full-year revenue improvement.

B'note Demonstrates Rapid Traction and Overseas B2B Upside

B'note's achievement of the top ranking across Musinsa's entire beauty category just eight days after its September 2026 product launch confirms rapid consumer penetration in the skincare segment.

The beauty division is led by a specialist with 17 years overseeing global product strategy at Amorepacific (Innisfree, Primera), and initial shipments to Japan were reportedly completed in H1 2026, with partner negotiations underway for the Middle East and India.

If B'note succeeds in establishing B2B export channels, the company's revenue mix could diversify meaningfully away from its fashion-centric base, reducing sensitivity to domestic consumption and currency fluctuations.

Government Recognition Bolsters Credibility and Export Access

In June 2026, CEO Kim Dong-jin received the Presidential Award of Commendation at the 2026 Korea Small Business Leaders Conference, and the company was designated in July 2026 as one of the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy's "2nd Innovative Premier 1000" enterprises, publicly recognizing its innovation and growth potential.

Inclusion in this program expands access to government export financing and business support channels, which can be materially meaningful for a small-cap company with constrained liquidity.

Adherence to KONEX disclosure obligations is expected to enhance governance standards, potentially improving the company's standing with external investors and financial institutions as the KOSDAQ uplisting process matures.

Bear factors

Persistent Operating Losses Amid High Fixed-Cost Structure

Operating losses have persisted through H1 2026 despite improving gross margins, confirming that selling, general, and administrative expenses remain a structural obstacle to income statement recovery.

The FY2025 operating loss of KRW 8.9 billion dwarfs the FY2024 operating profit of KRW 771 million, highlighting the sensitivity of earnings to cost overruns and raising questions about whether the current cost structure has been sufficiently rightsized.

Each additional quarter that operating breakeven is deferred risks further equity erosion, progressively compressing financial headroom from an already low total equity base of approximately KRW 4.6 billion.

Deteriorated Balance Sheet and Liquidity Risk

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to approximately 365% and the current ratio fell to 109% at year-end 2025, indicating heightened short-term repayment pressure across the balance sheet.

A debt dependence ratio of approximately 57% and a deeply negative interest coverage ratio suggest that financial charges will continue to weigh on the path to net profitability even after operating breakeven is achieved.

Should operating losses persist, additional external financing may be required; however, the characteristically low liquidity of the KONEX market constrains equity issuance as a capital-raising avenue, raising the risk of further debt accumulation.

Intensifying D2C Competition and Brand Durability Risk

The domestic online D2C women's fashion market sees rapid new entrant activity across Musinsa, W Concept, and 29CM, and consumer preference cycles are compressing brand lifecycles.

CITYBREEZE's relative dependence on the "Wrinkle-Free" line as its defining product creates concentration risk: if follow-on hit items fail to emerge with sufficient frequency, brand growth momentum could moderate.

Rising platform marketing costs, influencer dependency, and inventory management complexity represent structural vulnerabilities common to D2C small-cap brands, all of which could constrain Eastend's operating leverage as it pursues scale.

07

Risk factors

Domestic Consumer Spending Slowdown

Eastend's core demographic of women in their 20s and 30s is sensitive to macroeconomic conditions and consumer price pressures. A slowdown in domestic consumption or a downshift in fashion and beauty spending priorities could exert downward pressure on the ongoing sales recovery.

The offline expansion strategy into department stores entails higher fixed costs such as rent, meaning a softer revenue environment could offset cost-reduction efforts and delay the return to operating profitability.

Overseas Expansion Execution Risk

Targeting Japan, China, the United States, and the Middle East simultaneously risks dispersing management resources and escalating localization costs.

Establishing stable B2B partnerships in foreign markets typically demands considerable time and capital, and if key partner relationships fail to materialize or are disrupted, overseas revenue targets could be significantly delayed.

While elevated global interest in K-fashion and K-beauty provides a constructive backdrop, exchange rate volatility and international logistics cost fluctuations introduce additional earnings risks.

Financing and Financial Liquidity Risk

With FY2025 losses having reduced total equity to approximately KRW 4.6 billion and the current ratio to 109%, the need for additional external financing before operating profitability is restored cannot be ruled out.

The KONEX market's characteristically low trading volume constrains equity issuance as a near-term capital-raising channel, meaning further reliance on debt could add to an already elevated leverage profile.

As of October 27, 2026, the daily trading volume registered zero, confirming that secondary-market liquidity has yet to be established and that capital-market funding avenues remain limited in the near term.

08

Overall view

Eastend has traced a differentiated growth path in fashion and beauty via its D2C multi-brand strategy since its 2016 founding, formally entering the capital markets with its KONEX listing on October 26, 2026.

On the positive side, the 43.24% H1 2026 revenue rebound and gross margin improvement to 57.92% provide tangible evidence that the FY2025 channel restructuring has begun to yield results, while B'note's rapid Musinsa Beauty traction and the government's Innovative Premier 1000 designation add credibility to the company's growth narrative.

Counterbalancing factors include the persistence of operating losses into H1 2026, a year-end 2025 debt-to-equity ratio of approximately 365%, a current ratio of only 109%, and the structural fragility of a small-cap D2C brand in a competitive online fashion market.

The absence of trading volume on the first day of KONEX listing reflects the market's characteristically low liquidity, which constrains near-term capital-raising optionality.

The central metrics to watch are the timing of a return to operating profitability in the second half of 2026 and the pace at which B'note and CITYBREEZE's overseas B2B revenues materialize, as progress on both fronts will largely determine whether the current balance sheet trajectory can be stabilized.

Accumulating disclosure discipline and governance capability through the KONEX stage is a substantive precondition for the company's stated objective of eventually uplisting to KOSDAQ.
