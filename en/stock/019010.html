02

Business structure

VenueG runs three segments: wedding venues, hotels and golf courses. Based on FnGuide data, the 2025 revenue mix was 58.61% wedding venues, 29.21% golf courses and 12.18% hotels.

As of Q2 2026, the wedding venue segment accounted for 68.74% of consolidated revenue, according to independent research firm ARIS in an August 2026 report.

The flagship venue is The VenueG Seoul near Balsan Station on Seoul Subway Line 5, which the company promotes with parking for 1,200 vehicles and food and beverage service led by a chef from a hotel background.

The distribution business was wound down after the Grand Department Store Ilsan branch, its last store, ended operations at the end of February 2025, and it was classified as discontinued operations in 2025. The Ilsan building is now being renovated into a wedding hall-centered complex.

The Grand Department Store Ilsan branch, The VenueG Seoul, VenueG Country Club and Hotel VenueG are all real estate the company owns directly. A 2024 report by the research firm Valuefinder estimated the combined land and building value at about KRW 434.4 billion.

Demand centers on couples planning weddings, and the number of ceremonies and spending per couple are the key drivers of results. Golf and hotels are smaller than the venue business but diversify the revenue mix.

Competition among wedding halls turns on location, parking and food and beverage quality, and VenueG emphasizes subway access in the city and large-scale parking as its strengths.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 58.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 65 billion in 2023 (up 10.4% year on year) and KRW 70.1 billion in 2024 (up 7.8%), then fell to KRW 48.2 billion in 2025 (down 31.2%). The largest revenue year over 2022 to 2025 was 2024, and the smallest was 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 decline to the classification of its department store operations as discontinued operations. Operating profit was KRW 11.3 billion in 2022, KRW 9.22 billion in 2023 (down 18.4%), KRW 17.8 billion in 2024 (up 93.3%) and KRW 15 billion in 2025 (down 15.6%).

The largest operating profit year over 2022 to 2025 was 2024, and the smallest was 2023. The summer 2025 natural disasters were cited as the main cause of the 2025 operating profit decline. The operating margin was 19.2% in 2022, 14.2% in 2023, 25.4% in 2024 and 31.2% in 2025, the highest of the four years.

Securities analysts read the 2025 margin gain as reflecting lower fixed costs after the exit from the product distribution business.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 19.9 billion in 2022, a profit of KRW 13.8 billion in 2023 (a swing back to profit), a loss of KRW 12.6 billion in 2024 (a swing back to loss) and a profit of KRW 93.7 billion in 2025 (a swing back to profit).

The 2025 controlling-shareholder profit of KRW 93.7 billion far exceeded the KRW 15 billion operating profit, which suggests non-operating items carried a large share of earnings.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 10.7 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 13.8 billion in Q4 2025 (up 28.6% from the prior quarter), KRW 10.4 billion in Q1 2026 (down 25.0%), KRW 12.6 billion in Q2 2026 (up 22.0%) and KRW 9.24 billion in Q3 2026 (down 26.9% quarter on quarter and down 14.0% year on year).

Operating profit moved from KRW 1.37 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 4.74 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 1.92 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 5.36 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 1.3 billion in Q3 2026 (down 4.5% year on year).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 31.3 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 43.9 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 55 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 208 billion in Q2 2026, then turned into a net loss of KRW 89.4 billion in Q3 2026.

ARIS estimates that the first-half 2026 financial asset valuation gain was about KRW 309.1 billion and that it lifted first-half net income. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, totaled KRW 217.5 billion.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 903 million in 2022 to KRW 11.7 billion in 2023, KRW 17.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 18 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 76.4% in 2022 to 63.1% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 445.4 billion, of which KRW 390.6 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 280.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The key variable for the wedding venue market is the number of marriages. In an August 2026 report, ARIS noted that the rise in domestic marriages has raised expectations for an improvement in the industry. The number of ceremonies and average spending per event were also cited as the main drivers of venue earnings.

A company earnings summary on a securities information site said that in Q1 2026 a rebound in marriages and rising demand for small luxury weddings lifted average spending per couple and revenue. On the demand side, both the number of events and spending per event are showing signs of improvement.

Whether the marriage recovery will last should be checked against official annual marriage statistics. The cycle appears to be in a recovery phase for marriages, while the intensity of new wedding hall competition cannot be compared numerically with the data available here.

VenueG's differentiators include its Seoul location near a subway station, large-scale parking and food and beverage service led by a hotel-trained chef.

Golf courses are outdoor facilities and are exposed to weather, which is consistent with the summer 2025 natural disasters cited as the main cause of the operating profit decline.

Exiting the distribution business and concentrating on wedding venues, hotels and golf courses reduced the top line but made the business mix more focused.

06

Outlook

The most important item to confirm is the renovation of the Grand Department Store Ilsan branch. According to the ARIS report, the complex is set for completion in July 2027 and will include three large wedding halls, a large supermarket, food and beverage brands, specialty stores and a company-run fitness center.

Press reports cited a view that the new facilities could add roughly 50% of the existing wedding business's revenue once operating. ARIS forecast 2026 revenue of KRW 51.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 16.5 billion.

This is a securities analyst estimate, not a disclosure, so full-year results should be checked against the 2026 annual report. Operating profit in Q3 2026 was KRW 1.3 billion, down 4.5% year on year, so it remains to be seen whether quarterly core earnings track the full-year forecast.

On shareholder returns, ARIS argues that treasury shares equal to 16.6% of total shares issued could benefit from the amended Commercial Act requiring cancellation of treasury shares. Whether a cancellation is actually decided can be confirmed through a board resolution disclosure.

The company is estimated to hold about 1.5 million Samsung Electronics shares and about 40,000 SK hynix shares, so its asset value and quarterly valuation gains or losses move with those two stocks.

ARIS estimates the combined stake value at roughly KRW 720 billion to KRW 1.1 trillion depending on share prices and the timing of a sale. Because such estimates depend on price assumptions, actual fair values should be checked against the fair value figures in the quarterly reports.

Bull factors

Recovery in the wedding venue core

Per a securities information site, Q1 2026 revenue rose 13.9% year on year, with a rebound in marriages and demand for small luxury weddings lifting spending per couple. As of Q2 2026, the venue segment made up 68.74% of consolidated revenue, according to ARIS in August 2026. A venue-heavy revenue mix is described as a direct beneficiary of a recovering marriage market.

Asset value of listed-share holdings

ARIS assessed the fair value of domestic listed shares held at the end of Q2 2026 at about KRW 504.9 billion, roughly 2.5 times the market capitalization. The firm estimates the company holds about 1.5 million Samsung Electronics shares and about 40,000 SK hynix shares.

The same report said first-half valuation gains of about KRW 309.1 billion on financial assets lifted net income.

Treasury shares and shareholder return expectations

ARIS said treasury shares equal to 16.6% of total shares issued could benefit from the amended Commercial Act requiring cancellation of treasury shares. This is an analyst view, and an actual cancellation decision would need to be confirmed through a board disclosure.

The debt ratio of 63.1% at the end of 2025, lower than in 2022, is cited as widening room for shareholder returns.

Bear factors

Quarterly earnings dependent on valuation marks

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 89.4 billion in Q3 2026. Operating profit in the same quarter was KRW 1.3 billion, down 4.5% year on year.

The wide gap between operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income means much of the earnings arise outside core operations, and a change in fair value can shift quarterly results sharply.

Shrinking top line and business restructuring

Revenue fell 31.2% to KRW 48.2 billion in 2025, reflecting the reclassification of the department store operations as discontinued. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 9.24 billion was down 14.0% year on year, so the top line has not recovered quarterly after the restructuring.

Concentration in venues and external shocks

As of Q2 2026, the venue segment accounted for 68.74% of consolidated revenue, leaving the company highly dependent on marriage demand. Summer natural disasters were cited as the main cause of the 2025 operating profit decline, and golf courses with outdoor facilities are exposed to weather.

Whether the KRW 16.5 billion full-year 2026 operating profit forecast by ARIS is achieved depends on this concentrated structure.

Risk factors

Fair value risk on financial assets

Quarterly net income attributable to controlling shareholders is driven by fair value changes in the listed shares held. A decline in Samsung Electronics or SK hynix shares could produce valuation losses, and the Q3 2026 swing to a net loss is consistent with this structure. Share price moves are outside the company's control.

Marriage demand and concentration

With a high venue share of revenue, a turn in marriage trends would hit results directly. Whether the marriage recovery persists needs to be checked against official statistics. Higher per-event prices may be a side effect of demand recovery, so volume and price should be assessed separately.

Renovation timing and investment burden

The Ilsan renovation targets completion in July 2027, and any delay would push back the start of new revenue contribution. A large mixed-use complex brings construction costs and pre-opening burdens.

Total liabilities stood at KRW 280.9 billion at the end of 2025 and the debt ratio fell to 63.1%, but the scale of renovation spending still needs to be confirmed through disclosures.

Overall view

VenueG's core wedding venue business shows signs of improvement as the marriage recovery and higher spending per event come together, and it accounted for 68.74% of consolidated revenue as of Q2 2026.

Revenue fell 31.2% to KRW 48.2 billion in 2025, but the operating margin rose to 31.2%, with the exit from the distribution business behind the gain.

On the other hand, net income attributable to controlling shareholders depends heavily on valuation marks on the listed shares, so a KRW 93.7 billion profit in 2025 coexists with a KRW 89.4 billion net loss in Q3 2026.

The fair value of the listed shares, estimated at about KRW 504.9 billion at the end of Q2 2026, and treasury shares equal to 16.6% of total shares issued are presented as asset and shareholder return strengths, but these are independent research estimates.

The Grand Department Store Ilsan renovation targets completion in July 2027 and could become a new growth driver, but its timing and investment burden still need to be confirmed.

The next checkpoints are full-year operating profit in the 2026 annual report and venue results in the Q1 2027 quarterly report, and the key question is whether the core recovery can be seen separately from valuation marks at those points.
