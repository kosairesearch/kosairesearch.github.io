Report overview

From Losses to Profit, with AI Character Services Under Test

ANKEY AX (Topco Media) turned operating profit positive in 2025 and kept it in the second and third quarters of 2026, but its controlling-shareholder net income base is still thin, and shareholder selling and remaining convertible bond supply are the factors to watch.

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

ANKEY AX (Topco Media) has organized its business around the webtoon platform Toptoon. Etoday reported that the company wound down a chronically loss-making business and absorbed Toptoon, an unlisted affiliate, after which it entered a phase of improving results.

Following the Toptoon merger, one company now handles planning and production of webtoon IP as well as platform operations. The company attributed first-half 2026 earnings improvement to growth in its domestic platform business and a strategy shift in its Japan business.

The center of its AI push is Toptoon Chat, an AI character service built on its own webtoon IP, which launched in March 2026. The company said Toptoon Chat generated meaningful domestic sales within about three months of launch.

Toptoon and Toptoon Chat share the same coin payment system, so existing webtoon users can use their coins for AI character conversations. The company has also recently launched World Chat, which lets users talk with multiple characters at once.

The company plans to extend a single IP into multiple AI content formats in connection with group platforms such as Novelpia.

At an extraordinary shareholders meeting on September 10, 2026, it approved a name change to ANKEY AX and added four AI-related business purposes, including AI content, AI chatbots, virtual humans, and AI solutions.

In terms of competition, the key comparison is how far the company can extend its webtoon IP, paying users, and payment ecosystem into AI character services.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales went from KRW 22.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 19.2 billion in 2023, a 15.2% decline from the prior year, then rose to KRW 23.3 billion in 2024, up 21.7%, and to KRW 48.6 billion in 2025, up 108.2%.

Sales grew for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 through 2025 while 2023 was the smallest. Operating profit moved from KRW 341 million in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 2.65 billion in 2023, and the loss widened to KRW 3.73 billion in 2024.

Operating profit returned to KRW 3.67 billion in 2025, and the operating margin moved from -13.8% in 2023 and -16.0% in 2024 to 7.6% in 2025.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.03 billion in 2022, KRW 11.9 billion in 2023, KRW 4.72 billion in 2024, and KRW 2.32 billion in 2025, so the company recorded losses in each of the four years from 2022 through 2025, with the loss narrowing in 2024 and 2025.

Quarterly results show that Q3 2025 sales were KRW 13.9 billion, operating profit was KRW 1.67 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.79 billion.

In Q4 2025, sales rose 2.2% quarter on quarter to KRW 14.2 billion, but operating profit fell 21.2% to KRW 1.32 billion and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.87 billion.

In Q1 2026, sales fell 16.4% to KRW 11.9 billion and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 83.53 million, while the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed to KRW 126 million.

Q2 2026 sales rose 30.4% quarter on quarter to KRW 15.5 billion, operating profit was KRW 3.67 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.18 billion, a return to profit.

The company attributed this period partly to the inclusion of Toptoon Chat results in Q2, and reported that Q2 operating expenses of KRW 11.8 billion were slightly lower than KRW 11.9 billion a year earlier.

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 16.5 billion, up 6.4% quarter on quarter and 18.4% year on year, operating profit was KRW 3.38 billion, down 7.8% quarter on quarter but up 102.8% year on year, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.82 billion, down 11.3% quarter on quarter.

The four-quarter total of net income attributable to controlling shareholders from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026 is KRW 2 billion, a small cumulative figure relative to the quarterly operating profit scale.

05

Industry analysis

Compared with entertainment companies that earn from IP in the media and entertainment sector, such as HYBE, SM Entertainment, JYP Ent., and YG Entertainment, ANKEY AX (Topco Media) connects original webtoon IP to platform payments and AI character services.

Its revenue structure differs from music and artist-centered IP businesses, which is the starting point for comparison. In the webtoon platform market, the paying user base and competition to secure original works are the key variables, and an established payment ecosystem can act as an entry barrier for new services.

In generative AI character chat services, competitiveness depends on how faithfully original characters' personalities and worlds are reproduced and how quickly user responses are reflected in content.

The company says it is applying the PD production capability built in its existing content business to its AI character services. Content companies adding AI to their businesses are moving in two directions, production efficiency and new revenue sources, and ANKEY AX's approach leans toward the latter.

How much the AI services contribute to sales cannot be compared with figures, because the company has not disclosed such numbers.

Japan also appeared in the first-half 2026 earnings explanation as a business under strategic shift, so the division of roles between domestic and Japanese platforms is a distinguishing factor within the sector.

06

Outlook

Using the name change as a starting point, the company has said it will expand its business in webtoon IP combined with AI. Its articles of incorporation added four items, AI content, AI chatbots, virtual humans, and AI solutions, and the stock name on KOSDAQ is set to change once corporate registration is completed.

Following Toptoon Chat with World Chat, the company is diversifying the formats of its character services. It plans to combine its own IP with AI technology to extend one IP into multiple content formats and link it with group platforms such as Novelpia.

The pace at which the new businesses add to existing webtoon results, and the actual sales scale, are the next points to confirm. On the supply side, the exercise of rights on a KRW 13 billion private exchangeable bond has begun, and potential shares that could reach the market are a variable being discussed.

The remaining principal of the bond is about KRW 10.7 billion, and the exchangeable shares total about 4.1 million, equal to 8.31% of issued shares. The controlling shareholder, ANKEY Holdings, sold 2.09 million shares in an off-market sale on October 30, 2026, which lowered its stake to 62.59%.

The company said its room for artificial price support is limited and that it follows the principle of disclosing decisions through regulatory filings.

08

Bull factors

Profitability Holding Up

Operating profit turned to KRW 3.67 billion in 2025 from a loss, and quarterly operating profit stayed positive with KRW 3.67 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 3.38 billion in Q3 2026. Q3 2026 operating profit rose 102.8% from a year earlier. The company cited domestic platform growth and a strategy shift in Japan as drivers of first-half improvement.

Early Sales from the AI Character Business

The company said Toptoon Chat generated meaningful domestic sales about three months after its March 2026 launch. Because existing paying webtoon users can use the same coin payment system for AI character chats, the burden of acquiring new users may be relatively lower. The addition of World Chat has also widened the service formats.

Improved Earnings Structure

According to press reports, the operating margin in Q2 2026 was 24%. Operating expenses in that quarter were KRW 11.8 billion, slightly lower than KRW 11.9 billion a year earlier. The 2025 annual operating margin was 7.6%, moving out of the loss-making years of 2023 and 2024.

09

Bear factors

A Thin Earnings Base

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 2 billion over the last four quarters. In Q4 2025 the company swung to a net loss of KRW 3.87 billion attributable to controlling shareholders, and in Q1 2026 it posted an operating loss of KRW 83.53 million.

Quarterly profits have continued, but swings between quarters are large, so earnings persistence has been verified only over a short period.

Supply Overhang

The controlling shareholder sold 2.09 million shares in an off-market sale on October 30, 2026, lowering its stake to 62.59%. About 4.1 million exchangeable shares remain, equal to 8.31% of issued shares. Concern has been raised that potential supply could reach the market as the share price recovers.

Unconfirmed Monetization of New Businesses

The company described Toptoon Chat's sales as meaningful without giving a figure. The added business purposes still require corporate registration and a stock name change following the September 2026 shareholder meeting. Q3 2026 operating profit fell 7.8% from the prior quarter, so the quarterly trend is not smooth.

10

Risk factors

Supply and Overhang

If exercise of the convertible bond rights proceeds, about KRW 10.7 billion of remaining principal and about 4.1 million exchangeable shares could reach the market. Because the controlling shareholder reduced its stake through an October 2026 off-market sale, further disposals are also a focus. Supply factors can move the share price regardless of operating results.

Monetizing New Businesses

AI character services have a short operating history, and their sales scale is not disclosed in figures, which makes them hard to separate from existing webtoon results. If user retention and payment conversion for AI services fall short of expectations, their contribution to profit could stay limited.

Whether the renamed company and expanded business purposes translate into results will become clear in periodic reports.

Share Price Volatility and Reliance on Catalysts

A press report said the share price rose 23.69% from the prior day at the close on November 11, 2026. The same report emphasized that the duration of the catalyst and the timing of its reflection in results matter more than the surge itself.

Because the share price responds to catalysts such as the name change and AI business, any gap between disclosures and actual results could drive price swings.

12

Overall view

ANKEY AX (Topco Media) turned to annual operating profit of KRW 3.67 billion in 2025 from losses, and operating profit stayed positive in Q2 and Q3 2026. Q3 2026 sales were KRW 16.5 billion, up 18.4% from the prior year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was positive at KRW 3.18 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 2.82 billion in Q3 2026, but net losses in Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 keep the four-quarter total at KRW 2 billion.

Its new businesses include Toptoon Chat and World Chat, and the company is extending webtoon IP, paying users, and its payment ecosystem into AI character services. The sales scale of the new businesses has not been disclosed in figures.

On the supply side, off-market sales by the controlling shareholder and remaining convertible bond shares remain short-term variables. The share price is above the book value of equity, and earnings-based multiples are sensitive to quarterly results.

Profitability improvement and changes in the shareholder structure are proceeding at the same time, and the next checkpoint is whether the 2026 annual report keeps operating margin and net income attributable to controlling shareholders positive.
