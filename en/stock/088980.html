Report overview

Payout Gap, With the Data Center as Swing Factor

Macquarie Korea Infrastructure Fund faces a lower first-half 2026 payout and a possible further decline in 2027, while the ramp-up of the Hanam Data Center has become the key variable for medium-term cash flow.

02

Business structure

Macquarie Korea Infrastructure Fund is the largest listed infrastructure fund in Korea, established under the Act on Private Participation in Infrastructure. At the end of March 2026 it held 19 assets: 13 toll roads, one port, one railway, three city-gas businesses and one data center.

Of KRW 3.0 trillion invested, subordinated loans account for KRW 2.1 trillion (69%), equity for KRW 0.9 trillion (30%) and senior loans for KRW 15 billion (1%). Because subordinated loans dominate, interest income is the core earnings driver.

Samsung Securities estimated 2026 interest income at about KRW 290 billion and dividend income at about KRW 125 billion, with total operating revenue near KRW 415 billion. These are Samsung Securities estimates.

Since 2020 the fund has added a light-rail line, city-gas operators and a data center. As a result, the road share fell from 84% in 2019 to 55% at the end of March 2026.

Its main road holdings are a 64.05% stake in Incheon Bridge, 24.1% in Incheon International Airport Expressway (Shinkonghang Highway) and 18.16% in Seoul-Chuncheon Expressway. Incheon Bridge is the largest of these by invested amount.

Unlike the REITs in its sector classification, listed infrastructure funds have few direct peers, and its market capitalization is well above SK REIT (about KRW 1.6 trillion) and Lotte REIT (about KRW 1.2 trillion). Those peer figures come from an investment information site as of September 30, 2026.

In 2025 the fund began regular external fair-value appraisals, and a KRW 1.6 trillion valuation gain was booked in the fourth quarter. This is an accounting item that does not directly affect distributions.

03

Recent trends

As of 17:02 KST on October 27, the share price was KRW 9,590, down 1.34% on the day, with trading value of KRW 5.2 billion. The 52-week low is KRW 9,320 and the high is KRW 11,890, so the current price sits near the lower end of that range.

In its April 3, 2026 report, Samsung Securities cited a then-current price of KRW 11,300. In the first quarter of 2026, the end of the Baekyang Tunnel liquidation dividend cut operating revenue by 4.9% and net profit by 6.0% year over year; the prior-year quarter had included a KRW 14.6 billion liquidation dividend.

In the second quarter, operating revenue fell 9.1% and net profit 12.5% year over year, and Samsung Securities pointed to weak road results tied to the Middle East war.

Payouts also declined. According to KB Securities, the first-half 2026 dividend per share was KRW 350, down 7.9% year over year. Lower container volume at BNCT (Busan New Container Terminal) and a lower subordinated-loan rate after its restructuring drove the decline. Brokerage expectations have eased accordingly.

KB Securities cut its target price by 13.3% on June 30, 2026, and Samsung Securities set KRW 12,900 on July 30 while Shinyoung Securities set KRW 12,000 on September 8. Samsung Securities gave a target of KRW 11,300 on October 2.

On investor flows, over the 20 sessions to September 30, 2026, foreign investors were net sellers of about KRW 40 billion and institutions net buyers of about KRW 47.6 billion. In October the shares moved within a narrow range without notable disclosures or news.

04

Outlook

Samsung Securities expects BNCT's restructuring, the Baekyang Tunnel liquidation and the 2027 expiry of the Sujeongsan Tunnel concession to weigh on payouts in the short term.

The same report sees 2028 as the turning point, when ongoing repayment of senior loans on existing assets should raise their capacity to distribute. KB Securities said visibility on earnings and payouts should improve in the second half of 2027, or from 2028 at the latest, as new assets contribute more.

The two brokers differ on when the Hanam Data Center reaches full operation: KB Securities points to the second half of 2027, Samsung Securities to the second quarter of 2027.

Samsung Securities estimates the Hanam Data Center's 2027 rental income at KRW 85 billion and EBITDA at KRW 56 billion. Applying a 5.0% capitalization rate gives about KRW 1.1 trillion, and a 4.25% rate reflecting the scarcity of the master-lease structure gives about KRW 1.3 trillion.

These are brokerage assumptions, not confirmed appraisals. On new investment, the fund is reportedly considering the Jamsil MICE private project and additional data centers. With borrowing capacity up by KRW 2.3 trillion, converting that capacity into actual investments is the key to medium-term growth.

According to an investment information site's schedule, the third-quarter 2026 results are listed for October 30. Operating revenue for that quarter and the size of the second-half payout are the near-term checkpoints.

05

Bull factors

Hanam Data Center value contribution

According to Shinyoung Securities, the Hanam data-center SPC stake was acquired for KRW 23 billion, was valued at KRW 292.9 billion at the end of June 2026, and 99% of the space is under lease.

Samsung Securities said construction was completed, the ramp-up was 60% done in the first quarter, and EBITDA was KRW 5.6 billion, up 150.8% year over year. The higher the utilization, the larger this asset's cash-flow contribution, which marks room for improvement in operating metrics.

More borrowing room, less equity-raise pressure

An amendment to the PPP infrastructure act raised the borrowing limit for public infrastructure funds to 100% of capital, adding KRW 2.3 trillion of capacity.

Samsung Securities viewed it positively that the likelihood of a rights offering has fallen sharply, since past growth followed a cycle of new investment and equity raising. If new assets can be added without capital raising, dilution concerns for shareholders are reduced.

Diversified portfolio and valuation transparency

With the road share down to 55%, income sources are spread across city gas, rail and a data center. Samsung Securities noted that growth in accrued interest at BNCT slowed and total accrued interest at the fund fell 7.0% year over year in 2025, reversing earlier increases. Regular fair-value appraisals do not affect dividends but were assessed positively for transparency.

06

Bear factors

Extended stretch of lower payouts

The first-half 2026 dividend per share was KRW 350, down 7.9% year over year, and KB Securities expected the full year at around KRW 700. A Samsung Securities researcher said a decline in 2027 dividends is unavoidable. The burden is that a period of weaker income appeal could last until around 2028.

BNCT uncertainty

Samsung Securities said BNCT volumes fell amid geopolitical risk and the fund is prioritizing balance-sheet soundness. KB Securities cited lower volumes and a lower subordinated-loan rate after restructuring as the main reasons for the payout decline. The timing of a recovery in this asset's results remains hard to gauge.

Growth gap and lower expectations

Samsung Securities said meaningful dividend growth is expected around 2028. Among target prices published in the last 90 days, the newer half averages about 7% below the older half, indicating a downward revision trend. If new asset additions are delayed, the gap period could lengthen.

07

Risk factors

Macro

KB Securities cited a higher cost of equity from rising risk-free yields as one reason for lowering its target price. Income-oriented stocks tend to be sensitive to interest-rate changes. In addition, Samsung Securities said road results were weak due to the Middle East war. The effect of geopolitical variables on traffic and cargo volumes also needs monitoring.

Business

The Baekyang Tunnel liquidation and the 2027 expiry of the Sujeongsan Tunnel concession are cited as burdens on payouts. Road assets operate under fixed concession terms, so income ends when a term expires.

For assets such as Incheon International Airport Expressway, whose minimum revenue guarantee period has ended, actual traffic feeds directly into income. The key question is how much new assets can replace expiring concessions and guarantees.

Execution

KB Securities sees visibility on earnings and payouts improving only as new assets contribute more. The Hanam Data Center's utilization is around 50%, so a delay in the ramp-up would push back the cash-flow improvement.

New investments using borrowing capacity could produce varying outcomes depending on the debt burden and interest rates at the time of investment.

08

Overall view

Macquarie Korea Infrastructure Fund is a large listed infrastructure fund diversified across roads, a port, city gas and a data center, and its payout sits at the center of most investment assessments.

In the first and second quarters of 2026, operating revenue and net profit both declined year over year, and the first-half dividend per share fell to KRW 350. Brokerages describe payout pressure continuing through 2027, with an improvement phase starting around 2028.

On the other hand, wider borrowing capacity, rising utilization at the Hanam Data Center and the review of new assets are cited as factors that could support medium-term cash flow.

Brokers differ on when the Hanam Data Center reaches full operation and on payout forecasts, so the spread in estimates should be kept in mind. The third-quarter results, listed for October 30, the size of the second-half payout and any new investment announcement are the near-term checkpoints.
