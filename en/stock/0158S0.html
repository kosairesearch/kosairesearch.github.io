02

Business structure

Elice combines AI education, cloud services and AI infrastructure into one operating structure as an AI full-stack company. The business has four pillars. First, the public-education AI brand Elice School supplies AI courseware for information subjects from elementary to high school, along with teacher work tools.

Second, AI Helpy Chat, an enterprise-only generative AI solution, is a B2B product designed to let organizations use several generative AI models on one platform; its launch was reported in September 2025.

Third, the cloud-based AI education platform Elice LXP had 9,500 adopting institutions and 2.75 million cumulative users, according to reports from August 2025.

Fourth, the infrastructure business runs on Elice Cloud and an AI data center, and Elice Cloud is reported to hold the CSAP security certification used for government workloads.

In education content, Elice competes with domestic and overseas edtech firms; in infrastructure, it competes with cloud and AI infrastructure providers such as Samsung SDS and KT Cloud.

In August 2025, Elice was reported as one of the companies selected with Samsung SDS and KT Cloud under a government program supplying GPUs for AI research.

Beyond education, the company says it runs AI skills training for government and public agencies such as defense and healthcare, and for corporate clients in manufacturing and food. In December 2025, it announced that it had completed a demonstration of an education-focused AI agent running on domestic NPU chips.

03

Recent trends

Cumulative revenue for the first nine months of 2026 reached KRW 20.8 billion, up 132.0% year on year. Operating profit grew 1,163.7% to KRW 6.22 billion over the same period, and the operating margin calculated from disclosed figures is roughly 30%.

Net income rose 2,728.1% to KRW 6.85 billion, well ahead of operating profit growth. Profit growth far outpaced revenue growth, indicating a sharp expansion in operating leverage.

The fact that net income exceeds operating profit points to a contribution from non-operating items, and the breakdown is a matter to check in the notes to the periodic report. Because the disclosed figures are nine-month cumulative, quarterly trends require separate quarterly data.

Among indicators the company has publicized, the number of institutions adopting Elice LXP rose from roughly 4,000 to 9,500, a 2.4-fold increase reported in August 2025.

As of 20:24 KST on November 16, 2026, the share price stood at KRW 61,900, down 0.96% from the previous day, with a market capitalization of KRW 689.9 billion and trading value of KRW 59.7 billion.

Reports from late 2025 said AI textbook adoption fell from 37% in the first semester to 19% in the second, illustrating the volatility of policy-linked businesses.

04

Outlook

On the business side, Elice's AI Helpy Chat and Elice LXP were both selected for the 2026 edtech demonstration program in May 2026, adding to its public-education references.

At the Korea Education Expo in January 2026, the company unveiled AI courseware for information subjects from elementary to high school and an education-focused model called Helpy Edu.

Overseas, reports from December 2025 said Elice was selected as an AI textbook prototype supplier for the Singapore government, with a pilot in two subjects at secondary schools planned for 2026.

On infrastructure, the company outlined plans to pursue faster AI agent response times and a large-scale LLM demonstration in the second phase of its domestic NPU project. It also said it would expand the use of domestic NPU-based AI services from education to public and industrial sectors.

Conversely, continued declines in AI textbook adoption and reduced government support could weigh on the growth pace of education AI revenue. If adoption of AI Helpy Chat and Elice LXP among institutions keeps rising, the company gains a path to broaden revenue beyond education policy demand.

With operating margins at a high level, the balance between revenue scale expansion and cost structure is a key indicator to track.

05

Bull factors

Wider operating leverage

Cumulative disclosed results for the first nine months of 2026 show revenue up 132.0% and operating profit up 1,163.7%, with profit growth well ahead of sales growth. Among indicators the company has publicized, the number of institutions and users on Elice LXP has risen. Whether this improvement is recurring or one-off is a matter for quarterly data.

Growing public-education references

In May 2026, the company said AI Helpy Chat and Elice LXP were selected as demonstration services at Konkuk University in Seoul and Korea National University of Education in Chungbuk.

Both services run on Elice Cloud, which holds the CSAP security certification, and the company presented them as safeguarding student data. The company also plans to refine its products using teacher training and classroom feedback.

In-house AI infrastructure capability

In December 2025, the company announced it had built an NPU farm and cloud infrastructure on a domestic NPU prototype and completed an education-focused AI agent demonstration. It said independent test results exceeded targets across all evaluation items for the AI application model.

In August 2025, Elice was reported as selected alongside Samsung SDS and KT Cloud for a government program supplying GPUs for AI research.

06

Bear factors

Dependence on education policy

Reports from December 2025 said AI textbook adoption fell from 37% in the first semester to 19% in the second, after the textbooks were reclassified as educational material and government support ended. AI textbook developers reportedly restructured their staff as a result. The extent to which policy changes affect Elice's revenue can only be gauged with segment-level disclosure.

Small scale and earnings volatility

Disclosed revenue of KRW 20.8 billion is small compared with large IT services companies. Because net income well exceeds operating profit, non-operating items appear to carry significant weight, and whether they are one-off should be checked in the notes.

With only nine months of cumulative data, it remains to be seen whether profit levels hold as the business scales.

Early-stage overseas and infrastructure projects

The Singapore AI textbook pilot was still a planned project as of the December 2025 reporting, and its results are not yet visible in disclosed figures. The domestic NPU demonstration has a second-phase project still ahead. In cloud and AI infrastructure, Elice competes with large operators such as Samsung SDS and KT Cloud.

07

Risk factors

Demonstration conversion

Whether AI Helpy Chat and Elice LXP, selected for the May 2026 edtech demonstration program, move into formal adoption after the trial is the key to expanding the revenue base.

Demonstrations are trials with set terms and scope, so if results do not convert into paid contracts with schools and institutions, public-education references may not translate into revenue growth.

The timing and scale of any conversion have not yet been confirmed and should be gauged through future disclosures and reports. A delay in conversion could also change the assessment of how durable the profit gains are.

Macro

An economic slowdown and tighter agency budgets could reduce corporate training and public-sector orders. Corporate and public-agency education revenue may be exposed to the economic cycle. Shifts in exchange rates and interest rates could also affect the cost of cloud equipment investment.

Competition and technology

Rapid advances in generative AI models and the spread of free education tools from large platforms could intensify price competition for paid solutions. Education content is crowded with domestic and overseas edtech firms, so differentiation must be sustained. In cloud infrastructure, the scale gap with large operators could affect contract competition.

08

Overall view

Elice's cumulative disclosed results for the first nine months of 2026 show revenue up 132.0%, operating profit up 1,163.7% and net income up 2,728.1%, a marked improvement in profitability indicators.

Wider adoption of enterprise generative AI and education platforms, selection in public-education demonstrations, and the domestic NPU infrastructure demonstration indicate a broadening business base.

On the other hand, lower AI textbook adoption and reduced government support highlight the potential volatility of a business structure that relies on education policy.

Because revenue remains small and the disclosure covers only nine months, the durability of the profit gains and the quarterly trend are matters for further data. The reason net income substantially exceeds operating profit is also a point to examine in the notes to the periodic report.

The share price of KRW 61,900 and market capitalization of KRW 689.9 billion can be read alongside this earnings trend as factual reference points.
