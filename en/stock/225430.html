02

Business structure

KM PHARMACEUTICAL is a life-care company that makes oral care and cosmetic products at its Pyeongtaek site in Gyeonggi Province. The CEO said more than 80% of current revenue comes from its OEM and ODM business.

The Pyeongtaek plant is split into a first factory for medicated toothpaste and toothbrushes and a second factory for skincare, body and hair care products.

According to an Edaily report, the first factory already operates above its practical capacity because of orders from large domestic pharmaceutical companies and overseas brands.

Its own brands include the children's oral care line 'Pororo Toothpaste', the oral care brand FOAMGLE, and the scalp and hair care brand Zerocipe, and own-brand sales still make up less than 20% of revenue. The company has set a target of raising that share to about 50% within three years.

The nanographene material at the core of Zerocipe completed its patent registration in June 2026, according to the company. Sales through the Olive Young channel began after the product entered it in November 2024, as the company explained.

Overseas revenue rests mainly on a distribution deal in Malaysia, shipments of Pororo toothpaste to Vietnam and China, and a collaboration with a Swiss natural cosmetics company, whose cumulative production revenue an industry report put above KRW 7 billion.

Customers include domestic pharmaceutical and household goods companies as well as overseas brand owners, so changes in orders from a single client flow quickly into quarterly revenue.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue rose from KRW 15.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.8 billion in 2023 (up 13.7% year on year), fell to KRW 14.5 billion in 2024 (down 18.5%), and recovered to KRW 16 billion in 2025 (up 10.5%). Among the years 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2024 was the smallest.

The operating loss was KRW 1.25 billion in 2022, KRW 1.09 billion in 2023, KRW 2.57 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.92 billion in 2025, with operating margins of -8.0%, -6.1%, -17.7% and -18.3%. In 2024 and 2025 both the loss amount and the margin worsened.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.92 billion in 2022, KRW 1.32 billion in 2023, KRW 4.57 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.97 billion in 2025, and the net loss including non-controlling interests was disclosed at the same amounts.

The debt ratio rose from 41.2% in 2022 to 64.1% in 2025, and year-end 2025 total equity of KRW 28.3 billion was entirely attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 18.1 billion.

An Edaily report noted that revenue of KRW 22.9 billion in 2020 and KRW 22.7 billion in 2021 fell to KRW 15.6 billion in 2022, when the company moved into operating losses. The CEO attributed the operating losses to R&D and brand marketing costs that were concentrated temporarily.

On a quarterly basis, the operating loss widened from KRW 809 million in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 851 million in the fourth quarter to KRW 659 million, KRW 585 million and KRW 454 million in the first three quarters of 2026.

Quarterly revenue moved up and down across KRW 4.38 billion, KRW 4.29 billion, KRW 4.47 billion, KRW 3.84 billion and KRW 4.03 billion from the third quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026.

The first- and second-quarter 2026 figures add up to the first-half revenue of about KRW 8.3 billion that the company disclosed in August 2026.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 705 million in the third quarter of 2026, and the total for the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, was KRW 3.46 billion.

Five consecutive quarters of losses, from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, are on record.

05

Industry analysis

In domestic oral care OEM and ODM, orders from large pharmaceutical companies determine how much of the production capacity is used. According to an Edaily report, the first factory at KM PHARMACEUTICAL already operates above its practical capacity, and the company is pursuing expansion on the second factory site.

In cosmetics OEM and ODM, the company says inquiries from domestic and overseas brands are increasing, as reported by Sisa Week in March 2026. Overseas, the company said it has a Malaysian exclusive distribution agreement and is in supply talks in Thailand.

Shipments of Pororo Toothpaste reached about 180,000 units to Vietnam in the second quarter of 2026, and about 300,000 units were shipped to China from January to April 2026. In the United States, the company has kept its FDA cGMP facility certification since 2019 and completed the maintenance procedure in 2026.

Compared with Samsung Biologics and other listed companies in the biopharma sector that center on contract manufacturing for drugs, KM PHARMACEUTICAL focuses on quasi-drug and cosmetics production, so its earnings structure and customer concentration differ.

In competition, it faces large oral care manufacturers in toothpaste OEM and specialized firms in cosmetics OEM. The company presents its nanographene material as a point of differentiation in scalp and hair care.

Edaily reported that the CEO set a target of more than KRW 20 billion in revenue for 2026. Quarterly revenue stayed in the range of roughly KRW 4 billion from the first quarter to the third quarter of 2026, so whether the target is reached will be confirmed in the 2026 annual report.

On capacity, the company plans to expand equipment in stages through 2028 to reach annual output of about KRW 30 billion on an eight-hour basis and up to KRW 90 billion on a 24-hour basis.

The company aims to lift own-brand sales to about 50% of revenue within three years, expanding into dermo-cosmetics and aesthetic products such as skin boosters.

A March 2026 report said a scalp treatment product developed with a UK hair clinic was targeted for launch in summer 2026, and whether it launched is a point to confirm through company disclosures and press reports.

The company said the proceeds of the July 2026 rights offering will fund operating capital for new growth businesses, production equipment and balance sheet stability. The next checkpoints are the full-year operating loss to appear in the 2026 annual report and progress on the 2028 capacity expansion.

Bull factors

Signs of improvement in sales and losses

The company disclosed in August 2026 that first-half 2026 revenue reached about KRW 8.3 billion, up 13.4% year on year. It attributed the increase to new OEM and ODM orders and overseas shipments of its own brands.

The operating loss narrowed to KRW 659 million in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 585 million in the second and KRW 454 million in the third.

Planned capacity expansion

The company plans to complete equipment expansion by 2028 to reach a production system of up to KRW 90 billion on a 24-hour basis. Because the first factory already runs above capacity, it describes the second-factory expansion as a means of meeting larger order volumes. It set annual revenue of KRW 50 billion as the first target for 2028.

Overseas demand and partnerships

The company said about 180,000 units of Pororo Toothpaste shipped to Vietnam in the second quarter of 2026, and about 300,000 units shipped to China from January to April 2026.

An industry report said cumulative production revenue from a Swiss natural cosmetics partnership passed KRW 7 billion, with order volume up more than 40% year on year. The company plans to widen export markets to the Middle East and South America in the second half.

Bear factors

Listing procedure and administrative-issue risk

The September 14, 2026 delisting decision was suspended until a final judgment after the Seoul Southern District Court granted an injunction on October 2, 2026. Dilution and a third-quarter revenue decline

Risk factors

Customer and production concentration

Capacity funding and execution risk
