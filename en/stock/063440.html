02

Business structure

SM Life Design is a KOSDAQ-listed company centered on printing albums and photobooks for K-pop artists, with logistics services and studio rental alongside. Its main revenue comes from printing albums for artists of SM Entertainment and others.

In a Hana Securities report, the revenue mix on a first-half 2025 basis was 88% artist printed matter, 8% logistics, 2% studio rental and 2% other. The same report notes that the movie content unit was wound down and the dining unit sold in 2023, so the current structure is simpler and print-centered.

The company prints and produces albums for artists signed to SM Entertainment, and handles album and merchandise logistics for SM Entertainment and SM Brand Marketing. It also works on album printing with Starship Entertainment, a label under Kakao Entertainment.

Logistics agency revenue for the first three quarters of 2025 was KRW 2.77 billion, and the company said logistics carries a low cost share and contributes strongly to the operating margin. The Paju SM Studio, completed at the end of 2024, is a production space of 1,806 pyeong on a site of about 3,000 pyeong.

In 2026 the company set up an MD unit and is pursuing a move into album post-processing. No public data on market share by competing printer was found, so the competitive landscape can only be described qualitatively.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 50.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 47.3 billion in 2023 (down 5.9% year on year) and KRW 44.8 billion in 2024 (down 5.4%), then rebounded to KRW 53.3 billion in 2025 (up 19.1%). Among 2022-2025, 2025 was the largest year for revenue and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 2.53 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.69 billion in 2023, KRW 3.53 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.58 billion in 2025, rising for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, while the operating margin moved from 5.0% to 8.6%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.49 billion in 2022, KRW 4.74 billion in 2023, KRW 4.87 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.5 billion in 2025, also rising for three straight years from 2023, and net income including non-controlling interests is the same amount.

Operating cash flow was KRW 7.67 billion in 2023, then KRW 7.32 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.76 billion in 2025.

By quarter, the third quarter of 2025 had revenue of KRW 18.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.47 billion, but the fourth quarter of 2025 turned to revenue of KRW 10.8 billion (down 41.8% from the prior quarter), an operating loss of KRW 936 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 604 million.

The first quarter of 2026 returned to profit with revenue of KRW 9.69 billion and operating profit of KRW 703 million, and the second quarter rose to revenue of KRW 14.3 billion (up 48.0%) and operating profit of KRW 2.29 billion, and the company said its second-quarter operating margin was 15.9%.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 7.63 billion (down 46.8% from the prior quarter and 58.7% from a year earlier), operating profit was KRW 421 million and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 558 million, down 83.0% and 77.5% respectively from a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 to third quarter of 2026) totals KRW 3.29 billion, below the KRW 5.5 billion for full-year 2025.

According to press reports, cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 was about KRW 3.41 billion and net income about KRW 3.9 billion. No detailed company explanation for the sharp quarterly swings was found.

05

Industry analysis

According to Circle Chart, cumulative physical K-pop album sales in January-September 2026 were about 79 million units, roughly 7.6 million more than a year earlier. Annual sales had fallen from about 120 million in 2023 to about 98.9 million in 2024 and about 93.5 million in 2025.

September 2026 sales were down 38% from August and 37.8% from September 2025. A Circle Chart data journalist read this not as a market downturn but as a swing driven by artists' comeback cycles.

He projected that if fourth-quarter sales merely match the prior year, 2026 annual sales would exceed 100 million for the first time in three years. The sales share of platform albums that use QR codes or NFC rose from 11.3% in 2023 to 17.8% in 2026.

The effect of this format shift on printing volume and unit prices cannot be confirmed from public data. Music producers argued that the first-half 2026 increase was a temporary effect concentrated in large artists, and that album sales at most small and mid-sized agencies actually fell.

SM Life Design is exposed to volumes from the SM Entertainment group and from Starship Entertainment, so whether industry-wide sales and company revenue move in the same direction has to be checked quarter by quarter.

06

Outlook

No company guidance with revenue or profit targets was found; what is confirmed are business expansion and shareholder return plans. The company plans to widen cooperation with existing customers and to win new customers.

It plans to extend from album printing and merchandise logistics into goods planning and manufacturing. To that end it set up an MD unit and is pursuing entry into album post-processing. The revenue size and timing of these new businesses have not been disclosed.

In January 2026 a company official said that the effects of customer diversification, tighter cost control and the use of additional logistics warehouses would show from 2026.

With third-quarter 2026 operating profit at only KRW 421 million, how far this expectation is reflected in quarterly results is something to check in upcoming filings.

The company signed a KRW 1.6 billion treasury-share trust contract running from September 17, 2026 to March 17, 2027, and plans to cancel the acquired shares after the contract ends. It is pursuing a policy of returning about 20% of consolidated net income for each fiscal year from 2025 to 2027.

08

Bull factors

Improving profitability structure

The operating margin rose from 5.0% in 2022 to 8.6% in 2025, and operating profit increased for three straight years from 2023 to 2025. In the second quarter of 2026 operating profit was KRW 2.29 billion, with a company-stated operating margin of 15.9%.

The company explains that low-cost businesses such as logistics agency work contribute to margins. Room to lift the margin further as a business metric depends on whether post-processing and goods planning and manufacturing expand.

Solid balance sheet and shareholder returns

At the end of 2025 total liabilities were KRW 6.21 billion and total equity was KRW 67.3 billion, for a debt ratio of 9.2%. In April 2026 the company decided to cancel 709,790 treasury shares. In September 2026 it signed an additional KRW 1.6 billion trust contract. It is a confirmed fact that the three-year return policy is being carried out in practice.

Customer diversification and business expansion

In addition to volumes from the SM Entertainment group, cooperation with Starship Entertainment continues. The company said it expected Starship Entertainment-related revenue to exceed KRW 7 billion for full-year 2025.

In 2026 it is broadening revenue sources beyond printing by setting up the MD unit and pursuing goods planning and manufacturing. If new customers are won, customer concentration could ease.

Bear factors

Sharp drop in third-quarter results

Revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 7.63 billion, down 58.7% from a year earlier, and operating profit was KRW 421 million, down 83.0%. Because the fourth quarter of 2025 produced an operating loss of KRW 936 million, quarterly profit clearly does not move in one direction.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, KRW 3.29 billion, is below the KRW 5.5 billion for full-year 2025.

Dependence on key customers and comeback schedules

Revenue relies on volumes from a small number of agencies such as the SM Entertainment group and Starship Entertainment. Album sales swing sharply with the comeback schedules of a small number of fandoms. If customers' release schedules shift, printing and logistics volumes can change from quarter to quarter.

Lower operating cash flow and uncertain new businesses

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 7.67 billion in 2023 to KRW 7.32 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.76 billion in 2025. The revenue contribution of goods planning and manufacturing and of post-processing has not been disclosed, and cost burdens from equipment and staffing also need checking.

Hana Securities assessed that, excluding depreciation, the Paju studio can generate stable earnings. The presence of depreciation burden can be a variable for profit margins.

Risk factors

Customer concentration risk

Printing albums for SM Entertainment artists and logistics for SM Entertainment and SM Brand Marketing are the core of the business. A change in a major customer's release plans or contract terms could shift revenue and profit materially.

Revenue share by customer could not be confirmed through search and should be checked in the major customer section of periodic reports.

Industry and album format risk

Physical K-pop album sales fell in 2024 and 2025 and rose again in January-September 2026, but September was down 37.8% year on year. A rising share of platform albums could change the composition of printing volumes. Industry data is concentrated in large artists, so it may not match this company's own volumes.

Profitability and investment risk

Quarterly operating results moved sharply, from an operating loss of KRW 936 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 to operating profit of KRW 2.29 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 421 million in the third. The cost-of-sales and SG&A structure is hard to break down quarter by quarter from filings.

Depreciation and utilization tied to the Paju studio, the logistics center annex and printing equipment investment can affect profit swings.

Overall view

Backed by album printing and logistics, SM Life Design lifted its operating margin from 5.0% in 2022 to 8.6% in 2025, and its debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 9.2%.

In contrast, third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 7.63 billion and operating profit was KRW 421 million, down 58.7% and 83.0% respectively from a year earlier. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 3.29 billion, below the KRW 5.5 billion for full-year 2025.

Under its three-year return policy the company is cancelling shares and has signed an additional trust contract, while widening its business through MD planning and manufacturing and post-processing.

On the industry side, album sales in January-September 2026 were higher than a year earlier, but September fell 37.8% year on year, showing large swings tied to comeback schedules.

The bullish side points to margin improvement and financial stability, and the bearish side to quarterly volatility and customer concentration. The next things to check are fourth-quarter industry conditions, full-year 2026 results and the revenue contribution of new businesses.
