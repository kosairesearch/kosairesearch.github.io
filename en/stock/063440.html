02

Business structure

SM Life Design centers on album printing and production, and also runs MD logistics, studio rental, property leasing and wine distribution.

Its main on-going customer is SM Entertainment, for which it prints and produces albums for artists under contract and handles album and MD logistics for SM Entertainment and SM Brand Marketing. It also works with a Kakao Entertainment-affiliated label on album printing, which broadens its client base.

In 2025 the company said that album printing volume rose in the first half on steady growth in NCT, RIIZE and aespa releases, along with popularity among artists at Kakao-affiliated labels. It has invested in new printing equipment to diversify its client base.

Its scope is also widening: in Q3 2026 it created an MD unit that extends logistics from storage and shipping into MD planning and production, and it added a finishing process to album production. The company has also said it plans to expand into goods planning and manufacturing.

Because the business depends heavily on one major label's release volume, changes in the client mix are the largest variable for earnings. The revenue split by segment is a item to check in the business section of the annual report.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 50.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 47.3 billion in 2023 (down 5.9% year on year) and KRW 44.8 billion in 2024 (down 5.4%), then recovered to KRW 53.3 billion in 2025 (up 19.1%). 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025 by revenue.

Over the same period operating profit rose from KRW 2.53 billion to KRW 2.69 billion, KRW 3.53 billion and KRW 4.58 billion, growing for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. Operating profit grew 31.3% in 2024 even as revenue declined, which points to a change in the earnings structure.

The operating margin moved from 5.0% in 2022 to 5.7% in 2023, 7.9% in 2024 and 8.6% in 2025. The company said in its 2025 results that new investment and logistics efficiency improved operations, and it described 2025 as a record year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 2.49 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.74 billion in 2023 (up 90.5%), KRW 4.87 billion in 2024 (up 2.8%) and KRW 5.5 billion in 2025 (up 12.9%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts each year, so no non-controlling share appears. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 29.5% in 2022 to 9.2% in 2025, and total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 67.3 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders.

Total liabilities were KRW 6.21 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 3.01 billion in 2022, KRW 7.67 billion in 2023, KRW 7.32 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.76 billion in 2025, positive in all four years. On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 18.5 billion and operating profit KRW 2.47 billion.

Q4 2025 revenue fell to KRW 10.8 billion (down 41.8% from the prior quarter) and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 936 million, with a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 604 million. Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 9.69 billion with operating profit of KRW 703 million, a return to profit.

Q2 2026 revenue rose to KRW 14.3 billion (up 48.0% from the prior quarter) and operating profit to KRW 2.29 billion (up 225.1%).

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 7.63 billion (down 46.8% from the prior quarter and down 58.7% year on year), operating profit KRW 421 million (down 83.0% year on year) and net income attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 558 million (down 77.5% year on year).

Over the last four quarters, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 3.29 billion. News reports put cumulative revenue for the first nine months of 2026 at about KRW 31.7 billion, while the first nine months of 2025 were reported at KRW 42.5 billion.

The company announced a Q2 2026 operating margin of 15.9%, which it said has risen each year since 2023. The causes of the Q3 2026 drop and the Q4 2025 loss should be checked against cost and revenue explanations in the annual report.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for album and photobook production moves with comeback cycles and release volume of contracted artists. Because a high share of SM Life Design's volume comes from the SM Entertainment group, activity by group artists largely sets the direction of its results.

Within the media and entertainment sector, the company sits closer to production infrastructure such as printing and logistics than to talent agencies. Its revenue structure differs from listed agencies such as Hybe, SM Entertainment, YG Entertainment and JYP Ent., which earn from artist management, music and concerts.

On competition, the company has widened its client base beyond SM Entertainment to a Kakao-affiliated label and has invested in equipment to absorb volume. The company said 2025 was a record year, and quarterly results then fell sharply in Q3 2026.

News reports said Q1 2026 revenue rose 34.5% year on year, and the company attributed the Q2 2026 margin gain to higher volume and operating efficiency. If demand for physical albums shifts, the mix of printing volume could change too, so this should be checked against industry data separately.

Merchandise planning and production move with artist activity and are likely to follow a cycle similar to album production.

06

Outlook

The company is pursuing a policy to return 20% of consolidated net income for each fiscal year from 2025 to 2027 to shareholders through share buybacks, cancellations and dividends.

In March 2026 it entered into a share purchase trust agreement worth KRW 1.1 billion, and in April it decided to cancel 709,790 common shares. In September 2026 it announced a KRW 1.6 billion trust agreement, with the term running to March 17, 2027, and said the treasury shares acquired under it would be cancelled.

In business terms, the company says it will use MD planning and production and album finishing work to win new clients and improve operating efficiency. Expansion into goods planning and manufacturing is also a plan the company has stated.

First-half 2026 consolidated revenue was about KRW 24.03 billion and operating profit about KRW 2.99 billion. No numeric annual revenue or profit target appears in the filings reviewed, so the next check on the trend is the 2026 annual report.

With cumulative revenue for the first nine months of 2026 at about KRW 31.7 billion, whether Q4 2026 reverses the Q3 slowdown is the key point to watch. How much the goods expansion and MD production contribute to revenue will also show up in the segment information of the annual report.

Because the return amount is tied to net income, whether profit trends and the pace of share cancellations align is the next point to check.

08

Bull factors

Structural margin improvement

The annual operating margin rose from 5.0% in 2022 to 8.6% in 2025. The company announced a 15.9% operating margin for Q2 2026, more than double the Q2 2023 level. It cites new investment and logistics efficiency as the drivers.

Shareholder return policy and share cancellations

The company set a policy to return 20% of consolidated net income from 2025 to 2027. Trust agreements of KRW 1.1 billion in March 2026 and KRW 1.6 billion in September, plus the cancellation of 709,790 shares in April, followed. It has said the treasury shares acquired will be cancelled.

Financial capacity and cash generation

At year-end 2025 the debt-to-equity ratio was 9.2%, total liabilities KRW 6.21 billion and total equity KRW 67.3 billion. Operating cash flow was positive in all four years from 2022 to 2025. A solid capital base gives room to fund shareholder returns, which is a bull-side factor.

09

Bear factors

Sharp Q3 2026 profit decline

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 7.63 billion, down 46.8% from the prior quarter and 58.7% year on year. Operating profit of KRW 421 million fell 83.0% year on year. Cumulative revenue through the third quarter was reported at about KRW 31.7 billion.

Client concentration and related-party work

Album printing for SM Entertainment artists and album and MD logistics for SM Entertainment and SM Brand Marketing form the core revenue base. Shareholders related to the largest holder hold 29.79%, so changes in affiliate volume affect both results and governance.

Quarterly loss history and variable return capacity

In Q4 2025 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 936 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 604 million. Because the return base is 20% of consolidated net income, lower profit reduces the amount returned.

10

Risk factors

Client concentration

A large share of album printing and logistics depends on volume from the SM Entertainment group. Artist activity schedules and album release timing can move quarterly revenue. Whether client diversification shows up in the numbers is the key test.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Q2 2026 revenue rose 48.0% from the prior quarter to KRW 14.3 billion, then fell to KRW 7.63 billion in Q3. When revenue swings, fixed-cost weight can move the operating margin sharply. Whether the 15.9% Q2 2026 margin can be sustained remains an open question.

Return policy tied to earnings

Because the return base is 20% of consolidated net income, lower profit shrinks the return amount. Share buybacks have been explained as aimed at raising shareholder value and stabilizing the share price, so whether the timing of execution matches that aim needs checking.

12

Overall view

SM Life Design is a production infrastructure company centered on album printing and logistics for the SM Entertainment group.

Its 2025 annual revenue of KRW 53.3 billion, operating profit of KRW 4.58 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 5.5 billion were each the highest of 2022 to 2025. The operating margin rose from 5.0% in 2022 to 8.6% in 2025, a sign of improving profitability.

By contrast, Q3 2026 revenue fell to KRW 7.63 billion and operating profit to KRW 421 million, widening quarterly swings. Shareholder returns are running under a 20% of consolidated net income policy for 2025 to 2027, with trust agreements and cancellations in 2026.

Bull-side factors are margin improvement, execution of the return policy and a low debt ratio, while bear-side factors are the Q3 drop, client concentration and the record of quarterly losses. The weight of each side will be clearer from the quarterly trend in upcoming periodic reports.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results in the 2026 annual report, execution of the treasury share trust through March 17, 2027, and the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
