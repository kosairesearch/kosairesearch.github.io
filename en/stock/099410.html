02

Business structure

Dongbang Ship Machinery is a marine equipment maker specializing in ship piping, listed on KOSDAQ in November 2009. Its business has three segments: PIPE SPOOL (carbon and stainless steel pipe assemblies), environmental equipment including STP (sewage treatment plants), and coating (PIPE and steel outfitting).

In the first half of 2026, PIPE SPOOL generated about KRW 15.0 billion, or 72.1% of revenue of about KRW 20.9 billion; environmental equipment about KRW 4.85 billion (23.2%); and coating about KRW 973 million (4.7%), per a filing reported on August 14, 2026.

On production, PIPE SPOOL ran at 80% utilization, producing 63,395 units against 79,200 units of capacity, while environmental equipment produced 153 vessels against capacity of 235, or 65%.

The environmental equipment business expanded in 2024, when the company acquired the STP business unit of the Nongsan plant from its largest shareholder for KRW 12 billion. That year it launched a water-treatment unit and set a 2030 revenue target of KRW 100 billion.

A 2024 Korea IR Council report said the acquired STP business holds over 90% of the domestic market and 30% of the global market. Major customers include domestic shipbuilders such as HD Hyundai Mipo and K Shipbuilding, group affiliates and engine-parts makers, according to a 2024 securities report.

In Q3 2024, the revenue mix was 82.5% piping, 6.2% coating and 11.3% environmental equipment, so the environmental share has since risen to 23.2% in the first half of 2026. Captive volume from the controlling group was reported at under 30% of revenue in 2024.

The company is described as having one of the largest marine piping capacities in Korea, with multiple production bases.

04

Earnings analysis

On the filed annual figures, revenue rose from KRW 24.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 27.3 billion in 2023 (up 9.8%), KRW 35.7 billion in 2024 (up 30.8%) and KRW 41.9 billion in 2025 (up 17.3%). Revenue has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.41 billion in 2023 (up 144.0%), KRW 4.08 billion in 2024 (up 19.6%) and KRW 9.29 billion in 2025 (up 127.6%). The company attributed the 2025 increase to higher sales and price increases, per its February 27, 2026 filing.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 1.28 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.95 billion in 2025, and 2025 net income, including non-controlling interests, was also KRW 7.95 billion. Operating margin was 5.6% in 2022, 12.5% in 2023, 11.4% in 2024 and 22.2% in 2025, so 2024 was a temporary dip.

In 2024, revenue grew 30.8% but margin slipped, which a 2025 securities report attributed to automation investment to raise productivity limiting profitability gains. The 2025 margin rebound is consistent with the price increases the company cited.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 49.8 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 17.1 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio was 46.3% in 2022, 30.7% in 2023, 43.7% in 2024 and 34.3% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was negative in 2022 at -KRW 396 million, then KRW 5.08 billion in 2023, KRW 4.96 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.1 billion in 2025, which exceeded 2025 net income.

Quarterly, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 11.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 3.03 billion, and Q4 2025 rose to KRW 13 billion and KRW 5.15 billion, up 13.3% and 69.9% quarter on quarter. Q1 2026 revenue fell to KRW 9.2 billion and operating profit to KRW 1.55 billion, down 29.4% and 69.8%.

Q2 2026 recovered to KRW 11.7 billion in revenue and KRW 3.9 billion in operating profit, up 151.1% quarter on quarter. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 9.59 billion, down 17.7% quarter on quarter and 16.6% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 1.93 billion, down 50.5% and 36.1%.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 9.92 billion. AP Shinmun reported on August 20, 2026 that Q2 2026 operating profit rose 463.4% year on year.

05

Industry analysis

The company's core end market is Korean shipbuilding. Domestic shipbuilders won 37 of the 46 global gas carrier orders placed from January to May 2026, about 80% share, according to AP Shinmun on August 20, 2026.

A shipbuilding industry official noted a lag between orders and equipment input, so growth in mid- to long-term results may increase as high-value vessel backlogs move into construction.

A 2024 Korea IR Council report described the shipbuilding cycle as entering a super-cycle, with rising equipment volumes and higher selling prices. In cycle terms, the reporting points to a phase where orders have already risen and construction and equipment deliveries are picking up.

Earlier downturns hit equipment makers hard, and a 2022 securities report said recovery benefits had concentrated on those suppliers as orders rose.

On positioning, Dongbang Ship Machinery supplies shipbuilders with piping and is one of the largest domestic capacity holders in ship piping, while its STP business was cited in a 2024 report as holding over 90% domestic share.

Its 2026 first-half utilization was 80% in PIPE SPOOL and 65% in environmental equipment, which means the piping segment runs at a relatively high level and further volume could be constrained by capacity, while environmental equipment has room to add output.

Sector conditions change with order flow and build schedules, so their effect on quarterly results comes with a lag.

Outlook

The 2030 revenue target of KRW 100 billion was set in 2024, when the company launched its water-treatment unit, and it depends on expanding environmental equipment. The environmental share reached 23.2% of first-half 2026 revenue, which is one interim marker of that path.

In March 2025, Kiwoom Securities projected 2025 revenue of KRW 47 billion and operating profit of KRW 9.4 billion, while the filed 2025 figures were KRW 41.9 billion and KRW 9.29 billion, a sizable revenue gap versus the forecast.

At the March 31, 2026 shareholder meeting, all six agenda items passed, including articles changes and the election of an inside director, and Jung Dae-jin was appointed CEO the same day. The first full-year results under the new management will appear in the 2026 annual report.

Capacity-wise, first-half 2026 utilization stood at 80% for PIPE SPOOL and 65% for environmental equipment. As orders grow, the piping segment may need to decide on capacity additions, while the environmental segment can raise output within existing lines.

Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.9 billion compared with KRW 1.55 billion in Q1 and KRW 1.93 billion in Q3, so whether the drivers behind Q2 repeat is the key point for upcoming quarters. Whether the margin gains that followed the 2024 automation investment persist is also a question for the next periodic reports.

Bull factors

The company cited higher sales and price increases for the 2025 profit gain. Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.9 billion was above Q1 at KRW 1.55 billion and Q3 at KRW 1.93 billion, so quarterly results vary but the margin level is higher than before.

Room to expand the environmental equipment business

Bear factors

Large swings in 2026 quarterly profit

Operating profit was KRW 1.55 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 1.93 billion in Q3, far below Q2's KRW 3.9 billion. Q3 operating profit fell 50.5% quarter on quarter and 36.1% year on year.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 52.9% year on year to KRW 1.37 billion in Q3, so quarterly profit has not built up in one direction.

Signs of slowing revenue growth

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 9.59 billion fell 17.7% quarter on quarter and 16.6% year on year. Compared with Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 13 billion, the last two quarters show weaker sales momentum.

Revenue rose 17.3% in 2025, but 2026 quarterly sales have not kept that pace, so whether revenue recovers is the next point to watch.

Margin pressure seen after past automation spending

In 2024, revenue rose 30.8% but operating margin fell from 12.5% to 11.4%. A 2025 securities report said automation investment to raise productivity had limited profitability gains.

If equipment spending hits earnings first, margins can be pressured again, so whether the same pattern repeats after the 2025 rebound is an open question.

Risk factors

Dependence on shipbuilding cycle and orders

Revenue depends on domestic shipbuilders' orders and build schedules. PIPE SPOOL accounted for 72.1% of first-half 2026 revenue, so the piping segment carries high weight. If orders slow or construction is delayed, piping orders could also be delayed. Because orders and equipment input are separated by a lag, changes in the cycle may show up in results late.

Quarterly profit volatility and margin management

Quarterly operating profit ranged from KRW 1.55 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 3.9 billion in Q2 and KRW 1.93 billion in Q3, as quarterly revenue moved between KRW 9.2 billion and KRW 11.7 billion. Margins also fell in 2024 during automation investment. Whether margins hold steady under varying cost and utilization conditions is a central risk factor.

Governance and leadership change

On a March 31, 2026 filing, Jung Dae-jin was appointed CEO after the term of Kim Sung-wook expired. The board has four directors, one of whom is an outside director, a 25% ratio, and the audit function is one part-time auditor.

The largest shareholder is a group affiliate, and captive volume from the group was reported below 30% in 2024. Group dependence and the new management setup are items to monitor.

Overall view

Dongbang Ship Machinery grew revenue from KRW 24.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 41.9 billion in 2025, and operating profit from KRW 1.4 billion to KRW 9.29 billion. Operating margin rose from 5.6% to 22.2%, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell to 34.3% in 2025 from 46.3% in 2022.
