02

Business structure

Automobile & PCB was founded in 1981 and listed in 1988 as a printed circuit board (PCB) maker. PCB has been the core business since the company's founding, accounting for 83% of sales in Q2 2026, with automotive parts at 14% and electronics manufacturing services (EMS) at 3%.

The company entered automotive seat cover manufacturing in 2022 by acquiring a seat business, and a consolidated subsidiary, Scoa, now handles auto parts production. Major PCB customers include Hyundai Motor Group affiliates, which together account for more than 80% of sales.

Products fall into automotive electronics components and industrial products for information and communications and networking. The company has pursued a higher sales share of multilayer products with ten or more layers. As of Q1 2025, exports and domestic sales made up 42% and 58% of PCB sales.

The market has pointed to the expansion of electric vehicles and vehicle electrification as a tailwind. Among PCB peers, Simmtech and Daeduck Electronics are common comparison points, and KRW 117.5 billion in 2025 sales places the company among the smaller players.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 107.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 140.9 billion in 2023 (up 31.1% year on year), fell to KRW 113.5 billion in 2024 (down 19.5%), and reached KRW 117.5 billion in 2025 (up 3.5%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 10.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.82 billion in 2023, then widened to KRW 5.99 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.29 billion in 2025. The operating margin was -9.9% in 2022, -1.3% in 2023, -5.3% in 2024 and -6.2% in 2025.

Attributable net loss was KRW 30.4 billion in 2022, KRW 9.85 billion in 2023, KRW 10.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.1 billion in 2025, while the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 30.4 billion, KRW 10 billion, KRW 10.3 billion and KRW 14.2 billion over the same years.

For the 2025 widening, the company cited a surge in the exchange rate that raised the cost of raw and subsidiary materials and lifted the cost ratio. Operating cash flow was negative KRW 12.2 billion in 2022, positive KRW 5.72 billion in 2023, negative KRW 3.6 billion in 2024 and negative KRW 12 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q4 2025 sales of KRW 24.4 billion, down 21.0% from the prior quarter, marked the low, followed by increases to KRW 27.8 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 33.6 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 36.4 billion in Q3 2026.

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 2.22 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 1.11 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 236 million in Q2 2026, and Q3 2026 posted operating profit of KRW 883 million.

Attributable net income moved from a loss of KRW 6.69 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 2.72 billion in Q1 2026 to income of KRW 328 million in Q2 2026 and KRW 2.42 billion in Q3 2026. The sum of attributable net losses over the last four quarters is KRW 6.67 billion, which remains a loss.

05

Industry analysis

Automobile & PCB's main demand comes from automotive electronics and information and communications, so semiconductor industry indicators reach the company only indirectly. A May 2026 sector report projected that AI server shipments would rise 35.9% year on year to 2.941 million units in 2026.

The same report said growing AI accelerator demand is lifting HBM and CoWoS demand, and that memory makers would step up HBM capacity investment. An August 2026 report said ASML had already contracted most of its 2027 production, and that equipment shortages were spreading into advanced packaging.

These trends show demand strength in semiconductor equipment and back-end processes, but they are not a direct measure of the company's PCB sales. In automotive electronics, electric vehicles and the wider electrification of vehicles are cited as tailwinds.

In information and communications, expanding demand for multilayer PCBs is presented as a strategic direction, based on data as of Q1 2025. Compared with PCB peers such as Simmtech and Daeduck Electronics, the company's higher share of automotive electronics customers is cited as its distinguishing feature.

06

Outlook

The outlook starts from reported results and the quarterly trend. Sales reached KRW 36.4 billion in Q3 2026 after three consecutive quarterly increases, and operating profit turned to KRW 883 million.

The key question is whether this profit is a single-quarter event or whether the annual operating margin can move out of negative territory. In its Q1 2025 materials, the company said it would strengthen technology and sales capabilities to grow revenue in artificial intelligence, robotics and life sciences.

At the same time, expanding new businesses was presented as a challenge alongside weak results. The revenue scale of these new businesses cannot be separated from reported results, so they should be read apart from core PCB performance.

The seat business is reflected in results through a consolidated subsidiary, so it forms part of consolidated sales. The next checkpoint is the Q4 2026 operating profit and full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report. The 2027 Q1 quarterly report will then show whether the profit run continues. Operating cash flow direction and changes in the debt ratio should be read alongside these figures.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly profit turn

Attributable net income turned positive in Q2 2026 at KRW 328 million, and operating profit turned positive in Q3 2026 at KRW 883 million. The operating loss had narrowed from KRW 2.22 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 1.11 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 236 million in Q2 2026 before the Q3 profit.

Attributable net income rose 637.9% from the prior quarter in Q3. Whether this run continues is the premise of the bullish case.

Multilayer automotive PCB strategy

The PCB business supplies automotive electronics and industrial products for information and communications and networking, and the company is pushing to widen the sales share of multilayer products.

Q3 2026 sales of KRW 36.4 billion rose 17.9% from a year earlier, and quarterly sales growth has continued for three consecutive quarters. Expanding vehicle electrification is cited as the base of demand for automotive PCBs. The bullish argument starts from the premise that the recovery drivers lie in the core PCB business.

Low price relative to net assets

The current price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x. A share price low relative to book value can be cited as a bullish point on asset grounds. However, total liabilities of KRW 78.7 billion exceed total equity of KRW 20.5 billion, so the debt structure must be read alongside this ratio. The bullish point and the financial risk both come from the same balance sheet.

09

Bear factors

Four straight years of losses

Losses continued in every year from 2022 to 2025. The operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.82 billion in 2023 but widened again to KRW 7.29 billion in 2025. For 2025, the company cited a higher exchange rate that raised raw and subsidiary material costs and lifted the cost ratio.

The 2025 attributable net loss of KRW 14.1 billion was larger than the KRW 9.85 billion loss in 2023. The bearish case begins with the fact that an annual profit has not yet been recorded.

Balance sheet burden

The debt-to-equity ratio at end-2025 was 384.4%, higher than 255.4% in 2022 and 261.5% in 2024. Total liabilities of KRW 78.7 billion sit against total equity of KRW 20.5 billion. Operating cash flow was negative KRW 3.6 billion in 2024 and negative KRW 12 billion in 2025, two consecutive negative years.

Even if profit turns positive, the financial burden eases only when cash generation and debt reduction are confirmed together.

Quarterly earnings volatility

The attributable net loss of KRW 6.69 billion in Q4 2025 was the largest among the last five quarters. The attributable net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 6.67 billion, still a loss. The Q3 2026 profit is a single quarter, and quarterly results have been volatile. Until profit persists beyond one quarter, the bearish argument remains relevant.

10

Risk factors

FX and cost risk

Sharp currency moves and higher raw and subsidiary material costs were cited as main causes of the 2025 operating loss widening. If material costs cannot be passed through to selling prices, the cost ratio could rise again and push operating results back into a loss.

Whether the Q3 2026 profit came from cost stability, pricing or currency effects is a point to check in the cost structure disclosures of the annual report.

Customer concentration risk

Major PCB customers, including Hyundai Mobis and Hyundai KEFICO, are cited as accounting for more than 80% of sales. Volume changes at a single customer or revisions to vehicle production plans can pass through to sales quickly.

With exports and domestic sales at 42% and 58% of PCB sales as of Q1 2025, domestic vehicle demand is a major driver of earnings swings.

Financial structure and liquidity risk

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 78.7 billion, more than three times total equity of KRW 20.5 billion. Negative operating cash flow in both 2024 and 2025 raises the importance of borrowing and working capital management.

If the debt ratio, which rose from 255.4% in 2022 to 384.4% in 2025, keeps climbing, room for investment and expansion could be constrained.

Overall view

Automobile & PCB recorded operating losses and attributable net losses in every year from 2022 to 2025, and its debt-to-equity ratio stood at 384.4% at end-2025. Quarterly trends improved in 2026.

Sales rose for three consecutive quarters from the KRW 24.4 billion low in Q4 2025 to KRW 36.4 billion in Q3 2026, and operating profit turned positive at KRW 883 million in Q3 2026.

Attributable net income was also positive in Q2 2026 at KRW 328 million and in Q3 2026 at KRW 2.42 billion, two consecutive profitable quarters. However, attributable net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 6.67 billion, still a loss, and operating cash flow in 2025 was negative KRW 12 billion.

The automotive multilayer PCB strategy and vehicle electrification demand are cited as the business foundation, but cost ratios, currency swings and customer concentration remain variables at the same time.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and whether profitability persists in the Q1 2027 quarterly report. This information is provided for reference only.
