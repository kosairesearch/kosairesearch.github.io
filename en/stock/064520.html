02

Business structure

TechL is a memory semiconductor back-end (packaging and test) manufacturer, and its core business is the Storage SIP operation. In the second quarter of 2026, the SIP segment accounted for 92% of revenue and real estate leasing for 8%.

Its main product is semiconductor sensor memory cards, and in 2025 domestic sales made up 84.50% of revenue against 15.50% from exports. Production is based at the headquarters in Hwaseong, Gyeonggi Province and at a site in Vietnam.

Annual capacity is about 219.75 million units at the headquarters (wire bonding basis) and about 20.79 million units in Vietnam. Its Vietnam subsidiary, TECHL VINA CO., LTD., was established in April 2024 and makes semiconductors and memory cards.

Main raw materials are NAND flash chips, PCBs and controllers, with PCBs accounting for 55.51% of first-quarter 2026 raw material purchases. Samsung Electronics is named as a major customer. R&D spending in 2025 was KRW 924 million, or 5.09% of revenue, and headcount at the end of March 2026 was 69. Sector peers classified under semiconductors include Daeduck Electronics, Simmtech and HANMI Semiconductor.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue on the disclosed basis was KRW 20.7 billion in 2022, KRW 21.9 billion in 2023 (up 6.2%), KRW 25.3 billion in 2024 (up 15.4%) and KRW 18.1 billion in 2025 (down 28.3%). Of the 2022 to 2025 period, 2024 was the largest year for revenue and 2025 the smallest.

For the 2025 decline, the company cited in a March 9, 2026 filing the impact of tight supply of B-grade NAND flash and early stabilization costs at its Vietnam subsidiary.

The operating loss was KRW 3.81 billion in 2022, KRW 4.54 billion in 2023 (wider), KRW 375 million in 2024 (narrower) and KRW 5.6 billion in 2025 (wider), a loss in each of the four years from 2022 to 2025. Operating margin was -18.4%, -20.7%, -1.5% and -30.9% for 2022 through 2025, with 2024 the shallowest.

Controlling-shareholder net income swung to a profit of KRW 5.5 billion in 2023, then turned to a loss of KRW 472 million in 2024 and widened to a loss of KRW 4.67 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same levels for those years.

Operating cash flow declined from KRW 9.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.76 billion in 2023 and KRW 3.29 billion in 2024, and was -KRW 413 million in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 4.5 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 4.78 billion in Q4 2025, then fell to KRW 2.67 billion, KRW 1.77 billion and KRW 1.88 billion in the first three quarters of 2026.

Q3 2026 revenue was down 58.1% year on year, and the operating loss of KRW 2.35 billion extended a run of five consecutive loss-making quarters from Q3 2025.

In Q2 2026 the controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 178 million turned positive, but Q3 2026 returned to a net loss of KRW 3.73 billion, larger than the operating loss, so non-operating and tax items need checking. The controlling-shareholder net loss for the last four quarters totaled KRW 5.28 billion.

Industry analysis

Demand from downstream customers moves with the memory chip cycle and NAND flash supply. A 2024 press report said the semiconductor market had entered a full recovery phase.

However, the company's 2026 filing pointed to tight supply of B-grade NAND, which suggests grade-specific supply gaps affected revenue apart from the broader cycle.

The position in the cycle can be read from the disclosed revenue trend: revenue fell from KRW 25.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 18.1 billion in 2025, and quarterly revenue in Q3 2026 was KRW 1.88 billion.

Competitively, TechL's business is concentrated in memory cards and Storage SIP, so its operations differ from equipment-listed peers such as HANMI Semiconductor and EO Technics. Other semiconductor-sector listed companies include Daeduck Electronics, Simmtech, Wonik IPS and DB HiTek.

Samsung Electronics is named as a major customer, so customer concentration remains a variable. In production, a 2024 press report described a strategy of R&D in Korea and mass production lines in Vietnam. As overseas production grows, stabilization at the Vietnam subsidiary becomes a key driver of cost and yield.

Vulnerability in supply and cost structure

Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

The business is concentrated in the SIP segment, which accounts for 92% of revenue, and in memory cards. Samsung Electronics is named as a major customer, so changes in its orders can pass straight through to revenue. With domestic sales at 84.50%, dependence on a small set of customers and domestic demand is high.

Raw material supply and costs

Main raw materials are NAND flash chips, PCBs and controllers, and PCBs were 55.51% of first-quarter 2026 purchases. Because B-grade NAND supply was cited as a cause of lower sales, raw material sourcing terms feed directly into results. When cost of sales is high, room to adjust selling prices can narrow.

Stabilization of the Vietnam subsidiary

The Vietnam subsidiary, TECHL VINA, was established in April 2024, and the company has disclosed early stabilization costs as a drag on results. The same costs were cited for the 2025 decline in sales and net income. As overseas production grows, swings in yield and utilization feed through to results.
