Report overview

Legacy Memory Upcycle Meets an Accounting Sanction

Fidelix posted a third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 47.7 billion, but on October 28, 2026 the Securities and Futures Commission sanctioned it over false sales booked in 2018-2022, so earnings durability and accounting credibility are now both in question.

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Fidelix is a fabless memory chip company founded in 1990 and listed on KOSDAQ in 1997; it designs and sells DRAM, flash memory and multi-chip packages (MCP). In its filings the company describes itself as a fabless company and lists its supply method as outsourced production.

A current product mix was not available; the Korea IR Service research center reported, on a cumulative third-quarter 2023 basis, MCP at 70.0%, DRAM at 16.0%, NAND at 9.0% and NOR Flash at 5.0%. As of the second quarter of 2026, practically all revenue came from semiconductors.

Etoday reported that the company sells its own designs alongside memory sourced externally from Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. Exports account for about 70% of sales, supplied mainly to the United States, Europe, China and Japan.

Counterparties in recent supply contracts are IoT communications module makers and a chip distributor, pointing to communications, IoT and industrial uses. The largest shareholder changed to Dosilicon Co., Ltd. in June 2015.

The company is pursuing in-house LPDDR4 design as a new business as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix wind down LPDDR4 output.

The legacy segment that large producers are vacating while they focus on HBM and other high-value products is the core of the competitive picture, and Fidelix is more a price taker than the large suppliers.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 72.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 52.8 billion in 2023 (down 27.3%) and KRW 44.8 billion in 2024 (down 15.1%), then rose to KRW 53.6 billion in 2025 (up 19.6%); among 2022-2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 6.28 billion in 2022 and KRW 710 million in 2023 (down 88.7%), turned to an operating loss of KRW 4.53 billion in 2024, and the loss continued but narrowed to KRW 2.07 billion in 2025. Operating margin was 8.7% in 2022, 1.3% in 2023, -10.1% in 2024 and -3.9% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 8.25 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.06 billion in 2023, then became a net loss of KRW 2.21 billion in 2024 and a widening net loss of KRW 2.96 billion in 2025, two consecutive loss years in 2024-2025; net income including non-controlling interests is the same amount.

Operating cash flow was negative in both 2024 (-KRW 1.55 billion) and 2025 (-KRW 2.43 billion), and the debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 28.2% (total equity of KRW 44.8 billion and total liabilities of KRW 12.6 billion).

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 12.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 18.2 billion in the fourth quarter (up 45.5%), KRW 21.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 33.4 billion in the second quarter and KRW 84.5 billion in the third quarter (up 152.8% from the prior quarter and 574.0% from a year earlier), four straight quarterly increases.

After an operating loss of KRW 894 million in the third quarter of 2025, operating profit turned positive at KRW 1.04 billion in the fourth quarter, then rose to KRW 4.33 billion, KRW 9.61 billion and KRW 47.7 billion (up 396.5% from the prior quarter) in the first to third quarters of 2026, three straight increases, and third-quarter operating profit exceeded half of revenue.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a net loss of KRW 581 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.25 billion, KRW 5.25 billion, KRW 9.53 billion and KRW 32.3 billion, three straight increases, with a four-quarter total of KRW 48.3 billion.

The company said price and margin improvement from the supply-demand imbalance has continued since its fourth-quarter 2025 return to profit. No breakdown of the third-quarter jump into price, volume and inventory effects was found, so the composition of profit should be checked in the quarterly report notes.

The period flagged by the regulator (2018-2022) includes 2022, so whether the 2022 comparison figures are restated is also something to check in filings.

05

Industry analysis

The key to supply and demand is the production reshuffle at the large memory makers. Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and others cut the share of legacy lines to secure HBM and other next-generation capacity, and shrinking supply is pushing prices up.

According to Seoul Economic Daily, the DDR4 8Gb fixed transaction price rose 15.7% from the prior month to 8.1 dollars in November 2025, topping 8 dollars for the first time since September 2018.

TrendForce estimated that general-purpose DRAM contract prices rose 93-98% from the prior quarter in the first quarter of 2026 and would rise a further 58-63% in the second quarter.

DDR4 spot prices resumed rising after a March-May 2026 correction, reaching 81.2 dollars per 16GB on July 22, 2026, and an 8.5-fold rise in six months spread wariness about a short-term spike. Views on duration differ. Counterpoint Research expected memory price increases to continue into the first half of 2027.

BISI, by contrast, analyzed that DRAM prices could peak in mid-to-late 2026 and ease partially only in the second half of 2027. Fidelix benefits from this pricing environment but is not a share-holding price leader, so industry swings flow directly into its gross margin and selling prices.

Outlook

No annual earnings guidance figures from the company were found; what is confirmed is its supply-demand view and contract disclosures. The company believes the current cycle is likely to last through 2027 and 2028.

On July 22, 2026 it disclosed a KRW 6.64 billion (USD 4.5 million) contract with Semtech, and on September 10, 2026 a further Semtech contract worth KRW 6.0255 billion (USD 4.5 million). That contract carries 100% prepayment and non-cancellable terms.

On September 29, 2026 it disclosed a KRW 19.244 billion contract with Alliance Memory, equal to 35.8% of 2025 revenue. The contract signed on October 13, 2026 with Telit Communications Cyprus is worth KRW 6.09125 billion.

These amounts are order disclosures; the timing and size of revenue recognition depend on delivery schedules. On new products, the company is designing NPU-based Wide-I/O DRAM with KAIST in a four-year project and said it aims to finish design in the second half and start first prototypes in the third quarter.

Whether the prototype stage has been reached has not been confirmed. In a report dated June 30, 2026, Shinhan Investment Corp. assessed that benefits from the legacy memory supply shortage were likely to flow into Fidelix's earnings.

Bull factors

Legacy supply gap and pricing

Samsung Electronics and SK hynix have trimmed legacy lines, tightening supply. Fidelix is designing its own LPDDR4 to target that gap. Shinhan Investment Corp., in a report dated June 30, 2026, assessed that the benefit was likely to translate into earnings improvement. If the price-increase phase continues, the unit-price effect can keep flowing into quarterly results.

Operating leverage and earnings visibility

Operating profit rose for three straight quarters to KRW 4.33 billion, KRW 9.61 billion and KRW 47.7 billion in the first to third quarters of 2026, and third-quarter operating profit exceeded half of revenue. As a fabless company with no plant of its own, its fixed-cost burden is relatively light when sales rise. Operating margin improved sharply from -10.1% in 2024 on this business metric.

Prepaid contracts and balance sheet

The September 2026 Semtech contract and the October 2026 Telit contract include prepayment terms, which eases the up-front cost of outsourced production. The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was a low 28.2%, and the KRW 44.8 billion of total equity is entirely controlling-shareholder equity.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 48.3 billion over the last four quarters exceeds that equity base, adding to financial strength.

09

Bear factors

Debate over a cycle peak

DDR4 spot prices corrected in March-May 2026, and an 8.5-fold rise in six months raised wariness about a short-term spike. BISI analyzed that DRAM prices could pass their peak in mid-to-late 2026.

The effect of large producers' capacity additions is expected to reach the market in 2027, so whether third-quarter-level profit can be sustained is uncertain.

Accounting credibility and follow-up

The regulator put the total false bookings in 2018-2022 at KRW 15.069 billion and found that the external audit was obstructed. The company and its former chief executive, among others, were referred to prosecutors, and the fine has not been decided. The conduct dates from earlier years, but three years of designated auditors may raise audit burden and cost.

Reliance on outsourced sourcing and production

Fidelix resells memory sourced externally from Samsung Electronics and SK hynix and outsources production. Finance Today noted that outsourced production cost is a variable for profit contribution. A change in memory supply priority or procurement price could shake margins.

10

Risk factors

Regulatory and legal

The measures voted by the Securities and Futures Commission on October 28, 2026 include three years of auditor designation, a dismissal-equivalent recommendation for the former chief executive and another person, and referral to prosecutors.

The fine for the company and related persons is to be decided later by the Financial Services Commission. The outcome of any investigation and the size of the fine are unknown, and the effect on previously filed financial statements has not been confirmed.

Industry and pricing

TrendForce estimated that general-purpose DRAM contract prices rose 93-98% in the first quarter of 2026. After such a surge, prices can correct if capacity additions and weaker demand coincide. Fidelix's profit is sensitive to unit price, so after a peak the margin decline could be larger than the revenue decline.

Financial and cash flow

Operating cash flow was an outflow of KRW 1.55 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.43 billion in 2025. Working-capital burden can grow as purchases of externally sourced memory increase. Short-term borrowing increase decisions were disclosed on September 30 and October 6, 2026. The amounts and purposes were not confirmed in the sources reviewed, so the original filings should be checked.

Overall view

Fidelix swung back to profit in the fourth quarter of 2025 after two straight loss years in 2024-2025, and its operating profit reached KRW 47.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Behind this are the legacy memory supply cuts by Samsung Electronics and SK hynix and rising prices, and the company expects the cycle to last likely through 2027-2028. Institutions such as BISI, by contrast, point to possible easing after a price peak, so views differ.

Third-quarter revenue exceeded full-year 2025 revenue, but information on the composition of profit is limited. The October 28, 2026 sanction concerns accounting problems from 2018-2022, with the fine and the investigation outcome still pending.

The points to check are fourth-quarter DRAM prices, contract fulfillment and cash flow, and the follow-up to the sanction. This report is provided for information only, and investment decisions are left to the reader.
