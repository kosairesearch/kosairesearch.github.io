02

Business structure

SANGSANGIN INVESTMENT & SECURITIES holds licenses covering investment trading and brokerage as well as trust, investment advisory and discretionary investment management.

Its operations are split into four business divisions, Retail, Wholesale, Investment Banking (IB) and Asset Management, plus a headquarters management unit.

The Retail division handles brokerage and intermediation, the Wholesale division runs trading, and the IB division covers M&A advisory and corporate finance such as mezzanine investment and rights offerings.

On a cumulative net profit basis through September 2026, as announced by the company, IB contributed KRW 8.7 billion, Wholesale KRW 5.3 billion, Asset Management KRW 7.5 billion and Retail KRW 3.2 billion, while the headquarters unit remains loss-making.

In the first quarter of 2026 the IB division's operating profit was KRW 3.3 billion, or 52% of total operating profit. A 2023 press report said the IB business, which had relied on the sales network of a group savings bank, was disrupted by a Financial Services Commission order to sell the group savings bank stake.

The same report described the retail network as four branches including the head office. Stock brokerage fee income rose 68.1% from KRW 7.2 billion in 2024 to KRW 12.1 billion in 2025, passing KRW 10 billion for the first time.

In capital bond underwriting, the company ranked eighth in the first nine months of 2026 with KRW 2.03 trillion and a 4.43% market share.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 51.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 182.2 billion in 2023 (up 256.5% year on year) and to KRW 322.7 billion in 2024 (up 77.1%), making 2024 the largest year in the 2022–2025 period. Revenue fell to KRW 181.7 billion in 2025, down 43.7%.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 847 million in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 962 million in 2023, a turnaround to profit, before an operating loss of KRW 49.7 billion in 2024 turned the year into a loss.

The 2025 operating loss of KRW 9.09 billion kept the company in the red but narrowed the gap, and losses were recorded in two consecutive years, 2024 and 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 3.68 billion in 2022 to KRW 487 million in 2023 (down 86.8%), then to a net loss of KRW 47.3 billion in 2024, and a net loss of KRW 5.86 billion in 2025. Net profit including non-controlling interests carries the same annual values in the filings.

The operating margin was -1.7% in 2022, 0.5% in 2023, -15.4% in 2024 and -5.0% in 2025, with 2024 the lowest in the period.

Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 2.82 billion in the third quarter of 2025 was followed by an operating loss of KRW 1.93 billion in the fourth quarter, when revenue fell 3.6% quarter on quarter to KRW 45.3 billion.

Revenue recovered to KRW 58.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 28.3%) with operating profit of KRW 6.27 billion, then operating profit fell 65.3% to KRW 2.18 billion in the second quarter.

The third quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 59.4 billion, operating profit of KRW 3.47 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.7 billion. Over the last four quarters, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 13.1 billion.

In company-announced cumulative figures, operating profit for the first nine months of 2026 was KRW 12.0 billion and net profit KRW 14.8 billion, turning from an operating loss of KRW 7.2 billion and a net loss of KRW 4.2 billion in the same period of 2025.

The company also said cumulative bad-debt provisions fell 93.2% year on year, which it linked to earlier clean-up of impaired assets.

05

Industry analysis

Securities stocks were reported in early 2026 to be rising as the government's corporate value-up program coincided with expectations of interest rate cuts. Press coverage of the company's third-quarter 2026 results, however, framed the earnings rebound as having come in a falling market.

A 2023 press report, citing a credit rating agency, put the company's share of operating net revenue at the end of 2023 at 0.2% in brokerage, 0.3% in IB and 1.3% in asset management.

A share of that size leaves a sizable scale gap against large securities firms such as KIWOOM Securities, Samsung Securities and NH Investment & Securities.

In the first nine months of 2026 the company ranked eighth in capital bond underwriting with a 4.43% share, and its card bond underwriting share of 4.10% lifted it to eighth from twelfth in the first half.

Equity capital markets (ECM) lead-managed deals, excluding block deals, reached KRW 59.0 billion across three deals, placing it in the top ten. Stock brokerage fee income rose 68.1% in 2025 to KRW 12.1 billion.

A securities industry official said that if institutional trading continues to expand, earnings stability could be strengthened further. The company said it will keep a risk management system running to prepare for volatility in foreign interest rates.

06

Outlook

In its announcement of first-half 2026 results, the company set a target of at least KRW 18.0 billion in operating profit for full-year 2026.

Cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 12.0 billion on a company-announced basis, so reaching the target would require roughly KRW 6.0 billion or more in the fourth quarter.

The CEO said the company aims to sustain profitability through the fourth quarter and achieve a full-year return to profit. In the second half, the company plans to expand brokerage for institutional investors to lift corporate trading income.

The proprietary asset management (PI) unit was set up in the first half, and the company expects it to become a stable income source once it reaches full operation. The IB division plans to focus on traditional areas such as mezzanine investment, rights offerings and M&A advisory.

The Wholesale division plans to upgrade its arbitrage trading system and add specialist staff to secure new income sources. Real estate financing arrangement capability was strengthened from late 2025, and the project financing (PF) segment swung to operating profit of KRW 1.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

On the capital side, the net capital ratio was reported to have risen from 278.4% at the end of 2025 to 292.5%. The company's full-year return to profit refers to the 2026 fiscal year as a whole, which will be confirmed through the full-year provisional results and the annual report.
