02

Business structure

Plumb Fast was founded in 1992 and listed on KOSDAQ in January 2002 as a maker of plastic piping materials. Its core products are PB (polybutylene) pipes and PB fittings. The business runs as a single segment producing and selling plastic piping materials, with one subsidiary in China.

In the first quarter 2021 filing, product sales mix was 40.1% PB pipes, 32.1% PB fittings and 27.8% PPF pipes and related items, and the PPF items were sold as purchased goods rather than self-made products.

On the input side, synthetic resin accounted for 38.0% of purchases and brass materials for 9.7%, with synthetic resin serving as the raw material for PB piping. Customers are split between nationwide dealers and large domestic construction companies. The company's products are used mainly at construction sites.

Raw-material supply is concentrated among only three suppliers worldwide, which is cited as a high barrier to entry. By industry code the company falls under chemicals, but its substance is closer to plastic pipe manufacturing.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed basis, revenue went from KRW 30.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 29.5 billion in 2023 (down 2.5% year on year), KRW 29.7 billion in 2024 (up 0.6%) and KRW 25.2 billion in 2025 (down 14.9%). Revenue was largest in 2022 and smallest in 2025 across 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 1.53 billion in 2022, KRW 1.44 billion in 2023 (down 5.7%) and KRW 1.62 billion in 2024 (up 12.6%), before a KRW 444 million operating loss in 2025 that marked a swing into the red. Operating margin moved from 5.1% in 2022 and 4.9% in 2023 to 5.5% in 2024 and -1.8% in 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net income rose from KRW 1.32 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.42 billion in 2023 (up 7.0%) and KRW 1.92 billion in 2024 (up 35.4%), then fell to KRW 1.02 billion in 2025 (down 46.7%). 2024 was the largest year for that line.

Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed at KRW 1.32 billion, KRW 1.42 billion, KRW 1.92 billion and KRW 1.02 billion for the same years.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 38 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, total liabilities were KRW 3.7 billion, and the debt ratio was 9.7%. Operating cash flow was KRW 127 million in 2022, KRW 2.57 billion in 2023, KRW 2.01 billion in 2024 and KRW 560 million in 2025.

Quarterly, third-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 6.02 billion with an operating loss of KRW 116 million, and fourth-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 5.92 billion (down 1.6% quarter on quarter) with an operating loss of KRW 441 million. First-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 5.18 billion with an operating loss of KRW 331 million.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue rose to KRW 7.23 billion (up 39.7% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 33.98 million marked a swing into the black.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 5.09 billion (down 29.6% quarter on quarter and down 15.4% year on year), with an operating loss of KRW 216 million that widened the loss from a year earlier.

Controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 620 million, and the second quarter of 2026 is the only profitable quarter in that span. The key point in the disclosed figures is that profitability deteriorated while revenue declined.

05

Industry analysis

Plumb Fast's end demand depends on new housing starts and construction activity. Historically, its piping was used mainly for heating and hot-and-cold water supply in newly started housing, which made it sensitive to the construction cycle (per a 2017 article).

Because heating and water-supply piping follows new starts, a decline in new construction weakens the demand base. Synthetic resin is the core raw material, at 38.0% of purchases in the first quarter 2021 filing, with brass materials at 9.7%.

Raw-material supply is concentrated among only three suppliers worldwide, which can be read both as weaker cost bargaining power and as supply stability. In the domestic piping market, delivery track record and certifications such as KS and KC act as barriers to entry.

Chemical-sector peers such as LG Chem, Lotte Chemical and Kumho Petro Chemical are mainly large petrochemical and materials companies, so their products and customer bases do not directly overlap. The key variables for the industry picture are the pace of new housing starts and synthetic resin prices.

Those two variables can be cross-checked against revenue declines and margin changes in the disclosed figures. The third-quarter 2026 revenue decline of 15.4% year on year is not explained by the disclosed figures alone, so company-level results need to be read alongside the industry backdrop.

Outlook

The treasury-share plan calls for cancelling 500,000 shares in total by the end of June 2026, including 287,080 shares disclosed earlier. Cancellation was reflected in the July 16, 2026 listing change, with 510,609 shares removed.

On June 24, 2026, the company decided on a KRW 1 billion treasury-share acquisition trust contract running to June 23, 2027, and the acquired shares are to be cancelled. Remaining treasury shares are to be cancelled in stages over a period that depends on business plans and market conditions.

On October 6, 2026, the largest shareholder changed when CEO Won Jae-hee gifted 500,000 shares to director Won Yeon-kyung, lowering Won Jae-hee's stake from 14.48% to 11.99%. Won Yeon-kyung has been the largest shareholder since October 6, 2026 and sits on the board as an inside director.

The payout ratio for 2025 was reported at 61.32%. On November 18, 2026, an intraday treasury-share purchase request was included in the exchange disclosure list.

The next checkpoints are the annual operating margin and dividend decision in the 2026 annual report, and whether the trust-acquired shares are cancelled by the contract's end.

Risk factors

Construction-cycle exposure

The piping business depends on heating and hot-water demand tied to newly started housing. Fewer new starts can reduce volumes directly. Whether the third-quarter 2026 revenue decline reflects this channel cannot be confirmed from disclosures alone.

Raw-material and supplier concentration

Synthetic resin is the largest item in purchases. With only three suppliers worldwide, the company has limited room to change prices or supply terms. If cost increases cannot be passed on to selling prices, the operating margin could fall further.

Change in control

On October 6, 2026, the largest shareholder changed to director Won Yeon-kyung after CEO Won Jae-hee gifted 500,000 shares. The largest shareholder group's total stake of 33.97% was unchanged. How the shift affects management direction and shareholder-return policy needs to be checked in later disclosures.
