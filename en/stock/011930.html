Report overview

Record Orders, Profit Recovery Still in Progress

Shinsung E&G has built record clean-room orders, but revenue recognition has not yet caught up, so profit has only turned positive on a cumulative basis.

  1. 1

    Cumulative ENG (clean environment) orders through Q3 2026 reached KRW 769.4 billion, a record, and Q3 ENG orders alone rose 113% year on year to KRW 335 billion (provisional release, November 6, 2026).

  2. 2

    Operating profit fell from KRW 21 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.28 billion in 2025, turned positive at KRW 3.06 billion in Q2 2026, and dropped to KRW 610 million in Q3.

  3. 3

    Net income attributable to controlling shareholders has been negative since 2024, and it has posted losses for three straight quarters from Q1 to Q3 2026; the last four quarters total a KRW 7.28 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders.

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Shinsung E&G operates in two segments: clean environment (ENG) and renewable energy (RE).

The ENG segment builds clean-room equipment and handles engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) projects for semiconductor fabs, with key products including fan filter units (FFU), equipment fan filter units (EFU) and outdoor air control units (OAC).

The company says it holds more than 60 percent of the FFU market, a figure it cites from its own description rather than an independent count. In Q1 2026, semiconductors accounted for 75 percent of ENG orders, displays 7 percent, batteries 2 percent and other sectors 15 percent (provisional results, May 2026).

Project references include Samsung Electronics' Pyeongtaek campus, SK hynix's Yongin, Cheongju and Indiana fabs, and the Taylor project in the United States.

By region in the same quarter, domestic revenue was KRW 70.6 billion, the US KRW 67.1 billion, Asia KRW 13 billion and Europe KRW 3.8 billion, so overseas sales exceeded half of the total. The company said U.S. revenue reached a record KRW 169 billion in 2025 (conference call, March 2026).

The RE segment covers solar module manufacturing and EPC work including floating solar; it posted Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 10.2 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.6 billion.

Production of 645W-plus high-output modules began in August 2026 to lower costs, and the company plans to widen its scope into AI-based energy storage system (ESS) construction. In data-center cooling, it is pursuing fan wall units and an all-in-one modular air-conditioning system as new businesses.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 664.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 576.5 billion in 2023 (down 13.2 percent year on year), rose to KRW 582.3 billion in 2024 (up 1.0 percent), and reached KRW 567.5 billion in 2025 (down 2.5 percent). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 21 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.36 billion in 2023 (down 65.0 percent), KRW 4.99 billion in 2024 (down 32.2 percent) and KRW 1.28 billion in 2025 (down 74.3 percent), a decline for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

The operating margin narrowed from 3.2 percent in 2022 to 0.2 percent in 2025.

Since 2023 revenue has stayed in the KRW 560 billion to 580 billion range, while margins fell; the company attributed this mainly to lower demand in renewable energy and higher costs from weaker utilization (annual results release, February 2026).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 34.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.2 billion in 2023 (down 52.4 percent), turned to a net loss of KRW 14.1 billion in 2024, and stayed negative at a KRW 6.99 billion net loss in 2025, a smaller loss than the year before.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a KRW 8.19 billion net loss in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder line. The debt ratio moved from 173.5 percent in 2022 to 142.3 percent in 2023, then rose to 154.8 percent in 2024 and 157.5 percent in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 233.3 billion and total liabilities KRW 367.4 billion. Quarterly, operating profit rose to KRW 2.91 billion in Q4 2025 from KRW 250 million in Q3, then swung to an operating loss of KRW 2.23 billion in Q1 2026.

Q2 2026 revenue reached KRW 201.8 billion, up 31.3 percent from the prior quarter and the highest of the five quarters, with operating profit of KRW 3.06 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 158.4 billion (down 21.5 percent quarter on quarter and down 0.1 percent year on year), and operating profit was KRW 610 million (down 80.1 percent quarter on quarter). The controlling-shareholder net loss widened to KRW 5.97 billion in Q3 from KRW 1.31 billion in Q2.

The company described Q3 as a pause ahead of revenue recognition on large projects (provisional release, November 6, 2026).

05

Industry analysis

Semiconductor capital spending is lifting clean-room orders in 2026 as demand for AI chips and high-bandwidth memory (HBM) expands. The company said its Q1 2026 orders rose about 203 percent year on year (provisional results, May 2026).

It expects a favorable chip-industry cycle to continue through 2028, citing AI expansion (conference call, March 2026). Priority support for domestic chipmakers and an improving global market environment are also factors the company points to.

In 2025, customers changed or delayed investment plans, which raised fixed-cost burdens and was cited as a reason for weaker profitability. On competition, the figure of more than 60 percent FFU share comes from the company's own description and has not been cross-checked against an external count.

Demand is broadening from chipmakers to packaging and materials companies, pharmaceutical and bio clean rooms, and data-center cooling. The renewable energy market faced weaker domestic demand and price competition from Chinese modules.

With overseas sales above half of the total, the timing and cost of U.S. projects are the main variables for earnings swings.

06

Outlook

The company set a 2026 order target of at least KRW 700 billion (February 2026), and its cumulative total orders through Q3 2026 reached KRW 797.6 billion, already above that level (provisional release, November 6, 2026). It expects annual ENG orders to exceed KRW 950 billion.

Kiwoom Securities estimates 2026 revenue of KRW 760 billion and operating profit of KRW 19.5 billion, reported in November 2026; these would be increases of 34 percent and 1,419 percent year on year.

Reaching these estimates would require Q4 revenue to rise sharply from the nine-month cumulative revenue of KRW 513.9 billion (provisional). The company says orders already secured will be recognized as revenue in sequence from Q4, supporting a recovery.

In renewable energy, 645W-plus high-output modules and ESS construction are aimed at cost improvement and new orders. Kiwoom Securities said in an August 2026 report that it expects a first overseas reference for its data-center air-cooling solution in the second half of 2026.

Some brokers also see the AIO module and dry-room dryer businesses contributing in 2027. In a March 2026 conference call, the company said it aims to present a phased shareholder-return plan in the second half of 2026.

It has also said that revenue recognition on secured orders and project settlement would bring its debt ratio down to about 130 percent (November 6, 2026).

08

Bull factors

Record order intake

Cumulative ENG orders through Q3 2026 reached a record KRW 769.4 billion, and Q3 orders alone rose 113 percent year on year to KRW 335 billion (provisional release, November 6, 2026). The company says its customer base has widened beyond large semiconductor projects to new clients and data centers.

It expects annual ENG orders to exceed KRW 950 billion. The company's view is that profit improvement could grow larger as these orders convert into revenue.

Clean-room equipment position and customer base

The more-than-60-percent FFU market share is a figure the company itself describes. Semiconductors made up 75 percent of ENG orders in Q1 2026, and projects for Samsung Electronics' Pyeongtaek campus and SK hynix's Yongin, Cheongju and Indiana fabs are included in the order book.

The company said 76 percent of Q1 orders were in EPC projects, which carry better margins (provisional release, May 2026).

Operating results shifting from loss to profit

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 1.44 billion, reversing a year-earlier operating loss of KRW 1.63 billion (provisional release, November 6, 2026). Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.06 billion marked a return to profit.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders vary in size from quarter to quarter, but the operating line shows a clear recovery direction.

09

Bear factors

Delayed revenue recognition and weaker quarterly profit

ENG segment revenue in Q3 2026 was KRW 157.6 billion, below KRW 196.0 billion a year earlier. The company said revenue recognition on projects secured in the first half has not yet fully started.

As a result, group operating profit for Q3 was KRW 610 million, down 80.1 percent from the prior quarter (provisional release, November 6, 2026).

Continued losses in renewable energy

RE segment revenue in Q3 2026 was KRW 10.2 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 1.6 billion. The company attributed the KRW 3.4 billion RE operating loss in 2025 to weaker domestic demand and price competition from Chinese products.

It is counting on high-output modules and ESS construction to improve results, but those gains have not yet appeared in quarterly figures.

Controlling-shareholder net losses and balance-sheet load

The controlling-shareholder net loss in Q3 2026 was KRW 5.97 billion, extending a loss run of three straight quarters. The last four quarters total KRW 7.28 billion in net losses attributable to controlling shareholders.

The debt ratio stood at 157.5 percent at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 367.4 billion against total equity of KRW 233.3 billion, a load that weighs on the balance sheet.

10

Risk factors

Revenue recognition and project settlement

Large orders have been shifting into revenue at slower quarterly timing, which has increased earnings swings. The pace at which the order book converts into revenue will likely determine the direction of results. The company has said it is tightening cost control and project settlement. Higher raw-material and labor costs on overseas projects could raise both initial and ongoing cost burdens.

Semiconductor investment cycle and customer concentration

Most of the company's orders come from semiconductor capital spending, which made up 75 percent of ENG orders in Q1 2026. The company has described a 2025 episode in which customers changed or delayed investment plans and fixed-cost burdens rose. If the cycle turns, the order book could face gaps, which is the structural risk.

Renewable energy supply-demand and price competition

Weaker domestic demand and price competition from Chinese products have been cited as causes of RE segment losses. The company said delayed shipments began moving again from late April 2026. With the debt ratio at 157.5 percent, a prolonged loss would erode financial flexibility.

12

Overall view

Cumulative results through Q3 2026 show orders and profit moving in different directions. Cumulative ENG orders reached a record KRW 769.4 billion, and group cumulative operating profit turned to KRW 1.44 billion, from a year-earlier loss (provisional).

Revenue recognition, however, has not yet fully reflected the large orders from the first half, so Q3 revenue stayed near the prior-year level, and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued for three straight quarters.

The renewable energy segment is counting on high-output modules and ESS construction, but its Q3 operating loss was KRW 1.6 billion. On a DART-filed basis, operating profit fell from KRW 21 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.28 billion in 2025, and the quarterly pattern has begun to change in 2026.

The debt ratio was 157.5 percent at the end of 2025, and the company has said it will bring it down to about 130 percent through revenue recognition and settlements.

The company has guided to a recovery from Q4, and the next checkpoints are the Q4 provisional results in early February 2027 and the 2026 annual business report.

Broker views are also mixed: Kiwoom Securities said in an August 2026 report that it maintains a target price of KRW 27,000, and a November 2026 report cited its estimate of KRW 19.5 billion in annual operating profit.
