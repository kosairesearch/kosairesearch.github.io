Report overview

Water-purifier parts: sales up, margins down

Stormtec is a KOSDAQ chemicals company centered on water-purifier parts. Its 2025 revenue rose to KRW 69.7 billion, while the operating margin fell from 23.1% in 2022 to 17.1% in 2025.

  1. 1

    2025 revenue was KRW 69.7 billion (up 4.1% year on year), the largest year in 2022–2025. Operating profit was KRW 11.9 billion (down 3.9%), below KRW 12.4 billion in 2024.

  2. 2

    Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 14.4 billion in 2024 (up 47.0%) and fell to KRW 10.2 billion in 2025 (down 28.9%).

  3. 3

    Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 18.5 billion, down 2.7% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 3.44 billion, down 9.5%. Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.59 billion is the highest quarterly figure in the disclosed results since Q3 2025.

  4. 4

    In a December 2025 report, iM Securities pointed out that a subsidiary fully acquired in September 2024 was running operating losses. Separating core earnings from subsidiary results is the central point to track for this stock.

02

Business structure

Stormtec makes parts used in water purifiers. A June 2025 IPO report put the product mix at fittings 62%, valves 13%, faucets 5%, and tubing and assemblies 19%.

In a December 2025 report, iM Securities said the water-purifier parts business accounted for 92% of revenue and named Coway and CUCKOO Homesys as major customers, with rental sales at those operators feeding directly into Stormtec's sales.

The company fully acquired a subsidiary in September 2024 and is widening its scope into environmental components for next-generation home appliances through manufacturing and R&D. iM Securities also noted a negative view that the acquisition lacks synergy with the core business.

In valves, Stormtec's global share is around 4.3%, and the company has set a target of raising it to 30% within five years. On the production side, it cut capacity by 59.5% from the end of 2024 as of Q3 2025 by exiting low-margin lines, and utilization recovered to 53.8%.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 51 billion in 2022 to KRW 55.2 billion in 2023 (up 8.1%), KRW 66.9 billion in 2024 (up 21.3%), and KRW 69.7 billion in 2025 (up 4.1%), marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit over the same period was KRW 11.8 billion, KRW 11.7 billion (down 1.3%), KRW 12.4 billion (up 6.6%), and KRW 11.9 billion (down 3.9%), staying in a narrow band. Revenue grew while operating profit stalled because the operating margin declined, from 23.1% in 2022 to 21.1%, 18.6%, and 17.1% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 9.58 billion in 2022, KRW 9.77 billion in 2023 (up 1.9%), KRW 14.4 billion in 2024 (up 47.0%), and KRW 10.2 billion in 2025 (down 28.9%). Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same figures in each year.

Operating cash flow was KRW 10.6 billion in 2022, KRW 9.52 billion in 2023, KRW 14.2 billion in 2024, and KRW 13.7 billion in 2025, exceeding 2025 operating profit of KRW 11.9 billion.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 93.8 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 39.1 billion, with the debt ratio at 45.0% in 2022, 18.2% in 2023, 39.1% in 2024, and 41.7% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 19 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 15.4 billion in Q4 2025, then KRW 16.3 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 20.8 billion in Q2 2026. Quarterly operating profit followed a similar path, from KRW 3.8 billion to KRW 2.2 billion, then KRW 2.28 billion, and KRW 4.59 billion in Q2 2026.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 18.5 billion fell 11.0% from the prior quarter and 2.7% from a year earlier, and operating profit of KRW 3.44 billion fell 25.0% from the prior quarter and 9.5% from a year earlier. Over the last four quarters, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 10.3 billion.

The disclosed figures do not isolate one-off items, so how much subsidiary results weighed on quarterly earnings is a point for the next periodic report to clarify.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for water-purifier parts is tied to rental sales at finished-goods makers, so the sales trend at major customers is the reference point for the cycle. iM Securities attributed strong Q3 2025 results to rental sales growth at major customers including Coway and CUCKOO Homesys.

FnGuide describes Stormtec as diversifying through manufacturing and R&D in environmental components for next-generation home appliances. A growing rental market is a favorable external factor, but few forward market indicators in the disclosures allow it to be measured in numbers.

In valves, the global share is around 4.3%, which leaves room for share gains, while price and quality competition with rivals becomes a variable. The 30% share target depends on execution after new capacity starts, so competitive dynamics should be read alongside it.

Within KOSDAQ chemicals, Stormtec is concentrated in a narrow segment of water-purifier parts. Its scale and product mix differ sharply from large chemical groups, so the customer structure within the water-purifier parts industry is a more relevant comparison axis than broad sector metrics.

06

Outlook

The company guided for revenue growth of about 3% for 2025 at the start of that year, and reported 4.1% growth in 2025 revenue.

According to iM Securities, the factory expansion switched to demolition and reconstruction as of December 2025, with equipment introduction in early 2026, operations starting in early 2027, and earnings reflected from fiscal 2027 onward.

The report estimates the expanded plant at about 850 pyeong, with an investment in the tens of billions of won. Equipment orders are complete and can be installed as soon as construction finishes. iM Securities views other uncertainties as limited if the expansion proceeds on schedule.

Utilization recovered to 53.8%, which the broker read as a sign of structural improvement, but once the expansion starts with capacity cut by 59.5%, securing volume becomes the next task. The global share target of 30% within five years is a long-term goal that depends on capacity coming online.

How the subsidiary's results change after 2026 has not yet been shown in disclosures. The next checkpoints are the capex scale and subsidiary operating results in the 2026 annual report.

The operating margin fell from 23.1% in 2022 to 17.1% in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders dropped 28.9% to KRW 10.2 billion in 2025. Revenue growth has not translated into profit growth at the same pace.

Year-on-year declines in recent quarters

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 18.5 billion fell 2.7% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 3.44 billion fell 9.5%. Q4 2025 operating profit of KRW 2.2 billion was below the KRW 3.8 billion of Q3 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 10.3 billion over the last four quarters.

Subsidiary losses and expansion timing

iM Securities pointed to operating losses at a subsidiary fully acquired in September 2024 and noted a negative view that the deal lacks synergy with the core business. As of December 2025, the expanded plant's operations were moved to early 2027, and its earnings contribution to fiscal 2027 onward. Whether core recovery can offset subsidiary losses remains the open question.

Risk factors

Customer concentration and rental demand

Revenue tracks unit sales at water-purifier makers. As of Q3 2025, the water-purifier parts business made up 92% of revenue, so the business is concentrated in one area. A slowdown in rental sales at major customers could feed directly into order volumes and results.

Capacity expansion and utilization

The expansion is scheduled to begin operating in early 2027, but the schedule changed once as of the December 2025 report. With capacity cut by 59.5% and utilization at 53.8%, filling new capacity becomes the task once it comes online. A delay in operations would push back the earnings contribution as well.

Subsidiary results and diversification

Operating losses at the subsidiary acquired in September 2024 could weigh on consolidated profit. Whether diversification into environmental components for next-generation appliances creates synergy with the core business is not yet visible in reported results. The subsidiary's profit trend is a point for the next annual report to show.

Overall view

Stormtec is a KOSDAQ chemicals company making water-purifier parts. Based on disclosed results, 2025 revenue of KRW 69.7 billion was the largest year since 2022. The operating margin, however, fell from 23.1% in 2022 to 17.1% in 2025, so revenue growth has not carried through to profit growth.

Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 20.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.59 billion marked a quarterly rebound, but Q3 2026 revenue and operating profit both declined year on year.

Strengths include three consecutive years of revenue growth from 2023 through 2025, operating cash flow above operating profit, and utilization recovery after restructuring capacity.

Weaknesses include the margin decline, lower 2025 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders, operating losses at the subsidiary acquired in September 2024, and the adjusted timing of the expansion.

Valuation metrics show a price-to-earnings ratio well below the sector average and a dividend yield well above it, but profit swings are large, so the multiples depend on the earnings trend.
