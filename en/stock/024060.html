HeunguOil: Quarterly Profit Recovery Amid Annual Decline

02

Business structure

HeunguOil was founded in 1966 and listed on KOSDAQ in 1994 as a wholesale and retail company for petroleum products.

Its core business is supplying petroleum products bought from GS Caltex to the Daegu and Gyeongbuk region, and its profit comes from the spread between purchase and selling prices, known as the distribution margin. Media reports say it operates 13 gas stations and one branch office across Daegu and Gyeongbuk.

Property leasing runs alongside, using the HeunguDongsung and HeunguSamdeok buildings to generate rental income. Because the company buys finished products rather than refining crude oil, margin control at the distribution stage rather than raw material sourcing drives its profit.

The company's own disclosure summary attributes shrinking distribution margins to excessive competition for dealer relationships, which has weakened its earnings structure. The company says it operates environmental pollution prevention facilities and has tightened legal compliance.

As of a March 2026 report, the largest shareholder, CEO Kim Sang-woo, and three related parties held 32.11 percent. At the March 2026 annual general meeting, one outside director and one auditor were newly elected.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue declines and a shift into losses at the same time.

Revenue fell from KRW 146.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 126.7 billion in 2023 (down 13.6% year on year), KRW 116.9 billion in 2024 (down 7.8%), and KRW 107.9 billion in 2025 (down 7.7%), declining for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest among 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 1.36 billion in 2022 to KRW 86.98 million in 2023 (down 93.6%), then turned into an operating loss of KRW 283 million in 2024. The 2025 operating loss widened to KRW 995 million, making 2024 and 2025 two consecutive loss years.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 2.66 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.13 billion in 2023, KRW 620 million in 2024, and KRW 158 million in 2025, declining for three consecutive years. Net profit over the same period was reported at the same values as profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

The operating margin moved from 0.9% in 2022 to -0.9% in 2025, and operating cash flow fell from KRW 2.31 billion in 2024 to KRW 895 million in 2025. Quarterly results point in mixed directions.

Revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from KRW 25.1 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 27.6 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 9.7% from a year earlier in the latest quarter.

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 161 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 and rose to KRW 561 million in the first quarter of 2026, but the second quarter of 2026 posted an operating loss of KRW 691 million.

The third quarter of 2026 returned to an operating profit of KRW 336 million, a swing to profit from a year earlier. A loss in a quarter when revenue also rose shows that higher sales do not automatically translate into profit. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders summed to KRW 490 million over the last four quarters.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from downstream fuel consumption faces structural pressure. A company profile on a financial data site attributes the decline in petroleum demand to environmental regulation, the development of alternative energy, and weaker economic conditions after the pandemic.

The company's 2025 summary points to a prolonged blockade of the Strait of Hormuz during the US-Iran conflict, which unsettled crude supply and reduced petroleum demand and revenue. On the price side, the market sometimes prices in higher margins when oil prices rise, because inventory value increases.

During the oil price surge in February 2026, trading volume in HeunguOil shares rose sharply to about 42.16 million shares in a single day, and some commentary highlighted the profitability of a model that buys finished products for resale.

Coverage from the same period also noted that the rally could prove temporary if tensions in the Middle East ease. In competition, excessive rivalry for dealer relationships is squeezing distribution margins. Compared with the KOSDAQ distribution sector average, HeunguOil's operating profit scale is small.

Because selling prices and margins move with oil prices, the external price environment tends to widen the swings in quarterly profit.

06

Outlook

The company's medium-term direction is set out in the corporate value enhancement plan disclosed on April 20, 2026.

The plan covers a dividend policy to enhance shareholder value, management efficiency through diversified revenue structures and business restructuring, organizational streamlining, and maintaining financial soundness.

It also states that cost competitiveness will be strengthened to secure stable earnings and a wider operating margin, and that dividend funds will be secured through higher profitability.

This is the first corporate value enhancement plan disclosed in 2026, and the disclosure notes that it contains forward-looking information that may change.

On dividends, the company disclosed a 78.7% payout ratio for 2025, with total dividends of KRW 290.8 million in 2025 against KRW 436.2 million in 2024, a 33.3% decline. A cash dividend item was approved at the annual general meeting in March 2026.

The plan is framed mainly around directional goals, and the details and timing of the business restructuring have not yet been disclosed.

Revenue has risen for four quarters, but operating profit has alternated between profit and loss, so whether stronger cost competitiveness stabilizes quarterly profit is the next point to check. Whether the operating margin holds above zero will serve as the yardstick for the plan.

Bull factors

Shift in quarterly profit direction

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 161 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, followed by KRW 561 million in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 336 million in the third quarter. Revenue has risen for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 252 million in the third quarter of 2026 was reported up 42.1% from a year earlier. Unlike the annual loss pattern, the quarterly figures show a recovery in profit.

Parallel income from property leasing

Media coverage highlighted that operating 13 gas stations alongside building leasing secures a stable cash source. Rental income arises separately from distribution margin swings, which can cushion the business structure.

The use of the HeunguDongsung and HeunguSamdeok buildings as leased assets reflects an earnings base tied to owned property. The scale of rental income is not separately reported as a figure, so it should be read apart from core operating results.

Attention to the earnings model during oil price swings

Coverage noted that a model buying finished products for resale draws expectations of higher inventory value and better sales margins when oil prices surge. During the February 2026 oil rally, the stock's volatility increased and trading volume rose sharply.

If the cost competitiveness and operating margin goals set out in April 2026 show up in actual quarterly results, they would support the case for structural improvement. Because market attention leans heavily on oil price moves, whether margin gains come from the company's own efforts is the yardstick.

Bear factors

Three years of decline and two years of losses

Revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders also fell for three consecutive years from KRW 2.66 billion in 2022 to KRW 158 million in 2025. Operating losses occurred in 2024 and 2025, with the 2025 loss widening to KRW 995 million.

Operating cash flow dropped to KRW 895 million in 2025 from KRW 2.31 billion in 2024. On an annual basis, a clear sign of earnings recovery has not yet appeared.

Shrinking distribution margins and weaker demand

The disclosure summary explains that excessive rivalry for dealer relationships has shrunk distribution margins and weakened the earnings structure. Assessments point to falling fossil fuel consumption as environmental regulation and alternative energy spread.

A further explanation is that the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz in 2025 unsettled crude supply and weakened petroleum demand. When sales volumes decline and margins thin at the same time, cost reduction alone may not be enough to reverse the profit trend.

Share sales and quarterly profit volatility

Executive Seo Dong-jae sold all 3,193 shares held on January 26, 2026, according to a disclosure filed January 29. The largest shareholder Kim Sang-woo and related parties reduced holdings by 15,465 shares through open-market sales in a March 2026 report.

Major shareholder Seo Sang-deok also reported selling 800,000 shares on the open market in March 2026. In the second quarter of 2026, an operating loss of KRW 691 million showed the instability of quarterly profit.

Risk factors

Oil prices and geopolitical swings

Selling prices and inventory values move with oil prices and Middle East developments, so profit can swing widely. In February 2026, the stock moved 14.76 percent in a single day and trading volume surged to about 42.16 million shares, showing strong theme-driven flows.

Coverage also noted that these expectations could fade quickly if oil prices stabilize. The business carries high exposure to external price variables.

Thin operating margin

With the operating margin at -0.9 percent in 2025, close to zero, small changes in revenue can flip the sign of operating profit. The second quarter of 2026, when revenue rose yet an operating loss occurred, illustrates this.

The disclosure summary says competition for dealers is holding down distribution margins, so pricing power must recover. Whether stronger cost competitiveness translates into actual margin gains remains the open task.

Ownership concentration and liquidity

The largest shareholder and related parties hold 32.11 percent, which limits the share of freely traded stock. Executive and major shareholder sales on the open market continued into early 2026 and may be read as selling pressure. Small distribution stocks can see wide price swings when trading volume is thin. Total dividends also fell in 2025, so the scale of shareholder returns may move with earnings.

Overall view

HeunguOil is a regional company combining petroleum product distribution with property leasing. Its disclosed results show three consecutive years of declines in revenue and profit attributable to controlling shareholders since 2023, along with operating losses in 2024 and 2025.

In contrast, revenue has risen for four consecutive quarters since the fourth quarter of 2025, and operating profit was positive in the first and third quarters of 2026. The operating loss in the second quarter of 2026 shows how unstable quarterly profit has been.

On the bullish side, the quarterly return to profit and the cost competitiveness plan stand out, while on the bearish side, shrinking distribution margins, weaker demand, and share sales by executives and major shareholders weigh.

Coverage has noted that theme-driven trading tied to oil prices and Middle East developments has played a large role in the share price. The key question going forward is whether quarterly profit carries into annual profit and whether the operating margin settles above zero. The points to check are the 2026 annual report and the first quarter 2027 quarterly report.
