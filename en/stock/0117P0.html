02

Business structure

Piece Peace Studio is a fashion and lifestyle company built around its core women's designer brand, Mardi Mercredi.

Mardi Mercredi is an apparel and accessories brand based on the company's own graphic IP, and the company has expanded its categories around it to include the sports line Actif, the kids' line Les Petits, and the footwear line Les Pompes.

More recently, the company has added new brands including Vacant Archive, TW Records Seoul, and Hello Sunrise as it builds out a multi-brand portfolio.

Its sales channels are centered on a direct-to-consumer (D2C) structure through its own online mall, with Musinsa and its affiliate 29CM also serving as key online platforms.

Overseas, the company has built brand recognition through a flagship store in Daikanyama, Tokyo, and a presence on ZOZOTOWN, while in China it shifted from a licensing arrangement with a local partner to direct market entry following the establishment of a local subsidiary in April 2026.

Within Korea's fashion industry, the company is relatively small compared with large integrated fashion houses such as Handsome Corp., but it has been noted for a cost structure with lower distribution expenses owing to its online-centric sales model.

The company listed on KOSDAQ on June 8, 2026, and said it plans to use a substantial portion of its IPO proceeds for direct entry into the Chinese market and the expansion of new brands and categories. Headcount has remained relatively small, at roughly 130 to 145 employees, for a company of its revenue scale.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue rose 3.6% from KRW 113.8 billion in 2024 to KRW 117.9 billion in 2025, while operating profit fell 40.6% from KRW 28.2 billion to KRW 16.7 billion over the same period.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders also declined 35.2%, from KRW 17.6 billion to KRW 11.4 billion, and total net income including non-controlling interests fell from KRW 17 billion to KRW 11 billion. The operating margin dropped more than 10 percentage points, from 24.7% in 2024 to 14.2% in 2025.

The company attributed the decline in operating profit to investments tied to business expansion—renovation and construction at major flagship stores, overseas business expansion, headcount additions, and marketing expenses—along with a one-off increase in SG&A expenses related to stock compensation costs.

On a quarterly basis, revenue of KRW 23.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.03 billion in the first quarter of 2026 gave way to a sharp profitability contraction in the second quarter, with revenue falling 18.7% quarter on quarter to KRW 19 billion and operating profit falling 89.9% to KRW 206 million.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 95.6% quarter on quarter, from KRW 2.03 billion to KRW 88.74 million, while the sum of controlling net income over the trailing four quarters (Q1–Q4 2025) stood at KRW 11.4 billion.

This sequential weakness appears linked in part to a gap in China sales during the inventory clearance period that followed the termination of the local license contract in October 2025.

On the balance sheet side, however, the debt ratio fell sharply to 25.9% at the end of 2025 from 127.6% in 2024, and operating cash flow improved from KRW 12.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 33.9 billion in 2025, a contrast to the softening in reported earnings.

05

Industry analysis

In Korea's fashion industry, where many large integrated fashion companies operate with single-digit operating margins, Piece Peace Studio has been cited as a case that maintained a relatively high operating margin through its D2C structure centered on its own online mall.

However, as a smaller brand-focused company, it is also noted to be more exposed to brand life-cycle risk tied to shifting trends, given that revenue is concentrated in a specific brand.

In Japan, the company has built brand recognition through its Daikanyama flagship store and online platform presence, aided by interest in Korean fashion, while in China it is undergoing a structural shift from license-based distribution to direct market entry.

China remains a market with fast-shifting consumer trends and intense platform competition, and there have been concerns that discount sales during the former licensee's inventory clearance process could affect brand image.

In North America, the company has been expanding online channels including a presence on Amazon, making the pace of overseas revenue expansion a key variable for its medium-term growth path.

In terms of competitive positioning, large integrated fashion companies such as Handsome Corp. operate extensive offline networks and multi-brand portfolios, whereas Piece Peace Studio has pursued a differentiated strategy of online-centric expansion built around a specific brand IP.

06

Outlook

The company reported first-half 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 42.47 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.23 billion, explaining that the transition of its China business structure affected results during the period.

It established a local subsidiary in China in April 2026 and recorded roughly KRW 600 million in sales on the first day of direct operations, and it opened an additional online platform channel in China in June of the same year.

The company said it plans to open a flagship store in the Anfu Road area of Shanghai in the second half and gradually expand its direct-entry business foundation in China by linking online and offline channels.

On the product side, it plans to expand jackets and coats into a major product line for the autumn-winter season and introduce a new unisex line.

Co-CEO Seo Seung-wan has stated a goal of reaching KRW 100 billion in China revenue by 2028 through tighter direct quality control, with overseas revenue expected to reach 50% of the total.

That said, as of the latest half-year report, the company's Japanese and Chinese subsidiaries were recording net losses, suggesting it may take more time for expanded overseas investment to translate into revenue and profit.

The Japanese subsidiary SCREEN SHOT posted first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 4.039 billion, down 38.9% year on year, and a net loss of KRW 959.92 million, underscoring that monetization of overseas units remains a work in progress.

08

Bull factors

Improved Balance Sheet Strength

The debt ratio fell sharply to 25.9% at the end of 2025 from 127.6% in 2024, and total equity of KRW 113.2 billion is entirely attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow also improved from KRW 12.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 33.9 billion in 2025, a contrast to the softening in reported earnings.

This reflects both IPO proceeds inflow and working-capital management, supporting the financial capacity to continue overseas investment.

Multi-Brand and Category Expansion

Beyond Mardi Mercredi, the company has introduced new brands such as Vacant Archive, TW Records Seoul, and Hello Sunrise in an effort to reduce reliance on a single label. Expansion of its sports, kids, and footwear lines could also diversify revenue sources by leveraging its existing customer base.

If the D2C structure centered on its own online mall is maintained, new brands could also benefit from a relatively low-cost distribution model.

Potential Profit Internalization via China Direct Entry

Following the termination of its license agreement in October 2025, the company established a local subsidiary in April 2026, shifting from a royalty-based model to one capturing direct sales margins.

It has partnered with a local specialist operator to build out e-commerce and logistics, with a Shanghai flagship store opening planned for the second half. Management has said that by directly strengthening quality control, it aims to work toward its medium-term China revenue target.

09

Bear factors

Sharp Sequential Profitability Decline

Second-quarter 2026 revenue fell 18.7% quarter on quarter to KRW 19 billion, and operating profit fell 89.9% to KRW 206 million. Controlling net income also dropped sharply, from KRW 2.03 billion in the first quarter to KRW 88.74 million in the second.

This reflects a combination of a sales gap and rising costs during the China business transition, making it worth watching whether the following quarters show a rebound.

Continued Losses at Overseas Subsidiaries

As of the latest half-year report, both the Japanese and Chinese subsidiaries were recording net losses. The Japanese unit SCREEN SHOT posted first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 4.039 billion, down 38.9% year on year, with a net loss of KRW 959.92 million. It may take more time for the overseas expansion strategy to translate into improved consolidated profit.

Slowing Growth and Shifting Cost Structure

Revenue growth slowed to 3.6% in 2025, a notable deceleration from the company's previously rapid growth pace.

Over the same period, the operating margin fell from 24.7% to 14.2%, which the company attributed to rising SG&A expenses from store investment, overseas expansion, headcount additions, and one-off stock compensation costs. Whether these cost items prove temporary or structural will need to be confirmed in upcoming quarterly results.

10

Risk factors

Brand Concentration Risk

Revenue is concentrated largely in the single brand Mardi Mercredi, and given the trend-sensitive nature of the fashion industry, results could be affected by the brand's life cycle or shifts in consumer preference. As its new brand portfolio is still at an early stage, reducing this concentration may take time.

Execution Risk in China Business Transition

In transitioning from a licensing model to direct entry, risks include reliance on a local e-commerce and logistics operator and potential brand-image effects from discount sales during inventory clearance.

The pace of targeted revenue growth could vary depending on external factors such as local regulation, consumption trends, and intensifying platform competition.

Post-IPO Supply-Demand and Liquidity Risk

As a stock with a relatively short trading history since its June 2026 listing, changes in float from lock-up expirations could affect supply and demand. Its relatively small market capitalization also means trading liquidity may be limited.

Overall view

Piece Peace Studio continued to grow revenue through 2025, but operating profit and net income turned clearly lower, and quarterly profitability has continued to soften into 2026.

The structural shift of its China business from licensing to direct entry has brought short-term cost burdens and a revenue gap, and net losses have also been observed at overseas subsidiaries, including the Japanese unit.

That said, changes in financial strength—such as a markedly lower debt ratio and improved operating cash flow—have moved in a different direction from the earnings metrics, making it difficult to judge the full picture from profit figures alone.

With a short trading history since its June 2026 KOSDAQ listing, liquidity and supply-demand variables also warrant attention.

The key variables going forward are the pace at which the China direct-entry model becomes profitable and whether new brand and category expansion translates into actual revenue and profit, both of which can be tracked through upcoming quarterly reports and concrete events such as the flagship store opening.
