02

Business structure

Piece Peace Studio operates a set of contemporary lifestyle fashion brands, led by the designer label Mardi Mercredi. Its lineup centers on women's wear and is expanding into athleisure, kids and shoes.

The company has no production plants of its own and relies on outsourced manufacturing, while its R&D work targets high-performance fabrics and pattern technology. Sales run through a self-operated D2C mall, directly operated offline stores, and external online, offline and global distribution.

In overseas markets, the restructuring of the China business is the central variable. After its China licensing agreement ended in October 2025, the company set up a local subsidiary in April 2026 and moved to direct operation, starting direct sales on major online platforms from June 2026.

In Japan, unisex lines were introduced at stores in Daikanyama, Tokyo and Osaka in October 2026, and a new store opened in Bangkok, Thailand. On November 18, 2026, the company announced its Singles' Day figures: sales of about KRW 6.08 billion (30.4 million yuan) over 30 days from October 13 to November 11, 2026.

This is a sales figure, not disclosed revenue. The company said it is moving away from heavy discounting and placing more weight on new products and wider product lines.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results in 2024 and 2025 moved in opposite directions. Revenue rose from KRW 113.8 billion in 2024 to KRW 117.9 billion in 2025, up 3.6% year on year, while operating profit fell from KRW 28.2 billion to KRW 16.7 billion, down 40.6%. The operating margin declined from 24.7% to 14.2%.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 35.2% from KRW 17.6 billion to KRW 11.4 billion, while net profit including non-controlling interests went from KRW 17 billion to KRW 11 billion. The two profit measures differ because of whether non-controlling interests are included.

The balance sheet became lighter over the same period: the debt ratio fell from 127.6% to 25.9%, and at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 113.2 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 29.3 billion.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 12.4 billion to KRW 33.9 billion, moving against the drop in profit. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the four quarters from the first quarter to the fourth quarter of 2025 totaled KRW 11.4 billion, the same as the full-year 2025 figure.

Quarterly results weakened sharply in 2026. First-quarter revenue was KRW 23.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 2.03 billion. Second-quarter revenue was KRW 19 billion, down 18.7% from the prior quarter, and operating profit was KRW 206 million, down 89.9%.

Third-quarter revenue was KRW 19.9 billion, up 4.8% from the prior quarter, but the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.94 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.65 billion, swinging into loss.

According to the company's November 16, 2026 release, nine-month 2026 revenue was KRW 62.4 billion, down 31.0% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 300 million, down 97.9%. Net income swung to a loss, reflecting foreign exchange losses from currency moves.

The company cites the restructuring of its China business, overseas infrastructure investment and short-term costs from reduced discounting as the main causes.

05

Industry analysis

The apparel business is sensitive to seasonality and the economic cycle. For the autumn and winter season, the company said it is expanding jackets and coats as core lines and introducing unisex lines. In China, sales have shifted toward a platform-centered model.

According to the company, cumulative sales over the first two days of the Singles' Day pre-sale promotion were about KRW 1.54 billion, and it says it is keeping a strategy that does not rely on heavy discounts.

As China moved from licensing to direct operation, the company took on distribution margins and inventory risk itself, changing both how revenue is recognized and the cost structure. Market conditions at listing also mattered.

On the listing day, June 8, 2026, the broader Korean stock market fell sharply, triggering circuit breakers at the open and weakening investor sentiment toward newly listed shares.

Competitiveness will show up in category rankings on online platforms, the reception of new product lines and the sales performance of overseas stores.

The company said its new unisex sweatshirt ranked first in sales volume in the sweatshirt and fleece category on Taobao and Tmall on the first official day of Singles' Day.

06

Outlook

In the second half of 2026, the company said it will focus on expanding its direct China operations and strengthening its competitiveness in Korea and overseas. It presents a stronger autumn and winter product portfolio and wider online and offline channels as the basis for an earnings recovery.

In China, it plans to open a flagship store of about 740 square meters in the Anfu Road area of Shanghai in the first half of 2027, which the company says will be the largest of the brand's stores worldwide.

The company plans to keep investing in its global business base through the end of 2026 and to accelerate overseas growth in 2027. Its co-CEOs said that 2027 is when overseas growth should become full-scale and translate into higher sales and profitability.

On profitability, the company points to more efficient operation of sales channels and tighter control of store operating and marketing costs. The treasury share cancellation is scheduled for November 25, 2026, and the filing notes that the date may change depending on consultations with authorities.

Once completed, total issued common shares will fall from 14,168,911 to 12,334,071, with no change to share capital. Full-year 2026 results will be confirmed in the 2026 annual report. Whether the overseas store investment is reflected in fourth-quarter 2026 costs is the next checkpoint.

Bull factors

Treasury share cancellation and shareholder value

On November 17, 2026, the board approved cancelling all treasury shares worth KRW 10 billion. The scheduled cancellation date is November 25, 2026, which will reduce total issued common shares by 12.95%. Share capital will not change, and the company describes the move as a step to raise shareholder value.

Early sales momentum from direct entry in China

The company's announced Singles' Day sales were about KRW 6.08 billion over October 13 to November 11, 2026. It said a new unisex sweatshirt ranked first in its category on the first official sales day. The company also says sales have risen in Japan since it introduced unisex lines.

Financial headroom and higher operating cash flow

The debt ratio was 25.9% at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 29.3 billion. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 12.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 33.9 billion in 2025. Cash generation grew even as profit fell, which points to financial headroom.

Bear factors

Sharp deterioration in quarterly profit in 2026

Operating profit was KRW 206 million in the second quarter of 2026 and swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.94 billion in the third quarter. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 88.74 million in the second quarter to a net loss of KRW 3.65 billion in the third. Nine-month operating profit was KRW 300 million, down 97.9%.

Falling sales and the cost of restructuring

Nine-month 2026 revenue was KRW 62.4 billion, down 31.0% year on year. The company attributes the decline to ending its China licensing business and reorganizing around its local subsidiary. Investment in stores, sales channels, logistics and marketing infrastructure has been booked as cost over the same period.

Concern over financial investor share sales

Concern has been raised that shares held by financial investors could come to market. Right after the 2026 listing, the stock fell below the offer price during a sharp market decline. With the core business slowing, cash spent on buybacks and cancellations is also cited as a pressure point.

10

Risk factors

Inventory burden from China direct sales

Moving to direct operation in China means the company itself bears distribution margins and inventory risk. If sales momentum on online platforms such as Singles' Day does not continue, inventory could build up, and clearing it may require expanding discounts again.

The company says it is keeping a strategy that does not rely on heavy discounting, but inventory levels and sell-through speed will shape results. Inventory write-downs or higher selling and administrative costs could lower operating profit further.

Early performance of Japan and Southeast Asia stores

The unisex lines at the Daikanyama and Osaka stores in Japan and the new Bangkok store in Thailand are still at the stage where their sales trend must be confirmed after introduction. If sales at these stores fail to cover fixed store operating costs, improvement in overall overseas profitability could be delayed.

Because the company plans to make overseas growth full-scale in 2027, sales speed at each store will be a key variable for earnings recovery.

Change in revenue recognition

The Singles' Day sales figure the company announced is a separate metric from disclosed revenue. As China moved from licensing to direct operation, the way revenue is recognized has changed, so the link between platform sales and reported revenue may vary from quarter to quarter.

A change in recognition basis makes quarter-to-quarter comparisons harder and may take time to confirm whether sales indicators are turning into earnings improvement.

Overall view

Piece Peace Studio's disclosed results show revenue rising in 2025 while operating profit fell, and in 2026 quarterly operating profit dropped to KRW 206 million in the second quarter and an operating loss of KRW 1.94 billion in the third.

The company points to the shift from licensing to direct operation in China, local infrastructure investment and short-term costs from reduced discounting as the main causes.

Its financial headroom includes a debt ratio of 25.9% at the end of 2025 and operating cash flow of KRW 33.9 billion, and the cancellation of KRW 10 billion in treasury shares will reduce issued shares by 12.95%. The Singles' Day sales figure and sales momentum in Japan are positive signals as announced by the company.

On the other side, nine-month 2026 operating profit fell 97.9% and revenue fell 31.0%, and concern over financial investor share sales has been raised. Strengths and weaknesses are appearing in the same period, so whether overseas store investment translates into 2027 profit is the central question.

The next checkpoints are the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report and whether the Shanghai flagship opens in the first half of 2027.
