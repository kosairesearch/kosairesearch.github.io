Report overview

Operating profit back in the black, shareholder losses persist

Namu Tech returned to operating profit in 2025, but shareholder-level losses have run for three straight years and quarterly operating profit has slowed, making the pace of monetization in its AI infrastructure push the key item to watch.

  1. 1

    Operating profit of KRW 2.25 billion in 2025 turned positive from a KRW 2.23 billion operating loss in 2024, yet the shareholder net loss of KRW 2.81 billion marks a third straight year of losses.

  2. 2

    Q3 2026 revenue rose 10.7% year on year to KRW 35.7 billion, while operating profit of KRW 581 million was down 80.4% from the same quarter a year earlier.

  3. 3

    The push into AI infrastructure rests on its own software lineup (NAA, SPERO, Cocktail Cloud), GPU server partnerships, and supply to public research institutes.

  4. 4

    Operating cash flow reached KRW 10.1 billion in 2025, reversing a KRW 778 million outflow in 2024.

  5. 5

    A remaining KRW 4.6 billion payment for subsidiary preferred shares falls due in October 2027, adding to debt and cash-flow considerations.

02

Business structure

Namu Tech started from virtualization and cloud technology and is shifting its weight toward AI infrastructure platforms as an IT software company.

Its own product lineup includes the enterprise agentic AI platform NAMU Agentic AI (NAA), the multi and hybrid cloud management platform SPERO, the Cocktail Cloud platform, and the namuVIRT virtualization solution.

The company plans to use this lineup to supply infrastructure and software together to AI data centers and AI factories. In the second quarter of 2026, virtualization and cloud accounted for half of the standalone revenue of KRW 12.9 billion, according to the company.

NAA connects internal documents, databases and business systems to handle search, analysis and report writing, and it also supports on-premise and closed-network environments.

Its use is extending to expert report generation for clinical research organizations in bio, beauty and health supplements, and to content agents for tourism and MICE. On the hardware side, its software runs on GPU servers from a server maker, Kocolink, under an AI infrastructure partnership signed in August 2026.

Customers are widening from financial, public and manufacturing firms that run their own data centers to public research institutes. A KTL GPU equipment project announced on November 3, 2026 also includes its own software.

Against large system integrators such as Samsung SDS, Hyundai Autoever and LG CNS, the company points to virtualization and cloud management technology and a rising share of its own software as its differentiators.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 106.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 90.6 billion in 2023 (down 14.7% year on year), then rose to KRW 91.4 billion in 2024 (up 1.0%) and KRW 102.3 billion in 2025 (up 11.9%), marking two straight years of growth from 2024 to 2025. 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest among 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 2.18 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 750 million in 2023, then widened to an operating loss of KRW 2.23 billion in 2024 before returning to operating profit of KRW 2.25 billion in 2025. Operating margin ran at 2.1% in 2022, -0.8% in 2023, -2.4% in 2024 and 2.2% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 337 million in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 3.15 billion in 2023 and KRW 5.31 billion in 2024, then narrowed to a net loss of KRW 2.81 billion in 2025, which is the third straight year of losses.

Net income including non-controlling interests, a different measure, was a net loss of KRW 1.88 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 46.2 billion, of which KRW 34.6 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 55.1 billion, with the debt ratio easing from 142.6% in 2024 to 119.3% in 2025.

Operating cash flow moved from an outflow of KRW 778 million in 2024 to an inflow of KRW 10.1 billion in 2025, a gap far larger than the operating profit and one to check against working-capital details in the annual report.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 32.2 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 35 billion in Q4 2025 (up 8.6% quarter on quarter) and KRW 37 billion in Q1 2026 (up 5.6%), then eased to KRW 35.5 billion in Q2 2026 (down 4.0%) and reached KRW 35.7 billion in Q3 2026 (up 0.6% quarter on quarter and up 10.7% year on year).

Operating profit fell from KRW 2.96 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 1.18 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 415 million in Q1 2026, then edged up to KRW 343 million in Q2 2026 and KRW 581 million in Q3 2026.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 1.13 billion in Q3 2025 to a loss of KRW 747 million in Q4 2025, a profit of KRW 246 million in Q1 2026, and losses of KRW 315 million in Q2 2026 and KRW 71.2 million in Q3 2026, making two straight quarters of losses.

The shareholder net loss over the last four quarters totals KRW 886 million. The 96.7% year-on-year revenue jump in Q2 2026 is a company-announced figure from August 13, 2026, and the 10.7% growth in Q3 shows the pace has slowed sharply.

The company said its standalone operating loss narrowed year on year while it kept investing in its own software.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic AI adoption is splitting between public cloud and on-premise deployments in which customers run their own data centers. The company and its partners say they are targeting the on-premise AI market among financial, public and manufacturing customers.

In the public sector, rising demand for high-performance GPUs at research institutes is creating a need for resource allocation and AI model operations software.

Environments where many researchers share GPU resources depend on virtualization and cloud resource management, which overlaps with the company's existing capabilities. The AI infrastructure market moves as a bundle of server suppliers and software firms, which is why the company chose to partner with a server maker.

Revenue has moved in the KRW 35 billion range since Q4 2025, so whether new AI businesses translate into revenue growth is the key variable. Among competitors, large IT players such as Samsung SDS, Hyundai Autoever, LG CNS, NAVER and Kakao have larger capital and customer bases.

Namu Tech is trying to differentiate by raising the share of its own software. For a smaller company competing with large peers, how much of its own software revenue shows up in results is the central point for judging the business.

06

Outlook

The company said it will make its AI infrastructure platform results more concrete in the second half of 2026, aiming to lift both growth and profitability. The core strategy is to expand supply to AI data centers and AI factories on top of its existing virtualization and cloud customer base.

The KTL project is set to move from equipment introduction to supporting resource allocation and model operations. Following its participation in Japan IT Week, the company plans to pursue technical validation and business cooperation with customers and partners in Japan together with its Japan subsidiary.

Whether the on-premise AI partnership with IA Cloud brings customers in financial, public and manufacturing sectors through to reported results is the next point to check.

Whether Q4 2026 operating profit holds near the Q3 level and whether annual operating cash flow stays near the 2025 level will be visible in the next annual report.

The remaining KRW 4.6 billion payment for subsidiary preferred shares is scheduled for October 2027, and whether operating cash generation and debt rollover can cover it is the central question. With Q3 revenue growth slowing to 10.7%, whether AI platform sales lift quarterly top-line growth is the next point to watch.

08

Bull factors

Return to operating profit in 2025

Operating profit of KRW 2.25 billion in 2025 moved back into the black from a KRW 2.23 billion operating loss in 2024, with an operating margin of 2.2%. Operating cash flow also improved to KRW 10.1 billion from a KRW 778 million outflow. The shift from loss to profit is the first earnings-based argument on the bull side.

AI infrastructure platform lineup and references

The company has its own lineup including NAA, SPERO, Cocktail Cloud and namuVIRT, linked to supply for AI data centers and AI factories. Its software was supplied to a KTL GPU equipment project announced in November 2026, creating a public research reference. Partnerships with a server maker and a cloud firm extend the supply scope combined with hardware.

Revenue growth held in Q3 2026

Q3 2026 revenue rose 10.7% year on year to KRW 35.7 billion and has held in the KRW 35 billion range since Q4 2025. Q2 2026 revenue rose 96.7% year on year per the company's announcement. With top-line size maintained, raising the share of its own software is presented as room for profitability to improve.

09

Bear factors

Shareholder-level losses persist

The shareholder net loss has been recorded for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, and the last four quarters total a KRW 886 million net loss. Losses have continued for two straight quarters, Q2 and Q3 2026.

Net income including non-controlling interests is a different figure, so shareholder-level results must be read separately.

Slowing quarterly operating profit

Operating profit fell from KRW 2.96 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 1.18 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 415 million in Q1 2026. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 581 million was up 69.4% quarter on quarter but down 80.4% year on year. Profit has stayed small while revenue held in the KRW 35 billion range, which forms the bear case.

Acquisition payments and debt burden

In a deal to buy subsidiary preferred shares for KRW 11.3 billion, KRW 6.7 billion was paid first and KRW 4.6 billion remains due in October 2027. Press coverage pointed to unstable operating cash generation alongside the need to roll over existing borrowings and fund operations. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 55.1 billion, above total equity of KRW 46.2 billion.

10

Risk factors

Funding and refinancing risk

If the remaining preferred share payment and existing debt rollover coincide, cash headroom could tighten. Operating cash flow turned positive in 2025, but that is a single year, so its durability must be checked in the next annual report. Of total equity of KRW 46.2 billion at the end of 2025, KRW 34.6 billion belonged to controlling shareholders.

Public and project-based order volatility

Supply tied to public research equipment purchases, such as the KTL project, can shift in timing with budgets and project schedules. Quarterly revenue moves with project timing, as seen in the 4.0% quarter-on-quarter decline in Q2 2026.

Virtualization and cloud accounted for half of standalone Q2 2026 revenue per the company, so the performance of the existing business also needs to be tracked.

Competition and technology risk

Large IT companies and hardware vendors are entering the AI infrastructure and agentic AI markets at the same time. How clearly the company's products stand out in customer environments such as on-premise and closed networks is the key question. Overseas revenue contribution is still at the stage of sales expansion by its Japan subsidiary.

12

Overall view

Namu Tech is showing a clear shift in direction, with a return to operating profit in 2025 and a move toward AI infrastructure platforms built on its own software. On the strength side, the case rests on improved operating cash flow in 2025, Q3 2026 revenue growth year on year, and public-sector references such as KTL.

On the weakness side, shareholder-level losses have run for three straight years, the last four quarters total a KRW 886 million net loss, and quarterly operating profit has slowed.

Funding pressure from the remaining preferred share payment and debt rollover overlaps in the same period, so the strengths and weaknesses carry similar weight.

The next points to check are Q4 2026 operating profit and whether the shareholder net loss narrows in the 2026 annual report, and the cash position around the October 2027 payment. Because profit-based valuation metrics are not yet available, the multiple should be read together with the earnings trend.
