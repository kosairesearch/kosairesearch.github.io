Report overview

Boom-Market Earnings Meet a Real Estate Overhang

Daishin Securities posted quarterly operating profit of KRW 359.9 billion in Q2 2026 on surging trading volumes and valuation gains on its securities holdings, but a real estate finance exposure that is large relative to its equity, together with swings in valuation gains and losses, still determines how durable those earnings are.

  1. 1

    On a reported basis, Q2 2026 operating profit was KRW 359.9 billion, up 165.2% year on year, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 258.6 billion, up 249.7%.

  2. 2

    The Q2 result came from higher brokerage commissions and margin-lending income on rising trading volumes, plus valuation gains on securities the company holds.

  3. 3

    In a late-September 2026 report, NH Investment & Securities said it expects a Q3 valuation loss of roughly KRW 160 billion on Daishin Securities' Samsung C&T shares as that stock's price fell.

  4. 4

    In February 2026 the company disclosed a plan to cancel 15.35 million treasury shares and to pay tax-free dividends of up to KRW 400 billion over roughly four years.

  5. 5

    Designated Korea's 10th comprehensive financial investment business operator in December 2024, the company plans to pursue mega-IB status by 2028.

02

Business structure

Daishin Securities is a mid-to-large brokerage built around retail brokerage, trading, corporate finance (IB and project finance) and wealth management, with consolidated subsidiaries including Daishin F&I, Daishin Savings Bank, Daishin Asset Trust and Daishin Asset Management.

Similarly named listed companies Daishin Value REIT and DaishinInformation are separate entities and are not covered here. On a standalone basis, Q2 2026 net operating revenue was KRW 452.5 billion, up 39.7% quarter on quarter.

In that quarter, net brokerage commissions were KRW 187.8 billion and trading gains were KRW 178.8 billion. Together these two lines made up most of net operating revenue, so earnings depend heavily on market turnover and equity prices.

In corporate finance, total IB and PF revenue rose from KRW 49.6 billion to KRW 80.8 billion, and the company says its corporate credit balance has kept growing since its designation as a comprehensive operator. That designation lifts the corporate credit limit from 100% to 200% of equity capital.

Among competitors, Mirae Asset Securities, Korea Investment & Securities and NH Investment & Securities hold more than KRW 8 trillion of equity and run IMA businesses, while KB Securities, KIWOOM Securities, Samsung Securities and others issue promissory notes as mega-IBs.

Daishin Securities sits one tier below, as a comprehensive operator that does not yet have a promissory-note license.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell 8.9% from KRW 4.23 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.85 trillion in 2023, then rose for two consecutive years to KRW 4.09 trillion in 2024 and KRW 5.06 trillion in 2025. Because a brokerage's revenue includes gross derivative and product turnover, profit lines are the better gauge.

Operating profit slid from KRW 253.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 161.3 billion in 2023 and KRW 83.6 billion in 2024, before jumping 260.6% to KRW 301.4 billion in 2025, and operating margin recovered from 2.0% in 2024 to 6.0% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for three consecutive years from 2023, going from KRW 115.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 186.6 billion in 2025.

One-offs were large in 2025: Korea Investors Service said items such as the headquarters building sale (KRW 202.3 billion of non-operating income in Q1) made up a substantial part of that year's profit growth.

By quarter, Q4 2025 operating profit of KRW 53.2 billion was the lowest of the last five quarters, and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 6.07 billion. Press reports put that quarter's provisions at KRW 147.9 billion, up sharply from KRW 13.3 billion the quarter before.

Operating profit then rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 102.5 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 359.9 billion in Q2, while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 149.8 billion in Q1 and grew to KRW 258.6 billion in Q2.

Q2 still carried KRW 42.7 billion of domestic and overseas real estate provisions, which means the market boom covered the provisioning burden rather than removing it.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's brokerage industry went through a stretch in the first half of 2026 when trading volumes and margin balances surged with the equity rally. Average daily trading value in Q2 was KRW 81.5 trillion, well above Q1's KRW 66.6 trillion. The second half looked different.

In August 2026, the Daishin Securities research center noted that margin loan balances were 22.2% below their KRW 38.6 trillion peak. In mid-October 2026, press reports said the KOSPI had fallen sharply as doubts over the profitability of AI investment coincided with heavy foreign selling.

On the regulatory side, authorities are pushing brokerages away from real estate and toward corporate finance and venture capital: the cap on real-estate-related assets in promissory-note portfolios was cut from 30% to 15% in 2026 and will fall to 10% from 2027.

With a large brokerage weighting, Daishin Securities is sensitive to the trading-volume cycle, and its large real estate finance book puts it directly in the path of these policy changes.

06

Outlook

The company's medium-term path runs through capital expansion toward mega-IB status. In its February 2026 disclosure, it designated 2025 to 2028 as a capital-expansion period aimed at entering the mega-IB tier. Equity capital stood at KRW 4.59 trillion at the end of June 2026, above the KRW 4 trillion threshold.

A mega-IB license, however, requires holding at least KRW 4 trillion of equity for two years, so entry will take time. As part of its capital build-up, the company issued KRW 70 billion of hybrid capital securities in July 2026 at a 5.2% annual coupon.

For Q3 2026, an NH Investment & Securities analyst said right after the Q2 release that a market correction would likely limit further valuation gains. In a late-September 2026 report, NH Investment & Securities cut its target price on Daishin Securities from KRW 40,000 to KRW 35,000. Q3 2026 results had not been released as of this report.

08

Bull factors

High earnings leverage to trading volumes

In Q2, domestic equity brokerage commissions reached KRW 158.4 billion, up 212.7% year on year. Margin-lending income also rose sharply, showing that the retail franchise turns a market boom into profit quickly. If trading volumes stay high, brokerage earnings can absorb the provisioning burden.

Shareholder returns combining cancellation and tax-free dividends

Treasury share cancellation proceeds in stages at each quarter-end over six quarters, and all preferred treasury shares are to be retired. The company has provided dividend guidance since 2020, a first for the industry.

Tax-free dividends raise individual shareholders' after-tax income, so the policy is aimed at income-oriented investors.

Corporate finance growth on comprehensive-operator status

Financial advisory fees rose more than sixfold, from KRW 4.3 billion in Q1 to KRW 29.4 billion in Q2. Corporate lending and refinancing under the expanded credit limit are widening the revenue base. A mega-IB license in 2028 would add promissory-note issuance as a funding tool.

09

Bear factors

Real estate exposure large relative to equity

Real estate finance exposure reached 87% of equity capital at end-September 2025. Bridge loans make up 25% and mezzanine or subordinated positions 46%, a relatively high-risk mix. Korea Investors Service has said provisioning is somewhat below an adequate level, so further provisions could cut into earnings again.

Earnings volatility from valuation gains

The Q2 surge in trading profit came from valuation gains on securities holdings, so it works in reverse when share prices fall. NH Investment & Securities believes the company added to its Samsung C&T stake during the quarter and sees little chance of a near-term sale. That means a single holding's valuation swings will keep driving quarterly results.

Relatively weak soundness metrics

At end-March 2026, the adjusted net operating capital ratio was 169.4%, below the 183.5% average of the comprehensive operators Korea Investors Service compared it with. The benefit of new capital has been partly offset by growth in risk assets.

Promissory-note licensing reviews soundness and risk management as well as capital size, so the license timeline could slip.

10

Risk factors

Credit risk

Overseas assets make up 47% of the real estate book, leaving the company exposed to the global commercial property cycle. Provision coverage of precautionary-and-below assets is relatively thin, so realized losses could swing quarterly profit sharply. The Q4 2025 swing to a loss is a case in point.

Market risk

Brokerage and trading account for most net operating revenue, so falling volumes combined with falling prices hit earnings twice. Margin balances have dropped from their peak, and the index fell sharply in mid-October 2026. Valuation losses on equity holdings flow straight into operating profit.

Regulatory and licensing risk

Comprehensive operators' venture capital supply requirement rises in steps from 10% in 2026 to 20% in 2027 and 25% in 2028. Failure to reshape a real-estate-heavy risk asset mix could weigh on the promissory-note license review. A delayed license would push back the 2028 mega-IB target.

What to watch next

  1. Mid-November 2026

    In the Q3 2026 results, check the size of the Samsung C&T valuation loss and whether real estate provisions increased. This quarter will show how much of Q2's profit depended on valuation gains.

  2. Late December 2026

    Check that the staged quarter-end treasury share cancellation continues as planned. Together with the year-end dividend record, it shows whether shareholder-return commitments are being kept.

  3. January 2027

    The cap on real estate assets in promissory-note portfolios drops to 10% and the venture capital supply requirement rises to 20%. These become benchmarks for how fast Daishin Securities reduces its real estate weighting.

  4. February 2027

    Check the size of the 2026 tax-free dividend in the year-end dividend decision and the corporate value-up progress disclosure. The key question is how fast the KRW 400 billion ceiling is being used.

  5. March 2027

    In the 2026 annual report, check full-year results and trends in precautionary-and-below assets, guarantee balances and the net capital ratio. These show whether soundness is improving ahead of the mega-IB review.

12

Overall view

For Daishin Securities, the first half of 2026 was a period when a strong market lifted both retail brokerage and valuation gains on securities holdings.

On reported figures, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for three consecutive years from 2023, and after a swing to a loss in Q4 2025 it turned positive in Q1 2026 and grew in Q2.

Real estate provisions kept coming through over the same period, though, so a market downturn could bring valuation losses and provisions at the same time.

Treasury share cancellation and tax-free dividends now have concrete execution schedules, while mega-IB entry targets 2028 and still requires sustained capital and a soundness review. The next things to watch are the size of the Q3 2026 valuation loss, the provision trend and how quickly real estate exposure shrinks.
