Report overview

Q3 swings to loss; capital plans and property exposure in focus

Daishin Securities swung to a Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 22.3 billion, while cumulative nine-month operating profit still rose year on year; the share cancellation schedule and real estate exposure are the next items to track.

02

Business structure

Daishin Securities runs brokerage, trading, corporate finance (IB), wealth management (WM) and corporate lending, with Daishin Savings Bank and Daishin Asset Management as subsidiaries. Net operating revenue for January to September 2026 rose 10.7% year on year, led by a 438.0% jump in brokerage income.

Corporate finance and WM income rose 18.9% and 5.4%, respectively. In Q3 2026, standalone trading net operating revenue turned negative at minus KRW 101.8 billion from KRW 173.0 billion in the prior quarter, and standalone brokerage net operating revenue fell 43.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 107.1 billion.

Daishin Savings Bank and Daishin Asset Management posted pre-tax profits of KRW 9.5 billion and KRW 4.7 billion in Q3. Corporate lending has grown since the company gained comprehensive investment business status. Standalone equity capital stood at KRW 4.594 trillion.

The company announced that its 10-year IRP guaranteed-return product ranked first in the securities industry in the Financial Supervisory Service’s Q3 2026 comparison.

NH Investment & Securities said in a July 2026 report that Daishin’s earnings scale is not large relative to other major brokerages, which keeps its profile low. Much of the revenue depends on market trading value and mark-to-market gains on holdings.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 4.23 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.85 trillion in 2023, down 8.9% year on year, then rose to KRW 4.09 trillion in 2024 (up 6.2%) and KRW 5.06 trillion in 2025 (up 23.7%). Revenue grew in both 2024 and 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period, with 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 253.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 161.3 billion in 2023 (down 36.4%) and KRW 83.6 billion in 2024 (down 48.2%), then rose to KRW 301.4 billion in 2025 (up 260.6%). Operating margin moved from 6.0% in 2022 to 4.2% in 2023 and 2.0% in 2024, before recovering to 6.0% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 115.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 133.6 billion, KRW 144.8 billion and KRW 186.6 billion in 2025, increasing in each year from 2023 to 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 186.7 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 266.2 billion in 2022 to -KRW 2.8 trillion in 2023, -KRW 3.97 trillion in 2024 and -KRW 3.22 trillion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 679.0% in 2022 to 864.4% in 2025.

At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 4.06 trillion, of which KRW 4.05 trillion was attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 35.1 trillion. Q4 2025 posted a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 6.07 billion.

Q1 2026 returned to profit at KRW 149.8 billion attributable to controlling shareholders. Q2 2026 operating profit reached KRW 359.9 billion, up 251.3% from Q1. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 2 trillion, up 142.7% year on year and down 21.4% quarter on quarter, but the company posted an operating loss of KRW 2.23 billion.

A one-off gain of KRW 20.2 billion from the sale of a New York property cushioned the quarter. Preliminary cumulative nine-month operating profit was KRW 460.2 billion, up 85.4%, and cumulative net income was KRW 389.6 billion, up 102.7%.

05

Industry analysis

The brokerage business is structurally exposed to market trading value and mark-to-market gains on holdings. Brokerage income rose during the first-half market rally but slowed as trading value contracted in Q3.

SK Securities said in a November 12, 2026 report that it had reflected weaker Q3 results and a lower outlook for average daily trading value. In corporate finance, new PF deals declined in Q3, reducing advisory and guarantee income, while IPO underwriting fees increased.

The company targets approval for issuing short-term bills in 2028 as it pursues a large-scale investment bank profile, and it needs to demonstrate its IB and asset management capabilities after meeting the KRW 4 trillion equity requirement.

SK Securities viewed the non-taxed dividend as positive through 2028 but said leverage and shareholder returns would need to rise after that. Its smaller earnings scale relative to large brokerages keeps its profile lower, according to NH Investment & Securities. The IRP ranking supports the retail franchise.

Industry trading value remains sensitive to rates and market sentiment, so earnings volatility remains structural.

06

Outlook

The shareholder return plan involves share cancellations. The company will cancel 9.32 million common shares and 6.03 million preferred shares in six stages, targeting completion in the first half of 2027, with 3 million remaining treasury shares earmarked for employee incentives and an employee stock ownership plan.

The company has secured a non-taxed dividend funding pool of up to KRW 400 billion over about four years.

SK Securities said in a November 12, 2026 report that it cut its 2026 controlling-shareholder net income estimate from KRW 411.2 billion to KRW 293.1 billion and lowered its target price from KRW 40,000 to KRW 30,000, while maintaining a buy rating.

SK said the weak second-half outlook had already been largely priced into the share price.

NH Investment & Securities said in a September 28, 2026 report that it maintained its buy rating and cut its target price from KRW 40,000 to KRW 35,000 to reflect Q3 investment losses, and noted that the timing of a roughly KRW 80 billion provision for European commercial property is undetermined.

The next checkpoints are when the Q4 provisions and one-off costs are recognized and whether trading value recovers.

08

Bull factors

Capital building and shareholder returns run together

In February 2026 the company disclosed plans to cancel 9.32 million common and 6.03 million preferred shares in six stages, targeting completion in the first half of 2027. It also secured non-taxed dividend funding of up to KRW 400 billion over about four years. The plan pairs capital building for a large IB profile with shareholder returns.

Cumulative earnings and business diversification

Cumulative nine-month 2026 operating profit reached KRW 460.2 billion, up 85.4% (preliminary, announced November 9, 2026). Brokerage income rose 438.0% over the same period, with corporate finance and WM income also higher. Subsidiaries Daishin Savings Bank and Daishin Asset Management both reported pre-tax profits in Q3.

Corporate lending and retirement pension franchise

Corporate lending has grown since the company gained comprehensive investment business status. The company announced a 6.12% 10-year IRP guaranteed-return yield, ranked first in the industry in the Q3 2026 Financial Supervisory Service comparison. It aims for approval to issue short-term bills in 2028 as it builds IB and asset management capacity.

Bear factors

Earnings swings tied to trading value and mark-to-market

Q3 2026 posted an operating loss of KRW 2.23 billion. Standalone trading net operating revenue turned to minus KRW 101.8 billion, and brokerage net operating revenue fell 43.0% quarter on quarter. Gains of about KRW 160 billion in Q2 reversed into losses in Q3. A further decline in trading value would weigh on earnings outlooks.

Property exposure and provision burden

Project financing exposure is about KRW 2.03 trillion domestically and roughly KRW 1.4 trillion overseas. NH Investment & Securities said property exposure large relative to equity has led to provisions and loss recognition.

Once the timing of the European commercial property provision is set, it could affect earnings in the quarter in which it is booked.

Balance sheet and cash flow strain

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 679.0% in 2022 to 864.4% in 2025. Operating cash flow was -KRW 2.8 trillion in 2023, -KRW 3.97 trillion in 2024 and -KRW 3.22 trillion in 2025. Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 35.1 trillion against a much smaller equity base. Whether equity growth outpaces liabilities is the key question.

10

Risk factors

Market risk

Lower market trading value reduces brokerage commissions and interest income at the same time. The company cited weaker trading value as the main reason for the Q3 2026 slowdown. SK Securities lowered its outlook for average daily trading value in a November 12, 2026 report. Mark-to-market results on held equities also move sharply with share prices.

Real estate credit risk

The company carries about KRW 2.03 trillion in domestic PF exposure and roughly KRW 1.4 trillion overseas. Losses on overseas property were recognized in Q4 2025 results. The timing of the European commercial property provision is still undetermined. Additional provisions would add uncertainty to quarterly results.

Capital and policy risk

The 2028 target for short-term bill approval depends on meeting the KRW 4 trillion equity requirement and proving IB capacity. A delay would push back the earnings benefit of the large-IB strategy. The pace of share cancellations and non-taxed dividends also depends on regulation and capital capacity.

12

Overall view

Daishin Securities posted its largest operating profit of the 2022 to 2025 period at KRW 301.4 billion in 2025, and cumulative nine-month 2026 operating profit rose year on year on a preliminary basis.

However, Q3 2026 swung to an operating loss of KRW 22.3 billion as trading value fell and held equities posted mark-to-market losses. Results depend on both trading value and mark-to-market gains. On shareholder returns, share cancellations and non-taxed dividend funding are in place.

On capital, the 2028 short-term bill approval target and the KRW 4 trillion equity requirement remain open. Property exposure of about KRW 2.03 trillion domestically and roughly KRW 1.4 trillion overseas needs monitoring alongside the timing of the European provision.

NH Investment & Securities set a target price of KRW 35,000 in a September 28, 2026 report, and SK Securities set KRW 30,000 in a November 12, 2026 report; these are the brokers' own views. The next items to confirm are Q4 results and provisions, the pace of share cancellations, and whether trading value recovers.
