Report overview

Valuation Gains Lift Earnings; Cash Generation Is the Test

Igis Residence REIT posted the largest revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of the four fiscal years from 2023 to 2026 in the fiscal year ended June 2026, but much of the gain came from property valuation gains, while operating cash flow declined over the same period.

    Revenue for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was KRW 23.6 billion, up 42.9% year on year, while operating profit reached KRW 22.5 billion (up 57.3%) and profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 19.6 billion (up 64.4%), the largest year of the four-year window for all three measures.

02

Business structure

Igis Residence REIT is a listed REIT managed by IGIS Asset Management. It listed in August 2020 with The Sharp Bupyeong, a public-support private rental housing complex of 3,578 units, as its anchor asset, and it is widely described as the only listed residential REIT in Korea.

Its portfolio, as reported, includes domestic rental housing (The Sharp Bupyeong), domestic co-living assets (Deus Pangyo, Nudit Hongdae), US rental housing (Spring Creek Towers) and a US university dormitory investment. Fair value gains on these holdings are a large part of reported income alongside rent.

A 2024 report said Deus Pangyo's revaluation added KRW 35.3 billion of valuation gains, and a February 2026 disclosure cited a KRW 7.6 billion valuation gain on a beneficiary certificate tied to Spring Creek Towers as the main driver of the revenue change.

The Deus Myeongdong sale was reported as completed with an 8.5% investment return, and the REIT is entitled to 40% of the sale gain. Peers on the same sector list, such as Macquarie Korea Infrastructure Fund, SK REIT and Lotte REIT, hold different asset types, which limits direct comparison.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 20.6 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023, KRW 20.6 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024 (up 0.4% year on year), KRW 16.5 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025 (down 20.0%), and KRW 23.6 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026 (up 42.9%).

Operating profit rose from KRW 12.1 billion to KRW 13 billion (up 7.3%), KRW 14.3 billion (up 10.2%) and KRW 22.5 billion (up 57.3%) over the same years, and it has increased for three consecutive years from the fiscal year ended June 2024 through June 2026.

Operating profit still grew in the fiscal year ended June 2025, when revenue fell, which shows how strongly reported earnings depend on valuation movements; the 2024 report on a KRW 35.3 billion Deus Pangyo valuation gain and the February 2026 disclosure of a KRW 7.6 billion gain point the same way.

The operating profit margin moved from 58.9% to 62.9%, 86.7% and 95.5%. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7.63 billion, KRW 9.29 billion (up 21.7%), KRW 12 billion (up 28.7%) and KRW 19.6 billion (up 64.4%), and net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts.

The last four quarters, from the third quarter of 2025 to the second quarter of 2026, sum to KRW 19.6 billion, matching the annual figure. Operating cash flow was -KRW 1.75 billion, KRW 3.16 billion, KRW 1.8 billion and KRW 1.02 billion over the four years, so cash generation weakened even as reported profit rose.

The debt ratio was 46.7%, 27.7%, 30.4% and 42.1%, rising in the fiscal year ended June 2026. Because a large part of profit is valuation-driven, whether operating cash flow catches up with profit is the key point for the next periodic report.

05

Industry analysis

Residential REITs have been sensitive to interest rates. In a March 2025 report the company said falling market rates had lifted the corporate bond market, and it linked this to the stability of residential assets.

A 2024 report cited an analyst at Shinhan Securities who pointed to a rate-cut-driven favorable investor sentiment toward REITs as the reason for the strong 2024 rights offering, which drew a 111.52-to-1 subscription ratio and KRW 32 billion of proceeds. The company said the proceeds would first be used to repay loans.

A 2024 analysis also expected refinancing of the then 5.6% fixed-rate debt to fall into the mid-4% range, and the 4.7% one-year bond rate issued in 2025 is consistent with that direction.

Competitively, the company is widely described as the only listed residential REIT in Korea, so few listed companies share its asset structure and direct valuation or profitability comparisons are limited.

Because its main asset is public-support private rental housing, rental terms and policy changes can affect results. Interest rates and policy therefore act on both rental operations and funding at the same time.

08

Bull factors

Earnings Scale Has Expanded for Three Years

Operating profit has risen for three straight years, from the fiscal year ended June 2024 through June 2026, and KRW 22.5 billion in the latest year is close to double the KRW 12.1 billion of the fiscal year ended June 2023.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 19.6 billion, well above KRW 7.63 billion three years earlier. An operating margin of 95.5% shows how little cost sits between revenue and operating profit in this structure.

Lower Borrowing Costs

In March 2025 the company issued one-year corporate bonds at 4.7%, which was reported as the lowest rate for A- rated listed REITs with the same maturity. A three-month short-term bond was issued the same day at 4.3%. Locking in lower funding costs during a falling-rate period is cited as a strength.

Asset Recycling and Sale Gain Sharing

Reports said the Deus Myeongdong sale completed the first full cycle of buying, operating and selling an asset for the listed REIT, with an investment return of 8.5%. The REIT is entitled to 40% of the sale gain. A September 2024 book value about 1.6 times acquisition cost is also cited as evidence of asset value growth.

09

Bear factors

Heavy Reliance on Valuation Gains

The 95.5% operating margin rests on property valuation gains. As the KRW 7.6 billion gain cited in the February 2026 disclosure shows, reported profit moves with appraisals, and a reversal in valuations would reduce operating profit by a similar amount.

Weaker Cash Generation

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 3.16 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024 to KRW 1.02 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026, while profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 9.29 billion to KRW 19.6 billion. The widening gap between profit and cash is a bearish point.

Rising Leverage and Revenue Volatility

The debt-to-equity ratio climbed to 42.1% in the fiscal year ended June 2026 from 30.4% a year earlier, with total liabilities of KRW 140.2 billion at the end of June 2026.

Revenue fell 20.0% to KRW 16.5 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025 before rising 42.9% to KRW 23.6 billion, showing large swings from year to year.

10

Risk factors

Valuation Risk

A fall in appraised values could cut operating profit and net profit at the same time. Past valuation gains came from the Pangyo co-living asset and US holdings, so changes in those markets feed directly into reported results. Changes in valuation methods or appraisal outcomes are also sources of volatility.

Interest Rate and Refinancing Risk

Because borrowing and bond issuance recur, higher rates would raise refinancing costs. The March 2026 shareholders' meeting approved borrowing and bond plans, and borrowing disclosures continued in June and July 2026. If terms turn less favorable than before, interest costs could offset part of the profit gains.

Policy and Asset Management Risk

The core asset, The Sharp Bupyeong, is public-support private rental housing, so rental terms can be shaped by policy. Asset sales and new acquisitions would change the portfolio mix, and the size of sale gains and dividends may differ from expectations. The scale and timing of new acquisitions are not specified in the material available.

Overall view

Igis Residence REIT recorded KRW 23.6 billion in revenue, KRW 22.5 billion in operating profit and KRW 19.6 billion in profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the fiscal year ended June 2026, the largest year of the four-year window on all three measures.

Operating profit has risen for three consecutive years from the fiscal year ended June 2024, and the operating margin reached 95.5%.

These gains lean heavily on property valuation gains and asset sale gains, and operating cash flow of KRW 1.02 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026 is small relative to reported profit. The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 42.1%, with total liabilities of KRW 140.2 billion at the end of June 2026.

Borrowing costs fell after the March 2025 bond issuance, and the company's status as the only listed residential REIT in Korea is cited as a strength. On the other side, results swing with asset appraisals and policy conditions.

The next checkpoints are the December 2026 dividend record date and the half-year periodic report around mid-February 2027, where the share of valuation gains and the trend in operating cash flow will show how much of the profit is cash-backed.
