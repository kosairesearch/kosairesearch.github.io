Gritee: Flat Sales, Swinging Profitability

02

Business structure

Gritee is a lifestyle wear company centered on underwear and athleisure. According to a company profile, it runs brands including Gamtan, Wonderbra, Playtex and Benetton.

In the first quarter of 2026, underwear (bras, briefs and similar) generated KRW 35.68 billion, or 92.9% of revenue, while athleisure and other apparel contributed KRW 2.74 billion (7.1%).

By sales channel in the same quarter, domestic underwear sales were KRW 35.54 billion (92.5%), and overseas sales were KRW 143 million (0.3%). A late-2023 equity research report noted that home shopping accounted for about 65% of annual revenue in 2020.

Company profiles now describe a strategy centered on its own online store, with listings on Coupang and Market Kurly as well as home shopping and some offline channels. A 2020 securities report said Gritee holds domestic and Chinese rights to HBI brands including Wonderbra and Playtex.

The largest shareholder and related parties held 34.31% as of January 22, 2025. Listed peers in the same sector include Youngone, F&F, Handsome and BYC, and Gritee's sales are weighted toward domestic underwear.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on DART-filed annual figures, revenue rose from KRW 143.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 176.1 billion in 2023 (up 23.0% year on year), KRW 194.7 billion in 2024 (up 10.6%) and KRW 201.7 billion in 2025 (up 3.6%), an increase for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. Growth slowed sharply, from 23.0% to 3.6%.

Operating profit recovered from KRW 2.87 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.1 billion in 2023 (up 287.0%), then reached KRW 12.3 billion in 2024 (up 10.7%), the largest year of 2022 through 2025, before falling to KRW 7.85 billion in 2025 (down 36.2%). Operating margin was 2.0% in 2022, 6.3% in 2023 and 2024, and 3.9% in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.02 billion in 2022, KRW 8.49 billion in 2023 (up 319.5%), KRW 9.72 billion in 2024 (up 14.5%) and KRW 4.69 billion in 2025 (down 51.7%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.84 billion in 2022 and KRW 4.69 billion in 2025, the same level as controlling-shareholder profit that year.

Quarterly, the third quarter of 2025 posted revenue of KRW 56.5 billion, operating profit of KRW 3.55 billion and controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 2.41 billion.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, revenue fell 25.4% quarter on quarter to KRW 42.1 billion, operating profit was KRW 681 million (down 80.8%), and controlling-shareholder results turned to a net loss of KRW 402 million.

The first quarter of 2026 had revenue of KRW 38.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 587 million, with a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 376 million that continued but narrowed.

The second quarter of 2026 brought revenue of KRW 59.9 billion (up 55.9% quarter on quarter), operating profit of KRW 3.69 billion (up 528.2%) and controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 3.5 billion, a swing back to profit.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 47.4 billion (down 20.9% quarter on quarter and 16.1% year on year), operating profit was KRW 2.12 billion (down 40.4% year on year) and controlling-shareholder net profit was KRW 886 million (down 63.2% year on year).

Over the last four quarters, controlling-shareholder net profit totaled KRW 3.61 billion, roughly a third more than one-third of the KRW 9.72 billion posted for 2024 as a full year.

05

Industry analysis

The underwear and athleisure market that Gritee serves is shifting its sales channels. A late-2023 equity research report put the domestic underwear market at KRW 964.6 billion in the first half of 2022 and KRW 1.37 trillion in the second half.

The same report said Gritee's 2022 growth was supported by online distribution channels. The model is moving away from heavy reliance on home shopping toward its own online store, online platforms, and department stores and mixed shopping malls.

A 2025 first-quarter report described a plan to keep expanding through major department stores and mixed shopping malls. An August 2026 news report attributed second-quarter 2026 results to growth in the company's online store and higher overseas sales of the Gamtan brand.

The competitive structure combines overseas brand licenses with the company's own brands. Compared with listed peers in the sector, Gritee's sales are concentrated in underwear, so it is more exposed to shifts in demand for that category.

Outlook

Profit has swung sharply from quarter to quarter even after the second-quarter return to profit, which makes earnings durability a bearish factor.

Risk factors

Channel concentration risk

A 2020 securities report put home shopping at about 65% of revenue, and sales have since moved toward the company's own online store and platforms. A 2026 news report said second-quarter results leaned heavily on online store growth, so a slowdown in any single channel could feed quickly into earnings. How the channel mix changes in each periodic filing is a point to track.

Balance sheet risk

The debt ratio rose sharply to 70.5% at year-end 2025 from 26.7% in 2024, with total liabilities at KRW 58.8 billion. With operating margin down to 3.9%, higher debt could weigh on profit through financing and working capital costs. The change in debt levels and operating margin will need to be read together in the 2026 annual report.

Overseas business uncertainty

Overseas sales were just 0.3% of revenue in the first quarter of 2026, and the Japan entry is still described in reports as a medium-term driver from securities analysts. The periodic filings in the disclosed record do not show a separate revenue contribution from Japan. How far the overseas share actually grows is the key variable for this growth risk.

Overall view
