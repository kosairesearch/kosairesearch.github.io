Market Leader in Debt Collection Faces Slower Quarterly Profit Growth

02

Business structure

Korea Credit Information operates three businesses: debt collection, credit investigation and civil complaint agency services. According to the Q1 2026 quarterly report, debt collection made up 90.5% of consolidated revenue, credit investigation 6.0% and civil complaint agency services 1.3%.

Debt collection revenue is calculated by multiplying the amount recovered per collected account by the contracted fee rate, and the fee rate depends on recovery difficulty, which reflects the type of claim, the length of delinquency and the debtor's condition.

At the end of 2024 the claim mix was 45% commercial, 35% financial, 10% telecom and 10% other, and the company says it has kept this mix stable.

The company has held the number one position in the debt collection industry for 12 consecutive years on the credit information association's basis, and its market share rose to 20.4% in 2024 from 19% in 2023. Credit investigation revenue was about KRW 10.8 billion in 2024, up 10.7% from the prior year.

The civil complaint agency business handles resident registration inquiries and on-site investigation work, and its share fell from 2.0% in Q1 2025 to 1.3% in Q1 2026. Its main clients are financial institutions and companies that hold claims.

The Q1 2026 earnings commentary said rising demand from financial institutions and companies to make claims management more efficient lifted revenue slightly. The company has described retaining skilled staff and expanding new claim assignments as its sales strategy.

In 2021 there were 23 domestic debt collection firms, and Korea Credit Information ranked first among them. Revenue depends most heavily on the volume of claims assigned and the recoveries achieved.

04

Earnings analysis

On filed results, annual revenue rose from KRW 146.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 178 billion in 2025. After growth of 7.8% in 2023 to KRW 158.2 billion and 8.3% in 2024 to KRW 171.3 billion, the 2025 increase slowed to 3.9%.

Revenue rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span. Operating profit grew from KRW 12 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.7 billion in 2025, after KRW 15.3 billion in 2024, up 17.1% on the prior year, and the 2025 gain was 9.3%.

The operating margin rose every year, from 8.2% in 2022 to 8.3% in 2023, 8.9% in 2024 and 9.4% in 2025. In short, revenue growth slowed while margins kept improving. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 10.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.3 billion in 2025, but the 2025 increase was only 0.2%.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts in each year. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 7.47 billion in 2022 to KRW 20 billion in 2023 and KRW 20.4 billion in 2024, then came in at KRW 17.5 billion in 2025.

Quarterly results started with revenue of KRW 45.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.53 billion in Q3 2025, and peaked with revenue of KRW 45 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.7 billion in Q4 2025.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 43.8 billion and operating profit KRW 3.69 billion, down 21.6% from the prior quarter, marking the low point. Operating profit then rose to KRW 3.85 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 3.99 billion in Q3 2026, two consecutive quarterly gains, but Q3 2026 remained below Q3 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.45 billion in Q3 2026 fell 7.2% from a year earlier. Over the last four quarters, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 12.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

A domestic brokerage's April 2022 report described the debt collection market as steadily growing, from KRW 686.5 billion in 2019 to KRW 735.6 billion in 2020 and KRW 760.4 billion in 2021. The same report put the company's 2021 market share at 17.2% and ranked it first among 23 firms.

Its share then rose to 19% in 2023 and 20.4% in 2024. The report cited the expansion of internet banks, which focus on mid-rate lending, as a medium- to long-term opportunity for debt collectors.

Demand from financial institutions and companies for more efficient claims management was also cited as a driver of revenue in the Q1 2026 earnings commentary. In terms of cycle, debt collection volumes and recoveries tend to move with the economy and delinquency levels.

A slowdown can increase delinquent claims and create assignment opportunities, but weaker recovery conditions can reduce fee-based income. Competitively, the company holds the top share, while its credit investigation and civil complaint agency businesses are relatively small.

The lower weight of civil complaint services means greater focus on the core business, though it also limits revenue diversification. For this industry, the usual way to read the company is to track both industry growth and changes in its own share.

06

Outlook

The core item in the company's 2026 corporate value enhancement plan is managing its payout ratio at 40% or higher. In the same plan the company said it will raise capital efficiency through cost structure streamlining and tighter control of operating expenses.

It also disclosed that it will strengthen internal controls and review risks related to information protection. The company had earlier set goals for 2025 that included upgrading its claims management system and realizing shareholder returns.

The system upgrade has been described as a way to improve assignment management and recovery efficiency. Dividends are paid on a quarterly basis, with interim record date notices and quarterly dividend decision disclosures following in 2026.

On results, operating profit rose in both Q2 and Q3 2026, which reads as a recovery from the Q1 low. However, Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.99 billion remained below KRW 4.53 billion in Q3 2025, so the recovery has not yet returned to the prior-year level.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 43.9 billion was down 3.6% from a year earlier. The next checkpoint is the full-year operating margin and dividend delivery to be confirmed in the 2026 annual report. Q4 2026 operating profit should also be compared with KRW 4.7 billion in Q4 2025.

Whether the claims system upgrade lifts assignment volumes and recovery rates will shape the medium-term earnings path.

Bull factors

Leading position and rising share

The market grew from KRW 686.5 billion in 2019 to KRW 760.4 billion in 2021, and mid-rate lending expansion has been cited as a medium- to long-term opportunity.

Improving margins and balance sheet

The operating margin rose every year, from 8.2% in 2022 to 9.4% in 2025. The debt ratio fell over the same period from 122.0% to 81.5%. Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 17.5 billion, compared with KRW 7.47 billion in 2022.

Shareholder return plan and quarterly dividends

How the payout ratio management target in the 2026 corporate value enhancement plan is reflected in subsequent quarterly dividends is the point to check. The same plan pairs this with cost structure streamlining and tighter operating expense control. Quarterly dividend decisions are being disclosed, so dividends are running on a quarterly basis.

Operating profit rose for two straight quarters after the Q1 2026 low but has not returned to KRW 4.53 billion in Q3 2025.

Slowing revenue growth

Revenue growth slowed from 7.8% in 2023 and 8.3% in 2024 to 3.9% in 2025. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 43.9 billion fell 3.6% from a year earlier. Quarterly revenue declined from Q4 2025 through Q2 2026 before a 1.3% sequential gain in Q3.

Concentration and fee-based model

Debt collection accounts for more than 90% of revenue, so the company depends heavily on a single business. Revenue is set by the amount recovered per account multiplied by the contracted fee rate, making it sensitive to recovery conditions.

The civil complaint agency share fell from 2.0% in Q1 2025 to 1.3% in Q1 2026, narrowing revenue diversification.

10

Risk factors

Recovery environment and fee rates

The contracted fee rate depends on recovery difficulty, which reflects claim type, length of delinquency and debtor condition. If recovery becomes harder, both fee rates and recovered amounts can decline.

A deterioration in debtor conditions during an economic downturn can reduce profit even at the same assignment volume.

Information protection and internal controls

The company said in its 2026 corporate value enhancement plan that it will strengthen internal controls and review information protection risks. Debt collection involves handling personal debt information, so any data management incident could directly affect reputation and business. The risk may not show up in results immediately, but the outcome of these reviews is an important checkpoint.

Financial cycle and regulation

Debt collection volumes depend on the claims held by financial institutions and companies and on delinquency trends. Changes in the financial cycle can shift the mix of claims and assignment opportunities.

The client claim mix of 45% commercial, 35% financial, 10% telecom and 10% other is not heavily skewed to one sector, which provides some buffer.

Overall view

Korea Credit Information is a credit information specialist in which debt collection accounts for more than 90% of revenue, and it has held the top position in the debt collection industry for 12 consecutive years.

On filed results, revenue, operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders each rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and the operating margin climbed from 8.2% in 2022 to 9.4% in 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 122.0% to 81.5%, and total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 63 billion. On the other hand, quarterly profit has been mixed in 2026. Operating profit rose for two straight quarters after the Q1 low, but Q3 2026 operating profit was down 12.1% from a year earlier.

Revenue growth also slowed from 7.8% in 2023 and 8.3% in 2024 to 3.9% in 2025. Compared with the historical year-end multiple range, current multiples sit below its bottom, and the company has published a plan to manage its payout ratio at 40% or higher.

The next checkpoints are the full-year operating margin and dividend delivery in the 2026 annual report, and whether operating profit recovers in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
