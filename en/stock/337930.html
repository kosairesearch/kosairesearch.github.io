Xexymix: Overseas Growth vs. Margin Recovery

Overseas subsidiaries and Southeast Asian sales keep growing, but Xexymix's cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 fell year on year, so margin recovery is the central question.

  1. 1

    Cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 1,886 billion, down 3.4% year on year, and cumulative operating profit was KRW 93.7 billion.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Xexymix operates an athleisure brand selling both activewear and everyday wear, and after its 2025 name change it has positioned itself as a dedicated athleisure company.

The business is split into domestic self-operated online mall and offline stores, three overseas subsidiaries in Japan, Taiwan and China, and overseas e-commerce platforms plus local distribution partners.

Revenue from the three overseas subsidiaries rose 29.3% to KRW 32.0 billion in 2025, and the export share of total revenue stayed in the 10% range, up 3 percentage points.

Running apparel revenue rose 117% to KRW 19.6 billion in 2025 and became a core category, while innerwear revenue grew more than 186% and exceeded KRW 10.6 billion. According to the 2026 third-quarter release, running's share of domestic revenue rose from 7% to 9%, and innerwear's share rose from 4% to 7%.

Average transaction value at domestic self-operated online mall and offline stores has risen for five consecutive years, with the first three quarters of 2026 showing gains of 5.4% and 6.6% respectively.

Japan is the fastest-growing overseas market, with third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 5.41 billion, up 33.5% year on year, and cumulative revenue of KRW 16.5 billion, up 37.9%, as the company added regular stores in Osaka Shinsaibashi and Tokyo Jiyugaoka.

Taiwan's cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 7.5 billion, up 10.3%, and the company runs regular stores alongside pop-up stores there. In Southeast Asia, Indonesia's first-half 2026 revenue more than doubled, and Thailand supplies 67 Makro stores through CP AXTRA. Domestic peers in the same sector include Youngone, F&F, Handsome, LF and Hansae.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 212.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 232.6 billion in 2023, KRW 271.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 274.1 billion in 2025, marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025. However, the growth rate slowed to 0.9% in 2025.

Operating profit rose slightly from KRW 17.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 18.2 billion in 2023, peaked at KRW 24.9 billion in 2024 (up 36.9% year on year), and fell 30.3% to KRW 17.3 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 8.4% in 2022 to 7.8% in 2023, 9.2% in 2024 and 6.3% in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 profit decline to higher selling and administrative expenses, including marketing and new store openings.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 43.0% to KRW 11.8 billion in 2025 from KRW 20.7 billion in 2024, while net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 12.2 billion.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 119.6 billion, equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 116.2 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 55.8 billion, with the debt ratio easing from 50.8% in 2024 to 46.7% in 2025.

Operating cash flow dropped from KRW 29.8 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.72 billion in 2025. On a quarterly basis, revenue rose from KRW 69.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 78.9 billion in the fourth quarter, up 13.0% quarter on quarter, but operating profit fell 51.5% quarter on quarter to KRW 2.94 billion.

Revenue then dropped to KRW 53 billion in the first quarter of 2026, with operating profit of KRW 1.1 billion, the low point of the period. The second quarter recovered to KRW 74.4 billion in revenue and KRW 5.35 billion in operating profit, lifting the operating margin from 2.1% to 7.2%.

The third quarter revenue of KRW 61.2 billion fell 12.3% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 2.92 billion fell 51.8%, leaving a margin of 4.8%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 920 million in the third quarter, down 69.8% year on year, and totaled KRW 8.52 billion over the last four quarters. The first-half 2026 profit decline was attributed to upfront advertising spending from a large brand campaign and overseas store openings.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic athleisure demand is being shaped by the spread of running as a pastime. The company said the running trend strengthened sales stability even in the third quarter, which is traditionally a slow season for athleisure brands.

Running apparel revenue rose 117% to KRW 19.6 billion in 2025, and the company plans to expand its RX running lineup and sponsor running events in 2026. Early 2026 plans also called for a stronger men's category and a new gym wear NX line, in response to rising demand for men's products at home and abroad.

The company said it kept growing in 2025 despite slowing consumption in Korea and overseas. Overseas, Japan and Taiwan have grown mainly through offline stores and pop-ups, while Southeast Asia is moving into a full-scale phase with online and offline sales in Indonesia and entry into large Thai retail channels.

In competition, the company can be compared with listed peers in the same sector such as Youngone, F&F, Handsome, Hansae and BYC, and in overseas markets the pace of securing local sales channels appears to be the key variable.

The export share remains in the 10% range, so the company is still in the process of increasing its overseas weight within a business that has been domestically centered.

06

Outlook

The company said it will develop Southeast Asia into a new global growth axis in the second half of 2026.

Indonesia's revenue more than doubled in the first half of 2026, and the company plans to link its Jakarta flagship store, opened in June, with e-commerce platforms such as Shopee, Tokopedia and Lazada as well as its own online mall.

In Thailand, a sales agreement with CP AXTRA was signed in June 2026, with products to be placed in Makro and Lotus's stores from late August 2026 and a local online mall to follow.

In Korea, the company plans to widen its RX running lineup and strengthen its running community through race sponsorships and participation programs. In Japan, it opened its seventh regular store in Tokyo Jiyugaoka in September 2026 as offline expansion continues.

As an external outlook, the Korea Investors Service research center projected in a March 2026 report that consolidated 2026 revenue would reach KRW 304.1 billion and operating profit KRW 29.3 billion, with overseas subsidiary revenue of KRW 53.5 billion, up 66.7%.

Cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 1,886 billion and cumulative operating profit was KRW 93.7 billion, leaving a wide gap to that outlook. The company has named cost structure improvement and organizational restructuring as its 2026 priorities. The next checkpoint is how full-year results in the 2026 annual report compare with that outlook.

Bull factors

Overseas subsidiaries keep growing

Japan subsidiary revenue rose 33.5% year on year to KRW 5.41 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and cumulative revenue reached KRW 16.5 billion, up 37.9%. Taiwan's cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 7.5 billion, up 10.3%.

Revenue from the three overseas subsidiaries rose 29.3% to KRW 32.0 billion in 2025, confirming that overseas expansion is supporting top-line growth.

Running and innerwear categories expand

Running apparel revenue rose 117% to KRW 19.6 billion in 2025. Innerwear revenue grew more than 186% and exceeded KRW 10.6 billion. The third-quarter 2026 release showed running at 9% and innerwear at 7% of domestic revenue, indicating ongoing product diversification.

Domestic transaction value up, Q2 rebound

Average transaction value at domestic online mall and offline stores has risen for five consecutive years. Cumulative gains through the first three quarters of 2026 were 5.4% for the online mall and 6.6% for offline stores. The operating margin rebounded to 7.2% in the second quarter of 2026 from 2.1% in the first quarter.

Bear factors

Third-quarter decline and profit drop

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 61.2 billion fell 12.3% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 2.92 billion fell 51.8%. Cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 1,886 billion, down 3.4%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 69.8% to KRW 920 million in the third quarter, and press coverage noted that overseas growth in Japan did not offset the overall decline.

Margin decline and thinner cash flow

The operating margin declined from 9.2% in 2024 to 6.3% in 2025. Operating cash flow also fell sharply alongside the margin, and whether it recovers is the next point to check.

The company attributed the first-half 2026 profit decline to upfront advertising spending, including a brand campaign with the model and overseas store openings.

Gap between outlook and cumulative results

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 8.52 billion over the last four quarters.

Compared with the KRW 29.3 billion full-year 2026 operating profit projected in a March 2026 report by the Korea Investors Service research center, cumulative operating profit of KRW 93.7 billion for the first three quarters leaves a wide gap. A slower-than-projected recovery is the basis of this bear case.

10

Risk factors

Overseas expansion execution

Store expansion in Japan and Taiwan and market entry in Indonesia and Thailand carry operating and promotional costs. Thailand sales depend heavily on the CP AXTRA channel, so partner performance could largely determine revenue there.

With the export share still in the 10% range, whether overseas growth can absorb domestic weakness remains to be confirmed.

Marketing payback risk

The first-half 2026 profit decline was explained mainly by upfront advertising spending on a large brand campaign and overseas store openings. If sales catch up slowly to this spending, the margin recovery could be delayed. The operating margin was 4.8% in the third quarter of 2026, a low level.

Cash flow and dividend sustainability

Operating cash flow fell to KRW 2.72 billion in 2025 from KRW 29.8 billion in 2024. The debt ratio eased to 46.7% in 2025 from 50.8% in 2024, but debt levels still need monitoring. Dividend sustainability depends on whether earnings and cash flow recover.

Overall view

Bullish factors include growth at the Japan and Taiwan subsidiaries, rising sales in Indonesia and Thailand, expansion in running and innerwear, and higher domestic transaction value.

Bearish factors include year-on-year declines in third-quarter 2026 revenue and operating profit, a sharp drop in 2025 operating cash flow, and a modest earnings base with KRW 8.52 billion in net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters.
