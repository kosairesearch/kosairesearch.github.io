Report overview

Losses Persist Under Rehabilitation Despite a Profitable Quarter

STX is under rehabilitation proceedings, with sales shrinking and operating losses continuing, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive in the third quarter of 2026.

  1. 1

    STX filed for rehabilitation proceedings with the Seoul Rehabilitation Court on December 19, 2025, signed a conditional investment contract on March 24, 2026, and received court approval on March 27.

  2. 2

    Sales fell from KRW 1.19 trillion in 2022 to KRW 641.3 billion in 2025, declining for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

  3. 3

    Operating losses have continued for four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025, with the 2025 operating loss at KRW 61.9 billion.

  4. 4

    Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 322.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026, while that quarter still posted an operating loss of KRW 1.48 billion. The four-quarter total is KRW 94.9 billion.

  5. 5

    At the end of 2025, total equity stood at KRW 49.4 billion against total liabilities of KRW 510.5 billion.

02

Business structure

STX is a general trading company that runs raw materials import and export, energy, and machinery and engine businesses, and the raw materials import and export business is the largest of these segments.

Sales fell to KRW 641.3 billion in 2025 from KRW 1.19 trillion in 2022, slightly more than half of the earlier level.

The consolidated group includes STX Resort, which handles resort development, construction and operation, STX Aero Service, which handles aviation maintenance, and a valve manufacturing and sales subsidiary.

Overseas, the company operates trading and wholesale entities in Singapore and Vietnam, and its Malaysian entity handles a sawn-timber business. The many overseas bases make its trading-house structure clear, and a large share of its revenue is exposed to raw material prices and exchange rates.

In defense-related business, a Peru armored vehicle procurement project has been cited, and the company moved to ease distrust in Peru by indicating that its rehabilitation filing does not affect that project.

POSCO International, LX International and SKNetworks are cited as comparable trading companies, but because STX is in rehabilitation, restructuring its capital base comes before expanding the business.

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 510.5 billion against total equity of KRW 49.4 billion, a thin capital base for the size of its operations.

04

Earnings analysis

In the annual results disclosed in DART filings, sales fell from KRW 1.19 trillion in 2022 to KRW 903.8 billion in 2023 (down 24.0% year on year), KRW 827.8 billion in 2024 (down 8.4%), and KRW 641.3 billion in 2025 (down 22.5%), declining for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Within 2022 through 2025, 2022 was the largest year for sales and 2025 the smallest.

Operating results were a loss of KRW 1.96 billion in 2022, a loss of KRW 15 billion in 2023 (losses continued and widened), a loss of KRW 8.71 billion in 2024 (losses continued and narrowed), and a loss of KRW 61.9 billion in 2025 (losses continued and widened), which makes four consecutive years of operating losses from 2022 through 2025.

The operating margin moved from -0.2% in 2022 to -9.7% in 2025, and the largest decline also came in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 13.6 billion in 2022, KRW 50.5 billion in 2023, KRW 44 billion in 2024 and KRW 166.2 billion in 2025, four consecutive years of losses.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 166.4 billion in 2025, close in size to the controlling-shareholder loss. Operating cash flow moved from minus KRW 84.6 billion in 2022 and minus KRW 61.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 35 billion in 2024 and KRW 34.5 billion in 2025, turning positive.

On a quarterly basis, sales went from KRW 182.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 122.4 billion in the fourth quarter (down 33.0% from the prior quarter), KRW 60 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 51.0%), KRW 42.4 billion in the second quarter and KRW 40.9 billion in the third quarter (down 77.6% year on year), a decline for four consecutive quarters.

The operating loss was largest at KRW 43.9 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and narrowed to KRW 1.48 billion in the third quarter of 2026. Of the KRW 166.2 billion controlling-shareholder loss for 2025, KRW 126 billion was booked in the fourth quarter alone.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 322.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026, even though the operating loss continued, so the composition of non-operating items is central to interpreting that result.

05

Industry analysis

The general trading industry is structured so that sales and margins swing sharply with raw material prices, exchange rates and overseas demand. STX's sales decline may reflect both industry-wide factors and company-specific factors.

The energy business tends to see large earnings swings as oil prices move, and the machinery and engine business is affected by the equipment investment cycle. The Peru armored vehicle procurement project is an item that can indicate medium-term order flow in defense-related business.

Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 40.9 billion were down 77.6% year on year, so a recovery in top-line volume has not yet appeared in the figures. In cycle terms, the position is one where a contraction in raw material trading overlaps with the rehabilitation process.

Compared with POSCO International, LX International and SKNetworks, STX has a narrower range of strategic options while rehabilitation is under way. Total equity of KRW 49.4 billion is thin relative to total liabilities of KRW 510.5 billion, which also constrains its position within the industry.

From an industry standpoint, benefits from any upturn in the sector would reach STX's earnings only after its capital structure is reorganized.

06

Outlook

Under the rehabilitation process, STX is carrying out the follow-up steps of a conditional investment contract signed on March 24, 2026. The contract is structured as a sale process, with an accounting firm appointed as lead manager for the transaction, and the counterparty is a buyer consortium.

Reports say a court approved a rehabilitation plan and that delisting procedures are proceeding in parallel. A large third-party allotment rights offering has also been reported.

The exchange's improvement plan implementation report was submitted on June 30, 2026, and the listing committee review was scheduled for July 29, 2026. On the Peru armored vehicle procurement project, the company has moved to reassure local counterparts that the rehabilitation filing does not affect it.

Whether the items behind the third-quarter 2026 swing to net profit are one-off in nature, and whether operating results recover in the fourth quarter and beyond, are the central variables for the outlook.

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 43.9 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.48 billion in the third quarter of 2026, but quarterly swings are large, so a trend cannot be assumed.

Once the final execution of the transaction and the terms of the rights offering are fixed, the direction of the shareholder structure and the financial structure will be set together.

Possible capital restructuring through rehabilitation

STX signed a conditional investment contract on March 24, 2026, received court approval on March 27, and has been reported to have carried out a third-party allotment rights offering. If the sale and the offering are executed, the room to reduce debt and rebuild the capital base would open up.

Business base with subsidiaries and overseas bases

The company runs raw materials trading, energy, and machinery and engine businesses, and holds subsidiaries in resort development, aviation maintenance and valve manufacturing. On the Peru armored vehicle procurement project, the company has indicated that its rehabilitation filing does not affect that business.

Bear factors

Three straight years of sales decline

Annual sales have fallen every year since 2022, leaving a lower revenue base. Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 40.9 billion were down 77.6% year on year, so the contraction in revenue scale is continuing.

Four years of operating losses and a thin capital base

Operating losses continued for four consecutive years through 2025, with the 2025 operating loss at KRW 61.9 billion. Against total liabilities of KRW 510.5 billion, total equity of KRW 49.4 billion leaves a heavy financial burden.

Doubts over whether the profit swing will last

The third-quarter 2026 profit was recorded while the operating loss continued. Compared with the controlling-shareholder losses of KRW 44.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 57.1 billion in the second quarter, it is a single-quarter change, so it is too early to say the earnings flow has stabilized.

The third quarter of 2026 produced net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 322.1 billion, but the same quarter still carried an operating loss of KRW 1.48 billion, and the four-quarter total is KRW 94.9 billion.
