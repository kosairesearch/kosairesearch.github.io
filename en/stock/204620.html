Report overview

Tax-refund leader's next test is overseas expansion

02

Business structure

Global Tax Free is a tax-refund agent founded in 2005 whose core service helps foreign tourists recover domestic consumption tax paid at post-purchase duty-free shops.

Domestic fee revenue depends on the number of inbound visitors and fee revenue per visitor, and SK Securities analyzed that both metrics are improving together.

Fee revenue per Chinese visitor, who account for 41% of total fee revenue, rose from KRW 7,009 in the second quarter of 2024 to KRW 9,094 in the second quarter of 2025 and KRW 9,423 in the second quarter of 2026.

Major merchants include Olive Young and three domestic department store groups, so cosmetics and department store categories directly capture foreign spending. Subsidiaries Swanicoco and Fingerstory carry on cosmetics manufacturing and sales and a webtoon platform business.

Based on an SK Securities report, first-quarter 2026 regional sales were KRW 30.8 billion in Korea, KRW 3.9 billion in Singapore and KRW 0.8 billion in Japan, so overseas weight is still small. Overseas, the company operates the central refund counter at Changi Airport in Singapore.

In Japan, Planet, the second-largest global tax-refund operator, has taken an equity stake in the Japanese corporate unit and is working with the company to secure luxury brand merchants. Thailand is at the stage of preparing a full bid after receiving pre-qualification.

SK Securities estimated that cosmetic-surgery VAT refund sales accounted for about 20% on average in 2025. The company describes itself as the number one domestic tax-refund company.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show sales and profit growing together. Sales rose from KRW 49.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 93.4 billion in 2023 (up 89.2%), KRW 129.7 billion in 2024 (up 38.9%) and KRW 154.1 billion in 2025 (up 18.8%), an increase in each year from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 2.72 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 14.8 billion in 2023, the turn to profitability, then KRW 21.8 billion in 2024 (up 47.3%) and KRW 29 billion in 2025 (up 33.1%), two consecutive years of growth from 2024 to 2025.

Operating margin rose from -5.5% in 2022 to 15.8% in 2023, 16.8% in 2024 and 18.8% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders stayed negative at a loss of KRW 11.2 billion in 2022 and KRW 8.88 billion in 2023, turned to a profit of KRW 5.58 billion in 2024, and reached KRW 34.7 billion in 2025 (up 522.5%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 35.5 billion in 2025 and should be read separately from profit attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow moved from -KRW 10 billion in 2022 and -KRW 8.09 billion in 2023 to KRW 14.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 40.5 billion in 2025.

The company stated that 2025 consolidated sales and operating profit were record levels.

Quarterly sales peaked at KRW 45.1 billion in the third quarter of 2025, fell to KRW 29.7 billion in the fourth quarter (down 34.0% from the prior quarter), recovered to KRW 46.9 billion in the second quarter of 2026, and reached KRW 45.6 billion in the third quarter (down 2.8% from the prior quarter).

Fourth-quarter 2025 profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 127.6% to KRW 13.7 billion while operating profit was KRW 7.39 billion, so the two measures moved in opposite directions.

In the first quarter of 2026, SK Securities estimated that real domestic growth was about 50% after excluding roughly KRW 4.5 billion of cosmetic-surgery-related refund sales in the first quarter of 2025. Second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 9.04 billion rose 55.3% from the prior quarter. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 37.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Inbound foreign consumption, the demand base for the business, is growing strongly.

Cumulative foreign visitors from January to May 2026 reached 8.72 million, up 21% year on year, and visitors from China (2.56 million), Japan (1.6 million) and Taiwan (920,000) each rose 25%, according to the company's July 2026 announcement.

A Hankyung report from April 2026 said Chinese visitor demand to Korea has been building since the easing of travel restrictions. Overseas markets are at different stages. SK Securities noted that Thailand has roughly twice Korea's annual foreign visitors while its tax-refund infrastructure is still at an early stage.

In Singapore, the business could widen from store and airport refunds to a central system covering refund approval and settlement. For Japan, SK Securities estimated a new market of KRW 300 billion to KRW 600 billion a year once the post-purchase refund system takes full effect.

In competition, Planet, the second-largest global operator, is both a rival and, in Japan, a partner. In Korea, Olive Young and department store channels are cited as the merchant network through which foreign spending is captured.

Domestic growth, however, depends on visitor numbers and per-person spending, so it is exposed to diplomatic and policy variables.

06

Outlook

In a December 2025 statement, the company said that after the cosmetic-surgery refund expiry, sales and operating profit in 2026 would keep rising from 2025 levels. In July 2026 it said the second half is the traditional peak inbound season and that a further earnings rally could be expected.

Securities estimates point the same way. In a September 2026 report, SK Securities forecast 2026 sales of KRW 172 billion and operating profit of KRW 32.6 billion, up 11.6% and 12.5% year on year.

The same report said early overseas revenue, including Japan, would begin to be recognized in the fourth quarter of 2026, with a full-year contribution possible in 2027.

Japan depends on the joint work with Planet and on securing luxury brand merchants, and the pace of merchant onboarding will determine the scale of early sales. SK Securities sees Singapore's scope widening into a central system, and Thailand's full bid results could add meaningful earnings from 2027.

Because the outlook mixes company statements and brokerage estimates, the actual figures should be confirmed in the 2026 annual report. How quickly overseas revenue grows as a share of the total is the next thing to watch.

08

Bull factors

Structural growth in inbound demand

Foreign visitors from January to May 2026 reached 8.72 million, up 21%, and fee revenue per Chinese visitor rose to KRW 9,423 in the second quarter of 2026 from KRW 7,009 in the second quarter of 2024. Visitor growth and higher per-person spending are filling the gap left by the expired cosmetic-surgery refund. Operating margin improved to 18.8% in 2025 from -5.5% in 2022.

Overseas options led by Japan

Whether Japan's post-purchase refund system is fully implemented is the variable that determines the size of the overseas option. Planet has taken an equity stake in the Japanese unit and is combining its luxury brand network with the company's operations.

Possible expansion of Singapore's central system and Thailand's early-stage infrastructure are also cited as overseas options.

Turn in operating cash flow

Operating cash flow turned from negative through 2023 to positive from 2024. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 37.6% in 2022 to 31.6% in 2025. Total equity was KRW 139.9 billion at the end of 2025. Earnings capacity and financial stability have improved together.

09

Bear factors

Gap from the cosmetic-surgery refund expiry

The cosmetic-surgery VAT refund expired in January 2026, and SK Securities estimated this business made up about 20% of sales on average in 2025. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 7.92 billion fell 4.0% from a year earlier.

The company and brokers say visitor growth is filling the gap, but the size of the lost contribution remains a variable in the results.

High volatility in quarterly profit

Fourth-quarter 2025 sales of KRW 29.7 billion fell 34.0% from the prior quarter. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.09 billion in the first quarter of 2026, down 62.7% from the prior quarter, and KRW 6.58 billion in the third quarter, down 45.2%. Quarterly direction changes often, which makes trend judgment harder.

Market trust and the lag in overseas business

In a February 2026 report, SK Securities cited a loss of capital-market trust tied to the change in the largest shareholder. Overseas business is still small, with first-quarter 2026 Japan sales of KRW 0.8 billion, and Singapore and Thailand contributions depend on bid results and may only appear from 2027. If overseas contributions are slow, there is less buffer against domestic swings.

10

Risk factors

Policy and regulatory risk

As with the expiry of the cosmetic-surgery refund, changes to refund rules would alter the fee revenue structure. In Japan and Singapore, reform timing and bid outcomes determine the scope of operations. Results are directly exposed to how each country designs its refund system.

Tourism demand and diplomatic risk

Domestic results depend on foreign visitor numbers and per-person spending, led by visitors from China, Japan and Taiwan. Diplomatic disputes between countries have altered travel flows, as the reference to the easing of travel restrictions in recent reporting shows. The greater the reliance on a few nationalities, the larger the impact of a demand shift.

Overseas execution and governance risk

Early Japan revenue depends on the partnership with Planet and the pace of merchant onboarding. Singapore and Thailand scope will be defined only once bid results are final. Trust concerns raised around the change in the largest shareholder could affect financing for overseas expansion and investor sentiment.

12

Overall view

Global Tax Free reported 2025 sales of KRW 154.1 billion, operating profit of KRW 29 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 34.7 billion, supported by the structural rise in foreign visitors.

In 2026 it kept quarterly sales between KRW 35.5 billion and KRW 46.9 billion by offsetting the cosmetic-surgery refund expiry with visitor growth. However, third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 4.0% from a year earlier, and quarterly profit attributable to controlling shareholders has swung widely.

Overseas, the full start of Japan's post-purchase refund system, the partnership with Planet and the Singapore and Thailand bids are cited as the next growth drivers.

SK Securities expects overseas contributions to become meaningful in 2027, so how the company bridges the timing gap between domestic results and overseas expansion is the next thing to watch.

Operating margin improved from -5.5% in 2022 to 18.8% in 2025, and operating cash flow turned positive at KRW 40.5 billion in 2025. Valuation should be read alongside the earnings trend, and the next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report, first-quarter 2027 results and the overseas bid outcomes.
