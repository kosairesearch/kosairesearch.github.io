02

Business structure

TCC Steel is a surface-treated steel sheet manufacturer. Its business is split between surface-treated steel and other segments such as real estate leasing, per press reports.

Within surface-treated steel, tin-plated and chrome-treated steel are the core product lines, accounting for 72% of revenue according to a June 2026 report. Average selling prices for these products fell from KRW 1,689,000 per ton in 2024 to KRW 1,612,000 in Q1 2026.

Nickel-plated steel is used in battery cases and automotive tubes, and the company has positioned it as its new growth axis. Export share is 62%, with East Asia and the Americas as the main markets. Dongkuk Industries Company announced a nickel-plated steel facility plan in 2022, and competition has been intensifying.

In 2026 the company supplies 46-series battery can material to LG Energy Solution and Samsung SDI. Shares held by the largest shareholder group, led by chairman Son Bong-rak, stood at 30.88% in a 2025 report.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue went from KRW 684.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 624.4 billion in 2023, down 8.8% year on year, and then to KRW 582.1 billion in 2024, down 6.8%. It rebounded to KRW 611.5 billion in 2025, up 5.1%. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Profit moved far more sharply than revenue. Operating profit fell from KRW 44 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.3 billion in 2023, a 74.4% decline, rose 9.1% to KRW 12.3 billion in 2024, and turned into an operating loss of KRW 10.9 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 earnings swing mainly to weak demand in downstream industries and the effect of steel tariffs on profitability. Operating margin went from 6.4% in 2022 to 1.8% in 2023, 2.1% in 2024 and -1.8% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 29.3 billion in 2022 to a KRW 7.75 billion loss in 2023, back to a KRW 20.2 billion profit in 2024, and to a KRW 7.44 billion loss in 2025.

Profit for the year, which includes non-controlling interests, was a KRW 7.6 billion loss in 2025 and differs from the controlling-shareholder figure. Operating cash flow stayed positive, rising from KRW -12 billion in 2022 to KRW 25 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue grew for four consecutive quarters from KRW 148.6 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 190.4 billion in Q3 2026.

Over the same span, operating losses continued in Q4 2025 at KRW 3.91 billion, Q1 2026 at KRW 3.98 billion and Q3 2026 at KRW 8.15 billion, while Q2 2026 was the only quarter that turned to an operating profit of KRW 1.17 billion.

Press reports said Q1 2026 production fell 7% year on year and plant utilization dropped from 63.0% to 56.9%. The central issue in the results is that revenue growth has not yet translated into profit improvement.

05

Industry analysis

The tin-plated steel market depends on the canned beverage industry, where Japanese and Chinese producers are expanding into Southeast Asia. A 2025 brokerage analysis also pointed to this trend as a burden on export volumes. The company says Chinese low-priced imports and US tariffs have intensified price competition.

Falling average selling prices combined with lower utilization suggest the industry is still at an early stage of stabilization. A 2025 brokerage analysis judged the cylindrical battery market downstream of nickel-plated steel to be weak.

In a 2026 report, a company official said the secondary battery market is gradually recovering. Brokerages in 2025 identified Tesla's sales normalization as the key variable for nickel-plated steel demand. The company says the end of the US quota system opened room to sell more volume.

A 2023 report noted that its position as the only domestic nickel-plated steel producer could weaken as new entrants appear. With a 62% export share, exchange rates and tariff policy have a direct effect on results.

Against peers, the company leads with nickel-plated steel capacity, while its tin-plated business is exposed to price competition.

06

Outlook

According to a company analysis profile, the company aims for 46-series nickel-plated steel shipments to ramp in the second half of 2026 and for annual revenue recognition of about 5,000 tons. The same profile says revenue recognition for Samsung SDI's 46-series battery production began in March 2026.

The company started supplying small volumes of 46-series nickel-plated steel in the first quarter and expects utilization and volumes to rise from the second half as capacity expands.

In 2025, Heungkuk Securities estimated 2026 demand for 46-series nickel-plated steel at about 26,000 tons, more than three times the 2025 level. The same report projected 2026 revenue of KRW 600 billion and operating profit of KRW 17.1 billion.

The Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 8.15 billion runs counter to that profit path. A 2025 DB Securities note also cited demand from LG Energy Solution's Arizona plant. The next checkpoints are whether 46-series revenue approaches the annual goal and whether utilization recovers from the 56.9% level reported for Q1 2026. It also remains to be seen whether quarterly results return to losses after the Q2 2026 operating profit.

08

Bull factors

Expanding 46-series supply

Supply of nickel-plated steel as battery can material to LG Energy Solution and Samsung SDI for 46-series cells began in earnest in 2026. The company targets second-half shipment gains and annual revenue recognition of about 5,000 tons. The bull case rests on room for volume and utilization to improve if cylindrical battery demand recovers.

Four straight quarters of revenue growth

On a disclosed basis, revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from KRW 148.6 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 190.4 billion in Q3 2026, up 28.1% year on year in the latest quarter. Q2 2026 also produced an operating profit of KRW 1.17 billion. If top-line recovery carries into profit, the bull case gains support.

End of US quota and export base

Exports account for 62% of sales, with East Asia and the Americas as the main markets. The company says the end of the US quota system opened room to sell more volume. It also noted that tariffs raised export unit prices. An established overseas sales base is a bull-case factor.

09

Bear factors

Widening operating and net losses

The Q3 2026 operating loss was KRW 8.15 billion and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 11.6 billion, both wider. The last four quarters total KRW 24.8 billion in net loss attributable to controlling shareholders. The bear case centers on revenue growth not improving profit.

Price declines and lower utilization

Average selling prices for tin-plated steel fell from KRW 1,689,000 per ton in 2024 to KRW 1,612,000 in Q1 2026. Utilization dropped from 63.0% to 56.9% and production fell 7% year on year in the same quarter. Continued low-priced Chinese imports could keep pressure on prices.

Uncertain battery demand

Press reports say the secondary battery slump has weighed on the growth product as well. If Tesla's sales normalization is delayed, the 46-series demand estimates could be revised.

10

Risk factors

Raw materials and tariffs

Raw materials include steel sheet, nickel and tin. The company cited steel tariffs as one cause of the 2025 profit decline. With a 62% export share, changes in US tariff policy would affect both prices and volumes.

Customer concentration and downstream dependence

Supply of 46-series nickel-plated steel depends on a small number of battery customers, including LG Energy Solution and Samsung SDI. Delays in a customer's mass production schedule could push back revenue recognition. Winning additional 46-series customers would shape the growth pace.

Financial burden and new competition

Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 325.2 billion against total equity of KRW 248.9 billion, with a debt ratio of 130.7%. Continued losses would reduce room for interest burden and investment. Competition has also intensified since Dongkuk Industries Company announced nickel-plated steel facility plans.

Overall view

TCC Steel is a surface-treated steel processor building a new growth axis by supplying nickel-plated steel as 46-series battery material. On a disclosed basis, 2025 annual revenue rose to KRW 611.5 billion, but the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 10.9 billion.

Quarterly revenue has grown for four straight quarters since Q3 2025, yet operating and net losses continued through Q3 2026. Lower selling prices for tin-plated steel, Chinese imports and tariffs are weighing on profitability, and utilization fell to 56.9% in Q1 2026.
