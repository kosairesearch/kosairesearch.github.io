02

Business structure

Ssial Food is a food manufacturer founded in September 2007, headquartered in Bio Valley 1-ro, Jecheon, Chungcheongbuk-do. Its core business is manufacturing and selling cereals and cereal bars made from grains, nuts and vitamins.

Based on the 2020 business report, cereals accounted for about 90% of sales and bars for about 10%, a cereal-centric revenue structure. The product line covers granola and cornflakes as well as health-functional bars.

The cereal market is shifting from corn-based traditional products toward granola and muesli, and the top two players' combined share has fallen to about 80%. The remaining market is split among Orion, Lotte Wellfood and E-Mart.

The March 2025 shareholder meeting notice estimated the company's market share at about 10%, of which about 5% comes from private-label and national-brand products sold through E-Mart. The company cites its in-house research staff as the basis for developing products suited to Korean tastes.

Its brand awareness, however, is lower than that of multinational players such as Kellogg and Post, which is noted as a weakness. The same notice said the company was pursuing development and launch of its own products.

03

Recent trends

Revenue for fiscal 2025 reached KRW 18.33 billion, up 2.7% from the prior year. Operating profit was KRW 749.5 million, turning around from an operating loss of KRW 621.83 million in 2024. Net income was KRW 817.82 million, compared with a net loss of KRW 1.12 billion in 2024.

In 2024, separate-basis revenue fell 8% and the company slipped into an operating loss, with the net loss widening by 42.9%. The operating margin improved to 4.09% in 2025 from -3.48% in 2024.

As of December 31, 2025, total liabilities were KRW 13.46 billion, with an equity ratio of 9.64% and a borrowings dependence of 72.79%. Current liabilities of KRW 9.96 billion account for most of the liabilities.

Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 676.7 million, while investing cash flow was negative KRW 871.1 million. The company held investor briefings on May 15 and June 18, 2026, and disclosed company and business overview materials.

As of 02:10 KST on November 11, 2026, the share price was KRW 1,930 (+2.55% from the prior close), market capitalization was KRW 3.9 billion, and trading value was KRW 4.41 million.

04

Outlook

Future results are likely to be judged by whether the operating profit streak continues. The 2025 profit reflects both higher revenue and a recovering operating margin. In 2024, revenue fell and losses widened, so annual volatility is high.

The cereal market is shifting toward newer products such as granola and muesli, and how far the company's own products gain share will be the key test. Reliance on a small number of customers was already flagged in the 2021 business report, and changes in orders from major retail channels could directly affect revenue.

On the financial side, the large debt load and high share of current liabilities make it important to see whether profit recovery translates into cash generation and debt repayment. Positive operating cash flow in 2025 is a constructive signal, but the scale of investing cash outflows also needs to be tracked.

The third-quarter 2026 report will show whether operating profit stays positive and how the debt structure changes. As a KONEX-listed company with small trading volume, the share price can be volatile.

05

Bull factors

Improving operating margin

The operating margin improved to 4.09% in 2025 from -3.48% in 2024. Gross profit also rose to KRW 4.05 billion from KRW 3.81 billion. The revenue decline behind the 2024 loss turned into growth in 2025, which supports the margin recovery. Because this rests on one year, a trend will require more fiscal years of data.

In-house product development capability

The company says its in-house research staff enables development of products tailored to Korean tastes. The March 2025 shareholder meeting notice cited this structure as the basis for cost competitiveness. Unlike multinational rivals that operate under headquarters control, it can develop its own products. The company is pursuing its own product development to offset its lower brand awareness.

Sustained operating cash generation

Operating cash flow in 2025 stayed positive at KRW 676.7 million. It was lower than KRW 936.6 million in 2024, but cash was still generated during a loss year. Cash operating profit for 2025 was KRW 1.35 billion. How this cash is allocated between debt repayment and capital investment is the next item to track.

06

Bear factors

High leverage and a thin equity base

As of December 31, 2025, the debt ratio was 937.30% and the equity ratio 9.64%, indicating a thin equity base. Borrowings dependence stood at 72.79%, and current liabilities of KRW 9.96 billion make up most of total liabilities. Equity at the end of 2024 was also only in the KRW 600 million range. This financial burden could constrain the profit recovery.

Dependence on few customers and price pressure

The 2021 business report said revenue depends heavily on a few customers. The same report noted that as sales scale up, price-cut pressure from customers could emerge. Reliance on a small group of customers adds to revenue volatility. The filings do not quantify how much this structure contributed to the 8% revenue decline in 2024.

Low brand awareness and a shifting market

The cereal market is being reshaped by newer products such as granola and muesli, and the top two players' share has fallen to about 80%. Ssial Food's estimated share is about 10%, but its brand awareness is lower than that of multinational rivals. Separate-basis revenue fell 8% in 2024. Whether its own products gain traction will determine top-line growth.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Raw material, packaging and logistics costs for food products are affected by exchange rates and global grain prices. How quickly cost changes are passed on to selling prices can shift margins. Weaker consumer sentiment could affect price-sensitive private-label sales.

Financial

A debt ratio of 937.30% and an equity ratio of 9.64% point to vulnerability to changes in interest rates or borrowing conditions. In 2024, both operating and net losses occurred. If profit declines again, debt repayment costs could weigh on results directly.

Liquidity

As a KONEX-listed company, trading value was KRW 4,407,096 as of 02:10 KST on November 11, 2026, indicating limited liquidity. A market capitalization of KRW 3.9 billion is typical of small caps, which can amplify price volatility.

The speed at which disclosures reach the market and the level of institutional participation also shape price formation.

08

Overall view

Ssial Food appears to have moved out of its 2024 deterioration, with 2025 revenue growth and a return to operating and net profit. Its cereal-focused business, in-house research staff and positive operating cash generation are presented as strengths.

Its debt ratio of 937.30% and equity ratio of 9.64%, reliance on a few customers and low brand awareness remain weaknesses. Two years of data are not enough to determine whether the recovery is structural. The sustainability of profit and an improvement in the financial structure need to be confirmed together.
