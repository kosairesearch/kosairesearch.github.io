Semiconductor Pivot, Earnings Still Pending

LUKEN Technologies is shifting from display inspection equipment toward semiconductor test components, and it still posted an operating loss for 2025.

02

Business structure

LUKEN Technologies is a precision inspection-solutions company that was founded in 2007 and listed on the Konex market in 2017. It is converting its business from display inspection equipment and parts toward semiconductor inspection parts.

According to Korea IR Service, the 2024 revenue mix was inspection equipment KRW 11.0 billion (52.6%), inspection parts KRW 6.3 billion (30.0%), semiconductor parts KRW 1.4 billion (6.7%) and services KRW 2.3 billion (10.7%).

On the display side, LCD and OLED panel inspection equipment accounts for more than half of revenue, followed by inspection parts at about 30%. The semiconductor core is probe cards for wafer testing and test sockets for back-end package testing, both using M-POGO Pins made in its own MEMS fab.

In 2024 it acquired the TIU business from ISC, extending its scope to the interface boards that carry signals between the tester and the test socket. The company describes a cooperative arrangement in which customized interface boards (DSA) and test sockets are pre-installed in Teradyne test equipment.

On market size, probe cards were about USD 2.9 billion and test sockets about USD 2.1 billion in 2024. The probe card field is dominated by a few overseas makers built on customer-specific design, with FormFactor ranked first globally and Technoprobe strong in Europe and Asia.

LUKEN positions its own MEMS fab and vertical integration as differentiators as a smaller entrant in that structure. Exports were 73.2% of revenue in 2024.

03

Recent trends

Consolidated 2024 revenue fell 45.5% year on year to KRW 21.0 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 3.4 billion. Korea IR Service pointed to delayed production-line investment at its key customer LG Display as the backdrop.

According to the 2026-03-31 annual shareholders' meeting filing, 2025 consolidated results were revenue of KRW 17,308 million, an operating loss of KRW 1,198 million and a net loss of KRW 2,650 million. The operating loss narrowed from KRW 3.4 billion in 2024 but did not reach breakeven.

A Korea IR Service report dated 2025-10-14 had forecast 2025 revenue of KRW 24.1 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.8 billion. Against that forecast, reported revenue fell short while the operating loss came in smaller.

At end-2025, consolidated total assets were KRW 51,164 million, total liabilities KRW 37,598 million and total equity KRW 13,566 million, with a loss per share of KRW 237. The company issued KRW 7.0 billion of convertible bonds to SBI Investment and Daishin Securities in November 2025.

The share price moved from a KRW 3,060 close on 2026-03-30 to KRW 3,850 as of 2026-10-27, and turnover was KRW 11,933,970. We could not confirm 2026 quarterly or half-year results in this research.

04

Outlook

On 2026-06-15 the company said it would apply for a technology evaluation in the second half, once growth in semiconductor test product revenue becomes visible. A report on the same day also noted the switch of lead manager to IBK Securities.

However, at an investor briefing on 2025-11-06 the CEO said the company aimed to be trading on KOSDAQ in May-June 2027. Because the two statements differ in timing, the transfer-listing schedule cannot be treated as settled.

On products, the company said it had won orders for two DSA boards for the Chinese market, with delivery to be completed in July. That was the plan as of 2026-06-15, and we could not confirm the actual delivery outcome. The company is working with a domestic memory maker to localize HBM vertical probe cards.

Korea IR Service expected a full return to profit only after 2026, when the semiconductor test parts share grows further. In filings, answers to inquiry disclosure requests on 2026-06-16, 2026-07-15 and 2026-08-14 were all submitted as unconfirmed.

The transfer listing and new orders are therefore verifiable only through company plans and statements at this stage.

05

Bull factors

Vertical integration built on an in-house MEMS fab

The company states that it can produce 65-micron-pitch products through its MEMS process. Korea IR Service explained that on the back of in-house M-POGO Pin production, the company has built a chain that manufactures probe cards, test sockets and TIUs end to end.

That structure is cited as a differentiator in cost and supply stability. Offering a domestic alternative in a market dominated by overseas makers is also part of the bullish case.

Widening semiconductor customer base

The company was registered as a Samsung Electronics semiconductor test parts supplier in August 2024. In July 2025 it won an initial order for 100,000 M-POGO Pins from a Chinese and Taiwanese probe card specialist. In June 2026, a China DSA board order and cooperation with Teradyne were also reported. This is viewed positively as a move away from reliance on a single display customer.

Shift toward a consumables-based revenue mix

Semiconductor test consumables wear down through repeated contact and need periodic replacement. The company aims to raise the inspection parts share of revenue to above 50% over the long term to reduce the volatility of equipment-led sales.

The vertical probe card market is forecast to grow from about USD 428 million in 2024 to USD 514 million in 2027. A higher parts share could leave room for operating margin improvement.

06

Bear factors

Operating losses persist

The company recorded an operating loss of KRW 3.4 billion in 2024. For 2025 it again reported a consolidated operating loss of KRW 1,198 million and a net loss of KRW 2,650 million. Revenue fell short of the Korea IR Service forecast. Breakeven is also placed after 2026, so the loss-making phase could extend.

Stretched balance sheet

At end-2025, consolidated total liabilities of KRW 37,598 million are more than double total equity of KRW 13,566 million. The company issued KRW 7.0 billion of convertible bonds in November 2025. Liquidity and interest-coverage indicators in the Korea IR Service report were also weak. If further funding is needed, share dilution could follow.

Display dependence and a small semiconductor share

In 2024, semiconductor parts revenue was KRW 1.4 billion, only 6.7% of the total. Korea IR Service noted that capital-spending swings at display customers directly affect the company's results.

Much of the semiconductor progress is still described at the development, evaluation or order stage and has not yet been confirmed in revenue scale. If the transition slips, earnings volatility could continue.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Both the display and semiconductor cycles depend on customers' capital-spending plans. In 2024, delayed production-line investment at a key customer fed through to a sharp revenue drop. With exports making up most of revenue, exchange rates and the external trade environment also affect results. Expansion with overseas customers such as those in China is exposed to changes in external policy.

Listing timeline

A KOSDAQ transfer requires filing for and passing a technology evaluation, and we have not confirmed that a filing has been made. Company timelines diverge between pursuing a transfer within the year and a target of trading in May-June 2027. All three inquiry-disclosure answers were also unconfirmed. A delay or failure could affect market expectations and funding plans.

Company-specific

Semiconductor test parts must pass strict performance validation, and for advanced products such as HBM this can take several months to over a year. Completing development is a separate matter from moving to mass production and winning large orders.

With turnover at only about KRW 12 million, trading is thin and price swings can be large. Convertible bonds and stock options could become sources of future dilution.

08

Overall view

LUKEN Technologies is in a transition from a display inspection equipment maker to a semiconductor test parts supplier. Its own MEMS fab, the interface board acquisition, the Samsung Electronics supplier registration and the China and Taiwan orders are cited as grounds for expansion.

On the other hand, the 2025 consolidated results still showed an operating loss of KRW 1,198 million and a net loss of KRW 2,650 million, and total liabilities are more than double total equity.

With semiconductor parts at 6.7% of 2024 revenue, the results of the shift will need to be checked in future reported earnings. The KOSDAQ transfer is still at the company's planning stage, and its own statements on timing differ. Thin turnover adds a liquidity constraint. This report is for information only and leaves the judgment to the reader.
