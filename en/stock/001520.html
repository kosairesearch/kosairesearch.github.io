Tongyang: Capital reallocation amid ready-mixed concrete losses

02

Business structure

Tongyang is a Eugene Group affiliate that positions itself as a total solution provider in building materials, with ready-mixed concrete (RMC) manufacturing and distribution at its core.

Ready-mixed concrete accounted for 60.8% of revenue in the first half of 2026, and the company operates 17 plants nationwide, eight of them concentrated in the Gyeongsang region, according to a Newsquest report.

Because the product must be placed on site within about 90 minutes of mixing, regional construction starts and haulage distance largely determine plant utilization and profitability.

Based on the 2025 business report, Tongyang's RMC plant utilization was 20.3%, and its RMC segment sales that year were KRW 381.3 billion, according to a Herald Economy analysis.

The same analysis put Eugene's utilization at 33.3% and SAMPYO Cement's at 17.0%, showing a wide gap in plant utilization within the industry.

Concrete prices are largely set through annual collective bargaining with construction firms, which makes it hard to pass raw material cost increases directly to selling prices, according to a Newsis report.

The company installed CarbonCure production equipment at its Gimpo and Incheon plants, which inject captured CO2 into concrete, and said cumulative output reached 55,000 cubic meters as of July 2026.

The company said the technology raises concrete strength by 5 to 10% and cuts cement use by 3 to 5%, and it plans to link production data to carbon removal credit issuance and trading.

In real estate development, the Bucheon Samjeong AI Hub Center began construction in May 2026, is scheduled for completion in October 2028, and is planned for sale in the year after completion. The business structure combines RMC-driven site revenue with eco-friendly products and the recovery of development assets.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 767.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 866.3 billion in 2023, up 12.8% year on year, making 2023 the largest year of the 2022–2025 period.

Revenue then fell 13.4% to KRW 750.4 billion in 2024 and 16.0% to KRW 630.6 billion in 2025, a two-year decline from 2024 through 2025, and 2025 was the smallest year in the period.

Operating profit went from KRW 7.92 billion in 2022 to KRW 26.5 billion in 2023, up 235.0%, before the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 921 million in 2024 and a wider operating loss of KRW 20.7 billion in 2025, two straight loss-making years.

The operating margin moved from 1.0% in 2022 and 3.1% in 2023 to -0.1% in 2024 and -3.3% in 2025. Reports tied the 2023 improvement to higher RMC shipment volumes despite a construction slowdown and higher raw material costs, while weak private construction has weighed on shipments since 2024.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned from a KRW 74.2 billion loss in 2024 to KRW 20.1 billion in 2025, a swing back to profit.

The gap between an operating loss and positive net income in 2025, and the KRW 74.2 billion net loss in 2024 against a KRW 921 million operating loss, point to a large contribution from non-operating items.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 160.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 131.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 18.0% year on year. The operating loss has persisted for five consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, and it widened to KRW 6.96 billion in the latest quarter.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 30.8 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, KRW 6.53 billion in the first quarter of 2026, a KRW 17.3 billion loss in the second quarter, and KRW 9.61 billion in the third quarter. The sum for the last four quarters was KRW 29.6 billion.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 54.2 billion in 2023 to -KRW 10.2 billion in 2024 and -KRW 15.4 billion in 2025, and the debt ratio climbed from 25.5% in 2022 to 92.2% in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Ready-mixed concrete and cement are domestic-demand industries highly sensitive to construction activity. At a November 2025 seminar, a SAMPYO market research head projected 2026 RMC demand below the 2025 level and said the recovery in housing sales and construction starts was lagging.

The same session noted decoupling, with RMC volume falling by more than cement volume. Nominal RMC prices rose 2.6% in 2025, but a Kongju National University professor's November 2026 analysis found real prices, measured against per-capita real gross national income, fell 21% over 36 years.

With limited room to raise prices, profitability is seen as depending on product mix and operating efficiency. Cement makers entered the fourth phase of the emissions trading scheme in 2026, when the average annual allowance volume is 16.4% lower than in the third phase.

On November 13, 2026, cement and RMC shares rose on expectations of expanded housing supply, with large cement stocks weaker while small and mid-sized RMC names gained. Tongyang closed lower that day, showing divergence among stocks in the sector.

Among peers, Eugene is the largest domestic RMC producer, with plant utilization of 33.3% in 2025. Tongyang's relative strengths are cited as its nationwide RMC plant network and eco-friendly product lines such as CarbonCure.

06

Outlook

The most concrete timeline the company has disclosed is the Bucheon Samjeong AI Hub Center, which began construction in May 2026, is scheduled for completion in October 2028, and is planned for sale the following year. Newsquest expects the project to support results from 2027 onward.

However, gains from the development asset are concentrated around completion and sale, so the earnings impact could slip to 2028 or later.

In eco-friendly RMC, the company plans to keep expanding its sales force and production capacity based on the CarbonCure lines at its Gimpo and Incheon plants, and to pursue additional revenue by linking carbon removal credit issuance and trading.

On shareholder returns, the board resolved in June 2026 to cancel 24,611,979 treasury shares, equal to about KRW 71.9 billion at book value or 10.26% of total issued shares, and a two-for-one share consolidation was approved at an extraordinary shareholders meeting.

The private equity fund recovery is scheduled for an in-kind distribution on December 1, 2026, with about 3.29 million shares and an acquisition amount of about KRW 101.8 billion expected, and a total recovery of about KRW 166.8 billion including KRW 65 billion in prior cash distributions, according to press reports.

Yuanta Securities analyst Kwon Myung-joon said shareholder value enhancement measures are likely to be implemented. The recovery of the core RMC business depends on private construction starts and public demand, and the operating margin in the next annual business report for 2026 is the next checkpoint.

08

Bull factors

Shareholder return steps now executed

In June 2026 the board resolved to permanently cancel 24,611,979 treasury shares, about 10.26% of total issued shares, with a book value of roughly KRW 71.9 billion. A two-for-one share consolidation followed and trading resumed in July 2026. These steps restructure the share base.

Private equity fund recovery as a cash source

Total recovery from a private equity fund set up in 2021 was reported at about KRW 166.8 billion, including KRW 65 billion in earlier cash distributions. The in-kind distribution is scheduled for December 1, 2026, with about 3.29 million shares expected.

Operating cash flow has been negative in recent years, so the recovered funds could widen financial flexibility.

Eco-friendly RMC and data center development

CarbonCure RMC is marketed on 5 to 10% higher strength and 3 to 5% lower cement use, with cumulative output of 55,000 cubic meters at the Gimpo and Incheon plants. Carbon removal credits are reported to trade at prices well above conventional reduction credits.

The Bucheon Samjeong AI Hub Center is scheduled for completion in October 2028 and planned for sale in the following year.

09

Bear factors

Low RMC utilization and weak private construction

Tongyang's RMC plant utilization was 20.3% in 2025, below Eugene's 33.3%. Third-quarter 2026 revenue fell 18.0% year on year. Industry participants say the prolonged weakness in private construction and public demand that has fallen short of expectations are the main drags. The 2026 RMC demand outlook was projected below 2025.

Five straight quarters of operating losses

The company has posted operating losses for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. The latest quarter's operating loss of KRW 6.96 billion was wider than the prior quarter. The 2025 operating loss was KRW 20.7 billion and operating cash flow was -KRW 15.4 billion. The debt ratio rose from 25.5% in 2022 to 92.2% in 2025.

Support for affiliates and borrowing

According to press reports, Tongyang increased short-term borrowings to KRW 71.4 billion in 2023 to support affiliates. Short-term loans extended to Eugene Home Center were reported as not fully recovered. The KRW 74.2 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in 2024 added pressure on the capital base. Continued support for group affiliates could keep cash flowing out.

10

Risk factors

Construction policy and demand risk

Expectations of housing supply expansion lifted cement and RMC stocks in November 2026, but if those expectations do not translate into actual construction starts, the share price reaction could reverse. Industry analysts note that RMC earnings can swing widely with regional construction activity and transport costs. Whether public demand can offset private weakness is the key variable.

Cost and emissions regulation risk

Cement makers entered the fourth emissions trading phase in 2026, when average annual allowances are 16.4% lower than in the third phase. Because domestic demand makes it hard to pass costs on to customers, cost pressure could rise. Coal prices and electricity tariffs are cited as key variables for profitability. Whether CarbonCure equipment delivers cost savings also remains to be seen.

Development project and asset recovery risk

The Bucheon Samjeong AI Hub Center is scheduled for completion in October 2028, with its sale planned for the year after completion. Any delay would push back the timing of earnings from the development asset. The private equity recovery is an in-kind distribution, so the value of received shares will fluctuate. The realized value depends on when the recovery is completed.

12

Overall view

Tongyang's structure is domestic-demand driven, with ready-mixed concrete making up 60.8% of revenue, and it is highly exposed to the slowdown in construction investment. On a disclosed basis, 2025 revenue was KRW 630.6 billion and the operating loss of KRW 20.7 billion marked a second consecutive loss year.

Operating losses have continued for five straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 29.6 billion.

Treasury share cancellation and the share consolidation are completed steps in shareholder returns, and the roughly KRW 166.8 billion private equity recovery and the Bucheon Samjeong AI Hub Center represent separate cash and asset levers.

On the weaker side, RMC plant utilization of 20.3%, a 92.2% debt ratio and support obligations to affiliates remain. Whether operating profit returns to the black will depend on private construction starts and the cost structure, and the operating margin in the next annual business report is the next checkpoint.
