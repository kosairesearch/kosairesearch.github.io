02

Business structure

Hitejinro Holdings is a holding company that owns beverage affiliates. According to its affiliate list as of December 31, 2025, it holds 100% of Jinro Soju and 50.86% of Hite Jinro.

The soju, beer and bottled water businesses are run mainly by the operating subsidiary Hite Jinro, so segment sales and operating profit should be read at the subsidiary level. In Q3 2026, Hite Jinro's soju sales were KRW 367.5 billion and beer sales were KRW 204.4 billion, so soju was the larger revenue contributor.

Soju sales fell 3.5% year on year and beer sales fell 9.7%. Segment operating profit rose in both units, to KRW 45.0 billion for soju (up 7.0%) and KRW 11.7 billion for beer (up 8.4%).

Cumulative sales for January to September 2026 were KRW 1.1311 trillion for soju (down 1.9%), KRW 540.8 billion for beer (down 11.1%) and KRW 121.5 billion for bottled water (up 2.6%).

In soju, the Chamisul brand keeps a dominant market position, but a reformulation and higher marketing costs weighed on profitability in the first half of 2026. Beer sales declined under weaker consumption and sharper competition.

The company points to portfolio diversification around Terra Sparkling and Terra & Chamisul as its growth driver. Bottled water sales rose on a cumulative basis, but operating profit fell to KRW 2.1 billion from about KRW 7.2 billion a year earlier.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on reported results, annual revenue rose from KRW 2,484.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 2,506.9 billion in 2023 (up 0.9% year over year) and KRW 2,585.1 billion in 2024 (up 3.1% year over year), then fell to KRW 2,484.5 billion in 2025 (down 3.9% year over year).

Among 2022-2025, the largest year was 2024 and the smallest was 2022. Operating profit fell from KRW 202.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 138.1 billion in 2023 (down 31.8% year over year), then rose to KRW 221.8 billion in 2024 (up 60.6% year over year), and was KRW 183.3 billion in 2025 (down 17.4% year over year).

The operating margin was 8.2% in 2022, 5.5% in 2023, 8.6% in 2024 and 7.4% in 2025, meaning it recovered in 2024 after hitting a low in 2023 and then declined again in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders plunged from KRW 30.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 0.9 billion in 2023 (down 97.0% year over year), recovered to KRW 44.4 billion in 2024 (up 4,854.8% year over year), and then swung to a net loss of KRW 2.58 billion in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 72.0 billion in 2022, KRW 18.3 billion in 2023, KRW 90.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 15.9 billion in 2025, which differs from net profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

Operating cash flow was KRW -89.5 billion in 2022, then KRW 136.0 billion in 2023, KRW 274.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 212.0 billion in 2025, remaining positive after 2023.

Quarterly revenue fell from KRW 665.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 566.1 billion in the fourth quarter (down 15.0% quarter over quarter), then rose for three consecutive quarters to KRW 588.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 615.3 billion in the second quarter and KRW 629.7 billion in the third quarter.

Operating profit swung to an operating loss of KRW 6.88 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, then returned to profit at KRW 55.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026, and was KRW 64.4 billion in the second quarter (up 15.6% quarter over quarter) and KRW 59.6 billion in the third quarter (down 7.3% quarter over quarter).

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 11.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 16.0 billion in the second quarter and KRW 9.37 billion in the third quarter, a recovery from the net loss of KRW 45.6 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, but the third-quarter figure was down 41.6% quarter over quarter.

Regarding third-quarter net profit of KRW 23.2 billion (down 27.4% year over year), a November 13 news report said that a decline in financial income and an increase in other non-operating expenses had an impact.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic alcohol demand is in a contraction phase. In its Q3 2026 results announcement, the company said profitability improved through a profit-focused strategy and cost efficiency despite a shrinking alcohol market. Soju maintains its market position around Chamisul.

Beer sales fell more steeply than soju, with the subsidiary's beer sales down 9.7% and soju sales down 3.5% in Q3 2026. Soju is the more profitable unit: its operating margin was about 12.2% in Q3 2026, up from about 11.0% a year earlier, while beer's margin was about 5.7%, up from about 4.8%.

The two core businesses account for most of consolidated operating profit, so the holding company's consolidated results depend heavily on subsidiary volumes and cost control. Because the holding company's portfolio is concentrated in a single alcohol category, changes in consumption affect group results broadly.

06

Outlook

The company says it will keep pursuing cost efficiency and a profit-focused strategy. On products, it intends to strengthen market leadership and further improve results through portfolio diversification around Terra Sparkling and Terra & Chamisul.

Reporting frames overcoming the shrinking alcohol market and building a growth base around new products as the key task ahead. Cumulative sales and operating profit at the operating subsidiary both declined in January to September 2026, so the recovery still has hurdles.

At the holding-company level, operating profit stayed in the KRW 50 billion range in each of the first three quarters of 2026, while controlling-shareholder net income moved widely from quarter to quarter. No specific numerical guidance was confirmed.

The next regular report is the 2026 annual report, and whether full-year operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income reverse the 2025 loss is the next checkpoint. Whether new products can offset falling soju and beer sales will be the central variable for 2027 results.

Bull factors

Operating profit turned positive again in Q1 2026 at KRW 55.7 billion, then reached KRW 64.4 billion in Q2 and KRW 59.6 billion in Q3, keeping all three quarters in the KRW 50 billion range. Q3 operating profit rose 2.7% year on year.

Operating profit has held up since the KRW 6.88 billion operating loss in Q4 2025, which supporters cite as the main bullish point.

Soju profitability improved at the subsidiary

At the subsidiary Hite Jinro, Q3 2026 soju operating profit rose 7.0% to KRW 45.0 billion while sales fell 3.5%. The soju operating margin was about 12.2%, up from about 11.0% a year earlier. The company attributes the improvement to cost efficiency.

Cash generation and gradual balance sheet improvement

Operating cash flow stayed positive from 2023 through 2025, reaching KRW 212 billion in 2025. The debt ratio declined from 253.2% in 2024 to 242.8% in 2025. The credit rating was recorded at A as of June 2026.

Bear factors

Sales continue to decline

Sales fell 3.9% to KRW 2.48 trillion in 2025, and Q3 2026 sales of KRW 629.7 billion were down 5.4% year on year. Cumulative January to September 2026 sales at the subsidiary Hite Jinro declined 4.5%.

Controlling-shareholder net loss

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a KRW 2.58 billion loss in 2025. The Q4 2025 loss was KRW 45.6 billion, and the last four quarters total a KRW 8.91 billion net loss. Q3 2026 controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 9.37 billion fell 40.5% year on year.

Weak beer and bottled water profitability

At the subsidiary Hite Jinro, cumulative January to September 2026 beer operating profit fell 31.2% to KRW 17.7 billion. Bottled water operating profit dropped to KRW 2.1 billion from about KRW 7.2 billion a year earlier.

Soju profitability in the first half of 2026 was weighed down by a product reformulation and higher marketing costs.

Risk factors

Weaker alcohol demand

Weaker domestic alcohol demand is cited as the reason behind sales declines. Beer sales fell 9.7% in Q3 2026 under weaker consumption and sharper competition, and soju sales fell 3.5%, both at the subsidiary level. Whether cost reduction can keep operating profit intact as sales shrink is the key issue.

Financial leverage and earnings volatility

Total liabilities were KRW 2.77 trillion at end-2025, larger than total equity of KRW 1.14 trillion, and controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 553.9 billion. Quarterly controlling-shareholder net income swung widely, from a KRW 45.6 billion loss in Q4 2025 to KRW 16 billion in Q2 2026.

Ownership concentration and limited free float

Park Moon-deok and eight related parties hold 65.91% and the free float ratio is 26.16%, as of October 16, 2026. The limited number of freely tradable shares is a structural feature. With most shares held by a concentrated group, trading flows may be thin.
