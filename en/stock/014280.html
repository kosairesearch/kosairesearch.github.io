02

Business structure

Kumkang Kind was founded in 1979 and listed on the KOSPI in 1988 as a comprehensive maker of steel pipes and construction temporary materials.

Its main products include structural and piping steel pipes, formwork such as aluminum forms, general forms and euro forms, and temporary supports and work platforms, along with jacking systems for LNG carriers.

The group has 16 consolidated subsidiaries with production and supply bases in Korea, Malaysia, Vietnam, India, Indonesia and Kenya. A sales mix compiled by JobKorea as of March 2026 shows feed at 36%, aluminum form rental at 15%, steel pipe at 12%, forging at 11% and others at 26%.

Because this split comes from an external data site, its categories may differ from the company's own segment disclosure. The panel segment refers to domestic aluminum form rental, and its 2025 price declines and volume drops were cited as main causes of weaker results.

The steel pipe segment is positioned on galvanized white pipe sales growth and thick-walled pipe production for shipbuilding. A securities information summary also attributes steel pipe sales growth to higher export volumes to the United States.

The company describes its temporary materials business as having the largest comprehensive production scale in Korea. In the feed business, sales volume rose but revenue declined in the first quarter of 2026, according to the company's results commentary.

Steel pipe competitiveness depends heavily on the purchase price of HR coil, its key raw material. Because the portfolio combines construction-linked panels and pipes, shipbuilding temporary materials and feed, segment-level industry trends flow directly into consolidated results.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed basis, consolidated revenue rose from KRW 728.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 856.9 billion in 2023, up 17.6% year on year, making 2023 the largest year in the 2022-2025 window. Revenue then fell to KRW 801.4 billion in 2024, down 6.5%, and edged up to KRW 802.2 billion in 2025, up 0.1%.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 33.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 66.6 billion in 2023, up 96.2%, which marked the peak. It then dropped to KRW 33 billion in 2024, down 50.4%, and to KRW 10.5 billion in 2025, down 68.2%, so profit fell for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

The operating margin was 4.7% in 2022, 7.8% in 2023, 4.1% in 2024 and 1.3% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 50.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 38.4 billion in 2023, down 24.6%, and to KRW 5.52 billion in 2024, down 85.6%, before turning into a KRW 44.7 billion loss in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a KRW 37.4 billion loss in 2025.

The company attributed 2025 weakness to a downturn in downstream industries and intensifying competition, which lowered prices and volumes in the panel and steel pipe segments, and it also cited an impairment of panel-segment fixed assets as a drag on net income.

Quarterly revenue bottomed at KRW 187.2 billion in Q4 2025, down 8.8% from the prior quarter, then rose to KRW 196.7 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 229.1 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 235.7 billion in Q3 2026, three straight quarters of growth.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 39.37 million in Q3 2025 and a loss of KRW 564 million in Q4 2025 to operating profit of KRW 13.2 billion in Q1 2026.

Q2 2026 operating profit was KRW 18 billion, up 35.9% from the prior quarter, and Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 7.78 billion, down 56.7% from the prior quarter.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 14.3 billion in Q1 2026, fell to KRW 7.27 billion in Q2 2026, and returned to a KRW 5.75 billion net loss in Q3 2026.

Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 498.2 billion, of which KRW 360 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 689.8 billion. Operating cash flow was -KRW 26.9 billion in 2022, then KRW 49.4 billion in 2023, KRW 13 billion in 2024 and KRW 21.9 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Kumkang Kind's main downstream markets are construction, including formwork and temporary materials, and shipbuilding, including marine temporary materials and thick-walled pipes.

In its 2025 results commentary, the company cited a downturn in downstream industries and intensifying competition as reasons for lower prices in the panel and steel pipe segments. Its Q1 2026 results commentary expected each segment to recover on government policy and orders for eco-friendly vessels.

A securities information summary expected profitability in shipbuilding-related businesses to improve on rising orders for LNG carriers and eco-friendly ships, and it also saw government housing supply expansion lifting new housing starts.

That summary does not state a clear publication date, so it is difficult to treat it as a current read on the market. KG Dongbusteel, a steel and metals peer, reported Q3 2026 separate-basis revenue of KRW 803.3 billion, up 14.14% from a year earlier, while its operating profit fell 52.50% to KRW 10.7 billion.

That case shows revenue growth and profit pressure can diverge within the steel sector. Kumkang Kind's Q3 2026 revenue rose 14.8% from a year earlier and its operating profit turned positive, so sales and profit moved in the same direction at the group level. Panel pricing pressure persists, however, which leaves a clear gap between segments.

06

Outlook

The Q1 2026 results commentary expected each segment to recover on government policy and orders for eco-friendly vessels. According to the company's profile materials, the steel pipe segment is expanding production of thick-walled pipes to grow sales to the shipbuilding market.

The company also plans to strengthen sales capacity in Southeast Asian overseas markets based in Malaysia, aiming to secure export orders and open new markets. The panel segment is described as facing a domestic market structure that is unlikely to change easily.

The next periodic report, the 2026 annual report, will show Q4 2026 and full-year operating margin. Because the operating margin fell to 1.3% in 2025, the full-year 2026 margin level is the first checkpoint.

Operating profit stayed positive from Q1 through Q3 2026, so the Q4 trend will determine whether full-year profitability holds. Whether the panel-segment fixed-asset impairment recorded in 2025 recurs is another item to confirm. Operating cash flow relative to the KRW 21.9 billion recorded in 2025 is also worth tracking.

Bull factors

Three quarters of operating profit

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 13.2 billion in Q1 2026, then reached KRW 18 billion in Q2 and KRW 7.78 billion in Q3, extending profitability for three straight quarters. Revenue rose in the same period to KRW 196.7 billion, KRW 229.1 billion and KRW 235.7 billion. Bulls point to the swing to operating profit in Q3 2026 from a year-earlier loss as evidence of recovery.

Shipbuilding and steel pipe demand

A securities information summary expects profitability in shipbuilding-related temporary materials and steel pipes to improve on rising LNG carrier and eco-friendly ship orders. The company is expanding thick-walled pipe production to grow sales to the shipbuilding market. The Q1 2026 results commentary also records higher sales in marine engine and steel pipe businesses.

Trading below book value

Controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 360 billion of the KRW 498.2 billion total equity at end-2025, and the price-to-book ratio is below 1x. That ratio is lower than at the end of 2024. The Q1 2026 results commentary explained that cost improvements and expense cuts lifted profitability.

Bear factors

Net losses persist after the swing

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a KRW 44.7 billion loss in 2025, and returned to a KRW 5.75 billion loss in Q3 2026. Cumulative controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four reported quarters total KRW 15.2 billion. Operating profit in Q3 2026 fell 56.7% from the prior quarter, which points to sizable earnings volatility.

Panel segment weakness and impairment

The 2025 results commentary pointed to lower prices and volumes in the panel and steel pipe segments. The impairment of panel-segment fixed assets was cited as a reason for lower net income. The Q1 2026 commentary also says the panel segment was hit by both lower volume and lower pricing.

Financial burden and margin decline

Total liabilities were KRW 689.8 billion at end-2025, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 138.5%. The operating margin fell from 7.8% in 2023 to 1.3% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders dropped 49.0% from the prior quarter in Q2 2026, underscoring quarterly swings.

10

Risk factors

Downstream demand and pricing competition

Panel and steel pipe volumes depend on construction activity and shipbuilding orders. Competition intensified in 2025 and lowered prices, and panel pricing was still falling in Q1 2026. A prolonged downturn could raise the chance of another impairment in the panel segment.

Balance sheet and cash flow

Total liabilities of KRW 689.8 billion exceed total equity of KRW 498.2 billion at end-2025. Operating cash flow was negative in 2022 and KRW 21.9 billion in 2025. If net losses continue, borrowing costs could weigh more heavily on results.

Export conditions and raw material costs

Higher export volumes to the United States are cited as a driver of steel pipe sales. Changes in the trade environment could affect those volumes. Steel pipe margins depend on HR coil purchase prices, so weaker raw material costs and export conditions together would pressure margins.

Overall view

Kumkang Kind posted a consolidated operating profit of KRW 10.5 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 44.7 billion in 2025, but quarterly operating profit turned positive in 2026.

Operating profit was KRW 13.2 billion in Q1, KRW 18 billion in Q2 and KRW 7.78 billion in Q3 2026, and revenue grew for three straight quarters.

However, controlling-shareholder net income was positive only in Q1 and Q2 2026, and Q3 returned to a KRW 5.75 billion loss, leaving a cumulative four-quarter net loss of KRW 15.2 billion.

By segment, higher marine engine and steel pipe sales are the improvement drivers, while weaker panel (aluminum form rental) pricing and volume are the burden.

On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was 138.5% at end-2025 with total liabilities of KRW 689.8 billion, so the durability of any profit recovery matters. On valuation, the PER is not calculated, and the price-to-book ratio is below 1x, which are factual comparisons.

Both strengths and weaknesses are present, so the full-year 2026 operating margin in the annual report, Q1 2027 results and the panel-segment pricing trend are the next checkpoints.
