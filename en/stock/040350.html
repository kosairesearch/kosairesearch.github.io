02

Business structure

CreoSG is a KOSDAQ-listed company whose main businesses are financial IT solutions and bio drug development. In March 2024 it changed its name from Curo Com to CreoSG and appointed a bio specialist as CEO, shifting the focus toward biotechnology.

According to the business section of its quarterly report as of September 30, 2025, the solution segment (software development and maintenance) accounted for 90.8% of cumulative revenue of KRW 7.65 billion, while a real estate operating subsidiary accounted for 9.2%. The same table lists no revenue for the bio segment.

Solution customers are mainly financial institutions, and a securities data provider's earnings commentary cited new projects with several domestic banks and a forestry federation as a driver of first-half 2026 sales.

A contract disclosed on March 9, 2026 for operating a banking IT system with a domestic bank is worth KRW 1.46 billion, running from March 3, 2026 to February 28, 2027.

The bio segment centers on the SUV-MAP recombinant VSV vaccine platform, which the company internalized in 2024 by absorbing a subsidiary through merger.

The platform is applied to vaccine candidates for Zika, SFTS, hemorrhagic fever with renal syndrome and MERS, and the company is also pursuing clinical development of an AIDS vaccine candidate.

In a September 2026 interview, the CEO described plans to extend the platform into immuno-oncology and noted possible joint development or technology licensing with global pharmaceutical companies. The subsidiary Inferrex researches antibiotics against Staphylococcus aureus, and the parent holds 51.44% of it.

In solutions the company competes with large system integrators, and in vaccines with infectious-disease developers, but its scale is far smaller than the large integrators such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 9.78 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.76 billion in 2023 (down 0.3%) and KRW 8.16 billion in 2024 (down 16.4%), before reaching KRW 10.9 billion in 2025 (up 33.1%). The company attributed the 2025 increase mainly to higher revenue at its solution division and consolidated subsidiaries.

Operating losses were KRW 6.14 billion in 2022, KRW 3.73 billion in 2023, KRW 5.65 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.41 billion in 2025, so the company has posted an operating loss in each year from 2022 through 2025. The operating loss narrowed in 2023 and widened in 2024 and 2025.

The operating margin improved from -69.3% in 2024 to -59.0% in 2025, but the loss amount grew from KRW 5.65 billion to KRW 6.41 billion, so revenue growth did not reduce the absolute loss.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 10.4 billion in 2022, KRW 11.7 billion in 2023, KRW 12.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.4 billion in 2025.

The consolidated net loss including non-controlling interests narrowed to KRW 10.5 billion in 2025 from KRW 13.9 billion in 2024, and the company said lower fair-value gains and losses on convertible bonds it holds and lower equity-method valuation losses contributed to the narrowing.

Those are non-operating valuation items, so they should be read separately from operating performance. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 81.6% in 2024 to 92.0% in 2025, and total equity of KRW 45.3 billion at the end of 2025 fell below paid-in capital of KRW 48.8 billion, a partial capital impairment.

Operating cash flow was negative every year from 2022 to 2025, at -KRW 2.82 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 2.89 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 3.21 billion in Q4 2025 (up 11.4% quarter on quarter), KRW 2.63 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 3.18 billion in Q2 2026 (up 20.9% quarter on quarter) and KRW 2.64 billion in Q3 2026 (down 8.6% year on year).

The operating loss widened to KRW 1.94 billion in Q4 2025, narrowed to KRW 128 million in Q2 2026, and was KRW 684 million in Q3 2026. The company has reported operating losses in each of the last five quarters, from Q3 2025 through Q3 2026. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 4.77 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The financial IT system construction and maintenance market depends on banks' system replacement and migration demand, so project volumes move with those cycles.

Large system integrators build revenue on large contracts and long maintenance agreements, whereas smaller players' results depend heavily on winning specific financial-institution projects.

CreoSG's solution business sits closer to the smaller-player profile, and its revenue scale is far below large integrators such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS. Because project awards land at uneven points in time, quarterly revenue has moved up and down across the quarters from Q3 2025 through Q3 2026.

The bio segment has two parts: infectious-disease vaccines and immuno-oncology. Vaccine commercialization timing depends on outbreak patterns, government demand, and clinical and regulatory steps.

Immuno-oncology development can aim for standalone use or combination with existing cancer drugs, and in both cases joint development or licensing with multinational pharmaceutical companies is a key variable.

Because the bio segment has no revenue yet, its earnings structure differs from that of other listed IT companies. A company combining solutions and bio shows both steady project-based cash generation and the long R&D burden of a biotech in one structure.

Outlook

Based on confirmed facts, the direction of the solution business depends on winning financial-institution projects. The contract with a domestic bank for operating a banking IT system runs through February 28, 2027, so whether it is renewed and on what terms is the next checkpoint for solution revenue.

Quarterly revenue rose 20.9% quarter on quarter in Q2 2026 and then fell 17.1% in Q3 2026, which shows the wide swings in quarterly sales.

In October 2026 the company obtained a domestic patent for a COVID-19 vaccine built on its recombinant VSV platform, following earlier MERS vaccine patents in Saudi Arabia and Canada. Patents expand the intellectual property base but do not by themselves show clinical results or revenue.

In a September 2026 interview, the CEO said the main mechanism of the immuno-oncology platform has been confirmed in experiments and that the company is refining development candidates.

The same interview mentioned overseas expansion of the vaccine business and possible joint development or technology licensing with global pharmaceutical companies. In October 2025 a company representative said vaccine manufacturing for a US FDA phase 2 study was progressing at a French contract manufacturer.

On the capital side, the March 31, 2026 annual shareholders meeting approved a share consolidation, and the new shares were listed on May 12, 2026. In October 2026 the company disclosed an additional acquisition of shares in its largest shareholder's affiliate for about KRW 8.1 billion. The 2026 annual report will show bio R&D spending and the revenue mix of the solution business together.

Bull factors

Narrower losses and a quarterly profit

The profit lasted only one quarter, and a net loss returned in Q3 2026.

Patent base for the vaccine platform

In October 2026 the company obtained a domestic patent for a COVID-19 vaccine built on its SUV-MAP recombinant VSV platform. It already holds MERS vaccine patents in Saudi Arabia and Canada.

In September 2026 the CEO said the main mechanism of its immuno-oncology candidates has been confirmed in experiments, and kept open the possibility of joint development or technology licensing with global pharmaceutical companies.

Bear factors

Four straight years of operating losses

Operating losses have continued from 2022 through 2025, and the 2025 loss of KRW 6.41 billion was larger than the KRW 5.65 billion in 2024. The 2025 operating margin was still deeply negative at -59.0%. Bio R&D investment was cited as a cost burden limiting profitability. Even with revenue reaching KRW 10.9 billion, costs did not fall fast enough to offset the growth in sales.

Operating cash outflows and thinner capital

Operating cash flow was negative in every year from 2022 to 2025, reaching -KRW 2.82 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 81.6% in 2024 to 92.0% in 2025. Total equity of KRW 45.3 billion at the end of 2025 fell below paid-in capital of KRW 48.8 billion.

A KRW 10 billion rights offering planned in the first half of 2025 was not completed after an investor withdrew its commitment.

Flat quarterly sales and accumulated losses

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 2.64 billion fell 8.6% year on year, and Q1 2026 revenue dropped 18.1% from the prior quarter. The Q4 2025 operating loss widened to KRW 1.94 billion.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 4.77 billion, and operating losses have appeared in each of the last five quarters.

Risk factors

Clinical trials and licensing (bio)

Whether vaccine candidates enter clinical trials, whether immuno-oncology candidates advance, and whether joint development or technology licensing with global pharmaceutical companies closes are all unresolved. With no bio revenue, these events effectively determine when earnings could improve. If clinical or overseas regulatory timelines slip, the R&D cost burden could last longer.

Related-party transactions and governance

On April 10, 2026 the company lent KRW 6.33 billion to its largest shareholder at 6%, maturing April 9, 2029, bringing its total loan balance to KRW 9.15 billion. The disclosure said the loan converts a convertible bond held by that shareholder and involves no actual cash outflow.

In October 2026 the company agreed to acquire additional shares in the same shareholder for about KRW 8.1 billion, with the stated aim of recovering loan receivables through a debt-to-equity conversion.

Customer concentration and renewals (solutions)

Solutions account for the large majority of revenue, so results depend on winning financial-institution projects. The banking IT operations contract runs through February 28, 2027, and whether it will be renewed and on what terms has not been set. If new awards slow or large projects wind down in stages, quarterly revenue could decline again.

Overall view

CreoSG's revenue rose 33.1% in 2025 on the back of its financial IT solutions, and controlling-shareholder net income turned positive in Q2 2026. Operating losses, however, have run from 2022 through 2025, and the company returned to an operating loss in Q3 2026.

Operating cash flow was negative in every year over the same period, and total equity at the end of 2025 sat below paid-in capital, a partial capital impairment.

The bio segment is broadening its base through vaccine patents and immuno-oncology candidates, but it has no revenue yet and the outcomes of clinical trials and licensing remain undetermined. Loans and share transactions with the largest shareholder also need to be tracked for recovery.

The next checkpoints are the February 28, 2027 end date of the banking contract and the filing of the 2026 annual report. Together these will show the operating loss trend, the durability of solution revenue and the capital structure.
