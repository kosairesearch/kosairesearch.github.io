02

Business structure

DSK manufactures equipment for FPD (flat panel display) bonding systems and in-line systems, camera module equipment, and secondary-battery equipment. Based on the 2026 semi-annual report, product sales accounted for 59%, Linear and other items for 24%, and other revenue for 17%.

The FPD bonding and in-line systems are produced by only a few companies in Japan and Korea, and two to three companies form an oligopoly for each equipment type, according to the same report.

In secondary batteries, DSK supplies lamination (L&S) equipment for cell assembly to LG Energy Solution, and the value-chain data counts 11 listed companies in the same process. R&D spending in the first half of 2026 was KRW 1.7 billion, or 15.17% of revenue.

Its subsidiary in botulinum toxin products is presented in press coverage as a new business with expected long-term growth. As of December 31, 2023, DSK held a 67.30% stake in that subsidiary. Based on the disclosed data, timing differences in deliveries of order-based revenue may be behind quarterly revenue swings.

A share purchase agreement signed on August 6, 2025 transfers the largest shareholder stake, and the buyer group's planned ownership ratio was disclosed at 25.4%.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 48.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 71.5 billion in 2023, up 48.2% year on year, which was the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Revenue fell to KRW 22.6 billion in 2024, down 68.3%, the smallest year, and recovered to KRW 43.2 billion in 2025, up 90.7%.

Over the same period the operating loss was KRW 13.1 billion in 2022, KRW 9.71 billion in 2023, KRW 19.7 billion in 2024, and KRW 22.7 billion in 2025, marking four consecutive years of losses.

The loss narrowed in 2023 but widened again in 2024 and 2025, so the operating loss did not shrink even after revenue recovered in 2025. The operating margin was -27.2% in 2022, -13.6% in 2023, -87.0% in 2024, and -52.6% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a profit of KRW 3.26 billion in 2023, then became a net loss of KRW 7.84 billion in 2024 and a net loss of KRW 15.5 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, a different measure, was KRW 3.58 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 11.4 billion in 2024, and a net loss of KRW 19.8 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 635 million in 2022 to -KRW 10.3 billion in 2023, -KRW 8.44 billion in 2024, and -KRW 10.7 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, the third quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 4.81 billion and an operating loss of KRW 8.45 billion, followed by KRW 4.41 billion and KRW 5.4 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, and KRW 4.9 billion and KRW 5.72 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

The second quarter of 2026 had the highest revenue of the last five quarters at KRW 6.43 billion and the smallest operating loss at KRW 3.06 billion.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 4.7 billion and the operating loss was KRW 5.07 billion, narrower than a year earlier, while the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 8.34 billion.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 16.7 billion.

Industry analysis

In FPD equipment, bonding and in-line systems are produced only by a few companies in Japan and Korea, and the same report summary describes a market where two to three companies form an oligopoly for each equipment type.

For secondary-battery equipment, the 2026 semi-annual report cited industry data pointing to the maturing of the electric vehicle market, revisions to the US IRA, and reduced subsidies in Europe and China, and expected weaker demand and greater market uncertainty.

This outlook rests on the cited industry data rather than the company's own estimate. The cell assembly process counts 11 listed companies, and DSK's lamination equipment supply relationship is presented with LG Energy Solution.

In this structure, customers' investment schedules can affect the timing of DSK's revenue deliveries. A single contract with HL-GA Battery Company LLC, corrected in a disclosure on December 31, 2025, is one example of order flow on the battery side.

In FPD equipment, the oligopoly structure means that revenue contribution can rise significantly if demand for a given machine recovers.

Quarterly revenue rebounded in the second quarter of 2026 and fell in the third quarter, showing that demand directions across the two equipment groups can diverge from quarter to quarter.
