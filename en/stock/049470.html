02

Business structure

Bitplanet started as a public-sector IT and security company and now combines an SI (system integration) business, bitcoin holdings and a mining operation as a digital asset treasury (DAT) company.

Reports say the current structure took shape after SGA, a KOSDAQ-listed company, was acquired by a consortium in July 2025. Its bitcoin holdings stood at 300 BTC at the end of the first half of 2026, and the CEO has set a long-term target of 10,000 BTC. The business has three parts.

The first is the SI business, which reports put at more than KRW 10 billion in annual revenue. The second is bitcoin mining, for which the purchase of equipment was completed in September 2026.

The third is a domestic AI data center plan, where the CEO said the company is reviewing sites including areas near the capital region.

The fleet consists of 1,204 Bitmain units, 454 S21 XP Hydro and 750 S21e XP Hydro, placed in Oman and Paraguay where power costs are lower, and operated through a mix of outsourced operations and joint ventures.

The mining target is 7 BTC a month, or at least 84 BTC a year, and mined bitcoin is recognized as revenue for accounting purposes. Among domestic listed DAT companies, Bitplanet is seen as holding a relatively large bitcoin position.

Overseas, bitcoin-holding companies such as Strategy and Metaplanet are cited as comparisons, and the company says it aims to build its own cash flow through mining and AI infrastructure, unlike some overseas DAT companies.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 55 billion in 2022 to KRW 43.4 billion in 2023 (down 21.0% year on year), KRW 36.1 billion in 2024 (down 16.7%) and KRW 24.8 billion in 2025 (down 31.3%), declining for three straight years from 2023. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year by revenue and 2025 the smallest.

The operating loss widened from KRW 2.21 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.84 billion in 2023, then turned into an operating profit of KRW 250 million in 2024 before returning to an operating loss of KRW 4.52 billion in 2025. Operating margin was -4.0% in 2022, -15.8% in 2023, 0.7% in 2024 and -18.2% in 2025.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7 billion in 2022, KRW 8.55 billion in 2023, KRW 1.13 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.32 billion in 2025, a loss in each of the four years from 2022 to 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of the same amounts in each year.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 61.6 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 9.9 billion, a debt ratio of 16.1%.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 3.81 billion in 2022, -KRW 4.7 billion in 2023, KRW 1.17 billion in 2024 and -KRW 3.41 billion in 2025, so cash flowed out in every year except 2024.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 4.42 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 9.37 billion in Q4 2025 (up 111.9% quarter on quarter), then came in at KRW 4.84 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 7.95 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 4.14 billion in Q3 2026 (down 6.4% year on year).

The operating loss has run for five straight quarters, from KRW 1.4 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 1.97 billion in Q3 2026, and the Q2 2026 loss of KRW 767 million was the smallest of the five.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed from KRW 7.81 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 3.72 billion in Q2 2026, and the company turned to net income of KRW 2.88 billion in Q3 2026.

Reports put the first-half 2026 crypto revaluation decline at about KRW 10.3 billion and cite crypto valuation effects as one reason for the net loss.

05

Industry analysis

Bitplanet's end markets fall into two groups: a crypto market whose earnings track bitcoin prices, and an AI infrastructure market where demand for power and data centers is rising.

On the tax side, a 22% levy including local income tax on individuals' gains from virtual asset transfers is scheduled to take effect in January 2027.

Samsung Securities has argued that listed DAT companies could benefit directly, because individuals trading bitcoin would be taxed while most small shareholders trading listed stocks would not pay capital gains tax.

Mining profitability depends heavily on bitcoin prices and power costs, and the break-even level the company has given is about USD 81,000 to 85,000 per bitcoin. Reports described bitcoin prices in September 2026 as near that break-even level.

Nasdaq-listed IREN is cited as an overseas example of a miner that expanded into AI cloud. The company says its edge in AI data centers lies in securing power and keeping systems running, and it treats the power procurement know-how from mining as an asset.

The CEO also noted that domestic private capital has few routes into AI infrastructure. Some analysis holds that bitcoin holdings and the value of the SI business are both reflected in the market capitalization.

06

Outlook

The first mining target is at least 84 BTC a year, or 7 BTC a month, from the initial operation of the equipment. Another report put the annual figure at up to 90 BTC for the same fleet. Mining runs through a mix of outsourced operations and joint ventures.

On June 24, 2026, the company signed a strategic MOU on bitcoin mining with Nasdaq-listed Web3 financial firm Antalpha, covering mining supply chains and technical support.

The June report put the mining equipment investment at KRW 15 billion, funded from internal cash and outside financing, and KRW 5 billion was secured through the 17th convertible bond series. The purchase price of the mining machines disclosed in September 2026 was about KRW 13.4 billion.

Mined bitcoin is recognized as revenue for accounting purposes and held as a long-term financial asset, divided into liquidity reserves, risk hedge funds and reinvestment funds.

At a March 2026 interview, the AI data center plan was in an early stage, with sites including areas near the capital region under review and no fixed location or scale.

The CEO has named 2027 as the year to prove results across mining, AI data centers and the SI business, and said the company will report progress on new businesses and bitcoin accumulation each quarter. Reports point to whether mining reaches its target and how much bitcoin per share grows as the key variables.

08

Bull factors

Mining fleet secured and direct production

The purchase of 1,204 mining machines was completed in September 2026, targeting at least 84 BTC a year. Reports note that own mining output could raise bitcoin holdings without new share issuance. Mined bitcoin is recognized as revenue, adding a new revenue line.

SI business and a shifting tax backdrop

The SI business, which reports put at more than KRW 10 billion in annual revenue, remains an existing source of cash. A tax on virtual asset gains is scheduled from January 2027, and Samsung Securities has argued that listed DAT companies could gain relative to direct holdings on tax terms. The after-tax gap between direct bitcoin trading and holding listed shares is drawing attention.

Quarterly turnaround and AI data center optionality

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 2.88 billion in Q3 2026, and the Q2 2026 operating loss of KRW 767 million was smaller than the KRW 1.21 billion in Q1 2026.

The company treats the power procurement and utilization know-how built through mining as a core asset for AI data centers. IREN is cited as an overseas case of a miner expanding into AI cloud.

09

Bear factors

Recurring operating losses and shrinking revenue

Revenue has declined for three straight years from 2023, and Q3 2026 revenue fell 6.4% year on year. The operating loss has continued for five straight quarters from Q3 2025, and the Q3 2026 loss of KRW 1.97 billion was wider than in Q2 2026. The 2025 operating loss was KRW 4.52 billion, and operating cash flow was negative in every year except 2024.

Bitcoin price exposure and the mining break-even

The company is relatively sensitive to bitcoin prices, so a decline would enlarge valuation losses. The break-even level for mining is USD 81,000 to 85,000 per bitcoin, and bitcoin was near that range when reports were published in September 2026. The first-half 2026 crypto revaluation decline was reported at about KRW 10.3 billion.

AI data center plans still undefined

At a March 2026 interview, the AI data center plan was at an early stage of site review, with no fixed location or scale. The company has pointed to 2027 as the proof point, but neither the mining target nor the AI business has yet been shown in reported results. Running several businesses at once could also spread funding and management resources thin.

10

Risk factors

Crypto price and accounting volatility

The value of bitcoin holdings and mining revenue depend on prices, and crypto revaluation gains and losses can move quarterly net income sharply. The first-half 2026 revaluation decline was reported at about KRW 10.3 billion.

Q3 2026 showed an operating loss alongside positive net income, so non-operating items need to be read together with operating results.

Overseas sites, contracts and foreign exchange

The mining machines are placed in Oman and Paraguay and run through outsourced operations and joint ventures. The company reviewed contract terms, foreign exchange settlement and accounting step by step, and said the purchase schedule was partly adjusted. The purchase took about three months from the June disclosure to completion in September.

Funding needs and tax changes

The KRW 15 billion mining investment is being funded from internal cash and outside financing, with KRW 5 billion secured through the 17th convertible bond series. A tax on virtual asset gains is scheduled from January 2027, and whether it takes effect and how it is structured could affect the holding model. Depending on how funding is raised, dilution for shareholders could also vary.

12

Overall view

Bitplanet is building new bitcoin mining and AI data center businesses on top of reported results that show revenue declining for three straight years from 2023 and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders for four straight years from 2022 to 2025.

The 2025 operating loss was KRW 4.52 billion, and Q3 2026 still posted an operating loss of KRW 1.97 billion.

On the other side, net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 2.88 billion in Q3 2026, and the Q2 2026 operating loss of KRW 767 million was the smallest of the last five quarters. The purchase of 1,204 mining machines was completed in September 2026, with a target of 7 BTC a month.

However, the mining break-even of USD 81,000 to 85,000 per bitcoin means price swings feed directly into results. The AI data center plan has no fixed site or scale, and the company has pointed to 2027 as the year to prove results.

Bullish points include cash generation from the SI business, direct production through mining, and the tax changes scheduled for 2027 that could shift the position of listed DAT companies. Bearish points include recurring losses, crypto valuation swings and execution risk in the pivot.

The next checkpoints are the full-year operating results in the 2026 annual report and the scale of mining revenue in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
