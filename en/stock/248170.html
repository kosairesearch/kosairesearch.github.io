Report overview

Profit Recovery Stalls in Q3 as Holding Merger Looms

Sempio Foods Company's operating profit recovered to KRW 24.5 billion in 2025, but Q3 2026 operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders both fell sharply year on year, while the merger with its holding company announced in November 2026 is the next item to track.

02

Business structure

Sempio Foods Company sells soy sauce-centered fermented seasonings alongside non-fermented-seasoning products, and it operates as the core operating unit under the holding company Sempio Company.

In the first half of 2026, product and merchandise sales before sales deductions and returns provisions were KRW 228.5 billion, of which fermented seasonings accounted for KRW 118.8 billion and other products for KRW 109.7 billion.

The two categories are similar in size, so non-seasoning products are a meaningful part of the mix. Overseas sales in the same period were KRW 35.9 billion, about 15.7% of product and merchandise sales, with both categories contributing to growth.

Promotion work is handled by a group affiliate, whose sales fell from KRW 22.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 17.7 billion in 2025 and declined a further 14.9% in the first half of 2026, which the company attributed to adjustments in promotion staffing as large discount store outlets shrink.

Sempio Foods' own consolidated sales in the first half of 2026 were KRW 204.3 billion with operating profit of KRW 11.5 billion, matching the sum of the first and second quarters.

The company competes with large food makers in the fermented seasoning and condiment market, and based on 2025 results it had one of the lower operating margins among peers. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 265.1 billion and total liabilities were KRW 115.2 billion.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on reported results, annual revenue rose from KRW 371.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 383.4 billion in 2023 (up 3.3% year over year), KRW 404.9 billion in 2024 (up 5.6% year over year), and KRW 408.9 billion in 2025 (up 1.0% year over year), increasing for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Among 2022-2025, the largest year was 2025 and the smallest was 2022. Operating profit was KRW 11.1 billion in 2022, KRW 9.85 billion in 2023 (down 11.5% year over year), KRW 6.46 billion in 2024 (down 34.4% year over year), and KRW 24.5 billion in 2025 (up 278.9% year over year).

Among 2022-2025, the largest year was 2025 and the smallest was 2024. The operating margin declined from 3.0% in 2022 to 2.6% in 2023 and 1.6% in 2024, then rose to 6.0% in 2025. The company cited stabilized raw material prices and reduced promotional spending as the reasons for the improved 2025 results.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 13.1 billion in 2022, KRW 10.4 billion in 2023 (down 20.4% year over year), KRW 10.1 billion in 2024 (down 3.5% year over year), and KRW 19.9 billion in 2025 (up 98.2% year over year).

Net income including non-controlling interests is also shown as KRW 13.1 billion, KRW 10.4 billion, KRW 10.1 billion, and KRW 19.9 billion over the same period, and these figures should be read separately from net profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

Operating cash flow grew from KRW 15.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 58.3 billion in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 54.1% in 2022 to 43.5% in 2025. On a quarterly basis, third-quarter 2025 operating profit of KRW 12.8 billion became a high comparison base.

After that, it moved to KRW 2.94 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (down 77.0% quarter over quarter), KRW 6.84 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 132.9% quarter over quarter), KRW 4.66 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (down 31.9% quarter over quarter), and KRW 6.31 billion in the third quarter of 2026 (up 35.3% quarter over quarter and down 50.6% year over year).

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 110.4 billion, up 1.5% year over year, but profit declined.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 9.33 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then fell for two consecutive quarters to KRW 1.78 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.36 billion in the third quarter.

The sum of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the most recent four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 to third quarter of 2026) is KRW 14.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The fermented seasoning market where Sempio Foods operates is heavily affected by changes in distribution. As large discount store outlets shrink, the scale of on-site promotion staffing is being adjusted, and promotion service sales have declined accordingly.

By contrast, the monthly service fee per promotion worker rose from the 2.8 million won range in 2024 to the 3.0 million won range in the first half of 2026. On the cost side, lower cost of sales supported the 2025 profit recovery.

Within the industry, the company had one of the lower operating margins among peers in 2025, and the 2025 recovery narrowed that gap in part. Overseas sales of about 15.7% of the mix can offset some of the domestic channel shift.

In terms of cycle, the 2026 quarterly results show a phase in which profit recovers when raw material costs are stable and a phase in which higher marketing spending pressures profit again.

Against large food makers, the product mix centered on soy sauce and other fermented seasonings and the scale of that mix are the key comparison points. The outlook for the wider industry is not presented here as a numerical forecast, and channel restructuring and promotion costs remain the main variables.

06

Outlook

The nearest structural change is the merger in which the holding company Sempio Company will be absorbed into the operating company Sempio Foods Company. Under the announced terms, each share of Sempio Company is to receive about 2.088 common shares of Sempio Foods Company.

After the announcement, press reports said regulators had issued a correction order requiring supplementary disclosure. Headlines reported that combining the holding and operating companies was intended to secure funds for a factory investment. The scale and schedule of that investment have not been confirmed.

Once the merger proceeds, shareholders of the holding company will hold shares in the operating company, so governance and share allocation both change. On results, operating profit in Q3 2026 fell year on year, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders has declined for two consecutive quarters.

Whether sales at the promotion affiliate continue to shrink is another checkpoint. Because higher marketing spending weighed on profitability in Q2 2026, the level of promotion spending will likely shape the operating margin in coming quarters.

The next regular report is the 2026 annual report, which will fix the full-year 2026 operating margin and profit attributable to controlling shareholders as disclosed figures.

Risk factors

Distribution risk

If large discount store outlets keep shrinking, promotion staffing and promotion service sales could continue to decline. Sales at the promotion affiliate are tied to the group's internal transaction structure, which complicates earnings interpretation. Because domestic offline channels carry significant weight, the pace of channel restructuring matters.

Marketing and promotion cost risk

In Q2 2026, higher promotion costs from expanded brand and product marketing reduced operating profit. If promotion costs rise again, the operating margin recovered in 2025 could reverse. Whether the profit gains made during a period of stable raw material prices can be sustained is the next item to check.

Governance and merger risk

The absorption merger of the holding company could be delayed as supplementary disclosure and a correction order are discussed. After the merger, holding company shareholders will hold operating company shares, so share allocation and governance change together. Uncertainty remains until the merger terms and factory investment plans are specified.
