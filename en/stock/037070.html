02

Business structure

Paseco is an appliance maker founded in 1986.

Its seasonal lineup includes oil stoves, heaters and window-type air conditioners, its kitchen appliances include gas cooktops, hoods and induction cooktops, and its living appliances include humidifiers and water purifiers produced on an OEM and ODM basis (per the WiseReport Company Monitor).

Because seasonal products carry a large weight, quarterly results are sensitive to weather.

Its main export product, wick-type heaters, is sold in more than 40 countries across the Middle East, North America and Europe, which makes overseas sales a high share of revenue, according to a 2021 analysis by The Electronic Times.

Etoday reported in 2024 that cumulative heater exports passed KRW 1 trillion starting from September 2018.

Window-type air conditioners were reported to have sold more than 100,000 units in 2020 with a market share above 60%, and the same report said the company could lose market leadership over the medium to long term even while it remains No. 1.

The rise of seasonal products such as window-type air conditioners and circulators has eased the seasonality that once depended on overseas heater shipments. The customer mix is shifting from B2B toward consumers (B2C).

As of a 2024 report, the company was widening its kitchen appliance lineup, including dishwashers, kimchi refrigerators and dish dryers-sterilizers, while strengthening its own online mall and home shopping channels. R&D spending rose from 1.41% of sales in 2021 to 1.67% in a later year.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results began in 2022 with sales of KRW 200.5 billion and an operating profit of KRW 15.8 billion, an operating margin of 7.9%. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year by sales.

In 2023, sales fell 26.4% year on year to KRW 147.5 billion, the smallest year in the period, and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.24 billion, an operating margin of -0.8%. Etoday reported that domestic and export sales were both weak that year.

In 2024, sales rose 6.9% to KRW 157.8 billion, but the operating loss widened to KRW 16.8 billion, an operating margin of -10.7%, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was also KRW 16.8 billion.

In 2025, sales rose 6.4% to KRW 167.9 billion, the second consecutive annual increase after 2023, and operating profit turned to KRW 3.3 billion, an operating margin of 2.0%. Attributable net profit also turned positive at KRW 3.7 billion.

The 2025 operating profit was smaller than the KRW 5.36 billion posted in Q3 2025 alone, so quarterly swings dominate the annual figure. Q4 2025 sales fell 47.4% quarter on quarter to KRW 29.7 billion and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.49 billion.

Q1 2026 sales were KRW 28.1 billion with an operating loss of KRW 3.37 billion, as losses continued and widened. Q2 2026 sales rose 66.2% quarter on quarter to KRW 46.7 billion and operating profit returned at KRW 2.35 billion.

Q3 2026 sales reached KRW 57.9 billion, up 23.9% quarter on quarter, with operating profit of KRW 3.1 billion. However, Q3 2026 operating profit fell 42.2% year on year and attributable net profit of KRW 3.13 billion fell 43.1% year on year. Operating margin improved in 2025 to 2.0%, but the Q3 2026 profitability was below the level of a year earlier.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for seasonal appliances depends heavily on climate, and WiseReport's Company Monitor notes that raw material and logistics cost volatility also weighs on profitability in that segment. Kitchen and living appliances are described as hurt by the downturn in housing construction.

On the other side, the growth of single-person households and value-seeking consumers supports demand for small appliances, a point The Electronic Times viewed positively in 2021. In window-type air conditioners, a 2020 report put the company's share above 60% and kept it at No. 1.

The same report warned that its market dominance could weaken over the medium to long term. The heater business ties to overseas demand, so exchange rate and foreign economic shifts can affect earnings.

WiseReport's Company Monitor says the company holds an edge in wick-type heaters and window-type air conditioners and is widening its lineup into premium wellness appliances and commercial equipment. Whether that edge converts into sales and margins will need to be confirmed in quarterly results after 2026.

06

Outlook

The company said it is continuing product development, with R&D spending rising from 1.41% of sales in 2021 to 1.67% in a later year. As of a 2024 report, it was widening its kitchen appliance lineup to strengthen its position in the B2C market. It is also actively selling built-in appliances directly to consumers.

Q3 2026 sales grew only 2.5% year on year, so whether the new B2C lines add visible revenue is the next point to watch. Seasonal sales depend on winter heating-season demand, so Q4 2026 and Q1 2027 results should show how much heating products contribute.

Quarterly profits continued from 2025, but Q1 2026 posted a loss, so the sustainability of operating margin is the key item to check. The 2026 annual report, the next periodic filing, will disclose full-year operating margin and segment trends. Comparing that margin with the 2.0% recorded in 2025 is the next step.

08

Bull factors

Return to profit in 2025 and restored quarterly profits

Full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 3.3 billion turned around the KRW 16.8 billion operating loss of 2024. Both operating profit and attributable net profit were positive in Q2 and Q3 2026. Sales also grew for two straight quarters from Q2 through Q3 2026.

Whether the window-type air conditioner sales growth that the company cited continues is the core of this bull case.

Overseas distribution and specialized product lines

Wick-type heaters were reported in 2021 to be sold in more than 40 countries across the Middle East, North America and Europe. A 2020 report put the window-type air conditioner share above 60%. WiseReport's Company Monitor says the company has an edge in wick-type heaters and window-type air conditioners. This product base and overseas network could help cushion swings in seasonal demand.

Reduced seasonality and B2C expansion

A 2021 assessment held that seasonal products such as window-type air conditioners and circulators eased the reliance on overseas heater shipments. As of a 2024 report, the company was expanding its kitchen lineup, including dishwashers and kimchi refrigerators, to strengthen its B2C channels. Raising R&D spending to 1.67% of sales supports its ability to respond with new products.

09

Bear factors

Fragile quarterly earnings

Operating losses continued in Q4 2025 and Q1 2026, and the Q1 2026 loss widened. As a result, attributable net profit over the last four quarters totaled only KRW 717 million.

The 2025 annual operating profit of KRW 3.3 billion was smaller than the KRW 5.36 billion posted in Q3 2025 alone, showing wide quarter-to-quarter swings.

A gap between sales recovery and profit

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 57.9 billion, up 2.5% year on year, yet operating profit fell 42.2%. Attributable net profit also dropped 43.1% year on year. Margin recovery lagging the sales recovery is the core of the bear case.

Smaller earnings and high business volatility

Attributable net profit was KRW 9.19 billion in 2022 but KRW 3.7 billion in 2025. The company posted an attributable net loss of KRW 16.8 billion in 2024. In 2023, sales fell 26.4% as domestic and export demand were both weak, which points to high business volatility.

10

Risk factors

Climate and seasonal demand

Demand for seasonal appliances swings with temperature and weather. WiseReport's Company Monitor cites climate change as a negative factor for seasonal profitability. A short or mild heating season could reduce stove and heater sales and widen quarterly swings.

Raw materials, logistics and exchange rates

WiseReport's Company Monitor notes that volatility in raw materials and logistics costs weighs on profitability. A high share of overseas sales means exchange rates and foreign demand can also affect results. When cost pressure rises, operating margin may be the first line to move.

Housing market and competition

Kitchen and living appliances are described as weakened by the housing construction downturn. A 2021 report raised the possibility that window-type air conditioner dominance could erode over the medium to long term even with the No. 1 position. If both factors coincide, the scope of the sales recovery could be limited.

Overall view

Paseco's disclosed results show a move out of losses into profit. The 2025 annual operating profit of KRW 3.3 billion reflects the turn from the KRW 16.8 billion operating loss of 2024, and quarterly profits continued in Q2 and Q3 2026. However, sales growth has not translated into profit growth.

Q3 2026 sales rose 2.5% year on year while operating profit fell 42.2%. Attributable net profit over the last four quarters totaled KRW 717 million, so the earnings base is thin. The bull points are the profit improvement tied to window-type air conditioners, the overseas network and B2C expansion.

The bear points are quarterly earnings volatility, climate sensitivity and demand linked to the housing market. The next items to confirm are Q4 2026 and full-year preliminary results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
