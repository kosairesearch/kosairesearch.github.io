02

Business structure

Asicland is an application-specific IC (ASIC) design solution company that runs development and mass-production businesses on a turnkey model, supporting chip design through wafer production, packaging and testing.

Revenue comes from development fees for customer chip designs and from mass-production sales of finished products supplied on customer orders.

In the first half of 2026, consolidated revenue was KRW 113.7 billion, of which development revenue was KRW 92.6 billion and mass-production revenue was KRW 21.2 billion, about 18.6% of the total.

In Q3 2026, development revenue was about KRW 35.6 billion and mass-production revenue about KRW 12.8 billion, showing that the mass-production side is expanding quickly. The main mass-production product is the memory controller, with supply growing for enterprise SSD (eSSD), eMMC and SD card controllers.

SK hynix is cited as a key customer, and press reports said the design contract value for an eSSD controller signed in August 2026 was raised from about KRW 31.9 billion to KRW 41.0 billion.

The company highlights its design experience on TSMC advanced processes and its cooperation with global customers, and it runs an R&D center in Taiwan. Mass-production revenue recurs for as long as products keep shipping, which differs from design-service revenue that moves with project schedules.

In competitive terms, the ability to convert development projects into mass production is cited as the key differentiator.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 69.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 74.2 billion in 2023 (up 6.5% year on year) and KRW 94.1 billion in 2024 (up 26.8%), then fell to KRW 72.8 billion in 2025 (down 22.5%). Of the 2022 to 2025 period, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 11.4 billion in 2022 (operating margin 16.4%) to KRW 3.86 billion in 2023 (down 66.3%, margin 5.2%). The company swung to an operating loss of KRW 17 billion in 2024, and the loss widened to KRW 27.6 billion in 2025, with an operating margin of -37.9%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 5.15 billion in 2022 and KRW 3.64 billion in 2023 (down 29.3%) to a net loss of KRW 14 billion in 2024 and KRW 26.6 billion in 2025. Equity at end-2025 was KRW 55.3 billion against total liabilities of KRW 165.7 billion, a debt ratio of 299.7%.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 10.6 billion in 2023 and -KRW 6.3 billion in 2025. The company said 2025 was a year in which key project schedules were adjusted and R&D spending increased, and that it acquired advanced-process design capability and AI semiconductor technology in the process.

By quarter, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 16.2 billion with an operating loss of KRW 4.48 billion, and Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 26.2 billion with an operating loss of KRW 9.94 billion. Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 54 billion with an operating loss of KRW 2.99 billion, narrower than the prior quarter.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 59.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 7.98 billion, a swing to profit. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 48.3 billion was 19.1% lower quarter on quarter, but operating profit of KRW 1.4 billion kept the company in the black.

Q2 operating profit reportedly included a one-time settlement gain of about KRW 5 billion from the end of a CXL development project, according to an estimate by Shinhan Investment Corp.

The company said the Q3 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.01 billion reflected foreign exchange losses booked as non-operating losses. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 3.67 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from downstream markets centers on the expansion of AI and memory semiconductors. The company says it is strengthening its AI and memory development capability and widening mass production of products such as storage controllers.

When it reported 2025 results, the company said demand for custom AI chips is growing in global markets and expected 2026 to bring continued mass-production revenue and new global project wins.

Industry reports indicate that mass production of next-generation eSSD controllers would begin in the second half of 2027, with supply volumes expanding from 2028. The company's results move with the product roadmaps of memory-controller customers and with the timing of development project completions.

In Q2 2026, revenue was recognized as a CXL development project ended, and analysts noted lower cost burden as most edge AI projects concluded. Competitively, Asicland's selling point is its turnkey model covering design, wafer production, packaging and testing.

Design-focused firms without manufacturing assets recognize revenue according to their customers' development schedules, which differs from manufacturing peers. The rate at which development projects convert into mass production is therefore a key variable for the company's medium-term position. Investment adjustments by memory customers could also affect project volumes and deserve monitoring.

Outlook

The direction the company has set out is continued mass-production revenue growth in 2026 and new global project wins.

The CEO said first-half 2026 revenue was the largest half-year revenue since the company was founded, and said the company will expand high value-added AI and memory projects and follow-on mass-production work. Brokerage estimates for the full year diverge.

According to FnGuide's consensus as reported, 2026 revenue is expected at KRW 189.5 billion and operating profit at KRW 7 billion, a swing to profit from the prior year. Hana Securities was reported to expect second-half 2026 mass-production revenue of about KRW 30 billion, roughly 40% higher than the first half.

The same report estimated that of a KRW 26.5 billion annual SD card controller contract, about KRW 4 billion was recognized in Q2 and about KRW 20 billion would be added in the second half.

Within development work, the next-generation eSSD controller project for SK hynix is a key variable, and its design contract value was raised in August 2026 from about KRW 31.9 billion to KRW 41.0 billion.

Industry reports expect mass production of next-generation eSSD controllers to begin in the second half of 2027, with supply expanding after 2028. The next checkpoint is the business report covering full-year 2026 results.

Because Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 48.3 billion beat pre-announcement estimates of about KRW 40 billion, annual figures should be read against the values confirmed in filings.

Risk factors

Timing volatility of development revenue

Development revenue has moved sharply from quarter to quarter with the start and end of customer projects. The Q2 2026 operating profit reportedly included a settlement gain tied to the end of a CXL project, and Q3 revenue fell 19.1% from Q2.

Because the SK hynix next-generation eSSD project is cited as a key variable, delays or completions in a single customer project could affect results directly.

Balance sheet and cash flow burden

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 165.7 billion, against equity of KRW 55.3 billion. The debt ratio of 299.7% came down from 2,159.3% in 2022 but rose from 98.2% in 2024. Operating cash flow was negative at -KRW 6.3 billion in 2025, which raises the importance of funding and working capital management.

Exchange rates and FX gains and losses

In Q3 2026, foreign exchange losses from heightened currency volatility were booked as non-operating losses, which drove the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.01 billion. The quarter shows that currency swings can pull net results down even when operating profit is positive. The currency mix of the company's assets and liabilities is only visible through its filings.

Overall view

The clearest change in Asicland's 2026 results is that development and mass-production revenue have grown together, with operating profit positive for two consecutive quarters.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 48.3 billion, operating profit of KRW 1.4 billion and first-half mass-production revenue of KRW 21.2 billion are all figures confirmed in filings.

Still open are the two consecutive loss years from 2024 and 2025 (operating loss of KRW 27.6 billion in 2025), the debt ratio of 299.7% at end-2025, and negative operating cash flow in 2025.

Because the Q2 profit reportedly included a one-time settlement gain, whether profitability repeats will need to be confirmed in future filings.

Mass-production revenue relies on expanding eSSD and memory controller supply to customers including SK hynix, and brokerage estimates project a large increase in mass-production revenue for 2026 and 2027, although the estimates differ widely.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 business report due by the end of March 2027 and Q1 2027 results expected around mid-May 2027. This report contains no buy or sell opinion and is provided for information only, summarizing filed figures and schedules.
