02

Business structure

LG H&H runs three segments: Beauty, Refreshment (beverages), and Home Care & Daily Beauty (HDB).

Based on preliminary third-quarter 2026 results, Beauty generated KRW 660.8 billion in sales, about 42% of the total, while Refreshment contributed KRW 497.4 billion (about 32%) and HDB contributed KRW 419.2 billion (about 27%).

Key Beauty brands include The History of Whoo, Dr.Groot, Dominas, the oral-care brand Yusimol, and VDL, and the company said on November 3, 2026 that The History of Whoo returned to growth on a recovery in duty-free sales. In North America, Dr.Groot continues to see demand across retail and digital channels.

The results of the North American subsidiary The Avon Company, sold in September 2026, have been excluded from consolidation since then. In China, sales fell 21.1% in the third quarter as the online business structure is being reorganized.

In HDB, the laundry detergent brand Pigi Morakella ranked first in Coupang's laundry detergent category for three consecutive months, according to the company.

Refreshment is the beverage business handling Coca-Cola and Monster Energy products, and softer demand for drinks and higher raw material costs reduced its third-quarter 2026 operating profit by 19.9%.

On the same day, the board resolved to buy the remaining 25% stake in Vivawave, which operates the color cosmetics brand Hince, bringing its ownership to 100%. Among peers, Amorepacific, Cosmax, and Kolmar Korea are the comparison group.

Market commentary highlights that major Korean ODM makers focus on skincare capacity of 200ml or less, while LG H&H's household goods business gives it large-volume production facilities.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 7.19 trillion in 2022 to KRW 6.8 trillion in 2023 (down 5.3% year on year), were roughly flat at KRW 6.81 trillion in 2024 (up 0.1%), and dropped to KRW 6.36 trillion in 2025 (down 6.7%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 711.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 487 billion in 2023 (down 31.5%), KRW 459 billion in 2024 (down 5.7%), and KRW 170.7 billion in 2025 (down 62.8%), declining for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

The operating margin narrowed from 9.9% in 2022 to 7.2% in 2023, 6.7% in 2024, and 2.7% in 2025, with profit falling far faster than sales.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders dropped to KRW 142.8 billion in 2023 and recovered to KRW 189.1 billion in 2024 (up 32.5%), before swinging to a net loss of KRW 100.1 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests also turned to a net loss of KRW 85.8 billion in 2025.

The balance sheet remains comparatively sturdy: the debt ratio fell from 33.5% in 2022 to 23.3% in 2025, and at end-2025 total equity was KRW 5.57 trillion, equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.45 trillion, and total liabilities were KRW 1.3 trillion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 446.4 billion in 2025, below KRW 497.3 billion in 2022, though cash generation held up while profit weakened.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit of KRW 46.2 billion in the third quarter of 2025 was followed by an operating loss of KRW 72.7 billion in the fourth quarter, when the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 251.7 billion.

The first quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 107.8 billion, followed by KRW 102.8 billion in the second quarter (down 4.7% quarter on quarter) and KRW 70.4 billion in the third (down 31.5%).

Third-quarter operating profit rose 52.4% year on year, yet profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 8.39 billion, down 53.9% year on year, so the operating-level improvement did not carry through to the bottom line. The combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 85.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The two biggest variables for the cosmetics industry are China and duty-free. In an October 2025 report, LS Securities pointed out that recovery in China's cosmetics market was slowing while competitive intensity was rising.

In a May 2026 report, Kyobo Securities analyzed that the Chinese unit reached profitability faster than expected in the first quarter of 2026, and that duty-free still posted double-digit profitability despite volume adjustments.

However, third-quarter 2026 China sales fell 21.1% year on year to KRW 115.1 billion, showing that the effects of the online restructuring remain.

North America is moving differently: excluding the Avon sale effect, North American sales grew about 5%, and Hanwha Investment & Securities highlighted the growth of the company's own brands there in a November 4, 2026 report. In domestic household goods, detergent sales are rising in online channels.

The beverage business faces softer domestic drink consumption and raw material cost pressure, which makes its demand backdrop unfavorable.

On competitive positioning, large-volume household goods capacity is cited as a differentiator, since major Korean ODM makers concentrate on small-volume skincare production while LG H&H has large-volume production infrastructure through its household goods business.

Duty-free is in a recovery phase, but volume management room remains, and expanded global marketing spend and Middle East issues were flagged as variables.

06

Outlook

The company says that short-term earnings may fluctuate while it reshapes its portfolio, and that it is building medium- to long-term growth through product innovation and stronger business competitiveness.

Securing 100% of Vivawave, the operator of Hince, is a concrete item, and the company said it will keep Hince's distinct identity while supporting its global expansion.

DB Securities said in a July 30, 2026 report that new channels including Sephora and Costco will be added in the second half, and that SKU expansion should sustain strong sales growth. According to a company press release, Dr.Groot has entered more than 400 Sephora offline stores in the United States.

Shinhan Investment Corp. said in a November 4, 2026 report that restructuring-related costs are expected to continue until the first half of 2027, and advised a longer-term approach rather than expecting a quick rebound.

NH Investment & Securities said the disposal of non-core businesses is largely complete, and that stabilization of China profitability and sustained growth of the core North American brands will determine earnings visibility.

Hanwha Investment & Securities said in a November 4, 2026 report that it cut its 2026 and 2027 operating profit estimates by 25.3% and 12.3%, respectively, while keeping its target price at KRW 350,000.

The same NH report expects the beverage business to see lower profit despite price increases, given weak domestic demand and raw material costs. The next checkpoints are whether the decline in China sales narrows in the first half of 2027 and whether Dr.Groot's North American growth continues.

08

Bull factors

Beauty loss narrowing and duty-free recovery

Beauty segment operating loss narrowed to KRW 10.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026. The company said The History of Whoo returned to growth on higher duty-free sales, while Dr.Groot, Dominas, Yusimol, and VDL also grew strongly. Offline store efficiency also contributed to better profitability. First-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 107.8 billion serves as the reference point for later quarters.

Growth in core North American brands

Excluding the Avon sale effect, North American sales grew about 5%. Dr.Groot continues to see steady demand across retail and digital channels, and the company announced it has entered more than 400 Sephora offline stores in the United States.

Hanwha Investment & Securities highlighted in a November 4, 2026 report that growth in the company's own brands in North America continues. Brokers broadly view North America as a variable that could raise medium- to long-term earnings visibility.

Financial capacity and cash generation

The debt ratio at end-2025 was 23.3%, lower than 33.5% in 2022. Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 5.57 trillion and total liabilities were KRW 1.3 trillion. Operating cash flow was KRW 446.4 billion in 2025, showing cash generation continued even in a year when operating profit fell sharply.

Once the remaining Vivawave stake is acquired, the company will fully control the Hince color cosmetics business.

09

Bear factors

Quarterly profit decline and cumulative net losses

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 70.4 billion fell 31.5% from the prior quarter, with declines in both the second and third quarters. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 8.39 billion, down 53.9% year on year.

As net losses have built up over four quarters, the burden of recovering annual earnings has grown. According to Hanwha Investment & Securities, operating profit fell short of a market forecast of KRW 101.3 billion.

China sales decline and beverage weakness

China sales in the third quarter of 2026 fell 21.1% year on year to KRW 115.1 billion, and the online business is still being restructured. The Refreshment segment posted sales of KRW 497.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 50.3 billion, down 3.1% and 19.9% year on year.

Weaker drink demand, higher raw material costs, and intense promotional competition overlapped. NH Investment & Securities said the beverage business's profit decline is unavoidable.

Restructuring costs and lower estimates

In a November 4, 2026 report, Shinhan Investment Corp. expected restructuring-related costs to persist until the first half of 2027. On the same day, Hanwha Investment & Securities cut its 2026 and 2027 operating profit estimates by 25.3% and 12.3%.

Operating profit of KRW 170.7 billion in 2025 is well below KRW 711.1 billion in 2022. If the cost tail extends, the pace of earnings recovery could slow.

Risk factors

China market and online restructuring

China sales fell 21.1% in the third quarter of 2026, and the company is reorganizing its online business to improve brand health. In October 2025, LS Securities noted that China's cosmetics market recovery was slowing while competition was intensifying.

A delay in stabilizing China profitability would push back the timing of overall earnings recovery. This risk can be checked in first-half 2027 quarterly results.

Duty-free and marketing cost burden

In a May 2026 report, Kyobo Securities cited duty-free volume adjustments, higher global marketing spend, and Middle East issues as burdens for the second quarter. Second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 102.8 billion fell 4.7% from the prior quarter.

HDB's third-quarter operating profit of KRW 31.0 billion fell 3.1% as marketing investment increased. If marketing costs rise faster than sales recover, the scope for margin improvement could be limited.

Portfolio reshaping and deal execution

The North American subsidiary The Avon Company was sold in September 2026, removing its results from consolidation, and the company will also buy the remaining 25% of Vivawave. Overlapping disposals and acquisitions make quarter-on-quarter sales comparisons harder.

The beverage business faces both raw material cost increases and promotional competition. If acquisition funding needs and beverage margin pressure coincide, financial flexibility could narrow.

12

Overall view

Recent results show both improving and weakening signals for LG H&H. The improving side includes a narrower Beauty segment loss and growth in core North American brands, while the weakening side includes a decline in profit attributable to controlling shareholders and falling China sales.

On the filed annual figures, operating profit fell from KRW 711.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 170.7 billion in 2025, and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 100.1 billion in 2025.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 107.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026, and the second and third quarters declined.

The debt ratio of 23.3% at end-2025 leaves room, but the combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 85.4 billion, so an earnings-based multiple cannot be calculated.

Brokerage target prices differ, with DB Securities at KRW 380,000 (July 30, 2026), and Hanwha Investment & Securities at KRW 350,000 and Shinhan Investment Corp. at KRW 335,000 (both maintained on November 4, 2026).

The next checkpoints are preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results, the 2026 annual report, and whether restructuring costs end in the first half of 2027. The operating margin's movement from 2.7% in 2025 after the portfolio changes is the key indicator to track.
