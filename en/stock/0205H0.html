02

Business structure

SK Securities No.14 Special Purpose Acquisition Company is a blank-check vehicle formed solely to merge with another company and listed on the KOSDAQ market on October 27, 2026.

It is classified under financial support services and operates as a shell company that uses offering proceeds to search for a merger target, without any product or service sales. If no merger is completed within three years of listing, the listing is revoked.

The offering was led solely by SK Securities Co., Ltd. over two days beginning October 15, 2026, and the price was fixed at the standard SPAC level of 2,000 won.

The total offering amounted to about 13 billion won, and the proceeds are disclosed to be used entirely to identify and pursue a merger with a promising unlisted company. The combined subscription ratio at the close of the first subscription day was 1.69 to 1.

Target sectors are presented broadly, including renewable energy, biopharma and medical devices, IT convergence systems, robotics applications, advanced materials, auto parts manufacturing, and IT and semiconductors.

On the ownership side, SK Securities Co., Ltd., the de facto controlling shareholder, reported holding 1,000,000 shares or 13.05% as of October 28, 2026, consisting of 995,000 convertible bonds and 5,000 common shares, with the holding purpose recorded as influence over management.

Mason Capital reported holding 500,000 shares or 6.98% as of October 29, 2026 for passive investment. Because the company competes with other SPACs listed around the same time for merger targets, its ability to source a target is the central operating variable.

Recent trends

On the first trading day after its October 27, 2026 listing, the stock opened and closed at 1,946 won, 2.70% below the 2,000 won offering price. Intraday it rose to 4,160 won before declining and failed to hold near the offering price by the close.

On October 28, 2026, net selling of 723,023 shares by a single account, equal to 10.84% of 6,670,000 listed shares, triggered a one-day investment caution designation. The same day's close fell more than 5% from the prior close, meeting the second condition.

The rule applies when net trading by one account reaches at least 2% of listed shares and the close moves by at least 5%, and the company had no prior designations in the preceding 5 or 15 days.

SK Securities Co., Ltd. reported holding 1,000,000 shares or 13.05% as of October 28, 2026, with the 5,000 common shares recorded at an acquisition price of 1,000 won. Mason Capital reported 500,000 shares or 6.98% as of October 29, 2026, with the reason recorded as the new listing.

Because a SPAC is not formed to generate profit, press coverage repeatedly notes that its share price cannot be explained by operating profit or growth rates.

As of November 11, 2026, the share price was 1,871 won, up 0.38% from the prior close, with market capitalization of 12.5 billion won and trading value of 100 million won. The price is trading below the 2,000 won offering price.

Outlook

The key variable ahead is whether a merger target can be secured and a merger completed within three years of listing. Target sectors are presented broadly, but no specific target company or agreement appears in current disclosures.

A SPAC merger requires KOSDAQ preliminary listing review approval, and in the merger case of IBK Special Purpose Acquisition Company No.14, which listed in 2020, the review was filed on April 30, 2021 and approved on August 5, 2021.

The same offering document states that a large volume of share purchase claims by dissenting shareholders could cause the merger to fall through. Because most of the SK Securities Co., Ltd. stake consists of convertible bonds, the timing and extent of conversion could change the ownership structure.

Mason Capital's reported passive investment purpose is also relevant to reading the shareholder structure. If no merger is completed, the listing is revoked and funds are returned through liquidation with principal and a modest deposit interest, so the three-year deadline sets the operating benchmark.

The next checkpoints are a merger agreement disclosure, whether a preliminary listing review application is filed, and further ownership change reports from major shareholders.

Risk factors

Macro

Changes in interest rates and capital market conditions alter both the valuation and financing environment of potential merger targets. Because a SPAC targets unlisted companies, weak markets can lengthen negotiations or make target sourcing harder. This could delay deployment of the 13 billion won in offering proceeds.

Merger execution

If a merger is not completed within three years of listing, the listing is revoked and liquidation proceeds. Precedent disclosures state that large share purchase claims by dissenting shareholders can cause a merger to fail. Because preliminary listing review approval is required, timing uncertainty also remains.

Liquidity and flows

With trading value near 100 million won, single-account trades can have an outsized effect on the share price. On October 28, 2026, net selling by one account equaled 10.84% of listed shares.

Because a large portion of major shareholder holdings are convertible bonds, the number of tradable shares could change depending on conversion timing.
