Report overview

Nikke Reliance and a Launch Gap Push Shift UP into a Q3 Net Loss

Shift UP's reported revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for two straight years through 2025, but falling sales of its main titles and FX-related non-operating losses turned the third quarter of 2026 into a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 25.5 billion.

  1. 1

    Revenue of KRW 294.5 billion (up 31.4%) and operating profit of KRW 181.4 billion (up 18.8%) in 2025 were the highest figures of the 2023–2025 period.

  2. 2

    Third-quarter 2026 revenue fell 38.8% year on year to KRW 46.2 billion, and operating profit fell 58.2% to KRW 20.7 billion.

  3. 3

    The third-quarter net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 25.5 billion reflected non-operating losses of KRW 53.7 billion, which the company linked to foreign-currency valuation and translation losses.

  4. 4

    Nikke generated KRW 35.3 billion in the third quarter, roughly 76% of total revenue, so results remain concentrated in one title.

  5. 5

    The reveal timing for Project Spirit has been moved from a target within 2026, and the company expects fourth-quarter operating expenses to rise from the third quarter.

02

Business structure

Shift UP's revenue comes from three lines. The first is Goddess of Victory: NIKKE, which generated KRW 35.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 20.6% from a year earlier, and which media reports put at about 76.4% of total revenue.

The second is Stellar Blade, a console and PC action adventure whose third-quarter revenue was KRW 5.9 billion, down 78.6% year on year as PS5 and PC sales slowed after launch.

The third is IP business such as figures, goods, trading card games and music, with other revenue of KRW 5.0 billion, up 48.7% year on year; the company says most of this is recognized as royalty income from IP licensing. The company said the weaker dollar widened the decline in won-converted NIKKE revenue.

For the predecessor Stellar Blade, North America, Europe and Japan accounted for 45.9%, 22.0% and 13.5% of sales in the first two months after launch. The Switch 2 version of Stellar Blade launched on November 6, 2026 under self-publishing.

The pipeline includes Project Spirit, the next flagship title under a publishing deal with Tencent, and new titles from Unbound, the Japanese studio Shift UP acquired 100% of on April 1, 2026. Starting with the next Stellar Blade title, Shift UP plans to handle publishing itself.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results rose from KRW 168.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 224.1 billion in 2024 (up 32.9%) and KRW 294.5 billion in 2025 (up 31.4%), with revenue growing two years in a row from 2024 through 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 111.1 billion in 2023 to KRW 152.7 billion in 2024 (up 37.5%) and KRW 181.4 billion in 2025 (up 18.8%). Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 106.7 billion in 2023, KRW 148 billion in 2024 (up 38.7%) and KRW 191.4 billion in 2025 (up 29.4%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same KRW 106.7 billion, KRW 148 billion and KRW 191.4 billion for those years. The operating margin moved from 65.9% in 2023 to 68.1% in 2024 and then 61.6% in 2025.

Operating profit grew more slowly in 2025 (18.8%) than revenue (31.4%), which is why the margin fell.

The debt ratio was 12.6% in 2023, 5.2% in 2024 and 17.6% in 2025, and year-end 2025 total equity of KRW 921.2 billion was entirely attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 162.1 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 100.4 billion in 2023, KRW 114.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 157.2 billion in 2025. In the first quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 47.3 billion and operating profit was KRW 21.5 billion, and the company attributed the profit decline to higher new-title development costs.

The second quarter recovered, with revenue of KRW 55.7 billion (up 17.8% from the prior quarter), operating profit of KRW 28.1 billion (up 30.7%) and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 41.7 billion (up 10.4%).

The third quarter's revenue of KRW 46.2 billion (down 17.1% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 20.7 billion (down 26.4%) reflected lower NIKKE sales and slowing Stellar Blade sales.

The company put the third-quarter operating margin at 44.8%, and operating expenses of KRW 25.5 billion were down 1.7% year on year.

A non-operating loss of KRW 53.7 billion turned the result into a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 25.5 billion, compared with a profit of KRW 54.6 billion a year earlier.

Revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 149.2 billion (down 35.1% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 70.3 billion (down 51.2%). Profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 through third quarter of 2026) totaled KRW 112.7 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic game companies unveiled many titles aimed at overseas markets at Summer Game Fest 2026. Listed peer NC announced that the global version of AION 2 would launch simultaneously in North America, South America, Europe and Asia in September 2026.

Commentators say overcoming cultural differences to win Western audiences remains the main challenge for these launches. The company said major titles from other publishers are scheduled from late 2026 into early 2027, which is one reason it adjusted the reveal timing for Project Spirit.

NIKKE has stayed near the top of app-store charts in Korea and Japan, while a 2026 press report said its China service underperformed expectations and fell out of major charts.

A Samsung Securities analyst said NIKKE's global version is shrinking more slowly thanks to updates, while NIKKE China and Stellar Blade are declining faster. A Meritz Securities analyst described 2026 as a period in which sales must be defended with NIKKE alone.

Console packaged games tend to concentrate sales early after launch, and analysts say Stellar Blade's revenue moves sharply with its release cycle. Shift UP has shifted toward building its own publishing capability, taking over distribution and marketing that outside publishers previously handled. That change adds overseas service capacity to a company that has historically focused on development.

06

Outlook

The company expects fourth-quarter 2026 operating expenses to rise from the third quarter, which it describes as early investment in new IP development and in in-house PC and console publishing.

Initial sales of the Switch 2 version of Stellar Blade are in line with internal expectations and are expected to be reflected in fourth-quarter results.

The follow-up title Stellar Blade: Blood Lane is being developed with the aim of securing a long development period, and detailed information will be released in stages.

The reveal of next flagship title Project Spirit has been moved from a target within 2026, though the company said build evaluations were completed positively. Its launch is generally expected from 2027 onward, and a Samsung Securities analyst noted that it could slip to 2028 depending on circumstances.

Shareholder returns include a cancellation of 320,000 treasury shares; this is part of 620,836 shares acquired this year, and the year's total cancellations reach 650,000 shares, about 1.1% of issued shares. The company said it is reviewing an additional share buyback program from the second half of 2026 onward.

FnGuide, as reported in October 2026, projected 2026 revenue of KRW 213.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 106.4 billion. A securities-industry estimate for the third-quarter operating profit of KRW 25.4 billion, compiled before the release, came in above the actual KRW 20.7 billion.

The next checkpoints are the reveal schedule and launch platforms for Project Spirit, and the quarterly revenue trend of NIKKE.

08

Bull factors

NIKKE rebounds in the charts after updates

Goddess of Victory: NIKKE ranked first in sales in Korea and Japan right after its 4th-anniversary update on October 29, 2026. It also topped app-store sales in Korea and Japan after its 3.5th-anniversary update in April 2026.

Third-quarter revenue fell from the prior quarter, but the update-driven climb into the top rankings is cited as a positive.

Expansion to the Switch 2 platform

The Switch 2 version of Stellar Blade launched on November 6, 2026 under self-publishing. The company said its initial sales are in line with expectations. With PS5 and PC sales slowing, the new platform gives a bullish case for a wider sales base. The company expects these sales to be reflected in fourth-quarter results.

Wider IP business and pipeline

Other revenue was KRW 5.0 billion in the third quarter, up 48.7% year on year. In April 2026 the company acquired 100% of Japanese studio Unbound, securing publishing rights for PC and console titles. The company said Project Spirit completed its build evaluations positively.

09

Bear factors

Continued decline in existing titles

Third-quarter NIKKE revenue fell 20.6% year on year, and Stellar Blade revenue fell 78.6%. The weaker dollar also widened the decline in won-converted revenue. A Samsung Securities analyst said the decline in NIKKE China and Stellar Blade is faster.

A launch gap and rising costs

Analysts have noted that no new title is scheduled for release in 2026, and Project Spirit is targeted for 2027. The company expects fourth-quarter operating expenses to rise from the third quarter. Headcount reached 460 at the end of the third quarter, up 39.8% year on year.

Non-operating swings from exchange rates

The third-quarter non-operating loss of KRW 53.7 billion reflected valuation and translation losses on foreign-currency assets as the won strengthened. As a result, the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 25.5 billion despite an operating profit. The gap between operating profit and net profit can widen with exchange-rate moves.

10

Risk factors

Launch timing delay

Project Spirit's reveal has been moved from a target within 2026. Although 2027 is the stated launch goal, analysts have raised the possibility of a delay to 2028. A longer gap between titles extends the period of dependence on NIKKE.

NIKKE operations and China service

In the third quarter, signs of illegal program use were reported in NIKKE raids, and players protested that the sanctions were insufficient. A 2026 press report said the China service underperformed expectations and dropped out of major charts. Because NIKKE accounts for most revenue, operating issues are a central source of earnings volatility.

Valuation gains and losses on foreign-currency assets

The company attributed the third-quarter loss to non-operating valuation and translation effects on foreign-currency assets. As of the end of June, it held USD holdings of KRW 210.4 billion and yen holdings of KRW 16.4 billion.

Each exchange-rate move can shift non-operating results, which can widen the gap between operating profit and net profit.

12

Overall view

Shift UP's reported results rose from 2023 through 2025 across revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders, and 2025 was the largest year of the period with revenue of KRW 294.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 181.4 billion.

In 2026, however, falling sales of NIKKE and Stellar Blade cut operating profit for the first three quarters to KRW 70.3 billion, down 51.2% year on year.

The third quarter then added a non-operating loss of KRW 53.7 billion, turning the result into a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 25.5 billion.

Bullish factors cited include NIKKE's climb to the top of app-store charts after updates, the early performance of the Switch 2 version, 48.7% growth in IP revenue and the shift to self-publishing.

Bearish factors include reliance on one title for about 76% of revenue, the gap between releases, the company's warning of higher fourth-quarter costs and exchange-rate effects on foreign-currency valuations.

Project Spirit's reveal has been moved and its launch is generally targeted from 2027, so new-title effects may reach results with a time lag. The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 results, the 2026 annual report and the reveal schedule for Project Spirit.
