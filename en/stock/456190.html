Labeled-Compound Specialist Returns to Profit

Curachem is a KONEX-listed specialist in radioisotope-labeled compound synthesis for early drug-development studies, and its operating profit reached KRW 1,736.34 million in 2025 after a loss in 2024.

02

Business structure

Curachem was founded in June 2009 and is headquartered in Osong, Cheongju. The company states that it provides custom synthesis of 14C radiolabeled, 3H radiolabeled and stable-isotope labeled compounds.

These compounds are used in in vivo studies of absorption, distribution, metabolism and excretion (ADME) of drug candidates. ADME data link toxicity assessment and efficacy verification in drug development.

In 2023, all revenue came from radioisotope-labeled compound synthesis, and there had been no revenue from experiment brokerage services since 2021.

The company also offers medicinal chemistry and process development services, such as hit-compound analysis and focused-library synthesis. Detailed revenue breakdowns by service line could not be confirmed in this research.

External profiles classify Curachem as a CRO and service provider rather than a therapeutic developer. According to a press profile from around 2019, its main customers are multinational pharmaceutical companies in the US and Europe. The same profile reported that the company had ranked first in market share in Japan.

A 2013 report noted that the DMPK field had almost no competitors and that trust built with Japanese drugmakers was a barrier for new entrants. In the global market, large CROs such as LabCorp and Charles River are cited as competitors or alternatives.

Large biotech companies such as Samsung Biologics and Celltrion operate different business models at a far larger scale, so direct comparison is of limited use. Headcount was 29 as of August 2026.

This shows that profit margins can improve sharply at a small company when fixed-cost pressure eases. Whether the improvement persists must be verified through quarterly results.

Scarce capability and customer trust

A press profile described Curachem as the only provider of 3H labeled compound synthesis in Asia. Trust built with Japanese drugmakers was assessed as a barrier that new entrants would find hard to overcome. Handling radioisotopes requires specialized facilities and staff, so entry takes time. These assessments rest on past reports, so the current competitive landscape needs separate confirmation.

Service expansion and cleaner capital structure

The company has outlined plans to expand into GMP labeled-compound synthesis and labeling services for biologic drugs. As drug development moves toward clinical stages, the quality standards and service prices required may rise.

On July 14, 2026, 80,000 common shares were additionally listed from the conversion of preferred shares. Resolving preferred shares simplifies the capital structure, though the increase in tradable shares is also a factor to weigh.
