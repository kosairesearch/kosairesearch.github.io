02

Business structure

Uniquest Coporation was founded in November 1995 and listed on KOSPI in August 2004 as a semiconductor distributor. It imports chips from non-memory semiconductor suppliers under distribution agreements, sells them to domestic customers, and also handles sales and technical support.

On the WiseReport breakdown, semiconductor distribution accounts for 97.71% of main product sales, with technical support fees at 2.20% and building rental at 0.09%. Domestic sales made up 99.80% of product sales in 2025, so the business is almost entirely domestic.

Intel and Qualcomm are named among its suppliers in analyst materials. Its application areas include automotive semiconductors, information and communication devices, ADAS, artificial intelligence and smart appliances. AI server business is now the main growth driver.

The company said on November 16, 2026 that it supplies GPU servers and HPC infrastructure to large domestic chip and telecom groups, built on partnerships with global server makers such as Supermicro and ASUS.

The customer base is concentrated in large chip and telecom companies, so the capital spending of end customers largely determines volume. Distribution rights and customer relationships function as a barrier to entry.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual figures, sales fell 9.9% from KRW 739.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 665.9 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 710.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 730 billion in 2025, a two-year increase from 2024 through 2025.

Operating profit dropped 16.9% from KRW 40.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 34 billion in 2023, the smallest year in the 2022 to 2025 window, and recovered to KRW 34.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 36.7 billion in 2025. The largest operating profit year in that window was 2022.

The operating margin stayed around 5%, at 5.5% in 2022, 5.1% in 2023, 4.8% in 2024 and 5.0% in 2025, which shows how thin the distribution margin is even as sales grew.

Controlling-shareholder net profit fell from KRW 49.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 19.3 billion in 2023 (down 60.6%) and KRW 5.29 billion in 2024 (down 72.6%), then returned to KRW 20.6 billion in 2025 (up 289.7%).

Net profit for 2025 was KRW 19.3 billion, below controlling-shareholder net profit, so the non-controlling side posted a loss. Operating cash flow moved from -KRW 46.3 billion in 2022 and -KRW 4.76 billion in 2023 to KRW 2.15 billion in 2024, turning positive, and reached KRW 32.5 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales rose from KRW 176.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 212 billion in the fourth quarter, while controlling-shareholder net profit fell 67.7% to KRW 1.99 billion.

Sales then fell 20.3% to KRW 169 billion in the first quarter of 2026, rose to KRW 216.3 billion in the second quarter and reached KRW 443 billion in the third quarter.

Operating profit rose for three straight quarters after the fourth-quarter 2025 low, to KRW 11.5 billion, KRW 16.6 billion and KRW 35.8 billion in the first three quarters of 2026.

The company said that higher memory and CPU sales tied to AI investment and expanding HPC server business drove the third quarter, and that gross margin rose to 12.5% from 9.7% a year earlier. Third-quarter operating profit of KRW 35.8 billion came close to the full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 36.7 billion. Controlling-shareholder net profit for the last four quarters totaled KRW 67.7 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Semiconductor distribution sits between chip designers and end-demand companies, so it is directly exposed to demand cycles. The main demand driver now is AI infrastructure. Analysts have noted that data centers became the second-largest market in the semiconductor industry as AI and generative AI demand grew.

Industry commentary for early 2026 said AI chips are driving market growth, with rising demand for processors, HBM and networking components. Memory price recovery and HBM production have also been cited as contributors to DRAM supplier profits.

Automotive semiconductors are expected to grow with autonomous driving, and demand for MCUs in smart appliances is rising.

Most listed companies in the same retail and consumer goods sector run businesses such as department stores, supermarkets, convenience stores and rental services, where consumer spending and store operations drive results.

Uniquest Coporation differs in that its results turn on the semiconductor cycle and on AI server project wins. Its thin margin of around 5% resembles consumer retail, but its concentrated customer base and server-maker partnerships as supply channels make its structure distinct.

06

Outlook

The starting point for the outlook is whether the AI server and HPC supply that lifted third-quarter results continues beyond the fourth quarter. Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 443 billion were larger than the combined KRW 169 billion and KRW 216.3 billion of the first two quarters of 2026.

Quarterly sales moved from KRW 169 billion to KRW 443 billion within a single year, so the quarterly swings are very large. Third-quarter operating profit of KRW 35.8 billion came close to the full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 36.7 billion, meaning one quarter now approaches last year's annual level.

The next disclosed checkpoint is fourth-quarter 2026 results, and whether fourth-quarter sales hold near the third-quarter level will show whether AI server supply is one-off or continuing. The 2026 annual report will allow a comparison of full-year sales and operating margin with KRW 730 billion and 5.0% in 2025.

Whether the annual operating margin moves above the 5% range will show whether the margin structure has changed. How long supply volumes with global server-maker partners last will only be visible through disclosures and company announcements. Product roadmap changes at the chip suppliers holding distribution rights could also affect future volumes.

08

Bull factors

AI server supply lifted quarterly results

Third-quarter sales and operating profit both rose sharply from a year earlier. The company attributed this to higher memory and CPU sales tied to AI investment and expanding HPC server business. If this continues, GPU server supply to large domestic chip and telecom groups could become a new pillar of earnings.

Recovery in earnings capacity

Controlling-shareholder net profit for the last four quarters totaled KRW 67.7 billion, above the KRW 20.6 billion recorded for 2025 as a full year. Operating profit rose for three consecutive quarters from the first quarter to the third quarter of 2026. The company said gross margin in the third quarter rose to 12.5% from 9.7% a year earlier.

Cash generation recovery and industry demand

Operating cash flow moved from -KRW 46.3 billion in 2022 to positive in 2024 and reached KRW 32.5 billion in 2025. Analysts have noted that data centers became the second-largest semiconductor market. Demand for automotive chips and smart appliance chips is also reported to be rising.

09

Bear factors

A thin-margin distribution model

The operating margin fell from 5.5% in 2022 to 4.8% in 2024 and was 5.0% in 2025. Sales stayed in the KRW 700 billion range, yet the margin near 5% means higher sales do not translate directly into higher profit. Even with large sales, quarterly operating profit fluctuates widely.

Volatility in quarterly results

Sales fell 20.3% sequentially to KRW 169 billion in the first quarter of 2026 before jumping to KRW 443 billion in the third quarter. Controlling-shareholder net profit fell 67.7% in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.99 billion. With swings this large, the earnings trend is hard to read from only one or two quarters.

Leverage and capital structure

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 75.6% in 2022 to 112.7% in 2023 and was 110.3% at the end of 2025. Total liabilities of KRW 270.2 billion exceed total equity of KRW 244.9 billion.

Net profit of KRW 19.3 billion for 2025 was below controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 20.6 billion, so the non-controlling side affects the overall earnings structure.

10

Risk factors

Dependence on suppliers and distribution rights

Distribution volumes rely on distribution agreements with chip makers, so changes in supplier product strategy or contract terms could affect the revenue structure. Intel and Qualcomm are named as key suppliers in analyst materials. Supplier-level revenue shares can be checked in periodic reports.

Reliance on the AI investment cycle

Much of third-quarter profit came from expanding AI server and HPC supply. If AI facility investment slows, customer orders could decline, and quarterly sales could drop quickly as they did when first-quarter 2026 sales fell to KRW 169 billion. Partnerships with server makers do not guarantee volume.

Financial burden and quarterly swings

Total liabilities of KRW 270.2 billion and a 110.3% debt-to-equity ratio could reduce the cushion if interest or working-capital costs rise. Operating cash flow was positive in 2025 but negative in 2022 and 2023, so cash generation has varied widely by year. Concentration in semiconductor distribution also produces large swings in quarterly results.

12

Overall view

Uniquest Coporation is structured around semiconductor distribution, which makes up most of its revenue, with an operating margin that has stayed near 5%.

In the third quarter of 2026, AI server and HPC supply expansion produced sales of KRW 443 billion and operating profit of KRW 35.8 billion, a visible step up in earnings capacity.

Controlling-shareholder net profit for the last four quarters totaled KRW 67.7 billion, well above the KRW 20.6 billion for 2025 as a full year.

On the other side, quarterly swings are large, with first-quarter 2026 sales declining and fourth-quarter 2025 profit falling sharply, and the 110.3% debt-to-equity ratio and KRW 270.2 billion in total liabilities remain burdens.

The bullish factors are AI server demand and the pace of earnings recovery, while the bearish factors are the thin margin and concentration in semiconductor distribution. The next checkpoints are sales at the end of the fourth quarter of 2026 and the annual operating margin in the 2026 annual report.

Whether AI server supply carried through into quarterly results at those two points will indicate the company's underlying earnings capacity.
