Report overview

Sales Up, Margins Down: Terminal Redevelopment in Focus

GwangjuShinsegae's sales rose for two straight years through 2025, yet its operating margin fell from 35.5% to 28.5%, and quarterly operating profit has declined for three consecutive quarters since the fourth quarter of 2025.

02

Business structure

GwangjuShinsegae is a subsidiary in which Shinsegae holds a stake, operating the Gwangju Shinsegae department store in Gwangju alongside a passenger terminal and real estate leasing.

In the first quarter of 2025, the department store accounted for 95% of total sales of KRW 45.4 billion, or KRW 41.8 billion, while the terminal accounted for 5%, or KRW 3.5 billion.

By revenue type in 2022, specified-purchase income made up 63% of sales, leasing income 24%, and direct purchases 10%, according to a 2023 brokerage report. Cost of revenue as a share of sales fell from 13.1% in 2018 to 8.4% in 2022.

Gross sales in 2022 were KRW 768.9 billion and net sales were KRW 184.9 billion, with gross sales adding specified-purchase costs to accounting sales. Under a management fee contract with Shinsegae, the company pays 2.0% of gross sales for store management and purchasing services, per the same report.

Shinsegae became the largest shareholder in September 2021 after acquiring the stake held by Vice Chairman Jung Yong-jin. The main growth driver is the new wing.

In a July 2026 statement, the company said first-half department store sales rose about 10% year on year, with luxury brands and Play Spot sales in the new wing up about 15%. Hyundai Department Store's Gwangju outlet has been cited as the main competitive challenge.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales went from KRW 184.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 179.6 billion in 2023, down 2.9%, then rose to KRW 183.6 billion in 2024, up 2.2%, and KRW 184.2 billion in 2025, up 0.3%, marking two straight years of growth. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year.

Operating profit fell from KRW 65.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 54.9 billion in 2023, down 16.3%, and to KRW 52.3 billion in 2024, down 4.7%, before edging up to KRW 52.5 billion in 2025, up 0.3%. Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the smallest year.

Sales kept growing after 2024 while operating profit moved more gently, which explains the margin decline. Operating margin was 35.5% in 2022, 30.6% in 2023, 28.5% in 2024 and 28.5% in 2025.

In 2022, the rise in the selling and administrative expense ratio was mainly driven by higher commission fees and depreciation of property, plant and equipment, according to the 2023 brokerage report.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 56.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 48.3 billion in 2023, KRW 46.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 44.5 billion in 2025, declining for three consecutive years since 2023. Net income including non-controlling interests was the same in each of these years.

The debt ratio rose from 14.1% in 2023 to 46.7% in 2024 and was 46.0% in 2025. That jump coincides with the KRW 47 billion outlay for the Gwangju bus terminal site in 2024 and total investment spending of KRW 503.1 billion, per a 2025 media report.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 865.2 billion and total liabilities were KRW 397.6 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 68.5 billion in 2022, KRW 62.3 billion in 2023, KRW 77 billion in 2024 and KRW 67.6 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the peak was Q4 2025, with sales of KRW 52.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 17.7 billion.

Operating profit then fell for three consecutive quarters, to KRW 15.7 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 13.9 billion in Q2 and KRW 11.7 billion in Q3 on sales of KRW 48.7 billion, KRW 48.4 billion and KRW 44.1 billion. Q3 2026 sales rose 6.5% and operating profit rose 16.4% year on year.

Preliminary nine-month 2026 operating profit of KRW 41.3 billion was up 18.9%, disclosed on November 10, 2026. The company has said that holiday timing shifts between years can create gaps in reported results.

05

Industry analysis

The direction of the department store business can be read indirectly through parent Shinsegae's results.

Shinsegae reported first-quarter 2026 consolidated sales of KRW 3.2144 trillion and operating profit of KRW 197.8 billion, and said its department store business grew double digits year on year on rising foreign tourist visits. In the same release, sales at its Daegu and Gwangju stores rose 11%.

GwangjuShinsegae depends on consumption in the Gwangju area, so regional investment flows matter. In June 2026, its shares jumped more than 20% intraday on hopes for a government semiconductor investment plan in the Honam region, with Samsung Electronics and SK hynix named as possible investors.

That move was driven by policy expectations, so the scale and timing of any actual investment remain to be confirmed. On the competitive side, Hyundai Department Store's Gwangju outlet, The Hyundai Gwangju, has been cited as a rival.

The company runs one department store and a terminal site, which differs in scale and trade-area structure from Shinsegae's larger metropolitan stores. Expanding tenant brands from about 500 to more than 1,000 would change its local draw and competitive position if completed.

06

Outlook

The most important medium-term variable is the Gwangcheon terminal redevelopment. The city and GwangjuShinsegae finalized public contributions totaling KRW 149.7 billion, to be paid as KRW 12.9 billion in kind and KRW 136.8 billion in cash, according to a February 2026 report.

The project would consolidate bus boarding underground, expand the department store above ground and add hotel, cultural and office facilities. The city said it aimed to start construction by the end of 2026 after amending the district unit plan in the first half, with completion projected for 2033.

The company also agreed to share part of the cost of the Sangmu-Gwangcheon urban rail line, but a specific agreement will follow after inclusion in the national rail plan and a preliminary feasibility study.

In a 2025 report, the company set a 2030 target of KRW 350 billion in annual sales and KRW 150 billion in EBITA, from a base of roughly KRW 180 billion. Tenant brands are planned to rise from about 500 to more than 1,000 after the expansion.

In the latest monthly disclosure, October 2026 sales were about KRW 17.5 billion, up 0.5% year on year, and cumulative sales through October were KRW 158.7 billion, up 6.6%. These are preliminary figures disclosed on November 10, 2026 and may change after the external audit.

The next checkpoints are the start of construction, progress on the district unit plan amendment, and whether the Sangmu-Gwangcheon agreement is signed.

Bull factors

Honam semiconductor investment hopes

In June 2026, shares jumped more than 20% intraday on hopes for a government semiconductor investment plan in the Honam region. The thesis is that a regional economic lift could also boost sales at the Gwangju department store. The move rests on policy expectations, so actual investment scale and timing still need confirmation.

Growth led by the new wing

The company said first-half 2026 department store sales rose about 10% year on year. Sales in the new wing, centered on luxury brands and Play Spot, grew about 15%. Cumulative sales through October 2026 were up 6.6%, indicating continued top-line growth.

Public contribution fixed for redevelopment

The city and GwangjuShinsegae fixed the public contribution for the Gwangcheon terminal redevelopment at KRW 149.7 billion. If executed, the project would add department store expansion along with hotel, cultural and office space. The start-of-construction target was given as the end of 2026.

09

Bear factors

Three years of declining profit

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 48.3 billion in 2023, KRW 46.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 44.5 billion in 2025. Operating margin declined from 35.5% in 2022 to 28.5% in 2025. Sales rose after 2024, but that growth has not translated into higher profit.

Consecutive quarterly profit declines

Operating profit peaked at KRW 17.7 billion in Q4 2025, then fell to KRW 15.7 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 13.9 billion in Q2 and KRW 11.7 billion in Q3. Q3 2026 sales declined 8.8% from the prior quarter. Year-on-year figures improved, but the quarterly trend has weakened.

Debt burden and heavy investment

The debt ratio jumped from 14.1% in 2023 to 46.7% in 2024 and was 46.0% in 2025. In 2024, total spending reached KRW 503.1 billion, including a KRW 470 billion site purchase. That has raised concerns about financial strain.

10

Risk factors

Reliance on policy expectations

Honam semiconductor expectations depend on government plans and corporate investment decisions. If the hopes do not turn into actual consumption gains, share price volatility could increase since the stock has moved on expectations. A change in regional investment plans would also weaken the explanation for new-wing growth.

Funding and timing of the large project

The redevelopment requires large funding, including KRW 149.7 billion in public contributions. The share of Sangmu-Gwangcheon line costs still awaits a specific agreement, so timing could shift. Delays in public procedures would raise the investment burden and change when results reflect the project.

Regional concentration and rival stores

Department store sales are concentrated in Gwangju, so they are directly affected by local economic shifts. If rival stores such as Hyundai Department Store's Gwangju outlet keep expanding, competition for customers could intensify. Whether the tenant expansion defends against that competition is the key question.

In 2026, nine-month cumulative operating profit rose 18.9%, but quarterly operating profit has declined for three consecutive quarters since its Q4 2025 peak.
