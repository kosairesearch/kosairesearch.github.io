Report overview

Ad Wins Lift Q3 Profit; Full-Year Recovery Is the Test

Q3 2026 operating profit rose 48.2% year on year, but full-year 2025 operating profit remained below the 2022 level, so whether ad wins extend the quarterly rebound into a full-year recovery is the next test.

02

Business structure

Incross is a integrated marketing company that is described as a subsidiary of SK Networks, and its business is split into two pillars, advertising and commerce.

The advertising business centers on integrated marketing proposals that combine display and search advertising across online and offline media, along with an expansion of direct agency-of-record relationships with advertisers.

Full-year 2025 advertising revenue was KRW 417.7 billion, with transaction volume of KRW 530.6 billion, which accounts for the bulk of the KRW 50.5 billion consolidated revenue. Its search-advertising subsidiary handles search ads and creative production, and its Q4 2025 revenue grew 61.0% year on year.

The commerce business sells products through channels such as T deal and Hyundai Shop, with full-year 2025 commerce revenue of KRW 7.56 billion, up 6.7%. In 2026 the company was selected as operator of an online Korean beef marketplace and took on operating roles for two more commerce platforms.

Its AI business includes the Stellaize AI content marketing platform, and a paid tier was launched in October 2026. Because advertising spans gaming, finance, food and beverage, and public-sector advertisers, shifts in spending in any one sector feed directly into results.

In competition, the company is compared with large agencies such as Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide and with media representative firms such as kt nasmedia.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 53.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 46.8 billion in 2023, down 12.2%, then rose to KRW 48.8 billion in 2024, up 4.3%, and to KRW 50.5 billion in 2025, up 3.4%.

Revenue grew in 2024 and 2025, two consecutive years of increases after the 2023 trough. 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest for revenue.

Operating profit fell from KRW 19.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.6 billion in 2023, down 35.6%, and to KRW 10 billion in 2024, down 20.6%, before recovering to KRW 11.5 billion in 2025, up 14.8%. 2024 was the smallest year for operating profit.

Operating margin declined from 36.8% in 2022 to 27.0% in 2023 and 20.5% in 2024, then recovered to 22.8% in 2025. In 2023, operating profit fell by a far larger percentage than revenue, so margin compressed first.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 15.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.6 billion in 2023, down 18.0%, and KRW 9.73 billion in 2024, down 23.0%, before reaching KRW 12 billion in 2025, up 23.2%. Net income including non-controlling interests was also reported as KRW 12 billion for 2025.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 11.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 24.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 42 billion in 2024, then fell to KRW 26.1 billion in 2025. At year-end 2025, total liabilities were KRW 164.7 billion and total equity was KRW 130 billion, a debt-to-equity ratio of 126.7%.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 13 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 17 billion in Q4 2025, up 30.3% quarter on quarter, then fell to KRW 10.5 billion in Q1 2026, down 38.3%, before rising to KRW 11.7 billion in Q2, up 12.0%, and KRW 15.2 billion in Q3, up 29.5%.

Operating profit was KRW 1.54 billion at the Q1 2026 trough, then KRW 2.83 billion in Q2 and KRW 5.41 billion in Q3, up 91.3% quarter on quarter and 48.2% year on year. The company attributed the Q1 2026 decline to higher personnel costs and security system upgrades.

Advertising transaction volume rose 32.7% in Q2 2026 and search advertising reached record quarterly revenue for a second quarter. In Q3, large campaign wins from gaming and public-sector clients lifted advertising revenue by 20.3%, while commerce revenue fell 5.4% on lower transaction volume at key channels.

The company said operating leverage from higher revenue improved profitability, and the Q3 operating margin was 35.6%. In Q4 2025, weaker economic conditions increased demand for performance marketing, which lifted search advertising volume.

05

Industry analysis

The digital advertising market is showing rising demand for performance marketing as economic conditions weaken and advertisers focus on cost efficiency. Incross tied its subsidiary's search-advertising growth in Q4 2025 to this trend.

Campaign activity was concentrated in gaming, finance, and food and beverage, while some public-sector campaigns declined in Q4 2025. This shows that ad demand varies by sector and client type. In Q3 2026, large campaign wins from gaming and public-sector clients rose, so public-sector demand again supported results.

AI transformation is becoming a common challenge across the ad industry. Average monthly spending among paid generative AI users was KRW 35,932, according to a survey, which suggests consumers pay for AI services, although whether that translates into agency revenue needs separate confirmation.

Incross's AI offerings include Stellaize, its proprietary AI platform, and the iNova multi-agent tool. Among peers, Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide are much larger full-service agencies, while kt nasmedia is the closer comparison in media representation.

On the commerce side, transaction volume is volatile, and revenue moves quickly when volume at key channels falls, as seen in the Q3 2026 decline.

06

Outlook

The company describes Q4 2026 as the year-end advertising peak and plans to focus resources on large campaigns from major advertisers. Winning new advertisers and strengthening service competitiveness through AI-based solutions are also part of its Q4 plan.

In commerce, it plans to expand year-end promotions and large themed sales while strengthening the product lineup of key brands. In its Q1 2026 results, the company said new commerce platforms would contribute meaningfully to revenue from the second half.

In Q2, new operating contracts for the online Korean beef marketplace, Sonoa I Ready Mall, and Nate On Deal contributed to results. Stellaize is pursuing feature upgrades and new AI marketing solutions in the second half of 2026.

The CEO said the company would show clear earnings growth in the second half, and that is management's own view, which needs to be tested against Q4 results and full-year 2027 performance.

On shareholder returns, passing a capital reserve reduction resolution laid the groundwork for future tax-free dividend payments, which carry no dividend income tax under this structure. The stated policy is to maintain a 30% payout ratio.

Q4 2026 results will reflect both year-end advertising volume and commerce promotions. Whether advertising revenue growth holds at the Q3 pace and whether commerce transaction volume recovers will determine the direction of full-year operating margin.

08

Bull factors

Q3 profit rebound and margin improvement

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 15.2 billion and operating profit was KRW 5.41 billion. Operating profit rose 48.2% year on year, and the operating margin of 35.6% was 7.6 percentage points higher.

Revenue and operating profit have risen for two consecutive quarters since the Q1 2026 trough, which shows the continuity of the earnings recovery.

Advertising volume growth and large advertiser wins

Advertising transaction volume rose 32.7% year on year in Q2 2026. AOR expansion and record second-quarter search advertising revenue at the subsidiary supported growth. Large campaign wins from gaming and public-sector clients also increased in Q3.

Expanding shareholder return framework

The company canceled treasury shares equal to 5.02% of total issued shares. A capital reserve reduction resolution passed, creating the basis for tax-free dividends. The stated policy is to keep the payout ratio at 30%.

09

Bear factors

Full-year operating profit still below the 2022 level

Operating profit was KRW 11.5 billion in 2025, below KRW 19.6 billion in 2022. The operating margin was 22.8% in 2025 versus 36.8% in 2022. Revenue stayed near its 2022 level, so margin recovery has been limited even after the 2024 trough.

Commerce revenue decline and volatile transaction volume

Commerce transaction volume fell 3.5% in Q1 2026, and commerce revenue fell 5.4% year on year in Q3 on lower volume at key channels. Whether new operating contracts contribute meaningfully in the second half will need to be confirmed in Q4 results.

Lower operating cash flow and elevated leverage

Operating cash flow declined from KRW 42 billion in 2024 to KRW 26.1 billion in 2025. Total liabilities of KRW 164.7 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 130 billion at year-end 2025, a debt-to-equity ratio of 126.7%, compared with 86.5% in 2023. Whether earnings recovery and cash generation continue together remains to be confirmed.

10

Risk factors

Ad demand and sector concentration

Advertising revenue depends on spending by specific sectors and clients such as gaming, finance, food and beverage, and the public sector. Public-sector campaigns declined in part in Q4 2025, and such swings flow directly into quarterly results. Lumpy large-campaign wins can make the following quarter volatile.

Economic slowdown and demand for cost efficiency

When economic weakness leads advertisers to prioritize cost efficiency, performance marketing demand can rise while large brand budgets shrink. The company cited this shift in efficiency-focused marketing as a driver of search advertising growth. Changes in ad rates and budget allocation can alter the gap between transaction volume and revenue.

Cost increases and timing of new business monetization

Higher personnel costs and security system upgrades reduced operating profit in Q1 2026. The paid tier of Stellaize launched in October 2026, but its contribution to annual revenue has not yet been confirmed in disclosures.

If labor and AI investment costs rise, margins could come under pressure before new business results are visible.

Overall view

Incross posted Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 15.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.41 billion, with profit up sharply year on year and two consecutive quarters of recovery since the Q1 2026 trough. Advertising volume growth and large advertiser wins drove results, and the search-advertising subsidiary continued to grow.

However, full-year operating profit has not yet returned to the 2022 level of KRW 19.6 billion, and commerce transaction volume declined in Q3. Advertising demand varies by sector and client, and efficiency-focused demand is rising as the economy weakens.

On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was 126.7% at year-end 2025, and operating cash flow fell from KRW 42 billion in 2024 to KRW 26.1 billion in 2025. Shareholder returns continue through treasury share cancellation, a stated 30% payout policy, and the groundwork for a capital reserve reduction dividend.

The next checkpoints are preliminary Q4 2026 results and whether full-year operating margin and cash flow recover in the 2026 annual report.
