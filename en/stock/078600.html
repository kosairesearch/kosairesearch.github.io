Revenue keeps growing while profits swing with silver and FX

Daejoo Electronic Materials has posted four straight quarters of revenue growth through Q3 2026, but its profitability keeps swinging with silver prices and currency timing.

02

Business structure

Daejoo Electronic Materials was founded in 1981 and makes conductive pastes, silicon anode materials, phosphors and polymers for batteries and electronics. It is mass-producing silicon-based composite oxide anode material for electric vehicles and is focused on expanding its position in the global battery supply chain.

The anode material is used in EV battery cells and power tools, and its main customers reportedly include LG Energy Solution. Conductive pastes for multilayer ceramic capacitors (MLCCs) are the core of the business, and data center investment is supporting MLCC demand.

Phosphor sales slowed temporarily on weak end-market demand but recovered after a customer worked through its inventory. Silicon anode demand is being supported by strong demand for Panasonic's 2170 battery, according to iM Securities.

Silver paste for solar cells has emerged as a medium-term growth driver, and the company is working with SpaceX and Tesla on mass-production applications for solar cell electrode pastes.

Some analysts see its ability to produce both space-grade and ground-level solar pastes as an advantage among a small number of non-Chinese suppliers. Among silicon anode producers, Chinese and Japanese peers are also listed as mass-production players, according to a 2025 report.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 174.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 254.6 billion in 2025, with growth in each year from 2023 through 2025. Revenue in 2025 grew 16.1% year over year, but operating profit fell 29.5% to KRW 20.7 billion.

The company cited higher labor, depreciation and utility costs, which pushed up cost of sales. The operating margin was 6.9% in 2022, 3.4% in 2023, 13.4% in 2024 and 8.1% in 2025, with 2024 the highest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 36.9 billion in 2024 and fell 44.0% to KRW 20.7 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 20.9 billion in 2025, a different figure from controlling shareholders' net income.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 23.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 10.9 billion in 2025. At the end of 2025, the debt-to-equity ratio stood at 155.9%, with total equity of KRW 258.1 billion (KRW 253.7 billion attributable to controlling shareholders) and total liabilities of KRW 402.3 billion.

Quarterly revenue rose for four straight quarters from KRW 63.6 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 110 billion in Q3 2026. Operating profit peaked at KRW 9.22 billion in Q1 2026, then fell to KRW 6.78 billion in Q2 and KRW 5.14 billion in Q3, a two-quarter decline.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 3.83 billion in Q1 2026, returned to a profit of KRW 4.27 billion in Q2, and moved back to a loss of KRW 6.84 billion in Q3.

The company explained the Q3 margin drop by a timing lag: silver powder bought at high prices entered production, while lower silver prices and a weaker won were passed into selling prices later.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from downstream customers is being affected by inventory adjustments at customers and a slowdown in EV demand, which the market calls a 'chasm' or temporary demand dip. The silicon anode market is still regarded as being in an early stage.

Securities firms expect the company's silicon anode capacity to rise from about 3,000 tons in 2025 to 5,000 tons in 2026 and about 27,000 tons by 2028. Besides Daejoo, Chinese and Japanese producers are cited as silicon anode mass-producers, according to a 2025 report.

Conductive pastes are linked to MLCC demand and therefore to data center investment. In solar, U.S. supply-chain restructuring away from China is seen as an opening, alongside expanded solar manufacturing investment by SpaceX and Tesla.

In the cycle, revenue has grown three straight years since 2023, but operating profit moved sharply, at KRW 6.21 billion in 2023, KRW 29.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 20.7 billion in 2025, which points to high earnings volatility.

Compared with other listed battery materials companies, the pace of margin recovery differs by company, and this company's portfolio pairs silicon anodes with IT materials.

06

Outlook

The company pointed to higher operating profit from increased sales in Q4, expanded MLCC volume and its next-generation solar collaboration as growth drivers. Forecasts for Q4 differ among brokers. iM Securities projects Q4 2026 revenue of KRW 104.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 7.1 billion.

In the same report, iM Securities expects revenue to dip slightly from Q3 on weaker FX, so its revenue direction differs from the company's comments. Whether new supply contracts materialize, and FX and raw material price moves, are cited as key variables for future results.

One report says silicon anode next-generation products are planned for mass production from 2027. Over the longer term, the company is pursuing annual capacity of roughly 80,000 to 100,000 tons around 2030.

In solar, the next checkpoint is whether the SpaceX and Tesla mass-production collaboration turns into signed contracts. iM Securities said in its November 17, 2026 report that it maintained its 'Buy' rating and KRW 135,000 target price.

The same firm emphasized growth that it expects to become full-scale from 2027 rather than short-term FX and raw material swings.

08

Bull factors

Silicon anode capacity expansion

Securities firms expect capacity to reach about 5,000 tons in 2026 and about 27,000 tons by 2028. The company is pursuing roughly 80,000 to 100,000 tons of annual capacity around 2030. The early stage of the silicon anode market is cited as room for medium-term growth.

New solar paste customers

Collaborations with SpaceX and Tesla are in progress for mass-production applications of solar electrode pastes. iM Securities expects the company to become a leading metal electrode supplier for U.S. solar manufacturing as supply chains shift away from China. Confirmed contracts could change the revenue mix.

MLCC conductive paste demand

iM Securities said conductive pastes kept growing on MLCC demand tied to data center investment. The company said its main products, including solar electrode paste, silicon anode and MLCC electrode paste, all shipped record quarterly volumes.

09

Bear factors

Margin pressure from silver and FX timing

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 5.14 billion, down 14.2% year over year. iM Securities estimated the impact of the lag at about KRW 5 billion and said results excluding it were solid. Whether the decline is temporary or structural will show up in future quarterly margins.

Return to net loss and earnings volatility

Whether net income attributable to controlling shareholders returns to profit next quarter is the next checkpoint. Operating profit has declined for two straight quarters after peaking in Q1 2026, so the continuity of the recovery is not yet confirmed.

Customer concentration and unsigned contracts

In the short term, customer inventory adjustments and the EV slowdown are weighing on demand. The main silicon anode customer is reported to be LG Energy Solution, so demand depends on a few customers. The solar collaboration still depends on whether new supply contracts are finalized.

Risk factors

Raw material and FX timing

Silver bought at high prices entered production, while lower silver prices and FX moves reached selling prices later, pushing up the cost ratio. In periods when silver and FX move in different directions, quarterly operating profit can swing widely. The price-linked structure does not fully eliminate the lag.

Customer concentration and end-market demand

Its main customers reportedly include LG Energy Solution, so results are sensitive to battery cell production and inventory. A prolonged EV slowdown could delay silicon anode shipments. Conductive paste volumes depend on MLCC demand and therefore on investment cycles in specific downstream industries.

Capacity build-out and new business execution

Capacity is expected to rise to about 5,000 tons in 2026 and about 27,000 tons by 2028, but large capacity build-outs carry funding and utilization burdens. The debt-to-equity ratio was 155.9% at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 402.3 billion. Whether the solar business becomes revenue depends on finalizing new supply contracts.

12

Overall view

Daejoo Electronic Materials has grown revenue for four straight quarters through Q3 2026, so its top line is expanding. Silicon anode capacity expansion and solar electrode paste collaborations with overseas customers are cited as medium-term growth pillars.

Profitability, however, swings widely with silver prices and the timing of FX pass-through. Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 5.14 billion, down 14.2% year over year, and the company returned to a net loss of KRW 6.84 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

The four-quarter total for that line is a loss of KRW 3.3 billion. Broker views also differ: iM Securities projects Q4 operating profit of KRW 7.1 billion with slightly lower revenue, while the company has pointed to higher sales and operating profit.

The next checkpoints are whether solar collaborations become signed contracts, the Q4 2026 provisional results, and the full-year figures in the 2026 annual report. This report is for information only and contains no investment rating or target price.
