02

Business structure

DoubleUGames is built around social casino, with iGaming (online casino through Supernation) and casual games (through Paxie Games) added on top. Social casino remains the largest business, and its 2026 Q1 revenue of KRW 155.1 billion grew 8.6% year on year.

Casual and iGaming together accounted for 25.0% of revenue in 2026 Q2. Direct-to-consumer (DTC) payments reached 53.6% of social casino revenue in 2026 Q3, the first time above 50%. Company data put its 2026 Q3 social casino market share at 7.1%, fourth globally by quarterly revenue.

Paxie Games ran more than 100 titles as of late July 2026. Supernation posted Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 25.5 billion, up 17.3% year on year. AI-based new titles reached 81% of casual revenue in Q2 2026. Sell-side coverage cites Light & Wonder, Aristocrat and Playtika as peers.

Within social casino, DoubleDown Casino's DTC share is in the 60% range while W Casino's is in the mid-30% range.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual operating profit rose from KRW 183.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 248.7 billion in 2024, making 2024 the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Revenue fell to KRW 582.3 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 633.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 719.9 billion in 2025, two consecutive years of growth.

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 232.1 billion, down 6.7%, and the operating margin fell from 39.3% in 2024 to 32.2%. Yuanta-style sell-side analysis in March 2025 noted that marketing spending as a share of revenue fell from above 20% in 2022 to about 10% in 2024, while new subsidiaries needed higher marketing spend.

Profit attributable to owners swung from a net loss of KRW 132.2 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 149.1 billion in 2023, rose to KRW 187.2 billion in 2024, then fell 29.9% to KRW 131.3 billion in 2025. Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 179.8 billion in 2025 and is a separate figure.

Daishin Securities attributed 2025 weakness to subsidiaries and expects their revenue base to support profit contribution in 2026. Debt-to-equity fell from 22.8% in 2022 to 11.8% in 2025. Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 1.65 trillion, of which owners' interest was KRW 1.19 trillion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 274.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 236.9 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 186.2 billion in 2025 Q3 to KRW 205 billion in 2026 Q1, which the company described as a quarterly record, and to KRW 210.6 billion in 2026 Q2, also a quarterly record. 2026 Q3 revenue was KRW 195.4 billion, down 7.2% from the prior quarter and up 5.0% year on year.

Operating profit was KRW 63.5 billion in 2026 Q3, up 7.4% year on year. Platform fees were KRW 30.3 billion, or 15.5% of revenue, down 28.8% year on year. Marketing expense was KRW 45.6 billion, or 23% of revenue.

Profit attributable to owners peaked at KRW 56.5 billion in 2026 Q1 and declined for two consecutive quarters to KRW 7.69 billion in Q3, down 81.6% year on year. The gap between operating profit and owners' profit is a item to verify in the 2026 annual report. Owners' net profit over the last four quarters totaled KRW 145.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Social casino is a mature market with stalled growth, as Yuanta-style sell-side research flagged in 2025. Company data put 2026 Q3 market share at 7.1%, up 1.0 percentage point, and fourth globally by quarterly revenue.

Currency is a variable because most revenue is overseas, and a strong won reduced won-translated revenue from the prior quarter.

Shinyoung Securities said a strong won limits revenue growth and that additional M&A is needed to build growth momentum. iGaming faces regulatory pressure, with UK tax changes already affecting margins. Google Play's in-app payment fee cut was fully reflected from Q3 and lowered the company's cost base.

In casual games, AI-assisted development and a higher release cadence are the competitive axis. Sell-side coverage names Light & Wonder, Aristocrat and Playtika as peers, describing their growth as stalled.

Bull factors

Structural shift: DTC and lower fees

Nine-month 2026 platform fees fell 18.4% year on year, and variable costs fell to 25.7% of revenue from 30.1%. DTC share in social casino passed 50% for the first time in Q3 2026. The nine-month operating margin rose 0.8 percentage point to 33.1%. The company expects this trend to continue over the long term.

Diversification: casual and iGaming growth

The casual and iGaming share reached 25.0% in 2026 Q2, up 0.7 percentage point from Q1. The company said that in 2026 Q1 Supernation and Paxie Games both turned profitable, so every business unit contributed operating profit for the first time. Paxie Games runs more than 100 titles, broadening growth beyond social casino.

Sell-side view: AI capability

IBK Securities, in a September 2026 report, cited AI capability in raising its target price from KRW 70,000 to KRW 100,000 and maintaining its buy rating. The report pointed to AI automation across game development as the basis. This is the brokerage's view, and actual new-title performance will show in future results.

Bear factors

Sharp drop in Q3 owners' profit

Profit attributable to owners fell to KRW 7.69 billion in 2026 Q3, down 84.2% from the prior quarter and 81.6% year on year, and declined for two consecutive quarters. Operating profit rose 7.4% year on year to KRW 63.5 billion over the same period, so the divergence is the main open question. The breakdown should be checked in the 2026 annual report.

Strong won and stalled social casino

Shinyoung Securities judged that a strong won will limit revenue growth because most revenue is overseas. Sell-side research has pointed to stagnant social casino market growth since 2025. Together, these factors make top-line growth more dependent on casual and iGaming expansion.

Marketing burden and regulation

Marketing expense was KRW 45.6 billion, or 23% of revenue, a cost that weighs on the operating margin. Sell-side analysis has said higher marketing spend for newly acquired subsidiaries is unavoidable. UK tax changes have already affected margins and could keep pressuring profitability.

10

Risk factors

Regulation

The iGaming business is exposed to UK tax changes, which have already affected margins and could continue to pressure profitability. Platform policy changes on social casino could also reshape the fee structure.

Both affect quarterly results directly, so the scale and timing of regulatory changes should be tracked in filings.

Currency and revenue mix

Most revenue is overseas, so a stronger won reduces won-translated revenue. Won-translated revenue fell from the prior quarter in 2026 Q3, and Shinyoung Securities judged that a strong won limits growth.

FX direction is outside the company's control, so the gap between dollar-based growth and won-reported results should be checked each quarter.

Seasonality and marketing efficiency

The company relies on year-end demand in Q4, so quarterly results can swing widely. Casual growth depends on disciplined marketing and the success of new titles. With marketing at 23% of revenue in Q3, whether peak-season revenue exceeds marketing investment in Q4 is the key test.
