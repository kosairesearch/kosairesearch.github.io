02

Business structure

Paradise operates foreigner-exclusive casinos and hotels as a integrated resort business. Its casino sites are Walkerhill, Jeju, Busan and Paradise City in Incheon, and according to the company's website the Paradise City casino is the largest foreigner-exclusive casino in Korea with 449 gaming facilities.

Third-quarter 2026 casino revenue of KRW 268.0 billion accounted for most of the quarter's consolidated revenue of KRW 351.7 billion. The customer mix spans Chinese and Japanese VIPs, other VIPs such as Korean-descent and Greater China guests, and mass-market players.

In the third quarter, China VIP drop fell 9.3%, while Japanese VIP and other VIP drop rose 6.7% and 32.9%, and mass drop grew 6.0%. Total casino drop rose 8.3% to KRW 2 trillion in the same quarter. The hotel segment consists of the main hotel at Paradise City, Hotel Busan and the Hyatt Regency operation.

In the third quarter the Paradise City main hotel posted 86% occupancy and an average daily rate of KRW 496,000, while Hotel Busan posted 89% occupancy and KRW 391,000, which a securities analysis described as a quarterly high.

Competition centers on attracting foreign and VIP guests, and the scale of that inflow is the main variable for earnings.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 587.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 994.2 billion in 2023, up 69.2%, then to KRW 1.07 trillion in 2024, up 7.8%, and KRW 1.15 trillion in 2025, up 7.3%, growing for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year for revenue.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 10.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 145.8 billion in 2023, up 1,299.9%, eased 6.7% to KRW 136.1 billion in 2024, and recovered to KRW 155.8 billion in 2025, up 14.5%. The operating margin was 1.8% in 2022, 14.7% in 2023, 12.7% in 2024 and 13.6% in 2025.

Owner-attributable net profit rose from KRW 25.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 62.6 billion, KRW 75.6 billion and KRW 94.4 billion in 2023 through 2025, increasing for three consecutive years from 2023.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 144.8 billion in 2025, a different figure from owner-attributable net profit. In 2022, net profit of KRW 15.8 billion was below owner-attributable net profit of KRW 25.3 billion, which indicates that non-controlling interests recorded a loss.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit fell from KRW 39.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 16.2 billion in the fourth quarter, down 59.1%, then rebounded to KRW 37.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026, up 130.6%.

It slipped 4.7% to KRW 35.6 billion in the second quarter before reaching KRW 64.1 billion in the third, up 80.4%.

Over the same span, third-quarter 2026 owner-attributable net profit of KRW 26.3 billion rose 14.3% year on year, a slower pace than the 62.3% operating profit gain, so items below operating profit also need to be read alongside it.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 2.21 trillion, owner-attributable equity KRW 1.72 trillion and total liabilities KRW 1.87 trillion, and the debt ratio fell from 108.4% in 2022 to 84.4% in 2025. Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 223.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Casino revenue has been growing at a double-digit pace in 2026. Monthly casino revenue disclosed by the company reached KRW 826.0 billion in the January to October period, up 10.9% year on year, and October alone reached KRW 82.1 billion, up 12.8%.

Cumulative table drop over the same period rose 7.1% to KRW 6.37 trillion. On regulation, a proposed increase in the tourism fund is the key issue.

NH Investment & Securities said concerns over tighter regulation have largely been reflected in the share price, and that a departure-tax route to fund the increase could ease the impact compared with the original plan. There is also a legal variable.

The company's website lists a first-instance acquittal in April 2026 in a case involving alleged violations of the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act worth more than KRW 57 billion. On positioning, Paradise has been broadening its mix beyond Chinese VIP demand toward Japanese and other VIP customers.

In hotels, the normalization of the Hyatt Regency operation and high occupancy at the Paradise City main hotel have supported profitability.

Outlook

The company has not disclosed annual guidance in the figures reviewed, so the outlook rests on securities analyst views and the monthly casino revenue trend. In a November 11, 2026 report, NH Investment & Securities maintained a Buy rating and a target price of KRW 15,000.

In the same report, NH said casino indicators are improving and hotel expansion is continuing, and it expects stable casino earnings and hotel growth to carry into next year. Eugene Investment & Securities also maintained its rating and target in a report released at the same time.

An external company analysis describes a high-end hotel in Jangchung-dong, Seoul as having started construction, and lists stronger China VIP marketing and broader overseas customer inflow as growth strategies. The timing of earnings contribution from new hotel rooms has not been disclosed.

The next checkpoints are the monthly casino revenue disclosures and the 2026 annual report. The September figure was disclosed on October 2 and the October figure on November 3, so the November figure would be expected in early December 2026 on the same cycle.

The 2026 annual report will fix full-year revenue, operating profit and the quarterly cost structure. On policy, the timing of a decision on the tourism fund and departure-tax increase is the key variable.

08

Bull factors

Casino drop and hold rate improve

Third-quarter 2026 casino drop rose 8.3% to KRW 2 trillion, with Japanese VIP and other VIP drop up 6.7% and 32.9%. The quarterly hold rate climbed to 13%. Casino revenue of KRW 268.0 billion rose 23% year on year.

Monthly casino revenue for January to October was up 10.9%, indicating the improvement has spanned more than one month.

Hotel occupancy and Hyatt Regency normalization

The Paradise City main hotel posted 86% occupancy in the third quarter and Hotel Busan 89%. Hyatt Regency's occupancy rose from 31.7% in the first quarter to 73.4% in the third, moving from operating losses to a KRW 0.6 billion operating profit.

A securities analysis attributes part of this to improved room efficiency for casino VIP guests. Whether the hotel segment reduces reliance on casinos is the point to track.

Cost efficiency and margin recovery

Third-quarter operating costs rose only 1.8% from the prior quarter as staffing stabilized and comp operations became more efficient. Comp costs at Paradise City fell KRW 1.4 billion from the prior quarter. The annual operating margin was 13.6% in 2025, up from 12.7% in 2024.

One securities analysis also said concerns over tighter regulation have largely been reflected in the share price.

Bear factors

Cumulative profit and quarterly volatility

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 137.0 billion, down 1.9% year on year. Operating profit fell 59.1% in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 16.2 billion.

Owner-attributable net profit declined in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 16.5 billion, down 14.7%, and again in the second quarter to KRW 15.8 billion, down 4.1%. Whether the third-quarter gain is a one-quarter change requires more data points.

China VIP drop decline and hold-rate swings

China VIP drop fell 9.3% in the third quarter of 2026, offset by higher Japanese and other VIP drop. Casino earnings depend on drop multiplied by the hold rate, so a decline from the 13% quarterly level could slow revenue growth. Quarterly results remain exposed to high-stakes betting by specific VIP groups.

Policy variables and uncertainty around the profit path

If the tourism fund increase is finalized, it could weigh on the casino cost structure. Securities analysts said a departure-tax route could ease the impact relative to the original plan, but the final design is not yet set.

The gap between the 62.3% operating profit gain and the 14.3% owner-attributable net profit gain also raises questions about how profit flows below the operating line.

Risk factors

Policy risk

The proposed tourism fund increase and departure-tax changes bear on the cost burden for foreign casino customers. NH Investment & Securities said concerns over tighter regulation have largely been reflected in the share price. The size of the impact depends on the final design, so the timing and rate structure both need to be tracked.

Legal risk

The background to this risk is a case involving alleged violations of the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act in which a first-instance acquittal was issued. Subsequent proceedings could affect reputation and operating conditions. Developments should be tracked through official disclosures and press reports.

Demand and earnings volatility

Casino earnings rely heavily on VIP visits and betting size. Quarterly swings are large, with operating profit moving from KRW 16.2 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 64.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026. Hotel occupancy and room efficiency can also shift with external demand.

Overall view

Paradise's third quarter of 2026 showed a clear profit recovery, with revenue of KRW 351.7 billion, up 22.0%, and operating profit of KRW 64.1 billion, up 62.3%. Casino revenue of KRW 268.0 billion, up 23%, a 13% hold rate, higher hotel occupancy and the normalization of the Hyatt Regency operation supported the gain.

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 is still 1.9% below last year, and owner-attributable net profit fell in the first two quarters of 2026. The longer trend is visible in the disclosures, with annual revenue and owner-attributable net profit rising for three consecutive years from 2023.

Key variables include the decline in China VIP drop, the tourism fund and departure-tax proposals, and follow-up proceedings in the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act case. The price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x, and how the multiple is read depends on assumptions about quarterly profit volatility and policy risk.

The next checkpoints are the November casino revenue disclosure in early December 2026 and the 2026 annual report.
