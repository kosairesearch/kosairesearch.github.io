02

Business structure

Shinsung Delta Tech is an electronics and parts manufacturer founded in 1987, which started with plastic injection-molded home appliance parts and later expanded into secondary battery and auto parts and logistics services.

The business is organized into three segments: home appliance (HA), secondary battery and auto parts (BA), and logistics services (SVC). By 2023 sales mix, HA accounted for 43.03%, BA for 30.79%, SVC for 25.39%, and other operations, including venture investment, for 0.79%.

The HA segment produces parts for washing machines, dryers, refrigerators and vacuum cleaners and also makes handheld and robotic vacuum cleaners on an OEM basis.

The BA segment makes components for electric vehicles and energy storage systems, and its sales rose from KRW 187.0 billion in 2022 to KRW 211.6 billion in 2023. The SVC segment handles transport, storage and handling of materials and products, and in a 2021 report it was described as serving LG Group logistics.

As of the first quarter of 2021, the main customers were LG Electronics and LG Energy Solution. Production sites include plants in Changwon and Pyeongtaek in Korea and subsidiaries in Indonesia, Thailand and Mexico. In the first quarter of 2023, exports accounted for 57% of sales and domestic sales for 43%.

In HA, the company is pursuing higher profitability through cost reduction and a larger share of OEM and ODM work. The sales base is diversified across segments, but the BA business depends on a small number of large battery customers, so the structure combines sales expansion with customer concentration.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales were KRW 793.4 billion in 2022, KRW 833.7 billion in 2023 (up 5.1% year on year), KRW 909.7 billion in 2024 (up 9.1%), and KRW 945.4 billion in 2025 (up 3.9%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Over the same period, operating profit was KRW 32.1 billion in 2022, KRW 29.9 billion in 2023, KRW 27.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 27.7 billion in 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

The operating margin declined from 4.0% in 2022 to 3.6% in 2023, 3.0% in 2024 and 2.9% in 2025, so sales expansion did not translate into margin improvement.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 16.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.5 billion in 2023 (down 26.0%), turned into a net loss of KRW 995 million in 2024, and returned to a profit of KRW 21.7 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, which is a different measure, rose from KRW 7.45 billion in 2024 to KRW 24.2 billion in 2025.

Operating profit grew only 1.9% in 2025, while the recovery in profit attributable to controlling shareholders was far larger, so non-operating or one-off items may have weighed on the result. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 192.7% in 2022 to 125.1% in 2023, then rose to 144.0% in 2024 and 161.3% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 356.9 billion, of which equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 249 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 575.7 billion.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 2.91 billion in 2022, improved to KRW 36.1 billion in 2023, then recorded KRW 922 million in 2024 and KRW 14.4 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales moved from KRW 218.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 246.8 billion in the fourth quarter (up 13.1% quarter on quarter), KRW 276.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 12.1%), and KRW 299.2 billion in the second quarter (up 8.1%), before easing to KRW 284.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026 (down 4.9% quarter on quarter, but up 30.3% year on year).

Operating profit peaked at KRW 15.6 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and fell to KRW 10.9 billion in the third quarter (down 30.0% quarter on quarter). Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned into a net loss of KRW 2.05 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, the total was KRW 22.5 billion.

By segment, BA sales fell about 39% year on year in the first quarter of 2024, and a 2026 analysis attributed the BA profit decline to slower electric vehicle sales growth, while HA sales rose on premium appliance demand.

05

Industry analysis

The home appliance business is built around OEM work for washing machines and vacuum cleaners, so it depends on household appliance demand and customers' production plans.

A 2026 analysis attributed HA sales growth to premium appliance demand and new business potential, suggesting relatively steady demand in that segment. The secondary battery and auto parts business moves in step with the electric vehicle cycle.

A 2024 analysis pointed to weak electric vehicle demand worldwide as the reason BA growth stalled. Other views point to expanding markets for automotive molds driven by rising electric vehicle and battery demand, so the two interpretations coexist.

Energy storage systems are also cited as an application for BA components. Within the electronics sector of 17 listed companies on an annual basis, the company ranks second in sales and operating profit and fifth in operating margin, meaning its scale is in the upper tier while its margin sits in the middle.

Competitiveness in HA depends on cost reduction and a larger OEM share, while BA parts depend heavily on a few large battery customers. At the sector level, steady appliance demand and slowing electric vehicle demand are acting in opposite directions at the same time.

Shareholder returns and sales base

Bear factors

Third-quarter profit decline and return to loss

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 10.9 billion, down 30.0% from the prior quarter. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a profit in the second quarter to a net loss of KRW 2.05 billion. Sales rose but did not convert into profit, so the margin recovery in the next quarter is the key test.

Slower electric vehicle demand in the battery segment

A 2026 analysis attributed the decline in BA operating profit to slower electric vehicle sales growth. A report noted that BA sales fell about 39% year on year in the first quarter of 2024. The analysis linked the weakness to a global slump in the electric vehicle market.

Thin operating margin and debt burden

The operating margin declined from 4.0% in 2022 to 2.9% in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 125.1% in 2023 to 161.3% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total liabilities of KRW 575.7 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 356.9 billion. The dividend yield is below the sector average, so shareholder payouts are relatively small.

10

Risk factors

Customer and group concentration

The logistics business relies heavily on volumes tied to LG Group, and as of the first quarter of 2021 the main customers were LG Electronics and LG Energy Solution. A change in production plans at a single customer group can affect several segments at once. Customer concentration is both a source of sales growth and a source of vulnerability.

Electric vehicle cycle

The BA segment's operating profit declined because electric vehicle sales growth slowed. Analysts have described the secondary battery and auto parts business as stalled. Depending on electric vehicle and energy storage demand in the first half of 2027, segment profit could swing widely.

Margin and financial leverage

With the operating margin at 2.9% in 2025, profit can shift easily with cost or selling expense changes. The debt-to-equity ratio of 161.3% is above the 125.1% recorded in 2023. After the third-quarter 2026 return to loss, earnings volatility and financial burden must be read together.
