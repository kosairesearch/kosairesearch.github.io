Report overview

Etchant growth, but quarterly profits diverge

ENF Technology's operating profit rose to KRW 78.1 billion in 2025, and third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 193.6 billion, yet net income attributable to controlling shareholders has declined for two straight quarters since the first quarter of 2026.

02

Business structure

ENF Technology develops, manufactures and sells specialty chemicals used in semiconductor and display fabrication. Its core line is process chemicals, including etchants, thinners, developers and strippers, alongside Hwain chemicals (raw materials for photoresist), color pastes and CMP slurries.

The company reports as a single business segment. According to a compiled data set, of 2025 consolidated revenue of KRW 670.9 billion, product sales were KRW 531.2 billion (79%) and merchandise sales were KRW 129.4 billion (19%). By region, direct domestic sales accounted for 80.3% and exports for roughly 20%.

Key customers are memory chipmakers in Korea and abroad, including Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. On the display side, sales of OLED stripping solutions lifted results in the third quarter of 2025, according to a brokerage analysis.

In etchants, a domestic leader long held a dominant share, but ENF Technology has been widening its share with hydrofluoric acid-based etchants. The company is also developing and internalizing hydrofluoric acid raw materials, which analysts cite as a possible margin improvement.

Overseas bases are in Guangzhou, China and Kyle, Texas, where the company has reportedly begun supplying materials to Samsung Electronics' Taylor fab.

In the first quarter of 2026, it acquired additional shares of Pam Technology and moved it from an associate to a subsidiary, raising consolidated subsidiaries from three to four. Through an associate, it also participates in the high-purity sulfuric acid business for semiconductors.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results started from revenue of KRW 680.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 64.6 billion in 2022. In 2023, revenue fell to KRW 574.8 billion (down 15.5% year on year) and operating profit to KRW 24.7 billion (down 61.8%).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 17.5 billion in 2023 from a profit of KRW 46.8 billion in 2022, and net loss for the year was KRW 23.2 billion.

In 2024, revenue rose 1.3% to KRW 582.4 billion and operating profit rose 140.2% to KRW 59.3 billion, with net income attributable to controlling shareholders turning to a profit of KRW 31.1 billion.

In 2025, revenue reached KRW 670.9 billion (up 15.2%), operating profit KRW 78.1 billion (up 31.6%) and net income attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 51.9 billion (up 66.9%). Operating margin was 9.5% in 2022, 4.3% in 2023, 10.2% in 2024 and 11.6% in 2025, recovering from the 2023 low.

Operating profit rose for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025 after the 2023 low, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Net income for 2025 was KRW 50.3 billion; it includes non-controlling interests, so it differs from net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 51.9 billion. The debt ratio went from 69.1% in 2022 and 79.8% in 2023 to 80.3% in 2024 and then 66.5% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 60.9 billion in 2022, KRW 51.8 billion in 2023, KRW 107.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 103.1 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit in Q4 2025 was KRW 11.1 billion (down 49.5% from the prior quarter), and one analysis attributed this to one-off costs, including employee bonus payments and inventory valuation losses tied to a customer recipe change.

Operating profit then rose to KRW 24.9 billion in Q1 2026 (up 124.1% quarter on quarter), KRW 27.3 billion in Q2 (up 9.4%) and KRW 25.5 billion in Q3 (down 6.5%, but up 15.9% year on year).

Revenue bottomed at KRW 170.9 billion in Q1 2026 (down 3.3% quarter on quarter) and then rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 179.0 billion in Q2 and KRW 193.6 billion in Q3.

In Q3 2026, net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 11.1 billion, down 30.2% year on year, moving in the opposite direction from operating profit.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from downstream customers centers on expanded production of high-performance memory for AI servers, including HBM4, DDR5 and enterprise SSDs. The company said at its second-quarter 2026 earnings briefing that its supply of semiconductor process materials increased as a result.

Analysts also point to rising demand for high-value etching and cleaning materials as nodes shrink. On the display side, customers are reducing polarizer use and expanding low-reflection films, and the company developed an integrated OLED etchant to match this shift.

In etchants, a domestic leader long held a dominant position, but a brokerage analysis says ENF Technology is expanding share with hydrofluoric acid-based products. Because customer volumes move with memory investment cycles, the business is fairly sensitive to industry conditions.

On costs, petrochemical raw material prices feed directly into cost of sales, and the 2025 cost-of-sales ratio was reported at 76.4%, improved from 77.3% in 2024.

Customer diversification is also under way, with supply to Samsung Electronics' Taylor fab through the Kyle plant in Texas and mass-production supply of high-purity hydrofluoric acid to SK hynix as representative cases.

Customers' push to diversify suppliers is cited as an opportunity, while share competition with the market leader continues.

06

Outlook

At its second-quarter 2026 earnings briefing, the company set a target of commercial production of an integrated etchant for low-reflection OLED films in the second half of 2026.

It also targeted commercial production of a silicon etchant and a plating solution for HBM through-silicon vias in the second half of 2026, a high-selectivity phosphoric acid etchant in 2027, and a titanium etchant in 2028.

Post-CMP cleaner has passed customer line validation, with supply volumes set to expand meaningfully from 2027. The company said it succeeded in mass-production supply of high-purity hydrofluoric acid to SK hynix, drawing on its own production system in Cheonan.

One report says the hydrofluoric acid investment at the Cheonan site was raised to KRW 53.6 billion.

In a report published in late October 2026, BNK Investment & Securities projected 2026 revenue of KRW 740.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 92.7 billion, and 2027 revenue of KRW 861.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 114.2 billion.

The same report cited higher raw material prices, including oil, as a factor lowering third-quarter operating margin.

BNK's third-quarter projection (revenue of KRW 193.8 billion, operating profit of KRW 25.8 billion) was close to the reported figures (revenue of KRW 193.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 25.5 billion).

These are brokerage forecasts, so the next checkpoint is comparing them with confirmed figures in the 2026 annual report. Whether the new products reach commercial production on schedule will shape the revenue mix after 2027.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in operating margin

Operating margin recovered from a low of 4.3% in 2023 to 10.2% in 2024 and 11.6% in 2025. Operating profit of KRW 78.1 billion in 2025 was the largest among 2022 to 2025. An analysis points to possible margin improvement as the company internalizes hydrofluoric acid raw materials. Quarterly profitability can still swing with raw material prices and one-off costs.

New process material pipeline

An integrated OLED etchant, a silicon etchant and a plating solution for HBM through-silicon vias are targeted for commercial production in the second half of 2026. A high-selectivity phosphoric acid etchant is targeted for 2027 and a titanium etchant for 2028.

The company said post-CMP cleaner supply volumes will expand meaningfully in 2027. If these products reach mass production on schedule, the product range would extend beyond hydrofluoric acid-based etchants into phosphoric acid and CMP areas.

Customer diversification and supply expansion

Reports say the company has begun supplying materials to Samsung Electronics' Taylor fab from its Kyle plant in Texas. Mass-production supply of high-purity hydrofluoric acid to SK hynix has also begun.

With Pam Technology consolidated as a subsidiary in the first quarter of 2026, its hydrofluoric acid-based wet chemicals capability is now within the consolidated group. Customers' supplier diversification policies are cited as an opportunity for the company.

09

Bear factors

Slowing quarterly profit

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 19.5 billion in Q1 2026, then fell for two consecutive quarters to KRW 15.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 11.1 billion in Q3. Q3 2026 operating profit rose 15.9% year on year, but net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 30.2%.

Operating profit gains are not reaching net income in this period. Whether this gap is temporary or structural will show in the next annual report.

Raw material costs and one-off charges

Petrochemical raw material prices feed directly into cost of sales. In Q4 2025, one-off charges, including employee bonuses and inventory valuation losses, pulled operating profit down to KRW 11.1 billion.

BNK Investment & Securities expected third-quarter 2026 operating margin to fall on higher raw material prices, including oil. Cost changes pass through to margins quickly in this business.

Share competition in etchants

In the domestic etchant market, a leading supplier long held a dominant position. ENF Technology is gaining share, but analysts expect competition with the leader to continue. Customer volumes are concentrated in Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, so investment adjustments by a single customer could affect results. Competitive intensity varies across product lines such as etchants and thinners.

10

Risk factors

Memory investment cycle

A large share of revenue is tied to production plans of memory chip customers. If memory investment is adjusted, etchant and hydrofluoric acid volumes could fall from quarter to quarter. Revenue growth in the second and third quarters of 2026 was supported by expanded high-performance memory production. Whether this demand continues is the key variable for the next quarters' results.

Timing of new product commercialization

The OLED integrated etchant, silicon etchant and VMS are targeted for commercial production in the second half of 2026, phosphoric acid etchant in 2027 and titanium etchant in 2028. If customer evaluations run longer, these targets could slip. A delay would keep the business reliant on hydrofluoric acid-based products. Whether the targets are met will be confirmed through company announcements.

Group structure and related-party dealings

The largest shareholder, a related domestic alcohol company, holds 26.13% of the shares, and a holding company above it controls that stake. Some raw materials and solvents are sourced from group affiliates. Terms of related-party dealings and any change in the ownership structure are items to monitor. This structure brings business synergies along with a need to check transaction transparency.

Overall view

ENF Technology returned to profit after its 2023 loss, and operating profit and operating margin recovered in 2024 and 2025. Revenue reached KRW 670.9 billion in 2025 and operating profit KRW 78.1 billion, the largest among 2022 to 2025.

Revenue rose for two consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, but net income attributable to controlling shareholders has declined for two straight quarters since Q1, so the quality of earnings needs checking. New product commercialization targets and customer diversification are growth factors.

Memory investment cycles, raw material prices and share competition in etchants are the main weak points. Brokerage forecasts assume higher operating profit in 2026, but confirmed figures will come with the 2026 annual report.

The next checkpoints are whether new products reach commercial production in the second half of 2026 and the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027.
