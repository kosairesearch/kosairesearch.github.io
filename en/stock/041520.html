02

Business structure

ELC is a semiconductor equipment and control instrumentation company founded in 1984 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2003. The business has two segments.

The control instrumentation segment supplies flow meters and liquid flow controllers used in semiconductor wet-cleaning equipment, while the semiconductor equipment segment makes Strip Grinders for Advanced Packaging processes.

The product lineup also includes ultrasonic flowmeters, servo motors and drivers, and reducers. ELC acquired Seowoo Technology in 2020 to supply equipment to global semiconductor package makers and acquired S&C in 2023 to widen its scope.

Its customers are global semiconductor packaging manufacturers, and its track record of meeting high precision and specification requirements in customer processes is presented as evidence of its technology.

Competition comes from specialized domestic and foreign makers of semiconductor equipment parts and instruments, and whether the two-segment structure helps customer response is a matter to check in order-related disclosures. Segment revenue shares can be checked in the segment note of the annual report.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 137.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 87 billion in 2023, a 36.7% decline, and then to KRW 29.2 billion in 2024, a 66.4% decline, before rising 29.6% to KRW 37.9 billion in 2025. Within 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating losses were KRW 9.5 billion in 2022 and KRW 15.4 billion in 2023, widening, then narrowed to KRW 4.68 billion in 2024 and KRW 901 million in 2025. The operating margin was -6.9% in 2022, -17.7% in 2023, -16.0% in 2024, and -2.4% in 2025, the narrowest of the four years.

The company recorded operating losses in each year from 2022 through 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a loss of KRW 5.78 billion in 2022 and a loss of KRW 7.84 billion in 2023 to a loss of KRW 267 million in 2024, then turned to a profit of KRW 8.26 billion in 2025.

The gap between a KRW 901 million operating loss and an 82.6 billion-won profit in 2025 points to a large contribution from non-operating items, and the breakdown is a point to check in the annual report notes.

Quarterly, revenue was KRW 6.09 billion in Q3 2025 with an operating loss of KRW 1.6 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.96 billion.

Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 12.7 billion, up 108.5% from the prior quarter, with operating profit of KRW 800 million and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 11.4 billion. Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 10.3 billion, down 18.8% quarter on quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 279 million.

Revenue then rose to KRW 18.3 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 21.3 billion in Q3 2026, an increase in two consecutive quarters, and Q3 revenue was up 249.0% from a year earlier.

A corporate data provider attributed the Q1 2026 year-on-year revenue increase to demand for high-performance semiconductors and higher Strip Grinder sales.

The Q3 2026 divergence between an operating profit of KRW 5.56 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 75.71 million stems from non-operating items, and its size should be checked in the quarterly report.

05

Industry analysis

End-market demand is tied to rising demand for high-performance semiconductors driven by the expansion of the AI industry. According to a corporate data provider's comment on Q1 2026 results, equipment investment demand from global IDM and OSAT companies is increasing.

Strip Grinders, used in Advanced Packaging processes, are cited as the main driver of revenue growth. In a period when investment is concentrating on memory and AI chips, ELC is developing technology to extend its applications into those areas.

Where the industry sits in its cycle is hard to pin down, and the speed at which equipment investment converts into orders matters.

Compared with its own past scale, 2025 revenue of KRW 37.9 billion remains below the KRW 137.6 billion recorded in 2022, so how far a sector recovery translates into revenue is still an open question.

On competition, combining instruments such as flow meters and flow controllers with Strip Grinder equipment may serve as a differentiator in customer process support.

Equipment makers' earnings can swing quarter to quarter because of timing differences between investment announcements and delivery recognition, so quarterly revenue trends should be read alongside annual results.
