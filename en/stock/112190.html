02

Business structure

KC Industry designs, manufactures and installs precast concrete (PC) products. The company was founded in December 1995 and listed on KONEX in May 2016. It focuses on PC products and specialty earthworks for civil and building projects, with its headquarters and plant in Yeoju, Gyeonggi Province.

Civil PC business, such as culverts for power, telecom and utility conduits, accounts for more than half of revenue. A recent product-level breakdown could not be confirmed.

For reference, a Shinhan Investment report on 2020 showed PC box 46%, soundproof walls 12%, segments 12%, special PC 12%, DSM 10%, building PC 5% and other 3%.

Since 2013 the company has expanded into DSM (Divided Shield Method), a non-open-cut tunneling method. PC methods shorten construction time and help reduce noise, dust and traffic problems at job sites.

The company is reported to be the only domestic firm able to perform design, production and installation, and it holds the top position in civil PC. No reliable recent statistics on competitor market shares were found.

In 2020 it acquired a construction subsidiary and set up the modular subsidiary KC Modular, extending into building PC and modular construction. Its controlling shareholder Sungshin Cement operates cement, ready-mix concrete and trading businesses, which made up 54%, 11% and 33% of its revenue in 2025. KC Industry therefore gives Sungshin Cement a link from cement and ready-mix concrete into finished structures.

03

Recent trends

On a standalone basis, KC Industry reported 2025 revenue of 60.677 billion won, operating profit of 873 million won and net profit of 920 million won, with net profit exceeding operating profit. Year over year, revenue fell 28.0% and operating profit fell 90.8%, while net profit rose 98.3%.

Net profit rose even as operating profit collapsed, which suggests non-operating items played a role, but the breakdown could not be confirmed. PC culvert sales declined despite higher SOC budgets, while DSM tunnel results improved on stronger demand for double-track railways.

At the end of 2025, standalone total assets were 182.0010 billion won, total liabilities 102.1440 billion won and total equity 79.8570 billion won. On funding, total borrowings at the end of 2024 were 107.5 billion won, up from 88.9 billion won a year earlier.

The company said it sold 32 billion won of its privately placed convertible and warrant bonds to Sungshin Cement and a private equity partner.

On December 16, 2025, a private equity vehicle acquired 4,185,185 shares each through a convertible-bond conversion and a warrant exercise. The combined stake of Sungshin Cement and four related parties was 86.74% as of February 23, 2026. According to Seoul Economic Daily, the stake had risen to 89.88% as of July 2026.

On March 25, 2026, the two incumbent CEOs resigned and a new CEO was appointed. Quarterly results for 2026 could not be confirmed in this research.

The share price reached 1,759 won intraday on December 19, 2025 and stood at 1,190 won on March 25, 2026. The October 27, 2026 close of 991 won sits near the low end of the 52-week range of 930 to 1,946 won. Average daily volume over three months was about 670 shares, and the one-year return was -30.7%.

Data vendor FnGuide's company monitor showed, as of June 2026, a PER of 10.93 times and a PBR of 0.42 times. No company-specific disclosure or news explaining the day's decline was found.

04

Outlook

The downstream industry is likely to remain under pressure for some time. Domestic cement shipments in 2025 were 36.5 million tons, the lowest since 1991. The Sampyo Market Research Center projected 2026 ready-mix concrete demand at 91.1 million cubic meters and cement demand at 36.1 million tons.

With raw-material costs rising after the Middle East war, the Seoul metropolitan area agreed on a 4.3% increase in ready-mix concrete prices. KC Industry is not a ready-mix producer, but it is exposed to the same construction cycle, which can affect orders and pricing.

The company-specific variable is cooperation with Sungshin Cement. Sungshin Cement is reported to be considering a joint overseas model that supplies cement, ready-mix concrete and PC together on construction projects.

At its March 2026 shareholder meeting, KC Industry added overseas construction and related fields to its business purposes. No specific overseas order or revenue amount has been confirmed.

Most of the externally raised funds went into capacity expansion, but the financial position worsened before the expansion paid off. The utilization rate of the expanded facilities and the recovery of operating margin are therefore the key indicators to watch.

Domestically, SOC-related demand such as double-track railways supports the DSM segment. There is room for operating margin to improve, but that depends on utilization and order pricing. Future quarterly operating profit, the borrowing level and any concrete overseas cooperation should be checked in upcoming filings.

05

Bull factors

Top civil PC position and end-to-end capability

The company is reported to be the only domestic firm that can perform design, production and installation, and it holds the top civil PC position. PC methods offer advantages in shorter construction time and fewer complaints. In public SOC projects, this end-to-end capability can act as an entry barrier.

Financial support and overseas link from Sungshin Cement

Together with a private equity partner, Sungshin Cement bought 32 billion won of KC Industry's convertible and warrant bonds, and the company described the move as cooperation to diversify overseas PC business.

A plan to supply cement, ready-mix concrete and PC as a package to overseas construction projects has been reported. If the parent's business link becomes concrete, it could help sales channels and financial stability.

DSM demand and room to raise utilization

DSM tunnel results improved in 2025 on stronger demand for double-track railways. Most of the funds raised externally went into capacity expansion. Higher utilization of the new capacity could lift operating margin, but that depends on securing orders.

06

Bear factors

Sharp drop in 2025 operating profit

In 2025, revenue fell 28.0% and operating profit fell 90.8% year over year. Operating profit, once supported by economies of scale, shrank to about 900 million won. Profit is small relative to revenue, which leaves the company exposed to fixed-cost burdens and price pressure.

Debt-heavy balance sheet

At the end of 2025, standalone total liabilities of 102.1440 billion won exceeded total equity of 79.8570 billion won. Total borrowings at the end of 2024 were 107.5 billion won, up 20.92% from the prior year. With weak operating profit, interest costs weigh on profitability.

Thin trading and large price swings

The free float ratio is 10.11%. Average daily volume over the past three months is about 670 shares. On October 27, 2026, trading value was only 3,168,365 won, yet the price fell 12.30%, showing that small trades can move the price sharply.

Risk factors

Macro

Domestic ready-mix concrete shipments in 2025 were about 93 million cubic meters. Reports also say the private housing market remains frozen by project-financing concerns and surging construction costs. Delays in construction investment or SOC budget execution would weigh on PC demand and utilization. Raw-material cost swings tied to Middle East developments could also affect costs.

Financial and funding

Externally raised funds went into capacity expansion, but the financial position worsened before the expansion paid off. Total borrowings at the end of 2024 were 107.5 billion won. If operating cash flow stays weak, debt repayment and additional funding could become a burden.

The larger share count after convertible-bond and warrant exercises is also a dilution factor for existing shareholders.

Governance and market

Sungshin Cement's stake was reported at 89.88% as of July 2026. Dependence on the controlling shareholder is very high, so business direction and funding largely follow its strategy. KONEX is a market dedicated to early-stage and small companies. Low liquidity can make trading difficult and daily price moves large.

08

Overall view

KC Industry is a small construction-materials company with an end-to-end capability and the top position in civil PC. In 2025, revenue and operating profit fell sharply, leaving profitability low, and a debt burden remains.

At the same time, Sungshin Cement's rise to controlling shareholder adds new variables in financial support and overseas cooperation. However, there is not yet evidence that this has translated into overseas orders or better results. Downstream construction volumes remain low, so the timing of any recovery is unclear.

The small free float and thin trading also mean the share price can be highly volatile. This report is for information only, and investment decisions are left to the reader.
