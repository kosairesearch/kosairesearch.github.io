KC Industry: A Thinner Profit Cushion in PC Work

Sales and operating profit both fell in 2025 and the largest shareholder group reported an 86.74% stake, leaving PC demand recovery and cost ratios as the key variables for KC Industry's next earnings phase.

02

Business structure

KC Industry was founded in 1995 as a precast concrete (PC) specialist that handles PC products from design through production and construction in-house. Its segments are PC culverts, PC noise barriers, DSM, building PC, special PC and others, and PC culverts drive most of its sales.

As of 2022, PC culverts accounted for 51.01% of sales and building PC for 29.65%. DSM is a non-open-cut method for earthworks, and demand for it is cited as rising with urban development. DSM sales rose from KRW 3.76 billion in 2024 to KRW 9.95 billion in 2025.

Over the same period, PC culvert sales fell from KRW 43.41 billion to KRW 35.33 billion. Building PC is positioned as an answer to rising labor costs and the difficulty of quality control in cast-in-place construction. A NICE technical analysis report notes a seven-project PC modular win worth KRW 24.66 billion.

A 2021 press report described the company as the domestic leader in civil-engineering PC and one of the few firms able to handle design, production and construction in-house. Civil-engineering volumes, however, depend on the size of SOC budgets, which exposes the company to shifts in public orders.

03

Recent trends

A shareholder meeting on March 25, 2026 approved the financial statements for the 31st fiscal year, covering January 1 to December 31, 2025. Based on disclosed results, standalone sales were KRW 60.677 billion, operating profit KRW 873 million and net income KRW 920 million, with net income exceeding operating profit.

According to WiseReport data for the 2025 standalone results, sales fell 28.0% year on year, operating profit fell 90.8% and net income rose 98.3%. NICE attributes the profit decline to a higher cost of sales ratio, which reached 85.3% in 2025.

In 2024, sales were KRW 84.32 billion and operating profit KRW 9.45 billion (11.2% margin), but finance costs of KRW 10.25 billion cut net income to about KRW 460 million. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 356.6% at end-2024 to 127.9% at end-2025, and equity grew materially.

On February 12, 2026, the largest shareholder Sungshin Cement bought 1,758,879 shares off-market, and a related party bought the same number that day. Sungshin Cement financed the purchase with borrowings of about KRW 7.3 billion, and the transaction closed on March 20, 2026.

A March 27, 2026 filing showed the combined stake unchanged at 23,597,394 shares, or 86.74%. The share price was KRW 1,190 on March 25, 2026 and KRW 1,197 at the March 27 close, and stood at KRW 1,040 on November 11, 2026, up 1.07% on the day.

04

Outlook

A NICE technical analysis report estimates 2026 sales at KRW 62.5 billion, with PC culverts at KRW 37.0 billion and DSM at KRW 8.0 billion in the same estimate. Because these are estimates, they will be checked against future disclosed results.

Operating margin fell from 11.2% in 2024 to 1.4% in 2025, so whether the cost of sales ratio recovers remains the main gauge of earnings capacity, separate from the sales estimate.

A July 2023 technical analysis report also pointed to labor and commission costs in selling and administrative expenses as a drag on profitability. Civil-engineering linkage is also visible.

In 2024, operating profit rose 132.8% on a larger SOC budget, while in 2025 PC culvert sales fell despite the SOC budget increase. In the same year, DSM tunnel results improved on rising demand from double-track railway projects.

With the largest shareholder group holding 86.74%, the free float is limited, which interacts with thin trading on KONEX. The next checkpoints are the cost of sales ratio and finance cost trend in the third-quarter 2026 report, and the progress of PC modular projects.

05

Bull factors

Civil-engineering PC know-how and market position

A 2021 press report described KC Industry as a firm that handles design, production and construction in-house and as the domestic leader in civil-engineering PC. As of September 2022, the company held about 125 intellectual property rights and several performance and procurement certifications. A NICE report states that it has ranked first in domestic industry sales for 18 consecutive years since 2006.

Broader sales mix from DSM and PC modular

DSM sales rose from KRW 3.76 billion in 2024 to KRW 9.95 billion in 2025, increasing its share of total sales. The method is described as benefiting from urban development that requires non-open-cut earthworks. A NICE report cites seven PC modular projects worth KRW 24.66 billion.

Balance sheet repair and lower borrowing burden

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 356.6% at end-2024 to 127.9% at end-2025. Equity grew materially, which improved stability indicators. Because finance costs weighed heavily on 2024 net income, a lighter borrowing burden could be cited as a supportive factor for the earnings structure.

06

Bear factors

Profit slump and rising cost of sales in 2025

Standalone operating profit for 2025 fell 90.8% from the prior year, and sales declined 28.0%. NICE noted that the cost of sales ratio rose from 78.5% in 2024 to 85.3% in 2025. As a result, the operating margin dropped to about 1.4%.

Finance costs and a heavy debt load

Finance costs of KRW 10.25 billion in 2024 exceeded operating profit of KRW 9.45 billion for the same year. Total liabilities stood at about KRW 130.2 billion at end-2024, a heavy load relative to equity. NICE pointed to these finance costs as a factor behind the smaller net income.

Falling PC culvert sales and reliance on civil orders

PC culvert sales declined from KRW 43.41 billion in 2024 to KRW 35.33 billion in 2025. An analysis noted that the decline occurred even though the SOC budget increased. Civil-engineering orders move with SOC budget size, which leaves the segment exposed to changes in public orders.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Domestic construction activity and SOC budget size drive orders, so changes in fiscal policy feed directly into sales. Higher interest rates could raise finance costs for a balance sheet that relies on borrowing and pressure net income again. Labor and material cost swings flow through the cost of sales ratio into operating margin.

Market liquidity

Reports show KONEX average daily trading value falling to about KRW 223.5 million from KRW 1.846 billion in January. The same reports note that the number of KONEX-listed companies declined from 115 to 107 and that transfers to KOSDAQ have slowed. Reluctance among brokerages to underwrite such listings is cited as a factor behind the delays.

Ownership concentration

With Sungshin Cement and related parties together holding 86.74%, the shares available to the market are limited. Filings show that the February 2026 share purchase was funded with borrowings. Changes in the largest shareholder's funding structure and stake will need to be tracked through future filings.

08

Overall view

KC Industry's 2025 disclosed results show sales falling and operating profit dropping sharply in the same year. A higher cost of sales ratio pushed the operating margin down to the 1% range, and finance costs in 2024 were a major factor in net income.

On the other side, the improved debt-to-equity ratio and the growth of DSM and PC modular revenue are cited as signs of structural improvement. The PC culvert core is linked to SOC budgets and civil orders, so sales volatility remains.

The 86.74% largest shareholder stake points to limited free float and trading liquidity as a structural feature. NICE estimates 2026 sales at KRW 62.5 billion, and future disclosed results will test that estimate.

The indicators visible today are the cost of sales ratio, finance costs and changes in the largest shareholder's stake, and their direction remains the variable to watch for any earnings recovery. This commentary is a qualitative summary for information purposes only.
