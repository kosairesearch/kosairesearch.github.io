02

Business structure

Hanbit Soft, founded in 1999, develops and operates online and mobile games, with live services of existing titles as its core revenue source.

According to the company profile, the PC game Audition is a rhythm dance game with a cumulative user base of 700 million worldwide, and the company also runs the long-running MMORPGs Granado Espada and AIKA as well as Hellgate: London.

On mobile, it operates Club Audition, built on the Audition IP, and Doshi Eo Fishing M, a fishing game based on a TV entertainment IP. Granado Espada M sits at the center of the earnings trend.

The title accelerated its global expansion after its domestic launch in 2024, and the stable sales in Southeast Asia, Taiwan and Japan were cited as key drivers of the 2025 improvement. The company said contributions from subsidiaries and affiliates also grew in 2025.

Its distribution business, which handles industrial and agricultural drones under a partnership with DJI, is reported to have improved results. The customer base consists of players of PC and mobile MMORPGs and rhythm games, and the company is diversifying revenue beyond a single title by expanding overseas regions.

For the sales adjustment in 2Q 2026, the company attributed it to Granado Espada M entering a long-term stable phase.

In a market where large companies split share through big new releases and global publishing capabilities, Hanbit Soft is pursuing a strategy of protecting profitability through live operation efficiency of existing IP and cost reduction. The largest shareholder and related parties hold 32.07%, and the company had 74 employees as of June 30, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, sales fell from KRW 56.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 25.9 billion in 2023, down 54.0% year on year and the smallest year in the 2022–2025 window, before recovering to KRW 33.3 billion in 2024 (up 28.4%) and KRW 37.9 billion in 2025 (up 13.8%). 2022 was the largest year of the four.

Operating profit went from KRW 3.54 billion in 2022 (operating margin 6.3%) to an operating loss of KRW 6.52 billion in 2023 (margin -25.2%) and KRW 1.41 billion in 2024 (margin -4.2%), before turning positive at KRW 284 million in 2025 (margin 0.8%).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.03 billion in 2022, a loss of KRW 9.4 billion in 2023, KRW 1.72 billion in 2024, and KRW 5.21 billion in 2025 (up 203.5% year on year). Net income including non-controlling interests, which is a different figure, was KRW 5.44 billion in 2025.

In 2025, operating profit of KRW 284 million sat well below controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 5.21 billion, which points to a meaningful contribution from non-operating items. Operating cash flow was KRW 733 million in 2022, KRW 6.84 million in 2023, KRW 2.56 million in 2024 and KRW 4.62 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 20 billion, of which KRW 17.3 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 5.14 billion.

On a quarterly basis, 3Q 2025 sales were KRW 10.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 205 million and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 278 million. 4Q 2025 sales fell to KRW 7.41 billion (down 27.2% from the prior quarter) and the quarter posted an operating loss of KRW 349 million, yet controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 3.66 billion. 1Q 2026 sales were KRW 8.61 billion (up 16.1% from the prior quarter) with operating profit of KRW 2.19 billion. 2Q 2026 sales were KRW 6.77 billion (down 21.4% from the prior quarter) and operating profit was KRW 627 million (down 71.4%). 3Q 2026 sales were KRW 6.88 billion (up 1.6% from the prior quarter, down 32.4% year on year), operating profit was KRW 632 million (up 0.8% from the prior quarter, up 209.0% year on year), and controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 750 million (down 28.2% from the prior quarter).

Controlling-shareholder net income over the latest four quarters totals KRW 8.26 billion. For 2Q 2026 the company attributed the margin improvement to lower development and marketing costs supported by AI tools.

Whether the KRW 2.19 billion operating profit in 1Q 2026, which was far above the other quarters, was a one-off item should be confirmed in the periodic report.

05

Industry analysis

The game industry tends to show large quarterly swings, driven by the box-office success of new releases and the life cycle of existing titles.

Hanbit Soft relies less on launching large new titles and more on live services of long-running IP such as Audition and Granado Espada M, so its sales scale is sensitive to the life cycle of existing games.

For the sharp sales decline around 2022, one corporate analysis report attributed it to a combination of a gap in new releases and a downward stabilization of existing titles. Its overseas structure is also distinctive.

The Thai and Chinese launches both depend on collaboration with local publishers, so as overseas revenue grows, the company becomes more exposed to the pace of negotiations and launch timing with those partners.

Listed peers in the same industry, including Netmarble, PearlAbyss, Krafton and NC, have their own large new titles and global publishing capabilities, so the gap with Hanbit Soft in development scale and financial capacity is wide.

Hanbit Soft is a small-cap stock by market value, which means its share price may react more strongly to earnings swings. With a narrow new-title lineup, extending the life of existing IP and expanding overseas regions are the key variables for its position within the industry.

06

Outlook

The company has laid out three paths. First, the Thai launch of Granado Espada M is being prepared for release within 2026 in cooperation with a local publisher. Second, in China the company continues negotiations with a local publisher and localization work, and no launch date has been disclosed.

The company said it is negotiating with the Chinese publisher it signed earlier in 2026 to launch as quickly as possible. Third, it plans to broaden its revenue base through new markets in Southeast Asia and global service in North and South America.

The new title AIKA Mobile (provisional) is in development under a June 2026 IP agreement tied to the PC MMORPG AIKA Online, and features large-scale national war (RvR) content. Its commercial launch plan is due to be confirmed in the first half of 2027.

A company official said the company will keep the profitability improvement trend in place following the 3Q results. However, cumulative sales for January to September 2026 fell 27.0% year on year, which means a recovery in the top line has not yet been confirmed.

When the 2025 results were announced, the company said it expected sales growth and a wider operating profit increase in 2026, and the sales trend through 3Q sits some distance from that expectation.

The key variables are whether overseas launches translate into revenue and how much AIKA Mobile contributes to results after 2027.

Bull factors

Operating profit turning into a pattern

Operating profit turned positive in 2025 at KRW 284 million, and 3Q 2026 operating profit reached KRW 632 million, up 209.0% year on year. The company said operating profit has been positive for three straight quarters and credited lower development and marketing costs.

Following operating losses of KRW 6.52 billion in 2023 and KRW 1.41 billion in 2024, this marks a change in direction.

Improved financial capacity

The debt ratio fell from 76.8% in 2022 to 25.7% in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 5.14 billion against total equity of KRW 20 billion. The company said consolidated cash and cash equivalents rose about 45% from the prior year-end by the end of 3Q 2026. Operating cash flow also grew from KRW 2.56 million in 2024 to KRW 4.62 billion in 2025.

Overseas expansion and next title timeline

Granado Espada M is being prepared for a Thai launch within 2026, while the company advances localization for China and expands into Southeast Asia and North and South America.

The new title AIKA Mobile is a mobile MMORPG built on the IP of the PC MMORPG AIKA Online, with its commercial launch plan due to be confirmed in the first half of 2027. According to the company, a broader overseas revenue base could partly offset the weakness of a domestic-centered structure.

Bear factors

Continued sales contraction

3Q 2026 sales were KRW 6.88 billion, down 32.4% year on year, and cumulative sales for January to September 2026 fell 27.0%. Sales declined from KRW 56.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 37.9 billion in 2025.

Because the operating profit improvement has relied mainly on cost reduction, the durability of profitability could be limited without a recovery in the top line.

Concentration on a lead title

For the 2Q 2026 sales adjustment, the company cited Granado Espada M entering a long-term stable phase. Because the earnings base depends heavily on one title, that game's revenue trajectory largely determines overall results.

Quarterly sales swings, including the drop to KRW 7.41 billion in 4Q 2025 (down 27.2% from the prior quarter) and to KRW 6.77 billion in 2Q 2026 (down 21.4%), are linked to this structure.

Quality and volatility of earnings

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 284 million, while controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 5.21 billion, so non-operating items carried a large weight. In 4Q 2025 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 349 million, yet controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 3.66 billion.

Controlling-shareholder net income peaked in 4Q 2025 and has declined from 1Q 2026 through 3Q 2026. The 3Q 2026 financial statements have not been reviewed by an external auditor.

12

Overall view

Hanbit Soft has kept operating profit positive from its 2025 annual turnaround through 3Q 2026. The company emphasizes three straight quarters of profit and a profitability-focused management approach.

On the other side, 3Q 2026 sales fell 32.4% year on year, and cumulative sales for January to September 2026 declined 27.0%. Because the profit improvement has relied mainly on cost reduction, whether the revenue base recovers is the most important checkpoint.

On the financial side, the debt ratio has declined and cash and cash equivalents have grown, which points to greater financial capacity. However, 2025 operating profit was KRW 284 million while controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 5.21 billion, so the composition of earnings should be read together.

The share price sits close to book value, and the price multiple on the latest four quarters of earnings is on the lower side, but this describes the current level and is not a view on the direction of the share price.
