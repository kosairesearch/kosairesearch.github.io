02

Business structure

Bowon Chemical processes plastic raw materials such as PVC, TPO and PU into films, then supplies finished products through surface treatment and laminating.

In the first half of 2026, automotive interior surface materials accounted for 64.2% of sales, construction materials for 33.5%, and functional sheet materials for 2.3%. Its core TPO sheet product is described by one analyst as holding a share above 90% in five of seven key automotive interior parts.

The construction segment is reported as the sole domestic producer of SPC flooring, and its production line utilization rose from 68% in 2025 to 91% in the first half of 2026. Utilization of automotive interior fabric rose from 90% to 93% over the same period.

Sales are concentrated, as two major customers together made up 95% of first-half sales. Expanded supply to Hyundai Motor and KIA is cited as a driver of automotive sales. In October 2026, the company began initial supply of high-performance film for tablet PC covers to Logitech.

Investment continues in electronics surface materials, OLED encapsulation film and solar EVA film. High-performance materials were 7% of 2025 sales, and the medium-term target was reported as 25% in an August 2026 article and 30% in a November 2026 article.

04

Earnings analysis

Bowon Chemical's annual results show sales and profit moving in different directions. Sales rose 31.4% to KRW 52.2 billion in 2025 from KRW 39.7 billion in 2024, and operating profit rose 7.5% to KRW 5.12 billion from KRW 4.76 billion.

Because sales grew much faster than operating profit, operating margin fell from 12.0% in 2024 to 9.8% in 2025. A media report pointed to higher R&D, labor and defect-repair costs, along with raw material price increases, as pressure on margins.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 87.0% to KRW 218 million in 2025 from KRW 1.67 billion in 2024, and total net income followed the same path to KRW 218 million.

The same report said financial costs fell from KRW 3.9 billion in the first half of 2025 to KRW 900 million in the first half of 2026, easing non-operating pressure.

At end-2025, total equity was KRW 24 billion, of which controlling-shareholder equity was also KRW 24 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 46.2 billion. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 7.59 billion in 2024 to KRW 5.29 billion in 2025.

In Q1 2026, sales were KRW 15 billion and operating profit KRW 971 million, but a merger-related convertible bond cost booked below operating profit produced a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.25 billion.

Q2 sales were KRW 13.7 billion, down 8.6% from the prior quarter, operating profit was KRW 1.02 billion, up 5.5%, and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 887 million turned the quarter into a profit.

Q3 sales were KRW 14.6 billion, up 6.7%, and operating profit was KRW 1.07 billion, up 4.5%, extending the two consecutive quarterly increases since the Q1 2026 low, while controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 498 million was down 43.9%. Controlling-shareholder net income for the last four quarters (Q1 2025 to Q4 2025) totaled KRW 218 million.

05

Industry analysis

Bowon Chemical's business is tied to two demand sources, automotive interiors and construction materials. On the automotive side, rising demand for eco-friendly TPO materials is cited as a driver of sales growth.

On the construction side, a recovery in housing transactions and the resulting remodeling demand lifted utilization of SPC flooring lines, according to analysis. A report at the time of listing said global demand for SPC flooring is growing at a double-digit annual rate.

In competition, one analysis holds that supply share expanded after a competitor exited the TPO sheet business in 2022.

On raw material sourcing, one analysis viewed the company's non-purchase supply structure as easing cost-fluctuation risk, while a company official said the share of directly purchased materials has increased.

The company kept growing in the first half of 2026 despite rising raw material prices and Middle East geopolitical uncertainty.

Large petrochemical players such as LG Chem and Lotte Chemical have results driven heavily by product price cycles, while Bowon Chemical is a processed materials maker tied to customer demand, so its earnings structure differs.

Its small scale and concentrated customer base, however, mean order changes from a single customer feed quickly into results.

06

Outlook

Bowon Chemical first set its 2026 sales target at KRW 64.5 billion when it listed in April 2026, citing the full effect of capacity expansion. In a September 2026 report, a company official said first-half results suggest full-year sales slightly above KRW 60 billion, expected between KRW 60 billion and KRW 64 billion.

Construction materials sales growth and early sales from new businesses are cited as the factors that will shape second-half results. The company invested KRW 2.1 billion in Plant 3 for an expanded electronics surface materials line, with mass production planned to begin in November 2026.

The T-die line at Plant 5, backed by KRW 4.7 billion of investment, entered operation in October 2026, and additional lines for solar EVA film and OLED encapsulation film were planned.

A plan to expand the site from 53,000 square meters to about 75,000 square meters through additional land purchases was reported as confirmed.

In construction materials, the company secured a new annual order of 1.3 million square meters from a domestic building materials company and began initial production in October 2026.

On shareholder returns, an extraordinary shareholders meeting on December 15, 2026 is scheduled to consider reducing capital reserves by KRW 10 billion and transferring the amount to retained earnings. The company said it will review specific return measures such as share buybacks or dividends afterward.

08

Bull factors

Sales growth and new customer wins

First-half 2026 sales reached KRW 28.7 billion, up 11.1% year on year, with SPC flooring leading growth in construction materials. In October 2026, the company began initial supply of tablet PC cover film to Logitech and secured a new annual order of 1.3 million square meters from a domestic building materials company. The company cites these new customers as broadening its sales base.

Quarterly profit turning up

From the Q1 2026 low, operating profit rose to KRW 1.02 billion in Q2 and KRW 1.07 billion in Q3, extending two consecutive quarterly increases. Controlling-shareholder net income turned to a profit of KRW 887 million in Q2.

First-half operating margin was around 7%, below the 9.8% of 2025, but quarterly profit has moved away from its Q1 low.

Capacity investment and shareholder-return funding

Production lines at Chungju Plants 3 and 5, backed by a total of KRW 6.8 billion in investment, have entered operation, and construction materials line utilization rose from 68% in 2025 to 91% in the first half of 2026.

An extraordinary shareholders meeting on December 15, 2026 is scheduled to consider a KRW 10 billion capital reserve reduction. The company says it will review share buybacks or dividends afterward.

09

Bear factors

Margin decline and cost pressure

Operating margin fell from 12.0% in 2024 to 9.8% in 2025, and a media report says it dropped to around 7% in the first half of 2026. A company official explained that as directly purchased materials grow, sales rise but margins can structurally decline. Higher R&D, labor and defect-repair costs also weighed on operating profit.

Borrowing structure and cash outflow

Total borrowings were KRW 36.4 billion at end-June 2026, of which KRW 25.4 billion, or 69.7%, was short-term debt. Operating cash inflow in the first half was KRW 2.6 billion, but KRW 7.3 billion went to tangible asset purchases, so capital spending exceeded operating cash. Total liabilities were KRW 46.2 billion at end-2025.

Customer concentration and business-mix shift

Two major customers together accounted for 95% of first-half 2026 sales. High-performance materials were only 7% of 2025 sales, and the reported target differs between an August 2026 report at 25% and a November 2026 report at 30%. How much new-business sales will contribute to reported results has not yet appeared in periodic filings.

Risk factors

Customer concentration

Two major customers made up 95% of first-half sales, so a drop in orders from a single customer could quickly move results. The automotive interior business relies on expanded supply to Hyundai Motor and KIA, and is exposed to vehicle production swings. The construction segment is also linked to housing transactions and remodeling demand.

Financial and liquidity

Short-term debt accounts for 69.7% of total borrowings of KRW 36.4 billion, adding repayment pressure. Capital injections lowered the debt-to-equity ratio, but the share of short-term borrowing remains high. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 5.29 billion in 2025 while capital spending continues.

Share price and float

A media report said the share price has stayed weak since listing, and a change in tradable shares following the end of lock-up periods was cited as a factor. A September 2026 report cited KOSDAQ's market-cap listing maintenance threshold as KRW 20 billion.

Because the market capitalization is small, its distance from that threshold can change quickly with share price moves.

12

Overall view

Bowon Chemical is expanding sales on the back of automotive interiors and construction materials, with 2025 sales up 31.4%. However, operating margin fell from 12.0% in 2024 to 9.8% in 2025, and controlling-shareholder net income dropped 87.0% to KRW 218 million in 2025.

In 2026, a Q1 controlling-shareholder net loss was followed by a Q2 return to profit and two consecutive quarters of higher operating profit. Q3 controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 498 million was down 43.9% from Q2, so the pace of recovery varies by quarter.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 192.8%, but about 69.7% of KRW 36.4 billion in borrowings at end-June 2026 is short-term, so near-term financial pressure remains. Strengths cited include new customer wins, higher line utilization and funding for shareholder returns.

Weaknesses cited include customer concentration, lower operating margin and the borrowing structure. The company has adjusted its 2026 sales target from KRW 64.5 billion to a range of KRW 60 billion to KRW 64 billion, and the next checkpoint is full-year sales and operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
