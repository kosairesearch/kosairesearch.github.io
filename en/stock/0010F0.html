02

Business structure

Founded in 1995, Bowon Chemical processes raw materials such as polyvinyl chloride (PVC) and thermoplastic polyolefin (TPO) into surface materials supplied to customers.

The business is split into three areas: automotive interior surface materials, construction-material surface products (SPC flooring and wall panels), and high-function sheet materials.

Automotive interior surface materials—used for instrument panels, door trims, and sun visors—hold roughly 75-80% of the domestic market. In 2024, this segment accounted for 60% of revenue, and as of the third quarter of 2025 its share had risen to 65%.

These products are supplied to Hyundai Motor and KIA through Hanwha Advanced Materials.

In construction materials, Bowon Chemical is the only domestic producer of SPC flooring, which it supplies to builders through LX Hausys, and it has secured supply for an art-wall project at a Banpo Jugong reconstruction complex handled by a construction company.

High-function sheet materials include exclusive supply of non-slip pads used in display processes at a Samsung Display plant in Vietnam, along with EVA sheets for solar modules; in October 2026 the company began supplying an initial batch of tablet PC cover film to Logitech, broadening its application base.

As of the third quarter of 2025, Hanwha Advanced Materials accounted for about 63% of revenue and LX Hausys for 28%, meaning the two customers together exceeded 90% of sales. The company listed on KOSDAQ via a SPAC merger in April 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Full-year 2025 revenue came to KRW 52.2 billion, up 31.4% from KRW 39.7 billion in 2024. Operating profit rose 7.5% over the same period, from KRW 4.76 billion to KRW 5.12 billion, but this lagged revenue growth, pulling the operating margin down from 12.0% to 9.8%.

Net income attributable to owners of the parent fell 87.0%, from KRW 1.67 billion in 2024 to KRW 218 million in 2025, and net income including non-controlling interests declined by the same magnitude.

At the end of 2025, total equity stood at KRW 24 billion (equity attributable to owners of the parent was also KRW 24 billion) and total liabilities were KRW 46.2 billion, bringing the debt-to-equity ratio down sharply from 1,013.8% in 2024 to 192.8%.

Operating cash flow declined from KRW 7.59 billion in 2024 to KRW 5.29 billion in 2025.

By quarter, Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 15 billion with operating profit of KRW 971 million, but the company posted a net loss attributable to owners of the parent of KRW 1.25 billion after booking non-recurring, one-off accounting costs related to a fair-value valuation loss on convertible bonds tied to the merger.

In Q2, revenue was KRW 13.7 billion (down 8.6% quarter over quarter), operating profit was KRW 1.02 billion (up 5.5% quarter over quarter), and the company swung to net income attributable to owners of the parent of KRW 887 million.

The sum of net income attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters (Q1-Q4 2025) was KRW 218 million, matching the full-year 2025 result.

On a cumulative basis for the first half of 2026, the operating margin fell further to around 7% from 9.8% in 2025, as both cost of sales and selling and administrative expenses grew faster than revenue.

The company has characterized these costs as one-off and stated it expects to return to profit in the third quarter (announced August 2026, provisional).

05

Industry analysis

The automotive interior surface-material market in which Bowon Chemical operates is in the midst of a material shift driven by automakers' push toward eco-friendly, lightweight interiors.

Wiseport has forecast that the global automotive interior materials market will grow on demand for lightweight, eco-friendly materials tied to the shift toward electrification and autonomous driving.

In the domestic market, a competitor's exit from the interior materials business allowed Bowon Chemical to raise its market share, leaving the potential entry of new competitors as a variable to watch.

The SPC flooring market is expected to see strong growth as green building certification standards tighten, and the company has projected that the global SPC flooring market will grow from USD 2.635 billion in 2024 to USD 8.597 billion by 2030.

This figure, however, was presented by the company itself during the listing process and may differ from independent third-party assessments.

In high-function materials, the company is expanding into display, solar, and electronics-use markets, but these still represent a small share of revenue, meaning reliance on the automotive and construction-material pillars is likely to continue for some time.

Competitively, Bowon Chemical is a small to mid-sized chemical materials company, differing in scale and business scope from large chemical producers such as LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions, and SKC.

06

Outlook

Building on tenoner equipment expansion completed at Plant No. 4 in the first half of 2026, the company raised SPC flooring production capacity from 148,500 square meters to 200,000 square meters per month and is working to expand SPC flooring production and sales in the second half.

In the electronics-use surface materials business, the company invested KRW 2.1 billion in Plant No. 3 within the Jungwon Industrial Complex in Chungju to build a new 660-square-meter facility and ultra-precision coating production line, breaking ground in July and completing the setup in mid-October.

On this basis, the company announced on October 27, 2026 that it had supplied an initial batch of a high-function film developed over about a year, used as tablet PC cover material, to global computer hardware company Logitech.

At Plant No. 5 in Chungju, the company invested KRW 4.7 billion to add T-die equipment, which has completed safety certification and material quality verification and entered operation.

In the transfer printing paper and printed film business, the company secured a new annual order of 1.3 million square meters from a major domestic building materials firm and began initial production.

To expand its production base, the company is pursuing a project to add surrounding land to its existing 53,000-square-meter site across Plants No. 1, 2, and 5, expanding the total to roughly 75,000 square meters.

The company continues to invest toward its goal of raising the share of high-function materials from 7% last year to 25% over the long term, and in early September 2026 it stated that it now expects 2026 annual revenue of KRW 60-64 billion, below its initially presented target of KRW 64.5 billion.

08

Bull factors

Expansion in Construction Materials

As the only domestic SPC flooring producer, the company has a supply channel through LX Hausys and raised production capacity from 148,500 to 200,000 square meters per month through Plant No. 4 expansion.

It has also secured a reference project through confirmed art-wall supply for a Banpo Jugong reconstruction complex. The global SPC flooring market is projected to grow as green building certification standards tighten.

Customer Diversification via High-Function Materials

Bowon Chemical exclusively supplies display-process non-slip pads to a Samsung Display plant in Vietnam and has begun supplying tablet PC cover film to Logitech, broadening its reach into electronics.

It has also secured a new annual order of 1.3 million square meters from a domestic building materials firm for its transfer printing and printed film business. The company has set a goal of raising the share of high-function materials from 7% in 2024 to 25% over the long term.

Improved Financial Structure After Listing

Capital raised through the April 2026 SPAC merger listing helped bring the debt-to-equity ratio down from 1,013.8% in 2024 to 192.8% in 2025 and 142% at the end of June 2026. Operating cash flow has remained a steady net inflow.

The core business maintained operating profit through 2024-2025, and net income attributable to owners of the parent also turned positive in Q2 2026.

09

Bear factors

Deteriorating Profitability Trend

The operating margin fell from 12.0% in 2024 to 9.8% in 2025, and declined further to around 7% in the first half of 2026. In the first half, growth in both cost of sales and selling and administrative expenses outpaced revenue growth.

Rising R&D expenses, labor costs, warranty-repair costs, and depreciation from new facilities continue to pressure profitability.

High Customer Concentration

As of Q3 2025, Hanwha Advanced Materials accounted for about 63% of revenue and LX Hausys for 28%, pushing combined reliance on the two customers above 90%.

The automotive interior segment is concentrated on volume destined for Hyundai Motor and KIA, while the construction materials segment depends on the LX Hausys supply chain, meaning demand shifts at either customer could directly affect results.

Earnings Volatility from One-Off Costs

In Q1 2026, a net loss attributable to owners of the parent was recorded after booking non-recurring costs, including a fair-value valuation loss on convertible bonds tied to the SPAC merger.

Net income attributable to owners of the parent also fell 87.0% in 2025 versus the prior year, creating a gap between rising operating profit and the net income trend. Facility-investment-related expenses and one-off items may continue to sway quarterly earnings even after the listing.

Risk factors

Financial and Leverage Risk

Total borrowings stood at KRW 36.4 billion at the end of the first half of 2026, with short-term borrowings and the current portion of long-term debt totaling KRW 25.4 billion, or 69.7% of the total, leaving a short-term repayment burden.

In the first half, the company spent KRW 7.3 billion on property and equipment, exceeding operating cash flow of KRW 2.6 billion. While the debt-to-equity ratio has declined on capital raised, the structure of heavy reliance on short-term borrowing remains.

Listing Maintenance Requirement Risk

As of early September 2026, market capitalization had shrunk to roughly KRW 23.8 billion, narrowing the buffer above the KOSDAQ market-cap listing maintenance requirement of KRW 20 billion to just KRW 3.8 billion.

Market capitalization has since recovered to a higher level amid a share price rebound, but given the stock's small-cap volatility, the buffer against the listing requirement could narrow again.

Margin Pressure from Changing Cost Structure

As the company's procurement structure has shifted from simple contract processing to direct purchase of raw materials, revenue scale has increased while profitability has tended to decline.

Because price swings in raw materials such as PVC and TPO flow directly into cost of sales, raw-material market conditions have a significant impact on margins.

Overall view

Bowon Chemical's 2025 revenue grew 31.4% year over year to KRW 52.2 billion, but net income attributable to owners of the parent fell 87.0% to KRW 218 million, reflecting a gap between top-line growth and earnings strength during the year.

In 2026, the company swung from a net loss attributable to owners of the parent in Q1 to net income in Q2, but the cumulative operating margin for the first half fell further to around 7%, suggesting profitability recovery has not yet fully taken hold.

Alongside its automotive interior materials and SPC flooring businesses, the company is expanding into high-function materials such as tablet PC film and electronics-use surface materials, though dependence on Hanwha Advanced Materials and LX Hausys for revenue remains high.

Its financial structure improved markedly after the April 2026 listing, with the debt-to-equity ratio falling sharply, but a high proportion of short-term borrowing and ongoing capital expenditure burdens persist.

The company has guided for a return to profit in Q3 2026 and full-year revenue of KRW 60-64 billion, though these remain provisional figures not yet confirmed by regular disclosure filings.

Tracking the durability of the profitability recovery and the pace of new-business expansion through upcoming quarterly and annual reports appears to be the next step.
