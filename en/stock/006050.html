02

Business structure

KUK Young G M is a specialty glass processor that fabricates insulated and tempered glass and installs it at construction sites, and it has been listed on KOSDAQ since October 1994.

The company says it has built its glass processing know-how over more than six decades and mainly carries out glazing work for residential, commercial and public buildings. According to its company profile, all of its revenue comes from glass processing, with construction glazing as the core market.

The same profile states that the company keeps the top spot in the Korean construction capability rankings by contracted capacity, and that a major builder has recognized it as an excellent construction partner in building works.

Its product mix is widening into higher-value items, including heat-shielding and fire-resistant glass, bulletproof glass, heated glass, building-integrated photovoltaics (BIPV), defense-grade specialty glass and electromagnetic shielding glass.

It signed an official BIPV license partnership with Australia's ClearVue in November 2025, and the company says the domestic certification process for BIPV is nearing completion. Its clients range from large builders such as Samsung C&T to small and mid-sized construction firms.

Notable awards include a KRW 11.11 billion glass works contract for NCSOFT's global R&D center in Seongnam, signed in January 2025 and running to October 24, 2027, and a KRW 4.98 billion contract disclosed in June 2026 running to October 11, 2027.

A securities information provider's analysis says low-price offers and excessive competition from small and mid-sized builders are pressuring profitability.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on DART annual filings, revenue was KRW 63.6 billion in 2022, KRW 76.6 billion in 2023, KRW 75.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 60.2 billion in 2025. 2023 was the largest year in the period and 2025 was the smallest.

The 2025 revenue decline of 20.2% from the prior year was attributed by the company to a local unsold-housing problem in the property market, which cut sales by more than 20%. The same explanation said operating margin edged up as raw material supply stabilized and financial soundness was maintained.

Operating profit was KRW 2.12 billion in 2022, the largest year in the period, and KRW 900 million in 2024, the smallest. Operating margin fell from 3.3% in 2022 to 1.2% in 2023, then held at 1.2% in 2024 and rose to 1.5% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.58 billion in 2024, the largest year, and KRW 778 million in 2023, the smallest. The 231.5% jump in 2024 coincided with a negative income tax expense in a financial data aggregation, which lifted net income above pre-tax profit.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 34.3% to KRW 1.69 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow went from -KRW 4.15 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 6.81 billion in 2024, then declined to KRW 3.63 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 15.7 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 15.4 billion in the fourth quarter, down 1.8% from the previous quarter.

First-quarter 2026 revenue fell 28.7% to KRW 11 billion, then rose 20.1% to KRW 13.2 billion in the second quarter and was flat at KRW 13.2 billion in the third quarter, down 16.0% year on year.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 111 million in the third quarter of 2025 to an operating profit of KRW 717 million in the fourth quarter, then back to an operating loss of KRW 1.76 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

The loss narrowed to KRW 483 million in the second quarter and widened to KRW 514 million in the third quarter, which was also a larger loss than a year earlier. Operating losses have continued for three straight quarters from the first quarter of 2026 through the third quarter.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders were KRW 586 million, KRW 826 million and KRW 925 million in the first, second and third quarters of 2026.

Over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, net losses attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 1.32 billion. A securities information provider put the first-half 2026 standalone revenue decline at 17.0% year on year.

05

Industry analysis

KUK Young G M's end market is domestic construction, particularly residential building activity. A securities information provider's analysis as of late August 2026 points to a slump in domestic residential construction that has weakened demand for processed glass.

In its 2025 annual explanation, the company cited the local unsold-housing problem in the property market as the reason for the sales decline.

In redevelopment projects, a July 2026 report citing Seoul city data said that restrictions on relocation-related loans leave the financing of 14 of 35 redevelopment zones scheduled for relocation in 2026 uncertain.

On pricing, low-price offers and intensifying competition from small and mid-sized builders are cited as pressure points. Projects awarded by large builders carry bigger contract values but longer construction periods, so they feed into results with a lag.

By contrast, volume from small and mid-sized builders faces stronger price competition. The company says large builders have recognized it as a construction partner, and it presents those relationships as a competitive strength.

The pace of demand recovery depends on how quickly housing supply and redevelopment construction starts move, which is the key variable.

Outlook

In a July 2026 open letter to shareholders, the company said it would pursue large contract wins, expand new businesses and strengthen IR and dividends.

On contracts, it said it is pursuing major projects including the Suseo Station complex development, the Jamsil MICE project, the Bank of Korea redevelopment in Yeoksam-dong and a semiconductor cluster in Cheongju.

Among new growth businesses, BIPV is nearing completion of domestic certification, and the company is jointly developing a windscreen for a light armed helicopter with a defense research institute.

It is also developing specialty double-glazed glass that addresses radio wave blocking inside buildings in 5G and 6G environments.

For shareholder returns, it said it is reviewing and advancing higher dividends, treasury share purchases and cancellation, responsible share purchases by its major shareholder, and a bonus share issue.

Its treasury share purchase covered 100,000 shares between September 16 and November 13, 2026, and treasury holdings afterward were 102,563 shares, or 2.93%. A treasury share purchase results report was filed on November 16, 2026.

The largest shareholder, Choi Jae-won, held 596,519 shares, unchanged during the purchase period.

The company's project page lists the Yongin Everland Station Canvill apartment construction, with a work period ending November 30, 2026; the Jeonju Seoseo-dong Gamnamu-gol redevelopment, ending December 31, 2026; and the Seoul Arena complex, ending July 30, 2027. Whether revenue recovers will depend on progress at these sites and the size of new contract wins.

Bull factors

Visibility from contract periods

A KRW 4.98 billion contract signed in June 2026 has a work period running to October 11, 2027. The KRW 11.11 billion NCSOFT R&D center glass works awarded by Samsung C&T also runs until October 24, 2027. Because both work periods extend through autumn 2027, they provide some visibility on revenue.

The company also says it is pursuing major projects such as the Suseo Station complex and the Jamsil MICE project.

Shift toward shareholder returns

In its July 2026 open letter, the company said it is reviewing and advancing higher dividends, share buybacks and cancellation, purchases by its major shareholder and a bonus share issue. It acquired 100,000 shares between September 16 and November 13, 2026, and reported 102,563 treasury shares afterward, or 2.93%.

The treasury share purchase results report was submitted on November 16, 2026. Most of the dividend and cancellation measures have not yet been fixed in scale or timing through disclosures.

Higher-value products and margin room

The company says BIPV, under a license with ClearVue, is nearing completion of domestic certification. It is also developing a defense windscreen and radio wave-blocking double glazing for 5G and 6G environments. A larger share of higher-value products would create room to lift operating margin. The operating margin edged up to 1.5% in 2025 from 1.2% in 2024.

Bear factors

Revenue decline and cumulative losses

Revenue in 2025 was KRW 60.2 billion, down 20.2% from the prior year. Operating losses have continued for three straight quarters from the first quarter of 2026 through the third quarter, and the third-quarter loss of KRW 514 million was larger than a year earlier. Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.32 billion.

Listing-watch concerns

Market capitalization is below the KRW 20 billion listing-watch threshold. A market notice on September 15, 2026 flagged the company as a listing-watch concern.

The company argues that the share price decline is unrelated to any weakening of its business base, while also acknowledging shortcomings in shareholder communication. If the shortfall persists over a set period, the risk of a listing-watch designation rises.

Industry conditions and slower cash generation

Weak domestic residential construction and low-price competition from small and mid-sized builders are cited as factors weighing on profitability. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 6.81 billion in 2024 to KRW 3.63 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 sales drop of more than 20% to the local unsold-housing problem, and the pressure has continued into 2026.

Risk factors

New business certification timing

BIPV is nearing completion of domestic certification under its license agreement with Australia's ClearVue. If certification approval is delayed or granted with conditions, the timing of domestic sales for these higher-value products could slip.

Development of the defense windscreen and radio wave-blocking double glazing could also take longer, delaying their contribution to earnings. If new-business results come later than expected, they may not be enough to offset weaker results in the core glazing business.

Contract flow and cancellations

Contract size and construction volume depend on residential construction conditions and the financing environment for redevelopment projects. A July 2026 report also pointed to uncertainty in redevelopment financing.

A supply contract worth about KRW 3 billion was cancelled and disclosed in November 2023, so contract continuity is also a variable.

Raw materials and cost structure

The company said stable raw material supply helped defend operating margin in 2025. If raw material prices rise again or labor and other costs increase, profitability could come under pressure alongside low-price competition. With operating margin at around 1%, the company is sensitive to cost changes.

Overall view

KUK Young G M is focused on glass processing and construction, and its DART filings show 2025 revenue of KRW 60.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 930 million.

Revenue has declined for two consecutive years since the 2023 peak, and operating losses have persisted for three straight quarters from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.32 billion, and a securities information provider put the first-half 2026 standalone revenue decline at 17.0% year on year.

Strengths include large contract work running into October 2027, shareholder-return measures under review, treasury share buybacks and development of higher-value products such as BIPV.

Weaknesses include the slump in residential construction, low-price competition from small and mid-sized builders and market capitalization below the listing-watch threshold. The weight of these factors will depend on whether earnings recover, so current data does not clearly establish which side has the upper hand.

The next points to check are the operating results in the 2026 annual business report, first-quarter 2027 results and whether a listing-watch designation follows.
