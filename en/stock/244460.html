02

Business structure

Olipass is an RNA therapeutics developer founded in November 2006, and it positions its proprietary OliPass PNA (peptide nucleic acid) platform as its core technology. The platform has been presented as strong in binding target RNA and in cell permeability.

Its basic strategy applies the exon skipping principle to suppress production of disease-causing proteins. Its lead pipeline, the non-opioid pain killer OLP-1002, drew attention in 2020 when Australian Phase 1b approval was reported, and a hyperlipidemia candidate was presented at preclinical stage in the same period.

Early on, a cosmetics subsidiary, Olipass Cosmeceuticals, launched products on home shopping channels in December 2019 to broaden revenue.

However, the second-quarter 2026 revenue mix, as compiled by Butler company data, was 96% technology export and 4% rental income, so revenue depends heavily on technology transfer contracts. Platform-based joint research with global pharmaceutical companies is reported as ongoing.

In competition, OliX Pharmaceuticals and other domestic firms have entered RNA therapeutics, while Biogen, Pfizer and Roche have made technology transfer deals in the same field. No pipeline asset moving into commercialization has yet appeared in reported results. The company had 26 employees as of October 3, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales rose from KRW 2.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.29 billion in 2023, up 130.4% year on year, making 2023 the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Sales then fell 85.7% to KRW 756 million in 2024, the smallest year, and rose 7.9% to KRW 816 million in 2025.

Sharp swings in sales suggest revenue depends more on contract and technology transfer timing than on product sales. Operating loss narrowed each year, from KRW 24.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.7 billion in 2023, KRW 10.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.39 billion in 2025.

Operating margin ran at -1,050.9% in 2022, -259.1% in 2023, -1,338.5% in 2024 and -905.6% in 2025, with large swings because the sales base is small. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders shrank from KRW 24.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.32 billion in 2024, then widened to KRW 15.4 billion in 2025.

The 2025 net loss including non-controlling interests was also KRW 15.4 billion, so minority interest effects are not visible on an annual basis. Operating cash outflow narrowed from -KRW 15.5 billion in 2022 to -KRW 6.81 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 1.7 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 15.6 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio was 170.3% in 2022, 445.8% in 2023 and 121.6% in 2024, then jumped to 917.8% in 2025.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 124 million in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 129 million in the fourth quarter to KRW 91.37 million in the first quarter of 2026, then KRW 1.17 billion in the second quarter, up 1,178.5% from the prior quarter.

The second-quarter operating loss narrowed to KRW 117 million alongside that revenue spike, and since technology export made up 96% of the quarter's revenue per Butler's compilation, the revenue looks largely one-off.

Third-quarter sales fell 98.5% to KRW 17.6 million, and the operating loss widened to KRW 1.02 billion. Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders were KRW 1.92 billion, KRW 570 million and KRW 1.23 billion in the first three quarters of 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 5.36 billion.

Operating losses have continued for five straight quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

Industry analysis

RNA therapeutics block production of disease-causing proteins at the gene expression stage, which is described as a step beyond small molecules or antibodies that act on proteins already formed.

A 2020 securities research report named delivery of therapeutic molecules accurately into cells as the field's biggest difficulty. The same report cited a 2018 estimate that biopharmaceuticals accounted for 25% of the total drug market in 2017.

Overseas, Biogen, Pfizer and Roche have signed technology transfer deals for RNA therapeutic candidates. Domestically, biotech companies including OliX Pharmaceuticals have entered RNA therapeutics, raising competitive intensity.

Olipass highlighted cell permeability as a strength, but the same report pointed to clinical success of its own pipeline as the key driver of growth. Per Alphasquare's annual data for 89 companies in the life sciences and engineering sector, average sector sales were KRW 59.2 billion versus KRW 800 million at Olipass.

In the same data, Olipass's debt-to-equity ratio of 917.78% compares with a sector average of 115.23%, ranking second within the sector. Average sector market capitalization of KRW 636.9 billion also shows a wide scale gap. A securities industry source has pointed to a recurring pattern of distress among technology-listed companies.

06

Outlook

The listing procedure will shape the near-term path more than anything else. The KRX Kosdaq Committee resolved to delist Olipass on October 14, 2025, and the company filed for an injunction the next day, which put the clearing sale and other follow-up steps on hold.

In a January 15, 2026 correction filing, the company said the injunction case had been transferred from the Busan District Court Eastern Branch to the Seoul Southern District Court. In an August 14, 2026 notice, KRX said it would proceed with follow-up procedures once the related litigation is decided.

The half-year report filed the same day disclosed full capital impairment as of June 30, 2026, and KRX said this added a listing-eligibility review reason but did not start a separate procedure.

Press reports also said the half-year review came back with a disclaimer of opinion, with the capital impairment ratio given as 117.40%.

In 2024, the impairment ratio exceeded 50% and the stock was designated as an administrative issue, and in June 2024 the largest shareholder changed to Invest Partners No. 1, holding 12.22%, in a third-party rights offering described as aimed at management normalization.

On the business side, the timing of the next pipeline stage and whether new technology transfer deals convert into reported revenue are the next checkpoints. The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, which will update the annual capital structure and the basis for listing eligibility.
