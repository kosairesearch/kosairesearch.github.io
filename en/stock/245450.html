02

Business structure

CNS Link was founded in September 2000 as SIM Circuit and later changed its corporate name to the current one. It is a telecommunications and broadcasting equipment maker that listed on the KONEX market on June 24, 2016.

The company website presents LTE/5G routers, IoT access security, and a convergence unit as its three businesses. The router line consists of wireless communication terminals for industrial sites, corporate networks, and public infrastructure that the company designs and manufactures itself.

IoTPass is an automatic communal-door opening service that supports contactless access control through AI facial recognition and smartphone Bluetooth. The convergence unit develops AI/IoT-based in-vehicle dashcams, navigation devices, and fleet-management solutions.

The revenue mix in the 2023 annual report was as follows. Of total revenue of KRW 19,455 million, IoT devices accounted for 55.30%, dashcams for 21.93%, and navigation for 14.64%. The same report states that the company works with SK Telecom and KT in addition to LG Uplus.

Router demand is closely tied to swings in demand in the IoT smart-home market. Dashcams also had an export leg, with export sales of KRW 1,650 million in 2023, out of total dashcam sales of KRW 4,267 million.

Market-share data by competitor could not be verified, so qualitatively the company is a small manufacturer with heavy carrier exposure, competing against both large terminal and network vendors and smaller makers.

Headcount is in the range of about 40 or more employees, depending on the source, which makes it a very small organization.

03

Recent trends

The 2025 standalone disclosed results show revenue of KRW 17,944 million, operating profit of KRW 1,288 million, and net income of KRW 1,180 million.

The same filing lists total assets of KRW 10,537 million, total liabilities of KRW 6,736 million, and total equity of KRW 3,801 million, and earnings per share were positive, in line with the net income.

According to a financial-data aggregator, revenue grew 3.6% and operating profit rose 78.2%, for an operating margin of 7.2%. The profit recovery was therefore much larger than the revenue change.

Operating profit has improved from an operating loss of KRW 147.76 million in 2022 to KRW 596.73 million in 2023 and KRW 722.76 million in 2024. Revenue, by contrast, fell to KRW 19.45 billion in 2023 and KRW 17.32 billion in 2024 before rebounding to KRW 17.94 billion in 2025.

Net income rose 216.7% to KRW 1.18 billion in 2025. The company filed its annual and audit reports on March 19, 2026, and its March 27 annual general meeting approved every agenda item, including the financial statements and the reappointment of inside directors.

The share price was KRW 1,898 at 17:02 KST on October 27, 2026, down 0.05% on the previous day, for a market capitalization of KRW 3.0 billion. As of October 1, 2026, the 52-week range was KRW 1,084 to KRW 2,295.

On April 20, 2026 the stock closed at KRW 1,599, with a market value of KRW 2.4 billion and 1,579,960 shares outstanding. Quarterly or half-year results for 2026 could not be confirmed. However, the filing list shows an investor-relations briefing result dated June 22, 2026 and a briefing held on September 17, 2026.

04

Outlook

Future results depend on how fast routers and IoT access security grow. On its website the company says it is widening its range across LTE/5G routers, IoT door-access terminals, dashcams, and AI-based mobility devices. It also points to expansion into overseas markets. AIoTPass was launched in 2023.

Public materials do not show how much it contributes to revenue. In terms of business metrics, any room to lift the 7.2% operating margin depends on whether the revenue rebound continues and on changes in product mix.

Collaboration with carriers supports order stability, but it also exposes the company to pricing pressure and order-cycle swings. The 2026 results will need to be checked once the periodic reports are filed.

05

Bull factors

Stronger earnings base

Operating profit rose to KRW 1,288 million in 2025. The operating margin was recorded at 7.2%. Operating profit has improved for three consecutive years since the operating loss in 2022. With revenue at a similar scale, higher profitability suggests a better cost structure.

Carrier ties and product breadth

The company works with SK Telecom and KT in addition to LG Uplus. IoT devices were the largest line in 2023, at 55.30% of revenue. Spread across routers, access security, dashcams, and navigation, the company is less dependent on any single product. It also has newer lines such as facial-recognition access services.

Growing equity base

Total equity grew from KRW 2.62 billion in 2024 to KRW 3.80 billion in 2025. Accumulated net income has thickened the capital base. A financial-data aggregator rated the company in the upper tier of its industry on scale. This is a reference point for the financial footing of a small KONEX company.

06

Bear factors

Stagnant revenue

Revenue is lower, at KRW 17.94 billion in 2025 versus KRW 19.99 billion in 2022. The profit improvement owed more to cost efficiency than to top-line growth. The 2024 revenue decline shows sensitivity to demand swings. Unless sales grow again, the durability of the profit gain remains an open question.

Very low liquidity

According to an investing data site, average trading volume over the past three months is about 61 shares. Market capitalization is only KRW 3.0 billion, with 1,579,960 shares outstanding. Even small trades can move quoted and executed prices sharply. As a result, the link between disclosed earnings and the share price can be weak.

Balance-sheet strength

At the end of 2025, total liabilities of KRW 6,736 million were roughly 1.8 times total equity of KRW 3,801 million. One financial-rating site found strengths in profitability but rated stability lower than other categories. As a small manufacturer, working-capital needs and reliance on borrowing may make earnings sensitive to swings.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Routers and IoT terminals are closely tied to swings in demand in the smart-home market. Orders may change if consumer conditions or carriers' equipment and terminal spending shift. Dashcams carry an export share, so exchange rates and overseas demand also matter. Macro shifts can raise sales volatility for a small manufacturer.

Industry

Navigation devices and dashcams are product categories with substantial existing penetration. In 2023 the two together made up about 36% of revenue (21.93% and 14.64%). Smartphone-linked navigation and built-in vehicle functions could act as substitutes. A carrier-centered supply structure also brings limited pricing power and customer concentration.

Company-specific

With a headcount in the 40s, the company may depend heavily on a few key people. At the annual meeting, approval of the financial statements was 57.4% of total issued voting shares. The minority-shareholder mix and governance should be checked separately. As a KONEX-listed company with small trading volumes, its financing options are also limited.

08

Overall view

CNS Link improved its profit indicators in 2025, with standalone operating profit of KRW 1,288 million and net income of KRW 1,180 million. Revenue over the same period was KRW 17,944 million, below the 2022 level, so top-line growth is not confirmed.

Its business is centered on IoT and routers supplied in cooperation with carriers, while dashcams and navigation are comparatively mature product lines. A balance sheet in which liabilities exceed equity, and average volume of about 61 shares over three months, are items to weigh together.

Quarterly and half-year results for 2026, as well as the revenue contribution of new products, could not be confirmed and should be checked when periodic reports are filed. This report is for information only, and any judgment on the share price is left to the reader.
