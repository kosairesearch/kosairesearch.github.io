Center One Concentration, Japan Residential Diversification

02

Business structure

Miraeasset Maps Realty Investment Company is a listed public real estate investment company managed by Mirae Asset Global Investments.

Its corporate name in regulatory filings is the Miraeasset Maps Asia Pacific Real Estate Public Offering No. 1 Investment Company, classified as a KOSPI-listed entity. It was established in 2007 as a public real estate fund and grew in 2011 by buying a 50% stake in the Center One building in Euljiro.

As of December 9, 2025, the main asset weights were Center One land at 47.58%, the Center One building at 20.77%, and Inter Asset Holdings at 8.62%. Together, the Center One land and building account for a share in the high 60% range, so the structure depends heavily on a single office asset in central Seoul.

According to the third-quarter 2025 report, Center One's market-appraised value was about KRW 1.07 trillion, more than twice its book value of about KRW 460 billion. Also in the third quarter of 2025, total assets were about KRW 1.4 trillion and total borrowings were KRW 375 billion.

Overseas investment has widened into Japanese residential assets. In 2025 the company added four rental apartment buildings in Osaka with 499 units. On September 1, 2026, it announced the purchase of four rental apartment assets in Tokyo with 238 units for about KRW 90.6 billion.

Its Japanese residential holdings rose to about JPY 20.8 billion at purchase cost, covering 737 units. On the competitive side, office-focused listed REITs such as Shinhan Alpha REIT are natural comparables for dividend-seeking investors.

According to NH Investment & Securities, Shinhan Alpha REIT's market capitalization was about KRW 649.4 billion as of May 21, 2026. The separate listed REIT sponsored by the same group that holds the Gwanggyo retail asset is a different stock and should not be confused with this company.

03

Recent trends

On October 20, 2026, the company filed a value-enhancement plan covering better market valuation, dividend stability, portfolio efficiency and wider shareholder communication.

According to the plan, investment assets were valued at about KRW 1.5194 trillion and operating revenue was KRW 36.1 billion as of end-September 2026. Against the same point a year earlier, asset value rose 6.5% and operating revenue rose 7.4%.

The company said P/NAV improved from about 0.5x at end-September 2025 to 0.6x at end-March 2026 and 0.7x at end-September 2026. Investing.com data show a dividend of KRW 275 per share for the December 29, 2025 record date and an interim dividend of KRW 137 per share for the June 29, 2026 record date.

The share price moved over a wide range during this period. The close on February 12, 2026 was KRW 5,910. On the morning of September 11, 2026, the stock traded at KRW 7,200. Investing.com showed a 52-week range of KRW 4,525 to KRW 8,190 as of August 7, 2026.

The October 27, 2026 close was KRW 6,710, unchanged from the prior session, with a market capitalization of KRW 622.9 billion and trading value of KRW 300 million. Mirae Asset Life Insurance, a major shareholder, sold small blocks on February 11 and 12, 2026, lowering its stake to 27.85%.

Investing.com lists Mirae Asset Life Insurance at 27.67% (June 1, 2026) and Mirae Asset Global Investments at 25.00% (April 30, 2026) among top holders.

04

Outlook

The company's dividend targets are KRW 142 per share for the second half of 2026 and KRW 150 per share for the first half of 2027. Combined, the KRW 292 equals about 5.8% of par value. Over the longer term, the plan is to build a base for an annual dividend of KRW 300 per share, about 6.0% of par value.

For P/NAV, the medium- to long-term goal is 0.8x or the average of comparable listed real estate vehicles. To lift operating revenue, the company said it would raise occupancy at key assets and improve lease terms at renewal to reflect market conditions.

The sector backdrop contains many variables. NH Investment & Securities reported that the 3-year government bond yield set a new yearly high of 3.776% on May 15, 2026. The same report noted that refinancing has raised the share of floating-rate debt linked to the 91-day CD rate, which weighs on REIT earnings.

Shinhan Financial Group research said that prolonged Middle East geopolitical risk and a high-rate hold had weakened earlier expectations of rate cuts. No verified information on the rate mix or maturity of the company's borrowings was found, so the size of any rate impact is hard to state.

The dividend and P/NAV figures above are plans rather than results, and delivery will need to be confirmed in later disclosures.

05

Bull factors

Quantified shareholder-return plan

The company published its dividend and P/NAV goals as numbers and committed to disclose NAV and dividend direction through its website IR channel. It also plans to review progress on the plan each year and report it. Investing.com data show annual dividends running from KRW 200 in 2013 to KRW 275 in 2025. More disclosure makes it easier for investors to track the source of dividends.

Gap between appraised and book value

Center One's appraised value was more than twice its book value in the third-quarter 2025 report. If appraisals hold, NAV supports the company's asset base. The P/NAV the company disclosed was about 0.7x at end-September 2026, still below 1x. Investors may read that gap in different ways.

Diversification from Japanese housing

Japanese rental housing is being added to an income base tilted toward Seoul offices. All the Tokyo assets were completed in 2024-2025 and had an average occupancy above 95% at end-July 2026. The company said occupancy across the four Osaka properties was close to 97.2% at end-October 2025. The Tokyo purchase used cash on hand, so it did not add a separate borrowing burden.

06

Bear factors

Single-asset concentration

The Center One land and building make up a large part of assets, so changes in that building's occupancy and rents feed directly into earnings. Occupancy there rose in 2013 when Mirae Asset affiliates moved in. No verified information on the current tenant mix or the share of affiliates was found. If tenants are concentrated, volatility around lease expiries could be larger.

Interest rates and leverage

Total borrowings were KRW 375 billion in the third quarter of 2025. Higher rates can raise interest costs and shrink the pool available for dividends. NH Investment & Securities noted in its May 2026 report that higher government bond yields narrowed REIT dividend spreads. The company's floating-rate share has not been confirmed.

Ownership structure and thin liquidity

In Investing.com data, the two Mirae Asset-affiliated holders together own more than half the shares (as of April 30 and June 1, 2026). Free float may be limited, and trading value on October 27, 2026 was only KRW 300 million.

One news outlet assessed that actual delivery of the dividend-expansion target depends on cash flow. The targets are also not confirmed dividends.

07

Risk factors

Macro

The 3-year government bond yield reached a yearly high of 3.776% on May 15, 2026. If the high-rate hold and Middle East tensions persist, funding costs and the appeal of REIT dividends remain under pressure. The rate path can move sentiment across the whole sector.

Sector and credit

JR Global REIT's filing for rehabilitation proceedings heightened credit risk across the Korean REIT market and weakened sentiment. Concerns about distress spread mainly among REITs holding overseas assets. Because the company holds Japanese residential assets, it may be exposed to sector sentiment. No direct link between the company and that case has been confirmed.

Business and FX

With Japanese residential holdings at 737 units, the yen exchange rate and local rental market conditions can affect earnings. No verified information on the hedging policy or local financing structure was found. Lease expiries and rent negotiations at Center One are also variables for operating revenue.

08

Overall view

Miraeasset Maps Realty Investment Company is a listed public real estate investment company centered on its Center One stake in Seoul, with Japanese residential assets added.

As of end-September 2026, investment asset value and operating revenue were higher than a year earlier, and the P/NAV the company disclosed was about 0.7x. On October 20, 2026, the company set an annual dividend of KRW 300 per share and a P/NAV of 0.8x as medium- to long-term goals.

Against that, the concentration in Center One, the rate environment, the large affiliated shareholder base and thin trading value should be weighed in any reading. The dividend and P/NAV targets are plans rather than confirmed results, and delivery should be checked against future filings and IR materials.
