02

Business structure

P&S Robotics is a rehabilitation robot company founded in 2003, and the company states that it developed and commercialized Korea's first walking-rehabilitation robot system in 2011. Its flagship product is the Walkbot, a gait-rehabilitation robot supplied to medical institutions under a B2B model.

The company listed on KOSDAQ in July 2024. It says it has developed around 300 core robotics technologies, including humanoid, multi-joint, haptic and robot joint reducer technologies, and it sees room to extend them into upper-limb rehabilitation and wearable robots.

Upper-limb rehabilitation robots have been reported as new products, and the shoulder rehabilitation robot Healerbot was shown at the MEDICA exhibition in Germany. Upper-limb products are planned to use the distribution network built for the lower-limb robot.

Overseas sales dominate the mix: the export share was 85.1% in the third quarter of 2024, and the Russian share was reported at 25%. The Walkbot holds CE marking from 2013, FDA clearance from 2015, and EU MDR certification obtained in 2023.

Kiwoom Securities pointed to an export-related revenue share of 93.7% on a cumulative basis through the third quarter of 2025. Revenue is recognized only after products reach customers and are assembled, which tends to concentrate recognition in the fourth quarter. Analysts note that three companies, including P&S Robotics, compete in the domestic market.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 6.01 billion in 2023 to KRW 7.11 billion in 2024, an 18.2% increase, and then to KRW 7.64 billion in 2025, a 7.5% increase, marking two consecutive years of growth from 2024 through 2025. Of the 2023 to 2025 period, 2025 was the largest year for revenue and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit grew from KRW 1.32 billion in 2023 to KRW 1.62 billion in 2024, up 22.5%, then fell 38.0% to KRW 1 billion in 2025, which makes 2025 the smallest year in the period.

The operating margin moved from 22.0% in 2023 and 22.8% in 2024 to 13.1% in 2025, so profitability weakened even as revenue grew, which suggests development spending weighed on margins.

The company attributed the 2025 change mainly to higher R&D expenses and a temporary drop in operating profit caused by the timing of revenue recognition.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 1.41 billion in 2023 to KRW 2.32 billion in 2024, up 64.7%, and then to KRW 2.75 billion in 2025, up 18.4%, marking two consecutive years of growth from 2024 through 2025.

In 2025 that figure exceeded operating profit, which suggests non-operating items added to earnings. Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 2.75 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow moved the other way: it was KRW 807 million in 2023 and KRW 1.21 billion in 2024, then turned to -KRW 1.58 billion in 2025, and media reports said receivables rose from KRW 2.06 billion to KRW 4.77 billion.

In the third quarter of 2025, revenue was KRW 784 million, with an operating loss of KRW 818 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 156 million, and the company said prototype and exhibition costs were booked in selling and administrative expenses that quarter.

The first quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 932 million, an operating loss of KRW 357 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 57.58 million.

In the second quarter of 2026, revenue rose to KRW 1.73 billion, up 85.4% from the prior quarter, while the operating loss widened to KRW 458 million, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to KRW 218 million.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue reached KRW 2.29 billion, up 32.4% from the prior quarter and up 191.9% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 388 million, a swing to profit from a year earlier, but net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 52.95 million, down 75.7% from the prior quarter.

Over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 1.5 billion.

Industry analysis

Demand for rehabilitation robots is supported by structural factors such as an aging population and a shortage of therapists. Korea Investment & Securities researchers said in a 2025 report that accelerating population aging and the shortage of therapists were expected to persist for some time.

A December 2025 report by Asia Economy cited a securities analyst projecting that the rehabilitation robot market would grow at an annual rate of 27.2%, but that is a projection from the time of publication and should be rechecked against the original report.

In overseas markets, certifications and distribution networks act as entry barriers, and P&S Robotics reportedly holds 19 certifications and 42 patents while maintaining export contracts with 15 countries.

The domestic market has three competing companies, and Kiwoom Securities described the company's domestic performance as relatively weak.

Listed robotics peers such as Doosan Robotics, Rainbow Robotics and Robotis are centered on industrial and collaborative robots, so their business structures differ from medical rehabilitation devices.

The share price is also influenced by thematic flows in robotics stocks, and a November 2026 report said the stock rose strongly alongside a broad rally in robotics shares. Because revenue recognition concentrates in the fourth quarter, quarterly results are subject to seasonality.

Bull factors

Export base and accumulated certifications

The export share was 85.1% in the third quarter of 2024, and the company was reported to hold export contracts with 15 countries. The Walkbot holds CE and FDA clearances, and EU MDR certification was obtained in 2023. These certifications and patents can serve as a base for wider overseas distribution.

Recovery in quarterly results

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive in the second quarter of 2026, and operating profit turned to KRW 388 million in the third quarter. Revenue has risen for two consecutive quarters since the first-quarter 2026 low, and third-quarter revenue was up 191.9% year on year. The move from losses to profit is a supportive factor.

A nearly debt-free balance sheet

The debt-to-equity ratio was 4.3% at the end of 2025, and total liabilities were a small KRW 1.86 billion. Media reports said borrowings stood at zero as of the third quarter of 2025. This financial capacity lets the company pursue R&D and upper-limb mass-production preparation at the same time.

The company cited higher R&D expenses and the timing of revenue recognition, but whether this cost structure is temporary has yet to be confirmed in full-year results. If margins do not recover, concerns about earnings capacity could persist.

Cash flow and receivables pressure

Operating cash flow turned to -KRW 1.58 billion in 2025. Media reports said receivables rose from KRW 2.06 billion to KRW 4.77 billion over the same period, and cash and equivalents also declined.

Operating cash turning negative even with positive operating profit can be read as a sign that earnings are not converting into cash smoothly.

Volatility in quarterly earnings

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 52.95 million, down 75.7% from the prior quarter. Revenue is recognized after assembly at the customer site, which tends to concentrate it in the fourth quarter.

If profit and loss swing sharply from quarter to quarter even after the return to profit, the sustainability of earnings is harder to judge.

Risk factors

Export dependence and geopolitical exposure

The export share was 85.1% in the third quarter of 2024, and the Russian share was reported at 25%. Changes in policy, exchange rates or payment conditions in a given country could directly affect revenue. The cost of maintaining overseas certifications and responding to local regulation also remains a long-term risk.

Share price volatility and thematic flows

The share price rose 29.95% in a single session on October 28, 2026, showing large swings. A November 2026 report said the stock was strong amid a broad rally in robotics shares. Because thematic flows can move the share price independently of earnings, price drivers and earnings drivers should be separated.

New product mass production and contract execution

The upper-limb rehabilitation robot has entered mass-production preparation, but whether mass production and sales proceed as planned has not yet been confirmed.

If the sales approach using the distribution network of the lower-limb robot does not match actual demand, the revenue contribution from the new product could be delayed. Because a correction was filed on the supply contract, whether its size and performance period are confirmed is important.

Delays in mass production or problems in executing the contract could directly affect revenue flow in later quarters.

Overall view

From 2023 to 2025, revenue rose from KRW 6.01 billion to KRW 7.64 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 1.41 billion to KRW 2.75 billion.
