Trading halt follows first annual profit

02

Business structure

Founded in 2002 in Gwangmyeong, TL Engineering designs and builds cleanrooms for materials, parts and equipment, pharmaceuticals and biotech, food and cosmetics, and film and battery manufacturers.

It listed on the KONEX market on September 27, 2022, and is classified under architectural engineering and related technical services.

Its core offering is an end-to-end solution covering design, construction and maintenance, and press reports have described its control and HVAC technology as well regarded in the industry. Cleanroom construction remains the foundation, while energy-saving engineering is being built out as a second pillar.

The company set up a future-business team to grow its ESCO operations and is focusing on the wastewater heat recovery market. Its flagship Heat Saver unit is an energy-saving solution based on wastewater heat recovery, operated alongside heat pump and boiler linkage systems and smart farm heat exchangers.

A smaller wastewater heat recovery unit was launched in September 2025. Abroad, the company holds the Heat Saver trademark and has filed overseas applications in Europe, China, Japan and the United States.

By scale, its headcount counted under the National Pension Service stood at 18, which places it among small specialist firms relative to large construction and equipment groups.

03

Recent trends

Revenue reached KRW 13.65 billion in 2024, up 35% year on year, according to the company's preliminary figures. In 2025, growth reversed: on a standalone basis, revenue was KRW 10,463 million, operating profit KRW 117 million, and net income KRW 84 million. Revenue declined 23% from 2024 to 2025.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 601 million in 2023 and an operating loss of KRW 437 million in 2024 to operating profit of KRW 117 million in 2025. Profitability ratios were a net margin of 0.80% and an operating margin of 1.12%.

The balance sheet carried total liabilities of KRW 6,239,246 thousand, an equity ratio of 18.11%, and a borrowing dependence ratio of 52.11%. Cash flows were negative, with operating cash flow at KRW -924,676 thousand and investing cash flow at KRW -2,134,535 thousand.

The share price moved from 2,490 won on February 23, 2026 to 1,884 won on March 31 and 1,650 won on April 24, before trading near 1,975 won in May. On October 12, 2026, the stock closed at 2,120 won, up 9.56% from the prior session, and trading was suspended from 16:00 that day. Ownership is concentrated, with the largest shareholder, CEO Seo Chung-ok, holding 80.2%.

04

Outlook

The nearest variable is when the trading suspension ends. Resumption depends on signing a new sponsor-adviser contract, and the suspension stemmed from the termination of the previous one, which bears directly on the company's listing status.

The company has set a target of moving to KOSDAQ in 2027, and in 2026 said it would prepare in earnest for the KOSDAQ transfer. For overseas sales, the key question is whether the MOU converts into export contracts, and the agreement also includes discussions on investment attraction.

On products, the company argues that the smaller wastewater heat recovery unit can widen installation as high-rise apartments spread. On finances, the operating margin near 1% and negative operating cash flow remain the main metrics to track.

The next checkpoints are a new sponsor-adviser appointment disclosure, the 2026 annual results, and follow-on contracts under the overseas MOU.

05

Bull factors

2025 profit turn and energy-saving expansion

Operating profit turned positive in 2025, reaching KRW 117.48 million. The company is building energy-saving engineering into a second business line. Press coverage describes the expansion into this field as ongoing growth.

Heat Saver overseas beachhead

The company signed an MOU with a Dubai trading and finance firm to pursue overseas sales of Heat Saver. Middle Eastern markets and India were named as target countries. The company has also secured the trademark and filed overseas applications.

Smaller units widen the customer base

A smaller wastewater heat recovery unit was launched in September 2025. It is positioned for hotels, hospitals and apartment buildings with capacity around 150 tons per day. The company is shifting its sales focus toward mid-sized customers, and 2024 preliminary revenue grew 35%.

06

Bear factors

Revenue decline and thin margins

Revenue fell 23% in 2025. The operating margin was 1.12% and the net margin 0.80%. With net income of KRW 84 million, the earnings base is small and sensitive to swings in project volume.

Cash outflow and debt burden

Operating cash flow was negative at KRW -924,676 thousand, and investing cash flow was KRW -2,134,535 thousand. Total liabilities stood at KRW 6,239,246 thousand against an equity ratio of 18.11%. The borrowing dependence ratio of 52.11% is elevated.

Trading suspension and adviser vacancy

Trading in the common shares was suspended from 16:00 on October 12, 2026. The recorded reason was the termination of the sponsor-adviser contract. The suspension ends upon signing a new adviser contract, and trading value has been zero during the halt.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Cleanroom orders depend on capital spending by manufacturers in semiconductors, pharmaceuticals and food, which are sensitive to interest rates and the business cycle. A pullback in capex would affect both construction volume and order timing. Energy price moves also shape demand for savings equipment such as wastewater heat recovery units.

Regulatory and listing

The suspension rests on KONEX listing rules, Article 27 and the enforcement rule Article 23. The company's KOSDAQ transfer target is 2027, and any delay in adviser arrangements could push back the broader listing plan.

Business and governance

With the largest shareholder holding 80.2%, the free float is limited. If the overseas MOU does not translate into export contracts and revenue, the growth narrative lacks support. Continued operating cash outflows keep pressure on the company's internal funding capacity.

08

Overall view

TL Engineering has signs of improvement, including its 2025 return to operating profit, growth in energy-saving businesses, and an overseas MOU. Against that, revenue fell 23% in 2025, the operating margin stayed near 1%, and leverage remains elevated. Both operating and investing cash flows were negative.

Trading has been suspended since October 12, 2026, so price action cannot currently be used to read business developments, and resumption hinges on signing a new sponsor-adviser contract.

The bullish case centers on Heat Saver's overseas push and the smaller product line, while the bearish case centers on thin earnings, the balance sheet, and the timing of resumption. This summary covers business metrics and disclosed facts only and does not express a view on the share price.
