Trading Halt Meets a Business Pivot

02

Business structure

TL Engineering was founded in Gwangmyeong in 2002 and has designed and built cleanrooms directly for sectors including materials, parts and equipment, pharma and bio, food and cosmetics, and film and batteries.

The company describes itself as a pure cleanroom engineering firm founded by core members who left the Korean branch of Germany's M+W Zander. Its website lists cleanroom design and construction, fire protection and explosion-proofing, cold storage, energy saving (ESCO) and a storefront cleanroom service.

Since 2023 it has been building energy-saving engineering as a new pillar on top of its cleanroom know-how. The flagship product is Heat Saver, a system that extracts thermal energy from wastewater and feeds it back into the process.

In its ESCO business with KT, the company makes and sells this product itself, funding the installation up front and recovering the cost over roughly three years from the energy savings.

At the March 31, 2026 shareholders' meeting, the ESCO business, modular data center (PMDC) manufacturing and installation, and smart-farm system construction were added to the business purposes. Segment sales shares and competitor market shares could not be confirmed through research, so no figures are given. Headcount is 18 based on National Pension data, which marks it as a small organization.

03

Recent trends

For fiscal 2025 (the 24th term), standalone sales were KRW 10,463 million, operating profit KRW 117 million and net income KRW 84 million, and because net income was positive, earnings per share were also positive. Sales fell 23.38% from the prior year, when they were roughly KRW 13.65 billion.

The company posted an operating loss in 2024 and returned to an operating profit in 2025. The 2025 operating margin was a thin 1.12%. At the end of 2025, total assets were KRW 7,619 million, total liabilities KRW 6,239 million and total equity KRW 1,379 million.

The same data show a debt ratio of 452.19% and negative cash flows from both operating and investing activities. The shares closed at KRW 1,884 on March 31, 2026 and KRW 1,650 on April 24, 2026.

On October 12, 2026 the stock rose 9.56% to KRW 2,120, and trading was suspended from 16:00 the same day because the designated advisor contract was terminated. As of 17:02 on October 27, 2026, the quoted price is KRW 2,120 with zero trading value. This matches a market in which no trades are executing because of the suspension.

04

Outlook

The suspension is disclosed as lasting until a new designated advisor contract is signed, so follow-up disclosures are the most important item to watch. In an April 2025 press release, the company set a goal of transferring to KOSDAQ in 2027.

A transfer listing requires close work with an advisor and a listing underwriter, and disclosures so far do not show how this termination affects that timetable. At the HARFKO trade show in October 2026, the company showed its expansion into energy efficiency and data center cooling.

Heat Saver has a registered domestic patent and completed KOLAS performance testing, and green technology certification is being pursued. The small-capacity unit was developed for apartments and mid-sized hotels and hospitals.

Whether this broader customer base turns into revenue can be checked in the order and sales trend of future quarterly and annual filings. The operating margin remains around 1%, so there is room for improvement in that business metric, though it depends on recovering sales scale and controlling costs.

05

Bull factors

Technology credentials in energy saving

The company obtained a patent for its wastewater heat recovery device in March 2026 and said on April 14 that it had been selected for the energy innovation category of the 31st Jang Yeong-sil International Science and Culture Award. It has also completed trademark registrations in Europe and Japan.

These credentials can support product differentiation. The actual sales contribution still needs separate confirmation.

Return to operating profit

After an operating loss in 2024, the company swung to an operating profit of KRW 117 million in 2025. Better profit in a year of lower sales may reflect cost-structure adjustments. The amount is small, so further results are needed to judge durability.

New-business expansion and outreach

A March 2026 charter amendment added modular data centers and smart farms to the business purposes. The company also signed a memorandum of understanding with SAS Trading Company to promote overseas expansion. It also won the Best IR Award at the 13th Korea Konex Awards. These can be read as efforts to widen the business scope and investor communication.

06

Bear factors

Lower sales and thin profitability

Sales in 2025 fell 23.38% from the prior year. The operating margin was only 1.12%. A small operating profit like this can shift easily with sales swings or one-off costs.

High debt and weak cash flow

The debt ratio is 452.19% and the equity ratio is 18.11%. Cash flows from both operating and investing activities are negative. The ESCO model of investing first and recovering later front-loads funding needs, so balance with the capital structure is a key variable.

Trading halt and liquidity constraints

Trading has been suspended since October 12, 2026 because the designated advisor contract was terminated, and the suspension lasts until a new appointment contract is signed. Voting shares outstanding total only 1,351,515. The largest shareholder group's stake is reported at 80.2%, which limits the tradable float. Investors cannot trade their holdings during the suspension.

07

Risk factors

Regulatory and disclosure

Under the Konex listing rules, trading stops when a designated advisor contract ends until a new appointment contract is signed. No timetable for the appointment has been disclosed, and advisor changes have recurred.

The company disclosed on April 16, 2025 that it had changed its designated advisor to IBK Investment & Securities. The disclosure list on the company website also shows an advisor appointment contract change dated April 10, 2026.

Business and orders

Cleanroom work is an order-driven business in which sales swing with customers' capital-spending schedules. Sales and operating profit moved sharply from year to year between 2022 and 2025, which illustrates this.

The sales share of newer lines such as wastewater heat recovery units and data center-related work has not been confirmed. The pace of expansion may vary with competition from larger players and results from design-stage sales at new building sites.

Financial and liquidity

At the end of 2025, the debt ratio was 452.19% and borrowing dependence was 52.11%. Interest coverage is recorded as below one time. Growth in up-front-funded ESCO projects could raise working-capital and borrowing needs. A small market capitalization and the ongoing suspension may also constrain financing conditions.

08

Overall view

TL Engineering is a small Konex company reshaping its business by layering wastewater heat recovery and data center-related work onto a cleanroom engineering base. On a 2025 standalone basis, sales declined but the company returned to an operating profit of KRW 117 million.

The low operating margin, high debt ratio and negative operating cash flow leave limited financial cushion. Technical and promotional achievements such as the patent, awards and trade show appearances are accumulating, but it will take time for these to show up in results.

Trading has been suspended since the designated advisor contract was terminated on October 12, 2026, so the disclosure of a new advisor and the resumption date are the main items to check. This report is for information only, and investment decisions rest with the reader.
