WOT: Profit recovery backed by Samsung and SK hynix orders

02

Business structure

WOT makes environmental control equipment used in semiconductor and display manufacturing and was listed on KOSDAQ in 2023. Its main products are the ultra-precision temperature and humidity control system (THC), the fan filter unit (FFU) and the ultra-precision temperature control unit (TCU).

In the first quarter of 2026, THC accounted for 56.2% of sales and FFU for 33.2%, according to a research report published in June 2026. Its main customers are Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, where it is registered as a first-tier supplier, and it also supplies SEMES.

On June 28, 2026 it disclosed a THC supply contract with Samsung Electronics worth KRW 832 million, and on October 21 a KRW 860 million contract with SK hynix. Eight supply contracts disclosed between January 1 and August 19, 2026 total about KRW 12.5 billion.

In competitive terms, other companies' environmental control units have mostly been used on Tokyo Electron (TEL) tools, but the company says it has completed most THC evaluations for TEL track equipment, which gives it a base to widen application.

Samsung Electronics may also qualify multiple suppliers for supply stability. The company is also pursuing exports to Taiwan and Southeast Asia.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results started from sales of KRW 22.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 6.71 billion in 2022. Sales then fell to KRW 12.8 billion in 2023, a 43.7% decline from the prior year, and operating profit fell to KRW 1.47 billion, down 78.1%, making 2023 the smallest year of the period.

Sales recovered to KRW 15.2 billion in 2024, up 18.2%, and operating profit rose to KRW 2.03 billion, up 38.1%. In 2025 sales were KRW 15.2 billion, up 0.2%, and operating profit was KRW 2.48 billion, up 22.3%.

The operating margin fell from 29.4% in 2022 to 11.4% in 2023 and then rose to 13.4% in 2024 and 16.3% in 2025. In a January 2025 report, a Kiwoom Securities analyst attributed weak results to lower semiconductor investment and said one-off costs in the first half weighed on profitability.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 5.87 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.21 billion in 2023, a 62.4% decline, then rose to KRW 3.39 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.57 billion in 2025, and net profit for the same years was reported at the same levels.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 66 billion and total liabilities were KRW 2 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 1.12 billion in 2022, KRW 1.96 billion in 2023, KRW 5.86 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.93 billion in 2025, with wide swings between years.

Quarterly sales rose from KRW 2.42 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 5.95 billion in the fourth quarter, with operating profit moving from KRW 226 million to KRW 1.26 billion.

Sales then fell to KRW 4.33 billion in the first quarter of 2026, with operating profit of KRW 955 million, before rising to KRW 7.71 billion and KRW 2.08 billion in the second quarter and KRW 8.64 billion and KRW 2.6 billion in the third quarter.

Third-quarter 2026 sales rose 257.9% year on year and operating profit rose 1,049.4%. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.3 billion, up 343.8%, and the company said orders received from the second half of 2025 began converting into sales in the second quarter of 2026.

05

Industry analysis

The company's CEO expects capital investment by Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and Taiwanese chipmakers to continue for about three years.

According to a company profile summary dated September 2026, WOT supplied THC for HBM process track equipment and completed performance verification, and orders and deliveries for Samsung's Pyeongtaek P4 fab lifted results.

TCU orders are rising for HBM debonding processes and display inkjet equipment, and new fab construction is cited as an additional order opportunity. In terms of position, its first-tier supplier status with major domestic chipmakers and room for wider adoption on TEL equipment are strengths.

Still, if several suppliers are qualified on the same tools, its share could be split. There was also a liquid-cooling theme: after CES in January 2025, WOT was classified as a related theme stock and hit the upper limit in after-hours trading.

At the time, press reports noted that the company did not itself hold or commercialize immersion cooling technology. Interest also rose when Nvidia said its next-generation AI chips would adopt liquid cooling, given the company's chiller development history. Equipment demand that tracks chip capital spending remains the basic structure of its market.

06

Outlook

A June 2026 securities report projected full-year 2026 sales of KRW 25 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.5 billion, based on expanded THC equipment sales.

Adding the disclosed first-to-third quarter 2026 figures gives cumulative sales of about KRW 20.7 billion and cumulative operating profit of about KRW 5.6 billion, so operating profit has already passed that projection.

The second evaluation of THC at Samsung Electronics is under way with a second unit shipped, and the CEO expects it to finish within the first quarter of 2027. Whether formal supply follows will depend on the evaluation result and the customer's final purchase decision.

SK hynix has completed THC evaluation, including for EUV-related track equipment, and supply is proceeding. New fab construction and entry by a Taiwanese foundry are cited as factors for customer diversification.

On capacity, the company is reviewing use of spare land at its existing site, but no concrete expansion plan has been confirmed. Reports say KRW 25 billion in IPO proceeds, earmarked for a second plant and an R&D center, remain unspent, and the second plant investment is reported as undecided. The pace of supply expansion will depend on decisions about adding capacity.

Bull factors

Wider supply to Samsung and SK hynix

THC supply contracts worth KRW 832 million with Samsung Electronics in June 2026 and KRW 860 million with SK hynix in October 2026 were disclosed voluntarily. Eight contracts disclosed between January 1 and August 19, 2026 total about KRW 12.5 billion.

The company says most THC evaluations for TEL track equipment are complete, creating a base to widen application.

Quarterly recovery trend

Sales rose for two straight quarters, reaching KRW 7.71 billion in the second quarter and KRW 8.64 billion in the third quarter of 2026, with operating profit of KRW 2.08 billion and KRW 2.6 billion. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 6.88 billion.

Light balance sheet

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 2 billion against total equity of KRW 66 billion, a debt-to-equity ratio of 3.0%. Operating cash flow has been positive every year since 2022, reaching KRW 5.86 billion in 2024. Over the same period, the operating margin rose from 13.4% in 2024 to 16.3% in 2025.

09

Bear factors

Customer concentration and reliance on capex

Major customers are concentrated in a few domestic chipmakers, including Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. Sales fell 43.7% to KRW 12.8 billion in 2023 during a downturn in capital spending, showing how quickly results can reverse. A gap in downstream investment is cited as a potential risk.

Volatility in quarterly results

Sales fell 27.2% from the prior quarter to KRW 4.33 billion in the first quarter of 2026, and operating profit fell 24.0% to KRW 955 million. Operating profit was only KRW 226 million in the third quarter of 2025. Because quarterly swings are large, a trend cannot be judged from one or two quarters.

Uncertainty over expansion and fund use

Reports say KRW 25 billion in IPO proceeds has not been spent and the second plant investment is undecided. Use of spare land at the existing site is under review, but no concrete expansion plan has been confirmed.

With the timing of added capacity unclear, how much of the increase in orders can be absorbed still needs confirmation.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration

Sales depend on a small number of customers, so the timing of one customer's orders can drive quarterly results. Samsung Electronics has mentioned cross-applying products from multiple suppliers, which could split share.

The Samsung THC supply contract had an end date of October 31, 2026, so whether follow-on contracts are signed is a point to confirm.

Semiconductor capex cycle

Demand for THC and FFU tracks the scale of semiconductor capital spending, as the sharp sales drop in 2023 showed. The CEO expects investment to continue for about three years, but that is the company's own view, and the possibility of a gap in investment remains.

Qualification and investment timing

Samsung's second-stage evaluation is expected to conclude within the first quarter of 2027. Formal supply depends on that result and the customer's final purchase decision. Because the second plant and R&D investments remain undecided, the speed of supply expansion is also uncertain.

12

Overall view

WOT's annual operating profit has risen for two straight years since its 2023 low, and in 2026 sales and operating profit grew for two consecutive quarters.

The recovery centers on THC supply to Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, and third-quarter 2026 sales and operating profit are the highest among the disclosed quarters. Operating cash flow has been positive every year since 2022 and the debt-to-equity ratio was 3.0%, which are financial strengths.

On the other hand, customers are concentrated in a few chipmakers, and sales fell 43.7% in 2023, so reliance on the capital spending cycle is high. Quarterly results also swing widely, so trends need to be read across several quarters.

The price-to-book ratio above 1x should be read alongside the earnings recovery path. The next checkpoints are fourth-quarter 2026 results, the 2026 annual report and the result of Samsung's second THC evaluation.
