WOT: Earnings Recovery After 2023 Trough

02

Business structure

Founded in 2004, WOT is a KOSDAQ-listed developer and manufacturer of environmental control systems used in semiconductor and display manufacturing processes.

Its flagship product is the precision temperature-and-humidity control unit (THC), which is mounted on track equipment used in the photo process to precisely regulate temperature, humidity, and trace gases during photoresist coating and drying.

As of the first quarter of 2026, THC accounted for 56.2% of revenue and fan filter units (FFU) for 33.2%, with the remainder coming from thermo-control units (TCU) and clean modules (CM).

The company achieved localization of THC technology that had previously been dominated by Japanese suppliers, and holds the No.1 share of the domestic semiconductor environmental-control market.

Its core customers are Samsung Electronics, registered as a tier-1 supplier in 2020, and SK hynix, registered in 2018, and it also supplies semiconductor equipment makers such as SEMES, ZEUS, and ATI.

The THC market is an oligopoly held by a small number of players including WOT, with few competitors able to supply integrated humidity control and chillers together, which functions as a barrier to entry.

The company is expanding TCU applications into HBM de-bonding processes and display inkjet equipment, while also pursuing localization of EUV lithography chillers that currently rely heavily on imports.

To broaden its overseas revenue base, it is conducting THC performance evaluations with local partners in Taiwan and Southeast Asia.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell 43.7% from KRW 22.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.8 billion in 2023, then rose for two consecutive years to KRW 15.2 billion in 2024 (+18.2%) and KRW 15.2 billion in 2025 (+0.2%).

Operating profit dropped 78.1% from KRW 6.71 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.47 billion in 2023, before recovering for two straight years to KRW 2.03 billion in 2024 (+38.1%) and KRW 2.48 billion in 2025 (+22.3%).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders followed the same pattern, falling 62.4% from KRW 5.87 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.21 billion in 2023, then rising for two consecutive years to KRW 3.39 billion in 2024 (+53.5%) and KRW 3.57 billion in 2025 (+5.2%).

Operating margin declined from 29.4% in 2022 to 11.4% in 2023 before gradually recovering to 13.4% in 2024 and 16.3% in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was disclosed at the same amount as controlling-shareholder net income in every year, indicating a consolidated structure with no minority interest.

On a quarterly basis, the company posted a KRW 26.52 million operating loss on KRW 2.26 billion in revenue in the second quarter of 2025, then returned to profit in the third quarter with KRW 2.42 billion in revenue and KRW 226 million in operating profit, before revenue jumped 146.4% quarter-on-quarter to KRW 5.95 billion in the fourth quarter, lifting operating profit 455.6% to KRW 1.26 billion.

The first quarter of 2026 softened to KRW 4.33 billion in revenue (-27.2% quarter-on-quarter) and KRW 955 million in operating profit (-24.0%), before a sharp rebound in the second quarter to KRW 7.71 billion in revenue (+77.9% quarter-on-quarter, +241.1% year-on-year) and KRW 2.08 billion in operating profit (+117.4% quarter-on-quarter, a swing to profit year-on-year).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for the trailing four quarters (Q3 2025 through Q2 2026) totaled KRW 5.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

As semiconductor processes become finer and more densely integrated, environmental control that affects yield has grown more important, which translates into rising demand for THC and FFU equipment.

Kiwoom Securities stated that it estimates the global THC equipment market will grow from KRW 153.5 billion in 2023 to KRW 226.6 billion in 2028, a compound annual growth rate of 6.7%.

Analysts note that HBM production processes require additional track equipment, which is expected to lift demand for THC and FFU together.

The upstream memory semiconductor market showed renewed customer capital spending from the second half of 2025, which has been cited as a backdrop for WOT's quarterly earnings rebound.

However, the absolute size of the THC market remains small compared with large equipment categories such as lithography and etching, leaving the company's results relatively sensitive to swings in upstream investment cycles.

In terms of competitive structure, the THC segment is an oligopoly held by a small number of players including WOT, and most domestic listed peers in the same industry reportedly supply only temperature-control chillers rather than integrated humidity control.

At the customer level, the capital expenditure plans of Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, particularly the pace of HBM and EUV-related investment, have a direct bearing on the company's results.

06

Outlook

On August 7, 2026, the company disclosed first-half 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 12 billion and operating profit of KRW 3 billion, stating that it expects to sustain growth given supply volumes already secured for the second half.

In the same release, a company official said earnings growth should continue in line with customers' investment plans.

According to a company comment provided by corporate data provider WiseReport dated September 7, 2026, WOT completed performance verification after supplying THC units for HBM-related track equipment, and orders and deliveries tied to the Pyeongtaek P4 fab contributed to the earnings improvement.

The same comment noted that TCU orders are increasing for HBM de-bonding processes and display inkjet equipment, and that additional order opportunities are expected as new fab construction proceeds.

Based on contract disclosures, WOT signed a THC supply contract worth KRW 948 million with Samsung Electronics for its Pyeongtaek site running from November 30, 2026 to June 30, 2027, and a THC supply contract worth KRW 859.46 million with SK hynix for its Icheon headquarters running from October 20, 2026 to February 28, 2027.

On the overseas front, the company said on August 7, 2026 that it has been conducting THC performance evaluations with a local partner in Taiwan, having completed customer-required performance testing and moved into identity verification procedures ahead of mass production.

On the sell side, Kiwoom Securities stated in a June 23, 2026 report that it projects full-year 2026 revenue of KRW 25.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 6 billion, while KB Securities stated in a June 11, 2026 report that it estimates 2026 operating profit at KRW 5.5 billion, up 121.5% year-on-year.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly Earnings Rebound Gaining Traction

Second-quarter 2026 revenue rose 77.9% quarter-on-quarter and 241.1% year-on-year to KRW 7.71 billion, while operating profit of KRW 2.08 billion marked a swing to profit from a year-earlier loss.

Trailing four-quarter controlling-shareholder net income also climbed to KRW 5.1 billion, a notable recovery in earnings capacity. The company said it has already secured second-half supply volumes, leaving room for the improving trend to continue.

Expanding Reach into HBM and EUV

THC and TCU are expanding into both front-end and back-end HBM production steps as well as EUV lithography applications. If new fab investment announcements continue, rising demand for track equipment could translate into additional THC and FFU orders.

Orders and deliveries tied to the Pyeongtaek P4 fab have already contributed to the recent earnings improvement, according to company commentary.

Pursuing Overseas Customer Diversification

WOT is conducting THC performance evaluations with local partners in Taiwan and Southeast Asia, having completed customer-required testing and entered identity verification ahead of mass production. Securing overseas customers could help reduce reliance on its two large domestic customers.

Its position as the No.1 domestic THC supplier by market share provides a technical foundation for entering new markets.

09

Bear factors

Revenue Concentrated in Two Customers

Individual supply contracts with Samsung Electronics and SK hynix have repeatedly represented a sizable share of disclosed revenue. A slowdown in either customer's capital spending would directly affect results. KB Securities has pointed to a potential pause in upstream industry investment as a risk factor.

Profit Still Below 2022 Levels

2025 operating profit of KRW 2.48 billion remains well below the KRW 6.71 billion recorded in 2022. Given the sharp earnings decline experienced in 2023, a similar contraction cannot be ruled out if industry conditions turn down again. Because absolute revenue scale is small, fixed-cost leverage can work in both directions.

Highly Variable Quarter-to-Quarter Pattern

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue and operating profit fell 27.2% and 24.0%, respectively, from the prior quarter, temporarily interrupting the improving trend.

Because results depend heavily on short-term delivery schedules, analysts have noted that quarter-to-quarter swings are large enough that one or two quarters alone are not sufficient to confirm a trend. The company also recorded an operating loss as recently as the second quarter of 2025.

10

Risk factors

Customer Concentration Risk

A large portion of revenue stems from individual supply contracts with just two customers, Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. Any adjustment to either customer's investment schedule could meaningfully increase revenue volatility.

Contracts are also signed on a per-fab or per-process basis, so whether follow-on contracts materialize after a contract ends has a direct bearing on results.

Upstream Capex Cycle Risk

The company experienced a sharp decline in revenue and operating profit during the 2023 downturn in upstream semiconductor investment. The semiconductor industry is cyclical, moving with memory pricing and global demand, so a similar downturn cannot be ruled out. The relatively small absolute size of the THC market itself can also amplify the impact of such cycles.

Cash-Flow Risk Tied to Payment Terms

Some supply contracts include settlement via 60-day promissory notes after equipment delivery, so the timing of cash collection can diverge from revenue recognition. As order volumes rise, receivables and note balances tend to grow in tandem, potentially increasing working-capital burden.

It is also worth noting that 2025 operating cash flow of KRW 1.93 billion was lower than the KRW 5.86 billion recorded in 2024.

12

Overall view

WOT passed its earnings trough in 2023 and posted two consecutive years of revenue and operating profit growth through 2025, before a brief first-quarter 2026 dip gave way to a sharp sequential and year-on-year improvement in the second quarter.

Its core THC product maintains the No.1 domestic share in semiconductor environmental control and is expanding into HBM and EUV applications, with supply contracts with Samsung Electronics and SK hynix continuing into the second half of 2026.

At the same time, a large share of revenue remains concentrated with these two major customers, and the company's structure leaves it exposed to the kind of rapid contraction seen in 2023 if upstream investment pulls back again.

A low debt ratio and a history of consistent profitability provide a stable financial base, though 2025 operating profit still falls short of 2022 levels.

Ongoing efforts to secure overseas customers in Taiwan and Southeast Asia, along with any new fab investment announcements, stand out as key variables for the next phase of earnings direction.

The next checkpoint is the third-quarter 2026 quarterly report, which will show whether the second-quarter improvement carried forward.
