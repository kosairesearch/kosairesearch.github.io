Report overview

Losses persist as T Scientific shifts toward AI security

T Scientific returned to operating profit in 2025, but it recorded operating losses in each quarter from Q1 to Q3 2026 while it shifts its focus toward AI security.

  1. 1

    Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 264 million, turning positive, but the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.28 billion.

  2. 2

    Quarterly revenue in Q1-Q3 2026 stayed between KRW 6.17 billion and KRW 7.02 billion, short of the KRW 12.8 billion of Q4 2025. Whether Q4 revenue moves out of this range is the next point to check.

  3. 3

    The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 2.86 billion, a swing into loss from the prior-year quarter.

  4. 4

    A 5-to-1 reverse stock split in April 2026 and a third-party share issuance raising about KRW 3 billion in June 2026 changed the capital structure and share count.

  5. 5

    On the AI security side, a paper accepted to the ACL 2026 main conference and the start of SCOPE, an SBOM-based supply chain security system, are the confirmed milestones.

02

Business structure

T Scientific was founded in 1998 with a purpose centered on mobile platforms and content, and it listed on KOSDAQ in 2002. It entered information security and protection through a 2023 merger with iTnomads and broadened related capabilities through a 2024 absorption merger with a netcruz company.

In July 2024 it brought Happy Technology into its group as a subsidiary. The business is reported in two segments, information security and protection, and mobile coupons. In March 2025 it registered as an electronic financial business for issuing and managing prepaid electronic payment instruments.

In the security segment, the company says expanding public-sector sales and winning AI-based personal information protection research projects have upgraded its services.

Key products include the AI-based unstructured personal information detection solution P3R-Privacy Finder AI and the integrated AI operations platform HawkEye. In 2026 it began building SCOPE, an SBOM-based supply chain security system, under a support program run by the Ministry of Science and ICT and KISA.

R&D spending in 2025 was about KRW 2.05 billion, equal to 5.64 percent of revenue, according to an aggregated corporate data service.

Among domestic security companies, Ahnlab is a common comparison point in security solutions, but T Scientific combines mobile coupons with security, so its earnings structure differs from pure-play security firms.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 8.69 billion in 2022 to KRW 26.8 billion in 2023, a 207.9% increase year on year, then to KRW 43 billion in 2024, up 60.7%, which was the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Revenue fell 15.5% year on year to KRW 36.4 billion in 2025.

Operating losses were KRW 2.49 billion in 2022, KRW 5.62 billion in 2023 and KRW 9.85 billion in 2024, with losses widening in 2023 and 2024. The company then posted operating profit of KRW 264 million in 2025, a turn to profit.

The operating margin moved from -28.6 percent in 2022, -21.0 percent in 2023 and -22.9 percent in 2024 to 0.7 percent in 2025. The company attributed the 2025 profit turn to a higher share of its own products, which carry lower cost ratios, even as revenue declined.

It also said the 2025 revenue decline reflected a smaller coupon transaction volume and lower government agency project budgets. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.1 billion in 2022 and KRW 45.4 billion in 2023, with losses widening in 2023.

In 2024 the company posted a shareholders' net profit of KRW 2.22 billion alongside an operating loss of KRW 9.85 billion in the same year. In 2025 it swung to a shareholders' net loss of KRW 1.28 billion, and the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.6 billion.

The company said an impairment review of investment assets raised non-operating costs and drove the net loss. On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 8.47 billion with an operating loss of KRW 51.11 million.

Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 12.8 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.16 billion, a swing from the prior quarter. Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 6.17 billion, down 51.8 percent from the prior quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 1.05 billion.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 7.02 billion with an operating loss of KRW 536 million, a narrower loss. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 6.9 billion, down 18.5 percent year on year, with an operating loss of KRW 580 million, a wider loss than in Q2.

An aggregated corporate data service attributed the Q1 2026 weakness to lower revenue and higher cost burdens in information security despite steady demand, and to compliance costs in the mobile coupon segment.

Over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 4.09 billion. The debt ratio was 45.4 percent in 2022, 33.3 percent in 2023, 17.3 percent in 2024 and 19.2 percent in 2025.

Operating cash flow was negative in 2022 through 2024, at -KRW 2.26 billion, -KRW 11.1 billion and -KRW 13.2 billion, and turned to KRW 721 million in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for information security is shifting as generative AI and large language models expand, raising the need for personal data protection and re-identification risk management.

In May 2026, the company announced that a paper from its AI lab was accepted to the main conference of ACL 2026, a leading natural language processing venue. The paper found that even when more than 90 percent of text is de-identified, the protection rate for the data subjects is only about 33 percent.

A 2024 press interview cited an outlook in which the information security market grows about 10 percent a year. In supply chain security, the spread of cloud and AI has increased use of open-source and external libraries, and the company framed this as the reason for starting SCOPE.

In quantum security, a report said the company launched an alliance with Future System to pursue the post-quantum cryptography market together. In digital assets, it signed a strategic memorandum of understanding with the Solana Foundation to expand its Web3 ecosystem.

On the cycle, demand for information protection continues, but cost burdens and compliance spending are weighing on profitability.

The 2025 revenue drivers the company cited, a smaller coupon transaction volume and lower government agency budgets, point to a changing demand base as the business shifts toward AI security.

Among listed peers, Ahnlab and Daou Technology serve as comparison points in security and software, but the mobile coupon revenue makes a simple comparison difficult.

06

Outlook

In a 2024 press interview, the company said it aimed to become an AI-based IT security specialist and to challenge KRW 100 billion in revenue. Revenue in 2025 was KRW 36.4 billion, a wide gap from that target.

Based on the ACL 2026 paper, the company plans to integrate the SPIA framework and re-identification metrics into P3R-Privacy Finder AI in stages. In the second half of 2026 it plans to release a multimodal version that extends its Korean-focused LLM-based detection to images, audio and video.

SCOPE is to be validated on the company's own systems by the end of 2026, after which the company plans to expand supply chain security services to public institutions, financial firms and general software developers.

A report said the launch of the integrated AI operations platform HawkEye marks the start of a push into enterprise AI operations security.

On the financial side, the 5-to-1 reverse split in April 2026 was aimed at covering accumulated deficits and improving the capital structure, and a third-party share issuance in June 2026 raised about KRW 3 billion for operating funds.

Strengths include the AI security research results and public and financial sector expansion plans, the 2025 operating profit after the turn from losses, and the lower debt ratio.

Weaknesses include the gap between the revenue target and actual results, operating losses from Q1 through Q3 2026, and the wider shareholders' net loss in Q3 2026. The 2026 annual business report will confirm full-year 2026 revenue and operating margin. The next checkpoint is whether SCOPE and P3R commercialization shows up in the revenue mix.

08

Bull factors

Operating profit turn in 2025 and cost structure

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 264 million, a turn from the KRW 9.85 billion operating loss in 2024, with an operating margin of 0.7 percent. The company cited a higher share of its own products with lower cost ratios as the improvement driver. Q4 2025 operating profit of KRW 1.16 billion also showed a quarterly profit.

Lower debt ratio and equity base

The debt ratio fell from 45.4 percent in 2022 to 19.2 percent in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 168.9 billion and total liabilities were KRW 32.4 billion. Operating cash flow was negative from 2022 through 2024 but turned to KRW 721 million in 2025.

AI security products and research results

A paper from the AI lab was accepted to the ACL 2026 main conference, and the company plans to integrate the SPIA framework into P3R-Privacy Finder AI on that basis. A multimodal version is slated for the second half of 2026.

If the SCOPE supply chain security business expands into public and financial sectors, the business base could widen.

09

Bear factors

Operating losses in three straight quarters

Operating losses were KRW 1.05 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 536 million in Q2 and KRW 580 million in Q3, a loss in each of the three quarters. As operating losses continued, the net loss burden also accumulated, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 4.09 billion.

Revenue decline and dependence on specific drivers

Revenue in 2025 was KRW 36.4 billion, down 15.5 percent year on year, and the company attributed the decline to a smaller coupon transaction volume and lower government agency project budgets. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 6.9 billion fell 18.5 percent from the prior-year quarter. An aggregated corporate data service read compliance costs in the mobile coupon segment as a burden.

Capital raising and changes in share count

The 5-to-1 reverse split in April 2026 reduced the share count, and in June the company raised about KRW 3 billion by issuing about 1.54 million shares to a third party. The lock-up on the new shares runs through June 29, 2027.

A 2024 report described a dispute between the largest shareholder and minority shareholders, which remains a governance factor.

10

Risk factors

Expansion of supply chain security

SCOPE is planned to be validated on the company's own systems by the end of 2026 and then extended to public institutions, financial firms and software development and supply companies.

If validation is delayed or public and financial adoption slows, the point at which the supply chain security business is reflected in revenue could also be pushed back. Construction began under a support program of the Ministry of Science and ICT and KISA, but commercial results have not yet been confirmed.

AI security product launch schedule

The company plans to integrate the SPIA framework and re-identification metrics into P3R-Privacy Finder AI in stages, and to release in the second half of 2026 a multimodal version that extends detection to images, audio and video.

If the release schedule slips or detection performance of the new features falls short of expectations, the point at which AI security products contribute to revenue could also be delayed. The paper accepted to ACL 2026 provides a technical basis, but academic results do not translate directly into product sales.

Execution of new business alliances

The company signed a strategic memorandum of understanding with the Solana Foundation to expand its Web3 ecosystem and launched an alliance with Future System to pursue the post-quantum cryptography market together.

If these alliances do not lead to contracts and revenue, the personnel and costs committed could remain a burden on results. Running several new businesses alongside the shift of the business core toward AI security could also spread management resources thin.

Overall view

T Scientific's recent trend combines an operating profit turn in 2025 with operating losses in each of the three quarters from Q1 to Q3 2026. Operating losses continued from 2022 through 2024, and the 2025 operating profit of KRW 264 million changed the direction.

Quarterly revenue in 2026 was KRW 6.17 billion in Q1, KRW 7.02 billion in Q2 and KRW 6.9 billion in Q3, below the KRW 12.8 billion of Q4 2025. Strengths include the improved cost ratio, the lower debt ratio, the positive operating cash flow in 2025, and the AI security research and product schedule.

Weaknesses include the decline in quarterly revenue, the wider net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026, the KRW 4.09 billion net loss over the last four quarters, and the changes in share count and capital raising.

On valuation, the price-to-earnings ratio cannot be calculated and the share price sits well below net asset value, so the key variable for reading the share price is whether quarterly profit recovery is sustained.

The next checkpoint is the operating margin in the 2026 annual business report, due by the end of March 2027, along with progress in SCOPE and P3R commercialization.
