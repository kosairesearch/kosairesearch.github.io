02

Business structure

Eagon Holdings moved to a holding-company structure in 2017, when its window manufacturing business was split off into a separate subsidiary and the listed entity became a holding company. Its FY2025 annual report lists six business segments: Holding, Window, Timber, Forestry, Energy and Pallet.

Before consolidation adjustments, FY2025 segment sales were KRW 306.8 billion for Timber, KRW 124.9 billion for Window, KRW 34.6 billion for Energy, KRW 27.2 billion for Pallet, KRW 9.1 billion for Forestry and KRW 6.9 billion for Holding, so Timber accounts for roughly 60% of pre-elimination sales.

Window sales consist mainly of aluminum and PVC windows, with domestic sales of KRW 116.6 billion and exports of KRW 8.35 billion in 2025. Timber produces plywood and flooring, and its exports of KRW 155.9 billion exceeded domestic sales of KRW 150.9 billion in 2025.

Forestry sells logs, and log exports fell to KRW 9.1 billion in 2025 from KRW 16.6 billion in 2024. Energy supplies steam, while Pallet makes logistics packaging.

A financial summary on FnGuide describes how the Chilean subsidiary sources logs from the Andes region to cut logistics costs, and how free-trade access supports sales in the United States and Mexico. A 2023 press report said the company is pushing market expansion on the strength of quality and brand recognition. The Holding segment manages the subsidiaries and posted an operating loss of KRW 9.33 billion in 2025.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales moved from KRW 507.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 458.5 billion in 2023 (down 9.7%), recovered to KRW 497.4 billion in 2024 (up 8.5%), and then fell to KRW 447.1 billion in 2025 (down 10.1%), the smallest year of the 2022–2025 period.

Operating profit went from KRW 22.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.23 billion in 2023 (down 85.4%) and KRW 6.56 billion in 2024 (up 102.8%), before swinging to an operating loss of KRW 1.24 billion in 2025. The operating margin declined from 4.4% in 2022 to 0.7% in 2023, 1.3% in 2024 and -0.3% in 2025.

In the 2025 consolidated income statement, gross profit fell 16.1% and SG&A fell 4.1%, so the drop in gross profit appears to be the main background to the operating deficit. Other expenses (down 52.0%) and finance costs (down 34.3%) also fell, which narrowed the pre-tax loss.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 2.09 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 7.83 billion in 2023, a widened loss of KRW 11.9 billion in 2024, and a narrower loss of KRW 4.87 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 11.3 billion in 2025, and the non-controlling share of that loss was KRW 6.45 billion, larger than the controlling share.

Quarterly, operating profit turned positive in Q1 2026 at KRW 3.24 billion after an operating loss of KRW 7.09 billion in Q4 2025, and Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 11.6 billion rose 256.5% from the prior quarter.

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.42 billion fell 61.8% from the prior quarter but turned positive year on year. Controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 7.54 billion.

Over a longer span, sales rose from KRW 359.5 billion and operating profit from KRW 6.3 billion in 2010 to KRW 507.6 billion and KRW 22.1 billion in 2022, and sales have moved up and down since 2023.

05

Industry analysis

In the Timber segment, exports of KRW 145.8 billion were smaller than domestic sales of KRW 182.9 billion in 2024, but in 2025 exports of KRW 155.9 billion exceeded domestic sales of KRW 150.9 billion.

The export growth is linked in the company profile to Chilean log sourcing and to a sales network in free-trade partner countries. Window domestic sales fell to KRW 116.6 billion in 2025 from KRW 121.0 billion in 2024, and window exports dropped to KRW 8.35 billion from KRW 13.5 billion.

Forestry sales fell 45.15% as log sales declined. Energy sales of KRW 34.6 billion fell 10.41%, and Pallet sales fell 17.42% to KRW 27.2 billion. Windows, flooring and plywood are products sensitive to new construction and remodeling demand, so domestic construction conditions affect the volume of domestic sales.

Unlike large holding companies in the same sector such as SK and LG, which own semiconductor, chemical or telecom businesses, Eagon Holdings is centered on building materials, timber and forestry, so industry swings pass into consolidated results more directly.

A 2023 press report said the company is pursuing market expansion on the basis of quality and brand recognition, with a focus on products that combine function and design.

06

Outlook

The company disclosed that at its annual general meeting on March 26, 2026, the financial statements for the 39th fiscal year and the dividend-related agenda were all approved.

Quarterly sales in 2026 were KRW 101.1 billion in Q1, KRW 126.6 billion in Q2 and KRW 108.8 billion in Q3, with operating profit of KRW 3.24 billion, KRW 11.6 billion and KRW 4.42 billion respectively.

Controlling-shareholder net income turned positive at KRW 6.98 billion in Q2 and was KRW 2.56 billion in Q3, down 63.3% from the prior quarter. Q3 sales fell 2.3% year on year, but operating profit turned from a loss in the prior-year quarter to a profit.

Whether this pattern carries into a full-year recovery will be visible in the operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income in the 2026 annual report.

The first checkpoint is whether Window stays profitable, since its operating profit rose to KRW 957 million in 2025 from an operating loss of KRW 8.32 billion in 2024. The second is whether the operating losses in Forestry and Pallet continue into 2026.

The size of the Holding segment's operating loss of KRW 9.33 billion is another item for judging the direction of consolidated results. Timber, with its large export share, may see volume shifts depending on Chilean log sourcing and the mix of sales destinations.

08

Bull factors

Profit Trend in Recent Quarters

Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 11.6 billion rose 256.5% from the prior quarter. Q3 operating profit of KRW 4.42 billion turned from a prior-year loss into a profit. The change in direction from the Q4 2025 operating loss of KRW 7.09 billion is the main positive factor.

Window Segment Returns to Profit

Window segment operating profit moved from a loss of KRW 8.32 billion in 2024 to a profit of KRW 957 million in 2025. Window sales of KRW 124.9 billion fell 7.11% over the same period, yet profit turned positive. Improving earnings on lower sales is a positive sign for the segment.

Balance Sheet Improvement and Timber Export Mix

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 128.7% in 2022 to 104.7% in 2025. Timber exports of KRW 155.9 billion in 2025 exceeded domestic sales, raising the overseas share. The Chilean log sourcing setup is described as a factor that reduces logistics costs.

09

Bear factors

Three Consecutive Years of Controlling-Shareholder Losses

Controlling-shareholder net losses persisted from 2023 through 2025, with a loss of KRW 4.87 billion in 2025. Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 11.3 billion in the same year. The non-controlling share of KRW 6.45 billion means outside shareholders bore a sizable part of the loss.

Holding Costs and Forestry and Pallet Losses

The Holding segment's operating loss of KRW 9.33 billion remains a burden on consolidated operations. Forestry sales fell 45.15% and the segment swung to an operating loss of KRW 7.12 billion. Pallet also posted an operating loss of KRW 989 million as sales fell 17.42%.

Q3 Earnings Pullback and Sales Base

Q3 2026 operating profit fell 61.8% from the prior quarter, and controlling-shareholder net income fell 63.3%. FY2025 sales of KRW 447.1 billion were the smallest of the 2022–2025 period. Without a broader sales base, the durability of quarterly profits faces constraints.

10

Risk factors

FX, Raw Materials and Export Dependence

Timber exports of KRW 155.9 billion in 2025 exceeded domestic sales, raising exposure to overseas demand and exchange-rate movements. Log procurement and logistics costs depend on the Chilean subsidiary and shipping conditions.

Forestry log exports also fell to KRW 9.1 billion, so the effect of overseas volume shifts on consolidated sales should be tracked quarter by quarter.

Domestic Construction and Demand

Window domestic sales fell to KRW 116.6 billion in 2025 from KRW 121.0 billion in 2024. Pallet domestic sales also declined to KRW 26.5 billion from KRW 32.5 billion. Because many segments depend on domestic demand, results are sensitive to changes in domestic construction and remodeling activity.

Financial Burden and Governance

Total liabilities of KRW 375.6 billion exceed total equity of KRW 358.8 billion, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 104.7%. A 2023 press report covered a governance transition in which a second-generation owner family member was appointed as an inside director. Succession and business transfers among affiliates could draw market attention.

Overall view

Eagon Holdings is a holding company with Window, Timber, Forestry, Energy and Pallet segments, and FY2025 closed with an operating loss of KRW 1.24 billion and three consecutive years of controlling-shareholder net losses.

Quarterly operating profit was positive from Q1 to Q3 2026, and controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 7.54 billion. The return of Window to profit in 2025 and the decline of the debt-to-equity ratio to 104.7% are positive factors.

On the other side, the Holding segment's operating loss of KRW 9.33 billion, the Forestry operating loss of KRW 7.12 billion and the non-controlling share of losses at KRW 6.45 billion remain burdens.

The combination of a price-to-book ratio that has moved below 0.5 times and consecutive net losses is what is being discussed. Whether the full year recovers will be visible in the 2026 annual report, and the durability of quarterly results will be visible in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
