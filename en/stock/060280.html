Business structure

Curexo is a medical robotics company whose core business is surgical robots for orthopedic procedures. Its flagship product is the CUVIS-joint robot for knee arthroplasty, and the company disclosed a sales contract for the product on May 20, 2026. The counterparty is Biorad Medisys Pvt.

Ltd., and the contract amount is KRW 8.46 billion, equal to about 11.4% of the KRW 74.5 billion in recent annual sales. The contract period runs from May 19, 2026 to December 31, 2026, roughly seven months.

In its 2025 third-quarter cumulative results, the company said cumulative medical robot sales rose 80% year on year and attributed this to broadening its sales countries, including Japan, Taiwan, Indonesia, Malaysia and Pakistan.

The same release said India, where sales were temporarily weak the prior year, is recovering. A stock information site (AlphaSquare, as shown in late October 2026) tallies sales mix with domestic goods at 54.09%, the largest share, followed by exported products at 33.75% and domestic products at 11.70%.

That classification follows the site's own method and may not map directly onto the segment split in company filings. Export-related items account for a little more than one-third of the total, so results depend on hospital investment capacity and local healthcare systems in each country. Competitively, Curexo contests hospital adoption with leading global orthopedic robot makers.

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 65 billion in 2022 to KRW 72.9 billion in 2023, up 12.1% year on year, then fell to KRW 55.5 billion in 2024, down 23.8%. Sales rebounded to KRW 74.5 billion in 2025, up 34.2%, the largest year in the 2022–2025 span.

Operating profit was small at KRW 1.11 billion in 2022 and KRW 1.14 billion in 2023, up 2.2%, before turning into an operating loss of KRW 5.82 billion in 2024. The operating margin fell to -10.5% in 2024 and recovered to 3.2% in 2025.

In 2025 operating profit of KRW 2.36 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.77 billion both turned positive. Net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 2.77 billion in 2025.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders widened in 2023 and 2024, reaching KRW 8.76 billion in 2024. The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 5.1% in 2025 from 20.5% in 2022, with total liabilities of KRW 4.89 billion and total equity of KRW 96.2 billion. Quarterly results swing sharply.

Sales of KRW 17.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 341 million in the third quarter of 2025 gave way to sales of KRW 20.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.38 billion in the fourth quarter.

The first quarter of 2026 showed sales of KRW 16.1 billion and an operating loss of KRW 913 million, while the second quarter returned to profit with sales of KRW 23.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.44 billion.

The third quarter of 2026 reported sales of KRW 14.7 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.88 billion, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.35 billion was larger than the operating loss, so non-operating items and taxes need to be read alongside it.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 13.1 billion in 2022, KRW 5.08 billion in 2023, KRW 3.85 billion in 2024 and -KRW 11.3 billion in 2025, moving in a direction that diverges from the profit trend.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for orthopedic surgical robots is driven by the growth in degenerative joint disease that comes with an aging population. Robot-assisted surgery is gaining hospital adoption because it improves the precision of bone cutting and implant placement.

Equipment demand, unlike consumables, depends on hospitals' capital spending timing, which tends to make quarterly revenue lumpy. Adoption timing varies with hospital investment capacity, insurance systems and each country's medical infrastructure.

In global markets, leading American players hold large hospital and training networks, so smaller companies often enter through price competitiveness and local distribution partners.

Curexo has pursued this route by widening its overseas sales countries, which according to its 2025 release now include Japan, Taiwan, Indonesia, Malaysia and Pakistan. Domestically, adoption has centered on large general hospitals, and order intake moves irregularly with individual hospital purchasing decisions.

The company describes India as recovering from a temporary slump the prior year, and whether that demand is sustained should be confirmed through coming quarterly results. Within the domestic medical robotics field, Curexo sits among a small group of companies specializing in joint arthroplasty robots.

Overseas demand and country concentration

The company describes India as recovering after a temporary slump the prior year, but whether that continues remains to be seen. The KRW 8.46 billion sales contract disclosed in May 2026 runs through December 31, 2026, so its execution and revenue recognition could span quarter boundaries. A disclosed contract amount does not translate directly into quarterly revenue. Delays in execution could widen quarterly swings. How much of the contract value shows up in reported revenue should be checked in the annual report.
