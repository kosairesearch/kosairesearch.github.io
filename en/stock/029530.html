02

Business structure

Sindoh is an office equipment maker founded in 1960 that manufactures and sells digital color and monochrome multifunction printers, printers, fax machines and consumables.

The company reports a single manufacturing and sales segment, and in its FY2024 business report (separate basis) multifunction-printer-related goods accounted for 73.30% of sales. Sales of its own MFP products made up only 1.89% of that year's total, so most revenue comes from resale.

In 2022, the goods segment accounted for 78.8% of revenue and the product segment, which supplies A/S parts, toner and paper, for 21.2%. Domestic sales were KRW 176.5 billion and export sales KRW 184.7 billion in 2022, a similar scale.

Production was 111,592 units in the 65th fiscal year, down from 165,773 units in the 64th. Core demand comes from corporate and institutional offices, and the company has said it is widening into office solutions by adding AI and other software to its MFP platform.

It was named the top-ranked company in the MFP category of the 2026 Korea Quality Satisfaction Index (KS-QEI) for the 14th time. The company has exited its former 3D printer business.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue peaked at KRW 395.8 billion in 2023 and then declined for two consecutive years, to KRW 341 billion in 2024 (down 13.9%) and KRW 317.3 billion in 2025 (down 6.9%).

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 2.21 billion in 2022 to KRW 25.3 billion in 2023, a swing back to profit, and then fell for two consecutive years to KRW 20.2 billion in 2024 (down 20.4%) and KRW 6.25 billion in 2025 (down 69.0%).

The operating margin rose from -0.6% in 2022 to 6.4% in 2023, then eased to 5.9% in 2024 and 2.0% in 2025. The company's 2022 operating loss was attributed in an August 2023 research report to higher cost ratios and one-off retirement costs.

Attributable net profit rose from KRW 45 billion in 2022 to KRW 55 billion in 2023 (up 22.1%) and KRW 73.2 billion in 2024 (up 33.1%), then fell to KRW 20.1 billion in 2025 (down 72.6%).

Attributable net profit of KRW 73.2 billion in 2024 far exceeded that year's operating profit of KRW 20.2 billion, which suggests non-operating items may have contributed significantly and is a point to check in the footnotes of the business report.

On a quarterly basis, revenue peaked at KRW 86.7 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, then declined in each of the next three quarters: KRW 85.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 1.7% quarter on quarter), KRW 71.3 billion in the second (down 16.3%) and KRW 51.7 billion in the third (down 27.4%).

Operating profit swung to an operating loss of KRW 8.79 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and back to a profit of KRW 2.59 billion in the third. Attributable net results were a loss of KRW 2.16 billion in the second quarter and a loss of KRW 35.3 billion in the third, two consecutive loss-making quarters.

The third quarter of 2026 combined an operating profit with a much larger attributable net loss, making it the widest gap between operating and bottom-line results in the recent period. Operating cash flow peaked at KRW 95.4 billion in 2023, then recorded KRW 51.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 52.1 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Sindoh's core office multifunction printer market is described in press coverage as a business whose demand is shrinking as paper use declines (BusinessPost, reported in early 2025).

An August 2023 research report expected a quantitative decline in the domestic market after the installed base of printers and MFPs grew sharply over the prior two years. In 2022 domestic and export sales were of similar size, so overseas demand and currency moves feed into results.

Production fell to 111,592 units in the 65th fiscal year from 165,773 units in the 64th, showing volume pressure as well. Third-quarter 2026 revenue down 35.6% year on year suggests the trend has continued.

The cycle appears closer to stagnation or contraction than to growth, which is the common reading of both the press coverage and the research report. On product standing, the company's ranking as the top MFP brand in the 2026 KS-QEI survey for the 14th time supports its quality reputation.

Unlike other listed electronics and components companies tied to semiconductor, display or automotive demand cycles, Sindoh's earnings track office spending and paper usage, so a recovery depends on corporate office investment and document volume trends.

Outlook

Sindoh has said it is expanding from MFP hardware into an office solutions business by layering AI and other technologies onto its devices, as reported in October 2026.

Early 2025 press coverage said that after hiring a former investment banker as CEO, there had been no visible results yet from M&A or new-business investment.

With the 3D printer business exited, the company needs a new growth driver, so progress in new businesses and acquisitions is a key variable for its future structure. On shareholder returns, the financial statements for the 66th term (fiscal 2025) were approved in March 2026, and a common cash dividend was resolved.

Shares outstanding fell from 10,080 thousand at the end of 2025 to 9,716 thousand in the first quarter of 2026, and the reason should be checked in the equity change note of the next annual report.

The next periodic report, the 2026 annual business report, is where full-year 2026 revenue and operating margin, along with any non-operating items behind the third-quarter 2026 net loss, will be confirmed.

Fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results are expected in early 2027, and whether revenue declines for a fourth straight quarter will be closely watched. The cause of the second-quarter 2026 operating loss should also be checked against the quarterly report explanations. The recovery of the core business will show up in these three items.

Bull factors

Balance sheet and cash generation

Equity is more than 13 times liabilities, so the financial burden is light. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 95.4 billion in 2023 to KRW 52.1 billion in 2025 but stayed positive every year. Press coverage has also pointed to large retained earnings and real estate assets. This base may be read as capacity to absorb losses during a slowdown in the core business.

Operating profit back in the black

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 8.79 billion in the second quarter of 2026 to a profit of KRW 2.59 billion in the third. Operating profit was KRW 3.16 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 2.92 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

Keeping operating profit positive despite falling revenue may reflect cost adjustments. Quarterly operating margins have moved up and down without a clear direction.

Quality reputation and shareholder returns

Selection as the top MFP brand in the 2026 KS-QEI survey for the 14th time supports the product reputation. A common cash dividend was resolved in March 2026 alongside approval of fiscal 2025 financial statements. Shares outstanding were 9,716 thousand in the first quarter of 2026, down from year-end 2025. These elements can be cited as shareholder-return strengths outside the core business.

Bear factors

Sustained revenue decline

Revenue peaked at KRW 86.7 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and then declined for three straight quarters, with KRW 51.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026 down 35.6% year on year. Annual revenue has also fallen for two consecutive years since the 2023 peak of KRW 395.8 billion.

Press and research commentary point to shrinking paper demand and a saturated installed base reshaping the core demand structure. Whether this trend continues in coming quarters is the central element of the bearish case.

Widening bottom-line losses

The attributable net loss in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 35.3 billion, much wider than the KRW 2.16 billion loss in the second quarter. Attributable net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 9.98 billion. A net loss in a quarter with positive operating profit points to large non-operating or one-off items. Until the cause is confirmed, questions remain about the quality of earnings.

New-business gap and external exposure

Press coverage in early 2025 pointed out that no clear new growth engine had emerged after the exit from the 3D printer business. Export sales of KRW 184.7 billion in 2022 were similar in size to domestic sales, leaving the company exposed to overseas demand and currency moves.

Production also fell to 111,592 units in the 65th fiscal year from 165,773 units in the 64th. Unless a path beyond the core business becomes visible, earnings volatility may persist.

Risk factors

Operating profit sustainability risk

Operating profit returned to the black in the third quarter of 2026 after an operating loss of KRW 8.79 billion in the second quarter, but the profit is small and it is unclear whether the effect of cost adjustments will last.

With the operating margin at 2.0% in 2025, there is little room, so a further revenue decline could bring back an operating loss. Whether operating profit stays positive in the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results will show whether this risk materializes.

Cash generation capacity risk

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 95.4 billion in 2023 to KRW 52.1 billion in 2025. If attributable net losses continue, the cash cushion that absorbs losses could gradually thin.

Even with equity more than 13 times liabilities, a larger decline in cash generation could constrain room for shareholder returns such as dividends.

Goods resale dependence risk

Goods account for 73.30% of sales, while sales of the company's own MFP products are only 1.89%, so results depend heavily on the terms of goods resale. Changes in supply conditions or selling prices could directly affect revenue and margins.

The goods segment was 78.8% of revenue in 2022, showing that this structure has persisted for several years. With a small share of own products to respond to shifts in the product mix, the room for adjustment may also be limited.

Overall view

Sindoh's core office MFP revenue has been declining since its 2023 peak, with three straight quarters of decline in 2026. The operating margin fell from 6.4% in 2023 to 2.0% in 2025, and in the third quarter of 2026 operating profit was positive while attributable net loss reached KRW 35.3 billion.

On the financial side, total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 1.07 trillion with a debt ratio of 7.6%, and operating cash flow stayed positive every year. On the business side, goods resale makes up most revenue and visible results from new businesses have been limited since the 3D printer exit.

On valuation, the PER cannot be calculated given recent losses, and the price-to-book ratio has remained below 1x. This report does not present an investment rating or target price. The next checkpoints are the non-operating items in the 2026 annual business report and the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results.
