02

Business structure

DIT was founded in 2005 to manufacture and sell semiconductor and display equipment, and it listed on the KOSDAQ market in 2018. Its main product lines are AOI, LASER, and VISION AI Solution, which are applied in the semiconductor, display, secondary battery, and automotive industries.

According to the company profile, it builds on high-speed image processing and optical design technology to offer inspection equipment for defects in LCD, OLED, and touch panels, film inspection equipment, and laser cutting and polishing equipment.

Laser products account for by far the largest share of sales by product. According to a domestic corporate data aggregator, laser solutions made up 96% of Q2 2026 revenue, with the remaining 4% in other categories.

The business started with display inspection equipment and has shifted its center of gravity toward laser-based semiconductor equipment. SK hynix is a confirmed major customer.

On January 2, 2026, the company disclosed a supply contract worth KRW 21.16 billion for semiconductor manufacturing equipment with SK hynix, running from December 31, 2025 to April 11, 2026.

Because that contract period overlaps with the Q1 2026 revenue peak, the flow of disclosed orders appears to have influenced quarterly results. The largest shareholder and related parties hold 38.61% as of October 23, 2026, so ownership is fairly concentrated. Combining machine-vision image processing with laser technology is the core of the company's profile.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 132.9 billion in 2022, KRW 107.1 billion in 2023 (down 19.4% year on year), KRW 116.7 billion in 2024 (up 9.0%), and KRW 107.9 billion in 2025 (down 7.5%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for revenue and 2023 was the smallest.

Operating profit rose from KRW 5.53 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.64 billion in 2023 (up 56.2%) and KRW 24.1 billion in 2024 (up 178.7%), then edged down to KRW 23.1 billion in 2025 (down 4.3%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year for operating profit and 2022 the smallest.

Even in years when revenue fell or grew only modestly, the operating margin climbed from 4.2% in 2022 to 8.1% in 2023, 20.7% in 2024, and 21.4% in 2025. The specific reasons for the margin improvement cannot be pinned down from disclosed figures alone and would need to be checked in the notes to the annual report.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 9.27 billion in 2022, KRW 13.2 billion in 2023 (up 42.0%), KRW 28.9 billion in 2024 (up 119.8%), and KRW 30.4 billion in 2025 (up 5.0%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts in each year. Operating cash flow was KRW 7.88 billion in 2022, KRW 2.11 billion in 2023, KRW 38.1 billion in 2024, and KRW 24.7 billion in 2025, so it fluctuated widely from year to year.

The debt ratio fell from 43.3% in 2022 to 13.8% in 2023, 13.1% in 2024, and 12.4% in 2025.

Quarterly, revenue of KRW 30.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 8.58 billion in Q3 2025 dropped to KRW 26.8 billion and KRW 713 million in Q4 2025 (operating profit down 91.7% from the prior quarter), then jumped to KRW 48.5 billion and KRW 16.4 billion in Q1 2026 (operating profit up 2,205.2%).

Revenue then fell to KRW 41.6 billion with operating profit of KRW 14.1 billion in Q2 2026, and to KRW 32.5 billion with KRW 10.9 billion in Q3 2026, marking two consecutive quarterly declines. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 10.2 billion, up 13.3% year on year.

Over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 45.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The semiconductor equipment cycle is being led by rising AI server and HBM demand.

Industry commentary on the company's results says that AI server and HBM demand is bringing a full-scale recovery in the chip equipment market, with a surge in DRAM investment and advances in 3D NAND technology also contributing to improved results.

The company's laser annealing systems are a product line directly tied to this trend. Consolidated revenue in the first half of 2026 rose 77.6% year on year, operating profit rose 121.7%, and net income rose 94.4%, according to figures compiled as of September 5, 2026.

In cycle terms, revenue fell 19.4% in 2023, then profit scale expanded from 2024, and in 2026 quarterly revenue has been correcting after peaking. On the customer side, repeated disclosures of supply contracts with SK hynix suggest that memory leading-edge process investment is translating into equipment demand.

Domestic semiconductor equipment names such as HANMI Semiconductor, EO Technics, and Wonik IPS share the same sector classification, but DIT's product portfolio is concentrated in inspection and laser equipment.

Given that the company began with display inspection equipment, its exposure to display, secondary battery, and automotive applications diversifies end-market risk. However, if the memory investment cycle turns, equipment orders could decline, so the company remains highly dependent on industry conditions.

Bull factors

Recovery in earnings power

The operating margin rose to 21.4% in 2025 from 4.2% in 2022. Operating profit reached KRW 16.4 billion in Q1 2026, the highest of the last five quarters. Q3 2026 operating profit was up 27.7% from a year earlier, so the year-on-year recovery has continued.

HBM and DRAM investment driving laser equipment demand

Industry commentary on the company's results highlights a full-scale recovery in the chip equipment market led by AI server and HBM demand. It notes rising demand for laser annealing systems and a surge in DRAM investment contributing to improved results. Repeated supply contract disclosures with SK hynix also serve as evidence of demand tied to memory investment.

Stable financial structure

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 12.4%, with total liabilities of KRW 29.4 billion. Total equity of KRW 237.2 billion is entirely attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow was KRW 38.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 24.7 billion in 2025, supporting cash generation alongside profit.

Bear factors

Slowdown in quarterly profit

Revenue and operating profit both declined for two consecutive quarters in Q2 and Q3 2026. Q3 revenue of KRW 32.5 billion was below the Q1 peak of KRW 48.5 billion. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 was KRW 10.2 billion, down 25.7% from the prior quarter.

Dependence on a major customer and order disclosures

The confirmed major customer is SK hynix, and the contract disclosed in January 2026 ran through April 11, 2026. If follow-on orders do not appear in disclosures after the contract ends, quarterly revenue could have gaps. The structure makes quarterly results more volatile as order disclosures come and go.

Volatility in cash flow

Operating cash flow swung from KRW 2.11 billion in 2023 to KRW 38.1 billion in 2024. Revenue fell 7.5% and operating profit fell 4.3% in 2025. Because the conversion of profit into cash has varied sharply from year to year, the stability of cash flow needs to be watched.

Risk factors

Industry and investment cycle

If memory investment slows, equipment orders could decline and revenue volatility would increase. Revenue fell 19.4% in 2023, which is a past example. Because demand is centered on AI and HBM, dependence on specific investment flows is high.

Customer concentration and order gaps

The disclosed major supply contracts are concentrated with one customer, SK hynix. If follow-on orders are not confirmed in disclosures after contract periods end, quarterly results could show gaps. If the customer's investment schedule slips, the timing of revenue recognition could also be pushed back.

Shareholder structure and liquidity

The largest shareholder and related parties hold 38.61% as of October 23, 2026, and the free float ratio is 60.03%. With a high largest-shareholder stake, the number of shares available for trading is limited, which could increase share price volatility. Changes in governance need to be checked through disclosures.

Overall view

DIT's recent trajectory combines a sharp recovery in earnings power with a correction in quarterly results after their peak. The annual operating margin improved from 4.2% in 2022 to 21.4% in 2025, and the debt ratio at the end of 2025 was a low 12.4%.

On the other hand, revenue and operating profit declined for two consecutive quarters in Q2 and Q3 2026, and quarterly operating profit fell to KRW 713 million in Q4 2025, so quarterly volatility is high.

Demand for laser equipment is tied to AI server and HBM investment flows, which supports the recovery case, but dependence on a major customer and on order disclosures is a weakness. Operating cash flow also varies widely by year, so the stability of profit-to-cash conversion needs further checking.

The next checkpoints are the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report, the recovery level of quarterly operating profit in Q1 2027, and follow-on supply contract disclosures with major customers.
