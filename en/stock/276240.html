Robot Deal Meets a Fragile Balance Sheet

Elivision is trying to move beyond kiosks by adding a robotics subsidiary, but continuing losses and negative equity remain part of the picture.

02

Business structure

Elivision is an Incheon-based maker of digital signage and kiosks that was established in January 2004 and listed on the KONEX market on July 5, 2018. Its core activity is made-to-order production of information display devices, unmanned payment systems and smart mirrors.

It is classified in the electronics and components sector, but its small, finished-terminal business differs in nature from the high-volume component supply of Samsung Electro-Mechanics or LG Innotek. The scale gap is wide. LG Display reported consolidated first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 11,146.1 billion. Elivision's standalone revenue for 2025 was KRW 2,252 million.

Kiosks are the largest part of the business. The revenue mix is interaction kiosks 71%, intelligent digital signage 20% and maintenance 9%. A Hi Investment & Securities report likewise shows kiosk revenue ahead of signage revenue in 2021.

According to an earlier press report, the company started as a telecommunications construction firm in 2002 and entered digital signage in 2007.

The regulatory backdrop is favorable to the core business. Kiosk accessibility obligations under the Anti-Discrimination Against and Remedies for Persons with Disabilities Act took full effect in 2026, and barrier-free kiosk demand is expected to grow mainly among public institutions.

The company holds a patent related to automatic height adjustment based on the user's physical condition. Market-share data by competitor could not be confirmed in the sources reviewed, so competition is described only qualitatively.

03

Recent trends

On a standalone basis for 2025, Elivision reported revenue of KRW 2,252 million, an operating loss of KRW 313 million and a net loss of KRW 345 million. Revenue rose 17.25% from the prior year, after falling 38.71% to KRW 1,921,302 thousand in 2024.

In 2024 the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 782,827 thousand and a net loss of KRW 838,317 thousand, so the loss narrowed in 2025. The balance sheet remains weak, however. At the end of 2025, total assets were KRW 741 million, total liabilities KRW 2,691 million and total equity negative KRW 1,949 million. Because total equity is negative, the company is in full capital impairment.

Cash flow is still negative. In 2025, cash operating profit was negative KRW 449,339 thousand and cash flow from ordinary activities was negative KRW 508,550 thousand. Headcount fell from 26 in 2022 to 16 in 2025 and stood at 11 as of June 30, 2026.

At the annual general meeting on March 31, 2026, shareholders approved the 22nd-term financial statements and reappointed the auditor. First-half 2026 results could not be confirmed in the sources reviewed.

The share price has been volatile. On March 10, 2026, the stock closed at KRW 362, up 14.92% from the previous day. According to Hankyung Korea Market, the 52-week range is KRW 313 to KRW 800, and the three-month change as of October 1, 2026 was +31.45%.

On September 21, 2026, the price fell 14.23% from KRW 738 to KRW 633. As of October 27, 2026, the share price was KRW 636, market capitalization KRW 7.8 billion and trading value KRW 3,186,672.

04

Outlook

Elivision's medium-term plan is to widen its scope from kiosk terminals to automation solutions. The company signed a share exchange agreement to make Roboworks a wholly owned subsidiary and also signed a memorandum of understanding on joint business.

The two companies are pursuing smart-factory solutions that link Elivision's kiosk solutions with Roboworks' autonomous mobile robots and collaborative robots.

According to a September 2026 press report, Elivision signed a three-way memorandum of understanding with Roboworks and Kanavi Mobility covering AI robots, drones, defense and autonomous driving.

The three companies also plan to take part jointly in R&D projects involving the Ministry of National Defense and the Defense Acquisition Program Administration.

The timetable the company has given is break-even in 2026, accelerating earnings growth from 2027 and a KOSDAQ transfer listing in 2028.

One securities industry official said that if the robotics business translates into actual orders and earnings, it may be valued differently from existing kiosk companies during a transfer listing. That is the official's view, not the view of this report. Many points remain unconfirmed.

The exchange ratio, the size of new share issuance, Roboworks' financial condition and the completion date were not found in the sources reviewed.

In the core business, barrier-free kiosk demand driven by accessibility rules is a variable. How far that demand converts into Elivision's orders and revenue will need to be confirmed in future reported results. Whether break-even is achieved and how the negative equity is addressed are also items to monitor.

05

Bull factors

Accessibility rules may expand demand

With kiosk accessibility obligations fully in force from 2026, barrier-free kiosk demand is expected to grow, mainly among public institutions. Elivision holds a patent related to automatic height adjustment. Because 71% of revenue comes from kiosks, regulatory demand reaches the core business directly. Whether it converts into actual orders must be confirmed in future filings.

Narrower loss and revenue recovery

Revenue in 2025 rose 17.25% from the prior year. The operating loss narrowed from KRW 782,827 thousand in 2024 to KRW 313 million in 2025. Gross profit also increased over the same period, which suggests cost pressure eased somewhat. A return to profit is still some distance away.

Expansion into robotics and defense

The agreement to bring Roboworks in as a wholly owned subsidiary gives Elivision exposure to robot and drone technology. The three-way memorandum with Kanavi Mobility widens the scope to defense and autonomous driving.

The plan is to use kiosks as an interface to on-site robots, so success could move the business from terminal sales toward solutions. This is the company's stated concept, and any earnings contribution has not yet been confirmed.

06

Bear factors

Negative equity and heavy debt reliance

At the end of 2025, total liabilities of KRW 2,691 million compared with total assets of KRW 741 million, and total equity was negative KRW 1,949 million. The dependence on borrowings is shown as 214.22% at the same date. Cash is flowing out of operations, so the need for outside funding may continue. Any capital increase could also dilute existing shareholders.

Small revenue base and shrinking headcount

Revenue declined from KRW 3.32 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.25 billion in 2025. Headcount was 11 as of June 30, 2026. With a small revenue base and workforce, the capacity to absorb large orders may be limited. Thin internal resources are also a burden when pursuing a new business.

Uncertain payoff from the new business

The robotics, drone and defense businesses are reported to be at the memorandum and concept stage. The company said it plans to build a business model running from technology development to demonstrations, mass production and overseas entry. Orders and revenue may therefore take time to appear.

The terms of the share exchange and Roboworks' financial condition were not confirmed, which makes the effect of the acquisition hard to gauge.

07

Risk factors

Financial and funding

Negative equity and negative operating cash flow mean that funding conditions can affect the pace of any earnings improvement. If capital increases or borrowing occur, dilution and interest burden become variables. The financial position after the first half of 2026 could not be confirmed. Annual and quarterly report filings need to be tracked.

Business and execution

The Roboworks acquisition was reported at the stage of a signed share exchange agreement, and post-integration synergies have not yet appeared in reported results. A workforce of 11 must support both the kiosk core business and the new robotics and defense ventures.

Winning orders in smart-factory and public-safety markets is the key variable. Whether the 2026 break-even target is met also has to be confirmed through filings.

Market and liquidity

Trading on the KONEX market is thin, so even small trades can move the price sharply. Trading value on October 27, 2026 was KRW 3,186,672. On September 21, 2026, the stock fell 14.23% in a single day.

The 2028 KOSDAQ transfer listing is only the company's goal, and whether the listing requirements can be met is a separate matter to verify.

08

Overall view

Elivision is a small KONEX-listed maker of kiosks and digital signage, with 2025 standalone revenue of only KRW 2,252 million. Favorable factors include barrier-free kiosk demand, a narrower loss and the Roboworks acquisition.

On the other side, total equity is negative KRW 1,949 million and operating cash flow is negative. The robotics and defense ventures are at the memorandum and agreement stage, and their earnings contribution has not yet been confirmed.

The company has stated goals of break-even in 2026 and a KOSDAQ transfer listing in 2028, but whether they are met must be checked in future filings. Thin trading and large price swings are also relevant considerations. This report is provided for information only, and judgment is left to the reader.
