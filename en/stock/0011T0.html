Growth Widens, Losses Narrow

02

Business structure

Chaevi designs and manufactures electric vehicle fast chargers and handles installation, operation and after-sales service in-house. The business has two segments: charging services as a CPO and charger manufacturing.

The charging service segment booked KRW 15.8 billion of revenue in 3Q 2026 and accounted for about 70.2% of consolidated revenue, making it the core revenue source. The charger manufacturing segment generated KRW 6.7 billion in 3Q, and cumulative 1-3Q revenue reached KRW 27.9 billion, up 51.7% year on year.

The company cites a 52% share of Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment charger tenders and a 71% share of public sites as competitive strengths, figures it reports itself.

Expressway service area fast-charging stations have been operating since July 2026, and the company plans to install 269 chargers across 56 service areas nationwide by 2027.

The company says charging service revenue rises as utilization increases, and it intends to expand the network around high-profit locations such as expressway service areas.

It has launched a subscription charging product for Hyundai Motor customers in partnership with Hyundai, and has rolled out third-generation chargers with NACS connectors at Seoul-metropolitan hubs and expressway service areas to serve Tesla drivers.

Charger manufacturing shows seasonality, as high-margin overseas export shipments are concentrated mainly in the fourth quarter.

According to an announcement on 12 November 2026, Chaevi began integrated testing of a 1.2 MW megawatt charging system (MCS) at the Korea Electrotechnology Research Institute, extending its scope toward the heavy electric truck and bus charging market.

03

Recent trends

According to the DART periodic report, Chaevi's 3Q 2026 consolidated revenue was KRW 22.5 billion, up 28.8% from KRW 17.4 billion a year earlier. Cumulative 1-3Q revenue rose 29.5% to KRW 72.1 billion.

The operating loss narrowed to KRW 8.86 billion from KRW 9.43 billion a year earlier, and the cumulative 1-3Q operating loss of about KRW 28.6 billion was also smaller than in the prior-year period.

The net loss, however, widened to KRW 11.3 billion, and press reports put the increase in the net loss at 12.7% year on year. Charging service revenue reached a quarterly record of KRW 15.8 billion in 3Q, following KRW 13.9 billion in 1Q and KRW 14.6 billion in 2Q.

The segment's cumulative 1-3Q EBITDA margin improved 7.3 percentage points to -3.1% from -10.4% a year earlier. The 2026 full-year EBITDA margin outlook presented in the company's listing registration statement was -8.8%, and the first-half cumulative margin of -3.2% exceeded that outlook.

Charger manufacturing revenue reached KRW 6.7 billion in 3Q and KRW 27.9 billion cumulatively, up 51.7%. As of 16 November 2026 at 20:24 KST, the share price was KRW 3,760 (down 0.40% from the prior close), the market capitalization was KRW 176.5 billion, and the trading value was KRW 200 million.

04

Outlook

The company says it will expand its charging network and improve operating efficiency in parallel, as electric vehicle adoption lifts charging demand.

According to Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport statistics, cumulative electric vehicle registrations in Korea rose from about 900,000 at the end of 2025 to 1,191,873 as of September 2026. New electric vehicle registrations in Korea in the first half of 2026 rose 113.6% year on year to 198,509 units.

Press reports put the government's 2027 electric vehicle subsidy budget at KRW 2.1403 trillion. Chaevi plans to operate 269 chargers across 56 expressway service areas by 2027, which the company presents as an expansion into high-profit locations.

Integrated testing of the 1.2 MW MCS system is set to run through a 2,000-hour performance verification until early 2027, and its results will shape the pace of commercializing heavy commercial vehicle charging.

The company has said charger manufacturing results should be judged on a full-year basis because export shipments concentrate in the fourth quarter. Profitability turns on the timing of the move to net profit, and with the charging segment's EBITDA margin still negative, fixed-cost control will be central.

The next checkpoint is how the charging segment's EBITDA margin and operating profit trend close out in Chaevi's full-year 2026 results.

05

Bull factors

Consecutive Growth in Charging Revenue

Charging service revenue rose for three consecutive quarters, from KRW 13.9 billion in 1Q 2026 to KRW 14.6 billion in 2Q and KRW 15.8 billion in 3Q. Cumulative 1-3Q revenue reached KRW 44.2 billion, up 18.5% year on year. The company describes the model as one in which revenue grows alongside charging demand. Rising charger utilization is cited as the main driver of top-line growth.

Improving Earnings Structure

The charging segment's cumulative 1-3Q EBITDA margin improved 7.3 percentage points to -3.1% from -10.4% a year earlier. The company says this level has moved close to breakeven.

The company also highlights that the trend is running ahead of the -8.8% full-year EBITDA margin outlook in its listing registration statement. Group operating loss also narrowed to KRW 8.86 billion from the prior-year period.

EV Adoption and Policy Backdrop

Cumulative electric vehicle registrations in Korea rose from about 900,000 at the end of 2025 to 1,191,873 as of September 2026. New electric vehicle registrations in the first half of 2026 rose 113.6% year on year. Press reports put the 2027 electric vehicle subsidy budget at KRW 2.1403 trillion.

Minimum performance standards for fast and slow chargers took effect in 2026, and chargers that fall short are excluded from subsidy support.

06

Bear factors

Continued Operating and Net Losses

The 3Q 2026 operating loss of KRW 8.86 billion and net loss of KRW 11.3 billion mean the group has not yet reached consolidated profitability. Cumulative 1-3Q operating loss totaled about KRW 28.6 billion. The charging segment's EBITDA margin remains negative, sitting near breakeven rather than above it. Press reports put the net loss widening at 12.7% year on year.

Fixed Costs and Depreciation Burden

Fixed costs and depreciation tied to network expansion are cited as the main hurdles to improving profitability. The company itself names fixed-cost control and operating efficiency as its priorities.

The cumulative EBITDA margin was -3.2% in the first half and -3.1% in 1-3Q, so the quarter-by-quarter improvement has been limited. The widening net loss despite a narrower operating loss points to a heavier burden below the operating line.

Manufacturing Seasonality and Early-Stage Heavy-Vehicle Charging

High-margin overseas export shipments in charger manufacturing are concentrated mainly in the fourth quarter. The company has said results should therefore be read on a full-year basis.

The 1.2 MW MCS system is undergoing a 2,000-hour integrated test at the Korea Electrotechnology Research Institute, planned to continue into early 2027. The heavy commercial vehicle charging business remains at the verification stage.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Changes in interest rates and the broader economy can affect the cost of charging infrastructure investment and consumers' ability to buy electric vehicles. Chaevi's charging revenue is tied to cumulative EV registrations and charger utilization, so shifts in consumer conditions can reach its results through demand. This risk depends more on external conditions than on company-specific factors.

Policy changes, including subsidies and charger performance standards, can affect revenue and installation plans. Minimum performance standards for fast and slow chargers apply from 2026, and chargers that do not meet them are excluded from subsidy support. The size and allocation of the 2027 budget will shape the pace of charging infrastructure investment.

With a market capitalization of KRW 176.5 billion and a trading value of only KRW 200 million, the stock operates in a thin liquidity environment in which price swings can be pronounced. For a small-cap name, supply and demand factors beyond earnings releases can weigh heavily on the share price. With losses still persisting, the market reaction after disclosures could also be sizable.

08

Overall view

Chaevi's 3Q 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 22.5 billion, up 28.8% year on year, extended its growth trend. The charging service segment set a quarterly revenue record and its EBITDA margin improved, which strengthens the structure of its core business.

Still, an operating loss of KRW 8.86 billion and a net loss of KRW 11.3 billion mean the group has not yet reached consolidated profitability. On the bullish side, revenue growth, progress toward breakeven, rising EV registrations and policy support are cited.

On the bearish side, fixed-cost and depreciation burdens, a wider net loss and the fourth-quarter concentration of manufacturing remain variables for profitability.

Whether the network expansion and operating efficiency plans translate into a lower EBITDA margin and a narrower operating loss will be shown in upcoming results.

The liquidity profile, with a market capitalization of KRW 176.5 billion and a trading value of KRW 200 million, is a fact that belongs in any reading of the share price. This verdict is a qualitative summary for informational purposes.
