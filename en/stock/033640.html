Nepes: Profit Recovery Holds, Customer Mix Is the Test

Nepes has seen operating profit rise for three consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, following its return to operating profit in 2024, but its mobile-centric business mix and high debt ratio remain points to monitor.

  1. 1

    Operating profit reached KRW 23.8 billion in 2025, up 598.5% year on year, and then rose for three straight quarters from the first quarter to the third quarter of 2026 (KRW 10.9 billion, KRW 14.6 billion and KRW 16.8 billion).

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Nepes is a back-end semiconductor company centered on advanced packaging, which it runs alongside electronic materials, test services and secondary-battery components.

Of first-half 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 287.7 billion, the semiconductor segment accounted for KRW 205.6 billion, followed by electronic materials at KRW 53.4 billion and the secondary-battery segment at KRW 28.3 billion, according to the Pinpoint News report.

By product, packages including WLP bumping made up 60.25% of total revenue, while the test business represented 21.16%. The test business is handled by the subsidiary Nepes Ark.

Its core technologies are wafer-level packaging (WLP) and fan-out wafer-level packaging (FOWLP), which electrically connect chips to the outside world.

The electronic materials business supplies process chemicals such as developers, etchants, photoresists and cleaners, and has recently concentrated resources on materials for HBM processes.

Applications span smartphones, servers, high-performance computers and automotive system chips, and the packaging business still has a relatively high share of mobile products. Recently the company has been widening its supply into AI servers and high-performance semiconductors.

Rather than competing on scale, the company says it is focusing on advanced packaging, and on November 12, 2026 it signed a cooperation agreement with Hana Micron, a Korean OSAT peer, on back-end technology and business collaboration.

Its production base is the Osong campus in Cheongju, Chungbuk, and the company has moved production back to Korea after consolidating overseas facilities.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed basis, revenue went from KRW 588 billion in 2022 to KRW 480.1 billion in 2023 (down 18.4%) and KRW 464.3 billion in 2024 (down 3.3%), before recovering to KRW 521.4 billion in 2025 (up 12.3%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Profit moved far more sharply than revenue. Operating loss widened from KRW 6.71 billion in 2022 to KRW 70 billion in 2023, and in 2024 the company returned to operating profit of KRW 3.4 billion.

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 23.8 billion, up 598.5% year on year, with an operating margin of 4.6%, following margins of -1.1% in 2022, -14.6% in 2023 and 0.7% in 2024.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from KRW 77.6 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 98.5 billion in 2023, then a net loss of KRW 62.2 billion in 2024 (losses continued but narrowed), and turned to a profit of KRW 16.6 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, which is a different figure, was KRW 20.2 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, revenue of KRW 137.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 10.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 gave way to a fourth-quarter low, with revenue of KRW 123.7 billion (down 10.0% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 1.35 billion (down 87.5%).

From the first quarter of 2026, revenue rose to KRW 134 billion, KRW 153.7 billion and KRW 164.8 billion, and operating profit rose to KRW 10.9 billion, KRW 14.6 billion and KRW 16.8 billion, three straight quarterly increases; third-quarter operating profit was up 55.7% year on year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a net loss of KRW 2.42 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 619 million in the first quarter of 2026, turned to a profit of KRW 12.2 billion in the second quarter, and then fell 36.4% quarter on quarter to KRW 7.75 billion in the third quarter.

The last four quarters together total KRW 16.9 billion. Kyobo Securities attributed the margin improvement in its May 20, 2026 report to facilities whose depreciation has been completed.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from end markets is shifting from mobile toward AI servers and high-performance computing. A press report citing a Korea IR Council report said that as AI and high-performance computing grow, how efficiently chips are connected and arranged has become more important, raising the role of advanced packaging.

Nepes packaging still has a high share of mobile products but is widening supply into non-mobile items. The semiconductor segment's utilization rate was 85.4% in the first half of 2026.

Its 8-inch line is running close to full capacity apart from a portion reserved for development, while the 12-inch line has room but further expansion is under review in response to customer requests, according to an Etoday report.

New packaging products used to take three to four years from development to mass production, but that period has been shortening, and some items recently won by Nepes reached mass production in about 18 months.

On technology, the company publicly unveiled 8-layer RDL interposer technology at a conference in 2024 and is pitching fan-out-based redistribution interposers as a cheaper alternative to silicon interposers.

When Samsung Electronics adopted FO-WLP in the Exynos 2400 for the Galaxy S24, there was speculation that fan-out packaging could spread from flagship to mid-range and entry products (2024 reports).

Partnering with Hana Micron, another Korean OSAT company, points to a move from individual process steps toward full turnkey back-end offerings.

06

Outlook

The company has said it expects second-half 2026 revenue to grow about 10% year on year, according to an Etoday report from October 2026. The third-quarter revenue of KRW 164.8 billion, up 19.9% year on year, moved in the same direction as that expectation.

Memory controller supply began in early 2026, and supply of silicon capacitors is being pursued. The new items are expected to contribute meaningfully to revenue from 2027, after initial supply in 2026.

On production, further expansion of the 12-inch line is under review, and some customers reportedly support capacity increases through equipment support or higher supply prices.

The cooperation agreement with Hana Micron signed on November 12, 2026 links core processes such as wafer-based bumping, testing and flip-chip on a project-by-project basis, and no specific contract size has been disclosed.

Among broker estimates, Kyobo Securities projected 2026 revenue of KRW 527.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 40.3 billion in its May 20, 2026 report.

Operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026, summed from filed quarterly figures, was KRW 42.3 billion, already above that estimate, while revenue for the same period totaled KRW 452.5 billion.

Bull factors

Quarterly earnings power recovering

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 23.8 billion, up 598.5% year on year, and the operating margin rose from 0.7% in 2024 to 4.6% in 2025.

Operating profit grew for three consecutive quarters from the first quarter to the third quarter of 2026, and third-quarter operating profit of KRW 16.8 billion was up 55.7% year on year. Filed figures show the company moving from loss-making years into profitable ones.

New non-mobile items

Initial supply of memory controllers began in early 2026, and supply of silicon capacitors is being pursued. An Etoday report said the new items are expected to contribute meaningfully to revenue from 2027. Existing mobile volumes are also rising, so the growth base widens as the new items overlap with them.

Advanced packaging technology and wider partnerships

The 8-layer RDL interposer technology was publicly unveiled at a 2024 conference, and the company is developing fan-out-based 2.5D packaging. On November 12, 2026 it signed a full turnkey cooperation agreement with Hana Micron.

Semiconductor operating profit in the first half of 2026 was about KRW 24.1 billion, up sharply from about KRW 9.4 billion a year earlier.

09

Bear factors

Mobile dependence and capacity limits

Nepes packaging business still has a relatively high share of mobile products. Meaningful revenue from the new items is expected only from 2027 onward, so near-term results remain directly exposed to mobile demand swings. The 8-inch line is running close to full capacity, which limits room for volume growth and is a bearish factor.

Balance sheet and past losses

An operating loss of KRW 70 billion in 2023 and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 62.2 billion in 2024 remain on the record. At end-2025, total liabilities stood at KRW 450.8 billion and the debt ratio at 183.7%, with liabilities larger than total equity of KRW 245.4 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 103.5 billion in 2025, but given the size of the debt, capital spending and financial burden need to be weighed together.

Volatile quarterly net income

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a KRW 2.42 billion loss in the fourth quarter of 2025, and third-quarter 2026 profit of KRW 7.75 billion fell 36.4% from KRW 12.2 billion in the second quarter.

Operating profit has risen for three consecutive quarters, but net income attributable to controlling shareholders has swung widely, which is the bearish argument.

10

Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

With a high share of mobile products, a downturn in smartphone demand could hit utilization and revenue together. The company is broadening into AI servers and other areas, but meaningful contribution from the new items is expected only after 2027.

Some customers supporting capacity expansion with equipment or higher prices means supply terms can depend heavily on customer requests.

Capex and financial burden

Further expansion of the 12-inch line is under review, which could keep investment demands high. The debt ratio was 183.7% at end-2025, with total liabilities of KRW 450.8 billion. The ratio moved from 188.5% in 2022 to 240.1% in 2023 and 242.7% in 2024 before falling to 183.7% in 2025, but it remains elevated.

New-item ramp and partnership execution

If the new items do not move into mass production as planned, the timing of their revenue contribution could slip past 2027. The Hana Micron agreement links processes on a project-by-project basis, so tangible results will show up only through individual contracts. Signing an agreement does not translate directly into revenue, so confirming results may take time.

Overall view

Nepes has been rebuilding its earnings power since returning to operating profit in 2024, with operating profit of KRW 23.8 billion in 2025 and KRW 42.3 billion for the first three quarters of 2026 combined.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 164.8 billion was up 19.9% year on year, and in the first half the semiconductor segment's operating profit of about KRW 24.1 billion led the results.

The memory controller supply, the new items and the agreement with Hana Micron are factual steps toward expanding non-mobile business and full turnkey capability.

On the other side, the mobile-centric demand mix, the high utilization of the 8-inch line, total liabilities of KRW 450.8 billion and a debt ratio of 183.7% at end-2025, and quarterly swings in net income attributable to controlling shareholders remain factors to track.

Kyobo Securities presented a target price of KRW 36,000 in its May 20, 2026 report. The next checkpoints are the operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report, and the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results, whose release timing is expected in early 2027.
