Four Straight Years of Operating Losses; Revenue Recovery Is the Test

02

Business structure

Mobiis was founded in 2000 by researchers from Samsung Electronics' network division and multimedia research institute and listed on KOSDAQ in 2017. Its main purpose is developing and supplying application software and hardware. The business has three lines.

The first is smart factory solutions, including QRP, an all-in-one MES package based on ISO/TS16949 and IATF standards, supplied to domestic and overseas companies. The second is ultra-precision control systems for fusion power reactors and accelerators, built on an EPICS-based control architecture.

The third is a machine learning platform business. In 2025, smart factory accounted for 70.96% of revenue and fusion and other businesses for 29.04% (FnGuide data). The two lines sell to very different markets.

Smart factory revenue was entirely domestic in 2025, while 98.90% of fusion-related revenue came from exports. The company's profile material says it has localized the LLRF, a key accelerator device, and claims a domestic market monopoly, with performance verified at the Pohang 4th-generation synchrotron.

That is the company's own description, so the market share claim needs independent verification. Companies in the same IT and software sector include Samsung SDS, Hyundai Autoever, LG CNS and POSCO DX, which are far larger in revenue and customer base than Mobiis, which had 38 employees at the end of 2025 (FnGuide).

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue and profit moving in different directions.

Revenue was KRW 19.7 billion in 2022, the largest year in the 2022–2025 period, then fell to KRW 191 million... more precisely, KRW 19.1 billion in 2023 (down 3.0% year on year) and KRW 5.03 billion in 2024 (down 73.7%), the smallest year in the period. It rose 1.4% to KRW 5.1 billion in 2025.

The operating loss was KRW 4.01 billion in 2022, KRW 6.52 billion in 2023 (losses continued and widened), KRW 2.39 billion in 2024 (losses continued and narrowed) and KRW 2.94 billion in 2025 (losses continued and widened), so the company has posted operating losses for four straight years from 2022 through 2025.

In 2024 the operating loss shrank from 2023, but because revenue fell sharply the operating margin worsened to -47.6%. In 2025 revenue edged up, yet the margin deteriorated further to -57.6%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 5.76 billion in 2022 and KRW 385 million in 2023 (losses continued and narrowed), then turned to a profit of KRW 9.22 billion in 2024, before swinging back to a loss of KRW 1.05 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 6.2 billion in 2022 and KRW 4.26 billion in 2023. The gap between the total and parent-attributable figures in 2023 suggests non-controlling holders absorbed a large share of that year's losses.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio fell from 73.6% in 2022 to 18.9% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 33.6 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 6.34 billion.

Operating cash flow was negative in all four years: minus KRW 2.1 billion in 2022, minus KRW 4.8 billion in 2023, minus KRW 950 million in 2024 and minus KRW 2.61 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 896 million in Q3 2025 to KRW 1.26 billion in Q4 2025 (up 41.2% quarter on quarter), while the operating loss widened to KRW 1.25 billion.

It then fell to KRW 1.04 billion in Q1 2026 (down 17.6%) and KRW 401 million in Q2 2026 (down 61.5%), with operating losses of KRW 543 million and KRW 694 million.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 575 million, up 43.3% from the prior quarter and down 35.8% from a year earlier, and the operating loss was KRW 517 million, narrower than a year earlier.

Losses have continued for five straight quarters from Q3 2025, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 3.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Mobiis's end demand comes from large national science projects and automation investment at industrial sites. Its profile material describes supplying control systems and equipment for big-science facilities.

Synchrotron and fusion facilities involve large and irregular orders, so revenue tends to swing from quarter to quarter depending on the delivery timing of specific projects. That pattern is visible in the data, with revenue at KRW 401 million in Q2 2026 and recovering to KRW 575 million in Q3.

In 2025, 98.90% of the fusion and other business revenue, which accounted for 29.04% of the total, came from exports, indicating heavy reliance on overseas orders. The smart factory business accounted for 70.96% of 2025 revenue but sold entirely to domestic customers.

A securities information site's company summary points to cost reduction and the expansion of fusion and accelerator markets as the main themes (FnGuide).

Among same-sector listed companies, the large IT services firms have revenue scales in the hundreds of billions of won and above, so Mobiis differs clearly in business scale and financial capacity.

On cost, the cost of sales ratio rose from 85.79% in 2022 and 88.17% in 2023 to 95.37% in 2024 and 114.97% in 2025 (FnGuide). If the timing of project deliveries and the cost structure do not line up, profit and loss swing widely.

06

Outlook

The most direct way to read the outlook is through quarterly trends, the cost ratio and cash flow. In Q3 2026, revenue rose 43.3% from the prior quarter and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 517 million, a sign of smaller losses.

However, operating losses have continued for five straight quarters since Q3 2025, and Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 575 million remained below the KRW 896 million recorded in Q3 2025. With the cost of sales ratio at 114.97% in 2025, revenue recovery alone would not narrow losses quickly unless the cost structure improves.

Operating cash flow has also been negative for four straight years, so whether margin gains translate into smaller cash outflows is important. Fusion-related revenue depends heavily on exports, and the timing of orders and deliveries shifts from quarter to quarter, which limits the visibility of quarterly results.

On an annual basis, the 2026 annual report will confirm full-year 2026 revenue and operating margin, along with fourth-quarter figures. The next check after that is whether the cost of sales ratio has come back below 100% in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.

Company guidance is not part of the disclosed data reviewed here, so the outlook is described only through the trend in disclosed figures.

Bull factors

Quarterly Revenue and Losses Improving Together

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 575 million rose 43.3% from the prior quarter, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 517 million from KRW 694 million in Q2 2026. Compared with the KRW 1.25 billion operating loss in Q4 2025, the loss is now less than half that size. Revenue recovery and a smaller loss appearing in the same quarter are cited as a positive signal.

Core Technology and Export Track Record

The company's profile material says it localized the LLRF, a key accelerator device, and had its performance verified at the Pohang 4th-generation synchrotron. In 2025, 98.90% of fusion and other business revenue came from exports, showing that its products sell overseas.

Control systems for large science facilities are a field with high entry barriers, so accumulated technology could be a strength.

Low Debt Burden and Past Profit Experience

The debt ratio fell from 73.6% in 2022 to 18.9% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 6.34 billion and total equity was KRW 33.6 billion.

The company also swung to a profit in 2024, with net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 9.22 billion, and its relatively low debt is cited as a buffer.

Bear factors

Cumulative Deterioration in Profitability

The operating margin deteriorated over four years, from -20.4% in 2022 to -34.1% in 2023, -47.6% in 2024 and -57.6% in 2025. The 2025 cost of sales ratio was 114.97%, meaning cost of sales exceeded revenue (FnGuide data). The bear case is that revenue recovery alone would not narrow losses unless the cost structure changes.

Four Years of Negative Operating Cash Flow

Operating cash flow stayed negative in every year from 2022 to 2025, so the business did not generate cash from its operations. In 2025, the operating loss widened to KRW 2.94 billion and cash outflow increased again. Even with KRW 33.6 billion in total equity, a structure that keeps consuming cash is a bear point.

Revenue Volatility and a Small Workforce Base

Revenue fell 73.7% to KRW 5.03 billion in 2024 and dropped to KRW 401 million in Q2 2026. The company had 38 employees at the end of 2025 (FnGuide). Because the business depends on large science projects, revenue can swing sharply with the delivery timing of a small number of orders, which is cited as a bear point.

10

Risk factors

Profitability and Cost Risk

The cost of sales ratio rose from 85.79% in 2022 to 114.97% in 2025 (FnGuide data). If costs grow alongside revenue, operating losses will not be resolved. With losses continuing for five straight quarters, the pace of cost improvement is the most important variable.

Business Concentration and Order Dependence

Fusion and other businesses accounted for 29.04% of 2025 revenue, and 98.90% of that came from exports. If the timing of large project orders slips, quarterly revenue can drop sharply, as seen when Q2 2026 revenue fell to KRW 401 million.

Financial and Liquidity Risk

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were a low KRW 6.34 billion, but operating cash flow was negative for four straight years. How long the KRW 33.6 billion equity base can absorb the cash burn is the key question. As a small-cap stock, changes in trading liquidity can also affect the share price.

Overall view

Mobiis posted operating losses for four straight years from 2022 through 2025, and its operating margin fell from -20.4% to -57.6%. The most important issue is the cost burden, with the cost of sales ratio reaching 114.97% in 2025.

On the other hand, Q3 2026 revenue rose 43.3% from the prior quarter and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 517 million, a sign of smaller losses. The debt ratio of 18.9% also shows a lighter balance sheet.

However, operating cash flow was negative in all four years, and losses have continued for five straight quarters. The export-heavy fusion business and LLRF technology are strengths, but quarterly revenue is volatile because it depends on order timing.

The next checks are the full-year cost ratio and cash flow in the 2026 annual report, and whether revenue recovery holds in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
