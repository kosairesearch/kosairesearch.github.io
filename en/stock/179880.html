02

Business structure

Melcon develops and supplies environmental control equipment that precisely regulates temperature, humidity and air conditions inside semiconductor photolithography tools.

Its core products are the THC (Temperature Humidity Control) system, which manages temperature and humidity, and the XPS (Extreme Purifier System), which supplies ultra-high-purity air. THC is linked to coater/developer track equipment, while XPS is linked to exposure scanners.

XPS was localized in 2022 as the first domestic product of its kind and was qualified as an ASML approved supplier in 2025. The share of EUV-related products in THC sales rose from 20.6% in 2023 to 32.6% in 2024 and 41.0% in 2025.

Based on a three-year average, the company cites a domestic THC market share of 71%, and a 100% share in advanced-process THC as of 2025. The business extends beyond equipment sales into preventive maintenance, fault response, performance restoration, refurbishment and upcycling after installation.

Melcon operates a 24-hour response system supported by 11 domestic and overseas service bases and 89 engineers. Its customers are centered on Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, which together accounted for 65.5% of 2025 sales.

Overseas, the company has begun supplying Micron and is pursuing evaluation after supplier registration with TSMC. The company emphasizes that supplying both THC and XPS gives it a differentiated position among competitors.

03

Recent trends

On the disclosed nine-month basis for 2026, revenue reached KRW 13.1 billion, up 59.3% from the same period a year earlier. Over the same period, operating profit reached KRW 2.45 billion, up 166.0%, while net income was KRW 774 million, down 38.2%.

Operating profit growth far outpaced revenue growth, indicating improved profitability at the operating level. Net income, however, moved in the opposite direction, so the operating-level and bottom-line trends diverge.

Full-year 2025 consolidated revenue of KRW 41.83 billion and operating profit of KRW 6.158 billion were disclosed during the IPO process. Later company materials presented 2025 revenue of KRW 39.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.7 billion, so the basis of aggregation differs.

Press reports cited first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 22.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.6 billion, which cover a different period from the nine-month disclosed figures and should be read separately.

The share price opened on October 15, 2026, the listing date, against an offering price of KRW 12,300 and reached an intraday high of KRW 28,800. It closed at KRW 18,600 on October 16 and hit the daily upper limit to close at KRW 15,830 on October 27.

Press coverage noted that this rally occurred without clear disclosures or positive news. The current price of KRW 10,610 on November 14, 2026 (down 6.77% from the prior close) is below the offering price. The market capitalization stands at about KRW 133.7 billion.

04

Outlook

The company plans to expand annual THC capacity from 225 units to 400 units and XPS capacity from 70 units (50 units in some reports) to 100 units.

The planned offering proceeds were about KRW 26.8 billion to KRW 30.8 billion, and before the listing the company allocated KRW 13.36 billion to facilities and KRW 12.24 billion to operating funds.

The capacity expansion is tied to rising EUV investment, and the company said it completed development of THC for High-NA EUV at the end of 2025.

As of the September 2026 briefing, customer evaluations were under way for chillers and environmental control equipment for HBM test processes, and the hybrid bonding THC was in on-site evaluation. Overseas, supplying Micron and completing evaluation for TSMC are presented as key tasks.

Customer concentration remains an issue to address after the listing. The semiconductor capital expenditure cycle and the timing of EUV tool adoption are the main variables shaping earnings visibility. Return on equity below the industry average points to a capital-efficiency challenge.

05

Bull factors

Rising EUV mix and higher unit prices

The share of EUV-related products in THC sales rose from 20.6% in 2023 to 41.0% in 2025, nearly doubling. CEO Kim Sung-il said EUV products require more units than legacy tools and are priced 1.5 to 2 times higher.

The company completed THC development for High-NA EUV at the end of 2025, widening its coverage of next-generation exposure. Bulls point to leading-edge expansion as a shift in the revenue mix.

Domestic share and long supply history

Melcon reports an average domestic THC market share of 71% over three years and a 100% share in advanced-process THC as of 2025. The company has supplied Samsung Electronics and SK hynix continuously for more than 20 years, building a long reference record.

XPS is registered as an ASML qualified supplier, allowing Melcon to cover photo-process environmental control together with THC. The company itself highlights its customer qualification experience as a basis for winning new customers.

Margin recovery and low leverage

Operating profit rose from KRW 400 million in 2024 to KRW 6.2 billion in 2025, and the operating margin improved from 1.6% in 2024 to 16.5% in the first quarter of 2026 according to press reports. Gross margin has been reported at a steady 37% to 39%.

The consolidated debt-to-equity ratio at the end of the first half of 2026 was 15.13%, well below the industry average of 84.53%. Borrowing dependence of 7.24% is also below the industry average of 25.52%.

06

Bear factors

Concentration on two customers

Samsung Electronics and SK hynix accounted for 65.5% of 2025 sales, and press reports said their share exceeded 80% in the first half of 2026. The company has outlined a strategy to broaden its customer base through Micron and TSMC.

However, any adjustment in the investment schedules of these two customers flows directly into revenue volatility. Price pressure has also been cited as a challenge.

Falling net income and capital efficiency

On the disclosed nine-month basis for 2026, net income fell 38.2% to KRW 774 million, moving in the opposite direction from operating profit growth of 166.0%. Interest coverage swung widely with operating profit, from 6.48 times in 2024 to 33.29 times in the first half of 2026.

Return on equity was reported to be below the industry average. A clear link between operating-level gains and bottom-line growth has not yet emerged.

Post-listing volatility in a small-cap stock

On the listing day of October 15, 2026, the share price reached an intraday high 134.15% above the offering price. Limit-up closes followed, although press coverage noted the absence of clear disclosures or positive news.

Brokerage commentary cited profit-taking as a source of volatility for stocks that surge without catalysts. The current price of KRW 10,610 is below the KRW 12,300 offering price, so the shares have again fallen under the offering level.

07

Risk factors

Macro

The semiconductor cycle and capital expenditure plans determine order timing and revenue recognition for equipment suppliers. Expanded advanced-chip investment linked to AI demand is cited as an opportunity, but changes to fab completion or expansion schedules can delay tool delivery. A sharper industry downturn could also make quarterly results more volatile.

Customer and pricing

Dependence on Samsung Electronics and SK hynix remains high, so investment adjustments at either customer feed directly into Melcon's revenue. The company has identified price pressure alongside customer concentration as a challenge. Whether the overseas strategy converts into sales depends on the outcome of evaluations at Micron and TSMC.

Execution

Expanding THC capacity to 400 units and XPS capacity to 100 units, while entering HBM test and packaging, will raise facility and R&D spending. Much of the new business remains at the customer evaluation stage. If technology transitions lag, defending existing share could become harder.

08

Overall view

Melcon's operating profit has grown sharply on the back of a high share in advanced-process environmental control equipment and a rising EUV mix. On the disclosed nine-month basis, operating profit rose 166.0%, and press reports put the operating margin in the mid-16% range.

Net income, however, fell 38.2%, and return on equity remains below the industry average, so improvement at the bottom line is not yet evident. Concentration on Samsung Electronics and SK hynix remains high, keeping customer structure a key variable for earnings volatility.

The share price has moved sharply since listing, rising well above the offering price before falling back below it, which reflects supply-and-demand driven volatility.

Capacity expansion, High-NA EUV THC and HBM-related evaluations are presented as medium- to long-term growth drivers, but most remain at the customer evaluation stage. Overall, the stock combines growth potential, customer concentration and open questions on earnings quality.
