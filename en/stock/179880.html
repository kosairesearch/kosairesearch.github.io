Report overview

EUV Dominance Meets Customer Concentration Risk

Melcon holds a dominant position in South Korea's photolithography process environment control equipment market, targeting EUV process expansion and overseas customer diversification as growth drivers, while high dependence on Samsung Electronics and SK hynix and earnings cyclicality remain key variables to monitor.

02

Business structure

Melcon, founded in December 2003 in Dongtan, Hwaseong, Gyeonggi-do, is a vertically integrated specialist in semiconductor photolithography process environment control systems, covering R&D, manufacturing, sales, installation, and maintenance.

Its two flagship products are the THC (Temperature Humidity Control System), which precisely regulates temperature and humidity inside Tokyo Electron (TEL) coating and developing equipment (track), and the XPS (Extreme Purifier System), which delivers ultra-pure air to the lens section of ASML's lithography scanners to prevent contamination.

Photolithography is the critical semiconductor process that determines circuit pattern resolution, and internal environmental conditions directly affect device yield.

Melcon's THC held an average domestic market share of 71% over the past three years and achieved 100% domestic share in EUV and other advanced-node processes in 2025.

The XPS was the first product of its kind domestically localized in South Korea in 2022, and in 2025 Melcon became the world's second ASML Qualified Supplier—and the only domestic XPS provider.

Melcon is the only company worldwide that can supply both THC and XPS simultaneously; the 4–5 year customer certification process creates a structural barrier to new entrants.

In terms of revenue composition for the first half of 2026 on a consolidated basis, product revenue accounted for 76.9% and service revenue (Global Service) for 21.2%; service revenue grew 77% from 4.14 billion won in 2023 to 7.47 billion won in 2025, driven by steady accumulation of installed base.

Primary customers are Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, with whom Melcon has maintained continuous supply relationships of over 20 years, supported by 11 global service bases across South Korea, China, the United States, Singapore, and Taiwan, and 89 field engineers operating around the clock.

03

Recent trends

Melcon commenced its public market journey with a KOSDAQ listing on October 15, 2026.

The final offer price was set at 12,300 won—the top of the indicative range—with 2,405 domestic and international institutions participating in book-building at a 1,136.84-to-1 ratio, and an institutional lock-up commitment rate of 36.07%. On the first trading day, the stock opened at 27,400 won (+122.76% vs.

IPO price), surged to an intraday high of 28,800 won (+134.15%), then pared gains amid selling pressure to close at 17,470 won (+42.03%), with total trading value of 596.1 billion won. On a 2025 consolidated basis, revenue was 41.83 billion won, up 66.5% from approximately 25.1 billion won in 2024.

Over the same period, operating profit surged from approximately 400 million won to 6.158 billion won, with the operating margin recovering to 14.7%; net profit was 4.489 billion won.

The near-zero operating profit in 2024 was largely attributable to a contraction in domestic semiconductor capex, underscoring the earnings sensitivity to investment cycles.

In the first half of 2026 on a consolidated basis, revenue reached 22.1 billion won and operating profit approximately 3.65 billion won, lifting the operating margin into the 16% range.

EUV-related THC revenue more than doubled from 4.74 billion won in 2024 to 10.87 billion won in 2025, and XPS revenue expanded from 3.67 billion won to 4.46 billion won over the same period.

The balance sheet remains solid, with the first-half 2026 debt-to-equity ratio at 15.13%, well below the industry average of 84.53%.

04

Outlook

Melcon plans to deploy approximately 30.75 billion won in IPO proceeds toward production capacity expansion, next-generation product R&D, and overseas marketing and certification. The immediate priority is to expand annual THC production capacity from 225 to 400 units and XPS from 50 to 100 units.

EUV-related THC as a share of total THC revenue rose from 20.6% in 2023 to 41.0% in 2025; since EUV-compatible products are priced approximately 1.5–2x higher than conventional models, a continued mix shift could support operating margin improvement.

Melcon completed development of a High-NA EUV-compatible active THC system at end-2025, targeting sub-2nm advanced-node fabrication. On the customer diversification front, TSMC supplier registration is complete and shipments to Micron have begun; equipment evaluations with Sony of Japan are planned for 2027.

However, the export ratio stood at just 7.0% in the first half of 2026 and direct revenues from new overseas customers remain in an early stage, making the timeline for material contribution a key medium-term variable.

CEO Sung-il Kim has formally stated plans to enter new industries beyond semiconductors—targeting secondary battery ultra-low humidity environment control, data center high-efficiency cooling, and OLED display humidity management—as part of the company's long-term diversification strategy.

05

Bull factors

Dominant Supply Position in Advanced-Node Processes

In 2025, Melcon achieved 100% domestic market share in EUV advanced-node THC, and its XPS is both the sole domestically available product and one of only two ASML Qualified Suppliers globally.

As the only company in the world capable of supplying both THC and XPS simultaneously, Melcon creates very high switching costs for its customers. The 4–5 year certification requirement for new suppliers structurally limits competitive entry.

Over 20 years of continuous supply to Samsung Electronics and SK hynix provides a compelling reference base when approaching new global accounts.

ASP Improvement from EUV Mix and Growing Recurring Revenue

EUV-related THC revenue surged 129% from 4.74 billion won in 2024 to 10.87 billion won in 2025, and since EUV-compatible products are priced 1.5–2x higher, a rising EUV mix directly translates into higher average selling prices.

Service (Global Service) revenue, which has a recurring nature, grew 77% over two years from 4.14 billion won in 2023 to 7.47 billion won in 2025 as the installed base accumulated. Growing AI infrastructure investment increases EUV equipment deployment, creating a structural tailwind for Melcon's THC and XPS demand.

An operating margin in the 16% range in the first half of 2026 confirms that profitability recovery is underway.

Overseas Customer Diversification Moving from Pipeline to Reality

TSMC supplier registration was completed in 2025, and having finished qualification and equipment evaluation with Micron, Melcon has initiated shipments. In the first half of 2026, revenues from new overseas customers including GlobalFoundries began to materialize.

Eleven global service bases across South Korea, China, the United States, Singapore, and Taiwan provide the local infrastructure needed to serve overseas customers.

The company plans to allocate approximately 3.0 billion won from IPO proceeds to overseas marketing and approximately 300 million won to international certifications to accelerate new customer acquisition.

06

Bear factors

Revenue Concentration in Two Domestic Customers

Samsung Electronics and SK hynix together accounted for 81.9% of consolidated revenue in the first half of 2026, with SK hynix alone representing 48.5%. Any change in either customer's capex plans or order timing can disproportionately impact Melcon's quarterly results.

The operating profit collapse to approximately 400 million won in 2024 illustrates the magnitude of earnings volatility inherent in this concentrated structure. With direct overseas customer revenues still at an early stage, meaningful reduction in customer concentration will require sustained execution over time.

Overseas Direct Revenue Remains in Early Stages

The export ratio fell to 7.0% in the first half of 2026, and a significant portion of the 7.7 billion won in 2025 exports is attributed to an existing domestic customer's overseas fab supply rather than new overseas direct sales.

Direct revenues from newly acquired international customers were estimated at approximately 1.0 billion won in 2025. TSMC remains at the equipment evaluation stage following supplier registration, leaving the timing of material revenue contribution uncertain.

All 14 registered patents are domestic patents, creating a gap in intellectual property protection that represents a potential vulnerability in overseas market expansion.

Earnings Volatility and Capital Efficiency Challenges

Operating profit swung from approximately 6.0 billion won in 2022 to approximately 400 million won in 2024 before recovering to 6.158 billion won in 2025, reflecting significant year-to-year volatility.

Return on equity of 4.59% in 2023, 6.87% in 2024, and 5.81% in 2025 has consistently lagged the industry average of 7.98%. With IPO proceeds being deployed through 2027–2029, capital efficiency metrics may decline in the near term before planned capacity additions contribute meaningfully to revenue.

Of the approximately 4.37 billion won in net profit reported for the first half of 2026, approximately 919 million won consisted of one-time securities disposal gains, indicating that recurring earnings require separate scrutiny.

07

Risk factors

Semiconductor Capex Cycle

Melcon's revenues and earnings are directly tied to the semiconductor capital expenditure cycles of its major customers. The operating profit plunge to approximately 400 million won during the 2024 domestic semiconductor capex downturn starkly illustrates this structural vulnerability.

A global economic slowdown or any moderation in AI infrastructure investment could reduce orders for advanced-node equipment and directly impair profitability.

With order concentration heavily skewed toward Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, Melcon's results are disproportionately sensitive to capex decisions at those two accounts relative to more diversified peers.

Technology Transition and Competitive Dynamics

Should Melcon encounter setbacks in product development as semiconductor processes transition to High-NA EUV and beyond, its current market position could come under pressure.

While Melcon has announced completion of a High-NA EUV-compatible THC, customer certification and the path to volume supply still involve unresolved technical validation steps.

In the XPS segment, where only two companies globally hold ASML Qualified Supplier status, a strategic decision by a major global equipment maker to internalize this technology could alter the competitive landscape.

The company's technological advantage relies more heavily on proprietary know-how than on a formal patent portfolio (14 domestic patents), creating potential vulnerabilities in protecting intellectual property in overseas markets.

Macro, FX, and Geopolitical Risks

Appreciation of the Korean won against major currencies could reduce the won-denominated value of overseas revenues as Melcon pursues export growth.

Entering new markets such as Taiwan, the United States, and Japan involves additional costs associated with local certification, customs procedures, and potential tightening of trade regulations.

Escalating U.S.–China semiconductor export controls could restrict access to Melcon's existing service bases in China, reducing the operational value of that footprint.

Geopolitical tensions that disrupt the production plans of key target customers such as TSMC and Micron could delay the timeline for meaningful overseas revenue contribution.

08

Overall view

Melcon occupies a structurally differentiated competitive position as the world's only company capable of simultaneously supplying both THC and XPS for semiconductor photolithography, holding approximately 71% average domestic THC market share and 100% domestic XPS exclusivity.

On a 2025 consolidated basis, revenue of 41.83 billion won, operating profit of 6.158 billion won, and net profit of 4.489 billion won marked a substantial year-on-year improvement, with a 16%-range operating margin sustained into the first half of 2026.

The EUV product mix shift toward higher-priced units, growth in recurring service revenue as the installed base deepens, and the completion of High-NA EUV-compatible product development support a case for medium-term operating margin improvement at the business level.

Counterbalancing these positives are revenue concentration of over 80% in two customers, earnings volatility tied to semiconductor capex cycles (approximately 400 million won in operating profit in 2024), overseas direct revenues at an early stage, and ROE persistently below the industry average.

Concrete progress on production capacity expansion and global customer diversification through the deployment of IPO proceeds will be the central determinants of Melcon's intrinsic business value over the medium term.
