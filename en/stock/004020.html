Report overview

Quarterly profit recovery; annual earnings base still under test

Operating profit has risen for two straight quarters since its 1Q 2026 low, but how far annual earnings have recovered from the attributable net losses of 2024 and 2025 remains a question for the next annual report.

02

Business structure

Hyundai Steel Company produces flat products such as hot-rolled coil, heavy plate and automotive steel, along with long products such as H-beams and rebar.

The 2026 sales plan presented at the 2025 earnings call called for 11.83 million tons of flat products, up 114 thousand tons from 2025, and 5.515 million tons of long products, up 205 thousand tons.

Automotive steel pricing was being negotiated with Hyundai Motor and KIA, and the company said it was pursuing a price increase for shipbuilding heavy plate. Key end markets are automotive, shipbuilding and construction, and long products are especially sensitive to construction activity.

The company introduced a unified H-beam brand, H One, aimed at high-grade structural demand and advanced infrastructure projects.

In rebar, US data-center steel demand and high countervailing duties on rival exporters improved the export backdrop for Korean rebar, according to a March 2026 brokerage report, which projected 2026 US rebar exports rising 377% from a year earlier to 430 thousand tons.

Dongkuk Steel Mill reportedly posted 3Q 2026 operating profit of KRW 92.9 billion, which can be read alongside Hyundai Steel's KRW 87.1 billion in the same quarter.

Earnings depend largely on the spread between product prices and raw material and fuel costs, so changes in product mix flow directly into quarterly results.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 27.3 trillion in 2022 to KRW 25.9 trillion in 2023 (down 5.2%), KRW 23.2 trillion in 2024 (down 10.4%) and KRW 22.7 trillion in 2025 (down 2.1%), a three-year decline.

Operating profit fell from KRW 1.62 trillion in 2022 to KRW 798.3 billion in 2023 and KRW 159.5 billion in 2024, then rebounded to KRW 219.2 billion in 2025 (up 37.5%). The operating margin over the same years was 5.9%, 3.1%, 0.7% and 1.0%, so 2025 remained in the single digits.

Attributable net income went from KRW 1.02 trillion in 2022 and KRW 461.2 billion in 2023 to an attributable net loss of KRW 11.6 billion in 2024, the year it turned to a loss, and a smaller attributable net loss of KRW 6.92 billion in 2025, the second straight loss year.

Net income including non-controlling interests differs from attributable net income and was KRW 1.39 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow stayed around KRW 2 trillion a year, from KRW 2.18 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.05 trillion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit fell from KRW 93.2 billion in 3Q 2025 to KRW 43.3 billion in 4Q 2025 (down 53.6% from the prior quarter) and to KRW 15.7 billion in 1Q 2026 (down 63.7%). It then recovered to KRW 57.7 billion in 2Q 2026 (up 267.9%) and KRW 87.1 billion in 3Q 2026 (up 51.0%).

Attributable net income moved from a KRW 41 billion loss in 1Q 2026 to KRW 8.4 billion profit in 2Q and KRW 85.5 billion in 3Q. Against the same quarter last year, 3Q operating profit fell 6.5%, though a press report said it beat a market estimate of KRW 74.7 billion.

The company said that revenue fell 3.4% from the prior quarter on seasonal slowdown and holidays, while higher selling prices for key products and a larger share of high-value automotive steel lifted profit.

05

Industry analysis

The company expects anti-dumping duties on hot-rolled coil and heavy plate to take full effect in 2026, reducing inflows of low-priced Chinese steel. An anti-dumping investigation into coated and color-coated steel is also underway, which could widen the scope of import restraint.

The company expects long-product demand to recover gradually from a low 2025 base and delayed construction projects. However, a March 2026 brokerage report saw construction investment rising 0.5% to 2.6% but judged that a real recovery in steel demand would be limited.

Because construction takes two to three years from start to progress payments, the report saw meaningful contributions arriving in 2027 and 2028. On the input side, iron ore supply is expected to rise by about 4 million tons in 2026 and coke prices are seen stabilizing, which should ease cost pressure.

The same report noted that Korea's steel PMI was 45.4 in February 2026, below the 50 expansion line, with the new orders index hitting a fresh low. At its 30 October 2026 earnings briefing, company IR acknowledged that weakness in the steel market persists.

On competition, Dongkuk Steel Mill was reported to have posted 3Q 2026 operating profit of KRW 92.9 billion. US export opportunities have opened on data-center steel demand and high duties on rival exporters, but the current US 50% tariff and possible petitions by domestic US producers remain risks.

06

Outlook

In early 2026 the company said the steel cycle had passed its bottom and expressed confidence in restoring profitability under new leadership. The grounds it cited were anti-dumping effects on flat products, a low base effect for long products and the automotive steel pricing negotiations.

The Louisiana steelmaking project is expected to need about USD 1.5 billion in total investment on an equity-share basis, which the company said can be funded from internally generated cash by the time the investment is completed in 2028.

The company's own analysis said the investment would not impair its financial structure. The Incheon plant shutdown was explained as a measure to balance supply and cut fixed costs.

At the 30 October 2026 briefing, the timing of a shareholder return and corporate value-up policy was reported to have been pushed to the first or second quarter, and the company said it would communicate fully with the market. In 3Q IR, a stronger won was cited as limiting improvement in subsidiary operating profit.

A steel trade press report expected further margin improvement in the fourth quarter as high-value product sales expand. The confirmed next milestones are the 4Q 2026 results, the 2026 annual report and the shareholder return policy announcement.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly profit recovery

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 15.7 billion in 1Q 2026 and then rose to KRW 57.7 billion in 2Q and KRW 87.1 billion in 3Q. Attributable net income stayed positive in 2Q at KRW 8.4 billion and in 3Q at KRW 85.5 billion.

The company attributed the recovery to higher sales of high-value products and higher selling prices. The operating margin in 3Q was also higher than in 2Q.

High-value products and higher prices

A larger share of high-value products such as automotive steel was the core explanation for 3Q results. The push for higher shipbuilding plate prices and the anti-dumping effect on hot-rolled and heavy plate were cited as ways to reduce low-priced Chinese imports.

Because both flat and long products are expected to recover, improvement in selling prices and product mix together would widen the spread.

Financial capacity and cash generation

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 73.6%, down from 92.4% in 2022. Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 2.05 trillion, staying around KRW 2 trillion a year since 2022. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 19.8 trillion.

The company said the Louisiana project could be funded from internally generated cash through its 2028 completion.

09

Bear factors

Weak annual profitability

The operating margin fell from 5.9% in 2022 to 0.7% in 2024 and was still only 1.0% in 2025. Attributable net income was negative in both 2024 and 2025. Nine-month 2026 operating profit of KRW 160.5 billion was reported to be 8.8% lower than a year earlier. Whether the quarterly swing to profit will translate into a full annual recovery is still to be seen.

Uncertain pace of industry recovery

Company IR acknowledged that weakness in the steel market continues. A March 2026 brokerage report judged that a real recovery in steel demand would be limited. Because of the construction lag, the report expected meaningful contributions only in 2027 and 2028. If demand recovery is delayed, achieving the sales plan becomes a variable.

Falling revenue and currency and cost variables

Revenue declined for three straight years from 2023 through 2025. In 3Q 2026 revenue rose 2.8% from a year earlier, but operating profit fell 6.5%. The company said a stronger won limited improvement at its subsidiaries. Operating profit beat market estimates in 3Q but still declined from the prior year.

10

Risk factors

Trade and tariffs

The US currently applies a 50% tariff, and possible petitions by US producers could add product-specific duties. Flat-product prices such as hot-rolled coil and heavy plate may move with Chinese low-priced imports and the outcome of anti-dumping investigations. The rise in US rebar exports is directly exposed to changes in US trade policy.

Raw materials and demand

Expected iron ore supply growth and stabilizing coke prices should ease cost pressure. However, if higher product prices fail to offset costs, the spread could still narrow. If a real recovery in construction demand slips to 2027–2028, long-product sales could fall short of expectations. Currency moves also affect the won value of overseas sales and subsidiary profits.

Investment and funding execution

The Louisiana steelmaking project has an equity-share investment of about USD 1.5 billion, with completion targeted for 2028. The company said internal cash can fund it, but weaker cash flow during the build-out could affect the timing of shareholder returns. Facility adjustments such as the Incheon plant shutdown are running alongside the expansion.

Overall view

Hyundai Steel Company's revenue and operating profit fell after 2022 before operating profit rebounded in 2025, and in 2026 operating profit rose for two straight quarters after the 1Q low. Attributable net income of KRW 85.5 billion in 3Q 2026 followed a loss in 4Q 2025 and shows a swing back to profit.

However, nine-month 2026 operating profit was lower than a year earlier, and the 2025 operating margin of 1.0% remains low. Supporting factors on the bull side are higher sales of high-value products, higher selling prices, anti-dumping effects on flat products, a lower debt ratio and steady operating cash flow.

On the bear side are three straight years of falling revenue, two years of attributable net losses and the weak industry conditions acknowledged by company IR. On valuation, the small earnings base lifts the P/E multiple, while the price-to-book ratio sits below its historical average.

The next checkpoints are the 4Q 2026 results, the 2026 annual report, the shareholder return policy announcement and progress on the Louisiana investment. This report offers no investment rating or target price and is provided for information only.
