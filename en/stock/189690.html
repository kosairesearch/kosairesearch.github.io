Forcs: Annual Profit Recovery, Wider Quarterly Swings

Forcs' revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders all rose in the fiscal year ended June 2026, but quarterly earnings have swung widely since the fourth quarter of 2025.

  1. 1

    Revenue for the fiscal year ended June 2026 reached KRW 35.5 billion, up 8.0% year on year, extending a two-year run of increases from the low in the year ended June 2024.

  2. 2

    Operating profit for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was KRW 5.78 billion, up 31.3% year on year, and the operating margin rose to 16.3% from 13.4%.

  3. 3

    Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7.4 billion, up 62.7%, making the fiscal year ended June 2026 the largest year among the four years from fiscal 2023 to fiscal 2026.

  4. 4

    Revenue for the second quarter of 2026 was KRW 9.32 billion, up 31.3% year on year, while profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell for a second straight quarter to KRW 968 million.

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Forcs is a software company focused on electronic document generation and reporting solutions. Its main products are the reporting solution OZ Report, the electronic form development solution OZ e-Form, and the cloud e-contract service eformsign.

The company says OZ Report and OZ e-Form hold the number one domestic market share, but this has not been confirmed by independent market research.

The revenue mix compiled by the Alphasquare company page is 51.84% electronic documents, 45.51% reporting, 1.33% eformsign and 1.32% other, and the page does not state a reference date. Most sales therefore come from electronic documents and reporting, while the subscription-based e-contract segment remains small.

The company describes eformsign as a subscription (SaaS) product and says its customer base has widened from small firms to large corporations. A 2022 press report noted that major financial and public-sector projects were won alongside references from large corporate customers such as Hyundai Motor.

Dependence on any specific customer or customer group has not been confirmed. Competitively, Forcs is a small software firm specialized in electronic documents and reporting, with a business scope different from large SI providers such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, or platform operators such as NAVER. The company had 199 employees as of March 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was KRW 35.5 billion, up 8.0% year on year. The year ended June 2024 was the low point at KRW 31.3 billion, and revenue then rose to KRW 32.9 billion in the year ended June 2025 and KRW 35.5 billion in the year ended June 2026.

Operating profit fell from KRW 5.16 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 4.52 billion in the year ended June 2024 and KRW 4.4 billion in the year ended June 2025, before rising 31.3% to KRW 5.78 billion in the year ended June 2026.

The operating margin declined from 15.5% and 14.4% to 13.4%, then rebounded to 16.3% in the year ended June 2026. Because operating profit grew faster than revenue, cost growth appears to have trailed sales growth, although the detailed breakdown of cost of sales and SG&A was not confirmed.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 62.7% to KRW 7.4 billion in the year ended June 2026, and net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 7.4 billion.

Quarterly, profit attributable to controlling shareholders was only KRW 17.94 million in the second quarter of 2025, rose to KRW 310 million in the third quarter, and peaked at KRW 4.64 billion in the fourth quarter.

Fourth-quarter 2025 revenue of KRW 12.6 billion, up 67.1% from the prior quarter, fits a seasonal pattern in which a 2015 press report noted that sales concentrate in the October to December quarter.

In the first quarter of 2026 revenue was KRW 6.04 billion and an operating loss of KRW 49.56 million marked a return to losses, while the second quarter brought revenue of KRW 9.32 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.73 billion, a swing back to profit.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the second quarter of 2026 was KRW 968 million, down 34.5% from the prior quarter. The sum for the most recent four quarters, from the third quarter of 2025 through the second quarter of 2026, was KRW 7.4 billion.

Bull factors

Annual profit recovery

Operating profit for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was KRW 5.78 billion, up 31.3%, and the operating margin rose to 16.3% from 13.4% in the year ended June 2025. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 62.7% to KRW 7.4 billion, pointing to a recovery in earnings capacity. The fact that the profit recovery came alongside revenue growth can be cited as a bullish argument.

Financial capacity

Equity was KRW 81 billion and total liabilities were KRW 5.03 billion at the end of June 2026. The debt-to-equity ratio for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was 6.2%, and operating cash flow of KRW 7.62 billion was above KRW 5 billion in all four fiscal years shown. The light debt burden can be cited as a buffer during periods of earnings volatility.

Room for subscription growth

The company presents eformsign as a subscription (SaaS) e-contract service and says its customer base has expanded from small firms to large corporations.

Alphasquare's compiled data puts the eformsign segment at 1.33% of revenue, which is still small and leaves room for a higher subscription share, a central bullish argument. However, the pace of that shift and its profitability have not been confirmed.

Bear factors

Quarterly profit reversals

Operating profit of KRW 4 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.64 billion peaked in the fourth quarter of 2025.

The first quarter of 2026 brought an operating loss of KRW 49.56 million, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the second quarter fell 34.5% from the prior quarter to KRW 968 million. Quarterly profit reversing within one or two quarters is cited as a bearish factor.

Revenue concentration and seasonality

Revenue of KRW 12.6 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 was up 67.1% from the third quarter, and first-quarter 2026 revenue fell 52.1% from the prior quarter.

A 2015 press report mentioned that sales concentrate in the October to December quarter, so a structure in which revenue clusters in particular quarters can be read as reducing earnings predictability.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 9.32 billion was up 31.3% year on year, but quarter-to-quarter variation remains large.

Growth continuity and competition

Revenue growth for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was 8.0%, faster than 5.1% in the year ended June 2025, but revenue fell in the year ended June 2024, so the growth path has not been continuous.

Expansion of large SI providers and platform operators into e-contract and cloud areas could intensify customer competition, which is a bearish factor. The size of individual contract wins was not confirmed in public sources reviewed here.

Overall view

Forcs is a small software company focused on electronic document and reporting solutions, and its annual earnings recovered in the fiscal year ended June 2026, with revenue of KRW 35.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.78 billion.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.4 billion and an operating margin of 16.3% were above the 15.5% margin of the year ended June 2023.

Bullish factors include the annual earnings recovery, the return to operating profit in the second quarter of 2026, a financial structure with equity of KRW 81 billion and a debt-to-equity ratio of 6.2%, and operating cash flow that exceeded KRW 5 billion in all four fiscal years shown.

The main bearish factor is large quarterly earnings volatility, with profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaking at KRW 4.64 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and then declining for two straight quarters to KRW 968 million in the second quarter of 2026.

Revenue growth of 8.0% trailed operating profit growth of 31.3%, suggesting that cost control accounts for a large part of the recovery. Formal guidance and order backlog were not confirmed, and the small share of the subscription-based eformsign in revenue is another key variable.

The next checkpoints are the operating trend in the third-quarter 2026 quarterly report due in mid-November 2026 and the fourth-quarter revenue trend in the half-year report due in mid-February 2027. This report is for information only and does not contain an investment opinion or target price.
