02

Business structure

Dawon Nexview makes laser micro-bonding equipment used in semiconductor test and packaging processes. Its core product is pLSMB, a laser bonding tool for probe card manufacturing, and the company supplies its high-speed bonder (HSB) variant to major domestic probe card makers.

A press report on cumulative 3Q 2026 figures put pLSMB sales at KRW 16.45 billion, about 77% of total revenue. The second line is sLSMB, a laser solder ball bonding tool for semiconductor packaging aimed at flip-chip ball grid array (FC-BGA) substrate repair and co-packaged optics (CPO).

The company completed an sLSMB supply to a global ODM firm in 2025 and also supplied a Thai optical communication contract manufacturer.

The third line, dLSMB, covers laser equipment for advanced devices such as perovskite solar cells; its cumulative 3Q 2026 sales were KRW 3.34 billion, about 45% above full-year 2025 sales of KRW 2.3 billion.

Customers include domestic probe card makers, probe card makers serving Chinese memory producers, and a Japanese image sensor probe card maker. In China, sales run through a local dealer affiliated with a state-owned group.

According to the company, its leased plant of roughly 650 pyeong is operating at close to full capacity. Supply contracts disclosed in 2026 include KRW 4.2 billion in February, KRW 11.4 billion in May and KRW 5.3 billion in July.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 10.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 18.7 billion in 2024, up 75.5%, and then to KRW 26.9 billion in 2025, up 44.0%. Sales grew for two consecutive years, 2024 and 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2023-2025 period.

Operating performance moved from an operating loss of KRW 641 million in 2023 to an operating profit of KRW 1.28 billion in 2024, a turn to profit, and then to KRW 4.03 billion in 2025, up 215.0%. The operating margin progressed from -6.0% in 2023 to 6.8% in 2024 and 15.0% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 88.65 million in 2023, a net loss of KRW 4.14 billion in 2024 that turned the result into a loss, and KRW 3.48 billion in 2025, a return to profit. Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts for each year.

Operating cash flow was KRW 3.06 billion in 2023, KRW 1.25 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.95 billion in 2025, so 2025 cash generation exceeded operating profit. At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 18.8 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 13.5 billion.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 346.4% in 2023 to 82.9% in 2024 and 71.9% in 2025. On a quarterly basis, 4Q 2025 sales of KRW 14.3 billion (up 258.7% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 3.21 billion were concentrated in one period.

Sales dropped to KRW 3.16 billion in 1Q 2026 (down 77.8% from the prior quarter), then recovered to KRW 8.02 billion in 2Q (up 153.4%) and KRW 10.2 billion in 3Q (up 27.8%).

Operating profit was KRW 2.62 billion in 3Q 2026, up 55.0% from the prior quarter, which implies an operating margin in the mid-20 percent range for that quarter. The company attributed the recovery to first-half orders converting into sales.

A press report citing the 3Q filing put cash and equivalents at KRW 27.77 billion at end-September 2026, with nine-month operating cash flow of KRW 12.88 billion, and said the KRW 4.0 billion short-term borrowings were fully repaid.

05

Industry analysis

Probe cards are test components used in semiconductor wafer testing, and Dawon Nexview's equipment performs the laser bonding step in probe card manufacturing.

The company has said that rising AI and HBM investment is spreading demand for its high-speed bonders from advanced memory to general-purpose DRAM and NAND flash. The company also says HSB demand is rising at home and abroad as HBM demand and memory infrastructure investment expand.

Chinese memory makers' DRAM expansion is cited as a driver of demand from Chinese customers. In advanced packaging, the market research firm Yole Group has forecast that the CPO market for data centers will grow from USD 46 million in 2024 to USD 8.1 billion in 2030, according to a press report.

That is a third-party forecast, not a company sales target. The cycle position is hard to pin down, and customers' memory capex plans are the direct variable for orders.

On competition, press coverage highlights the broadening of customers to China, Japan and Taiwan beyond domestic probe card makers as a point of differentiation. Shinhan Securities issued a report on June 23, 2026 that framed the second half of 2026 as a period of expected earnings improvement.

Because the customer base is concentrated, the timing of orders from a few clients can strongly drive quarterly results.

06

Outlook

At its 3Q 2026 results announcement, the company said it aims to set new record sales and profit again this year. The company expects most of the KRW 20.95 billion in pLSMB HSB supply contracts secured in the first half to be recognized as revenue within 2026.

A November 2026 press report said Shinhan Securities expects 2026 sales to rise about 35% year on year to KRW 36.3 billion. Against nine-month cumulative sales of KRW 21.42 billion, that figure implies roughly KRW 14.9 billion of fourth-quarter sales, a simple calculation.

On the production side, the board approved on November 3, 2026 the purchase of a 7,055.8-square-meter site (about 2,134 pyeong) in Danwon-gu, Ansan, for KRW 16.87 billion. The balance payment and ownership transfer are scheduled for August 2027, and the completion date has not been set.

The company plans to expand capacity to 2 to 3 times the current level at the new site and to build pLSMB and sLSMB lines there.

The purchase price will be funded entirely with internal cash. sLSMB has a volume application record in FC-BGA substrate repair, and validation with domestic and overseas customers is ongoing. The company says it will address substrate repair equipment demand tied to FC-BGA volume expansion next year.

In CPO, it is pursuing more customers and additional orders. In China, a direct maintenance and technical support setup is planned from 2027.

The next checkpoints are the gap between the KRW 36.3 billion forecast and fourth-quarter results, the execution of the site payment, and the timing of sLSMB's shift into volume production.

Bull factors

Order backlog converting into revenue

The company says most of the KRW 20.95 billion in supply contracts secured in the first half should be recognized as revenue within 2026. Based on press-reported figures, nine-month cumulative sales of KRW 21.42 billion equal about 80% of full-year 2025 sales of KRW 26.9 billion.

Sales and operating profit rose in both 2Q and 3Q 2026, and the 3Q operating margin works out to the mid-20 percent range.

Capacity expansion and new equipment lines

The KRW 16.87 billion site purchase is aimed at lifting capacity to 2 to 3 times the current level. sLSMB has an application record in FC-BGA substrate repair, and after a supply to a Thai optical communication contract manufacturer, technical validation is underway with companies in Spain and Taiwan.

Yole Group's forecast that the data center CPO market reaches USD 8.1 billion by 2030 is cited in press coverage.

Financial capacity and a lighter debt structure

Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 4.95 billion, above operating profit of KRW 4.03 billion. A press report citing the 3Q filing put cash and equivalents at KRW 27.77 billion at end-September 2026 and said KRW 4.0 billion in short-term borrowings was fully repaid. The debt-to-equity ratio declined from 346.4% in 2023 to 71.9% in 2025.

09

Bear factors

Concentration in pLSMB and a small customer base

pLSMB accounts for about 77% of cumulative 3Q 2026 sales, so demand changes in one product line flow directly into total results. The main customers are probe card makers, and their capex plans and order timing are the direct variables for orders. If memory makers slow their investment, equipment orders may be adjusted as well.

Large swings in quarterly results

Operating profit was KRW 124 million in 3Q 2025, and 4Q 2025 concentrated KRW 14.3 billion in sales and KRW 3.21 billion in operating profit. Sales fell 77.8% from the prior quarter to KRW 3.16 billion in 1Q 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 7.18 billion over the last four quarters, and with a short earnings base, a shift in order timing could change the trend materially within one or two quarters.

Large capex and uncertain timing of revenue contribution

The KRW 16.87 billion site is being paid entirely from internal cash, with the balance due in August 2027. Because the completion date is not set, the timing of any revenue from expanded capacity remains uncertain. sLSMB and CPO are still in validation stages, so growing their revenue share will take time.

10

Risk factors

Demand and customer concentration risk

The high share of pLSMB and the reliance on probe card makers make results sensitive to order swings from specific customers or products. If AI and HBM investment slows, the order backlog could still weigh on sales from 2027 onward.

Capex and funding execution risk

The KRW 16.87 billion site purchase is funded from internal cash, and construction costs for the plant and office building have not been set. If completion or start-up slips, the company may struggle to meet order volumes and customer delivery demands.

Regional and policy risk

Sales in China run through a dealer affiliated with a state-owned group, and a direct local maintenance system is planned from 2027. As the China share grows, trade regulations or local policy changes could have a larger effect on orders and collections.

Overall view

Dawon Nexview moved from an operating loss of KRW 641 million in 2023 to operating profit of KRW 4.03 billion in 2025, and sales and operating profit rose in both 2Q and 3Q 2026.

The growth engine is pLSMB, the probe card bonding tool, which accounted for about 77% of cumulative 3Q 2026 sales, so the business is sensitive to memory capex. The KRW 20.95 billion in first-half supply contracts and KRW 10.2 billion in 3Q 2026 sales show orders converting into revenue.

At the same time, quarterly swings are large, as the drop to KRW 3.16 billion in 1Q 2026 shows, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 7.18 billion over the last four quarters, a short earnings base.

The KRW 16.87 billion plant is meant to lift capacity to 2 to 3 times the current level, but with no completion date set, the timing of its revenue contribution remains uncertain. For sLSMB and CPO, the key question is whether volume production begins, and the outlook could shift with customer demand and capex plans.

The next checkpoints are the full-year 2026 results disclosure, the 2026 annual report and the August 2027 balance payment.
