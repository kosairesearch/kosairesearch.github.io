K3I: Physical AI Pivot Under Continued Losses

Operating losses have persisted for five straight quarters through Q3 2026, while K3I is shifting its weight from XR content toward physical AI and digital twin platforms.

  4. 4

02

Business structure

K3I starts from extended reality (XR) immersive content production and runs digital twin and system integration work alongside it, as a KOSDAQ-listed software company.

According to a broker report at its 2024 listing, 2023 sales were split into XR immersive content construction at 70.6 percent, digital twin construction at 4.1 percent, and SI at 25.3 percent, and those shares are 2023 figures.

In January 2026 the company brought Kinder Kino, an exhibition venue operator, in as a wholly owned subsidiary, and it opened the first immersive space in Daegu in April 2026. Its Malaysia XR experience venue, Immersify KL, was set up at the end of 2025.

The product lineup has shifted toward platforms in 2026, led by a middleware linked to NVIDIA Omniverse, a digital twin platform called NeuroTwinX, and a 3D generative AI plugin for Unity.

In March 2026 the company signed an official partnership with Dassault Systèmes, and it signed a manufacturing AI transformation MOU with a domestic motor maker.

Research spending is rising: a press report put first-half 2026 R&D at KRW 788 million, or 12.9 percent of sales, up from 7.3 percent in 2024 and 8.2 percent in 2025. Competition runs on two tracks.

In immersive spaces the company positions Kinder Kino as a story-based model distinct from kids' cafés and other experience facilities, while in manufacturing digital twins and physical AI it builds solutions on top of global platforms and is therefore exposed to their technology direction. As of March 31, 2026, largest shareholder Lee Jae-young and related parties held 45.36 percent.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on DART annual reports, sales were KRW 13.1 billion in 2023, KRW 13.9 billion in 2024 (up 6.5 percent year on year), and KRW 16.6 billion in 2025 (up 19.7 percent). Sales rose in both 2024 and 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2023 to 2025 span.

Operating income went from KRW 1.12 billion in 2023 to an operating loss of KRW 3.72 billion in 2024, a swing into deficit, and then to an operating loss of KRW 3.51 billion in 2025, a deficit that continued but narrowed. Operating margins were 8.5 percent in 2023, -26.8 percent in 2024, and -21.1 percent in 2025.

Losses grew while sales expanded, which suggests costs rose faster than revenue, and a press report put R&D intensity up from 7.3 percent of sales in 2024 to 8.2 percent in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 1.19 billion in 2023 to a net loss of KRW 2.96 billion in 2024, a swing into deficit, and then to a net loss of KRW 3.85 billion in 2025, a deficit that continued and widened.

Net income including non-controlling interests, a separate measure, was a net loss of KRW 4.28 billion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder loss. Operating cash flow was -KRW 1.52 billion in 2023 and -KRW 2.52 billion in 2024, turning to KRW 2.07 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, Q3 2025 sales were KRW 4.63 billion with an operating loss of KRW 130 million. Q4 2025 sales were KRW 5.92 billion, up 27.9 percent from the prior quarter, the highest in the window, with an operating loss of KRW 576 million.

Q1 2026 sales fell 49.9 percent quarter on quarter to KRW 2.96 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 1.38 billion. Q2 2026 sales of KRW 3.14 billion came with an operating loss of KRW 1.61 billion, the widest loss in the window.

Q3 2026 sales of KRW 3.96 billion rose 26.1 percent quarter on quarter but fell 14.4 percent year on year, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 789 million. Controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four quarters totaled KRW 4.03 billion. Quarterly sales swung sharply, while operating losses widened through Q2 2026 before narrowing in Q3.

05

Industry analysis

The end market the company emphasizes is physical AI and digital twins in manufacturing. The company says robot adoption in factories, including humanoid robots, is entering a full-scale phase, which in its view raises demand for these technologies.

At Robo World, held November 4 to 7, 2026, the company previewed a demonstration linking an NVIDIA Isaac Sim-based virtual factory with collaborative robots and conveyors. In the value chain, K3I sits between platform software and on-site deployment.

It works with global platforms such as NVIDIA Omniverse, Unity, and Dassault Systèmes, which widens technology access but leaves it sensitive to changes in those platforms' standards. Views on where the cycle stands are mixed.

At its 2024 listing, an IB industry source noted that many XR and metaverse companies missed the earnings and targets they gave at IPO and said K3I was already profitable in its core business with a backlog more than double that of peers.

Since then, operating losses of KRW 1.38 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 1.61 billion in Q2 2026 have shown a different earnings path. On competitive position, a DART-linked company summary by FnGuide describes the company as claiming the most domestic orders in AR and VR immersive content.

In manufacturing AX, the company is taking part in a national R&D project run under the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy's machinery technology program, building ties with domestic manufacturers.

06

Outlook

The one confirmed date ahead is the extraordinary shareholders' meeting on November 17, 2026. The agenda covers articles amendments that change the company name and business purpose, plus director and auditor appointments, so the name change and the new management team will signal the business direction.

The national R&D project is a KRW 7.5 billion program under the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy's machinery technology program, targeting automation of 90 percent or more of the full process for medium and large motor housing production.

K3I is responsible for integrated digital twin monitoring and manufacturing process traceability. The company has said that after Robo World it will push proofs of concept on digital twin builds, process simulation, and robot integration at actual factory sites.

Its product roadmap, centered on AI-Powered Middleware, calls for sequential launches of core products in three areas: creative AI, industrial AI, and autonomous intelligence. On its own IP, the company plans to pursue overseas exports centered on Asian markets, starting with Kinder Kino.

On funding, a September 2026 report said KRW 18 billion in additional capital is expected to come in as the largest shareholder changes, and that the company classifies the new funds entirely as operating capital without a business-level allocation plan.

At its 2024 listing, the company planned to use KRW 18.1 billion of its KRW 21.3 billion net proceeds for operating capital, but a press report said KRW 7.6 billion had been spent on operations through the first half of 2026.

The next checkpoints are whether the proofs of concept convert into commercial orders and how the additional capital is deployed.

08

Bull factors

National project widens physical AI demonstrations

On November 9, 2026, K3I said it would take part in a machinery technology project run under the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy. The project totals KRW 7.5 billion and targets 90 percent or higher automation of motor housing production.

K3I is responsible for digital twin-based integrated monitoring and manufacturing traceability, widening its on-site demonstration scope.

Wider partnerships with global platform companies

K3I released its own AI middleware linked to NVIDIA Omniverse and, after forming an official partnership with Unity, launched a 3D generative AI plugin. In March 2026 it signed an official partnership with Dassault Systèmes for industrial virtual twin projects. Tying its products to major platforms could serve as a channel for product expansion.

Equity base and cash flow as a buffer

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 34.2 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 11.7 billion. Operating cash flow turned positive at KRW 2.07 billion in 2025. Even with continued losses, the equity base remains intact, which can be read as a financial cushion during the business transition.

09

Bear factors

Five straight quarters of operating losses

K3I has run operating losses for five consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 through Q3 2026, and controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four quarters totaled KRW 4.03 billion. The KRW 1.61 billion operating loss in Q2 2026 was the widest in the window. The KRW 789 million operating loss in Q3 2026 was still larger than a year earlier.

Largest shareholder stake sale and caution designation

A September 2026 report said that right after the lock-up on the largest shareholder's stake expired, a management sale process moved forward. On November 3, 2026, the Korea Exchange designated the stock as a one-day investment caution stock citing concentrated trading by a small number of accounts. The share price had fallen more than 15 percent over the prior three trading days at the time of designation.

Few figures yet showing the business pivot

No recent segment figures yet show how much revenue physical AI and platform businesses contribute. The profitability of Kinder Kino remains at the level of the company's own description of expected positive results.

Operating capital spent from the 2024 IPO proceeds totaled about KRW 7.6 billion through the first half of 2026.

10

Risk factors

Financial and earnings risk

Operating losses have continued since 2024, and quarterly losses widened through Q2 2026. The debt ratio rose from 13.0 percent in 2024 to 34.2 percent in 2025. Whether the 2025 shift to positive operating cash flow proves lasting or temporary will need to be checked in the next annual filings.

Governance and trading risk

If the largest shareholder's stake sale and the change in control proceed, management direction and business priorities could shift. A September 2026 report said about KRW 18 billion in additional capital is expected, but no allocation by business has been set.

Trading concentrated among a few accounts and the resulting caution designation leave volatility as a continuing factor.

Business and technology risk

Demand for physical AI and digital twins depends on the pace of robot adoption and on manufacturers' investment decisions. Because the company builds solutions on external global platforms, changes in those platforms' policies could affect its product roadmap. Whether demonstration projects convert into commercial orders and recurring revenue is still unproven.

12

Overall view

K3I posted an operating profit in 2023, swung to an operating loss in 2024, kept losing money in 2025, and has recorded five straight quarters of operating losses through Q3 2026.

Sales rose to KRW 16.6 billion in 2025, and Q3 2026 sales rose for a second straight quarter, but the operating loss peaked at KRW 1.61 billion in Q2 2026 and narrowed to KRW 789 million in Q3.

The business is moving its weight from XR content toward physical AI and digital twin platforms, and the KRW 7.5 billion national project and partnerships with global platforms are cited as evidence of demonstration-stage progress.

On the other side, a planned largest-shareholder stake sale, the November 3, 2026 caution designation, and share price weakness overlap, so market assessment remains divided.

On finance, total equity of KRW 34.2 billion at the end of 2025 and the 2025 swing to positive operating cash flow are cushions, but the debt ratio rose to 34.2 percent.

The points to confirm are the outcome of the November 17, 2026 shareholders' meeting, the direction of the stake sale and additional capital, and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report. Quarterly results have not yet shown that the business pivot is translating into earnings improvement.
