Report overview

Hybrid Canisters Drive Earnings Recovery at Korea Fuel-Tech

Korea Fuel-Tech's operating profit rose three years in a row through 2025 on demand for canisters used in hybrid vehicles, and it has risen for three straight quarters since the first quarter of 2026. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders, however, fell year on year in the third quarter of 2026, so the operating and net profit lines moved in different directions.

  1. 1

    On a disclosed basis, 2025 revenue was KRW 785.9 billion and operating profit was KRW 45.4 billion, both higher than in 2022.

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Korea Fuel-Tech makes carbon canisters and plastic filler necks for vehicle fuel systems, along with interior parts. The company is organized into a fuel-system business and an interior-parts business. Canisters, which are used to absorb evaporative emissions from fuel tanks, are its core product.

In the first half of 2026, canisters and interior parts each accounted for 40% of product sales, and plastic filler necks for 12%. The company says it holds the number one share of the domestic canister market and fourth globally.

Its heated-type canister is described as a world first, with patents in the United States and Korea. Canisters are supplied exclusively to the Hyundai Motor Group, and the customer base also includes KIA, KG Mobility, GM, the Volkswagen Group, Volvo, Renault-Nissan-Mitsubishi, Li Auto and Link&Co.

Overseas plants are located in China, India, Poland, Slovakia and the United States, with the US plant in Auburn, Alabama. A newly consolidated interior-parts affiliate added to the group in January 2026. The company is also developing ADAS algorithms and an in-vehicle display system.

Some profiles describe the company as the only domestic canister producer, while others describe it as the domestic market leader, so the market share claims should be read with that difference in mind.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 561 billion in 2022 to KRW 679.5 billion in 2023 (up 21.1% year on year), KRW 735.9 billion in 2024 (up 8.3%) and KRW 785.9 billion in 2025 (up 6.8%), and it has increased three years in a row since 2023.

Operating profit rose from KRW 19.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 45.4 billion in 2025 (up 21.0%), increasing three years in a row since 2023. The operating margin climbed from 3.5% in 2022 to 5.8% in 2025, so profitability improved alongside sales growth.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 9.35 billion in 2022 to KRW 30.5 billion in 2023 (up 226.7%), KRW 35.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 43.9 billion in 2025 (up 25.0%). Net profit including non-controlling interests, a different figure, was KRW 44.3 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 164.7% in 2022 to 95.9% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 267 billion, of which KRW 260.5 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 256.1 billion. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 48.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 69.2 billion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit fell to KRW 5.4 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, down 57.5% from the prior quarter, then rose to KRW 11.8 billion, KRW 15.2 billion and KRW 16.9 billion in the first three quarters of 2026. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 209 billion, up 10.4% year on year.

In the first half of 2026, operating profit was KRW 27.0 billion, down 1%, and the operating margin was 6.3%, down 0.6 percentage points, with the China unit's sales decline and domestic research building costs as the main factors.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 6.42 billion, down 47.8% from the prior quarter and down 48.1% year on year, so operating profit and net profit moved in different directions.

The sum of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters was KRW 35.8 billion. Factors cited for the second-half recovery include deferred mold sales revenue from the first half and the absence of last year's one-time costs.

05

Industry analysis

The biggest external variable for the company is hybrid vehicle sales by automakers. Brokers have pointed to tightening environmental and fuel-economy rules and rising hybrid popularity as drivers of higher use of high-performance canisters.

A 2026 report repeats the same link, with canister sales rising as hybrid sales grow. On the regulatory side, the EU plan to cut greenhouse gas net emissions 55% from 1990 levels by 2030 supports demand for eco-friendly parts.

On the other side, analysis suggests pure electric vehicles need no fuel tanks or canisters, so the related market could shrink over the long term.

Technically, the heated-type canister is cited as a differentiator because it addresses evaporative emission limits in hybrids, and e-fuel compatibility research is ongoing.

A 2025 brokerage note said the company sources its activated carbon raw material in the United States and produces locally, which limits tariff exposure. The fourth-place global share means competitive position should be judged against larger parts makers.

The US unit's sales rose sharply in the first quarter of 2025, and some brokers expected higher volume as Hyundai Motor Group expands local production in the US.

06

Outlook

Estimates from Hana Securities put 2026 revenue at KRW 843.6 billion, up 7%, and operating profit at KRW 50.1 billion, up 10%. The report was published in late August 2026. The second-half forecast is KRW 416 billion in revenue and KRW 23.1 billion in operating profit.

The disclosed third-quarter operating profit of KRW 16.9 billion is one of the two quarters in that second half, and the fourth quarter will be confirmed in the 2026 annual report.

On shareholder returns, the company signed a KRW 10 billion treasury share purchase trust in May, running through November 25, 2026, and had bought 353,000 shares through the second quarter. Whether and when the shares will be canceled has not been decided.

Hana Securities expects cancellation is likely given the purpose of shareholder value enhancement. Dividends have been increased for three consecutive years. On US capacity, a 2026 Hana report said the expansion would strengthen the ability to meet local demand.

The China unit's sales declined on lower customer production, so whether it recovers in the second half is a point to check in the next results.

08

Bull factors

Hybrid demand and higher-priced mix

Higher-priced hybrid canisters are driving growth. The operating margin rose from 3.5% in 2022 to 5.8% in 2025. Canister volumes supplied exclusively to the Hyundai Motor Group are directly linked to the group's hybrid sales.

Three straight quarters of operating profit growth

Operating profit rose in the first, second and third quarters of 2026, to KRW 11.8 billion, KRW 15.2 billion and KRW 16.9 billion. Third-quarter operating profit was up 32.6% year on year, and revenue of KRW 209 billion was up 10.4%. Deferred mold sales revenue and the absence of last year's one-time costs are cited as second-half drivers.

Shareholder return measures under way

The company bought 353,000 treasury shares through the second quarter of 2026. Dividends have risen for three consecutive years. Brokers see the potential for a narrower discount on auto parts shares if the treasury shares are canceled.

09

Bear factors

Slower profitability in the first half

First-half 2026 operating profit fell 1% to KRW 27.0 billion, and the operating margin dropped 0.6 percentage points to 6.3%. The China unit's sales decline and research building costs weighed on results. Revenue rose 7% but profit did not keep pace.

Lower third-quarter net profit

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 6.42 billion, down 47.8% from the prior quarter and 48.1% year on year, even as operating profit rose. The sum over the last four quarters was KRW 35.8 billion.

Long-term shrinkage risk from electrification

Hybrids are a near-term driver, but analysis suggests pure electric vehicles need no fuel tanks or canisters, which could shrink the market over time. Because canisters are supplied exclusively to Hyundai Motor Group, the group's strategy shifts would have a direct effect.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and electrification

Dependence on the Hyundai Motor Group's exclusive supply and on hybrid demand is high. Faster transition to pure electric vehicles could shrink canister demand. Development costs for ADAS and in-vehicle displays could weigh on profit.

Overseas units and tariffs

The China unit moved into a loss as sales fell on lower customer production, showing how volatile overseas units can be. Expanding US production is cited as a buffer against tariffs, but utilization swings at individual units can increase overall earnings volatility.

Uncertainty in shareholder returns

Whether and when treasury shares will be canceled has not been decided, so the shareholder-return effect is not yet fixed. Depending on how the shares are handled after the trust ends, market expectations and outcomes could diverge. Facility costs such as the research building expansion weighed on short-term profit.

Overall view

Korea Fuel-Tech has grown revenue and operating profit together from 2022 through 2025, centered on canisters. Revenue in 2025 was KRW 785.9 billion and operating profit was KRW 45.4 billion.

In the first half of 2026, operating profit fell 1% on a weaker China unit and research building costs, but third-quarter operating profit rose to KRW 16.9 billion, the third straight quarterly increase.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 6.42 billion in the third quarter, a different direction from operating profit. That gap can be checked on an annual basis in the 2026 annual report.

Growth rests on hybrid vehicle sales and exclusive supply to the Hyundai Motor Group, while the spread of pure electric vehicles remains a long-term variable. Broker estimates assume 2026 operating profit of KRW 50.1 billion and a stronger second half.

Shareholder returns center on treasury share purchases and dividend increases, and whether the shares are canceled is the key variable for valuation. The next checkpoints are the end of the treasury share trust on November 25, 2026, and the 2026 annual report.
