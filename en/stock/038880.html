Report overview

iA: Quarterly Profit Returns, Cumulative Losses Persist

iA posted operating profit in each of the last three reported quarters after operating losses in every year from 2022 to 2025, yet its net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters still totals KRW 22.6 billion.

  1. 1

    Revenue fell for three straight years from 2023 through 2025, to KRW 41.2 billion, and then ran between KRW 27.6 billion and KRW 30.1 billion per quarter from the first to the third quarter of 2026.

  2. 2

    Operating profit turned positive at KRW 185 million in the first quarter of 2026, then reached KRW 1.54 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.46 billion in the third quarter, after operating losses through the fourth quarter of 2025.

  3. 3

    The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 22.6 billion, driven largely by a KRW 24.8 billion loss in the fourth quarter of 2025; the third quarter of 2026 recorded KRW 1.27 billion of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

  4. 4

    The debt-to-equity ratio was 79.7% at the end of 2025, with total equity of KRW 58.9 billion, of which KRW 51.9 billion belongs to controlling shareholders. The price-to-book multiple sits well below 1x, while earnings-based multiples cannot be calculated because the last four quarters were net losses.

  5. 5

    Two changes stand out: a 5-to-1 free capital reduction that would cut share capital from about KRW 37.6 billion to about KRW 7.5 billion, announced in April 2026, and the push into AI data center business.

02

Business structure

iA was founded in August 1993 as a semiconductor design company and listed on KOSDAQ in April 2000. Its business centers on semiconductors and modules for automotive electronics, and its consolidated subsidiaries also handle power semiconductors, modules, and software development for automotive electronics.

The rise in consolidated revenue in the first half of 2026 came mainly from the consolidation of DC E Solution, which made the power electronics segment the bulk of sales, according to WiseReport's company monitor as of September 3, 2026.

The same source said the semiconductor and module segment kept steady growth on higher demand for automotive chips.

By contrast, automotive module and semiconductor sales, the core business, declined in 2025, which the company cited as the main reason for the fall in revenue that year, alongside higher other expenses from larger valuation losses at subsidiaries and affiliates. iA Cloud, an AI-oriented venture that bought an IaaS business from Tmax Cloud, recorded 2025 revenue of KRW 100 million and a net loss of KRW 10.2 billion, according to The Bell, and has not yet reached normal operations.

The company is pursuing an AI data center business based on domestic NPUs and a push into Europe's AI infrastructure market. On competition, FnGuide attributed the first nine months of 2025 revenue decline to lower global auto production and intensifying competition in the automotive parts market.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 74.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 59.2 billion in 2023 (down 20.3% year on year), KRW 50.7 billion in 2024 (down 14.3%), and KRW 41.2 billion in 2025 (down 18.8%), a three-year decline in a row. 2022 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025 and 2025 the smallest.

Operating losses ran from KRW 2.54 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.2 billion in 2023, KRW 6.44 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.32 billion in 2025, four straight years in the red. Operating margin was -3.4% in 2022, -17.3% in 2023, -12.7% in 2024 and -17.8% in 2025, improving slightly in 2024 before widening again in 2025.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders grew from KRW 2.64 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.1 billion, KRW 19 billion and KRW 34.3 billion in 2025, while the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 44.5 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 deterioration to lower sales of automotive modules and semiconductors and to higher other expenses from larger valuation losses at subsidiaries and affiliates.

Operating cash flow was negative every year from 2022 to 2025, reaching -KRW 10.1 billion in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio eased to 79.7% in 2025 from 82.1% in 2024.

Quarterly, the fourth quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 8.63 billion, an operating loss of KRW 2.1 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 24.8 billion.

The first quarter of 2026 brought revenue of KRW 27.6 billion, up 220.1% from the prior quarter, and an operating profit of KRW 185 million, while the second quarter reached KRW 30.1 billion in revenue and KRW 1.54 billion in operating profit.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 27.7 billion fell 7.9% from the prior quarter but rose 213.4% year on year, with operating profit of KRW 1.46 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.27 billion.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for two consecutive quarters from the second quarter to the third quarter of 2026, and the last four quarters total a net loss of KRW 22.6 billion.

Much of the revenue increase reflects the consolidation effect of DC E Solution, and the detail behind the larger fourth-quarter 2025 loss is a point to check in the notes to the periodic report.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for automotive electronics parts tracks vehicle production and the pace of electrification by model, so revenue is hit first when output falls.

FnGuide attributed the revenue decline in the first nine months of 2025 to lower global automobile production and intensifying competition in the automotive parts market. The same source also cited rising electric vehicle demand, which points to an uneven market that varies by vehicle type and part category.

Power semiconductors and automotive chips gain content per vehicle as electrification and autonomous driving spread, but iA's power electronics sales have grown in weight since the consolidation of DC E Solution, so it is worth separating self-developed sales from acquired-business sales.

Among sector peers, LG Innotek and Samsung Electro-Mechanics are large component makers with much bigger scale and customer bases, while Isupetasys and Korea Circuit operate in PCBs and KMW and Rfhic in communications and RF components, so the relevant comparison set differs by business area.

The AI data center business differs in customer base from the automotive-centred business and amounts to a shift in business structure, and the 2025 revenue of KRW 100 million at iA Cloud shows that securing sales after an acquisition is the key issue.

From a cycle perspective, falling production overlaps with the shift in electric vehicle demand, so the size and duration of automotive supply contracts will likely determine how fast the business recovers.

Relative to competitors, iA's position is clearly smaller in revenue scale and financial capacity, and its differentiation rests on handling both automotive semiconductors and modules.

06

Outlook

The company is pursuing an AI data center business based on domestic NPUs and a push into Europe's AI infrastructure market, a plan WiseReport's company monitor summarized as of September 3, 2026.

The 5-to-1 free capital reduction announced in April 2026 aims to offset accumulated deficits and was reported to cut share capital from about KRW 37.6 billion to about KRW 7.5 billion.

Reported details put the number of issued shares falling from roughly 37.6 million to roughly 7.5 million, and under the commercial act a deficit-offset reduction passes by ordinary resolution at a shareholders' meeting.

A capital reduction of this kind leaves total equity unchanged, so it should be read as an event that changes share count and per-share figures rather than the equity base.

The eighth convertible bond, paid in February 2025, matures in February 2030, and a February 2026 report said a conversion price adjustment lifted the CEO's stake from 3.06% to 3.37%.

Press reports from early 2026 gave a scheduled date of December 2026 for the final KRW 16.6 billion installment on the DC E Solution acquisition, and whether that schedule holds is the first checkpoint for cash flow.

Even with consolidated profit, WiseReport data show a separate-basis operating loss of KRW 151.14 million for the third quarter of 2026, so recovery in the parent's core business should be tracked in separate financial statements.

The scale and investment plan of the AI business should be checked against figures in the 2026 annual report. Whether quarterly revenue can hold between KRW 27.6 billion and KRW 30.1 billion is the test for the next reported results.

AI Push and a Share Price Low Relative to Net Assets

Bear factors

Four Straight Operating Losses and Negative Cash Flow

Operating losses ran for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, with an operating margin of -17.8% in 2025. Operating cash flow was negative in every year from 2022 to 2025, reaching -KRW 10.1 billion in 2025. The annual loss pattern has not yet been resolved despite quarterly profit in 2026.

Short Profit Run and a Large Fourth-Quarter Loss

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in the fourth quarter of 2025 widened sharply to KRW 24.8 billion. Over the last four quarters, that net loss totals KRW 22.6 billion.

Only three quarters, from the first to the third quarter of 2026, have shown profit, so the profitable stretch remains short relative to accumulated losses.

Core Revenue Decline and Rising Competition

The main driver of the 2025 revenue decline was lower sales of automotive modules and semiconductors. FnGuide also pointed to intensifying competition in automotive parts over the first nine months of 2025.

Because the 2026 revenue increase leans heavily on consolidation, the core business's organic growth needs to be checked separately.

Risk factors

Business Concentration and Reliance on Acquisitions

The 2026 revenue increase relies heavily on the consolidation of DC E Solution, so a weaker profit trend at that business could reverse consolidated results. Core automotive electronics sales declined in 2025, and competition in automotive parts may keep intensifying.

The cloud-services subsidiary recorded 2025 revenue of KRW 100 million and a net loss of KRW 10.2 billion, leaving normalization of that business as a major uncertainty.

Financial and Dilution Risk

The eighth convertible bond matures in February 2030, and conversion price adjustments can raise the share count. A February 2026 report said an adjustment lifted the CEO's stake from 3.06% to 3.37%.

The KRW 22.6 billion net loss over the last four quarters and negative operating cash flow through 2025 are factors to watch for future funding needs.

Governance and Related-Party Funding Risk

In an early 2026 report, Seoul Economic Daily said a large amount of company funds going into the acquisition of a company linked to a major shareholder had drawn concern. The company said the acquisition was meant to diversify its business. Fund flows and related-party transactions should be checked in the notes to the periodic reports.

Overall view

iA's revenue fell for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, and it posted operating losses for four straight years from 2022 to 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose above KRW 27.6 billion in 2026, operating profit stayed positive after the first-quarter turnaround, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for two consecutive quarters.

Much of that recovery is explained by the consolidation of DC E Solution, so whether the core automotive electronics business is recovering needs separate checking.

On the financial side, the debt-to-equity ratio fell to 79.7% at the end of 2025 from 82.1% in 2024, and a free capital reduction to offset deficits was announced in April 2026.

The last four quarters total a KRW 22.6 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, and operating cash flow was negative through 2025, so whether cash flow improves is the next thing to watch. The price-to-book multiple sits well below 1x, but earnings-based multiples cannot be calculated.

Strengths include the quarterly profit turnaround, a share price low relative to net assets and the AI push, while weaknesses include cumulative losses, declining core sales and possible dilution from convertible bonds.

The next checkpoints are the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report and first-quarter 2027 results.
