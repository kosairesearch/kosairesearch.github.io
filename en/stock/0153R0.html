Fast growth, margins still the test

02

Business structure

Baropharm began as an ordering platform for pharmacies and runs three business pillars: platform, partnership, and brand. The platform business earns brokerage fees, plus advertising, cloud, and data service revenue.

The partnership business buys products from pharmaceutical companies and external brands directly, supplies them to pharmacies, and handles marketing and logistics, and it is run through the subsidiary BRP Labs.

The brand business plans and sells proprietary products, including health functional foods and the cosmetics line Levicle. Partnership is the largest revenue contributor, with revenue of KRW 69.6 billion in 2025 and about 74% of first-half 2026 revenue.

Profit contribution runs the other way: in 2025 gross margins were 5.1% for partnership, 58.9% for platform, and 44.5% for brand, and the platform business generated 50.6% of total gross profit from only 17.1% of revenue.

The company says 24,787 pharmacies, about 90% of the national total, have joined, and it estimates the annual pharmacy drug supply market at roughly KRW 27 trillion, of which about 8% flows through its ordering connections.

Pharmacy membership base and repeat ordering are the central competitive factors, and the company cites an 86% reorder rate and cumulative ordering volume of KRW 7.2 trillion.

03

Recent trends

Third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 49.6 billion, up 77.9% year on year. Operating income of KRW 2.32 billion and net income of KRW 2.13 billion both swung to profit from the prior-year period.

Gross profit rose 82.4% in the same period, outpacing revenue growth, while selling and administrative expenses grew only 28.5%, lowering their share of revenue from 17.6% to 12.7%.

First-half 2026 consolidated revenue was KRW 69.0 billion with operating income of KRW 2.2 billion, a turn to profit, and the first-half operating margin was 3.2%. The partnership business posted an operating loss of about KRW 0.6 billion over the same period.

Revenue for 2025 was KRW 96.7 billion, up from about KRW 11.6 billion in 2023 and KRW 45.5 billion in 2024, yet the company still recorded an operating loss of KRW 3.93 billion and a net loss of KRW 9.68 billion that year.

Disclosure materials from the listing process show annual revenue growing from about KRW 0.3 billion in 2021 to KRW 96.7 billion in 2025, a compound annual growth rate of 337%. On the first trading day, the share price of KRW 11,780 was 28.17% below the KRW 16,400 offering price.

Institutional demand forecasting drew a competition ratio of 152.36 to 1, and the retail subscription ratio was 19.27 to 1. Trading value on that day reached KRW 244.0 billion, above market capitalization of KRW 141.0 billion.

04

Outlook

In the neutral scenario of its registration statement, the company projects 2026 revenue of KRW 174.6 billion and operating income of KRW 7.2 billion.

For 2027 it targets revenue of KRW 226.2 billion and operating income of KRW 18.5 billion, and for 2028 revenue of KRW 281.2 billion and operating income of KRW 30.2 billion.

These targets rest on a plan to grow revenue at a compound annual rate of 43% over the three years from 2025 and lift the operating margin to 10.7%.

First-half 2026 revenue of KRW 69.0 billion plus third-quarter revenue of KRW 49.6 billion equals about 68% of the full-year target, but combined operating income of roughly KRW 4.5 billion remains well short of the KRW 7.2 billion goal. How much fourth-quarter 2026 results narrow that gap is the next checkpoint.

In the company's 2028 forecast, the partnership business is the largest segment at KRW 188.3 billion, and the brand business is projected at KRW 65.3 billion. Brand revenue is expected to rise from KRW 4.1 billion in 2023 to KRW 29.2 billion in 2026.

Entry into Japanese select shops and drugstores and expansion of North American online channels are also under way. The company's stated goal is to lower the brand cost ratio from above 50% today to below 50% over the long term, and the production facility investment is tied to that aim.

Whether this cost improvement can offset the low-margin structure of the partnership segment will be the main driver of future results. Growth in receivables and whether pharmaceutical purchase terms hold are also points to track.

05

Bull factors

Rapid growth and operating leverage

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 49.6 billion rose 77.9% year on year, and operating income of KRW 2.32 billion swung to profit. Selling and administrative expenses grew only 28.5%, lowering their share of revenue to 12.7%.

The company attributes this to fixed costs being spread over a larger base after earlier investment. In first-half 2026, the platform business earned operating income of KRW 1.66 billion and the proprietary brand business KRW 1.17 billion.

Pharmacy network and platform profitability

About 90% of the country's pharmacies, or 24,787 locations, have joined the platform, and the reorder rate is 86%. In 2025 the platform business made up only 17.1% of revenue but earned a 58.9% gross margin and 50.6% of total gross profit.

Beyond brokerage fees, the company plans to widen revenue through advertising, cloud, and data services. This structure shows the ordering network can convert into higher-margin income.

Proprietary brands and overseas plans

The company expects brand revenue to rise from KRW 4.1 billion in 2023 to KRW 29.2 billion in 2026. It plans to place products in about 80 stores in Japan in sequence and to expand North American online sales channels.

Of the offering proceeds, KRW 7.61 billion is allocated to overseas expansion and KRW 14.0 billion to pharmaceutical production facilities. The company argues that in-house production creates room to improve cost ratios.

06

Bear factors

Thin margins in partnership business

The partnership business accounted for about 74% of first-half 2026 consolidated revenue yet posted an operating loss of about KRW 0.6 billion over the same period. Its 2025 gross margin was only 5.1%.

In the first half of 2026, selling and administrative expenses of about KRW 5.7 billion exceeded gross profit of KRW 5.1 billion in this segment. The company's overall operating margin was a thin 3.2% in the first half.

Loss history and falling gross margin

Consolidated operating loss in 2025 was KRW 3.93 billion and net loss was KRW 9.68 billion. Gross margin fell 44 percentage points, from 60.1% in 2023 to 16.1% in 2025.

After operating losses from 2023 through 2025, the company returned to operating profit in the first half of 2026, but first-half operating income of KRW 2.2 billion is small relative to revenue.

Receivables growth and share price volatility

Consolidated receivables rose from KRW 0.6 billion at the end of 2023 to KRW 10.3 billion at the end of the first half of 2026, with 82.9% arising at the subsidiary BRP Labs. The company says more than 95% of receivables are under three months old.

On the first trading day, the share price of KRW 11,780 was 28.17% below the offering price, and trading value of KRW 244.0 billion exceeded market capitalization of KRW 141.0 billion.

07

Risk factors

Policy and regulation

Pharmacy drug distribution and ordering brokerage are subject to related laws and distribution rules. Under the direct-purchase model, changes in pharmaceutical companies' purchase terms feed directly into revenue and margins. A change in terms with partners could spread across the profitability of the partnership business.

Supply-demand and volatility

The share price on the first trading day sat 28.17% below the offering price. Trading value exceeded market capitalization, indicating heavy short-term supply and demand effects.

CEO Kim Seul-gi's stake of 35.30% and the 40.58% held by six major shareholders could act as supply variables alongside future share distribution.

Execution risk

Offering proceeds go to AI technology, overseas expansion, and production facilities, and the timing of target achievement depends on execution speed and results. The 10.7% operating margin target for 2028 depends heavily on improving the low-margin partnership structure.

If overseas sales channels and in-house production do not ramp as planned, the cost-ratio improvement could be delayed.

08

Overall view

Baropharm began as a pharmacy ordering platform and is broadening into partnership and proprietary brand businesses, showing revenue growth and a return to profit at the same time. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 49.6 billion and operating income of KRW 2.32 billion reflect lower selling and administrative burden.

However, the partnership business carries the largest revenue share with a low gross margin, so revenue expansion does not translate directly into margin improvement. Much of the profit now comes from the platform and brand businesses, so the shift in business mix will shape the profitability trend.

The share price on the first trading day settled below the offering price, and the relation between market capitalization of KRW 141.0 billion and trading value of KRW 244.0 billion shows high short-term volatility.

Against first-half and third-quarter results, the 2026 operating income target of KRW 7.2 billion still leaves a visible gap. Fourth-quarter results and whether purchase terms in the partnership business improve are the key points to confirm next.
