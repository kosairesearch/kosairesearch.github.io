02

Business structure

Lotte Shopping operates through department stores, marts, overseas operations and subsidiaries. In the third quarter of 2026 the department store unit posted sales of KRW 808.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 112.9 billion, with sales at a record level for a third quarter.

The unit accounted for 64.3% of group operating profit in that quarter. Large stores including the main store, Jamsil, Busan Main and Incheon led traffic, with luxury goods at 25% of sales and foreign customers at 29% of sales at the main store.

The mart unit posted sales of KRW 1.4626 trillion and operating profit of KRW 21.6 billion in the same quarter.

Overseas, Lotte Mall West Lake Hanoi passed KRW 1 trillion in cumulative sales within three years of opening, and the overseas hypermarket business recorded sales of KRW 312.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.6 billion.

Among subsidiaries, the e-commerce unit Lotte ON posted sales of KRW 26.7 billion and an operating loss of KRW 7.2 billion, while the electronics retail subsidiary saw operating profit fall sharply. The automated logistics center in Busan, Zeta Smart Center, carries initial cost burdens.

Competition overlaps with Shinsegae and Hyundai Department Store in department stores and E-Mart in marts, and the mart unit benefited from the spillover of Homeplus's business suspension.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 15.5 trillion in 2022 to KRW 14.6 trillion in 2023 (down 5.9% year on year), KRW 14 trillion in 2024 (down 3.9%), and KRW 13.7 trillion in 2025 (down 1.8%).

Revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest within 2022-2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 386.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 508.4 billion in 2023 (up 31.6%), fell to KRW 473.1 billion in 2024 (down 6.9%), and rose to KRW 547 billion in 2025 (up 15.6%), making 2025 the largest year within 2022-2025.

Operating margin moved from 2.5% in 2022 to 3.5%, 3.4% and 4.0% in 2025, so margins improved while revenue shrank. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from a KRW 324.6 billion loss in 2022 to KRW 174.4 billion profit in 2023, a KRW 968 billion loss in 2024, and KRW 51.6 billion profit in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, which is a different measure, was a KRW 994.1 billion loss in 2024. The specific items behind the 2024 swing into loss, and whether they were one-off, are a point to check in the notes to the annual report.

Operating cash flow stayed between KRW 1.5879 trillion and KRW 1.7238 trillion across 2022-2025.

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 130.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 227.7 billion in the fourth quarter (up 74.4%), KRW 252.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 11.1%), KRW 89.9 billion in the second quarter (down 64.4%), and KRW 175.6 billion in the third quarter (up 95.3% quarter on quarter and up 34.5% year on year).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a KRW 56.6 billion loss in the third quarter of 2025 to a KRW 112.4 billion profit in the fourth quarter, KRW 128.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026, a KRW 2.85 billion loss in the second quarter, and KRW 131.5 billion in the third quarter.

The sum for the last four quarters is KRW 369.2 billion. The third-quarter recovery came mainly from department stores, and operating margin rose to 4.8% from 3.8% a year earlier.

05

Industry analysis

The department store unit's results were supported by demand from foreign tourists. Foreign customer sales at the main store rose 95% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, and the foreign share of total sales at that store reached 29%, up 10 percentage points.

This means domestic results now lean more on inbound foreign spending, and the durability of that spending is outside the company's control. Growth in luxury goods and luxury jewelry, which carry higher margins, contributed to profitability.

In marts, the base effect from consumer coupons, a shift in holiday timing and easing competition lifted sales. The spillover from Homeplus's business suspension was also cited as a factor lifting same-store sales.

Because this benefit reflects a temporary gap rather than a structural change in competition, it would be hard to repeat in full once the competitive environment normalizes.

In department stores the company competes with Shinsegae and Hyundai Department Store for traffic at large stores, and in marts it competes with E-Mart on price and assortment.

After sales declined through 2025, the third quarter of 2026 saw department stores and marts grow together, which could be read as a sign of a turn, though more quarters are needed before generalizing this to the wider industry.

06

Outlook

The company plans to keep renovating its key department store locations and run year-end Christmas season traffic campaigns to sustain growth. The December 2026 holiday season is the first test of whether foreign customer sales and luxury demand keep the third-quarter pace into the fourth quarter.

Overseas, Lotte Mall West Lake Hanoi passed KRW 1 trillion in cumulative sales, and overseas department store sales rose 14.8% year on year to KRW 35 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

The overseas hypermarket business reported a 10% gain in same-store sales, and profitability improved as higher sales eased fixed-cost pressure. On the subsidiary side, the operating loss at the e-commerce unit Lotte ON narrowed and advertising revenue grew.

The home shopping subsidiary shifted its product mix toward profitable categories such as health supplements and beauty. How quickly the initial cost burden at Zeta Smart Center in Busan declines will affect logistics costs and margins.

Whether the operating profit decline at the electronics retail subsidiary reverses during its restructuring is also worth tracking. The next step is to check in the 2026 annual report whether segment operating profit keeps the improvement seen in the third quarter.

08

Bull factors

Profit mix improving around department stores

Department store sales of KRW 808.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026 were a record for that quarter, and the unit's operating profit of KRW 112.9 billion made up 64.3% of group operating profit. Operating margin rose from 2.5% in 2022 to 4.0% in 2025 and reached 4.8% in the third quarter of 2026. Foreign customers at 29% and luxury goods at 25% of department store sales fit with a richer product mix.

Gradual balance sheet improvement

The debt-to-equity ratio declined from 187.3% in 2022 to 182.8% in 2023, 129.0% in 2024 and 124.8% in 2025. At the end of 2025, equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 15.2 trillion against total liabilities of KRW 21 trillion.

Operating cash flow stayed at or above KRW 1.5879 trillion in every year from 2022 to 2025, which provides a base for lower reliance on borrowing.

Quarterly profit recovery

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a KRW 56.6 billion loss in the third quarter of 2025 to a KRW 112.4 billion profit in the fourth quarter, KRW 128.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026, and KRW 131.5 billion in the third quarter. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 175.6 billion rose 95.3% quarter on quarter and 34.5% year on year.

Bear factors

Revenue decline and profit volatility

Revenue fell three straight years, from KRW 14.6 trillion in 2023 to KRW 14 trillion in 2024 and KRW 13.7 trillion in 2025. A KRW 968 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was recorded in 2024.

In the second quarter of 2026, operating profit fell 64.4% quarter on quarter to KRW 89.9 billion and a KRW 2.85 billion loss was posted. Profit swings this large from quarter to quarter raise questions about how durable the recovery is.

Mart results leaning on temporary factors

The factors cited for mart sales growth were the base effect from consumer coupons, a shift in holiday timing and easing competition. The spillover from Homeplus's business suspension was also named as a factor lifting same-store sales.

These factors can fade over time, so whether mart operating profit of KRW 21.6 billion, up 87.5%, can be repeated at the same pace is uncertain.

Subsidiary weakness and investment burden

The e-commerce unit Lotte ON posted a KRW 7.2 billion operating loss on sales of KRW 26.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026. The electronics retail subsidiary saw operating profit fall sharply.

Zeta Smart Center in Busan carries initial cost burdens, and the overseas hypermarket business earned only KRW 3.6 billion of operating profit on KRW 312.4 billion of sales in the third quarter.

10

Risk factors

Policy and consumption factors

If the consumer coupon effect turns into a base effect, sales growth in marts and department stores could slow. Shifts in holiday timing also move quarterly results. Part of the third-quarter 2026 sales growth came from such temporary factors and should be read with that in mind.

Shifts in competitive landscape

The spillover from Homeplus's business suspension could shrink once competitors return to normal operations. Department stores compete with Shinsegae and Hyundai Department Store for traffic at large stores, while marts compete with E-Mart. A change in the competitive environment could affect same-store growth in both formats.

Financial and earnings stability

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 21 trillion, compared with total equity of KRW 16.9 trillion. Earnings have not been stable enough, as shown by the KRW 968 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in 2024. Even with the lower debt-to-equity ratio, a return to large losses could raise financial strain.

Overall view

Lotte Shopping's revenue fell for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, yet its operating margin rose from 2.5% in 2022 to 4.0% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders alternated between profit in 2023 and 2025 and a loss in 2024, and the debt-to-equity ratio at the end of 2025 was 124.8%, below the 2022 level.

In the third quarter of 2026, department store sales reached a record for a third quarter and mart operating profit rose, lifting quarterly operating profit to KRW 175.6 billion.

Still, the department store unit accounts for 64.3% of operating profit, and part of the mart improvement reflects temporary factors such as the consumer coupon base effect and the spillover from Homeplus's business suspension.

The e-commerce subsidiary continued to post operating losses, and the electronics retail subsidiary's profit declined. Quarterly profit has also swung sharply, as in the second quarter of 2026, so whether results after the fourth quarter sustain the third-quarter recovery is the central thing to track.
