02

Business structure

Handsome, founded in 1987 and listed on KOSPI in 1996, is a women's-wear-centered fashion company within the Hyundai Department Store Group. Its core own brands include TIME, TIME Homme, SYSTEM, MINE and SJSJ. Own brands made up 70.7% of sales in the third quarter of 2026, up slightly from 70.2% a year earlier.

The rest comes from imported brands such as Lanvin and Tommy Hilfiger, and the import portfolio has widened to include SKIMS and Satisfy. By mix, garment products accounted for 71%, merchandise for 28% and garment commissions for 1% in the third quarter of 2025.

Offline channels dominate, at 77.6% of sales in the third quarter of 2026. The company runs 1,332 domestic offline stores and three online platforms. The heavy own-brand mix also raises raw material and manufacturing cost exposure.

Management is pursuing a two-track approach that combines building own brands with expanding distribution of overseas brands. Peers in the sector include F&F, LF and Shinsegae International, with competition centered on department store placement and brand portfolios.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show sluggish sales alongside a falling margin. Sales went from KRW 1.54 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.53 trillion in 2023 (down 0.9%) and KRW 1.49 trillion in 2024 (down 2.8%), before rising 0.4% to KRW 1.49 trillion in 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 168.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 100.5 billion in 2023 (down 40.3%), KRW 63.5 billion in 2024 (down 36.8%) and KRW 52.2 billion in 2025 (down 17.8%), a decline for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest across 2022 to 2025.

The operating margin slid from 10.9% in 2022 to 6.6%, 4.3% and 3.5% in 2025. Margin erosion outpaced sales declines, consistent with cost of sales rising as a share of revenue over the period. Management cited higher raw material prices from a strong dollar and rising labor costs.

Controlling-shareholder net profit fell from KRW 123 billion in 2022 to KRW 81 billion in 2023 and KRW 44.6 billion in 2024, then rose 3.6% to KRW 46.2 billion in 2025. Net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 46.2 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow rose to KRW 102 billion in 2024 and KRW 116.2 billion in 2025, moving opposite to operating profit.

Quarterly sales peaked at KRW 463.7 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (up 49.8% from the prior quarter) and then declined for three straight quarters to KRW 324.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Operating profit moved from KRW 2.47 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 27.2 billion in the fourth quarter and KRW 36.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026, dropped to KRW 4.61 billion in the second quarter and recovered to KRW 7.57 billion in the third quarter.

An analyst attributed third-quarter margin support to full-price sales improving from September after older-season inventory was cleared through August. Controlling-shareholder net profit over the last four quarters totals KRW 62 billion. Quarterly swings are large, so annual cumulative trends give the clearer picture.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic fashion demand showed signs of recovery starting in the fourth quarter of 2025. Heungkuk Securities judged that household spending on fashion is gradually shifting back to recovery. Another analysis held that domestic apparel consumption has begun recovering from three years of weakness.

Behind this is a view that rising asset prices are lifting household disposable income. In the third quarter of 2026, sales of transitional-season apparel were strong. In September, when the fall-winter season began, sales returned to double-digit growth, according to one analysis.

On the inventory side, merchandise inventory fell about 8% from a year earlier, which was cited as support for profitability. Handsome relies on department store channels, and management said that department store traffic drove second-quarter results.

Peers such as F&F and LF compete in similar channels, with brand portfolio mix and overseas distribution strategy as the main points of differentiation. Whether the sector recovery translates into margins will show up in cost of sales and full-price sell-through trends.

The direction of the apparel market depends on household income and asset effects, so macro indicators are worth reading alongside company data.

06

Outlook

Management plans to diversify its imported brand portfolio and strengthen the global competitiveness of its own brands TIME and SYSTEM. On November 1, 2026, the company signed an exclusive Japan distribution agreement for SYSTEM Paris with a Japanese consortium.

Beyond the recently launched imported brands, it is pursuing further overseas brand introductions. The shareholder return framework is a plan to buy back KRW 30 billion of shares over 2026 to 2028, at KRW 10 billion per year, and cancel them in full.

Open-market purchases ran from September 21 to October 22, 2026, with all acquired shares slated for cancellation. The dividend funding base was raised to at least 20% of standalone operating profit for 2026 to 2028.

Hanwha Investment & Securities maintained a BUY rating and a target price of KRW 36,000 in a report dated November 3, 2026. In a September 2026 report, Heungkuk Securities projected full-year 2026 operating profit at KRW 92.1 billion.

Third-quarter cumulative operating profit was KRW 48.7 billion on a preliminary basis, announced November 2, 2026, so the gap to the full-year estimate depends on how much the fourth quarter contributes.

08

Bull factors

A clear direction of margin improvement

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 7.57 billion rose 206.1% year on year, and the operating margin climbed from 0.8% to 2.3%. Own-brand sales grew 5.6%, ahead of the 3.2% growth in imported brands. Analysts attributed the gain to better full-price sell-through alongside lower selling and admin expenses.

Specific shareholder return commitments

Handsome plans to buy back and cancel KRW 30 billion of shares over 2026 to 2028, KRW 10 billion per year. The dividend funding base was raised from at least 15% to at least 20% of standalone operating profit. Analysts also highlighted supply-demand effects during the buyback period.

Expanding brand portfolio

Handsome launched the imported brands SKIMS and Satisfy and is pursuing more overseas brands. It signed an exclusive Japan distribution deal for SYSTEM Paris on November 1, 2026. TIME was listed on the official Paris Fashion Week calendar, which some see as a milestone for global expansion.

09

Bear factors

Three years of decline and a thin margin

Operating profit fell for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and the 2025 operating margin was 3.5%, compared with 10.9% in 2022. One analysis noted that third-quarter 2026 operating profit came in below the market consensus of KRW 8.7 billion.

Cost burden of own brands

Because own brands make up a large share of sales, raw material and manufacturing costs are seen as higher than at peers. Cost of sales as a share of revenue rose from 38.7% in 2022 to 41.4% in the first nine months of 2024. Exchange-rate-driven raw material costs remain a swing factor.

Offline dependence and consumer sensitivity

Offline channels generated 77.6% of third-quarter 2026 sales, so results track department store traffic and are exposed to consumer slowdowns. Online sales grew only 1.8% in the same quarter, indicating a slow pace of channel diversification.

10

Risk factors

Inventory and full-price sell-through

Clearing older-season inventory adds cost pressure, and weaker full-price sell-through would squeeze margins again. In the third quarter of 2026, costs weighed through August and full-price sales from September protected profitability. A weak start to the season could widen fourth-quarter swings.

Exchange rates and cost structure

Management cited exchange-driven raw material price increases and higher labor costs as cost pressures. With a high share of own production, currency and wage shifts flow directly into margins. Without lower cost of sales, sales growth alone may limit margin recovery.

Dependence on imported brands and overseas expansion

Revenue from imported brands such as Lanvin and Tommy Hilfiger depends on contract terms and brand popularity. Overseas projects such as the Japan distribution deal are at an early stage, and their revenue contribution is still limited. If overseas expansion slips, expectations placed on domestic brands could turn into a burden.

12

Overall view

Handsome's operating profit fell for three straight years from 2023 to 2025 before rising 206.1% in the third quarter of 2026, a sign of recovery. With own brands at 70.7% of sales, full-price sell-through and cost of sales drive margins.

The 2025 operating margin of 3.5% remains below 10.9% in 2022, so profitability is still rebuilding. On shareholder returns, the company has set a KRW 30 billion buyback and cancellation plan for 2026 to 2028 and raised the dividend funding base.

Expansion of imported brands and the Japan distribution deal are growth options, but their revenue contribution is not yet visible. Bullish factors are the profit rebound and shareholder returns, while bearish factors are cost pressure and offline dependence.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
