Report overview

Ad Core Recovery Is the Key After Turnaround

Mobidays' revenue has risen for three straight years since 2023, and operating profit and profit attributable to owners of the parent returned to the black in 2025. However, revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to owners all fell year on year in Q3 2026, so whether the core ad business keeps recovering is the next checkpoint.

02

Business structure

Mobidays calls itself a comprehensive media and content group, and its core business is data-driven digital marketing and ad technology (adtech). Built on performance marketing aimed at raising ad efficiency, it has widened into content, games and commerce.

The ad business depends on partnerships with major domestic and global platforms including NAVER, Google and TikTok. In an August 2025 press release, the company said it had been named a Google Premier Partner for four consecutive years.

At its February 2025 results announcement, the company said annual transaction volume in its ad business was maintained at roughly KRW 300 billion.

Subsidiaries include Mobicontents Tech, which operates the K-content global commerce platform COKODIVE and is expanding into B2B and new businesses, a game publishing unit, an education unit and a marketing unit. Mobicontents Tech is also preparing six to seven video IP titles.

The ad division is moving from a traditional agency model toward AI-based operating automation, performance analysis and AEO and GEO consulting for generative AI search. It competes for advertiser budgets with other ad agencies and adtech firms. Content and game revenue can vary sharply depending on how titles perform.

04

Earnings analysis

The annual figures show revenue growth and profit recovery arriving together. Revenue rose from KRW 16.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 23.9 billion in 2023 (up 48.8% year on year), KRW 37.2 billion in 2024 (up 55.8%) and KRW 44.4 billion in 2025 (up 19.1%).

Revenue grew for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period. The company described 2024 revenue as its best performance since its founding. The operating margin went from 28.6% in 2022 to -8.0% in 2023 and 0.8% in 2024, then recovered to 10.2% in 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 4.59 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 1.9 billion in 2023, returned to operating profit of KRW 315 million in 2024, and rose to KRW 4.51 billion in 2025 (up 1,330.6% year on year).

In the first half of 2025, the company attributed results to performance-focused campaign operations, internal efficiency gains and wins in large brand campaigns.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was a net loss of KRW 6.67 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 915 million in 2023 (losses narrowed), a net loss of KRW 1.71 billion in 2024 (losses widened), and turned to a profit of KRW 3.1 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 5.36 billion in 2022, -KRW 17.7 billion in 2023, -KRW 2.35 billion in 2024 and KRW 22.7 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 113.0% to 128.9%, 151.4% and 157.3% over the same period. Quarterly results were volatile.

Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 13 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.58 billion, then Q4 2025 revenue fell to KRW 8.08 billion (down 37.8% from the prior quarter) and an operating loss of KRW 222 million followed.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 9.56 billion (up 18.4% from the prior quarter) with an operating loss of KRW 167 million. Q2 2026 revenue rose to KRW 13 billion (up 36.1%) and operating profit of KRW 2.89 billion turned the quarter positive.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 10.7 billion (down 17.3% year on year), operating profit was KRW 1.44 billion (down 50.4% from the prior quarter and down 9.3% year on year), and profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 1.03 billion (down 30.9% year on year).

The company cited ad operating efficiency, expanded campaign execution, growth in content and IP commerce, and cost management as the drivers of the second-quarter improvement. Profit attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, totals KRW 1.25 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Advertisers are shifting budgets toward performance marketing and prioritizing efficient ad spending, and analysts describe overall ad-market growth as limited. According to the company, the ad industry as a whole was pressured by an economic slowdown in the first half of 2025.

In this setting, the ability to prove ad performance becomes central to how campaigns are bought. Some analysts also see AI-based personalized short-form video ads emerging as a new growth engine in digital advertising.

Mobidays is strengthening AI-based operating automation and performance analysis to respond to this shift. On the content side, the company cited the expansion of global K-POP fandom when explaining its 2025 improvement, which links to the demand base for K-content commerce platforms such as COKODIVE.

In terms of positioning, the company is a small-to-mid-sized ad group that sits between large platform operators and ad agencies, emphasizing data and adtech capabilities and partnerships with several platforms.

Its scale is far smaller than that of large platform companies, so how quickly it responds to platform policy changes is a variable for its competitiveness.

06

Outlook

The company has outlined three directions for the second half. First, in its ad and marketing division it plans to deepen AI-based operating automation and performance analysis and expand AEO and GEO consulting and solutions for generative AI search.

Second, it intends to strengthen cooperation with global platforms such as Google and with partners to shift toward a higher-value business structure centered on AI transformation (AX). Third, it aims to grow its content, IP and global businesses.

Mobicontents Tech is continuing its B2C business while expanding into B2B and new businesses, and it is pursuing a Konex listing within the year.

In video IP, following the film 'Sentimental Value', it is preparing six to seven titles, including 'De Gaulle' and 'The Invite', which are scheduled for release and distribution in the second half. Mobigames plans to launch several new games in the second half.

These plans are directional and timing-based, so whether they translate into results will show up in quarterly revenue mix and operating profit. Revenue fell to KRW 8.08 billion in Q4 2025, so quarterly volatility is a gauge of how quickly the plans can be executed.

With revenue and operating profit both down year on year in Q3 2026, the key next checkpoint is how the second-half transition shows up in Q4 results.

08

Bull factors

Earnings recovery on a profitable base

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 4.51 billion, up 1,330.6% year on year, and operating cash flow that year was KRW 22.7 billion. Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.89 billion turned the quarter positive.

Profit attributable to owners turned to a KRW 3.1 billion profit in 2025, which supports the case for earnings recovery.

Expansion in content and commerce

Mobicontents Tech operates the K-content global commerce platform COKODIVE. The company cited growth in content and IP commerce as a driver of the Q2 2026 improvement. Mobigames reached break-even in Q1 2025 on the domestic success of an idle RPG, according to the company's announcement at the time.

AI transition and a new ad growth vector

Some analysts say AI-based personalized short-form video ads are emerging as a new growth engine in digital advertising. The company has set AI-based operating automation and AEO and GEO solutions as its second-half direction. If the ad division executes this shift, its revenue mix and profitability could change.

09

Bear factors

Profit decline in Q3 2026

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 10.7 billion, down 17.3% year on year. Operating profit of KRW 1.44 billion fell 9.3% year on year, and profit attributable to owners of KRW 1.03 billion fell 30.9%. Q1 2026 posted an operating loss of KRW 167 million, showing how wide quarterly swings can be.

Higher debt burden

The debt-to-equity ratio climbed from 113.0% in 2022 to 157.3% in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 77 billion, above total equity of KRW 49 billion. Operating cash flow was -KRW 17.7 billion in 2023 and -KRW 2.35 billion in 2024.

Slow ad market and platform dependence

Analysts note that limited growth in the ad market is tied to companies shifting toward performance marketing. The company also said the ad industry was squeezed by an economic slowdown in the first half of 2025.

Because its main media partners are platforms such as NAVER, Google and TikTok, policy changes could have a large effect.

10

Risk factors

Ad cycle and platform policy

If the ad market grows slowly and advertisers focus on efficiency, both ad rates and volumes could be affected. Because the main channels are platforms, a change in their policies or fee structures could hit revenue directly. Q1 2026 revenue had already fallen 13.9% year on year.

Leverage and cash flow

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 157.3% in 2025 from 113.0% in 2022. Operating cash flow was negative in 2023 and 2024 before turning to KRW 22.7 billion in 2025. Whether that one-year recovery continues needs confirmation in future periodic reports.

Volatility in content and games

Video IP and game revenue can shift sharply with title performance. Q4 2025 revenue fell 37.8% from the prior quarter, an example of quarterly swings. The results of the second-half film releases and new game launches are not yet known.

Overall view

Mobidays' revenue has grown for three straight years since 2023, and in 2025 operating profit and profit attributable to owners turned positive. Q2 2026 operating profit turned the quarter positive.

However, revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to owners all fell year on year in Q3 2026, and profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters totals only KRW 1.25 billion.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 113.0% in 2022 to 157.3% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 77 billion above total equity of KRW 49 billion. Supporting factors include the earnings recovery, the positive operating cash flow in 2025, and the shift toward content, commerce and AI-based ad services.

Offsetting factors include limited ad market growth, platform dependence, quarterly volatility and financial leverage. The share price sits below book value, but the small earnings base makes it sensitive to quarterly swings.

The next checkpoints are the second-half film releases, the planned Konex listing within 2026, and the fourth-quarter results and debt metrics in the 2026 annual report.
