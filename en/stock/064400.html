Report overview

Finance and AI infrastructure drive growth as margins approach 9%

LG CNS posted three straight years of growth in revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders from 2023 to 2025. In the third quarter of 2026 it raised its full-year revenue growth guidance on finance projects and cloud and AI sales, while profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell in that quarter.

  1. 1

    Revenue reached KRW 6.13 trillion and operating profit KRW 551.8 billion in 2025, the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Revenue growth, however, slowed from 12.8% in 2023 to 2.5% in 2025.

  2. 2

    Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 1.66 trillion, up 9.3% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 138.2 billion, up 15.0%. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 21.4% to KRW 82.4 billion.

  3. 3

    Kyobo Securities projects revenue growth of 7.8% in 2026 and 8.7% in 2027, citing large finance next-generation system orders and corporate AX and AI infrastructure spending as the drivers.

  4. 4

    Bearish factors include weaker affiliate investment that hit the smart engineering segment, lower profit after the currency move, and macroeconomic uncertainty. Bullish factors are finance next-generation system wins and growth in cloud and AI sales.

  5. 5

    The AI data center business is widening through a colocation and outsourced operation contract disclosed in April 2026 and an entry into GPU cloud services (GPUaaS). Brokers have noted, however, that the DBO-centered structure carries lower profitability and has weighed on valuation relative to other AI data center operators.

02

Business structure

LG CNS supplies IT services to finance, public sector, manufacturing, logistics and other industries, and its business is split into three segments: Digital Business Services (DBS), Cloud & AI, and Smart Engineering.

DBS centers on building next-generation systems for financial institutions, and its revenue rose 21.3% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, according to an LS Securities report dated November 3, 2026.

Cloud & AI is built around AX projects on the AgenticWorks AI platform and data center and cloud operations, and it grew 9.3% year on year in the same quarter. Smart Engineering depends heavily on affiliate volume; it fell 3.9% in the same quarter, which the broker linked to a slowdown at affiliates.

Shinhan Investment & Securities said in a January 2026 report that non-captive revenue accounts for roughly 40%, above the 20 to 30% industry average. According to Yuanta Securities' tally, first-quarter 2026 Cloud & AI revenue was KRW 765.4 billion, up 6.7% year on year.

The client base spans finance, public sector, manufacturing, logistics and IT, which LS Securities describes as diversified. In robotics, the company plans total robot services built on its PhysicalWorks platform.

Among listed peers, it is compared with domestic IT services firms that have finance system experience, including Samsung SDS and Hyundai Autoever.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue rose from KRW 4.97 trillion in 2022 to KRW 6.13 trillion in 2025. Growth was KRW 5.61 trillion in 2023 (up 12.8%), KRW 5.98 trillion in 2024 (up 6.7%), and then slowed to 2.5% in 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 385.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 551.8 billion in 2025, increasing for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, while the operating margin climbed from 7.8% to 9.0% over the same period. Margin expansion while revenue growth slowed shows that profitability improved ahead of top-line expansion.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 265.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 437.9 billion in 2025, including a 20.1% gain in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 439.2 billion in 2025, close to the controlling-shareholder figure.

Operating cash flow peaked at KRW 715.4 billion in 2024 and fell to KRW 418.2 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 137.0% in 2022 to 79.7% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 2.94 trillion and total liabilities of KRW 2.34 trillion at year-end 2025.

Quarterly revenue peaked at KRW 1.94 trillion and operating profit at KRW 212 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, which Shinhan Investment & Securities attributed to a strong peak-season quarter. The first quarter of 2026 was the low point, with revenue of KRW 1.31 trillion and operating profit of KRW 94.2 billion.

Revenue then rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 1.52 trillion in the second quarter and KRW 1.66 trillion in the third, and operating profit rose to KRW 127.9 billion and KRW 138.2 billion.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 21.4% year on year to KRW 82.4 billion in the third quarter, which LS Securities linked to a currency move. The last four quarters together total KRW 458.9 billion in profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic IT services market is in a phase where finance next-generation system replacement demand and corporate AX (AI transformation) spending are supporting each other.

Kyobo Securities analyst Kim Dong-woo said large finance next-generation system orders are continuing into the fourth quarter and that AX and AI infrastructure investment is expanding. The same report judged that the finance next-generation project cycle is resuming.

In data centers, Kyobo noted that in Asia Pacific, including Korea, supply lags demand and cloud service providers are investing quickly.

On competitive position, the higher share of non-captive revenue versus the peer average has been cited as a differentiator, and Shinhan Investment & Securities sees the structure as favorable for entry into new industries such as defense and shipbuilding and for expansion of public projects.

Global collaboration is also widening: Kyobo highlighted growth in MSP business through partnerships with Palantir and OpenAI. Reports have also covered discussions on NVIDIA cooperation at the LG Group level. Macroeconomic uncertainty and weaker affiliate investment were presented as short-term burdens. Reading this business requires watching how investment in the end markets it serves evolves.

06

Outlook

The company's full-year revenue growth guidance was raised after the third-quarter 2026 results, from the mid-single-digit range to the high-single-digit range, according to LS Securities. Kyobo Securities expects revenue growth of 7.8% in 2026.

In a May 2026 report, Kyobo projected that DBS revenue would grow 11.0% and Smart Engineering revenue 9.8% in 2026, and that Cloud & AI growth would accelerate in earnest in 2027.

Its reasons include the ramp-up of AgenticWorks-based projects, the data center colocation and outsourced operation contract disclosed in April 2026, and MSP expansion with Palantir and OpenAI.

LS Securities said the first phase of the AIDC project will be completed within the year and that its data center space has already been largely pre-sold, with the next phase expected to move quickly using modular data centers.

Miraeasset Securities said in a September 2026 report that the company had decided on KRW 381.0 billion of investment in GPUs and infrastructure for an AI factory, and that its profit leverage as an AI data center operator would become clearer as GPUaaS plans take shape.

In the public sector, reports said the LG AI Research consortium advanced to the second round of evaluation for a national foundation AI model project, and the outcome could affect demand for AI solutions and public orders.

08

Bull factors

Finance next-generation and AI infrastructure demand

DBS revenue rose 21.3% year on year in the third quarter of 2026. Kyobo Securities projects revenue growth of 8.7% in 2027, based on large finance next-generation orders continuing into the fourth quarter.

Guidance raised and profit recovery

LS Securities reported that full-year revenue growth guidance was raised from the mid-single-digit to the high-single-digit range. Operating profit rose in the second and third quarters of 2026 after the first-quarter low, and the operating margin reached 9.0% in 2025, up from 7.8% in 2022.

Expansion of AI data centers and GPU cloud

The data center colocation and outsourced operation contract disclosed in April 2026 is in its ramp-up phase. LS Securities said the first phase of the AIDC project will be completed within the year with its space largely pre-sold. Miraeasset Securities reported a KRW 381.0 billion decision to invest in GPUs and infrastructure.

09

Bear factors

Decline in profit attributable to controlling shareholders

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 21.4% year on year to KRW 82.4 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 458.9 billion.

LS Securities linked the decline to currency movements, and operating profit growth and net profit decline appearing in the same quarter is a bearish point.

Slower affiliate investment and weaker Smart Engineering

Smart Engineering revenue fell 3.9% year on year in the third quarter of 2026. LS Securities pointed to a slowdown at affiliates and described weaker affiliate investment as a short-term burden. Dependence on group affiliate volume can add to earnings volatility.

Lower-margin structure and valuation constraints

Miraeasset Securities explained that the DBO-centered structure carries relatively lower profitability, which has earned lower valuations than other AI data center operators. Revenue growth slowed from 12.8% in 2023 to 2.5% in 2025, and slower top-line expansion could limit earnings leverage.

10

Risk factors

Affiliate dependence risk

Smart Engineering revenue depends on affiliate investment and conditions at group companies. Revenue declined in the third quarter of 2026 amid an affiliate slowdown. A more diversified external customer base partly offset the drop, but affiliate investment flows remain a source of short-term earnings volatility.

Investment burden and business structure risk

A decision to invest KRW 381.0 billion in GPUs and infrastructure has raised the investment burden. The payback timing will depend on how quickly GPUaaS plans take shape, and the DBO-centered business structure could limit the pace of margin improvement.

Macroeconomic and currency risk

LS Securities identified macroeconomic uncertainty as a short-term burden. The decline in third-quarter 2026 profit attributable to controlling shareholders was linked to a currency move. If corporate IT spending adjusts with the economic cycle, the timing of project awards and revenue recognition could shift.

12

Overall view

LG CNS grew revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, and its operating margin rose from 7.8% in 2022 to 9.0% in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 137.0% to 79.7% over the same period.

In the third quarter of 2026, DBS revenue rose 21.3% and full-year revenue growth guidance was raised. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders, however, fell 21.4% to KRW 82.4 billion in that quarter, and Smart Engineering revenue fell 3.9%.

Growth is shifting toward finance next-generation projects and AI data center and GPU cloud businesses, and some observers note that these businesses still carry the limits of a DBO-centered structure.

Brokers have presented target prices of KRW 111,000 from Yuanta Securities (June 2026), KRW 100,000 from Miraeasset Securities (September 2026), and KRW 101,000 from LS Securities (November 2026), but these are each broker's judgments.

Going forward, the points to check are AIDC first-phase completion, the full-year results in the 2026 annual report, and whether the first-quarter 2027 preliminary results align with the raised guidance and the trend in profit attributable to controlling shareholders.
