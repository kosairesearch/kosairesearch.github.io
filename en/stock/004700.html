02

Business structure

Chokwang Leather was founded in 1936 and makes leather materials for car seats, shoes, handbags and furniture. A press report said leather materials and car seat cut parts account for 97.98% of total sales. That figure reflects the report's timing, so the latest segment mix may differ.

Its main products are leather fabrics for car seats, shoes and handbags. Car seat materials are a large share of sales, which links results indirectly to vehicle production. More than 80% of raw and sub-materials are imported, procured through direct trade with hide suppliers.

Hide prices and exchange rates therefore pass directly into costs. By-products and materials account for only about 1% of sales. Customers span shoe and bag makers and the automotive seat supply chain.

In a late-2022 press assessment, the company was said to have slipped from the top of Korea's leather industry to around third place. Peers in the same sector such as Youngone, Hansae and Hyosung TNC differ in product stage and customer mix, which limits direct comparison.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 122 billion in 2022 to KRW 126.6 billion in 2023 (up 3.7% year on year), fell to KRW 90.2 billion in 2024 (down 28.7%), and recovered to KRW 97.9 billion in 2025 (up 8.5%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year for revenue and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 508 million in 2022 to KRW 6.34 billion in 2023 (up 1,150.0%), KRW 7.23 billion in 2024 (up 13.9%) and KRW 8.79 billion in 2025 (up 21.6%), rising three years in a row from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit grew in 2024 even as revenue fell, and the operating margin climbed from 0.4% in 2022 to 5.0%, 8.0% and 9.0% in 2025. That pattern points to margin gains doing more of the work than sales volume.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 6.06 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.05 billion in 2023 (up 49.2%) and KRW 12.6 billion in 2024 (up 39.3%), then fell to KRW 11.5 billion in 2025 (down 8.4%).

In 2025 operating profit rose while controlling-shareholder net profit fell, so the two moved in opposite directions. Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts across 2022 to 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 15.7 billion in 2022, KRW 8.18 billion in 2023, KRW 32.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 11.9 billion in 2025, with wide swings year to year.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 25.4 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 20.3 billion in Q4 2025 (down 20.2% from the prior quarter), recovered slightly to KRW 20.9 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 21.3 billion in Q2 2026, then fell to KRW 18.7 billion in Q3 2026 (down 12.0% from the prior quarter).

Quarterly operating profit fell from KRW 2.32 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 2.61 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 1.6 billion in Q1 2026 (down 38.6% from the prior quarter), then turned to an operating loss of KRW 1.45 billion in Q2 2026. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 451 million was down 80.6% year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 3.35 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 587 million in Q2 2026 and a net loss of KRW 552 million in Q3 2026. In Q3, revenue fell 26.2% year on year, and the drop in operating profit was steeper than the drop in revenue.

Over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026), net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 6.35 billion.

05

Industry analysis

End demand comes from car seat materials and fashion materials for shoes and handbags. Both markets follow finished-goods production and consumer spending, so orders move with those cycles. Car seat demand tracks vehicle output, while fashion demand tracks shoe and bag consumption.

If the two do not move together, quarterly revenue can swing. Because more than 80% of raw and sub-materials are imported, exchange rates and hide prices affect profitability independently of the industry cycle.

A late-2022 press assessment said the company had slipped from first place in the domestic leather industry to around third. Competition centers on fabric markets where Chinese and South American producers are active.

The Q3 2026 revenue decline may reflect weaker end demand, which is worth checking against the next quarters. Because car seat cut parts are a large share of sales, production adjustments by automakers flow directly into results.

Cycle position is best judged by looking at vehicle production and fashion consumption indicators together. Peers such as Youngone, Hansae and Hyosung TNC work at different product stages, so direct industry comparison is limited.

Bull factors

Profitability trend

The operating margin rose from 0.4% in 2022 to 5.0% in 2023, 8.0% in 2024 and 9.0% in 2025. Operating profit rose three years in a row from 2023 through 2025, reaching KRW 8.79 billion in 2025. Margins improved even though revenue fell 28.7% in 2024, which supports the case for a better cost structure.

Balance sheet capacity

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 84.1 billion and the debt ratio was 14.6%. All KRW 577.9 billion of total equity is attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow stayed positive at KRW 11.9 billion in 2025. With a light debt load, financial risk is relatively contained if the industry slows.

Shareholder return debate

Treasury shares were reported at about 46.57% of issued stock in early 2026. The 10%-plus shareholder has pressed for cancellation and higher dividends. The company received approval at its shareholder meeting for a treasury share holding and disposal plan.

If cancellation or a higher payout were carried out, it would be a shareholder-return change, which is the bullish argument here.

Shrinking earnings base

The bearish case is that operating gains have not carried through to the controlling shareholders' share of profit.

Governance disputes

Two outside directors resigned in March 2026, leaving three registered directors. The auditor appointment passed with 53.7% approval among shares voting. Alongside the regulatory referral the company disclosed and the litigation outcome, governance uncertainty remains.

Risk factors

Raw materials and FX

More than 80% of raw and sub-materials are imported and sourced directly from hide suppliers. Higher hide prices or a weaker won flow straight into costs and can swing quarterly margins. Quarterly operating profit has already moved from KRW 1.6 billion in Q1 2026 to a loss in Q2.

Slowing end demand

Most sales come from car seat cut parts and materials for shoes and handbags. Q3 2026 revenue fell 26.2% year on year, which may reflect weaker end demand. A slowdown in vehicle output or fashion spending would feed directly into lower orders and sales.

Governance and stake changes

The 10%-plus holder's stake stood at 10.12% as of October 19, 2026, after 50,000 shares were sold on the market on October 26. The company denies the related-party allegation and says the KRW 125 billion damage claimed by minority shareholders is untrue. While the disputes continue, decisions and shareholder return policy remain uncertain.
