02

Business structure

Watoscoreaco manufactures and sells plastic fittings used in sanitary ceramics, and it listed on the KOSDAQ market in November 2005. It started with the internal fittings of toilet tanks and has since widened into other bathroom products such as basin pop-up drains, toilet seats and shower heads.

According to a revenue breakdown that JobKorea compiled from NICE Information Service data, toilet fittings account for 47% of sales, other toilet-related fittings for 15% and basin and trap products for 10%, with the rest classed as other.

The two toilet-related lines together exceed 60% of sales, so dependence on one product family is high. Company profile data describe the industry as sensitive to domestic construction conditions, with results driven by residential construction orders and new housing move-in volumes.

The company had 90 employees at the end of December 2025 and is based in Jangseong County in South Jeolla Province. In its value-up plan decided on March 26, 2026, it set a goal of becoming the top domestic seller of toilet components by market share.

The largest shareholder changed in April 2024, and a filing dated March 30, 2026 shows the board of representative directors shrinking from three members to two. Market share by competitor and a list of major customers could not be confirmed from public sources in this research.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue declined from KRW 19.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.7 billion in 2023 (down 9.4%), KRW 17.5 billion in 2024 (down 1.0%) and KRW 14.1 billion in 2025 (down 19.3%), a decline for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Operating income of KRW 137 million in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 90.31 million in 2023, followed by losses of KRW 247 million in 2024 and KRW 4.32 billion in 2025, so the company has been loss-making for three consecutive years with losses widening.

The operating margin fell from 0.7% in 2022 to -1.4% in 2024 and -30.6% in 2025. In its February 13, 2026 filing on a standalone basis, the company cited lower revenue and operating profit amid an economic downturn as the main cause.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.31 billion in 2022, KRW 2.67 billion in 2023 and KRW 2.07 billion in 2024, then swung to a net loss of KRW 2.74 billion in 2025; with no non-controlling interests, net income follows the same path.

Financial statements that JobKorea compiled from NICE Information Service data show non-operating income in the mid-KRW 2 billion to low-KRW 3 billion range each year from 2023 to 2025, which suggests that earnings in 2022 to 2024 rested on non-operating items rather than the core business.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 4.49 billion in 2024 to negative KRW 519 million in 2025. Quarterly revenue ranged from KRW 3.33 billion in the second quarter of 2025 to KRW 3.58 billion, KRW 3.48 billion, KRW 3.02 billion and KRW 3.4 billion in the following four quarters.

The operating loss jumped to KRW 2.64 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.85 billion), then eased to KRW 834 million in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 480 million in the second quarter, making five consecutive loss-making quarters from the second quarter of 2025.

The second-quarter 2026 operating loss was wider than a year earlier, while the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 26.39 million was smaller.

The specific cause of the fourth-quarter 2025 loss could not be confirmed in this research, and the notes to the filings from the third quarter onward are the place to check.

05

Industry analysis

The company is classified under chemicals, but its business tracks the housing construction cycle more closely than the raw material cycles of large chemical makers such as LG Chem or Lotte Chemical.

A report dated October 18, 2026 citing the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport said nationwide housing starts in January to August 2026 reached 156,396 units, up 10.8% year on year, with apartment starts up 14.1%. The same report said housing starts in Seoul rose 41.1%.

At its first regular housing supply briefing on October 16, 2026, the government set a 2026 target of 268,000 housing starts in the Seoul metropolitan area.

By contrast, a 2026 outlook in the Construction Policy Journal (RICON) judged that the worst of weak private construction investment had passed, but said building activity remained very weak outside the capital region and a visible recovery looked unlikely.

Move-in volumes that the Korea Real Estate Board and R114 published on August 31, 2026 are 100,773 units for the second half of 2026, 226,953 for 2027 and 97,686 for the first half of 2028, concentrated in 2027.

Because sanitary fittings are installed at the finishing stage of a building, there may be a lag between rising starts and revenue. A comparison of market share against competitors could not be confirmed from public sources.

Bull factors

Narrowing quarterly operating losses

The operating loss fell from KRW 2.64 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 834 million in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 480 million in the second quarter. Quarterly revenue rose 12.6% from the previous quarter, from KRW 3.02 billion to KRW 3.4 billion, between the first and second quarters of 2026.

The second-quarter 2026 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 26.39 million was smaller than a year earlier, so the loss narrowed sharply on a net basis. The third-quarter periodic report will show whether this trend continues.

Signs of a housing-start recovery

A report citing the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport said nationwide housing starts in January to August 2026 rose 10.8% year on year. Since the company's results depend on new housing move-in volumes, rising starts and move-in volumes concentrated in 2027 are favorable for its delivery environment.

The company has also said it will respond so as to benefit when construction recovers. The lag between starts and deliveries still needs to be confirmed.

Thick financial cushion and shareholder returns

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 6.9%, with total liabilities of KRW 4.9 billion and total equity of KRW 71.1 billion. With no borrowing burden, short-term financial pressure is small even if operating losses continue.

The company decided on a year-end dividend and a capital-reduction dividend on February 13, 2026, and it reported the result of a trust-based treasury share purchase in May 2026. A willingness to return capital is confirmed, but its continuation is a separate matter.

Bear factors

Prolonged revenue decline and core-business losses

Revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and operating losses also ran for three consecutive years over the same period. The 2025 operating margin was -30.6%, and the operating loss has now run for five consecutive quarters.

The second-quarter 2026 operating loss was wider than a year earlier, so the sequential improvement has not yet carried over to a year-on-year improvement. As long as quarterly revenue stays in the mid-KRW 3 billion range, evidence for a return to breakeven remains thin.

Reliance on non-operating income and weaker cash flow

Net income in 2022 to 2024 owed much to non-operating income while operating income was close to zero. In 2025 operating cash flow turned to negative KRW 519 million and a net loss of KRW 2.74 billion attributable to controlling shareholders was recorded.

The longer the core business fails to generate cash, the greater the reliance on investment income and net assets.

Single product-family concentration and cycle lag

Toilet-related fittings exceed 60% of sales, so dependence on one product family is high. The Construction Policy Journal (RICON) pointed to weak building activity outside the capital region, and scheduled move-ins are relatively low in the second half of 2026. Even if starts statistics improve, deliveries may rise later because of the move-in lag.

Risk factors

Cycle and demand

Results are tied to residential construction orders and new housing move-ins, so a construction slowdown feeds directly into lower revenue. If increases in permits and starts prove temporary or concentrated in Seoul and the capital region, the delivery environment outside it may not change.

The government's target of 268,000 housing starts in the capital region is a target, and its achievement is not assured.

Earnings and accounting

An operating loss of KRW 2.64 billion and a net loss of KRW 2.85 billion attributable to controlling shareholders were booked together in the fourth quarter of 2025, but the specific cause could not be confirmed in this research. Whether the charge is one-off or repeatable changes how 2026 results should be read.

The notes to the periodic reports from the third quarter onward and the 2026 annual report must clarify the cause.

Overall view

Watoscoreaco is a construction-linked maker of plastic fittings for sanitary ceramics; its revenue fell for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025 and its 2025 operating margin dropped to -30.6%.

The operating loss narrowed from a peak of KRW 2.64 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 480 million in the second quarter of 2026, yet the company has posted five consecutive loss-making quarters from the second quarter of 2025 and the second-quarter 2026 loss was larger than a year earlier.

The bullish case rests on the financial cushion of a 6.9% debt ratio and KRW 71.1 billion in total equity, reports of rising housing starts, and shareholder returns through dividends and treasury share purchases.

The bearish case rests on prolonged core-business losses, reliance on non-operating income, operating cash flow of negative KRW 519 million in 2025, and a revenue mix concentrated in toilet-related fittings.

The cause of the fourth-quarter 2025 loss has not been confirmed, and neither a periodic report nor preliminary results for the third quarter of 2026 are available yet.

The gap between market capitalization and net assets and the high dividend yield are facts, but so are the points that the payout source was not earnings and that the timing of an operating normalization is uncertain.

Readers can form their own judgment after checking the third-quarter periodic report, housing starts and move-in indicators, and the dividend decision filings.
