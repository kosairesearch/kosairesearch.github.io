A Toilet-Parts Maker With Uneven Quarterly Losses

Watoscoreaco is a sanitary plastic parts maker centered on toilet fittings, and its sales have declined for three straight years while quarterly operating losses in 2026 narrowed and then widened again.

02

Business structure

Watoscoreaco makes sanitary plastic products, starting with water-supply control devices for toilet tanks and expanding into flange parts, sink traps and toilet seats. In the 2024 annual business report, toilet-related fittings accounted for 52.06% of KRW 17.5 billion in sales, roughly KRW 9.1 billion.

Sink and trap products made up 11.24%, other toilet fittings 11.86%, toilet seats 6.45%, other products 6.69%, and property rental only 0.85%. In the 2022 annual business report the toilet-fitting share was 59.97%, so the core product line's share has declined over two years.

Products are developed in-house and also made on an ODM (original design manufacturing) basis, with some sold under the WATOS brand. Sales move with sanitary-ware output, and the company attributed the 2025 decline to an economic slowdown.

Its registered patents cover variable trap structures, flush valve devices and bidet water-button structures.

The company states a goal of ranking first in domestic toilet-parts share, and its scale and product structure differ greatly from large chemical listings such as LG Chem or Hanwha Solutions, which limits direct comparison.

04

Earnings analysis

Under the DART annual reports, sales were KRW 19.5 billion in 2022, KRW 17.7 billion in 2023 (down 9.4% year on year), KRW 17.5 billion in 2024 (down 1.0%) and KRW 14.1 billion in 2025 (down 19.3%).

Sales have fallen for three consecutive years since 2023, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest in the 2022 to 2025 window.

Operating profit was KRW 137 million in 2022, turned to an operating loss of KRW 90.31 million in 2023, and then widened to an operating loss of KRW 247 million in 2024 and KRW 4.32 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 0.7% in 2022 to -0.5% in 2023, -1.4% in 2024 and -30.6% in 2025.

The 2024 financial statements show selling and administrative expenses easing slightly from KRW 4.90 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.70 billion in 2024, while gross profit fell from KRW 5.04 billion to KRW 4.45 billion, so the lost sales flowed straight into losses.

The company attributed the 2025 decline in sales and operating profit to an economic slowdown, according to a February 13, 2026 disclosure report.

Net income attributable to owners was KRW 5.31 billion in 2022, KRW 2.67 billion in 2023 (down 49.8%) and KRW 2.07 billion in 2024 (down 22.5%), then turned into a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 2.74 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 2.74 billion in 2025, and the 2022 to 2024 figures match the owner-attributable amounts.

Quarterly sales were KRW 3.58 billion in Q3 2025, with an operating loss of KRW 454 million, and KRW 3.48 billion in Q4 2025, with an operating loss that widened to KRW 2.64 billion. Sales were KRW 3.02 billion in Q1 2026 (down 13.2% quarter on quarter) and KRW 3.4 billion in Q2 2026 (up 12.6%).

Operating losses were KRW 834 million in Q1 2026 and KRW 480 million in Q2, then widened to KRW 773 million in Q3 2026, when sales were KRW 2.85 billion, down 20.4% year on year. Net losses attributable to owners over the last four quarters totaled KRW 3.39 billion, most of it from Q4 2025 at KRW 2.85 billion.

Operating cash flow stayed positive at KRW 3.12 billion in 2022, KRW 2.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 4.49 billion in 2024, then turned to -KRW 519 million in 2025. Because sales declines and wider losses have moved together, the key variable for the profit direction is whether the sales decline stops.

05

Industry analysis

The company's end market is linked to construction activity and sanitary-ware output. FnGuide's analysis of the first nine months of 2025 said lower sanitary-ware production reduced end-market demand and pushed product sales down.

The same analysis pointed to higher cost burdens that cut gross profit and widened operating losses. On the demand side, tighter water-saving policies are gradually expanding the market for water-saving fittings.

In its analysis of Q1 2026, FnGuide cited new water-saving products as a breakthrough amid a weak construction market. In a March 2026 disclosure, the company said it would keep developing products to benefit from any recovery in construction.

Jobkorea data show headcount fell from 113 in 2022 to 98 in 2025, and stood at 91 as of June 30, 2026. Quarterly sales in 2026 moved down in Q1, up in Q2 and down in Q3, so whether construction demand is turning up will need more quarters of data to judge.

On competition, the company targets first place in domestic toilet-parts share, so the pace of development for new products and structural changes will determine whether it can defend that position. Its scale and structure differ from large chemical companies, which limits direct industry comparisons.

06

Outlook

The value-enhancement plan the company disclosed on March 27, 2026 aims to hold a first-place domestic share in toilet parts by maintaining product competitiveness and responding to new products and structural changes.

In the same plan, the company said it would focus on its existing sanitary-ware parts business to keep sales stable. It also stated that it qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act, and shareholder returns are presented as one pillar of the plan.

On products, the disclosed material points to developing water-saving products such as water-saving flush valves to raise functionality and widen the business. According to FnGuide, Q1 2026 standalone sales fell 19.1% year on year and the operating loss rose 5.7%.

Quarterly sales in 2026 were KRW 3.02 billion in Q1, KRW 3.4 billion in Q2 and KRW 2.85 billion in Q3, rebounding in Q2 and then falling again. The operating loss was KRW 834 million in Q1, KRW 480 million in Q2 and KRW 773 million in Q3, smallest in Q2.

The first formal checkpoint for the plan is the 2026 annual business report, which will show how annual sales moved against the 2025 level of KRW 14.1 billion. Recovery in the operating margin from -30.6% in 2025 will show whether business indicators are improving.

The pace of construction recovery and the contribution of water-saving products to sales will determine whether the plan is realized.

08

Bull factors

A Low-Leverage Balance Sheet

Liabilities are light relative to equity, which leaves relatively more financial room even during a loss-making phase. The share price sitting in a low range against net asset value is also cited as a point in favor of asset value.

A Narrower Loss Phase in Q2 2026

The operating loss narrowed to KRW 480 million in Q2 2026 from KRW 834 million in Q1, and sales rose 12.6% quarter on quarter to KRW 3.4 billion. Compared with the KRW 2.64 billion operating loss in Q4 2025, the loss size in 2026 has been smaller. Whether this pattern holds after Q3 is the key checkpoint for the bullish case.

Water-Saving Product Demand

FnGuide's analysis points to a gradually expanding water-saving fittings market as water-saving policies tighten. The company says it is widening its business with water-saving flush valves and other new products.

Its stated goal of first place in domestic toilet-parts share signals a push to strengthen product competitiveness.

09

Bear factors

Three Straight Years of Sales Decline

Sales fell for three consecutive years, to KRW 17.7 billion in 2023, KRW 17.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.1 billion in 2025, with a 19.3% drop in 2025. Q3 2026 sales of KRW 2.85 billion were down 20.4% from a year earlier. Lower sanitary-ware production that reduced end-market demand is cited as an obstacle to a sales recovery.

Five Straight Loss Quarters

The 2025 operating loss reached KRW 4.32 billion, an operating margin of -30.6%, and operating losses have run for three straight years since 2023. The company has posted losses in five straight quarters from Q3 2025 through Q3 2026.

Net losses attributable to owners over the last four quarters totaled KRW 3.39 billion, and the Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 773 million was wider than a year earlier.

Operating Cash Flow Turned Negative

Operating cash flow moved from KRW 4.49 billion in 2024 to -KRW 519 million in 2025. This can be read as a sign that cash generation has weakened while losses continue.

If quarterly losses persist, as the Q3 2026 net loss attributable to owners of KRW 283 million suggests, the direction of this indicator becomes more important.

10

Risk factors

Dependence on End-Market Demand

Sales are tied to sanitary-ware output and construction activity, so a failure of end-market demand to recover could extend the sales decline. The company cited the economic slowdown as the reason for the 2025 decline. This structure depends on external variables the company cannot directly control.

Concentration in Products and Customers

Toilet-related fittings made up 52.06% of sales in the 2024 annual business report, so a demand drop in that product line would weigh heavily on total sales. A large part of production is made on an ODM basis, which exposes it to order changes from clients. The pace of the shift to new products is the variable that could reduce this risk.

Prolonged Losses and Capital Pressure

If five straight loss quarters extend further, equity could shrink and room for dividends and investment could tighten. The 2025 turn to negative operating cash flow compounds this risk. How many more quarters the loss narrowing lasts is the point to verify.

Overall view

Watoscoreaco is a sanitary plastic parts maker centered on toilet fittings, and its 2025 sales fell to KRW 14.1 billion while the operating loss widened to KRW 4.32 billion, a sign of weakening results. Sales have declined for three consecutive years since 2023, and operating losses have run for the same three years.

In 2026 the Q2 operating loss narrowed to KRW 480 million, but it widened again to KRW 773 million in Q3, extending a five-quarter loss streak. On the balance sheet, total equity of KRW 71.1 billion against KRW 4.9 billion of liabilities at the end of 2025 leaves relatively low leverage.

Operating cash flow, however, turned to -KRW 519 million in 2025. The bullish factors are financial room, a narrower loss phase in Q2 2026 and rising demand for water-saving products, while the bearish factors are weak end-market demand, a continuing sales decline and deteriorating cash flow.

The value-enhancement plan targets first place in domestic toilet-parts share and a focus on sanitary-ware parts, but a sales recovery to support it is not yet visible in the 2026 quarterly figures.
