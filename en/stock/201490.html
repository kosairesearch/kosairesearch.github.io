02

Business structure

ME2ON is a social casino game developer and operator founded in 2010, developing and running online social casino games built on social networks. It serves mobile and PC games and is headquartered in Seoul. The business has two pillars, games and content.

In 2025, the game division accounted for about 61% of total revenue and the content division for about 32%. The company described content rising above 30% of revenue as a shift from a game-centric model to an IP-based entertainment structure.

Its content business centers on subsidiary Ghost Studio, which preliminarily reported 2025 consolidated revenue of KRW 112.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 15.1 billion (disclosed February 27, 2026). In games, the company says its major social casino and casual titles remain solid.

In blockchain, the Ace Casino stablecoin casino is undergoing security and UX upgrades, and no revenue share for that unit has been disclosed. Kakao Games is listed as a major holder with 21.43% (as reported September 15, 2026). Within the sector, DoubleUGames, another social casino operator, is the closest comparison.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 112 billion in 2022 to KRW 109 billion in 2023 (down 2.7% YoY) and KRW 94.3 billion in 2024 (down 13.5%), before recovering to KRW 120.9 billion in 2025 (up 28.2%). Among 2022–2025, 2025 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

The company attributed 2025 growth to both games and content, with revenue from completed production of a Netflix series lifting the content division.

Operating profit went from KRW 27 billion in 2022 to KRW 22.1 billion in 2023 (down 18.1%) and KRW 11.8 billion in 2024 (down 46.6%), then edged up to KRW 12.6 billion in 2025 (up 6.8%). Operating margin fell from 24.1% to 10.4% over the same period.

The company credited AI-driven game operations automation and more efficient marketing creative production with lowering operating costs. Profit attributable to owners was KRW 8.66 billion in 2022, KRW 7.78 billion in 2023 (down 10.1%), KRW 551 million in 2024 (down 92.9%) and KRW 772 million in 2025 (up 40.1%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 3.69 billion in 2025, well above the owners' share, which points to a large non-controlling stake. Quarterly results were volatile.

Revenue in 4Q 2025 was KRW 52.9 billion, up 132.7% from the prior quarter, yet operating profit was KRW 3 billion (down 10.0%) and profit attributable to owners swung to a net loss of KRW 3.21 billion.

The gap between operating profit and owners' results may reflect non-operating or one-off items, which the full-year report notes should be checked. In 1Q 2026, revenue was KRW 23.1 billion, operating profit KRW 3.89 billion (up 29.6% QoQ), and profit attributable to owners KRW 1.35 billion, a return to profit.

In 2Q 2026, revenue was KRW 24.9 billion, operating profit KRW 4.77 billion, and profit attributable to owners KRW 2.12 billion, with owners' profit rising for two consecutive quarters through 3Q 2026.

In 3Q 2026, revenue was KRW 22 billion, down 11.4% QoQ and 3.1% YoY, and operating profit was KRW 2.6 billion, down 45.5% QoQ and 22.1% YoY. Profit attributable to owners was KRW 2.55 billion, up 178.1% YoY, so operating profit and owners' profit moved in opposite directions. Over the last four quarters, from 4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026, profit attributable to owners totaled KRW 2.82 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Social casino games earn revenue by attracting free users and selling virtual goods, so demand depends on overseas players' spending patterns and platform policies.

The company has said that in Korea, game-money top-ups are restricted under game rating rules, which makes casino-style games hard to launch domestically, and that it hopes for regulatory easing. Whether those rules loosen is a key variable for entry into Korea.

Among listed peers, DoubleUGames is the direct social casino comparison, while Netmarble, Com2us and Wemade differ in genre and revenue mix. On content, the company says it is strengthening K-content competitiveness in the global OTT market.

Pairing games with content gives room for IP expansion, but content revenue can swing quarterly results sharply depending on when production is completed. On operations, AI-driven automation and data-based marketing are broad industry trends, and ME2ON cites them as a source of cost savings.

Press reports describe the split as social casino at ME2ON and casual and content at Ghost Studio. The sector cycle is best read through the company's own revenue trend.

06

Outlook

At its 2Q 2026 results announcement, the company pointed to second-half new game launches, a new content lineup and expansion of its K-beauty business as growth drivers. The timing and scale of the new games have not been specified by the company.

In blockchain, the company plans to step up global marketing for Ace Casino from 2026. For the content division, the company said it expects the IP to continue performing through the series it planned to air in the first half of 2026.

In the 2Q 2026 announcement, the CFO said game growth and AI-driven marketing and operating efficiency were translating into results. On costs, the company highlighted stronger in-house game marketing and deeper user data analysis as drivers of operating profit improvement.

On shareholder returns, subsidiary Ghost Studio decided a dividend based on last year's results, equal to about half of annual operating profit, and said it will continue its shareholder return policy.

The points to watch are whether second-half launches lift quarterly revenue and whether the 3Q 2026 drop in operating profit proves temporary.

The company attributes its cost improvement to AI-driven game operations automation and deeper user data analysis. In 2Q 2026 it cited stronger in-house game marketing as a driver of operating profit. Unlike the earlier decline in margins, the company says efficiency gains are now reaching profit in 2026.

Lower financial burden and capital structure

In 4Q 2025, operating profit stayed positive, yet profit attributable to owners swung to a net loss of KRW 3.21 billion. Regulation and dependence on new titles

Risk factors

Regulatory risk

In Korea, game rating rules restrict paid game-money top-ups, which blocks domestic expansion of social casino games. Changes to overseas platform and payment rules could also affect revenue. Regulatory easing would open domestic entry, but the timing is uncertain.

Earnings volatility and content revenue timing

Revenue jumped to KRW 52.9 billion in 4Q 2025 and fell to KRW 23.1 billion in 1Q 2026. Content revenue can cluster in particular quarters depending on when production is completed, reducing predictability. As subsidiary Ghost Studio's weight grows, group results depend more on content schedules.

Shareholder structure and float

Kakao Games is listed as a major holder with 21.43%, so ownership is relatively concentrated. With a small market capitalization, trading liquidity and share price volatility can be sensitive. Bond redemption reduced potential overhang, but changes in major holders' stakes remain a point to monitor.
