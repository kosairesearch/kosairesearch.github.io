02

Business structure

Hyundai Department Store operates a department store business, a duty-free business, and the furniture and mattress affiliate Zinus.

Department store net sales in Q3 2026 rose 6.5% year on year to KRW 614.5 billion, with operating profit of KRW 114.1 billion and an operating margin of 18.6% (preliminary, announced November 4, 2026). Department store profit depends on higher-margin categories such as luxury goods, fashion and high-end jewelry.

Duty-free net sales in the same quarter rose 64.8% to KRW 366.6 billion, with operating profit of KRW 3.2 billion.

The duty-free business runs luxury-focused floors at the Trade Center branch, and in the second half of 2025 it cut its floor space from the 8th to 10th floors to the 8th and 9th floors, handing the 10th floor to the department store.

Zinus depends on US mattress demand, and its Q3 2026 net sales were KRW 225.2 billion, down 6.8% year on year, with an operating loss of KRW 12.5 billion.

Its main customers are domestic shoppers and foreign tourists, and the company said foreign spending at key stores such as Myeongdong and Yeouido lifted department store results.

Competition overlaps with large department store operators such as Shinsegae, Lotte Shopping and Hanwha Galleria, while the duty-free business competes with airport and city duty-free operators.

Revenue is reported as net sales, recognizing only commissions, so it differs sharply from total sales at customer selling prices, which were KRW 7.3765 trillion for the first nine months of 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on reported results, annual revenue fell from KRW 5.0141 trillion in 2022 to KRW 4.2075 trillion in 2023 (down 16.1% year on year), then to KRW 4.1876 trillion in 2024 (down 0.5%), before edging up to KRW 4.2303 trillion in 2025 (up 1.0%).

Within 2022-2025, 2022 was the year with the highest revenue and 2024 was the year with the lowest. Operating profit declined from KRW 320.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 303.5 billion in 2023 (down 5.4%) and KRW 284.0 billion in 2024 (down 6.4%), then jumped to KRW 377.9 billion in 2025 (up 33.1%), the highest in the period.

The operating margin was 6.4% in 2022, 7.2% in 2023 and 6.8% in 2024, then rose to 8.9% in 2025. A notable feature is that the margin improved while revenue stayed in the KRW 4 trillion range without moving much.

Net income attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 144.1 billion in 2022, then swung to a net loss of KRW 79.8 billion in 2023, and the loss continued in 2024 at KRW 35.9 billion, though the size of the loss narrowed.

In 2025, net income attributable to owners of the parent returned to profit at KRW 207.7 billion, while net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 141.5 billion that year.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 1.0103 trillion in Q3 2025 to KRW 1.0417 trillion in Q4 (up 3.1%), bottomed at KRW 950.1 billion in Q1 2026 (down 8.8%), then increased for two consecutive quarters, to KRW 1.0681 trillion in Q2 (up 12.4%) and KRW 1.1723 trillion in Q3 (up 9.8%).

Operating profit surged to KRW 105.9 billion in Q4 2025 (up 45.7%), then fell to KRW 98.8 billion in Q1 2026 (down 6.7%) and KRW 79.3 billion in Q2 (down 19.7%), before recovering to KRW 94.6 billion in Q3 (up 19.2% quarter on quarter and up 30.1% year on year).

Net income attributable to owners of the parent in Q3 was KRW 50.8 billion, down 26.1% from the previous quarter but up 12.2% year on year, and the total for the latest four quarters is KRW 237.2 billion.

A point to check is that the growth rates of operating profit and net income attributable to owners of the parent differed in Q3.

On a cumulative basis for the first three quarters, department store operating profit rose 40.7% to KRW 360.0 billion, and the duty-free business returned to profit with cumulative operating profit of KRW 12.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The department store market showed a recovery in Q3 2026, and Business Post attributed Hyundai Department Store's higher third-quarter operating profit to a favorable department store market.

The main drivers were rising foreign tourist numbers and sales of higher-margin categories, so the recovery leans more on visitor spending than on domestic consumption overall.

Duty-free demand is supported by rising passenger traffic, and the company said the DF2 zone at Incheon International Airport, which opened in April 2026, has grown steadily. The furniture and mattress business, by contrast, is under pressure from weaker US consumption.

Zinus saw lower mattress orders from US clients, and US tariff-driven price increases reduced sales through key channels. The two businesses are therefore moving in different directions, with visitor growth lifting the department store and duty-free units while US demand weakness hits furniture and mattresses.

In competition, the department store unit overlaps with Shinsegae, Lotte Shopping and Hanwha Galleria, and the duty-free unit is compared with airport and city duty-free operators.

Because the group is exposed to both department stores and duty-free, the flow of foreign visitors is the main variable behind earnings swings.

06

Outlook

The company said it will keep pushing restructuring across production and logistics to sustain profitability improvements into the fourth quarter.

It explained that Zinus order volumes are gradually normalizing and the effects of its profit-structure overhaul have started to show, improving its operating result sharply from the prior quarter in Q3 2026.

Zinus still posted an operating loss of KRW 12.5 billion in Q3, and its nine-month operating loss reached KRW 69.3 billion. The duty-free unit has stayed profitable for five straight quarters on rising passenger numbers and growth at the DF2 zone at Incheon International Airport.

For the department store unit, the next checkpoint is whether foreign visitor inflows and higher-margin category sales continue. A press report dated July 31, 2026 said the Trade Center branch is reviewing a full renovation of its basement food hall and food court, with a start targeted for 2027.

The company said nothing concrete has been finalized, and neither the construction start nor the tenant mix has been set. The 10th floor of the Trade Center branch is being reorganized around luxury brands, global gourmet content and rest areas.

The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, so annual operating profit and the Zinus profit trend will be the checkpoints at that time.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in the department store core

Department store operating profit for Q3 2026 rose 27.8% to KRW 114.1 billion, and cumulative operating profit for the first nine months reached KRW 360 billion, up 40.7%. Foreign customer inflows and sales of fashion, luxury and high-end jewelry supported a higher-margin mix. The operating margin of 18.6% shows profit rising faster than sales.

Sustained profitability in duty-free

The duty-free unit posted a KRW 3.2 billion operating profit in Q3 and has stayed profitable for five straight quarters. Its cumulative operating profit for the first nine months was KRW 12.8 billion, a swing to profit. Growth at the DF2 zone at Incheon International Airport and higher passenger numbers are cited as the drivers.

Improved balance sheet and cash generation

The debt ratio fell from 89.6% in 2022 to 78.3% in 2025. Operating cash flow rose to KRW 986.2 billion in 2025 from KRW 489.4 billion in 2022. The operating margin also improved to 8.9% in 2025 from 6.4% in 2022, so profitability and cash generation improved together.

09

Bear factors

Continued losses at Zinus

Zinus recorded Q3 2026 net sales of KRW 225.2 billion, down 6.8% year on year, with an operating loss of KRW 12.5 billion. Its nine-month operating loss was KRW 69.3 billion, a swing into loss. Weaker US consumption and tariff-driven price increases reduced mattress demand.

Volatility in quarterly profit

Operating profit fell from KRW 105.9 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 98.8 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 79.3 billion in Q2, before recovering to KRW 94.6 billion in Q3. Profit attributable to owners of the parent fell 26.1% from the prior quarter to KRW 50.8 billion in Q3. Large swings between quarters make it difficult to read the trend from one or two quarters.

Small duty-free scale and store reshaping

The duty-free unit's Q3 operating profit of KRW 3.2 billion is small in absolute terms. The Trade Center branch cut its duty-free floor space in the second half of 2025, and a renovation of its basement floor is under review.

Whether airport duty-free growth translates into a meaningful profit contribution will need to be confirmed in later quarterly results.

10

Risk factors

Foreign visitor flows

Department store and duty-free profits depend on rising foreign tourist numbers and passenger traffic. A slowdown in visitor flows could reduce sales of higher-margin categories and duty-free revenue together. Because earnings are concentrated among a specific customer group, volatility could increase.

US market exposure at Zinus

Zinus is affected by weaker US consumption and tariff-driven price increases. If order normalization comes later than expected, the pace of narrowing losses could slow. Until the restructuring effects are confirmed, it remains a drag on consolidated profit.

Store reshaping and investment costs

The basement floor renovation at the Trade Center branch is still under review, and neither the construction start nor the tenant mix is settled. If the renovation goes ahead, sales gaps and investment costs could arise during construction. The duty-free floor reduction and the 10th-floor reorganization are also changing how space is used.

Overall view

Hyundai Department Store is a company whose earnings recovery is confirmed on a disclosed basis, with operating profit of KRW 377.9 billion in 2025, up 33.1%, and profit attributable to owners of the parent turning to a KRW 207.7 billion surplus.

In Q3 2026, department store operating profit of KRW 114.1 billion, up 27.8%, and the duty-free surplus lifted results. Zinus, however, posted a Q3 operating loss of KRW 12.5 billion, and its nine-month operating loss reached KRW 69.3 billion.

Quarterly revenue has risen for two consecutive quarters since the Q1 2026 low, but operating profit and owner-attributable profit have swung widely from quarter to quarter. The debt ratio fell from 89.6% in 2022 to 78.3% in 2025, and operating cash flow was KRW 986.2 billion in 2025.

The price-to-book ratio sits below 1x. The next checkpoints are annual operating profit in the 2026 annual report and the Zinus profit trend.
