02

Business structure

Protec Mems Technology was founded in 2004 with the goal of manufacturing and selling semiconductor test equipment, and it listed on KOSDAQ in 2016.

The company uses MEMS technology to manufacture and sell probe cards for wafer testing, a component that checks the electrical function of chips while they are still on the wafer.

It has developed and supplied 2D MEMS probe cards since 2023, and a market commentary describes that supply to domestic and overseas chipmakers, including Samsung Electronics, as the start of a phase of expanding earnings.

By product, semiconductor test probe cards account for 98.11% of sales and other products for 1.89%, based on WiseReport company monitor data. In December 2025, probe card sales were split 49.52% domestic and 50.48% export. R&D spending equaled 20.81% of sales in 2025, compared with 21.07% in 2024.

Customer concentration shows in disclosures, with supply contracts with Samsung Electronics filed in May 2026 and October 2026. Probe cards are used at the wafer-test stage, so demand tracks customers' wafer output and capital spending plans.

When memory chip prices fall, customers cut test-equipment orders, and revenue can swing quickly. The business therefore combines product concentration with customer concentration.

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue fell from KRW 38.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 33 billion in 2023 (down 13.6% year on year) and KRW 24.4 billion in 2024 (down 26.2%), then rose slightly to KRW 25.3 billion in 2025 (up 3.7%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year by revenue and 2024 the smallest.

Operating losses were KRW 5.23 billion in 2022, KRW 5.27 billion in 2023, KRW 13.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 20.4 billion in 2025, a run of four consecutive loss years, with losses widening each year after 2022. The operating margin declined from -13.7% in 2022 to -15.9% in 2023, -53.7% in 2024 and -80.7% in 2025.

In 2025 the loss widened even though revenue edged up, and on a consolidated basis from a financial data provider, cost of sales reached KRW 37.8 billion against revenue of KRW 25.3 billion.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened every year, from KRW 3.92 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.55 billion in 2023, KRW 14.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 22.5 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow was negative in all four years, at -KRW 2.35 billion in 2022, -KRW 6.87 billion in 2023, -KRW 5.65 billion in 2024 and -KRW 9.54 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, the third quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 6.04 billion and an operating loss of KRW 2.53 billion, and the fourth quarter showed revenue of KRW 7.23 billion, up 19.7% from the prior quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 9.53 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026 revenue jumped to KRW 15.9 billion, up 119.7% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 162 million turned the result positive. The second quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 18.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.26 billion, up 675.2% from the prior quarter.

The third quarter of 2026 saw revenue fall to KRW 13.4 billion, down 27.5% from the prior quarter and up 121.6% from a year earlier, and an operating loss of KRW 762 million returned.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders ran for two consecutive quarters from the second quarter through the third quarter of 2026. Over the last four quarters that net loss totaled KRW 10.8 billion.

For the first nine months of 2025, a market commentary cited oversupply and weaker demand in the memory market, which pushed DRAM and NAND prices down, as the cause of the deterioration.

05

Industry analysis

Protec Mems Technology is classified under electrical equipment, but its actual business is closer to semiconductor test equipment in the form of probe cards. Probe card demand moves with customers' wafer output and capital spending, so memory chip conditions have a direct effect.

A WiseReport company monitor comment says that for the first nine months of 2025, oversupply and weaker demand in the memory market pushed DRAM and NAND prices down and weakened results. Over the same period, standalone revenue fell 12.3% year on year and the operating loss widened 64.1%.

This pattern shows how quickly revenue can fall when customers defer test-equipment orders during a price decline.

Quarterly revenue rose to KRW 15.9 billion and KRW 18.5 billion in the first two quarters of 2026, which can be read as a partial recovery in orders, yet revenue fell to KRW 13.4 billion in the third quarter, so quarter-to-quarter variation remains large.

On competition, the company has broadened its product line by supplying 2D MEMS probe cards since 2023. Peers in the same sector classification, such as LS ELECTRIC and Hyosung Heavy Industries, focus on power equipment, so their end-market demand and cycles differ from those of Protec Mems Technology.

The company's industry outlook is therefore best read against demand trends in the semiconductor test equipment market.

With the equity base this thin, the rights offering worth KRW 18.5 billion decided in April 2026 also needs to be considered.

Cash outflows and cyclical sensitivity
