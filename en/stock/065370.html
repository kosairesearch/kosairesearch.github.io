WISE iTech: Public-sector data software tests its profit recovery

WISE iTech's revenue recovered after its 2023 trough and kept rising through 2025, and third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 11.3 billion after two straight quarterly gains. However, the operating margin was only 2.1% in 2025, so whether profit strength can be sustained is the next question to watch.

02

Business structure

WISE iTech, founded in 1990, is an AI and big data software company focused on data quality management, business intelligence (BI), and an AI development platform. According to a BusinessPost report, big data solutions accounted for about 80% of 2024 revenue and AI solutions for about 19%.

Its main products are WiseProphet, an AutoML-based AI development platform, WiseIntelligence, a visual analytics tool, and WiseDQ, a data quality tool. At a June 2026 exhibition, the company also introduced WiseAIOps, an AI model operations platform, and iDocu, a trade document information extraction service.

Its customers are mainly public agencies and financial companies, and a 2022 brokerage report said financial and public-sector orders made up roughly 70% of results.

In the procurement channel, four core products ranked first by sales amount on the Public Procurement Service's digital service mall in 2024, according to ETNews. Its subsidiary linked its data management software with the ASTA product, and that subsidiary's revenue rose 120% year on year in 2024.

Competition overlaps with large system integrators such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS, as well as imported BI and data platform products. A 2022 research report attributed the expansion of public-sector orders partly to price competitiveness against imported software.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results started from revenue of KRW 37.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.3 billion in 2022. In 2023, revenue fell 27.1% year on year to KRW 27.6 billion, and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 2.87 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.29 billion.

DigitalDaily, citing the annual business report, attributed the deterioration partly to AI segment sales falling from KRW 12.4 billion to KRW 3.9 billion.

Over the same period, outsourced service purchases dropped from KRW 20.8 billion to KRW 14.6 billion, but higher internal staffing raised labor costs in the cost base.

In 2024, revenue rose 26.8% to KRW 35 billion and operating profit returned to KRW 928 million, while the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed to KRW 409 million. The company cited large public-sector project wins and win-back deals with private clients as growth drivers.

In 2025, revenue rose 9.2% to KRW 38.3 billion, the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period, but operating profit fell 15.3% to KRW 786 million, leaving an operating margin of 2.1%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a profit of KRW 1.45 billion, and the reasons behind net income exceeding operating profit should be checked in the periodic report's non-operating items.

Operating cash flow moved from KRW -2.35 billion in 2023 to KRW 1.52 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.2 billion in 2025. Quarterly revenue peaked at KRW 15.5 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, up 70.4% from the prior quarter, yet operating profit was only KRW 213 million.

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 7.91 billion with operating profit of KRW 390 million, and the company described this as its highest first-quarter revenue on record.

The second quarter posted an operating loss of KRW 853 million, and the third quarter recorded revenue of KRW 11.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.48 billion, up 26.8% year on year. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.79 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The end demand comes from public agencies and financial companies needing data quality management, analytics, and AI model operations.

In its first-quarter 2026 results announcement in May 2026, the company said demand is growing for platforms that integrate data quality management, analysis, and AI model development and operation as AI adoption spreads. Its position in the procurement market is confirmed for 2024.

ETNews, citing data from a government procurement data hub, reported that the company's products ranked first by sales amount in their categories. On the cycle, the company said its AI segment went through rapid growth after 2020 and entered a consolidation phase in 2023. Competition runs along two lines.

One is overlap with large system integrators in public projects, and the other is competition with imported BI and data platform products. A 2022 research report attributed the expansion of public-sector orders partly to price competitiveness against imported software.

Because the Public Procurement Service's digital service mall is the channel through which public agencies buy IT services, share in that channel is directly linked to public-sector revenue flow.

As of a 2022 research report, the public-to-private revenue mix was about 7 to 3, and the company said it would strengthen its private sales organization to rebalance it.

06

Outlook

In its corporate value-up plan disclosed on March 31, 2026, the company set targets of revenue growth, sustained profitability, expanded investment for growth, and stronger financial stability.

Execution items include the beta launch of the iDocu trade document extraction service and entry into the freight forwarding and logistics sector, expansion of industry-specific data quality projects for the AI era, and global SaaS expansion.

The same plan disclosed a 2025 payout ratio of 61.17% and stated that the company qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act.

In March 2026, the company announced it had won a KRW 6.1 billion integrated operations service project for the digital innovation office of the Bank of Korea.

In October 2026, it signed a strategic business agreement with AI computing infrastructure company Aiknot to pursue opportunities combining AI platforms and infrastructure in the United States and Latin America.

On November 12, 2026, its conversational BI solution WiseIntelligence received a public sector purchaser council award at the Global Intelligent SW Awards. On the product side, the AI Butler agent was described as supporting natural-language data analysis, chart and dashboard generation, and draft presentation writing.

Whether this plan is reflected in actual results can be confirmed through revenue and operating profit trends in the 2026 annual business report.

Bull factors

Position in public procurement

In 2024, four core products ranked first by sales amount on the Public Procurement Service's digital service mall. WiseIntelligence and WiseDQ recorded sales-based market shares of 40% and 47%, respectively.

In January 2026, the company said it had carried out a government-wide data management operations project for seven consecutive years, and it was selected for the Emerging AI+X TOP 100 for the fourth time overall.

Recovery in profit strength

Operating results moved from a loss in 2023 to an operating profit of KRW 928 million in 2024, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a profit of KRW 1.45 billion in 2025.

First-quarter 2026 operating profit returned to a KRW 390 million profit, and third-quarter operating profit reached KRW 1.48 billion, up 26.8% year on year. Quarterly revenue rose in the second and third quarters of 2026.

Expanding new services and partnerships

In March 2026, the company disclosed that it had won a KRW 6.1 billion integrated operations project for the digital innovation office of the Bank of Korea. In October 2026, it signed a strategic agreement with Aiknot to pursue the United States and Latin American markets.

The iDocu trade document automation service began its beta phase in 2026, opening entry into the freight forwarding and logistics sector.

Bear factors

Thin operating margin and quarterly volatility

The operating margin was 2.1% in 2025, below 3.4% in 2022. Revenue rose 9.2% in 2025, but operating profit fell 15.3%. An operating loss of KRW 853 million in the second quarter of 2026 shows that quarterly profit has not yet stabilized after the return to profitability.

Past sharp drop in the AI segment

According to DigitalDaily, AI segment revenue fell from KRW 12.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.9 billion in 2023. The company described this as a pause, but the decline shows how swings in this segment can affect overall results. In 2023, internal labor costs rose as outsourced spending was cut, widening the operating loss.

Reliance on public orders and financial burden

A 2022 research report estimated that financial and public-sector orders made up roughly 70% of results. Revenue timing may vary with public budgets and procurement schedules. The debt ratio fell from 133.1% in 2024 to 115.2% in 2025, but remains above 54.7% in 2023.

Risk factors

Quarterly earnings volatility

Fourth-quarter 2025 revenue peaked at KRW 15.5 billion, but operating profit was only KRW 213 million. The second quarter of 2026 recorded an operating loss of KRW 853 million, showing large swings between quarters. If revenue concentration and cost timing do not align, annual profit could be affected.

Concentration in public projects and business mix

A high share of orders from public agencies and financial companies makes the company sensitive to government budgets and procurement policy. Big data solutions made up about 80% of 2024 revenue, so the product mix leans heavily toward one line.

Whether new AI platforms and subscription services have grown large enough to offset public-sector revenue has not yet been confirmed.

Financial structure and investment burden

In its 2026 plan, the company paired expanded investment with stronger financial stability. The debt ratio fell from 133.1% in 2024 to 115.2% in 2025 but remains above 2023. Expanding new-business investment while keeping a 2025 payout ratio of 61.17% makes cash capacity a point to examine.

Overall view

WISE iTech's disclosed results show a return to profitability in 2024 after the 2023 loss, followed by a 2025 profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

Revenue reached KRW 38.3 billion in 2025, the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period, and third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 11.3 billion marked a second straight quarterly gain. Strengths include its share in the public procurement market, new 2026 orders, and rising demand for AI and data platforms.

Weaknesses include a low operating margin of 2 to 3%, quarterly volatility seen in the second-quarter 2026 operating loss, and reliance on public-sector orders. The debt ratio fell from 133.1% in 2024 to 115.2% in 2025 but remains above the 2023 level.

The next checkpoints are whether annual operating profit stays positive in the 2026 annual business report and whether revenue and operating profit in the first-quarter 2027 report continue the 2026 trend.
