02

Business structure

DNFCo was founded in 2001 as a thin-film materials specialist for semiconductor device formation and listed on KOSDAQ in 2007, according to WISEReport's company monitor.

Its main products are DPT and QPT, High-k, SiO/SiN and ACL materials, with precursors used in deposition and etch-related hardmask materials at the core of the business.

According to the aggregator openbizdata, semiconductor electronic materials accounted for KRW 74.2 billion, or 93.7%, of 2025 revenue of KRW 79.1 billion, while other chemical materials contributed KRW 5.0 billion (6.3%).

The same dataset shows KRW 71.6 billion in domestic sales and KRW 7.5 billion in exports, which points to a domestically weighted customer base. Its main customers are Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, and overseas buyers include TSMC, PSMC and Winbond.

Raw precursor materials are imported from the United States, Japan, Europe and China. After Japan's export restrictions in July 2019, reports said DNFCo's precursor sales grew alongside the push to localize semiconductor process materials.

Production takes place at the Daejeon No. 1 campus and the Ulsan plant, and the same dataset puts the overall 2025 utilization rate at 53.9%. Domestic precursor competitors exist, and a 2022 brokerage analysis named Dukesan Techopia as a key rival.

On the same dataset's basis, 2025 R&D spending equaled 9.7% of revenue, a fixed-cost burden that is a defining feature of the business.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 135.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 84.2 billion in 2023, a 37.7% decline, and then to KRW 73.9 billion in 2024, down 12.2%. Revenue rebounded to KRW 79.1 billion in 2025, up 7.0%, with 2022 the largest year and 2024 the smallest across 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit of KRW 16.3 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 2.86 billion in 2023, and the losses then narrowed to KRW 1.02 billion in 2024 and KRW 674 million in 2025.

The company recorded operating losses for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, and the operating margin moved from 12.1% in 2022 to -3.4% in 2023, -1.4% in 2024 and -0.9% in 2025.

In February 2026 the company said that higher revenue from a semiconductor market recovery and lower selling and administrative expenses narrowed the operating loss, as reported by Edaily.

Attributable net income was KRW 5.8 billion in 2022, KRW 5.17 billion in 2023 (down 10.8%), KRW 631 million in 2024 (down 87.8%) and KRW 1.24 billion in 2025 (up 96.5%). Net income including non-controlling interests was disclosed at the same amounts across 2022 to 2025.

Operating cash flow stayed positive in loss years, at KRW 16 billion in 2024 and KRW 17.2 billion in 2025, which likely reflects non-cash charges and working capital movements; the cash flow statement in the annual report holds the detail.

On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 21.1 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.03 billion. The fourth quarter of 2025 then posted revenue of KRW 22.4 billion, up 5.9% quarter on quarter, and operating profit of KRW 2.07 billion, a swing to profit.

The first quarter of 2026 was the low point, with revenue of KRW 20.9 billion, down 6.6%, and operating profit of KRW 710 million, down 65.7%.

Revenue then rose to KRW 21.7 billion in the second quarter with operating profit of KRW 705 million, and to KRW 22.1 billion in the third quarter with operating profit of KRW 1.24 billion, up 76.6% quarter on quarter and a swing to profit from the prior year period.

Attributable net income in the fourth quarter of 2025 was KRW 3.76 billion, well above that quarter's operating profit, which suggests non-operating items contributed.

Attributable net income for the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 1.65 billion, and the four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 total KRW 6.56 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from the downstream side depends on memory investment by Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. WISEReport's company monitor says AI expansion and rising HPC demand have pushed the semiconductor industry into a growth phase, and that stronger demand for semiconductor materials improved results.

The same source lists data center investment, HBM technology progress and the shift of domestic memory makers to advanced process nodes as additional demand drivers. In its 2025 results announcement, the company also attributed the narrower loss to a recovery in semiconductor industry conditions.

On the supply side, precursors are a category where localization has been relatively slow. The Korea International Trade Association said that as of 2020, imports supplied 79% of domestic precursor demand, and that more than 90% of imports by value came from China.

Securities industry sources were reported in 2023 as estimating China's share of precursor supply at more than 70%. That structure creates room for domestic materials makers, but raw material supply and customer qualification timing remain sources of earnings variability.

A 2022 brokerage analysis described DNFCo as a supplier of High-k and DPT precursors for DRAM processes, centered on Samsung Electronics. The same analysis named Dukesan Techopia as a main competitor.

Other semiconductor materials companies listed in the same sector include Soulbrain, Hansol Chemical and Dongjin Semichem.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly operating profit has stayed positive

Operating profit was KRW 2.07 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, then KRW 710 million, KRW 705 million and KRW 1.24 billion across the first three quarters of 2026. Third-quarter operating profit rose 76.6% quarter on quarter and swung to profit from the prior year period.

WISEReport's data shows first-half 2026 standalone sales up 19.4% year on year. The recovery in profit visible across several quarters is the core bull argument.

A light balance sheet

At the end of 2025 total liabilities were KRW 10.8 billion, total equity was KRW 149.4 billion and the debt ratio was 7.2%. Operating cash flow was KRW 16 billion in 2024 and KRW 17.2 billion in 2025, positive even during operating loss years. The strong balance sheet leaves room for continued facility and R&D investment, which is the bull-side reading.

AI and HPC demand and the memory transition

WISEReport's company monitor says AI expansion and rising HPC demand have moved the semiconductor industry into a growth phase. Data center investment, HBM technology progress and domestic memory makers' shift to advanced nodes are cited as widening materials demand.

Because demand for semiconductor materials is tied to memory investment, a recovery in the industry can show up directly in revenue.

09

Bear factors

Full-year profitability is not yet established

Operating losses continued for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, and the 2025 operating margin was -0.9%. Compared with the 12.1% margin in 2022, the margin recovery remains limited. Whether the 2026 quarterly profits carry into a full-year profit must be confirmed in the 2026 annual report.

Concentration in customers and raw materials

Major customers are Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, so any cut in memory investment flows directly into revenue. Precursor raw materials depend heavily on imports, exposing the company to supply and price swings. A raw material base with a large China share remains a supply chain risk.

A thin earnings base

Attributable net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 6.56 billion, which is a small base. Attributable net income in the fourth quarter of 2025 exceeded operating profit, so non-operating items had a large effect. Because the operating profit margin is still thin, quarterly results could move with small cost changes.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration risk

The company depends heavily on Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. A report several years old said Samsung accounted for more than 90% of revenue at the time. A change in one customer's investment schedule could move quarterly revenue and utilization together.

Raw material and supply chain risk

Precursor raw materials are imported from the United States, Japan, Europe and China. Localization has been pushed since Japan's 2019 export restrictions, but imports still supplied 79% of domestic precursor demand as of 2020. Export controls or trade friction could disrupt both supply and costs.

Ownership and earnings capacity risk

An investment data site records the largest shareholder, Soulbrain, at 35.61% as of an April 21, 2026 filing. Changes in stakes are confirmed through disclosures, and data sites may show different percentages. With high R&D and selling and administrative costs, profit could narrow again if revenue stops growing.

Overall view

DNFCo recorded annual operating losses from 2023 to 2025, but quarterly operating profit has appeared since the fourth quarter of 2025. Annual revenue in 2025 rose 7.0% to KRW 79.1 billion, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 674 million.

Revenue rose in the second and third quarters of 2026 after the first-quarter low, and third-quarter operating profit was KRW 1.24 billion with attributable net income of KRW 1.65 billion. On the industry side, expanding AI, HPC and HBM demand is a bull factor, while memory investment adjustments and supply chain variables are bear factors.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary 2026 annual results in early February 2027 and the 2026 annual report around late March 2027, which will show whether full-year operating profit is positive.
