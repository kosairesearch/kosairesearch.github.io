Report overview

Profit Recovery on a Carton and Corrugated Base; Leverage Is the Key Test

Hankuk Package makes carton packs and corrugated packaging. Its operating profit recovered through 2025, quarterly profit has swung more widely in 2026, and the debt-to-equity ratio of 96.1% at end-2025 remains the main balance-sheet item to track.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Hankuk Package grew out of Korea's first carton-pack manufacturing for liquid products such as milk cartons and now produces corrugated boards and paper containers as well. The product split differs by data provider.

Butler reports a packaging-materials share of 64% and carton packs of 36% based on second-quarter 2026 sales, while Alphasquare shows 70.80% for boxes and fabrics and 29.20% for carton packs.

Because the two sources use different bases, the exact segment figures should be checked against the segment information in the DART annual report. Carton-pack demand is linked to milk consumption, and a 2020 technical analysis report described the products as made to order with little inventory risk.

Corrugated demand is cited as rising with the growth of e-commerce. On sales, the same 2020 report put roughly 60% of output as consumed domestically. The March 2026 value-up plan aims to expand overseas sales from direct transactions outside Japan, with Southeast Asia as a target market.

Media reports say the company signed a five-year exclusive supply agreement with LAMIPAK, a global aseptic paper packaging maker, securing exclusive domestic import and sales rights for aseptic packs.

Among the 25 packaging-sector companies tracked by Alphasquare, the company ranked 17th in 2025 sales and 10th in operating profit.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales rose from KRW 217.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 228.1 billion in 2023, up 4.9% year on year, then declined 1.1% to KRW 225.6 billion in 2024 and 0.7% to KRW 224.1 billion in 2025, so sales fell for two consecutive years from 2024. 2023 was the peak sales year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit moved from KRW 586 million in 2022 to KRW 8.55 billion in 2023, up 1,359.2%, dropped 20.5% to KRW 6.79 billion in 2024, and then climbed 46.4% to KRW 9.94 billion in 2025, the largest year of the period.

The operating margin was 0.3% in 2022, 3.7% in 2023, 3.0% in 2024 and 4.4% in 2025, and the margin improvement during the two years of falling sales is the core of the profit recovery.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss of KRW 10.6 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 4.09 billion in 2023, fell to KRW 1.24 billion in 2024, and rose to KRW 5.69 billion in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests matched the controlling-shareholder figure in each of these years. In 2024, operating profit of KRW 6.79 billion compared with net profit of only KRW 1.24 billion, a gap that points to non-operating items.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 3.81 billion in 2022 to KRW 19.5 billion in 2025. On a quarterly basis, operating profit of KRW 2.87 billion in the third quarter of 2025 was followed by KRW 1.67 billion in the fourth quarter, down 41.8%, when controlling-shareholder net profit turned to a loss of KRW 26.62 million.

Operating profit then bottomed at KRW 1.18 billion in the first quarter of 2026, down 29.2% from the prior quarter, rose to KRW 2.06 billion in the second quarter, up 74.0%, and fell to KRW 1.55 billion in the third quarter, down 24.9%.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was down 46.2% year on year, while controlling-shareholder net profit rose 11.6% year on year to KRW 1.69 billion, so the two measures moved in opposite directions.

05

Industry analysis

Corrugated packaging is one segment of demand that commentary links to the growth of e-commerce and contactless consumption. Demand for eco-friendly containers is described as expanding alongside tighter environmental regulation.

A 2025 brokerage report noted that the anti-plastic policy included in a 2025 presidential campaign pledge could benefit eco-friendly products such as carton packs. That is an expectation about policy direction, and the pace of actual demand shift depends on enforcement and consumption patterns.

Carton-pack demand is closely tied to milk consumption, so changes in beverage consumption are a variable for earnings. On the cycle, the company's profit appears to move with raw material costs, selling prices and plant utilization.

On competitive position, among the 25 packaging-sector companies tracked by Alphasquare, the 2025 operating margin of 4.44% was above the sector average of 3.34%, ranking 9th. On the same basis, the debt-to-equity ratio of 96.12% is higher than the sector average of 62.92%.

Sales ranked 17th, below the sector average in scale, so comparisons center on profitability and balance-sheet structure. Overseas, the company plans to shift from Japan-centered sales toward other markets including Southeast Asia.

Media reports cite a leading share in the domestic sterilized pack market, so maintaining that position matters for the aseptic pack expansion.

06

Outlook

The value-up plan the company disclosed on March 27, 2026 has three parts. First, it targets maintaining a dividend payout ratio of 30% or more, backed by stronger cost competitiveness. Second, it aims to expand overseas sales beyond Japan through direct transactions and into markets including Southeast Asia.

Third, it plans to keep capital expenditure at a stable level over three years and introduce automation through facility upgrades. On dividends, the payout ratio for 2025, the most recent fiscal year, was 26.18%, below the 30% target. The 2025 dividend totaled KRW 1.49 billion, up 25% from the prior year.

The company holds exclusive domestic import and sales rights for LAMIPAK aseptic packs under a five-year contract, and media reports cite a domestic aseptic pack market of more than KRW 200 billion. That market figure is a media estimate, and the company has disclosed limited product-level sales targets.

Second-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 62.6 billion rose 13.9% from the prior quarter, showing the upper end of the quarterly range.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin and debt-to-equity ratio in the 2026 annual report, and whether overseas and aseptic pack sales appear as a meaningful share of segment revenue, which will show how the value-up plan is being carried out.

Balance-sheet improvement

Total equity stood at KRW 111.9 billion at end-2025, and operating cash flow of KRW 19.5 billion in 2025 was well above net profit of KRW 5.69 billion that year. Stronger cash generation creates room to reduce reliance on borrowing.

The plan also calls for expanding overseas sales beyond Japan.

Debt burden

Thin margins and small profit base

The operating margin was 4.4% in 2025, leaving limited room for error. Controlling-shareholder net profit rose from KRW 1.24 billion in 2024 to KRW 5.69 billion in 2025, but the last four quarters total only KRW 3.44 billion.

Risk factors

Demand structure and policy dependence

Carton-pack demand is linked to milk consumption, so shifts in beverage consumption affect volumes. Eco-friendly demand partly depends on regulatory direction such as anti-plastic rules, and slower enforcement or a change in direction could delay expected demand. Corrugated demand is tied to e-commerce growth.

Borrowing and financing costs

Total liabilities stood at KRW 107.5 billion at end-2025. Because debt is close in size to equity, interest rates and the maturity profile of borrowings affect earnings directly. The gap between operating profit and controlling-shareholder net profit in 2024 points to a meaningful non-operating cost burden.

Ownership structure and free float

Haesung Industrial Co., Ltd. is shown as the largest shareholder with 51.35% as of June 30, 2026. A high controlling stake can limit the shares available for trading, which can affect trading volume and price volatility. As a small-cap stock, trading flows can also be volatile.

Overall view

Hankuk Package moved from a net loss in 2022 to profit in 2023, and its 2025 operating profit of KRW 9.94 billion was the largest year of the 2022–2025 period. The operating margin rose from 0.3% in 2022 to 4.4% in 2025, and profitability improved even while sales declined, which is a positive factor.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 176.7% to 96.1%, and operating cash flow was KRW 19.5 billion in 2025. On the other side, sales have declined for two consecutive years from 2024, and third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 46.2% year on year, so quarterly profit remains volatile.

Controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 3.44 billion over the last four quarters also reflects a small earnings base. The March 2026 value-up plan includes a payout ratio of 30% or more and expanded overseas sales, while the 2025 payout ratio was 26.18%.

Strengths are the profit recovery, balance-sheet improvement and shareholder-return plan. Weaknesses are falling sales, quarterly earnings volatility and continuing debt.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin and debt-to-equity ratio in the 2026 annual report, and the profit trend in the first-quarter 2027 report.
