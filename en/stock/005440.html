Holding Structure Overhaul Meets a Softer Third Quarter

Hyundai GF Holdings is widening its direct holdings through the home shopping restructuring, yet third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 12.6% year on year to KRW 65.3 billion.

  1. 1

    Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 65.3 billion, down 12.6% year on year, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 45.3 billion, down 64.8%.

  2. 2

    Full-year 2025 sales reached KRW 8.09 trillion and operating profit KRW 274.1 billion, up 9.2% and 38.4% year on year, while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 41.0% to KRW 412 billion.

  3. 3

    The home shopping subsidiary became wholly owned after new shares were issued in June 2026, and its investment unit is scheduled to be spun off in December 2026 and merged into the holding company.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Hyundai GF Holdings is the holding company of the Hyundai Department Store Group. It does not run businesses directly and instead holds and manages stakes in its subsidiaries.

When it was launched in November 2023, it brought the department store and the food service unit under a single holding structure and had 27 domestic subsidiaries across retail, fashion, food and living.

Major affiliates include the department store, the food service unit, the home shopping unit, the apparel unit and the furniture and interior unit. The food service unit's strong catering performance was cited as a driver of second-quarter 2026 results.

New shares issued in June 2026 made the home shopping company a wholly owned subsidiary, and it is scheduled to split into an operating unit and an investment unit. After the investment unit merges into the holding company, the apparel, telecom and L&C subsidiaries would become its direct subsidiaries.

The group says the home shopping business environment has worsened compared with the past, which it cited as a reason for the split. The Chung Ji-sun side holds 77.15 percent, according to the ownership table in an IBK Securities report from July 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell 33.0% from KRW 3.92 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.63 trillion in 2023, the low point, and then rose to KRW 7.41 trillion in 2024 (up 181.8%) and KRW 8.09 trillion in 2025 (up 9.2%), increasing for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025. Of the years from 2022 through 2025, 2025 was the largest.

The surge in 2024 appears to reflect the consolidation of subsidiaries during the holding structure overhaul. Operating profit moved from KRW 60 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 11 billion in 2023, turned to KRW 198.1 billion in 2024, and reached KRW 274.1 billion in 2025, up 38.4%.

The operating margin moved from 1.5 percent in 2022 to negative 0.4 percent in 2023, then 2.7 percent in 2024 and 3.4 percent in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 1.13 trillion in 2023 (up 1,918.2%), then fell to KRW 698.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 412 billion in 2025, declining for two consecutive years.

Operating profit rose in some years when controlling-shareholder profit fell, which reflects a structure where non-operating items and equity-method income carry a large weight. Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 518.7 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 8.14 billion in 2022 to KRW 404.6 billion in 2025. On a quarterly basis, sales peaked at KRW 2.1 trillion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and declined for three consecutive quarters from the first quarter of 2026 through the third quarter.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 117.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and fell for two consecutive quarters to KRW 85.3 billion and KRW 65.3 billion. Third-quarter operating profit was 23.5 percent below a market estimate of KRW 85.4 billion, according to a Financial News report.

05

Industry analysis

Holding companies are often valued through the gap between the combined value of their subsidiary stakes and their own share price, known as the holding company discount. The company expects that reducing duplicate listings and grandchild-subsidiary layers will narrow this gap.

The duplicate listing of the home shopping company has already been removed. The home shopping business improved profitability in the first quarter of 2026 through a larger mix of high-margin broadcast products, yet the group describes its core environment as having worsened compared with the past.

Weak construction activity and department store trends are cited as variables for subsidiary earnings. Apparel earnings rose on a recovering consumer environment, according to coverage of the apparel subsidiary. Furniture and interior demand was weak, and the furniture unit held up through cost control.

Higher oil prices and a weaker won weighed on some subsidiaries' profitability. The food service unit is seen continuing a steady improvement trend. Because the subsidiaries span department stores, food, home shopping and fashion, a simple comparison with other listed holding companies is difficult.

06

Outlook

The company is placing shareholder returns at the center of its agenda. It bought treasury shares worth KRW 100.1 billion in February through April and again in August 2026, and it plans to cancel all of them on November 20, 2026. The amount is equal to about 4 percent of issued shares at acquisition cost.

The company says the group-wide treasury share cancellation totals KRW 350 billion. Heungkuk Securities analysts expect that once the investment unit merges, the holding company's financial statements will fully reflect cash and subsidiary stake values.

The company's investor relations material projects dividend income rising from KRW 62.4 billion in 2026 to about KRW 100 billion in 2027. One subsidiary plans to pay at least 20 percent of standalone operating profit as dividends through 2028, and another plans to more than double its dividend payout by the same year.

Uneven subsidiary earnings leave open whether the higher dividend income can be sustained. In an October 2026 report, Heungkuk Securities kept a target price of KRW 18,000 and projected 2026 full-year operating profit at KRW 342.6 billion. IBK Securities maintained a target price of KRW 19,000 in a July 2026 report. Both reports were published before the third-quarter results were announced.

08

Bull factors

Simpler structure and expected NAV growth

Once the investment unit merges, the apparel, telecom and L&C subsidiaries would become direct subsidiaries of the holding company. The company expects this to expand net asset value and reduce the holding company discount. Removing the duplicate listing points the same way. Whether that value appears in the financial statements can be confirmed only after the merger is completed.

Wider dividend base planned

The main basis for a larger dividend is that more subsidiary payouts will flow directly to the holding company. The company projects dividend income rising from KRW 62.4 billion in 2026 to about KRW 100 billion in 2027.

Treasury share cancellation and return scale

Treasury shares worth KRW 100.1 billion were bought in February through April and again in August 2026, and the full amount is set for cancellation on November 20, 2026. That is about 4 percent of issued shares. It also set out a plan to raise the minimum dividend per share.

09

Bear factors

Third-quarter operating profit down year on year

Both operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from a year earlier in the third quarter of 2026, with the decline in net profit larger than that in operating profit. According to a Financial News report, operating profit was 23.5 percent below a market estimate of KRW 85.4 billion.

Operating profit has fallen for two consecutive quarters since peaking in the first quarter of 2026. The open question is how far shareholder returns can offset the earnings decline.

External cost pressure

The company pointed to higher oil prices and a weaker won as the causes of the third-quarter shortfall. It said quarterly operating profit at some subsidiaries fell by KRW 10.8 billion from a year earlier. Its expectation of a recovery depends on oil prices stabilizing and the currency rebounding.

Uneven subsidiary earnings

Subsidiary results are mixed, with one apparel unit's operating profit rising while the telecom unit continues to post losses. The group itself has described the home shopping business environment as having worsened compared with the past.

Because subsidiary dividends fund the holding company's payouts, this divergence is a variable for dividend stability.

10

Risk factors

External macro factors

Higher oil prices and a weaker won weighed on profitability at some subsidiaries. The company says recovery depends on these factors stabilizing, but how long they persist is uncertain. Subsidiary earnings could swing with consumer demand and cost conditions.

Subsidiary divergence and dividend funding

The holding company's dividends depend on payouts from subsidiaries such as the apparel and telecom units. Because results are uneven, it is uncertain whether higher dividend income can be sustained. Continued losses at the telecom unit could affect the pace of the planned dividend increase.

Timing of the structural overhaul

The timing of the expected benefits from the overhaul depends on whether the spin-off and merger proceed as scheduled. The point at which the merged assets are reflected in the financial statements also needs to be confirmed.

If the process slips, the expected rise in net asset value and narrowing of the discount would also be pushed back.

Overall view

Hyundai GF Holdings is simplifying its subsidiary structure as the holding company of the Hyundai Department Store Group. Annual operating profit moved from a loss in 2023 to profit in 2024 and rose to KRW 274.1 billion in 2025, and the operating margin climbed from 1.5 percent in 2022 to 3.4 percent in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders, however, peaked at KRW 1.13 trillion in 2023 and declined for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025.

In 2026, operating profit peaked in the first quarter at KRW 117.6 billion and fell in the second and third quarters, with external oil and currency factors weighing on the third quarter. Positive factors include the simpler structure, the planned dividend increase and the scale of treasury share cancellation.

Negative factors include the quarterly earnings slowdown, uneven subsidiary results and exposure to external variables. The next checkpoints are the treasury share cancellation on November 20, 2026, the spin-off in December 2026, and the 2026 annual report.

Target prices of KRW 18,000 from Heungkuk Securities (October 2026 report) and KRW 19,000 from IBK Securities (July 2026 report) were published at different times and show how estimates have moved.
