HanaMicron: Memory Upcycle and the Push into Advanced Packaging

HanaMicron's second-quarter 2026 sales and operating profit set quarterly records according to the company, but sales and operating profit both fell from the prior quarter in the third quarter, making memory-cycle and overseas-unit swings the key variables to watch.

02

Business structure

HanaMicron is a back-end semiconductor specialist covering packaging, testing and module assembly, founded in 2001 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2005. Its main customers are Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, and its Asan plant in Chungnam handles system and memory chip volumes for Samsung Electronics.

In 2025 the company split into a back-end business (HanaMicron) and a materials business (Hana Materials), and Hana Materials remains a major subsidiary. Production is spread across the Korean headquarters, the Vietnam subsidiary and the Brazil subsidiary.

The Vietnam unit is expanding memory turn-key output at its Bac Giang plant and extending the cleanroom at its Bac Ninh plant.

A second plant in an industrial park in Bac Giang Province was completed with an investment of USD 1 billion (about KRW 1.3 trillion), and press reports say it handles AI chip packaging and test volumes under mid- to long-term outsourcing contracts running through 2027.

The Brazil subsidiary runs a brand business and is expanding server DRAM supply. The headquarters is raising system-chip sales, including Large Body products, alongside a gradual memory recovery, and the company said it is reviewing expansion of existing packaging lines and construction of a new line.

The stated medium- to long-term axes are system semiconductors and advanced packaging, aiming to widen the business beyond memory. Quarterly sales trends by subsidiary are covered in the earnings section.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual sales rose from KRW 894.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 968 billion in 2023 (up 8.2% year on year), KRW 1.25 trillion in 2024 (up 29.2%), and KRW 1.53 trillion in 2025 (up 22.7%).

Sales have grown for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 is the largest year in the 2022–2025 period. The company said 2025 sales were a record.

Operating profit fell from KRW 103.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 57.9 billion in 2023 (down 44.1%), then rose to KRW 106.8 billion in 2024 (up 84.4%) and KRW 127.7 billion in 2025 (up 19.6%), a second straight year of growth. The company attributed the 2023 slowdown to a memory downturn in a 2024 press report.

Operating margin was 11.6% in 2022, 6.0% in 2023, 8.5% in 2024 and 8.3% in 2025, so margin has not returned to the 2022 level even as sales grew.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.83 billion in 2022, turned to a net loss of KRW 13.5 billion in 2023, widened to a net loss of KRW 23.8 billion in 2024, and returned to a profit of KRW 38.1 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, a different measure, was KRW 65.6 billion in 2025. Quarterly sales climbed from KRW 432.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 683.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026, and operating profit reached KRW 72 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

Korea Investment & Securities attributed the first-quarter profit gain to margin improvement from price negotiations at the Vietnam plant (May 14, 2026 report). The company reported a 20.8% operating margin for the second quarter of 2026.

In the third quarter, sales were KRW 517 billion and operating profit was KRW 71.4 billion. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 168.5 billion.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 652.7 billion, of which KRW 398.8 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 1.37 trillion, giving a debt ratio of 209.2%. Operating cash flow recovered from KRW 41 billion in 2024 to KRW 174.9 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

HanaMicron's earnings are closely tied to the memory chip cycle. In a May 28, 2026 report, Meritz Securities analyzed that 90% of sales excluding non-memory testing and packaging benefit directly or indirectly from strong memory conditions.

The same report described the headquarters' results as improving gradually in a back-half-weighted pattern. In cycle terms, operating profit fell 44.1% in 2023 to a trough, recovered through 2024 and 2025, and reached record quarterly levels in the second quarter of 2026.

Sales declined in the third quarter, showing how turns in the cycle appear quarter to quarter. On demand, high-bandwidth memory (HBM), server DRAM and the shift to DDR5 are cited as the main downstream drivers.

According to a 2024 Korean press report citing Yole Intelligence, the advanced packaging market was expected to grow from USD 44.3 billion in 2022 to USD 78.6 billion in 2028.

Other Korean back-end players are moving in the same direction: an October 2026 News1 report said ISC is pursuing long-term supply contracts and dedicated lines, while HANMI Semiconductor is seeking wider AI system chip supply for foundries and OSATs.

HanaMicron is described as raising the share of higher-value products using its Korean and Vietnamese bases. In a November 2025 report, Sangsangin Securities argued that HBM production competition at memory makers accelerates the outsourcing of general back-end chip work.

06

Outlook

At its second-half shareholder meeting in August 2026, the company said it would expand production capacity at its main sites in the second half. The headquarters plans to add to existing packaging lines and is reviewing a new line.

The Vietnam unit is increasing memory turn-key output at its Bac Giang plant and extending the cleanroom at its Bac Ninh plant. The Brazil unit plans to expand server DRAM supply and commercialize new products. A March 2026 report said the share of DDR5 in the Vietnam unit's total DRAM output is planned to rise to 90%.

In advanced packaging, the company is developing 2.5D packaging and is taking part in a testbed project to localize 12-inch fan-out wafer-level packaging (FOWLP).

On its second-quarter 2026 conference call in July, the company said it is pursuing long-term supply contracts with several global big tech firms and plans to build dedicated lines funded by customer prepayments.

An October 2026 News1 report said 2.5D development is to be completed by the end of 2026, with commercialization starting in 2027. However, a July 2024 report had described visible results as expected in 2025, so whether this schedule holds is a key checkpoint.

On governance, a March 2026 report said the company plans to add independent directors and establish four committees that will operate fully from the second half.

Among broker estimates, Korea Investment & Securities presented 2026 full-year forecasts of KRW 2.4130 trillion in sales and KRW 343.3 billion in operating profit in July 2026. These are forecasts made at that time and should be compared against disclosed quarterly results as they come out.

08

Bull factors

Direct exposure to the memory recovery

Second-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 683.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 142.3 billion were, per the company, record quarterly levels. Vietnam unit sales topped KRW 250 billion in the quarter, and the Brazil unit posted KRW 272.5 billion, so overseas units led growth. The company plans to add capacity in the second half to sustain this trend.

Evidence of margin improvement

Korea Investment & Securities said the first-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 72 billion reflected margin gains from price negotiations at the Vietnam plant. The same report said the Brazil unit's operating margin was in the high teens percent.

The company's operating margin was 8.3% in 2025 but was reported at 20.8% in the second quarter of 2026. Bulls argue that a growing share of higher-margin units can sustain profitability gains.

Expansion into advanced packaging and system chips

The company is developing 2.5D packaging and taking part in a FOWLP localization testbed to build higher-value packaging capability. On its July 2026 conference call it said it is pursuing long-term supply contracts with several global big tech firms.

Headquarters system-chip sales, including Large Body products, are growing, which could gradually shift a memory-centered mix. Bulls see 2.5D commercialization starting in 2027 as a possible non-memory revenue source.

09

Bear factors

Dependence on the memory cycle

Operating profit fell 44.1% to KRW 57.9 billion in 2023, and in a 2024 press report the company attributed the slowdown to a memory downturn. Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 517 billion, down 24.3% from the prior quarter.

If most sales are tied to the memory cycle, as the Meritz analysis suggests, earnings swings could widen when the cycle turns. Quarter-to-quarter volatility is the starting point of the bearish case.

Customer concentration and subsidiary dependence

Samsung Electronics and SK hynix are the main customers, so results are sensitive to their capital spending decisions. The overseas units are concentrated in Vietnam memory turn-key work and Brazil server DRAM, so a fall in memory prices could hit both in the same direction.

The company said Brazil sales benefited from higher memory prices. That structure helps in a price upcycle but can weigh in a downcycle.

2.5D timing and investment burden

A July 2024 report said visible results from 2.5D packaging were expected within 2025, while an October 2026 report described development finishing by the end of 2026 with commercialization starting in 2027.

In a March 2024 interview the company said much of its internal funds goes to capital spending, leaving little room for near-term shareholder returns. If a new line is built and payback lengthens, financial strain could increase.

10

Risk factors

Memory cycle and pricing

HanaMicron's sales are directly linked to memory conditions and pricing. A drop in DRAM prices or cuts in customer capital spending could quickly reduce quarterly results. The third-quarter 2026 decline already shows how volatile the business can be. High dependence on a few customers also means their investment plans matter a great deal.

Balance sheet

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 1.37 trillion against total equity of KRW 652.7 billion, of which KRW 398.8 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders. The debt ratio rose from 188.6% in 2022 to 221.0% in 2024 and eased to 209.2% in 2025.

If expansion relies on external financing, borrowing costs and funding conditions become relevant. Operating cash flow recovered to KRW 174.9 billion in 2025 but has varied considerably by year.

Share price volatility and market measures

In its May 28, 2026 report, Meritz Securities noted the stock had been designated an investment warning issue after a sharp price rise, with June 8 cited as the first review date for lifting the designation. Broker target prices were presented in May and July 2026, but they do not guarantee results. Share prices can be volatile with the semiconductor cycle.

12

Overall view

HanaMicron posted record quarterly results in the second quarter of 2026, supported by the memory recovery and growth at its Vietnam and Brazil units. Sales in 2025 were KRW 1.53 trillion and operating profit KRW 127.7 billion, the largest year of 2022–2025.

However, third-quarter 2026 sales and operating profit both fell from the prior quarter, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders dropped to KRW 13.1 billion. Memory prices and capacity utilization are the variables that most determine direction, and quarterly volatility can grow when they diverge.

Advanced packaging and system chips are presented as medium- to long-term pillars, but the 2.5D commercialization timeline has already shifted compared with earlier reports. The debt ratio of 209.2% is a factor in how much cushion exists if the cycle turns.

Broker target prices reflect views from May and July 2026 and do not incorporate later results or share price moves. The next checkpoints are whether 2.5D development is completed by the end of December 2026 and the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027.
