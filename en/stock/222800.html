02

Business structure

Simmtech is a printed circuit board maker for semiconductor and communication equipment, spun off from Simmtech Holdings through an physical split in 2015. The company operates six domestic plants and an R&D center, and it holds a Chinese subsidiary and a Japan-based production subsidiary.

Its core products are memory module PCBs and semiconductor package substrates. Package substrates accounted for 76% of total revenue in the first half of 2026 according to a financial data provider.

Third-quarter 2026 segment revenue was KRW 56.9 billion for AI server-oriented SOCAMM modules, KRW 94.6 billion for system IC-related products, KRW 142.4 billion for HDI, and KRW 62.3 billion for Tenting, which the company classifies as a non-core product.

The company expects cumulative 2026 SOCAMM module revenue of about KRW 147.5 billion. Major customers are Samsung Electronics, SK hynix, and Micron, the three global memory makers, as reported in 2025.

A May 2026 report said Simmtech ranks first worldwide with a share of about 30% in memory module PCBs, BoC (Board on Chip), and pattern-embedded substrates. Between the fourth quarter of 2022 and 2024, the company posted operating losses amid a memory downturn and the market entry of several competitors. Daeduck Electronics, a sector peer, focuses on FC BGA substrates for CPUs, so its product mix differs.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 1.7 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.04 trillion in 2023, a 38.6% decline, then rose to KRW 1.23 trillion in 2024 (up 18.2%) and KRW 1.41 trillion in 2025 (up 14.5%), growing for two consecutive years. 2022 was the largest year in the disclosure period and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit swung from KRW 352.4 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 88.1 billion in 2023, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 47 billion in 2024. 2025 returned to operating profit at KRW 11.9 billion, with an operating margin of 0.8%.

Shareholders' net income fell from KRW 245.8 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 114.9 billion in 2023, then narrowed to a KRW 30.3 billion loss in 2024 before widening to a KRW 164.2 billion loss in 2025, three straight loss-making years.

Reports attributed the 2023 revenue drop to a memory downturn from the fourth quarter of 2022 through 2024 and the entry of several competitors. Operating cash flow was negative in each of 2023 through 2025, at KRW -93.4 billion, KRW -161.3 billion, and KRW -198.6 billion.

Quarterly operating profit bottomed at KRW 10.3 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, then rose for three consecutive quarters to KRW 13.7 billion, KRW 62.9 billion, and KRW 101.8 billion in the first through third quarters of 2026.

Securities analysts linked the first-quarter 2026 turnaround to partial pass-through of higher raw material costs into selling prices.

Third-quarter revenue of KRW 535.9 billion was up 43.7% year on year, but shareholders' net income of KRW 25.3 billion fell 43.4% from the prior quarter, so profit growth did not carry through to the bottom line at the same rate. Third-quarter operating profit was reported to be 19.4% above the market consensus of KRW 85.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The memory cycle is the starting point for demand in memory module substrates. A December 2025 equity research report expected global memory makers' 2025 capital expenditure to rise 39.4% year on year.

Growth in AI servers and HBM is raising the layer count and difficulty of advanced substrates, which creates room for a better product mix. The same report expected customers' commercialization of next-generation SoCAMM-2 and CXL products to expand orders for new substrates.

Analysts described 2026 as the turning point for normalizing earnings, as price increases and a richer product mix coincide. A 2024 analysis noted that customers allocating capacity mainly to HBM could limit benefits for a company with no HBM exposure.

Order backlog was reported to have risen about 8% from the prior quarter at the end of the second quarter of 2024. Daeduck Electronics focuses on FC BGA for CPUs, which ties it to PC demand, while Simmtech's memory module PCB and package substrate focus leaves it more directly exposed to memory cycles.

The company expects its system IC-related share of total revenue to expand to about 18%, indicating growth beyond memory customers.

Outlook

On November 4, 2026, the company projected fourth-quarter revenue of KRW 98.9 billion for SOCAMM modules and KRW 96.6 billion for system IC-related products. The company indicated that cumulative 2026 SOCAMM module revenue could reach about KRW 147.5 billion.

Daishin Securities, in a November 2026 report, estimated Simmtech's second-half 2026 operating profit at KRW 218.2 billion, which it said is 185% above the first half. The same firm expected expanding SOCAMM2-related revenue to drive second-half earnings improvement.

A February 2026 DB Securities report pointed to whether the company reduces its Tenting share as a key variable for profitability. On the equipment side, analysts said utilization of new capacity, depreciation, and raw material prices should be checked in the company's investor relations materials.

The next checkpoint is the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results, to see whether the company's forecasts for SOCAMM modules and system IC-related products translate into reported revenue.

The 2026 annual business report is due by the end of March 2027, when confirmed segment revenue and full-year operating margin will be disclosed.

08

Bull factors

Expansion of higher-value modules such as SOCAMM

Third-quarter 2026 SOCAMM module revenue was reported at KRW 56.9 billion. Reports differ on the quarter-on-quarter increase, with 35% cited by one outlet and 45% by another, so the exact figure needs cross-checking.

The company projected fourth-quarter SOCAMM revenue of KRW 98.9 billion and expects cumulative annual revenue of about KRW 147.5 billion.

Step-up in operating profit

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 101.8 billion rose 721.6% year on year and was reported to exceed the market consensus of KRW 85.2 billion by 19.4%. Revenue of KRW 535.9 billion grew 43.7% over the same period, so operating profit grew faster than sales. Coverage attributes the improvement to a higher share of technically demanding products.

Memory module PCB share and customer base

A May 2026 report put Simmtech first worldwide with a share of about 30% in memory module PCBs, BoC, and pattern-embedded substrates. Its customers include Samsung Electronics, SK hynix, and Micron.

A December 2025 equity research report expected commercialization of SoCAMM-2 and CXL to expand orders for new substrates. The company expects its system IC-related share of revenue to reach about 18%.

Bear factors

Loss history and recent net losses

Shareholders' net losses persisted for three straight years from 2023 through 2025, with a KRW 164.2 billion loss in 2025. The cumulative shareholders' net loss over the last four quarters was KRW 32 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 shareholders' net income of KRW 25.3 billion fell 43.4% from the prior quarter, so it did not move in the same direction as operating profit.

Raw material, pricing, and product mix variables

In February 2026, DB Securities said fourth-quarter 2025 profitability fell short of market expectations, citing higher gold and copper prices. Margins could be pressured again if raw material costs rise faster than the partial pass-through into selling prices. How far the company reduces its Tenting share also remains a variable for profitability.

HBM-focused capacity allocation and the memory cycle

A 2024 analysis noted that customers allocating capacity mainly to HBM could limit benefits for a company without HBM exposure. The operating losses from the fourth quarter of 2022 through 2024, caused by a downturn and new competitors, show sensitivity to the memory cycle. If the cycle turns down, earnings could revert quickly.

10

Risk factors

New product qualification and yield

New products such as SOCAMM may see large earnings swings depending on customer qualification, production yields, and the memory cycle. Third-quarter 2026 SOCAMM revenue grew, but concentration in a specific product line means qualification delays or yield problems could feed directly into earnings. Earnings visibility remains limited until high-layer substrate yields are confirmed.

Financial structure

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 106.7% in 2022 to 221.8% in 2024 before easing to 181.1% in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 1.04 trillion against total equity of KRW 576.4 billion.

Operating cash flow was negative from 2023 through 2025, so the ability to fund capital spending and working capital is a key item to monitor.

Utilization, raw materials, and FX

Utilization of newly expanded capacity, depreciation burden, and raw material prices are cited as key drivers of margins. In May 2026, Daishin Securities explained that a weaker won and rising server memory module revenue were central to its earnings upgrade. A reversal in the exchange rate could change profitability assumptions.

Overall view

Simmtech moved out of three straight loss-making years from 2023 to 2025 by returning to operating profit in 2025, and its operating profit has risen for three consecutive quarters from the first quarter of 2026.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 535.9 billion and operating profit was KRW 101.8 billion, with higher-value products such as SOCAMM at the center of the improvement.

However, shareholders' net income fell from the prior quarter in the third quarter, and the cumulative shareholders' net loss over the last four quarters was KRW 32 billion, so the sustainability of earnings is still being tested.

Bullish points include reports of the company's first-place global share in memory module PCBs, its fourth-quarter forecasts, and securities firms' second-half operating profit estimates. Bearish points include raw material cost swings, new product qualification and yields, and negative operating cash flow since 2023.

The debt-to-equity ratio eased to 181.1% in 2025 from 221.8% in 2024 but remains high. The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual business report, and first-quarter 2027 results.
