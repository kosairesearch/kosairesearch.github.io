02

Business structure

Mecaro was founded in 1999 and is headquartered in Pyeongtaek, Gyeonggi Province, as a semiconductor equipment parts company.

Its main product is the heater block, which holds and heats wafers during semiconductor manufacturing, and a Sisa Pool article explains that the temperature uniformity and stability of this part in the CVD process govern production yield.

The Korea IR Council said in a report dated November 15, 2025 that Mecaro was the first Korean company to develop and mass-produce metal heater blocks.

Its core technology coats aluminum surfaces with aluminum fluoride (AlF), which is reported to reduce corrosion, extend equipment life and improve surface temperature uniformity.

A Sisa Pool article cited a domestic market share above 90% for the main product based on Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, but this is an outside article's characterization and not a figure confirmed by a filing.

Domestically, the company supplies chipmakers directly, while overseas sales run through local sales subsidiaries and affiliates. In 2022 Mecaro spun off and sold its materials business, narrowing its focus to core semiconductor parts.

Newer areas include advanced ceramic components such as aluminum nitride and next-generation solar cells through a subsidiary.

In July 2026 the company filed a major-matter report on acquiring shares of another company and a post-merger report on an asset transfer, so any change in business scope should be checked in those original filings.

Because the core product is a semiconductor part, sales are heavily tied to chipmakers' equipment investment cycles.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 54 billion in 2022 to KRW 40.8 billion in 2023, down 24.5% year on year, then rose to KRW 62.9 billion in 2024, up 54.2%, and KRW 94.6 billion in 2025, up 50.5%.

Sales grew for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025 after the 2023 low, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period. Operating profit went from KRW 2.55 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 8.47 billion in 2023, a swing into loss, before returning to operating profit of KRW 4.12 billion in 2024.

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 15.4 billion, up 273.0% year on year, and the operating margin moved from -20.8% in 2023 to 16.2% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 43.7 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 2.36 billion in 2023, KRW 5.52 billion in 2024 and KRW 12.6 billion in 2025, up 128.7%.

In 2022 this figure far exceeded operating profit of KRW 2.55 billion, so non-operating items may have had a large effect, and a Sisa Pool article noted that the materials business was spun off and sold that year.

Net profit equaled net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in every year from 2022 to 2025, so no non-controlling interest effect appears in the filings.

Quarterly, Q4 2025 sales were KRW 24.5 billion, down 5.1% from the prior quarter, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 819 million, down 82.1%, the low point of the period.

Q1 2026 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.93 billion, up 501.3% from the prior quarter, the peak of the period, followed by KRW 4.69 billion in Q2 and KRW 3.14 billion in Q3, two consecutive quarterly declines.

Operating profit was KRW 4.11 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 4.49 billion in both Q2 and Q3, and Q3 operating profit was down 4.7% year on year. Q3 2026 sales rose 13.8% year on year, but profit did not follow that growth. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 13.6 billion over the latest four quarters.

05

Industry analysis

Mecaro's market is demand for parts that heat and hold wafers in semiconductor processes, so it is affected by chipmakers' production equipment investment.

In a report dated November 15, 2025, the Korea IR Council said that with rising AI chip demand, Mecaro is widening its market leadership on the back of technological superiority.

A Sisa Pool article noted that as line-width miniaturization advances, differences in equipment and parts technology feed directly into yield, a trend it viewed as favorable for Mecaro.

WiseReport's company monitor, in an earnings comment as of September 7, 2026, summarized that because of the temperature uniformity of its AlF film coating, competitors' products are being replaced by Mecaro's as line widths shrink.

Equipment parts must pass customer performance evaluation before supply starts, so once adopted they are hard to replace, but new entrants are also constrained.

Listed companies in the broader semiconductor equipment and materials parts group include Wonik IPS, Tokai Carbon Korea, LEENO Industrial and HANMI Semiconductor, but the overlap in product lines varies by business, which limits direct comparison.

A Sisa Pool article expected demand for high-precision heater blocks to grow as next-generation processes such as AI and power semiconductors expand. Mecaro's position rests on technology leadership in heater blocks, while customer concentration among chipmakers and their investment plans act as variables.

Outlook

In October 2025 the company said that the final quality test of a ceramic heater it developed earlier that year was in progress and that it expected sales to follow immediately if the test passed, according to a Sisa Pool article.

The result of this test is an important checkpoint for judging when products beyond heater blocks may start to contribute to sales. Advanced ceramic parts such as aluminum nitride components were described as being prepared for entry into high-value materials markets.

The solar cell business, run through a subsidiary, covers next-generation technologies including CIGS, perovskite and transparent solar cells, and the article mentioned the possibility of it becoming a new revenue source within five to ten years.

In April 2026 the company announced a decision to dispose of treasury shares, and in July 2026 it announced a decision to acquire shares of another company and a post-merger report on an asset transfer.

The size and purpose of these transactions should be checked in the original filings, since they show whether business direction is changing. Q3 2026 sales rose 13.8% year on year to KRW 29.3 billion, so whether this growth continues into Q4 2026 is the nearest checkpoint.

Operating profit in Q3 2026 was KRW 4.49 billion, the same as in Q2, so whether higher sales translate into a better operating margin also needs to be watched. The timing of ceramic test results and the reflection of new businesses in sales should be checked in the business description of the 2026 annual report.

Bear factors

Customer concentration and equipment investment cycles

Domestic sales go directly to chipmakers including Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, so results can depend heavily on the investment plans of a small set of customers. When equipment investment slows, heater block orders can slow as well. Overseas sales run through local sales subsidiaries and affiliates, so channel management remains a task.

Earnings volatility and recent slowdown

In 2023 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 8.47 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.36 billion, so it has a recent history of losses.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in Q4 2025 was KRW 819 million, down 82.1% from the prior quarter, and in Q3 2026 it was KRW 3.14 billion, down 31.5% year on year. The two consecutive quarterly declines in Q2 and Q3 2026 could be read as a sign that earnings momentum is weakening.

Execution risk in new businesses

According to a Sisa Pool article, in October 2025 the company said the final quality test of its ceramic heater was in progress. Whether the test passed and when sales were recognized should be checked in the next annual report.

The solar cell business has been described as a possible new revenue source within five to ten years, and the July 2026 share acquisition filing makes post-deal integration a task to monitor.

10

Risk factors

Demand concentration risk

Sales are tied to the equipment investment of domestic chipmakers, so a cut in customer investment could quickly reduce orders. If the pace of process miniaturization slows, demand growth for high-precision heater blocks could slow as well. In a downturn of the semiconductor cycle, earnings swings could widen.

Technology and quality competition risk

Heater blocks are products where temperature uniformity and surface condition govern yield, so a quality problem could lead to customer losses. If competitors close the gap in coating technology, the width of the technology lead could narrow.

Because technology gaps translate into earnings gaps in this field, the sustainability of R&D spending also needs to be checked.

Execution risk in new businesses and acquisitions

Customer qualification of new ceramic materials and expansion of the solar cell business carry investment burdens and possible schedule delays. The July 2026 share acquisition and asset transfer could fall short of expectations during integration. The specific terms of these filings should be confirmed in the original documents.

Overall view

Mecaro has a supply structure centered on domestic chipmakers, built on its heater blocks and AlF coating technology, and its 2025 sales of KRW 94.6 billion and 16.2% operating margin reflect a rise in earnings capacity in the filings.

Bullish factors include the trend of competitors' products being replaced as line widths shrink, a low 9.8% debt ratio, and Q3 2026 sales growth of 13.8% year on year.

Bearish factors include the 31.5% year-on-year drop in net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the same quarter and two consecutive quarterly declines in Q2 and Q3 2026. The company has also swung into operating loss before, in 2023, so earnings volatility remains high.

Customer concentration among domestic chipmakers means orders can depend on their equipment investment cycles. The contribution of new businesses such as ceramic materials and solar cells can be verified from filings only to a limited extent for now.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 and full-year provisional results and the 2026 annual report, and the key question is whether the operating margin holds near the 2025 level.
