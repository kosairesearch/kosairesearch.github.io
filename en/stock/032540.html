Karaoke Leader Holds Share as Profit Slows

TJ Media keeps a dominant share of Korea's coin karaoke market, yet its operating profit has fallen for two straight years since the 2023 peak and turned into an operating loss in the third quarter of 2026.

02

Business structure

TJ Media makes karaoke machines. The company started in 1981 as Taejin Eumhyang, entered the karaoke player market in 1993 and listed on KOSDAQ in 1997.

Because it keeps releasing new songs every month after a unit is sold, it earns content fees alongside equipment sales, so both machine and content revenue arise from the same installed base. More than 99% of revenue comes from karaoke-related businesses.

A June 2025 report by Global Economic put karaoke players at about 30% of sales, music content for players at about 22%, and exports of electronic song indexes to Japan at about 37%.

The app TJ App Noraebang is run through its subsidiary TJ Communication and accounts for roughly 3% of revenue, according to the same report. Exports reached 45% of 2025 sales, mostly from Japan, according to Hankyung.

The Japan business depends on a long-standing relationship with Daiichikosho, a large karaoke operator, so a slump at that customer could cause a sharp drop in sales. In Korea the company shares the market with Geumyoung.

Reports put its domestic share at about 65%, while a company strategy executive said it exceeds 70%. Its share of the coin karaoke market is reported at 97%. Products reach customers through a chain of distributors, dealers and venues.

The company says it is moving beyond machine making toward a platform business, using the 'duet battle' feature, expanded foreign-language songs and a membership app.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 85.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 96.4 billion in 2023, up 12.2% year on year, which is the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 span. It eased to KRW 91.9 billion in 2024, down 4.7%, and recovered to KRW 94.4 billion in 2025, up 2.8%.

Operating profit went from KRW 4.14 billion in 2022 to a peak of KRW 6.33 billion in 2023, up 52.9%, and then fell two years in a row to KRW 4.66 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.29 billion in 2025, the smallest year of the span. In 2025 revenue grew while operating profit fell 29.5%, so costs rose faster than sales.

The operating margin moved from 4.8% in 2022 to 6.6% in 2023, 5.1% in 2024 and 3.5% in 2025. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 4.7 billion in 2023, then declined to KRW 4.64 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.99 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 3.99 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow went from -KRW 2.71 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.65 billion in 2023 and KRW 13.4 billion in 2024, then eased to KRW 3.81 billion in 2025 while staying positive.

By quarter, revenue moved from KRW 24.8 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 20.7 billion in Q4 and KRW 17.4 billion in Q1 2026, then KRW 19.6 billion in Q2 and KRW 16.8 billion in Q3 2026.

Operating profit slid from KRW 1.75 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 546 million in Q4, swung to an operating loss of KRW 676 million in Q1 2026, returned to a profit of KRW 768 million in Q2, and returned to an operating loss of KRW 554 million in Q3 2026.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 4.48 million, down 99.7% year on year, and the four quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 total KRW 2.24 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The karaoke industry has entered a mature phase. According to a Global Economic report citing tax-agency business statistics, the number of karaoke rooms nationwide peaked at 37,994 in 2006 and has declined since, reaching 25,492 as of February 2025.

Coin karaoke has partly offset that decline, with Hankyung reporting 3,218 coin karaoke venues as of late April 2026. A company strategy executive said the karaoke market itself has reached its limit.

Against its rival, TJ Media's revenue rose by close to 70% from KRW 55.6 billion in 2020 to KRW 94.4 billion in 2025, while the No. 2 domestic player's revenue fell from KRW 32.5 billion to KRW 20.2 billion over the same period, widening the gap from 1.7 times to 4.7 times as reported by Hankyung.

The company attributes its 97% share of the coin karaoke market to chip technology and sound performance. In an August 2026 survey it released, 60% of respondents said sound equipment was the most important factor when choosing a karaoke venue. Inbound tourism also supports demand.

The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism reported 17.11 million foreign visitors in January to September 2026, up 21.5% year on year, and Shinhan Card's big-data institute found that karaoke usage counts by foreign tourists from four countries rose 18% in January to July 2025.

Those figures cover the whole karaoke sector and do not map directly onto the company's revenue. The company is classified under electronics and components, but its business is centered on karaoke equipment and content.

06

Outlook

The company keeps releasing products and services aimed at strengthening the karaoke experience. It launched the professional-grade player Real Master 3 on September 15, 2026, and on September 22 began a rental promotion for the A3 and P3 models, which include the 'duet battle' feature.

On September 29 it said J-POP performance usage had risen 104% over two years. It said on August 27 that duet battle usage in July 2026 rose 48.3% year on year.

Its membership app My TJ reported, as of a July 28, 2026 announcement, a 35% increase in new sign-ups and a 47% increase in monthly active users over half a year.

Hankyung reported that the company is pursuing entry into the United States and developing US-oriented software, including a feature for singing with a favorite artist. A later report said each unit carries more than 70,000 songs in 11 languages as standard.

On the new-business side, the company has put KRW 1.3 billion of borrowed funds into an aluminum raw-material trading arrangement. It describes this as a test, and cites raw-material prices, exchange rates, logistics and whether the counterparty performs as risk factors.

The counterparty's name will not be disclosed until July 9, 2027. Greened Economy reported that short-term borrowings rose from KRW 17.4 billion to KRW 30.4 billion after this funding.

08

Bull factors

Dominant coin karaoke share and a widening gap

Reports put the company's coin karaoke share at 97%, and revenue rose from KRW 55.6 billion in 2020 to KRW 94.4 billion in 2025, close to 70%. Over the same period the No. 2 domestic player's revenue declined, widening the gap. The company cites its chip technology and sound performance as the basis for that share.

Export base and foreign demand

Exports accounted for 45% of 2025 sales, mostly from Japan. Foreign visitors to Korea rose 21.5% year on year in January to September 2026, and the units carry songs in 11 languages. Reports say the company is pursuing US entry and developing software aimed at that market.

Platform metrics and financial capacity

The company reported that its membership app gained 35% in new sign-ups and 47% in monthly active users over half a year, and duet battle usage rose 48.3% in July 2026. Its debt ratio was 52.0% at the end of 2025, and a credit rating of AA- from Korea Credit Data was reported in March.

09

Bear factors

Quarterly revenue decline and losses

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 16.8 billion, down 32.5% year on year. The company posted an operating loss of KRW 554 million, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders was only KRW 4.48 million in the same quarter. The four-quarter total is KRW 2.24 billion.

A maturing karaoke market

The number of karaoke rooms nationwide has declined since its 2006 peak and stood at 25,492 as of February 2025. Growth in coin karaoke only partly offsets that.

As the number of rooms shrinks, the base for replacement and new machine demand narrows, so how much new demand such as coin karaoke makes up for the decline is the next point to check.

Concentration on a Japanese customer

Exports were 45% of 2025 sales, and electronic song index exports to Japan were reported at about 37% of revenue. The business relies on a long relationship with one large Japanese karaoke operator, and analysts note sales could drop sharply if that market weakens. Diversifying exports is cited as a task.

10

Risk factors

Platform and product execution

The company is pursuing its platform strategy through the Real Master 3 launch, the A3 and P3 rental promotion and the duet battle feature. If the costs of the rental promotion and new launches do not translate into sales, selling and administrative expenses could rise.

The pace at which the membership app and expanded foreign-language songs convert into content fees will determine whether this strategy succeeds. A slower conversion could delay recovery of operating profit.

New-business borrowing and commodity risk

The company has committed KRW 13.0 billion of borrowed funds to aluminum raw-material trading. It cites commodity prices, exchange rates, logistics and the counterparty's performance as risks. Short-term borrowings were reported to have risen from KRW 17.4 billion to KRW 30.4 billion.

Inbound foreign demand

Rising use of karaoke by foreign visitors to Korea supports demand for coin karaoke. If this trend weakens, venue usage counts could fall and affect sales of replacement machines and content fees. Foreign usage depends on exchange rates and travel demand, so its contribution to the company's revenue is hard to gauge. A slowdown in domestic demand at the same time could weigh on coin karaoke usage overall.

Overall view

TJ Media is a karaoke equipment maker built on a 97% share of the coin karaoke market and exports equal to 45% of sales. Revenue rose from KRW 85.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 94.4 billion in 2025, but operating profit fell from a KRW 6.33 billion peak in 2023 to KRW 3.29 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue in 2026 moved through KRW 17.4 billion, KRW 19.6 billion and KRW 16.8 billion, with operating losses in Q1 and Q3. On the financial side, the debt ratio was 52.0% at the end of 2025 and all KRW 83.8 billion of equity belongs to controlling shareholders.

Against that, a maturing karaoke market, concentration on one Japanese customer and a borrowing-funded commodity venture are the main burdens.

The company is pushing a platform strategy through duet battle, its membership app and expanded foreign-language songs, and the results will appear in revenue and operating profit.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, Q1 2027 results, and whether the counterparty is disclosed before July 9, 2027.
