SCD: Third-Quarter Turn to Profit Amid Margin Pressure

02

Business structure

SCD was founded in 1987 under a former name and listed on KOSDAQ in 2000. Its core business is home appliance components for refrigerators and air conditioners, and by product its domestic market share is 63% for ice makers, 89% for step valves and 40% for BLDC motors, according to FnGuide's company monitor.

All three product lines are components inside finished appliances, so they depend on finished-goods production plans and household appliance demand. Customers are appliance manufacturers, and component pricing and volumes tend to follow their production schedules.

In competition, Chinese local manufacturers are growing in mid- and low-end products, intensifying price competition. The same source notes that imports and distribution price competition weighed on the appliance component business, including motor products.

Costs are sensitive to raw material prices and exchange rates, which the company cited as factors behind the profit decline in 2025.

The company is pursuing an automotive parts manufacturing and sales business and holds several patents related to valve actuators for integrated thermal management modules in electric vehicles. Consolidated revenue for 2025 was KRW 216.1 billion, up 1.0% year on year.

In a January 2026 disclosure, the company said it had maintained operating profit in the black for 16 consecutive years since 2010.

Earnings analysis

Annual results moved in different directions for revenue and profit. Revenue fell from KRW 218.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 194 billion in 2023, down 11.4% year on year, then rose to KRW 214 billion in 2024, up 10.3%, and to KRW 216.1 billion in 2025, up 1.0%, marking two consecutive years of growth from 2024 to 2025.

Operating profit was largest in 2022 at KRW 11.3 billion among the 2022 to 2025 years and smallest in 2023 at KRW 5.75 billion, down 49.3% year on year. It recovered to KRW 9.34 billion in 2024, up 62.6%, before falling to KRW 6.31 billion in 2025, down 32.5%.

The operating margin was 5.2% in 2022, 3.0% in 2023, 4.4% in 2024 and 2.9% in 2025, so the 2025 margin was below even the 2023 level. Controlling-shareholder net profit was largest in 2024 at KRW 8.39 billion, up 73.4%, and fell to KRW 6.4 billion in 2025, down 23.8%.

In every year from 2022 to 2025, net profit and controlling-shareholder net profit were reported at the same amount. For 2025, the company attributed the profit decline to higher raw material prices and currency pressure amid US-China trade tensions and US tariff policy.

Operating cash flow was KRW 14.5 billion in 2022 and KRW 10 billion in 2023, turned negative at KRW -523 million in 2024, and returned to positive at KRW 5.91 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio declined from 40.0% in 2022 to 28.0% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 136.5 billion and total liabilities of KRW 38.2 billion at the end of 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit moved from KRW 1.31 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 589 million in the fourth quarter, down 55.0% from the prior quarter, then KRW 510 million in the first quarter of 2026, down 13.8%, and KRW 88.59 million in the second quarter, down 82.6%.

In the second quarter of 2026, controlling-shareholder results turned to a net loss of KRW 62.39 million. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 54.1 billion, up 13.0% from the prior quarter and up 2.0% year on year, but operating profit of KRW 326 million was down 75.1% year on year.

Controlling-shareholder net profit over the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.7 billion. The reported figures alone do not allow the size of any one-off items to be separated out.

Industry analysis

End demand comes from finished refrigerators and air conditioners. FnGuide's company monitor attributed the first-half 2026 decline in the appliance component business to the growth of Chinese local manufacturers, rising imports and intensifying distribution price competition.

That explanation puts more weight on price competition than on a slowdown in finished-appliance demand, which is a distinction worth keeping in view. In cycle terms, the segment appears to face both a question of finished-goods demand recovery and component price competition at the same time.

On costs, the company said raw material prices and currency movements affect profitability simultaneously. By product, SCD's domestic shares of 89% for step valves and 63% for ice makers are relatively high, while its 40% share in BLDC motors is comparatively lower.

Because high-share and low-share products sit side by side, competitive intensity may differ by product. Expansion by Chinese local manufacturers into mid- and low-end products is the most direct structural variable for both share and pricing.

The move into automotive parts rests on patents related to EV thermal management modules, but the revenue contribution of this business is not yet separable in the reported results.

Whether the company responds to price competition mainly through cost reduction or product mix adjustment will show up in cost-ratio changes in upcoming annual reports.

Risk factors

Tariffs and trade policy

The company linked the 2025 profit decline to an unstable external environment, including US-China trade tensions and US tariff policy. If US tariff levels or their scope change, finished-appliance customers' production plans and component trading terms could shift.

In that case, component volumes and prices could move together and widen earnings swings. This factor affects revenue structure separately from the cost line.

Finished appliance demand

Whether demand for finished appliances recovers has not yet been settled. Component volumes and prices tend to follow finished-goods production plans, so a cut in finished production could reduce component shipments.

Customers are concentrated among appliance manufacturers, so production adjustments by some customers feed directly into results. This factor is a separate volume-side driver of earnings.

New business uncertainty and earnings volatility

The automotive parts business has been described at a patent-securing stage, and its revenue contribution is not separable in the reported figures. The timing and scale of when a new business reaches the income statement are uncertain.

With controlling-shareholder net profit totaling KRW 1.7 billion over the last four quarters, small cost changes can move growth rates sharply. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 75.1% year on year.

Overall view

SCD holds product lines with high domestic market shares in home appliance components, and the company has emphasized maintaining operating profit in the black since 2010. Revenue rose to KRW 216.1 billion in 2025, but operating profit fell to KRW 6.31 billion, lowering profitability.

Controlling-shareholder net profit turned positive in the third quarter of 2026, but operating profit fell 75.1% year on year. The balance sheet looks relatively stable with a 28.0% debt ratio and KRW 136.5 billion in total equity, and operating cash flow turned positive in 2025.

On the other side, price competition from Chinese local manufacturers and raw material and currency costs are weighing on margins, and controlling-shareholder net profit over the last four quarters totaled only KRW 1.7 billion. Valuation shows a mix of a discount to book value and a high multiple on earnings.
