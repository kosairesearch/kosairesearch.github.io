02

Business structure

Dealicious is a fashion B2B platform company founded in 2011. Its core business is a proprietary platform that links Dongdaemun wholesalers with retail operators in Korea and abroad.

Recent press reports indicate that more than 80% of operating revenue in 2025 came from the platform business, mainly advertising products aimed at wholesalers and subscription services for wholesalers and retailers.

Platform operating revenue grew at an average annual rate of 30.4% from 2022 through 2025, while total operating revenue rose 23.7% a year over the same period to KRW 30.7 billion in 2025.

Cumulative transaction value on the platform is about KRW 5 trillion, and more than 2 million new products are registered each year. The company claims a 97% share of daily active users among fashion B2B platforms.

Independent research firm Value Finder reported about 48,000 average daily active users as of September 2025, far above two competing platforms with roughly 1,300 and 300 users. Because the share figure rests largely on company data, it cannot be read as an industry-wide statistic.

Overseas operations began with Japan shipments in 2022 and expanded to shipping services covering 91 countries, and direct shipping to Taiwan and Hong Kong was introduced in 2026. The company has stated that AI-based ad optimization roughly doubled ad click-through rates. Fashion SaaS, built on the Plus membership, is presented as the next growth driver.

03

Recent trends

Based on third-quarter 2026 DART disclosures, revenue reached KRW 8.28 billion, up 16.5% year on year. Operating profit for the same period was KRW 1.3 billion, up 1,763.5%, and net income turned to a profit of KRW 1.54 billion.

The third quarter is a seasonally slow period in fashion, and the company attributed the growth in revenue and profit to a restructuring that prioritizes high-margin businesses. According to press reports, both metrics still rose year on year even though fewer business days fell in September due to the Chuseok holiday.

Third-quarter operating profit fell 49.1% from the prior quarter, following second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.56 billion. Seoul Shinmun reported first-half 2026 consolidated operating revenue of KRW 17.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.2 billion, a margin of 18.2%.

For 2025 as a whole, revenue was KRW 30.7 billion and operating profit was KRW 1.6 billion, turning the company from loss to profit, and revenue rose 44.8% over two years from KRW 21.2 billion in 2023.

As of the snapshot date, the share price was 2,040 won (down 0.73% from the prior close), market capitalization was KRW 45.5 billion, and trading value was about KRW 200 million.

A November 2026 report said a shareholder meeting agenda would keep the annual director compensation limit at KRW 1.5 billion while pursuing a capital reserve reduction, which is an accounting step that brings no cash into the company. Third-quarter figures are preliminary and may change after the external audit.

04

Outlook

In a July 2026 press report, the company projected 2026 operating revenue of KRW 37.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 9.0 billion. These are company-issued projections and have not been independently verified, and the annual target can only be completed once fourth-quarter results are reflected.

The company said it plans to strengthen platform competitiveness and continue profit-focused management during the fourth quarter, the fashion industry's peak season.

On the revenue side, each increase in advertising and subscription sales is linked to operating profit, and Value Finder cited a 2025 contribution margin of 81%. Overseas, the key challenge is securing local retail operators in new markets such as Taiwan and Hong Kong, building on shipping to 91 countries.

The fashion SaaS business depends on expansion of the Plus membership, so the timing and scale of its revenue contribution have not yet appeared in disclosures.

On capital, press coverage has framed whether the capital reserve reduction will lead to the first dividend since listing as a point of interest, although the reduction itself brings no cash into the company. Third-quarter figures are preliminary and may change after the audit.

AI-based ad optimization, built on the user base and transaction data, remains the central variable for margin improvement.

05

Bull factors

Shift in earnings quality

The company describes its platform business as generating more than 80% of operating revenue, with advertising and subscriptions carrying little variable cost. Value Finder cited a 2025 contribution margin of 81% and described a structure in which higher sales flow through to profit.

Profitability improved quickly after losses from 2017 to 2022, which were incurred while building platform competitiveness, as fixed costs declined.

User base and transaction scale

Average daily active users were about 48,000 as of September 2025, well above two competing platforms according to an independent research report. The company claims a 97% daily active user share among fashion B2B platforms.

Cumulative transaction value is about KRW 5 trillion, and annual new product listings exceeding 2 million feed a growing data base.

Room to expand overseas and into new businesses

Shipping services, which started with Japan in 2022, now cover 91 countries, and direct shipping to Taiwan and Hong Kong was introduced in 2026. The company has said it plans to enter fashion SaaS, building on its Plus membership.

If overseas retail operator recruitment succeeds, platform transaction volume and advertising demand could both be affected.

06

Bear factors

Seasonality and quarterly volatility

Third-quarter operating profit fell 49.1% from the prior quarter, and the company itself describes the third quarter as a slow season for fashion. Reaching the annual target depends heavily on fourth-quarter peak-season results. Quarterly swings are large, which makes it hard to judge the trend from any single quarter.

Reliance on company-reported figures and preliminary results

Third-quarter results were disclosed as preliminary before external audit, so final figures may differ. The 97% share, the roughly doubled ad click-through rate, and the annual profit projection are all company-provided numbers. The scope of metrics that can be independently cross-checked is limited.

Thin liquidity and a short profit history

On the snapshot date, trading value was about KRW 200 million against a market capitalization of KRW 45.5 billion, indicating shallow liquidity. Losses accumulated from 2017 to 2022, and 2025 was the first year of annual operating profit, so the profit record is short.

Because the company has been listed only briefly, there is not yet enough data on how trading flows and the share price track earnings.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Fashion consumption and wholesale-retail activity are sensitive to interest rates, exchange rates and consumer sentiment. Expanding overseas retail operators increases exposure to currency swings and international shipping costs. A weaker macro backdrop could reduce wholesale volume and advertising demand at the same time.

Business and competition

If competing services change fee or advertising terms in fashion B2B platforms, the earnings structure could be affected. Revenue is concentrated in advertising and subscriptions, so user attrition would translate quickly into lower sales. Because the share figure is based on company data, independent verification is limited.

Liquidity

Trading value on the snapshot date was about KRW 200 million, so price moves can be large even for modest orders. Because the listing is recent, institutional and foreign investor flow data is limited.

The shareholder meeting agenda on director compensation and the capital reserve reduction may draw attention to shareholder returns but does not change cash flow.

08

Overall view

In its third-quarter 2026 DART disclosure, Dealicious reported revenue of KRW 8.28 billion, operating profit of KRW 1.3 billion and net income of KRW 1.54 billion, with profit growing sharply year on year.

A high share of platform revenue, centered on advertising and subscriptions, is the basis for the bull case that higher sales flow into profit. On the other side, third-quarter operating profit declined from the prior quarter, and meeting the annual projection depends on fourth-quarter results.

Many indicators, including the daily active user share and AI ad performance, are company-reported, which limits external verification. Overseas shipping expansion and fashion SaaS are presented as medium-term growth options, but their revenue scale has not yet appeared in disclosures.

A market capitalization of KRW 45.5 billion and trading value of about KRW 200 million on the snapshot date describe the liquidity profile.

The 2025 return to profit and the first-half 2026 margin of 18.2% support the improvement case, but the short profit record means it is too early to treat them as an established trend. This summary is a qualitative overview provided for information only.
