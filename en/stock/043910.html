02

Business structure

Natureand Environment is a construction and environmental company that combines precast concrete (PC) work as its core with soil remediation, climate-environment projects and modular housing.

The company set up its corporate environmental ecology engineering research institute in May 2001 and has continued work on eco-friendly materials, natural purification and ecological restoration.

At its May 2026 investor briefing, the company said it is widening its environmental business from the PC base into soil remediation, climate-environment works, rainwater storage tanks and ecological blocks.

Soil remediation is positioned around difficult sites such as military and industrial-complex land and national remediation programs. Press reports said soil remediation revenue rose from KRW 2.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 9 billion in 2025.

The PC contracts disclosed in 2026 were with large builders such as Gyeryong Construction Industrial, Woomi Development & Construction, SK Ecoplant and CJ Logistics, and several were joint-venture roles. Some contracts carry no down payment or advance and are paid monthly on progress billings.

In modular housing, a PC modular housing technology co-developed with the Korea Institute of Civil Engineering and Building Technology received New Construction Technology designation and industrialized-housing certification from the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport.

The company also cited a waste-plastic recycling project using its EcoC-Cube technology and the supply of carbon dioxide capture equipment to the Korea Electric Power Research Institute. Major inputs such as aggregate, cement and EPS blocks come from domestic producers and distributors.

04

Earnings analysis

[Filed results] Annual revenue went from KRW 73.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 69.7 billion in 2023 (down 5.6% year on year), KRW 40.7 billion in 2024 (down 41.6%) and KRW 37.3 billion in 2025 (down 8.4%), a decline for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. 2022 was the largest year in the period and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 464 million in 2022 and KRW 4.53 billion in 2023 (up 876.9%), but the company moved into an operating loss of KRW 2.12 billion in 2024 and the loss widened to KRW 3.98 billion in 2025. The operating margin fell from 0.6% in 2022 and 6.5% in 2023 to -5.2% in 2024 and -10.7% in 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 4.35 billion in 2023 turned into a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 3.08 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.6 billion in 2025, two straight years of losses.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 6.96 billion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder measure.

The company attributed the 2025 revenue decline to adjusted project start timing and to deliveries from its order backlog that slipped into later periods, and attributed the wider loss to added professional staff and R&D spending for its new modular business and a higher fixed-cost ratio as revenue fell.

Operating cash flow was KRW 4.2 billion positive in 2023 but negative at KRW -3.95 billion in 2024 and KRW -3.06 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 87.7 billion, equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 82.8 billion and total liabilities were KRW 44.5 billion, and the debt ratio moved from 62.6% in 2022 to 49.7% in 2024 and 50.7% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue reached a low of KRW 4.99 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, down 42.7% from the prior quarter, after KRW 8.7 billion in the third quarter.

It then rose to KRW 8.12 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 9.07 billion in the second and KRW 10.7 billion in the third, three straight quarterly increases.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 4.07 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 161 million in the first quarter of 2026, then KRW 290 million in the second quarter and KRW 288 million in the third.

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 247 million in the first quarter of 2026, then a loss of KRW 247 million in the second quarter and KRW 10.72 million in the third.

The cumulative controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters is KRW 4.35 billion, mostly from the fourth-quarter 2025 loss of KRW 4.34 billion. Only three quarters of operating profit have been recorded, so whether the turnaround holds needs more quarters to judge.

05

Industry analysis

The company itself cited the construction slowdown and the contraction of the real estate project financing (PF) market as the background to its 2025 earnings decline.

Because PC work depends on private construction starts, the company is diversifying its clients toward public orders, national remediation programs and logistics-center PC projects.

Press coverage says demand for soil remediation is rising as heavy-metal and petroleum hydrocarbon contamination becomes a social issue, since pollutants accumulate in soil and threaten groundwater and the food chain.

In PC work, the company participates through joint ventures led by large builders, using its own production method. The 2026 contracts run into 2027 through 2029, so orders may be recognized as revenue over several years.

The company highlights its remediation technology on difficult sites and the New Construction Technology designation and industrialized-housing certification for its PC modular housing as differentiators. Rainwater storage tanks are positioned around demand for responses to sudden heavy rain and urban heat islands.

Compared with large builders, its revenue base is small, so the timing of a few large projects can move quarterly results considerably.

06

Outlook

At its May 2026 investor briefing, the company said its KRW 122.7 billion order backlog supports a projection that 2026 revenue will reach KRW 46.9 billion, up about 22% from the prior year. This is the company's own forecast and not a filed result.

The company said delayed major projects will begin construction and shipment in earnest from 2026, lifting revenue and turning the business profitable. It described the backlog as about 3.2 times 2025 revenue and said it maintains a BBB- credit rating.

Press reports put the soil remediation order backlog at about KRW 25.2 billion as of April 2026.

Major contracts disclosed in 2026 include a soil remediation contract at the Godeok International Urban Development Area worth KRW 3.954 billion (scheduled to end May 26, 2027), a PC contract at the Dangjin Seokmun Industrial Complex worth KRW 4.301 billion (through March 31, 2029), a PC contract at Goyang Changneung worth KRW 2.436 billion (through September 30, 2028), a PC contract for a Namyangju logistics center worth KRW 9.375 billion (through November 30, 2027), a PC contract at Uiwang Station SK VIEW worth KRW 5.41 billion (through December 31, 2029), and a PC contract at Cheonan Upseong worth KRW 4.337 billion (through March 16, 2029).

Because construction runs into 2027 and beyond, revenue recognition from these contracts is likely to spread over several years.

Operating profit has been positive for three quarters of 2026, but confirming the company's KRW 46.9 billion revenue projection and a full-year turnaround requires the fourth quarter and full-year totals.

The 2026 annual business report, the next filed report, is the next checkpoint for confirmed revenue and operating margin.

10

Risk factors

Order execution risk

In 2025, revenue slipped into later periods because project starts were adjusted and some deliveries had not yet reached their timing. A large backlog does not guarantee quick revenue if construction and shipment are delayed. Many contracts disclosed in 2026 run into 2027 or beyond.

Cash flow risk

Cash outflow from operations persisted across both years, which could magnify the burden of the contract terms below. Some contracts have no down payment or advance and pay on monthly progress billings, which can add working-capital pressure. Whether the profit turnaround becomes cash inflow is a point for the next annual report.

Cost structure risk

The 2025 loss widening was attributed to added professional staff and R&D costs for the modular business, plus a higher fixed-cost ratio as sales fell. If revenue recovery is slower than the growth in fixed costs, margin recovery will be constrained. Results from the new business have not yet appeared clearly in reported profit.

Overall view

Natureand Environment's filed results show revenue declining for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and operating losses and controlling-shareholder net losses for two straight years in 2024 and 2025.

However, revenue has risen for three straight quarters in 2026, and operating profit was recorded in each of the first three quarters. The company projects a 2026 sales rebound supported by its KRW 122.7 billion backlog, but this is a company forecast, not a filed result.

Positive factors are the order backlog, the accumulation of profitable quarters and a debt ratio that has fallen since 2024.

Negative factors are the continued annual losses, controlling-shareholder net losses in the second and third quarters of 2026, and order timing shaped by the construction slowdown and the PF contraction.

Large PC contracts disclosed in October and November 2026 run into 2029, so how quickly orders convert into revenue is a key variable.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 preliminary full-year results and the 2026 annual business report, where full-year operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income will show the most direct evidence. This report does not present an investment opinion or target price.
