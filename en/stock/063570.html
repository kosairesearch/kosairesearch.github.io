NICE Infra: Earnings Recovery Meets Rising Leverage

NICE Infra's quarterly operating profit has risen for two consecutive quarters in 2026, but its debt ratio climbed to 244.8% at the end of 2025, keeping the balance sheet as the key checkpoint.

  2. 2

  4. 4

02

Business structure

NICE Infra operates and manages unmanned systems such as ATMs, unmanned parking, kiosks and EV charging. Its business is organized around three pillars: digital finance (ATM bulk management and ATM VAN), mobility (parking and EV), and unmanned solutions (kiosks and video).

According to WiseReport's company monitor, the digital finance segment keeps a market-leading position in bulk ATM management and ATM VAN, and expanded outsourcing of off-site ATM units has been cited as a driver of improved results.

Sales mix was 86% unmanned automated system operation and 14% cash logistics, based on Q2 2026 revenue per Butler. Domestic share of sales was 99.91% in 2025, with exports at 0.09%, so the customer base is domestic.

In parking, the company supplies payment units and visitor kiosks through its NICEPARK platform, and in May 2026 reports said it ranked first in the parking platform category of the 2026 Brand Customer Loyalty Awards.

At SECON 2026 in March 2026, it unveiled the AI BOX video analytics platform and unmanned operation systems for lodging and school commuting.

Between late August and early September 2026, notices of IT infrastructure contractor selections, including server maintenance, network restructuring and NAC replacement, followed.

Its customer base centers on B2B clients such as banks, card issuers and retail outlets, and competition is split between incumbents in ATM management and unmanned operators led by video and AI solutions.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 288.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 365.5 billion in 2023 (up 26.5% year on year), fell to KRW 338.7 billion in 2024 (down 7.3%), and reached KRW 384 billion in 2025 (up 13.4%), the largest year of 2022-2025.

Operating profit was KRW 18.9 billion in 2022, KRW 28 billion in 2023 (up 48.2%), KRW 18.7 billion in 2024 (down 33.2%) and KRW 30.7 billion in 2025 (up 64.2%), with 2024 the smallest year. Operating margin was 6.5%, 7.7%, 5.5% and 8.0% for 2022 through 2025, highest in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 8.56 billion in 2022, KRW 14.3 billion in 2023 (up 66.9%), KRW 2.46 billion in 2024 (down 82.8%) and KRW 7.09 billion in 2025 (up 188.5%), with 2023 the largest year.

Consolidated net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 7.19 billion in 2025, a different figure that should be read separately. Operating cash flow was KRW 70 billion in 2022, KRW 57.1 billion in 2023, KRW 65.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 82.6 billion in 2025, the largest of the four years.

Quarterly, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 101 billion, operating profit KRW 8.79 billion and controlling-shareholder net profit KRW 4.58 billion.

Q4 2025 revenue rose to KRW 106.2 billion (up 5.1% quarter on quarter), but operating profit fell to KRW 7.49 billion (down 14.7%) and the company posted a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 571 million.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 103.2 billion, operating profit KRW 7.47 billion and controlling-shareholder net profit KRW 1.75 billion, a return to profit. Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 110.5 billion (up 7.0%), operating profit KRW 9.65 billion (up 29.2%) and controlling-shareholder net profit KRW 4.95 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 115.4 billion (up 14.2% year on year), operating profit KRW 9.79 billion (up 11.3%), while controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 4.21 billion fell 8.0% year on year. Controlling-shareholder net profit over the latest four quarters totals KRW 10.3 billion.

LS Securities attributed the Q1 2026 improvement to unmanned parking site expansion, the effect of acquiring convenience-store equipment, and better EV charging operations.

05

Industry analysis

The company says the unmanned operation market is growing quickly, and it plans to strengthen competitiveness by combining AI video technology with unmanned operation systems. In digital finance, expanded outsourcing of off-site ATM units by banks is cited as a demand driver.

The company is also pursuing bank alternative channels such as co-branded ATMs and digital outlets. In EV charging, reports said it was selected as an operator of a 2025 government subsidy program for charging facilities.

In parking, it is widening its scope with unmanned parking equipment and platform-based solutions and pushing to expand orders. The decline in cash usage is a variable that could affect long-term demand for ATMs and cash logistics.

On the other side, AI video-based unmanned solutions are expanding into lodging, schools and industrial sites. Its digital finance segment holds a market-leading position, while parking, kiosks and video face many competing vendors, so competitive intensity differs by business line.

06

Outlook

In its corporate value-up plan disclosed on March 31, 2026, the company set the goals of upgrading its infrastructure business based on unmanned operation solutions and establishing stable, predictable shareholder return policies.

It plans to build on its digital finance, mobility and unmanned solution businesses while expanding new areas based on AI video technology. The same plan listed a 2025 payout ratio of 59.06% and stated that the company qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act.

The disclosure showed the dividend amount for the latest fiscal year rose 12.00% from the year before that. The plan includes forward-looking statements and may change.

On the earnings side, LS Securities estimated that acquiring 8,000 to 9,000 convenience-store units could add annual sales of KRW 20 billion to 25 billion and operating profit of KRW 4 billion to 5 billion. That is the broker's estimate rather than a company-confirmed figure.

In its Q3 2025 release, the company expected monthly operating profit to turn positive within Q1 2026, and Q1 2026 operating profit was KRW 7.47 billion.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 provisional results and the 2026 annual report, with attention to how the acquired equipment affects operating profit after Q4 2026 and to the debt ratio trend. Because quarterly earnings swing widely, whether net profit follows operating profit is also worth tracking.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly earnings recovery

After controlling-shareholder net profit returned to profit in Q1 2026, operating profit reached KRW 9.65 billion in Q2, up 29.2% quarter on quarter. Q3 operating profit of KRW 9.79 billion rose 11.3% year on year, extending the increase to two consecutive quarters.

Q3 revenue of KRW 115.4 billion was up 14.2% year on year. Whether the margin recovery continues is the core of the bullish case.

Market-leading digital finance business

According to WiseReport's company monitor, the digital finance segment keeps a market-leading position in bulk ATM management and ATM VAN. Expanded outsourcing of off-site ATM units is cited as a factor that improved results. A nationwide network is seen as a strength in bank-related contracts. This business base serves as the starting point for the newer unmanned operation businesses.

Dividend plan and shareholder return policy

In its 2026 corporate value-up plan, the company set a stable and predictable shareholder return policy. The 2025 payout ratio was listed at 59.06%, and the company said it qualifies as a high-dividend company. The dividend amount for the latest fiscal year rose 12.00% from the year before that. The disclosure states it will secure dividend funding through profitability-focused management.

09

Bear factors

Rising financial leverage

The debt ratio climbed from 183.3% in 2022 to 182.8% in 2023 and 203.5% in 2024, reaching 244.8% in 2025. Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 449.3 billion, well above total equity of KRW 183.6 billion. As liabilities grow against equity, financing costs and refinancing burdens can rise. If debt grows faster than operating profit, financial flexibility narrows.

Volatile quarterly net profit

Controlling-shareholder net profit swung to a KRW 571 million net loss in Q4 2025 before returning to profit in Q1 2026. Q3 2026 controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 4.21 billion fell 8.0% year on year. In 2024, controlling-shareholder net profit dropped 82.8% to KRW 2.46 billion.

The latest four-quarter total of KRW 10.3 billion reflects an earnings base that moves considerably from quarter to quarter.

Domestic focus and dependence on cash usage

Domestic sales accounted for 99.91% in 2025, with exports at just 0.09%. Cash logistics made up 14% of sales by Q2 2026 revenue. If cash usage declines, demand for ATM operation and cash logistics could both be affected.

With small overseas operations, the company is highly exposed to domestic market conditions and regulatory changes.

10

Risk factors

Financial structure

With liabilities well above equity, sustained high interest rates could erode operating profit through financing costs. Concentrated debt maturities could make refinancing terms a burden before earnings reflect it. If dividends and investment rise together, cash headroom could tighten. The debt ratio trend is the most direct indicator to monitor.

Changes in the business environment

Declining cash usage and shifts in bank branch strategy directly affect demand for ATM management and cash logistics. If banks change how they operate in-house, outsourcing volumes could fall. Parking and kiosk markets have many competing vendors, which could intensify price competition. Given the business structure, results are sensitive to changes in its core bases.

Realization of new businesses and acquisition effects

LS Securities estimated the earnings effect of acquiring convenience-store equipment in annual sales and operating profit terms, but that is the broker's analysis. Whether the acquired equipment is integrated and operated as planned still needs confirmation.

Timing of orders for AI video-based solutions is uncertain during development and sales. The company's plans include forward-looking statements and may change.

12

Overall view

NICE Infra is a domestically focused operator of unmanned infrastructure such as ATMs, unmanned parking and kiosks, and its 2025 revenue of KRW 384 billion and operating profit of KRW 30.7 billion were the largest of 2022-2025.

Earnings that fell sharply in 2024 have moved toward recovery in the 2025 and 2026 quarterly figures, and Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 9.79 billion rose 11.3% year on year.

At the same time, Q3 2026 controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 4.21 billion fell 8.0% year on year, so the operating recovery has not fully carried through to net profit.

The debt ratio rose to 244.8% at the end of 2025 from 183.3% in 2022, with total liabilities of KRW 449.3 billion exceeding total equity of KRW 183.6 billion. Bullish factors are the market-leading digital finance business, expansion in unmanned parking and video solutions, and the dividend plan.

Bearish factors are financial leverage, quarterly swings in net profit, and dependence on the cash usage environment. The share price is trading below book value per share, but how that level should be read depends on the debt structure and the sustainability of earnings.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 provisional results and the 2026 annual report's debt ratio and operating margin.
