02

Business structure

PRO2000 manufactures OLED and LCD display inspection equipment and semiconductor test equipment for DDI chip inspection. Its main products are display probe units for panel inspection and probe cards for semiconductor testing, and the probe units make electrical contact with panels to identify defects.

Display test equipment generated KRW 30.4 billion in 2025, or 96.4% of total revenue. The semiconductor share fell from 10.0% in 2023 to 4.5% in 2024 and 2.1% in 2025. Main customers are Samsung Display and LG Display, with BOE and CSOT as the main overseas buyers.

The company has supplied Carrier Jig products to Samsung Display since 2021. Revenue is recognized after the customer's quality conditions are met and the product is approved and delivered, so approval of new products is the gateway to revenue.

Probe cards serve NAND, DRAM, CIS and SoC testing, but customer mass-production qualification is the key condition. An interview carried by a job-listing site says the company holds 125 patents and ranks first in domestic display probe devices.

By sector classification, PRO2000 is grouped with TSE, PSK and LEENO Industrial among semiconductor equipment related stocks.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 38.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 33.4 billion in 2023 (down 12.6% year on year), recovered to KRW 36 billion in 2024 (up 8.0%), and dropped to KRW 31.5 billion in 2025 (down 12.5%), the smallest year in the four-year span.

Operating profit declined from KRW 3.07 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.47 billion in 2023 (down 52.2%) and KRW 1.18 billion in 2024 (down 19.9%), then recovered to KRW 2.77 billion in 2025 (up 135.5%). Operating margin moved from 8.0% in 2022 and 4.4% in 2023 to 3.3% in 2024 and 8.8% in 2025.

Margin improved in 2025 even as revenue fell, which points to a change in the cost structure. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.56 billion in 2022, KRW 3.45 billion in 2023 (down 24.3%), KRW 5.6 billion in 2024 (up 62.5%) and KRW 3.92 billion in 2025 (down 30.1%).

In 2024 operating profit fell while controlling-shareholder profit rose, so the two measures diverged, and the components should be checked in the periodic report. Net profit equaled profit attributable to controlling shareholders in all four years.

At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 42.9 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 5.25 billion. The debt ratio went from 12.0% in 2022 to 18.9% in 2023 and 12.2% in 2025.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 8.78 billion in 2024 to KRW 3.04 billion in 2025, weakening in the year operating profit recovered.

Quarterly, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 6 billion with an operating loss of KRW 347 million, and Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 8.22 billion (up 36.9% from the prior quarter) produced operating profit of KRW 1.67 billion.

Q1 2026 revenue fell 18.8% to KRW 6.68 billion with an operating loss of KRW 740 million, and Q2 2026 recovered to KRW 8.96 billion of revenue, KRW 1.23 billion of operating profit and KRW 5.12 billion of controlling-shareholder profit.

Q3 2026 revenue reached KRW 9.34 billion (up 55.6% year on year) and operating profit KRW 1.7 billion, but the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.3 billion. The four quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 total KRW 7.34 billion of controlling-shareholder profit.

05

Industry analysis

PRO2000's demand is tied to inspection needs at display panel makers. Changes in panel type or rising inspection difficulty create demand for more precise probe units, so the shift toward OLED in smartphones, foldables and tablets is a variable for orders.

Display test equipment revenue moved within a KRW 29 billion to KRW 34 billion range over 2023 to 2025, a narrow band for three years. Display demand is strongly linked to investment plans and utilization at major customers such as Samsung Display.

In semiconductors, probe cards could extend to memory and image sensor testing, but the 2.1% share in 2025 keeps this a secondary business.

Approval processes at overseas big tech companies are reported to be demanding, with approvals of equipment and parts reportedly made after visits to panel suppliers' partner plants, which implies long qualification periods for new customers.

The company emphasizes joint product discussions with customers from the development stage, so customer relationships underpin demand. Display test equipment orders tend to rise in panel investment upcycles and fall in adjustment phases, and recent quarterly results sit within that volatility.

06

Outlook

The company's stated guidance is the 2026 value-up plan published on March 30, 2026. The plan targets revenue growth of 20% through new business entry and higher market share. The same plan says collaboration on jig supply for an overseas big tech company's next-generation products is underway.

A media report says the company told the market it is developing advance products with Apple directly, but the company's filing uses the phrase overseas big tech company, so the customer's name should be checked against future filings.

Big tech supply involves customer-designated critical parts and approval steps for quality control, so full-scale delivery may take time. The plan also lists expanded shareholder return policies as a major 2026 task, and it records that the professional management structure was completed in March 2026.

In recent quarters, revenue rose in Q2 and Q3 2026 and operating profit stayed positive in Q3. The next checkpoints are how close 2026 annual revenue gets to the 20% target and whether Q4 operating profit holds above breakeven, which will be reported in the annual report due by late March 2027.

A shift from collaboration to full supply would add revenue sources beyond displays, but the timing and scale have not yet been disclosed.

Big tech collaboration and revenue target

The company said it aims to grow revenue 20% through new business entry and higher market share. If the jig supply collaboration with an overseas big tech company moves to full supply, it would add a revenue source beyond displays. Progress is tied to the company's disclosed plan.

Quarterly revenue recovery and shareholder return plan

Revenue rose in both Q2 and Q3 2026 after the Q1 low, and Q3 2026 revenue reached KRW 9.34 billion, up 55.6% year on year. The value-up plan lists expanded shareholder return policies as a major task. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 7.34 billion.

Bear factors

Volatility in quarterly net profit

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from KRW 5.12 billion in Q2 2026 to a KRW 1.3 billion net loss in Q3 2026, after KRW 970 million in Q1. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 8.78 billion in 2024 to KRW 3.04 billion in 2025.

Concentration in displays and customers

Display test equipment accounted for 96.4% of 2025 revenue. Main customers are concentrated in Samsung Display and LG Display, so results depend heavily on their panel investment plans. The semiconductor share was just 2.1% in 2025, so diversification remains limited.

Timing uncertainty in semiconductor expansion

The big tech collaboration is described in filings as ongoing, so the timing of any revenue contribution is unclear. Semiconductor probe cards require customer mass-production qualification first. 2025 revenue of KRW 31.5 billion was below the KRW 36 billion of 2024.

Risk factors

Customer investment cycle

If panel makers cut fab investment or lower utilization, orders for inspection equipment could fall too. Display equipment revenue moved between KRW 29 billion and KRW 34 billion over 2023 to 2025, and 2025 revenue fell 12.5%. High customer concentration means a single customer's order change feeds directly into results.

Verification of big tech collaboration

Big tech supply goes through lengthy verification, including factory visits to panel makers' partners and final approval. The plan's targets are not guaranteed, so achievement and timing remain uncertain. Filings use the phrase overseas big tech company, so the customer and volume must be confirmed in periodic reports.

Earnings and cash flow volatility

In Q3 2026, operating profit was positive but the company still posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, which signals low stability in earnings composition. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 3.04 billion in 2025.

Total liabilities of KRW 5.25 billion are small relative to KRW 42.9 billion of equity, but whether earnings and cash generation stabilize together remains to be seen.

Overall view

PRO2000 is a semiconductor test equipment company centered on display panel inspection, and display accounted for 96.4% of 2025 revenue. Annual operating profit recovered to KRW 2.77 billion in 2025 with an 8.8% margin, but revenue fell to KRW 31.5 billion.

Revenue rose in Q2 and Q3 2026 and Q3 operating profit was positive, yet the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.3 billion in Q3, so profit direction varies quarter to quarter.
