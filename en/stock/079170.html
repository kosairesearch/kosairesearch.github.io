Hanchang Industry: Q3 2026 Swing to Loss Tests Margin Recovery

02

Business structure

Hanchang Industry is a functional materials company whose main products are zinc dust and zinc phosphate. In an announcement dated August 13, 2026, the company said it has built technical and production competitiveness across functional materials including lithium bromide and zeolite.

Lithium bromide is generally known as an absorbent used in absorption-type heating and cooling equipment, while zeolite is used in adsorption-type oxygen generating equipment. The product range spans several downstream industries, which defines the business structure.

The company said demand from major downstream industries continues and that it maintains sales flows based on domestic and overseas customers. Segment revenue shares follow the business-segment disclosure in the periodic reports.

Listed companies in the same steel and metals classification include Korea Zinc, Youngpoong and SeAH Steel, but differences in product mix and customer base limit direct comparisons.

According to FnGuide shareholder data (last change dated February 7, 2024), the largest shareholder and related parties hold a stake in the 52% range.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 99.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 67 billion in 2023, down 32.6%, and then rose two years in a row to KRW 94.6 billion in 2024, up 41.4%, and KRW 98.6 billion in 2025, up 4.1%. Among 2022–2025, 2022 was the largest year for revenue and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 7.11 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.57 billion in 2023, down 77.9%, then rose to KRW 8.77 billion in 2024, up 459.0% and the largest year of 2022–2025. Operating profit was KRW 7.89 billion in 2025, down 10.1%.

The operating margin was 7.2% in 2022, 2.3% in 2023, 9.3% in 2024 and 8.0% in 2025, recovering after the 2023 low and easing slightly in 2025. The pattern reads as margins rising alongside a revenue rebound.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.12 billion in 2022, KRW 1.55 billion in 2023, KRW 6.82 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.58 billion in 2025. Total net income was reported at the same amounts each year, so no gap from non-controlling interests appears.

The debt ratio fell from 21.4% in 2022 and 38.4% in 2023 to 28.9% in 2024 and 19.1% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 72.5 billion and total liabilities were KRW 13.9 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 6.62 billion in 2022, KRW 12.7 billion in 2023, KRW 9.98 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.06 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 21.3 billion and an operating loss of KRW 175 million, followed by a fourth-quarter operating profit of KRW 397 million, a swing to profit. First-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 667 million, up 68.2% from the prior quarter.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 28.9 billion, up 36.3% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 2.22 billion, up 233.4%, were the largest in the five quarters shown.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 21.6 billion, down 25.6% from the prior quarter and up 1.0% year on year, with an operating loss of KRW 363 million, a swing to loss. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.23 billion, with the loss widening from a year earlier.

The net loss is far larger than the operating loss, suggesting that items below operating profit weighed on the result. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the most recent four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, totals KRW 2.06 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The company's products are intermediate materials for several industries, including absorption materials for heating and cooling equipment, adsorption materials for oxygen generation, and zinc-based materials, so the business is not tied to a single cycle such as finished vehicles or home appliances.

In its first-half 2026 results announcement, the company said demand recovery was delayed in some industries, and it cited heightened geopolitical uncertainty as a factor affecting results.

On costs, a summary of nine-month 2025 separate results noted that revenue rose while margins were low due to higher zinc ingot prices and manufacturing cost burdens. Apart from 2023, the operating margin has moved in a 7–9% range since 2022.

Within the same steel and metals sector, the company is compared with large listed names such as Korea Zinc and Youngpoong, but differences in product mix and scale limit their usefulness as direct benchmarks.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 21.6 billion rose only 1.0% year on year, consistent with a view that demand is not recovering quickly. As of November 14, 2026, the company's explanation that demand recovery is delayed in some industries is the most direct reference point for the cycle. The company, however, emphasized that production and plant operations remain on plan.

A diverse product range spanning zinc dust, zinc phosphate, lithium bromide and zeolite is also cited as a factor that could support new customer wins.

Traces of a quarterly rebound

Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 28.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.22 billion were the largest in the five quarters shown. The company said production and plant operations are proceeding stably as planned. If sales follow steady operations, quarterly results could improve again, which is cited as a bullish element.

Bear factors

Swing to loss in Q3 2026

The third quarter of 2026 recorded an operating loss of KRW 363 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.23 billion, a swing to loss. The loss widened from a year earlier.

Against the second quarter's profit, the swing in quarterly results is large, which is read as a bearish signal about the durability of the recovery.

Cost burden and thin margins

On a separate basis, nine-month 2025 revenue rose 6.2% while operating profit fell 14.6% (FnGuide summary). Higher zinc ingot prices and manufacturing cost burdens were cited as factors lowering margins.

If raw material costs are not passed through to selling prices, revenue growth is harder to convert into profit, which is the bearish case.

Delayed demand recovery and geopolitics

The company said delayed demand recovery in some industries and heightened geopolitical uncertainty affected first-half 2026 results. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 21.6 billion rose only 1.0% year on year.

If the recovery in downstream demand stays slow for longer, revenue growth may remain constrained, which is the bearish case.
