kakaopay: Payments Base Adds Finance Earnings

After turning to an annual operating profit in 2025, kakaopay has grown payments and finance-subsidiary revenue together, and in the third quarter of 2026 quarterly operating profit rose year on year while quarterly revenue fell from the prior quarter.

02

Business structure

kakaopay is a lifestyle finance platform built around payments, finance and platform services. Of third-quarter 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 302.1 billion (announced November 3, 2026), payment service revenue was KRW 154.7 billion, up 19% year on year, and finance service revenue was KRW 133.4 billion, up 41%.

Platform service revenue was KRW 13.9 billion, slightly lower year on year due to the seasonal advertising slowdown and tighter ad exposure standards. Finance services include investment, insurance and lending, and insurance service revenue rose 80% to a quarterly record.

Consolidated results include the securities subsidiary kakaopay Securities, with third-quarter revenue of KRW 74.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 15.7 billion, and the insurance subsidiary kakaopay General Insurance, with revenue of KRW 32.2 billion, up 93%.

Standalone revenue of kakaopay reached KRW 204.9 billion, passing KRW 200 billion for the first time in a quarter. Online payments grew both with Kakao-affiliated merchants and external merchants, and offline payment volume rose 44%.

In competition, easy-payment operators are expanding from online into offline, and kakaopay has chosen an asset-light approach that works with POS and kiosk operators rather than deploying its own terminals at scale.

Securities-subsidiary assets under custody stood at KRW 19.3 trillion at the end of September 2026, up 167% year on year. Payments provide the transaction base, while finance subsidiaries carry the goal of a larger revenue share.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 521.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 615.4 billion in 2023 (up 18.0%), KRW 766.2 billion in 2024 (up 24.5%) and KRW 958.4 billion in 2025 (up 25.1%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 45.5 billion in 2022, KRW 56.6 billion in 2023 and KRW 57.5 billion in 2024 to an operating profit of KRW 50.4 billion in 2025, a turn to profit. The operating margin improved from -8.7% in 2022, -9.2% in 2023 and -7.5% in 2024 to 5.3% in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 54 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.52 billion in 2023 (down 95.3%), turned to a net loss of KRW 13.7 billion in 2024, and returned to a profit of KRW 45.2 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests followed a different path: KRW 26.8 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 22.9 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 21.5 billion in 2024, and net income of KRW 55.7 billion in 2025.

Over the same period the debt-to-equity ratio climbed from 74.6% in 2022 to 105.0% in 2023, 131.0% in 2024 and 173.7% in 2025, so the return to operating profit came alongside a larger liability base. At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 3.39 trillion against total equity of KRW 1.95 trillion.

Operating cash flow went from -KRW 56.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 253.1 billion in 2023, KRW 127 billion in 2024 and KRW 118.6 billion in 2025, staying positive after 2022.

Quarterly revenue moved from KRW 238.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 269.8 billion in the fourth quarter (up 13.2% from the prior quarter), KRW 300.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 11.3%) and KRW 335.1 billion in the second quarter (up 11.6%), before falling 9.9% to KRW 302.1 billion in the third quarter.

Operating profit rose from KRW 15.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 20.8 billion in the fourth quarter to KRW 32.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 58.6 billion in the second quarter, then fell 44.6% to KRW 32.5 billion in the third quarter.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 4.05 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (down 73.3% from the prior quarter) to KRW 28.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 40 billion in the second, before easing 22.8% to KRW 30.9 billion in the third.

The third-quarter decline in operating profit coincided with a sharp fall at the securities subsidiary, which the company linked to weaker domestic and overseas stock trading and wider currency volatility. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the most recent four quarters totals KRW 103.5 billion.

05

Industry analysis

kakaopay's results are highly sensitive to domestic stock trading activity.

Securities-subsidiary stock trading volume reached KRW 81.6 trillion in the third quarter of 2026, up 139% year on year, yet securities revenue fell 38.8% from the prior quarter, so higher trading volume did not translate directly into higher profit.

The market cycle looks mixed, with active and slower trading periods alternating, which produces large quarter-to-quarter profit swings. The easy-payment market is shifting its competition from online to offline.

A May 2026 report from The Electronic Times said easy-payment operators were accelerating offline expansion, pushing competition into that area. kakaopay is responding with QR ordering and expanded card-company tie-ups, and Samsung Card is one payment-side competitor.

In securities, Kiwoom Securities, Mirae Asset Securities and NH Investment & Securities, all listed financial peers, compete for the same trading base.

On the banking side, cooperation with KakaoBank has become more important, and in a February 2026 conference call kakaopay said it runs a joint council with group companies including KakaoBank on won-denominated stablecoins.

Its scale is smaller than large bank holding groups, but it combines a payments transaction base with subsidiary expansion, which the company presents as a point of difference.

06

Outlook

In its February 2026 conference call on fourth-quarter and full-year 2025 results, the company guided to 2026 consolidated revenue growth of roughly 15% to 25%. Cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 reached KRW 937.4 billion on a preliminary basis, up 36.1% year on year, a pace above the guided range.

In payments, monthly offline payment users were about 6 million as of May 2026, and the company has set a target of 10 million by 2027. QR ordering has been applied at about 3,000 stores, with franchise chains including Isaac Toast, Pascucci and Saladhouse confirmed.

For overseas payments, the company plans to widen offline payment in Europe and North America through Mastercard's merchant network, and it had targeted introducing NFC payment on iOS in the first half of 2026.

In securities, the February 2026 conference call summary cited a goal of more than 50% revenue growth in 2026. However, third-quarter 2026 securities revenue fell from the prior quarter, which shows the path to that goal can be volatile.

The stablecoin business is at the stage of partner discussions at home and abroad, with cross-border remittance and settlement efficiency under review as a scenario.

The timing of revenue contribution from new businesses has not been disclosed, so the 2026 revenue guidance and the securities trend are the next points to watch.

Third-quarter operating profit of KRW 32.5 billion rose 105.4% year on year, and the operating margin has improved from 5.3% for full-year 2025. Daily active users passed 7 million for the first time, which points to a wider payment traffic base.

Growth at finance subsidiaries

Finance service revenue reached KRW 133.4 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 41%. kakaopay General Insurance posted quarterly revenue of KRW 32.2 billion, up 93% and a quarterly record, while insurance service revenue rose 80%.

Securities-subsidiary assets under custody reached KRW 19.3 trillion at the end of September, up 167%, and cumulative net inflows for 2026 were KRW 10.4 trillion.

Profit base and growth guidance

The company returned to annual operating profit of KRW 50.4 billion in 2025 and guided to 15% to 25% consolidated revenue growth for 2026. Cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 reached KRW 937.4 billion on a preliminary basis, up 36.1%.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 103.5 billion, a run of quarterly profits.

Bear factors

Earnings swings from slower stock trading

Securities-subsidiary revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 74.6 billion, down 38.8% from the prior quarter, and operating profit fell 60.4% to KRW 15.7 billion. The company cited weaker domestic and overseas stock trading and wider currency volatility.

As finance makes up a larger share of revenue, stock trading flows have become a stronger driver of consolidated earnings.

Quarterly revenue and profit reversal

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 302.1 billion fell 9.9% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 32.5 billion dropped 44.6% from KRW 58.6 billion in the second quarter. Revenue came in below the market estimate of about KRW 310.4 billion.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 30.9 billion was also 22.8% lower than in the second quarter.

Liability growth and balance sheet load

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 74.6% in 2022 to 105.0% in 2023, 131.0% in 2024 and 173.7% in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 reached KRW 3.39 trillion, above total equity of KRW 1.95 trillion.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was a net loss of KRW 13.7 billion in 2024, so whether profit recovery can offset the heavier balance sheet is the key question.

Risk factors

Market volatility risk

Revenue from securities and investment services depends on domestic and overseas stock trading and currency moves. In the third quarter of 2026 trading value rose 139%, yet securities revenue fell from the prior quarter, so higher volume did not directly lift revenue. A market slowdown could affect both asset valuations and fee income.

Competition and fee-structure risk

As easy-payment competition extends from online into offline, customer acquisition and merchant incentive costs may rise. Platform service revenue was KRW 13.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, affected by seasonality and tighter ad exposure standards. Changes to payment fee structures or ad exposure policy could alter the growth pace of platform revenue.

Expansion and operational risk

The stablecoin business is at the stage of discussions with partners at home and abroad, and its direction may change with launch timing and regulatory requirements. Operational issues related to some user experiences have been raised, so service reliability could affect the expansion of the user base. Higher spending on new businesses could also slow the pace of profit improvement.

Overall view

kakaopay turned from an operating loss to an annual operating profit of KRW 50.4 billion in 2025, and third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 32.5 billion rose 105.4% year on year.

Payment service revenue of KRW 154.7 billion grew 19%, and finance service revenue of KRW 133.4 billion grew 41%, so the finance share of the revenue mix is rising.

Against that, third-quarter 2026 revenue fell 9.9% from the prior quarter and operating profit dropped 44.6% from KRW 58.6 billion in the second quarter, which shows how much the securities subsidiary's dependence on market trading amplifies quarterly swings.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 74.6% in 2022 to 173.7% in 2025, so changes in the capital structure also need to be tracked. The company guided to 15% to 25% revenue growth for 2026, and cumulative revenue for the first three quarters reached KRW 937.4 billion on a preliminary basis, up 36.1% year on year.

On valuation, the market-cap multiple relative to profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, KRW 103.5 billion, is sensitive to the quarterly profit trend.

The next reference points are the preliminary fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results and the 2026 annual business report, and the key question is how those figures compare with the guided range and the operating margin trend.
