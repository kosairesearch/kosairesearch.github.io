Hunesion: Network-Separation Leader with Earnings Tied to Public Inspection Timing

Hunesion, the leader in network-separation security solutions, grew 2025 operating profit by 52.3% year on year, yet a 2026 first-quarter operating loss and a 3.3% decline in cumulative nine-month revenue show how uneven its quarterly results remain.

  1. 1

    2025 revenue of KRW 37.7 billion (up 2.3% year on year) and operating profit of KRW 4.8 billion (up 52.3%) were the largest figures of the 2022–2025 period.

  2. 2

    An operating loss of KRW 1.71 billion in the first quarter of 2026 was followed by operating profit of KRW 881 million in the second quarter and KRW 579 million in the third, while cumulative nine-month revenue of KRW 22 billion fell 3.3% year on year.

  3. 3

    The 2026 revenue estimate of KRW 40 billion cited by Korea IR Association would require roughly KRW 18 billion of fourth-quarter revenue, given the nine-month total of KRW 22 billion.

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Hunesion is a information security software specialist whose core business is network-separation security solutions. Reports put this business at more than 70% of total revenue, and analysts describe it as a long-running profit base.

Its main customers are public institutions and financial companies, where high security standards create steady demand.

The company says it has ranked first in the domestic network-separation security solution market for eleven consecutive years, from 2015 through 2025, and reports put its 2025 share on a Public Procurement Service basis at 47%.

Another report on the same market put its share at 50% as of the third quarter of 2025, so the figures vary with the counting basis and timing. Its product line covers bidirectional, cloud, unidirectional and cross-domain (CDS) products, and it is described as the only domestic provider with all seven types.

The portfolio is widening into system access control (NGS), network access control (NAC) and operational technology (OT) security. The company attributed part of its 2025 improvement to product diversification and growth in its cloud business.

On competition, holding first place for more than ten years is read as evidence of high entry barriers for rivals. The company also said it will pursue growth in AI, cloud and OT security with its affiliates OTwons and SecureSystems.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 30.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 36.1 billion in 2023 (up 18.8% year on year), KRW 36.9 billion in 2024 (up 2.1%) and KRW 37.7 billion in 2025 (up 2.3%). Revenue has grown for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022–2025 period.

Reports described 2025 consolidated revenue as the highest on record. Operating profit went from KRW 3.42 billion in 2022 and KRW 4.66 billion in 2023 (up 36.4%) to KRW 3.15 billion in 2024 (down 32.4%), before recovering to KRW 4.8 billion in 2025 (up 52.3%).

The company attributed the 2024 decline to relocation costs for its new headquarters, new hiring and higher recurring R&D spending. It cited revenue growth, cost reduction and better results at a subsidiary as the main drivers of the 2025 recovery.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a net loss of KRW 5.01 billion in 2022 to net profit of KRW 6.74 billion in 2023, the first profit in the period. It then reached KRW 3.73 billion in 2024 (down 44.7%) and KRW 5.77 billion in 2025 (up 54.7%).

The operating margin was 11.2% in 2022, 12.9% in 2023, 8.5% in 2024 and 12.7% in 2025, so excluding 2024 it has stayed in the 11–13% range.

At the end of 2025, total equity stood at KRW 51 billion, of which KRW 50.4 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 7.46 billion, a debt ratio of 14.6%. Quarterly results are uneven.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, revenue reached KRW 14.9 billion and operating profit KRW 4.17 billion, which accounted for most of the full-year operating profit of KRW 4.8 billion.

The first quarter of 2026 brought revenue of KRW 5.21 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.71 billion (a net loss of KRW 1.98 billion attributable to controlling shareholders).

The second quarter had revenue of KRW 8.82 billion and operating profit of KRW 881 million, and the third quarter had revenue of KRW 8 billion and operating profit of KRW 579 million, up 141.2% year on year.

Cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 22 billion, down 3.3% year on year, with a cumulative operating loss of KRW 252.66 million. The company said public projects concentrate inspections in the third and fourth quarters, which together generate about 60% of revenue.

05

Industry analysis

Demand in the network-separation market centers on public procurement and financial institutions, where Hunesion holds the top position by Public Procurement Service data. Security breaches at telecom and financial firms have been cited as a factor pushing demand for separated network operations.

On policy, the National Network Security Framework (N2SF) was expected to activate the market from the second half of 2025, and the government's information protection measures and N2SF guidelines were analyzed as contributors to 2025 revenue growth.

A shorter replacement cycle for hardware security solutions, from five years to one to three years, was presented as another supportive factor. Public-sector demand is relatively less sensitive to economic cycles. Expectations are that public IT budget execution will return to normal in 2026.

However, public projects run long from budgeting through inspection, so quarterly revenue clusters in particular periods. The shift toward cloud and AI environments is also cited as a change in security demand. On competition, the company's business is concentrated in a single category, network separation.

Compared with security software companies listed in the same IT and software industry, such as Ahnlab, its scope is relatively narrow.

06

Outlook

Korea IR Association projects 2026 revenue of KRW 40 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.4 billion, which would be increases of 6% and 12% from 2025. Another report also expects revenue of about KRW 40 billion in 2026 and double-digit operating profit growth.

However, cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 22 billion, so reaching the KRW 40 billion estimate would require roughly KRW 18 billion in fourth-quarter revenue.

Adding the cumulative nine-month operating loss of KRW 252.66 million, the KRW 5.4 billion full-year estimate would need fourth-quarter operating profit of about KRW 5.65 billion. The company said it has responded in advance to the shift toward AI and cloud environments and to government policy direction.

It also presented plans for an international joint R&D project to build a security model responding to the national network security framework and a supply chain security management platform for industrial control systems. The company also said participation in N2SF demonstration projects is leading to contract wins.

The direction of applying an AI-based intelligent engine across its product lines has also been reported. The next checkpoint is how close fourth-quarter 2026 results come to these estimates. The full-year 2026 revenue and operating profit confirmed in the annual business report will follow.

Bull factors

Leadership in the network-separation market

The company says it has held first place in the domestic network-separation market for eleven consecutive years, from 2015 through 2025, and reports put its 2025 share on a Public Procurement Service basis at 47%.

Its position as the only domestic provider with all seven product types is cited as a competitive advantage. Holding first place for more than ten years is read as evidence of high entry barriers.

Demand from policy changes and security incidents

Analysts argue that the shift in the national network security framework (N2SF) and security incidents at telecom and financial firms are increasing demand for separated networks. The shorter replacement cycle for hardware security solutions, from five years to one to three years, is also a supportive factor. Expectations for a normal execution of public IT budgets in 2026 are also cited.

Earnings recovery in 2025 and dividend record

Revenue reached KRW 37.7 billion and operating profit KRW 4.8 billion in 2025, with the latter up 52.3% year on year. The operating margin rose from 8.5% in 2024 to 12.7% in 2025. The company has paid dividends since its 2018 listing.

Bear factors

Quarterly volatility in 2026

The first quarter of 2026 swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.71 billion on revenue of KRW 5.21 billion. Third-quarter operating profit was 34.3% below the second quarter.

Concentration in one business and earnings swings

The network-separation business accounts for more than 70% of revenue, so results are directly exposed to budget execution and policy timing in that market. Operating profit fell 32.4% in 2024, which the company attributed to relocation costs and hiring. Diversification is underway, but network separation remains the center of the revenue mix.

What the 2026 estimates require

Reaching the KRW 40 billion revenue estimate from Korea IR Association requires roughly KRW 18 billion of fourth-quarter revenue. The KRW 5.4 billion operating profit estimate requires about KRW 5.65 billion in the fourth quarter.

That means a fourth quarter on the scale of late 2025, with KRW 14.9 billion of revenue and KRW 4.17 billion of operating profit, would need to recur.

10

Risk factors

Policy and budget timing

The timing of N2SF implementation and public IT budget execution is outside the company's control. If the policy schedule slips, inspection-driven revenue expected in the third and fourth quarters could move into the following year. A revenue base built on public procurement is sensitive to changes in budget allocation.

Quarterly concentration of results

The company says public inspections cluster in the third and fourth quarters, which together generate about 60% of revenue. If losses or thin profits repeat in the first and second quarters, full-year earnings depend heavily on a few quarters.

The fourth quarter of 2025, with KRW 4.17 billion of operating profit against a full-year total of KRW 4.8 billion, illustrates this pattern.

Free float and ownership structure

The largest shareholder and related parties hold 46.84%, and treasury shares add to the concentration, which limits the number of shares available for trading. With a small market capitalization, price moves can be larger on days with thin trading.

Until profit recovery after the 2026 quarterly losses proves durable, this structure remains a source of volatility.

Overall view

Hunesion is a specialist that has held the top domestic position in network-separation security solutions for eleven consecutive years. Its 2025 full-year results, with revenue of KRW 37.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.8 billion, were the largest of the 2022–2025 period.

However, results cluster around public inspection timing, and the first quarter of 2026 recorded an operating loss of KRW 1.71 billion. Cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 22 billion, down 3.3% year on year, while third-quarter operating profit of KRW 579 million rose 141.2% year on year.

The 2026 estimates of KRW 40 billion in revenue and KRW 5.4 billion in operating profit from Korea IR Association would require substantial fourth-quarter results.

Policy demand, a shorter replacement cycle and normalizing public IT budgets are cited as strengths, while more than 70% revenue concentration in network separation and quarterly swings are cited as weaknesses.

The share price has moved below book net asset value, and whether quarterly losses recur will shape how that gap is read. The next checkpoints are the preliminary 2026 results in early February 2027, the 2026 annual business report in March 2027, and the first-quarter 2027 report in mid-May 2027.
