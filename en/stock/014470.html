Bubang: Volatile Holding-Company Earnings and a Price Well Below Book Value

Bubang is a holding company whose earnings depend on its home appliance, retail and service businesses and on equity-method gains from affiliates. Profit fell sharply in 2025, and operating losses in the second and third quarters of 2026 leave the direction of any recovery unconfirmed.

    Revenue in 2025 was KRW 319.5 billion, down 5.0% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 1.75 billion, down 64.0% year on year. 2025 was the smallest revenue year in the 2022–2025 period.

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Bubang was founded in 1979 and listed on KOSDAQ in 1994. It converted into a holding company in 2015 and manages subsidiaries, leases real estate and runs new businesses. Its revenue mix is home appliances at 46.93%, retail at 31.62%, services at 22.57% and investment at 1.93%.

Small rice cookers are the core appliance product, and the company is shifting its product mix as demand for small appliances grows with smaller households. Retail runs through a consolidated subsidiary operating large discount stores.

The service segment is mainly software consulting and facility management, and the company attributed the 2025 profit decline to lower service revenue and smaller equity-method gains. Domestic sales made up 99.91% of service revenue and 96.47% of product sales in 2025.

R&D spending was KRW 3,886 million in 2025, or 1.22% of sales. The company is classified under real estate and REITs, but its revenue comes mainly from appliances and retail rather than leasing.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue went from KRW 338.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 331 billion in 2023 (down 2.2%), KRW 336.5 billion in 2024 (up 1.6%) and KRW 319.5 billion in 2025 (down 5.0%).

Operating profit was KRW 916 million in 2022, an operating loss of KRW 342 million in 2023 after turning to a loss, KRW 4.87 billion in 2024 after turning to a profit, and KRW 1.75 billion in 2025, down 64.0%. The operating margin stayed around 1% or below throughout, at 0.3%, -0.1%, 1.4% and 0.5% from 2022 to 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 31.2 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 26 billion in 2023, KRW 11.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.33 billion in 2025, down 79.8%. Net income including non-controlling interests followed the same path.

The 2023 net loss was much larger than the operating loss, pointing to non-operating items.

Quarterly results show a swing from a KRW 3.7 billion operating profit in the third quarter of 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 1.84 billion in the third quarter of 2026, with a KRW 912 million controlling-shareholder profit in that quarter. Operating cash flow remained positive each year. Over the last four quarters, controlling-shareholder net income totaled KRW 25.3 million.

Industry analysis

Bubang is classified under real estate and REITs, but its earnings come from home appliances, retail and services, with leasing as a smaller part of the business.

Unlike peers in the same classification that move mainly on rental income and distributions, Bubang's earnings depend more on consumer demand and equity-method gains from affiliates.

Demand for the appliance segment is linked to rising numbers of single-person and smaller households, which is driving growth in small rice cookers and a shift in product mix. The retail segment runs large discount stores and is directly exposed to domestic consumption.

The 2025 decline in service revenue lowered operating profit, and smaller equity-method gains reduced net income further. Reliable sector cycle indicators were limited for this report, so the analysis rests on the company's own results.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue fell year on year while the operating loss widened, showing that demand has not yet translated into profit. A debt-to-equity ratio of 45.1% at the end of 2025 leaves some capacity to absorb weaker operations.

Bull factors

Share price well below book equity

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 172.1 billion, and the price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x. A market value below book equity is cited as a basis for asset-based strength. Controlling-shareholder equity equals total equity, so non-controlling interests add little complexity.

Improving balance sheet

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 99.5% in 2022 to 90.6% in 2023, 88.0% in 2024 and 45.1% in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 77.7 billion. Lower borrowing gives more financial room if operations stay weak.

Small appliance demand and steady cash flow

The company says demand for small rice cookers is growing in volume as household sizes shrink, and that product mix is improving. Operating cash flow was positive every year, at KRW 2.2 billion in 2022, KRW 9.48 billion in 2023, KRW 8.84 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.6 billion in 2025. The business keeps generating cash even when operating profit is small.

Bear factors

Consecutive operating losses in 2026

The operating loss was KRW 631 million in the second quarter of 2026 and widened to KRW 1.84 billion in the third quarter, making two consecutive loss-making quarters. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 81.6 billion fell 6.4% year on year. The first-quarter operating profit of KRW 921 million did not carry into the following quarters.

Reliance on service revenue and equity-method gains

The company pointed to lower service revenue and smaller equity-method gains as the main causes of the 2025 decline. Operating profit fell 64.0% and controlling-shareholder net income fell 79.8%. Both drivers weakened at the same time, which thinned the earnings base.

Shrinking earnings base

Controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters totaled only KRW 25.3 million. The fourth quarter of 2025 swung to a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 40.66 million. With earnings this small, earnings-based measures can move sharply on minor swings.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility and equity-method swings

The operating margin has stayed around 1% or below, so revenue swings pass straight through to profit. Net income can move in a different direction from operating profit because of equity-method results. The 2023 net loss, much larger than the operating loss, shows this effect.

Governance and shareholder stake changes

Vice Chairman Park Jae-soon reported his shareholding in a disclosure dated April 3, 2026. In a holding company, changes in insider stakes can bear on management direction, so later ownership filings should be checked as well.

Leverage and small-cap liquidity

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 77.7 billion, close to half of total equity. The debt ratio has fallen, but if operating losses continue, borrowing costs could become more visible. As a small-cap stock, the share price can also swing more when trading volume is thin.
