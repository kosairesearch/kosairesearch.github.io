Vehicle and cooling orders hold up earnings as appliance demand lags

02

Business structure

LG Electronics operates four business units: the Home Appliance Solution Company (HS), the Media Entertainment Solution Company (MS), the Vehicle Solution Company (VS) and the LG Eco Solution Company (ES).

In 3Q 2026, HS posted revenue of KRW 7.04 trillion and operating profit of KRW 259.2 billion, MS revenue of KRW 5.02 trillion and operating profit of KRW 146.7 billion, VS revenue of KRW 2.83 trillion and operating profit of KRW 190.3 billion, and ES revenue of KRW 2.17 trillion and operating profit of KRW 87.2 billion.

VS centers on in-vehicle infotainment and serves global automakers as a B2B business. B2B revenue in 3Q 2026 was KRW 6.1 trillion, up 3.4% year on year, while subscription revenue, which includes product subscriptions and maintenance services, reached KRW 760 billion, up 8.6%.

HS runs a dual-track strategy covering premium and volume segments, and subscriptions, online sales and B2B sales have supported growth. MS benefited from higher sales of premium OLED TVs, growth in its webOS platform and expansion in emerging markets.

ES is led by overseas commercial HVAC markets and is building AI data center cooling as a new business. In appliances, competition from Chinese manufacturers is the central variable, and the company said its share in North America improved against Chinese rivals in the first half of 2026. The mix is shifting toward recurring revenue from B2B, subscriptions and vehicle orders.

04

Earnings analysis

On the annual disclosed figures, revenue rose from KRW 83.5 trillion in 2022 to KRW 84.2 trillion in 2023 (up 0.9%), KRW 87.7 trillion in 2024 (up 4.2%) and KRW 89.2 trillion in 2025 (up 1.7%). It has risen for three consecutive years from 2023, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit moved from KRW 3.55 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.55 trillion in 2023 (down 0.1%), KRW 3.42 trillion in 2024 (down 3.6%) and KRW 2.48 trillion in 2025 (down 27.5%). It has declined for three consecutive years from 2023, and 2022 was the largest year in the period.

The operating margin consequently fell from 4.3% in 2022 to 4.2% in 2023, 3.9% in 2024 and 2.8% in 2025, so revenue growth did not translate into profit growth, most clearly in 2025. In 4Q 2025 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 109 billion.

Reports at the time said HS, MS and ES had mostly booked operating losses, while VS was the exception with a profit.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 1.2 trillion in 2022 to KRW 712.9 billion in 2023 and KRW 367.5 billion in 2024, then rose 161.4% to KRW 960.6 billion in 2025, moving in the opposite direction to operating profit.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.22 trillion in 2025, a separate figure that should not be read as the same measure.

Quarterly, operating profit went from KRW 688.9 billion in 3Q 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 109 billion in 4Q 2025 and then to KRW 1.67 trillion in 1Q 2026, a turnaround to profit.

The 1Q 2026 level was the peak, followed by KRW 1.58 trillion in 2Q 2026 (down 5.7% from the prior quarter) and KRW 781.9 billion in 3Q 2026 (down 50.5%). Even so, 3Q 2026 operating profit rose 13.5% year on year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 815.7 billion in 1Q 2026 to KRW 668 billion in 2Q (down 18.1%) and KRW 663.3 billion in 3Q (down 0.7%).

05

Industry analysis

The appliance market remains in a slow-recovery phase. In its 3Q 2026 results, the company expected demand recovery to stay limited in 4Q and said higher logistics costs and marketing spending weighed on profitability.

With competition from Chinese manufacturers continuing, LG Electronics is pursuing a two-track approach that protects premium products while expanding sales in mass-market segments.

In TVs, rising panel prices were discussed as a pressure on margins for mid- and entry-level models, and the company said it would respond through supplier diversification and a higher share of premium products.

Vehicle demand faces slowing EV sales, but conversion of the order backlog into revenue lifted VS to record third-quarter revenue and operating profit in 3Q 2026. A May 2026 report cited the vehicle order backlog at roughly KRW 100 trillion.

In data center cooling, the company is developing high-capacity CDUs for dense AI servers and plans to widen project wins. Appliances and TVs face sharper competition and cost pressure, while vehicles and cooling are order-based B2B businesses, so the two groups differ in character.

06

Outlook

For 4Q 2026, the company outlined a direction for each business unit. HS plans to expand sales mainly in emerging markets, where demand is relatively resilient, while overall demand recovery is expected to stay limited.

VS expects limited recovery in automotive demand and is focused on converting its secured order backlog into revenue while improving cost competitiveness. ES named timely delivery of large-scale AI data center cooling projects as its top priority for the quarter.

LG Data Factory is scheduled to launch in Seoul in 4Q 2026, and the robotics unit is preparing actuator mass production while pursuing orders from global customers in parallel. Management expects meaningful robotics results around 2030, when humanoid robot use is expected to scale.

The company did not provide a formal revised earnings outlook. Capacity expansion centers on cooling equipment in Virginia in the United States, Pyeongtaek and Changwon, with KRW 220 billion being invested.

On shareholder returns, the company plans to retire all 730,000 treasury shares it bought in 2026 and to pursue about KRW 100 billion in additional returns in 2027. The next checkpoints are the delivery schedule of large cooling projects in 4Q 2026 and how quickly the vehicle backlog converts into revenue.

08

Bull factors

A recurring earnings base in vehicles

The Vehicle Solution Company posted 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 2.83 trillion and operating profit of KRW 190.3 billion, both record highs for a third quarter. Management described the unit as a stable cash generator built on B2B business. Conversion of the order backlog into revenue underpins the earnings base.

AI data center cooling as a new business

Cumulative orders for AI data center cooling solutions passed KRW 3 trillion through 3Q 2026, up sharply from about KRW 600 billion at the end of the first half. The total includes a large contract covering 5 gigawatts. The company is investing KRW 220 billion to expand production bases, including in the United States. Management said the long-term contract structure should generate stable revenue over several years.

A shift toward B2B and subscription revenue

B2B revenue in 3Q 2026 reached KRW 6.1 trillion, up 3.4% year on year, and subscription revenue rose 8.6% to KRW 760 billion. Operating profit turned to a profit in 1Q 2026, reaching KRW 1.67 trillion at the quarterly peak. Higher sales of premium TVs were cited as a cause of the improvement in the media business. A larger share of recurring revenue points toward lower earnings volatility.

09

Bear factors

Slow appliance demand recovery and cost pressure

The Home Appliance Solution Company faces interest-rate and currency volatility and a delayed recovery in global appliance demand. Its 3Q 2026 operating profit was KRW 259.2 billion, and the company said short-term logistics and marketing cost increases weighed on profitability.

It expects demand recovery to remain limited in 4Q 2026. Given the size of appliances in the mix, this trend feeds directly into annual earnings.

A falling operating margin and quarterly declines

The operating margin declined from 4.3% in 2022 to 4.2% in 2023, 3.9% in 2024 and 2.8% in 2025. Operating profit in 3Q 2026 fell 50.5% from the prior quarter, the second straight quarterly decline since the 1Q peak.

Revenue has moved sideways at roughly KRW 23 trillion since 1Q 2026, so the pace of margin recovery is the key question. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose in 2025 even as operating profit fell, so the durability of that mix also needs checking.

Timing of new businesses and vehicle and panel variables

Management expects robotics to produce meaningful results around 2030, so near-term profit contribution is limited. Vehicles are exposed to slowing EV demand and external uncertainty, and the company expects limited automotive demand recovery in 4Q 2026.

Rising TV panel prices pressure margins on mid- and entry-level models. These three variables could weigh at the same time.

10

Risk factors

Macro, geopolitics and costs

Middle East conflict, rising interest rates in major markets, swings in raw material prices and exchange rates, and higher logistics costs are weighing on profitability. The company itself warned that macroeconomic pressure, cost increases and weak consumer demand could persist.

It has absorbed part of the cost increases through cost structure improvements and manufacturing in lower-cost countries, but that absorption has limits. If cost absorption becomes harder, quarterly profit could fall again.

Appliance competition and panel prices

Competition from Chinese manufacturers continues in appliances, and rising TV panel prices pressure margins on entry-level models. The company said its share in North America improved against Chinese rivals in the first half of 2026, but expanding in mass-market segments can bring promotional and cost burdens.

It plans to respond through supplier diversification and a higher share of premium products. Whether that response works as intended will show up in future quarterly margins.

Large project execution and investment burden

For AI data center cooling, timely delivery of large projects is the challenge, and costs arrive first while the KRW 220 billion capacity expansion is executed. In 3Q 2026, ES profitability was partly affected by costs for new capacity and staffing.

Robotics will not contribute to earnings on a clear timeline until an external business model is set. The gap between how fast orders convert into revenue and how fast investment is recovered is the risk.

12

Overall view

LG Electronics recorded 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 23.8 trillion and operating profit of KRW 781.9 billion, both up year on year. Operating profit, however, has declined for two straight quarters since its 1Q 2026 peak, and the 2025 annual operating margin of 2.8% is below 4.3% in 2022.

The center of earnings is shifting toward the stable profitability of the Vehicle Solution Company and AI data center cooling orders, while the company expects appliance demand recovery to stay limited in 4Q.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 1.32 trillion over the last four quarters, and it has been in the black since 1Q 2026 after a net loss in 4Q 2025. Bullish factors include the vehicle order backlog, the cooling business and recurring B2B and subscription revenue.

Bearish factors include weak appliance demand, cost and logistics pressure, panel prices and the timing of returns from new businesses. Market capitalization sits below controlling shareholders' equity at year-end 2025, so how the valuation reads depends on whether the earnings trend holds.

The next checkpoints are 4Q 2026 results, delivery of large cooling projects and the earnings composition in the 2026 annual business report.
