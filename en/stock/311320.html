02

Business structure

GO Element, founded in 2005 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2021, supplies parts and materials for semiconductor thin-film deposition. Its main businesses are the precursor vaporization and delivery segment and the sputtering target segment.

The delivery segment makes parts and modules such as canisters, ultrasonic level sensors and vaporizers for ALD precursors. The canister, its flagship product, stores, vaporizes and delivers high-purity precursors.

In an October 2022 report, Shinhan Investment described ALD equipment-related sales as about 90% of revenue and named equipment makers such as Jusung Engineering and Wonik IPS, along with chipmakers, as customers.

In April 2024 the company acquired a stake in a home-appliance parts maker, reorganizing into a semiconductor division and a home-appliance division. That subsidiary makes surface heaters and evaporators used in water purifiers and similar products, and it has been profitable since Q1 2026 after passing break-even.

In a September 2026 report, BNK Investment & Securities cut its 2026 revenue forecast for the target division from KRW 4.6 billion to KRW 4.0 billion.

Exact segment revenue shares are not available from filings, so the mix is described qualitatively; profit contribution appears weighted toward the higher-margin ALD parts.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 28.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.8 billion in 2023 (down 37.8%), then rose to KRW 44.8 billion in 2024 (up 151.4%) and KRW 54.6 billion in 2025 (up 22.0%), two consecutive years of growth from 2024 to 2025, with 2025 the largest year in 2022-2025.

Operating profit was KRW 7.29 billion in 2022, KRW 761 million in 2023, KRW 5.31 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.43 billion in 2025, so in 2025 it fell 16.6% year on year even as revenue rose.

The operating margin went from 25.5% in 2022 to 4.3% in 2023, 11.9% in 2024 and 8.1% in 2025, and 2022 remains the year with the largest operating profit in 2022-2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 2.23 billion in 2023 to KRW 3.35 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.46 billion in 2025, two consecutive years of growth from 2024 to 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 4.79 billion in 2025, below the attributable figure, which suggests the non-controlling share was a loss, and operating cash flow of KRW 7.31 billion was above KRW 6.5 billion in 2024.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 15.6 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 13.1 billion in Q4, KRW 14.9 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 17.4 billion in Q2 and KRW 14.9 billion in Q3, while operating profit was KRW 1.44 billion, KRW 1.06 billion, KRW 2.08 billion, KRW 3.39 billion and KRW 2.03 billion.

In Q3 2026 revenue fell 14.0% quarter on quarter and 4.4% year on year, and operating profit fell 40.1% quarter on quarter but rose 41.6% year on year.

An IR representative said in May 2026 that the company expected both Q2 and Q3 to grow from Q1, yet Q3 reported revenue matched Q1 at KRW 14.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.03 billion was below Q1's KRW 2.08 billion.

Press reports put first-half 2026 standalone revenue at KRW 20.0 billion, operating profit at KRW 5.3 billion and the operating margin at about 27%.

Those standalone figures should be kept separate from consolidated results, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 7.69 billion.

05

Industry analysis

ALD is widely used across semiconductor manufacturing, including AI chips, memory and foundry processes. The company cited rising memory demand from AI infrastructure expansion and sales growth to downstream device makers as the drivers of its first-half 2026 improvement.

BNK Investment & Securities said in September 2026 that larger precursor storage and transport volumes are leading materials suppliers to keep buying more canisters.

By contrast, revenue fell in 2023 on a weak downstream market, and Sangsangin Securities likewise attributed the 2023 decline to that weakness in a February 2024 report. That history shows the company's results respond sharply to the semiconductor capex cycle.

In the target business, BNK Investment & Securities said customers are allocating volume to existing suppliers, which has delayed additional qualification tests. Reports also said that wider molybdenum adoption in foundry and DRAM could become a new growth driver.

Its products and customers differ from large electronics-component names such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek, so it is better viewed as a niche parts supplier tied to memory and ALD equipment investment.

06

Outlook

The most concrete timeline the company has given is for its solid-chemical ALD canister. The product supplies solid precursors needed for next-generation molybdenum-based processes and is being developed with SK hynix, SK trichem and another partner in a four-party consortium.

The company internally expects it to contribute to revenue from the second half of 2027. An August 2026 report said the molybdenum-precursor canister has entered customer testing, a first unit has been delivered, and production use in NAND processes is expected in 2027.

Because these products are expected to contribute from 2027 onward, they are unlikely to show up directly in Q4 2026 results. In September 2026, BNK Investment & Securities raised its 2026 operating profit forecast from KRW 10.8 billion to KRW 11.6 billion.

That estimate predates the Q3 filing, and unlike the same report's call for record quarterly revenue each quarter, Q3 reported revenue fell 14.0% from Q2. Whether the subsidiary stays profitable and how ALD parts shipments trend will shape the operating margin in Q4 and early 2027.

08

Bull factors

High share in a core consumable part

The company says its domestic share in canisters and level sensors is 90%. Shipments tend to grow as ALD equipment investment grows. Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.39 billion shows how this structure can translate into earnings.

Year-on-year profit gains continue

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.03 billion rose 41.6% year on year. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.68 billion also rose 33.2%. Against the 2025 operating margin of 8.1%, there is room for operating-metric improvement.

Molybdenum and solid canister products

The company's internal view is that the solid canister, developed in a four-party consortium that includes SK hynix, can contribute to revenue from the second half of 2027. A first molybdenum-precursor canister unit has already been delivered. If it succeeds, the product line widens and the share of higher-value products could grow.

09

Bear factors

Q3 revenue and profit both fell from Q2

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 14.9 billion fell 14.0% from Q2 and 4.4% from a year earlier. Operating profit also fell 40.1% quarter on quarter. Against management's growth expectations for Q2 and Q3, Q3 revenue only matched the Q1 level.

Lower margin despite revenue growth

In 2025 revenue grew 22.0% but operating profit fell 16.6%. The operating margin dropped from 25.5% in 2022 to 8.1% in 2025. Consolidated results include lower-margin operations, so the standalone margin does not carry through to the consolidated level.

New-product revenue is still distant

Revenue from the solid canister is expected only from the second half of 2027 on the company's internal view. Near-term results therefore depend on shipments of existing canisters and sensors. In the target business, BNK Investment & Securities lowered its 2026 revenue forecast because of delayed qualification tests.

10

Risk factors

Cycle and customer risk

Revenue depends heavily on ALD equipment investment and the memory cycle. In 2023, amid a weak downstream market, revenue fell 37.8% to KRW 17.8 billion. If capex slows, shipments and profit could swing again.

Subsidiary and diversification risk

The subsidiary's profitability rests on management's statement that the restructuring is largely complete. It has been past break-even since Q1 2026. If profits do not persist, the consolidated operating margin could fall again.

Development and ramp-up risk

Revenue timing for the solid and molybdenum canisters depends on customer testing and production approvals. The second half of 2027 is the company's internal expectation. If the schedule slips, higher R&D and production staffing costs could weigh on margins first.

12

Overall view

GO Element's revenue grew for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025, supported by its high domestic share in ALD canisters and level sensors. Operating profit fell 16.6% in 2025, pulling the operating margin down to 8.1%, and then recovered in the first two quarters of 2026 to KRW 2.08 billion and KRW 3.39 billion.

In Q3, revenue of KRW 14.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.03 billion declined from Q2, though operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders both rose from a year earlier. The bull case rests on market share, year-on-year profit gains and the molybdenum and solid canister products.

The bear case rests on the Q3 slowdown, the lower operating margin and new-product revenue deferred to 2027 and beyond. With a debt ratio of 23.9%, financial strain is small and there is capacity to fund development.

The next things to check are Q4 results in the 2026 annual report and the progress of customer testing for the new products.
