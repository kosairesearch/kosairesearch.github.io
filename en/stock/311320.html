02

Business structure

GO Element is a semiconductor thin-film deposition parts and materials company founded in 2005 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2021 as a technology growth company. The company is organized into a semiconductor division and a home-appliance division.

The semiconductor division makes and sells precursor vaporization and delivery parts for ALD (atomic layer deposition) processes, including canisters, ultrasonic level sensors and vaporizers, as well as sputtering targets for PVD processes.

Canisters store, vaporize and deliver high-purity precursors, and ALD is used across AI chips as well as memory and foundry manufacturing. Products are made to order for customers in semiconductors and displays in Korea and abroad. In Q1 2026, domestic sales rose 10.8% year on year and overseas sales rose 21.5%.

In 2024 the company acquired a subsidiary with home-appliance technology, which was reported to run the bake heater business. A 2024 report put the company's domestic share of canisters above 90%.

The solid canister for chemical use is being developed with SK hynix and two other partners in a four-party group, and is equipment that supplies solid precursors for next-generation molybdenum-based processes.

Its main customers are ALD equipment and precursor makers, so their investment plans feed directly into demand for the company's products.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual sales fell from KRW 28.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.8 billion in 2023 (down 37.8% year on year), then rose to KRW 44.8 billion in 2024 (up 151.4%) and KRW 54.6 billion in 2025 (up 22.0%).

After the 2023 low, sales rose for two consecutive years, from 2024 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Operating profit moved differently. It was largest in 2022 at KRW 7.29 billion and smallest in 2023 at KRW 761 million (down 89.6%).

It recovered to KRW 5.31 billion in 2024 (up 598.1%) and then fell to KRW 4.43 billion in 2025 (down 16.6%). The operating margin was 25.5% in 2022, 4.3% in 2023, 11.9% in 2024 and 8.1% in 2025, so in 2025 the margin declined even though sales grew.

The company said the subsidiary it consolidated in 2024 weighed on consolidated profitability, and that the subsidiary turned profitable in Q1 2026.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 2.23 billion in 2023 to KRW 3.35 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.46 billion in 2025, two consecutive years of growth.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 4.79 billion in 2025, below the controlling-shareholder figure, and the gap comes from the non-controlling interests' share of results.

In quarterly terms, Q2 2026 had the highest sales and operating profit among the reported quarters, at KRW 17.4 billion and KRW 3.39 billion.

Operating profit of KRW 2.08 billion in Q1 2026 was followed by KRW 2.03 billion in Q3 2026, a 40.1% quarter-on-quarter decline, and Q3 sales of KRW 14.9 billion were down 4.4% year on year. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 2.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.31 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

ALD is a core process for semiconductor thin-film deposition, and the company says its use spans AI chips as well as memory and foundry manufacturing. In May 2026 the company's IR representative said demand from global chipmakers and equipment makers had kept rising steadily since late 2025.

The same representative added that even if the chip cycle sees some adjustment, a sharp drop in this year's results is not considered likely. The company describes demand as growing alongside the overall ALD materials market rather than in a single equipment category.

The most direct share indicator is a 2024 report that put the domestic canister share above 90%. However, a company official said in 2024 that canisters alone make further growth in semiconductors difficult, and described efforts to apply the same technology elsewhere.

A 2024 brokerage report pointed to the shift from tungsten to molybdenum in NAND processes as the background for demand for solid-type products.

Its main customers were described as equipment and materials makers with high exposure to leading-edge process investment, and in a downturn, cuts in customer investment could translate quickly into order changes.

Outlook

In May 2026 the company said it expected results in both Q2 and Q3 to grow from Q1. Q2 sales of KRW 17.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.39 billion were the highest among the reported quarters.

By contrast, Q3 sales of KRW 14.9 billion were close to Q1, and operating profit of KRW 2.03 billion was below Q1's KRW 2.08 billion. Around June 2026, the company had said it expected record quarterly sales and operating profit in Q2. The solid ALD canister is being developed with SK hynix and two other partners.

In August 2025 a company official said mass production could begin from 2026, but as of May 2026 mass-production sales had not started, and the company internally expects sales contribution from the second half of 2027.

The company has said it is difficult to reference Q4 or full-year figures because no confirmed numbers exist yet. On shareholder returns, a June 1, 2026 extraordinary general meeting approved moving KRW 2.5 billion of capital surplus into retained earnings to secure dividend funds. The same meeting approved a stock option grant of 106,700 shares, equal to 0.85% of total issued shares.

Bull factors

Canister share and rising demand

A 2024 report put the company's domestic canister share above 90%. Q2 2026 sales of KRW 17.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.39 billion were the highest among the reported quarters. In its Q1 2026 results announcement, the company reported domestic sales up 10.8% and overseas sales up 21.5%. The IR representative said ALD demand from chip and equipment makers has kept rising since late 2025.

Subsidiary turns profitable and consolidated margins improve

The company said its subsidiary turned profitable in Q1 2026, helping lift consolidated profitability. Operating margin for the quarter was 14.0%, up 8.2 percentage points from 5.8% a year earlier. The company said restructuring is largely complete and it expects profits to continue.

Solid ALD canister product

The solid ALD canister for chemical use is being developed with SK hynix and two other partners, and is designed to supply solid precursors for next-generation molybdenum-based processes. The company internally expects it could contribute sales from the second half of 2027. As of May 2026, mass-production sales had not yet started.

Q3 2026 sales fell 14.0% from the prior quarter and operating profit fell 40.1%. Full-year 2025 operating profit declined 16.6%, and the operating margin fell from 11.9% to 8.1%. Sales rose 22.0% in 2025, but profit did not grow at the same pace, so revenue growth did not translate into earnings.

Dependence on canisters and customer investment cycles

A company official said in 2024 that canisters alone make further growth in semiconductors difficult. Because its main customers are equipment and materials makers, their investment adjustments can quickly affect orders.

In May 2026 the company said a sharp decline is not likely even if the chip cycle adjusts, but that is the company's own view.

Subsidiary acquisition debate and small-cap structure

Some in the market argued that the acquisition of the subsidiary, which had been loss-making, was overly aggressive, while the company has said it was made for long-term technology competitiveness rather than short-term earnings.

As of August 14, 2026, the largest shareholder and related parties held 70.73%, and the free float ratio was 28.20%. As of the same date, no investment opinion had been issued within the prior three months.

Risk factors

Customer concentration and chip cycle

Sales depend on a limited customer base of ALD equipment and materials makers, and their investment pace feeds directly into orders and results. Quarterly profit can swing sharply, as it did in Q3 2026.

The company says a sharp decline is unlikely even with a chip cycle adjustment, but that view depends on customer investment flows.

New product timing and subsidiary results

The company internally expects solid canister sales to contribute from the second half of 2027, a later timeline than the 2026 mass-production target it had mentioned in 2025. The subsidiary turned profitable in Q1 2026 after weighing on consolidated results through 2025. If its results deteriorate again, gains in the core business could be offset.

Governance and liquidity

The largest shareholder and related parties hold over 70%, and the free float is about 28%, so trading can be driven by a small number of shares. As of August 2026, no investment opinion had been issued in the prior three months, suggesting limited analyst coverage.

Because the subsidiary acquisition has drawn debate, capital allocation decisions will also draw market attention.

Overall view

GO Element is a semiconductor parts company centered on canisters and level sensors for ALD processes, and its 2025 sales of KRW 54.6 billion were the largest of 2022 to 2025 on a disclosed basis.

Operating profit, however, fell from KRW 7.29 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.43 billion in 2025, and the operating margin dropped to 8.1%.

In 2026, the subsidiary's turn to profitability and stronger semiconductor demand lifted Q2 sales to KRW 17.4 billion and operating profit to KRW 3.39 billion, the highest among the reported quarters.

In Q3, sales of KRW 14.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.03 billion both fell from the prior quarter, which increased quarterly volatility. Strengths include a high domestic canister share, rising customer demand and improved subsidiary results.

Weaknesses include product and customer concentration, dependence on customer investment cycles and a later timeline for the new product's sales. Controlling-shareholder net profit for the last four quarters totaled KRW 7.69 billion, above the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 5.46 billion.

On the asset side, the key relationship is between market capitalization and controlling-shareholder equity of KRW 58.9 billion at the end of 2025, and the concentrated ownership and low free float also deserve attention.

The next checkpoints are the annual operating margin in the 2026 annual report, the Q1 2027 quarterly report, and whether solid canister sales begin.
