Back-End Equipment Holding: Testing the Profit Recovery

PSK Holdings posted third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 35 billion, extending a two-quarter rise from the first-quarter low, while full-year 2025 operating profit stayed below the 2024 peak, so the durability of the recovery is the next question.

02

Business structure

PSK Holdings is a holding company that manufactures and sells semiconductor packaging equipment. Its main products are the Descum system, fluxless reflow equipment and Hot DI Water heating equipment, and sales are mainly semiconductor process equipment.

A Korean job-information aggregator puts semiconductor process equipment at 81% of sales and parts and service fees at 19%, but its sales scale uses a different basis from the disclosed results, so the disclosed figures govern the amounts.

The overseas business is run through SEMIgear Inc., a subsidiary in Austin, Texas, which sells semiconductor equipment and parts. The company merged with the unlisted PSK Holdings of Pyeongtaek in February 2020, which restructured the group.

It holds a 32.76% stake in the listed PSK Inc., which was split off from PSK Holdings in 2019 to run the front-end equipment business. Customers are global semiconductor manufacturers, and competitive factors include price competitiveness, productivity, short delivery times and fast technical service.

Because equipment delivery timing drives quarterly revenue, sales fell from KRW 90.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 28 billion in the first quarter of 2026 before recovering to KRW 83 billion in the third quarter.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual figures, revenue rose from KRW 72.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 215.5 billion in 2024 and then fell 3.6% to KRW 207.8 billion in 2025. 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest among 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 16.9 billion in 2022, KRW 27 billion in 2023 (up 59.6%), KRW 88.5 billion in 2024 (up 228.1%) and KRW 73.4 billion in 2025 (down 17.0%). Operating margin rose from 23.2% in 2022 to 41.1% in 2024 and eased to 35.3% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders grew from KRW 40.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 95.8 billion in 2024 and fell 4.3% to KRW 91.7 billion in 2025, and the disclosed net income figures match these amounts each year.

Operating cash flow rose every year, from KRW 19.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 81.4 billion in 2025, and the debt ratio fell to 15.5% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue peaked at KRW 90.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and then dropped 43.2% in the fourth quarter to KRW 51.4 billion, with operating profit down 65.6% to KRW 14.2 billion. The first quarter of 2026 was the low point at KRW 28 billion in revenue and KRW 9.17 billion in operating profit.

Revenue then recovered to KRW 47.3 billion in the second quarter and KRW 83 billion in the third, with operating profit reaching KRW 35 billion.

Gross profit rose to KRW 58.5 billion while selling and administrative expenses increased to KRW 23.5 billion from KRW 16 billion a year earlier, according to a news report based on the quarterly filing.

For the first nine months of 2026, revenue was KRW 158.3 billion, up 1.3%, and operating profit was KRW 58 billion, down 2.1%. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose for three straight quarters after the fourth-quarter 2025 low, to KRW 43.9 billion in the third quarter.

Operating profit fell 15.1% in that quarter while this measure rose 12.7%, which points to non-operating items or taxes as the source of the gap, a detail to confirm in the 2026 annual report.

05

Industry analysis

The core end market is advanced semiconductor packaging equipment. A Korean economic daily reported that capacity expansion at TSMC and OSATs (outsourced assembly and test providers) is concentrated among beneficiaries, and the company is named among them.

Hana Securities explained the third-quarter improvement through CoWoS-related equipment revenue that began to be recognized after being deferred from the prior quarter.

If that explanation holds, part of third-quarter revenue reflected timing carryover, so results after the fourth quarter depend more on new orders and the pace of customer capital spending. The cycle appears to be in a capex expansion phase, but whether that continues into 2027 depends on customer announcements.

Memory stacking and advanced packaging investment are a second source of equipment demand. Competitive positioning rests on price competitiveness, productivity, short lead times and fast technical service, which recur in the company's earnings commentary.

Market share and order backlog figures against peers were not available in the sources used.

The affiliate PSK Inc. focuses on front-end equipment, so the two share the same chip capex cycle while their product mix differs, and the affiliate's industry conditions feed directly into the value of the 32.76% stake held by PSK Holdings.

Risk factors

Customer capex swings

Advanced packaging equipment demand depends heavily on the capex plans of TSMC and the OSATs. If investment schedules slip or shrink, equipment orders and revenue recognition can both be pushed back.

Because third-quarter improvement leaned on deferred revenue, new order volume in the coming quarters determines whether the trend continues.

Earnings volatility and revenue timing

Quarterly revenue swung from KRW 90.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 28 billion in the first quarter of 2026. Because equipment revenue depends on delivery and acceptance timing, a single quarter is hard to read as a trend. Operating margin also fell from 41.1% in 2024 to 35.3% in 2025.

Holding structure and affiliate dependence

PSK Holdings holds 32.76% of PSK Inc., so its results are exposed to the affiliate's industry conditions and the valuation of that stake. How much of the subsidiary value a holding share price reflects depends on market assessment. Changes in the value of the stake can move group results and the share price separately from core operations.

Overall view

PSK Holdings is a holding company centered on semiconductor packaging equipment. On the disclosed figures, revenue and operating profit peaked in 2024 and slowed in 2025.

In 2026, revenue and operating profit recovered in the second and third quarters after the first-quarter low, with third-quarter operating profit of KRW 35 billion, up 153.8% from the prior quarter.

Yet third-quarter revenue was 8.3% below a year earlier, selling and administrative expenses rose, and cumulative operating profit for the first nine months fell 2.1%.

Because Hana Securities pointed to deferred CoWoS equipment revenue as the source of the third-quarter gain, results after the fourth quarter depend on new orders and customer capex execution. The price-to-book ratio sits well above 1x, which makes the durability of the profit recovery the central valuation question.

On the financial side, the debt ratio was 15.5% at the end of 2025 and operating cash flow rose each year. The next checks are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results and the 2026 annual report, where operating margin will be compared with 35.3% in 2025.
