02

Business structure

Nable is a communications and security solutions company founded in 2003 that listed on KOSDAQ in July 2012. It operates 4G and 5G based smart networks, the MCPTT disaster safety network core solution, Enterprise VoIP, and AIoT.

Based on Butler's aggregation of the second quarter of 2026, revenue mix was 54% wired-wireless convergence communications and IoT, 39% other, and 7% ALL-IP communications security.

Starting with a private network build at Ewha Mokdong Hospital, the company has extended 5G private network use into rail infrastructure and smart factories (FnGuide basis).

The counterparties to the large disclosed contracts are telecom operators, and the June 2026 5G private network contract for Hana Financial Group was worth KRW 241 million? Corrected: KRW 2.41 billion.

The SK Telecom contract, signed October 28, 2026 and disclosed October 29, is worth KRW 1.92 billion, or 10.01% of 2025 revenue. The two contracts combined total KRW 4.33 billion, about 22.56% of 2025 revenue.

In August 2026 the company agreed with LG Uplus on equipment supply and construction to add network redundancy and expand wireless coverage. On the AI side, it launched Smart Patent, an AI-based patent management platform (FnGuide basis). The largest shareholder and related parties hold 70.78% according to FnGuide.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue in the regular reports went from KRW 18.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.4 billion in 2023, down 26.5% year on year, then rose to KRW 17.2 billion in 2024 (up 27.9%) and KRW 19.2 billion in 2025 (up 11.6%).

Revenue rose for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest among 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 3.36 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 2.1 billion in 2023, turned positive at KRW 385 million in 2024, and reached KRW 2.61 billion in 2025, up 578.3% year on year. The operating margin moved from 18.4% in 2022 to -15.6% in 2023, 2.2% in 2024 and 13.6% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.28 billion in 2022, a loss of KRW 1.44 billion in 2023, KRW 1.29 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.52 billion in 2025, up 96.0%. Net income including non-controlling interests followed the same pattern in those years.

Operating cash flow was KRW 7.23 billion in 2022, then -KRW 2.56 billion in 2023, -KRW 653 million in 2024 and -KRW 706 million in 2025.

Quarterly revenue climbed from KRW 5.18 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 7.41 billion in the fourth quarter, up 42.9% quarter on quarter, before dropping to KRW 2.85 billion in the first quarter of 2026, down 61.5%.

Revenue recovered to KRW 3.45 billion in the second quarter and KRW 4.42 billion in the third quarter of 2026, which was down 14.7% year on year.

Operating profit was KRW 1.07 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 1.43 billion in the fourth quarter, then an operating loss of KRW 1.12 billion in the first quarter of 2026, narrowing to KRW 555 million and KRW 485 million in the next two quarters.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders were KRW 915 million, KRW 341 million and KRW 269 million in the first three quarters of 2026. The sharp rise in the fourth quarter of 2025 and drop in the first quarter of 2026 suggest quarterly swings driven by the timing of large contract deliveries.

05

Industry analysis

When it announced the LG Uplus contract in August 2026, Nable said demand for 5G private networks is moving from initial builds to reinforcing stability, expanding coverage and adding equipment (based on Electronic Times reporting).

The company says it is broadening its references in industries where stability matters, such as transportation infrastructure and semiconductor manufacturing.

Reporting notes that network redundancy and coverage quality are emerging as competitive factors in transportation, manufacturing, logistics and public infrastructure where a communications outage directly affects operations.

The SK Telecom contract is labeled as a 6th facility investment of 2026, which points to telecom capex being executed in batches. In Physical AI, industrial communications infrastructure that reliably carries data from robots and autonomous driving systems is seen as central.

Nable has proposed extending its industrial Eum 5G deployment experience into autonomous robots and smart factories. On the technology side, the company cites its network design, control and network software capabilities for private network design, construction, integration and optimization.

The October 2026 SK Telecom contract carries no down payment or advance, with full cash payment after delivery inspection. Because revenue is smaller than that of large IT services companies, a single contract accounts for around 10% of annual revenue.

06

Outlook

The SK Telecom contract worth KRW 1.92 billion runs from October 28, 2026 to December 31, 2026, with a delivery deadline of December 31, 2026. With no down payment or advance, cash collection follows delivery inspection.

The Hana Financial Group 5G private network contract (KRW 2.41 billion) runs from June 15, 2026 to January 7, 2027. The LG Uplus project upgrades network redundancy and wireless coverage at core transportation hubs, and its revenue contribution will be confirmed in later regular reports.

The Physical AI business rests on the October 29, 2026 MOU with SureSoft Tech, which the company said on November 10 it will advance through a joint working group, PoC and on-site demonstrations.

The company also plans to jointly identify government and public agency R&D and demonstration projects in Physical AI and smart manufacturing. On the AI side, expansion of the AI-based patent management platform Smart Patent continues in parallel (FnGuide basis).

The next regular report is the 2026 annual report, which will confirm fourth-quarter 2026 results and the full-year operating margin. The first-quarter 2027 results will show whether the losses that ran through the third quarter of 2026 turn into profit. Whether the large contract delivery timing that drives quarterly swings continues is another point to track.

08

Bull factors

Earnings recovery trend

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 2.1 billion in 2023 to KRW 385 million in 2024 and KRW 2.61 billion in 2025. The operating margin rose from 2.2% in 2024 to 13.6% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 2.52 billion in 2025, up 96.0%.

Consecutive large telecom contracts

Contracts disclosed in 2026 include the Hana Financial Group 5G private network deal (KRW 2.41 billion) in June and the SK Telecom contract (KRW 1.92 billion) in October. An LG Uplus 5G private network upgrade was announced in August.

The company frames these as expansion and upgrades of existing networks, which it cites as evidence of recurring demand.

Expansion into Physical AI

Nable plans to extend its industrial Eum 5G experience into autonomous robots and smart factories. An MOU signed on October 29, 2026 with SureSoft Tech combines its connectivity with AI and robot software verification. The AI-based patent management platform Smart Patent is also presented as part of its AI business.

09

Bear factors

Quarterly losses persist

The company posted operating losses in each of the first three quarters of 2026. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 269 million in the third quarter, a swing into loss from a year earlier. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 4.42 billion fell 14.7% year on year.

Concentration in contracts and counterparties

The disclosed large contracts are concentrated with SK Telecom. The October 2026 SK Telecom contract runs only from October 28 to December 31, 2026. Two SK Telecom contracts together total about 22.56% of 2025 revenue.

Limited free float

The largest shareholder and related parties hold 70.78%, according to FnGuide. With a small market capitalization, a limited free float can constrain trading. Until new business results become visible, profit improvement remains the main reference point.

10

Risk factors

Customer and contract concentration

Revenue is tied to telecom private network contracts, so a change in one customer's capex plans affects quarterly sales directly. A single SK Telecom contract equals 10.01% of 2025 revenue. The more contracts cluster in specific periods, the larger the quarterly swings.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Quarterly revenue swung from KRW 7.41 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.85 billion in the first quarter of 2026. Operating profit moved from KRW 1.43 billion to an operating loss of KRW 1.12 billion over the same span. Controlling shareholders' net loss over the last four quarters totals KRW 511 million.

Execution risk in new businesses

The Physical AI business must move from an MOU through a joint working group, PoC and on-site demonstrations before commercialization. Verified references in autonomous robots and smart factories are not yet visible in disclosures. New business spending could also slow the recovery of core profit.

12

Overall view

Nable's earnings base recovered, with operating profit turning positive in 2024 and reaching KRW 2.61 billion in 2025. However, operating losses ran from the first through the third quarter of 2026, and third-quarter revenue fell 14.7% year on year.

Operating losses narrowed each quarter, from KRW 1.12 billion to KRW 555 million and KRW 485 million. Revenue depends on telecom 5G private network contracts, so the timing of a single customer's contracts strongly shapes quarterly results.

Contracts in 2026 with SK Telecom and Hana Financial Group continued, and the LG Uplus upgrade and Physical AI MOU are widening the scope of business.

On the other hand, the largest shareholder's stake is high, leaving a limited free float, and the commercialization timing of the new businesses has not yet been confirmed in disclosures.

The 2026 annual report will confirm fourth-quarter results and the full-year operating margin, and the first-quarter 2027 results will show whether narrowing losses turn into profit.
