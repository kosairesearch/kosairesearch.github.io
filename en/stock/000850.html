02

Business structure

Hwacheon Machine Tool is a machine tool manufacturer headquartered and primarily based in Gwangju, operating a machinery division that produces CNC lathes, CNC milling machines, and machining centers (MC), alongside a materials division that makes robot components and industrial machinery castings.

According to recent disclosed data, the machinery division accounts for 83.6% of sales and the materials division for 16.4%.

Domestically, machine tools are produced at the Gwangju main plant and the Pyeongdong plant, while the Yeonggwang No. 2 plant produces industrial materials supplied directly to domestic clients and overseas customers including those in Japan.

Overseas machinery sales are handled directly by regional sales subsidiaries established across the Americas, Europe, Southeast Asia, India, Vietnam, and China.

Domestic distribution runs through the similarly named but separately listed Hwacheon Machinery, in which Hwacheon Machine Tool holds a 39.95% stake as the largest shareholder.

Core components such as controllers and bearings are largely imported, exposing the cost structure to currency movements in major sourcing countries, while export exposure also creates direct profit and loss sensitivity to exchange rates.

In the domestic machine tool market, DN Solutions is regarded as the industry leader, with Hwacheon Machine Tool counted alongside SMEC and Daesung Hi-Tech among the export-driving specialist makers.

The company's core products are general-purpose and CNC machines for automotive, mold and die, and general component machining, while the materials division supplies castings for robotics and industrial machinery as a diversification pillar.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales peaked at KRW 244.1 billion in 2022 before declining for three straight years to KRW 225.7 billion in 2023 (down 7.5%), KRW 222.2 billion in 2024 (down 1.6%), and KRW 221.8 billion in 2025 (down 0.2%).

Operating profit fell from KRW 8.18 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.56 billion in 2023 (down 44.3%), swung to an operating loss of KRW 741 million in 2024, and then turned profitable again at KRW 4.22 billion in 2025. The operating margin fell from 3.4% in 2022 to -0.3% in 2024 before recovering to 1.9% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to owners fell from KRW 37.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.6 billion in 2023 (down 71.8%) and KRW 8.65 billion in 2024 (down 18.1%), then rose to KRW 11.4 billion in 2025 (up 31.5%), with 2024 marking the smallest year in the 2022-2025 period.

On a quarterly basis, sales rose from KRW 58.5 billion in Q2 2025 to KRW 65.7 billion in Q3 2025 (up 12.2%), fell to KRW 51.2 billion in Q4 2025 (down 22.1%), then recovered to KRW 55.2 billion in Q1 2026 (up 7.8%) and KRW 74.9 billion in Q2 2026 (up 35.8% quarter-on-quarter and 28.0% year-on-year), marking two consecutive quarters of growth.

Operating profit posted a loss of KRW 1.68 billion in Q4 2025 before turning profitable at KRW 1.87 billion in Q1 2026 and jumping to KRW 6.48 billion in Q2 2026 (up 246.9% quarter-on-quarter and 212.2% year-on-year).

Net profit attributable to owners likewise fell to KRW 851 million in Q4 2025 before rising to KRW 4.73 billion in Q1 2026 (up 456.1%) and KRW 9.31 billion in Q2 2026 (up 96.8% quarter-on-quarter and 174.0% year-on-year), two consecutive quarters of growth.

The sum of net profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters (Q3 2025-Q2 2026) was KRW 20.5 billion, and the year-end 2025 debt ratio of 23.8% was down from 37.3% in 2022, reflecting a steady improvement in balance sheet metrics.

05

Industry analysis

According to the Korea Machine Tool Industry Association, total domestic machine tool orders reached KRW 1.1924 trillion in January-April 2026, up 11.2% year-on-year, with overseas orders of KRW 798.1 billion surging 23.3% to lead the growth.

April orders alone rose 9.8% year-on-year to KRW 316.5 billion, with overseas orders up 25.7% to KRW 202.8 billion, showing a similar pattern. An industry official noted that demand for ultra-precision machine tools used to produce data center cooling equipment and semiconductor inspection gear is rising.

Among domestic machine tool specialists leading exports, Hwacheon Machine Tool was named alongside DN Solutions, SMEC, and Daesung Hi-Tech.

At SIMTOS 2026, Korea's largest biennial manufacturing technology exhibition held in April 2026 at KINTEX in Goyang, major domestic players including DN Solutions, Hwacheon Machine Tool, and Hyundai Wia's machine tool unit reportedly presented visions of becoming platform providers managing the entire manufacturing process rather than simply selling equipment.

The industry appears to be at an inflection point where traditional automotive and mold-die demand remains stagnant while AI, semiconductor, and data center-related investment emerges as a new demand driver.

The competitive landscape is also being reshaped, as domestic leader DN Solutions acquired German machine tool maker HELLER in early 2026.

Hwacheon Machine Tool, a mid-sized integrated machine tool maker combining general-purpose and CNC machinery with a materials business, remains positioned to watch whether it benefits from the broader industry recovery.

Bull factors

Two Straight Quarters of Earnings Rebound

Following the Q4 2025 trough, sales, operating profit, and net profit attributable to owners all improved consecutively in Q1 and Q2 2026. Q2 operating profit of KRW 6.48 billion rose 246.9% quarter-on-quarter and 212.2% year-on-year.

While additional quarters are needed to confirm whether this is a base-effect bounce or a trend recovery, it is a clear short-term improvement signal.

Machine Tool Industry Recovery Led by Exports

Total domestic machine tool orders rose 11.2% year-on-year in January-April 2026, with overseas orders surging 23.3% to lead growth.

Rising demand for ultra-precision machining tied to data centers and semiconductors is cited as a background factor, and Hwacheon Machine Tool is named among the export-leading specialists. Diversification of end markets could support earnings stability if it continues.

Improving Balance Sheet and Continued Dividends

The debt ratio fell steadily from 37.3% in 2022 to 23.8% in 2025, while operating cash flow rose from KRW 5.27 billion in 2023 to KRW 15.8 billion in 2025. The company continued its dividend payments, with the board resolving a fiscal 2025 year-end cash dividend totaling KRW 2.86 billion in February 2026.

Strong financial flexibility would be advantageous for securing investment resources during an industry recovery.

Bear factors

Multi-Year Revenue Stagnation and Decline

Annual sales declined for three consecutive years from KRW 244.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 221.8 billion in 2025. Whether the first-half 2026 rebound can reverse this pattern remains unconfirmed. If structural stagnation in end-market demand persists, further top-line growth could remain limited.

Low Operating Margin and the 2024 Operating Loss

The operating margin fell from 3.4% in 2022 to -0.3% in 2024 and remained at only 1.9% in 2025. The company posted an operating loss of KRW 741 million in 2024, and the absolute level of profitability remains low. While Q2 2026 operating profit rose sharply, quarter-to-quarter volatility also warrants attention.

Exposure to FX and Imported Component Dependence

A significant portion of core machine tool components such as controllers and bearings are imported, exposing the cost structure to currency fluctuations in major sourcing countries. At the same time, export exposure means exchange rate movements directly affect profit and loss.

If the end-market cycle and currency movements turn unfavorable simultaneously, profitability could come under pressure.

Risk factors

Sensitivity to the Industry Cycle

Machine tool demand is heavily dependent on capital investment cycles in automotive, mold and die, and general manufacturing. While order recovery was confirmed in January-April 2026, whether this trend continues is uncertain, and a slowdown in end-market investment could cause orders and sales to decline again. Repeated quarter-to-quarter volatility in the past should also be factored in.

Foreign Exchange and Cost Structure

High dependence on imported controllers and bearings means exchange rate movements directly affect cost structure. At the same time, export exposure creates a dual exposure where currency moves affect both sales and profit and loss. Either won weakness or strength can introduce volatility to the operating margin.

Intensifying Competition

Competition is intensifying as domestic leader DN Solutions acquired Germany's HELLER in early 2026, among other moves by rivals to expand technology and scale. Hwacheon Machine Tool, as a mid-sized integrated machine tool maker, must compete simultaneously against larger rivals and specialized niche processing brands.

How much the company's new product and platform strategy contributes to maintaining market share requires ongoing confirmation.

Overall view

Hwacheon Machine Tool's results improved for two consecutive quarters across sales, operating profit, and net profit attributable to owners in Q1 and Q2 2026, following a trough in Q4 2025.

On an annual basis, however, this sits on a base of three consecutive years of declining sales since 2022, and the operating margin, after turning to a loss in 2024, remained at only 1.9% in 2025, meaning the absolute level of profitability stays low.

The steady decline in the debt ratio, rising operating cash flow, and a consistent history of cash dividend payments stand out as positive factors for financial stability.

On the industry side, domestic machine tool orders have shown an export-led recovery in 2026, raising the possibility that the end-market cycle has passed a trough, but how long this trend persists and how much it feeds through to company results will require confirmation from additional quarterly data.

Structural risks such as dual exposure to currency movements affecting both costs and revenue, along with intensifying competition, are also factors to weigh. The next quarterly report, industry order statistics, and the full-year 2026 results disclosure will be important reference points for future assessment.
