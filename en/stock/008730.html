Report overview

Back in Loss in Q3 2026; Battery Films Are the Swing Factor

Youlchon Chemical swung to an operating profit in 2025 and recovered in the second quarter of 2026, but returned to an operating loss of KRW 5.44 billion in the third quarter, leaving the growth pace of its battery-film electronic materials business as the key variable.

02

Business structure

Youlchon Chemical, founded in 1973, makes packaging and electronic materials and has grown on packaging volume from its Nongshim group food customers. The business has two segments.

The packaging segment produces flexible packaging, BOPP and CPP film packaging for food, household and industrial uses, while the electronic materials segment makes release paper and aluminum pouch film for secondary batteries. Segment weights depend on the basis used.

One press analysis put packaging at 71% and electronic materials at 29% for 2024, while a data aggregator's standalone tally for 2025 shows 65.8% and 34.2%. In the same tally, the electronic materials share rose from 22% in 2023 to 34% in 2025. Customer concentration is high.

Sales to the parent Nongshim account for 30% of the total and LG Energy Solution for 19.6%.

In electronics, the company supplies high-formability pouch film to domestic battery makers including LG Energy Solution and Samsung SDI, and media reports say it is running trial deliveries to major battery makers in North America and Europe.

Separately, a pouch supply contract with the GM joint venture Ultium Cells was terminated, and the settlement payment was reflected in sales and operating profit, which carries some one-off character. Raw materials centre on petrochemical inputs such as PP resin, which is linked to crude oil prices.

In packaging, the company is described as the number one domestic flexible packaging supplier, backed by deliveries to large groups such as Nongshim.

In battery cell pouch film, Japanese suppliers held a long-standing near-monopoly, and the company is reported to have localised aluminum pouch production for the first time in Korea.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 508.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 414.5 billion in 2023, down 18.6% year on year and the smallest year of 2022 to 2025, before rising to KRW 457.1 billion in 2024 (up 10.3%) and KRW 485.4 billion in 2025 (up 6.2%), so sales increased in both 2024 and 2025.

Operating results moved from a loss of KRW 4.62 billion in 2022 to losses of KRW 16.2 billion in 2023 and KRW 18.4 billion in 2024, with the losses widening in both years, before operating profit of KRW 8.51 billion in 2025 marked a turn to profit.

The operating margin went from -0.9% in 2022 to -3.9% in 2023 and -4.0% in 2024, then reached 1.8% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to shareholders was a loss of KRW 4.15 billion in 2022, a loss of KRW 19.1 billion in 2023 and a loss of KRW 8.53 billion in 2024, which narrowed, before turning to a profit of KRW 4.17 billion in 2025; net income including non-controlling interests also turned to a profit of KRW 4.17 billion in 2025.

The company cited higher export volumes by segment, currency effects and a portfolio adjustment away from low-margin products as the main drivers of the 2025 change. A data aggregator's analysis suggests that the 2025 results partly reflect the settlement payment from the terminated Ultium Cells contract.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 116.8 billion with an operating loss of KRW 2.32 billion, and Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 116.1 billion, down 0.6% from the previous quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 506 million.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 127.2 billion, up 9.6% quarter on quarter, and operating profit was KRW 561 million, a turn to profit.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 156.1 billion, up 22.7% quarter on quarter, with operating profit of KRW 9.54 billion, up 1,599.4% quarter on quarter, and net profit attributable to shareholders of KRW 5.79 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 135.6 billion, down 13.1% quarter on quarter and up 16.1% year on year, but the company posted an operating loss of KRW 5.44 billion, a turn to loss, and a net loss attributable to shareholders of KRW 10.2 billion, which widened the loss year on year.

The net loss attributable to shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 3.91 billion. The sharp rise in Q2 operating profit followed by the Q3 operating loss shows that quarterly earnings remain volatile.

The debt ratio rose from 95.7% in 2022 to 135.3% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 395.3 billion and total equity of KRW 292.2 billion at the end of 2025. Operating cash flow rose to KRW 27.1 billion in 2025 from KRW 8.34 billion in 2024.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from end markets depends mainly on the expansion of electric vehicles and energy storage systems. Media analysis attributes the recovery in profitability of the electronic materials segment to rising demand for high-value pouch film.

Battery demand, however, moves with vehicle maker and ESS order flows, so changes in customer shipment volumes can translate directly into segment results. The packaging segment is tied to food and household consumption, and PP resin, a key raw material, moves with crude oil prices, making input costs a variable.

Within the packaging sector, a data aggregator's tally shows Youlchon Chemical's operating margin below the sector average and its debt ratio well above the sector average.

In battery cell pouch film, Japanese suppliers held a long-standing near-monopoly, and the company is described as the first to localise aluminum pouch production in Korea.

For this reason, competitiveness depends more on passing customer qualification and securing volume, both at home and abroad, than on the technology alone. Whether trial deliveries to overseas customers turn into firm contracts is the key test for expanding electronic materials.

The packaging business is described as holding the number one share of the domestic flexible packaging market, but overall growth depends more heavily on electronic materials volumes.

Outlook

The company-level fact confirmed so far is its 2026 corporate value enhancement plan. In a disclosure dated March 25, 2026, the company raised its target for the governance key indicator compliance rate from 33.3% to 40% and said it will pursue higher overseas sales in electronic materials.

Media reports also cite a 2030 sales target of KRW 700 billion for the electronic materials segment. On capacity, reports say the company keeps investing in expansion and funds the spending partly through borrowings.

For full-year 2026, a data aggregator's consensus, as of September 2026, projects revenue of KRW 537.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 13.1 billion. That estimate predates the Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 5.44 billion, so the two may not be directly comparable.

The first checkpoint is whether the Q4 2026 provisional results and full-year operating profit approach that consensus level. The second is whether trial deliveries to overseas electronic materials customers convert into volume production. Packaging remains exposed to volumes from Nongshim and to raw material prices.

With borrowings rising, whether operating cash flow can cover investment needs is another point to check in the next regular report.

Bull factors

Operating profit in Q1 and Q2 2026

Operating profit of KRW 561 million in Q1 2026 turned the prior quarter's loss into a profit, and Q2 2026 delivered revenue of KRW 156.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 9.54 billion. Net profit attributable to shareholders in Q2 2026 was KRW 5.79 billion.

The quarterly results show the same direction of core earnings recovery seen in the 2025 annual turn to profit. The fact that this pattern did not carry into Q3 2026 must be considered alongside it.

Growth path for battery pouch film

As electric vehicle and ESS demand grew, demand for high-value pouch film rose, and the company is reported to have localised battery aluminum pouch production for the first time in Korea. The electronic materials share of sales rose from 22% in 2023 to 34% in 2025 in a data aggregator's tally.

The company supplies major domestic battery makers and is reported to be running trial deliveries to makers in North America and Europe. The reported 2030 sales target for electronic materials is KRW 700 billion.

Overseas expansion and governance targets

In a corporate value enhancement plan disclosed on March 25, 2026, the company said it would raise its governance key indicator compliance target from 33.3% to 40%. The same plan states that it will pursue higher overseas sales in the electronic materials segment. Whether rising overseas sales lower dependence on a few customers is the next point to check.

Bear factors

Turn back to loss in Q3 2026

Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 5.44 billion followed operating profit of KRW 9.54 billion in Q2, and the net loss attributable to shareholders of KRW 10.2 billion widened the loss year on year. Revenue of KRW 135.6 billion was up 16.1% year on year, but the top-line growth did not carry through to profit.

The net loss attributable to shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 3.91 billion, which leads some to see the 2025 return to profit as not yet a steady earnings trend.

High debt and interest coverage

The debt ratio rose from 95.7% in 2022 to 135.3% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total liabilities of KRW 395.3 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 292.2 billion. A press analysis reported interest coverage ratios of -1.62x in 2022, -3.64x in 2023 and -3.77x in 2024.

Reports also say operating profit exceeded interest expense in the first half of 2025, so improvement signals and the burden both remain.

Customer concentration and cost swings

Sales to Nongshim account for 30% and LG Energy Solution for 19.6% in a data aggregator's tally, so order changes from these two customers feed directly into results. Raw materials account for 62.7% of manufacturing cost in the same tally, and PP resin is linked to crude oil prices.

The settlement payment from the terminated Ultium Cells contract reflected in 2025 results has a one-off character, so underlying earnings excluding it should be assessed separately.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and battery demand

High dependence on specific customers such as Nongshim and LG Energy Solution means that changes in group volumes or slower battery demand flow straight into sales. If overseas customer expansion in electronic materials is delayed, the customer concentration issue is unlikely to ease. Timing of new battery customer projects is an external variable the company cannot control.

Raw material prices and currency

Because the main raw material PP resin is linked to crude oil, a sharp rise in oil prices raises input costs and pressures the operating margin if price pass-through lags. The company cited currency effects as a contributor to the 2025 improvement, so earnings can move with the exchange rate. Changes in sales mix from the adjustment of low-margin products are another variable.

Balance sheet and capex

With the debt ratio at 135.3%, reports say the company funds capacity expansion partly with borrowings. Operating cash flow improved to KRW 27.1 billion in 2025, but whether it can cover investment and repayment needs must be confirmed in the next regular report. A change in interest rates or borrowing terms could raise the interest burden.

Overall view

Youlchon Chemical recorded operating losses from 2022 through 2024, then turned to an operating profit of KRW 8.51 billion in 2025, and also posted operating profit in Q1 and Q2 2026. Q3 2026 brought an operating loss of KRW 5.44 billion and a net loss attributable to shareholders of KRW 10.2 billion.

The business splits into a packaging segment centred on food packaging and an electronic materials segment centred on battery pouch film, and the electronic materials share rose from 22% in 2023 to 34% in 2025.

Customers are concentrated in Nongshim and LG Energy Solution, and the debt ratio rose to 135.3% at the end of 2025. The price-to-book ratio is below 1x, and the price-to-earnings ratio is not calculated on the last four quarters.

Positive factors are the return to profit, growth in electronic materials and overseas expansion plans, while negative factors are unstable quarterly results, high debt and customer concentration. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 provisional results and the operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report.
