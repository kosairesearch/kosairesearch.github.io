Report overview

Truen: Earnings Volatility Amid Overseas Expansion Efforts

Truen, which has built a position in Korea's public-sector-driven AI video surveillance market, rebounded in Q2 2026 after a Q1 slowdown, and is exploring overseas public-market opportunities as US NDAA-related restrictions weigh on Chinese camera makers.

  1. 1

    2025 annual revenue of KRW 49.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 13.3 billion (27.0% margin) both declined year-on-year, though the prior three years (2022-2024) had shown consistent growth.

  2. 2

    Q1 2026 revenue and operating profit fell sharply to KRW 7.8 billion and KRW 1.7 billion, respectively, before rebounding clearly in Q2 2026 to KRW 16.7 billion and KRW 4.5 billion.

  3. 3

    The company is estimated to hold a significant share of Korea's public-sector video surveillance procurement market, which underpins its earnings base but also drives volatility tied to government budget execution timing.

  4. 4

    Amid US National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) restrictions on Chinese-made security equipment, the company is attempting to expand its overseas public-market footprint through Ambarella SoC-based new products and its AI algorithm development team.

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Truen's core businesses span IP cameras, video streaming solutions such as video servers, NVRs and VMS, and IoT solutions under the EGLOO brand, including smart home cameras, video doorbells and cloud storage services.

IP cameras account for the dominant share of revenue, historically exceeding 80% based on past disclosures, with video streaming solutions and IoT solutions making up the remainder.

The core customer base is Korean public-sector procurement demand from government offices, police stations and schools, and early acquisition of TTA (Telecommunications Technology Association) security certification is understood to have contributed to expanded public-sector supply.

Truen operates its own manufacturing at its Seoul Guro-gu headquarters, with a structure requiring minimal capital investment given the nature of its business. Truen's business structure does not require large-scale production facility investment on the order of tens of billions of won.

A small number of staff responsible for production assemble and inspect products once work orders come down from the development team, then ship them out. The IoT solutions segment is understood to be contributing more to growth as domestic and overseas sales of new EGLOO products expand.

According to brokerage research, amid normalization of government budget execution, the company was expected to sustain stable earnings growth based on its market dominance in public institutions, with an estimated 40% share of the public video surveillance market.

In the domestic competitive landscape, Truen competes with large security imaging companies such as Hanwha Vision and Idis, while price competition from low-cost Chinese products has intensified.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue grew steadily from KRW 38.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 53.6 billion in 2024, before declining to KRW 49.1 billion in 2025.

Operating profit likewise fell from KRW 15.5 billion (28.8% margin) in 2024 to KRW 13.3 billion (27.0% margin) in 2025, and net profit attributable to owners declined from KRW 14.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 12.1 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue and operating profit moderated gradually from KRW 14.6 billion and KRW 4.1 billion in Q2 2025, to KRW 11.3 billion and KRW 3.6 billion in Q3, and KRW 13.2 billion and KRW 3.2 billion in Q4.

In Q1 2026, revenue and operating profit contracted sharply to KRW 7.8 billion and KRW 1.7 billion, the lowest levels among the five most recent quarters.

However, Q2 2026 rebounded clearly, with revenue of KRW 16.7 billion, operating profit of KRW 4.5 billion and owners' net profit of KRW 4.0 billion, the highest figures across the presented five-quarter window.

This contrast between the Q1 weakness and Q2 rebound illustrates how seasonality tied to public-sector budget execution timing significantly affects results.

Recently disclosed standalone-basis figures similarly showed that Q1 2026 standalone revenue fell 21.0% year-on-year, operating profit fell 31.6%, and net profit fell 32.9%, attributed to the fact that IP camera segment performance deteriorated due to reduced public institution budget execution and lower vacation-season demand, while profitability was squeezed by price competition with Chinese products. Operating margin improved from 24.1% in 2023 to 28.8% in 2024, then eased slightly to 27.0% in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's AI video surveillance market is heavily influenced by public-sector safety-related budget execution, with demand for intelligent CCTV tending to rise when social safety issues come to the fore.

The domestic market features competition between large integrated security firms and smaller specialized players, and Truen is estimated to hold a meaningful share of public procurement.

In recent years, low-cost Chinese cameras have expanded their penetration in both domestic and overseas markets, intensifying price competition and pressuring the profitability of domestic makers.

Meanwhile, in the United States, NDAA-driven restrictions on Chinese security equipment have continued, and this policy environment is assessed as potentially creating an overseas public-market opportunity for non-Chinese camera makers.

Indeed, a recent analysis noted that US NDAA regulations are expanding overseas public-market opportunities, and the company is broadening its overseas market presence through its AI algorithm development team and Ambarella SoC-based products.

Still, variables related to overseas expansion such as tariffs and certification procedures remain. Domestically, procurement requirements such as TTA security certification act as a certain barrier to entry, giving companies that secured certification early a relative advantage.

06

Outlook

In the near term, the pronounced rebound in Q2 2026 largely offset the Q1 weakness, making it a key point to watch whether this recovery continues into upcoming quarters.

Public-sector budget execution in Korea tends to concentrate in the second half of the year, particularly toward year-end, leaving Q4 demand trends as an important variable.

Overseas, as US NDAA-driven restrictions on Chinese equipment persist, the company appears to be pursuing overseas public-market opportunities through Ambarella SoC-based new products and AI algorithm capabilities.

In the IoT solutions segment, expanded sales of new EGLOO-branded products have the potential to contribute to revenue diversification. However, price competition from Chinese products in the domestic IP camera segment may persist, leaving margin defense as a key issue.

No specific annual guidance or major new order disclosures from the company were confirmed as of this research, and further confirmation through future disclosures and IR materials will be needed.

Bull factors

Solid Position in Public Procurement Market

Truen is estimated to hold a significant share of Korea's public video surveillance procurement market, which serves as a foundation for demand expansion when social safety issues come to the fore. Early acquisition of TTA security certification is understood to have favored its entry into public institutions. This structural advantage may serve as a certain barrier to new entrants.

Q2 Rebound Confirms Resilience

After a sharp Q1 2026 slowdown, both revenue and operating profit rebounded to their highest levels among the five quarters in Q2, suggesting the weakness may have been a temporary factor tied to delayed budget execution.

Expanded sales of new products in the IoT solutions segment also appear to be contributing to revenue diversification. This may be interpreted as a signal that the single-quarter weakness was not necessarily structural damage.

Overseas Opportunity from US NDAA Restrictions

Amid continued NDAA-driven restrictions on Chinese security equipment in the United States, Truen is understood to be pursuing entry into overseas public markets through Ambarella SoC-based new products and AI algorithm development capabilities.

This is assessed as a relative opportunity factor for non-Chinese camera makers. However, the timing and scale of actual overseas revenue contribution have not yet been confirmed.

Bear factors

Earnings Volatility Tied to Public Budget Execution Timing

As shown by the sharp Q1 2026 contraction in revenue and operating profit relative to the prior quarter, quarterly results can vary significantly depending on the timing of public-sector budget execution. This seasonality reduces predictability. If weakness in a particular quarter recurs, it could also affect the annual earnings trend.

Intensifying Price Competition from Chinese Products

Recent disclosures have also confirmed that price competition from Chinese products is pressuring the profitability of the domestic IP camera segment. If low-price competition persists, defending operating margins may become more difficult. This is a structural risk that directly affects the IP camera segment, the company's core revenue source.

Revenue Base Concentrated in Region and Customer Type

With a substantial portion of revenue dependent on domestic public-sector procurement, results can be sensitive to changes in government budget policy or procurement systems.

Overseas revenue contribution is understood to remain relatively small, meaning this concentrated structure could persist until overseas expansion gains more traction. High dependence on a specific customer segment remains a challenge from a diversification standpoint.

Risk factors

Policy and Budget Risk

Changes in the timing of public institution budget planning and execution, as well as reforms to procurement systems, can directly affect results. Safety and security-related budget allocations could also change depending on shifts in government policy priorities. These policy variables are external factors that the company cannot directly control.

Intensifying Competition Risk

Simultaneous price competition from low-cost Chinese products and competition with large domestic security firms could place dual pressure on market share and margins. The possibility of a reshaped competitive landscape due to new entrants or technological changes cannot be ruled out. This is a factor that could affect the profitability structure over the medium to long term.

Overseas Expansion Regulatory and Tariff Risk

Variables that are difficult to control exist in the overseas expansion process, including the finalization of detailed NDAA provisions, changes in tariff policy, and local certification procedures. If policy direction does not remain favorable, expected overseas opportunities could be delayed or reduced.

Given that the overseas revenue base is still relatively small, the impact of these variables could be felt more acutely.

Overall view

Truen has sustained growth from 2022 to 2024 on the back of a significant position in Korea's public video surveillance procurement market, but experienced an earnings slowdown in 2025 and Q1 2026, followed by a pronounced rebound in Q2, reflecting a highly volatile trend.

The key drivers of this volatility are identified as the timing of public institution budget execution and price competition from Chinese products.

On the overseas front, assessments suggest that US NDAA-driven restrictions on Chinese equipment could serve as a relative opportunity for non-Chinese makers, though the timing and scale of any actual overseas revenue expansion remain uncertain.

On valuation, past brokerage research has assessed the stock as trading at a discount to industry-average multiples, but this was a point-in-time assessment that should be considered alongside subsequent changes in earnings trends.
