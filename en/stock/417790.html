Truen: Quarterly Recovery Tested Against Public-Sector Dependence

02

Business structure

Truen was founded in 2005 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2023 as a specialist in digital video surveillance systems and IoT solutions. It researches, develops, manufactures and sells IP cameras, IoT devices and solutions, and video streaming solutions, and it launched the industry's first H.264 IP camera.

Its competitive edge is firmware features and security certifications tailored to public institutions and the system integration (SI) market.

Its proprietary AI technology is built into IP cameras and its Zoom Block products, and R&D focuses on AI-based intelligent video analytics such as object recognition, tracking and incident detection. Segment mix varies by reporting period.

In the third-quarter 2023 business report, IP cameras accounted for 83.3%, video streaming solutions 8.1%, IoT solutions 5.5%, and products and others 3.1%. Reporting based on the first quarter of 2025 showed an IoT solutions share of 27.5%, so classification scope differences should be considered.

The same reporting said IoT solutions revenue rose 209.5% year on year to KRW 2.7 billion in the first quarter of 2025. Overseas, the on-device AI camera TA series has been supplied as samples to several countries.

Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek are listed in the same electronics and components sector, and Hanwha Vision is listed among video equipment companies, but their business structures differ and they are not direct comparison benchmarks.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 38.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 47.2 billion in 2023 (up 21.5% year on year) and KRW 53.6 billion in 2024 (up 13.6%), then fell to KRW 49.1 billion in 2025 (down 8.4%). 2024 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025 and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit grew from KRW 9.01 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.4 billion in 2023 (up 26.4%) and KRW 15.5 billion in 2024 (up 35.7%), then fell to KRW 13.3 billion in 2025 (down 14.2%).

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7.63 billion in 2022, KRW 11 billion in 2023 (up 43.6%), KRW 14.4 billion in 2024 (up 31.0%), and KRW 12.1 billion in 2025 (down 15.8%). The operating margin went from 23.2% in 2022 to 24.1% in 2023 and 28.8% in 2024, then eased to 27.0% in 2025.

In 2025, operating profit fell faster (14.2%) than revenue (8.4%), so the margin was below 2024. In the second quarter of 2025, a passive government budget execution was cited as a drag on revenue.

Operating cash flow was KRW 8.57 billion in 2022, KRW 11.6 billion in 2023, KRW 16.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 12.8 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 11.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 13.2 billion in the fourth quarter (up 16.8% from the prior quarter), KRW 7.82 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 41.0%), KRW 16.7 billion in the second quarter (up 113.2%), and KRW 10.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026 (down 37.1%).

Operating profit was KRW 4.45 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (up 165.3% from the prior quarter) and KRW 2.93 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 18.1% year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (fourth quarter 2025 to third quarter 2026) totaled KRW 11.5 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Truen's core demand comes from public-sector video surveillance. A November 2023 equity research report attributed solid IP camera demand to rising needs to monitor abnormal behavior amid higher crime. Public-sector demand is sensitive to the pace of budget execution.

Eugene Investment & Securities assessed that revenue dipped slightly in the second quarter of 2025 because of passive government budget execution. Standalone revenue for the first quarter of 2026 fell 21.0% year on year, which shows the public-sector slowdown in the numbers.

Overseas, the company said in its August 2026 results release that demand to replace Chinese-made products is strong. It attributed second-quarter growth to high-end products, software and overseas expansion.

On the cycle, annual revenue peaked at KRW 53.6 billion in 2024 and fell to KRW 49.1 billion in 2025, while quarterly revenue swung from KRW 7.82 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 16.7 billion in the second.

On competition, the differentiators are firmware tailored to the public SI market, security certifications and AI-based video analytics. Compared with video equipment peers such as Hanwha Vision, Truen stands out as an IP camera specialist.

Risk factors

Public budget and policy risk

Overseas growth relies on demand to replace Chinese-made products. Whether that demand lasts, and how export-country rules or trade conditions might shift, remains open. Whether TA series samples convert into volume revenue is also a point to watch.

Quarterly volatility and small-cap characteristics

Quarterly revenue in 2026 moved from KRW 7.82 billion to KRW 16.7 billion. Large swings in earnings make multiple interpretation unstable. As a small-cap stock, the share price can move sharply depending on trading conditions.

Overall view
