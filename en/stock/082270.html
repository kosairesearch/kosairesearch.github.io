02

Business structure

GemVax & KAEL runs two businesses: contamination-control components used in semiconductor and display manufacturing, and peptide-based drug development. The company was founded in 1998, reorganized around its bio business, and listed on KOSDAQ in June 2005.

Its main products are contamination-control parts for semiconductor and display applications, and reports indicated that this business's sales grew at an average annual rate of 22% from 2010 to 2013. The company attributed the growth in 2025 mainly to a sharp expansion in semiconductor filter sales.

It says continuous R&D has raised its filter technology and emphasizes its distinctive competitiveness in the field. The drug business centers on GV1001, a peptide-based candidate pursued for neurodegenerative diseases including Alzheimer's disease and progressive supranuclear palsy (PSP).

GV1001 is a 16-amino-acid peptide containing the hTERT sequence, which the company says works through multiple mechanisms as several telomerase functions are expressed at once.

Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety designated GV1001's PSP indication as a developmental-stage orphan drug, and a conditional approval application becomes possible if efficacy is confirmed in a domestic Phase 2 trial.

At year-end 2025, consolidated total assets were KRW 158.9 billion and total liabilities were KRW 101.6 billion, so funding allocation across the two businesses is tied to the financial burden.

Earnings analysis

Revenue rose 30.1% to KRW 81.5 billion in 2025, the largest year in the period.

Operating losses widened from KRW 3.95 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.87 billion in 2023 and KRW 38.3 billion in 2024, before 2025 posted operating profit of KRW 3.79 billion, a turnaround to profit.

The operating margin moved from -5.2% in 2022 and -10.9% in 2023 to -61.2% in 2024, when falling sales and a larger operating loss coincided, and reached 4.7% in 2025.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened from KRW 19.6 billion in 2022 and KRW 27.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 87.2 billion in 2024, then narrowed to KRW 11.3 billion in 2025. Net loss including non-controlling interests was also KRW 11.3 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The semiconductor filter business that lifted the company's results moves with the chip cycle. The company attributed 2025 growth to an unusual boom in the semiconductor industry, which drove a sharp rise in demand for filters as essential process consumables.

It said the semiconductor sector is in an unprecedented supercycle and expects its earnings momentum to continue. Filter demand can still shift with chip capital spending cycles, so the operating swings in 2026 despite higher quarterly revenue from Q2 onward call for watching both the cycle and the cost structure.

The drug side follows a different dynamic. Reports say global competition to develop PSP treatments has moved into late-stage clinical trials, so the differentiation of clinical data matters in a crowded field.

Compared with listed bio and pharma peers that have neurodegenerative pipelines, GemVax & KAEL's distinguishing feature is that its filter sales, not drug revenue, lead its results.

Because the bio sector is sensitive to clinical pace and partnering, how much cash the filter business can fund for drug development is a key yardstick for the company's position within the sector.

Bull factors

Steep recovery in semiconductor filter sales

Revenue rose 30.1% to KRW 81.5 billion in 2025, which the company attributed to higher semiconductor filter sales and lower selling and administrative costs. Revenue rose for two consecutive quarters from Q2 to Q3 2026, and Q3 revenue was up 30.2% year on year. If the company's read on the semiconductor boom holds, filter demand could keep supporting the revenue base.

Operating profit turned positive

The operating margin moved from -61.2% in 2024 to 4.7% in 2025, with operating profit of KRW 3.79 billion marking a turn to profit. Q4 2025 operating profit rose 923.0% from the prior quarter to KRW 7.52 billion.

Q2 2026 also posted operating profit of KRW 997 million, which shows some improvement in the operating earnings structure.

Orphan designation opens a drug pathway

The PSP indication for GV1001 was designated a developmental-stage orphan drug by Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety. With that designation, a conditional approval application becomes possible if efficacy is confirmed in a domestic Phase 2 trial. Related reports said patient enrollment in the PSP Phase 2 study had reached 66 patients, or 88%.
