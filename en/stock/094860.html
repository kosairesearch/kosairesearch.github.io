Neorigin: Narrowing Losses as Revenue Declines

Neorigin posted operating losses in each of the four years from 2022 to 2025, and its Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 3.48 billion was the lowest of the last five quarters, while the operating loss narrowed to KRW 956 million.

02

Business structure

Neorigin was listed on KOSDAQ in April 2007 and operates as a software development and supply company. After a change in its largest shareholder in 2020, it renamed itself Neorigin and started a game business as a new line of business.

In 2023 it spun off and sold its security business division, and in 2024 it absorbed a subsidiary through a merger. The game business is now the center of the company, and the Whiteout-style global operation of its game titles is described by WiseReport as the source of revenue.

The company attributed the 2025 revenue decline to weakness in its short-form content business and to the classification of a discontinued business. The game business appears to be taking over the revenue role previously played by the security and short-form businesses.

Competition in games has intensified, and the need to attract users has grown, which WiseReport cites as a factor behind the weaker results. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 23.7 billion, of which equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 22.7 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 16.2 billion.

Compared with large internet platform peers in the same sector such as NAVER and NHN, Neorigin has a narrower business scope and a higher share of game revenue.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 36.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 25.5 billion in 2023, a 30.5% decline year on year, recovered to KRW 35.1 billion in 2024, an increase of 37.6%, and then dropped to KRW 31 billion in 2025, down 11.6%. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2023 was the smallest.

The operating loss was KRW 2.37 billion in 2022, KRW 2.59 billion in 2023 and KRW 5.84 billion in 2024, widening each year until 2024, before narrowing to KRW 4.61 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from -6.5% in 2022 to -16.6% in 2024 and improved to -14.9% in 2025.

The company has reported losses for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.42 billion in 2022, KRW 6.3 billion in 2023, KRW 5.27 billion in 2024 and KRW 11.1 billion in 2025.

The company cited weakness in its short-form content business and the classification of a discontinued business as the main reasons for the change in revenue and profit structure in 2025.

Operating cash flow was negative in every year, at KRW -2.08 billion in 2022, KRW -4.21 billion in 2023, KRW -3.01 billion in 2024 and KRW -4.44 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue peaked at KRW 7.89 billion in Q4 2025, from KRW 6.17 billion in Q3 2025, and then declined to KRW 6.68 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 4.02 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 3.48 billion in Q3 2026. The operating loss was smallest in Q4 2025 at KRW 176 million and largest in Q2 2026 at KRW 1.92 billion.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders in Q1 2026 was positive at KRW 2.97 billion while the operating loss was KRW 1.07 billion, a divergence that the disclosed figures suggest came from non-operating items.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 8.44 billion. According to WiseReport as of September 5, 2026, first-half 2026 revenue fell 34.0% year on year, the operating loss widened 85.1%, and the net loss narrowed 88.9%.

05

Industry analysis

Neorigin's revenue comes from a game business that competes in a market where new releases and live-operated titles vie for the same players. WiseReport cites intensifying competition and a greater need to attract users as factors behind the recent weakness in results.

This points to an environment in which the cost of acquiring new users and retaining existing ones is rising together. The company itself identified weakness in its short-form content business as the cause of the 2025 revenue decline.

Whether short-form content competes with game playing time cannot be confirmed from the disclosures alone.

Among peers in the same sector, NAVER and NHN operate across search, commerce, advertising and games with a broad business scope, whereas Neorigin's higher share of game revenue tends to produce larger swings in its results.

Market-wide data on the position of the industry cycle is limited, so this analysis centers on the company's disclosed trends. On the sell-side side, a Hankyung market page shows no consensus estimate for the company, which limits comparison of external forecasts with reported figures.

Competitive intensity and user retention costs appear to be the main variables that determine the direction of quarterly revenue.

Risk factors

Concentration risk

Revenue appears to depend heavily on the game business, and the company itself cited weakness in its short-form content business and the classification of a discontinued business as causes of the 2025 revenue decline.

User attrition or intensifying competition in one business unit can feed directly into total revenue, so revenue volatility is likely to remain high until the results of new titles or operating strategies are visible.

Financial and liquidity risk

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 23.7 billion and total liabilities were KRW 16.2 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 24.1% in 2022 to 68.3% in 2025. With operating cash flow negative for four years, a larger debt burden could limit financing options depending on funding conditions.

Trading and regulatory risk

A designation as an investment-caution stock due to excessive trading was disclosed on February 3, 2026. With no consensus earnings estimate available, checking results against external forecasts is also difficult. As a small-cap stock, the share price can react strongly to a single disclosure.
