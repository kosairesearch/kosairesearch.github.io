02

Business structure

SK hynix is a memory chipmaker whose core products are DRAM and NAND flash. In DRAM, high-bandwidth memory (HBM) used in AI accelerators is the product driving growth.

In an October 2026 press report, the company said HBM4 12-high products have entered mass production and are shipping on schedules agreed with customers, with yields described as stable. The company also cited a sharp rise in server DDR5 sales as a driver of results.

It expects HBM4 to surpass HBM3E in both shipment volume and revenue from the fourth quarter of 2026. In NAND, supply of 321-layer TLC-based enterprise SSDs (eSSD) is expanding.

Its major customers include 14 companies, among them global cloud service providers, and it has signed long-term supply agreements (LTA) with them. The company said contracted volumes account for more than 50% of total production capacity in 2027 and 2028.

Among rivals, Samsung Electronics has been reported to pursue a turnkey strategy covering design, memory production, foundry and packaging for HBM4, and Micron also competes in HBM supply. Icheon M16 and Cheongju M15X are cited as the core HBM4 production sites.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on disclosed results, SK hynix has moved past its 2023 trough and recovered quickly. Annual sales were KRW 44.6 trillion in 2022 and KRW 32.8 trillion in 2023, a 26.6% decline from the prior year.

They then rose to KRW 66.2 trillion in 2024, up 102.0%, and to KRW 97.1 trillion in 2025, up 46.8%, with sales growing for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 7.73 trillion in 2023 to operating profit of KRW 23.5 trillion in 2024, then rose 101.2% to KRW 47.2 trillion in 2025. The operating margin was 15.3% in 2022, -23.6% in 2023, 35.5% in 2024 and 48.6% in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners swung from a net loss of KRW 9.11 trillion in 2023 to KRW 19.8 trillion in 2024, then rose 116.9% to KRW 42.9 trillion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests is a different figure, at KRW 42.9 trillion in 2025.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 4.28 trillion in 2023 to KRW 53.4 trillion in 2025, and the debt ratio fell from 87.5% to 45.9% over the same span. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 120.7 trillion.

In quarterly results, sales and operating profit rose quarter over quarter for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. Sales were KRW 52.6 trillion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 79.3 trillion in the second and KRW 104.6 trillion in the third.

Operating profit reached KRW 37.6 trillion, KRW 60.5 trillion and KRW 83.3 trillion across the same quarters. Profit attributable to owners peaked at KRW 93.8 trillion in the second quarter, then fell to KRW 39.5 trillion in the third.

The reason operating profit and owner-attributable profit diverged in the third quarter is not visible in the summary table and should be checked in the quarterly notes. Across the latest four quarters, profit attributable to owners totaled KRW 188.9 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

The memory industry sits in a cycle that moved from losses in 2023 to recovering results in 2024 and 2025. The company expects an environment where AI demand outpaces supply to persist through 2030.

It says advanced-node process complexity and the long lead time to build new facilities make it hard for supply growth to keep pace with demand. UBS expects SK hynix to reach a share of about 70% in HBM4 for NVIDIA's next-generation Rubin platform in 2026.

Research and brokerage analysis cited by the company expects HBM3E to account for about two-thirds of total HBM shipments in 2026, with HBM4 gaining share gradually. Samsung Electronics has been reported to pursue a turnkey approach covering design through packaging in-house.

Shinhan Investment Corp. analyzed in a May 28, 2026 report that rising general-purpose DRAM prices could lead to higher HBM prices. On the other side, the company says high-value HBM products require more complex processes and more resources, and bit growth through miniaturization is increasingly constrained.

That means capex increases do not translate directly into equal output gains, which is the company's response to past oversupply concerns. The key industry questions are whether HBM4 yields and capacity expansion speed align with the direction of general DRAM prices.

06

Outlook

The starting point for the outlook is HBM4, based on what the company has disclosed. The company said HBM4 will surpass HBM3E in both shipment volume and revenue from the fourth quarter of 2026. It said HBM4 yields are running above plan and that back-end yields exceed plan on a whole-process basis.

The company plans to raise Cheongju M15X capacity to its maximum level by year-end to meet 2027 demand. It said volume and price negotiations for 2027 supply with major customers have been completed. It also said customer HBM demand is more than twice the volume it can supply.

The company expects 2026 capex to rise substantially from 2025 but says it will keep capex discipline tied to demand visibility and investment efficiency.

Construction schedules for the Yongin cluster and new fabs such as M17 are being brought forward, while equipment inflows and volume ramp-ups will be flexibly adjusted to customer demand. The company said it will review customer financial health and supply chain readiness to manage overinvestment risk.

The next items to verify are how much HBM4 raises its share of revenue and how quickly capex translates into output.

08

Bull factors

Wider demand visibility from long-term contracts

The company said it has long-term supply agreements with 14 customers, and that contracted volumes represent more than 50% of total capacity in 2027 and 2028. It also said nearly all HBM output for the next three years has found buyers.

Hana Securities stated in a June 2026 report that such contract-backed earnings could justify a premium multiple over general memory.

HBM4 yield and product mix improvement

The company said HBM4 12-high products are in mass production and shipping on agreed schedules, with back-end yields above plan. It expects HBM4 to surpass HBM3E in shipments and revenue from the fourth quarter of 2026. A shift toward higher-value products is the basis for the margin improvement case.

Expected persistence of memory supply imbalance

The company expects demand for AI to exceed supply through 2030. Shinhan Investment Corp. said in a May 2026 report that diversification of data center products could deepen the memory supply imbalance. The bull case rests on pricing power persisting while supply growth stays limited.

09

Bear factors

Concern over AI investment overheating

Concerns about AI investment overheating have been raised in the market. Memory demand depends heavily on customers' AI infrastructure spending, so a slowdown could challenge HBM volume assumptions. The company has expressed confidence in medium- to long-term demand, but customer investment plans are what will test it.

Overinvestment and a possible return of oversupply

The company acknowledged concerns about a return of past oversupply and emphasized phased investment. It is bringing forward construction for the Yongin cluster and M17, which enlarges the investment base.

The 2023 results, with an operating loss of KRW 7.73 trillion and a net loss of KRW 9.11 trillion attributable to owners, illustrate how large losses can get in an oversupply phase.

Divergent quarterly profit trends and specification changes

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose 37.6% from the prior quarter, but profit attributable to owners fell 57.9%. Reports say some customers are considering lowering HBM stack height from 12-high to 8-high. Samsung Electronics is competing with a turnkey strategy, leaving share defense as an ongoing burden.

10

Risk factors

Price cycle

Memory earnings depend heavily on prices. The company posted an operating loss of KRW 7.73 trillion in 2023, and the recovery in 2024 and 2025 was driven by price increases. A turn in general DRAM prices could raise earnings volatility even with HBM-centered contracts.

Overinvestment and technology risk

Advanced-node complexity means yields and reliability could limit the pace of production expansion. New facilities take time to build, and if capex does not translate into output, investment efficiency weakens. The company said it will adjust equipment inflows and volume ramps to demand.

Competition and customer specification changes

Samsung Electronics is pursuing a turnkey approach to supply HBM4, and Micron is also competing in HBM. If customers lower HBM stack heights, product specifications and demand structure could shift, and the company says higher capacity per DRAM die can offset lost volume.

12

Overall view

SK hynix's annual sales rose from the 2023 trough to KRW 97.1 trillion in 2025, and its operating margin reached 48.6% that year. Across the 2026 quarters, sales and operating profit rose for four consecutive quarters, with third-quarter operating profit at KRW 83.3 trillion.

Profit attributable to owners, however, fell 57.9% from the prior quarter, diverging from the operating trend. The company says its HBM4 ramp and long-term agreements have widened visibility into 2027 and 2028 volumes. Broker target prices diverge widely, reflecting assumptions at the time of publication.

Bull factors are demand visibility and yield progress, while bear factors are AI investment overheating, the possibility of renewed overinvestment, and the diverging quarterly profit trend.

The next checkpoints are the M15X capacity target at end-December 2026, fourth-quarter 2026 results in January 2027, and the 2026 annual report due by March 31, 2027.
