02

Business structure

Rorze Systems makes automated transfer equipment that moves and stocks wafers and panels on semiconductor and display production lines. On the semiconductor side, its core products are logistics automation equipment such as mobile robots, EFEM and load ports.

On the display side, it supplies indexers, sorters, stockers and module equipment. Its laser glass cutting machine is introduced as a product the company developed first in the world and now mass-produces.

Hyundai Motor Securities analysts put the 2023 revenue mix at 58% semiconductor automated logistics, 22% display automated logistics and module back-end equipment, and 5% glass cutting machines. The same report listed the largest shareholder, Rorze International, at 40.1%.

A May 2023 research report described Rorze Systems' semiconductor equipment customers as mainly Samsung Electronics and overseas companies. On the display side, a manufacturing equipment supply contract with Samsung Display, disclosed in September 2024, stands out as a representative case.

Because orders and revenue move with customers' facility investment schedules, a investment adjustment by one major customer can flow directly into results.

A first-half 2026 earnings comment attributed revenue growth to semiconductor equipment demand from more advanced and larger manufacturing processes and to new display line and retrofit investment.

Competitive position depends on whether customers bring equipment in-house, and shifts to other vendors could reduce orders.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 144.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 105.3 billion in 2023, down 27.2%, then rose to KRW 160.1 billion in 2024, up 52.1%, the largest year in 2022 to 2025. Revenue fell again to KRW 124.9 billion in 2025, down 22.0%, and 2023 was the smallest year in that span.

Operating profit was largest in 2022 at KRW 26.4 billion and smallest in 2023 at KRW 11.9 billion, down 54.9%. It recovered to KRW 19.8 billion in 2024, up 65.8%, before falling to KRW 14.4 billion in 2025, down 27.1%.

The operating margin moved from 18.3% in 2022 to 11.3% in 2023, then 12.4% in 2024 and 11.6% in 2025, staying in the 11 to 12 percent range. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was largest in 2022 at KRW 20.3 billion and smallest in 2023 at KRW 8.87 billion, down 56.2%.

After the 2023 low, it rose to KRW 12.1 billion in 2024, up 36.6%, and to KRW 12.3 billion in 2025, up 1.2%, two consecutive years of growth. Net income was disclosed at the same amounts as net income attributable to controlling shareholders in each year, so the two annual figures match.

On a quarterly basis, revenue of KRW 16.9 billion in 3Q 2025 was the lowest of the last five quarters, followed by KRW 41.5 billion in 4Q 2025, up 145.0% from the prior quarter.

Revenue dipped 3.4% to KRW 40 billion in 1Q 2026, then rose 17.1% to KRW 46.9 billion in 2Q and 9.6% to KRW 51.4 billion in 3Q, two consecutive quarters of growth.

Operating profit has grown for four consecutive quarters from 4Q 2025 through 3Q 2026, with 3Q 2026 operating profit up 19.1% quarter on quarter, faster than revenue. The first-half 2026 earnings comment points to equipment demand and display line investment as the backdrop. The specific cause of the sharp revenue increase in 4Q 2025 is a point to check in the quarterly detail.

05

Industry analysis

Rorze Systems' end markets are facility investment by semiconductor manufacturing lines and display panel lines. The first-half 2026 earnings comment pointed to higher equipment demand from more advanced and larger semiconductor manufacturing processes.

The same comment also noted new display panel lines and retrofit investment in existing lines. In 2023, a research report expected earnings to decline on a weak end-market outlook, and revenue indeed fell 27.2% that year.

The same report judged that a sharp drop in orders was limited, since semiconductor equipment customers were mainly Samsung Electronics and overseas companies.

On the display side, a manufacturing equipment supply contract with Samsung Display, disclosed in September 2024, showed the effect of large-customer investment.

Glass-substrate equipment, cited as a new business, was described in an October 2026 news report as dependent on orders, customer acceptance and new equipment revenue.

On competition, the risk that customers bring equipment in-house or switch vendors is cited as a factor that could reduce orders, so changes in customer purchasing matter.

On cycle position, the 2026 recovery follows the 2025 annual decline, and whether facility investment recovery lasts several quarters is the next benchmark.

Fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results will be disclosed in the annual report, so the annual trend will be confirmed in a report due around mid-March 2027.

Customers' facility investment plans change quarter to quarter, so order flow is updated in each quarterly report. Whether quarterly results after 4Q 2026 continue will become visible in the first-quarter 2027 report, due around mid-May 2027.

Bull factors

Consecutive quarterly profit growth

Operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders have grown for four consecutive quarters from 4Q 2025 through 3Q 2026.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit reached KRW 8.95 billion, up 339.6% year on year, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 7.7 billion, up 447.4%. The latest four quarters total KRW 20.1 billion, above the KRW 12.3 billion for full-year 2025.

Demand across two end markets

Because Rorze Systems supplies both semiconductor and display lines, a slowdown in one area may be partly offset by the other. A first-half 2026 earnings comment attributed higher revenue and operating profit to semiconductor equipment demand from advanced processes and to new display line and retrofit investment. This demand has translated into higher quarterly revenue, underpinning the profit increase.

Low-debt financial capacity

Total liabilities were KRW 24.6 billion and total equity was KRW 137.8 billion at the end of 2025. A news report put first-half 2026 net cash at roughly KRW 52.5 billion. A balance sheet with limited borrowing can absorb production and working capital needs when orders increase.

Bear factors

Sensitivity to the investment cycle

Revenue fell 27.2% in 2023 and operating profit fell 54.9%. A May 2023 research report also judged that earnings would decline on a weaker end-market. Because orders and revenue fall together when customer investment slows, a recovery built on a few quarters of profit leaves results exposed to volatility.

Lingering full-year 2025 decline

Full-year 2025 revenue fell 22.0% to KRW 124.9 billion and operating profit fell 27.1% to KRW 14.4 billion. Whether the annual recovery holds will be confirmed only when the 2026 annual report is filed. Revenue also dipped 3.4% quarter on quarter in 1Q 2026, so the recovery did not move in a straight line.

Delayed validation of new businesses

As of an October 2026 news report, glass-substrate equipment depended on orders, customer acceptance and revenue recognition. Until an official order is announced, the expected benefit cannot yet be seen in reported results. If announcements are delayed, the new-business expectation could remain separate from earnings.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and in-house equipment

Semiconductor customers are mainly Samsung Electronics and overseas companies, so investment changes at a single customer feed directly into orders. If customers produce some equipment in-house or switch vendors, orders could decline. With a narrow customer base, quarterly order swings can be large.

Exchange rates and export exposure

Exports are reported to account for roughly half of sales, so won-dollar exchange rate moves affect won-converted revenue. The same order can produce different reported results when the exchange rate shifts. Exchange rates are an external factor outside the company's control.

Timing of glass-substrate mass production

Glass-substrate equipment is cited as a growth driver, but official orders and mass-production timing remain uncertain. If production starts later than expected, the expected revenue contribution would also shift later. Until new businesses contribute, the strength of the semiconductor and display recovery will determine results.

Overall view

Rorze Systems' annual revenue and operating profit fell in 2025, but operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders have risen for four consecutive quarters from 4Q 2025 through 3Q 2026, showing a clear profit recovery. The larger revenue base underpins the quarterly profit increase.

Semiconductor equipment demand and display line expansion and retrofit investment are cited as the backdrop to the recovery. Results remain sensitive to customers' investment cycles, and in 2023 a downturn cut revenue and operating profit sharply.

Glass-substrate equipment, cited as a new business, has not yet been backed by an official order announcement, and in-house equipment at customers is a medium-term variable. The balance sheet is relatively light, with total liabilities of KRW 24.6 billion and total equity of KRW 137.8 billion at the end of 2025.

On valuation, the share price sits below book value per share, and the pace of earnings recovery should be considered alongside it. The next checkpoints are the annual operating margin in the 2026 annual report, first-quarter 2027 results, and whether a glass-substrate order is disclosed.
