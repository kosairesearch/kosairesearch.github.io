02

Business structure

YW Company, founded in 1995 and headquartered in Seongnam-si, rents out office automation equipment, home appliances, medical devices, industrial equipment, smart unmanned stores and automated robots. The company runs three segments: Rental, Corporate Finance and Others.

The Corporate Finance segment uses funds to buy corporate bonds and provide loans.

In MarketScreener's 2025 full-year breakdown, the wholesale and retail line was about KRW 12.63 billion, Others about KRW 3.04 billion and Corporate Finance about KRW 0.87 billion, and the three together match 2025 revenue of KRW 16.5 billion.

Most of the 2023 revenue of KRW 37 billion came from the same wholesale and retail line, about KRW 32.4 billion, and in 2024 that line fell to about KRW 12.5 billion, pulling total revenue down to KRW 16.5 billion.

Revenue scale therefore depends mainly on the Rental segment, and the swings in 2023 and 2024 came from there. Others revenue stayed near KRW 3 billion from 2023 to 2025, while Corporate Finance revenue fell from about KRW 1.2 billion in 2023 to about KRW 0.87 billion in 2025.

Listed rental peers include Coway and LOTTE rental, but both operate on a much larger scale and with broader businesses. On a sector comparison as of September 22, 2026, the operating margin ranked first among 17 listed distributors on an annual basis, according to Alpha Square.

A share buyback announced in June 2023 for KRW 2.2 billion expired at the end of December 2024, having acquired 581,356 shares, or 6.93% of shares outstanding.

Earnings analysis

Based on reported results, annual revenue rose from 203 eok won (1 eok = 100 million won) in 2022 to 370 eok won in 2023 (up 82.3% YoY), then fell to 165 eok won in 2024 (down 55.3% YoY), and was unchanged at 165 eok won in 2025.

Of 2022-2025, the year with the highest revenue was 2023 and the year with the lowest was 2024. Operating profit was 30.6 eok won in 2022, 64.8 eok won in 2023 (up 111.9% YoY), 54.4 eok won in 2024 (down 16.1% YoY), and 57.8 eok won in 2025 (up 6.3% YoY).

Operating profit held at 54.4 eok won in 2024 even though revenue fell by more than half, because the margin structure changed. The operating margin rose from 15.1% in 2022 and 17.5% in 2023 to 32.9% in 2024 and 34.9% in 2025.

Net income attributable to owners of the parent was 30.3 eok won in 2022, 53.4 eok won in 2023 (up 76.3% YoY), 42.7 eok won in 2024 (down 20.1% YoY), and 50.8 eok won in 2025 (up 19.0% YoY), and net income was disclosed for the same years.

Operating cash flow was an outflow of -76.5 eok won in 2022 and -40.1 eok won in 2023, then turned positive at 64.4 eok won in 2024 and 173 eok won in 2025, far exceeding net income in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue of 27.1 eok won and operating profit of 15.9 eok won were the highest levels in this period. Q4 2025 revenue was 32.9 eok won (up 21.1% QoQ) and Q1 2026 revenue was 35.5 eok won (up 7.9% QoQ), but operating profit fell from 14.5 eok won in Q4 to 9.9 eok won in Q1, hitting a low.

Operating profit rose for two consecutive quarters, to 12 eok won in Q2 2026 and 13.3 eok won in Q3. Q3 2026 revenue was 44.4 eok won, up 63.6% YoY, but operating profit fell 16.6% and net income attributable to owners of the parent was 10.6 eok won, down 40.4%.

Because the operating margin fell during a period of rising revenue, how the profitability of the additional revenue shows up in the next quarter matters.

The sum of net income attributable to owners of the parent over the latest four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) is 41.5 eok won, which is less than the 50.8 eok won of full-year 2025.

Industry analysis

Rental businesses hold equipment and recover their investment through usage fees, so revenue builds with the cumulative contract base. YW pairs this with a corporate finance segment.

While wholesale and retail revenue fell from about KRW 32.4 billion in 2023 to about KRW 12.5 billion in 2024, Others revenue stayed near KRW 3 billion. This shows that the swings came from specific transaction structures within the Rental segment.

Coway and LOTTE rental are listed rental companies, but they are far larger and cover different product lines, so direct comparison has limits. Even so, YW ranked first on operating margin in the sector comparison, which points to a clearly different profitability structure within the same distributor group.

Its market capitalization ranked ninth among 17 companies in the sector. The company is therefore small in scale but high in margin. Data on demand and cycle position for the wider sector was limited, so this analysis relies mainly on company disclosures.

Bear factors

Revenue volatility and concentration

Revenue moved from KRW 20.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 37 billion in 2023, then fell to KRW 16.5 billion in 2024 and held at KRW 16.5 billion in 2025. The 2023 spike and 2024 drop were concentrated in the wholesale and retail line, about KRW 32.4 billion and KRW 12.5 billion. Heavy reliance on one line makes the revenue base easy to disrupt.

Revenue growth without profit growth

Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 63.6% from a year earlier, but operating profit fell 16.6% and profit attributable to owners fell 40.4%. The operating margin narrowed, so it is not yet clear that added revenue translates into profit.

Profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters totals KRW 4.15 billion, below KRW 5.08 billion for full-year 2025.

Shrinking corporate finance segment

Corporate Finance segment revenue fell from about KRW 1.2 billion in 2023 to about KRW 0.87 billion in 2025. The segment deploys funds through corporate bond purchases and loans, so its size can move with interest rates and counterparty conditions. It is the smallest of the three segments and is moving lower.

Risk factors

Concentration risk

Revenue is concentrated in the Rental segment and the wholesale and retail line. The 2023 and 2024 swings originated there, so a change in specific contracts or transaction structures could move total revenue sharply. The degree of diversification should be checked against segment disclosures in the annual report.

Margin compression risk

The operating margin was high at 32.9% in 2024 and 34.9% in 2025, but in the third quarter of 2026 revenue rose while operating profit fell, which narrowed the margin. Whether the high-margin structure holds depends on quarterly trends.

Operating profit fell 31.7% from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the first quarter of 2026, reaching the period low.

Small-cap liquidity risk

The free float is about 36.72%, according to MarketScreener. The company is small in market capitalization and trading volume is limited, so the share price can move apart from the broader market and trades may not execute smoothly. Price discovery can weaken when price swings widen.
