Quartz Recovery Lifts Operating Profit; Net Conversion Lags

Operating profit rose for four consecutive quarters from 4Q 2025 through 3Q 2026, yet 3Q 2026 net income attributable to controlling shareholders was about 36% of operating profit, making profit conversion the next checkpoint.

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Wonik QNC was spun off from Wonik in 2003 and makes quartz and ceramic products for semiconductor manufacturing. Its business is organized into quartz, cleaning and coating, and ceramics.

According to the WiseReport company monitor, first-half 2026 revenue growth was driven by higher quartz and cleaning/coating sales as global chipmaker customers expanded AI-related investment and utilization rose. Ceramics also improved on stronger demand for semiconductor materials.

Overseas expansion came through acquisitions: the US quartz business of Momentive in 2020 and a Japanese quartz crucible maker in 2022, for 18 overseas subsidiaries in total.

Momentive Technologies Holding Company, which handles quartz raw materials, is held at 50% plus one share, so part of its profit is attributed to non-controlling interests. Brokerage research cites Samsung Electronics and other equipment and chipmaker customers placing strong orders.

Production is centered in Anseong, Korea and Taiwan, and BNK Investment & Securities said the Taiwan quartz unit's 2025 revenue grew 29% year on year. Quartz parts demand moves with replacement needs and with utilization and capacity investment.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 783.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 805.9 billion in 2023, KRW 891.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 943.6 billion in 2025, growing for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit moved from KRW 115.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 83 billion in 2023, KRW 90.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 59.6 billion in 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

The operating margin fell from 14.7% in 2022 to 6.3% in 2025, and the company attributed the 2025 declines in operating profit and net income mainly to weak end-markets at its US subsidiary Momentive Technologies Holding Company.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 53.8 billion in 2022, KRW 38.1 billion in 2023, KRW 50.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 22.5 billion in 2025.

Total net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 958 million in 2025, below the controlling-shareholder figure, because non-controlling interests absorbed a loss.

By quarter, operating profit went from KRW 8 billion in 3Q 2025 to KRW 18.4 billion in 4Q 2025 (up 130.5% quarter on quarter), then KRW 21.9 billion, KRW 30.9 billion and KRW 44.4 billion in 1Q to 3Q 2026, rising for four consecutive quarters.

Alphabiz reported operating margins of 8.5% in 1Q, 11.4% in 2Q and 15.7% in 3Q 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 16.5 billion in 1Q 2026 and KRW 22.7 billion in 2Q before falling to KRW 16.1 billion in 3Q, down 29.1% quarter on quarter, and the last four quarters total KRW 57.9 billion.

Alphabiz said the 3Q conversion rate was 36%, about half the 75% and 74% of the first two quarters, and estimated Momentive was loss-making after a KRW 900 million non-controlling loss.

Because the first half carried almost no tax charge and the 1Q to 3Q effective tax rate was about 20%, it remains to be confirmed whether this is one-off or a normalization.

05

Industry analysis

Semiconductor demand is being read as entering a recovery phase in 2026. Shinhan Investment & Securities said in a March 2026 report that improving IT demand had pushed the chip segment into an accelerating growth phase.

Kiwoom Securities, in a February 2026 report, said a recovery in Samsung Electronics' NAND competitiveness and utilization could lift estimates for Wonik QNC, linking the Pyeongtaek Line 4 expansion and higher NAND sales to a quartz recovery.

Yuanta Securities said in a March 2026 report that a major North American customer was asking parts suppliers to raise inventory more than twofold, and expected quartz share gains at Taiwan customers to continue. Kiwoom also pointed to higher NAND demand tied to NVIDIA's inference memory storage adoption.

On positioning, quartz raw materials are supplied through the subsidiary Momentive, so the group covers both raw materials and processing. In the same cycle, Momentive's operating profit fell in 2025 on weak US end-markets, and how much of the upturn reaches the subsidiary is still unclear. Yuanta also said Momentive returned to operating profit of KRW 5.1 billion in 4Q 2025.

06

Outlook

According to Alphabiz, the company expects strong head-office volumes to continue in 4Q 2026 but expects Momentive's results to be similar to or lower than in 3Q.

Brokers differ on capex: Shinhan Investment & Securities estimated roughly KRW 25 billion of 2026 capex, adding about KRW 45 billion of capacity with partial operation starting in 4Q, while BNK Investment & Securities put 2026 capex at about KRW 130 billion and said it is roughly routine ex the US cleaning plant.

For full-year 2026 operating profit, Shinhan estimated KRW 98.1 billion in March 2026 (up 65% year on year), and Yuanta estimated KRW 112.4 billion for 2026 in a March 2026 report. The first three quarters of 2026 total KRW 97.2 billion, close to Shinhan's full-year estimate.

BNK also noted that orders from key customers including Lam, TEL and Samsung Electronics remain strong. These estimates date from early 2026, so 4Q actuals could move them.

Bull factors

Utilization recovery and consecutive profit gains

Operating profit rose for four consecutive quarters to KRW 44.4 billion in 3Q 2026, and the operating margin climbed from 8.5% in 1Q to 15.7% in 3Q per Alphabiz. WiseReport linked the quartz and cleaning/coating sales gains to higher customer utilization and AI-related investment. Shinhan said utilization and capacity investment drive growth in quartz parts.

Customer inventory build and share gains

Yuanta Securities reported in March 2026 that a major North American customer was asking suppliers to double or more their inventory. The same report expected quartz share gains at Taiwan customers to continue over three years. Shinhan called 2026 a first year of growth on higher utilization and wider product range.

Subsidiary turnaround and capacity additions

Yuanta said Momentive returned to operating profit of KRW 5.1 billion in 4Q 2025. Shinhan expected roughly KRW 45 billion of added capacity in 2026 with partial operation from 4Q. The company said strong head-office volumes should continue into 4Q.

09

Bear factors

Lower conversion into controlling-shareholder profit

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 29.1% quarter on quarter to KRW 16.1 billion in 3Q 2026. Alphabiz said the conversion rate was 36%, about half the 75% and 74% of the first two quarters. With almost no first-half tax charge, a normalizing tax rate could weigh on net income.

Momentive volatility

DART disclosures attribute the 2025 declines in operating profit and net income mainly to weak end-markets at Momentive Technologies Holding Company. Alphabiz estimated Momentive stayed loss-making in 3Q 2026 after a KRW 900 million non-controlling loss. The company expects Momentive's 4Q 2026 results to be similar to or below 3Q.

Balance-sheet burden and interest costs

Total liabilities were KRW 975.3 billion at end-2025, and the debt ratio moved only from 171.2% in 2022 to 170.9% in 2025. Alphabiz flagged acquisition borrowing costs as a drag on net income. Operating cash flow, however, rose to KRW 84.8 billion in 2025 from KRW 64.6 billion in 2022.

10

Risk factors

Semiconductor capex cycle

Quartz parts demand moves with customer utilization and capex. In 2025, weak end-markets at a subsidiary pulled down operating profit. If customers adjust investment, head-office volume growth could slow.

Subsidiary and non-controlling interest structure

Momentive is a source of volatility in consolidated results. Because Wonik QNC holds 50% plus one share, part of its profit flows to non-controlling interests. If the subsidiary weakens, net income attributable to controlling shareholders can fall well below operating profit.

Tax rate and external conditions

With the 1Q to 3Q 2026 effective tax rate near 20%, it is unclear whether this is a normalization or a one-off. Overseas results are exposed to exchange-rate moves and revaluation gains and losses on foreign-currency items.

US-China export controls on chip equipment and materials are an external factor that could affect overseas customer volumes.

  2. During 1Q 2027

The prior annual report went through a board resolution on March 5, 2026. Confirmed tax rate and capex scale, where broker estimates of KRW 25 billion and KRW 130 billion diverge, should be checked.

12

Overall view

Wonik QNC's results track customer utilization across quartz, cleaning and coating, and ceramics. Operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 totaled KRW 97.2 billion, extending a four-quarter run of gains.

Over the same period, net income attributable to controlling shareholders was shaped by swings at Momentive, the split with non-controlling interests, and tax changes.

The 3Q conversion rate of 36% per Alphabiz is below the 75% and 74% of the first half, making conversion the central question. 2025 revenue of KRW 943.6 billion rose for a third straight year, but operating profit of KRW 59.6 billion was below KRW 115.1 billion in 2022.

Strengths include utilization recovery, customer inventory orders and capacity additions, while weaknesses include Momentive volatility, borrowing costs and tax normalization.

The next checkpoints are whether the 4Q 2026 capacity starts operating, 4Q 2026 preliminary results in 1Q 2027, and the 2026 annual report around March 2027. Any valuation read depends on how quickly profit attributable to controlling shareholders recovers.
