02

Business structure

Ecstelecomco was founded in 1999 as a provider of enterprise communication solutions and services and listed on KOSDAQ in 2007.

Its business is split into Customer Success and Digital Workplace, with the Customer Success side offering contact-center solutions such as IPCC, AICC and CCaaS alongside integrated communications like cloud telephony and WebEx calling.

By 2026 Q1 revenue, the mix was ETaaS 43%, Customer Success 30% and Digital Workplace 27%. ETaaS integrates client systems and develops AI chatbot and agent-support systems. A 2022 technical analysis described the company as a leading provider of integrated enterprise voice communication (CC/UC) solutions in Korea.

Because the largest segment accounts for only 43% of revenue, dependence on a single segment is relatively limited. As a system integrator, however, project build revenue and recurring operating revenue can arrive at different times, which shows up in quarterly results.

Compared with large SI players in the same IT and software sector, such as LG CNS and Samsung SDS, the company's voice and contact-center focus is a narrower scope. Demand from corporate contact centers is the common starting point across all three segments.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 100.6 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2023 to KRW 91.8 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2024 (down 8.8%) and KRW 73.3 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2025 (down 20.1%), then recovered to KRW 91.2 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026 (up 24.4%).

The fiscal year ended March 2023 was the largest year in the period and the fiscal year ended March 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 4.23 billion to KRW 1.48 billion (down 65.2%) in the fiscal year ended March 2024, turned into an operating loss of KRW 2.69 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2025, and returned to a KRW 299 million operating profit in the fiscal year ended March 2026, an operating margin of 0.3%.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.64 billion, KRW 2.63 billion, KRW 626 million and KRW 1.66 billion across the four fiscal years, with the fiscal year ended March 2025 the smallest and the fiscal year ended March 2026 up 165.1%.

Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts in each year. At the end of the fiscal year ended March 2026, total equity was KRW 53.3 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 24.7 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio was 46.2%.

Operating cash flow moved from an inflow of KRW 10.1 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2024 to KRW 2.92 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2025 and then to a net outflow of KRW 1.86 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026.

On a quarterly basis, sales went from KRW 25.7 billion in 2025 Q3 to KRW 20.6 billion in 2025 Q4 (down 20.0% quarter on quarter), then KRW 23.4 billion in 2026 Q1, KRW 23.5 billion in 2026 Q2 and KRW 22.7 billion in 2026 Q3 (down 3.4% quarter on quarter and down 11.7% year on year).

Operating profit was KRW 707 million in 2025 Q3, KRW 32.49 million in 2025 Q4, KRW 35.7 million in 2026 Q1, KRW 1.13 billion in 2026 Q2 and KRW 156 million in 2026 Q3, which was down 86.2% quarter on quarter and 77.9% year on year.

In 2026 Q3, profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 546 million exceeded operating profit, and the non-operating items behind the gap appear in the quarterly report's income statement detail.

The four quarters from 2025 Q4 to 2026 Q3 together produced KRW 2.71 billion of profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

05

Industry analysis

The company's business is tied to how enterprises change the way they handle customer contact. A company summary covering the fiscal year 2025 described how AI advances have increased demand for AI solutions such as chatbots, speeding customer response and reducing agent workloads.

The same summary pointed to chatbot and call-bot technology in AICC, built on natural language processing, speech recognition and text analytics, which can now generate more natural answers after the adoption of generative AI.

This suggests that AI features layered onto existing contact-center platforms are the main source of demand. Whether that demand has translated into revenue growth must be checked against segment-level sales trends, and the year-on-year decline in 2026 Q3 sales is the point to verify.

In terms of competition, Ecstelecomco is a mid-sized specialist in voice and contact-center solutions. Large SI companies cover cloud and system building across the board, while Ecstelecomco concentrates on contact centers and collaboration tools.

Within the IT services sector, the company ranks in the lower part of the sector by market capitalization on the Alphasquare tally. The key industry variable for the company is how quickly it can commercialize AI features on its existing customer base.

With the whole sector in an AI transition, growth gaps can open within the sector depending on the pace of AI adoption.

06

Outlook

The nearest scheduled event is the cancellation of treasury shares on November 27, 2026. The shares to be cancelled are 1,187,000 of the 10,888,500 issued common shares, and the disclosure states that the total number of issued shares falls while registered capital does not. The planned amount is KRW 2.99 billion.

Because cancelling treasury shares reduces the share count and therefore changes per-share metrics, it should be read alongside the share count in the next periodic report. A September 2026 disclosure also indicated the planned acquisition of 30,000 treasury shares.

On the business side, the next checkpoint is whether 2026 Q4 sales and operating profit hold near the 2026 Q3 level, with the fourth quarter ending on December 31, 2026.

Operating profit rose to KRW 1.13 billion in 2026 Q2 and then fell to KRW 156 million in 2026 Q3, so whether that pattern continues in the fourth quarter is a reference point for the earnings base.

On sales, the question is whether the decline to KRW 22.7 billion in 2026 Q3, from KRW 23.4 billion and KRW 23.5 billion in the two prior quarters, reverses.

The annual report for the fiscal year ending March 2027 is due by the end of June 2027 under the statutory filing deadline, and it will finalize the 2026 Q4 and full-year figures. At that point, whether the company discloses the share of AI-based offerings in its revenue will indicate how fast the business is shifting.

08

Bull factors

Earnings recovery from loss to profit

Operating profit moved from a KRW 2.69 billion loss in the fiscal year ended March 2025 to KRW 299 million in the fiscal year ended March 2026.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 165.1% to KRW 1.66 billion, and sales recovered from KRW 73.3 billion to KRW 91.2 billion, moving in the same direction as profit.

Structural demand for AI-based contact solutions

Explanations of recent sector trends point to rising demand for AI chatbots and agent-support tools, which speed customer response and lighten agent workloads.

ETaaS, the largest segment at 43% of 2026 Q1 revenue, develops AI chatbot and agent-support systems, which places the company's largest business in line with this demand.

Financial capacity and shareholder return moves

Total equity was KRW 53.3 billion at the end of the fiscal year ended March 2026, against liabilities of KRW 24.7 billion and a debt-to-equity ratio of 46.2%.

The board approved cancelling 1,187,000 previously acquired treasury shares, with the cancellation scheduled for November 27, 2026, which suggests financial room for further shareholder returns.

09

Bear factors

Simultaneous decline in quarterly sales and profit

2026 Q3 sales of KRW 22.7 billion fell 11.7% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 156 million fell 77.9% year on year. Operating profit had reached KRW 1.13 billion in 2026 Q2 and dropped 86.2% quarter on quarter, which shows how unstable quarterly profit has been since the turnaround.

Reversal in operating cash flow

Operating cash flow fell from an inflow of KRW 10.1 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2024 to KRW 2.92 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2025, and then to a net outflow of KRW 1.86 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026.

Even as reported profit turned positive, cash generation weakened, and a continued gap between profit and cash flow remains a bearish factor.

Smaller scale within the sector and a narrow business scope

On the Alphasquare tally, the company ranks in the lower part of the IT services sector by market capitalization. Its business is concentrated in voice and contact-center solutions, a narrower scope than large SI companies that cover cloud and system building across the board. With annual sales around KRW 91.2 billion, scale could also limit expansion capacity.

10

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly operating profit ranged from KRW 707 million in 2025 Q3 to KRW 32.49 million in 2025 Q4, KRW 1.13 billion in 2026 Q2 and KRW 156 million in 2026 Q3, which makes a clear quarterly trend hard to establish.

How much one-off items affected these results is visible in the income statement detail of each quarterly report.

Pace of AI transition

Chatbot and generative AI adoption is moving quickly. If existing contact-center platforms cannot add AI features in time, demand could shift elsewhere. Because the whole sector is moving in the same direction, a company slow in technology transition could see its sales recovery delayed. The disclosure of AI-based revenue share would show how exposed the company is.

Small market capitalization and trading liquidity

Trading volume is thin on many days, and with a small market capitalization, daily price moves can become large regardless of earnings. A share price below book equity is also sensitive to small changes in supply and demand, and a shareholder base concentrated in small investors makes price signals harder to read.

12

Overall view

In the fiscal year ended March 2026, Ecstelecomco's sales recovered to KRW 91.2 billion, operating profit turned positive at KRW 299 million and profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 1.66 billion, so all three measures moved in a recovery direction.

Operating cash flow, however, turned to a net outflow of KRW 1.86 billion, so profit and cash moved in different directions.

In 2026 Q3, sales of KRW 22.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 156 million both declined year on year, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 2.71 billion.

The business is organized across ETaaS, Customer Success and Digital Workplace, with demand for AI chatbots and agent-support tools as its starting point. The price-to-book ratio sits below the sector average and the dividend yield above it.

The balance sheet, with total equity of KRW 53.3 billion and a debt-to-equity ratio of 46.2%, is stable. The next checkpoints are the treasury share cancellation on November 27, 2026, the earnings level of 2026 Q4, and the annual report due by the end of June 2027.
