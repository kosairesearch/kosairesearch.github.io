02

Business structure

Ecstelecomco was founded in 1999 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2007, with its headquarters in Seocho-gu, Seoul. The company builds on its long experience in enterprise communications to focus on its customer success and digital workplace businesses.

A securities firm's technical analysis report published in January 2022 described it as a leading provider of integrated enterprise voice communication (CC/UC) solutions and services, though that description predates the current product mix.

The business is split into three segments: ETaaS, customer success, and digital workplace. Based on the first quarter of 2026 revenue, Butler puts the mix at 43% ETaaS, 30% customer success, and 27% digital workplace.

Alphasquare's breakdown ranks the same segments differently, at 46.94% customer success, 29.78% ETaaS, and 23.28% digital workplace, so the ordering depends on the source's period and method. Major shareholders hold 28.65% and treasury shares account for 8.15% as of October 7, 2026, with a free float ratio of 61.51%.

In the customer-interaction automation market, chatbot and call-bot technology that combines natural language processing, speech recognition, and generative AI is advancing quickly.

The company's core customer-interaction and collaboration solutions sit close to this trend, so whether it builds voice AI capability in-house is likely to shape its business structure.

Its 2026 corporate value-up plan (June) and treasury share trust contract (August) are shareholder-return disclosures that sit outside the core product business.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, revenue was largest in the fiscal year ended March 2023 at KRW 100.6 billion and smallest in the fiscal year ended March 2025 at KRW 73.3 billion. Revenue fell 8.8% to KRW 91.8 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2024 and fell 20.1% to KRW 73.3 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2025.

It rose 24.4% to KRW 91.2 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026, a recovery that still sits below the fiscal 2023 level.

Operating profit fell 65.2% to KRW 1.48 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2024 after KRW 4.23 billion a year earlier, and the fiscal year ended March 2025 recorded an operating loss of KRW 2.69 billion.

The fiscal year ended March 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 299 million, with an operating margin of 0.3%.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was largest in the fiscal year ended March 2023 at KRW 4.64 billion and smallest in the fiscal year ended March 2025 at KRW 626 million, and it rose 165.1% to KRW 1.66 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 10.1 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2024 to KRW 2.92 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2025, then turned negative at -KRW 1.86 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026.

The debt ratio declined from 57.2% in the fiscal year ended March 2024 to 44.3% in the fiscal year ended March 2025 and stood at 46.2% in the fiscal year ended March 2026. On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 posted the highest revenue of KRW 25.7 billion and returned to operating profit of KRW 707 million.

The fourth quarter of 2025 marked the low point, with revenue of KRW 20.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 32.49 million.

Revenue then came in at KRW 23.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 23.5 billion in the second quarter, while operating profit reached KRW 35.7 million and KRW 1.13 billion respectively.

Operating profit moved from a loss of KRW 476 million in the second quarter of 2025 to a profit in the second quarter of 2026. Profit attributable to owners of the parent totaled KRW 3.05 billion over the last four quarters, from the third quarter of 2025 through the second quarter of 2026.

Because quarterly operating profit is small, the growth rate in the second quarter of 2026 looks large against a low base. The split of the changes into segment revenue and cost drivers cannot be separated from the reported figures alone.

05

Industry analysis

Ecstelecomco operates in the market for contact-center and collaboration solutions that handle voice and customer-service channels for corporate clients.

Earlier, the spread of remote work was seen as supporting demand for integrated voice communication solutions, a view that the January 2022 technical analysis report shared when it expected stable market share.

More recently, generative AI has widened what customer-service automation can do, shifting demand from traditional voice infrastructure toward solutions with built-in AI features. That shift is both an opportunity and a challenge for incumbent vendors.

Falling behind on features could erode an existing customer base, while adding AI functions quickly could capture automation demand. Alphasquare's industry comparison ranks the company 89th by market capitalization among 100 IT services stocks, which places it in the lower part of the group.

The same source shows a debt ratio below the industry average, so financial burden is relatively light. Profitability comparisons against the industry remain difficult to read because the company only recently returned to operating profit.

Large platform and systems-integration companies such as NAVER, Samsung SDS, LG CNS, Hyundai Autoever, and Kakao also operate in the same sector, but their scale puts them in a different competitive arena.

Ecstelecomco appears to compete by holding its position in voice and customer-service niches rather than confronting larger players head on.
