02

Business structure

Ecstelecomco was founded in 1999, listed on KOSDAQ in 2007, and is headquartered in Seocho-gu, Seoul. The company has built its business around integrated enterprise voice communication (CC/UC) solutions and services, and now runs three main areas: Customer Success, ETaaS and Digital Workplace.

Customer Success centers on solutions tied to contact-center and customer-service work, and the backdrop is rapid progress in chatbot and call-bot technology for AI-based customer contact (AICC). The detailed product lineup under ETaaS and Digital Workplace could not be confirmed in this review.

Segment revenue shares differ across sources, so this report does not settle on one split. One source put ETaaS at 43%, Customer Success at 30% and Digital Workplace at 27% as of 1Q 2026. Another source put Customer Success at 46.94%, ETaaS at 29.78% and Digital Workplace at 23.28% but did not state a reference period.

Customer-level revenue concentration and the main client mix were not confirmed, so they are not described. Ownership data as of 7 October 2026 show the largest shareholder and related parties holding 28.65% and treasury shares at 8.15%.

On scale, the company ranked 89th out of 100 IT services stocks by market capitalization in a 2 October 2026 tally, which shows a wide size gap with large IT services and platform companies. No source comparing its technology or market share with rivals was found, so the competitive view stays qualitative.

04

Earnings analysis

On the regular-report basis, revenue fell from KRW 100.6 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2023 to KRW 91.8 billion in the year ended March 2024 (down 8.8% year on year) and KRW 73.3 billion in the year ended March 2025 (down 20.1%), before recovering to KRW 91.2 billion in the year ended March 2026 (up 24.4%).

Within the period, the year ended March 2023 was the largest for revenue and the year ended March 2025 was the smallest. Operating profit went from KRW 4.23 billion to KRW 1.48 billion (down 65.2%), then swung to an operating loss of KRW 2.69 billion in the year ended March 2025, which was a shift from profit to loss.

The year ended March 2026 recorded operating profit of KRW 299 million, a turn back to profit. Operating margins ran 4.2%, 1.6%, -3.7% and 0.3% across the four years.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.64 billion, KRW 2.63 billion (down 43.4%), KRW 626 million (down 76.2%) and KRW 1.66 billion (up 165.1%). Net income including non-controlling interests is reported at the same amounts for each year.

Operating cash flow was KRW -3.49 billion, KRW 10.1 billion, KRW 2.92 billion and KRW -1.86 billion. In the year ended March 2026, net income was positive while operating cash flow was negative. Leverage measured as total liabilities over total equity was 51.6%, 57.2%, 44.3% and 46.2% across the same four years.

At the end of March 2026, total equity was KRW 53.3 billion, of which the controlling shareholders' stake was KRW 53.3 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 24.7 billion.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 21.5 billion in 2Q 2025, KRW 25.7 billion in 3Q 2025 (up 19.7% from the prior quarter) and KRW 20.6 billion in 4Q 2025 (down 20.0%).

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 476 million in 2Q 2025 to KRW 707 million in 3Q 2025, a turn to profit, and then fell to KRW 32.49 million in 4Q 2025 (down 95.4%). Revenue was KRW 23.4 billion in 1Q 2026 (up 13.8%) and operating profit was KRW 35.7 million (up 9.9%).

Revenue in 2Q 2026 was KRW 23.5 billion (up 0.4% from the prior quarter and up 9.4% year on year), and operating profit was KRW 1.13 billion (up 3,071.3% from the prior quarter and a turn to profit from a year earlier).

Revenue was nearly flat in 2Q 2026 while operating profit rose sharply, which points to a change on the cost side, though the breakdown could not be confirmed.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a loss of KRW 170 million in 2Q 2025 to KRW 883 million in 3Q 2025, then KRW 232 million in 4Q 2025 (down 73.8%), and reached KRW 716 million in 1Q 2026 and KRW 1.22 billion in 2Q 2026.

Over the last four quarters (3Q 2025 to 2Q 2026), controlling-shareholder net income totaled KRW 3.05 billion. No one-off items were confirmed in the quarterly results, so none are assumed here.

Industry analysis

The enterprise voice and customer-contact solution market is shifting toward AI-based customer contact (AICC). Industry material describes rapid progress in chatbot and call-bot technology built on natural language processing, speech recognition and text analytics, with generative AI making answers more natural.

On the demand side, the stated driver is companies' need to speed up customer response and reduce agents' workload. However, no market-size or order-flow statistics that would show where the cycle stands were found in this review.

Ecstelecomco was described as a leading provider of integrated enterprise voice communication (CC/UC) solutions and services in a January 2022 technical report from a Korean brokerage.

Listed peers in the same IT and software sector include NAVER, Samsung SDS, Kakao, LG CNS, Hyundai Autoever, Kinx, Elice, Daou Technology, Daou Data and Itcenglobal, but no revenue or margin comparison figures were confirmed for them. On market capitalization, the company is well below the sector average.

If technology shifts quickly, demand for existing voice-centered solutions could change, so how fast the company adds AI features to its current products is the main yardstick for its position in the sector.

Because the public material available here is limited, this report does not make a directional call on that point.

Revenue Recovery and a Return to Profit

The company's own comment says first-half 2026 standalone revenue, operating profit and net income all improved from a year earlier.
