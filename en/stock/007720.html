02

Business structure

Sono Square is a distribution and retail affiliate of the Daemyung Sono Group whose core business is MRO (maintenance, repair and operations) supply for hotels and resorts. Its MRO unit consolidates consulting and bulk purchasing for hospitality consumables such as guestroom amenities and cleaning equipment.

MRO expansion tied to new Sono Hotels & Resorts properties was the main driver of 2025 growth. The company says it is moving from straight product delivery to planned supply models tailored to each hotel's concept.

Its lifestyle brand SONOSEASON posted cumulative revenue of KRW 148 million for the first nine months of 2025, up 62.5% year on year, with six shop-in-shop counters in a major home-appliance retailer and expansion into regional markets planned.

Its room spray became the No. 1 deodorizer and diffuser item in a major health-and-beauty chain promotion in 2025. The retail unit GoodnGoods is expanding experiential product lines aimed at guests and foreign tourists.

In aviation, the group's airline acquisition in June 2025 led to the start of in-flight consumables supply. Dependence on group hotel demand remains high, which makes outside customer growth a key challenge. A nine-item bedding line for the 2026 fall-winter season was also launched.

04

Earnings analysis

On disclosed results, revenue fell from KRW 171 billion in 2022 to KRW 159.9 billion in 2023 (down 6.4%) and KRW 133.5 billion in 2024 (down 16.6%), then rebounded to KRW 162.9 billion in 2025 (up 22.0%). The largest year in 2022 to 2025 was 2022, and the smallest was 2024.

Operating loss narrowed from KRW 18.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.59 billion in 2023, operating profit of KRW 656 million in 2024 marked a turn to profit, and 2025 operating profit rose 112.9% to KRW 1.4 billion. The operating margin improved from -10.8% in 2022 to 0.9% in 2025 but remains below 1%.

The company attributed 2025 growth mainly to MRO expansion from new Sono Hotels & Resorts properties, together with its planned supply model. The narrower 2025 net loss of KRW 2.7 billion was explained by the company as reflecting lower financial asset trading losses.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 18 billion in 2022, KRW 48.77 million in 2023, KRW 10.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.7 billion in 2025, a four-year run of losses.

Quarterly, Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 49.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.75 billion were followed by an operating loss of KRW 462 million in Q4 2025, then operating losses of KRW 775 million in Q1 2026 and KRW 2.05 billion in Q2 2026.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 45 billion rose for a second straight quarter, and operating profit of KRW 492 million turned positive but was down 71.9% year on year. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 4.35 billion.

Segment reports put the wholesale and retail business at about KRW 5.6 billion in operating profit for 2025 and about KRW 1.1 billion for Q1 2026, while the mattress rental business posted an operating loss of about KRW 4.2 billion in 2025; these are segment figures, not consolidated ones.

The debt ratio rose from 45.8% in 2024 to 68.7% in 2025, and total equity fell from KRW 100.8 billion at end-2024 to KRW 75.1 billion at end-2025. That drop is larger than the 2025 net loss, so the cause should be checked in the filing notes. Operating cash flow was KRW 7.97 billion in 2025, down from KRW 11.6 billion in 2024.

05

Industry analysis

Sono Square's end demand comes from room operations and facility upkeep at domestic hotels and resorts. The 2026 Hotel Fair, presented as the country's largest hospitality trade show, shows that B2B buying remains concentrated around such events.

The company said it plans to widen outside sales in 2026 using its hospitality-focused MRO capabilities and a premium lifestyle brand. On the demand side, new resort openings and renovations of existing resorts were cited as adding supply opportunities.

However, results are closely tied to the pace of expansion at group properties, a variable separate from the industry cycle.

Among listed peers in the same sector, such as Cuckoo Homesys, Hanssem and Lotte Shopping, Sono Square's structure is distinct because its B2B procurement business leans heavily on its group hotel network.

Regulators tightened listing rules for stocks trading below KRW 1,000 in July 2026, which raised pressure on low-priced issuers to improve capital efficiency. That policy factor is separate from demand, and it is the context most relevant to the share reduction.

Outlook

The company said it will focus on widening outside sales and strengthening its brands in 2026. SONOSEASON plans to add stores in 2026 and to explore overseas expansion. In aviation, the plan is to build on in-flight consumables supply and develop in-flight retail with the lifestyle brand and the retail unit.

In the second half of 2026, the company stressed profit-focused management and maximizing peak-season sales, and said it would push a premium home-care subscription platform to make profitability gains visible.

On governance, the CEO resigned on October 1, 2026, with an executive director acting in the role, and a new CEO was appointed after the November 10, 2026 extraordinary shareholder meeting approved a new inside director. A cancellation of 2,326 common shares is scheduled for November 24, 2026.

The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, which will provide the first audited full-year figures for 2026 and the fourth-quarter numbers.

The bull case depends on whether planned supply and SONOSEASON growth keep quarterly profit positive, while the bear case depends on whether group dependence persists without outside customer growth. For that reason, the 2026 full-year operating margin is the key checkpoint.

08

Bull factors

Return to operating profit

Operating profit turned positive in 2024 at KRW 656 million, and 2025 operating profit rose 112.9% to KRW 1.4 billion. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 492 million also returned to profit. The margin remains around 1%, so the scale is small, but the direction has improved from the 2022 operating loss.

Group hotel network and planned supply

Expansion at Sono Hotels & Resorts properties drove MRO revenue, and the company says its planned supply model, tailored to each hotel's concept, is adding value. It has set outside sales growth as a 2026 goal. Extending sales to outside luxury hotels and resorts is presented as the growth path.

SONOSEASON brand growth

SONOSEASON posted cumulative revenue of KRW 148 million for the first nine months of 2025, up 62.5% year on year. It runs six shop-in-shop counters in a major home-appliance retailer and launched nine new bedding products for the 2026 fall-winter season. As an own brand, it could serve as a route to a customer base beyond the group.

09

Bear factors

Prolonged parent-level losses

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was recorded for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, and the 2025 loss was KRW 2.7 billion. Over the last four quarters, that net loss totaled KRW 4.35 billion. Even with operating profit back in positive territory, bottom-line profit has not yet followed.

Group dependence and quarterly volatility

Dependence on group hotel demand is high, which makes outside customer growth a challenge. Operating loss widened to KRW 2.05 billion in Q2 2026, and Q3 operating profit of KRW 492 million fell 71.9% year on year. Because quarterly direction keeps changing, a single profitable quarter is not enough to establish a trend.

Balance sheet and equity decline

The debt ratio rose from 45.8% in 2024 to 68.7% in 2025. Total equity fell from KRW 100.8 billion at end-2024 to KRW 75.1 billion at end-2025, a drop larger than the 2025 net loss. Operating cash flow also fell from KRW 11.6 billion in 2024 to KRW 7.97 billion in 2025.

Risk factors

Management change

CEO Jeong Hyeon-cheol resigned on October 1, 2026, citing personal reasons, and Kim Deok-won was appointed CEO on November 10, 2026. The new CEO's prior roles were at a group affiliate. Whether the change maintains strategy continuity or shifts the outside-sales direction cannot yet be judged from the disclosed plans.

Listing standards for low-priced stocks

From July 2026, regulators created a listing-removal standard for stocks trading below KRW 1,000. If a stock fails to stay at KRW 1,000 or above on at least 45 of 90 trading days, delisting procedures begin.

Reverse-split methods to lift prices were also restricted, and the company decided on the 5-for-1 reverse split in March 2026.

Affiliate risk

A group affiliate filed a preliminary listing review application with the exchange on June 26, 2026. Because this ties into group-level restructuring, the weight of related-party trade may draw attention in filings and the review process.

Overall view

Sono Square grew revenue to KRW 162.9 billion in 2025 and lifted operating profit to KRW 1.4 billion, but net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was recorded for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025.

In 2026, operating losses widened in Q1 and Q2 before Q3 operating profit of KRW 492 million turned positive, though that was down 71.9% year on year. Strengths include MRO supply built on the group hotel network and SONOSEASON's revenue growth, while weaknesses include group dependence and quarterly volatility.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio rose to 68.7% in 2025 and total equity declined, and the share price remains low relative to book value even after the reverse split.

The management change, the low-priced listing standard and the affiliate's listing review are external variables that could shape the business direction and listing conditions. The key checkpoints are the full-year 2026 operating margin and whether parent-level net income turns positive in the 2026 annual report.
