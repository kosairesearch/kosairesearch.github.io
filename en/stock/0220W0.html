Report overview

Hanwha M&S: Tech Momentum, Life Division Drag

Hanwha Machinery & Service Holdings, spun off from Hanwha in August 2026, is a newly established holding company spanning AI-vision and semiconductor equipment (Tech) and retail and hospitality (Life), which within two months of its KOSPI listing encountered a large-scale public tender offer framed around accountability management.

02

Business structure

Hanwha Machinery & Service Holdings (Hanwha M&S) is a KOSPI-listed holding company formed through the spin-off of Hanwha, organized around two operating pillars: Tech and Life.

The Tech division encompasses Hanwha Vision (AI security cameras and vision solutions), Hanwha Semitec (semiconductor equipment), Hanwha Momentum (secondary battery and display equipment), and Hanwha Robotics (collaborative robots).

The Life division consists of Hanwha Galleria (department stores), Hanwha Hotels & Resorts, and Ourhome (contract catering and food).

Including overseas entities and subsidiaries, the group spans 57 affiliates in total, with consolidated revenue of approximately KRW 6 trillion and total assets of approximately KRW 11.3 trillion as of end-2025.

Only three affiliates—Hanwha Vision, Hanwha Hotels & Resorts, and Ourhome—generated annual revenue exceeding KRW 1 trillion in 2025; Hanwha Vision and Hanwha Galleria are separately listed on KOSPI, making their market capitalizations a direct variable in holding-company NAV estimation.

In the security camera market, Hanwha Vision ranks among the top five global vendors (excluding China) with approximately 6% market share per Omdia, competing against Hikvision and Dahua in non-Chinese markets.

Hanwha Semitec has secured a key supplier position in TC bonders essential to SK hynix's HBM manufacturing process, competing directly with Hana Microdisplay in this equipment segment.

Hanwha Momentum's secondary-battery equipment business faces near-term revenue pressure from slowing battery market growth, while pursuing new customers in non-battery sectors.

Hanwha Robotics participates in the collaborative robot market but is considered a limited-scale competitor relative to Universal Robots and Doosan Robotics.

The holding company itself derives income primarily from affiliate dividends and brand royalties, with consolidated performance driven by the aggregated results of its subsidiaries.

03

Recent trends

Following its KOSPI listing on August 25, 2026, Hanwha M&S's share price underperformed initial expectations; at the first extraordinary shareholders' meeting held in September 2026, investors raised concerns about the share price trajectory and the company's growth strategy.

President Kim Dong-seon responded by disclosing on October 12, 2026, a personal tender offer to purchase up to 16.06 million common shares—approximately 19% of total outstanding—at KRW 8,000 per share, deploying approximately KRW 128.5 billion of personal capital.

The offer price carried a premium of approximately 32% over the last closing price of KRW 6,080 and approximately 27% over the one-month average price of approximately KRW 6,300. On the announcement date, the stock surged over 23% intraday to a high of KRW 7,710, with daily turnover exceeding KRW 71.9 billion.

As of October 19, 2026, the stock trades at KRW 7,470, approximately 6.6% below the tender offer price of KRW 8,000.

Key subsidiary Hanwha Vision reported Q1 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 441.4 billion (down 2.3% year-on-year) and operating profit of KRW 22.1 billion at a 5.0% margin, representing a 55.3% year-on-year decline in operating profit, per Hanwha Vision's official Q1 2026 IR disclosure.

Hanwha Semitec's revenue fell sharply to KRW 83.1 billion in Q1 2026—down from KRW 135.7 billion in Q4 2025—as TC bonder orders placed in 2025 had been fully recognized by year-end, pushing the segment into an operating loss.

In Q2 2026, TC bonder orders resumed and Hanwha Semitec posted record quarterly revenue of KRW 147.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 14.0 billion, while Hanwha Vision's security division also achieved a quarterly sales record of KRW 394.6 billion.

04

Outlook

If the public tender offer completes as scheduled by November 2, 2026, President Kim Dong-seon's stake would rise from 5.81% to 24.81%, surpassing Hanwha Energy's 23.94% to make him the single largest shareholder, while the combined related-party stake would increase from 60.32% to 79.32%.

After Hanwha Semitec's Q2 2026 rebound, the market anticipates continued order flow supported by SK hynix's HBM4 production ramp and new FO-PLP customer orders secured in Q1 2026.

Kiwoom Securities has projected that Samsung Electronics and SK hynix will begin adopting hybrid bonding equipment for 16-layer-and-above HBM production, positioning Hanwha Semitec's second-generation HCB equipment as a potential beneficiary.

Hanwha Vision's AI vision platform 'Blaze,' launched officially in 2026, is expected to incrementally expand software-related revenue streams, with sustained European demand and favorable currency effects supporting operating margin recovery.

Against these positives, Hanwha Robotics remains a profitability drag—having posted an operating loss of KRW 17.7 billion in 2024, with projected losses exceeding KRW 20 billion in 2025 per earlier assessments—constraining consolidated earnings improvement.

Hanwha Galleria's near-zero operating margin and ongoing labor and raw-material cost pressures at Ourhome and Hanwha Hotels & Resorts collectively limit the scope of Life division margin recovery.

The absence of a clearly articulated roadmap for converting affiliate asset value into consolidated cash flow and shareholder returns remains a factor in the ongoing process of price discovery for the newly listed holding company.

05

Bull factors

Hanwha Vision: Established Cash Generator with AI Transition Underway

Per Kiwoom Securities, Hanwha Vision's security division achieved record revenue of KRW 1.3351 trillion and operating profit of KRW 182.3 billion in 2025.

In Q2 2026, the security division again set a quarterly record of KRW 394.6 billion in revenue, reaffirming its role as the primary cash generator within the holding-company structure.

Expanding AI CCTV penetration, sustained European demand, and growing software revenue following the launch of the Blaze platform collectively support both top-line and margin improvement.

Hanwha Semitec: HBM4 Cycle Becoming More Visible

Hanwha Semitec posted record quarterly revenue of KRW 147.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 14.0 billion in Q2 2026, reflecting the resumption of TC bonder orders. The company also announced securing new FO-PLP customer orders in Q1 2026, with revenue recognition expected from Q3 onward.

Kiwoom Securities has analyzed that Samsung Electronics and SK hynix's adoption of hybrid bonding technology from H2 2026 onward could provide Hanwha Semitec with additional order opportunities.

Spin-Off Rationale: Enhancing Divisional Focus, Targeted Reduction of Conglomerate Discount

Hanwha undertook the spin-off to address the conglomerate discount that arose from the co-existence of disparate businesses within one entity, separating Tech and Life operations into a dedicated holding company.

The holding-company structure enables the Tech and Life divisions to pursue decision-making frameworks independently calibrated to their respective market dynamics.

At the time of its launch, Hanwha M&S expressed its expectation that the separation would accelerate investment and business expansion across both divisions.

06

Bear factors

Persistent Profitability Drag from Hanwha Robotics and Galleria

Hanwha Robotics recorded an operating loss of KRW 17.7 billion in 2024 due to heavy R&D spending on collaborative robot technology, with losses projected to exceed KRW 20 billion in 2025.

Hanwha Galleria's operating margin remains at approximately 0.1%, reflecting structural demand headwinds and cost pressures in the department store sector. The low-profitability profile of both units materially dilutes the earnings generated by the Tech division at the consolidated level.

Hanwha Semitec Order-Cycle Volatility Creates Near-Term Earnings Uncertainty

Hanwha Semitec's quarterly performance is closely tied to SK hynix's HBM capital expenditure timing, making it susceptible to sharp swings when customer investment decisions shift. The Q1 2026 revenue drop from KRW 135.7 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 83.1 billion illustrates this structural vulnerability.

The risk of TC bonder order delays or revisions to HBM investment plans continues to weigh on near-term earnings predictability.

NAV Opacity and Holding-Company Discount of a Newly Listed Entity

The extraordinary step of a public tender offer less than two months after listing reflects a market perception that Hanwha M&S's intrinsic value was not adequately captured in its share price.

As a newly established holding company with a very short track record of consolidated financial disclosures, its standalone dividend and capital allocation policies have yet to be clearly communicated to the market.

Korean holding companies structurally tend to trade at a discount to the sum-of-parts value of their listed subsidiaries, and Hanwha M&S as a newly listed entity is not exempt from this pattern.

07

Risk factors

Tariff and Supply Chain Risk

Hanwha Vision supplies global markets through manufacturing facilities in Vietnam and China; its official Q1 2026 IR materials explicitly cited tariffs and semiconductor price increases as sources of business environment instability.

Changes in U.S. tariff policy or supply chain restructuring could alter production cost structures, affecting security-division profitability. Escalating geopolitical tensions add uncertainty to global CCTV demand and could also introduce export-control risks in the semiconductor equipment segment.

Semiconductor Equipment Demand-Cycle Risk

Hanwha Semitec's earnings are tightly linked to the HBM capital expenditure cycle, making quarterly results highly sensitive to any reduction or delay in major customer investment commitments.

A deterioration in the global semiconductor environment or a delay in the next HBM technology transition timeline could suppress TC bonder orders again. Hanwha Momentum's secondary-battery equipment business also faces ongoing near-term revenue pressure as battery market oversupply conditions persist.

Governance Concentration Risk

If the tender offer completes as planned, the combined stake of President Kim Dong-seon and related parties would reach approximately 79.32%, significantly concentrating decision-making authority.

Minority shareholders would hold reduced voting influence, and the potential for conflicts of interest in related-party transactions or new business investment decisions may increase.

As a newly listed holding company, the perceived board independence and credibility of its shareholder return framework are key long-term corporate governance variables.

08

Overall view

Hanwha Machinery & Service Holdings is a newly established holding company that launched in August 2026, encompassing approximately KRW 6 trillion in consolidated revenue and 57 affiliates, yet with a very limited track record of independent consolidated financials.

The Tech division—led by Hanwha Vision and Hanwha Semitec—demonstrated record quarterly performance in Q2 2026 against a backdrop of AI CCTV expansion and HBM equipment demand, while Hanwha Robotics and Hanwha Galleria continue to weigh on consolidated profitability with persistent losses and near-zero margins respectively.

The public tender offer by President Kim Dong-seon on October 12, 2026, represents a personal capital commitment to accountability management; however, analysts note that the tender price of KRW 8,000 does not guarantee the company's long-term intrinsic value.

Key forward-looking variables include the sustainability of Hanwha Semitec's HBM4 order pipeline, Hanwha Vision's pace of software revenue conversion, Hanwha Robotics' path to loss reduction, and the clarity of the holding company's own dividend and capital allocation policy.

Tariff and geopolitical variables and semiconductor equipment order-cycle volatility remain sources of near-term earnings uncertainty, while the governance concentration following the tender offer completion also warrants consideration from a minority shareholder perspective.
