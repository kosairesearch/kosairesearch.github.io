Utilization recovery meets a Q3 2026 net loss

02

Business structure

Hana Materials, founded in January 2007, makes consumable silicon and silicon carbide (SiC) electrodes and rings used in the etching process of semiconductor manufacturing. The company reports two segments, semiconductor parts and new technology business finance.

By product, silicon parts accounted for 87.69% of 2025 revenue, other products 12.09% and new technology business finance 0.22%, according to one data source, while another listing shows 86.61% for silicon parts, so the split depends on the classification used.

Finer line widths have raised the importance of high-density plasma environments and dry etching, according to brokerage commentary. Major customers are global etch-equipment makers, and past press coverage named Tokyo Electron (TEL), SEMES, Applied Materials (AMAT) and Lam Research.

The 2021 product mix was reported as 46% electrodes, 47% rings and 7% other. Daishin Securities said logic and foundry-bound revenue had stayed at roughly 10% or so. Because most revenue tracks memory makers' equipment investment and utilization, results are sensitive to the memory cycle. The company is also pushing its SiC business to diversify beyond silicon parts.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue fell 24.0% from KRW 307.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 233.6 billion in 2023, then rose 7.7% to KRW 251.6 billion in 2024 and 8.7% to KRW 273.5 billion in 2025, two straight years of growth from 2024 to 2025.

Operating profit dropped from KRW 93.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 41.3 billion in 2023 (down 56.0%), then recovered to KRW 43.4 billion in 2024 (up 5.2%) and KRW 50.1 billion in 2025 (up 15.4%). The largest year of 2022 to 2025 was 2022 and the smallest was 2023.

The operating margin fell from 30.5% in 2022 to 17.7% in 2023, then stood at 17.3% in 2024 and 18.3% in 2025. Controlling-shareholder net profit was KRW 80.1 billion in 2022, KRW 34.2 billion in 2023 (down 57.3%), KRW 31.8 billion in 2024 (down 7.1%) and KRW 38.4 billion in 2025 (up 20.8%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts for each year. The debt ratio went from 61.2% in 2022 to 68.3% in 2023 and 62.2% in 2024, then fell to 47.9% at the end of 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 463.7 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 221.9 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 82.6 billion in 2022, KRW 44.2 billion in 2023, KRW 75.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 68.5 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 69.8 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 80.9 billion in Q4 2025 (up 15.9% quarter on quarter) and KRW 93.4 billion in Q1 2026 (up 15.4%), then slipped 3.5% to KRW 90.1 billion in Q2 2026 before reaching KRW 94.9 billion in Q3 2026 (up 5.3% quarter on quarter and 35.8% year on year).

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 12 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 20.9 billion in Q4 2025 (up 73.9%), KRW 21.3 billion in Q1 2026 (up 2.1%), KRW 26 billion in Q2 2026 (up 22.2%) and KRW 27.1 billion in Q3 2026 (up 4.2%, or 125.9% year on year), rising for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026.

Controlling-shareholder net profit was KRW 9.21 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 17.6 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 17.6 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 19.8 billion in Q2 2026, then swung to a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 6.65 billion in Q3 2026.

Over the last four quarters, controlling-shareholder net profit totaled KRW 48.3 billion. Yuanta Securities had estimated Q3 2026 net profit at KRW 1.6 billion, while the reported result was a loss.

Daishin Securities noted that Q1 2026 included one-off costs such as stock option expenses and still kept the operating margin in the 20% range.

05

Industry analysis

The central industry theme is memory makers' shift to advanced process nodes, which is lifting demand for etch consumables. Yuanta Securities cited NAND process conversion and rising demand for silicon parts for advanced DRAM as main reasons for better Q3 2026 results in an October 2026 report.

Daishin Securities said a larger share of advanced-node production is shortening replacement cycles for parts and widening the customer and application base.

DB Financial Investment said, in a report published before the Q1 2026 results, that capacity additions and higher utilization tied to a strong memory upcycle were continuing. Shinhan Investment Corp. said utilization rose from 68% in Q3 2025 to 80% in Q1 2026 and expected it to reach 85% to 90% in the second half.

On logic and foundry revenue of roughly 10%, Daishin Securities argued that finer process geometry leaves room to expand the penetration of silicon parts. The company supplies consumables tied to memory customers' equipment investment, so the scale of that investment and customer utilization determine volume.

Listed peers in the same semiconductor sector include Wonik IPS, Jusung Engineering, PSK, Soulbrain and Tokai Carbon Korea. DB Financial Investment expects new investment to increase gradually in 2027.

The industry picture is one where capacity investment and utilization gains overlap, and the next phase will depend on how fast customers execute their expansion plans.

Shinhan Investment Corp. forecast 2026 revenue of KRW 377.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 94.4 billion, with the operating margin rising from 18.3% in 2025 to 25%. Full-year estimates vary widely across brokers, so forecasts for the same year span a broad range.

In Q3 2026, the reported controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 6.65 billion ran against Yuanta's net profit estimate of KRW 1.6 billion, so the direction of non-operating items and one-off costs differed from expectations.

Daishin Securities expects silicon-part revenue to keep growing through 2027 as advanced-node work expands. DB Financial Investment expects new investment to rise gradually in 2027. On Shinhan's estimate, utilization would move from 80% in Q1 2026 to 85% to 90% in the second half.

Whether these forecasts materialize can be checked against the Q4 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report.

Bull factors

Utilization gains and memory capacity additions

Shinhan Investment Corp. said the utilization rate of silicon parts rose from 68% in the third quarter of 2025 to 80% in the first quarter of 2026, and expected it to climb to 85-90% in the second half.

DB Securities noted that memory makers continue to expand capacity and raise utilization, and projected that new investment will increase gradually in 2027. Because higher utilization improves equipment usage, room for an operating margin improvement could be confirmed in the business indicators.

Advanced-node shift and shorter replacement cycles

Daishin Securities analyzed that as the share of leading-edge processes grows, parts replacement cycles are shortening and the customer and application base is widening.

Yuanta Securities cited NAND process transitions and demand for silicon parts used in advanced DRAM as the basis for the improvement in third-quarter earnings. Daishin Securities' view is that, with logic/foundry accounting for only around 10% of sales, there is still room to widen the penetration of silicon parts.

Balance sheet and cash generation

The debt-to-equity ratio at the end of 2025 was 47.9%, down from 61.2% in 2022 and 68.3% in 2023. Total equity at the end of 2025 was 463.7 billion won and total liabilities were 221.9 billion won.

Operating cash flow was 68.5 billion won in 2025, higher than the 44.2 billion won in 2023, so the filings show that cash generation has recovered since the 2023 low.

Bear factors

Controlling-shareholder net loss in Q3 2026

Operating profit rose to 27.1 billion won in the third quarter of 2026, but net income attributable to owners of the parent swung to a net loss of 6.65 billion won. Even compared with Yuanta Securities' estimate of 1.6 billion won in third-quarter net income, the direction was different.

Which of non-operating items, income taxes, or one-off costs created the gap must be checked in the notes to the 2026 annual report.

Slower quarterly revenue momentum

Revenue in the second quarter of 2026 was 90.1 billion won, down 3.5% from the previous quarter. Third-quarter revenue recovered to 94.9 billion won, but quarter-on-quarter growth was only 5.3%. Compared with the 15.4% quarterly revenue increase in the first quarter of 2026, the pace of growth has slowed.

Memory-cycle dependence and earnings volatility

Operating profit in 2023 fell 56.0% from the previous year, and net income attributable to owners of the parent also dropped 57.3%. Because logic/foundry accounts for only around 10% of sales, the company depends heavily on the memory capital expenditure cycle.

Since customers are concentrated among etching equipment makers, an investment adjustment by a specific customer can be reflected directly in volumes.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and memory cycle

Most of the revenue is tied to memory makers' capital expenditures and to the utilization rates of etching equipment customers. As seen when operating profit fell 56.0% from the previous year in 2023, earnings could swing sharply if the industry turns down.

The fact that logic/foundry accounts for only around 10% of sales shows that customer diversification is still in progress.

Profitability and earnings structure

The swing to a net loss attributable to owners of the parent in the third quarter of 2026 runs opposite to the increase in operating profit, so the profit structure needs to be reviewed. Daishin Securities pointed out that one-off costs such as stock options were reflected in the first quarter of 2026. The operating margin fell from 30.5% in 2022 to 18.3% in 2025, so it has not yet recovered to its 2022 level.

Process transition and product response

The transition to leading-edge processes is a factor that brings forward parts replacement, but if the response in product shape and materials falls behind each time a process changes, the market share landscape could shift.

As of 2025, products other than silicon parts accounted for 12.09% of sales, so the pace at which new product sales expand is the key to diversifying earnings. If the customer qualification schedule for new products is delayed, the expected volumes could be booked later than anticipated.

Overall view

Hana Materials' reported figures show revenue rising for two straight years, in 2024 and 2025, after the 2023 low, with the operating margin recovering within the 17% to 18% range.

Quarterly revenue in 2026 was KRW 93.4 billion, KRW 90.1 billion and KRW 94.9 billion, staying in the KRW 90 billion range, and operating profit rose for four consecutive quarters.

By contrast, the controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 6.65 billion in Q3 2026 moved in the opposite direction to operating profit, making profit quality the point to verify.

Utilization gains and the shift to advanced process nodes are business-metric tailwinds, while brokers' projections for record FY2027 operating profit show a split outlook. These projections are broker estimates, not company guidance.

The share price trades at a premium to book value, and how it looks depends on which earnings period is used. The next checkpoints are the confirmed full-year results and non-operating items in the 2026 annual report, followed by whether utilization and revenue trends hold in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
