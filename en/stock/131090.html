Secuve: Two Quarters of Growth, Uneven Earnings Remain

Secuve's revenue and operating profit rose for two consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, but sharp quarter-to-quarter swings, including the first-quarter 2026 trough and the fourth-quarter 2025 spike, remain the defining feature of this small server-security software company.

02

Business structure

Secuve, founded on March 23, 2000, is a server-security software company whose core product is Secuve TOS, a secure operating system that controls access at the server OS level and prevents internal data leaks. iGRIFFIN, which manages account privileges, and LogGRIFFIN, which collects and analyzes system logs, form the other main product lines, and the company has extended its range into personal-data protection and authentication. iGRIFFIN is built on a combined authentication framework covering public certificates, smart cards and one-time passwords, and it was supplied to banks and securities firms as demand from the financial sector grew.

CERT1, a public-certificate security management solution, and mobile OTP authentication were also launched as part of its mobile security business.

Its customer base is centered on the financial sector, followed by large enterprises and public institutions that must strictly manage server access control and audit records.

The company's business description covers software development and sales alongside maintenance, so new solution deliveries and post-sale maintenance appear to form its two revenue pillars. The latest segment revenue mix should be checked in the segment information of the annual business report.

Secuve is compared with security companies such as Ahnlab, but it focuses on server operating systems and account, log and authentication areas, so its product routes differ.

Because the product portfolio is concentrated in specific areas, the impact of new products on total earnings should be checked through revenue trends by product line.

04

Earnings analysis

In the annual disclosures, revenue was KRW 13.7 billion in 2022 and KRW 14.4 billion in 2023, up 5.4% year on year, making 2023 the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Revenue fell 16.4% to KRW 12.1 billion in 2024, the smallest year in the period, before rising 11.3% to KRW 13.4 billion in 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 5.3 billion in 2022, the largest year in the period, then fell to KRW 4.45 billion in 2023 (down 16.0%) and KRW 3.42 billion in 2024 (down 23.2%) before rising 26.1% to KRW 4.31 billion in 2025.

The operating margin declined from 38.7% in 2022 to 30.8% in 2023 and 28.3% in 2024, then recovered to 32.1% in 2025. In 2024 operating profit fell faster than revenue, a pattern that points to a fixed cost base that did not shrink with sales.

Net profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 4.83 billion in 2022, KRW 5.79 billion in 2023 (up 19.9%), KRW 3.03 billion in 2024 (down 47.7%) and KRW 4.7 billion in 2025 (up 55.2%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts in each year, so the two lines coincide in the disclosed figures. Operating cash flow was KRW 7.6 billion in 2022, fell to KRW 3.58 billion in 2023 and KRW 3.12 billion in 2024, and recovered to KRW 6.32 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the fourth quarter of 2025 stood out with revenue of KRW 5.82 billion (up 115.7% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 3.38 billion (up 498.1%).

The first quarter of 2026 marked the trough, with revenue of KRW 2.32 billion (down 60.1%) and operating profit of KRW 6.94 million (down 99.8%).

Operating profit then rose to KRW 396 million in the second quarter and KRW 588 million in the third quarter, two consecutive quarterly increases, and net profit attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters totaled KRW 4.69 billion.

The specific contracts behind the concentration of revenue in the fourth quarter of 2025 are a point to check in the filed report.

Industry analysis

Demand for server access control and account and log management software in Korea appears closely tied to the IT security investment cycles of the financial and public sectors.

As companies tighten personal-data protection and internal control requirements, demand for audit trails and access control rises, but the timing of spending shifts with budget cycles and regulatory changes.

Secuve's revenue concentration in the fourth quarter of 2025 could be read in light of year-end budget execution, though that is an interpretation, and the individual contracts should be checked in the filings.

On a cycle view, the company went through a revenue decline in 2024, an annual recovery in 2025, and quarterly recovery in the second and third quarters of 2026.

Secuve is compared with security companies such as Ahnlab, but its product areas differ from vaccine or network security specialists, and it concentrates on access control at the server operating system level.

The speed at which corporate IT moves toward cloud and hybrid environments is a variable for growth in on-premises server security demand. The trend in the overall size of the Korean information security market should be checked against the latest official statistics.

Because the product range is narrow, investment decisions by a small number of customer groups can weigh heavily on quarterly revenue, which matters when reading the company's position in its industry.

Bull factors

Two consecutive quarters of recovery

Operating profit rose to KRW 396 million in the second quarter and KRW 588 million in the third quarter of 2026, two consecutive quarterly increases, and third-quarter revenue grew 5.1% year on year.

The rebound from KRW 6.94 million in operating profit in the first quarter is read as evidence of a floor in the business. Two quarters are too few to establish a trend, so whether the fourth quarter continues the pattern remains to be confirmed.

Operating margin recovery

The operating margin recovered from 28.3% in 2024 to 32.1% in 2025, and operating profit rose 26.1% to KRW 4.31 billion in 2025. The company moved out of the 2024 phase in which revenue and profit fell together, and the earnings structure improved.

Compared with 38.7% in 2022, the margin still has room to recover, which makes margin progress a checkpoint for business performance.

Low leverage and cash generation

Total liabilities were KRW 3.32 billion and total equity KRW 49.6 billion at the end of 2025, a debt ratio of 6.7%. Operating cash flow recovered from KRW 3.12 billion in 2024 to KRW 6.32 billion in 2025. A structure with low reliance on borrowing can act as a buffer against swings in demand and orders.

Overall view

Secuve is a small company whose core business is operating-system-level security software, and its annual operating margin recovered to 32.1% in 2025 from 28.3% in 2024.

After the first-quarter 2026 trough, revenue and operating profit rose for two consecutive quarters in the second and third quarters, and third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 2.84 billion and operating profit of KRW 588 million grew 5.1% and 4.2% year on year.

Over the same period, net profit attributable to owners of the parent fell 5.3%, so the operating gains did not carry through to the net profit line.

The KRW 5.82 billion in revenue and KRW 3.38 billion in operating profit recorded in the fourth quarter of 2025 remain a high base, so the fourth-quarter 2026 comparison will be affected by base effects. On the financial side, a debt ratio of 6.7% and total equity of KRW 49.6 billion at the end of 2025 are strengths.

On the other side, annual revenue of about KRW 13.4 billion, wide quarterly swings, a customer base centered on the financial and public sectors, and competition in the security market remain weaknesses.

Because strengths and weaknesses coexist, the next point to check is the annual operating margin and fourth-quarter earnings structure in the 2026 annual business report.
