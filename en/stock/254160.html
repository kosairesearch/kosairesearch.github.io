Wooden Deck Roads at the Core, Healthcare on the Side

JM-Multi's standalone revenue fell in 2025 while operating profit rose, and as a KONEX-listed stock its trading is extremely thin.

02

Business structure

JM-Multi was founded in March 2008 and listed on the KONEX market on December 13, 2021. The company is a sawmill and wood-processing business listed on KONEX on December 13, 2021. Its main product is a natural-timber deck road system, and it also makes wooden stairs, pedestrian bridges and landscape structures.

A Korea IR Service report put the product mix at deck roads 48%, wooden stairs 33% and pedestrian bridges 16%. That mix dates from the July 2023 report and may differ today. The same report said more than 90% of revenue comes from public institutions. The pace of budget execution by local governments and public bodies therefore drives results.

The report estimated JM-Multi's market share in its segment at 10-15% and described it as likely number one in Korea. Of total 2022 orders of KRW 273.3 billion, orders won by the company including its subsidiary were estimated at roughly KRW 30-33 billion.

Its direct competitors are unlisted companies, so like-for-like comparison with listed peers is limited. The company reportedly uses regional production sites to shorten delivery distances.

The new business sits with subsidiary People Multi. People Multi's main activities are developing and manufacturing sleep solutions plus radar and environmental sensors. People Multi launched an AI smart pillow and began selling it through Samsung.com.

Another subsidiary runs cliff-walkway projects, and the parent provides joint guarantees for defects and contracts on works that subsidiary has won. Although the company is classified under construction and building materials, its activities mix timber-structure construction with healthcare development.

03

Recent trends

Full-year 2025 results were reported on a standalone basis. Revenue was KRW 5.637 billion, operating profit KRW 952 million and net income KRW 2.107 billion, with an unqualified audit opinion. Earnings per share were positive, in line with the net profit. The implied operating margin is about 17%, so profitability of the core business improved.

The revenue trend is a gradual decline. A job-portal financial database shows revenue of KRW 9.42 billion in 2023 and KRW 5.63 billion in 2025. The same source shows 2025 operating profit up 51% from the previous year.

By contrast, Korea IR Service's 2023 report projected 2025 standalone revenue of KRW 21.4 billion, and the actual figure came in far below that.

Net income far exceeding operating profit points to the influence of non-operating items. The same portal data shows non-operating income of roughly KRW 3.8 billion for 2025. The nature of that income should be checked separately when judging earnings quality.

On the balance sheet, total assets at the end of 2025 were KRW 19,994 million, total liabilities KRW 12,942 million and total equity KRW 7,052 million. Dividing liabilities by equity gives roughly 184%. The subsidiary side carries more strain.

In a December 30, 2025 filing, JM-Multi acquired 100,000 People Multi shares for KRW 2 billion by converting existing loans to equity, giving it 100% ownership. People Multi reported revenue of KRW 207 million and a net loss of KRW 1.357 billion.

The stock was recently mentioned alongside a broader rally in building-materials shares. Pinpoint News named JM-Multi among several rising stocks in interior and finishing materials.

However, trading value at the time of writing was only KRW 373,400, so it is hard to judge whether the price reflects meaningful supply and demand.

04

Outlook

The core business depends on the public-order environment. With more than 90% of revenue coming from public institutions, local-government budgets for trails and eco-tourism are a key variable. Because operating profit rose in 2025 even as revenue fell, the next question is whether order volume recovers.

Reports describe an improved mood in the building-materials sector. An industry expert said stable raw-material prices alone are not enough, and that selling prices, utilization and logistics costs must also line up.

The same expert added that after a share-price rally, investors should check whether construction-site demand recovers and company-level margins actually improve. Because JM-Multi's direct competitors are unlisted, it is unclear whether listed-sector trends translate directly into its business results.

The new venture carries uncertainty over both cost burden and the timing of results. People Multi received a KRW 2 billion equity contribution to fund research, development and marketing aimed at securing the domestic healthcare market and expanding overseas.

The Samsung.com listing is a distribution milestone, but People Multi's revenue was only KRW 207 million for 2025.

On financials, the items to watch are whether the operating-profit improvement persists and whether the debt burden stays manageable. Since non-operating items heavily influenced net income, the composition of future earnings also needs checking.

With a large controlling stake, a quick increase in tradable shares looks limited. No plan to transfer to another market, such as KOSDAQ, has been confirmed.

05

Bull factors

Core profitability improved

Per job-portal data, 2025 operating profit rose 51% from the prior year. Operating profit reached KRW 952 million even though standalone revenue fell. A higher profit share of sales suggests cost and overhead management may have improved. If orders recover, there is room for the operating margin to improve further.

Leading domestic share

Korea IR Service estimated the market share at 10-15% and viewed the company as number one domestically. This is, however, an estimate from the July 2023 report. Most direct competitors are unlisted, and the company uses regional plants to improve its ability to handle volume. Its record of procurement-agency registrations and certifications helps with access to the public market.

Healthcare as an option

JM-Multi took part in People Multi's capital increase to fund R&D and marketing aimed at the domestic healthcare market and global expansion. People Multi listed an AI smart pillow on Samsung.com. This is cited as a business axis that could reduce reliance on public orders. Results are not yet visible in the numbers.

06

Bear factors

Falling revenue, missed projections

In job-portal data, revenue fell from KRW 9.42 billion in 2023 to KRW 5.63 billion in 2025. The gap between Korea IR Service's 2023 projection of KRW 21.4 billion for 2025 standalone revenue and the actual figure is large. This means top-line growth in the core business has not been confirmed.

Net income reliant on non-operating items

Standalone 2025 net income of KRW 2.107 billion is more than double operating profit of KRW 952 million. Job-portal data shows non-operating income of roughly KRW 3.8 billion, suggesting a large share of net income came from non-operating items. Whether such income recurs must be checked separately.

Subsidiary losses and funding

People Multi posted revenue of KRW 207 million and a net loss of KRW 1.357 billion. The parent put in KRW 2 billion by converting existing loans to equity. Losses are large relative to revenue, so funding pressure could persist.

An earlier report also showed a roughly KRW 1 billion stake purchase in a mobile-game company, so new-business investments have been spread across areas.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Construction conditions and regional fiscal capacity affect public orders. Interest rates and material prices influence costs and borrowing burden.

At the end of 2025, total liabilities of KRW 12,942 million were about 1.8 times total equity of KRW 7,052 million, so the company may be sensitive to changes in financial conditions.

Business

According to the July 2023 report, more than 90% of revenue comes from public institutions, so customer concentration is high. Cuts in local budgets or delayed orders could increase earnings volatility. Joint guarantees cover the works of a subsidiary, so defect or contract issues could pass through to the parent. The healthcare venture has yet to show results.

Liquidity and governance

CEO Park Hun-woong increased his holdings by 33,698 shares through on-market trades. The combined stake of Park and related parties was reported at 84.53%. Concentrated ownership leaves a small free float, trading value at the time of writing was only KRW 373,400, and even small trades can move the price sharply. As a KONEX stock, information disclosure and trading access are also limited.

08

Overall view

JM-Multi is a KONEX-listed company built on its wooden deck road business and, unlike its sector classification suggests, also runs a healthcare venture. On a standalone basis in 2025, revenue declined but operating profit rose, so profitability of the core business improved.

Against that, a large part of net income came from non-operating items, and revenue is well below the 2023 level.

The very high share of public-institution sales makes budget execution the main driver of earnings swings. Subsidiary People Multi has small revenue and large losses while funding continues to flow in. High ownership by the largest shareholder and extremely low trading value create clear liquidity constraints.

Reports describe an improved mood across the building-materials sector, but because the company's direct competitors are unlisted, whether sector trends translate into business results needs separate verification.
