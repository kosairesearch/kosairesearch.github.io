02

Business structure

A ONE Cubetech manufactures, sells and rents formwork products used in building construction, including aluminum forms, gang forms and euro forms.

According to the company's website, its core business is rental of construction temporary materials, and its introduced lineup centers on aluminum forms, gang forms and related products. Formwork is a temporary mold that shapes concrete while it cures, so demand moves with construction starts and project volume.

Rental generates revenue by reusing the same equipment across multiple sites, which makes utilization and asset turnover the key drivers of profitability.

Sales, by contrast, book larger one-off revenue but can see order gaps during an economic slowdown, so the mix between rental and sales shapes the range of earnings swings. The segment revenue split can be checked in the business-segment section of the DART annual report.

The exchange classifies the company under 'Others,' but its operations resemble construction-materials rental. Competition comes from small and mid-sized aluminum form makers and rental operators, where product specifications and on-site responsiveness serve as the main comparison points.

In March 2026 the company cited brand image and business diversification as the reasons for the name change. Details of any new business, including specific products and revenue scale, are limited in public sources, so the link to the existing formwork operations cannot yet be assessed on solid evidence.

Recent trends

On March 24, 2026, shareholders approved changing the Korean corporate name from A ONE ALFORM to A ONE Cubetech. The company disclosed the same day that the English corporate name will change from A ONE ALFORM CO., LTD to A ONE CUBETECH CO., LTD.

The new name took effect on March 30, 2026, and the company stated that the business registration number stays the same while only the business name changes. The company cited brand image improvement and business diversification as the reasons, and it had no name change in the preceding two years.

The stock has traded on KONEX for more than a decade since its listing on December 21, 2015. A Hankyung stock page shows a 52-week high of 1,138 won and a 52-week low of 700 won, placing the current price of 925 won near the middle of that range. The same page shows a 12-month change of -5.71 percent.

The market capitalization of about 6.4 billion won is consistent with 6,960,703 listed shares multiplied by the current price. Annual revenue mix and operating profit trends can be cross-checked in the DART annual and quarterly reports.

Recurring revenue from a rental-centered model

Formwork equipment can be rented repeatedly across multiple sites, so revenue can accumulate as long as utilization holds. Unlike one-off sales, the model builds up equipment assets, and the revenue base strengthens as rental contracts continue.

Existing rental contracts can cushion periods when construction orders temporarily decline. The size of this effect can be gauged through the rental share of revenue and utilization data.

English name alignment and business continuity

The English corporate name also changed to A ONE CUBETECH CO., LTD, so the new name will appear in external materials. The business registration number remains the same and only the business name changed, and the company guided that invoices will be issued under the new name.

Risk factors

Macro

Interest rate levels and the construction cycle are the largest external variables for formwork demand. A prolonged high-rate period can delay construction starts and new project orders, affecting rental utilization and sales orders.

Swings in raw material prices and labor costs also work indirectly through site construction costs and developer sentiment. These variables lie largely outside the company's control.

Execution

Execution risk around diversification is significant. If new products do not connect with existing formwork capabilities, returns on investment may be slow or modest. A name change alone does not alter operations, so shifts in the actual revenue mix will appear only in disclosures.

If capital goes into new businesses, the company must manage the financial burden alongside its existing rental equipment investment.

Market and liquidity

KONEX-listed companies generally have low trading liquidity, and information reaches the market relatively slowly. Given the characteristics of small caps, even modest trades can move prices. This structure can prolong periods when the share price diverges from the business picture. During quiet disclosure periods, the market has limited information to reference.
