02

Business structure

A ONE Cubetech is a small and medium-sized company founded on April 26, 2005. It manufactures, sells and leases building formwork, including aluminum forms, gang forms and Euro forms. Its headquarters and production base are in Goesan-eup, Goesan-gun, North Chungcheong Province.

It had 53 employees as of December 31, 2025. According to JobKorea's business breakdown, aluminum-form sales and related items account for 64%, aluminum-form rental 25%, TS rental 7% and porcelain panel sales 3%.

Because sales make up the majority, quarterly revenue can plausibly swing with the timing of site orders and deliveries. The company also exports its products to markets such as Libya and Singapore.

An earlier press report said that by aluminum-form rental revenue it ranked third behind two larger peers, and that rental prices began to fall after a new entrant arrived in 2015. At the March 24, 2026 general meeting it renamed itself from A ONE Alform to A ONE Cubetech, citing business diversification. The specific new businesses and their revenue contribution have not been confirmed.

Recent trends

For 2025 (the 21st fiscal term), standalone revenue was KRW 26,903 million, operating profit KRW 2,877 million and net profit KRW 2,979 million. Total assets were KRW 53,487 million, total liabilities KRW 16,613 million and total equity KRW 36,874 million.

According to JobKorea's financial data, in 2024 the company posted revenue of KRW 19,577 million, an operating loss of KRW 11,554 million and a net loss of KRW 14,808 million. In the same data, 2023 revenue was KRW 37,188 million and operating profit KRW 1,353 million.

The 2025 result therefore marks a return to profit after the large 2024 loss. In 2022 the company received a court decision to end its rehabilitation proceedings. The share price was KRW 928 on October 27, 2026, with a market capitalization of KRW 6.5 billion and trading value of zero.

Per Hankyung Korea Market, the 52-week high is KRW 1,138 and the low is KRW 700. The same site shows a 12-month change of -5.71% and 6,960,703 shares outstanding. Investing.com lists a three-month average volume of 40 shares, which shows how rarely the stock trades. Results for the first half of 2026 onward could not be confirmed.

Outlook

The construction sector, the key end market, is in a slow recovery. A KPI report said that in July 2026 the construction sector remained weak even as the wider economy improved on a semiconductor boom.

The same report noted that construction orders in April 2026 rose 39.3% from a year earlier, an improvement in a leading indicator. However, completed-work output is still falling year on year and building permit area has not fully stopped declining.

The Construction & Economy Research Institute of Korea forecast 2026 construction orders up 4.0% to KRW 231.2 trillion and construction investment up 2.0% to KRW 270.0 trillion.

A quarterly report from a listed aluminum-form peer for Q1 2026 shows aluminum-form exports of KRW 31,036 million against domestic sales of KRW 29,296 million, which suggests a rising weight of overseas demand.

A ONE Cubetech's export share and order backlog have not been confirmed, so it is hard to say whether the same pattern applies. An extraordinary general meeting to elect an independent director is scheduled for October 29, 2026, and can be read as a first signal of governance change.

Bull factors

2025 return to profit and a stable balance sheet

Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 2,877 million and net profit KRW 2,979 million. Total equity of KRW 36,874 million exceeds total liabilities of KRW 16,613 million. JobKorea's financial data show the debt ratio falling from 49.77% in 2023 and 62.84% in 2024 to 45.05% in 2025. Improved financial stability after the large 2024 loss is a positive.

Better order indicators and aluminum-form demand

Construction orders in April 2026 rose 39.3% from a year earlier. Orders act as a positive factor for future construction investment. Earlier industry coverage described aluminum forms as a method replacing steel formwork. If orders translate into construction starts, rental and sales volumes could benefit.

Overseas markets and a diversification attempt

The company exports to markets such as Libya and Singapore. JobKorea's company profile mentions efforts to open the Chinese and other overseas markets and to set up overseas branches. Business diversification was cited as a reason for the name change. The aim of reducing reliance on domestic construction leaves room for improvement in business metrics.

Bear factors

A history of volatile earnings

According to JobKorea's financial data, revenue fell from KRW 37,188 million in 2023 to KRW 19,577 million in 2024, with an operating loss of KRW 11,554 million. Whether the 2025 profit will continue has not been confirmed. With a high sales share, the presence or absence of large sites can weigh heavily on results. A single profitable year is not enough to judge earnings stability.

Intense competition and smaller scale

An earlier report said that the company ranked third by rental revenue, and that rental prices began to fall after a new entrant arrived. A listed peer's aluminum-form sales for Q1 2026 alone were KRW 60.3 billion, exceeding A ONE Cubetech's full-year 2025 revenue of KRW 26,903 million. The scale gap could be a disadvantage in pricing power and overseas production footprint.

Thin liquidity on Konex

Trading value is zero and market capitalization is KRW 6.5 billion. Investing.com lists a three-month average volume of 40 shares. Even small orders can face empty order books, which lowers the reliability of quoted prices. The limited investor base on Konex is often cited as a background to the weak trading.

07

Risk factors

Macro

The direction of the construction cycle depends on whether orders convert into starts and completed work, and on whether cost pressure from high oil prices and material costs eases. Construction investment in Q1 2026 fell 1.4% from a year earlier.

A worsening in interest-rate or property-financing conditions could prolong start delays. That would weigh on aluminum-form rental and sales demand.

Industry

The main raw materials for aluminum forms are ingots and scrap. A sharp rise in aluminum prices could squeeze sales margins and may be hard to pass on in rental rates. The rental market has seen price competition since new entrants arrived. Tighter safety rules could also delay site schedules.

Company and market

The company has a history of court-supervised rehabilitation that ended in 2022. Results swung from a large loss in 2024 to a profit in 2025. Because trading is sparse, selling or buying shares can cause large price impact. The new businesses have not been disclosed in detail, so the efficiency of any investment is hard to judge.

08

Overall view

A ONE Cubetech is a small formwork maker centered on aluminum forms, and it improved its balance sheet by returning to profit in 2025. Standalone revenue that year was KRW 26,903 million and operating profit KRW 2,877 million. By contrast, 2024 brought an operating loss of KRW 11,554 million, so earnings swung widely.

Construction order indicators are improving, but completed work and permits are weak, and the prevailing assessment is that recovery is gradual. Thin Konex liquidity and the unclear scope of new businesses should be factored into any assessment.

Items to follow include results from the first half of 2026 onward, the outcome of the October 29, 2026 extraordinary general meeting, and whether exports and new businesses take concrete shape.
