02

Business structure

Enbioco was founded in 1997 to manufacture and sell agricultural chemicals and listed on KOSDAQ in 2020. Its core products are a lime-sulfur formulation, a sulfur-based eco-friendly crop protection product, a broad-spectrum insecticide, and fungicide, insecticide and herbicide lines plus fertilizers.

The company says it has developed and registered 182 products over the past ten years and is expanding labor-saving formulations and eco-friendly crop protectants. Domestic sales run mainly through the Nonghyup agricultural cooperative system and local agricultural wholesalers, supported by 10 domestic branches.

A supply contract with Nonghyup Economic Group signed on February 13, 2026 has a contract value of KRW 9.31 billion and runs through December 31, 2026. The company said first-half 2026 Nonghyup channel sales rose 71% year on year, as announced on August 13, 2026.

Overseas, herbicide exports rose 567% and fungicide exports rose 153% in the same period, according to the company.

In new drugs, Enbioco plans to start full-scale commercialization in 2026 of a fungicide drug candidate selected with the Korea Research Institute of Chemical Technology under a Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy project running since 2020.

The company also plans to expand into supplying active ingredients to other producers. According to company statements from 2024, the global fungicide market is led by Syngenta, Corteva, Bayer and BASF, and resistance to existing substances is raising demand for new drugs.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue on the filings was KRW 34.2 billion in 2022, KRW 34 billion in 2023 (down 0.6%), KRW 35.7 billion in 2024 (up 4.8%) and KRW 34 billion in 2025 (down 4.8%), with 2024 the largest year and 2025 the smallest in the period.

Operating results were an operating loss of KRW 4.78 billion in 2022 and KRW 3.35 billion in 2023, a continued loss with a narrower gap, before a turn to operating profit of KRW 1.41 billion in 2024. Operating profit then fell to KRW 917 million in 2025, down 34.7%.

The operating margin was -14.0% in 2022, -9.9% in 2023, 3.9% in 2024 and 2.7% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 3.44 billion in 2022 and a loss of KRW 6.3 billion in 2023, which widened the loss, before turning to a profit of KRW 646 million in 2024.

It rose to KRW 1.01 billion in 2025, up 56.0%, moving in the opposite direction from operating profit. Quarterly revenue went from KRW 3.94 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 7.66 billion in Q4 2025 (up 94.7% from the prior quarter) and peaked at KRW 14.1 billion in Q1 2026.

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 3.13 billion in Q1 2026, but Q2 2026 operating profit was only KRW 172 million (down 94.5% from the prior quarter).

In Q3 2026, revenue was KRW 4.23 billion (down 59.5% from the prior quarter, up 7.5% year on year) and operating loss was KRW 1.93 billion, a switch to loss and a wider loss year on year.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.5 billion in Q3 2026, the second straight quarter of losses since Q2 2026.

For Q1 2026, a WiseReport company summary said revenue rose slightly on steady sales of core product lines, while operating profit eased slightly on higher costs for formulation products and new drug development.

05

Industry analysis

The crop protection (agrochemical) market where Enbioco operates is a small specialty niche even within the chemicals sector. According to company statements from 2024, the global fungicide market is about USD 20.4 billion, and resistance to existing substances is increasing the need for new drugs.

The company says food shortages from global warming and a rise in invasive pests are increasing demand for new products. In Korea, sales run mainly through the Nonghyup agricultural cooperative system, so terms of supply contracts with sales channels can affect quarterly revenue swings.

Large petrochemical and materials peers such as LG Chem and Lotte Chemical have different business structures, which limits direct comparison. With revenue of about KRW 34 billion in 2025, Enbioco is far smaller than large chemical groups, so its results are relatively more sensitive to swings.

In exports, herbicide and fungicide shipments grew sharply in the first half of 2026, though high growth rates can be affected by a low base in the prior year. In new products, labor-saving formulations and eco-friendly crop protectants are presented as the company's growth pillars.

In a market led by large global players, Enbioco is building its position on its Nonghyup channel and export products.

Outlook

The company's stated 2026 target is Nonghyup channel revenue of KRW 12 billion or more. First-half 2026 Nonghyup channel sales rose 71% year on year, and first-half revenue of KRW 24.59 billion (up 10.0%) and operating profit of KRW 3.3 billion (up 10.5%) were announced preliminarily on August 13, 2026.

The first-half totals match the sum of the Q1 and Q2 2026 quarterly results on the filings. However, Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 4.23 billion was down 59.5% from the prior quarter and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.93 billion, so the second half diverged from the first.

The Nonghyup supply contract signed on February 13, 2026 ends on December 31, 2026, so terms after year-end are the next point to watch. In new drugs, the fungicide candidate is moving toward commercialization preparations in 2026, and expanding active ingredient supply is presented as a medium-term strategy.

The timing of commercialization and its revenue contribution have not yet been disclosed in figures. In March 2026, CEO Lee Myung-jae said he would expand share buybacks, citing responsible management and shareholder value.

The 2026 annual report will confirm full-year Nonghyup sales and Q4 results, and whether the first-half growth carried through to year-end.

Thin operating margin and falling revenue

Rising costs and drug investment burden

Risk factors

Channel concentration

Sales run mainly through the Nonghyup system, so the contract period ending December 31, 2026 could shape the revenue trend. Renewal terms and whether the KRW 12 billion or more target is reached could widen swings in annual results. The full-year scale of overseas sales is also a point for the annual report.

Drug development costs and commercialization delay

Commercialization preparations for the fungicide candidate began in 2026, yet development costs have already shown up as a factor in operating profit swings. A delayed launch could prolong cost pressure and slow the earnings recovery. Whether product launch and active ingredient supply proceed as planned is the key medium-term risk.

Base effects in export growth and quarterly volatility

Herbicide and fungicide exports rose 567% and 153% in the first half of 2026, but such high growth rates can reflect a low base and may not repeat at the same pace. Quarterly revenue moved from KRW 3.94 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 14.1 billion in Q1 2026. This volatility lowers the predictability of annual results.

Overall view

Enbioco moved from an operating loss in 2022 to operating profit in 2024, an improvement in annual results. However, revenue and operating profit fell in 2025, and quarterly revenue declined for two straight quarters after peaking in Q1 2026.

The company recorded an operating loss of KRW 1.93 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.5 billion in Q3 2026, widening losses.

The company's preliminary announcement that Nonghyup channel and herbicide and fungicide exports drove first-half growth is consistent with the sum of the quarterly filings, so it is a useful reference.

Yet first-half strength did not carry into the second half, making revenue after the Nonghyup contract ends the key variable. Drug commercialization preparations and share buybacks are presented as medium-term and shareholder return elements, but their revenue contribution has not yet been disclosed in figures.

Valuation shows a discount to net assets alongside a high earnings-based multiple, and the reading depends on whether the earnings recovery lasts. The next checkpoints are the full-year results in the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
