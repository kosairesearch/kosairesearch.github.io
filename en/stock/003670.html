Report overview

Battery Materials Recovery: Anode and LFP Expansion Are the Key

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 82.1 billion is the largest of the last five disclosed quarters, yet revenue has fallen for two straight years since the 2023 peak and 2024 operating profit was only KRW 721 million.

  1. 1

    Operating profit reached KRW 82.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 207.8% from the prior quarter, and has risen for two consecutive quarters since the KRW 17.7 billion low in the first quarter of 2026.

  2. 2

    The battery materials segment posted operating profit of KRW 51.8 billion in the third quarter, supported by a cathode sales recovery, higher selling prices and customer compensation, according to reports on the November 2, 2026 disclosure. Anode sales to overseas customers rose despite an inventory valuation loss.

  3. 3

    After an attributable net loss of KRW 212.3 billion in 2024, the company returned to an attributable net profit of KRW 32.3 billion in 2025, and posted KRW 42.4 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

02

Business structure

POSCO Future M reports two business segments. Battery materials, which covers cathode and anode materials, generated KRW 459.6 billion of revenue and KRW 51.8 billion of operating profit in the third quarter of 2026, more than half of total revenue, according to preliminary figures disclosed on November 2, 2026.

Within that segment, cathode revenue was KRW 432.3 billion and anode revenue KRW 27.3 billion. The cathode business is shifting from nickel-rich NCM toward LFP, and part of the Pohang cathode line has been converted to LFP production for energy storage and entry-level EV demand.

In anodes, the company produces both natural and artificial graphite, and its Pohang artificial graphite plant has annual capacity of 8,000 tonnes. In March 2026 it signed a contract worth KRW 1.01 trillion to supply artificial graphite anodes to a global automaker.

The basic materials segment, which covers lime and hwasung by-product treatment as well as refractories and plant services, posted revenue of KRW 375.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 30.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Lime and hwasung revenue rose 29.2% year on year to KRW 273.7 billion, while refractories and plant revenue fell 21.6% to KRW 101.5 billion. The company describes itself as the only domestic producer of both cathode and anode materials.

Competitors include domestic cathode makers such as Ecopro BM and L&F, while Chinese anode producers have pressured prices.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results turned after the 2023 peak. Revenue rose from KRW 3.3 trillion in 2022 to KRW 4.76 trillion in 2023, a 44.2% increase, then fell 22.3% to KRW 3.7 trillion in 2024 and 20.6% to KRW 2.94 trillion in 2025, so revenue has declined for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

Among the years 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest and 2025 the smallest. Operating profit fell from KRW 165.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 35.9 billion in 2023, a 78.4% decline, and then to KRW 721 million in 2024, a 98.0% decline, with operating margin moving from 5.0% to 0.8% and then 0.0%.

It recovered to KRW 32.8 billion in 2025, up 4,451.5%, although the operating margin was only 1.1%. Attributable net profit was KRW 118.3 billion in 2022 and KRW 28.7 billion in 2023, a 75.7% decline.

The company then posted an attributable net loss of KRW 212.3 billion in 2024, turning from profit to loss, before returning to an attributable net profit of KRW 32.3 billion in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests followed a different path, at KRW 4.44 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 231.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 36.5 billion in 2025. Quarterly figures swing more sharply.

Revenue went from KRW 874.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 557.6 billion in the fourth quarter, down 36.3% quarter on quarter, and reached KRW 834.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Operating profit of KRW 66.7 billion in the third quarter of 2025 became an operating loss of KRW 51.8 billion in the fourth quarter, then an operating profit of KRW 17.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 26.7 billion in the second quarter and KRW 82.1 billion in the third quarter.

The third-quarter figure far exceeded the FnGuide consensus of KRW 19.4 billion, and the operating margin was 9.8%. Customer compensation for volume shortfalls supported the gain, and whether such items recur is a point to check in later quarters.

Attributable net profit moved from a loss of KRW 23.5 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.66 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 23.1 billion in the second quarter and KRW 42.4 billion in the third quarter.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 4.51 trillion, of which attributable equity was KRW 4.07 trillion, and total liabilities were KRW 4.63 trillion. Operating cash flow was -KRW 61 billion in 2022, -KRW 444.8 billion in 2023, KRW 670.9 billion in 2024 and -KRW 33.6 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from the battery sector is shifting from electric vehicles toward energy storage systems (ESS). IBK Investment & Securities, in a report dated November 4, 2026, estimated that potential LFP cathode demand for ESS from Korea's three battery makers would rise from 230,000 tonnes in 2027 to 350,000 tonnes in 2030.

The same report forecast total domestic cathode shipments above 100,000 tonnes next year, with LFP at least half. A media report from April 2026 said a recovery in cathode shipments was delayed by the halt of a major customer's operations and weak North American EV demand.

A Hanwha Investment & Securities analyst at the time said the cathode segment would inevitably post losses in the first half. In the third quarter of 2026, cathode revenue rose quarter on quarter as sales to existing customers recovered.

In anodes, Chinese producers continue to press prices, while overseas customer volumes have increased.

The United States delayed application of foreign entity of concern (FEOC) rules to graphite used in anodes until the end of 2026, yet automakers are reportedly moving quickly to secure non-Chinese graphite and anode supply.

A Hana Securities analyst said POSCO Future M is virtually the only non-Chinese company with a large long-term anode expansion plan (June 2026 report). In cycle terms, cathodes are at the early stage of a shipment recovery, while anodes are building contracts against a backdrop of supply-chain realignment.

06

Outlook

The company has set out three lines of business milestones. The first is LFP cathode.

Part of the Pohang cathode line has been converted to LFP, and according to an IBK Investment & Securities report the converted line has annual capacity of about 20,000 tonnes, with initial mass production and shipments planned for the fourth quarter of 2026.

The company plans to add more converted Pohang lines and reach 105,000 tonnes of in-house LFP capacity, with up to 50,000 tonnes more from a joint venture to serve long-term supply contracts. An LFP-dedicated plant at the Yeongilman industrial complex is being built with mass production targeted for 2027.

The second is anode. The company plans a KRW 357 billion artificial graphite anode plant in Thai Nguyen, Vietnam, with construction starting in the second half of 2026 and mass production targeted for 2028, on a site that can expand to 55,000 tonnes.

However, the IBK report puts phase-one production and sales in the second half of 2027, so reported timelines differ. Separately, the company signed a contract worth KRW 1.01 trillion in March 2026, and media reports say talks with additional customers are ongoing. The third is next-generation materials.

Silicon anode mass production is targeted for 2028, and the company is developing cathode materials for all-solid-state batteries with a Japanese automaker. In its third-quarter 2026 disclosure materials, the company also said it is developing cathode and anode materials for sodium-ion batteries in parallel.

IBK Investment & Securities expects additional fourth-quarter volumes to accelerate revenue growth from 2027. The next checkpoints are whether LFP mass production begins by the end of 2026, and when the Vietnam plant starts construction and phase-one operations.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly profit recovery

Operating profit rose for two consecutive quarters, from KRW 17.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 26.7 billion in the second and KRW 82.1 billion in the third. The third-quarter operating margin of 9.8% was up 2.2 percentage points from a year earlier. Cathode sales recovery and higher selling prices were reported as the main drivers.

Long-term anode contracts and supply-chain realignment

Following a KRW 671 billion natural anode contract in October 2025, the company signed a long-term artificial anode supply deal worth about KRW 1.01 trillion in March 2026. As customers seek non-Chinese anode supply, the Vietnam plant has been presented as a means to secure cost competitiveness and supply stability.

A Hana Securities analyst called it virtually the only non-Chinese company with a large long-term anode expansion plan.

LFP cathode and ESS demand growth

IBK Investment & Securities estimated that potential LFP cathode demand for ESS from Korea's three battery makers would rise from 230,000 tonnes in 2027 to 350,000 tonnes in 2030. The company plans to reach 105,000 tonnes of LFP capacity by combining converted Pohang lines with the new Yeongilman plant. A report dated October 30, 2026 said supply to LG Energy Solution has also been secured.

Bear factors

Two years of falling revenue

Revenue peaked at KRW 4.76 trillion in 2023 and then fell to KRW 3.7 trillion in 2024 and KRW 2.94 trillion in 2025.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 834.8 billion was down 4.6% year on year, and cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 also fell 4.6% (preliminary, per the November 2, 2026 disclosure reports).

Delayed cathode recovery and customer concentration

An April 2026 media report said cathode shipment recovery was being delayed by the halt of a major customer's operations and weak North American EV demand. Third-quarter cathode revenue of KRW 432.3 billion was 14.9% below KRW 508 billion a year earlier. The gain also includes customer compensation, so whether it recurs needs to be tested.

Anode price pressure and regulatory variables

In anodes, sales volumes rose despite Chinese price cuts, but the third-quarter inventory valuation loss left the segment's profit weaker than in the prior quarter. The FEOC rule is delayed until the end of 2026, so whether it applies afterward remains an open variable. Analysts also point to the less aggressive pace of US tariffs on Chinese anodes as a short-term factor.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Operating profit fell from KRW 165.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 721 million in 2024 before recovering to KRW 32.8 billion in 2025 and KRW 82.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Operating cash flow was negative in every year except 2024, at -KRW 61 billion in 2022, -KRW 444.8 billion in 2023 and -KRW 33.6 billion in 2025. Quarterly results are sensitive to items such as compensation and inventory valuation losses.

Balance sheet and investment burden

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 4.63 trillion, close to total equity of KRW 4.51 trillion. The debt ratio fell from 142.6% in 2023 to 102.7% in 2025 but remains above the 75.0% of 2022. The Vietnam plant at about KRW 357 billion and the LFP conversion could strain financial flexibility at the same time. The company issued KRW 250 billion of unsecured bonds in early 2026.

Policy and customer concentration

US prohibited foreign entity (PFE) requirements, FEOC rules and weaker North American EV demand directly affect customer utilization and volumes. An April 2026 report noted that a major customer's halt delayed cathode shipment recovery. Tighter rules would benefit non-Chinese supply, but a loosening could reduce that advantage.

12

Overall view

POSCO Future M's third-quarter 2026 results, with operating profit of KRW 82.1 billion, reached the highest level of the last five disclosed quarters, helped by a cathode sales recovery and customer compensation.

Annually, however, revenue has declined for one consecutive year since the 2023 peak, and in 2024 operating profit was KRW 721 million with an attributable net loss of KRW 212.3 billion.

The return to profit in 2025 and the quarterly recovery in 2026 are read positively, but the 2025 annual operating margin of 1.1% is still low. Bullish factors include the long-term anode contracts, the non-Chinese supply chain via the Vietnam plant and rising ESS demand for LFP cathodes.

Bearish factors include delayed cathode shipment recovery, customer concentration, Chinese price pressure on anodes and financial strain from a debt ratio above 100%.

Market capitalization is large relative to the last four quarters of attributable net profit, so the earnings multiple is high, and interpretation depends on how quickly LFP and anode expansion converts into reported earnings.

The next checkpoints are the start of LFP mass production by the end of 2026, the Vietnam plant's construction start, and the fourth-quarter and full-year operating margins in the 2026 annual report.
