Demand for high-performance panels grows as losses persist

02

Business structure

SY is a building materials company whose business centers on sandwich panels and exterior panels.

The main products it showcased at a recent trade show include Hidden Metal Panel, seismic and fire-resistant mineral wool panels, solar roof panels, slim brick rail systems, deck plates, an impact-noise reduction system, semi-noncombustible urethane insulation, and printed steel sheets.

Among them, Hidden Metal Panel minimizes the exposure of panel joints to sharpen the exterior design, while the mineral wool panel is specified for data centers with one to two hours of fire resistance.

The solar roof products SSR 400 and FL Roof System combine roof panels with solar modules so that power equipment can be installed without losing the roof's basic function. The slim brick rail system fits bricks into aluminum rails using a dry method, improving ease and cost of construction.

A subsidiary also handles deck plates and the SY Sonix impact-noise reduction system. In 2024 the company newly established SY Green Energy and SY Cosmetics, and in 2025 it absorbed SY Chemical and SY Coning through merger.

Media reports say SY supplied high-performance panels at scale to a Samsung Electronics P5 site and took exterior works at large industrial facilities such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics, along with additional orders from a global big tech data center project.

At the annual shareholder meeting on March 27, 2026, real estate development and supply, iron ore and nonferrous mineral wholesale, and import-export were added to the business purpose through an amendment to the articles of incorporation.

In competition, the company positions products that pair function with appearance, such as concealed panel joints, fire and seismic specifications, and solar-integrated roofs, as its differentiators.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 537.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 553.7 billion in 2023 (up 3.1% year on year) and KRW 561 billion in 2024 (up 1.3%), then fell to KRW 501.6 billion in 2025 (down 10.6%). Over 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 14 billion in 2022, KRW 13.6 billion in 2023 (down 3.3%), and KRW 21.9 billion in 2024 (up 61.8%), so the company was profitable through 2024 before swinging to an operating loss of KRW 6.63 billion in 2025.

The operating margin moved from 2.6% in 2022 and 2.4% in 2023 to 3.9% in 2024 and -1.3% in 2025.

Attributable net income of KRW 8.1 billion in 2022 turned into an attributable net loss of KRW 9.03 billion in 2023, returned to a profit of KRW 3.43 billion in 2024, and then became an attributable net loss of KRW 14.1 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests is a different measure, and it was a net loss of KRW 15.9 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 116.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 132.7 billion in the fourth quarter (up 13.9% from the prior quarter), dipped to KRW 126.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 4.8%), climbed to KRW 152.4 billion in the second quarter (up 20.7%), and reached KRW 149.1 billion in the third quarter (down 2.2% from the prior quarter but up 27.9% year on year).

Quarterly operating losses widened from KRW 954 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 3.58 billion in the fourth quarter, narrowed to KRW 1.21 billion and KRW 1.01 billion in the first and second quarters of 2026, and widened again to KRW 2.18 billion in the third quarter.

In several quarters revenue grew while the loss moved in the opposite direction, so sales growth has not translated directly into better operating results.

Attributable net losses were KRW 5.09 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, KRW 3.39 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 3.24 billion in the second quarter, and KRW 5.15 billion in the third quarter, totaling KRW 16.9 billion over the last four quarters.

The cumulative explanation for the first nine months of 2025 cited weaker panel demand and a construction slowdown for lower sales, and higher raw material prices and fixed costs for weaker profitability.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 29.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.85 billion in 2023 and KRW 7.84 billion in 2024, and was -KRW 15.3 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 247 billion, of which KRW 173.8 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 230.1 billion, with the debt ratio falling from 121.3% in 2024 to 93.2% in 2025.

Industry analysis

SY is classified under the steel and metals sector, but its business centers on building materials such as exterior construction panels and insulation. Recent trends in the construction market point in two directions.

A cumulative explanation for the first nine months of 2025 cited weaker panel demand and a construction slowdown as factors that pulled sales down. By contrast, a summary of first-quarter 2026 results noted that tighter regulation is driving demand for high-performance panels.

The company names data centers, advanced industrial facilities, and eco-friendly construction as its target markets, which aligns with a shift in demand from general construction toward more demanding facilities.

It is hard to pin down the position in the cycle, but the company's product mix appears to be shifting toward high-performance, higher-value products during a construction slowdown. On the raw material side, rising raw material prices were cited as a factor that weighed on 2025 profitability.

Compared with steel producers in the same sector such as Hyundai Steel and Dongkuk Steel Mill, SY places more weight on fabricated construction panels and construction solutions than on steel production.

The most direct demand variables are the pace of construction orders and data center investment, and if these move in different directions, the sales mix could shift as well.

Reported high-performance panel supply to a Samsung Electronics P5 site and exterior works at large industrial facilities are cases that show whether demand from advanced industries is reaching revenue.

Operating losses narrowed to KRW 1.21 billion and KRW 1.01 billion in the first and second quarters of 2026 before widening to KRW 2.18 billion in the third quarter, so the timing of any change in the quarterly direction of profit and loss is worth tracking.
