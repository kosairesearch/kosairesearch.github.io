02

Business structure

Curacle is a drug developer that treats endothelial dysfunction as a core disease mechanism and aims to normalize vascular function.

Its technology rests on two platforms: the SOLVADYS small-molecule discovery platform and the EAGLES antibody discovery platform, which together support both oral small molecules and antibody drugs. The main small-molecule assets are CU01 and CU06.

CU01 is an oral diabetic kidney disease candidate based on dimethyl fumarate (DMF), developed under a drug repositioning strategy that applies an existing ingredient to a new indication. CU06 is an oral endothelial dysfunction blocker targeting diabetic macular edema and is described as a first-in-class candidate.

On the antibody side, MT-103, a retinal disease bispecific antibody co-developed with Mabtics, was licensed to the U.S. company Memento in May 2026. The pipeline also includes MT-101 for kidney injury, MT-201 and MT-202 for antithrombotic use, and MT-40X in early oncology research.

In the Mabtics collaboration, development proceeds are split equally between the two companies. On November 24, 2026, Curacle signed an MOU with Mabtics and Pinobio for antibody and ADC-based anticancer drug development. Revenue tends to swing sharply from quarter to quarter depending on deal timing.

04

Earnings analysis

Reported annual results peaked in revenue in 2023 and have since shown a persistent loss pattern. Revenue rose from KRW 3.58 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.3 billion in 2023 (up 187.3% year on year), then fell 84.1% to KRW 1.63 billion in 2024 and 99.6% to KRW 7.1 million in 2025, marking two consecutive years of decline.

Operating losses were KRW 12 billion in 2022, KRW 10.5 billion in 2023 (losses narrowed), KRW 12.6 billion in 2024 (losses widened), and KRW 16.5 billion in 2025 (losses widened), a four-year run of operating losses from 2022 through 2025.

The operating margin was -232,393.8% in 2025, showing how far losses outran a very small revenue base. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 11.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.6 billion in 2023, KRW 14.9 billion in 2024, and KRW 18.1 billion in 2025.

Net loss including non-controlling interests followed the same amounts. The debt ratio rose from 20.5% in 2022 to 97.9% in 2024 before falling to 43.5% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 37.1 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 16.1 billion.

Operating cash flow was negative every year: -KRW 11.2 billion in 2022, -KRW 9.39 billion in 2023, -KRW 9.51 billion in 2024, and -KRW 13.4 billion in 2025. Quarterly results are volatile. Revenue for the third and fourth quarters of 2025 was not disclosed in the regular reports.

Revenue was KRW 1.05 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 7.18 billion in the second quarter (up 584.9% quarter on quarter), and KRW 891 million in the third quarter (down 87.6%). The second-quarter revenue spike falls in the same quarter as the May 2026 MT-103 licensing deal.

That quarter also swung to an operating profit of KRW 2.36 billion and a net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.23 billion.

The third quarter returned to an operating loss of KRW 2.67 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.85 billion, though the loss was smaller than a year earlier.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 7.45 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The retinal disease market is led by injected therapies such as Eylea and Vabysmo. Because the burden of repeated intraocular injections remains, demand for oral treatments is often cited.

Curacle positions CU06 as the first oral endothelial dysfunction blocker in its class, and some industry observers argue the market could be larger if patients who avoid or hesitate over injections are included. However, injected and oral drugs run different trial designs, so vision gains cannot be compared directly.

A 2022 analysis of real-world data on existing anti-VEGF injections reported an average gain of about 5 letters at one year. Curacle's Phase 2a CU06 figure comes from a 12-week subgroup of 67 patients, so it cannot be matched directly against that benchmark.

In kidney disease, CU01 targets patients whose proteinuria remains poorly controlled despite RAAS inhibitors and SGLT-2 inhibitors. This is a segment where a relatively large share of patients still see disease progression, and it is cited as a market with substantial unmet need.

The antithrombotic market is led by Eliquis and Xarelto, but bleeding risk is a recognized limit, which keeps competition for next-generation drugs intense. Against that backdrop, MT-201 and MT-202 are being developed with the aim of reducing bleeding side effects.

06

Outlook

The nearest company milestone is the U.S. Phase 2b IND for CU06. Curacle submitted the IND to the FDA on November 20, 2026, and said it would be automatically approved one month after filing if the FDA raises no objection.

The trial will enroll 156 patients with diabetic macular edema, with central macular involvement and best-corrected visual acuity of 25 to 69 letters. Patients will be randomized to CU06 200mg, CU06 300mg, or placebo and dosed once daily for 24 weeks.

The primary endpoint is the change in best-corrected visual acuity at week 24. Curacle said in February 2026 it received FDA feedback supporting a switch of best-corrected visual acuity, a secondary endpoint in Phase 2a, to the primary endpoint. Syneos Health will run the trial operations.

The company aims to verify the vision gains and safety seen in Phase 2a and select the dose for Phase 3. For CU01, the company is refining its development plan, including follow-on trials and regulatory strategy, based on the Phase 2b data. Memento targets entry into a global Phase 1 trial for MT-103 in 2027.

Shinhan Investment expected licensing talks on follow-on candidates such as MT-101 and CU01 to accelerate alongside MT-103's global Phase 1 entry.

Under the ADC MOU with Mabtics and Pinobio, the partners will first assess whether ADC technology can be applied to MT-40X and other candidates, with specific projects and timelines to be set in later discussions.

Bull factors

MT-103 licensing completed

In May 2026 global rights to MT-103 were licensed to the U.S. company Memento. Reported deal values differ between KRW 1.5 trillion and KRW 1.6 trillion, with an upfront payment reported at about KRW 11.6 billion. Memento then raised a USD 93 million Series A and targets global Phase 1 entry in 2027. For Curacle, the deal is an actual contract showing its antibody licensing capability.

Statistically significant Phase 2b for CU01

In a 240-patient, 24-week Phase 2b trial in diabetic kidney disease, CU01 reduced uACR versus placebo by 21.45% at 240mg and 22.21% at 360mg, with statistical significance in both dose groups. eGFR was maintained from baseline.

In patients whose proteinuria remained poorly controlled on standard therapy, add-on dosing reduced uACR by up to 35.20%. New-use patents have been filed in Korea, the U.S., China, and Japan, with a PCT application underway.

Broad pipeline and shared development

The independent research firm ARIS has noted that Curacle holds 16 pipeline assets in total. MT-101 targets acute kidney injury, an area with no approved therapy. In the Mabtics collaboration, development proceeds are split equally. The November 2026 MOU with Mabtics and Pinobio extends antibody assets into ADC-based oncology research.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 18.1 billion in the same year. The pattern shows losses growing on a thin revenue base.

Persistent operating cash outflow

The debt ratio fell from 97.9% in 2024 to 43.5% in 2025, but operating cash outflows continue to draw down the equity base.

Large swings in quarterly results

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 7.45 billion over the last four quarters. Because quarterly profit appears tied to deal timing, the sustainability of earnings has not been tested.

Risk factors

Clinical and regulatory

The U.S. Phase 2b for CU06 depends on the IND taking effect automatically. If the FDA raises objections, the timeline could slip.

The trial design switches the primary endpoint to best-corrected visual acuity based on a Phase 2a subgroup, so whether the same vision gains replicate in a 156-patient Phase 2b will only be known from results.

CU01's follow-on trial and regulatory path are still being set, and a change in regulatory direction could alter development plans.

Licensing and partner execution

Reported total deal value for MT-103 is in the mid-KRW trillions, but the upfront payment was reported at about KRW 11.6 billion. Remaining payments depend on development and approval milestones, so Memento's progress into clinical Phase 1 affects when they are received.

The ADC MOU with Mabtics and Pinobio has no defined projects or timelines yet. Licensing talks for MT-101 and CU01 have no set timing.

Funding

Curacle has said it will complete the CU06 Phase 2b on its own, through additional licensing or investment, with available funds of roughly KRW 20 billion. If cash outflows continue and licensing is delayed, the company may need new financing. The size and terms of any such financing have not been announced.

Overall view

In 2026 Curacle showed the potential of its business model through licensing and clinical progress. The MT-103 deal confirms its antibody licensing capability, and the CU01 Phase 2b reported a statistically significant reduction in uACR.

On the reported financials, however, revenue fell to KRW 7.1 million in 2025, and operating losses and cash outflows have continued for four consecutive years. The second-quarter 2026 swing to profit was followed by a third-quarter loss, so the durability of quarterly earnings is still untested.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio sits in a premium range, so expectations for pipeline outcomes are already reflected in the price.

The items to confirm are the automatic approval status of the CU06 IND, the start of the 156-patient Phase 2b, Memento's 2027 Phase 1 entry, and the full-year earnings and cash flow in the 2026 annual report.
