02

Business structure

CQV is known as a KOSDAQ chemicals company whose core business is pearlescent pigments, with automotive applications as its main line. Based on the 2Q 2026 sales mix compiled by data platform Butler, automotive accounted for 45%, cosmetics 35%, industrial 20%, and aluminum paste and others 1%.

Automotive and cosmetics together make up 80% of sales, so order flows in those two industries pass quickly into revenue.

The company is building colored aluminum and aluminum paste as new businesses to respond to home appliance market trends, and is developing products that combine pearlescent and aluminum paste technologies.

Total holdings of the largest shareholder and related parties stand at 50.87%, with the latest change recorded on October 1, 2025. The parent is GNMI, which also has a strategic business alliance with CQV.

In August 2025 GNMI said it had completed the acquisition of Merck's Surface Solution business (SuSo) for a total price of EUR 665 million, roughly KRW 1 trillion. The acquired unit was renamed SUSONITY and became a wholly owned subsidiary of GNMI.

The company expects the deal to complete a portfolio from low-priced to ultra-premium products and to widen its global sales network and production footprint. How much of this shows up in CQV's own sales mix is a point to confirm in the 2026 annual report's segment disclosure.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 46.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 51.1 billion in 2023 (up 10.4% year on year) and KRW 60.1 billion in 2024 (up 17.7%), then fell 6.0% to KRW 56.5 billion in 2025. Across 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 6.51 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.03 billion in 2023 (down 22.7%), then more than doubled to KRW 10.2 billion in 2024 (up 103.0%) and held nearly flat in 2025 (down 0.4%).

The operating margin was 14.1% in 2022, 9.8% in 2023, 17.0% in 2024 and 18.0% in 2025, so it rose in 2025 even as sales declined. The drivers behind that margin change are a point to check in the annual report's segment and product explanations.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.38 billion in 2022, KRW 3.43 billion in 2023 (down 36.4%), KRW 9.26 billion in 2024 (up 170.5%) and KRW 9.45 billion in 2025 (up 2.0%), rising in 2024 and 2025 after the 2023 low.

Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same yearly amounts. Operating cash flow was KRW 9.89 billion in 2022, KRW 9.03 billion in 2023, KRW 13.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.2 billion in 2025, peaking in 2024. The debt ratio fell to 22.6% at end-2025 from 30.9% in 2024.

Quarterly sales dropped to KRW 11.8 billion in Q4 2025 with operating profit of KRW 1.48 billion, before recovering to KRW 15.8 billion in sales and KRW 3.02 billion in operating profit in Q1 2026 (operating profit up 104.0% quarter on quarter).

Q2 2026 sales of KRW 20.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.54 billion (up 83.4% quarter on quarter) were the highest of the last five quarters.

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 15.4 billion (down 23.8% quarter on quarter, up 9.6% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 2.59 billion (down 53.2% quarter on quarter), but the company posted a KRW 1.49 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, a swing into loss from a year earlier.

Operating profit remained positive while net results turned negative, so the items below operating profit, including non-operating items and income tax, should be checked in the annual report. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totals KRW 7.6 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Pearlescent pigments are specialty colorants used in automotive paints, cosmetics and plastics, and their demand follows product trends in those customer industries.

On the automotive side, the shift from solid colors toward special-effect shades such as pearl and metallic finishes is cited as a driver of demand for premium paint pigments. In cosmetics, demand for differentiated color and texture in eyeshadow and lip products links directly to pigment demand.

Industrial uses such as plastics and inks are more general-purpose and tend to be more sensitive to economic cycles. CQV is introduced as a leader in automotive pearlescent pigments.

Among listed Korean chemical companies, LG Chem and Hanwha Solutions are centered on petrochemicals and battery materials, so their business structures differ sharply from CQV's.

In global competition, the company says GNMI's acquisition of Merck's surface solutions business could reshape the pearlescent pigment market. Cycle position is usually gauged through customer-industry indicators such as automotive output and cosmetics exports, which need to be checked separately.

Operating margin resilience

Quarterly profit recovery in 2026

Operating profit rose 104.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 3.02 billion in Q1 2026, then 83.4% more to KRW 5.54 billion in Q2. Q2 sales of KRW 20.2 billion were the highest of the last five quarters, so the profit recovery came with revenue growth.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders also climbed to KRW 2.41 billion in Q1 and KRW 4.81 billion in Q2.

Expected global portfolio expansion

GNMI acquired Merck's SuSo business and folded it in as SUSONITY. The company said the deal could complete a portfolio from low-priced to ultra-premium products, and that joint R&D and shared sales networks could lift market share.

Production bases in China, Korea, Germany, Japan and the US were also cited as expected benefits. How much of this shows up in CQV's own results is a point to check in the 2026 annual report.
