Order Growth Versus a Q3 2026 Net Loss at KEPCO Engineering & Construction

KEPCO Engineering & Construction Company posted three consecutive years of rising net income attributable to controlling shareholders from 2023 to 2025, but swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.03 billion in Q3 2026. Czech nuclear design revenue grew while the higher-margin reactor system design share shrank, so how fast the order backlog converts into profit is the key item to watch.

02

Business structure

KEPCO Engineering & Construction Company is an engineering firm that designs power plants, including nuclear power plants. Its business is split into a nuclear segment, within which reactor system design sits, and an energy new-business segment.

Reactor system design is generally understood to carry higher margins, and Kiwoom Securities said that in Q3 2026 the revenue share of this segment shrank while energy new-business revenue grew, pushing the operating margin below Q1 2026.

Its customers centre on nuclear utilities, including those within the Korea Electric Power group, and nuclear operators in Korea and abroad. Overseas, its role with Westinghouse is described as taking on stable design scope within a collaborative structure.

The main overseas project is Dukovany units 5 and 6 in Czechia, contracted in December 2025, including KRW 1.25 trillion for comprehensive design and KRW 370 billion for system design. Domestically, progress on the Shin-Hanul units 3 and 4 projects is rising.

New contract disclosures continued, including a KRW 32.32 billion pre-construction EPC engagement for the Wando Geumil offshore wind project disclosed on February 27, 2026, and a KRW 53.5 billion construction contract for the Gyeongju waste plastic pyrolysis oil plant disclosed on September 15, 2026.

The Gyeongju contract equals 10.31% of revenue and runs 18 months from construction start. The revenue base, once centred on nuclear design services, is widening into energy new businesses and construction-type contracts. The company competes with domestic and overseas nuclear design and EPC firms.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual figures, revenue rose from KRW 505.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 545.1 billion in 2023 (up 7.9% year on year) and KRW 553.4 billion in 2024 (up 1.5%), then fell to KRW 518.8 billion in 2025 (down 6.3%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit grew from KRW 13.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 28.6 billion in 2023 (up 104.9%) and KRW 54.8 billion in 2024 (up 91.9%), before falling to KRW 35.5 billion in 2025 (down 35.3%). Operating margin was 2.8% in 2022, 5.2% in 2023, 9.9% in 2024 and 6.8% in 2025, peaking in 2024.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 18 billion in 2022, KRW 32.7 billion in 2023 (up 81.9%), KRW 58.5 billion in 2024 (up 79.2%) and KRW 85.4 billion in 2025 (up 45.9%), rising three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed at the same amounts for each of these years. The 2025 pattern, with operating profit falling while net income rose, is the notable feature.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 114.6 billion, operating profit KRW 12.3 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 11.3 billion. Q4 2025 revenue jumped to KRW 205.5 billion (up 79.4% from the prior quarter) and operating profit to KRW 26.4 billion (up 115.7%).

Q1 2026 revenue fell 44.9% to KRW 113.3 billion with operating profit of KRW 14.1 billion. Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 103.3 billion, operating profit KRW 2.99 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 2.73 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue reached KRW 144.6 billion, up 40.0% from the prior quarter and 26.2% year on year, but operating profit was KRW 10.8 billion, down 11.5% year on year. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 1.03 billion.

The last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, total KRW 23.7 billion in net income attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow was KRW 6.76 billion in 2022, KRW 40.8 billion in 2023, KRW 49 billion in 2024 and KRW 29.3 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio went from 50.3% in 2022, 56.2% in 2023 and 64.0% in 2024 down to 41.7% in 2025. Press reports on Q3 2026 said the operating margin was 7.5% and that Kiwoom Securities had expected operating profit of about KRW 15 billion, below which the result landed.

05

Industry analysis

The downstream market for KEPCO Engineering & Construction is new nuclear construction and expanding overseas nuclear orders. In Korea, two large reactors and one SMR under the 11th Basic Plan are described as confirmed, and Samsung Securities estimated that tendering would occur between 2027 and 2028.

Overseas, the Dukovany order in Czechia opened the European market, and reports said the United States asked Korean firms to build reactors as it expands nuclear capacity.

NH Investment & Securities cited an upward revision of unit contract values for ten large U.S. reactors as the basis for raising its target price in April 2026, and said the firm is likely to take design scope within the Westinghouse collaboration.

Europe also shows a clear move toward expanding nuclear adoption after geopolitical risks. In terms of cycle position, the company is entering a phase in which nuclear design orders are materializing, but revenue recognition varies sharply from quarter to quarter depending on project progress.

On competition, the Czech project is seen as giving the company an overseas reference. Energy new businesses are extending into construction-type contracts such as the Wando offshore wind and Gyeongju pyrolysis projects.

Kiwoom Securities pointed to the lower profitability of this area relative to reactor system design as a factor behind the Q3 2026 margin decline.

06

Outlook

The company's confirmed pipeline runs along three lines. The first is Czechia, where Kiwoom Securities estimated 2026 Czech nuclear-related revenue at KRW 97.5 billion, and Czech large-reactor revenue more than doubled from Q2 to Q3 2026.

The second is domestic work, including comprehensive design for Shin-Hanul units 3 and 4 and new domestic reactor tenders. The third is additional overseas projects in the United States, the Middle East and Europe.

In a November 2026 report, Kiwoom Securities cut its 2026 full-year operating profit forecast 26% to KRW 55 billion and lowered its target price to KRW 120,000, while reflecting weak cumulative results through Q3. The same report said a meaningful improvement in operating margin is expected to appear from 2028.

NH Investment & Securities said in April 2026 that expanded Team Korea orders, centred on the United States, could push the order backlog above expectations.

The pre-construction EPC engagement for the Wando offshore wind project runs through December 31, 2026, so whether it converts into a main EPC contract is the next order checkpoint. Over the medium term, the recovery of the reactor system design revenue share will likely determine the trajectory of operating margin.

Bear factors

Q3 operating profit missed market expectations

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 10.8 billion, down 11.5% year on year, and press reports said it fell well short of a market consensus of about KRW 15 billion. Kiwoom Securities lowered its full-year operating profit forecast to KRW 55 billion. Revenue rose 40.0% from the prior quarter, but profit did not follow, which is the main bearish point.

Shrinking share of the higher-margin segment

Kiwoom Securities attributed the Q3 2026 margin decline to a smaller revenue share from reactor system design and higher energy new-business revenue. The same report expected a meaningful margin improvement only from 2028. A later recovery would add pressure to valuation interpretations.

Foreign-exchange losses and quarterly volatility

Press reports said foreign-exchange losses above KRW 7 billion from European projects such as Czechia pushed the pre-tax result into a loss. Revenue jumped to KRW 205.5 billion in Q4 2025 and then fell 44.9% to KRW 113.3 billion in Q1 2026. Such quarter-to-quarter swings make the earnings trend hard to read.

Risk factors

Order concentration and project progress

The Czech Dukovany project carries large revenue recognition, and quarterly sales move sharply with project progress. The gap between KRW 205.5 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 113.3 billion in Q1 2026 illustrates this. When revenue concentrates in a few large projects, earnings visibility declines.

Foreign-exchange and overseas project risk

In Q3 2026, foreign-exchange losses above KRW 7 billion on European projects contributed to a pre-tax loss. As overseas orders grow, profit volatility from currency and contract structures could also increase. The quarterly trend in currency-related gains and losses on overseas projects needs monitoring.

U.S. project timing and divergent broker outlooks

NH Investment & Securities raised its target price on the premise that U.S. large-reactor investment becomes visible, while Kiwoom Securities lowered its target price to KRW 120,000 in a November 2026 report. The divergence in broker views, which hinges on U.S. project progress, signals high uncertainty. If U.S. tendering is delayed, expectations could be adjusted.

Overall view

KEPCO Engineering & Construction is a nuclear design specialist whose revenue and operating profit both rose from 2022 through 2024 before the 2025 decline. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025 to KRW 85.4 billion.

However, operating profit fell 35.3% in 2025, and in Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 10.8 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.03 billion reversed the trend. The Dukovany 5 and 6 design orders and the sharp rise in Czech revenue in Q3 2026 show orders moving into revenue.

Meanwhile, the shrinking share of the higher-margin reactor system design and foreign-exchange losses weigh on profitability. Whether a large U.S. reactor project materializes is the key variable dividing broker views.

Kiwoom Securities lowered its target price to KRW 120,000 in a November 2026 report, while NH Investment & Securities raised its target in April 2026 on U.S. and European order expectations.

The next checkpoints are the non-operating items in the 2026 annual report, the 2026 Q4 preliminary results, and the timing of domestic reactor tenders in 2027 to 2028.
