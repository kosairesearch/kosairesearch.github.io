02

Business structure

Senko is a specialist maker of electrochemical gas sensors and gas detectors. In a breakdown reported on a cumulative basis through the third quarter of 2026, sensor instruments accounted for 77.06% of sales, environmental measuring instruments for 15.58% and sensors for 7.01%.

For 2020, sensor instruments were 70.5% of sales and environmental measuring instruments 24.8%, according to a 2021 research note. In Korea, sales of gas detectors supplied to SK hynix rose, and a fixed gas alarm order from the same customer was reflected in results.

The company said it recently completed supplier registration with Samsung Display and is seeking new supply opportunities. Overseas, demand for industrial gas detectors expanded, lifting cumulative exports to KRW 18.4 billion through the third quarter of 2026, up 42.8% year on year, and exports reached 57.66% of sales.

An earlier report said the company served North American demand through a local subsidiary in Texas.

In October 2026, Senko signed a single sales and supply contract with Busan Technopark to build a digital twin integrated industrial complex control platform, with a total contract value of KRW 6.37 billion, of which Senko's share is KRW 5.05 billion.

The company disclosed that contract amounts and periods may change as projects progress. Gas detection is a market where certification and customer qualification act as entry barriers, and its customer base differs from that of large power equipment makers in the same sector.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 40.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 34 billion in 2023, a 16.8% decline, then rose to KRW 35.9 billion in 2024 (up 5.6%) and KRW 36.7 billion in 2025 (up 2.4%), growing in two consecutive years since the 2023 low. 2022 was the largest year of the four for both revenue and operating profit, and 2025 revenue remains below that level.

Operating profit was KRW 2.76 billion in 2022, KRW 2.25 billion in 2023 (down 18.6%), KRW 2.72 billion in 2024 (up 21.0%) and KRW 2.5 billion in 2025 (down 7.8%). Operating margin was 6.8% in 2022, 6.6% in 2023, 7.6% in 2024 and 6.8% in 2025, moving within a narrow band.

Attributable net income was KRW 980 million in 2022, a net loss of KRW 1.21 billion in 2023, a return to profit of KRW 2.52 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.97 billion in 2025, up 97.6%. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 4.72 billion in 2025, a different figure from attributable net income.

Reports on 2025 separate-basis results cited a gain on the sale of the former headquarters building, a one-off item that lifted that year's profit.

Quarterly, revenue was KRW 8.32 billion in the third quarter of 2025 with operating profit of KRW 793 million and attributable net income of KRW 3.37 billion, then rose to KRW 12.5 billion in the fourth quarter (up 50.7% from the prior quarter) with operating profit of KRW 872 million.

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 8.84 billion (down 29.4% from the prior quarter) and operating profit was a loss of KRW 201 million, yet attributable net income was KRW 1.69 billion. In the second quarter, revenue reached KRW 12.6 billion and operating profit KRW 1.41 billion, turning profitable.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 11.1 billion (up 33.6% year on year) and operating profit KRW 959 million (up 20.9%). Attributable net income fell to KRW 55.3 million, down 98.4% from a year earlier, against a comparison base of KRW 3.37 billion in the third quarter of 2025.

Attributable net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 3.94 billion. The gap between operating profit and attributable net income appeared in several quarters, so the annual report's breakdown of non-operating items and taxes is a point to check.

05

Industry analysis

The gas detector and gas sensor market that Senko serves is commonly linked to tighter industrial safety rules and the spread of smart manufacturing. The company profile on the financial information site FnGuide expects growth in the global gas sensor market and high growth for electrochemical gas sensors.

A 2021 research note also expected safety reinforcement and prevention solutions to expand in earnest after the Serious Accidents Punishment Act took effect. Semiconductors are the most visible end market.

The company said cumulative domestic sales through the third quarter of 2026 rose 21%, helped by higher sales to SK hynix. Overseas demand for industrial gas detectors also grew, with cumulative exports up 42.8%. On the display side, supplier registration with Samsung Display is cited as a new point of demand.

This structure also means domestic sales can shift with semiconductor capital spending. Competitively, gas detection is protected by certification procedures and distribution channels, and an earlier report said the company was expanding both.

Peers in the same electrical equipment sector such as LS ELECTRIC and HD Hyundai Electric focus on power equipment with different customers and products, so the company is better compared with gas safety and instrumentation makers.

Whether semiconductor and overseas industrial safety demand move together is the key variable for reading the industry.

In an August 2026 report, the company attributed second-quarter growth to overseas sales and new supplies to SK hynix. Cumulative exports of KRW 18.4 billion, up 42.8%, support that expectation from the overseas side.

The digital twin contract with Busan Technopark is a long-term continuing contract, so it may be recognized across quarters, and its contribution will show in the quarterly revenue mix.

Whether Samsung Display supplier registration turns into actual orders and sales is another point to check in upcoming quarterly results.

Progress in qualification testing with Huawei and expanded supply plans with TSMC and Samsung Electronics were reported in earlier coverage, so how much of that has reached recent results needs confirming.

Whether overseas certifications and wider distribution channels translate into higher exports is also a point to watch. Whether operating margin holds in the 6% to 8% range will show whether sales growth is converting into profitability.

Bull factors

Expanding overseas exports

Demand for industrial gas detectors overseas lifted cumulative exports to KRW 18.4 billion in the first three quarters of 2026, up 42.8% year on year. Exports make up 57.66% of sales, so overseas demand feeds directly into top-line growth. Expanding certifications and distribution channels abroad is cited as a base for medium-term growth.

Stronger balance sheet

The liabilities-to-equity ratio fell from 76.2% in 2022 to 65.7% in 2023, 57.6% in 2024 and 23.4% in 2025. Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 57.8 billion against total liabilities of KRW 13.5 billion.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 2.86 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.55 billion in 2024, before easing to KRW 3.58 billion in 2025.

Profit recovery trend

Attributable net income swung from a net loss of KRW 1.21 billion in 2023 to KRW 2.52 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.97 billion in 2025. In the second quarter of 2026, revenue reached KRW 12.6 billion and operating profit KRW 1.41 billion. Bulls argue this recovery can continue if overseas and semiconductor demand keep expanding.

Bear factors

Operating profit was a loss of KRW 201 million in the first quarter of 2026, and attributable net income fell to KRW 55.3 million in the third quarter, down 94.4% from the prior quarter. Attributable net income has declined for two consecutive quarters since the first quarter of 2026.

Attributable net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 3.94 billion, so quarterly swings blur the read on annual results.

Thin margins and slow revenue recovery

Operating margin stayed within a 6.6% to 7.6% band across 2022 to 2025. Revenue of KRW 36.7 billion in 2025 remains below the KRW 40.8 billion of 2022, so sales have not regained the earlier peak. Operating profit of KRW 2.5 billion in 2025 fell 7.8% from the year before.

One-off gains and customer concentration

Reports attribute part of the 2025 separate-basis net income increase to a gain on the sale of the former headquarters building, which makes that year's profit harder to read as recurring. Domestic growth has been explained by higher sales to SK hynix, pointing to reliance on a single customer. A drop in that customer's orders could move domestic sales and quarterly profit together.

Earnings volatility and non-operating items

In some quarters the gap between operating profit and attributable net income is large, pointing to non-operating items and taxes. Overseas demand and currency

Exports account for 57.66% of sales, so overseas demand and currency moves feed directly into revenue. Stronger competition in overseas industrial gas detectors could pressure both prices and volumes. Costs tied to certification and the U.S. operation could also weigh on margins.

Overall view

Senko is a specialist maker of electrochemical gas sensors and gas detectors, and its 2025 revenue of KRW 36.7 billion and attributable net income of KRW 4.97 billion marked a recovery from the 2023 loss. Supplier status with SK hynix and Samsung Display and expanding exports are cited as growth drivers.

Quarterly swings are large, including the first-quarter 2026 operating loss and the sharp drop in third-quarter attributable net income, so annual trends alone do not settle the direction.

Reports say a gain on the sale of the former headquarters building was included in the 2025 separate-basis net income, so the quality of that year's profit needs a closer look. Operating margin stayed in a 6.6% to 7.6% band, which limits the room for margin expansion so far.

The liabilities-to-equity ratio fell from 76.2% in 2022 to 23.4% in 2025, which strengthens financial stability. The company expects record full-year sales in 2026, a claim that the fourth-quarter results will test.

The next checkpoints are the fiscal year-end on December 31, 2026 and the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027.
