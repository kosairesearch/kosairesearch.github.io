Report overview

Third-Quarter Profit Turn, Heavy Balance Sheet, Early-Stage AI Push

Lskairobot returned to a consolidated operating profit in the third quarter of 2026, but four consecutive years of losses in 2022–2025, a 241.6% debt ratio at end-2025, and an AI and robotics business still in its early stage remain the key constraints.

  1. 1

    Consolidated revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 13.4 billion, up 31.7% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 768 million turned the second quarter's operating loss of KRW 1.14 billion into a profit. Operating profit, however, fell 10.2% from a year earlier.

  2. 2

    On an annual basis, the company posted net losses attributable to controlling shareholders for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, and in 2025 it swung back to an operating loss.

  3. 3

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Lskairobot was founded in December 1999 and its core business is the research, manufacturing and sale of conductive materials. Materials remain the revenue base, with electrostatic discharge (ESD) carrier trays for IT manufacturing as a representative application.

Overseas production runs through its Vietnamese unit, Nano Chemtech Vietnam, which operates an ESD carrier tray line. The company renamed itself from Nano Chemtech to Lskairobot and positioned AI and robotics as its new growth engine.

The pivot rests on Onetouch AI, an AI application software company whose acquisition was completed at the start of 2026 and which holds computer vision and generative AI technology.

That technology is being applied in two directions: a dome-type optical vision AI inspection system deployed on the Nano Chemtech Vietnam line, and an AI content business built around the virtual artist 'Chaeyun', which combines generative AI with computer vision.

The company plans to extend the inspection work into supplying vision AI optical equipment and software to outside customers, using data gathered on its own production lines. It intends to fund the new businesses partly with about KRW 17.2 billion in cash raised from the sale of non-core assets.

Against large chemical and materials groups such as LG Chem and SKC, the company is a small specialty materials maker, and in its new businesses it may compete with established suppliers of inspection equipment and AI solutions.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 50.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 42.4 billion in 2023, a 15.5% decline, then rose to KRW 55.5 billion in 2024, a 30.9% increase that made it the largest year of 2022–2025. In 2025 revenue dropped 9.7% to KRW 50.1 billion.

Operating losses of KRW 2.44 billion in 2022 and KRW 1.84 billion in 2023 were followed by an operating profit of KRW 178 million in 2024, which turned back into an operating loss of KRW 262 million in 2025.

Operating margin was -4.9% in 2022, -4.3% in 2023, 0.3% in 2024 and -0.5% in 2025, so 2024 was the only profitable year. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed from KRW 16.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.72 billion in 2024 before widening to KRW 8.76 billion in 2025, a fourth consecutive year of losses.

Including non-controlling interests, the net loss was KRW 10.2 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow moved from -KRW 8.71 billion in 2022 and -KRW 4.54 billion in 2023 to positive figures of KRW 3.78 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.99 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue reached KRW 20.6 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, but operating profit was KRW 1.01 billion and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.48 billion. In the first quarter of 2026 revenue was KRW 8.56 billion with an operating loss of KRW 1.39 billion.

Revenue rose to KRW 10.2 billion in the second quarter with an operating loss of KRW 1.14 billion, then reached KRW 13.4 billion in the third quarter with an operating profit of KRW 768 million. Third-quarter operating profit was down 10.2% from a year earlier, while revenue was up 0.8%.

The second-quarter profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.47 billion came despite an operating loss, so non-operating items likely played a large role, and the breakdown is a point to check in the 2026 annual report.

According to the company, gross margin rose from 22.44% to 28.98% in the third quarter of 2026. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 4.48 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The materials business depends on demand from downstream industries such as IT and electronic components, displays and secondary batteries, which move with production volumes and capital spending.

Conductive materials for static dissipation and electromagnetic shielding gain uses as products become thinner and more precise, so technology shifts downstream drive demand.

The 2025 annual revenue decline and the sharp first-quarter 2026 drop followed by recovery in the second and third quarters are consistent with these swings in downstream demand.

Within the chemical sector, large materials groups such as LG Chem, SKC and Hanwha Solutions have extensive production bases in petrochemicals, batteries and films, whereas Lskairobot runs a small business centered on functional materials.

Scale competition is therefore less decisive than technological differentiation in specific applications. The vision AI inspection market already has many automation and inspection equipment suppliers, so the company is pursuing entry by showing outside customers applications verified on its own production lines.

The company said that the deployment could be the first case of vision AI inspection on a large tray production line in the industry. The AI content business sits in a market for generative-AI virtual IP that is only starting to take shape, which makes the scale of its revenue model hard to gauge. Where the sector stands in its cycle depends on the capital spending plans of downstream manufacturers.

06

Outlook

The core of the company's stated plan is the vision AI inspection system on the Vietnamese production line and its expansion to outside customers. The system was announced for phased rollout from early-to-mid November 2026 across production of 1.3 million trays a month.

According to the company, inspection time per part falls from 10 seconds under manual inspection to 0.5 seconds, and defect detection accuracy rises from a range of 70–95% to 98%.

The company expects inspection automation to cut quality inspection staffing by up to about 80%, which is its own projection and needs to be checked against results after deployment. The company intends to extend its vision AI optical equipment and software supply to outside customers, drawing on field data.

Revenue for the first half of 2026 was KRW 18.8 billion, up 15.7% from a year earlier, according to an announcement the company made in August 2026.

The virtual artist 'Chaeyun' was unveiled in September 2026 and is in beta testing, with plans to expand revenue through brand collaborations and intellectual property licensing. The company redeemed its eighth and eleventh convertible bond series before maturity, reducing potential share conversion.

Proceeds from the sale of non-core assets are being used to fund the new businesses.

The open question is whether the third-quarter 2026 profit becomes more than a single quarter, which the 2026 annual report will show through fourth-quarter operating profit and the breakdown of profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly profit turn and margin recovery

Consolidated operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 768 million, turning the second quarter's operating loss into a profit. The company said gross margin rose from 22.44% to 28.98%, and the standalone operating margin of 13.85% and recovery at subsidiaries both contributed.

Vision AI verified on the company's own production line

The company said vision AI inspection on its Vietnamese tray line cut inspection time per part from 10 seconds to 0.5 seconds. It plans to extend the business to supplying optical equipment and software to outside customers based on field data. Whether this deployment turns into external references is the test of expansion.

Balance sheet clean-up and cash raised

The company redeemed its eighth and eleventh convertible bond series early, reducing potential share conversion. It raised about KRW 17.2 billion from non-core asset sales and plans to use it for new businesses. Operating cash flow turned positive in both 2024 and 2025.

09

Bear factors

Four straight years of losses and a return to operating loss in 2025

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders ran for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025 and widened to KRW 8.76 billion in 2025. Operating profit in 2024 was followed by an operating loss of KRW 262 million in 2025. The operating margin of -0.5% in 2025 shows how brief the profitable stretch was.

High debt ratio and liabilities relative to equity

The debt ratio rose from 67.6% in 2022 to 241.6% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total liabilities of KRW 52.4 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 21.7 billion, of which equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 20.2 billion. Interest costs and financing conditions could constrain the pace of profit recovery.

Revenue from new businesses is at an early stage

The virtual artist 'Chaeyun' is in beta testing, and brand collaborations and IP licensing are presented as plans. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 10.2% from a year earlier.

The second-quarter 2026 profit attributable to controlling shareholders arose during an operating loss, so it may have depended on non-operating items.

10

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly operating results have flipped repeatedly, with profits and losses alternating across the last five quarters. Revenue fell 58.4% from the prior quarter in the first quarter of 2026 before rising for two straight quarters. If profits last only a quarter or two, the annual loss pattern may not change.

Capital structure and convertible bonds

The company redeemed its eighth and eleventh convertible bond series early, but any remaining convertible bonds and potential conversion shares should be checked in disclosures. With a 241.6% debt ratio, conversion would change the share count and ownership structure. If asset sale proceeds fund new investment, financial headroom shrinks.

Execution of the business pivot

The AI and robotics business began after the Onetouch AI acquisition, and few external supply contracts or revenue figures have been disclosed. The renaming and restructuring demand that the materials business keep its profitability while the new businesses receive investment.

Whether differentiation translates into actual orders against rivals in inspection equipment and AI solutions is the key test.

12

Overall view

Lskairobot showed a turn to profit in the third quarter of 2026, with consolidated revenue of KRW 13.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 768 million, and the company said gross margin rose to 28.98%.

Still, it posted net losses attributable to controlling shareholders for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, and its debt ratio stood at 241.6% at end-2025.

The new AI vision inspection and AI content businesses are at the production-line deployment and beta-testing stages, and few external revenue figures have been disclosed.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 4.48 billion, so an earnings-based multiple remains uncalculated. Strengths include the quarterly profit turn, early redemption of convertible bonds and cash from asset sales.

Weaknesses include continued annual losses, a heavy debt load and uncertainty over monetizing the new businesses.

The next checkpoint is fourth-quarter operating profit and the composition of profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the 2026 annual report, and whether profitability holds will be checked again in the first-quarter 2027 report.
