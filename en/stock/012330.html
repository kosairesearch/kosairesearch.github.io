02

Business structure

Hyundai Mobis is an automotive parts maker built around modules and core parts, aftermarket (A/S) parts, and electrification parts.

In the third quarter of 2026, the module and core-parts business recorded sales of KRW 12.27 trillion, and a report said the unit posted a profit for a second straight quarter (based on the results announced on October 30, 2026). The main electrification products are battery system assemblies (BSA) and chassis modules.

A BSA is a finished pack that combines battery-management electronics and controls, and chassis modules require large production and logistics investments, which typically lead to long-term supply contracts of 10 to 20 years.

Overseas order intake from global automakers excluding Hyundai Motor and KIA was USD 9.17 billion in 2025, and a large share of it was reported to come from battery-system and chassis-module contracts with North American and European customers.

In electronics, the company is scaling next-generation HMI and sound systems, which carry higher value. A/S parts were supported by higher global sales, favorable exchange rates, and lower tariff burdens in some countries.

Dependence on the Hyundai Motor Group remains high, and the company has set a target of raising the share of global-customer revenue to 40% by 2033. Future businesses include SDV software, ADAS, and robotics actuators, with a memorandum of understanding with Qualcomm on SDV and ADAS. R&D spending rose to KRW 1.75 trillion in 2024, and the 2026 R&D plan is about KRW 2.02 trillion.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, revenue was KRW 51.9 trillion in 2022, KRW 59.3 trillion in 2023 (up 14.2%), KRW 57.2 trillion in 2024 (down 3.4%), and KRW 61.1 trillion in 2025 (up 6.8%). 2025 was the largest revenue year in 2022–2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 2.03 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.3 trillion in 2023 (up 13.3%), KRW 3.07 trillion in 2024 (up 33.9%), and KRW 3.36 trillion in 2025 (up 9.2%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023. The operating margin was 3.9% in both 2022 and 2023, then 5.4% in 2024 and 5.5% in 2025.

In 2023 revenue grew while the margin stayed flat, and in 2024 the margin rose sharply even though revenue fell. Controlling-shareholder net income rose from KRW 2.49 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.42 trillion in 2023 (up 37.7%) and KRW 4.06 trillion in 2024 (up 18.5%), then fell to KRW 3.66 trillion in 2025 (down 9.9%).

Net income including non-controlling interests, which is a different figure, was KRW 3.66 trillion in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 2.15 trillion in 2022, KRW 5.34 trillion in 2023, KRW 4.25 trillion in 2024, and KRW 4.47 trillion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit moved from KRW 780.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 930.5 billion in the fourth quarter (up 19.2% quarter on quarter), then KRW 802.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 13.7%) and KRW 975.2 billion in the second quarter (up 21.5%).

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 914.1 billion was 6.3% lower than the prior quarter but 17.2% higher than a year earlier, and a report put the operating margin at about 5.8%, up 0.6 percentage points year on year, based on the October 30, 2026 disclosure.

Controlling-shareholder net income fell from KRW 1.06 trillion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 597.9 billion in the third quarter (down 43.5% quarter on quarter and 35.7% year on year). One report said third-quarter pre-tax income fell 27% year on year.

Controlling-shareholder net income for the latest four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 to third quarter of 2026) totaled KRW 3.3 trillion. Operating profit grew while net income fell, so items below the operating line need to be checked in the next periodic report.

05

Industry analysis

The EV parts market remains under pressure as demand growth slows and automakers adjust new-model launch plans. Hyundai Mobis reported that its 2025 overseas order intake from automakers outside Hyundai Motor and KIA was USD 9.17 billion, 23% above its target.

Electrification sales rose in the third quarter of 2026 on higher EV volumes in Europe, according to press reports. Signals from automakers are mixed.

Hyundai Motor said its global wholesale sales fell 11.3% year on year to 920,710 units in the third quarter of 2026, and it lowered its annual operating margin target from 6.3–7.3% to 5.5–6.5% over the same period. Some factors favor Mobis.

The average won-dollar rate in the third quarter was 1,423, up 2.8% year on year, and a weaker won helped convert overseas sales into more won. A shift in Hyundai Motor's sales mix toward hybrids was cited as a profitability factor for automakers. A/S parts also benefited from lower tariff burdens in some countries.

The company's competitive task is to widen its non-affiliated customer base. Mobis said it is pursuing ADAS and electrification orders in North America, Europe, and China.

06

Outlook

The company's 2026 global order target is USD 11.84 billion, about 29% above the 2025 result. The target reflects a similar scale of core-parts orders as in the prior year plus expected large module orders.

The non-affiliated core-parts order target is USD 8.97 billion, and a report on November 9, 2026 said cumulative orders through the third quarter were USD 1.15 billion, about 13% of that target, with the fourth quarter flagged as the period that needs to expand orders. Production footprint is already coming online.

A plant in Hungary makes chassis modules for Mercedes-Benz electrified models, and a battery-system plant in Navarra, Spain, produces battery systems for Volkswagen EVs.

The PE-system plant in Nováky, Slovakia can produce up to 280,000 units a year, and about KRW 250 billion has been invested in building the plant and upgrading its capacity.

In robotics, prototype production of Atlas actuators began in November 2026, and plans include securing hand grippers, perception modules, and controllers. Reports say the company has set a 2028 supply target for robotics parts.

In SDV, the company joined the Eclipse Foundation SDV working group and formalized its participation in the S-Core project. The medium-term benchmark is a 40% share of revenue from global customers by 2033.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual order result, the fourth-quarter 2026 operating margin, and the SX3 platform volumes scheduled for 2027 mass production.

08

Bull factors

Long-term EV supply contracts

Overseas order intake from automakers outside Hyundai Motor and KIA reached USD 9.17 billion in 2025, 23% above target, with a large share reported from North American and European battery-system and chassis-module contracts.

These products require heavy capital investment and typically run on supply contracts of 10 to 20 years. The 2026 order target of USD 11.84 billion is about 29% above the 2025 result.

Margin improvement trend

The operating margin rose from 3.9% in 2022 to 5.5% in 2025, and operating profit increased for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit reached KRW 914.1 billion, up 17.2% year on year, with the company citing higher electrification sales and a better product mix. The module and core-parts unit also posted a profit for a second straight quarter.

Robotics and aftermarket as added pillars

Prototype production of Atlas actuators began in November 2026, and the company is extending its automotive motor and power-electronics technology into robot parts. A/S parts delivered solid results on higher global sales, favorable exchange rates, and lower tariff burdens in some countries. Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 4.47 trillion.

Bear factors

Weak net income trend

Controlling-shareholder net income fell 9.9% to KRW 3.66 trillion in 2025, and third-quarter 2026 controlling-shareholder net income fell 35.7% year on year to KRW 597.9 billion. Operating profit rose while net income declined, so items below the operating line require attention. One report said third-quarter pre-tax income fell 27% year on year.

Pressure on the order target

The 2026 order target is above the 2025 result, so the pace of cumulative quarterly orders is the point to watch for whether the target is met. One report said cumulative non-affiliated core-parts orders through the third quarter were USD 1.15 billion, about 13% of the USD 8.97 billion target. The company itself has described the external environment as uncertain.

Automaker sales and slower EV demand

Hyundai Motor's global wholesale sales fell 11.3% year on year to 920,710 units in the third quarter of 2026, and it lowered its annual operating margin target to 5.5–6.5%. Production by Mobis's major automaker customers also fell 2.3% year on year in the third quarter. Automakers adjusting new-model launches amid slower EV demand adds to the pressure.

10

Risk factors

Overseas plant ramp-up

The Hungary chassis-module plant, the Navarra BSA plant in Spain, and the Nováky PE-system plant in Slovakia have already entered operation. If mass production at these plants starts later than customer model launch schedules, revenue recognition under long-term supply contracts could slip as well.

Because the Nováky plant can produce up to 280,000 units a year, a low utilization rate could raise fixed-cost burdens.

FX and tariff policy

Aftermarket parts earnings and the won translation of overseas sales depend heavily on the won-dollar exchange rate and tariff burdens. If the won strengthens or tariffs rise again in some countries, this support could shrink.

The third-quarter 2026 average won-dollar rate of 1,423 was higher than a year earlier, so a change in direction would alter its contribution to earnings.

Robotics and SDV execution

An industry observer views robotics parts and software-defined vehicle businesses as still at an early stage where results are yet to be confirmed. Meeting the 2028 supply target and expanding the customer base are cited as conditions. R&D spending is planned at about KRW 2.02 trillion in 2026, which could weigh on costs.

12

Overall view

Hyundai Mobis's reported results for 2022–2025 show revenue growth and a higher operating margin arriving together. In 2025 revenue was KRW 61.1 trillion, operating profit was KRW 3.36 trillion, and the operating margin of 5.5% was the highest of the four years.

However, controlling-shareholder net income fell 9.9% in 2025 and fell 35.7% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, so whether the operating-level gains carry through to net income needs further confirmation.

The electrification business is growing on European volumes and long-term contracts, but slower EV demand and lower Hyundai Motor Group sales are working against it.

On orders, a 2026 target of USD 11.84 billion has been set, yet one report said non-affiliated core-parts orders were about 13% of target through the third quarter, so the fourth quarter matters. Robotics is at the Atlas actuator prototype stage with a 2028 supply target, so it remains early.

On valuation, the latest four quarters of controlling-shareholder net income total KRW 3.3 trillion, and the stock trades at a discount to net assets.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results, the 2026 annual business report, the 2026 full-year order result, and the start of SX3 mass production in 2027.
