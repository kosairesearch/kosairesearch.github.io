Bio-Feedstock Sales Drive Revenue; Coating Recovery Is the Test

02

Business structure

Green Resource applies protective coatings to parts used in semiconductor and display etching equipment and provides recoating and cleaning services.

Its main business lines are thermal spray coatings and coating materials, ultra-high-density specialty coatings, superconducting wire equipment, and precision cleaning (Bloter, June 2026). The business mix has changed sharply.

Commodity sales such as rare earths and bio-feedstock were 6.1% of revenue in 2024 but reached KRW 76.6 billion, or 75.03% of revenue, in 2025 (Bloter, June 2026).

The CEO described the bio-feedstock unit as providing stability and the semiconductor coating unit as providing growth (Thinkpool, interview reported in January 2026). Ultra-high-density coatings are presented as high-value products tied to yield stability at advanced process nodes.

The main customer for the semiconductor coating business is the foundry segment, which generated KRW 9.8 billion in revenue through the third quarter of 2025, or 13.3% of total revenue of KRW 73.8 billion (Thinkpool). Customer concentration is therefore high.

Superconducting wire equipment sales reached KRW 4.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, and a company official said equipment sales also include semiconductor and display coating equipment (Bloter).

In competitive terms, a November 2023 IPO document from Eugene Investment & Securities named Tokai Carbon Korea as a peer in etching-process parts. The company says its differentiator is handling everything from material processing to equipment design and manufacturing.

04

Earnings analysis

In the annual disclosures, revenue fell from KRW 25.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.5 billion in 2023, a 34.2% decline, then rose to KRW 18.5 billion in 2024 (up 12.1%) and KRW 102.2 billion in 2025 (up 452.2%).

Revenue has grown for two consecutive years from the 2023 low through 2025, and 2025 is the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period. Operating profit was KRW 3.73 billion in 2022, KRW 3.27 billion in 2023 (down 12.4%), KRW 1.07 billion in 2024 (down 67.2%), and KRW 6.03 billion in 2025 (up 462.9%).

The operating margin fell from 19.8% in 2023 to 5.8% in 2024 and was 5.9% in 2025. Thinkpool reported that the 2024 profit decline reflected Samsung Electronics reducing investment in the foundry segment, its main customer, after the second half of 2024.

The first-half 2025 revenue of KRW 45.7 billion, up 443.50% year on year, was disclosed on August 11, 2025 (Newsis), and commodity sales drove that growth.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.47 billion in 2022, KRW 2.6 billion in 2023 (down 24.9%), KRW 2.32 billion in 2024 (down 11.0%), and KRW 10.2 billion in 2025 (up 342.4%).

On a quarterly basis, revenue peaked at KRW 28.4 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (up 1.0% from the prior quarter) and has declined for three consecutive quarters from the first quarter of 2026 through the third quarter.

Operating profit was KRW 2.48 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 331 million in the second quarter, and KRW 669 million in the third quarter.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7.65 billion in the third quarter of 2025, a net loss of KRW 1.45 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, KRW 6.44 billion in the first quarter of 2026, a net loss of KRW 3.82 billion in the second quarter, and KRW 957 million in the third quarter, with the sign changing almost every quarter.

The last four quarters total KRW 2.13 billion in net profit attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow moved from an inflow of KRW 4.14 billion in 2022 to a net outflow of KRW 3.14 billion in 2024 and KRW 17.9 billion in 2025. In the first quarter of 2026, commodity sales were KRW 11.2 billion, or 53.7% of revenue (IB Tomato).

05

Industry analysis

Demand for coatings on etching-equipment parts moves with foundry investment cycles. According to Thinkpool, the coating business underperformed after Samsung Electronics reduced foundry investment from the second half of 2024.

The same report said that in 2026 Samsung's foundry yields were improving and follow-on investment was being executed in stages. Expansion to global foundry players in China and Taiwan has also been mentioned.

On December 24, 2025, NH Investment & Securities said that a recovery in utilization at major foundry customers would revive demand for ultra-high-density specialty coatings and move the company into a growth phase centered on its core business.

In a separate Thinkpool report, an industry source said ultra-high-density coating sales had entered a rebound after bottoming in the first quarter of 2025.

On the technology side, the transition to advanced nodes such as 2nm brings early yield issues, which leads customers to rely more on materials and parts that minimize yield loss.

In terms of position, the company's earnings structure now leans more on commodity sales than on coating technology leadership, so cycles in semiconductors and bio-feedstock policy both matter.

Entry into the before-market segment, where coated parts are supplied during new equipment manufacturing, is seen as a way to expand revenue quickly if customers adopt the parts, because single orders can be large (Thinkpool).

06

Outlook

In an August 2025 report, the CEO said the company expected growth in its ultra-high-density specialty coating business in the second half of 2025 (Newsis).

Revenue then declined in the second and third quarters of 2026, so whether that expectation carried through to quarterly results is a point to confirm in the next periodic filings. For the before-market expansion, the key variable is whether customers adopt the coated parts at the new equipment stage (Thinkpool).

For bio-feedstock, the CEO has said the RPS program's strengthening trend should keep demand intact, and a company official cited legislation requiring coal-fired power plants to use some renewable substitute energy as support for demand (Thinkpool and Bloter).

Commodity sales still accounted for 53.7% of revenue in the first quarter of 2026, so dependence on bio-feedstock is likely to persist for now.

On the financing side, the company raised KRW 20.0 billion in convertible bonds and KRW 10.0 billion in bonds with warrants, and most of the proceeds are being used to buy bio-feedstock and fund existing call options on earlier mezzanine financing (IB Tomato).

Superconducting wire equipment recorded KRW 4.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, and the company supplies products to the US market through a partner, so the full-year cumulative trend of equipment sales is worth tracking.

No quantitative company guidance has been identified that is suitable for citation, so quarterly filings serve as the reference. The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, which will disclose fourth-quarter 2026 results, full-year operating cash flow, and the debt ratio.

08

Bull factors

Bio-feedstock demand and policy support

Commodity sales reached KRW 76.6 billion in 2025, or 75.03% of revenue (Bloter, June 2026). The company said the strengthening RPS program supports medium- to long-term demand, and it cited legislation as a factor lifting use of renewable substitute energy at coal-fired plants.

While bio-feedstock sales carried the top line, revenue expanded from KRW 18.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 102.2 billion in 2025.

Foundry yield gains and a rebound signal in ultra-high-density coatings

According to a January 2026 Thinkpool interview, Samsung Electronics' foundry yields have improved and follow-on investment is being executed in stages. The same interview described ultra-high-density coating sales as rebounding after bottoming in the first quarter of 2025.

NH Investment & Securities on December 24, 2025 cited a recovery in customer utilization as the basis for a coating demand rebound.

Rapid growth in superconducting wire equipment sales

Superconducting wire equipment sales reached KRW 4.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, already above the KRW 1.4 billion recorded for 2025 as a full year (Bloter). The company supplies products to the US market through a partner (IB Tomato). Whether these equipment sales hold up on a quarterly basis will determine how strong this new business line is.

09

Bear factors

Three straight quarters of revenue decline and volatile earnings

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 13.9 billion, down 50.3% year on year. Operating profit was KRW 669 million, down 65.5% year on year, and the second quarter of 2026 recorded a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.82 billion. Even after the profit in the first quarter of 2026, quarterly earnings direction has changed repeatedly.

Reliance on commodity sales and margin compression

The operating margin fell from 19.8% in 2023 to 5.9% in 2025. Commodity sales drove the 2025 revenue expansion, and they were still 53.7% of revenue in the first quarter of 2026 (IB Tomato). The core bear argument is that margins did not grow in step with revenue.

Financial burden and weaker cash flow

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 104.2% at the end of 2025 from 65.6% in 2022, with total liabilities of KRW 75.1 billion and total equity of KRW 72.1 billion. Operating cash flow was a net outflow of KRW 17.9 billion in 2025.

Much of the KRW 20.0 billion in convertible bonds and KRW 10.0 billion in bonds with warrants raised in June 2026 is directed to raw-material purchases and existing mezzanine financing (IB Tomato).

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and foundry investment cycle

The foundry segment was the main customer base, and Samsung Electronics' investment cuts from the second half of 2024 weighed on coating results. Foundry revenue was 13.3% of the total through the third quarter of 2025 (Thinkpool). A renewed cut in customer investment could slow any recovery in the coating business.

Policy and raw-material supply

A large share of commodity sales relies on RPS-related and bio-feedstock demand. If policy direction shifts or raw-material costs rise, both the scale and profitability of commodity sales could be affected. A company official also said that demand for bio-feedstock was influenced by legislation (Bloter).

Financing and dilution

In June 2026 the company issued KRW 20.0 billion in convertible bonds and KRW 10.0 billion in bonds with warrants (IB Tomato). If conversion or warrant rights are exercised, the number of shares outstanding could rise.

With quarterly profit swinging, the effect of this financing structure on per-share value is another item to watch.

12

Overall view

Green Resource began as a coating company for semiconductor etching-equipment parts, but commodity and bio-feedstock sales now fill a large share of revenue since 2025. Revenue of KRW 102.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 6.03 billion in 2025 show expansion, but the operating margin of 5.9% is below 19.8% in 2023.

In 2026, quarterly revenue has come in at KRW 20.9 billion, KRW 14.4 billion, and KRW 13.9 billion, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders has alternated between profit and loss.

Positive factors include improving foundry yields, signs of a rebound in ultra-high-density coatings, growing superconducting wire equipment sales, and the possibility of entering the before-market segment.

Negative factors include dependence on commodity sales, a debt-to-equity ratio of 104.2% and an operating cash outflow of KRW 17.9 billion in 2025, and the size of mezzanine financing. Both sides have figures to support them, so it is too early to settle on one direction.

The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report due by March 31, 2027, which will confirm fourth-quarter 2026 results and full-year cash flow. The first-quarter 2027 report, due by May 15, 2027, will show whether the revenue decline has stopped.
