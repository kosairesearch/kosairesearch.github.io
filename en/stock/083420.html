02

Business structure

Green Chemical was spun off from KPX Chemical in 2003 as a specialty fine chemicals company and listed on the KOSPI in 2005. The company manufactures and sells functional chemicals such as EOA, ETA, DMC and AM, using EO (ethylene oxide) as its main raw material.

Its business scope also includes trading of other chemical products alongside its functional chemicals. EOA and ETA are produced in a multi-product, small-batch system, which is viewed as limiting dependence on any single industry.

The company is pursuing higher-value products by expanding research and development on special grade products. EO-based products have margins that move with raw material prices and customer industry utilization, so quarterly revenue and operating profit often move together.

On the related-party side, transactions with Green Lifescience, which shares the same CEO, are significant. Green Lifescience's reliance on Green Chemical rose from 21.51% of its sales in 2024 to 50.65% in 2025 and 68.83% in Q1 2026.

Green Chemical buys inventory from Green Lifescience, and its 2025 annual report disclosed purchases of KRW 9.22 billion from that counterparty.

Compared with large domestic petrochemical peers such as Lotte Chemical, Kumho Petro Chemical and Hanwha Solutions, Green Chemical is a smaller specialty fine chemicals producer.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 324.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 282.3 billion in 2023, a decrease of 13.1%, then rose 17.0% to KRW 330.3 billion in 2024, the largest year in the 2022–2025 period. Revenue declined 8.0% to KRW 304.1 billion in 2025.

Operating profit dropped from KRW 17.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.95 billion in 2023, a decline of 71.2% and the smallest year of the period, then rose 115.8% to KRW 10.7 billion in 2024 before easing 5.0% to KRW 10.1 billion in 2025.

The operating margin moved from 5.3% in 2022 to 1.8% in 2023, 3.2% in 2024 and 3.3% in 2025, a modest recovery over two consecutive years from the 2023 low.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.87 billion in 2022, KRW 3.35 billion in 2023 (up 16.7%), KRW 8.19 billion in 2024 (up 144.6%) and KRW 9.65 billion in 2025 (up 17.8%), with three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025.

The 2024 jump in controlling-shareholder profit moved in the same direction as the 115.8% operating profit recovery. The debt-to-equity ratio went from 60.6% in 2022 to 67.3% in 2023 and 67.4% in 2024 before falling to 60.1% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 128.3 billion and total liabilities were KRW 77.1 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 18.8 billion in 2022, KRW 21.7 billion in 2023, KRW 19.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 16.7 billion in 2025, positive in every year of the period.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 77.3 billion with operating profit of KRW 5.05 billion, and Q4 2025 revenue fell 10.9% quarter on quarter to KRW 68.9 billion while operating profit fell 58.8% to KRW 2.08 billion.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 77.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 3.02 billion, and Q2 2026 revenue rose to KRW 91.9 billion, the highest of the last five quarters, with operating profit of KRW 7.52 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 9.62 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 87.8 billion, operating profit was KRW 3.8 billion, down 49.5% quarter on quarter and 24.7% year on year, and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 126 million.

Operating profit stayed positive in Q3 2026 while a line item below operating profit pushed the bottom line into a loss. In Q2 2026, controlling-shareholder net income exceeded operating profit, suggesting a meaningful contribution from non-operating items.

Over the most recent four quarters, from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 15.5 billion.

Industry analysis

Within the chemicals sector, Green Chemical specializes in EO-based functional specialty chemicals, so its business structure differs from large producers that manufacture commodity petrochemicals at scale.

Listed peers such as LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions, SKC, Lotte Chemical and Kumho Petro Chemical tend to have large plants and a higher share of commodity products. By contrast, Green Chemical is a small company by market capitalization.

Its multi-product, small-batch production is viewed as limiting its exposure to demand swings in any single industry. Specialty chemicals can see price moves that differ from commodity products, but EO raw material prices and customer plant utilization still affect margins.

On the regulatory side, tighter greenhouse gas rules are expected to increase demand for ETA-based carbon dioxide capture materials. However, the extent to which this demand shows up in quarterly revenue and profit is hard to judge from reported results alone.

The corporate monitoring service Wisereport noted as of June 3, 2026 that separate-basis revenue fell in Q1 2026 while profitability improved on cost reductions. Against larger rivals, Green Chemical has a smaller scale but occupies a niche position as a materials company with product specialization.

Its position in the industry cycle is hard to pin down, so sector indicators and the trend in quarterly operating margins should be read together.

Risk factors

Related-party transaction consistency

Transactions with Green Lifescience have grown in importance on both the sales and purchase sides. Reports of differing amounts for the same transactions make disclosure consistency a point to watch. Any change in the structure of these dealings could affect the quality of reported earnings.

Raw material costs and demand swings

Margins can move with EO and other raw material prices and with the utilization of customer industries. The operating margin falling to 1.8% in 2023 illustrates this sensitivity. With revenue down in 2025, the capacity to absorb cost swings could narrow.

Small-cap liquidity

The company is small by market capitalization, with a free float ratio of 37.39% and foreign ownership of 1.66% as of June 3, 2026 per Wisereport. Stocks with limited free float can see larger price swings when trading volume changes. Such characteristics can be more pronounced around quarterly earnings releases.
