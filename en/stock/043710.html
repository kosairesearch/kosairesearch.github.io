Report overview

Cosmetics Pivot Brings Growth and Rising Leverage

CosLeaguer Global has transformed into a cosmetics-focused company through its ODM subsidiary and the acquisition of the beplain brand operator, driving a sharp revenue jump in the first half of 2026, but the acquisition financing has sharply raised leverage and quarterly earnings remain volatile.

02

Business structure

CosLeaguer Global, formerly Seoul Leaguer, was founded in 1991 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2002.

The company previously operated diversified businesses spanning hospital management support (MSO), distribution, and green-tech, and began preparing a shift to a cosmetics-focused structure starting with the establishment of its ODM subsidiary Cosleaguer at the end of 2024.

Cosleaguer completed a 'Cosmetic Hub' plant in Siheung, Gyeonggi Province in August 2025, covering roughly 6,000 pyeong and described as the largest ODM production facility in the greater Seoul metropolitan area, spanning basic to color cosmetics development and manufacturing.

Based on this plant, the company secured a supply deal with Japanese drugstore chain Matsumoto Kiyoshi for seven private-brand products including capsule essence and began production, with sales set to start at roughly 3,500 Japanese stores from February 2026.

In December 2025, the company acquired an 85.94% stake in Moments Company, which operates skincare brand beplain, for approximately KRW 81.2 billion from Hwahae Global and its affiliates.

Moments Company's beplain brand is known for its 'Nokdu' (mung bean) product line, generating over half of its revenue overseas at KRW 45.6 billion, while its domestic flagship 'Nokdu Foam' cleanser gained attention by ranking highly at the Olive Young Awards.

The company changed its listed name from Seoul Leaguer to CosLeaguer Global effective May 4, 2026, and consolidated share par value around the same time, repositioning its identity as a cosmetics specialist.

On the ownership side, the controlling shareholder is Sesim Co., which is wholly owned by FINTEK, with the ultimate beneficial owner referred to in media reports as a figure associated with a well-known Botox-industry success story.

The group is pursuing a beauty value chain spanning planning, manufacturing, and distribution through its two pillars of manufacturing (Cosleaguer) and brand (Moments Company).

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 10.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 22.6 billion in 2023, KRW 19.3 billion in 2024, and KRW 35.7 billion in 2025, while operating losses persisted for four straight years at KRW 6.6 billion, 7.5 billion, 3.3 billion, and 8.3 billion respectively.

In 2024, net income attributable to owners turned positive at KRW 5.4 billion despite the operating loss, but in 2025 the net loss attributable to owners widened to KRW 8.7 billion and operating cash flow deteriorated to negative KRW 12.9 billion.

On a quarterly basis, the operating loss widened from KRW 0.8 billion on revenue of KRW 10.5 billion in Q2 2025, to a KRW 2.0 billion loss on KRW 10.6 billion revenue in Q3, and a KRW 4.2 billion loss on KRW 9.7 billion revenue in Q4.

Revenue then surged to KRW 39.5 billion in Q1 2026 with operating profit turning positive at KRW 1.5 billion, a result driven by Moments Company posting a 70% year-on-year revenue jump that lifted CosLeaguer Global's consolidated operating profit into positive territory and drove overall top-line growth.

Because the Moments Company acquisition only closed in late December 2025, its impact was barely reflected in FY2025 results but was fully consolidated starting in Q1 2026, which is why first-quarter 2026 revenue rose 707.7% year on year.

However, despite further revenue growth to KRW 42.4 billion in Q2 2026, operating income swung back to a loss of KRW 0.6 billion, and the net loss attributable to owners widened to KRW 6.4 billion from KRW 3.3 billion in the prior quarter.

Summing the most recent four quarters (Q3 2025 through Q2 2026), the net loss attributable to owners totals roughly KRW 15.5 billion, indicating that despite top-line growth, the path to breakeven still requires time.

The debt ratio, which had stayed stable between 26.8% and 38.9% from 2022 to 2024, jumped to 476.6% in 2025, reflecting the sharp rise in total liabilities from convertible bonds issued to fund the Moments Company acquisition.

05

Industry analysis

The K-beauty industry is in a structural, export-led growth phase, with Korea's cosmetics exports ranking third globally last year, behind only France and the United States, reflecting expanding overseas demand.

In particular, as export leadership shifts from large established brands to indie brands, smaller clean-beauty labels such as beplain are rapidly scaling through domestic channels like Olive Young as well as overseas e-commerce and social commerce.

In the ODM manufacturing segment, large listed ODM players with long-accumulated production scale and client portfolios dominate the market, making Cosleaguer—whose new plant only became operational in 2025—a small, early-stage entrant by comparison.

On the brand side, however, beplain has already approached roughly KRW 90 billion in annual revenue with overseas sales exceeding half of the total, placing it in a relatively mature position among indie brands.

On the demand side, the clean-beauty trend continues in major markets such as the United States and China, and the growth of new distribution channels such as TikTok Shop provides additional sales avenues for smaller brands.

That said, the strategy of combining ODM manufacturing and brand operations within a single corporate structure to complete a value chain is not yet common in the industry, so the sustainability of integration benefits still needs to be validated through further quarterly results.

06

Outlook

When announcing the Moments Company acquisition, the company stated a target of KRW 190 billion in consolidated revenue for 2026 (KRW 60 billion from manufacturing, KRW 130 billion from brands) and operating profit of KRW 15 billion or more, while Moments Company separately indicated plans to target KRW 133.3 billion in revenue and KRW 14.7 billion in operating profit in 2026 through expansion into new markets such as Mexico and Indonesia and diversification into channels like TikTok Shop.

These remain company-stated targets, and whether first-half 2026 results track this trajectory requires confirmation from upcoming quarterly reports.

On the production side, since supply of private-brand products to Matsumoto Kiyoshi in Japan has already begun, how much this contributes to annual results will serve as a gauge of growth in the ODM segment.

On the financing side, in August 2026 the company executed an early redemption of convertible bonds, purchasing KRW 3.5 billion in face value from FINTEK—the parent of its controlling shareholder—for KRW 36.0 billion including interest, at a conversion price of KRW 4,565; after the purchase, KRW 1.5 billion in bonds remain outstanding with a conversion period starting December 17, 2026.

Separately, a September 2026 amendment changed the stated use of proceeds for an existing convertible bond from facility investment to acquisition of securities in another company, and moved the subscription and payment date to October 6, 2026, raising the possibility of further equity investment or M&A.

The controlling shareholder group also expanded its stake through off-market purchases in September 2026, and further strategic direction is likely to emerge through future ownership disclosures and fund-use filings.

08

Bull factors

Vertical Integration of Manufacturing and Brand Complete

The company has combined its ODM subsidiary Cosleaguer with brand operator Moments Company (beplain) under one roof to build a value chain spanning planning, manufacturing, and distribution.

Once this structure became fully operational in Q1-Q2 2026, quarterly revenue expanded to roughly KRW 40 billion, far above prior levels. The manufacturing segment is also accumulating additional overseas references, such as the private-brand supply deal with Matsumoto Kiyoshi.

Export-Oriented Brand Riding the K-Beauty Wave

Beplain generates over half of its total sales overseas, aligning with the broader export growth of the Korean cosmetics industry. Centered on its Nokdu product line, the brand has also built a strong position in the domestic Olive Young channel.

Planned expansion into new markets such as Mexico and Indonesia, along with diversification into channels like TikTok Shop, could provide additional growth drivers.

Controlling Shareholder's Stake Increase and Bond Retirement

The controlling shareholder group, Sesim and related parties, raised their stake to 68.58% through off-market purchases in September 2026, explicitly citing an intent to affect management control.

In August of the same year, the company announced plans to acquire part of its convertible bonds early using internal funds and retire them, indicating a move to reduce potential future dilution. These actions can be read as factual indicators of the controlling shareholder's commitment to the business.

Bear factors

Sharply Higher Leverage and Cash Flow Strain

The debt ratio jumped sharply from 28.4% in 2024 to 476.6% in 2025, largely due to convertible bond financing used for the KRW 81.2 billion acquisition. Operating cash flow also deteriorated to negative KRW 12.9 billion in 2025, indicating the company has not yet generated cash from core operations.

Additional convertible bond issuances and changes in the stated use of proceeds could introduce further variables to the balance sheet going forward.

High Quarter-to-Quarter Earnings Volatility

Operating profit turned positive in Q1 2026 but reverted to a loss in Q2 2026, and the net loss attributable to owners has persisted for four consecutive quarters, actually widening in the most recent quarter. This shows that revenue growth has not yet translated into stable earnings improvement.

Costs associated with ramping up the new plant and early-stage integration could continue to weigh on profitability for some time.

Single-Brand Dependence and Integration Risk

A significant portion of consolidated growth is concentrated in a single, recently acquired brand, Moments Company's beplain, making results sensitive to changes in that brand's demand or distribution channels.

It will take additional time for synergies between manufacturing (Cosleaguer) and brand (Moments Company) to show up clearly in the numbers. The acquisition price itself drew some market commentary suggesting it was relatively high, warranting continued scrutiny of how quickly the investment pays off.

Risk factors

Financial / Liquidity

The debt ratio surged to 476.6% in 2025, and operating cash flow has been negative for four consecutive years, indicating heavy reliance on external financing. The conversion period for the remaining convertible bond tranche begins in December 2026, creating potential dilution depending on the conversion price.

The change in the stated purpose of an additional convertible bond issuance to 'acquisition of securities in another company' is also a point that warrants close monitoring of future funding needs.

Business / Integration

Start-up costs from the new ODM plant and one-off integration costs from the Moments Company acquisition could continue to weigh on profitability in the near term.

A large share of revenue is concentrated in a single brand, beplain, making the company vulnerable to a slowdown in that brand's demand or shifts in reliance on distribution channels such as Olive Young. Expansion into new overseas markets such as Mexico and Indonesia is also still at an early stage and unproven.

Governance

The controlling ownership structure runs through Sesim and FINTEK under a small group of individuals, and related-party transactions have recurred, including the off-market purchase of convertible bonds from the parent of the controlling shareholder.

Frequent convertible bond issuances, amendments, and changes in the stated use of proceeds place a burden on minority shareholders to continuously track disclosures. Any further stake expansion or M&A activity warrants close scrutiny of potential dilution to existing shareholder value.

Overall view

CosLeaguer Global is in the process of transforming from a diversified business structure into a cosmetics specialist built on two pillars: ODM manufacturing (Cosleaguer) and brand operations (Moments Company's beplain).

This shift sharply lifted revenue in the first half of 2026 compared with prior periods, but operating profit has swung between gains and losses, and the net loss attributable to owners has persisted for four consecutive quarters, meaning earnings stability has not yet been established.

The debt ratio jumping by several hundred percentage points within a single year, driven by convertible bond financing for the acquisition, is a variable that warrants continued monitoring from a financial soundness perspective.

The controlling shareholder group's stake increase and partial retirement of convertible bonds reflect confidence in the business, but the recurring related-party transactions and changes in fund-use purposes are governance-related points that deserve careful attention.

The Q3 2026 results, the start of the conversion period for the remaining convertible bonds, and whether the company meets its annual guidance are likely to be the key checkpoints going forward.
