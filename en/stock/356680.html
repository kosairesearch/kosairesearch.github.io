Revenue Up, Margins Thin as Axgate Shifts Toward Quantum Security

Axgate posted its largest revenue of the 2022–2025 span in 2025, at KRW 48.1 billion, while its operating margin fell from 14.1% in 2022 to 7.8% in 2025; whether quantum-security contracts translate into profit is the next thing to watch.

02

Business structure

Axgate is a network security company founded in 2010 whose core products are VPN and firewall systems. According to reports, VPN accounted for 40% (KRW 19.2 billion) and firewalls for 30% (KRW 14.5 billion) of 2025 revenue of KRW 48.1 billion. The existing AXGATE Series and SSL VPN form the stable earnings base.

The new businesses are Quantum VPN (Q-VPN) and home-network security; Q-VPN entered the defense market in 2025, and deliveries to public, local-government and defense customers have since expanded.

The quantum product family is built on the AX-Quantum platform, which combines in-house on-board quantum random number generators (QRNG) with post-quantum cryptography (PQC). The AXGATE-HOMES home-network security solution has been supplied mainly to major home-network makers.

The next-generation firewall (NGFW) line has added AI-based threat identification and natural-language device control. Main customers are the public procurement market and large domestic defense contractors, where the business is moving from completed pilots toward full-scale contracts.

The company cites its compatibility and proprietary technology as the basis for its revenue structure in public and enterprise markets. How quickly the quantum businesses grow their share of revenue is the key variable for the change in the business mix.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 38.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 42.8 billion in 2023 (up 11.8% year on year), KRW 43.2 billion in 2024 (up 0.9%) and KRW 48.1 billion in 2025 (up 11.4%). Revenue increased for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022–2025.

Operating profit over the same span went from KRW 5.42 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.1 billion in 2023 (down 24.3%) and KRW 3.49 billion in 2024 (down 15.0%), before recovering to KRW 3.75 billion in 2025 (up 7.7%). Revenue grew while operating profit peaked in 2022, so growth did not carry through to margins.

The operating margin fell from 14.1% in 2022 to 9.6%, 8.1% and 7.8% in 2025. The CEO attributed the flat margin to reinvestment in research and development for products five to ten years out.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 4.58 billion in 2022 to KRW 295 million in 2023 (down 93.6%), rebounded to KRW 4 billion in 2024 (up 1,256.5%) and was KRW 3.87 billion in 2025 (down 3.1%). Net income including non-controlling interests was disclosed at the same amounts in each year.

Operating cash flow was KRW 1.42 billion in 2025, the lowest of 2022–2025. Quarterly, fourth-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 21.2 billion (up 139.3% from the prior quarter), and operating profit of KRW 6.88 billion swung to a profit and exceeded the full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 3.75 billion.

First-quarter 2026 revenue fell to KRW 9.7 billion (down 54.3% from the prior quarter) and operating profit to KRW 248 million (down 96.4%), before the second quarter recovered to KRW 12.1 billion in revenue and KRW 373 million in operating profit.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 10.4 billion (up 17.6% year on year), but the quarter swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.48 billion from the prior quarter, with a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.27 billion.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 584 million.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for quantum security starts from concern that quantum computers could break today's public-key cryptography. The threat of harvesting encrypted data now and decrypting it later with quantum computers has become a major security issue for defense, defense industry and critical infrastructure.

In Korea, no PQC algorithm had been available in the KCMVP cryptographic module verification, leaving public agencies without a basis for adoption; the company says its hybrid KCMVP certification has created that foundation.

The government is running a 2026 PQC pilot conversion program, and Axgate has been selected as a final performing institution for the transport sector.

Defense procurement moves through several stages, including conversion to full contracts, budget allocation, procurement procedures and technical validation, so revenue recognition may come later than pilot milestones.

On competition, Axgate points to its VPN and firewall customer base and public procurement experience as strengths. The AI next-generation firewall market is shifting from simple detection toward intelligent analysis as encrypted traffic grows.

In domestic security, Axgate competes in public procurement with large security vendors such as Ahnlab. How quickly PQC demand turns into revenue depends on when full-scale orders are placed.

06

Outlook

The company has set a 2026 revenue target in the mid-KRW 50 billion range, and its CEO has said the company can exceed it. First-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 9.7 billion was reported as up 25.8% from a year earlier.

Yet quarterly revenue from the first to third quarters of 2026 stayed near the KRW 10 billion level, so reaching the target would require fourth-quarter revenue to be concentrated again.

In defense, the company has completed a quantum-security pilot with a large defense contractor and aims to expand deliveries to weapons systems and defense communications infrastructure. In the transport PQC project, Axgate is responsible for securing data transmission between roadside equipment and the control center.

In home-network security, the company says its order backlog secured for 2026 is sufficient. Its AI next-generation firewall includes natural-language device control and is slated for expansion into public, financial and smart-infrastructure markets.

The company intends to keep investing in research and development, so how much of that spending shows up in quarterly operating profit is a checkpoint. No public timetable for full-scale defense orders has been confirmed.

08

Bull factors

Early Quantum-Security Certification

The company says its AX-Quantum platform, which combines PQC and QRNG, earned the first hybrid KCMVP national certification in Korea. The company argues this opens procurement access because public agencies now have a basis to adopt PQC. Axgate was also selected for the 2026 transport-sector PQC pilot. The company's logic is that certification and pilot track records will support full-scale contract wins.

Established VPN and Firewall Base

VPN accounted for 40% and firewalls for 30% of 2025 revenue, so these two product lines carry most of the top line. Revenue rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, with 2025 the largest year of 2022–2025. The company says public procurement sales have kept rising year on year. Even if the new businesses lag, the established product lines provide a base for the top line.

Defense and Public-Sector Demand Path

The company says it completed a quantum-security pilot with a large domestic defense contractor and plans to expand into drones, unmanned vehicles, operational vehicles and radios. Q-VPN entered the defense market in 2025, and deliveries to public, local-government and defense customers have since increased.

In transport infrastructure, the company is securing the data link to the control center with its PQC extension technology. If public and defense demand turns into orders, the revenue mix could change.

Bear factors

Downward Trend in Operating Margin

The operating margin fell from 14.1% in 2022 to 9.6% in 2023, 8.1% in 2024 and 7.8% in 2025. Revenue rose from KRW 38.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 48.1 billion in 2025, but the margin did not recover with it. The company cites research reinvestment, but no disclosed timetable shows when that spending will return as profit. The bear case rests on revenue growth not converting into higher profit at a low margin.

Quarterly Volatility and Return to Loss

Revenue was KRW 21.2 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 but fell to KRW 9.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue of KRW 10.4 billion coincided with an operating loss of KRW 1.48 billion, a swing from the prior quarter, and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.27 billion.

Because quarterly results depend on large deliveries, a delay in one quarter can quickly turn into a loss. If the fourth-quarter concentration does not repeat, the annual target becomes harder to reach.

Share Price Weight Relative to Earnings

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totals KRW 584 million, a thin earnings base. Equity attributable to controlling shareholders at the end of 2025 was KRW 48.3 billion, and the price-to-book multiple sits in a large-premium range.

If the share price depends more on quantum-security expectations than on earnings, any delay in business milestones could add pressure. Operating cash flow falling to KRW 1.42 billion in 2025 is also a bear point on earnings quality.

Risk factors

Policy and Procurement Timing

Defense markets move through several stages, including conversion to full contracts, budget allocation, procurement procedures and technical validation. The transport PQC project is a pilot, so whether it leads to full contracts is uncertain.

Even with a basis for public PQC adoption, the timing and size of orders depend on government budgets. If pilot results do not lead to orders, the path to the revenue target weakens.

R&D Burden and Input Costs

The company intends to keep expanding research spending for products five to ten years out. If R&D costs grow faster than revenue, a margin recovery could be delayed. Rising memory prices have also created supply and cost pressure on components. The company says it bought memory and SSDs in advance for next year's needs, but price volatility remains high.

Theme Sensitivity and Share Price Swings

When quantum-related expectations weigh heavily on the share price, its moves can grow with the intensity of business news. Because quarterly results swing sharply, there are periods when the share price is hard to explain with earnings alone.

If the price moves independently of results, the basis for assessing the stock can become unstable. This risk stems more from shifts in market expectations than from the business itself.

Overall view

On a disclosed basis, Axgate's revenue has risen for three consecutive years from 2023, reaching KRW 48.1 billion in 2025. The operating margin, however, fell from 14.1% in 2022 to 7.8% in 2025, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders has swung widely from year to year.

Quarterly results in 2026 have been uneven, with first-quarter revenue of KRW 9.7 billion and a third-quarter operating loss of KRW 1.48 billion, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 584 million.

Quantum VPN, the PQC certification and the transport and defense pilots are presented as growth evidence, but the timing of full-scale orders and their revenue recognition is not yet confirmed. The established VPN and firewall lines carry a large part of revenue, which gives the top line a base.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter results and operating margin trend in the 2026 annual report, and the first-quarter 2027 quarterly flow.
