Camera Module Turnaround Hinges on New Businesses

02

Business structure

CammSys designs and manufactures camera modules for smartphones. Mobile camera modules remain the core of revenue, and the company said it expanded supply to all Galaxy S26 models and the Galaxy Z Fold8 series of its main customer Samsung Electronics.

The company attributed the 2025 losses to a shift toward lower-priced models that worsened product mix, even though module shipments grew.

The main production base is in Vietnam, where the company is reallocating processes and plans to reduce local production headcount from 1,455 in the first half of 2025 to 1,000 or fewer by the end of 2026.

Non-mobile efforts include security cameras, smart-appliance cameras, robotics cameras, facial recognition cameras and drone cameras, all built on AI vision technology.

According to an announcement in early November 2026, the company began supplying ultra-wide and AI camera modules for all models of Samsung Electronics' Bespoke AI Family Hub, securing its first home-appliance camera revenue.

It is also reported to be part of the camera supply chain for Hyundai Motor Group's humanoid robot. The Indonesian subsidiary serves as a hub for new businesses and for robotics and facial recognition cameras.

The competitive setting is a camera-module market where large electronics and component makers compete, and CammSys depends heavily on a small number of flagship customer models.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show a split between revenue scale and profitability. Revenue was KRW 733.7 billion in 2022, the largest year in the 2022–2025 period, and KRW 378.2 billion in 2024, the smallest.

Revenue in 2025 was KRW 489.9 billion, up 29.5% year on year, but the company said a shift toward lower-priced models worsened product mix and produced losses.

Operating profit fell from KRW 13 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.86 billion in 2023, a 47.4% decline, and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 11.7 billion in 2024. The 2025 operating loss narrowed to KRW 11.3 billion, but the company has now posted losses for two consecutive years, 2024 and 2025.

Operating margin was 1.8% in 2022, 1.4% in 2023, -3.1% in 2024 and -2.3% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.29 billion in 2022, then a net loss of KRW 14.1 billion in 2023, widened to KRW 19.3 billion in 2024 and narrowed to KRW 18.3 billion in 2025, marking three consecutive years of losses from 2023 to 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 18.6 billion in 2025, slightly larger than the controlling-shareholder loss. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 170.2% in 2022 to 356.0% in 2025.

Total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 54.9 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 195.5 billion. Operating cash flow declined from KRW 26.2 billion in 2022 and KRW 27.8 billion in 2023 to KRW 12.1 billion in 2024, then turned to -KRW 21.8 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, an operating loss of KRW 3.29 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 1.2 billion in the fourth quarter was followed by operating profit of KRW 6 billion in the first quarter of 2026, a swing to profit.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 121.8 billion, down 29.8% from the prior quarter, and operating profit fell 56.7% to KRW 2.6 billion. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 153.7 billion, up 26.1% from the prior quarter and 38.0% year on year, with operating profit of KRW 7.39 billion.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 8.72 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The camera-module industry is structured so that volumes and unit prices follow the camera-specification race among smartphone flagship models. Smartphone demand moves in quarterly waves tied to launch cycles, which fits the drop in CammSys second-quarter 2026 revenue from the prior quarter.

CammSys concentrated high-resolution, high-performance modules on flagship volumes and reduced its share of lower-priced models, and the company said this shift drove profitability gains in 2026 quarters.

Because flagship supply is central to profitability, whether a main customer adopts a given new model adds to earnings volatility. The company expects Galaxy Z Fold8 supply and supply for the S27 and A series, scheduled for 2027, to determine the size of its margin improvement.

Competitively, CammSys operates against large camera-module makers and is building its position by widening its flagship order base.

The company is extending mass-production capability from mobile into security, smart-appliance and robotics cameras, areas described as new demand where AI vision technology is combined with camera modules. Revenue from non-mobile businesses has not yet been disclosed as a separate figure.

06

Outlook

The company has set a target of closing 2026 with a full-year operating profit. The premise is that new non-mobile revenue, including security and smart-appliance cameras, will be reflected in results from the fourth quarter of 2026.

In the second half, Galaxy Z Fold8 supply is expected to be reflected, and supply for the S27 and A series slated for 2027 is expected to widen the margin improvement. The Vietnam subsidiary plans to reduce production headcount to 1,000 or fewer by the end of 2026 to lower fixed costs and raise productivity.

Among new businesses, robotics cameras and facial recognition cameras run through the Indonesian subsidiary are cited. Because first revenue from home-appliance camera mass production was secured in early November 2026, how much that revenue contributes to the full-year profit target is the next point to check.

The CEO said the company would strengthen the profitability and competitiveness of its mobile camera business in the second half and show visible results in non-mobile businesses. Whether the target is achieved will be judged on a full-year basis in the 2026 annual report.

Risk factors

Customer concentration

CammSys flagship orders rely heavily on its main customer adopting new models. If that customer changes volume allocation or model mix, quarterly revenue and profitability can both move sharply. The 2025 shift toward lower-priced models, which worsened product mix, illustrates this dependence.

New business ramp-up

Revenue from security, smart-appliance and robotics cameras has not yet been broken out as a separate figure. If the expected fourth-quarter contribution falls short, the full-year operating profit target becomes harder to reach. Customer qualification and mass-production timing also remain variables.

Balance sheet and production base

With the debt-to-equity ratio at 356.0% at the end of 2025, financing costs and the burden of further fundraising remain. Headcount adjustment and process reallocation at the Vietnam subsidiary, the main production base, could affect production stability. Exchange-rate movements and the concentration of production in one country also need to be monitored.

Overall view

CammSys revenue fell in 2023 and 2024 before recovering to KRW 489.9 billion in 2025, but profitability posted operating losses in both 2024 and 2025. In 2026, operating profit turned positive from the first quarter through the third quarter, and third-quarter revenue rose 38.0% year on year to KRW 153.7 billion.

The company attributes the turnaround to a flagship-focused product mix, lower cost ratios and efficiency at the Vietnam production base.

On the other side, the debt-to-equity ratio was 356.0% at the end of 2025 and operating cash flow was -KRW 21.8 billion, while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 8.72 billion, below the 2025 annual controlling-shareholder loss of KRW 18.3 billion.

Non-mobile ventures secured first revenue from home-appliance camera mass production in early November 2026, but the full-year profit target depends on how much new fourth-quarter revenue actually shows up in results.

Strengths include expanded flagship orders and cost improvement, while weaknesses include customer concentration, financial burden and quarterly revenue volatility. The next checkpoints are operating profit and non-mobile revenue in the 2026 annual report, and the first-quarter 2027 provisional results.
