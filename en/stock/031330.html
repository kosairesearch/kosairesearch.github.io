Report overview

Testing whether Samt's memory-boom profits can last

Samt's operating profit jumped to KRW 327.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026 on a memory price upswing, then fell in each of the next two quarters, leaving inventory levels and debt as the next points to watch.

  1. 1

    Full-year 2025 disclosed revenue was KRW 3.76 trillion (up 30.0% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 113.9 billion (up 69.1%), the largest year in the 2022–2025 span.

  2. 2

    Operating profit peaked at KRW 327.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then fell to KRW 147.6 billion in the second quarter and KRW 86.3 billion in the third, even as quarterly revenue rose to KRW 2.78 trillion.

  3. 3

    Inventory rose from 39.1% of total assets in 2024 to 60.3% in the first quarter of 2026, and the debt-to-equity ratio climbed from 93.5% in 2024 to 183.1% in 2025.

  4. 4

    Operating cash flow was negative KRW 139.1 billion in 2024 and negative KRW 120.3 billion in 2025, a gap against the profit growth over the same years.

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Samt, which describes itself as an IT marketing specialist, began in 1990 as a Samsung Group affiliate and distributes components for semiconductor, display and electronics makers. Leadeconomy reported that the company ranks first among distributors of Samsung Electronics' CMOS image sensors (CIS) and memory.

According to a company profile on WiseReport, it holds supply contracts with Samsung Electronics and sells electronic parts to large and mid-sized makers of mobile phones, OLED monitors and TVs. The company is expanding marketing for new products such as automotive memory and AI chips.

In its enterprise value enhancement plan disclosed on March 25, 2026, it said it would diversify its product line by sourcing from top-tier global manufacturers in semiconductors, displays, secondary batteries and IT devices.

Research Alum estimated in a 2025 report that memory, mainly DRAM, made up more than 40% of sales, and that the company held about 70% of domestic share among three Samsung Electronics semiconductor distributors.

By region in the first quarter of 2026, domestic sales were 54.0% (KRW 970.6 billion), followed by China at 18.9%, the United States at 13.7% and Hong Kong at 10.6%. Overseas sales run through subsidiaries including To-Top Electronics in Hong Kong, SAMT SG in Singapore and SAMT AMERICA LLC in the United States.

Because it is a distribution business, costs and inventory respond quickly to memory price changes, which may explain the larger swings in quarterly results.

04

Earnings analysis

On a full-year basis, revenue fell from KRW 2.54 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.15 trillion in 2023, down 15.7% year on year, and then rose to KRW 2.89 trillion in 2024 (up 34.7%) and KRW 3.76 trillion in 2025 (up 30.0%).

Operating profit moved from KRW 78.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 53.5 billion in 2023 (down 32.0%), then KRW 67.4 billion in 2024 (up 25.8%) and KRW 113.9 billion in 2025 (up 69.1%). Net income attributable to controlling shareholders bottomed at KRW 32.9 billion in 2023 and reached KRW 75.8 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 75.8 billion in 2025, the same figure as the controlling-shareholder number in that year. Operating margin, however, stayed thin at 3.1% in 2022, 2.5% in 2023, 2.3% in 2024 and 3.0% in 2025, so sales growth did not translate fully into margin gains.

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 27.2 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 45 billion in the fourth quarter, then to KRW 327.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026 on revenue of KRW 1.8 trillion.

In the second quarter, revenue reached KRW 2.58 trillion while operating profit fell to KRW 147.6 billion, and in the third quarter revenue was KRW 2.78 trillion with operating profit of KRW 86.3 billion.

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 242 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 105.1 billion in the second quarter and KRW 71.6 billion in the third, and the last four quarters totaled KRW 453.1 billion.

The first-quarter spike appears to reflect rising memory prices combined with the recovery in the value of inventory, and inventory rose to 60.3% of total assets that quarter. Whether that margin holds in later quarters will be the test of how durable the gain is.

Operating cash flow was positive at KRW 29.7 billion in 2022 and KRW 140.8 billion in 2023, but turned to negative KRW 139.1 billion in 2024 and negative KRW 120.3 billion in 2025. Debt-to-equity reached 183.1% at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 890.2 billion against total equity of KRW 486.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The memory chip cycle is the biggest variable for Samt's sales and earnings. In its quarterly report, the company said conservative capex focused on profitability would keep memory supply tight through the end of 2026, and it projected memory segment growth of more than 30% year on year.

Research Alum's 2025 report argued that AI infrastructure investment is driving rapid growth in server DRAM demand and that supply may lag demand for about two years. The same report said higher DRAM prices lift both distribution margins and the value of inventory.

Views on the 2026 chip cycle diverge across institutions, so there is no broad consensus on where the cycle stands. Operating profit fell in two consecutive quarters after the first quarter of 2026, which could indicate that the price-driven contribution has passed its peak, though that remains an interpretation.

In competition, distributors differentiate through supply contracts with manufacturers, inventory management and overseas subsidiary networks.

Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek, also in the electronic components sector, run manufacturing-based models, while Samt operates mainly as a distributor, so asset turnover and inventory valuation affect its earnings directly. The roughly 70% domestic share cited above is an estimate by Research Alum rather than a disclosed figure.

06

Outlook

The enterprise value enhancement plan disclosed on March 25, 2026 sets two goals: sustained revenue growth through profit-focused management and a global network, and maintaining financial soundness and profitability through risk management.

Its action items include diversifying the product line by sourcing from leading manufacturers, managing currency risk on an ongoing basis, and tightening credit and receivables control over customers.

In the same plan, the company disclosed that it qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act. The 2025 dividend amount was KRW 2.24 billion, up 15.0% from the prior year.

The company expects memory supply to stay tight through the end of 2026, and how that outlook shows up in fourth-quarter and later results is the central question. Because operating profit fell for two consecutive quarters after the first quarter, the next trend depends on memory prices and inventory burden together.

Overseas guarantees are also a variable. According to disclosures dated September 22 and 23, 2026, the company decided guarantees on loans including KRW 53.1 billion for a U.S. subsidiary and KRW 33.2 billion for a Singapore subsidiary, with the guarantee period set to end on June 30, 2027.

The company has not disclosed segment sales targets, so its direction is limited to expanding the product line. The 2026 annual report, the next periodic filing, will first show confirmed full-year results, whether operating cash flow turns positive, and how the debt-to-equity ratio moves.

08

Bull factors

Memory supply tightness expected to persist

In its quarterly report, the company said capex focused on profitability would keep memory supply short through the end of 2026, projecting memory segment growth above 30% year on year. Research Alum also argued that server DRAM demand could outrun supply for about two years. If this persists, both volume and unit prices could keep supporting sales growth.

High level of quarterly earnings

Operating profit was KRW 327.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026, and controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 242 billion. Third-quarter operating profit of KRW 86.3 billion was up 217.9% from a year earlier. Even after the peak, quarterly profit remains above earlier levels, which supports the bullish case.

Shareholder returns and high-dividend status

In a March 2026 disclosure, the company said it qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act. The 2025 dividend amount was KRW 2.24 billion, up 15.0% from 2024. Dividends rising alongside earnings is cited as a positive.

09

Bear factors

Earnings declining for two quarters since the peak

Operating profit fell 54.9% in the second quarter to KRW 147.6 billion from KRW 327.3 billion, then fell a further 41.5% to KRW 86.3 billion in the third. Revenue kept rising over the same period, which means margins thinned. Whether the first-quarter profit was one-off is central to the bearish case.

Rising inventory and debt burden

Inventory rose from 39.1% of total assets in 2024 to 60.3% in the first quarter of 2026, and the debt-to-equity ratio climbed from 93.5% to 183.1% by the end of 2025. If memory prices pass their peak, inventory valuation losses could erode earnings. This is the main bearish factor.

Accumulating guarantees for overseas subsidiaries

Disclosures dated September 22 and 23, 2026 show guarantees for loans including KRW 53.1 billion for a U.S. subsidiary and KRW 33.2 billion for a Singapore subsidiary. The U.S. guarantee equals 10.9% of equity. The repeated pattern of the parent guaranteeing overseas borrowing is cited as a burden.

Risk factors

Reversal in the memory price cycle

Earnings depend heavily on memory price gains and the recovery in inventory value. Operating profit has fallen for two consecutive quarters since the first quarter of 2026, which could signal that the price contribution is weakening. A price reversal could bring inventory valuation losses and margin compression at the same time.

Financial structure and cash flow

Debt-to-equity reached 183.1% at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 890.2 billion against total equity of KRW 486.1 billion. If cash continues to flow out of operating activities, reliance on borrowing could increase. If cash does not follow profit, reliance on borrowing could increase.

Largest shareholder stake changes and share price volatility

The largest shareholder, Samji Electronics, disclosed on-market sales of 18,076 shares on May 20, 2026, reducing its stake to 49.82%. Stake changes can affect the share price through supply and demand. The shares have been volatile, which adds another consideration.

Overall view

Samt's revenue grew to KRW 3.76 trillion in 2025, and operating profit rose to KRW 113.9 billion, increasing in both 2024 and 2025 after the 2023 low.

In the first quarter of 2026, operating profit jumped to KRW 327.3 billion during a memory price upswing, but it then fell in the second and third quarters, so the durability of earnings is the central question.

Bullish factors include the company's projection of tight memory supply, a high level of quarterly profit, and its high-dividend designation with rising dividend amounts.

Bearish factors include inventory at 60.3% of total assets, debt-to-equity of 183.1%, negative operating cash flow in 2024 and 2025, and a growing record of guarantees for overseas subsidiaries. Operating margin was still only 3.0% in 2025, so how far sales growth has translated into margin gains remains limited.

The next points to watch are results after the fourth quarter of 2026, cash flow and debt in the 2026 annual report, and profit levels in the first quarter of 2027. This report summarizes current facts and the competing views, and it contains no investment rating or target price.
