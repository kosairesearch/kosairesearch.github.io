Revenue Down, Losses Persist; Mass-Production Timing Is the Key

HiDeep reported 2025 revenue of KRW 1.77 billion, down 70.0% year on year, and an operating loss of KRW 11.7 billion. Losses have continued for five straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026, so the timing of mass-production approvals and its funding progress are the next points to track.

02

Business structure

HiDeep develops and supplies touch ICs for mobile devices as well as integrated touch-and-stylus solutions.

According to the company profile, its touch IC lineup serves smartphones, smartwatches, tablets, notebooks and casino displays, and it applies its own technology for stable touch performance on LTPO on-cell flexible OLED displays in mass production.

An equity research report published in April 2023 showed product sales of KRW 14.91 billion in 2022, of which smartwatch sales were KRW 13.94 billion, so revenue was concentrated in one device component. Product sales were split 83.91% domestic and 16.09% export in 2025, and service revenue was entirely domestic.

In February 2024 the company signed a contract worth about KRW 5 billion with a domestic display maker to develop next-generation touch ICs.

In May 2024 it was selected for a national project with a total budget of KRW 2.2 billion to develop a controller SoC that integrates touch and stylus support for ON-CELL flexible OLED.

The company has set out a staged expansion path covering smartphones, foldable phones and smartwatches in phase one, tablets and notebooks in phase two, and automotive displays in phase three.

Because revenue arises only after a customer finalizes a mass-production model, changes in customer schedules pass directly into sales.

For 2025, the company attributed the sales decline to strategy changes at customers and partners that partly adjusted mass-production schedules for its mobile touch IC and stylus pen products.

The differentiator the company emphasizes is that its integrated solution has passed performance evaluation at a global customer.

04

Earnings analysis

HiDeep's reported results moved toward lower revenue and larger losses. Revenue fell from KRW 18.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.5 billion in 2023, a 42.2% decline year on year, then to KRW 5.89 billion in 2024, down 43.8%, and to KRW 1.77 billion in 2025, down 70.0%.

The operating loss widened from KRW 2.27 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.61 billion in 2023, KRW 9.12 billion in 2024 and KRW 11.7 billion in 2025, and the operating margin moved from -12.5% in 2022 to -664.1% in 2025.

Over the same period total R&D spending rose from KRW 4.07 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.67 billion in 2025, according to a data aggregator on a December fiscal-year basis, which added to cost pressure as sales shrank.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened from KRW 6.98 billion in 2022 to KRW 14.8 billion in 2025, and net loss including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amount in each year.

The 2025 net loss of KRW 14.8 billion exceeded the KRW 11.7 billion operating loss, which suggests non-operating items played a role; the breakdown can be checked in the annual report.

Quarterly operating losses went from KRW 2.29 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 4.92 billion in the fourth quarter, narrowed to KRW 2.82 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then were KRW 2.93 billion in the second quarter and KRW 3.1 billion in the third quarter.

Operating losses have continued for five straight quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 17.7 billion.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 213 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, fell 89.7% quarter on quarter to KRW 21.93 million in the first quarter of 2026, and rose 14.2% to KRW 25.04 million in the second quarter. Operating cash flow in 2025 was -KRW 7.09 billion, the largest outflow among the four years from 2022.

05

Industry analysis

HiDeep operates in a market tied to the shift toward OLED displays in mobile devices such as smartphones and smartwatches. The company centers its pitch on LTPO on-cell flexible OLED touch technology and presents integrated touch-and-stylus driving for flexible panels as its differentiator.

Display driver and touch IC markets include large IC suppliers, so securing new design references is a key challenge for a small fabless company.

Revenue is recognized only after a customer confirms mass-production adoption of a new model, and the 2025 results showed how changes in customer product strategy flow directly into sales.

Rather than assigning a position in the cycle, the more accurate framing is that customer adoption schedules for new models remain the largest variable for the company's revenue.

The company describes its integrated approach as handling touch and stylus in a single chip, which it argues can reduce customers' component count. Against listed peers, HiDeep is small in revenue, so whether one or two customers adopt its products largely determines results.

The June 2026 designation as an investment-caution stock after a sharp price rise shows that trading activity can move apart from reported results.

06

Outlook

The company's account of the first quarter of 2026 is that its integrated touch-and-stylus solution passed performance evaluation at a global customer and that discussions with global customers have moved into a more advanced stage.

Revenue recovery, however, depends on customer decisions because finalizing a mass-production model takes time. Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 25.04 million rose 14.2% quarter on quarter but was too small to offset the KRW 2.93 billion operating loss.

The third-quarter 2026 operating loss of KRW 3.1 billion was wider than a year earlier, while net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed to KRW 3.15 billion from KRW 3.65 billion in the second quarter.

The company laid out a staged expansion path: smartphones, foldable phones and smartwatches in phase one, tablets and notebooks in phase two, and automotive displays in phase three.

On funding, the June 2026 third-party allotment covers 5,808,766 new common shares, aimed at raising KRW 3.09 billion for operating funds and KRW 3.5 billion for debt repayment.

The new shares were disclosed as subject to a one-year custody lock-up, and the allottees include the largest shareholder, CEO Ko Beom-kyu, along with related parties.

Related-party borrowings disclosed as taken on March 16, 2026 at a 4.6% interest rate carry a maturity of March 15, 2027, and part of those loans is slated for conversion into equity. Without quantitative guidance, customer mass-production adoption timing is the reference point for future results.

Bull factors

Mass-production validation completed and global talks

The company says its integrated touch-and-stylus solution passed performance evaluation at a global customer and completed mass-production validation. Its account describes discussions with global customers as having moved into a more advanced stage as of the first quarter of 2026.

The core bull argument is that delayed sales could be reflected in results once a mass-production model is confirmed.

Staged application roadmap and funded technology projects

The company has presented a phased roadmap widening applications from smartphones, foldables and smartwatches to tablets and notebooks and then to automotive displays. A broader application base could spread revenue that is now concentrated in a few device types, which supports the bull case.

The 2024 next-generation touch IC contract and national project selection show that technology development is continuing.

Partial narrowing of quarterly losses

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.15 billion in the third quarter of 2026, narrower than KRW 3.65 billion in the second quarter, and KRW 3.13 billion in the first quarter of 2026 was also smaller than KRW 7.73 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025.

Quarterly losses narrowed in part of the period, which is a positive factor. However, the operating loss of KRW 3.1 billion in the third quarter was wider than a year earlier, so whether this narrowing carries through at the operating level needs further confirmation.

Bear factors

Fragile revenue base

Revenue has declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, falling to KRW 1.77 billion in 2025. First-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 21.93 million fell 89.7% quarter on quarter, and second-quarter revenue of KRW 25.04 million remained small. With revenue this small, a single change in customer timing can move results substantially.

Deteriorating financial structure

Total equity fell to KRW 2.24 billion at the end of 2025, and the debt ratio rose from 109.5% in 2022 to 461.8% in 2025. Operating cash flow was -KRW 7.09 billion in 2025, the largest outflow among the four years.

The company borrowed KRW 3.5 billion from related parties in March 2026 and then raised funds through a rights issue in June 2026 for operating and debt repayment needs.

Recurring customer schedule adjustments

For the 2025 revenue decline, the company cited schedule adjustments for mass production tied to strategy changes at customers and partners. It also said first-quarter 2026 sales fell because finalizing a mass-production model took time.

If the same cause recurs, the timing of a revenue recovery could keep being pushed back, which underpins the bear case.

Risk factors

Customer mass-production timing

HiDeep's revenue arises only after customers finalize mass-production adoption of new models. If adoption slips, quarterly sales could remain in the tens of millions of won. Sales in the first and second quarters of 2026 illustrate this structure.

Financing and funding

Total liabilities of KRW 10.3 billion against total equity of KRW 2.24 billion leave limited financial room. The June 2026 issue of 5,808,766 shares and related-party borrowing are means of covering funding gaps. Repeated financing would increase both share dilution and financial burden.

Price overheating and trading volatility

On June 15, 2026, HiDeep was designated an investment-caution stock for one day after its shares rose 28.31% over three days and a single account's trading share met the exchange's criteria. In January 2026 an executive disclosed a sale of 10,000 shares on the open market. Because the share price can move apart from reported results, volatility is a significant risk.
