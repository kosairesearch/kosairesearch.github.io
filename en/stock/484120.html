Report overview

UTG Earnings Recovery; Vietnam Capacity Is the Next Test

Dowooinsys, a maker of ultra-thin glass (UTG) for foldable phones, saw 2025 profits shrink sharply but has posted operating profit gains in two consecutive quarters of 2026, with the start of the Vietnam second plant and customer launch volumes as the next tests.

02

Business structure

Founded in 2010, Dowooinsys makes ultra-thin glass (UTG) for foldable displays. Press reports say it commercialized the first UTG for foldable phones in 2019, and it runs production bases in Cheongju (Oksan) and Vietnam.

Revenue is concentrated in UTG, which accounted for 86.8% of sales in 1Q 2025, with exports at 81.8%. Its main customer channel is Samsung Display, through which UTG reaches Samsung Electronics foldables as well as Google and Chinese smartphone makers including OPPO, vivo and Xiaomi.

Reports say the company has been the sole supplier of UTG for all book-type Galaxy Z Fold models from the Fold 2 through the Fold 7, and Kiwoom Securities describes its share in book-type UTG as high.

Stated capacity is about 81,000 units a month in Korea and 100,000 in Vietnam, enough for roughly 20 million units a year. On diversification, it has completed large-area UTG development for Samsung notebook and IT products due in 2027 and is pursuing glass substrates for semiconductors.

It has two consolidated subsidiaries, both unlisted. Competition comes from CPI (colorless polyimide), a rival cover material, and from new entrants that are pressing on price. Factory automation in Oksan has lowered labor costs and improved yields, which is the company's main cost advantage.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show lower sales and weaker profitability. Sales fell 1.4% to KRW 139.8 billion in 2025 from KRW 141.7 billion in 2024, and the company cited lower unit volume and lower selling prices.

Operating profit dropped 65.1% to KRW 3.38 billion from KRW 9.7 billion, and the operating margin narrowed to 2.4% from 6.8%. A smaller foreign-currency translation gain as exchange rates moved also weighed on earnings.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 96.8% to KRW 490 million from KRW 15.3 billion, and net income including non-controlling interests moved in the same way, to KRW 490 million from KRW 15.3 billion.

Operating cash flow declined to KRW 4.08 billion from KRW 21.2 billion, so the earnings drop carried into cash generation. The balance sheet became lighter: the debt-to-equity ratio fell to 35.8% from 53.5%.

At end-2025, total equity was KRW 168.3 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 60.2 billion. Equity grew after the July 2025 KOSDAQ listing, which helps explain the lower ratio.

Quarterly results show a rebound from a low point. The company posted an operating loss of KRW 2.08 billion in 3Q 2025 and turned to an operating profit of KRW 3.79 billion in 4Q 2025. In 1Q 2026, sales fell 33.3% to KRW 26.8 billion and operating profit fell 52.0% to KRW 1.82 billion.

In 2Q 2026, sales rose 119.5% to KRW 58.9 billion and operating profit rose 350.6% to KRW 8.2 billion. In 3Q 2026, sales reached KRW 67.9 billion and operating profit reached KRW 13.4 billion, with operating profit rising for two consecutive quarters after the 1Q low and turning profitable from a year-earlier loss.

Higher utilization at the Vietnam plant and rising customer volumes are cited as drivers. In 1Q 2026, Kiwoom Securities attributed the gain to factory automation cost savings in Oksan and to exchange rates.

Controller-attributable net income has been less steady: KRW 7.49 billion in 4Q 2025, KRW 5.74 billion in 1Q 2026 and KRW 9.82 billion in 2Q 2026, then KRW 1.76 billion in 3Q 2026, down 82.1% from the prior quarter.

Operating profit rose in 3Q while this line fell, and the gap should be checked against the non-operating and tax items in the next filing. Over the last four quarters, controlling-shareholder net income totaled KRW 24.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The foldable smartphone market stalled in 2025, weighing on UTG suppliers' results. Kiwoom Securities expects volumes to rise from 2026 on new technologies and added models, and it cited global foldable smartphone market growth of 36% year on year.

In cycle terms, analysts place the company at the early stage of a medium-term growth phase, with new models in 2026 followed by capacity expansion and new customer wins in 2027.

Kiwoom expects Samsung Display panel mass production to ramp from the end of 2Q 2026 and a foldable iPhone launch around late 2026 to early 2027.

Dual UTG, which places a second UTG layer under the panel to reduce creasing, is expected to appear first in high-end models of some customers launching in 2H 2026, adding demand for back UTG beyond the front cover window.

On competition, a growing number of entrants makes price competitiveness and yield the key variables. CPI, a rival cover material, competes with UTG in foldable covers. Customer launch schedules and the pace of entrant ramp-ups will be the two main industry variables.

06

Outlook

The company has said it expects supply volumes to existing customers to increase in 2027.

The Vietnam second plant, reported to be twice the size of the existing factory, started construction in July 2026 and is planned to finish construction and equipment installation in 1Q 2027, with trial runs and production from 2Q 2027.

Kiwoom Securities separately reports plans to add production equipment for 1 million units a month in Vietnam, with construction starting in 2H 2026 and operations in 1H 2027. A pre-listing brokerage report put the total cost of the second plant at about KRW 53 billion.

Added equipment raises capacity, but the speed of utilization gains and yield stability will determine how much it contributes to profit. Kiwoom Securities projected 2026 sales of KRW 187.2 billion, operating profit of KRW 12.1 billion and a 7% operating margin.

That estimate came right after the 1Q 2026 results, and operating profit already reported for 1Q to 3Q 2026 (KRW 1.82 billion, KRW 8.2 billion and KRW 13.4 billion) totals more than the projected full-year figure.

Semiconductor glass substrates and the large-area UTG for Samsung notebook and IT products due in 2027 are cited as medium-term expansion axes. The 2026 annual report will show whether higher customer volumes translated into full-year sales and operating margin.

08

Bull factors

Vietnam utilization gains and expansion

Cumulative utilization at the Vietnam plant rose to 53.14% through 3Q 2026 from 33.37% for full-year 2025, driven by higher customer volumes. The second plant is planned to begin production in 1H 2027. If higher utilization translates into fixed-cost absorption, room for operating margin improvement grows.

Dual UTG and new model adoption

Kiwoom Securities expects dual UTG to be adopted first in high-end models from some customers launching in 2H 2026. Analysts view the company's volume production experience in book-type UTG as an advantage in that shift. The company itself has said it expects higher supply volumes to existing customers in 2027.

Quarterly earnings turnaround

Operating profit has risen for two consecutive quarters, from 2Q 2026 through 3Q 2026, and turned profitable in 3Q 2026 from a year-earlier loss. Sales climbed from the 1Q 2026 low of KRW 26.8 billion to KRW 58.9 billion in 2Q and KRW 67.9 billion in 3Q.

Bear factors

Sharp earnings decline in 2025

Operating profit fell 65.1% in 2025 and controlling-shareholder net income fell 96.8%. Lower unit volume and lower selling prices were cited as the main causes. Operating cash flow also dropped to KRW 4.08 billion from KRW 21.2 billion, so the earnings decline reached cash generation.

Concentration in product and customer

UTG accounted for 86.8% of sales in 1Q 2025, so product concentration is high. Dependence on the Samsung Display channel is also significant. Stalled growth in the foldable market during 2025 was cited as a background to weaker results.

Competitive entry and financial strain

A growing number of entrants makes it harder to keep price competitiveness. The company received a BB- credit rating in April 2026, and controlling-shareholder net income fell 82.1% from the prior quarter in 3Q 2026. The divergence between operating profit recovery and controlling-shareholder net income is also a weakness.

10

Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

UTG accounts for most revenue, and dependence on the Samsung Display channel is high. If customers' new foldable models sell poorly or foldable demand stalls, both volumes and selling prices could come under pressure. The 2025 decline from lower volumes and lower prices shows how directly this risk can hit results.

Expansion and utilization risk

The Vietnam second plant is slated to finish equipment installation in 1Q 2027 and begin trial runs in 2Q 2027, so any delay would slow the response to 2027 volumes. If the investment does not lift utilization, fixed-cost pressure could increase. Utilization stood at 53.14% year-to-date through 3Q 2026, only just above half of capacity.

Financial and currency risk

The BB- credit rating and the drop in 2025 operating cash flow constrain financial flexibility. As the smaller 2025 foreign-currency translation gain showed, exchange-rate moves can swing earnings.

Non-operating items and tax effects that make controlling-shareholder net income diverge from operating profit also need monitoring.

  2. End of 1Q 2027

  3. End of March 2027

  4. May 15, 2027

  5. Around July 2027

    Customer foldable launch events and the subsequent ramp to full production. Kiwoom Securities has noted that volume production typically starts from 2Q, timed to a July launch event.

12

Overall view

Dowooinsys is a company whose business is concentrated in UTG for foldable phones, and its 2025 annual results fell sharply on lower unit volumes and lower selling prices.

In 2026, higher Vietnam plant utilization and rising customer volumes lifted operating profit for two consecutive quarters, and the company turned profitable in 3Q 2026. Controlling-shareholder net income fell from the prior quarter in that same 3Q, which shows the earnings lines have not yet fully aligned.

On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio fell to 35.8% at end-2025, but the BB- credit rating and lower operating cash flow remain items to watch. Positive factors are dual UTG adoption, Vietnam capacity expansion and the recent recovery in quarterly profit.

Negative factors are concentration in product and customer, new competitors and the lingering effects of the sharp 2025 decline. The next checkpoints are the operating margin and controlling-shareholder net income trend in the 2026 annual report, and whether the Vietnam equipment starts operating in 1H 2027.
