Report overview

AI Agent Sales Pass Half of Revenue; Profit Recovery Still Quarter-Level

WISEnut's AI agent share of revenue passed half in the third quarter of 2026 and operating profit returned to a quarterly gain, but annual operating profit has fallen since its 2023 peak, so the durability of the recovery is still to be confirmed.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

WISEnut is an AI agent specialist founded in 2000 that builds on natural language processing technology. It started with enterprise search engines, moved through big data analytics and AI chatbots, and has widened into generative AI and AI agent businesses.

In the third quarter of 2026, AI agent revenue was KRW 5.03 billion, or 53.1% of total sales, compared with 26.6% in the same quarter of 2025, according to company figures. Including chatbots, AI-related revenue was about 31.6% of the total in the third quarter of 2025, as reported by Edaily.

Key products include the retrieval-augmented generation solution WISE iRAG, the large language model WISE LLOA, the agent development and management tool WISE Agent Labs, and the integrated platform WISE Agent Sphere.

WISE iRAG is supplied to public agencies such as the National Police Agency through the Public Procurement Service's digital service mall. Gyeonggi Province opened a generative AI platform, the first of its kind among local governments, and the company cited it as a public-sector AI adoption case.

The company said it served more than 5,500 client organizations as of 2025, and it claims the top domestic share in search engine and chatbot markets. A stock information site's company profile puts roughly 60% of total revenue in the public sector.

AI agents made up 83.1% of new orders in the second half of 2026 through October 30, with an average contract value of about KRW 620 million per deal. Competitors in the same IT software sector include large system integrators such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, as well as other AI software firms.

The company is positioning its domain-specific AI agents with reference projects in civil administration, industrial safety, logistics, finance and legal fields.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 34.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 35.2 billion in 2023, the highest of the four years, before declining 0.7% to KRW 34.9 billion in 2024 and 0.5% to KRW 34.7 billion in 2025.

Operating profit grew from KRW 3.05 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.46 billion in 2023 (up 13.5%), then fell to KRW 1.7 billion in 2024 (down 50.7%) and KRW 114 million in 2025 (down 93.3%). The operating margin moved from 8.8% in 2022 and 9.8% in 2023 to 4.9% in 2024 and 0.3% in 2025.

The company attributed the 2024 decline to higher costs incurred while maintaining its capability to serve the new generative AI market amid domestic political instability and an economic slowdown.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent swung from a KRW 5.9 billion net loss in 2022 to KRW 4.27 billion in 2023, then fell to KRW 3.05 billion in 2024 (down 28.5%) and KRW 1.75 billion in 2025 (down 42.5%).

In 2025, profit attributable to owners of the parent (KRW 1.75 billion) exceeded operating profit (KRW 114 million), and the reasons for that gap should be traced through non-operating items in the periodic report.

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 6.32 billion and total equity was KRW 67.7 billion, all of it attributable to owners of the parent, and the debt ratio fell from 15.2% in 2022 to 9.3% in 2025.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 5.88 billion in 2022, KRW 6.01 billion in 2023 and KRW 4.67 billion in 2024 to negative KRW 519 million in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue of KRW 8.81 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.17 billion in the third quarter of 2025 gave way to revenue of KRW 12.4 billion (up 40.5% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 2.15 billion in the fourth quarter.

Revenue fell to KRW 7.22 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 41.7% from the prior quarter), with an operating loss of KRW 1.43 billion, and then rose to KRW 7.43 billion in the second quarter with a narrower operating loss of KRW 165 million.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 9.47 billion (up 27.3% from the prior quarter and 7.4% year on year), operating profit was KRW 1.63 billion (up 39.7% year on year), and profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 1.87 billion (up 17.8% year on year).

The trailing four-quarter sum of profit attributable to owners of the parent is KRW 3.52 billion.

Most of the third-quarter operating profit improvement came from SG&A falling by roughly KRW 1.05 billion year on year, which the company linked to lower labor costs and the retroactive recognition of prior-period government R&D subsidies, a effect the company said was limited to that quarter. Over the same period, cost of revenue rose 32.8%, and gross margin fell from 57.3% to 47.1%.

05

Industry analysis

The company says the broader AX (AI transformation) trend across the market is strengthening, and it is shifting its center of gravity from search and chatbots toward AI agents built on its natural language processing technology.

In the public sector, it led a pilot project for an HR-affairs AI assistant for the Ministry of Personnel Management, and Gyeonggi Province's generative AI platform is an example of local government adoption.

Traditional search and chatbot revenue has been flat since 2023 while the AI agent share rose from 26.6% to 53.1%, which suggests the business is in a transition from conventional software to AI agents.

Cumulative total revenue grew only 7.9% in the first three quarters of 2026, well below the growth rate of AI agent revenue, which was about 3.4 times the prior-year level.

Competition overlaps with large system integrators such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, as well as AI software companies, in public and enterprise markets. The company is focusing on reference projects in civil administration, industrial safety, logistics, finance and legal fields.

AI agent build-out projects require hardware, external solution purchases and staffing, so expansion raises cost burden alongside revenue. The number-one domestic share in search engine and chatbot markets reflects the company's own description and has not been cross-checked against independent market data. Comparing profitability with peers will depend on tracking the cost-of-revenue ratio quarter by quarter.

06

Outlook

In a filing on November 16, 2026, the company entered into a trust contract to buy back KRW 5 billion of its own shares and said it plans to cancel all of the acquired shares.

The decision announced in November is the second treasury-share buyback and cancellation decided in 2026, following a purchase of about KRW 10 billion of shares from June to July that was fully cancelled in July. Cumulative new AI agent orders in the first three quarters of 2026 totaled KRW 1.708 billion.

According to an announcement on November 12, 2026, the company won a project to integrate the mobile and PC channels of the T-commerce channel Shopping N&T operated by TRN into AI agents, and it set a target of opening the full service in the first quarter of 2027.

The project applies the WISE iRAG retrieval-augmented generation solution and the WISE LLOA large language model, and uses WISE Agent Labs for service operation and usage analysis.

The CEO said public-sector AI adoption is expected to expand in 2027 toward task upgrades and administrative automation, and that the company will aggressively pursue both public and private AX markets. The company's stated direction is to balance shareholder returns with growth investment.

The next checkpoints are whether the share cancellation is executed as announced, whether the Shopping N&T project meets its first-quarter 2027 target, and whether AI agent revenue growth translates into margin gains as build-out costs fall.

Because the company says labor savings should persist while the subsidy catch-up was limited to the third quarter of 2026, SG&A trends should be read quarter by quarter.

08

Bull factors

Business mix shift visible in the numbers

AI agent revenue reached KRW 5.03 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 114.8% year on year, lifting its share of total sales to 53.1%. Cumulative AI agent revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 1.119 billion, about 3.4 times the prior-year level.

AI agents also made up 83.1% of second-half new orders, which the company cites as evidence that the shift is durable.

Quarterly profit has turned

Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 1.63 billion, turning positive from an operating loss of KRW 165 million in the prior quarter and rising 39.7% year on year. Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 1.87 billion in the same quarter, up 17.8% year on year. The reversal of the 2024 to 2025 decline is the core of the bullish case.

Balance sheet and capital returns

Total liabilities were KRW 6.32 billion at the end of 2025, and the debt ratio fell to 9.3% from 15.2% in 2022. Two treasury-share buyback and cancellation decisions were made in 2026, the latest for KRW 5 billion. Total equity of KRW 67.7 billion at the end of 2025 was entirely attributable to owners of the parent. The company points to its public-sector client base and more than 5,500 client organizations as strengths.

09

Bear factors

Cost burden in build-out projects

Cost of revenue rose 32.8% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, lowering gross margin from 57.3% to 47.1%. The company reported an operating loss of KRW 1.43 billion in the first quarter of 2026. The operating margin was only 0.3% in 2025.

SG&A reduction contributed heavily to the third-quarter operating profit improvement, and the company said the retroactive subsidy recognition was limited to that quarter.

Stagnant headline revenue and quarterly swings

Annual revenue peaked at KRW 35.2 billion in 2023 and then declined in 2024 and 2025, two years in a row. First-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 7.22 billion fell 41.7% from the prior quarter. Cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 rose 7.9% year on year, well below the AI agent growth rate. As the AI agent share rose from 26.6% to 53.1%, the share of the remaining business shrank.

Public-sector dependence and one-off items

A stock information site's company profile puts roughly 60% of total revenue in the public sector. Public procurement can make quarterly revenue lumpy depending on budget execution timing. The third-quarter 2026 SG&A reduction includes the retroactive recognition of prior-period government R&D subsidies.

The profit turn also came off a low base, with a 2025 operating margin of 0.3%, which is a variable for how durable the profit recovery will be.

10

Risk factors

Cost and execution risk

AI agent build-out projects carry significant hardware, external software purchase and staffing costs, so profitability can swing project by project. Press coverage reported that the company sees cutting equipment and staffing costs that rose with build-out expansion as a challenge.

The company expects labor savings to persist, but whether the cost ratio actually improves needs to be confirmed with quarterly figures.

Public procurement and policy risk

Because the public sector accounts for a large share of revenue, results are sensitive to government budgets, procurement timing and changes in procurement rules. The broader expansion of public AI adoption is a supportive trend, but concentrated orders in particular quarters can enlarge revenue swings. Quarterly revenue has been uneven, as the 41.7% sequential decline in the first quarter of 2026 shows.

Competition and technology change risk

Large system integrators and AI services built on big tech platforms compete for the same public and enterprise customers. As generative AI models and open-source ecosystems change quickly, the differentiation of in-house search and RAG technology could narrow.

The company is responding with a domain-specific strategy, and the key question is whether reference wins keep pace with competition.

12

Overall view

WISEnut is in the middle of shifting its business from a search and chatbot software provider to an AI agent specialist.

AI agents accounted for 53.1% of revenue in the third quarter of 2026, operating profit turned positive in the same quarter, and cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters also returned to a profit.

Annual operating profit, however, peaked at KRW 3.46 billion in 2023 and declined in 2024 and 2025, falling to KRW 114 million in 2025.

After an operating loss of KRW 1.43 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 165 million in the second quarter, the return to profit in the third quarter means durability has so far been confirmed over only a few quarters.

Build-out expansion raised cost of revenue by 32.8% and lowered gross margin, and part of the profit improvement relies on one-off factors such as lower SG&A and a retroactive subsidy recognition.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio was 9.3% at the end of 2025 and total equity was KRW 67.7 billion, and two treasury-share cancellations were decided in 2026.

On orders, cumulative new AI agent orders for the first three quarters of 2026 totaled KRW 1.708 billion, and the Shopping N&T AI project is targeting a first-quarter 2027 launch.

The next checkpoints are the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report, the gross margin and operating profit in the first quarter of 2027, and the actual execution of the treasury-share cancellation.
