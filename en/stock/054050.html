02

Business structure

NH Nongwoobio is a specialist company focused on vegetable seeds. As of the first quarter of 2026, seed operations accounted for 81.61% of revenue and farm inputs such as growing media and fertilizers for 18.39%.

Chili peppers are the largest item, while tomato, cucumber, onion and watermelon also contribute, and Korea IR Council judges that dependence on any single crop is not excessive.

The company estimates its share of the domestic vegetable seed market at 21% and says it accounts for more than 50% of domestic vegetable seed export volume. It operates six overseas units in the United States, China, India, Indonesia, Türkiye and Myanmar, and planned to set up a Mexico unit in early July 2026.

Its overseas sales network now covers more than 80 countries and 120 customers. Overseas sales first exceeded domestic sales in 2024. Growth has come mainly from the India, US, Türkiye and Indonesia units, while China and Myanmar grew only modestly.

Research and development spending is about 20% of standalone seed sales. The company says it ranks about 13th globally by seed market share.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 146.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 135.8 billion in 2023 (down 7.1% year on year), then rose to KRW 144.7 billion in 2024 (up 6.5%) and KRW 158.8 billion in 2025 (up 9.8%), growing for two consecutive years.

Operating profit went from KRW 11 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.94 billion in 2023 (down 18.8%), then rose to KRW 11.1 billion in 2024 (up 24.1%) and KRW 13.6 billion in 2025 (up 22.9%). The operating margin ran 7.5% in 2022, 6.6% in 2023, 7.7% in 2024 and 8.6% in 2025, the highest of the four years.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 9.88 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.3 billion, KRW 10.8 billion and KRW 12.3 billion in 2025, growing for three consecutive years from 2023. Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 12.3 billion in 2025, the same figure.

Operating cash flow was KRW 9.7 billion in 2022, KRW 16.3 billion in 2023, KRW 19 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.9 billion in 2025. In its 2025 mid-year release, the company cited growth in overseas exports and better results at its main overseas units as profit drivers. Quarterly results are seasonal.

According to Korea IR Council, the first and third quarters are peak seasons, while the fourth quarter carries disposal costs for unsold seeds. The fourth quarter of 2025 swung to an operating loss of KRW 851 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 958 million.

The first quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 5.37 billion. Second-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 46.1 billion rose 26.8% from the prior quarter, but operating profit of KRW 2.5 billion fell 53.4%.

Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 37.9 billion fell 2.6% year on year, while operating profit of KRW 5.89 billion rose 57.9%. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 12.6 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The global vegetable seed market was valued at KRW 18.7 trillion in 2025 and is expected to grow at an annual rate of 5.4%, according to market research firm Fortune Business Insights as cited by Hankyung.

The Mexican market is in the low-KRW hundreds of millions of US dollars and is growing 4 to 6% a year, according to the same report. China has raised the share of domestic varieties since its 2021 seed industry promotion policy, which changes the conditions for Korean seed companies selling there.

In Korea, vegetable seed sales fell 3.4% from KRW 416.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 402.6 billion in 2024. The company is responding to the domestic decline by diversifying into overseas markets.

On competition, Korea IR Council says accumulated breeding capability and a long verification system make it hard for new rivals to enter. Its Nonghyup distribution network and broad crop portfolio are also cited as sources of its domestic position.

Rising concern over climate change, pests and food security has raised the strategic value of superior varieties, according to analysts. The company is also pursuing localization of imported seed varieties.

06

Outlook

The company has set mid-term targets of entering the global top 10 seed companies by 2030 with annual sales of KRW 200 billion to KRW 300 billion, and an operating margin in the 10% range, according to Korea IR Council.

On January 27, 2026, the company said it had completed a new R&D facility at its Türkiye unit, aimed at commercializing tomato, pepper and cucumber varieties suited to Mediterranean climates.

In a January 2026 interview, the CEO said the company was considering acquiring a vegetable seed company through its US unit with roughly KRW 20 billion. The company planned to set up a Mexico unit in early July 2026 and later a Spanish unit to pursue European markets.

The company plans to expand from seven countries to ten by adding Europe and Southeast Asia. On September 29, 2026, the Nonghyup Agribusiness board approved folding the seed business of its seed center into NH Nongwoobio, with completion targeted by December 2026.

Korea IR Council forecast that the 2026 operating margin would ease from 8.6% in 2025 to 8.0%, citing higher seed production costs from a stronger exchange rate. On shareholder returns, the company raised its 2025 year-end cash dividend by 14% from the prior year.

08

Bull factors

Overseas growth is shifting the earnings center of gravity

Combined sales of the main overseas units rose from KRW 44.6 billion in 2021 to KRW 67.4 billion in 2025, a compound annual growth rate of 10.9%. India sales rose from KRW 14.2 billion in 2021 to KRW 27.2 billion in 2025 and overtook the Beijing unit in 2023. Overseas sales exceeded domestic sales for the first time in 2024, which shifts where the revenue base sits.

Domestic leadership and a high barrier in breeding

It invests about 20% of standalone seed sales in R&D. Korea IR Council views its accumulated breeding experience and verification system as a barrier that makes entry hard for new rivals. Its Nonghyup-affiliated distribution network is also cited as a base for domestic sales.

Net cash position and a low debt ratio

Total liabilities were KRW 74.5 billion at the end of 2025, and the debt ratio was 26.8%. Cash and cash equivalents were about KRW 32.2 billion at the end of the first quarter of 2026.

Korea IR Council judges that the net cash structure leaves room to pursue R&D, overseas expansion, acquisitions and shareholder returns at the same time.

09

Bear factors

Domestic sales decline and rising Chinese self-sufficiency

Domestic vegetable seed sales fell 3.4% from KRW 416.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 402.6 billion in 2024. China has pushed domestic varieties since its 2021 seed promotion policy, raising their share of its market. Whether overseas growth can offset the domestic decline is the key question.

Quarterly earnings volatility and a forecast margin dip in 2026

The fourth quarter of 2025 recorded an operating loss of KRW 851 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 958 million. Second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.5 billion fell 53.4% from the prior quarter. Korea IR Council forecast the 2026 operating margin would ease to 8.0%.

Persistent market discount and acquisition funding pressure

Korea IR Council views the valuation discount as hard to narrow without an improvement in profitability. The roughly KRW 20 billion under review for a US-led acquisition is a sizable amount against about KRW 32.2 billion in cash at the end of the first quarter of 2026. How the company funds such a deal is a point to watch.

10

Risk factors

Currency and cost pressure

A stronger exchange rate raises seed production costs and overseas seed multiplication expenses together. Higher prices for farm input raw materials can also push up the cost ratio. Korea IR Council forecast the 2026 cost of sales ratio would rise by about 1.6 percentage points.

Overseas concentration and costs of new regions

Growth at the Chinese and Myanmar units has been limited, so results lean on the India, US, Türkiye and Indonesia units. In new regions such as Mexico and Spain, startup costs and securing local customers are variables. A policy or trade change in one country could affect overseas sales broadly.

Execution of the integration and acquisitions

Integrating the seed business of the Nonghyup seed center by December 2026 is underway. Merging R&D and production and distribution teams can improve efficiency but also adds scheduling and organizational burden. An acquisition through the US unit also depends on target selection and the size of funding.

12

Overall view

NH Nongwoobio is a leading domestic vegetable seed company that recovered from its 2023 low, with 2025 sales of KRW 158.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 13.6 billion on a reported basis. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders has grown for three consecutive years from 2023.

Quarterly results in 2026 remain volatile, as third-quarter operating profit of KRW 5.89 billion rose while net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.95 billion fell.

Growth is coming from overseas units, whose combined sales grew at a compound rate of 10.9% from 2021 to 2025, with Mexico and Spain expansion and a US-led acquisition under review.

Weak points include the decline in domestic vegetable seed sales, currency and cost pressure, and Korea IR Council's forecast of a slight dip in the 2026 operating margin.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio was 26.8% at the end of 2025, and controlling shareholders' equity of KRW 277.7 billion accounts for most of total equity of KRW 278.4 billion.

The next checkpoints are completion of the seed business integration in December 2026, the 2026 operating margin in the annual report, and first-quarter 2027 results.
