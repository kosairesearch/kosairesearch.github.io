Report overview

Server orders drive a sharp quarterly revenue swing at Pentastone

Pentastone Electronics is widening beyond its power amplifier module core into systems and server hardware, and revenue rose sharply from the second quarter of 2026 while attributable net income fell in the third quarter.

02

Business structure

Pentastone Electronics focuses on power amplifier modules (PAM) and RF front-end modules (FEM) used in mobile handsets, and it is described as the only dedicated PAM specialist in Korea. It listed on KOSDAQ in 2020 and broadened its scope through acquisitions in 2024 and 2025.

The 2025 merger brought smart automation equipment into the group, and by the company's account systems and equipment reached 41.4% of 2025 revenue. The company is also growing an electronics contract manufacturing business through a subsidiary acquired in 2024 and 2025.

A third-party revenue table lists mobile RFFE at 93.93%, vehicle RFFE at 4.92% and other at 1.15%, but it does not state its scope or date, so it is hard to compare directly with the 41.4% figure. Samsung Electronics is described as a heavy customer concentration.

The AI server business started from selection as a global business partner of Super Micro Computer (SMCI), and the company initially plans distribution and maintenance before possibly moving into OEM work over the medium term. Global competitors in mobile PAM are cited as Skyworks, Broadcom, Qorvo and Qualcomm.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 51.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 68.1 billion in 2023 (up 32.6% year on year), KRW 122.1 billion in 2024 (up 79.2%) and KRW 125.5 billion in 2025 (up 2.7%). Revenue has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 is the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating profit rose from KRW 6.42 billion in 2023 to KRW 10.8 billion in 2024, then fell to KRW 3.91 billion in 2025 (down 63.7%), and 2024 is the largest year in that span. Operating margin was 8.4% in 2022, 9.4% in 2023, 8.8% in 2024 and 3.1% in 2025, so margin compressed sharply in 2025 even as revenue grew.

The company attributed the 2025 operating profit decline to higher personnel-related costs, intangible asset amortization and commission fees tied to the Yeongjin Hitech merger.

Net income including non-controlling interests moved from a loss of KRW 7.72 billion in 2024 to KRW 16.4 billion in 2025, which the company linked to higher gains on fair value through profit or loss financial assets.

Attributable net income was a loss of KRW 10.1 billion in 2022, a profit of KRW 19.3 billion in 2023, a loss of KRW 7.83 billion in 2024 and a profit of KRW 17.2 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 26.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 28.6 billion, KRW 35.7 billion, KRW 74.4 billion (up 108.7% quarter on quarter) and KRW 99.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 1.18 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 56.46 million, KRW 4.13 billion and KRW 13.1 billion, rising for two consecutive quarters from the second quarter of 2026.

Attributable net income peaked at KRW 15.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then fell to KRW 5.72 billion and KRW 767 million, declining for two consecutive quarters, and the last four quarters total KRW 27 billion.

The reason the third quarter's operating profit of KRW 13.1 billion translated into only KRW 767 million of attributable net income is not identified in the disclosed summary figures.

05

Industry analysis

Mobile RF demand is described as maturing with the smartphone market, while vehicle electronics are growing on the expansion of the global smart car market and 5G network development. The spread of 5G smartphones is widening the RF FEM product range.

The company is described as competing with six global firms using Doherty-type power amplifier design technology. Global competitors named in mobile PAM include Skyworks, Broadcom, Qorvo and Qualcomm.

On costs, raw materials and outsourced processing rose sharply in 2024, and imported wafer costs were reported to have doubled to KRW 28.8 billion. The company has said its server business is tied to rising investment in global AI data centers.

Automation equipment is viewed as a medium-term growth market in smart factories. Server equipment purchasing may carry a different margin structure from mobile PAM, but that difference cannot be confirmed from the disclosures reviewed.

06

Outlook

In vehicle electronics, a North American electric vehicle maker's order for 5G telematics RF FEMs for all 2026 production models was reported as confirmed in August 2025, with about KRW 30 billion to KRW 40 billion of new annual revenue expected.

Shinhan Investment projected 2025 revenue of KRW 212.1 billion in August 2025, while the disclosed 2025 revenue was KRW 125.5 billion. In servers, the supply contract disclosed on April 30, 2026 equaled about 81.99% of recent revenue and ran through August 14, 2026.

The company has described a plan to expand from distribution and maintenance into OEM work over the medium term. A private exchangeable bond totaling KRW 22.0 billion was reported, with proceeds going to server parts and equipment purchases.

On shareholder returns, the board decided in April 2026 to terminate a KRW 3.0 billion share buyback trust, and a KRW 5.0 billion share cancellation was disclosed in August 2026. In automation, semiconductor test handlers, robot arms and optical module automation equipment were named as new growth drivers.

08

Bull factors

Vehicle RFFE orders and revenue diversification

A North American electric vehicle maker's order for 5G telematics RF FEMs was reported for all 2026 production models. Vehicle RFFE is still a small share of the mix, but the direction is toward expansion as mobile matures.

Wider portfolio and a growing systems share

The 2025 merger brought automation equipment into the group, and systems and equipment reached 41.4% of 2025 revenue by the company's account. A mix that includes automation, contract manufacturing and servers could spread the volatility of a single mobile business.

Sharp 2026 revenue growth and a large server contract

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 35.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 99.7 billion in the third quarter, and the server supply contract was disclosed on April 30, 2026.

Operating profit rose for two straight quarters to KRW 13.1 billion in the third quarter, consistent with the company's account of server business scaling up.

09

Bear factors

The 2025 operating profit drop and thin margins

Operating profit fell 63.7% to KRW 3.91 billion in 2025, and the operating margin dropped to 3.1%. The company cited personnel costs, amortization and fees tied to acquisitions. Net income in 2025 rose on fair value gains, a different source from operating earnings.

Attributable profit fell in the third quarter of 2026

Attributable net income fell from KRW 15.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 5.72 billion and then KRW 767 million, declining for two consecutive quarters.

Against third-quarter operating profit of KRW 13.1 billion, the gap to attributable profit is large, and its cause is not identifiable from the summary figures.

Server contract timing and revenue durability

The server supply contract ran through August 14, 2026. The initial business is distribution and maintenance, with OEM work a medium-term task. Whether revenue holds after the contract period is not yet confirmed.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and mobile demand

Samsung Electronics is cited as a major revenue concentration. Mobile demand is maturing, so volume swings in the core product can pass straight through to results.

Business transition and post-acquisition costs

After the 2025 acquisitions, personnel and amortization costs rose and operating profit fell. Running server, automation and contract manufacturing at once puts cost and operating management under test.

Quarterly earnings volatility and financial burden

Quarterly results have swung widely, from an operating loss in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 13.1 billion of operating profit in the third quarter of 2026. Total liabilities were KRW 98.6 billion at the end of 2025 with a debt ratio of 73.9%. A KRW 22.0 billion private exchangeable bond was issued in 2026.

12

Overall view

Pentastone Electronics is moving from a mobile power amplifier module base toward vehicle RF, automation equipment and servers. Revenue rose sharply from the second quarter of 2026, and operating profit rose for two straight quarters to KRW 13.1 billion in the third quarter.

Against that, the 2025 operating margin fell to 3.1%, and attributable net income fell to KRW 767 million in the third quarter of 2026. Because the revenue jump overlaps with the server contract period, whether revenue holds after that contract ends is the key point to confirm.

The contribution of vehicle orders and automation to results is still limited in what the disclosures show. This report is for information only and contains no buy or sell opinion.
