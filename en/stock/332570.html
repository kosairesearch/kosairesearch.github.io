02

Business structure

The company started out supplying radio-frequency modules used in mobile handsets, with power amplifier modules, switches and filters as its main product lines. Its Yeongjin Hi-Tech acquisition in January 2025, followed by a merger in June, expanded its automation equipment business.

The business is now organized into three areas: wireless communication components, automation equipment and servers. Mix has shifted quickly: mobile electronic components fell from 86.7% of sales in 2024 and 45.7% in 2025 to 17.3% in the first nine months of 2026.

Servers made up 79% of third-quarter 2026 revenue, and newly generated server sales of KRW 78.77 billion exceeded the total year-on-year revenue increase.

Automation equipment is built to customer order, so quarterly sales depend on delivery and inspection timing; that business dropped from KRW 19.6 billion in the first quarter and KRW 22.5 billion in the second to KRW 4.3 billion in the third.

The company said it is expanding collaboration with several global top-tier server makers beyond its existing partners and is pursuing additional orders.

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated revenue rose from KRW 51.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 68.1 billion in 2023 (up 32.6%) and KRW 122.1 billion in 2024 (up 79.2%), then reached KRW 125.5 billion in 2025 (up 2.7%). Revenue has grown for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, but growth slowed sharply in 2025.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 10.8 billion in 2024 and fell to KRW 3.91 billion in 2025 (down 63.7%), with the operating margin dropping from 8.8% to 3.1%.

Attributable net profit swung to KRW 17.2 billion in 2025 from an attributable net loss of KRW 7.83 billion in 2024, but net profit rising while operating profit fell points to non-operating items, which the annual report notes should be checked.

Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 1.46 billion in the third quarter of 2025 became an operating loss of KRW 1.18 billion in the fourth quarter, then operating profit of KRW 56.46 million in the first quarter of 2026.

Operating profit rose to KRW 4.13 billion in the second quarter and KRW 13.1 billion in the third, up 802.9% from a year earlier, and the company said this was the highest of the last five quarters.

Third-quarter gross margin fell to 18.4% from 24.8%, but operating margin rose from 5.4% to 13.2% as selling and administrative costs shrank relative to sales. Profit mix moved from product sales to goods supply, and the product cost ratio was 103.8% in the third quarter.

Attributable net profit peaked at KRW 15.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then fell to KRW 5.72 billion in the second and KRW 767 million in the third, with the last four quarters totaling KRW 27 billion. Operating cash flow was a net outflow of KRW 40.56 billion in the third quarter.

Contract liabilities fell from KRW 76.68 billion to KRW 4.01 billion as deliveries were completed, and that reflects advance payments being recognized as revenue rather than cash coming back in.

Cash and equivalents fell from KRW 17.97 billion to KRW 8.07 billion, while short-term borrowings rose from KRW 33.0 billion to KRW 35.13 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for servers is the main backdrop, with the company and press reports both pointing to strong purchases of AI servers and related equipment. Server sales are lumpy, however, because they depend on when large supply contracts are delivered.

A KRW 69.7 million contract signed on April 30, 2026 finished recognizing revenue across the second and third quarters. The KRW 30.8 billion contract signed on July 22 had KRW 4.1 billion recognized in the third quarter, with the remaining KRW 26.7 billion expected in the fourth quarter.

The server cycle position therefore depends heavily on which quarter a few large contracts are recognized. Mobile is the segment that is shrinking in relative terms.

One senior researcher at a large brokerage was reported as saying the company is strengthening its position in the global supply chain as a leader in domestic 5G power amplifier localization.

The same report flagged profit-taking after a sharp rally and weaker demand from global economic slowdowns as continuing variables.

The company said it plans to extend its scope from sales, custom design, installation and management into server assembly, and to strengthen links with its semiconductor design and packaging, automation and automotive electronics businesses.

Figures comparing profitability or server market share with peers were not found in the sources reviewed, so the competitive position can only be described qualitatively.

06

Outlook

The company said it is expanding collaboration with several global top-tier server makers beyond its existing partners and is pursuing additional orders. Two contract timelines can be confirmed from disclosures. The April 30 contract finished recognizing revenue across the second and third quarters.

The remaining KRW 26.7 billion of the July 22 contract, a KRW 30.8 billion deal, is scheduled for recognition in the fourth quarter, and how much of it lands in fourth-quarter results will show the near-term server trajectory.

Carrying out additional orders requires advance procurement of large equipment and components and cash to pay suppliers, so working-capital management is a precondition. With operating cash flow turning to a net outflow in the third quarter, that point looks central to order expansion.

The company said it is considering various measures to raise corporate value and shareholder value, but no specific plan or timeline has been confirmed. Automation equipment sales fell to KRW 4.3 billion in the third quarter, and no clear recovery timing has been set out.

Three items are worth tracking: customer mix and working-capital detail in the third-quarter quarterly report, whether the remaining server revenue is recognized in the fourth quarter, and whether the 2026 annual operating margin recovers from 3.1% in 2025.

08

Bull factors

Server growth and a quarterly earnings rebound

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 99.7 billion and operating profit KRW 13.1 billion. The company attributed the improvement to rising demand for high-performance servers. The third-quarter operating margin was 13.2%, up from 5.4% a year earlier.

Customer base expansion and additional orders

The company is expanding collaboration with several global top-tier server makers beyond its existing partners and pursuing additional orders. It also plans to extend into server assembly. It expects synergies with its semiconductor design, packaging, automation and automotive businesses. Whether these plans convert into orders will need to be confirmed through later disclosures.

Lower mobile dependence and lighter overhead burden

Mobile electronic components fell from 86.7% of sales in 2024 to 17.3% in the first nine months of 2026, shifting the mix toward servers and automation. Selling and administrative expenses fell from 19.4% of sales to 5.2%, as revenue grew faster than fixed overhead.

09

Bear factors

Product-segment loss and cost pressure

Third-quarter product sales were KRW 17.34 billion against costs of KRW 17.99 billion, producing a gross loss of KRW 0.66 billion. The product cost ratio was 103.8%. Gross margin fell to 18.4% from 24.8% a year earlier.

Operating profit growth relied on profit from goods supply, so the source of earnings has shifted from in-house products to goods.

Operating cash outflow and rising borrowings

Third-quarter operating profit rose to KRW 13.14 billion, but operating cash flow was a net outflow of KRW 40.56 billion. Cash and equivalents fell from KRW 17.97 billion to KRW 8.07 billion, and short-term borrowings rose from KRW 33.0 billion to KRW 35.13 billion. Profit and cash moving in different directions is the main bearish point.

Customer concentration and business volatility

B company accounted for half of nine-month 2026 consolidated revenue. Automation equipment sales fell from KRW 22.5 billion in the second quarter to KRW 4.3 billion in the third. Attributable net profit fell from KRW 10.2 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 767 million in the third quarter of 2026. Results depend on a few customers and contract delivery timing, which makes quarterly volatility high.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration risk

Two customers each accounted for more than 10% of sales. A change in one customer's order schedule or contract terms could move quarterly revenue sharply. The company says it is diversifying its customers, but concentration remains high under the current revenue mix.

Working capital and borrowing risk

Continued server orders require stable working-capital management to fund equipment and component purchases in advance. Changes in operating assets and liabilities produced a cash outflow of KRW 51.35 billion in the third quarter.

With cash falling and short-term borrowings rising, reliance on external funding could grow if order volumes expand.

Business mix shift and demand risk

Server revenue is also concentrated around the timing of large supply contracts. Because servers account for 79% of revenue, a slowdown in server demand could shrink results materially. A brokerage researcher flagged weaker downstream demand from a global economic slowdown as a variable.

12

Overall view

Third-quarter 2026 results were a quarter in which revenue and operating profit both rose on the back of server sales expansion. At the same time, the product segment posted a gross loss, automation equipment sales fell sharply, and operating cash flow turned to a net outflow in the same quarter.

The recovery in quarterly operating profit after a weaker 2025 reflects a shift in business mix, but it leans heavily on the timing of large contract recognition. The fall in mobile's share to 17.3% also means greater reliance on servers and automation.

Because quarterly earnings swing widely, the interpretation of any multiple depends on the period chosen. Bullish factors are server demand, pursuit of additional orders and easing overhead; bearish factors are the operating cash outflow, rising borrowings and customer concentration.

The next checkpoints are cash flow in the third-quarter report, whether the KRW 26.7 billion of server revenue is recognized in the fourth quarter, and the annual operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
