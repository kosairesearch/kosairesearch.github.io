Special screens lift earnings; financing burden remains

02

Business structure

CJ CGV operates a theater business centered on film screens in Korea and overseas, with technology premium screens such as SCREENX, 4DX and IMAX as its growth axis. The company said the share of moviegoers using technology premium screens rose from 6.7 percent in Q3 2019 to 18.1 percent in Q3 2026.

In the same quarter, domestic theater revenue was KRW 259 billion, up 32 percent year on year, and operating profit was KRW 28 billion, turning from a KRW 5.6 billion loss a year earlier to a profit, according to company announcements on November 6, 2026.

Overseas, China posted revenue of KRW 80.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 6.6 billion, while Turkiye swung to profit with revenue of KRW 41.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.6 billion.

CJ 4DPLEX, which handles the 4DX technology, is tied to a global 4DX box office that reached USD 204 million in Q3 2026, up 33 percent.

The AI-transformation subsidiary posted Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 224.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 16.5 billion, and it is expanding AX project wins in AI factory, logistics, VFX and media AI.

Revenue sources are broadening toward premium screens, overseas operations and subsidiaries, but consolidated operating profit combines the theater core with subsidiary contributions, so the two need to be read separately.

KB Securities estimated the company's share at 35 percent in a July 2026 report and noted that some major competitors had applied for court-led rehabilitation. Box-office swings from a handful of releases still drive revenue.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 1.28 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.55 trillion in 2023 (up 20.6 percent), KRW 1.96 trillion in 2024 (up 26.7 percent) and KRW 2.28 trillion in 2025 (up 16.2 percent), marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025. 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit swung from an operating loss of KRW 76.8 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 49.1 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 75.9 billion in 2024 (up 54.8 percent) and KRW 96.2 billion in 2025 (up 26.7 percent), growing for two consecutive years.

Operating profit grew faster than revenue in 2025, lifting the operating margin to 4.2 percent from -6.0 percent in 2022, 3.2 percent in 2023 and 3.9 percent in 2024.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders were KRW 166.2 billion in 2022, KRW 96.2 billion in 2023 (narrower), KRW 171 billion in 2024 (wider) and KRW 143.8 billion in 2025 (narrower), a four-year run of losses.

Samsung Securities and KB Securities both pointed to financing costs as the reason operating gains did not reach the bottom line. Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 214.5 billion in 2022, KRW 123.4 billion in 2023, KRW 175.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 143.4 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 107.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 214.9 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 816.2 percent in 2022 and 1,122.7 percent in 2023 to 593.0 percent in 2024 and 533.9 percent in 2025, with total equity of KRW 650.3 billion (KRW 570.2 billion attributable to controlling shareholders) and total liabilities of KRW 3.47 trillion at the end of 2025.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 583.1 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 667.1 billion in Q4 2025 (up 14.4 percent quarter on quarter), dipped to KRW 573.4 billion in Q1 2026 (down 14.0 percent), then recovered to KRW 594 billion in Q2 and KRW 688.1 billion in Q3 2026 (up 15.9 percent quarter on quarter).

Operating profit moved from KRW 23.3 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 68 billion in Q4 2025 (up 191.1 percent) to KRW 8.72 billion in Q1 2026 (down 87.2 percent), KRW 11.5 billion in Q2 and KRW 73.6 billion in Q3 2026 (up 541.6 percent).

Attributable net results moved from losses of KRW 3.0 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 4.02 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 4.03 billion in Q1 2026 to income of KRW 2.76 billion in Q2 and KRW 3.66 billion in Q3 2026. Over the last four quarters, the attributable net loss totals KRW 1.63 billion.

A separate-statement view of the theater business showed a KRW 6.2 billion operating loss in Q2 2026 (Segye Ilbo report), so part of the consolidated profit reflects subsidiary contributions.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic box office attendance rose 22 percent year on year in Q3 2026, while CJ CGV attendance of 20.05 million rose 28.6 percent, outpacing the market, according to a Dealsite report. Average ticket price rose 4.2 percent to KRW 10,304 in the same quarter.

Daishin Securities attributed the recovery to Hollywood blockbusters and a slate weighted toward new releases. The share of moviegoers using technology premium screens rose from 6.7 percent in Q3 2019 to 18.1 percent in Q3 2026, a sign that viewing patterns are shifting.

KB Securities noted in a July 2026 report that several major competitors had applied for court-led rehabilitation and said CJ CGV could gain share as the market reshapes, while estimating its current share at 35 percent. In China, the company said its box office grew even as the local market contracted.

The global 4DX box office rose 33 percent in Q3 2026 to USD 204 million, and one report projected global 4D screen count to grow at an 11 percent annual rate through 2030. Daishin Securities forecast that the 2026 premium-screen share would reach 15.4 percent of attendance and 22.1 percent of box office.

In April 2026, Samsung Securities said structural weakness persisted because mid-sized hits were scarce, even with a short-term recovery led by a few titles.

06

Outlook

In its Q3 2026 results announcement, the company said it would keep raising profitability through the global spread of technology premium screens such as SCREENX and 4DX and by strengthening its domestic and overseas theater businesses.

The AI-transformation subsidiary plans to expand new AX orders in AI factory, logistics, VFX and media AI and to strengthen its enterprise AX business, according to company announcements.

The Turkish unit expects revenue growth from peak-season releases, along with expanded content sourcing, non-screen revenue and fixed-cost efficiency. Daishin Securities projected 2026 consolidated operating profit at KRW 150 billion in a November 2026 report.

Daishin also expected the 4DX business to post a 2026 annual revenue of KRW 180 billion and operating profit of KRW 21.9 billion, a record, according to an S Journal report.

On financing, Samsung Securities said in an April 2026 report that a sale of the entire stake held by financial investors in CGI Holdings was being pursued, but timing and completion remained uncertain.

The same report said the company decided in April and May 2026 to issue hybrid securities worth KRW 300 billion each to repay existing borrowings ahead of a step-up. Samsung Securities also mentioned in an August 2026 report that CJ 4DPLEX was reportedly seeking outside investment. Operating profit and financing costs should be tracked together as these financing steps proceed.

Bull factors

Shift toward premium-screen earnings

The share of moviegoers using technology premium screens rose from 6.7 percent in Q3 2019 to 18.1 percent in Q3 2026, according to company data. Domestic operating margin reached 10.8 percent in the same quarter, above 9.4 percent in Q3 2019 before the pandemic, per a Dealsite report.

The company says rising attendance eased fixed-cost pressure. Premium-screen demand supporting profitability is the core bull-side argument.

Overseas and subsidiary earnings improve together

China revenue rose 12.3 percent to KRW 80.1 billion in Q3 2026, with operating profit of KRW 6.6 billion, according to company announcements. Turkiye swung to operating profit of KRW 4.6 billion.

The AI-transformation subsidiary posted Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 224.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 16.5 billion as AX orders expanded. Improvement across overseas operations and subsidiaries is presented as a buffer against swings in the theater core.

Possible share gains as the market reshapes

In a July 2026 report, KB Securities said the market could be reshaped as major competitors sought court-led rehabilitation, leaving room for CJ CGV's share to rise over the medium term. The same report estimated its share at 35 percent.

The company's Q3 2026 attendance growth of 28.6 percent exceeded the 22 percent market rise, which is also cited in support.

Bear factors

Financing costs and high leverage

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 533.9%, with total liabilities of KRW 3.47 trillion against total equity of KRW 650.3 billion. The ratio stood at 1,122.7 percent in 2023. Samsung Securities said in an October 2026 report that high financing costs and other financial burdens persist, and it kept a Hold rating. The company decided in April and May 2026 to issue hybrid securities worth KRW 300 billion each.

Attributable losses and quarterly swings

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders ran four consecutive years through 2025 and continued in Q4 2025 and Q1 2026. Operating profit fell to KRW 8.72 billion in Q1 2026 before rising to KRW 73.6 billion in Q3, a wide quarterly swing.

On a separate-statement basis, the theater business posted a KRW 6.2 billion operating loss in Q2 2026, per a Segye Ilbo report. The consolidated profit reflects subsidiary results alongside the theater business.

Shortage of mid-sized hits and structural weakness

In an April 2026 report, Samsung Securities pointed to a short-term recovery led by a few titles while structural weakness continued because mid-sized hits were scarce. When demand concentrates in a few large releases, quarterly results can swing sharply. Market contraction in China during Q3 2026 also remains an overseas variable.

Risk factors

Financing and leverage

A 533.9 percent debt ratio and KRW 3.47 trillion in total liabilities remain fixed burdens that could offset operating gains. Samsung Securities said a full sale of the financial investors' stake in CGI Holdings is being pursued, with completion and timing uncertain. Delays in the sale and debt repayment could extend the financing burden.

Dependence on content hits

Revenue and attendance depend heavily on the release calendar for Hollywood blockbusters and domestic hits. Samsung Securities cited the lack of mid-sized films as a structural weakness in an April 2026 report. Box-office variance flows directly into quarterly results.

Share count growth and capital structure

According to a Segye Ilbo report, total shares outstanding rose 683 percent from 2019 to 2024. Of total equity of KRW 650.3 billion, KRW 570.2 billion is attributable to controlling shareholders, with non-controlling interests making the profit allocation structure more complex. Capital raising and share count changes can affect per-share metric interpretation.

Overall view

CJ CGV posted Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 688.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 73.6 billion, showing a domestic theater turn to profit, wider premium-screen adoption, and better overseas and subsidiary results.

On an annual basis, operating profit rose from a turn to profit in 2023 to KRW 96.2 billion in 2025, with a 4.2 percent operating margin. Attributable net losses, however, ran four consecutive years through 2025, and the last four quarters still total KRW 16.3 billion in losses.

Samsung Securities and KB Securities both cited financing costs in connection with the 533.9 percent debt ratio and KRW 3.47 trillion in total liabilities at the end of 2025.

Positive factors include a higher share of premium-screen attendance, improvement in China and Turkiye, and expanding AX orders at the subsidiary, while negative factors include financial burden, uneven box office and uncertainty over capital restructuring steps.

Target prices cited by brokers were KRW 6,200 from Samsung Securities and KRW 6,000 from KB Securities in October 2026 press reports, and KRW 8,500 from Daishin Securities in a November 9, 2026 report. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results announcement and the 2026 annual report due by late March 2027.
