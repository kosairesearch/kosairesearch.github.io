Dongwon Systems: Export Growth Versus Cost Pressure

02

Business structure

Dongwon Systems is a packaging-focused manufacturer whose business is split into materials, packaging and secondary battery segments.

Company-provided segment shares reported in the press, based on a article covering 2024 results, put flexible packaging, PET and glass bottles at 48.7% of sales, cans at 36.6% and aluminum materials at 6.7%. Within flexible packaging, high value-added items such as pet food and retort pouches have driven growth.

In 2025 the company said its recyclable polyethylene-based 'Uni-Material' packaging lifted demand from global customers. Materials exports reach North America, Central and South America and Southeast Asia, and about 40% of 2024 materials sales came from exports.

The company says it is preparing to enter the Middle East and Europe. The battery business builds on cylindrical battery can manufacturing know-how, and press estimates put battery-related sales below 10% of the total, booked partly in the can segment and partly in aluminum materials.

The company cites 45 years of accumulated manufacturing technology and R&D capability as its base. Aluminum and naphtha prices and domestic consumption feed directly into profitability, a feature of this business structure.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 1.44 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.28 trillion in 2023, an 11.2% decline, then rose to KRW 1.33 trillion in 2024 (up 4.5%) and KRW 1.37 trillion in 2025 (up 2.9%), a second straight year of growth. 2022 was the largest revenue year in the period and 2023 the smallest, while operating profit peaked at KRW 91.9 billion in 2024 and bottomed at KRW 66.2 billion in 2025.

Operating profit fell 28.0% in 2025, which the company and press reports attributed to higher aluminum and other raw material prices, high exchange rates and a weaker downstream market, while revenue growth was supported by flexible packaging exports from the materials segment.

The operating margin moved from 6.4% in 2022, 6.3% in 2023 and 6.9% in 2024 to 4.8% in 2025, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 72.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 53.7 billion in 2025, down 25.9%. Operating cash flow declined from KRW 214.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 138.7 billion in 2025.

By quarter, revenue bottomed at KRW 313.4 billion in Q4 2025, down 12.1% from the prior quarter, while operating profit fell to KRW 7.5 billion, down 62.8%.

Revenue then rose to KRW 337.8 billion in Q1 2026 (up 7.8%), KRW 402.2 billion in Q2 (up 19.1%) and KRW 403.1 billion in Q3 (up 0.2%), three straight quarters of growth. Operating profit peaked at KRW 31.4 billion in Q2 2026, up 140.7% from the prior quarter, before falling to KRW 24 billion in Q3 (down 23.5%).

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders dropped to KRW 5.22 billion in Q3 2026, down 73.7% from the prior quarter and 65.9% from a year earlier, and the last four quarters total KRW 53.2 billion, close to the 2025 annual figure of KRW 53.7 billion.

Revenue and operating profit both rose year on year in Q3 2026, so the sharp drop in profit attributable to controlling shareholders points to items below operating profit that merit close review.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for packaging moves with food and beverage consumption, and Dongwon Systems' flexible packaging and can businesses follow that pattern. In its 2025 results release, the company said higher aluminum and other raw material prices, a strong exchange rate and a weaker downstream market weighed on operating profit.

In Q1 2026 the company again pointed to weak domestic demand and rising naphtha and aluminum prices as burdens.

A Dongwon Industries equity research report dated February 2026 said higher aluminum and other raw material costs, together with weak total demand for alcohol, lowered packaging sales and kept full-year results weak.

Meanwhile, materials exports are growing mainly in the United States, Canada, South America and Southeast Asia, so overseas demand is taking a larger share of sales. In batteries, electric vehicle demand is in a temporary slowdown often called the chasm phase, and a Dongwon Group official said results would take time.

Whether raw material prices stabilize and whether overseas export demand holds will determine where the packaging business sits in its cycle. The three axes of raw materials, exports and batteries are moving at different speeds, which is the core of the industry picture.

Outlook

In its Q1 2026 results release the company said it would keep investing in new businesses such as battery materials and advanced films. In its 2025 results release, it said the battery materials business could begin contributing meaningfully to revenue from 2026 through a more diversified product portfolio.

Expanding exports of eco-friendly packaging was also presented as a way to improve results. Productivity gains and customer base expansion were cited as efficiency priorities.

On exports, the company set out in its 2024 results release a goal of raising the export share to 70% or more over the next ten years, along with preparations to enter the Middle East and Europe. Whether the sharp rise in operating profit to KRW 31.4 billion in Q2 2026 holds is another variable to track.

Revenue rose for three straight quarters through Q3 2026, but profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell sharply in Q3, so how investment costs and raw material burdens flow through to earnings needs to be confirmed. The biggest variable is whether battery materials sales actually grow after 2026.

The 2026 annual report, the next periodic filing, is where the operating margin and the scale of battery sales will be confirmed.

Bull factors

Rising export share in materials

The export share of materials sales rose from about 40% in 2024 to about 45% in the first half of 2025, according to the company. Management has set a target of lifting exports to 70% or more of materials sales over the next decade.

Because 2025 revenue growth came from flexible packaging exports, overseas demand has become the main driver of top-line growth.

Three straight quarters of sales growth in 2026

Revenue reached KRW 337.8 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 402.2 billion in Q2 and KRW 403.1 billion in Q3, three straight quarters of gains. The recovery started from the KRW 313.4 billion low in Q4 2025.

Operating profit rose 140.7% from the prior quarter to KRW 31.4 billion in Q2 2026, showing a rebound in profitability from the low point.

Expected contribution from battery materials

The company expects its battery materials business to contribute meaningfully to revenue from 2026 through a more diversified product portfolio.

The battery business rests on cylindrical battery can manufacturing technology, and the Q1 2026 results release reaffirmed continued investment in battery materials and advanced films. If sales scale up, the business mix could diversify beyond the can-centered structure.

Bear factors

Raw material and currency costs

Higher aluminum prices and a strong exchange rate were cited as factors behind the 2025 operating profit decline. The Q1 2026 results release also pointed to rising naphtha and aluminum prices as a burden. As a result, the 2025 operating margin fell to 4.8% from a 6.3% to 6.9% range over 2022 to 2024.

Weak demand and limits to profit recovery

Press reports say weaker domestic consumption reduced demand for some products, including PET and glass bottles. A securities research report attributed lower packaging sales to weak total demand for alcohol.

Q3 2026 revenue rose 13.1% year on year to KRW 403.1 billion, but profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 65.9% to KRW 5.22 billion, so sales growth did not carry through to profit.

Limited scale of battery sales

Press estimates put battery-related sales below 10% of the total. Commentary has noted that battery sales have not risen much, and a Dongwon Group official said results would take time to arrive after the slowdown in electric vehicle demand. If sales contribution is delayed while investment continues, cost burdens could rise first.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 65.9% year on year in Q3 2026, so the extent to which demand recovery has translated into profit improvement has been limited.

Overall view

Dongwon Systems is showing a sales recovery built on flexible packaging exports across 2025 and 2026. Revenue rose for three straight quarters from Q1 through Q3 2026, and operating profit reached KRW 31.4 billion in Q2 2026.

However, 2025 operating profit fell 28.0% to KRW 66.2 billion, and the operating margin narrowed to 4.8%. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 65.9% year on year to KRW 5.22 billion in Q3 2026, so whether the sales recovery is carrying through to earnings remains to be confirmed.

The debt ratio improved to 77.4% in 2025 from 99.0% in 2022, but operating cash flow declined to KRW 138.7 billion.

Raw material prices, exchange rates and domestic consumption are common variables for profitability, and battery materials are expected to contribute from 2026 but are still estimated at under 10% of sales.

Strengths such as export growth and sales recovery coexist with weaknesses such as cost pressure and profit decline. The next checkpoints are the operating margin and battery sales scale in the 2026 annual report, followed by the Q1 2027 provisional results.
