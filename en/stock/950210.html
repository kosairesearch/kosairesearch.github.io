02

Business structure

Prestige BioPharma is an R&D-focused drug developer specializing in antibody medicines, and its lead product is the trastuzumab biosimilar Tuznue (HD201), a Herceptin biosimilar. Tuznue received European Commission marketing authorization in September 2024 and launched in the European market in October 2025.

In May 2025 the company signed a license and supply agreement with Teva covering 31 European countries, and in October 2025 it received a first purchase order from Teva.

The company said revenue consists of milestone income recognized on Tuznue's approval, drug substance sales, and contract development (CDO) and contract packaging and analytical services.

In the pipeline, the Avastin biosimilar HD204 is in Phase 3 trials in 17 countries, with filings planned with the FDA and EMA during 2026.

The pancreatic cancer antibody PBP1510 is in Phase 1/2a trials in Europe, the United States, Australia and Singapore, and holds orphan drug designation from the EMA, FDA and Korea's MFDS.

Its subsidiary Prestige Biologics handles CDMO operations, and reports describe four plants with about 154,000 liters of total capacity. In the European trastuzumab biosimilar market, Celltrion and Samsung Bioepis were approved earlier, so the company faces a late-entrant competitive position. The company points to cost structure from in-house production and price competitiveness as its differentiators.

04

Earnings analysis

Prestige BioPharma reports in US dollars, and the figures here are KRW-converted.

Revenue rose from KRW 164 million in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to KRW 695 million in the year ended June 2024, KRW 13.7 billion in the year ended June 2025 and KRW 24.2 billion in the year ended June 2026, and it has grown for three consecutive fiscal years since the year ended June 2024.

Operating losses over the same period were KRW 70.7 billion, KRW 63.3 billion, KRW 64.5 billion and KRW 86.7 billion, a four-year run of operating losses.

The operating margin improved from -42,988.4% in the year ended June 2023 to -359.1% in the year ended June 2026, but this mostly reflects the larger revenue base, while the absolute operating loss was largest in the year ended June 2026.

For the year ended June 2025, the company cited milestone income recognized on Tuznue's approval, drug substance sales and higher CDMO service revenue as drivers.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 17.8 billion in the year ended June 2023 to a loss of KRW 33.2 billion in the year ended June 2024, back to a profit of KRW 21.4 billion in the year ended June 2025, and to a loss of KRW 84.8 billion in the year ended June 2026.

A profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the year ended June 2025 despite an operating loss of KRW 64.5 billion points to non-operating items or other factors outside operations, and the line items can be checked in the notes to the periodic report.

Net income including non-controlling interests moved from a profit of KRW 3.99 billion in the year ended June 2025 to a net loss of KRW 113.9 billion in the year ended June 2026. Operating cash flow was negative in all four fiscal years, at KRW -51.7 billion in the year ended June 2026.

By quarter, revenue was KRW 2.24 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 7.3 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, KRW 477 million in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 14.1 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 3.06 billion in the third quarter of 2026, a highly volatile pattern.

The operating loss has run for five consecutive quarters since the third quarter of 2025, narrowing to KRW 15.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Even as second-quarter 2026 revenue jumped to KRW 14.1 billion, the loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 39.3 billion, and the filed figures alone do not establish the cause.

05

Industry analysis

Reports have put the global Herceptin market at roughly KRW 5.5 trillion. An industry source said Herceptin biosimilars in Europe already account for more than half of the total market, which narrows the room for late entrants to gain share.

Celltrion and Samsung Bioepis already sell approved products in Europe, so Prestige BioPharma's share gains as a late entrant are the key question.

The company treats price competitiveness as the core of biosimilar share and says it targets prices below current Herceptin biosimilars, a claim that sales results will need to confirm.

On regulation, major markets including the US FDA have introduced steps to streamline biosimilar clinical requirements, which could reduce development time and cost. In CDMO, reports expect order competition in Japan and Korea after the US Biosecure Act.

Reports have also noted that the subsidiary's orders were heavily weighted toward the parent company, which makes customer diversification a central task.

06

Outlook

At a company briefing in August 2024, the company set targets of KRW 300 billion in annual revenue for 2026 and a market capitalization of KRW 30 trillion by 2030. Revenue for the year ended June 2026 was KRW 24.2 billion, far short of the 2026 revenue target.

The company also laid out a strategy to secure 10 biosimilars and 5 antibody drugs by 2030. Some observers have called the KRW 30 trillion market capitalization goal unrealistic. Filings with the FDA and EMA for HD204 are planned for 2026, and reports say Accord Healthcare will handle its European approval and sales.

Tuznue's European sales run through Teva's distribution network, and the company says joint marketing with Teva will expand its global share.

The subsidiary Prestige Biologics reported cumulative orders of KRW 65 billion as of the end of March 2026 and a follow-on clinical supply order from a customer ahead of commercial production. That clinical supply contract runs through the end of March 2027.

The company says PBP1510 showed positive safety and efficacy signals in Phase 1 and plans further data presentations and technology transfer talks. The next checkpoints are the HD204 filing, the conversion of Tuznue's European launch into sales, and whether clinical supply work leads to commercial production.

Bull factors

Tuznue enters commercial sales in Europe

Tuznue received EU marketing authorization in September 2024 and launched in Europe in October 2025. The Teva license covers 31 European countries, and the first order was disclosed at EUR 740,000.

With industry analysis putting European Herceptin biosimilars above half of the market, the partner's distribution capability will determine how fast sales expand.

Revenue scale-up and an improving loss ratio

Revenue grew from KRW 164 million in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 24.2 billion in the year ended June 2026, rising for three consecutive fiscal years from the year ended June 2024. The operating margin improved from -42,988.4% to -359.1% over the same span.

The third-quarter 2026 loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed to KRW 6.79 billion from KRW 39.3 billion in the second quarter.

Pipeline expansion and CDMO orders

HD204 is in Phase 3 trials across 17 countries, with FDA and EMA filings planned for 2026. The pancreatic cancer antibody PBP1510 is in Phase 1/2a trials and holds orphan designations in Europe, the US and Korea.

The subsidiary Prestige Biologics' cumulative orders are a variable that shapes how much contract development and manufacturing revenue is recognized.

Operating losses were KRW 70.7 billion, KRW 63.3 billion, KRW 64.5 billion and KRW 86.7 billion across the four fiscal years to June 2026, a four-year run of losses. Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters total KRW 79.6 billion. Revenue growth has not offset the rise in costs.

Total liabilities at the end of June 2026 were KRW 203.2 billion, with a debt ratio of 46.2%. Continued operating outflows could raise the need for additional funding.

A gap between targets and results, and late-entrant competition

Celltrion and Samsung Bioepis already hold European approvals, which makes share gains difficult for a late entrant.

Risk factors

Funding risk

Operating cash outflows have persisted for four fiscal years. Equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 376.4 billion against total equity of KRW 439.5 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 203.2 billion at the end of June 2026.

If losses continue, the company may need equity or debt financing, which could affect the value attributable to existing shareholders.

Revenue volatility and concentration risk

Quarterly revenue swung sharply, from KRW 477 million in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 14.1 billion in the second quarter and KRW 3.06 billion in the third. The company cites milestone income and drug substance sales among its revenue sources, so quarterly results can depend on the timing of events. Dependence on partners such as Teva is also a point to monitor.

Regulatory and clinical timing risk

The FDA and EMA filings for HD204 are planned for 2026, and review timing after filing rests with regulators. PBP1510 is in Phase 1/2a trials and needs time before commercialization. A change in trial results or approval timing could require the company to revise its pipeline value and revenue plans.

Overall view

Revenue rose from KRW 164 million in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 24.2 billion in the year ended June 2026, and the company attributes this to Tuznue's European launch and the Teva agreement.

Over the same period, however, the operating loss widened to KRW 86.7 billion, and operating cash flow was negative in all four fiscal years.

The third-quarter 2026 loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed to KRW 6.79 billion from KRW 39.3 billion in the second quarter, yet the four-quarter net loss total is KRW 79.6 billion.

Strengths include the revenue growth trend, the European approval and partner agreements, and the subsidiary's CDMO orders. Weaknesses include the persistent losses, cash outflows, late-entrant competition, and the gap between long-term targets and results.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio is 46.2%, equity attributable to controlling shareholders is KRW 376.4 billion, and the price-to-book ratio sits below 1x. The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 results, the HD204 filing, and whether clinical supply converts into commercial production.
