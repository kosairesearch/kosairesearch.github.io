Report overview

SK Telecom: Telecom Recovery Alongside AI Infrastructure Build-Out

Profit fell sharply in 2025 after the USIM hacking incident, but quarterly operating profit has since returned to the KRW 500 billion range while SK Telecom expands its AI data center investment.

  1. 1

    Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 4.5 trillion and operating profit was KRW 552.3 billion, with operating profit up 1,040.2% year on year.

  2. 2

    Operating profit fell 41.1% to KRW 1.07 trillion in 2025, and the company posted an attributable net loss of KRW 158.2 billion in Q3 2025.

  3. 3

    AI data center revenue was KRW 126.1 billion in Q3 2026 according to press reports, up 6.2% year on year but down 7.4% from the prior quarter.

  4. 4

    AI infrastructure is run through SK Hyper (launched July 2026) and SK Horizon (launched August 2026), with external financing planned alongside.

  5. 5

    The company said it will keep dividends at least at the current level and will announce its final shareholder return policy by Q1 2027.

02

Business structure

SK Telecom is a telecom operator that combines wireless and wireline services with AI data centers (AIDC) and AI services. Its wireless business has shifted marketing toward customer lifetime value (LTV), and revenue has been recovering gradually.

The company attributed the Q3 2026 wireless performance to premium device launches and an improved subscriber mix. Management said it will focus on acquiring high-value subscribers rather than spending heavily on marketing to grow subscriber counts.

The number of mobile phone subscribers declined slightly even as revenue rose. Wireline revenue grew 7.4% year on year in Q3 2026, driven by rising broadband subscribers.

The AI data center business has been organized so that SK Hyper handles business development and SK Horizon runs infrastructure operations and expansion. A new data center of about 180MW is under construction in Ulsan and Seoul. On the AI services side, the company applies its proprietary AI foundation model to A.

(A-dot) and enterprise AI services, and press reports said business-to-business and business-to-consumer AI transaction revenue rose 27.3% year on year in Q3 2026. The company is preparing a token gateway business that draws on its base of about 23 million subscribers and its payment systems.

Competition in the domestic telecom market continues, so the balance between wireless subscriber competition and marketing spending is a central variable for the whole business.

04

Earnings analysis

Among the annual results, 2024 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025 and 2025 was the smallest. Revenue rose from KRW 17.3 trillion in 2022 to KRW 17.6 trillion in 2023 (up 1.8% year on year) and KRW 17.9 trillion in 2024 (up 1.9%), then fell to KRW 17.1 trillion in 2025 (down 4.7%).

Operating profit rose from KRW 1.61 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.75 trillion in 2023 (up 8.8%) and KRW 1.82 trillion in 2024 (up 4.0%), then fell 41.1% to KRW 1.07 trillion in 2025. The operating margin declined from 10.2% in 2024 to 6.3% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 67.3% to KRW 408.4 billion in 2025, while net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 375.1 billion that year.

Press reports and company materials attribute the 2025 decline to lower profitability after the USIM hacking incident and the resulting personal data leak.

The debt-to-equity ratio was 132.4% at the end of 2025, down from 158.0% in 2024, while operating cash flow fell to KRW 3.92 trillion in 2025 from KRW 5.09 trillion in 2024. On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 was the low point, with operating profit of KRW 48.4 billion and an attributable net loss of KRW 158.2 billion.

Operating profit then rose to KRW 119.1 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 537.6 billion in Q1 2026 (up 351.3% quarter on quarter), KRW 566 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 552.3 billion in Q3 2026. Attributable net income in Q3 2026 was KRW 413.3 billion, a swing from a loss in the year-earlier quarter. Attributable net income over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 1.32 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic mobile market is mature, and customer competition among the major operators continues. Within this environment, SK Telecom shifted to a customer-value-focused strategy, and revenue has recovered since its wireless marketing changed direction.

The company said it ranked first in all three major domestic customer satisfaction surveys in 2026. Rival KT is pursuing enterprise AI and a token factory business, and LG Uplus is upgrading its K-EXAONE model.

In AI data center demand, the company argues that rising inference workloads and AI adoption in the public and industrial sectors are the drivers. However, AI data center revenue rose 92.5% year on year in Q2 2026 but only 6.2% in Q3, a clear slowdown.

Press reports attributed the slowdown to a gap in GPU-as-a-service infrastructure revenue. In the domestic AI data center race, SK Telecom is pairing with SK hynix, SK Ecoplant and SK Innovation across the value chain to improve cost efficiency.

06

Outlook

The company expects its earnings recovery to continue into Q4 2026 and said that, if current trends hold, it plans to keep quarterly operating profit at KRW 500 billion or more. Q4 2026 results have not yet been filed in a periodic report.

SK Hyper targets 15 gigawatts (GW) of capacity and plans to start from a 5 GW stage, depending on customer demand. The initial total commitment is KRW 750 billion, of which KRW 330 billion is slated for investment in 2026 and the rest in installments from 2027.

SK Hyper is in talks with three to four global operators and aims to secure initial customer demand by the first half of 2027.

At MWC26, the company outlined plans for more than 1 GW of AI data centers across Korea to become Asia's largest AI data center hub, including a southwestern-region facility developed with OpenAI.

Its proprietary foundation model, currently 519 billion parameters, is to be upgraded to more than 1 trillion parameters, with a multimodal version handling voice and video starting in the second half of 2026. On the telecom side, press reports pointed to a 5G standalone (SA) commercial launch in December 2026.

Whether AI data center revenue grows will depend on when new facilities begin operating and on firm customer contracts.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in quarterly earnings power

Quarterly operating profit held in the KRW 500 billion range for three straight quarters, at KRW 537.6 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 566 billion in Q2 and KRW 552.3 billion in Q3. That compares with KRW 48.4 billion in Q3 2025, a wide margin of recovery.

Wireless revenue recovered gradually after the shift to LTV-focused marketing, and wireline revenue grew 7.4% year on year in Q3 2026. The company said it plans to keep quarterly operating profit at KRW 500 billion or more in Q4 2026.

Room for AI data center expansion

SK Hyper targets 15 GW of capacity and plans to start from a 5 GW stage depending on customer demand. The target for securing initial customer demand is the first half of 2027. AI data center revenue rose 92.5% year on year in Q2 2026.

The company is also preparing a token gateway business that links its subscriber base of about 23 million and its payment systems to AI platforms.

Stated commitment to shareholder returns

The company said it will keep dividends at least at the current level even as it continues AI data center investment. Quarterly dividends stayed at the same level from Q1 to Q3 2026. It said it will announce the final shareholder return policy by Q1 2027. Attributable net income over the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.32 trillion.

09

Bear factors

Slowing AI data center growth

According to press reports, AI data center revenue in Q3 2026 was KRW 126.1 billion, up only 6.2% year on year and down 7.4% from Q2. Growth has slowed sharply from the 92.5% year-on-year increase in Q2. Press reports attributed the slowdown to a gap in GPU-as-a-service infrastructure revenue. Whether profitability can match the scale of new investment has been flagged as a challenge.

Large investment and reliance on external funding

The company said it will combine strategic partner investment and project financing for its AI data center expansion. SK Hyper's initial commitment totals KRW 750 billion, with KRW 330 billion invested in 2026 and the rest paid in installments from 2027.

The CEO said the overall investment would be in the trillions of won or more. If initial customer demand is not secured by the first half of 2027, the timing of investment recovery could slip.

Subscriber decline and marketing constraints

Mobile phone subscribers declined slightly in Q3 2026 even as revenue rose. Because the company is cutting excess marketing spend and focusing on high-value customers, the pace of subscriber base growth may be limited. Competition in the domestic telecom market continues. Restoring customer trust after the 2025 hacking incident also remains a task.

10

Risk factors

AI infrastructure execution risk

The company is pursuing large external funding while the new AI infrastructure business still carries execution risk. Customer talks are ongoing, but utilization and revenue structure remain uncertain until contracts are firm. Securing land, power and water is cited as a factor that determines project speed. Subsea cable expansion is also planned in stages, which could add to the investment burden.

Subscriber and competition risk

Competition in the domestic telecom market continues, and reducing marketing spend could slow the pace of net subscriber additions. The number of mobile phone subscribers declined slightly in Q3 2026. Changes in marketing costs tied to premium device launches could also affect profitability. Rivals including KT are expanding their own AI businesses at the same time.

Financial and shareholder return risk

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 17.2 trillion, exceeding total equity of KRW 13 trillion. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 3.92 trillion in 2025 from the prior year. Allocating financial capacity between maintaining dividends and expanding AI investment is a challenge that runs in parallel. Until the final shareholder return policy is announced, uncertainty about the dividend direction remains.

12

Overall view

SK Telecom's annual operating profit fell to KRW 1.07 trillion in 2025 after the hacking incident, but quarterly operating profit held in the KRW 500 billion range from Q1 through Q3 2026, restoring earnings power.

Wireless revenue recovered gradually after the shift to LTV-focused marketing, and wireline revenue rose 7.4% year on year in Q3 2026. By contrast, AI data center revenue rose 92.5% year on year in Q2 2026 but growth slowed to 6.2% in Q3.

Because large investment is running alongside external financing, the profitability and financial burden of the AI infrastructure business are both points to watch. The company has stated it will keep dividends at least at the current level, and it will announce its final shareholder return policy by Q1 2027.

The next checkpoints are whether quarterly operating profit stays at KRW 500 billion or more in Q4 2026, the initial customer wins at SK Hyper, and the full-year figures in the 2026 annual business report.

Strengths in the recovery of the core telecom business and weaknesses in the uncertainty of the AI business currently coexist.
