02

Business structure

A Plus Asset Advisor is an independent insurance agency (GA) founded in 2007 that listed on the KOSPI in 2020. It sells products from 34 insurers, and the company describes itself as the only GA listed on the main board of the Korea Exchange.

As of the end of the third quarter of 2025, consolidated revenue mix was 46.88% non-life insurance and 41.23% life insurance. The core asset is its agent network, which grew from 4,342 at end-2023 to 5,227 at end-2024, then to 7,836 at end-March 2026 (up 26.1% year on year) and 8,897 at end-June 2026 (up 28.8%).

The company said 41% of new agents hired in 2024 had no prior insurance experience. Growth in 2024 was mainly driven by sales of non-life and low-surrender-value protection products. Non-financial subsidiaries span funeral services, real estate and healthcare, and the listed subsidiary is Nano Entech.

Some market observers say the non-financial subsidiaries contribute only limited profit relative to the core insurance sales business. The company frames this as a total life-care platform strategy covering finance, healthcare and silver care.

Within the competitive landscape, agent retention and contract persistence are the key measures of the quality of a GA's earnings.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 270.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 354.6 billion in 2023 (up 31.0%), KRW 515.8 billion in 2024 (up 45.5%) and KRW 682.5 billion in 2025 (up 32.3%), growing for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. Within 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year for revenue.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.36 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.65 billion in 2023 (up 464.2%) and KRW 31.6 billion in 2024 (up 312.5%), then fell to KRW 29.3 billion in 2025 (down 7.1%). The operating margin was 0.5% in 2022, 2.2% in 2023, 6.1% in 2024 and 4.3% in 2025, peaking in 2024.

In 2025 revenue grew 32.3% while operating profit declined, so the margin fell. Net income attributable to owners of the parent went from KRW 50.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.2 billion in 2023 (down 79.7%) and KRW 2.48 billion in 2024 (down 75.7%), then recovered to KRW 22.1 billion in 2025 (up 792.0%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 65.5 billion, KRW 11.7 billion, KRW 10.1 billion and KRW 24.7 billion for 2022 through 2025, a different series because the gap reflects non-controlling interests.

Press reports attributed the low 2024 consolidated net income to an accounting disposal loss arising from consolidating subsidiary Nano Entech. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 261.2 billion, of which KRW 169.7 billion belonged to owners of the parent, and total liabilities were KRW 254.1 billion.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 85.5% in 2022 to 96.7% in 2023, then stood at 96.6% in 2024 and 97.3% in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 18.2 billion in 2022, negative KRW 3.2 billion in 2023, KRW 32.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 31.8 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 176 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 180.7 billion in the fourth quarter (up 2.7% quarter on quarter), KRW 186.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 3.2%) and KRW 199 billion in the second quarter (up 6.6%), then eased to KRW 194.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026 (down 2.0%).

The company said first-quarter 2026 revenue was the highest quarterly figure since its founding. Quarterly operating profit was KRW 15.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 133.9% quarter on quarter), KRW 10.1 billion in the second quarter (down 36.4%) and KRW 15 billion in the third quarter (up 48.6%).

Net income attributable to owners of the parent totaled KRW 34.9 billion over the last four quarters, with the first quarter of 2026 the largest at KRW 12.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Since insurers adopted IFRS 17, they have pushed for contractual service margin (CSM) through protection products, which has raised the importance of GA channels for protection insurance sales.

The company attributed its 2024 growth mainly to sales of non-life and low-surrender-value protection products and said competition among insurers continued into the second year of IFRS 17. Management expected a difficult insurance environment in 2025 and planned to respond by expanding its agent network.

Under that competition, agent retention, productivity and contract persistence serve as the key measures of GA earnings.

On the regulatory side, a four-year deferred payment system for insurance sales commissions takes effect in January 2027, which raises the importance of sales management and financial soundness at GA firms.

The company says it is focusing on strengthening its internal control systems and soundness management in preparation. Its status as the only GA listed on the main board is presented as a differentiator, though it also brings disclosure obligations.

Where the industry sits in its cycle depends on insurers' product design and commission policies, so competitive intensity and the earnings structure after the commission rule change are the factors to track.

06

Outlook

The company plans to keep expanding its agent network and to train inexperienced recruits effectively to strengthen its sales competitiveness. It attributed first-quarter 2026 results to the growth of the agent network, which it said drove better sales of protection insurance and higher revenue.

Maintaining contract persistence and upgrading its management of mis-selling ratios are also tasks the company has set out.

On shareholder returns, it completed a KRW 7 billion buyback in July 2026 and, at an October 2026 board meeting, decided on an additional KRW 3.5 billion buyback, bringing the 2026 total to KRW 10.5 billion. Repurchased shares are to be fully canceled as a matter of principle.

On governance, the company plans to acquire the minority stake in its real estate subsidiary A Plus Realty, in which it holds 95.3%, through a cash-settled comprehensive share exchange and make it a wholly owned subsidiary.

The company describes its direction as a total life-care platform covering finance, healthcare and silver care. Some observers say it still needs to verify whether non-financial subsidiaries contribute synergies with the core insurance business.

When the four-year commission deferral takes effect in January 2027, sales management and financial soundness capabilities will become a point to check in future results.

Bull factors

Earnings recovery

Cumulative operating profit for the first nine months of 2026 was KRW 41.1 billion according to the company, above full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 29.3 billion.

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 15.9 billion, KRW 10.1 billion and KRW 15 billion across the first three quarters of 2026, well above KRW 6.17 billion in the third quarter of 2025.

Net income attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters, at KRW 34.9 billion, exceeds the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 22.1 billion.

Agent network growth

The agent count reached 8,897 at the end of June 2026, up 28.8% year on year. The company says expansion of the organization and management of retention and productivity supported results. The company said first-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 186.6 billion was the highest quarterly figure since its founding.

Shareholder returns and governance

Share buyback decisions for 2026 total KRW 10.5 billion, and repurchased shares are to be fully canceled as a matter of principle. A plan to acquire the minority stake in a 95.3%-owned subsidiary through a share exchange and make it wholly owned is also in progress.

The company says simplifying the governance structure should improve decision speed and resource allocation within the group.

Bear factors

Volatile annual earnings

Net income attributable to owners of the parent fell to KRW 10.2 billion in 2023 (down 79.7%) and KRW 2.48 billion in 2024 (down 75.7%). Operating profit declined 7.1% to KRW 29.3 billion in 2025. The operating margin also dropped from 6.1% in 2024 to 4.3% in 2025.

Unproven contribution from non-financial subsidiaries

Some market observers say non-financial subsidiaries contribute only limited profit relative to the core insurance sales business. Others argue that capital efficiency and business synergies still need verification.

In 2024, weak subsidiary results and accounting adjustments left consolidated net income below the separate-basis figure.

Balance sheet and quarterly swings

The debt-to-equity ratio was 97.3% at the end of 2025, higher than 85.5% in 2022. Third-quarter 2026 revenue fell 2.0% from the previous quarter. Quarterly operating profit also swung, dropping 36.4% in the second quarter of 2026 before recovering in the third.

10

Risk factors

Regulatory risk (sales commissions)

A four-year deferred payment system for insurance sales commissions is scheduled to take effect in January 2027. The change could raise the burden of sales management and financial soundness at GA firms. The company says it is responding by upgrading its internal control systems.

Sales quality risk

As the agent network expands quickly, managing retention and contract persistence determines the quality of earnings. The company says it is upgrading its controls on mis-selling ratios. If headcount growth outpaces management capacity, consumer protection and reputational burdens could increase.

Governance and subsidiary risk

Insiders and related parties held 27.83% as of the December 23, 2025 change date, a concentrated ownership structure. If the minority-stake share exchange at a subsidiary proceeds, the shareholder base will change. Earnings swings at non-financial subsidiaries feed directly into consolidated profit.

Overall view

A Plus Asset Advisor's operating profit fell in 2025, but cumulative operating profit for the first nine months of 2026, at KRW 41.1 billion according to the company, has passed the full-year 2025 level, showing a recovery. Expansion of the agent network and management of sales efficiency metrics underpin the results.

Shareholder returns include the KRW 10.5 billion buyback decisions for 2026, a policy of fully canceling repurchased shares, and a plan to make a subsidiary wholly owned.

On the other hand, sharp declines in owners' net income in 2023 and 2024 and the drop in 2025 operating profit show how volatile annual earnings have been. The debt-to-equity ratio was 97.3% at the end of 2025, above the 2022 level, and third-quarter 2026 revenue fell from the prior quarter.
