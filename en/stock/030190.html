02

Business structure

NICE Information Service is a leading credit information company in Korea. It reports four segments: personal credit information, corporate information, asset management, and other businesses such as big data, advertising agency and bond valuation.

In 2025, before intersegment eliminations, the personal credit information segment generated KRW 375.9 billion, or 61.49% of the total. Corporate information contributed about KRW 99.7 billion (16.31%), asset management about KRW 75.2 billion (12.30%), and other businesses about KRW 60.5 billion (9.90%).

In the first quarter of 2026 the personal credit segment remained the largest at 62.82%, or KRW 99.3 billion. Its main consumer product is NICE Jikimi.

The corporate segment runs the NICE BizLINE online information service, corporate technology evaluation and a credit risk management system build-out for financial institutions. The asset management segment covers debt collection, credit investigation and lending-related businesses through subsidiaries.

The other segment includes advertising agency subsidiary Ainsmedia, consolidated from the third quarter of 2024. The main customers are financial institutions with strong demand for credit screening and risk management.

A Daishin Securities company report from September 2023 put the company's share of the personal credit bureau market at 64.2%. Unlike platform companies in the same IT and software sector, its revenue centers on data and evaluation services.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 479.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 485.7 billion in 2023, a 1.2% increase, then to KRW 535 billion in 2024, up 10.2%, and to KRW 602.1 billion in 2025, up 12.5%. Revenue has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 71.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 65.3 billion in 2023, a decline of 8.8%, then rose to KRW 86.8 billion in 2024, up 32.8%, and to KRW 104.4 billion in 2025, up 20.3%. It has increased for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

The operating margin went from 14.9% in 2022 to 13.5% in 2023, then to 16.2% in 2024 and 17.3% in 2025, the highest in the period. In 2023 profit fell while revenue grew only 1.2%, which points to heavy cost pressure that year.

The 2024 recovery came with 10.2% revenue growth, and the advertising subsidiary was consolidated from the third quarter of that year, adding to the top line.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 52.7 billion in 2022, KRW 56 billion in 2023 (up 6.4%), KRW 76.1 billion in 2024 (up 35.8%) and KRW 77.2 billion in 2025 (up 1.4%). In 2025 operating profit grew 20.3% while this figure grew only 1.4%.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 78.1 billion in 2025, a different figure. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 71.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 120.9 billion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit reached KRW 29.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 32.9 billion in the second quarter, then KRW 28.3 billion in the third quarter, down 14.0% from the prior quarter.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 13.5 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, then recorded KRW 25.2 billion, KRW 25.4 billion and KRW 18.7 billion in the first three quarters of 2026. The last four quarters total KRW 82.7 billion.

The reasons for the gap between operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income should be traced in the income statement items of the quarterly reports.

05

Industry analysis

Within the IT and software sector, the company belongs to financial data businesses that sell data and provide evaluation services, and its revenue model differs from platform companies. The first driver of demand is risk management spending by financial institutions.

A company summary compiled by WiseReport says demand for data-based evaluation is growing with financial risk management needs and rising non-face-to-face lending. The second driver is the MyData market. The same summary says market expansion and the entry of new providers are widening service opportunities.

In June 2026 the company announced its final selection for a MyData service support program, and in July it launched Carfin, a service based on vehicle maintenance records.

The 64.2% personal credit bureau share comes from a September 2023 Daishin Securities report, so it is a reference point for leadership rather than a current share. Data business expansion continues.

On October 2, 2026 the company launched an API that blocks malicious text messages, combining KISA malicious URL data with its own risk-scoring engine. In September 2026 it agreed to pursue a credit information business for technology firms with Korea Technology Finance Corporation (KOTEC).

Competition may intensify as platform companies such as NAVER and Kakao expand into financial services and compete for financial data. The cycle position depends on loan demand and financial institutions' investment in risk management.

06

Outlook

The corporate value-up plan disclosed on April 29, 2026 sets the medium-term direction. It targets 2027 revenue of more than KRW 690 billion and operating profit of more than KRW 110 billion. The 2025 baseline is KRW 602.1 billion in revenue and KRW 104.4 billion in operating profit.

For shareholder returns, the plan says DPS will rise by at least 5% a year from the prior year over 2026 to 2028, the consolidated payout ratio will stay at 35% or higher, and 1% of shares will be bought back and cancelled each year.

The governance key indicator compliance rate is targeted to rise from 60% in 2026 to 73% by the end of 2027. On the business side, new services came in the second half of 2026.

In July 2026 the company strengthened an AI and AX joint business with LG CNS aimed at global financial markets, and on October 14, 2026 it launched an AI-based recommendation service for government support programs.

Vietnamese subsidiary NICE CI received a private credit information license from the State Bank of Vietnam in May 2026, ten months after applying in July 2025. The next point to check is how much these new businesses contribute to the other segment's results and operating margin.

The 2026 annual report will show whether the dividend is confirmed and how the annual operating margin tracks the shareholder return plan.

08

Bull factors

Steady top-line growth on a personal credit base

Revenue has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and quarterly revenue stayed above KRW 150 billion from the first quarter to the third quarter of 2026. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 160.4 billion rose 6.2% year on year.

With 61.49% of 2025 revenue from personal credit information, the earnings base is concentrated, which can also be read as stability.

Operating margin recovery and rising operating profit

The operating margin went from 14.9% in 2022 and 13.5% in 2023 to 16.2% in 2024 and 17.3% in 2025, the highest of the period. Operating profit reached KRW 104.4 billion in 2025, up 20.3%.

Second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 32.9 billion rose 11.8% from the prior quarter, and third-quarter operating profit rose 4.7% year on year.

A more concrete shareholder return plan

The plan disclosed in April 2026 covers 2026 to 2028, with DPS rising by at least 5% a year, a consolidated payout ratio of at least 35%, and an annual buyback and cancellation of 1% of shares. A share cancellation decision was disclosed in July 2026. Because the targets are quantified, the company's progress can be checked year by year.

09

Bear factors

Slowing net income attributable to controlling shareholders

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 77.2 billion in 2025, up only 1.4%, far below the 20.3% growth in operating profit. The fourth-quarter 2025 figure of KRW 13.5 billion fell 39.8% from the prior quarter.

The third-quarter 2026 figure of KRW 18.7 billion fell 16.9% year on year, and the last four quarters total KRW 82.7 billion.

High personal credit exposure and regulatory sensitivity

The personal credit information segment accounted for 61.49% of 2025 revenue and 62.82% in the first quarter of 2026, above 60%. Personal credit inquiries and data provision are directly affected by changes in regulation. Operating profit fell 8.8% in 2023, which shows that margins can move with external conditions.

Challenges to reaching the growth targets

The value-up plan targets 2027 revenue of more than KRW 690 billion and operating profit of more than KRW 110 billion. That requires a substantial expansion from the 2025 base of KRW 602.1 billion in revenue and KRW 104.4 billion in operating profit.

The timing of revenue from new businesses such as the malicious text API, the Vietnam license and the foreign resident app remains uncertain.

10

Risk factors

Regulation and data policy

Changes to credit information laws or the MyData framework could alter the terms for personal credit inquiries and data sales. Because the personal credit segment makes up more than 60% of revenue, the impact of such changes would be direct.

Regulatory changes in debt collection and lending-related businesses in the asset management segment could also shift the operating environment.

Economic cycle and loan demand

Rising non-face-to-face lending has boosted evaluation demand, but a drop in loan demand or a pullback in financial institutions' spending could slow growth in corporate information and evaluation.

In a LS Securities report from August 2024, the corporate information segment fell 6.6% year on year to KRW 26.5 billion in the second quarter of 2024. This segment can be sensitive to the economic cycle.

Execution of new businesses

The number of new businesses is growing, including the malicious text API, the foreign resident app, the Vietnam license and a technology-firm credit business. The timing and size of their contribution to revenue and operating profit remain uncertain. If upfront costs come before revenue, the operating margin could come under pressure.

12

Overall view

NICE Information Service is a credit information company centered on personal credit data. Revenue has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and the operating margin climbed from 13.5% in 2023 to 17.3% in 2025. The disclosed annual results show that scale and profitability have grown together.

However, operating profit rose 20.3% in 2025 while net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose only 1.4%, and in the third quarter of 2026 operating profit rose 4.7% year on year while controlling-shareholder net income fell 16.9%.

With personal credit information above 60% of revenue, the earnings base is stable but exposed to regulation and the economic cycle. New businesses, the Vietnam license and data partnerships are presented as growth options, but the timing of their revenue is still uncertain.

The April 2026 plan specifies DPS increases, a payout ratio of at least 35% and share cancellation. Current multiples sit within the past year-end ranges. The next checkpoints are the annual results and dividend decision in the 2026 annual report and the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report.
