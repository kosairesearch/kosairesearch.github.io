Report overview

Revenue Up Three Years Running, Profit Flat

Innotena's revenue rose for three straight years to KRW 30 billion in 2025, but operating profit of KRW 1.97 billion has not kept pace, so the gap between top-line growth and earnings is the item to track in the next periodic filings.

02

Business structure

Innotena changed its corporate name from MOCOMSYS at a shareholders' meeting on March 27, 2026. The stated reasons were to improve the company's image and diversify its business.

The company treats its integration and security solutions as its two main pillars and is widening its scope to application performance monitoring (APM) and cloud solutions. Revenue in 2025 was KRW 30 billion and operating profit was KRW 1.97 billion.

Rather than building its own large language model, the company concentrates on core areas such as document access control and encryption, and connects external sLLMs through the MCP standard protocol in a modular structure.

Its sales model is also shifting from on-premises deployments toward cloud subscriptions, which the company says lowers customers' upfront build-out costs.

In the public sector, reports say it has won continuing orders for a large public agency's next-generation information system and then its integrated maintenance contract.

Partnerships include an MOU with a sovereign AI operator on an enterprise intelligent knowledge security platform, and joint development with an AI automation firm on a zero-trust RAG modeling and intelligent document centralization platform.

At end-2025, total liabilities were KRW 8.7 billion against total equity of KRW 23 billion, and the company has no borrowings. Listed IT and software peers often compared with it include Ahnlab, Samsung SDS and Daou Technology.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 23.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 24.6 billion in 2023 (up 6.0% year on year), KRW 28 billion in 2024 (up 13.7%) and KRW 30 billion in 2025 (up 7.1%). Revenue has risen for three straight years since 2023, with 2025 the largest year and 2022 the smallest in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit was KRW 1.55 billion in 2022, KRW 706 million in 2023 (down 54.4%), KRW 2.03 billion in 2024 (up 187.7%) and KRW 1.97 billion in 2025 (down 3.1%), with 2024 the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

The operating margin was 6.7% in 2022, 2.9% in 2023, 7.3% in 2024 and 6.6% in 2025, so margins varied sharply from year to year even as revenue grew.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a net loss of KRW 1.72 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 1.08 billion in 2023, a turn to profitability, before reaching KRW 2.36 billion in 2024 (up 118.2%) and KRW 2.15 billion in 2025 (down 9.1%).

Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed separately at the same values, so it should be read apart from the attributable figure. Quarterly revenue rose 13.6% to KRW 8.1 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, yet operating profit fell 70.7% to KRW 132 million.

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 7.04 billion (down 13.0% quarter on quarter) and operating profit was KRW 499 million (up 277.8% quarter on quarter).

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue of KRW 7.96 billion rose 11.7% year on year, but operating profit of KRW 425 million fell 5.9% and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 276 million fell 45.3%.

The company said its first-quarter results reflected expanded upfront investment in new cloud products and an intelligent document centralization platform, and the CEO said a turnaround would begin in the second half. Disclosed third-quarter operating profit, however, was below the prior-year level.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 to third quarter of 2026) totals KRW 1.67 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Reports on the sovereign AI trend describe a growing role for data infrastructure partners serving enterprises and the public sector. The company says it is accelerating its push into the public AX market and widening its presence across the full cycle of public IT infrastructure projects.

In a period of rising server and storage hardware prices, the company presents subscription models as an alternative that reduces customers' upfront investment burden.

Management argues that the key to AI adoption lies in controlling data access rather than the model itself, and the company is targeting governance of AI access to internal data in closed networks.

In competition, large listed IT and security companies such as Ahnlab, Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever contest the same public and enterprise markets. On revenue scale, Innotena is among the smaller listed peers, so public contract wins and new product sales are the key to growing its top line.

The position of the industry cycle is difficult to pin down. The observation point is how far public AX orders and AI access-control demand show up in revenue.

06

Outlook

The company has set a medium-term target of KRW 100 billion in revenue and KRW 15 billion in operating profit within five years. The CEO presents AX and cloud transformation together with M&A as the two main growth drivers. Compared with KRW 30 billion in 2025 revenue, the target implies more than a threefold increase.

Dealsite noted that annualizing first-half revenue simply would give roughly KRW 27 billion. The CEO said funds raised through zero-interest convertible bonds would be used to accelerate AX contract wins and pursue strategic M&A.

On products, the company is pushing to commercialize a governance layer that controls and logs AI access to internal data in closed networks, along with an MCP gateway. On shareholder returns, quarterly dividends are being made routine alongside a KRW 1 billion share buyback trust.

Media outlets said achieving the target would be a key yardstick for whether the restructuring is producing real results. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit, however, was below the prior-year level, so the gap between the second-half turnaround statement and the disclosed figures is the next point to check.

08

Bull factors

Debt-Free Balance Sheet and Equity Base

Total equity stood at KRW 23 billion and total liabilities at KRW 8.7 billion at end-2025, with no borrowings. The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 37.7% in 2025 from 43.1% in 2024. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 169 million in 2022 to KRW 4.01 billion in 2024, and was KRW 2.15 billion in 2025. The company says this base can fund its AX transformation investment and M&A.

Quarterly Dividends and Share Buybacks

Innotena paid quarterly dividends for three consecutive quarters from the first quarter to the third quarter of 2026. The payout ratio based on 2025 earnings was 25%, which led to its selection as a high-dividend company.

In September it signed a KRW 1 billion treasury share acquisition trust and has been buying shares on the open market. The company describes this as a sign of its commitment to making shareholder returns routine.

Expanding AX and Sovereign AI Partnerships

Innotena signed an MOU with a sovereign AI operator on an enterprise intelligent knowledge security platform and is jointly developing RAG modeling and a document centralization platform with an AI automation firm.

The company is pursuing commercialization of a governance layer that controls and logs AI access to internal data in closed networks, as well as an MCP gateway. Its push into the public AX market and its integrated maintenance contract wins are presented as factors that broaden the revenue base. On both products and partners, the business portfolio is widening.

Bear factors

Revenue Growth Not Matched by Profit

Revenue rose 7.1% in 2025, but operating profit fell 3.1%. In the third quarter of 2026, revenue rose 11.7% year on year, while operating profit fell 5.9% and net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 45.3%. The pattern of revenue growth without matching profit growth has continued into recent quarters.

Volatility in Quarterly Profit

The operating margin swung widely from year to year, from 2.9% in 2023 to 7.3% in 2024. Operating profit fell 70.7% quarter on quarter to KRW 132 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, and the recovery seen in the first quarter of 2026 reversed in the third quarter.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 1.67 billion, a small base where a single change in costs can move quarterly results sharply.

Gap to Target and Share Count Factors

The five-year revenue target of KRW 100 billion is more than three times 2025 revenue of KRW 30 billion. Reports say market capitalization has shrunk from the level at listing, leaving revaluation of enterprise value as an open task.

The zero-interest convertible bonds and the 5-for-1 share consolidation that followed a trading halt in April 2026 are factors that change the share count and capital structure.

10

Risk factors

Execution Risk in Expansion

Pursuing the target alongside M&A and new product investment could raise cost burdens first. The company said it expanded upfront investment in cloud products and a document centralization platform in the first quarter of 2026. If the investment does not convert into revenue, the operating margin could stay in a low range.

Quarterly Earnings Volatility Risk

Operating profit fell to KRW 132 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, and both operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined year on year in the third quarter of 2026.

If quarters continue in which revenue grows but profit does not follow, room for dividends and buybacks could also be constrained. Whether quarterly swings obscure the annual trend will show up in the next periodic reports.

Capital Structure and Share Count Risk

Zero-interest convertible bonds carry a low financing cost, but conversion would increase the share count and dilute per-share metrics. The share consolidation also moved the par value from KRW 100 to KRW 500.

The company says it maintains a debt-free operation, but the terms and size of the convertible bonds need to be confirmed in disclosures.

Overall view

Innotena's revenue rose for three straight years to KRW 30 billion in 2025, but operating profit fell to KRW 1.97 billion, so the revenue and earnings trends diverge.

In the third quarter of 2026 revenue rose 11.7%, yet operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined, leaving the question of whether sales growth converts into profit.

On the financial side, the company has no borrowings, the debt-to-equity ratio fell to 37.7% at end-2025, and operating cash flow grew substantially from 2022. On shareholder returns, quarterly dividends continued from the first to the third quarter of 2026, alongside a KRW 1 billion share buyback trust.

The medium-term targets are KRW 100 billion in revenue and KRW 15 billion in operating profit within five years, a wide gap from current scale. On the business side, AX and sovereign AI partnerships and public contract wins are presented as strengths, but the volatility of the operating margin remains an open issue.

The next checkpoints are the third-quarter dividend payment on November 24, 2026, the 2026 annual report due by March 31, 2027, and the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report. In particular, the operating margin confirmed in the 2026 annual report will be the first gauge of the path toward the target.
