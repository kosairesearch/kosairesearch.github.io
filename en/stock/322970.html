Report overview

Proprietary LNP Platform, Profitability Still a Long Road Ahead

Moogene Medi has built a pipeline targeting oncology, hair loss, and metabolic diseases on its proprietary LNP drug-delivery platform, but simultaneously shrinking revenue and widening losses in FY2024 cast a shadow over near-term profitability.

02

Business structure

Founded in 2016, Moogene Medi has grown as a nano-medicine specialist whose core competency lies in its LNP drug-delivery platform.

The company can load gene-editing materials (CRISPR/Cas9), protein therapeutics, and mRNA onto LNPs for targeted in-vivo delivery, and holds proprietary LNP composition patents that circumvent those held by global pharma majors.

Its business is structured around two pillars: the cosmetic raw materials and dermatological devices segment, which contributed approximately 73% of revenues in FY2024, and the LNP-based pharmaceutical CDMO segment, which accounted for roughly 27%.

The R&D pipeline spans the colorectal cancer candidate DREC-21, the pancreatic cancer candidate PDAC-11, the topical hair-loss therapy HUTERA, a type-2 diabetes treatment, an mRNA cancer vaccine, and rare-disease therapeutics.

HUTERA is differentiated from oral formulations by its nano-liposome microbubb structure containing finasteride or dutasteride, enabling follicle-cell delivery through simple scalp application.

In terms of competition, domestic LNP players include Yuhan, Enhanced Bio, and Inventage Lab, while globally Moderna, Pfizer/BioNTech dominate LNP know-how, leaving intense competitive pressure. Moogene Medi offsets its small scale via proprietary composition patents and an established GMP facility as a CDMO base.

Headquartered in Pangyo, Seongnam, the company operates with a staff in the mid-30s, reflecting its research-focused, small-enterprise character.

03

Recent trends

In FY2024 (standalone basis), Moogene Medi's financials deteriorated across the board. Revenue declined 16.4% YoY, operating loss widened by 21.3%, and net loss surged a steep 141.8%, indicating a broad-based erosion of financial health.

The revenue shortfall is attributed to weakness in the core cosmetic raw materials and dermatological devices segment, compounded by consistently rising R&D expenditures—a structural feature of a fixed-cost-heavy research organisation.

As of 7 June 2026, the stock stood at KRW 2,085, unchanged from the prior session, with market capitalisation negligible in absolute terms. Daily trading value of approximately KRW 9.23 million underscores extreme illiquidity and constrained price-discovery.

KONEX's limited institutional participation means trading is dominated by retail investors, further amplifying price volatility risk.

The LNP-based pharmaceutical CDMO segment accounted for roughly 27% of FY2024 revenue, but delays in commercialising clinical-stage pipeline assets make rapid CDMO revenue growth challenging near term.

Structurally subdued trading activity across the KONEX market adds a further layer of constraint to price formation for this stock.

04

Outlook

Moogene Medi's growth trajectory hinges on two key axes.

First is the pace of clinical progression for the topical hair-loss treatment HUTERA; the company is actively pursuing IND approval, and success in this competitive market—where novel mechanisms like oligo RNAi candidates are advancing—could establish a meaningful commercial foothold.

Second is the order intake of its mRNA-LNP CDMO business; with GMP capacity in place, landing external manufacturing contracts in a structurally expanding global mRNA drug market would visibly lift the CDMO revenue share.

However, competing against global-scale CDMO operators on price and capacity is a near-term constraint. The oncology pipeline (DREC-21, PDAC-11) is expected to take considerable time before clinical entry and is therefore a medium-to-long-term catalyst.

Given that the loss-widening trend driven by R&D costs is unlikely to reverse quickly, external fundraising or technology licensing-out deals emerge as pivotal variables for financial stability.

Bull factors

Proprietary LNP Patents & Platform

Moogene Medi has secured proprietary LNP composition patents in the US and Australia for type-2 diabetes and topical hair-loss treatments, bypassing patents monopolised by global pharma giants.

These compositions enabling loading of gene-editing materials and protein therapeutics onto LNPs serve as potential foundations for licensing or co-development partnerships. Given LNP technology's growing centrality to the mRNA vaccine and therapeutic landscape, the platform itself carries meaningful strategic value.

Structural Growth in mRNA CDMO Demand

The global mRNA drug market has entered a structurally expansionary phase post-COVID, with applications rapidly broadening into mRNA-LNP cancer vaccines and rare-disease therapeutics.

Having completed its GMP facility and expanding its mRNA-LNP CDMO offering, Moogene Medi is positioned to benefit from this demand growth. Proprietary LNP loading technology versus domestic peers provides a potential differentiation angle.

Expanding Hair-Loss Market & HUTERA Differentiation

The Korean hair-loss patient population continues to grow, and mounting safety concerns around oral treatments such as finasteride are intensifying demand for novel-mechanism alternatives.

Moogene Medi's topical hair-loss treatment HUTERA—designed for scalp application without oral intake—could reduce systemic side-effect risk versus existing oral agents. This differentiated delivery route provides a distinct patient-targeting angle for those seeking to avoid systemic exposure.

06

Bear factors

Simultaneous Revenue Decline & Loss Widening

In FY2024, Moogene Medi simultaneously saw revenue drop 16.4% YoY, operating loss widen 21.3%, and net loss surge 141.8%—a broad-based deterioration. This reflects a combination of softening demand in cosmetic and medical-device revenue, and unabating R&D cost inflation.

With an early reversal of the loss trend appearing unlikely, accumulated deficit growth and potential capital impairment become key medium-to-long-term financial health indicators to monitor.

Pipeline Clinical Delay Risk

Core pipeline assets—oncology candidates DREC-21 and PDAC-11, and HUTERA—remain in early clinical or IND-review stages, implying substantial time and capital needs before commercialisation.

Failure to demonstrate efficacy and safety in clinical trials, or delays in regulatory approvals, could significantly impair the pipeline's value. The company's small scale leaves limited financial buffer against clinical setbacks.

KONEX Listing Illiquidity

Listed on KONEX rather than KOSPI or KOSDAQ, institutional investor access is structurally limited and daily trading value of roughly KRW 9.23 million signals extreme illiquidity. Under such conditions, even modest order flow can drive disproportionate price swings, distorting price discovery.

A potential upward transfer to KOSDAQ—which could improve visibility and liquidity—faces hurdles given the current track record of persistent and widening losses.

07

Risk factors

Macro & Sector Risk

Shifts in the global interest-rate environment and heightened risk aversion toward biopharma can rapidly chill sentiment toward small clinical-stage biotech companies. KONEX-listed small-caps in particular are prone to concentrated selling pressure when broad market liquidity contracts.

As noted in a February 2026 Eugenie Securities sector report, the Korean bio-pharma sector has been underperforming the broader market in Q1, indicating a challenging sector sentiment backdrop.

Funding & Capital Risk

Despite negative operating cash flow, Moogene Medi requires continuous capital for clinical trials and GMP facility upkeep. Capital raises via rights offerings or convertible bonds would inevitably dilute existing shareholders.

Its small KONEX-listed profile makes external debt financing and institutional investment attraction structurally difficult, leaving liquidity stress risk as a persistent concern.

Competition & Technology Risk

In the LNP space, Moderna, Pfizer/BioNTech, and other global majors hold extensive patents and manufacturing scale, creating a high bar for Moogene Medi to prove commercially meaningful technological superiority.

Domestically, Yuhan, Inventage Lab, Enhanced Bio, and others also compete in LNP, intensifying CDMO contract rivalry. Clinical failures or rival technology proving superior would materially impair the company's core platform value.

08

Overall view

Moogene Medi is a small-cap biotech holding proprietary LNP drug-delivery patents and GMP facilities in a globally high-profile technology segment, suggesting latent medium-to-long-term technology value.

However, FY2024 results showed simultaneous revenue decline (-16.4%), operating loss expansion (-21.3%), and a sharp net loss surge (-141.8%), pointing to heightened financial fragility.

Core pipeline assets—HUTERA, DREC-21, and PDAC-11—all remain at early clinical or pre-clinical stages, requiring substantial time before commercialisation. KONEX listing results in extreme illiquidity and limited institutional access, impairing price discovery and elevating volatility risk.

Positive re-rating catalysts such as licensing deals, CDMO order wins, or clinical advancement are plausible in the medium term, but the current risk-reward balance remains skewed toward uncertainty, warranting a cautious near-term stance.
