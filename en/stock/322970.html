Co-Development Deals Meet a Strained Balance Sheet

Moogene Medi has assembled a multi-partner co-development structure for its topical hair-loss candidate, yet it also carries a partially impaired equity base and heavy short-term liabilities.

02

Business structure

Moogene Medi was founded in 2016 and specializes in drug-delivery systems that use lipid nanoparticles (LNP) to carry gene-based drugs into the body efficiently, and it listed on Konex on June 24, 2020. Its core technology is a nano-medicine platform that uses LNP to develop gene, protein and chemical drugs.

The pipeline consists of pancreatic and colorectal cancer candidates and a topical hair-loss candidate. The hair-loss candidate is applied to the scalp, which a Korea Technology Finance Corporation research report described as an approach intended to limit the side effects associated with oral dosing.

By business mix, the hair-loss treatment accounts for 92% and cosmetic ingredients and skin medical devices for 7%. The company registered as a cosmetics manufacturer in March 2025, which gives its ingredient business a legal footing.

At the time of the December 2024 research report, revenue came only from contract research services, and headcount stood at 16 as of December 31, 2025, so the organization is small.

On the competitive side, global drugmakers have monopolized LNP composition patents, which made patent avoidance difficult, and that acts as a barrier to entry.

Moogene Medi says it secured its own LNP composition technology, but competition with large drugmakers and contract manufacturers will only be tested after commercialization.

On market size, one report cited Health Insurance Review and Assessment Service data showing 243,609 domestic hair-loss patients treated in 2021, up 13.7% from 2017.

Recent trends

For 2025 on a standalone basis, revenue was KRW 1,410 million, operating loss KRW 1,584 million and net loss KRW 1,475 million. In 2024, revenue was only about KRW 117 million, operating loss about KRW 2.9 billion and net loss about KRW 6.2 billion.

The 2024 net loss was far larger than the operating loss because non-operating expenses of about KRW 3.3 billion were booked, and we could not confirm the breakdown. In 2025 revenue rose and the operating loss narrowed, but the business continued to consume cash from operations.

At end-2025, total assets were KRW 9,650 million, total liabilities KRW 7,114 million and total equity KRW 2,536 million, against paid-in capital of KRW 6,068 million. Because total equity is below paid-in capital, the company is read as being in partial capital impairment.

According to a financial-data site, the debt-to-equity ratio is 280.57%, and current liabilities of about KRW 6.57 billion far exceed current assets of about KRW 0.80 billion.

Against this backdrop, a KRW 5.0 billion convertible bond with Yuhan as the subscriber was decided on March 12, 2026, and a third-party rights offering to repay debt was disclosed on March 13. The share price was KRW 2,795 as of 16:10 on March 31, 2026, and it closed at KRW 1,829 on October 27, 2026.

Market capitalization that day was KRW 18.4 billion and trading value was KRW 3,133,472. We could not confirm results for periods after the first half of 2026, so they are not covered here.

04

Outlook

The first point to watch is execution of the AD-303 (HUTERA) co-development agreement. Under its terms, Moogene Medi carries out development including formulation technology and clinical work, while the partners handle clinical development and commercialization and hold sales and marketing rights.

The term runs for 10 years from first commercial sale, and the contract amount has not been disclosed. The disclosure states that the agreement is conditional and revenue recognition may depend on clinical development succeeding.

On clinical progress, what can be confirmed is that an investigational new drug application for a Phase 1/2a trial of AD-303 (HUTERA D) was approved in August 2025; trial results have not been confirmed.

In an earlier management interview, commercialization of the hair-loss drug was expected to be possible in the second half of 2025, but the trial application was approved only in August of that year, so the timeline appears to have lengthened beyond the original plan. The second variable is the financing terms.

The convertible bond has a conversion price of KRW 2,620 and a conversion window from March 26, 2027 to February 26, 2029, and it matures on March 26, 2029. The third factor is whether the cosmetic ingredient business expands.

A special clause in the bond gives the subscriber priority negotiation rights on three products using cosmetic ingredients that it designates. The company's 2022 stated goal of a KOSDAQ transfer listing within three years has not been realized, as the shares remained on Konex as of October 27, 2026.

05

Bull factors

Multi-partner co-development structure

AD-303 (HUTERA) is covered by a co-development agreement signed on December 12, 2025 with Yuhan and two other domestic companies. On February 3, 2026 a further partner was added, and the main terms of the original agreement were unchanged.

Because the partners handle clinical development and commercialization, a small company is spared the burden of building its own sales network. The contract amount, however, has not been disclosed.

Balance-sheet repair steps and a large drugmaker's backing

Of the KRW 5.0 billion convertible bond decided on March 12, 2026, KRW 2.0 billion is for working capital and KRW 3.0 billion for debt repayment. In the same period, the third-party rights offering converts existing borrowings into equity.

That Yuhan, a co-development partner, also subscribed to the bond is a fact that can be read as continuity in the relationship. These steps ease part of the short-term liquidity pressure.

Narrower losses and platform extensibility

The operating loss narrowed from about KRW 2.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 1,584 million in 2025, while revenue rose from about KRW 117 million to KRW 1,410 million. The company expects that LNP can deliver not only gene-based drugs but also proteins and synthetic drugs.

If the platform extends beyond hair loss and into cosmetic ingredients, revenue sources could diversify, although this has not yet been realized.

06

Bear factors

A fragile balance sheet

At end-2025, total equity of KRW 2,536 million was below paid-in capital of KRW 6,068 million, which is read as partial capital impairment. According to a financial-data site, the debt-to-equity ratio is 280.57% and the quick ratio is 12.15%.

Because current liabilities far exceed current assets, the ability to service maturing debt is a key item to check. Operating cash flow in 2025 also showed a net outflow of about KRW 1.3 billion.

Conditional contracts and a slipping timeline

The disclosure states that the co-development agreement may be terminated if clinical development fails or is halted. The revenue base is also still narrow, with the 2025 operating loss of KRW 1,584 million exceeding revenue of KRW 1,410 million.

There is a gap between the commercialization target given in an earlier interview and the actual approval of the trial application in August 2025, so schedule uncertainty remains. Trial results could not be confirmed.

Dilution and thin trading liquidity

Converting the KRW 5.0 billion bond at the KRW 2,620 conversion price works out to roughly 1.9 million shares, which is not small against 10,051,978 common shares before the rights offering. The October 27, 2026 close of KRW 1,829 is below the conversion price.

If conversion does not occur, repayment of the secured bond at maturity remains a burden. Trading value that day was only KRW 3,133,472, so small order flows can move the price substantially.

Risk factors

Macro

For drug developers, the external financing environment shifts materially with interest rates and sentiment toward the biotech sector. Moogene Medi's operating cash flow is negative, so it is sensitive to changes in funding conditions.

Trading on Konex is thin, so price swings can widen when the broader market turns risk-averse. No verified data on the correlation with specific macro variables was found, so this is described qualitatively only.

Clinical and Regulatory

AD-303 (HUTERA) is at the stage where a Phase 1/2a trial application was approved in August 2025, and trial results and marketing-approval timing have not been confirmed. The co-development agreement can be terminated if the trial fails or is halted.

The colorectal and pancreatic cancer programs are at early stages, so commercialization could take a long time. The possibility of disputes over LNP composition patents is also an industry-wide variable.

Financial and Liquidity

At end-2025, current assets were only about KRW 0.80 billion against current liabilities of about KRW 6.57 billion. The convertible bond raised in March 2026 is secured by a mortgage on real estate and matures on March 26, 2029.

The convertible bond and the convertible redeemable preferred shares could increase the share count in the future. If capital impairment persists, the possibility of further fundraising cannot be ruled out.

08

Overall view

Moogene Medi combines business progress, in the form of a co-development structure for a topical hair-loss candidate built on its LNP platform, with financial constraints in the form of partial capital impairment and a high debt ratio.

On a standalone basis, 2025 revenue rose to KRW 1,410 million and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 1,584 million, but the loss-making structure continued. The co-development agreement is conditional and its amount is undisclosed, so the timing and size of any revenue contribution depend on clinical progress.

The March 2026 convertible bond and rights offering eased short-term liquidity pressure but left future share dilution and maturity repayment as variables. Trading value of only KRW 3,133,472 on October 27, 2026 also points to low liquidity.

The key items to check going forward are trial-result disclosures, whether the convertible bond is converted, and whether the capital impairment is resolved, and the assessment is left to the reader.
