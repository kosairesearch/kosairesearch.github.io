Small Physical-Asset REIT: Checking Quarterly Earnings Swings

K-Top REITs is a self-managed REIT that earns rental income from commercial buildings in Seoul, the capital region and Busan. Its disclosed 2025 results improved, but it swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026, raising quarterly earnings volatility.

02

Business structure

K-Top REITs was established in 2010 and listed on KOSPI in January 2012. It is a self-managed REIT that spreads investments across several buildings in key commercial districts in Seoul, the capital region and Busan.

A 2021 report described how the company uses borrowings alongside its KRW 43.3 billion share capital to buy buildings and earn the spread between rental income and interest costs.

At year-end 2025, total liabilities stood at KRW 109.6 billion and total equity at KRW 106.2 billion, so borrowing plays a meaningful role in the balance sheet.

The Songpa office building acquired in August 2021 for KRW 53.4 billion was reported at the time as the largest investment property among domestic self-managed REITs. It is leased to the AJ Group, and AJ Networks appeared among the top five shareholders as of June 2026 with an 8.25% stake.

The Busan Judys Taepyeong Building had a 47.85% vacancy rate at the end of 2022, and a May 2025 report said it was being run at zero vacancy. Tenants are concentrated in beauty, fashion and food and beverage categories that draw foot traffic, including Musinsa Standard.

The company does not invest in project finance development, which a 2025 report cited as insulating it from the PF loan stress in the property market. The report also noted that the company pays dividends despite bearing corporate income tax, unlike typical asset-management REITs. Given its small portfolio, the company emphasizes asset quality over expansion.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 21.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 26.9 billion in 2023 (up 24.3%), fell to KRW 12.1 billion in 2024 (down 55.1%), and recovered to KRW 15.1 billion in 2025 (up 24.8%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 17.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 20.5 billion in 2023 (up 17.0%), dropped to KRW 7.92 billion in 2024 (down 61.4%), and reached KRW 10.6 billion in 2025 (up 34.2%). The operating margin fell from 81.1% in 2022 to 76.3% in 2023 and 65.5% in 2024, then recovered to 70.4% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 10.1 billion, KRW 11.6 billion, KRW 5.17 billion and KRW 6.22 billion from 2022 to 2025, which differs from total net income including non-controlling interests of KRW 9.9 billion, KRW 11.8 billion, KRW 5.27 billion and KRW 6.51 billion.

The 2025 revenue increase was attributed mainly to higher fair-value gains on investment properties, which the company noted are unrealized and not distributable reserves.

Operating cash flow was KRW 4.26 billion in 2022 and KRW 5.98 billion in 2023, turned to -KRW 6.56 billion in 2024, and returned to KRW 2.03 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 182.6% in 2022 to 103.2% in 2025.

Quarterly results stood out in Q4 2025, with revenue of KRW 8.81 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 5.29 billion.

Q1 2026 posted revenue of KRW 2.31 billion and attributable net income of KRW 493 million, and Q2 2026 posted revenue of KRW 2.36 billion and attributable net income of KRW 1.71 billion.

In Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 2.33 billion, up 11.5% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 1.4 billion, while the company recorded a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 362 million.

The disclosed figures alone do not identify which non-operating items drove the gap between operating profit and the Q3 net loss.

05

Industry analysis

Listed REIT share prices remained weak into the second half of 2025 even as the broader Korean market rallied. Industry participants hoped that a bill addressing failing REITs would support a gradual recovery after the long slump.

According to the Korea REITs Association's June 2026 tally, the 13 listed REITs, excluding hotel-sector vehicles, averaged a 97.6% occupancy rate, and their dividend yields stayed in a 6% to 9% range. Many REITs had already addressed refinancing risk ahead of time.

Industry officials cited K-Top REITs as a model for small REITs with quality assets and conservative management, and praised expansion that does not rely on rights offerings that investors have shunned.

A 2025 report noted that large-cap REITs tend to draw investors through their conglomerate affiliations, while smaller REITs face lower awareness.

Among commercial peers, the Busan office and retail market recorded a 14.2% average vacancy rate for mid-to-large shops in Q1 2025, which makes the zero-vacancy operation of the Busan building stand out. Its competitive position therefore rests on asset location and tenant management rather than scale.

06

Outlook

The company disclosed that shareholders approved its 17th-term business plan and borrowing and bond issuance plan at the March 2026 annual general meeting. With the borrowing plan approved, interest costs and refinancing terms will be key variables for future results.

In August 2026 the company disclosed borrowings of KRW 1.06 billion and KRW 1.2 billion aimed at extending existing debt, which the filings put at 9.98% and 11.30% of equity. Both loans mature around August 2027, so refinancing terms are the next checkpoint.

A May 2025 report said the company was pursuing a new business buying non-performing loans secured by real estate and had begun the registration process for a debt purchase and collection license. The company also said it could widen its target pool to NPLs held by financial institutions.

In Q1 2025 it acquired part of the third floor of the LG Palace Building, connected to Exit 9 of Hongik University Station on Seoul Subway Line 2. The company said it expects the purchases, made below market prices, to support asset value and dividend capacity.

Whether the NPL registration has been completed and whether investments have been executed cannot be confirmed from the disclosures, so the next annual report's business section will be the reference.

If fair-value gains keep a large share of revenue, the gap between that income and distributable reserves should be tracked quarter by quarter.

Operating profit rose in Q3 2026 while the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, so the composition of non-operating items should be confirmed in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Physical Assets With Little PF Exposure

A 2025 report described K-Top REITs as focused on physical building leases without PF development exposure. The Busan building's vacancy rate, 47.85% at the end of 2022, was reported as zero at the time of the report.

The operating margin recovered to 70.4% in 2025 from 65.5% in 2024, which points to improving profitability from the rental base.

Recovery in 2025 Results and Quarterly Operating Profit

2025 revenue rose to KRW 15.1 billion and operating profit to KRW 10.6 billion, both up from 2024. Q3 2026 operating profit reached KRW 1.4 billion, up 31.6% year on year, and Q3 revenue rose 11.5% year on year.

Operating cash flow moved from -KRW 6.56 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.03 billion in 2025, which indicates recovering cash generation.

Major Shareholder Buying and Asset Locations

Major shareholder Kim Cheol bought 190,000 shares on-market on March 18, 2026, and continued buying in April, reporting a 13.522% holding as of April 3, 2026. The company's portfolio centers on buildings in core commercial areas of central Seoul and the capital region, a location profile the 2025 report credited. The company said purchases below market prices could support asset value and dividend capacity.

09

Bear factors

Reliance on Unrealized Fair-Value Gains

The main driver of 2025 revenue growth was fair-value gains on investment properties, which the company said are unrealized and not distributable reserves. Such gains lift reported revenue without bringing cash in directly.

Operating cash flow fell to -KRW 6.56 billion in 2024, so the gap between reported profit and cash remains central to interpreting the results.

Swing Into Quarterly Net Loss

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 362 million, a swing into loss from a year earlier. Operating profit rose in the same quarter, but non-operating items pushed the bottom line into a loss.

With four-quarter attributable net income totaling KRW 7.13 billion, the reversal in quarterly direction raises questions about earnings stability.

Borrowing Dependence and Small-Cap Liquidity

Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 109.6 billion, close to total equity of KRW 106.2 billion. In August 2026 the company disclosed borrowings of KRW 1.06 billion and KRW 1.2 billion to extend existing loans.

A 2025 report noted that investors have limited understanding of REITs and tend to favor conglomerate-affiliated names, which adds to liquidity constraints for small listings.

10

Risk factors

Accounting Structure Risk

Revenue and operating profit can depend heavily on fair-value revaluations of investment properties. Because these gains are unrealized, they diverge from distributable reserves. Since this item drove the 2025 improvement, a valuation loss in a future period could cut reported profit sharply.

Borrowing and Refinancing Risk

Both borrowings disclosed in August 2026 extend existing loans and fall due around August 2027. With total liabilities close to total equity, interest rates and refinancing terms feed directly into interest costs. Operating profit growth alongside a Q3 2026 net loss makes financing costs a item to track.

Concentration and Market Conditions

With a small asset base and tenants concentrated in certain categories, changes at one tenant or one commercial district could weigh heavily on results. Listed REIT share prices remained weak through late 2025, and the sector recovery was described as gradual.

The new NPL business becomes a meaningful revenue source only once registration and actual investment are confirmed.

Overall view

K-Top REITs is a self-managed REIT that earns rental income from commercial buildings in Seoul, the capital region and Busan. Its disclosed 2025 results showed higher revenue, operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders, and the operating margin recovered from 2024.

The main driver of the 2025 revenue increase was unrealized fair-value gains, so distributable reserves and cash flow need to be read alongside reported profit.

In Q3 2026 operating profit rose, but the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 362 million, and four-quarter attributable net income totals KRW 7.13 billion.

Total liabilities and total equity are of similar size, and two borrowings disclosed in August 2026 extend existing loans, falling due around August 2027.

Major shareholder purchases and the sector's expected gradual recovery are supportive factors, while the liquidity constraints of a small REIT and the weak sector share price trend remain burdens.

The next points to confirm are the composition of non-operating items in the 2026 annual report, refinancing terms, and whether the NPL business is executed.
