Initech: Sales Rebound, Operating Losses Persist

02

Business structure

Initech, founded on June 13, 1997, is an information security solution company focused on authentication and encryption, integrated security management and financial IT services.

Its core products are IniSign, a PKI-based electronic signature solution, i-Safe, a PC security solution that combines keyboard protection and firewall functions, and the IniPass platform for managing multiple authentication methods in one place.

According to JobKorea company data as of March 2026, ITO accounts for the largest share of sales at 40%, followed by authentication, encryption and integrated security management at 16% and financial ASP at 15%. Revenue comes from solution sales as well as financial ASP, SI, ITO and maintenance contracts.

Its customers are centered on banks, card companies and insurers, and changes in their IT spending and security regulations shape demand.

Since 2025 the company has expanded real-time security monitoring and ransomware monitoring services, and the key question is whether these services lead to recurring revenue and margin improvement.

In AI security, its SecureAI platform won the Artificial Intelligence Technology Innovation award at the AI Service Award 2025 and has been applied in public, financial and aviation projects through its AI, data and cloud subsidiary.

Other subsidiaries include one established in September 2025, a defense-focused unit whose establishment was announced in November 2025, and one set up in December 2025.

Competition overlaps with Ahnlab in security software and with large IT service providers such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS in financial system integration and operation.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 53.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 45.7 billion in 2023 (down 15.2% year on year), KRW 38.9 billion in 2024 (down 14.8%) and KRW 32.8 billion in 2025 (down 15.6%), a three-year consecutive decline from 2023 through 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for sales and 2025 the smallest.

Operating results moved in different directions: an operating loss of KRW 2.5 billion in 2022 widened to KRW 3.54 billion in 2023, turned into an operating profit of KRW 231 million in 2024, and then returned to an operating loss of KRW 2.39 billion in 2025.

The operating margin was -4.7% in 2022, -7.8% in 2023, 0.6% in 2024 and -7.3% in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from KRW 201 million in 2022 to a loss of KRW 302 million in 2023, returned to a profit of KRW 2.34 billion in 2024 and rose 21.0% to KRW 2.83 billion in 2025.

A 2025 profit at the attributable level despite an operating loss suggests that items outside operations lifted the bottom line. Operating cash flow was -KRW 7.66 billion in 2022 and -KRW 708 million in 2023, turned positive at KRW 4.57 billion in 2024, and returned to -KRW 1.39 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio edged up from 5.4% in 2022 to 6.7% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 138 billion at the end of 2025, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 9.21 billion.

Quarterly, the third quarter of 2025 showed sales of KRW 7.9 billion and an operating loss of KRW 632 million.

The fourth quarter lifted sales to KRW 9.36 billion (up 18.5% quarter on quarter) but posted an operating loss of KRW 131 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 17.6 billion, which turned into a loss.

First-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 8.33 billion (down 11.0% quarter on quarter) with the operating loss widening to KRW 1.06 billion. Second-quarter 2026 sales rose to KRW 9.49 billion while the operating loss narrowed to KRW 431 million, and attributable net profit turned to KRW 6.45 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 sales reached KRW 11.8 billion, up 49.6% year on year, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 93.05 million. The combined attributable net loss for the last four quarters was KRW 12.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Initech's main customers are banks, card companies and insurers, so its business moves in step with financial-sector IT investment cycles. Changes in security regulation move demand for authentication and encryption solutions, which is a structural variable for the company's sales.

The company argues that as AI adoption spreads, security becomes an essential rather than optional element, and it is positioning its SecureAI platform around that view.

Product announcements on zero-trust based ICAM and hybrid security, aimed at leadership in next-generation security markets, were also reported in the first half of 2026. How quickly these announcements translate into sales is a matter for subsequent quarterly results and disclosures to verify.

In defense, its defense-focused subsidiary said it is in discussions over unmanned systems and communications security items with demand at home and abroad. However, the defense contribution to sales cannot yet be separated out in reported results.

In competitive terms, the company is compared with specialist listed security companies such as Ahnlab in security software, and overlaps with large players such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS in financial-sector SI and ITO work.

JobKorea classifies Initech as a small or mid-sized company, and its scale is far smaller than that of large IT groups.

06

Outlook

As a company-reported indicator, first-half 2026 security and financial IT sales were reported at KRW 17.82 billion, matching the combined first- and second-quarter sales in the disclosed figures. The main order backlog at the end of the half was KRW 6.9 billion for an internet banking build for Suhyup Bank.

In June 2026 the company signed an asset transfer agreement with Dynamic Solution and an MOU with Hyperknowlogy on AI-driven intelligence and digital security. In November 2025 it signed an MOU on generative AI security, and in October 2025 an MOU on a stablecoin authentication payment platform.

MOUs precede revenue, so the timing of any contribution to reported results needs to be tracked. On shareholder returns, the board resolved in January 2026 to cancel 1 million treasury shares, equal to 4.3% of total issued shares, using distributable profits in a profit-funded cancellation.

The company said it holds distributable profit of about KRW 50 billion or more as of the 2024 settlement and may consider further treasury share purchases and cancellations. In March 2026 the leadership moved from a sole CEO to co-CEOs.

The extraordinary shareholders meeting was moved from November 17 to December 3, 2026 at 10 a.m., and its venue and agenda will be set by the board and corrected in a later filing.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly sales rebound

First-half 2026 security and financial IT sales were reported at KRW 17.82 billion. Second-quarter sales rose 13.9% quarter on quarter to KRW 9.49 billion, and third-quarter sales rose 24.5% to KRW 11.8 billion, marking two consecutive quarterly increases.

Third-quarter sales grew 49.6% year on year, in contrast to the annual decline. The operating loss narrowed to KRW 431 million in the second quarter and KRW 93.05 million in the third quarter.

Capital capacity and shareholder returns

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 138 billion against total liabilities of KRW 9.21 billion, so financial burden is light. The debt ratio stayed low, moving from 5.4% in 2022 to 6.7% in 2025.

The company resolved in January 2026 to cancel 1 million treasury shares through profit-funded cancellation and said it holds distributable profit of about KRW 50 billion or more as of the 2024 settlement. Its review of further buybacks and cancellations is a shareholder-return factor in its favor.

AI security technology and new businesses

The SecureAI platform won the Artificial Intelligence Technology Innovation award at the AI Service Award 2025. Application cases built up across public, financial and aviation projects through its subsidiary support its technical credibility.

In defense, its defense-focused subsidiary is in discussions over unmanned systems and communications security items.

09

Bear factors

Three straight years of sales decline

Annual sales fell to KRW 45.7 billion in 2023, KRW 38.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 32.8 billion in 2025, a three-year decline with a 15.6% drop in 2025. Compared with KRW 53.8 billion in 2022, the 2025 level is markedly lower.

First-quarter 2026 sales fell 11.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 8.33 billion, so the quarterly recovery has not been smooth. Whether two quarters of rebound can reverse the annual decline is the bearish question.

Five consecutive quarters of operating losses

Operating losses have persisted for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. The 2025 operating margin was -7.3%, and the operating loss widened from KRW 131 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.06 billion in the first quarter of 2026. A KRW 93.05 million operating loss still remained in the third quarter of 2026.

Volatile attributable earnings and disclosure risk

The fourth quarter of 2025 swung from attributable net profit of KRW 5.85 billion in the third quarter to an attributable net loss of KRW 17.6 billion.

The company received a non-compliance designation notice over a delayed single sales and supply contract disclosure, which calls for scrutiny of its disclosure processes. On November 2, 2026, it was designated a one-day investment warning stock for concentrated trading by a small number of accounts.

10

Risk factors

Disclosure and trading risk

The non-compliance designation was deferred for six months, so whether any further disclosure delay occurs after the grace period ends in mid-November 2026 is the key question. Depending on the outcome, additional measures such as trading suspension or a listing qualification review could follow. Stability of disclosure procedures links directly to trading-related risk.

Concentration in financial-sector demand

The revenue base relies on IT spending by banks, card companies and insurers, making it sensitive to cuts in that spending or changes in security regulation.

The main backlog reported at the half-year end was KRW 6.9 billion for a single internet banking build for Suhyup Bank, so contract concentration also needs watching. Whether financial ASP and maintenance revenue stay stable is another point to monitor.

New business and management risk

Several subsidiaries in defense and AI security have been established, so new businesses may take time to show up in reported results. The leadership moved to co-CEOs in March 2026, and the agenda for the extraordinary shareholders meeting has not yet been set.

The pace at which new businesses become revenue and the stability of the management structure both need monitoring.

Overall view

Initech is a company in authentication, encryption and financial ASP that depends on financial-sector IT spending, and its sales have fallen for three straight years since 2023 to KRW 32.8 billion in 2025.

Sales rose in the second and third quarters of 2026, and third-quarter sales grew 49.6% year on year, showing a rebound.

However, operating losses have persisted for five consecutive quarters since the third quarter of 2025, and the fourth quarter of 2025 showed an attributable net loss of KRW 17.6 billion, pointing to a large non-operating effect. The third-quarter 2026 operating loss narrowed to KRW 93.05 million but remained a loss.

On the financial side, total equity of KRW 138 billion and total liabilities of KRW 9.21 billion at the end of 2025 are the basis of the bullish case on balance-sheet burden.

Against this, the combined attributable net loss over the last four quarters was KRW 12.2 billion, and the price-to-book ratio is below 1x, so the gap between asset value and earnings capacity should be read together.

On disclosure, the end of the non-compliance designation grace period and the December 3, 2026 extraordinary shareholders meeting are the next checkpoints, and the 2026 annual report will show whether the annual sales decline stops.

Defense and AI security businesses do not yet appear separately in reported results, so whether the sales rebound translates into operating profit is the central task.
