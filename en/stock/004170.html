Department store profit recovers, sales growth limited

02

Business structure

Shinsegae centers on its department store business and consolidates duty-free, home-furnishing, hotel and property, and TV home-shopping subsidiaries.

The department store business posted third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 1.86 trillion and operating profit of KRW 103.7 billion (preliminary, including separately incorporated stores in Gwangju, Daegu and Daejeon). By store, Main Store sales rose 44% year on year, the Gangnam store 17% and the Centum City store 14%.

The duty-free subsidiary's third-quarter sales fell 16.3% to KRW 451.1 billion, but operating profit turned positive at KRW 25.3 billion. The home-furnishing subsidiary's sales rose 85.0% to KRW 118.2 billion, a rise reported to reflect the acquisition of a lifestyle brand.

The hotel and property subsidiary posted sales of KRW 107.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 31.2 billion, while the TV home-shopping subsidiary posted sales of KRW 84.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.2 billion. The joint-venture outlet company Shinsegae Simon is not included in consolidated results.

Foreign-customer sales have become a larger part of the mix. Competition overlaps with large department store operators such as Hyundai Department Store and Lotte Shopping, and Shinsegae points to brand strength at its flagship stores as its differentiator.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 7.81 trillion in 2022 to KRW 6.36 trillion in 2023 (down 18.6%), then rose to KRW 6.57 trillion in 2024 (up 3.4%) and KRW 6.93 trillion in 2025 (up 5.5%), two straight years of growth from 2024 to 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 645.4 billion in 2022, KRW 639.8 billion in 2023 (down 0.9%), KRW 477 billion in 2024 (down 25.4%) and KRW 480 billion in 2025 (up 0.6%), with 2024 the smallest of the four years.

Operating margin rose from 8.3% in 2022 to 10.1% in 2023, then slipped to 7.3% in 2024 and 6.9% in 2025, the lowest of the four years, so margins did not follow the 2025 sales recovery.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 406.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 225.1 billion in 2023 (down 44.6%), KRW 107.8 billion in 2024 (down 52.1%) and KRW 13.9 billion in 2025 (down 87.1%), three straight years of decline.

The gap between KRW 480 billion of operating profit and KRW 13.9 billion of controlling net income in 2025 suggests large non-operating, tax or minority-interest effects, and the line items should be confirmed in the 2026 annual report.

Total net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 64.6 billion in 2025, larger than the controlling figure, which points to a sizable minority share.

The debt ratio rose from 129.5% in 2022 to 140.9% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 9.26 trillion and total equity of KRW 6.57 trillion at year-end 2025, of which controlling equity was KRW 4.46 trillion. Operating cash flow was KRW 990.6 billion in 2025, the highest of the four years.

Quarterly sales peaked at KRW 1.93 trillion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and then fell for three straight quarters to KRW 1.66 trillion in the third quarter of 2026.

Quarterly operating profit peaked at KRW 197.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then fell for two straight quarters, to KRW 167.1 billion and KRW 164.3 billion.

Controlling net income swung to a KRW 76.5 billion loss in the fourth quarter of 2025, returned to profit at KRW 120.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026, and reached KRW 118 billion in the third quarter, up 235.1% year on year. Controlling net income for the last four quarters totals KRW 251.8 billion.

Preliminary third-quarter 2026 releases attributed the profit gain to department store growth and a duty-free return to profit as Incheon Airport rent fell KRW 58.8 billion year on year to KRW 22.6 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The department store industry is reading as a phase in which a domestic consumer recovery and rising inbound demand appear together. When fourth-quarter 2025 results were released, brokerages interpreted the trend as a result of domestic consumption recovering alongside stronger inbound demand.

Foreign customers' share of sales widened to 5.7% in the fourth quarter of 2025, and cumulative foreign-customer sales for January to September 2026 more than doubled from a year earlier (preliminary).

Strength in luxury and high-priced categories was also reported, with department store luxury sales reported up 20% in the third quarter of 2026 (preliminary).

Duty-free demand is shrinking in revenue terms: the duty-free unit's third-quarter sales fell 16.3%, and reports tied the durability of profit gains to continued department store growth as duty-free sales keep declining.

In competition, Hyundai Department Store and Lotte Shopping operate in the same large department store format, so Shinsegae's foreign-customer growth and store-level sales growth serve as the comparison points.

Among its stores, Main Store showed the strongest foreign-customer growth, with the Centum City store also posting a high rate. Some consumer categories show signs of slowing, so it is worth checking whether luxury demand at department stores moves in the same direction as domestic consumption overall.

06

Outlook

The direction the company has stated centers on strengthening its department store core and sharpening each subsidiary's business focus. After the third-quarter release, Shinsegae said it will continue investing for the future and build growth momentum through sharper business focus at each unit (preliminary).

For the department store business, it cited reinforcing luxury and contemporary offerings and bringing in differentiated food and beverage brands. On shareholder returns, it plans to roughly double the quarterly dividend and pair it with share cancellations.

In duty-free, the end of operations at Incheon Airport's DF2 was completed in April 2026, and third-quarter airport rent fell KRW 58.8 billion year on year to KRW 22.6 billion. Duty-free sales are still declining, so whether revenue recovers after the rent savings is the next checkpoint.

For department stores, reports tied the durability of growth to the effects of the Main Store renovation and the expansion of high-priced categories. The joint-venture outlet company Shinsegae Simon is not included in consolidated results and moves separately from consolidated sales.

Fourth-quarter 2026 sales and operating profit are not yet available. The timing of the announced shareholder return steps should be confirmed through company disclosures.

08

Bull factors

Department store profit growth

Department store operating profit rose 23.5% to KRW 103.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and Main Store sales rose 44% (preliminary). Cumulative foreign-customer sales of KRW 850 billion for January to September were a record.

The third-quarter release confirmed that department store profit growth is the core of the overall earnings recovery.

Duty-free turnaround and lower rent

The duty-free unit turned profitable with third-quarter operating profit of KRW 25.3 billion, after the Incheon Airport DF2 operations ended in April. Third-quarter airport rent fell KRW 58.8 billion year on year to KRW 22.6 billion. Sales fell 16.3%, but a lower fixed rent burden changed the profit structure.

Expanded shareholder returns

The company said it will roughly double its third-quarter dividend and pair it with share cancellations. The plan comes as controlling net income has stayed positive since the first quarter of 2026. The timing and size of execution will be confirmed in later disclosures.

Bear factors

Flat sales and shrinking duty-free revenue

Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 1.66 trillion rose only 1.2% year on year. Quarterly sales have declined for three straight quarters since the first quarter of 2026. Duty-free sales fell 16.3% in the third quarter, so profit durability depends largely on the department store business.

Weaker earnings base and margins

Controlling net income fell 87.1% to KRW 13.9 billion in 2025, and the operating margin of 6.9% was the lowest of the four years. Operating profit has declined for two straight quarters since peaking in the first quarter of 2026. A full recovery in earnings would require margins to rebound.

Leverage and recent loss history

The debt ratio rose to 140.9% in 2025 from 129.5% in 2022. Total liabilities were KRW 9.26 trillion and total equity KRW 6.57 trillion at year-end 2025. Controlling net income swung to a KRW 76.5 billion loss in the fourth quarter of 2025, so the stability of quarterly profit needs further confirmation.

10

Risk factors

Foreign demand and exchange rates

Foreign-customer sales have become a core driver of department store growth, but they are exposed to overseas travel demand and currency swings. With cumulative foreign sales at a record, a slowdown in inbound demand could change growth rates sharply at Main Store and the Centum City store. Quarterly disclosures of foreign-customer sales are needed to track this.

Duty-free contraction and lease terms

Duty-free sales continue to decline, and rent burdens have risen before after airport rent relief ended. Reports noted that the end of Incheon Airport rent relief raised rent costs in an earlier quarter.

If the duty-free business keeps shrinking, the durability of its turnaround depends on lease terms and discount management.

Acquisition effects and joint ventures

Reports tied the home-furnishing unit's 85.0% third-quarter sales growth partly to the acquisition of a lifestyle brand. Acquisition-driven growth can slow once the base effect rises.

The joint-venture outlet company Shinsegae Simon is not included in consolidated results, so its performance does not appear in the consolidated figures.

Overall view

Shinsegae's third-quarter 2026 operating profit reached KRW 164.3 billion, supported by department store profit growth and a duty-free return to profit. This contrasts with controlling net income falling to KRW 13.9 billion for full-year 2025.

However, quarterly sales have fallen for three straight quarters since the first quarter of 2026, and duty-free sales fell 16.3%. The debt ratio rose to 140.9% in 2025, and the company posted a controlling net loss in the fourth quarter of 2025.

Shareholder returns are framed as a roughly doubled quarterly dividend and share cancellations. The share price sits in a low range relative to net assets, but whether quarterly earnings recovery continues is central to interpreting it.

The next checkpoints are fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
