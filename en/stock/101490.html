02

Business structure

S&S Tech makes blank masks, the master plates from which photomasks for semiconductor and display lithography are built, and is listed on KOSDAQ.

Blank masks generated KRW 234.8 billion in 2025, or 96.3% of revenue, while new technology financing and investment contributed 2.9% and property leasing and related services 0.8%. The business is effectively a single product, and its top four customers account for 61% of sales.

Customers include Samsung Electronics, Samsung Display, SK hynix and LG Display, plus overseas customers in Taiwan and China. Blank mask exports in 2025 were KRW 131.6 billion and domestic sales were KRW 103.2 billion, and exports were 54% of total sales, leaving the company exposed to the US dollar and the yen.

Japanese companies such as HOYA and Shin-Etsu Chemical hold more than 80% of the global market. S&S Tech was founded in 2001 and localized production in 2002–2003. A 2025 brokerage analysis put its share of the Chinese blank mask market above 40%.

On technology, the company opened a dedicated EUV center in Yongin in October 2025 and has invested about KRW 100 billion in its EUV business. EUV blank masks and pellicles are also produced at the Daegu plant. A pellicle is a consumable part that protects a photomask from contamination during EUV exposure.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue grew from KRW 123.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 150.3 billion in 2023 (up 21.7% year on year), KRW 176.0 billion in 2024 (up 17.1%), and KRW 243.7 billion in 2025 (up 38.5%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 16.0 billion in 2022 to KRW 50.4 billion in 2025, with growth of 70.9% in 2025. The operating margin improved from 13.0% in 2022 to 16.7% in 2023 and 16.7% in 2024, then to 20.7% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to owners of the parent grew from KRW 17.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 58.3 billion in 2025, up 91.1% year on year in 2025. Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 58.1 billion in 2025, almost the same as net profit attributable to owners of the parent.

Cash flow from operating activities increased from KRW 24.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 46.9 billion in 2025, showing cash generation close to the 2025 operating profit of KRW 50.4 billion. Over the same period, the debt-to-equity ratio rose from 19.1% in 2022 to 16.3% in 2023, 20.3% in 2024, and 29.2% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 304.3 billion (KRW 303.0 billion attributable to owners of the parent) and total liabilities were KRW 88.8 billion. The rise in the debt-to-equity ratio coincides with the period of capital expenditure, including the completion of the EUV Center in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the figures started from 3Q 2025 revenue of KRW 61.5 billion, operating profit of KRW 11.9 billion, and net profit attributable to owners of the parent of KRW 12.7 billion, and peaked in 4Q 2025 with revenue of KRW 63.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 13.3 billion, and net profit attributable to owners of the parent of KRW 23.5 billion.

In 1Q 2026, revenue was KRW 61.7 billion, operating profit was KRW 11.4 billion, and net profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 13.8 billion.

Revenue of KRW 66.4 billion in 2Q 2026 was the largest of the five quarters of reported results, but net profit attributable to owners of the parent fell to KRW 12.9 billion.

In 3Q 2026, the company recorded revenue of KRW 62.0 billion (up 0.7% year on year), operating profit of KRW 11.4 billion (down 4.3%), and net profit attributable to owners of the parent of KRW 8.04 billion (down 36.7%).

The fact that net profit attributable to owners of the parent in 4Q 2025 greatly exceeded operating profit is a point where the effects of non-operating income and expenses and of tax items should be checked in the income statement of the quarterly report.

The combined net profit attributable to owners of the parent for the most recent four quarters is KRW 58.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The blank mask market has been led by Japanese companies. One analysis puts HOYA, Shin-Etsu Chemical and Ulcoat at more than 80% of the global market. A 2025 report said Samsung Electronics also prepared additional sourcing from AGC as a contingency in case its quality evaluation failed or was delayed.

A global market research firm projects the EUV blank mask market to grow from about USD 300 million at the time of reporting to USD 1.3 billion by 2035.

Demand on the DUV side has been supported by China, where manufacturers using DUV-based multi-patterning have driven sharp growth in mask usage, according to a 2025 analysis. AI chips and OLED display demand are also cited as drivers of blank mask demand.

A 2025 brokerage report placed the company third in the semiconductor blank mask market, second in displays and first in Korea. On pricing, EUV blank masks are reported at KRW 10 million to KRW 100 million per sheet, at least ten times DUV masks, which underpins expectations for profitability.

On timing, EUV adoption has already slipped once: in July 2024 SK Securities expected EUV-related results to be pushed from 2025 into 2026. Bullish points center on Chinese demand and domestic EUV localization, while bearish points center on Japanese incumbents and the risk of further qualification delays.

06

Outlook

The company has outlined expanding EUV blank mask production. Once supply begins, it plans to add EUV lines and build advanced production facilities in stages, using its technology base.

Early-2026 reports said Samsung Electronics was running final evaluation of the company's EUV blank masks with application targeted for the second quarter. Initial use may be limited to some EUV lines, with the scope expanding gradually as quality and productivity are confirmed.

A 2025 report estimated EUV blank mask revenue for 2026 at a conservative KRW 15 billion to KRW 20 billion. That is a 2025-era estimate and should be checked against the FY2026 annual report. Data aggregated by investing.com showed Q2 2026 revenue below the consensus forecast.

Pellicle work is also a focus, with the company concentrating resources on mass production of EUV blank masks and pellicles. The next items to watch are how far EUV application spreads after 2026 and the scale of additional capacity at the Yongin site. The next confirmation point is the revenue mix and operating margin in the FY2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Domestic EUV supply advancing

Reports in early 2026 said Samsung Electronics was running final evaluation of the company's EUV blank masks with application targeted for the second quarter. Passing Samsung's quality and reliability tests could bring the company into leading-edge global processes.

Because EUV blank masks carry high unit prices, revenue and profit leverage could be large once supply ramps. The scope of use will depend on evaluation results.

China share and DUV demand

A 2025 analysis put the company's share of the Chinese blank mask market above 40%. Chinese manufacturers expanding DUV-based multi-patterning are increasing mask usage, which is a positive factor.

Because blank masks are designed into process recipes from the start, a qualified supplier tends to secure long-term supply and wider share.

Improving profitability

The operating margin rose from 13.0% in 2022 to 20.7% in 2025. Operating profit reached KRW 50.4 billion in 2025, up 70.9% on the prior year. A 2025 market estimate noted margins moving from the mid-teens toward nearly 20%. Product mix improvement is cited as a factor behind the margin gain.

09

Bear factors

Quarterly profit decline continues

Profit attributable to shareholders peaked at KRW 23.5 billion in Q4 2025 and then fell over three straight quarters to KRW 8.04 billion in Q3 2026. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 62 billion was up 0.7% YoY, but operating profit of KRW 11.4 billion was down 4.3%. Revenue has not translated into profit growth in recent quarters.

Concentration in customers and products

Blank masks make up 96% of revenue and the top four customers account for 61% of sales. Samsung Electronics is a key customer and shareholder, which eases the risk, but revenue can change sharply if industry conditions or customer supply-chain strategies shift.

With one product line accounting for most sales, a single customer's order change can move quarterly results significantly.

Currency and trade variables

Exports are 54% of sales and the company has dollar and yen exposure, so exchange rate moves affect earnings. The company says it adjusts raw material purchase prices in line with exchange rates as a hedge. It has also named tariffs and US-China supply chain decoupling as sources of volatility.

10

Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

Blank masks at 96% of sales and a top-four customer share of 61% mean results are sensitive to investment cuts or supply-chain changes at a few customers. A reduction in orders from one customer can move quarterly revenue materially.

Whether customer diversification is progressing can be checked in the customer disclosures in the annual report.

EUV qualification and ramp delays

EUV blank masks only generate revenue after customers pass process-fit testing. If that takes longer, recovery of equipment investment could be delayed. In July 2024, SK Securities expected EUV-related earnings to slip into 2026. Repeated delays could lengthen the payback period for these investments.

Exchange rates and US-China trade

Export sales at 54% of revenue and dollar and yen exposure mean exchange rate moves affect results. Tariffs and US-China supply-chain decoupling have been identified as sources of volatility. If US export controls on China widened to cover blank masks, the China revenue base could be affected.

12

Overall view

S&S Tech's revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to shareholders each rose for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and the operating margin reached 20.7% in 2025. Profit attributable to shareholders was KRW 58.3 billion in 2025, up 91.1%, and operating cash flow rose to KRW 46.9 billion.

By contrast, that profit fell for three consecutive quarters into 2026, reaching KRW 8.04 billion in Q3 2026. Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 66.4 billion was the highest of the five reported quarters, but it did not translate into higher profit. Sales are concentrated, with blank masks at 96% and the top four customers at 61%.

Bullish factors are domestic EUV adoption progress and China market share, while bearish factors are the quarterly profit decline, customer concentration, currency exposure and uncertainty over EUV timing.

The next checkpoint is the FY2026 annual report, due by end-March 2027, which will show full-year results and how much EUV revenue was recorded. Both views currently carry comparable weight.
