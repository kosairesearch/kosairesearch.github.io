Sewon Precision: Profit Recovery Amid Flat Sales

Sewon Precision's revenue slipped 0.8% in the fiscal year ended June 2026, yet its operating margin rose to 13.3%, and the company returned to operating profit in Q2 2026 after an operating loss in Q1.

  1. 1

    Revenue for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was KRW 178 billion, down 0.8% year on year, while operating profit rose 38.7% to KRW 23.6 billion.

  2. 2

    The operating margin climbed from 7.6% in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to 13.3% in fiscal 2026, the highest of the four years.

  3. 3

    Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 49.6 billion in fiscal 2026, up 6.7%, and the sum over the last four quarters is also KRW 49.6 billion.

  4. 4

    Quarterly operating profit swung from an operating loss of KRW 670 million in Q1 2026 to operating profit of KRW 11.3 billion in Q2 2026, while Q2 revenue fell 3.5% year on year.

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Sewon Precision Industry was founded in 1989 in the Seongseo Industrial Complex in Daegu and listed on the KOSPI in 1996, operating as an automotive parts maker focused on body reinforcement panels.

The company reports a single automotive parts segment, and segment revenue shares were not confirmed in the sources reviewed, so this report does not state them. Its main customers are Hyundai Motor and its affiliates, to which it supplies body parts, so revenue tracks vehicle sales volumes and export performance.

The company describes its just-in-time production as a way to cut inventory and support cost control and quality stability. On a consolidated basis it holds 12 domestic affiliates and a Chinese subsidiary, so reported results include both domestic and overseas subsidiaries.

In preliminary results disclosed electronically in September 2026, the company cited improved profit at subsidiaries as the main driver of higher operating profit, which means the parent's core business and subsidiary contributions should be read separately.

R&D spending was KRW 2.5 billion in fiscal 2026, or 2.67% of revenue, down from KRW 2.85 billion (3.12%) in fiscal 2025. Because the customer base is concentrated in the Hyundai group, new model awards and production volumes on existing models largely determine the direction of results.

A director was an independent director who resigned in July 2026, which changed the board composition. Listed peers in the same industry include Sungwoo Hitech, Hyundai WIA, Seoyon E-Hwa, Daewonkangup and Myoung Shin Industry, but this report does not compare their revenue or margins with Sewon Precision's figures.

04

Earnings analysis

On the audited annual basis, fiscal 2023 (ended June 2023) started with revenue of KRW 159.2 billion, operating profit of KRW 12 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 9.49 billion.

Fiscal 2024 revenue rose to KRW 162.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 19.2 billion, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned to KRW 55.4 billion.

Fiscal 2025 revenue of KRW 179.4 billion was the largest of the four years, yet operating profit fell 11.4% to KRW 17 billion and the operating margin dropped to 9.5%.

Fiscal 2026 revenue of KRW 178 billion was 0.8% lower, but operating profit rose 38.7% to KRW 23.6 billion, the largest of the four years, and the operating margin reached 13.3%. This pattern points to margin improvement lifting profit more than revenue scale did.

In preliminary results disclosed electronically on September 1, 2026, net profit was reported at roughly KRW 56 billion, while the regular report figure is KRW 55.3 billion.

The gap between that figure and KRW 49.6 billion of profit attributable to controlling shareholders reflects the inclusion of non-controlling interests in total net profit.

Total net profit moved from a net loss of KRW 31.3 billion in fiscal 2023 to KRW 55 billion in fiscal 2024, KRW 48.8 billion in fiscal 2025 and KRW 55.3 billion in fiscal 2026.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 60.6 billion in Q2 2025 with operating profit of KRW 8.14 billion, then fell to KRW 39.2 billion in Q3 (down 35.4% from the prior quarter) with operating profit of KRW 7.88 billion, and Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 42 billion with operating profit of KRW 5.12 billion.

Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 38.4 billion came with an operating loss of KRW 670 million, and Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 58.5 billion produced operating profit of KRW 11.3 billion.

Because Q2 revenue was 3.5% lower than a year earlier, the profit recovery likely owed more to cost factors than to sales growth, though the company's explanation was not found.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 1.76 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 23.6 billion in Q2, well above that quarter's operating profit, which suggests non-operating items and after-tax effects were included.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 60.4 billion in fiscal 2025 to KRW 34.8 billion in fiscal 2026, so higher profit and cash generation did not move in the same direction. One-time items were not separately identified in the disclosures reviewed.

Industry analysis

Sewon Precision's revenue tracks vehicle sales and export performance, so its end market is domestic and overseas automaker production and sales.

The industry research firm FnGuide-linked profile source, Wisereport's company monitor, cites growth in SUVs, the rise of eco-friendly vehicles and advances in autonomous driving technology as drivers of sustained growth in the auto sector.

That view is a qualitative assessment of industry structure and not a forecast of the company's volumes or sales growth. Slower sales or model discontinuations at automakers can directly affect body parts volumes, so the slight decline in fiscal 2026 revenue should be read alongside that trend.

Body parts are supplied against each customer's model-by-model production plans, so new model awards and output on existing models shape the direction of results.

In competition, Sungwoo Hitech, Hyundai WIA, Seoyon E-Hwa, Daewonkangup and Myoung Shin Industry are classified as listed peers in the same automotive parts industry, but this report does not compare their revenue or margins with Sewon Precision's figures.

Measured against the industry's average PER, Sewon Precision's multiple sits at a low level, which makes how the market values earnings durability and growth a key variable for its position within the sector.

The cycle position of the end market cannot be determined with confidence on the available data, and automaker sales data and company disclosures should be checked together.

Bull factors

Margin recovery

The operating margin for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was 13.3%, up from 9.5% in the fiscal year ended June 2025, and operating profit rose 38.7% to KRW 23.6 billion. Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 11.3 billion was up 38.8% year on year and marked a turn from the Q1 operating loss. It reads as a case of profit growth despite flat revenue.

Financial headroom

Total liabilities at the end of the fiscal year ended June 2026 were KRW 79.7 billion, with a debt ratio of 9.9%. With a light borrowing burden, the room for facility investment or R&D is relatively wide. How and how much of that capacity is used needs to be checked in disclosures.

Profitability held since fiscal 2024

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 55.4 billion in fiscal 2024, then came in at KRW 46.5 billion in fiscal 2025 and KRW 49.6 billion in fiscal 2026. Q2 2026 profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 23.6 billion was up 54.1% year on year. All three fiscal years since 2024 were profitable, which points to the direction of earnings capacity.

Bear factors

Flat revenue

Revenue for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was KRW 178 billion, down 0.8%, and Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 58.5 billion fell 3.5% year on year. Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 39.2 billion dropped 35.4% from the prior quarter, which shows wide quarterly swings. Without revenue growth, margin gains rely more heavily on cost and expense control.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Q1 2026 swung to an operating loss of KRW 670 million, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders dropped to KRW 1.76 billion. Operating profit for the fiscal year ended June 2025 fell 11.4%, so earnings did not rise steadily each year.

The last four quarters total KRW 49.6 billion in profit attributable to controlling shareholders, but quarter-to-quarter swings are large.

Cash flow and subsidiary reliance

Operating cash flow for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was KRW 34.8 billion, down from KRW 60.4 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025. Subsidiary profit improvement, cited as the main driver of higher operating profit in the preliminary results, means part of the gain depends on subsidiary performance. If subsidiary profits reverse, consolidated earnings could be affected.

Risk factors

Customer concentration

Sewon Precision supplies body parts to Hyundai Motor and its affiliates, so it is directly exposed to automaker sales volumes and export performance. Production plans or weak sales for models within that group can pass quickly into revenue and utilization.

Customer-level revenue shares were not found in the disclosures reviewed, so concentration cannot be quantified.

Subsidiaries and overseas operations

With 12 domestic affiliates and a Chinese subsidiary in the consolidation scope, consolidated results move with the combined profit of subsidiaries.

Because higher operating profit was attributed to subsidiary improvement in the September 2026 preliminary results, subsidiary swings should be assessed apart from core business results. Detailed figures on the Chinese unit's operating environment or cost burden were not found.

Disclosure figures and governance

Preliminary net profit was reported in the 560 billion-won range, while the regular report shows KRW 55.3 billion, so differences can arise between preliminary and final figures. An independent director resigned in July 2026, changing the board, and whether a successor will be appointed needs confirmation. This report uses the final figures from the regular reports.

Overall view

Sewon Precision's reported results show flat revenue and margin improvement at the same time. Revenue for the fiscal year ended June 2026 slipped slightly to KRW 178 billion, but operating profit rose to KRW 23.6 billion and the operating margin of 13.3% was the highest of the four fiscal years.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders has stayed positive since fiscal 2024, which points to a stronger earnings base. On the other side, the Q1 2026 operating loss, the year-on-year sales decline in Q2 and the lower operating cash flow raise questions about durability.

The subsidiary-driven profit gain is both evidence of recovery and a sign of reliance on subsidiary results. Margin recovery and flat sales are weighted equally here, so this report does not reach a single directional conclusion.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin and subsidiary breakdown in the Q3 2026 quarterly report and whether revenue resumes growth. This report does not offer an investment opinion or target price and is provided for information only.
