02

Business structure

Ecopro BM is a battery cathode materials company that develops and produces everything from precursors to cathode active materials, with high-nickel NCM cathodes as its core product. Its products fall into EV, power application and energy storage system (ESS) segments.

According to a Digital Daily report on the Q3 2026 results announced on November 5, 2026, Q3 EV-related sales were KRW 256.1 billion, ESS-related sales were KRW 123.6 billion and power application sales were KRW 169.7 billion.

Power application sales were supported by demand from power tools and the expansion of semiconductor plants and data centers. EV-related sales were squeezed by a slowdown in the North American EV market and inventory adjustments at European automakers.

Customers are domestic and overseas battery makers and automakers, and the weight of European customer volumes amplifies earnings swings. On the production side, the Hungarian plant with 54,000 tons of capacity was completed and was preparing for mass production, according to the company's April 2026 conference call.

The materials lineup is being widened into LFP cathodes (a 4,000-ton line is in place), cathodes for sulfide solid electrolytes and lithium manganese rich (LMR) cathodes. It is compared with fellow domestic cathode makers POSCO Future M and L&F.

In Q3, POSCO Future M's operating profit rose on customer compensation, while L&F posted a loss due to cost pressure.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results have followed a clear arc, peaking in 2023, falling through 2025 and then recovering in profit terms.

Revenue went from KRW 5.36 trillion in 2022 to KRW 6.9 trillion in 2023 (up 28.8% year on year), the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span, before falling to KRW 2.77 trillion in 2024 (down 59.9%) and KRW 2.53 trillion in 2025 (down 8.5%), the smallest year.

Operating profit fell from KRW 380.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 156 billion in 2023 (down 59.0%), swung to an operating loss of KRW 34.1 billion in 2024, and returned to operating profit of KRW 143.3 billion in 2025. The operating margin was 7.1% in 2022, 2.3% in 2023, -1.2% in 2024 and 5.7% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 232.3 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 8.73 billion in 2023 and a net loss of KRW 96.5 billion in 2024, before turning to a profit of KRW 39.4 billion in 2025.

Total net income, which includes non-controlling interests, was KRW 91.6 billion in 2025 and is a separate figure from controlling-shareholder profit. The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 142.2% at the end of 2025 from 118.7% in 2024, with total liabilities of KRW 2.87 trillion and total equity of KRW 2.02 trillion.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 670.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 214.7 billion in 2025. On a quarterly basis, operating profit eased from KRW 50.5 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 41.6 billion in Q4 2025 (down 17.7%) on revenue of KRW 497 billion.

Q4 2025 included a one-off benefit of KRW 31.8 billion from a change in the useful life of fixed assets, and Q1 2026 operating profit of KRW 20.9 billion was reported against that base.

Operating profit then came in at KRW 18 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 12 billion in Q3 2026, and according to a Newsway report the Q3 decline reflected the base effect of one-off gains booked a year earlier.

Q3 2026 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 9.75 billion marks a swing into loss year on year, and the four-quarter total of controlling-shareholder profit from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 is KRW 11 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Downstream demand is split between EVs and ESS. A April 2026 report from KB Securities attributed weaker North American EV shipments to the end of the Inflation Reduction Act, while European automakers' inventory adjustments cut Q3 2026 EV-related sales by 34% quarter on quarter.

ESS demand rose with AI data center investment, and ESS-related cathode sales grew 173% quarter on quarter in Q3 2026. According to a Newsway report, however, that figure was still down 25% from a year earlier, which suggests ESS cannot offset the EV weakness in the short term.

An Econovill article described non-EV applications as becoming a new revenue pillar as EV demand slows. On local production, IBK Securities said in an April 2026 report that Ecopro BM is the only domestic cathode maker with a European plant, which it saw as a positive.

The same broker said in an August 2026 report that rising adoption of Chinese batteries by European automakers could reduce second-half cathode shipments. Peer POSCO Future M's operating profit rose in Q3 2026 on customer compensation, while L&F posted a loss on cost pressure.

All three domestic cathode makers are seeing earnings swing sharply on one-off items. The cycle reads as a transition, with EV-led growth slowing and ESS and non-EV uses becoming the larger revenue base.

06

Outlook

On its Q3 2026 earnings call, the company said talks to finalize a supply contract worth several trillion won with a global automaker were in their final stage. It did not disclose the customer or the volume. Digital Daily reported that a large supply contract with a non-Chinese battery maker is also being pursued.

The company expects shipments to recover from 2027 as it adds new customers and expands local production in Europe. For LFP cathodes, it is finishing supply plans for a 4,000-ton line and is reviewing a separate new line of up to 50,000 tons, built as a standalone line rather than by converting the existing NCM line.

The sulfide solid electrolyte cathode has passed customer quality verification, with mass production targeted for 2027. Cathodes for all-solid-state batteries and LMR cathodes are also in cell-level validation with customers.

The company has said profitability could be pressured by the lower won-dollar exchange rate in the second half and costs related to the Hungarian plant.

A DS Investment report from August 2026, as covered by Dealsite, noted that the effective stake in the Indonesian nickel smelter BNSI and the size of any rights offering to fund it were not yet fixed.

Three points to track are whether the multi-trillion-won contract is signed, whether the LFP expansion is approved and how fast Hungarian plant shipments recover.

08

Bull factors

Rising ESS demand

As AI data center investment grows, ESS cathode sales reached KRW 123.6 billion in Q3 2026, up 173% quarter on quarter. The company also reported a 140% year-on-year rise in ESS cathode sales in Q1 2026. Because the demand comes from power infrastructure rather than EV sales, it is cited as a diversification argument.

Local production in Europe

The completed Hungarian plant, with 54,000 tons of capacity, gives the company a local supply base in Europe. IBK Securities said in an April 2026 report that rising demand for local production in Europe favors the one domestic cathode maker with a European plant. It also said the customer base could widen to Chinese and European battery makers.

Order and product pipeline

The company has described final-stage talks on a multi-trillion-won supply contract with a global automaker and pursuit of a large deal with a non-Chinese battery maker. A possible LFP expansion of up to 50,000 tons and sulfide solid electrolyte mass production targeted for 2027 are also cited as growth drivers. Neither the contracts nor the expansion decision has been finalized.

09

Bear factors

Weak EV sales

EV-related cathode sales fell 34% quarter on quarter to KRW 256.1 billion in Q3 2026. That reflects a slowdown in the North American EV market and inventory adjustments at European automakers.

IBK Securities said in an August 2026 report that more Chinese battery adoption by European automakers could reduce second-half shipments.

Pressure on profitability

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 12 billion, down 76.2% year on year, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 9.75 billion. Losses have continued for two straight quarters from Q2 2026.

The company cited a weaker exchange rate in the second half and Hungarian plant costs as profit pressures, and analysts pointed to initial fixed costs at the plant.

Balance sheet and funding needs

The debt-to-equity ratio was 142.2% at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 2.87 trillion. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 214.7 billion in 2025 from KRW 670.1 billion in 2024.

DS Investment noted that BNSI's effective stake and the size of any rights offering must be fixed before its profit contribution can be estimated.

10

Risk factors

Raw materials and FX

Cathode selling prices move with metal prices and the exchange rate. Average selling prices rose 2% in Q1 2026 on metal and FX effects, but the company has flagged a weaker exchange rate in the second half as a profit headwind. Swings in nickel and lithium prices can widen margin volatility.

Customer and regional concentration

Volumes from European automakers and North American EV customers drive results, so demand swings create large earnings volatility. EV-related sales fell 34% quarter on quarter in Q3 2026. A longer inventory correction or wider adoption of Chinese batteries in Europe could cut second-half shipments further.

Investment execution and funding

Several large projects are running at once, including the Hungarian plant, a new LFP line and the Indonesian nickel smelter. With the debt-to-equity ratio at 142.2% at the end of 2025 and the BNSI stake and rights offering size not yet set, funding is a concern. Newsway reported that new businesses will take time to contribute revenue and cost savings.

Overall view

Ecopro BM's revenue peaked in 2023 and fell through 2025, while operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders returned to the black in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit has since declined, from KRW 20.9 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 18 billion in Q2 and KRW 12 billion in Q3, and Q3 swung to a net loss of KRW 9.75 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

The Q3 weakness came from lower EV-related cathode sales, with ESS and power application sales partly offsetting it. Bull points are rising ESS demand, local supply from the Hungarian plant, talks on a multi-trillion-won supply contract and the LFP and solid electrolyte pipeline.

Bear points are weak EV demand, inventory adjustments in Europe, possible second-half shipment declines from wider adoption of Chinese batteries, lower profitability with high fixed costs, a 142.2% debt-to-equity ratio and possible rights offering.

Target prices in an August 2026 report ranged from KRW 100,000 at iM Securities to KRW 250,000 at DS Investment, and six brokers were reported to have lowered their targets. The share price sits at a premium to net asset value, so the pace of the earnings recovery is central to the valuation debate.

This report is provided for information only and contains no buy or sell opinion. The next checkpoints are whether the multi-trillion-won contract is signed, the 2026 annual report and Q1 2027 preliminary results.
