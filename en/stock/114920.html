02

Business structure

Daejoo Energy INnovation Technology is a steel pipe manufacturer founded in 1989 that produces insulated twin pipes used in district heating and elevator guide rails.

The company bids on projects as a turnkey provider covering design, manufacturing and construction of insulated twin pipes, and supplies customers including Korea District Heating Corporation.

Segment mix differs by source: a Saramin compilation citing NICE Credit Rating data shows insulated twin pipes at 63.5% and elevator guide rails at 34.8%, while JobKorea's business analysis shows guide rails at 96% and twin pipes at 4%.

The gap likely reflects different aggregation bases and dates, and a filing-based segment breakdown was not available. A 2015 securities research report put the company's domestic insulated twin pipe market share at 45% and described an oligopolistic position in elevator guide rails with more than 60% share.

Guide rails are produced at the Hwaseong plant at roughly 11,000 tons per year and supplied to elevator makers such as Hyundai Elevator. Its project work depends on competitive bidding, and an older press report cited this structure as a constraint on profitability.

Overseas, the company set up a branch in Mozambique in 2012 and took part in a Korea Gas Corporation city gas project there as a construction contractor.

It is classified under chemicals on the exchange, but its credit-based industry is steel pipe manufacturing, so its products and customers differ sharply from large chemical peers such as LG Chem and Lotte Chemical. Public filings also list it as an affiliate of the Daejoo Heavy Industries group.

Recent trends

On the fiscal 2025 business report, revenue was 60,726,767 thousand won (KRW 60,726.767 million, or 60.7 billion won), down 29.03% year on year. Operating profit was 3,756,786 thousand won (KRW 3.76 billion), down 58.18%, and net income was 1,122,015 thousand won (KRW 1.12 billion), down 59.78%.

For fiscal 2024, revenue was 85,566,277 thousand won, operating profit was 8,982,253 thousand won and net income was 2,789,517 thousand won. The operating margin declined from 10.50% in 2024 to 6.19% in 2025. Operating cash flow fell from roughly 16.2 billion won in 2024 to 1,363,361 thousand won in 2025.

As of 2025, the debt ratio was 103.20% and the equity ratio was 49.21%. On the disclosure side, the company filed a voluntary disclosure on a delayed audit report and an extension request for its business report on March 23, 2026.

At the March 31, 2026 annual shareholders meeting, four agenda items including the 36th fiscal year financial statements were approved, and a consolidated net loss was reported alongside them. On the same day, the share price closed at 2,410 won, down 14.39% from the prior session.

Current price is 2,695 won (-1.46%) with a market capitalization of 94 billion won. NICE Credit Rating's 2025 scores place profitability at 66 and stability at 68 in the upper tier, and growth at 12 in the lowest tier.
