Report overview

Sales Fall for a Third Year, Profit Holds

Daejoo Energy INnovation Technology, a KONEX-listed maker of pre-insulated pipes and elevator guide rails, stayed operating-profitable while its sales kept shrinking.

  1. 1

    Sales for 2025 were KRW 60.72 billion, the third consecutive annual decline since 2023.

  2. 2

    The operating margin eased from 10.50% in 2024 to 6.19% in 2025.

  3. 3

    Cash flow from operating activities dropped sharply, from about KRW 16.2 billion in 2024 to about KRW 1.36 billion in 2025.

  4. 4

    The exchange label is chemicals, yet the core products are pre-insulated pipes and guide rails, which calls for care in peer comparisons.

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Daejoo Energy INnovation Technology was founded in May 1989, and its business lines are pre-insulated pipes, direct steam piping and elevator guide rails with related parts. The Gongju plant in Chungnam makes the pipes, and the Hwaseong plant in Gyeonggi-do makes the guide rails.

According to the company, it has supplied pre-insulated pipes to the Korea District Heating Corporation, the Ansan urban development corporation and the Seoul urban development corporation, among others. The company states that it was the first in Korea to introduce direct steam piping (SIS).

The company says the Hwaseong plant produces 11,000 tons of guide rails a year and supplies Hyundai Elevator, Thyssenkrupp Dongyang Elevator and Schindler, among others. Products are sold in the domestic market and also overseas, including America, Europe and Australia.

The revenue split by segment could not be confirmed in this research. A 2015 disclosure put its pre-insulated pipe share at 46% at the end of 2014, about twice that of the runner-up, Shin-E Steel. That figure is more than ten years old and should not be read as the current share.

An earlier interview described the business as winning orders through competitive bidding, which limits profitability. The exchange classifies the company under chemicals, but the actual business is steel-pipe-based piping and elevator components. Daejoo Electronic Materials and Daejoo, which have similar names, are separate listed companies.

03

Recent trends

The annual report for the year ended December 2025 is dated March 31, 2026. On that basis, sales for 2025 were KRW 60.72 billion, down 29% from the prior year. Sales growth was -9.67% in 2023, -20.40% in 2024 and -29.03% in 2025, so the pace of decline accelerated each year.

Operating profit was KRW 3.75 billion in 2025, down by nearly 58% from KRW 8.98 billion in 2024. The operating margin was 7.94% in 2023, 10.50% in 2024 and 6.19% in 2025.

In 2025, non-operating income was about KRW 2.9 billion, non-operating expense was about KRW 5.8 billion, and pre-tax profit was about KRW 0.86 billion. Income tax was a negative amount, so net income came to KRW 1.12 billion, down by nearly 60% from KRW 2.79 billion in 2024.

At the end of 2025, total assets were about KRW 74.1 billion, the debt ratio was 103.20% and the equity ratio was 49.21%. Current assets of about KRW 27.1 billion were below current liabilities of about KRW 34.2 billion, and the quick ratio was 64.97%.

Cash flow from operating activities fell from about KRW 16.2 billion in 2024 to about KRW 1.36 billion in 2025. Quarterly and half-year results for 2026 could not be confirmed in this research.

The share price is KRW 2,895 (+0.35% day over day), and the market cap is KRW 10.0 billion. As of September 18, 2026, Morningstar showed a 52-week range of KRW 1,785 to 3,850 and 3.47 million shares outstanding. The same page showed volume of 1 share against an average of 310 shares, which points to very thin trading.

04

Outlook

No confirmed 2026 earnings figures were found, so the quarterly and half-year reports filed from here are the first items to check. The points to watch are whether the sales decline has stopped, whether the operating margin has room to return toward the 10.50% of 2024, and whether operating cash flow recovers.

Pre-insulated pipes depend on public and infrastructure orders such as district heating, while guide rails depend on installation and replacement demand from elevator makers, so construction activity and order timing are the key variables.

On December 9, 2024, the KS M 3420 standard for buried pre-insulated pipe assemblies for district heating was established, which put product requirements in writing. The effect of this standard on any single company's share or profitability has not been confirmed.

Global Market Insights estimates that the global district heating market will grow from USD 187 billion in 2024 to USD 308.3 billion by 2034. Mordor Intelligence, by contrast, expects annual growth of 1.47% from 2026 to 2031. The forecasts differ widely, and neither covers the Korean pre-insulated pipe market directly.

The International Energy Agency's analysis notes that district heating still depends heavily on fossil fuels, and that large heat pumps and electric boilers are gaining share in Europe. How a shift in heat sources would affect domestic piping demand is uncertain.

The current ratio and the debt burden also deserve attention, and the repayment schedule of short-term borrowings and the pace of cash-flow recovery are central to financial stability.

05

Bull factors

Two core lines and a long supply record

The company describes itself as having the longest history among domestic pre-insulated pipe makers. It says its guide rails are supplied to major elevator makers in Korea. Pipes and elevator parts have different customer bases, which can partly spread the dependence on any single source of demand.

The company also designs, manufactures and installs direct steam piping (SIS), so it can offer products and construction work together.

Operating profit sustained during the decline

The company stayed profitable in 2025, with operating profit of KRW 3.75 billion and net income of KRW 1.12 billion. The 6.19% operating margin is below the 7.94% of 2023, but the company remained in the black. The equity ratio of 49.21% is close to the 50.60% of 2024. The operating margin is a business metric that could recover if sales scale and the cost structure improve.

Market cap small against book equity

Total equity at the end of 2025 was about KRW 36.5 billion. The market cap of KRW 10.0 billion is less than one third of that amount. Whether the gap reflects the market pricing in risk and limited liquidity, or serves as a basis for a price view, differs by investor. This report presents the fact only and offers no view on direction.

06

Bear factors

Three straight years of lower sales

Sales fell from about KRW 107.5 billion in 2023 to about KRW 60.7 billion in 2025. The rate of decline widened from 9.67% in 2023 to 20.40% in 2024 and 29.03% in 2025. The specific causes of the decline could not be confirmed in this research. A continued shrinking of scale can raise the fixed-cost burden and weigh on the operating margin.

Weaker cash flow and liquidity

Operating cash flow fell from about KRW 16.2 billion in 2024 to about KRW 1.36 billion in 2025. Current liabilities exceed current assets, and the quick ratio is 64.97%. The interest coverage indicator declined from 5.05 in 2024 to 2.89 in 2025, and borrowings dependence is 36.80%.

Total equity also fell from about KRW 42.2 billion at the end of 2024 to about KRW 36.5 billion at the end of 2025 despite positive net income, but the reason could not be confirmed.

Bid-driven model and non-operating drag

An earlier interview described pre-insulated pipes and similar lines as won through competitive bidding, which limits profitability. In 2025, non-operating expense of about KRW 5.8 billion far exceeded non-operating income of about KRW 2.9 billion.

As a result, operating profit of KRW 3.75 billion shrank to pre-tax profit of about KRW 0.86 billion. Net income was affected by a tax reversal, so the quality of earnings deserves separate scrutiny.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Pre-insulated pipes and guide rails are tied to construction and infrastructure investment and to elevator installation demand. A slowdown in construction or delayed public orders could prolong the sales decline. A high-rate environment raises the interest burden for a company with meaningful borrowings.

Raw material price swings may affect margins depending on contract terms, but the specific sensitivity could not be confirmed.

Disclosure and regulatory

The company was indicted after disclosing financial statements that overstated construction revenue and receivables for three years from 2016. The fine imposed at that time was KRW 1.526 billion on the company and KRW 152.6 million on its chief executive.

Prosecutors sought a KRW 15 million fine, and the company said there was no impact on its financial statements. The final outcome of this case could not be confirmed, and accounting reliability remains an item for investors to monitor.

Market

As a KONEX stock, its average volume was only 310 shares as of September 18, 2026, so executing trades can be difficult. With thin trading, even small orders can move the price sharply. The 52-week range is wide, from KRW 1,785 to KRW 3,850.

Because the exchange classifies the company under chemicals, direct comparison with chemical-sector indicators can be misleading.

08

Overall view

Daejoo Energy INnovation Technology is a small manufacturer built on pre-insulated pipes and elevator guide rails, and its exchange classification differs from its actual business.

On the 2025 annual report, sales fell for a third consecutive year and the operating margin eased, yet operating profit and net income stayed positive. At the same time, operating cash flow fell sharply and current liabilities exceed current assets, so the financial position needs monitoring.

It is a fact that the market cap is below total equity at the end of 2025, but thin KONEX trading and the past accounting violation should be weighed alongside it.

Quarterly and half-year results for 2026 were not confirmed, so the sales trend, operating margin and cash flow in upcoming filings are the first things to check. This report is for information only, and investment decisions and their consequences rest with the investor.
