02

Business structure

TAJOY entertainment is an integrated planning and production company that houses a management team, a concert planning and production team, and an overseas concert operations team under one roof. Concert planning generated KRW 4,974 million in 2024, or 37.63% of total revenue, according to the 2024 business report.

The 2023 competition table in the business report split revenue into entertainer broadcast and concert income at 46% and domestic and overseas concert planning income at 54%. Its roster spans trot singer PARK SEO-JIN and KIM HYE-YEON as well as the four-member girl group I-CHILIN, covering a broad range of genres.

Press coverage from July 2026 reports that the company is expanding into short-form content production and distribution. Through its subsidiary Blue Brown Records, it launched the five-member girl group HEART OF WOMAN and entered idol production in earnest.

In regional events, it produced the Powerful K-Trot Festival at Daegu Stadium in 2023 and has built a track record of events in the Daegu and Gyeongbuk area. In April 2026 it signed a performance agreement with Maeil Shinmun, securing a regional media partner in Daegu and Gyeongbuk.

Compared with large listed agencies, its scale is small. Peer agencies in the same competition table also posted operating losses in 2023, which points to wide profitability dispersion across the sector.

Recent trends

Under the 2025 business report, revenue reached KRW 17,737 million, up 34.19% from the prior year. Operating profit fell 14.09% to KRW 1,118 million over the same period, and the operating margin slid from 9.85% in 2024 to 6.31%. The gross margin also declined to 16.51% in 2024 from 26.20% a year earlier.

Net income rose 4.74% to KRW 1,149 million, largely because income tax expense fell to KRW 10.87 million from KRW 189.5 million in 2024. Non-operating income increased to KRW 126 million, which supported net income.

Total assets at end-2025 rose 89.55% to about KRW 9,661 million, while total liabilities grew to about KRW 5,714 million, lifting the debt ratio to 144.77%. At end-2024, advances received of KRW 743 million and accrued expenses of KRW 1,027 million had increased sharply.

Operating cash flow swung to a deficit of about KRW 1,100 million, and investing cash flow recorded a net outflow of about KRW 2,494 million. Market capitalization fell from about KRW 25 billion on October 30, 2024 to about KRW 16 billion on August 28, 2025.

In a quote dated September 16, 2026, the share price was KRW 16,510, with a 52-week range of KRW 14,020 to KRW 23,900. As of November 11, 2026, the current price was KRW 15,100 and trading value was zero.

Outlook

On the business side, the key question is how far short-form content and idol production can broaden the revenue mix. Whether the girl group activities of Blue Brown Records become a durable revenue source will show up in the 2026 results.

Another key indicator is whether the decline in operating margin from 13.53% in 2023 to 6.31% in 2025 stops. Operating cash flow returning to a surplus, and the nature and settlement timing of advances received and accrued expenses, are also points to track.

The end-2024 balance sheet carried KRW 500 million of convertible bonds, and their subsequent redemption or conversion will be visible in disclosures.

The business report filed on March 18, 2026 was disclosed as subject to a correction request from the Korea Exchange, so the interpretation of the figures may change depending on the correction filing. Dependence of management revenue on artist activity and contracts also remains central.

On trading, the three-month average volume was shown at 65 shares, so price formation may be shaped by very thin trades.

Bull factors

Three Straight Years of Revenue Growth

Revenue grew from KRW 11,579 million in 2023 to KRW 13,218 million in 2024 and KRW 17,737 million in 2025, rising for three consecutive years. The 34.19% increase in 2025 outpaced the 14.16% gain in 2024.

Press reports indicate the company is extending into short-form content production and distribution, which could widen its growth base beyond concerts.

Broadening Business Portfolio

Through Blue Brown Records, the company debuted the group HEART OF WOMAN and entered idol production. It runs trot artists alongside I-CHILIN, pursuing genre diversification. The April 2026 concert agreement with Maeil Shinmun was reported as securing a regional media partner in Daegu and Gyeongbuk.

Low Borrowing Dependence

Borrowings dependence stood at 4.80% in 2025, a low level. Total equity rose from KRW 2,798 million at end-2024 to KRW 3,947 million at end-2025. The company stayed profitable in 2025 with net income of KRW 1,149 million.

06

Bear factors

Profitability Declining for Three Years

Operating profit declined for three consecutive years, from KRW 1,566 million in 2023 to KRW 1,302 million in 2024 and KRW 1,118 million in 2025. The operating margin fell from 13.53% in 2023 to 6.31% in 2025.

Even with rising sales, the gross margin dropped to 16.51% in 2024, so revenue growth has not translated into profit.

Swing to Operating Cash Deficit

Operating cash flow moved from a surplus of about KRW 1,489 million in 2023 to KRW 438 million in 2024 and a deficit of about KRW 1,100 million in 2025. Cash operating profit was also negative at roughly KRW 1,200 million in 2025. Investing activities recorded a net outflow of about KRW 2,494 million in 2025.

Rising Leverage and Filing Correction Risk

The debt ratio climbed from 38.62% in 2023 to 82.17% in 2024 and 144.77% in 2025. The business report filed on March 18, 2026 was disclosed as subject to a Korea Exchange correction request. Trading value was zero and the three-month average volume was shown at 65 shares, indicating limited liquidity.

Risk factors

Macro

Demand for concert planning may shift with domestic consumer conditions and advertising and event spending. With concert planning at 37.63% of 2024 revenue, the company is exposed to economic fluctuations. Regional event contracts may also depend on local government budget execution.

Artist Concentration

Management revenue depends heavily on the activities and contracts of specific artists such as PARK SEO-JIN. A slowdown in activity or contract expiry could increase revenue volatility. Whether the new girl groups achieve commercial success remains an open question for the business.

Liquidity and Disclosure

As a KONEX listing, the stock recorded zero trading value and very low average volume. The correction request noted for the March 2026 business report affects how the financial figures should be read.

The end-2024 balance sheet carried KRW 500 million of convertible bonds, whose treatment must be tracked through disclosures.

Overall view

TAJOY entertainment's revenue rose 34.19% in 2025, so its top-line expansion continued, but operating profit and operating cash flow weakened, making earnings quality the central issue.

Bearish factors are the operating margin decline to 6.31%, the swing to an operating cash deficit, and the debt ratio rise to 144.77%.

The March 18, 2026 business report was disclosed as subject to a Korea Exchange correction request, so the final confirmation of the figures should be tracked as well. As of November 11, 2026, the current price was KRW 15,100 and trading value was zero, leaving liquidity very limited.
