02

Business structure

TAJOY entertainment started in Mapo, Seoul in 2011 and has run celebrity management together with performance planning and direction. It renamed itself in 2023 and listed on the KONEX market in October 2024. The company writes its English trade name as TAJOY entertainment co.,LTD.

As of December 2025, revenue breaks down into 92% from planning and running performances for other agencies' singers and corporate-hosted events, and 8% from entertainment management, album production and merchandise.

In other words, performance-planning services make up most of revenue, rather than earnings from its own artists. The organization is an integrated planning-and-production setup with a management team, a performance planning and production team, and an overseas performance team.

Artists who are or have been signed include singers Kim Hye-yeon, Wink and Hardy, actress Hong Soo-ah, and comedians Jung Tae-ho and Lee Hyun-jung, and the company has recently expanded into short-form content production and distribution.

Trot singer Park Seo-jin left the roster when his exclusive contract ended on January 31, 2025.

In a shareholder meeting notice the company described the industry as people-driven, saying artist acquisition and development matter for entertainment and acoustic technology and planning expertise matter for performance planning.

Large agencies such as Hybe, SM Entertainment, JYP Entertainment and YG Entertainment build intellectual property on recordings and global fan bases, whereas this company is a small organization with a reported headcount of roughly 13 to 18.

In 2026 it entered idol production by launching the five-member girl group HEART OF WOMAN through its subsidiary.

03

Recent trends

Revenue was KRW 17.737 billion in 2025, up 34.19%, following KRW 13.218 billion in 2024 (up 14.16%) and KRW 11.579 billion in 2023, so it has grown for three straight years. Operating profit moved the other way.

It was about KRW 1.566 billion in 2023, KRW 1.302 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.118 billion in 2025, falling each year. The operating margin declined from 13.53% to 9.85% and then to 6.31%. Gross profit in 2025 was about KRW 2.261 billion, close to KRW 2.183 billion in 2024.

Dividing by revenue gives a simple gross margin of about 26% in 2023, 17% in 2024 and 13% in 2025, so the added revenue did not translate fully into profit. Net income in 2025 rose 4.74% to about KRW 1.149 billion, helped by income tax expense falling from about KRW 190 million to about KRW 11 million.

In the same year cash flow from ordinary activities was a net outflow of about KRW 1.102 billion and investing cash flow a net outflow of about KRW 2.494 billion.

At the end of December 2025, total assets were about KRW 9.661 billion, liabilities were entirely current at about KRW 5.714 billion, the debt ratio was 144.77% and the quick ratio was 147.85%. The share closed at KRW 16,510 on September 16, 2026, versus KRW 15,100 on October 27, 2026, with zero trading value.

The KRW 15.1 billion market capitalization works out simply to about 13 times 2025 net income and about 3.8 times year-end 2025 equity of about KRW 3.947 billion. Interim 2026 results could not be confirmed in this research, so the latest figures should be checked in exchange filings.

04

Outlook

The path ahead depends largely on how widely the company can broaden its base of performance-planning clients.

In November 2025 it handled performance planning for the Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance Healing Festa, and on April 25, 2026 it signed a performance cooperation memorandum with a leading Daegu and Gyeongbuk daily newspaper. Press coverage noted that the company had faced the task of diversifying revenue since listing.

The second pillar is the girl group HEART OF WOMAN. Idol production generally requires spending on debut preparation and promotion before revenue arrives, so the cost burden in the early period deserves attention.

The company says it intends to build on its staging skills and management experience to widen performance opportunities outside Seoul. On business metrics, the key question is whether gross and operating margins can move back toward their 2023 levels.

Other items to watch are whether operating cash generation catches up with reported profit and how the current-liability-heavy balance sheet changes. Interim 2026 results and the revenue contribution of the new businesses could not be confirmed, so no quantitative forecast is given.

05

Bull factors

Three straight years of revenue growth

Revenue rose from about KRW 11.579 billion in 2023 to about KRW 17.737 billion in 2025. The 2025 growth rate of 34.19% is higher than the 14.16% of 2024. Because most revenue is performance-planning services, growth rests on project orders and not only on one singer's activity. Reaching this scale with a small organization can be read as evidence of planning and staging capability.

Widening client and regional ties

The company has staged leading regional festivals in Daegu and Gyeongbuk, including a trot festival and a powerful song contest. It handled performance planning for the Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance Healing Festa in both November 2024 and November 2025.

In April 2026 it also signed a performance cooperation memorandum with a regional daily. Accumulated repeat orders and partnership channels may help reduce order volatility.

Low borrowing dependence

As of 2025, borrowing dependence was 4.80%, the quick ratio 147.85% and return on equity 34.08%. Liabilities appear to be mostly operating-related current items, so the interest burden is modest. The interest coverage ratio is also reported at a high level. These ratios partly reflect the small equity base, so they should be interpreted with care.

06

Bear factors

Revenue up, profit down

Operating profit fell 14.09% in 2025 and the operating margin slipped to 6.31%. With gross profit nearly flat while revenue grew, the simple gross margin fell from about 26% in 2023 to about 13% in 2025.

Performance-planning services may carry heavy outsourcing costs in cost of sales, but the cost breakdown could not be confirmed. The issue is whether growth that does not reach profit will continue.

Artist departure and key-person reliance

The exclusive contract with Park Seo-jin ended on January 31, 2025 without renewal. The company itself states that human resources play the central role in this industry. A change in the artist roster can unsettle management revenue.

Management accounts for only 8% of revenue as of December 2025, which also signals that proprietary artist intellectual property is thin.

Cash outflows and current-liability concentration

In 2025 cash flow from ordinary activities was a net outflow of about KRW 1.102 billion and investing cash flow a net outflow of about KRW 2.494 billion. Liabilities of about KRW 5.714 billion are entirely current and the debt ratio is 144.77%.

The reason cash flowed out despite reported profit could not be confirmed in this research. If working-capital needs or new-business investment grow, financial flexibility could be tested.

Risk factors

Macro

Because 92% of revenue is performance planning and operation, the company is exposed to corporate event budgets and regional festival budgets. A slowdown that trims corporate marketing or local-government event spending could weaken the order environment.

Performance demand is also sensitive to external factors such as weather, safety and public health. The actual revenue impact of these factors could not be confirmed in this research.

Business

In 2026 the company entered idol production by launching the girl group HEART OF WOMAN. A new group requires upfront production spending with uncertain results and competes in the same market as large companies such as Hybe, JYP Entertainment and YG Entertainment.

As a small organization, results can swing with a few key people and contracts. The investment size and revenue contribution of the new business have not been confirmed.

Market and liquidity

Press reported that trading value on the first listing day was only about KRW 5 million, and trading value was again zero on October 27, 2026. With a market capitalization of KRW 15.1 billion and 1,000,000 shares outstanding, even small orders can move the price or may not execute at all. As is typical of KONEX, access to information and the ability to trade are limited.

08

Overall view

TAJOY entertainment is a small agency that has grown revenue for three straight years, centered on performance-planning services. Over the same period, however, operating profit declined and the operating margin fell from 13.53% to 6.31%.

On the bullish side are revenue growth, a widening base of performance clients and regional partners, and low reliance on borrowing. On the bearish side are the margin decline, the experience of losing a key artist, and the 2025 cash outflows together with a current-liability-heavy balance sheet.

The 2026 move into girl-group production is an attempt to diversify income but carries investment burden and uncertain results. The near absence of trading on KONEX is a condition that must be weighed separately in any assessment.
