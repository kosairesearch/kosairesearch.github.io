02

Business structure

Doosan is a holding company that operates its own businesses and holds stakes in major affiliates. The core of its standalone business is Electronics BG, which produces copper clad laminate (CCL) and other electronic component materials.

In its disclosure on November 9, 2026, Doosan reported Q3 standalone business revenue of KRW 892.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 237.9 billion.

Electronics BG accounted for KRW 802 billion of that revenue, a record quarterly level, and the company attributed the growth to higher sales of AI accelerator, memory and optical module related products.

Doosan Enerbility reported Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 4.45 trillion and operating profit of KRW 261.2 billion, with cumulative orders of KRW 9.999 trillion. Doosan Bobcat posted Q3 revenue of KRW 2.46 trillion and operating profit of KRW 167.4 billion, with North American sales up 15%.

Changes in the value of listed holdings such as Doosan Robotics have also been cited by brokers as part of the holding company valuation. Electronics BG's demand is linked to global AI data center investment, and brokers expect CCL supply shortages to persist for two to three years.

Hana Securities, however, pointed to concerns about share loss at a key customer as a factor behind the share price decline. Because most standalone revenue comes from Electronics BG, the holding company's earnings are highly concentrated in one business.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 17 trillion in 2022 to KRW 19.1 trillion in 2023 (up 12.6%), fell to KRW 18.1 trillion in 2024 (down 5.2%), and recovered to KRW 19.8 trillion in 2025 (up 9.1%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 1.13 trillion in 2022, KRW 1.44 trillion in 2023 (up 27.6%), KRW 1 trillion in 2024 (down 30.1%) and KRW 1.06 trillion in 2025 (up 5.9%). Operating margin moved from 6.6% in 2022 to 7.5% in 2023, then 5.5% in 2024 and 5.4% in 2025, so the larger 2025 revenue did not restore the 2023 margin.

Net income attributable to shareholders was a loss of KRW 696.4 billion in 2022, a loss of KRW 388.3 billion in 2023 and a loss of KRW 226.2 billion in 2024, with the losses narrowing each year, before turning to a profit of KRW 75.8 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 249.5 billion in 2025, well above the figure attributable to shareholders, and the gap reflects the share held by non-controlling interests.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 12.2 trillion, of which the portion attributable to shareholders was KRW 1.56 trillion, and total liabilities were KRW 20.7 trillion. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 307.8 billion in 2024 to KRW 981.9 billion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 231.3 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 275.2 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 340.8 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 489.9 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 459.4 billion in Q3 2026, peaking in Q2.

Net income attributable to shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 78.4 billion, down 35.2% from a year earlier, so the operating profit gain did not carry through to shareholder earnings in that quarter.

05

Industry analysis

The downstream driver of Doosan's recent results is global AI infrastructure investment, and the company attributed Electronics BG's growth to AI accelerator, memory and optical module related products.

In an October 2026 report, DB Financial Investment projected optical module sales to rise 933% year on year and saw continued supply shortages in CCL for semiconductor packaging, which it said could support further price increases.

In a November 10, 2026 report, Hana Securities described demand for high-value CCL as solid and said the industry remained firm. At the same time, concerns about slower AI investment pushed the share price down sharply in the same period.

In CCL competition, declines in peer share prices fed into the valuation Hana Securities applied to Doosan, and the firm pointed to concerns about share loss at a key customer.

Brokers reported that Doosan is expanding capacity so that output reaches about 2.7 times current levels by the end of 2029, to meet expected CCL shortages over the next two to three years.

Doosan Enerbility's order flow from gas-fired power plants and SMR related equipment reflects the power equipment cycle, while Doosan Bobcat depends on North American construction equipment demand.

The holding company therefore combines an AI materials cycle with power equipment and construction equipment cycles, and the key point to watch is whether the other cycles can offset any weakening in AI-related demand.

06

Outlook

In its Q3 2026 results, Doosan said growth would continue in Q4, led by Electronics BG, and that standalone business revenue for 2026 would exceed KRW 3.2 trillion. Hana Securities noted that utilization at the new Chuangshu plant in China is rising quickly and said Q4 revenue could exceed the company's guidance.

In an October 2026 report, DB Financial Investment expected Electronics BG's Q3 operating profit to rise sharply and said the company's active hedging limits the effect of exchange rate moves. DB also noted that Doosan is pursuing capacity expansion in two main areas, network boards and optical modules.

On shareholder returns, Doosan declared a quarterly dividend of KRW 1,000 per share, with payment scheduled for December 9, 2026. NH Investment & Securities, in an April 2026 report, cited the company's plan to cancel all treasury shares within the year, excluding those for employee compensation.

Three points are worth tracking. First, whether Electronics BG's Q4 revenue fills the standalone business outlook. Second, how quickly utilization rises at the expanded plants. Third, how fast Doosan Enerbility's cumulative orders turn into profit.

If any of these falls short of expectations, the quarterly volatility of net income attributable to shareholders could increase.

The price-to-book ratio sits at several times the book value attributable to shareholders, which means market expectations for business growth are a large part of the share price relative to asset value.

The price-to-earnings ratio has limited historical comparability, because net income attributable to shareholders was a loss from 2022 to 2024 before turning to a profit in 2025.

08

Bull factors

AI demand for Electronics BG materials

Electronics BG recorded KRW 802 billion in Q3 2026 revenue, a record quarterly level according to the company. Sales of AI accelerator, memory and optical module related products rose, and the company expects growth to continue in Q4.

Hana Securities said CCL supply shortages could persist for two to three years, and brokers reported capacity expansion to about 2.7 times current levels by the end of 2029.

Improving affiliate earnings

Doosan Enerbility posted Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 261.2 billion, up 90.6% year on year, with cumulative orders of KRW 9.999 trillion. Doosan Bobcat's operating profit rose 25.3% to KRW 167.4 billion in the same quarter, with North American sales up 15%. Gains at both affiliates show that the holding company's earnings do not rest on Electronics BG alone.

Expanding shareholder returns

Doosan declared a quarterly dividend of KRW 1,000 per share, with payment scheduled for December 9, 2026.

In an April 2026 report, NH Investment & Securities mentioned a treasury share cancellation plan; because cancellation would reduce the number of shares outstanding, the actual cancellation disclosure is the next point to check. The same report said the holding company discount could narrow gradually as shareholder returns expand.

09

Bear factors

Concerns over slower AI investment and share price correction

On November 10, 2026, Hana Securities cut its Doosan target price by 47.5%, from KRW 2.42 million to KRW 1.27 million. The firm cited concerns over slower AI investment, possible share loss at a key customer, and lower peer share prices in CCL. Press reports described a sharp share price correction over the same period.

Quarterly swings in net income attributable to shareholders

Net income attributable to shareholders swung from a KRW 147.6 billion loss in Q4 2025 to KRW 355.1 billion in Q2 2026, then fell to KRW 78.4 billion in Q3, down 35.2% from a year earlier. Operating profit fell 6.2% from KRW 489.9 billion in Q2 to KRW 459.4 billion in Q3, so quarterly volatility remains large.

Rising financial leverage

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 153.5% in 2024 to 169.1% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 20.7 trillion at the end of 2025. Of total equity of KRW 12.2 trillion, the portion attributable to shareholders was KRW 1.56 trillion, so a large part of equity belongs to non-controlling interests. Financial burden at the holding company level can shift with affiliate results and ownership structure.

10

Risk factors

Customer and demand concentration

Electronics BG revenue depends heavily on AI infrastructure spending. Hana Securities pointed to concerns about share loss at a key customer, and if demand slows when new capacity comes online, utilization recovery could be delayed. The high dependence on a few customers and product lines is the starting point for this risk.

Earnings volatility and capacity expansion costs

Expanding CCL capacity to roughly 2.7 times current levels by the end of 2029 requires substantial investment. Because net income attributable to shareholders changes sharply from quarter to quarter, any mismatch between investment timing and utilization gains could increase earnings swings.

Governance and policy variables

In an April 2026 report, NH Investment & Securities said stronger government governance policy could support a narrower holding company discount. If policy direction or planned treasury share cancellations do not proceed as expected, that effect could weaken.

In a holding company structure, changes in the value of affiliate stakes can affect the share price separately from reported earnings.

12

Overall view

Doosan had a sharp recovery in Q3 2026, with operating profit of KRW 459.4 billion, up 98.6% year on year, driven by AI related materials sales at Electronics BG. However, annual operating margin was 5.4% in 2025, below 7.5% in 2023, and net income attributable to shareholders moved sharply from quarter to quarter.

The debt-to-equity ratio also rose to 169.1% in 2025. Broker views are divided: DB Financial Investment raised its target to KRW 2 million in October 2026, while Hana Securities cut its target to KRW 1.27 million on November 10, 2026 but described the AI industry as solid.

Strengths include Electronics BG demand, improving affiliate earnings and expanding shareholder returns, while weaknesses include concerns over slower AI investment, customer concentration and earnings volatility.

The next checkpoints are the December 2026 dividend payment, the treasury share cancellation within the year, preliminary Q4 2026 results in early 2027, and the 2026 annual report due by late March 2027.
