02

Business structure

Daishin Value REIT is a listed office REIT whose core asset is Daishin 343, the headquarters building of Daishin Financial Group located in Euljiro, Jung-gu, Seoul.

Structurally, it is a parent-child REIT in which Daishin Value REIT (the parent REIT) holds the entire equity of a private sub-REIT, which in turn directly owns Daishin 343.

The building operates under a triple-net master lease in which Daishin Financial Group affiliates lease the entire floor area for up to ten years and bear operating costs such as management fees, insurance, and public dues, structurally limiting vacancy risk.

Rent increases by 2.75% annually, with an additional market-rate adjustment of up to 10% permitted from the third contract year onward. The company listed on the KOSPI on July 10, 2025, at an offering price of KRW 5,000 per share, raising approximately KRW 96.5 billion.

Post-listing ownership consists of Daishin Securities at 18.1%, pre-IPO institutional investors at 49.6%, and public investors at 32.3%, with total shares outstanding of 59.78 million.

Since listing, the company has outlined a plan to sequentially inject group-owned or group-developed assets such as 343 Gangnam, Seorin 345, Sewoon District 5, Sobong Building, and Dress Garden, targeting KRW 1 trillion in assets within one year and KRW 2 trillion within five years.

Compared with other sponsor-backed office REITs such as SK REIT and SamsungFN REIT, Daishin Value REIT still maintains a single-asset structure, which has drawn comparisons noting its smaller scale and relatively lower target dividend yield.

Sponsor Daishin Securities has continued on-market share purchases since the listing, signaling its commitment to shareholder support.

04

Earnings analysis

Daishin Value REIT's 2025 revenue rose 5.8% year over year to KRW 9.45 billion from KRW 8.94 billion in 2024. Operating profit, however, declined 6.8% to KRW 6.67 billion in 2025 from KRW 7.16 billion in 2024, with revenue and profit moving in opposite directions.

As a result, the operating margin fell from 80.1% in 2024 to 70.6% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose 11.2% from KRW 1.86 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.07 billion in 2025, and net income including non-controlling interests followed the same pattern, rising from KRW 1.86 billion to KRW 2.07 billion.

The decline in operating profit despite higher revenue suggests that cost increases outpaced the rise in rental income. Conversely, the rise in controlling-interest net income despite lower operating profit points to improvements below the operating-profit line, such as financial costs or taxes.

Operating cash flow edged down slightly from KRW 2.46 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.27 billion in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose modestly from 144.8% to 146.5% as of year-end 2025.

Quarterly results for 2026 have not yet been confirmed through a formal periodic report; according to a disclosure filed on October 6, 2026, consolidated revenue and operating profit for that quarter were provisionally reported at KRW 8.826 billion and KRW 6.660 billion, respectively, but these figures are not periodic-report figures.

05

Industry analysis

The Seoul office market entered a phase of rising vacancy in 2026 as new supply concentrated in the Central Business District (CBD).

According to data compiled by R-SQUARE, the average Seoul office vacancy rate in the second quarter of 2026 rose 0.4 percentage points from the prior quarter to 6.5%, while the CBD, which absorbed more than 60% of the new supply, saw its vacancy rate climb 2.4 percentage points to 7.3%.

Even so, average rent for large CBD offices rose 8.5% year over year over the same period, indicating that increased supply did not immediately translate into falling rents.

Savills Korea projected that CBD vacancy could rise to the 8%-10% range in 2026 before gradually declining from 2027 onward as new assets stabilize.

In contrast, the Gangnam Business District (GBD) saw almost no new supply, with vacancy for ultra-large offices at just 0.3%, underscoring a growing divergence between districts.

Daishin Value REIT's core asset, Daishin 343, is under a triple-net structure with a long-term, full-floor lease to group affiliates, limiting direct short-term exposure to market vacancy swings, though district-wide rent and vacancy conditions could still affect asset valuation during future reappraisal or refinancing.

Unlike sponsor-backed peers such as SK REIT or SamsungFN REIT, which lease to multiple tenants, Daishin Value REIT depends entirely on a single group tenant, meaning the stability of its rental income is closely tied to the overall business stability of its sponsor group.

06

Outlook

Since its listing, the company has outlined plans to sequentially inject group-owned or group-developed assets, including 343 Gangnam, Seorin 345, and Sewoon District 5, to expand its asset base.

Prior market commentary has noted that such asset injections could be accompanied by equity raises, making the timing and funding method of future acquisitions a point to watch.

The senior secured loan the sub-REIT used to acquire Daishin 343 is scheduled to mature in March 2027, and the company has stated it plans to pursue refinancing after maturity.

Refinancing terms will depend on the prevailing interest rate environment, which could in turn affect future interest costs and dividend capacity.

The company maintains a quarterly distribution structure based on fiscal quarter-ends in February, May, August, and November, pursuing the seven-year average target dividend yield of 6.35% presented at listing.

Sponsor Daishin Securities disclosed as of August 2026 that it had made its 12th additional share purchase since listing, which the company described as a step to demonstrate confidence in the REIT's medium-term growth and dividend competitiveness.

Quarterly results for 2026 have not yet been confirmed through a periodic report, and confirmation of full-year 2026 results through the next periodic report will be an important point to monitor.

08

Bull factors

Structural Stability from the Triple-Net Lease

Daishin 343 operates under a triple-net master lease in which Daishin Financial Group affiliates lease the entire floor area for up to ten years, with tenants bearing operating costs such as management fees and insurance, limiting vacancy and cost-volatility risk.

Rent rises 2.75% annually and can be adjusted upward by up to an additional 10% from the third contract year based on market rates, providing a structural rent-growth path. This underpins near-term rental income stability even amid rising CBD vacancy.

Continued Sponsor Share Purchases

Daishin Securities disclosed that, as of August 2026, it had made 12 separate on-market share purchases in Daishin Value REIT since the listing. A sponsor brokerage steadily increasing its stake for more than a year after listing is relatively uncommon in Korea's REIT market.

However, this reflects Daishin Securities' own investment judgment and should be understood as a sign of the sponsor's commitment rather than a guarantee regarding share price direction.

Improving Controlling-Interest Net Income

Controlling-interest net income rose 11.2% from KRW 1.86 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.07 billion in 2025. This occurred even as operating profit fell 6.8% over the same period, suggesting improvement below the operating-profit line. Revenue also grew steadily, from KRW 8.94 billion in 2024 to KRW 9.45 billion in 2025.

Bear factors

Declining Operating Margin and Cost Pressure

Despite higher revenue, operating profit fell 6.8% year over year to KRW 6.67 billion in 2025, with the operating margin declining from 80.1% to 70.6%.

This implies that cost increases outpaced rent growth, and a similar pattern could recur if fixed-cost burdens such as management expenses rise during future asset injections.

Rising Leverage and the 2027 Refinancing Variable

The debt-to-equity ratio edged up from 144.8% in 2024 to 146.5% in 2025. The senior secured loan used to acquire Daishin 343 is set to mature in March 2027, and refinancing terms, depending on the interest rate environment, could affect future interest costs and dividend capacity. As of year-end 2025, total liabilities of KRW 428.5 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 292.5 billion.

Medium-Term Leasing Environment Shifts from CBD Oversupply

In the second quarter of 2026, CBD office vacancy rose 2.4 percentage points from the prior quarter to 7.3%, and Savills Korea projected CBD vacancy could climb to the 8%-10% range in 2026.

While Daishin 343's long-term, full-floor lease limits short-term impact, district-wide supply-demand conditions could become a reference point at future lease renewal or asset revaluation.

Risk factors

Asset Concentration Risk

Daishin Value REIT's income depends entirely on a single asset, Daishin 343, meaning portfolio diversification benefits are limited until additional assets are injected.

In addition, since tenants consist entirely of Daishin Financial Group affiliates, changes in the group's overall business environment could structurally affect rental stability.

Financing and Refinancing Risk

With the debt-to-equity ratio rising from 144.8% in 2024 to 146.5% in 2025, the sub-REIT's senior secured loan is set to mature in March 2027. Refinancing costs could vary depending on interest rate levels and loan terms, which in turn could affect future distributable resources.

Revenue Volatility and Capital-Related Disclosure from the REIT's Filing Structure

On October 6, 2026, the company filed a disclosure regarding revenue falling below KRW 5 billion or capital impairment thresholds; this stems from a recurring quarterly filing requirement tied to the fiscal structure in which dividend income from the sub-REIT fluctuates by quarter, even though consolidated revenue itself exceeds KRW 5 billion.

Still, repeated filings of this type could be perceived as a temporary concern in the market, and investors should review the detailed disclosure text alongside the headline filing category.

Overall view

Daishin Value REIT reported 2025 revenue of KRW 9.45 billion and controlling-interest net income of KRW 2.07 billion based on the triple-net lease structure of its single core asset, Daishin 343, showing improvement on the earnings side, even as the operating margin fell from 80.1% to 70.6% over the same period, revealing cost-side pressure as well.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose modestly to 146.5%, and the sub-REIT's senior secured loan is set to mature in March 2027, leaving refinancing terms as a future variable.

Sponsor Daishin Securities has demonstrated its commitment through 12 separate share purchases since listing, and the company continues to pursue an asset-expansion plan involving 343 Gangnam, Seorin 345, and Sewoon District 5, among others.

However, prior commentary has flagged the possibility that such injections could be accompanied by equity raises, making the timing and method an important point to monitor.

The CBD office market is in a phase of rising vacancy due to new supply, though large-office rents have risen in tandem, reflecting a diverging market.

Because quarterly results from 2026 onward have not yet been confirmed through a periodic report, the direction of earnings and financial structure should be reassessed once the upcoming annual report and refinancing outcome are disclosed.
