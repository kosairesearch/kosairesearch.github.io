Report overview

Narrowing losses at a special steel maker, with a convertible bond as the swing factor

KwangjinInd's cumulative sales rose and its operating loss narrowed through the third quarter of 2026, but five straight loss-making quarters and a KRW 32.0 billion convertible bond keep the balance sheet under pressure.

02

Business structure

KwangjinInd (026910) runs a steel segment that makes bar steel and special steel and a motors segment that handles imported motorcycles.

The steel segment centers on bar products such as ma-bonggang bars and filling bars, and according to the company, demand is rising for deformed bars and made-to-order special steel used in data centers, power plants, automation equipment and robotics.

Etoday reported 2025 segment sales of KRW 47.1 billion for bar steel and KRW 9.8 billion for motors, with motors down 23% from KRW 12.9 billion. The company cites synergy with Harley-Davidson and Triumph in its motors business and describes that segment as operating steadily.

According to the company, sales to AI data center and power equipment customers rose about 113% year on year in the first three quarters of 2026, while lower-margin trading sales fell about 7%.

Production moved to the new Busan Gijang plant, which began full operations in the second quarter of 2026, while the former Busan Sinpyeong plant is being marketed for sale to strengthen the balance sheet.

Other listed companies in the same industry include Hyundai Steel Company, Dongkuk Steel Mill, Daehan Steel and SeAH Steel, and KwangjinInd's sales scale is small by comparison. The company names higher-value made-to-order products as its core direction.

Reports say the largest shareholder changed several times in 2026 and that the company is pushing real estate and bio initiatives, so the business mix may keep shifting.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, sales fell from KRW 59.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 53.7 billion in 2023 (down 9.2% year on year), recovered to KRW 58.5 billion in 2024 (up 9.0%), and reached KRW 56.9 billion in 2025 (down 2.8%). Of 2022 through 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit of KRW 585 million in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 7.04 billion in 2023, and the loss widened to KRW 7.32 billion in 2024 before narrowing to KRW 4.25 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 1.0% in 2022 to -13.1% in 2023, -12.5% in 2024 and -7.5% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to KRW 2.83 billion in 2024 but returned to a net loss of KRW 4.86 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 339 million in 2022 and negative in each later year: -KRW 5.49 billion in 2023, -KRW 7.89 billion in 2024 and -KRW 1.1 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 177.8% in 2022 to 331.2% in 2023, 280.4% in 2024 and 363.1% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 20.1 billion and total liabilities were KRW 72.8 billion.

Quarterly, the operating loss was KRW 616 million in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 1.29 billion in the fourth quarter before narrowing to KRW 272 million in the first quarter of 2026.

The second quarter of 2026 posted an operating profit of KRW 169 million, a turn to positive from the prior quarter, while the third quarter returned to an operating loss of KRW 501 million, though the year-on-year loss was smaller.

According to the company, cumulative gross margin for the first three quarters of 2026 was 9.1%, up 4.8 percentage points year on year, and the company credits the end of production stoppages and inefficiencies during the Busan plant move.

05

Industry analysis

Steel prices, utilization rates and demand from the auto and machinery sectors are repeatedly cited in press coverage as drivers of KwangjinInd's profit and loss. Its end-market demand has two sides.

One is investment in AI data centers and power equipment, where the company says related sales rose about 113% year on year in the first three quarters of 2026.

The other is demand for deformed bars and made-to-order special steel used in automation equipment, robotics and power plants, which the company expects to keep growing. The company plans to cut its share of low-margin trading products and expand made-to-order sales based on firm customer orders.

That approach leans toward profitability through order-based products rather than volume. Hyundai Steel Company, Dongkuk Steel Mill, Daehan Steel and SeAH Steel are larger steelmakers in the same industry, and KwangjinInd's sales are smaller by comparison.

As a result, swings in raw material prices and plant utilization may affect its earnings more strongly. Its strength lies in exposure to growing end markets, while its weaknesses are scale and cost structure.

06

Outlook

The company views 2027 as the year when the full effect of its new plant will be reflected, and it plans to build a full-scale profitable structure through more higher-value made-to-order products and stronger cost competitiveness.

Workforce efficiency and cost reduction are running in parallel, so cost structure may improve before selling prices do. The sale of the Busan Sinpyeong plant is aimed at strengthening the balance sheet, and the proceeds are planned to reduce debt burden.

At the time of the Etoday report, there had been buyer inquiries, but no party had confirmed a final purchase intention. The KRW 32.0 billion convertible bond decided in November 2026 may provide short-term liquidity support, but conversion would increase the share count and dilute shareholders.

The profitable-structure target will be tested through the operating results reported in the 2026 annual report and the first-quarter 2027 report. The 2026 annual report is due by the end of March 2027, when full-year 2026 operating results and operating cash flow will be fixed in disclosed figures.

The first-quarter 2027 report has a statutory deadline of May 15, 2027, and whether quarterly operating profit turns sustainably positive is the next checkpoint.

Bull factors

Quarterly profit and margin gains after the new plant

Operating profit of KRW 170 million in the second quarter of 2026 turned positive from the prior quarter. The company said cumulative gross margin for the first three quarters of 2026 was 9.1%, up 4.8 percentage points year on year.

The company attributes this to the end of production stoppages and inefficiencies, and says the improvement continued quarter by quarter.

Demand growth from AI data centers and power equipment

According to the company, sales to data center and power equipment customers rose about 113% year on year in the first three quarters of 2026. The company says made-to-order bar steel orders for these applications continue.

It also said demand for deformed bars and special steel is growing in power plants, automation and robotics. The growth rate is a company-released figure and has not yet been verified in segment disclosures in periodic reports.

The 2027 profitability target and balance sheet plan

The company views 2027 as the year the full effect of its new plant will show, and says higher-value made-to-order sales and stronger cost competitiveness will build a profitable structure. Workforce efficiency and cost cuts are running in parallel.

Proceeds from the Busan Sinpyeong plant sale are planned for balance sheet improvement. If the sale proceeds reduce debt, the burden of a debt ratio of 363.1% at year-end 2025 would ease.

Bear factors

Financial burden from accumulated losses

The debt ratio rose to 363.1% at year-end 2025 from 280.4% at year-end 2024. Total liabilities of KRW 72.8 billion far exceed equity of KRW 20.1 billion.

Etoday reported that current liabilities of KRW 43.6 billion exceeded current assets of KRW 32.9 billion by more than KRW 10 billion at year-end 2025, which drew attention to short-term repayment pressure.

Even with the cumulative operating loss narrowing to KRW 600 million in the first three quarters of 2026, an annual operating profit has not yet been achieved.

Continued operating cash outflows and quarterly losses

Operating cash flow was negative in 2023 at -KRW 5.49 billion, in 2024 at -KRW 7.89 billion and in 2025 at -KRW 1.1 billion, after a KRW 339 million inflow in 2022. Cash generation has not followed the reversal.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued for five straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026, with a total of KRW 3.1 billion over the last four quarters. Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 13 billion were down 4.1% year on year, so the top-line recovery is also incomplete.

Shrinking motors segment and quarterly sales swings

Etoday reported that motors segment sales fell 23% to KRW 9.8 billion in 2025 from KRW 12.9 billion a year earlier. Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 13 billion were down 23.4% from the prior quarter. The company also said lower-margin trading sales fell about 7%.

Whether the KRW 16.9 billion sales level in the second quarter of 2026 was a temporary peak will become clear from subsequent quarterly results.

Risk factors

Liquidity and convertible bond dilution

Reports say the KRW 32.0 billion convertible bond is large relative to year-end cash. The largest single-tranche potential dilution is disclosed at 39.91%. Payment is scheduled for November 20, 2026, and conversion would increase the share count. Conversion terms and actual conversion volumes will appear in later disclosures.

Administrative-issue listing standard

Etoday reported that KwangjinInd's market capitalization had stayed below KRW 15.0 billion for 25 trading days at the time of its report. Under listing rules, 30 consecutive trading days below that level leads to designation as an administrative issue. Current market capitalization is also below that threshold.

The same report described delisting procedures if the threshold is not regained within 90 trading days after designation.

Share price volatility and changes in the largest shareholder

Reports said the stock fell 29.88% in a single day based on the November 11, 2026 close. Reports also say the largest shareholder changed several times in 2026 and that the company is promoting real estate and bio initiatives.

Share price volatility is high while the largest shareholder and business direction are both shifting. The scale of the new initiatives has not yet appeared in reported results.

12

Overall view

On a cumulative basis through the third quarter of 2026, KwangjinInd's sales rose 3.4% year on year and its operating loss narrowed to KRW 600 million.

The second quarter of 2026 recorded a quarterly operating profit of KRW 170 million, and the company targets a profitable structure in 2027 through its new plant and more made-to-order sales. Growth in AI data center and power equipment sales is evidence of a shift in business direction.

Against that, the debt ratio of 363.1%, total liabilities of KRW 72.8 billion against equity of KRW 20.1 billion at year-end 2025, three consecutive years of operating losses from 2023 through 2025, and the return to an operating loss in the third quarter of 2026 remain unresolved.

The KRW 32.0 billion convertible bond adds short-term liquidity support on one side and shareholder dilution concern on the other. Market capitalization below the listing threshold, the change in the largest shareholder and share price volatility also stand as facts on both sides of the debate.

The company's profitability plan and its financial burden are in tension, and the next checkpoints are operating profit in the 2026 annual report and the first-quarter 2027 report.
