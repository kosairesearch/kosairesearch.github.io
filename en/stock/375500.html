Report overview

DL E&C: Order recovery versus a Q3 2026 net loss

DL E&C's operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income both rose in 2025, but the company posted a controlling-shareholder net loss in Q3 2026, so how far order recovery and housing cost improvements can carry earnings is the open question.

  1. 1

    Audited 2025 results show revenue of KRW 7.4 trillion (down 11.0% year on year), operating profit of KRW 387 billion (up 42.8%) and an operating margin of 5.2%. Revenue fell while profit recovered.

  2. 2

    Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 1.51 trillion, down 21.0% from a year earlier, and the company swung to a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 26.7 billion. Operating profit in the same quarter was KRW 107.3 billion.

  3. 3

    Preliminary Q2 2026 results announced on July 30, 2026 showed new orders of KRW 3.12 trillion and an order backlog of KRW 28.85 trillion at quarter-end.

02

Business structure

DL E&C listed on KOSPI in January 2021 after its corporate split in 2021, and its core businesses are housing, civil engineering and plants.

Its housing business centers on urban redevelopment projects in Seoul under the high-end brand ACRO, and in 2025 it created a dedicated public redevelopment unit under its urban redevelopment team, winning major projects in Yeonhui, Jangwi and Jeungsan.

The building segment is handled jointly with the subsidiary DL Construction, and the company attributed its 2025 profit recovery to stronger process and cost control in its housing and building divisions and to a reduced share of high-risk projects. The plant segment is shifting toward power generation and energy.

In June 2026 the company won a KRW 550 billion East Jeju combined-cycle power project and is expanding power infrastructure work that includes combined-cycle and pumped-storage plants.

Drawing on its North American plant experience and its standard design capability for X-energy's small modular reactors, it is also pursuing entry into next-generation energy markets. In data centers, DL Construction won a KRW 126.8 billion Bucheon AI data center project in the first half of 2026.

In a Korean daily's tally of first-half 2026 public works orders, DL E&C ranked first among major builders, with HJ Shipbuilding & Construction, Kye-Ryong Construction Industrial and Hyundai Engineering & Construction also in the top group.

In Seoul redevelopment work it competes with large builders head to head, while public tenders are concentrated in large comprehensive-screening contracts.

04

Earnings analysis

On the audited basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 7.5 trillion in 2022 to KRW 7.99 trillion in 2023 (up 6.6%) and KRW 8.32 trillion in 2024 (up 4.1%), then fell to KRW 7.4 trillion in 2025 (down 11.0%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 497 billion in 2022 to KRW 330.7 billion in 2023 (down 33.5%) and KRW 270.9 billion in 2024 (down 18.1%), then recovered to KRW 387 billion in 2025 (up 42.8%). The operating margin moved from 6.6% in 2022 and 4.1% in 2023 to 3.3% in 2024 and 5.2% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 413.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 187.9 billion in 2023 (down 54.5%), then rose to KRW 229.2 billion in 2024 (up 22.0%) and KRW 370.2 billion in 2025 (up 61.5%), increasing for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 431.6 billion in 2022, KRW 202.2 billion in 2023, KRW 229.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 370.2 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 91.3% in 2022 to 100.4% in 2024 before falling to 84.4% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 5.24 trillion and total liabilities KRW 4.43 trillion. Operating cash flow was KRW 232.1 billion in 2025, up from KRW 187.9 billion in 2024.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit went from KRW 116.8 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 63 billion in Q4 2025 (down 46.0% from the prior quarter), then KRW 157.4 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 159.4 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 107.3 billion in Q3 2026, which was down 8.1% year on year.

Revenue in Q3 2026 was KRW 1.51 trillion, down 21.0% from a year earlier. Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 205.3 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 160.1 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 129.7 billion in Q2 2026, before a KRW 26.7 billion net loss in Q3 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 468.5 billion. Why Q3 operating profit stayed positive while the controlling-shareholder result turned negative cannot be separated from the disclosed line items alone.

05

Industry analysis

Signals in Korea's construction market are mixed for housing. In an April 2026 report, Eugene Investment & Securities noted that housing starts in the building and housing segment have risen since a trough in 2023.

Over the same period NH Investment & Securities expected the pace of earnings improvement to be gradual, citing a decline in housing starts in 2026. The two views differ on how long it takes for rising starts to convert into revenue.

According to the company, the main driver of the earnings recovery has been housing cost ratios, which fell in the first half of 2026: the consolidated cost ratio went from 88.2% to 83.7% and the housing cost ratio from 88.9% to 78.1% (preliminary, announced July 30, 2026).

In plants, demand is shifting toward power generation facilities and nuclear and SMR work, and the company says data center orders are rising with the expansion of AI. In a January 2026 report, Kiwoom Securities said the absence of large plant orders over the prior two years made a revenue decline unavoidable in 2026.

Competition in public works concentrated large comprehensive-screening contracts in the first half of 2026, and DL E&C ranked first in a Korean daily's tally of those orders.

Against peers Samsung C&T, Hyundai Engineering & Construction, Daewoo Engineering & Construction and GS Engineering & Construction, the difference lies in housing bid structure and the share of plant work.

06

Outlook

The company's 2026 targets are new orders of KRW 12.5 trillion and revenue of KRW 7.2 trillion. New orders in the first half of 2026 totaled KRW 5.25 trillion, and the order backlog at the end of Q2 was KRW 28.85 trillion, up from the end of 2025 (preliminary, announced July 30, 2026).

The company said it would keep pursuing core Seoul redevelopment projects, including Seongsu 2 district, in the second half. Large data center orders in the Chungcheong region and the Seoul metropolitan area are also cited as expected opportunities.

In July 2026 it signed a public housing construction contract with Korea Land & Housing Corporation for projects in Osan and Incheon, where the company's share is 51% of a KRW 556.7 billion total, or KRW 283.9 billion.

In power, besides the East Jeju combined-cycle project, the company plans to widen its power infrastructure orders across combined-cycle and pumped-storage plants. In nuclear and SMR work, it is pursuing entry based on its partnership with X-energy and its standard design capability.

Whether the KRW 7.2 trillion revenue target is met will be confirmed at the close of fiscal 2026 and in the annual disclosure. With Q3 2026 revenue down 21.0% year on year, the pace of revenue recovery in Q4 is central to reaching that target.

Bull factors

Profitability recovery from housing cost improvements

The operating margin rose to 5.2% in 2025 from 3.3% in 2024. The company said in preliminary results announced July 30, 2026 that the first-half 2026 operating margin reached 9.0%, up from 6.2% a year earlier, with housing cost ratio at 78.1% cited as the main factor.

Order recovery and business diversification

New orders in the first half of 2026 totaled KRW 5.25 trillion (preliminary, announced July 30, 2026), and the order backlog at the end of Q2 was KRW 28.85 trillion. The East Jeju combined-cycle and Bucheon AI data center wins are examples of broadening beyond housing.

Financial capacity and shareholder returns

The debt ratio fell to 84.4% at the end of 2025 from 100.4% in 2024. Net cash at the end of Q2 2026 was KRW 1.19 trillion (preliminary), and the company's corporate bond rating is held at AA- with a stable outlook. A KRW 55.5 billion buyback trust under the 2024–2026 policy runs to December 24, 2026.

09

Bear factors

Continued shrinkage in revenue

Revenue fell 11.0% to KRW 7.4 trillion in 2025. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 1.51 trillion was down 21.0% from a year earlier. The 2026 revenue target of KRW 7.2 trillion faces a burden from the decline seen through the third quarter.

Swing to a controlling-shareholder net loss in Q3

Controlling-shareholder net income swung to a loss of KRW 26.7 billion in Q3 2026. Operating profit in the same quarter was KRW 107.3 billion, down 32.7% from KRW 159.4 billion in Q2. How the gap between operating and net results is explained in the next regular report is the item to check.

Concerns over plant order gaps and falling housing starts

In a January 2026 report, Kiwoom Securities said the absence of large plant orders over the prior two years made a revenue decline unavoidable. In an April 2026 report, NH Investment & Securities expected a gradual pace of earnings improvement because of lower housing starts in 2026. Both views point to a possibly delayed recovery in revenue.

10

Risk factors

Overseas plant projects and geopolitical factors

Middle East and other overseas plant projects can see changes in start dates and contract timing depending on local policy and geopolitics. Kiwoom Securities cited prospective reconstruction work in Iran tied to a US–Iran memorandum, and any delay could adjust overseas order expectations. Because large plant orders tend to cluster in particular periods, quarterly results can also be volatile.

Housing start and sales cycle

The housing business has a lag structure in which revenue follows construction starts. With a forecast of lower housing starts in 2026, a slower recovery in starts could prolong the revenue decline. Even as redevelopment orders grow, revenue recognition can shift later depending on construction progress.

Cost ratio reversal and site management

The main driver of first-half 2026 profitability is the housing cost ratio falling to 78.1%. A reversal in that ratio could interrupt the margin recovery. The subsidiary's cost ratio also improved, but site-level process risk remains a source of earnings volatility.

Overall view

DL E&C's operating profit fell in 2023 and 2024 before recovering to KRW 387 billion in 2025, and controlling-shareholder net income also rose in 2024 and 2025.

In 2026 the housing cost ratio improvement lifted the first-half operating margin, but Q3 revenue fell 21.0% from a year earlier and the company swung to a controlling-shareholder net loss, which brings the durability of earnings back into question.

On orders, first-half 2026 new orders of KRW 5.25 trillion and a backlog of KRW 28.85 trillion were presented as the revenue base, while whether the annual target is met depends on order flow through year-end. On finances, a debt ratio of 84.4% at the end of 2025 and an AA- credit rating provide a cushion.

Shareholder returns continue through buybacks under the three-year policy, with the trust contract ending December 24, 2026. The target prices from Kiwoom Securities and Hyundai Motor Securities reflect those brokers' own judgments from May and June 2026.

The next items to check are the preliminary annual results for fiscal 2026 and the profit structure after Q3 in the 2026 annual report.
