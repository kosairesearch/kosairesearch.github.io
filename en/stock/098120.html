Revenue Up Four Straight Quarters, Profit Path Uneven

Micro Contact Solution's revenue has risen for four straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026 after a 44.0% jump in 2025, but operating profit fell from its second-quarter level, making the durability of profit the next checkpoint.

02

Business structure

Micro Contact Solution was founded in 1999 to manufacture semiconductor test sockets, and in 2022 it acquired a 98.41% stake in its subsidiary MSL to broaden its semiconductor equipment capabilities. The business is split into two segments, Semicon and Appliance.

The Semicon segment makes contact parts such as burn-in and module sockets, and the company's earnings commentary cited growing demand for ultra-precision sockets below 0.3mm as a driver of cumulative results through the third quarter of 2025.

The Appliance segment produces thermal protectors and electronic switchgear, and a 2025 news report said the switchgear is supplied to LS Electric on an OEM basis.

The same report said switchgear-related sales rose from KRW 1.0 billion in 2023 to KRW 11.8 billion in 2024 and reached KRW 10.3 billion in the first half of 2025 alone. According to the data aggregator Butler, the segment mix by sales for the second quarter of 2026 was 81% Semicon and 19% Appliance.

Customers are major domestic and overseas chipmakers, and a credit rating agency report cited the trust earned with these customers as a core strength.

Product competitiveness rests on socket design and materials technology for highly integrated package testing, supplied through a high-mix, low-volume production system. In the test equipment and parts field, the company is often compared with TSE, LEENO Industrial and EO Technics.

Because Semicon accounts for the larger share, the company is exposed to semiconductor capex cycles, while Appliance relies on separate demand tied to power grid replacement.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on DART annual reports, revenue was KRW 61.1 billion in 2022, KRW 64.8 billion in 2023 (up 6.2% year on year), KRW 69.7 billion in 2024 (up 7.5%) and KRW 100.3 billion in 2025 (up 44.0%). Revenue rose for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

Operating profit fell from KRW 7.89 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.43 billion in 2023 (down 5.9%), then rose to KRW 10.2 billion in 2024 (up 37.4%) and KRW 17.7 billion in 2025 (up 73.9%), increasing for two straight years.

In 2023 revenue grew 6.2% while the operating margin slipped to 11.5%, and in 2025 revenue grew 44.0% and the operating margin climbed to 17.7%.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 16.9 billion in 2025, up 58.1%, while net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 17 billion and is reported separately.

The company attributed the 2025 gains to stronger demand for key products such as test sockets in Semicon and electronic switchgear in Appliance, along with higher sales and profit from key products at its subsidiary PCR.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 25.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 27.2 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 27.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 41.9 billion in the second quarter and KRW 47.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026, marking four straight quarters of growth.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 13.9 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and fell to KRW 11.8 billion in the third quarter, while profit attributable to controlling shareholders dropped 43.0% from KRW 11.4 billion to KRW 6.5 billion.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 29.6 billion. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 10.5 billion in 2022 and KRW 9.25 billion in 2023 to KRW 4.66 billion in 2024 before recovering to KRW 18.6 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 81.4 billion, of which KRW 81.3 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 15.5 billion. The detailed causes of quarterly profit swings are covered in the business discussion of the quarterly reports.

05

Industry analysis

Semiconductor test sockets are components whose precision requirements rise as packages become finer and more highly integrated, making them sensitive to back-end investment and test volumes. The industry signal is stronger on the memory side.

Micron said in its third-quarter fiscal 2026 results released on June 24, 2026 that it posted record revenue, and it described DRAM and NAND industry demand as significantly exceeding supply.

This serves as a reference indicator of the direction of memory investment and test process demand, but no public data tied Micro Contact Solution's customer-level sales mix directly to the memory cycle, so the link is treated as a reference only.

The company's earnings commentary attributes demand for ultra-precision sockets in the Semicon segment to supply expansion by major chipmakers. Technology-based differentiation is central, and rising demand in the sub-0.3mm ultra-precision range is cited as the basis of its position.

The Appliance segment saw switchgear sales surge in a 2025 news report that linked the trend to grid equipment replacement demand in several countries and recovery demand after major fires.

The credit rating agency NICE Credit Rating said in its report that government support policies such as the Semiconductor Act are expected to broaden growth for semiconductor equipment and component companies.

The same report cited an analysis by Korea Technology Credit Rating that expanding the Appliance segment would raise both revenue stability and growth potential. In comparison with other test equipment makers, the company reads as a socket specialist that also has diversification through its Appliance segment.

Outlook

The key near-term variable is the divergence between revenue and profit seen in the third quarter of 2026.

Revenue of KRW 47.8 billion rose 88.1% from a year earlier, but operating profit of KRW 11.8 billion fell from the prior quarter, and the cost and product-mix factors behind that gap can be followed in the segment discussion of the quarterly report.

An earnings commentary from WiseReport said first-half 2026 revenue rose 45.7% year on year, and it describes the Semicon segment's improvement as coming from wider product supply and cost competitiveness.

In the Appliance segment, electronic switchgear depends on OEM volumes for LS Electric, so whether those volumes continue is central. A 2025 report said first-half switchgear sales of KRW 10.3 billion were close to the prior full-year level, which illustrates the segment's growth pace.

The company's explanation that results at its subsidiary PCR contributed to 2025 earnings is also something to track in the next annual report.

Fourth-quarter 2026 results are likely to first appear as a preliminary annual disclosure between January and February 2027, and the 2025 preliminary annual results were disclosed on February 12, 2026.

The 2026 annual report is due by the statutory deadline at the end of March 2027, and it will include segment revenue and customer composition. The first-quarter 2027 report is due by mid-May 2027 and will show whether the four-quarter growth run continues. Memory industry indicators remain reference points.

Bull factors

Four Straight Quarters of Revenue Growth

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 25.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 47.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026, a fourth consecutive quarterly increase. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was up 88.1% from a year earlier. An earnings commentary cited first-half 2026 revenue growth of 45.7% year on year.

Ultra-Precision Socket Demand and Semicon Improvement

The Semicon segment is described as having improved on rising demand for ultra-precision sockets below 0.3mm and wider supply to major chipmakers. Technology-based differentiation is cited as a core strength, with socket design and materials for highly integrated package testing as advantages. If back-end semiconductor demand continues, the volume base of this segment would widen.

Appliance Diversification and Cash Recovery

A 2025 report said switchgear-related sales rose from KRW 1.0 billion in 2023 to KRW 11.8 billion in 2024 and reached KRW 10.3 billion in the first half of 2025 alone. Operating cash flow recovered from KRW 4.66 billion in 2024 to KRW 18.6 billion in 2025. The debt ratio of 19.0% at the end of 2025 indicates a modest financial burden.

09

Bear factors

Profit Decline in the Third Quarter

Operating profit for the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 11.8 billion, down 14.8% from the prior quarter. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 43.0% to KRW 6.5 billion. Because profit fell even as revenue rose, the link between sales growth and profit growth weakened within a single quarter.

Customer and OEM Concentration

A 2025 report said electronic switchgear in the Appliance segment is supplied to LS Electric on an exclusive OEM basis. The Semicon segment also depends heavily on a small group of major chipmakers. Because a change in orders from a specific customer could affect total revenue, the range of earnings swings could widen.

History of Earnings Volatility

In 2023 revenue rose 6.2% while operating profit fell 5.9% and the operating margin slipped to 11.5%. Operating cash flow declined from KRW 10.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.66 billion in 2024. The 2025 recovery is clear, but the 2022–2024 record shows that revenue growth did not always convert into profit growth.

10

Risk factors

Customer and OEM Dependence

Electronic switchgear relies on OEM volumes for LS Electric, and the Semicon segment likewise depends on orders from a small number of large chipmakers. An order adjustment or change of supplier by a single customer could show up in results directly.

The extent of customer diversification can be followed in the customer composition discussion of the annual report.

Semiconductor Capex Cycle

The Semicon segment is sensitive to back-end equipment investment and test volumes. A memory boom can benefit test demand, but orders can fall quickly if capital spending enters an adjustment phase.

How the cycle position connects to the company's sales should be gauged by combining the annual report discussion with industry indicators.

Quarterly Profit Swings and Subsidiary Contribution

Operating profit moved from KRW 13.9 billion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 11.8 billion in the third, a large swing for a single quarter. Results at subsidiary PCR's key products were also cited as a driver of 2025 annual earnings. If the contribution from subsidiaries and segments shifts, the overall profit margin could also swing.

Overall view

Micro Contact Solution recorded its largest year on a DART-reported basis in 2025, with revenue of KRW 100.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 17.7 billion, and revenue has risen for four straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026.

Ultra-precision test sockets in Semicon and electronic switchgear in Appliance led growth, and operating cash flow recovered to KRW 18.6 billion in 2025.

On the other side, operating profit for the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 11.8 billion, down 14.8% from the prior quarter, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 43.0%. Dependence on LS Electric for switchgear OEM volumes and concentration on a small number of chipmakers can widen earnings swings.

Valuation can be read against KRW 29.6 billion of controlling-shareholder profit over the last four quarters and the gap between market value and book equity. The next checkpoints are the preliminary annual disclosure for 2026 and the 2026 annual report, where the drivers of the margin movement can be traced.
