Pneumatics Core, Robotics Bet: Testing the Turnaround

02

Business structure

TPC Robotics, founded in 1979, makes automation components and adopted its current name after a shareholder meeting in March 2026. Its pneumatics division, covering actuators, directional control valves and FRL units, generated about 76% of sales in the first nine months of 2026.

The robotics division, covering Cartesian robots, linear motors and linear robots, contributed about 24% over the same period. The company also operates bio and 3D printing and collaborative robot divisions.

Its pneumatic products serve semiconductor, electronics, display, automotive and secondary-battery customers, and one company profile describes the firm as the only domestic maker in this field to win large-scale projects.

Production runs through two domestic plants and overseas affiliates, three in China and one in the United States.

In January 2026 the company signed an MOU with a domestic partner on AI robot foundry cooperation, in February 2026 it signed a strategic cooperation and supply agreement with a Chinese partner, and in May 2026 it agreed with a Chinese humanoid robot maker on joint development and production of actuators.

In robotics, the company sits in the same industry group as Doosan Robotics and Rainbow Robotics. Robotis is a separate company that supplies modular actuators and should not be confused with TPC Robotics.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales were KRW 94.3 billion in 2022, fell 8.6% to KRW 86.2 billion in 2023, rose 0.3% to KRW 86.4 billion in 2024 and eased 0.1% to KRW 86.3 billion in 2025, so the top line has held in a narrow band. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 909 million in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 5.16 billion in 2023, and the loss then narrowed to KRW 2.48 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.3 billion in 2025.

The operating margin was 1.0% in 2022, -6.0% in 2023, -2.9% in 2024 and -2.7% in 2025, so margins improved inside the loss zone but were still negative on a full-year basis in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 26.45 million in 2022, a net loss of KRW 7.16 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 4.9 billion in 2024 and a net loss of KRW 2.57 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests is a different figure, a net loss of KRW 2.52 billion in 2025, and the gap reflects non-controlling interest results. Operating cash flow was -KRW 1.6 billion in 2022, KRW 3.26 billion in 2023, -KRW 4.98 billion in 2024 and -KRW 219 million in 2025.

The debt ratio moved from 164.6% in 2022 to 149.9% in 2023, 168.3% in 2024 and 185.4% in 2025, and at end-2025 total equity was KRW 37.4 billion, of which KRW 36.4 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 69.3 billion.

On a quarterly basis, 3Q 2025 sales were KRW 21.1 billion with an operating loss of KRW 778 million, and 4Q 2025 sales rose 17.4% quarter on quarter to KRW 24.8 billion with operating profit of KRW 518 million, a swing to profit. 1Q 2026 sales fell 3.6% to KRW 23.9 billion, the low point of the period, and the company returned to an operating loss of KRW 6.66 million. 2Q 2026 sales rose 3.9% to KRW 24.9 billion, but the operating loss widened to KRW 593 million. 3Q 2026 sales reached KRW 26.6 billion, up 7.0% quarter on quarter and 25.8% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 1.69 billion, a swing from a year-earlier loss.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 924 million in 4Q 2025, a net loss of KRW 289 million in 1Q 2026, a net loss of KRW 1.1 billion in 2Q 2026 and KRW 1.71 billion in 3Q 2026, and the four quarters from 4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026 total KRW 1.25 billion.

The filings do not separate one-off items from recurring results, so the durability of the quarterly profit cannot be judged from this data alone.

05

Industry analysis

The robotics business relies on expectations for humanoid robot demand. Goldman Sachs has been reported to forecast roughly 6.5 million humanoid robot shipments by 2035, an external estimate that has not been tied to any order for TPC Robotics.

Linear motors for semiconductor and display equipment are the area where the company said supply would scale up in the second half of 2026. Pneumatic demand depends on the investment cycles of semiconductor, electronics, display, automotive and secondary-battery customers.

An investment firm's report pointed to growing semiconductor package sales as a basis for the earnings rebound, and said the share of semiconductor-related sales rose from 4.0% in 2025 to 10.5% in 1Q 2026.

The same report claimed robot equipment utilization rose from 53% in 2025 to around 100% by its July 2026 publication, but these are the report's figures and have not been confirmed in company filings.

The report also judged that the company's coverage from joint actuators to robot hands and end effectors gives it a differentiated position against domestic pneumatic rivals with limited mass-production references.

In robot components, Robotis supplies modular actuators as a separate company and should be distinguished in any comparison. Company materials describe the pneumatics business as one of the largest air pressure equipment makers in Korea, but this review did not find a market-share comparison with overseas competitors.

06

Outlook

The company expects its results to turn around from the second half of 2026, led by robotics and linear motors, and has set a target of full-year profitability.

The seven humanoid-robot actuator models it launched in September 2026 are aimed at domestic and overseas humanoid robot makers and equipment suppliers, and the company plans to use the distribution network and project partners from its existing automation business.

It also said linear motor supply for mobile display manufacturing equipment would scale up in the same period. A company official stated a goal of significantly improving sales and profit by the end of the year.

An investment firm's July 2026 report judged that a 2026 turnaround was likely, attaching conditions that fixed-cost leverage, higher robot equipment utilization, growth in semiconductor-related sales and pneumatic price increases would need to coincide.

That view comes from one research house and is not a company guidance figure. Based on the filings, the simple sum of operating profit for 1Q to 3Q 2026 is positive, so whether full-year 2026 turns profitable depends on the sign of 4Q 2026 operating profit.

Actual customer supply contracts for the humanoid actuators and the timing of their revenue recognition have not yet been confirmed in disclosures. The next checkpoints are whether 4Q 2026 operating profit stays positive, how the robotics share of sales changes, and the size of humanoid actuator sales contracts.

Bull factors

Quarterly profit swing and sales rebound

Operating profit in 3Q 2026 was KRW 1.69 billion, a swing to profit from a year-earlier loss, and sales rose in two consecutive quarters, 2Q and 3Q 2026. The recovery in sales since the 1Q 2026 low is the basis for this trend.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 1.25 billion, in positive territory.

Expanding robotics product lineup

The company launched seven humanoid-robot actuator models in September 2026 and agreed with a Chinese humanoid robot maker in May 2026 on joint development and production. Expanding linear motor supply for semiconductor and display equipment has been presented as another driver of second-half results. Robotics accounted for about 24% of sales in the first nine months of 2026, leaving room to expand.

Established pneumatics position and overseas footprint

One company profile service notes that the pneumatics business has been winning large-scale projects. The structure of two domestic plants and four overseas affiliates could serve as sales channels. An investment firm cited price increases for pneumatic products as a factor behind the expected earnings turnaround.

Bear factors

Three straight operating loss years and negative full-year margin

The company recorded operating losses for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and the 2025 operating margin was -2.7%. In 2Q 2026 the operating loss widened to KRW 593 million. Whether the 3Q 2026 profit is a single-quarter result or carries into a full-year trend will be clearer only after the 4Q results.

Rising debt burden and volatile cash flow

The debt ratio rose from 164.6% in 2022 to 185.4% in 2025, and at end-2025 total liabilities of KRW 69.3 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 37.4 billion. Operating cash flow was -KRW 4.98 billion in 2024 and -KRW 219 million in 2025, not positive in either year. Rising robotics investment without balance sheet improvement could increase cash pressure.

Unproven profitability of the robotics business

The humanoid actuators were launched in September 2026, and mass-production references and customer sales have not yet been confirmed in disclosures. The company itself said that intensifying competition in the first half of 2026 caused losses.

Robotics accounted for about 24% of sales in the first nine months of 2026, which is not yet large enough to drive total results.

Risk factors

Earnings durability

A single quarterly profit and a full-year turnaround are different. With losses in 1Q and 2Q 2026, the sign of 4Q operating profit will determine whether the full-year target is met. Because one-off gains and losses are not separated in the filings, the quality of the profit is hard to judge.

Financial structure and cash flow

A rising debt ratio overlaps with volatile operating cash flow. Operating cash flow was -KRW 4.98 billion in 2024, and higher working capital needs could increase cash pressure. The borrowing structure and maturity profile need to be checked in the annual report.

Business transition and competition

The humanoid and robotics markets involve intense domestic and overseas competition, and the company itself cited intensifying competition as a cause of its first-half losses. Execution of the Chinese cooperation agreements and the operation of overseas affiliates are external variables. Until new businesses account for a larger share of sales, dependence on pneumatics will persist.

Overall view

TPC Robotics swung from a year-earlier loss to a 3Q 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.69 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.71 billion. This reverses a pattern of three consecutive full-year operating losses from 2023 to 2025.

However, 1Q and 2Q 2026 were loss-making, and the four-quarter net profit total of KRW 1.25 billion means durability is still to be confirmed. Pneumatics still drives the business at about 76% of sales in the first nine months of 2026, with robotics at about 24%.

The humanoid actuator launch and linear motor supply for semiconductor and display equipment are the expansion areas the company has presented, but sales and profitability from them have not yet been confirmed in disclosures.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio rose from 164.6% in 2022 to 185.4% in 2025, and total liabilities of KRW 69.3 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 37.4 billion at end-2025.

Valuation comparisons have become possible with the quarterly profit swing, but their interpretation depends on whether full-year profitability is achieved.

Strengths include the quarterly profit swing, the robotics product lineup and the established pneumatics position, while weaknesses include three straight loss years, the debt burden and unproven robotics profitability. The next checkpoints are the 4Q 2026 results and the 2026 annual report.
