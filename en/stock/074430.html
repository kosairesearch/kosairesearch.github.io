02

Business structure

Aminologics runs two businesses: amino acid derivative compounds and active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) supplied to domestic pharmaceutical manufacturers. In the third quarter of 2026, the amino acid segment generated KRW 6.78 billion of KRW 7.41 billion in sales, or 91.5%.

The 2023 mix was roughly 70% amino acids and 30% APIs. The amino acid lineup includes protected amino acids, amino esters and oligopeptides, which the company has commercialized to broaden its portfolio.

APIs go into medicines for stroke, eye diseases and dermatitis, as well as cosmetic ingredients, and are supplied to domestic drug makers. The company focuses on non-natural amino acids and chiral technology, areas where few producers worldwide have production capacity.

Customer concentration is high: third-quarter 2026 sales to the largest shareholder group were KRW 6.51 billion on a consolidated basis, or 87.8% of the total, and the same group is also a counterparty for raw material purchases and contract manufacturing. The company has two subsidiaries, both unlisted.

Its model differs from large contract manufacturers and biosimilar-focused peers, which limits direct comparison.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales were KRW 27.3 billion in 2022, KRW 20.9 billion in 2023 (down 23.6% year on year), and KRW 16.8 billion in 2024 (down 19.6%), before rebounding to KRW 22 billion in 2025 (up 31.1%). 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest within 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 7.03 billion in 2022 (operating margin 25.7%) to KRW 1.46 billion in 2023 (down 79.2%, margin 7.0%), then turned into an operating loss of KRW 2.15 billion in 2024 (margin -12.8%). Operating profit of KRW 3.59 billion in 2025 (margin 16.3%) marked a return to profit.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved between losses and profits: a net loss of KRW 7.28 billion in 2022, a profit of KRW 2.44 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 1.62 billion in 2024 and a profit of KRW 3.52 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 3.52 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, third-quarter 2025 sales of KRW 6.01 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.4 billion were followed by fourth-quarter sales of KRW 6.52 billion (up 8.6% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 1.46 billion, yet the fourth quarter posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 258 million.

First-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 5.38 billion (down 17.5%) and operating profit of KRW 630 million (down 57.0%) were followed by second-quarter sales of KRW 8.25 billion (up 53.2%) and operating profit of KRW 1.98 billion (up 215.0%).

Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 7.41 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.87 billion came with a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 270 million, so operating and net results diverged again. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.53 billion.

The debt ratio rose from 9.9% in 2024 to 50.3% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 28.2 billion and total equity of KRW 56 billion at year-end 2025. The KRW 20 billion convertible bond approved in June 2025 appears to be a factor in the higher liabilities.

Operating cash flow was KRW 5.23 billion in 2022, -KRW 218 million in 2023, KRW 7.47 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.54 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The core areas for Aminologics are non-natural amino acids and chiral technology. Few producers worldwide have production capacity in these areas, which are generally regarded as high value-added.

Amino acids are used in medicines, food, feed, cosmetics and agricultural chemicals, and the company focuses on pharmaceutical ingredients and amino acid derivatives within that range.

Downstream demand comes from orders by domestic drug makers, with stroke, eye disease and dermatitis treatments and cosmetic ingredients as the main uses. The prescription and production plans of those downstream products can therefore affect sales indirectly.

In terms of cycle position, sales fell in 2023 and again in 2024 from KRW 27.3 billion in 2022 before rebounding in 2025, and quarterly sales in 2026 ranged from KRW 5.38 billion to KRW 8.25 billion, so quarter-to-quarter variation is large.

Among listed peers in biopharma, large companies such as Samsung Biologics and Celltrion focus on large-scale production and biopharmaceuticals, so their business models differ.

A direct listed competitor in amino acid derivatives is therefore hard to identify, and the scarcity of non-natural amino acid technology is the main point of differentiation. Market share comparisons with numbers are limited.

Recovery in operating profitability

Operating profit was KRW 1.98 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 1.87 billion in the third quarter, with third-quarter operating profit up 33.4% year on year. Whether this level holds will be shown by the operating margin in the next periodic report.

Scarcity of non-natural amino acid technology

Non-natural amino acids and chiral technology are described as areas where few producers worldwide have production capacity. The company has commercialized protected amino acids, amino esters and oligopeptides as application products.

Amino acid segment sales were KRW 6.78 billion in the third quarter of 2026, or 91.5% of the total. Whether this technology scarcity turns into sales growth will show up in the amino acid segment trend.

Financial capacity and profit run

Total equity was KRW 56 billion and total liabilities were KRW 28.2 billion at year-end 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 2.54 billion in 2025, a positive figure.

After the 2025 return to profit, net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.07 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 990 million in the second quarter.

Risk factors

Dependence on related-party trade

Because most sales come from dealings with the largest shareholder, changes in orders from that counterparty can move results directly. A share of 87.8% in the third quarter of 2026 means customer diversification remains at an early stage.

Whether diversification progresses will show in the sales breakdown of the next periodic report. Press coverage also pointed to direct export expansion as a factor to watch.

Market alerts and share price volatility

The January 2026 Caution Stock designation followed a rise of more than 60% over five trading days. Under the caution stage, the exchange stated that trading restrictions apply, including a 100% margin deposit and no margin purchases. The exchange also said further sharp gains could lead to re-designation as a higher-risk stock.

Dilution and concentration of decision-making

Exercise of the convertible bond call option raised voting shares outstanding from 87,826,844 to 88,140,322. The largest shareholder group's stake rose to 41.79%, and the filing states the purpose as influence over management. For minority holders, dilution and concentrated decision-making occur together. The absence of an outside director requirement is another point to note on oversight.

Overall view

Aminologics is a bio-materials company that handles amino acid derivatives and pharmaceutical ingredients together, and its full-year 2025 results turned to a profit. Operating profit rose in the second and third quarters of 2026, showing a recovery in profitability.

However, most sales come from dealings with the largest shareholder and related parties, and in the third quarter of 2026 a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders occurred despite higher operating profit. The rise in the largest shareholder group's stake also gives it greater influence over management.

The strengths are the recovery in operating margin, the scarcity of non-natural amino acid technology and a stable capital structure, while the weaknesses are trade concentration, volatile net income and the market-alert history.

Both sets of factors deserve equal weight, and which side prevails will show in the numbers of the next periodic reports. The next checkpoints are the preliminary full-year 2026 results, the 2026 annual report and the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report.
