Auto-foam core holds margins as 2026 profits soften

02

Business structure

Youngbo Chemical, founded in 1979, is a manufacturer specializing in cross-linked polyolefin foam, and it has been listed on the KOSPI since 1997.

Polyolefin foam is a material made through a foaming process that provides cushioning and insulation, and the company sells it across an automotive segment and a general industrial segment.

In automotive, ceiling materials and door cushioning are the representative products, and sales of automotive polyolefin foam are the main revenue source. In industrial applications, the company supplies cushioning and packaging materials for IT devices, followed by sales of general-purpose materials.

Revenue is composed of 94.55% products and 5.45% merchandise, so self-manufactured products account for most sales. Overseas, the Chinese subsidiary is pursuing revenue growth through aggressive sales, and the 2026 value-up plan specifies expansion into China and India.

The company points to its proprietary cross-linked foaming technology and quality innovation as differentiators, and its company materials cite a leading domestic market position and 142 patents. R&D spending in 2025 was about 1.90% of revenue.

Its main raw material, polyolefin-based resin, is linked to petrochemical feedstock prices, so the cost ratio is sensitive to oil price movements. Customers span automotive interior and IT device supply chains, so production volumes in those end markets directly determine sales.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 83 billion in 2022 to KRW 90.3 billion in 2023 (up 8.8% year on year), KRW 117.6 billion in 2024 (up 30.2%), and KRW 120.1 billion in 2025 (up 2.2%), growing for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year for revenue.

Operating profit went from KRW 4.97 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.95 billion in 2023 (up 39.8%) and KRW 19.4 billion in 2024 (up 179.2%), then KRW 19.3 billion in 2025 (down 0.6%). Operating margin rose from 6.0% in 2022 and 7.7% in 2023 to 16.5% in 2024, and was 16.1% in 2025.

In 2024, operating profit growth of 179.2% far exceeded revenue growth of 30.2%, so the margin improvement concentrated in that year. Profit attributable to owners was KRW 6.69 billion in 2022, KRW 5.96 billion in 2023 (down 10.9%), KRW 22.6 billion in 2024 (up 278.4%), and KRW 16.8 billion in 2025 (down 25.4%).

In 2025, operating profit fell only 0.6% while profit attributable to owners fell 25.4%, a wide gap between profit levels, and operating cash flow fell to KRW 15.4 billion from KRW 21.2 billion in 2024.

Quarterly, revenue went from KRW 31.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 4.84 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 34 billion with KRW 6.32 billion in the fourth quarter (up 30.4% from the prior quarter), then fell to KRW 28.3 billion with KRW 3.13 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 50.5% from the prior quarter).

The second quarter of 2026 recovered to KRW 31.8 billion revenue and KRW 5.06 billion operating profit (up 61.7% from the prior quarter), but the third quarter of 2026 came in lower again at KRW 29.3 billion revenue (down 6.2% year on year) and KRW 4.52 billion operating profit (down 6.7%).

Profit attributable to owners in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 3.06 billion, down 31.5% year on year, and the latest four quarters total KRW 16.9 billion.

For the first-quarter 2026 decline, fnguide's company summary attributes it to automotive revenue growth being offset by lower sales of higher-value products such as ultra-thin foam for IT devices, which pushed gross profit and operating profit down year on year. The specific causes of the 2025 net profit decline can be checked in the notes to the annual report.

Youngbo Chemical's end markets are automotive and IT devices. In automotive, fnguide's company summary expects demand to rise on stable oil prices and a recovery in the auto market driven by premium interior materials.

In IT, sales of higher-value products such as ultra-thin foam fell in the first quarter of 2026 and weighed on profitability. Its main raw material, polyolefin-based resin, is linked to oil prices, so the cost ratio is sensitive to feedstock movements.

Within the chemical sector, Alphasquare's ranking of 122 listed companies places the company ninth on operating margin. In the same dataset, its dividend yield ranks fourth in the sector, and its debt ratio is well below the sector average.

Its market capitalization ranks 74th in the sector, placing it among smaller listed companies. The 2026 value-up plan calls for expansion into overseas markets such as China and India, and the Chinese subsidiary's sales push is a stated revenue strategy.

Because automotive production and IT device shipments drive sales swings, the production cycles of those two industries are external variables for results. On competition, the company emphasizes foam material quality and its cross-linked foaming technology.

Bull factors

Recovery hopes in auto interior demand

fnguide's company summary expects auto-market demand to rise on stable oil prices and a shift toward premium interior materials. Automotive revenue had already grown in the first quarter of 2026, according to the same summary.

The company cites quality improvements in automotive foam and expanded sales at its Chinese subsidiary as revenue drivers. Because automotive is the main revenue base, a demand recovery could feed directly into reported results.

Top-tier margins and low leverage

Operating margin was 16.5% in 2024 and 16.1% in 2025, which places the company near the top of the chemical sector. Alphasquare ranks its operating margin ninth among the sector's listed companies, well above the sector average.

The debt ratio was 10.2% at the end of 2025, far below the sector average, with total liabilities of KRW 19.4 billion against total equity of KRW 191.3 billion. A balance sheet with this much room can cushion periods of raw material price swings.

High-dividend policy and shareholder return base

The company has said it qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act and presented a 2025 payout ratio of 40.55%. Alphasquare ranks its dividend yield fourth within the chemical sector.

As long as this policy remains in place, a shareholder return base persists even during periods of profit decline.

Bear factors

Quarterly profit softness in 2026

Operating profit was KRW 3.13 billion in the first quarter of 2026, down 50.5% from the prior quarter. Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 4.52 billion, down 6.7% year on year, and profit attributable to owners fell 31.5% to KRW 3.06 billion.

The recovery in the second quarter lasted only one quarter, and third-quarter revenue also fell 6.2% year on year. Profits below the prior year in consecutive quarters make it harder to confirm that earnings have bottomed.

Lower sales of higher-value products

fnguide's company summary says that, although automotive revenue rose in the first quarter of 2026, lower sales of higher-value products such as ultra-thin foam for IT devices pushed gross profit and operating profit down.

Higher-value products carry larger margin contributions, so a shift in product mix directly pressures margins. If revenue growth in automotive does not translate into profit growth, the benefit of top-line expansion diminishes.

Slowing top line and profit decline in 2025

Revenue in 2025 was KRW 120.1 billion, up only 2.2% year on year, sharply below the 30.2% growth in 2024. Operating profit was KRW 19.3 billion, down 0.6%, and profit attributable to owners fell 25.4% to KRW 16.8 billion. Operating cash flow also fell to KRW 15.4 billion in 2025 from KRW 21.2 billion in 2024.

The failure to hold profits at 2024 levels raises the question of whether the 2024 improvement was structural or temporary.

Risk factors

Raw material and oil price swings

The main raw material, polyolefin-based resin, is linked to petrochemical feedstock prices, so cost ratios rise when oil prices spike. How quickly the company can pass raw material increases through to product prices is a key variable for margins.

Whether cost structure also played a role in the volatile 2026 quarters should be checked through quarterly cost ratio changes. When feedstock prices stabilize, cost pressure eases, but price pressure on products may follow.

Concentration in end markets and reliance on overseas subsidiary

Revenue is concentrated in two end markets, automotive interiors and IT device components. A decline in automotive production or IT device shipments would directly affect sales.

The Chinese subsidiary's sales expansion is presented as a core revenue strategy, so results could vary with demand and local competition in China. As overseas exposure grows, the effects of exchange rates and local regulation also grow.

Small scale and dividend burden amid profit decline

The company's market capitalization ranks 74th in the chemical sector, placing it among smaller listed companies. Smaller stocks can show different price volatility and trading liquidity from large caps.

With profit attributable to owners down 25.4% in 2025, maintaining a high-dividend policy could raise the dividend burden relative to earnings. Whether dividends can be sustained should be checked alongside the pace of profit recovery.

Overall view

Youngbo Chemical is a small materials company that supplies cross-linked polyolefin foam for automotive interiors and industrial uses. Its operating margin has stayed in the 16% range since 2024, but profit attributable to owners fell 25.4% in 2025.

In 2026, first-quarter profit dropped sharply, recovered in the second quarter, and declined again in the third quarter, so the profit trend has softened. Bullish factors include expected recovery in automotive interior demand, a top-tier operating margin and low leverage within the sector, and a high-dividend policy.
