02

Business structure

Shinhan Seobu T&D REIT is an entrusted-management real estate investment company that listed on the KOSPI in December 2021, and its assets were developed and operated by its sponsor, Seobu T&D. An external asset management company handles asset management.

The portfolio consists of five assets: Incheon Square One, a mixed shopping mall; the Grand Mercure Yongsan hotel; Nine Tree Hotel Dongdaemun; Gwanghwamun G Tower, a hotel and office property; and Shilla Stay Mapo.

Seobu T&D leases Square One and the Grand Mercure hotel on a master lease basis, Homeplus leases the mart section of Square One, a hotel operator leases Nine Tree Dongdaemun, and the Shilla hotel brand operator leases Shilla Stay Mapo for long terms.

Mall rent is indexed to inflation with a cap of up to 5%, so price changes pass through to rental income. Hotel income depends on the operators' room and sales performance, which makes the portfolio sensitive to tourism demand.

In a February 2026 disclosure, the company attributed the change in results mainly to partial rent increases and higher hotel sales. An individual investor analysis noted that Square One still accounts for a large share of the assets.

Among K-REITs, most underlying assets are offices and logistics centers, and only a few, including Lotte REIT and Koramco Life Infra REIT, hold hotels.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 16.5 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to KRW 18.1 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024 (up 9.6% year on year), KRW 32.1 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025 (up 77.7%), and KRW 57.2 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026 (up 78.3%).

Revenue has increased for three consecutive fiscal years from the fiscal year ended June 2024 through the fiscal year ended June 2026, and the fiscal year ended June 2026 was the largest of the four years.

Operating profit moved from KRW 10.6 billion to KRW 11.9 billion (up 12.0%), KRW 23.7 billion (up 99.2%), and KRW 42.9 billion (up 80.9%). In the fiscal years ended June 2025 and June 2026, operating profit grew faster than revenue, and the operating margin rose from 64.6% to 66.0%, 74.0%, and 75.1%.

This pattern lines up with the factors the company cited in its February 2026 disclosure, namely partial rent increases and higher hotel sales.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 5.31 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to KRW 10.3 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024 (up 93.3%), then fell to KRW 1.95 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025 (down 81.0%).

Total net income in the fiscal year ended June 2025 was KRW 5.92 billion, so the gap with controlling-shareholder income was wide, and the noncontrolling share was large that year.

The fiscal year ended June 2026 recorded controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 23.9 billion (up 1,123.5%) and total net income of KRW 27.8 billion. Controlling-shareholder net income for the last four quarters (Q3 2025 to Q2 2026), KRW 23.9 billion, covers the same period as the fiscal year ended June 2026.

The debt ratio was 139.4% in the fiscal year ended June 2023, 195.4% in June 2024, 209.1% in June 2025, and 186.0% in June 2026. At the end of June 2026, total liabilities were KRW 701.8 billion and total equity was KRW 377.3 billion, of which equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 277.6 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 10.2 billion, KRW 9.07 billion, KRW 17.3 billion, and KRW 12.4 billion in the fiscal years ended June 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026.

In the fiscal year ended June 2026, operating profit rose while operating cash flow fell from KRW 17.3 billion to KRW 12.4 billion, so earnings and cash generation did not move in the same direction.

05

Industry analysis

Most underlying assets of K-REITs are offices and logistics centers, so the portfolio of Shinhan Seobu T&D REIT, which combines hotels and a shopping mall, is structured differently from much of the sector.

Among K-REITs, Shinhan Seobu T&D REIT, Lotte REIT and Koramco Life Infra REIT are commonly cited as holders of hotel assets. The company said it aims to sustain dividends and operating performance on the back of improved hotel results and stable rental income from its retail assets.

Hotel demand is tied to tourism flows, and an individual investor analysis listed vulnerability to external shocks such as COVID as a weakness. The same analysis also pointed to the need for a strategy that responds to changing travel trends.

Hotel income depends on the brand and operating capability of each operator, and the REIT holds several hotel brands, including Nine Tree, Shilla Stay and Grand Mercure. In 2023, rental income rose from the prior year while dividend per share fell, and rising interest rates were cited as the reason.

That episode suggests that the direction of interest rates has fed through to the company's dividend level. A Samsung Securities report from April 2025, following a corporate day, carried a headline stating that risk related to Homeplus and the rights offering had been resolved.

Outlook

When the company listed, its chief executive said the REIT would add a range of assets as a complex-type developer anchor REIT to broaden its earnings base.

Shilla Stay Mapo was selected as the preferred bidder in early 2025 and its acquisition was pursued, with a scale of KRW 140 billion, and it is now included in the portfolio.

A Samsung Securities report from January 2026 covered the announcement of a medium-term guidance, and it said the company reflected a special dividend in that guidance. The special dividend is to be reflected in the fourth-quarter settlement, with payment planned for the first quarter of 2027.

The inflation-linked structure of mall rent works as a mechanism that adjusts rental income along with price trends. An analysis also expects hotel assets to benefit from the Yongsan international business district project. A 2025 corporate day report summarized the Homeplus-related risk as resolved.

Whether the rent increases and higher hotel sales that the company cited in its February 2026 disclosure continue will be gauged by operating profit and rental income in upcoming periodic reports.

In the next periodic report, the key business checkpoints are whether the operating margin holds near 75.1% and how the debt ratio moves from 186.0%.

08

Bull factors

Inflation-linked rent structure

Rent at Incheon Square One is indexed to inflation with a cap of up to 5%. Because the mall is held under a master lease, rental income moves with price trends. An individual investor analysis described this structure as lowering vacancy risk. The mechanism that passes price changes into rental income is cited as a portfolio strength.

Three years of growth and a higher margin

Revenue has risen for three consecutive fiscal years from the fiscal year ended June 2024 through June 2026, and operating profit has done the same. The operating margin rose from 64.6% in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to 75.1% in June 2026.

Controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 23.9 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026 shows a recovery from KRW 1.95 billion in June 2025.

Hotel recovery and a planned special dividend

The company cited improved hotel results, and its February 2026 disclosure named higher hotel sales and partial rent increases as drivers of the change in results. An analysis also expects benefits linked to the Yongsan international business district project.

A special dividend is planned for payment in the first quarter of 2027 after reflection in the fourth-quarter settlement.

Bear factors

High debt ratio

Total liabilities at the end of June 2026 were KRW 701.8 billion, above total equity of KRW 377.3 billion. The debt ratio eased from 209.1% in the fiscal year ended June 2025 but remains above the 139.4% recorded in June 2023. Higher interest rates open a path for interest costs to weigh on dividend capacity.

Earnings volatility and slower cash flow

Controlling-shareholder net income fell 81.0% to KRW 1.95 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025. The gap with total net income of KRW 5.92 billion was wide that year, reflecting a large noncontrolling share.

Operating cash flow was KRW 12.4 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026, below KRW 17.3 billion a year earlier, so cash generation did not track the rise in operating profit.

Tenant concentration and external shocks

Incheon Square One still accounts for a large share of the portfolio. Homeplus-related lease risk was flagged as a weakness in one analysis, and hotels were described as vulnerable to external shocks such as COVID. In 2023, rental income rose while dividend per share fell during a period of rising interest rates.

10

Risk factors

Tenant concentration

Square One and the Grand Mercure hotel are leased long term by Seobu T&D under master leases, which makes the sponsor a central dependency. The Homeplus lease on the mart section of Square One was flagged as a risk in one analysis. Changes in a tenant's operating conditions feed directly into rental income.

Leverage and interest rates

A debt ratio of 186.0% means liabilities are large relative to equity. The 2023 episode showed that rising rates can coincide with higher interest costs and lower dividends. Acquisition activity and the terms of borrowing also shape the financial burden.

Hotel demand and external shocks

Hotel income depends on operators' room and sales trends, and tourism demand is sensitive to external shocks. One analysis cited vulnerability to events such as COVID as a weakness. The higher the hotel share of the portfolio, the larger the effect of such shocks on rental income.

12

Overall view

Shinhan Seobu T&D REIT's disclosed results show revenue and operating profit rising from the fiscal year ended June 2023 through the fiscal year ended June 2026. The operating margin rose from 64.6% to 75.1%, which points to higher profitability from its hotel and mall rental structure.

Controlling-shareholder net income fell to KRW 1.95 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025 and recovered to KRW 23.9 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026. On the other side, the debt ratio remains high at 186.0%, and operating cash flow in the fiscal year ended June 2026 fell from the prior year.

Strengths include inflation-linked rent, the master lease structure and a planned special dividend, while weaknesses include debt burden, earnings volatility, tenant concentration around Square One, and vulnerability of hotels to external shocks.

Both sides carry comparable weight, and the available data does not tilt the picture decisively either way. On valuation, the price-to-book ratio sits below 1x, and the price-to-earnings multiple is well below the 20-plus times range shown for 2023 and 2024.

The next checkpoints are Q3 2026 quarterly results, the year-end dividend record date in late December 2026, the special dividend payment in Q1 2027, and annual results for the fiscal year ending June 2027.
