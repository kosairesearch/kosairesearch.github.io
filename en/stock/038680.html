Report overview

AI infrastructure orders bring profits back, but scale is still small

S NET Systems posted operating profits from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 as GPU and AI infrastructure orders reached its results, but its 2025 operating margin was 2.3% and attributable net income for the last four quarters totaled KRW 12.2 billion.

02

Business structure

S NET Systems was spun off from Samsung's network business unit in 1999 as an IT infrastructure company, starting with the design, consulting and maintenance of integrated network systems.

It has since expanded into system integration (SI) and positions itself as a broader ICT company that also designs, builds and operates data centers. Large system construction and maintenance remain its revenue base, and the company credited growth in both businesses for its improved first-half 2026 results.

AI infrastructure is now the main growth driver. In August 2026 the company said cumulative orders in its GPU and AI infrastructure hardware business had reached KRW 140 billion.

In July 2026 it signed a contract worth about KRW 25.9 billion with a Korean AI software company to supply NVIDIA enterprise software and build an operating environment that allocates and manages GPU resources.

On the operations side, it was selected as lead contractor for a bio AI infrastructure project at a local public institution and won a multi-vendor GPU optimization project for a domestic telecom operator.

A subsidiary is growing cloud and software license sales to corporate clients and has won several public-sector AX pilot projects built on NAVER Cloud. Its customer base is anchored by Samsung group affiliates, with financial institutions, public agencies and manufacturers also among its clients.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 389.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 477.1 billion in 2023, up 22.5% and the largest year of 2022 to 2025, before slipping to KRW 449.9 billion in 2024 (down 5.7%) and recovering to KRW 457.4 billion in 2025 (up 1.7%).

Operating profit climbed from KRW 2.23 billion in 2022 to KRW 14.5 billion in 2023, up 549.2% and the largest year in the period. It then fell to KRW 10.7 billion in 2024, down 26.3%, and stayed at KRW 10.7 billion in 2025.

The operating margin was 0.6% in 2022, 3.0% in 2023, 2.4% in 2024 and 2.3% in 2025, so it has stayed in the low 2% range since the 2023 peak.

Attributable net income swung from a net loss of KRW 3.51 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 19.9 billion in 2023, then fell to KRW 5.95 billion in 2024 (down 70.1%) and KRW 4.56 billion in 2025 (down 23.4%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 5.03 billion in 2025, a different measure from the KRW 4.56 billion attributable figure, so the two should be read separately. Operating cash flow was -KRW 27.2 billion in 2022 and then KRW 16.9 billion, KRW 33.2 billion and KRW 23.8 billion in 2023 through 2025.

Quarterly results were far more volatile: after an operating loss of KRW 1.88 billion in the third quarter of 2025, fourth-quarter revenue of KRW 191.4 billion (up 102.1% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 11.6 billion (a turn to profit) followed.

First-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 77.8 billion (down 59.4% from the prior quarter) with operating profit of KRW 50.76 million (down 99.6%), and the company attributed that profit to stable execution on large projects and expanded maintenance work.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 186.1 billion (up 139.1%) with operating profit of KRW 3.12 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 97.1 billion (down 47.8% from the prior quarter, up 2.5% from a year earlier), operating profit was KRW 1.95 billion (down 37.6% from the prior quarter, a turn to profit year on year), and attributable net income was KRW 1.21 billion. Attributable net income for the last four quarters totaled KRW 12.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand in this sector comes from AI data center (AIDC) construction driven by the spread of generative AI and from government support programs for GPU infrastructure.

A company press release in May 2026 said these two drivers were gaining momentum and would widen opportunities for firms with IT infrastructure design and construction capabilities.

An August 2026 announcement likewise tied the rise in GPU infrastructure orders to wider corporate AI adoption, though this is the company's own account. The company positions itself in a vertical niche that combines hardware construction, software supply and operations optimization.

Its examples include participation in NVIDIA Vera Rubin based infrastructure, an ongoing central-government GPU server project and the upgrade of manufacturers' AI projects to agentic AI.

On the other side, quarterly revenue swung from KRW 77.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 186.1 billion in the second and KRW 97.1 billion in the third, which shows reliance on large project-based orders.

The company highlighted a first-quarter profit even though the first quarter is usually a slow period for the IT industry.

Listed peers in the same IT sector include Samsung SDS, LG CNS, Hyundai Autoever and POSCO DX Company, and S NET Systems has a larger share of Samsung group affiliates among its clients than these peers.

06

Outlook

The company said it is preparing large orders for the second half of 2026 in AI computing infrastructure and solution construction, as well as AI GPU consolidation investments by domestic manufacturers.

An official said that with demand for AI computing infrastructure expanding, revenue and profitability would improve together in the second half, though this is the company's view. The company also set achieving its annual earnings target as a plan.

The central-government GPU server project is targeted for completion by the end of 2026, and in the bio AI infrastructure project at a local public institution it serves as lead contractor integrating servers, networks and software.

The timing at which participation in NVIDIA Vera Rubin based infrastructure turns into revenue is the next point to watch. The corporate value-up plan disclosed in March 2026 set shareholder returns, portfolio upgrades and a sustainable growth base as its main pillars.

The 2025 payout ratio was 40.5%, and the prior-year dividend amount increased from the year before. On the operating side, the key questions are how far the operating margin, at 2.3% in 2025, can rise and whether quarterly revenue continues without gaps between large projects.

The relationship between total liabilities of KRW 135.1 billion and total equity of KRW 116.3 billion at the end of 2025 is another item to track in future periodic reports.

Bull factors

Expanding AI infrastructure orders

Its scope now runs from hardware construction to enterprise software, GPU operations optimization and agentic AI upgrades. The broader scope gives a basis for tracking how the revenue mix shifts across quarters.

Quarterly return to profit

Operating profit in the fourth quarter of 2025 was KRW 11.6 billion, a turn to profit from the prior quarter, and third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.95 billion turned positive year on year. Second-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 186.1 billion with operating profit of KRW 3.12 billion. The company emphasizes that it stayed profitable in the first quarter, which is usually a slow season for IT.

Shareholder returns and balance sheet improvement

In March 2026 the company disclosed a value-up plan that placed shareholder returns among its core pillars. The 2025 payout ratio was 40.5%. The debt ratio fell from 263.0% in 2022 to 116.2% in 2025, reducing financial burden.

Bear factors

Small profit base and thin margins

The operating margin was 2.3% in 2025, below 3.0% in 2023. Attributable net income fell from KRW 19.9 billion in 2023 to KRW 5.95 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.56 billion in 2025. Four-quarter attributable net income of KRW 12.2 billion is small relative to annual revenue of about KRW 457 billion.

Large quarterly swings

Quarterly revenue in 2026 was KRW 77.8 billion in the first quarter, KRW 186.1 billion in the second and KRW 97.1 billion in the third. First-quarter attributable net income was a loss of KRW 323 million, and third-quarter attributable net income of KRW 1.21 billion fell 48.2% from the prior quarter. Quarterly figures alone make the underlying trend hard to read.

Project dependence and client concentration

Construction revenue is recognized project by project, so delays in a single large contract directly affect quarterly results. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 37.6% from the prior quarter, a figure that belongs with this volatility. A large share of clients are Samsung group affiliates, which ties results to group IT spending.

10

Risk factors

Order and project timing risk

AI infrastructure orders depend on the timing of government programs and corporate GPU spending. The central-government GPU server project targets completion by the end of 2026, so any slippage could push back revenue recognition. A delay in one large contract can move quarterly results as a whole.

Profitability risk

The operating margin fell from 3.0% in 2023 to 2.3% in 2025, and with revenue centered on construction and maintenance, margin room is limited. Whether expansion into software and operations will lift margins has not yet appeared in disclosed figures. If another low-margin quarter like the first quarter of 2026 recurs, staying profitable becomes a challenge.

Balance sheet and funding risk

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 135.1 billion against total equity of KRW 116.3 billion, a debt ratio of 116.2%. The ratio improved from 263.0% in 2022, but a rise in upfront funding for large projects could weigh on financial flexibility. Operating cash flow of KRW 23.8 billion in 2025 provides a cushion.

Overall view

S NET Systems posted operating profits from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 as GPU and AI infrastructure orders reached its results, and cumulative AI infrastructure orders reached KRW 140 billion.

On an annual basis, operating profit and attributable net income declined after peaking in 2023, and the operating margin was 2.3% in 2025. Attributable net income for the last four quarters totaled KRW 12.2 billion, which is small relative to revenue.

Quarterly results swung widely with large project timing, with revenue of KRW 77.8 billion in the first quarter, KRW 186.1 billion in the second and KRW 97.1 billion in the third of 2026. On the balance sheet, the debt ratio fell from 263.0% in 2022 to 116.2% in 2025, and the 2025 payout ratio was 40.5%.

The price-to-book ratio is below 1x, while the earnings base is small, and both facts sit side by side. The next checkpoints are completion of the central-government GPU server project by the end of 2026 and the operating margin and attributable net income in the 2026 annual report.
