Report overview

MiCo: Two Growth Engines, Uneven Earnings

Revenue rose to KRW 977 billion in 2025 and grew for two straight years, but MiCo swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 26.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026, so sales growth and profit trends are diverging.

  1. 1

    Annual revenue bottomed at KRW 387.3 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 540.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 977 billion in 2025, the largest year in the 2022-2025 period, up 80.8% from the prior year.

  2. 2

    Operating profit reached KRW 101 billion in 2025 after KRW 94.6 billion in 2024, yet the operating margin fell from 17.5% in 2024 to 10.3% in 2025.

  3. 3

    Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 16.3 billion, down 27.3% from a year earlier, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 26.8 billion, compared with a KRW 1.65 billion profit in the third quarter of 2025.

  4. 4

    The debt-to-equity ratio climbed from 94.3% in 2022 to 228.6% in 2025, and total liabilities at the end of 2025 stood at KRW 1.58 trillion.

  5. 5

    The business rests on two pillars, semiconductor ceramic parts and solid oxide fuel cells (SOFC), and the fuel-cell unit is pursuing a 10MW expansion in Anseong and a 30MW production setup in Pyeongtaek.

02

Business structure

MiCo runs a semiconductor segment and an energy and environment segment, making it a materials and parts company with two businesses.

According to a share estimate by Butler, the energy and environment segment, centered on solid oxide fuel cells, accounted for about 50% of second-quarter 2026 revenue, semiconductors for about 49%, and AI and IT services for about 1%, so the two main businesses carry similar weight.

The semiconductor side centers on ceramic parts such as heaters and electrostatic chucks (ESC) used in chipmaking equipment. Per fnguide's consolidated data, exports made up 53.6% of 2024 revenue from heater, ESC and ceramic parts, with the rest sold domestically.

The ceramics subsidiary MiCo Ceramics manufactures semiconductor equipment parts, and another consolidated subsidiary handles cleaning and coating of equipment parts. The energy segment centers on solid oxide fuel cells, and fnguide reports that all 2024 SOFC sales were domestic.

Capacity plans include a 10MW expansion in Anseong in 2025 and a 30MW production setup at a new Pyeongtaek plant in 2026. On materials, the company announced on March 19, 2026 that it had localized high-performance silicon nitride powder and set up mass production.

On March 25, 2026, its power-systems subsidiary HPS said it would build a heat recovery steam generator production line centered on its Sacheon plant in South Gyeongsang and secure a production base in Vietnam to diversify its supply chain.

Competition in semiconductor parts includes domestic and foreign equipment-parts suppliers, while the fuel-cell business is at an early stage of expansion.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show a clear shift in direction since 2022. Revenue fell from KRW 414.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 387.3 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 540.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 977 billion in 2025.

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 21.1 billion in 2023, down 67.2% from the prior year, and then rose to KRW 94.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 101 billion in 2025. However, the operating margin dropped from 17.5% in 2024 to 10.3% in 2025, so profit did not keep pace with sales growth.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a loss of KRW 55.2 billion in 2022 and a loss of KRW 27.3 billion in 2023 to a profit of KRW 18.1 billion in 2024, and reached KRW 29.5 billion in 2025, up 62.8%.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 76.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 74.9 billion in 2025, which is well above the profit attributable to controlling shareholders. Quarterly revenue climbed from KRW 245.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 370 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Operating profit moved from KRW 22.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 13.1 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, KRW 18.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 19.5 billion in the second quarter, and KRW 16.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Attributable net profit swung from a loss of KRW 11.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to a profit of KRW 21.5 billion in the second quarter and a loss of KRW 26.8 billion in the third quarter.

According to a 2026 first-half summary on the Wisereport company page, semiconductor sales rose on AI data center investment and demand for high-performance memory, while early investment in the fuel-cell business and losses in AI and IT services weighed on operating profit.

Over the four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, the combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 12.3 billion. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 120.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 24.8 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 94.3% in 2022 to 228.6% in 2025, reflecting heavier leverage.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from downstream markets centers on AI data center investment and the expansion of high-bandwidth memory (HBM).

According to fnguide's summary of cumulative results through the third quarter of 2025, demand for ceramic parts used in HBM manufacturing equipment and for deposition and etching equipment parts tied to finer process nodes lifted results. Signs of recovery also appear in memory.

Micron Technology said in its fiscal third-quarter 2026 release on June 24, 2026 that revenue rose sharply from a year earlier. Micron's figures are those of a memory maker, so whether they translate directly into MiCo parts demand needs separate confirmation.

In the first half of 2026, semiconductor sales rose while operating profit fell, which suggests that higher parts demand has not yet translated into better margins. On the fuel-cell side, a Jaeggyeong Ilbo report named MiCo as a key related stock in the SOFC theme.

Whether that market interest converts into revenue depends on capacity utilization and order results. On competition, MiCo combines in-house ceramic materials work, including silicon nitride powder, with a large export share in its parts business.

Semiconductor equipment parts remain exposed to the investment cycle of chip equipment makers, so swings in memory capital spending remain a demand variable.

06

Outlook

The expansion plans the company has disclosed fall into four areas: fuel-cell capacity, materials localization, overseas production, and a restructuring of subsidiaries.

In the fuel-cell business, according to the cumulative results update for the third quarter of 2025, the company was pursuing a 10MW expansion in Anseong in 2025 and setting up a 30MW production system at a new Pyeongtaek plant in 2026.

If that capacity runs, the SOFC revenue base widens, but because early investment costs weighed on operating profit in the first half of 2026, the timing of when expansion shows up in earnings matters.

On materials, the mass production setup for silicon nitride powder is a confirmed fact and matters for in-house sourcing of raw materials for ceramic parts.

HPS's Sacheon heat recovery steam generator line and Vietnam production base were announced on March 25, 2026, and whether supply-chain diversification turns into orders is the next checkpoint.

A decision to absorb the subsidiary MiCo Hitech through a merger has also been disclosed, and the company says the merger will improve business efficiency. However, whether new growth businesses such as SOFC produce tangible results remains the focus of market attention.

In semiconductors, the key question is whether parts demand linked to HBM and finer process nodes continues. Progress against capacity plans can be checked in annual figures in the 2026 annual report.

Bull factors

Demand from AI and HBM equipment

The semiconductor segment's first-half 2026 sales rose on AI data center investment and stronger demand for high-performance memory. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 370 billion was up 6.0% from the prior quarter and 50.8% from a year earlier.

Revenue has grown for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, showing that this demand is reaching quarterly results.

Fuel-cell capacity expansion

The fuel-cell unit pursued a 10MW expansion in Anseong in 2025 and set up a 30MW production system at a new Pyeongtaek plant in 2026. All 2024 SOFC sales were domestic. Fuel cells account for roughly half of revenue alongside semiconductors, so a working expansion would broaden the business mix.

In-house materials and supply-chain diversification

The company announced on March 19, 2026 that it had localized silicon nitride powder and set up mass production. On March 25, 2026, subsidiary HPS outlined an HRSG production line centered on its Sacheon plant and a Vietnam production base.

A decision to absorb MiCo Hitech through a merger has also been disclosed, as the group reorganizes materials and production structure.

09

Bear factors

Lower operating profit and a return to quarterly losses

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 16.3 billion, down 27.3% from a year earlier. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 26.8 billion turned a KRW 1.65 billion profit from the third quarter of 2025 into a loss.

Over the four quarters through the third quarter of 2026, the combined attributable net loss totals KRW 12.3 billion, and revenue growth has not yet produced profit gains.

Margin compression and early investment in new businesses

The operating margin fell from 17.5% in 2024 to 10.3% in 2025. Information-service summaries of first-half 2026 results attribute part of the operating profit decline to early fuel-cell investment and losses in AI and IT services. Until new capacity runs at scale, fixed costs could keep weighing on margins.

Higher leverage and slower cash generation

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 94.3% in 2022 to 228.6% in 2025, and total liabilities at year-end 2025 reached KRW 1.58 trillion. The decline in operating cash flow leaves less internally generated cash to absorb the higher debt load.

Equity attributable to controlling shareholders is much smaller than total equity, which weighs on the cushion available against debt.

10

Risk factors

Demand cycle

If chip equipment investment swings with the memory cycle, demand for ceramic parts and cleaning and coating services can move with it. Whether HBM demand keeps expanding is the key variable for parts orders.

Operating profit fell in the first half of 2026 even as sales grew, suggesting that demand changes may pass through to profit slowly.

Leverage and cash flow

A debt-to-equity ratio of 228.6% and total liabilities of KRW 1.58 trillion leave the company exposed to interest rates and refinancing conditions. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 24.8 billion in 2025, which reduces the room to fund equipment and fuel-cell capacity from operating cash.

A large non-controlling interest relative to controlling shareholders' equity also widens the swings in profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

New-business execution

Whether the SOFC expansions in Anseong and Pyeongtaek and the subsidiary merger turn into actual profit is still unproven. Because early fuel-cell investment has pressured 2026 operating profit, a delay in capacity utilization or orders could extend the loss pattern. Market interest in the SOFC theme should be distinguished from actual business results.

Overall view

MiCo expanded its top line after the 2023 low, with both revenue and operating profit rising in 2024 and 2025. However, the operating margin fell from 17.5% in 2024 to 10.3% in 2025, and the third quarter of 2026 brought a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 26.8 billion.

The semiconductor segment's quarterly revenue has risen for four consecutive quarters on AI and HBM-related demand, but profit has not followed the same path.

Fuel cells, where Anseong and Pyeongtaek expansions coincide with interest in the SOFC theme, form a new growth pillar, yet early investment costs weighed on operating profit in the first half of 2026.

On the financial side, the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 228.6%, and operating cash flow fell to KRW 24.8 billion in 2025. Strengths include demand exposure, capacity expansion, and in-house materials, while weaknesses include margin decline and balance-sheet burden.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and the start-up timing of the new Pyeongtaek plant.
