DAEMO: Profit Recovery in 2025, Operating Loss in Q3 2026

02

Business structure

DAEMO Engineering makes attachments mounted on excavators, including breakers and quick couplers. A 2020 securities research report described the company as a maker of excavator attachments such as breakers and quick couplers, and noted that it also pursued maintenance and rental businesses.

More recently, the company has expanded its lineup with excavator tuning equipment and smart reader devices. It has set a target of becoming a comprehensive maker of special-purpose construction equipment by 2030.

Because its sales depend on construction activity in its markets, it has pursued overseas markets and gradually raised its export share through trade-show participation and technology exchanges with global leaders.

Revenue follows the construction equipment cycle, and the company attributed the 2025 revenue decline to a global construction equipment downturn caused by the Russia-Ukraine war and inflation. Over the same year, it credited a stronger exchange rate and cost reductions for higher operating profit.

Attachment demand tends to move with excavator activity, and the company is widening its product range toward tuning equipment and special-purpose machines.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 54.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 44.4 billion in 2023 (down 19.0% year on year), KRW 41.3 billion in 2024 (down 7.0%), and KRW 40.7 billion in 2025 (down 1.4%). Revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest among 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit moved from KRW 2.13 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 378 million in 2023, a swing back to profit at KRW 117 million in 2024, and KRW 1.91 billion in 2025 (up 1,535.4%). The operating margin was 3.9% in 2022, -0.9% in 2023, 0.3% in 2024, and 4.7% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.29 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 244 million in 2023, KRW 132 million in 2024, and KRW 1.89 billion in 2025 (up 1,335.5%). The company attributed the 2025 profit increase to a stronger exchange rate and cost reductions.

Operating cash flow was KRW 2.57 billion in 2025, after -KRW 395 million in 2024. Quarterly revenue went from KRW 8.8 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 9.68 billion in Q4 2025 (up 10.0% quarter on quarter), then peaked at KRW 11.7 billion in Q1 2026 (up 21.3%).

Revenue then fell to KRW 11.6 billion in Q2 2026 (down 1.3%) and KRW 9.48 billion in Q3 2026 (down 18.2%), though Q3 was still up 7.7% year on year. Operating loss was KRW 118 million in Q3 2025 and KRW 489 million in Q4 2025.

Operating profit reached KRW 1.02 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 974 million in Q2 2026 (down 4.4%). Q3 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 9.49 million, which was narrower than a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.12 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 1.05 billion in Q2 2026, then a net loss of KRW 1.06 billion in Q3 2026. Over the last four quarters, that line totaled KRW 1.01 billion.

Revenue has been shrinking while the operating margin recovered, and quarterly profit is moving in opposite directions with wider swings.

Industry analysis

The construction equipment market slowed in 2025 as the Russia-Ukraine war and inflation weighed on demand, and the company's revenue followed the same trend. A stronger exchange rate supported earnings that year, and results for exporters move with the direction of the currency.

Within the machinery and equipment sector, Doosan Bobcat and HD Construction Equipment make complete construction machines, while DAEMO focuses more on attachments and tuning equipment. With annual revenue in the KRW 40 billion range, the company is far smaller than full-machine makers in scale.

Whether the declines in Q2 and Q3 2026 reflect a weaker industry or lower shipment volumes should be separated using the company's explanations and the next regular filings. Whether the construction cycle is recovering will show up in how Q4 2026 and Q1 2027 revenue compare with the prior-year quarters.

Because attachment demand moves with excavator activity, the key industry question is whether a recovery in excavator demand carries through to attachment sales. The 4.7% operating margin in 2025 is the company's own level, and a profitability comparison with full-machine peers needs filings prepared on the same basis.

Revenue came down from KRW 11.7 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 11.6 billion in Q2 and KRW 9.48 billion in Q3, so whether Q4 revenue stabilizes near the Q3 level is the next checkpoint. Financial capacity and cash generation

Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 48.1 billion against total liabilities of KRW 16.7 billion, a debt ratio of 34.7%. The debt ratio fell from 46.4% in 2022 to 32.4% in 2024. Operating cash flow was KRW 2.57 billion in 2025.

Lineup expansion and medium-term target

The company has expanded its lineup with excavator tuning equipment and smart reader devices. With a target of becoming a comprehensive special-purpose construction equipment maker by 2030, it is widening its business beyond attachments.

Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 11.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.02 billion marked the highest quarter in the recent series.

Overall view

DAEMO moved from an operating loss in 2023 to a profit in 2024 and recovered to KRW 1.91 billion in operating profit in 2025. Revenue, however, has declined for three consecutive years to KRW 40.7 billion in 2025, and it fell in Q2 and Q3 2026 after peaking in Q1 2026.

The Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 9.49 million and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.06 billion raise questions about whether the recovery can last. The balance sheet looks stable, with a debt ratio of 34.7% and total equity of KRW 48.1 billion, and operating cash flow was positive in 2025.

Strengths include the profit recovery, product lineup expansion, and financial capacity, while weaknesses include falling revenue, the return to quarterly losses, and reliance on exchange rates and the construction cycle.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual report, and the Q1 2027 quarterly report. This report does not include an investment opinion and presents the two sets of factors with equal weight.
