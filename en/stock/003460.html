02

Business structure

Yuhwa Securities was founded in 1962 and engages in investment trading, investment brokerage and discretionary investment management, according to Alphasquare's company profile. Interest income is the largest revenue component.

WiseReport's company monitor counted interest income at 45.76% of operating revenue on a standalone basis for the first half of 2026 and described bond trading and credit lending as the stable earnings base. Alphasquare notes that dividend income and gains on valuation and disposal of securities follow behind.

Butler, using second-quarter 2026 revenue, shows a split of 51% retail and 49% product trading. The two breakdowns define segments differently, so the shares should not be overlaid directly; the common thread is that interest, trading gains and brokerage fees move earnings together.

On safety, WiseReport's company monitor puts the net capital ratio at 1,131.69%, and Alphasquare describes the firm's safe-asset-centered investment approach as among the highest in the industry for stability.

Compared with large brokerages that widen revenue through investment banking and fee businesses, Yuhwa's smaller scale means that a single quarter's trading result carries a large weight in total earnings.

04

Earnings analysis

Looking at disclosed annual results, operating results moved from losses to profit and then declined. Operating losses were KRW 2.88 billion in 2022 and KRW 430 million in 2023, and the 2023 loss narrowed from the year before.

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 12.2 billion in 2024, and 2025 operating profit was KRW 5.92 billion, down 51.5% year on year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 4.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.34 billion in 2023 (up 74.8%) and KRW 17.8 billion in 2024 (up 142.3%), then fell to KRW 14.1 billion in 2025 (down 20.9%). Among 2022–2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts in each year, KRW 4.2 billion, KRW 7.34 billion, KRW 17.8 billion and KRW 14.1 billion.

In all four years, net income exceeded operating profit, which points to non-operating items supporting earnings; the breakdown of those items is found in the notes to the periodic filings. On the balance sheet, the debt ratio moved from 38.3% in 2022 to 58.7% in 2023, 50.9% in 2024 and 50.7% in 2025.

Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 537.9 billion and total liabilities were KRW 272.6 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 40.8 billion in 2022, minus KRW 41.7 billion in 2023, minus KRW 13.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 68 billion in 2025; for brokerages, operating cash flow typically swings with changes in trading assets.

Quarterly, operating profit was KRW 5.17 billion in Q3 2025, then turned to an operating loss of KRW 3.91 billion in Q4, returned to profit at KRW 10.6 billion in Q1 2026, fell into an operating loss of KRW 2.43 billion in Q2, and returned to profit at KRW 3.47 billion in Q3 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 11.2 billion in Q1 2026 within the last five quarters, followed by KRW 706 million in Q2 and KRW 5.28 billion in Q3. The four-quarter total was KRW 16.6 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Brokerage earnings move with trading value, interest rates and market volatility, and Yuhwa Securities is exposed to the same forces because interest income and trading gains make up much of its revenue. Its semi-annual report for the first half of 2025 recorded brokerage trade volume of KRW 2.687 trillion.

In its Q3 2026 disclosure, one mid-sized brokerage reported that brokerage commissions fell from the prior quarter while financial product sales hit a quarterly high. The same period also saw weaker equity markets and higher rates reduce asset management income.

This pattern shows financial product sales and trading results holding up earnings as transaction-based income slows. Yuhwa's Q3 2026 operating profit, however, fell 32.9% year on year, so company-level results diverged within the same market.

In Alphasquare's annual ranking, Yuhwa ranked 19th by market capitalization and 21st by revenue among 21 securities companies. That places it at the smaller end of the sector, and the scale gap with large brokerages is a structural factor in its earnings sensitivity.

Outlook

Yuhwa Securities' earnings outlook depends mainly on the direction of trading value, interest rates and trading gains. For a brokerage, results are driven more by market conditions than by scheduled business events such as contract wins or capacity expansions.

Since operating profit of KRW 10.6 billion in Q1 2026, quarterly results have alternated between profit and loss, and Q3 2026 net income of KRW 5.28 billion was above the KRW 711 million of Q2.

The four-quarter total of KRW 16.6 billion exceeds the KRW 14.1 billion reported for full-year 2025 on the same measure, which suggests the most recent four quarters partly offset the 2025 decline. The windows differ, so this does not establish a continuing upward trend.

Total equity of KRW 537.9 billion at end-2025 gives the firm a capital base to absorb earnings swings. Q4 2026 results have not yet reached the periodic filing stage. In the 2026 annual report, the next filing, full-year operating margin and operating cash flow are the next checkpoints.

With quarterly profit alternating between profit and loss, the annual total and the quarterly trend need to be read together.

Risk factors

Market and interest-rate risk

If trading value falls or the rate direction shifts, interest income and valuation and disposal gains can move together. Brokerage earnings tend to rely on several income lines that move in the same direction. That structure widens the swings in both upturns and slowdowns.

Reliance on non-operating income

In each of the four years from 2022 to 2025, net income exceeded operating profit. Non-operating items therefore supported reported earnings, and whether those items recur is a matter for the periodic-filing notes. Operating profit and net income tell different stories about earnings capacity.

Scale and disclosure-timing risk

Alphasquare's annual ranking places Yuhwa 19th of 21 securities companies by market capitalization, reflecting its small scale. Q4 2026 results have not yet reached the periodic filing stage, so the latest quarter is still pending formal disclosure. Quarterly figures can change until the periodic report is finalized.
