Report overview

Lotte Reit Adds Hotels; Refinancing Is the Test

Lotte Reit grew operating profit for three straight years from 2023 to 2025 on master leases with Lotte group affiliates. However, buying L7 Hongdae in 2026 added debt, and close to half of its total borrowings mature in 2027. That makes refinancing rates the key variable for dividend stability.

  1. 1

    Reported revenue rose from KRW 57.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 70.9 billion in 2025, and operating profit from KRW 34.1 billion to KRW 46.3 billion. Both grew for three straight years from 2023, and operating margin widened from 58.9% to 65.3%.

  2. 2

    Net profit attributable to owners fell to KRW 3.18 billion in 2023 as financing costs surged. It then rose for two straight years in 2024-2025 on lower-rate refinancing, reaching KRW 19.6 billion in 2025.

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Lotte Reit is an externally managed REIT. It buys commercial real estate owned by the Lotte group, leases it long-term to affiliates and pays out the rental income as dividends. Its largest shareholder is Lotte Shopping with a 42.04% stake, and it master-leases 17 assets to Lotte Shopping and other affiliates.

The assets include department stores, outlets and marts, with a combined purchase price of about KRW 2.9 trillion. Its retail and logistics assets are master-leased to Lotte Shopping and Lotte Global Logistics. Under triple-net terms, the tenants pay maintenance, insurance and taxes.

For ten assets in certain tranches, rent rises by a fixed 1.5% every October. The portfolio expanded into hotels and offices through the 2024 acquisitions of L7 Gangnam Tower and a stake in Gangnam DF Tower. It expanded further with the KRW 265 billion purchase of L7 Hongdae in January 2026.

Non-retail assets such as hotels and offices now exceed 20% of the portfolio. NH Investment & Securities said Hotel Lotte's role as master lessee has made rental income more stable. The REIT closes its books semi-annually in June and December and pays dividends twice a year.

Among competitors, it belongs with sponsor-backed conglomerate REITs such as SK Reit and Hanwha REIT, which stand out for their group asset pipelines and funding capacity.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, revenue grew for three straight years: KRW 57.9 billion in 2022, KRW 59.4 billion in 2023 (+2.6% YoY), KRW 65.6 billion in 2024 (+10.5%) and KRW 70.9 billion in 2025 (+8.0%).

Operating profit also grew for three straight years, from KRW 34.1 billion to KRW 36.1 billion, KRW 42 billion and KRW 46.3 billion. Operating margin widened from 58.9% to 65.3% because, under the triple-net structure, costs do not rise in step with rent.

The larger jump in 2024 appears to reflect new acquisitions such as L7 Gangnam Tower. Net profit attributable to owners fell 77.5%, from KRW 14.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.18 billion in 2023.

The cause was financing costs, which swelled to KRW 28.3 billion in the first half of 2023 and KRW 32.2 billion in the second half. It then grew for two straight years, to KRW 10.6 billion in 2024 (+234.0%) and KRW 19.6 billion in 2025 (+84.9%).

The company said its average funding rate fell from 4.14% at end-2024 to 3.52% at end-2025. Over the four quarters from 3Q25 to 2Q26, net profit attributable to owners totaled KRW 20.5 billion, slightly above the 2025 level. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 86.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 64 billion in 2025, moving opposite to earnings.

05

Industry analysis

As of end-November 2025, Korea had 25 listed REITs with total assets of about KRW 19.2 trillion. Credit risk in the Korean REIT market rose after JR Global Reit filed for rehabilitation in late April 2026. By Shinhan Securities' count, listed Korean REITs returned -7.7% year-to-date as of May 14, 2026.

REITs focused on overseas assets fell the most. Conglomerate-sponsored REITs, by contrast, were credited with defending their dividends. NH Investment & Securities also said having a sponsor is becoming more important in the REIT market. Funding conditions, however, have worsened from 2025.

Lotte Reit's secured bonds issued in August 2026 priced at 4.36% for one year and 4.75% for two years. The one-year rate was close to the 4.38% on Hanwha REIT's unsecured one-year bond issued in September.

Offline retail, its main end market, has stalled, which is a structural challenge for a REIT with a large retail weighting.

06

Outlook

The company has issued annual dividend-per-share guidance for 2026 and 2027. If met, the dividend would rise 12.9% in 2026 and 7.7% in 2027. In 2024, it set targets of KRW 4.5 trillion in AUM and KRW 220 billion in revenue by 2030.

After refinancing KRW 701 billion in 2026, it must refinance close to half of its borrowings again in 2027. The company plans to raise the fixed-rate share to above 50% and spread 2027 maturities out to 2029-2030.

It intends to absorb higher interest costs with rent increases and cash on hand to keep the 2027 dividend stable. Cash and equivalents stood at KRW 56.9 billion at end-June, up 51% from end-2025. That is more than enough to cover the KRW 40.2 billion 15th-period dividend.

NH Investment & Securities expects the REIT to keep finding new sectors with affiliates, such as logistics centers, data centers and senior housing. No specific timeline for further group asset acquisitions has been disclosed.

08

Bull factors

Affiliate master leases and triple-net terms

Lotte Shopping, Lotte Global Logistics and Hotel Lotte master-lease the assets, so vacancy is effectively not a concern. Tenants bear operating costs, which helped lift operating margin from 58.9% in 2022 to 65.3% in 2025. Contractual rent escalators make the annual revenue path easy to predict. The resilience of sponsor-backed REITs during rate swings also ties back to this structure.

Track record of meeting dividend guidance

After the 2023 dividend cut, refinancing helped net profit attributable to owners rise for two straight years. In the 15th period, part of the proceeds from a site sale was added as a special dividend, putting the payout above guidance.

First-half rental income rose 2.1% from the previous half even excluding L7 Hongdae. This shows the dividend base is growing from existing assets as well.

Diversification and asset value cushion

With non-retail assets such as hotels and offices now above 20%, dependence on retail has fallen. The company plans to raise this share to 30%. Investment property fair value exceeds purchase cost, leaving room for additional collateral and for gains if assets are sold. Group hotels, logistics centers and data centers are mentioned as acquisition candidates.

09

Bear factors

Heavy 2027 maturities

Much of the KRW 748 billion maturing in 2027 was raised at low rates in the past. Refinancing the KRW 160 billion of secured bonds maturing in April 2027, now at 3.26%, at 4.36% would add about KRW 1.76 billion in annual interest by simple math. Higher interest costs would work against the recovery in net profit attributable to owners.

Rising leverage

The debt-to-equity ratio rose every year, from 113.4% in 2022 to 127.7% in 2025. Media reports put it at 154.7% at end-June 2026, after the L7 Hongdae acquisition. Total borrowings grew from KRW 1.307 trillion to KRW 1.5743 trillion, and debt dependence rose from 50.4% to 54.8%. Funding further acquisitions with debt could add to the financial burden.

Floating-rate exposure

Floating-rate borrowings total KRW 912.5 billion, or 58.2% of the total. This helped cut costs when rates fell, but interest on existing loans also rises when market rates climb. The company is moving to raise its fixed-rate share, but the switch may require accepting higher fixed rates.

10

Risk factors

Rates and funding

Since the JR Global Reit episode, the market has set a higher bar for REIT credit quality. Even secured funding did not offer a clear rate advantage. If refinancing rates come in high, more cash on hand would be needed to maintain dividends. That would shrink the medium-term dividend base.

Tenant and retail exposure

Most assets are Lotte Shopping stores, so tenant concentration is high. If offline retail weakens, lease renewal terms or store restructuring could become issues. Part of the rent on the hotel assets is linked to room revenue, which exposes them to shifts in tourism demand.

Governance and dilution

Because assets are bought from sponsor affiliates, whether purchase prices and lease terms are fair directly affects shareholder value. The L7 Gangnam Tower acquisition came with a KRW 164 billion rights offering. Reaching the 2030 AUM target may require further equity raises. Any such raise would dilute existing shareholders.

    The 15th-period dividend (KRW 40.2 billion in total) is paid on this date. The cash left afterward will be the yardstick for the REIT's capacity to defend the 2027 dividend.

  2. Mid-November 2026

  3. Early February 2027

    The 16th-period dividend decision and record-date notice are expected around this time. They will show whether the company met its 2026 annual dividend guidance.

  4. April 2027

    KRW 160 billion of secured bonds at 3.26% mature. Their refinancing rate and tenor will be the first test of the KRW 748 billion in 2027 refinancing.

12

Overall view

Thanks to affiliate master leases and triple-net terms, Lotte Reit grew revenue and operating profit for three straight years from 2023. Operating margin also rose to 65.3%. Net profit attributable to owners, which fell in 2023 as financing costs surged, recovered to KRW 19.6 billion in 2025 on refinancing.

Hotel acquisitions lifted non-retail assets above 20%, changing the portfolio mix. However, the same acquisitions added debt, and close to half of total borrowings must be refinanced in 2027. With a high floating-rate share, market rate moves feed directly into earnings.

Going forward, refinancing rates, the pace of the shift to fixed rates and whether dividend guidance is met will be the key evidence for assessing the company. This report is for information only and contains no buy or sell recommendation.
