02

Business structure

GA Innodus was spun off from Hyundai Engineering & Construction in 1998 and has built its offshore construction capabilities on an underwater dredging license.

It now holds seven specialty construction licenses, which allow it to carry out land works such as ground improvement, paving, and reinforced concrete alongside underwater projects.

Its in-house fleet of 18 units includes a 20,000-ton floating dock, a revolving crane, and jumbo drills, and it owns one of only two 20,000-horsepower dredgers in Korea.

Ports and maritime infrastructure form the core of the business, with channel dredging, breakwater construction, and container wharf works at Jinhae New Port expanding in stages. Offshore foundation work for Gadeok Airport and dredging for the Saemangeum reclamation are cited as medium- to long-term volume sources.

In offshore wind, the company handled the installation of 18 jacket-type structures at the Southwest Sea offshore wind demonstration complex, which Hyundai Engineering & Construction served as EPC contractor.

Its main customers are Hyundai Engineering & Construction and DL E&C, so its results track the ordering patterns of these prime contractors. In the 2023 construction capability ranking compiled by the Korea Specialty Contractors Association, its underwater dredging category placed fourth among 441 firms nationwide.

Its order backlog at the end of 2024 stood at KRW 264.7 billion, equal to about 1.47 times annual sales, and included large projects such as national road construction ordered by the Busan regional land management office.

03

Recent trends

According to a summary of the 2025 business-year audit report, standalone sales were KRW 138.9 billion, operating profit was KRW 27.7 billion (27,161 million won in the filing's unit), and net income was KRW 79.1 billion.

Sales fell from KRW 193.1 billion in 2023 to KRW 143.2 billion in 2024, a 25.8% drop, and declined a further 3.0% in 2025, extending the downturn to three years.

Operating profit rose 4.0% in 2025, but net income fell 45.7%, and the 2024 net income included a gain on disposal of property, plant and equipment of about KRW 10.5 billion. On a consolidated basis, 2025 sales were reported at KRW 181.3 billion, operating profit at KRW 3.9 billion, and net income at KRW 7.9 billion.

At the end of 2025, equity stood at KRW 97,272 million and total assets at KRW 116,581 million. At the March 27, 2026 annual general meeting, a cash dividend of KRW 200 per common share was approved, with a total payout of KRW 821 million.

The 2025 payout ratio was reported at 10.4%, and a May 2026 press report described the shareholder return as rare among KONEX-listed companies. On November 4, 2026, the board resolved to invest KRW 27,855 million in hotel development in Yeoksam-dong, Gangnam-gu, equal to 28.64% of equity.

Market capitalization is about KRW 20.4 billion, and as of September 2026 the one-year share price return was -22.79%, with a 52-week range of KRW 3,800 to KRW 5,610.

04

Outlook

Large port and offshore projects, including Jinhae New Port and Gadeok Airport, are cited as the basis for medium- to long-term volume. The company emphasizes selective bidding focused on higher-margin projects to reduce earnings volatility.

The order backlog of KRW 264.7 billion at the end of 2024, about 1.47 times sales, serves as a reference point for near-term revenue.

Conversely, the rise in insolvencies among large builders and reduced ordering has been identified as a factor that lowers operating profit, making the ordering environment a key variable.

In March 2026, hotel operations were added to the company's articles of incorporation, explicitly citing business diversification and new revenue sources. The Yeoksam-dong hotel development runs from November 4, 2026 to December 14, 2028, with a planned total investment of KRW 27,855 million.

The filing states that funding will come from internal cash and borrowings from financial institutions, and that construction costs may change with design revisions, permits, and price movements.

Going forward, the pace of hotel construction and the scale of borrowing will be the main points to watch for their effect on financial structure indicators.

In offshore works, the key checkpoints are whether dredging and breakwater volumes at Jinhae New Port increase in stages and whether reclamation work for Gadeok converts into contracts. Whether the sales decline halts in the 2026 business-year audit report is another item to track.

Bull factors

Debt-free balance sheet

Borrowing dependence was 0.0% and the debt-to-equity ratio was 19.9% at the end of 2025. The company was reported to have operated without borrowings in 2024, with a current ratio of 316.1%. With equity of KRW 97,272 million, the low debt burden is read as a financial cushion. Standalone cash and cash equivalents at the end of 2025 were reported at about KRW 11 billion.

Large port and offshore projects

Dredging, breakwater, and container wharf works at Jinhae New Port are expanding in stages. Dredging for Phase 3 of the Saemangeum industrial complex reclamation was completed, and Phase 8 was reported to be nearing completion.

Participation in offshore foundation work for Gadeok Airport is presented as a medium- to long-term order base. The order backlog of KRW 264.7 billion at the end of 2024 equals 1.47 times sales.

Shareholder return record

The payout ratio was 7.4% in 2023, 4.0% in 2024, and 10.4% in 2025. In October 2024, a tender offer for 993,000 treasury shares was conducted, and in December 2025 100,000 shares were contributed to an in-house employee welfare fund. The company received the top Value-up Award at the 13th Korea KONEX Awards in 2026. The March 2026 annual general meeting approved a dividend of KRW 200 per common share.

06

Bear factors

Three straight years of sales decline

Sales fell from KRW 193.1 billion in 2023 to KRW 143.2 billion in 2024 and declined again to KRW 138.9 billion in 2025. Net income in 2025 was KRW 79.1 billion, down 45.7% year on year.

The 2024 net income included a gain of about KRW 10.5 billion on disposal of property, plant and equipment, so underlying core profitability warrants a conservative read.

Lower orders and prime contractor concentration

The insolvency of large construction companies and reduced orders have been cited as factors that weigh on operating profit. Key customers are concentrated among Hyundai Engineering & Construction and DL E&C, so ordering adjustments by either company feed directly into results. As Saemangeum dredging nears completion, new contracts are needed to replace that volume.

Thin liquidity and share price trend

Market capitalization is about KRW 20.4 billion, and the free float ratio is 31.74%. As of September 2026, the largest shareholder group held 48.22% and treasury shares accounted for 15.71%. The one-year share price return was -22.79%, and trading value is currently recorded at zero. Liquidity constraints are pronounced, consistent with a small KONEX-listed company.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Port and offshore works are sensitive to the size and execution pace of government social overhead capital budgets. Ordering timelines can accelerate or slip depending on national infrastructure budget allocations.

Because the hotel investment is partly funded by borrowings, financing costs will vary with the interest rate environment.

Concentration

Sales are concentrated among a small number of prime contractors, including Hyundai Engineering & Construction and DL E&C. The completion timing of one or two large projects can translate directly into earnings swings. Offshore work can be delayed by weather and tidal conditions at the site.

Investment

The KRW 27,855 million hotel investment equals 28.64% of equity, making it a large commitment relative to the balance sheet. The Yeoksam-dong building received its occupancy approval in 1989, and press reports cite an external accounting firm rating its management condition as poor. The filing notes that total investment may change with design revisions, permits, and price movements.

08

Overall view

GA Innodus has built offshore and port construction capabilities and maintains a debt-free balance sheet, but sales have declined for three consecutive years since 2023. Standalone sales of KRW 138.9 billion and net income of KRW 79.1 billion for the 2025 business year point to a smaller core business.

Large offshore projects such as Jinhae New Port and Gadeok Airport form a medium- to long-term order base, but earnings may swing sharply depending on ordering timing and customer concentration.

The KRW 27,855 million hotel development signals a diversification direction while also bringing funding and construction-related financial burdens. Shareholder returns have included a dividend increase and a treasury share tender offer, and the company received a value-up award at the 2026 KONEX Awards.

Conversely, market capitalization of about KRW 20.4 billion, a free float ratio of 31.74%, and zero recorded trading value underscore the liquidity constraints typical of small KONEX stocks.

With bullish and bearish factors coexisting, tracking the recovery in results and the outcome of the hotel project on a fact basis remains necessary.
