Report overview

A Dredging Specialist Branches Into Real Estate

GA Innodus is a Konex-listed specialty contractor built on top-tier dredging capacity and a low debt ratio, and its 33 billion won property purchase and treasury-share policy have become key variables for its business structure.

02

Business structure

GA Innodus is a marine civil engineering contractor that was founded in 1998 and listed on the Konex market in 2015. Its origin traces to a unit spun off from Hyundai Engineering & Construction in 1989. The business covers underwater works, dredging, port structure installation and land reclamation.

Its construction subsidiary was established in 2001. Its main clients were reported to be Hyundai Engineering & Construction and DL E&C. Its competitive edge centers on proprietary dredgers.

The 20,000-horsepower dredger GA-P20000 is the most powerful in Korea, and the company also operates another contractor's 20,000-horsepower dredger and DL E&C's 2,300-horsepower dredger under management agreements.

Among specialty contractors, it ranked fourth in underwater and dredging works by 2024 construction capability value of KRW 238.7 billion, and ranked first on the construction-performance component alone. A segment revenue breakdown could not be confirmed in this research.

As an order-driven business, revenue depends on tender timing and prime-contractor projects, which adds volatility.

03

Recent trends

Per filings, consolidated revenue for 2025 was KRW 181,251 million, consolidated operating profit was KRW 3,948 million and consolidated net profit was KRW 7,909 million. Standalone revenue was KRW 138,947 million and standalone operating profit was KRW 2,772 million.

Standalone revenue fell 3.0% from a year earlier and is well below the KRW 193,075 million recorded in 2023. Consolidated operating profit for 2024 was reported at KRW 5.9 billion, so the 2025 figure is lower.

One data provider pointed to the insolvency of large builders and fewer tenders as the backdrop to the weak 2025 operating profit. Net profit exceeds operating profit, but the breakdown of non-operating items could not be confirmed.

On the balance sheet, consolidated total liabilities were KRW 37,549 million against total equity of KRW 97,272 million, and the regular credit rating assigned on March 28, 2026 was BBB+.

In the market, the 52-week range was 3,800 to 5,610 won, while the price at the time of writing is 4,195 won and market capitalization is KRW 20.4 billion. One data provider shows a PER of 2.64 and a PBR of 0.18, which are that provider's own calculations. First-half 2026 results could not be confirmed.

04

Outlook

Port and airport infrastructure projects are cited as medium- to long-term demand drivers. One data provider wrote that the Jinhae New Port and Gadeokdo New Airport projects are gaining momentum and should broaden medium- to long-term demand.

At Saemangeum, dredging at Section 3 is complete and Section 8 is in its final stage, so winning follow-on tenders will shape the revenue path. The company says it is reducing earnings volatility through selective bidding focused on high-margin projects.

On new business, an amendment to its articles on March 27, 2026 added the hotel business to its stated purposes. The property purchase was interpreted as a long-term investment aimed at improving asset value, but specific plans such as leasing or redevelopment could not be confirmed.

On shareholder returns, the company has said it will review its policy in stages, based on earnings improvement and stronger financial stability.

Because an extraordinary shareholders' meeting on November 25, 2026 will consider the treasury-share holding and disposal plan, the outcome and details could affect the shareholder structure.

05

Bull factors

Hard-to-replace dredging assets

Only two 20,000-horsepower dredgers exist in Korea, and the company owns one. The marine construction market was described as seeing demand rise with larger vessels and new shipping routes. The company also ranks near the top in underwater and dredging capability rankings. The equipment barrier is often cited as a defensive element of the business.

Low leverage and a shareholder-return record

At the end of 2025, the standalone debt ratio was 19.9%. The dividend was approved at 200 won per common share, totaling KRW 821.25 million. The company also carried out an interim dividend in 2024 and a tender offer for its own shares. This record earned it the top Value-up Award at the 13th Korea Konex Awards.

Large infrastructure projects and order base

In November 2024 the company won a KRW 120 billion earthwork structure contract for a national road together with DL E&C. It set a 2025 order target of KRW 200 billion. Long-cycle projects such as Jinhae New Port and Gadeokdo New Airport are under way. Whether the target was met could not be confirmed in this research.

06

Bear factors

Shrinking top line and thin operating margin

Standalone revenue declined from KRW 193,075 million in 2023 to KRW 138,947 million in 2025. The standalone operating margin is thin at 2.0% in 2025. One analysis noted that shrinking work in its strongest areas of the domestic SOC market led to two straight years of revenue decline. If tenders do not recover, fixed-cost burdens could weigh on earnings.

Funding burden and delay in the property deal

The Mijoo Plaza purchase was put at 34.23% of total assets. Of the KRW 33 billion total, the KRW 22.8 billion balance had its payment date pushed to May 25, 2026. The cause of the delay was an ownership dispute.

Because the asset has limited links to the core business, views differ on cash liquidity and capital allocation efficiency. Whether the balance was paid and registration completed could not be confirmed in this research.

Thin liquidity and concentrated ownership

As a Konex stock, trading is thin. Per the data provider, the free-float ratio is 31.74%, and trading at the time of writing was limited. The largest shareholder group holds 48.22% and treasury shares account for 15.71%. With a small tradable supply, price gaps and swings can be pronounced.

07

Risk factors

Order and industry

Revenue relies heavily on prime contractors such as Hyundai Engineering & Construction and DL E&C and on public tenders. One data provider assessed that the insolvency of large builders and fewer tenders weighed on operating profit.

Delays in follow-on Saemangeum sections and in new port and airport work could disrupt utilization and revenue. The financial health of prime contractors could also affect settlements and orders.

Asset and financial

Funding and paying the KRW 22.8 billion property balance is large compared with cash of about KRW 11 billion at the end of 2024. The cash balance after 2025 could not be confirmed in this research. Once the acquisition closes, consolidated debt and interest burdens could change. Uncertain property value and use plans are additional variables.

Governance and market

Governance changed after the company bought back all shares held by Hyundai Engineering & Construction in a tender offer and the CEO became the largest shareholder.

At the November 25, 2026 extraordinary meeting, a proposal to approve the treasury-share holding and disposal plan will be considered, and the ownership structure could shift depending on recipients and method. Konex trading volume is low and information access is limited.

08

Overall view

GA Innodus is a specialty contractor with top-tier dredging equipment and a low debt ratio. However, its 2025 results show standalone revenue well below the 2023 level and a thin operating margin. Its shareholder-return record, including dividends, a treasury-share tender offer and a Konex award, is a balancing factor.

The property purchase, equal to 34.23% of total assets, leaves open questions on its link to the core business, payment of the balance and use plans. The timing of tenders for Jinhae New Port, Gadeokdo New Airport and follow-on Saemangeum sections is cited as a driver of future business indicators.

The outcome of the treasury-share item at the November 25, 2026 extraordinary meeting also warrants monitoring. This report is for information only, and investment decisions are left to the reader.
