Report overview

Subsidiary Earnings Set the Holding Company's Profit Path

LG's results are built from dividends, trademark income and equity-method gains from its subsidiaries, and in the third quarter of 2026 weaker chemical affiliates cut operating profit 21.8% year on year.

02

Business structure

LG is a holding company whose stakes include LG Electronics, LG Chem, LG CNS and LG Uplus. Its income comes mainly from dividends, trademark royalties and equity-method gains rather than direct product sales.

In the third-quarter 2026 filing released on November 12, 2026, cumulative consolidated revenue was roughly KRW 5.98 trillion, and consolidated subsidiaries accounted for about 82 percent of it. Trademark and rental income rose slightly to KRW 370.7 billion.

On a parent-only basis, trademark royalty income rose about 9 percent to KRW 278.9 billion, while rental income fell about 17 percent to KRW 91.8 billion after the sale of the Gwanghwamun building. Across affiliates, cumulative third-quarter revenue of the electronics group rose year on year to KRW 89.1 trillion.

The chemical group's revenue rose to KRW 46.7 trillion, but its operating margin fell from 4.5 percent to 3.0 percent. The telecom and services group's operating margin improved from 6.5 percent to 7.9 percent on KRW 16.5 trillion of revenue.

LG CNS posted cumulative third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 4.5 trillion, up about 7 percent, supported by growth in cloud service contracts. The shareholder structure is led by related parties at 43.4 percent, followed by foreign investors at 37.6 percent, institutions at 12.6 percent and individuals at 6.4 percent.

Parent-only cash and cash equivalents stood at roughly KRW 1.2 trillion at the end of the third quarter of 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual filings show revenue of KRW 7.19 trillion in 2022, KRW 7.45 trillion in 2023 (up 3.6% year on year), KRW 7.18 trillion in 2024 (down 3.6%) and KRW 7.25 trillion in 2025 (up 1.1%). The largest revenue year in the 2022–2025 span was 2023 and the smallest was 2024.

Operating profit moved from KRW 1.94 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.59 trillion in 2023 (down 18.2%), KRW 966.8 billion in 2024 (down 39.2%) and KRW 912.2 billion in 2025 (down 5.7%), and the decline ran for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

The operating margin fell from 27.0% to 21.3%, 13.5% and 12.6% over the same years, so revenue held roughly steady while margin nearly halved.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.98 trillion in 2022, KRW 1.26 trillion in 2023 (down 36.3%), KRW 574.7 billion in 2024 (down 54.4%) and KRW 737.2 billion in 2025 (up 28.3%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 2.12 trillion, KRW 1.41 trillion, KRW 790.5 billion and KRW 1 trillion for 2022 through 2025; the gap with controlling-shareholder profit reflects the non-controlling share.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 30.3 trillion, of which KRW 28.7 trillion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 3.65 trillion, giving a debt ratio of 12.0 percent.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 641 billion in 2022 to KRW 882.7 billion in 2023, KRW 1.36 trillion in 2024 and KRW 1.02 trillion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 418.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025, an operating loss of KRW 421.7 billion in the fourth quarter (a swing into deficit), KRW 413.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (a return to profit), KRW 533 billion in the second quarter (up 28.8% from the prior quarter) and KRW 327.7 billion in the third quarter (down 38.5% from the prior quarter and down 21.8% year on year).

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 315.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025, a net loss of KRW 362.9 billion in the fourth quarter, then KRW 339.8 billion, KRW 465.7 billion and KRW 206.8 billion in the three quarters of 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 649.5 billion.

Equity-method gains included in operating profit were KRW 107.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026 according to the provisional release of November 12, 2026, against KRW 223.3 billion a year earlier, as weaker chemical affiliates weighed.

In September 2026, Heungkuk Securities had forecast third-quarter consolidated operating profit of KRW 439.2 billion, which the reported figure fell short of.

05

Industry analysis

LG's earnings move with the combined performance of its affiliates. In an October 28, 2026 release, LG Electronics reported cumulative third-quarter revenue of KRW 71.38 trillion, up 9.2 percent, and cumulative operating profit of KRW 4.03 trillion, up 55.9 percent.

In the same release, its Vehicle Solution Company posted third-quarter revenue of KRW 2.83 trillion and operating profit of KRW 190.3 billion, both record highs for a third quarter.

Cumulative orders for AI data center cooling solutions exceeded KRW 3 trillion through the third quarter, up sharply from about KRW 600 billion at the end of the first half.

The chemical group, by contrast, is in a weaker phase: its revenue rose, but its operating margin fell from 4.5 percent to 3.0 percent, and LG Chem posted a net loss in the third quarter of 2026. The telecom and services group's margin rose to 7.9 percent and acts as a steadier earnings base.

Within electronics, LG Electronics said its home appliance business passed KRW 7 trillion in quarterly revenue while keeping stable profitability. For the holding company, the key variable is whether a recovery in chemical affiliates is added to the stability of the electronics, telecom and services groups.

Restructuring in the chemical group is seen as the main factor that could halt the decline in equity-method gains.

06

Outlook

The confirmed direction is shareholder returns and new-business investment. The 3.9 percent treasury stake was retired in full in tranches of 1.9 percent in September 2025 and 2.0 percent in May 2026, and the minimum payout ratio on parent-only adjusted net income was raised from 50 percent to 60 percent.

An interim dividend was paid in September 2026. In its third-quarter 2026 release, LG said it would expand investment in new businesses including AI, bio and clean tech. LG Electronics plans to invest an additional KRW 220 billion in production sites and capacity for AI data center cooling.

On the sell-side side, Heungkuk Securities' September 2026 report projected 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 8.29 trillion and operating profit of KRW 1.52 trillion, up 66.3 percent. That projection assumes recovery at affiliates and better equity-method gains.

The same report forecast third-quarter operating profit of KRW 439.2 billion, above the reported KRW 327.7 billion, so whether the full-year projection is reached depends on fourth-quarter affiliate results.

LG Electronics expects limited recovery in home appliance demand in the fourth quarter of 2026 and similarly limited recovery in automotive demand. The next checkpoints are the 2026 fourth-quarter provisional results and the 2026 annual business report.

08

Bull factors

Signs of Affiliate Earnings Recovery

LG Electronics reported that cumulative third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose 55.9 percent to KRW 4.03 trillion, and its Vehicle Solution Company posted record third-quarter revenue and operating profit.

Heungkuk Securities projected in its September 2026 report that LG's 2026 consolidated operating profit would rise 66.3 percent. Because affiliate profits flow into equity-method gains and dividends, this trend is a direct path to improved holding-level results.

Stronger Shareholder Return Policy

The 3.9 percent treasury stake was retired in full, with 1.9 percent in September 2025 and 2.0 percent in May 2026. The minimum payout ratio on parent-only adjusted net income rose from 50 percent to 60 percent, and an interim dividend was paid in September 2026. Treasury share retirement and a higher dividend floor now operate together as shareholder return tools.

Telecom and Services Affiliates and LG CNS

The telecom and services group's margin rose from 6.5 percent to 7.9 percent on cumulative third-quarter revenue of KRW 16.5 trillion. LG CNS posted third-quarter revenue of KRW 1.66 trillion and operating profit of KRW 138.2 billion, up 9.3 percent and 15.0 percent.

Trademark royalty income rose to KRW 278.9 billion on higher affiliate sales, adding a steadier source of direct holding-company income.

09

Bear factors

Chemical Weakness and Shrinking Equity-Method Gains

Equity-method gains included in third-quarter 2026 operating profit were KRW 107.7 billion, less than half of the KRW 223.3 billion recorded a year earlier. Cumulative equity-method income for the first three quarters fell about 11 percent year on year to KRW 707.2 billion on weak chemical operations.

With the chemical group's cumulative margin down to 3.0 percent, this remains a weakness until the equity-method line stabilizes.

Multi-Year Decline in Operating Profit

Operating profit declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, ending at KRW 912.2 billion in 2025. The operating margin fell from 27.0 percent in 2022 to 12.6 percent in 2025.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 327.7 billion was down 21.8 percent year on year and fell short of the KRW 439.2 billion that Heungkuk Securities had forecast in September.

High Quarterly Earnings Volatility

The fourth quarter of 2025 swung into deficit, with an operating loss of KRW 421.7 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 362.9 billion.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 465.7 billion in the second quarter of 2026, then fell 55.6 percent to KRW 206.8 billion in the third quarter. The last four quarters total KRW 649.5 billion, reflecting large swings between quarters.

10

Risk factors

Affiliate Demand Risk

A slower fourth-quarter recovery in home appliance and automotive demand would pass through to the holding company. Because affiliate conditions flow directly into equity-method gains and dividends, fourth-quarter results could weigh on the holding company. Geopolitical uncertainty and currency movements could also affect affiliate results.

Chemical Restructuring Execution Risk

Chemical restructuring must proceed smoothly for the decline in equity-method gains to stop, and this is seen as a key task. With the chemical group's margin at 3.0 percent, the outcome and timing of restructuring will shape when holding-level profit recovers. New businesses in AI, bio and clean tech may take time to generate earnings.

Rental Income and Dividend Capacity Risk

Rental income fell about 17 percent to KRW 91.8 billion in the first three quarters of 2026 after the Gwanghwamun building sale. The expansion of shareholder returns depends on parent-only adjusted net income and dividend funding, so weaker affiliate profits could affect dividend capacity. Related parties hold 43.4 percent, the largest block in the shareholder structure.

12

Overall view

LG's filed results for 2022 through 2025 show revenue holding in the KRW 7 trillion range, while operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell through 2024 before profit attributable to controlling shareholders recovered in 2025.

The operating margin declined from 27.0 percent in 2022 to 12.6 percent in 2025, and operating profit has fallen for three consecutive years from 2023. In 2026 it returned to profit in the first quarter and rose to KRW 533 billion in the second quarter, before falling to KRW 327.7 billion in the third.

The variables that will shape the direction are the stability of the electronics, telecom and services groups and the recovery of the chemical group. Strengths include affiliate earnings recovery, full treasury share retirement, a 60 percent minimum payout ratio and an interim dividend.

Weaknesses include lower equity-method gains from chemicals, large quarterly swings and limited fourth-quarter demand recovery at affiliates.

A brokerage forecast projects 2026 consolidated operating profit of KRW 1.52 trillion, but based on filings through the third quarter, reaching that path depends on fourth-quarter results.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 fourth-quarter provisional results and the 2026 annual business report, which will show whether equity-method gains recover.
