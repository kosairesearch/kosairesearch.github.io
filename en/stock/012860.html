Report overview

Sales Grew, but the Earnings Cushion Has Thinned

Revenue and profit attributable to controlling shareholders grew through 2025, but quarterly operating profit fell through 2026, leaving a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters.

  1. 1

    Revenue rose for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, reaching KRW 1.04 trillion in 2025, the highest level of the period.

  2. 2

    After peaking at KRW 279.9 billion in Q4 2025, revenue declined for three consecutive quarters through Q3 2026.

  3. 3

    Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 706 million, down 92.6% year on year, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 23.2 billion.

  4. 4

    The company attributes the cost squeeze mainly to a lag before raw material price increases are passed on to customer prices, and price negotiations are ongoing.

  5. 5

    The Mexico plant and the start of MobED robot-parts supply add new growth lines, and whether they lift earnings is a checkpoint for the next periodic report.

02

Business structure

Mobase Electronics makes automotive electronic and electrical components, with a focus on smart key systems and car lock sets, along with power window switches, multifunction switches, BCM units, wireless chargers and centralized control units.

Press coverage has described its smart key system as the top-share product in the domestic market, and its integrated body control unit (IBU) is cited as a product that lifts average selling prices as vehicle electrification deepens.

In electric vehicles, the company has won orders for and is mass-producing battery management modules (BMM) and battery management systems (BMS). Its customers center on domestic automakers such as Hyundai Motor and KIA.

On a cumulative basis through Q3 2025, the company said that higher order volumes from key customers including KIA and Volvo lifted its head-office sales.

Overseas, the start of Hyundai Motor's North American EV-dedicated plant and added supply items for KIA's Mexico plant increased HMI sales at its overseas subsidiaries. The new Mexico plant began full operation in Q1 2026, and the company said Mexico subsidiary sales grew by more than 50%.

In new businesses, the company began supplying main boards and wiring for Hyundai Motor Group's mobile robot platform MobED, and it is also developing robot grippers and driver-monitoring algorithms. Sales move with automaker production volumes and model launch schedules. The largest shareholder side holds close to half of the shares.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 922.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 939.4 billion in 2023 (up 1.9% year on year), KRW 971.5 billion in 2024 (up 3.4%) and KRW 1.04 trillion in 2025 (up 7.5%). Revenue grew for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit fell from KRW 43.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 27.7 billion in 2023 (down 36.8%), then recovered to KRW 34.4 billion in 2024 (up 23.9%) and KRW 36.8 billion in 2025 (up 7.0%). It grew for two straight years from 2024 to 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

The operating margin went from 4.8% in 2022 to 3.0% in 2023, then held at 3.5% in both 2024 and 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 8.02 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.27 billion in 2023 (up 15.6%), KRW 15.8 billion in 2024 (up 70.6%) and KRW 19.9 billion in 2025 (up 25.9%), a three-year run of growth.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 20.8 billion in 2025 and should be read separately from the figure for controlling shareholders. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 247.6% in 2022 and 258.3% in 2023 to 245.5% in 2024 and 208.6% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 251.1 billion, of which KRW 243.2 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 523.8 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 44.7 billion in 2022, KRW 4.48 billion in 2023, KRW 22.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 68.8 billion in 2025, falling sharply in 2023 and rising most in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue peaked at KRW 279.9 billion in Q4 2025, up 18.3% from the prior quarter, and then declined for three consecutive quarters to KRW 265.3 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 262.8 billion in Q2 and KRW 258.3 billion in Q3 2026.

Operating profit moved from KRW 9.53 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 9.8 billion in Q4 to KRW 7.42 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 2.84 billion in Q2 and KRW 706 million in Q3.

The company cited a one-off cost, including an ordinary-wage issue, in Q1; raw material inflation in memory chips and gold together with a supplier fire that disrupted key customer production in Q2; and a lag before rising PCB, electronic component and gold costs reached customer prices in Q3.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 8.36 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 8.08 billion in Q4 to KRW 5.99 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 1.64 billion in Q2, before a net loss of KRW 23.2 billion in Q3 2026 turned it into a loss.

The sum of the latest four quarters' losses attributable to controlling shareholders is KRW 7.55 billion.

In preliminary figures announced on November 13, 2026, cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 was KRW 786.4 billion (up 2.9% year on year) and cumulative operating profit was KRW 11.0 billion (down 59.3%), with a cumulative net loss of KRW 15.4 billion that included a non-cash foreign currency translation loss of KRW 14.0 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The company's own materials point to vehicle electrification, the spread of eco-friendly cars and autonomous driving as the backdrop for its business expansion. Press analysis says BMS orders have become a core product as global automakers broaden their EV lineups.

On raw materials, rising memory chip and gold prices pushed up costs in the first half of 2026, and in Q3 rising PCB and electronic component prices were added. Press coverage said a weaker Korean won, which lifts the won value of export-heavy auto parts makers' earnings, supported net income.

The same period also brought translation losses on foreign-currency assets and liabilities, so the exchange rate has worked in both directions. On competition, a Thinkpool analysis of Q1 2026 results placed the company's operating margin among the lowest of its peers.

Because sales depend on orders from a small number of automaker groups such as Hyundai Motor Group and KIA, the business is closely tied to automaker production cycles.

In its first-half 2025 results, the company also cited a slowdown in the economy and tariff negotiations as factors that weighed on output and consumer sentiment. In robotics, the company presented growth in the physical AI market as the reason for entering robot components.

06

Outlook

The company said it is negotiating with key customers to pass through raw material cost increases in the second half of 2026. A company official said it aims to recover the full amount of the cost increases through those talks.

In its announcement on November 13, 2026, the company said it will focus on cost control for the remaining quarter and raise operating efficiency so that overseas growth feeds into profit. Overseas, it is expanding supply to the North American market on the back of stable operations at the new Mexico plant.

For robot parts, mass production of main boards and wiring for Hyundai Motor Group's MobED platform began in Q1 2026 and supply continued from February. Development of robot grippers and a drowsy-driving monitoring algorithm offered to automakers is also in progress.

In EVs, orders and mass production of BMM and BMS continue. In its Q1 2026 results, the company said it expects profitability to improve as the cost structure stabilizes in the second half.

The next checkpoints are how much of the price increase shows up in Q4 and full-year 2026 results, whether Mexico subsidiary sales convert into margin, and how large robot-parts sales become.

08

Bull factors

Top-line growth and overseas expansion

Revenue reached KRW 1.04 trillion in 2025 and has risen for three straight years. The company said Mexico subsidiary sales grew by more than 50% after the new plant began operating in Q1 2026, and supply to North American customers is expanding.

Preliminary cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 rose year on year, with overseas subsidiary sales cited as a driver.

Ongoing cost pass-through negotiations

Talks with major customers on passing through raw material cost increases are ongoing. The company aims to recover the full amount of the increases in the second half of 2026. If the talks succeed, lower cost pressure could support a recovery in operating margin.

Expanding new-business options

Supply of main boards and wiring for Hyundai Motor Group's MobED platform generated robot-parts sales from Q1 2026. Robot grippers and a drowsy-driving monitoring algorithm are also being offered to automakers. Continued EV BMS orders would broaden the product mix of the electronics business.

Bear factors

Consecutive quarterly declines in operating profit

Operating profit fell from KRW 9.8 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 706 million in Q3 2026, and Q3 2026 operating profit was down 92.6% year on year. Operating profit has declined for three consecutive quarters. A Thinkpool analysis placed the company's operating margin among the lowest compared with peers.

Swing to losses and volatile currency gains and losses

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 23.2 billion in Q3 2026. The preliminary cumulative net loss for January to September 2026 of KRW 15.4 billion included a foreign currency translation loss of KRW 14.0 billion, so exchange-rate moves can swing reported earnings sharply.

Uncertain cost pass-through and production disruption

The price increases are still under negotiation and not yet confirmed. In Q2 2026, a supplier fire disrupted production at key customers. Because customer concentration is high, a drop in automaker output would hit both sales and profit.

10

Risk factors

Raw materials and FX

When memory chip, gold, PCB and electronic component prices rise, cost pressure hits first until the increases are reflected in selling prices. Translation losses on foreign-currency assets and liabilities are non-cash but can move net income sharply. The pace of profit recovery in the second half depends on the outcome of price negotiations.

Customer concentration and production disruption

Sales depend on a small number of automaker groups, including Hyundai Motor Group and KIA. When a supply-chain accident such as a supplier fire turns into a production disruption, quarterly results move quickly.

In its first-half 2025 results, the company also said external factors including tariff negotiations weighed on output.

Pace of the new-business shift

Robot-parts production began in Q1 2026, but sales from the segment are not yet clearly visible in quarterly results. The company has said it will focus its resources so new-area sales reach a steady footing without weighing heavily on results. Turning the new business into a contributor to profit could take time.

Overall view

Mobase Electronics grew revenue from 2022 through 2025, reaching KRW 1.04 trillion in 2025, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for three straight years. In 2026, however, revenue fell for three consecutive quarters, and operating profit dropped to KRW 706 million in Q3.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a net loss of KRW 23.2 billion in Q3, and the sum of the latest four quarters' losses is KRW 7.55 billion. The causes cited include a lag in passing raw material cost increases to customer prices, one-off costs and non-cash valuation losses from exchange rates.

Strengths include overseas sales growth from the Mexico plant, ongoing cost pass-through talks and entry into new businesses such as robot parts. Weaknesses include continued quarterly profit declines, earnings volatility from currency moves, and risks tied to customer concentration and production disruptions.

Because an earnings-based multiple cannot currently be used, valuation interpretation centers on the share price relative to book value and on whether quarterly profit recovers.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin in Q4 2026 and the annual business report, whether price increases are reflected, and Q1 2027 results. This report is for information only and does not include an investment rating or a buy or sell opinion.
