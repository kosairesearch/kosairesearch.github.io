Nuclear/SMR Orders Amid Push Out of Losses

02

Business structure

Founded in 1987, Enertork localized electric valve actuators that had previously relied on imports and listed on KOSDAQ in 2006.

Its core business is the manufacture and sale of actuators and reduction gears used as fluid control equipment in power plants, water and sewage facilities, water treatment plants, gas utilities, shipyards and steel mills, with overseas plant exports and national infrastructure projects accounting for more than 80% of its target market.

Revenue is effectively concentrated in a single segment of actuators and related equipment. In a June 2025 report, a company official said Enertork maintains roughly a 70% share of the domestic electric actuator market.

For nuclear applications the company offers its TU-Series, and in the first half of 2026 it signed a contract through KB Valve Trading to supply TU-Series actuators for an SMR that Daewoo E&C is building in Gijang, Busan.

In June 2026 Enertork also signed a contract to supply 70 electric actuators through 2028 for the ARA multi-purpose research reactor being advanced by the Korea Atomic Energy Research Institute.

Its Vietnamese production subsidiary, ENERTORK VINA, completed a local plant expansion and unveiled the NXQ series of small, high-performance quarter-turn actuators aimed at AI data centers as it pushes into Southeast Asian markets.

In September 2026 the company developed a gas-monitoring actuator called the Safety Guardian Actuator to prevent asphyxiation accidents in confined spaces, which received an innovative-product designation from the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy and underwent a demonstration with K-water.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose 2.5% from KRW 26.3 billion in 2022 to a peak of KRW 26.9 billion in 2023, then declined for two straight years to KRW 25.2 billion in 2024 (-6.6%) and KRW 23.5 billion in 2025 (-6.5%).

Operating profit swung from a profit of KRW 1.01 billion in 2022 to a loss of KRW 882 million in 2023, turned to a profit of KRW 83.99 million in 2024, and then swung back to a loss of KRW 4.1 billion in 2025.

The operating margin likewise swung sharply, from 3.8% in 2022 to -3.3% in 2023, 0.3% in 2024 and -17.4% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders also flipped each year, from a profit of KRW 723 million in 2022 to a loss of KRW 873 million in 2023, a profit of KRW 531 million in 2024 and a loss of KRW 4.13 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating losses persisted from Q2 2025 through Q4 2025 before narrowing in the fourth quarter, then the company swung to an operating profit of KRW 72.15 million in Q1 2026, which rose 390.1% quarter-on-quarter to KRW 354 million in Q2 2026, also marking a year-on-year swing to profit.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders posted two consecutive profitable quarters at KRW 94.25 million in Q1 2026 and KRW 68.49 million in Q2 2026, though the latter fell 27.3% from the prior quarter.

Still, the cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the trailing four quarters (Q3 2025–Q2 2026) stood at KRW 3.14 billion, indicating that the losses from earlier quarters have not yet been fully offset despite the 2026 quarterly profits.

Operating cash flow steadily declined from KRW 1.86 billion in 2023 to KRW 452 million in 2024 and KRW 167 million in 2025, while the debt-to-equity ratio jumped from 7.4% in 2024 to 20.8% in 2025, marking a notable shift in the balance sheet.

05

Industry analysis

Electric actuators are the core drive mechanism for remotely opening and closing valves at power plants and process facilities; in nuclear plants they are applied to safety-critical systems such as coolant and steam-control valves, making flawless reliability more important than price.

In Korea, the combination of a nuclear-expansion policy stance and SMR demonstration projects has drawn broad attention to the sector, and on KOSDAQ, buying interest in smaller parts and equipment makers such as Enertork has repeatedly tracked swings in large nuclear-related names such as Doosan Enerbility.

Enertork claims a leading position in the domestic electric actuator market, but it competes with global manufacturers in overseas plant export markets.

High-reliability products for nuclear and SMR applications are an area where few domestic firms hold manufacturing capability, but much of the company's revenue depends on an indirect order structure channeled through domestic EPC contractors and valve suppliers, which makes results sensitive to the timing and scale of individual projects.

The spread of AI data centers has newly highlighted demand for small, high-performance actuators, which is cited as a factor that could diversify demand away from traditional power and plant end markets.

However, the actuator business itself remains sensitive to customers' capital expenditure plans and policy shifts, producing wide quarter-to-quarter revenue swings.

06

Outlook

In a corporate value-up plan disclosed on March 26, 2026, Enertork said it would pursue overseas revenue expansion in Southeast Asia and the Middle East on top of its existing businesses in power generation, water and sewage, gas, steel and nuclear.

Detailed initiatives included expanding into Southeast Asian and other overseas markets through a Vietnam plant expansion in the first half of 2026, growing domestic and international nuclear orders including SMR, and expanding market share through new product development such as AI-enabled and compact products.

The plan also set goals for expanding in the U.S. power and water treatment markets and entering Middle East and Russian reconstruction projects, with a target dividend payout ratio of at least 40%.

In practice, Vietnamese subsidiary ENERTORK VINA said it completed its local plant expansion and established mass-production capability for the NXQ series aimed at AI data centers.

In the nuclear and SMR segment, the company has already secured a supply contract for the ARA research reactor and a delivery contract for the Busan Gijang SMR project, with sequential deliveries scheduled through 2028.

The company disclosed an investor relations event for August 14, 2026 and released business-condition materials, and in June 2026 it issued single sale/supply contract disclosures along with corrections, underscoring the need to track the gap between order timing and revenue recognition.

The company said its Safety Guardian Actuator gas-monitoring product has completed commercialization preparations with three related patents, registration as a technology product with the Korea Gas Corporation, and an SGS international certification test report, with demand expected to grow as the Ministry of Employment and Labor and local governments expand serious-accident prevention programs.

Bull factors

Expanding nuclear and SMR orders

Enertork has secured a supply contract for the ARA research reactor and a TU-Series delivery contract for the Busan Gijang SMR project, laying a foundation for sequential revenue recognition through 2028.

The fact that few domestic firms hold manufacturing technology for nuclear-plant electric actuators is cited as a factor supporting its relative position in related projects. If SMR commercialization advances further, related demand could continue over the longer term.

Two consecutive profitable quarters in 2026

Operating profit was KRW 72.15 million in Q1 2026 and KRW 354 million in Q2 2026, two consecutive profitable quarters, with Q2 also marking a year-on-year swing to profit. Revenue also rose to KRW 7.38 billion in Q2, up 38.4% quarter-on-quarter and 16.6% year-on-year. The recent quarters show a departure from the loss trend that persisted through 2025.

Overseas expansion and dividend policy targets

In its March 2026 value-up plan, the company set targets for a Vietnam plant expansion, entry into U.S., Middle East and Russian reconstruction markets, and maintaining a dividend payout ratio above 40%.

The Vietnamese subsidiary has in fact completed its local plant expansion and said it established mass-production capability for the NXQ series aimed at AI data centers. If these goals are executed, geographic diversification of the revenue base becomes possible.

Bear factors

Large 2025 loss and two straight years of revenue decline

2025 revenue fell to KRW 23.5 billion, marking a second straight annual decline, while the operating loss reached KRW 4.1 billion and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 4.13 billion. The operating margin also dropped to -17.4%, a sharply wider loss than the prior year.

Although the company swung to profit in 2026, given the scale of the 2025 loss this can be viewed as an early stage of recovery.

Accumulated losses and balance sheet shift

The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the trailing four quarters (Q3 2025–Q2 2026) reached KRW 3.14 billion, meaning prior losses have not yet been offset even as quarterly profits resumed in 2026.

Operating cash flow steadily declined from KRW 1.86 billion in 2023 to KRW 167 million in 2025, while the debt-to-equity ratio jumped from 7.4% in 2024 to 20.8% in 2025. The simultaneous slowdown in cash generation and rise in leverage are variables worth monitoring for financial stability.

Volatility tied to order-based revenue

Quarterly revenue has swung widely, falling 25.8% quarter-on-quarter in Q3 2025, then rising 78.3% in Q4 2025, before falling 36.3% again in Q1 2026.

The company also issued single sale/supply contract disclosures and corrections in June 2026, underscoring the need to distinguish contract value from the actual timing of revenue recognition for individual orders.

A business structure heavily dependent on a small number of large projects can lead to uneven quarterly results.

Risk factors

Order-to-revenue recognition timing risk

There have been cases where single sale/supply contract disclosures were followed by correction filings, meaning contract value, delivery period and actual revenue recognition timing can diverge. This can produce a structure in which an earnings surprise in one quarter is reversed in the next. Investors need to separately verify the revenue recognition schedule for each disclosed contract.

Balance sheet shift

The debt-to-equity ratio jumped from 7.4% in 2024 to 20.8% in 2025, while operating cash flow shrank from KRW 1.86 billion in 2023 to KRW 167 million in 2025. With investment execution such as the Vietnam plant expansion ongoing, a slowdown in cash generation could increase funding pressure. Whether this balance sheet shift persists needs to be checked in upcoming quarterly disclosures.

Small-cap liquidity and volatility

As a small-cap stock with a market capitalization of around KRW 50 billion, there have been reported instances where a single piece of news moved the price sharply on low-volume days. The stock's price can swing in tandem with shifts in broader nuclear and SMR theme-related sentiment, independent of fundamentals. If thematic buying interest fades, volatility in the opposite direction could similarly increase.

12

Overall view

Enertork has seen revenue decline for two straight years since its 2023 peak, with profitability shaken by a large operating loss and net loss in 2025.

Still, signs of a turnaround emerged as operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders both swung to profit in Q1 and Q2 2026, with nuclear and SMR actuator orders (the ARA research reactor, the Busan Gijang SMR project) and overseas expansion through a Vietnam plant expansion proceeding in parallel.

Even so, the cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the trailing four quarters reached KRW 3.14 billion, meaning past losses have not yet been fully resolved, and the rise in the debt-to-equity ratio alongside the decline in operating cash flow are balance-sheet variables that warrant attention.

The gap between order timing and revenue recognition, together with the volatility typical of small-cap stocks, also complicates interpretation of results. The Q3 2026 regular disclosure and the actual revenue recognition timing of the nuclear/SMR projects will be the next points to watch.
