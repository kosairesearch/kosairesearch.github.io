02

Business structure

Abko operates three segments: gaming gear, public business, and small home appliances. The gaming gear segment sells PC peripherals such as keyboards, headsets, mice and monitors under the ABKO, HACKER, COX and NCORE brands, and its main revenue comes from keyboards, headsets, mice, audio products and memory.

A stock information site shows a revenue mix of 53.00% gaming gear, 41.60% public business and 5.40% small appliances. This is an aggregator figure rather than a segment breakdown from the company's filings, so it should be read separately from disclosed results.

The public business segment supplies IoT charging storage units for classrooms under the PADBANK name, unmanned collection boxes and AI servers to public procurement markets and institutions.

In the second quarter of 2025 the company said Smart School division sales rose more than elevenfold year on year and that it had confirmed PADBANK deliveries to education offices in Daejeon, South Chungcheong and Jeju. The small appliance segment plans kitchen and pet products under the OHELLA brand.

In gaming gear the company cites price competitiveness and its domestic distribution network as advantages against overseas brands such as Logitech, Corsair and Razer. According to its company profile, the brands are sold in 18 countries, and since 2019 products have also reached North America through Amazon.

The largest shareholder, Oh Kwang-geun, holds 30.96% as of December 5, 2025. A structural feature is that public business revenue can depend on policy-driven contracts and education office budgets.

04

Earnings analysis

Abko's annual reported results moved from an operating loss in 2022 to a profit in 2023, and revenue peaked in 2023 before declining in both 2024 and 2025.

Revenue was KRW 82.7 billion in 2022, KRW 98.9 billion in 2023 (up 19.7% year on year), KRW 93.4 billion in 2024 (down 5.6%) and KRW 83.4 billion in 2025 (down 10.6%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating results went from an operating loss of KRW 15.5 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 2.15 billion in 2023, a turn to profit from the prior year, then KRW 4.82 billion in 2024 (up 123.6%) and KRW 3.18 billion in 2025 (down 34.1%).

The operating margin was -18.7% in 2022, 2.2% in 2023, 5.2% in 2024 and 3.8% in 2025, so the 2025 margin stayed above the 2023 level even as revenue fell.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from a loss of KRW 17.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.81 billion in 2023, then KRW 4.7 billion in 2024 (up 160.0%) and KRW 6.41 billion in 2025 (up 36.3%), rising for two consecutive years.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 6.41 billion in 2025. For the 2025 divergence between operating profit and net profit, the company cited higher income tax benefits from changes in the realizability of deferred tax assets, as explained alongside its February 5, 2026 disclosure.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 14.6 billion in 2022, KRW 15.4 billion in 2023, -KRW 8.23 billion in 2024 and KRW 11.1 billion in 2025. Quarterly results are more volatile.

After KRW 19.3 billion in revenue, KRW 1.14 billion in operating profit and KRW 1.18 billion in net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter of 2025, revenue rose to KRW 25.2 billion in the fourth quarter (up 30.4% from the prior quarter), but the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 826 million.

Revenue fell to KRW 16.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 35.0% from the prior quarter), yet operating profit turned positive at KRW 672 million and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.81 billion.

Revenue rose to KRW 20.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (up 27.1%), but the company posted an operating loss of KRW 308 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 782 million.

In the third quarter of 2026 revenue was KRW 15.6 billion (down 25.3% from the prior quarter and down 19.5% year on year), operating profit was KRW 1.62 billion (up 41.6% year on year), but net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 318 million, down 73.0% year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 4.6 billion. Non-operating items and tax effects are mixed into quarterly profit, so operating profit and net profit need to be read separately.

Industry analysis

Demand for gaming gear is influenced by PC replacement cycles and major game launches.

According to the Wisereport company monitor, as of December 2025 the company expected the launch of 50-series graphics cards in 2025, the arrival of a PC replacement cycle and the release of GTA6 to have a positive effect on the PC market.

That is an outlook from late 2025, so whether the effect showed up in 2026 results should be judged from reported revenue. Third-quarter 2026 revenue falling 19.5% year on year runs against that expectation.

The company said overseas exports rose year on year in the first quarter of 2026 and that it is strengthening marketing in line with growing demand from premium PC cafes. The public education market depends on digital education policy and education office budgets, so policy direction shapes project volume.

In gaming gear, the company cites price competitiveness and its domestic distribution network against overseas brands such as Logitech, Corsair and Razer. Some analysis articles point out that as the education charging storage business improves its profitability, competitors may enter the market more actively.

The industry picture combines hopes for a recovery in replacement demand with the pressure of price competition.

Outlook

On April 16, 2026 the company disclosed a corporate value-up plan, saying it would build a stable earnings structure through wider orders and a more diversified customer base. The same plan pairs expansion into new businesses and global markets with maintaining an appropriate dividend policy.

It also lists stronger cost competitiveness, more high value-added products and new product launches as ways to upgrade the portfolio. Dividends have indeed risen: the 2025 dividend amount was about KRW 7.9 billion, up from about KRW 900 million in 2024.

The Wisereport company monitor noted in December 2025 that new products using recent technology, such as wireless keyboards, 8K polling rate and 4K receivers, are expected to help market share and profitability.

In the new business area, news in June 2026 reported a new share subscription agreement tied to investment in a U.S. medical AI company, and in July 2026 the company acquired a 40% stake in Bullseye, becoming its second-largest shareholder.

The timing and scale of revenue from the medical AI investment are not yet reflected in reported results. In the 2026 annual report, the next regular filing, the trends in the public and gaming gear segments and the full-year operating margin are the next checkpoints.

Risk factors

Policy and budget dependence

Public education revenue can depend on government digital education policy and education office budgets. A policy shift or budget cut could change delivery volumes and the timing of orders. The larger the public business share, the greater the weight of this risk.

Gaming gear competition and demand

Gaming gear competes with overseas brands such as Logitech, Corsair and Razer, and price competition or new entrants could pressure margins. Revenue declined in 2025 and was down year on year again in the third quarter of 2026, which shows that the pace of demand recovery is uncertain. Whether new products add market share must be confirmed through reported results.

Uncertain returns on new investments

The medical AI investment is at an early stage, including the 40% stake in the U.S. company Bullseye acquired in July 2026. It will take time before returns show up in results, and neither the timing nor the scale of revenue appears in reported figures. If non-core investments grow, the direction of capital allocation and financial burden could also change.

Overall view

Abko is a PC peripherals company that started in gaming gear and has built up public education projects. Revenue peaked at KRW 98.9 billion in 2023, fell for two consecutive years and reached KRW 83.4 billion in 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 4.82 billion in 2024 to KRW 3.18 billion in 2025, but the operating margin improved from -18.7% in 2022 to 5.2% in 2024 and 3.8% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose in both 2024 and 2025, although the company said higher income tax benefits contributed to the 2025 increase.

Quarterly results have alternated: a loss in the fourth quarter of 2025, a profit in the first quarter of 2026, a loss in the second quarter and an operating profit in the third quarter. The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 67.1% at the end of 2025 from 77.6% in 2022, and total equity was KRW 62.1 billion.

Strengths include public education revenue growth, a stronger financial structure and higher dividends, while weaknesses include the declining revenue trend, volatile quarterly profit and a net profit structure that depends heavily on tax effects. The medical AI investment has not yet shown up in reported results.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary full-year 2026 results in early February 2027 and the 2026 annual report by the end of March 2027.
