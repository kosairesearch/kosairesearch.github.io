02

Business structure

Sungchang Autotech operates a single automotive parts segment, producing items such as inverters for vehicles, small electrical components such as ionizers, cabin air filters and sponge parts.

In the third-quarter 2023 report, domestic sales accounted for 46% and overseas sales for 54% of consolidated revenue, with the United States at 50% and China at 4%. US production runs through the consolidated subsidiary SCA Inc., whose main customer in the same report was HMMA, at 43.5% of that subsidiary's sales.

For the domestic entity, Hanon Systems was the largest customer at 69.4% in the same report, followed by Hyundai Mobis at 3.4% and other customers at 27.2%.

Because the company sells mainly to tier-one suppliers such as Hanon Systems and Hyundai Mobis rather than directly to automakers, its volumes depend on those suppliers' orders before reaching Hyundai Motor and Kia.

Among these customers, Hanon Systems represents the HVAC group and Hyundai Mobis the module and parts group. As a lower-tier supplier to these tier-one customers, Sungchang Autotech has limited pricing power and high dependence on their volume allocation. Current item-level shares can be checked in the sales and order status table of the next annual report.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales were KRW 184.8 billion in 2022, KRW 169.3 billion in 2023 (down 8.4% year on year), KRW 194.4 billion in 2024 (up 14.8%) and KRW 243.5 billion in 2025 (up 25.3%), rising two years in a row from 2024 to 2025, with 2025 the largest year.

Operating profit fell from KRW 6.95 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.06 billion in 2023 (down 41.6%), then rose to KRW 6.31 billion in 2024 (up 55.4%) and KRW 11.6 billion in 2025 (up 84.0%), two straight years of gains.

The operating margin bottomed at 2.4% in 2023 and reached 3.2% in 2024 and 4.8% in 2025, so profit growth outpaced sales growth. The company attributed the main driver of the 2025 change to higher automotive parts sales.

Attributable net income was KRW 2.34 billion in 2022, KRW 5.5 billion in 2023 (up 134.6%), KRW 5.46 billion in 2024 (down 0.8%) and KRW 8.49 billion in 2025 (up 55.6%).

In 2023 attributable net income exceeded operating profit of KRW 4.06 billion, and the non-operating items behind that gap should be checked in the report body. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 11 billion in 2025, a different figure from attributable net income.

The debt ratio went from 153.3% in 2022 to 193.8% in 2023, 196.7% in 2024 and 186.8% in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 13.1 billion in 2022, KRW 12.4 billion in 2023, KRW 14.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 24.2 billion in 2025, the highest of the four years.

Quarterly sales fell from KRW 64.1 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 57.3 billion in the fourth quarter (down 10.6% quarter on quarter), bottomed at KRW 56.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 1.6%), and then rose to KRW 58.8 billion in the second quarter and KRW 60.7 billion in the third, two straight quarters of growth, although the third quarter was down 5.3% year on year.

Operating profit climbed to KRW 2.61 billion in the second quarter of 2026 before falling to KRW 1.11 billion in the third (down 57.7% quarter on quarter and 40.7% year on year). Vegan News reported that operating profit for the first nine months of 2026 was KRW 5.76 billion, down 43.9% year on year.

Attributable net income moved from a KRW 2.05 billion profit in the first quarter of 2026 to a net loss of KRW 1.41 billion in the second quarter and a wider net loss of KRW 1.74 billion in the third.

WiseReport's analysis as of June 2026 said first-quarter gross profit rose on US subsidiary injection-molding sales and cost improvements, while operating profit fell on lower domestic HVAC sales and higher China subsidiary selling costs.

Industry analysis

Sungchang Autotech's demand depends on automakers' production volumes and the mix of hybrid and electric vehicles. As of June 2026, WiseReport said that in the first quarter of 2026, rising hybrid (HEV) exports to the United States and larger electric vehicle (EV) volumes for Europe drove exports.

The same analysis also noted that total sales slipped slightly amid external uncertainty. Electrification parts add demand for inverters and electrical components, but volumes can swing sharply with automakers' EV production pace, so it is hard to read this as a single growth curve.

Volumes in the HVAC group move with the production trends of customers such as Hanon Systems, and the decline in domestic HVAC sales in the first quarter of 2026 is linked to that flow. Among auto parts peers, Seoyon E-Hwa, Sungwoo Hitech and Hanon Systems are the reference points.

Overseas entities made up 54% of sales in the third quarter of 2023, so exchange rates and local production conditions weigh heavily on earnings. Injection-molding sales at the US subsidiary improved in the first quarter of 2026, which supported the recovery in overseas operations.

Overall view

Sungchang Autotech's sales and operating profit rose from 2022 through 2025, so the direction of the recovery was clear. Operating profit of KRW 11.6 billion and an operating margin of 4.8% in 2025 were the highest of 2022 to 2025.

In 2026, however, quarterly sales rose for two straight quarters while operating profit fell to KRW 1.11 billion in the third quarter, and attributable net income posted losses in the second and third quarters.

With attributable net income over the last four quarters at KRW 728 million, the key question is whether the 2025 recovery carries into 2026 on an annual basis. Customer concentration around Hanon Systems, swings in overseas subsidiary results, and a debt ratio of 186.8% constrain the pace and extent of the recovery.

On the other side, rising US-bound hybrid exports, expansion of eco-friendly vehicle parts, and cost gains at the US subsidiary in the first quarter of 2026 are positive factors.

For now, bullish and bearish factors stand roughly even, and the picture should become clearer when fourth-quarter 2026 profit and the debt structure are confirmed in the annual report.
