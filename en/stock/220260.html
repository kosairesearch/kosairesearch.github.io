Semiconductor materials lift sales; profit trend is the test

02

Business structure

Chemtros, founded in 2006, is a chemical materials company that produces semiconductor process materials, secondary battery electrolyte additives, display and OLED materials, and pharmaceutical intermediates.

As of the first half of 2023, the advanced materials segment accounted for 70.2% of sales, including 34.6% from electrolyte additives and 15.0% from semiconductor and display materials, according to a November 2023 research report. The mix has since tilted sharply toward semiconductors.

Segment sales for the third quarter of 2026, announced by the company on November 13, 2026, were KRW 14.2 billion from semiconductor materials (56% of the total), KRW 3.8 billion from electrical and electronic adhesives (15%), and KRW 3.4 billion from electrolyte additives (14%).

The semiconductor line centers on KrF polymers, photoacid generators and EUV process materials used as photoresist (PR) raw materials, and supply to domestic PR makers is growing. Shinhan Investment Corp. said in an October 2026 report that Chemtros had widened its KrF polymer customer base to two companies.

Shinhan described the PR raw materials market as one where Japanese materials suppliers hold a high share, and said Chemtros is absorbing volume that is being localized. Supply capacity expanded as output from the Jincheon No. 3 plant ramped up from the first half of 2026.

By region, domestic sales were 70.1% and overseas sales 29.9% as of the first half of 2023. Compared with large petrochemical names such as LG Chem or Lotte Chemical, Chemtros is much smaller in scale and concentrated in specialty materials.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 57 billion in 2022 to KRW 47.5 billion in 2023 (down 16.8% year on year), then rose to KRW 50.3 billion in 2024 (up 6.0%) and KRW 56.8 billion in 2025 (up 12.9%), growing for two consecutive years. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest for sales.

Operating profit went from KRW 4.44 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.43 billion in 2023 (down 22.7%) and KRW 3.79 billion in 2024 (up 10.3%), then fell to KRW 1.05 billion in 2025 (down 72.3%), the smallest year in the period. In 2025 sales rose while the operating margin dropped from 7.5% in 2024 to 1.8%.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from KRW 5.14 billion in 2024 to a net loss of KRW 11.8 billion in 2025, and the total net loss was also KRW 11.8 billion. The fourth-quarter 2025 loss of KRW 10.5 billion accounts for most of that annual loss.

The debt-to-equity ratio, calculated as total liabilities divided by total equity, rose from 65.3% in 2022 to 88.6% in 2024 and 127.0% in 2025, when total liabilities of KRW 69.5 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 54.7 billion. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 10.6 billion in 2024 to KRW 585 million in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 645 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.33 billion in the fourth quarter, then KRW 2.19 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 5.7% quarter on quarter), KRW 3.58 billion in the second quarter (up 63.3%) and KRW 3.99 billion in the third quarter (up 11.2%, and a swing to profit from a year earlier).

According to the company, the product cost of sales ratio fell from 88% a year earlier to 76% in the first half of 2026, which it attributed to production efficiency and higher sales of value-added products.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders, however, went from KRW 8.46 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 3.77 billion in the second quarter (down 55.4%) and KRW 2.56 billion in the third quarter (down 32.2%), a two-quarter decline.

Media reports from May 14, 2026 said the first-quarter net profit included about KRW 6.1 billion of non-operating income from a reversal of interest expense recognized on warrant bonds converted into shares.

The sum of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 4.24 billion, which is small relative to the operating profit run-rate.

05

Industry analysis

Demand on the semiconductor side is rising as process miniaturization and higher NAND flash layer counts increase the need for photoresist raw materials. The company says domestic PR makers diversifying their supply chains and localizing materials also lifted results.

Shinhan Investment Corp., in its October 2026 report, pointed to the high Japanese supplier share in PR raw materials and said Chemtros is quickly absorbing localized volume. In cycle terms, sales growth is concentrated in a period of strong semiconductor demand.

A company official said in May 2026 that the strong chip market was continuing into the second quarter. The share of electrolyte additives in sales fell from 34.6% in the first half of 2023 to 14% in the third quarter of 2026. Shinhan expects this business to rebound from its 2025 low.

The same report expects the display and pharmaceutical and fusion materials businesses to keep sales near their historical averages. Compared with large petrochemical names, Chemtros has a clearly different profile, with a majority of its sales coming from semiconductor PR raw materials.

06

Outlook

In its November 13, 2026 announcement, the company said it is widening its KrF polymer customer base, expanding production capacity for EUV process materials, and preparing to move ArF photoresist raw materials into mass production.

The company said output from the Jincheon No. 3 plant expansion has been ramping since the first half of 2026. Shinhan Investment Corp. forecast in its October 2026 report that semiconductor materials sales would rise 23% from KRW 47.9 billion in 2026 to KRW 59.1 billion in 2027.

A consensus compiled by FnGuide and reported on October 27, 2026 puts full-year 2026 sales at KRW 89.4 billion and operating profit at KRW 1.33 billion... more precisely KRW 13.3 billion. That consensus assumes a large jump from KRW 56.8 billion in sales and KRW 1.05 billion in operating profit in 2025.

The gap between sales for the first three quarters of 2026 and the full-year consensus must be closed by fourth-quarter 2026 results. Consensus is an average of broker estimates and may differ from reported figures.

Outside semiconductors, adhesive sales rose 15% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, providing a secondary contribution.

08

Bull factors

Rising semiconductor share

The semiconductor share rose from 9.7% in 2023 to 51% in the first half of 2026 and 56% in the third quarter of 2026, according to company figures. Shinhan Investment Corp. forecast in October 2026 that semiconductor materials sales would rise 23% from KRW 47.9 billion in 2026 to KRW 59.1 billion in 2027. If this trend holds, semiconductors will carry a larger share of total sales.

Cost ratio improvement and margin recovery

According to the company, the product cost of sales ratio fell from 88% a year earlier to 76% in the first half of 2026.

The third-quarter 2026 operating margin of 16% was higher than 15% in the second quarter, and quarterly operating profit has been positive in each of the four quarters since the fourth quarter of 2025. Compared with the 1.8% annual operating margin for 2025, the earnings structure has shifted within a short period.

Capacity and customer base expansion

Output from the Jincheon No. 3 plant expansion has been rising since the first half of 2026, and supply to domestic PR makers has grown.

The KrF polymer customer base has widened to two companies, and the company said it is pursuing EUV process material capacity expansion and the mass-production transition for ArF raw materials. Continued broadening of the customer base is the bull-case argument here.

09

Bear factors

Lingering effects of the 2025 swing to loss

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 1.05 billion, down 72.3% from the prior year, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 11.8 billion. Of this, the fourth-quarter 2025 loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 10.5 billion.

Compared with KRW 4.44 billion of operating profit in 2022, earnings power is still in the middle of returning to earlier levels.

Softening net profit trend

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 8.46 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then fell to KRW 3.77 billion in the second quarter and KRW 2.56 billion in the third. Operating profit rose over the same period, so items below the operating line appear to account for the gap.

The first-quarter figure includes an accounting reversal, so quarter-to-quarter comparisons need to adjust for it.

Weaker cash generation

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 10.6 billion in 2024 to KRW 585 million in 2025, after KRW 4.81 billion in 2022. Over the same period the debt-to-equity ratio rose from 65.3% to 127.0%. Whether core cash generation holds up while capital spending and working capital rise is the open question.

10

Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

Semiconductor materials made up 56% of third-quarter 2026 sales, so exposure to a single demand chain has grown. Supply is concentrated among domestic PR makers and KrF polymer customers, so volumes could shift with customer sourcing decisions or competition from overseas suppliers.

With electrolyte additives down to 14% of sales, other businesses have limited room to cushion a slowdown in semiconductors.

Industry cycle and utilization

Strong chip demand is cited as a driver of sales growth, so a slowdown in the industry could reduce volumes and utilization together. Expansion at the Jincheon No. 3 plant and EUV capacity requires capital spending, and lower utilization would weaken the cost ratio gains. The 76% product cost of sales ratio depends on production efficiency and is therefore sensitive.

Leverage and earnings quality

A debt-to-equity ratio of 127.0% and total liabilities of KRW 69.5 billion add to the burden during a period of capacity expansion. The first-quarter 2026 profit included an accounting reversal, so operating profit gains did not translate one-for-one into net profit gains.

A four-quarter total of KRW 4.24 billion in net profit attributable to controlling shareholders signals high quarter-to-quarter volatility.

12

Overall view

Chemtros has shifted its business toward semiconductor materials, and quarterly sales grew for four consecutive quarters to KRW 25.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026. Operating profit has been positive in every quarter since the fourth quarter of 2025, moving away from the 2025 losses.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders, however, has declined for two quarters since its first-quarter peak, and the first-quarter figure includes an accounting reversal. The 127.0% debt-to-equity ratio and KRW 585 million of 2025 operating cash flow are financial items to monitor.

Shinhan Investment Corp. forecasts semiconductor materials sales rising to KRW 59.1 billion in 2027, and the FnGuide consensus puts 2026 full-year sales at KRW 89.4 billion. Both are estimates and may differ from reported results.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio sits below its post-2021 average, while the four-quarter net profit base is small. The next checkpoints are full-year 2026 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the annual report and the weight of non-operating items in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
