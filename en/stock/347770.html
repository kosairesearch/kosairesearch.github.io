Pims: Customer Expansion Amid Continued Losses

Pims is widening its OMM product scope and customer base for OLED deposition, but it has posted losses for five consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026.

  1. 1

    In 2025 Pims recorded an operating loss of KRW 6.11 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.22 billion, its second straight year of losses.

  2. 2

    Pims supplies OMM to Samsung Display's Gen 5.5 to Gen 8 lines on a de facto exclusive basis and completed supplier registration with LG Display in January 2026.

  3. 3

    The operating loss narrowed to KRW 139 million in the first quarter of 2026, when net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a KRW 155 million profit, but the company has posted net losses again since the second quarter.

  4. 4

    A Korean brokerage report dated October 21, 2026 argues that supplying integrated solutions covering frames, coatings and sticks could raise the supply value per unit by three to five times.

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Pims was founded in 2016 and designs, manufactures and sells open metal masks (OMM) used in OLED display deposition, listing on KOSDAQ in September 2020. Its core product is the standalone OMM, and it is now expanding into integrated solutions that include frames, coatings and sticks.

A Korean brokerage report dated October 21, 2026 put the supply price of a standalone OMM at KRW 4 million to KRW 5 million and the integrated solution at up to KRW 20 million, arguing that the supply value per unit could rise three to five times. The customer base is centered on Samsung Display.

The same report viewed Pims as a de facto exclusive OMM supplier for Samsung Display's lines from Gen 5.5 to Gen 8. That structure also means results can be heavily influenced by one customer's investment and line conversion schedules.

Pims completed its formal supplier registration with LG Display in January 2026, and an April 2026 news report said it had secured all major domestic OLED panel makers as customers. Application areas are broadening toward automotive OLED growth and possible use in robot OLEDs.

Competitively, Pims is moving from a supply structure centered on Samsung Display toward diversified customers such as LG Display.

04

Earnings analysis

On the basis of disclosed results, annual revenue rose from KRW 83.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 85.3 billion in 2023, an increase of 1.7% year on year, then fell to KRW 60.7 billion in 2024, a decline of 28.8%, before recovering to KRW 63.4 billion in 2025, up 4.4%.

Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest. Operating profit was KRW 1.12 billion in 2022 and KRW 2.6 billion in 2023, an increase of 132.7%, but the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 4.87 billion in 2024 and the loss widened to KRW 6.11 billion in 2025.

The operating margin fell from 3.0% in 2023 to -8.0% in 2024 and -9.6% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 2.8 billion in 2023 to a net loss of KRW 5.65 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.22 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, which is a different figure, was a net loss of KRW 8.33 billion in 2025 and should be read separately. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 46.0% in 2023 to 78.3% in 2025.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 45.4 billion, of which KRW 46.7 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 35.5 billion. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 9.13 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.73 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue went from KRW 11.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 14 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 15.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 17.6 billion in the second quarter, before reaching KRW 16.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

The operating loss widened from KRW 3.04 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 3.3 billion in the fourth quarter, narrowed sharply to KRW 139 million in the first quarter of 2026, then widened again to KRW 1.2 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.7 billion in the third quarter.

In the first quarter of 2026, when the operating loss was smallest, net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a profit of KRW 155 million, and the non-operating items behind that can be checked in that quarter's quarterly report.

Over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 7.77 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for Pims is tied to investment in and operation of OLED panel production lines. An April 2026 news report said Pims had posted operating losses for the prior two years amid a sluggish OLED market and slower penetration of automotive OLEDs, but that the OLED market has since entered a recovery phase.

A Korean brokerage report dated October 21, 2026 said the company's competitiveness stood out during industry restructuring. Because the timing of mask orders moves with downstream demand, panel makers' line expansions and generation transitions are key variables.

Automotive OLEDs have been cited as an application distinct from smartphone-centered demand, and possible use in robot OLEDs has also been mentioned. These points come from brokerage analysis and news reports, not from disclosed figures.

On competition, Pims is differentiated by its move from heavy reliance on Samsung Display toward supplier registration with LG Display. Reports that it now serves all major domestic panel makers suggest a broader supply base.

Even so, until revenue grows meaningfully, a larger customer base alone is unlikely to change the loss pattern seen in disclosed results.

Overall view

Pims is broadening its customers and products along two lines, the LG Display supplier registration and expansion of integrated solutions, on top of an OMM supply structure centered on Samsung Display.

Disclosed results show operating losses and widening net losses since the turn to losses in 2024, with the 2025 operating loss of KRW 6.11 billion and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.22 billion as the latest annual figures.

The operating loss narrowed to KRW 139 million and net income turned to a profit in the first quarter of 2026, but net losses have returned since the second quarter of 2026. A Korean brokerage expects a return to profit and new revenue in 2027, but this is an estimate.

The share price sits at a large discount to book value, and the lack of a stable earnings base is part of the context for that. The trading suspension tied to the share consolidation and the listing schedule for the new shares are matters to confirm through disclosures.

The next checkpoints are the start of supply to LG Display, the timing of integrated solution supply, and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
