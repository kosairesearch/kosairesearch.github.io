02

Business structure

Zinitix is a fabless system semiconductor company that designs and supplies touch controller ICs, haptic ICs, AF driver ICs and DC-DC ICs. Touch ICs made up 71.4% of 2025 revenue, while other products accounted for 28.6%.

Since 2023 the company has supplied touchpad ICs for notebooks to a global set maker and has kept its share of that customer above 60%. Since 2024 it has also supplied all tablet book-cover models.

In wearables it has supplied ICs to Samsung Electronics, Xiaomi and BBK, and it has supplied about 2 million touch controller ICs for Amazon's Halo View fitness band since 2022. In a June 2025 CEO interview, the company said it had resumed touch IC orders for Samsung Electronics phones.

The company said in 2024 that it would expand into IT touchpad modules for notebooks, a strategy pursued since its largest shareholder, Halo Microelectronics, joined in August 2024. It is now expanding into physical AI, robot sensor prototypes and GaN driver ICs for AI PCs.

Its core touch technology and intellectual property are presented as key strengths. Dependence on its main product line remains high, so diversification is still an open task.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 27.6 billion in 2022, KRW 33.1 billion in 2023 (up 19.8% year on year) and KRW 54.1 billion in 2024 (up 63.5%), before falling to KRW 36.4 billion in 2025 (down 32.7%). 2024 was the largest year in the period.

The operating margin was -18.9% in 2022 and -18.3% in 2023, and it turned positive at 0.5% in 2024, when operating profit was KRW 282 million. In 2025 the company returned to an operating loss of KRW 8.55 billion, and the operating margin was -23.5%.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 653 million in 2024, above operating profit, which indicates non-operating items added to earnings. The 2025 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 9.58 billion.

Operating cash flow moved from KRW 4.79 billion of inflow in 2024 to -KRW 10.1 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 41.4% in 2022 to 145.4% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 8.94 billion and total liabilities of KRW 13 billion at the end of 2025.

Quarterly, revenue was KRW 9.58 billion in the third quarter of 2025 with an operating loss of KRW 1.9 billion. In the fourth quarter revenue fell 20.3% sequentially to KRW 7.64 billion and the operating loss widened to KRW 4.19 billion.

Revenue then rose for three consecutive quarters: KRW 8.51 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (operating loss KRW 862 million), KRW 9.11 billion in the second quarter (operating loss KRW 1.01 billion) and KRW 10.8 billion in the third quarter (operating loss KRW 1.65 billion).

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 8.04 billion. Reports said that higher non-operating income, including foreign exchange gains, helped narrow the third-quarter net loss, while rising raw material purchase prices weighed on operating results.

05

Industry analysis

Touch ICs started in smartphones and wearables and are now spreading to notebooks, tablets, automotive, home appliances and drones. In its DART quarterly report, the company said touch input is spreading to automotive, appliances and IoT devices, and it expects the touch IC market to keep growing.

This is the company's own outlook and should be read separately from third-party market data. In notebooks, the company has pointed to growth potential in AI notebooks and treats touchpad modules as a new growth driver.

TDDI integrates touch and display driver functions into one chip and is generally expected to carry a higher average selling price than conventional touch ICs. On costs, the company attributed the third-quarter 2026 operating loss partly to higher raw material purchase prices.

It is also pursuing price increases on existing products, and an independent research house said negotiations with major customers were proceeding smoothly.

On competition, the company stated in DART filings that it offers a full touch IC lineup across sizes and display types, from wearables to tablets up to 10.1 inches. As a fabless company, its revenue depends heavily on set demand and on customer adoption decisions.

06

Outlook

The company has pointed to three new growth areas. First, its CEO said in a July 2026 interview that TDDI is scheduled for commercialization between late 2026 and early 2027, and that Google has shown strong interest and is reviewing adoption.

Second, the company planned to complete a Chinese local subsidiary between late July and early August 2026 and intends to co-develop AI PC business there, using the local influence of its largest shareholder.

Third, it has begun developing high-voltage haptic driver ICs and is discussing in-vehicle large-display touch and haptic applications with Tesla. It also said it would expand cooperation with Amazon.

GaN driver ICs and robot sensor prototypes remain in development, with mass-production applications to be pursued in stages. On shareholder returns, the company said in the July 2026 interview that it would carry out measures including share buybacks after the extraordinary shareholders' meeting on August 10.

At the time of its third-quarter 2026 results, the company said it would focus on easing cost pressure and improving efficiency while expanding supply of its main products. An independent research house's August 2026 report said TDDI mass production and the AI infrastructure business could enlarge revenue from 2027.

If executed as planned, it estimated more than KRW 15 billion of revenue from AI infrastructure alone from 2027, while noting that the business still needs initial orders after the subsidiary's establishment.

Key items to confirm are when TDDI revenue actually begins and the order status of the AI infrastructure business.

08

Bull factors

TDDI commercialization and possible price increases

TDDI integrates touch and display driver functions, and an independent research house analyzed that it could carry a higher average selling price than conventional touch ICs. The company said in a July 2026 interview that commercialization is scheduled between late 2026 and early 2027. The analysis suggests that mass production could lift both sales volume and margins.

China AI infrastructure and price increases on existing products

An independent research house estimated that the China AI infrastructure business could generate more than KRW 15 billion of revenue from 2027 if it proceeds as planned. It also reported that price increase talks on existing products were proceeding smoothly with major customers. Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 18.5% from the prior quarter to KRW 10.8 billion, continuing the growth trend.

Customer base and cost efficiency

The company has kept its share of a global notebook customer's touchpad supply above 60%, and it has supplied all tablet book-cover models since 2024. In its preliminary third-quarter 2026 release, the company said selling and administrative expenses fell year on year. The operating loss also narrowed from a year earlier.

09

Bear factors

Continuing operating losses and cost pressure

Operating losses have continued for five straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026. The 2025 operating loss was KRW 8.55 billion, with an operating margin of -23.5%. The company attributed the loss partly to higher raw material purchase prices. Cost pressure could persist until price increases take effect.

Touch IC concentration and revenue decline

Revenue fell 32.7% to KRW 36.4 billion in 2025. Touch ICs made up 71.4% of total revenue, which leaves the company highly dependent on one product family. Products outside touch ICs accounted for only 28.6% of revenue, so the diversification effect remains limited.

Financial strain and capital raising

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 41.4% in 2022 to 145.4% in 2025. Operating cash flow was -KRW 10.1 billion in 2025. In March 2026 the company raised about KRW 6.3 billion through a third-party allotment to its largest shareholder, according to disclosure-based reports.

10

Risk factors

Customer and set-demand concentration

Revenue from notebook touchpads and mobile touch ICs depends heavily on adoption by a small number of set makers and on their product cycles. Lower purchases or weak set shipments could cut revenue quickly. The 32.7% decline in 2025 revenue illustrates this exposure.

Timing of new businesses

TDDI has a mass-production target within 2026 and a commercialization plan between late 2026 and early 2027, so the schedule could slip. The China AI infrastructure business still needs initial orders after its subsidiary was established. If new-business revenue arrives later than expected, the timing of an earnings recovery could also shift.

Funding structure and dependence on the largest shareholder

The largest shareholder, Halo Microelectronics, held about 57% as of the end of June 2026, according to a market data provider. The March 2026 capital raise was also a third-party allotment to that shareholder. With the debt-to-equity ratio at 145.4%, any further funding could bring both dilution and financial strain.

12

Overall view

Zinitix is a fabless company centered on touch ICs. It returned to an operating loss in 2025 after turning profitable in 2024. Revenue rose for three consecutive quarters in 2026, and the third-quarter 2026 operating loss narrowed from a year earlier.

Even so, operating losses have continued for five straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 8.04 billion.

Bullish factors include possible price gains from TDDI, the China AI infrastructure business and steady customer relationships. Bearish factors include touch IC concentration at 71.4%, a debt-to-equity ratio of 145.4% and a funding structure centered on the largest shareholder.

On valuation, the price-to-earnings ratio cannot be calculated and the price-to-book ratio is below 1x. The next items to confirm are the timing of TDDI commercialization, the first orders in China AI infrastructure, and the full-year results and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report.
