02

Business structure

Duksan Navcours is a navigation and anti-jamming specialist founded in December 2012, and it listed on KOSDAQ on September 30, 2026. Its portfolio covers satellite navigation, anti-jamming, integrated navigation and datalink equipment, handled from design through development, production and testing.

Its parent is DUKSAN HIMETALCO, which is a subsidiary of Duksan Holdings. In 2025, sales were split among Hanwha Aerospace (34%), LIG Defense & Aerospace (33%), Hyundai Rotem (15%) and others (18%). The company says it takes part in 82% of the 72 domestic mass-produced weapon systems where its products can be applied.

Its main product is the navigation unit for the Chunmoo guided rocket, and medium-altitude UAV datalinks are already in mass production.

Products in development include a trajectory-correcting fuze for 155mm shells that adds navigation and anti-jamming functions, the signal-processing unit of the RF seeker for the LAMD system, and counter-drone systems.

In space, it has supplied satellite navigation receivers for Nuri launches 1 through 5 and is pursuing low-earth-orbit satellite programs and the Korean Positioning System (KPS).

Its main customers are defense system integrators and government research institutes, and because the business is order-driven, cash flow depends on advance and interim payment schedules. Its order backlog stood at KRW 56.9 billion in Q1 2026, which is larger than 2025 sales of KRW 48.5 billion. Of its 184 employees, 79 (43%) work in R&D, and 34 of them hold doctoral or master's degrees.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 35 billion in 2022 to KRW 31.4 billion in 2023 (down 10.4% year on year), then rose to KRW 45.2 billion in 2024 (up 44.1%) and KRW 48.5 billion in 2025 (up 7.2%). Sales have risen for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in 2022–2025.

The company said 2024 sales were a record since its founding and cited growth in export business under the K-defense push, higher demand for anti-jamming technology, expanded test facilities and higher navigation-solution sales.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.38 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 5.83 billion in 2023, turned positive at KRW 2 billion in 2024, and reached KRW 4.08 billion in 2025 (up 103.6%). Operating margin ran at 3.9% in 2022, -18.6% in 2023, 4.4% in 2024 and 8.4% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a net loss of KRW 4.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 1.35 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.67 billion in 2025 (up 171.0%).

The debt ratio fell from 167.5% in 2023 to 72.6% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 30.6 billion and total liabilities of KRW 22.2 billion at year-end 2025. Operating cash flow went from KRW 7.46 billion in 2024 to -KRW 420 million in 2025, moving in the opposite direction from earnings.

Quarterly sales peaked at KRW 17.4 billion in Q4 2025, then fell 31.0% to KRW 12 billion in Q1 2026 and 1.5% to KRW 11.8 billion in Q2 2026, marking two consecutive quarterly declines. Q2 2026 sales were down 5.6% year on year.

Operating profit was KRW 1.95 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 227 million in Q1 2026 (down 88.4%) and KRW 524 million in Q2 2026, which is down 67.6% year on year. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 2.89 billion.

In Q1 2026, operating profit fell far more steeply than sales, so quarterly profitability moved more sharply than revenue.

05

Industry analysis

The company argues that GNSS jamming and radio interference threats are growing and that drone use is expanding, which raises the importance of navigation and anti-jamming technology.

It also says advancing defense capabilities are turning navigation and anti-jamming into core elements of next-generation weapon systems. Defense business results, however, move with the budgets and program schedules of large system integrators and government research institutes.

The company said its estimates assume government force-modernization plans such as the mid-term defense plan. A market research firm named the company as the only Asian firm in its global top 20 for anti-jamming technology. Continued participation in Chunmoo export programs links it to the K-defense export trend.

In counter-drone work, it has signed a memorandum of understanding with the Korea Anti-Drone Industry Association. The sector label is semiconductors, but the business is closer to defense and space navigation equipment.

Because demand drivers and business structures differ, Samsung Electronics and SK hynix are not straightforward comparisons. Against competitors, the company points to mass production of Chunmoo navigation units and datalinks, and to its anti-jamming certifications as key differentiators.

06

Outlook

The company has set targets of KRW 60.3 billion in sales for 2026, KRW 115.4 billion for 2028 and KRW 174.7 billion for 2030. The 2026 target of KRW 60.3 billion compares with KRW 48.5 billion in 2025, and Q1 and Q2 2026 sales together total KRW 23.8 billion.

The 2028 estimate in the registration statement puts sales at KRW 115.369 billion, operating profit at KRW 22.2 billion and net income at KRW 19.0 billion. Reporting on the filing noted that this estimate assumes sales rise 2.38 times and net income rises 5.18 times from 2025.

The company said the sales it projects are achievable as large core programs move into full production from 2027. It also says current capacity cannot cover planned production volumes, so a factory is needed.

Of the total offering proceeds of KRW 36.7 billion, KRW 20.3 billion is earmarked for factory and equipment and KRW 16.4 billion for R&D such as miniaturization and advancement of the technology.

The pipeline includes the trajectory-correcting fuze, the LAMD RF seeker signal-processing unit, counter-drone systems and medium-altitude UAV datalinks. An order backlog of KRW 56.9 billion as of Q1 2026 is cited as support for this pipeline.

Progress against the targets will show up in how quickly quarterly sales recover and in the factory expansion schedule.

08

Bull factors

Order backlog and production pipeline

The order backlog is larger than 2025 sales, which means volume still to be recognized as revenue has built up.

Navigation units for the Chunmoo guided rocket and medium-altitude UAV datalinks are in production, while the 155mm trajectory-correcting fuze and the LAMD seeker signal-processing unit are in development and production stages. Full-scale production of large programs is indicated from 2027.

Operating margin recovery

Operating margin climbed from -18.6% in 2023 to 8.4% in 2025. Operating profit reached KRW 4.08 billion in 2025, up 103.6% year on year. The company credits production process improvements for higher output efficiency. The debt ratio also fell from 167.5% to 72.6% over the same period.

Technology and certifications

The company says it became the first domestic PBA manufacturer to hold NADCAP Gold accreditation. It received A ratings in the technology evaluation for its technology-based listing. A market research firm listed it as the only Asian company in its global top 20 for anti-jamming technology. About 43% of its employees work in R&D.

09

Bear factors

Customer concentration

According to a media analysis, a single customer accounted for 35.89% of 2025 sales, and the top two customers for 68.47%. In Q1 2026 the top two share rose to 73.31%. Because sales depend on a few programs, a delay in one of them directly hits results.

Weak cash flow

Operating cash flow was -KRW 420 million in 2025, while net income was KRW 3.67 billion. The company acknowledges that negative operating cash flow appears despite positive reported earnings. Improvement will show up in upcoming periodic reports.

Slowing quarterly results

After peaking at KRW 17.4 billion in Q4 2025, sales fell to KRW 12 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 11.8 billion in Q2 2026, two consecutive quarterly declines. Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 524 million was down 67.6% year on year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totals a small KRW 2.89 billion.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and program delays

Defense system integrators and government research institutes are the main customers, and results move with budgets and program progress. With the top two customers at 73.31% of Q1 2026 sales, a delay in one program has a large effect on quarterly results. Much of the revenue is also tied to specific weapon systems, such as the Chunmoo navigation unit.

Cash flow and capital spending

Even with profits in 2025, operating cash flow was negative, leaving working capital management as an open issue. The company says a factory expansion is needed to meet planned production volumes. The pace at which the KRW 20.3 billion earmarked for facilities is spent could determine when added capacity arrives.

Governance and dual listing

The company listed after securing general shareholder consent at its parent, DUKSAN HIMETALCO, and it is the first case allowed under the exception to the dual-listing restriction. The exchange treats whether a subsidiary listing infringes the rights of the parent's general shareholders as a core review item.

The ownership structure and any intragroup dealings could still draw debate over shareholder rights and conflicts of interest.

Overall view

Duksan Navcours moved from an operating loss of KRW 5.83 billion in 2023 to an 8.4% operating margin in 2025, and it continues mass production of Chunmoo navigation units and medium-altitude UAV datalinks. The KRW 56.9 billion order backlog at Q1 2026 exceeds 2025 sales, which points to a medium-term order base.

On the other hand, quarterly sales have declined for two straight quarters in 2026, and Q2 operating profit fell 67.6% year on year. Customers are concentrated among major system integrators, with the top two at 73.31% of Q1 2026 sales. Operating cash flow was -KRW 420 million in 2025, moving opposite to net income.

The 2028 sales estimate of KRW 115.369 billion assumes a 2.38-fold increase from 2025, which the company says depends on large-program production from 2027 and factory expansion.

The next checkpoints are the Q3 2026 quarterly report, sales and cash flow in the 2026 annual report, and whether production starts from 2027 as planned.
