02

Business structure

SamsungFN REIT is a listed office REIT sponsored by Samsung Life Insurance and Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance and managed by Samsung SRA Asset Management.

Its portfolio consists of five office assets: FN Tower Daechi in Gangnam, S-One Tower (FN Tower Suhwa) in Jung-gu, FN Tower Pangyo in Seongnam, FN Tower Jamsil (formerly the Samsung Life Jamsil Building) in Songpa-gu, and A1 Tower Dangsan in Yeongdeungpo-gu.

As of end-April 2026, the portfolio's average occupancy stood at 97.8%, with FN Tower Daechi at 99.0%, FN Tower Suhwa and Pangyo at 100%, and FN Tower Jamsil at 92.1%.

FN Tower Jamsil's key tenants include Woowa Brothers and Samsung affiliates, while A1 Tower Dangsan is largely leased to Samsung Life Service and other Samsung financial affiliates. Rental income is generated through long-term leases with sponsor affiliates combined with quality non-group tenants.

Competitors include office-focused listed REITs such as SK Reit, Lotte Reit, Hanwha REIT and Shinhan Alpha REIT, which have shown a similar pattern of adding new assets each second half.

In 2026, the company acquired A1 Tower Dangsan from NH All-One REIT in what was described as the first direct real-estate transaction between two listed REITs in Korea, furthering portfolio diversification.

This acquisition approach aligns with the company's financial strategy of expanding its asset base without resorting to large-scale rights offerings.

04

Earnings analysis

Per disclosed annual figures, revenue was flat at KRW 9.28 billion in both 2022 and 2023, then rose to KRW 10.2 billion in 2024 (+9.8% YoY) and KRW 12.2 billion in 2025 (+20.1% YoY), extending a two-year increase from the 2023 trough.

Operating profit fell from KRW 6.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.61 billion in 2023 (-11.0%), then rose to KRW 5.93 billion in 2024 (+5.7%) and KRW 7.4 billion in 2025 (+24.8%), likewise increasing for two consecutive years after 2023.

The operating margin declined from 67.9% in 2022 to 60.5% in 2023 and 58.2% in 2024, before rebounding to 60.5% in 2025.

Controlling-interest net income fell from KRW 1.14 billion in 2022 to KRW 458 million in 2023 (-59.8%) and KRW 361 million in 2024 (-21.1%), then surged to KRW 3.07 billion in 2025, up 749.0% YoY — 2025 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest within 2022-2025.

The 2025 margin rebound suggests revenue growth outpaced cost growth that year. The debt ratio rose every year, from 97.4% in 2022 to 98.9% in 2023, 110.4% in 2024 and 113.5% in 2025, indicating that asset acquisitions were accompanied by expanding borrowings.

Operating cash flow improved markedly, from KRW 2.61 billion in 2022 and KRW 2.19 billion in 2023 to KRW 5.22 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.57 billion in 2025. The sum of controlling-interest net income over the last four quarters (Q1-Q4 2025) was KRW 3.07 billion, matching the annual figure.

After the disclosed period, the company completed the Jamsil and Dangsan acquisitions in 2026, and according to industry reports the 15th-period (May-July 2026) operating revenue reached KRW 15.1 billion, up 13.0% from the prior period, while net income fell to roughly KRW 2.9 billion, down 10.9%, due to rising interest expense (provisional, per an October 2026 report).

Industry analysis

Listed Korean office REITs have attracted investors by pointing to expectations of lower interest rates and stable dividends backed by quality tenants, but the method of funding new asset acquisitions has recently become a key industry variable.

Many REITs had relied on rights offerings to fund acquisitions, but repeated share dilution and subsequent price declines built up investor frustration. As a result, by 2026 a clear shift toward debt-based funding such as corporate bonds and short-term notes emerged across listed REITs including SamsungFN REIT.

In February 2026, SamsungFN REIT achieved a successful debut in the public corporate bond market, drawing KRW 400 billion in institutional orders against a KRW 200 billion target.

In the second half of 2026, SamsungFN REIT's acquisition of A1 Tower Dangsan from NH All-One REIT marked the first direct real-estate transaction between two listed REITs in Korea.

Industry observers noted that listed REITs in the second half have prioritized acquiring sponsor-linked assets with secured tenants to reduce vacancy risk. Peers such as SK Reit, Lotte Reit and Hanwha REIT have shown a similar pattern of adding new assets annually, following comparable growth paths.

06

Outlook

SamsungFN REIT is pursuing a 19-million-share rights offering to repay short-term bonds used to fund the A1 Tower Dangsan acquisition, with expected proceeds of KRW 97.7 billion based on a first-stage issue price of KRW 5,140.

The final issue price is set to be determined on October 29, with payment due on November 11; once completed, shares outstanding would rise 20.9%, from 91.05 million to 110.05 million.

The company said the Dangsan acquisition expanded total assets to roughly KRW 1.25 trillion, laying the groundwork for pursuing larger prime assets going forward.

An existing KRW 430 billion collateral loan and KRW 90 billion in short-term bonds were refinanced into a KRW 520 billion senior secured loan completed on September 28, 2026. The company stated that while the collateral loan rate could rise from prior levels, it would not be burdensome.

However, since Dangsan's rental income and funding costs will only be reflected from the 16th fiscal period onward, how the increased share count affects per-share dividend levels will need to be confirmed in the next quarterly dividend disclosure.

Over the medium term, the company reportedly keeps open the possibility of further diversifying funding through secured bond issuance.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly Dividend Stability

SamsungFN REIT has maintained quarterly dividends four times a year, declaring a dividend of KRW 69 per share again in September 2026.

Backed by the credit standing of sponsors Samsung Life Insurance and Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance, its February 2026 corporate bond demand reached roughly twice the targeted amount, confirming funding stability. This reflects a steadily expanding rental income base underpinning dividend resources.

Asset Growth Without Large-Scale Dilution

In 2026, SamsungFN REIT acquired both FN Tower Jamsil and A1 Tower Dangsan while prioritizing debt-based funding such as corporate bonds and short-term notes, minimizing equity dilution.

Notably, the Dangsan acquisition was sized within 30% of total assets, allowing a simpler regulatory notification rather than requiring formal approval from the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport. As a result, total assets expanded to roughly KRW 1.25 trillion.

Earnings Recovery Trend

Controlling-interest net income, which had fallen to KRW 361 million in 2024, rose sharply to KRW 3.07 billion in 2025, while the operating margin improved from 58.2% in 2024 to 60.5% in 2025. Both revenue and operating profit extended two consecutive years of growth from the 2023 trough.

Operating cash flow also rose from the KRW 2 billion range in 2022-2023 to the KRW 4-5 billion range in 2024-2025, improving the cash conversion of earnings.

Bear factors

Dilution Risk from Rights Offering

A 19-million-share rights offering, aimed at repaying KRW 80 billion in short-term bonds used for the Dangsan acquisition, targets payment on November 11, 2026, and shares outstanding would rise 20.9% once completed.

Industry observers note that maintaining the per-share dividend may be difficult unless total dividend payouts rise in step. Indeed, over the last three fiscal periods a sizable portion of dividend funding reportedly came from capital surplus transfers rather than net income, per an October 2026 report (provisional).

Rising Leverage and Interest Costs

The debt ratio rose every year from 97.4% in 2022 to 113.5% in 2025, and borrowings kept expanding into 2026 through a first KRW 200 billion public corporate bond issuance and a KRW 520 billion collateral loan refinancing.

In the 15th period (May-July 2026), interest expense reportedly rose 21.4% from the prior period and became a primary factor behind the decline in net income, per an October 2026 report (provisional). If further acquisitions continue, rising debt burdens could constrain the pace of net income improvement.

Tenant Concentration

Many assets in the portfolio are concentrated among sponsor affiliates such as Samsung Life Insurance and Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance, and a small number of large tenants.

Samsung financial affiliates including Samsung Life Service account for roughly 87.3% of A1 Tower Dangsan, while Woowa Brothers and Samsung affiliates occupy a large portion of FN Tower Jamsil.

A structure heavily dependent on specific tenants or a single corporate group could increase rental income volatility depending on future lease renewal terms or industry changes.

10

Risk factors

Interest Rate and Funding Risk

The company funds itself through a mix of collateral loans, corporate bonds and short-term notes, so interest expense sensitivity to market rate changes directly affects earnings.

In September 2026, when an existing collateral loan and short-term bonds were refinanced into a KRW 520 billion facility, the possibility of higher rate terms than before was raised. If further acquisitions follow, both the size of borrowings and interest coverage capacity will need continued monitoring.

Rights Offering Execution Risk

The issue price for the rights offering scheduled for November 2026 has not yet been finalized, and if the final price comes in below the first-stage price, the proceeds raised and dilution ratio could change.

If total dividend payouts do not rise proportionally after the new shares are allocated, downward pressure on per-share dividends could follow. Both the completion of the offering and its actual effect on per-share dividends need to be confirmed in the next quarterly dividend disclosure.

Tenant and Asset Concentration Risk

Many of the portfolio's five assets depend on Samsung financial affiliates or a small number of large tenants, so renewal decisions by specific tenants could have an immediate effect on rental income.

The Dangsan asset is located in a non-core submarket rather than a prime business district, which could differentiate it from core-district assets in terms of future rent growth potential or valuation. As more newly acquired assets are added, managing profitability gaps across the asset base remains an ongoing task.

Overall view

SamsungFN REIT showed a clear recovery through 2025, with disclosed revenue and operating profit both rising for two consecutive years and controlling-interest net income up 749.0% year-over-year.

In 2026, the company expanded total assets to roughly KRW 1.25 trillion through the acquisitions of FN Tower Jamsil and A1 Tower Dangsan, utilizing debt-centered funding including its first public corporate bond issuance and a collateral loan refinancing.

However, the debt ratio has continued its multi-year climb, rising from 97.4% in 2022 to 113.5% in 2025, and a 19-million-share rights offering scheduled for November 2026 is expected to raise shares outstanding by 20.9%.

Recent quarterly dividends reportedly exceeded net income on several occasions, funded instead by capital surplus transfers, per an October 2026 report (provisional).

With rental income growth from asset expansion and cost/dilution pressure from the offering and borrowings unfolding at the same time, the next quarterly dividend disclosure and the final terms of the rights offering will be key variables for gauging future per-share metrics.
