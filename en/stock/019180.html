02

Business structure

THN is an automotive parts maker founded in 1986 in Daegu, and its core product is the wire harness that carries power and data inside a vehicle. Its main customer is the Hyundai Motor Group, and it operates overseas subsidiaries in China, Brazil, Paraguay, the Philippines, Vietnam and Mexico.

According to WISE Report's compilation, wire harness sales in December 2025 were 40.21% domestic and 59.79% export, so exports account for more than half. The company is pursuing new businesses such as autonomous and future vehicles alongside a stable supply chain, and it emphasizes business diversification.

In May 2025 THN took over a related company's wire harness business for KRW 9.8 billion, and Asia Economy reported that this added a quarterly revenue contribution in the KRW 30 billion range. The same report cited stronger bargaining power in raw material sourcing and lower shared costs as scale benefits.

R&D spending in 2025 was about KRW 17.7 billion, or 1.79% of revenue (WISE Report compilation). On competition, expanding wire harness share within the Hyundai Motor Group is the key variable, and a research house cited this share expansion as a basis for growth in a May 2026 report. The product mix is shifting toward high-specification harnesses for electric and hybrid vehicles.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results recovered clearly from a low base in 2022 through 2025.

Revenue rose from KRW 469.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 586.3 billion in 2023 (up 24.8% year on year), KRW 651.1 billion in 2024 (up 11.0%) and KRW 989.1 billion in 2025 (up 51.9%), marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025. 2025 was the largest year of 2022 through 2025 by revenue.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.13 billion in 2022 to KRW 43.1 billion in 2023 (up 3,715.6%), fell to KRW 31 billion in 2024 (down 28.1%), and recovered to KRW 70.1 billion in 2025 (up 126.3%). In 2024 revenue grew 11.0% while the operating margin fell to 4.8% from 7.3% in 2023.

The 2025 operating margin rebounded to 7.1%. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a net loss of KRW 4.76 billion in 2022 to KRW 28.4 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 31.8 billion in 2024 (up 12.0%) and KRW 68.7 billion in 2025 (up 115.6%), with growth in both 2024 and 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests is a different figure and was KRW 33.5 billion in 2024. Operating cash flow increased from KRW 29.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 72.1 billion in 2025, so cash generation moved in the same direction as profit.

At end-2025 total equity was KRW 226.2 billion, of which KRW 224.7 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 386.9 billion. On a quarterly basis, the peak was Q4 2025, with revenue of KRW 289.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 24.3 billion.

Revenue then fell to KRW 276.3 billion in Q1 2026 (down 4.5% from the prior quarter) and operating profit to KRW 17.5 billion (down 27.9%), followed by KRW 287.7 billion and KRW 15.5 billion in Q2. In Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 253.2 billion (down 8.6% year on year) and operating profit KRW 7.2 billion (down 66.7%).

Operating profit has declined for three consecutive quarters from Q1 2026 through Q3 2026. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.78 billion in Q3 2026, down 78.5% year on year, and the last four quarters total KRW 58.4 billion.

A WISE Report compilation shows Q1 2026 operating profit up 417.4% year on year, but the quarterly trend has turned down since the Q4 2025 peak.

05

Industry analysis

The key variables for the end market are vehicle electrification and the shift toward autonomous driving. In a May 26, 2026 report, a research house argued that wire harness application volume and unit prices rise as vehicles move to electric and autonomous designs.

The same report cited share expansion within the Hyundai Motor Group and a product mix shift toward electric and hybrid models as growth drivers.

A domestic brokerage's March 2026 report explained that high-specification SUVs and eco-friendly models, including the hybrid version of the Palisade, use more advanced harnesses and therefore lift profitability. On inputs, copper price swings were identified as a factor affecting margins.

Separately from electrification, the May 2026 report presented the possible entry into humanoid robot electronics as a medium-term growth factor.

Viewed through the cycle, the 2025 revenue increase reflected new model launches overlapping with the business acquisition, while Q3 2026 revenue fell year on year, so new-model effects have not been even across quarters.

As a first-tier supplier, the company's competitive position depends heavily on volume from a single vehicle group.

06

Outlook

The outlook will be gauged by full-year 2026 results and whether Q4 recovers. In a May 26, 2026 report, a research house estimated 2026 consolidated revenue at KRW 1,206.3 billion and operating profit at KRW 86.5 billion, described as increases of 22% and 23.4% year on year.

That estimate runs in a different direction from the disclosed quarterly trend, in which operating profit declined for three consecutive quarters through Q3 2026. Reaching the estimate would require a substantial revenue recovery in Q4.

Market commentary points to the effects of the new Seltos and the new Brazilian HB20 models. The same research house expects the full-year effect of the 2025 business acquisition to lift profitability through scale benefits in 2026.

The move into robot electronics is a medium-term option not yet reflected in disclosed results, and whether concrete orders or revenue plans emerge is the next test. The next checkpoint is the full-year operating profit and Q4 figures in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Earnings power recovered in 2025

The operating margin rose from 0.2% in 2022 to 7.1% in 2025, and operating cash flow grew from KRW 29.9 billion to KRW 72.1 billion, so cash and profit moved together. Compared with the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in 2022, the company posted KRW 68.7 billion in 2025. The margin rise from 4.8% in 2024 is read as a sign that profitability kept pace with revenue expansion.

Scale from the acquisition and high-spec mix

Reports say the KRW 9.8 billion business acquisition in May 2025 added a quarterly revenue contribution in the KRW 30 billion range. Bargaining power in raw material sourcing and lower shared costs are cited as scale benefits.

A domestic brokerage's March 2026 report explained that higher volumes of SUV and eco-friendly models using advanced harnesses support profitability.

Electrification and robotics expansion case

In a May 26, 2026 report, a research house argued that wire harness content and unit prices rise as vehicles shift to electric and autonomous designs. The same report pointed to the possible entry into humanoid robot electronics as a medium-term growth factor.

This is a brokerage forecast, and robot-related revenue does not appear as a separate line in the disclosed results.

09

Bear factors

Quarterly profit down for three straight quarters

Operating profit peaked at KRW 24.3 billion in Q4 2025 and then fell to KRW 17.5 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 15.5 billion in Q2 and KRW 7.2 billion in Q3, a decline across three consecutive quarters.

Q3 2026 operating profit was down 66.7% year on year, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 78.5% to KRW 4.78 billion. Q3 revenue of KRW 253.2 billion was down 8.6% year on year, so lower sales and lower profit occurred together.

Customer and model concentration, raw material exposure

Dependence on the Hyundai Motor Group is high, and a domestic brokerage cited reliance on specific models and copper price effects as discount factors. According to a WISE Report compilation, 59.79% of wire harness sales in December 2025 were exports, which exposes the company to overseas demand and currency moves.

The operating margin recovered to 7.1% in 2025, but it could fall again if raw material costs or model mix shift.

Debt burden and shareholder transfer

At end-2025 total liabilities were KRW 386.9 billion, larger than total equity of KRW 226.2 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio was 171.1%. In January 2026 a disclosure reported that 3,686,920 common shares held by the de facto controlling shareholder were transferred through inheritance.

A research house judged the overhang from that transfer to be nearing its end, but the transfer could still weigh on share supply and demand.

10

Risk factors

Customer and model concentration

Revenue depends heavily on the Hyundai Motor Group and on volumes from specific models, which increases earnings volatility. Whether the Q3 2026 revenue decline reflects model changeovers or volume adjustments cannot be separated from the disclosed figures alone. If new model launches slip, the volume gains seen in 2025 could weaken.

Raw materials and currency

Copper price swings feed directly into cost of sales. With exports at 59.79% of wire harness sales, currency moves can affect profit and overseas subsidiary results. The 2025 operating margin of 7.1% could shift quickly if input cost conditions change.

Capital structure and ownership

Total liabilities of KRW 386.9 billion and a debt-to-equity ratio of 171.1% make the company sensitive to changes in interest rates and borrowing terms. Following the January 2026 inheritance transfer of major shareholder shares, whether those shares are sold remains a supply variable.

Because non-controlling interests exist, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders and total net profit show different figures.

Overall view

THN has recovered sharply from a shareholder net loss in 2022 to revenue of KRW 989.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 70.1 billion in 2025. Revenue grew for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and operating cash flow of KRW 72.1 billion in 2025 supported reported profit.

However, quarterly operating profit has declined for three consecutive quarters in 2026, to KRW 17.5 billion in Q1, KRW 15.5 billion in Q2 and KRW 7.2 billion in Q3, and Q3 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.78 billion.

The 2025 acquisition effect and the mix toward higher-spec models are cited as the main drivers of profit growth, and a research house estimated 2026 operating profit at KRW 86.5 billion. The disclosed quarterly trend runs in a different direction from that estimate.

Reliance on the Hyundai Motor Group, copper prices, a debt-to-equity ratio of 171.1% and the inheritance-related share transfer are variables that counter the bullish case. The next checkpoints are full-year 2026 operating profit and the Q4 trend in the annual report.
