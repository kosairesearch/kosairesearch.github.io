Wonpoong: Q3 2026 Net Loss Tests Earnings Recovery

02

Business structure

Wonpoong manufactures synthetic leather and industrial materials as a plastic processing company. Its main product is roof waterproofing material based on thermoplastic polyolefin (TPO) sheet, and it also produces eco-friendly materials and outdoor leisure materials.

The company says it is expanding exports in domestic and overseas markets on the back of its technology and production facilities. The business was rooted in industrial tarpaulin sheets, and a technical analysis report from a securities firm published in December 2020 showed exports accounted for 70.1% of 2019 revenue.

That figure is based on 2019 data, so the latest export share should be checked in the revenue breakdown notes of the annual report. In its new materials segment, the company is pursuing revenue growth by combining continuous product development with new investment in exclusive production lines.

On a separate-financials basis, revenue for the first three quarters of 2025 rose 9.5% year on year and operating profit rose 11.9%.

Raw material costs are linked to petrochemical resin prices, and in its 2025 annual earnings disclosure the company cited lower key raw material prices and the resulting cost structure improvement as a driver of profit recovery.

A higher exchange rate was also mentioned as supporting export price competitiveness and revenue growth. Product applications split into architectural roof waterproofing and leisure and industrial materials.

Its competitive position should be read against the fact that its market capitalization is small within the KOSDAQ chemicals sector.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 89.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 73.8 billion in 2023, a decrease of 17.5% year on year, and then rose 7.9% to KRW 79.6 billion in 2024 and 10.1% to KRW 87.6 billion in 2025, increasing for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest. Operating profit fell from KRW 7.76 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.55 billion in 2023, a decrease of 41.3%, and was roughly flat at KRW 4.55 billion in 2024, down 0.1%.

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 6.6 billion, up 45.0% year on year, and 2024 was the smallest year among 2022 to 2025. The operating margin declined from 8.7% in 2022 to 6.2% in 2023 and 5.7% in 2024, then recovered to 7.5% in 2025.

The company attributed 2025 movements to revenue growth from a higher exchange rate and a cost structure improvement from lower key raw material prices.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 8.49 billion in 2022, KRW 6.57 billion in 2023 (down 22.7%), KRW 7.11 billion in 2024 (up 8.2%), and KRW 7.04 billion in 2025 (down 0.9%).

In 2025 operating profit rose 45.0% while net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 0.9%, so the gap between operating profit and net income is worth checking in the notes to the annual report.

Operating cash flow was KRW 12.8 billion in 2022, KRW 7.06 billion in 2023, KRW 4.28 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.36 billion in 2025. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 93.1 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 5.48 billion.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit was KRW 979 million in Q3 2025 and KRW 464 million in Q4 2025, then peaked at KRW 3.22 billion in Q1 2026, a 594.4% quarter-on-quarter increase.

Operating profit then declined for two consecutive quarters, from Q2 2026 through Q3 2026, reaching KRW 973 million in Q3 2026, and Q3 2026 also swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 663 million, which was a swing into loss from a year earlier.

Industry analysis

The KOSDAQ chemicals sector in which Wonpoong sits has a large combined market capitalization, and Wonpoong is one of the smaller companies within it. Its margins are directly exposed to two variables, raw material prices and the exchange rate.

The 2025 operating profit recovery reflected both a higher exchange rate and lower raw material prices, as the company itself explained. Because both factors can reverse, it is worth separating which variable drove the decline in Q3 2026 operating profit.

Product applications split into architectural roof waterproofing and leisure and industrial materials, which means exposure to different demand cycles in construction and leisure. This is a general reading based on the business structure, and specific industry indicators should be checked separately.

In a sector comparison, Wonpoong's dividend yield appears above the KOSDAQ chemicals sector average.

Other listed companies in the same KOSDAQ chemicals group include Foosung, Advanced Nano Products and Hankuk Carbon, but because product areas and scale differ, they serve better as reference points for position within the sector than as direct comparison metrics.

Wonpoong's competitive position can be framed along three axes: export exposure, cost structure, and the expansion of its new materials segment.

Outlook

The most specific source on the company's direction is the value-up plan disclosed on March 30, 2026.

The plan states that the company will strengthen earnings through profitability-focused order and sales strategies, and will create stable operating profit through production process efficiency and strict cost management.

It also states a policy of maintaining a stable dividend based on net income, and the same disclosure showed a 2025 payout ratio of 67% and a 2025 dividend amount of KRW 4.72 billion. That dividend amount can be compared with 2025 net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.04 billion.

On October 2, 2026, the board decided on a treasury share purchase for employee share compensation. The purchase amount is KRW 1 billion, the expected purchase period runs from October 6, 2026 to December 30, 2026, and the purchase is to be made directly through the KOSDAQ market via Yuanta Securities.

The company held 1,508,800 treasury shares, or 12.57%, before this purchase. The disclosure notes that the actual number of shares and amount may change with share price movements.

The next checkpoint is whether operating profit in Q4 2026 holds near the KRW 973 million level of Q3 2026, which will be confirmed through the 2026 annual report.

Whether the decline after the Q1 2026 peak continues into Q4 or reverses as raw material and exchange rate effects feed through is the main fork in the outlook. Whether the Q3 2026 swing into net loss reflects one-off items or recurring factors should be clarified in the annual report notes.

Q1 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.22 billion was the largest quarterly figure in the disclosed results. Whether this recovery persists is the core of the bullish case.

A more concrete shareholder return policy

The value-up plan disclosed on March 30, 2026 set out a policy of maintaining a stable dividend based on net income. The same disclosure gave a 2025 payout ratio of 67%.

A KRW 1 billion treasury share purchase decided in October 2026 is scheduled to run through December 30, 2026, for employee share compensation purposes.

Low debt burden and cash generation

As of the end of 2025, liabilities account for a small share relative to equity. Total equity at the same point was KRW 93.1 billion. Bear factors

Swing to a net loss in Q3 2026

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 663 million, compared with net income of KRW 1.35 billion in Q3 2025. Operating profit in the same quarter was KRW 973 million, down 58.7% from the prior quarter.

Operating profit has declined for two consecutive quarters since Q2 2026, which is the starting point of the bearish case.

Stalled revenue scale

Revenue in 2025 was KRW 87.6 billion, higher than in 2024 but still below KRW 89.5 billion in 2022. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 21.8 billion fell 7.4% from the prior quarter, and its year-on-year increase was only 4.3%. Limited top-line growth weakens the base for a durable profit recovery.

Limits to margin recovery

The operating margin recovered to 7.5% in 2025 but has not yet returned to 8.7% in 2022. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders in 2025 was KRW 7.04 billion, down 0.9% year on year. The fact that the rise in operating profit did not carry through to net income is a reason to re-examine earnings quality.

Risk factors

Raw material prices and exchange rates

The two drivers of the 2025 improvement were lower raw material prices and a higher exchange rate. If these variables move in opposite directions, margins could come under pressure quickly. Given the high export share in the past, sensitivity to the exchange rate direction is also likely to be significant. Raw material costs are linked to petrochemical resin prices.

Earnings volatility and small-cap profile

Quarterly operating profit in the disclosed results moved within a range of KRW 464 million to KRW 3.22 billion, showing large swings. The KRW 7.42 billion total of net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters includes the Q3 2026 net loss.

Because the market capitalization is small, the share price and trading conditions can move more than earnings change.

Shareholder returns and cash use

Because the dividend policy is framed on net income, a decline in earnings could make the dividend burden relatively heavier. If earnings fall in 2026 while a 67% payout ratio is maintained, dividend capacity would narrow. The KRW 1 billion treasury share purchase is also a cash outflow. Operating cash flow having fallen to KRW 4.28 billion in 2024 is also worth considering.
