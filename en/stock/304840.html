02

Business structure

TimeX AI (formerly PeopleBio) changed its name at a July 2026 shareholder meeting and is building out AI infrastructure as a new growth engine. The company organizes its business around three pillars: AI data center infrastructure and servers, semiconductors, and the existing biomedical business.

The core of the biotech segment is AlzOn, a blood-based test for early Alzheimer's disease diagnosis. The product uses an ELISA method that can run on common laboratory plate readers, and the filing cites a Korean consumer supply price of about KRW 100,000, below amyloid PET and cerebrospinal fluid testing.

Biotech segment revenue rose more than 50% quarter on quarter in the second quarter of 2026 and led the recovery, with rental income from a building in Daechi-dong adding support. The flagship AI infrastructure project is a 9MW AI edge data center in the Samsung-dong International Business District in Seoul.

The company participates alongside a co-business partner, sharing project management, core equipment supply and operation, and hosting duties.

The semiconductor segment is negotiating the purchase of used 200mm wafer production equipment, with commercialization targeted for the second half, and it is also working with a refurbishment partner on aging equipment.

The AI and semiconductor businesses are expected to show revenue contribution mainly from the second half of 2026 onward, so the pace of the business restructuring is the key open question.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue went from KRW 4.44 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.45 billion in 2023 (up 0.2% year on year), which was the peak year, and then fell to KRW 3.73 billion in 2024 (down 16.2%) and KRW 2.85 billion in 2025 (down 23.6%), declining for two consecutive years.

Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2025 was the smallest. Operating loss was KRW 11.7 billion in 2022, KRW 15.2 billion in 2023, KRW 11.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.38 billion in 2025, a loss in each of the four years.

The operating margin was -263.2% in 2022, -342.4% in 2023, -308.2% in 2024 and -293.9% in 2025. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 14.3 billion in 2022, KRW 16 billion in 2023, KRW 10.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 15.7 billion in 2025, also a loss in each of the four years.

In 2025 the operating loss narrowed while the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened, and the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 16.9 billion. That gap suggests non-operating items weighed heavily, though the detailed causes should be checked in the notes to the periodic report.

Quarterly revenue fell from KRW 1.18 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 154 million in the fourth quarter (down 87.0% quarter on quarter), then rose to KRW 643 million in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 1.67 billion in the second quarter and KRW 2.49 billion in the third quarter, with three consecutive quarterly increases.

Third-quarter revenue was up 110.0% year on year. In its preliminary second-quarter release, the company reported revenue up 128% year on year. Operating loss narrowed from KRW 2.07 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 502 million in the third quarter of 2026, a five-quarter run of losses.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, however, widened to KRW 10.2 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and then to KRW 2.81 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and the four-quarter total is KRW 17.1 billion.

With operating losses shrinking but net losses growing, non-operating items and one-off factors are the main items to verify in the next periodic report.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for AI data centers starts with investment plans at large chipmakers.

Samsung Electronics is running a roadmap to build an intelligent semiconductor fab with AI embedded across the entire process, and SK hynix has made visible a 15GW-scale large data center investment, which the company sees as lifting demand across the related value chain.

Edge data centers are facilities built in urban areas close to where data is generated, minimizing response latency.

Large groups including SK and other major internet and conglomerate players are accelerating large-scale AI data center builds, while smaller AI infrastructure specialists are entering edge data centers, making competition increasingly polarized.

Smaller operators tend to differentiate through urban locations and leasing and hosting structures rather than matching scale.

The co-business partner is reported to aim for a high-margin model based on colocation rental and fixed-fee cross-connect revenue, comparable to the neutral network service model of a major subsidiary of a Korean internet group.

In semiconductors, refurbishment involves refitting aging equipment, and the company is adding specialist staff and engineering capacity to respond to custom orders. In biotech, the blood-based Alzheimer's test positions itself as a lower-cost alternative to amyloid PET and cerebrospinal fluid testing.

Given the large scale gap with dedicated AI infrastructure players, results depend heavily on the start of specific projects and the rental income structure. Insurance coverage and the speed of sales channel expansion are variables to check in upcoming results.

06

Outlook

The company expected revenue from its semiconductor segment to become visible from the second half of 2026 and AI-related revenue to expand, while AlzOn sales were expected to rise during the health checkup season.

It said that a full semiconductor revenue contribution, AI business expansion and continued biotech growth together would speed up the earnings turnaround.

For the 9MW edge data center in Samsung-dong, the timetable called for an electricity supply contract with the power utility around August 2026, a project financing target at the end of September, and construction to begin in October.

The co-business partner said it raised KRW 15 billion of operating funds from a real estate asset manager affiliated with a Korean financial group.

The same partner estimated the facility could generate more than KRW 28 billion in annual operating revenue and a business value of about KRW 400 billion, although this is the partner's own estimate.

In semiconductors, talks on acquiring used 200mm equipment are ongoing, with commercialization targeted for the second half. In biotech, the company filed for FDA Breakthrough Device designation and aimed for approval by August 2026.

Since that target month has passed, the company's disclosure of the designation outcome is the next checkpoint. The fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report will show whether the non-operating structure becomes clearer and whether the profit trend holds.

08

Bull factors

Revenue up for three straight quarters

Revenue rebounded from KRW 154 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 643 million, KRW 1.67 billion and KRW 2.49 billion over the next three quarters. Third-quarter revenue rose 110.0% year on year. The company said its biotech revenue rose more than 50% quarter on quarter and led the improvement.

Narrowing operating losses

Operating loss narrowed from KRW 2.07 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 502 million in the third quarter of 2026. On an annual basis, the 2025 operating loss of KRW 8.38 billion was smaller than KRW 11.5 billion in 2024.

The operating margin improved to -293.9% in 2025 from -342.4% in 2023, and the loss has been narrowing clearly even though the company remains in deficit.

AI infrastructure progress and balance-sheet cleanup

The 9MW AI edge data center in Samsung-dong is progressing, and the company said it is preparing for main construction and completing power connection procedures. Offsetting accumulated deficits with KRW 79.2 billion of capital surplus is also a step in balance-sheet cleanup.

09

Bear factors

Widening net loss attributable to controlling shareholders

Even as operating loss narrowed, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 2.81 billion in the third quarter of 2026 from the prior quarter. It reached KRW 10.2 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, and the four-quarter total is KRW 17.1 billion.

If non-operating items are dragging on earnings, operating improvement will not translate directly into a smaller net loss.

Small revenue base after two years of decline

Revenue in 2025 was KRW 2.85 billion, below the KRW 4.45 billion peak in 2023, and it declined in both 2024 and 2025. The debt ratio fell to 55.7% in 2025 from 255.3% in 2024, and total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 16 billion. With a small revenue base, slower recognition of new business revenue could slow the recovery.

Project timing and competitive intensity

The edge data center's project financing and construction start were initially timed for September and October. The FDA Breakthrough Device designation was targeted for August 2026.

With large groups expanding AI data center investment, competition for smaller operators is intensifying, and any schedule delay would push back the timing of revenue recognition.

10

Risk factors

Financing and project timing

The edge data center depends on project financing. The co-business partner has raised KRW 15 billion of operating funds, but the scale and timing of the main project financing need to be confirmed through disclosure. If the original schedule slips, the timing of new revenue recognition will also be delayed.

Regulatory and permitting

The FDA designation process involved supplemental responses to additional questions from the agency. The designation outcome could change the sales strategy for AlzOn and the timing of US market access. Electricity supply contracts and construction permits for the data center are also schedule variables.

Earnings volatility

Quarterly revenue has swung widely, from KRW 154 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.49 billion in the third quarter of 2026, reflecting a still-small revenue base.

Large non-operating items make net loss move differently from operating loss, and semiconductor refurbishment results will depend on equipment acquisition and order volume.

12

Overall view

TimeX AI (formerly PeopleBio) is shifting from a diagnostics-focused biotech into a three-pillar business that adds AI infrastructure and semiconductors.

On a disclosed basis, 2025 revenue of KRW 2.85 billion was the smallest in the period, but revenue has risen for three straight quarters since the first quarter of 2026 and operating losses have been narrowing.

Strengths include the revenue rebound, the narrowing operating loss, the dividend foundation created by offsetting KRW 79.2 billion of accumulated deficits, and progress on new projects such as the Samsung-dong edge data center.

On the other side, net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 10.2 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 2.81 billion in the third quarter of 2026, with a four-quarter total of KRW 17.1 billion.

The size and nature of non-operating items should be checked in the notes to the periodic report. Much of the new business depends on a partner's estimates and earlier timetables, so the timing of project financing and the FDA outcome matter.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results, the structure of the net loss in the 2026 annual report, the disclosure of main financing and construction for the edge data center, and the FDA designation outcome.
