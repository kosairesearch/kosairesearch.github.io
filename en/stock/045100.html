02

Business structure

Hanyang ENG designs, fabricates and installs process piping and chemical central supply systems (CCSS) for semiconductor and display fabs. The business runs in two divisions.

The Engineering Division covers high-tech facilities for semiconductors and displays, EPC work for industrial plants and aerospace, while the System Division manufactures CCSS and related equipment.

The external aggregator openbizdata put 2025 consolidated sales at KRW 954.1 billion (85.3%) for Engineering and KRW 164.1 billion (14.7%) for the System Division. A securities research report from March 2023 showed a 2022 split of 83.5% Engineering and 16.5% System, so the Engineering share has risen slightly since.

Profitability differs sharply by division. The same aggregator shows the System Division gross margin falling from 30.8% in 2023 to 13.4% in 2025.

Customers are concentrated in chipmakers. openbizdata calculated that the top standalone customers, including Samsung Electronics, Samsung C&T and SK hynix, account for about 84.9% of standalone sales.

A December 2021 technical analysis report put 2020 consolidated sales at 82.2% domestic, 15.0% China and 1.4% United States. The company also runs overseas subsidiaries, including a Xi'an unit in China and a US unit.

A September 2026 report introduced Hanyang ENG as ranked first in the mechanical facilities engineering capacity rating.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on DART periodic reports, revenue was KRW 1.16 trillion in 2022, KRW 1.03 trillion in 2023 (down 11.8% year on year), KRW 1.19 trillion in 2024 (up 15.6%) and KRW 1.12 trillion in 2025 (down 5.7%). Within 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 75.3 billion in 2022, KRW 85.1 billion in 2023 (up 13.1%) and KRW 86.2 billion in 2024 (up 1.3%), before falling to KRW 53.7 billion in 2025 (down 37.7%).

The operating margin was 6.5% in 2022, 8.3% in 2023, 7.3% in 2024 and 4.8% in 2025, so even in 2024, the year of largest sales, the margin stayed below the 2023 peak.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 77.4 billion in 2023 (up 10.9%), then came in at KRW 76.3 billion in 2024 (down 1.3%) and KRW 41.8 billion in 2025 (down 45.3%), declining for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 41.7 billion in 2025, close to the controlling-shareholder figure. Within the 2025 operating profit decline, the aggregator data links the drop to the System Division margin falling from 30.8% in 2023 to 13.4% in 2025.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 148.6 billion in 2023 and KRW 97.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.35 billion in 2025. The debt ratio improved from 54.9% in 2022 to 48.0% in 2025, and total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 624.2 billion.

Quarterly, fourth-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 307.4 billion (up 22.1% from the prior quarter), but operating profit was KRW 9.69 billion (down 19.8%). In the first quarter of 2026, revenue reached KRW 341.4 billion and operating profit rose to KRW 22.3 billion (up 129.8% from the prior quarter).

Second-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 28.5 billion (up 28.1%), and third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 25.8 billion (down 9.4% from the prior quarter).

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 26 billion, KRW 21.6 billion and KRW 22.4 billion in the first three quarters of 2026, and the last four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026 total KRW 78.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The company's results are directly tied to chipmakers' facility investment. FnGuide summarized that Engineering revenue declined on cyclical swings in the semiconductor and display industries and on adjustments to facility investment.

A March 2023 securities report explained that the 2022 rise in Engineering Division sales came from expanded equipment sales to SK hynix.

Unlike large construction companies in the same sector, which have broadened into housing and overseas plants, Hanyang ENG's sales are tied to semiconductor and display facilities. openbizdata noted that a slowdown in domestic investment by core customers caused the standalone Engineering gross margin to drop sharply.

In September 2026, the independent research firm Growth Research said Hanyang ENG performs work from primary piping and CCSS installation through secondary hook-up piping that connects manufacturing equipment, and has also secured overseas supply contracts.

The same report viewed the modular method, in which piping is prefabricated in factories and installed on site, as shortening construction periods. That approach may be an advantage for customers facing tight fab construction schedules.

Where the industry sits in its cycle cannot be settled here, and it should be tracked through customers' expansion announcements and the pace of order disclosures.

06

Outlook

The most recent large contract confirmed is the CDS package supply contract signed on May 25, 2026. The contract is worth KRW 121.38 billion, equal to 10.86% of 2025 revenue, and runs for about two years from May 25, 2026 to May 31, 2028. The company disclosed the contract on May 26, 2026.

Growth Research said the large supply contracts are raising earnings visibility. The same research firm said additional order opportunities are likely to widen across multiple stages of domestic and overseas fab expansion.

Performing work from primary piping through secondary hook-up piping means a single project can generate sales at several points in time. How much of the contract value is recognized as sales in each quarter will be verified in periodic reports.

The next periodic report, the 2026 annual business report, may show the annual sales mix and progress on the CDS contract together.

Three items are worth tracking: whether the operating margin recovers from 4.8% in 2025, whether the System Division gross margin improves from 13.4% in 2025, and whether order disclosures continue. On the shareholder side, the largest shareholder and related parties held 47.86% as of April 2026 according to FnGuide.

Bear factors

Customer concentration and investment cycle

openbizdata put the share of top standalone customers at about 84.9%. The same data set said that a slowdown in domestic investment by core customers caused the standalone Engineering gross margin to fall sharply. FnGuide also summarized that Engineering revenue declined amid adjustments to facility investment.

Margin compression

The operating margin fell from 8.3% in 2023 to 4.8% in 2025, and 2025 operating profit of KRW 53.7 billion was down 37.7% year on year. Per the aggregator data, the System Division gross margin dropped from 30.8% in 2023 to 13.4% in 2025. If margins do not recover, higher sales will not translate into higher profit.

Weaker cash flow and quarterly volatility

Operating cash flow dropped from KRW 97.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.35 billion in 2025. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders also fell 17.0% from KRW 26 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 21.6 billion in the second. Quarterly results can swing widely depending on when large projects are recognized as sales.

Risk factors

Customer concentration

With the top standalone customers at about 84.9% of sales, a change in equipment orders from a few clients could affect overall revenue. If investment schedules at chipmakers such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix slow, order disclosures and quarterly sales could weaken together. Progress on customer diversification should be checked in the customer disclosures of periodic reports.

Contract profitability

Even large orders translate into different profit amounts depending on costs and construction progress. The drop of the System Division gross margin to 13.4% in 2025 shows how much cost control matters. As overseas supply contracts grow, exchange-rate moves could affect won-denominated sales and costs.

Ownership structure and free float

According to FnGuide, the largest shareholder and related parties held 47.86% as of April 2026. When related-party stakes are large, the amount of freely tradable shares is limited, which can increase share-price volatility. Changes in holdings and any plans for treasury share acquisition or cancellation should be followed alongside.

Overall view

Hanyang ENG is a facilities specialist centered on semiconductor process piping and CCSS. Its 2025 annual results were KRW 1.12 trillion in revenue, KRW 53.7 billion in operating profit and KRW 41.8 billion in profit attributable to controlling shareholders, all below 2024.

Quarterly operating profit recovered to KRW 22.3 billion, KRW 28.5 billion and KRW 25.8 billion across the first three quarters of 2026. The KRW 121.38 billion CDS package supply contract signed in May 2026, running through May 2028, is cited as a factor raising order visibility.

On the other side, customer concentration of about 84.9% and the decline of the System Division gross margin from 30.8% in 2023 to 13.4% in 2025 are weakening factors. Operating cash flow falling to KRW 2.35 billion in 2025 also deserves attention.

Valuation can be compared against past year-end multiples and net asset levels, and the reading depends on whether the earnings recovery continues. The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual business report and the pace of order disclosures.
