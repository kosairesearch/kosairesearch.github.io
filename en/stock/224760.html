02

Business structure

NSCompany combines industrial plant construction, routine maintenance of power plants and the manufacture and supply of auto parts. Segment weights shown by Saramin's company profile are power plant maintenance at 41.2%, auto parts at 31.3%, plant construction at 21.3% and management support at 6.9%.

The maintenance unit covers nuclear, thermal and hydroelectric power facilities. The plant construction unit builds nuclear and thermal power plants and petrochemical facilities. The auto parts unit produces front knuckles and rear carriers at its Gyeongju plant.

JobKorea's business breakdown instead shows other construction work at 41%, auto parts at 16%, the Dangjin LNG tank construction at 16% and the SATORP package at 10%, so segment categories differ by data source.

The company runs four business divisions, including a construction division, and set up a new growth development division in 2024. That division covers solar and wind power projects, real estate development, corporate M&A and pyrolysis oil production from waste plastics.

In power plant construction and maintenance, the company competes within the same industry as large builders such as Samsung E&A, Hyundai Engineering & Construction and DL E&C. NSCompany is small in scale relative to those firms, so the profit impact of a single large project is comparatively significant.

Recent trends

Revenue for 2025 was KRW 71.62 billion, up 0.3% from KRW 71.44 billion in 2024. In the same year, the company posted an operating loss of KRW 6.28 billion after an operating profit of KRW 503.8 million in 2024. The net loss was KRW 2.99 billion, compared with a net profit of KRW 2.86 billion in 2024.

According to an FnGuide summary, one-off costs in the construction segment and slow recovery in demand for power plant maintenance weighed on profit. The operating margin fell to -8.78% from 0.71% in 2024.

The company also recorded an operating loss of KRW 9.09 billion and a net loss of KRW 5.26 billion in 2023, so two of the last three years were loss-making. At year-end 2025, total assets stood at KRW 73.6 billion, total liabilities at KRW 39.5 billion and equity at KRW 34.1 billion.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 115.79% from 86.82%, and operating cash flow shifted to an outflow of KRW 5.08 billion from an inflow of KRW 3.01 billion.

At the 32nd annual general meeting held on March 31, 2026, four agenda items including approval of financial statements passed, and a corrective disclosure followed on April 1 to fix a clerical error in total assets. The share price was KRW 3,305 on April 1, 2026, up 14.76% on the day, and KRW 2,605 on June 5, 2026.

The current price is KRW 2,480, and trading value shows zero because the reference time falls after the regular session closed.

Outlook

Three points remain to be tracked. The first is how quickly demand recovers in the power plant maintenance unit and how that flows into earnings after 2026. The second is whether one-off costs in the construction segment recur.

The third is when, and at what scale, the solar and wind, real estate development and pyrolysis oil projects run by the growth development division begin to contribute revenue. The 2026 annual business report was disclosed on March 23, 2026, and later quarterly results will be confirmed through quarterly filings.

On the financial side, the debt-to-equity ratio has risen to 115.79%, so borrowing capacity against equity of KRW 34.1 billion and the recovery of operating cash flow are the key issues. A reserve ratio of 1,631.96% at year-end 2025 points to a substantial capital cushion.

The auto parts unit follows demand patterns different from construction and may help smooth earnings swings. No disclosed figures yet show the profitability of the new businesses.

Related parties hold 94.64% of shares, based on the last change date of March 31, 2022, so the low free float is a structural feature affecting trading liquidity.

Bull factors

Maintenance demand anchored in power plants

Power plant maintenance is the largest unit at 41.2% of the business mix, according to Saramin's company profile. The unit covers nuclear, thermal and hydroelectric facilities, which spreads its customer base.

An FnGuide summary cited slow demand recovery in power plant maintenance as a 2025 drag, so a rebound would be the most direct route to better earnings.

A profitable year in 2024

In 2024 the company recorded revenue of KRW 71.44 billion, an operating profit of KRW 503.8 million and a net profit of KRW 2.86 billion. Bullish readings attribute the 2025 loss to specific factors such as one-off construction costs. Profit volatility has been large, however, with an operating loss of KRW 9.09 billion recorded in 2023.

Financial cushion and new business platform

At year-end 2025, equity stood at KRW 34.1 billion and the reserve ratio at 1,631.96%. Since the growth development division was set up in 2024, the company has pursued expansion into solar and wind, real estate development and corporate M&A.

The auto parts unit follows demand patterns different from construction orders and can spread earnings volatility.

Bear factors

Swing to losses in 2025

The operating loss for 2025 was KRW 6.28 billion, with an operating margin of -8.78%. One-off costs in construction and delayed recovery in maintenance demand acted at the same time. The clearest bearish point is that earnings resilience has weakened compared with 2024.

Rising financial leverage

The debt-to-equity ratio increased from 86.82% in 2024 to 115.79% in 2025, with total liabilities at KRW 39.5 billion. Operating cash flow turned to an outflow of KRW 5.08 billion, weakening cash generation. If borrowing dependence rises further, weak earnings could translate into financial strain.

Thin free float and share price trend

Related parties hold 94.64% of shares, based on a last change date of March 31, 2022, leaving a small free float. The share price moved from KRW 3,305 on April 1, 2026 to KRW 2,605 on June 5, 2026, and the current price is KRW 2,480. The zero trading value at the time of writing reflects a period after the regular session closed.

Risk factors

Macro

Power plant maintenance and plant orders are shaped by energy policy and facility investment plans. A policy shift in the mix of nuclear and thermal generation could change the direction of maintenance demand. Higher raw material and labor costs could raise construction costs and bring back one-off charges.

Project concentration

The 2025 results were affected by one-off costs in the construction segment. Large individual projects such as the Dangjin LNG tank work and the SATORP package carry significant weight, so cost swings in a single project can show up strongly in total results.

The new businesses under the growth development division remain at an early stage without disclosed profitability figures.

Financial and liquidity

A rising debt-to-equity ratio combined with negative operating cash flow is narrowing financial flexibility. The high stake held by the largest shareholder limits the free float, and as a KONEX-listed company, trading depth can be thin. Trading value at the time of writing is shown as zero.

08

Overall view

NSCompany's 2025 revenue edged up, but the company swung to an operating loss and a net loss. The earnings decline is attributed to one-off costs in the construction segment and slow recovery in power plant maintenance demand.

The bullish case rests on its power plant maintenance-based business, its profitable year in 2024, and a financial cushion supported by equity and a high reserve ratio.

The bearish case rests on the weaker operating margin, the rise in debt-to-equity and the swing in operating cash flow, along with constraints from the high stake held by the largest shareholder.

The new businesses under the growth development division have a stated direction, but no disclosed figures yet show their revenue contribution or profitability. Morningstar puts the price-to-book ratio at about 0.29 times.

This report is a qualitative summary for information only, and whether earnings recover and the financial structure improves are the most important points to monitor at present.
