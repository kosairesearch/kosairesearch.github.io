02

Business structure

Austem was founded in 1990 and listed on KOSDAQ in 1997 as an automotive parts maker. The company produces chassis modules, seat mechanisms and other vehicle components and supplies GM Korea and global GM.

A January 2021 technical analysis report listed steel wheels, body panels, chassis, seats and ceramic diesel filters as main products and named Hyundai Motor as another customer. Its core technologies are TWB laser welding, magnetic welding and hydroforming, used for lightweight body parts.

According to FnGuide, automotive parts accounted for 63.48% of revenue in December 2025 and other businesses for 36.52%. The automotive parts share was 69.88% in December 2022, 78.41% in December 2023 and 70.22% in December 2024.

As of March 2026, consolidated subsidiaries included the ceramic filter maker Austem EXT, the agricultural distribution unit Austem Bio, the technical services unit Austem GTC and the US production unit AUSTEM AMERICA.

The 2021 report said the anma-chair parts joint venture Bach began production in Q3 2018 and that its sales depend on chair sales volumes. The business is structured as a vertical relationship with vehicle makers, and listed peers include Hyundai WIA, Sungwoo Hitech, SNT Motiv and Myoung Shin Industry.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 110.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 142 billion in 2023, a 28.0% increase, then fell 9.9% to KRW 127.9 billion in 2024 and rose 0.5% to KRW 128.5 billion in 2025. Within 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 4.66 billion in 2022 to an operating profit of KRW 9.9 billion in 2023, a swing into profit. It then fell 95.6% to KRW 435 million in 2024 and rose 854.1% to KRW 4.15 billion in 2025.

The operating margin was -4.2% in 2022, 7.0% in 2023, 0.3% in 2024 and 3.2% in 2025, so margins diverged sharply even between 2024 and 2025 when revenue was similar.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a net loss of KRW 13.6 billion in 2022, a profit of KRW 8.65 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 38.6 billion in 2024 and a profit of KRW 928 million in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 39 billion in 2024, a larger deficit than the controlling-shareholder figure suggests. Quarterly revenue went from KRW 40.9 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 33.3 billion in Q4 2025, a 18.7% decline, then recovered to KRW 34.2 billion in Q1 2026, up 2.7%.

Revenue then fell to KRW 30.4 billion in Q2 2026, down 11.1%, and to KRW 25.8 billion in Q3 2026, down 15.0% quarter on quarter. Operating profit peaked at KRW 2.71 billion in Q1 2026, fell to KRW 668 million in Q2 2026, down 75.3%, and turned into an operating loss of KRW 2.71 billion in Q3 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.44 billion in Q1 2026 was well above that quarter's operating profit, which points to non-operating items having contributed.

Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 1.31 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.7 billion in 2023, KRW 8.29 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.63 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for automotive parts follows vehicle production volumes, so Austem's revenue is directly tied to the production plans of its customers.

A January 2021 technical analysis report described automotive parts makers as having vertical relationships with vehicle makers and as capital-intensive, with slitting, stamping and welding lines. The same report said the electrification of the auto industry was changing parts supply structures.

It also pointed to restructuring at large parts makers, including those linked to GM Korea, Ssangyong Motor and Mando, and expected employment in the parts sector to keep declining. That is a 2021 assessment and should be read separately from current conditions.

In competition, Austem sits alongside other listed parts makers that serve the same vehicle platforms, including Hyundai Mobis, Hyundai WIA, Sungwoo Hitech, SNT Motiv, Myoung Shin Industry and Hallacast.

The technology the company emphasizes is lightweight body parts built with TWB welding and hydroforming, which ties to demand for better fuel efficiency. A numerical comparison of revenue and margins against these peers is not included in this report.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in operating margin

The operating margin rose from 0.3% in 2024 to 3.2% in 2025, and it reached 7.0% in 2023. Annual operating profit also grew from KRW 435 million in 2024 to KRW 4.15 billion in 2025. If this trend continues after Q4 2026, it would be read as improvement in business metrics.

Rising operating cash flow

Operating cash flow increased from KRW 2.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 8.29 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.63 billion in 2025. Compared with negative KRW 1.31 billion in 2022, cash generation has grown. Positive operating cash inflows during years of small reported profit are a reference point for financial stability.

Broadening revenue mix

According to FnGuide, the share of other businesses rose from 20.13% in December 2022 to 36.52% in December 2025. The revenue base is broadening beyond automotive parts. Profitability of the other businesses should be checked separately against the annual operating margin trend.

Bear factors

Joint slowdown in quarterly revenue and profit

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 25.8 billion fell 36.9% year on year and declined for two consecutive quarters. The same quarter turned into an operating loss of KRW 2.71 billion. Whether falling revenue is blocking operating profit recovery is a point to verify in the next periodic report.

Customer concentration and past restructuring at large parts makers

The company's main customers are reported to be GM Korea and global GM. A 2021 report pointed to restructuring at large parts makers after weak performance at GM Korea and others. A cut in a single customer's production volume could hit revenue significantly.

Thin earnings base and higher leverage

The debt ratio rose from 84.4% in 2024 to 98.5% in 2025, and total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 65.3 billion. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 928 million in 2025, and the last four quarters totaled KRW 9.26 billion. With a small earnings base and higher leverage, the buffer against earnings shocks is weaker.

Risk factors

Customer production swings

Revenue is tied to production volumes for key customers such as GM Korea and global GM. Quarterly revenue has moved down and back up repeatedly from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026. A production halt or volume change on a specific model can quickly shift quarterly results.

Cost and profitability pressure

The operating margin fell to 0.3% in 2024 and turned into an operating loss in Q3 2026. Whether cost burdens are rising as revenue declines is a point to check in the notes of periodic reports. If profitability weakens again, sustaining a profit trend becomes harder.

Balance sheet and capital capacity

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 66.3 billion, total liabilities were KRW 65.3 billion and the debt ratio was 98.5%. With liabilities large relative to equity, consecutive losses could reduce financial flexibility. Changes in borrowing terms and cash flow need to be watched together.

Overall view

Austem is an automotive parts maker that supplies GM Korea and global GM. It swung to an operating profit of KRW 9.9 billion in 2023, then its operating margin fell to 0.3% in 2024 before recovering to 3.2% in 2025.

However, revenue has declined for two consecutive quarters since its Q1 2026 peak, and the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 2.71 billion in Q3 2026. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 9.26 billion, a narrow earnings base.

The price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x, which is a fact about the valuation relative to net assets, but it should be read alongside earnings volatility.

Strengths include rising operating cash flow and a broadening revenue mix, while weaknesses include customer concentration and a joint slowdown in quarterly revenue and profit. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 operating margin in the 2026 annual report and quarterly results for Q1 2027.
