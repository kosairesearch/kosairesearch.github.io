02

Business structure

Nuvotec was founded in 1990 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2002 as an environmental piping materials maker. Its starting point was the high-rigidity PVC double-wall pipe it developed in 1997, which local governments and construction firms use in place of concrete culvert pipes and cast iron pipes.

The business has three segments. The first is the water and sewerage segment, covering piping materials, retention basins and trenchless rehabilitation materials, and it is the core business. This segment accounted for 58% of sales in the first quarter of 2026 according to a Newspim report dated May 13, 2026.

The second is the de-icing agent segment, which supplies eco-friendly de-icers to local governments.

In the first half of 2026, despite lower snowfall, the company said it kept sales steady by capturing local governments' early stockpiling demand, according to reporting on its half-year report published August 14, 2026. The third is the waste recycling segment, which runs comprehensive waste recycling.

In June 2026 the company was named the final contractor for SK hynix's waste chemical container recycling project and began operations on July 1. Its customers are water and sewerage offices nationwide, local governments and large construction companies.

A 2023 technical analysis by NICE D&B also noted that other operators take part in plastic piping production. At an extraordinary shareholder meeting on October 6, 2026, shareholders approved an articles amendment adding solar power generation to the business purpose, citing diversification.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales were KRW 46 billion in 2022, rose to KRW 48.9 billion in 2023 (up 6.3% year on year), fell to KRW 43.4 billion in 2024 (down 11.3%), and were KRW 43.5 billion in 2025 (up 0.3%). The largest year in 2022 to 2025 was 2023 and the smallest was 2024. Profit moved more than sales.

Operating profit went from KRW 715 million in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 704 million in 2023, which was a swing back to loss, then to KRW 2.64 billion in 2024, a swing back to profit. Operating profit fell 50.8% to KRW 1.3 billion in 2025.

The operating margin was 1.6% in 2022, -1.4% in 2023, 6.1% in 2024 and 3.0% in 2025.

For the 2025 decline, the company cited a one-time base effect from the reversal of a litigation provision in the prior year and higher administrative and R&D costs tied to sales, according to a report on its disclosure dated March 3, 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 2.7 billion in 2022 and a loss of KRW 4.76 billion in 2023, when losses widened, before turning to a profit of KRW 1.3 billion in 2024 and rising 45.1% to KRW 1.88 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 2.7 billion in 2022, a loss of KRW 4.76 billion in 2023, a profit of KRW 1.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.88 billion in 2025.

For the 2025 increase, the company said higher public-sector sales tied to larger Ministry of Environment budgets offset weaker private-sector sales as construction activity slowed, according to a March 4, 2026 report.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 529 million in 2022, KRW 4.77 billion in 2023, KRW 4.22 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.49 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 7.7 billion with an operating loss of KRW 615 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 11.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 299 million in the first quarter of 2026.

Second-quarter sales were KRW 10.4 billion, down 9.1% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 179 million was down 40.1%.

Third-quarter sales were KRW 7.46 billion, down 28.2% from the prior quarter and down 3.0% year on year, and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 209 million from the prior quarter. The fourth quarter of 2025 has no quarterly figures in the disclosed data and is left out of the quarterly trend.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totals KRW 2.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Nuvotec's core market is tied to public infrastructure spending on aging water and sewer pipe replacement and sewer network upkeep. A 2023 NICE D&B technical analysis described government replacement and maintenance of aging sewer and water pipes as a stable sales base.

For 2025, the company again attributed higher public-sector sales to a larger Ministry of Environment budget. Private-sector demand, by contrast, was hit by slower construction activity, which reduced private-sector sales.

On inputs, volatility in key raw materials such as PVC resin and higher crude oil prices affect margins. The company said the first half of 2026 was delivered amid higher oil prices linked to the prolonged Russia-Ukraine and Middle East conflicts, PVC resin price swings and global supply chain instability.

It said it has responded to cost pressure through early raw material stockpiling, diversifying suppliers and process efficiency focused on higher-value products.

In terms of cycle position, both the company and press coverage frame the business as sitting on a medium- to long-term rise in demand to replace aging sewer lines.

Independent research firm Value Finder, in an August 5, 2026 report, described Nuvotec as the only integrated operator in Korea that has internalized water and sewer piping materials, trenchless technology and retention basins, and said it is moving toward a water technology platform.

The same assessment compared the direction to that of the global water technology company Xylem. This is the research firm's view, not the company's own assessment.

06

Outlook

The company has set out two directions: sustaining core profitability with cost control, and phasing in new growth engines.

In its first-half 2026 results announcement, the CEO said the company would gradually make new growth drivers visible on the back of a more stable business base, according to reporting dated August 14, 2026. The most concrete new business is the SK hynix waste container recycling project.

It covers collecting, transporting and safely recycling waste containers for advanced process chemicals from sites including Icheon, Cheongju and the planned Yongin mega cluster.

The company said this should become a long-term and stable sales and profit base as semiconductor output grows, though the size of the contract and its sales contribution are hard to gauge from disclosed data.

Nuvotec also signed an MOU with an AI-based spatial information and digital twin company to develop AI-driven sewer diagnosis and digital twin maintenance systems. The solar power business purpose approved on October 6, 2026 was justified as diversification, and the start date for actual operations is not yet known.

On shareholder returns, the company cancelled 169,653 treasury shares in April 2026 and in June entered a treasury share acquisition trust worth KRW 1.4 billion. As of the August 14, 2026 report, it said it had completed acquisition of 985,751 shares, about 7.39% of total issued shares.

The next checkpoint is whether the 2026 annual report shows an annual operating margin above the 3.0% recorded in 2025.

Bull factors

Recovery in core profitability

The operating margin rose from -1.4% in 2023 to 6.1% in 2024, and eased to 3.0% in 2025 while staying out of loss territory. The company said first-half 2026 production in the water and sewerage segment reached KRW 16.4 billion, up 17.6% year on year. It also said it offset raw material price swings through stockpiling and supplier diversification.

A link to new business

Nuvotec was selected as a contractor for SK hynix's waste chemical container recycling project and began operations on July 1, 2026. The company expects this to support medium- to long-term sales and profit as semiconductor output rises. The recycling segment could also open a customer base different from its core piping business.

Shareholder returns alongside

In April 2026 the company cancelled 169,653 treasury shares, and in June it entered a KRW 1.4 billion treasury share acquisition trust. As of the August 14, 2026 report, it said it had acquired 985,751 shares, about 7.39% of issued shares. Shareholder returns have appeared alongside the earnings recovery.

Bear factors

Quarterly sales decline and a return to operating loss

Second-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 10.4 billion, down 9.1% from the prior quarter, and third-quarter sales fell a further 28.2% to KRW 7.46 billion. The third quarter swung to an operating loss of KRW 209 million from the prior quarter, and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 115 million. The weaker quarterly trend after the first-quarter peak is the main bearish factor.

Stalled sales and volatile profit

Annual sales were KRW 46 billion in 2022 and KRW 43.5 billion in 2025, so revenue is slightly lower over the period. Operating profit fell 50.8% to KRW 1.3 billion in 2025, which the company linked partly to the base effect of a prior-year one-time reversal. The bear case rests on profit depending heavily on one-time factors.

Balance sheet burden and slower cash flow

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 32.8 billion, above total equity of KRW 22.8 billion, and the debt ratio was 143.8%. It improved from 259.7% in 2023 but remains above 100%. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 4.22 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.49 billion in 2025.

Risk factors

Raw materials and oil prices

Swings in key raw materials such as PVC resin and higher crude oil prices could pressure costs. The company said the first half of 2026 was achieved amid prolonged Russia-Ukraine and Middle East conflicts and global supply chain instability.

How far stockpiling and supplier diversification absorb this pressure will determine margins in coming quarters.

Dependence on policy and public orders

The water and sewerage business is closely tied to Ministry of Environment budgets and local government orders. In 2025 private-sector sales fell with slower construction, and higher public-sector sales offset part of the decline. A change in budget allocation or order timing could again move quarterly sales.

New business execution and one-time items

The sales contribution of the SK hynix contract is hard to gauge from disclosed data, and the start date for solar power operations is not yet known. Operating profit in 2025 reflected a base effect from a prior one-time reversal, so one-time items must be separated when reading the trend. Until new businesses show up in results, the core business structure explains most of the profit.

Overall view

Nuvotec's core business is water and sewerage piping materials based on the high-rigidity PVC double-wall pipe it developed in 1997, with de-icing agents and waste recycling operated alongside.

On disclosed figures, annual sales stalled, moving from KRW 46 billion in 2022 to KRW 43.5 billion in 2025, while operating profit turned to KRW 2.64 billion in 2024 and fell to KRW 1.3 billion in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose 45.1% to KRW 1.88 billion in 2025, and the debt ratio fell from 209.2% in 2022 to 143.8% in 2025.

By contrast, quarterly sales peaked in the first quarter of 2026 at KRW 11.4 billion and then fell in both the second and third quarters, with the third quarter swinging to an operating loss.

Bullish factors include recovery in core profitability, new links to business such as the SK hynix recycling contract, and share cancellation with treasury share trusts.

Bearish factors include stalled sales, a profit structure dependent on one-time items, and a balance sheet where total liabilities exceed total equity. The share price sits at a discount to net assets and recent earnings, but with earnings this small, multiples can move sharply on a few quarters of results. The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and sales in the first quarter of 2027.
