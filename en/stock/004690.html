02

Business structure

Samchully runs city gas supply in the Seoul metropolitan area as its core, alongside power generation, automobile sales and servicing, plant construction, district energy and an overseas hotel business.

Based on the 2025 annual sales mix compiled by a Korean data service, city gas accounted for 68.51%, power generation for 17.87%, automobile and servicing for 7.40%, and engineering and plant services for 5.27%. District energy (heat and electricity) was 0.90% and hotel services 0.60%.

An industry report said city gas once made up more than 90% of group sales until 2015 but has fallen to the high 60% range. The sales mix within gas is also changing.

A Korean business daily reported that first-half 2026 city gas volume rose 2.0%, with fuel cell demand up 28.6% while household demand fell 2.3% and industrial demand fell 0.8%. New fuel cell capacity and higher operating rates drove this shift.

On the diversification side, the company bought 100% of the food company Seonggyeong Food in February 2026 for KRW 119.5 billion. Industry analysts see price competitiveness pressure on city gas. Because wholesale LNG cost changes pass through to retail tariffs, raw material movements feed directly into profitability.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual basis, revenue went from KRW 5.79 trillion in 2022 to KRW 5.66 trillion in 2023, down 2.2% year on year, and then to KRW 5.12 trillion in 2024, down 9.6%. It recovered to KRW 5.28 trillion in 2025, up 3.0%. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year by revenue and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit rose from KRW 91.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 174.5 billion in 2023, up 91.3%. It fell to KRW 114.3 billion in 2024, down 34.5%, and recovered to KRW 159.6 billion in 2025, up 39.7%. The operating margin was 1.6% in 2022, 3.1% in 2023, 2.2% in 2024 and 3.0% in 2025.

The margin also fell in 2024, the year revenue declined most. Parent-attributable net profit was KRW 66.7 billion in 2022, KRW 120.2 billion in 2023 (up 80.2%), KRW 101.4 billion in 2024 (down 15.7%) and KRW 111.5 billion in 2025 (up 10.0%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 131.3 billion in 2025, a different figure from parent-attributable profit. At the end of 2025, total equity stood at KRW 1.88 trillion, of which KRW 1.68 trillion belonged to parent shareholders.

Operating cash flow was KRW 368.1 billion in 2022, KRW 110.2 billion in 2023, KRW 210.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 306.5 billion in 2025. Quarterly, 4Q 2025 revenue was KRW 1.45 trillion, up 58.1% from the prior quarter, but operating profit was KRW 10.7 billion, down 52.9%.

The parent-attributable result was a net loss of KRW 105 million, a swing into loss from the prior quarter. In 1Q 2026, revenue was KRW 1.81 trillion and operating profit KRW 110.2 billion, up 925.7% from the prior quarter, with parent-attributable net profit of KRW 94.2 billion as the profit swung back to positive.

In 2Q 2026, revenue fell to KRW 1.16 trillion, operating profit to KRW 28.1 billion and parent-attributable net profit to KRW 30.6 billion. In 3Q 2026, revenue was KRW 1.12 trillion, up 22.3% year on year, operating profit KRW 42.9 billion, up 88.1%, and parent-attributable net profit KRW 20.4 billion, up 119.7%.

The parent-attributable net profit for the last four quarters totals KRW 145.2 billion. A brokerage explained that the 1Q peak reflected the strongest city gas season.

05

Industry analysis

The city gas industry faces structural pressure on demand. An industry report said growth has slowed due to rising temperatures from global warming and electrification. Samchully has continued to reduce its city gas share and build alternative businesses.

On the supply side, falling international gas prices lowered supply costs, according to press coverage. Declines in city gas sales prices and the wholesale electricity price (SMP) were also cited in reporting on the group's results.

Wholesale LNG comes through supply structures such as KoreaGas, so international price moves flow into retail tariffs. Within this, fuel cell demand is growing and has a smaller seasonal swing than heating use, which helps spread winter demand, according to press reports.

On competition, among nine gas-sector companies on a 2025 annual basis, Samchully ranked fifth in operating margin and second in net margin. Its revenue and operating profit each ranked fourth in the same data.

Its scale sits in the upper middle of the sector, but its power generation business is tied to electrification trends, so that segment's direction also matters.

06

Outlook

In a June 2026 report, a domestic brokerage projected 2026 operating profit of KRW 178.8 billion, up 12%, and revenue of KRW 5.9 trillion. The same report said that, with the 1Q city gas improvement and the consolidation of the food subsidiary, full-year profit could reach a record level.

That forecast dates from June 2026, and 2Q and 3Q results have since been disclosed. 3Q operating profit was KRW 42.9 billion and parent-attributable net profit was KRW 20.4 billion. The same report cited a possible slowdown in district energy profitability in the second half as a downside factor.

On the city gas side, the key question is whether fuel cell sales keep growing, and press reports say new fuel cell supply is supporting that trend. How much the food subsidiary's consolidated results contribute to quarterly operating profit should be separated by business in periodic filings.

The next shareholder return question is whether dividends stay based on standalone net profit or move to a consolidated basis or a per-share framework. The same brokerage said a change in the long-held per-share dividend structure could increase the scope for a re-rating.

Fourth-quarter 2026 results have not yet been filed in a periodic report, so forecasts can be compared with actuals once full-year figures are finalized.

08

Bull factors

Fuel cell demand offsets the sales mix

City gas sales volume for fuel cells rose 28.6% in the first half of 2026, offsetting declines in residential and industrial use. According to an economic daily, new supply and increased operation drove this growth.

In the same report, standalone operating profit rose 3.6% to KRW 74.4 billion, which suggests the higher volume translated into improved earnings. Demand with smaller seasonal swings than heating was also cited as a factor that supports sales in the off-season.

Balance sheet and cash flow improvement

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 226.3% in 2022 to 136.9% in 2025. Cash flow from operating activities rose from KRW 210.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 306.5 billion in 2025. Hana Securities analyzed that the company turned to a net cash position on a consolidated basis in the first quarter of 2026. The same report explains that ample available cash gives it room to keep investing in new businesses.

Possible change in shareholder return policy

Dividends are set based on standalone net profit centered on city gas, and the dividend per share has stayed fixed for a long time.

In its June 2026 report, Hana Securities analyzed that the share price is likely to be re-rated if dividends move to a consolidated basis or a policy based on dividend per share becomes concrete. The core argument of this report is that whether the dividend policy changes is the key to the re-rating.

09

Bear factors

Structural pressure on city gas demand

According to an industry report, city gas revenue fell 11% over two years, from KRW 4.0082 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.5724 trillion in 2024. Explanations also point to rising temperatures and electrification as breaking city gas's growth momentum.

Lower supply prices due to falling international gas prices were also cited as a cause of the revenue decline. Annual revenue on a disclosure basis was KRW 5.1205 trillion in 2024, the smallest of 2022 to 2025.

Weakening quarterly earnings trend

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell for two consecutive quarters, from KRW 94.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 30.6 billion in the second quarter and KRW 20.4 billion in the third quarter.

There was also a quarter, the fourth quarter of 2025, in which it swung to a loss, with a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 100 million. Hana Securities cited the possible slowdown in district energy profitability in the second half as a downside factor.

The wide variation in quarterly results makes it hard to judge the annual trend, which is the basis for the bearish view.

Acquisition and execution load

In February 2026, KRW 119.5 billion was invested to acquire a 100% stake in Seonggyeong Foods. Seoul STV News interpreted the move into non-energy businesses such as food as an effort to compensate for the structural limits of the city gas business.

However, the earnings contribution and consolidation effect of the acquired business must be checked by segment in the periodic reports. With an operating margin of 3.0% in 2025, room to improve the core business margin also remains a challenge.

10

Risk factors

Tariff and wholesale price variables

A report on the group's results said that the city gas selling price and the system marginal price (SMP) both declined. Hana Securities analyzes that the district energy business may see slower profitability due to operator tariff adjustments in the second half.

The tariff system and raw material prices are variables the company can hardly control directly. How these variables are reflected in the operating margin is what to check in the next disclosure.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Operating profit was KRW 10.7 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 110.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026. Because the gap between quarters is large, seasonal and one-off factors must be separated when reading the results.

The combined net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters was KRW 145.2 billion, with a different pattern each quarter. This is why it is hard to define the direction of annual results.

Acquisition and new business execution risk

KRW 119.5 billion was invested in the Seonggyeong Foods acquisition, and plans to expand the business into food, dining, investment and other areas were reported. If the results of the new businesses fail to supplement core business profitability, the investment burden may remain.

Hana Securities viewed further expansion as possible given the large cash holdings, but this is a securities firm's analysis, and business performance has not been confirmed.

Overall view

Samchully operates city gas in the Seoul metropolitan area alongside power generation, automobiles, plant construction and district energy. On disclosed figures, 2025 operating profit of KRW 159.6 billion recovered from the 2024 decline, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 226.3% in 2022 to 136.9% in 2025.

In 2026, 1Q operating profit of KRW 110.2 billion was the peak, and profit shrank in 2Q and 3Q. In city gas sales, fuel cell demand is offsetting declines in household and industrial use. On the other hand, industry reports say city gas revenue fell from 2022 to 2024, and quarterly earnings swing widely.

The bullish arguments cited are improved balance sheet and cash flow and a possible change in dividend policy. The bearish arguments cited are weaker demand and lower quarterly profit. The next checkpoints are the 2026 full-year preliminary results and the dividend basis in the 2026 annual report. This report is for information only and does not include a buy or sell opinion.
