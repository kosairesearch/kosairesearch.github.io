GC MediAI: Sustaining the Profit Recovery Is the Key Test

After a return to net profit in 2025 and an operating profit rebound in 2026, the next tests are quarterly swings in net profit and the 2027 monetization timeline for AI and cloud EMR.

02

Business structure

GC MediAI is the top provider of electronic medical record (EMR) systems for primary care clinics in Korea. According to company IR materials, it serves 16,200 clinics and 8,300 pharmacies, with 11 million members on its patient platform Ttokdak and 33,000 e-commerce customers.

The business runs across four segments: Medical Infra, Data & MKT, Commerce and Platform. In Medical Infra, sales of EMR-based add-on solutions and higher revenue per customer have driven growth. Data & MKT is supported by the UBIST prescription data analytics service and related add-ons.

Commerce centers on brokered trading of medicines and medical supplies through Misomall, a platform built for medical institutions. In Platform, fees tied to Ttokdak and insurance claim integrations have started to be recognized as revenue, making this a new source of income.

On the consolidated side, Vivros, the operator of Ttokdak, was newly consolidated in the first half of 2026, and Crethem, a maker of automated pharmaceutical dispensing equipment, cut its first-half operating loss by KRW 1.7 billion through higher overseas sales and cost improvements.

The company exited the low-margin silver care and welfare equipment business and transferred part of the business of Hektun Project, a 75%-owned subsidiary. The structural feature of its competitive position is a Toll Road model, in which external services connect to its EMR network for fees.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 133.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 154 billion in 2023 (up 15.5% year on year), KRW 190.6 billion in 2024 (up 23.7%) and KRW 197.7 billion in 2025 (up 3.7%), extending growth for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with growth slowing sharply in 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 6.75 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.51 billion in 2023 (down 48.0% year on year), which marked the low point, and then rose to KRW 5.16 billion in 2024 (up 47.1%) and KRW 7.52 billion in 2025 (up 45.7%), an increase for two consecutive years.

The operating margin was 5.1% in 2022, 2.3% in 2023, 2.7% in 2024 and 3.8% in 2025, rebounding in 2025 but still below the 2022 level.

Net profit attributable to owners of the parent swung to a net loss of KRW 1.71 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 1.59 billion in 2024 with a narrower deficit, and then a profit of KRW 37 billion in 2025.

Because operating profit in 2025 was only KRW 7.52 billion, net profit attributable to owners far exceeded it, so the breakdown of non-operating items needs to be examined in detail to understand what drove the bottom line.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 37.8 billion in 2025, a separate figure from profit attributable to owners.

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 3 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 2.99 billion in the fourth quarter, then fell to KRW 2.24 billion in the first quarter of 2026 before rising to KRW 3.96 billion in the second quarter and KRW 5.86 billion in the third quarter, an increase for two consecutive quarters.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 52.7 billion, up 4.0% year on year, and net profit attributable to owners was KRW 6.16 billion, up 230.6% year on year.

However, the fourth quarter of 2025 posted a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 3.5 billion and the second quarter of 2026 a net loss of KRW 136 million, so quarterly bottom-line results remain volatile.

The four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 total KRW 2.6 billion of net profit attributable to owners. Operating cash flow was KRW 4.25 billion in 2022, KRW 2.49 billion in 2023 and KRW 2.35 billion in 2024, then rose to KRW 16.6 billion in 2025. The debt ratio climbed from 31.1% in 2022 to 48.5% in 2024 before easing to 43.3% in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The end market is one where EMR systems are already largely installed at clinics and pharmacies, and revenue is shifting from software sales toward AI clinical support and fees from external service integrations. The company is adding AI functions on top of the Uisarang EMR used at more than 16,000 clinics nationwide.

Uisarang AI, launched in August 2026, drew applications from more than 1,300 existing customer sites within a month of its pilot, and as of November 2026 about 800 sites were using it in actual patient care, according to the company.

This shows fast uptake, but because paid pricing has not started yet, it has not yet translated into revenue at scale. On the distribution side, the company's IR materials show first-half 2026 medicine transaction value of KRW 26 billion, up 180% year on year.

That figure reflects brokered volume in the Commerce segment, so its margin structure differs from EMR add-on solutions. Against competitors, GC MediAI holds the number one position in primary care EMR share, an installed base that new entrants would need to overcome switching costs to displace.

Whether that installed base becomes the springboard for AI adoption will determine how the company is positioned in this market.

06

Outlook

The company's 2026 consolidated operating profit guidance is KRW 17 billion, raised from KRW 13.5 billion set in May 2026, according to a disclosure on October 30, 2026.

In the same disclosure, the sales guidance was lowered from KRW 218.2 billion to KRW 174.4 billion, which the company attributed to the deconsolidation of part of Hektun Project after a partial business transfer.

The company expects to post operating profit above its previous guidance even after reflecting some business plan adjustments and additional costs such as direct-sales team incentives, provided that growth in core businesses and cost improvements continue.

In early November 2026, media reported that the CEO expects annual operating profit to exceed KRW 20 billion in 2027. The monetization timeline is phased. The company plans to secure more than 6,000 adoption references for Uisarang AI by the end of 2026 before moving to phased paid pricing in 2027.

The cloud-based EMR is scheduled for formal launch in March 2027, and the company plans to open its network of about 16,000 clinics and 8,300 pharmacies to external services, broadening revenue to pharmaceutical targeted digital marketing, API integration fees for external solutions, brokerage fees for insurance and financial transactions, and data transactions.

A B2C service called National Health Secretary, linked to the 11 million members of Ttokdak, is also planned for launch in the second half of 2027. Whether these plans translate into reported results will be confirmed in the 2026 annual business report and in quarterly results for 2027.

08

Bull factors

Operating profit recovery extends for two quarters

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 5.86 billion rose 95.7% year on year. It has increased for two consecutive quarters since the first-quarter 2026 low, and second-quarter operating profit of KRW 3.96 billion was up 76.7% from the prior quarter. Crethem's cost and cost-structure improvements have also supported consolidated profit.

Guidance raised alongside portfolio reshaping

The 2026 consolidated operating profit guidance was raised from KRW 13.5 billion to KRW 17 billion. The company cited the exit from the low-margin silver care and welfare equipment business and a refocus on AI, data and platform areas as the background. It expects operating profit to exceed the earlier guidance even after accounting for additional cost items.

Early results from the platform fee model

Fee revenue tied to insurance claim integrations is growing as the company connects its EMR network with the 11 million members of Ttokdak. Uisarang AI drew applications from more than 1,300 existing customer sites after its pilot, and about 800 are using it in patient care.

This customer base is the basis for the bull argument that it could become the starting point for paid pricing in 2027.

09

Bear factors

Thin operating margin base

The 2025 operating margin of 3.8% is below the 5.1% in 2022. The company passed through a low-margin period of 2.3% in 2023 and 2.7% in 2024 and is still recovering. Management has described fixed costs as the reason margins stayed in the 3% to 4% range under the traditional software distribution model.

Volatile quarterly bottom line

The fourth quarter of 2025 posted a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 3.5 billion, and the second quarter of 2026 a net loss of KRW 136 million. The four-quarter total is KRW 2.6 billion, and without the KRW 6.16 billion profit in the third quarter of 2026 it would have been negative.

The 2025 profit of KRW 37 billion far exceeded operating profit of KRW 7.52 billion, so the composition of earnings needs further review.

Slowing top-line growth

Revenue growth slowed from 23.7% in 2024 to 3.7% in 2025. First-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 49.8 billion fell 6.1% from the prior quarter, and third-quarter 2026 revenue rose only 4.0% year on year. With top-line growth modest, the profit recovery leans heavily on cost structure and subsidiary results.

10

Risk factors

Execution and monetization timing

Uisarang AI is planned to reach more than 6,000 adoption references by the end of 2026 before moving to phased paid pricing in 2027. If reference wins slow or clinics are slow to accept paid pricing, monetization of the AI business could be pushed back. The March 2027 formal launch of the cloud EMR carries similar execution risk.

Regulation, privacy and partner dependence

Fees from external service integrations, pharmaceutical targeted marketing and insurance or financial brokerage depend on medical data and privacy rules as well as on partner policies.

The B2C National Health Secretary service planned for the second half of 2027 draws on a base of 11 million members, so changes in the regulatory environment could affect its timing.

Subsidiary earnings and portfolio changes

Crethem plans to raise sales and improve profitability further in the second half, relying on normalization of its overseas distribution network and domestic after-sales capability. If overseas distribution normalizes slowly, the effect of narrowing the subsidiary's losses will be smaller.

Changes in the consolidation scope following the partial transfer of Hektun Project's business could still feed into costs or earnings swings.

12

Overall view

GC MediAI is the leading EMR provider for primary care clinics, and its net profit attributable to owners turned from a loss in 2023 to a profit in 2025.

Operating profit has risen for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025 after the 2023 low, and the increase has continued for two consecutive quarters from the second quarter of 2026.

The 2026 consolidated operating profit guidance was raised to KRW 17 billion, while the sales guidance was lowered after a consolidation change. On the strength side, earnings capacity is moving toward recovery, and the platform and AI-based revenue models are drawing customer applications in an early stage.

On the weakness side, the operating margin has not returned to the 2022 level, and quarterly net profit attributable to owners has alternated between profit and loss, leaving a four-quarter total of KRW 2.6 billion.

The monetization steps, including paid pricing for Uisarang AI and the cloud EMR launch, are concentrated in 2027, so there will be a lag before they appear in reported results.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 results and the 2026 annual business report on operating margin and the durability of profit attributable to owners, along with whether the 2027 monetization timeline is met.
