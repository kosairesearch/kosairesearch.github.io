Report overview

High operating margin, shrinking profit at an office REIT

IGIS Value Plus REIT's revenue, operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders all fell in the year ended August 2026, while the operating margin stayed high at 65.2% and the debt ratio was 45.1%.

02

Business structure

IGIS Value Plus REIT is a public REIT listed on the KOSPI market in July 2020, and its asset manager is IGIS Asset Management.

According to the company's introduction materials, it invests mainly in prime office assets, allocating more than 70% to core assets for stable dividends and no more than 30% to +Alpha assets with higher growth potential.

Investment is made indirectly through lower-tier vehicles such as funds or other REITs, not only through direct property acquisition.

Assets presented on the company website include office properties such as Taepyeongno Building, the Isu Chemical Banpo headquarters and Twin Tree Tower, data centers such as the North America DC portfolio and the Bundang Hostway IDC, and the Icheon Y logistics center.

The website cited assets under management of about KRW 1.48 trillion as of December 2022, a figure that may differ from current levels. A 2025 news report said the company moved to invest about KRW 400 billion in a Samsung Heavy Industries R&D center in Pangyo.

Listed peers in the same real estate and REIT sector include Koramco THE ONE REIT, Shinhan Alpha REIT, Lotte REIT and SK REIT. The current revenue mix by segment should be checked in the notes to the latest periodic report, and the current asset list should be reconciled against the latest disclosures.

04

Earnings analysis

On the basis of periodic report disclosures, revenue was KRW 53.6 billion in the year ended August 2023, KRW 10.4 billion in the year ended August 2024 (down 80.6% year on year), KRW 21.8 billion in the year ended August 2025 (up 109.3%) and KRW 16.5 billion in the year ended August 2026 (down 24.1%).

The year ended August 2023 was the largest year in the period and the year ended August 2024 was the smallest. Operating profit was KRW 40.1 billion, KRW 636 million (down 98.4%), KRW 16.6 billion (up 2,515.5%) and KRW 10.8 billion (down 35.2%) in the same years.

Volatility in the operating margin is the defining feature of this period.

A 2025 news report said that valuation gains of about KRW 30.2 billion and the recovery of KRW 29.7 billion in investment principal from restructuring real estate-related securities had lifted operating profit in the preceding fiscal year, which suggests one-off items can move the margin sharply.

The same report said funding costs tied to contributions for new investment assets and costs from refinancing existing securities raised operating expenses.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 34.2 billion in the year ended August 2023 to a net loss of KRW 2.31 billion in the year ended August 2024, a swing into loss; it returned to a profit of KRW 12.4 billion in the year ended August 2025 and then fell to KRW 7.82 billion in the year ended August 2026 (down 37.1%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 12.4 billion in the year ended August 2025 and KRW 7.81 billion in the year ended August 2026, close to the controlling-shareholder figure.

Operating cash flow was KRW 7.27 billion, KRW 2.27 billion, KRW 6.23 billion and KRW 8.65 billion across the four years from 2023 to 2026. The debt ratio was 38.8%, 26.9%, 52.2% and 45.1% in the same order.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 (September to November) through the third quarter of 2026 (June to August) totaled KRW 7.82 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Listed REITs in Korea operate with a dividend-centred structure, and because statutory dividend requirements apply, earnings trends are closely linked to distributable capacity. Office rental income depends on vacancy rates, rent levels and the lease expiry profile of large tenants.

Because this REIT holds a high share of prime offices, location and tenant quality are the key variables for income stability. Interest rate levels affect both asset valuation and borrowing costs.

As a 2025 news report indicated, higher funding costs tied to contributions for new assets can push up short-term operating expenses. Peers such as Koramco THE ONE REIT, Shinhan Alpha REIT, Lotte REIT and SK REIT differ in asset mix and scale, so direct comparison has limits.

This REIT's portfolio spans offices, data centers and logistics, and its stated policy of keeping core assets above 70% points to a strategy that prioritises asset stability. However, the cap of 30% on +Alpha assets limits the contribution from growth assets.

Whether office demand is recovering or new supply is pressuring rents will shape the return direction of office holdings. The company's income also depends on valuation gains and restructuring gains, not only rents, so the effect of individual transactions should be checked apart from sector conditions. Maintaining a high operating margin within the sector is a feature of cost control.

06

Outlook

The company's stated plan centres on adding quality prime office assets and pursuing sale gains through +Alpha assets. Its introduction materials say it will prioritise funds or REITs that hold quality office buildings.

Whether the Pangyo R&D center investment reported in early 2025 has been added to the portfolio and contributed to earnings should be confirmed in the investment asset details of the periodic report.

On the ownership side, a large-holding report showed Mirae Asset Global Investments holding 11.47% as of the report's reference date of February 27, 2026. Shareholder changes can affect supply and demand for the units and voting dynamics, so later reports should be reviewed together.

Dividends are shown as being paid on a quarterly basis, and the funding source is linked to operating cash flow and earnings trends.

Operating cash flow of KRW 8.65 billion in the year ended August 2026 exceeded net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.82 billion, which can be read as a positive sign for dividend capacity, but non-cash items such as valuation gains should also be considered.

The move from a higher debt ratio in the year ended August 2025 to 45.1% in the year ended August 2026 can be read as refinancing and asset-contribution costs being absorbed.

Numerical targets presented by the company can change with disclosures and IR materials, so they should be checked against the latest periodic reports and IR materials.

The direction of future earnings depends on the stability of rental income, the results of asset valuations and financing costs, and cannot be determined from current data alone.

08

Bull factors

A high operating margin structure

The operating margin was 74.9% in the year ended August 2023, 76.3% in the year ended August 2025 and 65.2% in the year ended August 2026, a high range. This points to comparatively small cost burdens in a REIT structure built on rental and investment income. The margin fell to 6.1% in the year ended August 2024, but the subsequent recovery is visible in the figures.

Improved cash generation

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 6.23 billion in the year ended August 2025 to KRW 8.65 billion in the year ended August 2026. Over the same period, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders declined while cash flow increased, widening the gap between earnings and cash. This can be read as a positive signal for the funding of dividends.

Stabilising financial structure

The debt ratio fell from 52.2% in the year ended August 2025 to 45.1% in the year ended August 2026. At the end of August 2026, total equity was KRW 446.2 billion and total liabilities were KRW 201.3 billion. The price-to-book ratio is below 1x, so the share price sits at a low level relative to book value.

Bear factors

Declining earnings

Revenue for the year ended August 2026 fell 24.1% year on year and operating profit fell 35.2%. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7.82 billion, down 37.1%. Earnings that had recovered in the year ended August 2025 declined again within a single year.

Earnings volatility

In the year ended August 2024, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 2.31 billion and operating profit fell to KRW 636 million.

A 2025 news report said the operating profit increase was helped by one-off items such as valuation gains from restructuring and recovery of invested principal. These factors make the sustainability of earnings harder to judge.

Financing costs and refinancing burden

A 2025 news report said funding costs for contributions to new investment assets and costs from refinancing existing securities increased operating expenses. Liabilities rose sharply in the year ended August 2025 against this backdrop. If the interest rate environment changes, this cost burden could grow again.

Risk factors

Interest rates and funding costs

Because a property REIT relies on borrowing and fundraising, higher rates raise interest costs and reduce distributable capacity. Transaction costs from new asset contributions or refinancing can cause short-term swings in operating profit.

The debt ratio of 45.1% in the year ended August 2026 is lower than 52.2% a year earlier, but total liabilities remain KRW 201.3 billion.

Volatility from asset valuation

Operating profit at this REIT has in the past depended heavily on valuation gains from restructuring real estate-related securities. If such gains shrink or turn into losses, operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders can change sharply. The net loss in the year ended August 2024 is an example.

Concentration in office assets

The company has stated a policy of at least 70% in core assets and no more than 30% in +Alpha assets, with prime offices at the centre. Vacancies or rent adjustments in the office market directly affect rental income. Tenant mix and lease expiry profile should be checked against the latest periodic reports.

12

Overall view

IGIS Value Plus REIT's year ended August 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 16.5 billion, operating profit of KRW 10.8 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.82 billion, all lower than a year earlier.

However, the operating margin of 65.2% and operating cash flow of KRW 8.65 billion stayed at high levels. The debt ratio fell from 52.2% in the year ended August 2025 to 45.1%, and total equity at the end of August 2026 was KRW 446.2 billion.

Positive factors include a high margin, higher cash generation and a lower debt ratio, while negative factors include declining earnings, large volatility seen in the net loss in the year ended August 2024, and financing cost burdens.

The fact that a meaningful part of earnings relied on valuation and restructuring gains is central to judging sustainability. The price-to-book ratio is below 1x while earnings have declined, so valuation interpretation depends on whether earnings recover and on asset valuation results.

The next checkpoints are the quarterly report for the fourth quarter of 2026 (September to November) and the periodic report for the year ending August 2027.
