Seoyon E-Hwa: Sales Up, Margins Under Pressure

Revenue has grown for three straight years since 2023, but the operating margin fell from 5.4% in 2023 to 3.6% in 2025, and the company posted an attributable net loss of KRW 36.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

02

Business structure

Seoyon E-Hwa makes automotive interior and exterior parts, including door trim, seats, pillar trim, consoles, headlining, package trays, cargo screens and bumpers. The company operates plants close to vehicle assembly sites in the United States, Mexico, China, India and Europe and supplies parts locally.

Consolidated revenue for the first nine months of 2025 was KRW 3.36 trillion, and overseas sales made up 74.5% of it. By region, the United States was the largest at 31.9%, followed by Europe at 15.8%, India at 13.1% and Mexico at 6.7%. The customer base leans heavily on Hyundai Motor and KIA.

Those two companies accounted for 87% of 2024 annual revenue of KRW 4.04 trillion. In the first nine months of 2026, their combined share was about 82.5%, with sales tied to KIA up 20.0% and sales tied to Hyundai Motor down 7.8% over the same period. Other customers include Volkswagen, Ford and Jaguar Land Rover.

Local press reported that new customers such as Mercedes-Benz, Ford and Volkswagen supported first-half 2026 results. The company states it holds about 70% of the domestic door trim market for the Hyundai Motor Group.

Its strength lies in the spread of plants and customers, while its weakness is concentration on the Hyundai Motor Group.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue rose from KRW 2.85 trillion in 2022 to KRW 4.51 trillion in 2025, growing three years in a row from 2023 to 2025. Growth slowed from 25.6% in 2023 to 13.1% in 2024 and 11.5% in 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022–2025.

Operating profit was KRW 150.7 billion in 2022, KRW 193.1 billion in 2023 (up 28.2%), KRW 154.4 billion in 2024 (down 20.0%) and KRW 161.8 billion in 2025 (up 4.8%), with 2023 the largest year.

The operating margin slipped from 5.3% in 2022 and 5.4% in 2023 to 3.8% in 2024 and 3.6% in 2025, so higher sales did not lift margins. A May 2025 Hana Securities report expected initial costs at new plants and US import tariffs to weigh on profitability, and the operating margin for the first quarter of 2025 was 3.7%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 55.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 157.8 billion in 2023 (up 183.4%), then fell for two years to KRW 147.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 60 billion in 2025 (down 59.3%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 61.1 billion in 2025, a different figure from the attributable amount. The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 160.0% at the end of 2025 from 154.3% in 2024, and operating cash flow fell to KRW 164.1 billion in 2025 from KRW 266.7 billion in 2024.

Operating profit in the fourth quarter of 2025 fell to KRW 21.2 billion, down 56.7% from the prior quarter, and the company recorded an attributable net loss of KRW 1.75 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026, operating profit was KRW 48.6 billion and attributable net income KRW 47.5 billion, a swing back to profit, and second-quarter revenue rose 5.5% quarter on quarter to KRW 1.25 trillion.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 1.19 trillion, up 8.2% year on year, but operating profit was KRW 24.8 billion, down 49.3%, and the attributable net loss was KRW 36.9 billion.

Press reports said third-quarter cost of sales rose 10.5% to KRW 1.03 trillion, outpacing the revenue gain, and financing costs including foreign currency translation losses also increased.

05

Industry analysis

Seoyon E-Hwa's end market is the vehicle production and sales of the Hyundai Motor Group, and its interior-parts volumes move with customers' production plans.

Local research firm KIS Monitor cited domestic auto demand recovery, a cut in the consumption tax on vehicles and a scrappage support policy as drivers of first-half 2026 results. In the United States, tariff policy remains a variable that changes the cost gap between imported parts and local production.

Currency moves cut both ways: a Bloter report said that although overseas sales exceeded 70% of revenue in the first nine months of 2025, net income fell sharply in a period of currency weakness. The business model rests on plants located near vehicle assembly sites.

Consolidated revenue roughly doubled from about KRW 2 trillion in 2019 to KRW 4.04 trillion in 2024, and the Americas share of sales rose from 23% to 37% over that span. The company says its domestic door trim share for the Hyundai Motor Group is 70%.

Against larger parts makers such as Hyundai Mobis or HL Mando, Seoyon E-Hwa has a narrower interior focus and heavier dependence on one customer group, so the business mix differs.

Because sales track customer-level demand closely, the production plans of the two Hyundai Motor Group automakers are a key reference point for where the cycle stands.

06

Outlook

A November 12, 2026 report said Seoyon E-Hwa will invest about KRW 225 billion in Tamil Nadu, India, together with Hwashin. Officials in Tamil Nadu said the two companies' plans fit the state's long-term industrial strategy.

The expansion upgrades equipment at existing sites to widen local operations, and sales in India rose 25.3% to KRW 5.51 billion in the first nine months of 2026 compared with a year earlier. KIS Monitor noted that consolidated revenue in the first half of 2026 rose 7.8% and operating profit rose 8.8%.

Since 2023, capacity has been built mainly through new plants, including a second plant in Slovakia, Krishnagiri in India, and Savannah and Texas in the United States. Whether volumes from new customers feed through to earnings is the next point to watch.

Whether the third-quarter rise in cost of sales carries into the fourth quarter will show in the preliminary results. US tariffs and exchange rates remain the variables that will decide profitability at production sites. In the 2026 annual report, the key test is how the full-year operating margin compares with 3.6% in 2025.

08

Bull factors

Overseas production base and customer diversification

Overseas sales were 74.5% of consolidated revenue in the first nine months of 2025, with the United States the largest at 31.9%. Plants in the United States, India, Europe and Mexico let the company follow automakers' local production volumes.

Local press cited new customers such as Mercedes-Benz, Ford and Volkswagen as a factor behind first-half 2026 improvement.

Domestic demand recovery and policy support

KIS Monitor summarized that domestic auto demand recovery, a consumption tax cut and a scrappage support policy lifted vehicle sales and parts demand in the first half of 2026. Consolidated revenue rose 7.8% and operating profit rose 8.8% over the same period. The domestic recovery supports demand for door trim volumes in Korea.

India expansion and investment plan

A November 12, 2026 report said Seoyon E-Hwa will invest about KRW 225 billion in Tamil Nadu, India, together with Hwashin. Indian sales reached KRW 550.9 billion in the first nine months of 2026, up 25.3%. The plan upgrades equipment at existing sites to widen local operations.

09

Bear factors

Dependence on Hyundai Motor and KIA

Sales tied to Hyundai Motor fell 7.8% over the period, while those tied to KIA rose 20.0%. The bear case rests on the risk that parts suppliers' sales shrink along with automakers' sales in a downturn.

Rising cost ratio and slower profitability

Third-quarter 2026 cost of sales rose 10.5%, outpacing the 8.2% revenue gain. Press reports put the cost ratio at 86.7%, up from 84.9% a year earlier, and the operating margin fell to 2.09%. Third-quarter operating profit of KRW 24.8 billion was down 49.3% year on year.

Currency swings, financing costs and balance sheet load

In the third quarter of 2026, higher financing costs including foreign currency translation losses produced an attributable net loss of KRW 36.9 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 160.0% at the end of 2025 from 154.3% in 2024.

Bloter noted that net income fell sharply during a currency upturn despite the company's high overseas sales share.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration

With Hyundai Motor and KIA above 80% of revenue, changes to their production plans pass straight through to sales. Those two companies accounted for 87% of 2024 revenue. A weaker automotive cycle could reduce volumes and hit earnings directly.

FX and tariff exposure

Overseas sales above 70% of revenue mean currency moves affect both translated revenue and foreign currency gains and losses. US tariff policy changes the cost gap between imported parts and local production. The utilization of US plants will show how much of that impact is offset.

Capex load and leverage

Capacity expansion in Slovakia, India and the United States has continued since 2023. Initial costs at new plants weighed on profitability in the first quarter of 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 154.3% in 2024 to 160.0% in 2025.

12

Overall view

Seoyon E-Hwa grew revenue from 2022 to 2025, reaching KRW 4.51 trillion in 2025. However, the operating margin fell from 5.4% in 2023 to 3.6% in 2025, and attributable net income declined for two years after its 2023 peak.

In the third quarter of 2026 revenue rose 8.2% year on year, but operating profit fell 49.3% and the company posted an attributable net loss of KRW 36.9 billion. Bullish factors include the overseas production base, new customers, the domestic demand recovery and the reported India investment.

Bearish factors include customer concentration with Hyundai Motor and KIA at about 82.5% of revenue, a higher cost ratio and selling expenses, currency and tariff exposure, and a rising debt-to-equity ratio.

First-half 2026 results improved on a year-earlier basis, but cost pressure reappeared in the third quarter, which is where the two trends meet. With the last four quarters' attributable net income totaling KRW 41.7 billion, the earnings base is small, so quarterly swings carry a large weight.

The next points to check are the execution of the India investment, the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
