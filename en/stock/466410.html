Slowing Growth, Accelerating AI Pivot

Synapsoft posted two straight years of revenue growth through 2025 while operating profit fell for two consecutive years, as the company attempts to shift its revenue mix toward new digital-document AI products.

02

Business structure

Founded in 2000, Synapsoft is a digital document software company whose revenue is split between a 'Digital Document Solutions' segment centered on its browser-based document viewer (alongside editor, office, and PDFocus products) and a 'Digital Document AI' segment comprising OCR, document analyzer, and assistant products.

The Digital Document Solutions segment's revenue share declined from 93.10% in 2023 to 89.00% in 2024 and 82.68% in 2025, while the Digital Document AI segment steadily expanded from 7.00% to 11.00% to 17.32% over the same period.

On January 26, 2026, the company launched 'Synap OCR IX,' a new product combining VLM and AI agent functions that intelligently combines recognition engines by document type.

Its customer base includes more than 7,000 private companies, including Samsung Electronics, SK, Hyundai, and LG, and covers roughly 80% of government institutions based on homepage viewer usage.

In 2025, the top five customers accounted for just 11.3% of total revenue, with no single client exceeding 10%, indicating low customer concentration.

Media reports have cited the company's document viewer market share (by number of public-institution websites) at 80% and document filter market share at 95%, pointing to a leading position in those segments.

The business model relies on repeat sales of once-developed products with minimal cost of goods sold and a largely fixed, labor-cost structure, supporting high operating margins.

Operations span both the enterprise (B2B) and public/education (B2G) markets, supplying products that address AI transformation (AX) and digital transformation (DX) demand in each.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 12.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 13.3 billion in 2024 (up 4.2% YoY) and KRW 13.5 billion in 2025 (up 1.7% YoY), marking two consecutive years of growth from 2024, though the growth rate itself decelerated.

Conversely, operating profit fell from KRW 6.43 billion in 2023 to KRW 5.07 billion in 2024 (down 21.1% YoY) and KRW 4.67 billion in 2025 (down 7.9% YoY), with the operating margin declining from 50.6% in 2023 to 38.3% in 2024 and 34.7% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to owners of the parent also fell for two consecutive years, from KRW 6.21 billion in 2023 to KRW 5.99 billion in 2024 (down 3.6% YoY) and KRW 5.7 billion in 2025 (down 4.8% YoY).

The decline in profit despite revenue growth stemmed from fixed operating costs such as personnel expenses rising faster than revenue, as 2025 operating revenue increased by KRW 219 million year-over-year while operating expenses rose by KRW 620 million.

On a quarterly basis, revenue climbed from KRW 3.02 billion in 2Q25 to KRW 3.14 billion in 3Q25 (up 3.9% QoQ) and KRW 4.49 billion in 4Q25 (up 43.0% QoQ), before reversing to KRW 3.44 billion in 1Q26 (down 23.3% QoQ) and KRW 2.86 billion in 2Q26 (down 16.8% QoQ and 5.2% YoY), declining for two straight quarters after the 4Q25 peak.

Operating profit followed the same pattern, rising to KRW 2.02 billion in 4Q25 before falling to KRW 1.09 billion in 1Q26 (down 45.8% QoQ) and KRW 489 million in 2Q26 (down 55.2% QoQ and 41.1% YoY).

Net profit attributable to owners of the parent also mirrored this trend, falling from KRW 2 billion in 4Q25 to KRW 1.45 billion in 1Q26 (down 27.6% QoQ) and KRW 893 million in 2Q26 (down 38.5% QoQ and 25.7% YoY), bringing the trailing four-quarter sum (3Q25-2Q26) to KRW 5.66 billion.

On the balance sheet, total equity stood at KRW 69.7 billion (entirely attributable to owners of the parent) against total liabilities of KRW 2.36 billion at year-end 2025, lowering the debt ratio from 23.0% in 2023 to 3.4% in 2025, while operating cash flow remained steady at KRW 6.4 billion, KRW 6.25 billion, and KRW 6.23 billion in 2023, 2024, and 2025 respectively, supporting overall financial stability.

05

Industry analysis

South Korea's digital document processing market is seeing its competitive landscape expand into OCR and document AI as cloud migration, SaaS adoption, and generative AI-driven unstructured data processing demand converge across the public and private sectors.

Synapsoft has previously been reported to hold an 80% market share in document viewers (by number of public-institution websites) and a 95% share in document filters, positioning it as a leader in traditional document processing.

However, this segment is already mature, as reflected in the Digital Document Solutions segment's revenue share declining from 93.10% in 2023 to 82.68% in 2025, shifting the business more toward maintenance and renewal revenue from existing customers rather than new demand.

In contrast, the OCR and document AI space is seeing growing participation from large platform companies such as NAVER and Upstage as well as specialized AI startups, and Synapsoft's expanding Digital Document AI revenue share (from 7.00% in 2023 to 17.32% in 2025) can be read as an attempt to capture share in this increasingly competitive market.

In the public sector, policy shifts such as the transition to open document formats (HWPX) and reduced reliance on Hangul and Computer's 'HWP' format are underway, prompting the company to strengthen HWPX support in its product strategy.

Relative to peers, Synapsoft's distinguishing features are its high operating margin, built on a low cost structure, and low customer concentration, with its top five customers accounting for just 11.3% of revenue.

A feature common across this industry is that quarterly order and revenue recognition timing can swing significantly depending on public-sector IT budget cycles and enterprise digital transformation investment cycles, as illustrated by the sharp 4Q25 revenue jump followed by the first-half 2026 pullback.

06

Outlook

The company is upgrading its product lineup toward intelligently combining recognition engines by document type, launching 'Synap OCR IX' on January 26, 2026, which combines VLM and AI agent functions.

In 2026, the company has held a series of seminars, including one on document data refinement technology for AI agents and another on document AI technology, stepping up sales efforts aimed directly at enterprise and institutional AI transformation demand.

It has also been selected for the Ministry of Science and ICT's generative AI talent cultivation program, linking its business to government-supported initiatives.

In the public sector, the company has announced a product strategy to strengthen HWPX support in response to the spread of open document formats, reflecting efforts to address public institutions' document format transition needs.

However, how much these new product and business investments can offset the revenue and profit slowdown seen in the first half of 2026 following the 4Q25 peak has not yet been confirmed in disclosed figures.

Around the time of its IPO, the company indicated that B2B revenue was growing rapidly, but subsequent disclosed results show the Digital Document Solutions segment's revenue share actually declining, meaning the pace of the actual revenue mix shift needs to be confirmed through upcoming quarterly reports.

With the 3Q26 regular report not yet disclosed, whether the revenue and profit slowdown seen over the two quarters through 2Q26 continues into the third quarter will be a key variable for gauging the company's earnings trajectory going forward.

Bull factors

Stable Balance Sheet and Cash Generation

At year-end 2025, the debt ratio was just 3.4% and total equity of KRW 69.7 billion was entirely attributable to owners of the parent, while operating cash flow remained steady in the KRW 6 billion range every year from 2023 to 2025.

The operating margin, while declining, still stood at a high 34.7% in 2025 relative to the software industry. The low cost structure inherent in its solution business model offers potential operating leverage on incremental revenue.

Structural Expansion of the Digital Document AI Segment

The Digital Document AI segment's revenue share has steadily expanded from 7.00% in 2023 to 17.32% in 2025, and in January 2026 the company launched 'Synap OCR IX,' a new product combining VLM and AI agent functions.

The company continues to extend the data and technology accumulated from its traditional viewer and filter business into OCR and document AI. Its selection for the Ministry of Science and ICT's generative AI talent cultivation program could also serve as a springboard for new business expansion.

Low Customer Concentration and Broad Reference Base

In 2025, the top five customers accounted for just 11.3% of total revenue, with no single client exceeding 10%, limiting the earnings impact of any individual customer loss.

The company's customer base of more than 7,000 private companies, including Samsung Electronics, SK, Hyundai, and LG, and roughly 80% of government institutions can also support reference-based sales efforts.

09

Bear factors

Maturation of the Core Business and Slowing Growth

While the core Digital Document Solutions segment's revenue share fell from 93.10% in 2023 to 82.68% in 2025, the company's overall revenue growth rate also slowed from 4.2% in 2024 to 1.7% in 2025.

The business is shifting toward greater reliance on maintenance and renewal revenue from existing customers rather than new demand, making it uncertain whether future revenue growth can match past rates.

Two Straight Years of Profit Decline and Margin Pressure

Operating profit fell for two consecutive years from KRW 6.43 billion in 2023 to KRW 5.07 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.67 billion in 2025, with the operating margin declining from 50.6% in 2023 to 34.7% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to owners of the parent also fell for two straight years, from KRW 6.21 billion to KRW 5.7 billion over the same period, reflecting a pattern in which revenue growth has not translated into profit growth.

Sharp Quarterly Earnings Volatility

Revenue (KRW 4.49 billion) and operating profit (KRW 2.02 billion) in 4Q25 surged 43.0% and 117.9% quarter-over-quarter, respectively, before revenue, operating profit, and net profit attributable to owners of the parent all declined for two consecutive quarters in 1Q26 and 2Q26.

In 2Q26, revenue fell 5.2% and operating profit fell 41.1% year-over-year, showing the company failing to surpass the prior-year growth base.

Risk factors

Earnings Seasonality and Volatility

Quarterly revenue and order recognition can swing significantly depending on the timing of public and enterprise customer budget execution, as shown by the two-quarter slowdown in the first half of 2026 following the 4Q25 peak.

Strong results in any single quarter are difficult to extrapolate into an annual trend, requiring confirmation through subsequent quarterly reports.

Risk of Intensifying Competition

The OCR and generative AI-based document processing market is seeing growing entry from large platform companies and specialized AI firms, meaning sustained expansion of the Digital Document AI segment's revenue share will require the company to maintain differentiation in technology and pricing against these competitors.

The competitive landscape in its traditional strength areas of document viewers and filters could also shift depending on changes in public-sector format policy.

Public-Sector Dependence and Policy Change

Because its business spans the public and education market (B2G), results can be affected by changes in government IT budget allocation or document format policy.

While policy directions such as the shift to open document formats (HWPX) could present opportunities, budget cuts or policy delays could negatively affect order and revenue timing.

Overall view

Synapsoft has shown a structural divergence over 2023-2025, with revenue growing for two consecutive years while operating profit and net profit attributable to owners of the parent both declined over the same period.

On a quarterly basis, momentum has also slowed, with revenue and profit falling for two consecutive quarters in 1Q26 and 2Q26 following the 4Q25 peak.

Still, the balance sheet remains stable, with a 3.4% debt ratio and steady operating cash flow, and early signs of a business mix shift are also visible, as the Digital Document AI segment's revenue share expanded from 7.00% in 2023 to 17.32% in 2025.

How much new products such as 'Synap OCR IX,' launched in January 2026, will support this shift has not yet been confirmed in disclosed results.

A diversified customer base with low reliance on any single client is a stabilizing factor, but quarterly volatility tied to public-sector budget cycles remains a variable requiring further interpretation.

The upcoming 3Q26 report and the adoption progress of new products are likely to be key checkpoints for gauging the company's future earnings direction.
