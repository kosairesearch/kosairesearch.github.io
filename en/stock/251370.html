02

Business structure

YMT develops and manufactures chemical materials used in printed circuit boards (PCBs) and semiconductor package substrates. Its business is centered on chemical products: in 2022, chemicals accounted for 59.1% of segment sales and substrate processing for 17.4%, according to a September 2023 KIRS research report.

Within chemicals, the portfolio includes final surface finish materials, copper plating materials and process chemicals. Copper oxidizes easily in air, so surface finish materials deposit films such as gold, silver or tin to protect the circuit.

Its customers are led by Samsung Electro-Mechanics and other global PCB makers, which supply high-end electronics makers, according to the company.

Its semiconductor package substrate business opened in 2020, when it began supplying electroless copper plating materials and process chemicals to major domestic substrate makers. Within chemicals, the share of sales to package substrate customers rose from 4.5% in 2019 to 15.3% in 2022.

The company is also building electronic materials such as copper foil and EMI shielding film as new businesses. The company says it entered the domestic and overseas electronic chemicals market, where Japan and Germany had held positions, with its own technology.

It also has a plant in Guangdong, China, which was described as nearing completion around 2025. Its glass-substrate (TGV) plating business is at the sample and pilot-production stage. Listed domestic peers in chemical materials include Soulbrain, Dongjin Semichem and Hansol Chemical.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 130.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 127.4 billion in 2023, down 2.4% year on year, then rose 7.7% to KRW 137.2 billion in 2024 and 5.4% to KRW 144.6 billion in 2025, two consecutive years of growth after the 2023 low. 2025 was the largest revenue year in the 2022–2025 period.

Operating profit was KRW 3.34 billion in 2022, an operating loss of KRW 3.17 billion in 2023 (a swing to loss from the prior year), KRW 3.59 billion in 2024 (a swing to profit) and KRW 12.9 billion in 2025, up 259.6%. The operating margin moved from 2.6% in 2022 to -2.5% in 2023, 2.6% in 2024 and 8.9% in 2025.

For the first half of 2025, the company cited the start of TGV glass-substrate supply, expansion of overseas PCB customers and better domestic unit pricing as drivers, and its cost of sales ratio fell from 81.3% to 75.0%.

An independent research firm noted that supply to China's VGT began in the second quarter of 2025, lifting the share of higher-priced products.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 52.19 million in 2022, KRW 2.89 billion in 2023 (losses continued and widened), KRW 4.34 billion in 2024 (losses continued and widened) and KRW 1.68 billion in 2025 (losses continued but narrowed), four consecutive years of losses from 2022 through 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 2.6 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 75.86 million in 2023, a net loss of KRW 2.33 billion in 2024 and net income of KRW 1.47 billion in 2025. In 2025 the two measures pointed in opposite directions, because non-controlling interests carried income.

By quarter, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 36.1 billion, operating profit KRW 3.21 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 882 million.

Q4 2025 revenue rose 6.3% to KRW 38.4 billion, operating profit rose 29.3% to KRW 4.15 billion, and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.44 billion, a swing to loss.

Q1 2026 revenue fell 8.9% to KRW 35 billion, operating profit fell 37.8% to KRW 2.58 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.17 billion, a swing to profit.

Q2 2026 revenue rebounded 17.8% to KRW 41.2 billion, but operating profit fell 38.9% to KRW 1.58 billion and the company swung back to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.63 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 41.7 billion, up 1.2% quarter on quarter and 15.4% year on year, operating profit was KRW 1.72 billion, up 9.1% quarter on quarter but down 46.4% year on year, and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.1 billion, a swing to loss from a year earlier.

Losses have continued for two consecutive quarters since Q2 2026, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 6 billion. Revenue has recovered, but profit has not followed, so whether revenue recovery converts into operating profit is the central quarterly question.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 13.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.5 billion in 2025, after dipping to KRW 7.03 billion in 2024. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 218.3 billion, of which KRW 189.2 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 78.3% in 2024 to 71.2% in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

YMT operates in the PCB and semiconductor package substrate materials market. Demand for high-performance chips from HPC, AI accelerators and HBM has drawn attention to glass substrates as a next-generation packaging technology that could replace or complement organic substrates.

The company's IR material says substrate line widths have narrowed from about 50 micrometers to about 35 micrometers within a year, which raises the precision required in plating. A September 2023 KIRS research report projected the total PCB market to reach USD 14.5 billion by 2026, growing about 11% a year.

The company's own IR material projected 3–6% annual growth in value terms from 2022, led by server and telecom infrastructure equipment and package substrates. In competition, the company emphasizes that it broke a market previously monopolized by Japanese suppliers with domestic technology.

An independent research firm described YMT as expanding from PCB chemicals into the AI semiconductor value chain. YMT's revenue is tied to PCB and semiconductor end-demand, so the position of that cycle drives results.

Domestic chemical peers include Soulbrain, Dongjin Semichem and Hansol Chemical, while substrate makers such as Simmtech and Daeduck Electronics sit in the same value chain.

Samsung Electro-Mechanics is both a key customer and a leading substrate maker, which makes it a useful reference point for YMT's demand structure.

06

Outlook

The most specific plan the company has disclosed is expanding glass-substrate plating capacity. Once the expansion is complete, capacity is planned to rise from about 300 sheets a month to 1,200–1,500 sheets a month.

A company official said equipment setup is nearly complete and that additional capacity could be secured as early as late 2026, and no later than early 2027, after permits are obtained. The timing and the scale of actual output gains depend on completing those permits.

TGV glass substrates are at the sample-verification stage, with pilot production and paid sample supply under way. An independent research firm said the company developed its TGV plating technology in-house, completed sample supply, and is working on mass production with JWMT and other partners.

The same firm noted that JWMT targets completing a new plant in late 2027, which would lift chemical and plating demand as well. In 2025, the company said it aimed to raise China-related product sales to the KRW 40 billion range in 2026.

That target dates from 2025, so how overseas sales appear in 2026 results needs to be checked. In August 2025, the company said quality and process requirement talks with a customer for a mass-production transition were ongoing.

Q4 2026 results have not yet been filed in a periodic report, so they are the next checkpoint for the annual trend. The 2026 annual business report will show how segment and regional sales are presented, which will indicate the actual weight of glass substrates and overseas operations.

Bull factors

Glass substrates and TGV growth

The company developed its TGV plating technology in-house, completed sample supply and is working on mass production with JWMT and other partners, according to a research firm. Plating capacity is planned to rise from about 300 sheets to 1,200–1,500 sheets a month. A new partner plant could expand chemical and plating demand as well, which underpins the growth case.

Higher-value chemicals and cost improvement

An independent research firm noted that supply to VGT began in the second quarter of 2025, raising the share of higher-priced products. In the first half of 2025, the cost of sales ratio fell from 81.3% to 75.0%. Cost structure improvement was one pillar of the operating margin recovery, a positive factor.

Improved financial stability

The debt-to-equity ratio at the end of 2025 was 71.2%, down from 78.3% in 2024. Operating cash flow rose to KRW 17.5 billion in 2025 from KRW 7.03 billion in 2024, and the operating margin rose to 8.9% from 2.6%. Equity attributable to controlling shareholders at the end of 2025 was KRW 189.2 billion.

Bear factors

Slowing quarterly profit

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.72 billion fell 46.4% year on year, and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.1 billion. Losses have continued for two consecutive quarters since Q2 2026, totaling KRW 6 billion over the last four quarters. Revenue rose to KRW 41.7 billion without carrying through to profit.

Dilution from warrant exercise

On April 15, 2026, 620,237 common shares were issued on exercise of the fifth-round warrants, equal to 3.51% of total shares outstanding before the exercise. Further exercise of remaining convertible and warrant bonds could add shares and dilute per-share measures.

Mass-production transition and earnings durability

In August 2025, the company said quality and process requirement talks with a customer on moving TGV glass substrates to mass production were ongoing. If those talks slip, the timing of glass-substrate contributions to earnings would also slip.

Four consecutive years of net losses attributable to controlling shareholders from 2022 through 2025 also weigh on earnings durability.

Risk factors

Customer and cycle concentration

Revenue is tied to PCB and semiconductor end-demand. New businesses proceed in consortia with key customers, so any slip in a customer's mass-production schedule delays revenue. Dependence on specific customers should be checked in the customer breakdown of periodic reports.

Capital structure and dilution

Exercise of warrants in April 2026 issued new shares equal to 3.51% of total shares outstanding. Exercise of remaining convertible and warrant bonds could keep diluting per-share earnings. The burden of borrowing and bonds can shift with interest rates and share price conditions.

Overseas operations and currency

The Guangdong plant in China was described as nearing completion around 2025. The company's goal of China-related sales in the KRW 40 billion range requires overseas production and sales to expand as planned. Overseas operations are exposed to currency swings and local regulatory changes.

12

Overall view

YMT's annual results improved sharply in 2025, with revenue of KRW 144.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 12.9 billion, yet the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.68 billion extended a run of four consecutive years of losses from 2022.

After a profit in Q1 2026, losses returned in Q2 and Q3, leaving a net loss of KRW 6 billion over the last four quarters.

Bullish factors include expanding glass-substrate plating capacity, TGV sample supply, customer expansion for higher-value chemicals such as ENEPIG, and the financial improvement shown by a debt-to-equity ratio of 71.2% and operating cash flow of KRW 17.5 billion in 2025.

Bearish factors include a 46.4% year-on-year drop in Q3 2026 operating profit, dilution from warrant exercise, and the fact that glass-substrate supply is still in customer approval and mass-production transition talks.

The price-to-book ratio is below 1x, but the company's losses mean the price-to-earnings ratio is not calculated.

The next checkpoints are the provisional Q4 2026 results, the operating margin and net income attributable to controlling shareholders in the 2026 annual report, and the timing of additional glass-substrate capacity.
