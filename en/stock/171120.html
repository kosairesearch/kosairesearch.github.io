Lion Chemtech: Earnings Strength Tested After Q3 2026 Net Loss

02

Business structure

Lion Chemtech makes artificial marble used as interior and exterior building material and synthetic wax added to plastics, paints and adhesives.

Artificial marble accounted for 75% to 80% of sales in 2022, but according to an Economic Times report it fell to 65.3% in the first half of 2026, while synthetic wax rose to 31.6%. Synthetic wax production is handled by a wholly owned subsidiary.

The artificial marble business is expanding sales in Europe with exterior-grade, wide-format and patterned products, and the company has also pursued engineered stone production facilities as a growth strategy.

Synthetic wax applications have widened beyond plastic processing lubricants to paints, inks, hot-melt adhesives and wire compounds. The company is also developing FDA-approved functional waxes and low-viscosity polyolefin copolymers.

Together with SK Innovation's chemicals unit, it reportedly became the second in the world to develop a hot-melt POE eco-friendly adhesive material. According to the same report, annual capacity in 2026 stands at 1,215,720 artificial marble sheets and 21,276 tonnes of synthetic wax. In 2021, Heungkuk Securities described the company as the leading domestic synthetic wax producer.

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 152 billion in 2022 to KRW 130.8 billion in 2023 (down 14.0% year on year), recovered to KRW 141.8 billion in 2024 (up 8.4%), and then declined to KRW 130.3 billion in 2025 (down 8.1%). Revenue was largest in 2022 and smallest in 2025 across the 2022-2025 span.

Operating profit fell from KRW 11 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.79 billion in 2023 (down 29.1%), then rose to KRW 9.38 billion in 2024 (up 20.3%) and KRW 11.4 billion in 2025 (up 21.3%), growing for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025. The 2025 operating profit was the largest in the 2022-2025 span.

The operating margin moved from 7.2% in 2022 to 6.0% in 2023, 6.6% in 2024 and 8.7% in 2025, peaking in the year revenue declined.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 8.85 billion in 2022, KRW 7.08 billion in 2023 (down 19.9%), KRW 8.82 billion in 2024 (up 24.6%) and KRW 8.5 billion in 2025 (down 3.7%).

In 2025, operating profit rose while profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell, so the two measures moved in opposite directions. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 18.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.7 billion in 2023 and KRW 6.06 billion in 2024, then jumped to KRW 27.1 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 36.6 billion in Q3 2025 with operating profit of KRW 4.78 billion, then fell in Q4 2025 to KRW 30.3 billion (down 17.2% from the prior quarter) with operating profit of KRW 3.53 billion (down 26.2%).

Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 42 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.19 billion were the highest in the last five quarters.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 36.3 billion was roughly flat year on year (down 0.7%), but operating profit fell to KRW 2.84 billion (down 45.3% from the prior quarter and 40.7% year on year), and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 98.17 million.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 11.4 billion. Quarterly disclosures alone do not separate one-off items from the drivers of the Q3 operating profit decline, so the notes and management commentary in the annual report need to be read alongside them.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for interior and exterior building materials moves with redevelopment, remodeling and the domestic construction cycle. In 2021, Heungkuk Securities said rising redevelopment and remodeling demand created conditions for a domestic construction recovery.

The same analysis said engineered stone, a premium artificial marble, was seeing demand growth from mid-sized and smaller builders as well as large construction firms, citing its natural-marble-like design and strength.

The report identified raw material prices, notably MMA, and exchange rates as key drivers of operating margin swings, and said margin volatility had eased in 2021 as raw material supply stabilized.

Synthetic wax functions as a performance additive across plastics, fibers, rubber, paints and adhesives, so its demand depends more on manufacturing utilization than on the construction cycle.

On a peer basis, Alphasquare's annual ranking of 21 building products companies places the company first in operating margin, 10th in market capitalization, 12th in revenue and fourth in dividend yield.

These peer comparisons and the sector analysis largely rest on 2021 commentary or annual aggregates, so the latest 2026 construction orders and raw material price trends need separate confirmation.

06

Outlook

The company's growth plans fall into three areas: capacity expansion, sales growth in Europe and new product launches. According to an Economic Times report, 2026 capacity stands at 1,215,720 artificial marble sheets and 21,276 tonnes of synthetic wax, and capacity expansion is ongoing.

Alphasquare describes the strategy as including the build-out of engineered stone production facilities, higher sales volumes of wide-format artificial marble, and revenue growth in the European market.

ValueLine says the company is developing dispersity-controlled waxes to enter paint and ink markets and has secured new markets such as hot-melt additives through FDA approval.

A 2021 Heungkuk Securities analysis also pointed to expanded domestic and overseas sales of the hot-melt POE material developed with SK Innovation's chemicals unit, and to added production facilities. In the near term, the 40.7% year-on-year decline in Q3 2026 operating profit is the key variable.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary 2026 fourth-quarter and full-year results, expected around January to February 2027 on a typical schedule, and the 2026 annual report.

Timing and investment size for the new facilities should be tracked through disclosures, along with whether the rising synthetic wax share translates into margin improvement.

08

Bull factors

Operating margin improvement

The operating margin rose from 7.2% in 2022 to 8.7% in 2025, the highest level in the period. Operating profit grew in both 2024 and 2025 after bottoming in 2023. Operating profit rose 21.3% in 2025 even as revenue fell 8.1%, which points to possible gains in cost and product mix. Alphasquare's annual data ranks the company first among building products peers on operating margin.

Wider synthetic wax mix and diversification

Synthetic wax's share of sales rose from 2022 to the first half of 2026, and its applications widened to paints, inks, hot-melt adhesives and wire compounds. An Economic Times report described synthetic wax as a support that spreads cyclical risk. Bulls point to wax sales as a potential buffer when artificial marble demand weakens.

Financial capacity and shareholder returns

The debt-to-equity ratio was 20.3% at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 28.2 billion and total equity of KRW 138.9 billion. Operating cash flow rose to KRW 27.1 billion in 2025 from KRW 6.06 billion in 2024.

At its annual general meeting on March 23, 2026, the company approved the 44th-term financial statements and a cash dividend.

Bear factors

Q3 2026 profit slump and return to loss

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 36.3 billion was down only 0.7% year on year, but operating profit fell 40.7% to KRW 2.84 billion. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 98.17 million. Operating profit also fell 45.3% from the prior quarter, the lowest of the last five quarters.

Shrinking revenue and mix shift in the core business

Revenue declined from KRW 152 billion in 2022 to KRW 130.3 billion in 2025, falling in both 2023 and 2025. Artificial marble's share of sales dropped from 75% to 80% in 2022 to 65.3% in the first half of 2026. The growth gap between the two businesses is now a key variable for the earnings direction.

Governance uncertainty

Press reports say two attempts to sell the controlling stake have failed. An industry source said the failure left many matters to address. Reports also said new business plans were shelved and the directors appointed for them stepped down.

10

Risk factors

Raw materials and FX

Heungkuk Securities's 2021 analysis cited MMA and other raw material prices and exchange rates as drivers of operating margin swings. Setting the 8.7% operating margin in 2025 beside the sharp Q3 2026 profit decline shows margin stability is still an open issue. How quickly raw material costs pass through to selling prices shapes the size of quarterly profit swings.

Dependence on Europe

A 2020 report said 40% of total export volume originated in Europe. Changes in European construction demand or logistics conditions would directly affect artificial marble sales.

Because the company has made European expansion a core strategy, the current share of European sales should be checked against the latest disclosures.

Capacity expansion execution

The start-up timing and investment size of the engineered stone production facilities and the synthetic wax capacity expansion are still undetermined. If the facilities start later than planned or fail to translate into sales, the investment burden could weigh on the operating margin.

Whether a larger synthetic wax share leads to margin improvement also depends on whether the expansion's effects are reflected in actual sales.

Overall view

Lion Chemtech's revenue declined from KRW 152 billion in 2022 to KRW 130.3 billion in 2025, but operating profit grew for two consecutive years from a KRW 7.79 billion low in 2023 to KRW 11.4 billion in 2025, and the operating margin rose to 8.7%.

The business mix is shifting from artificial marble toward synthetic wax, which an Economic Times report credited with spreading cyclical risk.

In Q3 2026, however, revenue was down only 0.7% year on year while operating profit fell 40.7%, and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 98.17 million.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 11.4 billion, but quarterly earnings volatility has clearly increased. On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was 20.3% at the end of 2025 and operating cash flow improved to KRW 27.1 billion.

On governance, two attempts to sell the controlling stake have failed, which keeps possible changes in management direction in view. Strengths are the margin recovery and financial capacity, while weaknesses are the Q3 profit slump, the revenue decline trend and governance uncertainty. The next checkpoints are the preliminary 2026 fourth-quarter and full-year results and the 2026 annual report.
