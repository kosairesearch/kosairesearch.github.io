02

Business structure

LEENO Industrial makes the test pins and sockets that connect chips to inspection equipment during electrical testing.

Its 2025 revenue by segment was KRW 243.9 billion from IC test sockets, KRW 87.1 billion from LEENO PIN products, KRW 38.1 billion from medical device components and KRW 3.4 billion from other items, figures taken from a May 2026 iM Securities report that tabulated 2025 results.

The four segments add up to the disclosed 2025 revenue of KRW 372.5 billion. Sockets are split into pogo-pin and silicone rubber types, and the company supplies pogo sockets, which suit low-volume production with many product variations.

The company once relied on imports for inspection probes and IC test sockets and now develops and sells them under its own brand. Customer mix is weighted to smartphone chips: Meritz Securities said in May 2026 that more than 60% of revenue comes from semiconductors installed in smartphones, such as AP and modem chips.

Korea Investment & Securities said in April 2026 that R&D-use sockets account for 60% of total revenue, so the two brokers use different bases. iM Securities said R&D-use and mass-production sockets were split 6 to 4 in first-half 2026 shipments, with R&D use larger. In a 2025 article, NICE Information Service rated the company top-tier in profitability within its industry.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue on a disclosed basis went from KRW 322.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 255.6 billion in 2023 (down 20.7% year on year), then rose to KRW 278.2 billion in 2024 (up 8.8%) and KRW 372.5 billion in 2025 (up 33.9%), with growth in both 2024 and 2025 after the 2023 low. 2025 was the largest revenue year in the 2022-2025 span.

Operating profit went from KRW 136.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 114.4 billion in 2023 (down 16.3%), KRW 124.2 billion in 2024 (up 8.6%) and KRW 177 billion in 2025 (up 42.5%). The operating margin moved from 42.4% in 2022 to 44.8% in 2023, 44.6% in 2024 and 47.5% in 2025, the highest of the four years.

In 2025 operating profit grew faster than revenue, reflecting margin gains. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 114.4 billion in 2022, KRW 110.9 billion in 2023 (down 3.0%), KRW 113.3 billion in 2024 (up 2.1%) and KRW 152 billion in 2025 (up 34.1%).

Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed at the same amounts in each year. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 731.2 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 60.7 billion, so the debt ratio rose from 4.6% in 2023 to 8.3% in 2025 but remains low.

Operating cash flow increased from KRW 110.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 179.5 billion in 2025.

By quarter, revenue fell from KRW 96.8 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 84.8 billion in Q4 2025 (down 12.5% from the prior quarter), recovered to KRW 99.8 billion in Q1 2026 (up 17.7%) and rose to KRW 143.2 billion in Q2 2026 (up 43.5%).

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 82.7 billion (down 42.2% from the prior quarter and down 14.6% year on year), operating profit KRW 39.4 billion (down 18.3% year on year) and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 22.6 billion (down 46.0% year on year).

Meritz Securities cited the high base effect from concentrated high-value mass-production volume at a North American mobile customer in Q2, plus strike-related delays in pins and test sockets, as reasons for the Q3 revenue decline (October 2026).

The sum of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, is KRW 168.5 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Test socket demand is tied to changes in semiconductor packaging.

DS Investment said in a January 2026 report that a shift from InFO to WMCM packaging at North American customers would raise pin counts and signal density, lifting socket prices and replacement demand. iM Securities wrote in October 2026 that WMCM-based mass production for North American smartphone AP chips is ramping up, and that other major AP customers are considering similar packaging.

The adoption of 2-nanometer processes is also cited as a demand driver: Hana Securities said in a 2025 report that broader 2-nanometer adoption would likely lift total test pin demand through WMCM. iM Securities also listed on-device AI devices and big tech in-house AI chip development as drivers.

Against this, Meritz Securities said in May 2026 that rising memory prices had lowered full-year smartphone demand forecasts. Meritz also noted that the fourth quarter is a seasonally slow period for mobile volumes (October 2026).

On competitive position, Shinhan Investment Corp. said in a 2025 report that the company had secured nearly all major AP makers as customers. Meritz Securities said CPO chip testing requires fine pitch and high reliability, making it a product line where the company's technology stands out (October 2026).

Outlook

In an October 2026 report, DS Investment said the company plans to raise Q4 production and shipments to achieve its existing full-year guidance as far as possible. Whether that guidance holds is cited as a gauge of how fast output recovers after the strike.

The figures below are broker estimates, not disclosed results. iM Securities estimated 2026 revenue at KRW 428.8 billion and operating profit at KRW 208.3 billion on a standalone basis, and 2027 revenue at KRW 505.5 billion and operating profit at KRW 245.6 billion (October 2026).

Meritz Securities estimated 2026 operating profit at KRW 209 billion (48.2% margin) rising to KRW 249.3 billion in 2027 (49.8% margin) (October 2026).

On capacity, DS Investment said in an early 2026 report that capacity from the new plant relocation would be phased in from October to November 2026, with annual capacity expanding toward KRW 900 billion by 2027.

For growth options, DS Investment named CPO test sockets, which it expects to become significant after 2027 as a key variable, and Meritz Securities expected the company to keep a high share in CPO mass production. iM Securities said an in-house AI chip from a big tech customer could provide a growth base once it enters full mass production in 2027.

Meritz Securities expected demand for high-value test sockets to stay strong in 2027. The risks cited include concerns in parts of the market about new orders for 2027 and continuing labor negotiations.

Bull factors

Socket demand shifts with WMCM packaging

DS Investment said in January 2026 that a shift to WMCM packaging at North American customers would raise pin counts and signal density, lifting socket prices and replacement demand. iM Securities wrote in October 2026 that WMCM-based mass production for North American smartphone AP chips is ramping up. Packaging changes are cited as the basis for higher per-socket prices and volumes.

New applications beyond 2026

iM Securities said an in-house AI chip from a big tech customer could become a growth base once it enters full mass production in 2027 (October 2026).

Meritz Securities expected CPO test sockets to expand the non-mobile socket mix after 2027 and to keep a high share in mass production (October 2026). iM Securities estimated 2027 operating profit at KRW 245.6 billion, a forecast rather than a disclosed figure.

Profit base and balance sheet

Operating margin rose from 42.4% in 2022 to 47.5% in 2025, the highest of the four years. Operating cash flow reached KRW 179.5 billion in 2025, up from KRW 110.1 billion in 2024. Total liabilities stood at KRW 60.7 billion at the end of 2025, and the 8.3% debt ratio is low. Production normalization after the strike is also cited as a positive.

Bear factors

Q3 strike shock and earnings volatility

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 82.7 billion fell 42.2% from the previous quarter and 14.6% from a year earlier. Operating profit of KRW 39.4 billion was down 18.3% year on year, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 22.6 billion fell 46.0%.

DS Investment named whether the existing annual guidance can be met as the key variable after the strike (October 2026).

Smartphone demand and customer concentration

Meritz Securities said in May 2026 that more than 60% of revenue comes from semiconductors used in smartphones. The same report noted that rising memory prices had pushed down full-year smartphone demand forecasts. Results also depend heavily on packaging changes and volumes at North American mobile customers.

2027 order concerns and labor talks

Meritz Securities said some market participants raise concerns about new orders for 2027 (October 2026). The same report said further labor negotiations continue, leaving residual risk. Although broker outlooks are largely positive, these two issues weigh on earnings visibility.

10

Risk factors

Labor and production disruption

The July 2026 full strike disrupted production and shipments, and it ended on September 23. Further labor negotiations continue, so a renewed stoppage could affect quarterly results and the annual guidance. The strike delayed production and delivery of pins and test sockets, hitting both revenue and profit.

Customer packaging and smartphone demand

If WMCM adoption and 2-nanometer process timing slip, the rise in test pin volumes and unit prices could be delayed as well. Smartphone demand is being revised down on higher memory prices. Because mobile makes up a large share, a product cycle change at a single customer feeds directly into results.

Timing of new businesses

The timing and scale of CPO test socket contributions remain to be confirmed. Development, customer qualification, product adoption and volume expansion must come before sales are booked. In-house AI chip volume in 2027 also depends on customer schedules, so any delay would affect the 2027 estimates.

Overall view

On a disclosed basis, LEENO Industrial raised its operating margin from 42.4% in 2022 to 47.5% in 2025, and 2025 revenue of KRW 372.5 billion was the largest in the 2022-2025 span. Q2 2026 revenue reached KRW 143.2 billion, but Q3 2026 fell to KRW 82.7 billion as the strike hit output.

Supporting the bull case are the shift to WMCM packaging, demand for high-value test sockets, and growth options from in-house AI chips and CPO sockets after 2027. Against it are reliance on smartphones, demand cuts tied to memory prices, concern about new 2027 orders, and residual labor risk.

Broker estimates project operating profit growth for 2026 and 2027, but these are not disclosed results. Broker target prices differ across firms and reflect the views of those firms.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual report, and the 2027 timing of capacity additions and product mass production.
