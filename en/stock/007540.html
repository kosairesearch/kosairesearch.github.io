Sempio: Profit Slowdown in 2026 Amid Holding Structure Overhaul

Sempio's operating profit recovered sharply in 2025, but quarterly profit slowed again through the third quarter of 2026, and the holding company structure is being reorganized through a merger with Sempio Foods Company.

  1. 1

    Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 24.1 billion, up 310.9% from the prior year, and the 5.9% operating margin was the highest in the 2022–2025 period.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Sempio Company is moving to merge with its core subsidiary Sempio Foods Company, with the exchange ratio set at 1 to 2.0881235.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Sempio is a holding company divided into a holding business segment and a food business segment. The food segment is centered on its subsidiary Sempio Foods Company, together with Yangpo Food, Sempio ISP and Jochiwon Food.

Holding segment revenue in 2024 was KRW 7.09 billion, of which equity-method income accounted for 67.9%, brand income 18.7%, rental income 10.7% and dividend income 2.7%.

The rental income share rose from 1.8% in 2023 to 10.7% in 2024, and the business report attributes this to rental income from the Changdong property. Most holding revenue therefore reflects subsidiary results carried through the equity method.

Within Sempio Foods, the 2025 gross product sales of KRW 455.1 billion were split between jang at KRW 233.3 billion (51.3%) and non-jang products at KRW 221.7 billion (48.7%). Jang covers soy sauce, doenjang, gochujang and ssamjang, while non-jang products include brands such as Yeondu, Fontana, Tiasia and Chaochai.

Overseas sales of Sempio Foods rose from KRW 50.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 66.1 billion in 2025, equal to 14.5% of its sales. In competition, Sempio Foods faces CJ CheilJedang and other makers in soy sauce and sauce and seasoning categories. R&D spending in 2025 was KRW 14.6 billion, up 7.3% year on year, or roughly 5% of sales.

04

Earnings analysis

On the basis of disclosed results, annual revenue rose from KRW 371.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 383.9 billion in 2023 (up 3.3%), KRW 405 billion in 2024 (up 5.5%) and KRW 409 billion in 2025 (up 1.0%), marking three straight years of growth from 2023 through 2025.

Revenue grew gradually, but profitability slipped in 2023 and 2024. Operating profit moved from KRW 10.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.13 billion in 2023 (down 21.3%) and KRW 5.88 billion in 2024 (down 27.7%) before jumping to KRW 24.1 billion in 2025 (up 310.9%).

The operating margin rose from 2.8%, 2.1% and 1.5% to 5.9% in 2025, and one report said the company credited stable raw material prices and lower promotional spending.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent fell from KRW 10.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.08 billion in 2023 (down 41.7%), then rose to KRW 6.57 billion in 2024 (up 8.0%) and KRW 9.74 billion in 2025 (up 48.3%), two consecutive years of growth.

Net income including non-controlling interests reached KRW 18.8 billion in 2025, a different measure from profit attributable to owners of the parent. Operating cash flow expanded from KRW 18.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 61.8 billion in 2025, and the debt ratio fell from 42.4% to 35.8%.

The quarterly pattern changed direction in 2026.

Operating profit went from KRW 12.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.71 billion in the fourth quarter, recovered to KRW 6.56 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then came in at KRW 4.4 billion in the second quarter and KRW 6.11 billion in the third quarter, down 52.3% year on year.

In the second quarter of 2026, operating profit at subsidiary Sempio Foods fell 36.6% to KRW 4.7 billion, and the company attributed this to higher promotional spending tied to brand and product marketing.

Third-quarter 2026 profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 808 million, down 77.9% year on year, and the last four quarters total KRW 6.41 billion.

Nine-month operating profit in the third-quarter filing was KRW 17.1 billion, down 20.3%, and one outlet attributed the margin decline to higher costs and global economic uncertainty.

05

Industry analysis

In the food sector Sempio operates in, jang and seasoning categories depend on household cooking demand. Competition is split between soy sauce and sauce and seasoning categories, where CJ CheilJedang is cited as a main rival.

The spread of dining-out culture and rising demand for ready-to-eat meals, meal kits and seasoned soy sauce are cited as reasons for the greater weight of non-jang products. Sempio Foods has moved beyond a jang-centered structure, with non-jang products reaching 48.7% of gross product sales.

However, overseas sales account for only 14.5%, leaving a high dependence on domestic consumption. Higher raw material costs are the factor one outlet linked to the margin decline in the third quarter of 2026, and whether this cost pressure spreads across the sector will be visible in later quarterly results.

Among listed holding companies, CJ, LG and SK are peers, and Sempio's structure stands out because subsidiary food results feed into holding income largely through equity-method income. The company reports about 3,000 microbial strains and more than 90 fermentation-related patents.

06

Outlook

The nearest structural change is the merger with Sempio Foods Company. The exchange ratio was set at 1 to 2.0881235 between Sempio Foods and Sempio Company, and once the merger is completed, CEO Park Jin-sun is scheduled to become the largest shareholder of Sempio Foods.

A news headline tied the holding company breakup to securing funds for factory investment. Another headline indicated that a correction order from financial regulators makes supplementary disclosure unavoidable.

How the merger process and the disclosure revisions are completed will determine when the holding company structure disappears. On the business side, one outlet described overseas operations as a new growth engine but noted the task of raising the 14.5% overseas share.

Diversification into non-jang brands continues, with Tiasia and Chaochai aimed at demand for Asian food culture. Because higher promotional spending in the second quarter of 2026 weighed on profit, how marketing costs are reflected in later quarterly operating margins is the key point.

Raw material price trends are another main variable for margin changes. The next checkpoints are the merger terms confirmed in the process and the annual operating margin in the 2026 business report.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in operating margin

Operating profit rose from KRW 5.88 billion in 2024 to KRW 24.1 billion in 2025, and the operating margin climbed from 1.5% to 5.9%. Within 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the year with the largest operating profit. Reports said the company pointed to stable raw material prices and lower promotional spending as drivers. Whether the margin recovery holds in the 2026 quarterly results is the key checkpoint for the bullish case.

Growth in overseas and non-jang products

Overseas sales at Sempio Foods rose from KRW 50.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 66.1 billion in 2025, and their share of sales increased from 12.1% to 14.5%. Non-jang products made up 48.7% of Sempio Foods' 2025 gross product sales, with brands such as Yeondu and Fontana broadening the sales base. R&D spending in 2025 rose 7.3% year on year to KRW 14.6 billion.

Financial stability and cash generation

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 35.8%, lower than 42.4% in 2022. Operating cash flow grew from KRW 18.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 61.8 billion in 2025, showing stronger cash generation. A headline on the merger tied the breakup of the holding company to securing funds for factory investment.

Bear factors

Slowing quarterly profit in 2026

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 52.3% year on year to KRW 6.11 billion, and profit attributable to owners of the parent fell 77.9% to KRW 808 million. Nine-month operating profit fell 20.3% to KRW 17.1 billion. A high base of KRW 12.8 billion in third-quarter 2025 operating profit also widened the decline.

Cost burden and higher input costs

Operating profit at subsidiary Sempio Foods fell 36.6% in the second quarter of 2026 because promotional spending increased. One outlet cited higher costs and global economic uncertainty as causes of the third-quarter margin decline. Third-quarter revenue rose 1.4% to KRW 110.2 billion, but higher costs offset the gain.

Stalled overseas share and domestic reliance

The overseas share of Sempio Foods' sales remains at 14.5%. An outlet pointed to heavy domestic dependence and to expanding local distribution and securing profitability as tasks. Even with overseas growth, the contribution to total sales remains small.

Whether the 5.9% operating margin of 2025 holds in 2026 quarterly results depends on these cost variables.

Dependence on equity-method income

Equity-method income accounted for 67.9% of holding segment revenue in 2024, so subsidiary results pass straight through to holding company earnings. Overseas sales reached KRW 66.1 billion in 2025, but their 14.5% share keeps heavy reliance on domestic consumption. Whether overseas growth translates into margin improvement will become visible in upcoming results.

Overall view

Sempio's revenue rose for three straight years from 2023 through 2025, and in 2025 operating profit recovered sharply to KRW 24.1 billion, lifting the operating margin to 5.9%. However, third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 52.3% to KRW 6.11 billion, and the nine-month total declined 20.3%.

Raw material prices and promotional spending have emerged as the main drivers of quarterly profit swings. The business combines a food segment centered on Sempio Foods with a holding segment that relies on equity-method income, and the non-jang share rose to 48.7%.

Overseas sales grew to KRW 66.1 billion in 2025, but their share remains 14.5%. Once the merger with Sempio Foods is completed, the holding company structure will disappear and the basis for financial analysis will change.

Strengths include financial stability and the margin recovery, while weaknesses include the slowdown in quarterly profit in 2026 and cost pressure. The next checkpoints are the annual operating margin in the 2026 business report and the disclosure details confirmed through the merger process.
