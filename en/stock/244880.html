02

Business structure

Nanoomtech is a medical device manufacturer founded on August 31, 2005, headquartered in Buk-gu, Gwangju. It is a specialist AED maker that listed on the KONEX market in 2016.

Market data place the company in electrical equipment, but the exchange's listing-stage materials described it as a medical device maker producing AEDs and bone densitometers, among other products.

It succeeded in domesticating AED production through joint research with the Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology in 2009. AEDs accounted for 87.0% of revenue in 2017, 87.5% in 2018 and 91.2% in 2019. The same report stated that more than 86% of sales over the prior three years came from the domestic market.

A government SME information page lists the main products as defibrillators, cervical massagers and bone densitometers, among others. As a leading domestic player, it recorded a 34% public procurement market share in 2024 and holds 38 patents. Competitors include domestic AED makers such as Mediana.

Mediana said, by its own account, that 80% of its revenue comes from exports. By comparison, Nanoomtech appears to rely more heavily on public procurement and the domestic market.

03

Recent trends

Nanoomtech held its annual general meeting on March 31, 2026, and approved the financial statements for the 21st fiscal term. Reported standalone results for 2025 were revenue of KRW 16,349 million, operating profit of KRW 3,338 million and net profit of KRW 3,258 million.

By FnGuide's figures, standalone revenue in 2024 was KRW 161 billion on a 100-million-won basis, i.e., KRW 16.1 billion, with operating profit of KRW 2.8 billion and net profit of KRW 3.0 billion. In 2024, revenue fell 8.2% and operating profit fell 28.6% from the prior year.

In comparison, 2025 revenue rose slightly and operating profit exceeded the 2024 level. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 26,635 million and total liabilities were KRW 2,300 million. The dividend per share for 2024 was KRW 100.

The March 31, 2026 shareholder meeting agenda included a cash dividend and amendments to the articles of incorporation. First-half 2026 results and the size of the latest dividend could not be confirmed through search. As of October 27, 2026, the share price was KRW 1,510, down 10.65% from the previous session.

The market capitalization of KRW 7.6 billion is smaller than total equity of KRW 26,635 million at the end of 2025. No disclosure or news report explaining the decline was found, and very low trading value means small orders can move the price sharply.

04

Outlook

One assessment notes that the AED market showed an overall decline in sales despite the enforcement of the Serious Accidents Punishment Act and rising corporate demand. This suggests that while regulation supports demand, pricing competition and public budgets still shape results.

A new low-power AED applying an AI-based ECG analysis algorithm is cited as a factor for improved earnings. Its actual sales contribution must be checked in disclosures after the first half of 2026. The operating margin declined from 25.17% in 2022 to 22.51% in 2023 and 17.53% in 2024.

That makes the operating margin a business metric with room for improvement. Overseas expansion requires certifications first, and changing a structure heavily reliant on the domestic market would take time.

In the broader industry, smart cabinets that remotely manage AED status were registered on the Public Procurement Service's innovation marketplace, indicating that the related ecosystem is expanding. Whether this trend translates into Nanoomtech sales is a separate point to verify.

05

Bull factors

Procurement market leadership and patent base

Nanoomtech is a leading domestic AED maker, with a 34% procurement market share in 2024. It holds 38 patents and has 2 applications pending. An exchange-sponsored report said the company kept a high-margin earnings structure on the back of a price advantage rooted in its intellectual property. A stable base of public procurement demand is a strength of this structure.

Low-leverage balance sheet

Total liabilities were KRW 2,300 million against total assets of KRW 28,935 million at the end of 2025. By FnGuide's figures, the debt ratio was 8.08% in 2024. The small borrowing burden limits exposure to interest rate changes. The company also has a record of returning profit through dividends. The dividend per share was KRW 50 in 2023 and KRW 100 in 2024.

AI-based new product and demand backdrop

A new low-power AED applying an AI-based ECG analysis algorithm is cited as a factor for improved earnings. Enforcement of the Serious Accidents Punishment Act has raised corporate demand for AEDs. If the new product gains traction in procurement channels, it could improve the sales mix. Its actual contribution still has to be confirmed through later disclosures.

06

Bear factors

Single-product and domestic dependence

AEDs accounted for 91.2% of revenue in 2019. The domestic market made up more than 86% of sales over the prior three years. With revenue concentrated in one product and one market, changes in public procurement volumes or pricing policy can swing earnings widely. Peer Mediana has a high export share and therefore a different business structure.

Margin erosion and a stalled market

Operating profit fell from KRW 4.0 billion in 2023 to KRW 2.8 billion in 2024. Over the same period, the operating margin declined from 22.51% to 17.53%. One assessment says the AED market showed an overall decline in sales despite demand-supporting factors. Even though operating profit rebounded in 2025, it has not been confirmed that this trend will continue.

Limited disclosure and liquidity

KONEX stocks receive less brokerage coverage and media attention than KOSPI or KOSDAQ names. This research also failed to find first-half 2026 results or recent business news. On October 27, 2026, trading value was extremely low while the share price fell 10.65%. With a market capitalization of KRW 7.6 billion, even small orders can move the price sharply.

Risk factors

Macro

Public procurement and government and local authority budgets are major variables for the company's sales. In 2020, COVID-19 cut related ministry budgets, shrinking procurement orders and reducing first-half sales by 25.7% year over year. A shift in fiscal spending could cause a similar shock again. Fluctuations in raw material and component prices also affect costs.

Industry

Competitors such as Mediana operate in the domestic AED market, and pricing competition is strong in public procurement. Public sales channels depend on performance certification and designation of products as excellent procurement items.

Medical device regulation and overseas certification procedures can hinder export growth. Because the sector label shows electrical equipment, applying comparison metrics from electrical equipment peers directly could be misleading.

Market & Liquidity

The KONEX market has a limited set of participants, so gaps in the order book form easily. On October 27, 2026, the share price was down 10.65% from the previous session on extremely low trading value. Even small sell or buy volumes can have a large price impact. No structural events such as a market transfer were found.

08

Overall view

Nanoomtech is a medical device maker specializing in AEDs; although market data classify it under electrical equipment, the nature of its business is closer to medical devices.

Reported standalone results for 2025 were revenue of KRW 16,349 million, operating profit of KRW 3,338 million and net profit of KRW 3,258 million. Its leading position in the procurement market, its patents and its low debt burden are cited as business strengths.

On the other side, dependence on a single product and the domestic market, the profitability decline recorded in 2024 and a stalled market backdrop are burdens. First-half 2026 results and the sales performance of the new product could not be confirmed in this research, and later disclosures are the key items to check.

The market capitalization of KRW 7.6 billion and very thin trading also imply high price volatility. This report is a qualitative summary for information only, and investment decisions are left to the reader.
