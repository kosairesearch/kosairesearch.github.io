02

Business structure

Sebang, founded in 1965 and listed on the main board in 1977, is a port handling and cargo transport-centered integrated logistics company.

As of end-2025, the revenue mix was cargo transport at 61.24%, port handling at 22.85%, CY/CFS and related services at 12.70%, and leasing and other businesses at 3.21% (FnGuide compilation).

The cargo transport segment covers domestic container, bulk and heavy cargo transport, while the port handling segment handles loading and discharging of import and export cargo. The CY/CFS segment handles container handling and storage, linking the stages of logistics inside and around ports.

Leasing and other businesses account for just over 3% of revenue, so earnings depend largely on the volume and unit rates of transport and handling. In port handling, SUN Kwang is the direct comparison as a specialist operator.

Broadening to domestic land transport and integrated logistics, CJ Logistics and HANJIN Logistics form the competitive set. Hyundai Glovis, centered on finished vehicle logistics, is an indirect comparison in integrated logistics.

Sebang's differentiation lies in its one-stop capability spanning port handling, transport and storage. However, it trails large integrated logistics companies in revenue scale and network reach, so its exposure to specific ports and changes in import and export volumes is relatively large.

The 2025 operating margin of 2.2% was lower than 3.5% in 2022, so the structure leaves thin margins even when volumes grow.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results over 2022-2025 show profit direction changing more often than sales. Sales went from KRW 1.29 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.14 trillion in 2023 (down 11.5% year on year), recovered to KRW 1.37 trillion in 2024 (up 20.3%), and fell again to KRW 1.24 trillion in 2025 (down 9.6%).

Across 2022-2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest. Operating income fell from KRW 45.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 28.7 billion in 2023 (down 36.5%) and KRW 24.5 billion in 2024 (down 14.5%), making 2024 the smallest year. Operating income rebounded to KRW 27.3 billion in 2025, up 11.5%.

The operating margin was 3.5% in 2022, 2.5% in 2023, 1.8% in 2024 and 2.2% in 2025, a slight recovery in 2025 but still below 2022.

Parent net income was KRW 105.4 billion in 2022, KRW 69.8 billion in 2023 (down 33.8%), KRW 85.5 billion in 2024 (up 22.5%) and KRW 65.7 billion in 2025 (down 23.2%), with 2022 the largest year.

In 2025 operating income rose while parent net income fell 23.2%, suggesting that items outside operations drove the direction of earnings. Quarterly figures show a similar pattern.

Sales hit a low of KRW 274.2 billion in 4Q 2025 (down 13.1% from the prior quarter), then rose to KRW 299.8 billion in 1Q 2026, KRW 307.3 billion in 2Q 2026 and KRW 347.3 billion in 3Q 2026, increasing for three consecutive quarters.

Operating income moved to a KRW 2.31 billion loss in 4Q 2025, returned to a KRW 6.88 billion profit in 1Q 2026, reached KRW 8.46 billion in 2Q 2026 (up 22.9% from the prior quarter), and was KRW 7.72 billion in 3Q 2026 (up 5.3% year on year).

However, parent net income in 3Q 2026 was a KRW 8.88 billion net loss, a swing into loss from a year earlier. Because operating income was positive in the same quarter, the effect of non-operating items and one-off factors should be checked in the quarterly report income statement.

Parent net income for the last four quarters, 4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026, totals KRW 41.6 billion. On financial stability, debt-to-equity fell from 30.5% in 2024 to 23.8% in 2025, and of total equity of KRW 1.21 trillion at end-2025, the parent's share was KRW 1.21 trillion.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 74.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 56.6 billion in 2023 and KRW 52.2 billion in 2024, then recovered to KRW 69.6 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Sebang's earnings are tied to import and export volumes and port throughput. Cargo transport, at 61.24% of end-2025 revenue, is the largest segment, so domestic container and bulk transport volumes and freight rates are the primary variables.

Port handling, at 22.85%, depends on import and export cargo throughput and vessel call schedules, while CY/CFS at 12.70% moves with container storage and handling volumes. Sales rose 10.1% year on year in 3Q 2026, but operating income rose only 5.3%, below the sales growth rate.

This means volume growth did not translate directly into margin expansion, so how the volume recovery plays out in freight rates and costs is the central question for the industry read.

In port handling, SUN Kwang is the comparison in the same segment, while CJ Logistics and HANJIN Logistics are compared on scale and network in land transport and integrated logistics.

Hyundai Glovis has a different business structure centered on finished vehicle logistics, and HMM serves as a shipping-side reference indicator for ocean cargo flows.

As a mid-sized integrated logistics company with both port handling and transport, Sebang has relatively large exposure to specific ports and import and export volumes. The position of the industry cycle is best gauged by reading port throughput and import and export volume indicators together.

Whether sales growth came from volume or from freight and unit price increases changes how the margin trend should be read, and this split should be confirmed in the segment discussion of the quarterly reports. Whether later disclosures explain the cause of the 3Q 2026 parent net loss is also an important checkpoint.

Operating profit held in the black

Operating income was KRW 6.88 billion in 1Q 2026, KRW 8.46 billion in 2Q and KRW 7.72 billion in 3Q, all in the black. This recovered from the KRW 2.31 billion operating loss in 4Q 2025. Low-leverage balance sheet

Operating cash flow rose to KRW 69.6 billion in 2025 from KRW 52.2 billion in 2024. A low debt burden can be read as capacity to absorb periods of volume swings.

Bear factors

Gap between operating and net profit

Parent net income in 3Q 2026 was a KRW 8.88 billion net loss, a swing into loss from a year earlier. Operating income in the same quarter was a KRW 7.72 billion profit. Non-operating items may have pulled net income down, but the breakdown needs to be confirmed in the quarterly report.

Multi-year decline in earnings

Parent net income declined from KRW 105.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 65.7 billion in 2025. The operating margin dropped from 3.5% in 2022 to 1.8% in 2024. Even though operating income rebounded in 2025, parent net income fell 23.2%, leaving earnings quality as an open issue.

Thin margin structure

Third-quarter 2026 sales grew 10.1% year on year, but operating income grew only 5.3%, diluting the margin. In a transport and handling structure with a high cost base, volume growth does not easily become profit growth.

Parent net income for the last four quarters totals KRW 41.6 billion, below the 2025 full-year figure of KRW 65.7 billion.

Risk factors

Industry and volume

If import and export volumes and domestic cargo transport demand decline, sales in cargo transport and port handling could fall together. Sales fell 9.6% in 2025, so volume swings feed directly into results. Distinguishing long-term trends from temporary factors is necessary.

Non-operating items and earnings quality

Operating income rose 11.5% in 2025 while parent net income fell 23.2%, and a net loss occurred in 3Q 2026. Because items outside operations often change the direction of earnings, the weight of one-off factors needs to be checked in quarterly reports.

Fuel and cost pressure

Transport businesses carry a large share of costs in fuel, labor and other expenses. If fuel prices or wages rise and cannot be passed on to freight rates, margins could narrow further. At a 2.2% operating margin, the impact of cost swings is relatively large.

Overall view

Sebang is an integrated logistics company centered on port handling and cargo transport, with 2025 full-year sales of KRW 1.24 trillion and operating income of KRW 27.3 billion. The operating line has moved toward recovery, staying in the black from 1Q 2026 through 3Q 2026 after the 4Q 2025 operating loss.

Sales rose to KRW 347.3 billion in 3Q 2026 for three consecutive quarters, and debt-to-equity was 23.8% at end-2025, lower than in 2024. By contrast, parent net income fell from KRW 105.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 65.7 billion in 2025 and swung to a KRW 8.88 billion net loss in 3Q 2026.

With the operating margin down from 3.5% in 2022 to 2.2% in 2025, the gain in profit from higher volumes is thin. The price-to-book multiple sits at a low level relative to book value, but the reason should be read together with earnings volatility and the durability of quarterly profit.

The next checkpoints are the non-operating items and full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and the net income trend in the 1Q 2027 quarterly report.

Because the operating recovery and the deterioration in net income point in different directions, the trend should be checked against disclosed figures rather than read in one direction.
