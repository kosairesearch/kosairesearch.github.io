02

Business structure

Taewonmulsan was founded in 1955 as Samyang Sangwhoe and historically supplied gypsum for cement and automotive water pumps. It exited the cement-related business in 2021 and expanded into food, youth game distribution and stationery. The business now has three arms: auto parts, food and IP licensing.

The auto parts unit supplies Korea GM, so its revenue depends on whether its water pumps are adopted for the customer's new models, and the discontinuation of the Chevrolet Spark and the non-adoption of a new model's water pump, along with weaker demand from HD Construction Equipment, pushed auto parts sales lower.

The food unit exclusively imports and distributes Samontana, an Italian frozen dessert brand, and has added frozen ready meals and imported dairy products. According to the company, food sales rose 104.0% in 2025, partly offsetting the auto parts decline.

The IP business began in 2025 with a licensing deal with Universal Studios and added SMG Holdings licenses for Dragon Ball and Haikyu in 2026. Reporting at the end of 2025 argued that food had overtaken auto parts as the largest revenue share.

Auto parts depend on a small set of vehicle customers, while food profitability depends on import margins and control of selling and administrative costs.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 11 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.21 billion in 2023, a 15.9% decline, then rose 25.6% to KRW 11.6 billion in 2024 and 28.5% to KRW 14.9 billion in 2025, marking two straight years of growth from 2024. Of the 2022 to 2025 span, 2025 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating losses were KRW 525 million in 2022, KRW 811 million in 2023, KRW 894 million in 2024 and KRW 978 million in 2025, so the company recorded four consecutive years of operating losses with the loss widening each year.

The operating margin was -4.8%, -8.8%, -7.7% and -6.6% over the same years, so the margin improved after the 2023 trough while the loss amount kept growing. The company said that in 2025 selling and administrative expenses fell but cost of sales rose, widening the operating loss slightly.

Attributable net income went from a loss of KRW 1.36 billion in 2022 and KRW 82.46 million in 2023 to a profit of KRW 17.5 billion in 2024, which was a swing to profit, and then to a loss of KRW 2.34 billion in 2025, a swing back to loss.

Analysis indicates that most of the 2024 profit came from discontinued operating income tied to the Ulsan gypsum sale and financial income.

Operating cash flow was KRW 286 million in 2022, minus KRW 918 million in 2023, KRW 1.74 billion in 2024 and minus KRW 4.94 billion in 2025, with the 2025 outflow the largest of the four years. At the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 41.7 billion and total liabilities KRW 3.74 billion, for a debt ratio of 9.0%.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 3.17 billion in the fourth quarter, then to KRW 5.34 billion in the second quarter of 2026, the highest in the reported quarters, before falling to KRW 3.91 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Operating losses have run for five consecutive quarters since the third quarter of 2025, with the first quarter of 2026 the largest at KRW 741 million.

Attributable net income swung to a KRW 2.71 billion loss in the fourth quarter of 2025, then to a KRW 139 million profit in the second quarter of 2026, and fell 95.4% to KRW 6.37 million in the third quarter of 2026. Attributable net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 3.06 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Taewonmulsan is classified under shipbuilding, but its revenue comes from auto parts, food and IP licensing.

Large shipbuilders such as HD Hyundai Heavy Industries and Hanwha Ocean are tied to ship orders and vessel price cycles, whereas Taewonmulsan moves with demand for automotive and construction equipment parts and food distribution.

A financial information service places the company in a transport equipment and parts peer group, which is the closer benchmark for its results.

In auto parts, the discontinuation of the Chevrolet Spark and the non-adoption of a new model's water pump were direct headwinds, and weaker demand from HD Construction Equipment added pressure. Food is described as relying on relatively steady demand for frozen desserts and ready meals.

The food business lifted revenue, but it has not translated fully into better operating profit, and selling and administrative costs remain a burden. The IP business is adding new revenue through more licensing deals, but its share of quarterly revenue must be tracked through segment disclosures.

Competitively, auto parts depend on a small set of vehicle customers, so the speed of winning replacement customers matters.

No annual revenue or profit guidance from the company was found, so the outlook rests on whether the cancellation is carried out and on the profit trend in the next periodic report.

Bull factors

No borrowings and a sizable equity base

Total equity stood at KRW 41.7 billion at the end of 2025 with a debt ratio of 9.0%. With no borrowings, financing costs are small, and the company is not in capital impairment even while operating losses continue. This balance sheet is the base from which it can attempt a business shift.

Revenue growth from food and IP

Revenue grew 28.5% to KRW 14.9 billion in 2025, and the company said food sales rose 104.0%. Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 5.34 billion was the highest in the reported quarters. The IP licensing that began in 2025 widened in 2026 with an SMG Holdings agreement covering additional titles.

Buybacks and a planned cancellation

The company resolved on September 1, 2026 to buy back shares worth KRW 1.2 billion and completed the purchase on October 21, 2026. Treasury stock holdings rose to 13.07%. A cancellation of 319,442 shares is planned for January 2027, which extends the shareholder return measures.

Bear factors

Four years of operating losses and five loss-making quarters

Operating losses grew each year from KRW 525 million in 2022 to KRW 978 million in 2025. The operating margin improved from -8.8% in 2023 to -6.6% in 2025, yet the reported figures show no return to operating profit.

Losses have continued for five straight quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

Dependence on auto parts customers and falling sales

Analysis points to slow profitability recovery in auto parts, given the discontinued Spark, non-adoption of a new model's water pump and weaker HD Construction Equipment demand. Third-quarter 2026 revenue fell 21.9% year on year to KRW 3.91 billion, and the operating loss widened to KRW 225 million in the same quarter.

Governance and minority shareholder burden

Minority shareholders number 1,502, or 98.6% of all shareholders, and hold 24.8% of issued shares. Buybacks and cancellation are under way, but calls for governance improvement continue.

Risk factors

Listing maintenance and market-cap threshold

Because market capitalization fell short, the stock was designated as a managed issue on August 13, 2026, trading was suspended that day and resumed on August 14. Reporting indicates the delisting threshold rises to KRW 50 billion starting in 2027, which would raise the burden of maintaining the listing.

Cash flow and the shift to new businesses

Operating cash flow in 2025 was minus KRW 4.94 billion, the largest outflow of the four years. Food and IP are lifting revenue, but whether that improves operating profit must be checked in the next periodic reports. If new businesses add costs before revenue, cash outflows could persist.

Share price volatility and investment-caution designations

The exchange designated the stock as a caution issue on June 4, June 8, July 8 and August 25, 2026, citing concentrated trading by a small number of accounts. Three-day price moves exceeding 15% were recorded several times. Under exchange rules, repeated sharp moves can lead to escalation to a warning stage.

Overall view

Taewonmulsan has no borrowings and total equity of KRW 41.7 billion, yet it recorded operating losses in each year from 2022 to 2025 and has posted five straight loss-making quarters since the third quarter of 2025.

Revenue rose 28.5% to KRW 14.9 billion in 2025, but much of the gain came from food while auto parts sales declined. The 2024 profit was driven largely by discontinued operating income from the Ulsan gypsum sale, and 2025 returned to an attributable net loss of KRW 2.34 billion.

Attributable net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 3.06 billion. The share buyback and the planned January 2027 cancellation are confirmed shareholder return measures.

At the same time, the managed-issue designation tied to the market-cap shortfall and the higher listing threshold from 2027 are constraints the company faces. Whether the core business recovers will be visible in operating results in the 2026 annual report and the first-quarter 2027 report.
