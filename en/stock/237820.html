Report overview

Ad Core Profit Recovers, Quarterly Swings Remain

PlayD's third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose 15.2% year over year to KRW 1.65 billion, and revenue has grown for two consecutive quarters, but the operating loss in the first quarter of 2026 and heavy dependence on its largest shareholder SOOP remain points to watch.

02

Business structure

PlayD was founded in 2010 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2020 as a digital-based full-service advertising agency. According to the company profile, advertising agency work accounts for most of its revenue, and the company presents AI and machine learning technology for ad operations as its core competitive edge.

In 2025 it acquired a creative company to strengthen its creative capabilities, and it moved its commerce business into a newly established subsidiary. Because of this separation, standalone revenue declined, while consolidated figures include subsidiary revenue, so both bases need to be read together.

In September 2026 the company appointed Heo Ik-seo as chief creative director and reorganized into four divisions covering marketing, growth strategy, creative solutions and management planning. Its in-house AI ad solution AdInsight is supplied to Naver and generates operating revenue beyond agency fees.

The company also built P.AI, an internal AI agent platform with 21 agents supporting ad operations, data analysis and content production. Its largest shareholder is SOOP, which acquired a 70% stake in PlayD in 2025 for KRW 73.5 billion.

The core of its strategy is an integrated service combining SOOP's platform and streamer content with PlayD's performance advertising capabilities. Competitively, it sits alongside full-service agencies such as Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide as well as digital specialist agencies.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 44 billion in 2022 to KRW 37.3 billion in 2023, a decline of 15.2% year over year, then rose to KRW 38.6 billion in 2024 (up 3.6%) and KRW 40.2 billion in 2025 (up 4.2%), an increase in each of 2024 and 2025.

Operating profit dropped from KRW 5.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.53 billion in 2023, a 54.8% decline, making 2023 the smallest year of the 2022–2025 period. It then rose to KRW 4 billion in 2024 (up 58.1%) and KRW 6.22 billion in 2025 (up 55.5%), and 2025 was the largest year of the period.

The operating margin moved from 12.7% in 2022 to 6.8% in 2023, 10.4% in 2024 and 15.5% in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.95 billion in 2022, KRW 2.89 billion in 2023 (down 41.7%), KRW 4.3 billion in 2024 (up 49.0%) and KRW 5.87 billion in 2025 (up 36.6%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests is reported at the same figures for each year, so the two lines match in the filed values. The debt ratio fell from 105.8% in 2022 to 66.7% in 2023 and 57.2% in 2024, then edged up to 59.8% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 91 billion, of which KRW 90.9 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 54.4 billion.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 15.25 million in 2022 to KRW 5.24 billion in 2023 and KRW 15.3 billion in 2024, then fell to KRW 6.91 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue climbed from KRW 9.46 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 13.3 billion in the fourth quarter (up 40.9% from the prior quarter), and operating profit reached KRW 3.05 billion in the fourth quarter (up 112.4%).

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue of KRW 8.99 billion (down 32.5% from the prior quarter) was the low point, and an operating loss of KRW 97.93 million marked a swing into deficit.

In the second quarter of 2026, revenue of KRW 11.1 billion (up 23.7%) and an operating profit of KRW 89.1 million marked a return to profit.

In the third quarter, revenue reached KRW 11.6 billion (up 4.0% quarter on quarter and 22.3% year on year), and operating profit reached KRW 1.65 billion (up 1,754.9% quarter on quarter and 15.2% year on year).

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.85 billion in the third quarter, up 36.0% year over year, and the sum for the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, was KRW 4.62 billion.

The company attributes the third-quarter improvement to the full reflection of revenue from advertisers won in the first half, higher video production revenue and cost efficiency.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic advertising spending is shifting online, with digital ads accounting for more than 60% of the market and growing mainly on online and mobile channels. This shift supports demand for digital ad operations and data analysis that can be measured by performance.

As spending moves further into digital, competition between platforms and agencies intensifies, making fee levels and advertiser acquisition more important. This analysis does not present a cycle indicator for ad spending as a figure, so the company's own results are used to gauge the effect of the industry.

PlayD's results since the 2023 low have moved with advertiser acquisition and cost structure, suggesting that company-specific factors weigh more than the market itself. Listed peers Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide are classified as full-service agencies, and their business mix differs from PlayD's.

PlayD puts its weight on digital performance advertising, AI solutions and platform advertising tied to the SOOP group. AI adoption competition directly affects ad operating efficiency and bidding competitiveness, and PlayD has deployed 21 AI agents internally in response.

The company is also expanding its game marketing and branding campaign business, so game industry ad demand acts as a downstream market.

06

Outlook

On March 27, 2026, the company voluntarily disclosed a corporate value enhancement plan that targets revenue growth through portfolio diversification and new contract wins, along with higher profitability through AI-based capabilities.

The plan states that the company will keep its core clients while diversifying its advertiser base in both quality and quantity, and will seek new business opportunities through collaboration with subsidiaries and sister companies.

The plan includes forward-looking statements, and the company notes that actual results may differ.

PlayD is expected to take part as a key operator in the integrated advertising and marketing brand that SOOP is pursuing, linking platform ads, streamer content, branded content and performance marketing, and the company says it is pushing to build the brand within 2026.

On the AI side, the company has built P.AI, an internal agent platform, and says it aims to improve ad operating efficiency and bidding competitiveness. To expand branding campaigns and game marketing, it has hired senior talent and set up a dedicated team.

The next checkpoint is when the integrated SOOP brand launches and how quickly that business is reflected in consolidated revenue. The fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 preliminary results are likely to come around mid-February 2027, based on the timing of last year's disclosure on February 11, 2026.

The 2026 annual report must be filed by the end of March 2027, and whether the operating margin holds near the 2025 level of 15.5% will be the checkpoint for that report. Whether the new advertiser effect that lifted third-quarter revenue continues beyond the fourth quarter has not yet been confirmed.

Bull factors

Recovery in quarterly operating profit

Operating profit for 2025 rose 55.5% to KRW 6.22 billion, and the 15.5% margin was the highest of the 2022–2025 period. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit reached KRW 1.65 billion, up 15.2% year over year, and quarterly profit has moved back toward recovery since the return to profit in the second quarter. The company cites the effect of new advertiser revenue and cost efficiency as the drivers.

New advertisers and video production growth

The company says revenue from advertisers won in the first half was fully reflected in the third quarter, and video production revenue also increased. Standalone revenue decline narrowed from 19.6% in the second quarter to 9.0% in the third, which the company cites as evidence of recovery in the core ad business. It also plans to expand creative services that span brand strategy through campaign production.

AI solutions as a potential new revenue source

The company says it supplies its in-house AI ad solution AdInsight to Naver and secures operating revenue from it. Its internal P.AI platform carries 21 agents aimed at improving ad operating efficiency and bidding competitiveness. How much these non-agency revenue sources contribute to results will be visible in the next periodic report.

Bear factors

Standalone revenue decline and changing consolidation scope

Standalone revenue fell 9.0% year over year in the third quarter of 2026 after the commerce business was moved into a subsidiary. Consolidated results include the effect of acquired subsidiaries, which makes it hard to isolate the growth rate of the core advertising business.

First-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 8.99 billion fell 32.5% from the prior quarter, showing that quarterly revenue remains volatile.

Volatility in quarterly profit

The company swung to an operating loss of KRW 97.93 million in the first quarter of 2026, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 96.5% from the prior quarter to KRW 87.2 million.

The second-quarter operating profit of KRW 89.1 million was positive but small, so it can react sharply to changes in advertiser mix or costs. Even after the profit improvement since the fourth quarter of 2025, the low point in the first quarter of 2026 remains a fact.

Group dependence and low free float

The largest shareholder, SOOP, holds more than 70% of the shares, leaving a free float of about 29%. Because the strategy is tied to SOOP's platform and content collaboration, changes in the parent's plans could affect the company.

The sharp share price decline over the past year, together with the low free float, remains a variable in trading conditions.

10

Risk factors

Advertiser concentration and revenue retention

Retaining core clients is presented as the starting point of the value enhancement plan, so the loss of major advertisers would directly affect results. If the effect of new advertisers is concentrated in a few quarters, sustaining revenue in later quarters could be difficult. Whether diversification of the advertiser base shows up in the revenue mix is the key question.

Group strategy and business allocation

If the integrated brand with SOOP does not deliver the expected results, the anticipated synergies may not reach reported earnings. As collaboration within the group grows, intra-group transaction terms and business allocation will shape how results are interpreted. How roles are divided after the September 2026 reorganization is a point to confirm next.

Uncertain monetization of AI investment

Building AI agents and supplying solutions can require costs upfront with revenue following later. The revenue contribution of AI solutions is still hard to separate from reported results.

If technology competition accelerates, the differentiation of in-house solutions could narrow, making the pace of monetization a variable.

12

Overall view

PlayD's 2025 operating profit of KRW 6.22 billion, up 55.5% year over year, and its 15.5% operating margin show earnings capacity recovering since the 2023 low.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 11.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.65 billion were both higher than a year earlier, which points to the effect of new advertisers.

On the other side, the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 97.93 million in the first quarter of 2026, and small quarterly profits mean results are volatile.

Standalone revenue declined due to the commerce separation, and consolidated results include acquired subsidiaries, so the two bases should be read separately.

With its largest shareholder holding more than 70% of shares, the free float is low, and the integrated brand and AI solutions have not yet reached a scale visible in reported results.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x, while small earnings make the price-to-earnings multiple sensitive to changes in quarterly profit.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 fourth-quarter preliminary results, the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and whether the SOOP integrated brand launches within 2026. Because strengths and weaknesses coexist, the direction that prevails will become clearer in upcoming disclosures.
