Sales Up Three Straight Quarters, Operating Margin Stuck at 4.8%

02

Business structure

DONGNAM CHEMICAL CO was founded in 1965 as a surfactant maker and listed on the Korea Exchange main market in 1996.

Surfactants give cleaning, emulsifying and dispersing functions by acting on the boundary between two phases that do not mix, such as water and oil, and they are used across detergents, textiles, paper, cosmetics and agrochemicals.

In first-half 2026 sales, surfactants accounted for 97.1% of revenue, while trading sales of items such as fatty acids and fatty alcohols made up 2.9%. Production runs at the Daesan and Tancheon plants in Chungnam, and the company's business summary cites an annual capacity of about 170,000 tons.

First-half 2026 output totaled 55,401 tons across the two plants at an average utilization of 63.4%. Main raw materials include ethylene oxide, and purchases of these materials were KRW 87.173 billion in the same period.

Core customers are detergent, textile, paper and cosmetics makers and industrial buyers, and the business summary points to domestic substitution of imported products as a driver of earnings. The company says it is widening its portfolio into cosmetics and personal care and into oil and gas.

Compared with larger listed chemical companies such as LG Chem, Kumho Petro Chemical and Lotte Chemical, DONGNAM CHEMICAL CO is a mid-sized firm focused on one material family. Its largest shareholder is Miwon Holdings.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on the DART annual filings, sales fell 13.8% from KRW 193.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 166.7 billion in 2023, then rose 11.8% to KRW 186.4 billion in 2024 and 4.2% to KRW 194.3 billion in 2025.

Sales grew in 2024 and 2025 after the 2023 low, two straight years of growth, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 span.

Operating profit fell 40.1% from KRW 13.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.06 billion in 2023, then recovered to KRW 8.93 billion in 2024 (up 10.8%) and KRW 9.37 billion in 2025 (up 4.8%), still short of the 2022 level.

The operating margin went from 7.0% in 2022 to 4.8% in 2023 and stayed at 4.8% in 2024 and 2025, and in 2025 sales growth (4.2%) and operating profit growth (4.8%) were similar in size, so the margin held.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 10.6 billion in 2022, KRW 5.95 billion in 2023 (down 44.0%), KRW 6.71 billion in 2024 (up 12.8%) and KRW 6.99 billion in 2025 (up 4.1%), and net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same four levels.

The debt ratio fell from 84.1% in 2022 and 88.3% in 2023 to 80.2% in 2024 and 74.5% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 40.6 billion and total equity of KRW 54.5 billion at the end of 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 12.9 billion in 2022, KRW 18.4 billion in 2023, KRW 6.05 billion in 2024 and KRW 15.2 billion in 2025.

By quarter, sales were KRW 51.5 billion in Q3 2025 with operating profit of KRW 3.29 billion, and in Q4 2025 sales fell to KRW 46.7 billion (down 9.3% quarter on quarter) and operating profit to KRW 2.1 billion (down 36.1%).

Q1 2026 sales were KRW 56.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 3.81 billion, and Q2 2026 sales rose to KRW 62 billion with operating profit of KRW 5.35 billion, up 40.4% quarter on quarter.

Q3 2026 sales reached KRW 64.2 billion (up 3.5% quarter on quarter and 24.8% year on year), while operating profit fell to KRW 2.95 billion (down 45.0% quarter on quarter and 10.3% year on year) and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.46 billion.

Over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 10.6 billion, and the quarter-to-quarter swings are large enough that raw-material costs and plant utilization need to be read alongside each result.

Industry analysis

DONGNAM CHEMICAL CO's customers are concentrated in detergents, textiles, paper and cosmetics, so output and utilization in those industries drive shipment volumes. Overseas relocation of domestic textile makers and shifts in detergent demand directly affect plant utilization.

The company's business summary describes demand rising as applications in detergents and cosmetics broaden. Domestic manufacturing progress that replaces imported products is cited as a driver of the earnings recovery.

An average first-half 2026 utilization of 63.4% suggests spare capacity, so higher volumes could improve fixed-cost absorption. If demand slows, the same utilization can leave fixed costs weighing on margins, which makes the pace of volume recovery central to the margin outlook.

Purchases of ethylene oxide and other raw materials reached KRW 87.173 billion in first-half 2026, so cost swings feed directly into margins. Tighter environmental rules could lift demand for biodegradable raw materials, and the company has said its biodegradable new products keep it at the top of market share.

Compared with larger chemical peers such as Kumho Petro Chemical, Lotte Chemical and LG Chem, DONGNAM CHEMICAL CO is a mid-sized company focused on one surfactant family, so its cycle sensitivity and diversification differ.

The current position in the industry cycle is hard to pin down, so the next quarters need to be checked against the 2026 profit swings.
