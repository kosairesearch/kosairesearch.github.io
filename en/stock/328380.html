Report overview

After an Annual Turnaround, Quarterly Losses Test the AI Pivot

Saltware posted an annual operating profit in 2025, but operating losses have run for three straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026, making the monetization of its AI-focused shift the key checkpoint.

  1. 1

    Operating profit of KRW 566 million in 2025 turned the company back into profit after three years of operating losses, while revenue of KRW 63.7 billion rose for a third straight year.

  2. 2

    Operating losses have continued for three straight quarters from the first quarter of 2026, and the third-quarter loss of KRW 848 million was wider than the second-quarter loss.

  3. 3

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Saltware is a comprehensive IT services company founded in 2003. It provides cloud-based IT services to a range of industries, including HW and SW infrastructure construction, enterprise portal solutions, cloud services as an AWS partner, and smart farm operations.

Within cloud, the company has been expanding managed services (MSP) and higher-value consulting work beyond simple build-and-run projects. Its customers are mainly large domestic corporations and public institutions, which form a stable revenue base.

The company said it commercialized three AI-based SaaS services in 2024. It is now pursuing commercialization projects for its own AI security, analytics and automation solutions under an AI transformation (AX) strategy.

A government-led AI agent demonstration project for semiconductor manufacturing, worth about KRW 3.1 billion and disclosed on May 12, 2026, is being described by the company as the turning point for moving from a cloud-centric business to an AI data platform business.

Its proprietary AI data platform, Sapie-ADP, is planned as an integrated service linked to the existing cloud business and an AI agent platform. Competition comes from large SI and cloud providers such as Samsung SDS, Hyundai Autoever and LG CNS, which contest the same large-enterprise and public customers.

Saltware's revenue is small relative to these larger players, so individual large projects can have a visible effect on its results.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 31.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 49.3 billion in 2023 (up 57.4% year on year), KRW 57.9 billion in 2024 (up 17.3%) and KRW 63.7 billion in 2025 (up 10.0%), a three-year run of increases from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

The company disclosed 2025 consolidated revenue as its largest annual figure. Operating losses narrowed from KRW 613 million in 2022 to KRW 88.86 million in 2023, widened to KRW 1.71 billion in 2024, and turned into operating profit of KRW 566 million in 2025.

The operating margin moved from -2.0% in 2022 and -0.2% in 2023 to -3.0% in 2024 before reaching 0.9% in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 761 million in 2023 and KRW 848 million in 2025, while 2024 showed a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 150 million.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 839 million in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder number.

The company attributed the 2025 improvement to a larger share of managed services and higher-value consulting in cloud, along with operating efficiency from embedding AI technology across its businesses.

Operating cash flow went from -KRW 1.01 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.53 billion in 2023, KRW 610 million in 2024 and KRW 70.32 million in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio moved from 66.2% in 2022 to 58.8% in 2023 and 59.1% in 2024 before rising to 64.6% in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Saltware swung to an operating profit of KRW 586 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 after a loss in the third quarter, but then posted operating losses of KRW 99.39 million in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 798 million in the second and KRW 848 million in the third, three straight quarters of losses.

Revenue peaked at KRW 19.9 billion in the second quarter of 2026, the highest of the last five quarters, then fell 13.5% quarter on quarter to KRW 17.2 billion in the third quarter, though it still rose 5.0% year on year. The controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters totals KRW 244 million.

05

Industry analysis

As generative AI spreads, enterprises are adopting more AI infrastructure and cloud services, which is lifting demand for cloud and data work, according to the prevailing industry view. Broader use of enterprise data is also expected to increase demand for AI model development and operation platforms.

In Korea, cloud-native migration projects at large public institutions are gaining momentum, raising demand for private and hybrid cloud. Saltware has secured large corporations and public institutions as its main customers. In the past, its profitability was limited by labor costs and project cost burdens.

In competition, it overlaps with large SI and cloud providers such as Samsung SDS, Hyundai Autoever and LG CNS, but its revenue scale is much smaller. An industry source has described its base of large corporate and public-sector customer references as favorable for spreading its AI solutions.

On the other side, analysts say research and development and talent investment needed to upgrade AI solutions will keep near-term profitability improvement limited. The key industry question is therefore whether expanding market demand translates into Saltware's operating profit.

06

Outlook

The '2026 Saltware Corporate Value-Up Plan' disclosed on November 9, 2026 sets mid-term targets of KRW 200 billion in revenue by 2030 and AI and data making up at least 30% of sales. That is roughly 3.1 times 2025 revenue of KRW 63.7 billion.

In the same plan, the company put first-half 2026 revenue at KRW 37.9 billion, up 22.5% year on year, on a preliminary basis.

Revenue expansion measures include offering Sapie-ADP, its own AI data platform, as an integrated service linked to the cloud business and an AI agent platform, continuing cloud business built on AWS and Red Hat, and growing sales of AI-specialized solutions.

The plan also states that the company will secure distributable reserves through profit expansion and pursue shareholder returns through dividends and treasury share purchases. A dividend in 2027 is included in the plan, while the plan does not include a plan to improve the price-to-book ratio.

At its March 2026 annual general meeting, shareholders approved transferring capital surplus to retained earnings to secure distributable reserves. The company said it will expand investor information and quarterly results briefings and disclose progress on the plan once a year.

The first item to confirm on the target path is whether quarterly operating profit returns to the black after the fourth quarter of 2026.

08

Bull factors

First Annual Profit in Three Years, With Revenue Growth

Operating profit of KRW 566 million in 2025 returned the company to operating profit after three years, and revenue rose for three straight years to KRW 63.7 billion. According to the company, preliminary first-half 2026 revenue was KRW 37.9 billion, up 22.5% year on year. The operating margin also improved from -3.0% in 2024 to 0.9% in 2025.

Pivot Toward an AI Data Platform Business

The government-led AI agent demonstration project for semiconductor manufacturing, worth about KRW 3.1 billion and disclosed on May 12, 2026, is cited as a concrete AI solution award. The company has outlined plans to offer its Sapie-ADP data platform as an integrated service tied to its cloud business.

It also plans to keep its AWS and Red Hat cloud business running while increasing AI-specialized solution sales.

Groundwork Laid for Shareholder Returns

At the March 18, 2026 annual general meeting, a proposal to transfer capital surplus to retained earnings passed, securing distributable reserves. On May 28, 2026, the company disclosed a KRW 1 billion treasury share acquisition trust agreement. The value-up plan includes a 2027 dividend and a push for shareholder returns.

09

Bear factors

Three Straight Quarters of Operating Losses

Whether operating losses continue into the fourth quarter is the next point to check. Infrastructure maintenance costs and expanded spending on AI service development have weighed on profitability. Third-quarter revenue fell 13.5% quarter on quarter, showing volatility in sales as well.

Gap Versus Pre-Listing Estimates

Pre-listing estimates for 2022 called for revenue of KRW 47 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.5 billion, while actual 2022 revenue was KRW 31.3 billion with an operating loss of KRW 613 million. Money Today reported in March 2025 that revenue fell 33.4% short of the estimate. The gap is cited as a record of how closely plans have matched execution.

Second-Generation Management and Share Price Challenges

Media reports say Lee Min-gyu, the son of the CEO and the second-largest shareholder, has taken a front-line role and is effectively leading the shift to an AI-centered business. The reports flagged improving the capital structure and supporting the share price as challenges.

Whether the AI-centered shift leads to better results and a stronger share price is presented as the key question.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly results have moved between profit and loss. Operating losses continued from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026, and analysts cite AI development costs as a near-term drag on profitability. Quarterly revenue has also swung by more than 10% in each direction.

Trading and share price volatility

After the closing price on June 10, 2026 rose more than 45% over the closing price five trading days earlier, the Korea Exchange flagged a possible investment-warning designation. In 2026, a 5-for-1 share consolidation that raised the par value from KRW 100 to KRW 500 went through a trading halt and a listing change. Rapid price moves combined with these structural changes can widen price swings.

Financial structure

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 66.2% in 2022 to 58.8% in 2023 before rising back to 64.6% in 2025. Of total equity of KRW 23.4 billion at the end of 2025, KRW 23.3 billion is attributable to controlling shareholders.

As the second generation leads the AI business, management structure and strategy execution are under evaluation.

Overall view

Saltware turned a profit for the year in 2025 with operating profit of KRW 566 million, its first in three years, and revenue rose for three straight years from 2023 to KRW 63.7 billion. The operating margin improved to 0.9% in 2025 from -3.0% in 2024.

However, operating losses have continued for three straight quarters from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026, and the third-quarter loss widened to KRW 848 million.

The controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters totals KRW 244 million, so whether the annual turnaround holds at the quarterly level is the open question.

On the business side, the company has won an AI agent demonstration project, outlined a proprietary data platform and set a KRW 200 billion revenue target for 2030. Preliminary first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 37.9 billion, disclosed on November 9, 2026, was up 22.5% year on year.

Whether the gap between the targets and current profitability narrows in the disclosures for 2027 is the item to confirm.
