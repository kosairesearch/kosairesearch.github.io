02

Business structure

Kyungbang runs two business lines: a textile business that produces cotton yarn and fabrics, and a retail and leasing business centered on the Times Square complex in Yeongdeungpo.

In the 2025 third-quarter business report, the textile segment and the shopping-mall segment accounted for 55:45 of revenue, but the shopping-mall segment's share of operating profit was more than seven times higher.

Times Square opened in 2009 on the former Yeongdeungpo factory site, after the company expanded into retail with a department store opened in 1995.

Textile production was reorganized by halting the Yongin and Gwangju plants and moving equipment to Vietnam, where the company has produced since 2013 and decided in 2017 to relocate the Gwangju plant. Older press reports describe the sale of the Yongin factory property, leaving the Banwol plant in Korea.

The textile business is focused on strengthening global brand partnerships and expanding eco-friendly product lines in the first half of 2026. The shopping-mall business benefited from theater attendance, strong sales at large tenants and expanded pop-up stores, while fashion demand grew mainly around SPA brands.

Listed peers in the same sector are typically apparel manufacturers such as Hansae and Youngone on the textile side, and fashion distributors such as Handsome and Shinsegae International on the retail side.

The company's structure combines property-based leasing and retail income at the center of profit with a textile business that accounts for about half of revenue.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 389.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 393.5 billion in 2023 (up 1.1%), KRW 397.3 billion in 2024 (up 1.0%) and KRW 412.5 billion in 2025 (up 3.8%), a three-year run of increases from 2023 through 2025. Among the years 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 31.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 16 billion in 2023 (down 49.9%), which marks the low point of the period, then rose to KRW 33.4 billion in 2024 (up 109.5%) and KRW 46 billion in 2025 (up 37.5%), two consecutive years of growth.

The operating margin moved from 8.2% in 2022 to 4.1% in 2023, 8.4% in 2024 and 11.2% in 2025. Margins recovered faster than sales grew, since the margin fell to 4.1% in 2023 before climbing to 11.2% in 2025 while sales were still rising.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from KRW 4.42 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 13.5 billion in 2023, returned to a KRW 23.6 billion profit in 2024, and reached KRW 47.4 billion in 2025, up 100.8%.

The company attributed the 2025 net profit increase mainly to improved textile operating profit from lower raw-material prices and valuation gains including the reversal of impairment losses, so part of the 2025 gain is not operating in nature.

By quarter, operating profit went from KRW 10.7 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 18.9 billion in the fourth quarter and KRW 17.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then fell to KRW 11.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026 on revenue of KRW 117.1 billion.

The second quarter of 2026 recorded a net loss of KRW 2.2 billion attributable to controlling shareholders. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 101.9 billion, down 13.0% from the prior quarter and up 5.1% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 17 billion, up 58.1% year on year.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 15.2 billion, down 4.6% year on year. The sum over the last four quarters was KRW 51.6 billion. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 29.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 60.1 billion in 2025, and the debt ratio fell from 65.3% to 54.7%.

05

Industry analysis

The textile business is a traditional manufacturing model in which profit moves with raw-material prices and orders from global brands, after production was shifted overseas.

The company's explanation that lower raw-material prices lifted textile operating profit in 2025 indicates how sensitive this segment is to input costs.

Older press reports said the textile business posted losses of several billion won each year, while the 2025 disclosure referred to improved textile operating profit, suggesting a change in direction. Commentary on the textile and retail industries as a whole, however, still describes weakness.

Listed peers in the sector split between export-oriented apparel manufacturers and brand-fashion distributors, and Kyungbang combines both models in one company. On the fashion side, demand expanded in the first half of 2026, led by SPA brands.

The macro backdrop improved, with the IMF raising its 2026 growth forecast for Korea to 3.9% on November 17, 2026. The IMF attributed that growth mainly to a boom in AI-related semiconductor exports, so it is not clear that the improvement carries over directly to textile and mall demand.

Revenue from the company's leasing and retail operations depends on consumer spending and tenant mix, so the link between macro indicators and the company's own sales should be tracked quarter by quarter.

Outlook

The starting point for the outlook is the first half of 2026. The company-monitoring summary from WiseReport, dated August 31, 2026, put first-half 2026 consolidated sales up 15.6% year on year, operating profit up 77.6% and net profit up 37.1%.

The same summary attributed the Times Square gains to theater attendance, strong sales at large tenants and more pop-up stores. Times Square is pursuing a merchandise-mix change and new store development, while the textile business is expanding its eco-friendly product line and strengthening global brand partnerships.

The third-quarter 2026 quarterly report was filed on November 16, 2026, showing operating profit of KRW 17 billion, up 58.1% year on year, on revenue of KRW 101.9 billion, up 5.1%. The next checkpoints are fourth-quarter 2026 results and the 2026 annual report.

The fourth quarter will be compared with revenue of KRW 118.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 18.9 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025. The 2026 annual report is due by the end of March 2027, and whether the annual operating margin holds near the 2025 level of 11.2% will be a key test of the profit structure.

Also to be tracked are how new Times Square stores and the merchandise changes affect the sales mix, and how much the eco-friendly textile lines contribute to revenue.

08

Bull factors

Mall segment contributes disproportionate profit

Based on the Q3 2025 business report, the Complex Shopping Mall division accounts for a smaller share of revenue than the Textile division, but its share of operating profit is more than seven times higher.

In the first half of 2026 as well, strong box-office results at the cinema, solid sales at large tenants, and an expansion of pop-up stores lifted revenue, according to Company Monitor. A strength is that rental and retail income supports earnings independently of the textile business cycle.

Continuity of profit recovery

Operating profit rose for two consecutive years, from a low of KRW 16.0 billion in 2023 to KRW 33.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 46.0 billion in 2025. Operating profit in the first half of 2026 increased 77.6% year on year, and Q3 operating profit of KRW 17.0 billion increased 58.1% year on year. The operating margin of 11.2% in 2025 was also the highest during 2022–2025.

Improving financial cushion

The debt ratio fell from 65.3% in 2022 to 54.7% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total liabilities were KRW 443.8 billion and total equity was KRW 811.8 billion. Cash flow from operating activities grew from KRW 29.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 60.1 billion in 2025, so earnings and cash generation are growing together. A more stable financial structure widens the capacity to absorb swings in the business.

09

Bear factors

Volatile textile profitability

Past media reports have pointed out that the textile business posts losses in the billions of won every year. Because the company explained that the improvement in textile operating profit in 2025 was mainly due to lower raw material prices, the same effect may not continue if raw material price trends change.

The fact that the operating margin fell to 4.1% in 2023 shows the volatility of this segment's earnings structure.

Quarterly earnings swings

Operating profit in Q2 2026 was KRW 11.2 billion, down 37.3% from the previous quarter, and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.2 billion. Revenue in Q3 2026 was KRW 101.9 billion, down 13.0% from the previous quarter. Uneven quarterly earnings are a weakness.

Ownership concentration and free float

According to Company Monitor, as of November 6, 2026, Kim Dam and 18 others held 55.97%, Samyang Holdings and 4 others held 11.43%, and treasury shares accounted for 5.21%. Because the stakes held by related parties are close to a majority, the volume of shares traded in the market is limited.

After the March 2026 shareholders' meeting, the board has a total of 4 directors, with 1 outside director, so the board composition is also narrow.

10

Risk factors

Raw materials and overseas production

The textile division has raised its share of overseas production, producing at a Vietnam plant since 2013 and deciding in 2017 to move the Gwangju plant to Vietnam.

Because lower raw material prices were cited as the reason for the improvement in textile operating profit in 2025, changes in raw material prices and in the overseas production environment directly affect profitability.

The halt of production at Yongin and Gwangju and the sale of the Yongin plant property show a trend of a shrinking domestic manufacturing base.

Tenant mix and consumer demand

Times Square's revenue depends on cinema box-office results, sales at large tenants, and pop-up stores, so it is sensitive to consumer sentiment and tenant sales trends. The company's push for MD replacement and new store development means the existing tenant mix may change. In this process, the stability of rental income could be put to the test.

Earnings quality and governance

Net profit in 2025 reflects valuation gains such as reversals of impairment losses, which may not recur. Therefore, when assessing the level of earnings, operating-level profit and valuation gains should be separated.

Related parties hold a large stake, and at the March 2026 shareholders' meeting, inside directors Kim Jun and Kim Dam were re-elected, so the management lineup was maintained.

Overall view

Kyungbang combines a textile business with a shopping-mall business centered on Times Square. Sales rose from KRW 389.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 412.5 billion in 2025, and the operating margin climbed from a 4.1% low in 2023 to 11.2% in 2025.

The 2025 profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 47.4 billion includes valuation gains such as lower raw-material costs and the reversal of impairment losses, so the speed and quality of the recovery need to be assessed separately.

In the first half of 2026, sales, operating profit and net profit all increased year on year, and third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 17 billion rose 58.1%. The second quarter of 2026 still posted a KRW 2.2 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, which leaves quarterly volatility in place.

Total equity of KRW 811.8 billion and a debt ratio of 54.7% reflect a stronger financial base. Supporting factors are mall income and the earnings recovery, while the variability of textile profitability, quarterly swings and ownership concentration weigh on the outlook. The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
