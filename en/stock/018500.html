02

Business structure

Dongwon Metal is a Korean auto parts maker centered on automotive body components, including door frames, door modules, impact beams and mufflers, which it sells in Korea and abroad. Its core technology is roll forming, which it uses to produce door frames and bumper beams.

Its main customers are global automakers including Hyundai Motor, KIA and GM. Its domestic production base consists of the Jinryang plant in Gyeongsan and the Yesan plant in Chungnam, and the Jinryang plant handles new R&D products and pre-mass-production work for new models.

The Yesan plant consolidated the Dunpo, Gunsan and Cheonan plants and began full operation in June 2025. Overseas, the company has operations in Alabama and Georgia in the United States, Mexico, the Czech Republic, Slovakia and Brazil, with production and sales networks across six countries.

Its steel pipe business sits in a subsidiary, so the revenue mix is weighted toward auto parts. Its eco-friendly product line includes hybrid-vehicle battery base plates, which protect the battery pack from vibration and shock.

As of June 2026 the R&D team had 46 people, working on door frames, battery frames, rail and seat frame products. Competitively, the company is a metal body-parts supplier whose volumes are set by automaker program awards, so the production plans and new model programs of its customers drive revenue.

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 538.6 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2023 to KRW 659.5 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026, increasing for three consecutive fiscal years from the year ended March 2024 through the year ended March 2026.

Operating profit was KRW 35.6 billion, KRW 35.7 billion, KRW 42 billion and KRW 35 billion across the four fiscal years, with the largest figure in the year ended March 2025 and the smallest in the year ended March 2026.

In the year ended March 2026, sales grew 5.3% but operating profit fell 16.6%, so revenue expansion did not carry through to operating income. The operating margin was 6.6% in the year ended March 2023, 6.3% in 2024, 6.7% in 2025 and 5.3% in 2026, the lowest of the four years.

Attributable net profit reached KRW 39 billion in the year ended March 2026, up 84.5%, and exceeded operating profit, which points to non-operating items and tax effects. The size of those items should be checked in the notes to the regular report.

In one external financial data compilation, quarterly tax expense was negative in the fourth quarter of 2025 and the first quarter of 2026, meaning tax items supported reported profit in those quarters.

Quarterly operating profit moved from KRW 13.1 billion in the second quarter of 2025 to KRW 8.45 billion in the third quarter (down 35.2% quarter on quarter), KRW 7.44 billion in the fourth quarter and KRW 6.06 billion in the first quarter of 2026, before rebounding to KRW 14.4 billion in the second quarter of 2026, up 137.8% from the prior quarter and 10.4% year on year.

Quarterly sales rose for three consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the second quarter of 2026, with the latest quarter at KRW 193.7 billion, up 15.3% year on year.

Attributable net profit rose for four consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the second quarter of 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 47.5 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026, operating profit was only KRW 6.06 billion while attributable net profit was KRW 13.1 billion, so quarterly net profit did not move in step with operating profit.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for Dongwon Metal is directly linked to automaker production and sales volumes. Because its customers are concentrated among automakers such as Hyundai Motor, KIA and GM, model-level output and new model launch timing set the direction of revenue.

The auto parts industry moves with the automotive sales cycle, and raw material prices such as steel and exchange rates also act as cost variables.

The shift toward eco-friendly vehicles brings both opportunity and challenge: rising demand for battery frames for hybrid and electric vehicles could add new product revenue, while falling volumes of internal combustion models would pressure existing product sales.

Measured against the auto parts industry average, the company's sales scale is on the small side, while its operating margin ranks above the industry average. Its debt-to-equity ratio is higher than the industry average.

Competitively, it faces domestic metal parts suppliers that produce body frame components using roll forming and press technology, as well as automakers' in-house production. Its overseas production sites across six countries follow the overseas factory demand of its automaker customers. The position of the industry in the cycle should be read from automaker production indicators.

The next checkpoints are whether the operating profit recovery seen in the second quarter of 2026 continues into the third quarter and whether new model program awards show up in results. Whether the operating margin rebounds from 5.3% in the fiscal year ended March 2026 will be visible in the next annual results.

Overall view

Dongwon Metal grew both sales and net profit in the fiscal year ended March 2026, with sales of KRW 659.5 billion and attributable net profit of KRW 39 billion.

Operating profit, however, fell to KRW 35 billion and the operating margin dropped to 5.3%, so it is necessary to separate whether the profit gain came from core operations or non-operating factors.
