02

Business structure

Dongwon Metal, founded in 1971, manufactures automotive body parts such as door frames, door beams, impact beams and mufflers, along with steel pipes. Based on first-quarter 2026 revenue, automotive parts account for 95% and steel pipes for 5%, according to the Butler data aggregator.

The link between the steel pipe segment and its subsidiary Dongwon Pipe could not be confirmed in this review. Major customers are Hyundai Motor and KIA, and the company's sustainability report identifies its high customer dependence as a risk.

Production sites include the domestic plants in Gyeongsan, Yesan and Asan, along with overseas facilities in Alabama and Georgia in the United States, Mexico, Brazil, the Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The company said the Yesan plant began full operation in June 2025 after consolidating the Dunpo, Gunsan and Cheonan plants. Its core technology is roll forming, and it is expanding development of battery base plates for hybrid (HEV) and electric (EV) vehicles. As of June 2026 the company had 46 R&D staff.

Listed peers in the same auto parts sector include Seoyon E-Hwa, Daewonkangup, Sungwoo Hitech and Hyundai WIA, but segment-level comparisons with these companies were not confirmed.

Segment revenue amounts and the overseas revenue share were also not confirmed, so this section focuses on segment mix and product composition.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, revenue rose for three consecutive fiscal years, from the year ended March 2024 through the year ended March 2026, when it reached KRW 659.5 billion, the largest year in the period.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 42 billion in the year ended March 2025 and fell 16.6% to KRW 35 billion in the year ended March 2026.

The operating margin moved from 6.6% in the year ended March 2023 to 6.3% in the year ended March 2024 and 6.7% in the year ended March 2025, before declining to 5.3% in the year ended March 2026.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 18.2 billion in the year ended March 2023 to KRW 39 billion in the year ended March 2026, up 84.5% year on year.

An external quarterly income table records negative income tax expense in Q4 2025 and Q1 2026, which means the tax line lifted net profit in those quarters.

The filings reviewed do not break down the drivers of the operating profit decline by segment, so it is unclear whether costs or selling prices were the main cause.

On a quarterly basis, revenue bottomed at KRW 155.6 billion in Q3 2025 and then rose for three consecutive quarters through Q2 2026, when it reached KRW 193.7 billion.

Operating profit fell from KRW 13.1 billion in Q2 2025 to KRW 8.45 billion in Q3 2025, a 35.2% decline, then reached KRW 6.06 billion in Q1 2026 before rising to KRW 14.4 billion in Q2 2026, up 137.8% from the prior quarter.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders has risen for four consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 through Q2 2026, beginning in Q2 2025, and the four-quarter total is KRW 47.5 billion.

Operating cash flow grew from KRW 59.9 billion in the year ended March 2023 to KRW 107.7 billion in the year ended March 2026, and the debt ratio fell from 443.8% to 254.9%.
