02

Business structure

Total Soft Bank develops and supplies software for the maritime and port logistics sector and was founded in the late 1980s.

Its core product is the CATOS container terminal operating system, supported by the PLUS port community system, shipping systems CASP, TSB Supercargo and Cloud CASP, e-learning and port simulators.

A 2021 press report put the revenue mix at 76.4% Marine Terminal, 14% Port Community, 7.5% Shipping and 2.1% E-learning, which means sales were heavily concentrated in container terminal operations.

In a 2020 nine-month disclosure, head-office export sales accounted for 70.00% and domestic sales for 25.96%, reflecting a high overseas customer share.

Customers are mainly overseas port operators and shipping companies, and products are largely delivered as technical services that require little production equipment or raw material input. Headcount was 119 as of end-June 2026.

The port operation software market is centered on specialist software vendors, while large IT services firms expand mainly into logistics platforms, so direct peers are limited. Revenue of KRW 27.5 billion in 2025 is small compared with large listed companies in the same sector.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 13.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.1 billion in 2023 (up 15.7% year on year), KRW 20.7 billion in 2024 (up 29.0%) and KRW 27.5 billion in 2025 (up 32.8%).

Revenue has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 through 2025. Operating profit grew from KRW 2.58 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.61 billion in 2023, KRW 5.17 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.36 billion in 2025 (up 81.0%), outpacing revenue growth.

The operating margin climbed from 18.6% in 2022 to 22.5% in 2023, 25.0% in 2024 and 34.0% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 2.64 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.53 billion in 2025 (up 39.8%), and net profit including non-controlling interests was disclosed at the same amounts in each year.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 36.6 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 18.4 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 60.1% in 2023 to 50.4% in 2025. Operating cash flow grew from KRW 3.08 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.5 billion in 2025.

Quarterly results have been volatile. Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 6.77 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.43 billion were followed by Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 5.75 billion (down 15.1% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 408 million (down 83.2%).

Revenue then reached a peak of KRW 9.6 billion in Q2 2026 (up 30.1% quarter on quarter), with operating profit of KRW 4.18 billion (up 125.0%), before Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 7.52 billion (down 21.7%) and operating profit of KRW 2.73 billion (down 34.7%).

In the same quarter, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 98.42 million, a turn from the prior-year period.

Operating profit stayed positive while the controlling-shareholder bottom line turned negative, so items below operating profit need to be examined in the quarterly report notes. According to fnguide, first-half 2025 standalone revenue rose 77.8% year on year and operating profit rose 705.6%.

Industry analysis

Demand for Total Soft Bank is tied to investment in maritime and port logistics facilities and in terminal operating efficiency. Container terminal operating systems tend to be adopted when new terminals are built or existing terminals are automated, so quarterly revenue moves with individual projects.

The shipping cycle is shaped by freight rates and fleet investment, and shipping companies' IT spending capacity tends to follow freight levels. On the port side, investment that expands terminal handling capacity continues in step with global trade volumes and the growing size of container vessels.

In Korea, port operation software is contested mainly by specialist vendors, while large IT services groups such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and NAVER expand into logistics platforms.

Compared with large listed companies in the same IT and software sector, Total Soft Bank is small, so swings in its industry cycle show up more visibly in its results.

Revenue peaked at KRW 9.6 billion in Q2 2026 and then fell to KRW 7.52 billion in Q3 2026, a pattern that can be read as consistent with project-based demand. Order backlog and revenue by contract stage are best confirmed from the original disclosures.

Bull factors

Larger disclosed profit base

Operating profit grew from KRW 2.58 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.36 billion in 2025, and the operating margin rose from 18.6% to 34.0%. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 8.53 billion in 2025, up 39.8%. The disclosed record shows three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025.

Cash generation and balance sheet

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 3.08 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.5 billion in 2025. At the end of 2025 the debt-to-equity ratio was 50.4%, with total equity of KRW 36.6 billion against total liabilities of KRW 18.4 billion. The link between profit growth and cash generation can be read as a balance sheet strength.

Port-focused product range

The product range, centered on CATOS, spans container terminal operations, port community systems and shipping systems, all within port logistics. Because overseas sales make up a large share, the company serves demand that differs from the domestic SI market. A narrow product focus also leaves room to widen offerings within the same customer base.

Bear factors

Q3 2026 net loss

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.73 billion rose 12.4% year on year, but net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 98.42 million. Operating profit and bottom-line results moved in opposite directions, which raises a question about how durable the earnings are.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 7.52 billion, below the FY2025 figure of KRW 8.53 billion.

Large quarterly swings

Operating profit in Q4 2025 fell to KRW 408 million, down 83.2% from the prior quarter. After KRW 4.18 billion in Q2 2026, Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 2.73 billion, down 34.7%. Quarterly revenue also fell 21.7% in Q3 2026 to KRW 7.52 billion, so the direction of quarterly results changes often.

Segment concentration and limited scale

A 2021 press report put the Marine Terminal segment at 76.4% of sales, indicating heavy dependence on one segment. At annual revenue of KRW 27.5 billion, the effect of a single contract change on results is large. Compared with large IT services firms, the narrower spread of business lines is a weakness.

Overall view

Total Soft Bank is a small company whose core business is port operation software, and its disclosed revenue, operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders all rose from 2022 through 2025. The operating margin climbed to 34.0% in 2025, confirming a larger profit base in the filings.

By contrast, Q3 2026 operating profit rose year on year while the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, so both earnings durability and items below operating profit need to be examined.

Quarterly results have also swung widely: operating profit fell to KRW 408 million in Q4 2025, recovered to KRW 4.18 billion in Q2 2026 and then declined to KRW 2.73 billion in Q3 2026.

The revenue mix in a 2021 press report was concentrated in container terminal operations, and the latest segment mix should be confirmed from periodic reports.

Compared with historical year-end PER and PBR ranges, valuation multiples sit on the lower side, but because quarterly earnings are volatile, the interpretation of those multiples also moves. The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report, the Q1 2027 quarterly report and large contract disclosures made as they occur.
