Report overview

Sales Growth, Flat Margins at WebCash

WebCash booked record quarterly sales in the third quarter of 2026, but higher costs for commercializing its financial AI services pulled operating income and net income attributable to controlling shareholders below the year-earlier level.

  1. 1

    Q3 2026 sales were KRW 22.3 billion, up 24.0% year on year, while operating income was KRW 3.35 billion, down 7.3%.

  2. 2

    From a first-quarter 2026 low, both sales and operating income rose for two consecutive quarters through the third quarter.

  3. 3

    The annual operating margin slipped from 23.4% in 2022 to 18.1% in 2025, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose 74.1% to KRW 12.5 billion in 2025.

  4. 4

    Eugene Investment & Securities lowered its target price from KRW 10,800 to KRW 9,100 in a November 2026 report and kept its Buy rating.

  5. 5

    The 2026 annual report, due by the end of March 2027, is the next filing that will show fourth-quarter results and the weight of financial AI revenue.

02

Business structure

WebCash is a B2B fintech company whose core products are cash management services (CMS) and financial software for corporates. Since 2025 the company has described itself as a financial AI agent company and is converting its existing CMS and finance and accounting solutions around AI agents.

Its main products include the AI cash assistant AICFO, an SME financial platform, the SERP ERP for small businesses, and branch-related solutions.

The company says it once built internet banking for more than 70% of Korean banks, withdrew from that business nine years ago to improve profitability, and restarted an AI system integration business for financial institutions in 2025.

Its financial AI business consists of five projects pursued in sequence, including agent banking that applies AI agents to corporate banking and an AI transformation project for public-sector treasury work.

NH Nonghyup Bank is building agent banking with the company, and an AI transformation project for a public education finance fund is also under way. Customers are split between public institutions and very large companies on one side and mid-sized and large corporates on the other.

In the third quarter of 2025 the public and very large company segment accounted for 29.3% of sales. Reports said that segment carries lower profitability, and its declining weight helped margins as mid-sized and large corporate sales rose.

Management and brokers attributed growth in the third quarter of 2026 mainly to public-sector and financial institution business. Compared with larger Korean IT services peers such as Samsung SDS and Hyundai Autoever, WebCash is narrower and centered on finance-specific B2B products.

FnGuide data show the largest shareholder group holding 34.30% and treasury shares at 7.85% (last change recorded April 2025).

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales went from KRW 87.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 73.6 billion in 2023, a 16.3% decline, then rose to KRW 74.2 billion in 2024 (up 0.8%) and KRW 74.4 billion in 2025 (up 0.3%), two straight years of gains after the 2023 low. 2022 was the largest year in the period and 2023 the smallest.

Operating income fell from KRW 20.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.7 billion in 2023 (down 33.4%), then moved to KRW 13.9 billion in 2024 (up 1.2%) and KRW 13.5 billion in 2025 (down 3.0%), staying in a narrow band since 2023.

The operating margin went from 23.4% in 2022 to 18.6% in 2023, 18.7% in 2024 and 18.1% in 2025, holding in the high teens since the 2023 step-down.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 5.61 billion in 2023 (down 51.4%) from KRW 11.5 billion in 2022, then recovered to KRW 7.16 billion in 2024 (up 27.6%) and KRW 12.5 billion in 2025 (up 74.1%), the largest year of the four.

Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 12.5 billion in 2025. A 3.0% fall in operating income alongside a 74.1% rise in controlling-shareholder profit suggests non-operating or tax items may have played a role.

At the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 111.2 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 20.2 billion and a debt ratio of 18.2%.

Operating cash flow was KRW 8.59 billion in 2022, KRW 6.96 billion in 2023, KRW 15.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 12.7 billion in 2025, positive in every year shown.

On a quarterly basis, sales were KRW 18 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 18.5 billion in Q4 2025 (up 2.9% quarter on quarter), and a low of KRW 17.8 billion in Q1 2026 (down 3.9%). They then rose to KRW 20.3 billion in Q2 2026 (up 14.2%) and KRW 22.3 billion in Q3 2026 (up 9.9% quarter on quarter and 24.0% year on year).

Operating income went from KRW 3.61 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 3.02 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 2.65 billion in Q1 2026, then recovered to KRW 3.34 billion in Q2 and KRW 3.35 billion in Q3 2026, which was down 7.3% year on year.

Eugene Investment & Securities attributed the year-on-year fall to rising costs for developing and commercializing new financial AI services. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.6 billion in Q3 2026, down 48.3% year on year and 42.4% quarter on quarter. The four quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026 total KRW 11.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The market WebCash serves is B2B software demand for digitizing cash and finance work at banks and public institutions. Demand is shifting toward AX (AI transformation), in which generative AI is built into everyday operations.

In a May 2025 announcement the company presented three strategies: expanding AI-based CMS, converting financial and public-sector systems to AI agents, and upgrading GPU-based infrastructure.

In August 2025 it said it had developed AI agent banking that can be built at 30% or less of the cost of a conventional banking system, a claim made by the company itself. Brokers view CMS and financial AI revenue as entering a period of real growth.

On the cycle, financial AI appears to be in an early commercialization stage. A 2025 report argued that AI CMS needs time before reaching broad adoption.

Against larger Korean IT services peers such as Samsung SDS, Hyundai Autoever and LG CNS, WebCash is far smaller and narrower in scope, and it concentrates on finance-specific B2B products.

Its history of building internet banking for a large share of Korean banks is an asset in the customer base, but re-entering after a withdrawal means competing against established players. Public-sector revenue can also vary from quarter to quarter depending on project timing.

06

Outlook

The business plans the company has disclosed or described fall into three strands. The first is upgrading its CMS step by step into AI CMS. The second is financial and public-sector AI projects, including agent banking with NH Nonghyup Bank and an AI transformation project for a public education finance fund.

The third is winning AI MIS projects and expanding adoption at new banks. Agent banking and the AX projects have been reported as under construction, but completion timing and when they will show up in revenue have not been disclosed.

Eugene Investment & Securities estimated fourth-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 22.6 billion and operating income of KRW 3.5 billion in its November 2026 report.

That is a forecast made before results are announced, and the same report expected record quarterly sales in the fourth quarter and gradually improving profitability. In its November 2026 report, Eugene Investment & Securities cut its target price from KRW 10,800 to KRW 9,100 and maintained its Buy rating.

The broker cited the gap between the share price and its target as the reason for the cut. The weight of financial AI revenue in total sales will be most directly visible in the business segment description of the 2026 annual report.

Fourth-quarter results will be confirmed through the preliminary annual release in early 2027 and the 2026 annual report. The first-quarter 2027 quarterly report will then show whether financial AI costs are easing on a quarterly basis.

Bull factors

Quarterly sales momentum

Q3 2026 sales of KRW 22.3 billion rose 24.0% year on year, which Eugene Investment & Securities described as a record quarter. Growth in public-sector and financial institution business drove the increase. The same broker estimated that fourth-quarter 2026 sales would set another quarterly high.

Financial AI revenue becoming visible

Eugene Investment & Securities assessed that revenue tied to CMS and financial AI is growing in earnest. Agent banking with NH Nonghyup Bank and an AI transformation project for a public education finance fund are under way. The company has said it plans to extend AI-agent conversion across its full product line.

Balance sheet and cash generation

Total equity stood at KRW 111.2 billion at the end of 2025 with a debt ratio of 18.2%. Operating cash flow was positive in each year from 2022 to 2025, reaching KRW 12.7 billion in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose 74.1% to KRW 12.5 billion in 2025.

Bear factors

Stalled margins and cost pressure

Q3 2026 operating income of KRW 3.35 billion fell 7.3% year on year. Rising costs for developing and commercializing new financial AI services were cited as the cause. The annual operating margin declined from 23.4% in 2022 to 18.1% in 2025 and has stayed in the high teens since 2023.

Volatile quarterly controlling-shareholder profit

Q3 2026 net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.6 billion fell 48.3% year on year and 42.4% quarter on quarter. The recovery from KRW 2.49 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 2.78 billion in Q2 reversed in the third quarter. The four quarters through Q3 2026 total KRW 11.3 billion, with wide swings between quarters.

Lower target price and commercialization timing

In a November 2026 report Eugene Investment & Securities lowered its target price from KRW 10,800 to KRW 9,100, citing the gap between the share price and its target. A 2025 report also argued that AI CMS needs time before reaching broad adoption. Whether growth in financial AI revenue translates into margin gains will be settled by quarterly results.

10

Risk factors

Business transition risk

Financial AI services are still in development and early commercialization, so costs can rise before revenue follows. The year-on-year drop in Q3 2026 operating income illustrates this pattern. If agent banking or the AX projects slip, the cost burden could weigh on profit for longer.

Customer and segment concentration risk

In Q3 2025 the public and very large company segment made up 29.3% of sales, and that segment's revenue had fallen 29.8% year on year in the same quarter. Public-sector revenue can swing from quarter to quarter with project timing and budgets. Q3 2026 growth also leans on public-sector and financial institution business, which carries the same exposure.

Small-cap and shareholding structure risk

WebCash is a relatively small listed company, and its largest shareholder group holds 34.30% while treasury shares account for 7.85%. This limits the freely traded float, so price volatility and trading liquidity should be read together. Share price moves can be large around earnings releases and financial AI news.

12

Overall view

WebCash booked record quarterly sales of KRW 22.3 billion in Q3 2026, yet operating income for the same quarter was KRW 3.35 billion, down 7.3% year on year.

On the annual disclosed figures, sales rose for two straight years after the 2023 low, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose 74.1% to KRW 12.5 billion in 2025.

Operating income, however, has stayed between KRW 13.5 billion and KRW 13.9 billion since 2023, and the operating margin fell from 23.4% in 2022 to 18.1% in 2025.

On the business side, the strengths are the broker view that CMS and financial AI revenue is growing and the expansion of public-sector and financial institution business. The burdens are the cost of commercializing financial AI and quarterly swings in public-sector revenue.

Eugene Investment & Securities lowered its target price to KRW 9,100 in November 2026 while keeping its Buy rating, and the same report estimated fourth-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 22.6 billion and operating income of KRW 3.5 billion.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio sits below 1x, so the shares trade under book value of equity. The next checkpoints are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results in early 2027, the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027, and the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report in mid-May 2027. The most important thing to watch is whether financial AI revenue translates into a better operating margin.
