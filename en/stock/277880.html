02

Business structure

TSI designs and manufactures mixing equipment and systems for the electrode process of secondary batteries. The mixing process is the first step of electrode manufacturing, in which active materials are weighed and ground into a liquid slurry for both cathodes and anodes.

The company designs each mixing line to fit the customer's production line before supplying the equipment. The business began in 1996 as Taesung Industrial and built its secondary-battery business into a core operation in the mid-2000s.

Its product lineup includes mixers such as the cathode-dedicated mixer, the Conex mixer and the Solu mixer. A circulating mixer aimed at lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries attaches to existing mixers and is said to cut working time and raise output by roughly twofold.

Key customers include LG Energy Solution and Samsung SDI, with more than 80 domestic and overseas companies cited as customers.

The KRW 26.66 billion contract signed in December 2025 was with LG Energy Solution's Michigan unit in the United States, while the KRW 71.67 billion contract signed in February 2026 has an undisclosed counterparty at the customer's request.

The company also disclosed a supply contract of about KRW 24.1 billion with Japan's DJK. A November 2024 report described plans for a carbon nanotube (CNT) dispersion plant and a research center alongside the new Cheonan factory.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 148.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 261.1 billion in 2023 (up 75.5% year on year) and KRW 272 billion in 2024 (up 4.2%), then fell to KRW 264.7 billion in 2025 (down 2.7%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year for sales and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 4.93 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.99 billion in 2023 (down 59.6%), then rose to KRW 15.6 billion in 2024 (up 684.5%) before dropping to KRW 8.25 billion in 2025 (down 47.2%).

The operating margin was 3.3% in 2022, 0.8% in 2023, 5.7% in 2024 and 3.1% in 2025, with 2024 the highest of the four years.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 3.14 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 4.88 billion in 2023, which was a swing into loss, then turned positive at KRW 29.4 billion in 2024, a swing into profit, before falling 86.8% to KRW 3.87 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 29.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.87 billion in 2025, a different figure from net income attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow was -KRW 34.6 billion in 2023 and -KRW 22 billion in 2024 and turned to KRW 46.9 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 126 billion, of which KRW 125.9 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 177 billion.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 sales of KRW 76.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.6 billion fell in Q4 2025 to sales of KRW 67.9 billion (down 11.3% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 1.36 billion (down 47.8%).

Q1 2026 sales of KRW 50.9 billion were the lowest in the window (down 25.1%), yet operating profit rose to KRW 3.8 billion (up 179.7%). Q2 2026 sales reached KRW 116.6 billion (up 129.3%) with operating profit of KRW 7.09 billion (up 86.6%), the largest quarter in the window.

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 98.6 billion (down 15.5% quarter on quarter, up 28.8% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 5.18 billion (down 27.0% quarter on quarter, up 98.9% year on year).

In the same quarter, net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 8.72 billion, a swing into loss both quarter on quarter and year on year. With operating profit positive, the gap to the net loss calls for checking non-operating items and income tax in the quarterly report.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for the four quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 totaled KRW 6.26 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Mixing sits at the front of the electrode process, so equipment performance affects yields in later steps. Scattered powder or trapped air lowers yields, which is why the process is regarded as technically demanding.

The company differentiates itself by designing customer equipment in three-dimensional models to shorten production-line build times.

The order disclosures show contracts in September 2025 (KRW 27.51 billion), October 2025 (KRW 28.36 billion), December 2025 (KRW 26.66 billion), February 2026 (KRW 71.67 billion) and March 2026 (KRW 29.2 billion), but the sizes vary widely.

Because contract values and periods differ from deal to deal, the timing of revenue recognition is also uneven across quarters. Demand for secondary-battery equipment follows battery cell makers' capacity expansion schedules and demand for electric vehicles and energy storage systems.

The current position of the industry cycle is hard to pin down with numbers, so customer capacity announcements and investment plans serve as the next reference points. Overseas expansion appears in the DJK contract and the review of a U.S. local delivery setup.

Domestic customer concentration is high, so results are sensitive to the investment timing of large cell makers such as LG Energy Solution.

Outlook

The confirmable schedule the company has laid out consists of contract periods and a capacity expansion plan. Its order backlog stood at KRW 507 billion as of September 2024.

The KRW 26.66 billion contract signed in December 2025 runs to December 30, 2027, the KRW 29.2 billion contract signed in March 2026 runs to February 15, 2027, and the KRW 71.67 billion contract signed in February 2026 runs to September 30, 2027.

The KRW 71.67 billion contract is paid 30% as a deposit, 60% and 5% as interim payments, and 5% as the balance.

A November 2024 report said the new Cheonan factory in Chungcheong was nearing completion around June 2025, with annual mixing-equipment capacity planned to rise from KRW 250 billion to KRW 550 billion after completion.

Whether the new factory is running and what capacity it actually delivers should be checked in later disclosures and the annual report. The same report also mentioned plans for a CNT plant and research center.

Whether the sharp rise in Q2 2026 sales and the Q3 2026 decline reflect the timing of revenue recognition on large contracts is a point to check in the quarterly sales breakdown.

The company has also noted that contracts may be changed or terminated, so the backlog and its link to sales should be reviewed at each contract end date.

Bull factors

Continuity of order disclosures

Following KRW 27.51 billion in September 2025, mixing-system contracts of KRW 28.36 billion, KRW 26.66 billion, KRW 71.67 billion and KRW 29.2 billion were disclosed through March 2026. One counterparty included LG Energy Solution's Michigan unit in the United States.

The continuing disclosures show that new equipment orders have not stopped, but contract values and revenue timing differ deal by deal, so how quickly they reach earnings should be checked in upcoming reports.

Balance sheet improvement

The debt ratio fell from 416.4% in 2022 to 140.5% in 2025. Operating cash flow turned from -KRW 22 billion in 2024 to KRW 46.9 billion in 2025. A news report that the company is reviewing early redemption of RCPS after the convertible bond repayment points to a priority on balance sheet strength.

Sales recovery in Q2 2026

Q2 2026 sales reached KRW 116.6 billion, up 129.3% from the prior quarter, with operating profit of KRW 7.09 billion. Operating profit had risen from KRW 3.8 billion in Q1 2026.

A 2024 report that the new Cheonan factory could substantially expand annual mixing-equipment capacity is cited as a medium-term growth factor.

Concentration and limited contract visibility

Its main customers are large cell makers such as LG Energy Solution and Samsung SDI, so demand is tied to the investment schedules of a few companies.

Risk factors

Order execution and revenue timing

Because order sizes vary widely, quarterly sales depend on when large contracts are recognized. The surge in Q2 2026 sales and the decline in Q3 point to this pattern. Since contracts can be changed or terminated, each contract end date should be checked against actual revenue.

Financial leverage

Total liabilities of KRW 177 billion exceed total equity of KRW 126 billion at the end of 2025. Operating cash flow was negative in 2023 and 2024 and turned positive in 2025. Whether that positive turn holds in the next annual report is the key point for financial risk.

Execution of new business and capacity investment

The Cheonan factory and CNT plant plans were reported in November 2024, and completion timing and investment scale may change. The timing of any contribution from new businesses is also uncertain. Heavier investment could conflict with the balance sheet improvement under way.

Overall view

TSI supplies mixing systems for secondary batteries and has continued to disclose orders ranging from KRW 27.51 billion to KRW 71.67 billion since September 2025. Sales peaked at KRW 272 billion in 2024, and both sales and operating profit declined in 2025.

Q2 2026 sales of KRW 116.6 billion were the largest quarter from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026, but Q3 2026 swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.72 billion.

On the financial side, the debt ratio fell from 416.4% in 2022 to 140.5% in 2025 and operating cash flow turned positive in 2025, which are strengths. Against that, total liabilities exceed total equity, main customers are concentrated among large cell makers, and some counterparties are undisclosed.

The share price trades below net asset value, but profit volatility keeps the earnings multiple highly sensitive. The 2026 annual report will confirm full-year operating margin and cash flow, and later quarterly reports will show revenue recognition by contract.
