02

Business structure

TSI makes mixing systems used in the electrode process of secondary batteries, and it was founded in 2011 and is listed on KOSDAQ. As of September 2026 it had 247 employees.

Its core line is mixing equipment that blends electrode materials uniformly, and because the contract value per system is large, quarterly revenue moves sharply depending on when contracts are booked.

Recent press reports said the end date of a KRW 32.5 billion mixing system supply contract with an overseas secondary battery maker was extended by one month to December 31. Revenue recognition timing and the composition of that contract amount have not been confirmed before the periodic filing.

Revenue by segment and by product line was not verified in this review, so no figures are given. Customer composition and customer concentration also need to be checked against filings. Competition comes from domestic and foreign mixing equipment makers, but market share figures could not be confirmed.

Because this is an equipment business, orders tend to cluster around customers' capacity expansion and process investment schedules, so quarterly revenue flow swings more than annual totals.

On a filed basis, revenue was largest in 2024 at KRW 272 billion and smallest in 2022 at KRW 148.8 billion, which shows the volatility of the business structure. Given the concentration on mixing equipment, new customer wins and product diversification are the medium-term points to watch.

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 148.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 261.1 billion in 2023 (up 75.5% year on year) and KRW 272 billion in 2024 (up 4.2%), then fell to KRW 264.7 billion in 2025 (down 2.7%), with 2024 the largest year in the 2022–2025 span.

Operating profit fell from KRW 4.93 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.99 billion in 2023 (down 59.6%), then rose to KRW 15.6 billion in 2024 (up 684.5%) before declining to KRW 8.25 billion in 2025 (down 47.2%). Operating margin moved through 3.3% in 2022, 0.8% in 2023, 5.7% in 2024 and 3.1% in 2025.

In 2024, operating profit grew far faster than revenue, but the cost structure or one-off items behind that improvement were not confirmed in this review.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 4.88 billion in 2023 from a profit in 2022, returned to a profit of KRW 29.4 billion in 2024, and fell to KRW 3.87 billion in 2025 (down 86.8%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 29.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.87 billion in 2025, close to the controlling-shareholder figures in both years.

By quarter, Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 76.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 2.6 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 2.99 billion weakened to Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 67.9 billion (down 11.3% from the prior quarter), operating profit of KRW 1.36 billion (down 47.8%) and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 715 million (down 76.1%).

Q1 2026 revenue fell to KRW 50.9 billion (down 25.1%), while operating profit rose to KRW 3.8 billion (up 179.7%) and controlling-shareholder net income rose to KRW 9.08 billion (up 1,170.2%).

Q2 2026 was the peak of the recent run, with revenue of KRW 116.6 billion (up 129.3%) and operating profit of KRW 7.09 billion (up 86.6%), while controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 5.19 billion (down 42.8%).

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 98.6 billion (down 15.5% from the prior quarter and up 28.8% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 5.18 billion (down 27.0% quarter on quarter and up 98.9% year on year), but the controlling-shareholder result was a net loss of KRW 8.72 billion, a swing into loss from the prior year.

Why operating profit stayed positive while the controlling-shareholder result turned negative may reflect non-operating items, income tax or non-controlling interest effects, but the specific cause was not confirmed in this review.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 9.09 billion in 2022 and -KRW 34.6 billion in 2023 to -KRW 22 billion in 2024, then turned positive at KRW 46.9 billion in 2025, while the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 416.4% in 2022 to 140.5% in 2025.
