02

Business structure

Youngkwang Ykmc was founded in 2012 as a semiconductor parts company and carries on the business of its predecessor Youngkwang Metal, established in 1989, producing precision parts for semiconductor and display manufacturing equipment.

Its core products are chambers and lids used in semiconductor deposition and etching equipment, and it runs precision machining of large metal parts, special surface treatments such as plasma anodizing and electroless nickel plating, ultra-high-vacuum precision cleaning, and assembly within a single production system.

Reporting segments are semiconductor parts, precision glass manufacturing equipment, and other. Backlog at the end of September 2026 was about KRW 53.6 billion, consisting of roughly KRW 40.2 billion in semiconductors, KRW 10.6 billion in precision glass equipment, and KRW 2.8 billion in other.

The semiconductor share of revenue rose from 58.2% in 2025 to 63.5% in the first half of 2026. Precision glass equipment revenue grew from about KRW 600 million in 2023 to KRW 7.5 billion in 2025, and reached KRW 6.2 billion in the first half of 2026, or 16.1% of total revenue.

Reported major customers include ASML, Applied Materials, and Lam Research among global semiconductor equipment makers. Overseas revenue was KRW 27.3 billion in 2025, or 52.9% of the total, with the United States, Malaysia, and Singapore as the main markets.

The company highlights its in-house process chain, which links machining, surface treatment, cleaning, and inspection to manage delivery times and quality, and it is also expanding into adjacent equipment areas such as precision glass manufacturing equipment.

03

Recent trends

On a DART filing basis, cumulative third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 23.1 billion, up 74.8% year on year. Over the same period, operating profit rose to KRW 4.02 billion, up 322.0%, and net income rose to KRW 2.82 billion, up 304.3%, so profit growth ran well ahead of revenue growth.

Recent press reports attribute the profitability improvement to a product mix shifted toward higher-value chambers and fixed-cost absorption from a recovery in plant utilization. Full-year 2025 revenue was KRW 51.6 billion, up about 39% from KRW 37.1 billion in 2024.

Full-year 2025 operating profit was KRW 4.476 billion and net income was KRW 2.708 billion, a swing from operating losses recorded in 2023 and 2024. The debt-to-equity ratio at the end of the first half of 2026 was 138.8%, lower than 147.0% at the end of 2025.

Press reports put the first-half 2026 operating margin in the 20% range. The stock hit an intraday high of 16,500 won on its first trading day, November 16, 2026, gave back those gains, and closed at 9,200 won, below the 11,000-won offer price.

As of November 17, 2026, the stock traded at 8,420 won, down 8.48% from the prior day, with a market capitalization of KRW 121.9 billion and trading value of KRW 80.3 billion.

04

Outlook

The company allocated KRW 15 billion of the IPO proceeds to facility funding, including KRW 4 billion for purchasing a third plant in 2027 and KRW 7 billion for a fourth plant in 2028. A new electroless nickel plating line and precision machining equipment were each allotted KRW 2 billion.

Management expects that mass production of 12 new chamber types by the first half of 2027 could add more than KRW 10 billion in revenue. Revenue from lithography equipment parts is targeted at more than KRW 10 billion in 2027.

Management expects semiconductor chamber shipments to rise from about 3,270 units in 2026 to more than 6,000 units in 2027. Institutional demand forecasting drew a 1,163.35-to-1 competition ratio, but only 7.21% of applications committed to a mandatory holding period, leaving a limited amount of long-term held shares.

Shares free to trade on listing day total 5,041,041 shares, or 34.81% of the total, and the tradable ratio could rise to 47.12% after one month and 52.82% after three months.

On the business side, the next checkpoints are utilization after the expansion, the change in the semiconductor revenue share, and the fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results.

05

Bull factors

Sharp rise in operating profit

On a DART basis, cumulative third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.02 billion rose 322.0% year on year, well above the 74.8% revenue growth. A company that posted operating losses in 2023 and 2024 turned profitable in 2025, which adds weight to the trend.

Backlog of about KRW 53.6 billion at the end of September 2026 is presented as a visibility indicator for upcoming quarters.

Semiconductor backlog and lithography parts growth

Backlog at the end of September 2026 was about KRW 53.6 billion, of which roughly KRW 40.2 billion was in semiconductors. The company said it is expanding its core chamber lineup from 10 types to 22 types.

Lithography equipment parts revenue rose from KRW 3.26 billion in 2025 to KRW 3.9 billion in the first half of 2026, pointing to demand for next-generation process parts.

Overseas sales and diversification

Overseas revenue was KRW 27.3 billion in 2025, or 52.9% of the total, with the United States at KRW 13.3 billion, Malaysia at KRW 6.6 billion, and Singapore at KRW 5.4 billion. Precision glass equipment revenue grew to KRW 7.5 billion in 2025, forming a revenue source outside semiconductors. With overseas sales above half of the total, the company is presented as less dependent on the domestic market.

06

Bear factors

Renewed concentration in semiconductors

The semiconductor share of revenue climbed again from 58.2% in 2025 to 63.5% in the first half of 2026. The company has said that if plant utilization falls on a weaker downstream market, operating profit could decline by more than revenue, causing a sharp deterioration in operating margin. The structure leaves results sensitive to customers' capital spending and order volumes.

Customer concentration and expansion burden

According to IB industry reporting, six key customers account for more than 77% of revenue. Broadening the customer base is cited as a challenge, and the pace of winning new customers could determine earnings volatility.

In 2020 the company invested KRW 24 billion in its second plant, but revenue from the new equipment took time to materialize, raising the depreciation burden.

Post-listing share price and supply pressure

The first-day close of 9,200 won on November 16, 2026 fell below the 11,000-won offer price, and the stock traded at 8,420 won on November 17, 2026, down 8.48% from the prior day.

Only 7.21% of applications committed to a long-term holding period, so supply pressure from increased tradable shares after listing is being cited. The tradable ratio could rise to 47.12% after one month and 52.82% after three months.

07

Risk factors

Macro

With overseas sales at 52.9% of revenue, the company is exposed to export conditions, currency movements, and trade policy changes in its main export markets. U.S. sales of KRW 13.3 billion are the largest single overseas item, so changes in trade conditions with the United States could have a direct effect. A global slowdown that weakens equipment investment sentiment could spread to overseas demand.

Industry

Semiconductor equipment demand is highly volatile and follows the capital spending cycles of customers. The company has said results can shift with changes in customer order volumes. Because expansion and new plant investments are proceeding, a drop in utilization could raise the fixed-cost burden.

Company and supply

The debt-to-equity ratio was 147.0% at the end of 2025, and as expansion funds are deployed, borrowing and depreciation burdens could rise again. Revenue is concentrated in six key customers, so an order change from any one of them could feed into earnings volatility.

Rising tradable shares after listing and a low lock-up commitment ratio are factors that could amplify share price volatility.

08

Overall view

Youngkwang Ykmc moved from operating losses in 2023 and 2024 to profitability in 2025, and cumulative third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose 322.0% year on year.

Margin indicators improved clearly, but the semiconductor share of revenue at 63.5% in the first half of 2026 leaves sensitivity to industry conditions and customer orders high.

Backlog of about KRW 53.6 billion at the end of September 2026 and growth in precision glass equipment are cited as evidence of diversification. On the other side, revenue concentration in six key customers, overseas sales at 52.9%, and depreciation from large-scale expansion are sources of earnings volatility.

The stock fell below the offer price on its first trading day and traded at 8,420 won as of November 17, 2026, and the increase in tradable shares after listing is a supply-side variable. How utilization and the semiconductor revenue share evolve after the expansion is the next checkpoint for the business structure.
