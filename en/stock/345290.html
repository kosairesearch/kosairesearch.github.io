02

Business structure

Youngkwang Ykmc machines precision parts such as chambers and lids used in semiconductor and display manufacturing equipment, and performs specialty surface treatments such as anodizing and electroless nickel plating along with ultra-high-vacuum (UHV) precision cleaning.

Its main competitive edge is a single in-house production line that links precision machining, surface treatment, cleaning and assembly. Semiconductor equipment parts form the core of its revenue, accounting for 58.2% in 2025 and rising again to 63.5% in the first half of 2026.

The order backlog at the end of September 2026 was KRW 53.6 billion, of which the semiconductor segment accounted for KRW 40.2 billion. The precision glass manufacturing equipment segment accounted for KRW 10.6 billion and other products for KRW 2.8 billion.

Customers include global equipment makers such as ASML, Applied Materials and Lam Research. The company has said it is expanding supply of core components for EUV and High-NA EUV equipment.

It is also extending into precision glass manufacturing equipment for displays, specialty surface treatment for aerospace, and solid oxide fuel cell (SOFC) components. Overseas sales were about 53% in 2025, with the United States, Malaysia and Singapore as key export markets. Among the four peer companies selected by the lead underwriter for IPO pricing, the average PER was 22.91x.

03

Recent trends

Based on DART-disclosed results, cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 23.1 billion, up 74.8% year on year. Operating profit rose 322.0% to KRW 4.02 billion, and net income rose 304.3% to KRW 2.82 billion. Profit growth outpaced revenue growth by a wide margin.

The company attributes the improvement to a shift toward higher-value chamber parts and to fixed-cost absorption as factory utilization recovered. The turnaround began in 2025. After operating losses of KRW 6.3 billion in 2023 and KRW 4.7 billion in 2024, operating profit reached KRW 4.476 billion in 2025.

The balance sheet also improved. The debt ratio fell from 147.0% at the end of 2025 to 138.8% at the end of the first half of 2026, and the current ratio rose from 102.1% to 111.9%.

According to recent press reports, the shares closed at KRW 9,200 on the first trading day, November 16, 2026, 16.36% below the KRW 11,000 offer price.

The institutional book-building drew a 1,163.35-to-1 competitive ratio and the retail subscription drew 1,342.66-to-1, with subscription deposits of KRW 4.2461 trillion.

At the time of writing, the share price was KRW 5,820 (down 5.67% from the prior day), giving a market capitalization of KRW 84.3 billion, so the gap between the offer price and the market price persists.

04

Outlook

The next phase depends on fourth-quarter 2026 results and on the effect of capacity expansion from 2027. The company set a 2026 revenue target of KRW 75.0 billion, while cumulative revenue through the third quarter was KRW 23.1 billion, so reaching the target requires a sharp increase in the fourth quarter.

Full-year results will be disclosed in the 2026 annual business report. Growth rests on three axes. First, the company plans to expand its semiconductor chamber line from 10 to 22 types and raise chamber output from 3,270 units in 2026 to more than 6,000 units in 2027.

Second, it targets EUV-related lithography component sales of at least KRW 10.0 billion in 2027. Third, it plans to expand mass production of aerospace surface-treatment parts from 2027 and is pursuing commercialization of SOFC components.

Expansion is funded by KRW 15.0 billion of the net IPO proceeds of about KRW 19.8 billion, earmarked for facilities, including KRW 4.0 billion for the third plant purchase in 2027 and KRW 7.0 billion for the fourth plant purchase in 2028.

The KRW 53.6 billion order backlog as of September 30, 2026 provides revenue visibility, with the semiconductor segment's KRW 40.2 billion at the center. Whether utilization holds after the new capacity comes online will remain the key variable for operating margins.

Bull factors

Sharp rise in nine-month profit

Cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 23.1 billion, up 74.8% year on year. Operating profit rose 322.0% to KRW 4.02 billion, and net income rose 304.3% to KRW 2.82 billion. Profit growth well ahead of revenue growth indicates a stronger earnings structure in the disclosed figures.

Supply record with global toolmakers and backlog

The order backlog at the end of September 2026 was KRW 53.6 billion, with the semiconductor segment accounting for KRW 40.2 billion of it. The company says it supplies parts to global equipment makers including ASML, Applied Materials and Lam Research after passing their quality qualifications. Expanding supply of core parts for EUV and High-NA EUV equipment is also cited as a supportive factor.

Balance sheet improvement and higher-value mix

The debt ratio fell from 147.0% at the end of 2025 to 138.8% at the end of the first half of 2026. The current ratio rose from 102.1% to 111.9% over the same period. Following the 2025 return to profit, financial burden appears to be easing, which points to more room for funding than before the listing. The company says its product mix is shifting toward higher-value chamber parts.

06

Bear factors

Share price below IPO price after listing

The IPO price was KRW 11,000, but the first-day close was KRW 9,200, 16.36% lower. The share price has continued to decline, and at the time of writing it stood at KRW 5,820, with a market capitalization of KRW 84.3 billion.

Long-term lock-up commitments covered only 7.21% of the offered shares, which limits long-term holding demand and is cited as a factor behind the weak market price. The gap between the IPO price and the market price does not currently track the improvement in reported results.

Loss history and depreciation burden

The company posted operating losses of KRW 6.3 billion in 2023 and KRW 4.7 billion in 2024. It invested KRW 24.0 billion in a second plant in 2020, but revenue from the new equipment took time to materialize, which raised the depreciation burden.

The return to operating profit in 2025 covers only one year, so the durability of earnings is still supported by a short track record.

Semiconductor concentration and utilization sensitivity

The semiconductor segment's share of revenue was 59.2% in 2023, 63.3% in 2024 and 58.2% in 2025, before rising again to 63.5% in the first half of 2026.

The company has said that if factory utilization falls as the downstream market weakens, operating profit would decline faster than revenue and margins could deteriorate sharply. Results are highly sensitive to the capital spending and order volumes of key customers.

07

Risk factors

Capacity and new business execution

Whether facility investments, including the third plant purchase in 2027 for KRW 4.0 billion and the fourth plant purchase in 2028 for KRW 7.0 billion, come online as planned is the central question. Additional KRW 2.0 billion allocations each go to a new plating line and precision machining equipment.

If targets of at least KRW 10.0 billion in EUV lithography sales and more than 6,000 chamber units in 2027 slip, the benefit of expansion would be delayed. Aerospace parts mass production, scheduled to expand from 2027, is also a timing variable.

Policy, geopolitics, FX

Because overseas customers, including those in the United States, Malaysia and Singapore, account for a high share of sales, the company is exposed to changes in trade policy. Shifts in export controls or tariffs on semiconductor equipment and parts could alter customers' order timing and direction. Movements in the won exchange rate also affect the won value of export revenue and profitability.

Ownership and share supply

Free float at listing was 34.81% of shares outstanding. If lock-ups on some financial and professional investors expire, the tradable ratio could rise to 47.12% one month later and 52.82% three months later. Shares held by the largest shareholder and related parties are restricted from sale for 30 months. Share supply entering the market at lock-up expiry could weigh on the share price.

08

Overall view

Youngkwang Ykmc's disclosed cumulative figures for the first three quarters of 2026 show revenue of KRW 23.1 billion, operating profit of KRW 4.02 billion and net income of KRW 2.82 billion, all up sharply from the same period a year earlier.

The improvement in earnings capacity, from operating losses in 2023 and 2024 to a return to profit in 2025, is visible in the reported numbers. The KRW 53.6 billion order backlog, expanding supply of EUV and chamber parts, and a lower debt ratio are supportive factors.

On the other side, the share price remains below the KRW 11,000 IPO price, the semiconductor segment's 63.5% revenue share and the sensitivity of margins to utilization remain weak points. The return to profit in 2025 covers only one year, so the durability of earnings is still supported by a short track record.

The timing of capacity investments, the achievement of 2027 new-product revenue targets and the share supply from lock-up expiry are the variables that will shape the next phase. This summary is a qualitative overview for information only and does not assign a rating or target price.
