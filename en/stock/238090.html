02

Business structure

Ndfos was founded in 2010 as a maker of functional double-sided adhesive tapes and window films and has been listed on KOSDAQ since 2016.

Its core business is double-sided tape for mobile devices supplied to global handset makers, which generated KRW 38.6 billion, or 76.6% of revenue, in 2025 according to press reports. Window film sales fell to KRW 5.4 billion in 2025 from KRW 10.8 billion in 2023, and the segment is export-oriented.

Of 2025 revenue of KRW 50.4 billion, product sales were KRW 39.4 billion, merchandise sales KRW 4.7 billion, and financial asset income KRW 5.6 billion, so revenue includes investment income.

In new businesses, Ndfos developed insulation sheets for EV batteries and acrylic foam tapes for vehicle exterior assembly, and began supplying Volvo's US-bound vehicles in May 2024.

The company said it has since expanded the target models to include the Tesla Cybertruck, KIA EV9 and Hyundai Motor Ioniq 6 for the US market.

Its wholly owned private equity fund manager subsidiary, established in April 2022, was reported to have generated KRW 5.6 billion in revenue and KRW 2.6 billion in net income in 2025. In mobile tape, the company emphasizes share gains through cooperation with Chinese handset makers.

Peers in the chemicals sector include Korea Petro Chemical IND, Miwon Specialty Chemical and ENF Technology, but product lines differ, so direct comparison is limited.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 89.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 48.9 billion in 2023, a 45.5% decline, then rose 7.3% to KRW 52.5 billion in 2024 and eased 3.9% to KRW 50.4 billion in 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating losses of KRW 1.38 billion in 2022 and KRW 4.12 billion in 2023 were followed by an operating profit of KRW 1.95 billion in 2024, a turn to profit. Operating profit rose to KRW 4.81 billion in 2025, up 146.8%, and the operating margin moved from 3.7% in 2024 to 9.5% in 2025.

Controlling shareholders' net income turned to KRW 5.24 billion in 2025, after a controlling shareholders' net loss of KRW 31 billion in 2024. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 5.25 billion in 2025, a separate figure from controlling shareholders' net income.

The company cited lower cost of sales and selling and administrative expenses, together with higher financial income and lower financial costs, as the main drivers of the 2025 change.

Press reports said a financial asset valuation loss of KRW 29.1 billion in 2024 narrowed to KRW 2.2 billion in 2025, with a KRW 2.6 billion disposal gain. The 2025 recovery therefore reflects both cost reduction and financial items.

Quarterly operating profit declined from KRW 1.74 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 1.58 billion in the fourth quarter to KRW 1.29 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then turned to an operating loss of KRW 1.48 billion in the second quarter.

The third quarter of 2026 posted an operating loss of KRW 254 million and a controlling shareholders' net loss of KRW 1.81 billion, a swing to loss from a year earlier. The sum of controlling shareholders' net losses over the last four quarters is KRW 2.38 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for mobile-device tape is tied to the launch cycle of new smartphone models. A March 2025 press report said Chinese handset makers launched many new models, which greatly increased tape supply volumes, and that battery-fixing tape is applied to large areas, creating stable demand.

In electric vehicle parts, the core products are insulation sheets for secondary batteries and acrylic foam tapes, linked to US-bound models from Volvo, Tesla, KIA and Hyundai Motor.

Mobile demand therefore depends on new-model launch timing, while EV demand depends on US EV sales and the production timing of each model.

A corporate information summary states that demand for high-performance products is expected to expand with AI demand, although this is a summary rather than backed by market-size figures. Industry cycle indicators that would show where the sector stands are limited.

Large petrochemical names in the sector, such as LG Chem, Lotte Chemical and Hanwha Solutions, have different raw material structures and customer bases, so they are not a clean benchmark for price cycles.

Ndfos is a specialized company focused on mobile double-sided tape and window film, which sets it apart from large chemical groups.

08

Bull factors

Operating turnaround in 2025

Operating profit was KRW 4.81 billion in 2025, up 146.8% year on year, and the operating margin rose from 3.7% in 2024 to 9.5% in 2025. Controlling shareholders' net income of KRW 5.24 billion reversed a KRW 31 billion net loss in 2024.

The company attributed the gain mainly to lower cost of sales and selling and administrative expenses. Bulls point to cost-structure improvement appearing alongside, not only through, financial items.

Financial capacity and cash generation

Operating cash flow was KRW 10.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.35 billion in 2025, both positive. Press reports said separate-basis cash and equivalents rose 83.1% to KRW 14.9 billion. A balance sheet with lower dependence on outside funding underpins investment capacity, which bulls cite as a positive.

Diversifying into EV component customers

Development of insulation sheets for EV batteries and acrylic foam tapes extended applications to US-bound models from Volvo, Tesla, KIA and Hyundai Motor. Supply to Volvo's US-bound vehicles began in May 2024. The company projected 2030 revenue of KRW 10 billion to KRW 15 billion based on models in development. Bulls cite efforts to build revenue outside mobile devices as a medium-term positive.

09

Bear factors

Quarterly operating losses return

Operating losses of KRW 1.48 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 254 million in the third quarter mark two consecutive loss-making quarters. Controlling shareholders' net loss of KRW 1.81 billion in the same quarter was a swing to loss, and the four-quarter total is KRW 2.38 billion. Bears question whether the 2025 improvement can be sustained.

Reliance on financial income

Press reports said the 2025 improvement reflected both higher financial income and lower financial costs. Revenue of KRW 50.4 billion includes KRW 5.6 billion of financial asset income.

Reports also said routine development spending in the core tape business fell from KRW 3.0 billion to KRW 1.7 billion, which raises concern about core investment. Until core profit is shown apart from financial swings, bears treat this as a negative.

Window film decline and governance litigation

Window film sales halved from KRW 10.8 billion in 2023 to KRW 5.4 billion in 2025. Press reports say litigation linked to an alleged breach of trust charge against a former executive of the largest shareholder, filed in 2023, is still ongoing.

Board composition changed at the 2026 annual general meeting, but analysts say litigation risk remains. Bears treat this as a negative.

10

Risk factors

Dilution and capital deployment

Ndfos raised funds through a KRW 10 billion rights offering in February 2026 and a KRW 11 billion convertible bond in March 2026, and combined potential new shares total 10.78 million. The fifth convertible bond would add 3,831,417 shares on full conversion, equal to 12.57% of total shares.

As of a May 2026 report, the targets for the KRW 20 billion acquisition fund had not been set. If acquisitions underperform, dilution could remain without a matching earnings gain.

Governance and litigation

Litigation tied to the largest shareholder is ongoing, and management and board composition changed after the founder sold control. Press reports say the company is run by a sole CEO.

The investment-partnership-centered shareholder base and a fund dissolution in which some shares were released without a shareholders' meeting are also cited as governance variables.

Concentration and customer dependence

Mobile double-sided tape accounted for 76.6% of 2025 revenue, so earnings track new mobile model launches and particular customer volumes. EV components have different production start dates by model, which makes the timing of their contribution uncertain, according to company materials. If diversification is slow, concentration risk persists.

Overall view

Ndfos turned from loss to profit in 2025, with operating profit of KRW 4.81 billion, up 146.8%, and controlling shareholders' net income of KRW 5.24 billion. Revenue fell 3.9% to KRW 50.4 billion, and the profit gain came from both cost reductions and financial items.

The balance sheet looks stable, with a 13.8% debt ratio and positive operating cash flow.

However, operating losses of KRW 1.48 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 254 million in the third quarter mark two consecutive loss-making quarters, and the four-quarter sum of controlling shareholders' net losses is KRW 2.38 billion.

EV components and the use of acquisition funds are not yet clearly visible in reported results. Potential new shares of 10.78 million and governance litigation are also variables to weigh.

Bull points are the 2025 turnaround and financial capacity; bear points are the return to quarterly losses, reliance on financial items and governance risk. The next checkpoints are operating profit trends in the 2026 annual report and the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report.
