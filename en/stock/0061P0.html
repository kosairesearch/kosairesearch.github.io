02

Business structure

H Steel is a metal products manufacturer founded in October 2021 and headquartered in Eumseong County, Chungbuk. Its main product is color-coated steel, made by applying surface treatment such as painting to steel sheet to add color and function.

Demand comes mainly from applications where appearance matters, including home appliance exteriors and interior and exterior building materials. As a result, sales depend on the production plans of finished-goods makers and on construction activity.

Raw material costs for cold-rolled and hot-rolled steel make up a large share of the cost base, so how far raw material price changes can be passed on to customers is the key variable for profitability.

Large steelmakers such as Hyundai Steel Company have advantages in production scale and raw material procurement, while smaller processors tend to differentiate through flexibility in order sizes and close ties with specific customers.

The company went through the KONEX listing process with Yuanta Securities as its designated advisor. As of November 6, 2026, its paid-in capital was KRW 5,093 million. Based on the listing materials, the business is concentrated in a single product line, so segment-level revenue shares are not separated.

04

Outlook

H Steel's earnings trajectory depends on how far its operating margin, currently in the low 1% range, can improve. Room for margin improvement can be tracked through operating indicators such as the pass-through of raw material costs, capacity utilization, and the share of higher-value color steel.

Given that liabilities exceed ten times equity, the key financial questions are whether capital raised through the listing strengthens the balance sheet and whether borrowings decline.

KONEX is a market designed for growth-stage small and mid-sized companies, so trading volume and information accumulation are more limited than on the KOSPI market where large steelmakers are listed.

On the other hand, the listing brings stronger disclosure obligations, so quarterly results and financial structure will be published on a regular basis.

On the demand side, appliance and construction activity drive demand for color steel, so production and construction starts in those sectors act as external variables for revenue. Changes in steel sheet prices can also move margins depending on how quickly selling prices are adjusted. These factors will be verified in numbers through quarterly disclosures starting in 2026.

Bull factors

Specialized products tied to appliance and building demand

H Steel focuses on color-coated steel supplied to appliance exteriors and building materials, where appearance matters. Sales of KRW 53,481 million in 2025 point to an established production base and customer relationships. The clear demand base for a specialized material can be viewed as a structural simplicity of the business.

Access to capital markets through the listing

The KONEX listing on November 27, 2026 gives the company a route to raise funds in public markets. With equity of KRW 2,590 million, the listing can serve as a means to reinforce the balance sheet. Regular quarterly disclosure as a listed company also raises the transparency of financial information.

Clear governance from concentrated ownership

The largest shareholder Hong Seong-bu holds 41.23%, and the six major shareholders hold about 72.7% combined. Concentrated ownership can be read as giving a clear decision-making structure. At the same time, it is often discussed alongside limited influence for minority shareholders.

Bear factors

Thin operating margin

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 696 million on sales of KRW 53,481 million, an operating margin of about 1.3%. Net income was KRW 104 million, leaving a net margin below 1%. If raw material cost increases cannot be passed on to selling prices quickly, profit can decline easily.

High debt burden

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 30,819 million, about 11.9 times equity of KRW 2,590 million. With year-end equity below paid-in capital, interest and repayment burdens can amplify swings in profit.

Even with capital raised through the listing, the extent to which debt declines will determine financial stability.

Liquidity constraints of the KONEX market

KONEX is designed for growth-stage small and mid-sized companies, and trading volume and analyst coverage are more limited than on KOSPI. Major shareholders hold about 72.7% of listed shares, so the float immediately after listing may be limited. With a narrower base of market participants, price discovery may also be weaker.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Demand for color steel is linked to appliance production and construction activity. Rising interest rates or an economic slowdown that reduces construction starts and appliance output could lead to lower sales.

Because the debt burden is high, interest rate changes would feed into net income more heavily through interest expense.

Raw materials

Costs change when the price of the main inputs, cold-rolled and hot-rolled steel, moves. If selling price adjustments lag behind cost changes, operating margin could narrow further. The ability to pass through raw material costs may be more limited than at large steelmakers.

Listing and liquidity

Right after listing, the tradable float is set by lock-up conditions for major shareholders. Limited trading volume on KONEX can amplify price volatility. How reliably the company operates its disclosure system after listing will also influence market confidence.

Overall view

H Steel is a color steel maker with 2025 sales of KRW 53,481 million, and its operating margin of about 1.3% leaves limited profit cushion. Liabilities of about 11.9 times equity and year-end equity below paid-in capital make financial stability a key point to monitor.

Strengths discussed include specialized products tied to appliance and building demand, access to capital markets through the listing, and clear governance from concentrated ownership. Weaknesses discussed include a thin operating margin, a high debt burden, and liquidity constraints in the KONEX market.

The listing date is November 27, 2026, and because the company is still pre-listing, no market price data exists yet. Operating margin improvement, the pass-through of raw material costs, and changes in debt levels are the indicators that quarterly disclosures will verify.
