Report overview

A City Gas Holding Company Driven by Investment Results

INVENI is a holding company that combines the Seoul city gas subsidiary Yesco with a financial investment arm, and while 2025 profit rose sharply, it posted an operating loss of KRW 36.6 billion in Q3 2026.

02

Business structure

INVENI was founded in 1981 as a city gas company; in 2018 it spun off the city gas business as Yesco and the surviving entity became a holding company. It changed its name from Yesco Holdings to INVENI in March 2025 and set targets of KRW 1 trillion in enterprise value and KRW 1 trillion in invested assets by 2030.

The business is organized into city gas, investment and holding, and other segments, and the other segment includes compressed natural gas charging and fuel cells.

The core subsidiary Yesco supplies city gas exclusively to about 1.45 million customers in parts of the Seoul metropolitan area, and accounted for 81% of INVENI's consolidated revenue over the latest three years according to Korea Ratings. That share reflects a credit rating report as of September 2025.

Because city gas operates under a licensed regional monopoly, direct competition inside the same service area is limited. Gas facility penetration in Seoul is at 100%, and sales are concentrated in the winter season.

The investment arm sits in the holding company itself, and gains and losses on financial assets, dividends and interest from the investment business are recognized as operating profit or loss. Korea Ratings noted that Yesco has paid INVENI a relatively stable dividend of roughly KRW 14 billion to KRW 17 billion a year.

Segment revenue figures could not be confirmed from disclosed data in this research, so the segments are described qualitatively.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue declined from KRW 1.47 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.43 trillion in 2023 (down 2.8% year on year) and KRW 1.16 trillion in 2024 (down 18.9%), then rebounded to KRW 1.33 trillion in 2025 (up 14.4%). Within 2022-2025, 2022 was the largest revenue year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 313 million in 2022, KRW 57.5 billion in 2023, KRW 24.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 111.7 billion in 2025, with 2025 the largest and 2022 the smallest of the period. The 2025 operating margin was 8.4%, up from 2.1% in 2024.

Korea Ratings data show INVENI's standalone adjusted operating profit for 2025 was KRW 107.9 billion, close to the consolidated figure of KRW 111.7 billion. This suggests that the improvement was centered on the investment business in the holding company itself.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose for two consecutive years, from KRW 23.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 29 billion in 2024 and KRW 86.7 billion in 2025. On a quarterly basis, Q1 2026 was the peak, with revenue of KRW 572 billion and operating profit of KRW 79.1 billion.

Q2 revenue fell to KRW 193.7 billion (down 66.1% quarter on quarter) with operating profit of KRW 11.2 billion. Q3 brought revenue of KRW 129.8 billion (down 33.0% from Q2 and 13.6% year on year), an operating loss of KRW 36.6 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 24.5 billion.

Revenue declined for two consecutive quarters from Q2 to Q3 2026. The specific cause of the Q3 loss could not be confirmed in this research.

Because valuation gains and losses on the investment portfolio flow into operating profit, the off-season for city gas and financial market swings may both have contributed, which is worth checking.

Controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 48.5 billion, below the KRW 86.7 billion of full-year 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 174.5 billion in 2023, then -KRW 72.1 billion in 2024 and -KRW 71 billion in 2025, diverging from the profit increase.

05

Industry analysis

INVENI's results hinge on two variables: the city gas market and the performance of its financial investments. City gas is a licensed regional monopoly, so the customer base is stable, but heating demand is concentrated in winter and quarterly revenue swings widely.

This matches INVENI's pattern of higher revenue in Q1 and Q4 and lower revenue in Q2 and Q3. Listed peers in the same energy and power sector include Samchully, which also supplies city gas, KoreaGas, a wholesale LNG supplier, and Korea District Heating, a heat supplier.

INVENI differs from them because gains and losses from its investment arm flow directly into operating profit. In 2026, reports said Dubai crude rose to about USD 120 a barrel after the outbreak of the US-Iran war in late February and the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, which raised energy price volatility.

The lag in passing fuel costs through to tariffs and the regulatory direction could affect Yesco's profitability, but Yesco's quarterly results for 2026 could not be confirmed in this research.

Korea Ratings assessed that INVENI maintains stable cash flow from dividend income and other sources, but earnings volatility is inherent as financial investment assets grow.

06

Outlook

The company's long-term targets are enterprise value of KRW 1 trillion and invested assets of KRW 1 trillion, supported by an investment management team and internal investment rules. No company guidance for 2026 earnings was found in this research.

On shareholder returns, the company disclosed a value-up plan in March 2026 that set a goal of canceling 450,000 treasury shares (on a pre-split basis) in two rounds.

It canceled 250,000 treasury shares in March, carried out the 5-for-1 split in April and canceled a further 1 million shares on a post-split basis in September, for a total of 2.25 million shares canceled this year. The same plan included maintaining its dividend policy.

Near-term results depend on city gas peak-season sales in Q4 2026 and Q1 2027 and on the direction of valuation gains and losses on investment assets. Future filings need to show whether the Q3 loss reflected temporary valuation losses or a weaker earnings base in the investment arm. The decline in the debt ratio from 187.1% in 2022 to 141.3% in 2025 can be read as a financial cushion.

08

Bull factors

Monopoly city gas base and dividend cash flow

Yesco runs a licensed monopoly serving about 1.45 million customers in parts of the Seoul metropolitan area. Korea Ratings said Yesco has paid the holding company dividends of roughly KRW 14 billion to KRW 17 billion a year. That gives a baseline cash inflow even when the investment arm's results fluctuate. Korea Ratings' credit rating for INVENI was AA- with a stable outlook in its September 2025 review.

Earnings recovery and a healthier balance sheet

Operating profit of KRW 111.7 billion in 2025 was the largest of 2022-2025, and the operating margin rose from 2.1% in 2024 to 8.4%. The debt ratio fell from 187.1% in 2022 to 141.3% in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 645.4 billion, equal to the equity attributable to controlling shareholders. The combination of an earnings recovery and a larger capital base is a financial cushion.

Treasury share cancellations and a stronger ownership position

Shares canceled in 2026 total 2.25 million on a post-split basis. The stake held by the largest shareholder and related parties rose to the 48% range through cancellations and open-market purchases. The company stated that the stock split was meant to widen the share count in circulation and improve market access. Shareholder return commitments are being carried out, which is the point usually cited on the positive side.

09

Bear factors

Large quarterly swings and the Q3 loss

Operating profit fell from KRW 79.1 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 11.2 billion in Q2, and Q3 brought an operating loss of KRW 36.6 billion. Controlling-shareholder net income also moved from KRW 57.2 billion in Q1 to a net loss of KRW 24.5 billion in Q3.

Because investment results flow directly into operating profit, similar swings could recur. An explanation for the Q3 loss has not yet been confirmed.

Operating cash flow diverging from profit

Operating cash flow was KRW 174.5 billion in 2023 but -KRW 72.1 billion in 2024 and -KRW 71 billion in 2025. Over the same period, operating profit rose to KRW 111.7 billion in 2025. Whether profit relied heavily on non-cash items such as valuation gains should be checked in the cash flow statement notes. If profit does not convert to cash, capacity for dividends and debt repayment could be limited.

Still-high leverage and a growing investment footprint

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 912.3 billion, for a debt ratio of 141.3%. Despite the improving trend, it remains above 100%. The company has raised funds through exchangeable bonds and public corporate bonds to expand its investment portfolio. As the investment share grows, the profit swings tied to market moves could widen.

10

Risk factors

Investment asset market risk

Because gains and losses on financial assets are recorded in operating profit, swings in equity and bond markets flow straight into quarterly results. Korea Ratings assessed that the investment business shows high earnings volatility depending on market conditions and the international situation.

As the investment arm grows, the degree to which city gas stability offsets it may shrink. It needs to be checked whether the Q3 2026 loss is an example of this risk.

Exchangeable bonds and governance

The exchangeable bonds issued in September 2025 are exchangeable into treasury shares, with an exchange price of KRW 13,960 per share after the stock split adjustment. The shares subject to exchange total 3.355 million.

Financial News reported that if the exchange occurs, the voting share of the largest shareholder group could fall below 50%. The exchange period runs to September 2030, so the ownership structure could change depending on the share price.

Energy prices and regulation

City gas is a licensed business, so tariffs and supply obligations are subject to regulation. In 2026, Middle East geopolitical risk increased volatility in global energy prices. Depending on the lag in passing fuel costs through to tariffs and on policy direction, Yesco's profitability could change. Yesco's 2026 quarterly results were not confirmed in this research, so the size of any impact is hard to judge.

12

Overall view

INVENI combines Yesco, which holds a monopoly city gas business in Seoul, with a financial investment arm, making it a holding company where a stable cash base coexists with volatile investment results.

In 2025, revenue of KRW 1.33 trillion, operating profit of KRW 111.7 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 86.7 billion showed a clear recovery. In 2026, however, quarterly operating profit fell from KRW 79.1 billion in Q1 to KRW 11.2 billion in Q2, and Q3 recorded an operating loss of KRW 36.6 billion.

On the positive side, the monopoly business base, a lower debt ratio and shareholder returns such as treasury share cancellations are cited. On the negative side are quarterly earnings volatility, negative operating cash flow, a debt ratio above 100% and ownership variables tied to the exchangeable bonds.

Market capitalization is below total equity at the end of 2025, but the fact that equity includes the carrying value of financial assets should be considered alongside. The cause of the Q3 loss, winter-season results and the cash flow in the annual report will be the basis for the next assessment.
