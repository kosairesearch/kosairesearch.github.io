Datasolution: AI infrastructure contracts expand, profit cushion still thin

02

Business structure

Datasolution is an IT services company centered on data analytics and digital transformation. The company is expanding a cloud business that combines cloud migration know-how built in the public, financial and manufacturing sectors with AI operation technology.

Its operations are organized into Data & AI, enterprise and infrastructure, AX and DX, and services, and it supplies DELL EMC storage products and data analytics programs. AI transformation support that combines generative AI, LLMs and cloud-native technology is presented as a core business line.

It has supplied data analytics solutions mainly to public institutions and financial clients, and recently has widened its scope to AI data center and GPU infrastructure builds.

In its big-data platform work, it is expanding traceability services for agricultural and livestock products through an upgrade of the N-Hub platform for a national agricultural cooperative. Drawing on big-data projects in finance, it has also outlined plans to extend into production and distribution.

The company said it completed an upgrade of an investment banking information system for an IB group at a domestic bank in August 2026. Its technology partners include IBM, Samsung SDS and Dataiku. Alongside data platform builds, it is cited as having AI computing hardware and systems integration capability.

Compared with large SI operators, its revenue base is smaller, so a single large contract has a considerable effect on reported results.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 99.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 99.3 billion in 2023 (up 0.1% year on year) and KRW 110.1 billion in 2024 (up 10.9%), then fell to KRW 103.8 billion in 2025 (down 5.7%). 2024 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating profit was KRW 2.66 billion in 2022, KRW 1.26 billion in 2023 (down 52.4%) and KRW 58.4 million in 2024 (down 95.4%), making 2024 the smallest year.

In 2024 revenue grew while profit shrank; according to the fnguide financial table on a consolidated basis, cost of sales rose from KRW 81.3 billion in 2023 to KRW 92.1 billion in 2024, and selling and administrative expenses rose from KRW 16.7 billion to KRW 18.0 billion.

In 2025 gross profit recovered to KRW 21.9 billion from KRW 18.0 billion, selling and administrative expenses were KRW 20.7 billion, and operating profit reached KRW 1.22 billion (up 1,984.2%).

The company attributed the main driver of the 2025 profit increase to higher profitability from business structure improvements. Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 2.4 billion in 2022, KRW 1.61 billion in 2023 (down 32.9%), KRW 236 million in 2024 (down 85.4%) and KRW 1.05 billion in 2025 (up 344.5%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same figures for each year. Operating cash flow was KRW 4.85 billion in 2022, KRW 1.44 billion in 2023, KRW 3.84 billion in 2024, and turned negative at -KRW 1.71 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 23.3 billion and an operating loss of KRW 386 million gave way to Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 42.1 billion (up 81.2% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 4.36 billion. Q1 2026 revenue fell 25.2% to KRW 31.5 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 1.07 billion.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 30.1 billion (down 4.5%) with operating profit of KRW 1.25 billion, and Q3 2026 revenue reached KRW 58.9 billion (up 95.7% quarter on quarter and 153.4% year on year) with operating profit of KRW 1.16 billion (down 7.2% quarter on quarter).

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 1.85 billion in Q3 2026, up 29.8% quarter on quarter and a turn to profit from a year earlier.

05

Industry analysis

The main industry trend is that generative AI adoption is rapidly increasing investment in data center infrastructure, including GPU servers, storage and networks. The government's push to expand AI computing infrastructure and the start of full-scale private AI data center investment are cited as the backdrop.

According to fnguide data summarizing the first nine months of 2025, hardware revenue grew on AI server spending and AI infrastructure build demand, while economic slowdown kept IT spending and new investment limited.

On the same basis, standalone revenue for the first nine months of 2025 rose 4.9% year on year, while operating losses widened 25.1%. This shows how sensitive IT services demand is to the economic cycle.

The company positions itself on owning AI computing hardware and systems integration capability alongside data platform builds. It has partnerships with global vendors including IBM and Dataiku, and its inclusion in the Samsung SDS supply chain is cited as a factor that could support further orders.

Its financial sector AX business is also expanding. Compared with large SI operators in the same industry, such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, its revenue base is smaller, so a single contract carries a larger weight in its results.

Its multi-year duration is cited as what distinguishes it from a one-off project. Coverage points to whether follow-on project awards come in and whether profitability improves as the variables that will decide future enterprise value.

The company has also signed a memorandum of understanding with a financial institution to commercialize a middleware integrated management portal. In big-data platforms, it is widening production and distribution data services through an upgrade of the N-Hub platform for a national agricultural cooperative.

The company said the upgrade of the investment banking system for an IB group at a domestic bank was completed in August 2026. On profitability, the 2025 operating margin was 1.2%, and with operating profit in the second and third quarters of 2026, the key question is whether this profit turnaround holds.

Because third-quarter 2026 revenue rose sharply from the prior quarter, how much of that contract-related revenue converts into profit will become visible in upcoming quarterly results. The next periodic filing, the 2026 annual report, is due by March 31, 2027.

Bull factors

Profitability recovering on business restructuring

The company attributed the 2025 operating profit increase to higher profitability from business structure improvements. Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 1.22 billion, up 1,984.2% from the prior year.

Operating profit stayed positive in the second and third quarters of 2026, showing a move away from loss-making quarters. The direction of the profit recovery is cited as a supporting factor.

Large multi-year contract secured

The company disclosed a contract worth KRW 438.1 billion with Samsung SDS for reinforcing AI computing resources. The contract value equals 422.04% of 2025 revenue, and the term runs through December 31, 2031, giving it a longer horizon than a one-off project.

Under the deal, the company will supply GPU servers, hardware for AI infrastructure builds and software services.

AI infrastructure demand and SI capabilities

Generative AI adoption is rapidly increasing investment in data center equipment such as GPU servers, storage and networks. Datasolution combines data platform builds with AI computing hardware and SI capabilities.

Its financial sector AX expansion is running in parallel, widening the demand base across several customer groups.

Bear factors

Thin operating margin

The operating margin was 0.1% in 2024 and 1.2% in 2025. In 2025 gross profit was KRW 21.9 billion, but selling and administrative expenses were KRW 20.7 billion, leaving limited room for profit. With revenue near KRW 100 billion, a margin in the low single digits points to cost structure constraints.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Operating losses appeared in Q3 2025 and Q1 2026, and the parent recorded net losses in those same quarters. Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 42.1 billion was up 81.2% from the prior quarter, and Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 58.9 billion was up 95.7% from the prior quarter. Because revenue moves this sharply, the durability of quarterly profit may still be at an early stage of proof.

Cash flow and sensitivity to IT spending

Operating cash flow turned negative at -KRW 1.71 billion in 2025. Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 33.2 billion, close in size to total equity of KRW 35.2 billion. On a first-nine-months 2025 basis, economic slowdown limited IT spending and new investment, which remains a source of demand volatility.

Risk factors

Customer and contract concentration

A single contract equals 422.04% of 2025 revenue, so results lean heavily on one large deal. If follow-on orders do not materialize, revenue volatility could rise. Budget execution timing at public and financial clients may also affect quarterly results.

Share price volatility and limited free float

The largest shareholder group holds 66.09%, which leaves a limited tradable float. The one-year beta of 1.44 is above the KOSDAQ average of 1.00. Share prices rose sharply over short periods in April and July 2026 around contract expectations and contract disclosures.

Financial and cash flow pressure

Operating cash flow turned negative in 2025, and an operating loss of KRW 1.07 billion appeared in Q1 2026. The debt ratio fell to 94.3% in 2025, but cash flow could swing again depending on payment timing during large contract execution.

As hardware supply grows, the gap between revenue recognition and cash receipts becomes a variable in reading results.

12

Overall view

On a disclosed-results basis, Datasolution's revenue peaked at KRW 110.1 billion in 2024 and declined to KRW 103.8 billion in 2025, while operating profit recovered to KRW 1.22 billion in 2025.

In the third quarter of 2026 it recorded revenue of KRW 58.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.16 billion, showing growth in sales and a turn to profit together. Its strengths are the KRW 438.1 billion contract with Samsung SDS and the expansion of AI infrastructure and financial sector business.

Its weaknesses are an operating margin that has stayed around 1% and large swings in quarterly results. The contract running through 2031 raises medium-term revenue visibility, but dependence on a single contract equal to 422.04% of 2025 revenue remains a concentration risk.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent totaled KRW 5.9 billion over the last four quarters, which shows a profitable run but also loss quarters, so the trend is not yet settled. The multiple is computed high because the earnings base is small.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report, due by March 31, 2027, and the Q1 2027 quarterly report, due by May 15, 2027.
