Report overview

Lotte Chemical: Losses Persist as Restructuring Cuts Debt

Operating losses ran from 2022 through 2025, and the Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 281.4 billion widened again, while the company is cutting debt through the Daesan carve-out and a shift toward high-value businesses.

  1. 1

    Annual operating losses were KRW 762.6 billion in 2022, KRW 347.7 billion in 2023, KRW 894.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 943.1 billion in 2025, a loss in each of the four years.

  2. 2

    Q3 2026 revenue rose 12.7% year on year to KRW 5.4 trillion, but the operating loss widened to KRW 281.4 billion.

  3. 3

    After operating profits of KRW 73.5 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 110.1 billion in Q2, the company returned to an operating loss in Q3.

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Lotte Chemical runs two main businesses: basic chemicals, which crack feedstocks such as naphtha and ethane into base olefins and resins, and advanced materials, which make high-value materials used in automotive and electronics.

In Q3 2026, the basic chemicals segment (including Lotte Chemical's basic materials business, LC Titan, LC USA and the Daesan petrochemical joint venture) posted revenue of KRW 3.7 trillion and an operating loss of KRW 2.2 trillion, which the company identified as the main driver of the group loss.

The advanced materials segment recorded revenue of KRW 1.1 trillion and an operating loss of KRW 15.4 billion, swinging from a KRW 132.5 billion operating profit in Q2.

Subsidiary LOTTE Fine Chemical posted revenue of KRW 525.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 22.0 billion, while subsidiary Lotte Energy Materials posted revenue of KRW 289.7 billion and an operating loss of KRW 21.3 billion on higher shipments to North American energy storage and global EV customers.

The advanced materials executive in charge of management planning said roughly 80% of revenue is exported, which raised logistics costs.

Commodity petrochemicals face intense price competition from capacity additions in China and the Middle East, and the company competes with listed chemical peers including LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions and Kumho Petro Chemical.

The company is pursuing a restructuring that reduces the weight of basic chemicals and shifts toward high-value businesses.

Mirae Asset Securities reported in August 2026 that the Daesan split was completed in June, transferring about KRW 1.6 trillion of borrowings, and that the report expects lower basic chemicals exposure to structurally reduce earnings volatility.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 22.3 trillion in 2022 to KRW 19.9 trillion in 2023 (down 10.5% year on year), recovered to KRW 20.4 trillion in 2024 (up 2.4%), and then declined to KRW 18.5 trillion in 2025 (down 9.5%). Revenue was largest in 2022 and smallest in 2025 across the four years.

Operating loss narrowed from KRW 762.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 347.7 billion in 2023, then widened to KRW 894.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 943.1 billion in 2025.

Operating margin was -3.4% in 2022, -1.7% in 2023, -4.4% in 2024 and -5.1% in 2025, and the company has recorded losses for four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 27.8 billion in 2022, turned to a loss of KRW 50 billion in 2023, and widened to losses of KRW 1.71 trillion in 2024 and KRW 2.04 trillion in 2025.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 2.48 trillion in 2025, larger than the controlling-shareholder loss.

Operating cash flow went from -KRW 167.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 789.5 billion in 2023 and KRW 1.54 trillion in 2024, then fell to KRW 488.9 billion in 2025, while the debt ratio rose from 55.1% in 2022 to 76.5% in 2025.

At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 17.6 trillion, of which controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 12.6 trillion, and total liabilities were KRW 13.5 trillion.

By quarter, an operating loss of KRW 433.5 billion in Q4 2025 was followed by an operating profit of KRW 73.5 billion in Q1 2026, which the company attributed partly to KRW 250 billion of positive lagging effects and KRW 50 billion of provision reversals.

Q2 operating profit was KRW 110.1 billion, up 49.9% from the prior quarter, before Q3 returned to an operating loss of KRW 281.4 billion, which the company attributed to reverse lagging and narrower spreads from falling feedstock prices, the exchange rate and higher freight rates.

The controlling-shareholder net loss over the most recent four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 1.34 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

The petrochemical cycle is shaped by oversupply of ethylene from China and weak global demand. In Q3 2026, geopolitical risk in the Middle East raised volatility in feedstock and product prices, and higher freight rates added cost pressure.

The company said feedstock price moves were not fully passed through to product prices, which narrowed spreads.

Reverse lagging refers to inventory valuation losses when feedstock prices fall, and BNK Investment & Securities noted in a May 2026 report that a fall in product prices could bring reverse lagging into full effect.

In the first half of 2026, higher product prices from the US-Iran conflict and cheaper feedstock supported spreads. Mirae Asset Securities, in an August 2026 report, expected a reverse lagging phase from Q3 and noted that once temporary supply disruptions fade, the structural oversupply problem returns.

Planned capacity additions in China and the Shahin project were cited in a May 2026 BNK Investment & Securities report as reasons the 2027 to 2028 outlook is hard to view positively.

In competition, the company faces commodity price pressure from listed chemical peers including LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions, Kumho Petro Chemical and SKC. Hanwha Investment & Securities said in a May 2026 report it expected LC USA's earnings improvement to offset weakness in other segments. The company says it will respond to cycle swings by shifting toward high-value businesses.

06

Outlook

At its Q3 2026 earnings release, the company said it expects the industry downturn to continue in Q4 because of oversupply of petrochemical products globally and delayed demand recovery. The company also said it will keep working to improve profitability through production optimization and stronger competitiveness.

On restructuring, it plans to shift toward a high-value business structure to build a more stable earnings base. The Yeosu restructuring plan is a company disclosure, and the timing of its earnings impact is a point to confirm in the next periodic report.

Press reports cited total borrowings of KRW 8.8 trillion at the end of Q3 2026, down KRW 1.5 trillion from the prior quarter. BNK Investment & Securities said in an August 2026 report that second-half earnings would return to prior profit levels.

On Lotte Energy Materials, BNK Investment & Securities said in a May 2026 report that earnings could improve from 2027. Planned capacity additions in China and the Shahin project keep a burden on the 2027 to 2028 outlook.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 provisional results, due in early 2027, and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Daesan carve-out cuts borrowings

Mirae Asset Securities said in an August 2026 report that the Daesan split completed in June moved about KRW 1.6 trillion of borrowings to the joint venture. Press reports said total borrowings fell by KRW 1.5 trillion from the prior quarter to KRW 8.8 trillion in Q3.

The same report explained that a smaller basic chemicals share structurally reduces earnings swings from market cycles.

Quarterly profits in early 2026

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 73.5 billion in Q1 2026, and Q2 operating profit rose 49.9% from the prior quarter to KRW 110.1 billion. The company said the Q1 profit included KRW 250 billion of positive lagging effects and KRW 50 billion of provision reversals.

Analysts noted that because these gains came from higher product prices, whether the trend continues depends on the direction of product prices.

Subsidiary demand from battery and fine chemicals

Subsidiary Lotte Energy Materials recorded Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 289.7 billion, and its revenue and profitability improved slightly year on year on higher shipments to North American energy storage and global EV customers. Its operating loss was KRW 21.3 billion.

Subsidiary LOTTE Fine Chemical kept its profit in the same quarter, with revenue of KRW 525.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 22.0 billion.

09

Bear factors

Q3 loss came in below consensus

The Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 281.4 billion was larger than the consensus operating loss of KRW 183.7 billion compiled by FnGuide. Both basic chemicals, with an operating loss of KRW 216.6 billion, and advanced materials, with an operating loss of KRW 15.4 billion, were in the red. The swing from a Q2 profit to a Q3 loss within one quarter is the main weakness signal.

Downturn and reverse lagging

The company expects the downturn to continue in Q4 because of global oversupply of petrochemical products and delayed demand recovery. It said reverse lagging, where falling feedstock prices create inventory valuation losses, weighed on Q3 earnings. Higher freight rates and Middle East geopolitical risk also added cost pressure.

Supply burden for 2027 to 2028

In a May 2026 report, BNK Investment & Securities said the added supply from China's capacity expansion and the Shahin start-up could slow the industry recovery through 2027 to 2028. The same report said the loss could narrow if the petrochemical restructuring is completed as planned. The uncertain timing of oversupply easing remains a weakness factor.

10

Risk factors

Geopolitics and feedstock/product prices

Prolonged Middle East geopolitical risk has increased volatility in feedstock and product prices, and higher freight rates raised costs in Q3. Analysts have warned that a fall in product prices could bring reverse lagging into full effect and sharply weaken earnings.

The company says its export-heavy revenue structure leaves it exposed to logistics costs and exchange rate moves.

Borrowings and balance sheet

Total borrowings were still KRW 8.8 trillion after falling in Q3 2026. The debt ratio rose from 55.1% in 2022 to 76.5% in 2025, and total liabilities were KRW 13.5 trillion at the end of 2025.

Operating cash flow fell to KRW 488.9 billion in 2025 from KRW 1.54 trillion in 2024, so the pace of debt reduction depends on cash generation.

Restructuring execution and subsidiary earnings

Execution speed and the timing of earnings effects are variables for the Yeosu restructuring and the joint venture with HD Hyundai Chemical. Mirae Asset Securities said in an August 2026 report that the integrated company would be launched from September 2026.

On Lotte Energy Materials, BNK Investment & Securities saw a possible turn to profit from 2027, but this is a brokerage forecast.

12

Overall view

Lotte Chemical posted operating losses from 2022 through 2025, and returned to an operating loss of KRW 281.4 billion in Q3 2026. It recorded operating profits in Q1 and Q2 2026, but the trend reversed in Q3 amid the industry downturn, reverse lagging and higher freight costs.

Positive factors include the transfer of about KRW 1.6 trillion of borrowings in the Daesan carve-out, a KRW 1.5 trillion reduction in total borrowings in Q3, and the restructuring toward higher-value businesses.

Negative factors include the company's expectation of continued oversupply and weak demand into Q4, and the burden on the 2027 to 2028 outlook from capacity additions in China and the Shahin project.

Controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.34 trillion, and operating cash flow fell to KRW 488.9 billion in 2025. Mirae Asset Securities and BNK Investment & Securities presented target prices of KRW 63,000 and KRW 64,000 in August 2026 reports, with neutral and hold ratings.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 provisional results due in early 2027 and the 2026 annual report due by March 31, 2027. This report is provided for information only and does not include an investment opinion.
