02

Business structure

Pnpoongnyun makes and sells pressure cookers, cookware, kitchen tools and small appliances, and also handles electrical appliances through a subsidiary. Based on second-quarter 2026 sales, the segment mix was distributed goods 58%, pressure cookers 38%, parts 3% and the subsidiary's products 1%.

Because much of revenue comes from selling externally sourced goods, the business leans on distribution margin more than on manufacturing margin.

The company says the pressure cooker brand's reputation supports its share in high-temperature, high-pressure product lines and raises barriers to new entrants, and it holds 26 intellectual property rights, including 22 patents and 4 utility models.

Sales run through seven channels, including wholesale and retail, department stores, home shopping, online, and exports, with more than 500 trading partners.

Offline distribution is moving from small retail shops toward large fixed-price household goods stores, and the company is pairing an overhauled direct online mall with social media marketing, live commerce and group buying.

Analysts note that the electrical appliance line at the subsidiary is still small, which suggests an early stage of expansion. The company competes for the same household spending as kitchen appliance makers such as Cuckoo Homesys.

Brand reputation and channel diversification are its strengths, but a shift in spending toward low-priced essentials would bring price competition pressure.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, sales went from KRW 57.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 57.2 billion in 2023 (down 0.7% year on year) and KRW 54.9 billion in 2024 (down 4.0%), before rising to KRW 56 billion in 2025 (up 2.0%). Of 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for sales and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit rose from KRW 1.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.09 billion in 2023 (up 49.2%), fell to KRW 920 million in 2024 (down 56.0%), and recovered to KRW 1.57 billion in 2025 (up 70.2%).

The operating margin was 2.4% in 2022, 3.7% in 2023, 1.7% in 2024 and 2.8% in 2025, so margin swings rather than sales moves drove most of the variation in profit.

Profit attributable to owners was KRW 1.77 billion in 2022, KRW 2.17 billion in 2023 (up 22.3%), KRW 1.59 billion in 2024 (down 26.9%) and KRW 2.88 billion in 2025 (up 81.3%), with 2025 the largest year.

In 2025 net profit grew faster than operating profit, which suggests non-operating income supported earnings; the JobKorea financial summary shows non-operating income higher than in 2024.

By quarter, the third quarter of 2025 had sales of KRW 14.1 billion, operating profit of KRW 602 million and profit attributable to owners of KRW 646 million. Operating profit in the fourth quarter of 2025 fell 94.0% from the prior quarter to KRW 36.31 million.

The first quarter of 2026 was the peak, with operating profit of KRW 749 million, followed by declines to KRW 310 million in the second quarter and KRW 49.15 million in the third quarter, two consecutive quarterly drops. Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 12.8 billion were down 9.5% year on year.

The company attributed the sales decline to weaker consumer sentiment and a shift toward low-priced products that reduced household goods market sales, while cost cutting helped protect profitability.

Profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totals KRW 1.43 billion. Gross margin was reported at 30.0% in 2025 against 29.6% in 2024 (Investing.com).

05

Industry analysis

The domestic kitchenware market is in a phase of shrinking sales as weaker consumer sentiment and a preference for value products reinforce each other.

The company describes the 2026 domestic economy as recovering gradually but with high external uncertainty, and it notes that expanded US trade protection and a deepening Middle East conflict have increased volatility in costs, exchange rates and logistics.

In offline distribution, large fixed-price household goods stores have gained ground since the stagflation-like three-high phase, reducing the share of traditional small retailers (FnGuide company overview).

Because the company competes with low-priced goods at these large stores, its room to pass cost increases on to customers may be limited, which is a key industry question. Whether online channels can replace lost offline volume is the main variable for sales in the coming quarters.

Compared with larger listed consumer and distribution companies such as E-Mart and Lotte Shopping, Pnpoongnyun's market capitalization is small, and its sales are concentrated in one product category, which shapes how fast it can diversify.

Its competition with kitchen appliance makers such as Cuckoo Homesys depends on product innovation speed and brand recognition, and its electrical appliance line remains at an early stage in that contest.

The share buyback decided on September 10, 2026 covers KRW 700 million, equal to 3.50% of market capitalization and 1.2% of sales according to the company's disclosure timeline.

A buyback reads as a shareholder return signal, but whether the shares are cancelled and how long execution takes must be confirmed in later filings.

The 14.1% rise in first-half 2026 operating profit is attributed to cost cutting, and whether that carries into the fourth quarter of 2026 is a variable that has not yet shown up in reported results.

The sharp drop in third-quarter 2026 profit appears to reflect falling sales combined with changes in cost and selling expense structure, and the detailed causes can be checked in the 2026 annual report. The 2026 annual report is the next regular filing, and it will fix full-year sales mix and selling expense trends.

How far the electrical appliance line at the subsidiary raises its share of sales is another indicator of how fast the expansion can go.

With quarterly profit slowing after the first-quarter 2026 peak, online channel reform and global expansion remain medium-term projects, and the growth path has not yet appeared in reported figures.

08

Bull factors

The Scale of the 2025 Earnings Recovery

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 1.57 billion, up 70.2%, and profit attributable to owners was KRW 2.88 billion, up 81.3%. The operating margin rose from 1.7% in 2024 to 2.8% in 2025, confirming a margin recovery.

Sales also rose again to KRW 56 billion in 2025 from KRW 54.9 billion in 2024, so the decline in revenue stopped.

Financial Capacity and Cash Generation

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 9.14 billion and total equity was KRW 51.9 billion, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 17.6%. Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 452 million in 2022 to KRW 3.29 billion in 2023, KRW 5.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.53 billion in 2025. The balance sheet gives the company room to run its business without heavy outside borrowing.

Cost Discipline and Shareholder Returns

First-half 2026 operating profit rose 14.1%, and the company explained that cost cutting protected profitability. A share buyback decision worth KRW 700 million was disclosed on September 10, 2026. Dividend decisions have continued, with one disclosed in February 2026, keeping a shareholder return channel open.

Bear factors

Stagnant Sales and Channel Pressure

Sales moved from KRW 57.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 56 billion in 2025, roughly flat. Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 12.8 billion, down 9.5% year on year, and press coverage put cumulative January-September 2026 sales at KRW 39.8 billion, down 5.8%.

As spending shifts toward low-priced essentials and large fixed-price stores gain share, the company faces pressure on its pricing power in those channels.

Sharp Contraction in Quarterly Profit

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 49.15 million, down 91.8% year on year, and profit attributable to owners was KRW 27.09 million, down 95.8%. Profit declined for two consecutive quarters after the first-quarter 2026 peak. Profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters totals KRW 1.43 billion, a thin earnings base.

A Distribution-Led Model and Cost Volatility

Distributed goods made up 58% of second-quarter 2026 sales, so distribution margin weighs more heavily than manufacturing margin.

The company describes high external uncertainty for the 2026 domestic economy and notes increased cost, exchange rate and logistics volatility from expanded US trade protection and the Middle East conflict. When procurement costs for distributed goods rise, the margin is harder to defend.

Risk factors

Consumer and Channel Risk

If weaker consumer sentiment keeps pushing demand toward value products, the kitchenware market itself could shrink. If distribution keeps reshaping around large fixed-price stores, entry terms and pricing for smaller brands could worsen. If the shift to online does not replace lost offline volume, the sales recovery will be delayed.

Cost, Exchange Rate and Logistics Risk

The company says expanded US trade protection and a deepening Middle East conflict have increased cost, exchange rate and logistics volatility. Because distributed goods make up a large share of sales, changes in procurement prices flow directly into margins.

Foreign-currency assets are small, but rising raw material and logistics costs would limit room to raise prices.

Non-Operating Profit Volatility

First-half 2026 operating profit rose 14.1% while net profit fell 41.6%, a gap that shows how volatile non-operating items can be. Equity-method or valuation gains and losses tied to the subsidiary could move net profit significantly.

Overall view

Pnpoongnyun is a kitchenware company built on its pressure cooker brand and distribution channels, and its operating profit and profit attributable to owners recovered sharply in 2025.

Sales have declined in 2026, however, and third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 91.8% year on year, weakening earnings strength again. Distributed goods make up 58% of sales, so the margin structure leans toward distribution, and cost, exchange rate and logistics volatility feed directly into margins.

The balance sheet is stable, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 17.6% and positive operating cash flow in most recent years.

The share price sits well below book value per share, but profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters totals only KRW 1.43 billion, so reading earnings multiples depends heavily on the profit trend.

The strengths are the 2025 earnings recovery and financial capacity, while the weaknesses are the sharp quarterly profit decline in 2026 and channel pressure. The next checkpoints are fourth-quarter 2026 results, execution of the share buyback, and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
