Report overview

Record Quarterly Sales, but Profit Down Three Quarters Running on AI Spending

Q3 2026 sales hit a quarterly record, but AI infrastructure spending has pushed operating profit lower for three straight quarters, while a roughly KRW 2 trillion AI Factory contract is positioned as the next growth driver.

  1. 1

    On disclosed annual figures, sales reached KRW 12 trillion and operating profit reached KRW 2.21 trillion in 2025, with both rising three years in a row from 2023.

  2. 2

    In Q3 2026, operating profit fell even though sales grew, meaning cost growth outpaced sales growth.

  3. 3

    The profit decline reflects expanded investment in AI infrastructure, content and offline data, plus sports IP and payment marketing costs that overlapped in Q3.

  4. 4

    The AI Factory project secured an initial customer through a contract of roughly KRW 2 trillion, with related revenue expected to be recognized in sequence from the first half of 2027.

  5. 5

    Korea Investment & Securities set a target price of KRW 300,000 on November 13, 2026, Hana Securities set KRW 340,000 on the same day, and DS Investment set KRW 450,000 on June 9, 2026.

02

Business structure

NAVER's business is split into three segments: NAVER Platform, Financial Platform and Global Challenge.

In Q3 2026, NAVER Platform sales were KRW 1,948.4 billion, up 7.3% year on year, Financial Platform sales were KRW 487.7 billion, up 14.1%, and Global Challenge sales were KRW 1,036.4 billion, up 15.9%, which by share of Q3 2026 sales is roughly 56%, 14% and 30%.

Within NAVER Platform, advertising sales were KRW 1,474.8 billion, up 5.5%, and service sales were KRW 473.6 billion, up 13.1%. Advertising growth came from expanded AI monetization attempts, while service growth was supported by more users of NAVER Plus Store and memberships and by stronger N Delivery.

Npay payments reached KRW 26.2 trillion in Q3 2026, up 15.6%, with external payments growing 21.8%.

In Global Challenge, C2C sales rose 47.8% to KRW 370.4 billion, helped by the consolidation of Wallapop and growth at Poshmark and Soda, while enterprise sales including the Saudi Arabia digital twin business also increased.

Content sales fell 5.1% to KRW 472.6 billion, which the company attributed to a stagnant Japanese market and the absence of hit original titles. In commerce, the NAVER Plus Store app accounted for more than 20% of Smart Store transaction volume, roughly three times its share in Q3 2025, according to the company.

In AI agents, global big tech firms such as OpenAI and Google are cited as rivals, and NAVER points to its own ecosystem spanning shopping, payments and reservations, along with the trust placed in its NAVER ID, as its differentiator.

04

Earnings analysis

On disclosed annual figures, sales rose from KRW 8.22 trillion in 2022 to KRW 12 trillion in 2025, growing three consecutive years from 2023, with 2025 the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Operating profit rose from KRW 1.3 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.21 trillion in 2025, also up three consecutive years from 2023.

The operating margin was 15.9% in 2022, 15.4% in 2023, 18.4% in 2024 and 18.3% in 2025, so the 2023 dip was reversed in 2024 and held near that level in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent went from KRW 760.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.92 trillion in 2024, up 90.0% in 2024, before reaching KRW 1.95 trillion in 2025, up only 1.6%.

Net profit including non-controlling interests, at KRW 1.82 trillion in 2025, is a different figure from profit attributable to owners and should be read separately. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 1.45 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.1 trillion in 2025, exceeding operating profit in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 29 trillion, of which KRW 27.6 trillion belonged to owners of the parent, and total liabilities were KRW 12.1 trillion, giving a debt-to-equity ratio of 41.9%.

Quarterly sales rose for four consecutive quarters from KRW 3.14 trillion in Q3 2025 to KRW 3.47 trillion in Q3 2026, and Q3 2026 sales, up 10.7% year on year, were the highest quarterly sales the company has reported.

Operating profit, by contrast, peaked at KRW 610.6 billion in Q4 2025 and then declined for three consecutive quarters to KRW 541.8 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 520.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 451.3 billion in Q3.

Q3 operating expenses rose 17.7% year on year to KRW 3.02 trillion, and the operating margin of about 13% was 5.2 percentage points lower than a year earlier. Korea Investment & Securities said Q3 operating profit came in slightly below its market estimate of KRW 493 billion.

The CFO said infrastructure costs overlapped in Q3 with sports IP spending tied to the World Cup and Asian Games and with offline payment marketing, and expects that burden to ease in Q4.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 698.6 billion in Q2 2026, up 144.8% from the prior quarter, which is well above that quarter's operating profit of KRW 520.3 billion, suggesting non-operating items contributed heavily.

Q3 2026 profit attributable to owners was KRW 399.5 billion, down 45.0% year on year, and the four-quarter total from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 is KRW 1.7 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

AI infrastructure spending is taking place as global big tech firms pour heavy costs into securing data centers and GPUs, and the industry view is that domestic platform companies find it hard to step back from this investment race.

Many in the industry see Q3 2026 as a phase that must be passed through before AI investment results fully come back. According to Korea Investment & Securities, NAVER has secured data center capacity beyond the 200MW it targeted through 2028.

The company stresses that it has secured most of the liquid-cooled data centers usable in Korea in advance, so it can supply the latest GPUs on schedule, as a competitive edge.

In advertising, the company said that applying an AI prediction model to Powerlink lifted click-through rates by 11% and conversions by 9% in Q3. In shopping, the app channel is growing, and the company said average time spent by NAVER Plus Store app users overtook the web for the first time.

In AI agents, global big tech firms such as OpenAI and Google are accelerating, and NAVER plans to counter with its own service ecosystem linking shopping, payments and reservations and with data management in its own domestic data centers.

Overseas, the Wallapop consolidation and growth at Poshmark are expanding C2C, but a stagnant Japanese webtoon market has weighed on content sales, so growth across overseas operations is uneven. Its position against peers is best measured by the pace of AI infrastructure build-out and the size of its own ecosystem.

06

Outlook

The AI Factory project is being pursued with Brookfield and NVIDIA, and the company said it secured an initial customer by signing a computing service supply contract of about KRW 2 trillion with a US AI inference company.

Related revenue is expected to be recognized in sequence alongside the commercial launch of the service in the first half of 2027. According to the company, a 20MW data center is expected to generate annual revenue of KRW 400 billion to KRW 500 billion.

A June 2026 report said NAVER and NVIDIA agreed to expand AI infrastructure step by step, starting with 55MW of capacity in 2027. Korea Investment & Securities assessed that, if the contract includes a mix of B300 and Vera Rubin GPUs, the contract value falls slightly short of market expectations.

The same firm noted that discounting may have been larger than the market expected because it is a first long-term contract with a single tenant.

In commerce, the company plans to set up a dedicated logistics subsidiary, which it expects to require close to KRW 1 trillion of investment through 2030, and says it is designing the impact on cash flow and profit to be limited.

The financial platform aims to become the largest domestic operator of active terminals by the first half of 2027 through wider rollout of the Npay Connect offline terminal.

In advertising, a revamp of Powerlink in 2027 is planned to allocate the budgets of about 500,000 advertisers across ad placements inside and outside NAVER based on performance. A personal AI agent app is planned for launch within 2026.

The next checkpoints are whether AI Factory revenue shows up in first-half 2027 results and whether the operating margin turns back up after Q4.

The price-to-book ratio sits below 1x, which means the stock trades at a price below book net assets.

The price-to-earnings ratio is an earnings-based multiple, and with profit attributable to owners of the parent totaling KRW 1.7 trillion over the last four quarters and wide quarter-to-quarter swings, reading the multiple from a single quarter can be distorted.

Profit attributable to owners rose for three consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, so earnings scale has grown, but operating profit has declined quarter after quarter in 2026, leaving the earnings-based multiple sensitive to the earnings trend.

The dividend yield shows the size of dividends relative to the share price, and during a period of heavy investment the balance between shareholder returns and funding for investment becomes the central issue.

Korea Investment & Securities lowered its target price from KRW 360,000 to KRW 300,000 on November 13, 2026, and Hana Securities lowered its target from KRW 400,000 to KRW 340,000 the same day.

DS Investment raised its target from KRW 300,000 to KRW 450,000 on June 9, 2026, stating that the company value could be re-rated as global AI infrastructure business takes off.

Because the three targets were set at different times and on different assumptions, the gap reflects differing views on how much the AI Factory business should be reflected in valuation.

08

Bull factors

AI Factory contract and data center base

The company said a computing service contract of about KRW 2 trillion secured an initial customer, with related revenue to be recognized in sequence from the first half of 2027. By the company's account, a 20MW data center would generate KRW 400 billion to KRW 500 billion in annual revenue.

Securing data center capacity beyond the 200MW target through 2028 is also presented as the business foundation.

Commerce app shift and membership growth

The company said the NAVER Plus Store app accounted for more than 20% of Smart Store transaction volume in Q3, about three times its share in Q3 2025. Purchases per app visit rose by nearly 50% compared with Q3 2025. Membership members make up more than 80% of app buyers, the highest level since launch.

Ad efficiency and external payment growth

The company said applying an AI prediction model to Powerlink lifted Q3 click-through rates by 11% and conversions by 9%, and a Powerlink revamp is planned for 2027. Npay payments rose 15.6% to KRW 26.2 trillion, with external payments up 21.8%. Financial Platform sales rose 14.1% to KRW 487.7 billion.

09

Bear factors

Operating profit decline and cost burden

Q3 operating profit fell 20.9% to KRW 451.3 billion, while operating expenses rose 17.7% to KRW 3.02 trillion, outpacing the 10.7% sales growth. The operating margin of about 13% was 5.2 percentage points below a year earlier.

Korea Investment & Securities analyzed that operating profit came in slightly below a market estimate of KRW 493 billion.

Slower growth in core ads and commerce

Korea Investment & Securities attributed the slowdown in platform sales growth to the Chuseok holiday, which weighed on both advertising and commerce, and to the fading base effect from a commerce fee increase. Hana Securities also pointed to core advertising and commerce results that came in below expectations. It judged that monetizing the AI tab would be unlikely to produce rapid growth in the short term.

Content decline and AI contract terms

Content sales fell 5.1% to KRW 472.6 billion in Q3, which the company attributed to a stagnant Japanese market and the absence of hit original titles.

Korea Investment & Securities noted that the AI Factory contract value could fall slightly short of market expectations and that the discount may have been larger than expected because it is a first long-term contract.

10

Risk factors

Prolonged investment burden

Investment in AI infrastructure, content, offline data and sports IP has pushed up operating costs through 2026. The company expects close to KRW 1 trillion of investment in its logistics subsidiary through 2030. If returns on these investments arrive late, the pace of operating margin recovery could slow.

AI Factory execution risk

AI Factory revenue depends on a commercial launch in the first half of 2027, so any delay would push back recognition in results. Whether the plan to pursue larger long-term contracts with major customers turns into signed deals remains to be seen. Given the discount risk raised by analysts, profitability levels are also a variable.

Competition and shifts in ad model

Global big tech firms such as OpenAI and Google are accelerating competition in AI agents, which could intensify rivalry in shopping and search. If the Powerlink revamp changes how ad budgets are allocated, advertiser reactions could shift the trajectory of advertising sales. The pace at which automated bidding spreads across ad products is also a variable.

12

Overall view

NAVER's disclosed results show sales, operating profit and profit attributable to owners of the parent each rising for three consecutive years from 2022 to 2025. At the end of 2025, owners of the parent held KRW 27.6 trillion of total equity of KRW 29 trillion, and the debt-to-equity ratio was 41.9%.

Q3 2026 sales of KRW 3.47 trillion were up 10.7% year on year, while operating profit of KRW 451.3 billion was down 20.9%. The main reason for the profit decline is expanded investment in AI infrastructure, content and offline data, and operating profit has now fallen for three consecutive quarters.

Strengths include the roughly KRW 2 trillion AI Factory contract, the shift of commerce toward the app and wider membership, and better ad efficiency from applying AI to Powerlink.

Weaknesses include a lower operating margin from higher costs, slower advertising and commerce growth after the Chuseok holiday and the fading fee-increase base effect, and lower content sales.

Target prices were lowered to KRW 300,000 by Korea Investment & Securities and KRW 340,000 by Hana Securities on November 13, 2026, while DS Investment set KRW 450,000 on June 9, 2026, so views remain divided.

The items to check next are Q4 2026 results, the 2026 annual report, and whether AI Factory revenue is recognized in the first half of 2027.
