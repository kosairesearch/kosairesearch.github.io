Report overview

A thermal packaging shift: profits positive, but still thin

Kostecsys is shifting its revenue mix from RF communication packages toward thermal packaging for power semiconductors; quarterly operating profit stayed positive from Q1 to Q3 2026, but net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals only KRW 2.89 billion.

02

Business structure

Kostecsys is a specialist maker of semiconductor packaging materials and components, focusing on parts that spread heat from high-power chips and improve package reliability.

Its core line began with RF communication packages, including gallium nitride (GaN) RF power semiconductor packages and silicon carbide (SiC) heat-dissipating spacers for power semiconductors.

Per Etoday's report citing DART filings, cumulative January–September 2026 sales were KRW 12.96 billion for RF communication packages and about KRW 2.8 billion for power-semiconductor spacers.

The company is widening its thermal packaging range, which includes spacers, block bonding, embedded copper coin and pin-fin base plates, built on low-expansion, high-heat-conduction material technology.

It is moving away from a structure centered on a single RF product toward AI data center and power semiconductor applications, and it emphasizes a one-stop system covering material development, package design, simulation and mass production.

The Plant 3 block bonding center was planned for completion at the end of 2025 and was later reported as completed on June 30, 2026, with annual capacity of about KRW 50 billion. A KRW 59.4 billion long-term contract with U.S. customer T is being executed through quarterly confirmed orders.

The company has completed supplier registration with a Tier-1 power semiconductor customer and received a purchase order for mass-production molds and initial samples of its next-generation pin-fin base plate, although mass production still requires prototype evaluation, reliability verification and production approval.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell 54.4% from KRW 25.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.5 billion in 2023, the low point, and then rose to KRW 14.2 billion in 2024 (up 23.1%) and KRW 15.2 billion in 2025 (up 7.1%), marking two consecutive years of growth. Of the 2022–2025 period, 2022 was the year with the largest revenue.

Operating profit moved from KRW 3.57 billion in 2022 (operating margin 14.1%) to an operating loss of KRW 1.32 billion in 2023, a swing into loss, and the loss widened to KRW 1.89 billion in 2024.

Operating profit of KRW 184 million in 2025 (operating margin 1.2%) marked a return to profit, which the company attributed to wider adoption of its products in high-power, high-heat applications such as AI data centers and power semiconductors.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss in each of the four years from 2022 to 2025, with losses of KRW 1.08 billion, KRW 11.4 billion, KRW 1.79 billion and KRW 563 million.

The 2023 net loss was far larger than that year's operating loss, which suggests a large hit from non-operating items; a Hana Securities report from May 2024 shows a large other non-operating loss in 2023 and a large financial-income loss in 2022.

Quarterly, the operating loss narrowed to KRW 320 million in Q3 2025 and turned into an operating profit of KRW 1.24 billion in Q4 2025. Operating profit was KRW 1.13 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 1.19 billion in Q2 2026, then fell to KRW 761 million in Q3 2026, when the operating margin was 15.3% according to Etoday.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 256 million, down 71.6% from KRW 900 million in Q2, and the gap between operating profit and net profit points to non-operating items and taxes that need checking in the quarterly report.

Operating cash flow was KRW 6.06 billion in 2022, then negative in 2023 and 2024 at -KRW 6.08 billion and -KRW 1.34 billion, and turned positive at KRW 539 million in 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 274.3% in 2022 to 46.1% in 2023, then rose to 151.1% in 2025, when total liabilities of KRW 33.7 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 22.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The demand driver for high-heat packaging is AI data centers and power semiconductors. The company argues that rising power density in AI data centers and the wider use of double-sided cooling in next-generation EV power semiconductors are making structural parts that move heat and current efficiently more important.

In the SiC power semiconductor market, the share held by onsemi and STMicroelectronics exceeds 50%, which makes it a concentrated structure. Hana Securities reported in May 2024 that Kostecsys was understood to have passed quality testing for SiC spacers for onsemi and that an order was expected within that year.

Whether that expectation shows up in 2026 revenue mix cannot be checked directly, because the filings do not break out spacer sales by customer. RF communication packages are at a different point in the cycle.

In Q3 2025 the company said results had stalled on a slowdown in downstream industries, and it expected gradual recovery in RF communication package demand, with new orders from telecom infrastructure investment.

For 2026 first-half sales growth, the company pointed to a higher share of high-value thermal management packages for AI infrastructure and power semiconductors. The RF and spacer businesses therefore sit in different phases of their cycles.

In comparison with listed packaging peers, the small annual revenue base of about KRW 15.2 billion and differences in customer mix are the key variables.

06

Outlook

The company says it will focus in the second half of 2026 on stabilizing operations at the block bonding center and raising production efficiency, expand supply of high-value thermal packaging products including spacers, and push ahead with mass-production readiness for new projects.

In November 2025 the company said it would enter a structural growth phase from Q4 2025 on the back of entry into the AI data center market and capacity expansion, and it expected a steadier growth phase from 2026. No official full-year revenue guidance from the company was found in the sources.

Kiwoom Securities projected 2026 revenue of KRW 49.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 10.3 billion in a February 2026 report.

However, cumulative sales for January–September 2026 were around KRW 15.7 billion per Etoday's report, so a large part of the projection would need to come through in the final quarter, and how this gap narrows will be the key test for full-year results.

Pin-fin base plate work for the next-generation product is in progress with mold production and initial sample supply, and the company says it will move to full mass production after prototype evaluation, reliability verification and production approval.

In Q1 2026 the company cited expanded customer quality certification as a driver of higher supply, and whether that certification carries into mass production is the next step.

The company has described higher utilization of the block bonding center as a main driver of 2026 results, but no utilization figures have been disclosed.

Bull factors

Revenue mix moving toward thermal products

Per Etoday's report, cumulative January–September 2026 power-semiconductor spacer sales were about KRW 2.8 billion.

The completed block bonding center adds annual capacity of about KRW 50 billion, and if the KRW 59.4 billion contract with customer T keeps moving into quarterly confirmed orders, the spacer share could grow.

The company's December 2025 investor materials described new revenue sources as accelerating a qualitative change in the business structure.

Operating profit positive quarter after quarter

Operating profit turned positive in 2025 and then reached KRW 1.13 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 1.19 billion in Q2 2026, staying above KRW 1 billion for two straight quarters.

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 761 million at a 15.3% operating margin, and the company said its shift toward a profitability-focused business structure is taking hold. Compared with an operating margin of -13.3% in 2024, the change in profitability shows up clearly in the quarterly filings.

Tier-1 supplier registration and wider product range

The company completed supplier registration with a Tier-1 power semiconductor customer and received a purchase order for mass-production molds and initial samples of its next-generation pin-fin base plate. Its product range now spans spacers, block bonding, embedded copper coin and pin-fin base plates.

Rising power density in AI data centers and wider use of double-sided cooling in EV power semiconductors are cited as drivers of demand for high-heat structural parts.

Bear factors

Three straight quarterly revenue declines and a gap to full-year forecasts

Revenue fell for three straight quarters from Q1 to Q3 2026, and cumulative January–September sales were about KRW 15.7 billion. Kiwoom Securities projected 2026 revenue of KRW 49.1 billion in February 2026, which would require a large share of that figure in the final quarter.

Hana Securities' May 2024 projection of KRW 27.4 billion for 2024 revenue was also far above the KRW 14.2 billion reported.

Small earnings base with large quarterly swings

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 71.6% from the prior quarter to KRW 256 million in Q3 2026. The last four quarters total only KRW 2.89 billion, so a single quarter's movement can shift margins and multiples sharply.

The company recorded losses on an attributable basis in each year from 2022 to 2025, so the profitable stretch is still short, which bears point to as a weakness.

RF-centered revenue and reliance on a few customers

According to Etoday's report of DART data, cumulative January–September 2026 sales of RF communication packages were KRW 12.96 billion, still the large majority of revenue. In Q3 2025 the company said results had stalled on a slowdown in downstream industries.

Heavy reliance on a few customers, such as customer T and a Tier-1 supplier, could make quarterly results sensitive to changes in order timing.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and order timing

Orders from customer T are confirmed quarter by quarter under the long-term contract, so any gap in orders would move quarterly revenue directly. How the three-quarter revenue decline relates to this ordering pattern needs to be checked in the quarterly filings.

The company is broadening its base through more Tier-1 suppliers, but when that shows up in revenue is still to be seen.

Mass-production transition and quality certification

The pin-fin base plate is at the mold and initial sample stage, and it must pass prototype evaluation, reliability verification and production approval before full mass production. The timing follows the customer's development schedule, so the company cannot set it alone. If approval is delayed, utilization of the block bonding capacity could fall short of expectations.

Balance sheet and cash flow

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 33.7 billion, above total equity of KRW 22.3 billion, and the debt ratio stood at 151.1%. Liabilities have risen sharply from the end of 2024.

Operating cash flow was negative in 2023 and 2024 and turned positive at KRW 539 million in 2025, so whether cash generation holds up quarter by quarter alongside capital spending and borrowing matters.

Overall view

Kostecsys recorded net losses attributable to controlling shareholders in each of the four years from 2022 to 2025, then turned operating profit positive in 2025, and quarterly operating profit stayed positive from Q1 to Q3 2026.

Its revenue mix is moving from RF communication packages toward thermal packaging for power semiconductors, and RF still accounts for the larger share on a cumulative January–September 2026 basis.

Bullish factors include the completed block bonding center with annual capacity of about KRW 50 billion, quarterly execution of the long-term contract with customer T, and progress toward mass production after Tier-1 supplier registration.

Bearish factors include three straight quarters of revenue decline in 2026, a gap between cumulative January–September sales and outside full-year forecasts, a small earnings base of KRW 2.89 billion over the last four quarters, and a 151.1% debt ratio.

The earnings multiple sits at a high level relative to that earnings base, so it is sensitive to quarterly profit changes. The next checkpoints are the Q4 and full-year 2026 preliminary results in early February 2027, the 2026 annual business report due by the end of March 2027, and Q1 2027 results in mid-May.

Operating margin and cash flow in the next regular report will indicate whether the shift to profitability holds.
