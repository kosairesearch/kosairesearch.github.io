Purit: A Semiconductor Refining Chemicals Maker in a Capacity Expansion Phase

Purit is a refiner of chemicals used mainly in semiconductors. Sales rose for three straight quarters through Q3 2026, but Q3 operating profit declined, so the next checkpoint is whether the KRW 35 billion new plant and KRW 17.5 billion equipment project lift capacity in 2027.

02

Business structure

Purit, founded in 2010, supplies materials for advanced IT industries including semiconductors, displays and secondary batteries, and it listed on KOSDAQ on October 18, 2023. Its core business is refining and synthesizing ultra-high-purity chemicals used in semiconductor processes.

Industry analysis points to its refining know-how, built over more than a decade, as its biggest competitive edge, extending to adjusting chemical structures and controlling impurities. Semiconductor materials form the center of the portfolio, followed by display and industrial materials.

In a 2024 analysis, display materials fell sharply as LCD lines shrank, becoming the main cause of the company-wide decline. Beyond refining and synthesis, it runs a recycling business that re-refines waste solvents.

In the second half of 2025, iM Securities said synthesis-based products carry higher operating margins than refined or recycled products, and that rising EL share in Samsung Electronics' semiconductor thinner raw materials would drive earnings growth.

Its customers are mainly domestic chipmakers including Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, and around its listing it was also pursuing global customers such as Intel and DuPont.

Unlike large listed chemical companies with heavy commodity petrochemical mixes, Purit is a small-to-mid-sized company specialized in semiconductor refining chemicals.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales fell from KRW 137.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 120.5 billion in 2023, down 12.3% year on year, then recovered to KRW 122.5 billion in 2024, up 1.7%, and KRW 142.5 billion in 2025, up 16.3%.

After the 2023 low, sales rose for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in 2022–2025. Operating profit was KRW 14.3 billion in 2022, KRW 12.8 billion in 2023 (down 10.1%), KRW 14 billion in 2024 (up 8.7%) and KRW 17.2 billion in 2025 (up 23.3%).

In 2025, operating profit growth outpaced sales growth, lifting the operating margin from 11.4% to 12.1%. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 11.6 billion in 2022, KRW 12.1 billion in 2023 (up 4.6%), KRW 11.8 billion in 2024 (down 2.2%) and KRW 15.2 billion in 2025 (up 28.2%).

In 2024, operating profit rose but profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell, so operating gains did not carry through to the bottom line. Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts for each year: KRW 11.6 billion, KRW 12.1 billion, KRW 11.8 billion and KRW 15.2 billion.

The balance sheet became lighter, with the debt-to-equity ratio falling from 67.1% in 2022 to 14.2% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 15.3 billion and total equity was KRW 107.5 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 17.5 billion in 2022, KRW 12.8 billion in 2023, KRW 14.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 22.3 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales bottomed at KRW 36.4 billion in Q4 2025 (down 1.5% quarter on quarter), then rose to KRW 37.8 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 44.9 billion in Q2 (up 18.8%) and KRW 47.4 billion in Q3 (up 5.6% quarter on quarter and 28.4% year on year), marking three straight quarters of growth.

Operating profit climbed to KRW 5.96 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 6.49 billion in Q2, then fell to KRW 6.03 billion in Q3 (down 7.0% quarter on quarter, up 14.9% year on year). In Q3, higher sales did not lift operating profit.

For Q2, Shinhan Investment Corp. said raw material cost increases were offset by low-cost inventory and a June price increase, with a weaker won (strong dollar) providing a favorable currency gain. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 18.7 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Industry reports broadly agree that the chip cycle is recovering, supported by rising demand for server HBM and growing AI data center investment.

Higher capital spending by Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, along with finer circuit patterns and more layered integrated circuits, raises demand for front-end process materials. Purit's path to benefit runs through utilization and production expansion at customer lines.

In Q1 2024, higher semiconductor utilization lifted semiconductor material sales, while display materials fell sharply as LCD lines shrank.

After Japan's export restrictions, Korea's push for semiconductor material self-sufficiency has strengthened, and analysts describe Purit as a niche core supplier in ultra-high-purity chemicals. Display demand follows a different cycle from chips because it depends on customer line reductions.

The secondary battery materials business, which is at the stage of localizing battery electrolyte additives and binder organic solvents, still requires checking on mass production timing and demand scale.

The recycling market is growing with ESG demands and tighter regulation, and new waste treatment licenses are rarely issued, which keeps entry barriers high. Semiconductor materials peers within the sector that are often compared include Foosung, ENF Technology and Lake Materials.

Purit is a small-to-mid-sized company built around refining technology, so its customer base and business structure differ from large petrochemical groups.

06

Outlook

On April 20, 2026, Purit's board approved building a new plant and equipment investment at Yesan Industrial Complex 2 in Chungcheongnam-do, according to a disclosure. The investment totals KRW 35 billion, aimed at expanding production capacity, with an investment period from April 20, 2026 through June 30, 2027.

The company said the amount and end date may change depending on construction progress and business conditions.

On October 28, 2026, it decided on a KRW 17.5 billion new facility investment for distillation columns and related equipment to produce recycled and industrial products, with an investment period through December 31, 2027. This amount equals 16.29% of equity.

At its 2023 listing, Purit said it would build a third plant of about 10,000 pyeong in Yesan, targeting capacity 2.5 times its prior level including the first and second plant expansions.

Shinhan Investment Corp. analyzed in its September 30, 2026 report that the company is entering a phase where new capacity expands its revenue base through localization of core materials.

In secondary batteries, Purit was reported in 2023 to be developing battery electrolyte additives and binder organic solvents with mass production targeted for 2024 or 2025. In recycling, it plans a new business to refine NMP waste liquid.

The next checkpoints are whether the new plant runs around its June 30, 2027 expected investment end date, and whether the distillation equipment contributes to recycled product sales. Exchange rates and raw material prices also remain variables for second-half margins.

08

Bull factors

Capacity expansion phase

Construction of a KRW 35 billion new plant and a KRW 17.5 billion distillation equipment project are under way. Shinhan Investment Corp. analyzed that new capacity is expanding the company's revenue base. At its listing, the company had set a target of capacity 2.5 times its prior level. If the plants run in line with demand, the revenue base could widen.

Localization and customer diversification

In the second half of 2025, iM Securities analyzed that EL's share in Samsung Electronics' semiconductor thinner raw materials had expanded. Stronger policies for domestic material self-sufficiency also work in the company's favor. Growing direct transactions with global customers and rising export share support the case for diversification.

Earnings base and financial capacity

The operating margin rose from 10.4% in 2022 to 12.1% in 2025. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 18.7 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 14.2% in 2025, and operating cash flow was KRW 22.3 billion in 2025. This financial capacity supports funding for facility investment.

09

Bear factors

Q3 sales growth did not reach profit

Q3 2026 sales rose 5.6% quarter on quarter to KRW 47.4 billion. Operating profit, however, fell 7.0% to KRW 6.03 billion, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 8.1% to KRW 4.83 billion. Sales growth without a matching gain in margin is a bearish factor.

Margin pressure from raw materials and currency

Shinhan Investment Corp. noted that geopolitical risk in the Middle East pushed oil above USD 100, adding pressure on raw material prices. It also said a sharp fall in the won could move second-half margins by 2–3 percentage points. The currency gain that helped in Q2 could reverse in direction.

Display decline and earnings volatility

Display materials fell sharply in Q1 2024 as LCD lines shrank. In 2024, operating profit rose while profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 2.2%. Annual results showed swings between 2023 and 2024. Profit exposure to customer lines and business structure is a bearish point.

10

Risk factors

Customer and cycle exposure

Sales depend heavily on the utilization and investment of domestic chip customers such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. A downturn in the chip cycle could reduce refined chemical volumes. Display sits on a separate cycle, so weakness there could weigh on overall results.

Investment execution and utilization

The company said the new plant's investment amount and end date may change with construction progress and business conditions. The distillation equipment project runs through December 31, 2027. Even if new capacity starts, fixed costs could rise if demand does not follow.

Raw materials, regulation and ownership

Raw material prices and currency moves can swing margins. Because new waste treatment licenses are rarely issued, the pace of recycling expansion could be limited. A 2024 analysis showed the largest shareholder side held a stake above 50%, which may limit the float.

Overall view

Purit is a small-to-mid-sized chemical company specialized in semiconductor refining chemicals. On a disclosed basis, its operating margin rose from 10.4% in 2022 to 12.1% in 2025, and its debt-to-equity ratio fell to 14.2%.

Sales rose for three straight quarters from Q1 to Q3 2026, but Q3 operating profit fell quarter on quarter to KRW 6.03 billion, so profit shows a slowdown signal. If the KRW 35 billion new plant and KRW 17.5 billion equipment project enter operation during 2027, the effect of capacity expansion should become visible.

Bullish points include capacity expansion, localization and customer diversification. Bearish points include raw material and currency swings, the display cycle, and earnings volatility. Shinhan Investment Corp. described the company as entering a capacity expansion phase but did not assign an investment rating.

Valuation reads as a relationship to past multiple ranges. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual report, and the timing of the new plant's completion and start-up.
