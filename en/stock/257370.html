02

Business structure

People & Technology Ms, founded in October 2001 as an industrial machinery maker, listed on KOSDAQ in 2018. Its core product is production equipment for lithium-ion battery separators, and it is reported to be the first domestic company with full-line separator production design and manufacturing capability.

It also has a record in display equipment, so revenue depends heavily on the timing of equipment deliveries.

In 2022 its parent PNT (People & Technology) became the largest shareholder through a third-party allocation rights issue, and since then the separator equipment role has been emphasized within PNT's vertically integrated structure spanning battery materials, equipment and cells.

Trading was halted in 2020 over disclosure defaults, and resumed in October 2024 as the company returned to KOSDAQ.

At the March 2026 general shareholders meeting, export-import, AI platform development and security equipment items were added to its business purposes, which the company attributed to business diversification. On the customer side, a 2025 analysis raised concerns about heavy dependence on specific customers.

Overseas, reports in early October 2026 said the company signed an MOU with Thailand's small and medium enterprise agency and is pursuing a Thai local subsidiary. The company is thus widening its base beyond domestic equipment orders through overseas separator equipment supply and new business purposes.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results in the DART annual filings show revenue of KRW 6.22 billion and an operating loss of KRW 5.91 billion in 2022, followed by revenue of KRW 25 billion in 2023, up 302.6% year on year, with an operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.06 billion.

In 2024 revenue reached KRW 26.2 billion, up 4.7%, and operating profit of KRW 1.18 billion turned the company profitable, with an operating margin of 4.5%; 2024 was the largest revenue year among 2022 to 2025.

In 2025 revenue fell 57.7% to KRW 11.1 billion, and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 2.12 billion, an operating margin of -19.2%. Attributable net loss was KRW 7.33 billion, compared with attributable net profit of KRW 1.44 billion in 2024.

For the nine months of 2025, reported drivers were lower demand for separator manufacturing equipment and reduced display equipment orders, with cost and fixed-cost burdens further weighing on profitability.

The 2024 return to profit also came with a sharp rise in inventory: KRW 16.2 billion at end-2024, up 144% from KRW 6.6 billion in 2023, and inventory turnover fell from 3.77 to 1.62 times (a March 2025 analysis by Newsworker).

Operating cash flow was -KRW 1.14 billion in 2022, KRW 6.94 billion in 2023, -KRW 1.25 billion in 2024 and -KRW 412 million in 2025.

Quarterly, revenue rose from KRW 361 million in Q3 2025 with an operating loss of KRW 482 million, to KRW 2.91 billion in Q4 2025 (up 706.6% quarter on quarter), though the operating loss widened to KRW 994 million.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 764 million (down 73.8% quarter on quarter) with an operating loss of KRW 1.49 billion. Q2 2026 revenue jumped to KRW 7.2 billion (up 842.3% quarter on quarter), producing operating profit of KRW 707 million.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 1.11 billion (down 84.6% quarter on quarter, up 206.5% year on year), with an operating loss of KRW 1.17 billion, returning the company to a loss. Because quarterly revenue swings so widely, the Q2 2026 profit lasted only one quarter.

05

Industry analysis

The company's results are closely tied to the timing of orders for lithium-ion separator equipment and display equipment, a typical equipment-cycle structure.

In the first nine months of 2025, lower demand for separator manufacturing equipment and reduced display equipment orders occurred at the same time, and revenue fell sharply. The temporary recovery in Q2 2026 followed by a decline in Q3 2026 appears to reflect the mismatch between order timing and delivery timing.

In separator equipment, the company is reported to be the first domestic player with full-line design and manufacturing capability, which is the main technical differentiator cited.

Thailand's government has outlined a plan to build separator equipment first and then grow battery technology and equipment step by step into a national strategic industry, according to a Moneytoday report from early October 2026.

The company has also highlighted that a Thai entry would be the first in the separator equipment field. Order continuity, however, will depend on domestic battery investment pace and the timing of overseas orders.

Its parent PNT is reported to run a vertically integrated structure spanning battery materials, equipment and cells, which raises the possibility of group-level volume linkages. Market share and backlog relative to peers in the same sector are not confirmed.

Because the business is tied to a single equipment cycle, earnings volatility relative to industry conditions is comparatively high.

06

Outlook

The near-term items the company has publicly described are establishing a Thai local subsidiary and concluding separator equipment supply contracts within 2026.

According to an early October 2026 report, the company is preparing a contract for two sets of separator equipment and is pursuing a contract to supply core battery component production equipment to Thailand by year-end.

Its chief executive has described a step-by-step expansion from separator equipment into the full range of battery process equipment (Moneytoday). The company has also reportedly started developing AI-based technology to improve yield and quality.

Based on the Q3 2026 periodic filing, revenue was KRW 1.11 billion and the operating loss was KRW 1.17 billion; Q4 2026 and full-year 2026 results have not yet been filed in a periodic report. The direction of full-year 2026 revenue and operating margin will therefore be settled only in the 2026 annual business report.

The next checkpoint is whether the Thai subsidiary and equipment contracts are actually signed. If they are, the scale of overseas revenue will be the next item to watch. It also needs to be confirmed whether the Q2 2026 revenue jump reflected a one-time delivery or a repeatable order flow.

Whether the newly added export-import, AI platform and security equipment businesses generate revenue will also show up in future periodic reports.

Bull factors

A Profitable Quarter Has Been Recorded

Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 7.2 billion rose 842.3% quarter on quarter, and operating profit of KRW 707 million turned the quarter positive. Compared with the KRW 1.49 billion operating loss in Q1 2026, this shows how quickly the profit structure can change in a quarter with heavy deliveries. However, the profit did not extend beyond that single quarter.

Full-Line Separator Capability and Overseas Expansion

The company is reported to be the first domestic player with full-line separator production design and manufacturing capability. In early October 2026 it signed an MOU with Thailand's small and medium enterprise agency and is pursuing a Thai subsidiary and a contract for two sets of separator equipment within the year. If the overseas contracts materialize, they could ease the swings in domestic equipment orders.

Links to the Parent's Battery Value Chain

PNT (People & Technology), the largest shareholder, is reported to have a vertically integrated structure spanning battery materials, equipment and cells. People & Technology Ms plays the separator equipment role within that structure. Equity attributable to shareholders was KRW 21.5 billion at end-2025, so the capital base remains in place.

Bear factors

The 2025 Revenue Drop and Return to Losses

Revenue in 2025 fell 57.7% to KRW 11.1 billion, and the company returned to losses with an operating loss of KRW 2.12 billion and an attributable net loss of KRW 7.33 billion.

For the nine months of 2025, lower separator equipment demand, reduced display equipment orders, and cost and fixed-cost burdens were cited together. The results showed how sharply earnings depend on the equipment order cycle.

Balance Sheet Strain and Inventory Burden

Total liabilities of KRW 31.4 billion at end-2025 exceeded total equity of KRW 21.5 billion, and the debt ratio rose from 101.3% in 2024 to 145.8%. Inventory reached KRW 16.2 billion at end-2024, up 144% from the prior year, and an analysis noted lower inventory turnover. Operating cash flow was negative in both 2024 and 2025.

Limited R&D Spending and Customer Concentration

A March 2025 analysis put 2024 R&D spending at KRW 130 million, about 0.5% of revenue. Excessive dependence on specific customers was also flagged as an urgent challenge. Q3 2026 revenue falling back to KRW 1.11 billion suggests that the timing of large deliveries feeds directly into results.

10

Risk factors

Equipment order cycle

The 2025 results showed that when separator and display equipment orders decline, revenue can fall by more than half within a single year. Quarterly revenue also swung from KRW 2.91 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 764 million in Q1 2026. If order lead times lengthen, profit and loss could keep changing from quarter to quarter.

Financial and cash flow risk

A higher debt ratio combined with rising inventory could increase working capital pressure. An analysis noted that total liabilities of KRW 31.4 billion against total equity of KRW 21.5 billion could create repayment pressure if interest rates rise or deliveries are delayed (Newsworker, March 2025). Operating cash flow was also negative in both 2024 and 2025.

Trading history and governance factors

Trading was halted in 2020 over disclosure defaults and resumed in October 2024. Management uncertainty eased after the change in largest shareholder, but given earlier embezzlement and breach-of-trust issues, governance review remains necessary. Together with customer dependence, earnings volatility from external factors also remains.

Overall view

People & Technology Ms has shown revenue and earnings that swing sharply with equipment orders.

After returning to profit in 2024, revenue fell to KRW 11.1 billion and the operating loss widened to KRW 2.12 billion in 2025, and the Q2 2026 profit did not carry into Q3, which posted an operating loss of KRW 1.17 billion.

On the balance sheet, total liabilities of KRW 31.4 billion against total equity of KRW 21.5 billion at end-2025 raised the debt ratio, alongside inventory burdens and negative operating cash flow.

Strengths cited include full-line separator capability, the Thai expansion effort, and links to the parent's battery value chain. Weaknesses cited include exposure to the equipment order cycle, dependence on specific customers, and R&D burden.

On valuation, no earnings-based multiple can be calculated, and the price-to-book ratio sits below 1x. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 and preliminary full-year 2026 results, the financial structure in the 2026 annual business report, and whether the Thai contracts are concluded.
