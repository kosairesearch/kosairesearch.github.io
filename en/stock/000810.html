Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance: Core Recovery and Shareholder Returns in Focus

Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance's cumulative controlling-shareholder net income for January to September 2026 rose 4.6% year on year on a preliminary basis, but third-quarter profit fell from the prior quarter. The durability of the core recovery and the timing of a concrete shareholder return policy are the next issues.

02

Business structure

Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance has a non-life insurance structure built on long-term insurance, auto insurance and general insurance, supplemented by investment income from its asset portfolio.

For January to September 2026, the long-term insurance segment's cumulative insurance profit rose 1.1% to KRW 1.2309 trillion on improved retention of protection-type policies and loss-ratio management (preliminary IR basis).

New contract CSM in the segment reached KRW 1.84 trillion over the same period, and the total CSM balance rose KRW 667.4 billion from year-end 2025 to KRW 14.84 trillion. The segment's risk loss ratio was 97.9% in the third quarter, similar to a year earlier.

Auto insurance prioritized portfolio quality over expansion, with cumulative insurance revenue slightly lower at KRW 4.1351 trillion, while cumulative insurance profit was a KRW 39.2 billion surplus.

On a standalone third-quarter basis, auto insurance profit was KRW 19.1 billion, turning from a KRW 64.8 billion loss a year earlier. General insurance cumulative insurance revenue rose 11.9% to KRW 1.4493 trillion, with a cumulative loss ratio of 62.4%.

Investment operations posted a cumulative investment yield of 3.33% and investment income of KRW 2.404 trillion on invested assets, up 3.9%. Overseas, the company raised its stake in Canopius to 80.6% to secure management control, following strategic investments since 2019.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on disclosed results, insurance revenue rose from KRW 15.2 trillion in 2022 to KRW 18.6 trillion in 2025. The 2025 increase of 7.9% was faster than the 4.8% rise in 2024. Operating profit grew from KRW 2.04 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.66 trillion in 2025, but the 2025 gain was only 0.4%, so earnings growth slowed.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 2.07 trillion in 2024, the highest in 2022 to 2025, and fell 2.7% to KRW 2.02 trillion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 2.02 trillion in 2025 and should be read separately from the controlling-shareholder figure.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 545.2% in 2022 to 353.0% in 2025, and total equity at end-2025 was KRW 21.3 trillion. Operating cash flow moved from -KRW 562.1 billion in 2023 to KRW 3.13 trillion in 2024.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit fell from KRW 664.1 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 391.3 billion in the fourth quarter, a 41.1% decline, then rebounded to KRW 861.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 885.3 billion in the second quarter.

It fell to KRW 577.4 billion in the third quarter, down 34.8% from the prior quarter and 13.0% from a year earlier. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 234.7 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 634.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 737.7 billion in the second quarter.

It then fell to KRW 493.2 billion in the third quarter, down 33.1% from the prior quarter and 8.3% from a year earlier. The last four quarters total KRW 2.1 trillion in controlling-shareholder net income.

Behind the third-quarter decline, long-term insurance profit was about KRW 351 billion, down 8.7% from a year earlier, as the expense variance swung from a gain of about KRW 7 billion to a loss of about KRW 35 billion.

Korea Investment & Securities said the rise in indirect expenses had no confirmed cause, treated it as a one-off, and did not reflect it in its estimates. Third-quarter investment income was KRW 255.5 billion, down 23% from a year earlier.

05

Industry analysis

In non-life insurance, rising repair costs are pushing up the cost per claim in auto insurance. Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance has responded by improving contract quality, which reduced accident rates and raised earned premium per coverage, according to the company.

On the policy side, Sangsangin Securities projected in June 2026 that the 8-week rule for minor patients and the inclusion of physical therapy in managed-care payments would gradually improve insurance profit in the second half.

Investment profit depends on market interest rates, and the same report judged that rates already reflect much of the possibility of a policy rate hike, which should lower volatility.

On competition, Korea Investment & Securities said in its November 2026 report that Samsung Fire's earnings volatility was the lowest among insurers.

Hanwha Investment & Securities said in its September 2026 report that the company showed the strongest fundamentals among listed insurers, citing improved internal expense variance, a low CSM adjustment rate and ample dividend capacity.

Compared with listed peers such as DB Insurance and HYUNDAI MARINE&FIRE INSURANCE CO, Samsung Fire's capital strength and room for shareholder returns stand out. Growth in new long-term insurance contracts, however, depends on sales conditions and regulation across the industry.

06

Outlook

The company has set a target of a 50% shareholder return ratio by 2028. Korea Investment & Securities said the return expectation tied to large dividend income is the key investment point, and flagged the delay in presenting a concrete shareholder return policy as a concern.

BNK Investment & Securities mentioned in its July 2026 report the possibility of a special dividend in 2027, alongside a 40% dividend payout and annual share cancellations of 2.5 to 3.0 percentage points.

The Canopius deal involves a total investment of USD 247 million, of which USD 167 million will raise the stake to 80.6%, and assumes closing at the end of March 2027.

The company said cumulative equity-method investment gains from Canopius for January to September 2026 were KRW 242.4 billion, up 200.9% from a year earlier.

Korea's press reported that about KRW 2.2647 trillion will go into the additional Canopius purchase and that the solvency ratio is expected to fall 25 percentage points, while the return target is maintained.

Sangsangin Securities forecast 2026 net income attributable to controlling shareholders at KRW 2.2 trillion, up 10.3% from a year earlier, on the assumption that Canopius equity income and Samsung Electronics dividend income support earnings.

The next earnings test is whether long-term insurance expense variance and auto insurance profit move in the same direction seen in the third quarter.

08

Bull factors

Auto insurance back in profit

Auto insurance profit in the third quarter was KRW 19.1 billion, turning from a KRW 64.8 billion loss a year earlier. Cumulative insurance profit for the segment is a KRW 39.2 billion surplus. The company attributed this to lower accident rates and higher earned premium per coverage from improved contract quality. Whether the surplus holds in upcoming quarters is the point to watch.

Expanding shareholder returns

The company has set a 50% shareholder return ratio target for 2028. BNK Investment & Securities pointed to a 40% dividend payout, annual share cancellations of 2.5 to 3.0 percentage points, and the possibility of a special dividend in 2027.

Korea Investment & Securities described the return expectation tied to large dividend income as a key investment point.

Capital strength and Samsung Electronics stake

Sangsangin Securities said the industry-leading capital strength, the Samsung Electronics stake and clearer shareholder returns should sustain a premium within non-life insurers. iM Securities said the company holds a stake of about 1.5% in Samsung Electronics. Hanwha Investment & Securities cited low volatility in its capital ratio as a strength.

Bear factors

Third-quarter earnings decline

Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 577.4 billion, down 34.8% from the prior quarter, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 493.2 billion, down 8.3% from a year earlier. Investment income fell 23% to KRW 255.5 billion. Cumulative gains alongside a single-quarter decline are a bearish point.

Long-term expense variance and new CSM

Long-term insurance profit in the third quarter was about KRW 351 billion, down 8.7% from a year earlier. The expense variance swung from a gain of about KRW 7 billion to a loss of about KRW 35 billion. Hana Securities analysis said new contract CSM fell 16% in 2025, so concern about slower new business growth remains.

Actuarial assumption changes and profit volatility

Sangsangin Securities said actuarial assumption changes in the second quarter of 2026 could raise volatility in insurance profit. It noted that more conservative assumptions on new-coverage loss ratios, non-life renewable loss ratios and expense assumptions could slightly reduce CSM amortization gains. This volatility needs to be tracked quarter by quarter.

Risk factors

Auto insurance profit volatility

The auto insurance surplus is small at KRW 19.1 billion for the third quarter and KRW 39.2 billion cumulative. Rising repair costs are increasing the cost per claim, which could offset gains from lower accident rates. The segment is small relative to full-year earnings but can change the direction of quarterly profit.

Interest rates and asset markets

Korea Investment & Securities said third-quarter asset trading and valuation gains were KRW 25 billion below expectations, and a KRW 30 billion trust loss was reflected. Investment income is sensitive to interest rates and asset market swings. The cumulative investment yield of 3.33% was stable, but quarterly volatility remains.

Overseas acquisition and capital burden

The funds going into the additional Canopius purchase are a factor that lowers the solvency ratio. The total purchase amount is USD 247 million, with closing assumed for the end of March 2027. A smaller capital cushion could affect the pace of expanding shareholder returns.

12

Overall view

Based on disclosed results, insurance revenue rose three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. Cumulative net income attributable to controlling shareholders for January to September 2026 rose 4.6% year on year on a preliminary basis.

However, third-quarter operating profit fell 34.8% from the prior quarter to KRW 577.4 billion, and the long-term insurance expense variance turned into a loss. Auto insurance returned to a KRW 19.1 billion surplus in the third quarter, supporting the profit recovery case.

A 50% shareholder return target by 2028 has been set, but the timing of a concrete policy is still open. The Canopius acquisition broadens the overseas base but carries a capital burden reflected in an expected solvency ratio decline.

Sell-side target prices diverge widely, and valuation methods and ratings differ by broker. The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual report, the Canopius closing and the first-quarter 2027 results.
