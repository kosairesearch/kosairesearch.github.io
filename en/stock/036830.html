02

Business structure

Soulbrain Holdings (036830) is a KOSDAQ-listed holding company whose operations run mainly through its subsidiaries.

Its main subsidiary, Soulbrain (357780), was established in 2020 through a spin-off of the semiconductor, display, secondary-battery electrolyte and electronics-related chemical materials business from Soulbrain Holdings, and listed on KOSDAQ the same year.

According to a January 2026 estimate by Shinhan Investment Corp., the semiconductor segment (etchants, precursors and slurries) accounted for most of the subsidiary's revenue in the fourth quarter of 2025.

Brokers note that the etchant's raw materials are mineral-based, such as fluorite and phosphoric compounds, so it is less exposed to oil-price swings than rivals that use petrochemical solvents. Chip materials revenue depends directly on customers' wafer input, so their equipment schedules shape the business.

The holding's bio-healthcare segment is described as growing on the Korean Wave, rising recognition of Korean cosmetic brands, and expansion of the OEM and ODM industry.

The secondary-battery segment has completed production capacity expansion at its U.S. and Hungarian subsidiaries in line with EV and energy storage growth. Subsidiary Soulbrain is building a next-generation materials portfolio and expanding into glass substrate processing materials.

Brokers say the display segment is in a structural adjustment phase but contributes a limited share of earnings. Consolidated results follow the figures in DART periodic reports.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 565.1 billion in 2022 to a peak of KRW 661.6 billion in 2023 (up 17.1% year on year), fell to KRW 515.4 billion in 2024 (down 22.1%), and recovered to KRW 556.6 billion in 2025 (up 8.0%).

Operating profit was KRW 73.5 billion in 2022 and KRW 121 billion in 2023 (up 64.6%), the largest year in the period, then declined to KRW 85.3 billion in 2024 (down 29.5%) and KRW 8.86 billion in 2025 (down 89.6%), a two-year decline from 2024 through 2025.

Operating margin moved from 13.0% in 2022 and 18.3% in 2023 to 16.6% in 2024 and 1.6% in 2025, making 2025 the year of sharpest margin erosion.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 743.2% to KRW 495.6 billion in 2025 after KRW 58.8 billion in 2024, and net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 495.6 billion.

Operating profit fell while net profit surged, so the size of non-operating items and one-off factors should be checked in the notes to the annual report. Operating cash flow was KRW 46.6 billion in 2025, down from KRW 85.1 billion in 2024, and remains far below controlling-shareholder profit.

Quarterly results started with revenue of KRW 98.4 billion and an operating loss of KRW 15.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025. Revenue then rose to KRW 140.2 billion in the fourth quarter (up 42.5% from the prior quarter), but the operating loss widened to KRW 18.4 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue reached KRW 176.3 billion (up 25.7% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 1.49 billion turned the result positive. The second quarter showed revenue of KRW 208.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 31.9 billion (up 2,037.7% quarter on quarter).

The third quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 209.1 billion, roughly flat on the quarter (up 0.2%), and operating profit of KRW 18.5 billion, down 42.0% quarter on quarter but a swing to profit from an operating loss a year earlier.

Revenue has grown for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totals KRW 446.9 billion.

Industry analysis

Holding-company results depend heavily on chip materials demand at subsidiary Soulbrain (357780), so the industry view starts with memory capital spending and utilization rates.

In January 2026, Shinhan Investment Corp. pointed to expectations for early DRAM capacity additions and NAND utilization recovery amid a supply shortage across memory products. The same report argued that expanded memory capex would lift demand across the materials sector.

Chip materials revenue is tied directly to customers' wafer input, and some analysts note that equipment arriving at new DRAM fabs from the second half of 2025 coincides with several lines running together in the second half of 2026.

Brokers broadly describe a back-end-weighted 2026 earnings path at the subsidiary, with stronger results expected in the second half than the first. Some observers said that if non-memory customer investments become visible in the first half of 2026, wafer input could rise sharply in the second half.

On the NAND side, Kiwoom Securities reflected expectations for a recovery in Samsung Electronics' NAND competitiveness, arguing that HBM expansion and a memory cycle rebound would lift materials shipments.

On competition, the etchant's mineral-based raw materials are seen as less exposed to oil shocks than petrochemical-solvent rivals. Competition from Chinese suppliers and the outcome of price negotiations with customers are cited as variables for the pace of margin recovery.

The display segment is in a structural adjustment phase but contributes a limited share of earnings, so chip materials are consistently identified as the core of holding-company results.

Bear factors

Gap in the 2025 earnings structure

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 8.86 billion, down 89.6% year on year, while profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 495.6 billion, up 743.2%, so the two measures diverge widely. Operating cash flow was only KRW 46.6 billion.

The share of non-operating items and one-off factors needs to be checked in the annual report notes, and the 1.6% operating margin in 2025 shows the operating profitability.

Large swings in quarterly profit

Operating losses continued in the third and fourth quarters of 2025, and operating profit fell 42.0% quarter on quarter in the third quarter of 2026. The 2,037.7% quarter-on-quarter jump in operating profit in the second quarter of 2026 came from a low base, and bears cite the large swing as a weakness. Quarterly results move with the timing of capital spending.

Concentration in the subsidiary and customer dependence

The holding company's results center on chip materials at subsidiary Soulbrain, which depend directly on customers' wafer input. If a specific customer's investment schedule slips, the earnings timing could slip as well.

Because price adjustments with customers were cited as a condition for margin recovery, margins could vary with the outcome of those negotiations.

Risk factors

Industry cycle and customer investment timing

If memory capex and customers' wafer input timing diverge, chip materials shipments could arrive later than expected. If the premise of concentrated operation in the second half of 2026 weakens, the back-end-weighted path itself could change.

Because the subsidiary carries a large share of results, holding-company earnings tend to move in the same direction.

Earnings quality and accounting factors

Operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders diverged sharply in 2025, and operating cash flow was KRW 46.6 billion, so annual profit levels are hard to interpret without tracing the source of earnings.

Non-operating items and consolidation adjustments should be checked in the annual report notes. If the gains were one-off, next year's comparisons could be distorted.

Display segment and new business execution

The display segment is in a structural adjustment phase, so its contribution to earnings is limited. New businesses such as glass substrate processing materials are expected to become visible within 2027, but timing could slip depending on mass-production schedules and customer wins. The speed at which capacity added in the U.S. and Hungary converts into revenue is also a variable.

Overall view

Soulbrain Holdings passed through a year in which operating profit fell sharply while profit attributable to controlling shareholders surged, and in 2026 it has posted quarterly profit and revenue growth.

Revenue peaked in 2023, declined in 2024, recovered in 2025, and has risen for four consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026.

Operating margin fell to 1.6% in 2025 and then turned positive quarterly in 2026, but whether the full-year margin returns close to the 2022 to 2024 range should be confirmed in the annual report.

Strengths include demand for chip materials at subsidiary Soulbrain from expanded memory capex, completed secondary-battery capacity expansion, and growth in bio-healthcare. Weaknesses include the gap in 2025 earnings structure, large swings in quarterly profit, and dependence on customers' investment schedules.

Several broker analyses view the subsidiary's industry recovery positively, but that does not guarantee the direction of the holding company's share price. The next checkpoints are the composition of full-year operating profit and non-operating items in the 2026 annual report and first-quarter 2027 results.
