02

Business structure

Sambo Corrugated Board was founded in March 1973 and manufactures corrugating medium and corrugated boxes. In the 2025 annual report basis, 98.90% of sales came from corrugated products and corrugating medium manufacturing, with the remaining 1.10% from other businesses.

Segment sales in the first half of 2026 were KRW 250.7 billion for corrugated products (73.3%) and KRW 91.2 billion for corrugating medium (26.7%), while consolidated sales after eliminating intra-group transactions were KRW 284.7 billion.

Corrugating medium is mainly produced by an affiliate, in which the company held an 85.76% stake as of the end of December 2024. Consolidated subsidiaries include other corrugated board and box makers.

About 99% of corrugating medium raw materials are sourced domestically, and purchases from two affiliated paper makers account for about 52% of total raw material purchases. Waste paper, the core input, is about 90% domestically sourced, while about 93% of pulp is imported.

Domestic sales accounted for 99.90% of revenue as of December 2024, so exports are minor. Average utilization of the company's own plants was 94.70% in the first half of 2026.

In competition, Sambo was the only company among the top five corrugated makers with a cost of sales ratio in the 70% range in the first half of 2024, when its operating margin was 9.4% against 6.5% for Shindaeyang Paper.

The business is limited to corrugating medium and boxes, which reportedly makes business separation or diversification difficult.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales moved from KRW 582 billion in 2022 to KRW 555.6 billion in 2023 (down 4.5% year on year), KRW 558.3 billion in 2024 (up 0.5%), and KRW 539.9 billion in 2025 (down 3.3%). Among 2022-2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 67.6 billion in 2022, KRW 67.4 billion in 2023 (down 0.3%), KRW 38.7 billion in 2024 (down 42.5%), and KRW 29.9 billion in 2025 (down 22.8%), and it declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

The operating margin moved from 11.6% in 2022 and 12.1% in 2023 down to 6.9% in 2024 and 5.5% in 2025. In 2024 operating profit fell 42.5% even though sales edged up, so the margin decline was the main driver of the profit drop.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 56.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 53.5 billion in 2023, KRW 32.5 billion in 2024, and KRW 27 billion in 2025. Net profit including non-controlling interests, a different figure, fell from KRW 63.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 31.8 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 72.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 74.9 billion in 2023, then fell to KRW 65.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 55.7 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 144.1 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 132.5 billion in the fourth quarter (down 8.1% quarter on quarter) and KRW 131.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 1.0%), then rose to KRW 153.5 billion in the second quarter (up 17.1%) and KRW 155.2 billion in the third quarter (up 1.1%), marking two straight quarters of growth.

Quarterly operating profit rose from KRW 5.34 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 16.5 billion in the second quarter (up 209.0%) and KRW 12.7 billion in the third quarter (down 22.9% quarter on quarter, up 93.2% year on year).

Third-quarter sales grew 7.7% year on year, while operating profit grew far faster, so quarterly profitability improved. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 12.9 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 11.1 billion in the third quarter, and the last four quarters totaled KRW 35.2 billion. The debt ratio of 28.3% at the end of 2025 was the highest of the 2022-2025 period.

05

Industry analysis

Reports have linked corrugated demand growth to the spread of parcel delivery and the packaging needs it creates.

A September 2026 report said average utilization of corrugated production capacity, including affiliates, was 90.54% in the first half of 2026, close to full utilization, and the company cited this as the background for its large capex.

In late March 2026, corrugated makers rose together on a de-plastics theme amid naphtha supply concerns, on expectations that corrugated board could replace plastic cushioning for industrial packaging. That was a stock-theme move and not a confirmed earnings fact.

Because most pulp is imported, raw material prices and exchange rates feed directly into cost. In competition, Sambo was the only company among the top five corrugated makers with a cost of sales ratio in the 70% range in the first half of 2024.

On an annual 2025 basis among 25 packaging-sector companies, its operating margin was above the sector average. The margin advantage is read as a result of both cost structure and transactions within the group.

Outlook

A September 2026 report said Sambo Corrugated Board's board approved a new plant in Pocheon, Gyeonggi Province, with investment of about KRW 123.8 billion. The stated purposes are expanding capacity at the Pocheon plant and replacing aging facilities.

The move is read as securing additional capacity while existing lines were running above 90% utilization. The report also framed the next task as converting the added capacity into new demand and earnings.

In recent quarters, sales rose for two consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, and third-quarter operating profit grew 93.2% year on year. Quarterly operating profit fell from KRW 16.5 billion in the second quarter to KRW 12.7 billion in the third quarter, so quarter-to-quarter swings remain large.

The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, and fourth-quarter 2026 results have not yet been disclosed. Past coverage also pointed to strong shareholder demands for higher shareholder returns.

As long as intra-group transactions remain a large part of sales, the composition of consolidated revenue remains a key item to track.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly profit rebound

Operating profit was KRW 16.5 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 12.7 billion in the third quarter, both above the KRW 6.5 billion of the fourth quarter of 2025. Third-quarter operating profit rose 93.2% year on year, against 7.7% sales growth over the same period. Profit growth far exceeded sales growth, which is the defining feature of the rebound.

Cost structure and raw material base

About 99% of corrugating medium raw materials are sourced domestically, and the company receives paper supply from an affiliate. In the first half of 2024, Sambo was the only company among the top five corrugated makers with a cost of sales ratio in the 70% range. Its operating margin in the same period was 9.4%, above 6.5% at Shindaeyang Paper.

Financial stability and expansion capacity

The debt ratio was 28.3% at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 189.6 billion and total equity of KRW 669.3 billion. Corrugated production utilization was 90.54% in the first half of 2026, providing a demand basis for expansion. Reports of about KRW 123.8 billion of investment in a new Pocheon plant have drawn attention to this capacity.

09

Bear factors

Three straight years of profit decline

Operating profit fell from KRW 67.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 29.9 billion in 2025, and the operating margin dropped from 11.6% to 5.5%. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 56.2 billion to KRW 27 billion over the same span. The three consecutive years of decline from 2023 to 2025 is the clearest weakness in the disclosed results.

Dependence on affiliated transactions

Because the group is concentrated in corrugated products, it depends heavily on work from affiliates. Press coverage noted that actual transactions with DaelimPaper did not decline after it was removed from related-party disclosures in 2018.

Higher intra-group transactions can strengthen cost efficiency, but they also draw more attention to transaction terms.

Ownership concentration and shareholder disputes

The largest shareholder and related parties held 68.05% as of February 2026. Early 2024 coverage reported that a minority shareholder group alleged breach of duty by the controlling shareholder and prepared a derivative suit. The company responded that it had no plan yet to seek damages from major shareholders or related parties.

10

Risk factors

Raw materials and FX

About 93% of pulp is imported, while about 90% of waste paper is sourced domestically. Unfavorable pulp prices or exchange rate moves would raise costs directly. Given this sourcing structure, the ability to pass through costs becomes a key variable for margins.

Execution of the new plant investment

Reports put the planned Pocheon plant investment at about KRW 123.8 billion. If demand does not keep pace with added capacity, utilization could fall and fixed-cost pressure could rise. The timing of any earnings contribution relative to this investment is not yet reflected in disclosed data.

Governance and litigation reports

In past reporting, minority shareholders questioned whether transactions involving the controlling shareholder constituted breach of duty. The company said it understood the matter was under review. Litigation or review outcomes could change how the market assesses governance.

Overall view

Sambo Corrugated Board makes corrugating medium and boxes within one group structure and sources most of its raw paper domestically. On the disclosed basis, operating profit declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and the operating margin fell from 11.6% in 2022 to 5.5% in 2025.

Meanwhile, operating profit in the second and third quarters of 2026 was above the fourth quarter of 2025, and third-quarter operating profit rose 93.2% year on year. Utilization of 90.54% and the Pocheon plant investment decision point to expansion capacity, but whether that converts into earnings is not yet visible.

Weaknesses include dependence on affiliate transactions, concentrated largest-shareholder ownership, and shareholder disputes reported in the past. Both strengths and weaknesses are present, so the next checkpoints are fourth-quarter 2026 results and the operating margin and capex execution in the 2026 annual report.
