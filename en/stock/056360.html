02

Business structure

Coweaver was founded in February 2000 and registered on KOSDAQ in 2001 as a maker of wired and wireless telecom equipment.

It began by developing optical transmission equipment and supplying major domestic carriers, then from 2006 widened its customer base to central and local government agencies, public institutions and financial firms.

Its product line centers on POTN, ROADM and carrier Ethernet devices, and PTN products generate its main revenue. Growth in PTN and WDM product sales has lifted product revenue, and in the first half of 2026 PTN products and power-transmission terminal devices led sales.

Its core customers are KT, LG Uplus and SK Telecom, and it owns a subsidiary at 100%.

In quantum-safe communications, its QKD device was developed domestically over about two years on technology transferred from KT, and it became the first nationally certified device after meeting National Intelligence Service security requirements.

A post-quantum cryptography encryption device passed TTA security function testing. On the optical side, the 2.4T-class POTN device was designated an excellent R&D innovation product, and a 52.6T-class high-capacity POTN device is ahead of launch.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual figures, revenue went from KRW 89.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 83.4 billion in 2023 (down 7.0%), fell to KRW 46.8 billion in 2024 (down 43.8%), the smallest year in the 2022–2025 span, and recovered to KRW 62.6 billion in 2025 (up 33.7%). The largest year in the span was 2022.

Operating profit of KRW 297 million in 2022 became an operating loss of KRW 2.6 billion in 2023, and the loss widened to KRW 7.63 billion in 2024. In 2025 operating profit was KRW 306 million, and the operating margin moved from 0.3% in 2022 to -3.1% in 2023, -16.3% in 2024 and 0.5% in 2025.

Parent net income was KRW 4.49 billion in 2022, KRW 799 million in 2023 (down 82.2%), a net loss of KRW 4.07 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.47 billion in 2025. Quarterly results swing sharply.

From a Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 13.2 billion, an operating loss of KRW 1.76 billion and a parent net loss of KRW 527 million, Q4 2025 revenue rose to KRW 26.4 billion (up 99.0% from the prior quarter), with operating profit of KRW 4.59 billion and parent net income of KRW 5.47 billion.

Q1 2026 revenue fell to KRW 12.4 billion (down 52.9% from the prior quarter), with an operating loss of KRW 2.19 billion and a parent net loss of KRW 1.71 billion. Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 12 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 1.27 billion and a parent net loss of KRW 422 million.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 16.7 billion (up 39.8% from the prior quarter and 26.3% year on year), with an operating loss of KRW 130 million and parent net income of KRW 280 million. Parent net income for the latest four quarters totals KRW 3.62 billion.

The first-half 2026 summary notes that sales of PTN products and power-transmission terminals grew, but that higher cost burdens reduced gross profit and widened the operating loss.

Revenue growth alongside continued losses points to cost ratios as the swing factor, so whether cost ratios recover is the next item to track.

Industry analysis

In domestic optical transmission equipment, Coweaver has been described as holding the leading position in the domestic market. Its customers are concentrated among the three domestic carriers, so carriers' capital spending cycles feed directly into its revenue.

Investment expansion periods create demand, while investment cuts increase quarterly revenue swings. In an industry comparison on an annual basis, its operating margin sits above the telecom equipment industry average, while its market capitalization ranks in the lower part of the 45-company group.

The quantum-safe communications market is still at an early stage. Brokers advise separating short-term theme-driven moves from medium- to long-term growth potential.

In 2026 telecom equipment names moved together with AI and semiconductor themes on several occasions, and one tracker counted 13 sharp rallies in the stock over the past year.

After a US AI infrastructure company reported results, Korean semiconductor and hardware names rallied, which shows how AI infrastructure sentiment can spread across equipment stocks.

The launch timing of the 52.6T device should be confirmed through company disclosures, and the quarterly revenue mix after launch is also worth watching.

Bull factors

The Q4 2025 profit and the Q3 2026 turn to parent net income can be cited as evidence of recovering earnings capacity.

Nationally certified quantum-safe credentials

The company holds a nationally certified quantum key distribution security credential, which is its main point of differentiation. The device was developed domestically on technology transferred from KT. Brokers argue that companies with proven technology and customers deserve selective attention.

Core customer base among the big three carriers and product upgrades

KT, LG Uplus and SK Telecom are core customers, giving the company an installed-base demand foundation. The innovation-product designation for the 2.4T-class POTN device is cited as support for public-sector sales growth. The planned 52.6T-class device launch could shift the revenue mix as products are upgraded.

Operating losses continued from Q1 through Q3 2026, with the largest at KRW 2.19 billion in Q1. The first-half 2026 summary attributes the decline in gross profit to higher cost burdens. With a 2025 operating margin of just 0.5%, revenue growth converts into profit only at a low rate.

Quarterly revenue swings and thin margins

Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 26.4 billion fell to KRW 12.4 billion in Q1 2026, a 52.9% quarter-on-quarter drop. Annual revenue reached a low of KRW 46.8 billion in 2024, and the operating margin was -16.3% that year. When quarterly revenue swings this widely, fixed costs can flip the profit and loss position quickly.

Gap between theme-driven prices and the earnings base

The share price has reacted sharply to theme events, including an intraday limit-up move on April 1, 2026. The quantum-safe communications market is still described as early-stage. With 2025 operating profit of only KRW 306 million, theme expectations can widen the gap between the share price and reported earnings.

Risk factors

Customer concentration and carrier capex cycle

Sales are concentrated among the three domestic carriers, KT, LG Uplus and SK Telecom, so their capital spending plans directly determine the company's revenue. Orders could fall during investment cuts, and it is not yet clear whether public-sector and financial customers added since 2006 can offset that.

Whether the public-sector sales growth expected from the 2.4T-class POTN device materializes should also be read alongside this trend.

Shareholder structure and float

The largest shareholder group holds 28.85%, treasury shares are 6.65%, and the free float ratio is 64.50%. A high insider stake limits the shares available to trade, and when volume concentrates a thin float can amplify price swings. Foreign ownership is a low 2.58%.

New-business commercialization and an early-stage market

Quantum-safe equipment and the AI-based OLS system have not yet shown a clear contribution to quarterly results. The market is described as early-stage, so the size and speed of demand carry significant uncertainty. The 52.6T device launch schedule should be confirmed through company disclosures.

Overall view

Coweaver returned to an annual operating profit in 2025 and left the loss phase, but it booked operating losses in all three quarters of 2026 through Q3.

Q3 showed improvement, with revenue of KRW 16.7 billion (up 26.3% year on year) and an operating loss of KRW 130 million, while cost pressure and large quarterly revenue swings remain.

Strengths include a PTN and optical transmission business backed by the three major domestic carriers, a nationally certified quantum key distribution security credential, and the innovation-product designation for the 2.4T-class POTN device.

Weaknesses include a low operating margin, limited float due to the large largest-shareholder stake, and share-price volatility tied to quantum themes.

Valuation shows market value well below net assets, but parent net income over the latest four quarters totals only KRW 3.62 billion, so earnings-based multiples are sensitive to quarterly results.

The next checkpoints are the 52.6T device launch, the annual operating margin and cost ratio in the 2026 annual report, and the Q1 2027 quarterly report. This report contains no buy or sell opinion and no target price.
