02

Business structure

Becu AI is an AI data company that acquires and processes news data and supplies it as AI training data and analytics platforms. The company was founded in 1998 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2022.

Its flagship product is AISurfer, a news-based analytics and monitoring platform; the next-generation version, rebuilt on a cloud-native architecture about seven years after the 2018 release, entered open beta testing in March 2026.

AISurfer provides search, summarization and source verification in one interface, based on news data from more than 3,000 media outlets. The RDPLINE data supply platform was officially launched in September 2025, and related product lines are scheduled for release within November 2026.

AISurfer accounts for a large share of revenue, and the company said sales of this main product declined slightly from the prior year in 2025. Customers fall into media and press organizations, major domestic tech firms, and public or government project demand.

The company was selected as a supplier for the 2026 Data Voucher Support Project run by the Ministry of Science and ICT. In August 2026 it won the Korea Press Foundation's broadcast archive digitization support project, with a contract size of about KRW 1 billion, equal to 5.61% of 2025 revenue.

Because AI training-data supply is recognized on a contract basis, quarterly revenue can swing sharply depending on contract timing. In competition, news content agreements and secured copyright are cited as differentiators, and the scale gap with large platform and IT service firms in the same sector is wide. Overseas, the company established a Vietnam subsidiary in May 2024 and moved its operations center there.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose 3.9% to KRW 16.9 billion in 2023 from KRW 16.3 billion in 2022, then grew 2.4% to KRW 17.3 billion in 2024, which was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period. Revenue declined 1.7% to KRW 17 billion in 2025.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 1.13 billion in 2022 to an operating profit of KRW 240 million in 2023, a swing back to an operating loss of KRW 812 million in 2024, and a wider operating loss of KRW 1.07 billion in 2025, when the operating margin was -6.3%.

The company cited a slight year-on-year decline in sales of its main product AISurfer, and the effect of annual operating costs incurred after the overseas subsidiary was set up, as key factors.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 597 million in 2024 and KRW 759 million in 2025, two consecutive loss years. Quarterly revenue fell 20.5% to KRW 3.72 billion in Q1 2026, then rose to KRW 4.28 billion in Q2 and KRW 4.42 billion in Q3, marking two consecutive quarters of growth.

Q3 2026 revenue was still down 0.1% from the year-earlier quarter, so the top-line recovery is not yet clear. The operating loss has persisted for five consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 through Q3 2026; the Q3 2026 loss of KRW 155 million widened from the prior quarter but narrowed from the year-earlier quarter.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive in Q4 2025 at KRW 13.7 million, then reached KRW 39.88 million in Q1 2026, KRW 24.04 million in Q2, and KRW 548 million in Q3, a 2,179.9% increase from the prior quarter.

Over the most recent four quarters the total was KRW 626 million, a figure that should be read separately from core operating results.

The debt ratio was 37.7% in 2022 and 35.0% in 2025, staying in the low-to-mid 30% range, while operating cash flow was KRW 1.61 billion in 2023 and remained in the KRW 200 million to KRW 400 million range in the other years.

05

Industry analysis

The AI training-data market is taking shape as large model developers compete to secure data while copyright disputes intensify.

In a November 2026 press statement, a Becu AI official cited Anthropic's payment of a copyright settlement of KRW 1.5 trillion to authors and others, and said big tech's competition for legitimate data sources would intensify.

The same official said the outcome of the New York Times lawsuit against OpenAI is expected to become visible in early 2027, which would strengthen this trend.

Domestically, the company says demand is rising for converting broadcasters' legacy archives into metadata that AI can use, and it points to the large volume of accumulated legacy broadcast data as supporting evidence.

In news data distribution, content agreements with media outlets and secured copyright are cited as the factors that separate competitors. Compared with larger companies in the same IT and software sector, such as NAVER, Kakao and Samsung SDS, the gap is wide in both revenue and market capitalization.

Government policy also acts as a demand driver, notably the 2026 Data Voucher Support Project supplier selection and a state-led national project on an independent AI foundation model.

Demand for data is expanding, but the revenue scale the company has reflected so far remains small, so its position in the cycle reads as early-stage.

06

Outlook

The company said that revenue from AI training-data supply to the Korea Press Foundation and major domestic tech firms will be recognized in earnest from Q4 2026. In the same period, it plans to officially launch the new RDPLINE platform and related product lines within November 2026.

A state-led national project to develop an independent AI foundation model is also scheduled for completion by year-end 2026. The broadcast archive digitization project won in August 2026 runs through December 18, 2026, with a contract size of about KRW 1 billion.

At its August 2026 IR event, the company set a short-term target of a 10% or higher operating margin in the first half of 2027.

Business expansion items added to its articles of incorporation include physical AI, learning-data platforms for multiple devices, and security token and digital asset platforms based on blockchain. Among these, contributions to earnings are most visible first in data supply and public-sector contracts.

The size of Q4 2026 revenue recognition will be confirmed in the 2026 annual report. On the overseas front, the company expects outcomes of copyright lawsuits, including the New York Times case against OpenAI, to influence big tech's data-acquisition competition.

Whether this view translates into actual contracts is the next checkpoint. Progress toward the operating margin target will be confirmed with first-half 2027 results.

08

Bull factors

Data Supply Revenue Set to Begin Scaling

The company said AI training-data supply revenue for the Korea Press Foundation and major domestic tech firms will be recognized in earnest from Q4 2026. If the sequential revenue growth recorded in Q2 and Q3 2026 continues, there is room for the core business to expand.

Selection as a 2026 Data Voucher Support Project supplier is also cited as evidence of public and small-business demand.

Proprietary Technology and Product Expansion

The company announced in January 2026 that its proprietary small language model RDP-N1, using a step-by-step reasoning dataset, improved performance by 93% over the base open-source 7B model. AISurfer moved to a cloud web platform, so it can be accessed without device or operating system limits.

If RDPLINE-related product lines launch in November 2026, the lineup will widen across both data supply and analytics.

Broadcast Archive Contract and Major Shareholder Commitment

The company disclosed in August 2026 that it won the 2026 broadcast archive digitization support project from the Korea Press Foundation, with a contract size of about KRW 1 billion equal to 5.61% of last year's revenue.

The company said the award demonstrated the supply-chain technology needed to build an AI copyright platform. In July 2026, its largest shareholder also pursued the acquisition of an additional stake of roughly 3% of issued shares, which was disclosed as a sign of management commitment.

09

Bear factors

Persistent Operating Losses

Operating losses occurred in both 2024 and 2025, and the 2025 operating loss widened to KRW 1.07 billion from the prior year. The 2025 operating margin was -6.3%. Operating losses have continued for five consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 through Q3 2026, and the Q3 2026 operating loss was KRW 155 million.

Reliance on Non-Operating Gains for Net Income

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 548 million in Q3 2026 reflected fair-value gains on the FuriosaAI stake, according to the company's disclosure and press coverage. Given an operating loss of KRW 155 million in the same quarter, a large part of net income depends on valuation gains.

Those gains move with the market value of the investment, so there is no assurance the same direction will continue in the next quarter.

Stagnant Revenue and Dependence on the Core Product

Revenue moved within a range of KRW 16.3 billion to KRW 17.3 billion between 2022 and 2025, with no clear growth trend. In 2025, sales of the main product AISurfer declined slightly from the prior year.

The contribution of newer products is not clearly visible in the filings, so the case for a larger top line depends on contract revenue from Q4 2026 onward.

10

Risk factors

Copyright and Regulatory Environment

The news and AI training-data business is directly exposed to copyright disputes and changes in law. The company expects the New York Times case against OpenAI to become visible in early 2027, but the valuation of data could shift depending on the direction of the ruling. Whether legislation on copyright in AI training data passes is also cited as a key variable.

Capital Structure and Share Trading

In May 2026 the company decided on a 5-for-1 free capital reduction to normalize its capital structure, and a trading suspension was disclosed in July 2026. Total equity was KRW 11.2 billion at the end of 2025. If core operating losses continue, equity could decline as accumulated losses build.

Share Price Volatility and Trading Flows

In January 2026, the share price hit the daily upper limit after the announcement of a lightweight small language model, and the Korea Exchange designated the stock as overheated on a disparity-rate basis. Trading concentrated among individual investors is cited as a factor that can amplify short-term volatility. As a small-cap stock, it can move sharply on a single piece of news.

Overall view

Becu AI's revenue stayed within KRW 16.3 billion to KRW 17.3 billion over 2022 to 2025, and operating losses have continued since 2024.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive in Q3 2026 at KRW 548 million, but because fair-value gains on the FuriosaAI stake contributed heavily, it should be read apart from core operations.

The operating loss has persisted for five consecutive quarters from Q3 2025, and the Q3 2026 operating loss was KRW 155 million. Revenue grew in Q2 and Q3 2026, two consecutive quarters, and the company said AI training-data supply revenue will be recognized in earnest from Q4 2026.

Bullish factors include expansion of data supply contracts, product launches and the broadcast archive award, while bearish factors include continued core operating losses, reliance on non-operating gains and concentration on the main product.

Whether the company achieves its 10% or higher operating margin target in the first half of 2027 is the most direct reference point.
