Becu AI: Quarterly Profit Turn Amid Annual Losses

02

Business structure

Becu AI, founded in 1998 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2022, is an AI data company whose core business is processing news content into data usable for AI training and services.

The company says it works with roughly 3,000 media partners in Korea and abroad to source news data, and supplies it to enterprises through its real-time data pipeline RDPLINE.

Preprocessing and labeling are automated on the AIROOT platform, and the company also runs RDP1, a news-specialized RAG model, and AI-Surfer, an AI-agent-based analytics platform. AI-Surfer is presented as the flagship product in enterprise and marketing analytics.

According to company statements, customers include large domestic firms such as Samsung Electronics, LG, SK Telecom and KT, and the company highlights its news copyright distribution agreement with the Korea Press Foundation as a differentiator.

The company's segment revenue and mix could not be confirmed from its own disclosures.

An outside data aggregator estimates that the AI-Surfer product accounts for 82% of revenue and that copyright fees of KRW 7.43 billion equal 44% of revenue, but this is third-party data rather than DART source data and should be treated as reference only.

In May 2024 the company set up a Vietnam subsidiary and moved its preprocessing operations there to strengthen cost competitiveness. Direct scale comparisons with NAVER, Kakao and Daou Data in the same IT and software sector could not be confirmed from available data.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 16.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.9 billion in 2023 (up 3.9% year on year) and KRW 17.3 billion in 2024 (up 2.4%), then edged down to KRW 17 billion in 2025 (down 1.7%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

The operating result moved from an operating loss of KRW 1.13 billion in 2022 to an operating profit of KRW 240 million in 2023, a swing to profit, before turning to an operating loss of KRW 812 million in 2024, a swing to loss. In 2025 the operating loss widened to KRW 1.07 billion as losses continued.

The operating margin was -6.9% in 2022, 1.4% in 2023, -4.7% in 2024 and -6.3% in 2025, so apart from 2023 the company operated at a loss relative to revenue.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was positive at KRW 733 million in 2023, then a net loss of KRW 597 million in 2024 and a net loss of KRW 759 million in 2025, which makes two consecutive loss years from 2024 to 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests showed a net loss of KRW 601 million in 2024 and KRW 759 million in 2025. The company reportedly attributed the 2024 swing to losses to higher costs from changes in the consolidation scope after it set up a wholly owned overseas subsidiary.

Media reports said the 2025 operating loss widened 32.48% year on year. Operating cash flow was KRW 392 million in 2022, KRW 1.61 billion in 2023, KRW 378 million in 2024 and KRW 265 million in 2025, having declined since 2023.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 11.2 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 3.93 billion, giving a debt ratio of 35.0%, down from 37.7% in 2022 to 33.1% in 2024 before rising again in 2025.

On the quarterly series, revenue rose from KRW 4.42 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 4.69 billion in Q4 2025 (up 6.0% quarter on quarter), fell to KRW 3.72 billion in Q1 2026 (down 20.5%), then recovered to KRW 4.28 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 4.42 billion in Q3 2026, rising for two consecutive quarters, although Q3 2026 revenue was down 0.1% year on year.

The operating loss has persisted for five consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026, and the Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 155 million was narrower than a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss of KRW 86.59 million in Q3 2025 to a profit of KRW 13.7 million in Q4 2025 and KRW 548 million in Q3 2026. The sum of the last four quarters' net income attributable to controlling shareholders is KRW 626 million.

The reasons why controlling-shareholder net income grew in Q3 2026 despite an operating loss cannot be identified from the disclosed figures and must be checked against the non-operating items in the quarterly report.

05

Industry analysis

Becu AI's business is tied to demand for training data for generative AI.

The company says the enactment of Korea's AI Basic Act has strengthened requirements for data transparency, and that LLM developers, AI startups and financial and media firms seeking training data free of copyright risk could become key customers. This demand is sensitive to policy.

The company has noted that since the AI Basic Act was enacted, attention on amendments to the AI copyright law and training-data rules has grown, and the value of legally sourced data could change with the policy direction.

Industry supply and demand statistics that would show where the sector sits in its cycle were not found in this review. On competition, Becu AI cites its news data archive built over two decades and its distribution agreement with the Korea Press Foundation as barriers to entry.

Conversely, if large IT companies expand their own data pipelines and LLMs, demand could overlap with smaller data suppliers, a structural variable.

Beyond news, the company is widening its scope to image and video data and expert question-and-answer data, and media reports say it has a partnership with Getty Images Korea, a stock content company. On cost, the company says its Vietnam preprocessing center lowers labor-based processing costs.

However, because acquisition costs make up a large share of revenue, the room for margin improvement may be limited.

Outlook

The company does not appear to have published a 2026 annual revenue guidance, so the outlook has to rest on disclosed contracts and the quarterly trend.

According to the August 6, 2026 disclosure, the single sales and supply contract with the Korea Press Foundation for a 2026 broadcast archive digitization project is worth KRW 955.68 million, equal to 5.61% of recent revenue, and runs from August 5, 2026 to December 18, 2026.

Payment is made after completion of the contract, subject to a performance guarantee deposit. How much of this contract is recognized in Q4 revenue, and whether follow-on revenue continues after completion, are the next items to verify.

In January 2026 the company announced progress on an ultra-lightweight small language model, which media linked to the government's push for AI industry growth. The Vietnam subsidiary's preprocessing setup is presented as a base for handling large-scale data processing orders.

In the same period the company opened a dedicated RDPLINE alliance channel connecting data suppliers and buyers. On capital structure, the 5-to-1 reverse split with a July 2026 record date reduced the number of issued shares, and trading resumed on August 4, 2026.

Because operating losses have persisted for five consecutive quarters, whether the operating loss narrows in the 2026 annual preliminary results is the key item. Based on last year's timing, the 2026 annual preliminary results could be disclosed around mid-February 2027.

08

Bull factors

Differentiation through copyright-cleared news data

The company says it is the only KOSDAQ-listed firm with a news copyright distribution agreement with the Korea Press Foundation. Its argument is that rising data-transparency requirements under the AI Basic Act could increase demand for training data with cleared copyright risk.

Per company statements, it supplies news training data to large domestic firms including Samsung Electronics, LG, SK Telecom and KT. These claims rest on company statements and have not been independently verified.

Improving quarterly trend

After a revenue low in Q1 2026, revenue rose for two consecutive quarters in Q2 and Q3 2026. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 548 million in Q3 2026, a swing to profit from a year earlier, and totaled KRW 626 million over the last four quarters.

It had also turned positive at KRW 13.7 million in Q4 2025. Whether this profit swing reflects operating improvement or non-operating items cannot be determined from the disclosed figures alone.

Public and media contracts and overseas cost structure

The Korea Press Foundation contract disclosed in August 2026 is worth KRW 955.68 million, equal to 5.61% of recent revenue. The company explains that it moved preprocessing to its Vietnam subsidiary, set up in May 2024, to lower processing costs.

The positive argument is that public contracts combined with an overseas cost base create room to take on large-scale data processing orders.

09

Bear factors

Continued annual losses

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders continued in 2024 and 2025, and the 2025 operating margin was -6.3%. The 2025 operating loss of KRW 1.07 billion widened from the prior year. Among 2022 to 2025, only 2023 was a year of operating profit.

Without confirmed annual improvement in profitability, quarterly profit swings have not yet reversed the cumulative losses.

Stagnant revenue and high acquisition costs

Annual revenue peaked at KRW 17.3 billion in 2024 and eased to KRW 17 billion in 2025, staying near KRW 17 billion since 2023. An outside aggregator puts copyright fees at 44% of revenue, though this is a third-party estimate rather than DART data. The bearish argument is that a high acquisition cost share can leave thin margins even when revenue grows.

Reverse split and trading volatility

The 5-to-1 reverse split in 2026 was decided to offset accumulated deficits. After a limit-up move in January 2026 on news of an ultra-lightweight small language model, the Korea Exchange classified the stock as overheated on its disparity index and urged investor caution. Trading was halted in July 2026 and resumed on August 4, indicating a volatile trading environment.

Risk factors

Regulation and copyright policy

Depending on the direction of AI copyright law amendments and training-data rules, the value and supply terms of legally sourced data could change. Because the core business rests on the rights structure of news content, policy shifts could affect both revenue and acquisition terms. The specific direction of the policy could not be confirmed in this review.

Financial and capital structure

Operating cash flow was KRW 265 million in 2025, sharply below KRW 1.61 billion in 2023. Total equity of KRW 11.2 billion at the end of 2025 is entirely attributable to controlling shareholders, but if operating losses persist for five consecutive quarters, the burden on equity could build.

The 2026 reverse split is a tool for tidying the balance sheet and does not by itself improve operating profitability.

Business concentration and governance

According to an outside aggregator, most revenue comes from the AI-Surfer product, so product concentration is high. Outside company profiles have noted that the largest shareholder also serves as chief executive.

The company appointed a new outside director in March 2026 as part of board changes, but the division of roles between management and the largest shareholder should be confirmed in the business report.

12

Overall view

Becu AI has a business structure that combines news data supply with AI analytics products, and 2026 has brought positive signals such as public and media contracts and a quarterly swing to profit.

On an annual basis, however, operating and controlling-shareholder net losses continued in 2024 and 2025, and the 2025 operating margin was -6.3%. Revenue moved from KRW 16.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 17 billion in 2025, so growth in scale has been limited.

Controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 548 million in Q3 2026 is a sign of a swing to profit, but with an operating loss of KRW 155 million in the same quarter, a turn to operating profit has not been confirmed.

The key variables for the earnings structure are acquisition costs such as copyright fees and changes in AI training-data policy. The debt ratio was 35.0% at the end of 2025, and all KRW 11.2 billion of total equity is attributable to controlling shareholders.

The next items to verify are the December 18, 2026 contract expiry, the 2026 annual preliminary results, and the non-operating items in the 2026 business report.
