Clean & Science: Filter Pivot Still Short of Profit Stability

Clean & Science posted operating losses in 2024 and 2025, returned to operating profit in the first two quarters of 2026, and slipped back into an operating loss in the third quarter, while the earnings contribution from its AI data center and U.S. HVAC filter businesses has not been disclosed.

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Clean & Science makes both finished filters and the filter media used to produce them. Filter sales are the main source of revenue, covering filters for air purifiers, air conditioners and HVAC equipment, automotive filter paper, industrial filter paper and medical masks.

The company is described as producing from melt-blown (MB) filter media through to finished filters in-house.

Its customers are centered on domestic home appliance makers and automotive-related demand, and one financial news outlet's company analysis pointed to revenue from domestic appliance makers, including Samsung, LG Electronics and Coway, as a stable base.

An independent research house estimated the company's share of the domestic air purifier filter market at about 70%, but that is an outside estimate rather than a company disclosure.

In the replacement filter market, quality disputes over compatible filters, including questions about performance and ingredient control, have emerged, and some analysts say quality control could become a selection criterion for suppliers alongside price.

The new businesses span HVAC filters and semiconductor and AI materials. The company has supplied HVAC filters to a U.S. company since December 2024, and the same research house put related 2025 revenue at about KRW 7 billion.

HVAC filter supply to a domestic AI data center began in July 2026, and in September 2026 the company signed a high-performance filter media supply contract with a global HVAC company.

On the semiconductor side, it supplies HEPA and ULPA filters and chemical filters for cleanrooms, and it has developed water-treatment filters used in high-spec AI MLCC production. The company has not disclosed what share of total sales the new businesses account for.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 86 billion in 2022 to KRW 92.5 billion in 2023, up 7.5%, and then fell to KRW 84.6 billion in 2024, down 8.5%. Revenue recovered to KRW 86.1 billion in 2025, up 1.7%, and within 2022 to 2025 the largest year was 2023 and the smallest was 2024.

Profitability moved from an operating loss of KRW 10.9 billion in 2022, an operating margin of -12.7%, to operating profit of KRW 938 million in 2023, a margin of 1.0%, which marked a turn to profit.

Operating losses of KRW 849 million in 2024 and KRW 965 million in 2025 followed, so the company has posted losses in two consecutive years, and the 2025 operating margin was -1.1%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 903 million in 2023, then a net loss of KRW 7.41 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.37 billion in 2025, a loss that narrowed from the prior year.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 16.3 billion in 2022, net income of KRW 903 million in 2023, a net loss of KRW 7.41 billion in 2024 and a net loss of KRW 3.37 billion in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 247.8% in 2022 to 238.3% in 2023, then 319.5% in 2024 and 343.8% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 616 million in 2022, KRW 7.35 billion in 2023, KRW 2.48 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.67 billion in 2025, positive in every year.

On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 17.3 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.82 billion, and the fourth quarter narrowed the operating loss to KRW 1.42 billion on revenue of KRW 17.7 billion, up 2.1% from the prior quarter.

Revenue jumped to KRW 24.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026, up 40.7% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 839 million turned the result positive. Second-quarter revenue was nearly flat at KRW 25 billion, but operating profit fell 53.6% to KRW 389 million.

Third-quarter revenue was KRW 22.8 billion, down 8.8% from the prior quarter and up 31.5% from a year earlier, and an operating loss of KRW 431 million returned, though the loss was smaller than a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.38 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 141 million in the second quarter, and a loss of KRW 2.32 billion in the third quarter. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 3.31 billion.

Because no segment disclosure has yet been published, the contribution of the new businesses to the quarterly revenue jump cannot be separated out.

05

Industry analysis

The filter industry splits into a commodity market that depends on appliance replacement demand and automotive parts, and an industrial and data center market that requires high-efficiency materials.

One analysis holds that the commodity appliance filter business has been hit by slowing growth in the air purifier and automotive filter markets. By contrast, a financial media analysis says demand for water-treatment filters used in MLCC production is rising as AI server capacity expands.

Tighter PFAS regulation is also cited as a business opportunity because demand for PFAS-free materials is growing. In North America, a supply contract with a global HVAC major was followed by qualification by a North American customer, which an analyst read as confirmation of possible entry into the local supply chain.

An independent research house did not give an investment opinion or target price, but it named the expansion of high-value filter sales as the key to re-rating the company. The same house said the question is whether U.S.

HVAC and PFAS-free sales growth leads to better margins, stronger operating cash flow and lower borrowings. The replacement filter market has also seen fiercer quality competition as compatible products have multiplied.

Most listed peers in KOSDAQ machinery and equipment are equipment-focused, so their business structures differ from a filter-media maker like Clean & Science and direct comparison is limited. Industry conditions are therefore better read within the filter and filtration-media business itself.

Outlook

One financial news outlet's company analysis cited a 2028 target presented by the company of KRW 155.8 billion in revenue and KRW 6.89 billion in operating profit. The figures are reported as the company's own presentation, and whether they will be achieved cannot yet be judged.

The nearest checkpoint is how revenue from AI data center and U.S. HVAC filters appears in quarterly results. Because the size of the July 2026 supply start and the September 2026 contract has not been disclosed, the new businesses' contribution is hard to separate in quarterly figures.

On the capital side, the company bought 161,862 shares on the open market from September 9 to 28, 2026 for about KRW 500 million, bringing treasury shares to 8.38% of the total. A further KRW 1 billion repurchase was reported as decided in the same month.

Director Choi Jae-won bought 32,000 shares directly, and an affiliated company bought about 116,000 shares. Former representative Choi Jae-ho was also reported to have bought more than 20,000 shares on the open market through October 23, 2026. The company said the buybacks aim to stabilize the share price.

On listing status, the stock was designated as an administrative-issue stock in August 2026 for falling below the KRW 20 billion market-cap requirement, and a court injunction later suspended the follow-up delisting procedures.

The 2026 annual report is due by the end of March 2027 under the statutory deadline, which will confirm full-year 2026 and fourth-quarter results.

Bear factors

High leverage on a thin equity base

Total liabilities were KRW 58 billion against total equity of KRW 16.9 billion at the end of 2025, a debt ratio of 343.8%. The ratio has climbed from 247.8% in 2022.

One independent research house flagged net borrowings of KRW 27.25 billion as a leverage burden, and outstanding convertible and warrant bonds were also mentioned as a factor.

Continued losses and volatile quarterly results

Operating losses continued in 2024 and 2025, with a 2025 operating margin of -1.1%. The third quarter of 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 431 million, breaking the run of profits. Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 3.31 billion over the latest four quarters.

Listing requirements and uncertain scale of new businesses

The stock was designated as an administrative-issue stock in August 2026 for falling below the KRW 20 billion market-cap requirement, and reports say it remains below the listing threshold. The court injunction suspended follow-up procedures, but the underlying requirement has not been resolved.

Because the size of the new contracts and their share of sales are undisclosed, the growth contribution is hard to gauge.

Risk factors

Listing-status risk

Reports say the market cap remains below the listing threshold even after the administrative-issue designation. The court injunction only suspended follow-up delisting procedures, and the main proceeding and its outcome are not yet set.

Treasury purchases and open-market buying by the major shareholder's family continue, but whether they are enough to satisfy the market-cap requirement is a separate question.

Balance sheet and financing risk

Total liabilities of KRW 58 billion at the end of 2025 are more than three times total equity of KRW 16.9 billion. Operating cash flow of KRW 3.67 billion in 2025 was positive but small relative to the debt load.

If repayment pressure on borrowings and convertible bonds rises, room to invest in the new businesses could be constrained.

New-business execution and quality-competition risk

The company has not disclosed the size of its U.S. HVAC and AI data center supply. The replacement filter market faces quality disputes over compatible products, which is raising demands for both price competitiveness and quality control.

Until new-business sales show up in quarterly results, it is hard to tell whether expectations will translate into reported earnings.
