Q3 Swing to Net Loss Tests Neowiz's Profit Recovery

02

Business structure

Neowiz operates three revenue lines: mobile games, PC and console games, and other businesses such as advertising and fan-based services. In Q3 2026 the mix was 51% mobile, 36% PC and console, and 13% other, according to company disclosures.

Mobile revenue was KRW 56.6 billion, down 3% year on year but up 6% from the prior quarter, and the core mobile RPG BrownDust2 anchors this segment. BrownDust2 lost sales to the base effect of its third-anniversary event, but extended events and collaboration content kept its fan base engaged.

Early results from the subsidiary's MMORPG Kingdom 2, launched in the same quarter, supported the sequential rebound in mobile.

PC and console revenue was KRW 39.2 billion, down 33% year on year but up 5% from the prior quarter, helped by the Nintendo Switch 2 version of Lies of P and the launch of Shape of Dreams on three consoles.

Overseas revenue share in Q3 was 97% for Lies of P, 92% for Cats & Soup, 86% for BrownDust2, and 47% for DJMAX RESPECT V. Company-wide, overseas sales were 64% of revenue, so the model leans clearly on global publishing and overseas distribution.

Other revenue of KRW 14.7 billion grew 43.8% year on year on advertising and fan-business growth. In competition, Neowiz is classified as a mid-sized game company, unlike large players such as Krafton and Netmarble, and it is typically compared with mid-tier peers such as Wemade, Webzen, and Com2us.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed basis, revenue rose from KRW 294.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 365.6 billion in 2023 (up 24.1%), KRW 366.8 billion in 2024 (up 0.3%), and KRW 432.7 billion in 2025 (up 18.0%), marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 19.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 31.6 billion in 2023 (up 61.5%), KRW 32.9 billion in 2024 (up 4.2%), and KRW 60 billion in 2025 (up 82.2%), also three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025.

The operating margin climbed from 6.6% in 2022 to 13.9% in 2025, so margin improvement was one driver of the profit increase.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 13.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 48.7 billion in 2023 (up 272.5%), then fell to KRW 2.39 billion in 2024 (down 95.1%) before recovering to KRW 50 billion in 2025 (up 1,987.8%).

The 2024 total net loss was KRW 6.95 billion, and 2025 total net income of KRW 45.1 billion marked a return to profit. Controlling-shareholder income exceeding total net income in 2025 suggests that the non-controlling interests posted a loss.

Operating cash flow was KRW 43.2 billion in 2022 and KRW 64.7 billion in 2023, fell to KRW 33.8 billion in 2024, and rose to KRW 76.1 billion in 2025. The debt ratio was 22.2% at the end of 2025, with total equity of KRW 555.8 billion, of which controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 531.2 billion.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 remains a high comparison base at KRW 127.4 billion in sales and KRW 26.5 billion in operating profit. Q4 2025 sales were KRW 106.3 billion (down 16.6% from the prior quarter) and operating profit was KRW 4.7 billion (down 82.3%), the trough of the window.

Operating profit then rose for three consecutive quarters to KRW 6.95 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 7.94 billion in Q2, and KRW 9.6 billion in Q3. Sales fell 4.6% to KRW 101.4 billion in Q1 2026 before rising for two consecutive quarters to KRW 102.1 billion and KRW 110.5 billion.

Controlling-shareholder income rose to KRW 16.2 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 18.6 billion in Q2, then swung to a net loss of KRW 9.99 billion in Q3. Company disclosures tied the Q3 swing mainly to valuation and translation losses on foreign-currency financial assets as the won strengthened, a non-operating factor. Over the last four quarters, controlling-shareholder net income totaled KRW 27.5 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's game sector is highly exposed to launch timing and to user flows in existing live-service titles. Press coverage attributed the weakness at both Wemade and Neowiz partly to fewer users of existing service games, and both companies are reported to be relying on new titles for a rebound.

Wemade said it plans to launch around 20 new titles from 2027, and its Q3 net income was reported to have fallen 90.9%. Neowiz's Q3 2026 PC and console revenue fell 33% year on year, a much steeper drop than the 3% decline in mobile.

That makes restoring the competitiveness of its console-focused proprietary IP the main challenge. With 64% of revenue coming from overseas, the won's strength and the resulting translation losses show high currency sensitivity.

In global console markets, Lies of P is widening its platform reach through the Nintendo Switch 2 version. In mobile, BrownDust2 continues to rely on fan-centered live operations to defend its base.

06

Outlook

Neowiz's disclosed development pipeline comprises nine titles: six self-developed and three externally sourced publishing projects. Among the self-developed titles, the life-simulation Project CF has entered production, and the next Lies of P is validating its core fun at the vertical-slice stage.

Project Rubicon (a story-driven RPG), Project FN (a subculture RPG), the soulslike Project Windy (prototype), and Legend of Heroes 3: White Witch (a Nihon Falcom IP, concept stage) are also in development. The externally sourced publishing slate includes a PC and console title from US studio Wolfeye Studios.

The company plans to reveal official titles and release schedules for major new games in sequence from 2027. In a May 2026 report, Round8 Studio head Park Sung-jun set a goal of at least 85 on Metacritic for all new titles.

The smaller publishing title Annyeong Seoul: Itaewon Edition released a new demo at Steam Next Fest in October 2026 and will appear alongside Sanabi at the 2026 Game Culture Festival in Busan on November 19–22.

Cats & Soup: Magic Recipe launched globally in April 2026, and Lies of P expanded through a Nintendo Switch 2 release in August 2026 and Shape of Dreams console launches in September 2026. Until new titles launch and their performance is known, the pace of PC and console revenue recovery remains the key variable.

Risk factors

Currency and valuation gains and losses

If the won keeps strengthening, valuation and translation losses on foreign-currency financial assets could again swing quarterly net income. Overseas revenue is 64% of the total, and key titles earn heavily abroad, so currency exposure is large.

The Q3 2026 swing to loss was driven by non-operating valuation losses, which is a point to watch in upcoming quarters.

New-title performance and timing

New titles sit at different stages, including production, vertical slice, prototype, and concept, so launch timing and performance are uncertain. Official titles and schedules for major games are due to be revealed in sequence from 2027, so confirmation will take time. If the new titles fall short, the recovery in PC and console revenue could be delayed.

Dependence on mobile live titles

Mobile revenue depends mainly on the live operation of BrownDust2, and quarterly sales swing with event base effects. Q3 2026 mobile revenue rose 6% from the prior quarter but fell 3% year on year. If user attrition accelerates, the recovery in the mobile segment could be constrained.

Overall view

Neowiz posted KRW 60 billion in operating profit in 2025, showing a profit recovery, and operating profit then rose for three consecutive quarters from Q1 2026 through Q3.

However, Q3 2026 operating profit fell 63.7% year on year, and the controlling-shareholder result swung to a KRW 9.99 billion net loss, so the direction of quarterly results remains mixed.

Supporting factors are the overseas success of Lies of P, the expansion onto console platforms, and profitability that climbed to a 13.9% operating margin in 2025.

Weighing against it are the steep decline in PC and console revenue, valuation losses from a stronger won, and a gap until major new titles are revealed from 2027. The debt ratio was 22.2% at the end of 2025, and controlling-shareholder net income totaled KRW 27.5 billion over the last four quarters.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results due in early 2027, the 2026 annual report, and the release schedules and early performance of major titles revealed from 2027. Whether new titles can offset the decline in existing IP revenue remains the key variable for the earnings trend.
