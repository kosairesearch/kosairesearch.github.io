Between Foldable Hopes and Core Profit Recovery

02

Business structure

BHCo was founded in 1999 to make and sell flexible printed circuit boards (FPCBs), listed on KOSDAQ in 2007, and moved to the KOSPI market in 2023. Its FPCB revenue is split between North American customers and domestic and other customers.

A Samsung Securities report from May 2026 shows FPCB sales to North American customers at KRW 1.18 trillion in 2025, a majority of total revenue. The same table puts domestic and other FPCB sales at KRW 269 billion and the BH EVS segment at KRW 345 billion for 2025.

The core lineup is smartphone and IT OLED FPCBs, and Samsung Securities described the smartphone wireless charging business as growing on a stable customer base and order backlog. BHCo is extending the same charging technology to humanoid robot chargers and showed a vertical charging station for robots at CES 2026.

In its review of the third quarter of 2025, KB Securities separately flagged the in-vehicle smartphone wireless charger unit and the automotive cable business. Securities research often describes iPhone-related sales as a central driver of results.

SK Securities noted in June 2025 that only a few FPCB makers remain in Korea. Listed peers in electronics and components include Samsung Electro-Mechanics, LG Innotek, Isupetasys and Korea Circuit.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 1.68 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.59 trillion in 2023, a 5.3% decline, before rising to KRW 1.75 trillion in 2024 (up 10.2%) and KRW 1.79 trillion in 2025 (up 2.2%). Revenue has risen for two consecutive years since the 2023 low, and 2025 was the largest year in the period.

Operating profit was largest in 2022 at KRW 131.3 billion, then moved to KRW 84.8 billion in 2023 (down 35.4%), KRW 87.1 billion in 2024 (up 2.7%) and KRW 54 billion in 2025 (down 38.0%).

The operating margin declined from 7.8% in 2022 to 5.3% in 2023, 5.0% in 2024 and 3.0% in 2025, so higher sales in 2024 did not restore margins.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 143.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 90.7 billion, KRW 67 billion and KRW 31 billion in 2023 through 2025, a three-year decline. Net income including non-controlling interests is a separate figure, at KRW 40.6 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 790.8 billion, of which KRW 748.4 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and the debt ratio was 73.3%. Operating cash flow was KRW 197.4 billion in 2022, KRW 90.5 billion in 2023, KRW 186.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 159.2 billion in 2025.

In quarterly terms, revenue was KRW 562.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 with operating profit of KRW 34.5 billion. The fourth quarter brought revenue of KRW 508 billion (down 9.7% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 28.8 billion (down 16.4%).

The first quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 371.6 billion (down 26.9%) and operating profit of KRW 10.7 billion (down 63.0%), and operating profit fell further to KRW 7.89 billion in the second quarter.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 432 billion (up 4.3% from the prior quarter, down 23.2% from a year earlier) and operating profit was KRW 30.5 billion (up 286.6% from the prior quarter, down 11.5% year on year).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.74 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 84.8% year on year, and KRW 50 billion over the last four quarters.

KB Securities cited IT OLED losses, low utilization in the automotive cable unit and U.S. tariff effects on the in-vehicle wireless charger as causes of weak third-quarter 2025 profit.

Meritz Securities expected in a June 2026 report that reused components at domestic customers, lower BH EVS prices and one-off labor costs would weigh on second-quarter profitability as memory prices rise.

For January to September 2026, cumulative revenue fell 5.2% year on year while cumulative operating profit rose 95.0%, and first-half operating profit turned positive from a loss a year earlier.

Industry analysis

Demand for FPCBs tracks shifts in smartphone form factors and display technology. In a June 2025 report, SK Securities expected a 2016-like upcycle as North American foldable launches and wider OLED adoption in tablets and notebooks lift ASPs and create new demand.

The same report saw the competitive landscape turning more favorable because only a few FPCB makers remain in Korea, and argued that an R&D-led high-value strategy matters more than capacity expansion.

In November 2025, Eugene Investment & Securities treated 2025 as the earnings low and said IT OLED and foldable growth drivers would lift results from 2026. In February 2026, iM Securities said foldable demand looks relatively steadier during a period of sharp DRAM price increases.

Meritz Securities noted in June 2026 that rising memory prices are prompting customers to cut costs, which could expand the use of reused components in domestic customer volumes. This shows memory pricing reaching FPCB volume mix through customers' sourcing decisions.

Because the North American FPCB business accounted for a majority of 2025 revenue, results are sensitive to the shipment flow of North American customers. IT OLED volumes depend on when panel lines start up, and KB Securities pointed to new line startups for tablet OLED programs in 2026 and 2027.

Bull factors

Expanding North American foldable volume

In a February 2026 report, Eugene Investment & Securities projected North American foldable-related revenue at KRW 330.5 billion in 2027. KB Securities estimated in November 2025 that unit prices on foldable iPhones would exceed those of standard iPhones.

Samsung Securities cited volume increases at a North American customer and better product mix as drivers of first-quarter 2026 results.

Narrowing IT OLED losses

In a November 2025 report, KB Securities named narrowing IT OLED losses as a key variable for the 2026 earnings rebound. Meritz Securities said in June 2026 that volume production for iPad mini OLED should support IT OLED revenue growth. Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 rose 95.0% year on year, in line with that direction.

Expansion into robot charging

Samsung Securities described the robot-charger push as building on the order backlog and stable customer base of the smartphone wireless charging business. The company unveiled a vertical charging station for robots at CES 2026.

In an August 2026 report, Kiwoom Securities said robot-sector entry could become a factor in raising corporate value depending on results.

09

Bear factors

Weak domestic customer volumes

In a June 2026 report, Meritz Securities expected domestic customer sales to fall short of expectations as rising memory prices lead customers to use more reused components. Domestic and other FPCB sales were KRW 269 billion in 2025, smaller than North American sales but still one leg of the revenue mix. Second-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 7.89 billion, down 26.0% from the prior quarter.

Thin margins and fixed-cost burden

The operating margin fell from 7.8% in 2022 to 3.0% in 2025. KB Securities explained that the IT OLED unit posted a sizable operating loss in the third quarter of 2025 because of fixed costs tied to new tablet OLED lines for 2026 and 2027. While those new lines ramp, the pace of margin recovery could remain limited.

Continued decline in controlling-shareholder net income

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. The third-quarter 2026 figure of KRW 5.74 billion was down 84.8% year on year.

KB Securities noted that the in-vehicle smartphone wireless charger unit recorded a lower operating margin than usual in the third quarter of 2025 because of U.S. tariffs.

Risk factors

Customer concentration

North American FPCB sales made up a majority of 2025 revenue, so shipment schedules and product specification changes at a single customer flow quickly into results. If the foldable launch slips or volumes are adjusted, gaps between broker estimates could widen.

Quarterly revenue falling 26.9% in the first quarter of 2026 from the prior quarter illustrates this volatility.

Cost pressure and domestic volumes

When memory prices rise, customers push to cut costs, which can expand the use of reused components and pressure both domestic customer sales and margins. Meritz Securities cited this shift in its June 2026 outlook for second-quarter domestic customer sales. One-off labor costs and lower BH EVS prices were also presented as burdens on profitability.

Timing of new businesses and external factors

The robot charging business is at the stage of a CES 2026 unveiling and announced plans, so when and how much it contributes to results will show up in later disclosures.

The in-vehicle wireless charger unit was hit by U.S. tariffs in the third quarter of 2025, and changes in tariff conditions could again shift that unit's profitability. If new business results come late, the timing of any confirmation of higher corporate value is also pushed back.

Overall view

BHCo's reported results show operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders declining after the 2022 peak, and the 2025 operating margin fell to 3.0%.

Revenue rose for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025, but profit declined over the same period, so top-line growth did not translate into earnings.

In 2026, third-quarter operating profit rose sharply from the prior quarter to KRW 30.5 billion, and cumulative operating profit for January to September rose 95.0% year on year.

However, third-quarter net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 84.8% year on year, so whether the profit recovery reached controlling shareholders still needs confirmation.

Bullish points include North American foldable volume, narrowing IT OLED losses and robot charging, while bearish points include concentration in North American sales, more reused components at domestic customers, and a thin operating margin with a heavy fixed-cost burden.

Broker target prices differed, with Meritz Securities at KRW 32,000 (June 29, 2026) and Kiwoom Securities at KRW 24,000 (August 4, 2026).

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results and the 2026 annual report, followed by the first-quarter 2027 results, which will show how much foldable volume and robot charging are reflected. This report provides information only and does not offer an investment opinion.
