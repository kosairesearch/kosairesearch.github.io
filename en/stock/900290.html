02

Business structure

GRT was founded in Hong Kong in 2012 as a non-financial holding company and listed on KOSDAQ in 2016. Its principal operations run through a subsidiary that develops, manufactures and sells packaging films and optical protective films.

Optical films form the core, alongside optical protective films, PE amorphous protective films and 3D display optical films. The company groups its business into four areas: precision coating adhesive and protective materials, functional coating materials, polymer thin-film materials, and optical films.

Product lines listed in its investor relations material include brightening film base films, polarizer protective films, electrochromic films, flexible films, food packaging films, pharmaceutical packaging films and nanoporous materials.

Customers are described as spanning flat panel displays, semiconductors and automotive applications, and the company is reported to be expanding into IoT and new application areas. Named competitors in China include 3M, TESA and NITTO DENKO.

Segment revenue shares and product-level sales were not confirmed in public sources, so no ratios are presented. The largest shareholder, ZHOU YONGNAN and one other party, is reported to hold 38.79% on an aggregator basis, and this concentration is a key governance variable.

The company is classified as a Chinese company listed in Korea, so its production footprint and regulatory conditions need to be read together.

Earnings analysis

The filings are denominated in CNY, so all amounts in this section are converted to KRW.

Revenue for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was KRW 1.18 trillion (converted to KRW), up 17.2% year on year, extending a path from KRW 822.6 billion in the year ended June 2023 and KRW 931 billion in the year ended June 2024 to KRW 1.01 trillion in the year ended June 2025, and it has risen for three consecutive fiscal years from the year ended June 2024.

Operating profit moved from KRW 84.4 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 162.4 billion in the year ended June 2024 (up 92.5%), then fell 11.3% to KRW 144 billion in the year ended June 2025.

Operating profit for the year ended June 2026 was KRW 180.7 billion, up 25.4%, making it the largest year in the June 2023 to June 2026 range.

The operating margin was 10.3% in the year ended June 2023, 17.4% in the year ended June 2024, 14.3% in the year ended June 2025 and 15.3% in the year ended June 2026, so the latest margin has not returned to the fiscal 2024 level.

Owner-attributable net income was KRW 58.4 billion in the year ended June 2023, KRW 98.8 billion in the year ended June 2024 (up 69.3%), KRW 76.9 billion in the year ended June 2025 (down 22.3%) and KRW 80.6 billion in the year ended June 2026 (up 4.8%), with the year ended June 2024 being the largest.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 128.9 billion in the year ended June 2026, and the difference from owner-attributable income is the share belonging to non-controlling interests.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 54.6 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 123.3 billion in the year ended June 2026, growing faster than profit.

On a quarterly basis, revenue for the second quarter of 2026 was KRW 319 billion, up 14.7% from the prior quarter and up 23.0% year on year, while the first quarter of 2026 fell 8.8% from the prior quarter to KRW 278.2 billion.

Operating profit of KRW 45.9 billion in the second quarter of 2026 was up 17.5% year on year, and owner-attributable net income of KRW 19.7 billion was up 16.0% year on year.

Owner-attributable net income peaked at KRW 24.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 among the disclosed quarters, dipped to KRW 16.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and recovered to KRW 19.7 billion in the second quarter.

Public sources did not confirm one-off items or the detailed cost structure, so this analysis is limited to the trend in reported figures.

05

Industry analysis

The precision coating functional materials that GRT produces are used across electronics, automotive and building energy-saving applications. The field is described as technology-intensive, with high entry barriers for new players.

GRT's core product, TFT-LCD optical films, is tied to display panel demand, so panel makers' capital spending and utilization directly affect its sales direction. Its customer base spanning flat panel displays, semiconductors and automotive applications partly dampens the cycle exposure of any single market.

In terms of competition, 3M, TESA and NITTO DENKO are named as major competitors in China. Market share by competitor was not confirmed in public sources, so the company's position is not compared numerically. No broker analysis on the current position of the display cycle was confirmed either.

It is hard to tell from filings alone whether the recent profit recovery came from price pass-through, higher volumes or raw material costs.

In summary, the industry picture shows technology barriers and customer diversification as strengths, while dependence on the display market and competitive price pressure remain weaknesses.

Bull factors

Operating profit recovery

The 25.4% increase made the year ended June 2026 the year with the largest operating profit in the June 2023 to June 2026 range. Second-quarter 2026 operating profit also rose 17.5% year on year to KRW 45.9 billion. The reversal of the decline seen in the year ended June 2025 is cited as a bullish argument.

Cash generation and balance sheet improvement

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 54.6 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 123.3 billion in the year ended June 2026. Over the same period, the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 92.8% to 68.5%. The wider financial room is cited as a bullish argument.

Share price relative to net assets

Total equity at the end of June 2026 was KRW 1.42 trillion, of which owner-attributable equity was KRW 1.07 trillion. The share price relative to asset value is a fact that can be cited as a bullish point.

Bear factors

Owner-attributable earnings below peak

Owner-attributable net income of KRW 80.6 billion for the year ended June 2026 is below KRW 98.8 billion for the year ended June 2024. The gap to net income of KRW 128.9 billion is the non-controlling share, so the profit attributable to shareholders is much smaller than net income. The fact that owner-attributable income has not grown as fast as operating profit is the bearish point.

Earnings volatility

In the year ended June 2025, operating profit fell 11.3% and owner-attributable net income fell 22.3%. Quarterly owner-attributable net income also dropped from KRW 24.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 16.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026. Large quarter-to-quarter swings are cited as a bearish point.

Concentrated ownership and China-based structure

The largest shareholder, ZHOU YONGNAN and one other party, is reported to hold 38.79%, and the free float is aggregated at about 60.51%. The company is classified as a Chinese company listed in Korea, which adds governance and regulatory points to check. This is cited as a bearish point for minority shareholders.

10

Risk factors

Governance and ownership concentration

A high controlling stake means major decisions could be dominated by a concentrated holder. Any change in shareholding or related-party transactions could alter the information and decision-making access available to minority shareholders. Comparing shareholding disclosures over time is necessary to verify the facts.

China-based operations and regulation

Production and the competitive landscape are concentrated in China, so the company is exposed to changes in local policy, exchange rates and trade conditions. Because control runs through a Hong Kong holding company, changes in governance-related regulation also matter. This risk is directly linked to whether the profit recovery can be sustained.

Display cycle and customer concentration

If the display panel cycle weakens in a structure where TFT-LCD optical films carry a large share, revenue swings can widen. Quarterly sales can move sharply, as seen in the 8.8% quarter-on-quarter decline in the first quarter of 2026. Until new product lines account for a larger share, dependence on existing display demand remains a risk.

Overall view

In the fiscal year ended June 2026, GRT's revenue of KRW 1.18 trillion (converted to KRW), operating profit of KRW 180.7 billion and operating cash flow of KRW 123.3 billion were each the largest in the June 2023 to June 2026 range.

However, owner-attributable net income was largest in the year ended June 2024 at KRW 98.8 billion, and the KRW 80.6 billion recorded in the year ended June 2026 is lower, so the quality of the recovery is not yet complete.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 92.8% in the year ended June 2023 to 68.5% in the year ended June 2026, and of total equity of KRW 1.42 trillion at the end of June 2026, KRW 1.07 trillion belongs to owners of the parent.

Second-quarter 2026 operating profit and owner-attributable net income both rose year on year, so the quarterly trend is moving in a favorable direction.

Bullish factors are the recovery in earnings and cash flow and the stronger balance sheet, while bearish factors are the volatility of owner-attributable earnings, the concentrated controlling stake, China-based operations and dependence on the display market.

The next checkpoint is revenue and operating profit in the third-quarter 2026 quarterly report, and for the fourth quarter the benchmark is whether owner-attributable net income exceeds the KRW 24.5 billion level of the third quarter of 2025.
