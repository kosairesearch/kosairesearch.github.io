02

Business structure

GRT is a non-financial holding company established in Hong Kong. Its operating subsidiary, Tongli Optoelectronics, develops, makes and sells packaging films and optical protective films.

The company was set up in Hong Kong in 2012 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2016, with packaging and optical protective films as its main products. Its business is organized into four areas: precision-coated adhesive and protective materials, functional coating materials and polymer thin-film materials.

The service screen classifies it under financials, but the actual business is functional film materials made at plants in China. According to its March 2026 investor presentation, GRT had 936 employees, and its main products are described as secondary-battery, automotive and chemical materials.

Revenue by segment could not be confirmed in this research, so the segment mix is described only qualitatively. On customers, the company said in June 2025 that its high-performance materials are used in products of key clients such as Tesla and Xiaomi.

Lens Technology, a maker of glass for smart devices, is reported to be a strategic investor with a stake in a key GRT subsidiary, and in 2024 the company said it signed a cooperation agreement with Apple supplier Luxshare ICT. The main Chinese competitors named in GRT's business description include 3M, TESA and Nitto Denko.

04

Earnings analysis

On reported figures (converted to KRW), revenue was KRW 458.2 billion in 2022, KRW 801.4 billion in 2023 (up 74.9%), KRW 907 billion in 2024 (up 13.2%) and KRW 980.2 billion in 2025 (up 8.1%), rising for three straight years from 2023 to 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 56 billion in 2022 and KRW 82.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 158.3 billion in 2024, then fell 11.3% to KRW 140.3 billion in 2025; 2024 was the largest year of 2022-2025. The operating margin moved from 12.2% in 2022 to 10.3% in 2023, 17.4% in 2024 and 14.3% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 22.3% from KRW 96.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 74.9 billion in 2025, while net income including non-controlling interests declined from KRW 119 billion to KRW 98.3 billion.

By quarter, revenue of KRW 269.6 billion in the first quarter of 2025 and KRW 297.2 billion in the second dropped to KRW 160.8 billion in the third (down 45.9% from the prior quarter), and operating profit fell from KRW 46.6 billion to KRW 11.7 billion.

The fourth quarter recovered, with revenue of KRW 252.7 billion (up 57.2% from the prior quarter and 1.2% from a year earlier) and operating profit of KRW 38 billion (up 225.3% from the prior quarter but down 8.9% from a year earlier).

The cause of the third-quarter weakness cannot be determined from the reported figures alone, so the notes to the periodic reports and company explanations need to be checked.

Among post-report information, a disclosure on October 1, 2026 showed consolidated revenue of KRW 1.2011 trillion for the July 2025 to June 2026 fiscal year, up 25.21%, and operating profit of KRW 184 billion, up 34.04% (converted to KRW; this is a separate disclosure rather than a periodic report, and its conversion basis differs from the reported table above, so the two are not compared directly).

In yuan terms, revenue rose 17.17% and operating profit 25.43%, which differs from the KRW growth rates. In that release, the company said its consolidated operating margin rose from about 14.3% to about 15.3%.

Operating cash flow grew from KRW 33.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 91.6 billion in 2025, and the debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 76.6%.

05

Industry analysis

GRT's end markets are spread across smart devices, automobiles, secondary batteries and electronic components such as MLCCs. In its March 2026 investor presentation, the company said niche segments such as optical film, MLCC release film and window film should grow by more than 10% a year.

The same material states that release film accounts for more than 25% of the manufacturing cost of high-end MLCCs.

On industry conditions, China-based Jiemei Electronic and Technology (002859 SZ) was reported to have said that release film sales have risen more than 100% year on year since March 2026 and that supply shortages should persist until the first half of 2027.

That company also plans to invest CNY 2.684 billion to expand high-end MLCC release film capacity. In Korea, an MLCC release film maker said on July 20, 2026 that it was moving its lines to full operation as orders rose with AI server and data center investment.

Demand signals are improving, but continued capacity additions by competitors also raise the question of medium-term oversupply and price competition.

GRT sits closer to a local supplier of a broad film range, positioned between large Japanese and US-based makers and Chinese rivals, and its share in MLCC release film could not be confirmed.

06

Outlook

The growth drivers visible in company statements are a new plant and a wider range of high-value films. GRT said it is preparing to build a new plant and expects growth to accelerate as plants are completed in sequence and the production base expands.

In its March 2026 investor presentation, the company described successful mass production of MLCC-related products.

For the July 2025 to June 2026 fiscal year, the company said its key subsidiary Jiangsu Huiji New Material Technology drove growth, with annual revenue of CNY 4.05 billion (about KRW 840.4 billion), up 25.3% from the prior year.

According to a securities information site, consolidated first-quarter 2026 revenue rose 20.4% from a year earlier, operating profit 34.3% and net income 6.3%. Net income rising more slowly than operating profit suggests that non-operating items and the non-controlling interests' share need to be checked.

Broker target prices and specific company guidance figures could not be confirmed in this research. The completion timing of the new plant and the pace of utilization gains still need to be clarified in disclosures and investor relations material, and the investment burden and funding method are also worth monitoring.

08

Bull factors

Larger revenue base with defended margins

On reported figures, revenue grew from KRW 458.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 980.2 billion in 2025, rising for three straight years from 2023 to 2025. The 2025 operating margin of 14.3% was below 2024 but above 2022 and 2023.

The company said its consolidated operating margin for the July 2025 to June 2026 fiscal year rose to about 15.3% from the prior year.

High-value demand such as MLCC release film

Chinese peer Jiemei Electronic and Technology said release film sales have risen sharply and expects supply shortages to last until the first half of 2027. GRT also described successful mass production of MLCC-related products in its March 2026 investor presentation. If demand holds, higher utilization at the new plant could translate into profit contribution.

Improving balance sheet and cash flow

The debt ratio improved from 92.8% in 2023 to 76.6% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 1.07 trillion and total liabilities were KRW 816.9 billion. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 33.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 91.6 billion in 2025, which supports capacity to fund investment.

09

Bear factors

Profit decline after the 2024 peak

Operating profit fell 11.3% from KRW 158.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 140.3 billion in 2025, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 22.3% from KRW 96.3 billion to KRW 74.9 billion.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, operating profit was down 8.9% from a year earlier and controlling-shareholder net income was down 29.5%. Profit did not keep pace with revenue growth in this stretch, which is a concern.

Quarterly volatility and limited explanation

Third-quarter 2025 revenue fell 45.9% from the prior quarter to KRW 160.8 billion, and operating profit fell 74.9% to KRW 11.7 billion. The fourth-quarter recovery notwithstanding, the irregular quarterly path makes annual trends an incomplete guide. The cause cannot be determined from the reported figures alone.

Limited profit reaching controlling shareholders

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 98.3 billion in 2025, but net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 74.9 billion. Of total equity of KRW 1.07 trillion at the end of 2025, equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 886.5 billion.

Lens Technology is reported to be a strategic investor with a stake in a key subsidiary, so part of subsidiary profit is shared with minority holders.

Risk factors

Currency translation

GRT reports in yuan, and the KRW amounts are converted values. For the July 2025 to June 2026 fiscal year, operating profit growth in yuan terms was 25.43%, versus 34.04% in KRW terms as reported by the media.

Exchange rate moves change the KRW size of results and equity, so the reporting currency should be checked when comparing figures.

Competition and customer reliance

Competitors named by GRT include 3M, TESA and Nitto Denko. In release film, Chinese and Korean makers continue to add capacity. The share of revenue tied to specific strategic customers and investors could not be confirmed and should be checked in later disclosures.

Governance and funding

The company previously pursued and then withdrew a voluntary delisting and withdrew a planned Nasdaq SPAC listing, and the stock has been criticized for swinging on such non-business factors. A disclosure aggregator also lists a board decision on August 31, 2026 to guarantee KRW 206.5 billion of debt.

The counterparty and terms must be checked in the original filing, and funding needs tied to the new plant are also worth watching.

12

Overall view

GRT is a Hong Kong-based holding company that makes precision-coated film materials in China, and despite its sector label its business is closer to materials manufacturing. On reported figures, revenue rose for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, reaching KRW 980.2 billion in 2025 (converted to KRW).

Operating profit, however, peaked at KRW 158.3 billion in 2024 and fell to KRW 140.3 billion in 2025, with the operating margin easing from 17.4% to 14.3%. The sharp drop in revenue and profit in the third quarter of 2025 shows that quarterly volatility is large.

Among post-report information, the company's October 1, 2026 disclosure for the July 2025 to June 2026 fiscal year showed higher revenue and operating profit, but it still needs verification against the periodic report.

The 76.6% debt ratio and higher operating cash flow are positives, while governance issues, currency translation effects and the non-controlling share are burdens. The next points to check are quarterly results, detailed items in the periodic report, and disclosures on the new plant and the debt guarantee.
