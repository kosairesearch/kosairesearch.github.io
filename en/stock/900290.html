02

Business structure

GRT is a Hong Kong-headquartered holding company that controls a production group based in Jiangsu Province, China. The company was founded in Hong Kong in 2012 as a non-financial holding company and listed on KOSDAQ in 2016.

Its main operating subsidiary develops, manufactures and sells packaging films and optical protective films. Its core product is TFT-LCD optical film, supplemented by optical protective film, PE amorphous protective film and 3D display optical film.

The company runs four business areas: precision coating adhesive and protective materials, functional coating materials, and polymer thin-film materials. Its customers span flat-panel displays, semiconductors and automotive, and it is extending into IoT and new applications.

The recent consolidated sales increase was attributed in the filing mainly to higher sales at its key subsidiary, Jiangsu Huizhi New Materials Technology. That subsidiary's sales for the year ended June 2026 were RMB 4.05 billion, up 25.3%, according to the company.

Sales of its optical protective film rose sharply after the third plant was completed, and the company said it has begun mass production of MLCC-related products. The company's IR material says the industry mix of its sales is shifting toward faster-growing sectors such as semiconductors and chemicals.

Competitively, it operates in an optical film market that moves with display panel demand, against domestic and overseas film and materials makers.

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated sales for the year ended June 2026 were KRW 1.18 trillion, up 17.2% year on year. Sales have risen for three consecutive years from the June 2024 year to the June 2026 year, moving from KRW 822.6 billion in the June 2023 year to KRW 931 billion (up 13.2%) and KRW 1.01 trillion (up 8.1%).

Among the June 2023 to June 2026 years, the June 2026 year had the largest sales. Operating profit rose 92.5% to KRW 162.4 billion in the June 2024 year from KRW 84.4 billion, the largest growth rate in the period.

In the June 2025 year, operating profit fell 11.3% to KRW 144 billion and operating margin dropped from 17.4% to 14.3%.

In the June 2026 year, operating profit recovered to KRW 180.7 billion, up 25.4%, with operating margin at 15.3%, and the company said profit growth outpaced sales growth, strengthening its earnings structure.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 98.8 billion in the June 2024 year, up 69.3%, and reached KRW 80.6 billion in the June 2026 year, up 4.8%. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 128.9 billion in the same year, and the difference is the non-controlling interests share.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 54.6 billion in the June 2023 year to KRW 123.3 billion in the June 2026 year, above controlling-shareholder net income.

Quarterly sales rose from KRW 259.4 billion in the second quarter of 2025 to KRW 305 billion in the fourth quarter, then fell 8.8% to KRW 278.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

Second-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 319 billion, up 14.7% from the prior quarter and 23.0% from a year earlier, and operating profit of KRW 45.9 billion rose 17.5% year on year.

Controlling-shareholder net income over the latest four quarters, from the third quarter of 2025 to the second quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 80.6 billion, matching the June 2026 year.

Currency also matters: income items were translated at an average of 207.37 won per yuan, and balance sheet items at 226.97 won per yuan as of June 30, 2026.

05

Industry analysis

Optical film and optical protective film are materials whose demand moves with display panel output. According to market research by Frost & Sullivan, which the company cites, the sub-segments including optical film, MLCC release film and window film are expected to grow at more than 10% a year.

The company says it is developing faster-growing products aimed at these segments. From a cycle perspective, the sharp rise in optical protective film sales after the third plant was completed shows capital spending moving into revenue.

Display panel conditions change cyclically, so optical film demand may follow the same pattern. Competitively, the company is spreading its customer base beyond flat-panel displays into semiconductors, automotive and IoT.

Its move into MLCC-related release film, used in multilayer ceramic capacitor processes, reads as an effort to broaden the demand base even if end markets shift. Comparisons with rival producers can only be made qualitatively on the basis of the information available.

06

Outlook

The company's IR material says operating profit and net income stepped up after the third plant was completed, and it expects further growth once the fourth plant is finished. The subsidiary expects production capacity to expand after the new plant is completed.

The IR material describes third-quarter 2026 results as showing sales growth and better profitability versus a year earlier, but these are directional statements rather than figures, so they should be checked against the quarterly report.

With MLCC-related products now in mass production, the next question is how far the portfolio extends beyond optical films. The fourth plant's start date and added capacity should be confirmed from the company's periodic reports.

Won-translated results move with the yuan-won rate, so each quarter's currency effect should be separated from operating factors. Because income and balance sheet items use different rates, asset and liability values in won also shift with the exchange rate.

Whether subsidiary-led sales growth continues to drive consolidated results is the point to check from the third quarter of 2026 onward.

Bull factors

Earnings recovery

Operating profit for the June 2026 year rose 25.4% to KRW 180.7 billion, and operating margin climbed to 15.3%. Profit that had fallen in the June 2025 year recovered within one year. The company said profit growth outpaced sales growth, which strengthened its earnings structure.

Capacity turning into earnings

The company's IR material says optical protective film sales rose sharply after the third plant, with operating profit and net income stepping up. Further growth is expected after the fourth plant. Capacity investment feeding into sales and profit is cited as a strength.

Stronger balance sheet

The debt ratio fell from 92.8% in the June 2023 year to 68.5% in the June 2026 year. Operating cash flow rose to KRW 123.3 billion, above controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 80.6 billion. A stronger capital structure could support further expansion.

Bear factors

The June 2025 setback

In the June 2025 year, operating profit fell 11.3% and controlling-shareholder net income fell 22.3%, with operating margin dropping from 17.4% to 14.3%. The June 2026 year recovered margin to 15.3%, still below the June 2024 level of 17.4%.

Reliance on one subsidiary

The filing attributes the June 2026 year's sales growth mainly to higher sales at its key subsidiary, Jiangsu Huizhi New Materials Technology. Its sales rose 25.3%, but concentration in one subsidiary means its utilization and operating conditions feed directly into consolidated results.

Quarterly swings and currency

First-quarter 2026 sales fell 8.8% from the prior quarter, operating profit fell 12.6% and controlling-shareholder net income fell 17.8%. Won-translated results depend on the yuan rate, so the same operating performance can show a different won amount.

Risk factors

Exchange rate and translation

The company keeps its books in yuan and discloses in won. Income items use average rates and balance sheet items use period-end rates, so the two rates differ. A move in the yuan changes won amounts for sales, profit, assets and liabilities together.

Subsidiary concentration and expansion execution

Sales growth is concentrated in the key subsidiary, and capacity expansion after the new plant is central to expectations. If plant start-up slips or customer demand falls short, the expansion benefit could reach earnings later. The fourth plant's start-up timing and utilization should be checked in the next periodic reports.

Ownership split and quarterly volatility

Of KRW 128.9 billion in net income, KRW 80.6 billion belongs to controlling shareholders, so the non-controlling share is large. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders is therefore smaller than total profit. Quarterly sales and profit also move up and down, so a single quarter is hard to read as a trend.

12

Overall view

GRT is a materials company whose main products are optical film and optical protective film. For the year ended June 2026 it recorded sales of KRW 1.18 trillion and operating profit of KRW 180.7 billion.

Sales have risen for three consecutive years from the June 2024 year, and operating margin recovered to 15.3% from 14.3% in the June 2025 year. However, profit dipped in the June 2025 year, and margin is still below 17.4% in the June 2024 year.

The gap between controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 80.6 billion and total net income of KRW 128.9 billion means the non-controlling structure must be read alongside results.

Growth leans on the key subsidiary and the third plant, so whether the fourth plant starts and whether subsidiary earnings persist are the key variables. Exchange rates directly affect won-translated results and should be separated each quarter.

Market value sits low against book net assets, while the dividend record is limited, so how the market reflects earnings durability and currency swings is the open interpretive question. The next checkpoint is the third-quarter 2026 periodic report and subsequent quarterly results.
