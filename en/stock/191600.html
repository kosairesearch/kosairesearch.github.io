A Loss-Making PCB Maker with Volatile Shares

Bluetop is a small KONEX-listed maker of automotive and telecom PCBs that slipped into an operating loss in 2025, while its share price has swung sharply over short periods.

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Bluetop is a PCB manufacturer headquartered in Incheon that listed on the KONEX market in 2016. Its core products are PCBs for automotive electronics and high-end PCBs for telecommunications. Another source describes its main products as automotive PCBs for items such as audio units and junction boxes.

Market data services label the company as a semiconductor name, but FnGuide classifies it under electronic equipment and instruments. Its business therefore differs from chipmakers such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, and direct comparison with them is of limited use.

According to a 2023 report, it also produces 5G network communication parts and mobile parts as main lines. The same report named Hyundai and Kia suppliers such as Yura Corporation and SL Corporation among its main customers.

The company said it planned to build synergies through process capabilities for special PCBs and for medical-device and defense PCBs. A segment-by-segment revenue breakdown could not be confirmed from the available material, so it is described only qualitatively.

Daeduck Electronics and Simmtech make semiconductor package substrates tied to AI chip demand, whereas Bluetop concentrates on automotive and telecom boards, which follow a different demand cycle.

03

Recent trends

On FnGuide figures, revenue was 41.2 billion won in 2022, 47.6 billion won in 2023 and 45.0 billion won in 2024. In the same data, operating profit moved from 1.0 billion won in 2022 to 2.5 billion won in 2023 and 0.4 billion won in 2024.

A JobKorea compilation puts 2025 revenue at 43.83 billion won, down about 3% from the prior year. The same compilation shows a 2025 operating loss of 3.64 billion won. Gross profit fell from about 5.28 billion won in 2024 to about 1.60 billion won in 2025, and the net loss was about 5.96 billion won.

At the end of 2025 the debt ratio was 543.48%, with operating cash flow of about negative 1.81 billion won and investing cash flow of about negative 6.38 billion won.

These figures come from a secondary compilation and should be checked against the original annual report on DART, and 2026 quarterly or half-year results could not be confirmed. The share price was 6,950 won on March 31, 2026. It closed at 14,780 won on October 1, 2026, and the 52-week range is 5,310 to 15,830 won.

On September 18, 2026 the shares rose 14.54% to 13,390 won. The stock closed at 11,460 won on October 27, up 14.71%, for a market capitalization of 32.8 billion won, but no disclosure or news item explaining the day's gain was found.

04

Outlook

Bluetop will hold an extraordinary shareholders' meeting at 9:00 a.m. on November 2, 2026, with the election of inside director Kim Seong-hwan on the agenda. The filings alone do not show how a change in board composition will affect business strategy or the plan to repair the balance sheet.

The largest variable is whether operating profit returns, which depends on a recovery in gross profit. In 2023 the company projected steady revenue growth from higher electric-vehicle volumes and broader telecom and mobile customer additions.

Revenue declined in 2025, so there is a gap between that outlook and actual results. FnGuide summarizes the situation as a search for a shift to higher value-added products amid weaker demand.

The 2025 interest coverage ratio was negative and borrowing dependence was 54.51%, so financing costs stand in the way of an earnings recovery. No broker consensus estimates appear on FnGuide, which limits external forecasts to rely on.

05

Bull factors

Automotive and telecom customer base

A 2023 report named Hyundai and Kia suppliers among its main customers. Automotive electronics is generally seen as a field where customers are not easily switched once qualification and delivery history build up.

Having high-end telecom PCBs in the lineup also gives some room for one end market to offset weakness in another.

Profit history and room for margin recovery

Operating profit was positive from 2022 through 2024, reaching 2.5 billion won in 2023. The operating margin that year was 5.27%. Because gross profit dropped sharply in 2025, the operating margin has room to improve on business metrics if costs and utilization normalize.

Institutional holder and board addition

As of December 31, 2025, KB Financial Group is listed as holding 7.00% of the shares, or 200,000 shares. The presence of a large financial group on the shareholder list is a confirmed fact, but it does not indicate any involvement in management or plans to raise the stake.

If the November 2 extraordinary meeting approves the new inside director, the composition of management will change.

06

Bear factors

2025 operating loss and weaker profitability

The 2025 operating loss was 3.64 billion won. Gross profit declined from about 5.28 billion won in 2024 to about 1.60 billion won in 2025. Revenue fell only about 3%, yet profit dropped sharply, which points to cost structure and fixed-cost burden as problems.

Weak balance sheet

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 543.48%, up sharply from 263.22% in 2024. The quick ratio was 40.39% and operating cash flow was a net outflow. With debt already elevated and investing outflows continuing, additional funding may be needed.

Thin trading and large price swings

The Investing.com page shows very low trading volume. Small KONEX stocks can move sharply on small volumes. The same page lists a day range of 9,800 to 13,120 won for October 27, which, alongside the 11,460 won close, suggests a wide intraday range.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Weaker vehicle production or electric-vehicle demand could reduce orders for automotive PCBs. Because borrowing dependence is high, the interest-rate level directly affects interest costs. Exchange rates and raw material prices such as copper also affect costs, but specific sensitivities could not be confirmed.

Industry

PCB makers are exposed to customer demands for lower prices and to price competition from rivals. FnGuide summarizes the current phase as a search for a shift to higher value-added products amid weaker demand.

Moving into higher-value products requires equipment and qualification spending, which can strain near-term cash flow.

Company

In March 2025 there was a filing on early acquisition of bonds after a convertible bond issuance. Continued equity-linked financing could dilute existing shareholders. Low liquidity on the KONEX market and management changes tied to the extraordinary meeting agenda also warrant monitoring.

08

Overall view

Bluetop is a small KONEX-listed manufacturer specializing in PCBs for automotive electronics and telecom equipment. It earned operating profits in 2022-2024 but recorded an operating loss of 3.64 billion won in 2025, and its debt ratio rose to 543.48%.

The bullish side points to its customer base, past profit record and the management reinforcement from a new inside director. The bearish side points to the sharp drop in gross profit, the weak balance sheet, thin trading volume and large price swings.

The 2026 results, the outcome of the November 2 extraordinary meeting and the method of any new financing are the key items to check.
