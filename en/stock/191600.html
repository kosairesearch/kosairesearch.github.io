02

Business structure

Bluetop was founded in 2002 as a printed circuit board (PCB) maker headquartered in Incheon and is listed on the KONEX market. Its industry classification is laminates for printed circuit boards, and it had 151 employees at the end of 2025, making it a small or medium-sized enterprise.

Its main end markets are telecommunications infrastructure and electric vehicle components, and investment cycles in those two areas strongly shape its sales. In the 2024 annual results, revenue declined as lower electric vehicle demand and reduced investment by three major telecom carriers weighed on sales.

The company has responded by expanding its PCB lineup built on low-dielectric materials to build a higher value-added product mix. Low-dielectric materials are generally used in high-speed and high-frequency signal transmission, so the share of this lineup is a key variable for the pace of margin recovery.

In competition, it is compared with listed PCB peers such as Daeduck Electronics and Simmtech, while its KONEX listing means trading volume and liquidity are smaller than those of KOSPI or KOSDAQ companies.

Although it is classified under semiconductors, its core business is board-related products rather than memory chips or front-end semiconductor equipment.

At the end of 2025, total assets were in the KRW 42.3 billion range, total liabilities in the KRW 35.7 billion range, and total equity in the KRW 6.5 billion range, indicating a balance sheet with high leverage.

03

Recent trends

On a standalone basis, 2025 sales were in the KRW 43.8 billion range, slightly below the KRW 45.0 billion range recorded in 2024. Operations moved from an operating profit in the KRW 400 million range in 2024 to an operating loss in the KRW 3.6 billion range in 2025.

The net result also widened, from a loss in the KRW 1.3 billion range in 2024 to a loss in the KRW 5.9 billion range in 2025. Operating margin declined from 5.27% in 2023 to 0.95% in 2024 and then to -8.32% in 2025.

Operating cash flow showed an outflow in the KRW 1.8 billion range, and investing cash flow showed an outflow in the KRW 6.4 billion range, reflecting heavy capital spending. Cash operating profit was positive in the KRW 870 million range for the same year, so the accounting loss and cash generation diverged.

At the end of 2025, total assets were in the KRW 42.3 billion range, total liabilities in the KRW 35.7 billion range, and total equity in the KRW 6.5 billion range, down sharply from equity in the KRW 12.5 billion range at the end of 2024.

The 24th annual general meeting, held with a disclosure dated March 31, 2026, approved the financial statements, director appointments, and compensation limits. In early November 2026, the company decided on a third-party placement of 53,750 common shares to raise KRW 430 million for operating funds.

According to the investing.com data as of October 27, 2026, the stock traded within a 52-week range of KRW 5,310 to KRW 15,830, and the current price as of November 11, 2026 is KRW 12,900.

04

Outlook

Near-term results depend on whether the premium PCB lineup can narrow the operating loss, as the disclosed figures show. Because operating margin fell to -8.32% in 2025, a combination of sales recovery and cost structure improvement would be needed to provide a basis for a return to profit.

Positive variables include the expansion of low-dielectric PCBs and the capital spending trend across the semiconductor sector.

Samsung Electronics has reportedly planned 2026 semiconductor facility investment of KRW 76.8 trillion, up 61.7% from the prior year, and SK hynix is expected to raise its investment to the upper KRW 40 trillion range.

However, whether this investment expansion translates into Bluetop sales depends on customer mix and supply arrangements. Negative variables include regulatory concerns over AI data centers in the United States after the midterm elections and the sell-off in chip stocks that followed.

The timing of a recovery in electric vehicle and telecom infrastructure demand is also not yet clear. On the financial side, high leverage and continued investing cash outflows remain constraints on financing capacity.

The third-quarter 2026 quarterly report will show whether sales are recovering and whether the operating loss continues, and the full-year results will be reviewed alongside the use of the proceeds from the share placement.

05

Bull factors

Expansion of low-dielectric PCB lineup

Bluetop is expanding its PCB lineup built on low-dielectric materials to raise the share of higher value-added products. If this shift takes hold, product pricing and operating margin could both improve. The 2024 annual results also presented this lineup as a driver of profitability.

Cash generation cushioning losses

Cash operating profit for 2025 was in the KRW 800 million range, a positive figure that diverged from the accounting operating loss. The operating loss did not translate directly into an equivalent cash outflow, which is a notable feature of the company.

Operating cash flow still showed an outflow in the KRW 1.8 billion range, but the positive cash operating profit provided some cushion during the loss period.

Indirect tailwind from chip capex

Samsung Electronics has reportedly planned 2026 semiconductor facility investment of KRW 76.8 trillion, up 61.7% from the prior year. SK hynix is also expected to lift its 2026 investment to the upper KRW 40 trillion range.

These capex expansions are an industry-level factor that could indirectly connect to board demand tied to memory production lines.

06

Bear factors

Swing to operating loss and margin erosion

The company posted an operating loss in the KRW 3.6 billion range in 2025, and its net loss widened to the KRW 5.9 billion range. Operating margin declined for two consecutive years, from 5.27% in 2023 to 0.95% in 2024 and then to -8.32% in 2025. Sales also fell from the KRW 47.6 billion range in 2023 to the KRW 43.8 billion range in 2025.

Weakening end-market demand

In the 2024 annual results, Bluetop's sales decline was attributed to lower electric vehicle demand and reduced investment by three major telecom carriers. If both end markets weaken at the same time, the recovery in PCB volumes could be delayed. The unclear timing of a rebound remains a negative factor.

Balance sheet strain and funding needs

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were in the KRW 35.7 billion range, well above total equity in the KRW 6.5 billion range. Investing cash outflows reached the KRW 6.4 billion range, larger than the operating cash outflow.

The third-party share placement decided in early November 2026 reads as a step to supplement operating funds from outside sources.

07

Risk factors

Macro

After Democrats won the U.S. midterm elections, concerns over AI data center regulation grew, and domestic chip and power equipment stocks fell sharply, according to news reports. The sell-off spread across semiconductor materials, parts, and equipment makers, which can also affect small PCB companies such as Bluetop. Possible delays in subsidy disbursement tied to policy changes have also been raised.

Industry

Chinese memory makers have been rapidly increasing R&D headcount and investment, which some analysts say could reshape competition in the commodity memory market. The large capex plans of Samsung Electronics and SK hynix also face medium-term uncertainty.

How exposed Bluetop is to these shifts depends on the concentration of its customers and the speed of its product transitions.

Liquidity and financing

As a KONEX-listed company, Bluetop has small trading volume and high share price volatility, trading between KRW 5,310 and KRW 15,830 over the 52 weeks to October 27, 2026. Liabilities well exceed equity, and cash outflows continued in both operating and investing activities. Because the third-party placement increases the share count, it also acts as a source of per-share dilution.

08

Overall view

Bluetop is a KONEX-listed PCB maker where the question of profitability recovery has become the central issue after a combination of lower 2025 sales and a swing to operating loss.

Operating margin declined for two consecutive years through 2024 and 2025, and the high-leverage balance sheet and investing cash outflows appeared alongside it. Positive factors include the expansion of the low-dielectric PCB lineup and the continued positive cash operating profit.

Capex expansion across the semiconductor sector is an indirect demand factor, but the path to Bluetop sales depends on its customer mix. Negative factors include slower electric vehicle and telecom infrastructure demand and macro-level concerns such as U.S. AI data center regulation.

Beyond funding operations, the third-party share placement remains a factor that changes the company's financing capacity. With a market capitalization of KRW 36.9 billion, the stock is also subject to high price volatility.
