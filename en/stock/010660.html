Hwacheon Machinery: Core Profitability Is the Key Test

02

Business structure

Hwacheon Machinery is a machine tool maker founded in 1975 that mainly produces CNC lathes, machining centers and general-purpose machine tools for domestic and overseas markets. Its business splits broadly into machine tools and automotive parts, which include processing of engine cylinder blocks.

Segment weights differ by source: JOBKOREA put automotive parts at 59%, CNC machine tools at 32% and general-purpose machine tools at 9% as of March 2026, while Alphasquare, without stating a date, showed CNC machine tools at 41.7%, automotive parts at 39.1% and general-purpose machine tools at 19.2%.

Both sources agree that automotive parts account for a large share. The machine tool unit also distributes CNC machines supplied by its affiliate Hwacheon Machine Tool to the domestic market.

In the first quarter of 2026, the machine tool unit posted sales of KRW 18.8 billion and an operating loss of KRW 2 billion, while the automotive parts unit posted sales of KRW 27.3 billion and an operating loss of KRW 3 billion, according to a Dealsite report dated June 17, 2026.

Industry commentary points to a narrow vertical-type machining center lineup of 44 models against only 5 horizontal models, which limits the company's standing as a full-line machine tool maker.

The company supplies casting parts for robots to overseas robot makers including Japan's FANUC and has introduced the high-productivity VESTA-1100 machining center.

Its March 2024 shareholder meeting added military supply manufacturing, 3D printers and software development to the business purpose, but the first-quarter 2026 report showed no military supply sales so far.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 189.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 161.9 billion in 2023 (down 14.7%), then rose to KRW 232.4 billion in 2024 (up 43.6%), the largest year in the 2022–2025 period. Revenue was KRW 214.5 billion in 2025, down 7.7%.

Operating profit moved from KRW 1.28 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 3.04 billion in 2023, a swing from profit into loss, and then returned to operating profit of KRW 2.03 billion in 2024. Operating profit fell 88.8% to KRW 228 million in 2025.

The company cited lower sales and a higher cost ratio as the main reasons for the decline, and also pointed to foreign-currency valuation losses from a weaker won. Profit attributable to owners of the parent reached KRW 5.92 billion in 2024, the largest year in the period, and was KRW 2.76 billion in 2025, down 53.3%.

Operating cash flow was KRW 20.2 billion in 2024, well above that year's parent-owned net income, and turned negative at -KRW 1.84 billion in 2025. On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 53.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 244 million.

The fourth quarter of 2025 saw revenue of KRW 47.8 billion (down 11.4% from the prior quarter) and an operating loss of KRW 1.11 billion. The first quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 46.2 billion (down 3.2% quarter on quarter) and an operating loss of KRW 604 million, marking the low point of the recent cycle.

The second quarter of 2026 lifted revenue to KRW 56.9 billion (up 23.0%) and returned to operating profit of KRW 905 million.

The third quarter of 2026 brought revenue of KRW 61.7 billion (up 8.5% quarter on quarter and 14.5% year on year), but an operating loss of KRW 274 million, a swing into loss from a year earlier.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent rose to KRW 1.13 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 2.38 billion in the second quarter, before a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 507 million in the third quarter.

05

Industry analysis

Hwacheon Machinery's earnings have tracked the cycle of its end markets, according to much of the industry commentary. Dealsite noted that the company posted operating profit in only six of the 11 years since 2015.

Industry observers say demand for mold and precision machining, once a strength, has weakened, and that its product lines are only loosely tied to demand from large automotive customers.

Commentary also suggests that its machines have lost price appeal relative to performance, and that it needs to differentiate on price, delivery and service.

Fnguide, a financial data provider, describes growing demand for large equipment from the expansion of IT and consumer electronics, and says the company is growing into a maker of special-purpose machines by combining machine tool manufacturing know-how with parts processing.

In Alphasquare's sector comparison on an annual basis, Hwacheon Machinery's operating margin was above the machinery sector average, and its debt ratio was well below the sector average. The same source says the company keeps a top-tier share of the domestic machine tool market.

Because the company combines machine tools with casting for robots and engine parts for automakers, its earnings can swing more when demand for machine tools and automotive parts moves in different directions.

06

Outlook

Specific medium-term numerical targets from the company are not widely reported. Some business-level trends are documented, however.

A press report on the first quarter of 2026 noted that automotive parts sales edged up while that unit's operating loss widened, and whether this segment returns to profit is a central variable for the group's recovery.

For the machine tool unit, commentary that its horizontal machining center lineup is thin means that broadening the product mix could affect its competitiveness in winning orders.

On new businesses, the first-quarter 2026 report showed no military supply sales, the company has described its software business as still in solution development, and 3D printer development has so far contributed little revenue.

On technology, the company has been developing AI-based smart machine tools under a government R&D project. In casting, it is expanding robot casting parts supply to raise the export share.

The next checkpoints are whether operating profit returns to a profitable trend in the fourth quarter of 2026 and the full year, and how the 2026 annual report breaks out profit by automotive parts and machine tools. Whether new businesses start generating revenue is a related point to track.

08

Bull factors

Balance sheet capacity and low leverage

Total equity was KRW 141.8 billion and total liabilities were KRW 33.2 billion at the end of 2025, a debt ratio of 23.4%. The debt ratio stayed between 21.8% and 25.4% over 2022 to 2025. Operating cash flow of KRW 20.2 billion in 2024 exceeded that year's parent-owned net income. This balance sheet can cushion investment in equipment or new businesses.

Quarterly recovery in the first half of 2026

After a low point in the first quarter of 2026, second-quarter revenue rose 23.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 56.9 billion, with operating profit of KRW 905 million. Revenue increased for two consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026.

Profit attributable to owners rose to KRW 1.13 billion in the first quarter and KRW 2.38 billion in the second. If this trend carries over to better profit in automotive parts, it would support a recovery case.

Expansion into casting and robot components

The company is widening its casting business by supplying robot casting parts to overseas robot makers including Japan's FANUC. It plans to raise its export share through automotive and robot castings.

The launch of the high-productivity VESTA-1100 machining center is a means of strengthening the machine tool unit's product competitiveness. AI-based smart machine tool development under a government R&D project is cited as a medium-term factor.

09

Bear factors

Thin operating margins persisting over time

The operating margin was 0.7% in 2022, -1.9% in 2023, 0.9% in 2024 and 0.1% in 2025, staying below 1% in every year. The company pointed to lower sales and a higher cost ratio. Even if sales recover, limited profit contribution would remain a bearish point unless the cost structure improves.

Losses in both units in early 2026

In the first quarter of 2026, both the machine tool unit and the automotive parts unit posted operating losses, according to a Dealsite report. The automotive parts unit's loss was reported to have widened. The third quarter of 2026 brought a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 507 million. With both units in loss, recovery in one unit alone would be unlikely to lift overall profitability.

Weakening product lineup and price appeal

Industry commentary notes that the company has 44 vertical machining center models against only 5 horizontal models, which weakens its standing as a full-line machine tool maker. Some observers also say its machines have lost price appeal relative to performance.

With mold and precision machining demand shrinking and few product lines tied to large automotive customers, a lack of clear differentiation in price, delivery or service is a bearish factor for order wins.

10

Risk factors

Customer and business concentration

Automotive parts focus on processing engine cylinder blocks, so earnings are sensitive to changes in production and parts demand from automotive customers. JOBKOREA puts automotive parts at 59% of the mix as of March 2026.

Commentary also suggests that a vertical-focused machine tool lineup leaves limited room to diversify customers. When demand weakens in one business, the other may not be able to absorb the shortfall.

Execution risk in new businesses

The company added military supply manufacturing, 3D printers and software development to its business purpose at its March 2024 shareholder meeting, but these contribute little revenue.

Its first-quarter 2026 report said the military supply business had no hiring of specialists, no project activity and no recorded sales. The software business is described as still in solution development.

If new businesses take time to reach revenue, they will not offset the weakness in the core business in the meantime.

Cost ratio and currency risk

For the 2025 decline in operating profit, the company cited lower sales and a higher cost ratio. A foreign-currency valuation loss from a weaker won was also a factor that year. If higher material and processing costs cannot be passed on to prices, cost pressure could keep eroding profit. Currency-driven valuation swings are another item that can move quarterly results.

12

Overall view

Hwacheon Machinery returned to operating profit in the second quarter of 2026, but the third quarter brought an operating loss of KRW 274 million and a swing back into loss. Its operating margin stayed below 1% in every year from 2022 to 2025, and operating profit fell to KRW 228 million in 2025.

A debt ratio of 23.4% and total equity of KRW 141.8 billion give it a sound financial base, which is a bullish factor.

On the other side, both the automotive parts and machine tool units posted losses in the first quarter of 2026, and commentary continues to point to a thin horizontal machining center lineup and weaker price appeal.

Robot casting supply, the VESTA-1100 launch and AI-based smart machine tool development are medium-term factors, but their revenue contribution is still small. The new businesses in military supply, 3D printers and software have so far contributed little revenue.

The priority items to track are whether operating profit returns to a profitable trend in the fourth quarter of 2026 and the full year, and how segment profit is reported in the 2026 annual report.
