02

Business structure

SGC Energy runs its business in three segments: power and energy, construction and real estate, and glass. The power and energy segment sells steam and electricity, and renewable energy certificates (RECs) and carbon emission allowances add to its revenue mix.

The company says it has expanded its use of domestically sourced unused forest biomass to manage fuel price swings. Power segment revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 247.2 billion, up 18.7% year on year, and steam sales rose 16.8% to KRW 124.9 billion, according to company figures.

A steam pricing strategy linked to fuel cost increases supported that growth. The construction and real estate segment, led by subsidiary SGC E&C, posted third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 267.8 billion, down 18.4%, as geopolitical instability in the Middle East delayed some plant work.

Its order book is concentrated in large overseas projects centered on Saudi Arabia and in domestic high-value industries such as semiconductors, pharmaceuticals and bio, as well as public and development projects.

The glass segment sells products such as Glasslock food containers and door glass for drum washing machines; third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 72.9 billion, down 5.9%, and the segment's operating loss widened to KRW 5.3 billion.

Power generation drove the group's operating profit gain in the third quarter, while construction held up through cost control. The company is pursuing AI data centers as a new growth axis, and its first 60MW project has already broken ground.

In power, competition overlaps with district energy operators such as Korea District Heating; in construction, it overlaps with domestic and overseas plant engineering firms.

04

Earnings analysis

On a full-year basis disclosed in DART filings, revenue went from KRW 2.82 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.02 trillion in 2023, the largest year in the period, before falling 22.1% to KRW 2.35 trillion in 2024, the smallest year. Revenue rose 4.5% to KRW 2.46 trillion in 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 209.4 billion in 2022, the largest year, then fell 49.0% to KRW 106.8 billion in 2023 before rising 77.5% to KRW 189.6 billion in 2024. In 2025 operating profit dropped 49.5% to KRW 95.8 billion, the smallest year in the period.

The operating margin was 7.4% in 2022, 3.5% in 2023, 8.1% in 2024 and 3.9% in 2025, so profitability moved with margins more than with sales volume.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 112.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 42.1 billion in 2023, rose 54.3% to KRW 65 billion in 2024, and turned into a KRW 61.4 billion net loss in 2025. Net profit including non-controlling interests was a KRW 94.8 billion net loss in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 345.7 billion in 2022, -KRW 33.3 billion in 2023, KRW 94.1 billion in 2024 and -KRW 32.7 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue peaked at KRW 624.8 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and then declined for three straight quarters, to KRW 611.5 billion, KRW 577 billion and KRW 575.6 billion.

Operating profit was KRW 25.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 18.8 billion in the fourth quarter, then rose to KRW 35.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 44.9 billion in the second and KRW 39.9 billion in the third, up 56.8% year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was a KRW 43.7 billion loss in the fourth quarter of 2025, turned to a KRW 3.17 billion profit in the first quarter of 2026, and rose to KRW 8.02 billion and KRW 8.73 billion in the next two quarters.

The last four quarters together produced a KRW 23.8 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders.

According to company explanations, the third-quarter 2026 profit gain came from power generation, where steam pricing linked to fuel costs, higher SMP and stronger carbon allowance prices all helped, while construction and glass were weaker.

05

Industry analysis

The system marginal price (SMP) for electricity is the key variable behind the power segment's selling prices. According to a securities firm, the average SMP was 107 won in the first quarter of 2026 and climbed to 119 won in April.

The same analysis linked higher oil prices from the Middle East conflict to the rise in SMP. According to the company, the average SMP in the third quarter of 2026 was 133 won per kWh, up 13% from the previous quarter, and an SMP increase is expected in the fourth quarter on seasonal power demand.

Carbon allowance prices are another key variable. The average allowance price in the third quarter was KRW 27,549 per ton, up 40% from the prior quarter and 204% from a year earlier. In the renewable energy certificate (REC) market, cumulative supply in 2026 reached 3.5 million RECs, up 17% year on year.

Securities analysts expect REC issuance and revenue to keep rising through 2027 on a higher REC weighting. In glass, one analysis points to weaker beverage and liquor consumption. Construction depends on overseas plant markets, which face Middle East geopolitical risk and the order cycle.

Within the energy and power sector, SGC Energy's differentiation has been cited as its strengthened biomass fuel base and REC revenue structure.

06

Outlook

The company set its 2026 full-year operating profit guidance at KRW 142 billion at the start of the year, and cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 of KRW 120.5 billion equals about 85% of that target, according to company announcements.

For the fourth quarter, the company expects an SMP increase on higher oil and LNG prices and seasonal power demand, and it sees limited cost pressure from a stable exchange rate.

It also expects carbon allowance prices to stay near 30,000 won per ton in the fourth quarter and plans to carry surplus allowances forward to realize profit in 2027. Hana Securities set a target price of KRW 68,000 in a report published on November 9, 2026.

The first 60MW data center project has broken ground, with power receipt targeted for June 2027 and commercial operation for January 2028. The second project is under discussion with several operators, will be sized to confirmed demand, and is expected to get a detailed business plan in the first half of 2027.

Securities analysts see the shift to district electricity business from 2027 possibly changing the power sales price structure. The company says its construction segment should benefit as overseas projects enter construction and orders concentrate in the second half.

The glass segment aims to improve results by widening overseas distribution channels and launching new products.

08

Bull factors

Power segment profit recovery

Power and energy segment operating profit reached KRW 33.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 147.8% year on year, according to company figures. Steam sales rose 16.8% over the same period, and higher SMP supported profitability.

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 rose 56.5% to KRW 120.5 billion. The company says fuel diversification helped it manage costs.

AI data center project underway

The first 60MW project has already broken ground, with power receipt targeted for June 2027 and commercial operation for January 2028. The second project is under discussion with several operators and is expected to get a detailed plan in the first half of 2027.

The company has named AI data centers as its next growth axis. A securities firm reported that the plan contemplates phased expansion up to 300MW.

Carbon allowance and REC revenue gains

The average carbon allowance price in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 27,549 per ton, up 204% from a year earlier. Cumulative REC supply in 2026 reached 3.5 million RECs, up 17% year on year. The company plans to carry surplus allowances forward to realize profit in 2027. Securities analysts also expect a higher REC weighting to lift issuance.

Bear factors

Swing to a 2025 loss

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 61.4 billion in 2025, a swing from profit, and net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 94.8 billion. The operating margin fell to 3.9% in 2025 from 8.1% in 2024. The fourth-quarter 2025 loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 43.7 billion.

Rising balance-sheet burden

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 339.2% at the end of 2025 from 286.9% in 2024, and total liabilities reached KRW 2.58 trillion. Operating cash flow was negative in two years, at -KRW 33.3 billion in 2023 and -KRW 32.7 billion in 2025. The debt load remains large relative to the company's earnings capacity.

Construction and glass weakness

Construction and real estate revenue in the third quarter of 2026 fell 18.4% to KRW 267.8 billion, as geopolitical instability in the Middle East delayed some plant work. The glass segment's operating loss widened to KRW 5.3 billion. Power segment profit offset this, but the gap between segments is wide.

10

Risk factors

Regulation and policy

The timing of the switch to district electricity business and changes to REC weighting could reshape the power segment's revenue structure. Securities analysts see the 2027-era transition possibly lifting sales prices. If the policy timetable slips, the expected earnings improvement would also be delayed.

Capacity expansion and new business execution

The first data center project targets power receipt in June 2027 and commercial operation in January 2028. The second project is still at the stage of sizing to confirmed demand, and its lease terms have not been disclosed. If construction or customer contracts fall behind plan, the timing of earnings could slip.

Commodities, FX and allowance prices

SMP moves with oil and LNG prices, while carbon allowances and bituminous coal and wood pellet fuel costs drive margins. If the expected fourth-quarter SMP increase fails to materialize or allowance prices reverse, the power segment's profit recovery could be smaller. The company's assumption is that a stable exchange rate limits cost increases.

Overall view

SGC Energy swung to a KRW 61.4 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in 2025, but in 2026 the power segment's recovery lifted quarterly operating profit to KRW 35.7 billion, KRW 44.9 billion and KRW 39.9 billion. Third-quarter operating profit rose 56.8% year on year.

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 of KRW 120.5 billion, per company announcements, equals about 85% of the KRW 142 billion full-year guidance. Operating profit rose 77.5% in 2024 before falling back to KRW 95.8 billion in 2025, so earnings have been volatile.

On the other hand, revenue declined for three straight quarters from the first quarter of 2026, and the construction and real estate and glass segments stayed weak. On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio of 339.2% and operating cash flow of -KRW 32.7 billion at the end of 2025 are the main burdens.

The growth axis is AI data centers, with the first 60MW project under construction and commercial operation targeted for January 2028. Hana Securities set a target price of KRW 68,000 in a report dated November 9, 2026.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual report and the second data center plan in the first half of 2027.
