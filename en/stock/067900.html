Shipping and Waste Units: Falling Profit and a Q3 2026 Net Loss

02

Business structure

Y-Entecco was founded in 1990 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2005, and it runs four business divisions: environmental services, marine transportation, golf course operation and ready-mixed concrete (WiseReport, data as of June 1, 2026).

The environmental services unit collects and transports industrial waste generated in the Yeosu National Industrial Complex and handles intermediate treatment by incineration and final disposal by landfill.

The company is reported to operate an 8th landfill site of about 1.7 million cubic meters, which supports operations through 2039, and its location next to the Yeosu industrial complex is cited as the basis of its share in the Honam region.

Its marine business runs tanker vessels that carry petrochemical products mainly between China and other Asian ports, and a 2023 research report counted eight tankers at that time. An NH Investment & Securities report from 2024 said the company planned to add five chemical carriers.

Jobkorea's business breakdown shows marine transportation as the largest segment at about 66% of sales, followed by intermediate treatment at about 13%, final disposal at about 8% and ready-mixed concrete at about 8%, but the reference date of that data is not clearly stated, so the divisional sales in the business report should be used for confirmation.

Ready-mixed concrete sales move with regional apartment and industrial-complex construction, while golf revenue fell after the pandemic boom faded. Structurally, marine transportation and waste treatment are the two main drivers of profit, while concrete and golf are more sensitive to economic cycles.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed basis, annual revenue was KRW 123.8 billion in 2022, KRW 121.4 billion in 2023 (down 1.9% year on year), and KRW 113.8 billion in 2024 (down 6.3%), before returning to KRW 117 billion in 2025 (up 2.8%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for revenue and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 33.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 32.1 billion in 2023 (down 4.1%), KRW 30.3 billion in 2024 (down 5.7%) and KRW 26.1 billion in 2025 (down 13.9%), and it declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

In 2025 revenue rose 2.8% yet operating profit fell 13.9%, so the higher sales did not carry through to profit. The operating margin moved from 27.1% in 2022 to 26.5% in 2023 and 26.6% in 2024, then to 22.3% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 24.6 billion in 2022, KRW 24.1 billion in 2023 (down 2.3%), KRW 31.3 billion in 2024 (up 30.1%) and KRW 30.4 billion in 2025 (down 2.8%).

In 2024 this figure rose sharply even though operating profit fell, which suggests non-operating items contributed, and the components should be checked in the notes to the annual report.

Net profit including non-controlling interests is recorded at KRW 24.6 billion, KRW 24.1 billion, KRW 31.3 billion and KRW 30.4 billion for the same years.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 29.2 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 30.6 billion in Q4 2025 (up 5.0% quarter on quarter), KRW 28.9 billion in Q1 2026 (down 5.6%), KRW 34.1 billion in Q2 2026 (up 18.0%) and KRW 31.2 billion in Q3 2026 (down 8.6% quarter on quarter, up 7.0% year on year).

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 6.38 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 6.03 billion in Q4 2025, fell to KRW 4.21 billion in Q1 2026, recovered to KRW 6.37 billion in Q2 2026, and dropped again to KRW 3.06 billion in Q3 2026 (down 52.1% year on year).

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned into a net loss of KRW 5.55 billion in Q3 2026 from a profit of KRW 4.69 billion in Q3 2025. The Q4 2025 figure of KRW 14.3 billion was well above that quarter's operating profit of KRW 6.03 billion.

The sum of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, is KRW 19.7 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Waste treatment requires permits and a specific site, so new entry is restricted.

A 2020 technical analysis report expected operators with incineration and landfill facilities to benefit as waste management regulations tightened, and it noted that new waste treatment facilities had stalled under high entry barriers while waste generation kept rising with economic growth.

That structure favors companies that already hold facilities. Y-Entecco's location next to the Yeosu National Industrial Complex is cited as a strength because generation sites and treatment facilities are close together.

That analysis dates from 2020, so current waste volumes and incineration prices should be checked against the business reports and industry statistics. Marine transportation depends on oil prices and petrochemical cargo volumes.

According to the company's Q1 2026 earnings commentary on WiseReport, lower oil prices lifted cargo volumes and demand for storage vessels, and tanker market conditions were favorable.

An NH Investment & Securities report from 2024 noted that the marine business earned a gross margin of around 20%, roughly double the level of the 2010s. Ready-mixed concrete is tied to regional construction activity and follows a different cycle from shipping or waste.

Golf revenue declined after demand from the pandemic period faded. In competition, the waste unit is compared on regional share and treatment capacity, and the marine unit on fleet size and route allocation capability.

06

Outlook

The confirmed outlook drivers fall into three groups: marine transportation, waste treatment and financial capacity.

In marine transportation, tanker market conditions were favorable in Q1 2026, and an NH Investment & Securities report from 2024 expected revenue growth of more than 10% in 2025 on the back of five additional chemical carriers.

Whether that expectation was reflected in the 2025 profit decline should be checked against the divisional figures in the annual business report.

In waste treatment, the company secured permits for incineration capacity of 141.6 tons per day as of a 2023 report, and the expansion facility was targeted to start construction in the first half of 2024 and begin operating in the second half of 2025.

The planned expansion would more than double capacity to 266.4 tons per day. Whether the facility is operating now should be confirmed from the facility section of the 2026 annual report. The company's own profile also refers to an ongoing incinerator expansion.

Ready-mixed concrete and golf both showed revenue declines as of Q3 2024, so a recovery in those units should be confirmed from divisional sales in the next periodic reports.

On the financial side, total assets at year-end 2025 were KRW 500.3 billion, total liabilities KRW 153.6 billion and total equity KRW 346.7 billion (Financial Junction, consolidated), and operating cash flow was KRW 35.3 billion in 2025.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 full-year preliminary results, the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report.

08

Bull factors

Shipping market supports earnings

The Q1 2026 earnings commentary on WiseReport said lower oil prices lifted cargo volumes and storage vessel demand, keeping tanker market conditions favorable. An NH Investment & Securities report from 2024 put the marine gross margin at around 20%, double the 2010s level. Marine transportation is counted as the largest segment by sales.

Waste capacity expansion and landfill headroom

Incineration capacity was 141.6 tons per day in a 2023 report, and the planned expansion would lift it to 266.4 tons per day. The 8th landfill site of about 1.7 million cubic meters, which supports operations through 2039, is cited as evidence of landfill headroom. A 2020 analysis argued that high entry barriers keep existing facilities scarce.

Financial capacity and steady operating cash flow

Total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 346.7 billion, and the debt ratio was 44.3%, only a modest rise from 40.7% in 2022. Operating cash flow was KRW 33.9 billion in 2022, KRW 30.3 billion in 2023, KRW 38.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 35.3 billion in 2025, staying in the KRW 30 billion range. Cash generation held steady even as operating profit declined.

09

Bear factors

Falling operating profit and margin

Operating profit declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and the operating margin fell to 22.3% in 2025 from 27.1% in 2022. In 2025 revenue rose 2.8% while operating profit fell 13.9%. The pattern shows sales growth that did not translate into higher profit.

Turn to a loss in Q3 2026

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.55 billion in Q3 2026, compared with a KRW 4.69 billion profit in Q3 2025. Operating profit that quarter was KRW 3.06 billion, down 52.1% year on year.

Quarterly operating profit fell below half of the KRW 6.37 billion recorded in Q2 2026, showing high volatility.

Shipping weight and cyclical exposure

Marine transportation being the largest segment means earnings are exposed to freight rates and cargo volumes. A Q3 2024 analysis showed declines in golf and waste revenue, and ready-mixed concrete fell with regional construction activity. A weakness in one unit is hard to offset through another under this mix.

10

Risk factors

Shipping market and freight rate swings

Tanker market conditions move with oil prices and cargo volumes. The favorable conditions seen in Q1 2026 are not guaranteed to continue in later quarters. If the market weakens, the marine contribution to profit shrinks, and the large share of this segment can widen earnings swings.

Expansion timing and permits

Incinerator expansion was targeted to start operating in the second half of 2025 according to a 2023 report. Delays or changes in permit conditions would push back the benefit of higher capacity. Changes in waste management rules would also affect the operating environment.

Earnings quality and non-operating swings

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 31.3 billion in 2024 even as operating profit fell, and the Q4 2025 figure of KRW 14.3 billion was well above that quarter's operating profit of KRW 6.03 billion.

Non-operating items can move net profit sharply, so they should be separated from core operating strength. In Q3 2026, operating profit of KRW 3.06 billion coexisted with a net loss of KRW 5.55 billion.

Overall view

Y-Entecco is a composite company whose core businesses are marine transportation and waste treatment.

On a DART-filed basis, revenue recovered to KRW 117 billion in 2025 from a low of KRW 113.8 billion in 2024, but operating profit fell for three consecutive years from KRW 33.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 26.1 billion in 2025, and the operating margin dropped to 22.3%.

Quarterly operating profit recovered to KRW 6.37 billion in Q2 2026 before falling to KRW 3.06 billion in Q3 2026, and the company posted a net loss of KRW 5.55 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

Positive factors are favorable tanker market conditions in Q1 2026, the planned expansion of incineration capacity and financial capacity with total equity of KRW 346.7 billion.

Negative factors are the declining operating profit trend, exposure to freight rates through the shipping weight and the Q3 2026 loss along with non-operating volatility.

Valuation is summarized by the decline in PER and PBR over time and a price-to-book ratio below 1 times, and it should be read alongside the earnings trend. The next checkpoints are the full-year 2026 preliminary results in early 2027, the 2026 annual report after March 2027 and the Q1 2027 report in May 2027.
