02

Business structure

Finecircuit is a printed circuit board (PCB) maker founded in 1991 that mainly produces double-sided and single-sided PCBs. As of the end of the third quarter of 2022, double-sided PCBs accounted for 88.64% of sales and single-sided PCBs for 9.48%.

Single-sided boards are used in home appliances such as refrigerators and washing machines, while double-sided boards serve large appliances and small office electronics.

Capacity in 2022 stood at 360,000 sheets for double-sided and 600,000 sheets for single-sided boards, and a second plant was completed in October 2022 with automated lines.

Major customers were presented as global companies including Samsung Electronics, and a 2022 news report noted that sales had steadily grown since the company was registered as a Tier 1 supplier to Samsung Electronics in 2011.

Based on 2020 data, the domestic single- and double-sided PCB market was about KRW 650 billion, with Finecircuit's share presented as 8.8%. After listing, the company said it would pursue multilayer PCBs (MLB) of eight layers or fewer for premium appliances and automotive electronics.

A corporate information service puts the PCB segment at 99.7% of business divisions, which makes it effectively a single-business PCB company. Because the products have a commodity-board character, cost control is likely to decide profitability. Price swings in copper foil, the core raw material, also feed directly into margins.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 64.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 69.2 billion in 2023 (up 7.5% year on year) and KRW 85.9 billion in 2024 (up 24.1%), before falling 4.8% to KRW 81.8 billion in 2025. Of the 2022–2025 period, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 4.12 billion in 2022, KRW 4.32 billion in 2023 (up 4.9%) and KRW 4.51 billion in 2024 (up 4.6%), but the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.49 billion in 2025. The operating margin declined from 6.4% in 2022 to 6.2% in 2023 and 5.3% in 2024, and reached -1.8% in 2025.

The controlling-shareholder net loss for 2025 was KRW 3.98 billion, and the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 3.77 billion.

In a February 2026 disclosure report, the company attributed the pressure to higher copper prices, a non-cash rise in selling and administrative expenses from bad-debt allowances, and non-cash costs related to convertible bonds.

By quarter, the operating loss was KRW 1.14 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 474 million in the fourth quarter, before turning to an operating profit of KRW 216 million in the first quarter of 2026.

The second quarter of 2026 brought revenue of KRW 20.4 billion (up 22.7% from the prior quarter) and an operating profit of KRW 1.41 billion (up 554.9%).

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue reached KRW 20 billion (down 2.1% from the prior quarter but up 18.0% year on year), yet the company posted an operating loss of KRW 2.98 billion, and the controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 3.56 billion was larger than a year earlier.

The third-quarter 2026 quarterly report showed cost of sales exceeding revenue, which produced a gross loss, and cumulative January–September 2026 revenue of KRW 56.9 billion fell 14.7% year on year.

The controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 through third quarter of 2026) totaled KRW 1.6 billion, with profitable and loss-making quarters alternating.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for PCBs is shaped by two forces moving together: the newer expansion of AI and data centers and traditional demand from home appliances.

Finecircuit's main product is double-sided PCBs, which differ in product tier from high-layer server boards, so whether AI server demand translates into its sales should be checked through its revenue trend.

A corporate profile states that growth in AI, IoT and data center industries is expected to advance the PCB industry, and the company says it is working to expand the home appliance market through major customers and global companies.

Copper prices are a core variable in PCB costs, and the company cited higher copper prices as a background to the swing to an operating loss in 2025. The domestic single- and double-sided PCB market was presented at about KRW 650 billion based on 2020 data, with Finecircuit's share at 8.8%.

Because the market figure dates from 2020, it is hard to compare directly with the current share. In 2022 the company said it would enter the automotive MLB business, but the 2025 disclosed results contain no segment revenue, so its contribution from automotive electronics cannot be measured.

The pace of recovery in appliance demand and the pace of AI-related board demand currently point in different directions, so the cycle position is not clear.

Outlook

Revenue of KRW 20.4 billion and an operating profit of KRW 1.41 billion in the second quarter of 2026 show that profitability is achievable in some quarters, while the operating loss of KRW 2.98 billion in the third quarter shows that cost burden remains a major variable.

The KRW 10 billion convertible bond decided on April 2, 2026 is an unsecured private placement maturing on April 10, 2029, set with a 0% coupon and a 1% yield to maturity.

The stated use of proceeds is acquiring securities of other companies, but the specific purpose was listed as undetermined at issuance, and the maximum acquisition amount was disclosed as KRW 3 billion. The issuer's early redemption right becomes exercisable 24 months after issuance, and the call right after 12 months.

At the regular shareholders' meeting on March 27, 2026, a cash dividend was approved, with total dividends of about KRW 2 billion.

At the same meeting, the proposal to appoint an auditor remained pending because the quorum was not met, and the company said the current auditor will continue to perform duties until a new one is appointed at the next shareholders' meeting.

Cumulative January–September 2026 revenue declined year on year, so whether full-year revenue recovers depends on the size of fourth-quarter sales. In the 2026 annual report, the key checkpoint is where the full-year operating margin moves from -1.8% in 2025.

Bull factors

Sales recovery

The first quarter of 2026 brought an operating profit of KRW 216 million, so part of the sales recovery reached profit. Whether annual revenue can again exceed the KRW 85.9 billion of 2024 is the checkpoint for scale.

Directional shift in profitability

Controlling-shareholder net income turned positive at KRW 510 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, and continued at KRW 521 million in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 931 million in the second quarter.

The second quarter of 2026 also produced an operating profit of KRW 1.41 billion, showing profitability at the operating level. Whether these profits last must be judged after the wider third-quarter loss.

Lower leverage and cash dividends

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 119.3% in 2023 to 88.9% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 43.5 billion, of which KRW 37.9 billion belonged to controlling shareholders. A cash dividend was approved at the regular shareholders' meeting in March 2026, with total payouts of about KRW 2 billion. Whether dividends continue once profits stabilize is the next checkpoint.

The company cited higher copper prices as a main reason for the 2025 swing to an operating loss. If raw material costs stay elevated, higher sales may still struggle to translate into profit.

Financing and governance burdens

At the March 2026 regular shareholders' meeting, the auditor appointment failed for lack of a quorum. If the bonds convert into shares, existing shareholders' stake could be diluted.

Risk factors

Raw material (copper) prices

Copper foil is a core raw material for PCBs, and the company cited higher copper prices as a source of cost pressure in 2025. If raw material costs cannot be passed on to selling prices, operating margins fall even when sales hold. Cost of sales exceeding revenue in the third quarter of 2026 is consistent with this risk materializing.

Customer and appliance demand concentration

The main double-sided PCB product is closely tied to the appliance industry, and major customers were presented as global companies including Samsung Electronics in a 2022 report. A reduction in customer orders would translate directly into larger swings in sales.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose year on year, but cumulative sales through September fell, so order flow needs to be checked quarter by quarter.

Non-cash charges and receivable collection

The company said its 2025 results reflected non-cash selling and administrative expenses from bad-debt allowances and non-cash costs tied to convertible bonds. Bad-debt provisions depend on accounting judgments about collectability, so cost recognition may continue until actual collection flows are confirmed. Operating cash flow was KRW 2.82 billion in 2025, up from KRW 1.15 billion in 2024 but still small.

Overall view

Finecircuit's revenue grew from KRW 64.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 85.9 billion in 2024 but fell to KRW 81.8 billion in 2025, when the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.49 billion.

Operating profit in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 1.41 billion in the second quarter showed a recovery phase, but the operating loss widened to KRW 2.98 billion in the third quarter. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 20 billion rose 18.0% year on year, yet cumulative January–September revenue fell 14.7%.

The two sets of factors currently carry similar weight, and which one prevails will become clearer through the 2026 annual report and 2027 quarterly results.
