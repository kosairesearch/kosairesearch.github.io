Fine Circuit Pivots Beyond Appliance PCB Toward AI and Dental

As Fine Circuit expands beyond its appliance-PCB-centric revenue base into AI data center PCBs and dental zirconia, the company has moved from a 2025 annual operating loss to two consecutive quarters of operating profit in the first half of 2026.

02

Business structure

Founded in 1991, Fine Circuit is a rigid printed circuit board (PCB) manufacturer whose long-standing cash cow has been double-sided and single-sided PCBs used mainly in home appliances such as refrigerators, air conditioners, and washing machines.

Its primary customer is Samsung Electronics, which the company states accounts for the majority of its sales, alongside other customers including LG Electronics, Winia Daewoo, Coway, and Kyungdong Navien.

The company has recently secured new customers such as Cuckoo while also pursuing expanded volume with existing customer LG Electronics.

In 2023, Fine Circuit acquired Uandc International, the domestic market leader in dental zirconia blocks for dental laboratories, entering the dental materials business; this subsidiary's share of consolidated revenue has grown to roughly 22%.

Since 2024, Uandc International has also expanded into distributing general dental materials and manages an export network spanning more than 30 countries through participation in overseas trade shows in Dubai and Germany.

The company's most recent strategic focus is entry into the AI data center PCB market, with management stating it is actively pursuing sales activity to make related revenue visible.

The rigid PCB industry has been characterized as an oligopolistic market built on domestic production's delivery responsiveness even amid low-cost competition from Chinese manufacturers, and Fine Circuit has maintained a stable position within this structure for appliance products.

As a result, Fine Circuit's business is being reorganized around three pillars: appliance-oriented rigid PCBs, dental zirconia, and the newly pursued AI data center PCB segment.

04

Earnings analysis

Fine Circuit's revenue rose for three consecutive years, from KRW 64.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 69.2 billion in 2023 and KRW 85.9 billion in 2024, before declining 4.8% to KRW 81.8 billion in 2025 as growth stalled.

The change in profitability was more pronounced: operating profit steadily improved from KRW 4.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.3 billion in 2023 and KRW 4.5 billion in 2024, then swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.5 billion in 2025, with net income attributable to owners also flipping from a KRW 4.5 billion profit in 2024 to a KRW 4.0 billion loss in 2025.

The quarterly pattern shows a clear loss phase, with Q3 2025 revenue falling to KRW 16.9 billion and the operating loss widening to KRW 1.1 billion, the largest in the recent window, while the net loss attributable to owners reached KRW 1.9 billion.

The loss narrowed in Q4 2025 to revenue of KRW 15.0 billion and an operating loss of KRW 0.5 billion, before the company returned to quarterly operating profit in Q1 2026 with revenue of KRW 16.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 0.2 billion.

In Q2 2026, revenue rebounded to KRW 20.4 billion and operating profit expanded to KRW 1.4 billion, extending the profitable streak to two consecutive quarters.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio stayed elevated at 119.3% in 2023 and 108.0% in 2024 before easing to 88.9% in 2025, still above the 60.0% level seen in 2022 but improving from its 2024 peak.

Operating cash flow fell sharply from KRW 11.9 billion in 2023 to KRW 1.1 billion in 2024, then recovered to KRW 2.8 billion in 2025, suggesting cash generation held up relatively better than the swings in reported earnings.

Overall, 2025 was a year in which weaker demand for appliance PCBs combined with early-stage costs from new businesses to sharply erode profitability, while 2026 has shown sequential quarterly improvement consistent with a bottoming pattern.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic single- and double-sided rigid PCB market has long been characterized as an oligopoly maintained by delivery-responsive domestic production despite low-cost competition from Chinese manufacturers.

Fine Circuit's core downstream market, the appliance industry, is sensitive to consumer spending cycles and new product launch timing, and the weak 2025 results appear linked to softer appliance demand.

In the broader PCB industry, however, expanding investment in AI servers and data centers has emerged as a new growth axis, with GPU/ASIC-based AI server investment, switch transitions, and shorter server replacement cycles cited as factors driving demand for high-density PCBs.

Market research estimates put the data center and AI server PCB market at roughly USD 9.71 billion in 2026, growing at a compound annual rate of 5.67% through 2031.

Data center PCBs require high layer counts and high reliability, making them a relatively higher-margin segment, which is part of the rationale behind Fine Circuit's effort to move beyond its low-value-add, appliance-centered structure.

Meanwhile, in the dental zirconia market, subsidiary Uandc International maintains the leading domestic market share and is expanding its overseas sales footprint through an export network spanning more than 30 countries.

Some peers within the broader PCB industry have seen earnings improve on expanded server and memory module volumes, meaning the outcome of Fine Circuit's AI PCB market entry will likely serve as a gauge of its relative position within the industry.

06

Outlook

The company has stated plans for capacity expansion in 2026 to address increased appliance-related volume and new customer demand, aligned with recently secured customers such as Cuckoo and efforts to expand volume with LG Electronics.

The AI data center PCB business remains at an early stage without substantial revenue contribution yet, with management stating it is actively pursuing sales activity to make related revenue visible.

On the dental side, Uandc International exhibited new products and zirconia blocks at AEEDC Dubai 2026 in January 2026, building brand recognition in the Middle East through three consecutive years of participation.

Uandc International has also stated plans to attend the IDS trade show in Germany in 2027, the world's largest dental exhibition, aiming to expand its distribution network into Germany and other Western European markets.

Management has indicated that a shift from the low-value-add, appliance-centered structure toward higher-value industries could support earnings improvement if realized.

However, the specific timing and scale of revenue contribution from the new AI PCB entry and capacity expansion have not yet been formally confirmed, warranting monitoring through future quarterly disclosures.

In the near term, whether the two consecutive quarters of operating profit seen in the first half of 2026 continue into the second half is considered the most important point to watch.

12

Overall view

Fine Circuit has long relied on appliance-oriented rigid PCBs as its cash cow, and 2025 was a difficult year in which revenue fell 4.8% amid softer appliance demand while both operating and net losses were recorded.

However, after bottoming in Q3 2025, results improved for four consecutive quarters, with operating profit posted in both Q1 and Q2 2026, alongside improvement in the debt ratio and operating cash flow.

At the same time, the company has grown dental zirconia subsidiary Uandc International's revenue share to about 22% and is pursuing entry into the AI data center PCB market as part of a broader effort to diversify beyond its appliance-centered structure.

That said, factors requiring balanced consideration include the continued high concentration of sales tied to Samsung Electronics, a confirmed regulatory compliance record in the new business segment, and the uncertainty inherent in the early-stage AI PCB business.

Whether future quarterly profitability continues, when capacity expansion and new customer volumes actually translate into revenue, and how regulatory risk in the new business segment is managed will likely be the key variables in gauging the success of this business transition.

Investment decisions should weigh these bullish and bearish factors comprehensively, with readers reaching their own conclusions.
