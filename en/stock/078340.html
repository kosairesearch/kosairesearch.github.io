Com2us: Testing Whether the Operating Profit Turnaround Holds

Com2us returned to operating profit in Q3 2026 from a year-earlier operating loss, and the next checkpoints are whether new launches keep contributing and what the 2026 annual report shows.

  2. 2

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Com2us develops and publishes mobile games and conducts its game business under the holding company Com2uS Holdings (063080). Its flagship title Summoners War: Sky Arena has been reported as a long-running global hit that drives RPG segment revenue.

The sports segment is led by Com2uS Pro Baseball V26, which set a record for quarterly revenue for the sixth consecutive quarter, according to the company's preliminary results released on November 12, 2026. The MMORPG Zeus, launched on August 26, 2026, was developed by a partner studio and is serviced by Com2us.

Company preliminary data released on the same day also indicated that RPG segment revenue topped KRW 100 billion in Q3 2026. The new pipeline includes Crimson Inferno, aimed mainly at Japan, and Project ES, a large MMORPG developed by a partner studio and published by Com2us.

The console and PC title Gachiakuta: BREAKOUT is scheduled for global release in 2027 on Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 5 and Steam. From Q3 2026, the subsidiary NP (formerly WiseWiz Studio) was deconsolidated, so year-on-year comparisons need to account for that change.

Competition comes from listed game companies such as Netmarble and Krafton, as well as mid-sized RPG developers in overlapping genres, and Com2us is responding through IP-based launches and genre diversification.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 717.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 739.6 billion in 2023, a 3.1% increase over the prior year, then fell 6.2% to KRW 693.9 billion in 2024 and edged up 0.4% to KRW 696.4 billion in 2025. Among the years from 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest and 2024 the smallest.

Operating loss was KRW 16.7 billion in 2022 and widened to KRW 33.2 billion in 2023, a continued loss with a larger deficit, before operating profit of KRW 6.13 billion in 2024 marked a turnaround to profit.

However, 2025 operating profit fell 57.0% to KRW 2.63 billion, with operating margin at 0.9% in 2024 and 0.4% in 2025.

Net income attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 33.4 billion in 2022 and KRW 39.8 billion in 2023, a 19.2% increase, then turned into a loss of KRW 107.8 billion in 2024 and returned to a profit of KRW 36.5 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests differs from this figure; it was a net loss of KRW 152 billion in 2024 and net income of KRW 9.36 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow went from KRW 15.5 billion in 2024 to negative KRW 39.2 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit of KRW 19.2 billion in Q4 2025 followed an operating loss of KRW 19.6 billion in Q3 2025, and after a low of KRW 5.14 billion in Q1 2026 it rose to KRW 7.16 billion in Q2 and KRW 20.2 billion in Q3, two consecutive quarterly increases.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 148.7 billion fell 5.3% from the prior quarter and 7.8% from a year earlier, so whether the margin gain came from cost structure will need to be checked in the annual report notes.

The company and press cited the early performance of the MMORPG launched at the end of August as a driver of the turnaround. Net income attributable to owners of the parent totaled KRW 49.7 billion over the last four quarters, and the one-off items behind the strong Q4 2025 result should be confirmed in the filings.

05

Industry analysis

In mobile RPG and MMORPG genres, long-running hits and new launches take turns supporting revenue. At the Zeus showcase in August 2026, the company said direct competition in competitive MMORPGs causes fatigue and concentrates rewards among top players, leaving mid and lower-tier users with less content.

In response, Zeus introduces a persona-based matchmaking system that pairs users with rivals of similar strength, along with an AI mode for automated grinding. On monetization, the company said core growth items such as weapons, armor and skills will not be sold directly for cash.

In sports, the baseball lineup has repeatedly set quarterly revenue records, showing steady demand within the genre.

The console and PC expansion extends to new releases based on animation IP, and the company's materials noted that the original Tougen Anki anime ranked fifth in Netflix's global non-English TV series category.

When comparing peers, the key metrics are launch cadence, ownership of proprietary IP, and overseas revenue share.

06

Outlook

The nearest checkpoint is the Japan launch of Crimson Inferno, a turn-based RPG the company plans to roll out globally with Japan as the focus, and press reports flagged a Japan release within 2026. Whether the anime fandomin Japan converts into game revenue will shape the overseas revenue share.

Project ES is a PC-centered, cross-platform MMORPG built on Unreal Engine 5 that emphasizes territory control and guild and alliance competition; a partner studio develops it and Com2us publishes, and its launch timing should be confirmed through company announcements.

The console and PC title Gachiakuta: BREAKOUT is scheduled for release in 2027 on Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 5 and Steam, with development updates to be posted through official channels. The company said Zeus will add or vary content every season, so user retention after the initial launch surge is the next focus.

Affiliate Com2uS Holdings is also widening its lineup, including the global release of Ares: The Iron Vanguard on September 15, 2026. Annual figures will be finalized in the 2026 annual report, and operating margin and operating cash flow shown there will be the next checks.

Bull factors

Consecutive quarterly operating profit gains

Operating profit rose for two consecutive quarters from a low of KRW 5.14 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 7.16 billion in Q2 and KRW 20.2 billion in Q3. Q3 marked a swing to profit from a year-earlier loss.

Operating profit had also turned positive in Q4 2025 at KRW 19.2 billion, so the quarterly recovery has now lasted more than one quarter.

Early signs from the new launch pipeline

The MMORPG Zeus, launched on August 26, 2026, was reported to have reached No. 1 grossing on both major app marketplaces right after release. The company cited the new title as a factor behind the Q3 turnaround.

Crimson Inferno is slated for a Japan launch within 2026, and the console and PC title Gachiakuta: BREAKOUT is scheduled for 2027.

Improving balance sheet trend

The debt-to-equity ratio declined from 56.8% in 2024 to 51.1% in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 1.07 trillion and total liabilities were KRW 546.5 billion. The capital base is large relative to recent earnings, which means financial room was preserved through the loss period.

Bear factors

Weak annual profitability

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 2.63 billion, down 57.0% from the prior year, and operating margin reached only 0.4%. In 2024 the company also swung to a net loss of KRW 107.8 billion attributable to owners of the parent.

Annual profit remains small even after the turnaround, so whether quarterly profits carry through to the full year still needs confirmation.

Revenue decline and quarterly volatility

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 148.7 billion fell 7.8% from a year earlier. Q1 2026 operating profit dropped 73.2% from the prior quarter, and net income attributable to owners of the parent was only KRW 2.45 billion in Q2 2026. The quarter-to-quarter swings are large, so a trend cannot be judged from one or two profitable quarters.

Cash flow and dependence on new titles

Operating cash flow turned negative KRW 39.2 billion in 2025 from positive KRW 15.5 billion in 2024. The company explained that its Q3 net loss reflected foreign exchange gains and losses from currency moves along with discontinued operations.

Because earnings lean on new launches, quarterly profit could fall again if the early performance of those titles fades.

10

Risk factors

Sustainability of new-launch revenue

The key question is whether Zeus's early success converts into sustained revenue and user retention. Competitive MMORPGs repeatedly face player fatigue and concentration of rewards among top users, so update cadence and season operations will determine how well churn is contained.

The monetization design that limits pay-to-win pressure is an advantage for long-term revenue, but it still needs to be proven.

Currency swings and FX gains or losses

Q3 consolidated net income was affected by foreign exchange gains and losses tied to currency moves. Game companies with large overseas revenue can see quarterly results swing with exchange rates.

The divergence between net income attributable to owners of the parent and total net income also calls for reviewing both FX effects and the non-controlling interest structure.

Changes in consolidation scope and accounting effects

From Q3 2026 the subsidiary NP was deconsolidated, which changed the basis for comparing revenue and profit. Discontinued operations also flowed into quarterly net results as a one-off factor. Judging underlying earnings requires separating the operating trend from consolidation changes and one-time items.

Overall view

Com2us saw operating profit fall in 2025, but quarterly operating profit has risen for two consecutive quarters since the Q1 2026 low, and Q3 swung to a profit from a year-earlier operating loss. The company and press cited the MMORPG Zeus, launched at the end of August, as a driver of the recovery.

On the other side, Q3 2026 revenue fell 7.8% from a year earlier, operating cash flow was negative in 2025, and the company posted a net loss attributable to owners of the parent in 2024.

On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio declined to 51.1% in 2025 and the capital base is large, so financial room appears preserved. On the business side, the pipeline of Crimson Inferno's Japan launch within 2026, Project ES and Gachiakuta: BREAKOUT in 2027 is positioned as the next growth leg.

However, the contribution of this pipeline has not yet been proven in quarterly results. The next checks are the 2026 Q4 preliminary results and the operating margin and cash flow in the 2026 annual report. Whether the turnaround lasts depends on sustaining new-launch revenue while narrowing the revenue decline.
