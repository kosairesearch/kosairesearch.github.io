Ecopro: Indonesian Nickel Bet Meets Volatile Earnings

Ecopro returned to an operating profit in 2025 and is widening its supply chain from raw nickel to cathode through a second-phase Indonesian smelter investment, but net income attributable to owners was negative for two straight years and swings sharply from quarter to quarter, so the provisional Q3 2026 results on November 5, 2026 and the BNSI smelter completion schedule are the next things to check.

  1. 1

    On a reported basis, 2025 revenue rose 9.1% year on year to KRW 3.41 trillion and the company swung to an operating profit of KRW 213.8 billion, an operating margin of 6.3%.

  2. 2

    Net loss attributable to owners was KRW 206 billion in 2024 and KRW 149.8 billion in 2025, two consecutive loss years with a narrowing loss. In 2026 the company posted owners' net income of KRW 210.4 billion in Q1 and KRW 50.7 billion in Q2.

  3. 3

    Ecopro and Ecopro BM secured a 39% stake in Indonesia's BNSI nickel smelter and became its largest shareholder, with total investment of about KRW 1.5 trillion. Combined with phase one, annual nickel offtake rights rise to 65,000 tons.

  4. 4

    Ecopro BM's rights offering, which funds the investment, was finalized at KRW 886.1 billion. The shareholder subscription rate announced on October 19, 2026 was 103.25%, so no public offering will be held.

  5. 5

    Q2 2026 operating profit fell 40.4% quarter on quarter to KRW 33.6 billion. The company attributed the drop to lower smelter utilization after heavy rain in Indonesia and said recovery would start in Q3.

02

Business structure

Ecopro is a KOSDAQ-listed holding company that controls battery-materials and environmental affiliates.

The core of consolidated results is listed subsidiary Ecopro BM, which makes high-nickel ternary cathode materials; based on Q2 2026 results announcements, Ecopro BM's consolidated revenue of KRW 576.7 billion was the largest among the affiliates. Precursors are handled by Ecopro Materials.

Ecopro Materials posted Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 178.8 billion and an operating loss of KRW 10.6 billion, and said it plans to begin shipments to a new North American customer in the second half.

Ecopro HN, which makes chemical filters and greenhouse-gas abatement equipment, recorded revenue of KRW 51.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.8 billion in the same quarter, with demand supported by semiconductor customers' capital spending.

The holding company also runs lithium processing and recycling businesses directly, so lithium selling prices and metal prices feed straight into its standalone earnings.

Upstream, it secures nickel feedstock through stakes in four smelters at Indonesia's IMIP park and the MHP smelter Green Eco Nickel, acquired in 2025. Domestic competitors include POSCO Future M and L&F, while Chinese materials makers pushing low-cost LFP are the biggest competitive pressure globally.

Ecopro's main point of difference is a vertically integrated structure that keeps nickel smelting, precursors, cathodes and recycling inside the group.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue rose 28.7% from KRW 5.64 trillion in 2022 to KRW 7.26 trillion in 2023, the largest year in 2022-2025. Revenue then fell 56.9% to KRW 3.13 trillion in 2024, the smallest year in the same period.

Falling metal prices and slowing EV demand hit at the same time: operating profit went from KRW 613.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 298.2 billion in 2023 before turning into an operating loss of KRW 293 billion in 2024.

In 2025 revenue grew 9.1% to KRW 3.41 trillion and the company swung back to an operating profit of KRW 213.8 billion, lifting the operating margin from -9.4% to 6.3%. Net loss attributable to owners, however, was KRW 206 billion in 2024 and KRW 149.8 billion in 2025, two consecutive loss years.

The 2025 net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 105.5 billion, smaller than the owners' loss, which means the outside shareholders' share of subsidiaries was profitable.

By quarter, Q3 2025 operating profit of KRW 149.4 billion was the highest of the last five quarters, while in Q4 2025 revenue fell to KRW 715.1 billion and the company booked an owners' net loss of KRW 190.1 billion.

That quarter included a derivative trading loss disclosed on February 4, 2026 and a write-down at Ecopro Materials on assets tied to a long-term sales contract. Revenue then grew for two consecutive quarters, reaching KRW 818.3 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 821.5 billion in Q2.

Q2 operating profit of KRW 33.6 billion was down 40.4% quarter on quarter but up 106.9% year on year, as higher lithium selling prices partly offset the smelter disruption.

Owners' net income of KRW 210.4 billion in Q1 2026 far exceeded operating profit of KRW 56.4 billion, showing large non-operating effects, and it fell 75.9% to KRW 50.7 billion in Q2. Owners' net income for the last four quarters totals KRW 140.7 billion.

Operating cash flow went from -KRW 518.6 billion in 2022 to positive territory from 2023, reaching KRW 339.1 billion in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio stood at 117.9% at end-2025.

05

Industry analysis

The cathode cycle has moved past its 2024 trough and is partly recovering on rebounding metal prices. Sisa Journal-e reported in June 2026 that lithium and nickel prices had risen roughly 280% and 20%, respectively, over a year.

Rising raw-material prices reduce inventory valuation losses because of the lag in passing costs into selling prices, which helps cathode makers' earnings. On the demand side, however, the mix has shifted against ternary chemistries.

SNE Research estimated that LFP accounted for 59% of global cathode loadings in Q1 2026, ahead of ternary at 41%. Ecopro BM's results also slipped slightly in Q2 2026 on weaker European EV demand, with AI data center and power application demand keeping it profitable.

As the United States designated China and others as prohibited foreign entities and tightened rules on related battery materials, securing nickel with little Chinese capital has itself become a competitive factor. Ecopro's larger BNSI stake, which cuts China's GEM from 55.13% to 21%, is aimed at that requirement.

Domestic rivals are racing to internalize raw materials as well, as POSCO Future M does through its group's South American lithium supply.

06

Outlook

At its Q2 results briefing on August 4, 2026, the company said earnings would recover gradually from Q3 as Indonesian smelter operations normalize. It also said it expects further improvement in Q4, when the IMIP smelters, including Green Eco Nickel, run at full capacity.

Provisional Q3 results are scheduled for disclosure on November 5, 2026. Capacity at the phase-two BNSI smelter is being raised to 90,000 tons a year; two of its three reactors had arrived by July 2026, with the third reported to arrive in September.

According to the company, BNSI could be completed and start trial production as early as end-2026, with full operation in Q2 2027. The holding company plans to invest about USD 490 million (KRW 735 billion at KRW 1,500 per dollar) in the project and had paid in about KRW 277.5 billion by the time of its Q2 results.

It said standalone cash and equivalents were about KRW 600 billion at the end of Q2 and that it has no plans for additional capital raising at the holding-company level. Ecopro BM's rights offering proceeds with payment on October 23, 2026 and listing of the new shares on November 5.

08

Bull factors

A nickel supply chain built for regulation

Once the BNSI phase-two investment is complete, 29,000 tons of existing nickel offtake will be joined by another 36,000 tons. The company says this nickel meets US non-prohibited-foreign-entity requirements.

Linking nickel smelting, precursors and cathodes inside the group could lower costs, the weak point of ternary chemistries, and support bids for North American customers.

Direct exposure to the metal price rebound

The Indonesian smelters have relatively fixed costs while selling prices track LME nickel, so margins widen when prices rise. In Q2 2026, lithium business profit rose despite lower volumes as higher lithium prices were passed through.

On its February 2026 call, the company said Indonesia-related profit could exceed earlier guidance by more than 20% at then-current metal prices.

Better cash flow and a well-subscribed offering

Operating cash flow has been positive for three straight years since 2023, a change from the large outflow in 2022. Ecopro BM's rights offering closed with no unsubscribed shares, at a 103.25% shareholder subscription rate, even as the stock market fell sharply.

Growth in Ecopro HN's environmental business on the back of the semiconductor upturn also gives the group a profit source that does not move with the battery cycle.

09

Bear factors

Quality and volatility of net income

Operating profit has turned positive, but results attributable to owners were losses for two straight years in 2024-2025. Some quarters, such as Q1 2026, saw non-operating items lift net income sharply, which makes the recurring level of earnings hard to judge.

As in Q4 2025, a combination of derivative losses and contract-asset write-downs can turn a quarter into a large net loss.

LFP adoption and weaker European demand

With LFP overtaking ternary chemistries in global cathode loadings, the market base for a high-nickel portfolio is narrowing. Ecopro BM is reviewing LFP cathode investment, but it would face cost competition in a field where Chinese makers lead.

Slower EV demand in Europe, a key market, could stretch the time needed to raise utilization at the Hungary plant.

Heavy investment and financial burden

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 112.0% in 2024 to 117.9% in 2025, and owners' equity was less than half of total equity at end-2025. Ecopro BM's offering shrank from the planned KRW 1.2 trillion to KRW 886.1 billion as the issue price fell.

Until BNSI reaches full operation, the holding company must fund its share from cash on hand and borrowing, so any delay would increase the burden.

10

Risk factors

Operations and costs

Local natural disasters hit results directly, as shown when heavy rain cut smelter utilization and profit in Q2 2026. Ecopro Materials has said about 75% of Indonesia's sulfur supply depends on the Middle East. If geopolitical factors such as the Strait of Hormuz push up sulfuric acid prices, smelting costs rise.

Policy and regulation

The core rationale for the BNSI investment is feedstock that complies with US prohibited-foreign-entity rules. Changes in how those rules are interpreted or applied could force a restructuring of ownership or reduce the expected order benefits.

Local resource policy, including the Indonesian government's limits on new smelter permits, is another variable to keep monitoring.

Governance and capital

The holding company's value relies heavily on its stakes in listed subsidiaries, so their share price moves pass straight through. The listing of new Ecopro BM shares on November 5, 2026 will increase the free float.

The company says it has no additional capital-raising plans, but falling metal prices or rising project costs could reopen the funding question.

  3. Late December 2026

  4. Early February 2027 (expected)

  5. Q2 2027

    Target date for full operation of the BNSI smelter. How quickly utilization ramps up, together with nickel prices, will determine the size of equity-method income and cost savings.

12

Overall view

Ecopro moved past its 2024 operating loss to return to an operating profit in 2025 and stayed profitable at the operating level in the first half of 2026.

Net income attributable to owners, however, was negative in both 2024 and 2025 and swings widely each quarter because of non-operating items, so it is too early to judge the underlying earnings power.

The group's growth case rests on a supply chain from Indonesian nickel smelting through cathodes that is designed to meet US regulatory requirements. The Ecopro BM offering that funds it closed with no unsubscribed shares, but it raised less than planned, and the group's debt-to-equity ratio has risen.

LFP adoption, weaker European demand, local natural disasters and cost pressures could all delay the payoff from this strategy. The provisional Q3 results on November 5, 2026 and the end-2026 BNSI completion schedule are the first tests of the recovery path the company has laid out.
