Ecopro: Own Businesses Grow as Earnings Turn Negative

02

Business structure

Ecopro is the holding company of the Ecopro group, founded in 1998 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2007.

Its main subsidiaries are Ecopro BM, a cathode maker with a 40.8 percent stake, Ecopro Materials, a precursor maker with a 41.2 percent stake, and Ecopro HN, an environmental equipment business with a 31.1 percent stake, as of the May 2026 disclosure, and there is also a separate lithium and recycling subsidiary.

The Fair Trade Commission notified that Ecopro no longer falls under holding-company rules because subsidiary share value fell below 50 percent of total assets as of end-2025.

The holding company's own business centers on metal trading and investments, and press reports showed its quarterly revenue rising in every quarter of 2025, from KRW 43.1 billion in 1Q to KRW 89.6 billion in 4Q. Metal trading revenue alone rose from KRW 33.3 billion to KRW 72.9 billion over the same period.

Holding-company own-business revenue was reported at KRW 78.3 billion in 3Q 2026. Ecopro BM sells to North American and European EV automakers and to ESS operators. Its EV-linked sales fell in 3Q 2026, while power application sales tied to AI data centers grew.

Ecopro BM ranks sixth globally in high-nickel NCM cathode share, and Ecopro Materials is described as having the largest precursor capacity in Korea. The largest shareholder group, led by Lee Dong-chae and 14 others, holds 25.53 percent as of October 21, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed basis, 2022 revenue was KRW 5.64 trillion and operating profit was KRW 613.2 billion, a 10.9 percent operating margin.

In 2023 revenue rose to KRW 7.26 trillion, up 28.7 percent and the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 window, but operating profit fell 51.4 percent to KRW 298.2 billion and the margin dropped to 4.1 percent.

In 2024 revenue fell 56.9 percent to KRW 3.13 trillion, the smallest year in the window, and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 293 billion, turning from profit to loss with a -9.4 percent margin.

That year the attributable net loss was KRW 206 billion and the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 295.4 billion.

In 2025 revenue reached KRW 3.41 trillion, up 9.1 percent, and operating profit turned positive at KRW 213.8 billion, a 6.3 percent margin, but the attributable net loss of KRW 149.8 billion means losses continued with a smaller deficit.

Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 4.49 trillion, of which KRW 1.97 trillion belonged to controlling shareholders, and the debt ratio was 117.9 percent versus 112.0 percent in 2024.

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 149.4 billion in 3Q 2025 to KRW 46.8 billion in 4Q, a 68.7 percent drop, then recovered to KRW 56.4 billion in 1Q 2026. It then declined to KRW 33.6 billion in 2Q and KRW 11.2 billion in 3Q, two consecutive quarterly declines from 2Q 2026 through 3Q 2026.

Revenue in 3Q 2026 was KRW 810.7 billion, down 1.3 percent from the prior quarter. Attributable net income of KRW 210.4 billion in 1Q 2026 far exceeded operating profit of KRW 56.4 billion, which suggests non-operating items had a large effect on that quarter.

The attributable net loss over the last four quarters, from 4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026, totals KRW 14 billion. The company attributed the 3Q slowdown to lower lithium prices, currency swings and destocking in downstream markets, and expects metal price declines and FX effects to continue to weigh in 4Q.

Reports cited a derivative valuation loss of about KRW 116 billion tied to a total return swap as the main driver of the 4Q 2025 net loss, while stating that no actual cash outflow occurred.

05

Industry analysis

Downstream demand is splitting in two directions. Slower growth in North American EV markets and European automakers waiting on model changes reduced EV cathode volumes, while ESS demand linked to AI data-center investment increased.

The company said lower lithium prices, a weaker won and destocking in downstream markets together cut battery materials sales in 3Q 2026. On raw materials, lagged effects from higher metal prices in the first half lifted average selling prices at Ecopro BM, although a stronger won partly offset that gain.

Because destocking was cited as a cause of lower volumes, material demand appears exposed to inventory cycles. Ecopro BM keeps sixth place globally in NCM cathode share, and Ecopro Materials has the largest precursor capacity in Korea.

By contrast, Ecopro HN is adding new semiconductor-related environmental equipment customers, and its 3Q 2026 operating profit rose 386.3 percent from a year earlier, supported by the semiconductor upcycle.

From the holding company's perspective, the battery materials cycle, semiconductor equipment and the holding company's own businesses are moving on different paths.

06

Outlook

The company expects Indonesian smelter utilization, around 80 percent in 3Q 2026, to recover to normal levels in 4Q. The smelters suffered utilization declines from a natural disaster, and after addressing regulatory points and re-inspecting equipment, operations resumed with both lines running.

Ecopro Materials said shipments of precursors to a new North American customer begin in 4Q and moved its equipment investment completion target to the end of 2027.

Ecopro BM is reviewing a new 50,000-ton LFP cathode expansion, and additional NCM capacity in Hungary is under review, according to the 1Q and 3Q 2026 earnings calls.

Ecopro HN said its Micron orders, KRW 93.6 billion for Singapore and KRW 19.8 billion for Idaho, were about 13 percent recognized in revenue by the end of 3Q 2026. Its greenhouse gas reduction segment accounts for roughly 80 percent of backlog at end-3Q and is expected to anchor 4Q revenue.

At the holding-company level, standalone cash and equivalents fell from KRW 604.8 billion at end-2Q to KRW 392.2 billion at end-3Q, while the standalone debt ratio improved from 86 percent to 73 percent. The company said liquidity risk is not large. The next confirmation points are the 4Q 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Steady growth in own businesses

Press reports for 2025 showed holding-company own-business revenue rising every quarter, led by metal trading. Own-business revenue was reported at KRW 78.3 billion in 3Q 2026. The mix is shifting from reliance on subsidiary dividends and equity-method income toward direct earnings. Quarterly swings can still be large given metal price and inventory timing effects.

Indonesian smelter recovery

Smelter utilization was about 80 percent in 3Q 2026, and the company expects a return to normal levels in 4Q. Both production lines are reported to be running. Smelting is cited as a profit base for the holding company's own business alongside raw material prices. Higher utilization could affect own-business revenue and equity-method income from subsidiaries.

ESS and semiconductor demand

Power application sales at Ecopro BM rose 16 percent from the prior quarter, supported by AI data-center expansion that drives ESS demand. Ecopro HN secured Micron orders for its Singapore and Idaho fabs, with about 13 percent recognized in revenue by end-3Q.

Its greenhouse gas reduction segment accounts for roughly 80 percent of backlog. How fast these orders convert into revenue is a key checkpoint for recovery.

Bear factors

Sharp 3Q slowdown and loss swing

Operating profit in 3Q 2026 was KRW 11.2 billion, down 92.5 percent from a year earlier, and the attributable result turned to a KRW 85 billion net loss. Operating profit has declined for two consecutive quarters from 2Q through 3Q 2026. The attributable net loss over the last four quarters totals KRW 14 billion. Whether the recovery continues in 4Q remains to be confirmed.

Weaker North American EV demand

Ecopro BM's EV-related sales fell 34 percent from the prior quarter. Slower North American EV growth and European automakers waiting on model changes both weighed on volumes. Lower cathode volumes pressure subsidiary results and therefore the holding company's equity-method income. Whether ESS demand offsets the EV decline is the central question.

Raw material and FX headwinds

The company cited lower lithium prices and currency volatility as drivers of weaker 3Q profitability. It expects metal price declines and FX effects to continue weighing in 4Q.

Precursor subsidiary Ecopro Materials posted revenue of KRW 181.1 billion and an operating loss of KRW 29 billion in 3Q 2026, with the loss widening. Raw material prices and FX are moving against margins at the same time.

Risk factors

Raw material prices and FX

Lithium and metal prices and the won-dollar exchange rate feed directly into subsidiary sales and margins. In 3Q 2026, lower lithium prices and a weaker won reduced profitability. The reverse lagging effect from input timing could continue into 4Q. Because price moves flow into quarterly results, quarterly earnings volatility remains high.

Funding and derivative valuation

Standalone cash and equivalents fell from KRW 604.8 billion at end-2Q to KRW 392.2 billion at end-3Q, as equity-raising payments and investment outlays overlapped. The company said financial risk is not large, but funding needs could persist if investment spending rises.

A derivative valuation loss tied to a total return swap was cited as a main driver of the 4Q 2025 net loss. Share-price-linked derivative results can move quarterly profit sharply.

Smelter shutdown risk

Indonesian smelters saw utilization fall after a natural disaster, and operations were halted and restarted while addressing regulatory points. At about 80 percent in 3Q 2026, utilization is still below normal levels. A delayed recovery would affect own-business revenue and equity-method income together. Whether utilization returns to normal in 4Q is the nearest checkpoint.

Overall view

Ecopro moved from a 2024 operating loss to a 2025 operating profit, but in 3Q 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 11.2 billion and the attributable result swung to a KRW 85 billion net loss.

The center of gravity of results sits on Ecopro BM's EV demand and raw material conditions such as lithium prices and FX, which is the defining feature of the current setup.

On the other side, recovery in Indonesian smelter utilization, growth in own-business revenue, and demand for ESS and semiconductor environmental equipment are cited as supporting factors.

On the balance sheet, the standalone debt ratio improved to 73 percent at 3Q 2026, but the decline in standalone cash should be checked alongside it. The consolidated debt ratio was 117.9 percent at end-2025, and the attributable net loss over the last four quarters totals KRW 14 billion.

Bullish and bearish factors carry comparable weight, so the next checkpoints are 4Q smelter utilization, new precursor shipments and the 2026 annual report.
