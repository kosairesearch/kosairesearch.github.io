02

Business structure

M2N started with plastic packaging containers for petrochemical products and has built its business around electric-vehicle thermal management and automotive electronic parts.

The company listed on KOSDAQ in 1997, changed its name to M2N in 2020, and expanded its electronics business with the 2023 acquisition of Shinhan Electric. It runs two segments: the drum division, which makes steel drums, and the electronics division.

In the first half of 2026, drum division revenue rose 10% year on year to KRW 18.1 billion and its operating profit rose 35%.

Over the same period, electronics division revenue fell because a key customer adjusted its orders for system-air-conditioner parts, while electric-vehicle parts remained relatively firm as more vehicle models adopted them.

Its PT sensor is described as the core product, and the company is preparing mass production of its next-generation refrigerant control valve (EXV). The company is also developing liquid-cooled thermal management for energy storage systems and data-center thermal management.

A cancer drug development unit was described in the December 2025 corporate summary as running FDA clinical trials. As of end-June 2026 the company held a 13.53% stake in Sillajen-related holdings listed as its largest shareholder position, and it sells system-air-conditioner parts to air-conditioner makers.

Its 2025 annual revenue of KRW 86.5 billion is small compared with large listed component makers in the same sector. The company says its thermal and electronic technology sets it apart from peers.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 28.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 77 billion in 2023, up 170.6% year on year, and to KRW 91.2 billion in 2024, up 18.5%. It then fell 5.1% to KRW 86.5 billion in 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit swung from an operating loss of KRW 4.84 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 3.21 billion in 2023, rose to KRW 3.74 billion in 2024, and fell 26.1% to KRW 2.77 billion in 2025. Operating margin was -17.0% in 2022, 4.2% in 2023, 4.1% in 2024 and 3.2% in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 decline to lower customer demand, which reduced revenue, and higher cost ratios. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.26 billion in 2022, KRW 3.23 billion in 2023, KRW 3.52 billion in 2024, and a net loss of KRW 594 million in 2025.

The 2025 net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 596 million, which is a different figure and should be read separately. Operating cash flow was -KRW 1.67 billion in 2022, KRW 4.71 billion in 2023, KRW 3.4 billion in 2024, and KRW 3.05 billion in 2025.

By quarter, revenue was KRW 21.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 with operating profit of KRW 1.06 billion, then KRW 19.3 billion with an operating loss of KRW 569 million in the fourth quarter.

The first quarter of 2026 recovered to KRW 21.9 billion and KRW 709 million, then operating profit fell for two straight quarters to KRW 659 million in the second quarter and KRW 72.38 million in the third quarter.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 81.12 million in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 4.28 billion in the second quarter, then to a net loss of KRW 1.88 billion in the third quarter.

The company explained the first-half 2026 net income increase with equity-method gains from an added associate stake and associate results. The last four quarters together produced KRW 1.33 billion of profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for the electronics division follows the electric-vehicle market, which one report described as entering a recovery phase, with a stable PT sensor supply and the next-generation EXV cited as possible sources of revenue growth.

Growing data-center capacity and rising demand for efficient thermal management in high-performance computing are areas the company has named as growth targets.

Domestic customers are pushing to localize import-dependent items because of supply-chain instability and higher import costs, which the company expects to support domestic sales. India, with GDP growth above 7% on average and fast-growing home appliance and automotive industries, is described as a key overseas market.

For system-air-conditioner parts, growing overseas air-conditioner demand, especially in India, was cited as a driver of the third quarter 2024 result. Large component makers are numerous in thermal and electronic parts, so M2N competes mainly on technology differentiation in specific product lines.

With 2025 revenue of KRW 86.5 billion, the company's results are sensitive to swings in end-market demand, as the 2025 customer demand decline showed. Whether electric-vehicle demand recovery can offset the order adjustments in system-air-conditioner parts is the key industry question to watch.

06

Outlook

The most concrete business milestone the company has described is preparation for mass production of its next-generation refrigerant control valve (EXV) in the electronics division.

The company is running research on liquid-cooled thermal management for energy storage systems and on data-center thermal management, and says it keeps reviewing related investment opportunities.

Its stated plan is to use its thermal sensor and valve design and manufacturing skills to expand from parts supply into system-level products. For overseas growth, the company has said it will push into India in earnest, using production capacity and a sales network at its Indonesian subsidiary.

A phased expansion into Southeast Asia, including China, was described in a March 2025 disclosure. On shareholder returns, the company cancelled 369,775 common shares from treasury stock in February 2026, equal to 0.87% of issued shares, and said it is reviewing how to handle the remaining treasury shares.

Much of the first-half 2026 equity-method gain came from an added associate stake, so whether that effect is one-off or recurring is an item to check in upcoming filings.

The 2026 annual report, the next periodic filing, is due by the end of March 2027, and it will show the full-year operating margin and the impairment assessment of the associate investment.

08

Bull factors

Steady electric-vehicle parts demand

The company said electric-vehicle parts continued to perform relatively well in the first half of 2026, supported by more vehicle models adopting them. Even as electronics division revenue fell on system-air-conditioner order adjustments, the electric-vehicle parts line did not decline, which supports the bull case.

One report said the electric-vehicle market is entering a recovery phase. Mass production of the next-generation EXV could change the electronics division's revenue mix.

Balance sheet repair and cash flow

In April 2026 the company redeemed and cancelled all 1,851,891 convertible preferred shares using its own funds rather than new financing. As a result, current liabilities fell and the debt ratio was reported at about 25% in the first half of 2026, down from 33.1% at the end of 2025.

Operating cash flow for the first half of 2026 was KRW 5 billion, up 17% year on year. Reduced overhang concerns from the preferred share redemption also count as a financial improvement.

Drum division growth and new business build-out

The company described the drum business as a source of steady cash flow that supports its financial stability. Research is under way to move its thermal technology into liquid-cooled thermal management for energy storage systems and into data-center thermal management. If these areas convert into revenue, the company could reduce the volatility of its electronics-led earnings.

Bear factors

Consecutive declines in core operating profit

Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 2.77 billion, down 26.1% from the prior year. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 72.38 million, down 93.2% from a year earlier, and it has fallen for two consecutive quarters since the second quarter of 2026. Operating margin was 3.2% in 2025, lower than in 2023. If core profitability does not recover, the factors behind the net income gains may prove one-off.

Net income leaning on associate results

First-half 2026 net income rose 485% year on year, while operating profit fell 39.9% over the same period. The company explained the net income increase through equity-method gains from an added associate stake and associate results.

In the third quarter of 2026, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened again to KRW 1.88 billion. Equity-method income moves with associate results, so the path of net income is hard to predict.

Customer order cuts and cost pressure

The company cited lower customer demand, which reduced revenue, and higher cost ratios as the main reasons for the 2025 decline. Revenue for 2025 was KRW 86.5 billion, down 5.1%. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 20.7 billion, down 3.7% from a year earlier.

If the system-air-conditioner order adjustments continue, a recovery in electronics division revenue could be delayed.

10

Risk factors

Governance and major shareholder share pledges

Amended filings for share-pledge agreements tied to a change in the largest shareholder have continued since June 2026, with the latest accepted on September 16, 2026. A share pledge can serve as a funding tool, but a high pledge ratio or frequently changed terms could make control unstable if the pledge is enforced. Press coverage has called for a clear explanation of the reasons for the repeated amendments.

Accounting risk on the associate investment

The auditor issued an unqualified opinion on the 2025 annual report, but the key audit matters included an impairment assessment of the associate investment. Changes in the associate's performance or asset values could affect the company's financial results.

Because equity-method gains contributed heavily to first-half 2026 net income, a weaker associate could shift the net income path quickly.

Working capital and reliance on external funding

Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 3.05 billion, but one analysis reported large cash outflows from investing activities and inflows from financing that turned total cash flow positive. Trade receivables rose slightly to KRW 10.51 billion, and inventory rose 7.8% to KRW 15.14 billion. If working capital keeps growing while revenue falls, cash collection could slow.

12

Overall view

M2N's core operating profit has declined since its 2025 swing to a full-year net loss, and third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 72.38 million. On the other hand, first-half 2026 net income rose sharply on equity-method gains, and the debt ratio fell after the convertible preferred share redemption.

Electric-vehicle parts and the drum division held up in the first half, while system-air-conditioner parts revenue in the electronics division declined.

Repeated amendments to major shareholder share-pledge agreements and accounting uncertainty around the associate investment are the main governance and earnings items to monitor.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.33 billion, showing large quarter-to-quarter swings.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 full-year results and the 2026 annual report, with the recovery of operating margin and the durability of equity-method income as the central questions.
