Report overview

Profit Recovery Led by FC-BGA Turnaround

02

Business structure

Daeduck Electronics is a printed circuit board maker whose core business is semiconductor package substrates.

Its product lines include memory package substrates, flip-chip chip-scale package (FC-CSP) and chip-scale package (CSP) substrates, flip-chip ball grid array (FC-BGA) substrates, and high-layer-count MLB boards. In Q3 2025, reported product mix by revenue was FC-CSP 41%, CSP 27%, FC-BGA 17%, and MLB 15%.

MLB revenue rose 21% in the same quarter on a higher mix of advanced products. FC-BGA revenue declined from the KRW 27 billion range in 2022 to KRW 22 billion in 2023 and KRW 17 billion in 2024, when it accounted for about 2% of total sales.

As automotive and server demand was added, the FC-BGA share rose to 17% in Q3 2025. By geography, domestic sales accounted for 52% in 2025, with China at 22% and the United States at 13%. Reports said MLB shipments are rising for AI, networking and aerospace uses.

In FC-BGA, leading global suppliers such as Ibiden and Unimicron are expanding capacity, but supply for AI servers still lags demand. Daeduck is viewed as holding a multi-substrate portfolio that combines memory package substrates with FC-BGA.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results moved from a peak in 2022 through a trough in 2024 to a recovery in 2025. Revenue went from KRW 1.32 trillion in 2022 to KRW 909.7 billion in 2023 (down 30.9% year on year) and KRW 892.1 billion in 2024 (down 1.9%), before rising to KRW 1.07 trillion in 2025 (up 19.4%).

Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for revenue and 2024 the smallest. Operating profit fell from KRW 232.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 23.7 billion in 2023 (down 89.8%) and KRW 11.3 billion in 2024 (down 52.6%), then recovered to KRW 49.1 billion in 2025 (up 335.7%).

The operating margin dropped from 17.7% in 2022 to 1.3% in 2024 and returned to 4.6% in 2025. Net profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 183.9 billion in 2022, KRW 23.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 47.6 billion in 2025, and net income including non-controlling interests is reported as a separate figure.

Quarterly results started at KRW 286.2 billion of revenue and KRW 24.4 billion of operating profit in Q3 2025, then reached KRW 317.9 billion and KRW 28.9 billion in Q4 2025 (up 11.1% and 18.4% quarter on quarter).

Operating profit then reached KRW 51.3 billion in Q1 2026 (up 77.2% quarter on quarter), KRW 70.3 billion in Q2 (up 37.0%) and KRW 87.9 billion in Q3 (up 259.4% year on year). Operating profit has risen for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, and its growth has far outpaced revenue growth.

Net profit attributable to owners was KRW 57.7 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 54.4 billion in Q3, down 5.7% quarter on quarter, and the last four quarters total KRW 183.2 billion. A one-off cost of more than KRW 10 billion was reported in Q4 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 71.6 billion in 2025, well below KRW 293.6 billion in 2022, so the gap between earnings recovery and cash generation remains.

05

Industry analysis

End demand is coming from AI servers and data centers. As server CPU and AI accelerator shipments rise, demand for the FC-BGA substrates that mount them rises directly, and demand for high-layer MLB boards used in server motherboards and networking equipment rises alongside it.

A Korea Investment & Securities analyst expects the FC-BGA market to enter a supply shortage in 2027. On the supply side, raw materials are a key variable: price increases for glass fiber and copper-clad laminate have begun, and constraints on T-glass, a key input, are seen lasting through 2027.

That is cited as a factor that could support pass-through of price increases to customers. Competitively, Daeduck supplies FC-BGA together with memory substrates and MLB, so it can benefit from expanded server CPU, memory and networking investment across several product lines rather than one.

A Ibiden investment is interpreted as a production restructuring tied to global customer needs and substrate technology roadmaps after 2027. On the memory side, a rise in DRAM content per device is expected to drive memory substrate demand.

In cycle terms, FC-BGA has moved past a period of oversupply and low utilization that produced losses, and many observers see the segment in a recovery phase since reaching breakeven in Q4 2025.

06

Outlook

The next disclosures are the Q4 2026 results and the 2026 annual report, which will fix full-year figures. In an April 2026 report cited by the press, brokers expected FC-BGA utilization to rise from 65% in Q4 2025 to above 90% within the year.

The same coverage said mass production of large-area server-grade substrates for networking would start in the second half of 2026, with new products for a North American customer possibly supplied from Q2 2027.

Early 2026 reports said new FC-BGA facility investment, halted in 2024, could resume in the second half of 2026. In a March 2026 report, Yuanta Securities expected the FC-BGA business to turn profitable this year at a single-digit operating margin.

The same report said growth in revenue would be led by memory substrates on higher DRAM content, with additional profit coming from non-memory substrates such as e-SSD FC-CSP and FC-BGA.

In an early November 2026 report cited by the press, Daishin Securities expected operating profit growth to exceed revenue growth in 2026 and 2027 and expected revenue expansion to lead growth from 2028. These forecasts could be affected by key customers' production plans, product demand and exchange rates.

The next points to watch are whether large-area server substrates actually enter mass production and how utilization develops after the investment program ends in December 2027.

08

Bull factors

Early breakeven in FC-BGA

Analysts said the FC-BGA unit reached breakeven in Q4 2025, one quarter earlier than expected. An April 2026 report projected utilization could climb above 90% within the year. Higher utilization would spread fixed costs and increase operating leverage.

Wider end markets in data centers and autonomous driving

Analysts say FC-BGA applications have broadened from TVs and automotive electronics into data center products. Rising server CPU and AI accelerator shipments directly lift demand for FC-BGA. Supply for autonomous driving chips was reported to begin in Q4 2025.

Richer mix and room for cost pass-through

Analysts argue FC-BGA's larger area and higher layer count can lift unit prices and margins. Even with gold and copper costs rising, a higher share of new-model shipments allows partial pass-through. Operating profit growth outpacing revenue growth fits this mix improvement.

09

Bear factors

Oversupply and utilization risk in FC-BGA

Capacity additions by Ibiden, Unimicron and others keep oversupply concerns alive in FC-BGA. A 2025 report put Daeduck's FC-BGA utilization below 50%, and some saw supply conditions staying tight through the second half of 2026. Low utilization raises depreciation burdens and can weigh on profit.

Dependence on memory package demand and customer plans

A 2025 report said memory package substrates had been hit by slower demand from key customers such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. Customer production plans, product demand and exchange rates can all drive substrate results. Analysts also note the need to confirm that AI investment turns into actual substrate orders and deliveries.

Earnings volatility and cash generation

Net profit attributable to owners fell 5.7% quarter on quarter in Q3 2026. Operating cash flow dropped from KRW 293.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 71.6 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 24.4% in 2024 to 31.3% in 2025, leaving a gap between earnings recovery and cash generation.

10

Risk factors

Raw materials and FX

Some see supply constraints on key inputs such as T-glass lasting through 2027. Rising gold and copper prices can squeeze costs, and the speed of pass-through depends on customer contract terms. Exchange-rate moves can also affect substrate results.

Customer concentration and order visibility

Brokers expect Tesla autonomous-driving chip volumes to feed into revenue from Q4 2025. The larger a single customer program becomes, the more results are exposed to that customer's schedules and design changes. Whether orders convert into mass production and sales still needs confirmation.

Capex and depreciation

The FC-BGA investment program runs from April 2022 to December 2027, and its schedule itself has been a source of volatility, since new facility investment was halted in 2024. A company official said in a 2025 report that adding capacity without sufficient utilization would enlarge losses.

12

Overall view

Daeduck Electronics' earnings structure has shifted quickly since the 2024 trough. On a reported basis, operating profit rose for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, reaching KRW 87.9 billion in Q3 2026. The FC-BGA unit is central to this recovery and is seen as having reached breakeven in Q4 2025.

Positive factors include wider data center and autonomous-driving applications, a richer product mix and room for cost pass-through. Negative factors include FC-BGA oversupply, dependence on customers for memory package demand, raw material constraints and capex burdens.

The decline in operating cash flow since 2022 and the rise in the debt-to-equity ratio to 31.3% in 2025 show the gap between earnings recovery and cash generation.

The current price-to-book ratio sits in a range of substantial premium to net assets, so the share price reflects the market's view on how durable earnings will be. The next points to watch are the Q4 2026 results, the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and the timing of any FC-BGA capacity resumption.
