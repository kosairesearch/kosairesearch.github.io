02

Business structure

Hyungji Elite is an apparel company rooted in the 'Elite' student uniform brand launched in 1969, and its business now spans three areas: student uniforms, workwear (corporate uniforms and consumer sales), and sports merchandising.

In the half-year from July to December 2025, consolidated sales were KRW 88.1 billion, of which sports merchandising accounted for KRW 33.9 billion, while student uniform sales rose 7.71% to KRW 24.8 billion.

Sports merchandising began with baseball merchandise in 2020, with contracts with SSG Landers, Hanwha Eagles and Lotte Giants, and has since expanded to FC Seoul, FC Barcelona, Real Madrid and Busan KCC Egis.

The student uniform business entered China's premium uniform market through the Shanghai Elite joint venture formed in 2016, even as a shrinking school-age population has weighed on the uniform market, and in 2026 it began supplying Japan, the first domestic uniform maker to do so.

Workwear began with corporate uniform supply (B2B) and has widened into consumer sales (B2C), with the brand renewed under the Wilbi Lab name. In January 2026 the subsidiary hyungjiROBOTICS was launched to pursue wearable robots.

The footwear affiliate, in which Hyungji Elite held a 48.16% stake as the second-largest shareholder according to a press report, has been weighed down by a long slump in footwear. The largest shareholder group, which includes related parties, held 23.82% as of September 9, 2026.

Because sports and workwear sit on top of a uniform-centered core, differences in growth speed across units show up directly in the earnings mix.

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated sales for the fiscal year ended June 2026 were KRW 177.8 billion, up 6.7% from the prior year.

Sales started from KRW 94.5 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023, rose 40.5% to KRW 132.7 billion in the year ended June 2024, then grew 25.5% to KRW 166.7 billion in the year ended June 2025, and have increased for three consecutive years through the year ended June 2026, which was the largest year in the period.

Operating profit rose from KRW 2.49 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to KRW 7.01 billion in the year ended June 2024, a 182.0% increase, then fell 5.2% to KRW 6.64 billion in the year ended June 2025 and recovered 26.7% to KRW 8.42 billion in the year ended June 2026.

For the decline in the year ended June 2025, the company attributed the drop to temporary effects of up-front investment in marketing and future growth drivers. The operating margin was 2.6% in the year ended June 2023, 5.3% in 2024, 4.0% in 2025 and 4.7% in 2026, dipping once and then recovering.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 1.29 billion in the year ended June 2025 and back to a profit of KRW 7.43 billion in the year ended June 2026.

The company explained the KRW 1.3 billion net loss in the year ended June 2025, including non-controlling interests, as an accounting effect from fair value losses on convertible bonds tied to a share price rise.

Quarterly, sales were KRW 57.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 6.39 billion in the second quarter of 2025, then KRW 44.6 billion (down 22.4% from the prior quarter) with operating profit of KRW 7.46 billion (up 16.7%) in the third quarter, when net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 6.2 billion, which press coverage described as a record quarter.

The fourth quarter of 2025 posted sales of KRW 43.5 billion and an operating loss of KRW 178 million. The first quarter of 2026 returned to profit with sales of KRW 41.4 billion, operating profit of KRW 1.53 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.69 billion.

The second quarter of 2026 brought sales of KRW 48.3 billion, up 16.7% from the prior quarter but down 15.9% from a year earlier, along with an operating loss of KRW 397 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 542 million.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the third quarter of 2025 to the second quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 7.43 billion.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 12 billion in the year ended June 2023, KRW 3.15 billion in 2024, -KRW 22.9 billion in 2025 and -KRW 11.1 billion in 2026, negative in three of the four years. Total equity at the end of June 2026 was KRW 117.3 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 84.3 billion.

Industry analysis

The student uniform market faces a structural headwind from a shrinking school-age population. Press coverage describes Hyungji Elite shifting weight toward sports merchandise and workwear as uniform demand declines. The student uniform market is described as stagnant because of the declining school-age population.

On the other side, a strong baseball season and rising merchandise demand are cited as drivers of the sports merchandising business.

Overseas, China's premium uniform market is described as having taken root mainly in private and international schools, while Japan's conservative uniform market is described as having been entered successfully with a casual design approach.

Fashion companies more broadly are under earnings pressure from a slowdown in demand, according to press reports.

The domestic footwear market, where the footwear affiliate operates, saw total sales at five major brands fall from KRW 454.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 439.0 billion in 2023 and KRW 391.8 billion in 2024, a second straight annual decline.

Competitively, Hyungji Elite is identified with the first domestic student uniform brand, while its sports merchandise sales move with the expansion of team contracts and team popularity.

Outlook

In announcing results for the fiscal year ended June 2026, the company said its key indicators improved evenly on the back of growth in sports merchandising and a management approach focused on fundamentals.

It also stated its intent to build a basis for sustained growth based on top-line expansion and better profitability. Coverage notes that 2026 features major sporting events, which is expected to extend the results of collaborations with global clubs.

The student uniform business is widening its supply base from China's premium market to Japan, and the company has also outlined plans to expand into the mid- to low-priced public school uniform market.

Workwear is diversifying consumer sales channels alongside corporate supply, so changes in the sales mix are a point to watch in future results.

The robot business is run by hyungjiROBOTICS, which signed a joint development agreement with a Chinese robot company in January 2026 and targets a product unveiling in the second half of the year. Press coverage reports that four of six robot-related trademark applications received registration decisions.

The first point to confirm is whether the operating loss of the second quarter of 2026 extends beyond the third quarter. Clarity on how the company will handle its investment in the footwear affiliate also remains a variable affecting consolidated net profit.

08

Bull factors

Sports merchandise sales expansion

Sports merchandising sales reached KRW 33.9 billion in the half-year from July to December 2025, up 180% from a year earlier. The growth came from baseball merchandise demand and collaborations with football clubs including FC Barcelona and FC Seoul, while student uniform sales also rose 7.71% to KRW 24.8 billion.

The company said it then signed a sponsorship with the professional basketball club Busan KCC Egis. The rapid growth of one pillar of the earnings mix is the main bullish factor.

Operating profit recovery and return to profit

Operating profit for the fiscal year ended June 2026 rose 26.7% to KRW 8.42 billion, and the operating margin improved to 4.7% from 4.0% a year earlier. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned to KRW 7.43 billion, helped largely by the absence of the prior year's convertible bond valuation loss. The debt ratio fell from 107.5% to 71.9%, reducing financial burden.

Overseas supply and new business expansion

The student uniform business gained a foothold in China's premium market through the Shanghai Elite joint venture formed in 2016, and began supplying Japan in 2026.

Workwear is widening its consumer sales channels, and the subsidiary hyungjiROBOTICS is preparing wearable robot products with a target unveiling in the second half. Whether the new businesses take root as additional pillars is the bullish factor to watch.

09

Bear factors

Return to losses in the second quarter of 2026

The second quarter of 2026 posted an operating loss of KRW 397 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 542 million. Sales for the quarter of KRW 48.3 billion rose from the prior quarter but fell 15.9% from a year earlier.

Following the KRW 178 million operating loss in the fourth quarter of 2025, the renewed loss means that only a short run of quarterly results is available to show whether profit can be sustained.

Structural shrinkage of the student uniform market

A shrinking school-age population that reduces uniform demand itself is cited as a downside risk to the core business. The student uniform business is offsetting this with supply to China and Japan, but the domestic uniform market remains stagnant.

When operating profit fell 5.2% in the year ended June 2025, the company attributed the decline to up-front investment, but if the core business keeps shrinking, the key question is how much new businesses such as sports can offset.

Affiliate impairment and negative operating cash flow

The footwear affiliate's carrying value was reduced from KRW 10.1 billion to KRW 6.9 billion, with an impairment loss in the KRW 3 billion range recognized at once. The company described the impairment as a book loss with no cash outflow, but equity-method results flow directly into consolidated net profit.

Operating cash flow remained negative at -KRW 11.1 billion in the year ended June 2026, and some assessments see the recovery of the affiliate as uncertain given the shrinking footwear market.

10

Risk factors

Earnings volatility and seasonality

Uniforms and sportswear are seasonal, with sales following the school calendar and public purchasing tender schedules. Sports merchandise demand moves with team performance and popularity. A quarter like the second quarter of 2026, with lower sales and an operating loss, could disrupt the trend of annual improvement.

Affiliate exposure and governance

The footwear affiliate's results are reflected in consolidated net profit through the equity method, which could dilute gains in the core business. The largest shareholder group holds 23.82%, and there is pressure on the company to clarify how it will treat its investment in the affiliate.

After the 2022 transfer of a 51% stake in the affiliate to a related shareholder, its management influence has become weaker than before.

Convertible bond valuation and cash flow

The net loss for the year ended June 2025 reflected fair value losses on convertible bonds as the share price rose. Because valuation gains and losses move with the share price, net profit can swing independently of the core business. Operating cash flow was negative in three of the four fiscal years reviewed.

Overall view

The fiscal year ended June 2026 for Hyungji Elite brought sales of KRW 177.8 billion, operating profit of KRW 8.42 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.43 billion, with both top line and earnings growing, and a return to profit as the prior year's valuation loss fell away.

Sports merchandising sits at the center of the growth, while the student uniform business complements it by widening supply to China and Japan.

On the other side, the second quarter of 2026 returned to an operating loss and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, and operating cash flow was negative in three of the four fiscal years reviewed. The shrinking school-age population and the weak footwear affiliate remain downside factors.

The debt ratio fell from 107.5% in the year ended June 2025 to 71.9% in the year ended June 2026, but the durability of earnings still depends on quarterly results from sports contracts. The stock fell sharply over the past year, and its price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x.

The next points to confirm are the operating profit trend in the quarterly report covering July to September 2026, the timing of the robot product unveiling, and how the affiliate investment will be treated.
