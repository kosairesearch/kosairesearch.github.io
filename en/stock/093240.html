Sports Growth Drives Profit Recovery; Listing Status Is the Variable

02

Business structure

Hyungji Elite runs three business units: the Elite school uniform brand, a B2B unit covering corporate and group uniforms and workwear, and a sports merchandising unit that supplies uniforms and goods to professional clubs.

According to press reports, sports merchandising accounted for 39% of sales in the year ended June 2026, B2B for 33.5%, and Elite for 27.3%. The same reports put sports sales at KRW 68.5 billion, up 41.3% from a year earlier, and Elite sales at KRW 48.0 billion, up 2.7%. B2B sales fell 15.2% to KRW 59.0 billion.

In the second half of 2025, according to a semiannual report cited by the press, sports merchandising sales rose 179.27% to KRW 33.8 billion, while student uniform sales grew 7.71% to KRW 24.8 billion. Sports partners include SSG Landers, Hanwha Eagles, Lotte Giants, FC Seoul, Hanwha Life Esports, and FC Barcelona.

Among the four major school uniform makers, Elite is reported to hold the largest market share. The workwear business has been rebranded under a new name. Overseas, the company set up a joint venture in China with a unit of a Chinese fashion group in 2016 and agreed to supply uniforms to two Japanese schools.

The student uniform market is stagnating as the school-age population shrinks, and the company is widening its revenue base through sports and new businesses.

04

Earnings analysis

Sales in the reported annual figures rose from KRW 94.5 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to KRW 132.7 billion in the year ended June 2024 (up 40.5% year on year), KRW 166.7 billion in the year ended June 2025 (up 25.5%), and KRW 177.8 billion in the year ended June 2026 (up 6.7%).

Sales have risen for three consecutive fiscal years from the year ended June 2024 through the year ended June 2026, and the year ended June 2026 was the largest year in the period from June 2023 to June 2026.

Operating profit went from KRW 2.49 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 7.01 billion in the year ended June 2024 (up 182.0%), then fell 5.2% to KRW 6.64 billion in the year ended June 2025, before rising 26.7% to KRW 8.42 billion in the year ended June 2026.

Operating margins were 2.6%, 5.3%, 4.0%, and 4.7% across the four years, so margin gains lagged sales growth.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2 billion in the year ended June 2023 and KRW 6.37 billion in the year ended June 2024, turned to a loss of KRW 1.29 billion in the year ended June 2025, and returned to a profit of KRW 7.43 billion in the year ended June 2026.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 7.43 billion in the latest year. According to press reports, the cost of sales ratio fell from 74.1% to 69.2% in the year ended June 2026.

On a quarterly basis, the September 2025 quarter posted sales of KRW 44.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 7.46 billion, then the December 2025 quarter swung to an operating loss of KRW 178 million.

The March 2026 quarter returned to operating profit of KRW 1.53 billion, and the June 2026 quarter posted an operating loss of KRW 397 million.

The September 2026 quarter recorded sales of KRW 37.5 billion, down 22.4% from the prior quarter and 15.9% from a year earlier, and operating profit of KRW 4.37 billion, down 41.4% from a year earlier. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 5.44 billion.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 12 billion in the year ended June 2023, KRW 3.15 billion in the year ended June 2024, -KRW 22.9 billion in the year ended June 2025, and -KRW 11.1 billion in the year ended June 2026. The debt ratio fell from 107.5% in the year ended June 2025 to 71.9% in the year ended June 2026.

05

Industry analysis

The school uniform market is described as stagnating because the school-age population is shrinking. In early 2026 the president called uniform purchase costs excessive, and the Ministry of Education said it would discuss the issue in stages with government agencies and provincial education offices.

Press reports said efforts were emerging to revise bidding structures and distribution practices as well. The company raised uniform prices, but reports also said its profitability had been declining. Sports merchandise demand is described as steady because it follows professional baseball and soccer league schedules.

Large sports events are seen as an opportunity for merchandise demand, while competition in sports goods is reported to be intensifying.

Consumer demand for exercise apparel also showed signs of growth, with an online platform reporting sharp increases in sweat-resistant wear and yoga apparel transactions in early January 2026.

Compared with its past dependence on uniforms, the company's revenue is described as less exposed to seasonality and to the shrinking uniform market, a view that rests on the rising share of sports sales since the second half of 2025. Elite is reported to hold the largest share among the four major school uniform makers.

Outlook

The company has said it is pursuing annual sports sales of KRW 100 billion, according to a report in early 2026, and it plans to use major sports events such as the Winter Olympics and the World Cup as catalysts. A sports fashion store opened at Lotte World Mall in Jamsil, Seoul, in April 2026.

In Japan, the company entered the market through supply contracts with schools including Kyoto International Secondary School, and in China its student uniform brand won a category award for a second consecutive year.

In the robotics business, a subsidiary named Hyungji Robotics was launched in January 2026, and the company signed an agreement with a Chinese robot maker to co-develop senior wearable robots. The company has said it is preparing the robot business with a target of the second half of 2026.

Press reports also said four of six trademark applications for the robotics brand had been approved for registration. At its September 2026 results announcement, the company said it would raise profitability in existing businesses while identifying new growth drivers, and it cited sustainable profit growth as its aim.

Because third-quarter 2026 sales fell from a year earlier, the next checkpoint is whether sports sales keep growing on a quarterly basis after the June 2026 quarter. The actual launch timing and revenue contribution of the robot wearables are also items to confirm.

Bull factors

Sports merchandising growth

Sports sales reached KRW 68.5 billion in the year ended June 2026, up 41.3%, and sports merchandising sales rose 179.27% in the second half of 2025 to KRW 33.8 billion. Partnerships with baseball, soccer, and esports clubs widened the supply base for uniforms, goods, and licensed products.

Steady demand tied to league schedules is seen as reducing revenue swings compared with the concentrated school-term pattern of uniforms.

Financial structure and profit recovery

Operating profit rose 26.7% to KRW 8.42 billion in the year ended June 2026, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned to KRW 7.43 billion. The debt ratio fell to 71.9% from 107.5% a year earlier.

According to press reports, the standalone current ratio rose to 200% from 171%, and standalone retained earnings grew 35.3% to KRW 226 billion.

Overseas school uniform expansion

The Elite school uniform brand won a Chinese student uniform category award for a second consecutive year at a 2026 awards ceremony. Press reports said a supply contract with two Japanese schools made it the first domestic school uniform brand to enter Japan.

The company has said it will raise its share in China and expand step by step into Japan and other Asian markets.

Cash generation has not kept pace with the return to profit, which is read as a financial burden. Total liabilities stood at KRW 84.3 billion at the end of June 2026.

No shareholder returns and listing pressure

Risk factors

Listing regulation risk

The company was designated a managed stock on August 12, 2026, and the reason was amended on September 2. On October 30, 2026, it filed a provisional injunction with the Seoul Southern District Court to suspend the designation.

The exchange temporarily suspended delisting procedures tied to market capitalization shortfall. The company decided on a five-to-one share consolidation to maintain its listing.

School uniform market and policy risk

The student uniform market is stagnating as the school-age population declines. In early 2026 the president said uniform costs were excessive, and the Ministry of Education said it would discuss the issue in stages. Press reports said pressure to cut prices could extend to bidding structures and distribution practices. Uniforms are a shrinking share of sales but remain a core revenue source.

Financial and group ownership risk

Operating cash flow was still negative at -KRW 11.1 billion in the year ended June 2026. Hyungji Global, the de facto controlling shareholder, sold 1,006,295 shares off-exchange in January 2026, and its stake was reported at 6.89%. Press reports in September 2026 also covered a credit rating downgrade at a group affiliate.

Overall view
