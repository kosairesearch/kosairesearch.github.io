02

Business structure

Hyungji Elite is a school uniform company that has marketed its Elite brand, which the company describes as Korea's first school uniform brand launched in 1969, and it listed on the KOSPI market in 2009. The business is split into three units: school uniforms, B2B uniforms and apparel, and sports merchandising.

According to a Topdaily report dated October 7, 2026, sports merchandising accounted for 39% of sales for the year ended June 2026, B2B for 33.5%, and the Elite uniform business for 27.3%.

On the same basis, sports sales reached KRW 68.5 billion, up 41.3%, Elite sales were KRW 48.0 billion, up 2.7%, and B2B sales were KRW 59.0 billion, down 15.2%. These segment figures come from press compilations rather than the periodic filing tables, so they do not exactly sum to consolidated sales.

The sports unit operates under the WILL BE PLAY brand and holds partnerships with professional baseball clubs including SSG Landers, Hanwha Eagles and Lotte Giants, the K League club FC Seoul, the esports team Hanwha Life Esports, and FC Barcelona.

The company opened its first permanent offline store at Lotte World Mall in Jamsil, Seoul, in April 2026. In the school uniform business, the company renewed its brand identity and widened its product line to include gym wear, casual wear, bags and indoor shoes.

Its Chinese joint venture, Shanghai Elite, serves the premium uniform market, and in 2026 the company signed supply contracts with schools in Kyoto and Osaka in Japan. The domestic school uniform market is viewed as stagnating because of a shrinking school-age population. Market share data for the sports unit was not confirmed in this review.

04

Earnings analysis

On the periodic filing basis, annual sales rose from KRW 94.5 billion for the year ended June 2023 to KRW 132.7 billion for the year ended June 2024 (up 40.5%), KRW 166.7 billion for the year ended June 2025 (up 25.5%), and KRW 177.8 billion for the year ended June 2026 (up 6.7%).

Sales have increased for three consecutive years from the year ended June 2024 through the year ended June 2026, and the year ended June 2026 is the largest year in the June 2023 to June 2026 window.

Operating profit went from KRW 2.49 billion for the year ended June 2023 to KRW 7.01 billion for the year ended June 2024 (up 182.0%), then fell to KRW 6.64 billion for the year ended June 2025 (down 5.2%), and recovered to KRW 8.42 billion for the year ended June 2026 (up 26.7%).

The operating margin was 2.6%, 5.3%, 4.0% and 4.7% across the four years, peaking in the year ended June 2024. The year ended June 2025 turned into a loss, with controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.29 billion and net loss of KRW 1.3 billion.

The company attributed the profit decline that year to temporary marketing and upfront investment, and press reports tied the net loss to fair-value losses on convertible bonds as the share price rose.

The year ended June 2026 returned to positive controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 7.43 billion, which press reports linked to the disappearance of the prior year's one-off valuation loss. Reported finance costs also fell from KRW 10.3 billion to KRW 4.3 billion according to the same coverage.

Operating cash flow was KRW -12 billion, KRW 3.15 billion, -KRW 22.9 billion and -KRW 11.1 billion for the four years ended June 2023 through June 2026, so three of the four years were negative.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 57.5 billion in the quarter ended December 2025 to KRW 44.6 billion in the quarter ended March 2026, KRW 43.5 billion, KRW 41.4 billion and then KRW 48.3 billion in the quarter ended June 2026, which was 16.7% higher than the prior quarter and 15.9% lower than a year earlier.

Quarterly operating profit moved from KRW 6.39 billion and KRW 7.46 billion in the two quarters ended December 2025 and March 2026 respectively, to an operating loss of KRW 178 million in the quarter ended December 2025, KRW 1.53 billion in the quarter ended March 2026, and an operating loss of KRW 397 million in the quarter ended June 2026.

Over the last four calendar quarters from the quarter ended September 2025 through June 2026, controlling-shareholder net income totaled KRW 7.43 billion, with the quarter ended September 2025 contributing the largest share at KRW 6.2 billion.

The cause of the controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 542 million in the quarter ended June 2026 was not confirmed in this review.

05

Industry analysis

Press coverage describes the school uniform market as stagnating because of a shrinking school-age population, a backdrop that has pushed the company to put more weight on new businesses such as sports. The sports merchandising unit depends heavily on professional baseball attendance and demand for club merchandise.

A November 2025 report said strong baseball interest lifted merchandise demand, and a pop-up store tied to a friendly match between two clubs also contributed to sales in that period.

An August 2026 report said the growing culture of attending baseball games and the 'block core' look, which wears team uniforms as everyday clothing, lifted related revenue. In workwear, the company is widening its channels from business-to-business sales to direct consumer sales.

A 2025 report noted that many domestic and overseas fashion companies were struggling amid weak consumer demand. Expansion into premium school uniforms in China and ASEAN is presented as the overseas growth path for the school uniform business.

Comparable data on sales growth and operating margins against listed apparel peers in the same industry, and recent industry-level indicators, were not obtained in this review.

Outlook

In its announcement of annual results in September 2026, the company said it would focus on raising corporate value based on earnings momentum and financial soundness.

Its new robotics business, through the subsidiary Hyungji Robotics launched in January 2026, is preparing wearable robot products, and the company said in May 2026 that it was preparing the robotics business with a second-half 2026 target.

In January 2026 the company signed an agreement with Shanghai Zhuoshi Robot to co-develop senior wearable products. Four of six trademark applications for robotics-related marks had received registration decisions as of a May 2026 report.

Overseas, the company signed a school uniform supply contract with a school in Osaka in June 2026 and continues to take part in the Shanghai Fashion Week kids show.

In sports, it added a sponsorship with the professional basketball club Busan KCC Egis, and it is expanding offline touchpoints from its Lotte World Mall store. One press report said the company expected sales to grow more than 25% in a year, and the reported sales growth for the year ended June 2025 was 25.5%.

On shareholder returns, no cash dividend has been paid since 2013, and the company disclosed that its CEO planned to buy 619,195 shares on the open market from October 22 to November 20, 2026.

Against the backdrop of a management-issue designation on August 12, 2026, a 5-to-1 share consolidation decision and a market capitalization shortfall below KRW 30 billion, the price-support measures and whether listing requirements are met are the main items to watch.

08

Bull factors

Sports merchandising growth

Sports unit sales for the year ended June 2026 rose 41.3% to KRW 68.5 billion, the largest share of sales at 39% per press reports. Partnerships across professional baseball, professional football and esports, plus the Lotte World Mall store, form the expansion base.

Press reports also cite baseball attendance culture and everyday demand for team uniforms as supports for sales.

Earnings recovery and return to profit

Operating profit for the year ended June 2026 rose 26.7% to KRW 8.42 billion, with an operating margin of 4.7%. Controlling-shareholder net income turned positive at KRW 7.43 billion. Press reports attribute much of the swing to the absence of the convertible bond valuation loss that caused the prior year's net loss.

Improved balance sheet

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 107.5% for the year ended June 2025 to 71.9% for the year ended June 2026. Total liabilities at the end of June 2026 were KRW 84.3 billion. A press analysis of the standalone filing said the current ratio rose to 200% from 171% a year earlier.

Bear factors

Quarterly earnings volatility

Second-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 48.3 billion were 15.9% lower than a year earlier, and the quarter posted an operating loss of KRW 397 million.

Of the KRW 7.43 billion controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters, KRW 6.2 billion came in the quarter ended September 2025, so profit was concentrated in one quarter.

Negative operating cash flow

Operating cash flow was negative in three of the four fiscal years reported. It was -KRW 11.1 billion for the year ended June 2026 and -KRW 22.9 billion for the year ended June 2025, the weakest of the four years. Profit has turned positive, but cash generation has not kept pace in several periods.

Shareholder returns and listing requirements

No cash dividend has been paid since 2013. The stock was designated for management issues on August 12, 2026, and the market capitalization shortfall was added as a reason. A 5-to-1 share consolidation was decided, with the new shares scheduled to list on October 20, 2026 per press reports. Minority shareholders held 72.7% at the end of June 2026.

Risk factors

Policy and regulation

A related report headline indicated that authorities had begun reviewing the appropriateness of school uniform prices. With the school-age population shrinking and the domestic school uniform market described as stagnant, pricing and volume in the uniform business could affect results. Because uniforms remain one pillar of total sales, this variable stays on the watch list.

New business execution

Wearable robot products are in development with a second-half 2026 target, but launch and commercialization timing has not been confirmed. Trademark registrations in robotics and machinery are progressing, yet this is a different business from apparel, so equipment, staffing and results will need separate verification. New-business costs could also narrow the margin improvement.

Listing status and trading conditions

The company faces a management-issue designation, a market capitalization shortfall, and a 5-to-1 share consolidation at the same time. Press reports said the new shares were scheduled to list on October 20, 2026, but whether trading resumed and whether listing requirements are met was not confirmed in this review. The CEO's planned open-market purchases were disclosed, but the outcome has not been confirmed.

Overall view

Hyungji Elite's periodic filing for the year ended June 2026 reported sales of KRW 177.8 billion, operating profit of KRW 8.42 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 7.43 billion. Both sales and operating profit were the largest in the June 2023 to June 2026 window.

Growth has come mainly from the sports merchandising unit, which press reports put at 39% of sales. The school uniform business is expanding in China and Japan, and the wearable robotics business has a second-half 2026 target.

On the other side, quarterly profit has become more volatile, with an operating loss of KRW 397 million in the quarter ended June 2026, and operating cash flow was negative in three of the four fiscal years.

No cash dividend has been paid since 2013, and listing-related issues such as the management-issue designation and the share consolidation are also present. The debt-to-equity ratio improved from 107.5% for the year ended June 2025 to 71.9% for the year ended June 2026.

The next checkpoints are the quarterly report for the quarter ended September 2026 due by November 14, 2026, the end of the CEO's planned share purchase window on November 20, 2026, and whether robotics products are unveiled.
