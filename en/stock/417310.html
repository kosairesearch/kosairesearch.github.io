Report overview

A Single-Office REIT Heads Into Liquidation After Its Asset Sale

Koramco THE ONE REIT is returning the gain from its June 2026 sale of its only asset through a special dividend and residual distributions, and it is moving toward liquidation. The jump in its August 2026 fiscal-year results coincides with that sale gain.

  1. 1

    The Hana Securities Building was sold for KRW 811.2 billion in June 2026, and press reports put the gain at about KRW 318 billion against the KRW 493.2 billion purchase price in 2020.

  2. 2

    The company chose to pay a special dividend first and then dissolve through an extraordinary shareholder meeting instead of buying new assets.

  3. 3

    The August 2026 fiscal year reported revenue of KRW 310.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 269.8 billion, with an operating margin of 87.0%. Compared with KRW 7.57 billion of revenue in the August 2025 fiscal year, the figures are dominated by the one-off sale gain.

  4. 4

    The planned schedule runs from a special dividend and extraordinary shareholder meeting in November 2026, through residual distributions between December 2026 and January 2027, to the close of liquidation in February 2027.

02

Business structure

Koramco THE ONE REIT's only asset was the Hana Securities Building in Yeouido, Seoul. The REIT acquired the building in November 2020, listed on the KOSPI in March 2022, and ran it as a single-asset vehicle with no further acquisitions.

The building is described as a prime office asset next to Yeouido Station on Seoul Subway Line 5, and its anchor tenant, Hana Securities, has a long-term master lease that lowers vacancy risk. Income depended on rent from this one building. The building was sold for KRW 811.2 billion on June 9, 2026.

In the bidding process, a rival bidder offered about KRW 800 billion and was picked as preferred bidder, but Hana Securities, the existing tenant, exercised its right of first refusal and became the final buyer. The final price was reported at about KRW 38.4 million per 3.3 square meters.

After the sale, the REIT held no assets. Because it had a single asset, there is no segment revenue mix to report. The largest shareholder, a Mirae Asset affiliate, held 13.2 percent as of March 31, 2026. Unlike peers that reinvested sale proceeds, this REIT chose not to acquire new assets.

04

Earnings analysis

In the audited annual figures, revenue rose modestly from KRW 7.14 billion in the August 2023 fiscal year to KRW 7.42 billion in the August 2024 fiscal year and KRW 7.57 billion in the August 2025 fiscal year. Operating profit went from KRW 4.78 billion to KRW 5.14 billion and then KRW 5.1 billion.

Operating profit rose 7.6 percent in the August 2024 fiscal year and fell 0.8 percent in the August 2025 fiscal year. Operating margins were 66.9 percent, 69.3 percent and 67.4 percent over the same years.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.84 billion, KRW 3.3 billion and KRW 2.95 billion, rising 16.2 percent in the August 2024 fiscal year and falling 10.6 percent in the August 2025 fiscal year.

Revenue has grown for three consecutive years, from the August 2024 fiscal year through the August 2026 fiscal year. The August 2026 fiscal year reflects the sale effect. Revenue reached KRW 310.3 billion, up 4,000.9 percent from the prior year, and operating profit reached KRW 269.8 billion, up 5,194.3 percent.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 269.1 billion, and net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 269.1 billion. The operating margin was 87.0 percent. The jump most likely reflects the June 2026 sale gain falling inside this fiscal year.

Press estimates of the gain range from about KRW 312.7 billion to KRW 318 billion. How that gain maps onto reported operating profit is a point to confirm in the notes to the periodic report.

The debt ratio, which was 153.5 percent in the August 2023 fiscal year and 156.8 percent in the August 2025 fiscal year, fell to 0.1 percent in the August 2026 fiscal year. Total equity at end-August 2026 was KRW 461 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 306 million. Operating cash flow was KRW 757.4 billion.

Over the last four quarters (Q4 2025, covering September to November, through Q3 2026, covering June to August), profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 269.1 billion, the same period as the August 2026 fiscal year.

05

Industry analysis

Prime office prices in Seoul's core business districts remain high. A Daishin Securities analyst judged that this environment helped the sale but would raise the investment burden on buying new assets in the same market. Rate volatility and an upward rate trend were cited as factors limiting target yields.

In the REIT sector, sale-then-reinvest and debt-repayment approaches are both visible. NH All-One REIT and D&D Platform REIT chose reinvestment or debt repayment after asset sales, while Koramco Life Infra REIT and ESR Kendall Square REIT are expanding asset recycling.

Koramco THE ONE REIT is classified as a case that chose liquidation over acquiring new assets. On the structure, distributing residual assets directly after dissolution could create corporate tax exposure for the REIT, so a special dividend was paid first.

Commentary describes the REIT as one of the rare listed REITs to complete the full cycle of investment, operation and exit. Another view says the case exposes the limits of the perpetual REIT model.

A broader concern is that domestic listed REITs could come to resemble closed-end property funds that buy and sell individual buildings rather than long-term growth platforms.

06

Outlook

In a July 2026 shareholder letter, the company said it would return the proceeds from the Hana Securities Building sale and liquidate the REIT. The plan called for the board to discuss the liquidation at an October 2026 meeting, with a special dividend payment and an extraordinary shareholder meeting in November.

The company stated that dissolution is ultimately decided by a special resolution of shareholders and is not yet final. After creditor protection procedures and cost settlement, residual assets are planned for distribution between December 2026 and January 2027, with liquidation to be completed in February 2027.

The record date was August 31, 2026, and the ex-dividend date was August 28, 2026, both already passed. The company said that after the ex-dividend date, the share price could converge toward the level of the residual distribution once the special dividend portion falls away.

The amount returned may change with the final results, audit, cost settlement and tax review. The company's expected return is a one-off payment funded by the sale gain and differs in nature from the yield of over 6.7 percent that the REIT paid after listing.

The next points to confirm are the actual special dividend amount, the size of the residual distribution, and the disclosure that closes the liquidation in February 2027.

08

Bull factors

A completed investment cycle

The REIT bought the Hana Securities Building for KRW 493.2 billion in November 2020, listed in March 2022, and sold it for KRW 811.2 billion in June 2026, realizing the gain. It is described as one of the few listed REITs to complete the full cycle from investment through operation to exit.

Press reports put the gain at roughly KRW 312.7 billion to KRW 318 billion. Once the special dividend and residual distributions are paid, the original investment and the asset price appreciation are passed to shareholders.

Sale price and demand backdrop

The Hana Securities Building sold for KRW 811.2 billion, above the roughly KRW 800 billion offered in the bidding, at a reported price of about KRW 38.4 million per 3.3 square meters. A Daishin Securities analyst said the price level of Yeouido prime offices worked in favor of the sale.

Its proximity to Yeouido Station and its long-term master lease are also cited as supporting the asset's value in the process.

Balance sheet cleanup

Total liabilities at end-August 2026 were KRW 306 million and total equity was KRW 461 billion. The debt ratio fell from 156.8 percent in the August 2025 fiscal year to 0.1 percent in the August 2026 fiscal year. The debt appears to have been cleared with the sale proceeds, so financial burden is much lower. The equity figure, however, includes cash that is about to be distributed to shareholders.

09

Bear factors

Limits of the growth model

With no new assets acquired after the single building was sold, the REIT is moving to liquidation, which departs from the core-asset growth model it described at listing. One commentary cites this as a limit of the domestic REIT market.

The concern is that listed REITs could come to resemble property funds more than long-term growth platforms. At listing, the company had said it would keep adding high-quality core assets.

Price structure after the ex-dividend date

The company warned that once the special dividend portion falls away after the ex-dividend date, the share price could drop sharply toward its estimate of the residual distribution. It described this as different from a normal ex-dividend event, because delisting procedures follow.

Passive ETF selling tied to index changes was also cited as a burden. The ex-dividend date was August 28, 2026, so this burden is already reflected in the current share price structure.

Reinvestment conditions and possible changes in the payout

The reason for liquidation was the judgment that new assets could not deliver yields close to the prior level, given rate volatility and high office prices. A Daishin Securities analyst said that with rates trending upward, securing target yields would be limited. The payout may change with cost settlement, tax review and the outcome of the final results and audit.

10

Risk factors

Liquidation process and shareholder vote

Dissolution is ultimately decided by a special resolution of shareholders and is not yet confirmed, according to the company. If the extraordinary meeting is delayed or the resolution conditions change, the special dividend and residual distribution schedule could also slip. Any delay pushes back the date on which cash reaches shareholders.

Payout amount changes and tax

The company said the payout amount can change with the final results and the audit. Distributing residual assets directly after dissolution could create corporate tax exposure for the REIT, which is why a special dividend was chosen first. Depending on the tax review, the amount received by shareholders and the timing of payment could still shift.

Liquidity and index changes

Volatility could rise as the share price moves toward the estimated residual value after the ex-dividend date. Passive fund selling tied to index changes has also been raised as a burden. As delisting proceeds, trading liquidity could decline, which is another risk factor.

12

Overall view

Koramco THE ONE REIT sold the Hana Securities Building, bought in 2020, for KRW 811.2 billion in June 2026, and press reports put the realized gain at about KRW 318 billion. It is now returning that value to shareholders through a special dividend and residual distributions as it liquidates.

Revenue of KRW 310.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 269.8 billion for the August 2026 fiscal year reflect the one-off sale gain and are hard to compare with the KRW 7.14 billion to KRW 7.57 billion range of the August 2023 to August 2025 fiscal years.

The debt ratio fell to 0.1 percent and total equity stands at KRW 461 billion. On the bullish side, the REIT completed a full investment cycle and cleaned up its balance sheet. On the bearish side, the liquidation structure limits its growth model and the share price structure changes after the ex-dividend date.

The payout may change with cost settlement and tax review, and dissolution itself requires a special shareholder resolution.

The next points to confirm are the November 2026 special dividend payment and extraordinary meeting outcome, the residual distribution between December 2026 and January 2027, and the close of liquidation in February 2027.
