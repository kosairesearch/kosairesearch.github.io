Hyundai WIA: Sales Growth Meets FX-Driven Earnings Swings

Sales have risen for three straight quarters, but Q3 2026 operating gains were outweighed by FX losses, leaving a KRW 13 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders.

02

Business structure

Hyundai WIA is an auto parts maker within the Hyundai Motor Group, focusing on engines, modules, drivetrain parts and thermal management components. According to the 2025 annual business report, automotive parts account for 92% of sales, defense 5% and mobility solutions about 3%.

The machine tool business, which the company had operated since its founding, was sold in July 2025, shifting the center of gravity toward auto parts, defense and robotics. A March 2025 report said the unit was being sold to a consortium of SMEC and Rilson Private Equity for roughly KRW 340 billion.

In preliminary Q3 2026 figures released on October 30, 2026, mobility sales reached KRW 2.29 trillion, about 93.1% of consolidated revenue, while mobility operating profit fell 4.4% to KRW 41.2 billion.

The other segment, which includes special business and robotics, posted sales of KRW 170.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 13.5 billion, up 55.2%. Within mobility, module sales rose 18% and thermal management sales rose 160% year on year.

Engine volumes are expected to shrink as electrification advances, since engines had accounted for roughly 40% of sales. Customers are concentrated within Hyundai Motor Group affiliates, so most revenue comes from inside the group. The company has set a target of KRW 14.8 trillion in consolidated sales by 2035.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on the disclosed annual figures, revenue rose from KRW 8.21 trillion in 2022 to KRW 8.59 trillion in 2023, a 4.7% increase and the largest year in the period. It then fell to KRW 8.18 trillion in 2024 before recovering to KRW 8.48 trillion in 2025.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 229.2 billion in 2023, then declined for two consecutive years, to KRW 218.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 204.4 billion in 2025. The operating margin stayed between 2.4% and 2.7% over the period, so higher sales did not translate into a wider margin.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 65.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 120.4 billion in 2024, the largest year in the period, before falling 17.8% to KRW 99 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, at KRW 129.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 116.9 billion in 2025, is a different figure and reflects minority shares.

In the Q1 2026 results, the auto parts segment's operating profit fell 9.7% to KRW 31.5 billion, while the other segment's operating profit rose 47.8% to KRW 20.1 billion, according to a press report from April 24, 2026.

Quarterly sales bottomed at KRW 2.09 trillion in Q4 2025 and then rose for three straight quarters, to KRW 2.18 trillion in Q1 2026, KRW 2.35 trillion in Q2 and KRW 2.46 trillion in Q3 2026.

Q3 operating profit of KRW 54.7 billion did not carry through to the bottom line, as pre-tax results turned to a loss and the controlling-shareholder net loss came to KRW 13 billion.

Reported FX losses of KRW 61.2 billion, driven by won strength affecting parent-company receivables and rouble weakness affecting the Russian subsidiary's dollar-denominated debt, were the main reason.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for vehicle parts moves directly with the production and sales of group affiliates' automakers. In Q3 2026, engine volumes for hybrid vehicles at the Mexico plant rose, while Russian engine sales declined. A Q4 2025 report attributed lower local volumes in Russia to the end of a scrappage incentive.

In Q1 2026, higher vehicle production and a larger share of four-wheel-drive models in domestic sales lifted drivetrain parts revenue.

The electrification shift cuts both ways: engine volumes shrink while thermal management and modules become growth engines, and the Q3 2026 thermal management growth was attributed to higher air-conditioning parts shipments.

The company also said a higher share of module sales and lower drivetrain and constant-velocity joint sales weighed on profitability. In thermal management, the company competes with specialist makers such as Hanon Systems.

The machine tool business held the number two domestic market share at 26.5% in 2023 before its sale. Many analysts expect demand for collaborative and logistics robots to rise as factories automate. In defense, exports of K2 tanks and K9 howitzers lifted sales in Q4 2025. Cost control and product mix improvement tied to the vehicle cycle remain open challenges.

06

Outlook

The company has set a target of KRW 14.8 trillion in consolidated sales by 2035 and plans to invest KRW 3.7 trillion in future mobility through 2035.

It is pursuing the sale of its special business unit, and the proceeds are expected to be focused on growth areas such as core components, thermal management and robotics. No timetable for completing that sale has been confirmed in the reports reviewed.

Inventories at the end of Q3 2026 stood at KRW 725.3 billion, up KRW 135.4 billion from KRW 589.9 billion at the end of 2025, which the company attributed to higher engine output in Mexico and the start of full production at the Slovakia plant. Of this increase, KRW 80.4 billion was linked to those factors.

Cash and cash equivalents were KRW 1.58 trillion at the end of Q3 2026 and borrowings were KRW 1.16 trillion, leaving net cash of KRW 422.8 billion. Operating cash flow for the first three quarters of 2026 was a net inflow of KRW 289.8 billion.

A company representative said the company would keep expanding electrification parts orders with automakers to build a stable profit base. The next checkpoints are how the inventory burden shows up in Q4 2026 sales and operating profit, and which growth businesses receive the proceeds from the special business sale.

08

Bull factors

Sales growth from thermal management and modules

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 2.46 trillion rose 14.3% year on year, and mobility sales rose 15.1%. Thermal management sales jumped 160% on higher air-conditioning shipments, and module sales rose 18%, with electrification-related products driving top-line growth. The company said it plans to keep expanding electrification parts orders from automakers.

Broader profit mix from defense and robotics

The other segment, covering special business and robotics, posted Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 13.5 billion, up 55.2% year on year. Special business sales rose 11%, and higher defense volumes and lower project costs supported profit growth.

Per a Q1 2026 press report, the other segment's operating profit rose 47.8%, partly offsetting the decline in auto parts profit.

Easing leverage and a low price-to-book level

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 102.1% in 2022 to 72.8% in 2025, and total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 2.96 trillion. Equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.61 trillion at the same date, and the price-to-book ratio sits at a low level. Proceeds from the machine tool sale are planned for future growth businesses.

09

Bear factors

Thinner margins and softer mobility profitability

The operating margin fell from 2.7% in 2023 to 2.4% in 2025. Mobility operating profit in Q3 2026 declined 4.4% to KRW 41.2 billion, and its margin dropped from 2.2% a year earlier to 1.8%. The company cited product mix changes and higher material and logistics costs.

FX swings absorbing operating gains

Q3 2026 FX losses, per preliminary figures, reached KRW 61.2 billion, widening by KRW 46.2 billion from a year earlier. Despite operating profit of KRW 54.7 billion, the company posted a pre-tax loss, and continuing-operations net income swung from a prior-year profit to a loss. The company cited won strength and rouble weakness as the main drivers.

Group dependence and inventory build

Most sales come from Hyundai Motor Group affiliates, so revenue moves with the group's sales cycle. Inventories rose to KRW 725.3 billion at the end of Q3 2026 from KRW 589.9 billion at the end of 2025, an increase of KRW 135.4 billion. The shrinking of the engine business, which once accounted for about 40% of sales, is also underway.

10

Risk factors

FX and foreign-currency debt

Won strength affects the parent's FX-denominated receivables, while rouble weakness affects the Russian subsidiary's foreign-currency borrowings and payables. The widening Q3 2026 FX loss was enough to push controlling-shareholder results into a net loss, making exchange rates a key variable for quarterly earnings. Non-operating results can offset operating gains depending on the currency direction.

Customer concentration and market shifts

Most revenue comes from group affiliates, so it is directly exposed to Hyundai Motor and KIA sales swings. EV market softness and tariff uncertainty have been cited as burdens on vehicle parts profitability. The key question is how quickly engine volumes decline and hybrid and electrified parts volumes replace them.

Execution of portfolio restructuring

Following the machine tool sale, the special business unit sale is being pursued, and the company's structure is still changing. Reaching the 2035 target of KRW 14.8 trillion in sales and the KRW 3.7 trillion investment plan will require new orders and better margins in thermal management and robotics.

The profit contribution of these new businesses is still a fraction of total operating profit, which makes it a checkpoint to watch.

12

Overall view

Hyundai WIA's annual sales rose from KRW 8.21 trillion in 2022 to KRW 8.48 trillion in 2025, but operating profit fell for two straight years after peaking in 2023, so core margin remains the central issue.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 2.46 trillion and operating profit of KRW 54.7 billion both rose, yet FX losses produced a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 13 billion.

Strengths include thermal management and module growth, defense and robotics contributions, a lower debt-to-equity ratio and a share price at a low level relative to book value.

Weaknesses include a thinner operating margin, FX volatility, concentration in group affiliate sales, a larger inventory and the shrinking engine business. The portfolio is being reshaped through the machine tool sale and the pending special business sale, with a 2035 sales target of KRW 14.8 trillion.

The next checkpoints are preliminary Q4 2026 results, the operating margin and FX figures in the 2026 annual report, and completion of the special business sale. This summary is for information only and does not include an investment opinion or target price.
