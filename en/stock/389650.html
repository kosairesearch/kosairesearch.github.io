02

Business structure

NEXT BIOMEDICAL develops and sells therapeutic medical devices and was listed on KOSDAQ in August 2024. Its flagship product is the endoscopic hemostatic agent Nexpowder, and press reports said Nexpowder sales reached about KRW 13.7 billion in 2025, up 66% from the prior year.

Nexsphere-F is an embolization device aimed at musculoskeletal pain, and its second-quarter 2026 revenue was about KRW 520 million, up 144% year on year. In the second quarter of 2026, Nexpowder accounted for 83% of total revenue, and about 97% of its sales came from exports.

By region, the split was 53% US, 41% Europe and 5% Japan, and the company said Japanese sales have grown quickly since the September 2025 official launch there.

In Europe, Nexsphere-F is sold through individual distributors, and in June 2026 the company signed a distribution agreement with Terumo covering some European countries.

Unlike conventional clip or thermal coagulation methods, Nexpowder sprays powder onto a bleeding site to form a gel, and it received US FDA approval for bleeding prevention in the lower gastrointestinal tract in November 2024.

Press reports said Nexpowder is the only product cleared in the US for bleeding prevention, and Medtronic is leading post-market clinical work and marketing as a partner. Revenue is concentrated in Nexpowder and overseas exports, so product and regional concentration is pronounced. The company is classified in biopharmaceuticals, but its sales are based on medical device sales.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 4.88 billion in 2023 to KRW 9.54 billion in 2024 (up 95.5%) and KRW 16.5 billion in 2025 (up 72.7%), marking two consecutive years of growth, with 2025 the largest year in the 2023 to 2025 period.

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 5.23 billion in 2023 to KRW 3.57 billion in 2024 and KRW 720 million in 2025, and the company has recorded losses for three consecutive years. The operating margin improved from -107.1% to -37.5% and then to -4.4%.

The main driver cited for revenue growth was higher Nexpowder exports. SG&A rose about 31% year on year on R&D and clinical spending, which the company described as investment in new products and global regulatory approvals.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.52 billion in 2023 turned into a net income of KRW 2.86 billion in 2024, then returned to a net loss of KRW 311 million in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests moved in the same direction.

Operating cash flow went from -KRW 4.16 billion in 2023 and -KRW 1.2 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.25 billion in 2025, a positive swing.

On a quarterly basis, the company posted its first quarterly operating profit of KRW 370 million in the third quarter of 2025, then an operating loss of KRW 442 million in the fourth quarter.

Operating losses were KRW 1.19 billion, KRW 1.03 billion and KRW 866 million in the first through third quarters of 2026, so the loss has narrowed since the second quarter.

Revenue bottomed at KRW 3.8 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (down 23.0% from the prior quarter) and then rose to KRW 4.12 billion, KRW 4.94 billion and KRW 5.25 billion across the first three quarters of 2026.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 3.47 billion in the third quarter of 2026, far larger than the operating loss, so the effect of non-operating items should be checked in the periodic report.

The combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters was KRW 4.29 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Press reports describe the global arthritis embolization treatment market as roughly KRW 5 trillion, and Nexsphere-F targets pain treatment demand within that market.

In endoscopic hemostasis, conventional clip and thermal coagulation methods are described as requiring skilled technique and having difficulty reaching some sites. Nexpowder is positioned on rapid hemostasis over wide areas and on degrading in the body, which removes the need for a second removal procedure.

In an April 2025 press report, the company's CEO said Japan accounts for about 10% of the total market and the US for about 40%. In the US bleeding-prevention segment, Nexpowder is the only cleared product, which is cited as a first-mover advantage.

The FDA clinical trial for Nexsphere-F is progressing with a target of completing patient enrollment within 2026. Results from a large domestic trial demonstrating bleeding-prevention effects were published in a gastroenterology journal, and press reports mention the possibility of listing as a standard treatment.

In terms of industry cycle, the company is in a stage where sales expansion and accumulation of clinical evidence proceed together, and unlike large biopharma companies, its operating profit is still in the loss zone.

Risk factors

Clinical and regulatory

The FDA trial for Nexsphere-F targets completing patient enrollment within 2026, and the timing of US market entry depends on trial results and approval. The company expects new revenue to build after FDA approval. Delays or uncertain trial results could raise costs.

Partner and distribution dependence

Overseas Nexpowder sales depend on global partners such as Medtronic and on a European distributor network. The Terumo distribution right for Nexsphere-F covers only some European countries. Changes in a partner's sales strategy or contract terms would directly affect revenue.

Profitability and funding

The combined net loss over the last four quarters of KRW 4.29 billion, compared with total equity of KRW 48.1 billion at the end of 2025, is a gauge of how fast equity is declining. If R&D and clinical spending keep rising, the loss period could lengthen. Whether positive operating cash flow holds on a quarterly basis is the key question.
