02

Business structure

NRobotics began as a machinery maker focused on food-processing equipment, and it has since renamed itself and moved toward robotics and automation. Its core lines are food automation equipment, library automation and RFID systems, and AI robots.

In libraries, it has installed automated systems at about 800 public and university libraries nationwide, and its cumulative share of Public Procurement Service sales stood at about 27% as of December 2025.

Designations as a superior procurement item and as a superior commercial product for the Ministry of National Defense have supported repeat orders from public institutions.

Its robotics subsidiary, whose acquisition was completed in January 2026, reported 2025 revenue of KRW 18.6 billion and net income of about KRW 21.28 million. In April 2026 the company disclosed plans to absorb this subsidiary through a merger with a merger date of June 30, 2026.

The ROMI library robot combines autonomous driving with RFID on a modular AI platform, and in October 2026 the company signed a global exclusive supply deal with Bibliotheca, a library solutions provider serving more than 30,000 libraries in over 70 countries.

NRobotics expects to sell more than 100 ROMI units over the next three years. In food processing, references with large food manufacturers are reportedly spreading to mid-sized and smaller manufacturers.

In April 2026 the company, together with an affiliate, acquired a 5% stake in Genoco, a space and defense communications firm, and disclosed the purpose as management participation.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 15 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.3 billion in 2023, a 31.2% decline, before recovering to KRW 21.5 billion in 2024, up 109.1%, and KRW 23.4 billion in 2025, up 8.7%.

Revenue rose for two consecutive years, 2024 and 2025, after the 2023 low, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period. Operating profit moved from KRW 899 million in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 2.3 billion in 2023, a swing into the red.

The 2024 operating loss of KRW 1.17 billion marked a continued loss with a narrower deficit, and 2025 brought an operating profit of KRW 581 million, a return to profit, with an operating margin of 2.5%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to profit in 2024 at KRW 4.61 billion and reached KRW 6.86 billion in 2025, up 48.7%. Net income including non-controlling interests was disclosed at the same amount as controlling-shareholder net income in every year from 2022 to 2025.

The gap between 2025 operating profit of KRW 581 million and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 6.86 billion is wide, so whether non-operating items contributed should be checked in the income-statement details of the regular report.

Operating cash flow was negative from 2022 to 2024 and turned to KRW 2.51 billion in 2025, while the debt ratio, total liabilities to total equity, fell from 40.6% in 2023 to 27.4% in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue of KRW 11.2 billion in 3Q 2025 was followed by KRW 6.68 billion in 4Q 2025, a 40.1% decline from the prior quarter. It then rebounded to KRW 9.05 billion in 1Q 2026, up 35.4%, before falling to KRW 7.71 billion in 2Q and KRW 5.37 billion in 3Q 2026, two straight quarterly declines.

Revenue in 3Q 2026 was down 51.9% from a year earlier. Operating profit of KRW 384 million in 1Q 2026 gave way to an operating loss of KRW 8.31 million in 2Q and an operating loss of KRW 1.98 billion in 3Q 2026, two straight loss-making quarters, with 3Q a swing into loss from a year earlier.

Controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 5.63 billion in 1Q 2026 became a net loss of KRW 1.25 billion in 2Q and KRW 6.56 billion in 3Q 2026, and controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 1.61 billion.

Press reports say the subsidiary acquired in January 2026 is being reflected in consolidated results, so comparisons with pre-2026 quarters should account for that change.

05

Industry analysis

The food industry faces labor shortages and rising wage costs, which is lifting demand for collaborative robots, intelligent logistics automation and AI-based real-time quality inspection. The company and press reports say orders are rising from mid-sized and smaller manufacturers pursuing equipment modernization.

In library automation, the company has installed systems at about 800 public and university libraries, and its cumulative share of Public Procurement Service sales of about 27% as of December 2025 makes public procurement the core channel.

The library robot combines autonomous driving with RFID and has previously been exported to the United States, the United Kingdom, Denmark, New Zealand, Taiwan and Japan.

Because Bibliotheca supplies solutions to libraries in more than 70 countries, its overseas distribution network appears central to expanding ROMI sales. In steel, high-emissivity ceramic coating is often applied by manual spraying and depends heavily on imported equipment, according to the company.

The company projects the global high-emissivity coating market to reach about USD 2.45 billion by 2033.

Compared with robotics peers such as Doosan Robotics, Rainbow Robotics, Robotis and Yuilrobotics, which center on collaborative robots and robot components, this company is weighted toward public procurement and food-processing equipment references.

On the industry cycle, the investment flows of small and mid-sized food manufacturers and public library orders appear to be the variables that drive results.

06

Outlook

The company expects to sell more than 100 ROMI library robots over the next three years and plans to use Bibliotheca's global sales and service network to do so. It plans to extend ROMI from public, educational and logistics uses to administrative services, security and manufacturing.

In a national R&D project announced in April 2026, NRobotics is responsible for developing an automated spray coating robot, and the project runs from 2026 to 2030 with total funding of KRW 9.75 billion over about four years and nine months.

After the merger, the company says it will accelerate building an end-to-end automated factory platform that combines process automation hardware with robot control and autonomous driving software.

In space and defense, it has linked its 5% stake in Genoco to a plan to combine satellite communications and avionics technology with its robot control technology.

Overseas, press reports said the company is pursuing exports of public service robot models to about ten countries, including Japan, Taiwan, Australia and the United States. With 3Q 2026 revenue down sharply from a year earlier, the next checkpoint is how much ROMI and national project revenue can fill that gap.

The 2026 annual business report, the next regular filing, will show full-year 2026 revenue, 4Q results and the effect of subsidiary consolidation. It will also show whether merger-related cost removal is visible in selling and administrative expenses.

Bull factors

Earnings recovery in 2025

Operating profit returned to the black in 2025 at KRW 581 million, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose 48.7% to KRW 6.86 billion. Revenue also grew for two consecutive years, 2024 and 2025, showing top-line expansion alongside the profit turn.

Operating cash flow turning to KRW 2.51 billion is read as a sign that earnings now have cash support. Whether the profit is one-off should be checked in the regular report's detailed line items.

Repeat orders built on public procurement

Library automation systems have been installed at about 800 public and university libraries nationwide, and cumulative share of Public Procurement Service sales was about 27% as of December 2025.

Public verification, including designation as a superior procurement item and a Ministry of National Defense superior commercial product, is cited as the basis for repeat purchasing. The company also says references with large food makers are spreading to mid-sized and smaller manufacturers.

Overseas channel for robotics business

ROMI signed a global exclusive supply deal with Bibliotheca in October 2026, and the company expects to sell more than 100 units over three years. Bibliotheca supplies solutions to more than 30,000 libraries in over 70 countries.

The company also joined a KRW 9.75 billion national R&D project for 2026 to 2030, broadening its robotics technology base.

Bear factors

Losses return in 2026 quarters

After a 2Q 2026 operating loss of KRW 8.31 million, 3Q 2026 brought an operating loss of KRW 1.98 billion, marking two straight loss-making quarters. Revenue in 3Q 2026 was KRW 5.37 billion, down 51.9% from a year earlier. Controlling-shareholder net income also swung to a net loss of KRW 6.56 billion in 3Q 2026 from a year earlier.

Thin earnings cushion

The 2025 operating margin of 2.5% is thin, and the margin fell to -22.3% in 2023. Controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 1.61 billion.

Some quarters, such as 1Q 2026 with a KRW 384 million operating profit against KRW 5.63 billion in controlling-shareholder net income, show wide gaps that make earnings quality hard to judge.

Unproven profitability of new businesses

The 100-unit ROMI sales target and the national project are still in the planning stage, and the Genoco stake is an investment made for management participation.

The company is pursuing the subsidiary merger, space and defense, and overseas expansion at the same time, which bears on the argument that profitability will take time. How much new-business revenue shows up in quarterly results is not yet clear.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Revenue rebounded in 1Q 2026 but then declined in 2Q and 3Q, and quarterly results moved between profit and loss. Annual profit rests on a thin 2.5% operating margin, so a small revenue drop could push results back into the red. The subsidiary consolidation effect also clouds quarter-on-quarter comparisons.

Integration and new-business execution

The company is pushing several initiatives at once, including the subsidiary merger, ROMI overseas expansion, a national R&D project and the space and defense stake. If resources are spread thin, core-business order intake and cost control could suffer.

Whether the integration and removal of duplicate functions after the merger show up in actual results still needs to be confirmed.

Shareholder structure and share price volatility

The major shareholder group led by CI Tech held 40.41% as of March 11, 2026, and the free float ratio was 59.55%. Press reports have cited several limit-up moves tied to news events, which points to relatively high short-term volatility. Share price moves can also diverge from reported results.

Overall view

NRobotics has a starting point for earnings recovery, with a 2025 return to operating profit at KRW 581 million and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 6.86 billion. Revenue rose in 2024 and 2025 after the 2023 low, reaching KRW 23.4 billion in 2025.

In 2026, however, revenue fell for two straight quarters after peaking in 1Q, and 2Q and 3Q brought operating losses and controlling-shareholder net losses. Controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 1.61 billion, so the durability of profits after the turnaround is still unproven.

Strengths cited include food automation and public library references and a cumulative procurement share of about 27%. The robotics pivot is taking shape through the ROMI overseas deal, a national R&D project and the subsidiary merger, but it is not yet clearly visible in quarterly results.

The bull case rests on the profit turn and public references, while the bear case rests on the return of quarterly losses in 2026 and a thin operating margin.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary full-year 2026 results, the 2026 annual business report and the revenue and operating profit trend in the 1Q 2027 quarterly report.
