02

Business structure

SEC builds inspection equipment for semiconductor, battery and defense customers, along with research instruments, based on electron-beam and X-ray technology.

By product mix, X-ray for semiconductors accounted for 42% of revenue in the second quarter of 2026, followed by other products at 24%, X-ray for batteries at 18%, Tabletop SEM at 9% and LINAC at 6%.

In 2024, semiconductor X-ray held 39.2%, battery X-ray 31.8%, LINAC 17.1%, Tabletop SEM 6.3% and other products 5.6%, according to the IPO report from Eugene Investment & Securities.

The semiconductor segment centers on HBM back-end inspection, and the NF120M HBM inspection tool is the product covered by the Micron contract. The defense segment supplies LINAC-based missile inspection equipment to domestic defense contractors and has some overseas exports.

The battery segment delivers electric vehicle battery inspection equipment to global customers. The company says it has localized its X-ray tube, the core source technology, which reduces reliance on overseas equipment for battery, semiconductor and automotive electronics inspection.

Major customers fall into semiconductor makers, battery cell makers and defense contractors, and semiconductor and battery inspection equipment account for most of the revenue mix.

The competitive field is concentrated among a small number of firms specializing in X-ray and electron-beam inspection, and comparisons are often drawn with inspection equipment peers such as Koh Young Technology.

Flash cancer therapy devices and 3D non-destructive inspection equipment are described as products still under development.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue rose 9.8% from KRW 53.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 59.2 billion in 2025. Over the same period, operating profit of KRW 1.34 billion turned into an operating loss of KRW 3.06 billion, and the operating margin fell from 2.5% to -5.2%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 2.07 billion in 2024 to a net loss of KRW 3.79 billion in 2025, while net income including non-controlling interests followed the same path, from KRW 2.07 billion to a net loss of KRW 3.79 billion.

Revenue grew on HBM demand in the first nine months of 2025, but cost increases widened the operating loss, according to the company's disclosure summary. Quarterly swings were larger.

Third-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 15.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 95.5 million, then fourth-quarter revenue rose to KRW 22.7 billion, up 44.6% from the prior quarter, and operating profit reached KRW 1.03 billion, up 980.1%.

First-quarter 2026 revenue fell 57.2% to KRW 9.73 billion, and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 3.75 billion. Second-quarter revenue was KRW 7.55 billion, down 22.4%, and the operating loss widened to KRW 3.96 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue recovered to KRW 16.6 billion, up 119.9% from the prior quarter and up 5.7% year on year, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.96 billion, though the quarter remained a loss against a profit a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 375 million in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 736 million in the fourth quarter, then a net loss of KRW 3.55 billion, KRW 3.77 billion and KRW 3.25 billion in the first three quarters of 2026, which marks three consecutive loss-making quarters.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 9.83 billion. The debt ratio fell from 279.9% in 2024 to 137.5% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 35.4 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities of KRW 48.6 billion at the end of 2025.

Operating cash flow, however, moved from KRW 2.4 billion in 2024 to negative KRW 5.31 billion in 2025. The reported figures do not include a one-off item, and the large quarterly swings are generally linked to the timing of revenue recognition on large contracts.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from the front end is driven by high-resolution non-destructive inspection as HBM stacks grow taller and finer.

The company argues that growth in AI chips and taller, finer HBM stacks is lifting demand for high-resolution non-destructive inspection, and that limits of optical and ultrasonic methods will drive a broader market for X-ray-based inspection equipment.

On the defense side, the company points to structural growth in demand for high-energy LINAC-based inspection as missiles get larger. Battery demand rests on the growing inspection needs of electric vehicles.

NH Investment & Securities, in a May 2026 report, described SEC as a likely beneficiary of rising demand for high-resolution non-destructive inspection across semiconductors, defense and secondary batteries, citing the substitution of imported equipment as a driver.

The same report expected a full earnings turnaround in 2027. The industry is in a phase of expanding HBM investment, but the timing of order revenue recognition varies from quarter to quarter, so earnings tend to be volatile.

SEC's strengths are its X-ray tube source technology and its HBM inspection references, while Koh Young Technology and similar firms compete in HBM back-end inspection.

The localization trend favors SEC, but global equipment makers' development speed and the time needed for customer qualification will affect order volume.

06

Outlook

In its May 2026 investor relations materials, the company set targets of KRW 111.6 billion in revenue and a 10.2% operating margin for 2027. Compared with 2025 revenue of KRW 59.2 billion, the revenue target is about 1.9 times that level, so quarterly sales would need to rise substantially.

The company is pursuing an HBM- and defense-led growth strategy with a better product mix, aimed at a profit-focused structure. On product development, it said it is evaluating HBM in-line inspection equipment with customers.

The Micron contract, which covers the NF120M HBM inspection tool, runs from November 9, 2026 to June 5, 2027. Payment is set at 80% due within 60 days after shipment and 20% within 60 days after the customer's final acceptance, so the acceptance date affects both final cash collection and revenue confirmation.

In 2026 the company disclosed national research projects in July and August, including one on flash radiation cancer therapy. In September 2026 it also disclosed a single-sale supply contract.

Whether the 2027 targets are reached will depend on volume supply of HBM inspection tools, stable defense and battery revenue, and the timing of exit from the loss-making stretch.

Bull factors

Expanding HBM inspection orders

SEC disclosed a supply contract with Micron Semiconductor Asia Operations for the NF120M HBM inspection tool, worth KRW 5.8 billion. That amount equals 9.80% of 2025 separate-basis revenue. The company also said it is evaluating HBM in-line inspection equipment with customers, which leaves room for further orders.

Demand diversifying into defense and batteries

LINAC-based missile inspection equipment has been supplied to domestic defense contractors, with some exports under way. Electric vehicle battery inspection equipment is also delivered to global customers.

The company disclosed a national research project selection in July 2026, extending its reach into medical applications.

Narrowing losses and medium-term targets

The operating loss in the third quarter of 2026 narrowed to KRW 1.96 billion from KRW 3.96 billion in the second quarter. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 16.6 billion rose 5.7% year on year. The company set a 2027 revenue target of KRW 111.6 billion and a 10.2% operating margin.

Bear factors

Three consecutive loss-making quarters

Operating losses and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026. The profit in the fourth quarter of 2025 was a single quarter, and losses resumed afterward.

Cash flow and debt burden

Operating cash flow swung from KRW 2.4 billion in 2024 to negative KRW 5.31 billion in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 48.6 billion, with a debt ratio of 137.5%. Total equity of KRW 35.4 billion provides a buffer, but continued losses could increase the burden of working capital financing.

Quarterly revenue swings and the gap to targets

Revenue fell to KRW 9.73 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 7.55 billion in the second, two consecutive quarterly declines. The 2027 revenue target of KRW 111.6 billion is about 1.9 times 2025 revenue of KRW 59.2 billion. Reaching it would require steady revenue recognition from large contracts on a quarterly basis.

Risk factors

Order and customer concentration

The Micron contract equals 9.80% of 2025 revenue and is a single large order. Payment is 80% due within 60 days after shipment and 20% within 60 days after final acceptance, so delays in acceptance could push back both cash collection and revenue confirmation.

Because orders are concentrated on specific customers and products, a single delay can move quarterly results sharply.

Technology commercialization and competition

HBM in-line inspection equipment is confirmed to be at the customer evaluation stage. If it does not move into volume production, the effect on order growth will be limited.

The inspection equipment market includes competitors such as Koh Young Technology, so customer qualification and pricing competition may both matter.

Financial capacity and funding

Operating cash flow was negative KRW 5.31 billion in 2025, and losses have continued for three quarters in 2026. Total liabilities stand at KRW 48.6 billion.

If equipment capacity expansion and R&D spending continue alongside losses, additional funding may be needed, so cash flow and borrowing structure should be reviewed in the annual report.

Overall view

Revenue rose to KRW 59.2 billion in 2025, but the company posted an operating loss of KRW 3.06 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.79 billion.

Losses have persisted for three consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, and the third-quarter operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.96 billion.
