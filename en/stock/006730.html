Report overview

Hotel Recovery and a Development Option at Seobu T&D

Seobu T&D grew operating profit to KRW 70.3 billion in 2025 on hotel demand, but operating profit fell to KRW 14.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and quarterly earnings have become more volatile.

02

Business structure

Seobu T&D operates a real estate-based business centered on tourist hotels, shopping malls, and tableware manufacturing and sales. In the cumulative third quarter of 2025, tourist hotels accounted for 72.8% of revenue, shopping mall operations for 16.1%, and tableware for 9.0%.

The core hotel is Seoul Dragon City in Yongsan, Seoul, with about 1,700 rooms. The shopping mall segment operates Square One in Incheon and earns revenue by applying contracted commission rates to tenant sales.

The tableware segment was consolidated in the third quarter of 2025 after the company acquired 10 million redeemable convertible preferred shares of Corelle Brands Asia Holdings for KRW 10 billion in July 2025, raising its stake to 66.67%.

Petroleum sales shrank steadily as a share of revenue and the company halted that business in the third quarter of 2025. Hana Securities put second-quarter 2026 hotel revenue at KRW 50.4 billion and hotel operating profit at KRW 17.1 billion.

Because hotels make up most of revenue, room rates and occupancy largely determine the direction of consolidated results. On competition, KB Securities pointed out that new hotel supply in Seoul is difficult to add in a short period.

According to Samsung Securities, the company is reviewing direct operation of hotels it secures after master lease contracts expire.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 135.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 169.2 billion in 2023 (up 25.0% year on year), KRW 187.5 billion in 2024 (up 10.8%), and KRW 248.3 billion in 2025 (up 32.5%), and it has grown for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 19.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 70.3 billion in 2025, and the operating margin climbed from 14.2% to 28.3%. Brokerage analysts attribute the growth mainly to hotel occupancy and room rates rising together.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders jumped from KRW 16.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 138.3 billion in 2023 (up 728.4%), a period when analysts linked the gain to rising values of held property assets.

It then fell to KRW 34.4 billion in 2024 (down 75.2%), when total net income of KRW 64.7 billion included a larger share attributable to non-controlling interests. In 2025, net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 79.1 billion (up 130.3%), while total net income was KRW 116.2 billion.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 38.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 106.1 billion in 2025. Quarterly operating profit peaked at KRW 24.9 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, up 16.7% from the prior quarter, after KRW 21.3 billion in the third quarter.

It then stood at KRW 17.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 28.5% quarter on quarter), KRW 18.3 billion in the second quarter (up 2.7%), and KRW 14.2 billion in the third quarter (down 22.4%).

Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 87.7 billion rose 32.7% from the prior quarter, but Hana Securities noted about KRW 8 billion of property tax and roughly KRW 1.5 billion of one-off labor costs tied to Sinjeong-dong development in that quarter.

In the third quarter of 2026, net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.05 billion, far below operating profit. Over the last four quarters, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 68.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The Seoul tourist hotel market is in a phase where rising foreign arrivals are lifting room demand. Hana Securities put Seobu T&D's hotel occupancy rate at about 79% in the second quarter of 2026 and average daily rate at KRW 210,000, up 7 percentage points and 9% year on year, respectively.

The same analysis put second-quarter hotel revenue at KRW 50.4 billion and operating profit at KRW 17.1 billion. In a September 2026 report, Hana Securities said a golden cross between mainland Chinese visitor demand to Korea and to Japan is emerging quickly.

KB Securities cited the difficulty of adding new hotel supply in Seoul in a short period as a driver of ADR gains. In a supply-constrained market, location and scale are seen as differentiators, which applies to the 1,700-room Yongsan property.

KB Securities, however, put the hotel segment's quarterly breakeven level at around KRW 25 billion, and given its high fixed costs, profit could fall sharply if occupancy declines. Because the hotel segment accounts for most consolidated revenue, the hotel cycle directly shapes the direction of company earnings.

The tableware business, consolidated after the Corelle acquisition, is a consumer goods operation that follows a different demand cycle from hotels.

06

Outlook

The direction of 2026 earnings can be gauged from brokerage estimates made in early 2026. In February 2026, Hana Securities maintained a buy rating and expected higher hotel occupancy and room rates to extend record results.

KB Securities expected operating leverage to keep earnings strong, given the hotel segment's quarterly breakeven level.

On development, Hana Securities expected construction of the Yongsan Najin shopping arcade and the Sinjeong-dong Seobu truck terminal site to begin between the fourth quarter of 2026 and the first quarter of 2027, with sales to follow.

If sales are completed, the same report said the development profit could be recognized from 2027 through 2031. Sale of the Nine Tree Dongdaemun hotel was planned for 2026, and Samsung Securities reported that the bid price exceeded KRW 90 billion against an acquisition cost of KRW 54 billion in 2023.

The company has said sale proceeds could support further investment through a REIT structure. In an October 2026 report, Eugene Investment & Securities pointed to growth in the Corelle business as a contributor to earnings improvement. Management is reviewing direct operation of hotels it secures after master lease contracts expire.

08

Bull factors

Occupancy and room rates rising together

Hana Securities put second-quarter 2026 hotel occupancy at about 79% and average daily rate at KRW 210,000. Operating profit reached KRW 70.3 billion in 2025, up 46.6%, with a 28.3% operating margin. Brokerages attribute this to foreign visitor demand combined with constrained hotel supply in Seoul.

Development profit as an added option

Hana Securities expects construction of the Yongsan Najin shopping arcade and Sinjeong-dong truck terminal site between the fourth quarter of 2026 and the first quarter of 2027. The same analysis says development profit could be recognized from 2027 through 2031 if sales are completed. Asset monetization and reinvestment through a REIT were also cited.

Improving balance sheet and cash generation

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 152.9% in 2022 to 134.7% in 2025. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 38.7 billion to KRW 106.1 billion over the same period, so cash generation grew alongside earnings.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 1.39 trillion, of which KRW 1.06 trillion belonged to controlling shareholders.

09

Bear factors

Slowing quarterly profit

Operating profit was KRW 14.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 33.5% from a year earlier. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 90.6% to KRW 3.05 billion in the same quarter.

Operating profit in the first quarter of 2026 also fell 28.5% from the prior quarter, so quarterly results have not improved in a steady line.

Sensitivity to demand at a concentrated hotel base

Tourist hotels accounted for 72.8% of cumulative revenue in the third quarter of 2025, which ties results closely to room rates and occupancy at a single property. Changes in foreign arrivals or the exchange rate affect hotel earnings quickly. The second quarter of 2026 also absorbed additional costs, including about KRW 8 billion of property tax.

Net income swings tied to revaluation effects

The 2023 jump in net income attributable to controlling shareholders to KRW 138.3 billion was linked to rising property values. Samsung Securities reported that a KRW 80.9 billion revaluation gain was reflected in the fourth quarter of 2025 from the year-end asset revaluation.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 75.2% in 2024, showing how revaluation effects can drive bottom-line results.

10

Risk factors

Tourism and currency demand risk

Hotel revenue is sensitive to foreign arrivals and the won exchange rate. If the recovery in mainland Chinese visitor demand slows, occupancy and room rates could decline together. High fixed costs in the hotel segment would magnify profit declines if room revenue falls.

Development timing and sales risk

Realization of development profit depends on the timing of construction and sales at the Yongsan and Sinjeong-dong sites. Delays or weaker-than-planned sales could push back the recognition of development profit beyond 2027.

The sale of the Nine Tree Dongdaemun hotel also determines how much capacity for REIT investment the company gains, depending on timing and terms.

Revaluation and accounting volatility

Year-end asset revaluation gains can add large amounts to net income in a given year, widening the gap between operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders. As in the third quarter of 2026, net income can fall sharply even while operating profit remains positive. Trend analysis therefore needs to separate operating margin from revaluation effects.

12

Overall view

Seobu T&D has grown revenue and operating profit together since 2022, supported by improving occupancy and room rates at its Yongsan hotel. Revenue of KRW 248.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 70.3 billion in 2025 were the largest figures of 2022 to 2025 on a disclosed basis, and the operating margin rose to 28.3%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders, however, swung widely, reflecting revaluation effects in 2023, a decline in 2024, and a revaluation gain in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit in 2026 moved between KRW 17.8 billion, KRW 18.3 billion, and KRW 14.2 billion, so hotel-driven volatility is visible quarter to quarter. Improved occupancy and room rates are a strength, while concentration in a single hotel-centered business is a weakness.

Development at Yongsan and Sinjeong-dong and the Nine Tree sale are still tasks whose timing has yet to be realized. The share price sits below book net asset value, and how that relationship evolves depends on realizing development profit and the stability of earnings.

The next checkpoints are the hotel operating margin and net income attributable to controlling shareholders in the 2026 annual report and the first-quarter 2027 report.
