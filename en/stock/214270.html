02

Business structure

FSN is a digital marketing group founded in 2015 that organizes its business into four pillars: Marketing, Tech, Commerce and Global.

The Marketing pillar covers advertising strategy, creative work and performance marketing, while the Tech pillar runs the in-house mobile ad network Cauly to deliver data-driven ad services.

The Commerce pillar centers on D2C commerce that combines marketing capability with a brand aggregator model, and blockchain-based new services are developed and operated through a subsidiary.

In the company's disclosure breakdown as of December 2024, the brand segment accounted for 41.21% of sales, followed by marketing at 17.36% and platform at 13.36%. As of September 2025, 26 companies were consolidated, with subsidiary networks in Southeast Asia including Thailand, Vietnam, Singapore, Taiwan and China.

Brand incubation is represented by LeMouton, and a Japanese subsidiary, LeMouton Japan Co., Ltd., was set up in April 2026. In January 2025 the company acquired the scalp-care firm Planb Bio.

Against peers such as Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide, which center on advertising agency work, FSN combines agency services with its own ad-tech platform and directly operated brands, so its business model differs.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed basis, 2025 sales were KRW 272.3 billion, up 2.5% year on year, extending the rise that began in 2024 (KRW 265.6 billion, up 51.3%) to two consecutive years of growth. Sales fell 14.8% to KRW 175.6 billion in 2023, making that the smallest year of 2022 to 2025, while 2025 was the largest.

Operating profit moved from KRW 173 million in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 5.97 billion in 2023, a turn into a deficit, then to KRW 832 million in 2024, a return to profit, and reached KRW 30.5 billion in 2025, up 3,570.2%.

The operating margin went from 0.1% in 2022 and negative 3.4% in 2023 to 0.3% in 2024 and 11.2% in 2025. Profit growth far outpaced sales growth in 2025, which suggests cost structure changes played a large role, though the specific drivers should be checked in the annual report notes and segment disclosures.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 10.7 billion in 2022, KRW 23.8 billion in 2023, KRW 8.04 billion in 2024 and KRW 18.9 billion in 2025, four consecutive years of losses, and the 2025 loss widened again from 2024.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 60.9 billion in 2024, far larger than the controlling-shareholder figure, and KRW 15.9 billion in 2025. The gap between the two figures is the non-controlling share.

Operating cash flow alternated in sign: KRW 9.96 billion in 2022, negative KRW 9.36 billion in 2023, KRW 18.5 billion in 2024 and negative KRW 269 million in 2025. The debt ratio rose every year from 2023 onward, from 77.1% in 2022 to 123.2%, 238.8% and 303.0% in 2025.

By quarter, sales fell from KRW 73.3 billion with operating profit of KRW 9.07 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 67.2 billion and KRW 4.22 billion in Q4 2025, a 53.5% quarter-on-quarter drop in operating profit, then reached KRW 59.3 billion and KRW 1.89 billion in Q1 2026 before recovering to KRW 76.7 billion and KRW 12 billion in Q2 2026.

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 66.6 billion, down 9.1% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 2.88 billion fell 68.3% year on year. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.04 billion turned into a loss compared with the prior-year quarter.

The main drivers of quarterly swings should be checked in the segment discussion and one-off item notes of the periodic reports.

05

Industry analysis

FSN operates where digital advertising, mobile ad-tech and brand commerce overlap. On the agency side, Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide are established large agencies, and kt nasmedia is a comparison point in digital ad distribution.

Unlike these peers, FSN owns the Cauly mobile ad network, which extends its scope into the ad distribution stage. Ad demand can shift with advertiser budgets and platform policy changes, which matters for a business whose quarterly sales move sharply.

The move from KRW 76.7 billion in Q2 2026 to KRW 66.6 billion in Q3 2026 illustrates that volatility. Growing commerce and brand operations appears aimed at cushioning swings in agency wins. The largest sales segment at the end of 2024 was brands, which supports this direction.

Among media and entertainment names such as Hybe and SM Entertainment, income is driven mainly by content such as music, concerts and albums, so the comparison axis differs from FSN's.

06

Outlook

FSN has pursued several expansion moves. It set up Getwell in May 2025, Bipinity in February 2026, and LeMouton Japan Co., Ltd. in April 2026. Subsidiary Boosters reported preliminary consolidated Q2 2026 sales of about KRW 61.1 billion and operating profit of about KRW 13.1 billion, a quarterly record, in July 2026.

The same announcement put first-half sales at about KRW 105.3 billion, up 9.2% year on year. First-half operating profit of about KRW 17.8 billion included one-off costs, and excluding them, adjusted operating profit was about KRW 20.6 billion, up 4.9%.

These subsidiary figures differ in period and scope from FSN's consolidated filings and should be read separately. On the financing side, the board approved a KRW 3.0 billion private convertible bond on November 4, 2026, with payment on November 12, 2026.

The proceeds are entirely for operating funds, planned at KRW 0.5 billion in 2026 and KRW 2.5 billion in 2027. The bonds cannot be converted for one year after issuance and mature on November 12, 2029.

Including outstanding convertible bonds, the total is KRW 8.55 billion, with convertible shares of 6,337,775, equal to 14.01% of the total issued shares.

Bear factors

Losses attributable to controlling shareholders persist

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders ran for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, and the last four quarters total KRW 26.9 billion in losses. In Q3 2026 the controlling-shareholder result swung to a loss of KRW 3.04 billion compared with a year earlier, and operating profit fell 68.3%. Operating-level recovery has not fully carried through to shareholder earnings.

Financial leverage burden

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 285.2 billion, above total equity of KRW 94.1 billion, and the debt ratio stands at 303.0%. Operating cash flow in 2025 was negative KRW 269 million, and it was also negative in 2023. Cash generation has not kept pace with the rise in operating profit.

Possible dilution of shareholder value

The 16th private convertible bond approved in November 2026 is worth KRW 3.0 billion. Including outstanding convertible bonds, the total is KRW 8.55 billion, with convertible shares of 6,337,775, equal to 14.01% of total issued shares. Conversion opens one year after issuance, so any change in the share count will show up in later filings.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly sales moved from KRW 67.2 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 76.7 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 66.6 billion in Q3 2026. Operating profit fell from KRW 12 billion in Q2 2026 to KRW 2.88 billion in Q3 2026, so quarter-to-quarter variance is large. Ad wins and brand sales vary by quarter, which makes it hard to read the annual trend from any single quarter.

Capital structure and cash flow

The debt ratio climbed from 77.1% in 2022 to 303.0% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 285.2 billion. Operating cash flow was negative in 2023 and 2025, so the gap between profit and cash has recurred. Rising borrowing and convertible financing can weigh on interest burden and financial flexibility.

Reading subsidiary figures and share changes

Subsidiary Boosters' preliminary Q2 2026 results are a record quarter, but they differ in period and scope from FSN's filed consolidated figures. Reading them as consolidated results could overstate the size of the earnings recovery.

Conversion of the bonds and changes in executive holdings affect the share count and control structure, so they should be tracked through periodic disclosures.
