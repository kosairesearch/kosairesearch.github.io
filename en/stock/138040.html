Steady Core Units, Investment Swings Drive Earnings

02

Business structure

Meritz Financial Group is a holding company whose main businesses are non-life insurance, financial investment and specialized credit finance.

Its core subsidiaries are Meritz Fire & Marine, which handles insurance and asset management, Meritz Securities, which handles investment trading, brokerage and collective investment, and Meritz Capital, which handles leasing, installment finance and new technology business finance.

Group earnings are made up of insurance profit, investment income from asset management, and securities corporate finance and retail fees.

For the third quarter of 2026, Meritz Fire & Marine's standalone net income was KRW 328.5 billion, down 29.2% year on year, as insurance profit rose 34.9% to KRW 404.7 billion while investment income fell 87.9% to KRW 39.4 billion, according to Shinhan Investment Corp.

Meritz Securities' consolidated net income for the same quarter was KRW 206.4 billion, up 3.2%, and its asset management and other income rose 93.7% to KRW 215.5 billion.

Daishin Securities noted in a May 2026 report that the securities unit's corporate finance-heavy model lagged peers in brokerage during bull markets, and cited the AI-based mobile trading system MOUM, scheduled for launch in the second half, as a tool for retail share gains.

On the insurance side, the company emphasizes growth centered on profitable long-term insurance, with third-quarter cumulative long-term personal insurance new contract revenue up 15.6% to KRW 105.6 billion.

Because insurance and securities are both sensitive to asset management results, the three businesses do not necessarily move in the same direction.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 67.2 trillion in 2022 to KRW 58.6 trillion in 2023 (down 12.9% year on year) and KRW 46.6 trillion in 2024 (down 20.5%).

Operating profit rose from KRW 2.89 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.93 trillion in 2023 (up 1.4%) and KRW 3.19 trillion in 2024 (up 8.7%), then fell to KRW 2.87 trillion in 2025 (down 9.9%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest for operating profit.

Operating margin was 4.3% in 2022, 5.0% in 2023 and 6.8% in 2024. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 1.39 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.04 trillion in 2023 (up 46.7%), then KRW 2.31 trillion in 2024 (up 12.9%) and KRW 2.3 trillion in 2025 (down 0.2%).

Total net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 2.13 trillion in 2022, KRW 2.13 trillion in 2023, KRW 2.33 trillion in 2024 and KRW 2.35 trillion in 2025.

The gap between the 1.4% operating profit increase and the 46.7% rise in attributable net income in 2023 suggests items outside operating profit mattered.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit went from KRW 862.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 338.9 billion in the fourth quarter (down 60.7% from the prior quarter), then recovered to KRW 854.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 152.2%) and KRW 967.6 billion in the second quarter (up 13.2%), before falling to KRW 519.9 billion in the third quarter (down 46.3% quarter on quarter and 39.7% year on year).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 654.7 billion, KRW 310.7 billion, KRW 667 billion, KRW 775.1 billion and KRW 470.7 billion across the five quarters from the third quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 2.22 trillion.

Shinhan Investment Corp. attributed the third-quarter drop to equity valuation and disposal losses of about KRW 140 billion and real estate financing provisions of KRW 29 billion.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 7.93 trillion in 2022 to -KRW 1.27 trillion in 2023, -KRW 3.92 trillion in 2024 and -KRW 6.47 trillion in 2025, staying negative in each year after 2022.

05

Industry analysis

In insurance, Meritz Fire & Marine said that increased respiratory disease claims and anticancer treatment costs reduced first-quarter 2026 insurance profit.

In a May 2026 report, Daishin Securities said the company's first-quarter loss ratio of 94.3% rose only 1.3 percentage points from the prior quarter, which it viewed as low relative to peers.

In capital markets, Kiwoom Securities noted in a November 12, 2026 report that a correction in domestic stocks and rising interest rates are creating an unfavorable backdrop. Shinhan Investment Corp. expects greater volatility in equity markets to weaken the company's standing as a defensive stock for now.

Meritz Fire & Marine's new-contract CSM conversion multiple was 12.6 times in the first quarter of 2026, 12.4 times in the second quarter and 11.7 times in the third quarter, a sequence that points to a gradual decline in the margin generated by new business.

Across the real estate project financing sector, provisions remain a variable for earnings after 2026.

Shinhan Investment Corp. noted that Meritz Fire & Marine has reflected the full balance of below-substandard project financing in provisions and reserves, while Meritz Securities has reflected 50%, and it expects related losses to fall sharply from 2027.

In securities, retail activity is becoming a growing profit base: combined brokerage and asset management net operating revenue rose 302% to KRW 92.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026, according to Shinhan.

Corporate finance was weaker, with roughly KRW 46 billion of credit loss provisions set aside in the second quarter.

06

Outlook

Meritz Financial Group's first-half 2026 total net income was KRW 1.47 trillion, up 8.3% year on year, and the Korea Financial News reported it as the record for a first half (based on the August 12, 2026 disclosure).

Second-quarter net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 775.1 billion in the same period. Shinhan Investment Corp. analyzed that, because the group returns at least 50% of consolidated net income, earnings volatility translates directly into uncertainty over the size of shareholder returns.

The same report said that if the current share price level holds, additional shareholder returns could come more through cash dividends than through share buybacks and cancellations.

Kiwoom Securities maintained its Buy rating and KRW 170,000 target in a November 12, 2026 report, and said managing risk through market volatility is important for a third-quarter recovery. Shinhan Investment Corp. maintained its Buy rating and KRW 142,000 target on November 12, 2026.

In a May 2026 report, Daishin Securities referred to reports that an acquisition of Accuon Capital and a savings bank package is under review, and said a deal would create corporate finance synergies with Meritz Capital.

The securities unit's MOUM mobile trading system was presented as scheduled for launch in the second half of 2026. On the Homeplus DIP loan, the company has said collection is secured by a guarantee from MBK Partners.

The next checkpoints are whether the acquisition decision is disclosed, the 2026 fourth-quarter and full-year preliminary results, and the confirmed figures in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Improving insurance profit

Meritz Fire & Marine's standalone insurance profit rose 34.9% to KRW 404.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026, according to Shinhan Investment Corp.

The company said at its May 14, 2026 conference call that contractual service margin stood at KRW 11.29 trillion at the end of the first quarter, up KRW 188 billion from the prior quarter.

Standalone operating profit for the fire unit rose 9% to KRW 769.9 billion in the second quarter of 2026, which Shinhan described as a record quarter. The new-contract CSM conversion multiple slipped from 12.6 times to 11.7 times, so the trend warrants monitoring.

Retail growth and asset management gains

Meritz Securities' consolidated net income rose 3.2% to KRW 206.4 billion in the third quarter of 2026, while its asset management and other income rose 93.7% to KRW 215.5 billion, according to Shinhan Investment Corp.

Combined brokerage and asset management net operating revenue rose 302% to KRW 92.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026, which the same source said shows retail becoming a growing profit base.

First-half 2026 total net income of KRW 1.47 trillion was reported as a first-half record, based on the August 12, 2026 disclosure. Credit loss provisions in corporate finance remain a burden.

Shareholder return structure

Shinhan Investment Corp. pointed out that Meritz Financial Group has a structure returning at least 50% of consolidated net income. Under this structure, larger earnings also mean a larger return amount.

The same source projected that if the current share price holds, additional returns could come more through cash dividends than through buybacks and cancellations. The possibility of more varied return tools is cited as a strength in shareholder returns.

Bear factors

Volatility in investment income

Equity valuation and disposal losses of roughly KRW 140 billion and real estate provisions of KRW 29 billion were recorded in the third quarter of 2026, according to Shinhan Investment Corp.

Third-quarter net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 470.7 billion fell short of the market consensus of KRW 634.5 billion. Kiwoom Securities said that a correction in domestic stocks and rising rates make it harder to keep the high profitability seen before. Because investment income swings widely, the direction of quarterly results can change quickly.

Real estate financing exposure

Group real estate financing exposure is KRW 29.8 trillion, with 89% senior loans and an average loan-to-value ratio of 46%, according to Shinhan Investment Corp. Reports raised concerns that additional DIP lending to Homeplus could increase risk. The company said collection is secured by a guarantee from MBK Partners.

The company's chief risk officer said on a conference call that existing loan provisions and reserves stand at KRW 240 billion.

Balance sheet and cash flow

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 124.2 trillion against total equity of KRW 11.3 trillion, giving a debt-to-equity ratio of 1,102.9%. Operating cash flow in 2025 was -KRW 6.47 trillion and stayed negative after 2023.

Shinhan Investment Corp. analyzed that swings in consolidated net income translate directly into uncertainty over the size of shareholder returns. A wider gap between earnings and cash flow would make the balance sheet a more important item to monitor.

Risk factors

Market and interest rates

A correction in stocks and rising rates are variables that move investment income and securities fee income together, according to Kiwoom Securities' November 12, 2026 report.

Because quarterly valuation gains and losses on equity assets can be large, a loss like the third quarter's can push net income below expectations. On the insurance side, higher loss ratios from items such as respiratory claims form a separate risk.

Real estate credit

Shinhan Investment Corp. expects losses from the real estate exposure to shrink from 2027 because provisions have been set aside for below-substandard project financing balances. Meritz Fire & Marine reflected the full balance and Meritz Securities 50%. If additional provisions become necessary, earnings volatility could widen again.

Regulation and affiliate matters

A KRW 64.6 billion penalty tied to a government bond primary dealer collusion case was flagged by Shinhan Investment Corp. as a potential risk. The Homeplus DIP loan is an exposure that could affect earnings depending on the outcome of the rehabilitation process.

The company stresses collection security, but progress of the rehabilitation process remains the benchmark for risk management.

12

Overall view

Meritz Financial Group's controlling-interest net profit for Q3 2026 was KRW 470.7 billion, down 28.1% year over year, and also fell short of market expectations.

The decline was driven by weaker investment results at Meritz Fire & Marine Insurance (losses on valuation and disposal of equities, and real estate finance provisions), while insurance results improved.

Among annual reported results, controlling-interest net profit peaked at KRW 2.3061 trillion in 2024 and held roughly steady at KRW 2.3004 trillion in 2025. Operating profit fell to KRW 2.8727 trillion in 2025, but the operating margin had risen to 6.8% in 2024.

In Q1 and Q2 2026, controlling-interest net profit recovered to KRW 667.0 billion and KRW 775.1 billion, then declined again in Q3.

Bullish factors cited include growth in the core insurance business and securities retail, and a structure that returns at least 50% of consolidated net profit to shareholders, while bearish factors cited include equity market volatility, real estate finance exposure of KRW 29.8 trillion, a debt-to-equity ratio of 1,102.9%, and operating cash flow of KRW -6.472 trillion.

In their reports dated November 12, 2026, Kiwoom Securities and Shinhan Investment Corp. set target prices of KRW 170,000 and KRW 142,000, respectively. The next checkpoints are the finalized figures in the 2026 annual report and Q1 2027 results.
