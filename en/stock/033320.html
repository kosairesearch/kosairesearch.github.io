02

Business structure

JCH Systems distributes PC components and peripherals, including graphics cards, motherboards, monitors and integrated cabling solutions. According to FnGuide, the computer segment accounted for 94.66% of revenue and the network segment for 5.65% as of December 2025.

The same data shows that graphics cards and motherboards, as well as monitors, were 100% domestic sales in December 2025. The company operates as an official partner of NVIDIA and DJI, and serves as an official supplier of GIGABYTE graphics cards and motherboards.

Corporate analysis platform Jasoseol said the company holds a share in the 20% range in domestic motherboard and graphics card sales. Its customers fall into consumer sales and corporate or OEM supply, and the company names expanding corporate and new OEM supply as a sales priority.

It also sells finished products, including UDEA-branded monitors and Jaeple-branded diffusion AI PCs and multi-GPU AI servers, which widens its business beyond distribution margins. In drones, it is expanding drone distribution and video surveillance solutions as the official DJI distributor.

Because PC component distribution depends on manufacturers' supply volumes and pricing, launch timing from a few key brands can move sales significantly.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 200.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 211.6 billion in 2023 (up 5.5%), KRW 263.9 billion in 2024 (up 24.7%) and KRW 313.8 billion in 2025 (up 18.9%). Sales have grown for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 is the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 2.37 billion in 2022 and a KRW 5.58 billion loss in 2023, which widened the deficit, to an operating profit of KRW 3.64 billion in 2024, a turn to profit, and KRW 7.7 billion in 2025, up 111.4%.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 286 million in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 2.7 billion in 2023, a swing back into losses, then KRW 1.86 billion in 2024, a return to profit, and KRW 8.73 billion in 2025, up 370.4%.

Net income including non-controlling interests, which is a different figure, was KRW 9.3 billion in 2025. Operating margin moved from -1.2% in 2022 and -2.6% in 2023 to 1.4% in 2024 and 2.5% in 2025. The company attributed the improvement to graphics-card-centred sales growth and lower SG&A expenses.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 80.5 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 92.3 billion in Q4 2025 (up 14.6% quarter on quarter), then KRW 74.3 billion in Q1 2026 (down 19.5%), KRW 76 billion in Q2 2026 (up 2.3%) and KRW 70.7 billion in Q3 2026 (down 7.0% quarter on quarter and down 12.2% year on year).

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 841 million in Q3 2025, KRW 2.66 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 3.58 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 499 million in Q2 2026 (down 86.1%) and KRW 2.6 billion in Q3 2026 (up 209.0% year on year).

In Q3 2026, sales fell from a year earlier while operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.77 billion rose. Operating cash flow was -KRW 31.9 billion in 2022, KRW 26 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.74 billion in 2025, so earnings and cash generation did not move in the same direction.

05

Industry analysis

Graphics card demand is being led by mid-to-high-end products as generative AI spreads and local AI adoption grows, according to corporate information platform WISEreport, which reflected this in its comment on the company's results.

FnGuide cites the Windows 11 upgrade cycle and rising AI PC demand as drivers of graphics card and motherboard sales. The company says collaboration with GPU-related industries lifted sales of high-end products in 2025.

PC component distribution tends to see demand concentrate around new product launches and operating system upgrades, so the business cycle can shift from quarter to quarter. Whether the 2026 quarterly swings reflect demand or supply factors is a question that the company's quarterly explanations need to resolve.

The market position rests on Jasoseol's description of a share in the 20% range in domestic motherboard and graphics card sales. Hyundai Department Store and E-Mart are also classified under KOSDAQ distribution and consumer goods, but their demand structure differs, so direct comparison of indicators is limited.

In drones, the company is expanding video surveillance solutions as the official DJI distributor, but the scale of any link to defense and security demand does not yet appear in disclosed figures.

06

Outlook

The company launched GIGABYTE G27Q26 as an official supplier in September 2026, and on November 13, 2026 it launched two new UDEA IPS monitors in 27-inch and 24-inch sizes for office and home entertainment use.

The company says the new models pair 100 to 120Hz refresh rates and IPS panels at a price in the low hundreds of thousands of won, targeting both corporate bulk purchases and home entertainment consumers.

In its AI lineup, the representative products are the Jaeple diffusion AI PC equipped with RTX 5090 and the Private LLM AI PC for closed-network environments. On February 10, 2026, the company announced the launch of QVP, an on-premise AI investment analysis solution based on RTX 5090.

In drones, it is pursuing a converged video platform that combines drone distribution, video surveillance and unmanned aircraft-based security solutions. However, how much the new products and new businesses contribute to quarterly sales and operating profit is not separately visible in disclosed figures.

The year-end cash dividend for fiscal 2025 was approved at the shareholders' meeting on March 26, 2026. Full-year 2026 results will be fixed when the 2026 annual report is filed. These two items are the official checkpoints for future earnings and shareholder returns.

Bull factors

Rising AI PC and graphics card demand

According to WISEreport, graphics card sales are being driven by mid-to-high-end products as generative AI spreads and local AI adoption grows. FnGuide points to the Windows 11 upgrade and rising AI PC demand as drivers of graphics card and motherboard sales.

The 18.9% sales increase in 2025 is consistent with this trend. The company is widening its AI lineup with the RTX 5090-equipped diffusion AI PC and the Private LLM AI PC for closed networks.

Operating profit turn and lower SG&A

Operating profit turned to KRW 3.64 billion in 2024 and rose 111.4% to KRW 7.7 billion in 2025. The company explained the increase by higher graphics-card-centred sales and lower SG&A expenses. Operating margin moved from -1.2% in 2022 to 2.5% in 2025.

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.6 billion was up 209.0% year on year, so the quarterly profit base has also grown.

Financial capacity and shareholder return record

The debt ratio was 34.5% at end-2025, with total equity of KRW 120.9 billion, of which KRW 115.9 billion belongs to controlling shareholders. The debt ratio has trended down from 42.6% in 2022.

A year-end cash dividend for fiscal 2025 was approved at the March 26, 2026 shareholders' meeting, continuing the dividend record. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 11.2 billion.

09

Bear factors

Quarterly sales volatility

Q1 2026 sales of KRW 74.3 billion fell 19.5% from the prior quarter. Q3 2026 sales of KRW 70.7 billion fell 7.0% quarter on quarter and 12.2% year on year. Q2 2026 sales rose 2.3% to KRW 76 billion, but operating profit fell 86.1% to KRW 499 million. Whether profit can be held up in quarters when sales decline remains an open question.

Thin operating margin and cash flow

Operating margin improved to 2.5% in 2025, but the company posted operating losses in 2022 and 2023. The 2023 operating loss of KRW 5.58 billion widened the deficit. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 26 billion in 2024 to KRW 8.74 billion in 2025, so earnings growth and cash generation did not track each other.

Supplier and product concentration

With the computer segment at 94.66% of revenue, dependence on graphics cards and motherboards is high. The business relies on supply relationships with brands such as NVIDIA and GIGABYTE. Changes in manufacturers' product launches or supply policies could shift sales quickly. New businesses such as drones and AI servers do not yet show a clear scale in disclosed results.

10

Risk factors

Supply chain and brand dependence

The company operates as an official NVIDIA partner and GIGABYTE supplier, so manufacturers' supply volumes are a precondition for sales. Inventory adjustments or product gaps at a key brand can cut quarterly sales quickly. The sales swings in Q2 and Q3 2026 illustrate this exposure.

Currency and inventory risk

A distribution business is sensitive to exchange rates and overseas manufacturers' pricing in its procurement costs. If inventory builds up, price cuts can follow and compress margins. With operating margin in the low single digits, cost changes have a relatively large effect on profit.

Uncertainty in new businesses

New businesses including drones and video surveillance, AI servers and UDEA monitors are expanding, but their sales contribution is not visible in disclosures. As these businesses grow, the margin structure could differ from today. With quarterly results this volatile, their profit contribution is difficult to gauge separately.

Overall view

JCH Systems is a KOSDAQ-listed company whose main business is distributing PC components such as graphics cards and motherboards, and its computer segment accounted for 94.66% of revenue as of December 2025.

Operating results moved from losses in 2022 and 2023 to a turn to profit in 2024 and KRW 7.7 billion in 2025, which the company attributed to graphics-card-centred sales growth and lower SG&A.
