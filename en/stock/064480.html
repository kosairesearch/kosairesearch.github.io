Report overview

Orders Keep Coming, but Quarterly Profit Swings

BRIDGETECCorp's third-quarter 2026 revenue and operating profit both rose year on year, and large BC Card-related contracts followed in November, yet 2025 full-year operating profit was only KRW 310 million.

02

Business structure

BRIDGETECCorp is a software company founded in 1995 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2008, whose core business is building call-center infrastructure.

The revenue mix shown on the company profile page is 72.25 percent call-center solutions, 18.16 percent maintenance, 6.43 percent IP-based wired and wireless multimedia services, and 3.16 percent cloud call-center services.

New build-out orders form the main axis of results, with maintenance revenue supporting it, so quarterly sales tend to shift with the timing of orders.

Its business areas fall into four groups: contact-center solutions such as IPCC, voice and video value-added services for telecom carriers, AI solutions based on speech recognition, and cloud-based contact-center services.

In 2026 the company launched TelloVoice, a cloud voice AI platform in which AI agents handle customer calls. In the cloud segment it obtained CSAP security certification for public cloud and has offered IPRON SaaS.

Financial institutions are the core customer group, and press reports state that 15 of 21 Tier-1 banks in Korea use its solutions.

The third-quarter 2026 quarterly report states that its special-type value-added telecom business, including secure-number call services, has completed development and service operations and is now in customer marketing.

In competition, large IT services firms such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS overlap with specialized contact-center vendors. The company highlights its in-house development and integrated supply of core solutions as its strength.

However, all three contracts disclosed in November 2026 name KT as the counterparty with BC Card as the end customer, so dependence on a single customer group is high.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue and profit moving in different directions. Revenue was KRW 58.9 billion in 2022, the largest year in the disclosed period, then fell to KRW 53.5 billion in 2023 (down 9.1% year on year) and KRW 43.6 billion in 2024 (down 18.6%), making 2024 the smallest year.

Revenue rebounded to KRW 49.7 billion in 2025, up 14.0%. Operating profit was KRW 3.92 billion in 2022 and KRW 4.16 billion in 2023 (up 6.2%), but turned into an operating loss of KRW 3.25 billion in 2024.

The operating margin fell from 6.7 percent in 2022 and 7.8 percent in 2023 to -7.5 percent in 2024, then recovered to 0.6 percent in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 2.76 billion in 2024 before turning to a profit of KRW 513 million in 2025, while net income including non-controlling interests also reached KRW 513 million in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 turnaround to profit improvement from higher sales and growth in new service revenue. The full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 310 million was smaller than the fourth-quarter 2025 figure of KRW 1.99 billion, which shows how uneven profit was across quarters within the same year.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 12.2 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 14.3 billion in the fourth quarter (up 16.7% from the prior quarter), then fell to KRW 11.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 21.5%), held at KRW 11.2 billion in the second quarter, and reached KRW 14.1 billion in the third quarter (up 26.4% from the prior quarter).

Operating profit peaked at KRW 1.99 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, dropped to KRW 347 million in the first quarter of 2026, then recovered to KRW 977 million in the second quarter and KRW 798 million in the third.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 4.27 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW -5.65 billion in 2024 and KRW -3.93 billion in 2025, a signal that differs from the profit trend.

At the end of 2025 the debt ratio was 33.2 percent, down from 45.0 percent in 2024, with total equity of KRW 46.7 billion and total liabilities of KRW 15.5 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Press coverage describes the contact-center industry shifting from generative AI toward Agentic AI, in which AI performs actual work. In this shift, build-out businesses centered on IPCC need to expand into AICC and cloud-based AI services.

Press reports say BRIDGETECCorp is extending from IPCC into AICC and cloud AI services, drawing on more than 30 years of build-out experience. Some reports also describe it as one of the largest firms in Korea's contact-center business.

Build-out projects see orders cluster in periods tied to IT investment schedules at financial institutions and telecom carriers. A 2023 report by the Korea IR Review Center noted that orders recognized as revenue in the second half of the year are typical.

Because the contract disclosed in November 2026 runs through July 2027, revenue recognition may spread across several quarters. Compared with large IT services firms such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS, BRIDGETECCorp is a smaller company specialized in contact-center solutions.

The main variables in its end market are the pace of IT investment at financial institutions and the speed of voice AI adoption.

Outlook

The contracts confirmed through disclosure were concentrated in November 2026.

The BC Card intelligent network and corporate telephony renewal facility supply, together with the 2026 BC Card dedicated network materials supply, with KT as the counterparty, carries a contract value of KRW 3,928,702,000 excluding VAT, equal to 7.9 percent of 2025 revenue, and runs through January 31, 2027.

The BC Card call-center consultation system rebuild, signed on November 11, 2026 with KT as the counterparty, carries a value of KRW 5,259,000,000, equal to 10.6 percent of 2025 revenue, and runs from November 12, 2026 to July 31, 2027.

Under this contract, 40 percent is paid at signing and 60 percent after the project passes acceptance inspection, with in-house and outsourced production used in parallel. The company has said contract amounts and periods may change as the work progresses.

The corporate value-up plan decided on March 26, 2026 targets shareholder returns including dividends, maintaining the R&D share of sales, and continuing debt-free management.

In February 2026 the company disposed of 362,046 treasury shares through an off-hours block trade to secure operating funds and improve its financial structure.

On the product side, TelloVoice, launched in 2026, handles AI agent phone interactions, and the special-type value-added telecom business is in customer marketing.

The next checkpoints are the quarterly revenue scale these contracts produce and the full-year operating margin and operating cash flow reported in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Large BC Card-related contracts disclosed back to back

The facility and materials supply contract disclosed on November 6, 2026 is worth KRW 3,928,702,000, equal to 7.9 percent of 2025 revenue. The call-center rebuild contract disclosed on November 11 is worth KRW 5,259,000,000, equal to 10.6 percent of revenue, and both name KT as the counterparty. Bulls point to orders of this size as support for a meaningful share of annual revenue.

Quarterly profit recovered from the first-quarter low

Revenue and operating profit both grew, and third-quarter profit rose sharply from a year earlier. Operating profit of KRW 977 million in the second quarter and KRW 798 million in the third has stayed above the KRW 347 million of the first quarter for two straight quarters. Bulls read this alongside the 2025 return to profit as a recovery in earnings capacity from losses.

Shareholder returns and a debt-free stance

The value-up plan disclosed in March 2026 targets shareholder returns including dividends, continued R&D investment, and continued debt-free management. The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 33.2 percent, down from 45.0 percent in 2024.

Bulls cite the debt-free stance and the dividend record since the 2008 listing as grounds for shareholder returns.

Bear factors

Volatility in annual earnings

Operating profit went from KRW 3.92 billion in 2022 and KRW 4.16 billion in 2023 to an operating loss of KRW 3.25 billion in 2024, and was KRW 310 million in 2025. The 0.6 percent operating margin in 2025 leaves little profit relative to revenue. Bears argue it is too early to tell whether the one-year return to profit will hold.

Negative operating cash flow

Cash did not come in even in 2025, when profit turned positive, which can signal a gap between reported earnings and cash. The payment structure, with 60 percent due after acceptance inspection, is also cited as a source of cash-flow swings.

Quarterly swings and customer concentration

Operating profit fell 82.6 percent from the prior quarter to KRW 347 million in the first quarter of 2026 and fell another 18.3 percent in the third quarter. Third-quarter revenue rose 26.4 percent from the prior quarter, yet profit declined, so revenue and profit did not move together.

With KT as the counterparty in all three November contracts, the reading of high dependence on a single customer group is possible.

10

Risk factors

Order timing and contract terms

Revenue varies sharply by quarter depending on when orders are concentrated. The company has said contract amounts and periods may change as the work progresses.

The BC Card call-center rebuild has different contractual and disclosed end dates, so delays in inspection or payment could push back revenue and cash recognition.

Competition in the shift to AI

As the contact-center market shifts toward Agentic AI, build-out revenue centered on IPCC could decline. The timing of when TelloVoice and cloud AI services show up in annual results is uncertain. Competition with large IT services firms such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS also continues.

Treasury share disposal and float expansion

In February 2026 the company disposed of 362,046 treasury shares through an off-hours block trade, citing operating funds and financial structure. The disposal increased the number of shares in circulation, although the company judged the impact limited. Per-share figures should be read with this increase in share count in mind.

12

Overall view

BRIDGETECCorp's results appear to have improved, with a return to annual profit in 2025 and year-on-year increases in third-quarter 2026 revenue and operating profit. However, the operating margin was only 0.6 percent in 2025 after -7.5 percent in 2024, and operating cash flow was negative in both 2024 and 2025.

Quarterly operating profit peaked at KRW 1.99 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, fell to KRW 347 million in the first quarter of 2026, and recovered to KRW 977 million and KRW 798 million in the second and third quarters, so the swings remain large.

The two large BC Card-related contracts disclosed in November equal 7.9 percent and 10.6 percent of 2025 revenue, and the longer one runs through July 2027.

Bulls cite order growth, the debt-free stance and shareholder-return plan, and the return to profit, while bears cite thin margins and cash flow, customer concentration, and possible changes to contract terms.

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 33.2 percent, lower than in 2024, and all KRW 46.7 billion of total equity belongs to controlling shareholders. Measured against net assets, the share price sits below book value.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin and operating cash flow in the 2026 annual report, the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report, and the inspection and payment timing of the BC Card contracts.
