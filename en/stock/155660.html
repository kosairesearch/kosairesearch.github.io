Business structure

DSR is a primary non-ferrous metal manufacturer whose main products are synthetic fiber ropes and stainless steel wire. The company was listed on the KOSPI market in May 2013.

Its operations are split between the Korea business and overseas subsidiaries, and DSR VINA, the Vietnamese subsidiary, makes wire ropes and synthetic fiber ropes and was established in November 2016.

By business segment, the Korea business made up 60% of sales on a Q2 2026 basis, the United States business 16%, the Vietnam business 13% and the China business 11%.

Taken together, the three overseas segments in the United States, Vietnam and China account for 40%, so exchange rates and local demand affect overall sales. The products fall into rope and wire categories, and order flow shifts with demand for industrial materials.

On the customer side, the company's 2026 corporate value-up plan set out goals of expanding its customer base, developing new products and widening the range of customer solutions it offers. This reads as an effort to widen its sales channels beyond existing customers and grow its product range. Within the sector, the company is compared with wire-product makers such as Kiswire.
