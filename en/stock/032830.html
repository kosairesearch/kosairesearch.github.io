Samsung Life: Investment Income Carries Earnings

Samsung Life's profit growth through the third quarter of 2026 has come mainly from investment income and dividend receipts, while weaker insurance profit and volatility linked to its Samsung Electronics stake remain open issues.

  1. 1

    On a disclosed basis, profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 1.9 trillion in 2023, then rose to KRW 2.11 trillion in 2024 and KRW 2.3 trillion in 2025, two straight years of growth.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Samsung Life Insurance is centered on life insurance, and insurance service income and investment income are its two main profit sources.

New contractual service margin (CSM) for January to September 2026 was KRW 2.63 trillion, split into health insurance at KRW 1.58 trillion, whole life insurance at KRW 848.0 billion and financial products at KRW 201.0 billion.

The health insurance share of new CSM by quarter fell from 66% in the first quarter of 2026 to 63% in the second and 52% in the third, as the product mix broadened toward whole life and financial insurance.

Its sales base is its exclusive channel, and exclusive planners stood at 45,446 at the end of the third quarter of 2026, up 3,350 or 8.0% from a year earlier. Exclusive FC channel accounted for 76.5% of cumulative new CSM.

In an August 2026 report, Daishin Securities said Samsung Life has the strongest exclusive channel competitiveness among domestic insurers.

On the investment side, cumulative dividend income for January to September 2026 rose 12.8% to KRW 1.49 trillion, and profit from subsidiaries and consolidation effects rose 37.2% to KRW 1.60 trillion.

In an August 2026 report, NH Investment & Securities said the value of the Samsung Electronics stake accounts for most of Samsung Life's corporate value. As a result, earnings and the share price track Samsung Electronics' results and share price. The cumulative figures in this section are based on results announced on November 12, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, insurance revenue rose from KRW 7.78 trillion in 2022 to KRW 8.54 trillion in 2023 (up 9.7% year on year), KRW 9.01 trillion in 2024 (up 5.6%) and KRW 9.72 trillion in 2025 (up 7.8%), growing three years in a row from 2023 to 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 2.18 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.58 trillion in 2025, also growing three years in a row from 2023, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 1.9 trillion in 2023 (down 12.7% year on year), then rose to KRW 2.11 trillion in 2024 (up 11.2%) and KRW 2.3 trillion in 2025 (up 9.3%), two years of growth.

Net profit including non-controlling interests ran at KRW 2.31 trillion, KRW 2.03 trillion, KRW 2.26 trillion and KRW 2.45 trillion from 2022 to 2025, a different path from profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 853.7% in 2024 to 440.9% in 2025, and operating cash flow rose from KRW 880.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.26 trillion in 2025. Among quarters, operating profit dropped to KRW 95.3 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, down 88.3% from the prior quarter.

In a February 2026 report, Samsung Securities said fourth-quarter profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell short of consensus, citing year-end expense increases and losses on onerous contracts from actuarial assumption changes.

Operating profit rebounded to KRW 1.36 trillion in the first quarter of 2026, up 1,325.3% from the prior quarter, then fell to KRW 622.6 billion in the second quarter, down 54.1%.

For the second quarter's profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 690.0 billion (down 42.7% from the prior quarter), brokers cited losses on variable insurance hedging and a wider gap between expected and actual insurance claims paid.

Operating profit recovered to KRW 937.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 50.6% from the prior quarter and up 14.9% from a year earlier, and insurance service income in the third quarter rose 102.4% year on year. However, third-quarter investment income fell 21.9% year on year.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 to third quarter of 2026) totaled KRW 2.87 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

The life insurance cycle is shaped by interest rate levels, new business sales trends, and the valuation of equity holdings, which together drive large swings in earnings. In an August 2026 report, Daishin Securities said new contract sales across the industry were somewhat weak in the second quarter of 2026.

The same report said Samsung Life can build its preferred portfolio through its exclusive channel and could significantly expand sales of whole life policies, whose margins have been rising.

New bond investment in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 5.1 trillion, up from KRW 3.4 trillion in the first quarter, and the yield on new bond investment was 4.48% in the third quarter, above the 2.76% average yield of the existing bond portfolio.

The company said it runs its sales strategy flexibly in a high-rate environment, weighing profitability alongside customer demand. Higher rates also pushed up the cost of equity used in valuation.

In August 2026, Samsung Securities raised the cost of equity in its target price model from 6.9% to 8.2%, citing market volatility, higher interest rates and a higher market risk premium.

Compared with peers, the company's differentiators are presented as its expanded exclusive planner network and its focus on health insurance sales.

06

Outlook

The company has said it will expand whole life and financial insurance sales in a high-rate environment while focusing on health insurance sales, claims payments and policy lapse management.

Whether the KRW 14.3 trillion CSM balance at the end of September 2026 translates into insurance profit over time is the next point to watch. On the value-up program, iM Securities expected it to be announced in early 2027.

In an August 2026 report, NH Investment & Securities said a value-up policy that shareholders find convincing, and its implementation, are needed. How the special dividend from Samsung Electronics will be used for shareholder returns is presented as still uncertain.

In an August 2026 report, Hana Securities expected that if the special dividend funds were distributed over three to five years, they could be added to annual dividends. iM Securities said that if the 50% medium-term payout target includes special dividends and gains on stake disposals, the discount applied to the value of the stake could narrow.

The fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results will show whether insurance profit recovery continues and how the gap between expected and actual claims develops. The 2026 annual report is the next regular filing and is due by the end of March 2027.

08

Bull factors

Rising investment income and dividend receipts

Cumulative investment income for January to September 2026 rose 40.0% to KRW 2.40 trillion, and dividend income rose 12.8% to KRW 1.49 trillion. Profit from subsidiaries and consolidation effects also expanded 37.2% to KRW 1.60 trillion.

Management said the third-quarter yield on new bond investment was 4.48%, well above the 2.76% average yield of the existing portfolio.

New CSM growth and a shifting product mix

Cumulative new CSM for January to September 2026 rose 14.3% to KRW 2.63 trillion. The CSM balance at the end of September reached KRW 14.3 trillion, up KRW 1.1 trillion from the start of the year. Hana Securities said profitability improved across product lines including health and death protection insurance.

Capital strength and shareholder return expectations

The K-ICS ratio at the end of September 2026 was 209%, and the basic capital ratio was 176%. Both are well above the regulatory guideline of 130%.

Hana Securities pointed to the possibility that Samsung Electronics' special dividend funds could be distributed to Samsung Life, and that expectation was cited as a basis for its target price increase.

09

Bear factors

Lower insurance profit and weak second quarter

Cumulative insurance service income for January to September 2026 was KRW 1.06 trillion, down 2.8% year on year. The company attributed the decline partly to higher losses on CSM.

In the second quarter of 2026, losses on variable insurance hedging and a worse gap between expected and actual claims weighed on profit, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 42.7% to KRW 690.0 billion.

Lower new business multiple and a smaller health share

The new business multiple in the third quarter of 2026 was 11.6x, lower than 12.5x in the second quarter but higher than 10.9x in the first. Management said refinements to actuarial assumptions and changes in product mix affected the figure. The health insurance share of new CSM fell from 66% in the first quarter of 2026 to 52% in the third.

Volatility tied to the Samsung Electronics stake

Brokers have noted that the share price has become more volatile because it is linked to affiliate share prices, especially Samsung Electronics. In August 2026, Samsung Securities cut its target price from KRW 400,000 to KRW 350,000 and raised its cost of equity from 6.9% to 8.2%.

NH Investment & Securities said Samsung Electronics' share price moves are directly reflected in Samsung Life's share price under the current structure.

10

Risk factors

Interest rates and investment valuation gains and losses

Although the yield on new bond investment has risen, interest rate movements can swing investment income and valuation gains and losses.

In the third quarter of 2026, general account investment income fell 64.0% year on year, and valuation gains on general insurance turned into a KRW 42.0 billion loss from a KRW 28.0 billion gain a year earlier.

Market corrections and stake value swings

Swings in the Samsung Electronics stake and in the broader market affect both capital and investment income at the same time. In the third quarter of 2026, CSM profit fell 8.8% to KRW 968.0 billion, reflecting a market correction and a base effect from a property sale gain a year earlier. Several brokers cited lower stake values as a reason for cutting target prices.

Capital ratio and regulatory benchmarks

The K-ICS ratio of 209% at the end of September 2026 is above the regulatory guideline of 130%. However, rising interest rates and a higher market risk premium have raised the cost of equity used in valuation, so the volatility of the capital ratio also needs monitoring. Because the share price reflects the capital base to a large extent, the trend in capital metrics matters.

12

Overall view

On a disclosed basis, insurance revenue and operating profit both grew for three straight years from 2023, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders grew for two straight years after its 2023 low. Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year for all three of these measures.

For January to September 2026, investment income growth of 40.0% and higher dividend income lifted profit attributable to controlling shareholders, while insurance service income fell 2.8% year on year.

New CSM rose 14.3% and the end-September balance reached KRW 14.3 trillion, but the third-quarter new business multiple was lower than in the second quarter. Equity attributable to the capital base reached KRW 64.8 trillion at the end of 2025, and the K-ICS ratio was 209% at the end of September 2026.

Brokerage target prices, which reflect changes in the value of the Samsung Electronics stake, were presented at KRW 440,000 by Daishin Securities, KRW 390,000 by NH Investment & Securities and KRW 370,000 by Hana Securities, all in August 2026, and iM Securities said on November 13, 2026 that it lowered its target to KRW 294,000.

Strengths include investment income and possible dividend expansion, while weaknesses include softer insurance profit and volatility linked to the Samsung Electronics stake. The next points to confirm are the fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results, the 2026 annual report and whether a value-up policy is announced.
