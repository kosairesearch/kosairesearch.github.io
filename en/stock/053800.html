02

Business structure

Ahnlab is an integrated security company founded in 1995 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2001. Its core offerings are enterprise endpoint detection and response (EDR), managed detection and response (MDR), network security, and zero-trust related products and services.

At its Q3 2026 results announcement on October 28, 2026, the company said its network security business grew more than triple digits year on year, with rising EDR and MDR demand feeding into revenue growth.

In the domestic market, EDR and XDR platform-based endpoint security references are expanding in public institutions and finance. The global business centers on Rakeen, a joint venture formed in 2024 with the Saudi security firm SITE.

The company said global business again posted double-digit growth in Q3 2026 after doing so in the prior quarter. It also has subsidiaries in Japan and China. Within the IT and software sector, Samsung SDS and Axgate are common comparison points.

Enterprise security demand is contested where specialist security firms and large IT services companies overlap.

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated revenue in 2025 was KRW 267.7 billion, up 2.7% year on year, and it has risen for three consecutive years since 2023. Of 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year.

Operating profit fell from KRW 27 billion in 2022 to KRW 26.4 billion in 2023, a 2.1% decline, then rose to KRW 27.7 billion in 2024 (up 4.8%) and KRW 33.3 billion in 2025 (up 20.2%), growing for two consecutive years from 2024.

The operating margin dropped from 11.8% in 2022 to 11.0% in 2023 and 10.6% in 2024, then recovered to 12.4% in 2025. Revenue grew only 2.7% while operating profit grew 20.2%, so margin improvement drove most of the profit gain.

Net profit attributable to shareholders rose from KRW 14.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 35.8 billion in 2023 (up 152.8%), fell to KRW 33.7 billion in 2024 (down 6.0%), and reached KRW 54.3 billion in 2025 (up 61.2%), the largest year.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 51.5 billion in 2025, which differs from the shareholder figure, so the two should be read separately. Quarterly, Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 83.6 billion rose 29.6% from the prior quarter and operating profit of KRW 20.9 billion rose 165.1%.

Q1 2026 marked the low point, with revenue of KRW 59.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.93 billion. Operating profit then grew for two straight quarters, reaching KRW 5.38 billion in Q2 and KRW 10.9 billion in Q3.

However, net profit attributable to shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 8.58 billion, down 36.8% year on year, so operating and net profit trends diverge. The sum of net profit attributable to shareholders over the latest four quarters is KRW 44.8 billion.

The nine-month 2026 cumulative revenue of KRW 209.8 billion (up 14%) and operating profit of KRW 18.2 billion (up 47.3%) are preliminary figures announced on October 28, 2026.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for integrated enterprise security is expanding as AI-driven attacks grow more sophisticated and ransomware threats increase. A corporate monitoring report describes this demand as having driven rising security solution sales at Ahnlab.

At its first-half 2026 results announcement on July 29, 2026, the company said its involvement in EDR, XTG, and N²SF and zero-trust related products had been increasing. Growing EDR and XDR platform references in public institutions and finance also broaden its market position.

Overseas, it operates in the Middle East through the Saudi joint venture Rakeen. Within the IT and software sector it is compared with Samsung SDS and Axgate. In cycle terms, the environment reads as a phase of expanding security demand, but whether that continues depends on large customers' IT investment plans.

As cloud and AI adoption widen the attack surface, the speed of technical response shapes product competitiveness. Timing of security purchases follows enterprise IT budget priorities, so quarterly revenue can be uneven.

08

Bull factors

Margin recovery

The operating margin rose to 12.4% in 2025 from lows of 11.0% in 2023 and 10.6% in 2024. Operating profit growth of 20.2% far exceeded revenue growth of 2.7%, showing a better earnings structure. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 10.9 billion, up 38.7% year on year, shows the recovery continuing on a quarterly basis.

Overseas growth

The company said global business posted double-digit growth for two consecutive quarters through Q3 2026. The Saudi joint venture Rakeen, formed in 2024, has been cited as the axis of overseas revenue. If overseas revenue grows alongside domestic public and financial demand, the business portfolio broadens.

Structural security demand

Market commentary points to growing enterprise demand for integrated security as AI-driven attacks and ransomware threats increase. Ahnlab is adding references in public institutions and finance with platform-type products such as EDR, MDR, and XDR. The company also said its involvement in zero-trust and national network security related products is rising.

09

Bear factors

Net profit decline

Operating profit rose in Q3 2026, but net profit attributable to shareholders fell 36.8% year on year to KRW 8.58 billion. Net profit attributable to shareholders also fell 6.0% in 2024, so profit levels at different stages have diverged before. The source of the gap between operating and net profit needs to be checked in the periodic reports.

Dependence on the joint venture

Rakeen anchors global revenue growth, so dependence on a single joint venture is high. If overseas performance slows, overall revenue growth could be directly affected. Whether domestic business growth alone can support total earnings is not yet clear.

Quarterly volatility

Q1 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.93 billion fell 90.8% from the prior quarter. Revenue rose 29.6% in Q4 2025 to KRW 83.6 billion, then fell 29.4% to KRW 59.1 billion in Q1 2026, showing large quarterly swings. Even with an improving annual trend, the size of quarterly moves can make it harder to judge the direction.

10

Risk factors

Joint venture dependence

Because overseas growth relies on the joint venture Rakeen, changes in local operating conditions or the partnership structure would affect growth. The weight of this risk increases as overseas revenue becomes a larger share. Growing the domestic base alongside is the way to reduce it.

Technology and competition

AI-driven attacks and defense technologies are changing quickly, so maintaining product competitiveness requires continued R&D spending. Intensifying competition from large IT services firms and specialist security companies could pressure pricing and share. The effect on profitability will show up in future quarterly operating margins.

Timing of demand

Public procurement and large customers' IT budget execution tend to cluster in certain periods, which can make quarterly results volatile. This can appear as quarter-to-quarter swings, like the Q1 2026 low. Reading results on an annual basis is the starting point for interpreting this risk.

12

Overall view

Ahnlab posted its highest profitability year of 2022 to 2025 in 2025, with operating profit of KRW 33.3 billion (up 20.2%) and a 12.4% operating margin.

In 2026, quarterly operating profit grew in Q2 and Q3 after the Q1 low, and nine-month cumulative operating profit of KRW 18.2 billion (up 47.3%) was announced as preliminary on October 28, 2026.

However, net profit attributable to shareholders in Q3 2026 fell 36.8% year on year to KRW 8.58 billion, so the profit levels diverge. Growth drivers are network security, EDR and MDR demand, and overseas business centered on Rakeen.

Weak points are dependence on the joint venture, quarterly earnings volatility, and technology and competition changes. Market capitalization sits in a range with a large premium to net assets, and with a low dividend yield, valuation leans more on earnings growth expectations.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 results. This report does not include investment opinions, buy or sell recommendations, or target prices.
