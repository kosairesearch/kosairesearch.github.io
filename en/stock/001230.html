Quarterly Operating Profit Rebounds, Equity-Income Reliance Remains

Operating profit has risen for three straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026, but holding-company net profit attributable to shareholders still swings with equity-method investment gains and losses.

02

Business structure

Dongkuk Holdings is the holding company of the Dongkuk Steel Mill group and says it acts as the control tower for group strategy. Its main affiliates are Dongkuk Steel Mill, which produces bar and section steel and heavy plates, and Dongkuk Coated Metal, which makes coated steel products such as color-coated sheets.

Both are equity-method investees, so their results reach the holding company's books as equity-method gains or losses. Because of this structure, the company attributed the decline in 2025 profit mainly to equity-method losses from affiliates hit by weaker steel market conditions.

Dongkuk Steel Mill's key demand sources are large industrial projects such as semiconductor plants and AI data centers, while its heavy plate business is tied to shipbuilding steel demand and overseas sales, according to its Q3 2026 preliminary results.

Dongkuk Coated Metal exports about 70% of its output, which exposes it to exchange rates, trade protectionism and raw material costs. Across the domestic steel market, low-priced Chinese steel imports are cited as a shared pressure.

In October 2026, press reports said the holding company is reviewing an AI data center investment on idle land within its plants and plans new businesses through a corporate venture capital (CVC) arm.

Because affiliate results drive so much of the holding company's profit, whether these new-business plans are carried out will shape the next change in its business structure.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show a clear downtrend since the 2022 peak. Revenue fell from KRW 8.51 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.84 trillion in 2023 (down 78.4% year on year), then rose 8.6% to KRW 2 trillion in 2024 and eased 0.7% to KRW 1.99 trillion in 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 743.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 60.1 billion in 2023 (down 91.9%), KRW 58 billion in 2024 (down 3.4%) and KRW 39.5 billion in 2025 (down 32.0%), declining for three consecutive years from 2023. The operating margin narrowed from 8.7% in 2022 to 3.3% in 2023, 2.9% in 2024 and 2.0% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to shareholders fell from KRW 413 billion in 2022 to KRW 225 billion in 2023 (down 45.5%) and KRW 11.7 billion in 2024 (down 94.8%), then recovered to KRW 16.1 billion in 2025 (up 36.9%).

Net income including non-controlling interests went from KRW 425.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 20.7 billion in 2025, so the gap between the two profit measures should be read together. The debt ratio moved from 96.3% in 2022 to 36.3% in 2023, 41.0% in 2024 and 37.7% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 1.96 trillion, of which shareholders' share was KRW 1.8 trillion. Operating cash flow declined from KRW 692.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 54.7 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from KRW 497 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 821.1 billion in Q3 2026, when it was up 65.2% year on year. Operating profit bottomed at KRW 1.61 billion in Q4 2025 and recovered to KRW 32.5 billion in Q3 2026 (up 217.2% year on year).

Net profit attributable to shareholders jumped to KRW 97.5 billion in Q2 2026 before falling to KRW 3.7 billion in Q3 (down 37.0% year on year). The four-quarter total from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 is KRW 111.8 billion, and Q2 accounts for most of it, so the total depends heavily on a single quarter.

Because operating profit and attributable net profit moved in different directions, both operating and equity-method items need to be checked together.

05

Industry analysis

Dongkuk Holdings' results depend on affiliate profits tied to steel market conditions. In its May 28, 2026 report, Hyundai Motor Securities said steel prices in major global regions have kept rising since the start of the year and expected improved results in the steel and trading segments.

In its Q2 2026 preliminary results, Dongkuk Steel Mill said semiconductor plant expansion and stronger overseas exports lifted profits from bar and section steel. The same company reported that in Q3 2026, seasonal slowdown cut revenue 6.4% and operating profit 34.4% from Q2.

Dongkuk Coated Metal posted an operating loss of KRW 6 billion and a net loss of KRW 36.7 billion in Q3 2026, with currency swings and cost pressure cited as causes.

In the color-coated steel segment, whether Korea imposes anti-dumping duties on Chinese steel was flagged as a key variable, with a decision scheduled for May 2026.

On the demand side, large projects such as semiconductor plants and AI data centers, along with shipbuilding plate demand, are seen as support, while low-priced Chinese supply is seen as a burden.

Dongkuk Steel Mill said it expanded a dedicated export team and unified sales, trade and logistics to protect margins in a high-exchange-rate environment.

From the holding company's perspective, the slowdown in steel demand and the limited earnings capacity of the holding company itself are viewed as structural challenges.

06

Outlook

The mid-term direction the company has set is a plan to raise corporate value. Dongkuk Holdings disclosed on October 22, 2026 that it aims to reach a price-to-book ratio of 0.3x or higher by 2028.

The company argues that the value of the holding company and its affiliates, as well as its shareholder return policy, has not been fully reflected by the market, and that weaker steel demand and the holding company's limited ability to generate its own earnings have held back baseline profitability.

Its stated priorities are to improve affiliate profitability, expand higher-value products and reduce reliance on investment gains. On shareholder returns, Dongkuk Holdings and its two steel affiliates plan to set minimum dividends to make payouts more predictable.

On capital structure, Dongkuk Holdings announced in February 2026 a plan covering the cancellation of all treasury shares, a free capital reduction and a stock split.

The company said that once these steps are completed, capital stock would fall from 41.1% of net assets (KRW 271.1 billion) to 11.8% (KRW 77.8 billion), freeing about KRW 200 billion for more efficient use.

New businesses under review include an AI data center on idle land within its plants and new ventures pursued through a CVC arm.

Dongkuk Steel Mill said it will keep a profitability-focused strategy in Q4 2026, and Dongkuk Coated Metal said it plans to adjust its export mix and increase the share of higher-value products in Q4.

Whether this outlook carries through to holding-company results can be checked in the next annual report's equity-method results and operating margin.

08

Bull factors

Consecutive quarterly profit recovery

Operating profit rose for three straight quarters, from KRW 6.22 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 20.1 billion in Q2 and KRW 32.5 billion in Q3. Q3 revenue reached KRW 821.1 billion, up 65.2% year on year, showing expansion alongside the profit recovery.

Given a full-year 2025 operating margin of 2.0%, the direction of the core profit recovery is clear. Absolute quarterly operating profit remains far below the KRW 743.5 billion posted in 2022.

Improving equity-method affiliate results

Dongkuk Steel Mill reported Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 45.6 billion, up 52.3% year on year. Its Q3 2026 preliminary operating profit of KRW 29.9 billion was up 22.0% from a year earlier.

Bar and section steel demand tied to semiconductor plants and AI data centers, along with shipbuilding plate demand, is cited as an improvement factor. As an equity-method investee, the affiliate's profit gains could flow into the holding company's equity-method results.

Shareholder return and capital structure plans

In February 2026, Dongkuk Holdings outlined a plan including cancellation of all treasury shares, a free capital reduction and a stock split. The company said that once these steps are implemented, the capital stock ratio would fall, leaving about KRW 200 billion to be deployed more flexibly.

The holding company and its two steel affiliates agreed to set minimum dividends to improve the predictability of returns. A target price-to-book ratio of 0.3x or higher by 2028 was presented alongside.

09

Bear factors

Reliance on investment gains and quarterly volatility

Equity-method investment gains and losses from associates and joint ventures totaled KRW 87.1 billion in the first half of 2026. Newsway reported that excluding these investment gains would sharply lower the holding company's profitability.

Net profit attributable to shareholders fell from KRW 97.5 billion in Q2 2026 to KRW 3.7 billion in Q3, down 37.0% year on year. If operating profit grows without net profit following, questions about earnings quality remain.

Thin margins and three years of decline

Annual operating profit declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and the 2025 operating margin was 2.0%. Revenue in 2025 was KRW 1.99 trillion, well below the KRW 8.51 trillion recorded in 2022. Operating cash flow also fell from KRW 692.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 54.7 billion in 2025. With a thin margin, shifts in steel market conditions have a larger effect on profit.

Loss-making affiliate and export dependence

Dongkuk Coated Metal posted an operating loss of KRW 6 billion and a net loss of KRW 36.7 billion in Q3 2026, remaining in the red. With exports at about 70% of output, it is heavily exposed to exchange rates and trade protectionism. Dongkuk Steel Mill's Q3 2026 operating profit also fell 34.4% from Q2. When seasonal and external factors coincide, equity-method results can swing widely.

10

Risk factors

Steel cycle and Chinese supply

Domestic and global steel prices and demand directly determine affiliate profits. Low-priced Chinese steel imports and the scope of anti-dumping duties on color-coated steel are uncertain factors. Slower construction and industrial investment could also weigh on bar and section steel volumes. Because holding-company results pass through equity-method accounting, these swings can be magnified.

Exchange rates, trade protection and raw material costs

Dongkuk Coated Metal's high export share means exchange-rate swings, trade protectionism and higher raw material costs all affect it at once. Its Q3 2026 operating loss was explained by these factors. High exchange rates can lift export margins in some periods and raise cost pressure in others. These variables are largely outside the holding company's control.

Investment gain volatility and execution of capital policy

The holding company's net profit attributable to shareholders moves sharply by quarter with equity-method investment results. The gap between KRW 97.5 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 3.7 billion in Q3 illustrates this.

The effect of capital structure changes and shareholder return plans depends on disclosure steps and timing. Whether the stated price-to-book target is reached should also be read alongside earnings capacity.

12

Overall view

Dongkuk Holdings' annual operating profit has declined for three consecutive years since its 2022 peak, and the 2025 operating margin was 2.0%. Quarterly operating profit has risen for three straight quarters in 2026, showing a recovery in core profit.

However, net profit attributable to shareholders is heavily influenced by equity-method investment results and varies widely by quarter, as the gap between KRW 97.5 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 3.7 billion in Q3 shows.

Among affiliates, the improving demand for Dongkuk Steel Mill's bar and section steel contrasts with Dongkuk Coated Metal's continued losses. On valuation, the share price sits well below book net asset value, and the company has set a target price-to-book ratio of 0.3x or higher by 2028.

On shareholder returns, a minimum dividend policy and a capital structure overhaul have been announced, and their execution should be confirmed through disclosures. Hyundai Motor Securities presented a target price of KRW 14,400 in its May 28, 2026 report.

Strengths and weaknesses coexist, so the operating margin and equity-method results in the next annual report are the next checkpoints.
