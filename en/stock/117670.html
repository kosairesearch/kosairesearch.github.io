02

Business structure

Alphachips is a system semiconductor design company founded in 2002 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2010. Its business has two pillars, design services and fabless operations. The design service segment offers end-to-end turnkey work from RTL design through SoC chip implementation to packaging and testing.

The fabless segment sells in-house system semiconductor products such as Mobile Multimedia ICs and Security ICs, and IR receiver IC sales also fall under this segment.

A company summary attributes the recent sales decline to fewer high-difficulty design service projects, development delays, and weak demand for consumer electronics in the fabless business.

After a change in its largest shareholder in December 2024, the company divested non-core businesses such as display manufacturing equipment and pharmaceuticals and restructured around semiconductors, according to a January 2026 announcement.

In the same period it hired a semiconductor specialist from Samsung Electronics to strengthen a foundry-linked strategy spanning design through foundry collaboration.

Alphachips was selected as a DSP of Samsung Electronics' foundry in 2018 and has been described as a core hub in the design ecosystem; a 2026 report said it is working with Samsung Electronics as well as domestic DSP and EDA companies.

Its Mixed Signal business unit is developing four optical communication and optical sensor ICs, including a POF-based optical receiver IC. Unlike memory-focused large-cap chipmakers such as SK hynix, the company is a small structure combining design services with its own fabless products.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported annual basis, sales fell from KRW 90 billion in 2022 to KRW 79.9 billion in 2023, an 11.3% decline, then rose to KRW 85.3 billion in 2024, up 6.8%, before dropping to KRW 82.3 billion in 2025, down 3.5%. 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest among the four years.

Operating losses were KRW 3.09 billion in 2022, KRW 2.78 billion in 2023, KRW 1.41 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.04 billion in 2025, so the loss narrowed in 2024 and widened again in 2025. The operating margin was -3.4% in 2022, -3.5% in 2023, -1.7% in 2024 and -2.5% in 2025, with 2024 showing the smallest loss rate.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders shrank from KRW 52.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 23 billion in 2023, KRW 8.43 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.48 billion in 2025, and the net loss including non-controlling interests moved in the same direction.

Operating cash flow went from negative in 2022 and 2023, at -KRW 1.54 billion and -KRW 3.06 billion, to positive in 2024 at KRW 661 million and in 2025 at KRW 2.79 billion.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 25.2 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 21 billion in Q4 2025, a 16.9% quarter-on-quarter decline, with the low point at KRW 19.7 billion in Q1 2026. Sales then rose to KRW 21 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 32.1 billion in Q3 2026.

Operating results moved the other way: after KRW 1.47 billion of operating profit in Q3 2025, the company posted operating losses of KRW 225 million in Q4 2025, KRW 472 million in Q1 2026 and KRW 1.16 billion in Q2 2026.

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 510 million turned positive from the prior quarter but fell 65.3% from a year earlier. Q3 sales rose 27.3% year on year while operating profit declined, which shows that the sales recovery has so far carried over to profit only to a limited extent.

The sum of profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, is a net loss of KRW 762 million. A company summary attributes the sales decline to fewer complex design projects and weak consumer electronics demand, without quantifying the impact.

05

Industry analysis

The system semiconductor design market in which Alphachips operates divides the work: fabless firms design chips, while design houses and DSPs handle implementation and verification. The company has said it will expand its advanced-node AI ASIC business.

As demand for AI accelerators and custom chips grows, demand for design services tends to rise with it, and the company is positioning its collaboration with Samsung Electronics' foundry as a core pillar. Design service revenue is project-based, which makes results volatile.

In periods of sales decline, fewer complex projects and development delays were cited as causes. The fabless side was affected by weaker consumer electronics demand.

Within the semiconductor sector, Alphachips differs from memory-focused players such as SK hynix and Samsung Electronics because its design service share is high, and it also differs from equipment-centered companies such as HANMI Semiconductor and EO Technics.

Where the cycle stands in its end markets is hard to pin down from publicly available figures. The point to watch for its position is whether order flow from AI ASIC and advanced-node projects continues.

06

Outlook

The core of the company's expansion plan is headcount. According to a September 2026 report, it nearly doubled its development engineering staff from the start of the year and held its first open recruitment of new graduates in its history.

The move was described as a step to strengthen its response to AI ASIC and advanced-node projects. The same report said additional projects from existing customers and business talks with new global customers are continuing.

On the product side, the company announced on September 18, 2026 that it had started developing four optical communication and optical sensor ICs. According to the plan, the products will go through fab-out (wafer shipment) by the end of 2026, module evaluation in Q1 2027, and market entry in Q3 and Q4 2027.

If the schedule holds, revenue contribution from these products would begin in earnest after the second half of 2027. The products are still in development and testing, so whether they move to mass production and customer adoption will have to be confirmed as the schedule unfolds.

Full-year 2026 results will be confirmed in the annual report due by the end of March 2027. Until then, whether quarterly operating profit improves alongside growth in AI ASIC project orders is the key to the earnings outlook.

Bull factors

Two Straight Quarters of Sales Growth

Sales rose from a low of KRW 19.7 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 21 billion in Q2 and KRW 32.1 billion in Q3. Q3 2026 sales were up 27.3% from the year-earlier quarter. The change in sales direction since Q4 2025 is the main basis for the bullish case.

Return to Profit and Better Cash Flow

Operating profit of KRW 510 million and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 430 million in Q3 2026 both turned positive. Operating cash flow was positive at KRW 661 million in 2024 and KRW 2.79 billion in 2025. This contrasts with 2022 and 2023, when operating cash flow was negative.

Stronger Balance Sheet and Completed Restructuring

The debt ratio fell from 73.6% in 2022 to 41.4% in 2025, and total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 42.3 billion. Following the change in its largest shareholder in December 2024, the company says it divested non-core businesses and completed its restructuring around semiconductors.

Bear factors

Four Straight Years of Operating Losses

Operating losses and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders persisted from 2022 through 2025. The 2025 operating loss of KRW 2.04 billion widened from the prior year. The controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters totals KRW 762 million.

Profit Recovery Lags Sales Growth

Q3 2026 sales rose 27.3% from a year earlier, while operating profit fell 65.3%. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders also declined 73.9%. Operating losses widened to KRW 1.16 billion in Q2 2026.

Project Volatility and Timing of New Products

Fewer complex design service projects and development delays were cited as causes of sales declines. The fabless business was affected by weaker consumer electronics demand. Market entry for the optical communication and optical sensor ICs is planned for Q3 and Q4 2027, so it will not be reflected in 2026 results.

Risk factors

Earnings Capacity

Losses persisted from 2022 through 2025, and operating losses also occurred in Q1 and Q2 2026. If the Q3 2026 profit proves to be a one-quarter event, earnings-based assessment remains difficult.

If sales recovery does not outpace the burden of selling, general and administrative and R&D expenses, the profitable trend could weaken.

Business Concentration and Project Volatility

Design services depend on project-based orders, so quarterly sales can swing sharply. Because the company's collaboration with Samsung Electronics' foundry is central, changes in that customer and ecosystem could have a large effect on results. The fabless segment is directly exposed to weaker consumer electronics demand.

New Business Execution and Staffing Costs

Nearly doubling the development engineering staff from the start of the year raises personnel costs. The four optical IC products must pass fab-out by the end of 2026 and module evaluation in Q1 2027, so any slip would delay their contribution. With total equity of KRW 42.3 billion, the room for large investments is also limited.

Overall view

Alphachips is a system semiconductor design company founded in 2002 that runs design services and fabless businesses together. Operating losses and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders persisted from 2022 through 2025, narrowing after 2024 before the operating loss widened again in 2025.

After the Q1 2026 low, sales rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 32.1 billion in Q3, and Q3 turned to profit with operating profit of KRW 510 million and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 430 million.

Both profit measures were lower than in the year-earlier quarter, and the controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters totals KRW 762 million. The debt ratio fell from 73.6% in 2022 to 41.4% in 2025, and total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 42.3 billion.

Bullish factors are the sales recovery and balance sheet improvement, while bearish factors are the cumulative losses, volatile project orders, and the fact that market entry for new products is planned for 2027 or later.

The next checkpoints are the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027, and the module evaluation results for the optical ICs in Q1 2027.
