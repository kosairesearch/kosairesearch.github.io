Losses narrowing under court-led rehabilitation; listing status is the key test

02

Business structure

Eutilex is a Kosdaq-listed immuno-oncology developer that was listed on December 24, 2018 and describes itself as a specialist in immuno-oncology drugs. Under the 2025 annual report, the consolidated operating segments are the bio segment, the IT segment and three biologics-manufacturing subsidiaries.

IT segment revenue was KRW 8,533,992 thousand in 2025 against KRW 2,130,423 thousand for the bio segment, so the IT segment accounted for most segment revenue, and the 2024 pattern was similar. The company attributed 2025 IT revenue growth to equipment sales and software revenue recognized over time.

On the bio side, the company announced that its EU101 antibody received FDA clearance for a 1/2 phase trial in January 2021. EU103 is described as a dual-function antibody that targets macrophages and killer T cells at the same time.

The 204 (EBViNT) therapy is in a 1/2a trial for EBV-positive lymphoma and solid tumors. An Armored CAR-T program is also in clinical testing, and the annual report states that the CAR-T production facility has been completed.

Against global drugmakers such as Pfizer and BMS, the company argues that its first-discovery technology gives it an edge. Peers in the same sector for comparison include Alteogen, ABL Bio and LigaChem Biosciences.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue went from KRW 216 million in 2022 to KRW 132 million in 2023, a 39.0% decline, then jumped to KRW 9.53 billion in 2024, a 7,136.1% increase. Revenue reached KRW 10.3 billion in 2025, up 8.1% year on year, making 2025 the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

Segment figures show that IT segment revenue rose sharply in 2024 and drove the change in the revenue mix. Operating loss was KRW 36.7 billion in 2022, KRW 29.8 billion in 2023, KRW 26.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 18.9 billion in 2025, a steady narrowing that still left the company in the red every year.

The company attributed the 2025 reduction to lower selling and administrative expenses from company-wide cost cuts and budget reductions, and said that a settlement of joint research costs with a subsidiary raised non-operating income.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 34.5 billion in 2022, KRW 27.7 billion in 2023, KRW 29.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 23.9 billion in 2025, with the 2024 loss widening. Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 25.8 billion in 2025.

Operating margin was -17,032.8% in 2022 and -22,622.8% in 2023, when revenue was very small, and improved to -278.3% in 2024 and -183.8% in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 15.0% in 2022 to 56.6% in 2024 and 161.0% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 25.4 billion and total equity of KRW 15.8 billion at the end of 2025, of which KRW 15.3 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders.

Operating cash outflow narrowed from KRW 29.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 14.1 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue fell from KRW 3.53 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.39 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 1.25 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 535 million in the second quarter, before rebounding to KRW 1.24 billion in the third quarter, a 132.6% quarter-on-quarter increase but a 64.8% year-on-year decline.

Operating loss was KRW 3.84 billion, KRW 5.03 billion, KRW 2.59 billion and KRW 2.15 billion across the four quarters before the third quarter of 2026, when operating profit was KRW 65 million.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 11.5 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, and the third quarter of 2026 showed net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 194 million.

The sum of net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, was KRW 16.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Immuno-oncology and cell therapy, the field in which Eutilex competes, is a sector where clinical data and licensing deals drive company value.

In earlier press reports, the co-CEO said that licensing deals struck after a phase 1 trial carry much larger upfront payments relative to deal size than deals struck at the preclinical stage. The same reporting said the company would work to accelerate the phase 1 trial of its faster-moving 103 candidate.

The company also said it keeps licensing open as an option alongside clinical development. These statements come from older reports and are not tied to any current negotiation.

The company argues that EU101 is an original technology because it is built on the immune checkpoint-activating substance its founder discovered, placing it ahead of global drugmakers researching the same substance. That is the company's own comparison and not an independent assessment.

The field includes many listed biotech companies competing on mechanism differentiation, and peers in the same sector include Samsung Biologics, Celltrion, Alteogen and SK biopharmaceuticals.

Eutilex depends heavily on its IT segment for revenue, so its bio pipeline alone is not a complete basis for assessing the business. Two public-sector IT contracts signed in January 2026 support IT segment revenue in the near term.

06

Outlook

The nearest checkpoint is the progress of the rehabilitation proceedings. After the March 30, 2026 commencement order, management authority over operations and asset disposal passed to a court-appointed administrator, and shareholder meetings came under court supervision.

As a result, the 2026 annual general meeting was not held, and the company said it will approve financial statements under the enforcement decree of the External Audit Act. The timing of an approved rehabilitation plan depends on court proceedings and no date has been announced.

On the listing side, the auditor's disclaimer of opinion, citing a scope limitation and uncertainty about going-concern status, created delisting grounds, and the company filed an objection.

The Korea Exchange granted an improvement period through April 10, 2027, ten days after the statutory filing deadline for the next annual report, and that end date can shift if a filing extension or a waiver of the late-filing sanction is approved.

After the period ends, the company must submit an improvement plan implementation report within 15 business days. In the IT segment, two contracts signed in January 2026 run through November 30, 2027 and December 27, 2027, so delivery under these contracts is central to revenue.

On the bio side, the planned phase 2 entry for the 204 candidate and the phase 1 trial for the 103 candidate were set out in older reports, and their current status should be confirmed through company disclosures.

The next annual report is the 2026 business report, so the full-year 2026 operating margin and the capital-impairment status will be the next confirmation points. The company says its CAR-T production facility is complete, so trial pace and any licensing deal will be the other points to track.

Pipeline and production base

Legal process and funding risk

Overall view

On a disclosed basis, Eutilex narrowed its operating loss from KRW 36.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 18.9 billion in 2025, and posted operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter of 2026.

Quarterly revenue fell sharply over the same stretch, the profit has appeared in only one quarter, and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 16.4 billion. Revenue depends heavily on the IT segment, while the bio pipeline remains in clinical stages.

The commencement of rehabilitation on March 30, 2026, the postponed annual general meeting, and the delisting objection following the disclaimer of opinion, with an improvement period through April 10, 2027, make listing status and business recovery the main variables.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was below paid-in capital, which places the company in a capital-impaired state, and the debt ratio rose to 161.0%. Operating cash outflows narrowed but continue.

Positive factors include the smaller loss, the third-quarter profit swing, the clinical pipeline and the IT contract base; negative factors include legal proceedings, the capital structure and falling revenue.

The next checkpoints are the approval timing of the rehabilitation plan, the 2026 annual report and the end of the improvement period on April 10, 2027.
