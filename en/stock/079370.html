Zeusco: Operating profit turns positive, net loss persists

Zeusco returned to operating profit in Q3 2026, but attributable net losses have run for five straight quarters, and the earnings recovery at its Japanese subsidiary J.E.T. is the key swing factor.

02

Business structure

Zeusco was founded in 1970 and makes single-wafer wet cleaning equipment for semiconductor front-end processes, along with display equipment, inspection and process tools, and industrial robots.

Figures compiled by an equity data platform put semiconductor cleaning equipment at 73.62% of sales, industrial robots and vacuum equipment at 17.51%, plug valves and other devices at 5.53%, and display equipment at 3.34%.

These shares come from an aggregator rather than DART filings and should be read as a reference only. The line-up covers both single-wafer and batch-type cleaning tools.

Its main customers are Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, and a Korean business daily, citing securities and industry compilations, reported that new cleaning equipment sales to their HBM manufacturing lines exceeded KRW 100 billion in 2024.

In March 2026 the company delivered HBM packaging cleaning test units to the largest US memory chipmaker and said it aimed to become a formal vendor within the first quarter of 2026. The company is also reported to be developing photonic debonding automation equipment for HBM4 and HBM5 production.

Its Japanese subsidiary J.E.T., listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange and held at a 66.28% stake, accounts for 35.7% of consolidated revenue according to a domestic research report. In cleaning equipment, the company competes with domestic and overseas front-end equipment makers.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue was KRW 509 billion in 2022, the largest year of 2022–2025, and KRW 383.3 billion in 2025, the smallest. Revenue of KRW 402.9 billion in 2023 fell 20.9% year on year, KRW 490.8 billion in 2024 rose 21.8%, and 2025 revenue dropped 21.9% again.

The company attributed the 2025 revenue decline mainly to lower equipment investment by major semiconductor customers. Operating margin was 9.1% in 2022, 1.8% in 2023, and 10.0% in 2024, then turned to -1.2% in 2025 as the company moved into an operating loss.

In 2024, operating profit of KRW 49.2 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 41.9 billion reflected a sharp improvement from the prior year.

In 2025 the operating loss was KRW 4.46 billion, the attributable net loss was KRW 5.83 billion, and the total net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 14.1 billion.

The total net loss is larger than the attributable loss because losses absorbed by non-controlling interests are included, and a domestic research report said the Japanese subsidiary J.E.T. posted a net loss of KRW 22.2 billion that pulled consolidated results down even though the parent was profitable on a standalone basis.

Operating cash flow was KRW 8.1 billion in 2022, minus KRW 39.2 billion in 2023, KRW 58 billion in 2024, and KRW 28 billion in 2025. By quarter, revenue rose to KRW 86.8 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 98.8 billion in Q4 2025, but operating losses continued.

Q1 2026 revenue fell 44.4% quarter on quarter to KRW 54.9 billion, and the operating loss widened to KRW 17.1 billion.

Revenue then rose to KRW 82.4 billion in Q2 and KRW 85.3 billion in Q3 2026, two straight quarters of growth, and operating results moved from a KRW 9.64 billion loss in Q2 to an operating profit of KRW 6.95 billion in Q3.

Q3 2026 revenue was still 1.8% lower than a year earlier, and the attributable net loss was KRW 4.1 billion. The attributable net loss for the last four quarters totaled KRW 13.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Zeusco's end market moves with semiconductor capital spending and the expansion of HBM production at memory makers. The 2025 revenue decline was tied to lower equipment investment by major customers, which shows how much the customers' investment cycle drives sales.

As HBM capacity grew, demand for wet cleaning equipment rose as well, and reports say Zeusco expanded its position in the HBM supply chain by offering both single-wafer and batch-type tools.

Samsung Electronics and SK hynix together dominate the HBM market, and the company's tools are reported to have passed their demanding quality tests and to be used on mass-production lines.

A report that the largest US memory chipmaker has started building new fabs for HBM4 production has put a spotlight on whether Zeusco can widen its customer base beyond its two Korean customers.

On the cycle, the business recovered from a Q1 2026 low in Q2 and Q3, but Q3 2026 revenue was still below the year-earlier level. Competition in cleaning equipment comes from domestic and overseas front-end equipment makers, and domestic peers in the same equipment group include PSK and Jusung Engineering.

Because Zeusco also sells display equipment and industrial robots, its exposure to the chip cycle differs by business unit.

06

Outlook

The company's own annual guidance is not available in the material reviewed here. The confirmed business milestones are the delivery of HBM packaging cleaning test units reported in March 2026 and a stated target of formal vendor registration within the first quarter of 2026.

Development of photonic debonding automation equipment for HBM4 and HBM5 production is cited as a medium-term growth option. In March 2026, Hanwha Systems approved a strategic equity investment in a planned entity referred to as 'ZEUS', reported as part of a portfolio restructuring.

J.E.T. posted a 17.8% operating margin in Q3 2026 and returned to profit, and reports said its order backlog doubled within three months.

A domestic research house, in its own estimates, assumed consolidated revenue of KRW 410 billion and operating profit of KRW 19 billion for 2026 with a return to profit, and KRW 500 billion and KRW 40 billion for 2027.

These estimates assume subsidiary margins hold, the backlog converts to sales, and domestic equipment investment resumes, and they are not company guidance.

By contrast, J.E.T. is reported not to publish annual forecasts, and its 2025 summary results were released only in late May 2026, which raises disclosure-reliability concerns.

The next checkpoint is J.E.T.'s Q4 2026 operating margin, scheduled for February 2027, which is the test of whether the subsidiary's profit is durable.

Signs of improvement in quarterly trends

Bear factors

Shrinking revenue base

FY2025 revenue of KRW 383.3 billion was the smallest of 2022–2025, and the company cited lower investment by major customers. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 85.3 billion rose from the prior quarter but was 1.8% lower than a year earlier.

Persistent attributable net losses

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have been reported for five straight quarters, from Q3 2025 through Q3 2026. The Q3 2026 loss was KRW 4.1 billion, and the last four quarters total KRW 13.4 billion. The operating swing to profit has not yet carried through to the bottom line.

Dependence on a subsidiary and disclosure concerns

One analysis says J.E.T.'s Q3 profit came from cost adjustment and product mix rather than a sales recovery. The subsidiary's Q3 revenue fell 14.3% year on year. J.E.T. does not publish annual forecasts, and its 2025 summary results came only in May 2026, which has drawn disclosure-discount concerns.

10

Risk factors

Customer capex cycle

The 2025 revenue decline stemmed from lower investment by major semiconductor customers, so a renewed cut could hit both sales and operating profit. Q1 2026 revenue fell 44.4% quarter on quarter, showing how quickly the cycle can turn.

Because customer concentration is high, a change in one customer's timing shows up directly in quarterly results.

FX and non-controlling interests

Zeusco holds 66.28% of J.E.T., so 33.72% of the subsidiary's profit flows to non-controlling shareholders. A weaker yen could affect consolidated results, and fixed costs such as R&D and financing charges also weigh on sustainability. Whether the subsidiary's profit is a one-off should become clearer from its next quarterly margins.

Timing of HBM supply confirmation

Formal vendor registration was targeted for Q1 2026, but whether test deliveries become volume supply depends on customer qualification. New equipment such as photonic debonding has not yet been proven in reported results before mass production. The investment structure tied to a planned entity also leaves room for the business structure to change.

Overall view

Zeusco's reported results began with lower 2025 revenue and a swing to operating loss, then moved to two straight quarters of revenue growth from the Q1 2026 low and an operating profit in Q3 2026.

However, net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have been reported for five straight quarters, totaling KRW 13.4 billion over the last four. Growth drivers include customer expansion for HBM packaging cleaning equipment and the return to profit at subsidiary J.E.T.

Weakness comes from dependence on customer capital spending, the non-controlling interest structure, and limits in subsidiary disclosure. On valuation, the price-to-book ratio is below 1x, but an earnings-based multiple cannot yet be calculated.
