02

Business structure

Topsun was founded in October 2008 and listed on Konex in October 2017 as a solar specialist. Its core is solar module manufacturing and sales, and it has extended into power-plant construction, wind, ESS and transmission and substation equipment.

The Korea Exchange technical analysis report classifies it as other electrical equipment manufacturing. Compared with listed companies in the same machinery and equipment group, such as Doosan Enerbility, its scale and business profile are very different.

In the 2025 annual report, power-plant construction services are the largest line at about KRW 93.0 billion, and the goods segment adds about KRW 12.2 billion. Within goods, solar module sales fell from about KRW 27.6 billion in 2023 to about KRW 11.8 billion in 2025.

Product sales of self-made modules are shown as KRW 6,855 thousand in the filing table, so their weight is negligible. This indicates that earnings have shifted from module sales toward construction work.

The company developed and completed a 57MW plant, described as the largest of its kind in Korea, and has handled large projects such as Haenam 94MW, Jeju 48MW and Sinan 150MW. On products, it secures high output with n-TOPCon modules and is developing next-generation HJT technology.

In ESS, it is entering the utility-scale market in cooperation with battery makers such as Samsung SDI and CATL. The annual report also cites a project selected as preferred bidder in the second ESS central contract market auction of 2025.

According to a report citing regulatory filings, it set up two UK entities to invest in and build EPC for 72.8MW of solar and BESS.

03

Recent trends

According to the 2025 annual report, revenue was KRW 132,533 million, up 11.6% from a year earlier. Operating profit was KRW 15,941 million, up 101.6%.

The company attributed the rise to the new 137MW Sinan Jeungdo award, progress-based revenue recognition on the 53.4MW Sinui phase 1, and better costs and profitability. Operating profit for 2024 was reported at KRW 7.9 billion, so the 2025 figure is roughly double that level.

However, another report shows 2024 revenue of KRW 110.0 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.0 billion, so comparison figures may differ depending on the basis of aggregation.

The 2025 annual report was filed on March 23, 2026 and amended on April 30, 2026. Quarterly results for 2026 could not be confirmed as of this report's date. The shareholder structure has been in motion.

On March 27, SK ecoplant disclosed that it had signed a sale agreement with Trustone Asset Management for a 19.69% stake and convertible bonds in Topsun. Dealsite reported that the sale, originally due to close in the first half, was pushed back at the request of the convertible bond buyer.

The shares were at KRW 3,400 on October 22, 2026, with a 52-week range of KRW 3,150 to KRW 9,600. A Money Today article of January 21, 2026 listed the price at KRW 4,580. The October 27 price is KRW 3,585, down 0.42% from the prior day, for a market capitalization of KRW 33.5 billion. Trading value that day was only KRW 659,405, so pricing can be sensitive to small orders.

04

Outlook

Kiwoom Securities analyst Oh Hyun-jin expected Topsun's growth to improve as distributed power combining solar and ESS expands, and forecast full-scale growth from 2026 on a high order backlog. This is one broker's view, and actual outcomes need to be checked against 2026 results filings.

The Sinan solar plant EPC contract with SK Innovation is worth KRW 160 billion, with commercial operation targeted for December 2026. Construction progress on this project is a key variable for revenue recognition.

The market backdrop has supportive elements. A brokerage report dated March 17, 2026 projected that Korea's solar market will expand by an average of about 10GW a year over the next five years. If that materializes, construction volume could rise, but bidding competition and cost pressure could also grow.

How fast the project selected as preferred bidder in the ESS central contract market turns into actual revenue also needs to be confirmed.

On governance, the key question is whether the E1 deal is finalized. As reported, E1 would become the largest shareholder if it converts the bonds into shares. E1 stated, however, that it is reviewing various options and that nothing has been decided.

The operating margin improved in 2025, but whether that level holds depends on order mix and cost trends. Low investor attention and thin trading on Konex are structural conditions unlikely to ease quickly.

05

Bull factors

Larger profit base

Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 15,941 million, up 101.6% year on year. Revenue grew 11.6%, so profit growth was far larger than revenue growth. The company cited better costs and profitability as reasons. If this continues, there may be room for further improvement in the operating margin.

Full-cycle plant capability

Topsun can run a solar project end to end, from module manufacturing to plant construction, and has a record of developing and completing the largest plant of its kind in Korea. Based on its high-efficiency module manufacturing, it offers an integrated solution covering EPC and O&M. This structure matters because module cost competitiveness can be linked to construction earnings.

Large orders and ESS expansion

In July 2025 Topsun signed a KRW 160 billion EPC supply contract with SK Innovation for the Sinan solar plant. The annual report also lists a project selected as preferred bidder in the ESS central contract market. If large construction and ESS proceed together, the revenue mix could become more diversified.

06

Bear factors

Construction reliance and volatility

Power-plant construction services were about KRW 93.0 billion in 2025, most of total revenue of about KRW 132.5 billion. Construction revenue can swing widely by quarter depending on award timing and percentage of completion. Module goods sales fell each year from 2023 to 2025. A weaker recurring product revenue base is a drag on earnings stability.

High leverage

The Korea Exchange technical analysis report shows a 2024 debt ratio of 310.3%. Large construction jobs typically carry working-capital and borrowing needs. Changes in the capital structure after 2025 could not be separately verified in this report. If interest rates or project financing conditions worsen, the financial burden could rise.

Thin trading and price swings

Three-month average volume is about 2,540 shares and the 52-week range runs from KRW 3,150 to KRW 9,600. The one-year change is shown at about -63%. With little trading, earnings improvement may not be reflected in the price immediately, and price swings can be wide. Money Today noted that neglect of Konex companies has been deepening.

07

Risk factors

Policy and macro

Demand for solar construction is closely tied to government renewable energy policy and deployment targets. One brokerage report projects the domestic solar market expanding by an average of 10GW a year, but that outlook could change if policy direction shifts. Interest rates and module price swings also affect project profitability.

Ownership and dilution

SK ecoplant became the second-largest shareholder in June 2022 by buying KRW 79.9 billion of convertible bonds, and founder CEO Yoon Jeong-taek is the largest holder with 27.07%. There was a report that the sale schedule was delayed at the request of the convertible bond buyer.

If the bonds are converted into shares, the share count rises and existing holders can be diluted. Whether and when the deal closes remains uncertain.

Market and liquidity

The Konex market has a limited investor base and light trading, so trades can have a large price impact. The KRW 659,405 trading value on October 27 illustrates this. With a market capitalization of about KRW 33.5 billion, even small shifts in supply and demand can move the price. Trading near the 52-week low is another factor that can raise volatility.

08

Overall view

Topsun is a Konex-listed company that has expanded from solar module manufacturing into power-plant construction and ESS. In 2025, revenue rose 11.6% and operating profit rose 101.6%, a clear improvement in profitability.

Construction revenue from large projects such as Sinan is at the center of that improvement, so volatility tied to order timing and construction progress is built into the model.

On ownership, SK ecoplant's sale and E1's reported interest overlap, and whether the deal closes and when the convertible bonds are converted are the open variables. On the market side, trading is very thin and the 52-week range is wide, which limits how reliable the price information is.

The bull factors (a larger profit base, full-cycle capability, large orders) and bear factors (construction reliance, high leverage, low liquidity) should be weighed together, alongside 2026 quarterly results and share transaction filings.
