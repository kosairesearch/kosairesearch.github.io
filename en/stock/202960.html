02

Business structure

Pandora TV is a first-generation Korean venture company founded in 1999. It listed on the KONEX market on August 11, 2014. The company announced the shutdown of its video service, citing profitability that had deteriorated over several years. The service ended on January 31, 2023.

Its operations now consist of KMPlayer, a multimedia player; Cobak, a crypto community; and MovieBloc, a blockchain-based short-film platform. According to the company, KMPlayer is used by more than 300 million people in over 230 countries.

Cobak is a blockchain-based community platform that the company acquired in February 2020. The company added blockchain software development and crypto-asset brokerage to its business purposes, formalizing its shift to a blockchain company.

In the 2022 annual report, the revenue mix was advertising platform 38.3%, service platform 36.4% and blockchain platform 25.3%. We could not confirm a more recent segment breakdown. Separately, Dealsite reported that the blockchain platform's revenue share fell 4.4 percentage points from the prior year to 21.4%.

On the competitive front, KMPlayer overlaps with many free playback tools, and Cobak overlaps with other crypto community services. Soop, a listed video platform in the same sector, differs sharply in scale and revenue model, so a direct comparison is hard. No reliable market-share data was found.

03

Recent trends

For fiscal year 2025 (the 27th term), consolidated revenue was KRW 14,102 million, operating profit KRW 1,689 million and net profit KRW 891 million. Consolidated revenue moved from KRW 15.9 billion in 2023 to KRW 16.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.1 billion in 2025.

Operating profit over the same years was KRW 4.1 billion, KRW 1.3 billion and KRW 1.7 billion. Operating margins were 25.71%, 8.06% and 11.98%. FnGuide summarized that revenue fell on lower search-advertising sales and crypto market volatility, while cost cuts and operational efficiency improved profitability.

On a standalone basis, revenue was KRW 4,007 million, operating profit KRW 451 million and net loss KRW 248 million. The audit opinion was unqualified for both standalone and consolidated statements. Consolidated total assets were KRW 30,227 million, liabilities KRW 12,727 million and equity KRW 17,501 million.

On the share price, the CEO bought 27,500 shares on the open market between February 4 and 6, 2026, at prices from KRW 657 to KRW 708. The stock was then at KRW 600 on March 31 and has since fallen to KRW 289 (down 15.00% on the day). No filing or news report explaining the latest drop was found. Results for the first quarter of 2026 onward could not be confirmed in this research.

04

Outlook

Near-term earnings are likely to depend heavily on search-advertising revenue and the crypto market environment. Because these two variables were cited for the 2025 revenue decline, their recovery is the key to the revenue trend.

Quarterly results for 2026 could not be confirmed, so the current trajectory cannot be stated with certainty. The margin improvement from cost efficiency is a business-metric upside, but its durability needs testing while the revenue base is shrinking.

Dealsite assessed that the Cobak and MovieBloc coins have contributed little to blockchain revenue because of credibility and volatility issues.

The company's own timeline records the 2025 Bithumb listing of the Cobak token (CBK), MovieBloc passing one million monthly active users, and the rebranding of KMPlayer's premium subscription. Whether that user base converts into revenue has to be verified in future filings.

The next checkpoints are the third-quarter 2026 report and the fiscal 2026 annual results, along with the trend in liabilities and operating cash flow.

05

Bull factors

Operating profit up on cost efficiency

Consolidated revenue fell 15.5% in 2025, yet operating profit rose 25.6%. The operating margin improved from 8.06% in 2024 to 11.98% in 2025. Defending profitability while revenue shrank points to cost-control capability. The 25.71% margin recorded in 2023 remains a historical reference point for margin potential.

Unqualified audit opinion and equity base

The 2025 audit opinion was unqualified for both standalone and consolidated statements, and consolidated equity was KRW 17,501 million. The current market cap of KRW 3.4 billion is roughly one-fifth of that equity. The gap between book capital and market pricing is a fact, but how to read it is left to the reader.

Because audit opinions are tied directly to delisting reviews, an unqualified opinion means the basic requirement is met.

Global user base and Web3 assets

The company describes KMPlayer as a playback solution used by over 300 million people in more than 230 countries. Cobak has been described as a leading Korean crypto community with 60,000 daily active users and 300,000 cumulative downloads.

Both are user touchpoints built over a long operating history and count as business assets. The CEO's large stake could also be read as supporting consistency in management direction.

06

Bear factors

Falling revenue and ad dependence

Consolidated revenue fell from KRW 16.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 14.1 billion in 2025. The decline was attributed to lower search-advertising sales and crypto market volatility. Cost cuts lifted operating profit, but they have limits, and a shrinking revenue base can undermine earnings sustainability. Net profit also fell 55.6% in 2025.

Unproven blockchain pivot

According to Dealsite, the Cobak token price fell by nearly two-thirds, from the KRW 1,500 range early in the year to the KRW 500 range at the time of reporting. MovieBloc was once designated an investment-caution asset after a gap emerged between planned and actual circulating supply in 2022.

Concerns were also voiced that falling blockchain revenue and reduced headcount and R&D would keep the company tied to its Web2 structure. The results of the pivot have not yet been proven in the numbers.

Higher debt and weaker cash flow

Consolidated liabilities more than doubled from KRW 6.2 billion in 2024 to KRW 12.7 billion in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 37.39% to 72.72%. Operating cash flow was a net outflow of KRW 0.3 billion in 2025, against a net inflow of KRW 1.8 billion in 2024.

The composition of the added liabilities could not be confirmed, and the standalone net loss of KRW 248 million is a further drag.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Revenue is sensitive to search advertising and the crypto market environment. A weaker crypto market could dampen Cobak user activity and token value at the same time. A soft advertising market could reduce ad revenue further. Both are external factors that the company can hardly control.

Business and regulatory

The company issues its own token and includes crypto-asset brokerage in its business purposes. Changes in crypto regulation or exchange listing policies could directly affect the business model. The earlier investment-caution designation of MovieBloc shows that transparency in token management can become an issue. Execution risk also remains until new revenue sources are established after the video service closure.

Liquidity and governance

Turnover at the time of writing was only KRW 7,247,975. Thin trading means even small orders can move the price sharply, as the 15.00% one-day drop illustrates. With the CEO holding 56.19%, minority shareholders have limited influence.

At the March 2026 annual meeting, approval of the financial statements was 69.4% of total voting shares outstanding.

08

Overall view

Pandora TV is a small KONEX company that closed its video platform and pivoted to Web3 businesses. It stayed profitable in 2025, with consolidated operating profit of KRW 1,689 million and net profit of KRW 891 million, and received an unqualified audit opinion.

On the other hand, revenue declined, and liabilities and operating cash flow worsened from the prior year. The blockchain pivot depends on token prices and the regulatory environment, and its results are not yet visible in the numbers.

The share price has fallen from KRW 600 on March 31, 2026 to KRW 289, and turnover is very small, so price discovery itself is of low reliability. No filing explaining the decline was found, which makes future quarterly disclosures and company explanations important checkpoints. The bullish and bearish arguments are set out above, and the final judgment is left to the reader.
