Between Web2 Ad Revenue and the Web3 Pivot

02

Business structure

Pandora TV was founded in January 1999 and is an online streaming platform operator listed on KONEX. Once the largest domestic first-generation video sharing platform, it lost users after YouTube entered Korea in 2008, according to a Media Today report.

The company ended its video service on January 31, 2023, and then added blockchain-based software development and crypto-asset trading and brokerage to its business purpose, formally pivoting toward Web3. Its main products are KMPlayer, advertising, video platform fees, MovieBloc, and Cobak.

According to a Dealsite report, the 2024 revenue mix was advertising platforms at 50.6%, the video service platform at 28%, and the blockchain platform at 21.4%. The advertising share rose 3.9 percentage points from 2023, while the blockchain share fell 4.4 percentage points.

This structure makes advertising the central pillar of earnings. The company is trying to expand its Web3 ecosystem by linking KMPlayer's global traffic with Cobak and MovieBloc. Analysts, however, say both tokens have contributed little to blockchain revenue because of credibility and volatility issues.

MovieBloc was also once designated an investment-warning stock in 2022 over a gap between planned and actual circulating supply. The domestic video sharing market itself has been reshaped by growth at overseas platforms, and competition constrains the business as a whole.

03

Recent trends

On the basis of disclosed results, 2025 consolidated revenue was KRW 14.1 billion, operating profit KRW 1.7 billion, and net income KRW 0.9 billion.

Compared with 2024 consolidated revenue of KRW 16.7 billion, operating profit of KRW 1.3 billion, and net income of KRW 2.0 billion, revenue declined while operating profit rose.

FnGuide attributed the revenue decline to lower search advertising sales and crypto market volatility, and the profit improvement to cost cuts and operating efficiency. On a standalone basis, 2025 revenue was KRW 4,007 million, operating profit KRW 451 million, and a net loss of KRW 248 million.

Standalone revenue fell from KRW 4.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 4.0 billion in 2025. According to Dealsite, selling and administrative expenses in 2024 reached KRW 6.6 billion, up 53.5% from the prior year, a rise it linked to a larger share of spending on advertising and promotion.

On the same data, R&D spending as a share of revenue fell from 31.5% in 2022 to 25.1% in 2023 and 24.4% in 2024. As of October 2025, the company had 24 National Pension Service subscribers, three fewer than a year earlier.

At its annual general meeting on March 31, 2026, shareholders approved all main agenda items, including a KRW 1.0 billion cap on director compensation. As of 2:10 a.m. KST on November 11, 2026, the share price was KRW 245, up 8.41% from the previous close, with a market capitalization of KRW 2.9 billion. FnGuide's price summary showed a one-year return of -29.78% and a three-month return of -4.71%.

04

Outlook

The success of the Web3 pivot depends on how much blockchain revenue can grow. Dealsite reported that the drop in the blockchain platform share to 21.4% in 2024, along with lower headcount and R&D intensity, has raised concern that the company will struggle to move beyond its existing Web2 structure.

The company has proposed building up MovieBloc as a real-world asset (RWA) issuer specialized in the film industry and said at a mid-2025 investor briefing that it was running a social investment pilot for joint film financing.

At the same event, the CEO outlined plans for a Web3 toolbox launch and paid membership in the second half of 2025.

An industry source said cutting headcount and R&D spending could reflect a focus on existing cash cows, and suggested that upgrading reward systems to increase token value and circulation could be an alternative.

The Cobak token fell from the KRW 1,500 range in early 2025 to the KRW 500 range by the time of a December 2025 report. Because advertising platforms are the largest revenue source, trends in the ad market and search advertising sales are the key variables for the earnings direction. The next checkpoint is the revenue mix and selling and administrative expense trend in the Q3 2026 report.

05

Bull factors

Ad-centred revenue base

Advertising platforms were the largest part of 2024 revenue at 50.6%, up 3.9 percentage points from 2023. A structure centred on advertising may reduce exposure to price swings in the blockchain segment, which is one way to read the mix. However, lower search advertising sales weighed on 2025 results, so ad revenue trends remain the key variable.

Profit defended through cost efficiency

Operating profit rose in 2025 even as revenue fell, a result FnGuide linked to cost reduction and operating efficiency. The lower R&D intensity also points to a change on the cost side. Whether this cost base can hold as revenue shrinks is the question the figures leave open.

MovieBloc RWA expansion plan

The CEO said MovieBloc would expand into an RWA issuer focused on the film industry. The company said it was piloting social investment for joint film financing. Broadening its role as a security token issuer is a business direction that could become a new blockchain revenue line.

06

Bear factors

Revenue decline and softer search ads

Consolidated revenue in 2025 declined to KRW 14.1 billion from KRW 16.7 billion in 2024. FnGuide cited lower search advertising sales as a main cause. Selling and administrative expenses rose 53.5% in 2024, adding cost pressure as the revenue base narrowed.

Weak blockchain revenue and token performance

The blockchain platform's share of revenue fell 4.4 percentage points to 21.4% in 2024. The Cobak token dropped from the KRW 1,500 range in early 2025 to the KRW 500 range by December 2025. MovieBloc was once designated an investment-warning stock over a supply discrepancy in 2022.

Shrinking headcount and R&D investment

R&D spending as a share of revenue fell from 31.5% in 2022 to 24.4% in 2024. As of October 2025, National Pension Service subscribers numbered 24, down three from a year earlier.

Even as the company promotes a Web3 pivot, the shrinking technical workforce and investment raise questions about the pace of that transition.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Assessments point to the late-2000s real-name internet rules and copyright law amendments, which applied only to domestic platforms and gave overseas services like YouTube room to grow. The company lost users in that process, and its video service ended on January 31, 2023. Regulation of domestic platforms remains an external variable that can constrain strategic direction.

Crypto market

Crypto market volatility was cited as a factor behind the 2025 revenue decline. Blockchain revenue can swing sharply with token credibility and price movements. Both the Cobak and MovieBloc tokens have faced questions over credibility and volatility.

Liquidity and small-cap

With a market capitalization of KRW 2.9 billion, the company is a small KONEX-listed name with limited trading scale. Daily price moves are large, as shown by the 8.41% gain against the previous close. FnGuide's one-year return of -29.78% also reflects wide price swings.

08

Overall view

Pandora TV has restructured around advertising platforms and blockchain businesses after exiting its video platform. Consolidated operating profit rose to KRW 1.7 billion in 2025, which the company's cost reductions and efficiency gains help explain.

Revenue, however, fell to KRW 14.1 billion, with lower search advertising sales cited as the main cause. The blockchain platform's share dropped to 21.4% in 2024, and token performance and credibility remain open challenges. Shrinking R&D intensity and headcount add to questions about the pace of the Web3 pivot.

The MovieBloc RWA expansion and the linkage of KMPlayer traffic to Web3 tokens are business plans that could become new revenue sources, but the timing of any impact on reported results is still unclear.

As a small KONEX-listed company with a market capitalization of KRW 2.9 billion, trading volatility is also a factor to weigh.
