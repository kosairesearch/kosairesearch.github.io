02

Business structure

JS is a manufacturer of women's handbags and apparel that produces them on an original equipment manufacturing (OEM) basis for overseas brands. The company was founded in 1985 and set up local subsidiaries in China in 1994 and Indonesia in 1999 to widen its production base.

In the first half of 2025, handbag manufacturing revenue was KRW 157.7 billion, up 9.13% year on year, while apparel manufacturing revenue was KRW 379.9 billion, up 30.28%, making apparel the larger engine.

Reported figures show that about 72% of apparel sales depend on Gap and Old Navy, and about 45% of bag sales depend on GUESS. The acquisition of Grand Hyatt Seoul in June 2024 added a hotel segment, which operates under a management contract with a global brand.

According to News Way, the hotel segment booked roughly KRW 78.1 billion of revenue and KRW 11.0 billion of operating profit in July to December 2024, a 14.1% operating margin. From April 2026, the results of a garment OEM business acquired in 2026 have been consolidated.

Production sits in Boyolali, Indonesia and Guatemala, and factory expansions there coincided with the hotel purchase, lifting borrowings. Reported total borrowings were KRW 835 billion in the first half of 2025, still above KRW 800 billion.

Competition is framed against listed apparel OEM peers such as Youngone and Hansae, and dependence on a few overseas brands is the defining structural feature.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, revenue fell from KRW 975.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 862.8 billion in 2023 (down 11.5%), then rose to KRW 1.12 trillion in 2024 (up 29.5%) and KRW 1.29 trillion in 2025 (up 15.3%), growing for two consecutive years, with 2025 the largest year in 2022–2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 81.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 84 billion in 2023 (up 3.2%), KRW 121.3 billion in 2024 (up 44.3%) and KRW 140.5 billion in 2025 (up 15.9%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023. The operating margin climbed from 8.4% in 2022 to 10.9% in 2025 and stayed at 10.9% after 2024.

The hotel segment's addition appears to have influenced the 2024 expansion, and News Way reported that hotel revenue in July to December 2024 was about KRW 78.1 billion with operating profit of KRW 11.0 billion, a 14.1% margin.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 64.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 44.3 billion in 2023 (down 31.4%), KRW 97.4 billion in 2024 (up 120.0%) and KRW 76.6 billion in 2025 (down 21.4%).

The 2024 jump in net income, well above operating profit growth, appears to reflect one-off gains, and Eugene Investment & Securities estimated that excluding a one-off gain of KRW 29.1 billion in 3Q 2024, net income would have been about KRW 14.8 billion.

In 2025 operating profit rose while controlling-shareholder net income fell, and net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 85.8 billion against KRW 76.6 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

The debt ratio rose from 108.9% in 2022 to 260.8% in 2024 before easing to 242.9% in 2025, while operating cash flow fell from KRW 123.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 36.1 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 127.9 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit moved from KRW 41.1 billion in 3Q 2025 and KRW 42.9 billion in 4Q 2025 down to KRW 30.8 billion in 1Q 2026 (down 28.3% from the prior quarter), then recovered to KRW 56.5 billion in 2Q 2026 (up 83.4%) and KRW 53.9 billion in 3Q 2026 (up 31.2% year on year).

Revenue rose for two consecutive quarters, to KRW 453.5 billion in 2Q 2026 and KRW 480.2 billion in 3Q 2026, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 91.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

End-market demand depends heavily on US consumer spending and trade policy.

A March 2025 report from Shinhan Investment Corp. said US consumption had shifted from premium goods toward more value-driven purchases, and it noted planned capacity increases of 21% for bags and 9.5% for apparel that year in response to order trends.

Reporting in the same year pointed to dupe-driven consumption as supportive for customers' sales. Meanwhile, tariffs weighed on handbag and apparel results in 3Q and 4Q 2025.

In an April 2026 report, Shinhan Investment Corp. projected dollar-based growth for 1Q and 2Q 2026 of -2% and +4% for handbags and +10% and +20% for apparel, and expected growth to accelerate in the second half on a low base from the prior year.

The same report forecast 1Q 2026 revenue of KRW 312.9 billion, while reported 1Q 2026 revenue of KRW 308 billion came in below that estimate. Listed apparel OEM peers such as Youngone and Hansae serve as the natural comparison set, and JS differs by carrying hotel income as a non-manufacturing revenue stream.

The cycle position is one shaped by tariffs and consumer sentiment, and the second-half 2026 results will show how far the recovery in these forecasts carries.

Outlook

Full-year 2026 results will be fixed in the 2026 annual report, due by the end of March 2027. Because Ontide has been consolidated since April 2026, year-on-year growth in 1Q 2027 will still reflect the acquisition's effect.

A JS subsidiary acquired 33.45% of Ontide's common shares and all of its convertible preferred shares, which the company disclosed as giving it de facto control.

The hotel operates under a management contract with a global brand, but News Way reported in April 2025 that operating and renovation costs fall partly on JS, leaving rising fixed costs as a profitability variable.

On debt, the company said it would fund repayments through extended short-term borrowing deadlines and free cash flow, as reported in 2025. Operating cash flow recovered to KRW 127.9 billion in 2025, which matters for that funding plan.

Reporting for the first half of 2025 said capital expenditure fell sharply to KRW 4.3 billion, easing investment pressure. A March 2025 Shinhan Investment Corp. report referenced capacity additions for bags and apparel at the time, and whether overseas production expansion widens the revenue base remains the question.

The next checkpoints are the 4Q 2026 preliminary results and the operating margin and borrowing level in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Operating margin held near 11%

The operating margin rose from 8.4% in 2022 to 10.9% in 2025, and 3Q 2026 operating profit of KRW 53.9 billion was up 31.2% year on year. The hotel segment's 14.1% operating margin in July to December 2024 is cited as a factor lifting the consolidated margin.

Quarterly operating profit recovered from KRW 30.8 billion in 1Q 2026 to KRW 56.5 billion in 2Q and KRW 53.9 billion in 3Q.

Garment base widened through consolidation

Ontide's results have been consolidated since April 2026, and 2Q 2026 revenue of KRW 453.5 billion rose 47.2% from the prior quarter. Whether the effect of gaining control carries into later quarterly revenue is the next point to check. The wider garment OEM capacity is cited as the basis for a larger revenue base.

Cash flow recovery and repayment plan

Operating cash flow recovered from KRW 36.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 127.9 billion in 2025. Reporting for the first half of 2025 said capital expenditure fell to KRW 4.3 billion, leaving free cash flow of KRW 19.2 billion. The company said it would fund repayments through extended short-term borrowing deadlines and free cash flow.

09

Bear factors

Borrowing dependence and a high debt ratio

The debt ratio rose from 108.9% in 2022 to 260.8% in 2024 and stood at 242.9% at year-end 2025. Total borrowings staying high is a factor holding up the debt ratio. With borrowings this large, interest rate moves and repayment timing can weigh directly on earnings and cash flow.

Dependence on a few overseas brands

Reporting puts about 72% of apparel sales with Gap and Old Navy and about 45% of bag sales with GUESS. A cut in orders from one brand could move utilization and revenue together. Given this concentration, whether the customer base broadens is a key point to monitor.

Narrow conversion into shareholder profit

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 97.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 76.6 billion in 2025, down 21.4%, while operating profit rose 15.9% in the same year. Interest costs and the share belonging to non-controlling interests appear to reduce the portion attributable to controlling shareholders. Rising fixed costs, including hotel renovations, are also cited as a profitability variable.

10

Risk factors

Tariffs and trade policy

Analysts pointed to tariffs weighing on handbag and apparel results in 3Q and 4Q 2025. Orders from overseas brand customers are sensitive to US consumption and trade policy, so changes in tariff rules can affect orders and margins quickly. Whether revenue growth from 3Q 2026 continues is the checkpoint for this risk.

Financial leverage

Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 1.28 trillion, larger than total equity of KRW 527.5 billion. Because borrowing rose with the hotel purchase and factory expansions, interest rates and refinancing terms could weigh on earnings. Whether the repayment deadline extensions and free cash flow plan are carried out as stated needs confirmation.

Operations and integration

Integrating Ontide's production and organization could weigh on production efficiency. The hotel is run under a management contract, but JS bears part of the operating and renovation costs. Both the hotel and manufacturing carry fixed-cost elements, so a drop in orders could widen earnings swings.

12

Overall view

JS is an OEM company that has grown revenue and operating profit together since 2022, spanning apparel and handbags. Revenue of KRW 1.29 trillion and operating profit of KRW 140.5 billion in 2025 were the highest levels in 2022–2025.

Quarterly operating profit recovered in 2Q and 3Q 2026 after the 1Q low, and the Ontide consolidation and the hotel segment have become central to revenue.

On the other side, the 242.9% debt ratio, total liabilities of KRW 1.28 trillion and a customer base concentrated on a few overseas brands remain the main negatives.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 97.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 76.6 billion in 2025, a period in which operating profit growth did not carry through to shareholder profit.

The price-to-book ratio is below 1x, and broker comparisons have placed the earnings multiple below the apparel OEM average. The next fixed checkpoint is the operating margin and borrowing level in the 2026 annual report. Bullish and bearish factors remain in balance.
