02

Business structure

Dongyang Express was founded in 1968 and listed on the KOSPI market in 2005. Its core business is intercity express, charter and regional bus transport, supported by terminal operations and vehicle maintenance.

The company owns terminals in Masan, Asan and Pyeongtaek, and runs bus maintenance facilities in Asan and Anyang. Demand is tied to long-distance passenger travel. An analysis based on cumulative results through the third quarter of 2025 credited premium coach introductions with improving gross profit.

Centralized dispatch and integrated vehicle management have also been cited as supporting cost reduction. The company holds a 0.17% stake in the Seoul Express Bus Terminal, linking its asset value indirectly to the terminal site.

Segment revenue shares are not presented in numbers here; passenger transport is the center of the business, with terminals and maintenance supporting it.

Chunil Express, a listed express bus peer, is the second-largest shareholder of the Seoul Express Bus Terminal with a 16.67% stake, and the two often move together on the same redevelopment theme. Socar operates a different business model but is often compared within the transport sector.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 104.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 120 billion in 2023 (up 14.9% year on year), KRW 121.7 billion in 2024 (up 1.5%) and KRW 125.2 billion in 2025 (up 2.8%). Revenue has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating loss narrowed from KRW 11.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 288 million in 2023, then widened to KRW 1.53 billion in 2024. The company returned to operating profit of KRW 5.1 billion in 2025, with the operating margin moving from -10.7% in 2022 and -0.2% in 2023 to -1.3% in 2024 and 4.1% in 2025.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 12.8 billion in 2022, KRW 3.09 billion in 2023 and KRW 16.7 billion in 2024, before turning to a profit of KRW 2.97 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 2.95 billion in 2025, a different figure from the profit attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow improved from negative KRW 5 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.81 billion in 2023, KRW 7.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.36 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the fourth quarter of 2025 marked a peak, with revenue of KRW 35 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.51 billion, up 362.4% from the prior quarter. Operating profit then fell to KRW 622 million in the first quarter of 2026, down 82.3% from the prior quarter.

The second quarter swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.4 billion, and the third quarter narrowed the loss to KRW 140 million. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 32.6 billion rose 8.6% year on year, but the operating result turned to a loss from a year-earlier profit.

Revenue has held in the low-to-mid KRW 30 billion range, while the thin margin leaves quarterly operating results sensitive to swings.

05

Industry analysis

Intercity bus operating results are directly affected by passenger demand, fares, fuel costs and vehicle depreciation. Expansion of exclusive bus lanes has been cited as improving schedule reliability and competitiveness. Expansion of transfer rest areas is linked to more mid-trip boardings in smaller cities.

The spread of premium coaches has become a fare-based differentiator. Long-distance travel competes with rail and other alternative transport modes. Dongyang Express has a smaller operating base than peers such as Lotte Rental, AJ Networks and Red Cap Tour, and it is classified as a small-cap stock.

Within the sector it trails on scale but has been noted for a fast profit recovery rate. Its shared stake in the Seoul Express Bus Terminal with Chunil Express is a sector-specific factor.

Because fuel and vehicle costs weigh heavily on quarterly operating margins, fare levels and cost trends should be tracked together each quarter.

06

Outlook

Seoul City has designated operators for pre-negotiations on the mixed-use redevelopment of the Seoul Express Bus Terminal site in Seocho-gu. Two developers were chosen as pre-negotiation counterparts in September 2025, including a Shinsegae group affiliate.

The plan reportedly includes a mixed-use tower of up to about 60 floors with cultural and convenience facilities, with the bus terminal and parking placed underground. Media reports said terminal shareholders could realize significant development gains once the project is completed.

However, the company's 0.17% stake is small, so how much development value reaches its results will depend on the project structure and the terms of any stake settlement.

One analysis said the core business has not fully regained its earnings capacity and that the redevelopment pace and detailed project structure remain uncertain. In May 2026, the stock rose 81.77% in a single week and ranked first in weekly gains on the KOSPI.

Whether recovery in core earnings keeps pace with the development timeline is the key item to monitor. Because 2026 fourth-quarter results have not yet been filed in a periodic report, the direction of full-year 2026 operating results is the next checkpoint.

Bull factors

Asset revaluation expectations

The company holds a 0.17% stake in the Seoul Express Bus Terminal, so the value of the site could be reflected in its assets if the city-led mixed-use redevelopment proceeds. Reports describe a mixed-use tower of up to about 60 floors with an underground terminal.

However, the small stake and undetermined project structure and settlement terms leave the extent of any benefit uncertain.

Annual turnaround and improving cash flow

Operating profit of KRW 5.1 billion in 2025 marked a turnaround from the 2024 operating loss, with an operating margin of 4.1%. Operating cash flow rose from negative KRW 5 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.36 billion in 2025. Revenue has also grown for three consecutive years, reaching KRW 125.2 billion in 2025.

Improving direction in the balance sheet

The debt ratio declined from 208.4% in 2024 to 186.4% in 2025. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a profit of KRW 93.94 million in the third quarter of 2026. If quarterly profit recovery continues, it could support further reduction of the debt burden.

Bear factors

Renewed quarterly operating losses

Operating profit in the first quarter of 2026 fell 82.3% from the prior quarter. The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 712 million.

High debt burden

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 102.3 billion, exceeding total equity of KRW 54.9 billion. The debt ratio fell to 186.4% but remains high. With a small earnings base, interest and borrowing costs could amplify volatility in results.

Theme-driven trading and development uncertainty

The share price has repeatedly surged and fallen within short periods on news about the Seoul Express Bus Terminal redevelopment. On October 12, 2026, volume reached 2.14 million shares and the intraday range exceeded 35% of the prior close. One analysis pointed to uncertainty over the pace and structure of the project.

Risk factors

Development timing and structure risk

Administrative steps after pre-negotiations and the final project structure have not been set, so the development timeline could slip. With a 0.17% stake, the share of development gains attributable to the company may be limited. A delay in redevelopment news could weaken asset-revaluation expectations.

Transport cost risk

Passenger demand, fares, fuel costs and vehicle depreciation directly affect operating results. Cost pressure may have contributed to the operating losses in the second and third quarters of 2026. With a thin operating margin, higher fuel prices or vehicle costs could deepen quarterly losses.

Small-cap liquidity and volatility

As a small-cap stock with limited market capitalization and float, the share price reacts strongly to news flow. In May 2026 the stock rose 81.77% in one week, and the swings since then have been large. If theme-driven demand fades, the share price could move sharply regardless of earnings.

Overall view

Dongyang Express turned from losses to profits in 2025, with operating profit of KRW 5.1 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.97 billion, while revenue has grown for three consecutive years since 2023.

However, operating losses returned in the second and third quarters of 2026, so it is too early to say core earnings capacity has stabilized. The debt ratio fell to 186.4% in 2025, but total liabilities still exceed total equity.

The Seoul Express Bus Terminal redevelopment is an asset-value theme linked indirectly through a 0.17% stake, and reports of pre-negotiation designation and shareholder development gains exist, but the project structure and timing are not yet set.

The share price has moved sharply on development news and theme-driven trading, so its link to reported earnings has been weak.

Bullish factors include the annual turnaround, improving cash flow and development expectations, while bearish factors include renewed quarterly losses, high leverage and theme-driven volatility.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary 2026 full-year results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, followed by whether losses persist in the first-quarter 2027 report.
