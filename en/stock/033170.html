02

Business structure

Signetics is a back-end semiconductor company that assembles chips produced in the front-end process into packages and tests their electrical characteristics.

It handles packaging for both memory and non-memory semiconductors, and its main product lines are reported as laminate packages, flip-chip packages and leadframe packages. According to a technical analysis report from March 2021, 67% of sales came from non-memory semiconductors.

Its core technology is the Recon flip chip, which the company has developed and is now mass-producing.

To serve lighter and more multifunctional electronics, it is also investing in LAB technology for SiP, Large Body and Fine Pitch packages, and it has secured initial mass-production infrastructure for its Advanced SiP Module. Its customers are reported to be domestic and overseas semiconductor companies.

The same 2021 report said reduced chip shipments from Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and Broadcom led to weaker related packaging demand. Competitiveness depends on packaging technology and equipment utilization.

A fnguide company summary said that as of the first three quarters of 2025, weaker semiconductor demand, fewer customer orders and lower utilization raised fixed-cost burdens and costs.

Unlike equipment-focused listed peers such as HANMI Semiconductor and EO Technics, Signetics sells packaging and test services directly, so its results track customer utilization closely.

The largest shareholder group, including the company's own shareholder and related parties, holds 38.56% according to the fnguide market summary, with the last change dated August 12, 2024.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue went from KRW 287.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 185.5 billion in 2023 (down 35.5% year on year), KRW 118.2 billion in 2024 (down 36.3%) and KRW 103 billion in 2025 (down 12.8%), so revenue has fallen for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Among the 2022 to 2025 years, 2022 was the largest and 2025 the smallest. Operating profit of KRW 8.88 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 15 billion in 2023, widened to an operating loss of KRW 25.8 billion in 2024, and narrowed to an operating loss of KRW 23.8 billion in 2025.

The operating margin was 3.1% in 2022, -8.1% in 2023, -21.9% in 2024 and -23.1% in 2025. In 2025 the operating loss shrank, but because sales fell, the operating margin was slightly worse than in 2024.

For 2025, the company cited weaker semiconductor market conditions as the main reason for lower sales, and said a smaller recognition of impairment losses on property, plant and equipment versus the prior year reduced the pre-tax loss from continuing operations, according to a February 20, 2026 disclosure reported by Digital Today.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 7.53 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 15.4 billion in 2023, KRW 50.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 18.3 billion in 2025.

The 2024 net loss was larger than the operating loss, which suggests non-operating items such as impairment losses likely contributed to the wider loss that year.

Operating cash flow was KRW 14.4 billion in 2022 and KRW 4.49 billion in 2023, then turned to -KRW 3.54 billion in 2024 and -KRW 21.5 billion in 2025, so the cash outflow grew.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 25.8 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 25.7 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 26.5 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 33.5 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 26.7 billion in Q3 2026, with Q2 2026 the highest in the period.

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 3.99 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 461 million in Q2 2026 but widened to KRW 2.88 billion in Q3 2026 from the prior quarter. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.53 billion in Q3 2026, against KRW 566 million in Q4 2025 and KRW 3.87 billion a year earlier.

The company has reported losses for five consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026, and the combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters was KRW 8.82 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Results in back-end semiconductor services are closely tied to the utilization rates and inventory cycles of downstream memory and system chip makers. When customers cut shipments, packaging and test volumes fall with them, and when utilization drops, fixed-cost pressure weighs on earnings.

A fnguide company summary also said that as of the first three quarters of 2025, weaker semiconductor demand, fewer customer orders and lower utilization were behind the sales decline and deteriorating profitability.

Across the industry, the importance of packaging and test processes is rising as demand for advanced packaging grows. How much of that trend reaches Signetics' sales can only be gauged from quarterly revenue.

Among listed peers in the same KOSDAQ semiconductor sector, equipment makers such as HANMI Semiconductor, EO Technics, Wonik IPS and PSK tend to see results move with equipment orders and installation schedules.

Signetics sells packaging and test services directly, so its revenue moves with utilization and shipment volumes, a different structure. The mix between memory and non-memory work also changes how sensitive results are to each demand cycle.

Whether the KRW 33.5 billion revenue in Q2 2026 signals a broader recovery or a temporary volume increase needs to be confirmed in later quarters, especially since revenue fell to KRW 26.7 billion in Q3 2026.

How much these three areas will contribute to revenue is not broken out separately in quarterly results.

At the regular shareholders' meeting on March 27, 2026, the financial statements, a partial amendment to the articles of incorporation (adding wholesale and retail to the business purpose) and the re-election of one outside director were all approved.

Whether the added business purpose leads to a change in direction needs to be confirmed through later disclosures.

Revenue rebound in Q2 2026

Investment in advanced packaging technology

According to a fnguide company summary, as of the first three quarters of 2025 the company was expanding Recon flip chip mass production and investing in LAB technology and the Advanced SiP Module.

Growing demand for highly integrated packages such as SiP could lead to new business revenue, but the disclosures do not break out how much these investments contribute to sales.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 21.5 billion in 2025, a larger outflow than -KRW 3.54 billion in 2024. Total liabilities of KRW 54.9 billion exceed total equity of KRW 52.2 billion, and the debt ratio rose to 105.2% at the end of 2025 from 52.2% in 2024.

Losses widened again in Q3 2026

Risk factors

Industry cycle and utilization

The company cited weaker semiconductor market conditions as the main reason for lower 2025 sales. Back-end revenue is sensitive to customer shipments and equipment utilization.
