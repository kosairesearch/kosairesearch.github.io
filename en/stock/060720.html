Foldable Hinge Recovery, New Business Pivot

02

Business structure

Founded in 1995 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2002, KH Vatec is a precision metal-processing parts maker whose core business is the hinge assembly module used in foldable smartphones.

On a cumulative nine-month basis through Q3 2025, the product revenue mix was 63.5% for the assembly module (foldable hinge) segment, 30.9% for other items including metal casings, and 5.5% for automotive parts.

According to company data released in February, full-year 2025 total revenue was KRW 424.9 billion, of which hinge (assembly module) revenue was KRW 253.9 billion.

The company's customer base is skewed toward Samsung Electronics, with roughly 70-80% of revenue estimated to be Samsung-related, which is the structural reason quarterly results are so closely tied to Samsung's new foldable phone launch schedule and sales volumes.

Beyond its core hinge business, the company is building automotive parts (end plates, moving mechanisms) and robot casing parts as new growth drivers, leveraging its aluminum die-casting and precision machining capabilities.

KH Vatec said it signed a supply contract for 53 types of cobot casing parts with Korean robotics company Rainbow Robotics, with the parts to be applied across all eight models of Rainbow Robotics' RB cobot lineup.

It is also developing its own reduction gears for robot actuation and conducting performance evaluations, extending beyond casing-part supply.

As smartphone form factors standardized into bar shapes, most hinge part makers disappeared from the industry, but hinges became a key premium smartphone component again once foldable phones emerged in 2019, with technical requirements becoming far more demanding than before.

This has kept the supplier base for foldable hinges limited given high technical barriers, though the possibility of new entrants catching up is a recurring theme.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue declined from KRW 363.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 363.6 billion in 2023 (-0.1%) and KRW 311.0 billion in 2024 (-14.5%), before rebounding to KRW 424.9 billion in 2025 (+36.6%).

Operating profit followed a similar path, moving from KRW 32.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 33.6 billion in 2023 (+3.4%), KRW 21.9 billion in 2024 (-35.0%), and KRW 31.8 billion in 2025 (+45.3%).

However, net income attributable to owners peaked at KRW 30.9 billion in 2023 and fell for two consecutive years to KRW 21.3 billion in 2024 (-31.1%) and KRW 20.4 billion in 2025 (-4.3%), meaning the operating-level recovery did not fully carry through to the bottom line.

The operating margin fell from 9.2% in 2023 to 7.0% in 2024 before a modest recovery to 7.5% in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue peaked at KRW 168.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 14.1 billion in Q3 2025, before plunging to revenue of KRW 58.5 billion (-65.3%) and operating profit of KRW 2.35 billion (-83.3%) in Q4 2025, underscoring pronounced seasonality.

The slowdown continued into Q1 2026 with revenue of KRW 55.1 billion (-5.8%) and operating profit of KRW 1.68 billion (-28.5%), before rebounding in Q2 2026 to revenue of KRW 103.9 billion (+88.6% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 4.97 billion (+195.7% quarter on quarter).

Even so, Q2 2026 revenue and operating profit were down 23.9% and 57.1%, respectively, from a year earlier, indicating the recovery is not yet complete on a year-on-year basis.

Net income attributable to owners rose to KRW 11.4 billion in Q3 2025, then fell sharply to KRW 1.77 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 293 million in Q1 2026, before recovering to KRW 3.09 billion in Q2 2026, with the trailing four quarters (Q3 2025-Q2 2026) totaling KRW 16.5 billion.

Operating cash flow shrank from KRW 38.9 billion in 2022 and KRW 37.3 billion in 2023 to KRW 15.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 15.1 billion in 2025, showing cash generation remains below earlier levels despite the revenue recovery.

05

Industry analysis

The foldable smartphone market has largely grown around the expansion of Samsung Electronics' Galaxy Z Fold and Flip lineup, and Samsung selected KH Vatec as its hinge parts supplier when it first launched a foldable phone in 2019, and has chosen KH Vatec as its foldable hinge supplier every year since.

Industry observers note that if Apple launches a foldable phone in the second half of 2026, the global foldable phone market could shift to sharp growth in 2027.

However, KH Vatec does not supply hinges to Apple directly; Apple's entry is interpreted as an indirect benefit by expanding the overall foldable phone market.

At the same time, a risk flagged amid Samsung's rising foldable phone sales is the possibility that the hinge supplier base expands, pressuring KH Vatec's market share and average selling price.

After most feature-phone-era hinge part makers disappeared once smartphone form factors standardized into bar shapes, the advent of foldable phones re-established a supplier-concentrated structure centered on high technical barriers.

Markets for robotics, automotive, and AI data center metal parts remain a small share of KH Vatec's revenue, but are viewed as early-stage growth markets attracting entrants with precision metal-processing capabilities.

06

Outlook

The company is pursuing diversification into automotive, AI data center, and robotics parts, and analysts note that automotive segment revenue from brackets and moving mechanisms is expected to begin partial recognition by the end of this year, with full-scale deliveries expected from 2027 onward.

Metal parts and high-heat-dissipation connectors for AI data centers are also under development, with full-scale sales planned for 2027 following customer evaluation within the year, while the robotics segment, also viewed as a mid- to long-term growth driver, is supplying cobot parts to a domestic robotics company, and the company has completed development of its own reduction gear design and is conducting performance evaluation.

SK Securities assessed that KH Vatec would transform into a robotics and automotive parts company in earnest from the second half of 2026, reducing its smartphone dependence and securing new growth drivers.

The same report said the company is targeting KRW 600 billion in revenue by 2028, with new business revenue including robotics expanding to around KRW 150 billion, and it has set a goal of KRW 140-150 billion in robotics segment revenue by 2030 through expanding its cobot casing parts business.

Brokerages expect foldable-phone-related external hinge revenue to be recognized more fully from the third quarter of 2026 onward, while also taking a conservative approach by trimming full-year estimates from a year earlier, making both the pace of new-business monetization and foldable phone order trends worth monitoring.

08

Bull factors

Dominant Hinge Supplier Position

Samsung selected KH Vatec as its hinge supplier when it first launched a foldable phone in 2019 and has chosen KH Vatec as its foldable hinge supplier every year since. If new form factors such as tri-fold models are added, both hinge volume and unit prices could rise. This long-standing supply relationship is viewed as evidence of technical reliability.

Progress on Business Diversification

The company signed a supply contract for 53 types of cobot casing parts with Rainbow Robotics, to be applied across Rainbow Robotics' entire RB series lineup. Automotive and AI data center parts are also progressing through development, offering scope for revenue diversification over the medium to long term. Concrete contract wins that reduce customer concentration are viewed positively.

Expectation of Foldable Market Expansion

There is a view that if Apple launches a foldable phone in the second half of 2026, the global foldable market could shift to sharp growth in 2027. Although Apple is not a direct customer, an expanding overall market could provide indirect benefits. Combined with Samsung's lineup diversification, this could widen the base of hinge demand.

Bear factors

High Customer Concentration

With roughly 70-80% of revenue estimated to be Samsung Electronics-related, results can swing significantly with changes in a single customer's product strategy. Slow customer diversification could also weaken bargaining power. This structure is likely to persist until new businesses reach meaningful scale.

Pronounced Earnings Seasonality

Quarterly revenue swung sharply from KRW 168.4 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 58.5 billion in Q4 2025, a 65.3% decline. The slowdown continued into Q1 2026 at KRW 55.1 billion before rebounding in Q2, making it difficult to judge trends from any single quarter. Revenue recognition timing is highly dependent on new product launch schedules.

Potential Delay in New Business Monetization

Automotive brackets, moving mechanisms, and AI data center metal parts are expected to see only partial recognition by year-end, with full-scale revenue anticipated in 2027, meaning their current contribution to total revenue is limited.

Delays during customer evaluation and mass-production validation cannot be ruled out. More time appears necessary before new businesses meaningfully scale up.

Risk factors

Pricing and Competition Risk

Even amid rising Samsung foldable phone sales, an expanding hinge supplier base could pressure KH Vatec's market share and average selling price. If competitors join the supply chain, pricing power could weaken. Maintaining a technology gap remains the key variable.

Currency Fluctuation

Given the business's high export exposure, won-dollar exchange rate movements can affect profitability. Past analysis also noted that whether the won-dollar exchange rate environment is favorable has been cited as a factor in earnings assessments. An unfavorable currency shift could increase cost burdens.

New Business Investment Burden

Rising automotive R&D costs, the pace of global foldable market growth, and limited initial shipment volumes for new models have been cited as risk factors. R&D spending on developing proprietary robot reduction gears and AI data center parts could weigh on near-term profitability. A period of cost front-loading ahead of new business monetization is possible.

Overall view

KH Vatec emerged from 2024's slump with 2025 revenue and operating profit up 36.6% and 45.3%, respectively, yet net income attributable to owners fell for a second consecutive year and quarterly volatility has remained wide into 2026.

The earnings base still rests on the Samsung Electronics foldable hinge business, and the high customer concentration is both a strength and a risk factor.

Diversification into automotive, AI data center, and robotics parts is backed by concrete contracts and development progress, but meaningful revenue contribution is generally flagged for around 2027, limiting any immediate earnings boost.

Operating cash flow has declined from earlier levels even as the debt-to-equity ratio has been kept relatively stable. Brokerages have maintained their ratings while lowering target prices, suggesting the market is weighing near-term earnings softness against the medium- to long-term growth narrative.

The next earnings release and the pace of new-business monetization appear to be the key variables for future assessment.
