Report overview

Foldable Hinge Recovery, a Swing to Loss, and the New-Business Test

KH Vatec's revenue rose for two straight quarters through 3Q 2026 on a recovery in foldable hinges, but the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in 3Q 2026, and its new businesses in AI data centers, automotive and robotics are still at an early stage.

  1. 1

    3Q 2026 revenue was KRW 141.7 billion, up 36.4% from the prior quarter but down 15.8% from a year earlier. Operating profit was KRW 7.18 billion, up 44.3% from the prior quarter.

  2. 2

    3Q 2026 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.15 billion, a swing into loss both from the prior quarter and from a year earlier. The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 994 million.

  3. 3

    2025 revenue was KRW 424.9 billion, up 36.6% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 31.8 billion, up 45.3%. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell for a second straight year to KRW 20.4 billion.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

KH Vatec was founded in 1995, listed on KOSDAQ in 2002, and specializes in foldable hinge, automotive and robotics parts.

Based on the cumulative consolidated figures for the first nine months of 2025, product mix was 63.5% assembly modules (foldable hinges), 30.9% other items such as metal cases, and 5.5% automotive parts.

The core of the assembly-module business is hinges for Samsung Electronics' foldable phones, and 2025 hinge revenue was reported at KRW 253.9 billion. Its main customer is Samsung Electronics, and a February 2026 research report estimated Samsung-related sales at roughly 70 to 80 percent of the total.

The same report expected the share of key customers to decline as automotive and robotics parts grow. Production bases are centered on Vietnam (Thai Nguyen and Hanoi) and India, while the Huizhou unit in China handles metal die-casting and module assembly for phones.

Automotive parts include battery end plates, heat sinks and display brackets, and a February 2026 report said supply of about KRW 96.9 billion to Hyundai Mobis through the Thai Nguyen unit was ongoing. In April 2026 the company said its Indian unit was registered as a supplier to Visteon.

The company says it is extending design and tooling know-how from foldable hinges into vehicle display brackets.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, 2025 revenue was KRW 424.9 billion, up 36.6% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 31.8 billion, up 45.3%, making 2025 the largest revenue year in the 2022 to 2025 period. 2024 was the smallest year, with revenue of KRW 311 billion, down 14.5%, and operating profit of KRW 21.9 billion, down 35.0%.

According to press reports, hinge revenue from Samsung Electronics' foldable phones declined for the first time in 2024 and rebounded 44% within a year in 2025. The operating margin moved from 8.9% in 2022 and 9.2% in 2023 down to 7.0% in 2024 and 7.5% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 30.9 billion in 2023, then fell 31.1% to KRW 21.3 billion in 2024 and 4.3% to KRW 20.4 billion in 2025, a second straight annual decline. Net profit including non-controlling interests followed the same path.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 38.9 billion in 2022 and KRW 37.3 billion in 2023 to KRW 15.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 15.1 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 50.3% in 2022 to 38.6% in 2023, then rose to 47.6% in 2024 and 46.6% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 275.7 billion and total liabilities were KRW 128.5 billion.

On a quarterly basis, 4Q 2025 revenue was KRW 58.5 billion, down 65.3% from the prior quarter, and operating profit was KRW 2.35 billion. 1Q 2026 was the low point at KRW 55.1 billion of revenue and KRW 1.68 billion of operating profit, followed by KRW 103.9 billion and KRW 4.97 billion in 2Q and KRW 141.7 billion and KRW 7.18 billion in 3Q.

Daishin Securities attributed the 3Q 2026 swing to a loss, which yielded a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 6.15 billion, to items including foreign-exchange translation losses.

05

Industry analysis

The key driver of the downstream market is Samsung Electronics' foldable phone sales and production volume. Daishin Securities attributed the 3Q 2026 improvement to the launch of three foldable models and solid sales of the Galaxy Z8 Wide.

The same brokerage expects earnings to slow in 4Q 2026 because of lower smartphone production and cost pressure from higher memory chip prices. On pricing, an April 2025 Daishin initiation estimated that the average supply price of foldable hinge modules would rise about 25 to 30 percent year on year.

In AI data centers, a November 2026 report cited TrendForce's forecast that the share of AI chips adopting liquid cooling would expand from 33% in 2025 to 60% in 2027. The government has announced plans to build 18.4 gigawatts of AI data center capacity in Korea by 2035.

KH Vatec plans to enter first as a production partner for an overseas cooling-module company, build mass-production track record, and then expand into domestic supply. In automotive, digital cockpit display brackets are emerging as a key product.

On positioning, the company is moving precision metal processing skills built in smartphone structures into AI cooling and vehicle parts.

06

Outlook

A November 2026 report said KH Vatec announced its mid-term management vision 'WISE210' and a target of 20% average annual revenue growth. Daishin Securities estimated 4Q 2026 revenue at KRW 63.7 billion and operating profit at KRW 2.6 billion, implying a slowdown from 3Q.

The same brokerage expects revenue diversification into AI data centers, robotics and vehicle electronics to become full-scale in 2027. For AI data center cooling parts, the plan is to produce inner manifolds, cold plates and server casting housings in one line at the Thai Nguyen base in Vietnam.

The company is negotiating component supply with a global cooling-module maker, and no contract signing has been announced.

On the automotive side, the Indian unit is registered as a Visteon supplier, and the Vietnam unit plans to begin mass production of display brackets at year-end 2026, with supply routed through the Indian unit. Robotics parts are described as being at an initial supply stage.

For reporting, the 2026 annual report will be the formal basis for full-year results. 4Q 2026 results are expected in early 2027 after a preliminary release, and the share of new-business revenue and the pace of operating margin recovery will shape the outlook.

08

Bull factors

Hinge revenue recovery and two straight quarters of growth

2Q 2026 revenue was KRW 103.9 billion, up 88.6% from the prior quarter, and 3Q 2026 revenue rose a further 36.4% to KRW 141.7 billion. Daishin Securities said 3Q results beat its prior estimates, and assembly-module revenue including hinges reached KRW 105.4 billion, up 51.7% from the prior quarter. Press reports described 2025 hinge revenue of KRW 253.9 billion as the highest on record.

Wider entry into new-business supply chains

The Indian unit is registered as a Visteon supplier for digital cockpit display brackets, and Vietnam mass production is scheduled to begin at year-end 2026. A February 2026 report said supply of about KRW 96.9 billion of automotive parts to Hyundai Mobis through the Vietnam unit was ongoing. AI data center liquid-cooling parts are under supply negotiation with a global cooling-module maker.

Financial capacity and asset base

The debt-to-equity ratio at end-2025 was 46.6%, lower than 50.3% in 2022. Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 275.7 billion and total liabilities were KRW 128.5 billion. With the price-to-book ratio below 1x, the pace of earnings recovery is the key variable for valuation.

09

Bear factors

Dependence on Samsung Electronics and smartphone production swings

Samsung-related sales are estimated at roughly 70 to 80 percent of the total, according to a February 2026 report, so results depend heavily on foldable phone production and sales. Daishin Securities cited lower smartphone production as a drag on 4Q 2026. Until automotive and robotics sales grow, this concentration is likely to persist.

Weaker profitability and a swing to loss

3Q 2026 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.15 billion, a swing into loss, and operating profit fell 49.1% from a year earlier. The operating margin was 7.0% in 2024 and 7.5% in 2025, below 8.9% in 2022 and 9.2% in 2023.

Cost pressure and weaker cash generation

Daishin Securities expects memory chip price increases to weigh on 4Q 2026 earnings through higher costs. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 38.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.1 billion in 2025. Foreign-exchange translation losses were reported as part of the 3Q 2026 net results.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration

Heavy reliance on one customer means model changes or production adjustments at Samsung Electronics pass through directly to results. Hinge revenue recovered in 2025, but whether the next foldable generation carries similar volume has not been confirmed. The pace of customer diversification determines how large this risk is.

New-business execution and contract uncertainty

The company itself said that prototyping and stable mass production of AI data center cooling parts are very different challenges. Supply contracts with a cooling-module maker are still under negotiation, and Visteon-bound bracket production begins at year-end 2026 or later. If new-business revenue arrives late, it will be harder to offset swings in hinge-driven earnings.

Cost and currency volatility

Higher memory chip prices could feed into cost pressure alongside smartphone production adjustments at customers. Foreign-exchange items have been large enough to swing quarterly net results. Operating cash flow has stayed in the KRW 15 billion range since 2024, which could affect investment capacity.

Overall view

KH Vatec's revenue rose for two straight quarters through 3Q 2026 on hinge recovery, and 3Q operating profit of KRW 7.18 billion was up 44.3% from the prior quarter.

However, the same quarter brought a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 6.15 billion, and the cumulative figure over the last four quarters is KRW 994 million in losses.

In the 2022 to 2025 annual record, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked in 2023 and declined for two straight years in 2024 and 2025, although 2025 saw both revenue and operating profit rise.

Customer concentration remains high, so production and sales of Samsung Electronics' foldable phones are the largest variable for results. Automotive, AI data center cooling and robotics parts are mostly at the registration or negotiation stage, so their revenue contribution is not yet settled.

Daishin Securities said in a 17 November 2026 report that it lowered its target price to KRW 14,000 and maintained a Buy rating. The debt-to-equity ratio of 46.6% at end-2025 is below 2022 levels, but operating cash flow has stayed in the KRW 15 billion range since 2024.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary 4Q 2026 results and the 2026 annual report, where the share of new-business revenue and the pace of operating margin recovery will be central.
