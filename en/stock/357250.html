02

Business structure

Miraeasset Maps REIT 1 is an externally managed REIT listed on the KOSPI in August 2020, with its core asset being the commercial facilities at Gwanggyo Central Prugio City in Gwanggyo New City.

The lease structure runs through the company as lessor, GS Retail as the primary lessee and sub-lessor, and Lotte Shopping as the actual store operator and sub-lessee. The lease contract is not sales-linked but features a fixed structure with annual rent escalation, running through 2035.

In August 2023, the company made its first additional investment since listing, acquiring equity securities in KG Tower, an office building near Gangnam Station, issued by Kocref No.47 REIT, funded through a rights offering.

It later added preferred units in Majesta City Tower 1 issued by Kocref No.66 Fund, bringing the portfolio to three assets. According to asset management reports, all three assets reported a 0% vacancy rate.

Total assets under management stood at roughly KRW 304.2 billion, placing the company among the smaller listed REITs. Most revenue derives from rental income at the Gwanggyo commercial asset, leaving the company more concentrated in a single property than many of its KOSPI-listed REIT peers.

Earnings analysis

Revenue rose from KRW 5.95 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.9 billion in 2023, up 83.3%, before falling 36.9% to KRW 6.88 billion in 2024 and edging up 0.5% to KRW 6.92 billion in 2025.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 3.15 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.1 billion in 2023, a 157.3% increase, then dropped 50.4% to KRW 4.01 billion in 2024 and rose only 1.9% to KRW 4.09 billion in 2025.

The operating margin moved from 52.9% in 2022 to 74.2% in 2023, before easing to 58.3% in 2024 and recovering slightly to 59.1% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders surged 781.3% from KRW 633 million in 2022 to KRW 5.58 billion in 2023, then declined for two consecutive years following that 2023 peak, falling 72.5% to KRW 1.53 billion in 2024 and 83.0% to KRW 261 million in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests followed the same pattern at KRW 633 million, KRW 5.58 billion, KRW 1.53 billion, and KRW 261 million, respectively.

At end-2025, total equity stood at KRW 96.1 billion, entirely attributable to controlling shareholders, while total liabilities reached KRW 204.9 billion, putting the debt ratio at 213.2%. The debt ratio moved from 223.1% in 2022 to 220.7% in 2023 and 200.0% in 2024, before rising again to 213.2% in 2025.

Operating cash flow came in at KRW 2.56 billion, KRW 2.71 billion, KRW 2.38 billion, and KRW 1.85 billion across 2022-2025, with the most recent two years lower than the prior year.

The combined net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (Q3 2025-Q2 2026) totaled KRW 195 million, indicating that the profit contraction has continued.

Miraeasset Maps REIT 1 holds fewer assets and a smaller asset base than these peers, placing it among the smaller names on the KOSPI REIT roster.

The core Gwanggyo commercial asset operates on a fixed, non-sales-linked rent structure that passes cyclical swings on to lessee GS Retail first, though reports indicate the trade area has seen declining sales amid intensifying competition.

Interest-rate conditions directly affect funding costs across the REIT sector, and reports have noted that the recent rate-cut cycle has eased interest expense pressure for the domestically focused Miraeasset Maps REIT 1.

By contrast, REITs holding overseas assets have faced rising funding rates, leading to diverging dividend capacity across the sector. Smaller REITs tend to have limited trading value and liquidity, making their share prices more sensitive to shifts in institutional flows.

Bull factors

Cash Flow Backed by a Long-Term Lease Commitment

The core Gwanggyo commercial asset has a lease with GS Retail running through 2035, structured as a fixed arrangement rather than one linked to sales performance. According to asset management reports, all three holdings currently report a 0% vacancy rate.

Given the creditworthiness of lessee GS Retail and sub-lessee Lotte Shopping, the near-term risk of unpaid rent is viewed as comparatively low.

Eased Interest Expense in a Rate-Cutting Cycle

As a REIT focused on domestic assets, Miraeasset Maps REIT 1 has reportedly seen reduced interest expense on borrowings during the recent rate-cutting cycle. Unlike REITs holding overseas assets, it is tied only to domestic rates without currency exposure, allowing a more direct benefit from falling rates.

This point, however, is based on market reporting rather than disclosed annual results and warrants further confirmation through regular filings.

Track Record of Portfolio Diversification

Since listing with a single Gwanggyo commercial asset in 2020, the company has added KG Tower equity securities in 2023 and preferred units in Majesta City Tower 1, expanding its asset base over time. Investments in preferred-unit or equity-security form are structured to rank ahead of common equity in loss absorption. Continued additions of this kind could reduce reliance on a single asset going forward.

Liquidity Constraints Typical of a Small-Cap REIT

Risk factors

Tenant Credit and Lease Renewal Risk

Rent on the Gwanggyo commercial asset is paid by GS Retail, which in turn collects sub-lease payments from Lotte Shopping, meaning changes in the credit standing of either company could directly affect the REIT's cash flow.

While the contract runs through 2035, renewal terms or possible mid-contract changes remain a variable that warrants ongoing monitoring.

Interest Rate and Leverage Risk

The debt ratio rose to 213.2% at end-2025 from 200.0% a year earlier. With liabilities more than double total equity, rising rates could erode net income quickly. Given reports that falling rates recently benefited the company, a reversal in rate direction could have a comparably sized negative effect.

Trade-Area Competition and Asset Aging Risk

Reports indicate the trade area surrounding the Gwanggyo commercial asset has experienced declining sales amid intensifying competition. Aging of the property or shifts in the surrounding consumer base could also result in less favorable terms during future rent renegotiations.

Given the heavy reliance on a single asset, such trade-area risk carries a relatively large effect on overall results.

Miraeasset Maps REIT 1 is a small-cap KOSPI-listed REIT built around the stable, long-leased Gwanggyo commercial asset, with its portfolio reinforced by equity securities in KG Tower and Majesta City Tower 1.
