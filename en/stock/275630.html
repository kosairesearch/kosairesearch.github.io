02

Business structure

SSR was founded in 2010 as a specialist in information security consulting and security software and listed on KOSDAQ in 2018. Its flagship product is SolidStep, an automated IT infrastructure vulnerability assessment solution, and MetiEye, which detects web shells and defends web servers, is listed as a key product.

A securities data site's breakdown puts the solutions segment at 66.27% and the services segment at 33.21%, but does not show the base year. Services include technology consulting and management consulting, as well as security inspection work such as penetration testing.

Export shares in 2025 were 10% for the web hacking prevention solution and 3.29% for the vulnerability assessment solution, so revenue remains centered on the domestic market.

Key customers are financial institutions, government agencies and large IT companies, and SolidStep is described as deployed at more than 500 client companies. MetiEye has also entered the Japanese market. At the end of 2025 the company had 92 employees, and consolidated R&D spending was 13.21% of revenue in 2025.

The company completed an absorption merger with its subsidiary Trinity Soft on April 28, 2026, and closed a business transfer procedure with an effective date of October 1, 2026.

After the business transfer, total assets rose by KRW 579.04 million, and the share purchase claims will be confirmed through a corrective disclosure.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 12.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.2 billion in 2023 (down 2.8% year on year) and to KRW 11.6 billion in 2024 (down 4.5%), before rebounding to KRW 13 billion in 2025 (up 12.4%). 2025 was the largest year in 2022–2025 and 2024 the smallest.

The operating margin declined from 19.1% in 2022 to 13.2% in 2023, recorded -2.7% in 2024, and recovered to 2.5% in 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 2.39 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.6 billion in 2023 (down 32.8%), turned to an operating loss of KRW 312 million in 2024, and turned back to an operating profit of KRW 321 million in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined from KRW 1.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.72 billion in 2023 (down 9.5%), then became a loss of KRW 252 million in 2024 and a loss of KRW 1.66 billion in 2025, meaning two consecutive years of losses.

Total net income, including non-controlling interests, showed a net loss of KRW 254 million in 2024 and KRW 1.66 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 3.96 billion in 2022, KRW 829 million in 2023, KRW 1.61 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.99 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, an operating loss of KRW 417 million in the third quarter of 2025 was followed by fourth-quarter revenue of KRW 5.13 billion (up 110.9% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 1.46 billion, while the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in that quarter was KRW 1.12 billion.

First-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 1.75 billion (down 65.8% from the prior quarter) with an operating loss of KRW 1.27 billion.

Revenue then rose to KRW 2.31 billion in the second quarter and KRW 2.65 billion in the third quarter, two consecutive quarterly gains, while operating losses narrowed to KRW 606 million and KRW 386 million.

An information site's commentary attributed the wider first-half 2026 operating loss to limited first-half revenue from orders concentrated in the second half and a one-off merger cost. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 2.86 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for the company moves with regulation and the timing of procurement. Valueline describes tightening rules, including the Personal Information Protection Act and the Credit Information Use and Protection Act, as widening the scope of mandatory vulnerability inspections and expanding demand.

Financial institutions, government agencies and large IT companies, the main customers, are the principal buyers of such inspections. The 55% market share for the vulnerability assessment automation solution is calculated on a procurement-results basis and should be read with that in mind.

The company registered its products on the Public Procurement Service's digital service mall in December 2024, giving it a public-sector procurement channel.

The security market includes large listed security companies such as Ahnlab, and SSR's product scope, centered on vulnerability assessment and web defense, is narrow by comparison.

Seasonality is also pronounced: fourth-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 5.13 billion, up 110.9% from the prior quarter, and first-quarter 2026 revenue fell to KRW 1.75 billion. A securities information site's commentary cited orders concentrated in the second half as a reason for weak first-half 2026 results. Overseas exposure is limited, with the export share of the web hacking prevention solution at 10% in 2025.

06

Outlook

Three confirmed corporate events frame the outlook. First, the absorption merger with the subsidiary Trinity Soft was completed on its merger date of April 28, 2026, a no-new-shares merger at a 1-to-0 exchange ratio.

Second, the business transfer closed with an effective date of October 1, 2026, and total assets rose by KRW 579.04 million after the transfer. Third, the share purchase claims tied to the business transfer will be confirmed through a corrective disclosure after the claim period ends.

Because these events change assets and business scope, how they are reflected in future periodic reports on operating results and asset composition is the item to watch.

For the fourth quarter of 2026, whether orders concentrate at the end of the second half as they did in the fourth quarter of 2025 will be a key variable.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 9.0% year on year and the operating loss narrowed, so whether fourth-quarter revenue sustains an annual operating profit is the next point to watch.

The 2025 operating margin of 2.5% remains well below the 19.1% of 2022, and the margin trajectory in the next annual report is the profitability checkpoint. Demand expansion from stricter regulation is cited as a medium-term driver. Overseas expansion is limited to MetiEye's presence in Japan. The 2026 annual report is due by March 31, 2027.

Risk factors

Quarterly swings from order timing

Orders concentrated in the second half limit first-half revenue and make quarterly results swing sharply. The gap between KRW 5.13 billion of revenue in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 1.75 billion in the first quarter of 2026 illustrates this. If orders do not concentrate in the fourth quarter, the annual profit trend could weaken.

Integration burden after merger and business transfer

The April 2026 absorption merger and the October 2026 business transfer changed the company's business and asset composition. A one-off merger cost has been cited as widening the first-half 2026 operating loss.

The amount of share purchase claims tied to the business transfer will not be fixed until the corrective disclosure, so the size of the cash burden remains undetermined.

Intra-group dealings and listing-status questions

A press report described a plan to raise corporate value through in-kind contributions from affiliated companies. The article's headline framed the question of whether the company can avoid the risk of delisting. The terms of intra-group transactions and related disclosures are the next items to review.

Overall view

SSR is a KOSDAQ company centered on security consulting and vulnerability assessment solutions, and its 2025 annual revenue of KRW 13 billion was the largest in 2022–2025.

The company turned to operating profit of KRW 321 million that year, but the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.66 billion marked a second consecutive year of losses.

The 2026 quarterly trend shows recovery signs, with revenue rising for two consecutive quarters in the second and third quarters and operating losses narrowing from KRW 606 million to KRW 386 million.

On the other side, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders has continued for five consecutive quarters, and the four-quarter total is KRW 2.86 billion. The 55% share of the flagship product on a procurement basis and expanding inspection demand from tighter regulation are cited as strengths.

Quarterly revenue swings sharply with order timing, and the operating loss widened to KRW 1.27 billion in the first quarter of 2026. On valuation, the price-to-book ratio is below 1x, while an earnings-based multiple cannot be calculated because of recent losses.

The points to confirm are whether orders concentrate in the fourth quarter of 2026, the corrective disclosure on share purchase claims, and full-year results in the 2026 annual report.
