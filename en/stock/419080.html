Enjet: Sales Off Their 2026 Peak as Losses Persist

02

Business structure

Enjet is an EHD (electrohydrodynamic) inkjet printing and coating company founded in 2009 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2022 under the technology growth company special listing route.

In 2021 on a consolidated basis, EHD inkjet printing solutions accounted for 55.5% of sales, EHD coating solutions for 29.9%, and other sales for 14.6%. In Q2 2026, other sales made up 68% of revenue and EHD inkjet printing solutions 32%.

Its core products are equipment for display and semiconductor production processes, and in 2024 one customer labeled D accounted for 68.10% of sales. A June 2023 research report explained that most first-quarter 2023 sales were tied to Apple.

Technically, the company is pursuing a multi-nozzle printhead based on its proprietary iEHD technology to expand market share. In 2025 it established a Vietnam subsidiary to widen applications into film replacement and thin-film coating.

It also cited mass-production entry for biosensor diagnostic kits as a growth area. In March 2026 it signed a display repair equipment supply contract with AUO Corporation's Taoyuan branch, running from February 25, 2026 to December 31, 2026, with supply to Taiwan. It entered the glass-substrate market through an MOU with glass-substrate producer JWMT.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 21.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.1 billion in 2023 (down 44.3% year on year), KRW 6.26 billion in 2024 (down 48.1%), and KRW 5.41 billion in 2025 (down 13.5%).

Operating profit of KRW 5.3 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 2.12 billion in 2023, widened to KRW 6.53 billion in 2024, and narrowed to KRW 6.28 billion in 2025.

The operating margin moved from 24.4% in 2022 to -17.5%, -104.4% and -116.0% in 2023, 2024 and 2025, showing costs failing to keep pace with the sales decline.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 4.11 billion in 2022 to KRW 314 million in 2023 (down 92.4%), turned into a net loss of KRW 3.48 billion in 2024, and widened to a net loss of KRW 7.22 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the wider 2025 net loss to revenue carried over because of customer inspection delays and to the accounting treatment of corporate taxes. Operating cash flow went from a KRW 3.5 billion inflow in 2022 to outflows of KRW 1.41 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.87 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales fell from KRW 882 million in Q3 2025 to KRW 531 million in Q4 2025, then rose to KRW 4.37 billion in Q1 2026, the peak, before declining to KRW 4.07 billion in Q2 and KRW 3.01 billion in Q3 2026.

Operating profit of KRW 107 million in Q2 2026 turned positive from the prior quarter, but Q3 returned to an operating loss of KRW 1.46 billion. Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued for five consecutive quarters since Q3 2025, totaling KRW 6.44 billion over the last four quarters.

Sales have fallen since the Q1 2026 peak while losses have not narrowed meaningfully, so the annual operating margin in the next annual report is the key point to watch.

05

Industry analysis

Enjet's end markets are display and semiconductor production equipment, where quarterly results can swing sharply with customer production schedules. A June 2023 research report tied its quarterly improvement outlook to the iPhone production schedule.

The same report projected 2023 sales of KRW 44.5 billion, but actual 2023 sales were KRW 12.1 billion, so the gap between that outlook and reported results was wide.

The report also expected supply visibility to rise in the second half for micro LED TV repair equipment, UTG coaters for smartphones, and coaters for secondary batteries.

At its 2022 listing, the offering materials described Enjet as the only mass-production company for EHD technology worldwide, and positioned its inkjet resolution as beyond the limits of conventional inkjet.

In glass substrates, the company has said it developed AI software able to detect TGV micro-defects down to 3 micrometers. In display repair equipment, the AUO supply contract keeps it connected to panel makers.

Several listed companies share the KOSDAQ machinery and equipment classification, but their product areas differ, so direct comparison is limited. The industry comparison table shows the sector's operating profit as positive while Enjet is loss-making.

Its competitive position depends on whether EHD mass-production know-how can be combined with glass-substrate inspection capability.

06

Outlook

The nearest confirmed business milestone is the AUO display repair equipment supply contract, whose term ends on December 31, 2026. Because sales have fallen for two consecutive quarters since the Q1 2026 peak, the first checkpoint is the Q4 2026 sales level during the contract period.

The glass-substrate push is planned to be funded by a KRW 6 billion first-round private convertible bond, with all proceeds earmarked for upgrading the AI software and developing glass-substrate post-process equipment.

The AI software is reported to detect TGV defects down to 3 micrometers, but whether it translates into volume sales has not yet appeared in reported results.

Whether the Vietnam subsidiary's film-replacement and thin-film coating business contributes to sales can be checked in the business description of the next annual report.

For biosensor diagnostic kits, mass-production entry and additional orders are cited as growth grounds, but the revenue scale is not shown in the disclosures. The key earnings checkpoint is how far the annual operating margin moves from -116.0% in 2025 in the 2026 annual report.

Given the wide gap between the 2023 outlook of KRW 44.5 billion and actual sales of KRW 12.1 billion, the new-business outlook should be read alongside reported results.

08

Bull factors

Broader application base

The 2025 Vietnam subsidiary widens the company's film-replacement and thin-film coating applications. Development of a multi-nozzle printhead is a technology investment aimed at higher market share. Biosensor diagnostic kits are also cited as a new demand source. The glass-substrate inspection software is presented as the technical starting point of the new business.

Display repair contract base

A display repair equipment supply contract with AUO's Taiwan operations runs from February 25, 2026 to December 31, 2026. If carried-over revenue is recognized once customer inspections are completed, quarterly sales volatility could ease. The recovery from KRW 531 million in Q4 2025 to KRW 4.37 billion in Q1 2026 can be read in this context.

Equity base and low leverage

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 34.4 billion, against total liabilities of KRW 9.03 billion. The debt ratio was 13.5% in 2022, 9.6% in 2023, 13.1% in 2024 and 26.2% in 2025, a modest level relative to equity. The equity base maintained since the company's listing supports its ability to fund ongoing development.

09

Bear factors

Three years of operating losses and margin erosion

Operating losses have continued for three years from 2023 through 2025. The operating margin fell from 24.4% in 2022 to -116.0% in 2025. While revenue shrank to KRW 5.41 billion, the cost base has not been absorbed, which is the core weakness.

Customer concentration and quarterly volatility

In 2024 one customer labeled D accounted for 68.10% of sales, a high level of customer concentration. Quarterly sales swung from KRW 531 million in Q4 2025 to KRW 4.37 billion in Q1 2026 and then down to KRW 3.01 billion in Q3 2026. Results are therefore highly sensitive to the timing of orders and acceptance from specific customers.

Wider cash outflows and a rising debt ratio

The debt ratio rose from 13.1% in 2024 to 26.2% in 2025, nearly doubling within a year. Cash is continuing to drain while the company remains loss-making.

10

Risk factors

Financing and dilution

Enjet has decided on a KRW 6 billion first-round private convertible bond. If it converts, the number of shares would rise and dilute per-share value.

The proceeds are planned to fund AI software upgrades and glass-substrate post-process equipment, and a delay in that business could extend the need for further financing.

New business execution

The glass-substrate defect-inspection software and post-process equipment are at the MOU stage with JWMT, and the timing of mass-production customers and revenue recognition is not set. An MOU differs from a binding supply contract, so whether development results will translate into earnings is uncertain. Equipment development costs come first, so losses could persist for some time.

Customer contract renewal and acceptance timing

The AUO display repair equipment supply contract ends on December 31, 2026. Without an extension or follow-on orders, revenue from this Taiwan display customer base could decline.

The company cited customer acceptance delays and carried-over revenue as factors in the wider 2025 net loss, and when acceptance slips, revenue recognition is also delayed.

12

Overall view

Enjet's sales fell from KRW 21.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.41 billion in 2025, and operating losses have continued since 2023. Sales peaked at KRW 4.37 billion in Q1 2026, then declined in Q2 and Q3, with Q3 returning to a KRW 1.46 billion operating loss.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 6.44 billion over the last four quarters.

On the positive side, the company is widening its application base through the Vietnam subsidiary and printhead development, holds a display repair equipment supply contract, and has an equity base with low leverage.

On the negative side, the operating margin of -116.0% in 2025, a sales base concentrated on one customer, and widening cash outflows weigh on results.

The new business centers on glass-substrate AI software and post-process equipment, funded in part by a convertible bond, but the timing of volume revenue has not been confirmed.

The next checkpoints are the end of the AUO contract term on December 31, 2026, the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027, and the Q1 2027 report due by May 15, 2027.
