02

Business structure

ilShinbiobase is a bioequipment specialist founded in 1988 that mainly makes freeze dryers and ultra-low temperature freezers. Its line runs from small laboratory models to large plant-scale freeze dryers for pharmaceutical and food industry use, and it also produces blood bank refrigerators, ice makers and shakers.

A business-segment tally on a Korean recruitment site shows Lyoph-Pride series freeze dryers making up a majority of the mix. Its customers include pharmaceutical companies, vaccine and biologics makers, and producers of probiotics and health foods.

In January 2026, a supply contract for an aseptic-formulation automated freeze dryer worth KRW 4.29 billion and a separate freeze-dryer contract worth KRW 2.25 billion were disclosed.

Both amounts equal 39.7% and 20.8% of 2024 revenue of KRW 10.8 billion, which shows that single orders carry a large weight in this business. According to company profile material, the Dongducheon plant has capacity for more than 200 freeze dryers a year.

Exports are expanding to markets including Austria, Chile and Malaysia, and the company has shifted to direct sales by country to strengthen local sales and technical support.

Competition comes from overseas freeze-dryer makers as well as domestic import-substitution demand, and precise control, energy efficiency and temperature stability are cited as differentiators.

A patented shelf structure that keeps temperature variation within plus or minus 1 degree Celsius is also presented as a technical basis.

04

Earnings analysis

On DART periodic-report figures, annual revenue went from KRW 20.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.4 billion in 2023 (down 19.6% year on year) and KRW 10.8 billion in 2024 (down 34.2%), before rising to KRW 14.5 billion in 2025 (up 34.3%). Across 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit moved from KRW 5.14 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.21 billion in 2023 (up 20.9%), fell to KRW 2.26 billion in 2024 (down 63.6%), and recovered to KRW 4.55 billion in 2025 (up 101.0%).

Operating margins were 25.2%, 37.8%, 20.9% and 31.3% for 2022 through 2025, so the weakest margin came in the lowest-revenue year. This pattern suggests that margins swing widely with order volume, since fixed costs are spread over a smaller or larger revenue base.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.82 billion in 2022, KRW 6.23 billion in 2023 (up 63.4%), KRW 3.81 billion in 2024 (down 38.8%) and KRW 4.79 billion in 2025 (up 25.8%). Operating cash flow of KRW 5.27 billion in 2025 exceeded that attributable profit.

Quarterly, third-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 4.87 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.91 billion; fourth-quarter revenue slipped 13.0% to KRW 4.23 billion and operating profit to KRW 1.08 billion. The first quarter of 2026 was the low point, with revenue of KRW 2.52 billion and operating profit of KRW 454 million.

The second quarter rebounded to revenue of KRW 4.17 billion (up 65.7% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 1.53 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 3.66 billion (down 24.7% year on year), operating profit of KRW 781 million (down 59.0%) and attributable profit of KRW 499 million (down 74.1%) reversed that recovery, and attributable profit over the last four quarters totaled KRW 4.59 billion.

The disclosed figures alone do not pin down the direct cause of the quarterly swings, though the timing of revenue recognition on large orders may have played a role.

05

Industry analysis

The global freeze-dryer market is growing on long-term preservation demand for high-value medicines such as biopharmaceuticals, vaccines and cell therapies. Valueline projects that the market will grow from about USD 2 billion in 2024 to USD 4.5 billion in 2033.

The same profile describes ilShinbiobase as leading import substitution in Korea. A summary site on disclosures attributed the 2025 revenue increase partly to a partial recovery in biopharma industry conditions.

The company's revenue bottomed in 2024 and rebounded in 2025, which fits the view that customers' equipment investment timing drives order timing. Where the company sits in the cycle is hard to call, as the 2025 recovery and the slowdown in the third quarter of 2026 appeared together after the 2024 low.

Competition comes from overseas freeze-dryer makers as well as domestic players. ilShinbiobase has widened its lineup from laboratory units to plant-scale equipment, which spreads its customer base.

In June 2026, the company was reported to be taking on the development of intelligent sealed freeze-drying equipment within a KRW 26.3 billion national research project led by a Chonbuk National University team on a DCS biopharma platform, which broadens its demand base toward research institutions and government projects.

Risk factors

Order volatility

Because single contract sizes drive quarterly revenue, a mismatch in the timing of large orders can cause sharp swings in quarterly results. Revenue of KRW 2.52 billion in the first quarter of 2026 rose 65.7% quarter on quarter to KRW 4.17 billion in the second quarter, then fell to KRW 3.66 billion in the third. Order flow also depends on when customers invest in equipment.

Listing-maintenance requirements

If the share price stays below 1,000 won, further exchange warnings may follow. Reports describe the tightened requirements as targeting stocks under 1,000 won and companies that fail to maintain a certain level of market capitalization. A larger listing-maintenance problem could weigh directly on the share price and trading conditions.

Ownership concentration and free float

The largest shareholder group's stake rose to 32.15%, and CEO Hong Sung-dae held 29.63% as of May 18, 2026. Concentrated holdings leave a limited free float, which can increase trading volatility. Director share purchases show a change in holdings but do not by themselves indicate the direction of the share price.
