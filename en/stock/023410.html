Quarterly Profit Returns Amid Construction Slump, Annual Earnings Shrink

02

Business structure

Eugene is a construction materials company centered on ready-mixed concrete, which also runs building-materials distribution, aggregates and logistics operations.

According to a 2025 Newswave report citing company disclosures, ready-mixed concrete accounted for 66% of standalone revenue in January to September 2025, and it exceeded 50% of consolidated revenue including subsidiaries.

Because the product is used directly at construction sites, sales track construction activity closely. Customers are mainly construction companies, which have pressed for price cuts, and a April 2026 report said the agreed industry unit prices have also fallen.

In a sector with many small producers, the ability to raise prices is limited. The company has pushed digital operations, joining an LH smart ready-mix management MOU and obtaining a patent for a ready-mixed concrete information-sharing system on July 4, 2025.

A 2026 report, with reporting done in April, put average plant utilization at 33%. At the Eugene Group level, building materials and distribution account for about 47% of group revenue, a group-wide figure rather than Eugene alone.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue on a disclosed annual basis rose from KRW 1.41 trillion in 2022 to a peak of KRW 1.47 trillion in 2023, up 4.7%, then fell 5.4% to KRW 1.39 trillion in 2024 and 4.4% to KRW 1.33 trillion in 2025, a second straight annual decline. 2023 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit rose from KRW 54.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 84.4 billion in 2023, up 54.3%, then fell 34.9% to KRW 55 billion in 2024 and 41.1% to KRW 32.4 billion in 2025, a second straight decline. Operating margin moved from 3.9% in 2022 to 5.7% in 2023, 3.9% in 2024 and 2.4% in 2025.

Press coverage of the 2025 filing attributed the decline to lower ready-mixed concrete shipments, higher raw material costs and higher selling and administrative expenses.

Attributable net income peaked at KRW 65.7 billion in 2023, then turned to a KRW 57.7 billion net loss in 2024 and narrowed to a KRW 10.2 billion net loss in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, a different measure, was a KRW 100.7 billion net loss in 2024 and a KRW 24.5 billion net loss in 2025, and the 2025 improvement was linked to higher equity-method income. Quarterly results were volatile.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, operating profit of KRW 22.9 billion rose 208.1% from the prior quarter, yet attributable net income swung to a KRW 141 billion loss.

In the first and third quarters of 2026, operating profit was KRW 11.8 billion and KRW 6.39 billion, while attributable net income was positive at KRW 65.5 billion and KRW 77.3 billion, which points to a large non-operating contribution whose breakdown should be checked in the periodic report notes.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 368.3 billion rose 9.8% from the prior quarter. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 76.8 billion in 2024 to KRW 31.4 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The ready-mixed concrete industry moves directly with construction activity. An April 2026 industry report said national shipments in 2025 fell to roughly 93 million cubic meters, below the level seen during the IMF crisis, and plant utilization dropped to the low teens.

Prices and costs are moving in opposite directions. Agreed unit prices have fallen under pressure from construction companies, while transport and fuel costs have risen with truck mixer operations. Admixtures depend heavily on Middle East supply, and a weaker won adds to raw material costs.

Industry participants describe the situation as a survival question rather than an ordinary cycle, and warn that some producers could exit if prices do not recover.

Within this cycle, a 2025 report said Eugene's average ready-mixed plant utilization fell below 30%, while aggregate plant utilization was in the 50% range. A 2026 report put the overall average at 33%.

Production capacity has been roughly flat for more than a decade, and aggregate capacity was cut from 6 million tons to 3.74 million tons in 2022. Diversifying into building-materials distribution, aggregates and logistics is the company's main response to this environment.

06

Outlook

In a 2025 report, the company said it would pursue cost reduction and a focus on core operating activities on the assumption of weaker construction demand. Quarterly operating profit in 2026 was KRW 11.8 billion in the first quarter, KRW 14.2 billion in the second and KRW 6.39 billion in the third, a wide spread.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 336.5 billion fell 8.6% from the prior quarter but rose 5.1% year on year. The key to demand recovery is construction activity.

In a July 2026 report, a business community official said reconstruction work could begin in earnest once the Russia-Ukraine and US-Iran conflicts end, and that the government may expand infrastructure investment to revive the property market. This is a forecast, not a confirmed order or contract.

On the growth side, the company is building a digital ready-mixed concrete management platform based on its information-sharing patent. At the group level, an affiliate has added real estate development to its business purpose and is pursuing an AI data center on idle group land, according to a May 2026 report.

Its Bucheon site was bought for KRW 32 billion in January 2026. No disclosed figures yet show how that project will affect Eugene's results. In the near term, seasonal weakness and cost pressure are likely to persist.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly Profit Recovery and Steady Revenue

Attributable net income turned positive in the first and third quarters of 2026, at KRW 65.5 billion and KRW 77.3 billion. Third-quarter attributable net income rose 105.3% year on year. Quarterly revenue stayed above KRW 330 billion across the three quarters, and third-quarter revenue rose 5.1% from a year earlier.

Diversification Beyond Ready-Mixed Concrete

Although ready-mixed concrete is the largest business, the company has expanded into building-materials distribution, aggregates and logistics. Ready-mixed concrete was 66% of standalone revenue in January to September 2025 and more than half of consolidated revenue.

The company is pursuing operating efficiency through a digital ready-mixed management platform and participation in an LH smart management MOU.

Discount to Net Assets and Group Assets

Attributable equity was KRW 882.1 billion at the end of 2025, and the share price sits well below that net asset value. The Eugene Group is directing large land holdings and logistics infrastructure built during the construction boom toward AI and robotics.

An affiliate is pursuing data center development on idle sites in Bucheon and Incheon, according to a May 2026 report.

09

Bear factors

Two Straight Years of Net Losses and Shrinking Operating Profit

Attributable net income turned to a KRW 57.7 billion loss in 2024 and remained negative in 2025 at a KRW 10.2 billion loss. Operating profit declined from KRW 84.4 billion in 2023 to KRW 32.4 billion in 2025, a 2.4% margin. Operating cash flow also fell from KRW 76.8 billion in 2024 to KRW 31.4 billion in 2025.

Ready-Mixed Downturn and Cost Pressure

An April 2026 industry report said shipments fell below IMF-crisis levels and plant utilization dropped to the low teens. Unit prices are falling while transport, fuel and currency costs rise, which the report said is eroding profit quickly. Eugene's own average utilization was reported at 33%.

Large Swings in Quarterly Results

The fourth quarter of 2025 showed a KRW 141 billion attributable net loss despite KRW 22.9 billion of operating profit. The second quarter of 2026 also swung to a KRW 10.6 billion attributable net loss.

Over the last four quarters, attributable net income combined to a KRW 8.87 billion loss, so the direction of quarterly results has been inconsistent.

10

Risk factors

Construction Demand

Ready-mixed demand follows construction starts and site progress. A 2026 report pointed to rising unsold housing and weak apartment demand hitting both builders and materials suppliers. A prolonged slump would delay any recovery in shipments.

Input Costs and External Factors

Transport accounts for a large share of ready-mixed costs, so higher fuel prices flow directly into costs. Admixtures depend heavily on Middle East supply, and currency swings lift raw material prices. With agreed unit prices lower, passing cost increases on to customers is difficult.

Balance Sheet and Group Exposure

Total liabilities stood at KRW 1.32 trillion at the end of 2025, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 125.6%. Operating cash flow has also declined.

In 2024, a group broadcasting affiliate's losses were reported to have flowed into Eugene's results through equity-method losses, and expanding group investments could add pressure to consolidated results.

Overall view

Eugene is a ready-mixed concrete-centered company that also runs building-materials distribution, aggregates and logistics, and construction-related shipment declines and cost pressure drove the weaker results in 2024 and 2025.

On a disclosed basis, revenue fell for a second straight year to KRW 1.33 trillion in 2025, and attributable net income has been negative since 2024.

In 2026, attributable net income turned positive in the first and third quarters but was negative in the second, while operating profit ranged from KRW 11.8 billion to KRW 14.2 billion and KRW 6.39 billion across the first three quarters.

Strengths include a large discount to net asset value, business diversification beyond ready-mixed concrete and third-quarter revenue growth year on year.

Weaknesses include the industry-wide slump in shipments and utilization, unit prices moving against rising costs, and a debt-to-equity ratio of 125.6% alongside lower operating cash flow. The next checkpoints are the operating margin and non-operating items in the 2026 annual report and first-quarter 2027 results.
