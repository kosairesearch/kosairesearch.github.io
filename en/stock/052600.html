Hannet: Operating margin rises to 16% on CD-VAN and kiosk business

Hannet's operating margin rose from 11.3% in 2022 to 16.3% in 2025, yet net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 19.6% year on year in Q3 2026.

02

Business structure

Hannet became independent in 1997 from the VAN division of Hankook Computer and listed on KOSDAQ in 2001. Its core business is CD-VAN, which installs cash dispensers outside financial institutions and earns fees from them. The company is described as the first private operator to install off-branch cash dispensers.

As of March 2026, the revenue mix was about 51% CD-VAN, 46% unmanned devices, product and service sales including settlement, and 3% rental income. The unmanned segment covers kiosk sales and cash settlement, and combines equipment sales with settlement services.

Installation sites are centered on public places such as subway stations, large retail stores and convenience stores where financial-sector operators have not entered. Retail operators outsourcing their settlement work has been cited as a driver of profitability.

The competitive landscape is led by financial-sector operators, and Hannet has expanded installations in locations those operators have not covered. Earnings of a CD installation business depend on cash withdrawal demand and on securing sites. The largest shareholder group holds 45.52% and the free float is 54.48%. The company had 87 employees as of March 31, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue and profitability rising together. Revenue went from KRW 22 billion in 2022 to KRW 24.9 billion in 2023 (up 13.3% year on year), KRW 25.9 billion in 2024 (up 4.0%) and KRW 26.8 billion in 2025 (up 3.6%).

Operating profit rose from KRW 2.49 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.33 billion in 2023 (up 33.8%), KRW 4.2 billion in 2024 (up 26.2%) and KRW 4.37 billion in 2025 (up 4.0%), and it increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

The operating margin moved from 11.3% to 13.4%, then 16.2% and 16.3%, with the big step-up in 2024 and a flat year in 2025. In 2024 operating profit grew 26.2% against revenue growth of 4.0%, which is the main source of the margin expansion.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 1.96 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.46 billion in 2023 (up 25.6%), KRW 3.43 billion in 2024 (up 39.1%) and KRW 3.65 billion in 2025 (up 6.5%). Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed with the same yearly values.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 4.56 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.52 billion in 2023, KRW 6.44 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.09 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue rose from KRW 6.35 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 7.04 billion in Q4 2025 (up 10.9% quarter on quarter), then fell to KRW 6.59 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 6.46 billion in Q2 2026 before recovering to KRW 7.05 billion in Q3 2026 (up 11.1% year on year).

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.51 billion rose 39.9% quarter on quarter and 43.3% year on year, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 762 million fell 47.4% quarter on quarter and 19.6% year on year.

The reasons for the divergence between operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income are items to check through non-operating income and tax line items. Over the last four quarters, controlling-shareholder net income totaled KRW 4.17 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The market Hannet operates in is cash dispenser installation, led by financial-sector operators. Hannet has expanded installations in public places such as subway stations, large retail stores and convenience stores where financial-sector operators have not entered. Installations are described as growing steadily.

In the VAN segment, rising credit card usage has been cited as a factor behind improving results. In the unmanned segment, unmanned automation is accelerating in service businesses, which is increasing use of unmanned devices. Retail operators outsourcing settlement work is also cited as a demand factor.

However, the long-term effect of declining cash withdrawal demand on CD installation earnings has no quantified data, so the direction is the point to watch. A shift toward card and simple-payment mix could change the VAN fee structure as well.

Compared with large platform or system integration companies in the same IT and software sector, Hannet's business structure differs, so its position is better read within payment infrastructure and unmanned equipment markets than by direct revenue comparison. Against financial-sector operators, Hannet's position is distinguished by the nature of its installation sites.

08

Bull factors

Structural margin improvement

The operating margin rose from 11.3% in 2022 to 16.3% in 2025. In 2024 operating profit grew 26.2%, well above 4.0% revenue growth. Operating profit reached KRW 4.37 billion in 2025, close to double the KRW 2.49 billion of 2022, and the margin gain came alongside a larger revenue base.

Demand from unmanned adoption and outsourced settlement

A corporate monitoring summary for Q1 2026 pointed to wider adoption of unmanned payment systems and outsourced settlement by retail operators as contributors to results. Unmanned automation in service businesses is described as accelerating. The unmanned segment accounts for about 46% of revenue, the second pillar after CD-VAN.

Improved financial stability

The debt ratio fell from 43.8% in 2024 to 29.8% in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 11.8 billion against total equity of KRW 39.7 billion. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 4.56 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.09 billion in 2025, showing profits converting into cash.

Bear factors

Divergence in Q3 earnings direction

Q3 2026 operating profit rose 43.3% year on year to KRW 1.51 billion. In the same quarter, controlling-shareholder net income fell 19.6% year on year to KRW 762 million and 47.4% quarter on quarter. The failure of the operating gain to carry through to controlling-shareholder earnings is the weakness.

Slowing growth

Revenue growth slowed from 13.3% in 2023 to 4.0% in 2024 and 3.6% in 2025. Revenue of KRW 26.8 billion in 2025 reflects modest expansion. Revenue declined in both Q1 and Q2 2026, so whether top-line growth picks up again is an open question.

Scale and market structure constraints

Revenue is around KRW 26.8 billion and total equity is KRW 39.7 billion. In a market led by financial-sector operators, installation sites tend to be limited to public places outside their coverage. With a 45.52% largest-shareholder stake and a 54.48% free float, the trading base is not broad.

Risk factors

Business concentration

CD-VAN and unmanned devices and settlement together account for about 97% of revenue. A change in installation conditions or fee structure in either segment would weigh heavily on total results. Rental and other income is only about 3%, so diversification is limited.

Shift in payment environment

Changes in cash withdrawal demand and in the share of card payments directly affect CD installation income and VAN fees. Rising credit card use has been cited as a factor in VAN results, but a shift in the payment mix could change that trend. Regulatory changes in payment infrastructure are also a variable for the business environment.

Earnings volatility and liquidity

Controlling-shareholder net income over the last five quarters was KRW 948 million, KRW 947 million, KRW 1.01 billion, KRW 1.45 billion and KRW 762 million, with large swings between quarters. In Q3 2026, operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income moved in opposite directions.

With a high largest-shareholder stake and a 54.48% free float, earnings changes may pass through to trading and share price sharply.

Overall view

From 2022 to 2025, Hannet's revenue, operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income all rose, and the operating margin climbed from 11.3% to 16.3%. The debt ratio fell from 43.8% in 2024 to 29.8% in 2025, and operating cash flow rose from KRW 4.56 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.09 billion in 2025.

Revenue growth, however, slowed to 4.0% in 2024 and 3.6% in 2025. In Q3 2026, revenue of KRW 7.05 billion rose 11.1% year on year and operating profit of KRW 1.51 billion rose 43.3%, but controlling-shareholder net income fell 19.6% to KRW 762 million.

Over the last four quarters, controlling-shareholder net income totaled KRW 4.17 billion, and quarterly earnings vary widely. The business is concentrated in CD-VAN and unmanned segments, and with a 45.52% largest-shareholder stake, the free float is limited.

The next checkpoint is whether operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income move back in the same direction in the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
