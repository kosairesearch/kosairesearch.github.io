02

Business structure

BG T&A develops, manufactures and sells VHF/UHF receivers and terminal devices that connect to wired and wireless communication networks, and it is an export-oriented company. Its main products are black boxes (including RDVR combo units), radar detectors (RD) and two-way radios (TWR).

A company profile summary puts the sales mix at 46.9% black boxes and RDVR combos, 26.7% radar detectors and 25.8% TWR, making black boxes the largest revenue line. A research report from around 2024 described the company as the largest ODM partner for the US consumer electronics brands Cobra and Escort.

It holds a strategic partnership with Motorola Solutions, which the same report said holds about half of the global TWR market. Production runs at a Philippine base with modern surface-mount technology lines, which the report linked to yield and price competitiveness.

Most RD products are sold in North America, so revenue is exposed to US tariffs and the won-dollar exchange rate. Compared with large electronics component makers, BG T&A works in a narrower niche of wireless and safety devices as an ODM supplier. Reliance on a few customers and markets adds to earnings volatility.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue rose from KRW 147.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 170.7 billion in 2023 (a 15.7% increase), the peak year. It then fell 10.7% to KRW 152.5 billion in 2024 and 2.3% to KRW 148.9 billion in 2025, two straight years of decline.

Operating profit was KRW 15.3 billion in 2022 and KRW 17.1 billion in 2023 (up 11.4%), then dropped 25.5% to KRW 12.7 billion in 2024 and 46.7% to KRW 6.79 billion in 2025. The operating margin slid from 10.4% in 2022 and 10.0% in 2023 to 8.4% in 2024 and 4.6% in 2025.

In its 2025 disclosure, the company attributed the sales decline to lower orders from US customers after US tariffs, and the profit decline to higher selling and administrative costs from pre-emptive investment, including ordinary R&D spending for future growth industries.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent rose from KRW 4.75 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.4 billion in 2023 (up 182.3%), then KRW 13.3 billion in 2024 (down 1.2%) and KRW 4.23 billion in 2025 (down 68.1%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 7.07 billion in 2025, down from KRW 17.7 billion in 2024; it is a different measure from profit attributable to owners.

The balance sheet improved: the debt ratio fell from 47.0% in 2022 to 23.6% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 102.2 billion (KRW 73.1 billion attributable to owners) and total liabilities of KRW 24.1 billion at end-2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 18.2 billion in 2025, against KRW 10.7 billion in 2022.

Quarterly, an operating loss of KRW 1.23 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 326 million in the first quarter of 2026 was followed by operating profit of KRW 407 million in the second quarter, a turn to profit.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.39 billion rose 488.2% from the prior quarter but fell 17.6% from a year earlier. Profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.79 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The market BG T&A serves is wireless communication and safety devices, centered on consumer black boxes, radar detectors and two-way radios (TWR). A research report published around 2024 projected the global TWR market to grow from USD 9.87 billion in 2024 to USD 13.58 billion in 2029.

The same report said Motorola holds about half of global TWR share and that BG T&A's TWR business sales rose 63% to KRW 44.0 billion in 2023.

Most RD products are sold in North America, and the report noted that Chinese-made RD products have been leaving North America since US-China tensions escalated, which supports share gains. On the other side, US tariffs reduced orders from US customers in 2025 and cut sales.

Recent revenue swings therefore appear more sensitive to tariff and trade policy than to demand alone. As an ODM supplier to Cobra and Escort, the company has built a position inside its customers' supply chains. These market figures come from a report of around 2024, so current market size needs separate confirmation.

Compared with large electronics component makers, BG T&A is much smaller and focused on finished-product ODM work.

06

Outlook

In its disclosures, the company has pointed to continued pre-emptive R&D investment tied to future growth industries. That spending was one reason selling and administrative costs rose and operating profit fell in 2025. Quarterly trends have tilted toward recovery.

Sales climbed from KRW 29.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 39.5 billion in the second and KRW 48.9 billion in the third, two consecutive quarterly gains, and operating profit turned positive in the second quarter.

However, third-quarter operating profit of KRW 2.39 billion was 17.6% below a year earlier, so sales recovery has not yet translated into profit recovery. The third-quarter operating margin improved from the prior quarter, but the full-year margin still needs to be confirmed against the 4.6% recorded in 2025.

The next checkpoint is the full-year operating margin and product mix in the 2026 annual report. On the sales side, the effect of US tariff policy on North American orders remains the key variable. Whether R&D spending slows the pace of profit recovery is also worth tracking. Continued on-market purchases by the largest shareholder's related parties will show up in future disclosures.

08

Bull factors

Sales rebound and return to quarterly profit

Third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 48.9 billion, up 31.6% year on year, and sales have risen for two consecutive quarters since the second quarter. A larger revenue base can absorb fixed-type costs such as R&D more easily.

The return to operating profit of KRW 407 million in the second quarter, after a fourth-quarter 2025 loss, marks a change in the direction of quarterly results.

Partnerships and share gains in wireless devices

BG T&A has a strategic partnership with Motorola Solutions, which is reported to hold about half of the global TWR market. A research report said the exit of Chinese-made RD products from North America could support the company's share gains. The same report, published around 2024, projected growth in the global TWR market through 2029.

Improved balance sheet

The debt ratio fell to 23.6% at end-2025 from 47.0% in 2022, with total equity of KRW 102.2 billion and total liabilities of KRW 24.1 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 18.2 billion in 2025, above KRW 10.7 billion in 2022. A lighter debt burden gives the company some cushion during periods of weaker earnings.

Bear factors

Two straight years of operating profit decline

Operating profit fell from KRW 17.1 billion in 2023 to KRW 12.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.79 billion in 2025. The 2025 operating margin of 4.6% is below half the 10.4% recorded in 2022.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.39 billion was still 17.6% lower than a year earlier, so the sales recovery has not lifted profit.

Tariffs and North American dependence

The company said US tariffs reduced orders from US customers in 2025, lowering sales. Most RD products are sold in North America, so the business is directly exposed to changes in tariff policy. Sales in 2025 were KRW 148.9 billion, down 2.3% from the prior year.

Quarterly losses and concentration

The company recorded an operating loss of KRW 1.23 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 326 million in the first quarter of 2026. Black boxes and RDVR combos account for 46.9% of sales, so product concentration is high.

Reliance on ODM orders tied to a few brands such as Cobra and Escort makes results sensitive to customer conditions.

10

Risk factors

Tariffs and trade policy

The company explained that US tariffs reduced orders from North American customers, which lowered 2025 sales. Because most products are sold in North America, a change in tariff policy could quickly alter the sales path. Shifts in competition with Chinese-made products are another variable on the same axis.

Exchange rate

Because most RD products are sold in North America, won-dollar exchange rate moves affect won-denominated sales and profitability. A change in the exchange rate's direction could widen quarterly profit swings. With a high export share, the exchange rate is a core variable in any earnings assumption.

Ownership structure and free float

As of November 12, 2026, the largest shareholder's related parties held 26.14%, and their stake rose through on-market purchases. A high controlling stake can limit the number of shares traded in the market, which may affect share price volatility. Changes in holdings continue to be disclosed.

12

Overall view

BG T&A is in a phase where sales and profit are moving in different directions. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 48.9 billion, up 31.6% year on year, is the largest quarterly figure disclosed since the third quarter of 2025.

However, operating profit of KRW 2.39 billion in the same quarter was 17.6% lower than a year earlier, so the profit recovery is still in progress.

Bullish factors include two consecutive quarters of sales growth, the Motorola partnership and possible share gains as Chinese-made RD products exit, and the return to quarterly operating profit after the second quarter of 2026.

Bearish factors include two straight years of operating profit decline, a 4.6% operating margin in 2025, lower North American orders under US tariffs, and concentration in black boxes and a few customers. On the balance sheet, a 23.6% debt ratio and KRW 18.2 billion of operating cash flow in 2025 provide a cushion.

Given profit attributable to owners of KRW 1.79 billion over the last four quarters, valuation is sensitive to earnings swings. The next checkpoints are fourth-quarter 2026 results, the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and North American order flow in the first quarter of 2027.
