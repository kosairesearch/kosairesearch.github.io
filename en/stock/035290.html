02

Business structure

Gold&S is a KOSDAQ-listed education company that the exchange classifies under media and entertainment, but its business is concentrated in education. Education accounted for 100% of revenue by sector in Q2 2026 according to one data service.

The company runs Talking Club, Siwon School LAB and Clue&Key education services, and it expanded its revenue base through the January 2026 transfer of the education business of Siwon School from SJW. The transfer took effect on January 31, 2026, and results are being reflected on a consolidated basis in stages.

According to the company, the Siwon School education business generated KRW 21.7 billion in 2024; this is the figure for the transferred business and should be kept separate from Gold&S's own 2024 revenue of KRW 9.6 billion.

After integration, Siwon School focuses on conversation courses in 17 languages, while Siwon School LAB specializes in exam English, and the two brands play complementary roles.

An AI learning service for second foreign languages was introduced in December 2025, and the company said about 16% of package buyers chose the AI option within three months of launch. The company aims to lift AI-related revenue to more than 40% of total sales by 2027.

Profit by brand is not disclosed, so profitability by unit cannot be pinned down from outside. Competition has intensified through new entrants, private-tutoring curbs and slowing household income, and in edtech through AI innovation and M&A among education companies.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 9.49 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.95 billion in 2023 (up 4.9% year on year), the peak year, and then fell to KRW 9.59 billion in 2024 (down 3.6%) and KRW 9.02 billion in 2025 (down 6.0%), a two-year decline from 2024 through 2025.

Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating results were an operating loss of KRW 1.14 billion in 2022, an operating loss of KRW 1.37 billion in 2023 (losses continued and widened), an operating loss of KRW 486 million in 2024 (losses continued and narrowed) and an operating loss of KRW 1.88 billion in 2025 (losses continued and widened), which makes four consecutive years of operating losses from 2022 to 2025.

The operating margin was -12.1% in 2022, -13.7% in 2023, -5.1% in 2024 and -20.8% in 2025, the lowest of the four years. The company attributed the 2025 decline in operating profit to higher early-stage operating costs tied to new business investment.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was a net loss of KRW 6.25 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 2.96 billion in 2023 (losses continued and narrowed), a net profit of KRW 61.32 million in 2024 (a turn to profit) and a net loss of KRW 1.47 billion in 2025 (a turn to loss).

Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed at the same amounts in each year. The debt-to-equity ratio moved from 28.7% in 2022, 28.0% in 2023 and 22.3% in 2024 to 43.8% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 572 million in 2022, -KRW 518 million in 2023, KRW 292 million in 2024 and -KRW 155 million in 2025. Quarterly results changed in 2026. Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 7.65 billion, up 244.7% from the prior quarter, with operating profit of KRW 653 million, a turn to profit.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 5.77 billion (down 24.7% from the prior quarter) and operating profit fell to KRW 187 million (down 71.3%). Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 6.6 billion (up 14.5% from the prior quarter, up 165.5% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 455 million (up 143.1% from the prior quarter).

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, totals KRW 2.01 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The main end demand for Gold&S is private education, especially foreign-language courses. The education market has faced several pressures at the same time.

New competitors have emerged, private-tutoring curbs remain in place, and slowing economic growth has reduced household income, which has intensified the contest for share in online and offline education.

In edtech, AI innovation and rising learning demand are changing business models quickly, and M&A among education companies is active as players try to scale. Gold&S expanded its foreign-language education scale by taking over the Siwon School business in this environment.

In December 2025 the company said that after the transfer its annual sales would exceed KRW 30 billion in 2026.

The transferred Siwon School education business generated KRW 21.7 billion in 2024, a figure that belongs to the transferred business and differs from Gold&S's own results, whose 2024 revenue was KRW 9.6 billion.

The exchange classifies the company under media and entertainment, but its operations are education services, so comparisons are most meaningful against education peers.

06

Outlook

In its December 2025 announcement of the Siwon School transfer, the company said it would grow into an education company with annual sales above KRW 30 billion in 2026.

Combined revenue for Q1 to Q3 2026 is above KRW 20 billion, and how close that comes to the company's expectation will become clear with Q4 2026 and the full-year results.

The company aims to lift AI-related revenue to more than 40% of total sales by 2027, and in a May 2026 report it said the AI service carries a more profitable structure than the earlier package products.

Positive factors include the consolidated effect of the transfer, operating profit in Q1 and Q3 2026, and a positive sum of profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters.

Negative factors include the Q2 2026 drop in operating profit, the 58.9% quarter-on-quarter fall in Q3 profit attributable to controlling shareholders, and the 2025 operating cash outflow.

One equity research note cited a turn to profit in Q3 and in operating cash flow as conditions for improvement, and listed a return to quarterly losses, deteriorating cash flow with rising receivables, and additional dilutive fundraising as signals that would undermine the thesis.

That view is third-party analysis and should be read separately from company guidance. Full-year 2026 results and the AI revenue share have not yet been reported.

08

Bull factors

Larger quarterly revenue base

Quarterly revenue ran at KRW 7.65 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 5.77 billion in Q2 and KRW 6.6 billion in Q3, all more than double the KRW 2.22 billion of Q4 2025. Q3 2026 revenue rose 165.5% year on year.

The company said that integrating content development, marketing and IT infrastructure after the transfer produced growth and better profitability at the same time. The expansion is confirmed by the periodic report figures.

Quarterly profits have appeared

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 653 million in Q1 2026, and Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 455 million was also a turn to profit year on year. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 2.01 billion.

Compared with the operating losses reported in the quarters before, the profit structure has shifted. Whether profitable quarters continue in sequence is the next point to check.

AI service economics

The company says its second-language AI learning service carries a more profitable structure than the earlier package products. It also reported that about 16% of package buyers chose the AI option within three months of launch. The company targets AI-related revenue above 40% of total sales by 2027. These figures come from the company and should be read apart from the disclosed results.

Bear factors

Four straight years of annual losses

Operating losses ran for four straight years from 2022 through 2025, and the 2025 loss widened to KRW 1.88 billion. Revenue fell 6.0% in 2025, a second consecutive annual decline after 2024. The company attributed the increase to early operating costs of new business investment. Whether the annual result turns to profit is the largest weakness in the bear case.

Volatile quarterly profit

Q2 2026 operating profit fell 71.3% from the prior quarter to KRW 187 million. Q3 2026 profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 501 million, down 58.9% from the prior quarter. One analysis named a return to quarterly losses as a signal that would break the investment thesis. Large swings between quarters make the trend hard to read, which is the core of the bear argument.

Cash flow and balance sheet strain

Operating cash flow in 2025 was negative at -KRW 155 million. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 22.3% in 2024 to 43.8% in 2025. The 2025 audit report carried an unqualified opinion, but it also referred to material uncertainty about the company's ability to continue as a going concern. If cash generation does not recover, the burden of raising capital could grow.

Risk factors

Business concentration and competition

Education accounts for effectively all revenue, so the business is directly exposed to the cycle and policy in that one market. Private-tutoring curbs, weaker household income and new entrants have intensified share competition. Whether integration efficiencies and the AI shift proceed as planned is an execution risk. Profit by brand is not disclosed, which makes unit profitability hard to check from outside.

Capital structure and governance

The largest shareholder group holds 50.34%. A share consolidation decision was disclosed on March 23, 2026, and the name-change proposal was rejected at the AGM on March 31, 2026. A 2024 report noted free capital reductions, rights offerings, convertible bond issues and a spin-off within the previous five years. Changes in the shareholder base and the way capital is raised need continued monitoring.

Audit and cash flow risk

The 2025 audit report gave an unqualified opinion but included references to going-concern uncertainty and internal control matters. Operating cash flow alternated between negative and positive from 2022 through 2025.

With operating profit down after Q1 2026, whether cash generation keeps pace with earnings is the key test. Until cash flow turns clearly positive, the assessment of financial stability stays limited.

Overall view

Gold&S expanded its quarterly revenue base after the January 2026 transfer of the Siwon School education business, and it posted operating profits in Q1 and Q3 2026. Annual operating losses, however, ran for four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025, and the 2025 loss widened.

Operating cash flow was negative in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 43.8%. Profit swings between quarters in 2026 are large, which makes the trend hard to read. On the balance sheet, total equity of KRW 11.5 billion at end-2025 exceeds total liabilities of KRW 5.04 billion.
