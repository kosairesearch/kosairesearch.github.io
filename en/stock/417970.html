Report overview

Recovery Since 2023 Meets a Sharp Q3 2026 Earnings Dip

Model Solution's operating profit recovered from its 2023 low and passed its 2022 level in 2025 at KRW 8.07 billion, but Q3 2026 operating profit fell 60.5% from a year earlier to KRW 1.31 billion, making the durability of the recovery the key open question.

02

Business structure

Model Solution is a prototyping and mold company within the Hankook & Company Group, focused on design mockups and rapid tooling.

Its structure is built on integrated manufacturing capability that runs from prototype production through mold making and injection molding to mass production, which the company cites as the basis for supporting customers' product commercialization.

The company attributed the main driver of 2025 results to revenue growth in its injection molding business (IMB). Mass-production project orders and sales in the IMB and TSB divisions have expanded, moving the business beyond a prototype-centered model.

The company says it is reducing dependence on specific customers and diversifying its client portfolio. In new areas, it launched the MS-AR20SE single-eye AR HMD in 2024 and, according to press reports, is co-developing an AI smart goggle with AI and XR firm Vernet.

In medical devices, it is building CDMO capability on ISO 13485 certification and an ISO Class 7 cleanroom line.

It also participates in a government-backed project on ultra-personalized mobility service design, running from April 2025 to December 2027, and press reports say it will receive about KRW 1.7 billion in R&D support over 33 months.

Against competitors, the company presents its integrated capability, spanning design, molds, injection and mass production under one roof, as its differentiator.

04

Earnings analysis

On the DART-filed annual basis, revenue fell 4.0% to KRW 64.7 billion in 2023 from KRW 67.4 billion in 2022, then rose 5.1% to KRW 68 billion in 2024 and 12.4% to KRW 76.5 billion in 2025, so it grew two years in a row from 2024 to 2025. 2025 was the largest revenue year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit fell 72.4% to KRW 2.18 billion in 2023, the smallest year in the period, and then rose 92.0% to KRW 4.19 billion in 2024 and 92.8% to KRW 8.07 billion in 2025, growing two years in a row. The operating margin moved from 11.7% in 2022 to 3.4% in 2023, 6.2% in 2024 and 10.6% in 2025.

The company cited IMB revenue growth and cost reduction as the main drivers of 2025 profitability. Profit attributable to owners of the parent rose 29.1% to KRW 6.83 billion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 3.31 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 2.61 billion in Q4 2025 (down 21.1% quarter on quarter), then KRW 748 million in Q1 2026 (down 71.4%), rebounded to KRW 1.79 billion in Q2 2026 (up 138.7%), and reached KRW 1.31 billion in Q3 2026 (down 26.7%).

Profit attributable to owners of the parent swung to a net loss of KRW 1.97 billion in Q1 2026 before returning to a KRW 1.73 billion profit in Q2 and falling to KRW 132 million in Q3. Over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, the combined profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 2 billion.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 15.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.81 billion in 2025, and the 2025 business report's cash flow statement shows a large negative change in net working capital as a main drag. Year-on-year comparisons for Q3 2026 are limited because the two periods use different financial statement scopes.

05

Industry analysis

Within the machinery and equipment sector, Model Solution is described less as a maker of finished robots and more as a prototyping company that supplies early-stage prototypes, molds and injection parts.

The company calls itself a hardware platform business and is expanding into areas with new-product development demand, including robot design competitions, industrial AR, personal mobility and medical devices.

Demand for prototypes and mass-production molds is tied to customers' new-product investment, so order flow can show up quickly in quarterly results.

In Q3 2026, revenue rose 2.0% from the prior quarter while operating profit fell 26.7%, showing that the link between sales volume and profitability varied from quarter to quarter. An analysis from the Korea IR Board said fixed-cost pressure relative to sales eased from 2025, bringing a full operating leverage effect.

The same analysis estimated that 2026 revenue would rise 10.1% and the operating margin would improve as the fixed-cost share declined. It also said that expanding IP-based businesses such as AMR drive units could lead to a revaluation, a view that rests on an external analyst's assumption of such expansion.

Peers in the same sector classification do not share the same business model, so the link between sector trends and company results should be read on a company-specific basis.

Outlook

In its Q2 2026 results release dated August 6, 2026, the company said it would expand new customer and mass-production project orders at TSB in the second half. Whether this plan is executed will be visible in order disclosures through the end of December 2026 and in later business reports.

The company says it continues to diversify its customer portfolio to reduce reliance on specific clients and to build high-value mass-production work into a new growth pillar. The Korea IR Board projected 2026 revenue growth of 10.1% and an operating margin of 11.7%, but that estimate predates the Q3 2026 results.

With Q3 2026 operating profit down 60.5% year on year, the gap between that projection path and actual quarterly results is a point to verify in upcoming quarters.

The government-backed mobility project runs to December 2027 and is being pursued over three years with a design firm and an autonomous driving company, aimed at a mobility concept for people with mobility difficulties. Beyond the project's end, how it links to the business is limited to what has been disclosed so far.

Bull factors

Margin recovery trend

The operating margin rose from a 3.4% low in 2023 to 6.2% in 2024 and 10.6% in 2025. The company cited IMB revenue growth and cost reduction as drivers. Full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 8.07 billion exceeded the KRW 7.89 billion of 2022.

Shift to mass production and customer diversification

Mass-production orders and sales in the IMB and TSB divisions have grown, shifting the model beyond prototypes. The company says it is lowering reliance on specific customers and diversifying its client base. It also plans to expand TSB new-customer orders in the second half.

Improved financial structure

Total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 72.3 billion and total liabilities were KRW 25.1 billion. The company said it fully repaid borrowings in Q2 2026.

Bear factors

Sharp profit drop in Q3 2026

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 1.31 billion, down 60.5% year on year. Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 132 million, down 95.7% year on year. Quarter on quarter, operating profit fell 26.7% and parent profit fell 92.4%.

Large swings in quarterly profit

Profit attributable to owners of the parent swung to a KRW 1.97 billion net loss in Q1 2026, then returned to a KRW 1.73 billion profit in Q2. Over the last four quarters the combined total is only KRW 2 billion. Quarterly profit swings are large, so earnings stability is limited.

Lower operating cash flow

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 15.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.81 billion in 2025. The 2025 business report's cash flow statement shows a large negative change in net working capital. Q3 2026 figures are on a standalone basis, which limits direct comparison with earlier consolidated results.

Risk factors

Concentrated major shareholder

According to the company's IR website, minority shareholders hold 35.0%. The concentrated ownership structure may make trading liquidity and price formation differ from a more widely held company.

Dependence on projects and government funding

Expanding TSB new mass-production orders in the second half is a plan the company announced, and firm order volumes have not been shown in disclosures. The government R&D support runs over 33 months, so funding after the project ends in December 2027 needs separate confirmation.

Comparison limits and base effects

Q3 2026 was prepared on a standalone basis after a subsidiary was liquidated, while Q3 2025 is consolidated, so the year-on-year decline mixes differences in scope. Because Q1 2026 was a net loss and Q2 turned to profit, the base for later quarterly comparisons is also uneven.

Overall view

Model Solution's DART-filed results show a recovery since the 2023 low, with revenue and operating profit each rising two years in a row. The KRW 8.07 billion operating profit in 2025 exceeded the KRW 7.89 billion of 2022, but the operating margin of 10.6% remains below the 11.7% of 2022.

On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 46.3% in 2022 to 34.7% in 2025, and the company said it fully repaid borrowings in Q2 2026. However, Q3 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 1.31 billion, down 60.5% year on year, and profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 132 million.

Q1 2026 had a KRW 1.97 billion net loss attributable to owners of the parent, and the last four quarters total KRW 2 billion. Because Q3 2026 is on a standalone basis, comparison with the consolidated Q3 2025 is limited.

The next checkpoints are the second-half 2026 TSB mass-production order disclosures, the full-year 2026 preliminary results and business report, and progress on the 2027 government project.
