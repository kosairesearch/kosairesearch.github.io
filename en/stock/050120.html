02

Business structure

ES Cube was founded in 1977 to manufacture and sell tents and outdoor goods and listed on the KOSDAQ in 2002. The business consists of one segment, leisure and functional tents, and tents accounted for 100% of sales based on second-quarter 2026 revenue.

Products are manufactured at subsidiary plants in China and Vietnam. Finished goods are sold domestically and exported to large overseas buyers in Japan, North America and Europe.

The company's own introduction describes it as an ODM (original design manufacturing) exporter ranking roughly third in global tent production. Because the export base is concentrated among overseas buyers, sales move together with those buyers' order cycles and exchange-rate trends.

The customer base is widely described as well-known global camping brands. Within the leisure products sector of eight listed stocks, the company ranked eighth by 2025 annual revenue, with revenue just above 10% of the sector average. On the same basis it also ranked lowest in operating margin.

A structure in which equity-method affiliates make up most of the separate-basis balance sheet assets is another defining feature.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 75.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 63.3 billion in 2023 (down 15.8% year on year), then to KRW 16.2 billion in 2024 (down 74.5%) and KRW 13.9 billion in 2025 (down 13.9%).

Revenue declined for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest in the 2022–2025 span. The company attributed the 2025 decline to a downturn in its business environment, which led to operating and net losses, in a filing made on March 16, 2026.

Operating profit fell from KRW 15.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.8 billion in 2023 (down 29.7%), then turned into an operating loss of KRW 3.74 billion in 2024 and widened to KRW 12.8 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 20.4% in 2022 to 17.0% in 2023, -23.1% in 2024 and -92.1% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from KRW 25.7 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 27.9 billion in 2023, a profit of KRW 909 million in 2024 and a net loss of KRW 2.05 billion in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 24.7 billion in 2022, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder number.

By quarter, the third quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 3.37 billion and an operating loss of KRW 780 million, while the fourth quarter brought revenue of KRW 2.21 billion (down 34.3% from the prior quarter) and an operating loss of KRW 6.24 billion.

Revenue peaked at KRW 5.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 162.1% from the prior quarter), and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 16.34 million.

Revenue then fell to KRW 4.8 billion in the second quarter (down 17.3%) and KRW 3.74 billion in the third quarter (down 22.1%), a two-quarter decline, although third-quarter revenue rose 10.9% year on year. Operating losses widened again to KRW 127 million in the second quarter and KRW 898 million in the third quarter.

Controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 2.11 billion in the third quarter was a swing to profit from the prior quarter but down 42.4% year on year.

Controlling-shareholder net profit for the last four quarters totaled KRW 3.2 billion, and because operating losses and net profit moved in different directions, the contribution of non-operating items is a point to check in the next periodic report.

Operating cash flow ranged from KRW -53.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.7 billion in 2023, KRW 4.6 billion in 2024 and KRW -6.6 billion in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 91.3 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities of KRW 7.12 billion gave a debt ratio of 7.8%.

In the 2025 annual ranking of eight leisure products stocks, the company placed fourth by market capitalization but eighth by both revenue and operating margin. Its operating margin sits well below the sector average.
