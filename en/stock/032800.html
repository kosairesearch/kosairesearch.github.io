Business structure

Fantagio is an entertainment management company founded in 1991 and listed on KOSDAQ in 1997. The company says it is expanding from artist management into film and drama production, record production and overseas concerts.

Its roster includes Baek Yun-sik, Kim Seon-ho, Moon Ga-young, Lee Sung-kyung, Ong Seong-wu, Lee Se-young, Cha Jun-hwan, ASTRO, Lee Chang-sub and Lunate, with ASTRO and Weki Meki among its main music acts. On November 10, 2026, the company announced an exclusive contract with actor Kim Jae-young.

Management revenue depends on how often its artists appear in dramas, release records or perform, so quarterly results can swing with that schedule. Production revenue tends to be recognized when titles are scheduled, which widens quarterly variation.

The revenue split by segment should be checked in the business report footnotes. Listed peers in the same industry include HYBE, SM Entertainment, JYP Ent. and YG Entertainment. Compared with these companies, Fantagio differs in the scale of its group IP and in its overseas revenue share.

Reports of tax allegations involving affiliated actors have also drawn attention to reputational risk as a structural weakness.

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 38.5 billion in the June 2023 fiscal year to KRW 72.3 billion in the June 2024 fiscal year, then fell to KRW 38.2 billion in the June 2025 fiscal year (down 47.2% year on year) and KRW 37.2 billion in the June 2026 fiscal year (down 2.5%).

Revenue has declined for two consecutive years since the June 2024 peak, and the June 2026 fiscal year was the smallest revenue year in the period. The KRW 34.1 billion drop in the June 2025 fiscal year shows how far scale contracted, and the drivers of that decline should be traced in the segment footnotes.

Operating losses were KRW 4.91 billion, KRW 1.54 billion, KRW 8.03 billion and KRW 7.52 billion across the June 2023 to June 2026 fiscal years, which is four consecutive years of losses. Operating margins were -12.8%, -2.1%, -21.0% and -20.2%, so the smallest loss rate came in the year with the largest revenue.

That pattern suggests that revenue scale is closely tied to covering fixed costs. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 18.3 billion, KRW 6.4 billion, KRW 9.19 billion and KRW 11.8 billion over the same years.

Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 3.63 billion to positive KRW 2.44 billion and KRW 12.7 billion, then turned to negative KRW 9.39 billion in the June 2026 fiscal year.

On a quarterly basis, revenue fell from KRW 12.7 billion in Q2 2025 to KRW 10.4 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 9.28 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 5.98 billion in Q1 2026. Q2 2026 revenue recovered to KRW 11.6 billion, up 93.5% from the prior quarter but down 8.7% year on year.

The operating loss in Q2 2026 was KRW 2.81 billion, wider than KRW 2.22 billion in Q1 2026, while the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 6.56 billion, a gap that points to non-operating items. The line items should be confirmed in the quarterly report.

Over the last four quarters, from Q3 2025 to Q2 2026, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 11.8 billion.

Industry analysis

The entertainment industry is highly dependent on the success of individual artist groups. Record sales, concerts, drama appearances and overseas distribution move together, so how often a roster is active feeds directly into revenue.

Listed peers in the same industry include HYBE, SM Entertainment, JYP Ent. and YG Entertainment, which have built their businesses around owned artist IP and rising overseas revenue shares. Fantagio's revenue has fallen for two straight fiscal years after its peak, which places it in a downward phase of the cycle.

Still, the quarter-on-quarter rebound in Q2 2026 revenue shows that new activity is showing up at the quarterly level. In drama production, broadcaster and OTT scheduling demand affects producer revenue.

When production clusters into particular quarters, quarterly results swing more, so annual and quarterly trends should be read together.

Against larger agencies that hold several major group IPs, Fantagio's roster is relatively concentrated, which magnifies the effect of an inactive artist or a reputational issue on total results. Adding new actors and expanding production can be read as efforts to spread that concentration across a broader roster.
