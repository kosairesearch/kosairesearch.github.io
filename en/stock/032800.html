Earnings analysis

On disclosed figures, Fantagio's revenue rose from KRW 38.5 billion in FY June 2023 to KRW 72.3 billion in FY June 2024, then fell to KRW 38.2 billion in FY June 2025 and KRW 37.2 billion in FY June 2026, a second straight annual decline. FY June 2024 was the largest year in the period, and FY June 2026 the smallest.

FY June 2025 saw the steepest drop, down 47.2% year on year, while FY June 2026 fell 2.5%. Operating losses were KRW 4.91 billion in FY June 2023, KRW 1.54 billion in FY June 2024, KRW 8.03 billion in FY June 2025 and KRW 7.52 billion in FY June 2026, a four-year run of losses.

Operating margin was -12.8%, -2.1%, -21.0% and -20.2% across the same years, so the loss narrowed in FY June 2024, widened in FY June 2025, and narrowed slightly in FY June 2026.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders shrank from KRW 18.3 billion in FY June 2023 to KRW 6.4 billion in FY June 2024, then widened to KRW 9.19 billion in FY June 2025 and KRW 11.8 billion in FY June 2026. The net loss including non-controlling interests for FY June 2026 was also KRW 11.8 billion.

Operating cash flow moved from an inflow of KRW 12.7 billion in FY June 2025 to an outflow of -KRW 9.39 billion in FY June 2026. The debt ratio rose from 54.3% in FY June 2024 to 80.8% in FY June 2025 and 89.1% in FY June 2026.

At end-June 2026, total liabilities were KRW 37 billion and total equity KRW 41.5 billion, of which KRW 41.4 billion belonged to controlling shareholders.

Quarterly revenue fell from KRW 12.7 billion in Q2 2025 to KRW 10.4 billion in Q3, KRW 9.28 billion in Q4 and KRW 5.98 billion in Q1 2026, then rebounded to KRW 11.6 billion in Q2 2026.

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 5.18 billion in Q2 2025 to KRW 915 million in Q3, then widened to KRW 1.58 billion in Q4, KRW 2.22 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 2.81 billion in Q2 2026. The company has posted losses in five straight quarters since Q2 2025.

In Q2 2026, revenue rose while the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 6.56 billion from KRW 2.49 billion in Q1. The causes of the Q2 2025 loss spike and the composition of the Q2 2026 revenue rebound were not confirmed in this review, so they are items to verify in the Q3 periodic report.

On disclosed figures, operating losses were KRW 4.91 billion in FY June 2023, KRW 1.54 billion in FY June 2024, KRW 8.03 billion in FY June 2025 and KRW 7.52 billion in FY June 2026, a four-year run of losses. The debt ratio rose from 54.3% in FY June 2024 to 89.1% in FY June 2026. Total liabilities at end-June 2026 were KRW 37 billion, a larger debt burden relative to the equity base.
