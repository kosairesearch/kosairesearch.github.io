02

Business structure

Serim B&G, founded in 2003, is an eco-friendly packaging company that runs three business segments: vacuum forming, eco-friendly products, and robotics. By 2025 sales, the mix was 74.3% vacuum forming, 23.8% eco-friendly products, and 1.7% robotics (The Bell, reported March 2026).

The vacuum forming segment produces PET and PP food packaging trays and lunch containers, and it accounts for close to three-quarters of sales. The eco-friendly segment makes biodegradable shopping bags, pharmacy bags, and mulching film from biodegradable raw materials that the company synthesizes from PBAT and PLA.

The robotics segment started by distributing cleaning and logistics robots from Pudu Robotics in Korea through existing customer and sales networks. Robotics sales were reported at about KRW 800 million in 2025, and the company sees room for expansion even though the scale is still small.

A July 2025 report put the export share at 1.4%, so most sales are generated domestically. Demand for eco-friendly products is reported to be rising as environmental regulation tightens.

Competitively, Serim B&G faces other domestic small and mid-sized makers that handle both plastic food containers and biodegradable film. Citing difficulty in securing cost competitiveness in its core business, the company was reported in March 2026 to be reviewing M&A in food or robotics.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 52.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 58.2 billion in 2023 (up 10.9% year on year), then fell 4.9% to KRW 55.4 billion in 2024 and 13.4% to KRW 48 billion in 2025, a two-year decline from 2024 through 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 2.69 billion in 2022, peaked at KRW 4.3 billion in 2023 (up 59.7%), then dropped to KRW 3.08 billion in 2024 (down 28.3%) and KRW 914 million in 2025 (down 70.4%). The operating margin was 5.1% in 2022, 7.4% in 2023, 5.6% in 2024, and 1.9% in 2025.

The company cited lower sales and higher promotion costs for new businesses as the main factors behind the 2025 decline (Digital Today, reported March 2026). On fnguide's calculation, the cost of sales ratio rose from 80.44% in 2023 to 83.96% in 2025, adding cost pressure.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.81 billion in 2023, KRW 2.32 billion in 2024 (down 39.0%), and KRW 1.87 billion in 2025 (down 19.6%). Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts for those years.

Quarterly results moved from operating profit of KRW 783 million on revenue of KRW 13.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 73.61 million on revenue of KRW 10.5 billion in the fourth quarter, a swing into loss.

The first quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 380 million, followed by KRW 393 million in the second quarter (up 3.4%) and KRW 951 million in the third quarter (up 142.2% from the prior quarter and up 21.5% year on year), marking two consecutive quarterly increases.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 12.6 billion was 7.3% lower than a year earlier, so profitability improvement offset a sales decline.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders bottomed at KRW 439 million in the first quarter of 2026, then rose to KRW 730 million and KRW 975 million in the second and third quarters. The debt ratio was 42.6% at the end of 2025, up from 40.2% in 2024.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 6.49 billion in 2023 and KRW 5.89 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.24 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Serim B&G's end markets are food packaging and the market for environmentally regulated products. The company's own commentary links growth in its vacuum forming business to expanding convenience foods and online food distribution, as of June 2026.

The eco-friendly segment is benefiting from tighter environmental regulation that is lifting purchases of biodegradable products. After the government said it would prepare a roadmap for reducing plastic use, the company drew attention as a representative eco-friendly plastics maker.

However, concern once arose over the suspension of environmental labeling certification, and a company official said in a May 2025 report that related sales were only slowly recovering after the certification was extended.

The same official said that repeated policy shifts had led customers to hesitate over purchasing decisions. Developing and selling its own biodegradable raw materials is a point of differentiation that extends the business to the materials stage.

With a market value in the small-cap range, Serim B&G is far smaller than large petrochemical companies, so individual customers and policy variables likely weigh more on results than the broader industry cycle.

Its earnings direction can be read as tied to food packaging demand and the strength of environmental regulation. The recent quarterly pattern shows flat sales alongside improving profitability.

In March 2025, the company's chief executive stated a goal of growing into a business with KRW 200–300 billion in sales through new businesses. Whether robotics sales reach the internally expected range is the key variable for changes in the profit structure.

Robot cleaner distribution through existing sales networks is presented as the basis for scalability, but the gap against the 2025 target means execution still needs to be confirmed. In the eco-friendly segment, the pace of demand recovery depends on environmental certification and the direction of anti-plastic policy.

The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, and whether annual sales recover and which way the operating margin moves are the next checkpoints.
