Profit Turn Holds but Quarterly Results Swing; Overseas Platforms Are the Variable

KidariStudio returned to annual profit in 2025, but quarterly earnings have swung since then, making growth at its overseas webtoon platforms the key variable for profit trends.

  2. 2

02

Business structure

KidariStudio is a content company that secures intellectual property such as webtoons and web novels and extends it into platforms, merchandise and video. The company operates Bomtoon, a webtoon platform aimed at female readers, while a subsidiary runs Lezhin Comics, an adult general webtoon platform.

Webtoon platforms are the center of the business, and the company said sales at Bomtoon and Lezhin Comics rose more than 15% in 2025. Overseas it runs Bomtoon TW in Taiwan, a Japanese platform, and Lezhin US in North America, tailoring strategy to the user characteristics of each country.

According to company statements, Lezhin US recorded payments of about KRW 25 billion in 2025, and the Japanese platform passed 1 million cumulative subscribers. Merchandise and pop-up stores under its MD business are expanding as a channel for moving popular IP offline.

The new short-drama platform Lezhin Snack launched simultaneously in Korea, the United States and Japan in early February 2026, reworking webtoon IP into short vertical video. The company says extending IP into video and games should lead viewers back to the original webtoons, forming a virtuous cycle.

On competition, the depth of original IP and a local user base overseas appear to be the main differentiators among platforms. However, few public figures show the revenue scale of the new businesses, so their share relative to the webtoon platforms is hard to pin down.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 169.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 171 billion in 2023 (up 0.9%), KRW 205.2 billion in 2024 (up 20.0%) and KRW 217.5 billion in 2025 (up 6.0%), marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025.

The growth rate was largest in 2024 at 20.0% and slowed to 6.0% in 2025, and 2025 was the largest year among 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit moved from KRW 4.26 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 5.74 billion in 2023, a swing into loss, then to KRW 2.19 billion in 2024, a return to profit, and to KRW 12.4 billion in 2025, up 465.7%.

Operating margin was 2.5% in 2022, -3.4% in 2023, 1.1% in 2024 and 5.7% in 2025, the highest of the four years.

Controlling-shareholder net income went from a loss of KRW 2.82 billion in 2022 and a loss of KRW 34.5 billion in 2023 (losses widened) to a loss of KRW 7.6 billion in 2024 (losses narrowed), before turning to net income of KRW 6.63 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 6.52 billion in 2025, a different figure from controlling-shareholder net income.

The company attributed the 2025 operating improvement to systematized translation and content sourcing costs, restructured overseas platforms and a unified structure for local overseas corporations.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 9.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 21.3 billion in 2025, while the debt-to-equity ratio climbed from 37.9% to 68.4%, raising financial leverage.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit was KRW 3.56 billion in 3Q 2025 and KRW 6.43 billion in 4Q 2025 (up 80.7% from the prior quarter), then fell to KRW 3.44 billion in 1Q 2026 (down 46.5%).

Controlling-shareholder net income in 1Q 2026 was KRW 6.28 billion (up 631.1% from the prior quarter), moving in the opposite direction to operating profit, which suggests non-operating items may have had a large effect.

Operating profit recovered to KRW 5.52 billion in 2Q 2026 (up 60.3%) and then fell to KRW 4.31 billion in 3Q 2026 (down 21.9%), while the quarter swung to a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.89 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The webtoon industry as a whole is in a downturn, yet Money Today reported in August 2026 that KidariStudio's overseas platforms continued to grow. Domestically, press reports said Bomtoon's sales rose 21% year over year in 1Q 2026, suggesting its home base is holding up.

Taiwan platform Bomtoon TW was reported to have grown more than threefold over the same period. In North America, after repositioning Lezhin Comics as an adult general webtoon platform, the company said monthly active users rose 29% and new sign-ups rose 35% in 2026 compared with the prior year.

These figures come from company materials and are not independently verified indicators. Webtoon platforms have long IP accumulation cycles and earnings that move with the success of new titles, so quarterly swings tend to be large.

The global short-drama market is drawing attention as a new revenue source for turning webtoon IP into video, but the sales scale of the short-drama segment has few public figures, making direct comparison with webtoon platforms difficult.

Listed Korean media and entertainment companies have different business structures, so simple comparisons with KidariStudio are limited. Its competitive position appears to depend on the strength of its original IP and its user base in overseas markets.

06

Outlook

The company said it will keep expanding investment in original IP and new businesses. The 2026 WEFESTA event, a women-oriented content festival, will run from November 20 to 22, 2026 at the P-Factory venue in Gwangjin-gu, Seoul.

Besides the webtoon platforms, the event will showcase the short-drama platform Lezhin Snack and the character chat service Lezhin Teaz, with cast members of Lezhin Snack and webtoon writers scheduled to appear on stage.

As of May 2026 reporting, the short-drama adaptation of the webtoon IP Yahwacheop was scheduled for release on Lezhin Snack, but no confirmed release date has been announced.

The company said it would roll out offline global events in sequence, starting with a domestic pop-up store from late May 2026 and moving to Taiwan, China and the United States.

The AI character chatbot Lezhin Teaz, announced for release in October 2026, is part of a plan to extend the IP value chain across platforms, MD, short drama and AI services. The next regular checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, which will contain full-year 2026 sales and operating margin.

For the new businesses, the key question is whether Lezhin Snack's revenue contribution shows up in the figures reported in that annual report.

Bull factors

Overseas platforms keep growing

Lezhin US payments rose past KRW 20 billion in 2024 and reached about KRW 25 billion in 2025. Bomtoon TW was reported to have grown more than threefold year over year in 1Q 2026. The company said monthly active users at Lezhin Comics in North America rose 29% in 2026 versus the prior year. As overseas platforms grow, reliance on a single domestic platform may ease.

Profitability recovery

Operating margin rose from 1.1% in 2024 to 5.7% in 2025, and operating profit of KRW 12.4 billion was up 465.7% from the prior year. The company attributes the improvement to cost efficiency, including systematized translation and sourcing costs and unified overseas operations.

Operating profit of KRW 3.44 billion in 1Q 2026 and KRW 5.52 billion in 2Q 2026 shows the operating improvement holding in some quarters.

IP expansion strategy being tested

The short-drama platform Lezhin Snack launched simultaneously in Korea, the United States and Japan in early February 2026. The company says extending webtoon IP into video and games should lead audiences back to the original works.

Offline events such as WEFESTA and MD merchandise are additional channels for widening IP revenue. Once the revenue scale of the new businesses is disclosed, their contribution to this structure can be checked in figures.

Bear factors

Unstable quarterly earnings

The controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.89 billion in 3Q 2026 turned the quarter into a loss, after KRW 6.28 billion in 1Q 2026 and KRW 1.98 billion in 2Q 2026. Controlling-shareholder net income for the last four quarters totals KRW 7.22 billion, a thin earnings base.

Because operating profit and net income moved in opposite directions in some quarters, the quality of earnings also needs to be examined.

Past losses and rising leverage

In 2023 the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 5.74 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 34.5 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio climbed from 37.9% in 2022 to 68.4% in 2025, raising financial leverage. The 2025 return to profit is a single year of results, so its continuity needs further confirmation.

Slowing growth and industry pressure

Sales growth slowed from 20.0% in 2024 to 6.0% in 2025 after 0.9% in 2023. Press reports describe a broad downturn in the webtoon industry, which may limit room for growth in the domestic market. Until the new businesses account for a larger share, growth at the webtoon platforms will decide overall results.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 6.43 billion in 4Q 2025 to KRW 3.44 billion in 1Q 2026, recovered to KRW 5.52 billion in 2Q 2026 and fell to KRW 4.31 billion in 3Q 2026. Controlling-shareholder net income has swung sharply from quarter to quarter, alternating between losses and profits. When non-operating items weigh heavily, improvements in core operations may not carry through to net income.

IP and intangible asset impairment

A company with a large share of intangible assets from acquired subsidiaries or content rights can record impairment losses if those assets underperform expectations. The profitability of webtoon IP varies widely by title. How much the company invests in which IP will shape its cost structure in the following years.

New business and overseas expansion costs

New businesses such as short drama, AI services and offline events may incur upfront investment costs before generating revenue. Overseas platforms are exposed to exchange rates, local regulation and shifts in user tastes. Whether the new businesses weigh on operating margin needs to be checked against quarterly cost trends.

Overall view

KidariStudio returned to annual profit in 2025, with operating profit of KRW 12.4 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 6.63 billion. Growth at overseas platforms and at the domestic platforms Bomtoon and Lezhin Comics drove the recovery, and the company also emphasized cost efficiency.

However, the controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.89 billion in 3Q 2026 means the continuity of quarterly profit is still being tested. Controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 7.22 billion over the last four quarters is a small base, so quarterly swings are large.

On the balance sheet, total equity of KRW 203.5 billion and total liabilities of KRW 139.2 billion were disclosed at the end of 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose from 37.9% in 2022 to 68.4% in 2025.

The share price sits in a range below net asset value, and management said through a share buyback decision that the price did not fully reflect intrinsic value, a view that belongs to the company.

The points to check are the response to WEFESTA on November 20–22, 2026, full-year results in the 2026 annual report, and the new-business contribution and operating margin trend in 1Q 2027 results. Those three points will show whether overseas platform growth is carrying through to profit.
