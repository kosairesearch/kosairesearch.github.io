02

Business structure

UNID btplus manufactures and sells medium-density fiberboard (MDF). The company was spun off from the wood business of Unid in November 2022 and listed on KOSPI on November 28 of that year.

MDF, its core product, is used as a material for furniture, interiors, packaging and household goods, and the company has been described as the top domestic MDF producer by market share.

In a 2022 share table compiled from Korea Plywood and Board Association data, the company held 27.5%, the highest among the five producers listed, with Dongwha Enterprise at 26.6%. Sales are split roughly 92% MDF and formaldehyde-related products and 8% PB and sawn timber, according to Butler's compilation for 2Q 2026.

The company also makes LPM and flooring products and launched its own flooring brand, OLGODA, to widen consumer contact. On the technology side, it holds a patent on an adhesive manufacturing method that lowers formaldehyde emissions to the SUPER E0 level.

Its new business includes a hydrogen gas leak detection product, and in 2024 it said it was discussing supply to China. Demand is tied to furniture and interior materials, so the business is exposed to construction and property cycles.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 29.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 168.8 billion in 2023, an increase of 477.6% year on year, and reached KRW 194.9 billion in 2024, up 15.5%, which was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Sales then fell 19.2% to KRW 157.5 billion in 2025, when the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 9.15 billion. Operating margin moved from 4.9% in 2022 to -2.4% in 2023, 1.6% in 2024 and -5.8% in 2025.

Operating profit of KRW 1.43 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 4.07 billion in 2023, and returned to an operating profit of KRW 3.05 billion in 2024.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 960 million in 2022, KRW 862 million in 2023 (down 10.2%) and KRW 6.4 billion in 2024 (up 642.4%), before a net loss of KRW 3.96 billion in 2025.

In 2024 net income attributable to controlling shareholders far exceeded the operating profit of KRW 3.05 billion, so non-operating items should be considered alongside operating results.

On a quarterly basis, 3Q 2025 sales were KRW 37.9 billion with an operating loss of KRW 1.44 billion, and 4Q 2025 sales fell 12.2% quarter on quarter to KRW 33.3 billion with an operating loss that widened to KRW 6.47 billion.

In 1Q 2026 sales recovered 21.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 40.3 billion and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.91 billion. In 2Q 2026 sales reached KRW 43 billion and operating profit was KRW 2.02 billion, a swing back to profit from the prior quarter, with a controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 3.13 billion.

In 3Q 2026 sales fell 25.2% quarter on quarter and 15.2% year on year to KRW 32.1 billion, and the operating loss widened to KRW 6.06 billion from a loss a year earlier. The controlling-shareholder net loss in 3Q 2026 was KRW 5.55 billion.

A FnGuide summary attributes weaker domestic and construction demand and higher cost burdens from raw-material supply shortages to the profitability decline, which fits the pattern where higher sales did not translate into sustained profit.

05

Industry analysis

MDF is tied to demand for interior building and furniture materials, so it is sensitive to construction and housing activity. A FnGuide summary describes weaker domestic and construction activity in 2026 as reducing MDF demand, which is a downward pressure on volumes.

On market share, the company held 28.6% in 2020, 28.8% in 2021 and 27.5% in 2022, a slight decline in 2022, while Dongwha Enterprise moved from 28.0% to 28.5% and then 26.6% over the same years.

Both leading producers lost share in 2022, and the table does not show whether the gap between them has widened or narrowed since. Raw-material supply is a second variable, since shortages raise costs and cost defense largely determines margins.

In 2024 the company stressed carbon neutrality and the use of domestic forest resources as a differentiator in its products. Industry cycle data in a quantified form is limited, so the next reference points are whether construction activity and MDF demand indicators improve.

06

Outlook

In a voluntary disclosure dated October 22, 2026, the company set out its 2026 corporate value-up plan, which targets a return to profit through better profitability and a price-to-book ratio of 0.35x or higher by 2028.

The plan also includes maintaining the competitiveness of existing businesses, reviewing possible portfolio improvements, and a stated intention to pursue a treasury share acquisition trust agreement.

On products, the company is widening consumer contact through its OLGODA flooring brand, and its hydrogen gas leak detection product has been described in reports since 2024 as a new business under development. A 2024 article mentioned talks on supplying hydrogen detection tape to China.

Because the operating loss widened in 3Q 2026, the key reference point is how 4Q 2026 and full-year 2026 results align with the return-to-profit target. The 2025 preliminary annual results were announced on January 28, 2026, so 2026 preliminary results may follow a similar timeline.

The 2026 annual business report will consolidate quarterly results into full-year figures and confirm the trend in operating margin and debt ratio. The 2028 targets are goals rather than outcomes, and progress will show up in quarterly results and future disclosures.

08

Bull factors

Top-tier share in domestic MDF

In a 2022 share table, the company held 27.5%, the highest among the five producers listed, after 28.6% in 2020 and 28.8% in 2021. A 2024 article also described it as the top domestic MDF producer by share.

It reportedly became the first in Korea to pass the highest grade of the building material emissions standard test. The bull argument is that this share base could anchor a sales recovery if furniture and interior demand improves.

Return to operating profit in 2Q 2026

The company moved from an operating loss of KRW 1.91 billion in 1Q 2026 to an operating profit of KRW 2.02 billion in 2Q 2026, and controlling-shareholder net profit was KRW 3.13 billion. Sales rose from KRW 40.3 billion to KRW 43 billion over the same period.

Among the last five quarters, only 2Q 2026 recorded an operating profit, so whether the profit can be sustained is the key check for this argument. The argument rests on margin recovery when sales grow.

Financial capacity and shareholder return plans

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 28.5 billion and total equity was KRW 191.9 billion, a debt ratio of 14.8%. Operating cash flow stayed positive at KRW 2.97 billion in 2025. The 2026 value-up plan states an intention to pursue a treasury share acquisition trust agreement. A low debt burden alongside a planned shareholder return measure is cited as a supportive factor.

Bear factors

Swing to an annual loss in 2025

The operating margin was -5.8%. The controlling-shareholder net loss was KRW 3.96 billion, compared with a controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 6.4 billion in 2024. Lower sales and a swing to loss in the same year form the starting point of the bear argument.

Renewed loss in 3Q 2026

Sales in 3Q 2026 fell 15.2% year on year to KRW 32.1 billion, and the operating loss widened to KRW 6.06 billion. The controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters totals KRW 8.58 billion. The bear argument is that the 2Q 2026 profit may prove a one-off. If profitable quarters do not repeat, an annual loss structure could persist.

Weaker construction demand and cost pressure

A FnGuide summary says weaker domestic and construction demand reduced MDF volumes, while raw-material supply shortages raised cost burdens. When both factors apply at once, higher sales may not translate into operating profit. A 2024 article also linked the company's results to the property cycle. Unfavorable demand and cost conditions at the same time form the core of the bear case.

Risk factors

Demand and construction cycle

MDF demand is linked to furniture and interior material demand, so slower construction and housing activity directly affects sales. Sales in 3Q 2026 fell 15.2% year on year, which shows the demand pressure. A delayed demand recovery could weigh further on profit through utilization and fixed-cost burden.

Costs and raw-material supply

A FnGuide summary cites cost burdens from raw-material supply shortages as a driver of weaker profitability. If raw-material sourcing remains unstable, margins may struggle to recover even as sales grow.

The swing from profit in 2Q 2026 to a loss in 3Q 2026 within the same year suggests cost volatility is feeding into earnings volatility.

Execution of targets and new businesses

The 2028 targets of a 0.35x or higher price-to-book ratio and a return to profit are plans whose outcomes are not yet determined.

The hydrogen detection product and the OLGODA consumer brand are still in expansion stages, and the progress of the China supply talks mentioned in a 2024 article is not shown in later disclosures. The company's small market value also leaves share price volatility and trading liquidity as ongoing risks.

Overall view

UNID btplus is among the top domestic producers of MDF, with a low debt burden and positive operating cash flow through 2025. Profitability, however, swung from a profit in 2024 to a loss in 2025, and after returning to an operating profit in 2Q 2026 it posted an operating loss of KRW 6.06 billion in 3Q 2026.

The controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters totals KRW 8.58 billion, and an earnings-based multiple cannot be calculated at present.

On the demand side, weaker domestic and construction activity is cited as a downside factor, and on the cost side, raw-material supply shortages are cited as a burden on margins.

The company's 2026 value-up plan targets a return to profit and a price-to-book ratio of 0.35x or higher by 2028, and whether these targets are met has not yet been determined.

The next reference points are whether 4Q 2026 and full-year 2026 results move toward operating profit in the 2026 preliminary and annual reports. The 1Q 2027 quarterly report will also show whether the 2Q 2026 profit is repeated.
