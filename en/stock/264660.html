C&G Hi Tech: Annual Recovery, Wider Quarterly Swings

C&G Hi Tech recovered from its 2024 low, with 2025 sales of KRW 196.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 24.7 billion, but quarterly results have swung widely since an operating loss in the first quarter of 2026.

  1. 1

    2025 sales reached KRW 196.2 billion and operating profit KRW 24.7 billion, leaving the 2024 trough behind. The operating margin rose to 12.6% in 2025 from 8.6% in 2024.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

C&G Hi Tech manufactures and sells CCSS (chemical supply systems) that deliver process chemicals to semiconductor and display fabs.

According to JobKorea company data as of March 2026, the sales mix was 38% fluorine resin and related products, 25% installation and set-up services, 14% piping set-up works, 12% chemical regeneration equipment and 11% other.

Its chemical mixing equipment, the largest product line, had 98.37% of 2025 sales from domestic customers. Its main customers are Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. Installation and piping services make up a meaningful share, so the company earns revenue beyond equipment sales.

New businesses in preparation include heat-dissipating boards, lining sheets, low-dielectric FCCL and glass substrates.

Etching gas is a high-difficulty product where a few suppliers dominate, and the company entered it through a joint venture with Daikin Industries, in which Daikin Industries holds 67%, C&G Hi Tech 23% and Samsung C&T 10%.

The company invested about KRW 13.8 billion in the venture, and a news headline reported that its principal in the stake was fully recovered. The glass substrate market is led by Corning and SCHOTT, and the company says its domestic process from materials to processing is a differentiator.

Production is shifting to the Anseong Techno Valley, where a groundbreaking ceremony was held in March 2026 with about KRW 33.2 billion invested.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 192.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 166.7 billion in 2023 (down 13.5% year on year) and KRW 151.2 billion in 2024 (down 9.3%), then rebounded to KRW 196.2 billion in 2025 (up 29.8%).

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 12.7 billion in 2023 (down 49.9%), then rose to KRW 12.9 billion in 2024 (up 1.6%) and KRW 24.7 billion in 2025 (up 91.2%), with increases in both 2024 and 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders dropped to KRW 6.88 billion in 2024 (down 46.4%) before recovering to KRW 19.5 billion in 2025 (up 183.7%).

For the 2024 decline, the company attributed the sales drop to customer investment plan changes typical of order-based businesses and the net profit decline to an impairment on an equity-method investment. The operating margin went from 13.2% in 2022 to 7.6% in 2023, then 8.6% in 2024 and 12.6% in 2025.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 26.6 billion in 2022 and KRW 26.8 billion in 2023 to KRW 820 million in 2024 before recovering to KRW 14.9 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio declined from 65.7% in 2022 to 38.0% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 143.2 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 54.3 billion.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 20.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 55.7 billion in the fourth quarter, dropped to KRW 17.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 69.3% from the prior quarter), then reached KRW 72.3 billion in the second quarter and KRW 44.3 billion in the third quarter.

Operating results swung from an operating loss of KRW 2.17 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to an operating profit of KRW 9.33 billion in the second quarter, then KRW 6.03 billion in the third.

A business-data service attributed the first-quarter 2026 deterioration to weaker investment in semiconductors and displays and a slowdown in CCSS equipment demand.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 956 million in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 5.69 billion in the second and KRW 14.6 billion in the third. Because quarterly sales move sharply with order timing, quarterly margins should be read alongside the annual trend.

05

Industry analysis

C&G Hi Tech's sales are sensitive to chipmaker and display equipment investment cycles. The sharp first-quarter 2026 decline was attributed to weaker investment and an economic slowdown that reduced CCSS equipment demand. The company also said in 2024 that changes in customers' investment plans drove sales swings.

Etching gas is a high-difficulty market dominated by a few suppliers, and the company entered it through a joint venture. The glass substrate market is led by Corning and SCHOTT, and the company reported in August 2025 that its proprietary M-PVD process produced large-area glass substrates.

Prototypes were provided to major customers such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix for technical review. Interest in glass substrates for AI semiconductors has drawn attention to related stocks, but that interest only reaches reported earnings once orders and mass production are confirmed.

Because Samsung Electronics' and SK hynix's facility plans largely set CCSS demand, their investment trends serve as the industry signal to track. The company says moving to Anseong, closer to large semiconductor clusters, should shorten its distance to key customers.

06

Outlook

The nearest order indicator is the KRW 38.15 billion semiconductor equipment contract with Samsung Electronics, signed on April 10, 2026, equal to about 19.44% of recent sales, with a contract period running through November 30, 2026.

The next checkpoints are how revenue is recognized as that period closes and whether follow-on orders are disclosed. On capacity, the company said current annual CCSS capacity of roughly KRW 150 billion to KRW 200 billion would rise to KRW 300 billion to KRW 400 billion after the move.

This is the company's own projection, and actual output will depend on completing the move and utilization. The company has given early 2027 as the target for completing the relocation. About KRW 33.2 billion is being invested in the move, with a groundbreaking in March 2026.

The company also plans to expand production of new products such as glass substrates and heat-dissipating boards. It exhibited large-area glass substrate prototypes at KPCA Show 2025 in September 2025 to reach potential customers.

New products will only contribute meaningful revenue once customers confirm mass production. The 2026 annual report will confirm fourth-quarter and full-year results, showing whether profitability holds up through quarterly volatility.

Bear factors

Quarterly earnings volatility

First-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 17.1 billion, down 69.3% from the prior quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 2.17 billion. Sales rose to KRW 72.3 billion in the second quarter and fell to KRW 44.3 billion in the third. Quarterly results move sharply with order timing.

Exposure to industry slowdown

The first-quarter 2026 decline was explained by weaker semiconductor and display investment and a slowdown that cut CCSS equipment demand. The chemical mixing equipment is the largest product line, so a turn in equipment investment hits it directly. In 2024, changes in customer investment plans also reduced sales.

Uncertain timing of new-business revenue

New businesses such as glass substrates, heat-dissipating boards and FCCL are described as in preparation, and their revenue contribution does not appear separately in filed results. An equity-method investment tied to the Daikin venture caused an impairment that reduced net profit in 2024. The point at which new businesses offset core volatility is not yet clear.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and capex cycles

Sales depend on capex plans of a few large customers, including Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. If customers adjust investment timing, revenue recognition can slip even after orders are disclosed. The sharp first-quarter 2026 decline illustrates this exposure.

Joint venture and equity-method investments

Results from the Daikin joint venture are recognized under the equity method, so net profit can swing with its operations and profitability. In 2024 an impairment on an equity-method investment was cited as a cause of lower net profit. Joint-venture gains and losses are harder to forecast than core sales.

Relocation investment and cash flow

About KRW 33.2 billion is being invested in the Anseong relocation, which broke ground in March 2026. Operating cash flow once fell to KRW 820 million in 2024. If investment spending and working capital needs overlap, cash flow pressure could rise.

Overall view

C&G Hi Tech recovered from its 2024 trough with 2025 sales of KRW 196.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 24.7 billion, and its operating margin rose to 12.6%. The debt-to-equity ratio also fell to 38.0% from 65.7% in 2022.

However, quarterly results remain volatile, including an operating loss of KRW 2.17 billion in the first quarter of 2026. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 14.6 billion in the third quarter of 2026, but quarterly flows depend heavily on order timing.

On orders, the KRW 38.15 billion Samsung Electronics contract signed in April 2026 is central, and its period runs through November 30, 2026.

New businesses such as glass substrates, heat-dissipating boards and the etching gas venture have a basis for expectations, but time is needed before their revenue contribution appears in filed figures. On the industry side, weaker semiconductor and display investment was cited for the first-quarter 2026 decline.

The next checkpoints are fourth-quarter and full-year results in the 2026 annual report and the timing of the Anseong relocation.
