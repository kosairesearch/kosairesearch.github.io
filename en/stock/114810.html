Chip-part profits recover; Ochang expansion is the next test

02

Business structure

Hansol IONES, founded in 2005, makes precision parts for semiconductor and display equipment, machining and cleaning-coating ultra-precise components that go inside production tools. The business has two pillars: precision machining and cleaning-coating.

Reporting on 2025 put precision machining for semiconductors at 78.47% of sales and precision cleaning-coating at 18.98%.

For 2024, KB Securities reported precision machining revenue of KRW 121.8 billion with operating profit of KRW 18.4 billion, and cleaning-coating revenue of KRW 35.3 billion with operating profit of KRW 4.7 billion.

The decline in cleaning-coating profit was attributed to higher costs after the Anseong new plant began operating. Coverage has presented Samsung and SK as major customers.

The company has supplied chamber parts for etching equipment to Applied Materials since December 2016 and has widened its supplied items since then. In 2024 it added a Dutch lithography equipment maker as a new customer.

The company has pointed to reuse of lithography-tool parts (re-use) and the cleaning-coating material Iconic as new revenue sources. Hansol Technics, which took a 34.47% stake and management control in January 2022, is the largest shareholder and brought the company into the Hansol group.

Reporting on the first half of 2025 put the split at 81.9% precision machining and 18.1% cleaning-coating, so segment weights vary by period.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 163.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 123.9 billion in 2023, down 24.4% year on year, then rose 26.8% to KRW 157.1 billion in 2024 and 26.5% to KRW 198.8 billion in 2025, so revenue increased in both 2024 and 2025. 2025 was the largest revenue year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit fell from KRW 36.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.22 billion in 2023, down 77.2%, then rose to KRW 23.1 billion in 2024, up 180.7%, and KRW 33.3 billion in 2025, up 44.4%. Operating margin was 22.0% in 2022, 6.6% in 2023, 14.7% in 2024 and 16.8% in 2025, recovering from the 2023 trough but still below 2022.

Reporting attributed the 2023 weakness to a downturn in the semiconductor market (DealSite). Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 28.3 billion in 2022, KRW 2.13 billion in 2023, KRW 31.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 28.4 billion in 2025.

Net profit in 2024 exceeded operating profit, which suggests non-operating items contributed, and the breakdown is something to confirm in the annual report. Quarterly, operating profit went from KRW 9.38 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.94 billion in the fourth quarter, down 68.6% from the prior quarter.

It then reached KRW 7.12 billion in the first quarter of 2026, up 141.8%, and KRW 6.56 billion in the second quarter, down 7.8%, before reaching KRW 12.4 billion in the third quarter, up 88.7% quarter on quarter and 32.0% year on year. Third-quarter revenue was KRW 65 billion, up 26.9% year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter was KRW 9.7 billion, up 50.6% year on year. Over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, that profit totaled KRW 24.2 billion.

Based on preliminary figures released on October 23, 2026, revenue for January to September 2026 was KRW 167.9 billion, up 14.13%, while cumulative operating profit was KRW 26.1 billion, down 14.21%.

The debt ratio fell from 50.8% in 2022 to 21.0% in 2025, with year-end 2025 total equity of KRW 210.3 billion and total liabilities of KRW 44.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The memory chip cycle is the largest variable for the company's earnings. Earnings fell during the 2023 semiconductor downturn, and brokerages have read the later improvement in memory conditions as the driver of more orders for precision machining.

The company has said that rising market share at top-tier global equipment makers supports steady growth in its precision machining business. Within the same machinery and equipment sector, listed companies tied to semiconductor equipment include Protec, People & Technology, Philoptics and Sunic System.

Their product mix and customer structure differ, so direct comparisons of sales scale or margins are limited. As a parts supplier, Hansol IONES depends on equipment makers' utilization rates and capital spending cycles.

A April 2026 report pointed to orders for cleaning-coating parts tied to HBM (high-bandwidth memory). Because the company has not disclosed an HBM revenue figure, the quarterly trend in the cleaning-coating segment is the practical way to gauge that contribution.

Earlier, in October 2025, the company had also said that a broader chip market recovery and new customer additions would support gradual earnings improvement in the second half of 2025.

06

Outlook

In an October 2025 report, the company said that a gradual earnings improvement should continue in the second half of 2025, supported by the global chip market recovery and new customer additions. The new revenue sources it has pointed to are the re-use of lithography-tool parts and Iconic, a cleaning-coating material.

The company says Iconic has four times the heat and wear resistance of conventional quartz, which reduces replacement cycles. On October 23, 2026, the board approved a new facility investment at a business park site in Ochang-eup, Cheongju, Chungcheongbuk-do.

The investment totals KRW 126 billion, or 59.92% of equity, and is intended to expand production capacity for growing semiconductor demand. The disclosed investment period runs from October 23, 2026 to February 29, 2028, and the end date is a scheduled date that may change with permits and construction progress.

In April 2026 the company decided to cancel 450,000 common shares, with the cancellation scheduled for May 12, 2026. On preliminary figures, revenue for January to September 2026 rose while cumulative operating profit fell.

Whether that pattern continues, and when the Ochang plant starts running and how it affects capacity and unit costs, are the next points to confirm.

08

Bull factors

Profit recovery led by precision machining

Operating profit recovered from KRW 8.22 billion in 2023 to KRW 33.3 billion in 2025, and operating margin rose from 6.6% to 16.8% over the same span. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 12.4 billion was the largest of the last five reported quarters. Preliminary cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 rose 14.13%, showing continued top-line growth.

Ochang plant expands production capacity

The Ochang new-plant investment disclosed on October 23, 2026 totals KRW 126 billion and is aimed at expanding capacity for rising semiconductor demand. With the investment period scheduled through February 2028, output from the plant could feed into revenue from 2027 onward. The company has consistently pointed to new customer additions as a driver of recovery.

New customers and materials business

Whether lithography-tool parts re-use and Iconic translate into actual revenue is the next point to check. The company says Iconic has four times the heat and wear resistance of conventional quartz.

A report from around April 2026 pointed to HBM-related cleaning-coating orders, which raised the possibility of growth in that segment.

09

Bear factors

Dependence on the chip cycle and the 2023 lesson

Operating profit fell to KRW 8.22 billion in 2023, down 77.2%, when the semiconductor market weakened, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 2.13 billion. Precision machining for semiconductors made up 78.47% of 2025 sales, so changes in the memory cycle pass straight through to results. A structure concentrated in one segment magnifies profit swings when the cycle turns.

Volatility in quarterly profit

Operating profit of KRW 2.94 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 was the lowest of the last five reported quarters. Second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 6.56 billion was down 7.8% from the first quarter.

Preliminary cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 fell 14.21%, so revenue growth has not translated into steady profit growth.

Investment burden and share-price volatility

The Ochang investment of KRW 126 billion equals 59.92% of equity, a large commitment for the company. On August 5, 2026, the exchange designated the stock a caution-listed stock for one day because of trading concentrated in a few accounts, with a 25.52% price change over three days. Cash outlays during construction and early-stage operating costs could weigh on margins.

10

Risk factors

Customer and cycle concentration

Precision machining for semiconductors accounted for 78.47% of 2025 sales, which leaves results sensitive to chip equipment investment cycles. A shift in orders from major customers can flow straight through to revenue. The sharp profit drop in 2023 is an example of this risk materializing.

Expansion costs and utilization

Coverage reported that higher costs from the new cleaning-coating plant in Anseong cut that segment's 2024 operating profit to KRW 4.7 billion. The Ochang plant could likewise pressure margins through depreciation and labor costs early in its operation. If utilization lags the added capacity, fixed-cost pressure would increase.

Share-price volatility and shareholder structure

As in the August 2026 caution designation, trading concentrated in a few accounts can produce large swings in the share price. The exchange said that if market warning levels are raised, further steps such as trading halts could follow.

Changes in the largest shareholder's stake, which was 34.47% when acquired in 2022, could affect the free float.

Overall view

Hansol IONES' recent results have been recovering since the 2023 trough.

Revenue reached KRW 198.8 billion in 2025 and operating profit KRW 33.3 billion, and in the third quarter of 2026 revenue was KRW 65 billion, operating profit KRW 12.4 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 9.7 billion.

Sales are concentrated in semiconductor precision machining, which was 78.47% of 2025 revenue, so results remain sensitive to the memory cycle. Quarterly profit has rebounded from the fourth quarter of 2025, but preliminary cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 declined year on year.

The KRW 126 billion Ochang investment aims to expand capacity and equals 59.92% of equity, so the financing burden during construction also matters. On the share price, the stock has shown high trading volatility, as seen in the August 2026 caution designation.

The next points to confirm are preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results, the 2026 annual report, and when the Ochang plant starts operating and how its costs appear in the accounts.
