02

Business structure

Auros Technology makes overlay metrology equipment that measures the alignment between layers in semiconductor wafers, mainly for front-end processes. The company started with wafer overlay tools for front-end lines and has since widened its lineup to packaging overlay and inspection equipment.

According to company IR material, HBM pad overlay and IR overlay tools are in development or validation, and 2D and 3D bump metrology tools are presented as entering customer qualification. Segment shares differ by data provider.

JobKorea's breakdown as of March 2026 shows OL-12inch tools at 46%, other products at 29%, services at 21% and merchandise at 4%, while AlphaSquare's aggregation puts OL-12inch at 74.59%.

Major customer SK hynix is reported to account for a large share of sales, and press reports say the company has also supplied Samsung Electronics. In 2024 it was reported to have supplied overlay metrology tools to Kioxia in Japan. The benchmark competitor in front-end overlay is US-based KLA.

Heungkuk Securities said in a December 2025 report that the company localized overlay tools competing with KLA and keeps more than 50% share at key customers. Overseas, the company set up a Tokyo branch in July 2024 and a Wuxi production subsidiary in August 2024, but withdrew its US subsidiary in June 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 35.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 45.5 billion in 2023 (up 28.6% year on year) and KRW 61.4 billion in 2024 (up 34.9%), before falling 15.1% to KRW 52.1 billion in 2025. Of the 2022 to 2025 period, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 3.29 billion in 2022 to an operating profit of KRW 2.37 billion in 2023, and then KRW 6.08 billion in 2024 (up 156.7%). The operating margin was 5.2% in 2023 and 9.9% in 2024, before turning to -16.1% in 2025 with an operating loss of KRW 8.37 billion.

Controlling-shareholder net income fell from KRW 5.91 billion in 2024 to a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 6.42 billion in 2025, and net income including non-controlling interests was also a net loss of KRW 6.42 billion.

Operating cash flow moved from negative KRW 5.28 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.67 billion in 2025. Quarterly, sales were KRW 7.57 billion in the third quarter of 2025 with an operating loss of KRW 5.81 billion.

In the fourth quarter, sales jumped to KRW 13.8 billion (up 82.7% from the prior quarter) and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.59 billion. In the first quarter of 2026, sales fell 50.7% to KRW 6.81 billion and the operating loss widened to KRW 6.82 billion.

In the second quarter, sales recovered to KRW 11.4 billion (up 66.8%) and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 2.29 billion. In the third quarter of 2026, sales reached KRW 16.9 billion (up 48.3% from the prior quarter and 122.7% from a year earlier) and operating profit was KRW 413 million.

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 369 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 232 million in the third quarter of 2026, while the third quarter of 2025, the first quarter of 2026 and the second quarter of 2026 were controlling-shareholder net losses.

The recovery appears to be sales-driven, but the first quarter of 2026 shows that losses widen quickly when sales drop.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from the downstream market centers on the expansion of AI servers and HBM supply. Heungkuk Securities said the spread of 2.5D and 3D advanced packaging is raising the importance of back-end metrology equipment.

On May 6, 2026, semiconductor and equipment stocks rallied at the open, and the market read this as an early stage of a structural upswing linked to AI server investment and HBM supply shortages. Auros Technology was cited that day as one of the stocks posting double-digit gains.

The same report also warned that volatility may rise after a sharp short-term rally, so stock-by-stock selection matters. Sector analysts treat the cycle as dependent on the pace of AI server investment and whether HBM tightness persists. In competition, Auros is positioned as a domestic player that competes with KLA.

In a 2024 report, an industry source said the company's estimated global share is low, but the metrology equipment market itself is growing. In AlphaSquare's annual 2025 ranking of 153 semiconductor and equipment stocks, the company ranked 106th by sales and 123rd by operating margin.

Because it shares the customer investment cycle with other back-end equipment peers, the direction of customer capital spending is closely tied to the sector's overall trend.

06

Outlook

The verifiable near-term agenda for the company is product diversification and customer expansion. IR material says the company is developing an IR overlay metrology tool for hybrid bonding, the HE-900ir, and is trying to enter a leading customer as a bonding overlay supplier.

Because this material is dated August 2025, progress should be checked against later disclosures and business announcements. The company acquired a stake in Coniss Co., Ltd. in January 2025 and a stake in the Singapore company ADVANCED SEMICONDUCTOR PRODUCTS PTE. LTD. in August 2025.

It moved its headquarters to Dongtan, Hwaseong, Gyeonggi Province, in March 2026. Overseas, it withdrew its US subsidiary in June 2026 as part of a footprint adjustment.

Among brokers, Heungkuk Securities initiated coverage in December 2025 with a Buy opinion, expecting results to recover from the fourth quarter of 2025 on higher investment by key customers and full-scale growth from 2026.

Since that outlook, operating losses continued in the first and second quarters of 2026 before the third quarter returned to profit. Whether this recovery path shows up in full-year 2026 results is therefore the next test.

How quickly new products convert into customer shipments and whether the customer base widens will determine how visible future earnings become.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly turnaround signal

After the first-quarter 2026 low of a KRW 6.82 billion operating loss, sales rose in the second and third quarters. The third quarter of 2026 delivered KRW 16.9 billion in sales and KRW 413 million in operating profit, a swing to profit that also applies year on year.

Controlling-shareholder net income was positive in the fourth quarter of 2025 and the third quarter of 2026. Whether this pattern holds into the next quarter is the next checkpoint.

Back-end products and customer diversification

Heungkuk Securities noted in a December 2025 report that new customers were won with back-end products such as HBM pad overlay tools and warpage tools for wafer-level packaging. Press reports of supply to Samsung Electronics and Kioxia suggest the customer base is widening beyond SK hynix. If the IR overlay tool for hybrid bonding enters customer qualification, the product mix could broaden further.

Financial capacity and shareholder return steps

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 28.7 billion, smaller than total equity of KRW 63.5 billion. A KRW 1 billion treasury share trust disclosed on November 10, 2026 lists employee compensation and share price stability as its purpose. Operating cash flow returned to a positive KRW 1.67 billion in 2025 from negative KRW 5.28 billion in 2024.

Bear factors

Cumulative losses and a short profit record

The 2025 operating loss of KRW 8.37 billion and controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 6.42 billion marked a return to annual losses. The controlling-shareholder net loss totaled KRW 8.08 billion over the last four quarters, and the first quarter of 2026 saw the operating loss widen to KRW 6.82 billion. Quarterly profit has so far appeared in only one quarter, the third quarter of 2026.

Rising leverage and volatile cash flow

The debt ratio rose from 14.1% in 2022 to 45.2% in 2025. Operating cash flow was negative KRW 5.28 billion in 2024 before turning to KRW 1.67 billion in 2025, and it was also negative in 2022. Short-term borrowings at the end of March 2026 were reported as higher than at the end of 2025.

Customer concentration and market signals

Press reports point to a high share of sales from SK hynix, which makes customer concentration a burden. In May 2026, executive director Yoo Jae-man disclosed open-market sales that reduced his holding to 10,000 shares.

On November 6, 2026, the stock was designated an investment caution issue over sell-side concentration among a few accounts.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and capex cycle

Sales are sensitive to the capital spending schedules of a small number of customers. The 50.7% quarter-on-quarter drop in the first quarter of 2026 shows how wide quarterly swings can be. A cut in key customer investment could widen the operating loss again.

Technology competition and new product timing

The company competes with KLA in front-end overlay, and the timing of customer entry for the hybrid bonding IR overlay tool has not been fixed in public material. If customer adoption of new products slips, the benefit from diversification will be delayed by the same margin. The withdrawal from the US subsidiary also means the overseas sales route needs to be reassessed.

Share price volatility and trading risk

After the November 6, 2026 investment caution designation, the Korea Exchange said it could escalate to investment warning and risk stages if sharp price swings continue. As a small-cap stock, its price can move sharply when trading concentrates in particular accounts or institutions. The series of executive share sale disclosures also feeds into how supply and demand are read.

Overall view

Auros Technology is a KOSDAQ semiconductor equipment company centered on overlay metrology tools for front-end and back-end processes.

After recording KRW 61.4 billion in sales and a KRW 6.08 billion operating profit in 2024, the company swung to a KRW 52.1 billion sales base and a KRW 8.37 billion operating loss in 2025.

In 2026, sales rose for two straight quarters after the first-quarter low, and the third quarter showed a quarterly operating profit of KRW 413 million. However, the KRW 8.08 billion controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters means whether profit carries into full-year results is not yet proven.

Strengths include the earnings turnaround, customer and product diversification, and total liabilities of KRW 28.7 billion, which are smaller than total equity of KRW 63.5 billion.

Weaknesses include reliance on a few customers, a rising debt ratio, the November 2026 investment caution designation and executive share sale disclosures. The withdrawal from the US subsidiary and competition centered on KLA are also variables for the pace of expansion.

The next checkpoints are the full-year 2026 operating margin in the annual report, fourth-quarter 2026 results and the progress of the treasury share trust.
