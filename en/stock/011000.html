02

Business structure

GeneOne Life Science is a biopharmaceutical R&D company focused on nucleic-acid-based biologics, operating a CDMO business through its U.S. Texas subsidiary VGXI that produces cGMP plasmid DNA used as raw material for gene therapies and DNA vaccines.

Its pipeline once comprised ten candidates including a MERS vaccine (GLS-5300), a Zika vaccine (GLS-5700), and a COVID-19 vaccine (GLS-5310), but eight of these are now discontinued or on long-term hold, leaving mainly an SFTS prevention vaccine (GLS-5140) and an HBV prevention vaccine (GLS-8000).

The CDMO segment has been built around VGXI since its acquisition in 2008, with the company shifting strategy toward contract plasmid DNA manufacturing as the global CDMO market expanded, led by players such as Samsung Biologics.

However, production at VGXI's Woodlands Plant 1 and Conroe Plant 2 in Texas was fully suspended throughout the first half of 2026, during which the CDMO segment generated no revenue.

The company has stated that the suspension is not a legal or administrative business stoppage but a technical, temporary hold tied to internal process efficiency work and standard operating procedure validation.

Beyond existing clients, the customer base includes a new client with whom a $16.9 million commitment letter was signed in June 2026, in preparation for a definitive contract.

Compared with CDMO peers such as Samsung Biologics or ST Pharm, the gap in production scale and revenue base is substantial, and the company describes its CDMO business as a supporting manufacturing platform for its R&D activities.

The organization operates with a workforce numbering in the dozens, consistent with a small-to-mid-sized company structure.

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated revenue fell for three consecutive years, from KRW 48.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 40.2 billion in 2023 (down 17.5% year on year), KRW 35.7 billion in 2024 (down 11.2%), and KRW 23 billion in 2025 (down 35.5%). Among these years, 2022 was the largest (KRW 48.7 billion) and 2025 the smallest (KRW 23 billion).

Operating loss widened from KRW 40.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 48.4 billion in 2023, narrowed to KRW 43 billion in 2024, and widened again to KRW 44 billion in 2025, marking four consecutive years of operating losses from 2022 through 2025.

Net loss attributable to owners followed a similar four-year loss streak, at KRW 38.1 billion in 2022, KRW 77.8 billion in 2023, KRW 48.5 billion in 2024, and KRW 51.9 billion in 2025, while net loss including non-controlling interests moved almost identically at KRW 38.2 billion, KRW 77.8 billion, KRW 48.5 billion, and KRW 51.9 billion, respectively.

The operating margin deteriorated from -82.3% in 2022 to -120.3% in 2023, -120.5% in 2024, and -191.1% in 2025, as fixed-cost burden outpaced the pace of revenue decline. The company cited a decrease in CDMO orders as the main reason for the revenue decline.

On a quarterly basis, revenue of KRW 6.81 billion in Q2 2025 fell for four consecutive quarters to KRW 5.08 billion in Q3 2025 (down 25.5% quarter on quarter), KRW 2.65 billion in Q4 2025 (down 47.8%), KRW 2.58 billion in Q1 2026 (down 2.7%), and KRW 2.06 billion in Q2 2026 (down 20.3% quarter on quarter and down 69.8% year on year).

Quarterly operating loss widened from KRW 10.2 billion in Q2 2025 to KRW 11.7 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 13.1 billion in Q4 2025, narrowed sharply to KRW 2.34 billion in Q1 2026, then widened again to KRW 12.8 billion in Q2 2026, extending the loss streak to five consecutive quarters, with the trailing four-quarter (Q3 2025 to Q2 2026) net loss attributable to owners totaling KRW 48.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The global CDMO market has grown on the back of expanding demand for biopharmaceutical contract manufacturing, with large domestic players such as Samsung Biologics continuously expanding capacity.

Production of nucleic-acid-based medicines (DNA, plasmid, mRNA) is considered a niche area with growing demand tied to gene therapy and vaccine development.

GeneOne Life Science entered this space through subsidiary VGXI, but CDMO-related production at VGXI's Woodlands Plant 1 and Conroe Plant 2 remained suspended throughout the first half of 2026, during which the CDMO segment recognized no revenue.

VGXI was in a state of complete capital impairment at the end of last year, with liabilities exceeding assets, while parent GeneOne Life Science's debt-to-equity ratio rose to 191.5% at the end of 2025.

Competitors such as Samsung Biologics and ST Pharm maintain market positions backed by large-scale facilities and stable client bases, creating a clear gap versus smaller players like GeneOne Life Science.

Since the transition of COVID-19 to an endemic phase, investment incentives for infectious disease vaccines have diminished, weakening the commercialization momentum of former flagship pipeline candidates for MERS, Zika, and COVID-19.

The company now relies on a narrowed pipeline of the SFTS vaccine GLS-5140 and the HBV vaccine GLS-8000, reflecting a reduced industry position in drug development versus its past standing.

06

Outlook

In September 2026 the company decided on a KRW 15 billion third-party share placement to Revolt Investment Partnership, issuing 6,224,066 new shares at KRW 2,410 per share, with payment due September 30, 2026 and new shares scheduled to list on October 16, 2026.

The proceeds are allocated to KRW 13 billion of working capital, including a KRW 7 billion loan to cover VGXI's plant operating costs and KRW 6.1 billion for headquarters operations, plus KRW 1.9 billion for debt repayment to Sangsangin Savings Bank.

Once the placement is completed, the largest shareholder is set to change from Shared Growth Investment Partnership No. 1 (8.49% stake) to Revolt Investment Partnership (25.51% stake).

VGXI signed an agreement with C2R in the U.S. extending the exercise deadline for the Conroe Plant 2 repurchase right from September 1 to December 15, 2026, with a remaining balance of $35.9 million out of a total repurchase amount of $39.9 million due to be settled on December 15.

The company disclosed plans to convert VGXI's Plant 1 (Woodlands) into a small-scale finished-drug production project leveraging its proprietary patented technology, targeting a total of 26 production runs within the next ten months.

Plant 2 (Conroe) has had no production output since construction, with plans to proceed through commissioning and requalification after funding before resuming operations.

On the commercial side, the company signed a $16.9 million commitment letter with a new client in June 2026 in preparation for a definitive contract, and disclosed receiving additional production quote requests from two existing clients.

However, whether these plans have translated into actual revenue recovery has not yet been confirmed through periodic reports, and since an August 2026 disclosure referenced a $170 million lawsuit against the company and VGXI, the progress of that litigation remains a variable that could affect future financial burden.

Bull factors

Balance Sheet Restructuring and New Capital Inflow

The company restructured its capital base through a 5-for-1 reverse stock split in the first half of 2026 and a KRW 15 billion third-party share placement in September.

New largest shareholder Revolt Investment Partnership secured a 25.51% stake under a one-year lock-up, forming a shareholder structure with limited near-term sell pressure. The exercise deadline for VGXI's Conroe Plant 2 repurchase right was also extended to mid-December, partially easing immediate asset-loss pressure.

Preparations to Restart CDMO Operations

VGXI passed a U.S. FDA GMP regulatory inspection in May 2026, reaffirming its technical capability in plasmid DNA production. The company disclosed plans to convert Plant 1 into a small-scale finished-drug production project targeting 26 production runs within ten months.

Commercial activity continues, including a $16.9 million commitment letter with a new client, leaving room for a base recovery in CDMO revenue should the restart materialize.

Cost Discipline Leading to Periods of Narrower Losses

Operating loss narrowed sharply to KRW 2.34 billion in Q1 2026 from KRW 13.1 billion in the prior quarter. Annual operating loss also narrowed to KRW 43 billion in 2024 from KRW 48.4 billion in 2023, showing a precedent for cost control reducing losses during restructuring periods.

However, the operating loss widened again to KRW 12.8 billion in Q2 2026, so whether this pattern persists requires further confirmation.

Bear factors

Ongoing Listing Eligibility Risk

In July 2026 a listing eligibility review trigger arose due to VGXI's production and sales suspension, and on August 7 the company was designated subject to a listing eligibility committee review. An adverse half-year review opinion added further uncertainty around continued listing.

Accumulated penalty points from a delayed disclosure on the change of largest shareholder also weigh on disclosure credibility.

Revenue Base Gap and Persistent Losses

Q2 2026 revenue fell 69.8% year on year to KRW 2.06 billion, while net loss attributable to owners in the same quarter widened year on year to KRW 18.9 billion. The trailing four-quarter (Q3 2025 to Q2 2026) net loss attributable to owners totaled KRW 48.9 billion, as the CDMO revenue gap weighs on overall performance.

Operating cash flow was also negative every year from 2022 through 2025, deepening reliance on external financing.

Financial Distress at Subsidiary VGXI

Even after recognizing an KRW 80.3 billion impairment loss on long-term loans and accrued receivables, VGXI remains in complete capital impairment with liabilities exceeding assets.

Ownership of the Conroe Plant 2 has been temporarily transferred to the creditor under a collateralized repayment-in-kind arrangement, and failure to fully settle the repurchase amount by the deadline risks loss of the asset.

A $170 million lawsuit against GeneOne Life Science and VGXI is also ongoing, making further contingent liabilities difficult to rule out.

Risk factors

Listing Eligibility and Accounting Risk

The Korea Exchange designated GeneOne Life Science subject to a listing eligibility committee review on August 7, 2026 due to the CDMO business suspension at VGXI. An adverse half-year review opinion subsequently overlapped with this listing review process.

Depending on the committee's decision, outcomes ranging from trading suspension to an improvement period or delisting remain all on the table.

Liquidity and Capital Structure Risk

At the end of 2025, the debt-to-equity ratio stood at 191.5% with total equity of only KRW 40.9 billion, significantly reducing the company's financial buffer.

The company has raised funds through a reverse stock split, multiple rights offerings, and convertible bond issuances, which can lead to dilution for existing shareholders. VGXI's complete capital impairment and the upcoming payment schedule tied to the Conroe Plant 2 repurchase right are additional liquidity burdens.

Business and Litigation Risk

Eight of the company's ten drug pipeline candidates are discontinued or on long-term hold, leaving the commercialization timeline for the remaining candidates uncertain.

The CDMO business contracted to the point of generating no revenue in the first half of 2026, and whether the restart plans will be executed has not yet been confirmed. A $170 million lawsuit against GeneOne Life Science and VGXI was disclosed in August 2026, and the outcome could create additional financial burden.

Overall view

GeneOne Life Science's revenue declined for three consecutive years from KRW 48.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 23 billion in 2025, while operating loss and net loss attributable to owners continued for four consecutive years over the same period.

In 2026, financial and listing-related uncertainties emerged simultaneously, including production suspension and complete capital impairment at subsidiary VGXI, a listing eligibility review, and an adverse half-year review opinion.

The company has pursued a series of measures to secure funding and preserve assets, including a 5-for-1 reverse stock split, a KRW 15 billion third-party share placement, and an extension of VGXI's Conroe Plant 2 repurchase right.

Plans to restart the CDMO business, including a new client commitment letter and conversion to a small-scale finished-drug production project, have also been disclosed, though whether they have translated into actual revenue remains unconfirmed.

Through Q2 2026, revenue has declined for four consecutive quarters while operating loss and net loss attributable to owners have continued for five consecutive quarters, making it premature to assess whether the trend will reverse in the near term.

The final outcome of the listing eligibility review and the normalization of VGXI's asset base remain the key variables that will determine the company's future capital structure and business continuity.
