02

Business structure

IL Robotics is a KONEX-listed company founded in 2011, formerly named IL Conus (Korean: Aiel Keonuseu). A Korea Exchange technical analysis report describes its core activities as building energy management, road infrastructure control and living-environment monitoring.

Product revenue falls into three lines: IoT control systems, smart tunnel control systems and fine-dust-measuring security lights. Greened reported that the company runs project-based work designing and installing smart-control solutions, with all hardware sourced externally.

The same report said about 70% of 2025 revenue came from the affiliate IL. The company moved its headquarters to Jeju in September 2025 and listed on KONEX in November. At an extraordinary shareholders' meeting on December 29, 2025, shareholders approved changing the name to IL Robotics.

On May 7, 2026, the company said it had signed an exclusive partnership with AgiBot for the Korean market and planned humanoid and service-robot supply, experiential showrooms, and lease and RaaS models.

According to the affiliate's side of the story, IL handles industrial humanoids while IL Robotics handles humanoid businesses outside industrial use. The similarly named Irobotics (066430) is a separate company that produces polyethylene film and should not be confused with IL Robotics.

03

Recent trends

On a filed basis, 2025 (fiscal term 15) standalone revenue was KRW 1,793 million, with an operating loss of KRW 443 million and a net loss of KRW 47 million.

The same filing shows total assets of KRW 1,879 million, total liabilities of KRW 1,777 million, total equity of KRW 102 million and paid-in capital of KRW 929 million. Revenue rose from KRW 0.15 billion in 2023 to KRW 1.22 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.79 billion in 2025.

The top line grew, but operating results worsened sharply in 2025 versus the prior year. Greened pointed out that the company borrowed about KRW 400 million from IL while repaying KRW 1.3 billion during the year, and that its debt ratio was around 1,800%.

At the annual shareholders' meeting on March 30, 2026, all agenda items passed, including approval of the fiscal term 15 financial statements, a partial amendment of the articles of incorporation, and director and auditor pay caps. First-half and third-quarter 2026 results had not been confirmed by the time of writing.

On the business side, the company signed a robotics cooperation memorandum with Geek+ on March 16, 2026, opened a dedicated humanoid rental website, and continued humanoid demonstrations at events such as COEX in September.

The closing price on 2026-10-27 was KRW 1,999 (+9.12% day on day) and market capitalization was KRW 19.9 billion, a figure far above 2025 revenue of KRW 1,793 million and equity of KRW 102 million. Trading value that day was only KRW 3,647,902.

04

Outlook

The key variable is whether the robotics business turns into actual revenue. In an April 2026 report, Greened noted that no robot-related revenue or concrete business content had been confirmed even after the name change.

By contrast, an external technical analysis report described a diversification from 2026 into sales and rental of humanoid, collaborative and quadruped robots in cooperation with AgiBot.

For the affiliate IL, Hana Securities reported that about 3,300 hours of operation from February to July 2026 improved key production indicators by 30% to 97%, and the same broker said earnings growth from the humanoid business could become substantial after the first quarter of 2027.

IL's consolidated first-half 2026 revenue was KRW 50.1 billion and operating profit was KRW 2.9 billion. IL also carried out a KRW 11 billion third-party rights offering and issued KRW 17.2 billion of convertible bonds.

These figures belong to IL, however, and no evidence has been found that they flow through to IL Robotics' revenue or funding. The legacy IoT business is influenced by policies expanding smart cities, ZEB and intelligent transport systems, along with rising demand for energy efficiency.

The affiliate IL is scheduled to take part in Robot World 2026 at KINTEX in Ilsan from November 4 to 7, so group-level robot promotion is likely to continue.

05

Bull factors

Exclusive AgiBot partnership in Korea

The company signed an exclusive partnership with AgiBot covering robot supply and business development in the Korean market. Its website presents the company as AgiBot's official Korean agent.

If a model spanning supply, showrooms, and lease and RaaS materializes, it could create a recurring revenue base distinct from one-off installation sales.

Affiliate IL's field trials and improved finances

IL posted a first-half 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.9 billion, swinging from an operating loss in the first half of 2025. Hana Securities viewed IL's large-scale field data from its own production facilities as a differentiating factor. If IL Robotics links into this trial environment, it may share part of the burden of validating products.

Sensor know-how and public-infrastructure demand

The Korea Exchange technical analysis report noted that the company designs and develops its own occupancy, motion, vehicle and environment sensors, giving it a high degree of technological self-reliance.

The same report also mentioned plans to expand into robot system integration, such as improving object-recognition accuracy through multiple sensors. Smart-city and energy-efficiency policy trends form a backdrop that could widen the reach of the existing IoT business.

06

Bear factors

No visible robot revenue or proprietary technology

In an April 2026 report, Greened said IL Robotics focuses on IoT installation and solutions and has no robotics-related technology or revenue. Robots on the IL side were also reported to be brought in from overseas makers such as AgiBot.

IL Robotics describes combining AgiBot hardware with its own software, but the software's performance and revenue contribution cannot be verified from filings.

Affiliate revenue dependence and opaque cash flows

About 70% of 2025 revenue came from the affiliate IL. IL and IL Robotics are related companies in which the same individual is the largest shareholder. A high share of related-party transactions makes it hard to judge how self-sustaining the external customer base is.

Thin equity and a swing to loss

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 102 million and total liabilities were KRW 1,777 million. An operating loss of KRW 443 million in the same year leaves little buffer.

Headcount based on the national pension data was 10 employees, a small organization, so staffing and funding for a robotics expansion remain open questions.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Shifts in interest rates and risk appetite can swing sentiment on small robotics-related stocks sharply. KONEX trading is thin, and with trading value of just KRW 3,647,902 on 2026-10-27, small orders can move the price considerably. For that reason, a single day's percentage move is a poor guide to the direction of underlying value.

Industry

The humanoid market is still testing when commercialization will arrive and which revenue models will work. Because hardware supply depends on a Chinese manufacturer, supply terms, pricing and cross-border regulation can all affect results.

Many domestic and overseas players compete in supply, services and software, so a dealer position alone may not secure differentiation.

Company

Related-party dealings, borrowing and repayment, and a change of business direction together make governance and fund management transparency a point of debate. Greened said it received no reply from the company to its inquiries.

Because news on IL (307180) and Irobotics (066430) can be mistaken for news on this company, checking the filings directly is advisable.

08

Overall view

IL Robotics is a small KONEX company running a sensor-based IoT installation business, and its 2025 filings show revenue growth together with an operating loss of KRW 443 million.

In 2026 it formalized a move into robotics through an exclusive AgiBot partnership in Korea, rental and RaaS plans, and cooperation with Geek+. Its affiliate IL reported a first-half 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.9 billion and is running field trials, but no evidence shows that this flows into IL Robotics' finances.

With about 70% of revenue from an affiliate and total equity of only KRW 102 million, the financial base for the transition is still weak. A market capitalization of KRW 19.9 billion is far above the reported revenue and equity, and trading value is very small, so caution is warranted in interpreting price formation.

Going forward, whether robot revenue appears in filings, how dependence on affiliates changes, and how capital is raised will be the key reference points. This report is for informational purposes only and does not recommend buying or selling any security.
