Third-Quarter Turn to Profit, but Four Years of Losses Still Frame CS

CS, a small-cap KOSDAQ maker of mobile telecom repeaters, saw 2025 revenue fall 36.4% and annual losses continue, but both sales and operating profit improved in the third quarter of 2026.

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

CS makes mobile telecom repeaters that amplify base-station signals for indoor spaces, basements and tunnels where radio coverage is weak. The company was selected as a supplier of 5G optical repeaters in 2019 and 5G RF repeaters in 2021, supplying its own developed products to telecom operators.

Its customers are domestic and overseas carriers, and because repeater orders follow carriers' network investment plans, results are sensitive to the capex cycle. Recent carrier investment cuts reduced output and delayed deliveries, which is cited as a factor behind the revenue decline and wider operating loss in 2025.

The company has pursued acquisitions for several years to diversify, and press reports say it secured a KRW 11 billion loan facility guaranteed by Korea Technology Finance Corporation to fund the acquisition of Mirae Information Technology.

This reads as an effort to add business areas beyond a single repeater franchise. Compared with listed telecom equipment peers, its market capitalization is small, so its earnings can swing more with industry conditions.

At the regular shareholder meeting in March 2026, a share consolidation proposal was approved, and trading of the shares was suspended from April 27, 2026 due to the electronic registration change. Because the share count changed, per-share metrics should be compared across periods with this in mind.

04

Earnings analysis

In the annual figures from DART filings, revenue went from KRW 35.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 33.7 billion in 2023, down 4.3% year on year, then rose to KRW 37.2 billion in 2024, up 10.4%, which was the largest year in 2022 to 2025.

Revenue fell to KRW 23.6 billion in 2025, down 36.4%, the smallest year in the same period. Operating loss was KRW 4.13 billion in 2022, KRW 1.58 billion in 2023, KRW 200 million in 2024 and KRW 1.18 billion in 2025, which means four consecutive years of losses from 2022 to 2025, with 2024 the smallest loss.

The operating margin improved from -11.7% in 2022 to -0.5% in 2024 but slipped back to -5.0% in 2025. Attributable net income turned to a profit of KRW 1.1 billion in 2024, which, given an operating loss that year, suggests non-operating income contributed to the profit.

In 2025 attributable net loss was KRW 1.0 billion, a return to losses, and net loss including non-controlling interests was also KRW 1.0 billion. Operating cash flow was -KRW 5.64 billion in 2022, -KRW 40.48 million in 2023, KRW 2.76 billion in 2024 and -KRW 1.91 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the fourth quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 10.3 billion, up 72.9% from the prior quarter, and an operating profit of KRW 140 million that turned the quarter profitable. The first quarter of 2026 reverted to revenue of KRW 3.31 billion, down 67.8%, and an operating loss of KRW 369 million.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 4.31 billion rose 30.2%, yet the operating loss widened to KRW 597 million. Third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 9.6 billion, up 61.3% year on year, with an operating profit of KRW 324 million that turned the quarter profitable from a year earlier.

Attributable net income in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 52.95 million, and the attributable net loss over the last four quarters totals KRW 423 million. Because quarterly revenue swings widely, quarterly results should be read alongside delivery timing and revenue recognition.

05

Industry analysis

The mobile repeater market where CS operates follows carriers' network investment cycles. According to company-related reporting, carriers cut investment recently, which reduced output and delayed deliveries, cited as a reason for weaker results in 2025.

On the other side, 5G expansion and indoor coverage demand are mentioned as potential drivers of repeater orders. Whether that demand converts into revenue depends on order backlog and delivery schedules.

The third-quarter 2026 rebound suggests some carrier orders resumed, but because revenue fell sharply in the first quarter and the loss widened in the second quarter of the same year, the durability of orders needs to be tested over more quarters.

In competitive terms, CS is small among listed telecom equipment companies, so its results can react more sharply to changes in customer investment schedules than larger peers. Within the sector, its own product lineup and the share of revenue from particular carriers are likely to drive differences in results.

Whether the telecom capex cycle is recovering or still contracting will be most visible when carriers disclose their investment plans. For CS, it is more practical to track the direction of quarterly revenue than to place the company precisely on the industry cycle.

Outlook

Three factors stand out in the outlook. First, third-quarter 2026 revenue and operating profit both improved, and whether the quarterly profit turn holds is the most important point to track.

Second, a share consolidation was approved at the regular shareholder meeting in March 2026, and trading was suspended from April 27, 2026; its effect on share count and liquidity will show in later disclosures.

Third, press reports say the company secured a KRW 11 billion loan facility to fund the Mirae Information Technology acquisition. If the acquisition is completed, the revenue mix and debt level could change materially, so completion status and terms should be checked in disclosures.

Against total liabilities of KRW 1.61 billion at the end of 2025, an executed loan would substantially change the debt structure. Whether carriers resume investment and whether new supply contracts are signed will also determine how durable the revenue rebound is.

The near-term direction is therefore best judged by whether quarterly revenue keeps rising and operating results stay in the black.

Risk factors

Quarterly earnings volatility
