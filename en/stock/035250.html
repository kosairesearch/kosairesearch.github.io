Report overview

Kangwon Land: Shareholder Returns Amid a Renovation Gap

Operating profit fell 17.9% year on year to KRW 59.7 billion in Q3 2026, and profit attributable to owners of the parent turned to a net loss. Renovations are creating a revenue gap, while share buybacks and a high payout policy are presented as the core of shareholder returns.

  2. 2

02

Business structure

Kangwon Land operates a casino open to Korean nationals along with the Grand Hotel, Mountain Condo, golf, a water park and ski operations, forming a casino and resort business.

Company-level commentary from a corporate monitoring service describes the casino as holding an exclusive position for Korean nationals, supporting a stable revenue base. In Q3 2026, general gaming floor operating revenue was KRW 294.9 billion, down 4.8% year on year.

Member gaming floor operating revenue in the same quarter was KRW 64.5 billion, up 9.6%, as visitors rose 11.1% and drop rose 5.2%. Non-casino revenue was KRW 41.7 billion, down 17.4%, which the company attributed mainly to lower hotel and condo room revenue and water park revenue during room renovations.

Casino visitors in the quarter totaled 640,390, down 3.2%. The slot machine manufacturing business, a subsidiary-level operation, posted losses for six consecutive years from 2020 to 2025, and sales of general units at its Philippine office fell from 42 in 2024 to zero in 2025.

Regulatory measures such as the sales volume cap are cited as constraints on improving profitability. Recent investment is concentrated on VIP gaming floor renovation, room renovation and construction of a second casino.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 1.27 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.39 trillion in 2023 (up 9.3%), KRW 1.43 trillion in 2024 (up 2.8%) and KRW 1.48 trillion in 2025 (up 3.5%), marking three consecutive years of increases from 2023 to 2025.

Operating profit rose to KRW 282.3 billion in 2023 (up 29.7%) and KRW 285.8 billion in 2024 (up 1.2%), then fell to KRW 235.3 billion in 2025 (down 17.7%). The operating margin went from 17.1% in 2022 to 20.3% in 2023 and 20.0% in 2024, then dropped to 15.9% in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent grew from KRW 115.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 340.9 billion in 2023 (up 194.8%) and KRW 456.9 billion in 2024 (up 34.0%), then fell to KRW 318.2 billion in 2025 (down 30.4%).

The 2023 gain in parent-attributable profit far outpaced the growth in operating profit, which suggests non-operating items played a large role; the breakdown can be checked in the annual report's income statement. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 487 billion in 2024 to KRW 294.5 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio stood at 22.2% at the end of 2025, the highest level in the 2022 to 2025 range, with total equity of KRW 3.96 trillion and total liabilities of KRW 880.7 billion.

Quarterly, operating profit was KRW 72.7 billion in Q3 2025 and fell 59.0% to KRW 29.8 billion in Q4 2025, when personnel costs including retirement payments and other operating costs rose more than expected.

Q1 2026 operating profit was KRW 68.9 billion, up 131.0% from the prior quarter but down 7.2% year on year, as operating costs rose 6.2%. Q2 2026 operating profit was KRW 43.8 billion, down 36.4% from the prior quarter.

Q3 2026 operating profit recovered to KRW 59.7 billion, up 36.2% from the prior quarter, but a fair-value loss on financial assets tied to rising bond yields was recorded in non-operating items, and profit attributable to owners of the parent turned to a net loss of KRW 2.05 billion.

Over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026), profit attributable to owners of the parent totaled KRW 215.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In the casino market open to Korean nationals, Kangwon Land holds an exclusive position. However, regulatory measures such as the sales volume cap act as constraints, and commentary points to limits on improving profitability.

Meanwhile, analysis has suggested that from 2026 the exclusion of foreign-visitor casinos from the sales volume calculation could widen the volume headroom available to Kangwon Land.

On the other hand, expansion of the foreign-visitor integrated resort in Incheon, the Osaka integrated resort (IR) targeted for 2030, and Thailand's move to legalize casinos are cited as possible sources of intensifying regional competition.

On demand, visitors in Q2 2026 were about 560,000, down 3%, and Kiwoom Securities noted that weakness in entries and drop continued through July.

Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 43.2 billion fell short of the market expectation of KRW 47.6 billion, and brokerage tallies show Q3 operating profit came in 6% below the FnGuide consensus. Combined hotel and condo revenue in Q2 2026 was KRW 20.3 billion, down 15%.

Kiwoom Securities pointed out that, given the high share of fixed costs in the business structure, profit erosion was larger than the decline in revenue. Casino visitors in Q3 2026 fell 3.2%, with only member gaming floor visitors rising 11.1%.

06

Outlook

The 2026 budget approved by the board in December 2025 set revenue at KRW 1.51 trillion, costs at KRW 1.29 trillion and investment at KRW 147.4 billion. Cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 was KRW 1.09 trillion, down 1.6%, and cumulative operating profit was KRW 172.4 billion, down 16.1%.

The 2026 investment budget is KRW 145.4 billion, of which KRW 110.1 billion had been spent through Q3. Major projects are VIP gaming floor renovation, room renovation and construction of a second casino. Condo renovation is scheduled for completion in Q4 2027 and hotel renovation in Q1 2028.

The second casino will expand from 4,390 pyeong to 6,129 pyeong and add 50 general tables and 250 machines, and Samsung Securities expects revenue and profit to improve together with the opening of the second casino and normalization of hotel operations in 2028.

The company plans to buy back KRW 20 billion of its own shares within 2026, with details to be disclosed right after a board decision. Its shareholder return policy calls for a payout ratio of at least 50% and a total shareholder return ratio of 60%.

Hana Securities analyzed shareholder return capacity on the assumption that 7.4% of treasury shares held will be cancelled by August 2027. Samsung Securities expects growth to slow inevitably in 2026 and 2027, with full-scale growth appearing after 2028.

Bull factors

Shareholder return policy and buyback plan

The company has set a payout ratio of at least 50% and a total shareholder return ratio target of 60%. A KRW 20 billion buyback is planned within 2026, and Hana Securities expects 7.4% of treasury shares to be cancelled by August 2027. Samsung Securities said the payout ratio is in line with the global casino industry average.

Expected capacity expansion after renovations

Samsung Securities expects revenue and profit to improve together as the second casino opens and hotel operations normalize in 2028. The second casino will expand from 4,390 pyeong to 6,129 pyeong, with 50 general tables and 250 machines added. In Q1 2026, VIP revenue was strong and results beat Kiwoom Securities' consensus.

Volume headroom and possible legal recovery

Analysis suggests that from 2026, excluding foreign-visitor casinos from the sales volume calculation could widen the volume headroom available to Kangwon Land. Member gaming floor visitors rose 11.1% in Q3 2026 and drop rose 5.2%.

In October 2025, Kiwoom Securities projected a refund of about KRW 188.7 billion if the company wins the mining area development fund lawsuit.

Bear factors

Revenue gap from room renovations

Renovation of 757 rooms began in March 2026, sharply reducing room supply. According to Hana Securities, combined hotel and condo revenue in Q2 2026 was KRW 20.3 billion, down 15%, and room count fell 41%. Samsung Securities expects growth to slow inevitably in 2026 and 2027.

Q3 non-operating valuation loss and cost pressure

Q3 2026 operating profit fell 17.9% to KRW 59.7 billion, and a KRW 55.9 billion valuation loss on financial assets from rising bond yields was recorded in non-operating items. Profit attributable to owners of the parent turned to a net loss of KRW 2.05 billion.

In Q1 2026, levies including the mining area development fund rose to KRW 95.1 billion, up KRW 4.4 billion, and depreciation rose to KRW 22.2 billion.

Regulation, competition and weak diversification

Regulatory measures such as the sales volume cap are seen as constraining profit improvement. The Incheon foreign-visitor integrated resort expansion, the Osaka IR targeted for 2030 and Thailand's move to legalize casinos are cited as competitive pressures.

The slot machine business has posted six consecutive years of losses totaling KRW 14.8 billion, and general unit sales at the Philippine office fell from 42 in 2024 to zero in 2025.

10

Risk factors

Visitor and drop trends

Casino visitors in Q3 2026 totaled 640,390, down 3.2%, and at general gaming floors visitors and drop fell 3.4% and 6.5%. Kiwoom Securities noted that weakness in entries and drop continued through July 2026.

Growth in member gaming floors partly offsets this, but whether general customer traffic recovers remains to be seen.

Cost structure and fixed costs

NH Investment & Securities identified the difficulty of cutting personnel costs at a public corporation as a factor weighing on near-term margins. In Q4 2025, personnel costs including retirement payments and other operating costs rose more than expected, cutting operating profit by 59.0% from the prior quarter. The high share of fixed costs means revenue declines can translate into larger profit erosion.

Quarterly volatility from financial income

In Q3 2026, a valuation loss on financial assets of KRW 55.9 billion from rising bond yields was recorded in non-operating items. The company said that although principal and interest are guaranteed if the bonds are held to maturity, fair-value accounting pushes valuation gains and losses into quarterly net profit.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent rose 177.0% to KRW 109.9 billion in Q2 2026, showing that quarterly net profit is heavily influenced by financial income.

12

Overall view

Kangwon Land is a company built around its exclusive casino business for Korean nationals and a shareholder return policy. Revenue rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, but operating profit fell 17.7% to KRW 235.3 billion in 2025 and profit attributable to owners of the parent fell 30.4%.

Cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 was KRW 1.09 trillion, down 1.6%, and with room renovations under way, brokers have pointed to the possibility of earnings declines in 2026 and 2027. The price-to-book ratio is below 1x, and total equity of KRW 3.96 trillion suggests a sound financial base.

Positive factors include the 60% total shareholder return target, the share buyback and expectations for the second casino opening in 2028. Negative factors include the revenue gap from renovations, the Q3 valuation loss on financial assets and regulatory constraints such as the sales volume cap.

The next checkpoints are the buyback decision disclosure within 2026, the 2026 full-year preliminary results in early 2027 and the 2026 annual report around late March 2027, while renovation completions are scheduled for Q4 2027 and Q1 2028.
