02

Business structure

Valofe is a global game company centered on game publishing and platform operations. Its business rests on three pillars: publishing external games, developing titles on its own IP, and operating platforms aimed at overseas users.

The flagship IP Last Origin is described by the company as having a loyal user base of roughly 500,000 in Korea and abroad. Its follow-up, Last Origin 2, was announced as entering development on May 8, 2026, and expands the setting from Earth to space.

Its self-developed AVA RE:BIRTH is a PC online FPS built on the Alliance of Valiant Arms IP licensed from Neowiz, with an extraction shooter mode added.

Full service was scheduled for October 28, 2026 across major overseas markets including Japan, Thailand, Brazil, North America and Europe, through the Pmang and VFUN platforms. Overseas operations are led by the Japanese subsidiary G.O.P, which has more than 18 years of experience running A.V.A in Japan.

In China, the Shanghai subsidiary Miracle Technology, founded in 2010, runs a development team of about 100 people covering licensing approvals, localization and live service.

A relaunch project for Master of Fantasy, a 2D side-scrolling MMORPG that ended service in 2010, is also under way, including a move of its in-house engine to Unreal Engine 5.8.

Among Korean game companies, Valofe is compared with Netmarble, Com2us and Neowiz, and its shift toward a larger share of its own IP is presented as the main point of difference.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART filing basis, annual sales rose from KRW 30.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 36 billion in 2023 (up 17.4% year on year), eased to KRW 35.6 billion in 2024 (down 1.1%), and reached KRW 37.8 billion in 2025 (up 6.3%), the largest of the four years.

Operating profit fell from KRW 3.33 billion in 2022 to KRW 229 million in 2023 (down 93.1% year on year), turned to an operating loss of KRW 195 million in 2024, and returned to KRW 2.88 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 10.8% in 2022 to 0.6% in 2023, -0.5% in 2024 and 7.6% in 2025.

In 2023 and 2024, sales stayed around KRW 35 billion to KRW 36 billion, yet the operating margin stayed below 1%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 1.58 billion in 2022 to KRW 909 million in 2023 (down 42.4%), then KRW 1.71 billion in 2024 (up 88.5%) and KRW 1.81 billion in 2025 (up 5.6%), rising for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests followed the same path, at KRW 1.58 billion, KRW 910 million, KRW 1.71 billion and KRW 1.81 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 1.52 billion in 2022, KRW 5.93 billion in 2023, KRW 1.11 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.86 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio, calculated as total liabilities divided by total equity, rose from 27.5% in 2022 to 50.1% in 2023, 49.3% in 2024 and 74.1% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 32.4 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 24 billion.

Quarterly sales climbed from KRW 8.94 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 11.4 billion in Q4 2025 (up 27.7% from the prior quarter), then ran at KRW 10.8 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 12.4 billion in Q2 2026, before falling to KRW 10.2 billion in Q3 2026 (down 17.6% from the prior quarter).

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 1.82 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 975 million in Q1 2026 (down 46.4% from the prior quarter), KRW 1.74 billion in Q2 2026 (up 78.6%), and KRW 45.41 million in Q3 2026 (down 97.4% from the prior quarter and down 95.7% year on year).

Q3 2026 sales rose 14.5% year on year, while operating profit shrank sharply. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a net loss of KRW 354 million in Q3 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 3.2 billion.

Why a net loss arose when almost no operating profit remained is a point to check against the detailed items in the filing.

05

Industry analysis

Game publishing depends on overseas platforms and localization capability to drive results. Valofe runs platforms and local teams across several regions, including Japan, China and North America.

Japan has deep demand for subculture titles and PC online FPS games, and the company cites its own figures of about 3 million cumulative A.V.A users and cumulative sales above KRW 100 billion (JPY 10 billion) there. China is a market where approval of game service licenses is the key hurdle.

In a July 2026 report, the company said it expected approvals for Bless Unleashed and Black Squad during the third quarter. The company said AVA RE:BIRTH, which adds an extraction shooter mode, was designed to remain playable on lower-end PCs.

The Last Origin 2 development team includes staff with experience developing and running Korean subculture titles such as Granblue-style projects, Soulworker and Starseed.

On the industry cycle, a report describes Valofe as moving from a model built around external publishing and maintenance toward in-house development and an IP-centered model.

Compared with Netmarble, Com2us and Neowiz, which also combine their own IP with publishing, the timing of when new in-house titles contribute to earnings is the largest gap. The outcome of China approvals and new title launches should be read alongside the broader performance of the Korean game sector.

Outlook

The nearest business milestone the company has disclosed is the full service of AVA RE:BIRTH, which was scheduled to begin on October 28, 2026. The company said pre-registrations passed 200,000, and it plans to add an equipment cost limit system and clan features through follow-up updates after full service.

Last Origin 2 was announced as entering development on May 8, 2026, and a July 2026 report described veteran development staff joining the team and a deck-building combat system that is being newly introduced. A report says detailed content and service schedules will be disclosed in stages.

Reports also say the company is discussing investment and service strategy with major Japanese platform operators. In its first-quarter 2026 earnings release, the company said new IP-based titles would follow in the second half of 2026, beginning with Critica, then Last Origin 2 and Soulworker.

A mobile title, Chaos W, was reported in May 2026 to have passed 1 million pre-registrations. On shareholder returns, the company signed a KRW 1 billion treasury share purchase trust with NH Investment & Securities on May 6, 2026, and on June 29, 2026 it decided to cancel all 371,692 treasury shares acquired.

In China, the outcome of approvals for Bless Unleashed and Black Squad is the next point to confirm. How quickly new titles feed into reported results remains the variable that will shape the 2026 and 2027 earnings direction.

Bear factors

Sharp Drop in Quarterly Profit

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 45.41 million, down 97.4% from the prior quarter and down 95.7% year on year. Sales were KRW 10.2 billion, up 14.5% year on year, yet the operating margin stayed below 1%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 354 million, breaking a run of profitable quarters.

Rising Debt and Capital Structure

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 49.3% in 2024 to 74.1% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 24 billion and total equity of KRW 32.4 billion at year-end 2025. Because all equity is attributable to controlling shareholders, there is no non-controlling buffer. The reason for the higher liabilities needs to be checked in the detailed filing items.

Uncertainty Over New-Title Results

The launch results of AVA RE:BIRTH and the service schedule of Last Origin 2 are difficult to gauge from disclosed material. The China approvals for Bless Unleashed and Black Squad, which the company expected in the third quarter, also need to be confirmed. If new titles do not translate into profit as expected, quarterly earnings may keep swinging.

Risk factors

Trading and Regulation

The Korea Exchange designated the stock as an investment-caution issue for November 18, 2026. The reason was a 15.75% decline over three days alongside high selling involvement from a small number of accounts. Further measures depending on the market warning stage were noted as a possibility.

Earnings Volatility

The gap between Q4 2025 operating profit of KRW 1.82 billion and Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 45.41 million opened within three quarters. Even with sales holding near KRW 10 billion to KRW 12 billion, profit can shift sharply with cost structure or one-off items. Given the size of quarterly swings, the trend is better read on an annual basis.

IP, License and Regional Dependence

AVA RE:BIRTH relies on the A.V.A IP license held by Neowiz, so license terms affect the business. Last Origin 2 is still in development with no launch date disclosed. Overseas service depends on specific platforms and regions, so delays in China approvals could slow expansion.
