Report overview

Non-bank growth; asset quality recovery is the key test

BNK Financial Group's net profit attributable to controlling shareholders has risen for two straight years since the 2023 low, but its 2026 quarterly earnings are moving with one-off costs and provisions, and its asset quality indicators remain somewhat weaker than industry peers.

  1. 1

    Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 815 billion in 2025, up 11.9% year on year and the largest year in the 2022-2025 span.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

BNK Financial Group is a regional financial holding company built around its Busan-based and Kyongnam-based banking subsidiaries, with capital, securities, savings bank, asset management and venture capital units added on the non-bank side.

For January-September 2026, bank-segment net profit was KRW 557.3 billion, down KRW 113.1 billion year on year, with the Busan bank accounting for a KRW 99.1 billion decline and the Kyongnam bank for KRW 14.0 billion (preliminary, announced October 26, 2026).

Non-bank net profit over the same period rose KRW 47.7 billion to KRW 213.7 billion, partly offsetting the bank decline and enlarging the non-bank share of group earnings.

Cumulative net profit by unit was KRW 7.8 billion for the capital unit, KRW 20.8 billion for the securities unit, KRW 4.4 billion for the savings bank, KRW 13.7 billion for asset management and KRW 0.8 billion for venture capital.

In the first half of 2026 the company said all five non-bank units named in its disclosure improved year on year. NH Investment & Securities described the group in May 2026 as having a relatively strong regional base among local financial holding companies and a comparatively well-balanced non-bank portfolio.

A domestic brokerage noted in February 2026 that household lending was expected to center on credit loans to professionals, while corporate lending would center on large companies in the Busan-Ulsan-Kyongnam region.

Because the bank franchise is regional, local economic conditions and asset quality of regional corporate loans feed directly into group earnings.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual operating profit fell from KRW 1.16 trillion in 2022 to KRW 801.2 billion in 2023 (down 30.7% year on year), then rose to KRW 875.9 billion in 2024 (up 9.3%) and KRW 902.7 billion in 2025 (up 3.1%), increasing for two consecutive years. 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest in the 2022-2025 span.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 785 billion in 2022, KRW 639.8 billion in 2023 (down 18.5%), KRW 728.5 billion in 2024 (up 13.9%) and KRW 815 billion in 2025 (up 11.9%), rising for two consecutive years from the 2023 low, with 2025 the largest year in the span.

In 2023 the decline in operating profit (30.7%) was steeper than the decline in net profit attributable to controlling shareholders (18.5%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 833.2 billion in 2022, KRW 678.9 billion in 2023, KRW 750 billion in 2024 and KRW 836.2 billion in 2025, a different measure from the figure attributable to controlling shareholders.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 3.61 trillion in 2022 to -KRW 5.8 billion in 2023, -KRW 1.34 trillion in 2024 and -KRW 2.02 trillion in 2025, and has been negative since 2023.

At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 11.2 trillion, of which KRW 10.8 trillion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 149.9 trillion, with the debt-to-equity ratio rising from 1,169.1% in 2022 to 1,333.0% in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit fell from KRW 380.4 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 60.6 billion in Q4 2025 (down 84.1% from the prior quarter); a domestic brokerage in January 2026 noted large one-off costs in the fourth quarter, and another in February 2026 cited year-end voluntary retirement and a wider scope for ordinary wages as one-off factors in selling and administrative expenses.

Q1 2026 operating profit was KRW 283.1 billion (up 367.3% from the prior quarter) and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 211.4 billion (up 369.7%), which Kyobo Securities attributed to solid interest income, higher fee income from a strong stock market, and lower provisions.

Q2 2026 operating profit was KRW 235.7 billion (down 16.8%) and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 200.4 billion, with the company citing a KRW 44 billion settlement loss on the external-stake purchase in the Yeouido core office fund as an accounting one-off.

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 239.5 billion rose 1.6% from the prior quarter but fell 37.0% year on year, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 210.5 billion fell 28.5%.

Over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 667.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In the competition among regional financial holding companies, BNK Financial Group's strengths are cited as its regional base and non-bank portfolio (NH Investment & Securities, May 2026 report).

The same report said its NPL coverage ratio in the 80% range is the lowest among financial holding companies and called for faster provisioning. The company itself said its Q3 2026 asset quality indicators are somewhat weaker than industry peers.

A domestic brokerage in July 2026 argued that a high share of secured loans limits the likelihood of a sharp rise in final loss rates. Kyobo Securities in May 2026 attributed higher fee income to a strong stock market.

Kiwoom Securities in May 2026 noted plans to increase dividends in light of separate taxation of dividend income, alongside share buybacks and cancellations. The company said it will pursue the profitability tasks of its Value-Up 2.0 plan announced in September 2026.

Outlook

In its July 28, 2026 disclosure, the company said the roughly 3.49 million shares bought back in the first half (about KRW 60 billion) would be fully cancelled in the third quarter.

Samsung Securities in a July 29, 2026 report judged that an additional KRW 50 billion in buybacks could allow a 45% shareholder return ratio for 2026. A domestic brokerage in a July 1, 2026 report projected the 2026 total shareholder return ratio would rise to around 45.0%.

Another domestic brokerage in a July 10, 2026 report forecast 2026 full-year net profit attributable to controlling shareholders at KRW 813.1 billion, similar to 2025. The ratio had fallen in Q2 2026 from the prior quarter, which the company attributed to asset growth centered on high-quality assets and its shareholder return policy. The company said it will carry out the profitability tasks of its Value-Up 2.0 plan announced in September 2026. Full-year confirmed results and asset quality figures will be confirmed in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Wider non-bank earnings base

Cumulative January-September 2026 non-bank net profit was KRW 213.7 billion, up KRW 47.7 billion year on year (preliminary, announced October 26, 2026).

The company said all five non-bank units named in its first-half disclosure improved, including capital, securities, savings bank, asset management and venture capital. The non-bank side offsetting part of the bank decline is cited as the main support for the bullish case.

Expanding shareholder returns

On October 26, 2026 the board approved a second-half buyback and cancellation of about KRW 60 billion along with a quarterly cash dividend. BNK Investment & Securities, in a July 1, 2026 report, said the 2025 total shareholder return ratio was 40.5% and projected it would rise to around 45.0% in 2026.

Samsung Securities, in a July 29, 2026 report, judged that an additional KRW 50 billion in buybacks could allow a 45% return ratio for 2026.

Lower provisions and steadier core income

Kyobo Securities, in a May 2026 report, attributed the Q1 2026 earnings increase to solid interest income, higher fee income and lower provision charges. Samsung Securities analyzed that core income, combining interest and fee income, rose 0.5% from the prior quarter in Q2 2026. The company said adjusted operating profit rose and provision charges fell in the first half of 2026.

09

Bear factors

Asset quality weaker than peers

The substandard-and-below loan ratio was 1.45% at Q3 2026 and the delinquency ratio was 1.40%, the latter up 0.06 percentage points from Q2. The company said both indicators are somewhat weaker than industry peers and need continued management.

NH Investment & Securities, in a May 2026 report, said the NPL coverage ratio in the 80% range is the lowest among financial holding companies and called for provisioning closer to full coverage.

One-off swings in quarterly earnings

Operating profit in Q4 2025 was KRW 60.6 billion, down 84.1% from the prior quarter. In Q2 2026 a KRW 44 billion settlement loss on the external-stake purchase in the Yeouido core office fund cut first-half net profit by 13.5% year on year.

Q3 2026 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 210.5 billion fell 28.5% year on year, with the high base from last year's one-off gains still weighing on the comparison.

Negative operating cash flow and a high debt ratio

Operating cash flow went from KRW 3.61 trillion in 2022 to -KRW 5.8 billion in 2023, -KRW 1.34 trillion in 2024 and -KRW 2.02 trillion in 2025, staying negative since 2023. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 1,169.1% in 2022 to 1,333.0% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 149.9 trillion at year-end 2025.

Given the nature of bank balance sheets, these figures do not directly signal deteriorating asset quality, but they remain a reference point for capital capacity.

Risk factors

Credit losses and provision swings

A domestic brokerage in a July 2026 report argued that the high share of secured loans limits the scale of a sharp rise in final loss rates, while NPL coverage is likely to stay low for some time. The same report expected provisions to rise. The delinquency ratio rose to 1.40% at Q3 2026 from the prior quarter, so the trend remains to be tracked.

Swings in non-interest income and securities gains

Kiwoom Securities, in a May 2026 report, noted weak selling and administrative expenses and securities-related gains in Q1 2026. The company said a decline in non-interest income was a factor in the lower cumulative net profit for January-September 2026. Accounting one-offs such as the KRW 44 billion settlement loss in Q2 2026 widen quarterly swings.

Balance between capital ratios and shareholder returns

The common equity Tier 1 ratio was 12.14% in Q2 2026, down 0.16 percentage points from Q1, and rose to 12.18% in Q3, up 0.04 percentage points. The company cited asset growth centered on high-quality assets and its ongoing shareholder return policy as factors behind the Q2 decline.

With second-half buybacks and quarterly dividends continuing, the balance between capital ratios and return scale is a point to monitor.

Overall view

BNK Financial Group's reported results show net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rising for two straight years after the 2023 low, with 2025's KRW 815 billion the largest year in the 2022-2025 span.

In 2026 quarterly operating profit has recovered from the Q4 2025 low, with Q1 at KRW 283.1 billion and Q3 at KRW 239.5 billion, but Q3 operating profit still fell 37.0% year on year. Non-bank net profit rose to KRW 213.7 billion for January-September 2026, partly offsetting the bank decline.

Asset quality shows a substandard-and-below loan ratio of 1.45% and a delinquency ratio of 1.40% at Q3 2026, which the company said are somewhat weaker than peers, and Samsung Securities judged that a valuation recovery requires stronger recurring earnings and key indicator improvement.

Shareholder returns continued through the second-half buyback and cancellation of about KRW 60 billion and quarterly cash dividends, and brokerages projected a 2026 total shareholder return ratio near 45%.

On the other side, the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 1,333.0% at year-end 2025 from 1,169.1% in 2022, and operating cash flow has been negative since 2023. The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, due by the end of March 2027, for confirmed full-year results and asset quality figures.
