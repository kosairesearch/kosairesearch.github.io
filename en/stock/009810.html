Q3 operating profit turns positive; new-business results are the test

MDSsphere (formerly Playgram) reported an operating profit in Q3 2026, but net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued for five straight quarters, so the durability of earnings and the revenue contribution of new businesses are the next checkpoints.

02

Business structure

MDSsphere was founded in 1974 and listed on the KOSPI in 1988, and it has mainly operated in MRO purchasing agency services and video content.

At an extraordinary shareholders' meeting on July 31, 2026, the company changed its name from Playgram to MDSsphere and added embedded system manufacturing and sales, IT system and solution consulting, and information and communication construction to its business purposes.

In the first three quarters of 2026, cumulative solution sales were KRW 136,152 million, or 58.91% of revenue, while MRO sales were KRW 48,702 million and parent-company video content sales were KRW 12,528 million, according to a summary of the company's filing.

The core of solution sales is the listed subsidiary (ticker 086960), which is benefiting from wider physical AI demand through higher sales of NVIDIA Jetson and DGX product lines. After a share consolidation in April 2026, the parent disclosed on May 8, 2026 that it holds a 25.28% stake in that subsidiary.

In September 2026 the company acquired the engineering division of MDS Intelligence to enter embedded software. In security, it has partnered with Kuntech to supply Kuntech's software supply-chain security solution AEZIZ v3.0 to defense, aerospace and automotive markets in Korea. The company is shifting its identity from MRO and video content toward IT solutions.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 131.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 214.4 billion in 2023 (up 63.3% year on year) and KRW 268.3 billion in 2024 (up 25.1%), then fell to KRW 250.9 billion in 2025 (down 6.5%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 was the smallest.

Operating losses were KRW 6.86 billion in 2022, KRW 2.59 billion in 2023, KRW 5.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.4 billion in 2025, which makes four consecutive years of operating losses from 2022 through 2025.

The operating margin improved from -5.2% in 2022 to -1.2% in 2023, then slipped to -1.9% in 2024 and -1.8% in 2025. Even in 2024, the year of largest revenue, the margin did not reach breakeven, so top-line growth did not translate directly into better profitability.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 1.31 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 37.6 billion in 2023, a swing into loss, followed by net losses of KRW 1.32 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.46 billion in 2025, which makes three consecutive years of losses since 2023.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 3.94 billion in 2022, KRW 43.2 billion in 2023, KRW 2.31 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.98 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow was -KRW 10.5 billion in 2022, KRW 26.5 billion in 2023, -KRW 383 million in 2024 and KRW 11.6 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 65.4% in 2022 to 59.1% in 2025. On a quarterly basis, the operating loss of KRW 194 million in Q3 2025 widened to KRW 2.71 billion in Q4 2025, then turned to an operating profit of KRW 1.67 billion in Q1 2026.

Operating profit was KRW 2.54 billion in Q2 2026 (up 52.1% from the prior quarter) and KRW 2.46 billion in Q3 2026 (down 3.2% from the prior quarter).

Revenue bottomed at KRW 65.7 billion in Q1 2026 (down 0.4% from the prior quarter) and then rose two straight quarters to KRW 81.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 84.1 billion in Q3.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, however, widened from KRW 546 million in Q2 2026 to KRW 3.79 billion in Q3 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 11 billion. The company's Q3 2026 release attributed the larger net loss to non-operating items such as equity-method investment losses.

05

Industry analysis

The main source of MDSsphere's recent earnings improvement is demand for physical AI hardware. In its Q3 2026 results the company attributed the swing to operating profit to higher sales of NVIDIA product lines at its listed subsidiary (ticker 086960).

As the physical AI market expands, demand for Jetson and DGX product lines has grown, and the semiconductor and automotive embedded solutions business has also expanded.

Industry observers, however, have said that because hardware demand drove the improvement, the revenue contribution of software verification and security businesses will determine further growth.

Defense, aerospace and automotive are sectors where software complexity is rising and supply-chain security risks must be managed from the development stage, which is why the company is pushing its security business with Kuntech.

In cycle terms, the company posted losses from 2022 through 2025, returned to operating profit in 2026, and remains early in its business restructuring.

Because solution sales account for more than half of revenue, sales are linked to demand for specific product lines, which could translate into earnings volatility if that demand slows. The company's existing MRO and video content businesses are being reorganized alongside the new businesses.

06

Outlook

The company said it plans to start software functional-safety and security-verification projects for defense, aerospace and automotive clients from Q4 2026.

A November 2026 report said that through its partnership with Kuntech, the company will supply Kuntech's AEZIZ v3.0 to customers in those sectors in Korea and support clients in building software security management systems.

In September 2026 the company acquired the engineering division of MDS Intelligence, and after its July name change it added new business purposes.

On the funding side, its board approved a third-party allotment rights issue of about KRW 8.5 billion on July 28, 2026, and the company disclosed that all proceeds will be used for business-transfer funding.

Including a KRW 1 billion small public offering in the same month, the company raised KRW 9.5 billion in total in July. Consolidated cash and cash equivalents were about KRW 61.7 billion at the end of March 2026.

The value-up plan decided on October 22, 2026 set out segment profit management and operating efficiency, lower financing costs through debt repayment, and a recovery in net income as its tasks. Because it is the first disclosure of such a plan, the performance review item was marked as not applicable.

A company official said the company will expand market adoption of its new security business to strengthen its growth base. The next checkpoints are whether security-verification projects starting in Q4 are recognized as revenue, and whether financing costs fall after debt repayment.

08

Bull factors

Operating profit turned positive

Operating profit was KRW 1.67 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 2.54 billion in Q2 and KRW 2.46 billion in Q3, so operating profit has been positive for three straight quarters. Q3 revenue rose 34.5% year on year to KRW 84.1 billion. If that holds, it would mark a clear change from the operating losses recorded from 2022 through 2025.

Revenue growth tied to physical AI demand

The company said higher sales of NVIDIA Jetson and DGX product lines at its listed subsidiary drove the improvement. It also announced that cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 rose 24.9% year on year. As long as demand for physical AI hardware continues, the basis for revenue growth remains intact.

Security business and a value-up plan

The company has a supply-chain security partnership with Kuntech and plans to start functional-safety and security-verification projects in defense, aerospace and automotive from Q4. The value-up plan decided on October 22, 2026 includes reducing financing costs through debt repayment and recovering net income. If the new business shows up in revenue, it would support diversification of the business structure.

Bear factors

Continued and widening losses for controlling shareholders

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued for five straight quarters from Q3 2025 through Q3 2026. The Q3 2026 loss widened to KRW 3.79 billion from KRW 546 million in Q2.

Over the last four quarters the total is KRW 11 billion, so operating profit has not yet turned into net profit for controlling shareholders.

Non-operating items and financing burden

The company's Q3 2026 release cited non-operating items such as equity-method investment losses as the reason for the larger net loss. Total liabilities were KRW 107.4 billion at the end of 2025, and the debt ratio was 59.1%.

The company has said it will reduce financing costs by repaying borrowings, which implies that financing costs remain a drag on profit.

Volatile operating cash flow and dilution risk

Operating cash flow was -KRW 10.5 billion in 2022, KRW 26.5 billion in 2023, -KRW 383 million in 2024 and KRW 11.6 billion in 2025, changing direction each year. The company decided on a roughly KRW 8.5 billion third-party rights issue in July 2026 and raised KRW 9.5 billion that month in total. Continued fundraising would add dilution as the share count grows.

10

Risk factors

Concentration and demand swings

Solution sales made up 58.91% of cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026, and at their center are sales of NVIDIA product lines at the listed subsidiary. A slowdown in physical AI hardware demand could affect both revenue and operating profit. Reliance on a specific product line and a subsidiary adds to earnings volatility.

Execution risk in new businesses

After the July 2026 name change, the company added embedded systems, IT solution consulting and information and communication construction to its business purposes. The September engineering-division acquisition and the Kuntech partnership have not yet shown a confirmed revenue contribution. If the security-verification business does not start as planned, the restructuring benefit could be limited.

Funding and capital structure

Consolidated cash and cash equivalents were about KRW 61.7 billion at the end of March 2026. Total liabilities were KRW 107.4 billion at the end of 2025, and controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 69.7 billion.

Rights-issue proceeds are earmarked entirely for business-transfer funding, so results from acquisitions must be weighed against the financial burden.

12

Overall view

MDSsphere disclosed a Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.46 billion on revenue of KRW 84.1 billion, and has posted operating profit for three straight quarters since Q1 2026. The improvement centers on higher sales of NVIDIA product lines at the listed subsidiary and is linked to physical AI hardware demand.

On the other side, net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued for five straight quarters since Q3 2025 and widened to KRW 3.79 billion in Q3 2026. The company's release attributed this to non-operating items such as equity-method investment losses.

On an annual basis, operating losses were recorded in each year from 2022 through 2025, and the debt ratio was 59.1% at the end of 2025. In July 2026 the company changed its name, added new business purposes, and said it would begin security-verification projects for defense, aerospace and automotive clients from Q4.

Strengths include sustained operating profit, physical AI demand and the new-business plan, while weaknesses include continued net losses, non-operating items and financing costs, and repeated fundraising.

The next checkpoints are whether new-business revenue materializes, the full-year net income attributable to controlling shareholders in the 2026 annual report, and how financing costs change after debt repayment.
