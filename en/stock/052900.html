AI Data Center Demand Lifts Earnings; Capacity Is the Test

02

Business structure

KX Hitech produces plastic trays that carry products through semiconductor manufacturing and external cases for SSDs, and is listed on KOSDAQ. The company divides its business into a plastic division and a metal division.

The plastic division, centered on IC trays and module trays, supplies companies such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix and forms a stable revenue base, according to one Korean brokerage.

The core of the metal division is SSD cases, and demand is shifting from consumer SSDs toward enterprise eSSDs with higher average selling prices, according to the same brokerage. That brokerage estimated that eSSD cases carry an average selling price about 2.5 times that of consumer SSD cases.

Standalone revenue in Q1 2026 was about KRW 22.0 billion, of which eSSD revenue was KRW 11.73 billion. Customers include Samsung Electronics, SK hynix, Micron and major OSAT companies, and the company's ties to all three large memory makers are cited as a strength.

Production bases are in Korea, China, the Philippines and Vietnam, and the Vietnam plant in Bac Ninh has run around the clock since March 2026. The company localized front-end materials for semiconductors and brought SSD front-end processes in-house, which it says secures cost structure and production stability.

Key products include IC-Tray, Module-Tray, Carrier Tape, SSD Case and Turnkey Box. In high-capacity eSSDs, heat control has emerged as a key challenge, and the same brokerage said companies with that technology are benefiting. The largest shareholder, KX Innovation, holds a stake of about 35%.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue went from KRW 156.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 154.7 billion in 2023 (down 1.1% year on year), KRW 132.3 billion in 2024 (down 14.5%) and KRW 126.3 billion in 2025 (down 4.5%), and fell for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Revenue was largest in 2022 and smallest in 2025 within 2022–2025. Operating profit fell from KRW 12.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.12 billion in 2023 (down 33.8%), recovered to KRW 9.21 billion in 2024 (up 13.4%), and dropped to KRW 5.36 billion in 2025 (down 41.8%).

The company attributed the 2025 decline to weaker results at a subsidiary, which lowered consolidated profitability. Operating margin moved from 7.8% in 2022 to 5.3% in 2023, 7.0% in 2024 and 4.2% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 14.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.42 billion in 2023 (down 69.4%), rose to KRW 9.75 billion in 2024 (up 120.5%), and was KRW 4.56 billion in 2025 (down 53.2%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 4.29 billion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder number. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 13.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 20.8 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 64.5% in 2022 to 48.4% in 2025, and the company said conversions of convertible bonds into shares reduced debt and increased share capital. Quarterly, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 28.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 2.5 billion.

Q4 2025 revenue rose to KRW 37.9 billion (up 33.8% from the previous quarter), but operating profit was KRW 1.26 billion (down 49.5%).

Q1 2026 operating profit jumped to KRW 7.49 billion (up 493.2% from the previous quarter), which one brokerage attributed to higher eSSD case revenue and a subsidiary returning to profit. Q2 2026 operating profit was KRW 7.27 billion (down 2.9%).

In Q3 2026, revenue reached KRW 52.4 billion (up 29.5% from the previous quarter) and operating profit KRW 9.69 billion (up 33.2%). Revenue has risen for two consecutive quarters from Q2 to Q3 2026.

Q3 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.14 billion (up 36.5% year on year), a smaller increase than operating profit. Over the last four quarters, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 14.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Rising AI server and data center investment has sharply increased demand for enterprise SSDs, and higher-capacity SSDs also raise orders for external cases. In a May 2026 report, one Korean brokerage analyzed that the eSSD case market has entered a shortage phase in which supply cannot keep up with demand.

The same report said data center investment is leading the current semiconductor cycle and that eSSD demand is rising sharply alongside HBM. A November 2026 news report said demand for SSD cases remains strong alongside a NAND flash supply shortage.

The brokerage's reading of the cycle is that the market has entered a structural growth phase, but that view depends on AI investment continuing. On competition, KX Hitech counts all three major memory makers as customers, which one brokerage said demonstrates quality and mass-production capability.

On inputs, higher crude oil prices and tight naphtha supply have pushed up plastic raw material costs, and the company says long-term pricing arrangements with customers will limit the impact.

Heat control becoming a central challenge for eSSDs raises technical requirements, which the brokerage sees as favorable for suppliers with the relevant technology. The growth engine is also shifting from consumer to enterprise products.

06

Outlook

The company is expanding production capacity for SSD cases, IC trays and module trays at its Vietnam plant, and says it will build the new lines quickly and stabilize them. The KRW 11.0 billion convertible bond issued in August 2026 accompanies this expansion.

In Q1 2026, the core eSSD process was reportedly running close to full production capacity, and the Vietnam eSSD plant's utilization was reported at nearly 100%. Until the expansion is complete, some processes are handled through subcontractors.

In a June 2026 report, one Korean brokerage projected 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 142.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 20.74 billion, up 12.9% and 287.1% respectively. That estimate came before the Q3 2026 results.

Cumulative operating profit for January–September 2026 of KRW 24.5 billion, as announced by the company, already exceeds that full-year estimate.

Another Korean brokerage, in a May 2026 report, estimated 2026 revenue of KRW 141.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 24.1 billion, and said operating profit could reach the mid-KRW 30 billion range in 2027 if the expansion is completed within the year.

The first brokerage added that 2027 operating profit could exceed KRW 30 billion. Entry into module tray supply for a U.S. memory customer was also mentioned as a possibility in the second half of 2026.

These estimates could change with the pace of expansion and customer order flow, and delays in the expansion would limit the ability to meet orders.

08

Bull factors

eSSD Demand and Higher Selling Prices

Growing AI data center deployments are lifting demand for enterprise eSSD cases, and a higher average selling price than consumer products is changing the revenue mix. One Korean brokerage estimated that eSSD cases carry about 2.5 times the selling price of consumer cases.

Holding all three large memory makers as customers is cited as evidence of quality and mass-production capability that supports winning orders as demand expands.

Rapid Quarterly Profit Recovery

Operating profit recovered from KRW 1.26 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 7.49 billion in Q1 2026, and reached KRW 9.69 billion in Q3 2026, up 287.4% year on year. Revenue has risen for two consecutive quarters from Q2 to Q3 2026, reaching KRW 52.4 billion.

According to the company, cumulative operating profit for January–September 2026 was KRW 24.5 billion, well above the 2025 full-year operating profit of KRW 5.36 billion.

Capacity Expansion and High Utilization

The Vietnam eSSD plant is reportedly running at nearly 100% utilization, and the company is investing to expand production capacity. It issued KRW 11.0 billion in convertible bonds in August 2026. Brokerages have framed completion of the expansion as the key condition for the company to meet additional orders.

09

Bear factors

Concentration in Customers and Demand

Revenue depends on investment and order flow from memory makers such as Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and Micron. If memory market conditions or AI server investment slow, orders could fall quickly. Annual revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and operating margin fell to 4.2% in 2025.

Raw Material Costs and Margins

Plastic raw material prices are rising on higher crude oil prices and tight naphtha supply. The company says long-term pricing policies will limit the impact, but how quickly price increases pass through still needs confirmation. Operating margin was 4.2% in 2025, below 7.8% in 2022, so cost pressure could feed directly into margins.

Dilution and Attribution of Profit to Shareholders

Conversion rights exercised in March 2026 led to the issue of 4,106,776 new shares, equal to 6.28% of total shares outstanding. A further KRW 11.0 billion in convertible bonds was issued in August 2026.

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 9.69 billion, while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.14 billion, so the rise in operating profit did not translate proportionally to shareholder earnings.

10

Risk factors

Demand Cycle

If the AI server and data center investment cycle slows, or memory makers adjust the pace of eSSD investment, order volatility could increase. Much of the brokerages' analysis depends on AI investment continuing. Customer mix and revenue concentration by customer are points to check in the annual report.

Expansion and Raw Materials

If the Vietnam expansion is not completed on schedule or stabilizes slowly, the company's ability to meet eSSD orders would be constrained. If higher naphtha-linked raw material prices cannot be passed through to selling prices, margins could narrow.

The company is using subcontractors for some processes, and a larger outsourced share could weigh on the cost structure.

Capital Structure and Ownership

Conversion of convertible bonds led to new shares in March 2026, and another KRW 11.0 billion in convertible bonds was issued in August 2026. With KX Innovation holding about 35%, the free float and supply-demand balance remain variables.

Because weaker subsidiary results reduced consolidated profitability in 2025, the effect of group subsidiaries on consolidated results also needs monitoring.

12

Overall view

KX Hitech's disclosed results show revenue and operating profit declining from a 2022 peak through 2025, followed by a rapid quarterly recovery in 2026. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 52.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 9.69 billion were described as record results in the company's announcement and in press coverage.

The driver of the recovery is demand for eSSD cases used in AI data centers, and the company is expanding capacity through the Vietnam plant and convertible bond issuance.

On the other side, annual revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, raw material costs are rising, customer concentration in memory makers remains, and dilution from convertible bond conversions is ongoing.

Brokerage estimates put 2026 operating profit at KRW 20.74 billion and KRW 24.1 billion, but cumulative operating profit of KRW 24.5 billion for January–September 2026, as announced by the company, already exceeds those estimates, so whether they are revised is the next point to watch.

The share price multiple is low relative to the recent profit recovery, but quarterly profit has been volatile, so interpretation depends on whether profit can be sustained.

The next checkpoints are the Vietnam expansion completion at end-2026, Q4 2026 preliminary results in early 2027, and the 2026 annual report at end-March 2027.
