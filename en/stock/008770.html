Report overview

Duty-free losses narrow as hotel growth lifts earnings

After ending duty-free operations in the DF1 zone at Incheon International Airport, Hotel Shilla posted operating profit in each quarter from the first quarter to the third quarter of 2026, but revenue has fallen for two consecutive quarters, so the recovery so far is in profit rather than sales.

  1. 1

    Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 reached KRW 136.7 billion, well above the KRW 13.5 billion operating profit for full-year 2025.

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Hotel Shilla runs two core segments, the duty-free (TR) segment and the hotel and leisure segment. According to NH Investment & Securities, the company operates 11 duty-free stores in Korea and overseas, along with hotels in Seoul and Jeju and the Shilla Stay brand.

The duty-free business includes city stores and airport stores, and the end of duty-free operations in the DF1 zone at Incheon International Airport removed a unit that had been loss-making under a heavy rent burden.

Duty-free sales have long depended on shuttle traders (daigong), and individual tourists are taking a growing share. Kyobo Securities noted that individual tourists spend less per visit, which can raise rent as a share of sales.

TR segment revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 760.3 billion, down 10.5% year on year. Over the same period the hotel and leisure segment generated KRW 198.7 billion in revenue and KRW 27.9 billion in operating profit, and Shilla Stay revenue grew 19%, the fastest rate within the segment.

Kyobo Securities said average daily room rates in the third quarter of 2026 grew in the low-double-digit range in Seoul, mid-double-digit in Shilla Stay, and low-double-digit in Jeju.

On competition, Hotel Shilla did not take part in the Incheon Airport re-tender, and industry observers expect the operators that won space there to earn profits at those airport stores (Asiatime, February 2026).

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue hit its low in 2023, falling 27.5% year over year from KRW 4.922 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.5685 trillion. It then rose for two consecutive years, from 2024 to 2025, to KRW 3.9476 trillion in 2024 (up 10.6% year over year) and KRW 4.0683 trillion in 2025 (up 3.1% year over year).

Among 2022 to 2025, the year with the highest revenue is 2022 and the year with the lowest is 2023. Operating profit was KRW 78.3 billion in 2022 and KRW 91.2 billion in 2023 (up 16.4% year over year), but it swung to an operating loss of KRW 5.18 billion in 2024.

In 2025, it returned to profit with operating profit of KRW 13.5 billion. The operating margin was 1.6% in 2022 and 2.6% in 2023, fell to -0.1% in 2024, and was 0.3% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned from a net loss of KRW 50.2 billion in 2022 to a net profit of KRW 86.0 billion in 2023. However, it swung back to a net loss of KRW 61.5 billion in 2024, and the loss widened to KRW 172.8 billion in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests followed the same pattern. Asia Times expected that one-off costs, such as interior-related costs from the withdrawal from the Incheon Airport duty-free stores and inventory clearance, likely affected 2025 results (reported in February 2026).

On a quarterly basis, the loss narrowed from an operating profit of KRW 11.4 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 149.2 billion in Q3 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 4.12 billion and a net loss of KRW 16.6 billion in Q4.

This was followed by operating profit of KRW 20.4 billion in Q1 2026 (a return to profit), KRW 61.1 billion in Q2, and KRW 55.2 billion in Q3. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.0 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 21.4 billion in Q2, and KRW 38.4 billion in Q3.

The sum of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the most recent four quarters is KRW 49.2 billion. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 55.2 billion came in slightly below the market consensus of about KRW 57.7 billion (reported by Infostock Daily).

The debt-to-equity ratio at the end of 2025 was 220.1%, up from 197.0% in 2024. Total equity at the end of 2025 is KRW 1.1062 trillion, and total liabilities are KRW 2.4352 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

The duty-free industry is sensitive to shuttle trader demand and to the won-dollar exchange rate. In its October 1, 2026 report, Korea Investment & Securities expected third-quarter duty-free industry sales to be similar to or slightly below the second quarter.

Expectations for the resumption of visa-free group travel from China had not translated into a recovery in duty-free sales, according to the same analysis. Because duty-free goods are largely bought in dollars, a stronger won eases cost pressure, while a weaker won raises it.

Analysts attributed the third-quarter cost-ratio increase to higher discount rates in the off-season combined with a stronger won. Kyobo Securities said discount rates at city stores are being managed at stable levels, with no sign of excessive discount competition.

NH Investment & Securities estimated that daigong discount rates fell by about 2 percentage points in the first quarter of 2026. Foreign visitor spending is spreading from duty-free stores to department stores, so the benefit from inbound tourism is not concentrated in duty-free companies.

In hotels, rising foreign arrivals and higher room rates are reinforcing each other, and foreign visitors in July and August 2026 rose 22.1% year on year according to Korea Investment & Securities.

In duty-free competition, Hotel Shilla's exit from the Incheon Airport re-tender created a revenue gap that the hotel and leisure segment must help fill.

06

Outlook

The company said it will pursue operating efficiency in the TR segment with a focus on improving profitability, and will concentrate the hotel and leisure segment on growing foreign guest demand and year-end demand (company remarks reported on October 28, 2026).

Brokerage forecasts for full-year 2026 operating profit have varied by date. NH Investment & Securities presented KRW 164.9 billion on April 27, 2026 and KRW 170.1 billion on July 3, 2026.

Kyobo Securities presented 2026 operating profit of KRW 181 billion and revenue of KRW 3.93 trillion, down 3.4%, on October 12, 2026, and projected 2027 operating profit of KRW 213.5 billion.

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 136.7 billion, so full-year figures will depend on fourth-quarter results. Kyobo Securities expects that if exchange-rate volatility eases in the fourth quarter, cost pressure will ease and duty-free profitability can recover.

The key question is whether growth in hotel room rates can partly offset the revenue lost from the airport exit. NH Investment & Securities said that a clearer medium-term revenue growth strategy would speed up the recovery in corporate value (July 3, 2026). For the hotel business, the sustainability of the rise in foreign arrivals is the point to watch.

08

Bull factors

Hotel and leisure room-rate growth

The hotel and leisure segment generated third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 198.7 billion, up 12.8% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 27.9 billion, up 28.0%. Kyobo Securities analyzed that average daily room rates in Seoul, Shilla Stay and Jeju all grew in the double digits in the third quarter.

Shilla Stay revenue rose 19%, the fastest growth in the segment. Rising foreign visitor numbers are supporting this trend.

Effect of the duty-free restructuring

With the end of duty-free operations in the DF1 zone at Incheon International Airport, a unit that had been loss-making under a heavy rent burden dropped out of the results. Hotel Shilla attributed the main driver of third-quarter 2026 operating profit growth to this effect.

NH Investment & Securities analyzed that city-store profitability is improving as daigong discount rates fall and individual tourists gain share. The return to duty-free operating profit in the first quarter of 2026 is consistent with this trend.

Consecutive quarterly profit recovery

Operating profit went from KRW 20.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026, a return to profit, to KRW 61.1 billion in the second quarter and KRW 55.2 billion in the third. Controlling-shareholder net income rose from KRW 6 billion in the first quarter to KRW 21.4 billion in the second and KRW 38.4 billion in the third.

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 of KRW 136.7 billion is well above the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 13.5 billion. Compared with the operating loss of KRW 4.12 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, earnings capacity has recovered quickly.

Bear factors

Duty-free demand and cost-ratio pressure

Analysts pointed to a stronger won combined with higher off-season discount rates as raising duty-free cost ratios. Third-quarter operating profit of KRW 55.2 billion came in slightly below a consensus of about KRW 57.7 billion.

If duty-free industry sales stay flat or keep declining from second-quarter levels, the profit recovery could slow.

Shrinking revenue and the airport gap

Hotel Shilla's exit from the Incheon Airport re-tender reduced airport duty-free sales, and third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 959 billion fell 6.5% year on year. Revenue has declined for two consecutive quarters, in the second and third quarters of 2026.

Kyobo Securities projects full-year 2026 revenue of KRW 3.93 trillion, down 3.4%. Some observers have questioned whether hotel growth is large enough to fill the duty-free revenue gap.

Financial strain and a thin earnings base

The 2025 controlling-shareholder net loss widened to KRW 172.8 billion, and the debt ratio rose to 220.1% from 197.0% in 2024. Controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 49.2 billion, a modest base.

Kyobo Securities analyzed that a rising share of individual tourists could raise rent as a share of sales. NH Investment & Securities said concerns about medium-term revenue growth have slowed the recovery in corporate value (July 3, 2026).

Risk factors

Exchange rates and duty-free cost ratio

Sharp swings in the won-dollar rate move duty-free purchase costs and, combined with discount policy, can destabilize profitability. In the third quarter of 2026, a stronger won and higher off-season discount rates combined to raise the cost ratio. A reversal in the exchange rate could also create cost pressure. Whether the exchange rate stabilizes in the fourth quarter is the next point to watch.

Tourism policy and demand dispersion

Expectations for resumed visa-free group travel from China did not translate into duty-free sales recovery, according to analysts. If foreign visitor spending keeps spreading from duty-free stores to department stores, duty-free sales could fall short of expectations. A delay in policy changes or a longer shift in demand could push back the timing of a duty-free recovery.

Financial structure and earnings volatility

Given the 2025 net loss of KRW 172.8 billion and a debt ratio of 220.1%, the financial burden is sensitive to earnings swings. At the end of 2025, total equity stood at KRW 1.11 trillion against total liabilities of KRW 2.44 trillion.

Even with profits in consecutive quarters, the pace of earnings recovery will determine how fast the balance sheet improves. Quarter-to-quarter swings in operating profit have been sizable as well.

12

Overall view

After ending duty-free operations in the DF1 zone at Incheon International Airport, Hotel Shilla posted operating profit in each quarter of 2026 from January to September, reaching a cumulative KRW 136.7 billion, well above the KRW 13.5 billion full-year 2025 figure.

Third-quarter operating profit rose 382.4% year on year, but revenue fell 6.5%, so a sales recovery has not yet appeared. The hotel and leisure segment grew both revenue and operating profit, supported by room-rate growth and foreign visitor demand.

In duty-free, a stronger won and discount pressure raised the cost ratio, and third-quarter operating profit came in slightly below consensus. The 2025 controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 172.8 billion and the 220.1% debt ratio show that financial strain persists.

Brokers including Kyobo Securities and Korea Investment & Securities have presented target prices, but their duty-free assumptions differ. The next points to check are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report.
