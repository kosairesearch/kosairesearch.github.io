02

Business structure

APS was founded in 1994 to make semiconductor equipment and software, spun off its equipment business through a physical split in 2017, and adopted the current name in 2023.

Its structure spans a holding segment, software, skin-care medical devices, and semiconductor components and equipment, with the holding segment containing both a holding unit and a new-materials unit.

In the first three quarters of 2025, stable dividend income from holdings and growth in the new-materials unit lifted sales, while the software and skin-care device units widened operating losses and semiconductor equipment remained weak.

The subsidiary UPI was reported as acquired for KRW 18 billion on July 31, 2026, and the board approved absorbing it on October 26, 2026. Major shareholder Jeong-gi-ro and five others held 35.60% as of March 3, 2026, and treasury shares stood at 6.62%.

Customers appear centered on display and semiconductor manufacturing demand, consistent with the KRW 19.8 billion FMM mass-production plant investment disclosed in February 2020. Segment revenue mix should be checked in the annual report's segment disclosure.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales were KRW 40.8 billion in 2022 and KRW 41 billion in 2023 (up 0.6% year on year), jumped to KRW 94 billion in 2024 (up 128.9%), and fell to KRW 84.9 billion in 2025 (down 9.7%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating losses widened from KRW 15.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 18.6 billion in 2023 and KRW 22.6 billion in 2024, then narrowed to KRW 6.85 billion in 2025, and the company has recorded operating losses in each year from 2022 through 2025.

Operating margins were -37.4%, -45.3%, -24.1% and -8.1% across 2022 to 2025, so the 2025 loss rate was the smallest of the four years.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 12.8 billion in 2023 (up 38.7%), turned into a net loss of KRW 1.32 billion in 2024, and widened to a net loss of KRW 3.49 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 8.68 billion in 2025, larger than the controlling-shareholder loss, which suggests the remainder was booked to non-controlling interests.

A 2025 disclosure reported that pre-tax income from continuing operations turned positive, while the company attributed the improvement to lower cost of sales and selling and administrative expenses after a business restructuring.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 19.3 billion in 3Q 2025 to KRW 26.3 billion in 4Q 2025 (up 35.9% quarter on quarter), then fell to KRW 20.9 billion in 1Q 2026 (down 20.6%) and KRW 17.9 billion in 2Q 2026 (down 14.3%), before recovering to KRW 25 billion in 3Q 2026 (up 39.8%).

Quarterly operating losses were KRW 1.02 billion in 3Q 2025 and KRW 299 million in 4Q 2025, widened to KRW 733 million in 1Q 2026 and KRW 3.95 billion in 2Q 2026, and narrowed to KRW 1.59 billion in 3Q 2026.

The KRW 9.82 billion profit attributable to controlling shareholders in 1Q 2026 ran against an operating loss in the same quarter, so non-operating or one-off items likely had a large effect. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters (4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026) totaled KRW 4.06 billion.

05

Industry analysis

APS's signature technology is fine metal mask (FMM) for OLED display production. A 2022 research note highlighted that 1,000 PPI resolution was becoming visible in fine metal masks. FMM is a component used in OLED panel deposition, so it tracks display panel investment cycles.

On the semiconductor side, weak equipment demand was cited as a factor limiting profitability in the first nine months of 2025.

The upstream chip cycle is strong: Samsung Electronics reported consolidated 3Q 2026 preliminary sales of KRW 195 trillion and operating profit of KRW 107.4 trillion, with high-bandwidth memory and server DRAM sales driving profitability.

APS's semiconductor equipment revenue is mixed into consolidated sales, so whether the chip upcycle has translated into APS equipment orders must be checked in the segment information of the annual report.

The new-materials unit is reported within a broader segment, which makes direct comparison with competitors difficult.

APS is classified under finance, but its operations are closer to equipment and materials manufacturing plus holding-company dividends, so comparing it with financial holding companies such as KB Financial Group on valuation or earnings structure is hard. Display FMM technology and the upstream outlook for chip equipment are the two axes that drive results.

Outlook

On October 26, 2026, the board approved absorbing wholly owned subsidiary UPI, with the merger agreement dated October 27, 2026 and the shareholder record date set for November 10, 2026. The merger ratio is 1 to 1 under a no-issuance structure, because APS holds 100% of UPI shares and no new shares will be issued.

The merger was recorded as a small-scale merger. Having decided on the absorption soon after acquiring UPI, the move reads as a restructuring of intra-group assets. APS started a KRW 1 billion treasury-share acquisition trust with Samsung Securities running from November 3, 2026.

The filing also states there is no change in treasury shares after the merger. Sales showed a recovery through 3Q 2026, but operating losses have persisted for five straight quarters, so the timing of a return to operating breakeven has not yet appeared in reported results.

Broadening external customers for the new-materials unit remains an open task. 4Q 2026 preliminary results are typically released in early 2027. The 2026 annual report is due by the end of March 2027, when full-year operating margin and net income attributable to controlling shareholders will be confirmed.

Holding dividends and FMM technology base

Stable dividend income from the holding segment was cited as a factor supporting 3Q 2025 cumulative sales. A 2022 research note pointed to visible progress in 1,000 PPI resolution in fine metal masks as a technology base. The UPI absorption and the treasury-share trust can be read as governance and shareholder-return adjustments.

Two straight quarters of controlling-shareholder net losses

Whether the KRW 9.82 billion profit in 1Q 2026 was one-off, and whether the losses are structural, needs to be checked in the annual report.

Negative operating cash flow and debt burden

Operating cash flow was negative in each year from 2022 to 2025, at -KRW 12.9 billion, -KRW 12.1 billion, -KRW 11.3 billion and -KRW 11.7 billion. Total liabilities were KRW 158.7 billion at end-2025.

Risk factors

The four-quarter total of KRW 4.06 billion is thin, so one non-recurring item could shift the annual result. The nature of non-operating items must be checked in the annual report notes.

Subsidiary restructuring and acquisition cost

UPI was acquired at a cost of KRW 18 billion and then slated for absorption, so the effect on the post-merger balance sheet will become visible only once the process is complete. Because it is a small-scale merger, the change in the surviving company's financial statements may be limited. Accounting treatment of the merger should be confirmed through disclosures.

Concentration in end markets

FMM and semiconductor equipment are the core of results, so earnings can swing sharply with display investment cycles and chip equipment orders. Weak semiconductor equipment demand limited profitability in the first nine months of 2025.

The shareholder structure is also concentrated, with the largest shareholder group holding 35.60% as of March 2026.
