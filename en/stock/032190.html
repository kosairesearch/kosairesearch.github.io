02

Business structure

Daou Data was founded in 1992, and its parent-level business rests on two pillars: distribution of enterprise software, and VAN and PG payment services. Software distribution accounts for most of the distributor revenue, while hardware distribution for servers, networks and storage contributes little.

The company distributes software from about 30 companies. Its customer base has moved from VMware-centered work toward premium solutions such as Citrix and Nutanix. The company has named AI, security and networks as its three core growth areas and is reorganizing its portfolio around them.

Through CXK, a domestic independent operating company set up with Cloud Software Group, it runs Citrix sales and technical support in Korea with local staff. In security it has assembled a line-up including Sophos and Kaspersky and plans to build its own security operations center.

Its Digital Zone online marketplace is expanding beyond software into personal hardware. In the payment business, VAN and PG are being combined as a foundation for service innovation. On a consolidated basis, financial subsidiaries carry a large share of revenue, so parent-level performance should be read separately. Peers include Ahnlab in security and Samsung SDS and LG CNS in IT services and infrastructure operations.

04

Earnings analysis

On the consolidated figures from the periodic reports, revenue rose from KRW 9.75 trillion in 2022 to KRW 10.3 trillion in 2023, up 6.0% year on year, then to KRW 12.1 trillion in 2024, up 17.4%, and KRW 18.1 trillion in 2025, up 48.7%.

Revenue has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 is the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating profit went from KRW 776.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 667.9 billion in 2023, down 14.0%, and then rose for two consecutive years to KRW 1.21 trillion in 2024 and KRW 1.6 trillion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 8.0% in 2022 to 6.5% in 2023, 9.9% in 2024 and 8.9% in 2025, with 2024 the highest.

The company attributed the 2025 improvement to a favorable market environment in which income from financial subsidiaries within the consolidated group increased.

Revenue grew far faster than operating profit, which suggests that the expansion in top-line figures largely reflects the size of the consolidated financial subsidiaries.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 203.7 billion in 2022, KRW 63.6 billion in 2023 (down 68.8%), KRW 164.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 218.8 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.2 trillion in 2025, so the gap between the two measures reflects a large non-controlling share and consolidated profit should not be read as profit for controlling shareholders.

Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 831.6 billion in the second quarter of 2026 was the largest of the last five quarters, and the third quarter of 2026 fell 46.3% from the prior quarter to KRW 446.9 billion.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 309.1 billion.

Operating cash flow moved from negative KRW 1.94 trillion in 2022 to negative KRW 6.53 trillion in 2025, a wider deficit, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 984.5% in 2025 from 792.6% in 2024.

05

Industry analysis

Daou Data's results span two industry cycles. One is the financial business, which accounts for a large share of consolidated results and moves with trading volumes and investment conditions in the stock market. The profit decline in 2023 and the increase in 2025 show this pattern.

The other is the parent's enterprise IT distribution business, where license and solution revenue depends on corporate infrastructure investment.

The company names AI infrastructure, security and networks as its growth areas, and on the AI side it handles Nutanix's integrated enterprise AI operations platform and Rescale's HPC-based engineering AI platform.

In security it is moving toward bundled managed detection and response services alongside integrated security products.

In enterprise IT distribution, relationships with supplier headquarters are central, so a domestic independent operating structure that reduces dependence on overseas headquarters is presented as a competitive advantage.

In competition, security solutions compare with domestic listed security companies such as Ahnlab, while IT infrastructure operations and system integration compare with listed firms such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS. Their revenue structures differ, so direct comparison has limits. The most direct gauge of where the cycle stands is stock-market trading volume.

Outlook

The company's direction is to widen its portfolio into next-generation IT infrastructure such as AI and cloud, while strengthening license and consulting capabilities for each customer.

According to the 2026 semi-annual report, parent-level revenue in the first half rose 51% year on year and operating profit rose 37%, and the company attributed this to its restructuring of core businesses and stronger sales and technical support.

Against the 2027 targets in the medium-to-long-term value-up plan announced in December 2024, the 2025 progress report disclosed in April 2026 put revenue and operating profit at 80% or more of target levels. The next checkpoint is whether the planned security operations center translates into security service revenue.

Whether the Citrix domestic operating business centered on CXK widens its customer base is also worth tracking. Because consolidated results depend on financial subsidiaries, growth indicators for the parent and consolidated figures should be followed separately.

Annual preliminary results for fiscal 2026 are likely around late February 2027, and the 2025 annual preliminary results were disclosed on February 25, 2026. The annual report for fiscal 2026 will be the next periodic filing. The company has maintained a policy of keeping parent-level dividend payout at 50% or more.

08

Bull factors

Three years of revenue growth and operating profit recovery

Consolidated revenue rose for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025 and reached KRW 18.1 trillion in 2025. Operating profit rose for two consecutive years after the 2023 low, and 2025 came in at KRW 1.6 trillion. The operating margin was highest in 2024 at 9.9% across 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit of KRW 831.6 billion in the second quarter of 2026 was the largest of the last five quarters on the disclosed record.

Parent-level restructuring and first-half growth

The company has named AI, security and networks as its core areas and is pursuing Citrix domestic operations through CXK, AI infrastructure work based on Nutanix, and a security operations center.

According to the 2026 semi-annual report, parent-level revenue in the first half rose 51% and operating profit rose 37% year on year. The shift of the customer base toward premium solutions is also cited as a bullish argument.

Shareholder return policy and progress on medium-term targets

A policy of keeping parent-level dividend payout at 50% or more has been presented, and the payout ratio for the latest fiscal year was 84.44%. The 2025 progress report said revenue and operating profit reached 80% or more of the 2027 targets. Annual cash dividends maintained each year are also a bullish factor on the return side.

09

Bear factors

Earnings volatility tied to the financial market cycle

Consolidated growth was attributed mainly to higher income from financial subsidiaries as the stock market rallied. In 2023, operating profit fell 14.0% and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 68.8%, showing how sharp the impact can be when the cycle turns.

In the third quarter of 2026, operating profit of KRW 446.9 billion fell 46.3% from the prior quarter, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 40.6% year on year.

The gap in the controlling shareholders' share

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.2 trillion in 2025, while profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 218.8 billion, a wide gap. Of total equity of KRW 7.71 trillion, equity attributable to controlling shareholders is KRW 1.61 trillion.

The share that actually belongs to shareholders is smaller than consolidated profit or consolidated equity, which is the bearish argument that consolidated growth cannot be read directly as shareholder value growth.

Burden from the balance sheet

Total liabilities were KRW 75.9 trillion, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 984.5% in 2025 from 792.6% in 2024. Operating cash flow moved from negative KRW 1.94 trillion in 2022 to negative KRW 6.53 trillion in 2025, a wider deficit.

Because large movements in financial subsidiaries' assets and liabilities are reflected in the statements, reading these balance sheet indicators requires care, which is a bearish factor.

Risk factors

Financial market cycle

Because financial subsidiaries' income moves with trading volumes and the investment climate, consolidated operating profit can fall quickly when markets cool. Operating profit fell 14.0% in 2023, and in the third quarter of 2026 it fell 46.3% from the prior quarter. Even with a larger revenue base, profit remains highly exposed to market swings.

Ownership structure and shareholder attribution

The controlling shareholders' share of consolidated profit is small, leaving a large portion to non-controlling interests. In the third quarter of 2026, profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 38.8 billion, down 40.6% year on year.

The last four quarters totaled KRW 309.1 billion for controlling shareholders, which is a useful benchmark for the scale of attributable profit.

Execution of the parent-level business shift

Reorganizing around AI, security and networks requires several projects at once, including CXK, the security operations center and an organizational restructuring. If partnership terms or supply agreements with overseas headquarters change, the effect of the domestic independent operating structure could also shift.

Whether these plans translate into revenue and profitability is a point to confirm in upcoming periodic reports.

12

Overall view

Daou Data's consolidated revenue and operating profit both rose from the 2023 low through 2025, and operating profit in the second quarter of 2026 was the largest of the last five quarters.

However, much of consolidated profit comes from financial subsidiaries and can fall sharply with the market environment, as it did in 2023.

Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 rose 1.1% year on year, but profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 40.6%, showing a clear gap between consolidated profit and shareholder-attributable profit.

Controlling shareholders hold only part of total equity, the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 984.5%, and the operating cash flow deficit widened. On the parent side, the reorganization around AI, security and networks and Citrix operations through CXK showed growth in the first half on a parent-only basis.

The next checkpoints are the annual preliminary results around February 2027 and the fiscal 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027, where parent-level growth and consolidated indicators can be separated.
