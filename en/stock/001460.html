BYC: Leasing and Development Gain Weight Beside Apparel

Apparel revenue has declined for three consecutive years, while leasing and property development now generate most of operating profit.

02

Business structure

BYC is a domestic intimate-apparel and underwear maker founded in 1955, with knit goods and sewn products as its core lines under brands such as BYC, Bodyheat and Bodydry. In the FY2024 business report, knit goods accounted for 35.35% and sewn products for 34.57% of textile segment sales, together about 70%.

Manufacturing is anchored in production facilities at PT.BYC INDONESIA. Property has become the second pillar.

According to press reports, leasing sales were KRW 45.1 billion, or 28%, of 2025 consolidated revenue of KRW 163.2 billion, and leasing generated KRW 18.2 billion, or 68%, of the KRW 26.6 billion operating profit. Investment property at fair value was reported at KRW 1.278 trillion.

The company secured land in major commercial districts, built buildings with direct-operated stores on lower floors, and leased or sold the remaining space. From 2008 to 2016 it completed four mixed-use and urban-type residential buildings under its in-house residential brand.

The GD Metro Tower project in Daerim-dong, Yeongdeungpo-gu, connects to Guro Digital Complex Station on Subway Line 2 and targets G-Valley workers as its core local demand.

Against peers, BYC is comparable to fashion distributors and export-oriented apparel makers only in part, since it combines manufacturing, direct retail and leasing within one group.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 169.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 168.4 billion in 2023 (down 0.8% year on year), KRW 165.2 billion in 2024 (down 1.9%) and KRW 163.2 billion in 2025 (down 1.2%).

Revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 25.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 27.5 billion in 2023 (up 8.6%), fell to KRW 23.8 billion in 2024 (down 13.3%) and recovered to KRW 26.6 billion in 2025 (up 11.5%).

In 2023 operating profit rose while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 32.9% to KRW 23.7 billion, so the gap between the two lines points to non-operating items and income tax, which should be checked together.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 35.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 18.4 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 19.6 billion in 2025 (up 6.4%). Operating margin was 14.9% in 2022, 16.3% in 2023, 14.4% in 2024 and 16.3% in 2025.

Operating cash flow grew from KRW 10.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 34.2 billion in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 26.8% in 2024 to 37.9% at end-2025, with total liabilities of KRW 213.3 billion against total equity of KRW 562.5 billion.

On a quarterly basis, Q4 2025 was the peak, with revenue of KRW 49.2 billion (up 25.9% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 11.3 billion.

Revenue then fell to KRW 36.3 billion in Q1 2026 and operating profit to KRW 5.59 billion (down 50.7% quarter on quarter), and Q2 2026 operating profit dropped to KRW 2.99 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 37.3 billion, down 4.7% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 4.97 billion, up 66.0% from the prior quarter but down 39.8% year on year. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 6.69 billion, up 6.6% year on year.

The last four quarters total KRW 23.1 billion for this line. In first-half 2026 data cited by the press, leasing revenue rose 4.0% to KRW 23.2 billion, while operating profit in the construction, sales and leasing segment fell 9.8% to KRW 5.7 billion and that segment's margin dropped from 28.6% to 24.8%.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic intimate-apparel manufacturing is a mature consumer business where demand moves gradually. BYC's reported revenue decline in each year since 2022 fits that pattern. The figures alone do not establish that the decline is entirely cyclical.

Production sits in Indonesia, so cost and currency movements can affect profitability. Within the sector, BYC differs from export-oriented apparel makers such as Youngone and Hansae, which rely on overseas order production, and from fashion brand distributors such as F&F, LF and Handsome.

Its distinguishing feature is owning urban land and leasing assets alongside its manufacturing base. That mix changes the profit structure, but if the core apparel business is slow to improve, leasing income could come to mask weakness in that business.

The property business depends on conditions in the sales market, where interest rates and housing demand determine how fast sale proceeds are collected. The Daerim-dong sale began with 770 units in Building B in October 2026.

06

Outlook

The most concrete milestone is the GD Metro Tower redevelopment of the former headquarters site in Daerim-dong, Yeongdeungpo-gu. The disclosed investment, excluding VAT, is KRW 393.97 billion, equal to 70% of equity.

The investment period runs from September 2026 to January 2031, and the company notes that amounts and timing may change with permits and construction progress. The project comprises 1,095 officetel units across two towers of B1 to 40 floors, plus office and commercial space.

In October 2026, 770 units of 24 to 53 square meters in Building B were offered first. Project economics depend on sale prices, contract rates and the pace of down payment and balance collection.

Low contract rates or late payments would raise financing needs and funding costs, while cutting prices to lift contract rates would reduce profitability.

In August 2026 BYC entered a KRW 3 billion treasury share trust contract with Shinhan Investment Corp., running from August 6, 2026 to February 5, 2027, with share price stability and enhanced corporate value stated as the purpose.

Leasing revenue rose in first-half 2026 while segment margins fell, so whether leasing income grows and segment margins recover together is the next thing to confirm.

08

Bull factors

Leasing-based profit structure

Investment property at fair value was reported at KRW 1.278 trillion. The argument for strength is that leasing assets support profit even as core apparel revenue declines.

Cash generation and earnings recovery

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 10.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 30.3 billion in 2023 and KRW 34.2 billion in 2025. Over the same span, operating margin improved from 14.9% in 2022 to 16.3% in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 6.4% to KRW 19.6 billion in 2025, pointing to an earnings recovery.

Shareholder return measures

The treasury share trust contract approved by the board on August 5, 2026 is worth KRW 3 billion. It runs from August 6, 2026 to February 5, 2027, with share price stability and enhanced corporate value as its stated purpose.

Before the contract, the company held treasury shares equal to 2.19% of common shares and 4.75% of other shares.

Bear factors

Continued decline in core apparel sales

Revenue fell from KRW 169.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 163.2 billion in 2025, down for three consecutive years from 2023. 2025 was the smallest year of 2022 to 2025. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 37.3 billion was down 4.7% year on year, so the decline also appears in the quarterly figures.

Volatile quarterly profit

Q1 2026 operating profit of KRW 5.59 billion fell 50.7% from the prior quarter, and Q2 fell further to KRW 2.99 billion. Q3 operating profit of KRW 4.97 billion was down 39.8% year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 23.1 billion, with wide swings between quarters.

Higher debt and lower segment margin

The debt ratio rose from 26.8% in 2024 to 37.9% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 213.3 billion at end-2025. The Daerim-dong investment of KRW 393.97 billion equals 70% of equity. In first-half 2026, the construction, sales and leasing segment's operating margin fell from 28.6% to 24.8%.

10

Risk factors

Development execution risk

Profitability of the Daerim-dong project depends on sale prices, contract rates and the pace of payment collection. Low contract rates would require additional funding and raise financing costs. Cutting prices to lift contract rates would reduce project returns. Amounts and timing may also shift with permits and construction progress.

Core demand and profitability

Apparel revenue has declined for three consecutive years from 2023, and operating margin fell to 14.4% in 2024 before recovering to 16.3% in 2025. If core demand does not recover, leasing income could continue carrying overall profit for an extended period. Production in Indonesia also means currency and cost swings could affect margins.

Balance sheet and asset valuation

The debt ratio rose to 37.9% in 2025 from 26.8% in 2024. The composition of the increase needs to be checked in the notes to the financial statements in the annual business report.

Investment property at fair value of KRW 1.278 trillion differs widely from book equity, so a change in the asset valuation basis could alter how net assets are read.

12

Overall view

BYC is a company in which leasing and property development are moving to the center of profit while apparel manufacturing revenue has declined for three consecutive years from 2023.

Operating profit recovered to KRW 26.6 billion in 2025 with a 16.3% operating margin, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 19.6 billion. Operating profit in Q2 and Q3 2026 was low at KRW 2.99 billion and KRW 4.97 billion, respectively.

The debt ratio rose to 37.9% in 2025, and the Daerim-dong investment equals 70% of equity, making financing burden and sales performance the key variables.

Strengths include leasing-based cash generation, higher operating cash flow, shareholder return measures through the treasury share trust, and urban property in Seoul. Weaknesses include the decline in core apparel sales, volatile quarterly profit, and sales and financing risks in the development project.

The next checkpoints are the treasury share trust end date on February 5, 2027, the 2026 annual business report due by the end of March 2027, and the Q1 2027 quarterly report in mid-May 2027.
