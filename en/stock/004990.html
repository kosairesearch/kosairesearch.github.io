Lotte: Recovery Amid Equity-Method Losses

Lotte's operating profit rose in the second and third quarters of 2026, but the company returned to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter, leaving a gap between operating and bottom-line results.

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Lotte is a pure holding company set up in 2017 when the investment division of Lotte Confectionery was spun off, so it earns income mainly by holding stakes in subsidiaries rather than selling products.

Its income comes from dividends from subsidiaries, trademark royalties paid by group companies for using the Lotte brand, and management service fees. As of reporting in March 2026, its major stakes were Lotte Wellfood at 48.13%, Lotte Chilsung Beverage at 45%, Lotte Chemical at 25.31%, and Lotte Shopping at 40%.

The food segment centers on Lotte Wellfood (confectionery, ice cream and home meal replacement) and Lotte Chilsung Beverage (soft drinks and alcoholic beverages), and Lotte Wellfood is consolidated into Lotte's financial statements, so its results flow in directly.

Reports indicate that in 2025 Lotte Wellfood accounted for about 27% of consolidated revenue and about 46% of operating profit.

In chemicals, Lotte Chemical is the largest shareholder of Lotte Energy Materials and LOTTE Fine Chemical, and because Lotte's stake is 50% or less, Lotte Chemical is treated as an affiliate whose results are reflected through equity-method gains and losses. Lotte Shopping is classified the same way as an affiliate.

One credit assessment says Lotte Wellfood and Lotte Chilsung Beverage hold leading positions in the food and beverage industry. Chemicals and retail face weak conditions, including pressure from Chinese oversupply.

Overall, food subsidiaries support the holding company's profit while the chemical and retail affiliates drive swings in net income.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 14.1 trillion in 2022 to KRW 15.2 trillion in 2023 (up 7.4% year on year) and KRW 15.8 trillion in 2024 (up 3.9%), then fell to KRW 15.5 trillion in 2025 (down 1.4%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year for revenue and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 493.7 billion in 2023 (up 0.8% year on year), the largest of the four years, and then declined for two consecutive years to KRW 340.5 billion in 2024 (down 31.0%) and KRW 239.4 billion in 2025 (down 29.7%).

The operating margin narrowed from 3.5% in 2022 and 3.3% in 2023 to 2.2% in 2024 and 1.5% in 2025, so margins thinned while revenue stayed near KRW 15 trillion.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a KRW 170.8 billion profit in 2022 to a KRW 15.4 billion loss in 2023, widened to a KRW 1.02 trillion loss in 2024, and narrowed to a KRW 647.6 billion loss in 2025, marking three consecutive loss-making years.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 946.1 billion in 2024 and a loss of KRW 595 billion in 2025. In 2025 the loss attributable to controlling shareholders was larger than the total net loss, which implies that non-controlling interests recorded a profit for the year.

For the causes, reports said losses at affiliate Lotte Chemical weighed heavily on equity-method results in the first nine months of 2024. The debt ratio rose from 127.1% in 2022 to 139.4% in 2023, 146.3% in 2024 and 144.9% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 9.42 trillion, of which the controlling shareholders' stake was KRW 6.08 trillion, and total liabilities were KRW 13.6 trillion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 904.1 billion in 2022, KRW 1.24 trillion in 2023, KRW 1.05 trillion in 2024 and KRW 1.16 trillion in 2025, exceeding KRW 1 trillion in each of the last three years.

Quarterly operating profit moved from KRW 185.1 billion in Q3 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 105.8 billion in Q4 2025, then to KRW 46.1 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 129 billion in Q2 (up 179.9% from the prior quarter) and KRW 157.6 billion in Q3 (up 22.2%), rising for two consecutive quarters.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 79 billion in Q3 2025 and a loss of KRW 363.7 billion in Q4 2025, turned to a profit of KRW 6.55 billion in Q1 2026, rose to KRW 35.6 billion in Q2, and returned to a loss of KRW 42.1 billion in Q3 2026.

The combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters was KRW 363.7 billion. Revenue bottomed at KRW 3.62 trillion in Q1 2026 and rose to KRW 4.09 trillion in Q2 and KRW 4.12 trillion in Q3, although Q3 revenue was 0.7% lower than a year earlier.

The central question in Q3 is that operating profit improved while the bottom-line result turned negative again.

05

Industry analysis

Petrochemicals drive the swings in Lotte's net income. Reports say Lotte Chemical swung to an operating loss of KRW 762.6 billion in 2022 and has continued to post losses since.

In its December 2025 assessment, one Korean credit rating agency judged that Lotte Chemical's supply-demand environment remains unfavorable because of supply pressure from China. A March 2026 report noted that the retail and chemical sectors, the two pillars of the group, are both going through a difficult period.

Analysts also argued that the low valuations of Lotte Chemical and Lotte Shopping could hinder the group's restructuring. Food is regarded as a defensive domestic sector, and Lotte Wellfood has reportedly remained profitable for more than a decade. Still, food subsidiaries are not immune to industry shifts.

Lotte Chilsung Beverage reported that its Q3 2026 operating profit fell 37.9% year on year to KRW 57 billion in a disclosure on November 9.

On credit ratings, Lotte Chilsung Beverage and Lotte Wellfood are rated AA, while Lotte Chemical and Lotte Shopping are rated AA-, so the food side carries relatively higher credit quality.

Within the group structure, Lotte is described as the control tower that effectively decides personnel, strategy and future businesses. Credit assessments also reflect that the holding company's debt is structurally subordinated.

06

Outlook

Future results will depend on how durable food subsidiary profits are and how quickly the affiliated petrochemical business recovers. Operating profit rose for two consecutive quarters through Q3 2026 but was still 14.8% below the year-earlier quarter, so the recovery remains smaller than last year's level.

One checkpoint is how the 37.9% fall in Lotte Chilsung Beverage's Q3 2026 operating profit and its lower net income feed into consolidated earnings. On governance, reports describe ongoing restructuring centered on the holding company under the 'New Lotte' vision led by Chairman Shin Dong-bin.

A Korean credit rating agency said in May 2024 that bringing Hotel Lotte into the holding structure remains an outstanding task, but it did not expect further restructuring immediately.

A March 2026 report said the low valuations of Lotte Chemical and Lotte Shopping could hinder the reorganization, and it expected group individuals and entities to sell or transfer some Lotte shares during the process.

On funding, the same agency assessed that, as of the end of September 2025, cash and short-term financial assets of about KRW 550 billion fell well short of debt maturing within one year of KRW 2.1 trillion, including lease liabilities. Stable cash flows from subsidiaries were cited as a mitigating factor.

Dividends have expanded since 2019, and one assessment says those payments weigh on cash flow. The next regular report is the 2026 annual report, which will show full-year 2026 operating margins and the trend in net income attributable to controlling shareholders.

08

Bull factors

Earnings cushion from food subsidiaries

Reports say Lotte Wellfood has stayed profitable for more than a decade and accounted for about 46% of group operating profit in 2025. Lotte Chilsung Beverage and Lotte Wellfood are core affiliates rated AA by a credit agency. Food earnings partly absorbing weakness in chemicals and retail is the core of the bullish argument.

Two consecutive quarters of operating recovery

Operating profit rose from KRW 46.1 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 129 billion in Q2 and KRW 157.6 billion in Q3, a second consecutive quarterly gain, with Q2 up 179.9% from the prior quarter. Bulls point to the quarterly rebound after the operating margin fell to 1.5% in 2025. The recovery is still below year-earlier levels.

Discount to net assets and cash generation

The price-to-book ratio is well below 1x, meaning a wide discount to net assets. Operating cash flow of KRW 1.16 trillion in 2025 was far larger than the total net loss for the year. Continuing discussion of group restructuring is another factor bulls cite.

09

Bear factors

Volatility from affiliate results

For the first nine months of 2024, reports cited the KRW 681.4 billion loss at affiliate Lotte Chemical as the largest driver of equity-method losses. The total net loss for 2025 was KRW 595 billion, and Q3 2026 returned to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 42.1 billion. Bears argue that weakness in the chemical affiliates can pull net income down again.

Slowdown at a food subsidiary

Lotte Chilsung Beverage reported Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 57 billion, down 37.9% year on year, with revenue down 2.7%. Net income attributable to its owners fell 32.4%. Even if food earnings cushion the holding company, a slowdown at the subsidiary reduces that cushion.

Financial burden and dividend sustainability

The debt ratio rose from 127.1% in 2022 to 144.9% in 2025, and total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 13.6 trillion. One credit assessment judged cash and short-term financial assets of about KRW 550 billion, as of September 2025, to be well short of KRW 2.1 trillion in debt maturing within a year. Bears also cite dividend expansion as a cash flow burden.

Risk factors

Prolonged petrochemical downturn

One credit assessment says Lotte Chemical's supply-demand environment remains unfavorable because of Chinese oversupply. Because affiliate results flow through equity-method accounting, a prolonged downturn translates into volatility in Lotte's net income. Reports say Lotte Chemical has posted losses since 2022.

Balance sheet and credit rating

The debt ratio was 144.9% at the end of 2025, above 127.1% in 2022. A credit assessment says the group's combined financial burden continues to expand. Lotte's rating was also downgraded from AA to AA- in 2023.

Governance restructuring and share sales

Low valuations of Lotte Chemical and Lotte Shopping have been flagged as an obstacle in the group's restructuring. Reports expect group individuals and entities to sell or transfer some Lotte shares.

The high treasury stock ratio is explained by the holding company absorbing a large volume of affiliate shares at its founding.

Overall view

Lotte is a pure holding company in which food subsidiaries provide a floor under earnings while chemical and retail affiliates drive swings in net income. Operating profit rose for two consecutive quarters in 2026, from KRW 46.1 billion in Q1 to KRW 129 billion in Q2 and KRW 157.6 billion in Q3.

However, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders returned in Q3 at KRW 42.1 billion, and the combined loss over the last four quarters is KRW 363.7 billion.

On an annual basis, operating profit fell from KRW 493.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 239.4 billion in 2025, and the loss attributable to controlling shareholders has continued for three consecutive years.

The debt ratio of 144.9% in 2025 is higher than in 2022, while operating cash flow exceeded KRW 1 trillion in each of the last three years, reaching KRW 1.16 trillion in 2025. The price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x, but the continuing losses leave the price-to-earnings ratio uncalculated.

Bulls point to the earnings cushion from food subsidiaries and the discount to net assets, while bears point to affiliate industry conditions, financial burden and dividend sustainability.

The next checkpoints are the provisional Q4 2026 results and the 2026 annual report, and the key question is whether net income attributable to controlling shareholders moves into a sustained profit.
