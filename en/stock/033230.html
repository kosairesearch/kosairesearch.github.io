02

Business structure

Insung Information is an IT infrastructure solutions company that reports its business in four segments: IT infrastructure, network equipment, software and healthcare.

According to FnGuide's compilation, the revenue mix as of December 2025 was 38.49% IT infrastructure, 31.82% network equipment, 30.35% software and 0.75% healthcare. IT infrastructure accounted for 45.38% of revenue in 2022, while software rose from 23.85% to 30.35% over the same period.

The software segment is described as being driven by AI-based security and cloud security solutions. The network equipment segment is affected by the spread of cloud-based IT infrastructure and demand for AI servers.

The company has eight consolidated subsidiaries and overseas affiliates in Hungary, the United States and Indonesia. Segment figures are presented only as revenue shares because segment revenue amounts are not part of the filed results.

On competition, FnGuide's nine-month 2025 commentary pointed to intensifying price competition as technologies standardized. R&D spending in 2025 equaled about 0.2% of sales, a small scale compared with large system integrators.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 314.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 365.2 billion in 2023, up 16.1% and the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span. Sales then fell 1.5% to KRW 359.6 billion in 2024 and 4.7% to KRW 342.7 billion in 2025, a two-year decline from 2024 through 2025.

Operating profit improved from KRW 2.91 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.21 billion in 2023, up 79.1%, before the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 4.75 billion in 2024. The 2025 operating loss of KRW 4.28 billion continued the losses but was smaller than the year before.

The operating margin moved from 1.4% in 2023 to -1.3% in 2024 and -1.2% in 2025, and according to FnGuide, gross profit also declined from KRW 36.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 34.7 billion in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 1.17 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 279 million in 2023, widened to a net loss of KRW 8.45 billion in 2024, and narrowed to a net loss of KRW 3.36 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, a different measure, was a net loss of KRW 9.41 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.11 billion in 2025.

By quarter, the third quarter of 2025 had sales of KRW 80.5 billion and an operating loss of KRW 252 million, while the fourth quarter brought sales of KRW 126.1 billion, up 56.5% from the prior quarter, and an operating profit of KRW 3.22 billion.

The first quarter of 2026 saw sales of KRW 74.6 billion, down 40.8% from the prior quarter, and an operating loss of KRW 1.98 billion, while the second quarter returned to operating profit of KRW 869 million on sales of KRW 100 billion.

The third quarter of 2026 recorded sales of KRW 91.7 billion, down 8.3% from the prior quarter, and an operating loss of KRW 958 million. Sales in the first quarter of 2026 rose 22.3% year on year according to WiseReport.

In the third quarter of 2025, the company posted an operating loss but a positive profit attributable to controlling shareholders, showing that non-operating items shift results from quarter to quarter. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 3.58 billion. According to FnGuide, operating cash flow in the first quarter of 2026 was negative KRW 11.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand in IT infrastructure, network equipment and security software, where Insung Information operates, is shifting with the transition to AI and cloud. WiseReport attributed the first-quarter 2026 improvement to wider adoption of cloud-based IT infrastructure and rising demand for high-performance servers tied to AI.

In the same account, AI-based and cloud security solutions were cited as the core growth engine of the software segment. FnGuide's nine-month 2025 commentary, however, pointed to intensifying price competition as technologies standardized, which it linked to lower sales and weaker profitability.

Demand growth alone has not guaranteed pricing power. Sales declined in 2024 and 2025, showing that a recovery in demand has not translated directly into revenue growth. Among peers, large system integrators such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever are considerably larger in scale.

In security solutions the company overlaps in part with Ahnlab, and in infrastructure build-out and distribution with Daou Data. Because network equipment distribution is a large share of its business, its results are more sensitive to equipment demand and pricing than those of the large integrators.

Outlook

Quarterly sales in 2026 moved up and down, at KRW 74.6 billion in the first quarter, KRW 100 billion in the second and KRW 91.7 billion in the third. Whether fourth-quarter sales hold near the KRW 126.1 billion level of the fourth quarter of 2025 will be a key variable for the full-year 2026 operating result.

At the annual general meeting on March 26, 2026, the financial statements for the 34th term and director appointments were approved, while a partial amendment to the articles of incorporation was rejected for failing to meet the special resolution threshold. Outside directors accounted for 33.33% of the board.

Inventory rose from KRW 37.1 billion at year-end 2025 to KRW 53.2 billion at the end of March 2026, while cash and cash equivalents fell from KRW 39.4 billion to KRW 25.1 billion, according to FnGuide.

Whether the inventory build converts into sales, and how much cash the operations generate, are the next points to check. The 2026 annual report will be the first filing to include the fourth-quarter 2026 results together with full-year operating profit and operating cash flow.

The largest shareholder side's stake rose slightly as of November 12, 2026, and further changes will appear in disclosures. Whether AI-based security solution sales in the software segment hold up quarter to quarter can be checked in the quarterly reports.

Bull factors

Profitable quarters on record

Operating profit of KRW 3.22 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 869 million in the second quarter of 2026 came after loss quarters. Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 91.7 billion rose 13.8% year on year. Profitable quarters on sales near KRW 100 billion point to room for operating improvement.

Rising software mix

The software segment's share of revenue rose from 23.85% in 2022 to 30.35% in 2025. WiseReport cited AI-based and cloud security solutions as the core driver of first-quarter 2026 sales growth. Sales in that quarter rose 22.3% year on year.

Improving balance sheet

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 204.1% in 2022 to 157.8% in 2025. Operating cash flow moved from negative KRW 28.4 billion in 2022 and negative KRW 8.34 billion in 2023 to KRW 15.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 21.4 billion in 2025. The share price trades at a discount to net asset value, which can be read as a cushion on the asset side.

Operating losses of KRW 4.75 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.28 billion in 2025 mark two consecutive loss years. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders has been negative for three straight years from 2023 through 2025. The third quarter of 2026 saw the operating loss widen from a year earlier.

Headwinds in IT infrastructure

The IT infrastructure segment's share of revenue fell from 45.38% in 2022 to 38.49% in 2025. FnGuide's nine-month 2025 commentary cited lower IT infrastructure sales and price competition from technology standardization as reasons for weak results. If price competition persists, margin recovery could stay limited even as sales recover.

Cash and inventory pressure

According to FnGuide, operating cash flow in the first quarter of 2026 was negative KRW 11.4 billion. If inventory does not convert into sales, working capital pressure could increase.

12

Overall view

Insung Information saw sales decline from their 2023 peak through 2025 and has carried operating losses. The 2025 operating loss of KRW 4.28 billion and the net loss of KRW 3.36 billion for controlling shareholders narrowed, but the company remains in a loss pattern.

In 2026 sales rose year on year and some quarters returned to profit, yet the third quarter's operating loss widened even as sales increased. On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 204.1% in 2022 to 157.8% in 2025, and annual operating cash flow was positive in 2024 and 2025.

On the other side, cash and cash equivalents declined and inventory increased, with total liabilities of KRW 139.2 billion at year-end 2025. The strengths are AI and cloud security demand, infrastructure demand and a growing software mix, while the weaknesses are price competition and unstable quarterly results.

The next checkpoints are the operating result in the 2026 annual report and the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report.
