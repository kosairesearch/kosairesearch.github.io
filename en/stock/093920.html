Three Years of Revenue Decline and Mixed Quarterly Earnings

Seowonintech's revenue, driven by mobile accessories and components, has fallen three years in a row since 2023. Sales rose in the second and third quarters of 2026, but the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.83 billion in the third quarter.

02

Business structure

Seowonintech is an electronic components maker founded in 1983 and based in Gunpo, Gyeonggi Province. Its main products are mobile phone keypads, component materials and accessories, and it also makes battery protection circuits.

Based on second-quarter 2026 sales, the business segment was reported as 100% manufacturing. A 2024 first-half media report put the mix at 98.3% accessories and component materials, 1.6% battery protection circuits and 0.1% other.

The component materials are functional parts such as tape, rubber, sponge and heat-dissipation sheets used inside and outside smartphones, while accessories include flip covers, protective cases and silicone cases.

Samsung Electronics is a first-tier vendor supplying phone parts and accessories, and LG Energy Solution receives protection circuit modules such as SCM and PCM that Seowonintech supplies and develops.

Most sales are tied to the mobile phone ecosystem, so the discontinuation of a specific model or a change in volume flows quickly into results. The company attributed part of the 2025 sales decline to a customer discontinuing some items.

Battery protection circuits are a small share, so the battery segment has limited power to change the revenue mix for now. Larger component makers in the same sector, such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek, have different product mixes, which limits direct comparison.

Seowonintech's profile leans toward component materials and accessories, which are sensitive to product replacement cycles.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, revenue went from KRW 316.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 262.8 billion in 2023 (down 17.0%), KRW 229.3 billion in 2024 (down 12.8%) and KRW 194.9 billion in 2025 (down 15.0%), declining three years in a row from 2023 to 2025. 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest among 2022 to 2025.

NewsQuest reported that 2022 sales of KRW 316.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 22.6 billion were a record. Operating profit went from KRW 22.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.99 billion in 2023 (down 55.7%), KRW 8.86 billion in 2024 (down 11.3%) and KRW 3.43 billion in 2025 (down 61.2%).

The operating margin fell from 7.1% in 2022 to 3.8% in 2023, edged up to 3.9% in 2024, and dropped to 1.8% in 2025. In 2025 operating profit fell by a larger share than sales, which declined 15.0%.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.59 billion in 2025 (down 62.7%), while net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 5.53 billion.

In 2024 operating profit fell 11.3%, yet net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 22.4% to KRW 17.7 billion, which suggests non-operating items were a factor that year.

Quarterly, third-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 50.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.36 billion, then fourth-quarter revenue fell to KRW 37.9 billion (down 25.2% from the prior quarter) and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 2.85 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026 revenue was KRW 36.5 billion (down 3.7% from the prior quarter) and operating profit turned to KRW 1.01 billion. Second-quarter revenue rose 19.5% to KRW 43.7 billion, with operating profit of KRW 1.25 billion (up 23.2%).

Third-quarter revenue was KRW 47 billion (up 7.8% from the prior quarter, down 7.3% from a year earlier), but operating profit was KRW 981 million (down 28.0% from a year earlier), and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.83 billion.

The gap between positive operating profit and a larger controlling-shareholder net loss in the third quarter suggests swings in non-operating items and non-controlling interests. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 3.16 billion.

Industry analysis

Sales of mobile component materials and accessories depend on smartphone shipment volumes and the model mix that carries these parts.

In a 2024 media report, analysts expected shipments at a key customer to edge up about 1%, and expected component-material sales growth to exceed shipment growth as more models adopted those parts.

Korea IR Council forecast 2024 sales of KRW 249.9 billion (down 4.9%), but reported 2024 sales of KRW 229.3 billion came in below that estimate. Its operating profit forecast of KRW 10.4 billion also compared with reported 2024 operating profit of KRW 8.86 billion.

That gap suggests model-level volumes fell faster than expected. The 2025 decline tied to a customer discontinuing some items fits the same pattern.

Per AlphaSquare data, Seowonintech ranked 27th among 54 companies in the mobile phone parts industry by 2025 annual sales, against an industry average of KRW 283.7 billion.

Larger component makers in the same electronics and parts sector have broader businesses, so their demand cycles may not move in line with Seowonintech's. Battery protection circuits track demand at LG Energy Solution, but their share is small, so changes in that end market have limited effect on company-wide results.

Assessing the cycle position requires tracking smartphone shipment trends and the timing of customer model changes.

Whether the third-quarter revenue recovery carries through to operating profit is the open question. The durability of the sales rebound and the direction of the operating margin therefore become the key variables in upcoming results. Changes in customer model timing and in the number of models using component materials are also worth tracking.

Sales rebound in 2026 quarters

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 1.01 billion in the first quarter, and the second quarter's KRW 1.25 billion was up 23.2% from the prior quarter. Whether the third-quarter sales recovery carries into profit is the core of the bullish case.

Shareholder returns and voluntary disclosure

Bear factors

Three straight years of revenue decline

Revenue fell every year from 2023, reaching KRW 262.8 billion, KRW 229.3 billion and KRW 194.9 billion. The company attributed the 2025 decline mainly to a customer discontinuing some items. Because the business relies on a few customers and models, a single discontinuation translates directly into lower sales.

Shrinking profitability

The operating margin fell from 7.1% in 2022 to 1.8% in 2025, about a quarter of the earlier level. Operating profit of KRW 3.43 billion in 2025 was 61.2% lower than the year before. The fourth quarter of 2025 recorded an operating loss of KRW 2.85 billion.

Weak third-quarter earnings

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 981 million was 28.0% lower than a year earlier. The company posted a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 2.83 billion in the same quarter, swinging into a loss compared with the prior year. Controlling-shareholder net profit for the last four quarters totaled only KRW 3.16 billion.

Risk factors

Battery protection circuit demand

Battery protection circuits are supplied to LG Energy Solution as SCM and PCM modules that the company also co-develops. Their share of revenue is small, so the battery segment has limited power to change the overall sales mix for now.

If mass production of newly developed products is delayed, growth in the battery segment's revenue share could slow. Because these circuits move with downstream demand, a weaker end market could also reduce volumes.

Expansion of component-material models

Component-material sales rise with the number of smartphone models that carry these parts. If new models adopt tape, rubber, sponge and heat-dissipation sheets more slowly, volume growth may fall short of shipment growth.

The number of adopting models is tied to customer model replacement schedules, so it needs to be checked each quarter.

Dividend capacity and shareholder returns

The dividend yield is well above the industry average, which makes shareholder returns a notable feature. However, sustaining the dividend level depends on earnings, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders has fallen to a low level.

A dividend decision at the annual general shareholders' meeting in late March 2027 could be adjusted in a way that does not match the earnings trend.

Overall view

Seowonintech's reported results show revenue and operating profit both declining after the 2022 peak, with the operating margin reaching 1.8% in 2025. The company attributed the 2025 sales decline mainly to a customer discontinuing some items.

Revenue rose in the second and third quarters of 2026, a sign of rebound, but third-quarter operating profit was lower than a year earlier and the company posted a controlling-shareholder net loss.

The balance sheet is relatively stable, with total equity of KRW 217.2 billion and a debt-to-equity ratio of 15.0%, so the financial burden is small if profits recover. Valuation looks high on earnings and well below book value on net assets, so the reading depends on the earnings trend.

Because sales are concentrated in the Samsung Electronics phone ecosystem, model timing and shipment volumes are the key variables. The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and the revenue trend in the first-quarter 2027 report.
