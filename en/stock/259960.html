Report overview

Record Nine-Month Sales, Softer Q3 Profit

Krafton's cumulative 2026 revenue and operating profit for the first three quarters reached record levels, while Q3 operating profit fell year on year and net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss.

  1. 1

    Q3 2026 revenue came to KRW 971.4 billion, up 11.6% year on year, while operating profit fell 35.5% to KRW 225 billion, which the company attributed partly to costs tied to its gamescom participation.

  2. 2

    Cumulative revenue for the first nine months of 2026 reached KRW 3.63 trillion and operating profit KRW 1.20 trillion, which the company said were record highs for the first three quarters.

  3. 3

    PUBG franchise revenue for the first nine months of 2026 rose 11% year on year, and reports said Subnautica 2 early-access sales passed 5 million copies.

  4. 4

    The company put 2026 shareholder returns at KRW 917.2 billion, combining amounts executed through Q3 with an additional Q4 buyback and cancellation. Over the same period, the debt ratio rose from 16.0% in 2024 to 31.3% in 2025.

02

Business structure

Krafton develops and operates games centered on PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS. Its Q3 2026 revenue by segment was KRW 514.3 billion from mobile, KRW 238.9 billion from PC, KRW 8.6 billion from console and KRW 219.6 billion from other segments.

The other segment includes KRW 198.4 billion of advertising and content revenue from the ADK Group, so non-game business is part of consolidated results. By Chang-up Ilbo's calculation on total Q3 revenue, mobile accounted for about 52%, PC about 25%, other about 23% and console about 1%.

The company said collaborations with global IPs such as Naruto and Ferrari lifted mobile revenue in the quarter. PC revenue fell 32.5% year on year and 57.4% quarter on quarter, as the number of PC content updates in Q3 was lower than usual.

Cumulative revenue from the PUBG IP rose 11% year on year in the first nine months of 2026, and monthly active users on PC and console kept growing in double digits.

New IPs include Subnautica 2, developed by subsidiary Unknown Worlds, and PUBG: DED.NET, while NO LAW, being developed by Swedish subsidiary Neon Giant, was also unveiled.

Competition is segmented by genre, with Krafton facing domestic and overseas developers for the same players in battle royale, mobile and action RPG categories. Its customer base spans PUBG and mobile players worldwide.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue of KRW 1.85 trillion in 2022, KRW 1.91 trillion in 2023 (up 3.1%), KRW 2.71 trillion in 2024 (up 41.8%) and KRW 3.33 trillion in 2025 (up 22.8%). Revenue has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit went from KRW 751.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 768 billion in 2023 and KRW 1.18 trillion in 2024 (up 54.0%), then fell to KRW 1.05 trillion in 2025 (down 10.8%). In 2025 revenue grew while the operating margin dropped to 31.7% from 43.6% in 2024.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 1.31 trillion in 2024 and was KRW 734.8 billion in 2025 (down 43.7%). At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 7.18 trillion and total liabilities KRW 2.25 trillion, with the debt ratio rising to 31.3% from 16.0% in 2024.

Operating cash flow grew every year, from KRW 512.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.05 trillion in 2025. Quarterly operating profit fell to KRW 2.43 billion in Q4 2025, down 99.3% from the prior quarter, then recovered to KRW 561.6 billion in Q1 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 514.8 billion in Q1 2026, then posted losses of KRW 27.4 billion in Q2 and KRW 64.1 billion in Q3, the second consecutive loss quarter.

The four quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026 total KRW 403.3 billion of net income attributable to controlling shareholders. Q3 costs were KRW 746.4 billion, up 43.0% year on year, with the ADK consolidation cited as a factor in a conference call summary.

CFO Bae Dong-geun said the KRW 305.8 billion non-operating loss in Q3 reflected foreign currency translation losses from a weaker dollar.

05

Industry analysis

The game industry has large quarter-to-quarter swings that depend on the reception of new releases, and Krafton's 2026 quarterly pattern reflects this. PC revenue fell sharply in Q3 2026, and the company cited fewer PC content updates than usual as a main cause.

Mobile remained the largest segment at KRW 504.3 billion in Q3. Listed domestic peers include Netmarble, NC, PearlAbyss, Shift UP and Kakao Games, and Krafton competes on the strength of its battle royale IP across PC and console.

According to a March 2026 gameple report, none of the more than ten major new titles scheduled for full release shares a genre with another. The same report said many in the industry view the second half of 2026 and 2027 as the period when Krafton's new titles are expected to gather momentum.

At gamescom and G-STAR 2026, Krafton showed new titles spanning action RPG, cooperative adventure, platformer and strategy RPG genres. The company also acknowledged in its IR that advertising results from ADK are behind its original plan. Market-size and share figures for the wider industry are not included in this report.

06

Outlook

Krafton will present five titles at G-STAR 2026 in Busan's BEXCO from November 19 to 22, including PUBG, TARAE: The Unbound, Age Twisters, Waltz and Jam and Overgeared Hero.

TARAE: The Unbound will show its new Tuegwi class for the first time anywhere, and the company is offering playable demos ahead of a December 3 global launch for one of its titles. Management said at the Q3 results briefing that it expects a rebound in Q4.

On November 3, 2026 the board resolved to buy back and cancel KRW 300 billion of shares during Q4. Subnautica 2 has passed 5 million early-access copies, and the company plans roughly two years of content updates before a full release, although no launch date has been fixed.

The company said it is preparing large-scale content for PUBG's 10th anniversary in 2027, and it previewed new releases such as inZOI and Palworld Mobile for next year. The company has also unveiled its in-house AI model Raon-1 mini, although its performance claims still require external verification. Q4 results may look different from Q3 depending on the currency environment and the timing of new releases.

Bull factors

Cumulative growth in the PUBG franchise

The company said cumulative revenue from the PUBG IP rose 11% year on year in the first nine months of 2026, while monthly active users on PC and console kept growing in double digits. Mobile revenue reached KRW 504.3 billion in Q3, up 3% year on year and 12% quarter on quarter. Growth on a cumulative basis has held even as PC revenue declined in individual quarters.

New IPs and a broader release slate

Reports said Subnautica 2 passed 5 million early-access copies and kept a 91% positive rating on Steam. The company is adding new IPs such as PUBG: DED.NET to widen growth beyond the core PUBG title.

A March 2026 gameple report noted that none of the major upcoming titles shares a genre, which it cited as a basis for portfolio diversification.

Shareholder returns and equity base

The company said its 2026 shareholder returns consist of amounts executed through Q3 and an additional Q4 buyback and cancellation. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 514.8 billion in Q1 2026, showing wide quarterly swings. Total equity of KRW 7.18 trillion at the end of 2025 provides the base for these returns and investments.

Bear factors

Softer Q3 profitability

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 225 billion, down 35.5% year on year. The operating margin had already slipped from 43.6% in 2024 to 31.7% in 2025, and quarterly profitability has not yet steadied. Q3 costs rose on marketing tied to gamescom and on the ADK consolidation.

Net losses and currency exposure

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was negative in both Q2 and Q3 2026, with the Q3 loss at KRW 64.1 billion. CFO Bae Dong-geun said the KRW 305.8 billion non-operating loss reflected foreign currency translation losses from a weaker dollar. Currency-driven non-operating losses can offset operating gains.

PC weakness and advertising drag

PC revenue was KRW 238.9 billion in Q3, down 32.5% year on year and 57.4% quarter on quarter. The company pointed to fewer PC content updates than usual as a reason. Management said ADK's advertising results are behind plan and that coordination with the existing Japanese business is taking longer than expected.

Risk factors

Dependence on one IP and earnings volatility

PUBG remains the core profit source, so slower growth in the franchise would weigh on overall results. Q4 2025 operating profit fell to KRW 2.43 billion, and quarterly operating profit moved from KRW 561.6 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 225 billion in Q3. Quarterly results can vary widely depending on release timing and reception of new titles.

Currency and non-operating items

Foreign currency translation losses tied to a weaker dollar pushed the Q3 non-operating loss to KRW 305.8 billion. Non-operating items can move net income sharply, separately from operating profit.

Total liabilities of KRW 2.25 trillion and the 31.3% debt ratio at the end of 2025 are also items to watch alongside currency exposure.

Execution on new titles and integration

PUBG: Blind Spot began global early access in early February 2026 but was shut down early at the end of March. Subnautica 2 has no fixed full-release date yet. Integrating the ADK advertising business with the existing Japanese operations is taking longer than expected.

12

Overall view

Krafton's cumulative revenue and operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 reached record levels by the company's account, supported by continued growth in the PUBG franchise and new IPs such as Subnautica 2.

Q3 operating profit alone was KRW 225 billion, down 35.5% year on year, and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders continued into Q2 and Q3. Non-operating items were a KRW 305.8 billion loss in Q3 driven by foreign currency translation effects from a weaker dollar.

The operating margin slipped to 31.7% in 2025 from 43.6% in 2024, and the debt ratio rose to 31.3% at the end of 2025. Shareholder returns expanded to KRW 917.2 billion for 2026, but quarterly earnings have swung sharply, so returns and earnings should be read together.

The next confirmation points are the Q4 rebound, the reception of December titles and the full-year figures in the 2026 annual report.
