02

Business structure

Cell Biotech is a probiotics specialist now in its 31st year, running an integrated production system that covers strain discovery, fermentation, raw material production and finished goods manufacturing on its own technology.

Production is based at five plants in Gimpo, Gyeonggi Province, and products are supplied to 56 countries. Its flagship brand is DUOLAC, which the company differentiates with its dual-coating technology for survival in the intestine.

Overseas sales were reported to have exceeded 50% of the total in 2025, shifting the business focus from domestic health functional foods toward exports.

Reports say the company has held the No. 1 ranking in Korean probiotics exports for 13 consecutive years, and it accounted for KRW 25.9 billion of the KRW 59.6 billion in total Korean probiotics exports in 2023.

A January 2026 report put DUOLAC at No. 1 in Denmark's pharmacy probiotics market with a 46.6% share, although a securities information summary lists the same market at No. 2, so the ranking reads differently depending on the aggregation basis.

The company also runs a business-to-business supply operation providing lactic acid bacteria to European companies. In the domestic market, sales fell in Q1 2025 amid a slump and price competition in health functional foods, while the company said domestic sales grew in Q2 2026 on the DUOLAC advertising campaign.

In pharmaceuticals, the microbiome colorectal cancer candidate PP-P8, which applies genetic engineering, is in Phase 1 and is being advanced while the company reviews relevant standards with the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 50.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 53.8 billion in 2023, a 6.5% increase, then fell to KRW 49.9 billion in 2024, a 7.2% decline, before recovering to KRW 53.1 billion in 2025, a 6.4% increase. Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year for revenue and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 6.05 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.2 billion in 2023, a 63.7% drop, and then rose to KRW 6.8 billion in 2024, up 209.6%, and KRW 7.91 billion in 2025, up 16.4%, so it grew two years in a row from 2024 to 2025.

The operating margin moved from 12.0% in 2022 to 4.1% in 2023, 13.6% in 2024 and 14.9% in 2025, and 2025 was the largest year for operating profit in the period. The company attributed the 2024 operating profit increase to expanded higher-margin exports and cost efficiency.

Net income that year was also lifted by financial income, including foreign exchange gains, reaching KRW 15.1 billion, up 140.0%.

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 8.28 billion in 2022, KRW 6.3 billion in 2023 (down 23.9%), KRW 15.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.1 billion in 2025 (down 33.1%), making 2024 the largest year in the period.

Quarterly operating profit reached KRW 3.05 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 3.19 billion in Q1 2026, the highest levels among the reported quarters.

Operating profit then dropped to KRW 1.64 billion in Q2 2026, down 48.5% from the prior quarter, and recovered to KRW 2.01 billion in Q3 2026, up 22.4% quarter on quarter but down 9.4% year on year. Q3 2026 turned into a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 4.27 billion.

Controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, totaled KRW 9.08 billion.

Provisional figures for January to September 2026 showed revenue of KRW 45.2 billion, up 17.6%, operating profit of KRW 6.8 billion, up 40.6%, and net income of KRW 4.7 billion, down 18.2%, disclosed on November 6, 2026. These are figures before external audit review and may change.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 131.6 billion, controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 131.6 billion, total liabilities were KRW 4.94 billion, and the debt ratio was 3.7%. Operating cash flow was KRW 12.8 billion in 2022, KRW 7.82 billion in 2023, KRW 19.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 11.1 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's health functional food market slumped in Q1 2025 on temporary weakness and intensifying price competition, and the company said higher-margin export results offset the resulting sales decline.

Over the same period the domestic health functional food industry ran a trade deficit, yet probiotics exports kept the No. 1 position for 13 consecutive years. Total Korean probiotics exports were KRW 59.6 billion in 2023, of which Cell Biotech accounted for KRW 25.9 billion, roughly 43%.

Overseas, reports cited a No. 1 position in Denmark's pharmacy market and an announcement that cumulative exports to Indonesia passed USD 30 million in 2025. The company holds 11 strains on the FDA GRAS list, which it cites as the highest number worldwide and a differentiating factor.

Price competition in the domestic lactic acid bacteria market is a constraint the company itself has pointed to. To respond to tariffs and trade barriers, the company is studying joint ventures and technology transfers in Southeast and Central Asia.

In microbiome drugs, which apply genetic engineering to a new development approach, clinical trials are proceeding while relevant standards are reviewed with the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety.

Outlook

In 2025, the 30th anniversary year, the company framed itself as entering full-scale development as a biopharmaceutical company, and it set a long-term vision of becoming a global leader in microbiome drugs by 2055.

Export markets include Denmark, Turkey, Russia and Mexico, with the country count reported as 55 in some materials and 56 in others. Overseas production bases are still at the review stage, and no concrete location or investment plan has been confirmed.

Domestically, the customized probiotics business 'Cellbiome', based on NGS testing, has launched as a new business pillar. In the drug pipeline, the Phase 1 trial of PP-P8 started at Seoul National University Hospital in 2025, and follow-on development direction will become clearer depending on trial results.

The advertising campaign lifted domestic sales in Q2 2026, showing consumer-reach strategy feeding into reported results. The next checkpoint is the annual revenue and operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and Q1 2027 provisional results will be compared against Q1 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.19 billion.

Whether overseas production investment is executed and the outcome of the Phase 1 trial remain the variables that will shape the medium-term growth path.

Overall view

Cell Biotech's revenue has moved around KRW 50 billion since 2022, and operating profit rose two years in a row in 2024 and 2025. Overseas sales above 50% and 13 consecutive years at the top of Korean probiotics exports are cited as strengths.

On the other side, revenue did not regain the KRW 53.8 billion of 2023, and Q3 2026 turned into a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 4.27 billion. Provisional January to September 2026 operating profit of KRW 6.8 billion rose year on year, but it is before external audit review.

On financials, total equity of KRW 131.6 billion and a 3.7% debt ratio at the end of 2025 indicate a stable position. The PP-P8 pipeline is in Phase 1, so the timing of any contribution to earnings is uncertain.

With strengths and weaknesses both present, the annual operating margin in the 2026 annual report and Q1 2027 provisional results are the next checkpoints.
