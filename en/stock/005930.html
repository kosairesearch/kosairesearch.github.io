02

Business structure

Samsung Electronics runs a chip division (DS), a device division covering smartphones, home appliances and networks (DX), the automotive and audio subsidiary Harman, and a display business. Per the Q3 2026 segment results, the DS division posted revenue of KRW 147.1 trillion and operating profit of KRW 106.5 trillion.

In the same quarter, the DX division recorded revenue of KRW 52.1 trillion and an operating loss of KRW 0.8 trillion, Harman posted revenue of KRW 4.3 trillion and operating profit of KRW 0.6 trillion, and Display posted revenue of KRW 8 trillion and operating profit of KRW 1.1 trillion.

Reports put memory at about 72% of total company revenue, which makes memory the center of the revenue structure. Within memory, HBM4 is the key product, and sales of server DRAM and enterprise SSDs are also growing.

Foundry revenue rose on 4nm-based HBM base die sales, and orders are expanding mainly from 2nm HPC customers, including large hyperscalers. On the customer side, the company said it has signed long-term supply agreements with 10 large customers and is negotiating with 10 more.

In competition, Counterpoint Research data put the Q2 2026 HBM share at 50% for SK hynix and 33% for Samsung, with Samsung's share up from 21% in Q1. Reports put Samsung's DRAM share at 38% in the same quarter.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue went from KRW 302.2 trillion in 2022 to KRW 258.9 trillion in 2023, down 14.3% year on year, then rose to KRW 300.9 trillion in 2024, up 16.2%, and to KRW 333.6 trillion in 2025, up 10.9%, a two-year run of growth.

Operating profit fell from KRW 43.4 trillion in 2022 to KRW 6.57 trillion in 2023, down 84.9%, then recovered to KRW 32.7 trillion in 2024, up 398.3%, and to KRW 43.6 trillion in 2025, up 33.2%.

The operating margin was 14.4% in 2022, 2.5% in 2023, 10.9% in 2024 and 13.1% in 2025, and the 2023 trough coincided with a memory downturn.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 54.7 trillion in 2022, the highest year in the period, then KRW 14.5 trillion in 2023, down 73.6%, before rising to KRW 33.6 trillion in 2024 and KRW 44.3 trillion in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests, at KRW 45.2 trillion in 2025, is a different figure from profit attributable to owners of the parent.

On a quarterly basis, revenue rose from KRW 86.1 trillion and operating profit of KRW 12.2 trillion in Q3 2025 to KRW 195.3 trillion and KRW 107.4 trillion in Q3 2026, passing through operating profit of KRW 57.2 trillion in Q1 2026, up 185.1% quarter on quarter, and KRW 89.5 trillion in Q2 2026, up 56.4%.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 75.2 trillion in Q3 2026, and the trailing four-quarter total of that figure was KRW 212.9 trillion.

The company said a weaker dollar and euro cut Q3 operating profit by about KRW 10 trillion quarter on quarter, so the rise in operating profit shows that memory volume and pricing outweighed the currency drag.

The DX division remained loss-making with an operating loss of KRW 0.8 trillion in the same quarter, and higher memory prices added cost pressure on set businesses.

The debt-to-equity ratio edged up from 26.4% in 2022 to 29.9% in 2025, and operating cash flow reached KRW 85.3 trillion in 2025, the largest figure in the period.

05

Industry analysis

The memory market is described as one where AI infrastructure demand keeps outpacing supply growth. At its Q3 2026 earnings call, the company said demand growth continues to exceed the pace of supply expansion despite its capacity additions.

Shinhan Investment Corp. said in a May 2026 report that a memory demand-exceeds-supply environment is likely to last at least through 2027. The company expects tight supply conditions to extend into 2028.

It also noted that a rising share of wafer output shifting between HBM and standard DRAM limits how fast physical supply can expand. The company dismissed concerns that the chip cycle has peaked.

In competition, Counterpoint Research data put the Q2 2026 HBM share at 50% for SK hynix and 33% for Samsung, with Samsung's share up from 21% in Q1. Hankyung estimated Samsung's HBM share in Q3 2026 at close to 40%. The company says co-development with its foundry unit on a 4nm-based HBM4 base die is a differentiator.

On the other side, weak mobile demand weighed on System LSI, and the networks business declined on lower North American and software sales.

06

Outlook

The company expects its chip business to keep posting strong results in Q4, with overall company results growing. The memory unit said it will expand sales of HBM4, high-performance DDR5 and QLC-based server SSDs.

The company shipped HBM4E samples for the first time in May 2026, reports positive early customer evaluations, and plans to start mass production in the first half of 2027 and ramp output in the second half.

The company said 2027 HBM supply has been fully contracted at target prices, with major customers still requesting additional volume. It is targeting long-term supply agreements covering 60% to 70% of capacity through 2030, and it has completed agreements with 10 large customers.

Reports describe a planned 2026 facility investment of KRW 84.4 trillion, of which KRW 76.8 trillion is allocated to semiconductors. Foundry investment is centered on the Taylor fab in the United States, and growing 2nm HPC orders is presented as its growth axis.

The company also noted that HBM4E and HBM5 have larger die sizes and more metal wiring layers, which makes production-transition constraints unavoidable. The payoff from these investments depends on production timing from 2027, so the pace of actual supply expansion needs to be confirmed in later quarterly results.

08

Bull factors

Rising HBM share and surging HBM4 sales

HBM4 revenue more than tripled quarter on quarter in Q3 2026 and made up 60% of total HBM revenue. Counterpoint Research data show Samsung's HBM share rising from 21% in Q1 2026 to 33% in Q2. The company said it appears to have already reached, in Q3, its goal of an HBM share on par with its overall DRAM share.

Long-term contracts and 2027 supply sold out

The company said it has completed long-term supply agreements with 10 large customers and is negotiating with 10 more. It also said the chance of reaching long-term agreements covering 60% to 70% of capacity through 2030 has grown. It said 2027 HBM supply has already been sold out at target prices.

Financial capacity and cash generation

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 436.3 trillion, of which KRW 424.3 trillion is attributable to owners of the parent. The debt-to-equity ratio edged up from 26.4% in 2022 to 29.9% in 2025. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 73 trillion in 2024 to KRW 85.3 trillion in 2025.

09

Bear factors

Profit concentrated in chips

Of the KRW 107.4 trillion operating profit in Q3 2026, the DS division accounted for KRW 106.5 trillion, so about 99% of profit came from the chip business. The DX division posted an operating loss of KRW 0.8 trillion in the same quarter, and System LSI was hit by weak mobile demand.

When profit sits in one division, the size of any shock depends on whether long-term contracts hold up earnings.

Currency and cost pressure

The company said a weaker dollar and euro cut Q3 2026 operating profit by about KRW 10 trillion quarter on quarter. Higher memory prices added cost burden on set businesses.

A higher mix of premium products lifted average selling prices and improved DX results from the prior quarter, but the division remains loss-making.

Heavy investment and a softer-demand view

Reports say KRW 76.8 trillion of the planned KRW 84.4 trillion 2026 facility investment is allocated to semiconductors. BNK Investment & Securities cut its rating to Hold as of September 14, 2026, citing softer memory demand.

In early November 2026, concerns over US AI data center regulation coincided with a sharp drop in the Philadelphia semiconductor index.

10

Risk factors

Demand and industry cycle

If the current setup, in which AI infrastructure demand outruns supply expansion, changes, memory prices and earnings could adjust together. The company says a rising shift of output between HBM and standard DRAM limits how fast physical supply can expand. Whether long-term contracts convert into actual volume is the key to reducing cycle exposure.

Currency and input costs

The company said a weaker dollar and euro reduced Q3 2026 operating profit by about KRW 10 trillion. Higher memory prices increase cost burdens on mobile and appliance sets. Currency and cost factors affect the DX division's loss separately from chip profits.

Investment execution and regulation

Most of the planned KRW 84.4 trillion 2026 facility investment is allocated to semiconductors. Large investment could weigh on utilization if supply shortages ease before new capacity comes online. Concerns over US AI data center regulation led to a sharp semiconductor index drop in early November 2026.

12

Overall view

Samsung's disclosed results show revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to owners of the parent rising two straight years after the 2023 low. Q3 2026 operating profit reached KRW 107.4 trillion, the largest quarterly figure in the record, and memory, including HBM4, sits at the center of that growth.

The company has said 2027 HBM supply is sold out and that it has signed long-term contracts with 10 large customers. Strengths include rising HBM share, long-term contracts and financial capacity, with the debt-to-equity ratio at 29.9% at the end of 2025.

Weaknesses include the near-total concentration of profit in one chip division, the DX division's operating loss, currency swings and the execution burden of a planned KRW 84.4 trillion facility investment.

Some analysts see memory supply staying tight beyond 2027, while others have cut ratings on softer demand, so views remain divided.

This report does not provide an investment rating or target price, and the reference points to watch are the long-term contract structure in the 2026 annual report and the timing of HBM4E mass production in the first half of 2027.

Whether the goal of long-term contracts covering 60% to 70% of capacity through 2030 turns into signed volume is the key variable for earnings durability.
