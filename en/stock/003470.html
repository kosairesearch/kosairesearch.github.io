02

Business structure

Yuanta Securities Korea is a domestic securities firm engaged in investment trading, brokerage, advisory, discretionary investment, trust business and ancillary businesses. As of end-June 2025 it had 1,771 employees, 53 domestic branches and two overseas subsidiaries.

Its largest shareholder, Yuanta Securities Asia Financial Services Private Limited, held 58.77% of common shares as of the June 2025 semi-annual report. The company manages revenue across six categories: brokerage, financial products, asset management, underwriting, funding, and other.

In the third quarter of 2026, on a standalone basis and under the company's own classification, brokerage accounted for 50% of business-related revenue, financial products 23%, asset management 13%, funding 14%, and underwriting 1%.

Because brokerage makes up about half of revenue, changes in trading value feed directly into earnings. Financial products centre on fund, wrap, bond and ELS sales, and the company has named strengthening its wealth management capabilities as a priority.

Underwriting is expanding through rights offering lead management, mezzanine issuance, bond underwriting and structured finance, but it still accounts for about 1% of revenue. Underwriting returned to a KRW 3.4 billion profit in the third quarter of 2026 after a KRW 1.9 billion loss in the second quarter.

In a November 2025 report, Korea Ratings praised the company's broad branch network and retail client base, while noting that its IB and asset-based businesses have relatively low revenue weight and market position.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 2.48 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.86 trillion in 2023 (up 15.2% year on year), fell to KRW 2.71 trillion in 2024 (down 5.0%), and then reached KRW 3.46 trillion in 2025 (up 27.6%), the largest year in the period.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 43.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 129.1 billion in 2023 (up 198.1%), then dropped to KRW 94.8 billion in 2024 (down 26.6%) and rose to KRW 99.4 billion in 2025 (up 4.9%), which still did not reach the 2023 level.

The operating margin was 1.7% in 2022, 4.5% in 2023, 3.5% in 2024 and 2.9% in 2025. In 2025 revenue grew 27.6% while operating profit grew only 4.9%, so top-line growth did not translate into a higher margin.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 45.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 95.6 billion in 2025, increasing for three consecutive years from 2023. Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed at the same amounts for each year.

The debt-to-equity ratio moved from 761.8% in 2022 to 927.9% in 2023, 928.0% in 2024 and 950.9% in 2025, when total liabilities were KRW 18.1 trillion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 518 billion in 2022, then -KRW 381.5 billion in 2023, -KRW 187.5 billion in 2024 and -KRW 915.4 billion in 2025, the largest outflow in the period.

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 39.1 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 20.5 billion in the fourth quarter (down 47.5% quarter on quarter), KRW 72.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 254.7%) and KRW 86.5 billion in the second quarter (up 18.8%).

The third quarter of 2026 recorded KRW 53.7 billion (down 37.9% quarter on quarter, up 37.3% year on year), the lowest of 2026.

Per the company's November 12, 2026 announcement, third-quarter brokerage revenue was KRW 109.1 billion, down 44.1% from KRW 195.1 billion in the second quarter, and asset management revenue was KRW 27.8 billion, down 25.5% year on year.

Financial product revenue reached a quarterly high and underwriting swung to a KRW 3.4 billion profit. Cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 213 billion, up 170% year on year, which exceeds full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 99.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The 2026 market backdrop has been uneven. An October 2026 media report said the first-half earnings rebound was supported by a strong stock market.

In the third quarter, a market slump and lower trading value cut brokerage revenue, and asset management revenue fell 25.5% year on year on weaker stocks and higher interest rates. Securities revenue moves sharply from quarter to quarter with trading value and the rate environment.

Brokers are therefore expanding wealth management and investment banking to lower their reliance on commissions. On the regulatory side, a comprehensive financial investment business operator, which requires at least KRW 3 trillion in equity, is permitted to extend corporate credit and related business.

Yuanta's standalone equity was reported at about KRW 1.95 trillion at end-June 2026, while one large domestic brokerage had about KRW 13.2 trillion over the same period, a gap of roughly 6.8 times. Korea Ratings has noted that the company's IB and asset-based businesses need strengthening against larger peers.

The company, however, has built a steady retail client base on its broad branch network and long operating history.

06

Outlook

Three plans have been disclosed or reported. First, when it issued KRW 170 billion in hybrid capital securities in November 2025, the company said the proceeds would strengthen financial product sales, expand margin financing and broaden its corporate finance base.

Second, a September 2026 media report said the company agreed to underwrite 50% of the unsubscribed shares in a rights offering of about KRW 100 billion by a listed company, alongside a large domestic brokerage.

If the entire offering went unsubscribed, the potential underwriting amount for each firm would be about KRW 50.8 billion, or 2.6% of Yuanta's equity. Third, an October 2026 media report said the company cancelled about KRW 62.4 billion of treasury shares this year, equal to about 3.3% of issued shares.

Analysts reported that the gap to the KRW 3 trillion equity threshold for a comprehensive financial investment business operator remains large. The largest shareholder, a Taiwan-based Yuanta group entity, is a variable for future capital-raising.

The 2026 annual report is due by late March 2027 under statutory deadlines, and the annual operating margin and operating cash flow in that filing will be the next checks.

08

Bull factors

Record Quarterly Financial Product Revenue

The company said on November 12, 2026 that financial product revenue hit a quarterly record in the third quarter on strong sales of funds, wraps, bonds and ELS. Media reports put the year-on-year increase at 109.9%.

The argument for strength is that this revenue partly cushions swings in brokerage income tied to trading value.

Cumulative 2026 Profit Already Exceeds Full-Year 2025

The company announced cumulative operating profit of KRW 213 billion for the first three quarters of 2026, up 170% year on year. That matches the sum of the quarterly figures of KRW 72.8 billion, KRW 86.5 billion and KRW 53.7 billion.

It exceeds full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 99.4 billion, so the earnings recovery is visible in the numbers.

Capital Build-Up and the Comprehensive Broker Threshold

The company issued KRW 170 billion in hybrid capital securities in November 2025 to build capital. A comprehensive financial investment business operator is a securities firm with at least KRW 3 trillion in equity that may extend corporate credit, and Yuanta has been mentioned as a candidate. Analysts note that a large gap to the KRW 3 trillion requirement remains.

Bear factors

Revenue Tied to Trading Value

Brokerage revenue fell 44.1% to KRW 109.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026 from KRW 195.1 billion in the prior quarter. Brokerage accounts for half of standalone business revenue, so lower trading value fed directly into profit. Third-quarter operating profit of KRW 53.7 billion was the lowest quarter of 2026.

Shrinking Investment Banking Revenue

Investment banking operating revenue fell from KRW 55.8 billion in 2023 to KRW 40.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 36.1 billion in 2025. Lower fees from real estate project financing advisory and debt guarantees were cited as the cause. Underwriting returned to a KRW 3.4 billion profit in the third quarter of 2026, but the scale is still small.

Underwriting Exposure Against Equity and Cash Flow

A September 2026 report said the company would underwrite 50% of the unsubscribed shares in a listed company's rights offering. If nothing is subscribed, the potential underwriting amount is about KRW 50.8 billion, or 2.6% of equity. Operating cash flow in 2025 was -KRW 915.4 billion, the largest outflow in the period. The debt-to-equity ratio of 950.9% is also high.

10

Risk factors

Market Sensitivity

Brokerage and asset management revenue are directly affected by market slumps and lower trading value. In the third quarter of 2026 both fell together, and quarterly operating profit dropped 37.9% from the prior quarter.

Financial products and underwriting partly offset the decline, but the offset may be limited if the market slump lasts.

Balance Sheet Structure

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 18.1 trillion, large compared with equity of KRW 1.91 trillion. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 761.8% in 2022 to 950.9% in 2025. Operating cash flow was negative in 2023, 2024 and 2025, which is a point to monitor for funding needs.

Capital-Raising Path

Analysts note a large gap remains to the KRW 3 trillion equity threshold for a comprehensive business operator. Because the largest shareholder is a Taiwan-based Yuanta group entity, any further capital raise may depend on group-level allocation decisions.

The hybrid securities carry call options after five and seven years, so the timing of redemption is also a point to monitor.

Overall view

Yuanta Securities has grown both annual revenue and operating profit every year since 2022, and in 2025 it recorded revenue of KRW 3,461.3 billion, operating profit of KRW 99.4 billion, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 95.6 billion.

However, its operating margin of 2.9% in 2025 is lower than the 4.5% in 2023, and its debt ratio is high at 950.9%. In 2026, operating profit continued with KRW 72.8 billion in Q1, KRW 86.5 billion in Q2, and KRW 53.7 billion in Q3, bringing the cumulative total for Q1 to Q3 to KRW 213.0 billion.

The Q3 results were driven by a decline in brokerage business, which was partly offset by financial products and underwriting.

Bullish factors are expanded sales of financial products, the turnaround of IB to profit, capital expansion, and treasury share cancellation, while bearish factors are sensitivity to trading volume, a trend of shrinking IB revenue, and negative operating cash flow.

The next checkpoint is how the annual operating margin and operating cash flow turn out in the 2026 business report (filing deadline at the end of March 2027). This report is an informational document that summarizes the facts and the bullish and bearish framework.
