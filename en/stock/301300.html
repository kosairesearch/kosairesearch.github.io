02

Business structure

VAIV company, founded in July 2000 and listed on KOSDAQ in October 2020, is an AI and big-data software company. Its main products are Sometrend, a social and news data analytics service, and VAIV GeM, its in-house generative AI model built on natural language processing.

In 2026 Sometrend MCP received formal approval on ChatGPT Apps and a paid subscription opened, moving the product toward monetization. The business splits into public-sector AX (AI transformation) projects and AI product sales to private companies.

On the public side, the company won a Ministry of Foreign Affairs intelligent diplomatic-security data platform project and a Ministry of Food and Drug Safety food information system project in 2026, and it is running operations for the aT public catering platform and upgrading a Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism policy opinion system.

In August 2026 it was reported to be upgrading generative AI features of KINDA, a national research institute data platform. On the private side, it sells VAIV Agent Platform, VAIV AI DATA and VAIV KMS, and an external company-information service says supply to private financial customers is expanding.

In 3Q 2025 its AI segment posted revenue of KRW 2.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 300 million, according to Edaily. Competition includes large system integrators and platform companies such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS, NAVER and Kakao, and the company is smaller in scale than these peers.

Since January 2026, an affiliate in which the company holds a 33% stake has moved from subsidiary status to associate status after another shareholder's voting proxy was terminated.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 36.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 30 billion in 2023 (down 18.0% year on year), KRW 26.3 billion in 2024 (down 12.1%) and KRW 19 billion in 2025 (down 27.8%). Revenue has declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest within 2022 to 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 revenue decline to a smaller SI business and a smaller text-mining report business.

Operating loss was KRW 18.2 billion in 2022, KRW 9.23 billion in 2023, KRW 5.99 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.76 billion in 2025, a loss in each of four consecutive years, with the size of the loss shrinking every year. The operating margin moved from -49.8% to -30.8%, -22.7% and -19.8%.

The company credited the smaller operating loss to cost reductions and selling and administrative efficiency from a restructured business.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a net loss of KRW 21 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 32.6 billion in 2023, a turnaround that came in a year with a KRW 9.23 billion operating loss, which points to a large contribution from non-operating items.

It returned to a net loss of KRW 9.32 billion in 2024 and widened to a net loss of KRW 18 billion in 2025. The company cited higher non-operating expenses from impairments of associates and investment stocks as the main reason for the wider 2025 net loss.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 18.4 billion in 2025. During 2025, an associate recorded a one-off deemed disposal gain of about KRW 43 billion during its rights offering, and the absence of that gain widened the net loss in the first half of 2026.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 5.26 billion with an operating loss of KRW 766 million in Q3 2025, then rose to KRW 6.46 billion (up 22.8% from the prior quarter) with an operating profit of KRW 660 million in Q4 2025.

Revenue fell to KRW 3.13 billion in Q1 2026 (down 51.5% from the prior quarter), and operating losses have continued for three consecutive quarters from Q1 to Q3 2026. Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 2.71 billion with an operating loss of KRW 839 million.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 3.9 billion (up 43.6% from the prior quarter and down 25.9% year on year) with an operating loss of KRW 107 million.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 15.2 billion in Q4 2025, then narrowed to KRW 3.01 billion, KRW 1.32 billion and KRW 1.1 billion in Q1 to Q3 2026, and totaled KRW 20.6 billion over the latest four quarters.

Consolidated revenue in the first half of 2026 was affected by accounting changes after the affiliate's status changed, while standalone first-half revenue rose 11.7% year on year.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic AI demand moves along two tracks: public-sector AX (AI transformation) project orders and generative AI adoption by private companies.

VAIV company has taken part in government AI programs, having been selected as a supplier for a hyperscale-AI platform usage support project in June 2024 and again in September 2025.

Public-sector contracts are awarded project by project across agencies such as the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety, the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and aT, so revenue timing depends on order schedules.

Quarterly revenue in 2026 was KRW 3.13 billion in Q1, KRW 2.71 billion in Q2 and KRW 3.9 billion in Q3. The company said public and private orders and AX product sales are expected to be reflected sequentially from the second half of 2026, as reported on August 18, 2026.

On the private side, the company is widening its AI agent and data platform lineup in response to enterprise demand for AI use. Competition spans large system integrators, platform companies and domestic LLM and big-data analytics firms. The company is smaller in headcount and scale than large system integrators.

Listed companies in the same IT and software sector include NAVER, Kakao, Samsung SDS and LG CNS. Where the industry stands in its cycle depends on government order timing and the pace of private adoption, and the available sources do not allow a firm conclusion.

Whether this outlook holds will be confirmed in the 2026 annual report's full-year operating and net income.

On products, Sometrend MCP opened a paid subscription in July 2026, VAIV Agent Platform launched in June 2026, VAIV AI DATA was unveiled in May 2026, and Sometrend received a major generative AI update on October 23, 2026, so several offerings are in commercial stages.

The new shares are scheduled to list on December 28, 2026.

Fourth-quarter 2026 results will be confirmed through the 2026 annual report rather than a separate quarterly filing, so the next regular checkpoint is the direction of full-year operating and net income attributable to controlling shareholders.

Shrinking operating loss and margin gains

The operating margin improved from -49.8% in 2022 to -19.8% in 2025. The operating loss narrowed from KRW 18.2 billion to KRW 3.76 billion over the same period, and the company credited cost reductions and selling and administrative efficiency. The Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 107 million was smaller than a year earlier.

Public-sector AX wins and product monetization

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs data platform and Ministry of Food and Drug Safety food information system projects were on the company's 2026 order list. A paid subscription for Sometrend MCP opened in July 2026, and VAIV Agent Platform launched in June 2026.

The company said public and private orders and AX product sales will be reflected sequentially from the second half.

Quarterly revenue rebound and standalone growth

Q3 2026 revenue rose 43.6% from the prior quarter to KRW 3.9 billion. Standalone first-half 2026 revenue rose 11.7% year on year, according to a Newspim report. The company said the consolidated revenue decline reflected accounting effects from the change in the affiliate's status.

Bear factors

Five consecutive quarters of controlling-shareholder net losses

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued for five consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 through Q3 2026. The four quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026 total KRW 20.6 billion.

The largest quarterly loss was KRW 15.2 billion in Q4 2025, and later quarters narrowed to KRW 3.01 billion, KRW 1.32 billion and KRW 1.1 billion.

Three years of revenue decline and quarterly swings

Annual revenue fell from KRW 36.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 19 billion in 2025, a three-year decline. Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 3.13 billion was down 51.5% from the prior quarter, and Q2 2026 revenue fell further to KRW 2.71 billion. Large quarterly swings make it harder to confirm whether the earnings trend is sustained.

Impairments at associates and non-operating swings

Impairments of associates and investment stocks raised non-operating expenses and affected the size of the 2025 net loss. The absence of a one-off gain recorded during 2025 at an associate widened the net loss in the first half of 2026. Non-operating swings therefore have a large effect on the direction of net income.

Risk factors

Market risk: investment warning designation

The Korea Exchange designated VAIV company's common shares as an investment warning stock from November 17, 2026, citing a sharp share-price rise. The exchange said trading could be suspended if the rise continues. The first review of whether to lift the designation is scheduled for November 30, 2026.

Financial risk: liquidity and cash flow

Operating cash flow improved from -KRW 15.1 billion in 2022 to -KRW 1.08 billion in 2025 but remains negative. A company-information aggregate puts the 2025 current ratio at 49.5%, down from 95.4% in 2024. Korea Enterprise Data gave a credit rating of BB0 as of April 3, 2026.

Business risk: public-sector dependence and ownership

Because the business relies on public-sector projects, revenue can depend on government order timing and project completion dates. On November 12, 2026, the board approved a KRW 5 billion third-party placement, and the largest shareholder, Kim Kyung-seo, agreed to sell 1 million common shares to the placement investor.

Under a shareholder agreement, Kim will exercise voting rights in the same direction as the allottee after closing.

12

Overall view

VAIV company has narrowed its operating loss every year since 2022, improving the operating margin from -49.8% to -19.8%. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 3.9 billion and an operating loss of KRW 107 million show a narrower quarterly gap.

However, net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued for five consecutive quarters, and the latest four quarters total KRW 20.6 billion.

Supporting factors include the smaller operating loss, public-sector AX wins, and the move of products such as Sometrend MCP and the agent platform toward monetization.

Opposing factors include three consecutive years of revenue decline, continued controlling-shareholder net losses, impairments at associates and volatile non-operating items, the investment warning designation from November 17, 2026, and the KRW 5 billion third-party placement.

The return to full-year profit the company expects will be confirmed by full-year operating income in the 2026 annual report. The next items to confirm are the direction of full-year earnings and the ownership structure after the placement payment.
