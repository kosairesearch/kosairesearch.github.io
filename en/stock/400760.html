Report overview

NH All-One REIT: Seoul Office Rebalancing Meets Higher Leverage

NH All-One REIT is continuing to sell regional assets and add Seoul core-district offices, but a rising debt ratio and net losses over the last four quarters are appearing at the same time.

  1. 1

    The sale of A-One Tower Dangsan was completed at KRW 163 billion, about 25% above its February 2020 purchase price of KRW 130.3 billion, according to Etoday. The gain is earmarked for a special dividend.

  2. 2

    Revenue for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was KRW 26.6 billion, down 4.5% year on year, with operating income of KRW 15 billion and an operating margin of 56.2%.

  3. 3

    The fiscal year ended June 2026 posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.83 billion, swinging back into deficit. The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the third quarter of 2025 to the second quarter of 2026, is also KRW 1.83 billion.

  4. 4

    The liabilities-to-equity ratio stood at 380.0% at the end of the fiscal year ended June 2026, compared with 267.3% for the fiscal year ended June 2023. Total liabilities were KRW 728.5 billion against total equity of KRW 191.7 billion.

  5. 5

    Operating cash flow for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was KRW 8.1 billion, higher than KRW 2.19 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024. The movement of operating cash flow alongside operating income indicates the direction of the rental cash-generating capacity.

02

Business structure

NH All-One REIT is a real estate investment company presented as the commercial property investment platform of the NH Nonghyup group, listed on KOSPI in November 2021.

Its assets are held through a mother-child REIT structure, in which the company owns 100% of the child REIT (JaRI) and therefore holds the voting rights on major decisions. The strategy is core-plus, centered on mid-sized offices in core Seoul-area districts, logistics hubs, and assets used by the Nonghyup group.

The portfolio page lists Bundang Square (including the Vision World parking lot, office occupancy 100%), the Doji logistics center (logistics, occupancy 96%), N Square (retail, occupancy 100%), the Hite-Jinro Seocho headquarters building (held by NH9 REIT, office, occupancy 100%), and NH Nonghyup Tower (held by NH9 REIT).

Bundang Square is a sectionally owned asset in which the company holds the office section and a fund holds the retail section.

A-One Tower Dangsan, an office in Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul, was acquired for about KRW 130.3 billion in 2020 and was occupied by a Samsung Life Insurance group affiliate; its sale has since been completed.

The regional assets A-One Tower Geumnam-ro and A-One Tower Gwangju Station were sold in 2025, and the Suwon Ingye building was also disposed of.

D-Tower Donuimun is acquired through a share of beneficiary certificates held by NH9 REIT, and Etoday and Edaily reported that DL Group accounts for 75% of tenants and NH Nonghyup Bank for 14%.

Compared with Lotte REIT, which is centered on retail, NH All-One REIT is characterized by a multi-sector mix of offices, logistics, and retail.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on the disclosed figures, revenue fell from KRW 20.5 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to KRW 19.3 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024, down 5.9% year on year, then rose to KRW 27.9 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025, up 44.5%, before declining to KRW 26.6 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026, down 4.5%.

Among the fiscal years ended June 2023 through June 2026, the largest was the fiscal year ended June 2025 and the smallest was the fiscal year ended June 2024.

Operating income was KRW 12.5 billion, KRW 10.3 billion (down 17.4% year on year), KRW 18.8 billion (up 82.2%), and KRW 15 billion (down 20.2%) across the same four fiscal years. The operating margin moved from 60.8% to 53.4%, then up to 67.3%, and back down to 56.2%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 1.98 billion to a net loss of KRW 1.05 billion, which was a swing into deficit, then to a profit of KRW 1.39 billion, which was a swing back into profit, and finally to a net loss of KRW 1.83 billion, which was another swing into deficit.

Net income including non-controlling interests followed the same direction, and the gap from controlling-shareholder net income was small. In the fiscal year ended June 2025, operating income rose sharply but net income was only KRW 1.39 billion, so the gap between operating income and net income was wide.

Etoday reported that the Hite-Jinro Seocho headquarters acquisition was funded by borrowing, so this gap should be read alongside financing costs. The concrete amount of financing costs is not estimated here.

Revenue increased in the fiscal year ended June 2025, and Etoday reported that beneficiary certificates in the Hite-Jinro headquarters and the NH Nonghyup Tower were added in the same period. The decline in revenue for the fiscal year ended June 2026 may reflect lower rental income after regional asset disposals.

Operating cash flow was KRW 5.28 billion, KRW 2.19 billion, KRW 7.71 billion, and KRW 8.1 billion, rising through the fiscal year ended June 2026. The liabilities-to-equity ratio rose from 267.3% and 254.6% to 360.1% and 380.0%.

Over the last four quarters, from the third quarter of 2025 to the second quarter of 2026, the cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.83 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Listed REITs in Korea are sensitive to asset prices and interest rate trends, and Etoday reported that REITs that sold buildings are dividing between reinvestment, debt repayment, and liquidation.

In its first-half 2023 IR, NH All-One REIT cited market uncertainty during a rising-rate environment and made strengthening operating stability a key strategy. The company's IR materials describe prime offices in core Seoul districts as characterized by scarcity and low vacancy.

In line with this, the company is disposing of regional assets and reshaping the portfolio around the CBD and GBD districts, according to Etoday. Market observers read the rebalancing as reducing the share of regional assets and raising the share of Seoul business-district assets.

Funding new assets from proceeds of existing asset sales, rather than through large rights offerings, is also noted as a feature. In competition, compared with large listed REITs such as SK REIT and Lotte REIT, NH All-One REIT has a smaller asset base and more diversified use mix.

Competition for Seoul office assets among REIT managers continues, so purchase prices and lease terms can determine returns. Because the structure relies heavily on borrowing, changes in interest rates and borrowing terms directly affect the earnings structure.

06

Outlook

The company has said it plans to use the proceeds of the Dangsan sale to repay borrowings at the mother REIT, lowering the portfolio loan-to-value ratio from 63.4% to 60.5%. The gain on sale is earmarked for a special dividend, which Etoday reported is expected to be paid in March 2027.

The remaining lease term on the Hite-Jinro Seocho headquarters runs to June 28, 2032, and an official at the NH Nonghyup REIT asset manager said the company plans value-add measures such as rent increases and conversion.

The same official said the company would set aside about KRW 8 billion over the seven years of the remaining lease term to fund those measures.

D-Tower Donuimun is structured as an investment of about KRW 30 billion in beneficiary certificates through NH9 REIT, and NH9 REIT's total investment cost was reported at KRW 291 billion.

At the annual general meeting held on March 24, 2026, the business plan and borrowing plan for the 14th and 15th terms were approved as proposed. Some observers view the Dangsan sale as closing the current asset rebalancing cycle.

The next points to check are whether the loan-to-value ratio actually declines after the repayment, whether the special dividend is paid as expected, and how quickly the rent increases and use conversion at the Seocho building progress. New acquisitions may be constrained by sale proceeds and borrowing capacity.

08

Bull factors

Asset rebalancing and realized sale gains

A-One Tower Dangsan was sold for KRW 163 billion, about 25% above its 2020 purchase price of KRW 130.3 billion. The gain is earmarked for a special dividend, while sale proceeds are also used to repay borrowings. Reducing regional assets in favor of core Seoul-district assets is cited as a positive factor.

High operating margin and rising operating cash flow

The operating margin for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was 56.2%, remaining in the mid-50s or higher since the fiscal year ended June 2023. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 5.28 billion to KRW 8.1 billion through the fiscal year ended June 2026. The margin did not collapse even in the year revenue declined, which can be seen as a strength.

Share price below book value

The share price sits below 1x book value, meaning it trades under the book net asset value. SPI has described NH All-One REIT as having one of the wider gaps between net asset value and share price. The bull-side argument is that the gap could narrow if the rebalancing and special dividend are executed.

09

Bear factors

Recurring net losses

The shift from profit in the prior year to a loss is the core burden in this item. The cumulative figure over the last four quarters is also KRW 1.83 billion. Despite operating income of KRW 15 billion, a net loss was recorded, indicating that costs below the operating line are eroding profit.

Financial burden from higher borrowing

The liabilities-to-equity ratio rose from 254.6% in the fiscal year ended June 2024 to 380.0% in the fiscal year ended June 2026. At the end of June 2026, total liabilities were KRW 728.5 billion against total equity of KRW 191.7 billion.

With a high borrowing share, worsening interest rates or refinancing terms could reduce both earnings and distribution capacity.

Revenue decline and reinvestment execution risk

Revenue for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was KRW 26.6 billion, down 4.5% year on year. Regional asset disposals may have reduced rental income. If reinvestment in Seoul assets and value-add work at the Seocho building do not proceed as planned, the revenue recovery could be delayed.

10

Risk factors

Financial and interest rate

The liabilities-to-equity ratio is 380.0%, with total liabilities of KRW 728.5 billion far exceeding total equity of KRW 191.7 billion. Reports based on the April 2025 IR cited a weighted average borrowing rate of about 4.2%. If rates remain high or refinancing terms worsen, interest costs feed directly into net income.

Tenant and use concentration

The Hite-Jinro Seocho headquarters is currently a master-leased asset, and the company has reportedly considered converting it to other uses to prepare for a tenant departure. D-Tower Donuimun has a high tenant concentration, with DL Group at 75%, and DL Group is reported to be relocating to the Magok district. The departure of one or two large tenants could significantly affect vacancy and income.

Dilution and earnings durability

A shareholder rights offering of about KRW 37 billion was carried out in 2025, increasing the share count. With net losses attributable to controlling shareholders continuing over the last four quarters, earnings-based distribution capacity is limited. Because special dividends depend on the timing of asset sales, a regular distribution flow is not assured.

Overall view

NH All-One REIT is reshaping its portfolio by selling regional assets and moving toward core Seoul-district offices. A-One Tower Dangsan was sold for KRW 163 billion, above its purchase price, and the gain is earmarked for a special dividend.

The operating margin of 56.2% for the fiscal year ended June 2026 is high, and operating cash flow has risen.

On the other hand, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.83 billion for that fiscal year, and the same cumulative amount over the last four quarters, show that earnings have not yet stabilized.

The rise in the liabilities-to-equity ratio to 380.0% increases sensitivity to interest rates and refinancing terms. With the price-to-book ratio below 1x, the next data points are whether the rebalancing is executed, the special dividend is paid, and the loan-to-value ratio improves.

This report presents strengths and weaknesses with equal weight and does not offer an investment opinion or target price.
