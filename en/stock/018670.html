Report overview

SK GAS: Power and LNG Added to an LPG Core

SK GAS is broadening its earnings base from LPG distribution into power generation, LNG terminals and trading, and its KRW 442.8 billion operating profit in 2025 was the highest of 2022–2025.

    Operating profit rose 54.2% to KRW 442.8 billion in 2025, and the operating margin climbed from 4.0% in 2024 to 5.8%.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

    Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders bottomed at KRW 178.8 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 235.2 billion in 2025; the last four quarters total KRW 400.1 billion.

  5. 5

02

Business structure

SK GAS started from LPG distribution and has expanded into LNG-fired power generation, an LNG terminal, overseas trading and propane dehydrogenation (PDH). LPG sales in 2025 were KRW 6.92 trillion, up 0.1%, while power sales reached KRW 760.1 billion, up 309.9%, according to a February 2026 press report.

LPG remains the bulk of revenue, and domestic LPG volume fell 14.8% in 2025 to 3.03 million tons while overseas volume rose 14.6% to 4.63 million tons. The company attributed the overseas growth to trading that took advantage of volatile global LPG market conditions.

The core of the power segment is its 1,212MW Ulsan plant, a dual-fuel facility that can run on LNG or LPG depending on fuel cost. The company sold a 49% stake in that plant to a domestic infrastructure investor for KRW 1.224 trillion in cash, with the transfer date disclosed as June 30, 2026.

The PDH business has been run by the PDH subsidiary SK Advanced, which took a 25% stake from the Kuwait PIC in February 2026, lifting SK GAS's holding from 45% to 70%, and completed its merger into SK GAS in early November 2026.

The LNG terminal business sits with Korea Energy Terminal (KET), in which SK GAS holds 47.6%, and KET's sales rose from KRW 28.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 105.9 billion in 2025. Competitively, SK GAS and E1 are described as splitting the domestic LPG distribution market, and both run overseas trading subsidiaries.

04

Earnings analysis

Sales moved from KRW 8.07 trillion in 2022 to KRW 6.99 trillion in 2023, a 13.3% decline, before rising 1.5% to KRW 7.1 trillion in 2024 and 8.2% to KRW 7.68 trillion in 2025, which is two consecutive years of growth.

Operating profit fell from KRW 390.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 303.6 billion in 2023 (down 22.2%) and KRW 287.2 billion in 2024 (down 5.4%), then rose 54.2% to KRW 442.8 billion in 2025, the largest year of 2022–2025.

The operating margin over the same period went from 4.8% and 4.3% to 4.0% in 2024 and 5.8% in 2025, so profit grew faster than sales in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was highest in 2023 at KRW 316.3 billion, lowest in 2024 at KRW 178.8 billion (down 43.5%), and recovered to KRW 235.2 billion in 2025 (up 31.5%). Net profit including non-controlling interests, a different figure, was KRW 233.8 billion in 2025.

At year-end 2025, total liabilities were KRW 4.89 trillion and total equity KRW 3.14 trillion, of which controlling shareholders' equity was KRW 2.93 trillion. Operating cash flow was KRW 140.1 billion in 2022, KRW 617.2 billion in 2023, KRW 373.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 672.6 billion in 2025.

Quarterly results are more volatile: operating profit of KRW 173.5 billion in Q3 2025 fell to KRW 35.8 billion in Q4 (down 79.4% from the prior quarter), rebounded to KRW 227.7 billion in Q1 2026, then turned into an operating loss of KRW 43.9 billion in Q2.

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 147.7 billion turned positive from the prior quarter but fell 14.9% year on year, while sales rose 39.0% to KRW 2.71 trillion.

In the preliminary release dated November 4, 2026, LPG operating profit was KRW 103.7 billion, up KRW 12.4 billion year on year, while power operating profit was KRW 44.0 billion, down KRW 38.2 billion, and Ulsan plant power sales fell 20.6% to 1,298 GWh.

Pre-tax profit of KRW 285.8 billion exceeded operating profit in Q3, which one brokerage attributed to large derivative gains.

05

Industry analysis

The LPG distribution business is structured so that its profit swings with the timing gap between international prices and domestic selling prices.

In Q1 2026, international LPG import costs surged, but government price-stability policy limited domestic supply price increases, and both SK GAS and E1 absorbed margin pressure, according to a Todays Energy analysis.

Yuanta Securities had forecast that the LPG segment would return to profit in Q3, and the preliminary Q3 release showed LPG operating profit of KRW 103.7 billion. iM Securities expects LPG prices to weaken as oil falls while LNG stays firm on supply concerns after damage to Qatar's liquefaction facilities.

In that setting, iM Securities argues SK GAS can benefit from both higher trading volumes in LPG and gains from the price spread between LNG and LPG. Power generation depends on the system marginal price (SMP), fuel costs and plant utilization, and the Q3 2026 SMP was KRW 133.1 per kWh, up KRW 16.2.

The domestic LPG market is split between SK GAS and E1, and credit rating agencies view SK GAS as keeping a dominant position in that market.

Petrochemical demand is also a variable: in Q3 2025, halted operation of a DH1 line at major petrochemical customers reduced domestic LPG sales, which the rating side attributed to a weak petrochemical cycle.

PDH profitability depends on the spread between propane and propylene prices, plant utilization and production costs. E1 relies more heavily on domestic LPG distribution and is more exposed to international prices and price policy, while SK GAS has diversified toward LNG, power, terminals and trading.

06

Outlook

At its CEO investor day in October 2026, the company framed 2027–2030 as a period of growth investment, with new-business profits expected to be fully reflected from 2031.

It set a target of KRW 450 billion in average annual pre-tax profit, and according to the company, average annual pre-tax profit rose from roughly KRW 100 billion in 2015–2019 to the KRW 300 billion range in 2020–2025.

New business areas cited include power supply for AI-driven demand and investment in North American gas fields, along with a review of upstream investment opportunities and expansion of overseas customers. The company said it will balance growth investment with shareholder returns.

In an October 2026 report, Yuanta Securities projected the power segment's 2027 operating profit at KRW 241.8 billion, up 54.5% from 2026.

A credit rating agency said in June 2026 that the large power investment cycle has ended and expected capital expenditure over the next five years to total about KRW 1 trillion, roughly half of past spending.

Press reports in May 2026 said the market expects proceeds of KRW 1.2242 trillion from the Ulsan plant stake sale to be used for debt repayment, new investment and shareholder returns. A 1st tank at KET, which SK GAS operates, is scheduled for completion in the first half of 2027, according to a February 2026 report.

Yuanta Securities identified three key variables: whether the pressure from international LPG price swings eases, whether power-segment growth shows up in actual results, and whether the shareholder return policy is made concrete.

08

Bull factors

Power and LNG becoming a second earnings pillar

Power generation posted KRW 158.8 billion in operating profit in 2025, up 561.7%, making it a pillar of the earnings mix. Yuanta Securities said in an October 2026 report that it expects the power segment's 2027 operating profit to rise 54.5% from 2026 to KRW 241.8 billion.

KET's sales also climbed from KRW 28.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 105.9 billion in 2025. The company has said it plans to expand LNG and reduce the weight of LPG.

Overseas trading and volume growth

Overseas LPG sales rose 14.6% in 2025 to 4.63 million tons, and total sales rose 0.8% to 7.66 million tons. A company official said the overseas increase came from trading that used volatile global LPG market conditions.

In a November 2026 report, iM Securities said the combination of weaker LPG and firmer LNG prices could expand trading volumes and create optionality gains. LPG segment operating profit in Q3 2026 was KRW 103.7 billion, up KRW 12.4 billion year on year.

Financial stability and cash proceeds

Domestic credit rating agencies kept SK GAS's senior unsecured bonds at AA-/Stable in June 2026. The 49% stake sale in the Ulsan plant brings in about KRW 1.2242 trillion in cash, which the market expects to be used for debt repayment, new investment and shareholder returns.

A rating agency estimates capital expenditure over the next five years at about KRW 1 trillion, roughly half of past spending. The debt ratio was 155.4% at year-end 2025, lower than in 2024.

09

Bear factors

LPG margin timing and quarterly losses

Q2 2026 brought an operating loss of KRW 43.9 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 36.5 billion, a swing into loss. Operating profit in Q4 2025 was KRW 35.8 billion, down 79.4% from the prior quarter.

Yuanta Securities attributed the volatility in LPG-segment profits to international price increases being reflected late in domestic selling prices. Government price-stability policy, which limited supply price increases, adds to the same pressure.

Lower power volumes and bid-rate decline

Ulsan plant power sales in Q3 2026 were 1,298 GWh, down 20.6% year on year. Power-segment operating profit was KRW 44.0 billion, down KRW 38.2 billion (preliminary release, November 4, 2026). The system price rose KRW 16.2 to KRW 133.1 per kWh, but a lower bid rate reduced sales volume. Power profit therefore did not track the price increase.

Reliance on derivative gains and Q4 reversal risk

Q3 2026 pre-tax profit of KRW 285.8 billion was explained by large derivative gains (iM Securities). The same firm expects part of those gains to reverse in Q4, leading to unavoidable derivative losses.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 43.5% in 2024 and recovered 31.5% in 2025, showing how much annual earnings can move with external variables.

10

Risk factors

International prices and derivative volatility

The gap between LPG import costs and selling prices, and the timing of physical and derivative gains, can swing quarterly results. A rating agency also noted that profit volatility exists as LPG import costs and selling prices move. Part of the Q3 2026 derivative gains could reverse in Q4, which may widen the swings.

Policy and domestic demand

Government price-stability policy has limited increases in domestic LPG supply prices. Domestic sales fell 14.8% in 2025 to 3.03 million tons, and halted operation at petrochemical customers reduced domestic sales. Structural demand shifts, including a decline in LPG vehicles, are also cited as a longer-term variable.

Portfolio restructuring and investment execution

Restructuring is under way, with the 49% sale of the Ulsan plant stake and the merger of the PDH subsidiary. The company treats 2027–2030 as a growth investment phase and expects new-business profits to be fully reflected from 2031, so profit contribution in the interim may be limited. Balancing growth investment with shareholder returns remains a challenge.

12

Overall view

SK GAS is moving from an LPG-centered model toward a diversified energy company that adds power generation, an LNG terminal and trading. Its 2025 operating profit of KRW 442.8 billion was the highest of 2022–2025, and the operating margin rose to 5.8%.

However, Q2 2026 brought an operating loss of KRW 43.9 billion, and Q3 turned back to operating profit of KRW 147.7 billion, which still fell 14.9% year on year. Q3 pre-tax profit exceeded operating profit because of large derivative gains, so both earnings quality and quarterly volatility need to be watched.

Bullish factors include the power and LNG earnings base, growing overseas trading, the AA-/Stable credit rating and KRW 1.2242 trillion in cash from the stake sale.

Bearish factors include the timing gap between international LPG prices and domestic selling prices, government price policy, lower power sales volume and delayed profit contribution during the investment phase.

The target prices cited above were issued in October and November 2026, so the date and each firm's assumptions should be checked alongside them. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual report due by March 31, 2027, and completion of KET's 1st tank in the first half of 2027.
