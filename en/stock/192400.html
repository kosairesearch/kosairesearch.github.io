Report overview

A rice-cooker holding company between export growth and domestic slowdown

Cuckoo Holdings posted its best year on record in 2025 on rice-cooker exports, but third-quarter 2026 operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders both fell from a year earlier.

  1. 1

    Consolidated 2025 sales reached KRW 920.9 billion (up 10.4%) and operating profit reached KRW 114 billion (up 10.4%), the largest year in the 2022–2025 window. News1 reported the result as the company's best ever.

  2. 2

    Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 245.2 billion, up 0.8% from a year earlier, while operating profit fell 10.8% to KRW 27 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 66.0% to KRW 12.9 billion.

  3. 3

    Cumulative operating profit for January–September 2026 rose 2.4% to KRW 80.1 billion according to press reports citing the quarterly filing, so the cumulative and third-quarter trends diverge.

  4. 4

    Exports drove growth: rice-cooker exports rose 25.1% to KRW 191.3 billion in 2025, while domestic rice-cooker sales were reported to have declined for a second year.

  5. 5

    Earnings come mainly from Cuckoo Electronics (100% owned), while rental is handled by listed subsidiary Cuckoo Homesys (40.6% stake). The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report.

02

Business structure

Cuckoo Holdings is a holding company that owns stakes in subsidiaries and oversees their businesses. On December 1, 2017, its rental division was spun off as Cuckoo Homesys, its appliance division became Cuckoo Electronics, and the remaining company was renamed Cuckoo Holdings.

The holding company's own income comes from trademark royalties, management consulting fees and dividends, and it is small in scale. Substantive earnings come from Cuckoo Electronics, a wholly owned subsidiary that sells IH pressure rice cookers, hot-plate pressure rice cookers and electric warming rice cookers.

Reports put 2025 sales of the three main rice-cooker categories at KRW 658.3 billion, with IH pressure cookers at KRW 442.5 billion, hot-plate pressure cookers at KRW 121.9 billion and warming cookers at KRW 93.9 billion (News1 basis).

Other product sales rose 20.6% to KRW 224.7 billion in 2025, as the company widened its range to induction cooktops and dishwashers. Reports put 2025 revenue at 77% domestic and 23% overseas.

Rental is run by Cuckoo Homesys, a listed subsidiary in which the company holds a 40.6% stake, and reports said its cumulative rental accounts passed 3 million in August 2025.

Reports say the company holds more than 70% of the domestic electric rice-cooker market and competes with domestic rice-cooker rivals and with smaller, premium product lines.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show sales and profit rising together. Sales went from KRW 755.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 772.3 billion in 2023 (up 2.2%), KRW 833.8 billion in 2024 (up 8.0%) and KRW 920.9 billion in 2025 (up 10.4%), rising three years in a row from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 87.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 86.6 billion in 2023 (down 1.4%), then rose to KRW 103.2 billion in 2024 (up 19.2%) and KRW 114 billion in 2025 (up 10.4%), growing two years in a row from 2024 through 2025.

The operating margin rose from 11.6% in 2022 and 11.2% in 2023 to 12.4% in 2024 and held at 12.4% in 2025. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 116.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 145.6 billion in 2025, growing three years in a row from 2023.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 137 billion in 2024 and KRW 145.2 billion in 2025, slightly below the controlling-shareholder figure, which means the non-controlling share was a small loss.

Operating cash flow peaked at KRW 100.4 billion in 2023 and then fell to KRW 78.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 56.9 billion in 2025, so profit growth and cash generation moved apart.

The debt ratio rose from 14.5% in 2024 to 20.5% in 2025, and total liabilities increased from KRW 173.5 billion at the end of 2024 to KRW 271.3 billion at the end of 2025.

Quarterly profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 38 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 54.2 billion in the fourth quarter (up 42.4% on the quarter), then KRW 39.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 27.2%) and KRW 43.4 billion in the second quarter (up 10.0%), before falling to KRW 12.9 billion in the third quarter (down 70.2%).

In the third quarter of 2026 operating profit fell 10.8% while profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 66.0%, which points to non-operating items or taxes as a possible factor. The next quarterly filing's income-statement breakdown is the place to check this. The last four quarters total KRW 149.9 billion of profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

05

Industry analysis

Reports put Cuckoo's share of the domestic electric rice-cooker market above 70%, so domestic rice-cooker demand feeds directly into core earnings. A February 2026 report said domestic rice-cooker sales had declined for a second year.

The same report put the 2025 third-quarter revenue mix at about 80% domestic and 20% export. Rice-cooker exports, by contrast, rose 25.1% to KRW 191.3 billion in 2025 from KRW 152.9 billion a year earlier, and cookers made up more than 90% of the KRW 211.3 billion total export figure.

Commentary credits the growth to stepped-up marketing in China, the United States and Vietnam and to recipe-function cookers tailored to local tastes. Product differentiation rests on extending IH heating technology from rice cookers to induction cooktops and on a compact line for households of three or fewer.

In appliances, the company competes with domestic rice-cooker makers and, in induction cooktops and dishwashers, with other home-appliance brands. In rental, Cuckoo Homesys competes with dedicated rental operators such as water purifier providers.

Research and development spending fell 24% from KRW 7.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 5.7 billion in 2025, and one report noted that the R&D-to-sales ratio has declined for three years even as sales grew.

06

Outlook

Cumulative sales for January–September 2026 rose 6.5% to KRW 717.9 billion, and operating profit rose 2.4% to KRW 80.1 billion. By quarter, operating profit recovered in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 31.1 billion, up 41.6% from the first quarter, but fell 13.1% to KRW 27 billion in the third quarter.

The next checkpoints are whether operating profit regains the second-quarter level and whether the pattern of controlling-shareholder profit falling much faster than operating profit continues.

Overseas, the company returned to IFA 2025, Europe's largest home-appliance show, after four years and showed 50 product lines, more than double the 21 shown in 2021. Whether rice-cooker export growth can offset the domestic slowdown is the central variable.

In rental, Cuckoo Homesys was reported to have passed 3 million cumulative rental accounts in August 2025. On shareholder returns, a three-year dividend policy was announced in 2024, and the 2025 year-end dividend totaling KRW 48.1 billion was approved at the annual general meeting on March 24, 2026.

A 2025 report mentioned plans for a listing of Cuckoo Homesys's Malaysian unit, and the progress of that plan should be checked in later disclosures. Whether funds raised through exchangeable bonds backed by treasury shares are directed to growth investment is also a focus.

Bull factors

Exports as a growth pillar

Rice-cooker exports reached KRW 191.3 billion in 2025, up 25.1%, and rice cookers made up more than 90% of the KRW 211.3 billion total export figure. Stepped-up marketing in China, the United States and Vietnam, plus recipe-function cookers tailored to local tastes, are credited for the gain. Overseas sales were reported at 23% of 2025 revenue, making exports a second pillar of growth.

Earnings base and balance sheet capacity

Operating profit grew from KRW 87.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 114 billion in 2025, and the operating margin held at 12.4% in 2024 and 2025. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose three years in a row through 2025, and the last four quarters total KRW 149.9 billion. Total equity stood at KRW 1.32 trillion at the end of 2025.

Expanding shareholder returns

A three-year dividend policy was announced in 2024, and the 2025 year-end dividend totaling KRW 48.1 billion was approved at the March 24, 2026 annual general meeting.

NH Investment & Securities said in a first-half 2026 report that it lowered its target price but that wider dividends and possible treasury-share cancellation could support the share price.

09

Bear factors

Stalling domestic rice-cooker demand

Domestic rice-cooker sales were reported to have declined for a second year. In the third quarter of 2025, domestic sales made up about 80% of revenue and rice cookers made up 69.53% of sales, so a domestic slowdown passes directly into group results.

With a share above 70% of the domestic market, the company has less room to offset domestic weakness through other channels.

Third-quarter 2026 profit slowdown

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 10.8% to KRW 27 billion, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 66.0% to KRW 12.9 billion. First-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 22 billion, down 38.5% on the quarter, and cumulative operating profit growth for January–September was only 2.4%. Large swings between quarters limit how stable the earnings flow has been.

Slower investment, cash flow and leverage

R&D spending fell 24% from KRW 7.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 5.7 billion in 2025, and operating cash flow fell from KRW 100.4 billion in 2023 to KRW 56.9 billion in 2025. The debt ratio rose to 20.5% in 2025 from 14.5% in 2024, and total liabilities increased from KRW 173.5 billion to KRW 271.3 billion. Controversy has also arisen over exchangeable bonds issued using treasury shares.

10

Risk factors

Overseas sales and currency

With overseas sales at 23% of revenue, local competition in China, the United States and Vietnam and currency moves become key variables. A stronger won can reduce the won value of overseas sales. Whether export strength continues depends on local demand and price competition.

Holding structure and subsidiary dependence

Holding-company results depend on the performance of subsidiaries such as Cuckoo Electronics and Cuckoo Homesys. The 40.6% stake in Cuckoo Homesys is below a majority, so subsidiary dividend and capital policies feed directly into the holding company's capacity to pay dividends. If subsidiary earnings slow, the funds available for dividends could shrink too.

Ownership concentration and capital policy

As of March 23, 2026, Gu Bon-hak and three related parties held 64.86% and treasury shares accounted for 12.61%. Exchangeable bonds backed by treasury shares have raised questions about their effect on shareholder value and the use of proceeds. Whether the funds raised translate into actual investment is the next point to check.

12

Overall view

Cuckoo Holdings showed a multi-year pattern in which sales, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders were all larger in 2025 than in 2022.

Consolidated 2025 sales of KRW 920.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 114 billion reflect a business mix that includes rice-cooker exports and subsidiary rental. Rice-cooker exports rose 25.1% to KRW 191.3 billion, and whether that growth can offset the domestic rice-cooker decline is the key question.

In the third quarter of 2026 sales rose while operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell, so earnings quality and non-operating items need to be read together. On the balance sheet, the debt ratio rose to 20.5% in 2025 against total equity of KRW 1.32 trillion and warrants checking.

Shareholder returns combine a rising dividend trend, treasury shares at 12.61%, and the exchangeable bond controversy, so the direction of capital policy matters. Valuation can be read only as factual comparisons of earnings and net assets.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 preliminary full-year results, the operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report, and the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report.
