02

Business structure

Itcencore is an IT company centered on ERP and internal accounting solutions for the construction sector, and it is listed on KONEX. Its business history is recorded at 22 years, and its headcount was 121 as of August 2026 according to TheVC.

According to the FnGuide company profile, the business is expanding beyond the construction sector. Its product lines fall into four categories, EVC, ITO, ERP and ESG, and by FnGuide's 2024 revenue mix, EVC accounted for 37.57%, ITO 27.87%, ERP 20.30% and ESG 14.26%.

In 2023, EVC stood at 38.92% and ERP at 28.53%, but by 2024 the ITO share had grown from 18.98% to 27.87%, while the ERP share fell to 20.30%. The cost of sales ratio rose from 72.88% in 2022 to 80.84% in 2024.

In the competitive landscape, large SI providers such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever lead major projects, and Itcencore is considerably smaller in scale. Its FY2025 revenue of roughly KRW 29 billion is modest compared with these large SI operators. Segment shares follow the product classification used by FnGuide.

03

Recent trends

Based on the FY2025 standalone results (22nd fiscal year), revenue reached KRW 29.24 billion, up 4.64% from 2024, according to recent reports. Operating profit was KRW 564 million, up from KRW 400 million in 2024, with an operating margin of 1.93%. Net income of KRW 45.54 billion was far larger than operating profit.

Total assets were KRW 9.83 billion, total liabilities KRW 3.02 billion and total equity KRW 6.81 billion as disclosed. On a 2024 standalone basis, the company posted revenue of KRW 27.9 billion, operating profit of KRW 400 million and a net loss of KRW 6.7 billion, a return to loss-making.

In 2023, net income was KRW 11.3 billion, underscoring large swings in annual earnings. Financial stability indicators improved: the debt ratio fell from 108.64% in 2024 to 44.32% in 2025, and the equity ratio rose from 47.93% to 69.29%.

The share price was KRW 13,960 on March 26, 2026, and the current price as of November 11, 2026 was KRW 11,980 (up 4.26% from the previous day), with a market capitalization of about KRW 34.7 billion. The composition of FY2025 net income remains an item to confirm in the annual report notes.

04

Outlook

The outlook for earnings will likely depend on how much the core operating margin improves. Revenue rose in both 2024 and 2025, but the growth rate slowed from 8.57% to 4.64%. How the shift toward ITO and away from ERP affects the margin structure is a key point to monitor.

If net income relied on non-operating items, whether a similar amount recurs in FY2026 will be the central question. Quarterly reports filed after the first quarter of 2026 will show the gap between operating profit and net income. FY2026 results will be disclosed in the annual report filed in March 2027.

The lower debt ratio can serve as a financial buffer. Management changes recorded in August 2025 and January 2026 make management continuity and strategic direction important items to track. Given KONEX trading characteristics, the share price may be sensitive to supply and demand factors beyond earnings.

In general, IT demand from public and construction sectors is linked to budget allocation and construction activity, so related indicators are worth tracking alongside company results.

05

Bull factors

Improved balance sheet

The debt ratio fell from 108.64% in 2024 to 44.32% in 2025 (JobKorea financial data). Over the same period, total equity rose from KRW 22.6 billion to about KRW 68.1 billion, and the equity ratio climbed from 47.93% to 69.29%.

The equity increase appears linked to the net income inflow, and reduced reliance on borrowing gives the company a financial buffer.

Two years of revenue growth and profit

Revenue rose from KRW 25.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 27.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 29.2 billion in 2025, growing in both 2024 and 2025. Operating profit increased from KRW 400 million in 2024 to KRW 564 million in 2025, keeping the company in the black. Operating results weakened in 2024 relative to 2023 but recovered in 2025.

Expanding ITO share

On FnGuide's classification, the ITO share of revenue rose from 18.98% in 2023 to 27.87% in 2024. The company profile describes an effort to expand its market beyond construction.

ITO generally includes recurring operations and maintenance revenue, so the diversification of the product mix can be viewed as a stability factor.

06

Bear factors

Thin core profitability

The operating margin fell from 6.58% in 2023 to 1.27% in 2024 and stayed at 1.93% in 2025 (FnGuide and JobKorea data). The company posted a net loss of KRW 6.7 billion in 2024. The cost of sales ratio rose from 72.88% in 2022 to 80.84% in 2024, indicating higher cost pressure.

Large gap between net income and operating profit

FY2025 net income of KRW 45.54 billion far exceeded operating profit of KRW 564 million. The composition of the non-operating items behind this gap, and whether they are one-off, must be checked in the annual report notes. If the source does not recur, core profitability remains at the operating margin level.

Slowing growth and smaller ERP share

The revenue growth rate slowed from 8.57% in 2024 to 4.64% in 2025 (JobKorea financial data). The share of ERP, once a core product, fell from 28.53% in 2023 to 20.30% in 2024. With growth moderating and the core product share shrinking, the source of future revenue momentum remains an open issue.

Risk factors

Macro

Domestic interest rates and economic conditions can delay IT investment by private companies. In a business with significant public-sector demand, changes in budget allocation and execution timing can affect when revenue is recognized. A slowdown in construction activity can also weigh on demand for construction-sector ERP.

Liquidity and market

Given the limited trading volume typical of KONEX, share price volatility can be high. The share price moved from KRW 13,960 on March 26, 2026 to KRW 11,980 as of November 11, 2026 (up 4.26% from the previous day).

With a market capitalization of about KRW 34.7 billion, the stock can react sensitively to small changes in supply and demand.

Management and governance

Disclosure records show a non-executive director appointment and an inside director resignation in August 2025, and a CEO change in January 2026. Frequent management changes can raise uncertainty about strategic continuity. Governance and related-party transactions should be checked in the related-party section of the annual report.

08

Overall view

Itcencore combines positive signals in FY2025, including revenue growth, operating profit in the black and an improved balance sheet, with challenges such as a thin operating margin and a large gap between net income and operating profit.

On the bullish side, the lower debt ratio and larger equity base stand out for financial stability. On the bearish side, the operating margin remains below 2%, which limits core profitability.

Whether the composition of the KRW 45.5 billion net income is sustainable can only be judged after reviewing the annual report notes and subsequent quarterly disclosures. The slowing revenue growth and smaller ERP share are also factors that can be viewed neutrally.

As a KONEX-listed company, liquidity and information access may be constrained. This summary is a qualitative overview based on disclosures and reports for informational purposes only, and it does not express a view on the direction of the share price. Based on the data currently available, bullish and bearish factors are roughly balanced.
