02

Business structure

Itcencore was founded on September 30, 2004 and has been listed on the KONEX market since October 31, 2016. Its listing date was October 31, 2016. It belongs to the ITCEN group and is headquartered in Gwacheon, Gyeonggi Province. FnGuide describes it as a leading player in the ERP and internal accounting control solutions market.

In 2024, EVC accounted for 37.57% of revenue, ITO 27.87%, ERP 20.30% and ESG 14.26%. EVC is the company's own segment label, and its detailed contents could not be confirmed in this research. Maintenance made up 48.08% of revenue in 2022. A high maintenance share tends to make revenue more recurring.

In 2024, however, the cost-of-sales ratio was 80.84% and the SG&A ratio was 17.89%. Together these two ratios leave very little room for operating profit.

The newer growth line is a cloud-based HR platform aimed at small businesses and SMEs. The platform automates the process from clock-in records through attendance management to payroll calculation. Its scale and customer base differ from large listed IT services groups such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and POSCO DX Company. Headcount at the end of 2025 was 121.

03

Recent trends

On a 2025 standalone basis, revenue was KRW 29.236 billion, operating profit KRW 564 million and net income KRW 45.536 billion. Revenue is shown as up 4.6% from the 2024 level of about KRW 27.9 billion. In 2024, revenue was KRW 27.94 billion, operating profit KRW 353.8 million and the net loss KRW 6.68 billion.

The operating margin declined from 12.61% in 2022 to 6.58% in 2023 and 1.27% in 2024. Operating profit stayed very small relative to revenue in 2025, so a margin recovery has not yet been confirmed. The reason net income far exceeded operating profit could not be confirmed in this research.

In 2024, non-operating expenses were KRW 10.516 billion. Non-operating items appear to drive net income, but the 2025 breakdown needs to be checked in the annual report.

At the end of 2025, standalone total assets were KRW 98.343 billion, total liabilities KRW 30.2 billion and total equity KRW 68.143 billion. Equity in 2024 was KRW 22.6 billion. The share price was KRW 13,960 as of 4:10 p.m. on March 26, 2026. It was KRW 8,000 on July 9, 2026.

On October 27, 2026, the shares traded at KRW 8,200, up 7.47% from the previous day, for a market capitalization of KRW 23.7 billion. Trading value that day was KRW 12,280,080. Quarterly and half-year results for 2026 could not be confirmed in this research.

04

Outlook

The near-term focus is how quickly its HR platform spreads. The July 8, 2026 agreement aims to reduce HR and payroll burdens on SMEs as labor rules change, including tighter rules on comprehensive wage contracts.

Under the agreement, the partners plan to extend services so that payroll transfers, account inquiries and cash pooling can be used on one platform. KB Kookmin Bank said it is running a payroll-transfer promotion for platform users through the end of November 2026.

The number of subscribing companies and the revenue contribution were not confirmed, so the timing of any earnings impact is unclear.

The company is also widening its reach into AI-based internal control. According to recent reports, it held a seminar on operating internal controls with AI. In April 2026, the ITCEN group set up a joint affiliate booth at World IT Show to present AI-based solutions.

Conditions in the group's IT services arms appear to be improving. One group affiliate's standalone operating margin was 8.5% in the first half of 2026, holding at 8.0% or higher for four straight quarters. That figure belongs to a different company and does not guarantee Itcencore's results.

Itcencore's earnings depend on both operating improvement and non-operating factors. With the operating margin at around 1%, changes in the cost-of-sales and SG&A ratios need to be seen first. Valuation changes in its Itcenglobal stake may also affect net income and equity. The 2026 half-year and annual filings are the next items to check.

05

Bull factors

KB Kookmin Bank Tie-up and a New Platform

The core of the partnership is combining a non-financial platform with traditional banking services. The platform is a cloud-based tool that automates HR tasks such as clock-in records, attendance management and payroll calculation.

Adding the bank's payroll-transfer and cash-pooling functions could raise usage frequency among client companies. Tighter rules on comprehensive wage contracts, which raise SMEs' compliance burden, are also cited as a demand backdrop.

Stake Assets and a Light Debt Load

Standalone total liabilities of KRW 30.2 billion at the end of 2025 are less than half of total equity of KRW 68.143 billion. An investment data site records a 12.28% holding (2,849,314 shares) in Itcenglobal, displayed at a value of roughly KRW 71.8 billion.

That displayed value is larger than the KRW 23.7 billion market capitalization. The purpose of the holding and any pledge are unconfirmed, and the value moves with Itcenglobal's share price.

Recurring Revenue and the Group Backdrop

Maintenance accounted for 48.08% of revenue in 2022, giving the company a recurring revenue base. ERP, internal accounting control and ESG solutions are tied to companies' regulatory-compliance needs.

Itcenglobal's IT services segment posted an operating profit of KRW 11.1 billion in the first half of 2026, turning around from a KRW 3.1 billion operating loss a year earlier. Better conditions in the group's IT services can be read as a favorable backdrop for cooperation among affiliates.

06

Bear factors

Thin Operating Profitability

The operating margin fell from 12.61% in 2022 to 1.27% in 2024. The SG&A ratio rose from 11.10% in 2023 to 17.89% in 2024. Operating profit of KRW 564 million in 2025 is very small against revenue of KRW 29.236 billion. Until the new platform contributes to revenue, improvement in core earnings may be limited.

Volatile Net Income

Net income swung from KRW 11.3 billion in 2023 to a net loss of KRW 6.7 billion in 2024. In 2025, net income was reported at KRW 45.536 billion. The large gap with operating profit makes it hard to judge how sustainable earnings are.

Until the specific non-operating items are confirmed, net income has limited value as a gauge of operating performance.

Liquidity and Ownership Constraints

Trading value on October 27 was KRW 12,280,080, so thin trading can move transaction prices sharply. Major shareholders hold 37.05% and treasury shares 27.11%, while the 5%-plus shareholder stake fell to 5.20% as of January 14, 2026. Together the first two exceed 60%, which limits the volume available for trading. Headcount fell from 138 in 2024 to 121 in 2025.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Its customers are mainly small businesses and SMEs, so domestic demand and interest-rate changes can reduce IT spending. System-build and internal-control investments tend to be postponed in slowdowns. The cloud HR platform also depends on subscribers' headcount and business conditions. These external variables were not quantified in this research, so they are described qualitatively only.

The number of subscribing companies and the revenue contribution of the new platform are unconfirmed. Parts of the agreement, such as the financial-function integration, are planned services, so timing carries uncertainty. The HR and payroll software market has many vendors, creating competitive pressure. If regulation-driven demand weakens, adoption could slow.

The 2025 financial statements were approved with a 49.1% approval rate measured against total issued shares. Itcenglobal's share price fell from KRW 71,900 on March 12, 2026 to KRW 31,200 on June 19.

If the valuation of the Itcenglobal stake feeds into net income, affiliate share-price swings could translate into earnings volatility. Itcenglobal also faces a rise in potential conversion shares from convertible-bond repricing. The effect on Itcencore was not confirmed.

08

Overall view

Itcencore is a small KONEX-listed company built on ERP, internal accounting control and ESG solutions plus IT outsourcing. Its 2025 standalone revenue rose 4.6% to KRW 29.236 billion, but operating profit was only KRW 564 million.

Net income of KRW 45.536 billion diverges sharply from operating performance, so the quality of earnings needs scrutiny. The bull case rests on the HR and payroll platform partnership with KB Kookmin Bank, a light debt load and the Itcenglobal stake.

The bear case rests on a margin of around 1%, volatile net income, thin trading and limited free float. The operating profit trend and the breakdown of non-operating items in the 2026 half-year and annual filings are the checkpoints separating the two views. This report is for information only, and any investment judgment is left to the reader.
