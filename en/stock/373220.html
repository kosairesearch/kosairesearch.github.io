02

Business structure

LG Energy Solution runs two main lines: EV batteries (pouch and cylindrical) and energy storage systems (ESS). The company attributed its third-quarter 2026 sales growth mainly to a sharp rise in ESS shipments and volume growth in EV pouch and cylindrical products.

ESS shipments rose by more than 60% from the prior quarter, and ESS grew to the low-30s percent share of total sales.

The customer base is also broadening: alongside utility-scale grid projects, the company signed its first direct BESS supply contract with a big tech company, installed behind the meter to target AI data centers. It is the only Korean battery maker that NVIDIA has named a DSX Ready BESS partner.

In EVs, it signed new supply contracts to expand volumes of next-generation high-voltage mid-nickel cells for the European market.

Production is concentrated in North America: the Holland, Michigan plant began ESS mass production in June 2025, and the company bought the 49% stake in its Canadian joint venture NextStar Energy held by Stellantis, making it a wholly owned subsidiary.

In competitive terms, LG Energy Solution was the only Korean battery company on the BloombergNEF ESS Tier 1 list, while Samsung SDI and SK On were not included.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 25.6 trillion in 2022 to KRW 33.7 trillion in 2023 (up 31.8%), the peak year, then fell to KRW 25.6 trillion in 2024 (down 24.1%) and KRW 23.7 trillion in 2025 (down 7.6%), a two-year decline. 2025 was the smallest sales year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit was highest in 2023 at KRW 2.16 trillion and lowest in 2024 at KRW 575.4 billion (down 73.4%), before recovering to KRW 1.35 trillion in 2025 (up 134.0%). Operating margins were 6.4% in 2023, 2.2% in 2024 and 5.7% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a KRW 1.24 trillion profit in 2023 to a net loss of KRW 1.02 trillion in 2024, and the loss widened to KRW 1.07 trillion in 2025.

Total net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 80.8 billion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder result. Operating cash flow stayed above KRW 4 trillion in 2023 through 2025 after a KRW 579.8 billion outflow in 2022.

Quarterly sales rose for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 122 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 207.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 113.3 billion in the second quarter, and KRW 756 billion in the third. The third-quarter figure includes KRW 416.9 billion of AMPC subsidies.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 481.5 billion reflected a non-operating loss of KRW 987.2 billion, including derivative valuation losses, interest expense and an impairment on small IT pouch assets.

Since the first quarter of 2026, the company has presented North American production subsidies differently, so prior-year comparisons in press materials can look different from earlier filings.

05

Industry analysis

The company's view is that the EV market has entered a period of slower growth. It has defined 2026 as a transition year away from EV-centered growth and is shifting its business mix toward ESS.

North America is the center of ESS demand, where grid investment and rising power demand from AI data centers are driving growth. The policy environment is also changing.

In August 2026, a US executive order tightened supply-chain security standards for grid equipment, including BESS and inverters, reinforcing a non-Chinese supply chain. In September 2026, revised IAA guidance narrowed what counts as local production in Europe, raising localization requirements.

The company believes suppliers with regional production capacity will gain competitiveness.

On competition, the BloombergNEF ESS Tier 1 list, as reported in February 2026, included LG Energy Solution as the only Korean battery maker after no Korean company appeared the year before; Samsung SDI and SK On were left off, and ESS linked to renewable projects is excluded from the evaluation.

North American production subsidies are a key profit variable: they accounted for more than half of third-quarter 2026 operating profit, so earnings are sensitive to policy changes.

06

Outlook

The company set a target of more than tripling ESS sales in 2026 and a new ESS order target of more than 90GWh. ESS order backlog was about 160GWh at the end of the third quarter of 2026, and the company said volumes through 2028 are largely secured against planned capacity.

Industry estimates put first-half 2026 new orders at about 30GWh, so second-half order additions will determine whether the target is met.

The company plans to have more than 50GWh of North American capacity by the end of 2026 and more than 70GWh of ESS capacity in North America by the end of 2027. 46-series production starts at the Arizona plant at the end of 2026, and a Poland 46-series line is targeted for 2029.

The company said it has secured more than 440GWh of 46-series order volume from existing automakers and EV startups.

For the fourth quarter of 2026, it expects sales to rise about 10% from the prior quarter, while it judges that profitability will partly slow because one-off gains recognized in the third quarter will not recur and early fixed costs arise at new North American ESS lines.

In 2027, the schedule includes sodium-ion test shipments to a North American customer, mass production of tabless 2170 cylindrical cells targeted for the first half, and preparation for LFP 2170 BBU production in the second quarter. Reports said capital spending will be adjusted to raise utilization of existing lines.

08

Bull factors

ESS backlog and volume visibility

ESS order backlog was about 160GWh at the end of the third quarter of 2026. The company said volumes through 2028 are largely secured against planned capacity. The direct BESS contract with a big tech company widens demand beyond utility customers, which can be read as a sign of a more diversified order mix.

Quarterly sales scale and operating profit recovery

Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 9.64 trillion were reported as the largest quarterly sales in the company's history. Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 122 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to a profit in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 756 billion in the third.

The company cited higher utilization in Poland, cylindrical volume growth and productivity gains at new ESS sites as drivers.

Possible benefit from US supply-chain policy

Stricter US supply-chain security standards for grid equipment have raised the importance of non-Chinese supply chains and local production capacity. The company said it sees this as a moment when producers with local capacity become more competitive. The 46-series order volume above 440GWh and the Arizona start-up are cited as support for medium-term volumes.

09

Bear factors

Dependence on subsidies

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 756 billion included KRW 416.9 billion of North American production subsidies. Excluding them, operating profit was KRW 339.1 billion, a margin of about 3.7%.

Analysts noted that subsidy income accounts for more than half of total operating profit, leaving the pace of core profitability recovery as the key variable.

Non-operating losses and continued controlling-shareholder net losses

Although operating profit was positive, non-operating losses exceeded it, and the result attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss. Derivative valuation losses, interest expense and an impairment on small IT pouch assets produced a non-operating loss of KRW 987.2 billion.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025.

Expected fourth-quarter margin slowdown and cash flow

The company expects fourth-quarter 2026 sales to rise about 10% from the prior quarter, but judges that profitability will partly slow. Slower EV growth is cited as part of the operating backdrop. Operating cash flow in the third quarter of 2026 was a net outflow of KRW 338.0 billion.

10

Risk factors

Policy and regulation (subsidies and supply chain)

North American production subsidies and US supply-chain rules affect both profit and orders. A change in subsidy size or eligibility would alter the operating profit structure.

To meet non-Chinese supply requirements, the company is expanding LFP cathode suppliers to four or more and diversifying anode, separator and electrolyte sourcing.

EV demand and utilization

The company's 2026 strategy assumes slower EV growth. Because pouch and cylindrical volumes drive plant utilization, changes in EV demand directly affect how fixed costs are absorbed. The company also cited early fixed costs at new North American ESS lines as a pressure on fourth-quarter profitability.

Financial structure and funding

The debt-to-equity ratio was 129.0% at the end of 2025, up from 86.0% in 2022, and total liabilities were KRW 37.8 trillion. The company said it has no plans for additional parent-level borrowing in 2026 and aims to minimize fundraising in 2027.

Because that stance depends on adjusting capital spending, the balance between order-driven investment needs and financial capacity is the key issue.

12

Overall view

LG Energy Solution's third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 9.64 trillion and operating profit of KRW 756 billion both reached record quarterly levels for the company. However, operating profit includes KRW 416.9 billion of North American production subsidies, and excluding them, operating profit was KRW 339.1 billion.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued for four consecutive quarters, totaling KRW 2.41 trillion over the last four quarters.

The bullish factors are the ESS order backlog of about 160GWh, the company's statement that most volumes through 2028 are secured, and the direct contract with a big tech company that diversifies demand.

The bearish factors are reliance on subsidies, a larger non-operating loss, a debt-to-equity ratio that rose to 129.0%, and the company's warning of a fourth-quarter profitability slowdown.

On valuation, the price-to-earnings ratio is not calculated, and the price-to-book ratio sits at a premium to book value, so the pace of earnings recovery remains the key variable.

The next checkpoints are new orders during the fourth quarter of 2026, the start of 46-series production in Arizona and North American capacity at the end of 2026, and the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results and 2026 annual report in early 2027.
