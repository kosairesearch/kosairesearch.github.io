Report overview

Shrinking Sales and Losses; Restructuring Results Still Ahead

Rftech is reshaping itself around 5G antennas and a merger, while its phone-accessory core still shows shrinking sales and losses.

  1. 1

    Sales for 2025 were KRW 266 billion, down 25.8% from the prior year, and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 25.2 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 27.5 billion.

  2. 2

    Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 77.1 billion, up 12.1% year on year, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.71 billion, while the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 7.21 billion.

  3. 3

    The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 36.2 billion, and the disclosed results show losses in five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

  4. 4

    The company plans to invest KRW 49.2 billion in 5G base-station antenna and filter businesses over 2026 to 2030, but it has stated that no firm supply contracts are currently in place, according to a Dealsite report.

  5. 5

    First-half 2026 sales of 5G base-station antenna modules were KRW 2.9 billion, only 2.4% of consolidated sales, according to a Dealsite report.

02

Business structure

Rftech designs, manufactures and sells IT wireless communication devices, including smartphone chargers, data link cables (DLC) and wireless-charging combo antennas.

According to FnGuide, the 2025 sales mix before the sale of the biotech unit was DLC 34.77%, equipment 16.17%, TA (phone chargers) 15.55%, ANT·STY 13.91%, bio 7.11%, 5G base-station antenna modules 1.13%, and other items such as ACC 11.35%.

TA's share fell from 32.57% in 2022 to 15.55% in 2025, while DLC rose from 21.91% to 34.77% over the same period.

The equipment share climbed from 1.87% in 2023 to 16.86% in 2024, and a news report tied the increase in the first nine months of 2024 to diversification into equipment such as secondary-battery logistics systems at its subsidiary Hanjoo Hitech.

The bio business, run by a subsidiary that sells hyaluronic acid fillers, was sold, and Bloter reported that the sale brought in KRW 20 billion. After a change in the largest shareholder, the combined stake held on the largest shareholder's side is about 40%, according to Bloter.

About 70% of sales depend on a single customer, and the company has said it plans to extend its prior Samsung Electronics supply experience to Ericsson and others, according to a Dealsite report. Within the 57-company phone parts sector, Rftech ranked 18th by 2025 annual sales, according to alphasquare.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales were KRW 360.6 billion in 2022, KRW 314.4 billion in 2023 (down 12.8%), KRW 358.3 billion in 2024 (up 14.0%) and KRW 266 billion in 2025 (down 25.8%). Sales were largest in 2022 and smallest in 2025 within 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 20.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.04 billion in 2023 (down 75.9%) and KRW 1.81 billion in 2024 (down 64.1%), then turned into an operating loss of KRW 25.2 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 5.8% in 2022 to 1.6% in 2023, 0.5% in 2024 and -9.5% in 2025.

Over the same years, FnGuide's cost-of-sales ratio rose from 85.19% in 2024 to 91.38% in 2025, suggesting that falling sales and higher cost ratios overlapped.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 11.9 billion in 2022 and KRW 4.04 billion in 2023 (down 66.0%), then became a net loss of KRW 18.3 billion in 2024 and widened to a net loss of KRW 27.5 billion in 2025, marking two consecutive loss years from 2024 to 2025.

Total net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 30.5 billion in 2025, a different measure from the controlling-shareholder figure. Operating cash flow went from KRW 27 billion in 2022, KRW 27.9 billion in 2023 and KRW 19.4 billion in 2024 to -KRW 1.79 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, the operating loss was KRW 4.35 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 9.63 billion in the fourth quarter, then narrowed to KRW 7.31 billion, KRW 2.29 billion and KRW 1.71 billion across the first three quarters of 2026.

Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 77.1 billion were up 43.2% from the prior quarter and 12.1% from a year earlier.

In the same quarter, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 7.21 billion from KRW 1.72 billion in the second quarter, so the operating line and the controlling-shareholder line moved in different directions. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 36.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Rftech's core products are smartphone accessories, so its end demand is tied to the smartphone market. A company-monitor comment dated June 4, 2026 said the smartphone market has matured, demand has stalled, and cost burdens have weakened profitability.

Among the 57 phone-parts companies, Rftech's 2025 annual operating margin was above the sector average, according to alphasquare.

Its 5G base-station antenna and filter business, which the company has positioned as a new growth pillar, generated KRW 2.9 billion of sales in the first half of 2026, only 2.4% of consolidated sales, so the scale is still small.

The merger filing projects RF communication sales rising from KRW 11.9 billion in 2026 to KRW 22.8 billion in 2027, KRW 50.3 billion in 2028 and KRW 151.7 billion in 2030. A Dealsite analysis said the plan requires an annual growth rate of 89.0%.

The core assumption is that Ericsson-related sales rise from KRW 7 billion in 2027 to KRW 90 billion in 2030. Jeong-dong Accounting Corporation, the financial adviser for the merger valuation, set the probability of achieving Samsung Electronics-related sales at 50% and Ericsson-related sales at 25%. Listed peers in communication antennas and RF components include KMW, RFHIC and Intellian Technologies.

06

Outlook

The company's target for RF communication sales is KRW 11.9 billion in 2026, the starting point for a plan that reaches KRW 22.8 billion in 2027.

The planned 2026 spending on new businesses is KRW 13.6 billion for the year, a figure larger than the estimated 2026 RF communication sales of KRW 11.9 billion, according to a Dealsite report.

The 2026 to 2030 new-business spending plan totals KRW 49.2 billion, including KRW 15.5 billion of R&D and KRW 33.7 billion of facility investment.

Through the merger, the merged subsidiary's cash and cash equivalents of KRW 63.4 billion will pass to Rftech, and the company has said it will use that cash to expand its 5G antenna and filter business. The company stated in its merger filing that it has no firm supply contracts at present.

Growing the customer base beyond its current concentration depends on adding new customers such as Ericsson and converting development projects into mass-production orders after customer validation and approval.

The merged subsidiary's automotive unit reported first-half 2026 sales of KRW 32 billion, well below its 2025 annual sales of KRW 186.9 billion, and a research lab was voluntarily cancelled, according to Dealsite.

Dealsite said the execution of the medium-term plan can be gauged by investment spending, progress in customer development, approval and mass production, and whether the RF communication sales target is met.

08

Bull factors

Growth option in 5G antennas

The company has laid out a plan to grow RF communication sales from KRW 11.9 billion in 2026 to KRW 22.8 billion in 2027 and KRW 151.7 billion in 2030. The core assumption is that Ericsson-related sales rise from KRW 7 billion in 2027 to KRW 90 billion in 2030.

Cash of KRW 63.4 billion passing through the merger is earmarked for new-business investment. The company has nonetheless said it has no firm contracts at present.

Narrowing operating loss and sales recovery

The operating loss narrowed each quarter, from KRW 7.31 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 2.29 billion in the second and KRW 1.71 billion in the third. Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 77.1 billion were up 12.1% from a year earlier, which can be read as a sign of sales recovery.

Compared with the KRW 9.63 billion operating loss in the fourth quarter of 2025, the operating-level deficit has narrowed considerably. The operating line and the controlling-shareholder net income line have not yet moved in the same direction.

Financial restructuring and asset-based valuation room

Bloter reported that the sale of the biotech subsidiary brought in KRW 20 billion. The combined stake on the largest shareholder's side is about 40%. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 186 billion.

Because the price-to-book ratio sits below the sector average, the bull case holds that the valuation could change if profit recovery is confirmed.

Bear factors

Falling sales and persistent losses

Sales for 2025 were KRW 266 billion, down 25.8% from the prior year. The operating loss of KRW 25.2 billion marked a turn into losses, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 27.5 billion.

Losses persisted for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025, and the four-quarter total of losses attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 36.2 billion.

Cost pressure and weaker profitability

According to FnGuide, the cost-of-sales ratio rose from 85.19% in 2024 to 91.38% in 2025. The operating margin fell from 5.8% in 2022 to -9.5% in 2025. A company-monitor comment dated June 4, 2026 cited smartphone market maturity and cost burdens as factors weighing on profitability.

Customer concentration and new-business uncertainty

More than 70% of sales depend on a single customer. Five-G antenna sales were KRW 2.9 billion in the first half of 2026, only 2.4% of consolidated sales. Second-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 53.9 billion fell 20.2% from the prior quarter. The debt ratio rose from 60.2% in 2022 to 119.0% in 2025.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration

Because more than 70% of sales depend on one customer, order changes from that customer flow directly into results. The company says it will reduce this dependence by adding customers such as Ericsson, but no firm orders have been announced.

Quarterly sales volatility is also large, as the 20.2% quarter-on-quarter drop in the second quarter of 2026 shows.

Merger and capital restructuring

The merger brings in KRW 63.4 billion of cash from the subsidiary, but the subsidiary's automotive unit posted first-half 2026 sales of KRW 32 billion against KRW 186.9 billion for all of 2025, according to Dealsite.

Post-merger business mix and profit contribution will change, which complicates the interpretation of consolidated figures. Dealsite also reported signs of downsizing, including the voluntary cancellation of a research lab.

Cash flow and financial burden

Operating cash flow turned negative at KRW -1.79 billion in 2025. If the KRW 49.2 billion new-business spending proceeds, the cash burden could increase. The debt ratio of 119.0% indicates a structure with more liabilities than equity.

12

Overall view

Rftech recorded two consecutive loss years from 2024 to 2025, as a decline in sales of its phone-charger and cable core combined with a higher cost ratio.

Quarterly operating losses narrowed to KRW 7.31 billion, KRW 2.29 billion and KRW 1.71 billion through the first three quarters of 2026, and third-quarter 2026 sales rose to KRW 77.1 billion.

However, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 7.21 billion in the same quarter, and the four-quarter total of such losses reached KRW 36.2 billion.

The 5G base-station antenna business, the center of the new strategy, generated about KRW 2.9 billion of sales in the first half of 2026, and the KRW 151.7 billion target for 2030 is a plan without firm orders at present.

The biotech sale and the absorption of the merger target are changing the capital structure and business mix, so figures such as total equity of KRW 186 billion and total liabilities of KRW 221.4 billion may change in future periodic reports.

On the strength side, the narrowing operating loss and customer diversification plans stand out, while on the weakness side, shrinking sales, persistent losses and reliance on one customer stand out.

The next checkpoints are RF communication sales and operating results in the 2026 annual report, and progress against the KRW 22.8 billion target in the first-quarter 2027 report.
