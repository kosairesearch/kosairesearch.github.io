02

Business structure

Noroo Paint & Coatings is a paint specialist focused on architectural and industrial coatings, and it positions color development and tinting technology as its core strength. The company launched four premium PANTONE paint products based on the 2026 Pantone Color of the Year, Cloud Dancer, on December 23, 2025.

The line covers interior, multi-purpose, exterior and wood-and-metal uses. The company says it can reproduce Pantone's more than 2,800 colors without error.

Its color expertise has also extended into public urban design, through participation in the Seoul Color program from 2024 to 2026 and development of six Seoul Safety Colors for accident prevention.

Based on reporting dated November 13, 2026, the company was selected as a corporate winner of the 2026 Seoul Design Awards. Its main customers are the construction and interior markets and industrial sites, and in domestic paint it competes with large coatings makers such as KCC.

The corporate value-up plan disclosed on April 22, 2026 names bio, eco-friendly coatings and advanced materials as future core technology fields. Segment revenue shares can be checked in the business-segment section of the regular reports.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 753.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 780.5 billion in 2023 (up 3.6% year on year) and KRW 793.8 billion in 2024 (up 1.7%), then fell 2.9% to KRW 771.1 billion in 2025. Across 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year for sales and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 26.1 billion in 2022 (operating margin 3.5%) to KRW 41.9 billion in 2023 (up 60.5%) and peaked at KRW 43.7 billion in 2024 (up 4.3%). In 2025 operating profit fell 30.9% to KRW 30.2 billion, and the operating margin dropped from 5.5% in 2024 to 3.9%.

Owner net income rose from KRW 11.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 27.9 billion in 2023 (up 141.3%) and KRW 35.2 billion in 2024 (up 26.2%), then fell 53.2% to KRW 16.5 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was disclosed at the same amounts in each year, so the two measures show no gap in this period.

Operating cash flow peaked at KRW 61 billion in 2023 and fell to KRW 32.7 billion in 2025. The debt ratio fell from 89.5% in 2022 to 64.1% in 2024 and edged up to 66.3% in 2025, when total equity stood at KRW 416 billion and total liabilities at KRW 276 billion.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 sales were KRW 195 billion with operating profit of KRW 8.87 billion and owner net income of KRW 7.96 billion. Q4 2025 sales fell 8.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 179.5 billion, operating profit fell 71.1% to KRW 2.56 billion, and owner net income swung to a loss of KRW 608 million.

In Q1 2026 sales dipped 1.8% to KRW 176.2 billion, yet operating profit rose 209.5% to KRW 7.93 billion and owner net income turned positive at KRW 6.88 billion.

Q2 2026 sales rose 30.5% to KRW 229.9 billion, operating profit rose 207.0% to KRW 24.3 billion, and owner net income rose 169.7% to KRW 18.6 billion, the quarterly high in the period; the figures were announced on August 14, 2026.

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 186.2 billion (down 19.0% quarter on quarter and down 4.5% year on year), operating profit was KRW 10.6 billion (down 56.6% quarter on quarter and up 19.3% year on year), and owner net income was KRW 4.35 billion (down 76.5% quarter on quarter and down 45.3% year on year).

Whether the quarterly swings come from raw materials, selling prices or one-off items can be separated using the business and notes sections of the regular reports.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for paint tracks the cycles of downstream industries such as construction, automobiles, shipbuilding and steel. Architectural coatings move with new construction starts and renovation demand, while industrial coatings follow capital spending cycles at automakers, shipyards and steelmakers.

Raw materials are mainly petrochemical-based resins and pigments, so the speed at which price increases are passed through to customers determines margins when input costs rise. This structure means paint makers' operating margins can shift considerably from quarter to quarter as demand and cost conditions change.

Noroo's annual operating margin rose from 3.5% in 2022 to 5.5% in 2024 and then fell to 3.9% in 2025. In competition, KCC is a larger architectural coatings rival, and Noroo is trying to differentiate through color development and tinting technology and design-linked products such as PANTONE and Seoul Color lines.

Whether this differentiation supports pricing can be tracked through the cost-of-sales ratio and margin trend in the regular reports. When the new-construction market slows, renovation and maintenance demand becomes relatively more important. Supply conditions across the broader petrochemical industry also shape the direction of raw material prices.

The plan also points to strengthening future growth engines in bio, eco-friendly coatings and advanced materials, and to reinforcing liquidity through financial soundness and cash flow.

On the product side, the four PANTONE-based products launched in December 2025 and the commercialization of Seoul Colors are extending the company's color differentiation.

The Seoul Design Award reported on November 13, 2026 recognizes that its color development and standardization capabilities have also gained public-sector acceptance.

The company stated that it qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act, and a payout ratio of 49.74% for 2025 was disclosed.

Because third-quarter 2026 sales and profit both fell from the prior quarter, whether quarterly profit can be sustained is a question for the regular reports that follow. The next regular report, the 2026 annual report, will show whether the operating margin improves from 3.9% in 2025.

On the new businesses, the key point is how bio and advanced materials contribute to revenue in the business-segment disclosures. Progress on the financial soundness goal can be tracked through changes in the debt ratio and operating cash flow.

Bull factors

Profit rebound in Q2 2026

Q2 2026 sales of KRW 229.9 billion, operating profit of KRW 24.3 billion and owner net income of KRW 18.6 billion were the highest quarterly figures among the last five quarters. Operating profit rose 207.0% from the prior quarter. Whether profit near this level can persist after Q3 2026 is the central premise of the bullish case.

High-dividend status and shareholder returns

The company stated that it qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act. The 2025 payout ratio was disclosed at 49.74%, and the dividend amount rose 14.28% from the year before.

Keeping the dividend framework in place in a year when profit declined is the basis of the shareholder-return argument.

Color technology and public design recognition

Reporting dated November 13, 2026 covers its selection for the Seoul Design Awards, recognizing its Seoul Color development and standardization in public urban design. If tinting and standardization skills translate into product differentiation, there is room for the operating margin to improve.

Bear factors

Reversal in quarterly profit

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 10.6 billion fell 56.6% from the prior quarter, and owner net income of KRW 4.35 billion fell 76.5%. Sales fell 19.0% to KRW 186.2 billion. The rapid reversal after the second-quarter peak is the starting point of the bearish case.

Annual profit decline in 2025

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 30.2 billion, down 30.9%, and the operating margin fell to 3.9%. Owner net income fell 53.2% to KRW 16.5 billion. Measured against the 2024 level, the extent of the profit recovery is still at the confirmation stage.

Lower cash flow and a higher debt ratio

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 61 billion in 2023 to KRW 32.7 billion in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 64.1% in 2024 to 66.3% in 2025. With profit declining, weaker cash generation and financial headroom are the basis of the bearish case.

Risk factors

Downstream demand swings

Paint demand depends on construction activity and capital spending at automakers, shipbuilders and steelmakers, so fewer construction starts or weaker industrial investment would reduce volumes. Whether the 4.5% year-on-year sales decline in Q3 2026 signals weaker demand needs confirmation in later quarters. A slower demand recovery could make quarterly profit even more volatile.

Raw material costs and pass-through

When petrochemical-based raw material prices rise and price increases lag, margins come under pressure. Whether the drop in the operating margin to 3.9% in 2025 coincided with a period of higher input costs can be checked in the cost items of the regular reports. Raw material price swings are a factor that widens margin differences from quarter to quarter.

Execution and investment burden in new businesses

The value-up plan names bio, eco-friendly coatings and advanced materials as future growth engines, but for now it sets out a direction rather than detailed results. New businesses typically carry upfront investment costs before revenue materializes, with uncertain timing, and this could weigh on near-term profit.

Because the company also stresses financial soundness, balancing growth investment against financial headroom is a central challenge.

Overall view
