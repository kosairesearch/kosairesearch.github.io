Report overview

Daegu-Busan Dependence Meets GTX-C Expansion

KB Balhae Infrastructure Fund is layering GTX-C, energy and data-center assets onto a subordinated-loan cash flow base that is still centered on the Sindaegu-Busan Expressway.

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

KB Balhae Infrastructure Fund is the first listed infrastructure fund from a Korean financial group, investing in social infrastructure. It was set up in 2006 as a private infrastructure fund with capital from 17 institutions, including Kookmin Bank, public pension funds and insurers.

It listed on the KOSPI on 2024-11-29. The fund invests in the equity and loan receivables of infrastructure project companies. Its distributable income comes from subordinated-loan interest and principal repayments and from dividends, which in turn rest on toll revenue.

As of 2026-06-30, the asset mix is 72% loans and 28% equity. By asset, the Sindaegu-Busan Expressway is 63%, the Namyangju urban expressway 10%, the Sanseong Tunnel 9%, the Boryeong LNG terminal 9%, the Yongma Tunnel 5% and the Suwon ring road 5%.

Total investment on that date was KRW 956.4 billion, of which KRW 590.8 billion was in the Sindaegu-Busan Expressway company. The expressway is an 82.1 km, four-lane road with a concession running from 2006-02-12 to 2036-02-11. The fund holds a 50.16% stake in the project company.

Under the corporate tax law, income distributed at 90% or more of distributable profit is deducted from taxable income, so the fund plans to distribute at least 90%. Toll income reflects changes in the consumer price index, and borrowing by infrastructure funds is capped at 30% of capital.

Since listing, the fund has widened its targets from roads and tunnels to energy and data centers. The Boryeong LNG terminal and a data-center project are the main examples. The main listed competitor is Macquarie Korea Infrastructure Fund.

One commentary noted that KB Balhae Infrastructure Fund has a higher weighting in traditional infrastructure such as tunnels and roads than Macquarie Korea Infrastructure Fund.

Four new investment commitments and executions have followed since listing: Boryeong LNG, the Jeonnam data center, the Suwon ring road and the Jeju Handong-Pyeongdae offshore wind project. On 2026-09-29 the fund disclosed a financing agreement with the GTX-C project company.

04

Outlook

The key medium-term question is how fast the fund can reduce its reliance on the Sindaegu-Busan Expressway. The company expects to recover more than KRW 110 billion of invested principal from that expressway over five years and plans to use it, along with borrowings, to diversify the portfolio.

GTX-C is a build-transfer-operate (BTO) project with total project costs of KRW 4.9 trillion, and the fund's commitment totals KRW 220 billion. Press reports indicated that the investment would be disbursed in stages over roughly five years of construction. The timing of any contribution to distributions therefore depends on drawdown schedules and completion dates.

The financing structure is another variable. Borrowings stood at KRW 165 billion at the end of 2025, and the amended Private Participation in Infrastructure Act took effect on 2026-02-19, creating a basis to raise the borrowing limit of public infrastructure funds to 100% of capital.

An amendment to the enforcement decree is needed before the higher limit actually applies. A higher limit would widen room to invest without a rights offering, but it would also raise the interest burden.

In its second-quarter 2026 analysis, Samsung Securities cited the expansion of the domestic infrastructure market and co-investment opportunities with affiliates as growth drivers. By contrast, the minimum revenue guarantee (MRG) on the Sindaegu-Busan Expressway ended in February 2026.

The company says compensation for the toll cut agreed in the 2020 restructuring continues until the end of the concession, even after the MRG ends. Quarterly results from late 2026 onward will show how this structure affects distributable income.

05

Bull factors

Cash flow built on subordinated loans

Loans account for 72% of assets, so interest and principal recoveries form a large part of distributable income. Toll income reflects changes in the consumer price index, so revenue is linked to inflation. The company says the KRW 650 annual distribution should remain sustainable. That is the company's own outlook, and distributions will vary with operating results.

New assets and portfolio diversification

Four new investments have followed since listing: Boryeong LNG, the data center, the Suwon ring road and Jeju offshore wind. On 2026-09-29 the fund committed a total of KRW 220 billion to GTX-C through equity and mezzanine and subordinated loans. Broadening from roads and tunnels into rail, energy and data centers eases concentration.

Policy backdrop and group ties

Samsung Securities cited the expansion of the domestic infrastructure market and co-investment with affiliates as growth drivers.

The amended Private Participation in Infrastructure Act took effect on 2026-02-19, providing a legal basis for broader investment scope and a higher borrowing limit for public infrastructure funds. It was also noted that KB Financial Group affiliates are taking part in the GTX-C project. The policy direction supports deal sourcing.

06

Bear factors

Concentration in one asset

The Sindaegu-Busan Expressway accounts for 63% of investment assets as of 2026-06-30. The weight is trending lower with new investments, but traffic volumes and concession terms at a single asset still have an outsized effect on distributions.

One commentary argued that traffic on the route is tied to national economic growth and could stay flat or slow rather than grow.

Rising interest cost and modest earnings growth

Second-quarter 2026 operating revenue rose 2% year over year, but net income also rose only 2% because of higher interest expense. When new investments are funded with debt, costs can precede income contributions in the early period. Borrowings stood at KRW 165 billion at the end of 2025.

Yield and liquidity

When the share price rises, the nominal distribution yield falls. At the current price of KRW 10,050, an annualized KRW 650 gives a yield below the 7.7% presented at listing. With trading value around KRW 600 million, liquidity may constrain large trades. Overhang concerns involving existing shareholders and underwriters were raised in the early listing period.

07

Risk factors

Macro

The company acknowledges that market volatility and higher oil prices can temporarily reduce road traffic. An economic slowdown or weaker consumption could affect traffic volumes and toll revenue. Interest rate levels also influence borrowing costs and the relative appeal of listed infrastructure fund yields.

Business

New projects such as GTX-C are under construction, so their contribution to distributable income is limited before completion. It has also been pointed out that private investors bear demand risk in GTX-C.

The Sindaegu-Busan Expressway concession ends on 2036-02-11, so replacement assets must be built up well before then.

Policy and financing

Raising the borrowing limit to 100% takes effect only after the enforcement decree is amended. The current limit is 30% of capital. If the increase is delayed, a rights offering could be considered to fund large investments, and a larger share count would dilute existing holders. More borrowing would also raise interest expense and sensitivity to rate changes.

08

Overall view

KB Balhae Infrastructure Fund is a listed infrastructure fund that pays semiannual distributions from subordinated-loan cash flows centered on the Sindaegu-Busan Expressway. Distributions for the first and second halves of 2025 were KRW 325 per share each.

According to Samsung Securities, second-quarter 2026 operating revenue and net income each rose 2% year over year. Its strengths are loan-based stable cash flow, inflation-linked tolls and the pace of new asset additions.

Its weaknesses are single-asset concentration, rising interest expense and the lag before construction-stage assets contribute income. The drawdown schedule and funding method for the KRW 220 billion GTX-C commitment signed on 2026-09-29 could affect future distributable income and the shareholder base.

The confirmed first-half 2026 distribution and the next quarterly filing are worth checking. This report is provided for information only, and any view on the share price is left to the reader.
