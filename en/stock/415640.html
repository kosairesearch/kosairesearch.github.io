02

Business structure

KB Balhae Infrastructure Fund invests in the shares, equity, bonds and loans of companies that operate social infrastructure projects under the Private Participation in Infrastructure Act.

Its holdings are concentrated in toll roads, with subordinated loans at 72% and equity at 28% of the investment mix as of 2025 press reports. The Shin Daegu–Busan toll road asset alone represents 71% of operating assets, which makes the business structure heavily dependent on one asset.

Average daily traffic on that road rose 1.5% to 52,210 vehicles in 2024. Toll reductions are compensated by the Korea Expressway Corporation, and year-end consumer price index increases are reflected in the next year's toll revenue differential. Borrowings and bonds totaled KRW 120.8 billion at the end of 2024.

In 2024 the fund added investments in the Daegu–Busan toll road asset and raised its post-transaction stake from 40.92% to 50.16%. The closest domestic comparison is Macquarie Korea Infrastructure Fund, and the two are often cited together as domestic infrastructure fund benchmarks.

03

Recent trends

According to the 2024 IR materials, distributable amount was KRW 78.2 billion, compared with KRW 80.5 billion in 2023, or roughly 97% of the prior year. Tax-basis income in 2024 was KRW 26,890 million, up from KRW 26,200 million in 2023.

Operating assets at the end of 2024 were KRW 1,002,683 million, total assets were KRW 1,004,656 million, and total liabilities were KRW 123,483 million. Net assets rose to KRW 881,174 million from KRW 794,860 million at the end of 2023.

Distributions are paid twice a year, and the annual distribution for 2024 was KRW 625 per share. Both halves of 2025 paid KRW 325 per share, with the second-half payment made in February 2026.

In June 2025 the senior loans of the Shin Daegu–Busan asset, KRW 970 billion, were fully repaid, improving distribution capacity. The share price was KRW 10,730 on July 15, 2026, with a 52-week high of KRW 11,380 and a low of KRW 8,080.

The current price of KRW 9,990 on November 11, 2026 is up 1.83% from the previous close. A DART filing on April 9, 2026 showed that Samsung Asset Management reduced its shareholding and stake.

04

Outlook

Distribution capacity depends on the pace of subordinated loan repayments and the cash flow contribution of new assets.

A 2025 press report estimated subordinated loan interest inflows at KRW 40 billion to 50 billion a year, with repayments of a similar scale expected to lift annual cash recoveries to about KRW 90 billion. The same report put borrowing capacity at KRW 248 billion.

Borrowing is capped at 30% of capital, so any expansion depends on regulatory change. A 2025 Samsung Securities report explained that leverage could become available if the government's private investment revitalization measures pass the National Assembly.

On May 26, 2026, the fund disclosed a share purchase agreement for the Suwon outer ring road (north) private investment project and a new investment in the Jeju Handong and Pyeongdae offshore wind complex.

The toll-reduction compensation is set to continue until the end of the project term even after the MRG arrangement ended in February 2026. The record date for the second-half 2026 distribution is December 31, 2026. A 2025 press report also cited a possible rise in asset value if interest rates fall.

05

Bull factors

High payout and a distribution record

The 2024 IR materials reported a dividend yield of 8.05% for 2024. A 2025 Samsung Securities report described the 7.7% yield as among the highest in the industry. The fund distributed all distributable profits in every fiscal year from its founding through 2023.

Inflation-linked toll structure

Under the government agreements, each asset's toll revenue reflects changes in the consumer price index, which supports earnings during inflation. Average daily toll revenue for the Shin Daegu–Busan asset rose 3.0% in 2024. Year-end price increases are carried into the next year's revenue differential.

Improved financial headroom

Full repayment of KRW 970 billion in senior loans for the Shin Daegu–Busan asset in June 2025 improved financial soundness and distribution capacity. A 2025 Samsung Securities report cited the low debt ratio as a strength. With senior debt cleared, more of the subordinated income can flow to distributions.

06

Bear factors

Concentration in one asset

The Shin Daegu–Busan asset accounts for 71% of operating assets, so its traffic and contract terms feed directly into distributions. Subordinated loans make up 72% of the investment mix.

The 2024 IR data also show that traffic on the Namyangju toll road fell 7.1% from the prior year, which highlights variation across assets.

Supply overhang after listing

At listing, the retail subscription ratio was only 0.27 to 1, and the share price fell below the offering price shortly after. A DART filing on April 9, 2026 showed a reduced holding by Samsung Asset Management. Another institutional holder's stake rose from 15.44% to 16.61% according to a DART report. Changes in the shareholder structure can keep supply-driven volatility in place.

Leverage and regulatory constraints

Borrowing is capped at 30% of capital, which limits external financing for new asset purchases. The MRG arrangement ended in February 2026, changing the cash flow structure. Any increase in the borrowing limit depends on whether the private investment measures pass the National Assembly, so timing is uncertain.

07

Risk factors

Macro and rates

Interest rate moves affect the relative appeal of the dividend yield and the value of assets. A 2025 Samsung Securities analyst noted that average yields for listed REITs and infrastructure funds exceed 6.9% even with low borrowing ratios.

The inflation-linked structure offers protection, but its comparison with bond returns can shift when rates rise.

Policy and regulation

The timing of any increase in borrowing depends on whether the private investment measures pass the National Assembly. Toll-reduction compensation is set to continue until the end of the project term even after the MRG arrangement ends. Changes to toll policy or compensation mechanisms would affect the profitability of individual assets.

Traffic and asset risk

Traffic trends differ across assets. The Shin Daegu–Busan road's average daily traffic rose 1.5% to 52,210 vehicles in 2024, while the Namyangju toll road fell 7.1%. With seven assets under management, traffic changes at any single asset feed directly into distribution levels.

08

Overall view

KB Balhae Infrastructure Fund is best characterized as an infrastructure fund showing stable distributable income and dividend flows in the 2024 IR data. Distributions rely heavily on the Shin Daegu–Busan toll road, so traffic and contract terms are the largest variables.

On the bull side, the inflation-linked toll structure, improved headroom after senior loan repayment, and a dividend yield rated among the highest in the sector are cited.

On the bear side, asset concentration, a borrowing cap at 30% of capital, the cash flow changes after February 2026, and supply pressure after listing are cited. A 2025 Samsung Securities report viewed dividend sustainability and policy benefits positively, though this is one analyst's view.

Any increase in borrowing depends on legislation and asset purchases and is not yet settled. Contribution from new assets will become visible in future quarterly results and disclosures.
