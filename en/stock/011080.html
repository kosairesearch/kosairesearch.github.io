HYUNGJI I&C: Narrower Losses Amid Market-Watch Pressure

HYUNGJI Innovation and Creative Company returned to operating profit in the second quarter of 2026, but annual losses widened through 2025 and the stock now faces a caution designation and administrative-issue listing requirements.

02

Business structure

HYUNGJI Innovation and Creative Company was founded in 1976 for apparel manufacturing, wholesale and retail, and fashion design, and listed on KOSDAQ in 2004.

Its own brands are the shirt label YEZAC, the menswear label BON, the womenswear label CARRIES NOTE, and BON:E, sold through both offline stores and online channels. The company says cumulative YEZAC unit sales have reached 8.49 million.

In April 2026 it expanded its existing online team into a VC (Velocity Commerce) division reporting directly to the CEO to speed online decisions.

The online-only brand BOLDINI was reported for public launch in September 2026, and the company targets KRW 2.5 billion in first-year sales and a KRW 10 billion brand within three years.

Its store network is being reshaped around key hubs, with low-efficiency stores closed and renovations and pop-ups concentrated in strong locations, while direct sales through its own mall are being expanded to reduce third-party platform fees.

Overseas, it has secured a trademark in China and is pursuing entry into Japan through CARRIES NOTE on the SHOP CHANNEL platform. The company highlights its Asian-fit pattern design as a core strength, but it competes for online and overseas share with listed peers such as Handsome, LF, F&F and Shinsegae International.

Its market capitalization sits below the 20 billion won listing-maintenance threshold, placing it among the smaller stocks in the sector. The largest shareholder side held 12.74% as of September 30, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 70.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 65.3 billion in 2023 (down 7.3% year on year), KRW 56.7 billion in 2024 (down 13.1%), and KRW 50.8 billion in 2025 (down 10.5%).

Revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit dropped from KRW 2.39 billion in 2022 to KRW 653 million in 2023 (down 72.7%), then turned into an operating loss of KRW 5.02 billion in 2024 and widened to KRW 7.04 billion in 2025.

The operating margin moved from 3.4 percent in 2022 to 1.0 percent in 2023, -8.8 percent in 2024 and -13.9 percent in 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 1.86 billion in 2023 was a turnaround from the 2022 loss, but the company then posted controlling-shareholder net losses of KRW 3.87 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.91 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests shows the same direction, with net losses of KRW 3.87 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.91 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 1.13 billion positive in 2022 but turned negative at KRW -2.81 billion in 2023, KRW -6.01 billion in 2024 and KRW -3.4 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio stood at 80.4 percent at the end of 2025, down from 129.9 percent in 2024, with total equity of KRW 34.3 billion and total liabilities of KRW 27.6 billion.

On a quarterly basis, fourth-quarter 2025 revenue rose 39.6 percent from the prior quarter to KRW 14.4 billion, but the operating loss widened to KRW 2.29 billion.

Revenue was KRW 12.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 with an operating loss of KRW 1.28 billion, then reached KRW 14.5 billion in the second quarter with operating profit of KRW 51.7 million.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 9.38 billion fell 35.5 percent from the prior quarter and 8.9 percent from a year earlier, and the operating loss of KRW 1.15 billion was smaller than a year earlier.

The company attributed the first-half improvement to a roughly 4 percent cut in selling and administrative expenses and to closing low-efficiency stores.

05

Industry analysis

Industry-wide demand indicators are not presented here as numbers, so the sector direction is read through the company's channel shifts and overseas demand trends. The company said its online sales rose 41 percent year on year in the first half of 2026, with visitors up 108 percent.

Online growth only lifts profitability if fees paid to third-party platforms and marketing costs fall in step, which makes channel mix a key variable for margins.

Press reports say the Korean Wave has raised overseas preference for Korean fashion brands, and the overseas expansion of brands such as MIXXMIX, Handsome and Lululemon-style activewear makers was cited as an example.

Listed peers in the same sector, including Youngone, F&F, LF, Handsome and Shinsegae International, are also widening online and overseas channels.

HYUNGJI Innovation and Creative Company's differentiators are its pattern know-how from menswear shirts and suits and its Asian-fit design, while its weaknesses are reliance on offline stores and the small profit contribution from its early-stage online shift.

The position of the sector in its cycle is hard to pin down, so company-level results are the more direct reference. With the market capitalization below the listing-maintenance threshold, fundraising capacity and listing requirements weigh more on the shares than the sector backdrop.

Outlook

The company announced a fourth-quarter 2026 revenue target of KRW 21.0 billion on October 12, 2026. This is a management target, not a confirmed result.

The reported growth of about 33 percent year on year does not match the figure implied by the reported fourth-quarter 2025 revenue of KRW 14.4 billion, so the comparison base should be rechecked against the 2026 annual report.

The company cited strong sales of its fall-winter lineup and online growth as the basis for the target, and said online sales were up 89 percent year on year as of October 18, 2026. Overseas, a Japan launch was targeted for November 2026, with orders placed from August and first shipments planned for year-end.

In China, a pop-up at a large Shanghai shopping complex and a Douyin brand account in the second half were planned. The online-only brand BOLDINI was slated for public launch in September 2026, and the company presented its own projection of KRW 10 billion in 2028 sales and KRW 2.1 billion in operating profit.

Following the revised offering, reports said the proceeds would go to launch investment for the new online brand. The company presented cost efficiency and direct-to-consumer expansion as drivers of a second-half rebound.

The checkpoints are whether fourth-quarter 2026 revenue approaches the KRW 21.0 billion target and whether online growth translates into better operating profit.

Bull factors

Narrower losses and a quarterly turnaround

Operating profit of KRW 51.7 million in the second quarter of 2026 marked a turnaround, and the third-quarter operating loss of KRW 1.15 billion was smaller than a year earlier. The company credits a roughly 4 percent cut in selling and administrative expenses and the closure of low-efficiency stores.

Whether this cost structure produces sustained quarterly improvement will be visible in the fourth quarter and the 2026 annual report. The key test is whether the operating margin, which was -8.8 percent in 2024 and -13.9 percent in 2025, changes direction.

Fast growth in online channels

According to the company, online sales in October 2026 rose 89 percent year on year as of October 18, and cumulative 2026 sales through its own mall and Naver brand store rose 71 percent. The company also said first-half 2026 online sales grew 41 percent and CARRIES NOTE online sales jumped 182 percent.

If online expansion brings lower fees and better marketing efficiency, it could support a margin recovery. These growth rates are company-announced, so they still need to be checked against periodic-report figures.

Overseas entry and new-brand pipeline

In Japan, orders were placed toward a November 2026 local launch, and in China the company secured a trademark and is preparing a Douyin account and pop-ups. The company presented its own projection of KRW 10 billion in sales and KRW 2.1 billion in operating profit for the online-only brand BOLDINI in 2028.

It cites cumulative YEZAC sales of 8.49 million units as evidence of brand equity. When and how much overseas revenue is actually recognized will be visible in future periodic reports.

Bear factors

Consecutive losses and negative operating cash flow

Operating losses were KRW 5.02 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.04 billion in 2025, a second straight year of losses, and the 2025 controlling-shareholder net loss was KRW 8.91 billion. Operating cash flow stayed negative from 2023 through 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 7.45 billion, so the cumulative deficit remains large. Without the quarterly turnaround carrying into a full-year profit, the loss structure cannot yet be called resolved.

Smaller fundraising and short-term liquidity pressure

Total proceeds from the rights offering fell from KRW 13.08 billion to KRW 4.43 billion after revisions, and debt repayment was removed from the stated use of funds.

An analysis of figures as of the end of March 2026 said short-term liabilities due within a year reached KRW 19.4 billion, well above cash of KRW 2.5 billion. The company must still execute overseas and new-brand spending within limited cash. Whether its plans for extending borrowing maturities and using pledged assets are carried out will matter.

Market warnings and price volatility

The stock was designated under investment warning on October 14, 2026, released on October 28, and then on November 17, 2026 was designated under investment caution for one day after closing down 22.98 percent.

The KOSDAQ listing-maintenance market-cap threshold was raised to 20 billion won from July 2026, and the company's market value is below it. Overall view

HYUNGJI Innovation and Creative Company has seen annual revenue decline since 2022, and its operating and controlling-shareholder net losses widened from 2024. Still, it recorded an operating profit in the second quarter of 2026, showing an improving quarterly trend.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 9.38 billion fell year on year, and the operating loss of KRW 1.15 billion was smaller than a year earlier. Operating cash flow has been negative since 2023, and the rights offering was scaled down well below the original plan, which forced a revision of funding arrangements.

Online sales and overseas entry are the growth axes the company emphasizes, but their profit contribution is to be confirmed in the 2026 annual report.

The October 2026 investment warning, the November 2026 caution designation and the administrative-issue status overlap, making short-term volatility and listing requirements the central issues.

Strengths include the direction of profit improvement and online growth, while weaknesses include accumulated losses, cash flow, a smaller fundraising base and listing requirements.

The next checkpoints are whether fourth-quarter 2026 revenue reaches the target, the progress of the Japan launch, and full-year results in the 2026 annual report.
