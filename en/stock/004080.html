Business structure

Shinhung's business is centered on dental products. According to Alphasquare's company overview, main revenue comes from dental consumables, with dental treatment chairs and dental alloys following.

A breakdown in the same source puts product sales at about 69 percent of the mix, with treatment chairs, dental alloys, alloy primary-tooth crowns and other products each in the 5 to 10 percent range, and a leasing segment at 4.6 percent.

The source does not state its reference date, so the mix is read only as a rough guide to structure. Revenue in 2025 was KRW 102.6 billion, below KRW 118.2 billion in 2022. As of September 2023, standalone exports rose 28 percent while domestic sales fell 7.3 percent, so exports partly offset weaker home demand.

A 2024 results summary presented cost reduction and export expansion as the path to improvement, although consolidated revenue for June 2024 fell 8.9 percent year on year. Customers are likely dental clinics and dental laboratories.

Competition comes from domestic and overseas makers of dental consumables and equipment, contested on price, quality and distribution. Shinhung is classified under distribution and consumer goods, but its earnings structure is closer to a manufacturing-style consumables business.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, revenue fell from KRW 118.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 112.4 billion in 2023 (down 4.9 percent year on year) and to KRW 101.8 billion in 2024 (down 9.5 percent), then edged up to KRW 102.6 billion in 2025 (up 0.8 percent). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit rose from KRW 10.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.4 billion in 2023 (up 8.6 percent), dropped sharply to KRW 6.28 billion in 2024 (down 44.7 percent), and recovered to KRW 7.09 billion in 2025 (up 13.0 percent).

The operating margin was 8.8 percent in 2022, 10.1 percent in 2023, 6.2 percent in 2024 and 6.9 percent in 2025, so it has stayed below the 2023 level since 2024.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 9.23 billion in 2023, fell to KRW 5.23 billion in 2024 (down 43.4 percent) and to KRW 2.82 billion in 2025 (down 46.0 percent), declining in both 2024 and 2025.

The similar drops in operating profit (44.7 percent) and controlling net income (43.4 percent) in 2024 suggest the earnings decline began at the operating level.

Total net income for 2025 was KRW 2.93 billion including non-controlling interests, and it should be read separately from the KRW 2.82 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

By quarter, revenue peaked at KRW 30.4 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (up 25.9 percent from the prior quarter), when controlling shareholders recorded a net loss of KRW 312 million, a swing from profit.

Operating profit then fell to KRW 806 million in the first quarter of 2026 (down 74.6 percent from the prior quarter), jumped to KRW 3.64 billion in the second quarter (up 352.3 percent), and fell again to KRW 1.78 billion in the third quarter (down 51.3 percent).

In the second and third quarters of 2026, controlling net income exceeded operating profit, and the non-operating items behind that gap are detailed in the periodic reports. Controlling net income over the last four quarters totaled KRW 7.63 billion.

Risk factors

Product and customer concentration

A large share of revenue depends on dental consumables, so changes in dental treatment demand and customer purchasing cycles can feed directly into sales. With product sales at about 69 percent of the mix, shifts in product mix could also widen profitability swings.

Currency and export dependence

As the export share grows, exchange rate moves and changes in overseas orders affect revenue and margins. The structure has relied on export growth to offset domestic weakness, so a slowdown in exports could weaken revenue resilience.

Gaps between profit levels

In the second and third quarters of 2026, controlling net income exceeded operating profit. Non-operating items and taxes that separate the profit levels can amplify earnings swings, so the detail needs to be checked in the periodic reports. Whether the gap is one-off or recurring changes how earnings quality should be read.
