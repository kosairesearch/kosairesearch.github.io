Brokerage Gains Meet Weaker Trading and IB Earnings

Hyundai Motor Securities has grown brokerage and financial product income with higher trading volumes, but operating profit has fallen since its 1Q 2026 peak, leaving the durability of earnings recovery as the key item to track.

02

Business structure

Hyundai Motor Securities runs brokerage and financial products, asset management, investment banking (IB), and proprietary trading and asset management as its main businesses.

According to a November 2025 evaluation by Korea Investors Service, the 2024 breakdown of operating net revenue was roughly 40% proprietary trading and asset management, 30% brokerage, 10% asset management, and 20% IB.

In the asset management segment, bond brokerage and fund income are the main revenue sources, and bond trading volume is relatively large. The brokerage segment moves sharply with domestic stock trading volumes.

First-half 2026 brokerage and financial product operating net revenue on a separate basis reached KRW 121.8 billion, more than triple the KRW 39.5 billion of the same period a year earlier.

The IB segment is being reshaped away from a project financing (PF) centered structure toward corporate finance and new technology investment. Retirement pension fees provide stable income in the asset management business, and DC and IRP reserves reached the KRW 3.4 trillion range in 1Q 2026, up 20.1%.

The Hyundai Motor Group sales network, including retirement pension management and affiliate bond underwriting and brokerage, is seen as supporting the earnings base. Korea Investors Service has said the business is diversified and holds relatively good shares in each segment.

A 2022 evaluation classified the company as a mid-sized securities firm with a mid-tier market position across business segments, and it competes directly with larger securities firms in brokerage and IB.

04

Earnings analysis

On the consolidated basis in the audited figures, operating profit was KRW 114.6 billion in 2022, then fell to KRW 65.2 billion in 2023 (down 43.1% year on year) and KRW 54.7 billion in 2024 (down 16.1%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit had peaked at KRW 156.5 billion in 2021, and the trend changed after 2022. Operating profit rose 32.1% to KRW 72.3 billion in 2025, a move back toward recovery.

However, the operating margin declined from 9.7% in 2022 to 4.1% in 2023 and 3.0% in 2024, while revenue grew from KRW 1.19 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.8 trillion in 2024. The scale expanded while margins thinned.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 87.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 53.5 billion in 2023 (down 38.6%) and KRW 36.1 billion in 2024 (down 32.4%), then rose 59.7% to KRW 57.7 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests is reported separately as KRW 57.7 billion for 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW -1.62 trillion in 2023 and KRW -153.6 billion in 2025, both outflows. The debt-to-equity ratio was 735.6% in 2022, 806.7% in 2023, 821.0% in 2024 and 758.9% in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 14.1 billion in 3Q 2025, then KRW 4.05 billion in 4Q 2025 (down 71.2% from the prior quarter), and KRW 32.3 billion in 1Q 2026 (up 698.4%).

Brokerage and financial product income in that quarter reached KRW 73.8 billion, up 108.4% year on year, according to a Korea Financial News report dated April 30, 2026.

Operating profit was KRW 19.1 billion in 2Q 2026 (down 40.8% quarter on quarter) and KRW 12.7 billion in 3Q 2026 (down 33.8% quarter on quarter, and down 10.0% year on year).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders in 2Q 2026 of KRW 32.7 billion exceeded operating profit, and this was tied to about KRW 24.5 billion of non-operating income from the conclusion of a lawsuit in the first half.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders in 3Q 2026 was KRW 15.4 billion, up 25.7% year on year. The sum of the last four quarters of net income attributable to controlling shareholders is KRW 80.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Securities earnings depend heavily on domestic stock trading volumes and the interest rate environment. In 1Q 2026 and the first half, stronger stock markets lifted trading volumes and brokerage income rose sharply.

In 3Q 2026, however, lower trading volumes and greater bond rate volatility created an unfavorable market environment, according to a press assessment. Bond trading gains and losses widen when market rates move sharply.

Bond trading results weakened after a sharp rise in market rates in 4Q 2025, and in the first half of 2026 rate volatility linked to Middle East geopolitical risk weighed on trading performance. The recovery in the real estate PF market remains limited.

In 3Q 2026, the IB segment still faced uncertainty over real estate recovery and funding conditions. Over this period, the PF exposure ratio relative to equity was 36.9% in 1Q 2026, down from 38.3% at the end of 2025.

Relative to peers, Hyundai Motor Securities is classified as a mid-sized securities firm smaller than the large players, and it competes on its brokerage and IB market shares. The company also highlights government capital market revitalization policies as opening new opportunities across the securities industry.

06

Outlook

The company has set 'AI innovation and business restructuring' as its 2026 management priority and says it is reorganizing its business from client access through asset management and IB.

The stated aim is to move away from a brokerage-centered structure and raise the share of steadier income sources such as financial products, asset management and retirement pensions.

In IB, the company plans to expand venture fund formation to strengthen its corporate finance deal pipeline and to increase the share of higher-yield assets such as buyout funds. It has also recruited staff for digital asset planning and strategy, and it intends to expand STO and digital asset businesses.

At the 3Q 2026 results, a company official said the firm would continue restoring profitability across all business segments and diversifying income sources in 4Q. The company also cited a next-generation system and an AI platform version 2.0 as future growth drivers.

It has said it will faithfully carry out its corporate value enhancement plan to improve medium- to long-term profitability. These plans are framed around directions and tasks rather than numerical targets.

The next checkpoints are whether 4Q 2026 operating profit recovers to around the 3Q level, and whether asset management results rebound after the sharp first-half decline.

Whether expanded retirement pension and financial product sales translate into fee income will be visible in the segment information of the next periodic report.

08

Bull factors

Brokerage income tied to trading volumes

Brokerage and financial product income rose 108.4% year on year to KRW 73.8 billion in 1Q 2026. First-half 2026 brokerage operating net revenue on a separate basis reached KRW 121.8 billion, more than triple the prior-year figure.

The company attributes this mainly to higher trading volumes from a strong domestic stock market. If trading volumes hold, brokerage income may continue to offset weakness in other segments.

IB segment returns to profit

IB segment net operating revenue swung from a KRW 2.1 billion loss in 1Q 2025 to a KRW 20.6 billion profit in 1Q 2026. A lower provisioning base and expanded arrangement and advisory fees drove the change.

The company says it has reorganized to expand non-real-estate and corporate finance activities beyond the real estate PF focus. The PF exposure ratio relative to equity was 36.9% in 1Q 2026, down from 38.3% at the end of 2025.

Corporate value plan and earnings recovery

The company has announced a corporate value enhancement plan and says it will carry it out. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose 59.7% to KRW 57.7 billion in 2025. With the price-to-book ratio well below 1x, whether the earnings recovery continues is the item to track.

09

Bear factors

Sharp drop in asset management earnings

Asset management operating net revenue on a separate basis fell 94.8% to KRW 3.2 billion in the first half of 2026, from KRW 62.1 billion a year earlier. Korea Investors Service described bond trading positions as sensitive to interest rate moves.

The company acknowledged that bond valuation losses were the main cause of the decline. This segment's volatility can drive overall earnings when rates move sharply.

Operating earnings power and one-off gains

Consolidated operating profit in the first half of 2026 was KRW 51.4 billion, below KRW 54.1 billion a year earlier, while net income rose. About KRW 24.5 billion of the KRW 26.2 billion in non-operating income came from a lawsuit conclusion. The operating margin fell from 9.7% in 2022 to 3.0% in 2024. Some analysts argue that the net income gain should not be read as a recovery in core operating strength.

PF exposure and high leverage

The debt-to-equity ratio was 758.9% at the end of 2025, above 735.6% in 2022. The real estate PF market recovery remains limited, and in 3Q 2026 the IB segment faced continued uncertainty over funding conditions. Total liabilities of KRW 11 trillion far exceed total equity of KRW 1.44 trillion. Financing costs and refinancing conditions at project sites affect asset quality.

10

Risk factors

Trading volumes and market volatility

Brokerage and financial product income moves sharply with domestic stock trading volumes. In 3Q 2026, lower trading volumes were cited as a factor slowing brokerage-related earnings. If market activity cools, the gains seen in 1Q 2026 could fade quickly.

Interest rates and bond valuation gains and losses

Bond trading results suffer valuation losses when market rates rise sharply. A sharp rise in rates in 4Q 2025 and rate volatility linked to Middle East geopolitical risk in the first half of 2026 weighed on trading performance.

The company says it shifted toward fixed-income assets and cushioned part of the valuation losses. If the rate environment turns unstable again, asset management earnings could keep swinging.

PF asset quality and reliance on one-off gains

Financing costs and refinancing conditions at real estate PF sites affect asset quality. The company says it can manage these through maturity extensions and discussions with lenders.

First-half 2026 net income growth included non-operating one-off gains from a lawsuit conclusion, which makes it hard to treat as recurring.

Overall view

Hyundai Motor Securities' audited operating profit declined through 2024 after 2022 and recovered to KRW 72.3 billion in 2025. In 2026, higher trading volumes sharply raised brokerage and financial product income, and the IB segment returned to profit in 1Q.

On the other hand, asset management operating net revenue fell to KRW 3.2 billion in the first half of 2026, and operating profit fell from its 1Q 2026 peak of KRW 32.3 billion in 2Q and 3Q.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 80.2 billion over the last four quarters, but first-half 2026 net income included about KRW 24.5 billion of non-operating income from a lawsuit conclusion.

On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was 758.9% at the end of 2025, and PF exposure and interest rate sensitivity are the main risk factors.

Bullish factors are trading-linked income and the IB return to profit, while bearish factors are volatile trading results and operating profit that has not yet recovered its earlier strength.

The next checkpoints are 4Q 2026 operating profit, the 2026 full-year preliminary results, and the segment information in the 2026 annual business report.
