Ad growth cushions earnings as platform donations lag

02

Business structure

SOOP operates a live-streaming platform, and its revenue is split into platform, advertising and other segments. The platform segment centers on donation-based sales such as star balloons and subscriptions that flow to streamers.

Platform revenue was KRW 66.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 21.3% year on year, which the company attributed to fewer broadcasts by core streamers and some streamers leaving.

The advertising segment depends on game leagues, large events and game-publisher campaigns, and content-linked ad revenue reached KRW 19.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 54.6% from the prior quarter. Large non-gaming advertisers have also started to join.

Advertising accounted for 29% of total revenue in the first quarter of 2026. The other segment rose to KRW 2.5 billion in the second quarter of 2026, up 70.5% from the prior quarter, driven by commerce at a subsidiary.

The group plans to launch the integrated marketing brand SOOPHERE with its advertising and marketing affiliates in the first quarter of 2027. In platform competition, the most direct rival is CHZZK, the service operated by Naver.

Average monthly unique visitors in the third quarter of 2026 were 6.62 million, up 2% year on year, but that traffic did not translate into more payments. The company said that competition for content across a growing number of platforms is weighing on donation revenue.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 289.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 344 billion in 2023, KRW 413.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 466.6 billion in 2025, with growth of 19.0%, 20.1% and 12.9% year on year, so the pace slowed in 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 82.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 124.8 billion in 2025, increasing for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period. Operating margin was 28.5% in 2022, 26.3% in 2023, 27.5% in 2024 and 26.8% in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent reached KRW 101.2 billion in 2024, up 35.7%, but only edged up 0.6% to KRW 101.8 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 103.7 billion in 2025, a different figure from profit attributable to owners of the parent.

Operating cash flow peaked at KRW 157.5 billion in 2024 and fell to KRW 121.4 billion in 2025. The debt ratio declined from 92.9% in 2022 to 75.8% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 503.2 billion and equity attributable to owners of the parent of KRW 467 billion at the end of 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue slid from KRW 125.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 103.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026, then edged up to KRW 105.7 billion in the third quarter.

Operating profit fell from KRW 36.3 billion to KRW 12.6 billion over the same span and recovered to KRW 14.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026. The third-quarter operating margin of 13.7% improved from 12.2% in the prior quarter but remained below half of the 28.8% recorded a year earlier.

Operating expenses of KRW 91.2 billion did not fall with revenue, as depreciation rose 22.5% to KRW 7.3 billion, tied to data-center investment, and relay and other commission fees rose 43.5% to KRW 13.1 billion.

The sum of profit attributable to owners of the parent over the latest four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, was KRW 69 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In Korea's internet broadcasting market, SOOP once held a leading position, but the competitive landscape is shifting. According to viewing-data provider Streams Charts, third-quarter 2026 watch time was 333.02 million hours for SOOP and 335.19 million hours for CHZZK, which edged ahead.

SOOP had led by 4.47 million hours in the prior quarter, so the lead flipped within a single quarter. Reports said the viewing gap between the two platforms widened from about 800,000 users in April 2026 to 2.2 million later.

An analyst at Eugene Investment & Securities said platform revenue will need time to recover and that advertising expansion can support near-term results but a return to past profitability will be hard.

On the demand side, advertising is supported by esports and game leagues, but total ad revenue in the third quarter of 2026 fell 5.5% year on year and remained below the prior-year level. The company said new and returning users rose after its account and data linkage with Nexon began.

It plans to widen partnerships with global game publishers including Riot Games, Tencent, Blizzard Entertainment, The Pokémon Company and Supercell to strengthen its position in esports broadcasting. The key variable for the sector is that a growing number of platforms has intensified competition for content.

06

Outlook

In its second-quarter 2026 results, the company expected that once one-off costs were cleared, game league schedules would normalize from the third quarter and earnings would start to improve.

Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 instead fell 60.0% year on year, so that expectation was not borne out in reported results. The platform priority is to respond to the return of key streamers and support the settling-in of new and returning streamers.

The company introduced a program for existing streamers to discover and develop new streamers and an achievement-based certification system, and about 20 content partner companies joined its partner mentoring program.

On the service side, the company plans to unveil a UI and UX overhaul featuring AI-based personalized recommendations, subtitles and chat translation at a streamer event at year-end 2026, with phased rollout from the first half of 2027.

On advertising, the integrated marketing brand SOOPHERE will have its organization and operating structure in place by year-end 2026, with an official launch and integrated sales from the first quarter of 2027.

The CFO said that if the order inflow continues, content production costs could rise in the fourth quarter from the third quarter. Shareholder returns are built on a policy of paying cash dividends of at least 25% of consolidated net income while meeting the high-dividend company criteria.

The company said it will present its medium-term capital allocation direction, including the use of remaining treasury shares and dividends, at its regular shareholders meeting in March 2027. It cancelled 306,375 treasury shares in September 2026.

Bull factors

Quarterly rebound in advertising

Third-quarter 2026 ad revenue reached KRW 36.8 billion, up 35.4% from the prior quarter, and content-linked ad revenue rose 54.6% to KRW 19.5 billion. The company attributed the gain to major game leagues, large events and new large advertisers from outside gaming.

The launch of the integrated brand SOOPHERE in the first quarter of 2027 will consolidate advertising sales into a single channel.

User metrics and new-user channels

Average monthly unique visitors in the third quarter of 2026 reached 6.62 million, up 2% year on year. The company said the Nexon account and data linkage mostly hit its targets on the first day and that new and returning users rose at both companies. The streamer achievement system and partner mentoring are presented as tools supporting new user inflows.

Balance sheet and shareholder returns

The debt ratio was 75.8% at the end of 2025, down from 92.9% in 2022, and equity attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 467 billion within total equity of KRW 503.2 billion.

The company said it maintains a policy of paying cash dividends of at least 25% of consolidated net income and meeting the high-dividend company criteria. It cancelled 306,375 treasury shares in September 2026.

Bear factors

Continued decline in platform donations

Third-quarter 2026 platform revenue of KRW 66.1 billion fell 21.3% year on year and 10.8% from the prior quarter. The company blamed fewer broadcasts by core crews and some streamers leaving the platform.

Visitors grew, but that did not translate into donation payments, and the co-CEO said a full recovery in platform revenue will take time.

Viewing competition with CHZZK

According to Streams Charts, third-quarter 2026 watch time was 333.02 million hours for SOOP and 335.19 million hours for CHZZK, which took the lead. Because the leader changed within a single quarter, the effect of the watch-time deficit on donation payments and advertising pricing is the next point to check. An analyst at Eugene Investment & Securities said a return to past levels of profitability will be difficult.

Lower profitability and rising costs

The third-quarter 2026 operating margin of 13.7% was less than half the 28.8% of a year earlier. Depreciation rose 22.5% year on year on data-center investment, and relay and other commission fees rose 43.5%.

The company said content production costs may increase further in the fourth quarter as advertising production demand grows.

10

Risk factors

Tax-related costs

The second quarter of 2026 absorbed a total of KRW 7.6 billion, including one-off costs from the tax audit outcome, back taxes and related fees. Whether further charges arise in later quarters can be checked in periodic filings. Repeated tax issues could increase volatility in net income.

Ad demand and game league schedules

Advertising revenue depends heavily on the timing of game leagues and large events, and it fell 10.9% from the prior quarter in the second quarter of 2026 as league schedules shifted. Third-quarter 2026 ad revenue was down 5.5% year on year, so the sequential rebound did not restore the prior-year level. Higher production and broadcast rights costs that come with expanding advertising could limit margin gains.

Streamer departures and platform competition

As more platforms compete for streamers, the contest for content has intensified. Whether key streamers resume activity will be a major factor in fourth-quarter platform revenue. The company said it is widening support for partner streamers to diversify a revenue base concentrated in certain content.

Overall view

SOOP's annual results show revenue and operating profit rising from 2022 through 2025, with operating margin holding in a range of the mid-26% to 28%. In 2026, however, platform donation revenue fell year on year, and third-quarter operating profit dropped 60.0% from a year earlier.

Ad revenue rose 35.4% from the prior quarter in the third quarter of 2026 and supported results, but it remained down year on year. CHZZK edged ahead on watch time in the third quarter of 2026, and the company has said platform recovery will take time.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio fell to 75.8% in 2025, and the policy of paying cash dividends of at least 25% of consolidated net income remains in place.

The next checkpoints are the year-end 2026 UI and UX unveiling, the launch of SOOPHERE in the first quarter of 2027, and the capital allocation announcement at the March 2027 shareholders meeting. How far platform revenue recovers in the fourth quarter of 2026 is the central indicator to watch.
