02

Business structure

Starflex was converted into a corporation in 1996 under its former trade name and changed its name to Starflex in 2006. Its main product is FLEX fabric, which accounts for the bulk of sales and is used for outdoor advertising panels.

Because the fabric covers advertising boards, demand is tied to the scale of outdoor advertising campaigns and installation volumes. Product mix is concentrated in one product family, so changes in order flow pass directly into quarterly revenue, a pattern that fits the sharp Q3 2026 decline.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 71.2 billion and total liabilities were KRW 27.8 billion. Compared with large listed chemical peers such as LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions and SKC, which run petrochemical, materials and battery-material businesses in parallel, Starflex's business scope is narrow.

Revenue by customer and by product is not included in the filed results used here, so it should be checked in the business-segment note of the next annual report. How exposed the fabric is to material substitution trends is a separate product-level question.

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell 32.1% from KRW 104.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 70.7 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 80.2 billion in 2024 (up 13.4% year on year) and KRW 98.7 billion in 2025 (up 23.1% year on year). 2023 was the low point, and revenue grew in both 2024 and 2025.

Operating profit of KRW 3.96 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 3.41 billion in 2023, and the operating loss continued in 2024 at KRW 158 million, with the loss narrowing.

Operating profit returned in 2025 at KRW 6.57 billion, with operating margins of 3.8% in 2022, -4.8% in 2023, -0.2% in 2024 and 6.7% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.55 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 14.7 billion in 2023, KRW 4.65 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.47 billion in 2025 (down 25.4% year on year).

Net income including non-controlling interests shows different values for the same years, at KRW 6.54 billion, a net loss of KRW 14.7 billion, KRW 4.65 billion and KRW 3.47 billion.

Operating cash flow declined from KRW 15.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.38 billion in 2023 and KRW 2.26 billion in 2024, then turned negative at -KRW 266 million in 2025.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 24.1 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 25.7 billion in Q4 2025 (up 6.6% quarter on quarter), KRW 24.6 billion in Q1 2026 (down 4.5%) and KRW 25 billion in Q2 2026 (up 2.0%), staying near KRW 25 billion.

Operating profit moved from KRW 3.14 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 1.68 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 1.4 billion in Q1 2026, then recovered to KRW 2.95 billion in Q2 2026 (up 111.0% quarter on quarter).

In Q3 2026 revenue fell to KRW 14.6 billion (down 41.6% quarter on quarter and 39.3% year on year), and the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 1.38 billion, a swing into loss. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders also turned into a net loss of KRW 8.46 billion in the same quarter.

The net loss is far larger than the operating loss, which suggests non-operating items drove the gap, and the breakdown is a point to check in the next periodic report notes. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totals KRW 2.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for outdoor advertising fabric moves with the scale of advertising campaigns and the volume of outdoor signage installations. Because advertisers' spending cuts show up first as reduced fabric orders, business conditions can feed into revenue quickly.

The swings in annual revenue, down 32.1% in 2023 and up 23.1% in 2025, fit this demand pattern. However, the filed data alone do not show whether the declines and gains were driven by advertising conditions or by other factors.

Large listed chemical peers such as LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions, SKC and Lotte Chemical move with petrochemical cycles and end-market supply and demand. Starflex relies on a narrow advertising-fabric market instead, so its industry benchmark differs from that of large chemical companies.

The 2025 return to profit came alongside revenue growth, but in Q3 2026 revenue and operating profit fell together. The cycle position is hard to pin down, and whether revenue holds up over the two or more quarters after Q4 2026 is the next marker for judging the industry trend.
