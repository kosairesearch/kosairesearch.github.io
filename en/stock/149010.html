Report overview

Thinner Margins, Sturdy Balance Sheet

IK Semicon is a Konex-listed fabless designer of automotive and appliance ICs whose revenue and profit both declined in 2025, although leverage is low and dividends continue.

  1. 1

    On a standalone basis for 2025, revenue was KRW 17,150 million and operating profit was KRW 1,683 million, down 3.8% and 13.8% year on year, respectively.

  2. 2

    Automotive semiconductors rose from about 5% of revenue in 2013 to 28% in 2023, becoming the core of the business mix.

  3. 3

    As a fabless firm with no production lines of its own, capital expenditure needs are small, and the consolidated debt ratio for 2024 was 21.03%.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

IK Semicon is a fabless chip designer with no production lines of its own, and it outsources manufacturing to foundries and to packaging and test specialists. It is a Konex-listed design house that relies on contract manufacturing.

Revenue-contributing products include circuit-breaker ICs, motor controllers, display power-management chips, LED drivers for refrigerator lighting, and interface chips for CCTV and set-top boxes. Development of automotive semiconductors began in 2011.

The automotive share of total revenue grew from about 5% in 2013 to 28% in 2023. According to the Korea IR Service research note, the automotive share rose from 14% to 22% to 28% over three years, and automotive revenue in 2023 was KRW 4.4 billion.

A press report from around March 2025, by contrast, put the automotive share at 23% of 2024 revenue. The two figures use different bases, so it is hard to say whether the share fell, and appliances and other applications make up the remainder.

Automotive customers span Korean carmakers as well as buyers in China, Japan and India. On the competitive side, global players such as NXP, Infineon, Texas Instruments and STMicroelectronics are the key names in the market.

Domestically, Telechips, LX Semicon and ABOV Semiconductor are cited as peers with a similar business model. The company had 20 employees at the end of December 2023, making it a very lean organization. The largest shareholder and related parties held 68.11% as of December 31, 2023.

03

Recent trends

Fiscal 2025 reported results, as disclosed around the annual general meeting, are as follows. On a standalone basis, revenue was KRW 17,150 million, operating profit KRW 1,683 million, and net income KRW 1,526 million, with earnings per share equal to about 15% of the current share price.

On a consolidated basis, revenue was KRW 17,225 million, operating profit KRW 1,644 million and net income KRW 1,507 million, with unqualified audit opinions on both sets. Standalone revenue fell 3.8% and operating profit fell 13.8% from the prior year.

In 2024, standalone revenue was KRW 17.82 billion and net income was KRW 2.04 billion. Based on the Korea IR Service note, 2022 revenue was KRW 21.4 billion and operating profit was KRW 4.0 billion. Revenue has not returned to that peak and has stayed in the KRW 17–18 billion range since.

First-half 2025 revenue was KRW 9.29 billion and operating profit was KRW 1.32 billion. Compared with the full-year figures, second-half profitability appears to have been lower than the first half. The shares opened at KRW 5,190 and closed at KRW 7,990 in a sharp February 2025 rally.

At the time of writing the price is KRW 3,230 and market capitalization is KRW 10.1 billion, which is smaller than shareholders' equity of KRW 14.74 billion at the end of 2025. According to an investing data site, the 52-week range runs from KRW 2,930 to KRW 6,190.

Three-month average volume is reported at 294 shares, which signals very thin trading. Quarterly or half-year results for 2026 could not be confirmed through search.

04

Outlook

The automotive semiconductor market as a whole is expected to grow over the medium term. Straits Research estimated that the market would expand from USD 100.8 billion in 2025 to USD 183.76 billion by 2034. Estimates from research houses differ widely, however, so the absolute figures are best read as directional only.

For analog and mixed-signal ICs, the segment closest to IK Semicon, GMI projected growth from USD 10.73 billion in 2025 to USD 20.93 billion in 2035, a 7.01% compound annual rate. At the same time, mature process nodes attract less investment, which raises the risk of recurring shortages in low-end MCUs and analog ICs.

Whether rising industry demand translates into revenue growth for a small fabless firm is a separate question. Automotive chips are designed into platforms years before vehicles are assembled, so there is a long lag before design wins show up in earnings.

At the company level, the points to watch are a larger automotive share, a recovery in appliance demand and room for operating margin improvement from the cost structure.

The Korea IR Service forecast 2024 revenue of KRW 18.0 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.4 billion, whereas actual consolidated 2024 revenue was KRW 17.9 billion and operating profit was KRW 2.0 billion. That gap shows how estimates for a small fabless company can miss.

05

Bull factors

Rising automotive mix and diversified customers

The automotive share of revenue grew from about 5% in 2013 to 28% in 2023. Automotive ICs require long qualification periods, so an adopted part can act as a barrier to entry. In March 2016, ETNews reported that development to certification takes two to three years, followed by one to two years of vehicle validation.

A customer base that extends beyond Korean carmakers to China, Japan and India also reduces regional concentration.

Light balance sheet and dividends

The consolidated debt ratio for 2024 was 21.03% and the operating margin was 11.30%. The fabless model keeps capital spending on fixed assets small. The 2025 year-end dividend was KRW 200 per share, totaling KRW 624 million, with a stated dividend yield of 3.76%.

Market capitalization of KRW 10.1 billion is below shareholders' equity at the end of 2025, and the reading is open: it could reflect a cautious view of growth or a structural gap tied to small size and low liquidity.

Room for margin improvement if revenue recovers

The Korea IR Service noted that because a fabless company has few fixed assets, operating profit can grow faster than revenue when sales rise. The gap between the 18.53% operating margin in 2022 and 11.30% in 2024 shows how widely profitability can swing with revenue scale.

With revenue still below 2022 levels, there is room for the operating margin to improve. That is, however, a conditional scenario that depends on a revenue recovery.

06

Bear factors

Stalled revenue and softer profit

Revenue fell from KRW 21.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.7 billion in 2023 and has stayed in the KRW 17 billion range since. Standalone revenue fell 3.8% and operating profit fell 13.8% in 2025. Profit looked solid in the first half of 2025 but slowed over the full year, which points to a weaker second half. Pre Stock News reported that the share price fell and retail investors sold despite the first-half results.

Scale limits and low liquidity

With only 20 employees at the end of 2023, the loss of key staff or a development delay would have an outsized impact. As a Konex stock, three-month average volume of about 294 shares makes it hard to trade at a desired time.

The reported turnover of KRW 37,230 at the time of writing suggests days with almost no trades. The controlling holder and related parties own about 68%, which further limits the floating supply.

Large competitors and application concentration

The main players in automotive chips are large global semiconductor groups, and IK Semicon cannot be compared with them on revenue scale. ETNews reported that its alternator regulator product competes with products from the global supplier Bosch.

A large part of revenue still comes from appliances and other applications, and the Korea IR Service stated that array, LED driver and interface revenue were unusually weak in 2023. That shows how earnings can swing with household appliance demand.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Appliance and automotive demand is sensitive to the economic cycle and consumer sentiment. Because half of total revenue comes from overseas, exchange-rate moves and changes in the trade environment can affect earnings.

In 2023, when the global semiconductor cycle slowed, this company's results fell along with those of large producers. A repeat of inventory adjustments or swings in vehicle output could transmit through the same channel.

Industry

A fabless company outsources manufacturing, so it is exposed to foundry utilization and pricing. The industry's tilt toward faster-growing, higher-margin markets such as AI and hyperscale data centers could worsen supply conditions for products that depend on mature nodes.

Global majors keep launching new automotive products, which adds technology competition. The long lag from design win to volume revenue also means order success reaches earnings with a delay.

Company-specific

A small staff, a high controlling stake and a board with a family-management character are governance points to monitor. Director Won-young Yoon is the son of CEO Kyung-deok Yoon and handles overall management. At the March 31, 2026 annual general meeting, Won-young Yoon was reappointed as an inside director.

The Konex market has fewer disclosures and less brokerage coverage, so information access is limited. With thin trading, even small orders can move the price sharply.

08

Overall view

Based on reported 2025 results, IK Semicon is a small fabless company with standalone revenue of KRW 17,150 million and standalone operating profit of KRW 1,683 million. The growing automotive mix, low leverage and continued dividends are positives for business stability.

On the other side are flat revenue since 2022, lower profit in 2025, a workforce of around twenty and thin trading volume. The automotive chip market is projected to grow over the medium term, but market-size estimates differ widely between research houses and should be read with care.

Quarterly results for 2026 were not confirmed in this research, so the half-year and quarterly figures disclosed later will be the key data for judging the trend in automotive mix and operating margin.

The fact that market capitalization is below shareholders' equity can be read in different ways, and this report does not take a side. Readers should check the original filings and trading conditions and form their own judgment.
