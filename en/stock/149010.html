02

Business structure

IK Semicon was founded in July 2000 as a fabless designer of semiconductors. It owns no fabrication lines or production equipment and entrusts manufacturing to foundries and outsourced packaging and test (OSAT) providers. The business runs along two lines, automotive and home appliance and other applications.

Development of automotive chips started in 2011, and the automotive share rose from about 5% of sales in 2013 to 28% in 2023. Leading automotive products include motor-control ICs used for window, seat and cooling-fan functions and regulators for vehicle alternators.

On the appliance side, TR Array ICs for display applications in home appliances have been the largest product line, with their share falling from 35% in 2020 to 24% in the first half of 2022.

Other products include ICs for earth-leakage breakers, motor controllers, display power management and LED drivers for refrigerator lighting. Roughly half of sales come from overseas, and automotive customers have broadened from Korean automakers to customers in China, Japan and India.

Among KOSDAQ fabless peers with similar models, such as Telechips and LX Semicon, the 28% automotive share was described by a 2024 research report as solid given the relatively short development history. Headcount was 24 as of 2025.

03

Recent trends

Financial figures below are drawn from third-party corporate data aggregators and a KOSDAQ research institute report. Revenue in 2023 was about KRW 15.7bn, operating profit KRW 2.0bn and net profit KRW 1.8bn.

Consolidated revenue rose 14% in 2024 and net profit increased to KRW 2,035.17 million, while the operating margin was 11.30% in that year. This roughly matched the 15% revenue growth forecast in a March 2024 report.

On a 2025 basis, revenue was KRW 17,150.2 million, down 3.8%, and operating profit was KRW 1,683.02 million, down 13.8%; net profit was KRW 1,526.27 million. First-half 2025 revenue was KRW 9,290.17 million, operating profit KRW 1,324.24 million and net profit KRW 983.71 million, according to press reports.

Subtracting first-half operating profit from the full-year figure implies roughly KRW 350 million in second-half operating profit, a sharp step-down in the calculated comparison. The share price closed at 8,000 won in February 2025 and was recorded at 6,200 won on 14 August 2025. The current price is 3,590 won and the market capitalization is KRW 11.2bn.

04

Outlook

The company has set a minimum revenue target of KRW 21.0bn for 2026. That is about 22% above 2025 revenue of KRW 17,150.2 million, and attainment depends on mass production of new automotive chips and a recovery in appliance demand.

According to the company history, development of an insulated semiconductor for electric vehicles was completed in November 2025, and a small motor-driver chip for electric vehicles was under development from July 2024.

If new automotive chips move into automaker production, the automotive share of sales would become larger. Press reports said the company faced earnings pressure from tariffs in 2025 and indicated it would consider a market transfer once sales pass KRW 25.0bn.

Because production is outsourced, foundry costs and packaging charges remain variables for profitability. A past business report showed outsourced production costs at 35.97% of sales in 2019.

An industry analysis also expects automotive semiconductor demand to grow over the medium to long term as electronic content per vehicle rises. The next checkpoint is whether the revenue trend in the third-quarter 2026 quarterly report tracks the KRW 21.0bn target path.

05

Bull factors

Rising automotive mix

The automotive share of sales rose from about 5% in 2013 to 28% in 2023. Development work has run for more than a decade since 2011, which analysts cite as building reliability and technical credentials. The company history records that an insulated semiconductor for electric vehicles was completed in November 2025.

Overseas sales and customer diversification

A KOSDAQ research report cited roughly half of sales coming from overseas as a positive. Press reports put the export share at 46.08% in the first half of 2025. Automotive customers have expanded from Korean automakers to customers in China, Japan and India.

Low financial leverage

On a standalone basis for 2024, the debt-to-equity ratio was 20.10% and the reserve ratio was 652.51%. Return on equity was 15.32%. Because the company has no production plants, capital expenditure needs are light, and its fixed-cost structure is comparatively lean.

Bear factors

Revenue turned lower

Revenue in 2025 was KRW 17,150.2 million, down 3.8% from the prior year. Operating profit fell 13.8% to KRW 1,683.02 million, so top-line and profitability moved in the same direction. The 2024 revenue gain proved to be a one-year increase.

Appliance dependence and second-half profitability

Apart from the 28% automotive share, the remaining sales still come mainly from home appliances and other applications. Subtracting first-half 2025 operating profit of KRW 1,324.24 million from the full-year figure implies roughly KRW 350 million in second-half operating profit. This is a simple arithmetic difference of aggregated figures.

Tariff exposure and outsourcing costs

With an export share of 46.08%, tariffs and trade conditions can feed directly into results, according to press coverage. In a fabless model, foundry and packaging costs pass straight through to cost of sales. A past business report put outsourced production costs at 35.97% of 2019 sales.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Slower semiconductor cycles and shifting trade conditions affect results. Revenue also fell in 2023 when the industry slowed, and tariff burdens have been reported. Export reliance makes the company sensitive to external conditions.

Industry

Automotive revenue depends on automaker production schedules and new model adoption. If appliance demand recovery is delayed, total revenue growth stays constrained. The 2025 revenue decline following 2024 recovery expectations illustrates this exposure.

Market and company

As a KONEX-listed stock, trading liquidity can be limited. Major shareholders and related parties held 68.11% as of 31 December 2023, which keeps the free float limited. With about 24 employees, the organization is small, so staffing changes carry a larger effect.

08

Overall view

IK Semicon has reshaped its product mix over time, lifting the automotive share of sales to 28% in 2023. Supportive factors include a more diversified automotive customer base, a high overseas sales share, and a light balance sheet, with a standalone debt-to-equity ratio near 20% for 2024.

Offsetting factors include the decline in 2025 revenue and operating profit, the continued weight of appliance products, and direct exposure to cost swings in an outsourced manufacturing model. The calculated drop in second-half 2025 profitability versus the first half is another source of volatility.

The share price was 6,200 won on 14 August 2025 and is 3,590 won at the time of this report. Trading liquidity and information flow on the KONEX market also remain variables. The 2026 revenue target of KRW 21.0bn and progress on new automotive chips are the key indicators for the business direction. This is a qualitative summary for information purposes only.
