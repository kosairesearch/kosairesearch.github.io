02

Business structure

IK Semicon is a fabless semiconductor company founded in 2000 that has no in-house production line and entrusts manufacturing to foundries and packaging and test providers. Its business is divided into three areas: automotive ICs, power management ICs (PMICs), and lighting and display driver ICs.

Automotive chips are used in engine, body and motor-drive applications and accounted for about 28% of total sales in 2023.

The power management segment produces ICs for residual-current circuit breakers, which account for more than 70% of domestic output, along with control ICs for industrial step motors and brushless motors. TR ARRAY products, which made up more than 30% of total sales in 2022, are a core product line.

The lighting and display segment is centered on home appliances, including refrigerator lighting, fluorescent and street-light replacement lamps, and LED constant-current driver ICs. Automotive customers include Korean automakers and buyers in China, Japan and India, and about half of total sales come from overseas.

As of 31 December 2023 the company had about 20 employees, so it needs sufficient sales scale to absorb foundry and packaging costs and generate profit. Press reports put the export share of sales at 46.08% in the first half of 2025.

03

Recent trends

Based on 2025 financial data, revenue was KRW 17.15 billion, down 3.8% year on year. Operating profit for the same year was KRW 1.68 billion, down 13.8%, and net profit was KRW 1.53 billion, below KRW 2.04 billion in 2024.

Revenue in 2024 was KRW 17.82 billion, up from KRW 15.60 billion in 2023, although another compilation shows 17.9 billion KRW, so sources differ slightly. Operating profit in 2024 fell 0.6%, so sales growth did not carry through to profit.

In 2023 revenue dropped from KRW 21.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.7 billion as the semiconductor cycle weakened, and the operating margin fell from 18.5% to 12.7%.

Press reports on the first half of 2025 show revenue of KRW 9.29 billion, operating profit of KRW 1.32 billion and net profit of KRW 984 million, meaning operating profit rose year on year while net profit declined.

Comparing full-year operating profit with the first-half figure suggests the second half contributed less to profit. The share price moved from 8,000 KRW (closing) in February 2025 to 6,200 KRW on 14 August 2025, and the current price is 3,400 KRW with a market capitalization of KRW 10.6 billion.

Reports also indicate that individuals sold about KRW 52.5 million of shares during the first seven months of 2025, which institutions and other corporations absorbed.

04

Outlook

A March 2024 report from the Korea Investor Relations Council forecast 15% revenue growth and 20% operating profit growth for 2024, but operating profit actually fell 0.6% that year. The gap between the forecast and the result shows how long it takes for business plans to turn into profit.

In the automotive pipeline, an insulated gate driver for EVs (IK8009) completed development in November 2025, and a small motor driver for EVs (IK8510) was under development as of July 2024.

Automotive chips take two to three years from development to certification, followed by one to two years of vehicle validation, so the lag before revenue is recognized is long. The key checkpoint is whether these new products move into mass production and lift automotive revenue.

Per fnguide data, the automotive semiconductor market is expected to keep growing as autonomous driving and EV adoption increase the number of electronic parts in vehicles. On the operating side, the question is whether the operating margin can reverse its decline from 18.5% in 2022 to 11.3% in 2024. Full-year 2026 results will be finalized through disclosure in early 2027.

05

Bull factors

Automotive share at about 28%

The automotive share of sales rose from 2015 onward to about 28% in 2023. Automotive sales held at the prior-year level even as the semiconductor cycle weakened in 2023. Customers span Korean automakers and buyers in China, Japan and India.

A research report described this share as solid for a company that entered automotive chips little more than a decade earlier.

EV product pipeline

The company history records that development of an insulated gate driver for EVs (IK8009) was completed in November 2025. A small motor driver for EVs (IK8510) was under development as of July 2024. A LIN communication chip for automotive use was also in development in April 2023. Rising electronic content per vehicle provides a backdrop for expanding this product range.

Roughly half of sales overseas

About half of total sales come from overseas. Press reports indicate the export share of sales was 46.08% in the first half of 2025. The company has built distribution networks in China, Japan and India, which provides a base for reducing reliance on any single market. Overseas demand flows directly into sales.

06

Bear factors

Sales and profit both fell in 2025

Based on 2025 data, revenue was KRW 17.15 billion, down 3.8%. Operating profit fell 13.8% to KRW 1.68 billion, a steeper decline than revenue. Net profit was KRW 1.53 billion, below 2024. The first-half rise in operating profit did not carry through to the full year.

Operating margin on a downward path

The operating margin fell from 18.5% in 2022 to 12.7% in 2023 and 11.3% in 2024. Revenue dropped from KRW 21.4 billion to KRW 15.7 billion in 2023 as the semiconductor cycle weakened. Samsung Electronics and SK hynix also reported weaker results during that period, which points to a sizable cycle effect. Operating profit declined again in 2025, so the recovery did not continue.

Small revenue base and lean headcount

Revenue moved between KRW 15.7 billion and KRW 17.8 billion over 2023 to 2025, staying below KRW 20 billion. As of 31 December 2023 the company had about 20 employees. A fabless model needs enough sales to absorb foundry and packaging costs, so a small revenue base limits the ability to absorb unit costs.

07

Risk factors

Technology · development

Press reports note that automotive chips take two to three years from development to certification and one to two more years of vehicle validation. A small motor driver for EVs (IK8510) is still in development, so certification delays would push back mass production and revenue recognition.

The timing of revenue from new products has not been set. Business schedules could shift depending on customer validation results.

Reliance on outsourced manufacturing and customers

With no in-house production line, the company depends on the capacity and pricing of foundries and packaging and test providers. Its business report states that it supplies Korean automakers and global automotive manufacturers. A drop in orders from any major customer could cause larger swings in revenue. Delays at outsourced production partners would affect both delivery timing and revenue recognition.

FX and export conditions

With an export share of 46.08% in the first half of 2025 according to press reports and roughly half of sales from overseas, exchange-rate moves could affect KRW-translated revenue and margins. Changes in overseas customer demand and export conditions also flow through to results. Trade-environment shifts in key export markets are another variable for revenue.

08

Overall view

IK Semicon raised its automotive share of sales to about 28% by 2023 and continues to develop new EV products, which are the main strengths in its business direction. On the other side, 2025 revenue fell 3.8% to KRW 17.15 billion and operating profit fell 13.8%, showing weaker profitability in the results.

The operating margin declined from 18.5% in 2022 to 11.3% in 2024, and the recovery did not continue in 2025. The gap between the 2024 outlook and the actual outcome illustrates how slowly business plans turn into profit.

The pace of certifying and ramping new products, and the exposure to exchange rates through overseas sales, are the main variables for future results. As a KONEX-listed stock with a market capitalization of KRW 10.6 billion, trading volume is small, so price and liquidity volatility are relevant. The current price of 3,400 KRW sits after a path from 8,000 KRW in February 2025 to 6,200 KRW in August 2025.
