02

Business structure

BCnC makes consumable parts used in the etching and thin-film deposition processes of semiconductor front-end manufacturing. Its main products include Ring, Tube, Dome, Baffle, Liner, Inner Cover and Plate, made from synthetic quartz, natural quartz, silicon and ceramic materials.

By material, QD9 and QD9+ parts accounted for 68% of revenue in the first half of 2025, followed by natural quartz at 16%, silicon at 7%, ceramics at 5% and other items at 4%.

The company localized synthetic quartz that it had previously imported and now produces in-house, citing lower costs, longer part life and suitability for finer processes as advantages over natural quartz.

The synthetic quartz material QD9+ is in mass supply, while CD9, a new material that replaces CVD-SiC parts, and monocrystalline and polycrystalline silicon ingots are supplied to customers in Korea and abroad.

Mass supply of silicon ingots to a North American customer started in earnest after the material localization succeeded. In summarizing its nine-month 2025 results, the company pointed to the success of material localization as the driver of improvement.

Sales run through two routes, direct delivery to chipmakers and delivery to semiconductor equipment makers, and chipmakers accounted for 67% of revenue as of the third quarter of 2025. The company describes demand as tied to faster replacement cycles for consumable parts as chip miniaturization advances.

Because synthetic quartz parts make up a large share of sales, material supply stability and customer qualification periods shape the revenue trajectory.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 82.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 65.3 billion in 2023, down 20.4% year on year, then rose 18.5% to KRW 77.4 billion in 2024 and 13.2% to KRW 87.6 billion in 2025, two straight years of growth from 2024 to 2025, with 2025 the largest revenue year of 2022 to 2025 and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 11.6 billion in 2022, a 14.1% margin, and turned into an operating loss of KRW 41.86 million in 2023, a -0.1% margin, as the company moved into the red. The operating loss widened to KRW 2.06 billion in 2024, a -2.7% margin, as losses continued.

Operating profit returned to KRW 3.93 billion in 2025, a 4.5% margin, a swing from loss to profit. Attributable net income fell 87.6% year on year to KRW 1.24 billion in 2023, became an attributable net loss of KRW 2.28 billion in 2024, and returned to attributable net income of KRW 1.15 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, a separate figure from attributable net income, was KRW 10.1 billion in 2022, KRW 1.27 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 2.27 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.15 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 21.9 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 23.9 billion in Q4 (up 9.3% quarter on quarter), dipped to KRW 23.7 billion in Q1 2026 (down 0.9%), then rose to KRW 26.8 billion in Q2 (up 12.9%) and KRW 30 billion in Q3 (up 12.0% quarter on quarter and 37.0% year on year), two consecutive quarters of growth after the Q1 2026 low.

Quarterly operating profit moved from KRW 1.38 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 1 billion in Q4 (down 27.2%) and KRW 802 million in Q1 2026 (down 20.2%), then recovered to KRW 1.84 billion in Q2 (up 129.1%) and KRW 2.5 billion in Q3 (up 36.3% quarter on quarter and 81.5% year on year).

Attributable net income fell from KRW 1.51 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 706 million in Q4 and KRW 285 million in Q1 2026, then rose to KRW 467 million in Q2 and KRW 1.24 billion in Q3, although the Q3 figure was down 18.1% year on year.

Attributable net income for the last four quarters, Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, totaled KRW 2.7 billion. Operating cash flow went from KRW 10.2 billion in 2022 to a negative KRW 643 million in 2023 and a negative KRW 547 million in 2024, before turning to KRW 2.12 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 51.3% in 2022 to 79.8% in 2023, 121.1% in 2024 and 125.3% in 2025, and at year-end 2025 total equity was KRW 78.8 billion, of which KRW 78.5 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 98.7 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In semiconductor etching, the importance of the process has risen as DRAM shrinks and NAND stacks higher, and demand for the high-priced consumable parts used in it has grown along with it.

Consumable parts have shorter replacement cycles as miniaturization advances, so repeat sales build up apart from new equipment investment.

Because a large share of BCnC's revenue comes from synthetic quartz parts, substitution competition with imported natural quartz and existing materials such as CVD-SiC is an important variable separate from the cycle. Localizing a material that had been imported is read as reducing dependence on outside suppliers.

BCnC sits in the same front-end value chain as listed peers Wonik IPS, Jusung Engineering and Tokai Carbon Korea, but product mixes and customer structures differ across companies. With chipmakers accounting for 67% of revenue as of Q3 2025, BCnC is directly exposed to memory and foundry investment flows.

The sales decline to KRW 65.3 billion in 2023 and the recovery in 2024 and 2025 overlap with the downturn and upturn of the equipment investment cycle, but a direct causal link is hard to establish.

Investment directions at large DRAM and NAND producers such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix are a major variable for consumable parts demand. With quarterly results in a recovery phase in 2026, how long the upturn lasts depends on the investment plans of these large manufacturers. Substitution competition and cycle swings together drive the direction of quarterly results.

Bull factors

Material localization and product diversification

QD9+, a synthetic quartz material, is in mass supply, and the company cites lower costs and longer part life as advantages. CD9 and monocrystalline and polycrystalline silicon ingots are also supplied to customers in Korea and abroad, which the company presents as product diversification. Replacing previously imported materials is read as reducing external supply-chain exposure.

Quarterly operating profit recovery

Operating profit has risen for two consecutive quarters since the Q1 2026 low. Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 2.5 billion, up 81.5% year on year, and revenue was KRW 30 billion, up 37.0% year on year. At the operating level, the quarterly trend is moving in a favorable direction.

Profit base and cash flow recovery

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 3.93 billion in 2025, and operating cash flow turned positive at KRW 2.12 billion. The recovery of operating cash flow, which was negative in 2023 and 2024, is a positive factor for earnings quality.

Operating profit has not yet returned to the KRW 11.6 billion level of 2022, so the extent of the recovery remains limited.

Bear factors

Operating gains not reaching net income

Attributable net income in Q3 2026 was KRW 1.24 billion, down 18.1% year on year. Attributable net income for the last four quarters totaled only KRW 2.7 billion. Compared with attributable net income of KRW 10 billion in 2022, the recovery in earnings scale is limited.

Higher financial leverage

The debt ratio rose from 51.3% in 2022 to 125.3% in 2025, and total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 98.7 billion. Negative operating cash flow in 2023 and 2024 may have added to reliance on borrowing. Even after the 2025 return to profit, the debt ratio was higher than in 2024.

Product and customer concentration, cycle sensitivity

QD9 and QD9+ parts accounted for 68% of revenue in the first half of 2025, a concentration in one product family. Chipmakers accounted for 67% of revenue as of Q3 2025. If customers' investment cycles turn down, demand for consumable parts could also be affected.

Risk factors

Industry and customer investment cycle

With chipmakers accounting for a high share of revenue, demand for consumable parts depends on front-end equipment investment and the pace of miniaturization. The drop in revenue to KRW 65.3 billion in 2023 shows how sensitive this structure is. Investment adjustments by large customers can quickly show up in quarterly results.

Material substitution and technology change

Even after localizing synthetic quartz, substitution relationships with other materials such as CVD-SiC keep shifting. The pace at which CD9 expands and the length of customer qualification could change the product mix and profitability.

In a structure concentrated on specific materials, demand changes in a single product family feed heavily into total revenue.

Financial structure and major shareholder stake transfer

The debt ratio of 125.3% is high, and operating cash flow was positive in 2025 but negative in 2023 and 2024. A September 2026 filing showed that 1,279,793 common shares held by the CEO decreased through gift, and that major shareholder Kim Dong-seok acquired the same number of shares as a gift. The effect of this stake transfer on management direction will become visible in later periodic filings.

Overall view

BCnC has rebuilt quarterly operating profit after turning to profit in 2025, supported by localizing materials such as synthetic quartz. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 30 billion and operating profit was KRW 2.5 billion, up 37.0% and 81.5% year on year respectively.

Attributable net income in the same quarter was KRW 1.24 billion, down 18.1% year on year, so the pass-through of operating gains to net income is still in progress.

Compared with the KRW 11.6 billion operating profit of 2022, the earnings scale is in a recovery phase, and attributable net income for the last four quarters totaled KRW 2.7 billion.

On the financial side, the debt ratio rose from 51.3% in 2022 to 125.3% in 2025, while operating cash flow turned positive at KRW 2.12 billion in 2025.

Strengths are material localization and quarterly improvement, while weaknesses are slow net income recovery, higher financial leverage, and concentration in products and customers.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report, where the link between operating profit and attributable net income will become visible. This report is for information only and does not include a buy or sell opinion or a target price.
