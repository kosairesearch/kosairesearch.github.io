Konex Dental Micromotor Maker Faces Thinner Margins

02

Business structure

Micro-NX was founded in July 2001 and is headquartered in Dong-gu, Daegu. It manufactures dental medical devices and listed on the Konex market on December 21, 2022.

Its business report says it was the first in Korea to secure source technology for compact Swiss-type precision micromotors, and it makes dental devices in-house for domestic and overseas sale.

The product range covers dental surgical handpieces, clinical micromotors, implant surgery products, endodontic products, laboratory products and nail care products. The company website says it developed a precision brushless motor of up to 150,000 rpm and registered 20 patents on compact Swiss-type BLDC motors.

It also cites more than 30 intellectual property rights and a 2011 selection as a World-Class Product producer in the dental handpiece category. It further states that it holds manufacturing technology for precision small motor parts for robotics and aerospace, but the revenue share of that area has not been confirmed.

As of the 2022 business report, BA Korea was a subsidiary and Ningbo Micro-NX Medical Instrument of China was a 50%-owned associate. Revenue by product line and market share are not available in public materials, so they are described only qualitatively.

In the global implant market, large players such as Straumann, Dentsply Sirona, Zimmer Biomet and Nobel Biocare are developing proprietary implant systems. Micro-NX is a small supplier in the motor and handpiece equipment portion of that treatment ecosystem.

Large electronic-component names that share its sector tag, such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek, run very different businesses and are not appropriate direct comparables.

Operating profit fell from about KRW 528 million in 2024 to about KRW 368 million in 2025, and the operating margin moved from 7.07% (2023) and 6.40% (2024) to 4.70%.

04

Outlook

On the demand side, the domestic dental implant industry is expanding. Per Ministry of Food and Drug Safety statistics, production of dental implant fixtures rose 12.2% to KRW 2,442.9 billion in 2025. Dental implant surgical instruments ranked eighth among production items at KRW 202.3 billion.

Implant fixture exports reached USD 399.37 million, second among medical device exports. Market research firms project global implant growth of roughly 7% to 9% a year, although their market-size estimates differ widely.

For example, Coherent Market Insights projects a 7.5% CAGR for 2026-2033, while Grand View Research projects 9.0%. The industry is shifting from analog care toward digitally integrated implant dentistry, which raises the importance of digital-workflow compatibility for handpiece and motor suppliers.

Whether this industry growth feeds into Micro-NX's revenue has not yet shown up in reported results, because the company's revenue fell from 2022 to 2025 while the industry indicators rose.

Items to monitor are whether the revenue decline stops, whether the operating margin recovers, how non-operating costs and the borrowing structure change, and how much the precision motor business for robotics and aerospace actually contributes.

05

Bull factors

Proprietary motor technology and IP

The company points to a precision brushless motor of up to 150,000 rpm, 20 patents on compact Swiss-type BLDC motors and more than 30 intellectual property rights in total. Compact precision motors are generally not an easy field to enter, and the products also serve nail care and dental laboratory uses beyond clinics.

The stated capability in robotics and aerospace components is a potential avenue for expansion. Whether it has translated into revenue diversification has not been confirmed.

Growth in the implant industry

Domestic implant fixture production rose 12.2% in 2025, and surgical instruments ranked eighth among production items. Higher procedure volumes can broaden the demand base for treatment equipment such as motors and handpieces. A forecast also puts global implant market growth at a 7.5% CAGR. This is an industry-level tailwind and does not guarantee results for an individual company.

Operating profit and cash inflow maintained

Operating profit was positive in every year from 2022 to 2025. In 2025, operating cash flow was an inflow of about KRW 391 million and cash operating profit was about KRW 888 million. The net loss stemmed mainly from non-operating costs rather than operations, which indicates the core business still generates earnings. The 4.70% operating margin has come down, but room for margin improvement remains.

Bear factors

Three straight years of revenue decline

Revenue growth was -4.38% in 2023, -1.22% in 2024 and -5.19% in 2025, three consecutive declines. Given that domestic implant production grew over the same period, one reading is that the company's growth has not kept pace with the industry. The operating margin also slid from 7.07% to 4.70%. Without segment revenue data, the cause of the decline cannot be pinned down.

Heavy balance sheet burden

Non-operating expenses of about KRW 730 million in 2025 were nearly double operating profit of about KRW 368 million. The debt ratio was 118.50%, borrowings were 47.57% of assets, current liabilities exceeded current assets and the quick ratio was 33.31%.

The structure carries a relatively heavy short-term repayment burden. Net income could be sensitive to interest rates and borrowing terms.

Extremely thin trading and limited coverage

Three-month average volume was only 334 shares, and traded value on October 27, 2026 was KRW 711, about one share. The 52-week price range of KRW 550 to KRW 2,425 is wide, so even small trades can move the price sharply. No brokerage research or target-price assessments could be confirmed. Limited information access and liquidity are points for investors to weigh.

07

Risk factors

Macro

The company sells domestically and exports, so it is exposed to exchange rates and overseas dental demand. High treatment costs and limited reimbursement for implants are cited as constraints on market growth.

A consumer slowdown or lower disposable income could reduce procedure volumes and spill over into equipment demand. With a high share of borrowings, interest-rate changes can also affect net income.

Industry and competition

Premium and value implant systems are splitting the market, and suppliers now compete on clinical evidence and digital compatibility rather than hardware alone. Large implant companies may bundle treatment equipment into their own platforms.

Small equipment suppliers could see their bargaining power weaken in that process. The cost of developing and certifying new products is also a burden.

Company-specific and liquidity

The company recorded a net loss in fiscal 2025 and its current liabilities exceeded current assets. Given the Konex market's characteristics, trades are infrequent and price impact can be large.

As of May 2025, Enlight Youth Startup Fund 1 was recorded as holding 5.37%, and changes in major holdings can affect supply and demand. The trend should be tracked through 2026 interim results and disclosures.

08

Overall view

Micro-NX is a Konex-listed maker of dental medical devices built on compact precision motor technology, and despite its sector tag its business differs from that of large electronic-component names.

In fiscal 2025 revenue fell for a third straight year, the operating margin slipped to 4.70% and the company posted a net loss. Non-operating costs and the borrowing burden emerged as the swing factors for net income.

On the other hand, operating profit and operating cash inflow were maintained, and domestic implant production and exports grew in 2025, which kept the industry backdrop supportive. Whether that industry growth feeds into a revenue recovery at this company is hard to judge because 2026 results could not be confirmed.

With a market capitalization of KRW 1.9 billion and very thin trading, information and liquidity are significantly constrained, so follow-up checks should focus on revenue trend, margins and the borrowing structure in upcoming periodic reports.
