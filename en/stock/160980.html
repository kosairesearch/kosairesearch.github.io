Cymechs: Profit Recovery Tied to the Equipment Cycle

Cymechs has shown a profit recovery since the first quarter of 2026, with revenue and operating profit rising together after a net loss in the fourth quarter of 2025, but its results still depend heavily on chip equipment investment cycles.

  1. 1

    Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 71.1 billion, up 72.5% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 13.4 billion, up 116.5%.

  2. 2

    Full-year 2025 revenue was KRW 164.4 billion, down 0.4%, and operating profit was KRW 23.3 billion, down 0.3%, a broadly flat year. The operating margin was 14.2%.

  3. 3

    Profit attributable to owners of the parent swung from a net loss of KRW 436 million in Q4 2025 to a profit of KRW 3.96 billion in Q1 2026, and reached KRW 10.7 billion in Q3 2026.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Cymechs makes wafer transfer equipment that moves wafers between process tools in semiconductor fabs. According to its 2025 annual report, the company manufactures CTS, EFEM and LPM wafer transfer equipment.

Its core lineup includes EFEM units, LPM (FOUP opener) units, ATM robots for EFEM and vacuum robots, packaged as transfer chambers. Orders move with the timing of new line additions and process transitions at chipmakers.

On the back-end side, the company has secured TC bonder equipment makers as customers to expand back-end wafer transfer sales for HBM production. It supplies wafer transfer equipment to major customers including HANMI Semiconductor and ASMPT.

On the front-end side, it expects orders to rise as Samsung Electronics resumes investment for its P4 and Taylor fabs. The company is in-sourcing Japanese-made articulated ATM robots to pursue sales growth and higher margins.

It has broadened its back-end line-up with probe stations and test handlers, and is pursuing entry into advanced materials for semiconductors. Using its wafer transfer capacity, the company aims to keep its market share at a high level and grow sales over the long term. As of June 30, 2026, it had 227 employees.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 195.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 156.6 billion in 2023 (down 19.7% year on year), recovered to KRW 165.1 billion in 2024 (up 5.4%), and stood at KRW 164.4 billion in 2025 (down 0.4%). 2022 was the largest year for revenue and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 21.2 billion in 2022, KRW 7.11 billion in 2023 (down 66.4%), KRW 23.4 billion in 2024 (up 229.1%) and KRW 23.3 billion in 2025 (down 0.3%). 2024 was the largest year for operating profit and 2023 the smallest.

The operating margin fell from 10.8% in 2022 to 4.5% in 2023 and rose back to 14.2% in both 2024 and 2025. Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 7.64 billion in 2022, KRW 11 billion in 2023 (up 44.2%), KRW 15.4 billion in 2024 (up 39.9%) and KRW 14.9 billion in 2025 (down 3.1%).

In 2023 operating profit fell while owner profit rose, so non-operating items matter when reading that year. Operating cash flow moved from KRW -627 million in 2022 to KRW 27.6 billion in 2023, KRW 12.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 43.7 billion in 2025.

In Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 41.2 billion, operating profit KRW 6.2 billion and owner profit KRW 4.21 billion. Q4 2025 revenue fell to KRW 29.1 billion (down 29.4% quarter on quarter) and operating profit to KRW 2.35 billion (down 62.1%), and the company posted a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 436 million.

Because owner profit fell more sharply than operating profit, the reported figures alone do not separate out non-operating effects. In Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 40.5 billion (up 39.3%), operating profit KRW 4.73 billion (up 101.7%) and owner profit KRW 3.96 billion, a return to profit.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 62.5 billion, operating profit KRW 11.3 billion and owner profit KRW 9.01 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue reached KRW 71.1 billion, up 13.7% quarter on quarter and 72.5% year on year; operating profit was KRW 13.4 billion, up 18.8% and 116.5%; and owner profit was KRW 10.7 billion, up 155.1% year on year. Operating profit growth outpaced revenue growth, so profit recovered faster than sales. Over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, owner profit totaled KRW 23.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Wafer transfer equipment moves with front-end and back-end fab investment. The company's own commentary on its Q1 2026 results says the semiconductor equipment industry is directly affected by device makers' investment cycles, and that results fell on investment adjustments by device makers.

The same commentary says AI infrastructure investment is concentrating equipment spending on advanced processes. A securities research report published in April 2026 judged that steady back-end revenue growth is expected as HBM investment continues.

The same report said sample testing of transfer equipment with major domestic glass substrate makers is under way, with mass production to be decided after results are checked in the second half of 2026. The report expected the ASP of glass substrate equipment to be at least twice as high as existing equipment.

In cycle terms, annual revenue has moved sideways around KRW 160 billion since the 2023 low, and quarterly revenue has risen from the prior quarter in 2026. HANMI Semiconductor, which operates in the same back-end equipment flow, is both a customer of Cymechs and an HBM equipment maker.

The company's profile materials present maintaining a near-monopoly share in transfer equipment as a long-term goal.

Bull factors

Fast quarterly profit recovery

Owner profit turned to a profit of KRW 3.96 billion in Q1 2026, then rose to KRW 9.01 billion in Q2 and KRW 10.7 billion in Q3. Q3 2026 operating profit grew 116.5% year on year, ahead of the 72.5% revenue growth. Over the last four quarters owner profit totaled KRW 23.3 billion. The recovery in profit has been larger than the recovery in sales in this period.

Financial cushion and cash generation

The debt ratio fell to 15.3% at end-2025 from 46.7% in 2022. Operating cash flow reached KRW 43.7 billion in 2025, up from KRW 12.3 billion in 2024. Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 194.2 billion, and equity attributable to owners of the parent was the same amount. The financial cushion gives the company room to respond during equipment investment phases.

HBM back-end and front-end order hopes

The company says it has secured TC bonder equipment makers as customers to expand back-end wafer transfer sales for HBM production. Whether front-end orders increase depends on how quickly Samsung Electronics resumes investment at its P4 and Taylor fabs.

A securities report published in April 2026 judged that steady back-end revenue growth is expected as HBM investment continues. Whether this flow continues will show up in future quarterly orders and revenue.

Bear factors

Earnings swings with the cycle

Revenue fell 19.7% in 2023 and operating profit fell 66.4%, with the operating margin dropping to 4.5%. The company's commentary on Q1 2026 says sales and profit fell year on year on investment adjustments by device makers.

The historical figures show that transfer equipment revenue can fall quickly when equipment investment is adjusted.

Flat 2025 and a fourth-quarter loss

Full-year 2025 revenue fell 0.4% and owner profit fell 3.1%. In Q4 2025, revenue dropped 29.4% from the prior quarter and the company posted a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 436 million. Even after the profit recovery, annual revenue stayed near the 2024 level. Whether the quarterly rebound adds up to a larger annual figure will show in the next annual report.

Uncertain payoff from new businesses

Glass substrate transfer equipment is at the sample testing stage, and a securities report expects mass production to be decided after results are checked in the second half of 2026. Entry into advanced materials and in-sourcing of ATM robots are hard to see clearly in reported earnings so far.

If new businesses are slow to pay off, it becomes harder to judge whether the current recovery can last. When these businesses show up in earnings will be visible in the company's quarterly disclosures.

Risk factors

Equipment investment cycle

The company's commentary says the equipment industry is directly affected by device makers' investment cycles. In 2023 revenue fell 19.7% and operating profit fell 66.4%, showing the size of swings. In investment adjustment phases, quarterly results can decline year on year, as in Q1 2026. How much the profit recovery depends on the upcycle will be seen in later quarters.

Dependence on customer investment timing

Whether investment resumes at Samsung Electronics' P4 and Taylor fabs and turns into orders depends on the customer's investment timing. In back-end, revenue moves with equipment spending by specific customers such as TC bonder makers, HANMI Semiconductor and ASMPT.

A delay at one large customer could affect quarterly revenue. Changes in the customer mix will appear in quarterly revenue trends.

Execution on new businesses and products

Mass production of glass substrate transfer equipment, ATM robot in-sourcing and entry into advanced materials are all still at the execution stage. Glass substrate equipment needs dedicated tools because its size and material differ from existing wafer and PCB equipment.

If new product mass production is delayed, the timing of planned margin gains could also slip. Progress on these projects will continue to show up in company disclosures and quarterly results.
