02

Business structure

Elensys designs and manufactures core components for home appliance and security solution products, and has been listed on KOSDAQ since June 1, 2017.

Its main products include stepping motors, nozzles and motis locks, with air-care and home appliances such as bidets, vacuum cleaners and water purifiers, along with digital door locks, as its key end markets.

A Jobkorea company profile shows a product mix of motis locks at 45 percent, nozzles at 16 percent, geared motors at 14 percent and stepping motors at 14 percent, though the reference date for that mix is not stated.

Customers are mostly domestic and overseas appliance makers and door-lock manufacturers, including Samsung Electronics, according to a BusinessReport article.

Production is centered on two overseas subsidiaries in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City in Vietnam, and the dual base is cited as a source of cost competitiveness and supply stability. On the competitive side, the company's technology strength rests on specialized R&D staff and continuous development.

Its scope has widened toward robot components, and it was described in January 2026 coverage as a core supplier of reducers for a Samsung wearable robot after localizing robot reducers. No public figure exists for the sales contribution of robot components.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales were KRW 82.6 billion in 2022, fell 17.1 percent to KRW 68.4 billion in 2023, rose 4.5 percent to KRW 71.6 billion in 2024, and fell 2.8 percent to KRW 69.6 billion in 2025. Over 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for sales and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 5.47 billion in 2022, KRW 5.45 billion in 2023 (down 0.4 percent), and KRW 5.85 billion in 2024 (up 7.5 percent), before falling 33.6 percent to KRW 3.89 billion in 2025.

The operating margin moved from 6.6 percent in 2022 to 8.0 percent in 2023 and 8.2 percent in 2024, then dropped to 5.6 percent in 2025. The company attributed the 2025 decline to lower sales and profitability in its air-care and home appliance segment, as reported by Digital Today.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.17 billion in 2022, KRW 4.98 billion in 2023, KRW 4.99 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.24 billion in 2025, down 14.9 percent. The quarterly pattern runs the other way.

Sales rose from KRW 16.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 17.6 billion, KRW 19.1 billion, KRW 20.7 billion and KRW 23.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026, which is four consecutive quarterly increases.

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 671 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, down 41.5 percent from the prior quarter, then rose to KRW 1.18 billion, KRW 1.37 billion and KRW 1.44 billion, which is three consecutive quarterly increases.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders, however, went from KRW 1.76 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 184 million in the second quarter, a drop of 89.5 percent, before recovering to KRW 1.44 billion in the third quarter.

The wide gap between operating profit and controlling-shareholder net profit in the second quarter of 2026 shows how volatile quarterly earnings have been. Over the most recent four quarters, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 4.34 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for appliance components went through a downturn after the COVID-19 boom ended, when domestic and overseas appliance markets contracted.

In a 2023 report, a Nice D&B analyst said the premium and small appliance markets maintained steady growth, and the same report pointed to Elensys's active R&D in premium appliance and security solution components. A 2025 earnings article also credited rising demand for premium appliance lines with lifting results.

The new demand source is robotics. Hyundai Motor Group showed its next-generation electric Atlas at CES in January 2026 and said it would allocate KRW 50 trillion over five years to future businesses including AI and robots.

Elensys has been classified as a beneficiary of robot market growth because it supplies components for robot vacuums and wearable robots. Concerns were also raised at the same time that the robot market could take considerable time to reach full scale.

In cycle terms, the pace of recovery in appliance demand and the pace of robot commercialization are moving on separate tracks.

Within the competitive landscape, the company designs its motor, nozzle and door-lock drive parts in-house and tailors them to customer specifications, which places it as a component supplier rather than a finished-goods maker.

Product development milestones it can be checked against include a solar TCU in June 2025, a water purifier heater and a WIDE BOWL CLEAN unit in September 2025, and the DPM-10D bidet pump in June 2026, according to Saramin company information.

On the robotics side, the key question is whether localized robot reducers and wearable robot supply expand, as Wide Daily reported. Production efficiency through the Vietnam subsidiaries and expanded R&D spending are cited as medium-term improvement factors by ThinkPool.

Whether both trends continue into the fourth quarter of 2026 is a point the annual report will show. Whether the company discloses robot component sales as a figure is another central variable. The segment explanations in future filings will show whether home appliances or robotics carry more weight.

Bull factors

Consecutive quarterly gains in sales and operating profit

Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 23.2 billion, up 36.9 percent year on year, and operating profit was KRW 1.44 billion, up 25.9 percent. Sales have risen for four consecutive quarters since the fourth quarter of 2025, and operating profit has risen for three consecutive quarters since its low in that quarter.

The quarterly turn comes after the 2025 operating margin fell to 5.6 percent, which is the basis for the bullish reading.

Expansion into robot components

Elensys has been named a core supplier after localizing robot reducers and supplying them for a Samsung wearable robot, according to Wide Daily. Hyundai Motor Group's plan to allocate KRW 50 trillion to AI and robotics over five years led to its mention among robot component stocks.

No public figure exists for the sales contribution from robot components, so the business weight will need to be confirmed in future filings.

A balance sheet close to debt-free

The debt-to-equity ratio was 23.4 percent at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 12.6 billion against total equity of KRW 53.9 billion. The ratio has declined each year from 43.7 percent in 2022.

Operating cash flow was KRW 6.33 billion in 2022, KRW 9.56 billion in 2023, KRW 7.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.16 billion in 2025, positive in all four years. ThinkPool cites Vietnam production efficiency and expanded R&D as medium-term improvement factors.

Annual sales have not returned to the 2022 level of KRW 82.6 billion, reaching KRW 69.6 billion in 2025 after falling to KRW 68.4 billion in 2023.

Sharp swings in quarterly net profit

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 89.5 percent from the prior quarter to KRW 184 million in the second quarter of 2026, while operating profit that quarter was KRW 1.37 billion. The gap between operating profit and net profit was especially wide in that quarter.

A high premium relative to earnings

Market capitalization is more than twice total equity at the end of 2025, a sizable premium over net asset value. Risk factors

Customer concentration and appliance demand

Revenue is concentrated in home appliance and door-lock components, which makes the company sensitive to changes in domestic and overseas appliance demand. The 2025 decline in the air-care and home appliance segment is an example.

Order changes from a few customers can move quarterly results significantly, and customer disclosures in the annual report are the place to check this.

Pace of robotics commercialization

Expectations for the robot market are high, but a BusinessReport article noted that it could take considerable time to reach full scale. Because robot component sales have not been disclosed as a figure, expectations can run ahead of reported results. The timing at which robot orders or supply appear in reported numbers is the key point to watch.

Quarterly earnings volatility and cost pressure

FnGuide reported that cost burdens and higher expenses reduced profitability in the first nine months of 2025. Operating profit bottomed at KRW 671 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 184 million in the second quarter of 2026. Because quarterly swings are large, the annual trend needs to be read with care.
