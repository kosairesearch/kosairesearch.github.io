02

Business structure

Luxpia is a small enterprise founded in April 2000 that designs and manufactures high-output LED packages and modules for the semiconductor and display industries.

According to the company profile, its main products are SMD-type LEDs used in mobile phone, PDA and LCD backlights, and vertical-type LEDs used in traffic signals, electronic billboards and automotive applications.

Based on the JobKorea company profile, the business mix is LED packages 69%, LED modules 30% and AI dolls 1%. The 2020 business report shows LED packages at 99.5% of revenue, which suggests the module share has risen since then.

The business began with an OEM production agreement with a Japanese LED maker in 2006, and a strategic alliance agreement was added in 2013. In 2014 the company began supplying automotive LEDs to Volkswagen, and in 2017 it received SQ certification from Hyundai and Kia.

The same 2020 business report lists an operating rate of 92.5% for the 1608-size product and 41.8% for the TOP VIEW product, a wide gap across product lines. THE VC's company profile names Jeju Semiconductor, Woori Bio, Raontech and Advanced Power Semiconductor as similar companies.

As of December 31, 2025, the company had 66 employees, consistent with a small enterprise, and its headquarters is in the Nano Device Specialized Fab Center in Suwon, Gyeonggi.

Recent trends

On a separate-financial-statement basis for fiscal 2025 (26th term), revenue was KRW 15,937 million, operating loss was KRW 1,263 million and net loss was KRW 1,858 million, with a loss per share of KRW 255.

At the March 23, 2026 annual general meeting, the 26th-term financial statements were approved, and the external auditor issued an unqualified opinion on the separate statements.

According to a Digital Today AI disclosure report, total assets on the same basis were KRW 18,709 million, total liabilities were KRW 7,053 million and total equity was KRW 11,655 million.

Revenue rose from KRW 15,300 million in 2019 to KRW 22,500 million in 2020, then fell to KRW 16,700 million in 2021, when operating profit was KRW 1,000 million. In 2025, revenue sat well below the 2020 level, and the company moved from operating profit to operating loss.

An investor relations briefing was held on April 15, 2026, and a corrected 2025 business report was disclosed on June 29, 2026. Outlook

The key variable for the business going forward is whether the revenue base recovers. Revenue fell from about KRW 22,500 million in 2020 to KRW 15,937 million in 2025, so demand for the core LED packages and module volumes will likely drive results.

According to the company overview, Luxpia is expanding its North American base, has begun contacts with new agents to enter Europe, and is considering establishing a local subsidiary for LED packaging. In 2024 it carried out national R&D projects covering fixed-type, windshield and SPAD-based technologies.

The business is moving toward industries that use LED light sources, including TVs, lighting, displays and automobiles. If operating losses continue in 2025, total equity of KRW 11,655 million would decline, so improvement in profitability is a central issue.

The next disclosure point is the third-quarter 2026 report, which will show quarterly revenue and operating profit or loss trends. Because the company carries the fixed-cost and raw-material cost structure typical of manufacturers, changes in utilization feed directly into margins.

Risk factors

Macro

Demand for LED packages moves with the cycles of end-use industries such as TVs, lighting, displays and automobiles. A slowdown in display and automotive component demand could reduce orders. Exchange rates and raw material prices also affect manufacturing costs.

Because the cost base is weighted toward fixed and raw-material expenses, the company is sensitive to changes in utilization.

Business

The business relies on contracts and technical cooperation with an overseas partner, so changes in those terms could affect operations. With revenue down since 2020, delays in winning new customers could extend the losses. The local subsidiary and European entry are still under review, so timing and cost are not yet set.

Market liquidity

As a small KONEX-listed company with market capitalization around KRW 5.2 billion, its shares trade in thin volume. Prices can move sharply over short periods, and the current price is far below the past peak. Because the company is small, analyst coverage tends to be limited.

Overall view

Luxpia is a KONEX-listed small enterprise focused on LED packages and modules, and for fiscal 2025 it reported revenue of KRW 15,937 million and an operating loss of KRW 1,263 million. Revenue declined from about KRW 22,500 million in 2020, and earnings moved from operating profit in 2021 to losses in 2025.

On the negative side, the key questions are whether losses persist, whether revenue recovers, and the liquidity constraints tied to a market capitalization of about KRW 5.2 billion.

Overseas expansion and national R&D projects are presented as factors that could widen the medium-term revenue base, but the timing of any link to reported results has not yet appeared in disclosures.

The central issues at this point are the direction of profitability and whether the revenue decline stops, both of which can be tracked through quarterly reports.
