02

Business structure

Luxpia was founded in April 2000 and, in South Korea, produces and sells LED devices and application products, developing and manufacturing LED packages for TVs, lighting, displays and automobiles. It listed on the KONEX market on October 30, 2015, and is classified as a semiconductor manufacturer.

The classification refers to optical semiconductors (LEDs), so the business differs sharply from memory and foundry-centered large caps such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix.

In the fiscal 2020 annual report, LED packages made up 99.5% of sales and LED modules 0.5%. We found no more recent segment breakdown, so the current mix is described only qualitatively, and packages appear to account for most of revenue.

The company is reported to receive LED chips and phosphors through a partnership with Japan's Nichia, the world's largest LED maker. Reliance on a specific partner for inputs is both a strength and a dependency.

In automotive, the company holds a record of converting to the IATF16949:2016 standard and obtaining Hyundai and Kia SQ certification in 2017, administered by Hyundai Mobis. A 2020 press report said it supplied LEDs as a tier-1 vendor to Renault Samsung, Volkswagen and GM.

The same report also mentioned supply to customers in China, the United States and Japan, and a review of setting up overseas local subsidiaries. In scale terms, it is a small manufacturer with 66 employees according to TradingView.

03

Recent trends

On a disclosed standalone basis for fiscal 2025 (26th term), total assets were KRW 18.709 billion, total liabilities KRW 7.053 billion and total equity KRW 11.655 billion.

On the same basis, revenue was KRW 15.937 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 1.263 billion, a net loss of KRW 1.858 billion and a loss per share of KRW 255. Revenue fell 10.8% from the prior year.

In fiscal 2024, revenue rose sharply to roughly KRW 17.858 billion, but the company still recorded an operating loss of roughly KRW 1.074 billion. Growth in sales therefore did not lift the company out of operating losses.

The long-run record shows large revenue swings. On FnGuide's figures, 2020 revenue was KRW 22.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 3.9 billion, and 2021 revenue fell to KRW 16.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.0 billion. Revenue was then KRW 14.82 billion in 2022 and KRW 10.15 billion in 2023. On the balance sheet, the current ratio declined from 435.5% in 2023 to 197% in 2025.

The share price is volatile, as is common for small KONEX names. The 52-week range is KRW 406 to KRW 1,146 (as of 2026-10-01). The all-time high is KRW 6,490, reached on 2021-10-21. On 2026-10-16, the stock closed at KRW 590, up 11.95%.

As of 17:02 KST on 2026-10-27, the price is KRW 680, down 4.09% from the previous session, and quoted turnover is KRW 60,980. On 2025-12-09, a KONEX-listed Luxpia hit the daily upper limit on a single-share trade.

Early July 2026 coverage reported that Luxpia was designated an investment-warning stock, and trading-halt notices were reported the same month. We could not confirm the specific reasons for either action.

Outlook

We found no earnings guidance or order plan published by the company in this research. The outlook is therefore limited to a qualitative framework based on the disclosed financial trend and market structure. The first variable is revenue scale.

Because sales rose and fell sharply after 2023, the level at which revenue settles in fiscal 2026 determines the fixed-cost burden.

The second variable is operating margin. With operating losses in both fiscal 2024 and fiscal 2025, any narrowing of the loss through better utilization and product mix would be an improvement in business metrics.

Whether higher value-added areas such as automotive and high-power LEDs change in weight is also worth watching. We could not confirm specific mix data.

The third variable is the KONEX market environment. According to the Korea Exchange, at the end of July 2026 KONEX had 109 listed companies, a market capitalization of KRW 3,392.9 billion and average daily turnover of KRW 1.28 billion. The same article put individual investors at about 90% of KONEX turnover.

A December 10, 2025 report said financial authorities and the exchange were discussing a market overhaul that includes revitalizing KONEX, with related policy expected to take shape in 2026. The content and timing of any policy change are not settled.

The company disclosed the result of an investor relations (IR) event on 2026-04-15 and a corrected fiscal 2025 annual report on 2026-06-29. Later filings and the fiscal 2026 quarterly and half-year results will provide the concrete basis for assessment.

05

Bull factors

Book equity above market capitalization

Total equity is KRW 11.655 billion on a disclosed basis. Market capitalization is KRW 5.0 billion on the 2026-10-27 quote, so book equity exceeds it. The debt ratio is reported at 60.52%. Still, the realizable value of assets and earning power are separate matters, so book figures alone cannot settle the question.

Automotive certifications and a global partner

The company has a record of converting to the IATF16949 standard and obtaining Hyundai and Kia SQ certification. Automotive components are generally an area with long qualification processes that make entry harder for new suppliers.

Sourcing chips and phosphors through the Nichia partnership is also a quality-side foundation. Whether this foundation translates into profitability must be checked against future disclosed results.

Revenue rebound record and room to narrow losses

Revenue was KRW 10.15 billion in fiscal 2023 but grew to roughly KRW 17.858 billion in fiscal 2024. This shows the company has scaled sales quickly in the past when demand returned. In a fixed-cost-heavy manufacturing model, higher utilization leaves room for operating margin to improve. However, revenue fell again in fiscal 2025 and operating losses continued.

06

Bear factors

Two straight years of operating losses and a wider net loss

Fiscal 2025 standalone operating loss was KRW 1.263 billion and net loss was KRW 1.858 billion. Because the net loss exceeds the operating loss, non-operating items or tax effects may have added to it. We could not confirm the detailed cause in this research. Fiscal 2024 also recorded an operating loss of roughly KRW 1.074 billion.

Large revenue swings and a lower current ratio

Revenue of KRW 22.5 billion in 2020 fell to KRW 16.7 billion in 2021, and dropped to KRW 10.15 billion in 2023. Fiscal 2025 revenue also declined 10.8% year on year. The current ratio fell from 435.5% in 2023 to 197% in 2025, which reduces short-term payment capacity. If losses continue, the pace of equity erosion could weigh on this metric.

Extremely thin trading and price distortion

Quoted turnover on 2026-10-27 is KRW 60,980. On 2025-12-09, a single-share trade produced an upper-limit close, and one more share then traded 4.8% lower. Average daily turnover for the whole KONEX market was only KRW 1.28 billion at the end of July 2026.

In such conditions, quotes may not fully reflect business value, and trading at a desired time may be difficult.

Risk factors

Macro

LED packages are components whose end demand in TVs, lighting and automobiles is sensitive to the economic cycle. With some overseas customers, changes in exchange rates and the trade environment could affect sales and costs. Price competition within the industry could also weigh on profitability. We could not confirm the company's revenue split by region or its currency exposure in this research.

Market and liquidity

One report says individuals account for about 90% of KONEX turnover, while institutions account for only 4.97%. When a few trades move the price sharply, a stock can become subject to the market warning system. Luxpia was also reported as designated an investment-warning stock in early July 2026. Investment-warning stocks are barred from margin trading, and trading may be halted if prices surge further.

Business and financial

As a small manufacturer, fixed-cost burden and input sourcing directly affect earnings. Receiving chips and phosphors through the Nichia partnership means exposure to any change in supply terms. If operating and net losses continue, total equity could shrink and liquidity metrics could weaken further. We could not confirm the company's borrowing structure or maturity schedule in this research.

08

Overall view

Luxpia is a small manufacturer centered on LED packages; it is classified as a semiconductor company, but its business differs in nature from the large semiconductor names.

On a disclosed basis, fiscal 2025 brought revenue of KRW 15.937 billion, an operating loss of KRW 1.263 billion and a net loss of KRW 1.858 billion. An operating loss was also recorded in fiscal 2024, so a recovery in core profitability has not yet shown up in disclosed results.

On the other side, total equity of KRW 11.655 billion, a debt ratio in the 60% range, automotive certifications and a global partner are cited as foundations of the business.

The extremely thin trading typical of KONEX, along with price volatility and the possibility of market-warning designations, is a separate consideration.

The key items to check going forward are whether revenue stabilizes and how the operating loss changes in the fiscal 2026 quarterly and half-year results; this report is for information only, and investment decisions are left to the reader.
