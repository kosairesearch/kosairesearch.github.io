02

Business structure

Konasol is a metal composite materials maker with its headquarters and plant in Dangjin, South Chungcheong Province. It began in 1990 as a sole proprietorship, went through several name changes, took its current name in March 2022 and listed on KONEX in June of that year.

The Korea Exchange's technical analysis report classifies its industry as other fabricated metal products. Its business therefore does not directly overlap with the large listed companies labeled as chemicals.

The main products are rolling rolls, guide rollers and delivery rolls, which are core parts of steel production equipment. According to the report, it is the only company in Korea to have localized titanium carbide alloy steel.

Customers include domestic steelmakers such as POSCO, Hyundai Steel and Dongkuk Steel, as well as global equipment makers such as Danieli, Primetals and SMS Meer. Sales lean heavily toward steel, and in 2023 the steel business generated KRW 16.4 billion against KRW 1.0 billion for the mobility business.

In new businesses, it is developing and mass-producing sputtering targets for semiconductors, film heaters for electric vehicles and neutron absorbers for nuclear power. It has signed a letter of intent on strategic cooperation in sputtering targets with Materion of the United States. The company also sells some lubricant performance enhancers that use nanoparticle dispersion technology.

03

Recent trends

Konasol's results are drawn from K-GAAP standalone figures. In 2022 it reported sales of KRW 22.1 billion, operating profit of KRW 1.7 billion and net profit of KRW 1.4 billion. In 2023 lower sales, a weaker cost ratio and heavier SG&A pushed it to an operating loss of KRW 2.6 billion.

In 2024 sales were KRW 18.9 billion with an operating loss of KRW 3.3 billion. According to Dealsite, 2025 sales were KRW 19.9 billion and the operating loss was KRW 2.3 billion, a narrower loss than a year earlier.

Net profit of KRW 18.0 billion was a swing back to profit that reflected the effect of a tangible asset sale. The company filed a decision to transfer tangible assets on 2 June 2025, and that gain is largely one-off in nature. The shares closed at KRW 3,345 on 13 February 2026, with a 12-month change of -47.38%.

On 31 March 2026 the shares closed at KRW 2,870. The price on 27 October 2026 is KRW 2,000, and the 52-week range compiled by Itooza runs from KRW 1,801 to KRW 6,990. Trading value that day was only KRW 913,741, so liquidity is very thin. We could not confirm results after the first half of 2026 through our searches.

04

Outlook

The nuclear-materials business is central to the outlook. According to Dealsite, the first shipment of metal-matrix material was planned from August 2026. Whether it actually shipped and was booked as revenue could not be confirmed through our searches.

Earlier, a 10-year neutron absorber supply agreement with Orano was described as worth KRW 40 billion, expandable to KRW 120 billion if additional wet-storage orders were won. The mass-production contract reported this time is only about KRW 600 million, a wide gap from that framework agreement.

The start of mass production has also shifted several times, as seen in a 2023 report of mass production beginning in April and a report describing a November 2024 production start.

In Korea, the company plans to seek a role in the dry storage business for spent nuclear fuel as demand expands, but no specific order has been confirmed.

In its core business, it brought in a high-performance large HIP unit in July 2024 and completed localization verification, which underpins production of large single-block rolls.

Room for operating margin improvement depends on growth in new-business sales, a recovery in steel demand and the trend in raw material prices.

05

Bull factors

Entry into the nuclear supply chain

The company secured supplier qualification in a field with high entry barriers, where nuclear-grade quality assurance and demanding product evaluations must be passed. Industry observers regard winning a global nuclear company as a customer as a base for further orders and expansion.

Rising demand for spent fuel storage could leave room for new-business sales to grow. The pace of expansion still has to be confirmed by actual orders.

Technology base and customer network in steel parts

There was a report that it supplies Alloytic guide rollers exclusively to the three largest rolling mill equipment makers. Its integrated production system from powder manufacturing to finished products is also cited as a technological strength.

Having both major domestic steelmakers and global equipment makers as customers gives it a base for extending into new products.

Change in the balance sheet

The debt ratio was 239.5% in 2024. In the 2025 standalone statements reported by Digital Today, total liabilities were smaller than total equity. This largely reflects the gain from the asset sale flowing into equity.

The lighter financial burden gives the company more capacity to fund new-business investment and working capital, but whether the improvement carried through to core cash generation must be checked separately.

06

Bear factors

Three straight years of operating losses

Operating losses were KRW 2.6 billion in 2023 and KRW 3.3 billion in 2024, and an operating loss of KRW 2.3 billion continued in 2025. Korea Technology Finance Corporation noted that operating losses persisted in the first half of 2024 as cost burdens rose despite higher sales. For a third year, higher sales did not translate into profit.

Gap between new-business scale and timing

Compared with the framework agreement of about KRW 40 billion signed in early 2022, the mass-production supply contract is only about KRW 600 million. The start of mass production has differed across reports, suggesting repeated delays.

The semiconductor sputtering target business is still at the letter-of-intent stage, so its revenue contribution cannot be confirmed.

Share price decline and thin liquidity

The 12-month change was -47.38% as of 13 February 2026, and the price has since fallen to KRW 2,000. Trading value on 27 October 2026 was KRW 913,741, so even small trades can move the price sharply. Market capitalization of KRW 11.7 billion is small and limits investor access. The weak price discovery typical of the KONEX market should also be taken into account.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Sales are concentrated in steel equipment parts, so the company is highly exposed to the steel cycle. The report explains that weaker domestic and overseas steel demand caused the earnings decline in 2023.

It also points out that prices of key raw materials such as metal, ceramic and high-speed tool steel powders have been rising steadily. A weak cycle combined with higher costs could prolong operating losses.

Business

The new businesses depend heavily on orders from particular customers and on nuclear quality certification. The nuclear materials business centers on a contract with Orano alone, so customer concentration is high.

Participation in the domestic dry storage market is still at the planning stage, and timing could shift with policy and ordering schedules. Investment costs for the new businesses could also arrive before revenue growth does.

Financial and liquidity

The 2025 net profit was driven largely by a one-off asset sale, so a profit of the same size should not be assumed to recur. On 31 March 2026 it granted stock options on 100,000 common shares to one unregistered executive, to be settled with new shares, which could increase the share count.

The current redemption burden of the 500,000 redeemable preferred shares issued earlier at KRW 10,000 each must be checked separately in filings. Trading on KONEX is infrequent, which can make it hard to buy or sell positions.

08

Overall view

Konasol is a small metal composite materials company that is extending from steel rolling rolls, where it has a stable customer base, into semiconductor, nuclear and mobility materials. Its exchange classification is metal fabricated products, so it is not comparable with the large listed chemical companies.

Operating losses continued from 2023 to 2025, and the return to net profit in 2025 reflected the effect of an asset sale. The Orano neutron absorber business is cited as a bullish factor, but the confirmed mass-production supply contract is about KRW 600 million, far below the framework agreement.

Market capitalization of KRW 11.7 billion and trading value of KRW 913,741 on the day show the liquidity constraint. Key items to watch are results after the first half of 2026, actual shipment and revenue recognition for the neutron absorbers, and any orders in semiconductor materials.
