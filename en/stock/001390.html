02

Business structure

KG Chemical began in 1954 as Korea's first private fertilizer maker and now produces eco-friendly organic fertilizers, plant-based crop protectants and microbial agents. The company groups its operations into fertilizer, construction materials, environmental, port logistics and other (leasing) businesses.

Its main products include concrete admixture raw materials, water treatment agents, exhaust gas purifiers and Adblue vehicle urea solution.

According to its filings, the chemical segment is run jointly by KG Chemical and KG Enechem, with key customers including large domestic and foreign companies, NACF and local agricultural cooperatives. The chemical segment, however, accounts for a small share of consolidated sales.

As of the second quarter of 2023, automobile manufacturing and sales made up the largest revenue share at 44.4%, followed by steel and port operations at 36.2%, electronic payments at 10.0% and food service at 4.4%.

Chemicals were 2.8% of revenue in that period, and because this data is from 2023 the current mix may differ. On a consolidated basis, results are influenced by affiliates such as KG Steel, KG Mobility and KG Inicis.

On a standalone basis, the company credits eco-friendly fertilizers and agricultural materials for the improvement.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 6.61 trillion in 2022 to KRW 8.93 trillion in 2023, up 35.2% year on year, dipped to KRW 8.86 trillion in 2024 (down 0.8%), and reached KRW 9.1 trillion in 2025 (up 2.7%), the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 window.

Operating profit over the same period was KRW 501.1 billion in 2022, KRW 442.6 billion in 2023 (down 11.7%), KRW 318.8 billion in 2024 (down 28.0%) and KRW 305.7 billion in 2025 (down 4.1%), declining for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating margin therefore narrowed from 7.6% in 2022 to 5.0%, 3.6% and 3.4% in the following years, even as sales grew.

Controlling-shareholder net income fell from KRW 303.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 95.8 billion in 2023 (down 68.4%) and KRW 61.9 billion in 2024 (down 35.4%), before rebounding to KRW 69.7 billion in 2025 (up 12.6%).

Total net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 952.8 billion in 2022 and KRW 244 billion in 2025, a gap large enough that the two measures must be read separately.

At the end of 2025, total equity stood at KRW 3.94 trillion, of which controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 999 billion, so non-controlling interests account for a large part of the equity base.

Quarterly operating profit was volatile: KRW 90.2 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 30 billion in Q4 2025 (down 66.7%), KRW 64.6 billion in Q1 2026 (up 115.0%), KRW 90.9 billion in Q2 2026 (up 40.7%) and KRW 4.98 billion in Q3 2026 (down 94.5%).

The company attributed the third-quarter drop to temporary factors, including the effect of the won exchange rate on export profitability, foreign-currency translation losses and maintenance on a new vehicle production line at KG Mobility.

The third-quarter controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 25.6 billion marked a swing from a profit a year earlier.

05

Industry analysis

KG Chemical's core businesses, including fertilizers, agricultural materials, water treatment agents and Adblue urea solution, are tied to domestic agriculture and environmental regulation.

The company credited strong eco-friendly fertilizer and agricultural material sales for standalone improvement in January to September 2026. Adblue vehicle urea solution is a product designed to reduce nitrogen oxide emissions from vehicles.

Chemical industry conditions, however, are sensitive to raw material and oil prices. Among peers, LG Chem's CFO pointed to higher oil prices tied to Middle East geopolitical risk and greater raw material price volatility, and said profitability in its petrochemical segment had weakened.

GS said its petrochemical segment posted a loss because naphtha cost increases were not fully passed through to selling prices. These conditions are largely outside KG Chemical's own chemical segment, which is a small share of revenue, so the direct effect on consolidated results is limited.

Affiliate exposure in steel, port operations and mobility matters more for consolidated profit swings. In the 2023 data, steel and port operations made up 36.2% of revenue.

Market-share trends in domestic agricultural materials are not established by the sources used here, so the comparison stays at the level of business structure.

06

Outlook

The company has pointed to expanding high-quality, customized supply of eco-friendly fertilizers and agricultural materials while raising cost competitiveness through process efficiency. It also said it maintained orders in its chemical materials business.

On a consolidated basis, the company is emphasizing links with affiliated mobility operations, and a news headline on the results highlighted stronger mobility linkage in the fourth quarter.

There is no confirmed schedule for when maintenance on the new KG Mobility vehicle line will end or how many quarters its effect will last.

Because exchange-rate effects and foreign-currency translation losses were reflected in third-quarter consolidated results, how the currency trend carries into the fourth quarter is the next checkpoint.

Standalone operating profit rose to KRW 21.6 billion in January to September 2026, while consolidated operating profit fell 41.8% to KRW 160.5 billion over the same period, and narrowing that gap is central to the outlook.

The second-quarter 2026 controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 36.1 billion was the largest quarterly figure since Q3 2025, so whether that level holds in Q4 is the next check.

Full-year figures will be confirmed in the 2026 annual report, which will set the reference values for annual margin and controlling-shareholder net income.

08

Bull factors

Standalone core profitability improves

Standalone revenue for January to September 2026 rose 3.5% to KRW 180.1 billion, while operating profit rose 35.2% to KRW 21.6 billion. The company attributed the gain to higher-value products and process efficiency. Operating profit growing faster than revenue is the basis for the improvement case.

First-half 2026 quarterly profits recover

Operating profit recovered from KRW 30 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 64.6 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 90.9 billion in Q2 2026. Controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 36.1 billion in Q2 2026 was the largest quarterly figure since Q3 2025. The direction of quarterly profit is the basis for the bullish view.

Balance sheet and cash generation improve

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 111.9% at the end of 2025 from 116.2% in 2024. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 277.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 383.8 billion in 2025. Cash generation has continued despite volatile earnings, which supports the bullish case.

09

Bear factors

Consolidated loss in Q3 2026

Consolidated operating profit in Q3 2026 was KRW 4.98 billion, down 94.5% from the prior quarter, and the controlling-shareholder result swung to a KRW 25.6 billion loss from a profit a year earlier. Consolidated operating profit for January to September fell 41.8% to KRW 160.5 billion. The sensitivity of group-level profit to affiliates is the bearish point.

Three straight years of lower operating profit and thinner margins

Operating profit declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, making 2025, at KRW 305.7 billion, the smallest year in the 2022 to 2025 window. The operating margin fell from 7.6% in 2022 to 3.4% in 2025. Margins did not recover even in 2025, the year of largest revenue, which is the bearish basis.

Thin controlling-shareholder equity within a large group structure

At the end of 2025, controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 999 billion within total equity of KRW 3.94 trillion, against total liabilities of KRW 4.41 trillion. Because controlling-shareholder profit is exposed to affiliate swings, its volatility is high.

The large gap between total net income and controlling-shareholder net income is also a bearish point to monitor.

10

Risk factors

Affiliate earnings volatility

Consolidated revenue leans heavily on affiliates such as KG Steel, KG Mobility and KG Inicis. Temporary factors, including maintenance on a new KG Mobility production line, weighed heavily on third-quarter consolidated operating profit.

Until the end of that maintenance and the recovery pace of each affiliate are confirmed, consolidated earnings volatility remains.

Exchange rates and foreign-currency translation

The company cited export profitability deterioration from exchange-rate moves as a factor in third-quarter results, along with foreign-currency translation losses. If the currency trend shifts again, quarterly profit in export-linked businesses could be affected.

Raw material and oil price conditions

Among chemical peers, LG Chem's CFO cited higher oil prices tied to Middle East geopolitical risk and greater raw material price volatility as sources of uncertainty. GS said its petrochemical segment posted losses because naphtha cost increases were not fully passed through to prices. A difficult pass-through environment could also weigh on KG Chemical's input costs.

Overall view

From 2022 to 2025, KG Chemical's revenue grew from KRW 6.61 trillion to KRW 9.1 trillion, while operating profit fell from KRW 501.1 billion to KRW 305.7 billion, lowering the operating margin.

Quarterly operating profit recovered to KRW 64.6 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 90.9 billion in Q2 2026, then fell to KRW 4.98 billion in Q3 2026, with a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 25.6 billion.

In the same period, the company reported standalone January-September operating profit of KRW 21.6 billion, up 35.2%, and emphasized profitability gains in its own business. Consolidated results swing sharply on exchange rates and affiliate maintenance, so both bullish and bearish factors coexist.

Controlling-shareholder equity is only part of total equity, and the debt-to-equity ratio stands at 111.9%, which are also financial conditions to weigh. A price-to-book ratio well below 1x is a factual comparison against book value and does not indicate the direction of the share price.

The next checkpoints are fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report, where the consolidated operating margin and affiliate contributions will be the key items.
