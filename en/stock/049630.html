OIS Supply Expansion Lifts Operating Profit, Net Income Slows

02

Business structure

Jaeyoung Solutec began as a precision mold maker and has restructured into a specialist in smartphone camera actuators, a KOSDAQ-listed company. It expanded from voice coil motors (VCM) for autofocus and encoder-type drives into optical image stabilization (OIS) products that correct hand-shake.

Its core product is the hybrid OIS actuator co-developed with Samsung Electronics, and growth in higher-value products is central to its profit recovery.

Production is centered on Jaeyoung VINA, the Vietnam manufacturing subsidiary founded in 2016, into which the company has invested roughly KRW 100 billion over about five years for line expansion and automation.

The company said ball-type OIS and encoder sales both rose year on year in the third quarter of 2026, and hybrid OIS continued to grow on new model supply. Customer-side, it is pursuing higher share within key hybrid OIS customers.

Competitively, it operates in a camera module component market alongside larger listed companies in the same electronics and components sector, and it says its patent portfolio raises technological entry barriers as cameras move to higher pixel counts and slimmer designs.

Because the product mix is concentrated in smartphone cameras, customer model adoption largely determines revenue scale.

The company is also preparing to enter adjacent fields such as robotics, automation systems and mobility, but management describes these new businesses as still at the stage of reviewing technical applicability and market potential, and it is focusing on strengthening its mobile actuator business for the near term.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed basis, revenue fell from KRW 119.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 107.2 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 111.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 164.5 billion in 2025. Revenue has risen for two consecutive years from the 2023 low through 2024 and 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

The company attributed the 2025 revenue increase mainly to higher sales of high-value hybrid OIS actuators. Operating profit rose from KRW 4.9 billion in 2023 to KRW 10 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.7 billion in 2025, while the operating margin fell from 9.0% in 2024 to 6.5% in 2025.

That gap coincides with yield weakness on a new hybrid OIS line in the first half of 2025, when operating profit was only about KRW 270 million according to press reports. Yields stabilized in the second half, which brought full-year operating profit to KRW 10.7 billion.

Quarterly operating profit climbed from KRW 3.86 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 6.62 billion in the fourth quarter and KRW 9.13 billion in the first quarter of 2026, which the company described as a record quarter.

Operating profit then fell to KRW 7.97 billion in the second quarter of 2026 before recovering to KRW 9.2 billion in the third quarter.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders, however, peaked at KRW 9.29 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then declined to KRW 6.53 billion and KRW 1.41 billion in the second and third quarters, so the profit trend weakened even as operating profit recovered.

Net income in 2025 was also supported by lower financing costs after convertible bonds converted into common shares and by lower borrowings after the sale of a plant in Namdong. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 221.7% in 2022 to 111.5% in 2025, and total equity stood at KRW 102.2 billion at the end of 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 13.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.36 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Smartphone cameras are moving toward higher pixel counts and slimmer designs at the same time, and the company says it is strengthening its patent portfolio in line with that trend. OIS adoption is also spreading from high-end models into mid- and low-priced models.

The growing number of models that adopt high-spec actuators is read as a favorable direction for Jaeyoung Solutec.

However, OIS volumes shift quarter to quarter with smartphone makers' new model launches and demand changes, and the decline in operating profit from the first to the second quarter of 2026 is one example of that volatility.

In February and May 2026, the company secured OIS-related patents that improve positioning precision and structural stability in camera modules, which can be seen as an effort to maintain a technology gap with later entrants.

The same electronics and components sector includes large listed companies that handle camera modules and parts, so Jaeyoung Solutec occupies a position specialized in a narrow product line of OIS actuators. That specialization raises technological focus but also increases dependence on a small number of customers.

Robotics, automation and mobility are presented as applications where its precision control technology can extend, but these have not yet contributed to revenue. The position of the industry cycle depends on the timing of smartphone model launches and on customer adoption decisions.

Outlook

The company has said it will strengthen the supply competitiveness of its main products, including hybrid OIS, and raise cost competitiveness through production efficiency. It also plans to expand supply for new smartphone models.

When it announced 2025 annual results, the company said that with hybrid OIS mass-production stabilized, it expected revenue growth and margin improvement to continue in the year. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose while net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell.

Whether that gap is temporary or structural can be checked in the non-operating income and financing cost items of the annual business report. Press reports said automation and production-efficiency gains lifted the operating margin to about 16% in the first half of 2026.

Whether that level holds, however, depends on new smartphone model volumes and line utilization, a view shared by the company and the press. Robotics and mobility are presented as longer-term directions, but no specific revenue targets or timelines have been disclosed.

The company also stated a shareholder-value policy alongside its reverse stock split. The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual preliminary results and the 2026 annual business report, where the fourth-quarter operating margin and the recovery of net income can be reviewed together.

Bull factors

Capacity Investment and Lower Fixed-Cost Burden

The company said it invested roughly KRW 100 billion over about five years, mainly in its Vietnam production subsidiary. It explained that new lines moved into stable mass production and process efficiency improved, raising productivity.

It also said that higher volumes eased the burden of fixed costs, which contributed to operating profit growth. These effects appear alongside the quarter-level increases in operating profit during 2026.

Expanding Supply of High-Value Hybrid OIS

Higher sales of high-value hybrid OIS actuators were the main driver of revenue growth in 2025. Hybrid OIS is spreading into mid- and low-priced models, and the company is raising its share within key customers. Press reports said OIS sales rose 202% year on year in 2025. A higher share of high-value products can work favorably for the cost structure and operating margin.

Balance Sheet Repair and Cash Generation

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 221.7% in 2022 to 111.5% in 2025. Press reports said the consolidated ratio dropped further to 87.9% in the first half of 2026 and total borrowings fell from about KRW 83.5 billion at the end of 2024 to about KRW 51 billion.

The same reports said operating cash flow in the first half of 2026 rose sharply from the prior-year period. Lower borrowings reduce interest burden, which is favorable for the net income structure.

Bear factors

Consecutive Declines in Net Income

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 9.29 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 6.53 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.41 billion in the third quarter, a decline across two consecutive quarters.

Third-quarter operating profit rose to KRW 9.2 billion, yet net income fell 68.6% from the same quarter a year earlier. The reason for the gap between rising operating profit and falling net income still needs to be confirmed from the detailed filing. Questions about earnings quality remain open.

Concentration in Customers and Products

The company is concentrated in smartphone camera actuators, and its hybrid OIS was co-developed with Samsung Electronics. Because revenue depends heavily on customer model adoption, changes in customer models or volume adjustments can quickly show up in results.

The company itself has said it responds to demand shifts in the smartphone market. Product diversification remains at an early stage.

Uncertainty in New Businesses

Entry into robotics, automation systems and mobility is presented as a direction the company is preparing for. The company, however, describes these new businesses as still at the stage of reviewing technical applicability and market potential, and says it will focus on the mobile actuator business in the near term.

No specific revenue targets or timing have been disclosed. Results from new businesses could take time to appear in financial statements.

Risk factors

Customers and Smartphone Demand

Volumes for OIS actuators shift with new smartphone launches and demand changes by customers. The decline in operating profit from the first to the second quarter of 2026 is one example of this volatility. A change in customer model adoption could significantly move quarterly results. Although the company plans to expand supply for new models, the scale depends on customer decisions.

Yield and Equipment Operations

In the first half of 2025, temporary yield weakness occurred during the expansion of a new hybrid OIS line, and operating profit fell sharply during that period. Yields stabilized in the second half and results recovered, but the same risk could recur whenever new lines are added.

With investment of roughly KRW 100 billion, a drop in utilization could raise the burden of fixed costs again. Concentration of production in a single base in Vietnam can also be seen as an operational risk.

Financial Leverage and Earnings Quality

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 221.7% in 2022 to 111.5% in 2025, but liabilities still exceed equity. Financial factors such as lower financing costs and reduced borrowings also contributed to the net income improvement in 2025.

In 2026, operating profit increased while net income attributable to controlling shareholders decreased, which calls for a review of the earnings structure. Whether this gap is temporary or lasting can be determined in the annual business report.

Overall view

Jaeyoung Solutec has shifted from precision mold making to a specialist in smartphone camera actuators, with growth in high-value hybrid OIS products at the center of its results.

On a filed basis, revenue rose from the 2023 low to KRW 164.5 billion in 2025, and operating profit grew to KRW 10.7 billion in 2025 and KRW 9.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026. The balance sheet also improved, with the debt-to-equity ratio falling from 221.7% to 111.5%.

On the other side, the operating margin fell from 9.0% in 2024 to 6.5% in 2025, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders has declined for two consecutive quarters after its first-quarter 2026 peak.

The business relies heavily on smartphone cameras and on customer model adoption, so quarterly results can swing widely. New businesses remain at the review stage without a confirmed contribution to earnings.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter operating margin in the 2026 annual preliminary results and annual business report, along with the non-operating items that explain the gap between operating profit and net income.
