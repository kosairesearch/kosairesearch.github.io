02

Business structure

Isupetasys is a specialist maker of multi-layer boards (MLB), printed circuit boards that stack many layers to handle high-speed data. PCBs account for 100% of revenue, products are made to customer order, and exports exceed 95% of sales.

The Korean headquarters runs four plants and a research center, while a Chinese overseas plant and sales offices in the United States, Taiwan and Shanghai complete the footprint.

The company and media describe it as the domestic and global number one in MLB, but a 2025 report ranked it third globally in the 18-layer-and-above segment, so the ranking depends on the evaluator and criteria.

High-layer and multi-layer products are made at the Korean headquarters, while conventional-process mid-layer products are made in China. A 2025 report said the Chinese unit is increasing production of AI server mainboards (UBB).

Key customers include global big-tech firms such as Google and NVIDIA, and AI accelerators and switches and other high-speed network equipment are the core demand sources. In Daegu, a KRW 50.3 billion drilling-process plant was agreed in November 2025, with first mass production planned from January 2026. A late-2025 report projected roughly KRW 1.5 trillion in revenue by 2030 once the new plant is complete.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results bottomed in 2023 and improved sharply in 2025. Revenue rose from KRW 642.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 675.3 billion in 2023 (up 5.0%), KRW 836.9 billion in 2024 (up 23.9%) and KRW 1.09 trillion in 2025 (up 30.0%).

Revenue has grown for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

Operating profit fell from KRW 116.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 62.2 billion in 2023 (down 46.7%), then rose to KRW 101.9 billion in 2024 (up 63.9%) and KRW 204.7 billion in 2025 (up 100.9%), growing for two consecutive years.

The operating margin was 18.1% in 2022, 9.2% in 2023, 12.2% in 2024 and 18.8% in 2025, returning in 2025 to the 2022 level.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 47.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 160.5 billion in 2025, and net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 160.5 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue climbed from KRW 296.1 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 389.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and operating profit from KRW 58.4 billion to KRW 94.9 billion.

Operating profit in the fourth quarter of 2025 was KRW 56.5 billion, down 3.2% from the prior quarter, and securities analysts attributed part of the dip to a change in revenue recognition timing, with an impact of about KRW 20 billion, and a one-time cost for fourth-quarter bonuses.

Operating profit has grown for three consecutive quarters since the first quarter of 2026. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 218.8 billion, above the 2025 full-year figure of KRW 160.5 billion.

Gains came from higher sales of high-margin AI accelerator and switch products and a rising share of multi-layer products. The flip side is that multi-layer processing involves two to three times as many process steps as conventional MLB, which keeps yield risk in view.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from the front end continues to center on AI accelerators and high-speed network equipment such as switches. In a 2025 report, securities analysts described the equipment substrate market as being in a supply-shortage phase due to surging AI demand.

The same report noted that competitors' capacity additions are concentrated mainly in Southeast Asia, so high-end MLB production will take time to catch up.

The company acknowledged that its capital spending is smaller than that of Taiwanese and Chinese rivals, but said it is focusing investment on high-layer network and HDI markets. Technical barriers are high, and analysts cite the difficulty and high yields of advanced MLB technology as an entry barrier.

On product mix, a February 2026 securities analysis put the ASP of 800G multi-layer MLB at two to three times that of 400G products.

On the cycle, DB Securities said in a May 2026 report that demand growth still exceeds the increase in CAPA even after accounting for new capacity, while noting that a decline in mid-to-long-term share among key customers is unavoidable but not caused by technological inferiority.

According to Meritz Securities, multi-layer products now account for more than 20% of the order backlog. Competitive positioning is strong in technology and customer base, while equipment scale and the lack of HDI mass-production history are cited as weaknesses versus rivals.

06

Outlook

In May 2026 the company shared a plan to raise multi-layer capacity from 3,000 square meters in the first half of 2026 to 8,000 in the second half of 2026, 13,000 in the first half of 2027 and 15,000 in the first half of 2028.

On August 18, 2026, the company said it is reviewing follow-on equipment investment for its sixth plant, which is reported at a mid-KRW 100 billion scale.

Meritz Securities expects monthly capacity at the headquarters to reach about KRW 120 billion once the second-stage expansion finishes in the third quarter, and about KRW 150 billion by the second quarter of 2027 after the sixth plant's additional investment is complete.

Kiwoom Securities said the company secured two new hyperscale customers and raised its monthly revenue guidance from KRW 140 billion to KRW 155 billion starting in the first quarter of 2027. Backlog grew to KRW 622.2 billion at the end of the second quarter of 2026, a sign that demand is running ahead of supply.

The company is also reviewing a seventh plant dedicated to HDI production, which Hana Securities expects to take a concrete form within 2026. Hana Securities has said the lack of HDI mass-production history has worked as a valuation discount versus peers, and that a dedicated HDI facility could be a re-rating factor.

The next points to confirm are whether the sixth-plant investment is finalized, whether monthly revenue guidance for the first quarter of 2027 is met, and whether the seventh-plant HDI plan takes shape.

08

Bull factors

Profitability gains from the multi-layer shift

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 94.9 billion rose 62.4% year on year. Higher sales of AI accelerator and switch products and a larger share of multi-layer products improved profitability. Analysts also point to higher average selling prices for 800G multi-layer products than for 400G products.

Resilient backlog and customer demand

Backlog grew sharply from a year earlier, so the pace at which it converts into revenue in coming quarters is the next point to check. Multi-layer products account for more than 20% of the backlog, according to Meritz Securities. Hana Securities noted that lead times are lengthening as multi-layer demand grows.

Expansion roadmap and new customer wins

Kiwoom Securities said the company won two new hyperscale customers and raised monthly revenue guidance to KRW 155 billion from the first quarter of 2027. Meritz Securities expects headquarters monthly capacity to expand to about KRW 120 billion from the third quarter.

Follow-on investment at the sixth plant and a review of a seventh HDI plant are cited as medium-term capacity drivers.

09

Bear factors

Possible share loss within key customers

DB Securities said in a May 2026 report that a decline in mid-to-long-term share among key customers is unavoidable, while stating that it does not reflect technological inferiority. Concentration of demand among a few big-tech customers is another source of variability.

Yield burden in multi-layer processing

A securities analysis put the process load of multi-layer products at about 2.5 times that of the VIPPO method. Media reports say all processing steps in multi-layer production increase two to three times, which keeps yield risk in view.

In the fourth quarter of 2024, early-stage yield issues on new products and one-time costs pushed operating profit slightly below market expectations.

Smaller capex scale and no HDI track record

The company acknowledged that its capital spending is smaller than that of Taiwanese and Chinese rivals. Hana Securities said the lack of HDI mass-production history has worked as a valuation discount versus peers. If capacity growth fails to keep pace with demand, defending share could become harder.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration

The company counts global big-tech firms such as Google among its key customers, so changes in investment plans or designs at specific customers can directly affect revenue. Share shifts within those customers are another source of earnings volatility.

Winning new hyperscale customers would reduce this concentration, but their revenue contribution will take time to appear.

Process yield and cost

Yield risk also exists. As process steps increase for multi-layer products, lower yields can raise costs and offset the benefit of higher selling prices. Price increases have been mentioned since the second quarter of 2026, but yield stabilization is key to sustaining margins.

Execution of expansion and funding needs

Follow-on investment at the sixth plant is under review at the mid-KRW 100 billion scale, and the Daegu drilling plant planned first mass production from January 2026. If expansion timelines slip, guidance and order fulfillment could be affected.

The debt ratio fell to 70.5% at the end of 2025, but sustained large investment could raise financial burden again.

Overall view

Isupetasys has recovered sharply from its 2023 low, with operating profit reaching KRW 204.7 billion in 2025, and revenue has risen for four straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 94.9 billion and backlog of KRW 622.2 billion at the end of the second quarter point to demand running ahead of supply in high-layer MLB. The expansion roadmap targets multi-layer capacity of 15,000 square meters by the first half of 2028.

Still unresolved are a possible decline in mid-to-long-term share within key customers, yield risk in multi-layer processing, and the lack of HDI mass-production history.

The valuation sits at a high level relative to recent earnings, and the target prices cited are August 2026 securities forecasts that may change with later results and plans.

The next points to confirm are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results, the 2026 annual report, final decisions on the sixth and seventh plants, and delivery against first-quarter 2027 guidance.
