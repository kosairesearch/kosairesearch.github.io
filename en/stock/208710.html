02

Business structure

Photon, founded in 2012, makes smartphone camera module components, supplying AF/OIS and F/Z FPCB assemblies used in camera actuators to first-tier vendors at large Korean companies.

Its core lines are autofocus and optical image stabilization (OIS) assemblies and Folded Zoom (F/Z) assemblies, and the company says in-house component production supports both quality and price competitiveness.

By segment, the sales share of Folded Zoom assemblies, which carry a high raw-material cost share, has declined, while OIS assemblies grew from 46% of sales in 2025 to 61% in the first half of 2026.

The company also acquired a Vietnam subsidiary in 2025, and it attributes part of its margin improvement to production efficiency there. On the process side, it has confirmed the installation of 252 winding automation units by September 2026.

Because it operates as a first-tier vendor, volumes and unit prices depend on changes in camera specifications at the final set makers. Competitively, it is a small component supplier compared with large camera module component makers such as LG Innotek and Samsung Electro-Mechanics. The company says it is also pursuing gradual results in automotive and other new businesses.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 106.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 123.5 billion in 2023 (up 15.9% year on year) and KRW 135.7 billion in 2024 (up 9.9%), then fell to KRW 128.7 billion in 2025 (down 5.1%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year for revenue and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit of KRW 426 million in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 3.26 billion in 2023, a swing to loss, and the loss widened to KRW 3.42 billion in 2024.

In 2025 the operating loss narrowed to KRW 45.9 million, and the operating margin moved from 0.4% in 2022 to -2.6% in 2023, -2.5% in 2024 and -0.0% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 257 million in 2022 to a loss of KRW 11.8 billion in 2023, then narrowed to losses of KRW 9.75 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.36 billion in 2025.

Including non-controlling interests, the net loss was KRW 13.7 billion in 2023, KRW 9.61 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.36 billion in 2025.

For 2025, gross profit rose from KRW 4.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 8.2 billion according to FnGuide's compilation, but one-off costs were cited as the reason operating profit stayed negative.

Quarterly, operating profit went from KRW 117 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.46 billion in the fourth quarter, a jump of 2,003.6% quarter on quarter.

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 26.7 billion (down 34.2% quarter on quarter) with an operating loss of KRW 268 million, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 474 million.

In the second quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 37.9 billion (up 41.8% quarter on quarter) and operating profit was KRW 739 million, a swing to profit.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 29.6 billion (down 22.0% quarter on quarter and down 13.7% year on year) and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 897 million. Over the last four quarters, net losses attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 4.08 billion.

The company's first-half 2026 announcement of KRW 64.6 billion in revenue and KRW 470 million in operating profit matches the sum of the quarterly figures. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 67.8% in 2022 to 94.9% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 47.6 billion and total liabilities of KRW 45.1 billion at year-end 2025.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 4.85 billion in 2022 to KRW 858 million in 2024 and to negative KRW 75.93 million in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Smartphone camera zoom and stabilization features are becoming more advanced, which raises the technical requirements for internal actuator components. FnGuide's 2025 earnings commentary cited rising demand and technical innovation in the AF/OIS and F/Z segments as reasons gross profit improved.

Photon makes FPCB assemblies inside camera modules rather than finished sets, so its revenue moves with design wins and production volumes at large set and module makers. Revenue fell 5.1% in 2025, and third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 29.6 billion was down 13.7% year on year.

In the first half of 2026, the mix shifted as Folded Zoom assemblies declined and OIS assemblies rose to 61% of sales, which the company cites as a driver of profitability.

Competitively, large camera module component makers such as LG Innotek and Samsung Electro-Mechanics operate in the same industry, and Photon's scale and customer range are far narrower. Smartphone shipment cycles also directly affect its sales. The company says it is preparing automotive electronics as a new growth area.

Because the third quarter of 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 897 million, whether operating profit holds in later quarters is the next checkpoint. The company's announcement says the OIS share of sales expanded to 61% in the first half of 2026. The decision in March 2026 to issue KRW 4.0 billion in convertible bonds could change the funding structure. Bull factors

Return to profitability

The company's first-half 2026 announcement reported operating profit of KRW 470 million. Press coverage said the first-quarter operating loss shrank by about 82% year on year.

Improving product mix

Folded Zoom assemblies, which carry high raw-material costs, declined as a share of sales, while OIS assemblies rose from 46% in 2025 to 61% in the first half of 2026. The company attributes this to a higher share of higher-margin shipments. If the mix shift continues, the operating margin has room to improve.

Production efficiency and automation

The company confirmed the installation of 252 winding automation units by September 2026. Production efficiency and process stabilization centered on the Vietnam subsidiary are cited as contributors to profitability. The company says cost and production efficiency efforts are gradually feeding into better results.

Revenue decline and customer dependence

Because the company supplies through first-tier vendors to large Korean companies, it is sensitive to design changes and volume decisions made upstream. Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

Revenue centers on AF/OIS and F/Z assemblies and depends on camera specification changes at final set makers. Because Folded Zoom assemblies still carry a high share, a drop in demand for that product could cause larger revenue swings. Whether automotive and EdgeQ-centered businesses show visible results is the next point to watch.

Financing and capital

At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 47.6 billion and total liabilities were KRW 45.1 billion, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 94.9%. The March 2026 decision to issue KRW 4.0 billion in convertible bonds could affect the capital structure and share count. Because operating cash flow turned negative in 2025, cash generation needs further confirmation.

Share price volatility and theme-driven trading

The Korea Exchange designated Photon as a short-selling overheated stock on April 23, 2026. In May 2026 the stock hit its daily limit as a quantum-related name, but press coverage pointed out that the link between its camera actuator business and quantum technology is weak. Overall view

Operating profit was positive for the first half of 2026, but the third quarter returned to weakness, with revenue of KRW 29.6 billion and an operating loss of KRW 897 million.

The business centers on AF/OIS and F/Z FPCB assemblies, and the mix has moved toward OIS, which rose to 61% of sales in the first half of 2026 from 46% in 2025.
