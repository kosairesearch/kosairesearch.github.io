02

Business structure

Bixolonco was spun off from Samsung Electro-Mechanics in 2002 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2007. The company is focused on printers, which account for 100% of its business segments. A securities report from April 2023 put the sales mix at POS printers 42%, label printers 22.6% and mobile printers 21.5%.

A breakdown compiled by JobKorea shows POS at 34 to 35%, label at 26 to 30% and mobile at 17 to 19% depending on the period, so POS remains the largest segment while label's share has grown.

POS printers are used by retail and service businesses to issue receipts and kitchen orders, and label printers include linerless models supplied to a global convenience store operator since 2022.

Mobile printers serve delivery and contactless payment sites, and the company is widening its range into kiosk printer mechanisms and RFID printers. Exports account for more than 70% of sales, sold under the BIXOLON brand through about 200 distributors in more than 80 countries.

Overseas subsidiaries are in the United States, Germany and Mexico, and the Mexico unit was established in February 2024. In global competition, a Japanese market leader is followed by second-tier players, and the top four makers hold more than 70% of the world market, with Bixolonco positioned in the second tier.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales fell from KRW 134.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 108.1 billion in 2023, a 19.8% decline, and then rose for two consecutive years to KRW 113.5 billion in 2024 (up 5.0%) and KRW 133.5 billion in 2025 (up 17.6%). The largest year in 2022 to 2025 was 2022, and the smallest was 2023.

Operating profit fell from KRW 25.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.27 billion in 2023, a 63.1% decline, then edged up to KRW 9.42 billion in 2024 (up 1.6%) and recovered to KRW 15.9 billion in 2025 (up 68.8%). The operating margin dropped from 18.6% in 2022 to 8.6% in 2023 and 8.3% in 2024, then climbed to 11.9% in 2025.

Sales in 2025 came back close to the 2022 level, but the margin remains below 2022, so profitability has recovered by less than sales. Attributable net income rose from KRW 10.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 11 billion in 2023, KRW 13.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 18.3 billion in 2025, increasing for three consecutive years.

In 2023 operating profit fell while attributable net income rose slightly, which suggests non-operating items shaped the earnings trend that year. Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 18.3 billion in 2025, the same figure as attributable net income.

Operating cash flow was KRW 16.2 billion in 2022, KRW 10.8 billion in 2023 and KRW 18.6 billion in 2024, before turning to -KRW 4.06 billion in 2025. At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 45.9 billion against total equity of KRW 225.2 billion, and the debt ratio rose from 11.3% in 2024 to 20.4%.

On a quarterly basis, sales of KRW 46.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 10 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 were the highest of the last five quarters. Sales then came in at KRW 37.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 42.7 billion in the second and KRW 39.2 billion in the third.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.93 billion fell 52.1% from the prior quarter but rose 103.3% from a year earlier.

Attributable net income of KRW 2.77 billion in the same quarter fell 36.8% year on year, and the last four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026 total KRW 26.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for POS printers is growing mainly in emerging markets as tax-reporting systems become established, according to a securities research summary. Label printers are used across many industries, and securities analysts have pointed to continued growth in demand for linerless printers.

Mobile printer demand has risen with the spread of smart devices, contactless payments and expanding parcel logistics.

The company once earned operating profit above KRW 10 billion a year from 2010, but operating profit fell to the KRW 4 billion range in 2020 as COVID-19 curbed outside activity at restaurants and department stores.

Recovery began in 2021 as outside activity resumed and mobile printer demand surged, and operating profit reached KRW 25.1 billion in 2022 on a disclosed basis.

Because restaurant and retail equipment investment cycles and the spread of contactless payments push in different directions, the product segments follow different cycles. An April 2023 securities report noted that utilization had already reached 120%, which constrained sales growth until new capacity came online.

In competition, Bixolonco sits in the second tier behind a Japanese market leader, and the top four makers hold more than 70% of the global market in an oligopoly. In such a structure, technology and customer competition among the leading makers largely determines results.

With a low domestic sales share and overseas sales above 70%, the equipment investment cycles of overseas customers feed directly into the company's results.

Because this plan rests on a 2024 report, the start of operations and actual capacity should be checked against later disclosures and periodic reports.

Quarterly sales in 2026 were KRW 37.5 billion in the first quarter, KRW 42.7 billion in the second and KRW 39.2 billion in the third, moving within a range of roughly KRW 40 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 4.93 billion, so the pace of profitability recovery in the fourth quarter and beyond is the next focus.

The fourth quarter of 2025 posted KRW 10 billion in operating profit, the largest of the last five quarters, so whether later quarters return to that level is a checkpoint.

The 2025 results disclosed on February 4, 2026 showed a 69% rise in operating profit alongside a dividend increase, which sets a precedent for annual preliminary results appearing around early February.

The 2026 annual business report must be filed by the end of March 2027, when the fourth-quarter and full-year figures will be finalized. Whether sales of kiosk and RFID products grow as a share of the business mix can also be checked in the business description of the periodic report.

Bull factors

Earnings recovery trend

Attributable net income reached KRW 18.3 billion in 2025 after three straight years of increases, and the operating margin climbed from 8.6% in 2023 to 11.9% in 2025. The fourth quarter of 2025 produced the largest sales and operating profit of the last five quarters, at KRW 46.3 billion and KRW 10 billion. The 18.6% margin of 2022 serves as the reference point for how much room the margin has to recover.

Second-tier position in an oligopoly

The global printer market is an oligopoly in which the top four makers hold more than 70% share, and Bixolonco is placed in the second tier behind a Japanese leader. Overseas sales above 70% of the total, supported by subsidiaries in the United States, Germany and Mexico, underpin that position. Expansion into higher-value kiosk and RFID product lines is another factor on the strength side.

Planned capacity expansion

According to an August 2024 report, a new Chungju plant costing KRW 19.028 billion is expected to lift annual capacity from 500,000 units to about 1 million units by 2027. An April 2023 securities report pointed to utilization of 120%, so completed expansion would add room for volume. Progress on the plan still needs to be confirmed through later disclosures.

Bear factors

Uneven quarterly sales

Sales fell from KRW 134.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 108.1 billion in 2023 and recovered to KRW 133.5 billion in 2025, yet quarterly sales in 2026 moved up and down between KRW 37.5 billion, KRW 42.7 billion and KRW 39.2 billion. Third-quarter 2026 sales fell 8.2% from the prior quarter. The lack of a steady direction in quarterly sales is a weakness.

Mixed profit indicators

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose 103.3% year on year, but attributable net income fell 36.8% to KRW 2.77 billion. The 2025 operating margin of 11.9% remains below the 18.6% of 2022.

The fourth quarter of 2025, at KRW 9.49 billion, makes up a large part of the KRW 26.8 billion total of the last four quarters, so earnings are concentrated in a few quarters.

Cash flow and debt ratio

Operating cash flow turned to -KRW 4.06 billion in 2025 from KRW 18.6 billion in 2024. The debt ratio rose from 11.3% to 20.4% over the same period.

Total equity of KRW 225.2 billion leaves financial room, but whether the ability to generate cash from operations has recovered needs to be confirmed in the next periodic report.

10

Risk factors

Overseas demand and currency

Exports account for more than 70% of sales, so overseas customer demand and currency movements feed directly into results. With distributors across more than 80 countries, a slowdown in one region may be diluted, but a shared factor could move several regions at once. Currency sensitivity cannot be quantified from the disclosed figures alone.

Capacity expansion execution

The new Chungju plant requires an investment of KRW 19.028 billion, with the goal of lifting annual capacity to about 1 million units by 2027. A delay could extend utilization constraints, while completion during a demand slowdown could raise fixed-cost burdens. Either case could amplify swings in quarterly profit.

Free float and shareholder concentration

The free float is about 25% of issued shares, and the largest shareholder group holds 50.79%. A thin free float can mean low trading volume and larger price swings. The open-market purchases by a related party are confirmed by disclosure, but their meaning should be read from later stake filings.

Overall view

Bixolonco has recovered earnings after a period of swings, reaching KRW 133.5 billion in sales, KRW 15.9 billion in operating profit and KRW 18.3 billion in attributable net income in 2025. The operating margin rose to 11.9% in 2025 but remains below 18.6% in 2022.

The debt ratio climbed to 20.4% at the end of 2025, and operating cash flow turned to -KRW 4.06 billion.

Quarterly sales in 2026 moved between KRW 37.5 billion, KRW 42.7 billion and KRW 39.2 billion, and third-quarter operating profit of KRW 4.93 billion rose year on year while attributable net income of KRW 2.77 billion fell.

Overseas sales above 70% and a second-tier position in an oligopoly where the top four makers hold more than 70% are strengths. Quarterly profit volatility, weaker cash flow and whether capacity expansion is executed are weaknesses that should be weighed alongside them.

The open-market purchases by a related party and the Chungju expansion plan are items to follow through upcoming disclosures and periodic reports.
