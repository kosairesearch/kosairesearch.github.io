Hite Jinro: Sales Shrink, Profit Holds Up

Hite Jinro's operating profit rose in the third quarter of 2026 even as sales fell, but weak beer sales and thin net profit remain open issues.

  2. 2

02

Business structure

Hite Jinro is a liquor company centered on soju and beer, and these two segments account for more than 90% of total sales. Soju sales in 2025 were KRW 1.52 trillion, down 1.7% year on year, and beer sales were KRW 758.1 billion, down 7.8%.

Its flagship soju brand is Chamisul, and in beer it competes with Terra against the market leader Cass. Third-quarter 2026 soju sales were KRW 367.5 billion and beer sales were KRW 204.4 billion, both lower than a year earlier.

Cumulative January to September 2026 sales in the water segment rose 2.6% to KRW 121.5 billion, but segment operating profit fell to KRW 2.1 billion from about KRW 7.2 billion a year earlier. Overseas sales were KRW 267.5 billion in 2025, roughly flat against KRW 267.1 billion in 2024.

The company targets KRW 500 billion in overseas soju sales by 2030, and a April 2026 report said its Vietnam production plant was due for completion at the end of 2026. The plant is the company's first overseas production base, with annual soju output of about one million cases planned. Domestic liquor demand therefore remains the core of the earnings base.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 2.5 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.52 trillion in 2023 (up 0.9%) and KRW 2.6 trillion in 2024 (up 3.1%), then fell to KRW 2.5 trillion in 2025 (down 3.9%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 190.6 billion in 2022, KRW 123.9 billion in 2023 (down 35.0%), KRW 208.1 billion in 2024 (up 68.0%) and KRW 172.3 billion in 2025 (down 17.2%). The operating margin was lowest in 2023 at 4.9%, recovered to 8.0% in 2024, and eased to 6.9% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to owners was KRW 87 billion in 2022, KRW 35.6 billion in 2023 (down 59.0%), KRW 95.9 billion in 2024 (up 168.9%) and KRW 41.5 billion in 2025 (down 56.7%). Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 41.1 billion in 2025, a different figure from profit attributable to owners.

Operating cash flow moved from negative KRW 74 billion in 2022 to KRW 164.4 billion in 2023, KRW 294.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 232.6 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, sales were KRW 669.5 billion and operating profit KRW 54.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025, and the fourth quarter of 2025 posted an operating loss of KRW 9.24 billion with sales of KRW 569.7 billion (down 14.9% quarter on quarter). Net loss attributable to owners in that quarter was KRW 63.6 billion.

The first quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 55.9 billion on sales of KRW 590.8 billion, and the second quarter reached operating profit of KRW 64.9 billion (up 16.1% quarter on quarter) on sales of KRW 618.6 billion.

The third quarter of 2026 recorded sales of KRW 632.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 57.7 billion. Sales have risen for three consecutive quarters since the fourth quarter of 2025.

Net profit attributable to owners was KRW 28.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 29.0% quarter on quarter, and the four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026 total KRW 41.9 billion.

The company attributed the operating profit gain to cost efficiency, with the cost of sales ratio falling from 56% to 55.5%. Lower net profit was reported to reflect reduced financial income and higher other non-operating expenses.

Cumulative sales for January to September 2026 were KRW 1.84 trillion, down 4.5%, and operating profit was KRW 178.6 billion, down 1.6%.

05

Industry analysis

Demand in Korea's liquor market continues to weaken, and sales are contracting. Diluted soju shipments fell 10.9% in 2025, from 915,596 kiloliters to 815,712 kiloliters, while beer shipments fell 4.6%, from 1,715,995 kiloliters to 1,637,210 kiloliters.

In its third-quarter 2026 results the company also cited the contraction in the liquor market. One analysis says Chamisul keeps an overwhelming share of the soju market. In beer, the market leader Cass and Hite Jinro compete head to head as the main rivals.

Second-quarter 2026 beer sales were KRW 176.3 billion, down 15.3% year on year, a steeper decline than in soju. Soju sales were KRW 382.7 billion in the second quarter of 2026, up 0.1%, and the company says it kept its leading position despite weak demand.

Against peers, Hite Jinro's profit structure leans on soju and cost efficiency. Overseas sales were flat at KRW 267.5 billion in 2025, so the shift away from a domestic-centered model is slow. Signs of a demand recovery have not yet appeared clearly in reported results.

06

Outlook

At its third-quarter 2026 results the company said portfolio expansion, including Terra Sparkling and Terra & Chamisul, would strengthen its market position and lift results further. In beer, it plans to widen consumer reach with new products such as Terra Zero.

Overseas, the Vietnam production plant is due for completion at the end of 2026 and is planned to produce about one million cases of soju a year. The target of KRW 500 billion in overseas soju sales is set for 2030.

This plan comes from an April 2026 report, so the completion timing and production plan should be rechecked against later disclosures and reports. Whether beer recovers is likely to shape the overall sales trend.

Cumulative beer sales for January to September 2026 were KRW 540.8 billion, down 11.1%, and cumulative beer operating profit was KRW 17.7 billion, down 31.2%. The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, due by the end of March 2027, which will show fourth-quarter 2026 results and the full-year operating margin.

After that, the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report will show whether cumulative beer operating profit recovers.

08

Bull factors

Soju Protects Profit

Third-quarter 2026 soju operating profit rose 7.0% to KRW 45.0 billion, and cumulative January to September soju operating profit rose 3.1% to KRW 155.5 billion. Profit grew even as soju sales declined, which shows the cost structure is improving. The cost of sales ratio fell to 55.5% from 56% a year earlier.

Cost Efficiency and Profit-Focused Management

Third-quarter 2026 selling, general and administrative expenses were KRW 223.4 billion, down 6.9% from a year earlier. The company credits profit-focused management and cost efficiency for the improvement. The operating margin reached 8.0% in 2024, which shows the business has room to recover.

Cash Generation and Easing Financial Load

Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 232.6 billion, well above net profit attributable to owners of KRW 41.5 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 199.4% in 2024 to 186.5% in 2025. Financial load is moving in a lighter direction, which supports the bullish case.

09

Bear factors

Continued Sales Contraction

Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 632.4 billion, down 5.5%, and cumulative January to September sales fell 4.5%. Annual sales in 2025 declined 3.9% from the KRW 2.6 trillion recorded in 2024. Soju shipments fell 10.9% and beer shipments fell 4.6% in 2025, showing that demand weakness also appears in volumes.

Weak Beer Segment

Cumulative January to September 2026 beer sales fell 11.1% to KRW 540.8 billion, and cumulative beer operating profit fell 31.2% to KRW 17.7 billion. Second-quarter 2026 beer sales fell 15.3%. If beer recovery is slow, soju profit could remain the main support for overall earnings.

Weak Net Profit

Net profit attributable to owners fell 56.7% to KRW 41.5 billion in 2025, and the fourth quarter of 2025 posted a KRW 63.6 billion net loss attributable to owners. Third-quarter 2026 net profit attributable to owners was KRW 28.9 billion, down 29.0% quarter on quarter. Reduced financial income and higher other non-operating expenses were reported as causes of the drop.

10

Risk factors

Prolonged Demand Weakness

If the liquor market contraction lasts, soju and beer volumes could decline further. Whether the 10.9% drop in soju shipments seen in 2025 continues is the key variable. Cost cuts and lower SG&A can only protect operating profit within limits.

Beer Competition and New Product Effect

Competition with the market leader in beer continues. Whether new products such as Terra Zero and Terra Sparkling show up in cumulative beer results still needs confirmation. Second-quarter 2026 beer sales fell 15.3%, so the sales contribution from new products has not yet clearly appeared.

Leverage and Stalled Overseas Growth

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 2.13 trillion, larger than total equity of KRW 1.14 trillion. Overseas sales were KRW 267.5 billion in 2025, roughly unchanged from 2024. If export growth lags after the Vietnam plant opens, the overseas growth pillar could weaken.

12

Overall view

Hite Jinro's third-quarter 2026 sales fell 5.5% to KRW 632.4 billion, yet operating profit rose 6.2% to KRW 57.7 billion, showing profit defense in the quarter.

Soju operating profit rose 7.0% to KRW 45.0 billion and held up earnings, while beer sales and cumulative operating profit both declined and remain an open issue. On an annual basis, 2025 sales were KRW 2.5 trillion and net profit attributable to owners was KRW 41.5 billion, below 2024.

The fourth quarter of 2025 swung to a KRW 63.6 billion net loss attributable to owners, but operating profit stayed in the black from the first to the third quarter of 2026 and sales rose for three consecutive quarters. However, net profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters totals only KRW 41.9 billion.

On the financial side, the debt-to-equity ratio of 186.5% and operating cash flow of KRW 232.6 billion at the end of 2025 sit side by side. Bullish factors are soju profit defense and cost efficiency, while bearish factors are shrinking sales, weak beer, and weak net profit.

The next checkpoints are the Vietnam plant completion and the fourth-quarter and full-year results in the 2026 annual report.
