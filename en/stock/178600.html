02

Business structure

Daedong Korea Ginseng is a red ginseng manufacturer with its head office and plant in Gunbuk-myeon, Geumsan-gun, Chungcheongnam-do. The company website describes its business as manufacturing and selling ginseng and red ginseng products and health functional foods. It was founded in 2002 and listed on KONEX in 2013.

Its range covers whole red ginseng, red and black ginseng concentrates, stick products, capsules and powders, as well as teas and candies. Its own brands are BULROGEON, DUHAM and GINSSEN.

The company's English website cites annual production capacity of 7,730 tons and exports to 20 countries, and says it serves client orders on contract-manufacturing and development-manufacturing terms.

Export markets mentioned include China, Japan, Turkey, the UAE, Hong Kong, the United States, Vietnam, the Philippines and Australia. No confirmed filing figures were found for the revenue split among own brands, contract manufacturing and exports, so these are described qualitatively only.

In the competitive landscape, KGC (Cheong Kwan Jang) is the dominant leader. KGC recorded 2025 production of KRW 249.2 billion, and slipped to third place in the production ranking.

As the market shifts from a single large brand toward online and small brands, some observers say the influence of contract manufacturers has grown. Daedong Korea Ginseng is a red ginseng specialist of mid-to-small scale that holds both its own brands and manufacturing capability.

Recent trends

Standalone results for the fiscal year ended June 2026 (25th term) are as follows. Revenue was KRW 45.40 billion, operating profit was KRW 1.94 billion and net profit was KRW 294 million. The prior year had revenue of KRW 45.73 billion, operating profit of KRW 1.17 billion and a net loss of KRW 1.42 billion.

Revenue dipped slightly, but gross profit rose to about KRW 9.4 billion from about KRW 7.8 billion, which lifted operating profit. The operating margin improved to 4.28% from 2.57%, and a negative income tax expense also helped the swing to a net profit.

Even so, operating profit had fallen from KRW 5.19 billion in 2023 to KRW 2.43 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.17 billion in 2025 before rebounding, so it remains below earlier levels.

At the annual general meeting on September 30, 2026, shareholders approved all four agenda items, including the financial statements and a dividend resolution.

The balance sheet remains a point of pressure. At the end of June 2026, total assets were KRW 68,666 million, total liabilities KRW 41,588 million and total equity KRW 27,078 million. The debt-to-equity ratio was 153.59%, borrowings dependence was 52.06% and interest coverage was about 1.1 times.

The shares closed at KRW 1,530 on October 27, 2026, down 14.95% from the prior session. Market capitalization was KRW 8.8 billion on January 23, 2026, per Hankyung Korea Market. It now stands at KRW 6.8 billion, and no filing or news report was found that explains the latest drop.

04

Outlook

The sector backdrop mixes headwinds for red ginseng with tentative signs of stabilization. In 2025 data from the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety, red ginseng production fell 14.2%, which industry observers attribute to a shift from immunity-driven to everyday health-care consumption.

In the same data, vitamins and minerals overtook red ginseng in domestic sales for the first time. Meanwhile, the Korea Health Supplements Association said the 2025 domestic supplement market recovered 0.2% to KRW 5.96 trillion and that the red ginseng market also recovered slightly.

One survey found red ginseng had the largest purchase share among consumers aged 61 and over, so population aging may support a demand floor. Health supplement exports rose 14.2% to USD 318.17 million in 2025, though export items are diversifying from red ginseng toward vitamins, probiotics and omega-3.

At the company level, the key question is whether an operating margin in the low-4% range can be held or improved. With sales flat, cost-of-goods and selling expense control drove the profit result, and no earnings guidance was found.

Given the heavy interest burden, operating cash flow and the pace of debt repayment also warrant monitoring. KONEX average daily turnover stayed at the KRW 600 million level in July 2026, the first month below KRW 700 million since December 2014.

One report also noted six KONEX delistings this year attributed to deteriorating management, so market-wide liquidity conditions can affect share-price volatility independently of company fundamentals.

Bull factors

Operating profit recovery and return to net profit

Operating profit for the year ended June 2026 was KRW 1.94 billion, up 66% from the prior year. The company moved out of the prior year's net loss of KRW 1.42 billion to post a net profit of KRW 294 million.

Gross profit grew even as revenue declined, which suggests room for further improvement in profitability metrics. Whether the trend continues will be tested in future results.

Production base and export footprint

The company cites annual capacity of 7,730 tons, about 50 types of production equipment and exports to 20 countries. It says its lines meet global standards such as GMP, HACCP and FSSC 22000.

Health supplement exports posted double-digit growth for a third consecutive year through 2025, which is a supportive backdrop for expanding overseas sales. The company's export share and export results were not confirmed in separate filings.

Senior demand and signs of stabilization

Among consumers aged 61 and over, red ginseng had the largest purchase share. Purchase experience among senior households rose 2.6 percentage points year on year in 2025. The Korea Health Supplements Association put the red ginseng market at KRW 953.6 billion, a slight recovery.

Such indicators are cited as evidence that red ginseng demand could hold up on a senior base without a sharp exodus.

Bear factors

Flat sales and structural shift in the category

Revenue declined from KRW 48.35 billion in 2023 to KRW 46.07 billion in 2024 and KRW 45.73 billion in 2025. Revenue for the year ended June 2026 slipped further to KRW 45.40 billion, so top-line growth is not evident. Domestic red ginseng production fell 14.2% in 2025.

Vitamins and minerals also overtook red ginseng in sales for the first time, showing intensifying competition within the supplement market.

Heavy borrowing and thin interest coverage

The debt-to-equity ratio is 153.59%, borrowings dependence is 52.06% and interest coverage is about 1.1 times. Net profit of KRW 294 million is small relative to operating profit, as non-operating expenses offset a large share of earnings. The quick ratio of 24.97% also leaves limited short-term liquidity headroom. A worsening rate or cost environment could push net results back into a loss.

Low liquidity on KONEX

Average daily turnover on KONEX in July 2026 was KRW 628.85 million. One listed-company executive said consecutive limit-down moves caused by a lack of volume are common. Daedong Korea Ginseng's own turnover on October 27, 2026 was listed at just KRW 5,294, yet the shares fell 14.95% in the session. Because quotes can swing on tiny volumes, it is hard to judge how well the price reflects company value.

Risk factors

Market structure

Reports say KONEX is small in market capitalization and revenue, trading is sluggish and transfer-listing screening to KOSDAQ has become stricter. Six KONEX companies have been delisted this year because of management difficulties.

Market-wide sentiment and funding conditions directly affect the tradability and volatility of individual stocks. No plan by Daedong Korea Ginseng to transfer its listing was confirmed.

Industry

Growth in traditional items such as red ginseng and probiotics is slowing while demand fragments toward vitamins, minerals and omega-3. The domestic supplement market has hovered around KRW 6 trillion for three straight years.

KGC is also broadening from red ginseng into general health foods, so competition with large players could intensify. Raw-material and other cost variables may also affect earnings, but no specific figures were confirmed.

Financial

Borrowings dependence is 52.06% and interest coverage is about 1.1 times. Even a modest decline in operating profit could quickly erode the ability to carry interest costs. In the year ended June 2025, the company posted a net loss despite an operating profit. The maturity profile and interest terms of its borrowings were not confirmed.

08

Overall view

Daedong Korea Ginseng is a KONEX-listed company specializing in red ginseng manufacturing, and both operating and net profit improved in the year ended June 2026. Revenue edged lower over the same period, so profitability recovered without top-line growth.

Category indicators are mixed: red ginseng production fell in 2025, while association data showed a slight recovery. On the financial side, heavy borrowing and low interest coverage are variables for judging how durable the earnings recovery is.

KONEX's thin liquidity creates an environment in which the share price can move sharply even on days of minimal turnover. This report is for information only, and how to weigh the bullish and bearish factors is left to each reader.
