Atec: Operating Profit Recovers, Quarterly Swings Widen

Atec's 2025 revenue of KRW 160.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 8.7 billion both grew sharply, yet net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 394 million and quarterly profits in 2026 have swung widely.

  1. 1

    Revenue in 2025 rose 246.5% year on year to KRW 160.2 billion, and operating profit rose 494.8% to KRW 8.7 billion.

  2. 2

    Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 97.5% to KRW 394 million in the same year. The company attributed this to discontinued operations following a business transfer and spin-off, along with an impairment on investments in subsidiaries.

  3. 3

    Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 4.73 billion, down 13.0% year on year, while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 442 million, turning positive from a loss a year earlier.

  4. 4

    In September 2026 the company disclosed a KRW 19.1 billion branch IT equipment maintenance contract with a Korean bank, running from August 1, 2026 to July 31, 2028.

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Atec was founded in 1993 to make and sell LCD display application products and listed on KOSDAQ in 2001. Its company profile describes financial automation equipment, including ATMs, and logistics automation systems as its operations, combining the latest IT with financial services.

Another corporate information site presents PC and monitor supply to public institutions and procurement channels as the core business, so descriptions of the portfolio differ by source.

In 2025 Atec took over the production and sales operations for financial automation equipment such as ATMs from a subsidiary and added an IoT unit to its group. A spin-off in the same year changed the business structure, and discontinued operations were reflected in consolidated results.

Demand in financial automation is tied to digital transformation at banks and to replacement of branch equipment. A KRW 19.1 billion branch IT equipment maintenance contract with a Korean bank, disclosed in September 2026, runs from August 1, 2026 to July 31, 2028 and equals 11.94% of 2025 revenue.

Supply contracts disclosed from January 1 to September 1, 2026 totaled two deals worth KRW 25.0 billion.

In public procurement, quality certifications act as a competitive gate, and the company is described as holding an innovation product certification, reportedly a first in the domestic PC industry, and an S-grade quality procurement mark. Overseas, it has supplied large-scale ATMs and is widening its position as a global ATM maker.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 91.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 71.9 billion in 2023, down 21.5%, and to KRW 46.2 billion in 2024, down 35.7%, before rising to KRW 160.2 billion in 2025, up 246.5%. Within 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit rose from KRW 896 million in 2022 to KRW 919 million in 2023, up 2.5%, then to KRW 1.46 billion in 2024, up 59.1%, and to KRW 8.7 billion in 2025, up 494.8%, marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025.

The operating margin climbed from 1.0% in 2022 to 5.4% in 2025, with operating profit growing faster than revenue in each year shown.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 5.36 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.8 billion in 2023, down 10.5%, rose to KRW 15.5 billion in 2024, up 223.9%, and then dropped to KRW 394 million in 2025, down 97.5%. Within 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts across 2022 to 2025.

The company explained that the 2025 results reflected discontinued operations after a prior-year business transfer and spin-off, and that an impairment on investments in subsidiaries reduced non-operating income and net profit.

Operating cash flow was KRW 4.23 billion in 2022, KRW 7.79 billion in 2023, and KRW 29.8 billion in 2024, before falling to KRW 10.2 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 11.1% in 2022 to 74.1% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 99 billion, of which KRW 98.2 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 73.3 billion.

Quarterly results began with revenue of KRW 47.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 5.43 billion, and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.32 billion in the third quarter of 2025.

The fourth quarter of 2025 posted revenue of KRW 51.7 billion, up 8.7% from the prior quarter, but an operating loss of KRW 2.64 billion, a swing into loss, and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 14.9 billion, with losses widening.

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 41.5 billion, down 19.8% from the prior quarter, while operating profit of KRW 4.19 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.03 billion both turned positive.

The second quarter of 2026 brought revenue of KRW 53.6 billion, up 29.1%, operating profit of KRW 10.5 billion, up 150.0%, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 9.62 billion, up 138.8%.

The third quarter of 2026 showed revenue of KRW 47.4 billion, down 0.3% year on year, operating profit of KRW 4.73 billion, down 13.0% year on year, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 442 million, a swing back to profit from a year earlier.

05

Industry analysis

The most frequently cited driver of Atec's profit recovery is rising demand for unmanned operations. A report on the cumulative results through the third quarter of 2025 named expanding unmanned demand as the main reason for the sharp improvement.

The explanation of first-quarter 2026 results pointed to unmanned trends and wider overseas market expansion together. Digital transformation in banking translates into demand for replacing branch equipment and automated machines, which is cited as the demand base for the financial automation segment.

Descriptions of the domestic financial automation equipment industry note that the company moved into distribution automation on the back of large project wins and proprietary technology. Overseas, the company is described as building large-scale ATM supply experience and widening its position as a global ATM maker.

In public procurement, innovation product certification and quality procurement grades function as competitive advantages. The industry cycle depends on the equipment investment plans of financial institutions.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue, down 0.3% year on year, suggests that the expansion in unmanned demand has not flowed straight through to quarterly sales. On the competitive side, maintenance contracts with large financial clients and public procurement certifications are presented as points of differentiation.

Bull factors

Improving operating margin

The operating margin rose from 1.0% in 2022 to 5.4% in 2025, indicating improved profitability. Operating profit reached KRW 10.5 billion in the second quarter of 2026, up 150.0% from the prior quarter, showing improvement at the quarterly level as well.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.03 billion in the first quarter and KRW 9.62 billion in the second quarter of 2026, both in the black.

Large maintenance contract and overseas ATM demand

A KRW 19.1 billion maintenance contract with a Korean bank, signed in 2026, runs for 730 days through July 31, 2028. Whether the disclosed supply contracts translate into revenue in the coming quarters is the next point to check.

The company is building large-scale ATM supply experience overseas, and expanding unmanned demand was cited as a factor behind the improvement in the cumulative results through the third quarter of 2025.

CEO share purchases and price relative to book value

Between March 3 and 10, 2026, CEO Shin Seung-young bought 52,929 shares on the open market, raising his stake to 26%, according to a disclosure. Director Shin Jong-su also acquired 7,023 shares through open-market purchases in the same period. The price-to-book ratio below 1.0x is cited by some as one of the bullish arguments.

Bear factors

Weaker net profit base

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 97.5% to KRW 394 million in 2025. The company cited discontinued operations and an impairment on investments in subsidiaries as contributing factors. Combined over the last four quarters, the result is a loss, so the recovery has not yet accumulated.

Quarterly earnings volatility

In the fourth quarter of 2025 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 2.64 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 14.9 billion. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 47.4 billion fell 0.3% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 4.73 billion fell 13.0%.

Because quarterly results have alternated between profit and loss, a single quarter's trend is hard to read as a direction.

Rising debt ratio and subsidiary valuation swings

The debt ratio rose from 11.1% in 2022 to 63.8% in 2024 and 74.1% in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 73.3 billion against total equity of KRW 99 billion, so the debt burden has grown.

The company said an impairment on subsidiary investments reduced 2025 net profit, meaning changes in subsidiary value feed directly into reported earnings.

Risk factors

Business restructuring

Discontinued operations from the 2025 business transfer and spin-off make direct comparison with pre-2024 results difficult. It is not fully clear from disclosures alone which segments generate profit after the restructuring. Until the changes settle, the range of interpretations for reported results remains wide.

Contract concentration and disclosure cadence

Supply contracts disclosed from January 1 to September 1, 2026 numbered two, and no supply contract disclosures were made in the same period of 2025. Revenue flow can depend on one or two large contracts, so the size and timing of contract disclosures matter. Long-term maintenance deals add stability, but a gap in new orders could create a revenue hole.

Subsidiary impairment and board changes

An impairment on investments in subsidiaries can move non-operating income and net profit together. How much this item affected 2025 results should be confirmed in the annual report.

After the March 2026 annual general meeting, registered directors increased from three to five, so the management direction under the new board is another item to watch.

Overall view

Atec's 2025 revenue of KRW 160.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 8.7 billion expanded both its scale and its operating profitability. The operating margin rose from 1.0% in 2022 to 5.4% in 2025, and operating profit reached KRW 10.5 billion in the second quarter of 2026.

On the other hand, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 394 million in 2025, and combining the last four quarters still leaves a net loss. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.73 billion fell 13.0% year on year, so quarterly profit trends are uneven.

The debt ratio rose from 11.1% in 2022 to 74.1% in 2025, marking a change in the financial structure. On valuation, the price-to-book ratio sits below 1.0x and the dividend yield is above the KOSDAQ average, while the price-to-earnings ratio cannot be calculated because of the loss structure.

Large maintenance contracts, the CEO's share purchases, and overseas ATM supply are bullish factors, while discontinued operations, the impairment, and quarterly profit swings are bearish factors.

The next checkpoints are fourth-quarter 2026 results and the operating margin and net profit level in the 2026 annual report.
