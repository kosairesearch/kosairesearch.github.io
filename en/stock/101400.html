02

Business structure

N Citron is a non-memory semiconductor company founded in 2000, centered on fully digital audio amplifier chips for digital TVs. As of the report basis, its segments are semiconductor chips, food service, and other, and an information site describes royalty income from IP use alongside chip sales.

Per a Dealsite report from September 2026, food service generated KRW 9.0 billion (51%) of first-half 2026 consolidated sales of KRW 17.7 billion, versus KRW 7.4 billion (42%) for semiconductors. Semiconductors were 100% of sales at listing but fell below half in 2025.

The core chip is the fully digital audio amplifier chip, which WISEreport's company monitor put at a 21% market share as of September 2025. Key customers are reported to be Chinese appliance makers TCL and Hisense, and an FnGuide company summary also points to stable sales from these clients.

The food service business centers on operating Gordon Ramsay-branded restaurants, and the new management has said it plans to refocus the company on semiconductors.

The company therefore combines two very different businesses, TV audio chips and restaurants, and which one becomes the center is the starting point for assessing its structure.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales rose from KRW 24.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 35.9 billion in 2023 (up 44.4% year on year), then reached KRW 36.3 billion in 2024 (up 1.2%), the largest year in 2022 to 2025. Sales fell to KRW 34.4 billion in 2025 (down 5.4%).

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 175 million in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 421 million in 2023 (a turnaround to profit), then to an operating loss of KRW 2.46 billion in 2024 (a return to loss) and KRW 5.06 billion in 2025 (losses continued and widened), making 2024 and 2025 two consecutive loss years.

The operating margin was negative in every year except 2023 (1.2%), and the -14.7% in 2025 was the lowest of the four years. One company summary attributed the profitability decline to rising costs.

Controlling-shareholder net income swung from a KRW 1.08 billion profit in 2023 to net losses of KRW 1.75 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.24 billion in 2025, with the 2025 loss narrower than 2024.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 2 billion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder loss of KRW 1.24 billion, and the two should be read separately. Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 1.12 billion in 2024 to positive KRW 1.46 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales peaked-to-trough in a pattern where KRW 11.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 fell to KRW 6.92 billion in the fourth quarter (down 41.3% from the prior quarter), then recovered to KRW 8.47 billion, KRW 9.22 billion and KRW 9.83 billion across the first three quarters of 2026.

Although the operating loss in the third quarter of 2026 (KRW 1.06 billion) was smaller than in the third quarter of 2025, the controlling-shareholder net loss widened to KRW 1.83 billion from KRW 994 million.

The controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 through third quarter of 2026) totals KRW 2.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for digital TV audio semiconductors is tied to the trend toward larger and higher-resolution TV screens, so the product is linked to TV set shipments. The company has said it is pursuing market expansion based on the technical edge of its fully digital audio amplifier chip.

In 2026, global chip supply has been constrained by the expansion of AI, the Middle East war, and prolonged US-China tensions, and according to the company, major electronics makers are building inventory early, which is also lifting component demand.

In March 2026 the company said that rising advance orders from large Chinese customers mean demand is exceeding supply. That is the company's own statement, and if customers change their inventory strategies, order flow could shift, so short-term and structural factors should be separated.

The 21% share is a single-product figure as of September 2025. Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, also listed in the same semiconductor sector, are centered on memory, which limits direct comparison of business structure.

With the semiconductor share of sales at 42% in the first half of 2026, the industry read should separate the audio chip business from the food service business.

06

Outlook

In a March 19, 2026 announcement, the company said advance orders were rising, centered on large Chinese customers.

Order volume reportedly grew from about 9.01 million units in the fourth quarter of 2025 to about 9.6 million in the first quarter of 2026, and the company expected it to reach about 15.1 million units in the second quarter.

It said it is running expanded production and coordinating supply schedules with some customers. On disclosed figures, second-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 9.22 billion, up 8.9% from the prior quarter, and third-quarter sales were KRW 9.83 billion, up 6.6%.

Comparing the order forecast with actual sales growth will require the quarterly volume explanations in the 2026 annual report.

The 3-for-1 free capital reduction and 2-for-1 share consolidation disclosed on March 17, 2026 were aimed at covering accumulated deficits and stabilizing the share price, according to the company, with the consolidation effective April 25, 2026. Paid-in capital fell from KRW 35.2 billion to KRW 11.7 billion.

On September 3, 2026 the largest shareholder changed to investment partnerships linked to the private equity firm behind them, and the KRW 6 billion third-party rights offering is for operating funds. The company plans M&A using existing cash and has left open a possible sale of its food service business.

The first board reshuffle took place at an extraordinary shareholders' meeting on August 28, 2026, and for the next two years and six months, the existing and new sides hold nomination rights in a 4-to-3 split.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly sales up for three straight quarters

After a low of KRW 6.92 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, sales rose to KRW 8.47 billion, KRW 9.22 billion and KRW 9.83 billion across the first three quarters of 2026. The recovery in sales has come alongside a narrower operating loss, which is the main positive. The steady climb from the low is consistent with core demand not having fully weakened.

Rising advance orders from Chinese customers

In March 2026 the company said advance orders from large Chinese customers were rising, with order volume increasing from about 9.01 million units in the fourth quarter of 2025 to about 9.6 million in the first quarter of 2026. It also projected second-quarter volume of about 15.1 million units.

These are company figures, so how much of this reached sales will need to be confirmed in later periodic reports.

Change in largest shareholder and plan to refocus on semiconductors

Investment partnerships tied to the private equity firm Cactus PE became the largest shareholder in September 2026, and the new management said it plans to refocus the business on semiconductors.

The KRW 6 billion rights offering added operating funds, and M&A of semiconductor-related companies using existing cash is under review. The change in funding and management direction could be read as a starting point for rebuilding the core business.

09

Bear factors

Continued losses and wider losses in some periods

Operating losses ran for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025, and the company has reported losses in each of the five quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. The controlling-shareholder net loss in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 1.83 billion, wider than a year earlier.

Even with sales recovering, the earnings structure has not yet returned to profit, which is the main bearish point.

Shrinking core share and falling sales

In the first half of 2026 the semiconductor share of consolidated sales was 42%, below the 51% from food service. Sales fell 5.4% in 2025, and third-quarter 2026 sales were down 16.6% from a year earlier.

Although quarterly sales rose from the prior quarter, they remain below year-ago levels, which is the main bearish evidence.

Capital reduction and governance transition

On March 17, 2026, the day the free capital reduction and share consolidation were announced, the stock fell 21.88% intraday, and the market raised concerns about shareholder dilution and weaker sentiment.

Control is passing in stages, with the existing side holding four board nomination rights and the new side three over the next two years and six months. The governance transition adds uncertainty to how quickly the business plan can be executed.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration

Key customers are concentrated among Chinese appliance makers TCL and Hisense. The advance orders the company cited came from customers building inventory against supply disruptions, so if that stockpiling eases, orders could fall with it. Changes in strategy at a few large customers could feed directly into sales.

Execution of business reshaping

No target company or timing for the M&A funded by existing cash has been disclosed, and whether the food service business will be sold has not been decided. If the acquisition target does not fit the semiconductor core, or a food service sale is delayed, the pace of restructuring could slow. Depending on how this plays out, consolidated sales and the profit mix could shift substantially.

Profitability and capital structure

The operating margin was -14.7% in 2025, and controlling-shareholder losses continue. The capital reduction shrank paid-in capital, but profits do not return automatically once deficits are covered. The debt ratio of 25.2% is low, yet operating cash flow was negative in 2024, so cash generation has not been stable.

Overall view

N Citron combines TV audio amplifier chips with a restaurant business, and in the first half of 2026 food service accounted for 51% of sales against 42% for semiconductors.

On disclosed figures, quarterly sales rose for three consecutive quarters from the fourth-quarter 2025 low through the third quarter of 2026, and the operating loss narrowed from the third quarter of 2025.

However, the controlling-shareholder net loss widened to KRW 1.83 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and losses have run for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025.

The company said in March 2026 that Chinese advance orders were rising, but that is a company statement, and its effect on sales needs to be confirmed in the 2026 annual report.

The change in largest shareholder, the plan to refocus on semiconductors, and the review of M&A using existing cash are positives, but the targets and timing have not been set. The governance transition is ongoing, and the stock was volatile during the capital reduction, which leaves uncertainty.

Taken together with the price-to-book discount and the continued net losses, the market's view depends on whether earnings recover. The next annual operating margin and any M&A disclosure are the first points to check.
