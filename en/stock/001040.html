02

Business structure

CJ is a business holding company that oversees CJ CheilJedang, CJ ENM, CJ OliveYoung and CJ Logistics. As of the 2025 annual report, it held stakes in eight subsidiaries including CJ CheilJedang and CJ ENM.

As of 31 March 2026, the CJ group was organized around four core business groups alongside the holding company: food and food service, biotechnology, logistics and new retail, and entertainment and media.

The holding company's own operating revenue comes from brand royalties, rent on investment property and dividends from subsidiaries. As a result, its standalone earnings depend on subsidiary dividend flows and trademark income.

In food, CJ CheilJedang reorganized its business into three divisions, Lifestyle Food, Technology Materials and Core Materials, effective 1 July 2026. Logistics and new retail has grown in weight within group sales.

According to reports, that segment, which includes CJ OliveYoung and CJ Logistics, accounted for 44.1% of group sales in the first half of 2026, while weakness in biotechnology slowed group profitability.

CJ OliveYoung booked standalone sales of KRW 1.729 trillion in 3Q 2026, and its domestic store count fell by 63 stores over the year to 1,331. Its foreign-customer offline sales rose 42.5% in the same quarter.

At the holding-company level, subsidiaries operate in different industries, so they are hard to group under a single competitor, and each subsidiary's business conditions need to be read separately.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on DART periodic reports, annual sales rose from KRW 40.9 trillion in 2022 to KRW 41.4 trillion in 2023 (up 1.0%), KRW 43.6 trillion in 2024 (up 5.5%) and KRW 45 trillion in 2025 (up 3.1%). Sales have grown for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit was highest in 2024 at KRW 2.55 trillion (up 24.9%), then slipped 0.8% to KRW 2.53 trillion in 2025. 2023 was the smallest year, with operating profit of KRW 2.04 trillion (down 5.3%). Operating margin was 5.3% in 2022, 4.9% in 2023, 5.8% in 2024 and 5.6% in 2025.

Owner-attributable net income went from KRW 202.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 194.8 billion in 2023 (down 3.6%) and KRW 100.5 billion in 2024 (down 48.4%), before recovering to KRW 143.3 billion in 2025 (up 42.7%).

Net income including non-controlling interests fell from KRW 686.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 142.6 billion in 2025, so the gap between the two measures reflects changes in the non-controlling share.

Quarterly, the company posted an owner-attributable net loss of KRW 233.7 billion in 4Q 2025 and returned to profit of KRW 75.7 billion in 1Q 2026. 1Q 2026 operating profit of KRW 460.7 billion, down 35.3% from the prior quarter, was the low point.

Operating profit then rose to KRW 531.8 billion in 2Q and KRW 618.1 billion in 3Q, two consecutive quarterly increases. Even so, 3Q 2026 operating profit fell 7.1% year on year, and owner-attributable net income of KRW 105.7 billion was down 26.4%.

The sum of owner-attributable net income over the latest four quarters is KRW 9.07 billion. NH Investment & Securities attributed the profit miss to fewer working days and higher fuel costs at CJ Logistics, the sale of the F&C business at CJ CheilJedang, and early costs for new ventures at CJ OliveYoung.

Operating cash flow was KRW 4.04 trillion in 2022, KRW 5.02 trillion in 2023, KRW 4.83 trillion in 2024 and KRW 4.99 trillion in 2025, staying in the KRW 4 trillion range.

05

Industry analysis

CJ's results move with external variables such as domestic consumption, foreign visitor demand, oil prices and logistics costs. CJ OliveYoung's sales growth in 3Q 2026 was 11%, roughly half the 23% rate of 2Q.

The company attributed the slowdown partly to the exclusion of its US entity's sales from global mall results and to a high base from the consumer coupon boost in 3Q 2025. Analysts described domestic customer sales as close to flat.

Foreign-customer purchasing, by contrast, remains strong: cumulative foreign purchases at domestic offline stores passed KRW 1 trillion in August 2026. In logistics, higher fuel costs have weighed on expenses.

In food, one report said CJ CheilJedang's operating profit returned to growth on strong sales of overseas food products and bio materials. Weakness in biotechnology, however, has been cited as a drag on group profitability.

CJ CheilJedang is also trimming low-margin, non-core businesses while restructuring its portfolio. The position of the industry cycle cannot be determined from these sources alone, so the direction of quarterly sales and profit is the thing to watch.

06

Outlook

NH Investment & Securities expects the value of unlisted subsidiary CJ OliveYoung to be gradually reflected in the holding company's share price once the dual-listing guideline takes effect. The same report said 3Q earnings fell short of market expectations, but it focused more on direction than on short-term results.

CJ OliveYoung's cumulative sales for 1Q to 3Q 2026 reached KRW 5.06 trillion. Reaching annual sales of KRW 7 trillion, a figure cited in the industry, would require KRW 1,936.1 billion in 4Q 2026, according to press reports, which is 22.5% above the same quarter a year earlier.

CJ OliveYoung attributed its costs to upfront investments in opening North American offline stores, building local logistics hubs, and overseas logistics and marketing expenses. Those costs were booked in 3Q net income.

Whether the North American investment turns into sales growth, and when those costs ease, will determine the pace of earnings recovery. CJ CheilJedang announced on 1 July 2026 that it would reorganize into Lifestyle Food, Technology Materials and Core Materials divisions, and it is also exiting low-margin businesses.

At the group level, the weight of logistics and new retail continues to rise. The 2026 annual report, the next periodic filing, will show full-year operating margin and owner-attributable net income, providing a baseline against the quarterly trend.

08

Bull factors

OliveYoung leads top-line growth

CJ OliveYoung's standalone sales for 3Q 2026 reached KRW 1.729 trillion, up 11% year on year, and cumulative sales for 1Q to 3Q topped KRW 5 trillion. Foreign-customer offline sales rose 42.5%, and visitor spending is working as one growth engine. If this pattern continues, the sales base of the subsidiary widens.

Possible reflection of holding value in the share price

In a 17 November 2026 report, NH Investment & Securities said the value of unlisted subsidiary CJ OliveYoung would gradually be reflected in the holding company's share price if the dual-listing guideline takes effect.

In the same 17 November 2026 report, NH Investment & Securities kept its Buy rating and KRW 175,000 target price. If that linkage takes hold, subsidiary value becomes a larger factor in explaining the holding company's share price.

Cash generation and a quarterly profit rebound

Operating cash flow staying in the KRW 4 trillion range each year shows that the cash-generating base held up even as profit fluctuated. Quarterly operating profit bottomed at KRW 460.7 billion in 1Q 2026 and then rose to KRW 531.8 billion and KRW 618.1 billion in the next two quarters.

Owner-attributable net income also returned to profit in 1Q 2026. The evidence of a recovery at the quarterly level is a supportive factor.

09

Bear factors

North American investment costs weigh on profit

CJ OliveYoung's net income for 3Q 2026 was KRW 138.8 billion, down 8.4% year on year. The company described upfront costs for North American store openings, a local logistics hub and overseas logistics and marketing as the reason.

In the same quarter, its domestic store count fell by 63 over the year to 1,331, and total offline sales grew only 11%. Overseas costs are being booked while domestic customer sales are stagnant.

A thin base of owner-attributable earnings

Owner-attributable net income fell to KRW 100.5 billion in 2024, and the company posted a net loss of KRW 233.7 billion in 4Q 2025. Owner-attributable net income over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 9.07 billion. In 3Q 2026 it was KRW 105.7 billion, down 26.4% year on year.

NH Investment & Securities noted that 3Q operating profit came in below market consensus. Because the swings are large, one or two quarters are not enough to determine the direction.

Debt burden and subsidiary cost structure

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 172.0% in 2022 to 176.0% in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 29.9 trillion against total equity of KRW 17 trillion. Operating margin eased from 5.8% in 2024 to 5.6% in 2025. At CJ Logistics, fewer working days and higher fuel costs added expenses. Rising costs at subsidiaries limit how quickly the holding company's earnings can recover.

Risk factors

Antitrust regulation

The Fair Trade Commission imposed collusion fines on CJ CheilJedang totaling KRW 372.9 billion, including KRW 138.3 billion on sugar, KRW 131.7 billion on flour and KRW 102.9 billion on starch sweeteners. Such fines weigh directly on food-division profit.

The pace at which subsidiary value is reflected in the share price may also vary with the detailed rules of the dual-listing guideline.

Foreign visitor demand shifts

CJ OliveYoung's sales growth has come to rely more on purchases by foreign visitors to Korea. Cumulative foreign purchases at domestic offline stores passed KRW 1 trillion in August 2026, but if visitor demand falls or purchasing patterns shift, the pace of sales growth could slow quickly.

As foreign demand accounts for a larger share, the impact of changes in that demand on subsidiary sales also grows. A decline in foreign purchases could reduce the company's growth drivers.

Food portfolio restructuring

CJ CheilJedang reorganized into Lifestyle Food, Technology Materials and Core Materials divisions on 1 July 2026 and is exiting low-margin businesses. The scope and pace of these exits could change the sales scale and profit structure of the food division.

As logistics and new retail account for a larger share of group sales, the outcome of food-division adjustments may become another variable for holding-company earnings. The timing at which the reorganization's effects appear in quarterly results has not been set.

12

Overall view

CJ is in a phase where its subsidiaries are moving in different directions. Annual sales rose from KRW 40.9 trillion in 2022 to KRW 45 trillion in 2025, but operating profit slipped to KRW 2.53 trillion in 2025 after peaking at KRW 2.55 trillion in 2024.

Owner-attributable net income recovered to KRW 143.3 billion in 2025, yet the latest four quarters totaled only KRW 9.07 billion, so the earnings base is thin. Operating profit rose for two consecutive quarters after the 1Q 2026 low, but 3Q 2026 still declined year on year.

Subsidiary CJ OliveYoung is in a phase where sales growth and North American investment costs appear at the same time.

On the supportive side, NH Investment & Securities expects subsidiary value to be gradually reflected in the share price once the dual-listing guideline takes effect, and operating cash flow has stayed in the KRW 4 trillion range.

On the other side are volatile owner-attributable earnings, a higher debt ratio (172.0% in 2022 and 176.0% in 2025), and fines in the food division. The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report and the 1Q 2027 quarterly report.
