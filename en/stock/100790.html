Profit recovery from AI exits, with quarterly volatility as the test

02

Business structure

Mirae Asset Venture Investment is a venture capital firm that forms and manages funds investing in small and venture companies. It pools capital from limited partners (LPs) into investment partnerships and, as general partner (GP), earns management fees and performance-linked distributions.

Successful exits raise performance fees and distribution income; the company has been completing exits such as SEMIFIVE. The company runs 55 funds and had 16 employees as of August 2026. A 2025 press report put assets under management at KRW 1,318.3 billion.

The portfolio concentrates on AI value-chain and deep-tech companies, with Rebellions (AI chip design), SEMIFIVE (chip design platform) and Moloco among the names cited.

The company invested KRW 36.3 billion in SEMIFIVE and recovered about KRW 29.8 billion in December 2025, and SEMIFIVE listed on KOSDAQ on December 29, 2025. Its GP commitments have run well above the customary 5 percent level, and in some funds above 15 percent.

Fundraising also depends on policy-institution capital, with the National Pension Service, KIF, Korea Post Insurance and the Export-Import Bank of Korea among the sources cited in reports. Fund performance depends on investment results and exit timing.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 218 billion in 2022 to KRW 217 billion in 2023 (down 0.4% year on year), then rose to KRW 233.9 billion in 2024 (up 7.8%) and KRW 239.8 billion in 2025 (up 2.6%), so revenue increased for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

The largest year in 2022 to 2025 was 2025, and the smallest was 2023. Operating profit went from KRW 59.2 billion in 2022 (operating margin 27.2%) to KRW 31.9 billion in 2023 (down 46.1%) and KRW 15.7 billion in 2024 (down 50.8%), then recovered to KRW 35.1 billion in 2025 (up 123.6%, operating margin 14.6%).

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders followed the same path: KRW 56.1 billion in 2022, KRW 24.5 billion in 2023, KRW 8.56 billion in 2024 and KRW 30.9 billion in 2025 (up 261.5%). Net profit for 2025 including non-controlling interests was KRW 30.9 billion.

The operating margin fell from 27.2% in 2022 to 6.7% in 2024 and rebounded to 14.6% in 2025, still below the 2022 level. The debt-to-equity ratio declined from 249.7% in 2022 to 190.8% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 378.3 billion, of which KRW 301.2 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 721.8 billion. Operating cash flow was -KRW 53.3 billion in 2022, KRW 15.7 billion in 2023, KRW 129.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 48.8 billion in 2025.

Quarterly swings were larger. Revenue in Q4 2025 was KRW 113.8 billion (up 422.5% from the prior quarter) and profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 19.2 billion (up 165.3%).

In Q1 2026 revenue fell to KRW 53.7 billion (down 52.8%) and profit attributable to controlling shareholders to KRW 11.2 billion (down 41.5%). In Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 86.1 billion, operating profit KRW 22.4 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 16.4 billion.

In Q3 2026 the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 36.6 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 28 billion, swinging into the red.

Because quarterly results for a venture investor move with portfolio valuations and exit timing, a single quarter is a weak guide to the underlying trend.

05

Industry analysis

The venture capital industry moves on two axes: inflows of policy capital and the state of the exit market. A 2025 press report described a strategy of maximizing portfolio value while exit markets were frozen and building new funds in parallel, and the company extended the term of some maturing funds by one year.

In 2026 policy-institution capital expanded. The company formed a KRW 107 billion project fund to invest in the AI chip designer Rebellions under the National Growth Fund, and formed seven funds backed by government and policy institutions.

Competition for AI chip deals is intense: in April 2026 the company said it would lead a pre-IPO round in Rebellions with KRW 120 billion. A China-only AI fund with a target size of KRW 38 billion was reported in May 2026.

On exits, SEMIFIVE listed in December 2025, and Rebellions and Moloco are cited as candidates for listing in 2027. Competitive position depends on securing policy capital and recovering value through pre-IPO and post-IPO exits. Among domestic VC firms, the concentration on AI names is a differentiating factor for this company.

06

Outlook

The one confirmed schedule is new fund formation. The target for a new venture fund was raised to as much as KRW 300 billion. The plan is to complete a first closing within 2026 and then add investors through June 2027 to reach that size.

The target rose step by step: after the company was selected as GP in the Korea Post Insurance program, the target went to KRW 250 billion, and after it secured a GP role in the Export-Import Bank of Korea program, back to KRW 300 billion.

A report in August 2026 said the company expected to form another KRW 460 billion of funds by year-end, which would take policy-related fund formation above KRW 900 billion for the year. Including those, assets under management were expected to exceed KRW 2.4 trillion in 2026.

These are press-reported expectations, not company guidance. On the portfolio side, the listing timing of Rebellions and Moloco is the key variable. Part of the SEMIFIVE stake is under lock-up, so exits proceed in stages.

The items to watch are the size of the first closing within 2026, the year-end fund formation result, and the exit results and valuation gains disclosed in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Direction of the profit recovery

Operating profit rose 123.6% to KRW 35.1 billion in 2025 from KRW 15.7 billion in 2024, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 261.5% to KRW 30.9 billion. The operating margin improved from 6.7% to 14.6%. It remains below the 27.2% of 2022, but the direction of earnings capacity has been up.

Expansion of policy-backed fund formation

By August 2026 the company had formed KRW 469 billion of new funds on a cumulative basis. Selection as a National Pension Service venture fund manager and a GP role in the Export-Import Bank program raised the target to KRW 300 billion, which would widen the base of management fees if achieved.

Exit track record and high GP commitment

The company invested KRW 36.3 billion in SEMIFIVE and recovered about KRW 29.8 billion in December 2025, after SEMIFIVE listed on KOSDAQ on December 29, 2025. With GP commitments well above the customary level, fund valuation gains flow more visibly into company income.

09

Bear factors

Swing to losses in the third quarter of 2026

The company posted an operating loss of KRW 36.6 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 28 billion in the third quarter of 2026, after profitable results in the prior quarter.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 18.8 billion, below the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 30.9 billion. Bears point out that quarterly profit weakened before the recovery was confirmed as sustained.

Exit market and fund maturity pressure

A 2025 press report described the exit market as frozen, and the company extended the term of a maturing fund by one year. That means portfolio companies still await exits. If extensions accumulate, the timing of management fee and performance fee realization could be pushed back.

Governance and conflict-of-interest questions

During the screening process, questions were raised about a conflict of interest involving the chairman of Mirae Asset Financial Group. Because he co-chairs the strategy committee that drafts the master plan for the National Growth Fund, the selection of this affiliate drew fairness challenges.

The committee is an advisory body and does not directly take part in individual GP selections, but some market participants argue the conflict cannot be fully dismissed.

10

Risk factors

Valuation swings in investments

Operating results depend heavily on valuation gains and disposal gains on held stakes. The third-quarter 2026 operating and net losses show how this structure can produce a quarterly loss. When valuation gains swing sharply from quarter to quarter, annual earnings become harder to forecast. The split between valuation and operating results is the point to monitor.

Delayed listings and exit schedule

If the listings of Rebellions and Moloco, which are cited as candidates for a 2027 listing, are delayed, exit timing and fund liquidation plans would slip. SEMIFIVE shares are subject to lock-up, so exits proceed in stages. Uncertain listing timing can widen the gap between fund maturities and exit timing.

Achievability of fundraising targets

The new venture fund target depends on a first closing within 2026 and an increase through June 2027. Because policy-institution capital is a large share, changes in institutional conditions or budgets would directly affect fund size. A shortfall would narrow the management fee base and the capacity for new investments.

What to watch next

  1. Within 2026

    Completion and size of the first closing, which must be formed within one year of the National Pension Service selection. How much of the KRW 300 billion target is filled at this stage indicates fundraising strength.

  2. End of December 2026

    Whether the planned KRW 460 billion of additional fund formation by year-end is completed. The gap between the planned and actual amounts will determine how reliable the 2026 assets-under-management outlook is.

  3. End of March 2027

    Filing of the 2026 annual report, when full-year 2026 results including Q4 are formally disclosed, with operating profit, profit attributable to controlling shareholders and operating cash flow. It is the checkpoint for whether earnings recovery held after the third-quarter loss.

  4. June 2027

    Deadline for the target to raise the new venture fund to as much as KRW 300 billion through multiple closings. Whether the increase is completed shows if the plan to grow assets under management is on track.

12

Overall view

Mirae Asset Venture Investment recovered from its 2024 low, with operating profit of KRW 35.1 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 30.9 billion in 2025.

However, it posted an operating loss of KRW 36.6 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 28 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 18.8 billion.

The business relies on 55 funds and expanding policy-institution capital; cumulative new fund formation of KRW 469 billion through August 2026 and a KRW 300 billion target for a National Pension Service-backed fund underpin the growth in assets under management.

On exits, the listing timing of SEMIFIVE and of portfolio companies such as Rebellions and Moloco is the key variable.

Bull factors are the earnings recovery, expanding fund formation and high GP commitments; bear factors are the third-quarter swing to losses, the risk of a weak exit market and the conflict-of-interest questions. Because the multiple changes depending on the earnings base, the choice of base matters for interpretation.

The next checkpoints are the first closing within 2026 and the operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the 2026 annual report, due by the end of March 2027.
