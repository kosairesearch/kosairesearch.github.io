02

Business structure

SKAI is an IT software company that builds data structuring and AI solutions on graph database (Graph Database) technology. Based on Q1 2026 revenue, general IT system integration (SI) accounted for 63% and Graph Database revenue for 37%.

The company is growing its knowledge-based AI analysis solution, Ontobia, which structures an organization's digital knowledge and provides organizational AI infrastructure. It has introduced graph RAG technology to improve the reliability of generative AI answers, while also supplying combined software and hardware.

In the 2025 preliminary results disclosed on February 27, 2026, the company attributed the revenue decline to lower hardware sales in its infrastructure business. The same disclosure cited higher DBMS and advertising revenue as drivers of improved profitability.

In the first half of 2026, infrastructure and solution revenue fell and losses widened, while the DB management unit is pursuing AI solution integration and security reinforcement but demand recovery has lagged.

Because the mix spans hardware supply and software licensing and technical services, revenue volatility by segment is relatively large.

Compared with large SI players such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, SKAI's revenue scale is far smaller, making the differentiation of its graph DB technology a central competitive variable.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 19.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 27.8 billion in 2023 (up 45.2% year on year), then fell 28.7% to KRW 19.8 billion in 2024 and 21.2% to KRW 15.6 billion in 2025, a two-year decline after the 2023 peak. Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating income swung from KRW 1.62 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 13.3 billion in 2023, followed by an operating loss of KRW 12.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.39 billion in 2025, a three-year run of operating losses in which the loss has been narrowing.

The operating margin fell from 8.5% in 2022 to -47.7% in 2023 and -60.8% in 2024 before improving to -28.1% in 2025. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened from KRW 0.44 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.7 billion in 2023 and KRW 24.8 billion in 2024, then narrowed to KRW 15.1 billion in 2025.

In 2024, the net loss of KRW 24.8 billion exceeded the operating loss of KRW 12.1 billion, so costs below the operating line added substantially to the deficit. The debt-to-equity ratio jumped from 107.3% in 2022 to 239.4% in 2023 and stood at 190.5% in 2025.

At end-2025, total equity was KRW 13.2 billion, of which KRW 12.6 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 25.2 billion.

Operating cash flow moved from KRW 2.89 billion in 2022 to KRW -9.93 billion in 2023 and KRW -11.8 billion in 2024, before narrowing to KRW -3.83 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 2.52 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 4.2 billion in Q4 2025 (up 67.2% quarter on quarter), then KRW 3.23 billion in Q1 2026 (down 23.1%) and KRW 2.74 billion in Q2 2026 (down 15.3%), before reaching KRW 4.33 billion in Q3 2026 (up 58.1% quarter on quarter and 72.1% year on year).

Over the same span, operating losses were KRW 2.67 billion, KRW 1.35 billion, KRW 1.65 billion, KRW 2.42 billion and KRW 2.51 billion, a run of five straight loss-making quarters since Q3 2025. The pace at which losses are narrowing has been slower than the pace of the revenue recovery.

05

Industry analysis

Graph databases and knowledge graphs are drawing attention as data technologies that help improve the accuracy of generative AI answers. One Korean market data service described high growth expected in the B2B AI agent market, and SKAI is positioning graph RAG and knowledge-based analysis solutions to match this trend.

However, slower demand recovery in the first half of 2026 weighed on revenue in the same period. Large peers such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever operate on a large-scale SI base, while SKAI's 2025 revenue of KRW 15.6 billion puts it far smaller.

In security, Ahnlab, and in data-driven services, NICE Information Service overlap with SKAI only partly, so direct competition differs by segment.

The infrastructure business, which includes hardware supply, tends to swing with equipment investment and project awards, so whether software-centric revenue grows its share will shape the company's resilience across cycles.

The advertising business run alongside the AI solutions also contributes a separate revenue stream. Pinning down where SKAI sits in its industry cycle requires segment-level order data that the company has not yet made fully visible.

06

Outlook

The next periodic report is the 2026 annual business report, and in the meantime the facts to track are the acquisition and the cash position. According to press reports, of a KRW 25.35 billion equity purchase price, KRW 22.815 billion would be paid in convertible bonds.

The same reports noted that consolidated cash and cash equivalents at end-June 2026 were about KRW 157 million, and that operating cash outflows also occurred. An appraisal for the target company projects its revenue rising from KRW 11.3 billion in 2026 to KRW 96.538 billion in 2031.

The same appraisal assumes operating profit turning from a KRW 6.845 billion loss in 2026 to a KRW 4.592 billion profit in 2027. Revenue from AI video-generation software-as-a-service (SaaS) is shown rising from KRW 13.066 billion in 2027 to KRW 73.835 billion in 2031.

The appraisal's 2031 operating margin estimate is 34.79%. These figures are assumptions inside the target company's appraisal, not SKAI's disclosed outlook or a target price. A Korean financial news report characterized the valuation as resting on overlapping optimistic assumptions.

The appraisal also assumes that combined cost of sales and selling and administrative expenses grow only 3.47 times between 2026 and 2031. No company-level guidance figures for SKAI have been presented.

The next checkpoints are whether operating results in Q4 2026 break the five-quarter loss run, and whether the operating margin in the 2026 annual report improves from -28.1% recorded in 2025.

Bull factors

Revenue recovery

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 4.33 billion, up 58.1% quarter on quarter and 72.1% year on year. Quarterly revenue returned to the KRW 4 billion range following KRW 4.2 billion in Q4 2025.

Narrowing losses

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 13.3 billion in 2023 to KRW 4.39 billion in 2025. The operating margin improved from -60.8% in 2024 to -28.1% in 2025. The Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 2.51 billion was smaller than a year earlier.

AI product transition

The company is pushing graph DB-based knowledge structuring through Ontobia and graph RAG to improve the reliability of generative AI. An appraisal for the target company assumes AI video-generation SaaS revenue rising from KRW 13.066 billion in 2027 to KRW 73.835 billion in 2031. This is an appraisal estimate, not a figure SKAI has disclosed.

09

Bear factors

Persistent losses

The company has posted operating losses for three straight years from 2023 to 2025 and five straight quarters from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026. The four-quarter total of net loss attributable to controlling shareholders is KRW 26.4 billion. Operating losses remain around KRW 2.5 billion per quarter even as revenue increases.

Limited financial capacity

At end-2025, total liabilities of KRW 25.2 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 13.2 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio was 190.5%. Consolidated cash and cash equivalents at end-June 2026 were about KRW 157 million, with operating cash outflows also reported. Operating cash flow remained negative at KRW -3.83 billion in 2025.

Acquisition uncertainty

Of a KRW 25.35 billion equity purchase price, KRW 22.815 billion would be paid in convertible bonds, which creates potential dilution if converted. Press coverage pointed to a valuation resting on overlapping optimistic assumptions. If the target's earnings turnaround does not follow the appraisal, the financial burden could increase.

Risk factors

Business mix and revenue volatility

Total revenue is exposed to swings in infrastructure hardware sales. The company cited lower hardware sales in its infrastructure business as the cause of the 2025 revenue decline. In the first half of 2026, lower infrastructure and solution revenue was identified as a driver of weaker results.

Funding and dilution

With little cash on hand and operating cash outflows, additional funding may be needed. A small third-party allotment of roughly KRW 1 billion was completed with one subscriber. If the convertible bonds convert into shares, the number of shares outstanding would rise and dilute existing holders.

Supply-demand and deal execution

As of November 6, 2026, short interest stood at about 2.17% of market capitalization. If the target's results diverge from the appraisal assumptions, the acquisition value and the company's financial burden could both be affected. Business focus may also shift during deal closing and post-acquisition integration.

Overall view

SKAI is a software company that combines graph DB-based AI solutions with IT system integration, and its 2025 revenue of KRW 15.6 billion was below the 2023 peak. The operating loss narrowed from KRW 13.3 billion in 2023 to KRW 4.39 billion in 2025, and Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 4.33 billion was up 72.1% year on year.

However, operating losses have persisted for five straight quarters from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026, and the four-quarter total of net loss attributable to controlling shareholders is KRW 26.4 billion. Negative operating cash flow and a debt-to-equity ratio of 190.5% at end-2025 show that financial capacity is limited.

The 2031 revenue and operating margin estimates in the target's appraisal are not SKAI's disclosed results, and press coverage characterized those assumptions as optimistic.

Strengths include the revenue recovery and narrowing losses, while weaknesses include the continuing losses, constrained cash, and uncertainty around the acquisition.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin and cash flow in the 2026 annual report, and the closing of the acquisition and the conditions for converting the bonds.
