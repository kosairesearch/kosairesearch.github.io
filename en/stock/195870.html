02

Business structure

Haesung DS makes lead frames and package substrates used in semiconductor packaging. According to an external company data compilation for 2025, lead frame sales were KRW 494 billion, or 75.6% of the total, and package substrate sales were KRW 159.4 billion, or 24.4%.

Lead frames are metal frames used in automotive and analog semiconductors, and their customers include IDM companies such as Infineon and STMicroelectronics as well as OSAT companies such as ASE and Amkor.

The company highlights its PPF plating technology, and THE ELEC described it as the only lead frame maker in Korea. Package substrates centre on memory products such as DDR4 and DDR5, with Samsung Electronics and SK hynix as major customers.

Exports in 2025 were KRW 634.7 billion against domestic sales of KRW 18.7 billion, so overseas sales account for nearly all revenue. Key raw materials are gold, copper, silver and palladium, and gold and copper accounted for 36.9% and 32.1% of raw material purchases in 2025 according to one compilation.

The company says it links raw material prices to selling prices, which passes part of metal price changes into product pricing. In competition, lead frames are viewed as a top two group globally, while package substrates are compared with domestic substrate makers such as Simmtech and Daeduck Electronics. Facility investment in 2025 was KRW 126.9 billion according to the same compilation.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results have trended down since a peak in 2022. Sales went from KRW 839.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 672.2 billion in 2023 (down 19.9%) and KRW 603 billion in 2024 (down 10.3%), then rose to KRW 653.4 billion in 2025 (up 8.4%).

Operating profit went from KRW 204.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 102.5 billion in 2023 (down 49.9%), KRW 56.9 billion in 2024 (down 44.5%) and KRW 46.5 billion in 2025 (down 18.2%), so it fell three years in a row from 2023 through 2025.

In 2025 sales rose while operating profit fell, and the operating margin dropped from 24.4% in 2022 to 7.1% in 2025. Controlling-shareholder net income declined from KRW 159.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 84.4 billion, KRW 58.7 billion and KRW 23.8 billion in 2025, down 59.4% in that year.

One external compilation says the 2025 gross margin was 15.0% versus 18.5% in 2024, and that other operating income fell from KRW 23.8 billion to KRW 13.5 billion, which deepened the net profit decline. The quarterly picture turned.

From KRW 178.6 billion in sales and KRW 16.1 billion in operating profit in Q3 2025, Q4 2025 reached KRW 179.9 billion in sales (up 0.8% quarter on quarter) and KRW 21.8 billion in operating profit (up 35.3%).

Q1 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 11 billion (down 49.6%), the low point, then Q2 2026 rose to KRW 24.9 billion (up 126.5%) and Q3 2026 to KRW 33.8 billion (up 35.9%), an increase in two consecutive quarters.

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 245.3 billion (up 16.2% quarter on quarter and 37.4% year on year), and THE ELEC reported an operating margin of 13.8%.

Controlling-shareholder net income, however, fell from KRW 19.1 billion in Q2 2026 to KRW 15.5 billion in Q3 (down 18.6%), so operating gains did not fully carry through to net income. Controlling-shareholder net income for the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, totals KRW 55 billion.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 196.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 60.6 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 67 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Lead frame demand is tied to automotive semiconductor demand. The company says the spread of electric and autonomous vehicles has increased the number of automotive chips per vehicle, which has lifted lead frame volumes.

Securities reports in 2025 attributed the rise in automotive lead frame volumes to customers normalizing their inventories. Lead frames for power semiconductors are described as growing steadily alongside IDM customers.

Package substrates are highly exposed to the memory cycle, and a 2025 compilation shows their share of sales shrinking on weak memory demand. The shift from DDR4 to DDR5 became a full-scale effort from the second half of 2025, according to the company.

Next-generation DDR6 substrates have more layers than roll-to-roll processes can handle, so the company plans to adopt panel-based processing. In lead frames the company competes with Taiwanese rivals, and a Meritz Securities report from late 2025 noted that Taiwanese peers had moved to raise prices.

A common view is that automotive is in a recovery phase after inventory adjustment, while memory is in a DDR5 transition phase. Domestic substrate peers such as Simmtech and Daeduck Electronics are the reference group for package substrates.

06

Outlook

A October 2026 report said the company plans to spend KRW 190 billion building facilities in unused space at its Changwon site. The same report described a plan to buy an affiliate plant in Ansan for KRW 68 billion and expand new capacity in the Seoul metropolitan area.

To produce multi-layer substrates, the company plans to adopt panel-based processing for DDR6 products and expand capacity for high-density, multi-variety substrates.

In the first half of 2026 it spent KRW 8.4 billion on items including a new manufacturing building, etching equipment and CO2 laser drills, according to a report. The company says it intends to stay competitive not only in automotive chips but also in AI and memory package markets.

Commissioning timing and utilization at the new Changwon facilities will show how much capacity is actually added. The pace of the electric vehicle market recovery and the pricing trend for automotive lead frames are the next variables.

Quarterly margins may shift depending on the timing gap between gold and copper prices and the pass-through to selling prices. Whether AI and server memory demand lifts DDR5 substrate volumes will be the key factor for earnings after 2027.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in automotive lead frame volumes

The spread of electric and autonomous vehicles has increased automotive chip content, and lead frames accounted for 75.6% of sales in 2025 according to a compilation. According to THE ELEC, Q3 2026 sales of KRW 245.3 billion were reported as the highest quarterly sales on record. The company's position as the only domestic lead frame maker is cited as a basis for its competitiveness.

DDR5 package substrates and customer base

For memory package substrates, the company said DDR5 volumes expanded from the second half of 2025, and its customers include Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 33.8 billion, up 109.8% year on year.

The plan to adopt panel-based processing for DDR6 is cited as a way to build competitiveness in next-generation substrates.

Pass-through of raw material prices to selling prices

According to Bloter, the company protected profitability in the first half of 2026 through a pricing linkage even as purchase costs for gold and silver rose sharply. Average export unit prices for lead frames rose 16.2%, from USD 7.82 per 1,000 pieces in 2025 to USD 9.09 in the first half of 2026. When cost changes pass through to selling prices, raw material swings have less effect on earnings.

Bear factors

Long-term decline in annual earnings

The operating margin fell from 24.4% in 2022 to 7.1% in 2025, and controlling-shareholder net income dropped from KRW 159.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 23.8 billion in 2025. According to an external compilation, the 2025 gross margin was 15.0%, below 18.5% in 2024.

Whether the three-year decline from 2023 to 2025 has turned into a sustained recovery is not yet visible in annual figures.

Volatility in quarterly earnings

Q1 2026 operating profit was KRW 11 billion, down 49.6% from the prior quarter. Controlling-shareholder net income in Q3 2026 was KRW 15.5 billion, down 18.6% from KRW 19.1 billion in Q2, moving against the operating trend. Quarterly swings are large enough that one or two quarters are not enough to establish a trend.

Cost and financial burden

Gold and copper make up a large share of raw materials, so if costs rise first and pass-through to selling prices lags, margins could come under pressure. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 47.5% in 2022 to 56.0% at the end of 2025.

Per an external compilation, facility investment in 2025 was KRW 126.9 billion, and if utilization does not follow, fixed cost burden could increase.

10

Risk factors

Raw materials and FX

Exports were close to 97% of 2025 sales, so FX movements feed directly into results. Gold and copper prices are also swing factors for profit. The pricing linkage reduces volatility, but timing gaps and currency effects remain.

Customer and demand concentration

Package substrates depend on memory demand from Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, while lead frames rely on IDM customers such as Infineon, STMicroelectronics and NXP and on automotive demand. A 2025 compilation shows the substrate share of sales shrinking during a memory slowdown.

Investment and financial burden

If the KRW 190 billion Changwon facilities and the KRW 68 billion Ansan purchase proceed, depreciation and borrowing costs could rise. The debt-to-equity ratio stood at 56.0% at the end of 2025. Until commissioning timing and utilization are disclosed, the earnings contribution from the investment is hard to gauge.

Overall view

Haesung DS's annual operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income fell three years in a row from 2023 through 2025 after a 2022 peak, and the 2025 operating margin was 7.1%.

Quarterly results changed direction in 2026, with Q1 operating profit of KRW 11 billion as the low point, followed by increases in Q2 and Q3. Q3 2026 sales of KRW 245.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 33.8 billion are the largest of the last five disclosed quarters.

Lead frames have strengths in automotive chip demand and the pricing linkage, while panel-based DDR6 processing and the DDR5 transition are cited as the next steps for substrates. Annual earnings have not yet returned to 2022 levels, and quarterly net income has at times moved against operating profit.

Raw material prices, the memory cycle and investment burden all remain as factors of similar weight. The next checkpoints are the preliminary Q4 2026 results and the annual figures in the 2026 annual report.
