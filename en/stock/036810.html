Pellicle and chiller growth, profit turnaround still pending

02

Business structure

Fine Semitech was founded in 1987 as a maker of pellicles and chillers used in semiconductor processes.

The company mainly produces pellicles and chillers, and through subsidiaries it makes pellicle frames, multilayer ceramic substrates, silicon carbide semiconductor parts and conductive paste for electronic components.

In 2024 it acquired IMD, a producer of paste raw materials for electronic components, widening its materials business. Operations are reported in two areas, materials (including pellicles) and equipment (including chillers), and securities research also estimates earnings along these two lines.

A pellicle is a film that protects photomasks from contamination during lithography. According to wisereport company data as of September 2025, the company holds an 80% domestic share of semiconductor pellicles and 60% of FPD pellicles.

A ThinkPool research summary says chillers hold more than a 50% share within the infrastructure equipment vendor of a major customer. The company is developing pellicles for EUV processes and has completed development of EUV pellicle mounting and demounting equipment and inspection equipment.

Some products moved into sales in 2023 after customer demonstrations. R&D spending in 2024 was KRW 18.16 billion, equal to 7.65% of revenue on a consolidated basis. The new T-line at the Osan plant was completed in December 2024.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 223.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 197.6 billion in 2023 (down 11.5% YoY) and then rose to KRW 237.4 billion in 2024 (up 20.1% YoY) and KRW 280.3 billion in 2025 (up 18.1% YoY).

Revenue grew for two years in a row, from 2024 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022–2025 period while 2023 was the smallest.

Operating profit moved from KRW 6.18 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 10.8 billion in 2023 (a turn to loss from the prior year), then to operating profit of KRW 2.29 billion in 2024 (a turn to profit), and back to an operating loss of KRW 547 million in 2025 (a turn to loss).

The operating margin was 2.8% in 2022, -5.5% in 2023, 1.0% in 2024 and -0.2% in 2025, so revenue growth did not translate into margin recovery.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 44.1 billion in 2022 to a loss of KRW 13.6 billion in 2023, a profit of KRW 1.45 billion in 2024, and a loss of KRW 12.3 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 14.3 billion in 2025, a larger loss than the parent-shareholder figure.

A ThinkPool research summary attributed the 2025 revenue increase to expanded DUV pellicle sales and new customers, and said the widened net loss mainly reflected equity-method losses from associates.

By quarter, revenue was KRW 65.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 89.3 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 91.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026, rising for two consecutive quarters from the second quarter of 2026.

Operating profit was KRW 1.42 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (a turn to profit), KRW 9.62 billion in the second quarter (up 577.3% QoQ), and an operating loss of KRW 3.68 billion in the third quarter (a turn to loss QoQ).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a profit of KRW 7.32 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and a loss of KRW 7.9 billion in the third quarter.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 6.01 billion.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 60.6% in 2022 to 122.9% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 242.3 billion at year-end 2025 (of which KRW 241.3 billion attributable to controlling shareholders) and total liabilities of KRW 297.8 billion.

Operating cash flow turned positive at KRW 14.1 billion in 2025 after KRW -5.39 billion in 2024. FnGuide's company summary notes that a rise in one-off costs is limiting profitability.

05

Industry analysis

A common backdrop for the industry is rising demand for advanced semiconductors, including AI data center servers and HBM, which is increasing use of higher-value products according to FnGuide's company summary. The same summary also points out that a rise in one-off costs is constraining profitability.

Pellicles protect photomasks in lithography, so their demand is tied to chip production volumes and shifts in process generations. The EUV pellicles the company is developing could change its business structure depending on customer demand and the timing of commercialization.

Chillers manage temperature in process equipment, and a ThinkPool research summary links the expansion of investment at Pyeongtaek P4 and the Hwaseong plant to rising orders.

The same summary also outlined a scenario in which supply-chain disruptions for global components bring forward Pyeongtaek P4 East and P5 investment, or additional orders for eco-friendly chillers for the Taylor fab in the United States, could push equipment results above estimates.

Industry peers include equipment listed companies such as HANMI Semiconductor, Jusung Engineering and Wonik IPS, and materials listed companies such as Soulbrain and Dongjin Semichem.

The pellicle business holds a relatively high domestic share, but because the company combines equipment and materials, segment results may move differently through the cycle. In 2025, equity-method losses from associates widened the net loss, so non-core items affected results during that period.

06

Outlook

A ThinkPool research summary projects 2026 chiller revenue of KRW 124.0 billion, up 11.3% YoY. The same summary expects 2026 revenue in the materials division of KRW 188.5 billion, up 13.3% YoY.

FnGuide's consensus shows 2026 revenue of KRW 307.0 billion, operating profit of KRW 23.1 billion and net income of KRW 13.0 billion, and for 2027 revenue of KRW 353.1 billion, operating profit of KRW 37.1 billion and net income of KRW 26.0 billion.

The 2026 consensus operating profit of KRW 23.1 billion is more than three times the sum of the operating results disclosed for the first through third quarters of 2026, which is about KRW 7.36 billion, so fourth-quarter profit will determine whether that path is achieved.

The expansion at Pyeongtaek and Hwaseong cited as the basis for chiller orders needs to convert into actual orders. In the materials business, it is worth watching whether pellicle sales growth continues and whether display or semiconductor pellicles drive it.

The EUV pellicle and the mounting and inspection equipment have completed development, but the commercialization timing and sales scale have not been disclosed. If raw material and component prices keep rising, the effect on gross margin also needs to be checked.

The 2026 annual report must be filed by the end of March 2027, when the direction of the annual operating margin and debt ratio will be confirmed again.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly operating profit has shifted from a profit in the first quarter of 2026 to a wider profit in the second quarter and then a loss in the third, so earnings have not stabilized. On an annual basis the company posted operating losses in 2023 and 2025.

Because chiller and pellicle sales depend on specific customers and facility investment schedules, quarterly profit swings may widen.

Balance sheet and associates

Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 297.8 billion, above total equity of KRW 242.3 billion, for a debt-to-equity ratio of 122.9%. Equity-method losses from loss-making associates have widened the net loss before, so non-core items can affect results. Operating cash flow was positive at KRW 14.1 billion in 2025 but negative at KRW -5.39 billion in 2024.

Raw materials and technology transition

FnGuide's company summary points to rising one-off costs, including raw materials and components, as a constraint on profitability improvement. The EUV pellicle and related equipment have completed development, but their commercialization timing and sales scale have not been disclosed. If the pace of process transition differs from expectations, demand for the current sales mix could change.

Overall view

Fine Semitech has grown revenue two years in a row, in 2024 and 2025, supported by an 80% domestic share of semiconductor pellicles and expectations for chiller order growth.

However, it posted an operating loss of KRW 547 million and a parent-shareholder net loss of KRW 12.3 billion in 2025, and its operating margin stayed at -0.2%.

It returned to operating profit of KRW 9.62 billion in the second quarter of 2026, but slipped back to an operating loss of KRW 3.68 billion in the third quarter. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 6.01 billion.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 122.9%, and equity-method losses from associates are cited as a factor widening the net loss.

Consensus projects operating profit of KRW 23.1 billion for 2026 and KRW 37.1 billion for 2027, but the sum of operating results disclosed for the first through third quarters of 2026 falls short of that.

The key checkpoints are fourth-quarter profit, the annual operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report, and whether chiller orders convert into revenue.
