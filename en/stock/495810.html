02

Business structure

UVC mirrors factory equipment and data in a 3D virtual space and analyzes them with AI, positioning itself as a digital twin company.

Its core products are the OPC UA-based autonomous manufacturing platform OCTOPUS and the cloud-based FLEXING CPS, which the company says covers data collection through 3D virtualization, simulation and predictive maintenance on one platform. Revenue falls into three business types.

According to a June 2025 research report, on-premise digital twin build-outs generated KRW 3.65 billion in 2024, or 69.9% of total revenue, while solutions contributed KRW 1.18 billion (22.6%) and SaaS digital twin revenue KRW 60 million (1.1%). Other revenue was KRW 330 million (6.4%).

Because build-out projects made up most of 2024 sales, the timing and size of project awards drive results. Major customers include Samsung SDI, Samsung Heavy Industries, Hyundai Motor and KEPCO.

In competition, large system integrators such as LG CNS, Samsung SDS and Hyundai Autoever operate across broad manufacturing IT, so UVC is positioned to differentiate through digital twin and OPC UA-based platforms.

As of June 2025, the company had about 30 employees focused on software development and AI-based process optimization. The company is preparing a shift to SaaS, but SaaS revenue in 2024 remained at KRW 60 million.

03

Recent trends

Revenue for 2025 was KRW 3.585 billion, down 31.33% from KRW 5.221 billion in 2024. The operating loss was KRW 3.89 billion, wider than the roughly KRW 2.0 billion loss in 2024. The net loss was KRW 4.01 billion.

In 2023, revenue was about KRW 3.9 billion and the operating loss about KRW 1.2 billion, so 2024 saw sales grow alongside a larger loss.

The 2024 revenue increase came from on-premise build-outs rising to KRW 3.65 billion, while the 2024 operating loss reflected upfront spending on platform upgrades, hiring and preparation for SaaS.

A June 2025 research report forecast 2025 revenue of KRW 11 billion and operating profit of KRW 300 million, and the reported 2025 results diverged sharply from that outlook. Equity rose to KRW 1.32 billion at the end of 2025 from KRW 328 million at the end of 2024, and the equity ratio improved to 35.00%.

Operating cash outflow from ordinary activities widened to KRW 2.806 billion in 2025 from KRW 1.638 billion in 2024. A January 15, 2026 report confirmed the share price at 13,120 won, and the current price is 13,130 won.

At the March 31, 2026 general shareholders' meeting, agenda items including approval of the 16th-term financial statements passed, with an approval rate of 96.5% of issued shares.

04

Outlook

The key to future results is whether build-out orders continue to translate into annual revenue and whether SaaS sales become a recurring revenue base.

Because 2025 results fell well short of the forecast, the extent to which the large project expansion and the resumption of public substation digitalization, which underpinned the return-to-profit outlook, actually materialized remains an open question.

A 2025 company analysis listed sustaining profitability, establishing a recurring SaaS revenue structure, diversifying the customer base and expanding into global markets as future tasks.

On the financial side, equity grew and the equity ratio improved, but continued operating losses and cash outflows from ordinary activities could increase the need for additional funding.

The KONEX Grand Prize innovation award reported in May 2026 and the GS Grade 1 certification for OCTOPUS HUB point to technical credibility. Whether these translate into better profitability will show up in reported results.

With customers concentrated among large manufacturers and public institutions, their capital spending plans and procurement timing drive revenue swings.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 third-quarter report and the 2026 annual settlement disclosures, which will show the pace of revenue recovery, whether operating profit turns positive and whether cash flow improves.

05

Bull factors

Build-out track record and customer trust

On-premise digital twin build-outs generated KRW 3.65 billion in 2024, or 69.9% of revenue. The company says it has run projects for large manufacturers and public institutions including Samsung SDI, Hyundai Motor and KEPCO. A May 2026 report cited a KONEX Grand Prize innovation award.

Some analysts regard a record of work with customers that have demanding security and quality requirements as a commercial asset in winning similar projects.

Proprietary platform and certifications

OCTOPUS is described as an autonomous manufacturing platform built on OPC UA with eight solution modules. In 2025 the company received a Minister of Science and ICT Prize for its OCTOPUS solution at WSCE and was selected for the G-Next Global Expansion Support Program. OCTOPUS HUB obtained GS Grade 1 certification from TTA in December 2025.

Improved equity base

The equity ratio rose to 35.00% at the end of 2025 from 7.92% at the end of 2024. Paid-in capital increased from KRW 294 million to KRW 371 million, and total equity reached KRW 1.32 billion. The thicker capital base can be read as a buffer for funding R&D and the SaaS transition.

06

Bear factors

Wide gap between forecast and results

A June 2025 research report forecast 2025 revenue of KRW 11 billion and operating profit of KRW 300 million, anticipating a return to profit. Reported 2025 revenue was KRW 3.585 billion and the operating loss was KRW 3.89 billion. Both revenue and profit fell well short of the forecast. The gap suggests the forecast assumptions on project timing and cost structure did not match actual results.

Persistent losses and weaker cash flow

The 2025 operating loss widened from 2024 and the net loss reached KRW 4.01 billion. Cash outflow from ordinary activities grew to KRW 2.806 billion in 2025 from KRW 1.638 billion in 2024. The larger operating loss in 2025 came alongside falling revenue.

Small organization and reliance on borrowing

As of June 2025 the company had about 30 employees, a small organization. Borrowing dependence fell to 36.20% in 2025 from 54.02% in 2024 but remains high. The shares trade on KONEX, and market data show a trading value of zero won, indicating limited price discovery.

Risk factors

Macro

A slowdown in manufacturing capex could reduce digital twin build-out orders from large customers. Batteries and automobiles are industries with volatile capital spending, and UVC's key customers are concentrated there. Currency and interest rate moves could shift the timing of customer investment decisions.

Policy and procurement

Public institution projects depend on budget allocation and procurement timing. The resumption of KEPCO substation digitalization has been cited, but its timing and scale may change with budget execution. Heavy reliance on government R&D projects and public orders could leave revenue gaps when projects end.

Liquidity and capital

KONEX-listed companies have limited trading liquidity, which can produce sharp price moves. Market data show a trading value of zero won, and reports in early 2026 cited a share price of 13,120 won. Continued losses could require new equity or borrowing, which could dilute existing shareholders.

08

Overall view

UVC is a KONEX-listed company with a track record of digital twin build-outs for large manufacturers and public institutions, a proprietary platform and technical certifications.

However, 2025 revenue fell 31.33% year on year and the operating loss widened to KRW 3.89 billion, a sharp departure from the return-to-profit forecast a June 2025 research report had issued.

Strengths cited include the build-out track record, technical certifications and the improved equity ratio, while weaknesses include continued losses, a wider cash outflow from ordinary activities and heavy reliance on build-out revenue.

The balance between these factors will shift with the pace of revenue recovery and whether operating profit turns positive. Reported share prices of 13,120 won in early 2026 and zero-won trading value in market data mean price signals alone offer limited insight into the company's value.

Technical capability and customer references are documented, but a return to profitability has not yet been demonstrated in reported results.
