02

Business structure

Point Mobile, founded in 2006, is a maker of industrial mobile devices. The company sells industrial PDAs and mobile computers used in logistics, retail and production sites, along with Bluetooth barcode scanners, RFID readers and mobile payment terminals under its own brand.

Until 2012 it produced devices on an OEM and ODM basis for global companies including Honeywell and Datalogic, and it began launching its own-brand products in 2013. At a 2020 investor briefing, CEO Kang Sam-kwon said own-brand supply accounted for more than 70% of revenue.

A January 2021 report by the Electronic Times described the company as the top domestic supplier of industrial PDAs, with overseas sales above 90% of revenue and sales in 79 countries.

According to a November 2026 company announcement, Point Mobile supplies the PM86 industrial mobile computer, the PM5 wearable device and the TR54 tablet to Amazon.

In Europe, projects with retail stores in Poland and a railway project are under way, and the Japanese subsidiary began deliveries for the Yamato project in 2026. The company also cited higher warranty revenue, which carries high margins, as a factor lifting its cumulative operating margin.

Revenue share by business segment could not be confirmed as of November 10, 2026, so product-line weights are described qualitatively.

In competition, the company overlaps with many overseas brands in the global industrial mobile computer market, and it presents product breadth and customized total solutions as its differentiators.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 87.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 80 billion in 2023 (down 8.6% year on year) and KRW 77.3 billion in 2024 (down 3.4%), before recovering to KRW 87.2 billion in 2025 (up 12.8%). The largest year in the period was 2022 and the smallest was 2024.

In November 2025 the company said reaching record annual revenue this year was within reach, yet 2025 revenue of KRW 87.2 billion remains below the KRW 87.6 billion recorded in 2022.

Operating profit rose from KRW 316 million in 2022 to KRW 4.64 billion in 2023 (up 1,370.1%), swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.41 billion in 2024, and returned to a profit of KRW 3.01 billion in 2025. Operating margin was 0.4% in 2022, 5.8% in 2023, -1.8% in 2024 and 3.4% in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent moved from a net loss of KRW 2.51 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 4.84 billion in 2023, then KRW 1.59 billion in 2024 (down 67.2%) and KRW 2.85 billion in 2025 (up 79.6%).

Equity totaled KRW 51.9 billion at the end of 2025, all of it attributable to owners of the parent, and total liabilities were KRW 43.1 billion. The debt ratio rose from 43.6% in 2023 to 83.2% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 13.8 billion in 2023 and KRW 8.01 billion in 2024, then turned to -KRW 165 million in 2025.

Quarterly, revenue went from KRW 27.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 3.24 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 30 billion and KRW 2.35 billion in the fourth quarter, then fell to KRW 18.1 billion and KRW 372 million in the first quarter of 2026.

Revenue then rose to KRW 28.4 billion with KRW 4.2 billion in operating profit in the second quarter, and to KRW 30.5 billion in the third quarter, marking revenue growth in each quarter from the second quarter of 2026 through the third.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.54 billion was down 15.9% from the prior quarter but up 9.2% year on year, while profit attributable to owners of the parent of KRW 370 million was down 92.0% from the prior quarter and 90.2% year on year; the items behind that gap were not identified in sources available as of November 10, 2026.

The preliminary cumulative results for January to September 2026, announced by the company on November 10, 2026, show revenue of KRW 77.1 billion (up 34.7%), operating profit of KRW 8.1 billion (up 1,130.7%) and net income of KRW 6.6 billion, which the company attributed mainly to expanded Amazon supply.

Industry analysis

In its November 10, 2026 announcement, the company said it secured key components ahead of a rise in memory prices, which eased cost pressure.

The same announcement attributed growth mainly to expanded Amazon supply, and a November 7, 2025 announcement said cumulative Amazon-related revenue for January to September 2025 reached KRW 20.6 billion. In the domestic market, a January 2021 Electronic Times report described the company as the leading supplier of industrial PDAs with a share of about 30%.
