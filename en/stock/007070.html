Report overview

Supermarkets and home shopping lift profit as convenience stores slow

GS Retail's third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose to KRW 132.1 billion, but convenience store profit fell and profit attributable to owners dropped 46.7%, so where the earnings come from is the key question.

  3. 3

02

Business structure

GS Retail is a diversified retailer built around the GS25 convenience store chain, the GS The Fresh supermarket chain, and the GS SHOP home shopping channel. It listed on the KOSPI in December 2011 and is classified under general retail. Convenience stores remain the core business.

According to the third-quarter 2026 preliminary segment results announced on November 4, 2026, GS25 posted sales of KRW 2.47 trillion, up 1.1% year on year and a record quarterly high, but operating profit fell 8.1% to KRW 78.2 billion.

The company expanded its fresh-food-focused stores to 1,074 and grew vegetable and meat sales sharply. It also booked about KRW 9.4 billion of losses on inventory bought for promotions.

In the same quarter, GS The Fresh recorded sales of KRW 527.7 billion, up 14.9%, and operating profit of KRW 23.9 billion, up 159.8%, with 608 stores after a net addition of 27.

GS SHOP posted sales of KRW 253.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 24.2 billion, up 108.6%, while exclusive beauty and fashion product sales rose 17%. Quick commerce sales grew 52.2% and reached 10.1% of total sales for the first time in double digits. Sales to foreign tourists rose 69.4%.

The company said its profit engine is broadening from convenience stores to supermarkets and home shopping. In convenience retail, the company is typically grouped with BGF retail as one of the two leading players.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales rose from KRW 11.2 trillion in 2022 to KRW 12 trillion in 2025 and grew for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. The 2025 sales figure, up 2.8% year on year, was the largest of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit went from KRW 245.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 394 billion in 2023 (up 60.7%), KRW 239.1 billion in 2024 (down 39.3%), and KRW 292.1 billion in 2025 (up 22.2%). 2023 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

The operating margin moved through 2.2%, 3.4%, 2.1% and 2.4%, so revenue grew steadily while margins peaked in 2023, dipped in 2024 and recovered in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners fell from KRW 47.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.7 billion in 2023 (down 62.9%) and KRW 2.55 billion in 2024 (down 85.6%), before rising to KRW 43.4 billion in 2025 (up 1,604.9%). Because operating profit and owner profit did not move together, the two need to be read separately.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 47.6 billion in 2022, KRW 22.1 billion in 2023, KRW 9.79 billion in 2024 and KRW 50.2 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow was highest in 2025 at KRW 938.6 billion, after KRW 660.2 billion in 2024.

On a quarterly basis, the fourth quarter of 2025 was the low point: operating profit fell from KRW 113.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 53.3 billion, then rose to KRW 58.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 109.4 billion in the second quarter (up 87.7%) and KRW 132.1 billion in the third quarter (up 20.8%).

Owner profit swung to a net loss of KRW 73.1 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and returned to profit in the following three quarters, totaling KRW 85.5 billion over the last four quarters.

Operating results for the third quarter of 2025 and the second and third quarters of 2026 include discontinued operations gains and losses from a subsidiary that stopped operating, so quarter comparisons need care.

Samsung Securities attributed the convenience store profit drop to a high base, unfavorable weather and one-off costs. Preliminary cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 299.8 billion, up 25.5%.

05

Industry analysis

Convenience retail is in a low-growth phase, and the industry is going through store efficiency gains and restructuring.

In a July 29, 2026 report, Hana Securities cited the convenience store low-growth phase as a factor that lowers valuation, while arguing that store efficiency and the restructuring of weak businesses could create operating leverage once household income supports a consumption recovery in 2027.

Kiwoom Securities, in a June 30, 2026 report, pointed to a continued benefit from solid domestic consumption, sales growth in general merchandise at convenience stores, slower depreciation growth and possible gains from competitors' store restructuring.

Brokers also see growth in foreign tourist visits as a profit tailwind. In the supermarket channel, the government's distribution sales data showed SSM sales falling 2.9% in July 2026 and rising only 5.0% in each of August and September.

Over the same period, GS The Fresh's same-store sales grew 10.7%, above the monthly growth rate of SSM sales. In home shopping, GS SHOP has posted four consecutive quarters of improvement since the fourth quarter of 2025.

Compared with BGF retail, the other leading convenience store operator, GS Retail's profit mix leans less on convenience stores, and supermarkets and home shopping are in the phase of widening their contribution.

A June 2026 article noted that the shares of the two leading convenience store operators had lagged for an extended period despite expectations of improved earnings. Across the sector, the convenience store business is in a slower-growth phase, while fresh-food-focused supermarkets and home shopping are growing.

06

Outlook

The company said it will raise competitiveness in each business by strengthening fresh food, expanding quick commerce and developing exclusive products for home shopping.

In convenience stores, its scrap-and-build approach of enlarging stores or moving them to better locations, along with the expansion of fresh-focused stores, was cited as the core of same-store competitiveness.

GS SHOP is pushing sales strategies that link TV, mobile and social media, along with wider AI-based shopping services. GS The Fresh is combining new store openings with locally tailored operations.

In a September 14, 2026 report, Daishin Securities projected convenience store same-store sales growth of about 2% and supermarket same-store growth in the double digits. The same report said a rising share of foreign customer sales could support a revaluation.

Samsung Securities said on November 5, 2026 that it raised its earnings estimates for next year after the third-quarter results. Kiwoom Securities, in a June 2026 report, estimated second-quarter operating profit at KRW 105.1 billion, and the disclosed second-quarter figure was KRW 109.4 billion.

The next regular report is the 2026 annual business report, which will confirm full-year operating margin and debt ratio. Fourth-quarter 2026 results are the next data point to check alongside the annual figures.

Because convenience store profit fell in the third quarter, the pace of recovery in the core business is the main variable. Whether inventory losses and promotion costs persist into the fourth quarter also needs watching.

Several brokers tie a broader consumption recovery to 2027, so much of the outlook depends on next year's household spending.

08

Bull factors

Wider profit contribution from supermarkets and home shopping

Preliminary third-quarter 2026 data show GS The Fresh's operating profit rising 159.8% to KRW 23.9 billion, with same-store sales up 10.7%. GS SHOP's operating profit rose 108.6% to KRW 24.2 billion, partly offsetting the convenience store decline. Quick commerce reaching 10.1% of sales is cited as evidence of channel diversification.

Sustained recovery in operating profit

Operating profit rose from KRW 239.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 292.1 billion in 2025 and has increased for three straight quarters from the first quarter of 2026. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 132.1 billion was up 16.0% year on year. The operating margin recovered from 2.1% in 2024 to 2.4% in 2025.

Convenience restructuring and a possible consumption rebound

Kiwoom Securities said competitors' store restructuring could bring spillover benefits. Hana Securities argued that convenience store efficiency gains and restructuring could create operating leverage in a 2027 consumption recovery driven by household income. Both are presented as paths toward recovering convenience store profit.

09

Bear factors

Lower convenience store operating profit

Preliminary third-quarter 2026 data show GS25's operating profit down 8.1% to KRW 78.2 billion. Samsung Securities cited a high base, unfavorable weather and one-off costs as causes. With about KRW 9.4 billion of inventory losses booked, recovery in the core business remains a task.

Gap between operating profit and net profit

Profit attributable to owners fell to KRW 2.55 billion in 2024, and the fourth quarter of 2025 posted a net loss of KRW 73.1 billion. Third-quarter 2026 owner profit of KRW 50.4 billion fell 46.7% year on year. Operating gains have repeatedly not carried through to owner profit. Media reports noted the filing did not separately explain the drop in net profit.

Debt burden and a low-growth sector

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 128.4% in 2025 from 138.3% in 2024, but total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 4.21 trillion against total equity of KRW 3.28 trillion. Hana Securities cited the convenience store low-growth phase as a factor lowering valuation. Sales growth has not translated into margins above the mid-2% range.

10

Risk factors

Convenience store sector

Convenience stores face slow consumption growth and store competition at once. In the duopoly with BGF retail, the pace of store restructuring and new openings could shape profit. If same-store growth falls below forecasts, the pace of group-wide recovery would slow too.

Comparability of reported results

Results for the third quarter of 2025 and the second and third quarters of 2026 include discontinued operations gains and losses from a subsidiary that stopped operating. The third-quarter 2026 preliminary figures were disclosed before external audit completion and may differ from final results. Quarterly comparisons should account for these items.

Inventory losses and cost swings

About KRW 9.4 billion of inventory losses tied to promotional purchases were booked against third-quarter convenience store operating profit. Weather and the base effect from past consumer coupon payouts also move results quarter to quarter. If such costs recur, margin gains could be smaller.

Overall view

GS Retail's third quarter of 2026 showed operating profit rising, with supermarkets and home shopping pulling the group higher. Operating profit at the core convenience store business fell, and profit attributable to owners declined sharply year on year.

On a disclosed basis, operating profit has risen for three straight quarters, and owner profit moved from a fourth-quarter 2025 net loss to profit in the first quarter of 2026 before fluctuating. Annually, the 2025 operating margin recovered from 2024 but has not moved far above the mid-2% range since 2022.

Brokers have set targets from KRW 31,000 to KRW 37,000, which reflect each firm's view and serve as reference points. With convenience retail in a low-growth phase, the 2027 consumption trend and same-store growth are the next points to check. The 2026 annual business report will confirm full-year operating margin and debt ratio.
