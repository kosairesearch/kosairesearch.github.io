02

Business structure

Systemand Application Technologies operates two businesses: manufacturing, installation and maintenance of transportation equipment, and supply of mobile-related parts.

According to company introduction materials, its main transportation products are weigh-in-motion scales and static weighing scales, while its mobile-side products include die-cut products and printed matter.

The company says it is exploring new road-traffic businesses such as CCTV and VMS, building on its weighing-equipment know-how.

A technical analysis report published in March 2022 described its in-house modules for intelligent transport systems, including overload enforcement, traffic counting and traffic management systems. The same report also listed mobile phone component production within its scope.

In short, one pillar is road infrastructure equipment and the other is component supply for electronics. Segment revenue shares have not been confirmed in filings, so no figures are given. Major customer names and individual competitor shares have not been verified, so the description stays qualitative.

Because the 2022 business description predates current operations, the present mix should be checked against the segment disclosures in the next annual report. Consolidated sales were KRW 159.7 billion in 2025, below KRW 174.4 billion in 2022.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell 12.7% from 1,744 hundred million won in 2022 to 1,522 hundred million won in 2023, then rose for two consecutive years: 1,551 hundred million won in 2024 (up 1.9% year on year) and 1,597 hundred million won in 2025 (up 3.0% year on year).

Over 2022–2025, revenue was highest in 2022 and lowest in 2023.

Operating profit dropped 57.5% from 111 hundred million won in 2022 to 47.1 hundred million won in 2023, hit its lowest point at 33.5 hundred million won in 2024 (down 28.8% year on year), and then recovered to 39.1 hundred million won in 2025 (up 16.7% year on year).

The operating margin stayed low at 6.4% in 2022, 3.1% in 2023, 2.2% in 2024, and 2.4% in 2025. In other words, revenue has grown, but the margin has not returned to its 2022 level.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from 91.4 hundred million won in 2022 to 16.2 hundred million won in 2023 (down 82.3%), 25.3 hundred million won in 2024 (up 55.8%), and 7.9 hundred million won in 2025 (down 68.9%).

Net income for 2025 was 4.8 hundred million won, lower than the net income attributable to controlling shareholders, which implies that a loss was allocated to the non-controlling interests' share.

Cash flow from operating activities declined from 122 hundred million won in 2022 to 21.6 hundred million won in 2024, then recovered to 107 hundred million won in 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 63.8% in 2022 to 57.1% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total equity was 1,030 hundred million won and total liabilities were 588 hundred million won.

On a quarterly basis, fourth-quarter 2025 revenue of 479 hundred million won and operating profit of 31.5 hundred million won are the highest operating profit in the period reviewed.

Operating profit fell to 12.3 hundred million won in the first quarter of 2026, then rose for two consecutive quarters to 20.4 hundred million won in the second quarter and 21.9 hundred million won in the third quarter.

However, in the third quarter of 2026 the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of 14.1 hundred million won, and the sum of net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the most recent four quarters is 51.2 hundred million won. No specific one-off factors were identified in the body of the disclosure.

Bull factors

Sales recovery

Quarterly sales stayed high at KRW 48.6 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 47.4 billion in Q3 2026. Q3 2026 sales grew 15.1% year on year. The carry-through of sales growth to operating profit is cited as a bullish factor.

Quarterly operating profit rebound

Operating profit fell to KRW 1.23 billion in Q1 2026 but rose to KRW 2.04 billion in Q2 and KRW 2.19 billion in Q3, two straight quarters of increases. Q3 operating profit grew 17.4% year on year. The KRW 3.15 billion recorded in Q4 2025 was the highest in the window. The direction of recovery at the quarterly level is cited as a bullish basis.

Balance sheet improvement and cash generation

The debt ratio declined from 63.8% in 2022 to 57.1% in 2025. Operating cash flow recovered from KRW 2.16 billion in 2024 to KRW 10.7 billion in 2025. Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 58.8 billion, below total equity of KRW 103 billion. The lighter debt burden is cited as a bullish factor.

Q3 swing to a loss

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 1.41 billion in Q3 2026. The prior quarter had recorded KRW 1.86 billion. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totals KRW 512 million, which is not large.

The cause of the deterioration at the net profit level has not been confirmed, and this is cited as a bearish factor.

Earnings volatility and business uncertainty

New business execution

New road-traffic businesses such as CCTV and VMS are at a stage the company describes as exploring entry. The start timing, investment scale and customer wins have not been confirmed in filings. If the new businesses fail to contribute to the existing profit structure, the earnings recovery could be delayed. Checking execution is a key point of risk monitoring.
