Sena Technologies: Mesh Communication Widens Beyond Motorcycles

Sena Technologies started in motorcycle team communication and is extending into industrial WGC and robotic golf caddies. The company describes its 2026 cumulative results as record levels, but third-quarter profit fell sharply from the second quarter.

02

Business structure

Sena Technologies is a team communication wearables company founded in 1998 and newly listed on KOSDAQ in November 2025.

Its core is motorcycle product lines such as helmet intercoms, and the company said second-quarter 2026 motorcycle revenue of about KRW 64.2 billion rose roughly 40% year on year, making up most of total second-quarter revenue of KRW 75.5 billion.

Cumulative motorcycle revenue through the third quarter of 2026 reached about KRW 144.1 billion, up 15.7% year on year. Outdoor products such as cycling and marine sports reached about KRW 17.6 billion over the same period, up 42.8%.

Industrial products grew 79.3% year on year in the first quarter of 2026 and reached about KRW 3.1 billion in the second quarter, up roughly 28%.

The industrial line is built on mesh communication, and its work group communication (WGC) solution supports worker-to-worker communication in noisy sites; initial supply to a data center of a global cloud service provider has been confirmed.

The new business is the Drone Caddie Rover, an autonomous golf caddy robot that the company said has completed stability testing at domestic golf courses.

The company is diversifying sales channels, including online channels such as Amazon, and Hana Securities said in a May 2026 report that 80% of sales come from Europe and the United States.

NH Investment & Securities estimated in a February 2026 report that the company holds about a 60% share of the global motorcycle team communication market; this is a brokerage estimate, not a figure the company has disclosed.

The structure combines consumer-oriented motorcycle products with an industrial B2B business, and the pace of that shift is measured through industrial supply results.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, 2025 revenue was KRW 179.3 billion, up 7.0% from the prior year, and the company described it as its highest annual revenue. Operating profit for the same year was KRW 16.3 billion, down 24.4%, and the operating margin fell from 12.9% in 2024 to 9.1% in 2025.

The company and securities reports cited hiring for new businesses (466 employees at the end of 2025, up 118 from a year earlier), listing-related costs and stronger global marketing as reasons for the decline.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 12.0% from KRW 19 billion in 2024 to KRW 16.7 billion in 2025, while net profit including non-controlling interests was reported as KRW 19 billion in 2024 and KRW 16.7 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow dropped from KRW 29.4 billion to KRW 14 billion, the debt ratio fell from 26.5% to 16.7%, and total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 176.2 billion.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, revenue was KRW 34 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 3.16 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 657 million.

First-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 43.8 billion, slightly below the prior year as motorcycle demand slowed, while operating profit of KRW 3.85 billion turned profitable from the prior quarter, with a gross margin of 47.4%.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 75.5 billion and operating profit KRW 18 billion, up 367.9% from the prior quarter, with net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 14.8 billion.

The company said second-quarter gross margin was about 51% and operating margin about 24%, both described as the highest for any quarter.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 52.2 billion and operating profit KRW 4.87 billion, down 73.0% from the prior quarter, with net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.23 billion, down 91.7%. The company said third-quarter selling and administrative expenses rose 21.6% year on year. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 19.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The company treats the third quarter as the traditional seasonal peak and said seasonal demand recovered after March.

In the first quarter of 2026, the company explained, weather extremes in Europe and North America and the Middle East conflict weighed on consumer sentiment in major markets, and motorcycle revenue slowed temporarily.

Hana Securities said in a July 2026 report that a large market is opening up under the government's three mega projects, which focus on physical AI.

The same brokerage said in a May 2026 report that robots must communicate even where 5G or 6G networks are absent, and that Sena's mesh network, with its own relay function, could serve as an alternative.

Industrial products are still small, with second-quarter sales of about KRW 3.1 billion against total revenue of KRW 75.5 billion, so the pace at which that share grows is a gauge of differentiation within the sector.

By market capitalization, the gap with large component makers in the same electronics and components sector, such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek, is wide. Hana Securities also assessed that the company could become a core player in robot-to-robot swarm communication in the physical AI era.

The WGC market includes established wireless communication equipment suppliers, so how quickly new orders show up in reported results is tracked through quarterly disclosures.

06

Outlook

In its July 2026 second-quarter results release, the company said it is working through a U.S. tariff refund process, and that if the refund is reflected in second-half results, profit could rise further.

In its October 2026 third-quarter release, the company said it will start supplying its work group communication solution to a data center from the fourth quarter and expand its application to manufacturing facilities and infrastructure construction sites.

The Drone Caddie Rover robotic golf caddy is planned for initial mass production of 1,000 units in Q1 2027, with overseas supply from Q2.

In its 2025 results release, the company aimed for continued revenue growth in 2026 and said it would convert its market position into profit in 2027, expanding earnings substantially.

NH Investment & Securities projected in a February 2026 report revenue of KRW 212.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 21.4 billion for 2026; these are estimates as of February.

The company began a KRW 5 billion share buyback in early April 2026, reported about 45% progress at the time of its first-quarter release, and said it plans to cancel all repurchased shares after the trust contract expires.

Cumulative operating profit in 2026 has recovered, but quarterly swings are large, so the speed of selling and administrative expense growth and the pace of industrial revenue growth are the next checkpoints in post-fourth-quarter results.

The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, which will disclose fourth-quarter results and whether the tariff refund is reflected.

08

Bull factors

Record second-quarter results

The company reported second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 75.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 18 billion, with a gross margin of about 51%, which it described as the highest quarterly level.

The operating margin was about 24% in the same quarter, and the company said profit grew even after absorbing higher hiring and marketing costs. This strength rests on figures the company itself released.

Third-quarter profit then fell sharply, so the durability of this level is a point to check in upcoming quarterly results.

Industrial WGC supply begins

The company said industrial product revenue rose 79.3% year on year in the first quarter of 2026. Initial WGC supply to a data center of a global cloud service provider has been confirmed and starts in the fourth quarter.

Built on mesh communication, the solution supports worker communication in noisy sites, and the company plans to extend it to manufacturing facilities and infrastructure construction sites.

Robotics plans and broker view

The company plans to begin initial mass production of 1,000 units of the Drone Caddie Rover autonomous golf caddy in Q1 2027 and to supply overseas markets from Q2.

In a July 2026 report, Hana Securities kept its Buy rating and 8 million won target price, citing demand for robot-to-robot communication as physical AI spreads. This is the brokerage's view, not a result the company has confirmed.

09

Bear factors

Concentration in motorcycle products

Motorcycle product revenue of about KRW 64.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026 accounts for most of the quarter's total revenue of KRW 75.5 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026, weather extremes in Europe and North America and the Middle East conflict weighed on consumer sentiment, and motorcycle revenue slowed temporarily. Demand swings in the core product therefore move total results considerably.

Cost growth and volatile quarterly profit

Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 16.3 billion, down 24.4%, and the fourth quarter of 2025 recorded an operating loss of KRW 3.16 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.87 billion fell 73.0% from the prior quarter, and the company said selling and administrative expenses rose 21.6% year on year in that quarter. When hiring and marketing investment will translate into profit is not yet clear.

New businesses still at an early stage

Robotics businesses, including the robotic caddy and autonomous mobile robots, center on production plans for 2027, so their contribution to results has yet to begin in earnest. Industrial WGC revenue is growing quickly but was only about KRW 3.1 billion in the second quarter, small relative to total revenue.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 29.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 14 billion in 2025, so cash generation is also an open issue.

10

Risk factors

Demand and consumer environment

The core motorcycle products see quarterly revenue move with consumer sentiment in Europe and the United States. The company cited external shocks, including weather extremes and the Middle East conflict, for the first-quarter 2026 slowdown.

Cumulative revenue rose through the third quarter, but the strength of seasonal peak demand could determine fourth-quarter results.

Tariffs and currency exposure

Because overseas sales are a large share of revenue, currency movements can directly affect profit and loss. The company said it is working through a U.S. tariff refund process, but the timing and size of any refund in quarterly results will show up in later disclosures. Changes in tariff policy could affect export pricing and demand.

Cost structure and earnings volatility

The company said headcount was 466 at the end of 2025, up 118 from a year earlier, and it added 109 people year on year in the first quarter of 2026. Because investment costs for new businesses hit earnings first, quarterly operating margin swings can be wide. Share buybacks and cancellations are a shareholder return policy, but they do not offset earnings volatility.

Overall view

Sena Technologies generated KRW 179.3 billion in 2025 revenue from its motorcycle team communication core, but operating profit fell to KRW 16.3 billion.

In 2026, second-quarter revenue of KRW 75.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 18 billion were followed by third-quarter revenue of KRW 52.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.87 billion, a sharp change in the quarterly pattern.

The company describes its cumulative third-quarter 2026 results as record levels, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 19.2 billion.

Bullish factors include the confirmed data center WGC supply, the robotic caddy production plan and the improving gross margin, and Hana Securities maintained its positive target price stance on the strength of the physical AI transition.

Bearish factors include dependence on motorcycle products, quarterly earnings swings from higher selling and administrative costs, and the early stage of new business contributions.

Which way the balance tips will depend on how the industrial revenue share and the selling and administrative expense ratio move in upcoming quarters. The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, which will show whether fourth-quarter WGC supply and the tariff refund are reflected.
