02

Business structure

MedPacto was founded in 2013 as a spin-off from the drug discovery unit of Terragenetex and focuses on developing anticancer drugs.

Its lead pipeline is Vactosertib (development code TEW-7197), an oral TGF-β receptor (ALK5) inhibitor that the company has pursued in combination strategies to raise the effect of existing therapies in tumors that respond poorly to immuno-oncology drugs.

The company has recently focused on rare cancers such as osteosarcoma, and in the Phase 1 osteosarcoma trial of Vactosertib, an objective response rate (ORR) of 36.4% was reported among 11 patients with relapsed or refractory disease. The second axis is next-generation pipeline.

MP010 is an Fc bifunctional fusion protein immuno-oncology candidate that targets EDB-FN and TGF-β at the same time, while MP2021 is a bone disease candidate aimed at the novel target TM4SF19 as a first-in-class molecule.

MP2021 was submitted to Australia's human research ethics committee for a Phase 1 trial in July 2026, and reports followed that it had received approval.

Vactosertib was mostly tested in combination with immuno-oncology drugs such as Keytruda and Imfinzi, and critics say those designs did not establish monotherapy efficacy and did not lead to licensing deals.

Revenue comes from pharmaceutical distribution, genomic analysis and health functional food businesses, and the company said in its 2025 results announcement that this revenue allowed it to meet listing maintenance requirements.

The company is a drug developer, but its revenue base relies on distribution and food businesses separate from its clinical development work.

05

Industry analysis

TGF-β signaling inhibitors have been studied as a way to overcome resistance to immuno-oncology drugs, yet an April 2026 report noted that this is a path global big pharma companies have abandoned.

Vactosertib has been tested mostly in combination with immuno-oncology drugs such as Keytruda and Imfinzi, and a August 2025 analysis by Mirae Asset Securities pointed to combination designs that were not used to establish monotherapy efficacy.

Osteosarcoma is a rare cancer with limited treatment options, and Vactosertib holds FDA Fast Track designation, which raises the possibility of early approval based on Phase 2 data. Agenus's AGEN-1721 has been cited as a competing pipeline for MP010, and as of a 2025 report it was also at the preclinical stage.

Competition around PD-1xVEGF bispecific antibodies is intensifying, and Mirae Asset Securities noted in August 2025 that adding TGF-β to such combinations could be a point of interest. MP2021 is the second candidate after Vactosertib to reach clinical trials at MedPacto.

Compared with large listed peers in the sector such as Samsung Biologics and Celltrion, MedPacto has a smaller revenue base and a business centered on clinical development. As of a July 2026 report, Vactosertib had not yet produced a licensing deal, and calls to diversify the later-stage pipeline have grown.

As candidates move from preclinical work into the clinic, licensing terms with global pharmaceutical companies will shape how the business is commercialized.

06

Outlook

An FDA-approved change in October 2026 added a 200mg twice-daily mid-dose arm to the Vactosertib osteosarcoma Phase 2 trial, and Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety approved the same change in September 2026.

The trial is running in Korea and the United States at the same time, with the aim of comparing treatment response across doses to identify the best dose. In February 2026, the company said initial Phase 2 data could be seen as early as the first half of 2026.

The company has described the 36.4% objective response rate in the Phase 1 osteosarcoma trial as more than three times that of existing therapies.

For MP010, the company said its evaluation in three pancreatic cancer animal models showed cure rates of 75.0% to 87.5% and long-term survival of 87.5%, but these are animal results, and efficacy and safety in humans must be confirmed in clinical trials.

Timing for the MP010 IND filing has moved from the late 2026 target stated in July 2026 to early 2027 in a November 2026 company statement. MP010 was presented to global pharmaceutical companies at BIO Japan in October 2026 and BIO-Europe in November 2026, and the commercialization approach has not yet been decided.

For MP2021, the company plans to confirm safety and tolerability in healthy adults in its Australian Phase 1 trial and then pursue early licensing.

MedPacto is in the process of advancing its pipeline into clinical stages with MP010 and MP2021, and whether licensing agreements are signed will be a key marker of its next business results.

Bull factors

Osteosarcoma data and dose expansion

In the Phase 1 osteosarcoma trial of Vactosertib, the objective response rate among 11 patients with relapsed or refractory disease was reported at 36.4%.

The program holds FDA Fast Track designation, and an October 2026 FDA-approved change added a mid-dose arm to the Phase 2 design for comparing dose-specific responses. The company said the mid-dose group showed comparatively better response in Phase 1.

Next-generation pipeline reaching the clinic

MP2021 was reported in July 2026 to have received approval for a Phase 1 trial in Australia, and it targets TM4SF19 as a first-in-class molecule.

MP010 is a bifunctional fusion protein that targets EDB-FN and TGF-β at the same time, and its preclinical results were selected as a cover paper in the November issue of Cancer Cell. Both candidates are approaching clinical entry, which moves the pipeline to a more advanced stage.

Financial capacity and listing requirements

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 46 billion, with KRW 46 billion attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 2.68 billion. The debt ratio fell to 5.8% in 2025 from 233.2% in 2022.

The company said 2025 revenue of KRW 3.9 billion above the KRW 3 billion level cited for listing maintenance addressed concerns about administrative issue designation.

Risk factors

Clinical outcome risk

Initial Phase 2 data for Vactosertib in osteosarcoma were expected by the company in the first half of 2026, and the timing and results of disclosure could change how the program is judged.

MP010 and MP2021 have not yet been tested in humans for safety and efficacy, since they are at or near the start of clinical development. The gap between animal data and clinical results remains a possibility to monitor.

Trading flow and share price volatility risk

In late July 2026 the company said recent sharp share price moves reflected temporary trading flows from concentrated trades in a small number of accounts. On November 11, 2026 the share price rose to the daily limit after reports on MP010 preclinical results.

Share price movement has repeatedly responded to events and trading flows more than to reported business results.

Financial and listing requirement risk

Revenue of KRW 3.9 billion in 2025 exceeded the KRW 3 billion level cited for listing maintenance, but because the revenue base depends on distribution and food businesses, whether this continues must be confirmed in the next regular report.

Quarterly revenue has declined over three consecutive quarters and operating losses persist. Even with total equity of KRW 46 billion, continued operating cash outflows could increase the need for additional funding.
