02

Business structure

Shinhan Alpha REIT is a externally managed real estate investment company established in December 2017 and listed on the KOSPI market in August 2018. It holds 12 office buildings in Seoul and Pangyo as investment assets, and its asset management is handled by Shinhan REIT Management.

Revenue comes entirely from real estate leasing, so a single business line means rent differences across individual assets drive the results. Key assets include the Pangyo office asset, the Yongsan office asset, Shinhan L Tower in Euljiro, and City Square.

According to a January 2026 report, Shinhan L Tower, City Square and the Yongsan office asset are partly held as stakes, while the rest are wholly owned. Because the assets cluster in Seoul's central business district (CBD) and the Pangyo business area, the REIT has high CBD exposure.

As of January 6, 2026, the largest shareholder, an asset manager, held 13.93% after the stake rose by 0.1 percentage point. Industry observers view the financing capacity and affiliate synergies of Shinhan Financial Group as a positive for dividend stability and growth strategy.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on regular filings, revenue rose from KRW 47.9 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2023 to KRW 58.3 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2024 (up 21.8% year on year), KRW 82.9 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2025 (up 42.1%), and KRW 90.1 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2026 (up 8.7%).

Revenue has grown for three consecutive years from the fiscal year ended September 2024 through the fiscal year ended September 2026, and the fiscal year ended September 2026 is the largest year in the period from 2023 to 2026.

Operating profit was KRW 25.1 billion, KRW 33.8 billion (up 34.4%), KRW 51.1 billion (up 51.2%) and KRW 56.1 billion (up 9.8%). The operating margin moved from 52.5% to 57.9%, 61.7% and 62.3%, so profit grew faster than revenue for most of the period.

By contrast, profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 69.5 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2023 to KRW 4.65 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2024 (down 93.3%).

It recovered to KRW 11.3 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2025 (up 143.1%), then fell to KRW 5.72 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2026 (down 49.4%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 10.1 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2026, and the difference from the controlling-shareholder figure reflects non-controlling interests.

Operating cash flow was KRW 6.83 billion, KRW 11.4 billion, KRW 22.4 billion and KRW 22.3 billion across the four fiscal years, staying at a higher level after the fiscal year ended September 2025. The debt ratio rose from 201.6% to 299.7%, then eased to 286.4% before reaching 296.1%.

A January 2026 report said refinancing in 2025 cut annual interest costs by roughly KRW 4.65 billion. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 through third quarter of 2026) totals KRW 5.72 billion. On October 26, 2026, a disclosure of a change of 30% or more in revenue or profit structure was filed.

05

Industry analysis

NH Investment & Securities views new supply of CBD office space as limited through 2028, and it expects Shinhan Alpha REIT, with high CBD exposure, to keep the upper hand on rent negotiations. At the same time, the same brokerage said the risk-free rate was raised from 3.0% to 3.4%, which lifted the cost of equity.

The REIT's credit rating is A-, and one notch of upgrade would bring a rate cut of about 50 basis points, according to an analysis.

Key factors in REIT credit ratings include assets under management, anchor tenants and loan-to-value, and the absence of an anchor tenant is a point the REIT needs to offset through asset growth and debt management.

On dividends, the REIT Act allows excess distributions up to 100% of the current year's depreciation, so payouts can exceed rental income. A May 2026 report cited total acquisition cost of KRW 2.9559 trillion and a market value of KRW 3.8693 trillion based on a recent appraisal. Asset value is a separate measure from distributable amounts.

06

Outlook

According to press reports, the REIT plans to actively improve lease terms during the period when about 59% of its leases expire between 2026 and 2028, aiming to lift profitability.

Most leases at the Pangyo office asset, roughly 21,000 pyeong, expire in 2028, and an article said rental income is expected to improve significantly around that time.

The REIT plans to use a Shinhan blind fund set up by Shinhan Financial Group to lower its funding burden and to secure high-quality assets from the group's development pipeline. Reports said one to two new asset acquisitions are under review and in progress for 2026, focused on office assets in Seoul and Pangyo.

Co-investors are also expected to be brought in to reduce the burden of a single investment structure. On financing costs, the REIT plans to refinance early-repayable loans at lower rates before maturity.

On asset sales, NH Investment & Securities assumed a sale price of KRW 243.3 billion for the Seocho office building and noted a possible special dividend tied to it. A credit rating upgrade is cited as a key step for improving the funding structure through public bond issuance.

08

Bull factors

Rising operating margin

The operating margin rose from 52.5% in the fiscal year ended September 2023 to 62.3% in the fiscal year ended September 2026. Revenue growth slowed to 8.7% in the fiscal year ended September 2026, but operating profit rose 9.8%, so profit growth outpaced revenue growth. The margin gain came in a single leasing business where cost increases are limited.

Improving renewal terms

NH Investment & Securities expects the REIT to keep its rent-negotiation advantage because new CBD office supply is limited through 2028. The effect of better renewal terms can build as more space is re-leased.

Special dividend from asset sales and group funding

NH Investment & Securities assumed a sale price of KRW 243.3 billion for the Seocho office building and focused on a possible special dividend. A Shinhan Financial Group blind fund and access to the group's development pipeline are being considered as ways to reduce funding costs. Reports also said a 2025 refinancing cut annual interest costs by about KRW 4.65 billion.

09

Bear factors

Sharp decline in profit attributable to controlling shareholders

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 93.3% in the fiscal year ended September 2024 to KRW 4.65 billion, and fell 49.4% to KRW 5.72 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2026. Operating profit grew over the same period, which points to a gap below the operating line.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 10.1 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2026, and the controlling-shareholder share differs from it.

High debt load

The ratio rose to 299.7% in the fiscal year ended September 2024, eased to 286.4% and then rose back to 296.1%, so there is no clear improving trend. With a high debt share, interest rate changes feed directly into interest costs and dividend capacity.

Low occupancy at the Yongsan office

A May 2026 report said the average occupancy of the buildings held was 96.7% at the end of 2025, but the Yongsan office asset had the lowest rate at 77.1%. Even with a high average, vacancy at one asset can add swings to rental income. The pace of recovery at the Yongsan asset will matter alongside the overall rent improvement.

Risk factors

Interest rates and funding

NH Investment & Securities explained that the risk-free rate rose from 3.0% to 3.4%, lifting the cost of equity. The REIT is rated A-, and a one-notch upgrade would produce a rate cut of about 50 basis points. The rating path and the rate environment are the variables that shape borrowing costs and dividend capacity.

Lease expiries and renewals

About 59% of the portfolio's floor area reaches lease expiry between 2026 and 2028. Most leases at the Pangyo office asset, about 21,000 pyeong, end in 2028. If renewal terms fall short of expectations, the path to higher rental income could be delayed.

Execution of asset sales and acquisitions

Whether the Seocho office sale and the special dividend proceed as planned is a key variable. The sale price and dividend size are estimates by NH Investment & Securities based on an assumed price per pyeong.

Whether one to two new asset acquisitions in 2026 are completed will also affect asset scale and the dividend base.

12

Overall view

Shinhan Alpha REIT's operating profit grew from KRW 25.1 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2023 to KRW 56.1 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2026, and its operating margin reached 62.3%.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders, however, fell 49.4% year on year to KRW 5.72 billion in the fiscal year ended September 2026, so the earnings path is uneven. The debt ratio of 296.1% is high, with total liabilities of KRW 2.32 trillion.

On the positive side are a 24% improvement in effective net rent on renewals through the third quarter of 2026, limited new CBD office supply in Seoul, and funding support from Shinhan Financial Group.

On the other side, about 59% of leased floor area expires between 2026 and 2028, the Yongsan office asset has low occupancy, and a higher cost of equity from rising rates remains unresolved.

On dividends, a special dividend linked to the Seocho office sale appears in NH Investment & Securities' analysis, but the timing and size are not settled. On valuation, the price-to-book ratio sits slightly above book value, and earnings-based multiples swing widely.

The next points to track are the occupancy trend in monthly reports, the renewal outcome at the Pangyo office asset in 2028, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders and the debt ratio in the next regular filing.
