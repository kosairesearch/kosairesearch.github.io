02

Business structure

LX International is organized into three segments: Resources, Trading and New Growth, and Logistics. Based on its FY2023 annual report, segment shares of sales were 7.4% for Resources, 45.8% for Trading and New Growth, and 46.8% for Logistics.

Resources centers on mining and palm operations, including a 60% stake in the GAM coal mine in Indonesia with rights to sell all output, and an investment in the Shenzhen coal mine in China.

Trading and New Growth covers coal, palm oil, methanol and other petrochemical products, and steel trade, and is expanding into next-generation IT components.

Logistics consists of freight forwarding and contract logistics, which covers warehouse operation and inland transport management; logistics sales were about KRW 6.79 trillion in 2023.

Samsung Securities projected 2026 sales shares of 15.3% for coal and 45.5% for logistics, using its own classification that differs from the company's segment reporting. The same estimate put the two businesses at a combined 63.7% of operating profit.

Against POSCO International and Samsung C&T, which have larger steel trading exposure, LX International's mix is weighted toward coal and logistics.

A 2024 report noted that steel demand slowdown reduced those two peers' trading profits in the second quarter, while LX International rose slightly on LCD panel and methanol trading.

04

Earnings analysis

On a consolidated basis, annual sales peaked at KRW 18.8 trillion in 2022 and were lowest at KRW 14.5 trillion in 2023, down 22.6% year on year. Sales then rose 14.6% to KRW 16.6 trillion in 2024 and 0.4% to KRW 16.7 trillion in 2025, the two consecutive years of growth.

Operating profit was highest in 2022 at KRW 965.5 billion and lowest in 2025 at KRW 292.2 billion. In 2022 the company described the year as its best since its founding, citing coal price increases, higher freight rates and a stronger exchange rate (2023 report).

Operating profit fell 55.1% to KRW 433.1 billion in 2023, and the operating margin slipped from 5.1% to 3.0%. In 2024, operating profit recovered to KRW 489.2 billion, up 12.9%, and parent-shareholder net income rose 50.1% to KRW 175.7 billion.

In 2025, operating profit fell 40.3% to KRW 292.2 billion, the margin dropped to 1.7%, and parent-shareholder net income fell 19.3% to KRW 141.8 billion.

Quarterly profit went from KRW 64.8 billion in 3Q 2025 to KRW 55.5 billion in 4Q 2025, a 14.4% decline, before rebounding to KRW 108.9 billion in 1Q 2026, up 96.2% quarter on quarter.

In 4Q 2025 the company posted a parent-shareholder net loss of KRW 53.4 billion, which turned into a profit of KRW 62.3 billion in 1Q 2026. Q3 2026 sales were KRW 5.19 trillion, operating profit KRW 143.1 billion and parent-shareholder net income KRW 76.6 billion, up 79.8% year on year.

Provisional figures announced on November 4, 2026 put January-to-September cumulative operating profit at KRW 369.8 billion, above full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 292.2 billion.

The gains are attributed to a 61.9% rise in the ICI4 Indonesian coal index, which lifted sales prices at the GAM mine and trading profits, and to a more than doubled SCFI freight index. Parent-shareholder net income over the latest four quarters totals KRW 153.7 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Coal is the central variable behind the company's earnings. The ICI4 index for Indonesian coal rose 61.9% year on year in Q3 2026, which feeds directly into sales prices at the GAM mine and into resource trading profits.

Freight rates moved in the same direction: SCFI more than doubled year on year, but freight can shift with global cargo volumes, vessel supply and route-specific changes.

Because of this, Q3 2026 results were read as showing both the competitiveness of the resource and logistics businesses and their sensitivity to external market conditions. Coverage from November 2026 also flagged diversifying the business to reduce reliance on market cycles as a task ahead.

Within the general trading sector, the company's portfolio differs from POSCO International and Samsung C&T, which carry larger steel trading exposure. It has less exposure to steel price cycles and more to coal and freight cycles.

06

Outlook

On November 4, 2026 the company said it would raise profitability in its existing thermal coal and nickel businesses and widen its mineral portfolio into bauxite and copper.

It plans to gradually expand palm operations on the back of stable earnings and to look for new opportunities in biomass, steel, renewable energy and food.

Samsung Securities, in an October 2026 report, projected 2026 consolidated sales of KRW 19.4 trillion and operating profit of KRW 495 billion, equal to 16.2% and 69.5% growth over 2025.

Set against provisional January-to-September operating profit of KRW 369.8 billion, reaching that estimate would require about KRW 125.2 billion in 4Q 2026 operating profit, slightly above the nine-month quarterly average of about KRW 123.3 billion.

The estimate assumes coal prices and freight rates hold their current levels, so it would change if either index reverses. Items to watch are whether 4Q 2026 provisional operating profit holds near the Q3 level and whether specific investment disclosures for new mineral projects follow.

08

Bull factors

Direct exposure to rising coal prices

The company holds a 60% stake in the GAM coal mine and the right to sell all its output, so international coal prices pass through to resource profitability. The ICI4 index rose 61.9% in Q3 2026, lifting GAM sales prices and resource trading profits. If coal prices stay at elevated levels, they provide a base for resource-segment earnings.

Freight rates and volumes improving together

SCFI more than doubled year on year and cargo volumes increased, improving logistics results. Samsung Securities' 2026 estimate put logistics at 46.2% of operating profit (October 2026 report). With logistics anchoring part of earnings, it can partly offset swings in coal prices.

Consecutive quarterly profit recovery

Operating profit has risen for three consecutive quarters since the 4Q 2025 low, running from 1Q to 3Q 2026. Parent-shareholder net income also rose in both 2Q and 3Q 2026 after turning positive in 1Q. The Q3 2026 figure of KRW 76.6 billion was up 79.8% year on year.

09

Bear factors

Earnings structure highly dependent on market cycles

Coal prices and freight rates are cited as the main drivers of the recovery, so a reversal in either could reduce profit quickly. Freight can move in either direction depending on global cargo volumes and vessel supply. Coverage from November 2026 pointed to diversification as a task for lowering dependence on market conditions.

The 2025 profit decline and 4Q loss

Operating profit fell 40.3% to KRW 292.2 billion in 2025, and the operating margin dropped to 1.7%. In 4Q 2025 the company swung to a parent-shareholder net loss of KRW 53.4 billion. The 2026 quarterly recovery started from that low, so its durability needs to be tested over more quarters.

Financial leverage burden

Total liabilities were KRW 5.79 trillion at end-2025, larger than total equity of KRW 3.54 trillion, and the debt-to-equity ratio was 163.8%. The ratio fell from 169.0% in 2024 but remains above 154.2% in 2022. If resource investment expands further, borrowing costs and repayment pressure could rise.

10

Risk factors

Commodity price risk

Swings in the ICI4 coal index pass directly into GAM mine sales prices and resource trading profits. A reversal in coal prices could quickly reduce resource-segment earnings. A large part of the Q3 2026 improvement rested on higher coal prices, which shows the degree of exposure.

Freight and global trade risk

Freight rates depend on global cargo volumes, vessel supply and route-specific conditions. A fall in rates or a drop in volumes would weaken logistics profitability. Because logistics accounts for a large share of group operating profit, the impact would be significant.

Execution risk in new mineral investments

The company has said it will widen its portfolio into bauxite, copper and nickell. New assets typically require long development periods and face price exposure, so returns on investment may be delayed. Whether the expansion strains the balance sheet should be examined alongside the investment pace.

12

Overall view

LX International's operating profit fell to KRW 292.2 billion in 2025 and its margin dropped to 1.7%, and quarterly profit has since recovered on coal and logistics gains in 2026.

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 143.1 billion and provisional January-to-September cumulative operating profit of KRW 369.8 billion both exceed full-year 2025 operating profit.

The gains rest on higher Indonesian coal prices, higher freight rates and larger trading volumes, and the company has set profitability improvement in thermal coal and nickel and expansion into copper and bauxite as medium-term tasks.

Samsung Securities' 2026 operating profit estimate of KRW 495 billion depends on profits staying at high levels in 4Q as well.

Strengths are the resource ownership structure, logistics volumes and consecutive quarterly profit gains, while weaknesses are dependence on market cycles, a short recovery record since the 2025 low and a debt-to-equity ratio of 163.8%.

With the price-to-book ratio well below 1x and earnings moving sharply with market conditions, the next items to check are provisional 4Q 2026 results and the operating margin in the FY2026 annual report.
