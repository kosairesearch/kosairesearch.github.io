02

Business structure

OA was founded in 2014 as KTOCI, changed its name to OA in 2018, and listed on KOSDAQ in 2025 through a merger with Mirae Asset Vision Special Purpose Acquisition Company 2.

Its core business is small home appliances such as electric toothbrushes, oral irrigators, humidifiers and massage devices aimed at one- and two-person households and compact living, sold under its own brands OA and Voar.

Its health-food unit sells protein products, arginine and high-protein drinks under the Samdaeobaek brand, with products aimed at needs such as post-workout recovery, weight management and meal replacement.

The company plans and distributes about 700 product lines and cites its direct-to-consumer planning capability, which analyzes consumption data from major online platforms to launch trend-matched products quickly, as its core competitive edge.

Sales mix, which was initially concentrated in seasonal appliances, has diversified into living and kitchen appliances. On April 22, 2026 the board resolved to spin off the health-functional-food business (Samdaeobaek) into a newly established company. The schedule set July 1, 2026 as the spin-off date.

According to the spin-off disclosure, the surviving company will handle electronics wholesale and retail, the new company will handle health-functional-food wholesale and retail, and the latest fiscal-year sales were KRW 83.1 billion for the surviving business and KRW 20.1 billion for the new one.

These are reference figures on the spin-off disclosure basis and should be read alongside the consolidated revenue of KRW 103.3 billion in the periodic report, with the basis difference noted.

On logistics, the company bought its Pohang logistics center in 2021 to improve inventory management and logistics efficiency, and is testing a physical-AI packaging automation system combining in-house vision recognition and control software at the same site.

Among listed peers, small appliances overlap with names such as Coway and Cuckoo Homesys, and health-food distribution touches retail-channel companies, while OA positions its combination of product planning and online direct sales in one structure as its differentiator.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue was KRW 96.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 103.3 billion in 2025, up 6.6% year on year. Over the same period operating profit fell 13.7%, from KRW 9.46 billion to KRW 8.17 billion, and the operating margin declined from 9.8% to 7.9%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from KRW 6.9 billion in 2024 to a net loss of KRW 3.33 billion in 2025.

The company said listing-related costs for accounting, legal and IR were reflected and that operating profit declined slightly, while profitability of the core business excluding one-off costs had strengthened.

That is management's framing, and the periodic report figures alone do not separate the size of those one-off costs. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 3.67 billion in 2024 to KRW 6.63 billion in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 166.5% to 81.9%.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 42.9 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 35.2 billion.

On a quarterly basis, revenue moved from KRW 27.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 26.6 billion in the fourth quarter (down 2.7% quarter on quarter) and KRW 25.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 4.4%), then rose to KRW 27.5 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (up 8.0%) and KRW 29.8 billion in the third quarter (up 8.4%).

Operating profit moved the other way, from KRW 1.93 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.25 billion in the fourth quarter (down 35.3%) and KRW 1.06 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 15.5%).

The second quarter of 2026 recorded an operating loss of KRW 127 million, and the third quarter of 2026 returned to a small profit of KRW 109 million, down 94.3% year on year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 4.81 billion in the third quarter of 2025, a loss of KRW 2.56 billion in the fourth quarter, a profit of KRW 732 million in the first quarter of 2026, and losses of KRW 248 million and KRW 130 million in the second and third quarters of 2026.

The four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 add up to a net loss of KRW 2.2 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

Revenue has grown while margins compressed, so the key question is how the mix shift toward health food and fixed selling and administrative costs are affecting the operating margin.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for compact home appliances is built around category products matched to one- and two-person households and compact living.

The company says it is strengthening competitiveness around category-defining products such as electric toothbrushes, oral irrigators, humidifiers and massage devices suited to one- and two-person households and small-home trends.

The category is cyclical, with demand shifting with consumer sentiment and housing conditions, so revenue concentrated in one season carries higher volatility. Diversifying the sales mix away from seasonal appliances is therefore cited as a buffer against that volatility.

In health products, demand splits by purpose, such as post-workout recovery, weight management and meal replacement, and OA targets this with protein-focused products.

Among listed peers, appliance companies such as Coway and Cuckoo Homesys differ in brand reach and rental or sales channel structure, and OA's position rests on quickly launching its own planned products online.

Compared with large retail channels such as E-Mart and BGF retail, OA's mix leans more toward its own-brand direct sales. Overseas, the Hong Kong joint venture serves as a base for pushing into China, Europe and the United States, which would broaden a sales structure that has been concentrated domestically.

In logistics automation, the company is preparing to use AI robots with a robotics partner for picking and packing in its distribution centers, extending the business into logistics technology.

With operating margin lower on KRW 103.3 billion in 2025 revenue, company-specific cost structure appears more decisive for profit recovery than the cycle alone.

06

Outlook

In early 2026 the company set a plan to exceed KRW 130 billion in revenue in 2026 through expansion of the Samdaeobaek lineup, the launch of a new beauty brand, and full operation of overseas subsidiaries.

Revenue through the first three quarters of 2026 totals KRW 82.7 billion, so reaching the target would require about KRW 47.3 billion in the fourth quarter, far above the third quarter's KRW 29.8 billion.

Overseas, the company has secured a 60% stake in a Hong Kong joint venture and is using it as a base to expand distribution in China, Europe and the Americas. The acquisition amount was KRW 4.39 billion, and the disclosure set the planned acquisition date as April 30, 2026.

In logistics and AI, an extraordinary shareholders' meeting on November 26, 2026 will consider a name change and additional business purposes. The company acquired new common shares of logistics robot firm PyBrain AI Inc. to secure a 24.07% stake.

The company describes its direction as becoming an integrated distribution, logistics and AX (AI transformation) company. On funding, the CFO said the new businesses use existing distribution and logistics infrastructure, so the additional investment burden is not large.

On shareholder returns, the company cancelled treasury shares and paid quarterly dividends in March 2026. The spin-off disclosure said no further restructuring is confirmed within one year after the split.

The next checkpoints are whether annual revenue approaches the target and how the overseas and robotics businesses appear in periodic reports. The public disclosures that would support a quantitative outlook remain limited, so direction should be checked each time new filings appear.

Bull factors

Revenue up for two straight quarters

Revenue reached KRW 103.3 billion in 2025, up 6.6%, and rose for two straight quarters to KRW 27.5 billion in the second quarter and KRW 29.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026. Third-quarter revenue was up 8.9% year on year, so growth held on a year-on-year basis as well. The recovery since the first-quarter 2026 low of KRW 25.4 billion is the main bullish point.

Growing health-food mix

Plans to expand the Samdaeobaek lineup and launch a new beauty brand support diversification. Reducing reliance on seasonal appliances could buffer demand swings.

Improved balance sheet and cash generation

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 166.5% in 2024 to 81.9% in 2025, and total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 42.9 billion. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 3.67 billion in 2024 to KRW 6.63 billion in 2025. The company has used treasury share cancellation and quarterly dividends in 2026 as shareholder-return tools.

Bear factors

Operating margin still falling

The operating margin fell from 9.8% in 2024 to 7.9% in 2025. The second quarter of 2026 swung to an operating loss of KRW 127 million, and third-quarter operating profit of KRW 109 million was down 94.3% year on year. Revenue growth not reaching profit is the core bearish concern.

Cumulative net losses and no earnings-based valuation

The third quarter of 2026 still posted a net loss of KRW 130 million. Without positive earnings, earnings-based multiple comparisons are not possible until results recover.

Investment and execution load from new businesses

The Hong Kong joint venture stake cost KRW 4.39 billion, and the investment in the logistics robotics firm was KRW 650 million for a 24.07% stake. Reaching the KRW 130 billion target would need KRW 47.3 billion of revenue in the fourth quarter, a heavy execution load. Until new businesses convert to profit, cost burdens may appear first.

Risk factors

Target-achievement risk

Revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 totals KRW 82.7 billion, so the KRW 130 billion target requires KRW 47.3 billion in the fourth quarter. That is a large step up from the third quarter's KRW 29.8 billion. A shortfall would change both the business plan and how valuation is interpreted.

Business restructuring risk

The spin-off of the health-food unit, the planned name change and added robotics and automation purposes, and the overseas joint venture are advancing at the same time. Running several businesses at once could spread management resources. How spin-off basis sales relate to consolidated revenue in periodic reports is also a point to watch.

Small-cap liquidity and ownership concentration

The largest shareholder group holds 47.33% and the free-float ratio is 52.37%. A small cap with limited tradable shares can react sharply to changes in trading volume. Changes in the ownership structure should continue to be tracked through disclosures.

Overall view

OA grew revenue to KRW 103.3 billion in 2025, but operating profit fell to KRW 8.17 billion and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.33 billion.

Revenue has risen for two straight quarters since the second quarter of 2026, yet operating profit was only a KRW 127 million loss in the second quarter and KRW 109 million profit in the third. The last four quarters total a net loss of KRW 2.2 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

Bullish factors are the rising health-food share, a more diversified sales mix, and stronger equity and cash flow, while bearish factors are the margin decline, cumulative losses and the investment load from new businesses.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and whether profitability holds in the first-quarter 2027 report.
