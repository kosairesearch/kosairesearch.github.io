02

Business structure

TN entertainment is a KOSDAQ-listed company in broadcast program production and distribution, listed on June 14, 2011. It changed its name from Chorokbaem E&M in August 2023. The business has two pillars, entertainment and food and beverage (F&B).

A tally based on second-quarter 2026 revenue puts F&B at 57% and entertainment at 43%. The entertainment side centers on broadcast appearances by managed artists and digital content production, and YouTube content production and channel advertising agency work have been added as new revenue sources.

A company monitor comment covering the first nine months of 2025, however, cited fewer artist appearances and slower digital content revenue as reasons for the weak entertainment segment.

The F&B side runs the chicken franchise Fried Chamjal Haneun Jip alongside brands such as Sesangui Modun Achim and Sadaebu Jip Gotgan. Franchise stores reached 275 in the first half of 2025, but franchise sales kept falling after the Yeouido direct-store lease expired and restaurant competition intensified.

In 2023 the company exited its loss-making chemical business as part of a restructuring. The largest shareholder is Chorokbaem Media, with a 68.25% stake as of a November 10, 2026 filing.

Listed peers in the same industry include Hybe, SM Entertainment, JYP Entertainment, YG Entertainment and CJ ENM, but the heavy F&B weighting gives the company a revenue structure different from pure-play entertainment companies.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results peaked in 2023 and then turned down. Revenue rose from KRW 71.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 150.3 billion in 2023 (up 109.0% year on year), then fell to KRW 142.9 billion in 2024 (down 4.9%) and KRW 118.3 billion in 2025 (down 17.2%), declining for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 3.25 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.5 billion in 2023 (up 316.1%), then fell to KRW 9.7 billion in 2024 (down 28.3%) and KRW 3.35 billion in 2025 (down 65.5%). The operating margin moved from 9.0% in 2023 to 6.8% in 2024 and 2.8% in 2025.

The company said 2023 was the first year its annual revenue topped the KRW 150 billion range and operating profit topped the KRW 10 billion range.

However, press reports say the 2022 revenue of KRW 71.9 billion covered a six-month period from July to December 2022 after a change in fiscal year-end, so the 2023 growth rate is not a like-for-like comparison. Net results moved in a different direction from operating profit.

In 2023 the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 17.8 billion despite KRW 13.5 billion in operating profit. It returned to a profit of KRW 2.19 billion in 2024, then net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 85.5% to KRW 317 million in 2025.

Operating cash flow also declined from KRW 22.5 billion in 2023 and KRW 18.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 3.5 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the fourth quarter of 2025 turned operating profit positive at KRW 1.08 billion on KRW 29.1 billion in revenue, but the first quarter of 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 882 million on KRW 19.3 billion in revenue (down 33.6% from the prior quarter).

Second-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 20 billion, up 3.7%, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 526 million. In the third quarter revenue fell to KRW 16.5 billion (down 28.4% year on year) and the operating loss widened to KRW 853 million.

The same quarter turned net income attributable to controlling shareholders positive at KRW 8.23 billion.

The swing from a KRW 10.1 billion attributable loss in the second quarter to this profit is far larger than the change in operating results, so the non-operating items behind it are a point to verify in the footnotes of the periodic report.

Industry analysis

In a 2025 industry position assessment compiled by NICE Bizinfo, the company ranked first in revenue within its industry classification. The same assessment placed its stability in the top tier and its scale in the highest tier, while growth ranked in the bottom tier and profitability and activity sat in the middle.

Because this is a relative position within an industry classification, it should not be read as a market share or growth rate figure. Demand for the entertainment segment depends on broadcast appearances and digital content production volume.

On a cumulative basis through the third quarter of 2025, fewer artist appearances were cited as a cause of weakness. In its 2025 results disclosure, the company attributed the revenue decline to business cycle fluctuations and changes in the operating environment.

The F&B segment is directly exposed to intensifying competition in the restaurant industry, and it accounted for 57% of second-quarter 2026 revenue on the tally cited above.

Direct comparison with listed peers in the same industry, including Hybe, SM Entertainment, JYP Entertainment and YG Entertainment, is limited because their revenue mix differs.

Because the business is split between entertainment and restaurant franchising, a recovery in entertainment alone may not offset weakness in F&B, and the balance between the two segments will shape the overall earnings path.

Recovery in operating profitability remains a separate item to verify.

Quarterly Profit Turnaround

Operating profit of KRW 1.08 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 marked a turn to profit, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.23 billion in the third quarter of 2026 also turned positive. The third quarter turned positive compared with the same quarter a year earlier as well.

An operating loss of KRW 853 million remained in the same quarter, so whether profit continues at the operating level is a matter for upcoming quarterly results.

Larger Controlling Stake and Settled Dispute

The largest shareholder group reported buying 125,204 shares on the open market between October 22 and 29, 2026, and a further 123,074 shares in a November 10 filing, bringing its holding ratio to 68.25%.

The breach-of-trust dispute with former executives was closed by a settlement on September 22, 2026, and the company will receive KRW 9 billion in installments over 18 months. The controlling holder's ownership ratio is now higher.

The cumulative attributable net loss over the last four quarters is KRW 4.44 billion. The third-quarter profit is a single-quarter result and has not reversed that cumulative loss.

Exchange Market Surveillance Signals

Risk factors

Business structure and industry conditions

Both the entertainment and F&B segments are sensitive to external conditions. In its 2025 results disclosure, the company cited business cycle fluctuations and changes in the operating environment as reasons for lower revenue.

The entertainment segment depends on broadcast appearance volume, while F&B depends on franchise store sales, lease terms and competition. With third-quarter 2026 revenue down 28.4% year on year, the timing of recovery in either segment is not yet visible in the disclosed results.

Trading and float structure

The reverse split reduced the share count to 6,449,344, and the largest shareholder group holds 68.25%. With a small tradable share base, price volatility can be larger.

On July 29, 2026 the stock was designated a caution-warranted issue because of concentrated trading in a small number of accounts, and a notice of possible administrative-issue designation was disclosed as of September 21, 2026.

Governance and legal process

On August 4, 2026, co-CEO Yoon Dong-hyun resigned, leaving Kim Dong-jun as sole CEO, and Kim also serves as CEO of the largest shareholder.

The breach-of-trust matter involving three former executives was closed by a settlement on September 22, 2026, but the KRW 9 billion payment runs over 18 months, so the performance of the agreement remains open.

How the relationship with the controlling shareholder and the overlapping executive roles appear in future disclosures is worth monitoring.

Overall view

TN entertainment's results turned down from a peak in 2023, when annual revenue reached KRW 150.3 billion and operating profit reached KRW 13.5 billion, and both revenue and operating profit declined for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025.

Operating losses persisted for three straight quarters from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026, yet net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 8.23 billion in the third quarter.

On the financial side, the debt ratio fell to 33.1% at end-2025, and all KRW 67 billion of total equity belongs to controlling shareholders.

On the governance side, the company moved to a single CEO in August 2026, the largest shareholder group's stake rose to 68.25%, and the share count fell to 6,449,344 after the reverse split.

On the market side, a caution-warranted designation and a notice of possible administrative-issue designation both appeared in 2026.

Strengths include financial capacity, a quarterly return to profit and the settlement of the executive dispute, while weaknesses include falling revenue, a cumulative loss over the last four quarters and pressure in both business segments.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual business report, due by late March 2027, and the first-quarter 2027 report, which will show whether the gap between operating results and attributable net income narrows.
