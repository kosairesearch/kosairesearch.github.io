Report overview

Pond Group: Non-Fashion Units Gain Share of Profit

Pond Group is shifting from a fashion-licensing core toward cosmetics distribution and live commerce. Operating profit reached KRW 57.4 billion in 2025, and the second quarter of 2026 at KRW 21.7 billion was the largest of the last five quarters. Quarterly results remain seasonal and depend on acquisition effects, so the fourth-quarter peak season is the next checkpoint.

02

Business structure

Pond Group traces its roots to the fashion division spun off from a former parent in December 2023, and it listed on KOSDAQ in February 2024. The business runs in three segments: fashion, cosmetics and live commerce.

Third-quarter 2026 segment revenue was KRW 92.4 billion for fashion, KRW 16.2 billion for cosmetics and KRW 10 billion for live commerce. The fashion lineup includes Superdry, BBC Earth and Off-White, and a women's apparel company acquired in early July 2026 began contributing from the third quarter.

The cosmetics unit centers on a distribution subsidiary acquired in September 2024 and a distributor merged into the company. That subsidiary distributes K-beauty products to Costco in the United States, Sephora across Asia and Canada, while the merged distributor exports to roughly 40 countries.

The in-house skincare brand SELEVE has entered Chinese e-commerce and Turkey's Gratis retail channel. Live commerce runs through the Clickmate platform and accounted for 15% of company operating profit in the second quarter of 2026. The company said cosmetics reached 20% of group revenue in that quarter.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, revenue rose from KRW 2.78 billion in 2023 to KRW 368.7 billion in 2024, an increase of 13,154.5%, and reached KRW 485.6 billion in 2025, up 31.7%. Revenue increased for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

The 2023 base was small because the spin-off took place that year, which inflates the early growth rates. Operating profit went from KRW 668 million in 2023 to KRW 41.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 57.4 billion in 2025, also rising for two consecutive years.

The operating margin moved from 24.0% in 2023 to 11.2% in 2024 and 11.8% in 2025. As revenue scaled up, the margin settled in the low double digits while absolute profit grew.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 34.5 billion in 2025, up 37.1%, while net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 44.2 billion, so the two figures differ. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 71.19 million in 2023 to KRW 3.07 billion in 2024 and KRW 54.1 billion in 2025.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, revenue reached KRW 176.2 billion, up 94.1% from the prior quarter, and operating profit was KRW 20.1 billion; the company ties this period to winter apparel and year-end shopping.

Revenue was KRW 116.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026 with operating profit of KRW 17 billion, then KRW 149.2 billion and KRW 21.7 billion in the second quarter. Third-quarter revenue fell 20.2% from the prior quarter to KRW 119 billion, and operating profit fell 58.1% to KRW 9.09 billion.

The company attributed the quarter to a seasonal lull and currency effects from a strong won. The company also said revenue and operating profit have grown year on year in every quarter since 2025. Growth in the third quarter of 2026 was 31.1% for revenue and 28.9% for operating profit. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 41.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Pond Group's three businesses follow different seasonal patterns. Fashion sales concentrate in the fourth quarter, when winter apparel demand rises, while cosmetics sales build ahead of year-end shopping events such as Black Friday and Christmas.

The company expects live-commerce broadcast sales to grow in the fourth quarter as winter apparel gains share. Currency matters most for the overseas-weighted cosmetics unit: the company said K-beauty export distribution drove growth despite a strong won.

Overseas distribution runs through several channels in the United States, Asia and Canada, so channel expansion is the main growth lever. On the live-commerce side, Clickmate transaction volume exceeded KRW 7 billion in the first quarter of 2026, with a record KRW 25 billion month in March.

October 2026 posted another monthly record of KRW 25 billion. Brokerage commentary expects a record fourth quarter as apparel season and cosmetics distribution expansion overlap. That view is a market estimate, not company guidance.

Holding fashion licensing, cosmetics distribution and live commerce together is the company's distinguishing structure within the consumer sector.

06

Outlook

The company describes the fourth quarter as its peak season, when winter apparel sales and the global year-end shopping season overlap, and it aims for a record quarterly result through expanded sales across all units.

Newly consolidated subsidiaries have been contributing since the third quarter, and two affiliates that joined in May and June began reporting in full from the third quarter. An MCN live-commerce firm acquired recently is set to be fully reflected from the fourth quarter.

For the fall and winter season, fashion sales of Superdry, BBC Earth and Off-White are being expanded, and three French women's apparel brands were launched. The cosmetics unit plans to expand overseas sales around Black Friday and Christmas. SELEVE is broadening its distribution through Gratis in Turkey.

For live commerce, the 2026 transaction target is above KRW 300 billion, and a first-quarter report said that level was expected to be reachable. The cosmetics share had been targeted at 20% or more of revenue by 2026, and the second quarter of 2026 already reached that level.

The open questions are whether the fourth quarter becomes the record quarter brokers expect, and whether newly consolidated affiliates smooth quarterly swings.

08

Bull factors

Year-end peak season and new consolidations

The fourth quarter of 2026 is the company's peak season, when winter apparel and year-end shopping overlap, and management aims for a record quarter. Two affiliates contribute from the third quarter and an MCN firm from the fourth. Brokers are reported to expect a record fourth quarter.

Rising profit share from new businesses

The company said new cosmetics and live-commerce businesses contributed more than half of profit for the first time in the second quarter of 2026. Live commerce alone grew to 15% of operating profit.

The second-quarter operating profit of KRW 21.7 billion, the highest of the last five quarters, coincides with this shift.

Clickmate transaction growth

Cumulative Clickmate transaction volume through the third quarter of 2026 exceeded full-year 2025 volume. October 2026 set a monthly record of KRW 25 billion. A daily order record of KRW 2.3 billion was reported in November. Larger transaction volume may widen room for commission-type income.

09

Bear factors

Seasonal drop in the third quarter

Third-quarter 2026 revenue fell 20.2% from the prior quarter to KRW 119 billion, and operating profit fell 58.1% to KRW 9.09 billion. The company cited the seasonal lull and currency effects. Profit drops sharply in quarters without the fourth-quarter peak, which is the main bearish point.

Dependence on acquisition effects

Press reports attribute recent revenue and profit growth largely to an aggressive acquisition strategy. Several businesses were added in 2026, so integration and proof of performance are both needed. If acquisition effects fade, organic growth may become more visible.

Currency and overseas channel exposure

A strong won weighed on third-quarter results. Cosmetics growth relies heavily on overseas distribution, and SELEVE is expanding abroad through channel contracts. A high overseas sales share can amplify volatility from currency and channel factors.

10

Risk factors

Currency and overseas channels

The overseas-weighted cosmetics unit is sensitive to a strong won, which the company cited in the third quarter. Concentration in a few overseas channel partners could transmit partner changes directly into results.

Seasonality and inventory

Fashion and live commerce are concentrated in the fourth-quarter peak. If peak-season sales fall short, inventory pressure could swing quarterly profit. The 58.1% quarter-on-quarter drop in third-quarter operating profit shows the scale of that exposure.

Ownership concentration and float

According to the FnGuide company profile, the largest shareholder and related parties hold 76.29%, with the last change dated December 20, 2024. Concentrated ownership leaves a smaller tradable float, which can increase share-price volatility.

12

Overall view

Pond Group's reported results show revenue and operating profit rising for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025, with operating profit reaching KRW 57.4 billion and a margin of 11.8% in 2025.

The mix is moving from fashion toward cosmetics and live commerce, and the company said those two new businesses exceeded half of profit contribution in the second quarter of 2026.

The third quarter, however, saw revenue and operating profit decline from the prior quarter, which the company attributed to seasonality and currency. Much of the growth also rests on acquisition effects, which needs to be weighed alongside the organic trend.

Bullish factors include the fourth-quarter peak, newly consolidated affiliates and rising Clickmate volume. Bearish factors include quarterly seasonality, currency exposure, acquisition dependence and concentrated ownership. The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual business report.
