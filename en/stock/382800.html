Chip and solar orders lift sales; quarterly profit swings

02

Business structure

GnBS eco makes eco-friendly process equipment that treats harmful gases generated in semiconductor and solar manufacturing. Its core product is the scrubber, an air-pollution abatement system. In the first quarter of 2026, scrubber sales were KRW 27.6 billion, more than half of quarterly revenue of KRW 41.8 billion.

Semiconductor and solar sales were KRW 17.9 billion and KRW 14.8 billion in the same quarter, together about 78% of total revenue.

In the first half of 2026, semiconductor sales reached KRW 40.9 billion, up 35.6% year on year, while solar sales reached KRW 17.0 billion, more than 13 times the prior-year level of KRW 1.3 billion. By region, Korea accounted for 44% of first-half sales, China 31% and India 21%.

Exports were about 61% of first-quarter sales. The main semiconductor customer is SK hynix, while solar orders are concentrated among large Indian companies.

The company says it holds more than 95% of India's solar scrubber market and more than 80% of China's solar scrubber segment, though these are management statements. Overseas, its Chinese subsidiary in Wuxi and its US subsidiary handle local customer support, installation and maintenance.

A new business, the DeNOx system for nitrogen oxide treatment, is slated for full commercialization from 2027, according to the company. The core plasma scrubber is promoted for its higher treatment temperature, which widens the range of gases it can process.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales and profit moved in different directions. Sales rose from KRW 65.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 89.3 billion in 2023 (up 36.2% year on year), fell to KRW 70.8 billion in 2024 (down 20.8%), and recovered to KRW 91.9 billion in 2025 (up 29.9%), the largest year of 2022–2025.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 14.7 billion in 2022 only in the sense of the base year; the peak year was 2023 at KRW 18.3 billion (up 24.3%). It then fell to KRW 11.6 billion in 2024 (down 36.3%) and KRW 7.76 billion in 2025 (down 33.4%), two consecutive annual declines.

Operating margin slid from 22.4% in 2022 to 20.5% in 2023, 16.5% in 2024 and 8.4% in 2025. Controlling-shareholder net income rose to KRW 17.6 billion in 2023 (up 19.3%), then fell to KRW 12.5 billion in 2024 (down 29.0%) and KRW 6.32 billion in 2025 (down 49.5%).

Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed at the same levels in each year. In 2025, sales grew while profit shrank, and the solar business had virtually no sales that year, which may have weighed on profitability.

Quarterly sales were KRW 18.1 billion with operating profit of KRW 556 million in Q3 2025, then KRW 29.6 billion and KRW 4.52 billion in Q4 2025. Q1 2026 was the peak quarter, with sales of KRW 41.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 9.04 billion.

Q2 2026 posted sales of KRW 36.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.08 billion, and Q3 2026 posted sales of KRW 23.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.55 billion, a two-quarter decline.

Q3 2026 operating profit rose 538.7% year on year, but the controlling-shareholder result was a net loss of KRW 1.61 billion, a swing into loss from a year earlier. The company attributes the Q3 net loss to foreign-exchange valuation effects rather than weaker core operations.

Operating cash flow went from a KRW 3.62 billion outflow in 2023 to KRW 3.25 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.9 billion in 2025. At end-2025, total equity was KRW 117.8 billion, total liabilities were KRW 21.7 billion and the debt ratio was 18.4%. Controlling-shareholder net income for the most recent four quarters totaled KRW 17.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand in semiconductors depends mainly on capital spending. In March 2026, NH Investment & Securities said semiconductor segment sales would grow strongly on an industry recovery and resumed equipment investment, and expected record annual results in 2026.

The company says it is responding to scrubber demand tied to SK hynix fab expansion. In solar, rising capital spending in India and wider adoption of high-efficiency HJT solar cell equipment are lifting demand. The company says large US companies continue to invest in solar.

Government support and tighter ESG and environmental rules are also cited as drivers of demand for harmful-gas treatment equipment. Solar, however, can see orders halt when policy or overseas project schedules shift, as the 2024–2025 gap in Indian orders showed.

Sales therefore track semiconductor fab investment and Indian solar project timing directly. Market data sites classify the company under machinery and equipment and semiconductors rather than automotive, so the choice of peer group depends on which classification is used.

On competitive position, the company claims high shares in India and China solar scrubbers, while public competitor share data is limited.

06

Outlook

The company has set a 2027 sales target of KRW 200 billion or more, based on expanded supply volumes to SK hynix. It says its assembly-centered production structure allows output of KRW 300 billion to KRW 350 billion a year without a new factory.

In 2026 it hired more than 40 production and customer-support staff and is pursuing additional hiring. In Q4 2026, part of the Indian shipments originally scheduled for Q3 is expected to be recognized as sales.

Among brokerage estimates, Kiwoom Securities projected 2026 sales of KRW 150.3 billion, up 64% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 26.0 billion, up 236%, in an August 2026 report, and it put 2027 operating profit at KRW 35.4 billion. In the same report, Kiwoom did not present an investment rating or target price.

In solar, the potential beneficiary angle is the rising adoption of HJT equipment in India. The company says it has worked with China's M, a tier-one solar maker, since the early stages of HJT development, and reports indicate it is discussing HJT equipment adoption with India's R.

The DeNOx system is planned for commercialization in 2027. The points to verify in 2027 are how quickly chip-customer volumes flow into results, progress on the Indian HJT discussions, and the year-end exchange rate.

08

Bull factors

Chip-customer volumes and capacity

The company says scrubber supply to SK hynix has grown and a dedicated production line is now running. It says its assembly-centered structure allows output of KRW 300 billion to KRW 350 billion a year without a new factory. The 2027 sales target of KRW 200 billion or more rests on wider chip-customer volumes.

India solar recovery and HJT ties

After a gap in 2024–2025, Indian solar orders resumed, lifting first-half 2026 solar sales to KRW 17.0 billion, more than 13 times the prior-year level. Kiwoom Securities pointed to possible gains from wider HJT equipment adoption in India.

According to an NH Investment & Securities report, the company has standardized a system that pairs its scrubber with HJT mass-production equipment for China's M.

Evidence of profitability recovery

The company reported that its operating margin recovered from 8.4% in 2025 to 17.3% for the first three quarters of 2026. Controlling-shareholder net income for the latest four quarters totaled KRW 17.8 billion, well above the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 6.32 billion. Kiwoom Securities expects 2027 operating profit to rise to KRW 35.4 billion.

09

Bear factors

Consecutive quarterly profit declines

Operating profit peaked at KRW 9.04 billion in Q1 2026, then fell to KRW 5.08 billion in Q2 and KRW 3.55 billion in Q3, two straight quarterly declines. Q3 sales of KRW 23.7 billion were 35.5% below the prior quarter. The company says part of the Indian shipments originally scheduled for Q3 moved into Q4.

Net income sensitive to exchange rates

The company attributes the Q3 2026 controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.61 billion to foreign-exchange valuation effects. It says more than half of sales are still exports, so quarterly net income can swing by tens of billions of won with exchange-rate moves.

The earlier decline in operating margin, from 16.5% in 2024 to 8.4% in 2025, still has to be reconciled with the 2026 trend.

Concentration in customers and projects

Semiconductor sales depend on expanded supply to SK hynix. Solar orders have stopped under past Indian project timelines, and solar sales were virtually absent in 2025. First-half 2026 sales were still concentrated, with Korea at 44%, China at 31% and India at 21%.

Risk factors

Foreign exchange

Exports exceed half of sales, so exchange rates can move net income through foreign-currency valuation effects. The company says full-year net income will depend on the exchange rate at the end of 2026. Operating profit and net income could move in opposite directions in some quarters.

Customer and regional concentration

Semiconductor sales rely on SK hynix supply volumes, while solar is tied to capital spending and project timing in India and China. As seen when part of Indian shipments slipped into Q4 2026, schedule changes can shift quarterly sales. A change in solar policy support could also alter demand.

Execution of targets and new businesses

The 2027 sales target of KRW 200 billion or more is the company's own goal and depends on hiring and securing volumes. The DeNOx system is planned for 2027 commercialization, but when it contributes profit must be confirmed through earnings disclosures. The profit contribution of the Chinese and US subsidiaries also needs to be verified in filings.

Overall view

GnBS eco recorded the largest sales year in its disclosed history in 2025, at KRW 91.9 billion, but operating profit has fallen for two consecutive years since its 2023 peak, to KRW 7.76 billion in 2025.

In 2026, renewed Indian solar orders and supply growth to SK hynix combined to produce first-quarter sales of KRW 41.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 9.04 billion.

Sales and operating profit both declined in Q2 and Q3, and Q3 posted a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.61 billion, which the company attributes to foreign-exchange valuation effects.

The company says cumulative sales through Q3 2026 already exceeded full-year 2025 sales, and it points to a 2027 sales target of KRW 200 billion or more backed by production capacity.

Supporting factors include chip-customer volume growth, the India solar and HJT equipment ties, and an operating margin that recovered from 8.4% in 2025 to 17.3% for the first three quarters of 2026.

Offsetting factors are quarterly earnings volatility, net income sensitive to exchange rates given the high export share, and dependence on a few customers and solar project timelines.

On valuation, the current multiples sit within the company's own year-end history, and whether earnings grow as forecast will be seen in results from Q1 2027 onward. The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 results.
