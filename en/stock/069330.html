02

Business structure

U.i.display is a KOSDAQ-listed company whose core business is display processing and materials, and this report classifies it under the semiconductor sector. According to the company overview, its main revenue comes from GLASS FILTER, LCD etching and ITO COATING processing segments.

A segment breakdown compiled by Alphasquare shows LCD etching at 40.00 percent, ITO COATING at 29.80 percent, GLASS LAMINATION (DM) at 29.00 percent and real estate at 1.20 percent, and the source does not state a reference date, so these figures should be read as indicative.

The GLASS LAMINATION business laminates hard-type materials onto functional films used in TV and outdoor advertising signage models, with display cover glass as its main product line.

FnGuide noted that demand for digital signage and outdoor display cover glass is rising in this business and that high yields are strengthening its competitiveness.

The LCD etching business is paired with the ITO coating front-end process, which the company says supports stable supply volumes and contributes to cost reduction. Because the three segments are linked through process, a change in utilization in one can carry over to volumes in the others.

The three segments move together, so shifts in display panel and signage demand feed directly into total revenue. Real estate leasing is only 1.20 percent of the mix and does not change the structure of the business.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported annual basis, revenue went from KRW 62.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 53 billion in 2023 (down 15.2 percent year on year), KRW 53.6 billion in 2024 (up 1.1 percent) and KRW 49.7 billion in 2025 (down 7.2 percent).

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 325 million in 2023 back to operating profit of KRW 244 million in 2024, a swing from loss to profit, and rose to KRW 1.3 billion in 2025, up 433.4 percent.

Operating margin was 6.2 percent in 2022, negative 0.6 percent in 2023, 0.5 percent in 2024 and 2.6 percent in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.77 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 3.31 billion in 2023, KRW 660 million in 2024 and KRW 1.29 billion in 2025.

For the 2025 improvement, FnGuide reported that cumulative revenue through the third quarter of 2025 fell 10.4 percent while operating profit rose 87.9 percent, and it attributed the margin gain to cost reduction.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 5.82 billion in 2022 to KRW 452 million in 2023, then KRW 2.95 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.75 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 62.4 percent in 2022 to 130.9 percent in 2023, and at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 20.8 billion, total liabilities KRW 25 billion and the ratio 120.5 percent.

On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 had revenue of KRW 12.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 664 million, and the fourth quarter had revenue of KRW 12.2 billion (up 0.3 percent quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 727 million (up 9.4 percent).

The first quarter of 2026 marked the peak, with revenue of KRW 15.6 billion (up 27.7 percent quarter on quarter), operating profit of KRW 3.49 billion (up 380.0 percent) and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.73 billion.

The second quarter of 2026 turned to losses, with revenue of KRW 12.5 billion (down 19.9 percent), an operating loss of KRW 740 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 467 million.

The third quarter of 2026 saw losses widen, with revenue of KRW 12.4 billion (down 0.8 percent quarter on quarter, up 1.8 percent year on year), an operating loss of KRW 1.42 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.96 billion.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totals KRW 2.19 billion, and whether the first-quarter spike was one-off can only be judged from the notes in the periodic report.

Industry analysis

U.i.display's core products are components and materials used in display panels, digital signage and outdoor displays, so its end demand depends on panel production and on installation demand for advertising displays.

FnGuide cited rising demand for digital signage and outdoor display cover glass as a factor strengthening the competitiveness of the GLASS LAMINATION business.

LCD etching and ITO coating are front-end processes tied to LCD panel output volumes, so production adjustments by panel makers can pass through to earnings quickly.

In an industry comparison by Alphasquare covering 89 display and component companies on an annual basis, the company's 2025 operating margin of 2.62 percent sat above the industry average of negative 4.02 percent. The same source put the debt-to-equity ratio of 120.50 percent slightly above the industry average.

Memory-cycle indicators used for large semiconductor companies do not explain this business well, and demand indicators for display processing and materials are more direct reference variables.

In June 2026, the Asia Economy newspaper reported that the company is pursuing a revaluation through M&A, ESS and chemical materials expansion. The same report said that BSS, a target of the acquisition, had gone through a court-supervised rehabilitation process due to liquidity problems.

Whether the existing display materials business and the new businesses follow different cycles will shape where the company sits within its industry.

Bull factors

Profit recovery in 2025

FnGuide attributed the rise in operating profit of 87.9 percent through the third quarter of 2025, despite a 10.4 percent decline in cumulative revenue, to cost reduction. Because the recovery came alongside shrinking sales, cost management appears to be the main axis of the improvement.

Display demand and yields

FnGuide describes the GLASS LAMINATION business as strengthened by rising demand for digital signage and outdoor display cover glass, supported by high yields. The LCD etching business works with the ITO coating front-end process, which supports stable supply volumes and cost reduction.

All three segments rest on display processing, so a recovery in that demand could lift several businesses at once.

New business expansion and valuation perception

In June 2026, the Asia Economy newspaper reported that the company is pursuing a revaluation through expansion into M&A, ESS and chemical materials. The same report explained that a low valuation is preventing a normal assessment of corporate value.

If the new businesses translate into reported results, the share price range relative to net assets could shift, which is the bullish case cited.

The speed at which new-business sales and profit appear in reported results is uncertain, leaving a gap between expectations and results as a risk.

Overall view

However, operating losses continued in the second and third quarters of 2026, and two consecutive loss-making quarters were reported, while revenue also fell in two consecutive quarters.
