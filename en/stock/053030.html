Binex: Osong Expansion Nears Completion, Losses Persist

Revenue rebounded in 2025, but Binex has posted four straight quarters of controlling-interest net losses through mid-2026, leaving the completion of its Osong plant expansion and cGMP certification as the key variable for the next phase.

02

Business structure

Binex was founded in 1957 as Suncheondang Pharmaceutical, changed its name to Binex in 2000, and listed on KOSDAQ in 2001.

The business runs on two axes: a chemical pharmaceuticals operation making finished drugs such as digestive aids, ophthalmic solutions, and cardiovascular treatments at its Busan plant, and a biopharmaceutical CDMO operation that develops and manufactures antibody biosimilars on contract at its Songdo and Osong plants.

As of the first quarter of 2026, biopharmaceutical contract manufacturing reportedly accounted for the largest share of revenue at 43%, followed by ophthalmic solutions at 14%, cardiovascular treatments at 10%, digestive probiotics at 9%, and other products at 24%.

On a consolidated basis, 2025 biologics CDMO revenue rose 79.0% year over year to KRW 81.7 billion, lifting its share of total revenue from 35.1% to 48.5%.

The core client of the biologics CDMO business is Celltrion, for which Binex has handled global commercial production of the Actemra biosimilar 'Avtozma' (CT-P47) since signing a CMO localization agreement in 2021. The Songdo plant obtained cGMP approval from the European Medicines Agency and then the U.S.

FDA in 2024, qualifying it to supply commercial biologics to the three major markets of the United States, Europe, and Japan.

The Osong plant is undergoing an expansion that adds two 5,000-liter lines to its existing 7,000-liter capacity, bringing total capacity to 10,000 liters, as a basis for pursuing FDA approval and a shift to commercial production.

The chemical pharmaceuticals business has maintained its market share in digestive aids and ophthalmic solutions, though growth has been limited by factors such as drug price cuts.

Industry observers view Binex, unlike large-scale players such as Samsung Biologics or Celltrion, as a small to mid-sized CDMO specializing in diversified, small-batch production, and one securities analyst projected that the company's market presence would build over the next one to three years.

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated 2025 revenue was KRW 168.5 billion, up 29.5% year over year and the largest figure in the 2022-2025 period, while 2024's KRW 130.1 billion was the smallest.

Profitability is still recovering, however: operating profit fell from KRW 17.2 billion in 2022 to just KRW 1.04 billion in 2023, then swung to an operating loss of KRW 30.8 billion in 2024, and the company posted a further operating loss of KRW 4.53 billion in 2025, a second consecutive loss-making year.

Controlling-interest net income followed the same pattern, narrowing from a net loss of KRW 35.1 billion in 2024 to a net loss of KRW 3.23 billion in 2025, but still failing to exit two straight years of losses.

On a quarterly basis, Binex held modest operating profits of KRW 1.02 billion and KRW 242 million in the second and third quarters of 2025, respectively, before swinging to an operating loss of KRW 6.34 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, followed by operating losses of KRW 8.97 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 9.15 billion in the second quarter of 2026, marking three consecutive quarters of widening losses.

Revenue also peaked at KRW 45.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 before declining to KRW 38.8 billion in the fourth quarter and KRW 35.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then rebounding slightly to KRW 37.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026, still down 15.1% from a year earlier.

Controlling-interest net losses widened for four consecutive quarters, from KRW 965 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 3.68 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 9.62 billion in the first quarter of 2026, and KRW 15 billion in the second quarter of 2026, totaling KRW 29.3 billion over that span.

The fact that operating losses deepened even as revenue rebounded quarter over quarter suggests that fixed-cost burdens from the Osong plant expansion and facility conversion are outweighing the pace of revenue recovery.

On the financial side, the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 66.1% at the end of 2025 from 53.8% a year earlier, and operating cash flow turned more negative, from negative KRW 9.43 billion in 2024 to negative KRW 23.3 billion in 2025, pointing to continued pressure on cash generation.

05

Industry analysis

The global biopharmaceutical CDMO market remains in a structural growth phase as biosimilar and new drug pipelines expand and outsourcing penetration continues to rise.

The United States' biosecurity legislation passed at the end of 2025 has increased demand to replace Chinese CDMOs, and new order flows are already said to be affected.

Many observers expect Korean CDMO companies to benefit over the medium to long term from this realignment, with Binex frequently cited as a leading beneficiary candidate given its solid contract development project track record and FDA history.

Within the domestic CDMO competitive landscape, however, Samsung Biologics leads with overwhelming large-scale capacity and Celltrion leverages its own biosimilar pipeline, placing Binex in a distinctly smaller-scale category.

Binex is seen as occupying a niche as a small to mid-sized Asian CDMO specializing in diversified, small-batch production, with differentiated contract development capabilities and a global track record.

Some analysts also point to accumulated order experience with large domestic biotech firms as a basis for expecting mid- to long-term benefits as a small-to-mid-sized CDMO.

At the same time, there are indications that a meaningful step-up in Binex's revenue is likely to occur only after the Osong plant expansion is completed and U.S. FDA approval is obtained, making the timing of capacity conversion into actual revenue a pivotal factor for strengthening its industry position.

06

Outlook

The variable most closely watched by both the company and securities analysts is the timeline for the Osong plant expansion and certification. The Osong plant is expanding from an existing 7,000-liter capacity to a total of 10,000 liters by adding two 5,000-liter lines, with some analysis pointing to a target of U.S.

FDA plant inspection during 2026 followed by a shift to commercial production.

Under an investment agreement with North Chungcheong Province and Cheongju City, additional new construction and expansion are also planned at the Osong Life Science Complex site through 2029, suggesting the mid- to long-term capacity expansion trend will continue.

At the Songdo plant, commercial production of Celltrion's Avtozma continues, and reports of additional product orders from Celltrion suggest the biologics business's revenue base is broadening.

That said, if the Osong expansion completion and FDA approval timeline slips, the timing of revenue expansion could also be pushed back, which warrants attention.

IBK Securities projected that in 2026, commercial production of Avtozma would continue at the Songdo plant while production for one additional PPQ order would also take place at the Osong plant.

The chemical pharmaceuticals business is expected to show a modest trend centered on maintaining its existing share in digestive aids and ophthalmic solutions, and the structure in which the expanding share of biologics CDMO drives overall results is likely to persist for the time being.

08

Bull factors

Growing Share of Biologics CDMO

In 2025, biologics CDMO revenue rose 79.0% year over year to KRW 81.7 billion, lifting its share of total revenue to 48.5%. Commercial production of Celltrion's Avtozma is becoming established, and news of additional product orders suggests a broadening revenue base. Once the Osong plant expansion is completed, there could be additional room for revenue from expanded capacity.

Global Quality Certifications Secured

The Songdo plant has sequentially obtained cGMP approval from the European Medicines Agency and the U.S. FDA, qualifying it to supply commercial biologics to the three major markets of the United States, Europe, and Japan.

This track record could serve as a foundation for additional Osong plant certification and new overseas client acquisition going forward. Based on this, industry observers have identified Binex as a candidate beneficiary of order realignment driven by U.S. biosecurity legislation.

Narrowing Annual Losses

On an annual basis, the operating loss narrowed from KRW 30.8 billion in 2024 to KRW 4.53 billion in 2025, and the controlling-interest net loss shrank from KRW 35.1 billion to KRW 3.23 billion, showing a trend of shrinking losses.

If revenue growth continues, the pace of profitability improvement could accelerate through greater fixed-cost absorption.

Bear factors

Four Straight Quarters of Losses

Controlling-interest net losses have continued for four consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 through Q2 2026, with the loss widening each quarter. Over the same period, operating losses also widened for three consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q2 2026.

Even in the second quarter of 2026, when revenue rebounded quarter over quarter, profitability actually worsened, leaving the timing of a profitability recovery unclear.

Risk of Delayed Expansion and Certification

Targets for the Osong plant's 10,000-liter expansion and FDA approval have been pushed back multiple times. If approval is delayed further than expected, the point at which capacity expansion translates into revenue could also be pushed back.

The longer the shift to commercial production is delayed, the longer fixed-cost burdens could continue to weigh on earnings.

Rising Financial Strain

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 66.1% at the end of 2025 from a year earlier, and operating cash flow turned more negative to KRW -23.3 billion in 2025 compared with 2024.

If the company continues to fail to generate cash from operations while expansion investment continues, the burden of external financing could increase.

10

Risk factors

Utilization and Expansion Risk

The Osong plant remains in the expansion and certification stage, and if utilization does not rise as much as expected, fixed-cost burdens could persist longer than anticipated.

If order volume does not sufficiently support the capital investment, depreciation expenses will continue to weigh directly on costs, delaying a profitability recovery.

Customer Concentration Risk

A significant portion of biologics CDMO revenue depends on Celltrion-related products, so earnings can be heavily affected by a specific client's production schedule or regulatory approval progress.

If new overseas client acquisition is delayed, the risk-diversification benefit from customer diversification could remain limited.

Regulatory and Policy Risk

The inspection schedules and approval outcomes of overseas regulators such as the FDA and EMA are variables the company cannot directly control.

While policy changes such as U.S. biosecurity legislation could be favorable for a realignment of CDMO demand, changes in the pace of implementation or detailed criteria could alter the expected timing of any benefit.

12

Overall view

Binex showed signs of recovery in 2025 with revenue growth and narrowing losses, but profitability recovery remains incomplete as both operating losses and controlling-interest net losses widened simultaneously from Q4 2025 through Q2 2026.

The key variable is the timeline for the Osong plant's 10,000-liter expansion and FDA certification, which must be completed before capacity expansion can translate into actual revenue growth.

The growing share of biologics CDMO revenue and deepening cooperation with Celltrion could form a basis for mid- to long-term growth, but customer concentration and the risk of expansion delays are factors to weigh alongside this.

On the financial side, a rising debt-to-equity ratio and deteriorating operating cash flow have occurred together, making how investment burdens are managed another point to watch.

The next quarterly report and disclosures related to Osong plant certification are likely to provide important clues for assessing the company's future earnings path.
