One Profitable Quarter Amid Continued Losses and Thin Equity

02

Business structure

DAP manufactures and sells build-up printed circuit boards (PCBs), which are widely used to make electronic products thinner, lighter and smaller. Its main customers are reported to be mobile device makers, so shifts in smartphone set demand and customer product cycles feed directly into revenue.

In Q3 2024, the PCB manufacturing segment accounted for 72.3% of revenue and the air transport segment for 27.7%, with the air transport growth driven by its subsidiary AeroK.

Based on Q2 2026 revenue, one data source shows PCBs at 91% and other by-product sales at 9%, so changes in consolidation scope or segment classification should be checked in the segment notes of the next periodic report.

Coverage has described how net losses at an air transport subsidiary acquired in 2022 weighed on the parent's financial health. A disclosure on November 14, 2025 said DAP would acquire shares of its subsidiary AeroK Holdings, which media linked to improving its capital structure.

Domestic PCB peers Daeduck Electronics and Simmtech (Simmtech) are the natural comparison points.

DAP also holds an air transport subsidiary, so its profit structure differs from pure-play board makers, and comparing the profitability trend of its PCB core business separately is more appropriate than comparing headline figures.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 333.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 403.5 billion in 2023, KRW 488.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 543.2 billion in 2025, marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 to 2025, with 2025 up 11.1% year on year. Among the four years from 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest by revenue.

Profit moved in the opposite direction: operating profit of KRW 8.97 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 13.1 billion in 2023, then widened to KRW 30.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 57.8 billion in 2025.

The operating margin fell from 2.7% in 2022 to -3.2% in 2023, -6.2% in 2024 and -10.6% in 2025, so revenue growth did not carry through to profit.

Profit attributable to owners went from KRW 9.12 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 4.8 billion in 2023, KRW 32.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 50.4 billion in 2025, three consecutive years of losses.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 64.8 billion in 2025, which is larger than the loss attributable to owners. In Q3 2024, coverage tied a sharp rise in operating loss to higher personnel and selling and administrative costs, even as subsidiary-driven revenue grew.

By quarter, Q3 2025 had revenue of KRW 124.8 billion, an operating loss of KRW 29.6 billion and a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 27.1 billion, the widest loss in this window. Q4 2025 revenue rose 19.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 148.5 billion while the operating loss narrowed to KRW 6.93 billion.

Q1 2026 revenue fell 36.8% quarter on quarter to KRW 93.8 billion, the operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.83 billion, and profit attributable to owners swung to KRW 97.1 billion.

Because the net profit was far larger than the operating loss that quarter, non-operating items were likely a large factor, and their components should be checked in the quarterly report notes. In Q2 2026 the operating loss widened to KRW 9.29 billion and the net loss attributable to owners was KRW 7.34 billion.

In Q3 2026, revenue was KRW 80.8 billion, the operating loss KRW 6.46 billion and the net loss attributable to owners KRW 5.99 billion, with both losses narrower than a year earlier. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 15 billion in 2022 and KRW 29.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 1.93 billion in 2025.

Industry analysis

DAP's core build-up PCBs are used to make electronic products thinner, lighter and smaller, and its main customers are reported to be mobile device makers, so industry conditions depend heavily on smartphone set demand.

Coverage of the PCB sector points to growing demand from the advancement of IT and automotive electronics, with the market expected to expand around high-performance, high-density products.

On the semiconductor side, the market research firm TechInsights projected that 2026 equipment spending would rise by more than 50% from 2025, with an investment peak forming in the second half of 2026. That forecast covers front-end and back-end equipment spending and links to PCB demand only indirectly.

Advanced packaging investment tied to AI servers and high-bandwidth memory could also affect demand for high-density substrates, but the share of that segment in DAP's product mix should be checked in the business description of the next periodic report.

Reports that major global equipment makers are rushing to sign supply contracts for 2028 point to a capital spending cycle that is still in an expansion phase.

Among domestic PCB competitors, Daeduck Electronics and Simmtech are the natural comparison points, but DAP also holds an air transport subsidiary, so its profit structure differs. Profitability comparisons are most accurate when based on matching periods of disclosed results and operating margin trends.

08

Bull factors

Narrowing operating losses

Comparing the Q3 2025 operating loss of KRW 29.6 billion with the Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 6.46 billion shows a much smaller loss in the same quarter. The operating loss narrowed further to KRW 1.83 billion in Q1 2026. This trend points to room for the operating margin to improve.

Swing to profit attributable to owners in Q1 2026

Profit attributable to owners reached KRW 97.1 billion in Q1 2026, and the four-quarter total from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 is KRW 74.6 billion. That total also includes the net losses of Q2 and Q3 2026. If the quarterly report explains what drove the Q1 profit, it will help gauge how durable earnings are.

Measures on the subsidiary's capital structure

In a disclosure on November 14, 2025, DAP said it would acquire shares of its subsidiary AeroK Holdings. Media coverage linked the move to improving the financial structure. Its effect on consolidated capital can be checked in the notes to the 2026 annual report.

Bear factors

Continued operating losses and margin decline

The operating margin fell from 2.7% in 2022 to -3.2% in 2023, -6.2% in 2024 and -10.6% in 2025. The 2025 operating loss of KRW 57.8 billion was wider than in 2024. The Q2 2026 operating loss widened to KRW 9.29 billion from the prior quarter.

Thin equity cushion

At end-2025, total equity was KRW 20.8 billion, of which KRW 5.22 billion was attributable to owners. Total liabilities of KRW 576.2 billion are very large relative to that equity base. At the end of Q3 2025, total equity was negative, a state of full capital impairment.

Revenue decline and weaker cash flow

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 80.8 billion fell 35.3% year on year. Operating cash flow dropped to KRW 1.93 billion in 2025 from KRW 29.7 billion in 2023. If the revenue decline combines with fixed cost burdens, the loss structure could persist.

10

Risk factors

Financial soundness

With total equity of KRW 20.8 billion against liabilities of KRW 576.2 billion, whether capital impairment reappears is a central risk. Total equity was negative at the end of Q3 2025. Year-end capital figures will be fixed in the 2026 annual report.

Customer concentration and quarterly volatility

Key customers are concentrated among mobile device makers, so results are sensitive to their product cycles. Q1 2026 revenue fell 36.8% quarter on quarter, showing wide quarterly swings. Unless the customer mix changes, this volatility could continue.

Subsidiary results and durability of the Q1 profit

Swings in the air transport subsidiary's results can obscure the profitability of the core PCB business in consolidated figures. The Q1 2026 profit attributable to owners of KRW 97.1 billion differs widely from the operating loss of KRW 1.83 billion, so non-operating items may have had a large effect. If those items were one-time, profits may be hard to repeat in later quarters.

Overall view

DAP extended three consecutive years of losses, with an operating loss of KRW 57.8 billion and an operating margin of -10.6% in 2025, and total equity was only KRW 20.8 billion at end-2025.

It swung to a profit attributable to owners of KRW 97.1 billion in Q1 2026, but that quarter's operating loss was only KRW 1.83 billion, so a large part of the profit may come from non-operating items.

Net losses attributable to owners continued in Q2 and Q3 2026, and the Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 6.46 billion was narrower than a year earlier. On the bullish side, the narrower losses and the subsidiary capital measures stand out.

On the bearish side, the revenue decline, the thin equity cushion and weaker operating cash flow weigh. The business is concentrated in PCBs for mobile device makers, so customer demand shifts pass directly into results.

Expanding semiconductor equipment investment is an indirect industry factor, and how closely it links to substrate demand should be checked in the segment disclosures.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary Q4 2026 results, year-end capital figures in the 2026 annual report, and the trend of the Q1 2027 operating loss.
