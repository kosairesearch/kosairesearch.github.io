Report overview

AI Demand Lifts Earnings; Capacity Build-Out and Long-Term Contracts Are the Key Variables

Samsung Electro-Mechanics reported Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 691 billion, up 165.5% year on year, with AI data center MLCCs and FC-BGA substrates at the center of the earnings recovery.

  1. 1

    Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 3.76 trillion, up 30.3% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 691 billion, up 165.5%. The company said both figures were the highest quarterly results on record.

  2. 2

    The operating margin rose to 18.4% in Q3 2026 from 12.7% in Q2, a 5.7 percentage point gain in one quarter. Compared with the 8.1% annual margin for 2025, profitability has shifted quickly.

  3. 3

    Component (MLCC) division revenue was KRW 1.88 trillion, up 36% year on year, while the Package Solutions (FC-BGA) division grew 58% to KRW 939 billion.

  4. 4

    The company said it expects strong sequential earnings growth every quarter starting in Q1 2027, and projected that more than half of 2027 MLCC revenue will come from long-term supply agreements.

  5. 5

    Total FC-BGA capacity investment across Sejong and Vietnam is KRW 6.78 trillion, with mass production set to start in phases from 2027 and a full revenue contribution expected from 2028.

02

Business structure

Samsung Electro-Mechanics operates three divisions: Component (multilayer ceramic capacitors, or MLCCs), Package Solutions (FC-BGA semiconductor package substrates), and Optical Solutions (camera modules and related products).

In Q3 2026, the Component division generated KRW 1.88 trillion, up 36% year on year and 14% quarter on quarter. Package Solutions generated KRW 939 billion, up 58% year on year and 22% quarter on quarter. Optical Solutions generated KRW 941 billion, up 3% year on year but down 9% quarter on quarter.

The three divisions add up to the consolidated Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 3.76 trillion, and the company said MLCC and substrate businesses have grown to about three-quarters of total revenue. MLCCs keep current flowing stably in electronic circuits and prevent signal interference between components.

Key end markets include data center infrastructure such as AI servers, networking and power supply equipment, automotive electronics including ADAS, and IT devices. FC-BGA growth is driven by higher-value products for server CPUs, AI accelerators and networking equipment.

The company said it secured investment support and long-term supply agreements from major big-tech customers. It also presents itself as the only supplier that can provide both passive components and FC-BGA substrates with embedded passives.

According to the company, supply-capable vendors for leading-edge, high-performance MLCCs for AI servers are limited.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show a recovery from the 2023 low. Revenue fell from KRW 9.42 trillion in 2022 to KRW 8.91 trillion in 2023, a 5.5% decline, then rose to KRW 10.3 trillion in 2024 (up 15.5%) and KRW 11.3 trillion in 2025 (up 9.9%).

Revenue has increased for two consecutive years, from 2024 through 2025, after the 2023 low, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 1.18 trillion in 2022 to KRW 639.4 billion in 2023 (down 45.9%), then recovered to KRW 735 billion in 2024 (up 14.9%) and KRW 913.3 billion in 2025 (up 24.3%).

The operating margin was 12.6% in 2022, 7.2% in 2023, 7.1% in 2024 and 8.1% in 2025, so it has improved modestly from the 2023 level.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 993.5 billion in 2022, KRW 423 billion in 2023 (down 57.4%), KRW 679.1 billion in 2024 (up 60.6%) and KRW 706.1 billion in 2025 (up 4.0%).

Total net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 731 billion in 2025, a different measure that should be read separately. The debt ratio rose from 42.9% in 2022 and 41.9% in 2024 to 49.0% in 2025, when total liabilities were KRW 4.8 trillion. Quarterly results improved sharply in 2026.

Revenue went from KRW 2.9 trillion in Q4 2025 to KRW 3.21 trillion in Q1 2026, KRW 3.46 trillion in Q2 and KRW 3.76 trillion in Q3, with four consecutive quarterly increases from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026.

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 239.5 billion in Q4 2025, down 8.0% quarter on quarter, and then rose to KRW 280.6 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 440.4 billion in Q2 and KRW 691 billion in Q3, with three consecutive quarterly increases from Q1 through Q3 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders also increased for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, totaling KRW 1.26 trillion over those four quarters.

The company attributed the margin gain mainly to higher MLCC selling prices and a richer product mix for AI data centers, and one report said MLCC average selling prices rose more than 10%. Results came in despite a sharp fall in the won against the dollar during the quarter.

05

Industry analysis

The main demand driver is investment in AI data center infrastructure. The company said supply-capable vendors of leading-edge MLCCs for AI servers are limited and capacity takes considerable time to add, so tight supply is expected to continue.

It also presented an AI MLCC rule under which the capacitance required per GPU or ASIC doubles every year; this is the company's own view and should be read as a reference rather than a market-size estimate. On inventory, one report said MLCC stock fell by more than one week in Q3 to about three weeks.

In FC-BGA, the company said demand for AI data center memory and rising raw-material costs led to industry price increases that supported Q3 growth.

Both products are described as being in a tight supply phase, but the new capacity is largely scheduled to come online from 2027, so supply expansion will arrive with a lag.

On competition, the company says few vendors can supply the most advanced AI-server MLCCs, and in substrates it competes with other domestic substrate makers while emphasizing that it supplies both passive components and embedded substrates.

Securities firms cited rising prices and tight supply for MLCCs and FC-BGA as the basis for raising earnings estimates.

06

Outlook

For Q4 2026, the company said continued expansion of AI server infrastructure at its big-tech customers should keep MLCC and FC-BGA supply tighter than in Q3, and that revenue should grow strongly quarter on quarter from richer product mix and higher average selling prices.

For 2027, the company said it expects record annual results with strong sequential growth every quarter starting in Q1. It said it has signed or is pursuing long-term supply agreements with about 50 customers and expects more than half of 2027 MLCC revenue to come from them.

The FC-BGA expansion announced in September 2026 totals KRW 6.78 trillion, with KRW 4.27 trillion for Sejong and KRW 2.51 trillion for Vietnam. The company said mass production will start in phases from 2027, with a full revenue contribution from 2028.

Hana Securities, in an October 23, 2026 report, said it expects new Sejong production lines to ramp up in the second half of 2027, and raised its 2027 operating profit estimate from KRW 4.7 trillion to KRW 5.8 trillion.

A KRW 1.5 trillion supply contract for silicon capacitors was disclosed in May 2026, and the company is preparing for full operation in 2028. The company also said 2026 capital spending will likely exceed its earlier outlook once land, infrastructure and advance investment are included.

08

Bull factors

AI data center MLCC supply and pricing

The company says supply-capable vendors for the most advanced AI-server MLCCs are limited and capacity takes time to add. One report said MLCC average selling prices rose more than 10% in Q3 2026, and the company expects price increases for high-capacity, high-voltage products to persist for a considerable period.

Its statement that more than half of 2027 MLCC revenue will come from long-term agreements is cited as a factor that improves demand visibility.

Differentiated FC-BGA supply structure

The company describes itself as a supplier that can provide both passive components and FC-BGA substrates with embedded passives. It says it has secured investment support and long-term supply agreements from major big-tech customers. Package Solutions revenue reached KRW 939 billion in Q3 2026, up 58% year on year.

Margin improvement and upward estimate revisions

The operating margin reached 18.4% in Q3 2026, up from 12.7% in Q2. In an October 23, 2026 report, Hana Securities said it raised its 2027 operating profit estimate from KRW 4.7 trillion to KRW 5.8 trillion, mostly on FC-BGA estimate revisions.

The upward trend in estimates is cited as evidence that business-level improvements are feeding into reported earnings.

09

Bear factors

Large capital spending and financial burden

The FC-BGA expansion plan, announced in September 2026, commits KRW 4.27 trillion in Sejong and KRW 2.51 trillion in Vietnam, for a total of KRW 6.78 trillion. The debt ratio rose from 42.9% in 2022 to 49.0% in 2025, when total liabilities were KRW 4.8 trillion.

The company said this year's capital spending will likely be larger than initially expected, so the investment burden is rising alongside earnings.

Exchange rate and raw material cost variables

One report noted that Q3 2026 results came against a sharply weaker dollar-won backdrop. Rising raw material costs were also cited as a background factor in industry price increases for FC-BGA.

If won strength and higher material costs persist together, margin improvement for an export-oriented component maker could narrow.

Concentration on the big-tech investment cycle

Growth is concentrated in products tied to AI data centers, so demand can move sharply with the capital spending plans of major big-tech customers. The phased start of new FC-BGA production from 2027 could also limit how fast supply expands.

If demand slows or customer spending is delayed, the timing of revenue under long-term agreements could be affected as well.

10

Risk factors

Demand concentration risk

Q3 2026 results depended heavily on AI data center MLCC and FC-BGA sales. The company said it will keep raising the share of revenue from AI data centers.

If big-tech infrastructure spending slows, the tight MLCC supply and price increases could weaken together, so concentration in one end market adds earnings volatility.

Currency and cost risk

The won weakened sharply against the dollar in Q3 2026 and the company cited FX as a headwind. Higher raw material costs supported FC-BGA price increases, but if costs outpace those increases, margins could come under pressure. Simultaneous moves in FX and input costs make quarterly earnings direction harder to predict.

Capacity execution and price normalization risk

The FC-BGA expansion in Sejong and Vietnam is set to start mass production in phases from 2027, with a full revenue contribution from 2028. If the build-out slips or supply rises faster than planned, today's tight market and price increases could ease.

The company said it will decide the timing and size of further investment based on market conditions and supply-demand status.

12

Overall view

Samsung Electro-Mechanics recovered from its 2023 low, with revenue and operating profit both rising in 2024 and 2025. In 2026, quarterly revenue rose for four consecutive quarters and operating profit for three.

The Q3 2026 operating margin of 18.4% was above the 12.7% of Q2, and the earnings gain is centered on AI data center MLCCs and FC-BGA substrates. The company guided to sequential growth every quarter from Q1 2027 and expects more than half of 2027 MLCC revenue to come from long-term agreements.

On the other side, KRW 6.78 trillion of FC-BGA investment has raised the debt ratio to 49.0% at end-2025, and the capital spending burden is growing. Won weakness and rising raw material costs remain variables for margins.

Demand is concentrated in big-tech AI infrastructure spending, so customer investment timing is a key variable for earnings. Securities firms have set target prices based on higher earnings estimates, but those valuations would shift if the pricing and capacity assumptions behind them change.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results, the 2026 annual report, and the ramp-up of new FC-BGA lines in the second half of 2027.
