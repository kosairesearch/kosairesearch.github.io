3S: Quarterly Profit Turn Emerges After Two Loss Years

3S Korea posted losses in both the fiscal years ended March 2025 and March 2026, but its net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 474 million in Q3 2026.

02

Business structure

3S Korea was founded in 1991 and specializes in semiconductor wafer carrier boxes, calorimeters for refrigeration and air-conditioning, and environmental test equipment.

The business runs in two segments: wafer carriers and environmental test equipment, where the company develops and manufactures products such as calorimeters and environmental control systems on a build-to-spec basis.

A portal's aggregation puts the revenue split at 53.5% environmental and 46.5% wafer carrier, though the aggregation does not state its reference date.

Wafer carrier products were 62.91% domestic and 37.09% exports in the fiscal year ended March 2026, while other products in the calorimeter line were 77.97% domestic and 22.03% exports. A corporate monitoring service puts the company's share of the domestic FOSB market at 10%, with no reference date given.

FOSB is a container used to carry wafers between process steps. R&D spending was KRW 532 million in the fiscal year ended March 2026, or 1.87% of revenue, down from KRW 940 million (3.24%) a year earlier. Consolidated revenue of KRW 28.4 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026 is the sum of the two segments.

The wafer carrier segment is sensitive to wafer shipment volumes in semiconductors, and the test equipment segment depends on the timing of customer facility investment. At the end of March 2026, total liabilities were KRW 12.2 billion and total equity was KRW 44.6 billion.

04

Earnings analysis

3S Korea's results turned down after the fiscal year ended March 2024. Revenue rose from KRW 41.9 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2023 to KRW 43.6 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2024, a 4.2% increase.

It then fell 33.4% to KRW 29 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2025 and declined 2.2% to KRW 28.4 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026. The company attributed the fiscal 2025 decline to weaker end-market conditions, along with higher manufacturing costs.

Operating profit fell from KRW 2.18 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2023 to KRW 1.16 billion a year later, a 46.6% drop, and then turned into an operating loss of KRW 5.83 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2025. The loss narrowed to KRW 5.11 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026.

The operating margin moved from 5.2% and 2.7% in the two earliest years to -20.1% and -18.0% in the two loss years.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 2.61 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2024 to a net loss of KRW 10.1 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2025, and KRW 5.48 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 10 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2025, slightly different from the controlling-shareholder figure.

Operating cash flow swung from a negative KRW 4.43 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2025 to KRW 3.22 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026. On a quarterly basis, operating losses were KRW 2.68 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 1.06 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 1.62 billion in Q1 2026.

Q2 2026 posted an operating profit of KRW 5.68 million, and Q3 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 432 million. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was positive at KRW 474 million in Q3 2026.

That profit despite an operating loss suggests non-operating items contributed, and the item-level detail is a point to check in the next periodic report. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 3.41 billion. The debt ratio at the end of March 2026 was 27.4%, down from 51.0% a year earlier.

05

Industry analysis

3S Korea's results are tied to the semiconductor cycle and the timing of facility investment. A corporate monitoring service attributed the wafer carrier segment's weakness to lower wafer shipment volumes from a semiconductor downturn, and the test equipment segment's weakness to delayed facility investment.

Wafer carrier demand moves with semiconductor fab utilization, while environmental test equipment demand depends on when customers schedule their facility projects.

At its March 2026 investor event, the company tied its next-year record revenue target to organizational streamlining, normalization of customer relationships and capacity expansion. A corporate monitoring service puts its share of the domestic FOSB market at 10%, without a reference date.

Industry classification also varies by data provider: the same monitoring service classifies the company under KOSDAQ medical and precision instruments and under WI26 machinery, which differs from the power equipment label used in this report.

Peers grouped under power equipment, such as LS ELECTRIC and Hyosung Heavy Industries, have product mixes and customer bases that differ from 3S Korea's, so the company is better read through its two demand drivers than through the sector average.

The pace of semiconductor recovery and the timing of facility investment restarts are the main reference points for its position in the cycle.

Outlook

At its March 26, 2026 investor relations event, the company stated a target of returning to break-even. It also described an ongoing normalization of operations following a management overhaul. The company attributed the sharp deterioration to the previous management period.

Its next-year revenue target was framed as record revenue rather than a specific amount. Set against the results, the KRW 5.11 billion operating loss in the fiscal year ended March 2026 remains some distance from break-even.

Q3 2026 turned net profit attributable to controlling shareholders positive at KRW 474 million, but an operating loss of KRW 432 million remained, so operating-level profitability has not yet been confirmed as sustained.

Because Q2 2026 was the only operating profit quarter in the recent stretch, whether quarterly profits repeat is the next point to check. The most direct measure of progress in the periodic reports is how far the -18.0% operating margin improves alongside revenue.

The Q4 2026 quarterly report and the fiscal year ending March 2027 annual report will show whether revenue and operating results move in the targeted direction. The pace of the semiconductor recovery and the timing of customer facility investment are external variables that will shape whether the targets are reached.

Risk factors

Industry cycle

If the semiconductor downturn lengthens, wafer carrier shipments could fall again. The test equipment segment's revenue shifts with the timing of customer facility spending. If demand weakens in both segments at once, the quarterly profit trend could reverse.

Execution of normalization

The company says it is in a normalization phase after the management change, but the break-even target has not yet appeared in results, with an operating loss of KRW 5.11 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2026.

Whether organizational streamlining and the rebuilding of customer relationships proceed as planned is the key issue. If gaps between targets and results recur, rebuilding market confidence could take longer.

Concentration of valuation basis

Because the last four quarters include losses, the price-to-earnings ratio cannot be calculated, leaving valuation to depend heavily on net assets and on whether quarterly profits persist. With no dividend, shareholder return provides little support. If profitable quarters remain limited to one or two, earnings-based assessments could become less stable.
