Business structure

EM-Tech operates a parts business that supplies mobile-phone components and a products business covering e-cigarette devices, health and beauty care devices, wireless earsets and smart hearing aids.

On a Q2 2026 sales basis, parts accounted for about 92% and products for about 8%, according to a data aggregator's breakdown. FnGuide's business summary says the parts unit makes components for mobile communication devices, while the products unit makes the device lines listed above.

WiseReport's Q1 2026 commentary notes that speaker parts depend heavily on the front-end handset market, so results move with handset trends. In Q1 2026 the core parts business posted lower sales as demand for smart devices changed.

Within products, e-cigarette device sales declined in the cumulative period through Q3 2025, while health, beauty and hearing-aid lines are being expanded. According to FnGuide's financial statements, R&D expense was KRW 18.8 billion in 2025 versus KRW 25.0 billion in 2024.

FnGuide's Q1 2025 summary also says the company is pursuing automotive electronics parts and technology development for TWS earphones. Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek are listed in the same electronics and components sector, and EM-Tech differs in that most of its revenue comes from parts.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed basis, annual revenue went from KRW 415.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 263.5 billion in 2023 (down 36.6% year on year) and KRW 167.6 billion in 2024 (down 36.4%), before recovering to KRW 205.1 billion in 2025 (up 22.4%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the year with the largest revenue and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit of KRW 33.9 billion in 2022, with an operating margin of 8.2%, turned into an operating loss of KRW 14.6 billion in 2023 (margin of -5.5%). The 2024 operating loss widened to KRW 30.5 billion (margin of -18.2%), and the 2025 operating loss narrowed to KRW 19.9 billion (margin of -9.7%).

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 33.4 billion in 2022 became a net loss of KRW 11.8 billion in 2023, widened to KRW 44.5 billion in 2024, and narrowed to KRW 11.5 billion in 2025.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 11.9 billion in 2025 and should be read separately from the controlling-shareholder figure.

On the cost side, FnGuide's financial statements show selling and administrative expenses fell to KRW 38.6 billion in 2025 from KRW 48.8 billion in 2024, while gross profit edged up to KRW 18.7 billion from KRW 18.3 billion.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 29.7 billion in 2022 and KRW 30.3 billion in 2023 to KRW 7.31 billion in 2024 and minus KRW 15.8 billion in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 57.5% in 2022 to 73.1% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 143.8 billion and total equity of KRW 196.7 billion at year-end 2025.

Quarterly revenue ran at KRW 52.1 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 46.4 billion in Q4 2025 (down 11.0% from the prior quarter), KRW 48.6 billion in Q1 2026 (up 4.7%), KRW 40.4 billion in Q2 2026 (down 16.7%) and KRW 53.9 billion in Q3 2026 (up 33.2%).

Operating losses were KRW 4.38 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 10.1 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 4.8 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 7.32 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 3.1 billion in Q3 2026, the smallest of the last five quarters.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 16.3 billion over the last four quarters, and the Q3 2026 loss of KRW 3.73 billion was larger than the KRW 2.77 billion of the year-earlier quarter.

Industry analysis

EM-Tech's results depend heavily on handset demand. WiseReport's Q1 2026 commentary attributes the decline in parts sales to changes in demand for smart devices. Speaker parts tend to see volume shift with handset customers' new-model cycles and changing trends.

In products, e-cigarette device sales declined in the cumulative period through Q3 2025, leaving e-cigarette demand and customer volumes as the main variables for that unit.

A securities report notes that the company began e-cigarette OEM supply to a domestic tobacco maker at the end of 2017, and the product lineup has since widened toward health and beauty devices and hearing aids.

On the sales trend, revenue fell from KRW 415.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 167.6 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 205.1 billion in 2025.

In the same electronics and components sector, Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek are large parts makers, and the gap in market capitalization and business scope with EM-Tech is wide. EM-Tech's parts share of about 92% is high, while its product lines account for roughly 8% of sales.

Information on where the industry sits in its cycle is limited, so tracking handset demand alongside the parts sales trend is the more useful reference point for this company.
