02

Business structure

NGVI is a fuel storage and supply system maker headquartered in Chuncheon, Gangwon State. It listed on the Konex market on September 30, 2019.

It developed Korea's first fuel storage system for compressed natural gas (CNG) vehicles, supplied customers including Hyundai Motor and Edison Motors, and later widened its range to LNG and hydrogen fuel systems.

A job-portal company profile shows a revenue mix of 64% natural gas vehicle fuel container modules, 19% pressure vessels, 9% other parts such as CNG vessels and valves, and 8% other. This points to heavy dependence on parts for natural gas commercial vehicles.

Agent AI, formerly DGP, took a 46.87% stake and became the largest shareholder. In 2023 NGVI signed a fuel cell module supply contract with Canada's Loop Energy and pursued a hydrogen electric bus project in Ulsan. In January 2025 it supplied a hydrogen storage system for a hydrogen-electric hybrid bus.

It had 11 employees as of December 31, 2025. Its scale cannot be compared with large automotive-sector names such as Hyundai Motor, Hyundai Mobis and HL Mando, and the direct competitive landscape in natural gas and hydrogen fuel tanks could not be confirmed through search.

03

Recent trends

On a 2025 standalone basis, revenue was KRW 862 million, operating loss was KRW 571 million and net loss was KRW 676 million. FnGuide summarized the 2025 results as revenue up 33.8%, with the operating loss and net loss down 17.2% and 10.2% respectively.

By FnGuide's figures, the operating loss was KRW 2.1 billion in 2023, KRW 700 million in 2024 and KRW 600 million in 2025, while the operating margin improved from -107.18% to -66.29%. The loss narrowed, but the operating deficit persisted for a third straight year.

Total assets were KRW 925 million and total liabilities were KRW 3,362 million, leaving the company in full capital impairment. Paid-in capital was KRW 3,010 million, total equity was KRW -2,436 million and loss per share was KRW 112.

For the 2025 half-year, it reported revenue of KRW 247 million, an operating loss of KRW 126 million and a net loss of KRW 475 million. Results for the 2026 half-year and later could not be confirmed through search.

The share price closed at KRW 413 on October 6, 2026, against a 52-week high of KRW 1,565 and low of KRW 361, with a 12-month change of -74.28%. On October 27, 2026 it closed at KRW 468 with zero trading value.

Outlook

No broker consensus was found, and Hankyung Korea Market also shows no consensus for the stock. Future results can therefore be gauged only through company disclosures and order news. FnGuide's business summary cites expansion of the hydrogen vehicle market as a growth driver.

However, 2025 revenue was only about KRW 862 million, so the speed and scale at which hydrogen commercial vehicle demand reaches earnings remain uncertain. Whether the narrowing of operating losses continues depends on the balance between revenue growth and fixed-cost burden.

At the largest shareholder, Agent AI, its own largest shareholder changed to Prime Value Investment Partnership on August 12, 2026, and a rights offering decision was withdrawn on August 31, 2026. These items concern Agent AI's governance and financing, and their effect on NGVI's ownership is not confirmed.

Market conditions are also a headwind. Reports noted that average daily Konex trading value fell to KRW 1.1 billion, a record low. Items to watch in future disclosures are any plan to resolve the capital impairment, new orders and the extent of shareholder support.

05

Bull factors

Revenue growth and narrower losses

In 2025, revenue rose 33.8% and the operating loss narrowed by 17.2% year on year. The operating margin improved from -107.18% to -66.29%. The decline in operating loss from KRW 2.1 billion in 2023 to KRW 600 million in 2025 points to room for further improvement in operating metrics. A move to profit, however, has not been confirmed.

Hydrogen and natural gas storage know-how

It developed Korea's first CNG fuel storage system and widened its portfolio to LNG and hydrogen fuel systems. In January 2025 it also logged a supply of a hydrogen storage system for a hydrogen-electric hybrid bus. Delivery experience in eco-friendly commercial vehicles gives a small company an operating base. How much of this experience converts into earnings still needs separate confirmation.

Anchor shareholder with a large stake

Agent AI, formerly DGP, secured a 46.87% stake and management control. At the time of the acquisition, DGP said it would work with NGVI to develop hydrogen plant equipment. The existence of a shareholder business-linkage plan can be noted as a neutral fact. No case of this plan producing confirmed results was found through search.

06

Bear factors

Full capital impairment

Total liabilities of KRW 3,362 million against total assets of KRW 925 million leave the company in full capital impairment. Total equity is KRW -2,436 million. By FnGuide's figures, equity was KRW -1.0 billion in 2023, KRW -1.8 billion in 2024 and KRW -2.4 billion in 2025, a deficit that widened every year. As long as losses accumulate, the balance sheet keeps weakening.

Persistent operating deficit and cash outflow

The operating loss narrowed, yet the company still posted an operating loss of KRW 571 million in 2025. A job-portal company profile shows 2025 current assets of KRW 689 million, current liabilities of KRW 2,991 million and operating cash flow of KRW -665 million.

Short-term debts far exceed short-term assets, which weighs on working capital. Until a profitable base is confirmed, reliance on outside funding is likely to remain high.

Very thin liquidity and small scale

Trading value was zero on October 27, 2026, and Hankyung Korea Market also showed zero volume on October 6, 2026. Reports noted that average daily trading value across Konex had fallen to KRW 1.1 billion, a record low.

With a market capitalization of KRW 2.8 billion and 11 employees, a handful of trades can move quotes sharply. Orders may not be filled at the desired time.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Demand for natural gas and hydrogen commercial vehicle parts is shaped by fuel prices, eco-friendly vehicle policy and the commercial vehicle cycle. Specific subsidy sizes and demand figures could not be confirmed through search. Slower hydrogen vehicle adoption could delay plans to expand the hydrogen business. As a small company, its earnings could swing widely with industry conditions.

Financial and listing

Operating and net losses continue under full capital impairment. Most liabilities are current, so repayment and refinancing pressure is concentrated in the short term. A prolonged impairment could add to the burden on fundraising and on maintaining the listing. No plan to resolve the impairment was confirmed.

Governance and funding

The largest shareholder, Agent AI, has been going through changes in its own governance and funding. On August 31, 2026, Agent AI withdrew its decision on a rights offering with a public offering of unsubscribed shares.

The parent's funding position could affect its capacity to support NGVI, but the extent is not confirmed. Any change in major shareholders or stake transactions could also alter the business direction.

08

Overall view

NGVI improved some operating metrics in 2025, with higher revenue and a narrower operating loss. However, the operating deficit and full capital impairment persist, so the financial structure remains fragile.

Its natural gas and hydrogen fuel storage technology and delivery record form a business base, but the small revenue scale makes it hard to gauge the effect. The largest shareholder's funding position and the thin trading liquidity of the Konex market are further variables to weigh.

Results after the 2025 half-year and broker consensus were not confirmed. Future disclosures on any move to profit, a plan to resolve the impairment and new orders are the starting point for judgment. This report is for information only, and investment decisions rest with the reader.
