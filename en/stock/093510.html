02

Business structure

NGVI, founded in July 2000, makes fuel storage and supply systems for automobiles and is classified under engine parts manufacturing for vehicles.

Its core product is the CNG (compressed natural gas) fuel storage module, which according to JobKorea company data made up 64% of 2025 sales, followed by CNG pressure vessels at 19%, other parts such as CNG pressure vessels and valves at 9%, and other items at 8%.

The company built its integration know-how by combining CNG pressure vessels, valves and storage systems, and has supplied fuel storage systems to domestic commercial vehicle makers.

Hydrogen is the expansion axis: NGVI developed hydrogen vehicle fuel storage systems, delivered samples to a hydrogen bus customer in 2024, and has generated new revenue from vehicle commercialization since 2025.

Government roadmaps for the hydrogen economy support vehicle deployment and charging station build-out, which form the external backdrop for this shift. Past press reports described a distribution agreement with Canada-based Loop Energy to sell hydrogen fuel cell systems to domestic bus and truck operators.

Compared with large auto parts makers such as Hyundai Mobis and Hanon Systems, NGVI's revenue and organization are very small, with 11 employees as of December 31, 2025.

The company exited rehabilitation proceedings in 2014 through an M&A transaction that brought in a new CEO, and its registered production site is listed in Ulju County, Ulsan.

03

Recent trends

For the standalone fiscal year 2025 (ended December 31, 2025), revenue was KRW 862,735 thousand (KRW 862.7 million), up 33.72% from KRW 645,183 thousand in 2024, according to JobKorea financial data.

Operating loss was KRW 571,897 thousand, narrowing from KRW 691,475 thousand in 2024, and sharply below the KRW 2,084,591 thousand loss recorded in 2023. The net loss was KRW 676,552 thousand, smaller than the KRW 753,641 thousand posted in 2024. The operating margin improved to -66.29% in 2025 from -107.18% in 2024.

Operating cash flow was KRW -664,839 thousand, a smaller deficit than KRW -879,729 thousand in 2024, but still negative. Total equity, however, fell to KRW -2,436,828 thousand from KRW -1,760,871 thousand, and total liabilities of KRW 3,362,471 thousand far exceed total assets of KRW 925,643 thousand.

The borrowing dependence ratio rose to 275.99% in 2025 from 242.61% in 2024. Basic EPS for 2025 was KRW -112. At the annual general meeting held on March 26, 2026, all agenda items, including approval of the 26th-term financial statements, passed.

The share price was KRW 413 at the close on October 6, 2026 and KRW 466 as of October 31, 2026, while the price at the report date is KRW 609 (-0.16% versus the prior close).

Outlook

On the positive side, commercialization of hydrogen vehicle fuel storage systems has produced new revenue since 2025, broadening NGVI's sales base beyond CNG. Revenue growth, a narrower operating loss and a smaller operating cash deficit point to improving operating indicators.

Whether these gains translate into the reversal of capital impairment has not yet appeared in the financial statements. On the negative side, negative total equity for three consecutive years since 2023 is the heaviest burden.

With high borrowing dependence and weak operating cash generation, results may be sensitive to external financing conditions. Market capitalization of about KRW 3.7 billion and trading value recorded at zero at the report date mean the company holds a very small weight in the market.

The next checkpoint is the third-quarter 2026 report, due for filing by November 14, 2026, which will show equity and operating profit and loss trends. Full-year 2026 results are expected to be disclosed through the annual report filed in March 2027. Earnings trends and changes in the capital structure remain the key variables for this stock.

05

Bull factors

Hydrogen storage revenue now commercial

Revenue from hydrogen vehicle fuel storage systems has been recognized since 2025 as vehicle commercialization began. NGVI moved from delivering samples to a hydrogen bus customer in 2024 to commercial supply.

Government roadmaps for the hydrogen economy support vehicle deployment and charging infrastructure, which backs the transition. The company's CNG pressure vessel know-how applied to hydrogen tanks and system integration is cited as a differentiating factor.

Sales growth and a narrower loss

Revenue for 2025 was KRW 862,735 thousand, up 33.72% from KRW 645,183 thousand in 2024. The operating loss shrank to KRW 571,897 thousand from KRW 691,475 thousand, and the operating margin improved to -66.29% from -107.18%.

Compared with the KRW 2,084,591 thousand operating loss in 2023, the direction of operating indicators is clear. Whether this trend continues through fiscal 2026 is the next test.

Smaller cash outflows

Operating cash flow was KRW -664,839 thousand in 2025, a smaller deficit than KRW -879,729 thousand in 2024. Cash operating profit improved to KRW -541,130 thousand from KRW -623,782 thousand. Investing cash flow also showed a smaller outflow, at KRW -689,362 thousand versus KRW -912,529 thousand. The reduced cash burn may ease reliance on outside funding, though the cash flows remain negative.

06

Bear factors

Third year of full capital impairment

Total equity moved from KRW -1,015,972 thousand in 2023 to KRW -1,760,871 thousand in 2024 and KRW -2,436,828 thousand in 2025, widening each year. Total liabilities of KRW 3,362,471 thousand far exceed total assets of KRW 925,643 thousand.

The 26th-term standalone statements were also reported in a state of full capital impairment. Until the impairment is resolved, the balance sheet remains a weight that can offset operating improvements.

Borrowing reliance and weak cash generation

Borrowing dependence rose each year, reaching 153.90% in 2023, 242.61% in 2024 and 275.99% in 2025. Cash operating profit remained negative at KRW -541,130 thousand in 2025, so internal cash generation is limited.

Current liabilities of KRW 2,991,433 thousand far exceed current assets of KRW 689,433 thousand, creating a notable short-term liquidity burden.

Thin trading and large price swings

According to one data provider, the one-year share price change as of October 31, 2026 was -54.09%, while another shows -74.28% over twelve months. Trading value was recorded at zero at the report date. With market capitalization of about KRW 3.7 billion, price moves can be large even on limited trading.

Risk factors

Macro

Adoption of CNG and hydrogen vehicles depends on commercial vehicle output and the pace of charging infrastructure build-out. A slowdown in vehicle demand or delayed capital spending could reduce delivery volumes.

Because revenue is small, quarterly results may swing widely depending on the timing of individual project deliveries.

Policy and regulation

Growth in the hydrogen business relies heavily on government roadmaps and deployment support for the hydrogen economy. Changes in policy direction or budgets could affect demand for hydrogen fuel storage systems.

The fuel cell sales plans described in past press reports are not separately visible in the current revenue breakdown.

Liquidity and listing

If capital impairment persists for an extended period, the possibility of KONEX listing-maintenance measures may be raised. Given low trading value, price formation through trading is limited, and share price moves can become large regardless of earnings trends. The small market capitalization makes large institutional trading difficult to establish.

08

Overall view

NGVI shows improving operating indicators, with revenue growth and a narrower operating loss, while full capital impairment and borrowing dependence remain in place. Commercial revenue from hydrogen vehicle fuel storage systems is a positive factor that broadens the CNG-centered business.

However, the widening negative equity and liabilities far exceeding assets weigh on the balance sheet. The cash deficit has narrowed, but weak operating cash generation makes access to outside financing a variable for earnings durability.

The share price moved from KRW 466 on October 31, 2026 to KRW 609 at the report date, but the link between price moves and earnings trends cannot be established from current data alone. Given its market capitalization and trading scale, direct comparison with large auto parts makers is limited. This is a qualitative summary provided for informational purposes.
