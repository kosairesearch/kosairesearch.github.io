BGF: Subsidiary Restructuring and Early Signs of Recovery

02

Business structure

BGF is a holding company whose core subsidiary is BGF Retail, the operator of the CU convenience store chain.

BGF holds 30.0% of BGF Retail, which is classified as an associate, so BGF Retail's revenue and operating profit are not added to BGF's consolidated results, and BGF instead relies on dividends from BGF Retail as a main source of income.

BGF received KRW 21.3 billion in dividends from BGF Retail in the first half of 2026, more than 90% of its total dividend income of KRW 23.5 billion for the period. BGF's own income comes from brand usage fees, management service fees, and dividend and rental income from subsidiaries.

Consolidated results are driven mainly by the materials businesses. BGF Eco Materials, which makes engineering plastics and in which BGF holds a 69.95% stake, reported preliminary third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 104.425 billion, up 3.6% year on year.

KNW, which makes electronic and automotive parts materials, became a direct BGF subsidiary after BGF bought a 56.9% stake from BGF Eco Materials in October 2026.

KNW's subsidiary BGF Eco Specialty makes semiconductor specialty gases and completed an anhydrous hydrogen fluoride plant in Onsan, Ulsan, in September 2026 with annual capacity of about 50,000 tonnes, roughly half of domestic demand.

Some press coverage groups KNW under semiconductor materials, while the company's disclosure classifies it under electronic and automotive parts materials.

Press reports said first-half 2026 sales in the electronic and automotive materials unit fell 20.5% to KRW 12.1 billion, and semiconductor materials sales fell 6.6% to KRW 23.0 billion.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 435 billion in 2022 to KRW 443.2 billion in 2023 (up 1.9%), then fell to KRW 434.1 billion in 2024 (down 2.1%) before reaching KRW 469.5 billion in 2025 (up 8.2%), the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

Operating profit was KRW 61.2 billion in 2022, KRW 70.5 billion in 2023 (up 15.3%), the largest year, KRW 55.2 billion in 2024 (down 21.7%), the smallest year, and KRW 61.2 billion in 2025 (up 10.8%).

Operating margin moved from 14.1% in 2022 to 15.9% in 2023, 12.7% in 2024 and 13.0% in 2025, so in 2025 margin stayed below the 2023 level even as revenue grew 8.2%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 23.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 77.8 billion in 2023 (up 229.5%) and KRW 92.1 billion in 2024 (up 18.4%, the largest year), then fell to KRW 59.1 billion in 2025 (down 35.8%).

Total net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 56.4 billion in 2025, below the controlling-shareholder figure, which means the non-controlling portion worked in the direction of a loss.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 207 million in 2022 to KRW 70.3 billion in 2023, then KRW 49.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 52.7 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 11.6% in 2022 to 18.5% in 2025; at year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 2.02 trillion, of which KRW 1.79 trillion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 375.1 billion.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit of KRW 26.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 was followed by KRW 11.8 billion in the fourth quarter (down 56.0% from the prior quarter).

The first quarter of 2026 was the trough, with revenue of KRW 104.9 billion, operating profit of KRW 8.48 billion and net income to controlling shareholders of KRW 9.81 billion.

Revenue and operating profit then rose for two consecutive quarters, to KRW 123.6 billion and KRW 13.6 billion in the second quarter and KRW 130.7 billion and KRW 23.9 billion in the third quarter.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was still 11.0% lower than a year earlier, and net income to controlling shareholders of KRW 19.2 billion was down 26.3%.

Over the same period, subsidiary BGF Eco Materials reported preliminary third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.138 billion, down 28.5%, and a net loss (announced November 5, 2026), while associate BGF Retail's preliminary operating profit of KRW 100.8 billion is not consolidated.

The sum of net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (fourth quarter 2025 to third quarter 2026) was KRW 59.5 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In convenience stores, growth at BGF Retail is being driven by foreign customers.

Foreign-customer sales growth widened from 53.3% in the first quarter of 2026 to 60.9% in the second and 66.8% in the third, and foreign-customer sales accounted for 35.5% of the increase in total store sales in the third quarter (based on preliminary results announced November 5, 2026).

The company said differentiated foods and processed snacks held up despite a base effect from last year's consumer coupon program and a hot summer. The materials side is the opposite.

BGF Eco Materials' third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 3.6%, but its operating profit fell 28.5%, and press reports said first-half 2026 sales in the electronic and automotive materials unit fell 20.5%.

The semiconductor specialty gas business now has a completed plant, but press coverage said it still needs large investment in quality improvement, production stabilization and additional capacity, which is the rationale for management at the holding-company level.

On valuation context, press reports noted that BGF was included in a list of low price-to-book listed companies compiled by the Korea Exchange. Compared with other holding companies, BGF's consolidated earnings exclude its convenience store affiliate and depend on the strength of its materials subsidiaries.

Growth in convenience stores rests on differentiated products and foreign demand, while the materials units must recover end-market demand and narrow losses.

06

Outlook

In October 2026, BGF brought KNW directly under the holding company by buying its stake, and press reports said the company plans to keep investing in semiconductor materials under holding-company-level management.

Proceeds from the KNW stake sale will be allocated by BGF Eco Materials to three areas: strengthening core competitiveness, improving the capital structure, and enhancing shareholder value. A plan to cancel all 842,699 treasury shares, about 1.3% of total issued shares, in November 2026 was reported in October 2026.

According to a disclosure dated August 19, 2026, BGF Eco Materials decided to acquire 800,000 shares of a related plastics company for KRW 38.4 billion under a put option, which would raise its stake to 100%.

The Ulsan anhydrous hydrogen fluoride plant was completed in September 2026, and quality improvement and production stabilization are now the stated priorities. The scale and timing of further capacity additions have not been fixed.

Associate BGF Retail is building growth around foreign customers and differentiated products, with cumulative 2026 third-quarter operating profit of KRW 224 billion, up 18.0% (preliminary results announced November 5, 2026).

Because BGF Retail's results are not added to BGF's consolidated operating profit, a recovery at the holding-company level depends more on the materials subsidiaries and dividend flows.

08

Bull factors

Simpler ownership structure after subsidiary reorganization

In October 2026, BGF brought KNW in as a direct subsidiary, reducing the ownership chain from four tiers to three. The company said direct management of the materials businesses is meant to speed investment and decision-making.

Proceeds from the stake sale are to be split among core competitiveness, capital structure improvement and shareholder value.

Earnings momentum at associate BGF Retail

BGF Retail's preliminary third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 100.8 billion, up 3.2% year on year, and cumulative operating profit reached KRW 223.8 billion, up 18.0% (announced November 5, 2026). Foreign-customer sales growth widened to 66.8% in the third quarter.

BGF received KRW 21.3 billion in dividends from BGF Retail in the first half of 2026, so the affiliate's profit flows through to the holding company's dividend income.

Quarterly earnings recovery in 2026

After the first-quarter 2026 low, revenue, operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose for two consecutive quarters. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 130.7 billion was up 1.8% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 23.9 billion was up 75.4% from the prior quarter. Whether the recovery continues will be shown by results in later quarters.

09

Bear factors

Materials subsidiary swings to a loss

Subsidiary BGF Eco Materials reported preliminary third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.138 billion, down 28.5% year on year, and a net loss of KRW 2.954 billion, a swing into loss (announced November 5, 2026).

Press reports said first-half 2026 sales fell 20.5% in the electronic and automotive materials unit and 6.6% in the semiconductor materials unit. The materials businesses have not yet been able to support consolidated profit.

Lower consolidated earnings

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 35.8% to KRW 59.1 billion in 2025, and the third-quarter 2026 figure of KRW 19.2 billion was down 26.3% year on year. The last four quarters total KRW 59.5 billion. Compared with the KRW 92.1 billion peak in 2024, the earnings base is lower.

Flat dividends and a lower payout ratio

Total dividends were KRW 12.4 billion in 2025, unchanged from 2024, and the payout ratio fell from 44.6% in 2022 to 21.0% in 2025. Over the same period, subsidiary BGF Retail held total dividends flat at KRW 70.8 billion for four consecutive years. The holding company's shareholder returns depend on subsidiary dividends, which is seen as a weakness.

Risk factors

Associate accounting structure

BGF Retail is an associate with a 30.0% stake, so improvements in convenience store results do not flow directly into BGF's consolidated revenue or operating profit. Consolidated results depend more on the operating trends of the materials subsidiaries, and weakness there hits group earnings immediately.

Dividends received from BGF Retail make up a meaningful part of the holding company's income, which is a related risk.

Semiconductor materials investment burden

BGF Eco Specialty completed its Ulsan plant in September 2026, but press coverage says large investment is still needed for quality improvement, production stabilization and additional capacity. The debt ratio rose from 11.6% in 2022 to 18.5% in 2025, which narrows financial room. How expanded investment weighs on cash flow and capital allocation is the key risk.

Reorganization and cash deployment

BGF Eco Materials decided, under a put option disclosed in August 2026, to acquire 800,000 shares of a related plastics company for KRW 38.4 billion to raise its stake to 100%. Combined with the treasury share cancellation and the KNW stake purchase, cash uses are growing. Whether the reorganization translates into earnings improvement will be shown in later quarterly results.

12

Overall view

BGF is a holding company that holds 30.0% of BGF Retail as an associate, and its consolidated results depend heavily on the materials subsidiaries.

On a DART-filed basis, revenue in 2025 was KRW 469.5 billion, the largest year in 2022–2025, but operating profit of KRW 61.2 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 59.1 billion both fell after the 2024 peak.

Revenue, operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders have risen for two consecutive quarters since the first-quarter 2026 low. However, third-quarter 2026 operating profit was down 11.0% year on year, and subsidiary BGF Eco Materials swung to a net loss in the same quarter.

The ownership structure is being simplified through the KNW consolidation, treasury share cancellation and put option acquisition, but the effect has not yet appeared in quarterly results. Total dividends stayed at KRW 12.4 billion in 2025 and the payout ratio fell to 21.0%. The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, due by March 31, 2027.
