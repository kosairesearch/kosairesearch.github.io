Report overview

Genians: Profit Recovery Meets the Subscription Test

Genians' operating profit fell in 2025 but has risen for two consecutive quarters in 2026, and the next checkpoints are the quantum security gateway launch planned within 2026 and the 2026 annual report.

  1. 1

    Cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 8.1 billion, with the operating margin at 20.9% versus 6.9% a year earlier (company announcement, November 12, 2026).

  2. 2

    On a reported basis, 2025 operating profit was KRW 7.01 billion, down from KRW 9.84 billion in 2024, and the operating margin fell to 14.5%.

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Genians is a cybersecurity company whose core businesses are network access control (NAC) and endpoint detection and response (EDR).

Among its main products, Genians Insights E integrates EDR and antivirus on a single endpoint security platform, and Genians Home, a segment-separation solution launched in early 2026, secured a large Seoul-area apartment complex as its first customer, according to the company. Revenue is product-centric.

For the first three quarters of 2026, product revenue, mainly solution supply, was KRW 31.9 billion, or 82.55% of the total, and maintenance and technical support services brought in KRW 6.75 billion.

Over the same period, domestic revenue was KRW 37.37 billion and export revenue was KRW 1.332 billion, so overseas exposure is still small. The customer base spans public institutions, local governments, defense and medical institutions, large manufacturers and financial companies.

The company says it held the top position in the EDR public procurement market for seven consecutive years. Its subscription business is also expanding.

Cumulative cloud NAC customers exceeded 300 companies, and the number of managed detection and response (MDR) customers, which provides EDR as a managed service, has grown.

Overseas, Middle East revenue rose more than 50% in the first half of 2026, and the company added new customers in the United States and Asia-Pacific. In competition, Genians is compared with domestic security software peers such as Ahnlab in the same sector.

The core structural shift is a move from build-to-install products toward a larger share of subscription revenue.

04

Earnings analysis

The reported annual results peaked in 2024. Revenue rose from KRW 38.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 42.9 billion in 2023 (up 11.5% year on year) and KRW 49.6 billion in 2024 (up 15.7%), then fell to KRW 48.4 billion in 2025 (down 2.5%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 6.92 billion in 2022, KRW 6.47 billion in 2023 (down 6.5%), KRW 9.84 billion in 2024 (up 52.2%) and KRW 7.01 billion in 2025 (down 28.8%). The operating margin was 18.0% in 2022, 15.1% in 2023, 19.8% in 2024 and 14.5% in 2025, so it rose in 2024 and fell back in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7.5 billion in 2025, down 31.3% from the prior year. Cash flow moved in the same direction. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 9.79 billion in 2024 to KRW 6.03 billion in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 22.0% in 2024 to 29.2% in 2025.

At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 62.2 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 18.2 billion. The quarterly picture moved differently. Revenue in the third quarter of 2025 was KRW 10.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.07 billion.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, revenue reached KRW 17.2 billion (up 64.8% from the prior quarter) and operating profit reached KRW 4.84 billion (up 352.6%). In the first quarter of 2026, revenue fell to KRW 11.7 billion (down 31.6% from the prior quarter) and operating profit fell to KRW 1.74 billion (down 64.0%).

The company said the margin improvement was large given that the first quarter is seasonally slow for security companies. In the second quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 13.2 billion and operating profit KRW 2.57 billion.

In the third quarter, revenue was KRW 13.8 billion (up 4.5% from the prior quarter and up 32.2% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 3.78 billion (up 47.0% from the prior quarter and up 253.0% year on year).

Revenue and operating profit have risen for two consecutive quarters, from the second quarter through the third quarter of 2026. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 12 billion.

Cumulative revenue of KRW 38.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 8.1 billion for the first three quarters of 2026 are figures the company announced on November 12, 2026.

05

Industry analysis

The end market is in a phase where AI-driven attacks are pushing up demand. The company says that increasingly sophisticated threats, including autonomous hacking using generative AI and fileless ransomware, are accelerating the shift from after-the-fact response to real-time active defense.

The independent research firm ValuFinder projected, in a May 2026 report, that the EDR market would grow more steeply than before.

On the policy side, an amendment to the Personal Information Protection Act that raises the penalty for breaches to 10% of revenue is cited as a factor that could stimulate security spending.

Genians' market position can be summarized as the EDR public procurement lead and NAC wins in public, defense and medical institutions. The company credited large public-sector NAC projects from public institutions and local governments for third-quarter NAC growth.

Because these projects depend on the timing of government orders, quarterly revenue has been volatile, falling from KRW 17.2 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 11.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

Network security integration demand from large manufacturers and overseas site expansion are cited as additional demand sources. From a cycle standpoint, demand for responses to AI threats is rising, but market size forecasts differ across research houses, so they are hard to reduce to a single figure.

Compared with peers, the strengths are the public procurement lead and the cloud NAC customer base, while the overseas revenue share is relatively small. Set against Ahnlab and other sector peers, its public-sector position appears to be the point of difference.

06

Outlook

The company has said it will keep expanding its integrated security platform that combines EDR and zero trust through the second half of 2026, aimed at rising attacks that exploit generative AI.

A quantum security gateway that applies post-quantum cryptography (PQC) has completed core technology development and is targeted for launch within 2026, according to an August 2026 announcement. Genians is pursuing the GSP AI security integrated platform and the quantum security gateway as new businesses.

For the AI security integrated platform, the company plans an operating model that combines threat intelligence (CTI) and MDR services with product supply. Overseas, the company said it passed 200 cumulative overseas customers in the first half of 2026 and is expanding its US local partner network.

Continued growth in the Middle East is another factor the company highlights. In the subscription business, cloud NAC and MDR customer growth is expected to continue. On shareholder returns, the company has said it will maintain a dividend payout ratio of 20% to 25%.

The company has not published a numerical annual target in the materials used for this report, so the outlook is summarized from its stated plans. The nearest public event is the planned launch of the quantum security gateway within 2026, and the next reported financial results will come in the 2026 annual report. No revenue contribution from the new businesses has been disclosed.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly profit recovery

Cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 8.1 billion, with the margin at 20.9% versus 6.9% a year earlier. The company attributes the improvement to combined growth in NAC and EDR.

Subscription customer base

Cloud NAC customers passed 300 companies, and MDR customers have also increased. The company says it is widening the subscription share of a build-to-install business. A larger recurring revenue base could help reduce quarterly revenue swings.

Public-sector lead and AI-threat demand

Rising AI-driven attacks are cited as a driver of demand for real-time active defense. The research firm ValuFinder projected that the EDR market would grow more steeply than before.

09

Bear factors

Annual profit decline in 2025

Operating profit fell 28.8% to KRW 7.01 billion in 2025. The operating margin dropped from 19.8% in 2024 to 14.5%. Operating cash flow also declined from KRW 9.79 billion to KRW 6.03 billion, so the profit decline showed up in cash flow as well.

Quarterly revenue volatility

Revenue fell 31.6% from the prior quarter, from KRW 17.2 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 11.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026. The swing suggests quarterly results are sensitive to the timing of large public-sector orders.

The company describes the first quarter as seasonally slow for security companies, so seasonality and order timing both matter.

Balance sheet and overseas share

The debt ratio rose from 22.0% in 2024 to 29.2% in 2025. For the first three quarters of 2026, export revenue was KRW 1.332 billion against domestic revenue of KRW 37.37 billion. Overseas expansion is underway, but its revenue contribution remains limited.

10

Risk factors

Public orders and timing

NAC growth relies on large projects from public institutions and local governments. If orders cluster in particular quarters, revenue can spike and then fall the following quarter. The swings between the fourth quarter of 2025 and the first quarter of 2026 illustrate this possibility.

Competition and policy

As demand for AI security grows, domestic and foreign security vendors may intensify competition. Tighter regulation such as the Personal Information Protection Act supports demand, but a change in direction could shift spending timing. Competition with peers such as Ahnlab in the public sector also continues.

New-business execution

The quantum security gateway and the GSP AI security integrated platform are new businesses without disclosed revenue contributions. A delay in the target launch within 2026 or slower customer acquisition could affect the durability of the margin gains. Investment costs for these businesses could also weigh on the operating margin.

Overall view

Genians' operating profit peaked in 2024, fell in 2025, and has risen for two consecutive quarters in 2026. Cumulative operating profit of KRW 8.1 billion and an operating margin of 20.9% for the first three quarters of 2026 reflect strong NAC and EDR results.

Cloud NAC customers passing 300 companies and the EDR public procurement lead are cited as strengths. On the other side, the 2025 annual operating margin of 14.5%, the decline in operating cash flow and the rise in the debt ratio to 29.2% are weaknesses.
