02

Business structure

Inventera is a nano-medicine developer founded in November 2018 that develops high-resolution MRI contrast agents and supplies them to hospitals and pharmaceutical companies under a B2B model.

Its core platform is the in-house nanostructure platform Invinity, which the company positions as a differentiator because it uses iron-based materials instead of gadolinium to achieve MRI contrast.

The pipeline spans musculoskeletal, lymphatic and pancreatobiliary areas, and in musculoskeletal imaging the lead candidate is INV-002 (generic name NEMO-103 juu).

INV-002 is a drug dedicated to MR arthrography, and it met both primary and secondary endpoints in its domestic Phase 3 trial, as disclosed in September 2026. The lymphatic contrast agent INV-001 is also in the pipeline.

A pancreatobiliary diagnostic agent received news of US Phase 1 trial plan approval in late October 2026. The CEO has described a model in which Inventera handles everything from original technology to product approval in-house, then partners with capable commercial partners to generate stable revenue.

As of June 2026, a corporate profile service listed the company as clinical-stage and pre-commercial, with no sales recorded.

Segment revenue mix does not apply at this stage, and the main competitive comparison is the iron-based material against gadolinium-based contrast agents, which the company says reduces rare-earth supply-chain risk.

As of June 26, 2026, the largest shareholders were Shin Tae-hyun and one other person with 18.88% and Gong Eun-ah with 11.08%.

05

Industry analysis

Inventera was classified as a pharmaceutical company in the wave of new listings in the first half of 2026, and it listed on KOSDAQ on April 2, 2026. The MRI contrast agent market has relied on gadolinium-based materials, and rare-earth gadolinium supply risk is seen as a structural burden.

Inventera presents its iron-based technology as an alternative that addresses this material supply risk. Its customers are hospitals and pharmaceutical companies, and the company says it earns revenue through both product sales and technology transfer.

In terms of cycle position, the company has completed Phase 3 and is just ahead of commercialization, so whether approval is obtained is the dividing line for entering the revenue stage.

Compared with larger listed peers in the same biopharma sector such as Celltrion and Samsung Biologics, which generate earnings from sales, Inventera is at a different business stage, so comparisons should focus on pipeline progress and approval timing.

INV-002, a musculoskeletal-focused contrast agent, is the first commercial candidate, so differences in indications from existing agents could be a variable in how fast prescriptions spread.

Platform differentiation lies in expanding from diagnostics into therapeutics and antibody-drug conjugates, and how this strategy is positioned within the sector needs further checking.

Outlook

The company said it plans to submit the approval application for INV-002 to the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety within Q4 2026. The CEO said he expects INV-002 sales to begin in earnest from 2027, after approval.

Under the business model, sales after product launch are described as being generated through cooperation with commercial partners.

The independent research firm Aris said in a report dated October 20, 2026 that the company had entered a commercialization phase on completion of INV-002 Phase 3, and it projected a turnaround driven by a global technology transfer in 2028.

Expansion into therapeutics and ADCs is presented as a medium- to long-term direction, with Drug Conjugate and Invinity-ADC research ongoing. The pancreatobiliary contrast agent has received news of US Phase 1 trial plan approval, which is cited as support for pipeline expansion.

The points to check are whether the approval application is completed, the review progress, and the terms of agreements with commercial partners. Given no revenue at present, whether operating losses continue will determine if post-approval sales growth translates into narrower losses.

In the 2026 annual report, the first checkpoint is how the annual operating loss and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders moved compared with KRW 8.6 billion and KRW 8.05 billion in 2025.
