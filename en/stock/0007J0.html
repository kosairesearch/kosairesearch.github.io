02

Business structure

Founded in November 2018, Inventera listed on KOSDAQ in April 2026 under the technology special listing track as a nanomedicine development company.

Built on its proprietary 'Invinity' platform, the company develops iron-based nano-MRI contrast agents designed to reduce the retention and toxicity concerns associated with conventional gadolinium-based agents.

Its core pipeline consists of three candidates: INV-002 (generic name Ferucadextran) for MR arthrography, INV-001 for lymphatic imaging, and INV-003, an oral pancreaticobiliary contrast agent.

In its Korean Phase 3 trial, INV-002 demonstrated statistical superiority over non-contrast MRI on all three primary endpoints—joint distension, contrast and sharpness.

No contrast agent is currently approved specifically for MR arthrography, meaning INV-002 would be the sole approved product for that indication if cleared.

All three pipeline candidates have received US FDA IND clearance, with the lead INV-002 and the lymphatic candidate INV-001 having passed the gateway into US clinical development.

The company's business model, branded 'FIDDO' (Fully Integrated Drug Development Organization), combines licensing-out with self-commercialization.

In March 2024, Inventera signed an agreement with Dongkook Lifescience granting exclusive domestic sales rights for INV-002 and INV-001 along with export registration rights for China, Japan and Southeast Asia, and it separately established a raw-material manufacturing and supply contract with LG Chem.

Earnings analysis

Inventera's operating loss widened from KRW 6.37 billion in 2024 to KRW 8.6 billion in 2025, marking two consecutive years of operating losses from 2024 through 2025.

The controlling-shareholder net loss similarly widened from KRW 6.04 billion in 2024 to KRW 8.05 billion in 2025, extending the same two-year losing streak.

Net income including non-controlling interests mirrored this, posting a net loss of KRW 6.04 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.05 billion in 2025—identical in scale to the controlling-shareholder figures, indicating minimal non-controlling interest impact.

The debt ratio edged up from 5.6% in 2024 to 8.1% in 2025 but remained at a low level. At the end of 2025, total equity stood at KRW 14.3 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, while total liabilities were only KRW 1.15 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The global MRI contrast agent market remains dominated by gadolinium-based products supplied by a handful of multinational pharmaceutical companies.

Persistent safety concerns about gadolinium retention and toxicity in the body have underpinned demand for alternative materials, and Inventera's iron-based nano contrast agents are positioned as one such alternative.

MR arthrography in particular currently has no approved dedicated contrast agent, meaning INV-002 would open a new indication if approved.

Securing US FDA IND clearance across all three pipeline candidates—with lead candidate INV-002 and the lymphatic candidate INV-001 having cleared the gateway into US clinical development—is a relatively uncommon achievement among Korean nanomedicine developers.

Building a dual partnership structure spanning raw material supply, manufacturing and distribution with a major domestic pharmaceutical company (Dongkook Lifescience) and a chemical materials company (LG Chem) is cited as a competitive factor for the commercialization stage.

However, the statistical probability of a clinical-stage drug, including contrast agents, ultimately receiving marketing authorization is known to be around 10%, meaning commercialization timing can shift depending on regulatory review outcomes.

While the contrast agent market itself continues to grow steadily, competitive substitution against incumbent gadolinium suppliers and reimbursement pricing remain commercialization variables to monitor.

06

Outlook

Having obtained the final clinical study report for INV-002's Korean Phase 3 trial, the company stated it plans to file for marketing authorization with the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS) in the fourth quarter of 2026 and to pursue domestic commercialization in 2027.

The lymphatic candidate INV-001 has received US Phase 2a IND clearance and is proceeding in parallel with a domestic Phase 2a trial. The oral pancreaticobiliary contrast agent INV-003 received US FDA Phase 1 IND clearance in October 2026, with a disclosed expected completion date of October 17, 2027.

As of the company's securities registration filing (March 2026), it had not yet signed any licensing-out agreement for its pipeline.

Company projections disclosed around the same time outlined revenue of KRW 4.2 billion and an operating loss of KRW 9.7 billion in 2027, before turning profitable in 2028 with revenue of KRW 25.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 7.2 billion, and reaching revenue of KRW 37.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 22.2 billion (a 59% operating margin) in 2029 as a mid-to-long-term target.

These targets are not confirmed figures, as they are contingent on clinical outcomes, approval timing, and whether additional licensing-out deals are concluded. Hanyang Securities issued a report on September 16, 2026 giving Inventera a 'Not Rated' investment opinion.

Going forward, the key variables are the actual timing of INV-002's approval and whether additional licensing-out agreements are signed.

Overall view

Inventera has secured US FDA IND clearance across all three of its pipeline candidates based on its differentiated iron-based nano-MRI contrast agent technology, and its lead candidate INV-002 demonstrated statistical superiority on all primary endpoints in its Korean Phase 3 trial, positioning the company for a marketing authorization filing in the fourth quarter of 2026.

Before forming a view, it is worth sequentially tracking the actual INV-002 marketing authorization filing and review outcome alongside the clinical progress of INV-001 and INV-003.
