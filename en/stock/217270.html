02

Business structure

Neptune Company runs two pillars: a game business and an ad-tech (advertising platform) business. Its largest shareholder is Krafton, and for 2026 the company set entering the Indian ad-tech market and building a hybrid casual game business as its priorities.

Hybrid casual games combine in-app purchase and in-app advertising revenue models, and the company plans to pair in-house development with a stronger publishing push. The game portfolio includes in-house titles, subsidiary titles and third-party titles it publishes.

In Q1 2026, its published title Erpis generated revenue of KRW 2.35 billion, while a subsidiary posted revenue of KRW 10.35 billion.

On October 29, 2026, the company launched Geomsin Zero, a martial-arts RPG from a Chinese studio, globally on Google Play, the Apple App Store and One Store, with a Steam expansion planned afterward.

The ad-tech business works as an advertising trading platform, and the company said its ad-tech platform revenue grew 7% from the prior quarter in Q3 2025 despite a weak market. The Indian subsidiary was slated to be set up in the first half of 2026, with major titles adopting ad-tech in the second half.

In mobile casual and hybrid casual genres, the company competes for users and advertisers with listed peers such as Netmarble, Com2us and Wemade. Over the longer term, the company aims to become a full-stack ad-tech company.

04

Earnings analysis

The defining feature of Neptune Company's results is that profit swung between gains and losses while revenue scaled up.

Annual revenue went from KRW 29.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 99.7 billion in 2023 (up 238.5% YoY), then KRW 121.6 billion in 2024 (up 21.9%) and KRW 122.5 billion in 2025 (up 0.8%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 28.7 billion in 2022 to an operating profit of KRW 2.16 billion in 2023, rose to KRW 9.61 billion in 2024 (up 345.6%), then fell 74.1% to KRW 2.49 billion in 2025.

The operating margin went from -97.3% in 2022 to 2.2% in 2023 and 7.9% in 2024, before easing to 2.0% in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 1.95 billion in 2024, but returned to a loss of KRW 40.2 billion in 2025.

The wide gap between operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income suggests non-operating items weighed heavily that year. Operating cash flow stayed positive at KRW 1.46 billion in 2023, KRW 21.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.91 billion in 2025.

At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 316 billion, controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 316.1 billion and total liabilities were KRW 43.4 billion, with the debt ratio at 13.7%, the highest of the four years.

Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 569 million in Q3 2025 turned into an operating loss of KRW 1.59 billion in Q4 2025, then returned to an operating profit of KRW 1.72 billion in Q1 2026. Operating losses of KRW 946 million in Q2 2026 and KRW 2.37 billion in Q3 2026 followed, making two straight loss-making quarters.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 39.4 billion rose 48.2% from the prior quarter, yet a reported KRW 17.5 billion in advertising expenses lifted operating costs over the same period. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 48.5 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The hybrid casual format Neptune Company has prioritized pairs in-app purchases with in-app advertising, so one title can generate payment and ad revenue at the same time. Results in this model tend to depend on in-house development capability and the ability to select and publish third-party titles.

A large share of the company's key new releases rests on publishing deals with Chinese developers. Erpis is a title whose Chinese developer, Yungui, signed a publishing agreement with Neptune in July 2025.

Geomsin Zero comes from the Chinese studio behind the martial-arts RPG Hanga Gangho, which reported 7 million global downloads and KRW 45 billion in revenue, and the studio's Sand of Salzar, which sold more than 2 million copies on Steam.

Those results belong to the developer and are separate from Neptune's reported earnings. Compared with other listed game companies, Neptune combines game development and publishing with an advertising platform in one structure.

Its entry into the Indian ad-tech market relies, in the company's own account, on local assets held by its parent Krafton.

06

Outlook

The company's 2026 plan, announced at the start of the year, called for 16 new titles to launch in sequence during the first half, including seven hybrid casual games and nine titles in other genres.

In its Q1 2026 results announcement, the company said it would launch more than 10 mobile games in Q2, including in-house and subsidiary titles. Geomsin Zero went live globally on October 29, 2026, and the company plans to expand it to Steam later. No fixed date for the Steam expansion was given.

The Indian ad-tech plan was to complete the local subsidiary's setup in the first half and apply ad-tech to major titles in the second half. The company cited strategic partnerships with major Indian media and the phased expansion of its ad-tech business as growth drivers after Q2.

However, advertising expenses of KRW 17.5 billion, a large share of Q3 2026 operating costs, show that marketing spend is weighing on profitability. The next checkpoint is therefore whether lower advertising intensity lets the operating margin return to positive territory. Over the longer term, the company has framed its goal as becoming a full-stack ad-tech company.

Bull factors

New title pipeline and Steam expansion

The company planned 16 new titles for the first half of 2026 and more than 10 mobile launches in Q2. Geomsin Zero went live globally on October 29, 2026 across Google Play, the Apple App Store and One Store, with a Steam expansion announced. The model widens the release pipeline by publishing titles from Chinese developers.

Ad-tech and India expansion

Ad-tech platform revenue grew 7% from the prior quarter in Q3 2025. In 2026 the company planned to pursue the Indian ad-tech market through a local subsidiary, using assets from its parent Krafton. It has set a medium-term goal of becoming a full-stack ad-tech company.

Earnings recovery trend

Operating profit rose from an operating loss of KRW 28.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.61 billion in 2024, and returned to a KRW 1.72 billion operating profit in Q1 2026. Operating cash flow stayed positive from 2023 through 2025. The debt ratio rose to 13.7% in 2025 from 12.3% in 2022, but remains at a modest level relative to equity.

Bear factors

Q3 loss returns

Q3 2026 posted an operating loss of KRW 2.37 billion, turning a year-earlier profit into a loss, and the second straight loss-making quarter followed. Reported advertising expenses of KRW 17.5 billion made up a large share of operating costs over the same period. Whether marketing spend keeps offsetting revenue gains is the key bearish factor.

Earnings volatility and cumulative losses

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 40.2 billion in 2025, and totaled KRW 48.5 billion over the last four quarters. The company returned to a loss within a year of its 2024 return to profit. The wide gap between operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income means earnings remain unstable.

Slowing revenue growth

Annual revenue growth slowed from 21.9% in 2024 to 0.8% in 2025. Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 26.6 billion fell 15.3% from the prior quarter. Q1 2026 revenue was also reported to have declined from a year earlier.

Risk factors

Variability in new title performance

New releases are the core driver of results, and with plans for 16 titles in the first half and more than 10 in Q2, performance variance across titles can flow straight into earnings. Geomsin Zero launched only recently, so few figures yet show its scale. Some title launch schedules were adjusted in 2025, so whether releases proceed on plan is also a variable.

Marketing cost structure

Advertising expenses of KRW 17.5 billion in Q3 2026 lifted operating costs and contributed to the operating loss. Heavier ad spending during launch-heavy periods can destabilize the earnings structure. If ad efficiency falls, operating losses may persist even as revenue grows.

Change in controlling shareholder and partner dependence

After Krafton became the largest shareholder in 2025, the company went through a post-merger integration process and adjusted some title launch schedules. Because a high share of its titles comes from Chinese developers, partner development timelines and performance feed into results. The Indian ad-tech business is new, so the pace at which it shows up in earnings is an open variable.

Overall view

Neptune Company's Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 394 billion shows the top line is still growing. However, an operating loss of KRW 2.37 billion extended the streak of loss-making quarters to two, and higher advertising expenses were reported as a driver.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 48.5 billion over the last four quarters, and how many quarters the Q1 2026 profit can be sustained is not yet settled. Positive factors include the planned new releases, the global launch of Geomsin Zero and the push into Indian ad tech.

Negative factors include uneven performance across new titles, the burden of advertising spend and earnings volatility, with a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 40.2 billion in 2025.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x while no earnings-based multiple can be calculated, so readings differ depending on whether the share price is anchored to net assets or to earnings.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report, and the key question is whether the operating margin stays in positive territory.
