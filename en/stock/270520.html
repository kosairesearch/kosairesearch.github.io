Geothermal Core Shrinking as Aptn Pivots Toward Data Centers

Aptn's geothermal heating and cooling revenue has declined for two straight years and losses have persisted for four years, while the company pursues a data-center platform and a free capital reduction to reshape both its business and capital structure.

02

Business structure

Aptn designs and builds geothermal and hydrothermal heating and cooling systems, and its technology scope also includes fuel cell systems. Geothermal heating and cooling is positioned as an energy-efficiency technology for building climate control, while fuel cells are used as a clean power generation technology.

Its core revenue decline reflects both lower geothermal heating and cooling sales and higher raw material prices. On top of this base, the company is expanding into lithium, brain disease therapeutics and digital asset businesses.

In the digital asset area, it announced an agreement to acquire the coin exchange Bitkmon, a Korean crypto exchange. In May 2026 it announced a strategic technology partnership with the defense and industrial AI company MROD.

The two companies plan to combine Aptn's geothermal and cooling infrastructure technology with MROD's on-device AI CCTV and AI predictive diagnostics to build a smart data-center operations platform.

The model aims to move beyond a construction-centered structure toward infrastructure with real-time AI-based equipment monitoring and autonomous operations management. Aptn is a subsidiary of Aprogen (ticker 007460).

Consolidated borrowings at the end of 2025 were KRW 62.3 billion against cash and equivalents of KRW 9.8 billion.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue rose from KRW 17.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 20.4 billion in 2023, up 16.2% year on year, and then declined two straight years to KRW 18.4 billion in 2024, down 10.0%, and KRW 16.9 billion in 2025, down 8.1%. Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating losses were KRW 3.13 billion in 2022, KRW 6.55 billion in 2023, KRW 12.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 11.2 billion in 2025, a four-year run of losses. The loss widened sharply in 2024 and narrowed in 2025. The operating margin was -17.8% in 2022, -32.1% in 2023, -69.4% in 2024 and -66.0% in 2025.

The widening losses were linked to lower geothermal heating and cooling sales combined with higher raw material prices, which turned gross profit into a loss. The 2025 narrowing is attributed to cost reduction, including reduced investment in new businesses.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.25 billion in 2022, KRW 5 billion in 2023, KRW 19.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 23.4 billion in 2025. Net loss including non-controlling interests shows the same amounts.

Operating cash flow moved from a KRW 1.15 billion inflow in 2022 to outflows of KRW -4.68 billion in 2023, KRW -10.2 billion in 2024 and KRW -40 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 5.17 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 2.32 billion in the fourth quarter (down 55.1% from the prior quarter), KRW 4.54 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 95.6%), KRW 3.37 billion in the second quarter and KRW 2.44 billion in the third quarter.

Quarterly operating losses have persisted for five consecutive quarters since the third quarter of 2025, and the third-quarter 2026 loss of KRW 2.94 billion was larger than in the prior quarter.

The four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 total KRW 26.9 billion in net losses attributable to controlling shareholders. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 95.2 billion, up from KRW 80.4 billion in 2024, while paid-in capital rose over the same period.

05

Industry analysis

Aptn's core business is a building heating and cooling efficiency technology, and demand in that market depends on renewable energy policy and building equipment investment.

Because the company itself is searching for new growth engines to offset weakness in its core business, the pace of recovery in its core market largely determines its earnings direction. In new businesses, data-center cooling and equipment management demand connects with AI infrastructure investment.

The company has presented a solution that uses AI CCTV to monitor geothermal cooling systems and power equipment around the clock and detect anomalies. Whether this leads to actual orders from data-center operators has not yet appeared in reported results.

The digital asset exchange business, meanwhile, has earnings that vary widely with trading volume and market sentiment. Listed biopharma companies in the same exchange sector classification have revenue models built on clinical and licensing activity, so their revenue structure differs greatly from Aptn's.

For peer comparison, construction and equipment installers and renewable equipment companies are closer to Aptn's business structure. On profitability, Aptn's operating margin has stayed negative, so comparisons with equipment peers would first require evidence of a return to profit and improved cost ratios.

06

Outlook

Based on confirmed facts, Aptn's path has three strands. First, whether the partnership with MROD announced in May 2026 becomes a commercial smart data-center operations platform will largely determine the success of its new business. Second, whether the decline in geothermal heating and cooling revenue reverses.

Revenue fell in the second and third quarters of 2026 after peaking at KRW 4.54 billion in the first quarter. Third, capital structure adjustment.

Shareholders approved a share consolidation and articles amendment at the March 2026 general meeting, and on November 13, 2026, the board resolved a free capital reduction consolidating ten common shares into one.

The company disclosed that the reduction aims to offset accumulated deficits, and paid-in capital will shrink as a result. The company announced an agreement to acquire the coin exchange Bitkmon, but completion and the timing of any contribution to results have not been confirmed by disclosure.

Concrete timelines and figures for the data-center business have not yet been presented. The key observation points are therefore when and how much new-business revenue appears in reported figures, and whether the 2026 annual report separates new-business revenue.

08

Bull factors

Narrowing losses in 2025

Operating loss narrowed to KRW 11.2 billion in 2025 from KRW 12.8 billion in 2024, and the operating margin improved from -69.4% to -66.0%. Cost reduction and lower new-business investment are cited as contributing factors.

Quarterly net losses attributable to controlling shareholders also narrowed from the prior quarter in some periods of 2026.

Signs of balance-sheet normalization

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 155.1% in 2024 to 68.2% in 2025, with total liabilities at KRW 64.9 billion at year-end 2025. Total equity rose to KRW 95.2 billion over the same period. Lower liabilities and higher equity can be read as a positive change in financial capacity.

Data-center option in new businesses

In May 2026 Aptn partnered with MROD to plan a smart data-center operations platform that combines geothermal cooling with AI-based equipment monitoring. The strategy aims to move away from a construction-centered model toward a higher-value infrastructure model. If it reaches revenue, it could offset weakness in the core business.

Bear factors

Widening operating cash outflow

The outflow widened each year, leaving 2025 operating cash flow far from the 2022 inflow. If cash keeps leaving the business, financial strain accumulates even when book equity holds. Consolidated borrowings at the end of 2025 were around KRW 62.3 billion.

Revenue decline and cost pressure

Lower geothermal heating and cooling sales combined with higher raw material prices reportedly turned gross profit into a loss. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 2.44 billion fell 52.9% from a year earlier. When revenue shrinks while cost pressure persists, narrowing the operating loss becomes harder.

Accumulated losses and capital restructuring

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have persisted for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, and the last four quarters total KRW 26.9 billion in losses. The free capital reduction to offset accumulated deficits shows the scale of the cumulative losses. Until new businesses fill the gap, further capital restructuring cannot be ruled out.

Risk factors

Liquidity and financial risk

Operating cash outflow widened to KRW -40 billion in 2025 and borrowing obligations remain. If cash outflows continue, additional financing may be needed, which could bring shareholder dilution or further capital adjustments. Cash flow and debt maturity structure in the next annual report are the points to check.

New-business execution risk

The data-center platform and the crypto exchange acquisition appear to be at announcement stage, and timing and scale of revenue contribution have not been confirmed by disclosure. If new-business investment fails to offset the decline in the core business, losses could continue. The digital asset business can see large swings in results depending on trading volume and market conditions.

Capital structure and listing-related changes

Shareholders approved a share par value consolidation and articles amendment in March 2026, and a free capital reduction consolidating ten common shares into one has been resolved ahead of the December 15, 2026 record date.

Changes in share count and paid-in capital can alter how trading conditions and shareholdings are read, so disclosures after the reduction should be checked.

Overall view

Aptn recorded operating losses for four consecutive years while its core geothermal heating and cooling revenue declined under cost pressure, and the loss narrowed in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 68.2% at the end of 2025 and total equity rose to KRW 95.2 billion, but operating cash outflow widened to KRW -40 billion. In 2026, quarterly revenue declined after peaking in the first quarter, and the third-quarter operating loss widened from the prior quarter.
