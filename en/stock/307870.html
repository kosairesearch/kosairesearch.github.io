02

Business structure

B2En is a software developer and supplier built on AI and big data technology, listed on KOSDAQ in December 2018. Its core products are the SDQ (Smart Data Quality) data quality management solution, the SPORTAL data integration portal, and the HYPERGLORY retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) platform for enterprises.

SDQ carries a function that automatically recommends and diagnoses data quality check items for public institutions and local governments, and the company has filed two patent applications on its automated diagnosis technology.

HYPERGLORY received both the KTR Mark AI certification and a Grade 1 GS certification, and the company has designated 2026 as the year its broader rollout begins. Its main customers are described as public-sector bodies and large enterprises, with data governance and AI adoption demand at the core of the business.

Overseas, it works with the domestic AI chip company Rebellions and a consortium partner on an AI-based digital administration project for the Mongolian Customs Office.

New initiatives include development of a marketing system for Binance Pay, an ERP project based on Odoo, and medical care services spanning silver care and emergency care. The October 8, 2026 listing of NC& Co. new shares added automotive video devices and video processing chips to the business mix.

At the March 2026 annual shareholders meeting, power and energy businesses were approved as additional corporate purposes. Segment revenue is not broken out here; the business mix is described by product and project.

Competitively, the company sits between large IT service providers such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever and smaller data solution firms.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 27.1 billion in 2022 to a peak of KRW 32 billion in 2023 (up 18.3% year on year), then fell to KRW 25.1 billion in 2024 (down 21.6%) and KRW 14.6 billion in 2025 (down 41.7%). 2025 was the smallest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

The company attributed the wider operating loss to the revenue decline. The operating loss was KRW 2.19 billion in 2022, KRW 2.46 billion in 2023, KRW 2.65 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.75 billion in 2025, a loss in each of four consecutive years, with the largest widening in 2025.

The operating margin moved from -8.1% in 2022 and -7.7% in 2023 to -10.5% in 2024 and -46.1% in 2025. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.22 billion in 2022, KRW 12.6 billion in 2023, KRW 6.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 12 billion in 2025, narrowing in 2024 before widening again.

Net loss including non-controlling interests reached KRW 12.2 billion in 2025, up from KRW 6.93 billion in 2024, and the company cited higher interest expense and other non-operating costs. Operating cash outflow was KRW 5.69 billion in 2025, the largest of the four years shown.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 131.1% in 2022 to 45.0% in 2025, and at year-end 2025 total equity was KRW 31.4 billion with total liabilities of KRW 14.2 billion.

Quarterly revenue ran KRW 3.91 billion, KRW 4.32 billion, KRW 2.64 billion and KRW 3.75 billion through the four quarters of 2025, then KRW 2.76 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 2.14 billion in the first quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.38 billion in the first quarter of 2026, yet the company has posted losses for five straight quarters.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 4.75 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and shrank to KRW 396 million in the first quarter of 2026. The total for the last four quarters was KRW 9.56 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In the public sector, the company says the maturing of the national public data quality diagnosis and evaluation system is raising demand for automated data quality tools.

Smaller local governments with few IT staff, however, find evaluation responses difficult, so tailoring SDQ to small institutions is presented as a path to market expansion.

In AI chips, the domestic NPU company Rebellions is pursuing an IPO while expanding overseas, and B2En is linked to that ecosystem through its fund investment and the Mongolian Customs Office project.

In automotive vision, NC& Co. handles in-vehicle video devices and Nextchip, in which NC& Co. holds a stake, works on automotive video processing and recognition chips, giving B2En's data and AI technology a point of contact with both.

Domestic IT services are led by large system integrators such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever along with platform companies. B2En focuses on a niche that combines data quality and governance with AI.

Its revenue scale and order continuity remain far smaller than those of the large integrators, and the automotive vision and chip businesses are still at an early commercialization stage.

06

Outlook

The company has designated 2026 as the year its HYPERGLORY platform begins wider rollout and plans to extend its RAG technology into an AI service supporting work processes across organizations.

In early 2026 it was reported to have signed a platform development contract with Binance, under which B2En is building the marketing system for Binance Pay in Korea.

In June 2026 it entered a top-priority strategic partnership with the open-source ERP company Odoo to pursue ERP implementation and AI-driven operational projects for domestic firms. It has also signed a strategic MOU with Megazone Cloud on a cloud-based generative AI platform.

In healthcare, it joined an AI-based digital transformation consortium at Central Veterans Hospital to build a one-stop emergency response system, extending its scope from silver care to emergency care. Overseas, the Mongolian Customs Office AI project runs over two years and continues through 2026.

After the NC& Co. acquisition, the company said it will first examine commercialization in areas linked to existing businesses, such as commercial vehicle safety management, in-vehicle video data analysis and AI video control, and over the medium to long term it plans to expand into industrial AI for robots, drones and smart factories.

Concrete joint projects and revenue scale have not yet been set, which makes commercialization results the key variable.

Of the KRW 10 billion convertible bond, KRW 8.5 billion is earmarked for operating funds used for AX business upgrades and basic big data operations, with planned use of KRW 1 billion in 2026, KRW 5 billion in 2027 and KRW 2.5 billion from 2028 onward.

08

Bull factors

An AI, Vision and Chip Value Chain

New NC& Co. shares listed on October 8, 2026, giving B2En a 27.73% stake, or 44.36% including related parties. The company envisions linking NC& Co.'s in-vehicle video devices and Nextchip's video processing chips with its data and AI technology across collection, processing and analysis.

It has named commercial vehicle safety management, vehicle video data analysis and AI video control as priority areas.

Public Data Quality Demand and Certifications

The company said SDQ became the first to include a function that automatically recommends and diagnoses data quality check items for public institutions and local governments.

Two patent applications were filed on the automated diagnosis technology, and the upgrading of the national public data quality evaluation system is cited as the demand backdrop. The HYPERGLORY RAG platform received the KTR Mark AI certification and a Grade 1 GS certification.

Overseas Public-Sector AI Experience

B2En is taking part in an AI-based digital administration project for the Mongolian Customs Office, combining Rebellions' server AI chips with its own AI and data technology. The consortium completed the first-year demonstration in December 2025, and the two-year project runs through 2026.

The company intends to use this experience to pursue opportunities in overseas projects for domestic AI and chip firms.

09

Bear factors

Two Straight Years of Revenue Decline

Annual revenue fell from the KRW 32 billion peak in 2023 to KRW 25.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.6 billion in 2025, a decline in two consecutive years. First-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 2.76 billion was 29.3% lower than a year earlier. The company cited the operating loss increase tied to lower sales as the main driver of the swing.

Weaker Operating Margin and Cash Flow

The operating margin reached -46.1% in 2025, far outside the -7.7% to -10.5% range of 2022 to 2024. Operating cash outflow of KRW 5.69 billion in 2025 was the largest of the four years.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed to KRW 396 million in the first quarter of 2026, but the four-quarter total stands at KRW 9.56 billion.

Dilution From Convertible Bonds

The KRW 10 billion seventh private convertible bond approved in November 2026 carries a 2.0% coupon and a 6.0% maturity yield, maturing on November 20, 2029. Full conversion would issue 9,852,216 common shares, equal to 11.71% of total shares. Previously issued convertible bonds still outstanding total KRW 16.5 billion and add to the dilution picture.

10

Risk factors

Financing and Dilution Risk

Operating cash outflow reached KRW 5.69 billion in 2025, and the company is now issuing convertible bonds to secure operating funds. Of the proceeds, KRW 8.5 billion goes to operating funds and KRW 1.5 billion to debt repayment.

In January 2026 the company also withdrew a decision to sell treasury convertible bonds by mutual agreement, so the stability of its funding plans is an item to watch.

Governance and Management Restructuring Risk

A change of largest shareholder in April 2026 is moving the company from its former controlling group's management structure to an independent one. The new largest shareholder fund holds 23.05% of B2En, and a one-year lock-up has been completed on both existing and newly issued shares.

At the March 2026 annual meeting one executive director was newly appointed, and the board has ten directors, three of them outside directors.

Execution Risk in New Businesses

Concrete collaboration projects and revenue scale linked to NC& Co. and Nextchip have not yet been set. Automotive video and chip businesses face high entry barriers in automotive supply chains, which can lengthen the time to commercialization.

With core data and AI revenue declining from 2024 into 2025, whether the new businesses translate into earnings contribution is the key task.

12

Overall view

B2En's revenue fell in both 2024 and 2025 after its 2023 peak, its operating loss persisted in each year from 2022 through 2025, and the widening was largest in 2025. In the first quarter of 2026 the operating loss was the smallest of the last five quarters, but revenue fell 29.3% year on year.

On the business side, the 27.73% stake in NC& Co. secured in October 2026 underpins a structure linking automotive video and chips with AI and data, alongside a public data quality solution and an overseas public-sector AI reference.

On the other side, revenue scale from the NC& Co.-linked businesses has not yet been set, and the KRW 10 billion convertible bond approved in November 2026 adds dilution equal to 11.71% of total shares. Governance is being restructured after the change of largest shareholder in April 2026.

Bullish factors are the new business structure, public-sector AI references and the narrowing quarterly losses; bearish factors are continued revenue decline, a weaker operating margin and funding pressure.

The next checkpoints are the convertible bond payment on November 20, 2026, the completion of the Mongolia project by end-2026, and whether revenue recovers and NC& Co. consolidation effects appear in the 2026 annual report.
