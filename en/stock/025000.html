Q3 2026 Swings to a Loss as Sales Recover but Profit Diverges

KPX Chemical's Q3 2026 revenue rose for a third straight quarter to KRW 264.3 billion, yet operating profit fell to KRW 9.99 billion and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 27.2 billion. The gap between sales recovery and profit is the central issue.

02

Business structure

KPX Chemical was founded in 1974 and became the first company in Korea to begin producing polypropylene glycol (PPG).

The company has manufactured and sold PPG and electronic materials, but in December 2025 it split off its electronic materials division into a separate company, which narrowed the business structure around PPG.

As of March 2026, PPG, PU resin and chemical products accounted for 86% of sales, while merchandise made up 14%. PPG is a petrochemical intermediate used in products such as polyurethane, so results are affected by the conditions and economic cycles of construction and industrial customers.

The company has concentrated its R&D on low-density high-quality PPG, PPG designed to reduce VOCs and odor, and flame-retardant PPG for insulation. R&D spending equaled 0.58% of sales in 2025, lower than the 0.66% recorded in 2024.

Overseas, it has manufacturing or sales subsidiaries in China, the United States, India, Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia and Malaysia. The largest shareholder is KPX Holdings, with a 58.25% stake as of October 28, 2026, and the free-float ratio was 34.19% as of March 2026.

Compared with listed chemical peers such as LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions, Kumho Petro Chemical and Lotte Chemical, KPX Chemical is a mid-sized company by sales scale.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show sales and profit declining together. Revenue fell from KRW 983.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 911.1 billion in 2023, a 7.4% decline, then recovered slightly to KRW 913.4 billion in 2024, a 0.2% increase, before dropping to KRW 808.4 billion in 2025, an 11.5% decline and the smallest figure of 2022–2025.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 58.4 billion in 2023, a 25.5% increase, then fell to KRW 45.4 billion in 2024 (down 22.2%) and KRW 27.4 billion in 2025 (down 39.7%), declining for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

The operating margin was 4.7% in 2022, 6.4% in 2023, 5.0% in 2024 and 3.4% in 2025, the lowest of the period. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 64.9 billion in 2023, a 126.8% increase, then slipped to KRW 64.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 50.6 billion in 2025, a 21.1% decline.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 51.6 billion in 2025 and differs from the controlling-shareholder figure, so the two should be read separately. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 59 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 78.3 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q4 2025 marked the low point, with revenue of KRW 160.2 billion (down 25.2% from the prior quarter) and an operating loss of KRW 4.63 billion. The company returned to operating profit of KRW 5.44 billion in Q1 2026.

In Q2 2026, revenue reached KRW 245 billion, operating profit rose to KRW 23 billion (up 323.7% from the prior quarter) and net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 61 billion (up 484.0%).

In Q3 2026, revenue grew to KRW 264.3 billion (up 23.4% year on year), but operating profit was KRW 9.99 billion (down 16.4% year on year) and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 27.2 billion, a swing into loss.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 53.8 billion. The debt ratio rose to 31.6% at the end of 2025 from 25.6% in 2024.

05

Industry analysis

PPG is a petrochemical intermediate, and its results move with the conditions and economic cycles of industrial customers such as construction and manufacturing. According to a earnings report by Thinkpool, Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 160.2 billion was the lowest of the last three years, 25% below the level of Q2 2025.

Revenue then rose for three consecutive quarters from Q1 to Q3 2026, which can be read as a sign of demand recovery. Operating profit over the same period, however, peaked at KRW 23 billion in Q2 and fell to KRW 9.99 billion in Q3, so the sales recovery did not carry through to profit.

Raw material prices, including the main inputs PO and EO, also affect product margins and should be considered as a source of earnings volatility. Among chemical peers, large petrochemical companies such as LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions, Kumho Petro Chemical and Lotte Chemical generally have broader product portfolios.

KPX Chemical is a mid-sized company concentrated in PPG and PU-related products, so its results are more directly tied to demand and pricing in those product lines.

Its overseas subsidiaries in China, the United States, India, Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia and Malaysia may partly spread regional demand differences across markets.

Operating profit fell from KRW 23 billion in Q2 2026 to KRW 9.99 billion in Q3, while revenue continued to grow to KRW 264.3 billion, so the next checkpoint is whether higher sales translate into a better operating margin.

Shareholders' equity was KRW 665.3 billion at the end of 2025, of which KRW 656.2 billion was attributable to controlling shareholders. Total liabilities were KRW 210.1 billion, and the debt ratio rose to 31.6%, so whether this level climbs further is worth monitoring.

Bear factors

Annual operating profit down for two years

Operating profit peaked at KRW 58.4 billion in 2023, then fell to KRW 45.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 27.4 billion in 2025, declining for two consecutive years. The 2025 operating margin of 3.4% was the lowest of 2022–2025. Revenue was also the smallest of the period at KRW 808.4 billion, so both sales and profitability weakened.

Q3 operating profit decline and swing to a loss

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 9.99 billion, down 56.6% from the prior quarter and down 16.4% year on year. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 27.2 billion, turning negative from a year earlier. The decline in profit despite higher sales is cited as a weakness.

Rising debt ratio

The debt ratio moved from 30.5% in 2022 and 27.2% in 2023 to 25.6% in 2024 before rising to 31.6% in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 210.1 billion. With operating profit declining while the debt ratio rose, the change in financial capacity could act as a weakness.

Risk factors

Demand and industry risk

PPG is an intermediate product, so results are tied to demand from construction and manufacturing customers. Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 160.2 billion was the lowest level of the last three years. If demand weakens again, the sales recovery may not carry through to profit.

Earnings volatility and non-operating items

Quarterly operating profit swung from an operating loss of KRW 4.63 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 23 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 9.99 billion in Q3. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 61 billion in Q2 to a loss of KRW 27.2 billion in Q3, a gap from operating profit that widened. If earnings depend heavily on non-operating items, predictability could decline.

Ownership concentration and free float

The largest shareholder, KPX Holdings, held 58.25% as of October 28, 2026. The free-float ratio was 34.19% as of March 2026, so the share of stock available for general trading is limited.

In this structure, changes in the largest shareholder's stake and trades among minority holders may have a sizable effect on the share price.

Overall view

KPX Chemical is a mid-sized chemical company centered on PPG and PU-related products, and both sales and operating profit declined together in 2025.

In 2026, revenue rose for three consecutive quarters to KRW 197.7 billion in Q1, KRW 245 billion in Q2 and KRW 264.3 billion in Q3, but operating profit fell from KRW 23 billion in Q2 to KRW 9.99 billion in Q3. Q3 also brought a swing to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 27.2 billion.

Total equity of KRW 665.3 billion, the cash dividend approved in March 2026 and the larger stake held by the largest shareholder are the positive facts. On the other side, the debt ratio rose to 31.6% at the end of 2025 from 25.6% in 2024, and the 2025 operating margin of 3.4% was the lowest of the period.

The strengths are the sales recovery and the equity base, while the weaknesses are the decline in operating profit and the large swings in quarterly earnings. The fact that PPG demand is tied to construction and industrial conditions also needs to be considered.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report and whether operating profit recovers in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
