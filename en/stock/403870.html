02

Business structure

HPSP manufactures and sells high-pressure hydrogen annealing (HPA) equipment used in front-end semiconductor processes. Annealing is a heat treatment that reduces defects at wafer surfaces and interfaces to stabilize the electrical characteristics of devices.

HPSP's tools use hydrogen or deuterium gas under high pressure to perform this work at relatively low temperatures. The equipment combines a chamber that processes wafers with high-pressure gas control, temperature control and automated transfer.

Its customers are split between foundry (non-memory) and memory companies, and in a patent filing HPSP argued that it supplies equipment to Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and TSMC. KB Securities said in a July 2026 report that the application range of HPA tools is widening from logic and foundry toward memory.

The same report noted a lag of about six months between an equipment order and revenue recognition. A 2023 Samsung Securities report assumed parts and service revenue at about 7% of equipment sales and expected high-pressure oxide equipment to generate revenue from 2024. Equipment sales dominate the mix, so individual tool orders can move quarterly revenue considerably.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales rose from KRW 159.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 179.1 billion in 2023 (up 12.4% year on year) and KRW 181.4 billion in 2024 (up 1.3%), then fell 4.7% to KRW 173 billion in 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year for sales and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 85.2 billion in 2022 to a peak of KRW 95.2 billion in 2023 (up 11.8%), then declined 1.3% to KRW 93.9 billion in 2024 and 4.3% to KRW 89.9 billion in 2025, two consecutive years of decline.

The operating margin moved within the low-to-mid 50% range, from 53.5% in 2022 to 52.0% in 2025, and did not move sharply even as sales stayed roughly flat after 2023.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 66 billion in 2022 to KRW 86.3 billion in 2024, the largest year in the period, before falling 15.8% to KRW 72.7 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed at the same amounts as this figure for each year.

Operating cash flow reached KRW 100.2 billion in 2025, the largest in the period, and rose even in the year profit declined.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 32 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 52.7 billion in the fourth quarter (up 64.4% from the prior quarter), then fell to KRW 31.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 39.4%).

Sales then rose for two consecutive quarters, to KRW 46.1 billion in the second quarter and KRW 51.6 billion in the third. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 24.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then declined for two consecutive quarters, to KRW 24.1 billion and KRW 16.5 billion.

KB Securities viewed the first quarter of 2026 as the low point, with customer investment execution gradually expanding.

05

Industry analysis

In a July 2026 report, KB Securities said HPSP could benefit from leading-edge process conversion investments by global foundry and memory makers. The same report argued that process shrinkage and the shift to gate-all-around structures raise demand for controlling interface defects.

On the memory side, expanding investment by DRAM customers was cited as a basis for expected growth in equipment units. A 2023 Samsung Securities report assumed the 2026 purchase market would be more than three times the 2023 level as DRAM 1c adoption began, but that is a scenario from 2023.

On the competitive side, a domestic rival, Yesti, said it shipped 75 high-pressure hydrogen annealing tools to a global foundry customer in March 2026 and had won a 125-unit order from another global chipmaker for August delivery. Press coverage said HPSP's exclusivity had effectively been broken as a result.

In the patent dispute, the two sides fought over a chamber opening and closing patent in cancellation suits, and in February 2026 the patent tribunal accepted HPSP's correction request for that patent. As of an April 2026 report, the Patent Court's ruling was scheduled for June 2026.

Because customer investment timing determines quarterly results in this equipment business, the conversion of investment expansion since the first quarter of 2026 into revenue is the key variable to watch.

06

Outlook

In an October 6, 2026 report, KB Securities said third-quarter results would fall short of consensus and expected a clearer improvement to begin in the fourth quarter. The same report said major order results would be reflected intensively toward year-end.

The disclosed third-quarter sales of KRW 51.6 billion exceeded that report's estimate of KRW 46.5 billion. The report also raised its three-year operating profit compound annual growth estimate from 36% to 47%, citing the expansion of the revenue base from foundry into NAND and DRAM.

The next periodic report, the 2026 annual report, will bring fourth-quarter sales and operating profit into formal disclosed figures. Whether Yesti's 125-unit delivery leads to customer mass-production use will signal the direction of HPSP's share.

The follow-up to the Patent Court's ruling could also affect the equipment sales landscape. Press coverage pointed to the expansion of high-pressure process applications, customer diversification, R&D spending and the speed of new equipment qualification as items to watch together.

The first-quarter 2027 result will show whether the recovery from the first-quarter 2026 low of KRW 31.9 billion continues.

Bull factors

Leading-edge investment expansion

In a July 2026 report, KB Securities said global foundry and memory makers' leading-edge conversion investments are expected to benefit HPSP. The same report noted that gate-all-around transitions raise the importance of interface defect control. It also said rising memory customer investment could lift equipment unit shipments.

Two straight quarters of sales growth

From a first-quarter 2026 low of KRW 31.9 billion, sales rose to KRW 46.1 billion in the second quarter and KRW 51.6 billion in the third. Third-quarter sales were up 61.0% year on year. Operating profit of KRW 27.8 billion held near the KRW 28.3 billion of the second quarter.

Financial capacity and cash generation

Total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 308.2 billion against total liabilities of KRW 61.5 billion, a debt ratio of 19.9%. Operating cash flow of KRW 100.2 billion in 2025 was the largest in the disclosed period. Continued cash generation could cushion swings in customer investment.

Bear factors

Erosion of exclusivity

In March 2026, a domestic rival shipped 75 high-pressure annealing tools to a foundry customer and said it had won a 125-unit order for August delivery from another chipmaker. A multi-supplier market is therefore taking shape in an area HPSP had dominated. The rival has also filed invalidity and related trials against several of HPSP's patents, widening the dispute.

Profit decline and quarterly volatility

Operating profit declined for two consecutive years, to KRW 93.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 89.9 billion in 2025, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 15.8% to KRW 72.7 billion in 2025.

Third-quarter 2026 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 16.5 billion, down 31.7% from the prior quarter, following a first-quarter 2026 peak. Quarterly profit swings with the timing of equipment revenue recognition.

High multiples and volatility

The price-to-book ratio sits in a range of substantial premium to net assets, so if earnings recovery stalls the multiple remains a burden. A October 16, 2026 press report recorded a single-day move above 10%, showing short-term volatility.

A quarterly lull like the first quarter of 2026, when sales fell 39.4%, could make that multiple burden more visible.

10

Risk factors

Patent and competitive risk

The patent dispute between HPSP and the rival spans multiple cases. The Patent Court ruling is the next point to check. The outcome could change the equipment sales landscape and customer negotiation terms.

Customer investment timing risk

Press coverage noted that equipment revenue can vary by quarter with customers' investment and delivery or acceptance timing. KB Securities also explained a roughly six-month lag between an order and revenue recognition. The 39.4% quarter-on-quarter decline in first-quarter 2026 sales illustrates this pattern.

Qualification and customer diversification pace

A 2023 Samsung Securities report judged that securing additional customers takes time given safety certification and qualification test periods. High-pressure processes demand demanding control of hydrogen concentration and pressure, so qualification speed for new tools shapes the pace of revenue. If diversification lags, dependence on a small set of customers could persist.

Overall view

HPSP has kept operating margins in the 50% range in high-pressure hydrogen annealing equipment, and third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 51.6 billion rose 61.0% year on year.

However, annual operating profit declined for two consecutive years after its 2023 peak through 2025, and third-quarter 2026 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 16.5 billion fell from the prior quarter.

Two consecutive quarters of sales growth can be read as leading-edge investment expansion gradually reaching revenue. Revenue recognition timing and customer investment schedules can still produce wide quarterly swings.

On the competitive side, the rival's shipments and the patent dispute are variables that could change HPSP's exclusive position. The debt ratio of 19.9% and total equity of KRW 308.2 billion describe the balance sheet, while the share price trades at a high multiple to net assets and earnings.
