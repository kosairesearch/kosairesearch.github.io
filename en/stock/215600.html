02

Business structure

SillaJen is a biotech company focused on developing anticancer drugs. Its main pipeline includes the solid tumor candidate BAL0891, the immuno-oncology agent Pexa-Vec, and the next-generation oncolytic virus platform SJ-600 series.

The FDA granted BAL0891 orphan drug designation for acute myeloid leukemia (AML), and early clinical results were presented at the American Society of Clinical Oncology (ASCO) meeting in June 2026.

In those results, the recommended phase 2 dose for monotherapy was set at 160mg, and an eight-patient cohort treated with paclitaxel reached a disease control rate of 87.5%.

Pexa-Vec is being developed for renal cell carcinoma, melanoma, and prostate cancer according to a 2025 press report, and the phase 3 trial in liver cancer was halted in August 2019. On the platform side, a study of the intravenously administered oncolytic virus SJ-650 was accepted by a journal in January 2026.

On the sales side, a pharma division was set up after the merger of Woosung Pharm, and drug sales in the first half of 2026 reached KRW 4.9 billion, up 145.0% year on year. That accounted for most of the company's total first-half revenue of KRW 5.1 billion.

According to press reports, pharma division sales exceeded KRW 9 billion in 2025 and were expected to top KRW 10 billion in 2026. Technology licensing centers on BAL0891 and the SJ-600 series, and in October 2026 SillaJen was selected for the second round of the national new drug development project.

Operating losses were KRW 24.5 billion in 2022, KRW 21.3 billion in 2023 (losses continued, with the loss narrowing), KRW 26.8 billion in 2024 (losses continued, with the loss widening), and KRW 25.6 billion in 2025 (losses continued, with the loss narrowing).

The company has been in the red for four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025. The operating margin moved from -488.2% in 2022 to -541.8% in 2023, -681.7% in 2024, and -277.4% in 2025. The 2025 improvement owed more to revenue more than doubling than to a sharp cut in the loss.

Net loss including non-controlling interests is disclosed at the same levels for each year. The debt ratio fell from 31.0% in 2022 and 33.9% in 2023 to 13.0% in 2024 and 6.1% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 110.1 billion and total liabilities were KRW 6.69 billion.

Industry analysis

Anticancer drug development carries high failure risk at each clinical stage, and whether technology licensing deals close has a large effect on how companies are valued.

After the 2019 halt of the Pexa-Vec phase 3 liver cancer trial, SillaJen went through a management change and litigation, and its pipeline focus shifted from Pexa-Vec to BAL0891.

Press reports describe weak conditions in the biotech sector, while SillaJen has been building expectations through clinical and academic milestones.

Peers often cited for comparison include LigaChem Biosciences and ABL Bio, which have anticancer licensing experience, and Alteogen, known for platform technology licensing.

Compared with these companies, SillaJen's clinical programs are still at an early stage, and its licensing targets center on the SJ-600 series and BAL0891. Both Pexa-Vec and BAL0891 target solid tumors, so comparing trial design with competing agents matters.

The oncolytic virus platform's intravenous and repeated dosing potential is at a validation stage, and analysts argue this validation could affect negotiating leverage in licensing.

Few listed biotech companies in Korea run a pharma sales business alongside pipeline development, so SillaJen's revenue structure differs from that of pipeline-focused peers. Sector conditions and company-specific factors overlap, so sector averages alone make it hard to judge its results.

Clinical signals and regulatory designation

BAL0891 received FDA orphan drug designation for acute myeloid leukemia. At ASCO in June 2026, the recommended phase 2 dose for monotherapy was set at 160mg, and among eight patients treated at 120mg or higher, one achieved a complete response and one a partial response.

The paclitaxel combination cohort of eight patients reported a disease control rate of 87.5%. The company said these results provide grounds for entering phase 2.

Expanding revenue base

Revenue rose 134.9% to KRW 9.23 billion in 2025. Third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 3.1 billion, up 12.0% from a year earlier. According to press reports, drug sales in the first half of 2026 reached KRW 4.9 billion, up 145.0% year on year. Press coverage describes the pharma division as a source of cash generation separate from new drug development.

Narrowing losses and low leverage

The operating loss narrowed to KRW 3.85 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down from a year earlier. The net loss attributable to shareholders also narrowed to KRW 2.97 billion. The debt ratio fell from 31.0% in 2022 to 6.1% in 2025, and total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 6.69 billion. The relatively light debt burden supports the company's capacity to fund its pipeline.

Legacy of the Pexa-Vec trial halt

The phase 3 Pexa-Vec trial in liver cancer was halted in August 2019 following a recommendation from an independent data monitoring committee, and the share price fell sharply afterward. Around the same time, an executive's share sales before the recommendation raised allegations of insider information use. BAL0891 remains at an early clinical stage, so there is no assurance it will avoid a similar setback.

Governance and litigation burden

After management misconduct allegations including embezzlement and breach of trust came to light, minority shareholders sued former executives and the Korea Exchange for damages, and lost on appeal. Former CEO Moon Eun-sang stepped down and Han Sang-gyu took over as CEO.

Overall view

SillaJen's revenue more than doubled to KRW 9.23 billion in 2025, and its operating loss narrowed to KRW 3.85 billion in the third quarter of 2026, so the losses are moving in a narrower direction.

However, operating cash flow has been negative every year from 2022 through 2025, and the company has posted losses for five consecutive quarters since the third quarter of 2025. Its balance sheet shows a debt ratio of 6.1% and total equity of KRW 110.1 billion at the end of 2025, a relatively light debt burden.

In the pipeline, BAL0891 has FDA orphan drug designation and early ASCO data, but it remains an early-stage trial with a small sample. Pexa-Vec's liver cancer phase 3 trial was halted in 2019, and the company has since shifted its focus to BAL0891.

Governance is in transition through a CEO departure and a change in the controlling shareholder, and litigation history remains. On a price-to-book basis, the market is pricing in a premium to net assets, so pipeline value and the pace of earnings recovery are the central questions in valuation.

The next checkpoints are full-year operating loss in the 2026 annual business report, quarterly results for the first quarter of 2027, and the announcement of BAL0891 phase 2 entry.
