02

Business structure

Handok Clean Tech was founded in 2003 and mainly produces carbon-block filters for water purifiers using a compression molding process.

These filters are core components made by mixing powdered activated carbon with a polymer binder and pressing it under high heat and pressure, and the company has said it introduced the technology to Korea in 1989.

A December 2022 feature in E Today reported that the company held more than 50% of the domestic water purifier filter market. Customers named in listing-era coverage were major domestic water purifier makers, and a company profile also lists Samsung Electronics among its customers.

In that 2022 feature, the CEO described sales as effectively all B2B and mentioned a long-term plan to expand into B2C through its own brand. Its product range now extends from home purifier filters to a vehicle carbon-block filter named ZERO ON, along with air purification and industrial filters.

On the finished-product side, it launched AQUON, a home under-sink purifier that users install and whose filters they replace themselves. Pre-listing material named a few smaller filter makers and in-house filter operations at purifier makers as competitors.

Under a B2B supply model, customers' product lineups and replacement cycles drive volumes, so sales trends at major customers are the basic variable for results.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 59.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 65.1 billion in 2023 (up 10.0%), KRW 68.2 billion in 2024 (up 4.9%) and KRW 69.4 billion in 2025 (up 1.8%), rising for three straight years from 2023. 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 5.07 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.21 billion in 2023 (up 2.7%), the peak, before falling to KRW 3.5 billion in 2024 (down 32.9%) and KRW 2.82 billion in 2025 (down 19.4%), down for two consecutive years from 2024.

Coverage of the 2025 results in March 2026 attributed the profit squeeze to cost pressure: a securities analyst cited a 9.0% rise in total labor costs, a 1.1 percentage point drop in gross margin, and overseas sales growth as a support to revenue.

The operating margin declined from 8.6% in 2022 to 8.0% in 2023, 5.1% in 2024 and 4.1% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.07 billion in 2022, KRW 5.34 billion in 2023 (up 5.5%), KRW 3.62 billion in 2024 (down 32.2%) and KRW 2.73 billion in 2025 (down 24.6%), while net income including non-controlling interests was disclosed at the same levels over the same years.

Operating cash flow was KRW 3.06 billion in 2022, KRW 3.57 billion in 2023 and KRW 5.5 billion in 2024, then fell to KRW 2.11 billion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 787 million in Q3 2025, dropped to KRW 70.63 million in Q4 (down 91.0% from the prior quarter), then recovered to KRW 910 million in Q1 2026, KRW 2.43 billion in Q2 and KRW 1.68 billion in Q3, which was up 113.5% from a year earlier.

Revenue in Q3 2026 was KRW 20.6 billion, up 11.6% year on year but down 2.5% from Q2. Controlling-shareholder net income for the four quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 totaled KRW 5.26 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Water purifier filters are replaceable consumable components inside purifier units, so demand moves with the installed base of purifiers and replacement cycles. The company was reported to hold more than 50% of the domestic water purifier filter market, but that figure dates from a December 2022 report.

In a September 2026 report, a company representative said the Chinese water purifier market is growing and related volumes are rising. The same report said the company pushed price increases on some products from Q2 2026 to offset higher raw material costs, with the increases being applied in stages during Q3.

Because its customers are purifier makers, those customers' ability to source or negotiate filter prices is a variable in margins. Competition from smaller specialist filter makers works through both specification and price.

The company sits in the machinery and equipment sector, but its business is water purifier filter materials and components, so its structure and earnings model differ from listed peers and direct comparison is limited.

Overseas expansion adds to replacement demand from domestic customers and broadens the volume base, but the share and profitability of overseas sales must be read from the regular filing figures.

Outlook

A securities analyst outlook reported by Daily Invest in March 2026 projected 2026 revenue of KRW 75.2 billion (up 8.2%) and operating profit of KRW 5.3 billion (up 88.8%).

That outlook rested on cost efficiency from automated production lines, with the operating margin expected to improve by 3.0 percentage points to 7.1%.

Disclosed quarterly revenue for Q1 to Q3 2026 was KRW 19.3 billion, KRW 21.1 billion and KRW 20.6 billion, so whether the full-year projection is met depends on the size of Q4 revenue.

Operating profit was positive in each of the first three quarters of 2026, so the gap to the projection will likely show up in Q4 profit and cost trends. On overseas sales, new supply to a global Chinese appliance maker has begun, and new channels in Southeast Asia are being pursued.

Price increases on some products were applied in stages through Q3 to pass on raw material cost increases, so their full effect may appear in Q4 2026 results. The contribution of the finished-product business, including AQUON and the ZERO ON vehicle filter, has not yet appeared in reported figures.

A 2022 report said the company had begun expanding a factory of about 1,000 pyeong and aimed to complete it in February 2023. Whether that capacity translated into sales needs to be confirmed in later filings.

The next checkpoint is how close the full-year 2026 revenue and operating profit in the annual report come to the projection.

Bull factors

Position in water purifier filters

The company says it introduced compression-molded carbon-block technology to Korea in 1989, and a December 2022 report described it as holding more than 50% of the domestic water purifier filter market. Its major domestic purifier makers as customers give replacement demand a base for recurring sales. IPO-era coverage also described the market position as built on the same technology.

Quarterly profit recovery in 2026

Operating profit was KRW 910 million in Q1 2026, KRW 2.43 billion in Q2 and KRW 1.68 billion in Q3, all positive. Quarterly profit had fallen to KRW 70.63 million in Q4 2025 and has since recovered. Q3 2026 operating profit was up 113.5% from a year earlier. A securities analyst cited automated production lines as a profit improvement factor.

Overseas customers and price increases

A September 2026 report said the company began supplying a global Chinese appliance maker and is pursuing Southeast Asian channels. Price increases on some products, pursued from Q2 and applied in stages through Q3, reflect higher raw material costs. A securities analysis cited overseas sales growth of 17% in 2025, centered on China and the United States.

Bear factors

Operating margin down to 4%

The operating margin fell from 8.6% in 2022 to 4.1% in 2025, and operating profit declined for two consecutive years after its 2023 peak. Margins fell even as revenue rose for three straight years, showing that top-line growth has not carried through to profitability. Operating profit dropped 19.4% in 2025.

Labor cost pressure

A securities analyst said total labor costs rose 9.0% in 2025, pushing gross margin down 1.1 percentage points. Labor was described as roughly 20% of the cost base. Price increases pursued from Q2 2026 are not yet visible in disclosed figures as a full offset to raw material inflation.

Quarterly volatility and weaker cash flow

Operating profit fell 91.0% in Q4 2025 to KRW 70.63 million before recovering to KRW 910 million in Q1 2026. Swings of this size suggest sensitivity to volume and fixed costs. Operating cash flow dropped from KRW 5.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.11 billion in 2025, weakening cash generation alongside lower profit.

Risk factors

Customer concentration and purifier demand

Revenue depends on B2B supply to purifier makers, so changes in major customers' production plans and product lineups directly affect volumes. Slower purifier installations or a shift toward in-house filter sourcing could weaken the base of replacement demand. Growth in overseas customers can also raise dependence on specific regions.

Costs, labor and raw materials

A securities analyst linked the 2025 labor cost increase to a 1.1 percentage point drop in gross margin, and labor is a large share of costs. Passing raw material inflation through price increases requires customer negotiation and lags in time. If pricing does not fully offset cost increases, the operating margin could stay around 4% or lower.

Overseas expansion and finished products

New supply to Chinese customers and Southeast Asian channels appear to be at an early stage, and their volume and profit contribution are not yet visible in filings. Finished products such as AQUON and the ZERO ON vehicle filter require distribution and marketing capabilities that differ from B2B components. If new-business costs rise before results appear, short-term profit could come under further pressure.

Overall view

Handok Clean Tech is a B2B maker of carbon-block filters for water purifiers, and disclosed revenue has risen for three straight years since 2023.

Operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income, however, peaked in 2023 and have fallen for two consecutive years from 2024, with the operating margin down from 8.6% in 2022 to 4.1% in 2025. Operating profit dropped sharply in Q4 2025 and was positive in each quarter from Q1 to Q3 2026.

Bullish factors include its position in the domestic water purifier filter market, the start of supply to a global Chinese customer, pass-through of raw material costs through price increases, and expected cost efficiency from automated lines.

Bearish factors include labor and cost pressure, concentration on purifier makers, weaker operating cash flow in 2025 and quarterly profit volatility.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio sits below 1 times, and a securities analyst and Korea IR Council described the current level as attractive relative to past P/E averages. That view depends on whether profit recovery persists, so interpretations may diverge.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary full-year 2026 results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
