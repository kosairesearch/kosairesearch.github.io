02

Business structure

Cosmax BTI was created when Cosmax was spun off in 2014, and it operates as the holding company that oversees the group's subsidiaries. Its core business is health functional food ODM, supported by new business development and the operation of health food subsidiaries.

Domestic health functional food sales in 2025 were KRW 263.8 billion, up 10.89% from the prior year, while exports fell 15.39% to KRW 191.9 billion, so growth has shifted toward the home market.

Standalone revenue in 2025 was KRW 177.7 billion, up 56.56%, reflecting the liquidation of the gel mask maker Cosmax iCure and its absorption into an internal business unit. Health food subsidiary Cosmax Bio posted 2025 revenue of KRW 168.2 billion, up 14.66% from the prior year.

The pharmaceutical unit recorded 2024 sales of KRW 39.1 billion, down 17%, with an operating loss of KRW 6.3 billion. In China, the company is working with a local partner to strengthen R&D and production for the Chinese market. An Indonesian halal certification business is also under way.

As of 2025, chairwoman Seo Seong-seok is the largest shareholder and added shares through market purchases that year. The same year, the leadership roles at the group's two affiliates were switched between the sons.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 617.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 628.6 billion in 2023, up 1.8%, fell to KRW 597.6 billion in 2024, down 4.9%, and recovered to KRW 645.2 billion in 2025, up 8.0%.

Reports attributed the 2023 operating profit of KRW 20.2 billion to growth in health food sales from a wider overseas customer base and to cost structure improvements in health food and pharmaceuticals.

Operating profit fell to KRW 16.8 billion in 2024, down 17.1%, when the health food segment's operating profit dropped 52% to KRW 5.7 billion. Operating profit rose to KRW 27 billion in 2025, up 60.7%, and the operating margin reached 4.2%, up from 0.6% in 2022.

Net profit attributable to owners moved from a loss of KRW 6.53 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 9.06 billion in 2023, then to KRW 16.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 29.2 billion in 2025, up 77.5%.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 18.7 billion in 2025, below the figure attributable to owners, which suggests a loss in non-controlling interests drove the gap. Operating cash flow was KRW 35.2 billion in 2025, up from KRW 23.8 billion in 2024.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit was KRW 3.18 billion in Q3 2025 and jumped to KRW 7.36 billion in Q4 2025, up 131.4%. Q1 2026 revenue reached KRW 205.2 billion and operating profit peaked at KRW 16.4 billion, up 122.9% from the prior quarter.

Q2 2026 revenue rose to KRW 239 billion, but operating profit was flat at KRW 16.3 billion, down 0.9%. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 241 billion, while operating profit fell to KRW 11.8 billion, down 27.5% from the prior quarter.

Net profit attributable to owners was KRW 29.3 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 15.1 billion in Q3 2026, so quarterly owner profit did not track operating profit. Net profit attributable to owners totaled KRW 80.8 billion over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026. Total liabilities were KRW 710.7 billion at the end of 2025, against total equity of KRW 295.7 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for health functional foods is recovering as wellness trends spread, according to press reports. Online channels remain dominant, with a Korea Health Functional Food Association survey showing a 71% online purchase share in 2025.

Large discount stores rose from 5.5% in 2024 to 5.9% in 2025, and drugstores rose from 0.6% to 0.7%. Convenience stores and Daiso outlets are also widening access. Indie health food brands designed by licensed professionals are gaining ground, expanding social commerce channels.

These trends create demand for ODM producers that design and manufacture products for brand owners. Cosmax, a listed cosmetics ODM company in the same group, disclosed record quarterly revenue in Q3 2026 despite the seasonally weak period.

Cosmax BTI's health food exports, however, fell 15.39% in 2025, so overseas demand has recovered more slowly than the home market. The company also exited local manufacturing at its US subsidiary and converted it into a sales entity, which lightens the fixed cost base. Market share comparisons against rival health food ODM companies are not included here.

06

Outlook

The company has said it stopped local production at its US subsidiary and will keep manufacturing only in Korea and Australia. It expects fixed cost reductions to lift profitability from 2026, which is a company statement whose effect must be confirmed in quarterly operating margins.

Operating profit was KRW 16.4 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 16.3 billion in Q2, and KRW 11.8 billion in Q3, so whether the restructuring shows up in the earnings trend is the next test. In China, a partnership with a local firm is being used to strengthen R&D and production.

In Indonesia, halal-certified products and an online channel strategy are being used to pursue local consumers. A pilot entry into B2C through a customized cosmetics platform has also been described.

Efforts to attract small indie brands include lower minimum order volumes and smart factory automation for small-batch, multi-SKU production. The 2026 annual report, the next regular filing, will show how the full-year operating margin and debt ratio were settled.

08

Bull factors

Domestic health food sales growth

Domestic health food sales rose 10.89% to KRW 263.8 billion in 2025. Wider retail access and the continued online purchase share of 71% in the market support this trend. As the health food share grew, annual revenue expanded to KRW 645.2 billion in 2025.

Restructuring of the US subsidiary

The US subsidiary stopped local production and was converted into a sales entity. The company said lower fixed costs would make profitability gains visible from 2026. Reducing the production-heavy cost base could become one pillar of margin recovery.

Two consecutive years of owner profit growth

Net profit attributable to owners rose to KRW 16.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 29.2 billion in 2025. Over the last four quarters it totaled KRW 80.8 billion. In Q2 2026 it reached KRW 29.3 billion, the highest quarterly figure in the period shown.

09

Bear factors

Declining export sales

Health food exports fell 15.39% to KRW 191.9 billion in 2025, from KRW 226.8 billion. Overseas recovery is lagging domestic sales growth. Whether export channels recover will determine how sustainable top-line growth is.

Two quarters of falling operating profit

Operating profit peaked at KRW 16.4 billion in Q1 2026 and then fell to KRW 16.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 11.8 billion in Q3. The Q3 figure was down 27.5% from the prior quarter. Revenue kept rising while profit weakened, which raises questions about the durability of margin recovery.

High debt burden

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 187.4% in 2022 to 240.3% in 2025. Total liabilities of KRW 710.7 billion at the end of 2025 are well above total equity of KRW 295.7 billion. Debt service may limit how much of the profit recovery reaches shareholders.

10

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Profit attributable to owners and operating profit do not move in the same direction each quarter. In Q2 2026 owner profit rose to KRW 29.3 billion while operating profit was flat at KRW 16.3 billion. Non-operating items and non-controlling interest results make quarterly trends hard to read from one metric.

Governance and succession

As of 2025, chairwoman Seo Seong-seok is the largest shareholder and increased her stake through market purchases. The same year, leadership roles at the group's affiliates were switched between the sons.

Because governance is centered on the family, the link between management changes and earnings or shareholding needs to be tracked.

Overseas restructuring

Halting local production at the US subsidiary and converting it to a sales entity is expected to reduce fixed costs. The process may involve one-time costs and shifts in sales channels. Whether profitability improves as the company describes must be confirmed in quarterly results.

Overall view

Cosmax BTI posted 2025 revenue of KRW 645.2 billion, operating profit of KRW 27 billion, and net profit attributable to owners of KRW 29.2 billion, showing a full-year recovery. Owner profit has risen for two consecutive years through 2025, and the operating margin climbed from 0.6% in 2022 to 4.2% in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit, however, peaked at KRW 16.4 billion in Q1 2026 and has fallen for two quarters, reaching KRW 11.8 billion in Q3. Domestic health food growth and the US subsidiary restructuring are the main strengths, while lower exports and a 240.3% debt-to-equity ratio are the main weaknesses.

Net profit attributable to owners of KRW 80.8 billion over the last four quarters places the multiple in a low range relative to market capitalization. The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report, where the operating margin and debt ratio will show the direction of earnings.
