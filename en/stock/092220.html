KEC posted an operating profit of KRW 214 million in Q3 2026, but net losses attributable to owners over the last four quarters still total KRW 16.9 billion.

02

Business structure

KEC designs, manufactures and supplies non-memory power semiconductors used in home appliances and automobiles. The company counts leading Korean electronics firms among its major customers, and it has stated its aim to expand automotive semiconductors.

Its push to widen the business from home appliances into automotive electronics is central to the current restructuring. The company operates 11 overseas subsidiaries and sales networks, with exports centered on China, Japan and the US.

On the production side, a KRW 64.8 billion new facility investment at the Gumi plant is underway, and its end date was extended by one year to December 2026. The pace at which the automotive share of revenue grows can be tracked through segment information in periodic reports.

Compared with memory leaders such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, whose investment is centered on AI and HBM, KEC focuses on non-memory power devices and is further from the direct beneficiary structure of the AI investment cycle.

In test equipment, ISC is often cited for its silicone socket business, but its product lines differ from KEC's core area, so it is not a direct comparison.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 256.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 206.9 billion in 2023, a 19.2% decline, then recovered to KRW 243.2 billion in 2024, up 17.5%, before dropping to KRW 229.6 billion in 2025, down 5.6%. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 18 billion in 2022, with a 7.0% operating margin, before turning into an operating loss of KRW 33 billion in 2023, a margin of -15.9%. The operating loss narrowed to KRW 13.3 billion in 2024, with a margin of -5.5%, then widened again to KRW 21.8 billion in 2025, a margin of -9.5%.

Losses have continued for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. Net profit attributable to owners of KRW 21.9 billion in 2022 turned into a net loss of KRW 38.1 billion in 2023, followed by a net loss of KRW 11.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 26.6 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 22.3 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 37.8 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 11 billion in 2024, and a net loss of KRW 26.6 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 6.75 billion in 2022, negative KRW 672 million in 2023, KRW 29.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.28 billion in 2025, with 2024 the largest of the four years.

On a quarterly basis, the operating loss narrowed each quarter from KRW 7.41 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 6.15 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 4.84 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 3.27 billion in Q2 2026, before Q3 2026 posted an operating profit of KRW 214 million.

Net loss attributable to owners went from KRW 7.38 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 13.2 billion in Q4 2025, then KRW 5.05 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 2.79 billion in Q2 2026, before Q3 2026 recorded net profit attributable to owners of KRW 4.17 billion.

Quarterly revenue of KRW 65 billion in Q3 2026 was down 4.2% from the prior quarter but up 23.8% from a year earlier. Per FnGuide's aggregation, Q1 2026 revenue rose 1.1% year on year while the operating loss increased 57.1%, showing that cost pressure persisted into the first quarter.

05

Industry analysis

In the semiconductor cycle, AI server and high-bandwidth memory (HBM) investment has become the central axis. Based on a forecast cited in the company's IR material, the global semiconductor market is projected to grow 26.3% in 2026.

Even within this market, memory, system semiconductors and power semiconductors are driven by different demand factors. KEC sits in the non-memory power semiconductor segment, so it is further from the structure in which AI investment benefits concentrate on memory leaders.

In inspection and packaging supply chains, ISC has been reported to hold about 90% of the global silicone test socket segment. That figure applies to the silicone socket segment only, not to the total test socket market.

ISC has also been reported to derive KRW 59.1 billion, or 81% of its sales, from AI-related business in Q2 2026. This shows AI-driven test demand spreading across the supply chain, but it does not directly connect to KEC's revenue mix.

The pace of the automotive share depends on automakers' adoption and production plans for power semiconductors, so customer order flow is the key variable.

KEC's revenue rose slightly year on year in Q1 2026 while its operating loss grew sharply, so top-line recovery and profitability recovery did not move at the same pace.

Outlook

In its IR material, the company has presented expanding automotive semiconductors and maintaining R&D investment as its main directions.

The KRW 64.8 billion new facility investment at the Gumi plant has had its end date extended to December 2026, and how automotive production capacity changes after completion is the next checkpoint.

Because operating profit turned positive in Q3 2026, whether the operating margin stays positive in subsequent quarters is the key test. Quarterly revenue of KRW 65 billion in Q3 2026 was down from the prior quarter, so whether revenue holds in the mid-KRW 60 billion range also needs to be watched.

The IR summary highlighted a recent 5-for-1 reverse stock split and the acquisition of shares in KEC's Thai subsidiary as key moves. Whether the global growth outlook translates into automotive power semiconductor demand should be checked against customer orders and mass-production schedules.

Full-year 2026 results will be finalized in the 2026 business report due around late March 2027. The fourth-quarter operating and net results in that report will show the quality of the turn to profitability. The Q1 2027 quarterly report will indicate whether the profit trend continues.

08

Bull factors

Narrowing Losses and a Q3 Turn to Profit

The operating loss narrowed every quarter from KRW 7.41 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 3.27 billion in Q2 2026, and Q3 2026 posted an operating profit of KRW 214 million. Net profit attributable to owners of KRW 4.17 billion also moved from loss to profit in Q3 2026.

The change in direction is visible in reported quarterly results. If this trend holds, it would be the basis for moving out of the annual loss structure.

Automotive Pivot and Overseas Footprint

The company has presented automotive semiconductors as a main expansion direction and exports to China, Japan and the US through 11 overseas subsidiaries and sales networks. A KRW 64.8 billion new facility investment at the Gumi plant is underway.

A larger automotive product share could change the demand mix away from a home-appliance-centered structure. The speed at which this shift shows up in reported results is central to the bullish case.

Operating Cash Flow and a Moderate Debt Level

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 325.9 billion and total liabilities were KRW 123.1 billion. The debt ratio rose to 37.8% at the end of 2025 from 2022 but remains moderate relative to equity. Operating cash flow stayed positive, at KRW 29.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.28 billion in 2025. This financial capacity serves as a buffer while the business remains in a loss-making period.

Bear factors

Three Consecutive Years of Losses

Operating losses and net losses attributable to owners continued for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. The 2025 operating loss widened to KRW 21.8 billion from KRW 13.3 billion in 2024, with an operating margin of -9.5%.

The net loss attributable to owners also grew to KRW 26.6 billion in 2025 from KRW 11.4 billion in 2024. The turn to profit has so far appeared in a single quarter, so a structural change on an annual basis is yet to be shown.

Shrinking Revenue and a Recent Quarterly Decline

Annual revenue declined from KRW 256.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 229.6 billion in 2025, with a 19.2% drop in 2023. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 65 billion was down 4.2% from the prior quarter. Net losses attributable to owners over the last four quarters total KRW 16.9 billion, leaving accumulated losses. If revenue does not recover, fixed cost burden could again weigh on results.

Rising Debt Ratio and Lower Cash Generation

The debt ratio rose from 25.5% in 2023 to 37.8% in 2025. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 29.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 9.28 billion in 2025. Total equity stood at KRW 325.9 billion at the end of 2025, and the equity base has been shrinking as losses accumulate. A gradually thinning financial cushion forms the basis of the bearish case.

Risk factors

Industry and Demand Risk

KEC's revenue moves with production plans at its home-appliance and automotive customers. The strategy to expand the automotive share depends on adoption timing at automakers, so delays could slow the recovery in revenue.

Because demand for non-memory products does not move in step with the memory-centered AI cycle, a strong industry backdrop may not translate directly into results. Whether quarterly revenue stays stuck in the mid-KRW 60 billion range needs monitoring.

Financial and Cash Flow Risk

Annual operating losses have continued for three years, and the debt ratio rose to 37.8% at the end of 2025. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 9.28 billion in 2025 from KRW 29.9 billion in 2024. A single profitable quarter does not confirm a stable cash generation structure. If capital expenditure overlaps with weak operations, the financial burden could increase.

Share Liquidity and Volatility Risk

According to the IR summary, the company carried out a 5-for-1 reverse stock split recently. After the split, the share price level is harder to compare directly with the past. As a small-cap stock, trading volume and price swings can be larger than in large-cap names. Price moves around earnings releases may also diverge from the content of the results.

Overall view

KEC recorded a Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 214 million and net profit attributable to owners of KRW 4.17 billion, a quarterly turn to profit.

However, operating losses and net losses attributable to owners persisted for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and the last four quarters total KRW 16.9 billion in net losses attributable to owners.

Revenue declined from KRW 256.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 229.6 billion in 2025, so a recovery in top-line growth has not yet been confirmed.

The automotive expansion and the Gumi plant investment form the axis of the business restructuring, but the speed at which these effects appear in results is still at an early stage. The debt ratio rose to 37.8% at the end of 2025, and operating cash flow declined in 2025.

The next step is to confirm whether the turn to profit is sustained in the 2026 business report and the Q1 2027 results. This report is for information purposes only and does not include a buy or sell opinion or a target price.
