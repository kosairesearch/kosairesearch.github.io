Profit Recovery Continues; Margin Trend Is the Key Test

02

Business structure

TSE makes semiconductor test equipment and peripherals, was founded in 1995, and has been listed on KOSDAQ since 2011. Its core lineup covers test parts used across the chip test flow: probe cards for front-end electrical die sorting, interface boards and test sockets for final test.

In 2024, KB Securities analyst Yoo Woo-hyung described TSE as one of the few companies producing all three of these core test components.

The test socket business handles both rubber sockets and pogo-pin sockets, and the high-speed non-memory socket ELTUNE is positioned for testing ultra-fast chips used in 5G, autonomous driving and AI.

On the memory side, TSE announced in February 2021 that it had begun mass-supplying a HBM die carrier socket built on its own patented technology to a global memory chip maker.

The company said it expected annual revenue from this product to rise by more than KRW 10 billion within one to two years of the initial supply. Customers center on overseas large chip companies, including memory makers and SoC designers.

In the test socket category, ISC and LEENO Industrial are often mentioned alongside TSE as related names. The business mixes memory die-level test products with non-memory and SoC sockets and probe cards.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results started in 2022 with revenue of KRW 339.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 56.6 billion. In 2023, revenue fell 26.6% to KRW 249.1 billion, operating profit turned into a loss of KRW 2.38 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders dropped 99.8% to KRW 121 million.

In 2024, revenue rose 39.7% to KRW 348.1 billion and operating profit returned to profit at KRW 39.9 billion, with attributable net income recovering to KRW 42.5 billion. Revenue in 2025 reached KRW 428.9 billion, up 23.2% and the largest year in 2022–2025, and operating profit rose 23.2% to KRW 49.2 billion.

Attributable net income in 2025, however, fell 10.0% to KRW 38.2 billion, moving in a different direction from operating profit. The operating margin went from 16.7% in 2022 to -1.0% in 2023 and then to 11.5% in both 2024 and 2025. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 2.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 48.6 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, the debt ratio was 30.9%, with total equity of KRW 464.6 billion, of which KRW 391.3 billion belonged to controlling shareholders. On a quarterly basis, revenue expanded from KRW 104.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 11.5 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 217.4 billion and KRW 52.7 billion in Q3 2026.

Revenue has risen for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, and operating profit has also risen for four consecutive quarters over the same period.

The largest quarterly operating profit gain came in Q1 2026, at KRW 42 billion, up 112.4% quarter on quarter, followed by KRW 52.5 billion in Q2 (up 25.0%) and KRW 52.7 billion in Q3 (up 0.5%).

Attributable net income peaked at KRW 36.9 billion in Q1 2026, then came in at KRW 36.9 billion in Q2 (down 0.2%) and KRW 17.8 billion in Q3 (down 51.7%), with two consecutive quarterly declines. The last four quarters together total KRW 111 billion in attributable net income.

The divergence between operating profit and attributable net income can be read as a change in items below operating profit, such as non-operating items, income tax and non-controlling interests, and those details need to be checked in the annual report.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for semiconductor test parts tracks the inspection volume in front-end and back-end processes. As production of AI accelerators and high-bandwidth memory (HBM) expands, demand for die-level and high-speed signal testing is widely cited as a common industry direction.

In 2024, KB Securities analyst Yoo Woo-hyung viewed memory makers' demand for dual sourcing as positive for TSE's earnings and cited rising average selling prices tied to wider DDR5 penetration as another driver.

On the company's own cycle, revenue fell 26.6% in 2023 to a trough, recovered in 2024 and 2025, and has risen for four consecutive quarters through Q3 2026. Because test part orders move with customers' capital spending, TSE's results are sensitive to the investment cycle of its major chip customers.

In test sockets, ISC and LEENO Industrial are commonly mentioned alongside TSE. TSE's combination of probe cards, interface boards and test sockets is a distinguishing feature, and its ability to supply high-speed sockets and die carriers together may matter for competitive position as HBM test demand grows.

Order timing and volumes from large overseas customers are outside the company's control and can widen quarterly swings. Market share comparisons with peers should be limited to figures disclosed by the companies.

Outlook

TSE's next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, which must be filed within 90 days of fiscal year-end, by March 31, 2027. The Q3 2026 report was filed with the Financial Supervisory Service on the 16th of November 2026.

Fourth-quarter 2026 and full-year 2026 figures will therefore first appear in formal periodic reporting in the annual report.

The central fact in the outlook is that revenue has risen for four consecutive quarters, and KRW 217.4 billion in Q3 2026 revenue and KRW 52.7 billion in operating profit are the highest levels in the recent five quarters. Whether that level holds in subsequent quarters is the next checkpoint.

The HBM die carrier socket, announced for mass supply in 2021, remains a product line tied to memory test demand, while the high-speed ELTUNE socket serves non-memory test customers. The revenue contribution of each line should be confirmed from segment-level disclosures in periodic reports.

The Q1 2027 quarterly report is due by May 15, 2027, and it will show whether operating profit holds near the Q3 2026 level. It will also show whether the two-quarter decline in attributable net income reverses.

Dividend decisions are made at the annual general meeting, so the 2026 fiscal-year dividend will be confirmed through the meeting disclosures.

08

Bull factors

Sustained revenue growth

Revenue has risen for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, and Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 217.4 billion was up 108.2% year on year. Full-year 2025 revenue of KRW 428.9 billion was the largest year in 2022–2025.

The bull case points to supplying both memory and non-memory test parts as a way to widen exposure to demand growth.

Profitability recovery

The operating margin recovered from -1.0% in 2023 to 11.5% in 2024 and held at 11.5% in 2025. Quarterly operating profit reached KRW 42 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 52.5 billion in Q2 and KRW 52.7 billion in Q3.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 2.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 48.6 billion in 2025, which bulls cite as stronger cash backing for earnings.

HBM and high-speed socket portfolio

The HBM die carrier socket announced in 2021 and the high-speed ELTUNE socket are product lines tied to AI chip test demand. In 2024, KB Securities described TSE as a company producing probe cards, interface boards and test sockets. That same 2024 report viewed memory makers' demand for dual sourcing as positive for the company.

09

Bear factors

Slowing operating profit growth

Q3 2026 revenue rose 15.2% from the prior quarter, but operating profit of KRW 52.7 billion grew only 0.5%. That compares with a 25.0% quarter-on-quarter gain in Q2 2026, a sharp slowdown in profit growth.

Bears read this as a signal to check whether less of each revenue increase is reaching operating profit, and whether the cost or SG&A structure has shifted.

Two quarters of falling attributable profit

Attributable net income fell from KRW 36.9 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 36.9 billion in Q2 and KRW 17.8 billion in Q3, with Q3 down 51.7% from the prior quarter. Year on year it was still up 55.9%, but the recent direction is downward.

Bears argue that if the reason for the gap between stable operating profit and lower shareholder earnings is not clear, questions about earnings quality remain.

Cycle sensitivity and the 2023 loss

In 2023, revenue fell 26.6% year on year, the company posted an operating loss of KRW 2.38 billion, and attributable net income fell to KRW 121 million. The operating margin dropped to -1.0% in that year.

Bears cite this as evidence that earnings can drop sharply when customers cut investment, given the sensitivity of test part orders.

10

Risk factors

Customer investment cycle

Orders for test parts move with the capital spending and production plans of chip customers. As the 26.6% revenue decline in 2023 showed, orders can fall quickly in a downturn. Timing and volumes from large overseas customers are hard for the company to control, which can widen quarterly swings.

Durability of the profit recovery

The operating margin has stayed around 11.5% since 2024, and Q3 2026 operating profit rose only 0.5% from the prior quarter. Whether the recovery depends on revenue scale or on margin improvement will shape the stability of future results.

If profits turn down while the share price already reflects the recovery, the reading of the valuation could change significantly.

Patent and technology competition

Test sockets are an area where technology competition and patent disputes are frequent. TSE has been reported as winning a first-instance patent case over a rubber socket used in semiconductor testing.

Whether any later appeals or other disputes remain open should be confirmed in the litigation disclosures of periodic reports, and faster socket technology from competitors could also shift share.

Overall view

TSE's disclosed results show a recovery path: after an operating loss and attributable net income of KRW 121 million in 2023, the company returned to profit in 2024 and posted operating profit of KRW 49.2 billion in 2025.

Revenue has risen for four consecutive quarters through Q3 2026, and Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 217.4 billion was up 108.2% year on year. The bull case rests on the durability of revenue growth and the HBM and high-speed socket portfolio.

The bear case rests on flat operating profit in Q3 2026, two consecutive quarterly declines in attributable net income, and earnings swings tied to customer investment cycles. Both readings draw on the same disclosed figures but reach different conclusions.

The valuation sits at a large premium to net assets after the profit recovery, and the dividend return is low. The next checkpoints are the operating margin and the line items below operating profit in the 2026 annual report, and the profit level in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
