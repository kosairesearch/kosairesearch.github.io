02

Business structure

Simmtech Holdings (036710) is a KOSDAQ-listed holding company, and its operating business is run by its subsidiary Simmtech (222800). Simmtech listed on KOSDAQ in August 2015 and positions package substrates and memory modules as its core businesses.

The company says it supplies substrates and modules as a one-stop solution, and it plans to expand into next-generation LPCAMM, CXL module and substrate products. In Q3 2026, the subsidiary's MSAP sales were KRW 331.2 billion, up 8% from the prior quarter.

MSAP is a high-value packaging substrate built on fine-circuit technology and used in high-density chips and high-performance memory products. In the same quarter, HDI sales were KRW 142.4 billion, up 62% year on year, while system IC sales were KRW 94.6 billion, up 24% from the prior quarter.

Tenting sales were KRW 62.3 billion, down 22% from the prior quarter. SOCAMM2 substrates began shipping in 2026 for NVIDIA AI accelerators. The company says it is maintaining a top global market share.

As the share of technically demanding products grows, the mix is contributing positively to profitability compared with general memory substrates. Because most of the holding's consolidated results move with the subsidiary, the subsidiary's product mix is the starting point for reading the holding's earnings.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 1.71 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.09 trillion in 2023, a 36.1% decline. 2022 was the largest year of the four and 2023 the smallest. Revenue then rose to KRW 1.3 trillion in 2024 (up 19.1%) and KRW 1.53 trillion in 2025 (up 17.4%), growing for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 299.9 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 206.3 billion in 2023, a swing into deficit. The operating loss narrowed to KRW 131.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 34.3 billion in 2025, and the company recorded operating losses for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

The operating margin moved from 17.5% in 2022 to -18.9% in 2023, -10.1% in 2024 and -2.2% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 33.1 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 98.7 billion in 2023, then narrowed to net losses of KRW 78.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 37.8 billion in 2025.

Total net income including non-controlling interests is a separate figure: KRW 186.5 billion in 2022, then net losses of KRW 196.3 billion, KRW 112.3 billion and KRW 151.1 billion in 2023 to 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 421.1 billion in 2022 and stayed negative at -KRW 122.5 billion, -KRW 107 billion and -KRW 139 billion in 2023, 2024 and 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 123.7% in 2022 to 427.3% in 2024 before easing to 327.0% in 2025.

At end-2025, total liabilities were KRW 1.22 trillion and total equity KRW 373.9 billion, of which the controlling shareholders' stake was KRW 25.6 billion.

On a quarterly basis, revenue rose from KRW 398.1 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 592.2 billion in Q3 2026, growing for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025, and Q3 2026 revenue was up 48.8% year on year.

Operating profit was KRW 2.53 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 3.75 billion in Q4 2025, fell to KRW 965 million in Q1 2026 (down 74.3%), then rose to KRW 57.6 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 104.7 billion in Q3 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a loss of KRW 667 million in Q3 2025 and a loss of KRW 11.9 billion in Q4 2025 to a loss of KRW 7.86 billion in Q1 2026, a gain of KRW 1.78 billion in Q2 2026 and a loss of KRW 11.3 billion in Q3 2026.

Operating profit grew far faster than the bottom line attributable to controlling shareholders, which is the key reading of the latest quarters. Subsidiary Simmtech said its Q3 2026 operating margin was about 19%, up 6.8 percentage points from the prior quarter.

Industry analysis

The semiconductor substrate market is shifting toward higher-value products as AI server demand and higher-spec memory modules expand.

In January 2026, NH Investment & Securities pointed to a recovering memory cycle, rising orders and exports, and a favorable currency environment as grounds for medium- to long-term growth at subsidiary Simmtech.

On raw materials, NH Investment & Securities analyzed in November 2025 that sharp rises in gold and copper prices, which account for about half of raw material costs, weighed on Q3 2025 operating profit. The same report also noted that the shrinking share of low-value BOC substrates was slower than expected.

In November 2026, Hana Securities said that as customers diversify, substrate suppliers are entering a phase of stronger pricing power at subsidiary Simmtech. The same report cited expanding supply of high-value non-memory package substrates alongside upgraded specifications for memory package substrates.

In May 2026, Daishin Securities estimated that subsidiary Simmtech holds the largest SOCAMM2 supply share among the three major memory makers. Among Korean holding companies, SK Square (SK Square) shows a similar structure in which share prices track the value of a listed subsidiary.

In the same substrate industry, Samsung Electro-Mechanics (Samsung Electro-Mechanics) and other large makers keep technology competition in high-value substrates active. A SOCAMM capacity-reduction issue that surfaced in June 2026 added volatility to the subsidiary's share price.

Outlook

As the company said in its November 9, 2026 preliminary disclosure, most of the holding's consolidated results depend on subsidiary Simmtech, so the subsidiary's quarterly trend is the nearest checkpoint.

In its November 4, 2026 investor relations materials, subsidiary Simmtech said it expects system IC sales to rise to KRW 96.6 billion in Q4 2026. The same materials expected full-year system IC sales of KRW 329.3 billion, up 63% year on year.

The company also outlined plans to expand into next-generation LPCAMM and CXL modules and substrates. In a November 2026 report, Hana Securities raised its 2027 operating profit estimate for subsidiary Simmtech by 17% and expected 2027 results to improve substantially from 2026.

However, this outlook is at the subsidiary level and does not guarantee that the gains will flow through to the net profit attributable to controlling shareholders at the holding level. How much subsidiary profit is attributed to controlling shareholders should be checked in the consolidated notes of periodic reports.

The next periodic report, the 2026 annual report, will disclose segment and holding-level profit structure together.

The nearest checkpoints are how much SOCAMM2 and high-value substrate sales show up in Q4 preliminary results, and whether net profit attributable to controlling shareholders moves out of the Q3 2026 loss.

Pace of operating profit recovery

Subsidiary Simmtech said its Q3 2026 operating margin rose 6.8 percentage points to about 19%. Operating profit growth far outpacing revenue growth is cited as the core bullish evidence.

Expansion of high-value AI server products

Subsidiary Simmtech's SOCAMM-related module sales reached KRW 56.9 billion in Q3 2026, up 35% from the prior quarter. MSAP sales rose 8% to KRW 331.2 billion. Supply of SOCAMM2 substrates for NVIDIA AI accelerators, which began in 2026, is cited as a basis for medium- to long-term growth.

Recent profit at the controlling-shareholder level

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a gain of KRW 1.78 billion in Q2 2026, improving from a loss of KRW 7.86 billion in Q1 2026. The quarterly record shows that the controlling-shareholder line can turn positive while operating profit grows. How far subsidiary earnings carry through to consolidated profit is the premise of the bullish case.

Financial and cash flow

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 1.22 trillion, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 327.0%. Operating cash flow was -KRW 139 billion in 2025. With KRW 25.6 billion attributable to controlling shareholders, the cushion against continued losses is limited.

Overall view

Simmtech Holdings is a holding company whose consolidated profit moves with the substrate business of its subsidiary Simmtech. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 104.7 billion rose 4,033.0% year on year, yet the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 11.3 billion in the same quarter.

Operating losses continued from 2023 through 2025, and in 2026 operating profit rose in Q2 and Q3 after a Q1 low. On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio was 327.0% at end-2025, and only KRW 25.6 billion of equity belongs to controlling shareholders, which leaves limited room to absorb further losses.

Positive factors include higher sales of high-value products such as SOCAMM2 and MSAP, and analysis pointing to better pricing power as customers diversify.

Negative factors include the KRW 29.3 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, dependence on one subsidiary, raw material price swings and possible dilution from convertible bond conversions.

No price-to-earnings ratio is calculated, and the price-to-book ratio sits well above 1x, so the share price reflects how the market reads the medium- to long-term earnings path of the subsidiary's substrate business.

The next checkpoints are preliminary Q4 and full-year 2026 results, the cash flow and debt-to-equity ratio in the 2026 annual report, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in Q1 2027.
