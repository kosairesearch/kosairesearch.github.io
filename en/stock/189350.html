02

Business structure

Coset is an optical components maker founded in 1999 and headquartered in Gwangju, listed on KONEX in December 2013. The company manufactures and sells components that combine lasers and optical fibers used in optical communication equipment.

In its 2025 sales breakdown, optical communication transceiver modules and components account for 100% of revenue, so the business is concentrated in a single product group.

By product, optical amplifier devices contribute the largest share of sales, optical communication OSA sales also grew, and optical transceiver modules reportedly grew more than eightfold year on year.

The market data presented in the company's business report put the 2025 industrial laser market at about USD 10.5 billion with 8.5% projected annual growth, and cited 11.5% annual growth for defense laser applications.

The company's 2026–2030 investment plan covers industrial optical sensors, high-power semiconductor laser modules, high-power VCSEL heaters and LiDAR modules, extending beyond optical communication. On production, the disclosed capacity table shows 2025 output of 101,413 units against 57,400 in 2024. In the sector, TAIHAN Fiberoptics is classified alongside Coset as a listed optical components peer.

03

Recent trends

Coset's results have swung considerably since 2023. Revenue was KRW 14,165.01 million in 2023, fell to KRW 9,793.11 million in 2024, and that year recorded an operating loss of KRW 1,603.55 million and a net loss of KRW 1,991.63 million.

In 2025 revenue rose 8.62% to KRW 10,637.03 million, and the company returned to profit with operating profit of KRW 1,605.07 million and net income of KRW 1,208.90 million.

Gross profit increased from KRW 966.98 million in 2024 to KRW 3,797.29 million in 2025, while SG&A expenses fell from KRW 2,570.53 million to KRW 2,192.22 million. Based on the disclosed figures, the 2025 operating margin is about 15% and the gross margin about 35.7%.

At the end of 2025, total assets were KRW 19,584 million, total liabilities KRW 14,723 million and total equity KRW 4,861 million. The share price was KRW 1,400 on March 24, 2026, KRW 1,820 on September 29, 2026, and has traded within a 52-week range of KRW 935 to KRW 2,400.

On the financial side, total liabilities of roughly three times equity keep debt service and earnings continuity under scrutiny. Whether the 2025 return to profit carries into quarterly and semi-annual reports from 2026 is the second checkpoint.

On the market side, KONEX daily trading value fell year on year in 2026, and no company had transferred from KONEX to KOSDAQ year to date as of August 2026. Bull factors

Profit structure improved in reported figures

Gross profit rose from KRW 966.98 million in 2024 to KRW 3,797.29 million in 2025. SG&A expenses declined to KRW 2,192.22 million, which carried through to the return to operating profit. Based on disclosed figures, the gross margin improved from about 9.9% in 2024 to about 35.7% in 2025. This suggests that cost and expense structure changed alongside the revenue recovery.

Development roadmap beyond optical communication

The company's business report lists industrial optical sensor development and medical laser module diversification as its first 2026 priority. Industrial and defense high-power semiconductor laser modules are slated for 2027, and high-power VCSEL heaters and advanced defense lasers for 2028.

LiDAR modules and quantum sensor materials and components appear as plans from 2029 onward. The same material cites 11.5% annual growth for the defense laser market.

Sharp increase in production volume

The disclosed capacity table shows 2025 capacity of 150,000 and output of 101,413. Compared with 57,400 units of output in 2024, this is an increase of roughly 77%. Output growth outpaced the 8.62% revenue increase, pointing to a change in product mix or operating rate. The link between volume and revenue can still vary with product mix.

Bear factors

High leverage and limited equity cushion

Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 14,723 million against total equity of KRW 4,861 million. Liabilities account for about 75% of total capital, leaving limited financial headroom. In 2024, a net loss of KRW 1,991.63 million cut equity to KRW 1,453.96 million before the 2025 profit restored it. With a thin equity base, earnings swings feed directly into financial stability.

Single product group and revenue volatility

Optical communication transceiver modules and components account for 100% of sales. Revenue fell about 30.9% from KRW 14,165.01 million in 2023 to KRW 9,793.11 million in 2024. The 2025 recovery to KRW 10,637.03 million still left sales below the 2023 level.

With revenue resting on one product group, order changes for specific products or customers can flow through to results quickly.

KONEX liquidity and governance items to monitor

KONEX's average daily trading value in July 2026 was KRW 1,280 million, a thin level. Individual investors accounted for more than 90% of buy-side trading value across the market. A representative director stepped down in December 2025.

At the March 2026 shareholders' meeting, approval of the 27th financial statements drew support equal to 43.3% of issued voting shares.

Business

Revenue depends on optical communication transceiver modules and components, so demand shifts in that product group feed directly into company-wide results. Optical amplifier devices hold the largest sales share, which makes order flow for that product a key variable.

Timing for new application development to convert into revenue has not been specified in the disclosures reviewed.

Liquidity & governance

On September 29, 2026, the share price traded at KRW 1,820, below the previous close of KRW 2,140, within an intraday range of KRW 1,820 to KRW 2,145. Liquidity is thin enough that trading value can be zero, so price moves can be sharp.

The December 2025 representative director departure and the 43.3% support ratio at the March 2026 meeting remain items to monitor on management continuity.

08

Overall view

Coset is a small optical components maker that recorded a return to profit on a separate basis in 2025. Gross profit and operating profit both improved, and output growth and the 2030 development plan stand out as business-side positives.

On the other hand, total liabilities are roughly three times total equity, and 100% of sales come from optical communication transceiver modules and components, a high degree of concentration. Sharp swings in revenue and earnings from 2023 through 2024 remain a weakness for earnings stability.

On the market side, KONEX trading value has contracted and individual investors account for most buy-side volume, so price signals lack continuity. On governance, the December 2025 representative director departure and the 43.3% support ratio at the March 2026 meeting remain items to track.
