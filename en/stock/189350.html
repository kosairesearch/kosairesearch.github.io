A Small Optical-Parts Maker Back in the Black

Coset returned to an operating profit of KRW 1.61 billion in 2025, and the question is whether that result can last amid AI data-center demand for optical communications.

02

Business structure

Coset was founded in October 1999 as a maker of laser modules for optical communications. It is headquartered in Buk-gu, Gwangju, and listed on the KONEX market in December 2013.

According to its annual report, the company manufactures and sells components that combine lasers with optical fiber for optical communication equipment.

Its core product is the pump laser module for optical amplifiers, and its website also presents transmit and receive parts such as TOSA and ROSA for 400G-class transceivers. The business is effectively a single segment. In 2025, product sales made up most of revenue, while service revenue was only KRW 82.38 million.

Press reports indicate that in 2023, optical-amplifier device sales of KRW 12.42 billion accounted for most of the KRW 14.17 billion total revenue.

The company says it intends to widen its applications to optical sensor modules and high-power laser modules for industrial, medical and defense uses. Its approach is to keep products with core technology in domestic production while using overseas partners for component production and some assembly.

It also plans to diversify suppliers of key inputs such as high-power semiconductor laser chips and VCSEL array chips. The 2025 annual report shows production capacity of 90,000 units, output of 56,340 units and a utilization rate of 62.6%.

Among listed peers in the same sector, TAIHAN Fiberoptics makes optical fiber and cable, which contrasts with Coset's laser-module position. KMW sits in the telecom-equipment end of the market. Coset is a small component maker at the laser-device and module stage rather than a downstream system supplier. It had roughly 78 employees as of 2025. R&D expense in 2025 was KRW 1.08 billion.

03

Recent trends

According to the 2025 annual report, Coset's revenue was KRW 10.64 billion, up from KRW 9.79 billion in 2024, an increase of about 8.6%. Operating profit was KRW 1.61 billion, a turnaround from an operating loss of KRW 1.60 billion in 2024. Net profit was KRW 1.21 billion.

Cost of sales drove the improvement. It fell to KRW 6.84 billion in 2025 from KRW 8.83 billion in 2024. Gross profit rose from KRW 0.97 billion to KRW 3.80 billion. Selling and administrative expenses also declined, from KRW 2.57 billion to KRW 2.19 billion.

The longer record shows large swings. In 2023, revenue was KRW 14.17 billion and operating profit was KRW 0.68 billion. In 2024 revenue fell and the company swung to a loss, and in 2025 it returned to profit. The 2025 operating margin was about 15.1%.

On the balance sheet at the end of 2025, total assets were KRW 19.58 billion and total liabilities were KRW 14.72 billion. Total equity was KRW 4.86 billion. Filings show an investor-relations briefing announced on June 5, 2026 and its result reported on June 24, 2026. We could not confirm first-half 2026 earnings figures through our searches.

As of 17:02 KST on October 27, 2026, the share price was KRW 2,000, down 3.85% from the previous session. Market capitalization was KRW 9.6 billion. Market-data providers show a 52-week low of KRW 935 and a high of KRW 2,400. Traded value was only KRW 454,700, which points to very sparse trading.

04

Outlook

Coset's outlook depends heavily on AI data-center investment and on supply conditions for optical components. An August 18, 2026 report cited Bank of America as forecasting that the global optical transceiver market will more than double, from under USD 13 billion in 2025 to USD 26 billion in 2026.

In the same report, the US optical-communications company AAOI said customer demand exceeds the volume it can supply by 20% to 40%. Such shortages suggest demand may carry through to component makers, but whether this applies directly to Coset's products needs separate confirmation.

In its annual report, the company said use of 14xx Raman pump modules is rising sharply as AI data centers become more advanced. Its strategy is to lower the cost of this product and so raise revenue and operating profit. Among business indicators, the 62.6% utilization rate leaves room for improvement. The actual extent will depend on future orders and the cost structure.

There is also a policy variable. According to the same report, the US Federal Communications Commission is considering a ban on imports of new Chinese optical data components, but this is not a confirmed measure. Chinese makers accounted for more than half of global optical transceiver shipments in 2025.

Some observers point to possible extra demand for non-Chinese supply chains, but data-center parts require certification and mass-production validation. Coset's own customer mix and North American delivery record could not be confirmed in our research.

The company says it will expand sales into high-power sensors and defense applications and pursue factory automation and supplier diversification. Whether these plans translate into results will have to be checked in future quarterly, half-year and annual reports.

05

Bull factors

Return to profit and a wider margin

The 2025 operating profit of KRW 1.61 billion reversed the loss of 2024. Gross profit rose from KRW 0.97 billion to KRW 3.80 billion. The operating margin reached about 15.1%. If the cost structure keeps improving, margins have room to improve further from a utilization rate of 62.6%.

AI data-center demand for optical parts

The company says use of the 14xx Raman pump modules in AI data centers is increasing. An August 18, 2026 report relayed Bank of America's forecast that the global optical transceiver market will more than double in 2026. Industry comments in the same report said demand for some optical components exceeds supply. This is a supportive backdrop for demand for Coset's main products.

Wider applications and non-China supply-chain themes

Coset says it will widen its business into industrial, medical and defense sensors and high-power laser modules. This direction could help offset a revenue base concentrated in one field. A report that the US is considering a ban on imports of Chinese optical components could raise interest in non-Chinese suppliers. That measure is not confirmed, however.

06

Bear factors

Earnings volatility and low utilization

Coset swung from an operating profit in 2023 to an operating loss in 2024 and back to a profit in 2025. Its 2025 utilization rate was 62.6%, which leaves spare capacity. That is room for improvement but also signals a heavy fixed-cost burden. With a single-segment business, a drop in orders could erode results quickly.

Liabilities large relative to equity

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 14.72 billion, about three times total equity of KRW 4.86 billion. Current liabilities were KRW 8.87 billion. Although the company turned a profit, its financial cushion is not large. Any rise in investment or working-capital needs could bring borrowing pressure to the fore.

Scale limits of a small supplier

Coset has about 78 employees and annual revenue in the KRW 10 billion range. It relies on domestic and overseas partners for some key inputs such as high-power semiconductor laser chips. Its capacity to meet price, delivery and certification demands from larger overseas rivals may be limited. Its data-center customer mix and order sizes are also not clearly confirmed in its filings.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Demand for optical components is tied to the scale of AI data-center investment by large North American cloud operators. Cuts or delays in capital spending plans could affect component orders. Coset has an export-oriented profile, so it is also sensitive to exchange rates and trade rules. The company itself listed securing outside advisers on currency and trade regulation as part of its response.

Industry

Chinese makers account for more than half of global optical transceiver shipments. A change in the direction of US regulation could alter the supply chain and pricing. The regulatory review is not final and could be revised or withdrawn.

Technology shifts such as silicon photonics and co-packaged optics could also change the structure of component demand.

Company and liquidity

Coset is listed on KONEX with a market capitalization of KRW 9.6 billion. Traded value on October 27, 2026 was only KRW 454,700. Even small volumes can move the price sharply, and it may be hard to trade a desired quantity at a desired price. There may also be periods when the share price does not track reported results.

08

Overall view

Coset is a small KONEX-listed maker of pump laser modules for optical communications. Its 2025 annual report shows revenue of KRW 10.64 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.61 billion, a turnaround from the operating loss of 2024. The profit improvement owed more to lower cost of sales than to revenue growth.

On the industry side, AI data-center investment and a larger optical transceiver market form a supportive backdrop. On the other hand, the durability of earnings, liabilities that are large relative to equity, and thin trading liquidity should be considered alongside.

Results after the first half of 2026 and progress on cost improvement for the 14xx Raman pump modules should be checked in future filings. This report is provided for information only, and the judgment is left to the reader.
