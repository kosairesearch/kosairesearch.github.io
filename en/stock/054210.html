02

Business structure

Founded in 1982 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2002, Elentec is an electronic components manufacturer whose core businesses span phone cases, battery packs, chargers, e-cigarette original design manufacturing (ODM), and new mid-to-large battery pack ventures.

According to a Dailyinvest report, in the third quarter of 2025 revenue mix by segment was phone cases 40.5%, e-cigarettes 23.4%, phone battery packs 9.8%, e-cigarette cartridges 3.7%, power-tool battery packs 2.2%, chargers 2%, and ESS battery packs 0.04%, meaning phone cases and e-cigarettes together account for a large share of sales.

The e-cigarette segment is an ODM business manufacturing KT&G's 'lil SOLID' heated-tobacco product line, and the company later took on LG Electronics' residential energy storage system (ESS) under the same ODM model.

The mid-to-large battery pack business began with residential ESS and has expanded into mobility and robotics battery packs, and, as finalized in November 2025, EV battery packs for China's Chery Automobile.

The EV battery pack business involves Elentec participating as a production partner in an 8-gigawatt-hour (GWh) 46-series cell supply agreement that LG Energy Solution signed with Chery Automobile, a volume equivalent to roughly 120,000 electric vehicles.

In 2026 Elentec also signed an additional contract to supply battery management systems (BMS) for LG Energy Solution's ESS business, broadening its customer base across both automakers and energy companies.

Production sites include the company's Dongtan headquarters in Korea as well as overseas subsidiaries in Vietnam and India as it pushes further into global markets.

Traditional mobile component businesses such as phone cases and chargers remain the revenue base, even as the new mid-to-large battery pack business emerges as a fresh growth pillar.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 997.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 751.2 billion in 2023 (down 24.7% year on year) and KRW 556 billion in 2024 (down 26.0%), before rebounding to KRW 753.2 billion in 2025 (up 35.5%); among 2022-2025, revenue was largest in 2022 and smallest in 2024.

Operating profit fell from KRW 79.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 30.4 billion in 2023 (down 61.8%) and KRW 16.1 billion in 2024 (down 47.1%), before rising to KRW 43.2 billion in 2025 (up 168.1%), with the operating margin improving from 2.9% in 2024 to 5.7% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 54.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 355 million in 2024 before surging to KRW 26.7 billion in 2025 (up 7,414.4%), highlighting a wide gap between the smallest year (2024) and largest year (2022).

On a quarterly basis, revenue rose from KRW 154.6 billion in Q2 2025 to KRW 225.6 billion in Q3 (up 45.9%) and KRW 251.9 billion in Q4 (up 11.7%), peaking before falling for two consecutive quarters to KRW 235.2 billion in Q1 2026 (down 6.6%) and KRW 213.5 billion in Q2 2026 (down 9.2%).

Quarterly operating profit likewise peaked at KRW 20.9 billion in Q4 2025 before declining for two straight quarters to KRW 17.5 billion in Q1 2026 (down 16.1%) and KRW 7.11 billion in Q2 2026 (down 59.5%), even as Q2 2026 revenue grew 38.2% from a year earlier.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss of KRW 1.89 billion in Q2 2025 to a profit of KRW 11.5 billion in Q3, rising to KRW 22.9 billion in Q4, before falling back to KRW 14.9 billion in Q1 2026 (down 35.0%) and KRW 3.23 billion in Q2 2026 (down 78.3%).

The sum of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters (Q3 2025 to Q2 2026) was KRW 52.5 billion, exceeding full-year 2025 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 26.7 billion.

Operating cash flow fell to KRW 34.6 billion in 2025 from KRW 79.5 billion in 2024, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 118.6% from 102.7%, indicating heavier working-capital pressure even as the top line recovered.

05

Industry analysis

The global ESS and EV battery market has been expanding in 2026, led by North America. According to a Seoul Shinmun report, LG Energy Solution posted record quarterly results in Q3 2026 with revenue of KRW 9.6434 trillion and operating profit of KRW 756 billion, driven by North American ESS market growth.

According to a report by The Elec, LG Energy Solution is expanding 46-series cylindrical battery production at its Ochang plant and an Arizona facility in the United States, with customers including Tesla, Rivian, Mercedes-Benz, Chery Automobile, and King Long Motor.

Elentec participates as a battery pack production partner in the Chery Automobile 46-series supply agreement, positioning it to benefit from growth at the downstream end of the EV value chain.

In ESS, Elentec continues residential ESS ODM supply centered on LG Electronics and has begun supplying mid-to-large ESS battery management systems to LG Energy Solution, broadening its customer base across automakers and energy companies.

However, Elentec's traditional businesses—phone cases, chargers, and e-cigarette ODM—are tied to the product cycles and demand of a small number of client companies, resulting in significant quarter-to-quarter volatility.

Within the electronic components sector, companies such as Seojin System, Solum, and Shinsung Delta Tech are increasingly supplying battery and ESS-related components as well, making the competitive landscape in mid-to-large battery packs progressively more complex.

Outlook

Elentec began recognizing revenue in 2026 from the Chery Automobile 46-series EV battery pack supply agreement finalized in November 2025, an 8-gigawatt-hour (GWh) contract equivalent to roughly 120,000 electric vehicles.

In 2026 Elentec also added a contract to supply battery management systems for LG Energy Solution's ESS business, extending its relationship with LG Energy Solution from EVs into ESS.

In a March 2026 report, SK Securities projected that the earnings downturn that bottomed out in 2024 had ended, with 2025 marking a rebound and 2026 expected to see the ESS and EV battery pack businesses move into full gear.

However, both Q1 and Q2 2026 results showed declines in revenue and operating profit versus Q4 2025, indicating that the ramp-up of new businesses and seasonal softness in legacy businesses such as e-cigarettes and phone cases are both in play.

The company is also developing additional new businesses, including robotics battery packs and a battery-swap system for Honda. The next quarterly results are scheduled for disclosure in mid-November 2026, with the key point to watch being how much the mid-to-large battery pack segment's revenue share actually grows.

08

Bull factors

Diversification into New Mid-to-Large Battery Pack Businesses

Elentec is simultaneously pursuing multiple new businesses: EVs (46-series packs for China's Chery Automobile), ESS (ODM for LG Electronics and BMS for LG Energy Solution), and robotics/mobility (a battery-swap system for Honda, among others).

The EV battery pack contract covers 8 gigawatt-hours (GWh), equivalent to about 120,000 vehicles, making it a meaningful single project. In ESS, the relationship with LG Energy Solution has broadened from residential ODM to mid-to-large BMS supply, diversifying the customer base.

With several new businesses advancing in parallel, there is potential to reduce dependence on any single customer or product.

2025 Earnings Rebound and Profit Recovery

Revenue and operating profit grew 35.5% and 168.1% year on year, respectively, in 2025, pulling the company out of its 2024 trough. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders also rose sharply, from KRW 355 million in 2024 to KRW 26.7 billion in 2025.

The sum of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters (Q3 2025-Q2 2026) was KRW 52.5 billion, exceeding the full-year 2025 figure. This shows profit recovered largely on improved profitability in existing businesses, even before the new businesses fully ramped up.

Increased Controlling Shareholder Stake

The controlling shareholder group led by Hae-sung Lee increased its stake from 33.14% in March 2026 to 33.53% in October 2026, partly through on-market purchases by affiliate Elensys. The addition of a related party and the on-market stake increase are noteworthy from a supply-demand perspective.

However, this is a governance and ownership fact rather than a signal guaranteeing any particular share price direction.

Bear factors

Quarterly Results Softened for Two Straight Quarters

After peaking in Q4 2025, revenue, operating profit, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders all declined for two consecutive quarters in Q1 and Q2 2026. Q2 2026 operating profit fell 59.5% from the prior quarter to KRW 7.11 billion. While revenue rose year on year, a clear sequential slowdown was evident.

Rising Balance-Sheet Pressure

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 118.6% at end-2025 from 102.7% a year earlier. Over the same period, operating cash flow fell to KRW 34.6 billion from KRW 79.5 billion in 2024. Rising working-capital pressure even as revenue grows underscores the need to monitor financial capacity as new businesses expand.

Customer Concentration Risk in Legacy Businesses

Phone cases and e-cigarette ODM, which account for a large share of revenue, are tied to the product cycles of a small number of clients, producing significant quarterly volatility. The large swings in quarterly revenue from Q2 2025 through Q1 2026 are related to this structure.

Until the new businesses are firmly established, cyclical swings in legacy businesses could continue to affect results.

Risk factors

Customer and Order Concentration

Elentec's core businesses depend heavily on a small number of clients, including KT&G (e-cigarettes), LG Electronics and LG Energy Solution (ESS), and Chery Automobile (EVs). A delay in a client's new product launch or a reduction in orders could directly affect results. The new businesses are also still at an early stage, with customer diversification not yet fully achieved.

Pace of New-Business Ramp-up

Both the Chery Automobile EV battery pack and the LG Energy Solution ESS BMS supply are new businesses whose revenue began ramping up around 2026. The pace of revenue growth could vary depending on production yield, quality validation, and additional order wins.

The earnings slowdown in Q1-Q2 2026 shows that new-business revenue has not yet fully offset seasonal swings in legacy businesses.

Balance Sheet and Working Capital

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 118.6% at end-2025 from a year earlier, and operating cash flow also declined. If working-capital pressure persists amid simultaneous revenue growth and new-business investment, the need for additional financing could increase. Changes in the balance sheet warrant continued monitoring through subsequent regulatory filings.

Overall view

Elentec's 2025 revenue and operating profit grew sharply from a year earlier, pulling the company out of its 2024 trough, but growth momentum cooled in Q1 and Q2 2026 as revenue and operating profit each declined for two consecutive quarters.

The new mid-to-large battery pack business is expanding through EV battery packs for China's Chery Automobile and ESS BMS supply for LG Energy Solution, and whether this becomes a growth pillar complementing the legacy phone case and e-cigarette ODM revenue base is the key question.

On the balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 118.6% at end-2025 from a year earlier and operating cash flow declined, indicating heavier working-capital pressure alongside the top-line recovery.

The controlling shareholder group's stake stood at 33.53% as of an October 2026 filing, up slightly from earlier in the year, and the next quarterly results are scheduled for disclosure in mid-November 2026.

How much, and how quickly, the new businesses contribute to revenue remains the central variable for judging future earnings and financial structure.
