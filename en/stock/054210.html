Battery-pack transition; Q3 2026 turns to a loss

02

Business structure

Elentec was founded in 1982 to manufacture and sell electronic components and listed on KOSDAQ in 2002. Built on battery-pack manufacturing, it produces smartphone battery packs, cases in plastic and metal, and chargers for global handset makers.

According to a breakdown of 2025 sales in SK Securities' March 2026 materials, smartphone cases contributed KRW 297.9 billion, e-cigarettes KRW 223.4 billion, IT and small-appliance battery packs KRW 91.6 billion, and other items KRW 103.9 billion.

In the same materials, smartphone cases were KRW 228.6 billion and e-cigarettes KRW 110.8 billion in 2024. Medium-to-large battery packs, which cover power tools, notebooks, vacuum cleaners and ESS, accounted for about 4% of 2025 sales.

Production sites include overseas subsidiaries in Malaysia, Vietnam (Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City) and India. Customers include Samsung Electronics and other global companies, and the company says it has in-house design and manufacturing for protection circuit modules.

Because smartphone cases are the largest line and e-cigarettes follow, demand in the handset market carries heavy weight. Competition takes place at the pack and protection-circuit stage rather than at the cell level. If medium-to-large packs grow, the customer and product base could diversify.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 997.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 751.2 billion in 2023 (down 24.7%) and KRW 556 billion in 2024 (down 26.0%), then rose to KRW 753.2 billion in 2025 (up 35.5%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 79.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 30.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 16.1 billion in 2024, then rose to KRW 43.2 billion in 2025 (up 168.1%). The operating margin was 8.0%, 4.1%, 2.9% and 5.7% over the same years.

The company attributed the 2025 change to higher sales of mobile and e-cigarette products and better profitability. SK Securities cited new e-cigarette products and markets (up 101.6%), more smartphone-case volume (up 30.3%) and company-wide cost restructuring.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 355 million in 2024 to KRW 26.7 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow, however, fell from KRW 79.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 34.6 billion in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 118.6%.

Quarterly operating profit peaked at KRW 20.9 billion in Q4 2025 after KRW 15.4 billion in Q3 2025, then fell to KRW 17.5 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 7.11 billion in Q2 2026.

Q3 2026 revenue rose to KRW 266.2 billion, but operating profit fell to KRW 9.05 billion and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.31 billion. That quarter shows revenue growth that did not carry through to profit.

05

Industry analysis

Smartphones remain the largest end market. Analysts attribute demand for handset cases to the strong launch of Samsung Electronics' foldable phones and to rising share in India's super-premium segment. The e-cigarette business is described as growing steadily.

In medium-to-large batteries, ESS demand is the key variable. After a bill to block Chinese ESS imports was introduced in the US Congress in February 2026, Korean ESS-related companies drew attention, and Elentec was named as a possible beneficiary.

In 2025 on an annual basis, Elentec ranked fifth in revenue and third in operating profit among the 54 listed companies in the mobile-phone parts industry. SK Securities judged that the earnings downturn bottomed in 2024.

Smartphone-case volume still depends heavily on customers' new-model cycles, so the cycle's swings remain large. Battery packs for Chery's 46-series vehicles and the ESS BMS supply to LG Energy Solution are forming new pillars of the medium-to-large segment.

06

Outlook

In a March 2026 report, SK Securities estimated 2026 revenue at KRW 904.1 billion, operating profit at KRW 54.6 billion and an operating margin of 6.0%. These are broker estimates and not company guidance. Medium-to-large growth has several routes.

Battery packs for Chery's 46-series vehicles were confirmed in November 2025, with roughly KRW 30 billion in sales expected in 2026, the first supply year. In ESS, sales of LFP ESS battery-pack BMS for Europe begin in 2026, and the same report expected full-scale sales growth from North America starting in 2027.

In robotics, the company supplies or is developing battery packs for serving robots, companion robots, AMR robots and humanoid developers. A February 2026 industry report said the company agreed to supply battery management systems for LG Energy Solution's ESS business.

The next point to check is how operating profit for the first three quarters relates to the annual estimate of KRW 54.6 billion, with Q4 2025 operating profit of KRW 20.9 billion as the comparison base. Whether medium-to-large sales rise toward the estimated share in 2026 is the other point to watch.

Bull factors

Expansion of medium-to-large battery packs

SK Securities expects ESS battery-pack BMS sales for Europe to begin in 2026 and North American growth to become full-scale from 2027. Battery packs for Chery's 46-series vehicles were confirmed in November 2025, with about KRW 30 billion in sales expected from 2026. Robotics supply and development, including serving and AMR robots, is also under way.

Profit recovery and cost structure in 2025

2025 revenue and operating profit rose 35.5% and 168.1% to KRW 753.2 billion and KRW 43.2 billion. The cited drivers were new e-cigarette products and markets, higher smartphone-case volume from new models, and company-wide cost restructuring.

The operating margin recovered from 2.9% in 2024 to 5.7% in 2025, showing the earnings base rising from a low point.

ESS policy environment and customer access

After a US congressional bill to block Chinese ESS imports, Korean battery and ESS-related companies drew attention. Industry reports in February 2026 said Elentec agreed to supply battery management systems for LG Energy Solution's ESS business.

The reports describe this as entry into the global ESS value chain and a wider customer base in medium-to-large batteries.

Bear factors

Q3 swing to loss and profit trend

Q3 2026 operating profit fell 41.2% year on year to KRW 9.05 billion, and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.31 billion. Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 7.11 billion had fallen 59.5% from the prior quarter. Revenue rose 18.0% to KRW 266.2 billion, but profit did not follow. The four-quarter total of KRW 32.7 billion includes the loss quarter.

Cash flow and rising financial burden

Operating cash flow fell to KRW 34.6 billion in 2025 from KRW 79.5 billion in 2024. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 102.7% to 118.6%, and total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 366.8 billion. Cash generation fell even as profit rose, which raises questions about working capital or investment needs. Combined with the lower operating profit in 2026, the room in the balance sheet could narrow.

Concentration in smartphone cases and e-cigarettes

In 2025, smartphone cases were the largest line at KRW 297.9 billion and e-cigarettes followed at KRW 223.4 billion, per SK Securities' breakdown. Medium-to-large battery packs were only about 4% of 2025 sales.

If that segment does not grow as estimated for 2026, earnings will stay tied to smartphone-case volume and the timing of customers' new models. Case demand moves from quarter to quarter with customers' product launch schedules.

Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

Smartphone cases and e-cigarettes are the main revenue sources, so results are sensitive to specific customers' orders and new-model timing. A slowdown in smartphone demand or lower customer volumes could widen quarterly swings.

The medium-to-large customer base is also concentrated in a few companies, including LG Energy Solution, so changes to their supply plans would have an effect.

Policy and ESS demand uncertainty

A US bill to block Chinese ESS imports is cited as an opportunity for Korean companies, but its passage and timing are not settled. Elentec has supplied residential ESS to Europe since May 2025. If North American sales do not expand as planned, the growth path SK Securities outlined for 2027 could slip.

Ownership structure and liquidity

As of July 23, 2026, a group led by Lee Hae-sung held 38.15% and the free float ratio was 60.25%. With a large controlling stake, tradable supply is limited, and price moves can be amplified when volume is thin. Treasury stock holdings are also small.

Overall view

Elentec's results recovered in 2025, with revenue of KRW 753.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 43.2 billion, before a Q3 2026 swing to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.31 billion.

The business centers on smartphone cases and e-cigarettes, while medium-to-large battery packs were about 4% of 2025 sales. Battery packs for Chery's 46-series vehicles, ESS BMS for Europe and the supply to LG Energy Solution are the cited basis for expansion.
