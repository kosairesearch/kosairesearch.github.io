Report overview

Core Business Recovers, Subsidiary Losses Weigh

E-Mart's standalone results for Q3 2026 showed a clear core-business recovery, but losses at subsidiaries such as the Starbucks operator and Shinsegae E&C pulled consolidated operating profit down to KRW 46.1 billion.

  1. 1

    Consolidated operating profit for Q3 2026 fell 69.6% year on year to KRW 46.1 billion, while standalone operating profit rose 46.3% to KRW 166 billion (preliminary, announced November 11, 2026).

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

E-Mart operates a standalone retail business centered on its hypermarket E-Mart, the warehouse club Traders, and the supermarket chain Everyday, alongside affiliates including the online unit SSG.COM, the convenience store chain E-Mart24, SCK Company, which runs Starbucks in Korea, and the construction affiliate Shinsegae E&C.

In Q3 2026 the hypermarket division posted total revenue of KRW 3.26 trillion, up 9.9% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 90.8 billion, according to Hana Securities and Korea Investment & Securities reports dated November 12, 2026.

Traders recorded total revenue of KRW 1.17 trillion, up 16.6%, and operating profit of KRW 55.2 billion, up 40.5%, which the company described as its highest quarterly results to date. Everyday posted Q3 revenue of KRW 399.5 billion, up 7.7%, and operating profit of KRW 11 billion, up 11.1%.

SSG.COM improved its profit and loss by KRW 15.8 billion year on year, and E-Mart24 cut its operating loss by KRW 4.9 billion to KRW 2.9 billion (preliminary figures announced November 11, 2026).

The company credits store-format innovation at Starfield Market locations, private-label products aimed at cost-conscious shoppers, and expansion of quick commerce for the core-business gains. On the competitive side, store closures by rival hypermarkets such as Homeplus are cited as a tailwind from market reshuffling. By contrast, Starbucks and the construction unit add business risks that flow into consolidated results.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual basis, revenue was KRW 29.3 trillion in 2022, KRW 29.5 trillion in 2023, KRW 29 trillion in 2024, and KRW 29 trillion in 2025. Within 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest, and revenue fell for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 135.7 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 46.9 billion in 2023, turned to KRW 47.1 billion in 2024, and rose to KRW 322.5 billion in 2025, up 584.8%. Operating margin stayed in a low range, at 0.5% in 2022, -0.2% in 2023, 0.2% in 2024, and 1.1% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 1.03 trillion in 2022 to a loss of KRW 89.1 billion in 2023 and a loss of KRW 590 billion in 2024, then turned to a profit of KRW 136.2 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 246.3 billion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder measure. The gap between operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income reflects non-operating items, and equity-method losses of KRW 50.6 billion were reported in Q3 2026.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit of KRW 151.4 billion in Q3 2025 was followed by an operating loss of KRW 9.86 billion in Q4 2025, a recovery to KRW 178.3 billion in Q1 2026, an operating loss of KRW 43 billion in Q2 2026, and operating profit of KRW 46.1 billion in Q3 2026.

Revenue slid from KRW 7.4 trillion in Q3 2025 to KRW 7.31 trillion, KRW 7.12 trillion, and KRW 6.92 trillion through Q2 2026, then rose to KRW 7.49 trillion in Q3 2026, up 8.3% quarter on quarter. The sum of net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 245.8 billion.

Q3 2026 net income of KRW 0.6 billion on a consolidated basis reflects the elimination in consolidation of a gain of more than KRW 200 billion from the in-kind contribution of the Myeongil store property to Shinsegae E&C, which is why it differs sharply from standalone net income of KRW 451.5 billion.

05

Industry analysis

For the domestic hypermarket market, the key variable in 2026 has been a reshuffling of competition. Homeplus announced plans in May 2026 to halt operations at 37 hypermarkets, which analysts said could ease competition for discount stores.

An E-Mart official attributed Q3 2026 sales gains partly to a reshuffling of trade areas after rival stores closed. Amid high inflation, value-oriented private-label products and large discount events have been used to draw shoppers. Monthly results are volatile due to shifts in the timing of the Chuseok holiday.

Hana Securities data showed October 2026 same-store sales down 6% at the hypermarket and down 8% at Traders, while Everyday rose 8%. Sales at the November Ssuk Day event rose 10% year on year.

Shinhan Investment Corp. said the company could benefit over the long run from weaker operating capacity at rivals and from regulatory easing, but that near-term credibility of earnings outside the core business is the key issue.

Within the retail sector, E-Mart is positioning its hypermarket and warehouse-club operations as its competitive core.

06

Outlook

The company expects earnings to improve from Q4 onward, citing core-business competitiveness efforts led by Chairman Jung Yong-jin and the progress of large mixed-use projects such as Starfield Cheongna.

Hana Securities estimated Q4 2026 consolidated operating profit at KRW 48.1 billion, a return to profit with an operating margin of 0.7%, in a report dated November 12, 2026, and said it expects a positive effect from large year-end events. Broker views are split.

Samsung Securities cut its target price to KRW 81,000 and kept a Buy rating in a report dated November 12, 2026, and said resolving subsidiary uncertainty is needed for better visibility. Korea Investment & Securities cut its target from KRW 115,000 to KRW 95,000 and kept a Buy rating on the same date.

Shinhan Investment Corp. cut its target price and said normalizing subsidiaries and improving the capital structure are needed to recover the share price. Hana Securities cut its target price and kept a Neutral rating.

Hanwha Investment & Securities, in a report dated November 12, 2026, cut its target from KRW 100,000 to KRW 67,000 and downgraded its rating to Hold, citing subsidiary uncertainty that outweighed core-business improvement. The next checkpoints are the preliminary Q4 2026 results and the loss trends at SCK Company and Shinsegae E&C.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in Hypermarkets and Traders

Q3 2026 standalone revenue rose 11.1% year on year to KRW 5.10 trillion, and operating profit rose 46.3% to KRW 166 billion (preliminary, announced November 11, 2026). Korea Investment & Securities said same-store sales growth at the hypermarket reached 10.6%, the highest since 2013. The company said Traders reported its highest quarterly results on record.

Easing Competition and Regulatory Relief

An E-Mart official said the reshuffling of trade areas after nearby rival stores closed had a positive effect on sales. Shinhan Investment Corp. said it expects long-term benefits from weaker rival operations and regulatory easing. Whether Homeplus store closures translate into actual gains remains to be confirmed.

Narrowing Losses at Some Subsidiaries

SSG.COM improved its profit and loss by KRW 15.8 billion year on year, and E-Mart24 cut its operating loss by KRW 4.9 billion to KRW 2.9 billion in the Q3 2026 preliminary release. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 136.2 billion in 2025. Hana Securities estimates that consolidated operating profit will return to profit in Q4 2026.

09

Bear factors

Losses at the Starbucks Operator and Construction Unit

SCK Company recorded an operating loss of KRW 45.3 billion, and Shinsegae E&C posted an operating loss of about KRW 99 billion, driven by allowances for doubtful accounts tied to discounted sales at its Busan and Guri Galmae projects, according to Hana Securities.

Equity-method losses of KRW 50.6 billion were also recorded. These losses outweighed the core-business gains and held consolidated operating profit to KRW 46.1 billion.

Consecutive Losses and Lower Target Prices

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders continued for two straight quarters, at KRW 156.3 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 1.09 billion in Q3 2026. Q3 operating profit fell well short of the market consensus of KRW 159.5 billion, according to Hanwha Investment & Securities. Hanwha cut its target price to KRW 67,000 and lowered its rating to Hold in its report dated November 12, 2026.

Capital Allocation and Balance Sheet Burden

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 19.8 trillion, above total equity of KRW 13.7 trillion, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 144.6%. Hanwha Investment & Securities said it has doubts about the appropriateness of capital allocation, including a Paramount stake financed through borrowing. Shinhan Investment Corp. said uncertainty needs to be resolved through an improved capital structure.

10

Risk factors

Subsidiary Earnings Volatility

Results at SCK Company and Shinsegae E&C swing with marketing events, bad-debt allowances, and the settlement of property sales.

A gain of more than KRW 200 billion from the in-kind contribution of the Myeongil store property was offset in consolidation, so one-off standalone gains are not carried into consolidated results. Hana Securities said the size of Shinsegae E&C's operating losses is hard to estimate.

Balance Sheet and Borrowing

The debt-to-equity ratio was 144.6% at the end of 2025, after 157.4% in 2024. Equity attributable to controlling shareholders stood at about KRW 11 trillion, while total liabilities were KRW 19.8 trillion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 1.32 trillion in 2025 and has stayed above KRW 1 trillion since 2023, but financial flexibility depends on borrowing and investment levels.

Consumer Environment and Timing Effects

Monthly same-store results swing with the timing of the Chuseok holiday. Hana Securities data showed hypermarket same-store sales falling 6% in October 2026. Intensifying price competition amid high inflation could limit margin gains, and the operating margin was only 1.1% in 2025.

12

Overall view

E-Mart's standalone results for Q3 2026 showed quarterly total revenue exceeding KRW 5 trillion for the first time, reflecting a visible recovery in the core retail business.

On a consolidated basis, however, losses at the Starbucks operator and Shinsegae E&C offset those gains, holding operating profit to KRW 46.1 billion and extending net losses attributable to controlling shareholders into a second consecutive quarter.

On the disclosed basis, earnings turned positive in 2025 but weakened again in 2026. Brokers moved to lower target prices in reports dated November 12, 2026, and investment opinions are divided between Buy and Hold or Neutral.

A price-to-book ratio well below 1x is consistent with market concern over subsidiary uncertainty and capital allocation. Bullish factors include the core-business recovery and a shifting competitive landscape, while bearish factors include subsidiary results and balance sheet burden.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary Q4 2026 results, whether subsidiary earnings normalize, and the confirmed figures in the 2026 annual report.
