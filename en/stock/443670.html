Report overview

Profit Rebounds, but Operating Margin Keeps Falling

SPSoft's net income attributable to controlling shareholders recovered to KRW 3.81 billion in 2025, but operating profit has declined for three straight years and the operating margin fell to 6.6%.

  1. 1

    Revenue reached KRW 54.5 billion in 2025, up 20.8% from the prior year and the largest year in the 2022-2025 period. Operating profit, however, fell 3.7% to KRW 3.62 billion.

  2. 2

    According to the company, the net income rebound reflects the absence of one-off costs tied to the 2024 SPAC merger listing and share-exchange costs from the subsidiary acquisition.

  3. 3

    Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 580 million, down 50.4% year on year and the lowest of the last five quarters on a reported basis.

  4. 4

    The Microsoft SPLA distribution position and the data-center subsidiary form the two pillars of the revenue base, while the Adobe partner status and Microsoft security solution sales are presented as new growth drivers.

  5. 5

    Kinx is the largest shareholder with a 41.28% stake as of June 17, 2026, and a treasury-share special money trust contract was terminated upon expiry on May 29, 2026.

02

Business structure

SPSoft is an IT and software company that supplies and distributes Microsoft software and also runs data-center services. The company was listed on KOSDAQ on February 15, 2024, through a SPAC merger.

It has built its business on a distributor position for Microsoft products sold under the Services Provider License Agreement (SPLA) model, and a May 2025 report described most revenue as coming from software sales and distribution.

The same report cited IBK Investment & Securities, which estimated in a June 2024 publication that the company held a 70% share of the domestic Microsoft SW SPLA market.

According to an IBK Investment & Securities report from June 2025, the business is divided into three areas: Microsoft software, desktop as a service (DaaS), and solutions. Data-center revenue through a subsidiary acquired on August 30, 2024 added to the expansion in 2025.

The company attributed its 2025 performance to supply of its core AI software products and to higher data-center revenue at the subsidiary. In new business, it obtained Adobe VIP Marketplace partner status, launched an integrated software platform combining its licenses, and is extending into AI as a Service (AIaaS).

In April 2026 the company said Microsoft security solution sales had risen sharply as AI-enabled security threats grew. Its customers are mainly small and mid-sized companies and hosting providers, and the May 2025 report noted that large companies tend to adopt AI through ICT affiliates within their groups.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on reported figures, revenue rose from KRW 33.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 35.2 billion in 2023, KRW 45.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 54.5 billion in 2025, with 2025 up 20.8% from the prior year.

Operating profit over the same period went from KRW 4.87 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.75 billion, KRW 3.76 billion and KRW 3.62 billion, declining for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. The operating margin fell from 14.6% in 2022 to 13.5%, 8.3% and 6.6%.

Revenue grew faster than operating profit, and in 2024 the SPAC listing and subsidiary acquisition costs overlapped with the decline.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 4.11 billion in 2022 and KRW 4.38 billion in 2023 to KRW 302 million in 2024, a 93.1% drop, before rising to KRW 3.81 billion in 2025, up 1,162.7%.

The company attributed the 2024 decline to one-off SPAC listing costs and share-exchange costs, and the 2025 recovery to those costs no longer recurring. Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts as controlling-shareholder net income in every year from 2022 to 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 13.2 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 14.1 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 12.9 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 13.1 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 12.9 billion in Q3 2026.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.17 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 890 million, KRW 848 million, KRW 852 million and KRW 580 million in Q3 2026, the lowest of the five quarters.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 1.29 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 910 million, KRW 622 million and KRW 641 million in the first three quarters of 2026, and the four-quarter total from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026 is KRW 3.46 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 4.05 billion in 2022, KRW 7.22 billion in 2023, KRW 1.31 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.3 billion in 2025, a volatile pattern.

The debt ratio at end-2025 was 17.4%, the lowest of the four years, with equity of KRW 67.7 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities of KRW 11.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Because the company's main distribution product is Microsoft software, Microsoft's pricing and AI product strategy feed directly into its results.

In a June 9, 2026 report, IBK Investment & Securities cited Microsoft 365 price increases and higher public-sector budgets as grounds for expecting improved second-half 2026 earnings. A June 2025 IBK report also pointed to small and mid-sized companies' growing AI adoption as aligned with the company's customer base.

On policy, the National Intelligence Service has proposed a relaxed network-separation framework that classifies work information into confidential, sensitive and open tiers and introduces intermediate-network servers where AI can be used, and the company said it could supply AI services on those servers (May 2025 report).

Security threats are also a source of demand: in April 2026 the company said that AI-enabled hacking risks had prompted proactive responses from domestic firms and that its security software sales had risen sharply. Listed peers in the same industry include Samsung SDS, LG CNS, Hyundai Autoever, NAVER and Kakao.

The company's distinguishing position rests on its 70% share of the domestic Microsoft SW SPLA market as estimated in June 2024 by IBK Investment & Securities, and on its channel to small and mid-sized companies. A June 2025 IBK report described the web-hard business as high-margin but facing a stagnant end market.

On the cycle, 2025 earnings benefited from the absence of one-off costs, while operating profit has continued to decline from 2022, so whether the core business is recovering remains to be confirmed.

06

Outlook

In February 2026 the company said its core business and data-center revenue continued to grow, and expected results to stay on an upward path driven by new services such as cloud storage. In April 2026 it stated that a Microsoft 365 security solution promotion would run through the end of 2026.

In a June 9, 2026 report, IBK Investment & Securities expected second-half earnings to improve on Microsoft 365 price increases and higher public budgets.

However, operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 580 million, down 50.4% year on year, so the second-half improvement has not yet appeared in quarterly results.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders also fell across the first three quarters of 2026, to KRW 910 million, KRW 622 million and KRW 641 million.

New services such as the integrated Adobe-based platform and AIaaS have not been separately quantified in reported third-quarter 2026 figures, so their contribution will need to be checked against the annual business report's revenue breakdown.

A treasury-share special money trust was terminated on May 29, 2026 upon expiry of the trust period. The next checkpoint is whether the operating margin in the 2026 annual business report falls below the 6.6% recorded for 2025.

Whether security-related revenue holds up after the promotion ends at the end of 2026 is another point to follow.

08

Bull factors

Market position as a Microsoft distributor

The company operates under a Microsoft SPLA distributor position, and IBK Investment & Securities estimated a 70% share of the domestic Microsoft SW SPLA market in a June 2024 publication.

In a June 2025 report, the same brokerage cited the right to sell Microsoft products to small and mid-sized companies as a driver of related revenue. If this core partnership holds, rising Microsoft product demand can flow through to revenue.

Expected second-half 2026 earnings improvement

In a June 9, 2026 report, IBK Investment & Securities expected second-half earnings to improve on Microsoft 365 price increases and higher public-sector budgets. In April 2026 the company said its Microsoft security solution sales had risen sharply.

If these factors show up in the 2026 annual business report, they will serve as evidence for the direction of operating profit.

Expansion into data centers and AI services

The company said data-center subsidiary revenue contributed to the expansion of consolidated sales in 2025. It obtained Adobe VIP Marketplace partner status, launched an integrated software platform, and is extending into AI as a Service. In February 2026 the company said it expected its new services to keep supporting results.

09

Bear factors

Continued decline in operating margin

The operating margin declined from 14.6% in 2022 to 13.5%, 8.3% and 6.6% in 2025. Operating profit fell for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 580 million was down 50.4% year on year and the lowest of the last five quarters on a reported basis.

One-off nature of the net income rebound

The company explained that the 2025 rebound in net income attributable to controlling shareholders to KRW 3.81 billion reflected the disappearance of one-off costs in 2024. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 3.46 billion.

Quarterly figures of KRW 910 million, KRW 622 million and KRW 641 million in the first three quarters of 2026 stayed below the KRW 1.29 billion recorded in Q4 2025.

Concern over stagnation in legacy businesses

A June 2025 IBK Investment & Securities report said the web-hard system business has limited room to add new customers, so revenue could decline gradually. Given the weight of these legacy operations, their effect on total revenue is a point to watch.

10

Risk factors

Dependence on supplier and policy

The business depends heavily on Microsoft's licensing structure and product policies. Under the SPLA distribution model, changes in supplier terms feed directly into profitability.

Because the company cited expanded Microsoft partnerships as a basis for growth in its 2025 results, any change in partner terms is a key variable for assessing performance.

Concentrated ownership by the largest shareholder

Kinx holds a 41.28% stake as of June 17, 2026, making it the largest shareholder. Concentrated ownership can affect the volume of shares available for trading and the outcome of any changes in the shareholder base. On May 29, 2026, a treasury-share special money trust contract was terminated upon expiry.

Cost structure and subsidiary variables

Revenue expanded after the data-center subsidiary was acquired, but in 2024 merger and share-exchange costs reduced net income attributable to controlling shareholders to KRW 302 million. Whether one-off costs recur will determine the stability of net income. On an operating basis, profit declined further to KRW 3.62 billion in 2025.

12

Overall view

SPSoft built revenue to KRW 54.5 billion in 2025 on two pillars, its Microsoft distributor position and its data-center subsidiary, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders recovered from KRW 302 million in 2024 to KRW 3.81 billion in 2025.

However, operating profit has declined for three consecutive years from 2023, and the operating margin fell from 14.6% in 2022 to 6.6% in 2025.

The company attributes the earnings rebound to the absence of one-off costs in 2024, and that explanation should be weighed against the underlying earnings base shown in reported figures.

Quarterly revenue has moved sideways between KRW 12.9 billion and KRW 14.1 billion through 2026, while operating profit fell to KRW 580 million in Q3 2026.

Positive factors include the Microsoft partnership-based market position, expectations for second-half improvement from Microsoft 365 price increases and higher public budgets, and rising Microsoft security solution sales.

Negative factors include the declining operating margin, lower quarterly net income, stagnation in legacy web-hard businesses, and concentrated ownership by the largest shareholder.

The next checkpoints are the annual operating margin in the 2026 fiscal-year business report and the revenue trend after the security promotion ends at the end of 2026. This report does not present an investment opinion or target price.
