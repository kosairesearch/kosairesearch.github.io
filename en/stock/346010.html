02

Business structure

Tide uses power-line communication (PLC) and LTE wireless technology to supply smart-grid, energy-management and IoT solutions. The company website lists domestic and overseas power utilities, telecom carriers and IoT-related companies, including Korea Electric Power Corporation, as its customer base.

A KRX technical analysis report by Seoul Credit Rating & Information (as of September 10, 2025) said the company can develop and supply PLC, LTE communication modules, DCUs and server platforms (HES/MDMS) in-house.

The same report cited DLMS/COSEM-based international-standard compatibility and experience commercializing broadband PLC (HomePlug Green PHY) as technical strengths.

Tide is an electronic-components maker that listed on the Konex market on December 28, 2022. Its effective controlling shareholder is Yoochang Metal Industrial.

In a market-data service's revenue breakdown, merchandise is the largest line at 59.02%, followed by other products at 21.37%, LTE modems at 16.38%, other services at 2.54%, PLC at 0.65% and DCU at 0.04%. This mix suggests that merchandise sales are far larger than in-house communication hardware.

The reference period for these weights is not stated explicitly by the service. Verified figures on competitor market share or Korea Electric Power supply volumes could not be found, so competition is described only qualitatively.

Given the nature of the business, dependence on utility-type buyers appears high, and revenue may swing with the timing of orders.

For 2025 it shows an operating loss of KRW 1.22906 billion and a net loss of KRW 1.73979 billion, both narrower than the year before. Total assets are listed at KRW 1.706 billion with an equity ratio of -361.48%, which means shareholders' equity is negative.

Trading has been halted since 9:15 on April 6, 2026, citing an audit disclaimer. On April 27, 2026, an improvement period was granted and the criterion for ending the halt was changed. The share price was KRW 1,000, unchanged, as of 16:10 on April 27, 2026.

04

Outlook

The halt continues until the delisting decision date that follows the end of the improvement period, which runs until the tenth day after the statutory filing deadline of the next fiscal year's annual report. The fiscal 2026 audit opinion is therefore the key item to watch.

Financial authorities announced that from July 1, 2025, two consecutive audit-opinion shortfalls would trigger immediate delisting. That announcement covers KOSPI and KOSDAQ, so how it applies to Konex should be confirmed in exchange rules and filings.

A law-firm note explains that even if a company later obtains an unqualified opinion through re-audit or the next audit, it becomes subject to a substantive listing-eligibility review.

On the business side, the question is how the scope limitation and going-concern uncertainty behind the disclaimer will be resolved. No filing describing a capital-raising plan or the details of an improvement plan was found in this research.

The controlling shareholder's larger stake can be read as a sign of control stability and support, but it should also be weighed against the fact that the change occurred while trading was restricted.

Demand for smart-metering and smart-grid equipment is tied to grid modernization, yet verified figures on Tide's order backlog or overseas results were not found.

With revenue stuck near KRW 6 billion, the room for improvement in operating metrics lies in narrowing operating losses and normalizing the earnings structure.

Bull factors

In-house communication technology

The KRX technical report says the company can develop and supply PLC, LTE modules, DCUs and server platforms in-house. It cites DLMS/COSEM international-standard compatibility and a 94% meter-reading rate in a Korea Electric Power technology verification as supporting evidence.

Supplying both equipment and platforms is a foundation that could be used when expanding into overseas grid projects. Actual order results, however, must be verified separately.

Narrower losses and a revenue uptick

In the market-data service, 2025 revenue grew 1.55%, a slight rebound after a 29.92% drop in 2024. The same data show the operating and net losses narrowing from the prior year. On operating metrics alone, profitability is moving in a better direction. The company nonetheless remains loss-making.

An improvement period, not immediate removal

An improvement period was granted on April 27, 2026, deferring the delisting decision until after that period ends. Controlling shareholder Yoochang Metal Industrial reported in a June 11, 2026 filing that its stake had risen to 38.27%.

The larger stake can be read as willingness to support the company, but it does not guarantee that the listing continues. The audit opinion during the improvement period is what matters.

Bear factors

Audit disclaimer and trading halt

The halt reasons are a scope-limitation disclaimer and going-concern uncertainty. That the external audit was unfinished by the March 27, 2026 shareholder meeting also reflected strain in the financial-reporting process. While the halt lasts, investors cannot trade the shares and price quotes carry limited meaning. The open possibility of delisting is the greatest weakness.

Negative shareholders' equity

Per the market-data service, total assets are KRW 1.706 billion, the equity ratio is -361.48% and the debt ratio is 461.48%. In 2025 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.22906 billion and a net loss of KRW 1.73979 billion. Continued losses could deepen the capital deficit. No confirmed filing describes a capital-raising plan.

Small revenue base, thin in-house product share

Fiscal 2025 revenue of KRW 6.1634 billion remains below the KRW 8.65978 billion of fiscal 2023. In the revenue mix, merchandise accounts for 59.02% while PLC is only 0.65% and DCU 0.04%. The small contribution from core technology products leaves open whether technical capability converts into earnings. The reference period for the mix is unclear.

Risk factors

Listing and regulatory

The halt lasts until the delisting decision date after the improvement period, so trading is unlikely to resume before a final decision. Financial authorities set a direction of immediate delisting after two consecutive audit-opinion shortfalls. How this applies to Konex needs confirmation in exchange rules. Even an improved opinion may be followed by a substantive review.

Financial

With an equity ratio of -361.48%, shareholders' equity is negative and leverage is high. Continued operating losses could make working-capital funding a burden. The going-concern uncertainty behind the disclaimer is directly tied to this financial structure. No external financing plan has been confirmed.

Business and macro

The customer base is concentrated among large buyers such as power utilities and telecom carriers. Public-sector-type projects can cause large revenue swings depending on ordering schedules and budget execution. Exchange rates and overseas grid investment cycles may also affect export orders. Overall view
