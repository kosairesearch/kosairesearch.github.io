02

Business structure

Tide is an electronics component maker built around power-line communication (PLC)-based automated meter reading infrastructure (AMI). The company was founded on October 13, 2010, and its CEO is Jo Kyung-jong. It uses HPGP-standard PLC technology to collect household and corporate electricity usage data remotely.

Its product lines include PLC modems, LTE communication modules, data concentrator units (DCUs) and server platforms (HES and MDMS), which the company says it can develop and supply in-house.

A 2025 technical analysis report described its DLMS/COSEM standard compatibility and its multiple domestic and overseas demonstration projects.

A market-data site lists product sales at 59.02% of revenue, followed by other products at 21.37%, LTE modems at 16.38%, other services at 2.54%, PLC at 0.65% and DCUs at 0.04%.

Its record includes PLC modem supply to a smart shipyard project for a major shipbuilder, DCU and PLC modem supply to Jamaica, and a low-voltage AMI project with the Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO).

Compared with larger domestic meter and communications equipment makers, Tide is a small supplier that appears heavily dependent on utility orders. Its largest shareholder, Yoochang Metal Industrial, held a 38.27% stake as of a June 11, 2026 filing. The company was listed on KONEX on December 28, 2022.

03

Recent trends

Sales for 2025 were KRW 6,163.4 million, up 1.55% from the prior year. The operating loss for the same year was KRW 1,229.06 million, and the net loss was KRW 1,739.79 million. Sales fell from KRW 18,533.0 million in 2021 to KRW 8,659.8 million in 2023 and KRW 6,069.1 million in 2024 before recovering slightly in 2025.

Market-data sites show total assets of KRW 1.706 billion, a capital ratio of -361.48% and a liability ratio of 461.48%, which points to capital impairment.

A March 27, 2026 filing showed that the external audit of the 2025 fiscal year was not completed by the shareholders' meeting date, so the first agenda item was left pending and the financial statement approval was moved to an adjourned session on April 10, 2026.

Trading was suspended from 9:15 a.m. on April 6, 2026, and an April 27, 2026 filing changed the resumption criteria tied to the audit disclaimer to the period after the improvement period.

The improvement period runs until the tenth day after the day following the statutory deadline for the next fiscal year's business report. The share price stood at KRW 1,000 on April 27, 2026, unchanged from the prior session.

Investment-data sites show a 52-week range of KRW 364 to KRW 1,840, and the market capitalization is about KRW 4.9 billion. A June 11, 2026 filing showed that Yoochang Metal Industrial increased its holding to 2,280,268 shares, or 38.27%. As of November 11, 2026, the share price was KRW 1,000 with zero trading value.

04

Outlook

The current questions center on the audit opinion for the 2025 fiscal year and whether the listing is maintained. The improvement period runs until the tenth day after the day following the statutory deadline for the next fiscal year's business report, and the delisting decision follows that window.

Whether trading resumes depends on the audit outcome and the procedures under the KONEX listing rules. On the business side, the field-deployment record from the KEPCO AMI work and the overseas supply history could serve as references for new orders.

However, sales rose slightly in 2025 while operating and net losses continued, so it is not yet visible whether revenue recovery is translating into better profitability. Whether capital impairment eases will show up in future financial statement disclosures.

The scale and timing of public utility orders will remain a key driver of revenue volatility. With trading liquidity this thin, the share price is difficult to read as a signal for individual disclosure events.

The next checkpoints are the final audit opinion for the 2025 fiscal year, quarterly results for fiscal 2026, and any changes in the largest shareholder's stake.

Bull factors

Validated PLC and AMI technology

A September 2025 technical analysis report noted that Tide holds DLMS/COSEM standard-compatible technology and has secured several domestic and overseas demonstration projects. The same report cited a 94% meter-reading rate in a KEPCO new-technology verification.

Its in-house capability across PLC modems, LTE modules, DCUs and server platforms is cited as a technical strength.

Overseas supply and reference record

The company's history records DCU and PLC modem supply to Jamaica. Domestically, it has supplied PLC modems for a smart shipyard project and took part in a low-voltage AMI project with KEPCO.

The technical report also refers to a business direction aimed at overseas power grid markets, which leaves room to broaden a mainly domestic revenue base.

Revenue decline has paused

Sales in 2025 were KRW 6,163.4 million, up 1.55%, after a 29.92% decline in 2024. Sales had dropped from KRW 8,659.8 million in 2023 to KRW 6,069.1 million in 2024. The end of the decline can be read as a sign of stabilization, though one year of data is too short to establish a trend.

06

Bear factors

Audit disclaimer and delisting review

The external audit of the 2025 fiscal year had not finished by the March 2026 shareholders' meeting, leaving the financial statement agenda pending. Trading has been suspended since April 6, 2026, and the delisting criteria tied to the audit disclaimer were adjusted to run through the improvement period.

Under this structure, the audit result and whether requirements are met within the improvement period are the core conditions for maintaining the listing.

Continued losses and capital impairment

Operating and net losses for 2025 totaled KRW 1,229.06 million and KRW 1,739.79 million, respectively. Market-data sites show a capital ratio of -361.48%, which indicates negative equity. Because losses are large relative to sales, whether the company can restore self-generated cash flow remains an open task.

Thin liquidity and concentrated ownership

Yoochang Metal Industrial held a 38.27% stake as of a June 11, 2026 filing. The free float is limited, and trading value is recorded at zero, so price formation through trading is weak. Even when changes in insider holdings are disclosed, they are unlikely to translate into market trading.

Risk factors

Macro

Changes in interest rates and exchange rates can affect public power infrastructure budgets and the profitability of overseas supply. Overseas public tenders are sensitive to local budget cycles and currency moves. If an economic slowdown delays utility equipment investment, order timing could also slip.

Regulatory

Articles 27 of the KONEX Market Listing Regulations and Article 23 of its enforcement rules serve as the basis for the trading suspension and delisting review. If the audit requirements are not satisfied by the end of the improvement period, a delisting decision could follow. The regulatory timetable advances in a fixed sequence regardless of the company's operational progress.

Customer concentration

Given its repeated cooperation projects with KEPCO, the company appears to depend heavily on public utility orders. Delays in order timing or budget cuts could increase revenue volatility.

As a small equipment supplier, Tide could see limited improvement in its profit structure if it cannot secure larger contracts consistently.

08

Overall view

Tide combines PLC-based AMI technology, a utility cooperation record with KEPCO and overseas supply references, but its financial position remains weak. Sales rose slightly in 2025, yet operating and net losses continued, and a capital ratio of -361.48% on market-data sites points to capital impairment.

A delisting review tied to the audit disclaimer for the 2025 fiscal year has applied, and trading has been shown as suspended since April 6, 2026.

Strengths include its technical validation and supply history, while weaknesses include the audit issue, continued losses, capital impairment and very thin trading liquidity. As of November 11, 2026, the share price was KRW 1,000 with zero trading value, so price signals are limited.

The 38.27% stake held by the largest shareholder and its June 2026 increase are documented facts, and interpretations of them differ. The next issues are the final audit opinion for the 2025 fiscal year, whether the listing is maintained, and 2026 business-year results.
