Report overview

Orders Top KRW 3 Trillion, Margins Remain the Test

Dongbu's cumulative new orders for 2026 have passed KRW 3 trillion, and operating profit returned to the black in 2025. With operating margin still in the low single digits, how fast backlog converts into profit is the key test.

  1. 1

    Operating profit reached KRW 42.6 billion in 2025, turning positive from a KRW 96.9 billion operating loss in 2024. However, the 2025 operating margin of 2.4% remains below the 2.8% recorded in 2022.

  2. 2

    On November 17, 2026, the company announced that cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 rose 147% year on year to KRW 42.8 billion, with a cumulative operating margin of 2.7%.

  3. 3

    The company said cumulative new orders for 2026 have exceeded KRW 3 trillion. The total includes public housing projects in Goyang Changneung and Yangju Goeup (about KRW 567.7 billion) and Section 10 of the South Inland Railway (about KRW 271 billion).

  4. 4

    Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.71 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 77.3% from a year earlier. Operating profit improved in that quarter, but that gain did not carry through to controlling-shareholder net income.

02

Business structure

Dongbu operates across civil engineering, building construction, housing and urban redevelopment, railways, and advanced industrial facilities. New orders in 2026 were secured across public civil works, public housing, urban redevelopment, private building projects, and high-tech facilities.

In civil works, the company won the Hwarang-ro underpass project in the Goyang Changneung public housing district and Lot 3 of the Gangwha–Gyeongchang expressway construction.

In building construction, it took Lot 7 of the Incheon Gyeyang A-19BL integrated public rental housing project, with a total construction cost of about KRW 79 billion and 576 households, along with integrated private-participation public housing projects in Osan, Incheon Geomdan, and Incheon Yeongjong.

In railways, Dongbu was selected as the detailed-design qualified bidder for Section 10 of the South Inland Railway, about KRW 271 billion, covering main subgrade, track, power, and signaling work in Sadeung-myeon, Geoje. In housing, it holds the Bangbae-dong street-home redevelopment and Sinnae-dong Motown projects.

In high-tech facilities, it secured the SK hynix Yongin Campus support facility (KRW 192.4 billion) and the Amkor Technology Korea Songdo test building expansion (KRW 190.3 billion) in 2025.

Its 2026 construction-capability evaluation amount was KRW 2.0215 trillion, ranking 25th, with the increase partly driven by semiconductor construction work. Major clients are LH and the Korea Rail Network Authority, while semiconductor project volumes depend on the investment schedules of large corporate customers. The revenue share by segment is difficult to map directly onto the order mix.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 1.46 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.9 trillion in 2023, a 30.0% increase, making 2023 the largest year in the 2022–2025 period. Revenue then fell 11.1% to KRW 1.69 trillion in 2024 and recovered 4.2% to KRW 1.76 trillion in 2025.

Operating profit declined from KRW 41.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 30.2 billion in 2023, a 26.9% decrease, and shifted to an operating loss of KRW 96.9 billion in 2024. In 2025 operating profit of KRW 42.6 billion returned to the black, with an operating margin of 2.4%.

The operating margin path ran 2.8% in 2022, 1.6% in 2023, -5.7% in 2024, and 2.4% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 40.1 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 4.41 billion in 2023 and a net loss of KRW 106.5 billion in 2024, before turning to a profit of KRW 52.2 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 44.5 billion in 2025, a different figure from controlling-shareholder net income. Operating cash flow was -KRW 20.8 billion in 2022, KRW 7.88 billion in 2023, KRW 17.1 billion in 2024, and -KRW 2.19 billion in 2025, showing wide swings.

Quarterly results show operating profit of KRW 674 million in the third quarter of 2025, which then rose to KRW 25.3 billion in the fourth quarter on revenue of KRW 523.7 billion.

Operating profit fell to KRW 10.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 before recovering to KRW 18 billion in the second quarter, and then reached KRW 14.6 billion in the third quarter on revenue of KRW 568.4 billion.

Controlling-shareholder net income in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 2.71 billion, down 77.3% from a year earlier, so the operating gain did not fully reach shareholders in that quarter.

The four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 combined to KRW 71.1 billion in controlling-shareholder net income.

According to the company, cumulative selling and administrative expenses for the first three quarters of 2026 fell 10.2% year on year, which it cited as a contributor to the higher cumulative operating margin.

05

Industry analysis

The construction sector is facing high raw-material prices that have become entrenched, persistently high interest rates, and restructuring of real estate project financing (PF), leaving mid-sized and large domestic builders broadly exposed to weak orders and deteriorating profitability.

Press coverage citing Statistics Korea's construction trend data and the Korea Research Institute for Construction Policy describes this environment.

Against that backdrop, builders that reduced reliance on private housing contracts and shifted toward public civil works and non-housing segments such as semiconductor and advanced manufacturing facilities are seen as holding up relatively better.

Dongbu ranked 25th in the 2026 construction-capability evaluation for civil and building works, with an evaluated amount of KRW 2.0215 trillion, up 15.4% from the prior year and three places higher. Semiconductor projects for SK hynix and Amkor Technology Korea were cited as drivers of that increase.

Because the capability ranking reflects accumulated past work, it does not by itself guarantee 2026 profitability. The company has lifted its operating margin by cutting selling and administrative costs, but the level remains in the low single digits. Whether cost-ratio improvements continue will therefore shape Dongbu's position within this sector environment.

06

Outlook

In its second-half 2026 management strategy, announced July 22, 2026, the company put profitability ahead of order expansion and cited project management and AI-based productivity gains as levers for improving results.

At the bidding stage it reviews project feasibility and cost appropriateness, and after construction starts it links process, cost, and cash-flow control. Section 10 of the South Inland Railway carries a construction period of about 58 months from the start date, so revenue recognition will extend over a long period.

The Goyang Changneung B-1BL and Yangju Goeup A-12BL public housing projects, totaling 1,732 households, are currently at the preferred-bidder stage with LH, and they will move forward after negotiations and a project agreement with LH.

According to the company and press reports, secured backlog will be recognized as revenue as construction progresses. The company plans to broaden its portfolio around urban redevelopment, private building projects, and advanced industrial facilities.

However, if operating margin stays in the low single digits, higher order volume will translate into limited profit growth. The next focus is whether cost management holds as the backlog converts into revenue after 2026.

08

Bull factors

Expanding order backlog

The backlog spans public civil works, public housing, railways, and advanced industrial facilities, which limits dependence on any single project. The 25th place in the 2026 capability evaluation is three places higher than the prior year.

Return to profit and margin recovery

Operating profit swung from a KRW 96.9 billion loss in 2024 to a KRW 42.6 billion profit in 2025. The cumulative operating margin for the first three quarters of 2026 rose to 2.7% from 1.4% a year earlier. A 10.2% decline in selling and administrative expenses is cited as a driver.

Improving balance sheet trend

The debt ratio fell from 264.7% in 2024 to 206.4% in 2025. On November 17, 2026, the company announced that the debt ratio had dropped to 193.7% at the end of the third quarter of 2026, reflecting both capital build-up and lower debt.

09

Bear factors

Thin margins and volatile cash flow

Operating margin was 2.4% in 2025, still in the low single digits. It was -5.7% in 2024 and 1.6% in 2023. Operating cash flow was negative at -KRW 2.19 billion in 2025.

Gap between operating profit and net income

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose sharply to KRW 14.6 billion, yet controlling-shareholder net income fell 77.3% to KRW 2.71 billion. Non-operating items or taxes may have played a role. Quarterly swings of this kind widen the range of interpretation for earnings quality.

Backlog does not convert to revenue immediately

Section 10 of the South Inland Railway has a construction period of about 58 months. The Goyang Changneung and Yangju Goeup public housing projects are at the preferred-bidder stage with negotiations still ahead.

If construction weakness and raw-material and interest-rate pressures persist, converting backlog into profit could take longer.

10

Risk factors

Cost and execution risk

Construction earnings swing sharply by quarter depending on project progress and when costs are recognized. The KRW 96.9 billion operating loss in 2024 and the 60.0% quarter-on-quarter drop in first-quarter 2026 operating profit illustrate this. If raw-material and labor costs rise above contract assumptions, margins can narrow even with a full backlog.

Balance sheet leverage risk

Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 1.14 trillion, with a debt ratio of 206.4%. Total equity stood at KRW 550.8 billion. If cash flow weakens while borrowing costs are high, collecting construction receivables and servicing debt could become harder at the same time.

Project timing and public-client risk

Major wins are concentrated in public clients such as LH and the Korea Rail Network Authority, so project timing and budget execution affect outcomes. The Goyang Changneung and Yangju Goeup projects still require negotiations and a project agreement with LH. Any delay in construction starts would push back when backlog turns into revenue.

Overall view

Dongbu's cumulative new orders for 2026 have passed KRW 3 trillion, and operating profit returned to the black in 2025 after a KRW 96.9 billion operating loss in 2024.

Cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 reached KRW 42.8 billion, with a cumulative operating margin of 2.7%, and lower selling and administrative expenses contributed to the improvement.

Still, operating margin remains in the low single digits, and controlling-shareholder net income for the third quarter of 2026 fell 77.3% year on year to KRW 2.71 billion. Operating cash flow of -KRW 2.19 billion in 2025 and a debt ratio of 206.4% are financial points to track.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x, and controlling-shareholder net income for the last four quarters totaled KRW 71.1 billion. Strengths such as order growth and balance sheet improvement coexist with weaknesses such as thin margins and cash-flow volatility.

The next checkpoints are operating margin and operating cash flow in the 2026 annual business report, and how much secured backlog converts into revenue in the first-quarter 2027 report.
