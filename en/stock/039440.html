02

Business structure

Systems Technology Incorporated was founded in 1997 to manufacture and sell semiconductor manufacturing equipment and listed on KOSDAQ in 2002. Its main products, C.C.S.S.

(central chemical supply systems) and Wet System equipment, together account for about 90% of sales, and C.C.S.S. supplies high-purity chemicals centrally inside fabs.

The company entered the main semiconductor process market in 2013 and has since widened its business into OLED front-end equipment and touch panel tools.

Its customers are mainly domestic and overseas semiconductor manufacturers, and Samsung Electronics is among them: in April 2026 the company disclosed a supply contract worth KRW 39.47 billion with a term ending November 30, 2026.

In December 2025 it disclosed a new power semiconductor equipment order equal to 29.3% of 2024 sales, with a term through September 2027. The same month the company announced an equipment purchase contract worth about USD 66.5 million with a Chinese power semiconductor maker through a joint venture.

The planned product is a heat dissipation component manufacturing system for power semiconductors, with mass production starting in 2027 and automated inline capacity to be built in stages. Revenue moves with fab investment timing, so order announcements and revenue recognition can fall into different quarters.

New product lines are expanding toward power semiconductors and displays but remain smaller than the core C.C.S.S. and Wet System business. A 1Q 2026 earnings summary from a securities information site noted that supply contracts for key products continued while overall sales declined.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 422.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 319.5 billion in 2023 (down 24.4% year on year), rose to KRW 334 billion in 2024 (up 4.6%), and came to KRW 328.1 billion in 2025 (down 1.8%). 2022 was the largest year by revenue and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 34.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 23.9 billion in 2023 (down 31.1%), recovered to KRW 27.3 billion in 2024 (up 14.0%), and fell to KRW 19.7 billion in 2025 (down 27.7%). The operating margin was 8.2% in 2022, 7.5% in 2023, 8.2% in 2024 and 6.0% in 2025.

In 2025 the profit decline was much steeper than the revenue decline, pushing the margin to the lowest level of the period.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 15.4 billion in 2025 (down 43.2%), the smallest of the four years, while net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 15.4 billion. The company attributed the 2025 change mainly to lower margins from the reduction of large projects.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 50.0% in 2022 to 27.6% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 287 billion and total liabilities of KRW 79.3 billion at the end of 2025. Operating cash flow dropped from KRW 71.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 29.2 billion in 2023, KRW 15.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 18.7 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue reached KRW 86.5 billion in 4Q 2025, up 23.2% from the prior quarter, and operating profit peaked at KRW 6.81 billion in the same quarter.

Operating profit then dropped to KRW 365 million in 1Q 2026 (down 94.6% quarter on quarter), before rising to KRW 2.9 billion in 2Q and KRW 4.13 billion in 3Q, two consecutive quarterly increases.

Even so, 3Q 2026 produced a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.06 billion, a swing from profit in the prior-year quarter. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters (4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026) totaled KRW 12.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The semiconductor equipment market is characterized by order timing that clusters and scatters with customers' capital expenditure (CAPEX) cycles.

When fabs are built or expanded, central chemical supply equipment such as C.C.S.S. tends to be ordered early, so any delay in a customer's investment schedule quickly affects quarterly revenue.

The company's own explanation for the 2025 margin decline, a reduction in large projects, reflects this project-based revenue pattern. A 1Q 2026 earnings summary from a securities information site noted that supply contracts with domestic and overseas chipmakers continued while overall sales declined.

Daol Investment & Securities said in a December 9, 2025 report that it expected 2026 operating profit to exceed KRW 100 billion, and it raised its target price to KRW 43,000 at that time.

Compared with that forecast, operating profit for 1Q to 3Q 2026 was KRW 365 million, KRW 2.9 billion and KRW 4.13 billion, a wide gap.

In terms of positioning, the company focuses on process infrastructure equipment, so its scope of equipment and customers is distinct from peers in the same machinery and equipment sector such as Protec, People & Technology and Philoptics.

In display equipment, it is compared with Sunic System, which handles OLED front-end deposition tools, while power semiconductor heat dissipation equipment is a new area with a still-small share of results.

Outlook

The nearest checkpoint is the end of the Samsung Electronics supply contract term on November 30, 2026. How much of that contract is recognized as revenue in the quarters after its completion will set the starting point for the trend after 4Q 2026.

The power semiconductor equipment order runs through September 2027, so progress on that project is likely to affect revenue through the first half of 2027.

For the China joint venture, the company's stated plan is to start mass production of heat dissipation components in 2027 and build automated inline capacity in stages toward a final target of 210k units per month.

The company has described the site as a core hub for supplying power semiconductor heat dissipation components within China. The 2025 preliminary annual results were approved by the board on February 5, 2026, so the timing of the 2026 preliminary annual results in early 2027 is the next checkpoint on the same schedule.

The 2026 annual business report must be filed within 90 days of fiscal year-end, by the end of March 2027, and it will set the official 2026 operating margin and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

On a quarterly basis, the test is whether 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 80.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.13 billion are sustained, and whether 4Q revenue returns toward the KRW 86.5 billion recorded in 4Q 2025.

How far new equipment orders shift the business away from the C.C.S.S.-centered structure will also be a key measure of the medium-term trend.

Bear factors

Project-driven earnings volatility

The company cited lower margins from the reduction of large projects as the main driver of 2025 earnings changes. Revenue fell 1.8% in 2025, but operating profit dropped 27.7% and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders dropped 43.2%.

The bearish argument starts from the fact that quarterly earnings can swing sharply when the timing of a single large project shifts.

Limited scope of earnings recovery

3Q 2026 produced a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.06 billion, a swing from profit in the prior-year quarter, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 12.8 billion. 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 80.9 billion remained below the KRW 86.5 billion of 4Q 2025.

A December 2025 brokerage forecast projected 2026 operating profit above KRW 100 billion, while reported 1Q to 3Q 2026 operating profit was KRW 365 million, KRW 2.9 billion and KRW 4.13 billion.

Shrinking cash flow and reliance on customer capex

Operating cash flow declined from KRW 71.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 29.2 billion in 2023 and KRW 15.8 billion in 2024, and stood at KRW 18.7 billion in 2025. If revenue depends on the investment schedules of a few large customers, cash generation can weaken when those investments are delayed. The China joint venture is expected to contribute little to earnings before mass production in 2027.

Risk factors

Customer capex timing

Equipment revenue shifts with customers' fab investment timing, and in 2025 the reduction of large projects pushed margins lower. Revenue recognition from disclosed orders varies by quarter, which lowers the predictability of quarterly results. Delivery progress around the end of contract terms can determine next-quarter revenue.

China joint venture and regulation

The joint venture with a Chinese power semiconductor maker could be affected by changes in US-China semiconductor regulation and the local investment environment. Even with a large purchase contract, earnings contribution would come later if mass production slips beyond 2027. A larger share of overseas revenue would also expose results to currency movements.

Earnings volatility and cash flow

In 3Q 2026 operating profit rose while the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, so non-operating items and the details of the income structure can decide the bottom line.

Operating cash flow of KRW 18.7 billion in 2025 was far below KRW 71.5 billion in 2022, making working capital management important. With quarterly revenue moving in the KRW 70 billion range, fixed cost burden has a sizable effect on margins.
