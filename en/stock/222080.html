02

Business structure

SFA Nexel (Creative & Innovative System) was founded in 2002 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2017. It makes battery electrode process equipment as well as deposition and inspection equipment. Valueline says a December 2024 merger with SNU Precision added display, semiconductor and PCB inspection and measurement equipment.

According to WiseReport, the product mix is 55.97% battery electrode process equipment, 26.00% deposition and inspection/measurement equipment and 18.03% other. Its core electrode lines are coaters, calenders (roll presses) and slitters.

In the 2025 year-end breakdown, export shares were 80.93% for coaters, 78.14% for calenders, 10.97% for slitters and 76.74% for deposition and inspection equipment.

Major customers include LG Energy Solution and Samsung SDI, and it supplies production equipment to plants in Korea, Europe, China, Japan and North America. Valueline says its OLED deposition equipment and PS height measurement technology hold a global share above 80%.

In electrode equipment it competes with Japanese and Chinese makers. The company changed its name from CIS to SFA Nexel in June 2026 and is developing dry electrode and all-solid-state battery production solutions.

Preparation for mass production of sulfide-based solid electrolyte and development of a dry coater are also under way.

04

Earnings analysis

On disclosed figures, revenue rose from KRW 159.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 310.2 billion in 2023 (up 94.6%) and KRW 508.5 billion in 2024 (up 64.0%), then fell to KRW 407.2 billion in 2025 (down 19.9%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 7.81 billion in 2022 to KRW 38.9 billion in 2023 (up 398.5%) and KRW 59.3 billion in 2024 (up 52.4%), then fell to KRW 33.5 billion in 2025 (down 43.5%). Operating margin was 4.9% in 2022, 12.6% in 2023, 11.7% in 2024 and 8.2% in 2025, peaking in 2023.

Revenue grew further in 2024, but the margin was slightly below 2023, so top-line expansion did not translate into margin expansion.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 11.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 28.9 billion in 2023 and KRW 56.2 billion in 2024, then fell to KRW 28.7 billion in 2025 (down 48.9%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 11.5 billion, KRW 28.6 billion, KRW 56.2 billion and KRW 28.7 billion for the same years, so the two measures differ.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 55.2 billion in 2022 to -KRW 71.6 billion in 2023, then recovered to KRW 29.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 101.4 billion in 2025. The debt ratio fell from 217.1% in 2022 to 30.0% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 507.7 billion and total liabilities were KRW 152.5 billion.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 87.7 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 82.1 billion in the fourth quarter (down 6.3% quarter on quarter) to KRW 53.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 34.9%), then rebounded to KRW 69 billion in the second quarter (up 29.2%).

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 58.4 billion was down 15.4% from the prior quarter and down 33.4% from a year earlier. Operating profit ran KRW 7.55 billion, KRW 7.59 billion, KRW 2.16 billion, KRW 13.6 billion and KRW 4.01 billion from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 29 billion. R&D spending rose to about KRW 9.4 billion in 2025 from about KRW 3.4 billion in 2024.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for the company's equipment depends on capital spending by battery cell makers at home and abroad. WiseReport attributes the weak first quarter of 2026 to rising global interest rates, changes in EV subsidy policy and geopolitical risk, which it says reduced demand for battery electrode process equipment.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue also fell from the prior quarter, so a clear demand recovery is not yet visible in quarterly sales. On the other side, European policy support for bringing battery supply chains in-house continues and is cited as a counterweight.

In June 2026 the company signed an NDA with a cell maker pursuing a large European battery production project and is discussing electrode equipment supply and next-generation process cooperation. Its main competitors in electrode equipment are Japanese and Chinese makers.

Valueline says the company's electrode process technology is recognized in the industry. In display, the company's OLED deposition equipment is the basis for its diversification. Disclosed results do not separate out the revenue contribution of dry electrode and all-solid-state process equipment.

06

Outlook

The most explicit company-side outlook comes from parent SFA. In its third-quarter disclosure commentary, SFA said that an easing of Europe's EV-related slowdown and a possible revival of US tax-credit discussions, which it linked to a policy pullback, have brightened SFA Nexel's future order outlook.

The same commentary expected SFA Nexel's consolidated results to improve significantly. This is the parent's view and is not a figure from SFA Nexel's own disclosures.

The company's expansion rests partly on the Hungarian local subsidiary set up in early August 2026, which is expected to serve as a base for equipment installation and local operations in Europe.

On the technology side, it is combining SFA Group manufacturing capability to grow dry electrode and all-solid-state battery process solutions, and it is developing a hybrid coater as a new battery growth line. Sulfide-based solid electrolyte is described as being in mass-production preparation.

For orders, the key checkpoint is whether the NDA with the European cell maker turns into a supply contract. When these businesses will show up in reported results can be checked in the next periodic report.

The first checkpoint is how the full-year 2026 operating margin in the business report compares with 8.2% in 2025.

Bull factors

Overseas order base and a European footprint

Exports made up about 88% of first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 122.4 billion (per an Edaily report), giving the company a broad overseas customer base. It signed an NDA with a European cell maker in June 2026 and set up a Hungarian subsidiary in early August to serve equipment installation and local operations in Europe. Parent SFA has said the order outlook will improve, citing the possible easing of Europe's EV slowdown.

Stronger balance sheet

The debt ratio fell from 217.1% in 2022 to 30.0% in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 507.7 billion and total liabilities were KRW 152.5 billion. Operating cash flow recovered from -KRW 71.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 101.4 billion in 2025.

Next-generation processes and technology portfolio

The company is building dry electrode, all-solid-state battery production and hybrid coater businesses as growth lines. Valueline says its OLED deposition equipment holds a global share above 80%.

09

Bear factors

Demand slowdown and falling revenue

First-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 53.4 billion fell 34.9% from the prior quarter, and FnGuide data show a 50.5% drop from a year earlier. WiseReport attributes weaker demand for electrode process equipment to rising interest rates, changes in EV subsidy policy and geopolitical risk.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 58.4 billion was down 33.4% from a year earlier, so the recovery is not yet clear.

Large swings in quarterly profit

Operating profit went from KRW 7.59 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.16 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then KRW 13.6 billion in the second quarter, before falling to KRW 4.01 billion in the third quarter. Full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 33.5 billion was down 43.5%. The size of the quarterly swings makes the annual trend harder to read.

Customer concentration and competitive pressure

Major customers include LG Energy Solution and Samsung SDI, so results are tied to the investment timing of a few battery makers. The company competes with Japanese and Chinese electrode equipment makers. R&D spending rose to about KRW 9.4 billion in 2025 from about KRW 3.4 billion in 2024, adding to cost pressure.

10

Risk factors

Policy and demand risk

Demand for electrode equipment is sensitive to policy variables such as interest rates, EV subsidies and US tax credits. The tax-credit discussions the parent cited as a basis for the order outlook are not settled. If policy shifts delay cell makers' capital spending, the timing of a revenue recovery could slip as well.

Customer and export dependence

About 88% of first-half 2026 revenue came from exports, so overseas demand and trade terms weigh heavily. Customers are concentrated among a few battery makers, so one customer's investment cut could have a large effect on sales.

The Hungarian subsidiary was set up to support installation and local work, but its early operating results are not yet visible in disclosed figures.

Execution risk in new businesses

Dry electrode, all-solid-state, hybrid coater and sulfide-based solid electrolyte businesses are described as in development or mass-production preparation. The European NDA is a confidentiality agreement, not a supply contract. If investment costs rise before revenue follows, margins could come under pressure.

12

Overall view

On disclosed figures, SFA Nexel's revenue peaked at KRW 508.5 billion and operating profit at KRW 59.3 billion in 2024, then fell to KRW 407.2 billion and KRW 33.5 billion in 2025. Quarterly results in 2026 traced a first-quarter low, a second-quarter rebound and a third-quarter slowdown.

Supporting factors are an overseas revenue base with exports at about 88% of first-half sales, a European NDA and a Hungarian subsidiary, preparation in dry electrode, all-solid-state and hybrid coater businesses, and a debt ratio that fell to 30.0%.

Weighing against these are weaker electrode equipment demand linked to interest rates, subsidies and geopolitics, large quarterly profit swings, and dependence on a few customers and battery policy. Disclosed results do not yet separate out the revenue from next-generation businesses.

Parent SFA says the order outlook is improving, but that is the parent's view rather than a figure in SFA Nexel's own disclosures. On valuation, the share price sits close to book value, and thin analyst coverage limits external comparisons.

The next checkpoints are preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results, the revenue and operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and whether the European collaboration converts into orders.
