Report overview

Subscription revenue grows, operating losses persist

Alchera's subscription revenue and Physical AI data backlog grew in 2026, but operating losses have persisted for five consecutive quarters, making the timing of a profit turn the key checkpoint.

02

Business structure

Alchera operates three areas built on video AI technology: facial recognition AI, abnormal situation detection AI, and AI training data production.

The facial recognition AI is supplied as on-premise and cloud models for payment systems, mobile banking, immigration, and public-sector security, and the abnormal situation detection area includes fire detection solutions.

According to the company, facial recognition accounted for KRW 1.36 billion, or 68.2%, of revenue in the first quarter of 2026 (announced June 8, 2026).

Monthly usage of subscription identity verification in bank identity checks, ID verification and access control has moved the business from one-off build projects toward recurring revenue.

Subscription revenue reached KRW 1.57 billion in the first half of 2026, or 33.1% of total revenue, up from 23.4% a year earlier (announced September 7, 2026).

The Physical AI data business started from KRW 11.4 billion in cumulative autonomous driving revenue over five years and is expanding into humanoid and manufacturing data (Robot Newspaper, April 27, 2026).

In July 2026, Alchera signed a KRW 2.66 billion data factory contract with a global IT company, equal to about 18% of the prior year's revenue.

It formed a strategic partnership with China's national humanoid innovation center X-Humanoid and joined a Ministry of Science and ICT and IITP-led national R&D project with a total size of KRW 49.7 billion as a co-research institution.

Customers span finance, the public sector and industry, and the company names domestic robot makers, large corporate research units and university labs as its Physical AI data targets.

Competition forms among firms that combine video AI solutions with advanced data processing, and by market capitalization the company is much smaller than large platform and IT services peers in the same sector.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue moved from KRW 11.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.6 billion in 2023 (up 4.3% year on year), KRW 17.3 billion in 2024 (up 49.2%) and KRW 14.8 billion in 2025 (down 14.2%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating losses were KRW 16.9 billion in 2022, KRW 18.5 billion in 2023, KRW 10.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.2 billion in 2025, a run of four consecutive years of losses, and the operating margin was -152.0%, -160.2%, -60.5% and -69.3% over the same years.

The loss narrowed in 2024, but the 2025 margin was lower than in 2024, and the 14.2% revenue decline is the backdrop to that deterioration.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders were KRW 13.9 billion in 2022, KRW 26.8 billion in 2023, KRW 9.87 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.57 billion in 2025, while the 2025 net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 2.54 billion.

Because that total was smaller than the controlling-shareholder loss, non-controlling interests contributed a small profit in 2025.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 13.9 billion in 2022, -KRW 16.3 billion in 2023, -KRW 4.4 billion in 2024 and -KRW 7.03 billion in 2025, so cash outflow grew in 2025 even though the operating loss was smaller than in 2024.

The debt ratio fell from 340.8% in 2023 to 62.3% in 2025, and total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 22.2 billion, of which controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 22.4 billion, with total liabilities of KRW 13.8 billion.

Quarterly operating losses were KRW 2.23 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 205 million in Q4 2025, then widened to KRW 4.31 billion in Q1 2026.

Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 9 billion, up 269.7% quarter on quarter, points to revenue concentrating in the fourth quarter, and Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 2 billion was down 77.8% from the prior quarter.

Operating loss was KRW 3.2 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 3.8 billion in Q3 2026, which widened from a year earlier, while the Q3 2026 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.07 billion turned from a profit in the same quarter of 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for facial recognition AI in Korea is spreading into biometric authentication, financial payments and access control as non-face-to-face methods expand (WISEreport company information, as of October 2, 2026).

The growth in demand for subscription identity verification moves in the same direction as Alchera's explanation of its first-quarter 2026 results.

The Physical AI data market is expanding from autonomous driving into humanoid and manufacturing applications, and global market research firms forecast rapid growth in it (Robot Newspaper, April 27, 2026).

In autonomous driving, data collection, labeling and quality verification markets formed first, and some analysts expect the market to be reorganized around firms with advanced data processing capability.

According to the company, the Physical AI data share of the separate-basis order backlog rose from 10.8% to 60.9% at the end of the first quarter of 2026 (announced June 8, 2026). In cycle terms, demand for Physical AI data is still at an early stage of expansion, so order flow can be volatile.

Large platform and IT services companies in the same sector have far greater capital and headcount, so they are difficult to compare directly, while Alchera competes in specific areas of facial recognition and data construction.

The company lists GS certification Grade 1 achievements in its official history, and it presents these certifications as evidence of its technical competitiveness.

06

Outlook

In its first-half 2026 announcement, the company explained that revenue concentrates in the second half and said that the higher first-half operating expenses of KRW 1.07 billion should ease in the second half (announced September 7, 2026).

Management said it would raise the subscription share further in the second half and expand its Physical AI business globally. Whether fourth-quarter 2026 revenue builds as strongly as the KRW 9 billion booked in Q4 2025 is the key variable for annual results.

Revenue in Q3 2026 fell year on year and the operating loss widened, so whether the second-half concentration appears in fourth-quarter revenue is the point to confirm.

The pace at which the KRW 7.1 billion data backlog converts into revenue also matters, including recognition timing for the KRW 2.66 billion data factory contract signed in July.

In Physical AI, the key question is whether the X-Humanoid partnership and national R&D participation lead to business results visible in regulatory filings.

On the financial side, cash and equivalents of about KRW 15.4 billion at the end of the first half of 2026 support investment capacity, but operating cash outflow grew to KRW 7.03 billion in 2025, so whether investment spending turns into cash burn is also a point to watch.

08

Bull factors

Shift toward subscription revenue

Subscription revenue in the first half of 2026 was KRW 1.57 billion, up 100% year on year, and its share of total revenue widened from 23.4% to 33.1% (company announcement, September 7, 2026). The company describes a move from project-based orders toward recurring and more predictable revenue. Monthly usage growth in facial recognition identity verification supports this shift.

Growing Physical AI data order backlog

At the end of the first quarter of 2026, the separate-basis Physical AI data backlog was KRW 7.05 billion, up about 6.7 times from KRW 1.06 billion a year earlier (company announcement, June 8, 2026). The 2026 semiannual report put the data-segment backlog at KRW 7.1 billion, up 123% year on year.

The two figures use different bases and dates, so they should be read together with that caveat. A KRW 2.66 billion data factory contract was signed in July.

Financial capacity and a lower debt ratio

Cash and equivalents at the end of the first half of 2026 were about KRW 15.4 billion (company announcement, September 7, 2026). The debt ratio fell from 340.8% in 2023 to 62.3% in 2025, and total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 22.2 billion.

A November 2024 news report referred to a KRW 15 billion rights offering, and the disclosed figures show a thicker equity base since then.

09

Bear factors

Five consecutive quarters of operating losses

Operating losses have continued from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and the third-quarter 2026 operating loss of KRW 3.8 billion widened from a year earlier. In the same quarter, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.07 billion turned from a profit a year earlier.

Over the last four quarters, that loss totaled KRW 10.9 billion, and no turn to profit has yet appeared in regulatory filings.

Large swings in quarterly revenue

Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 9 billion rose 269.7% from the prior quarter, but Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 2 billion fell 77.8% quarter on quarter. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 2.35 billion declined 3.6% year on year, which shows quarterly revenue depending on project acceptance and payment timing.

The company describes second-half concentration as seasonal, so the fourth quarter carries a large share of annual results.

Operating cash outflow

Operating cash flow was negative in all four years, at -KRW 13.9 billion in 2022, -KRW 16.3 billion in 2023, -KRW 4.4 billion in 2024 and -KRW 7.03 billion in 2025. The 2025 outflow was larger than in 2024 even though the operating loss narrowed.

In the first half of 2026, operating expenses rose KRW 1.07 billion year on year, which the company attributes to up-front investment in new businesses.

10

Risk factors

Business concentration and seasonality

The facial recognition segment accounted for 68.2% of first-quarter 2026 revenue, so dependence on one business is high (company announcement). Revenue concentrates in the second half, so quarterly results are uneven, and a delay in project acceptance could move annual results.

Funding needs and dilution risk

A November 2024 news report referred to a KRW 15 billion rights offering, and the company was reported to be fully capital-impaired in the third quarter of 2024. Further capital raising could increase the share count.

Cash of about KRW 15.4 billion at mid-2026 may limit medium-term investment capacity given the size of operating losses.

Regulation and overseas partnership variables

Facial recognition is subject to regulation on biometric data processing. A September 2026 press interview cited handling of foreign-name phone lines as a challenge in introducing facial authentication.

Overseas cooperation, including the partnership with China's X-Humanoid, may move at a different pace as geopolitical conditions change.

12

Overall view

Alchera's first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 4.74 billion, up 41.0% year on year according to the company, and a subscription share of 33.1% point to a more recurring revenue structure. The data-segment order backlog rising to KRW 7.1 billion in the 2026 semiannual report also supports the growth in scale.

On the other side, operating losses have persisted for five consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 10.9 billion.

Revenue concentrates in the fourth quarter because of seasonality, so quarterly results vary widely, and Q3 2026 revenue fell year on year while the operating loss widened. Operating cash outflow grew to KRW 7.03 billion in 2025 even as the loss narrowed.

On the financial side, equity of KRW 22.2 billion and a 62.3% debt ratio at the end of 2025 show improvement from 340.8% in 2023, and cash of about KRW 15.4 billion was held at mid-2026.

Strengths and weaknesses both exist, so the next points to check are fourth-quarter 2026 revenue and operating loss in the 2026 annual report and how the first-quarter 2027 operating loss compares with the prior year.

Whether Physical AI orders reach profit at the same pace as subscription revenue is the benchmark for judging the business transition.
