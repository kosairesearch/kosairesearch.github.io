Robot Reducers Grow as Quarterly Profit Softens

SPG is scaling its robot precision reducers, but declining motor sales and a net loss in 3Q 2026 keep its earnings trend mixed.

02

Business structure

SPG, founded in 1991, makes precision control motors and reducers and has been listed on KOSDAQ since 2002. A Samsung Securities report issued in March 2026 put geared motors at 30% of total sales and fan motors at 65%, with robot precision reducers at only 3%.

Geared motors serve automation lines and are produced in high-mix, low-volume runs, with an operating margin cited at around 10%. Fan motors used mainly in white goods are made in low-mix, high-volume runs, with a cited operating margin of 2–3%.

Profitability therefore rests on the existing motor business, while robot reducers are a small but growing new business. The same report describes SPG as the only domestic company able to produce harmonic, RV and planetary reducers. Its customers span factory automation, office automation and home appliances.

The corporate information service Wisereport states that SPG keeps a share above 50% in the domestic standard AC geared motor segment. However, the move of finished-goods makers' production bases overseas has been pulling down sales in that segment.

According to Hana Securities, overseas sales account for about 71% of the total, and as of 3Q 2025 subsidiaries made up about 33% of sales and the parent company about 67%. In robotics, SPG supplies reducers for collaborative robots to Rainbow Robotics, which is backed by Samsung Electronics.

Japanese makers such as Harmonic Drive and Nidec are named as main rivals in precision reducers, and the company points to delivery reliability and customization capability as its strengths.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual figures, sales fell from KRW 440.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 393.8 billion in 2023 (down 10.6% year on year), KRW 388.5 billion in 2024 (down 1.3%) and KRW 341.7 billion in 2025 (down 12.0%). Sales declined for three straight years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 25.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 16 billion in 2023 (down 37.3%) and KRW 12.3 billion in 2024 (down 22.8%), then recovered to KRW 17.9 billion in 2025 (up 45.4%). The operating margin moved from 5.8% in 2022 to 4.1% in 2023, 3.2% in 2024 and 5.3% in 2025.

The 2023 profit decline was attributed to a downturn in downstream industries caused by high interest rates. The 2025 recovery came while sales were shrinking, and Heungkuk Securities, analyzing the first half of 2025, pointed to a product mix shift away from low-margin home appliance motors and cost cuts.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 19.7 billion in 2022, KRW 11 billion in 2023, KRW 13.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.13 billion in 2025. In 2024 operating profit fell while controlling-shareholder net income rose 19.3%, so the two moved in opposite directions.

Quarterly, 3Q 2025 showed sales of KRW 84.1 billion, operating profit of KRW 4.93 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 3.65 billion. Operating profit peaked in 4Q 2025 at KRW 5 billion on sales of KRW 83.8 billion.

Sales then moved to KRW 80.8 billion in 1Q 2026, KRW 88.8 billion in 2Q and KRW 82.5 billion in 3Q, while operating profit fell to KRW 4.49 billion, KRW 4 billion and KRW 3.43 billion, the third straight quarterly decline. Operating profit in 3Q 2026 was down 30.5% year on year.

Controlling-shareholder net income swung to a loss of KRW 38.59 million in 4Q 2025, returned to a profit of KRW 3.46 billion in 1Q 2026, fell to KRW 2.44 billion in 2Q, and turned into a net loss of KRW 262 million in 3Q 2026. Controlling-shareholder net income over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 5.6 billion. Operating profit was still up in the first half of 2026 but turned down in the third quarter.

05

Industry analysis

SPG's end demand moves along two lines. Its existing geared and fan motors depend on factory automation investment and white goods production, and sales shrank in 2023 when high interest rates weakened downstream industries.

On the robot side, Hana Securities judges that the spread of physical AI is lifting demand for manufacturing and logistics automation. The same report said 2026 is likely to be the year when results in the reducer market become visible.

Heungkuk Securities noted rising demand for reducers that replace imported products in overhaul work on equipment such as OHT systems in semiconductor fabs.

On competition, the company promotes its in-house SPG Direct Drive solution, which integrates the reducer, motor and controller, and Hana Securities rated its vibration, noise, heat and weight performance as superior to overseas rivals.

Japanese makers Harmonic Drive and Nidec are the main rivals in precision reducers, and price competition from them has been flagged as a risk. In the existing business, a domestic share above 50% in standard AC geared motors forms the base of its position. The pace of robot growth depends on orders from domestic robot makers such as Rainbow Robotics.

06

Outlook

In a January 2026 report, Hana Securities estimated 2026 sales of KRW 418.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 27.1 billion and an operating margin of 6.5%, which would mean increases of 19.4% and 46.4% year on year.

The same report expected pure robot sales to expand beyond KRW 30 billion in 2026, roughly 7.2% of total estimated sales.

Drivers cited include wider reducer supply to Rainbow Robotics, replacement demand for reducer parts at an overseas plant, initial volumes of precision reducers for defense unmanned projects, and new product requests from global companies.

The company has also signed a memorandum of understanding with LG Science Park on technology cooperation for high-efficiency actuators and reducers. Securities industry reports said the company aims to start mass production of its SDD solution in the first half of 2026 and to sell 5,000 units a year.

However, cumulative sales for January to September 2026 were KRW 252.1 billion, down 2.2% year on year, while operating profit in the first half of 2026 was up 5.9%. Reaching the full-year estimate would require a sharp sales recovery in the fourth quarter.

Whether the SDD mass-production start and the annual sales target were met can be checked in the company's investor relations materials and in the annual report.

Operating profit fell in 2023 and 2024, which pushed earnings-based multiples higher, and the 2025 recovery in operating profit worked to bring them down. In 2026 quarterly losses appeared, which made earnings-based multiples over the latest four quarters less stable.

If earnings reach securities firms' estimates, the multiples would fall even at the same share price. Market capitalization sits at a large premium to total equity, and securities firms cite growth in robot reducers as the basis for that premium.

08

Bull factors

Robot reducer sales expansion path

Hana Securities expects pure robot sales to exceed KRW 30 billion in 2026, about 7.2% of estimated total sales. Cited drivers are wider reducer supply to Rainbow Robotics, replacement demand at an overseas plant, initial defense unmanned project volumes and new requests from global companies. The same report also views the technology MOU with LG Science Park as a platform for business expansion.

Recovery in operating margin

The operating margin rose from 3.2% in 2024 to 5.3% in 2025, and operating profit grew 45.4% in 2025. The corporate information service Wisereport notes that operating profit in the first half of 2026 was up 5.9% year on year.

Heungkuk Securities cites a product mix shift away from low-margin home appliance motors and cost reductions behind the margin improvement.

Domestic share and overseas exposure

Holding a share above 50% in the domestic standard AC geared motor segment underpins the existing business. With overseas sales at about 71% of the total, demand trends in overseas automation feed directly into results. The company operates geared motor manufacturing and sales subsidiaries in the United States, China and Vietnam.

Bear factors

Consecutive weakening of quarterly profit

Operating profit fell for three straight quarters, from KRW 4.49 billion in 1Q 2026 to KRW 3.43 billion in 3Q, with 3Q down 30.5% year on year. Controlling-shareholder net income swung into losses twice, with a net loss of KRW 38.59 million in 4Q 2025 and a net loss of KRW 262 million in 3Q 2026. Controlling-shareholder net income over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 5.6 billion, a thin earnings base.

Decline in core business sales

Sales declined for three straight years from 2023 through 2025, and 3Q 2026 sales were down 1.9% year on year. Cumulative sales for January to September 2026 were KRW 252.1 billion, down 2.2%. Finished-goods makers moving production overseas is cited as a structural drag on motor sales.

Estimate gap and rising competition

Hana Securities' January 2026 estimate of KRW 418.6 billion in 2026 sales is far above the cumulative results through September. In robot reducers, price pressure from Japanese rivals Harmonic Drive and Nidec is cited as a variable. If estimates prove too high, the credibility of the robot growth story could weaken.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

The operating margin swung widely, from 5.8% in 2022 to 3.2% in 2024 before rebounding to 5.3% in 2025. Quarterly operating profit has fallen for three straight quarters, and net income has moved between profit and loss. With a small earnings base, small changes in costs or volumes can change the direction of net income.

Customer and demand concentration

Robot sales depend on expanding volumes from specific customers such as Rainbow Robotics. If robot demand slows or customers delay investment plans, reducer volumes could fall. On the motor side, the relocation of finished-goods makers overseas is shrinking the sales base.

Valuation and share price volatility

On full-year 2025 figures, the multiples were well above the sector average. Quarterly losses have made earnings-based multiples harder to interpret. On the morning of November 13, 2026, a static volatility interruption was triggered and then lifted, illustrating how large the share price swings have been.

12

Overall view

SPG makes precision motors and reducers. Geared motors and fan motors account for most of its sales, while robot precision reducers are still only 3% of the total. On disclosed figures, sales fell from KRW 440.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 341.7 billion in 2025.

Operating profit recovered to KRW 17.9 billion in 2025 but remains below the KRW 25.5 billion of 2022. On a quarterly basis, 4Q 2025 was the operating profit peak, and operating profit has fallen for three straight quarters through 3Q 2026.

Controlling-shareholder net income swung back to a net loss of KRW 262 million in 3Q 2026, and the latest four quarters total KRW 5.6 billion. The bull case rests on expectations for robot reducer sales growth, the operating margin recovery since 2025 and the high domestic share in geared motors.

The bear case rests on three straight quarters of falling operating profit, three straight years of sales decline and multiples well above the sector average.

The next checkpoints are the 4Q 2026 and full-year preliminary results, and the operating margin and robot segment sales in the annual report due by the end of March 2027.
