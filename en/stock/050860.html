02

Business structure

Asia Technology makes and sells agricultural machinery, including hand-guided tillers, SS (speed sprayer) machines and tractors. In its corporate value-up plan disclosed on October 21, 2026, the company also listed the manufacture and sale of tillers, SS machines and tractors as its main businesses.

According to a 2024 press report, tillers accounted for 55.4% of annual sales on a 2023 basis, while combines, tractors and other items made up the remaining 44.6%. The company says few rival products can replace its tillers, and their multi-purpose use and low running costs keep replacement demand steady.

SS machines are spraying equipment; at the national touring exhibitions held from February 27 to March 6, 2026, the company showcased the spray-dedicated ASS5 and ASS6 and the multi-purpose ATS6 as new models.

Tractors are imported and sold under a technology partnership with a UK tractor maker, and a premium Massey Ferguson line was also displayed. As of a 2024 press report, exports covered 34 countries, with a focus on China and India, and the company has a Chinese subsidiary set up in 1998.

A October 29, 2026 report said the company planned to unveil three AI autonomous-flight agricultural drones at a Daegu expo running from November 4 to 7.

Domestic farm machinery is reported to be dominated by Daedong, TYM and LS Mtron, while Asia Technology's domestic share fell from 5.43% in 2021 to 5.26% in 2022 and 4.35% in 2023.

04

Earnings analysis

Sales were KRW 123.6 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023, KRW 123.8 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024 (up 0.2% year on year), KRW 113.3 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025 (down 8.5%), and KRW 121.1 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026 (up 6.9%).

The largest year in the period was the fiscal year ended June 2024, and the smallest was the fiscal year ended June 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 11.9 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to KRW 6.39 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024 (down 46.2%) and KRW 3.99 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025 (down 37.6%), before recovering to KRW 6.52 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026 (up 63.6%).

The operating margin moved from 9.6% to 5.2% and 3.5%, then rose to 5.4% in the fiscal year ended June 2026; in the fiscal year ended June 2024, however, the margin declined even though sales rose 0.2%.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 9.59 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to KRW 2.77 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025, then rose to KRW 5.55 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026 (up 100.3%), and net profit was reported at the same amounts in each year.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 5.18 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023 and -KRW 20.1 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024, then turned positive at KRW 13.2 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025 and KRW 11.5 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026.

Quarterly, an operating loss of KRW 1.47 billion in the third quarter of 2025 turned into operating profit of KRW 1.43 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, followed by KRW 200 million in the first quarter of 2026 and an expansion to KRW 6.37 billion in the second quarter of 2026.

The third quarter of 2026 brought sales of KRW 6.49 billion (down 85.9% from the prior quarter and down 54.1% year on year) and an operating loss of KRW 3.93 billion, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.29 billion.

Over the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 to third quarter of 2026), net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 2.57 billion, with profit concentrated in the second quarter of 2026.

Farm machinery is a seasonal industry in which government loans and off-season production versus busy-season sales are tied together, which is cited as a background to quarterly swings. Whether any items were one-off should be checked in the income statement lines of the quarterly report for the fourth quarter of 2026.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for farm machinery has relied on domestic demand created with government support, and according to a 2024 press report the company described it as moving with government subsidies and fiscal policy. Domestic farm machinery is reported as an oligopoly led by three large domestic makers on a 2023 basis.

Asia Technology's domestic share declined from 5.43% in 2021 to 5.26% in 2022 and 4.35% in 2023. Competition in large farm machinery has intensified at home and abroad, while small tillers are an area where the company has long built expertise.

The company says few rival products can replace its tillers, which keeps it advantaged in that market. Exports were in the KRW 900 million range in 2023, which was small relative to domestic sales, and the company is trying to widen its reach in large agricultural countries such as China and India.

In spraying, drones and SS machines are used together, and the company is reported to be trying to enter precision spraying with drones that combine LiDAR and 4D millimeter-wave radar.

Whether the third-quarter 2026 sales drop reflects policy and seasonal factors or shifts in industry demand needs to be read against industry demand indicators.

Thinner margins and sales base

Sales of KRW 121.1 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026 are still below KRW 123.6 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023.

Risk factors

Policy and seasonality

Farm machinery demand moves with government loans, subsidies and fiscal policy, as the company described in a 2024 press report. Seasonality between off-season production and busy-season sales makes quarterly results volatile.

Whether the third-quarter 2026 sales drop reflects this structure needs to be separated using quarterly disclosures, and any delay in policy funding could shake quarterly sales directly.

Execution risk in overseas expansion

Exports reach 34 countries according to press reports, but export sales were in the KRW 900 million range in 2023, small relative to domestic sales. Results from expanding in China and India and from entering new businesses such as drones may vary widely with execution.

A Thai production base was at the review stage as of a 2024 press report, and its scale and timing should be checked in periodic reports.

Shareholder structure and liquidity

The largest shareholder and related parties hold 35.10% and treasury shares account for 19.16%, as of August 21, 2026, so the number of shares that actually trade may be limited. A small market capitalization can produce large share price swings.

The shareholder return plan was presented without quantitative targets, which makes the scale of actual execution hard to gauge from outside.

Overall view
