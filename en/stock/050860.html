Report overview

Farm Machinery Profit Recovery, Quarterly Swings Remain

Asia Technology lifted operating profit by 63.6% in the fiscal year ended June 2026, but quarterly results remain volatile after the operating loss recorded in the third quarter of 2025.

  1. 1

    Sales for the fiscal year ended June 2026 were KRW 121.1 billion, up 6.9% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 6.52 billion, up 63.6%. The 5.4% operating margin remains below the 9.6% recorded in the fiscal year ended June 2023.

  2. 2

    Sales in the second quarter of 2026 reached KRW 46.2 billion and operating profit was KRW 6.37 billion. Sales have risen for three consecutive quarters since the fourth quarter of 2025.

  3. 3

    According to FnGuide data, cost of sales as a share of revenue rose from 74.18% in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to 79.13% in the fiscal year ended June 2025. This points to a factor that has weighed on margins.

  4. 4

    The value-up plan disclosed on October 21, 2026 sets out cost structure improvement, portfolio diversification, export diversification and wider shareholder returns, and includes a PBR improvement plan.

  5. 5

    Farm machinery products such as cultivators and SS-type machines account for about 90.7% of sales, and domestic sales made up 99.2% of that product group as of June 2025. Both product and market exposure are concentrated.

02

Business structure

Asia Technology was founded in 1978 to manufacture and sell farm machinery and has been listed on KOSDAQ since 2010. Its main products are cultivators, SS-type machines, binding machines and parts, and company profiles describe it as holding a high market share in small farm machinery.

Aggregated data from an information site puts farm machinery products such as cultivators and SS-type machines at 90.70% of sales, engines and related items at 6.80%, and power carriers and other items at 2.50%, although the reference date of that aggregation is not stated.

According to FnGuide, domestic sales accounted for 99.20% of cultivator and SS-type product sales as of June 2025, with exports at 0.80%.

Distribution data from a 2020 technical analysis report showed dealers at 73.2%, NH Central (Nonghyup) at 17.7%, exports at 0.9% and other channels at 8.2%, but since this is older data the current structure may differ.

An information site describes the farm machinery industry as seasonal, with government loans and production in the off-season and sales in the farming season. Headcount was 291 as of the end of June 2025.

The largest shareholder and related parties held 35.10% as of January 1, 2026, and treasury shares rose from 14.72% in January 2025 to 19.16% as of January 20, 2026.

No recent market share figure was confirmed, and the last confirmed figure is 5.96% for the fiscal year ended June 2020, so the competitive position is described qualitatively only.

04

Earnings analysis

Sales for the fiscal year ended June 2026 were KRW 121.1 billion, up 6.9% year on year.

Sales edged up from KRW 123.6 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to KRW 123.8 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024, then fell to KRW 113.3 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025, which was the smallest year in the period, while the fiscal year ended June 2024 was the largest.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 11.9 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023, fell to KRW 6.39 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024 (down 46.2%) and to KRW 3.99 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2025 (down 37.6%), then rose to KRW 6.52 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2026 (up 63.6%).

Operating margin moved from 9.6% to 5.2%, 3.5% and 5.4%, rebounding from the trough in the fiscal year ended June 2025 but still below the fiscal year ended June 2023 level.

FnGuide data show cost of sales as a share of revenue rising from 74.18% in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to 76.89% and then 79.13% in the fiscal year ended June 2025, a pattern consistent with falling sales and rising costs squeezing margins.

The cost ratio for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was not confirmed, so the margin recovery cannot be attributed to cost improvement. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 9.59 billion, KRW 4.61 billion, KRW 2.77 billion and KRW 5.55 billion for the fiscal years ended June 2023 through June 2026.

Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at KRW 9.59 billion, KRW 4.61 billion, KRW 2.77 billion and KRW 5.55 billion for the same years.

Operating cash flow was negative at -KRW 5.18 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023 and -KRW 20.1 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2024, then turned positive at KRW 13.2 billion and KRW 11.5 billion in the fiscal years ended June 2025 and June 2026.

The debt ratio rose from 33.6% to 49.8%, 51.5% and 50.8%, staying around 50% since the fiscal year ended June 2024. Quarterly sales were KRW 37.2 billion in the second quarter of 2025, then fell 62.0% to KRW 14.1 billion in the third quarter with an operating loss of KRW 1.47 billion.

They recovered to KRW 28.9 billion in the fourth quarter, with operating profit of KRW 1.43 billion, then KRW 31.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026 with operating profit of KRW 200 million, and KRW 46.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026 with operating profit of KRW 6.37 billion.

Sales have increased for three consecutive quarters since the fourth quarter of 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for the four quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the second quarter of 2026 totaled KRW 5.55 billion.

The drivers of the second-quarter 2026 jump and any one-off items were not confirmed in the disclosures or search results and need to be checked in the quarterly report.

Industry analysis

An information site describes the farm machinery industry as strongly seasonal, with government loans and sales concentrated in the farming season.

Quarterly results can therefore swing more than annual trends, and the sharp drop in sales in the third quarter of 2025 and the jump in the second quarter of 2026 may reflect both seasonality and individual orders. No data separating seasonal effects from one-off factors were confirmed.

Industry-wide demand outlooks and market size statistics were not confirmed in this search, so the position in the cycle is not determined here.

On competition, the company is described as holding a high share in small farm machinery, but no recent share figure was confirmed, and the last confirmed figure is 5.96% for the fiscal year ended June 2020.

On balance sheet structure, an information site aggregate as of September 14, 2026 shows a machinery sector average debt ratio of 92.26%, while Asia Technology's debt ratio of 51.52% sits below that average.

The same aggregate puts the sector average dividend yield at 2.17%, and the company's yield is shown above that level. Few listed machinery companies are pure-play farm equipment makers, which limits direct comparison with the sector average.

Because cost of sales as a share of revenue has risen since the fiscal year ended June 2023, it remains to be checked whether cost changes such as materials and labor affect farm machinery makers broadly.

Government policy loan volumes and disbursement trends are also key demand variables, but no specific figures were confirmed in this search.

Outlook

The value-up plan disclosed on October 21, 2026 sets out three tasks: strengthening operating competitiveness and improving cost structure in the existing business, building a base for future growth through portfolio diversification, and pursuing sustained growth and higher profitability through export diversification.

Shareholder-friendly measures include expanding shareholder return policies, diversifying IR channels and revitalizing regular IR activities. The plan includes a PBR improvement plan and does not include a plan for technology growth companies. No sales target or profitability figure was confirmed in the plan.

At the regular shareholders meeting, the financial statements for the 48th term, an amendment to part of the articles of incorporation, the re-election of an executive director, and the approval of a plan to hold and dispose of treasury shares were approved, while the proposal to approve the director compensation limit was rejected.

The size and timing of the treasury share disposal plan were not confirmed.

The business report for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was submitted on September 11, 2026, and the auditor, Samduk Accounting Corporation, gave an unqualified opinion on the standalone financial statements, with no going-concern uncertainty flagged.

Sales or operating profit guidance, order backlog, and plant expansion or new product launch schedules were not confirmed in this search.

Because the export diversification task starts from an export share of 0.80% as of June 2025, its progress should be checked through changes in the export share reported in quarterly filings.

The next checkpoints are whether the third-quarter 2026 quarterly report explains the sales increase and the cost ratio, and whether the IR activities and treasury share disposal are actually carried out.

Operating cash flow stayed positive at KRW 13.2 billion and KRW 11.5 billion in the fiscal years ended June 2025 and June 2026.

Consecutive quarterly sales growth

Sales have risen for three consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the second quarter of 2026. Operating profit in the second quarter of 2026 was KRW 6.37 billion, up 479.2% year on year.

Shareholder returns and PBR plan

The treasury share ratio rose from 14.72% in January 2025 to 19.16% as of January 20, 2026. A plan to hold and dispose of treasury shares was approved at the regular shareholders meeting.

Bear factors

Cumulative margin pressure

The operating margin for the fiscal year ended June 2025 was 3.5%, the lowest in the period. Over the same span, FnGuide data show cost of sales as a share of revenue rising from 74.18% in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to 79.13% in the fiscal year ended June 2025.

The cost ratio for the fiscal year ended June 2026 was not confirmed, so it is hard to tell whether the recovery reflects cost improvement.

Large quarterly swings

Sales in the third quarter of 2025 fell 62.0% from the prior quarter to KRW 14.1 billion, and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.47 billion. Operating profit in the first quarter of 2026 was KRW 200 million, down 86.0% from the prior quarter. Because quarterly swings are large, annual results alone make the trend hard to judge.

Concentration in product and market

Aggregated data put farm machinery products such as cultivators and SS-type machines at about 90.70% of sales, though the reference date was not confirmed. The export share of cultivator and SS-type products was 0.80% as of June 2025. Dependence on one product group and the domestic market means demand changes feed directly into results.

Risk factors

Seasonality and reliance on policy funding

The farm machinery industry shows seasonality, with government loans and production in the off-season and sales in the farming season. Changes in policy loan conditions or farm demand could move quarterly sales sharply. Policy loan volumes and disbursement data needed to size this risk were not confirmed in this search.

Costs and margins

Cost of sales as a share of revenue rose from 74.18% in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to 79.13% in the fiscal year ended June 2025, weighing on the operating margin. Operating profit rose sharply in the second quarter of 2026, but one quarter is not enough to tell whether the cost structure has changed. The effect of raw material and labor cost changes on profit should be checked in quarterly reports.

Limited free float

Taken together, the largest shareholder and related parties at 35.10% and treasury shares at 19.16% leave a limited free float. An information site puts the free float ratio at 45.73%, based on data around June 2026. Thin trading conditions can make the share price move sharply on small volumes.

Overall view

Asia Technology is a farm machinery maker whose main products are cultivators and SS-type machines, and its recovery is visible in the fiscal year ended June 2026, with sales of KRW 121.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 6.52 billion.

The operating margin fell from 9.6% in the fiscal year ended June 2023 to 3.5% in the fiscal year ended June 2025 before rebounding to 5.4%, so the recovery is still short of the earlier level.

Sales in the second quarter of 2026 reached KRW 46.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 6.37 billion, extending a rise over three consecutive quarters, but the drivers and any one-off items remain unconfirmed.

On the financial side, the debt ratio sits around 50% and operating cash flow has been positive since the fiscal year ended June 2025.

The value-up plan includes cost structure improvement, portfolio diversification, export diversification, expanded shareholder returns and a PBR improvement plan, but no concrete targets were confirmed in the disclosure.

Strengths include the earnings recovery, consecutive quarterly sales growth and the shareholder return plan, while weaknesses include the rise in cost ratio, large quarterly swings and concentration in farm machinery and the domestic market.
