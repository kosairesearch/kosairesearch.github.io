02

Business structure

Asia Technology was founded in September 1978 and makes and sells agricultural machinery; it listed on KOSDAQ in January 2010.

Its main products are multi-purpose tillers, riding tillers, SS (speed sprayer) machines, tractors and binders, and company profile materials describe agricultural machinery sales as the main source of revenue.

The company says its multi-purpose tiller has an international reputation and is exported to several countries, and that its binder was the first Korean product exported to eight European countries, where it received a quality certification mark.

According to its company introduction, it also holds contracts with livestock machinery makers in Germany and Japan for domestic sales, so its model combines distribution of foreign brands with its own products.

Domestic sales run through regional offices in provincial capitals, county dealers and agricultural cooperative purchasing channels, and a 2018 IR document cited 145 county dealers and 500 purchasing cooperatives.

The company presents its tiller as the world's top product by production and sales, but this is the company's own description and should be read apart from independent market-share data.

Among Korean farm machinery makers, it is described as one of the general producers, with its portfolio concentrated in tillers and sprayers.

As of December 2023, chief shareholder Kim Shin-gil held 25.53% and treasury shares accounted for 5.41%, and Kim Eun-su took office as co-representative director in November 2023. Because revenue depends on farm incomes and farmers' purchasing capacity, quarterly sales tend to move sharply.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filing basis, revenue went from KRW 123.6 billion in the June 2023 fiscal year to KRW 123.8 billion in the June 2024 fiscal year (up 0.2% from the prior year), then fell to KRW 113.3 billion in the June 2025 fiscal year (down 8.5%).

Revenue recovered to KRW 121.1 billion in the June 2026 fiscal year (up 6.9%), but remained below the June 2024 level.

Operating profit went from KRW 11.9 billion in the June 2023 fiscal year to KRW 6.39 billion in the June 2024 fiscal year (down 46.2%) and KRW 3.99 billion in the June 2025 fiscal year (down 37.6%), before rebounding to KRW 6.52 billion in the June 2026 fiscal year (up 63.6%).

Operating margins of 9.6%, 5.2%, 3.5% and 5.4% show that margins stayed in a low range while annual revenue hovered near KRW 110 to 125 billion after the June 2024 fiscal year.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 9.59 billion, KRW 4.61 billion, KRW 2.77 billion and KRW 5.55 billion across the four fiscal years, with the June 2026 figure up 100.3%.

Net profit including non-controlling interests is reported at the same amounts in each year, so the filing does not show a gap between the two lines.

Operating cash flow moved from an outflow of -KRW 20.1 billion in the June 2024 fiscal year to inflows of KRW 13.2 billion and KRW 11.5 billion in the June 2025 and June 2026 fiscal years, while the debt-to-equity ratio rose from 33.6% to 50.8% over the same period.

On a quarterly basis, Q2 2025 revenue of KRW 37.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.1 billion fell to KRW 14.1 billion in Q3 2025 (down 62.0% from the prior quarter), where the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.47 billion.

Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 28.9 billion (up 104.3% from the prior quarter) produced operating profit of KRW 1.43 billion, returning to profit. Q1 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 200 million (down 86.0% from the prior quarter) on revenue of KRW 31.9 billion.

Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 46.2 billion (up 44.9% from the prior quarter and 24.3% year on year) carried operating profit of KRW 6.37 billion (up 479.2% year on year). Revenue has risen for three straight quarters from Q4 2025 through Q2 2026.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders in Q2 2026 was KRW 5.38 billion, which accounts for most of the KRW 5.55 billion total of the last four quarters and matches the June 2026 fiscal year figure.

Because Q2 2026 controlling-shareholder profit came in below that quarter's operating profit, the items that bridged the two lines, including non-operating items and taxes, are a point to check in the quarterly report detail.

Industry analysis

The farm machinery market that Asia Technology serves is driven by farm incomes and government agricultural policy, unlike the construction equipment or industrial robot markets in the same machinery and equipment sector.

Doosan Bobcat and HD Construction Equipment focus on construction equipment, while Doosan Robotics and Rainbow Robotics focus on robots, so their end markets differ from Asia Technology's farm equipment business.

The Korean farm machinery market includes both general manufacturers and specialist makers, and the company is introduced as one of the general producers. Its core tiller line is tied to demand for small farm equipment, so its competitiveness rests on brand recognition and its sales network.

On exports, the company has shipped multi-purpose tillers to several countries and binders to eight European countries, which gives it partial exposure to overseas markets.

Revenue has moved in a range of about KRW 110 billion to KRW 125 billion since the June 2024 fiscal year, so whether the recovery in the industry is translating into revenue growth will show in the next quarters' sales.

The pace of margin recovery depends on product mix and cost structure, which should be checked in segment disclosures. Farm equipment demand is generally described as affected by farm incomes, rural labor conditions and government subsidy programs, and the direction of these variables is hard to call.

Whether the rise in Q2 2026 revenue reflects seasonal shipments or a one-time effect should be read against the full-year trend.

Incomplete margin recovery

The operating margin has moved from 9.6% in the June 2023 fiscal year to 5.4% in the June 2026 fiscal year. Operating profit of KRW 6.52 billion in the June 2026 fiscal year is a little more than half of the KRW 11.9 billion recorded in the June 2023 fiscal year. The margin has not returned to earlier levels even as revenue recovered.

Concentration of quarterly profit

Of the KRW 5.55 billion total for the last four quarters, Q2 2026 contributed KRW 5.38 billion, which accounts for most of it. Q1 2026 controlling-shareholder profit was KRW 132 million, and Q3 2025 posted a net loss of KRW 319 million. Heavy reliance on a single quarter for annual profit is a weakness for earnings durability.

Risk factors

Demand and industry conditions

Farm equipment demand is generally described as affected by farm conditions and government subsidy policy, so a weakening of these factors could reduce revenue again. The 8.5% revenue decline in the June 2025 fiscal year shows that this risk has already materialized once. The operating margin also fell to 3.5% during that downturn.

Whether profit is one-off

Q2 2026 profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 5.38 billion is below that quarter's operating profit of KRW 6.37 billion, so non-operating items and taxes affected the earnings structure. Whether these items are one-off or recurring should be confirmed in the quarterly report. If they are one-off, the strength of the June 2026 fiscal year profit recovery may read lower.

Balance sheet and cash flow

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 33.6% in the June 2023 fiscal year to 50.8% in the June 2026 fiscal year. Operating cash flow recorded an outflow of -KRW 20.1 billion in the June 2024 fiscal year.
