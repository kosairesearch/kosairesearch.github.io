Report overview

Annual Profit Turn, but Quarterly Sales Keep Falling

Flask posted a controlling net profit of KRW 300 million for 2025, but sales have fallen for three straight quarters through Q3 2026, and the company swung to a controlling net loss of KRW 1.61 billion in that quarter.

02

Business structure

Flask was founded in 1998 and listed on KOSDAQ in November 2002. Its core is the comprehensive interior business, which accounted for KRW 13.1 billion of KRW 14.2 billion in 2024 revenue, or about 92%.

Because revenue comes from contracted construction projects, sales are closely tied to construction activity and order flow.

To diversify, the company expanded into games: it acquired a game channeling business in May 2025, and a company summary on Enterprise Monitor noted that the IT segment posted operating profit after that acquisition.

A blockchain-based subsidiary filed for bankruptcy on July 31, 2025; Flask had invested about KRW 17 billion in it, and the full amount was booked as an impairment after the business stalled and capital impairment persisted.

On November 6, 2026, the board approved acquiring 100% of a chemical machinery and refrigeration parts maker for about KRW 8.5 billion, which the company said aims to diversify into a new business. The purchase equals 20.54% of equity.

The largest shareholder is BNM Holdings, and in March 2026 the cancellation of a preferred bidder for a sale of the largest shareholder's stake left an open governance question.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 39.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 22.8 billion in 2023 (down 42.6% year on year) and to KRW 14.2 billion in 2024 (down 37.8%), then rebounded to KRW 18 billion in 2025 (up 27.1%). Of 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating loss widened from KRW 13.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 23 billion in 2023, then narrowed to KRW 11.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.96 billion in 2025, and the company recorded operating losses in each of the four years from 2022 to 2025.

Operating margin moved from -34.1% in 2022 to -100.9% in 2023, -78.7% in 2024 and -10.9% in 2025. Controlling net loss was KRW 25.4 billion in 2022 and KRW 37.5 billion in 2023, narrowed to KRW 5.61 billion in 2024, and turned into a controlling net profit of KRW 300 million in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 300 million in 2025. The company attributed the 2025 turn to higher core sales and lower costs from business restructuring. In 2024, gains from monetizing investment property boosted non-operating results, so one-off items helped the earnings recovery.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 13.4 billion in 2022, -KRW 18.8 billion in 2023, -KRW 11 billion in 2024 and -KRW 2.15 billion in 2025, a narrower outflow. Quarterly, the operating loss of KRW 89.64 million in Q3 2025 became an operating profit of KRW 1.31 billion in Q4 2025, when revenue peaked at KRW 7.1 billion.

Revenue then fell to KRW 2.57 billion in Q1 2026 (down 63.8% from the prior quarter), KRW 2.53 billion in Q2 and KRW 1.69 billion in Q3, a third straight decline. Operating losses were KRW 778 million, KRW 863 million and KRW 1.17 billion in the three quarters of 2026.

Controlling net income was KRW 239 million in Q1 2026 and KRW 200 million in Q2, but Q3 2026 showed a controlling net loss of KRW 1.61 billion. In Q3 2025, controlling net income of KRW 945 million came despite an operating loss, showing that non-operating items drove profit in some quarters. Controlling net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 671 million.

05

Industry analysis

Flask's results are highly exposed to the construction cycle. The company attributed the 2024 sales decline to a slump in construction and downstream markets. A 2026 company summary on Enterprise Monitor also cited a weaker construction market alongside rising costs, so the industry backdrop still reads as a headwind.

Revenue held up in the first half of 2026 as existing contracts were delivered, which shows the role of the order backlog in supporting results. Yet Q3 2026 revenue falling to KRW 1.69 billion shows how quickly a slowdown in new orders shows up in quarterly sales.

Interior construction profitability depends on cost control and project selection, so the company's plan to win more profitable projects and tighten costs will matter until the industry recovers. On the cycle, the prevailing summaries describe construction as still under pressure.

Games expanded through the 2025 channeling acquisition, but the failure of the blockchain subsidiary shows that new areas carry impairment risk. A numerical comparison with listed peers is outside what the filings here support, so this section stays qualitative. Results will likely depend on both an industry recovery and the company's own margin repair.

06

Outlook

The company's stated direction is margin improvement. A company summary on Enterprise Monitor said Flask plans to win more profitable projects and tighten cost control to improve profitability. The IT segment's operating profit after the game channeling acquisition is one part of that plan.

The main new business step is the purchase of 100% of the chemical machinery and refrigeration parts maker. The filing lists a KRW 4 billion deposit paid on November 6, 2026, and a balance of about KRW 4.5 billion due on February 19, 2027.

The scheduled transfer date may change depending on fulfillment of conditions precedent and agreement between the parties. The company stated that no specific restructuring plan had been decided as of the filing date.

The filing also states that there is no plan for a third-party allotment capital increase within six months. The next checkpoint for operating performance is the 2026 annual report's full-year revenue and operating loss.

With quarterly sales down for three straight quarters, whether Q1 2027 revenue rebounds will be the first signal of a change in the earnings structure. After the acquisition closes, how much the new business contributes to consolidated results will also be a key point to watch.

08

Bull factors

Narrower losses and a 2025 return to profit

Operating margin improved from -100.9% in 2023 to -10.9% in 2025. Controlling net income reached KRW 300 million in 2025, turning around from a controlling net loss of KRW 5.61 billion in 2024. The company cited higher core sales and lower costs from business restructuring as the drivers.

Lower debt burden and a stronger balance sheet

Total liabilities at 2025 year-end were KRW 5.71 billion, and the debt ratio fell to 13.8% from 107.1% in 2023. Total equity was KRW 41.4 billion, with the same amount attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash outflow narrowed to KRW 2.15 billion in 2025 from KRW 18.8 billion in 2023.

Diversification through the planned acquisition

The target is a chemical machinery and refrigeration parts maker, and the deal is a 100% stake acquisition. The company stated the aim is diversification into a new business. The price equals 20.54% of equity.

Bear factors

Three straight quarters of falling sales

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 1.69 billion, down 33.2% from the prior quarter and 73.8% from a year earlier. Sales have declined since the peak of KRW 7.1 billion in Q4 2025. As quarterly revenue shrank, the operating loss and the sales decline have moved in the same direction.

Three straight operating losses and a Q3 net loss

Operating losses were KRW 778 million in Q1 2026, KRW 863 million in Q2 and KRW 1.17 billion in Q3, a third straight quarter of losses. Controlling net income was positive in Q1 and Q2 but swung to a KRW 1.61 billion loss in Q3. The KRW 1.31 billion operating profit in Q4 2025 was confined to a single quarter.

Weak construction market and a history of subsidiary impairment

A company summary on Enterprise Monitor cited a weaker construction market alongside rising costs. A blockchain subsidiary filed for bankruptcy on July 31, 2025, and the roughly KRW 17 billion invested was fully written off as an impairment.

The 2023 annual report received a disclaimer of opinion due to a scope limitation, which triggered a delisting criterion.

Risk factors

Governance and largest shareholder

On March 9, 2026, the company disclosed notice that the preferred bidder for a sale of the largest shareholder's stake had been cancelled. The company said the matter had no direct effect on operations or financing. How the largest shareholder's stake is ultimately structured remains a governance variable. The history of a disclaimer in 2023 and a clean opinion in 2024 also keeps audit matters in focus.

Concentration and industry exposure

Interior construction made up about 92% of 2024 revenue, so sales are concentrated in one business. A slowdown in construction directly reduces project volume and revenue. Rising costs could work against the company's margin improvement plan.

Execution and funding of the new acquisition

The acquisition involves a KRW 4 billion deposit scheduled for November 6, 2026, and a balance of about KRW 4.5 billion scheduled for February 19, 2027. The filing says the transfer date may change depending on conditions precedent and agreement between the parties. Until the new business produces results, an investment equal to 20.54% of equity remains a financial burden.

12

Overall view

Flask posted operating losses in each year from 2022 to 2025, but the operating loss narrowed sharply from KRW 23 billion in 2023 to KRW 1.96 billion in 2025. It returned to a controlling net profit of KRW 300 million in 2025 and cut its debt ratio to 13.8%.

In 2026, however, quarterly revenue fell to KRW 2.57 billion, KRW 2.53 billion and KRW 1.69 billion over three straight quarters, operating losses continued, and Q3 turned into a controlling net loss of KRW 1.61 billion.

With controlling net income totaling only KRW 671 million over the last four quarters, how far the annual profit can hold in quarterly results is still hard to gauge.

The core interior business is exposed to a weak construction market and rising costs, while the planned 100% acquisition of the machinery maker is a diversification step that also carries a KRW 8.5 billion funding commitment.

On governance, the cancellation of the preferred bidder for the largest shareholder's stake in March 2026 leaves a variable open. Strengths are the return to profit and low debt; weaknesses are falling sales and wider quarterly losses.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report's audit opinion and full-year results, the balance payment scheduled for February 19, 2027, and whether Q1 2027 revenue rebounds.
