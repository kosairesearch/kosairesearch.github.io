02

Business structure

Nobland International was founded in October 1994 and is a KOSDAQ-listed apparel manufacturer with 224 employees as of September 2026.

The company acquired Unico Global, an outdoor apparel specialist, in 2024 and later acquired Banao, a maker of shirts, blouses and dresses, which it says gives it production capability across all product lines. Its model is OEM and ODM, so revenue moves with customer order flow.

Revenue fell in 2025 after US tariff increases, and order intake has recovered as customers asked for production shifts to Vietnam. Listed peers in the same textile, fashion and lifestyle sector include Youngone and Hansae on the OEM side and F&F, LF, Handsome and Shinsegae International on the fashion-brand side.

Segment revenue mix is not shown in this report, so the business is described at the total-revenue level. Production location and US buyer orders are the main sources of earnings variability.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 459.1 billion in 2023, KRW 546.2 billion in 2024 (up 19.0% year on year) and KRW 602.2 billion in 2025 (up 10.2% year on year), rising two years in a row from 2024 to 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 10.5 billion in 2023 to KRW 17.6 billion in 2024 (up 67.6%), then fell to KRW 13.3 billion in 2025 (down 24.1%). 2024 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest across 2023 to 2025. Operating margin was 2.3% in 2023, 3.2% in 2024 and 2.2% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from KRW 842 million in 2023 to a net loss of KRW 17 billion in 2024, then returned to profit at KRW 5.08 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests followed the same annual pattern.

Operating cash flow was KRW -13.4 billion in 2023, KRW 13.7 billion in 2024 and KRW -15.5 billion in 2025. The debt ratio was 178.9% in 2023, 155.4% in 2024 and 165.0% in 2025.

In quarterly terms, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 163.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 8.99 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.59 billion.

Q4 2025 revenue fell 16.3% quarter on quarter to KRW 136.5 billion, operating profit dropped to KRW 651 million, and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.02 billion.

Q1 2026 revenue rose 26.5% to KRW 172.6 billion with operating profit of KRW 7.54 billion and net income of KRW 2.66 billion. Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 173.4 billion, operating profit fell 55.3% to KRW 3.37 billion, and the company posted a net loss of KRW 117 million.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 197 billion, up 13.6% quarter on quarter and 20.7% year on year. Operating profit was KRW 5.95 billion, up 76.5% quarter on quarter but down 33.8% year on year. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.78 billion, up 26.0% year on year.

Revenue has risen for three quarters since Q4 2025, but operating profit in Q3 2026 remained below the prior-year level, so sales growth has not yet carried through to profit.

Industry analysis

Nobland International operates in an apparel OEM and ODM market that is directly exposed to US tariff policy and production relocation. According to fnguide's company summary, revenue fell in 2025 after US tariff increases, and order intake later turned higher as customers requested production shifts to Vietnam.

That pattern matches the 2025 revenue decline and the quarterly revenue gains in 2026. Competitive positioning can be viewed across two groups: OEM players such as Youngone and Hansae, and fashion brand players such as F&F, LF, Handsome and Shinsegae International.

The 2024 Unico Global deal and the later Banao deal broadened the product range into outdoor, shirts, blouses and dresses, which widens the scope of orders the company can take. However, Q3 2026 revenue grew 20.7% year on year while operating profit fell 33.8%, so order growth has not yet translated into margin gains.

Cost structure and expense trends need to be checked in the next periodic report to understand this gap. A debt ratio of 165.0% and operating cash outflow of KRW 15.5 billion in 2025 point to working-capital pressure as production scales.

Bear factors

Thin margin and falling operating profit

Operating margin fell to 2.2% in 2025 from 3.2% in 2024. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 5.95 billion was down 33.8% year on year, so profit fell even as revenue grew. Q2 2026 produced a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 117 million.

Leverage and cash flow

At end-2025, total liabilities exceed total equity. Operating cash flow was negative in both 2023 and 2025, at KRW -13.4 billion and KRW -15.5 billion. Working-capital needs from order growth could limit financial flexibility.

Large swings in quarterly profit

Q4 2025 operating profit was KRW 651 million with a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.02 billion. Operating profit fell 55.3% quarter on quarter in Q2 2026 after recovering in Q1. Profit has alternated between gains and losses across quarters.

Risk factors

Trade and tariff policy

The company profile notes that 2025 sales fell after US tariff increases. Further changes in US tariff policy could affect production-shift demand and order flow. Vietnam expansion also depends on local production costs and supply-chain conditions.

Acquisition integration and product expansion

Nobland International acquired Unico Global in 2024 and later Banao to widen its production range. The integration of both acquired businesses' production systems and customer order management needs to proceed as planned for the order expansion to take effect.

Delays in integration or uneven production efficiency across product lines could leave a gap between the stated full-product capability and actual order results.

Durability of US buyer orders

Orders from US buyers are cited as a main source of variability in Nobland International's revenue. Whether the Q3 2026 revenue growth continues into Q4 will show whether the order recovery is temporary or sustained.

If orders concentrate in certain periods or buyers reassign production locations, quarterly revenue could fall again.
