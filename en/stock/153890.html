Back in the Black, Durability Still Open

Justek returned to cumulative profitability through Q3 2026, but customer concentration and quarterly volatility will determine whether the turn holds.

02

Business structure

Justek, founded in 1999, makes ultra-precision motion control products and has vertically integrated the chain from core parts such as linear motors, DD motors and controllers to precision stages and specialized motion systems.

As of 2025, the business centers on semiconductor and display motion systems, with the semiconductor share rising from 24.8% in 2023 to 41.6% in 2025 while the display share fell from 58.3% to 45.9% over the same period.

Its key semiconductor product is precision stages used in laser cutting and annealing, and the company is extending these into HBM and advanced packaging processes. The display business is built around stages for OLED processes and is described as a stable revenue base.

The industrial automation unit is expanding LMS applications for logistics and automotive production lines. The space unit has developed reaction wheels and control moment gyroscopes for satellite attitude control, but its 2025 sales were only about KRW 200 million.

Customer concentration is high, with the largest customer at 25.5% of 2025 revenue and the top five at about 69%.

Few domestic listed companies offer directly comparable ultra-precision motion systems, so a practical benchmark is semiconductor equipment component makers in the same machinery sector, such as Philoptics and Sunic System.

The company established a Japanese subsidiary in 2018 and has been growing high-performance stage and special motor sales there, while preparing for expansion into China, Vietnam, the United States and Europe.

03

Recent trends

On a DART-filed basis, cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 was KRW 7.42 billion, up 154.7% from the same period a year earlier. Operating profit over the same period was KRW 610 million, a swing to profit, and net income was KRW 421 million, also a swing to profit.

On a 2025 consolidated basis, revenue was KRW 22.16 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 840 million and a net loss of KRW 1.52 billion. The company attributed the 2025 loss to failing to recognize KRW 1.808 billion in revenue due to a payment dispute with a specific counterparty.

The company itself noted that delayed or uncollectible payment cannot be fully ruled out. Retained earnings turned into an accumulated deficit after the 2025 loss, while the debt ratio fell from 351.21% in 2022 to 93.32% in 2025, a trend reported as improving financial stability.

First-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 3.26 billion with an operating loss of KRW 610 million and a net loss of KRW 610 million, so which quarter produced the cumulative profit can be verified only through quarterly filings. Shares opened on June 29, 2026 at KRW 17,530, 40.24% above the KRW 12,500 offering price.

As of 6:26 a.m. KST on November 13, 2026, the share price was KRW 6,520, below the offering price, and the market capitalization was KRW 79.2 billion.

04

Outlook

In its securities registration statement, the company projected revenue of KRW 33.4 billion for 2026, KRW 49.1 billion for 2027 and KRW 67.4 billion for 2028, and forecast a return to operating profit of KRW 4.3 billion in 2026.

Compared with cumulative revenue of KRW 7.42 billion for the first nine months of 2026, reaching the full-year estimate would require revenue to be heavily concentrated in the fourth quarter.

These are company projections based on more intensive inspection and metrology processes and new customer supply, so whether they are achieved will be confirmed through the annual business report.

In semiconductors, the company is pursuing entry into the HBM front-end overlay measurement and back-end inspection stage market and set a target of completing localization of wafer inspection stages in 2026.

For space, the company targets expanding sales from about KRW 200 million in 2025 to roughly KRW 4 billion in 2028, but it has acknowledged that timing can slip with government budgets, policy changes and launch schedules.

Of the KRW 16.8 billion in offering proceeds, KRW 7.0 billion is allocated to facilities, KRW 7.4 billion to operations and KRW 2.0 billion to debt repayment, and some proceeds fund a new plant scheduled for completion in 2028.

The items to track are whether fourth-quarter revenue follows the full-year path, whether collections in the payment dispute proceed, whether the semiconductor share holds around 40%, and whether space project deliveries stay on schedule.

05

Bull factors

Rising semiconductor mix and growth outlook

The semiconductor share of motion-system sales reached 41.6% in 2025, and a Korea Economic Daily report cited a share of 30 to 40% and a semiconductor sales CAGR of about 61% through 2028. Precision stages for HBM and advanced packaging are presented as a core axis of expansion.

The company has also widened its semiconductor equipment customer base since 2025. The growth projection rests on press reporting and should be separated from figures in company filings.

Cumulative profit swing and sharp revenue growth

On a DART-filed basis, cumulative January to September 2026 revenue rose 154.7% year on year, and both operating and net income swung to profit. Compared with the 2025 operating loss of KRW 840 million and net loss of KRW 1.52 billion, the direction of earnings has reversed.

The debt ratio fell to 93.32% in 2025 and the current ratio improved to 142.29%. Whether this carries into quarterly results will be confirmed in filings from the fourth quarter onward.

Vertical integration and localization in motion control

The company says it designs its own linear motors, DD motors, sensors, drivers and controllers, reducing reliance on external parts. Its success in localizing linear motors in Korea is presented as the technical foundation of the business.

Management states that it can shorten delivery times by three to four months against leading overseas suppliers and price at more than 30% below them. These are company statements, and actual competitiveness will be reflected in customer decisions and order disclosures.

06

Bear factors

Customer concentration and lumpy quarterly sales

The largest customer accounted for 25.5% of 2025 revenue, and the top five together for about 69%. Quarterly sales have been concentrated in certain periods, with the share of fourth-quarter revenue reaching 35.6% in 2025 and above 50% in 2024.

Results depend on customer investment schedules and delivery timing, which raises questions about how stable the nine-month profit is on a quarterly basis. Display still accounted for 45.9% of 2025 sales, leaving the company exposed to a downturn in that segment.

Collection uncertainty and the 2025 loss legacy

The company attributed the 2025 operating loss to KRW 1.808 billion in revenue that could not be recognized because of a payment dispute with a specific counterparty. It also acknowledged that delayed or uncollectible payment cannot be ruled out.

The 2025 loss pushed retained earnings into an accumulated deficit, and that balance-sheet effect persists until collection is resolved. How the dispute is reflected in future quarterly results is a key checkpoint.

Post-listing share price below the offering price

After opening at KRW 17,530 on the first trading day, June 29, 2026, the share price stood at KRW 6,520 at 6:26 a.m. KST on November 13, 2026, below the KRW 12,500 offering price.

Free float immediately after listing was limited to about 29.4% of listed shares, and the largest shareholder and related parties agreed to multi-year lock-ups. Because the company was loss-making in 2025, a P/E at the offering price was not meaningful, and valuation was debated during the IPO process. Share prices also reflect supply, demand and market sentiment around earnings releases.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Semiconductor equipment spending moves sharply with memory market conditions and capex cycles. Sentiment in the chip sector deteriorated around the time of the June 2026 listing on concerns about slowing memory demand.

With semiconductors at about 40% of sales, customer capex adjustments could affect revenue and profitability directly. Exchange rates and overseas sales, including Japan, are also variables to track.

Business and policy

The company itself noted that its space business is pursued project by project and can be delayed or changed by government budgets, policy shifts or launch schedules. Space sales were only about KRW 200 million in 2025, so the near-term effect on total results is limited, although the 2028 target depends on it.

Construction of a new plant and cleanroom uses offering proceeds, and schedule slippage could affect utilization and cost structure.

Liquidity and supply

Free float was limited right after listing, but it will widen in stages as lock-ups expire, with the filing materials showing a substantial increase about one year after listing. With trading value currently modest, large share releases could increase price volatility. Changes in holdings by the largest shareholder and related parties should be checked through disclosures.

08

Overall view

On a cumulative basis through the third quarter of 2026, Justek showed both a sharp revenue increase and a swing to operating and net profit, reversing the 2025 loss trend. A semiconductor share of motion-system sales that reached 41.6% suggests the business mix is shifting.

However, a largest-customer share of 25.5%, a top-five share of about 69%, and the concentration of quarterly sales in the fourth quarter make the durability of profit difficult to judge. The collection outcome of the payment dispute cited as the cause of the 2025 loss is also not yet settled.

The company's 2026 revenue estimate of KRW 33.4 billion and 2028 target of KRW 67.4 billion are far above cumulative results, so they can only be verified through annual disclosures.

The share price of KRW 6,520 is below the KRW 12,500 offering price, and filings alone cannot establish whether the gap reflects earnings changes or supply and demand. The present assessment is therefore best framed as a confirmed profit swing alongside unresolved questions about its persistence.
