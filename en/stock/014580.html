02

Business structure

Taekyung BK is centered on lime manufacturing and sales. By the sales mix compiled by WiseReport, lime accounts for 53.72%, artificial marble for 23.83%, wax for 11.47%, and carbon dioxide gas for 10.98%.

The lime business produces quicklime, slaked lime and calcium hydroxide for steel, chemicals, construction and environmental uses. The company sources its raw material from limestone it mines directly from its own mines, and it stresses cost competitiveness and supply stability.

The company is presented as a leader in the domestic lime market with a share in the 20% range. In 2024 it acquired a 55.58% stake in a subsidiary that added the artificial marble and wax businesses, and that subsidiary is described as the No. 1 synthetic wax maker and No. 3 artificial marble maker.

In petroleum coke, a July 2025 report said the company holds exclusive domestic supply with US-based Oxbow. Its carbon dioxide gas business links to carbon capture and utilization and other eco-friendly technology. The company had 271 employees as of June 30, 2026.

Some sources also list highway rest-stop operations among its revenue sources, and since segment classifications differ across sources, the business segments should be checked against the regular report.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 345.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 294.2 billion in 2023, a 14.7% decline, then rose 4.9% to KRW 308.6 billion in 2024 and 25.1% to KRW 385.9 billion in 2025, marking two straight years of growth from 2024 through 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 31.9 billion in 2022, KRW 40.1 billion in 2023 (up 25.5%), and KRW 42.2 billion in 2024 (up 5.4%), making 2024 the largest year of 2022–2025; it then fell 3.4% to KRW 40.8 billion in 2025. Operating margin rose from 9.3% in 2022 to 13.6% in 2023 and 13.7% in 2024, then fell to 10.6% in 2025.

Revenue grew sharply in 2025 while margins declined, so top-line expansion did not translate into better margins. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 23.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 24 billion in 2023 and KRW 27.3 billion in 2024, then fell 12.6% to KRW 23.9 billion in 2025.

Total net income in 2025 was KRW 32.8 billion, and the gap with the controlling-shareholder figure is the share belonging to non-controlling interests. Operating cash flow was KRW 8.63 billion in 2022, KRW 54.7 billion in 2023, KRW 38.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 64.5 billion in 2025, above operating profit in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 419.9 billion, equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 235.5 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 206.1 billion; the debt ratio rose from 23.6% in 2024 to 49.1% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue ran KRW 102.2 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 102.2 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 103.3 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 119.5 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 116.3 billion in Q3 2026.

Operating profit ran KRW 12.2 billion, KRW 9.41 billion, KRW 10.8 billion, KRW 9.9 billion and KRW 7.6 billion over the same quarters, peaking in Q1 2026 and falling for two consecutive quarters. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 23.4 billion.

Based on the Q3 2026 quarterly report, nine-month 2026 revenue was KRW 339.1 billion, up 19.5%, and operating profit was KRW 28.3 billion, down 9.7%. Whether quarterly profit swings included one-off items should be checked in the quarterly report notes and expense lines.

05

Industry analysis

WiseReport classifies the company under the non-metallic minerals sector on the KOSPI. Lime is a basic material used in steel, chemicals, construction and environmental applications, so demand is not concentrated in one industry.

The company attributes improvement in its lime segment to rising steel demand and demand from pollution-prevention projects. It is presented as holding a share in the 20% range of the domestic lime market, and it says it has the largest production capacity in Korea.

Its carbon dioxide gas business ties into eco-friendly technologies such as greenhouse gas capture and utilization and recycling of waste resources. In 2025 annual data for 25 non-ferrous metals companies, the company ranks first in operating margin and 13th in market capitalization.

In a July 2025 report, US tariff action on Chinese graphite drew attention to the company's exclusive petroleum coke supply deal. In May 2026, a midsize-company executive said that AX (AI transformation) build-out costs weigh on firms competing over cost reductions of 1% to 2%.

In the company's own description, construction is one of several end uses, so results are not tied to a single construction cycle.

Outlook

First-half 2026 consolidated revenue rose 22.8% year on year, operating profit rose 8.0%, and net income rose 30.8%. The artificial marble business grew sharply on the consolidation effect and stronger demand for premium products, and wax benefited from demand for high-performance products.

However, nine-month 2026 operating profit fell 9.7% to KRW 28.3 billion, and Q3 operating profit fell 37.6% year on year, reversing the first-half trend. In lime, steel demand and pollution-prevention demand remain the cited supports.

The company's chief executive has asked for government support for AX investment costs, stressing the need to keep cost competitiveness. Full-year 2026 results will be confirmed in the annual business report due by the end of March 2027.

The key checkpoint for the annual trend is how Q4 2026 operating profit compares with KRW 9.41 billion in Q4 2025. If the March 27, 2026 shareholder meeting is the precedent, the 2027 annual general meeting may also fall around March.

The Q1 2027 quarterly report is due by May 15, 2027, and the next checkpoint is whether the operating profit decline that began in Q2 2026 continues into Q1 2027.

Bull factors

Profitability Among Sector Leaders

Operating margin was 13.6% in 2023 and 13.7% in 2024, and it held at 10.6% in 2025. Against the non-ferrous metals sector on 2025 annual data, the company ranks first on operating margin. Its own limestone mines are cited as support for cost control.

Broader Mix From Acquisition

The company took a 55.58% stake in a subsidiary in 2024 and added artificial marble and wax. First-half 2026 consolidated revenue rose 22.8% year on year. Artificial marble sales are described as growing sharply on the consolidation effect and premium demand. A larger non-lime share spreads earnings exposure across more businesses.

Lime Leadership and Demand Base

The company is presented as holding a share in the 20% range of the domestic lime market. It credits growing steel and pollution-prevention demand, along with a stable supply system, for improved lime results. Its dividend yield is above the sector average on 2025 data.

Bear factors

Consecutive Quarterly Profit Declines

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 7.6 billion, down 37.6% year on year, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.15 billion, down 57.8%. Operating profit has fallen for two straight quarters since the Q1 2026 peak. Nine-month operating profit fell 9.7%.

Annual Profit Decline and Revenue Gap

Operating profit fell 3.4% to KRW 40.8 billion in 2025, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 12.6% to KRW 23.9 billion. 2024 was the largest year for both operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income in 2022–2025. Revenue rose 25.1% but profit did not keep pace, so margins declined.

Higher Financial Leverage

The debt ratio rose from 23.6% in 2024 to 49.1% in 2025. Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 206.1 billion, and equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 235.5 billion of total equity of KRW 419.9 billion. How the filings explain the borrowing increase determines how the financial burden should be read.

Risk factors

Demand Concentration

The company attributes lime segment gains to rising steel and pollution-prevention demand. A slowdown in that demand could affect lime volumes and prices. Whether the Q3 2026 profit decline came from lime or from artificial marble and wax should be checked against segment-level quarterly data.

Balance Sheet and Ownership

The debt ratio was 49.1% in 2025, up from 23.6% in 2024. Filings list Taekyung Industrial as the largest shareholder of Taekyung BK with a 43.58% stake, a structure in which the controlling holder's influence is large. Because non-controlling interests are sizable, controlling-shareholder profit is smaller than total net income.

Cost and Trade Policy

Midsize companies compete over cost reductions of 1% to 2%, and AX build-out and workforce training are cited as hurdles. The petroleum coke business holds an exclusive domestic supply arrangement, but trade policy shifts, such as the US tariff action on Chinese graphite, can change demand and market attention.

Overall view

Taekyung BK is built on lime manufacturing with artificial marble and wax added through acquisition. Its 2025 revenue of KRW 385.9 billion was the largest of 2022–2025, but operating margin fell from 13.7% in 2024 to 10.6% in 2025.

In first-half 2026, the consolidation of acquired businesses lifted revenue and operating profit. Operating profit, however, has declined for two straight quarters since Q2 2026, and Q3 operating profit fell 37.6% year on year. On the balance sheet, the debt ratio rose to 49.1% in 2025.

Against the sector, the multiple on earnings and book value is lower and the dividend yield is higher, but the earnings trend changes how those multiples should be read.
