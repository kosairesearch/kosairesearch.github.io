02

Business structure

Aiobio is an oral healthcare company founded in August 2011 and listed on the KONEX market in December 2022. It was originally incorporated under a different name and adopted its current name in March 2017. Its core products are fluorescence-based oral screening devices, including QrayCam Pro, Qraypen C and Qscan Plus.

The technology images tooth autofluorescence and the fluorescence of bacterial metabolites using specific light wavelengths, letting clinicians and patients see biofilm, plaque and tooth condition intuitively.

The LinkDens software links imaging, record-keeping, comparison, patient explanation and preventive care, and a domestic pilot service started in June 2024.

In Korea, the quantitative light-induced fluorescence caries test received national health insurance coverage in May 2021, and coverage was widened in February 2025 to patients aged 15 and under, once every three months.

Market data classify the stock as electrical equipment, but the actual business is medical devices, so demand drivers and competition do not overlap with power-equipment listings such as LS Electric or Hyosung Heavy Industries.

A breakdown of revenue by product or service could not be confirmed in the available filings, so it is treated qualitatively. Overseas distribution runs through AIOBIO USA, a wholly owned subsidiary established in October 2023 to distribute the company's medical devices and medical AI software in the US.

Market-share data for competitors could not be confirmed, and the company emphasizes its insurance-reimbursement track record and overseas clearances as differentiators.

04

Outlook

On 2026-08-25 the company announced the start of sales through Patterson Dental's official US network and said it would commercialize in China, Ireland and the UK, and Japan in sequence.

In China, four fluorescence diagnostic devices were registered as Class I medical devices with the NMPA and a first shipment was completed in early September 2026.

Under the company's plan, sales in Ireland and the UK start in October, and exports to Japan start in November under a contract with the local distributor FEED. The actual pace of adoption and the timing of revenue recognition will need to be confirmed through future filings.

The gap between past US targets and results is another variable. In September 2024 the CEO outlined a plan for KRW 3 billion of US sales in 2025, whereas the annual report shows AIOBIO USA's 2025 revenue at KRW 129,746 thousand. A KOSDAQ transfer listing is also under way.

The company appointed Kiwoom Securities as listing underwriter in September 2025, and management said it intends to pursue a technology special listing route. The review schedule and outcome could not be confirmed.

On market size, Fortune Business Insights estimated the US caries diagnostic device market at about USD 116.9 million in 2022, with a forecast CAGR of 6.2% for 2023 to 2030. This figure was cited in 2024 press coverage, and a more recent value could not be confirmed.

05

Bull factors

Insurance coverage and overseas clearances

In Korea, the fluorescence-based caries test is covered by national health insurance, which gives dental clinics an institutional basis for adoption. In the US, FDA Class II clearance in September 2024 set up the conditions to use the non-radiographic dental diagnostic billing code D0600.

Together with China's NMPA Class I registration and Taiwan's Class II clearance, the company has cleared part of the entry barriers in several markets. Clearance does not equal revenue, however, so it still has to show up in sales.

Geographic widening of distribution

The company has entered the network of the large US dental distributor Patterson Dental, and in China it has followed up with training programs and a first shipment. In November 2025 it secured clearances and signed distribution partners in China, Vietnam and Australia in quick succession.

The company said training for Chinese dentists and distribution partners would continue on a regular basis in November and December. A distributor-led model can ease the sales-staffing burden, which leaves room for improvement in operating metrics.

KOSDAQ transfer plan and balance-sheet repair

The company appointed Kiwoom Securities as listing underwriter in September 2025 and is pursuing a KOSDAQ transfer listing. In the annual report, management said it would strengthen strategic financial management, including encouraging conversion of convertible bonds into equity.

The conversion of preferred shares into common shares in June 2026 is one part of the changing capital structure. If carried out, such plans could ease the financial burden, but their timing and completion have not been confirmed.

Bear factors

Negative total equity

Total equity at the end of 2025 was negative KRW 6,109 million, with total liabilities of KRW 8,622 million far above total assets of KRW 2,513 million. Interest expense rose to KRW 676,713,888, up sharply from the prior year, and weighed on earnings.

Without capital increases or conversions, financial stability is hard to improve. Financial requirements could also weigh on the KOSDAQ transfer review.

Cash burn and reliance on outside funding

Operating cash flow in 2025 was negative KRW 3,474,778,225, and year-end cash was only KRW 69,628,843. The shortfall was covered by KRW 4,000,000,000 of convertible bonds and KRW 1,062,610,500 of new equity, among other sources.

If revenue does not grow with wider distribution, further funding may be needed, which could dilute existing shareholders. The March 2026 shareholder meeting also approved 240,000 stock options for 36 grantees.

Unproven overseas results and thin trading

Aiobio's sales to its US subsidiary were KRW 676,379 thousand, but AIOBIO USA's own revenue was KRW 129,746 thousand, so end demand there remains small. The subsidiary posted a net loss of KRW 662,575 thousand, and an equity-method loss was also recorded.

With a market capitalization of KRW 5.9 billion and displayed turnover of only KRW 40,308, trade execution and price formation can be unstable. The share price moved from KRW 3,550 on 2026-03-31 to KRW 2,130 on 2026-10-27.

07

Risk factors

Macro

With a heavy reliance on borrowings and convertible bonds, the company is sensitive to interest rates and credit conditions. As overseas sales grow, won exchange-rate swings can affect earnings. Dental equipment depends on clinics' capital-spending capacity, so adoption can slow in downturns. Small KONEX companies can see funding conditions deteriorate faster when risk appetite weakens.

Policy and regulatory

Domestic sales are affected by the scope of national health insurance coverage and fee policy. Coverage was widened in February 2025, which was favorable, but future changes to the criteria cannot be ruled out.

Overseas, medical device clearance and billing systems differ by country, so it can take time before sales begin. The KOSDAQ transfer listing could also shift in timing depending on the exchange's review outcome.

Company and market structure

Because total equity is negative, failure to raise additional funds could affect business continuity. Conversion of preferred shares and convertible bonds into common stock, and the exercise of stock options, would increase the share count and could dilute existing holders.

The KONEX market has limited participants, so order-book gaps and abrupt price moves are common. Heavy dependence on a subsidiary and local distributors for overseas sales is also a point to monitor.

08

Overall view

Market data classify Aiobio as electrical equipment, but it is a medical device company making fluorescence-based oral screening equipment and software.

Domestic insurance coverage, FDA Class II clearance in the US, NMPA registration in China and entry into Patterson Dental's US network are positive facts for its business foundation.

At the same time, an operating loss of KRW 3,746 million against 2025 revenue of KRW 3,427 million, total equity of negative KRW 6,109 million and year-end cash of KRW 69,628,843 show a heavy financial burden.

Whether the overseas network translates into actual revenue and profit has to be verified in upcoming quarterly and half-year filings. The KOSDAQ transfer plan and the intended conversion of convertible bonds are variables for the capital structure, and their timing is not confirmed.

The extremely thin trading typical of a KONEX stock should also be considered, and this report is for information only, leaving any judgment to the reader.
