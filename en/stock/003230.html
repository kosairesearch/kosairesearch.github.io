Report overview

Overseas Sales at 83.5% as Buldak Drives Profit Growth

Samyang Foods posted an 83.5% overseas sales share and a 24.8% operating margin in the third quarter of 2026, yet net income attributable to controlling shareholders has fallen for two straight quarters since its first-quarter peak, so operating and net profit trends need to be read together.

  1. 1

    On a filed basis, annual operating profit rose from KRW 90.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 524.2 billion in 2025, and the operating margin climbed from 9.9% to 22.3% over the same span.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 144.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 137.2 billion in the second and KRW 89.8 billion in the third, down 18.5% from a year earlier in the third quarter.

02

Business structure

Samyang Foods is a noodle and snack company, and its Buldak brand drives most of its results. In the first half of 2026, the noodle and snack segment generated KRW 1.34 trillion, or 90.6% of consolidated revenue, and exports of KRW 1.18 trillion were about seven times domestic sales of KRW 161.7 billion.

Overseas sales in the third quarter of 2026 reached KRW 660.8 billion, or 83.5% of consolidated revenue, while domestic sales were KRW 131.1 billion, up 7.9% from a year earlier.

By region, the U.S. subsidiary booked KRW 212.5 billion, up 36.6%, supported by mainstream retail channels such as Walmart and shipments to Mexico.

The China subsidiary posted KRW 208.9 billion, up 13.2%, as snack channels expanded, and the European subsidiary grew 65.5% to KRW 86.9 billion, the fastest rate among the regions. Buldak brand cumulative sales passed 10 billion units at the end of May 2026.

Production is centered on domestic plants such as the Miryang plant, which supply instant noodles to the U.S., China and Europe.

A July 2026 report said Nongshim and CJ CheilJedang raised product prices on raw material, labor and logistics costs, while Samyang Foods kept domestic prices unchanged and aims to protect profitability through overseas sales growth and production efficiency.

Because overseas sales make up such a large share, securing local retail channels is the key variable in the business structure.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 909 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.19 trillion in 2023 (up 31.2%), KRW 1.73 trillion in 2024 (up 44.9%) and KRW 2.35 trillion in 2025 (up 36.1%), extending growth for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Operating profit grew from KRW 90.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 524.2 billion in 2025, with the 2024 gain of 133.6% to KRW 344.6 billion the fastest of the three years, and the operating margin rose from 9.9% to 22.3%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders climbed from KRW 79.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 389.4 billion in 2025, while net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 388.7 billion in 2025, a different figure.

Operating cash flow rose to KRW 357.9 billion in 2024 before easing to KRW 309.3 billion in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 103.4% in 2022 to 72.7% in 2025.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 1.27 trillion, of which KRW 1.26 trillion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 925 billion.

Quarterly revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from KRW 632 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 791.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026, with the latest quarter up 25.3% from a year earlier.

Operating profit went from KRW 130.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 177.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, dipped to KRW 176.2 billion in the second quarter (down 0.5% on the quarter), and reached KRW 196.2 billion in the third quarter, up 49.9% from a year earlier and about 5% above the KRW 187.1 billion consensus compiled by FnGuide.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 144.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then fell to KRW 137.2 billion and KRW 89.8 billion, down 18.5% from a year earlier in the third quarter, with the last four quarters totaling KRW 467.2 billion.

The divergence between rising operating profit and falling controlling-shareholder net income should be checked against non-operating items and income tax lines. The company attributed operating profit growth to wider global distribution and better operating efficiency at domestic plants including Miryang.

05

Industry analysis

A July 2026 report said Nongshim and CJ CheilJedang raised prices on raw material, labor and logistics costs, while Samyang Foods held domestic prices and is leaning on overseas sales growth and production efficiency to protect margins.

Because overseas sales make up most of revenue, currency moves feed directly into results. The company said a weaker won helped profitability in the second quarter of 2026, while another report pointed to a stronger won and higher global cost burdens as external uncertainties.

Demand in the Americas is supported by retail channel expansion: the region booked KRW 203.6 billion in the second quarter of 2026, up 54% from a year earlier. China's snack-channel expansion lifted sales to KRW 181.0 billion, up 44%.

On the supply side, production capacity had been the constraint on overseas demand, and the expansion of the second Miryang plant and local production in China are easing it. The shift from exporting from Korea to producing near key markets is changing the industry structure.

Regional production should reduce logistics and tariff exposure. Growth is tied to overseas channels, so domestic market dependence is low, but a call to strengthen domestic competitiveness has also been raised.

06

Outlook

Brokerages expect 2026 annual revenue to rise 32.9% from a year earlier to KRW 3.12 trillion. A May 2026 report said the full operation of the second Miryang plant increased supply and contributed to growth.

The Jiaxing plant in China targets operation in 2027, handling domestic Chinese demand and freeing some export volume from Korea for other markets such as the U.S.

The Vietnam plant carries a total investment of KRW 218.6 billion and targets completion in January 2029, with up to eight production lines; the company will invest KRW 131.1 billion in the local production unit.

Initially one dry-noodle line will be built, and the timing of seven additional instant-noodle lines has not been set. Brokerages estimate global annual capacity could reach up to 4.9 billion servings once all eight lines are in place, with about 3.9 billion servings at the early operating stage in 2029.

Until the Vietnam plant is completed, major export volumes to Europe and the U.S. will depend on domestic plants. In a July 2026 report, the company said it would respond to cost pressure through production efficiency and channel mix rather than further price increases.

The next checkpoints are the timing of the Jiaxing plant start-up in 2027, the overseas sales share in the 2026 annual report, and whether net income attributable to controlling shareholders recovers.

08

Bull factors

Broad-based overseas demand growth

Third-quarter 2026 overseas sales reached KRW 660.8 billion, up 29.4% from a year earlier, and the U.S., China and Europe subsidiaries each grew by at least double digits. Channel expansion into mainstream retail and snack channels is visible.

Margin defense and operating profit growth

The operating margin stayed in the 20% range for seven straight quarters, at 24.8% in the third quarter of 2026. Third-quarter operating profit rose 49.9% from a year earlier, outpacing the 25.3% revenue increase. The company said channel mix improvement and production efficiency supported margins.

Capacity expansion schedule

After the expansion of the second Miryang plant, the Jiaxing plant in China targets operation in 2027. The Vietnam plant is planned for up to eight lines with completion targeted for January 2029. Brokerages expect global annual capacity of up to 4.9 billion servings once all eight lines are in place.

Bear factors

Heavy overseas dependence and supply chain concentration

Overseas sales reached 83.5% of revenue in the third quarter of 2026, so logistics disruptions, tariff changes and geopolitical tensions could affect supply. Until the Vietnam plant is completed, volumes to Europe and the U.S. depend on domestic plants.

Strengthening domestic competitiveness is also flagged as a task given the higher dependence on overseas markets.

Controlling-shareholder net income moving in the opposite direction

Operating profit rose to KRW 196.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026, yet net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 18.5% from a year earlier to KRW 89.8 billion. It peaked at KRW 144.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then declined in both the second and third quarters. Whether non-operating items and income tax changes explain the gap needs to be confirmed in the annual report.

Currency swings and slower cash flow

One report cited a stronger won and higher global cost burdens as external uncertainties. Operating cash flow declined from KRW 357.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 309.3 billion in 2025. With overseas plant investment continuing, cash generation needs to be watched alongside earnings.

Risk factors

Overseas concentration and supply chain

With overseas sales above 80% of revenue, concentrating production in a few countries could expose supply to logistics disruptions, tariff changes and geopolitical conflict. The company said it will spread risk across production bases in Korea, China and Vietnam. The Vietnam plant is targeted for completion in January 2029, so concentration risk remains until then.

Currency and raw material costs

A third-quarter 2026 report cited a stronger won and global cost burdens as external uncertainties. An August 2026 report said the company attributed part of its profitability gains to a weaker won. Profit swings could widen depending on the direction of the exchange rate.

Investment burden and timing of added capacity

The Vietnam plant requires a total investment of KRW 218.6 billion, and the equity injection into the local production unit is KRW 131.1 billion. The timing of the seven additional instant-noodle lines has not been fixed. As plant spending grows, cash flow and debt management become more important.

Overall view

Samyang Foods grew revenue, operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income every year from 2022 to 2025, and the operating margin rose from 9.9% to 22.3%.

In the third quarter of 2026 revenue reached KRW 791.8 billion and operating profit KRW 196.2 billion, which the company and media described as the quarterly record, with overseas sales at 83.5% of revenue. The U.S., China and European subsidiaries led overseas growth, supported by rising Buldak brand sales.

Controlling-shareholder net income, however, declined in the second and third quarters after peaking in the first quarter of 2026, so whether operating gains carry through to net income still needs confirmation.

Capacity expansion continues, with the Jiaxing plant in China targeting 2027 operation and the Vietnam plant targeting completion in January 2029. An overseas sales share above 80% increases exposure to currency, logistics, tariff and geopolitical variables.

Valuation sits in a range where market capitalization is well above net assets, so the link between earnings trends and multiples is a key area to watch.

The next checkpoints are the non-operating and income tax lines in the 2026 annual report, the Jiaxing start-up timing in 2027, and whether additional Vietnam lines are approved.
