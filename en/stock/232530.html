02

Business structure

Energy Material Technology was founded in February 2010 and operates its head office and plant in Chungju, North Chungcheong Province. Its annual report lists the manufacture and sale of cathode active material precursors, a secondary battery material, as the main business.

The shares have been listed on Konex since December 30, 2015. The market classifies the company under machinery and equipment, yet the actual business sits in battery materials.

The core product is NCM (nickel-cobalt-manganese) precursor for electric vehicles and energy storage systems, with annual capacity of 6,120 tons.

In a 2025 report, the Korea Investor Relations Service said the line was converted from continuous to batch operation, which supports impurity control and stable high-purity output.

Kiwoom Securities earlier cited the company's technology for regenerating precursor raw material from scrap cathode material as the basis of its cost competitiveness. The ownership structure changed in 2026.

On March 20, 2026, the largest shareholder changed from Jiangsu-affiliated parent Jaese Energy to a newly spun-off entity, which inherited a 72.44% stake. The competitive landscape mixes large chemical and cathode-linked producers.

A 2017 report named Sumitomo Chemical, L&F's subsidiary JH Chemical and Ecopro among the main precursor players. Because precursor is the single product line, there is no meaningful split of revenue by segment.

03

Recent trends

According to FnGuide, revenue rose from KRW 9.1 billion in 2023 and KRW 9.8 billion in 2024 to KRW 36.4 billion in 2025, and operating results moved from a KRW 1.0 billion loss in 2024 to a KRW 4.9 billion profit in 2025.

The standalone statements in the shareholder-meeting disclosure show 2025 revenue of KRW 36,396 million, operating profit of KRW 4,871 million and net profit of KRW 2,683 million.

The same filing reports total assets of KRW 78,428 million, total liabilities of KRW 77,010 million, capital stock of KRW 12,807 million and total equity of KRW 1,418 million. The March 27, 2026 shareholder meeting also approved a change of fiscal year-end month. Later disclosures accumulate figures from April 1, which suggests a move to a March year-end.

Media reports put revenue for the first quarter of 2026 at KRW 22.2 billion and operating profit at KRW 3.7 billion. In an internal-settlement disclosure on standalone basis dated October 14, 2026, the first quarter of the new fiscal year showed revenue of KRW 38,591 million and operating profit of KRW 4,770 million.

The second quarter (July 1 to September 30) recorded revenue of KRW 68,710 million and operating profit of KRW 1,120 million, and the cumulative figures since April 1 are revenue of KRW 107,302 million and operating profit of KRW 5,890 million.

Revenue grew, but the operating margin dropped visibly between the two quarters, and the disclosure does not explain why.

In July 2026 trading was halted because the company missed the deadline for its first annual and audit reports, then resumed after filing before the grace period ended. The share price has been volatile. The 52-week range runs from KRW 2,960 to KRW 15,900. The high of KRW 15,900 was set on May 8, 2026.

As of September 30, 2026, the change was -7.02% over three months, +11.99% over six months and +178.19% over twelve months. The price at the time of writing is KRW 10,000.

04

Outlook

The near-term question is whether higher revenue translates into profit. Because second-quarter operating profit fell sharply from the prior quarter, the next disclosures should show whether raw material sourcing, product mix or selling prices drove the change.

There is still room for improvement in operating indicators such as utilization. The Korea Investor Relations Service noted that the production base could support KRW 120–130 billion of annual revenue at full operation.

Cumulative revenue from April 1 to September 30, 2026 was KRW 107,302 million, so the half-year pace is moving toward that range.

Product mix toward high-nickel grades is a variable. A 2025 report said the company began exporting precursor with over 90% nickel content to Japan and set a second-half revenue target of KRW 60 billion. Since full-year 2025 revenue was KRW 36,396 million, there was a gap between that target and the actual result.

The same research body argued that the manufacturing technology could also apply to next-generation batteries such as lithium-sulfur, lithium-metal and solid-state.

On industry demand, another domestic precursor supplier disclosed a high-nickel NCM precursor order dated October 26, 2026, which shows that demand for this grade continues. In terms of market structure, no company has moved from Konex to KOSDAQ so far in 2026.

A change of trading venue is therefore not a confirmed development for this company. Future regular settlements will follow the revised fiscal year ending in March, and the quarterly disclosures remain the main reference for tracking results.

05

Bull factors

Turnaround and Larger Scale

According to FnGuide, standalone revenue in 2025 rose 269.6% from the prior year, and both operating profit and net profit turned positive. Cumulative disclosed revenue since April 1, 2026 is KRW 107,302 million, which already exceeds full-year 2025 revenue of KRW 36,396 million within half a year.

Operating profit also stayed positive in both quarters of the new fiscal year. The faster scale-up spreads fixed costs over a larger base.

Process Technology and Cost Structure

The company holds technology to recover precursor raw material from scrap cathode material and its own particle-size control process, and it runs a batch production system. It has also obtained ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and IATF 16949 certifications.

Kiwoom Securities earlier assessed that the use of recycled raw material supports cost competitiveness against peers. That view comes from an older report, so whether it shows up in recent quarterly margins needs separate checking.

Capacity Headroom and Vertical Linkage

The Korea Investor Relations Service said capacity of roughly 6,000 tons a year leaves room to raise output if demand grows. This points to scope for improvement in operating indicators such as utilization and operating margin.

The largest shareholder belongs to a cathode material group, and an earlier report described a vertical supply relationship between precursor and cathode. The exact share of related-party transactions was not confirmed in this research.

06

Bear factors

Sharp Profit Drop Despite Higher Sales

Standalone second-quarter operating profit was KRW 1,120 million, down 76.52% from KRW 4,770 million in the prior quarter. Revenue grew from KRW 38,591 million to KRW 68,710 million over the same period, so the operating margin fell sharply.

The disclosure gives no cause, which makes it hard to tell whether the drop is temporary or structural. The figures are also unaudited internal settlements.

Thin Equity and High Leverage

At the end of 2025, standalone capital stock was KRW 12,807 million, but total equity was KRW 1,418 million and total liabilities were KRW 77,010 million. FnGuide put the debt ratio at 5,428.94%. Equity is far below capital stock, so the capital base is partly eroded, and it was negative at the end of 2024. With this structure, earnings swings have an outsized effect on equity.

Thin Trading and Large Price Swings

Trading value at the time of writing was only KRW 5,732,820. Investing.com puts average daily volume at 764 shares. The 52-week high of KRW 15,900 is more than five times the low of KRW 2,960. Small orders can move the price, which weakens execution and price formation.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Precursor makers' earnings are generally affected by demand for electric vehicles and energy storage, metal prices such as nickel, and exchange rates. The company has tried to export to Japan and other overseas buyers, so it is exposed to currency moves and order swings from foreign customers.

The gap between the 2025 second-half revenue target and the actual full-year result shows the uncertainty in demand and sales planning. How metal price changes pass through to selling prices and costs was not confirmed in this research.

The company could not finish submitting the materials needed to form an audit opinion and asked for more time, and the external auditor could not complete the required procedures because of missing materials. Reports said trading would resume once the filings were completed.

The episode raises the need to check the internal closing process and audit readiness. The change in fiscal year-end month and the change of largest shareholder occurred around the same time, which is another reason to read the filings closely.

Konex-listed companies have a limited pool of participants, so gaps in bid and ask quotes are common. With the largest shareholder holding 72.44%, the number of shares that actually trade is limited. On June 24, 2026 the stock rose 14.29% in a single day, and on the prior session it fell 9.09%. Swings of that size can occur on small trading volumes alone.

08

Overall view

Energy Material Technology is a Konex-listed NCM precursor producer with a confirmed turnaround in 2025 and a sharp revenue increase since April 2026. At the same time, second-quarter operating profit fell steeply from the prior quarter, so scale and profitability are moving in different directions.

The thin equity base, the large liabilities and the delayed audit report filing in July 2026 are items to monitor. The Korea Investor Relations Service assessed that the production base could support KRW 120–130 billion of revenue at full annual operation, which speaks to room for better utilization.

The share price ranged between KRW 2,960 and KRW 15,900 over 52 weeks and average trading is very small, so price volatility and liquidity constraints are significant. Because the fiscal year-end change alters the comparison basis, upcoming quarterly disclosures will matter for checking margins and the balance sheet. This report is for information only, and the judgment is left to the reader.
