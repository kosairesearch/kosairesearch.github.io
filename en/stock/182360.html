02

Business structure

Cube Entertainment earns revenue mainly from album, music, concert, and advertising activities of its artists, including the group i-dle and the third-year group NOWZ. The company's revenue lines are reported as records, music, CF, events, broadcasting, content, merchandise, and other.

Among these, records, events, and content swing sharply from quarter to quarter and are the main source of earnings volatility. For the 2025 decline, the company cited fewer full-group album releases and world tours by i-dle compared with the prior year.

It also said that higher content production and marketing costs tied to NOWZ added to the drop in revenue and profit. This shows a structure in which revenue moves sharply with the activity cycles of its leading groups.

In 2026, i-dle continued its fourth world tour, Syncopation, from February, and its Taipei show was held at Taipei Dome, a first for a K-pop girl group. The mini album We made was announced for release on July 6, 2026, and appearances at Lollapalooza on July 31 and a North American tour from August were also announced.

Compared with large agencies such as HYBE, SM Entertainment, JYP Ent., and YG Entertainment, the company's revenue depends more heavily on the activity of a few leading artists.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 120.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 142.3 billion in 2023, up 17.9% year on year, and then to KRW 218.3 billion in 2024, up 53.4%. 2024 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span. Revenue fell 60.0% to KRW 87.2 billion in 2025, which was the smallest year in the span.

Operating profit reached KRW 15.4 billion in 2023, up 122.6%, and KRW 16.6 billion in 2024, up 7.4%, with operating margins of 10.8% and 7.6%. In 2025, the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 7.26 billion and an operating margin of -8.3%, turning from profit to loss.

Controlling-shareholder net income went from KRW 16 billion in 2024 to a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 5.19 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests, a separate figure, was a net loss of KRW 5.86 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow ranged widely, at KRW 41.4 billion in 2022, KRW 3.84 billion in 2023, KRW 2.31 billion in 2024, and KRW 42.7 billion in 2025, so cash flow and profit moved in opposite directions in 2025.

Quarterly, 3Q 2025 revenue of KRW 50.1 billion came with an operating loss of KRW 5.24 billion, and the 4Q 2025 operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.99 billion. 1Q 2026 revenue of KRW 15.1 billion came with an operating loss of KRW 5.18 billion, the low point of the series.

In 2Q 2026, revenue rose to KRW 21.5 billion, up 42.7% from the prior quarter, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 2.04 billion. In 3Q 2026, revenue reached KRW 32.7 billion, up 51.7% from the prior quarter and down 34.8% from a year earlier, and operating profit of KRW 4.29 billion swung to profit.

Controlling-shareholder net income in 3Q 2026 was KRW 3.75 billion. Over the last four quarters, cumulative controlling-shareholder net income was only KRW 963 million.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 169.5% in 2022 to 60.5% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 160.9 billion and total liabilities of KRW 97.4 billion at the end of 2025.

Industry analysis

Demand in the sector rests on the interplay of domestic and overseas music charts, overseas tours and festivals, and advertising and content production. Cube Entertainment's results move from quarter to quarter with the intensity of its artists' activities.

The company's main explanation for the 2025 decline, fewer full-group activities, shows that the activity cycle of its artists was the key variable.

The digital single Mono (Feat. skaiwater), released in January 2026, was reported to have reached the top ranks of major domestic music sites and China's QQ Music and iTunes charts. Overseas activity continued through a world tour that began in February 2026 and moved from Seoul to Taipei and Bangkok.

Among competitors, large agencies such as HYBE, SM Entertainment, JYP Ent., and YG Entertainment have more artists and more business lines, so there is a clear gap in scale.

Within the same sector, Cube Entertainment's quarterly results tend to reflect the performance of a few groups quickly, which makes its earnings more volatile. This difference is better read as a feature of the company's revenue mix than as a feature of the whole industry.

How much of the second-half 2026 overseas schedule showed up in revenue can be checked in the segment discussion of the next annual report.

Expanding overseas activity

Bear factors

Earnings base still small

Cumulative controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters was only KRW 963 million. 1Q 2026 posted an operating loss of KRW 5.18 billion, and 3Q 2026 revenue was down 34.8% year on year. The return to profit has so far lasted only one quarter, so earnings continuity is not yet established.

Dependence on activity cycles

Revenue fell 60.0% in 2025, and the company attributed the drop mainly to fewer full-group albums and world tours. Higher production and marketing costs for NOWZ compounded the profit decline. When leading groups pause, revenue and profit both swing, which is a structural exposure.

Cash flow and profit moving apart

Operating cash flow was KRW 3.84 billion in 2023 and KRW 2.31 billion in 2024, then jumped to KRW 42.7 billion in 2025. In 2025, cash inflow was large in a year when the net loss was KRW 5.86 billion. If cash flow and profit keep diverging, more evidence will be needed to judge earnings quality.

10

Risk factors

Artist activity risk

Revenue depends heavily on album releases and tour schedules of the company's groups. A 60.0% drop in 2025 followed a reduction in full-group activity. If schedules slip or group activity narrows, quarterly results could swing again.

Cost structure risk

Higher content production and marketing costs for NOWZ significantly reduced profit in 2025. Fixed costs tied to developing and promoting artists do not fall easily when revenue drops. Whether the 3Q 2026 profit reflected cost control or revenue growth is a question for the quarterly breakdown.

Overseas schedule and environment risk

Overseas schedules such as the North American tour and Lollapalooza depend on local conditions and ticket demand. Because the world tour runs region by region, a change in one region could affect annual results. How overseas activity is reflected in disclosed results should be confirmed in the annual report.

Overall view

Cube Entertainment has seen large swings in revenue and earnings since 2022. Revenue peaked at KRW 218.3 billion in 2024, fell to KRW 87.2 billion in 2025, and the company swung to an operating loss and a controlling-shareholder net loss.

Since the 1Q 2026 low, revenue has risen for two consecutive quarters, and 3Q 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.29 billion marked a return to profit. On the financial side, the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 169.5% to 60.5%, and total equity stood at KRW 160.9 billion.

On the other side, cumulative controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters was only KRW 963 million, so the earnings base remains thin. Heavy dependence on the activity of its artists remains the main source of earnings volatility.

Strengths and weaknesses coexist, so the key checks are whether the profit run and revenue recovery continue in the 4Q 2026 provisional results and the 2026 annual report.
