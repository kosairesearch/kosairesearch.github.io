Profit holds as sales shrink; housing cycle is the swing factor

Sales have fallen for three straight years while operating profit has stayed in the black, so whether housing transactions and construction recover enough to restore top-line growth is the central question.

02

Business structure

Hanssem is a home interior and furniture company whose business divides into B2C (direct sales to households) and B2B (business-to-business). The B2C Rehouse segment covers core spaces such as kitchens and bathrooms, and its sales rose 13% year on year in the second quarter of 2026.

Sales of premium kitchen products grew 40% in the same quarter, according to a brokerage's review. In home furnishing, sales of the Signature storage furniture line rose 12% and the Swivel sofa series rose 46%, with higher-grade products driving growth.

Online, the company restructured Hanssem Mall around its own brands, and first-half 2026 sales there rose 20% year on year, while offline it is closing inefficient stores and concentrating on key commercial districts.

The B2B segment centers on residential contract supply such as apartment kitchens and storage furniture, and B2B sales fell 29% year on year in the same quarter as the construction slowdown deepened.

The merger that absorbed the subsidiary Hanssem Nexus, completed on July 31, 2026, combined its mid-to-high-end interior expertise with Hanssem's nationwide sales and installation network, targeting redevelopment complexes in central Seoul, residences and office furniture.

The company positions a one-stop offering covering design, sourcing of imported building materials and furniture, installation and after-sales service as a core competitive point in bidding. On costs, the company identified cost pressure in B2C as a factor that weighed on third-quarter profitability.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales were KRW 2 trillion in 2022, KRW 1.97 trillion in 2023 (down 1.7% year on year), KRW 1.91 trillion in 2024 (down 3.0%) and KRW 1.74 trillion in 2025 (down 8.6%), falling for three consecutive years. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 21.7 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 1.95 billion in 2023, a swing back into the black, and then rose to KRW 31.2 billion in 2024, up 1,504.3% year on year.

Operating profit fell 40.8% to KRW 18.5 billion in 2025, and the operating margin slipped from 1.6% in 2024 to 1.1%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from a loss of KRW 71.3 billion in 2022 and a loss of KRW 62.2 billion in 2023, where the deficit narrowed, to a profit of KRW 151.1 billion in 2024, and then fell 69.4% to KRW 46.3 billion in 2025.

The 2024 controlling-shareholder profit well exceeded operating profit, which suggests non-operating items played a large role. Net income including non-controlling interests matched the controlling-shareholder figure at KRW 46.3 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 146.9% in 2022 to 206.8% in 2023 and 204.4% in 2024, then eased to 148.4% in 2025, when total liabilities were KRW 593.2 billion and total equity KRW 399.8 billion.

Operating cash flow was negative KRW 21.3 billion in 2022 and then positive at KRW 80.4 billion in 2023, KRW 48.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 69.4 billion in 2025.

By quarter, sales were KRW 441.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 6.84 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 400.3 billion with KRW 2.95 billion in the fourth quarter, down 56.9% from the prior quarter, before rebounding to KRW 399.4 billion and KRW 10.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, up 241.3% from the prior quarter.

Sales were KRW 417.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026 with operating profit of KRW 11.3 billion, and fell to KRW 357.6 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.61 billion in the third quarter, down 76.5% year on year.

In its November 9, 2026 preliminary release, the company cited lower housing transactions, raw material cost pressure from the Middle East, one-off merger costs and consumer purchases shifted by the September holiday and October promotions as reasons for the third-quarter decline.

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was about KRW 23 billion, up roughly 48% year on year, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 15.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The demand floor for furniture and interiors is housing transactions. Apartment sales volume rose 22.6% year on year in the first quarter of 2026, but in the third quarter higher interest rates and lending curbs reduced housing transactions.

Shinhan Investment Corp. said in August 2026 that tighter lending rules and tax changes would slow transactions in the Seoul metropolitan area. Conversely, Mirae Asset Securities said in July 2026 that the expiry of the surcharge on multi-home sellers' capital gains tax would keep housing sales transactions healthy.

Hyundai Motor Securities analyzed that B2B sales lag construction starts by about two years and lead completions and occupancy by about two quarters.

According to Hyundai Motor Securities, completed construction volume fell 41.4% year on year on a cumulative basis through July 2026, and was expected to stay weak for the next three years.

Samsung Securities said in August 2026 that fewer housing transactions and fewer move-in units would make the environment unfavorable for both B2C and B2B for some time. On materials, analysts see vendors under pressure to raise unit prices as raw material costs rise.

The furniture industry as a whole is facing greater difficulty from weaker housing transactions and rising raw material costs.

Within this, Hanssem has chosen to protect profitability by expanding higher-value product mix, which has been read as a shift toward profitability-focused restructuring rather than expansion of sales.

06

Outlook

For the fourth quarter, the company said it will convert demand shifted from the third quarter into sales through the Ssam Festa promotion and a new-school-year student room campaign.

The company expects the organizational integration and cost efficiencies from the Hyundai-related merger to appear from the fourth quarter.

Hyundai Motor Securities raised its 2026 operating profit estimate from KRW 35 billion to KRW 37 billion in September 2026, while lowering its 2026 sales estimate to KRW 1.657 trillion. The same brokerage also cut its 2027 sales estimate by 8.3% to KRW 1.714 trillion.

Shinhan Investment Corp. lowered its 2027 operating profit estimate by 21.5% in August 2026. Samsung Securities said in August 2026 that a recovery in second-half results is likely to slow because of merger costs and stronger marketing spending.

On shareholder returns, the company set a target return ratio and resolved in June 2026 to buy back KRW 50 billion of treasury shares. Mirae Asset Securities said the key question is whether treasury shares will be cancelled, and that buybacks could raise the treasury share ratio from 29.5% to 35.0%.

The deadline for cancellation is September 2027. The next checkpoint is how the Ssam Festa carryover demand and merger synergies are reflected in sales and operating profit in the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results.

08

Bull factors

Higher-value mix defends margins

Premium kitchen sales rose 40% year on year in the second quarter of 2026, and the Swivel sofa series grew 46%. Operating profit that quarter was KRW 11.3 billion, up 12.0% from the prior quarter, and Shinhan Investment Corp. analyzed that the operating margin improved slightly to 2.7% from 2.5% in the first quarter.

Shareholder returns and treasury share cancellation

In June 2026 the company set a target shareholder return ratio of at least 40% of annual adjusted separate net income and resolved to buy back KRW 50 billion of shares. Mirae Asset Securities raised its rating to Buy in July 2026 and named treasury share cancellation as the key issue. The cancellation deadline is September 2027.

B2B merger synergy and redevelopment contracts

The merger completed on July 31, 2026 combined mid-to-high-end contract expertise with a nationwide installation network. The company is targeting Seoul redevelopment complexes, residences and office furniture, and expects merger effects from the fourth quarter. Hyundai Motor Securities raised its 2026 operating profit estimate to KRW 37 billion in September 2026.

09

Bear factors

Slower housing transactions and fewer move-ins

Higher interest rates and lending curbs reduced housing sales transactions in the third quarter. Shinhan Investment Corp. expected slower Seoul metropolitan transactions in August 2026 and cut its 2027 operating profit estimate by 21.5%.

Samsung Securities said fewer housing transactions and move-in units will keep conditions unfavorable for both B2C and B2B for some time.

B2B sales decline and weak construction

Shinhan Investment Corp. reported that second-quarter 2026 B2B sales fell 29% year on year. Hyundai Motor Securities said cumulative completions through July 2026 were down 41.4% year on year and expected them to stay weak for three years. Because B2B sales lag construction starts by about two years, the recovery is expected to be slow.

Cost pressure and one-off charges

Third-quarter operating profit was KRW 1.61 billion, down 76.5% year on year, and operating profit was below 1% of sales. The company cited Middle East-linked raw material cost pressure and one-off merger costs. Hyundai Motor Securities analyzed that vendors face pressure to raise unit prices.

10

Risk factors

Policy and interest rate risk

If lending curbs, tax tightening and high rates keep suppressing housing sales, both B2C Rehouse demand and B2B contract orders could weaken together. Mirae Asset Securities saw the expiry of the multi-home surcharge supporting transactions, but that assumption depends on policy changes.

Cost and expense risk

If Middle East-linked raw material prices, freight costs and a strong exchange rate persist, vendor price increases could compress margins. In August 2026 the company said housing uncertainty and rising raw material and freight costs would continue into the second half.

Execution risk in restructuring

The absorption merger is complete, but whether the integration benefits show up in fourth-quarter results has not yet been tested. Whether treasury shares are actually cancelled also remains a point of focus, and the shareholder return reading depends on it.

12

Overall view

Hanssem's sales have fallen for three straight years since 2023, and operating profit fell 40.8% in 2025, yet the company has kept annual operating profit in the black and operating cash flow positive.

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 rose to about KRW 23 billion in the company's preliminary release, while third-quarter operating profit fell to KRW 1.61 billion.

Bullish factors include the higher-value product mix, expanded treasury share buybacks and shareholder returns, and B2B efficiency after the merger.

Bearish factors include weak housing transactions and construction starts and completions, rising raw material and freight costs, and one-off merger charges, and some brokerages see profit gains as limited without a sales recovery.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 fourth-quarter preliminary results, the 2026 annual report and whether treasury shares are cancelled.

Whether the B2C shift to higher-value products translates into sales growth, and whether B2B turns with a construction recovery, are the variables that will decide the earnings direction. Which recovers first, profitability or sales, will become clear in the fourth-quarter 2026 results released in early 2027.
