Report overview

Shrinking Sales, Narrowing Losses, and a Listing Review

Iljin Display's sales have declined since 2025, and the company has posted operating losses for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. Following the halt at its Eumseong plant, a listing eligibility review now sits alongside the restructuring of its business as the key item to watch.

02

Business structure

Iljin Display operates two divisions: the DS division, which makes touchscreen panels, and the Sapphire (AD) division, which makes sapphire wafers for LED use.

The DS division supplies capacitive touchscreen panels used in notebooks, tablet PCs, smartphones and automotive electronics, and the company describes ongoing work on new structures and productivity to widen its applications.

The Sapphire division produces sapphire ingots and wafers, the base materials for light-emitting diodes (LEDs). An older analysis of unclear date put the touch business at more than 90 percent of sales and the sapphire business below 10 percent.

Samsung Electronics is often cited as the main customer, so sales are sensitive to its smartphone and tablet shipments.

Production is being reorganized: the Eumseong plant, which housed the ingot and wafer lines, generated KRW 22.6 billion of sales in 2024, or 25.4 percent of consolidated sales of KRW 89.2 billion, and its operations were halted by a board decision in September 2025.

Reports indicated the Pyeongtaek plant had no production since 2024, and touch panel output has shifted mainly to the Vietnam subsidiary. Market saturation in smartphones and longer replacement cycles have reduced demand for touch panels and intensified competition among suppliers. As of October 2, 2026, the largest shareholder group held 52.43 percent of shares.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed basis, annual sales went from KRW 124.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 54.3 billion in 2023 (down 56.3% year on year), rose to KRW 89.2 billion in 2024 (up 64.1%), and fell to KRW 57.8 billion in 2025 (down 35.1%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

The operating margin moved from 0.8% in 2022 to -14.7% in 2023, 1.6% in 2024 and -8.4% in 2025. An operating profit of KRW 1.05 billion in 2022 became an operating loss of KRW 7.96 billion in 2023, turned to an operating profit of KRW 1.39 billion in 2024, and then to an operating loss of KRW 4.88 billion in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 3.26 billion in 2022, a loss of KRW 14.9 billion in 2023 with losses widening, a profit of KRW 5.65 billion in 2024 after returning to the black, and a loss of KRW 6.28 billion in 2025.

In 2024, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 5.65 billion exceeded the operating profit of KRW 1.39 billion, so non-operating items played a large role that year.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 3.26 billion in 2022, a loss of KRW 14.9 billion in 2023, a profit of KRW 5.65 billion in 2024 and a loss of KRW 6.28 billion in 2025. The debt ratio fell from 305.5% in 2022 and 338.8% in 2023 to 123.9% in 2024 and then edged up to 135.7% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 28.6 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 38.8 billion. Operating cash flow was negative at -KRW 2.74 billion in 2022, -KRW 8.4 billion in 2023 and -KRW 6.19 billion in 2024, then turned to KRW 105 million in 2025.

Quarterly sales fell from KRW 13.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 3.82 billion in the fourth quarter (down 72.2% quarter on quarter), rebounded to KRW 11.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 208.3%), and then declined to KRW 10.7 billion in the second quarter (down 9.4%) and KRW 8.02 billion in the third quarter (down 24.9% quarter on quarter and down 41.8% year on year).

Operating losses narrowed from KRW 2.48 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 612 million in the fourth quarter and KRW 611 million in the first quarter of 2026, then widened to KRW 676 million and KRW 1.21 billion, and the company has posted losses for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 690 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, a swing back to profit, followed by losses of KRW 580 million, KRW 771 million and KRW 1.74 billion in the first three quarters of 2026.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 2.4 billion. The company attributed the 2025 decline in sales and profit mainly to weak conditions in related industries.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from end markets is shaped by market saturation in smartphones, reduced innovation and longer replacement cycles. An industry information service describes shipments as declining year on year. The company's quarterly sales decline since 2025 appears to coincide with the downswing of this cycle.

The DS division's utilization was reported at 53.2 percent in the first half of 2025, up from 37.1 percent a year earlier, with higher output at the Vietnam plant cited as the reason.

The touch panel industry faces intense price and volume competition, and the company cited intensifying business competition when it announced the Eumseong halt. An older analysis of unclear date argued that demand for high-quality wafers could grow with micro LED adoption.

Samsung Electronics and LG Innotek in the same electronic components sector are much larger, so direct comparison is difficult. Iljin Display is a small component maker whose market capitalization makes comparison with larger peers difficult, and its operating margin remains in loss territory.

06

Outlook

When the Eumseong ingot and wafer lines were halted, the company said sales could decline in the short term but that its business structure and financial base would improve over the long run. It also said it will focus on profitability-driven operations.

If the sapphire business shrinks, sales mix could tilt further toward touch panels. A larger share of output at the Vietnam subsidiary could support utilization, but with third-quarter 2026 sales down and losses wider, the pace of improvement will show up in quarterly results.

On listing, reports said trading suspension would continue during the improvement period. When that period ends, the Korea Exchange will review the company's improvement plan and decide whether the listing can be maintained. The half-year audit report for 2025 flagged a material uncertainty over going concern.

The next checkpoints are the annual sales and operating margin in the 2026 annual report, followed by first-quarter 2027 results as a gauge of the Eumseong halt's effect.

Bull factors

Narrower operating losses

Operating losses narrowed from KRW 2.48 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 611 million in the first quarter of 2026, and the fourth quarter of 2025 returned to a profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

The company cited excess inventory and intensifying competition as reasons for the Eumseong halt and said it will focus on profitable operations. Lower fixed costs and inventory burdens could improve the earnings structure.

Vietnam production base

The DS division's utilization in the first half of 2025 was reported at 53.2 percent, up from 37.1 percent a year earlier, supported by higher output at the Vietnam plant. As domestic sites shrink, concentrating production overseas and raising utilization is meaningful for the cost structure.

Options for business shifts

An older analysis suggested that demand for high-quality sapphire wafers could rise as micro LED markets grow. The company has described expansion into touch sensors and integrated modules in an AI-driven environment. Both items are dated, so they should be separated from current project progress.

Bear factors

Weakening sales base

Annual sales fell 35.1 percent in 2025 to KRW 57.8 billion, and third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 8.02 billion were down 41.8 percent from a year earlier. Sales have declined for two consecutive quarters since the first quarter of 2026.

In an environment of falling smartphone shipments and longer replacement cycles, the evidence for a sales recovery has not yet appeared in quarterly results.

Losses widening again

The operating loss widened to KRW 1.21 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 1.74 billion, extending losses for three consecutive quarters.

The fourth-quarter 2025 profit was a single quarter, so the earnings structure cannot yet be described as having improved.

Listing and financial risks

After the Eumseong halt, trading was suspended and a listing eligibility review was triggered, with an improvement period granted. The half-year audit report for 2025 flagged a material going-concern uncertainty.

The debt ratio was 135.7% at the end of 2025, and the largest shareholder group holds 52.43 percent, so decision-making is concentrated.

Risk factors

Listing maintenance risk

When the improvement period ends, the Korea Exchange will review the improvement plan and decide whether the listing can be maintained. If trading remains suspended, liquidity is limited, and the outcome could change the trading structure significantly. This risk can move separately from operating results.

Customer concentration and demand risk

Samsung Electronics is often cited as the main customer, so sales are sensitive to shipment swings at a single key client. Smartphone market saturation and longer replacement cycles are structural factors that are unlikely to resolve quickly. Changes in one customer's shipments can have a large effect on quarterly sales.

Financial capacity and cash flow

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 38.8 billion, above total equity of KRW 28.6 billion. Operating cash flow was negative from 2022 through 2024 and turned to KRW 105 million in 2025, but that is a single year's result. With operating losses continuing, borrowing costs could constrain capital spending and restructuring.

Overall view
