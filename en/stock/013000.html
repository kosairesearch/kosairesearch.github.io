02

Business structure

Sewoo Global was founded in 1978 and listed on the Korea Exchange in 1989 as a plastic raw material distribution company.

It holds the domestic agency status for SABIC Innovative Plastics and sources engineering plastics and general-purpose resins from overseas producers including Polyplastics and BASF for sale in the domestic market.

Beyond pure distribution, the company also operates in-house production of ABS and PC, PC/ABS compounding products supplied to customers.

It runs a nationwide sales network centered on its Incheon headquarters covering the greater Seoul and central regions, extended to the southern region through a Yeongnam branch based in Miryang, South Gyeongsang Province, with offices in Daegu and Busan.

According to recent company disclosures, it also operates a wholly owned subsidiary in Vietnam, Sewoo Global Vietnam.

Its core customer base consists of automotive and electronics manufacturers requiring low-volume, high-variety purchases, with timely supply and stable raw material sourcing cited as key competitive strengths.

In terms of ownership, CEO Ahn Baek-sun and related parties held a combined 38.44% stake as of a March 2026 disclosure, making it a small-cap listed company with concentrated ownership.

The domestic plastic raw material distribution market is characterized by competition among numerous small and mid-sized distributors, requiring both price competitiveness and technical sales capability.

04

Earnings analysis

Sewoo Global's annual revenue rose 28.7% from KRW 28.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 36.1 billion in 2023, then declined for two straight years to KRW 35.9 billion in 2024 (down 0.7%) and KRW 32.7 billion in 2025 (down 8.9%).

Over the same period, operating profit grew from KRW 1.56 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.37 billion in 2023 (up 52.0%) and KRW 2.45 billion in 2024 (up 3.6%), before edging down 0.8% to KRW 2.43 billion in 2025.

Despite the revenue decline, the operating margin improved every year from 5.6% in 2022 to 7.4% in 2025, indicating a clear focus on profitability management over top-line growth.

Controlling net income rose for three consecutive years from KRW 1.52 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.55 billion in 2023 (up 67.8%), KRW 3.46 billion in 2024 (up 35.4%), and KRW 3.73 billion in 2025 (up 7.7%), outpacing the improvement in operating profit.

On a quarterly basis, revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from KRW 8.09 billion in the second quarter of 2025 through KRW 10.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (up 25.0% quarter-on-quarter and 34.0% year-on-year).

Quarterly operating profit fell to a low of KRW 508 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, then rebounded for two consecutive quarters to KRW 717 million in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 923 million in the second quarter of 2026.

Controlling net income also jumped to KRW 1.74 billion in the second quarter of 2026, up 293.9% year-on-year, bringing the trailing four-quarter total (third quarter 2025 through second quarter 2026) to KRW 5.46 billion.

The debt ratio declined from 12.5% in 2023 to 8.4% in 2025, and operating cash flow, which dipped to KRW 1.86 billion in 2024, recovered to KRW 3.43 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic engineering plastics distribution market is assessed to be seeing steady demand growth for high heat-resistant, high-rigidity materials as the automotive and electronics industries advance technologically.

However, a structural limitation is noted in that relatively underdeveloped domestic injection molding machine technology means precision products rely entirely on imported equipment from Japan and Germany.

In engineering plastics distribution, the ability to respond promptly to low-volume, high-variety orders and to secure raw materials stably is cited as the core competitive strength, a factor behind market consolidation around a small number of players with specialized marketing capability and technical sales networks.

Sewoo Global is assessed to have maintained relatively stable profitability despite intense domestic competition, leveraging its domestic agency status for SABIC Innovative Plastics.

Growing demand for high-quality products amid industrial sophistication, along with trends toward smaller, lighter, and more premium components, are cited as factors supporting demand for high-performance resins.

However, there are few large listed companies directly comparable to Sewoo Global within the KOSPI-listed plastic raw material distribution segment, meaning external research material to gauge its industry position is also limited.

With low foreign ownership and a free float ratio in the 60% range, it is not a stock with a particularly deep supply-demand base.

Bull factors

Earnings Momentum Recovery

Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 10.8 billion and controlling net income of KRW 1.74 billion rose 34.0% and 293.9% year-on-year, respectively, with operating profit rebounding for two consecutive quarters after bottoming in the fourth quarter of 2025.

Revenue also grew for four consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the second quarter of 2026, showing clear signs of a top-line recovery. Whether this trend continues further requires confirmation from upcoming quarterly disclosures.

Solid Financial Structure

As of the end of 2025, the debt ratio was just 8.4% against total equity of KRW 54.8 billion, reflecting almost no financial leverage. Operating cash flow also recovered to KRW 3.43 billion in 2025 from the prior year. This gives the company capacity to run operations without external funding even in adverse circumstances.

Demand Base for High-Performance Materials

Demand for high heat-resistant, high-rigidity engineering plastics is assessed to be growing alongside the technological advancement of the automotive and electronics industries.

Sewoo Global combines its domestic agency status for SABIC Innovative Plastics with in-house ABS and PC compounding production capability, giving it a hybrid distribution-and-manufacturing structure. This is cited as a differentiating factor compared with competitors that rely solely on brokerage margins.

Bear factors

Annual Revenue Decline Trend

Annual revenue peaked at KRW 36.1 billion in 2023 and then declined for two consecutive years to KRW 35.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 32.7 billion in 2025. While quarterly revenue rebounded in the first half of 2026, whether this will translate into an annual trend reversal has not yet been confirmed.

Given the nature of the distribution business, revenue is subject to considerable volatility tied to raw material prices and sales volume.

Structurally Thin Distribution Margins

The operating margin improved to 7.4% in 2025 but remains in single digits, reflecting the inherently thin margin structure of the distribution business. Pricing power is split between raw material suppliers and customers, which may limit the scope for margin expansion. If the share of in-house production remains small, the pace of profitability improvement could be constrained.

Low Liquidity and Concentrated Ownership

Combined ownership by the largest shareholder and related parties exceeds 38%, leaving a relatively small free float. Foreign ownership also remains low, limiting inflows from institutional and foreign investors.

Trading volume is typically low but spikes sharply at certain points, which can heighten share price volatility.

Risk factors

Raw Material Price and FX Volatility

As the engineering plastics raw materials Sewoo Global distributes are sourced from overseas producers, purchase prices can be affected by fluctuations in international crude oil and naphtha prices as well as exchange rates. Delays in passing costs through to customers could temporarily compress margins. This is a common factor across the distribution industry as a whole.

Dependence on End-Market Cycles

Order volumes depend on the capital expenditure and production plans of the company's main automotive and electronics customers. A slowdown or inventory adjustment in these end markets could directly affect revenue. The revenue declines seen in 2024 and 2025 can be viewed as an example of this sensitivity.

Governance and Liquidity Risk

The high stake held by the largest shareholder's family may limit the influence of minority shareholders in decision-making. With a free float ratio in the 60% range and high trading volume variability, sharp short-term price swings tied to supply and demand are possible. This structure is commonly observed among small-cap KOSPI-listed companies.

Overall view

Sewoo Global showed a clear rebound in both revenue and controlling net income in the second quarter of 2026, continuing a quarterly earnings improvement trend from the fourth-quarter 2025 trough.

On an annual basis, however, revenue peaked at KRW 36.1 billion in 2023 before declining for two consecutive years through 2025, so whether the recent quarterly recovery translates into an annual trend reversal requires confirmation from further quarterly disclosures.

The operating margin steadily improved from 5.6% in 2022 to 7.4% in 2025, and controlling net income rose for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, showing a clear improvement in earnings quality beyond top-line figures.

A low debt ratio of 8.4% and total equity of KRW 54.8 billion are factors worth noting from a financial stability standpoint. On the other hand, the stock is classified as one with relatively high supply-demand-driven volatility given concentrated ownership by the largest shareholder's family and a limited free float.
