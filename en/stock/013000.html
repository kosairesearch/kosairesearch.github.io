02

Business structure

Sewoo Global is a specialty distributor of plastic raw materials and has been listed on KOSPI since 1989. Its product range covers engineering plastics, high-performance resins and commodity plastics.

The company is a domestic agent for SABIC Innovative Plastics and also supplies materials from BASF, Mitsubishi and Chevron. Its head office is in Incheon, and its sales network centers on Yeongnam and Daegu branches with reach into the southern industrial belt of Pohang, Ulsan, Changwon, Gwangyang and Yeosu.

The company has said it pursues qualitative growth through high-value products developed on new technologies. In the first half of 2025, its report also described plans to secure SABIC IP's sales network to widen market share.

Because it is a distribution business, revenue depends on raw material prices and the operating conditions of downstream demand, and crude oil and naphtha price movements are cited as a driver of results. End users appear to be mainly manufacturers that process these materials into products.

The business rests on its distributor role, sales network and supply relationships more than on in-house production.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose 28.7% year on year to KRW 28.1 billion in 2022 and KRW 36.1 billion in 2023, the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span. It then fell to KRW 35.9 billion in 2024 (down 0.7%) and KRW 32.7 billion in 2025 (down 8.9%), declining for two consecutive years.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 1.56 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.37 billion in 2023 (up 52.0%), and reached KRW 2.45 billion in 2024 (up 3.6%), the largest year in the span. It slipped to KRW 2.43 billion in 2025 (down 0.8%).

Even as revenue shrank, operating margin rose steadily from 5.6% in 2022 to 6.6%, 6.8% and 7.4% in 2025, which points to improving sales margins.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders grew from KRW 1.52 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.55 billion in 2023 (up 67.8%), KRW 3.46 billion in 2024 (up 35.4%) and KRW 3.73 billion in 2025 (up 7.7%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023.

In the filed figures, net income including non-controlling interests equals this amount in each year. Net income growth outpaced operating profit growth, and non-operating items and tax effects may explain the gap, which later filings should clarify.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 54.8 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 4.61 billion. The debt ratio went from 8.8% in 2022 to 12.5% in 2023, then 9.2% in 2024 and 8.4% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 2.85 billion in 2022, KRW 4.21 billion in 2023 and KRW 1.86 billion in 2024, before recovering to KRW 3.43 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue moved from KRW 8.17 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 8.19 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 8.67 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 10.8 billion in the second quarter, before falling to KRW 9.69 billion in the third quarter of 2026 (down 10.6% from the prior quarter, up 18.6% from a year earlier).

Quarterly operating profit rose to KRW 923 million in the second quarter of 2026 and then fell to KRW 841 million in the third quarter (down 8.9% quarter on quarter).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.74 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 1.07 billion in the third quarter (down 38.5% quarter on quarter, down 2.9% year on year).

The sum over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, is KRW 5.43 billion.

Industry analysis

Plastic raw material distribution is exposed to petrochemical feedstock prices and to the operating rates of downstream industries.

The stock is classified as one whose results move with crude oil and naphtha prices, and in March 2026 reports tied a sharp share price rise to raw material supply concerns linked to the Middle East situation and petrochemical theme trading.

On the demand side, a trend toward smaller, lighter and higher-grade products was cited in a securities data service's earnings commentary as supporting demand for higher-quality materials. The same commentary noted that the company maintains stable profitability despite intense domestic competition.

Other assessments point out that demand is growing while competition is intensifying. Cumulative separate revenue for the first nine months of 2025 fell 9.0% year on year, while operating profit rose 3.4%.

Within this setting, Sewoo Global operates as an intermediary distributor handling products from several suppliers, including SABIC, BASF, Mitsubishi and Chevron. Its relative position depends mainly on the scale of its sales network and on securing supply lines.

An operating margin that climbed into the 7% range suggests it has defended sales margins in a competitive market. Revenue rose quarter on quarter in the second quarter of 2026 and then fell in the third, which shows sensitivity to downstream demand and raw material price swings.

How the supply shock linked to the Middle East affected selling prices and inventory valuation is the next point to verify.
