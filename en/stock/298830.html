02

Business structure

Suresofttech is a software verification platform company founded in 2002. It supplies automated testing and verification solutions for mission-critical sectors such as automotive, defense and nuclear power. It acquired big-data company Mobigen in 2023 and listed on KOSDAQ in April of that year.

The business is organized around two pillars: software testing and verification, and big data and AI. One company profile puts the 2025 mix at 63.1% software verification, 36.8% big data and AI, and 0.1% other.

A separate data provider breaks revenue down by sector, showing automotive at 56.58%, big data and AI for telecom and public sector at 19.49%, defense and aerospace at 11.39%, new business and other at 8.70%, and nuclear and energy at 3.82%. The two classifications differ, so their figures cannot be matched directly.

The VERIFAI series (VERIFAI-M, VERIFAI-A, VERIFAI-D and VERIFAI-V) anchors its AI verification offering, which the company positions within its Test of AI business. Customers are mainly domestic and overseas B2B clients. A company profile cites more than 300,000 customers across more than 35 industry sectors. As of August 20, 2026, a group led by CEO Bae Hyun-seop held 39.62%, and Hyundai Motor held 7.20%.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 43.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 63.3 billion in 2023 (up 45.7% year on year), KRW 88.8 billion in 2024 (up 40.3%), and KRW 93.9 billion in 2025 (up 5.8%).

Revenue has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period. Reports attribute part of 2024 revenue growth to new sales through subsidiary Mobigen.

Operating profit was KRW 9.67 billion in 2022, KRW 11.5 billion in 2023 (up 18.7%), KRW 7.89 billion in 2024 (down 31.2%) and KRW 12.6 billion in 2025 (up 59.7%), with 2024 the smallest year. The operating margin fell from 22.3% in 2022 to 18.1% in 2023 and 8.9% in 2024, then recovered to 13.4% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 9.45 billion, KRW 5.06 billion (down 46.5%), KRW 10.8 billion (up 113.6%) and KRW 8.58 billion (down 20.6%) across the same years.

Net income including non-controlling interests ran at KRW 9.46 billion, KRW 5.83 billion, KRW 10.1 billion and KRW 9.63 billion, a different path from controlling-shareholder profit.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 121.9 billion, of which KRW 107.1 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 57.1 billion. The debt ratio declined from 74.6% in 2022 to 46.8% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue peaked at KRW 31.9 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, up 35.5% from the prior quarter, with operating profit of KRW 10.9 billion, up 202.9%. In the first quarter of 2026, revenue fell 36.7% to KRW 20.2 billion and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 415 million.

Operating profit returned to positive at KRW 2.76 billion in the second quarter, and in the third quarter revenue was KRW 26.6 billion and operating profit KRW 4.65 billion, up 68.8% from the prior quarter.

Revenue rose in the second and third quarters of 2026, but the third-quarter gain was only 0.4% quarter on quarter.

05

Industry analysis

The automotive software verification market is entering a phase of broader demand as the shift to software-defined vehicles, or SDV, gains pace. The company treats automotive software as its core area and says it runs a mobility center that develops and verifies vehicle software to international standards.

Tighter verification requirements for automotive software would expand demand for testing tools, which is a supportive factor. Against that, automotive accounts for a large share of revenue, so automaker investment cycles pass directly through to results.

The AI verification market is still at an early stage, and the company positions Vertical AI, Physical AI and Trustworthy AI as its three core pillars. Reports say it partnered with a French AI verification firm to explore responses to European AI regulation.

The big data and AI unit came through the Mobigen acquisition and serves telecom and public-sector clients, a customer base different from its automotive business. Among 79 software-sector companies tracked by one data provider, the company ranks 17th on annual revenue and about tenth on operating profit.

The company's own profile highlights its localization of verification technology previously supplied by foreign vendors as a point of strength.

06

Outlook

The company set a 2025 revenue target of KRW 95 billion to KRW 105 billion, according to press reports. Disclosed 2025 revenue of KRW 93.9 billion fell below the bottom of that range, and the KRW 100 billion mark was not reached in 2025. MOUs came in a steady stream during 2026.

They covered AI agent verification in finance, industrial robot collaboration, sensor collaboration spanning defense, robotics and 6G, and autonomous golf cart solutions.

An MOU signed on October 29, 2026 for Physical AI built on 5G industrial networks pairs a telecom infrastructure company's capabilities with the company's AI and software verification. Such agreements typically move through a joint working group and proof-of-concept stage before commercialization.

Whether these partnerships translate into revenue will need to be confirmed in periodic filings. On the product side, the formal launch of VERIFAI-A and an expanded KOLAS accreditation for data quality testing are confirmed announcements.

The company plans to attend the 2026 KSAE autumn conference from November 18 to 21, 2026, a venue for gauging demand in automotive software verification. Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose only 0.4% from the prior quarter, which suggests the quarterly revenue trend has flattened.

The next periodic report, the 2026 annual report, will show whether annual revenue and operating margin improve on 2025.

08

Bull factors

Automotive verification and SDV demand

Automotive software verification is a core area for the company and connects directly to the SDV transition. One data provider puts automotive at 56.58% of revenue. Hyundai Motor holds 7.20%, which reflects a close relationship in this field.

The bull case holds that stricter vehicle software verification requirements could lift demand for testing tools.

Earnings recovery in the second half of 2026

Operating profit stayed positive, moving from KRW 2.76 billion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 4.65 billion in the third quarter, which was up 29.3% from a year earlier. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 4.01 billion in the third quarter, up 155.2% year on year. The last four quarters total KRW 13.8 billion, above the KRW 8.58 billion posted for all of 2025.

Financial room and expanding AI partnerships

The debt ratio fell to 46.8% at the end of 2025 from 74.6% in 2022. Operating cash flow rose to KRW 17.9 billion in 2025 from KRW 9.47 billion in 2024. Partnerships in financial AI agents, industrial robots and Physical AI built on 5G networks in 2026 are widening the reach of the company's AI verification offerings.

Bear factors

Volatility in quarterly results

Revenue in the first quarter of 2026 fell 36.7% from the prior quarter to KRW 20.2 billion, and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 415 million. Compared with KRW 31.9 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, quarter-to-quarter swings are large. Third-quarter revenue rose only 0.4% quarter on quarter, which the bear case reads as a slow pace of recovery.

Gap versus past margins and profit levels

The operating margin was 22.3% in 2022 and 13.4% in 2025, still below the earlier level. Revenue growth slowed to 5.8% in 2025 from 40.3% in 2024. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 20.6% in 2025, and 2025 revenue came in below the KRW 95 billion bottom of the company's target range.

Share price volatility and concentrated ownership

The stock was designated a one-day investment caution issue on August 5, 2026, after it rose 25.27% over three days and a single account's buying involvement reached 8.45%. A group led by CEO Bae Hyun-seop holds 39.62%, and that ownership structure can also shape trading flows. Such trading volatility can move the share price independently of reported earnings.

Risk factors

Customer and sector concentration

Automotive accounts for 56.58% of revenue by one classification, so a single end market's investment cycle has a large effect on results. Cuts in verification budgets or in-house testing at automakers could hit revenue directly.

How far revenue diversifies into big data and AI and into defense and aerospace determines the size of this risk.

New business and subsidiary investment burden

The company absorbed big-data business through the 2023 Mobigen acquisition, and new-business investment has been cited as a factor behind earnings swings. Operating profit fell from KRW 11.5 billion in 2023 to KRW 7.89 billion in 2024, a period when new-business spending was also discussed. Wider collaboration in Physical AI and autonomous driving could raise the investment burden again.

Regulatory environment and share price volatility

Demand for AI verification could expand as Europe and Korea tighten AI rules, but changes to verification standards or certification schemes could affect the business model. The share price has swung sharply on short-term trading, as the August 2026 investment caution designation showed. When the gap between results and share price widens, filings become the main reference point.

Overall view

Suresofttech built its base in automotive software verification and has extended into big data, AI and an AI verification product line. Disclosed revenue rose from KRW 43.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 93.9 billion in 2025, and operating profit recovered to KRW 12.6 billion in 2025 from a low point in 2024.

Operating profit has stayed positive in the second and third quarters of 2026 after a first-quarter loss. On the other side, first-quarter 2026 revenue fell 36.7%, third-quarter growth was only 0.4%, and the operating margin remains below its 2022 level.

Automotive concentration and new-business investment burden remain key variables. On valuation, the pace of earnings recovery and the premium over book value are the reference points.

The next checks are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results and the 2026 annual report, which will indicate whether the earnings recovery holds. This report is for information only and does not include a buy or sell opinion.
