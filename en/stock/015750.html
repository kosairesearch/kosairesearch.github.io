Sales Base Holds, but Q3 2026 Net Loss Tests Profit Defense

02

Business structure

Sungwoo Hitech was founded in 1977 and listed on KOSDAQ in 1995 as a maker of automotive body parts. An analysis cited by a press report from March 2025 put automotive parts, including battery cases, bumpers and door parts, at 86% of product sales, with other items at 14%.

The same source put domestic sales at 45%, Europe at 27%, India at 11%, North America at 9% and China at 8%. A regional breakdown for 2025 showed domestic at 43.2%, Europe at 26.2%, North America at 15.4%, India at 9.0% and China at 6.4%, with North America up from 12% in 2023.

Key customers are Hyundai Motor, KIA and GM Korea, and the company also holds basic contracts with Hyundai Mobis. Overseas subsidiaries supply global automakers including Volkswagen, BMW and GM.

The company's newsroom says it runs 22 production sites in 10 countries, including Germany, the Czech Republic, China, the United States and Mexico.

On electrified parts it works on battery system, battery module and battery case programs, and it is developing infrared and lidar sensors for autonomous driving as new businesses. Its core body parts are mainly steel-panel products, so sales move with the production volumes of each customer model.

The newsroom also says overseas sales exceed 60% of the total, a figure that does not match the regional breakdown above.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 3.98 trillion in 2022 to KRW 4.32 trillion in 2023, up 8.7%, fell 1.8% to KRW 4.25 trillion in 2024, and reached KRW 4.38 trillion in 2025, up 3.2%. Among 2022–2025, 2025 was the largest year for sales and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 99.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 256.7 billion in 2023, up 158.8%, dropped 19.8% to KRW 205.9 billion in 2024, and recovered 17.9% to KRW 242.7 billion in 2025. Operating margin was 2.5% in 2022, 5.9% in 2023, 4.8% in 2024 and 5.5% in 2025.

Sales stayed within a fairly narrow range, so margin differences drove most of the operating profit swings. Attributable net income was KRW 38.9 billion in 2022, KRW 169.9 billion in 2023 (up 336.5%), KRW 135.1 billion in 2024 (down 20.5%) and KRW 177.8 billion in 2025 (up 31.7%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 175.7 billion in 2023, above the attributable figure, while in 2025 both measures were KRW 177.8 billion.

Quarterly sales were KRW 1.19 trillion in Q3 2025, KRW 994.7 billion in Q4 2025 (down 16.1% quarter on quarter), KRW 1.07 trillion in Q1 2026 (up 7.5%), KRW 1.13 trillion in Q2 2026 (up 5.3%) and KRW 1.08 trillion in Q3 2026 (down 4.1% quarter on quarter and down 8.8% year on year).

Operating profit was KRW 63.4 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 55.6 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 54.5 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 65.1 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 36.3 billion in Q3 2026, which fell 44.1% quarter on quarter and 42.7% year on year.

Attributable net income was KRW 57.7 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 63.3 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 31.9 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 30.8 billion in Q2 2026, before Q3 2026 turned to an attributable net loss of KRW 26.8 billion.

The gap between positive operating profit and the Q3 2026 net loss needs to be traced through non-operating items in the filed quarterly report. Attributable net income for the last four quarters totaled KRW 99.2 billion.

At end-2025, total equity was KRW 2.07 trillion, of which attributable equity was KRW 1.7 trillion, and total liabilities were KRW 2.79 trillion.

Industry analysis

Automotive parts demand moves closely with automaker production and shifts in global supply chains. On a 2025 full-year basis, rising hybrid vehicle demand at home and abroad lifted automaker plant utilization, which in turn supported Sungwoo Hitech's sales.

By contrast, a brokerage analysis cited in March 2025 said slower battery electric vehicle supply had limited new orders. The same analysis flagged whether Mexican tariffs are imposed as a variable that makes annual sales estimates difficult.

In the 2026 earnings period, slower electric vehicle demand and tariff risk are still cited as factors weighing on valuation. In March 2026, rising utilization at automaker plants globally and expanding new orders were cited as drivers of auto parts stock prices.

On the 2025 full-year ranking among 137 listed auto parts companies, the company ranked 10th in sales and 8th in operating profit, both in the upper group. Its operating margin ranked 31st, so its scale rank and profitability rank differ.

In electrified parts, the battery system business runs alongside Hyundai Mobis, so the electrification schedule of the Hyundai Motor group is a key variable.

The cycle position cannot be determined from filed data alone, and the period looks like a transition in which hybrid expansion and slower electric vehicle growth appear at the same time.

Outlook

At the annual shareholders meeting held on March 27, 2026, the company's 45th-term financial statements were approved and a cash dividend was resolved. With the debt ratio down to 134.8% at end-2025, financial headroom is wider than in 2022.

New businesses cited include electric vehicle battery systems, hydrogen, cameras and lidar, and a brokerage report from March 2025 expected them to become growth bases alongside the core business.

A 2023 brokerage report said the Mexico plant was being expanded to match KIA volumes, and the same report described the Tennessee plant supplying GM. On Russia, a March 2025 brokerage analysis suggested that restoring production capacity there could add more than KRW 200 billion to KRW 300 billion in annual sales.

This is a conditional scenario that depends on a ceasefire and production restart, not a target stated by the company. The company's newsroom announced it had reached a USD 600 million export tower milestone. Full-year 2026 results will be confirmed in the annual report due by late March 2027.

The next check is whether the sharp drop in Q3 2026 operating profit continues into Q4, and whether new electric vehicle orders are disclosed.

Risk factors

Customer concentration

The company depends heavily on automakers such as Hyundai Motor, KIA and GM Korea. When customer production plans shrink, volumes and sales adjust together. New model items are awarded through open bidding and development requests, so securing new volume depends on those schedules.

Tariff, currency and regional risk

Mexican tariff issues were flagged in a March 2025 analysis as a source of uncertainty for annual sales estimates. With North America at 15.4% and Europe at 26.2% of sales in 2025, trade policy and currency moves could affect sales and profit. Production spread across 22 sites in several countries also means cost structures differ by region.

Financial leverage and earnings volatility

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 2.79 trillion, above total equity of KRW 2.07 trillion, with a debt ratio of 134.8%. If the Q3 2026 net loss continues, financial headroom could narrow. Margin swings have moved profit sharply, as operating margin fell from 5.9% in 2023 to 4.8% in 2024.

Overall view

Sungwoo Hitech's sales rose from KRW 3.98 trillion in 2022 to KRW 4.38 trillion in 2025, and operating margin improved from 2.5% to 5.5%, showing a profitability recovery. Among the years in the period, 2025 was the largest for sales and attributable net income, and 2023 was the largest for operating profit.

However, in Q3 2026 sales fell 8.8% year on year to KRW 1.08 trillion, operating profit fell 42.7% to KRW 36.3 billion, and the company swung to an attributable net loss of KRW 26.8 billion. Attributable net income for the last four quarters totaled KRW 99.2 billion, more than half of the 2025 full-year figure.

On financials, the debt ratio fell to 134.8% at end-2025, and operating cash flow rose to KRW 453.3 billion in 2025. Strengths include the overseas production network and electrified parts expansion, while weaknesses include the pace of electric vehicle orders, tariff variables and the sharp Q3 profit drop.

On valuation, price-to-book and price-to-earnings multiples have stayed in low ranges on a year-end basis, while earnings volatility and the Q3 net loss remain variables in reading those multiples. The next checkpoints are the Q4 results in the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
