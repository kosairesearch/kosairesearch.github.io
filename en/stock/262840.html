Profit has risen, but the operating margin has narrowed

02

Business structure

Iquest was founded in 1996 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2021 as a B2B software company. It develops and supplies ASP-based SaaS subscription software and SI-style on-premise ERP for small and mid-sized businesses, and its main revenue comes from the Eulmaeyo ERP product family.

Eulmaeyo Works, launched in March 2026, combines mail, messenger and electronic approval with ERP and targets smaller firms that lack dedicated accounting staff.

The same lineup includes the HR platform HR365, the e-contract service Sign365 and the small-business management app Eulma365, and the company is concentrating resources on settling these AI-based products in the market. In fintech, it is widening B2B financial services built on the Eulma simple remittance service.

Its subsidiary Defocus handles data analytics, cloud services and AI research and development. Customers are mainly small and mid-sized businesses and small merchants, and the company was also selected as a supplier in a government program that promotes cloud services for SMEs.

In competition, it is compared with large system integrators in the same IT and software sector such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, but Iquest focuses on ERP and subscription services for smaller firms, so its business scope is narrower.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue rose from KRW 26.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 40.1 billion in 2023 (up 52.0% year on year), then to KRW 40.6 billion in 2024 (up 1.4%) and KRW 43.6 billion in 2025 (up 7.2%), growing for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 4.35 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.62 billion in 2023 and KRW 3.42 billion in 2024, then recovered to KRW 4.28 billion in 2025 (up 25.0%), while the operating margin declined from 16.5% in 2022 to 9.8% in 2025.

Revenue has grown while the margin has not returned to its 2022 level, and the cost drivers behind this cannot be pinned down from these figures alone.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent grew from KRW 2.87 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.61 billion in 2025, rising for three consecutive years, with a 48.6% increase in 2025. Because operating profit in 2025 stayed close to its 2022 level while owner profit rose sharply, non-operating items may have contributed.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 5.96 billion in 2025 and should be read separately from owner profit, and operating cash flow stayed above KRW 9 billion in both 2024 and 2025.

The debt ratio jumped to 87.5% at the end of 2025 from 14.9% in 2024, with total equity of KRW 5.99 billion, owner equity of KRW 5.29 billion and total liabilities of KRW 5.25 billion.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 11.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 10.6 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 9.7% year on year.

Operating profit fell from KRW 1.28 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 634 million in the first quarter of 2026 (down 50.6% quarter on quarter), then rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 919 million in the third quarter.

The company said a temporary drop in its subsidiary's operating profit in the first quarter of 2026 reflected AI research spending and organizational restructuring.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent peaked at KRW 4.41 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then fell for two consecutive quarters to KRW 153 million. The company attributed the gap between first-quarter operating profit and owner profit to investment asset gains.

The company announced first-quarter 2026 consolidated net income of KRW 5.8 billion on May 8, 2026, calling it a quarterly record on a preliminary basis, but it is not the same measure as owner profit in the regular filings.

In a November 13, 2026 disclosure, the company reported preliminary cumulative third-quarter 2026 consolidated net income of KRW 7.1 billion, up 79% from a year earlier. Owner profit for the last four quarters totals KRW 7.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for cloud ERP and SaaS among small and mid-sized businesses is supported by the spread of subscription cloud services and wider adoption of management software by small merchants and smaller firms.

On the policy side, Iquest was selected as a supplier in the 2025 government program for spreading cloud services to SMEs, in which the government and suppliers jointly fund 80% of service fees.

In the SME groupware market, the company began a push in July 2026 with a product that combines mail, messenger and electronic approval with ERP, and it highlights the ability to handle accounting and inventory data alongside approvals.

In AI, it is promoting a shift toward an AI business platform, featuring a patented automated accounting and voucher function, a chatbot, and apps for subsidies and logistics.

In one market data site's IT services comparison, Iquest's operating margin ranks in the upper group within its industry, while the industry average operating margin in the same dataset is negative, indicating weak profitability across the sector.

Competition overlaps with large system integrators and cloud companies such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS, Hyundai Autoever and NHN in customer reach, but Iquest specializes in ERP and subscription software for smaller firms.

Platform companies such as Kakao and NAVER differ in scale and customer base, so direct comparison is limited. Pricing tactics such as free-use promotions for new customers are becoming a competitive variable in the SME market.

06

Outlook

The company said it will concentrate its resources through the end of 2026 on settling its new lineup, including the Eulmaeyo ERP series, HR365, Sign365 and Eulma.

Since its March 2026 launch, Eulmaeyo Works has been offered with free electronic approval and messenger features for six months to new Eulmaeyo ERP customers. In May 2026 the company stated plans to raise its technology competitiveness and barriers to entry through investment in AI.

Its subsidiary Defocus is focusing on revenue growth and profitability after bringing in outside experts and finishing organizational streamlining, and the company expects a turnaround from the second quarter as large-project license revenue is recognized.

In a November 13, 2026 disclosure, the company reported preliminary cumulative third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 195 million at Defocus, with net income of KRW 31 million at that subsidiary level.

On shareholder returns, the company said it is actively reviewing and pursuing a combination of share buybacks and cancellations, and in September 2026 it decided to cancel treasury shares worth about KRW 1.5 billion, citing share price stability and shareholder value.

Expectations are rising for a larger year-end cash dividend, and the company has said it will review shareholder-friendly policies. The next checkpoint is whether the large Defocus project revenue appears in fourth-quarter results in the 2026 annual report, and the direction of the full-year operating margin.

Bear factors

Persistent decline in operating margin

The operating margin fell from 16.5% in 2022 to 9.0% in 2023, then stood at 8.4% in 2024 and 9.8% in 2025. Revenue grew after 2023, but the margin did not return to its 2022 level. Operating profit of KRW 4.28 billion in 2025 was roughly in line with KRW 4.35 billion in 2022.

Weakness in third-quarter results

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 10.6 billion fell 9.7% year on year. Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 153 million in the third quarter, down 81.1% from a year earlier, and has fallen for two consecutive quarters. Owner profit for the last four quarters totals KRW 7.4 billion.

Reliance on non-operating gains and balance sheet pressure

The gap between first-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 634 million and owner profit of KRW 4.41 billion was explained by investment asset gains. The debt ratio at the end of 2025 rose to 87.5% from 14.9% in 2024.

The subsidiary's profitability slowed temporarily in the first quarter of 2026 due to AI research costs, which points to large quarterly swings.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly operating profit swung from KRW 1.28 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 634 million in the first quarter of 2026, and owner profit fell from KRW 4.41 billion in the first quarter to KRW 153 million in the third.

Much of this variation depends on timing of subsidiary project revenue and investment gains, which makes earnings hard to forecast. Annual results could also change direction depending on non-operating factors.

Balance sheet and rising liabilities

The debt ratio rose from 14.9% in 2024 to 87.5% in 2025, and total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 5.25 billion. Quarterly balance sheet data also show total assets and total liabilities rising together from the second quarter of 2025. If profit slows while debt stays high, the financial burden could weigh more heavily on results.

Market adoption of new products and competition

The revenue contribution of Eulmaeyo Works and the 365 series is not separately visible in the disclosed figures. The free promotion program may also suggest that winning new customers is not straightforward. Competition from large system integrators and cloud companies could intensify in the SME market as well.

12

Overall view

IQuest's 2025 revenue was KRW 43.6 billion, up for a third consecutive year, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders also rose for a third straight year to KRW 5.61 billion in 2025.

However, the operating margin fell from 16.5% in 2022 to 9.8% in 2025, so top-line growth has not translated into margin improvement.

In 2026, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 4.41 billion in the first quarter and then declined for two consecutive quarters, which widened the swings in quarterly earnings.

The company cited gains from managing its investment assets and revenue from a large project at its subsidiary Defocus as the background to the earnings volatility. On the financial side, the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 87.5% at the end of 2025, with both assets and liabilities increasing.

On shareholder returns, the company paid year-end dividends for the 16th consecutive term, was selected as a high-dividend company, and decided to cancel treasury shares.

The next points to check are the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report, whether Defocus revenue is reflected in the fourth quarter, and the share of non-operating income and losses in the first quarter of 2027.
