02

Business structure

Hybe is an entertainment company that runs record and music, concerts, merchandise and licensing, and content businesses centered on its artists.

Per the company's November 3, 2026 announcement, Q3 segment revenue was KRW 726.8 billion from concerts, KRW 334.6 billion from merchandise and licensing, KRW 282.8 billion from albums and music, and KRW 56.7 billion from content.

Revenue from direct artist participation reached KRW 1.08 trillion, about 70% of the total, while indirect participation revenue such as merchandise and licensing came to KRW 450.4 billion. BTS remains the largest earnings driver, and the company said its European and US tour drew about 70,000 attendees per show.

Management said reliance on BTS is declining as ENHYPEN, KATSEYE, &TEAM, CORTIS and TOMORROW X TOGETHER expand their global activities. Weverse serves as both a fan community and a merchandise sales channel, and the Weverse pickup service was cited as a factor behind the merchandise improvement.

Under its multi-home, multi-genre strategy, the company is localizing operations by market and developing Southeast Asian artists. Album sales for January to September 2026 totaled 27.97 million copies on a Circle Chart basis.

04

Earnings analysis

On a full-year basis, revenue rose from KRW 1.78 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.18 trillion in 2023 (up 22.6% year on year), KRW 2.26 trillion in 2024 (up 3.6%) and KRW 2.65 trillion in 2025 (up 17.5%), growing three years in a row from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 295.6 billion in 2023 (up 24.8%) and then fell 37.7% to KRW 184 billion in 2024 and 73.2% to KRW 49.3 billion in 2025, declining for two consecutive years.

Operating margin moved from 13.3% in 2022 and 13.6% in 2023 down to 8.2% in 2024 and 1.9% in 2025, so revenue growth did not carry through to profit.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 187.3 billion in 2023 before dropping to KRW 9.38 billion in 2024 and turning into a net loss of KRW 237.3 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 254.4 billion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder result. Operating cash flow declined from KRW 347.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 107.5 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, Q3 2025 posted an operating loss of KRW 42.2 billion and Q4 2025 an operating profit of KRW 3.97 billion, a swing to profit, before Q1 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 196.6 billion.

The company said that excluding one-time costs incurred in Q1, cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 417.9 billion; this is a company-announced figure, not a filed result.

Q2 2026 revenue reached KRW 1.45 trillion (up 107.6% quarter on quarter) with operating profit of KRW 170.9 billion, and controlling-shareholder net income turned positive at KRW 102.3 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 1.53 trillion and operating profit of KRW 188.5 billion were the highest quarterly figures on company announcement, with an operating margin of 12.4%.

However, Q3 still recorded a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 6.84 billion, and the last four quarters total a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 328.2 billion.

Personnel costs rose by more than KRW 150 billion from the prior quarter, which the CFO attributed mainly to conservatively accruing performance bonuses tied to strong results rather than headcount growth. Non-operating foreign exchange losses were also cited as weighing on Q3 net income.

05

Industry analysis

In media and entertainment, the core of revenue is artists' direct activity and fan spending. Per company materials, concerts and merchandise and licensing have grown together, so revenue concentrates in tour periods.

Concert revenue moves sharply quarter to quarter with tour schedules, which makes the position in the cycle a driver of earnings volatility. The company said BTS's tour will continue into the first half of 2027. In music and records, new and repackaged albums and streaming supported revenue.

Among listed peers, the closest comparisons are SM Entertainment, JYP Entertainment, YG Entertainment and CJ ENM, and Hybe emphasizes that several groups touring at once is its differentiator.

Local competition is intense in new markets such as India, Latin America and Southeast Asia, which the company identifies as an execution risk.

06

Outlook

The confirmable schedule the company has cited includes the BTS tour continuing into the first half of 2027, and the North America results of ENHYPEN's tour being reflected in results.

The company said its global label service will serve as marketing and promotion infrastructure across Korea, the Americas, Japan and India. Management said a Southeast Asian artist development project is in progress.

The company said it improved planning, production and distribution across merchandise to address the earlier pattern of sales concentrated at the start of tours. Reports said about one million fans visited merchandise sales points across 12 cities during BTS's 45-day North America tour, with 2 million items sold.

Management, however, disclosed only limited information about BTS's plans after the current tour, so the concert revenue outlook beyond 2027 is not clear.

The next regular report is the 2026 annual report, and the direction of full-year operating margin and controlling-shareholder net income in that filing is the next checkpoint.

08

Bull factors

Record quarterly results

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 1.53 trillion and operating profit of KRW 188.5 billion were the highest quarterly figures on company announcement. Concert revenue of KRW 726.8 billion was reported as up 196.7% year on year. Operating profit has now returned to profit in Q2 and Q3 2026 after the loss in Q1.

Shift in merchandise and licensing

Merchandise and licensing revenue reached KRW 334.6 billion in Q3, the highest quarterly level on record. Reports said about one million fans visited merchandise points during BTS's North America tour and 2 million items were sold.

The company said it improved planning, production and distribution to reduce the earlier concentration of sales at the start of tours.

Improving balance sheet

The debt ratio declined from 71.9% in 2023 to 54.5% in 2025. Controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 3.26 trillion within total equity of KRW 3.55 trillion at end-2025. Total liabilities of KRW 1.93 trillion mean the balance sheet has some capacity to absorb losses.

09

Bear factors

Dependence on the BTS tour

Much of Q3 growth came from the BTS tour. Management disclosed only limited information about BTS's plans after the current tour. Whether concert revenue holds after the tour ends cannot be judged from information disclosed so far.

Annual profit erosion and weaker cash flow

Operating margin fell to 1.9% in 2025 from 13.3% in 2022. The controlling-shareholder net loss was KRW 237.3 billion in 2025, and the last four quarters total KRW 328.2 billion. Operating cash flow also declined from KRW 347.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 107.5 billion in 2025.

Cost structure and FX pressure

Personnel costs rose by more than KRW 150 billion quarter on quarter in Q3, which the company attributed to performance bonuses accrued in advance. Non-operating foreign exchange losses were cited as weighing on net income. A larger share of concert revenue can change the cost structure and pressure margins.

10

Risk factors

Concentration in artist activity

If BTS's tour or group activities slow, concert and merchandise revenue could fall together. Whether global results from newer groups can fill that gap is the key question. The company stresses simultaneous activity across many artists, but the weight of a single group remains large.

FX and non-operating risk

Q3 operating margin held at 12.4% despite an unfavorable FX environment, but non-operating FX losses reduced net income. With a large foreign-currency revenue base, exchange-rate swings can distort the reading of results. Whether operating improvement carries through to net profit is the next point to check.

Competition and execution in new markets

Local competition is intense in expanding markets such as India, Latin America and Southeast Asia. The local artist development project is still in development. Converting fan interest into album and merchandise purchases has become critical to execution.

  2. Early 2027

  3. By March 31, 2027

12

Overall view

Hybe posted record quarterly revenue of KRW 1.53 trillion and operating profit of KRW 188.5 billion for Q3 2026 on company announcement. Much of the growth came from concert revenue tied to the BTS world tour, and merchandise and licensing also set a quarterly high.

On the other side, operating margin fell to 1.9% in 2025 and the company swung to a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 237.3 billion. The last four quarters total a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 328.2 billion, and Q3 still ended with a loss of KRW 6.84 billion.

The balance sheet improved, with the debt ratio at 54.5%, but operating cash flow has declined from 2022. The bull factors are the quarterly recovery and business diversification, while the bear factors are dependence on BTS and erosion of annual profit.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results and the direction of operating margin and controlling-shareholder net income in the 2026 annual report.
