02

Business structure

Jeju Semiconductor is a fabless designer of low-power memory chips, founded in 2000. It focuses on design and development, while wafer production is entrusted to foundries in Taiwan and elsewhere, and assembly and test are also outsourced.

The company moved its headquarters from Seoul to Jeju Island in 2005, but it operates no production plant on the island. Its product lineup covers MCP (multi-chip package), DRAM, NAND flash application products and legacy memory, serving IoT, consumer, mobile and automotive applications.

According to the company, mobile memory revenue began in earnest in 2025, and it continues to supply DRAM to smartphone makers in Greater China. It says it has more than 200 customers at home and abroad and is not concentrated on any single client.

Its technology certifications include LPDDR5 low-power DRAM for 5G IoT chipsets from Qualcomm and LPDDR4X NAND flash MCP for 5G RedCap chipsets from MediaTek.

A 2024 press report said that, excluding Micron, Jeju Semiconductor was the only memory fabless company to hold both the Qualcomm and MediaTek 5G IoT chipset certifications.

Samsung Electronics and SK hynix focus on high-capacity and HBM products, while Jeju Semiconductor targets the low-capacity, low-power memory market. The same report said low-capacity and specialty memory tends to see smaller price swings than high-capacity products.

Because it must secure foundry and assembly and test capacity from outside suppliers, supply-chain capability is the central variable for the business.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results moved from revenue of KRW 175 billion and operating profit of KRW 28.2 billion in 2022 to revenue of KRW 145.9 billion (down 16.6% year on year) and operating profit of KRW 17.8 billion (down 36.8%) in 2023.

Revenue recovered to KRW 162.3 billion in 2024 (up 11.3%), but operating profit fell to KRW 9.58 billion (down 46.3%), pushing the operating margin down to 5.9%.

In 2025, revenue reached KRW 302.2 billion (up 86.2%) and operating profit KRW 35.9 billion (up 274.4%), with the operating margin recovering to 11.9%; 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Controlling-shareholder net income rose two years in a row, from KRW 16.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 19.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 39.5 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 39.5 billion in 2025 and KRW 19.4 billion in 2024, which differs from the controlling-shareholder figure.

Operating cash flow turned from -KRW 9.68 billion in 2024 to KRW 25.6 billion in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 22.4% in 2024 to 47.9% in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue fell from KRW 111 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 91.8 billion in Q4 2025 (down 17.3% quarter on quarter), then rose for three consecutive quarters to KRW 180.5 billion, KRW 289.9 billion and KRW 346.1 billion in Q1 to Q3 2026.

Operating profit went to KRW 67.1 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 121.9 billion in Q2 and KRW 159.9 billion in Q3, and press reports put the Q3 margin at about 46%. Controlling-shareholder net income climbed to KRW 78.1 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 114.8 billion in Q2, then fell 41.4% to KRW 67.3 billion in Q3.

Because operating profit and net income moved in different directions in Q3, non-operating items and taxes are worth checking in the quarterly report. Controlling-shareholder net income for the four quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 totaled KRW 275.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The memory cycle hinges on the spread of AI servers. The company's chief executive said memory content per AI server is on average about four times that of a conventional server.

The same report said Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and Micron have concentrated production on high-value HBM, reducing general-purpose DRAM supply, and that demand for mid- and low-capacity memory moved to smaller fabless firms.

In China, unstable DRAM supply tied to US export restrictions reportedly pushed buyers looking for alternative suppliers toward Jeju Semiconductor. On pricing, TrendForce forecast in a May 2026 report that PC DRAM contract prices would rise 43% to 48% quarter on quarter in Q2 2026.

The cycle is in a price-rising phase, but a company official said that in a fabless model higher prices do not quickly become higher profitability.

Against peers, Samsung Electronics and SK hynix center on high-performance memory and HBM, while Jeju Semiconductor relies on outsourced production and focuses on low-capacity, low-power memory. That difference suggests profits may reflect price cycles at a different speed and magnitude. Industry analysts have noted that without in-house production, securing supply could become a constraint.

Outlook

In a report published on November 26, 2025, company management said Chinese DRAM supply was not keeping pace with demand and expected the trend to continue into 2026. In the same report, the company said 70% to 80% of its revenue comes from China.

An August 2026 report said the company received Qualcomm certification for LPDDR5 for 5G IoT chipsets. In a May 2026 report, a company official said it would expand memory applications to drones and robotics.

On the mobile side, a 2026 report said the company continues to supply DRAM to smartphone makers in Greater China. On supply, a February 2026 report said the company's ability to secure foundry, assembly and test capacity is limited, and that procuring outsourced volume could become harder.

The company said it will keep working on R&D and supply-chain security. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results and the 2026 annual report. The Q4 2026 preliminary figures will show whether the revenue and operating profit growth that began in Q1 2026 continues.

The annual report will provide audited full-year figures on China revenue share, the debt ratio and operating cash flow. The timing of any normalization in Chinese DRAM supply and the trend in general-purpose DRAM prices are also external variables that will shape earnings durability.

Bull factors

A Sharp Recovery in Quarterly Earnings Power

Operating profit in Q3 2026 was KRW 159.9 billion, up 1,050.1% year on year. It has risen for four consecutive quarters since Q4 2025, and the press reported Q2 and Q3 2026 as record quarterly results. The operating margin recovered to 11.9% in 2025 from 5.9% in 2024.

Certifications and a Broad Customer Base

Qualcomm's LPDDR5 certification for 5G IoT chipsets and MediaTek's LPDDR4X NAND flash MCP certification show the products are designed into global chipset platforms. The company reports more than 200 customers at home and abroad, across IoT, consumer, mobile and automotive uses. A May 2026 report described plans to extend applications to robotics and drones.

A Gap in General-Purpose Memory Supply

Analysts say the shift of general-purpose DRAM supply toward HBM has moved mid- and low-capacity demand to smaller fabless firms. TrendForce forecast in a May 2026 report that PC DRAM contract prices would rise 43% to 48% in Q2 2026. Buyers seeking alternatives amid Chinese DRAM supply disruption are also cited as a factor.

Bear factors

Limits to Profit Pass-Through in a Fabless Model

A company official said that because wafer production and assembly and test are outsourced, higher memory prices do not quickly translate into better margins. Analysts also noted that limited foundry and assembly and test capacity could restrict volume. The Q3 2026 operating margin of about 46% does not change this structure.

China Concentration and Concerns Over Temporary Demand

Management said 70% to 80% of revenue comes from China. Reports indicate that a significant part of this demand reflects buyers seeking alternatives amid Chinese DRAM supply disruption. If supply conditions change, the regional mix and earnings trajectory could shift together.

Diverging Quarterly Net Income

Controlling-shareholder net income in Q3 2026 was KRW 67.3 billion, down 41.4% from KRW 114.8 billion in Q2. Over the same period, operating profit rose from KRW 121.9 billion to KRW 159.9 billion.

In Q4 2025, controlling-shareholder net income also fell 33.6% from the prior quarter while operating profit was flat, showing a similar divergence.

Risk factors

Customer and Regional Concentration

Management says 70% to 80% of revenue comes from China, so a change in Chinese DRAM supply could reshape demand. The more than 200 customers offer diversification, but because supply disruption in China was reported as a driver of earnings, regional concentration remains a major exposure.

Reversal of the Memory Price Cycle

Forecasts reported for PC DRAM prices in Q2 2026 pointed to sharp increases, but price cycles can turn. If Samsung Electronics and SK hynix expand general-purpose DRAM output, supply and demand in the mid- and low-capacity market could shift. A price decline would also affect the earnings growth seen since 2025.

Outsourced Production and Balance Sheet

If access to outsourced wafer production and test capacity is limited, revenue growth could stall. The debt ratio rose to 47.9% at the end of 2025 from 22.4% in 2024, with total liabilities of KRW 108.9 billion.

Operating cash flow turned to KRW 25.6 billion in 2025 after being negative KRW 9.68 billion in 2024, so it has been volatile.

Overall view

Jeju Semiconductor is a memory fabless company whose annual results in 2025 and quarterly results in 2026 improved sharply from the 2023 low. Operating profit rose for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, and Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 159.9 billion was up 1,050.1% year on year.

Positive factors include the shift of mid- and low-capacity memory demand to smaller fabless firms after the spread of AI servers, Qualcomm and MediaTek certifications, and more than 200 customers.

Negative factors include the limited ability to pass higher prices into profit under an outsourced production model, the high share of revenue from China, and the 41.4% quarter-on-quarter drop in Q3 2026 controlling-shareholder net income.

The debt ratio rose to 47.9% at the end of 2025 from 22.4% in 2024, with total liabilities of KRW 108.9 billion. Because multiples shift considerably with the period chosen, the durability of earnings is the key point to watch.

The picture combines a documented earnings recovery with structural limits, and the next confirmations are the Q4 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report.
