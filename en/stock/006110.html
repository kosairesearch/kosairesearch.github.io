Sama Aluminium: Checking Whether the Profit Turn Lasts

Sama Aluminium returned to operating profit in the second quarter of 2026 and stayed profitable at the operating line in the third quarter, but a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.17 billion remained, so whether the turn lasts is the next thing to check.

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Sama Aluminium was founded in 1969 as an aluminum foil producer and listed on the Korea Exchange in 1980. Its core is the rolling division, which thins aluminum into foil, and the company said battery and ESS products are now the main line.

It holds one consolidated subsidiary, and its processing division makes retort packaging, ALU-ALU pharmaceutical packaging and films for vacuum insulation panels.

A corporate summary describes the rolling division as still loss-making on a slowing global economy and weak EV demand, while the electronic components sales division recorded operating profit on rising AI data center demand.

At its July 2026 corporate day, the company said it has secured 15 battery and automotive customers. The same session said one domestic battery customer's ESS volume alone can fill current utilization.

On competition, the company stressed that its key rolling mills come from a single German supplier and that control capability is the core. It also cited fire vulnerability in batteries as a reason foil production is difficult.

The company said it aims to widen sales into aluminum products for space and shipbuilding use, and that its aluminum strip in-house line has been completed.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 312.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 268 billion in 2023 (down 14.1% year on year) and KRW 251.7 billion in 2024 (down 6.1%), before recovering to KRW 271.5 billion in 2025 (up 7.8%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 22.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.77 billion in 2023 (down 83.3%), then turned into an operating loss of KRW 9.59 billion in 2024 and widened to an operating loss of KRW 17.6 billion in 2025. The company has posted losses for two consecutive years, 2024 and 2025.

The operating margin moved from 7.2% in 2022 to 1.4% in 2023, -3.8% in 2024 and -6.5% in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 17.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.41 billion in 2023 (down 80.2%), then became a net loss of KRW 9.36 billion in 2024 and KRW 24.9 billion in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests also stood at a net loss of KRW 24.9 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW -13 billion in 2022, KRW 13.8 billion in 2023 and KRW 5.19 billion in 2024, then turned to -KRW 4.97 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio (total liabilities divided by total equity) was 108.9% in 2022, 58.6% in 2023, 79.0% in 2024 and 107.4% in 2025. Quarterly, the operating loss was KRW 4.39 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 7.49 billion in the fourth quarter and KRW 4.39 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

In the second quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 96.9 billion (up 43.7% from the prior quarter) and operating profit was KRW 1.74 billion, a turn to profit.

In the third quarter, revenue was KRW 107.2 billion (up 60.8% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 914 million, while the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.17 billion.

The four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 add up to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 19.1 billion.

For the earlier losses, one brokerage pointed to weak EV demand that slowed lithium-ion battery foil output and a line mix shifted toward low-margin general products. For the 2026 recovery, the company cites higher utilization and a shift of low-margin volume to outsourcing toward higher-margin customers.

05

Industry analysis

Battery materials makers across Korea are struggling as growth in the electric vehicle market has slowed. Against that backdrop, the market is treating expansion of energy storage systems (ESS) and the restructuring of global battery supply chains as new demand drivers.

In an April 2026 report, SK Securities said ESS-driven LFP demand is rising sharply and that orders are increasing for Sama Aluminium because it can consolidate post-processing. That view rests on the fact that LFP cathode foil requires an extra post-processing step unlike the earlier ternary-cathode foil.

The same report projected average utilization in the battery value chain rising from 40% toward 70 to 80% in the second half, while Sama Aluminium's utilization was expected to exceed 90% in the second half. This is an estimate from April 2026 and should be read separately from later quarterly results.

At its July 2026 corporate day, the company claimed its utilization is the highest among battery materials, and said some industrial and processed-goods customers could face foil supply shortfalls in the second half.

On raw materials, the Middle East conflict has raised uncertainty over aluminum procurement, while one brokerage highlighted the company's preparation through a more stable aluminum supply chain.

Pricing pass-through also matters, since the company said raw material purchases follow prices from one month earlier while customer deliveries follow prices from six months earlier, so timing gaps can change the spread. Within Korean battery materials, the company said it is shifting its mix toward battery foil.

06

Outlook

The company's business plan targeted KRW 300 billion in revenue and an end to losses at the start of the year, but at its July 2026 corporate day it said the plan is being fully reset and that revenue could be raised to around KRW 400 billion. The company has described that target as not yet fixed.

On capacity, it said it is reviewing a new rolling mill and an additional carbon-coating line for post-processing. One brokerage said in an analysis published in the first half of 2026 that a new line would take about a year to reach commercial production.

The same analysis said the size of expansion and the orders secured against it would determine medium-term earnings. On diversification, the company said it has run samples with a Chinese battery maker over the past year, completed an on-site audit at its Pyeongtaek plant, and is discussing whether to collaborate.

It also set a goal of entering aluminum products for space and shipbuilding. On earnings, the company had expected a turn to profit in the second quarter and gradual margin improvement toward year-end, and the second-quarter operating profit has since been confirmed by filing.

The company also said spreads would widen in earnest from the third quarter. Start-up timing for the new line and the date for a fixed business plan have not been disclosed. The next thing to watch is whether capacity decisions and customer contracts appear in filings or investor communications.

Bull factors

ESS demand and utilization recovery

Operating profit turned to KRW 1.74 billion in the second quarter of 2026, and third-quarter revenue reached KRW 107.2 billion, up 60.8% year on year. In April 2026, SK Securities projected that Sama Aluminium's utilization would exceed 90% in the second half. The brokerage's main argument rests on rising ESS volume.

Customer diversification and capacity review

The company said it has secured 15 battery and automotive customers. It has completed an on-site audit at its Pyeongtaek plant with a Chinese battery maker and is discussing collaboration. It also said a new rolling mill and a carbon-coating line are under review.

Raw material integration and equipment barriers

The company said its aluminum strip in-house line has been completed. It also said its key rolling mills come from a single German supplier and that control capability is central. It stressed that foil production is difficult because of fire vulnerability in batteries, which it presents as a technical barrier.

Bear factors

Consecutive losses and shrinking equity

After an operating loss of KRW 9.59 billion in 2024, the 2025 operating loss widened to KRW 17.6 billion. Total equity fell from KRW 243.6 billion in 2024 to KRW 217.9 billion in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 234.1 billion, and the debt ratio was 107.4%.

Net losses despite operating profit

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 914 million, but the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.17 billion. The four-quarter total of that net loss is KRW 19.1 billion. One brokerage noted that rolling-division utilization recovered to 66% in the fourth quarter of 2025.

EV demand stall and general-product mix

Battery materials makers across Korea are struggling with slower EV market growth. One brokerage said lithium-ion battery foil output weakened during the EV slowdown and that lines shifted toward low-margin general products. Changing that mix requires ESS orders to continue.

Risk factors

Customer concentration

The company said one domestic battery customer's ESS volume alone can fill current utilization. A drop in orders from that customer could move utilization and revenue together. SK Securities listed a deeper shipment slump at battery customers as a risk.

Raw materials and price spread

An SK Securities report flagged the risk that a prolonged Middle East conflict could disrupt aluminum procurement. The company said raw material costs follow one-month-old prices while deliveries follow six-month-old prices, so timing gaps can shift the spread.

It also said some industrial and processed-goods customers could face foil supply shortfalls in the second half.

Financial load and capacity execution

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 234.1 billion, above total equity of KRW 217.9 billion. The debt ratio rose from 79.0% in 2024 to 107.4% in 2025. One brokerage noted a new line takes about a year to reach commercial production and that expansion size and secured orders will determine medium-term earnings.

Overall view

Sama Aluminium posted losses in 2024 and 2025 and returned to operating profit of KRW 1.74 billion in the second quarter of 2026.

It stayed profitable at the operating line in the third quarter with KRW 914 million, but a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.17 billion remained, so the recovery is not yet complete. Revenue of KRW 107.2 billion in the third quarter, up 60.8% year on year, marks a change in direction.

Bullish factors are ESS demand, 15 customers, and improvement potential in business indicators such as capacity and raw material integration under review. Bearish factors are shrinking equity after consecutive losses, a 107.4% debt ratio, dependence on one customer and raw material spread risk.

Brokerage views from April 2026 assumed higher utilization, and the company's plan upgrade was still unconfirmed as of July 2026. On valuation, the price-to-earnings ratio cannot be calculated and the price-to-book ratio sits at a premium to net asset value, so earnings sustainability is the key variable.

The next checkpoints are full-year 2026 results in the annual report and whether operating profit stays positive in the Q1 2027 quarterly report. This report is for information only and does not include investment advice.
