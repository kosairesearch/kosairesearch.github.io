Report overview

Profit Recovery on Room Rates; Jeju and Q4 Are the Watch Points

GS P&L moved from an operating loss in 2024 to operating profit in 2025 on rising foreign visitor demand and room rates, and operating profit has risen for two consecutive quarters since Q2 2026. Third-quarter operating profit, however, fell short of consensus, so the Jeju hotels and Q4 2026 results are the next checkpoints.

02

Business structure

GS P&L is a holding company spun off from GS Retail through an inspection-free split in December 2024, with the hotel operator Parnas Hotel as its core subsidiary. Parnas Hotel runs five-star properties including Grand InterContinental Seoul Parnas in Samseong-dong, The Westin Seoul Parnas, and Parnas Hotel Jeju.

It also operates six Nine Tree by Parnas business hotels in areas such as Myeongdong, Insa-dong and Dongdaemun, and earns rental income from Parnas Tower and Parnas Mall. A food-supply subsidiary serves hotel, restaurant and catering channels, and its link to contract catering is cited as a source of synergy.

According to the information provider Butler, property-level revenue shares for Q2 2026 were 37% Grand Hotel, 25% COEX Hotel, 19% Nine Tree Hotels, 12% Parnas Tower and 8% Jeju Hotel. These are aggregated property-level figures rather than segment disclosures by the company, so they should be read as reference points.

The Westin Seoul Parnas reopened in September 2025 with its room count reduced from 656 to 564, and a pre-reopening report projected average daily rates would rise by more than 50% from earlier levels. Hotel revenue comes from room sales, food and beverage and other facility income, and rental income.

Brokerage reports note that high-end hotels carry a large share of the company's profit structure. Competition centers on five-star properties in central Seoul and Jeju's prime locations, and limited new hotel supply is cited as a factor supporting room rates.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results changed sharply between 2024 and 2025. Revenue rose from KRW 38.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 481.7 billion in 2025, up 1,156.9% year on year, while operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 38.2 million in 2024 to KRW 78 billion in 2025, a turn to profit.

The 2024 base covers only December 2024, the month after the spin-off, so the size of the growth rate should be read with that in mind. The operating margin rose from -0.1% in 2024 to 16.2% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a loss of KRW 932 million in 2024 to KRW 19.4 billion in 2025, while total net income was KRW 29.9 billion in 2025, above the controlling-shareholder figure. Operating cash flow shifted from -KRW 4.62 billion in 2024 to KRW 119.6 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 122 billion and operating profit of KRW 19.9 billion rose to KRW 153.1 billion and KRW 31.3 billion in Q4 2025. Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 130.4 billion fell 14.8% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 24.2 billion fell 22.8%, marking the quarterly low.

Revenue then reached KRW 159 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 166.1 billion in Q3 2026, with operating profit of KRW 29.4 billion and KRW 41.3 billion, and operating profit has risen for two consecutive quarters.

The 107.3% year-on-year rise in Q3 operating profit was attributed to a base effect from the reopening of The Westin Seoul Parnas and expanded attraction of foreign guests.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 80.2% quarter on quarter to KRW 1.67 billion in Q4 2025, then rose to KRW 9.43 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 12.8 billion in Q2 and KRW 18.8 billion in Q3, a three-quarter run of increases. The trailing four quarters total KRW 42.7 billion.

In Q4 2025, operating profit rose while controlling-shareholder net income fell, so the link between the two is a point to verify in the notes to the annual report. Third-quarter operating profit came in 4.3% below the FnGuide consensus.

05

Industry analysis

The main demand driver is inbound foreign travel. According to a press report citing the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, foreign visitors to Korea reached 10.71 million in the first half of 2026, up 21% year on year and a record. New hotel supply remains limited, and room rates have continued to rise.

IBK Securities expects limited supply growth and continued foreign demand, and said room-rate gains could persist. Domestic demand varies by quarter depending on the timing of the Chuseok holiday. One analysis noted that the Chuseok timing shift meant domestic tourists made up a smaller share in Q3 2026.

Rising demand alongside constrained supply is common across the hotel sector, but the company's concentration of five-star assets in Seoul's Samseong-dong and Myeongdong-Dongdaemun areas and in Jeju is a differentiating feature.

Commentary also notes the company can focus on its hotel business after the holding-company split. Assessing its position relative to peers requires comparing supply plans and room-rate trends across competing hotels.

Because foreign demand depends on visitor flows from specific countries, monthly arrival statistics are a key gauge for the sector.

06

Outlook

The company's medium-term direction is laid out in its corporate value-up plan disclosed on November 4, 2026. In that plan, it set a separate-basis payout ratio of at least 50% as a target and aimed to lower the debt ratio to 90% or below by 2028.

The company official said The Westin Seoul Parnas is settling into the market after its reopening, and cited steady performance gains at existing hotels and stable growth in rental income. IBK Securities expects a temporary renovation at Parnas Hotel Jeju in Q4 2026 could slow the growth rate.

The same brokerage estimated operating profit at KRW 135 billion for 2026 and KRW 153.6 billion for 2027. For that estimate to hold, Q4 2026 operating profit would need to rise meaningfully from the prior quarter, which is the next key question.

A 2025 report described entry into senior housing as a medium-term initiative. The most direct checkpoints are whether foreign demand and room rates hold up, and whether room metrics recover after the Jeju renovation.

The pace at which the debt ratio moves toward the 2028 target can be tracked in the annual report and quarterly financial data.

08

Bull factors

Foreign demand and room rates

Record foreign visitor demand underpins room demand. Limited new supply in Seoul's core areas lets room-rate increases lift profit. Because high-end hotels hold a large share of the profit structure, attracting foreign guests feeds directly into profitability.

Profit recovery after the Westin reopening

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 41.3 billion, up 107.3% year on year. Operating profit has risen for two consecutive quarters starting from Q2 2026, and controlling-shareholder net income has increased for three consecutive quarters since Q1 2026. The recovery from the Q4 2025 low is visible quarter by quarter.

Shareholder return plan and cash generation

The company set a target of a separate-basis payout ratio of at least 50%. Operating cash flow turned strongly positive in 2025. Profit growth and cash generation appearing together is the basis for the return target.

Bear factors

Q3 miss versus consensus and a 52-week low

The Q3 operating profit shortfall against consensus is the starting point of the subsequent share-price pressure. On November 5, 2026, the day after the results were released, the share price fell to a 52-week low, and the gap versus expectations was cited as the reason for selling. Even with strong growth, results below market expectations can produce a separate share-price reaction.

Jeju room metrics and renovation

An NH Investment & Securities analyst said Jeju property metrics in September fell somewhat short of expectations. Average daily rate rose sharply after the renovation, but short-term visitor sentiment weakened on local safety concerns, leaving room occupancy at 88.2%. IBK Securities said a temporary renovation at Parnas Hotel Jeju in Q4 2026 could slow the growth rate.

Target price cut

IBK Securities said on November 6, 2026 that it lowered its target price from KRW 80,000 to KRW 70,000. It said third-quarter revenue and operating profit fell about 5% short of market expectations, and that the target adjustment reflected changes to its earnings estimates.

10

Risk factors

Concentration in foreign demand

Hotel revenue depends heavily on foreign visitor demand and room rates, so a drop in arrivals or a change in conditions could move occupancy and rates together. One analysis noted that the Chuseok timing shift reduced the share of domestic tourists in Q3 2026. The balance between domestic and foreign demand is a variable that shapes earnings volatility.

Property concentration and Jeju volatility

According to Butler's aggregation, the Jeju hotel accounts for about 8% of revenue, but renovation and safety concerns together could amplify short-term volatility. IBK Securities said the Q4 Jeju renovation could slow growth.

Because core Seoul hotels carry a large share, an operating disruption at any one of them can affect overall results.

Financial leverage

The debt ratio rose to 95.3% at the end of 2025 from 83.5% in 2024, and the company has set a target of 90% or lower by 2028. Total net income for 2025 was KRW 29.9 billion, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 19.4 billion, so profit should be assessed on the controlling-shareholder basis. The relationship between borrowing and the pace of profit growth should be checked in the annual report.

  4. End of March 2027

    Around the statutory deadline for the 2026 annual report, check the trend in the debt ratio, whether the payout target was implemented, and the gap between controlling-shareholder net income and total net income. These figures show how far the value-up plan is being carried out.

12

Overall view

GS P&L turned to operating profit of KRW 78 billion in 2025, and Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 41.3 billion rose 107.3% year on year. The improvement rests on rising foreign visitor demand and higher room rates, and the reopening of The Westin Seoul Parnas amplified the base effect in Q3.

On the other hand, Q3 operating profit fell 4.3% short of consensus, and the share price hit a 52-week low on November 5, 2026. Room metrics at the Jeju property and a slower growth rate from the Q4 2026 renovation are cited as near-term variables.

On the financial side, the debt ratio of 95.3% at the end of 2025 remains above the 90% target for 2028, and the company set a separate-basis payout ratio of at least 50% as a goal.

Controlling-shareholder net income has risen for three consecutive quarters since Q1 2026, with a trailing four-quarter total of KRW 42.7 billion. IBK Securities said on November 6, 2026 that it cut its target price to KRW 70,000 while keeping a buy rating.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results, room metrics after the Jeju renovation, and the debt ratio and dividend implementation in the 2026 annual report.
