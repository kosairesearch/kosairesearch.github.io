02

Business structure

The Korea Economic Broadcasting operates through three segments: broadcasting, an internet business, and other ancillary operations. The broadcasting segment centers on TV advertising sponsorship and viewing fees, and the company distributes its economic news channel across multiple devices.

The internet segment is built on securities information platforms such as Wownet, Jusikchang and the US stock service, with revenue generated partly from expert broadcasts. The other segment groups education, the Wow Stock Loan service, K-VINA business center and content production and distribution.

In the 2022 quarterly report, based on the prior-year financial statements, the segment shares were 26.9% broadcasting, 63.4% internet and 9.7% other.

FnGuide's company profile instead shows 48.8% broadcasting, 47.1% investment information and 4.1% other, so because classification and reference dates differ across sources, the weights are better read as ranges.

Depending on the source, the broadcasting or internet side appears larger, but securities information platforms are a major pillar in every view. In broadcasting, the company competes with other economic news channels, while in the internet segment it overlaps with rival securities information platforms.

Because it earns both advertising and investment-information revenue, it is exposed to both the ad cycle and stock trading activity. Unlike terrestrial or general programming channels, the securities information platform forms a large part of its business structure, which is its structural differentiator.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue and profit both declining from 2022. Revenue fell from KRW 88.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 82.3 billion in 2023 (down 7.1% year on year), KRW 79.5 billion in 2024 (down 3.4%) and KRW 75.9 billion in 2025 (down 4.5%), a three-year consecutive decline from 2023 to 2025.

Operating profit dropped from KRW 8.44 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.66 billion in 2023 (down 44.7%) and KRW 1.13 billion in 2024 (down 75.8%), and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 2.38 billion in 2025.

Revenue declined at single-digit rates, while operating profit fell by roughly 40% to 75% in each year, so the profit shock was far larger than the sales decline.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 13.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.7 billion in 2023, KRW 8.45 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.79 billion in 2025 (down 67.0%). Net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 2.79 billion in 2025 as disclosed.

The operating margin declined from 9.5% in 2022 to 5.7% in 2023, 1.4% in 2024 and -3.1% in 2025. At the end of 2025 the debt ratio was 13.1%, total equity of KRW 147.8 billion was entirely attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 19.4 billion.

Operating cash flow moved from -KRW 279 million in 2022 to KRW 11.1 billion in 2023, then KRW 6.68 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.63 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating losses of KRW 821 million in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 1.7 billion in the fourth quarter were followed by operating profit of KRW 540 million in the first quarter of 2026, a turn to profit.

In the second quarter of 2026 revenue was KRW 29.3 billion and operating profit KRW 3.14 billion, up 481.4% from the prior quarter.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 22.8 billion rose 24.1% year on year, yet operating profit was only KRW 193 million and profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 500 million.

Over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 5.81 billion, and the split of quarterly swings by cause cannot be drawn from these figures alone.

Weak Ad and Trading Backdrop

If advertising and equity trading weaken together, the company's two revenue sources could limit the scope of any recovery.

Risk factors

Advertising Cycle Risk

The broadcasting segment depends partly on TV sponsorship income, so a weak ad market feeds directly into revenue. Revenue has fallen for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. The outdoor advertising business intended to broaden the ad base has not yet shown its results in reported figures. If ad volumes and rates do not recover, the decline in the broadcasting segment could continue.

Dependence on Trading Value

The internet segment centers on securities information services such as Wownet and Jusikchang, so it is sensitive to trading value. The company has explained that this segment grew strongly during past stock-market booms, and the reverse also applies when trading value falls. In third-quarter 2026 results, another brokerage reported lower commissions as trading value declined.

Quarterly Earnings Volatility

Operating profit swung from KRW 540 million in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 3.14 billion in the second and KRW 193 million in the third. Because annual operating profit is small, modest changes in costs or revenue have a large effect on the margin.

The shift from operating losses in late 2025 to profits in 2026 has not yet lasted several quarters, which makes earnings stability hard to judge.

Overall view

The Korea Economic Broadcasting has seen revenue decline for three consecutive years since 2022, with the operating margin falling from 9.5% to -3.1% and an operating loss of KRW 2.38 billion in 2025.

In 2026 the company returned to quarterly operating profit, with KRW 540 million in the first quarter and KRW 3.14 billion in the second, but profit fell to KRW 193 million in the third quarter, so the durability of the recovery is not yet established.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio is 13.1%, total equity of KRW 147.8 billion is entirely attributable to controlling shareholders, and operating cash flow was positive in 2025.

The business rests on two pillars, broadcasting advertising and securities information platforms, so it is exposed to both the ad cycle and trading value.

Weak advertising and lower trading value at the same time form the current headwind, while a rebound in trading value and expansion of the ad base through outdoor advertising are the offsetting factors.

On valuation, the market capitalization is large relative to profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 5.81 billion over the last four quarters, while the price-to-book ratio sits below 1x.

The next checkpoints are operating profit in the 2026 annual report and whether operating profit recovers in the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report.
