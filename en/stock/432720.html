02

Business structure

Qualitas Semiconductor was founded in 2017 as a designer of high-speed interconnect semiconductors and listed on KOSDAQ in 2023. Its core business is interface IP licensing, in which it supplies design IP blocks to customers and earns usage fees, and it also offers design services.

The product lines are MIPI for mobile and embedded use, PCIe for high-speed data transfer, and UCIe for chiplet-to-chiplet connection, all built on SerDes technology. According to Kyobo Securities research, the 2025 cumulative revenue mix was MIPI 50%, PCIe 18%, and chipset 31%.

A Bloter report put the first-half 2026 mix at MIPI 36%, PCIe 38.5%, and UCIe 14.1%, showing UCIe gaining share quickly. Because sources use different segment classifications, some reports show different mixes for the same period. Counterparties in 2026 disclosed contracts include SK hynix, Coasia Semi, and Mobilint.

Overseas, the company supplied PCIe 6.0 and CXL 3.0 IP to VeriSilicon in the United States in early 2025 and signed an additional deal with edge AI chipmaker Ambarella.

Competition is concentrated among a few firms, and only a small number, including larger overseas IP vendors, are reported to have completed silicon verification of UCIe IP. In January 2026 the company established a U.S. subsidiary, Qualitas Semiconductor, Inc., and acquired 100% of its shares.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 10.8 billion in 2022, KRW 10.8 billion in 2023 (down 0.1% year on year), KRW 6.07 billion in 2024 (down 43.6%), and KRW 5.35 billion in 2025 (down 11.9%), a decline for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. Among the years from 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest and 2025 the smallest.

The operating loss widened from KRW 3.67 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.2 billion in 2023, KRW 22.7 billion in 2024, and KRW 24.5 billion in 2025, a loss in each of four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025. The operating margin deteriorated from -34.0% in 2022 to -103.8% in 2023, -373.6% in 2024, and -457.4% in 2025.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened from KRW 2.28 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.11 billion in 2023, KRW 19.1 billion in 2024, and KRW 23.1 billion in 2025, and the consolidated net loss including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts.

Margin pressure has come from R&D costs hitting the income statement in full while revenue shrank. The company expenses all development costs rather than capitalizing them, and first-half 2026 R&D spending was KRW 12 billion, equal to 105.2% of revenue (Bloter report).

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 842 million in Q3 2025 to KRW 1.71 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 5.15 billion in Q1 2026, and KRW 6.34 billion in Q2 2026, then fell 46.5% quarter on quarter to KRW 3.4 billion in Q3 2026.

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 6.57 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 865 million in Q2 2026, then widened to KRW 5.97 billion in Q3 2026. The company has reported losses for five consecutive quarters, from Q3 2025 through Q3 2026.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.88 billion in Q3 2026, and the total for the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, was KRW 19.7 billion. Total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 36.3 billion and total liabilities were KRW 46.7 billion, a debt ratio of 128.6%.

05

Industry analysis

The IP market that Qualitas Semiconductor serves is seeing rising demand for high-speed interfaces as AI chips spread. The company is expanding PCIe and UCIe IP aimed at high-value applications such as AI, HPC, and automotive.

In an April 2026 report, Kyobo Securities saw domestic-substitution demand for IP and wider AI chip development as drivers of top-line growth. The same report said a turnaround would still be insufficient in 2026 and forecast 2026 revenue of KRW 10.2 billion and an operating loss of KRW 11.5 billion.

First-half 2026 revenue of KRW 11.5 billion has already exceeded that revenue forecast. The core assets are IP based on Samsung Electronics foundry 4, 5, and 8 nanometer processes, and foundry utilization directly affects the company's revenue.

Truefriend Securities judged that 2025 results would inevitably decline because of weak Samsung foundry utilization and tariff risk. In the same period, escalating U.S.-China tariff tensions led to the cancellation of a Chinese customer project that was under way at the Samsung foundry.

In competition, the small number of firms that have completed silicon verification for UCIe IP creates a barrier to entry. Still, overseas IP vendors such as Rambus, CEVA, eMemory, and M31 compete in the same interface space.

A company official said development achievements are now connecting to orders and revenue, a positive cycle (Bloter report).

06

Outlook

The largest confirmed order flow is the IP supply contract with SK hynix. Through a revised disclosure, the total contract value rose from KRW 2.42 billion to KRW 3.92 billion, and the contract runs through March 16, 2027.

Contracts disclosed in 2026 started with a KRW 1 billion deal in January and included KRW 2.4 billion and KRW 1.8 billion contracts with SK hynix in March and April, totaling KRW 8.6 billion; including a later Mobilint contract, the total exceeds KRW 9.2 billion (Bloter report).

The company stresses that orders for PCIe and UCIe IP are translating into revenue. The first-half 2026 mix, with PCIe at 38.5% and UCIe at 14.1%, is consistent with the rising share of high-value IP in reported results.

Overseas, a U.S. subsidiary was approved in January 2026, and in March the company paid in USD 300,000 in cash to acquire 100% of its shares (business report).

In government projects, the company was selected in April 2026 for a system semiconductor technology development program and in May 2026 for the Credit Hero 50 program of the Korea Credit Guarantee Fund (company profile records).

The company said it could consider capitalizing development costs once a track record builds and the requirements are met (Bloter report). Results for 2026 will be fixed in the annual business report, due by the end of March 2027, which will include Q4 2026 figures.

Because short-term borrowing has risen, whether operating cash flow improves from 2027 onward will shape the financing structure.

08

Bull factors

Revenue Growth and Rising High-Value IP Mix

First-half 2026 revenue reached KRW 11.5 billion, up 311% year on year (Bloter report). UCIe rose from 0.8% of revenue in 2025 to 14.1% in the first half of 2026. A 2025 Truefriend Securities report said rising sales of high-value PCIe and UCIe IP could come with better profitability.

Expanded SK hynix Supply Contract

A revised disclosure raised the value of the SK hynix IP supply contract above its original amount. Industry reports said the parties adjusted the contract value as they upgraded the performance of the supplied IP (The Elec report). The contract runs through March 16, 2027, with payment made in three installments.

Domestic Substitution and AI Chip Demand

In an April 2026 report, Kyobo Securities cited domestic-substitution benefits for IP and wider AI chip development demand as drivers of top-line growth. The same report mentioned medium- to long-term structural improvement based on domestic-substitution references. Expansion of optical communication projects was also named as a future revenue factor.

09

Bear factors

A Cost Structure Where R&D Exceeds Revenue

First-half 2026 R&D spending was KRW 12 billion, equal to 105.2% of revenue (Bloter report). Because all development costs are expensed rather than capitalized, development investment hits the income statement immediately. The 2025 operating loss was KRW 24.5 billion, an operating margin of -457.4%.

Quarterly Revenue Volatility and Renewed Losses

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 3.4 billion, down 46.5% from the prior quarter. The operating loss widened from KRW 865 million in Q2 to KRW 5.97 billion in Q3, reversing the narrowing of the prior quarter. The reported losses have now run for five consecutive quarters.

Cash Outflows and Rising Borrowing

Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW -16.5 billion. Short-term borrowings rose from KRW 5 billion at year-end 2025 to KRW 12 billion at the end of the first half of 2026, and KRW 4.4 billion of cash flowed out from operations in the first half (Bloter report). Total liabilities of KRW 46.7 billion at year-end 2025 exceed total equity of KRW 36.3 billion.

10

Risk factors

Customer and Process Concentration

Results are affected by utilization at Samsung Electronics' foundry processes. In 2025, tariff tensions led to the cancellation of a Chinese customer project, which fed directly into results. The pace at which the company broadens its revenue base across other processes and customers remains a risk factor.

Financial Capacity

The debt ratio rose from 26.4% in 2024 to 128.6% at year-end 2025. In the first half of 2026, short-term funding came from a KRW 7 billion loan from IBK and the use of a credit guarantee facility from the Korea Credit Guarantee Fund (Bloter report). If operating cash outflows continue, the burden of additional financing will grow.

Accounting Treatment and Technology Transition

The company currently expenses all development costs, but has said it could consider capitalization once a track record builds and requirements are met (Bloter report). A switch to capitalization would change how the income statement should be read.

Speed in bringing next-generation interface standards to silicon and competition with global IP vendors remain challenges.

12

Overall view

Qualitas Semiconductor is a fabless company supplying high-speed interface IP. Its 2025 revenue fell to KRW 5.35 billion, but first-half 2026 revenue rose sharply to KRW 11.5 billion. The growth reflects a larger share of high-value PCIe and UCIe IP and expanded contracts with customers such as SK hynix.

However, the operating loss was KRW 24.5 billion in 2025 and widened to KRW 5.97 billion in Q3 2026, so revenue gains have not yet translated into improved profitability. R&D costs that exceed revenue, together with the expense treatment of development costs, are driving the income statement.

Cash flow and borrowing are also burdens, with operating cash flow at KRW -16.5 billion in 2025 and short-term borrowings at KRW 12 billion at the end of the first half of 2026.

Strengths include the shift in business mix, expanding supply contracts, and domestic-substitution and AI demand, while weaknesses include persistent operating losses, quarterly revenue swings, and rising borrowing. Both sets of factors coexist.

Market capitalization is large relative to equity and revenue, and no earnings-based multiple can be calculated. The next checkpoints are the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual business report and the expiry of the SK hynix contract period on March 16, 2027.
