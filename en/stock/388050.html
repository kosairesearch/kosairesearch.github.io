Report overview

Public-Sector Orders Underpin Profitability; Nuclear and ESS Are the Next Tests

G2Power has kept quarterly operating profit positive since the third quarter of 2025 on public-sector switchgear and solar orders, and whether nuclear switchgear and immersion-cooled ESS convert into revenue will shape its next growth phase.

02

Business structure

G2Power supplies power equipment by combining switchgear, solar power generation systems, energy storage systems (ESS), inverters and condition monitoring diagnosis (CMD) technology into a smart grid offering.

The company was founded in 2010, listed on KOSDAQ in 2022, set up the solar SPC G2Energy in 2023, and began commercial operation in 2024, extending into renewable energy operation and management. Its product mix shifted considerably over time.

In the first half of 2025, switchgear accounted for 71.66% of sales, solar power generation systems for 20.32% and ESS for 5.06%, while in the first quarter of 2026 solar systems made up 53.72%, switchgear 42.05% and ESS 3.22%.

Its core switchgear customers are public institutions and local governments, the public procurement market. The company said its CMD technology was recognized by government agencies as a new technology and product, and that it holds the number one share of public-sector switchgear.

Kiwoom Securities also analyzed that its share of switchgear at the Public Procurement Service stands at 6%, the top position. In nuclear, the company signed a KRW 43.0 billion high-voltage switchgear contract for Shin-Hanul Units 3 and 4 in February 2025 and is also bidding on Hanbit Units 3 and 4.

It is widening its reach into private-sector and overseas customers and set up G2 America to target North America. The immersion-cooled ESS is being developed under a national R&D project, and the company said it buys battery cells from Samsung SDI and LG Energy Solution.

In public procurement, certifications such as KEPIC and Q-Class and the designation as a procurement-excellent product lead to negotiated contracts, which act as an entry barrier.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 37.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 49.4 billion in 2023 (up 32.2% year on year), KRW 55.4 billion in 2024 (up 12.2%) and KRW 77.4 billion in 2025 (up 39.6%).

Revenue has grown for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year and 2022 the smallest in the 2022–2025 period.

Operating profit went from KRW 204 million in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 945 million in 2023, turning to a profit of KRW 3.67 billion in 2024 and reaching KRW 8.81 billion in 2025, up 139.9%. The operating margin moved from 0.5% in 2022 to -1.9% in 2023, 6.6% in 2024 and 11.4% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 736 million in 2022, KRW 671 million in 2023 (down 8.8%), KRW 4.21 billion in 2024 (up 527.4%) and KRW 5.55 billion in 2025 (up 31.9%), rising for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests is reported separately and carries the same amounts as controlling-shareholder net income in each year. Operating cash flow moved from negative KRW 2.81 billion in 2022 to KRW 18 billion in 2025. Quarterly results are more uneven.

Revenue of KRW 37.5 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, up 139.9% from the prior quarter, was the highest of the last five quarters, with operating profit of KRW 6.3 billion.

Revenue fell 48.9% quarter on quarter to KRW 19.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and 45.7% to KRW 10.4 billion in the second quarter, then recovered to KRW 14.8 billion in the third quarter, up 42.2%.

Third-quarter revenue was down 5.4% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 1.73 billion fell 22.8% year on year. Controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 to third quarter of 2026) totals KRW 6.79 billion.

Public-project delivery and inspection timing is cited as the main driver of quarterly swings.

The company attributed the first-quarter 2025 swing into loss to valuation losses from delayed delivery on a large project, and a brokerage analyzed that faster solar inspections and budget execution in the first quarter of 2026 pulled forward revenue that had previously concentrated in the fourth quarter.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for the company's products is tied to policy and power infrastructure spending. A March 2026 report cited nuclear order expectations, government-led power infrastructure investment and rising power demand from AI as the drivers behind the share price rally.

Because public procurement makes up a large share of the business, analysts see public orders translating directly into order growth. Kyobo Securities presented 8.1% and 9.4% as the growth rates of government and local government budgets for 2026 and read this as widening G2Power's order pool.

Solar demand is linked to renewable energy targets: Kiwoom Securities said reaching 100 GW of renewable capacity by 2030 requires annual solar additions of more than 10 GW a year.

Press reports estimate the market for high- and low-voltage switchgear tenders from five power generation companies at more than KRW 200 billion. On the cycle, the backlog of KRW 106.9 billion at end-March 2026 exceeded 2025 revenue of KRW 77.4 billion, which supports visibility.

The competitive position is strongest in public procurement, where the company holds the top share in switchgear at the Public Procurement Service and ranked first in switchgear and ESS in the 2025 procurement market.

Nuclear switchgear is at an early stage, with one Shin-Hanul contract as its reference, and no large private-sector results have yet appeared.

06

Outlook

Kiwoom Securities, in a June 2, 2026 report, projected 2026 revenue of KRW 105.0 billion and operating profit of KRW 13.3 billion, implying increases of 36% and 51% year on year.

The same report said new order events, including commercialization of the immersion-cooled ESS and high-voltage switchgear tenders for nuclear power plants, are lined up for the second half of 2026. Kyobo Securities estimated 2026 revenue at KRW 104.4 billion in a February 2026 report.

Hana Securities, after the first-quarter 2026 results, said the company is entering a stage in which nuclear deliveries and immersion-cooled ESS commercialization add to a public-sector profit base, and that second-half order results would mark a turning point for re-rating.

Shinyoung Securities expected 2026 full-year results to exceed the prior year on expanded solar and ESS sales. Hana Securities also noted that more than half of public-sector revenue falls in the second half, so the annual earnings pattern will be more pronounced than the first half.

The Shin-Hanul Units 3 and 4 switchgear, worth about KRW 43.0 billion, is set to begin contributing revenue from 2027.

Reports say the Yongin Techno Valley site is scheduled to start construction in the second half of 2026 and finish in 2027, with capital expenditure of roughly KRW 20 billion and capacity four to five times that of the head office.

Shinyoung Securities' report described a plan to complete certification and commercialization of the immersion-cooled ESS in the first half of 2026 and begin mass production and delivery in the second half. As of the February 2026 Kyobo report, overseas business was described as stalled.

08

Bull factors

Order competitiveness proven in the public sector

Kiwoom Securities analyzed that the company holds a 6% share of switchgear at the Public Procurement Service, ranked first, while Shinyoung Securities noted a number one share in switchgear and ESS in the 2025 procurement market.

Designation as a procurement-excellent product and NEP certification allow negotiated contracts, which Shinyoung described as a more favorable order structure than general manufacturers enjoy.

Shinyoung also pointed to the KRW 106.9 billion backlog at end-March 2026, which exceeds 2025 revenue, as a basis for visibility.

Nuclear switchgear widens the business scope

The KRW 43.0 billion Shin-Hanul Units 3 and 4 high-voltage switchgear contract became the company's first reference in the nuclear equipment market. Its revenue is expected to be recognized in stages over three years starting in 2027.

Press reports put the high- and low-voltage switchgear tender market for five power generation companies at more than KRW 200 billion, and the company is also participating in additional bids, including for Hanbit Units 3 and 4.

Recovering earnings power and improving cash generation

The operating margin rose from 0.5% in 2022 to 11.4% in 2025, and operating cash flow moved from negative KRW 2.81 billion in 2022 to KRW 18 billion in 2025. Shinyoung Securities analyzed that the solar system cost ratio fell year on year in the first quarter of 2026, improving profitability. Operating profit in the first quarter of 2026 turned to a profit from a loss in the same period a year earlier.

09

Bear factors

Seasonality and large quarterly swings

Because more than half of public-sector revenue falls in the second half, visibility into first-half results is limited. Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 10.4 billion fell 45.7% from the prior quarter, and third-quarter operating profit of KRW 1.73 billion was down 22.8% year on year.

In the first quarter of 2025, valuation losses tied to delayed delivery on a large project pushed the company into a loss.

Rising debt and financial leverage

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 45.3% in 2022 to 113.3% in 2025, and total liabilities of KRW 47.7 billion at year-end 2025 exceed total equity of KRW 42.1 billion. At its first-quarter 2025 IR, the company stressed that it had operated without external borrowing since 2021, but its debt load has since grown. Changes in interest rates or working capital could weigh more heavily on earnings.

Uncertain timing of new businesses reaching revenue

The immersion-cooled ESS was targeted for certification and commercialization in the first half of 2026 with mass production and delivery in the second half, but ESS accounted for only 3.22% of first-quarter 2026 sales. The growth in the backlog could also vary with the results of further nuclear tenders.

10

Risk factors

Policy and budget dependence

A high share of public-sector orders means results depend on how quickly central and local government budgets are executed. Solar systems made up 53.72% of first-quarter 2026 sales, making the company sensitive to changes in renewable deployment policy. Slower budget execution or a shift in deployment policy could widen quarterly revenue swings.

Project delivery timing and cost ratios

Delivery delays on large public projects can trigger valuation losses, as seen in the first quarter of 2025 when the company moved into a loss. Kiwoom Securities attributed the cost ratio improvement to stronger purchasing power for raw materials and cost reduction, and whether this lasts is the key question. A reversal in the cost ratio could lower the operating margin.

Preliminary figures and share price volatility

The company said its fourth-quarter 2025 preliminary results were based on unaudited internal accounts and could change after audit. The largest shareholder group holds 35.44%, which limits the free float. Commentators have also noted that inflows into the power equipment theme could increase short-term volatility.

Overall view

G2Power is a public-sector-focused power equipment maker whose results have shifted from an operating loss in 2023 to an operating profit of KRW 8.81 billion in 2025, with an operating margin of 11.4%.

Annual revenue grew from KRW 37.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 77.4 billion in 2025 and has risen for three consecutive years since 2023. Quarterly operating profit stayed positive through the first three quarters of 2026, but quarterly revenue swings are large, with second-quarter 2026 revenue at KRW 10.4 billion.

Strengths include its share of public procurement, a negotiated-contract structure based on certifications, and a backlog of KRW 106.9 billion at end-March 2026.

Weaknesses include seasonality tied to public orders and budget execution, a debt-to-equity ratio that rose to 113.3%, and immersion-cooled ESS and additional nuclear tenders that have not yet converted into revenue.

Once nuclear switchgear revenue begins in 2027 and the Yongin Techno Valley site is completed, production capacity and the revenue mix could change. The next checkpoints are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results, the 2026 annual report and the start of revenue recognition for Shin-Hanul Units 3 and 4. How multiples read depends on how quickly orders convert into revenue and on the margin trend.
