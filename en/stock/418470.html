02

Business structure

kt millie seojae operates an e-book subscription platform with two pillars: direct consumer subscriptions (B2C) and telecom-partner bundles (B2B2C). The company reported 10 million cumulative members and 260,000 titles as of October 2026.

Its product line has expanded from e-books to audiobooks, docent books, webtoons and web novels. It launched the Millisutori bundle of web novels and webtoons in June and September 2025, and subscribers using it account for about 10% of the total base.

Its own IP business is extending through the Millirod creative platform and original print books, with about eight original print titles published in the first half of 2026, one of which reportedly sold about 30,000 copies.

In Q2 2026, however, telco-partner sales stalled and B2B2C revenue declined, and the company attributed the drop in new sign-ups to a hacking incident and penalty-waiver issue involving a telecom partner.

The participatory services 'Audiobook I Made' and '3-Minute Review' ended on July 24, 2026, as the company refocuses on webtoons and web novels. The company said about half of customers who accepted the earlier price increase have kept the higher price.

On marketing, it said performance-marketing efficiency has fallen and announced a referral-based channel called Milli Partners.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 45.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 56.6 billion in 2023 (up 23.4% year on year), KRW 72.6 billion in 2024 (up 28.3%) and KRW 88.2 billion in 2025 (up 21.5%). Revenue has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 is the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

Operating profit went from KRW 4.17 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.4 billion in 2023 (up 149.6%), then KRW 11 billion in 2024 (up 5.7%) and KRW 14.4 billion in 2025 (up 30.6%). The operating margin moved from 9.1% in 2022 to 18.4% in 2023, then 15.2% in 2024 and 16.3% in 2025.

In 2023 operating profit growth far exceeded revenue growth, lifting the margin sharply, while in 2024 revenue grew 28.3% but operating profit grew only 5.7%, so the margin fell.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 13.3 billion in 2022, KRW 14.5 billion in 2023, KRW 11.5 billion in 2024 (down 20.9%) and KRW 15.8 billion in 2025 (up 37.0%).

Net income including non-controlling interests is recorded with the same yearly values, so the gap between the two items should be checked against the original annual report filing.

Quarterly operating profit fell from KRW 4.28 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 1.63 billion in Q4 2025 (down 62.0% quarter on quarter), then rebounded to KRW 3.9 billion in Q1 2026 (up 139.9%). It then held at KRW 3.39 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 3.67 billion in Q3 2026, the latter down 14.3% year on year.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent for the last four quarters totals KRW 15.5 billion, and operating cash flow was KRW 12.8 billion in 2025, below KRW 14 billion in 2024.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic e-book subscription market is shifting from subscription reading platforms to competition over broader content types. The company describes itself as the largest reading platform in Korea and cites 10 million cumulative members and 260,000 titles as its strengths.

Demand for audiobooks is rising: according to the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, the adult audiobook reading rate in 2023 was 5% higher than in 2021, and the share of adults agreeing that listening to audiobooks counts as reading rose 46% over the same period.

The company said it strengthened audiobooks and docent books to attract new sign-ups in line with this trend. Still, growth from e-book subscriptions alone may be limited, so expansion into webtoons, web novels and original IP is a common direction in the sector.

Within media and entertainment, Studio Dragon is a reference point for a business model that extends IP into video. kt millie seojae is likewise moving from an e-book focus toward an IP platform, citing supply to overseas web novel and webtoon platforms and adaptation as revenue sources.

Telecom-partner channels are a shared acquisition route, so sign-up trends can shift sharply with partnership terms and carrier promotions. The stall in this channel in Q2 2026 suggests that dependence on it remains high.

Industry-wide statistics are mostly audiobook consumption surveys, which makes direct market-share comparison in e-book subscriptions difficult.

06

Outlook

The company expects telco-partner revenue to recover in the second half of 2026 and expects new sign-ups to increase again after launches of new smartphones from Samsung Electronics and Apple.

From August 2026 it ended the free first month on its web service and plans to keep attracting new customers through a 50% discount promotion on the first Wednesday of each month. To improve marketing efficiency it plans to introduce Milli Partners, a referral-based channel, alongside content-led acquisition.

Internally, all employees are using paid AI models to automate work and raise productivity, and the company aims to keep personnel costs at about 25% of revenue.

Using AI to lower production costs, it plans to re-expand secondary content businesses it had paused and to launch 15-minute summary content to draw new subscribers.

On IP, it plans to supply original IP and distribution rights to overseas web novel and webtoon platforms and earn additional revenue through short-form video and adaptation. Original print titles by authors Jo Ye-eun and Cheongye are scheduled for release in the second half of 2026.

The company said it will maintain a dividend payout ratio of about 30%, and it stated a cautious stance on any additional share-price support measures while communicating with the market.

Most of the stated directions are qualitative, so the operating margin in the next annual report will be the main gauge of whether these plans are working.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in earnings base

Operating profit in 2025 rose 30.6% to KRW 14.4 billion, and the operating margin of 16.3% is above 9.1% in 2022. Profit attributable to owners rebounded to KRW 15.8 billion in 2025, up 37.0% after the 2024 decline.

Operating profit also rose to KRW 3.9 billion in Q1 2026, showing the earnings base did not collapse in the short term.

Room to expand content IP

Subscribers using the Millisutori bundle account for about 10% of the total subscriber base, and webtoons and web novels are becoming a second pillar of subscriptions. Some of the eight original print titles published in the first half of 2026 sold about 30,000 copies. Supply to overseas web novel and webtoon platforms and adaptation give IP-based revenue room to grow.

Low financial burden and shareholder returns

The debt ratio fell from 265.8% in 2022 to 26.6% in 2025, and total equity at end-2025 was KRW 86.9 billion. Total liabilities of KRW 23.2 billion are smaller than equity. The company said it will continue a dividend payout ratio of about 30%, giving it room to fund content investment and shareholder returns together.

09

Bear factors

Stalled telco-partner channel

Telco-partner sales stalled in Q2 2026, and B2B2C revenue declined. The company attributed lower new sign-ups to a hacking incident and penalty-waiver issue involving a telecom partner.

Recovery of partner revenue is expected after new device launches in the second half, and the timing of any actual rebound remains unproven.

Slower profitability and lower marketing efficiency

Q3 2026 revenue rose 7.6% year on year to KRW 24.5 billion, but operating profit of KRW 3.67 billion fell 14.3%. The company said performance-marketing efficiency has fallen compared with the past. If revenue growth continues without matching operating profit growth, margin pressure may persist.

Slower growth and cash flow

Revenue growth slowed from 28.3% in 2024 to 21.5% in 2025. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 14 billion in 2024 to KRW 12.8 billion in 2025. Operating profit grew only 5.7% in 2024, so the slowing pace shows up in the annual figures as well.

10

Risk factors

Dependence on telco partners

A large share of new sign-ups comes through telecom-partner channels, so revenue can swing with partnership terms and carrier promotions. The hacking and penalty-waiver issue in Q2 2026 led to lower sign-ups. If recovery is delayed, the decline in B2B2C revenue could continue.

Acquisition cost and price acceptance

The company said cherry-picking customers have increased, lowering sign-up efficiency per marketing dollar. About half of customers have kept the price increase. How the discount promotions and the end of the free first month affect the balance between acquisition and profit needs monitoring.

Execution of new businesses and service restructuring

The 'Audiobook I Made' and '3-Minute Review' services ended on July 24, 2026. The company said it is pruning low-growth services to focus on core content.

If webtoons, web novels, original IP, overseas supply and adaptation do not translate into revenue as planned, the contribution from new businesses may remain limited.

12

Overall view

kt millie seojae's 2025 disclosed results show revenue of KRW 88.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 14.4 billion, the largest values in the 2022–2025 period, with an operating margin of 16.3% versus 9.1% in 2022.

However, Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.67 billion fell 14.3% year on year, and telco-partner sales stalled in Q2 2026, reducing B2B2C revenue.

Strengths include the recovery in earnings, expansion into webtoons, web novels and original IP, a low debt ratio of 26.6%, and a policy to keep the payout ratio at about 30%. Weaknesses include reliance on partner channels, lower marketing efficiency, and slower revenue growth and operating cash flow.

These factors should be weighed equally, and this report offers no investment rating or target price. The next checkpoints are whether telco-partner revenue recovers in Q4 2026 results and the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
