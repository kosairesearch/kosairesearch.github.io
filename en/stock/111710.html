02

Business structure

Namhwa Industrial operates golf courses and was founded in February 1990 before listing on KOSDAQ in November 2018. Its business covers golf admissions, rentals, food and beverage, and lodging, with golf admissions the largest component.

A 2022 securities research report, based on 2021 figures, put golf admission fees at KRW 19.77 billion out of KRW 25.89 billion in total sales, followed by rentals at KRW 4.29 billion, food and beverage at KRW 1.61 billion and lodging at KRW 220 million.

That is a past mix from 2021, and with lodging small, sales rely mainly on admissions and rentals. The company attributed its 2025 decline to fewer golf visitors amid an economic slowdown, along with lower equity-method income from affiliates.

Net income attributable to the parent exceeded operating profit in every year from 2022 to 2025, which appears to reflect non-operating items such as affiliate equity-method gains. Standalone revenue in Q1 2026 fell 8.6% year on year, with fewer visitors cited as the driver.

The March 2026 value-enhancement plan calls for flexible pricing to attract more visitors and stronger customer service. The company is also pursuing construction of a village-style golf hotel to attract longer-stay guests.

The KTX Muan Airport route, scheduled to open in December 2027, is expected to improve access for out-of-town visitors.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue on a DART-filed basis fell from KRW 30.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 24.7 billion in 2023 (down 18.9% year on year), KRW 20.7 billion in 2024 (down 16.1%) and KRW 16.8 billion in 2025 (down 19.0%).

Revenue has declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, with 2022 the largest year and 2025 the smallest in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating profit fell from KRW 17.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.8 billion in 2023 (down 33.4%), KRW 8.01 billion in 2024 (down 32.1%) and KRW 4.09 billion in 2025 (down 48.9%), also declining three years in a row from 2023.

Operating margin moved from 58.2% to 47.8%, 38.7% and 24.4%, and in 2025 the drop in operating profit was more than double the drop in revenue.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 20.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.7 billion in 2023 (down 14.5%), KRW 10.9 billion in 2024 (down 38.1%) and KRW 6.67 billion in 2025 (down 39.0%), while net income including non-controlling interests followed the same amounts.

In 2025, profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 6.67 billion exceeded operating profit of KRW 4.09 billion, and the company pointed to lower equity-method income from affiliates as a factor in the decline.

The debt ratio eased from 8.4% in 2022 to 7.1% in 2025, while operating cash flow fell from KRW 16.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.4 billion in 2023, KRW 10 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.2 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit in Q3 2025 was KRW 25.98 million, rose to KRW 2.28 billion in Q4 2025 (up 8,684.5% from the prior quarter), and revenue reached KRW 5.55 billion in Q4 (up 99.2%).

In Q1 2026, revenue fell to KRW 1.99 billion (down 64.2% from the prior quarter) and an operating loss of KRW 459 million turned the quarter into a loss, with a loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 178 million.

Q2 2026 revenue rose to KRW 5.49 billion (up 176.4%) and operating profit of KRW 1.09 billion turned the quarter profitable, with profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.94 billion, a wide gap that points to a large contribution from non-operating items.

In Q3 2026, revenue of KRW 3.05 billion rose 9.4% year on year, yet operating profit of KRW 316 million fell 71.1% from the prior quarter and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 753 million fell 22.8% year on year. Over the last four quarters, profit attributable to controlling shareholders totals KRW 6.41 billion.

Shareholder Return Policy

In its March 2026 plan the company set a 40% payout target, and it showed a 61.7% payout ratio for 2025. It stated that it qualifies as a high-dividend company under Korean tax law. The total cash dividend for the 2025 fiscal year was KRW 4.12 billion.

Margin Defense and a Return to Profit

The 2025 operating margin of 24.4% is above the company's 20% target. Q2 2026 turned profitable with operating profit of KRW 1.09 billion. Quarterly swings were large, but a profitable quarter following a loss quarter is read as a sign of recovery.

Revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders all declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. Operating profit of KRW 4.09 billion in 2025 is far below KRW 17.7 billion in 2022. Operating margin also fell from 58.2% in 2022 to 24.4% in 2025.

Quarterly Profit Moving Against Sales

In Q1 2026, an operating loss of KRW 459 million and a loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 178 million were disclosed. Q3 2026 revenue rose 9.4% year on year to KRW 3.05 billion, but operating profit fell 71.1% from the prior quarter to KRW 316 million.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 22.8% year on year, so higher sales did not carry through to profit.

Net Income Relying on Non-Operating Items

The company cited lower equity-method income from affiliates as a factor in the decline. When non-operating items support profit, swings in equity-method income pass straight through to net income.
