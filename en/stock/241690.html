ESS Restores Operating Profit, Net Result Back in Loss

Industrial ESS parts are helping Unitekno keep quarterly operating profit positive through 2026, but the net result returned to a loss attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter.

02

Business structure

Unitekno was founded in 2000 as an auto parts maker and listed on KOSDAQ in 2016; it is an engineering plastics precision injection molding company. Its core products are battery cell cases for vehicles, plastic injection parts for engine powertrains, and vehicle motor parts, with household ESS parts added on top.

Mass production of industrial ESS parts began in the second half of 2025, which is when its non-mobility business became a real pillar. The company has set a target to shift the mobility-to-non-mobility revenue mix from 80:20 to 75:25 by 2030.

Named major customers include Sebang Lithium Battery, LS EV Korea and Samsung SDI. Overseas, a China subsidiary and the Mexico unit Uniteknomex are consolidated, and the San Luis Potosi plant has annual capacity of roughly KRW 70 billion.

That plant mainly makes injection and assembly parts for battery cells supplied to Toyota and Honda, and part of the volume originally planned for Mexico was moved to the Busan No. 2 plant in response to US tariff changes.

The company says its order wins rest on production track records with each customer and on its precision injection technology. As of June 30, 2026 it had 325 employees and is classified as a small and mid-sized enterprise.

04

Earnings analysis

On the basis of filed results, revenue rose from KRW 82.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 101.9 billion in 2023 (up 23.7% year on year), KRW 105.1 billion in 2024 (up 3.2%) and KRW 107.2 billion in 2025 (up 1.9%), and has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 6.12 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.4 billion in 2023 (up 87.0%), which was the peak, and then fell to KRW 8.12 billion in 2024 (down 29.1%) and KRW 2.05 billion in 2025 (down 74.7%), a two-year decline from 2024 through 2025.

The operating margin ran at 7.4% in 2022, 11.2% in 2023, 7.7% in 2024 and 1.9% in 2025, so 2025 was both the largest revenue year and the lowest margin year in the period.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders grew to KRW 9.12 billion in 2023 (up 86.9%) and KRW 10.6 billion in 2024 (up 16.6%), then turned into a net loss of KRW 3.22 billion in 2025; net income including non-controlling interests was also a net loss of KRW 3.22 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 swing mainly to higher depreciation from large facility investments and to one-off costs from relocating and stabilizing new facilities.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 8.28 billion in 2022, KRW 9.96 billion in 2023 and KRW 12.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 238 million in 2025, while the debt ratio rose from 32.9% to 74.6%.

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 102.4 billion and total equity was KRW 137.3 billion, of which the controlling shareholders' stake was also KRW 137.3 billion.

Quarterly, operating profit was KRW 400 million in the third quarter of 2025, then an operating loss of KRW 456 million in the fourth quarter, before returning to profit at KRW 1.47 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 2.82 billion in the second quarter (up 91.2% from the prior quarter) and KRW 2.45 billion in the third quarter (down 13.1% from the prior quarter).

Revenue grew for three consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025, which was the low at KRW 26.5 billion, to KRW 36.4 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 884 million in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 3.18 billion in the second quarter, but a net loss of KRW 1.74 billion in the third quarter turned it back into a loss.

The gap between operating profit and the controlling-shareholder net loss in the third quarter should be traced through non-operating items and income tax in the quarterly report.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from downstream markets runs along two lines. One is auto parts for internal combustion, hybrid (HEV) and electric (EV) vehicles, where the company says higher supply of industrial ESS and HEV/EV parts, combined with rising utilization, improved profitability.

The other is industrial ESS, where the company cites expanding renewable energy, growth in AI industries, data center power stability and rising demand for peak power management.

On the auto side, a Wisereport company monitor noted that higher production costs for shared eco-friendly and combustion products and for secondary battery products pressured profitability in the first half of 2025.

Changes in US tariff policy add uncertainty to North American supply chains, and the company responded by shifting some Mexico volume to its Busan No. 2 plant.

In terms of industry position, a Blotter report said the price-to-book ratio in late 2024 and the first half of 2025 sat below the KOSDAQ transport equipment and parts industry average.

Compared with large suppliers that deliver directly to automakers, Unitekno is a small and mid-sized injection parts maker, and its order wins in battery and ESS cell cases, which demand complex shapes and high precision, rest on proven mass-production capability.

External market statistics that verify the growth pace of industrial ESS demand remain limited beyond the company's own statements.

06

Outlook

For 2026 on a consolidated basis, the company set targets of KRW 134.2 billion in revenue and KRW 8.1 billion in operating profit, disclosed through a fair disclosure filing in July 2026. Cumulative progress through the third quarter stood at 73.8% of the revenue target and about 83% of the operating profit target.

The company says it will keep supplying its existing business steadily in the fourth quarter while raising non-mobility revenue, including industrial ESS, to reach the annual targets.

For the medium term, a September 2026 filing raised the 2030 revenue target from KRW 200 billion to KRW 220 billion and lifted the non-mobility revenue target from KRW 40 billion to KRW 55 billion. The company has also said it aims to deliver an operating margin of 7% or more on a stable basis starting in 2027.

On orders, the company expects mass production of the busbar housing for customer L to begin in 2027, with revenue becoming visible from 2028; its internal estimate is about KRW 11 billion per year on average from 2028 to 2031.

In physical AI, actuators for autonomous mobile robots are going through customer validation and preparation for mass production, and a drive system module for drones and armored vehicles has been completed with related party NMC.

Once the 2026 annual report is filed, the fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results will be fixed as filed figures.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly operating profit stays positive

Operating profit was KRW 1.47 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 2.82 billion in the second and KRW 2.45 billion in the third, a run of three profitable quarters. According to a Newspim report, the cumulative operating margin through the third quarter of 2026 rose to 6.8% from 3.1% a year earlier.

The company attributes the gain to higher supply of industrial ESS and HEV/EV parts plus higher utilization of facilities built ahead of demand, such as at Busan No. 2.

Long-term industrial ESS volumes secured

Industrial ESS parts were confirmed as a long-term supply of about KRW 6 billion a year through 2031. The busbar housing for customer L was presented as a revenue source of roughly KRW 44 billion in total, according to the company's internal estimate. That estimate puts the average at about KRW 11 billion a year from 2028 to 2031.

Raised 2030 targets and non-mobility expansion

A September 2026 filing raised the 2030 revenue target from KRW 200 billion to KRW 220 billion. The non-mobility revenue target rose to KRW 55 billion, and the mobility-to-non-mobility target mix is 75:25.

Physical AI and actuators for autonomous mobile robots were presented as the next growth engines over the medium term.

Bear factors

2025 earnings deterioration and swing to loss

The sharp drop in operating profit carried through to a swing to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders. The company cited higher depreciation and one-off costs from relocating and stabilizing new facilities. The operating margin fell to 1.9% in 2025.

Gap between operating profit and net losses

Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 2.45 billion, yet the controlling-shareholder net result swung back to a loss of KRW 1.74 billion. Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters total KRW 1.7 billion. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 238 million in 2025 from KRW 12.9 billion in 2024.

Financial burden and customer and tariff exposure

The debt ratio rose from 32.9% in 2022 to 74.6% in 2025. Named major customers include Sebang Lithium Battery, LS EV Korea and Samsung SDI, so changes in volume from any one of them carry a large effect. US tariff policy changes remain a factor that can alter production plans for North American customers.

Risk factors

Tariffs and supply chain

Changes in US tariff policy shifted part of the volume planned for the Mexico plant to the Busan No. 2 plant. The company says existing volumes and customer delivery schedules are unaffected, but uncertainty in North American supply chains remains.

With the Mexico plant's annual capacity at roughly KRW 70 billion, changes to its operating plan could affect the revenue mix.

Profitability and fixed costs

Large facility investments raised depreciation and the fixed-cost burden, which was part of the background to the 2025 swing to loss. The 2026 profit recovery leans on higher utilization, so a softer demand picture could change how quickly profit recovers.

On the auto side, rising costs for shared eco-friendly and combustion products and for secondary battery products have pressured profitability before.

Execution of new businesses

The 2030 targets of KRW 220 billion in revenue and KRW 55 billion in non-mobility revenue depend on converting industrial ESS orders and physical AI projects into actual sales.

Actuators for autonomous mobile robots are still in customer validation and mass-production preparation, and expansion into drones and defense remains at the planning stage. Whether the raised targets turn into actual orders and mass production is the point to check.

12

Overall view

Unitekno started as a maker of battery and electronics plastic injection parts for automakers and is widening into industrial ESS parts as a small and mid-sized injection molding company.

In 2025, higher depreciation and stabilization costs for new facilities pushed both operating profit and the controlling-shareholder net result into loss.

In 2026, operating profit stayed positive from the first through the third quarter, and the cumulative operating margin through the third quarter rose to 6.8% from 3.1% according to a Newspim report.

Still, the controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.74 billion in the third quarter of 2026 shows that whether operating profit carries through to net profit remains an open task. The bullish case rests on the busbar housing order from customer L, long-term industrial ESS volumes and the raised 2030 revenue target.

The bearish case rests on the cumulative net loss after the swing to loss, a rising debt ratio, the sharp drop in operating cash flow, concentration on a few major customers and US tariff exposure.

On valuation, a price-to-book ratio below the industry average sits alongside an earnings-based multiple that cannot be calculated. The next checkpoints are the filed full-year figures in the 2026 annual report, the first-quarter 2027 report, and the start of North American ESS mass production in 2027.
