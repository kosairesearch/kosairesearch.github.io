Report overview

IT Distributor Attempts Pivot Toward AI Infrastructure

Zungwon EN-Sys, long centered on server and storage distribution, is pursuing both a quarterly earnings recovery and an expansion into AI infrastructure business, while governance risks persist alongside these efforts.

02

Business structure

Zungwon EN-Sys was established in 1969, began IT business in earnest from 1978, and listed on KOSDAQ in 2000 as an enterprise IT infrastructure distribution and integration company.

Its business is organized into an IT Infrastructure segment supplying servers, storage, and computer equipment, an IT Distribution segment, and a customer support (maintenance, MA) segment.

In the first quarter of 2026, IT Infrastructure accounted for KRW 48.1 billion, or 63.9% of total revenue, followed by IT Distribution at KRW 19.3 billion and customer support (MA) at KRW 7.9 billion.

The company serves as a distributor for HPE and Handreamnet, and also partners with Dell Technologies, Hitachi, and eXfusion across its IT infrastructure, distribution, and support businesses. Its subsidiary Zaensys is a distributor for Fujitsu, handling IT hardware distribution and maintenance services.

Peer companies with similar business structures in Korea's IT infrastructure distribution and system-integration market include ITCEN ENTEC, ITCEN CTS, Insung Information, and S.Net Systems.

More recently, the company has been pursuing a strategy to extend its existing IT infrastructure distribution capabilities into AI operating environment construction, signing a strategic memorandum of understanding with AI specialist AI Nation and opening an AI Center.

Management has stated that consultations for AI server and GPU infrastructure builds, along with proof-of-concept inquiries, have been increasing in tandem.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose for four consecutive years, from KRW 166.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 179.8 billion in 2023, KRW 194.5 billion in 2024, and KRW 200.9 billion in 2025.

Operating profit, however, moved independently of revenue growth, coming in at KRW 0.68 billion in 2022, KRW 2.85 billion in 2023, KRW 1.56 billion in 2024, and KRW 1.94 billion in 2025, with operating margin staying low, between 0.4% and 1.6%.

Net profit attributable to owners also fluctuated, at KRW 1.20 billion in 2022, KRW 1.70 billion in 2023, KRW 0.95 billion in 2024, and KRW 1.12 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 saw revenue of KRW 50.8 billion but an operating loss of roughly KRW 6 million and a net loss of KRW 0.14 billion, before recovering in the fourth quarter with revenue of KRW 65.8 billion, operating profit of KRW 1.59 billion, and net profit of KRW 0.61 billion.

In 2026, the first quarter posted revenue of KRW 75.3 billion, operating profit of KRW 1.28 billion, and net profit of KRW 0.73 billion, while the second quarter recorded revenue of KRW 58.3 billion, operating profit of KRW 1.87 billion, and net profit of KRW 1.12 billion, marking two consecutive quarters of profit improvement.

Combined net profit attributable to owners over the most recent four quarters (third quarter 2025 through second quarter 2026) totaled roughly KRW 2.32 billion.

The company reported that first-quarter 2026 revenue rose 87.7% year over year and operating profit swung from a loss to a profit, an outcome it attributed to the effect of a state AI infrastructure equipment supply contract.

Management has noted that quarterly results are inherently volatile because they depend on individual project contract terms and revenue recognition timing, and this variability is also visible in cash flow, where operating cash flow swung from negative KRW 11.6 billion in 2024 to positive KRW 16.1 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's enterprise IT infrastructure market depends heavily on informatization and digital-transformation projects from public agencies and large corporations, with a structure combining large system integrators and smaller distribution and solution firms.

More recently, the spread of generative AI has driven rising demand for computing infrastructure such as servers, GPUs, and storage, and a series of trillion-won-scale AI data center investment agreements have been announced across the country, expanding the related ecosystem.

Against this backdrop, distributors of servers and storage such as Zungwon EN-Sys have indicated they are experiencing early benefits from expanding demand in the form of increased AI infrastructure consultation and proof-of-concept inquiries.

At the same time, competition has intensified among similar IT distribution and system-integration companies such as ITCEN ENTEC, Insung Information, and S.Net Systems as more participants enter the market.

IT infrastructure distribution carries strong hardware-resale characteristics, which tends to compress margins and produce earnings volatility that depends heavily on individual project contract terms and revenue recognition timing.

Within this structure, Zungwon EN-Sys is a relatively small KOSDAQ-listed company, with a considerable scale gap compared to larger IT service companies such as LG CNS and Daou Technology.

06

Outlook

Since 2026, the company has positioned a transition to becoming an 'AI infrastructure provider' as its core strategy, expanding related organizations and partnerships.

In May, it signed a strategic memorandum of understanding with AI specialist AI Nation covering joint research and development, joint product and brand development, and joint sales, stating it would combine its own IT infrastructure capabilities with AI Nation's MLOps platform technology to expand AI transformation business targeting manufacturing, financial, and public-sector clients.

In July, it hired Kwon Chan-young, who previously worked at Naver and SAP Labs Korea, as head of its AI strategy office to oversee AI business strategy, new service development, and technology roadmap design.

A company representative stated that as market demand for AI server and GPU infrastructure adoption grows, consultation requests and proof-of-concept inquiries have been increasing together.

However, the specific timing and scale at which these new initiatives will translate into revenue and profit have not yet been detailed.

The company's previous flagship state AI infrastructure equipment supply contract was completed with final payment collection in April 2026, meaning that securing new orders will be a key factor for future results.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly Profit Recovery Trend

After posting a net loss in the third quarter of 2025, the company achieved three consecutive quarters of profit from the fourth quarter through the first and second quarters of 2026. Second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.87 billion was the highest level among the most recent five quarters.

The improvement appears to coincide with the effect of the state AI infrastructure equipment supply contract combined with revenue expansion.

Expansion Into AI Infrastructure Business

The company has assembled AI-related organizations and partnerships in succession, including a strategic MOU with AI Nation, the establishment of an AI strategy office, and the opening of an AI Center.

It has stated that consultation requests and proof-of-concept inquiries from customers considering AI server and GPU infrastructure adoption are increasing. It is pursuing productization of AI hardware and solutions by leveraging its existing distribution network.

Improving Financial Structure

The debt ratio declined for four consecutive years, from 131.0% in 2022 to 101.8% in 2025. Operating cash flow also improved sharply, from negative KRW 11.6 billion in 2024 to positive KRW 16.1 billion in 2025.

The company and its board chairman have reportedly been carrying out both a corporate share buyback and an expansion of the chairman's personal stake in parallel.

09

Bear factors

Governance and Ownership Dispute Risk

Zungwon EN-Sys has had a long-running governance dispute between the founding family side and major shareholder Wims. Recent disclosures have continued to include an injunction for accounting book inspection and a lawsuit seeking to invalidate a shareholders' meeting resolution.

The stock has also previously been designated an investment warning stock on KOSDAQ, indicating that governance uncertainty has not been fully resolved.

Low and Unstable Operating Margin

From 2022 through 2025, operating margin remained at a low level of between 0.4% and 1.6%. The hardware-distribution-centered business structure imposes structural constraints on margin expansion. Quarterly results are also volatile, as seen in the third quarter of 2025 when operating profit turned negative.

New Business Revenue Contribution Still Unclear

The newly established AI strategy office and the MOU with AI Nation remain in early stages, and no specific order volumes or revenue contributions have been disclosed. The previous flagship large-scale state AI infrastructure supply contract was already completed with final payment collection in April 2026. Whether a comparably sized new contract can be secured going forward remains uncertain.

10

Risk factors

Governance Risk

Multiple lawsuits related to control of the company are ongoing, and related disclosures continue to be filed. The stock has previously been designated a KOSDAQ investment warning stock, which could weigh on market confidence.

Depending on the outcome of the litigation, changes to board composition or management direction cannot be ruled out.

Earnings Volatility Risk

Because revenue recognition depends on the contract terms and timing of individual projects, quarterly results can vary significantly. There is a possibility that a specific quarter could swing to a net loss, as occurred in the third quarter of 2025.

Given the low operating margin structure, profit can be easily eroded by changes in costs or foreign exchange rates.

Business Transition Risk

The transition toward AI infrastructure business remains at an early stage, still centered on building organizations and partnerships. If competition from larger IT service companies and AI specialist firms intensifies, the delivery of results from the new business could be delayed.

If new orders fail to materialize in time to replace the completed flagship contract, a revenue gap could emerge.

12

Overall view

Zungwon EN-Sys has shown signs of profit recovery, posting consecutive profits in the first and second quarters of 2026 following a net loss in the third quarter of 2025, even as annual revenue grew for four consecutive years.

With the state AI infrastructure equipment supply contract completed in April 2026, the key point to watch going forward is how much actual revenue the new AI-related business generates through the MOU with AI Nation and the newly formed AI strategy office.

Changes on the financial structure side, including a declining debt ratio and improved operating cash flow, have also been observed. However, operating margin remains at a low level in the low single digits, and quarterly results continue to show significant volatility.

Litigation related to the governance dispute and the company's past investment warning stock designation remain sources of governance-related uncertainty.

Overall, this appears to be a phase in which positive signals from profit recovery and new business expansion coexist with persistent factors such as a low-margin structure and governance risk.
