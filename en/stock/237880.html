02

Business structure

CLIO Cosmetics is a cosmetics maker and distributor built around the CLIO color cosmetics brand, which is known as the first color-cosmetics specialist brand in Korea.

Its portfolio also includes peripera, the clean-naturalism skincare brand Goodal, the dermocosmetic brand DERMATORY, and the hair care brand Healingbird. The current product strategy adds skincare and hair care to a color-focused core.

Domestic distribution centers on health and beauty (H&B) chains such as Olive Young, followed by online vertical platforms, home shopping, the Daiso mall, and the company's own mall CLC.

In 2026 the channel mix has been shifting away from home shopping and Daiso, which carry higher commissions or lower profitability, toward the company's own mall and direct sales.

By region, Japan was the largest overseas sales market in 2025 at 26%, followed by Southeast Asia at 19%, North America at 18%, and China at 16%. In the first half of 2026 the mix spread to China at 23%, North America at 22%, Southeast Asia at 19%, Japan at 18%, and Europe and other regions at 12%.

The company acquired 100% of the Japanese subsidiaries Clio Japan and the import agent Kiwami in May 2024, but media reports said their first-half 2026 sales fell 33.7% and 63% year on year to KRW 9.5 billion and KRW 3.0 billion.

A 2024 media report cited aging domestic color brands and intensifying competition as reasons for lower share in core channels.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual sales rose from KRW 272.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 330.6 billion in 2023, up 21.3% year on year. Sales reached KRW 351.4 billion in 2024, the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 window, before falling 6.4% to KRW 328.9 billion in 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 17.9 billion in 2022 to a peak of KRW 33.8 billion in 2023, up 89.1%. It then fell 27.2% to KRW 24.6 billion in 2024 and 33.2% to KRW 16.4 billion in 2025, declining for two consecutive years. The operating margin was 6.6% in 2022, 10.2% in 2023, 7.0% in 2024 and 5.0% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 10.1 billion in 2022, KRW 27.3 billion in 2023, KRW 27.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 13.6 billion in 2025, down 50.1% in the last year. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 13.2 billion in 2025, a different figure that should be read separately.

A March 2026 media analysis said that although the company cut manufacturing costs in 2025, lower sales and higher marketing spending raised the share of selling and administrative expenses.

Operating cash flow was KRW 28.2 billion in 2022, KRW 35.7 billion in 2023, KRW 17.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 13.2 billion in 2025, moving in the same direction as earnings. Quarterly results turned in the third quarter of 2025, when sales were KRW 83.8 billion and operating profit KRW 4.72 billion.

Operating profit rose to KRW 7.05 billion in the fourth quarter, up 49.3% from the prior quarter, before falling to KRW 5.79 billion in the first quarter of 2026 on sales of KRW 78.3 billion.

In the second quarter of 2026, sales rose 10.8% quarter on quarter to KRW 86.8 billion and operating profit rose 100.5% to KRW 11.6 billion.

Hyundai Motor Securities attributed the 13.4% second-quarter operating margin to lower costs from fewer large H&B collaborations and better commission rates after cutting home shopping and Daiso channels.

Third-quarter sales were KRW 80.2 billion, down 4.3% year on year, while operating profit was KRW 7.66 billion, up 62.2%, so profitability improved even as sales fell.

Net profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 6.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 13.9 billion in the second and KRW 3.37 billion in the third, and the last four quarters total KRW 27.5 billion.

The second-quarter parent net profit exceeded operating profit, so the non-operating items should be checked in the detailed disclosures.

05

Industry analysis

In domestic health and beauty (H&B) channels, CLIO's domestic sales in the second quarter of 2026 rose 9.9% year on year.

Hyundai Motor Securities attributed this to growth in online vertical platforms, H&B channel sales that rose 24% year on year, and strong sales of new color products and the Goodal sun care line, in its September 2026 report.

The same report said that cutting lower-margin home shopping and Daiso channels improved commission rates. Color cosmetics are sensitive to product launch cycles and channel share. A 2024 media report pointed to aging domestic color brands and intensifying competition as the cause of lower share in core channels.

Overseas, the China and North America shares rose to 23% and 22% in the first half of 2026. Hyundai Motor Securities said growth in the US and Chinese markets supports earnings improvement.

The same report flagged the absence of large US discount events and the concentration of offline store launches such as Costco in the fourth quarter as short-term variables.

Japan was expected to see sales decline in the third quarter of 2026 because of local organizational changes, and Japanese subsidiary sales kept falling in the first half of 2026.

Compared with large diversified cosmetics groups such as Amorepacific and LG H&H, CLIO is a smaller company centered on color-cosmetics brands, pursuing overseas diversification alongside.

Outlook

Estimates for CLIO's 2026 full-year results differ across brokers. In its September 2026 report, Hyundai Motor Securities forecast full-year sales of KRW 338.0 billion and operating profit of KRW 32.0 billion.

A Korean IR research firm, in an October 2026 media report, forecast sales of KRW 338.6 billion, up 3.0%, operating profit of KRW 33.3 billion, up 102.4%, and an operating margin of 9.8%.

Both forecasts assume that fourth-quarter operating profit runs above the prior-quarter level, given that the provisional first-to-third-quarter 2026 operating profit disclosed on November 12, 2026 totaled KRW 25.07 billion.

On the investment side, the company decided in September 2026 to acquire a new logistics center in Icheon, Gyeonggi Province, with construction costs of KRW 71.6 billion to KRW 84.6 billion. Once the investment is complete, inventory storage capacity is expected to expand to about four times the current level.

The funding comes from exchangeable bonds issued using treasury shares. An October 2026 media report identified expanding distribution in North America and Greater China as key to top-line growth. The same report argued that cost cuts alone have limits, so sales growth must support a lasting profit recovery.

On products, the company plans to keep its color-focused brands while broadening skincare and other new categories. The figures above are broker and research-firm estimates rather than company targets.

08

Bull factors

Rapid quarterly operating profit recovery

Operating profit rose 100.5% quarter on quarter to KRW 11.6 billion in the second quarter of 2026, and Hyundai Motor Securities put the operating margin at 13.4%. In the third quarter, sales fell 4.3% year on year to KRW 80.2 billion, yet operating profit rose 62.2% to KRW 7.66 billion. The broker attributed this to cost improvements alongside the reduction of lower-margin channels.

Rising overseas share

Media reports said overseas export sales reached KRW 69.4 billion in the first half of 2026, or 42% of total sales. Overseas revenue is spread across China at 23%, North America at 22% and Southeast Asia at 19%. The mix has shifted from the 2025 pattern, when Japan was the largest market.

Cost structure and logistics investment

Media reports said first-half 2026 selling and administrative expenses fell to KRW 65.0 billion from KRW 73.2 billion a year earlier. Sales commissions dropped from KRW 14.9 billion to KRW 5.4 billion.

The company decided to invest KRW 71.6 billion to KRW 84.6 billion in the Icheon logistics center, which is expected to raise storage capacity to about four times the current level.

09

Bear factors

Stalled and declining sales

Sales fell 6.4% to KRW 328.9 billion in 2025, and third-quarter 2026 sales declined 4.3% year on year. Media reports said first-half 2026 sales of KRW 165.1 billion were roughly flat compared with KRW 164.4 billion a year earlier. Without top-line growth, the profit recovery depends on cost efficiency.

Incomplete recovery after two years of decline

Operating profit declined in both 2024 and 2025, and the 2025 operating margin of 5.0% remained well below the 10.2% of 2023. Parent net profit fell 50.1% to KRW 13.6 billion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit recovered in 2026, but third-quarter parent net profit of KRW 3.37 billion was far below the KRW 13.9 billion of the second quarter.

Weak overseas subsidiaries and duty-free decline

Media reports said first-half 2026 sales at the Japanese subsidiaries Clio Japan and Kiwami fell 33.7% and 63% year on year. A March 2026 analysis said duty-free sales plunged 33% in 2025 and led the decline in overseas results. Marketing spending for overseas expansion may continue to weigh on margins.

10

Risk factors

Durability of profitability

The 13.4% operating margin in the second quarter of 2026 is a high quarterly figure, and third-quarter operating profit fell 34.1% from the second quarter. Much of the cost reduction came from commission and channel cuts, so the effect may be limited if sales do not grow. Quarterly profit could swing more if marketing spending rises again with new product launches.

Overseas channels and regional concentration

Overseas sales are spread across China at 23% and North America at 22% in the first half of 2026, but a higher China share ties results to local policy and consumption trends.

Japanese subsidiary sales continued to fall, and the timing of North American launches and the lack of large discount events were flagged as short-term variables. The decline in duty-free sales is another drag on overseas results.

Investment funding and exchangeable bond structure

The logistics investment of KRW 71.6 billion to KRW 84.6 billion will be partly funded through exchangeable bonds issued using treasury shares. Dilution depends on how the share price compares with the exchange price.

A September 2026 media report said the exchange appeal was limited because the share price was below the exchange price. Capital allocation burden should be monitored alongside earnings until the investment is complete.

Overall view

CLIO's disclosed results show sales and profit falling together since the 2023 peak. Sales declined to KRW 328.9 billion in 2025, and operating profit fell for two consecutive years to KRW 16.4 billion.

Quarterly operating profit recovered in 2026, reaching KRW 11.6 billion in the second quarter and KRW 7.66 billion in the third, both above the prior-year levels. Much of this recovery came from cost factors such as lower sales commissions and the reduction of lower-margin channels, while sales have not clearly grown.

Overseas share rose to 42% in the first half of 2026, but weakness at the Japanese subsidiaries and the decline in duty-free sales remain. The Icheon logistics investment and its exchangeable bond funding structure are items to watch for capital allocation and financial burden.

Broker estimates place 2026 full-year operating profit at roughly KRW 32.0 billion to KRW 33.3 billion. The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report, which will show whether the quarterly recovery holds on a full-year basis.
