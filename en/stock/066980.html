02

Business structure

Hansung Cleantech is a water treatment specialist that designs, procures and builds (EPC) ultrapure water, industrial water and wastewater systems for semiconductor and electronics plants.

Its core is the environmental facilities EPC business, which accounted for 63% of KRW 44.9 billion in 2026 Q1 consolidated sales according to EBN Industry Economy.

A 2026 Q2 breakdown put environmental facility construction at 63%, environmental facility O&M at 22% and liquid designated waste treatment at 15%, and the split differs somewhat by source and period.

The parent handles EPC, while two wholly owned subsidiaries handle liquid designated waste treatment and a third handles facility O&M. The liquid waste business made up 17.3% of sales in 2025 Q1, partly cushioning EPC-driven earnings swings.

Major customers include Samsung Electronics' Pyeongtaek campus, ultrapure water systems ordered by Samsung E&A, SK hynix wastewater projects in Yongin and Cheongju, and DB HiTek's Eumseong plant.

Between 2021 and 2025 the company took part in a national ultrapure water localization program and built a 1,200-ton-per-day demonstration plant, and in April 2026 it was selected for a next-generation ultrapure water production project.

It also said in 2025 that it planned to pursue EPC orders in Vietnam through a local unit. Because EPC margins depend on cost ratios and project progress, quarterly profitability can swing sharply.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales on a disclosed basis were KRW 322.5 billion in 2022, KRW 368.4 billion in 2023, KRW 155.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 199.9 billion in 2025. 2023 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 span and 2024 the smallest. Sales in 2024 fell 57.9% year on year, with an operating margin of -32.7%.

The company attributed that loss to higher raw material costs from inflation, a sharp drop in orders amid a weak semiconductor cycle and heavier fixed costs.

In 2025 it said revenue recognition rose as major projects progressed, cost structure improved, and one-off costs such as bad-debt write-offs were cleared, which returned operating profit of KRW 2.84 billion. The 2025 operating margin of 1.4% is thin relative to sales.

Controlling-shareholder net profit in 2025 was KRW 106 million while net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 539 million, and the gap reflects the non-controlling share.

Operating cash flow was KRW 29 billion positive in 2024 despite the loss, versus negative KRW 24.5 billion in 2023 and KRW 3.34 billion in 2025.

By quarter, operating profit of KRW 506 million in 2025 Q3 became an operating loss of KRW 1.74 billion in Q4, followed by a KRW 548 million operating loss in 2026 Q1, then operating profit of KRW 1.5 billion in Q2 and KRW 1.53 billion in Q3, up 2.0% from the prior quarter.

Controlling-shareholder results moved from a net loss of KRW 786 million in 2026 Q2 to a net profit of KRW 262 million in Q3, while the KRW 2.8 billion loss in 2025 Q4 was the largest of the quarters shown.

Operating profit quarters that still ended in net losses mean non-operating items and financing costs also matter.

05

Industry analysis

Hansung Cleantech's earnings are closely tied to the semiconductor capital spending cycle. In 2024, some environmental facility projects for major chip customers were partly halted or delayed, producing an operating loss. Coverage in 2025 generally attributed the rebound to construction resuming.

In May 2026 an independent research house, Value Finder, described 2026 as the year the large capacity expansions at Samsung Electronics and SK hynix begin to show up in earnings. The same report said recognition of its ultrapure water localization technology could broaden its order base beyond major customers.

Expectations for a large semiconductor production base in the Honam region were raised in July 2026 coverage, but one brokerage official noted that investment approval, EPC tendering, water treatment package bidding and individual contract awards remain.

On competition, the company points to its ultrapure water design capability, demonstration experience and project history at Pyeongtaek and with SK hynix. It is also diversifying into biotech, secondary battery and wastewater reuse.

Where the cycle stands depends on whether orders are converting into revenue and whether that revenue lifts the operating margin.

Outlook

The major contracts disclosed in 2026 run from late 2026 into the summer of 2027. The Pyeongtaek P4 Ph4 ultrapure water work runs from November 5, 2025 to December 31, 2026. The P5 Ph1 ultrapure water work, revised in a September 22, 2026 disclosure, is worth KRW 24 billion and runs from June 17, 2026 to July 31, 2027.

A P5 Ph1 piping work worth KRW 19.9 billion runs to May 31, 2027. A Yongin FAB 1 Ph1 wastewater treatment work worth KRW 11.9 billion runs to January 31, 2027. A DB HiTek South Fab ph2 ultrapure water project worth KRW 15.5 billion runs to August 31, 2027.

Value Finder said these orders begin to be recognized as revenue in the second half of 2026, which would drive a turnaround. Further wastewater volumes after Yongin Phase 1 Ph2, scheduled for 2027 to 2029, and Yongin Phase 2 volumes were cited as pipeline.

Because contract values are large, progress rates and cost ratios can swing quarterly results, so revenue recognition pace and operating margin after 2026 Q4 are the key points to track. Whether the Vietnam plans outlined in 2025 turn into signed contracts is another open item.

08

Bull factors

Order backlog and large customer orders

Contracts disclosed in 2026 include large ultrapure water and piping work at Pyeongtaek P4 and P5, wastewater work in Yongin and DB HiTek's ultrapure water project. Value Finder described the order backlog as at a record level.

Sales rising for two consecutive quarters in 2026 Q2 and Q3 fits the reading that this backlog has begun converting to revenue.

Recognition of ultrapure water localization

Between 2021 and 2025 the company took part in a national ultrapure water localization program and built a 1,200-ton-per-day demonstration plant, and in April 2026 it was selected for a next-generation production project. The company has said it intends to shift toward higher-value orders on this basis. Analysts see the technical recognition as possibly widening the order base beyond major customers.

Non-EPC revenue from subsidiaries

Liquid designated waste treatment and facility O&M together account for a meaningful share of sales. One 2026 Q2 breakdown put O&M at 22% and liquid waste at 15%. These steadier businesses offset some of the volatility of construction-linked EPC earnings.

09

Bear factors

Durability of the profit recovery

The company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.74 billion in 2025 Q4 and a KRW 548 million operating loss in 2026 Q1. Operating profit returned in Q2 and Q3 of 2026, but net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters total KRW 4.2 billion. With profit not yet sustained across consecutive quarters, it is too early to call a structural change.

Cost ratios and gross margin pressure

According to EBN Industry Economy, the gross margin in 2026 Q1 fell from about 11.0% to 6.9%. Operating results also swung from a profit in the year-earlier quarter to a loss. EPC margins can fluctuate sharply quarter to quarter even when order sizes are large.

Dependence on the semiconductor cycle

The KRW 50.7 billion operating loss in 2024 was explained by a sharp drop in orders during the semiconductor downturn and heavier fixed costs. When major customers delay capital spending, revenue recognition slips with it.

Because results depend on a handful of large projects, the company is sensitive to changes in customer investment schedules.

Risk factors

Financing and working capital

The debt ratio was 148.0% at end-2025 and total liabilities were KRW 93.6 billion. Per EBN Industry Economy, receivables rose from about KRW 15 billion to KRW 23.3 billion by end-March 2026 while cash fell by about KRW 3.7 billion.

Large EPC contracts without advance payments add working capital pressure, which makes the financing side, including the KRW 11 billion private bond, a point to monitor.

Project concentration and tender timing

A large share of orders is concentrated in Pyeongtaek projects tied to Samsung E&A and in SK hynix-related work. A brokerage official said investment approval, EPC tendering and water treatment package bidding for the Honam project are still pending. If tenders slip, the revenue recognition expected from the second half of 2026 could be delayed.

Cost inflation and one-off charges

In 2024, higher raw material costs from inflation were cited as a cause of the loss. The 2025 turnaround also reflected the clearing of one-off items such as bad-debt write-offs. With a 2025 operating margin of 1.4%, a single cost overrun or provision could push a quarter back into loss.

Overall view

Hansung Cleantech swung to operating profit in 2025 and again in 2026 Q2 and Q3, and sales rose for two consecutive quarters through 2026 Q3.

Over the same period, disclosed orders concentrated on large semiconductor projects at Pyeongtaek P4 and P5 and the Yongin cluster, and an independent research house described the backlog as at a record level.

On the other side, net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters total KRW 4.2 billion, and the gross margin fell to 6.9% in 2026 Q1. The debt ratio was 148.0% at end-2025 and receivables rose by end-March 2026.

The bullish factors are order volume and technical recognition, while the bearish ones are cost ratios, working capital and dependence on tender timing. The next checkpoints are the confirmed 2026 Q4 results in the annual report and revenue recognition and operating margins as contracts end in late 2026 through 2027.
