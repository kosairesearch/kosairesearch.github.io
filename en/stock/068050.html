Drama Profitability Squeeze Leaves Losses Behind

PAN ENTERTAINMENT CO., LTD. posted an operating loss of KRW 3.47 billion in 2025 and its quarterly revenue has fallen for three straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026, making the recovery of drama production profitability the central point to track.

02

Business structure

PAN ENTERTAINMENT CO., LTD. was listed on KOSDAQ on July 7, 2006, and operates in the production and distribution of films, video works and broadcast programs.

Drama production is the core business, and since its founding in 1998 the company has worked as a commissioned drama producer, beginning with Winter Sonata in 2002. Of the KRW 123.8 billion in total 2023 revenue, drama production accounted for KRW 119.1 billion, according to 2024 press coverage.

Using a 2021 breakdown reported in a 2023 securities firm report, drama made up 83% of revenue, music and recordings 5%, variety, film and management 3%, and building leasing 9%.

Customers are domestic broadcasters and global OTT platforms, and in 2023 and 2024 two Netflix originals, including When Life Gives You Tangerines, were the largest export titles. Drama export sales fell from KRW 62.8 billion in 2023 to KRW 20.7 billion in 2024.

In film, the company acquired MEDIA CASTLE, a subsidiary that imports and distributes overseas films and animation. Its music business combines OST production with the Nuplay label. In 2024 it acquired the restaurant operator Fanfnb to enter food and beverage, which generated about KRW 500 million in revenue that year.

Commissioned drama income depends on broadcaster and OTT scheduling and production budgets. The 2023 Korean Wave white paper noted that production costs rose beyond what domestic platform operators could comfortably bear as global OTT investment expanded.

When schedules slip, revenue can concentrate in one year or disappear from another, so drama scheduling is the main driver of earnings swings.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 25.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 123.8 billion in 2023, an increase of 389.0%, making 2023 the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Revenue then fell 72.0% to KRW 34.7 billion in 2024 and edged up 1.4% to KRW 35.2 billion in 2025.

The operating margin slipped from 3.7% in 2022 and 3.5% in 2023 to 0.4% in 2024 and -9.9% in 2025. Operating profit of KRW 4.37 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.58 billion in 2023 turned profitable from the prior year.

In 2024, operating profit fell 97.1% to KRW 129 million and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.09 billion.

In 2025, the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 3.47 billion, a swing into the red from the prior year, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 7.7 billion. This is two consecutive loss-making years, 2024 and 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 deterioration to lower profitability in drama production, along with bad-debt provisions on long-term prepayments and impairment of intangible assets, which weighed on both operating and net results.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 8.13 billion in 2025, a larger loss than the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow turned to an outflow of KRW 3.37 billion in 2025 from an inflow of KRW 9.23 billion in 2024.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 60.9% in 2022 and 37.4% in 2023 to 72.7% in 2024 and 90.0% in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 4.31 billion in the third quarter of 2025 with an operating profit of KRW 324 million, then jumped to KRW 13.9 billion in the fourth quarter, up 223.5% from the prior quarter, but swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.18 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 5.45 billion.

The first quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 83.88 million on revenue of KRW 9.83 billion, while the second quarter posted revenue of KRW 7.7 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.08 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 5.14 billion, down 33.2% from the prior quarter and up 19.2% from a year earlier, with an operating loss of KRW 544 million that swung into the red year on year. Revenue has declined for three straight quarters since the fourth-quarter 2025 peak.

Whether the long-term prepayment write-offs and intangible impairments were one-off items for 2025, or will recur, is the question the next annual report will need to answer.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic drama production has grown on demand from global OTT platforms, but rising production costs are pressuring the profitability of both platforms and producers.

The 2023 Korean Wave white paper noted that production costs rose beyond what domestic platform operators could comfortably bear as global OTT investment expanded. Press analysis has suggested that even hit series may yield limited extra income for producers, depending on settlement terms with platforms.

Investment banking analysts, however, have argued that producers holding a portfolio of strong titles may secure better terms when raising funds for their next projects.

Drama production appears to be moving from a phase of expanding OTT orders to one in which production-cost burdens are rising, and scheduling and IP securing are cited as factors that separate profitability.

The company said in 2021 that it aimed to secure IP before production and to release titles simultaneously on broadcasters and OTT platforms or as OTT originals.

In drama production, Studio Dragon is the most direct point of comparison, while HYBE, SM Entertainment, JYP Ent. and YG Entertainment are music and artist-centered businesses with different economics, which limits direct comparison.

The company is widening its scope through film distribution and a music label, but its revenue still depends heavily on drama scheduling.

In September 2025, the Netflix series When Life Gives You Tangerines won the grand prize in the mid-length category of a new media content award, adding external recognition of its production capability.

Outlook

Among the company's disclosed schedules, the treasury share acquisition and the shareholders' meeting are confirmed.

On June 26, 2026, the company entered into a KRW 1.0 billion treasury share trust agreement with NH Investment & Securities, with the stated aim of enhancing shareholder value through share price stabilization. On October 23, 2026, it terminated the trust early after completing the acquisition.

Before termination, the company held 1,527,178 common shares, equal to 5.51% of total issued shares. An extraordinary shareholders' meeting is scheduled for December 21, 2026, with a record date for voting rights of November 23, 2026.

Largest shareholder and CEO Park Young-seok transferred 275,000 common shares by gift on March 4, 2026, lowering his stake to 32.16%, and his total stake including related parties is 35.93%. A related party, executive Park Sang-ik, received 137,500 shares by gift, raising his stake from 1.39% to 1.88%.

On the business side, a disclosure dated February 9, 2026 reported a production and supply contract with MBC for the drama When the Brilliant Season Comes, with a total contract value of KRW 15.2 billion.

Drama supply revenue is recognized according to production progress, so the year in which this contract is reflected in results is a point to track.

In music, the company began a global music business in July 2025 with JUNHEE, leader of the group A.C.E, as its first artist, and in August 2025 it signed a contract with singer-songwriter VAHN.

The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, and whether revenue in the fourth quarter of 2026 holds near the KRW 5.14 billion level of the third quarter is the starting point for annual results.

Risk factors

Dependence on scheduling and hits

Drama revenue depends on broadcaster and OTT scheduling and the commercial performance of each title. A securities firm analysis in 2022 noted that delays in scheduling major titles caused revenue to fall sharply.

If platforms' production-cost burdens grow, commissioned pricing and scheduling opportunities could change as well.

Financial capacity

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 56 billion and total equity was KRW 62.2 billion, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 90.0%. Operating cash flow turned to an outflow of KRW 3.37 billion in 2025. Additional bad-debt provisions or impairments could further reduce financial capacity.

Ownership structure and shareholder base

CEO Park Young-seok holds 32.16%, and including related parties the total stake is 35.93%. In March 2026, shares were transferred by gift to related parties. How the treasury shares held after the early termination of the trust are handled will depend on follow-up disclosures.

Overall view

PAN ENTERTAINMENT CO., LTD. is a commissioned drama producer whose revenue reached KRW 123.8 billion in 2023 before falling to KRW 34.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 35.2 billion in 2025.

The company posted an operating loss of KRW 3.47 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.7 billion in 2025, and operating cash flow turned to an outflow of KRW 3.37 billion.

The company attributed the 2025 deterioration to weaker drama production profitability, bad-debt provisions on long-term prepayments and intangible asset impairment. The 2026 quarterly picture is mixed.

Operating profit returned to the black in the first quarter, and the third-quarter operating loss of KRW 544 million was narrower than the second quarter's, but quarterly revenue has declined for three straight quarters since the fourth-quarter 2025 peak of KRW 13.9 billion.

The price-to-book ratio sits in a deep discount to net assets, but earnings-based multiples cannot be calculated, so valuation reading depends on net asset levels and whether earnings recover.

Strengths include drama IP and production capability and narrowing quarterly losses, while weaknesses include falling revenue, a rising debt-to-equity ratio and the possibility of further impairments.

The next reference point is the 2026 annual report, and until then quarterly revenue and profit trends are the key measures.
