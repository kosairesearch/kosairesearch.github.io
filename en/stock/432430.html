02

Business structure

Ylab is a webtoon IP and video content production company established in November 2010, listed on KOSDAQ on July 20, 2023. The company secures webtoon source material and expands its IP value through video and drama production, overseas localization, and webtoon education.

A securities research report dated April 2024 divided its business into webtoon production, video production, webtoon education, webtoon localization, and webtoon OSMU. The same report put the shares of major customers at 52.4% for Naver Webtoon and 39.0% for one drama production client.

According to the company, video business revenue reached KRW 25.1 billion in 2025, up 91.5% from KRW 13.1 billion in 2024, and this segment was the main driver of total revenue growth in 2025. In February 2025 the company signed an original series production deal with Netflix for a popular webtoon IP.

Its Japanese subsidiary, Ylab Studios, received a JPY 1.5 billion investment from BANDAI NAMCO in 2024 and is developing IP planning and development. The company also recently acquired a K-POP artist IP-based fandom business, which it says is strengthening its IP monetization structure.

In February 2025 it launched a communication efficiency solution called Oz Viewer, and through a second-tier subsidiary it is pursuing development of a webtoon AI solution. In terms of competition, Studio Dragon and CJ ENM are the listed drama and video production peers most often compared with Ylab. Kang Cheol-gu, appointed CEO on March 31, 2026, previously served as CEO of Studio Dragon.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 29.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 19.4 billion in 2023, a 34.8% decline, before rising to KRW 25 billion in 2024 (up 28.6%) and KRW 37.1 billion in 2025 (up 48.5%), marking two consecutive years of growth. Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year for revenue and 2023 the smallest.

Operating losses were KRW 435 million in 2022, KRW 5.51 billion in 2023, KRW 10 billion in 2024, and KRW 8.74 billion in 2025, a loss in each of four consecutive years, with the loss narrowing in 2025. The operating margin was -1.5% in 2022, -28.4% in 2023, -40.2% in 2024, and -23.6% in 2025.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 572 million, KRW 6.08 billion, KRW 8.35 billion, and KRW 6.74 billion over 2022 to 2025, while the 2025 net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 8.63 billion, a different figure.

The company cited growth in its video production segment as the main reason for the 2025 revenue increase and improved profitability. Operating cash flow went from KRW 680 million in 2022 to -KRW 1.67 billion in 2023, -KRW 14.6 billion in 2024, and -KRW 11.5 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 31 billion, of which KRW 27.6 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 9.81 billion, with the debt ratio falling from 73.7% in 2022 to 31.6% in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue fell from KRW 10 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.23 billion in the fourth quarter (down 87.7% from the prior quarter), then rose to KRW 12.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 20.5 billion in the second, and KRW 21.4 billion in the third, three consecutive quarters of growth; third-quarter revenue was up 114.1% year on year.

Operating losses were KRW 2.13 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 2.1 billion in the fourth, and KRW 4.05 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then narrowed to KRW 1.98 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.06 billion in the third.

The first quarter of 2026 shows revenue rising while the loss widened, so revenue scale and profitability have not yet moved together. A quarterly report covering January to September 2026 reportedly showed cumulative revenue of KRW 54.7 billion and an operating loss of KRW 7.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The webtoon industry has seen growth slow since the pandemic due to oversupply of content, according to an assessment cited by a securities firm. Within this environment, whether demand for adapting source IP into video becomes a stable revenue stream is the key variable for the sector.

Ylab's video business has grown as a share of revenue, and video segment growth lifted total results in 2025. Studio Dragon, a drama production peer, is often cited for export results through partnerships with major OTT platforms.

Ylab's CEO Kang Cheol-gu is a former Studio Dragon CEO, which means drama production know-how is being brought into the management team.

Video revenue can fluctuate quarterly depending on title scheduling and release timing, so whether the rise from KRW 12.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 21.4 billion in the third continues will need to be checked against later quarterly results.

On the source side, the relationship with major webtoon platforms matters, and a 2024 securities report listed one platform at 52.4% of the shares it presented. In the broadcasting and OTT production market, competition for IP is intense, so original-work contract terms and production costs shape profitability.

Within the sector, Ylab's operating margin of -23.6% in 2025 places it in loss territory, a meaningful difference from larger media and entertainment peers that are profitable at the operating level.

The company has posted losses for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, with a combined KRW 5.92 billion net loss over the last four quarters. No turn to profit is reflected in the disclosures so far.

Operating cash outflow

Operating cash flow was -KRW 14.6 billion in 2024 and -KRW 11.5 billion in 2025, negative in both years. It was positive at KRW 680 million in 2022 before turning negative in 2023. Even as the reported loss narrows, continued cash outflow would keep reducing financial flexibility.

Quarterly volatility and dilution risk

Risk factors

Dependence on video adaptation results

Video business revenue can vary sharply from quarter to quarter depending on title scheduling, release timing, and audience performance. The gap between KRW 1.23 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 12.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026 illustrates this. Changes in contract terms or partner relationships with major platforms could also shift the revenue structure.

Funding needs and share dilution

A KRW 2 billion private convertible bond was issued in November 2026, with proceeds planned for use as KRW 500 million in 2026 and KRW 1.5 billion in 2027. Common shares issued on conversion were disclosed at about 5.97% of total shares outstanding. With operating cash outflow continuing, additional funding could become necessary.

Management transition and governance

On March 31, 2026, Kang Cheol-gu was appointed CEO at the annual general meeting and founder Yoon In-wan took the role of board chairman.

On the same day, a candidate elected as an outside director for the audit committee withdrew, leaving a vacancy, and the company said the director's rights and duties continue under the Commercial Act until a successor is elected.

Overall view

Ylab's revenue has grown in 2024 and 2025 and has risen for three consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, driven by the video production business.

The operating margin improved from -40.2% in 2024 to -23.6% in 2025, but operating losses have persisted for four consecutive years and a KRW 1.06 billion operating loss remained in the third quarter of 2026.

Operating cash flow was negative in both 2024 and 2025, and whether improved profit is converting into cash has not yet been confirmed. The debt ratio fell from 73.7% in 2022 to 31.6% in 2025, but the KRW 2 billion convertible bond issued in November 2026 introduces possible share dilution.

Strengths include sustained revenue growth, a narrowing loss, and overseas IP partnerships, while weaknesses include long-running losses, cash outflow, and quarterly volatility. The next checkpoint is whether the new management's global IP expansion translates into revenue and cash generation.

The next regular report is the 2026 annual business report, and the operating loss and operating cash flow direction in the first-quarter 2027 results will serve as the factual benchmark.
