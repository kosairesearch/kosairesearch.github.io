02

Business structure

Intekplus was founded in 1995 and manufactures appearance inspection equipment. Its main products are 3D and 2D automated optical inspection systems and modules that acquire and analyze surface image data using machine vision. The business is organized into three divisions.

Division 1 covers appearance inspection for semiconductor packages, SSDs and memory modules, Division 2 covers flip-chip and package substrate inspection, and Division 3 covers secondary battery and display inspection equipment.

A July 2024 securities report noted slow order recovery in Division 1 and a North American customer's investment delay, while the outlook for Division 2 was lowered on weak substrate demand. Samsung Electronics and SK hynix are reported as its major domestic customers.

A February 2024 report said Intekplus was working with SK hynix on inspection equipment for HBM module testing. In September 2026, reports said the company won its first order for glass-substrate R&D automated optical inspection equipment from a major Japanese substrate maker.

A KRW 30.9 billion substrate inspection equipment contract disclosed in October 2026 was the largest single supply contract disclosed that year.

An August 2023 securities report discussed Onto Innovation and Camtek alongside Intekplus, which it classified as a supplier of package appearance and bump inspection equipment.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue fell 37.1% from KRW 118.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 74.8 billion in 2023, then rose 12.1% to KRW 83.9 billion in 2024 and 7.0% to KRW 89.8 billion in 2025, marking two consecutive years of growth from 2024 to 2025.

A 2024 press report attributed the 2023 decline to production cuts by existing customers.

Operating profit was KRW 19.4 billion in 2022 (16.3% margin), turned into an operating loss of KRW 11.1 billion in 2023, widened to a KRW 15.6 billion loss in 2024 (-18.6% margin), and narrowed to a KRW 3.73 billion loss in 2025 (-4.2% margin), so losses ran for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 16.3 billion in 2022 to net losses of KRW 10.8 billion in 2023, KRW 11.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.31 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of KRW 3.32 billion in 2025, close to the controlling-shareholder figure, so the minority effect was small. Operating cash flow was -KRW 7.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.74 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio eased from 172.6% in 2024 to 158.6% in 2025, but total liabilities of KRW 72.9 billion at end-2025 still exceeded total equity of KRW 46 billion. Quarterly results are uneven.

After an operating loss of KRW 4.92 billion on revenue of KRW 9.93 billion in the third quarter of 2025, the fourth quarter brought revenue of KRW 40.3 billion (up 305.9% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 4.08 billion.

The first quarter of 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 1.91 billion on revenue of KRW 12.8 billion, and the second quarter's operating loss widened to KRW 5.12 billion on revenue of KRW 8.96 billion.

The third quarter of 2026 posted revenue of KRW 28.2 billion, up 183.8% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 3.54 billion, a swing from a year-earlier loss. Controlling-shareholder net income for the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.16 billion.

After the third-quarter 2025 results, securities analysts expected a fourth-quarter rebound from the recognition of deferred shipments. Inspection equipment has separate order and revenue recognition timing, which makes quarterly results lumpy.

05

Industry analysis

The back-end semiconductor inspection market is seeing inspection equipment gain importance as investment in HBM and advanced packaging grows. An August 2023 securities report saw more inspection and measurement steps being needed to raise yields in stacked and heterogeneous integration structures.

In the same report, Intekplus was classified as a supplier of package appearance and bump inspection equipment, and Onto Innovation and Camtek were named as overseas competitors.

A July 2024 securities report expected additional orders for 2.5D packaging inspection equipment as domestic customers expanded HBM production. The same report judged that Intekplus had a comparative technology edge in large-area, high-speed inspection.

At the same time, it noted that order recovery in Division 1's semiconductor appearance inspection was not fast, and the outlook for Division 2 was lowered on weak substrate demand.

In 2026, a KRW 30.9 billion substrate inspection contract disclosed in October and a first glass-substrate inspection order reported in September widened the range of applications from packages to substrates and glass substrates.

Bull factors

Expanding order backlog

According to the 2026 half-year report, the semiconductor inspection equipment backlog stood at KRW 103.9 billion, up 66.6% from a year earlier, with total orders of KRW 120.2 billion. The KRW 30.9 billion substrate inspection contract disclosed in October 2026 added to that base. Bulls cite this as evidence of a broader order foundation.

Repeated quarterly swings to profit

Operating profit turned positive in the fourth quarter of 2025 and the third quarter of 2026. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 28.2 billion rose 183.8% year on year. The annual loss narrowed in 2025 after three consecutive loss years, which reads as a direction of earnings recovery.

Expansion from packages to substrates and glass

A 2024 press report discussed talks with SK hynix on HBM module appearance inspection equipment. In September 2026 the company won its first glass-substrate inspection order from a Japanese substrate maker. Bulls argue that the range of applications is widening from packages to substrates and glass substrates.

Bear factors

Annual losses and a thin earnings base

Operating losses and controlling-shareholder net losses ran for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 46 billion against total liabilities of KRW 72.9 billion. Controlling-shareholder net income for the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.16 billion, leaving the earnings base thin.

Large swings in quarterly results

Second-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 8.96 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 5.12 billion, and the loss widened from the prior quarter. Compared with KRW 40.3 billion of revenue in the fourth quarter of 2025, the quarterly spread is very wide. Because order and revenue recognition timing differ, results can cluster in particular quarters.

Shortfall against outlook and customer concentration

An April 2026 securities report projected a second-quarter operating margin of -1%, but the actual second-quarter operating loss was KRW 5.12 billion. Major domestic customers are concentrated in Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, so their investment adjustments feed directly into results.

Risk factors

Customer concentration and investment cycles

Revenue has been volatile when major customers adjusted investment, including Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. The 2023 revenue decline was reported to stem from production cuts by existing customers.

A July 2024 securities analysis that North American customer investment delays slowed Division 1 order recovery points in the same direction.

Balance sheet and cash flow

The debt ratio at end-2025 was 158.6%, with total liabilities of KRW 72.9 billion. Operating cash flow was -KRW 7.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.74 billion in 2025, a wide swing between years. If revenue recognition lags orders, working capital needs could rise.

Technology competition and differentiation

Intekplus is compared with overseas inspection equipment makers such as Onto Innovation and Camtek, so technology differentiation and order retention are the key issues.

A July 2024 securities report judged that it had an edge in large-area, high-speed inspection, but whether that edge carries over into new processes still needs to be confirmed.

Overall view

Intekplus posted operating profit in 2022, then losses from 2023, and in 2025 the operating loss narrowed to KRW 3.73 billion. Operating profit turned positive in the fourth quarter of 2025 and the third quarter of 2026, while the first and second quarters of 2026 were loss-making.

Controlling-shareholder net income for the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.16 billion, so the earnings base is still thin. By contrast, the backlog stood at KRW 103.9 billion at end-June 2026, up 66.6% year on year, and a KRW 30.9 billion substrate inspection contract was disclosed in October 2026.

The first glass-substrate inspection order and the widening of HBM and substrate inspection applications show a broader business scope. Customer concentration, quarterly swings and a 158.6% debt ratio remain financial and operational burdens.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin and year-end backlog in the 2026 annual report, and whether profit persists in the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report.
