Report overview

Optrontec: Operating profit returns, net losses persist

Optrontec recorded operating profit in each quarter of 2026 so far, yet attributable net losses have run for five consecutive reported quarters, so the turnaround is not yet complete.

  1. 1

    Revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 totaled KRW 179.2 billion, about 92% of full-year 2025 revenue of KRW 195.7 billion, which followed two consecutive years of decline.

  2. 2

    Operating profit was KRW 4.41 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 2.65 billion in Q2 and KRW 2.98 billion in Q3, all positive. Attributable net loss was the widest among the quarters reported in Q4 2025 at KRW 18.2 billion and narrowed to KRW 140 million in Q3 2026.

  3. 3

    The new businesses are automotive camera lenses supplied to a North American automaker since January 2026, optical image stabilization (OIS) actuators produced at the Vietnam plant, and optical parts for military drones sold to a European defense contractor. The company targets a roughly 50:50 split between mobile and non-mobile sales in 2027.

  4. 4

    Financial strain remains: the debt-to-equity ratio rose from 291.3% at end-2025 to 339.2% at end-H1 2026, and the company issued KRW 35 billion of convertible bonds in May 2026.

  5. 5

    The company's 2026 revenue target of KRW 265.4 billion is higher than the KRW 228.7 billion forecast SK Securities published in its July 2026 report.

02

Business structure

Optrontec, founded in May 1999, is an optical components specialist that makes optical actuators for smartphone cameras, image-sensor filters, and optical lenses and modules.

Of cumulative revenue of KRW 179.2 billion in the first three quarters of 2026, optical actuators accounted for KRW 84.7 billion (47.3%), image-sensor filters KRW 50.8 billion (28.3%) and optical lenses and modules KRW 37.8 billion (21.1%), according to the company announcement of November 12, 2026.

Full-year 2025 splits differ by source: The Electronic Times reported filter sales of KRW 58.8 billion (30%) and optical actuator sales of KRW 94.8 billion (48%), while SK Securities presented a mix of 55% OIS, 30% filters and 15% lenses.

The customer base is also shifting, as the company said the share of sales from its largest customer fell from 76% in 2020 to 47% in 2025. The main new business is automotive camera lenses, supplied to a North American automaker since January 2026, which The Electronic Times reported at roughly 2 million units a year.

The Vietnam subsidiary runs a self-developed hinge-type OIS line, and its first line started operating in April 2026. In defense, the company has received production orders for optical parts used in military drones from a European defense contractor.

SK Securities introduced Optrontec as the only optical filter developer in Korea. Revenue from defense and robotics has not been disclosed separately.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 199.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 232.1 billion in 2023, up 16.3% year on year, before falling 6.1% to KRW 218 billion in 2024 and 10.2% to KRW 195.7 billion in 2025, which made two consecutive years of decline from 2024 to 2025.

Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest. Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 48.9 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 15 billion in 2023, then back to operating losses of KRW 4.45 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.11 billion in 2025, two consecutive loss-making years.

The operating margin went from 6.5% in 2023 to -2.0% in 2024 and -1.6% in 2025.

The company cited a 2025 impairment related to its China business and investment assets, the build-out of a North American automotive lens line, and OIS equipment setup with low initial utilization as 2025 drags (The Elec, reported in September 2026).

Attributable net income was KRW 26.8 billion in 2024, up 188.9%, but turned into an attributable net loss of KRW 21.8 billion in 2025.

In the first half of 2026 operating profit was KRW 7.1 billion, but financing costs rose to KRW 13.4 billion from KRW 8 billion a year earlier, and the net loss widened to KRW 3.3 billion (Blotter, reported in November 2026).

By quarter, Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 58.7 billion, up 29.9% from the prior quarter, yet the company posted an operating loss of KRW 5.42 billion and an attributable net loss of KRW 18.2 billion.

Q1 2026 turned to operating profit of KRW 4.41 billion on revenue of KRW 59.3 billion, which the company attributed mainly to fixed-cost reductions from the restructuring carried out in 2025 (The Bell, reported in May 2026).

Q2 revenue was KRW 62.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 2.65 billion, down 40.1% from the prior quarter. Q3 revenue was KRW 57.4 billion, up 27.0% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 2.98 billion, up 224.8% year on year.

Attributable net losses were KRW 573 million in Q1 2026, KRW 2.73 billion in Q2 and KRW 140 million in Q3. Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 35.2 billion in 2024 to positive KRW 838 million in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The company judges that the mobile optics market has entered a mature stage. Slower smartphone growth and continued pressure to cut component prices have pushed Optrontec to extend its optical technology into automotive applications.

On the automotive side, demand is supported by rising camera content in advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) and autonomous vehicles.

Autonomous driving lenses belong to the automotive electronics market, which has longer product lives and higher reliability requirements, and this fits the company's aim to reduce dependence on smartphone demand and on the product strategy of a single customer.

In defense, the reshaping of European supply chains is a variable, as European buyers are moving to reduce reliance on China. The company said its Korean R&D organization and production bases in Korea, Vietnam and China allow it to respond to Non-China supply-chain requirements.

It is also expanding production lines for robotaxis and humanoid robots, which it describes as physical AI markets. On competition, SK Securities said rivals are scaling back and volumes are being reallocated.

Few domestic companies develop optical filters in-house, so whether filter technology can carry from mobile into automotive and defense will shape the company's position.

06

Outlook

The company's 2026 revenue target is KRW 265.4 billion, about 36% above 2025 revenue. Its medium-term targets are KRW 474.5 billion for 2028 and KRW 533.6 billion for 2030.

Measured against the first three quarters of 2026, which total KRW 179.2 billion, reaching the target requires about KRW 86.2 billion of revenue in Q4, roughly 1.5 times the KRW 57.4 billion reported in Q3.

SK Securities, in its July 2026 report, forecast 2026 revenue of KRW 228.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 14 billion, a return to profit; on that forecast, Q4 would need revenue of about KRW 49.5 billion and operating profit of about KRW 4 billion. Timing differs across businesses.

Supply expansion for the North American autonomous driving lens, which had been expected in the first half of 2026, was pushed back after the customer's software update, and the timing of hardware supply was adjusted in Q2. The company says it is preparing for a resumption of supply in the second half.

Of six fully automated OIS lines at the Vietnam plant, two have been set up and are in mass production, and once all six are built, annual capacity will exceed KRW 150 billion. The company expects OIS to contribute to revenue from the second half of 2026.

In defense, it plans to secure a large mass-production order at year-end 2026. It is also pursuing production line expansion for robotaxis and humanoid robots. Its 2027 goal is to bring mobile and non-mobile revenue shares each to about 50%. SK Securities forecast capital expenditure falling from KRW 35 billion in 2026 to KRW 13 billion in 2027.

Bull factors

Quarterly operating profit in the black

Operating profit turned positive in Q1 2026 at KRW 4.41 billion, then came in at KRW 2.65 billion in Q2 and KRW 2.98 billion in Q3, all in the black. Q3 operating profit rose 224.8% year on year. The company attributes the Q1 result mainly to fixed-cost reductions from the 2025 restructuring. The operating margin was -2.0% in 2024 and -1.6% in 2025, and has been positive on a quarterly basis in 2026.

Mass-production base for new businesses

Mass supply of autonomous driving camera lenses to a North American automaker has continued since January 2026. Two of the six OIS lines at the Vietnam plant are set up and in mass production, and once all six are built annual capacity will exceed KRW 150 billion.

The company has also received a mass-production order for military drone optics from a European defense contractor. It has set a 2027 goal of bringing non-mobile revenue to about half of total sales.

Customer diversification and filter technology

Dependence on a single large customer has eased, pointing to a more diversified customer base. SK Securities introduced Optrontec as the only domestic optical filter developer in Korea and said the company developed all new optical filters for strategic domestic customers over the past 15 years. Image-sensor filters accounted for 28.3% of revenue in the first three quarters of 2026.

Q4 2025 posted KRW 18.2 billion, Q2 2026 widened to KRW 2.73 billion and Q3 2026 narrowed to KRW 140 million. Full-year 2025 attributable net loss was KRW 21.8 billion.

Revenue recovery pace and schedule delays

Risk factors

Balance sheet

Total liabilities were KRW 170.3 billion against equity of KRW 58.5 billion at end-2025. With the debt-to-equity ratio at 339.2% at end-H1 2026, financing costs could offset gains at the operating level.

The repayment and conversion terms of the May 2026 convertible bonds, along with the trend in financing costs, are the next points to watch.

Customer concentration and demand

The largest customer still accounted for 47% of sales in 2025. Smartphone camera components remain a business where results depend heavily on a specific customer's product strategy and demand. The autonomous driving lens supply schedule has already slipped once because of a customer software update.

New-business monetization and impairments

The company said OIS is at an early stage of mass production, so depreciation of new equipment and yield-stabilization costs are being recognized first. Losses in 2025 included impairments related to the China business and investment assets.

Because defense and robotics revenue is not broken out, the point at which these businesses contribute to profit cannot be confirmed in figures.

12

Overall view

After an operating loss in 2022 and operating losses in 2024 and 2025, Optrontec posted operating profit in each quarter of 2026 so far. Revenue for the first three quarters totals KRW 179.2 billion, about 92% of full-year 2025 revenue, and the quarterly operating margin has been positive.

The new businesses have moved into mass supply or production stages in North American autonomous driving lenses, Vietnam OIS and European defense drone optics. Against that, attributable net losses have run for five consecutive reported quarters, and the last four quarters total KRW 21.7 billion.

The debt-to-equity ratio was 339.2% at end-H1 2026, and financing costs are offsetting operating gains. Reaching the company's target requires about KRW 86.2 billion of Q4 revenue, and SK Securities' forecast implies Q4 operating profit of about KRW 4 billion.

The key variables are whether operating profit carries through to attributable net income and how customer schedules and OIS utilization show up in results. This report is provided for information only and does not state an investment opinion or target price.
