02

Business structure

SGA Solutions is an integrated IT security company whose core products cover server security, application security and endpoint security.

In an interview published in March 2025, the company said server security accounted for about 60% of revenue, application security 25% and endpoint security 15%, a mix that predates the Creon Unity consolidation.

Its flagship server security product, RedCastle, enforces user behavior controls at the operating system kernel level, and the company said it held the top share of public-sector server security for two consecutive years. The structural change came from the consolidation of Creon Unity, acquired in 2025.

Of first-half 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 75.1 billion, the hardware supply and operations segment contributed KRW 47.66 billion, about 63.5%.

Over the same period, the solutions segment generated KRW 20.8 billion, up 24.5% from a year earlier, and the investment segment recorded KRW 6.6 billion in revenue and KRW 2.7 billion in operating profit.

In 2026 the company also merged subsidiaries including SGA EPS to build an integrated base for products and sales teams. Its main customers are government agencies, public institutions and financial firms, where demand is driven by the N2SF transition and zero-trust adoption.

In competition, the company overlaps with other listed security specialists such as AhnLab and with large systems integrators in public and financial markets. The revenue mix therefore combines solution sales with hardware supply and operations.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 37.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 54.7 billion in 2023, a 46.6% increase, then fell to KRW 42.9 billion in 2024, a 21.6% decline, before reaching KRW 61.6 billion in 2025, a 43.6% increase and the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 3.84 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.28 billion in 2023, a 66.8% decline, and the company then posted an operating loss of KRW 4.11 billion in 2024. In 2025 it returned to operating profit of KRW 14.8 billion, lifting the operating margin from -9.6% to 24.1%.

ZDNet reported the 2025 operating profit of KRW 14.8 billion as a record, and the company said the results reflected cash recovered from investment affiliates.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 17.5 billion in 2025, while net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 17 billion, a different figure.

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 9.01 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 8.67 billion in the fourth quarter, after an operating loss of KRW 2.86 billion in the first half of 2025 according to the August 2026 release. The 2025 turnaround therefore leaned heavily on the second half.

Revenue in the first quarter of 2026 was KRW 26 billion, up 11.5% quarter on quarter, but operating profit fell to KRW 761 million, down 91.2%. In the second quarter revenue reached KRW 49.1 billion, up 89.3%, and operating profit recovered to KRW 1.36 billion.

The company announced first-half 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 75.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.13 billion on August 18, 2026. Third-quarter revenue was KRW 32.3 billion, down 34.2% quarter on quarter but up 61.6% year on year, and the quarter swung to an operating loss of KRW 256 million.

The company said it had spent early costs on reorganizing sales and technical support and on pursuing large project orders, and whether those costs are temporary is a point for the next periodic report to clarify.

The debt ratio climbed from 66.3% in 2022 to 186.9% in 2025, while operating cash flow rose to KRW 14.1 billion in 2025 from KRW 8.89 billion in 2024.

05

Industry analysis

Demand in the sector centers on zero-trust adoption and the transition to the National Network Security Framework (N2SF).

The company's five 2026 cybersecurity forecasts were stronger internal controls, full-scale N2SF adoption, more attacks on aging servers, more advanced generative AI-based phishing, and supply-chain ransomware.

N2SF adoption has accelerated among government ministries and public agencies and was added as a scored item in cybersecurity evaluation indicators, making it a required public-sector initiative.

Early in 2026, the spread of AI models capable of autonomous vulnerability detection raised cybersecurity concern, and the theme lifted zero-trust-related shares on the day.

The company argues that cloud migration, server build-outs and AI data center expansion widen the set of protected assets and access paths, which raises server infrastructure security demand. In financial markets, micro-segmentation is drawing attention as a way to limit lateral movement after a breach.

Competitively, SGA Solutions has a strong position in public-sector server security, and in financial services it has taken a reseller partnership with a global zero-trust segmentation company to extend coverage from applications down to the operating system and kernel. 06

Outlook

The company has described 2026 as the first year of broader zero-trust adoption and has said it aims to secure a leading position in public and financial markets.

In February 2026 it framed 2026 to 2027 as a period in which top-line growth and stronger earnings capacity would appear together, and it expected growth to accelerate in 2026 as Creon Unity results were fully consolidated.

In its August 2026 release, the company said it would speed the conversion of second-half public and financial server security and zero-trust project wins into revenue to improve solutions-segment profitability.

That plan has not yet shown up in third-quarter 2026 results, so the next check point is the trend in solutions-segment profitability after the fourth quarter of 2026.

On partnerships, in October 2026 the company signed a reseller agreement with Illumio, a global zero-trust segmentation company, and plans to link its RedCastle and vAegis server access and kernel-level technologies with Illumio's application segmentation.

The strategy was presented at the FISCON 2026 financial security conference on November 9, 2026 as full-stack micro-segmentation. On the shareholder side, in February 2026 the company said it would pursue a share consolidation that changes the par value from KRW 100 to KRW 500.

The company has given directional rather than numerical outlook, so order volumes and revenue timing will be verifiable only through periodic reports.

08

Bull factors

Zero-trust and N2SF demand expansion

The company says the adoption of N2SF has become a required public-sector task, which is lifting demand for server access control and account authentication. It says zero-trust overlays can be applied in stages on top of existing infrastructure. If this demand converts into second-half orders, the path to better solutions-segment profitability opens.

Consolidation provides a profit base

First-half 2026 consolidated operating profit of KRW 2.13 billion came in above the total because Creon Unity's operating profit alone exceeded it. The company says the consolidation was the core driver of the return to profit. The hardware supply and operations segment reported first-half operating profit of KRW 2.95 billion.

Realization gains in the investment portfolio

The investment segment posted first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 6.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.7 billion, with fund liquidation proceeds contributing. The company has said further recovery events such as IPOs and M&A remain possible. If realizations continue, they could cushion losses in the core business.

09

Bear factors

Third-quarter swing to loss and consecutive losses

The third quarter of 2026 recorded an operating loss of KRW 256 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.64 billion, the third straight loss quarter since the first quarter of 2026. Compared with operating profit of KRW 9.01 billion in the third quarter of 2025, the swing is large. Whether the up-front costs the company cited are temporary is the most important point to check.

Distribution-heavy revenue and weaker solutions profit

Press reports put IT distribution products at 64.26% of first-half 2026 revenue, and analysts note that the mix of solutions revenue and distribution margins determines earnings quality.

The security solutions business grew revenue but recorded a wider operating loss, and on a standalone basis the first-half operating loss widened from KRW 1.2 billion to KRW 3.4 billion.

Higher debt ratio

The debt ratio rose from 66.3% in 2022 to 186.9% in 2025, and total liabilities of KRW 99 billion at year-end 2025 exceeded total equity of KRW 53 billion, of which equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 34.5 billion. Debt that grew alongside consolidation and investment activity could make earnings more sensitive to swings.

10

Risk factors

Timing of orders and revenue conversion

The company says it will accelerate the conversion of second-half orders into revenue, but public projects can see revenue recognition slip with budgets and procurement timetables. If costs for large project pursuits are incurred before revenue follows, quarterly earnings will remain volatile.

Dependence on consolidated subsidiary results

About 63.5% of first-half 2026 consolidated revenue came from the hardware supply and operations segment, so results at the subsidiary level drive the consolidated outcome.

The drop from KRW 49.1 billion of revenue in the second quarter to KRW 32.3 billion in the third is an example of the quarterly volatility this structure produces.

Balance sheet and share consolidation

The debt ratio of 186.9% is high, and total liabilities exceed total equity. In February 2026 the company said it would pursue a share consolidation changing the par value from KRW 100 to KRW 500. With a major shareholder group holding 42.68%, the free float is limited, a factor that can amplify share price swings.

12

Overall view

SGA Solutions' reported revenue grew from KRW 37.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 61.6 billion in 2025, and its operating margin moved from -9.6% in 2024 to 24.1% in 2025, a swing from loss to profit. Quarterly results have been volatile in 2026, however.

After operating profit of KRW 761 million in the first quarter and KRW 1.36 billion in the second, the third quarter swung to an operating loss of KRW 256 million, and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have run for three consecutive quarters.

Much of the top-line growth came from the Creon Unity consolidation and from distribution and hardware businesses.

Bullish factors include N2SF and zero-trust demand and realization gains in the investment portfolio, while bearish factors include the third-quarter loss, the debt ratio of 186.9%, and how long the up-front costs persist.

Both sets of factors carry comparable weight, and which side prevails is a matter for the figures in the periodic reports. The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and solutions-segment profitability in the first half of 2027.
