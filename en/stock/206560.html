02

Business structure

Dexter studios is a Seoul-based content production company whose core business is visual effects (VFX) for films, dramas and advertising, and it has expanded into digital intermediate, virtual production and sound work.

In the 2025 Q3 quarterly report's revenue-by-type table, domestic VFX production accounted for about 40.5% of revenue, advertising services about 38.8%, and content production and investment income about 19.1%.

On a full-year 2024 basis, advertising services were 42.1%, domestic VFX production 41.3% and content production and investment income 8.4%. VFX had accounted for 99.4% of standalone revenue in 2023, so the revenue mix has broadened since then.

Through its subsidiary Dexter Pictures the company also develops its own drama IP. A long-term post-production contract with Netflix was signed in July 2021, and the company handled VFX for the Netflix series Parasyte: The Grey. In April 2026 it acquired the influencer marketing firm Mediance.

Its main competitors are global VFX houses such as ILM, DNEG, Weta Digital and MPC, and the company positions itself on a premium strategy against them.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 65.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 67.7 billion in 2023, up 2.7% year on year, then fell to KRW 53.7 billion in 2024, down 20.7%. Revenue recovered to KRW 55.4 billion in 2025, up 3.2%, but remained below the 2023 level.

Operating losses were KRW 2.51 billion in 2022, KRW 41.36 million in 2023, KRW 8.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.51 billion in 2025, which is four consecutive years of operating losses. The 2023 loss narrowed sharply, widened again in 2024 along with the revenue decline, and narrowed in 2025.

Operating margins were -3.8% in 2022, -0.1% in 2023, -16.6% in 2024 and -11.7% in 2025. Attributable net losses were KRW 6.17 billion in 2022, KRW 886 million in 2023, KRW 10.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.13 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 change mainly to labor-cost reductions and one-time non-operating income from an asset sale; the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 6.79 billion.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 3.39 billion in 2022, KRW 11.8 billion in 2023 and KRW 2.62 billion in 2024 to -KRW 7.71 billion in 2025. On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 13.3 billion in Q3 2025 with operating profit of KRW 998 million, then KRW 15.5 billion in Q4 2025 with operating profit of KRW 325 million.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 10.6 billion with an operating loss of KRW 3.85 billion, a turn into loss, and Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 9.85 billion came with a wider operating loss of KRW 5.07 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 19.2 billion was up 94.6% from the prior quarter and 43.6% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 131 million turned positive. Attributable net loss in the same quarter was KRW 83.1 million, so losses continued. The last four quarters together show an attributable net loss of KRW 7.24 billion.

Because revenue growth has not yet translated into quarterly profit, it is worth checking whether the Q3 turn came mainly from cost cuts or from revenue recovery.

05

Industry analysis

VFX production is a post-production business whose order flow follows the number of films, dramas and advertising projects. At its Q3 2026 results announcement on November 16, 2026, the company cited a difficult environment with fewer film and drama productions.

On the other side, one analysis dated September 6, 2026 pointed to rising overseas VFX demand from global OTT platforms expanding investment in Korean content. VFX for films is also described as high-risk, high-return, which makes earnings volatile.

Among competitors, the company is compared with global houses such as ILM, DNEG, Weta Digital and MPC, and domestically it differentiates itself by bundling digital intermediate, virtual production and sound work. Advertising services at roughly 40% of revenue make results sensitive to ad-market cycles.

The company attributed the 2024 revenue decline to slow recovery in the film and advertising industries, which reduced new orders, as reported alongside its 2024 preliminary results.

R&D spending fell from the low-KRW 2 billion range in 2021 to the high-KRW 1 billion range in 2023, and the company is leaning on AI-based production processes to cut fixed costs.

06

Outlook

The company disclosed a drama VFX contract signed in September 2026. The contract amount is KRW 8.5 billion, disclosed as 15.34% of revenue, with a contract period from September 21, 2026 to January 14, 2028. Separately, a drama project with a total contract value of KRW 9.8 billion was reported as won.

Through Mediance, acquired in April 2026, the company is also expanding into influencer marketing. Management said it will focus on projects under way in Korea, Japan, China and India. It also said it will continue cutting costs through AI-based production processes and operating efficiency.

A November 2026 report covered an AI VFX media art exhibition, Flashback Gyerim, which points to continued expansion into consumer-facing businesses. First-half 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 20.4 billion fell 23.3% year on year, so whether the Q3 revenue rise carries into Q4 is the next thing to check.

The company has also proposed a share consolidation under which two common shares of KRW 500 par value become one share of KRW 1,000 par value, and the effect of that change will be watched.

Bull factors

Cost structure improvement in Q3

The company reported that standalone operating expenses in Q3 2026 fell 19.2% year on year to KRW 6.26 billion. Cumulative standalone operating losses narrowed 24.1%, from KRW 4.24 billion to KRW 3.22 billion. Management attributed the reduction to AI-based production processes that lowered fixed costs.

Signs of revenue recovery

Consolidated revenue in Q3 2026 rose 43.6% year on year to KRW 19.2 billion. Cumulative VFX revenue rose 6.8% to KRW 17.5 billion over the first three quarters. Quarterly revenue in Q3 2026 is the highest of the last five quarters in the filed results.

Order pipeline

A drama VFX contract worth KRW 8.5 billion was signed in September 2026 and runs to January 14, 2028. A drama project with a total contract value of KRW 9.8 billion was also reported. Expanding investment in Korean content by global OTT platforms is cited as a driver of overseas VFX demand.

Bear factors

Cumulative losses and cash flow

Operating losses were recorded in each year from 2022 to 2025, and operating cash flow turned to -KRW 7.71 billion in 2025. Attributable net losses have continued for three consecutive quarters since Q1 2026.

Wider consolidated cumulative loss

On the Q3 2026 quarterly report basis, consolidated cumulative operating loss widened from KRW 6.83 billion a year earlier to KRW 8.79 billion. Standalone cumulative losses narrowed, but the consolidated figure moved the other way. The breakdown of that gap needs to be checked in the quarterly report.

Equity decline and revenue weakness

Total equity fell from KRW 43.3 billion at the end of 2025 to KRW 38.1 billion at the end of June 2026. The debt ratio rose from 111.8% in 2024 to 156.1% in 2025. First-half 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 20.4 billion declined 23.3% year on year.

Risk factors

Regulatory and legal risk

The Financial Services Commission finalized a penalty of KRW 946.3 million on Dexter studios for accounting violations, and a combined KRW 167.3 million on two former executives. A prosecutorial probe is ongoing, and its outcome could add costs or reputational impact. No timing for the outcome has been confirmed.

Listing maintenance risk

A September 2026 report noted that with the share price below KRW 1,000, the company faced a possible administrative-issue designation for low-priced stocks. The company is moving to a share consolidation that changes the par value basis. Post-consolidation trading and share price will determine whether listing criteria are met.

Order volatility and revenue concentration

VFX revenue moves with film and drama volume and new orders, and VFX accounted for 99.4% of standalone revenue in 2023. Revenue fell 20.7% in 2024 as new orders declined. Advertising and content production have grown as shares of revenue, but earnings remain sensitive to project timing.

Overall view

Dexter studios recorded consolidated revenue of KRW 19.2 billion in Q3 2026, up 43.6% year on year, and turned to a small operating profit, which the company attributes to lower standalone operating expenses and AI-based production processes.

However, operating losses were recorded in each year from 2022 to 2025, and attributable net losses have continued for three consecutive quarters since Q1 2026. Total equity fell from KRW 43.3 billion at the end of 2025 to KRW 38.1 billion at the end of June 2026, and the 2025 debt ratio was 156.1%.
