Casino Volume Grows, but Costs Outpace Profit

02

Business structure

GKL (Grand Korea Leisure) operates three foreigner-only casinos under the Seven Luck brand: Seven Luck Gangnam COEX, Seven Luck Dragon City, and Seven Luck Busan Lotte. All three operate in hotel space under lease arrangements, and industry sources put annual rent at roughly KRW 30 billion to KRW 40 billion.

Revenue depends on casino operations, and an industry official noted that, unlike private rivals that run integrated resorts, GKL has limited non-casino revenue channels. Its customers include VIP players and mass-market guests from Japan, China, Taiwan, Mongolia and Thailand.

The company sends staff to Japan, Taiwan, Mongolia and Thailand to meet VIP customers directly, while it keeps non-face-to-face marketing for Greater China customers. Nine-month 2026 admissions fell 1.9% to 771,576, with Japanese visitors up 4.2% to 289,400 and Chinese visitors down 8.7% to 307,539.

Over the same period, casino drop rose 9.5% to KRW 2.97 trillion and casino net revenue rose 5.8% to KRW 337.2 billion. These figures are preliminary. Its peers in foreigner-only casinos are Paradise and Lotte Tour Development, and Paradise runs four properties in Seoul, Incheon, Busan and Jeju. English-language coverage describes GKL as a state-controlled company.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 262.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 396.7 billion in 2023 (up 51.3% year on year), dipped to KRW 396.4 billion in 2024 (down 0.1%), and reached KRW 422.9 billion in 2025 (up 6.7%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 13.9 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 51 billion in 2023, a swing to profit. It then fell to KRW 38.3 billion in 2024 (down 24.9%) and recovered to KRW 52.6 billion in 2025 (up 37.4%).

Operating margin was -5.3% in 2022, 12.9% in 2023, 9.7% in 2024 and 12.4% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a loss of KRW 22.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 43.8 billion in 2023, a swing to profit, then fell to KRW 33.1 billion in 2024 (down 24.6%) and rose to KRW 47.1 billion in 2025 (up 42.4%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests is reported at the same amounts in each year. For the 2025 recovery, press coverage cited the easing of visa-free entry for Chinese group tourists. On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 109.4 billion and operating profit KRW 17.3 billion.

Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 102.8 billion, down 6.1% from the prior quarter, and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 801 million, a swing from profit. Q1 2026 operating profit was KRW 18.1 billion and Q2 2026 operating profit was KRW 20.8 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 109.7 billion and operating profit KRW 9.62 billion, down 53.8% from the prior quarter. In the preliminary Q3 filing, customer transport costs were KRW 13.8 billion, lodging costs KRW 22.4 billion and promotion costs KRW 2.9 billion, rising 10%, 9% and 4% respectively.

Cost of sales and selling and administrative expenses are reported as the main drags on operating profit. Nine-month 2026 revenue was KRW 340.8 billion, up 6.4%, while operating profit was KRW 48.6 billion (down 9.1%) and net profit was KRW 40.1 billion (down 16.1%), all on a preliminary basis.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's foreigner-only casino operators are benefiting from record inbound tourism. According to the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, foreign visitors passed the previous annual record of 18.94 million as of October 27, 2026.

Greater China arrivals totaled 7.92 million in the first nine months, and coverage reported growth of 29.1%. Peer results have been rising. Paradise posted Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 351.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 64.1 billion, up 22% and 62.3% year on year.

In October 2026, Paradise's casino revenue rose 12.8% to KRW 82.1 billion, while Lotte Tour Development's rose 24.1% to KRW 62.5 billion, a record monthly level. GKL's casino revenue for the same month was KRW 29.3 billion, up only 2.5%.

In a July 2026 report cited by the press, Hana Secururities analyst Lee Ki-hoon expected casino growth to continue in the second half, citing rising Japanese arrivals and a weaker won. The industry treats the autumn, with Chinese National Day and Japanese holidays, as the peak season.

In the second quarter of 2026, Chinese Labour Day and Japanese Golden Week overlapped and boosted foreign arrivals. In Q1 2026, GKL's drop rose 12.6% to KRW 931 billion, but casino revenue fell 1.4% to KRW 106.7 billion and the hold rate fell 1.6 percentage points to 11.5%. How much drop converts into revenue is a key variable in the competitive picture.

06

Outlook

The company is sending staff to Japan, Taiwan, Mongolia, Thailand and other key Asian markets to win overseas customers, and says it plans VIP baccarat tournaments and dinner shows tailored to customers by nationality.

Preliminary figures showed Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 20.8 billion, up 30.5% year on year, with drop up 13.9% to KRW 1.984 trillion. At a Q3 2026 foreigner VIP event, drop reached KRW 2.1629 trillion, 22.9% above target, but participating customers reached only 87.7% of target.

Coverage noted that spending cuts did not translate into higher profit. Q3 W/L fell 29.3% from the prior quarter. Samsung Securities analyst Lee Hyein expects rising labor and customer-incentive costs to continue into Q4 2026.

The near-term test is whether higher drop converts into revenue while the hold rate holds up, and whether cost growth slows below revenue growth. Those two conditions will shape any recovery in operating margin.

Over the longer term, the hotel-lease structure and limited non-casino revenue channels will shape how much cost efficiency is possible.

08

Bull factors

Inbound demand and drop are rising

According to the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, foreign visitors reached 18.94 million as of October 27, 2026, a record. Growth in Greater China arrivals is cited as a background for a wider casino customer base.

GKL's nine-month casino drop rose 9.5% on a preliminary basis, a point cited as a positive for the revenue base.

Financial capacity is improving

The debt ratio fell from 59.4% in 2022 to 47.5% in 2025, and total equity at end-2025 was KRW 441.5 billion. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 45.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 104.7 billion in 2025. Lower leverage and stronger operating cash generation give the company more room to absorb temporary cost increases.

Operating margin has recovered before

Operating margin moved from -5.3% in 2022 to 12.9% in 2023 and was 12.4% in 2025. Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 20.8 billion rose 30.5% year on year on a preliminary basis. Drop growth of 13.9% alongside rising Japanese admissions shows room for margin improvement if cost efficiency improves.

09

Bear factors

Costs are rising faster than revenue

Q3 2026 revenue rose 0.3%, but operating profit fell 44.4% and operating margin fell to 8.8%, based on preliminary press figures. First-half cost of sales rose 10% and labor costs rose 12.8%. Customer-benefit spending rose 9%, so higher drop has not yet translated into profit.

Lagging visitor growth

Nine-month admissions fell 1.9%, and Chinese visitors fell 8.7%. Even with record foreign arrivals in Korea, GKL's admissions did not grow. Its October 2026 casino revenue rose only 2.5%, compared with 12.8% for Paradise and 24.1% for Lotte Tour Development.

Limits of a casino-centric model

All three casinos operate in leased hotel space, with rent reportedly KRW 30 billion to KRW 40 billion a year. Industry sources note limited non-casino revenue and a location farther from the airport. Compared with rivals running integrated resorts, the model is viewed as weaker in attracting VIP players.

10

Risk factors

Delayed recovery in Chinese visitors

Chinese admissions fell 8.7% in the first nine months of 2026, even as overall Greater China arrivals grew. If Chinese customers do not recover, drop growth could concentrate in Japanese customers. Heavier dependence on a single nationality is a stability risk.

Customer-acquisition and labor costs

Nine-month customer-benefit spending rose 9% to KRW 54.1 billion, and first-half labor costs rose 12.8% to KRW 80.8 billion. Samsung Securities expects labor and incentive cost increases to continue into Q4 2026. If these costs become fixed, margin recovery could stay limited even as drop grows.

Possible audit adjustments to preliminary results

The company said its Q3 2026 filing was prepared before the external audit was complete, and some content may change during the audit. All nine-month 2026 figures are preliminary. Until audited figures are released, these comparisons remain on a preliminary basis.

12

Overall view

GKL is benefiting from the industry tailwind of rising foreign arrivals and higher casino drop, but Q3 2026 operating profit fell 44.4% year on year to KRW 9.62 billion. Nine-month drop rose 9.5% on a preliminary basis, while admissions fell 1.9%, with a sharp decline in Chinese visitors.

On costs, first-half labor costs rose 12.8% and customer-benefit spending rose 9%, so revenue growth has not yet reached profit. Financially, the debt ratio fell to 47.5% at end-2025 and operating cash flow reached KRW 104.7 billion in 2025, leaving room to absorb costs.

Operating profit recovered to KRW 52.6 billion in 2025, so margin recovery remains possible if cost efficiency improves. However, the casino-centric model, hotel lease costs and slower growth than peers remain structural constraints.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 cost trend, preliminary full-year 2026 results in early February 2027, and audited figures in the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027.
