02

Business structure

F&F Holdings (007700) is a holding company whose operations run through its apparel subsidiary F&F (KOSPI 383220). The subsidiary's main brands are MLB and Discovery, and it also runs Duvetica, an Italian padding brand, and Sergio Tacchini, a sportswear brand.

On a preliminary basis for Q3 2026, the subsidiary's domestic sales were KRW 141.3 billion, down 2.9% year on year, with MLB sales of KRW 87.7 billion, up 4.4%.

Discovery domestic sales were KRW 43.5 billion, down 13.3%, and the decline was linked to a reduction in store count from 277 at the end of Q3 2025 to 260 at the end of Q3 2026. Overseas sales were KRW 310.8 billion, down 5.4%, and China accounted for KRW 272.4 billion, or 87.5% of overseas sales.

Outside China, Hong Kong sales rose 4.5% to KRW 17.7 billion and Southeast Asia sales rose 21.8% to KRW 14.3 billion. Because overseas sales are concentrated in China, shipment adjustments there pass straight through to group results.

Under the holding structure, a gap between total net income and net income attributable to controlling shareholders arises from minority interests; in 2025 the figures were KRW 367.9 billion and KRW 125.7 billion respectively.

Hanwha Investment & Securities reported on November 11, 2026 that F&F was reportedly in due diligence to consider acquiring TaylorMade.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales moved from KRW 1.82 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.99 trillion in 2023, up 9.3%, the largest year in the 2022-2025 period, then fell 4.4% to KRW 1.9 trillion in 2024 and recovered 1.9% to KRW 1.93 trillion in 2025. 2022 was the smallest year in the period.

Operating profit was KRW 486.2 billion in 2022, KRW 514.4 billion in 2023 (up 5.8%), KRW 416.4 billion in 2024 (down 19.1%) and KRW 434.2 billion in 2025 (up 4.3%). The operating margin fell from 26.8% in 2022 to 25.9% in 2023 and 21.9% in 2024, then edged up to 22.5% in 2025.

In 2024 the drop in operating profit was far steeper than the 4.4% sales decline, which points to cost pressure weighing on margins. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 132.2 billion in 2022, KRW 122.3 billion in 2023, KRW 107 billion in 2024 and KRW 125.7 billion in 2025 (up 17.5%).

Total net income over the same years was KRW 417.6 billion, KRW 393.6 billion, KRW 322 billion and KRW 367.9 billion, and the gap reflects minority interests.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 3.64 trillion, of which KRW 2.31 trillion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 700.8 billion; the debt ratio fell from 24.4% in 2022 to 19.2% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 332.6 billion in 2022, KRW 467.9 billion in 2023, KRW 402.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 361.6 billion in 2025. Quarterly, operating profit was KRW 119.8 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 124.3 billion in Q4 2025 (up 3.8% quarter on quarter), then KRW 145.7 billion in Q1 2026 (up 17.2%).

Q2 2026 operating profit dropped to KRW 77.2 billion, down 47.0% from the prior quarter, on sales of KRW 399.8 billion, down 28.7%.

Q3 2026 rebounded to KRW 100.7 billion (up 30.5% quarter on quarter), but remained 15.9% below the year-earlier quarter, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 44.5% year on year to KRW 20.4 billion. Press reports attributed part of the Q3 weakness to foreign-currency-related losses.

05

Industry analysis

F&F Holdings' results depend heavily on the operating subsidiary's China MLB shipments and on how Discovery stores run in Korea. In Q3 2026 the company said it adjusted local supply in China to improve inventory health, and that MLB consumer sales in China stayed roughly flat year on year.

That suggests end-demand held while wholesale shipments were cut to reduce channel inventory. In Korea, MLB grew across flagship stores, direct stores and dealers, while Discovery faced store consolidation and weak sales in some categories.

Cost pressure also showed up: cost of sales as a share of revenue rose from 34.8% to 36.2% in Q3 2026, a 1.4 percentage point increase, as lower sales spread fixed costs over a smaller base.

Hong Kong and Southeast Asia showed growth, with per-store sales improving and flagship openings continuing, which partly offsets reliance on mainland China.

Shinhan Investment Corp. said on November 11, 2026 that the China inventory adjustment looks close to complete but that a real sales recovery still needs to be confirmed. If the TaylorMade acquisition proceeds, golf would become a new segment, and that market's demand cycle would need separate assessment.

06

Outlook

The company expects Q4 2026 results to improve as China supply normalizes and winter outerwear sales pick up in the peak season. It described the China shipment adjustment as nearing completion in Q3.

The Q4 comparison base is clear: Q4 2025 sales were KRW 575.2 billion and operating profit was KRW 124.3 billion, so the strength of any recovery will be judged against those figures. Broker outlooks differ.

Hanwha Investment & Securities, in a November 11, 2026 report, projected 2026 consolidated sales of KRW 1.98 trillion and operating profit of KRW 471 billion for the operating subsidiary, implying sales up about 3% and operating profit roughly flat year on year.

Shinhan Investment Corp. said on November 11, 2026 that it had cut its earnings estimates for the subsidiary by more than 20%. Daishin Securities, in a November 13, 2026 report, pointed to slowing growth but kept the TaylorMade issue as a near-term driver.

On the acquisition, Hanwha Investment & Securities expected a conclusion by year-end 2026 or early 2027 at the latest. Once a decision is made, the holding company's financial structure and earnings mix could change, so the terms will need to be checked.

The 2026 annual report is due by the end of March 2027, and its full-year figures will become the reference point against these forecasts.

08

Bull factors

China shipment adjustment nearing completion

The company said the China shipment adjustment entered its final phase in Q3 and that local supply should normalize in Q4. Shinhan Investment Corp. also described the China inventory adjustment as nearing completion in its November 2026 report.

The company also said MLB consumer sales in China held roughly flat, which points to underlying demand holding up.

Quick quarterly profit rebound history

After operating profit fell to KRW 77.2 billion in Q2 2026, Q3 rebounded to KRW 100.7 billion, up 30.5% from the prior quarter. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 65.2 billion in Q1 2026, up 42.5% quarter on quarter.

This pattern of fast quarterly recovery is a reference point for judging how much profit could return once China shipments normalize.

Growth in newer overseas markets and balance sheet capacity

Hong Kong sales rose 4.5% to KRW 17.7 billion and Southeast Asia sales rose 21.8% to KRW 14.3 billion in Q3 2026, showing growth outside China. The debt ratio was 19.2% at the end of 2025, down from 24.4% in 2022, and operating cash flow was KRW 361.6 billion in 2025. This financial capacity widens the room to pursue external growth options such as acquisitions.

09

Bear factors

Large swings in quarterly results

Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 77.2 billion fell 47.0% from the prior quarter, and Q3 operating profit was 15.9% below the year-earlier quarter. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 44.5% year on year to KRW 20.4 billion in Q3. Such large quarterly swings make the full-year trend harder to read.

China concentration and rising cost ratio

China accounted for KRW 272.4 billion of KRW 310.8 billion in overseas sales, or 87.5%, so shipment changes there hit results directly. Cost of sales as a share of revenue rose from 34.8% to 36.2% in Q3. Foreign-currency-related losses were also reported as a factor weighing on third-quarter net income.

Discovery weakness in Korea and overseas base effects

Discovery domestic sales fell 13.3% to KRW 43.5 billion, and its store count dropped by 17. Europe and US sales fell 60.2% to KRW 6.4 billion, reflecting the base effect of a one-off Sergio Tacchini sale in the prior year. If one of the main domestic brands continues to shrink, the room for a broader sales recovery could be limited.

10

Risk factors

China market and currency

China accounts for most overseas sales, so local inventory adjustments, consumer sentiment and yuan exchange-rate moves all affect results at once. Foreign-currency-related losses were reported as a factor in Q3 net income. If China shipment adjustments extend into Q4, the year-end peak season could be weaker than expected.

Execution of the TaylorMade acquisition

The TaylorMade review is aimed at securing external growth, but the financial burden depends on deal terms and financing. Hanwha Investment & Securities expected a decision by year-end 2026 or early 2027. Alongside the change in portfolio, integration uncertainty in reported earnings also needs to be assessed.

Holding structure and minority interests

In 2025 total net income was KRW 367.9 billion, but net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 125.7 billion, a large gap. If the subsidiary ownership structure or profit allocation changes, the share of earnings reaching holding company shareholders would change as well.

Results should be read separately for controlling shareholders, against KRW 2.31 trillion of equity, and for the subsidiary as a whole.

12

Overall view

F&F Holdings' disclosed results rose to a peak in 2023, adjusted in 2024, and recovered modestly in 2025 on both sales and operating profit. In 2026, quarterly figures have been volatile, with a strong Q1, a sharp Q2 drop and a partial Q3 rebound.

The direct drivers of the Q3 decline were China MLB shipment adjustments to normalize inventory and Discovery store consolidation in Korea. Positives include the company's expectation of China supply normalizing in Q4, growth in Hong Kong and Southeast Asia, and a lower debt ratio.

Negatives include reliance on China for most overseas sales, a higher cost of sales ratio, the continued decline of Discovery, and a holding structure in which earnings attributable to controlling shareholders are far below total net income.

The TaylorMade review should be read as both an external growth option and a financial burden. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report, which will show in numbers whether China shipments normalized and whether Discovery recovered.
