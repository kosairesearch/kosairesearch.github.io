ODTech: Revenue Grows, Operating Losses Persist

ODTech's third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 38.4% year over year to KRW 12.5 billion, but it posted an operating loss of KRW 1.57 billion, extending losses for a third consecutive quarter.

  3. 3

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

ODTech was founded in 1999 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2007 as a non-memory semiconductor specialist.

According to a Korean securities information site's company overview, it runs a vertically integrated structure that produces sensor modules and LED application products on the basis of its semiconductor manufacturing process. The business has two pillars, semiconductor chips and sensors.

Chips centre on Zener diode chips and sub-mount chips, while sensors centre on optical sensors for automotive electronics, sensor modules and defense sensors. Revenue mainly comes from semiconductor product sales, followed by sensor products.

The company is widening its high value-added lines into automotive and defense sensors, sensor packages and bio healthcare sensors. Overseas, it operates ODT VINA, a sales entity in Vietnam, and holds a semiconductor-related subsidiary in Nanjing, China.

Compared with large component makers in the same electronics and components sector, such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek, it is a small specialist with a much smaller scale. The largest shareholder group held 25.63% as of October 8, 2026.

Because the business combines semiconductor chip sales with high value-added sensor sales, its earnings structure depends on the mix between the two.

04

Earnings analysis

On the basis of DART-filed results, annual revenue fell 13.4% from KRW 40.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 35 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 37 billion in 2024 (up 5.8% year over year) and KRW 38 billion in 2025 (up 2.6%), extending growth for two consecutive years.

Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the year with the largest revenue and 2023 the year with the smallest.

Operating losses widened from KRW 3.37 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.25 billion in 2023 and KRW 6.36 billion in 2024, then narrowed to KRW 2.28 billion in 2025, and the company has been in operating loss for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025.

The operating margin moved from -8.3% in 2022 and -12.2% in 2023 to -17.2% in 2024 before improving to -6.0% in 2025. The company said the 2025 improvement reflected higher revenue and cost efficiency measures. Controlling shareholder net income in 2025 was KRW 891 million, down 83.0% from KRW 5.24 billion in 2024.

The fact that net income fell sharply even as the operating loss narrowed suggests that non-operating items and tax expenses played a larger role, and the line-item detail sits in the periodic report notes.

Operating cash flow turned negative at -KRW 440 million in 2025, after positive readings of KRW 3.49 billion, KRW 3.82 billion and KRW 2.56 billion in 2022 to 2024.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 9.07 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 9.89 billion, KRW 11.1 billion, and KRW 12.6 billion in the second quarter of 2026, then eased slightly to KRW 12.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

After a fourth-quarter 2025 operating profit of KRW 105 million, operating losses were KRW 2.34 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 1.49 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.57 billion in the third quarter, making three consecutive loss quarters.

A securities information site summarized that first-half 2026 revenue rose 24.4% while the operating loss widened 132.4%, with sensor costs outpacing sales.

Controlling shareholder net income moved from KRW 515 million in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 783 million in the fourth quarter to a net loss of KRW 1.04 billion in the first quarter of 2026, net income of KRW 1.68 billion in the second quarter and a net loss of KRW 4.43 billion in the third quarter, and the sum of controlling shareholder net losses over the last four quarters is KRW 3.01 billion.

05

Industry analysis

ODTech's end markets span automotive electronics, defense, non-memory semiconductor chips and LED application products.

A securities information site's earnings commentary said demand for non-memory chips keeps rising as their range of applications widens, and that demand from robotics and physical AI supports continued growth.

On the automotive side, rising vehicle electrification is driving higher demand for optical sensors, which aligns with the company's push into high value-added sensors.

Defense sensors generally go through lengthy qualification processes, so their contribution tends to show up as mid-term revenue rather than near-term profit.

In competition, Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek are large component makers covering a broad product range, while ODTech is a small company focused on semiconductor chips and optical sensors. That focus means shifts in demand for specific applications can be reflected strongly in its results.

Vertical integration across the semiconductor manufacturing process can be an advantage in technology response and cost control, but as the first half of 2026 shows, rising cost burdens delay margin improvement.

Looking at the cycle, demand from growth applications such as automotive and robotics underpins the company's revenue expansion. However, the recent quarterly results show that a favorable industry backdrop has not translated directly into better operating results.

06

Outlook

At the regular shareholders meeting on March 27, 2026, the company approved amendments to its articles that add gas detector and gas instrument manufacturing and wholesale and retail, instrument calibration and testing services, and automotive new electrical device manufacturing to its business purposes.

Beyond its existing semiconductor and sensor operations, this reads as a move to widen the business into gas measurement and automotive electronics.

A securities information site summarized that the company is pursuing optical sensors for automotive electronics, defense sensors, sensor packages, bio healthcare sensors, entry into the Vietnamese market, and process automation for gas detectors and dispensers.

In its first-half 2026 earnings commentary, robotics and physical AI demand was cited as a growth driver. However, in the quarterly results on file, revenue growth has not yet translated into operating profit improvement, so how quickly the new businesses become profitable is the key variable.

The next checkpoint is whether operating results in the fourth quarter of 2026 end the three-quarter run of operating losses. Full-year 2026 revenue and operating loss will be fixed in the 2026 annual report. The same report will show whether operating cash flow recovers from the 2025 negative reading.

The revenue contribution of the new businesses can be checked through segment disclosures in the periodic reports.

08

Bull factors

Revenue growth

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 12.5 billion, up 38.4% year over year, and second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 12.6 billion was the highest of the last five quarters. The growing scale of high value-added sensor sales is the core bullish argument.

A larger revenue base creates room to spread fixed costs, so any operating improvement could have a larger effect.

Vertically integrated manufacturing

Its vertically integrated structure, which produces sensor modules and LED application products on top of its own semiconductor manufacturing process, is cited as a strength.

Expanding the share of high value-added products such as optical sensors for automotive electronics and defense sensors could lead to a better product mix. A securities information site's view that the application range of non-memory chips is widening also supports the business base.

Financial capacity

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 5.2 billion against total equity of KRW 128.9 billion, a debt ratio of 4.0%. The 2025 operating loss of KRW 2.28 billion narrowed from KRW 6.36 billion in 2024, and the company said the profit structure improved on higher revenue and cost efficiency.

The cash dividend resolved at the March 2026 regular shareholders meeting is also a fact on the shareholder return side.

Bear factors

Four consecutive years of operating losses

The company has posted operating losses for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, and the KRW 6.36 billion loss in 2024 was the largest over that span. The operating margin of -17.2% in 2024 was also the lowest in the period.

Operating losses have continued for three consecutive quarters from the first quarter to the third quarter of 2026, so the loss pattern has not yet ended.

Operating losses widened by cost burden

The third-quarter 2026 operating loss of KRW 1.57 billion widened versus the year-earlier period. A structure in which revenue growth does not outpace cost growth is the main bearish factor.

Controlling shareholder losses and cash flow

The third-quarter 2026 controlling shareholder net loss of KRW 4.43 billion was larger than the operating loss of KRW 1.57 billion in the same quarter. Operating cash flow turned from positive readings in 2022 to 2024 to -KRW 440 million in 2025. Weakness in both earnings and cash flow is the bearish case.

Risk factors

Delayed earnings recovery

Operating losses have continued through the third quarter of 2026, making the timing of a break-even point hard to gauge. Controlling shareholder net results swing sharply by quarter, with a profit in the second quarter and a loss in the third quarter of 2026 within the same year. If the cost structure does not improve, revenue growth may not translate into earnings recovery.

Application concentration and industry cycle

Revenue can swing widely if demand in automotive electronics, defense and semiconductor chips moves with industry conditions and customer investment schedules. As a small company, a high share of a few applications means a change at one customer or product line is reflected strongly in results.

The timing of profitability for new businesses such as bio healthcare and gas measurement is not fixed by any publicly available schedule.

Overseas subsidiaries and small-cap traits

Overseas operations through the Vietnam sales entity and the Nanjing subsidiary are exposed to exchange rates and local regulation. As a small-cap stock, trading can be volatile.

The largest shareholder group held 25.63% as of October 8, 2026, so the influence of the ownership structure on decisions is also a relevant factor.
