02

Business structure

Mdevice is a storage solutions company whose core product is the solid-state drive (SSD). It handles the full process in-house, from designing products that combine memory chips with SSD controllers to manufacturing, assembly and inspection, and sales and distribution.

Pre-processed wafers are reported to be supplied by Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. The product lineup covers enterprise SSDs, consumer SSDs and ultra-small BGA SSDs.

The company has recently expanded its former SSD division into a semiconductor storage business, widening its range to DRAM modules, eMMC, SD cards and USB drives, and is also pursuing data center server manufacturing. Its customers and revenue are concentrated in China.

Growth accelerated after it signed an SSD supply contract with a Chinese server maker in 2023, and it was selected as an SSD supplier for the national 'East Data, West Computing' project covering eight Chinese data centers.

Since 2025 it has entered the server market to diversify its customer base, and it set up a subsidiary the same year. The company is reported to be testing enterprise SSDs for the European market.

According to a securities report, its competitiveness rests on PCB substrate and firmware design capabilities applied to server SSD production.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed-basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 9.85 billion in 2023 to KRW 48.3 billion in 2024 (up 390.4% year on year) and KRW 96.5 billion in 2025 (up 99.8% year on year). Revenue grew two years in a row from 2024 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2023 to 2025.

Operating profit swung from an operating loss of KRW 4.74 billion in 2023 to operating profit of KRW 4.98 billion in 2024, a turnaround to profit, and then rose 132.0% to KRW 11.5 billion in 2025. Operating margin was -48.1% in 2023, 10.3% in 2024 and 12.0% in 2025.

Industry media reported that the company attributed the 2024 recovery partly to China-bound revenue generated in the fourth quarter of 2023 being recognized in the 2024 accounts.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from a net loss of KRW 7.34 billion in 2023 to KRW 3.19 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.7 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 18.1 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 33 billion in the fourth quarter (up 82.3% from the prior quarter), then moved to KRW 20.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 38.0%), KRW 28.1 billion in the second quarter (up 37.4%) and KRW 23.1 billion in the third quarter.

Profit moved more sharply. In the first quarter of 2026, operating profit of KRW 3.15 billion coexisted with a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.14 billion.

In the second quarter, net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 13.6 billion far exceeded operating profit of KRW 4.83 billion, suggesting that non-operating items had a large effect.

In the third quarter, operating profit of KRW 977 million fell 55.5% from a year earlier, and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 6.82 billion.

The sum of net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 to third quarter of 2026) is KRW 5.18 billion. Operating cash flow was -KRW 1.95 billion in 2023, -KRW 3.11 billion in 2024 and -KRW 17.1 billion in 2025. A securities report pointed to higher memory prices as a cost burden.

05

Industry analysis

Rising investment in AI data centers is lifting demand for high-capacity storage. An independent research firm argued that AI server expansion increases demand for SSDs for high-capacity data storage alongside GPUs and HBM, and said Mdevice could be seen as a beneficiary given its high share of enterprise SSDs.

NAND prices are another key variable. Reported data show that the price of 1Tb TLC wafers rose about 4.5 times, from USD 5.5 in September 2025 to USD 25 in March 2026, while OEM SSD prices rose from USD 58 to USD 190 over the same period.

Because raw material and finished product prices are rising together, revenue grows, but how cost burdens and inventory valuation flow into margins is the key issue. The lower operating margin in the third quarter of 2026 shows this margin volatility appearing in actual quarterly results.

Media reports also say the company is expanding into industrial, automotive and medical equipment markets, where miniaturization and stability matter.

Its position can be summarized by its status as a supplier to a national Chinese project, its server-linked solutions and its efforts to diversify customers, including European testing. However, China still accounts for most revenue, so quarterly results may move with the timing of orders from Chinese customers.

06

Outlook

The 2025 annual revenue guidance of KRW 90 billion that the company had indicated was exceeded by the disclosed revenue of KRW 96.5 billion. A securities report from December 2025 said existing inventory made that guidance achievable.

For 2026, an independent research firm indicated revenue could expand to KRW 190 billion to KRW 230 billion and operating profit to KRW 24.7 billion to KRW 32.2 billion.

However, adding up disclosed quarterly revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 gives KRW 71.7 billion, so reaching the low end of that range would require fourth-quarter revenue of at least KRW 118.3 billion.

Quarterly operating profit over the same period was KRW 3.15 billion, KRW 4.83 billion and KRW 977 million. In its corporate value enhancement plan made public in July 2026, the company set out direction centered on strengthening its SSD and server products and on medium- to long-term growth targets.

Reports said the plan placed more weight on SSD and server investment than on near-term dividend increases, and that no specific plan to improve dividend procedures has been set yet.

The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, where regional revenue mix and operating margin will show how actual results compare with these forecasts.

Bull factors

Sales growth and operating profitability

Operating margin exceeded 10% in both 2024 at 10.3% and 2025 at 12.0%. The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 102.0% in 2025 from 556.0% in 2023.

AI data center demand and NAND prices

An independent research firm said that as AI infrastructure expands and enterprise SSD demand grows, Mdevice could benefit. Reported price data show 1Tb TLC wafer prices rising from USD 5.5 in September 2025 to USD 25 in March 2026, which was cited as a factor behind the earnings improvement.

Business expansion and customer diversification

The company has expanded its former SSD division into a semiconductor storage business, widening its product range to DRAM modules, eMMC, SD cards and USB drives, and is pursuing server manufacturing.

Its selection as an SSD supplier for China's 'East Data, West Computing' project and its European enterprise SSD testing are cited as evidence of customer diversification.

Bear factors

Heavy concentration in China

The company has said that China's share is dominant and that it plans to diversify into markets such as Europe. Because of this structure, changes in Chinese project timing or policy could directly affect results.

Volatility in quarterly results and net income

Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 977 million, down 79.8% from the prior quarter, and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 6.82 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026, operating profit of KRW 3.15 billion coexisted with a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.14 billion. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 5.18 billion.

Cash flow and shareholder returns

Operating cash flow widened to -KRW 17.1 billion in 2025. The company has no dividend record over the past three years, and its July 2026 value-up plan placed more emphasis on SSD and server investment than on near-term dividends. Rising memory prices have been flagged as a cost burden.

Risk factors

Customer and regional concentration

Most revenue comes from China, and quarterly results can vary widely depending on large project awards there. Whether testing in new markets such as Europe converts into revenue is an area that has not yet been confirmed in disclosures.

Raw material and inventory price swings

If NAND wafer and SSD prices rise sharply, revenue can grow while cost burdens and inventory valuation pressure margins. A December 2025 securities report said existing inventory would help achieve the 2025 revenue guidance.

Whether costs and selling prices move in the same direction should be checked through quarterly operating margins.

Balance sheet and cash flow

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 48.9 billion, close to total equity of KRW 47.9 billion. The operating cash outflow widened to -KRW 17.1 billion in 2025, so working capital financing needs could persist. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 556.0% in 2023 to 102.0% in 2025, but it remains a point to monitor.
