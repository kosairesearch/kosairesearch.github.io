02

Business structure

Cronex is a natural science and engineering R&D company that listed on the Konex market on December 28, 2015. It develops and produces transgenic minipigs using CRISPR gene editing and applies them to customized research models and services for drug development and xenotransplantation research.

Per a Korea Technology Finance Corporation-linked KRX report, of KRW 4.7 billion in 2023 revenue, CRO services contributed KRW 2.7 billion (57.4%) and biomaterials such as minipigs and collagen KRW 2.0 billion (42.6%).

Its CRO clients are described as including LG Electronics and Classys in dermatology and Osstem Implant in dental work. The Suwon non-clinical center, set up in 2022, provides efficacy testing on medium-sized animals such as pigs, goats and rabbits.

The Jeju Minipig Research Center (JMRC) was described as a facility targeting simultaneous housing of about 800 animals and annual output of more than 1,200. Entry barriers in non-clinical CRO are relatively high, but competition from foreign CROs and price competition among domestic players are unavoidable.

At the March 2026 shareholders' meeting, an amendment to the articles widened the business purpose to areas such as health functional foods, cosmetics, medical devices and healthcare AI.

The business differs sharply in nature and scale from sector heavyweights such as Samsung Biologics and Celltrion, as Cronex is a very small research-services and materials company.

Recent trends

The standalone financial statements for the 14th fiscal term (2025), disclosed for the March 31, 2026 shareholders' meeting, show revenue of KRW 5,534 million, operating profit of KRW 642 million, net loss of KRW 2,569 million and a loss per share of KRW 498.

In the Korea Technology Finance Corporation-linked report (K-GAAP, standalone), 2023 figures were revenue of KRW 4.7 billion, an operating loss of KRW 0.8 billion and a net loss of KRW 1.4 billion. The company therefore reported an operating profit in 2025, but bottom-line earnings remained negative.

We could not confirm the non-operating items behind the gap between operating profit and net loss. The same filing shows total assets of KRW 14,238 million, total liabilities of KRW 12,371 million, total equity of KRW 1,867 million and capital stock of KRW 3,154 million.

Because total equity is below capital stock, the company appears to be in partial capital impairment. The share price was KRW 1,259 as of 4:10 p.m. on March 31, 2026, down 10.01% from the previous day.

The price stood at KRW 597 on October 27, and Hankyung Korea Market lists a 52-week high of KRW 2,300 and a low of KRW 509.

The filing list shows a conversion price adjustment for the fifth convertible bond series on August 31, 2026, and a corrected report on a convertible bond issuance decision was filed on September 11, 2026.

A corrected filing on a decision to acquire shares and equity securities of another company followed on September 30, 2026. The detailed terms could not be confirmed in this research.

04

Outlook

In 2025 press coverage, the company said it would complete the Jeju native pig genetic resource center at the end of 2025 and run it from 2026, develop high-purity collagen medical devices, and aim for KRW 50 billion in revenue within three years.

Against standalone 2025 revenue of KRW 5,534 million, that target presumes a very large step-up. The center's actual operating status and the timing of collagen product approvals and sales are not confirmed in filings or press reports.

The company said it planned to pick a listing underwriter by the end of October 2025 and begin KOSDAQ listing preparation, but we could not confirm what happened afterward.

According to Seoul Economic Daily, average daily trading volume on Konex fell 30.88% in 2026 versus 2024, and no Konex company has applied for a KOSDAQ transfer listing in the past year. The Financial Services Commission's push to split KOSDAQ into two tiers is also cited as a factor that could narrow Konex's standing.

The September 2026 filings on convertible bonds and the acquisition of another company's shares may relate to financing diversification, but no confirmed target or size is available. Room for improvement in operating margin depends on utilization of the center and the share of higher-value materials sales. The debt load and thin equity remain constraints on the pace of new investment.

05

Bull factors

Operating-level profit

Standalone operating profit for 2025 was KRW 642 million. In the Korea Technology Finance Corporation-linked table covering 2019 to 2023, operating results were losses in every year. Moving out of an operating loss suggests a change in the business base. The profit is small, though, so its durability must be checked in later results.

Minipig-based differentiation

The company says it has cases of removing immune-rejection genes in Jeju native pigs and is producing pigs with multiple edited genes. Its aim is a value chain running from the supply of source animals to efficacy-evaluation support.

Linking CRO and materials businesses within one company is a structural point that sets it apart from competitors. How much of this asset converts into revenue is a separate question.

Infrastructure and wider scope

The Jeju center was described as a facility combining laboratory minipig production, transgenic animal development, in-house product validation and outsourced CRO work. The March 2026 amendment to the articles also widened the business purpose to medical devices, cosmetics and health functional foods.

If operations stabilize, there is room for better utilization of fixed assets. This refers only to the potential improvement in business metrics.

06

Bear factors

Net loss and thin equity

Standalone net loss for 2025 was KRW 2,569 million against total equity of KRW 1,867 million. Total liabilities of KRW 12,371 million are several times equity. Equity below capital stock of KRW 3,154 million points to partial capital impairment. With a small financial cushion, reliance on borrowing and convertible bonds could grow.

Gap between targets and results

The company set a medium- to long-term target of KRW 50 billion in revenue within three years. Standalone revenue for 2025 was KRW 5,534 million. Filings do not confirm whether the center's start-up and collagen commercialization proceeded on schedule. Reaching the target requires a leap in scale.

Konex liquidity limits

It is noted that in a low-liquidity market, small trades can swing prices, making it hard for a company's value to be fully reflected. Trading value on October 27 was only KRW 56,197. A low market capitalization formed on Konex is also cited as a reference point for valuation at a later transfer listing. With trading this thin, the reliability of the quoted price may be low.

07

Risk factors

Financial and funding

With a heavy debt load and thin equity, further funding may be needed. The filing list shows a conversion price adjustment for the fifth series on August 31, 2026, indicating that convertible bonds are outstanding.

If bond terms include refixing or early-redemption clauses, dilution and repayment burdens could increase. We could not confirm the detailed terms of the corrected September 2026 convertible bond decision.

Business and regulatory

The CRO industry faces unavoidable competition from foreign CROs and price competition among domestic firms. Medical collagen and xenotransplantation-related businesses may see revenue recognition delayed by approval and quality-certification timelines.

The Jeju center was described as being built to the ISO 22442 standard, with ISO 13485 certification also planned. Delays in certification and approvals could affect the commercialization schedule.

Market and institutional

Reports say brokerages are avoiding underwriting Konex companies and that the KOSDAQ two-tier reform plan is expected to narrow Konex's standing. If the transfer-listing route is blocked, the feasibility of the company's KOSDAQ move could decline.

With a market capitalization of KRW 4.0 billion, prices of such a micro cap can move sharply on small trades. A narrow trading base may also limit the ability to convert holdings into cash.

08

Overall view

Cronex is a micro-cap Konex-listed bio company combining minipig-based CRO services with biomaterials. On a standalone basis for 2025 it recorded an operating profit of KRW 642 million but still ended with a net loss of KRW 2,569 million.

A financial structure with large liabilities relative to assets, and equity below capital stock, can constrain expansion investment. The Jeju center, the collagen business and the wider business purpose are presented as growth paths, but actual operation and revenue contribution are not confirmed in filings.

Weak trading on Konex and a harsher transfer-listing environment are variables that affect the reliability of price formation. The bullish factors are operating improvement and differentiated assets, while the bearish factors are the financial burden, the gap between targets and results, and limited liquidity. This report is for information only, and the judgment is left to the reader.
