Report overview

Profit Turnaround Tested by Core Margins and Debt

Union Materials returned to operating profit in 2025 after two years of losses, but an operating loss in the third quarter of 2026 and rising short-term borrowings make sustained core profitability the next test.

  1. 1

    Operating profit was KRW 5.43 billion in 2025, turning positive from an operating loss of KRW 16.5 billion in 2024, while revenue fell to KRW 101.9 billion, a third straight annual decline.

  2. 2

    In the third quarter of 2026 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.98 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 6.5 billion. The sum of such net losses over the last four quarters is KRW 679 million.

  3. 3

    Standalone short-term borrowings rose from KRW 60.6 billion at the end of 2025 to KRW 70.0 billion at the end of the first half of 2026, according to press reports. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 stood at KRW 116.8 billion against total equity of KRW 19.6 billion.

  4. 4

    A 5-to-1 share consolidation took effect on October 29, 2026, reducing outstanding shares from 42 million to 8.4 million and raising the par value from KRW 500 to KRW 2,500.

  5. 5

    Largest shareholder Union Corporation reported a 41.00% stake as of November 12, 2026, and press reports said it bought 270,000 shares on the open market from August 24 to 26, 2026.

02

Business structure

Union Materials makes ferrite magnets and ceramic components. Ferrite magnets used in automotive and home-appliance motors are the core product, alongside ceramic inserts and magnetron stem (ceramic) parts.

Market shares compiled by the WiseReport corporate database as of June 2026 are 45% for domestic ferrite magnets, 40% for domestic ceramic inserts, 22% for global magnetron stems, 7% for global ferrite magnets and 3% for global ceramic inserts, so the company is strong at home and small abroad.

Export shares in 2025 were 60.42% for the magnet-and-other product group and 92.64% for the cutting-tool-and-other group. Overseas operations include a Vietnam-based ferrite magnet manufacturing subsidiary established in January 2019 and a ceramics wholesale subsidiary.

R&D spending in 2025 was KRW 1.64 billion, or 1.61% of revenue. Competition in ferrite has intensified as Chinese producers expand share on price competitiveness, according to the same database summary.

Ferrite revenue in the first half of 2026 was KRW 31.3 billion, down 6.0% from KRW 33.3 billion a year earlier, per press reports. Demand is tied to the business cycle of finished-goods industries, which also shapes earnings swings.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results were a period in which revenue declined for three consecutive years while profit swung between loss and profit.

Revenue fell from KRW 126.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 116.5 billion in 2023 (down 7.7% year over year), KRW 108.4 billion in 2024 (down 7.0% year over year), and KRW 101.9 billion in 2025 (down 5.9% year over year); among 2022-2025, the largest year was 2022 and the smallest was 2025.

Operating profit turned to a loss, going from KRW 0.85 billion in 2022 (operating margin of 0.7%) to an operating loss of KRW 4.67 billion in 2023, and the loss widened to KRW 16.5 billion in 2024 (operating margin of -15.2%).

In 2025, it returned to profit with operating profit of KRW 5.43 billion (operating margin of 5.3%). Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders showed a net loss of KRW 18.1 billion in 2023 and a net loss of KRW 46.5 billion in 2024, as the loss grew, and in 2025 it turned to a net profit of KRW 2.46 billion.

Net income including non-controlling interests was also a profit of KRW 2.46 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow turned positive, moving from KRW -12.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.2 billion in 2023, and was then KRW 12.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 11.9 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio was 143.6% in 2022, 193.6% in 2023, 796.4% in 2024, and 594.9% in 2025. On a quarterly basis, revenue peaked at KRW 26.9 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and then fell to KRW 25.7 billion in the third quarter, and third-quarter revenue rose 6.0% from the same period a year earlier.

Operating profit declined to KRW 1.05 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 70.7% quarter over quarter) and KRW 0.71 billion in the second quarter (down 32.3% quarter over quarter), then turned to an operating loss of KRW 1.98 billion in the third quarter.

Operating profit of KRW 3.6 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 5.04 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 were the largest profits among the latest five quarters, but because the net profit was larger than the operating profit trend, it reads as a quarter in which non-operating items had a large impact.

The media also reported that the return to net profit in the first half of 2026 was largely driven by an increase in exchange-rate-related other income, such as foreign currency translation gains and foreign exchange gains.

05

Industry analysis

Ferrite magnet demand is tied to motor demand in automobiles and home appliances. A corporate summary describes the ferrite business as sensitive to the business cycle of finished-goods industries and says share gains by Chinese producers on price competitiveness have intensified competition.

Domestic shares of 45% for ferrite magnets and 40% for ceramic inserts contrast with global shares of 7% and 3%, so the domestic base is solid while overseas sales face price and volume pressure.

A summary of the first nine months of 2025 described expansion into renewable energy and eco-friendly industries, new development of automotive electric-drive motors, and expected growth in ferrite magnet demand.

Quarterly revenue eased in the third quarter of 2026 after peaking in the second, so whether demand improvement carries into subsequent quarters remains to be seen in the next reported results.

Press reports tied the first-half 2026 decline in ferrite revenue to both a slowdown in finished-goods demand and price competition. Because the company is concentrated in ferrite and ceramic parts, its profitability depends more directly on the cycle of these specific products than on a diversified portfolio.

06

Outlook

The outlook rests on three areas. The first is new business: a summary of the first nine months of 2025 pointed to new development of automotive electric-drive motors and higher ferrite demand, and whether these reshape the revenue mix remains open. The second is financial capacity.

Press reports said standalone financial-institution borrowing limits were about KRW 86.8 billion at mid-2026, with roughly KRW 12 billion of headroom on a simple calculation, but many loans are already fully drawn and some limits are restricted to facility funding, so cash generation from operations must recover.

The third is the listing threshold. The market-capitalization requirement on the KOSPI is set to rise from KRW 30 billion to KRW 50 billion from 2027, and press reports said the company's market value at the time of reporting fell short of that level.

The share consolidation that took effect on October 29, 2026 reduced the share count but does not by itself change market value. The largest shareholder bought shares on the open market in late August 2026 in what press reports framed as support for the share price, and reported a 41.00% stake after the consolidation.

The next checkpoints are the fourth quarter of 2026 and the 2026 annual report, where the operating margin and the net result attributable to controlling shareholders will show whether the third-quarter loss is temporary or structural.

Bull factors

Return to profit in 2025

Operating profit moved from a KRW 16.5 billion loss in 2024 to KRW 5.43 billion in 2025, a 5.3% operating margin. Operating cash flow stayed positive, at KRW 11.9 billion in 2025. A summary of the first nine months of 2025 cited cost reduction and new businesses as drivers of improved profitability.

Strong domestic market position

The WiseReport database puts domestic shares at 45% for ferrite magnets and 40% for ceramic inserts as of June 2026. Magnetron stem (ceramic) parts hold a reported 22% global share. Products tied to automotive and home-appliance motor demand give the existing customer base a stabilizing role.

New business and shareholder support

A summary of the first nine months of 2025 cited new development of automotive electric-drive motors and rising ferrite demand as drivers. Press reports said the largest shareholder bought 270,000 shares on the open market from August 24 to 26, 2026, raising its stake to 40.64%. A 41.00% stake was reported after the October 2026 consolidation.

09

Bear factors

Quarterly profit declined in 2026

Operating profit fell from KRW 1.05 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 714 million in the second, and the third quarter posted an operating loss of KRW 1.98 billion. The third-quarter net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 6.5 billion is the largest loss among the last five quarters. Revenue rose 6.0% from a year earlier in the third quarter but did not translate into profit.

Borrowing burden and high leverage

At the end of 2025 total liabilities were KRW 116.8 billion against equity of KRW 19.6 billion, a debt-to-equity ratio of 594.9%. Press reports said many credit lines are already fully drawn, which limits additional borrowing.

Ferrite competition and earnings quality

Ferrite revenue in the first half of 2026 was KRW 31.3 billion, down 6.0% from a year earlier, according to press reports. Chinese producers are gaining share on price competitiveness, which makes price defense a challenge.

Press reports attributed the first-half 2026 net profit mainly to foreign-currency translation and exchange gains, so it should be read separately from core operating profit.

10

Risk factors

Balance sheet and repayment pressure

Total liabilities are several times equity, and the debt-to-equity ratio reached 796.4% in 2024. With short-term borrowings rising, weaker operating cash generation would raise refinancing pressure.

Operating cash flow was KRW 11.9 billion in 2025, but with quarterly operating profit falling in 2026, its durability is an open question.

Listing maintenance requirements

Press reports said the KOSPI market-capitalization requirement will rise from KRW 30 billion to KRW 50 billion from 2027. At the time of an August 2026 report, market value fell short of that level.

The share consolidation changes the per-share price but leaves total market value unchanged, so the company must still raise its value.

Currency and export exposure

Export shares in 2025 were high at 60.42% for the magnet-and-other group and 92.64% for the cutting-tool-and-other group. Exchange rates affect both revenue translation and the valuation of foreign-currency assets and liabilities.

Because press reports said the first-half 2026 net profit leaned heavily on exchange-related gains, currency moves can swing quarterly results.

12

Overall view

Union Materials posted declining revenue and widening losses from 2022 to 2024 before turning operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders positive in 2025. Its core products are ferrite magnets and ceramic parts, with a strong domestic share but a small global share.

The company returned to an operating loss in the third quarter of 2026, and first-half ferrite revenue fell. Rising standalone short-term borrowings and a high debt-to-equity ratio remain a burden on the balance sheet.

The market-capitalization requirement and value recovery after the share consolidation are also open issues.

Positive factors include the 2025 return to profit, domestic market share and new-business expectations, while negative factors include quarterly profit declines, higher borrowings and price competition from Chinese producers. The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report and the first-quarter 2027 report.
