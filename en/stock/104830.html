02

Business structure

WONIK Materials was spun off in December 2006 from the specialty gas business of Wonik IPS and makes and sells specialty gases for semiconductors and displays.

The company produces items such as N2O, NH3, F2 mix, C4F8, Xe and CO2 in-house, and resells products such as Si2H6, GeH4 Mix and CH2F2 that it sources externally. According to WiseReport's December 2025 breakdown, product sales made up 63.51% of main product revenue, merchandise 35.59% and other items 0.90%.

A high product share reflects in-house synthesis and purification capability, and a 2019 research note found product gross margins far higher than merchandise margins.

Its gases serve etching, deposition and cleaning steps, and a 2022 securities research report described about 100 varieties made in small-lot, multi-product custom orders.

Semiconductors account for the bulk of demand; that 2022 report estimated Samsung Electronics at about 80% and SK hynix at about 10% of semiconductor specialty gas sales in 2021, with semiconductors at roughly 90% of sales. The merchandise line showed a 91.29% domestic share in December 2025.

The company is a semiconductor-division affiliate of the Wonik Group and holds subsidiaries in the United States and China. Listed semiconductor materials peers include Soulbrain and Dongjin Semichem.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results moved from a 2022 peak through a 2023 trough and a recovery through 2025. Revenue went from KRW 581.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 391.7 billion in 2023 (down 32.6% year on year) and KRW 310.7 billion in 2024 (down 20.7%) before reaching KRW 322.5 billion in 2025 (up 3.8%).

Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for revenue and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 88.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 24.7 billion in 2023 (down 72.2%), the smallest year in the period, then rose to KRW 51.9 billion in 2024 (up 110.3%) and KRW 56.5 billion in 2025 (up 8.8%), growing for two consecutive years.

The operating margin moved from 15.3% in 2022 to 6.3% in 2023, 16.7% in 2024 and 17.5% in 2025. In 2023 the drop in operating profit (72.2%) was far steeper than the drop in revenue (32.6%), so lower volume weighed heavily on profit.

In 2024 operating profit rose 110.3% even as revenue fell 20.7%, which suggests a shift in product mix or cost structure may have played a role. In its third-quarter 2025 release, the company credited recovering demand for key products and cost efficiency.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 57.7 billion in 2022, KRW 13.8 billion in 2023 (down 76.0%), KRW 31.8 billion in 2024 (up 129.7%) and KRW 48.7 billion in 2025 (up 53.1%). Net income for the same years is reported at KRW 57.7 billion, KRW 13.8 billion, KRW 31.8 billion and KRW 48.7 billion.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 9.83 billion in 2022, then KRW 133.9 billion in 2023, KRW 98.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 77.7 billion in 2025, staying positive after 2022.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 82.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 86.6 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 88.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 92.3 billion in the second quarter and KRW 102.9 billion in the third quarter, rising for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025.

Operating profit ran KRW 14.2 billion, KRW 16.3 billion, KRW 15.7 billion, KRW 14.6 billion and KRW 20.3 billion across the same quarters, so revenue growth did not always carry through to profit. Profit attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters totalled KRW 56.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Specialty gases are consumed in front-end chip processes such as etching, deposition and cleaning, so demand tracks wafer input volumes and process transitions.

A March 2022 securities research report put the correlation between WONIK Materials' sales and Samsung Electronics' semiconductor sales over 2011 to 2021 at 0.92. The same report expected specialty gas consumption to rise sharply as EUV lithography is applied in foundry processes around 3-nanometer nodes.

A 2025 research note expected DRAM makers' capacity expansion to widen supply from the second half of that year. In the second quarter of 2025, one brokerage said short-term demand weakened as key customers adjusted utilization and inventory, leaving results below consensus.

In the third quarter of 2025 the company said the semiconductor market was entering a recovery phase and shipments of its main gas products increased. Reported results have since risen across each quarter through the third quarter of 2026.

On cycle position, earnings have recovered since the 2023 trough, but quarterly profit still moves up and down, so it is too early to call the trend established. Listed semiconductor materials peers include Soulbrain and Dongjin Semichem.

The 2022 report cited the ability to produce about 100 varieties in small-lot, multi-product custom orders as a strength. Display demand accounted for roughly 10% of sales in 2021, leaving the semiconductor market as the main variable.

Outlook

In its third-quarter 2025 release, the company said recovery in the second half looked clear and that it expected full-year results to improve year on year. That expectation was borne out, with 2025 operating profit of KRW 56.5 billion, up 8.8% from the prior year.

Revenue continued to rise through 2026, reaching KRW 88.2 billion in the first quarter, KRW 92.3 billion in the second and KRW 102.9 billion in the third.

A 2025 securities research note said the company is strengthening its response to foundry and non-memory processes beyond its earlier memory-centric mix, while also supplying specialty gases for displays. Adjusting the product mix and entering new markets were also cited as medium-term tasks.

The fourth-quarter 2025 operating profit of KRW 16.3 billion is the comparison base for the fourth quarter of 2026. Fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results are expected in early 2027, and the 2026 annual report must be filed by the end of March 2027, when full-year figures are fixed.

The points to watch are the direction of customer capex and whether changes in the product mix keep the operating margin near 17.5%. A rise in selling prices was cited as a driver of 2022 revenue, and price trends remain a variable.

Bull factors

Quarterly earnings recovery

Revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 20.3 billion was up 43.7% year on year. The margin recovery came alongside higher revenue.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 102.9 billion, up 24.2% year on year, shows sales and profit growth arriving together.

Low financial burden

Total equity is more than four times total liabilities. Operating cash flow stayed positive from 2023 through 2025, reaching KRW 77.7 billion in 2025. The low debt burden leaves room to fund facility and R&D spending from internal cash flow.

Customer capacity and process-shift demand

A 2025 research note expected DRAM makers' capacity expansion to lift supply from the second half of that year. A 2022 securities report expected EUV-based foundry processes to raise specialty gas consumption.

Both arguments tie company sales directly to chipmakers' capacity and process decisions, which is the structural case for demand strength.

Bear factors

Dependence on a few customers

A March 2022 securities research report estimated Samsung Electronics at about 80% of semiconductor specialty gas sales in 2021. Changes in one customer's capex or utilization flow quickly into company revenue. Given that concentration, whether supplier diversification progresses is a task to watch alongside results.

Earnings volatility through the cycle

Operating profit fell from KRW 88.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 24.7 billion in 2023, down 72.2%, and profit attributable to owners of the parent dropped 76.0%. The operating margin fell to 6.3% in 2023. Whether profit returns to the 2022 level remains unproven.

Quarterly profit and merchandise mix

Operating profit fell 3.5% quarter on quarter in the first quarter of 2026 and 7.1% in the second, so sales growth and profit growth did not always move together. WiseReport showed merchandise at 35.59% of main product sales in December 2025.

A 2019 research note found merchandise margins below product margins, so the product mix matters for profitability.

10

Risk factors

New market entry execution

The company treats expanding its foundry and non-memory process response, beyond its memory-centric base, as a medium-term task. If this shift is delayed, the product mix may stay weighted toward memory and limit revenue growth.

Product qualification and supply setup tailored to new customer processes can take time, so new-market sales may be recognized later than planned. If the product mix adjustment lags, sustaining the current margin level may prove difficult.

Display demand swings

Display specialty gas supply runs alongside the main semiconductor business and accounted for roughly 10% of sales in 2021. If display customers cut investment or utilization, shipments in this segment could fall together.

Because semiconductors dominate the sales mix, display demand offers limited cushion against a chip-side downturn. If display demand moves in a different direction from the semiconductor market, quarterly results could diverge more widely.

Preliminary figures and regulation

Preliminary figures are issued before external audit and can change when the regulatory report is finalized. Some fluorinated gases may face environmental regulation, which could force product mix adjustments. Changes in supply terms for purchased merchandise gases could also affect costs.

Overall view

WONIK Materials' annual profit recovered from the 2023 trough through 2024 and 2025, and its disclosed quarterly revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 20.3 billion, up 43.7% year on year, stands out among recent quarters. On the financial side, a 21.4% debt ratio and KRW 77.7 billion of operating cash flow in 2025 show a stable structure.

On the other side, the high dependence on a few customers and earnings swings tied to the memory investment cycle remain core risks, as the sharp drops in 2022 to 2023 showed. Some quarters in 2026 saw operating profit fall even as revenue rose, so the durability of margins is an open task.

Valuation is characterized by a price-to-book ratio below the industry average and a dividend yield that runs below the industry average, and readings depend on whether earnings recovery continues.
