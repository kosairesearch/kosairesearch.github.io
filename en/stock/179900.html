02

Business structure

UTI is a precision glass processor that makes camera windows for smartphones and cover glass. Camera windows accounted for KRW 10.7 billion (62%) of cumulative third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 17.2 billion, while foldable UTG generated KRW 1.5 billion (8%), according to a ZDNet Korea report dated November 16, 2026.

Customers are mainly mobile and notebook makers in Korea and overseas, and past press reports cited sales to major domestic strategic customers and to China.

In the last week of October 2026, the CEO told an extraordinary shareholders' meeting that UTG cover-window supply for iPhone Duo panels had begun at Samsung Display. The CEO also acknowledged that UTI started UTG deliveries later than competitors.

The company plans to apply its SLP process know-how from camera windows to glass substrates for semiconductor packaging. Corning is the second-largest shareholder with an 8.42% stake as of August 6, 2026, and a group led by Park Deok-young holds 23.48%.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue fell from KRW 36.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 19.4 billion in 2023 (down 46.9% year on year) and KRW 18.6 billion in 2024 (down 4.1%), then rose to KRW 20 billion in 2025 (up 7.6%).

Over the same period the operating loss widened from KRW 4.64 billion to KRW 19.7 billion, KRW 32.2 billion and KRW 48.3 billion, and the 2025 operating margin was -241.9%.

For the 2025 widening, the company cited higher depreciation from large investments in new businesses and operating costs at its new Vietnam subsidiary, as reported in February 2026.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 12 billion in 2022, KRW 29.5 billion in 2023, KRW 22.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 37.4 billion in 2025, and the 2025 net loss including non-controlling interests was also KRW 37.4 billion.

Operating cash flow swung from KRW 3.15 billion positive in 2022 to negative figures of KRW 8.36 billion, KRW 31.3 billion and KRW 35.2 billion in 2023 through 2025. The debt ratio rose to 227.4% at the end of 2025 from 115.5% in 2024.

Quarterly operating losses were KRW 13.3 billion, KRW 15.7 billion, KRW 13.2 billion, KRW 15.9 billion and KRW 14.5 billion from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

In the second quarter of 2026, net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 13.9 billion despite an operating loss of KRW 15.9 billion, and the third quarter returned to a net loss of KRW 20.7 billion.

Whether the second-quarter gain came from non-operating items should be checked in the footnotes of the filings. The four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026 total a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 23.1 billion. Some press reports say operating losses have continued since 2021 (ZDNet Korea, reported November 16, 2026).

05

Industry analysis

UTG is a cover material for foldable smartphones, so demand moves with foldable device shipments.

Hyundai Motor Securities' November 2025 report estimated about 10 million North American foldable phones for 2026 and UTG demand of 13 million to 15 million sheets, and said UTI could secure at least a 50% share after finishing its Vietnam expansion.

In a March 2026 interview, the CEO said global foldable shipments could grow more than 20% a year. UTG supply has several vendors, and the company itself said it started later than competitors, so share targets need to be checked against actual deliveries.

On the demand side, Apple's foldable iPhone supply chain is cited as a key variable. In terms of cycle position, UTG has moved from development into early mass production. Camera-window pricing has come under pressure from rising demand for mid- and low-end devices, and cost burdens have grown.

Glass substrates for AI chip packaging are seen as a next-generation material, but UTI's substrate business has not yet shown revenue contribution. Press reports also say Corning is considering Korea as a glass substrate production base for AI chips.

06

Outlook

The company's confirmed plans can be grouped into a few points. UTG supply to Samsung Display began in late October 2026, and the company expects up to 550,000 to 600,000 units by the end of 2026.

At a June 2026 shareholder meeting, it said planned supply to a North American customer was cut from 3 million units to 1.2 million, citing factory capacity and environmental regulation.

One of its two Vietnam plants cannot operate because of wastewater treatment rules, and the company said daily output would rise to 60,000 units in 2027 once that issue is resolved.

For expansion, the company said it is in talks with a strategic investor and on mid- to long-term funding for 20 million units of capacity in 2027. It is also reviewing short-term liquidity options, including selling part of the land and a factory in Vietnam and borrowing against headquarters assets.

The company said in March 2026 that new UTG products target an operating margin above 30%, and that its Vietnam unit targets a return to profit in the second half of 2026. Consolidated operating loss in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 14.5 billion, so that target still needs to appear in future filings. Glass substrates and glass platters for data storage are also under review as new applications.

08

Bull factors

UTG mass production begins

UTI said UTG supply to Samsung Display began in the last week of October 2026. The company expects up to 550,000 to 600,000 units by the end of 2026. Confirmed mass production could change a revenue mix that is centered on camera windows.

Revenue up for two straight quarters

Revenue rose in the second and third quarters of 2026, reaching KRW 6.97 billion in the third quarter. Third-quarter revenue was up 51.0% year on year. Cumulative UTG sales rose from KRW 1.1 billion in the first half of 2026 to KRW 1.5 billion through the third quarter.

High-margin products and new applications

In March 2026 the company said new UTG products target an operating margin above 30%. It is also reviewing applications such as glass substrates, glass platters for data storage and glass encoders for robot joints. These are company plans that have not yet appeared in reported results.

09

Bear factors

Operating losses continue

The third-quarter 2026 operating loss was KRW 14.5 billion, and losses continued. The cumulative operating loss through the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 43.6 billion, more than twice the cumulative revenue of KRW 17.2 billion. Operating losses have exceeded revenue every year since 2023.

Funding needs and dilution

The debt ratio was 227.4% at the end of 2025, and operating cash flow was -KRW 35.2 billion. Put options on convertible bonds were exercised, and in October 2026 the company decided a third-party rights issue of KRW 1.3243 billion.

The company says it is negotiating mid- to long-term funding with a strategic investor, but no result has been confirmed.

Volume cuts and production limits

Press reports from June 2026 said planned UTG supply to a North American customer was cut from 3 million units to 1.2 million at a shareholder meeting. One Vietnam plant cannot operate under wastewater rules. The CEO said the overall UTG mass production schedule slipped by about one quarter.

10

Risk factors

Glass substrate commercialization delay

The glass substrate business applies SLP process know-how built in camera windows to semiconductor packaging, but no revenue contribution has been confirmed yet. If commercialization is delayed, expectations for growth beyond UTG could weaken.

Press reports say Corning is considering Korea as a glass substrate production base for AI chips, which could change the competitive landscape. If capital and equipment invested in the new business are not recovered, the timing of profit improvement could slip.

Customer concentration and yield

Press reports say UTG volume is concentrated in one U.S. Big Tech customer, referred to as Company A. If early yield stabilization is delayed, deliveries could fall short of plans. Cumulative UTG sales through the third quarter of 2026 were KRW 1.5 billion, only 8% of total revenue.

Camera window pricing and cost pressure

Camera windows account for 62% of cumulative third-quarter 2026 revenue, making them the core business. Analysts say price competition has intensified as demand for mid- and low-end devices grows, and cost burdens have also increased.

If price cuts and cost increases continue, overall profitability may improve only slowly even as UTG volumes grow. The 30%-plus operating margin target for new UTG products could also be affected by this trend.

Overall view

UTI is moving from a camera-window business toward mass production of UTG for foldable phones. Disclosed 2025 results show revenue of KRW 20 billion and an operating loss of KRW 48.3 billion, and the cumulative operating loss through the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 43.6 billion.

Revenue rose for two consecutive quarters in the second and third quarters of 2026, and supply to Samsung Display has begun. On the other side, operating losses continue, the debt ratio and convertible bond repayments weigh on the balance sheet, and past volume plans have been cut or delayed.
