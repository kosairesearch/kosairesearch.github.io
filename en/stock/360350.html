02

Business structure

Coxem, founded in 2007, makes industrial multi-purpose equipment built on scanning electron microscopes (SEM) and listed on KOSDAQ on February 23, 2024. Its core is the tabletop SEM (Tabletop SEM) used mainly in research settings.

According to a Korea IR Council research report from August 2024, Tabletop SEM sales were KRW 9.4 billion in 2022 and KRW 10.2 billion in 2023. The same report lists general SEM at KRW 1.2 billion and ion miller and other equipment at KRW 1.3 billion for 2023.

Butler company data shows that, on 2Q 2026 sales, scanning electron microscopes accounted for 68%, fluorescence microscopes 13%, services 7%, merchandise 7%, and peripherals 6%.

The IP-SEM, which combines ion milling, targets industrial customers, and Kiwoom Securities said in a June 2026 report that the company has been supplying ion beam modules to AP System.

The atmospheric SEM 'Eirtron' keeps the sample chamber at atmospheric pressure, and in an April 2024 report the company said it was developing the product for commercialization in 2026. In May 2026 the company launched the FE-SEM, a high-resolution field emission model.

Overseas sales depend on a distributor network, and about 30 distributor representatives attended the 2026 global distributor meeting in June. Competition in the research-oriented tabletop market has intensified against large global players.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed basis, annual sales rose from KRW 12.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.7 billion in 2023 (up 9.6% year on year) and KRW 14.7 billion in 2024 (up 7.3%), then fell to KRW 13.4 billion in 2025 (down 9.1%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 1.78 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.5 billion in 2023 (down 15.6%), and the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 236 million in 2024, its first loss year in this period. In 2025 the operating loss widened to KRW 1.53 billion.

The operating margin went from 14.2% in 2022 and 10.9% in 2023 to -1.6% in 2024 and -11.4% in 2025. In a June 2026 report, Kiwoom Securities said weakness in research-market demand has persisted and that R&D costs for new equipment widened the loss.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.72 billion in 2022, KRW 1.68 billion in 2023 (down 1.8%), and KRW 1,000 million in 2024 (down 40.6%), then turned into a net loss of KRW 652 million in 2025.

Total net income, including non-controlling interests, was KRW 664 million in 2024 and a net loss of KRW 871 million in 2025, a different figure from net profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

Operating cash flow was KRW 1.91 billion in 2022, then -KRW 142 million in 2023, -KRW 421 million in 2024, and -KRW 520 million in 2025. On a quarterly basis, 4Q 2025 sales reached KRW 4.81 billion (up 91.7% quarter on quarter) and operating profit was KRW 318 million, a swing to profit.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 844 million in that quarter exceeded operating profit, so non-operating items had a large effect. 1Q 2026 sales fell 47.6% quarter on quarter to KRW 2.52 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 1.08 billion. 2Q 2026 sales were KRW 2.72 billion (up 7.8% quarter on quarter and down 6.8% year on year), and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 741 million, but losses have continued for two quarters since 1Q 2026.

The combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from 3Q 2025 to 2Q 2026, was KRW 925 million.

05

Industry analysis

The electron microscope market is affected by business cycles, and Coxem's core tabletop SEM is used mostly for research. In a June 2026 report, Kiwoom Securities said weak research-market demand has kept results under pressure, and that growth has slowed in Europe and North America as well as in China.

The same report forecasts 2026 sales of KRW 14.5 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.3 billion, which implies continued losses. By contrast, a summary of Kiwoom's investment information puts the global SEM market's expected annual growth at 8%.

The growth focus is therefore shifting from research to industrial applications. The company says rising demand for high-resolution analysis tools, driven by finer semiconductor process nodes and growth in secondary batteries, supports the FE-SEM, which it expects to compete with overseas equipment makers.

At ECTC 2026 in June 2026, a study on through-glass via (TGV) inspection using the atmospheric SEM was presented, and the researchers cited non-destructive inspection and high throughput versus vacuum SEM. Whether research demand has bottomed will likely become clearer in results after 2026. In the tabletop market, competition with large global players is intensifying on both price and product lineup.

06

Outlook

Coxem's new-product schedule falls into three tracks. First, it launched the high-resolution FE-SEM in May 2026 and outlined plans to expand the next-generation FE-SEM overseas at its June 2026 global distributor meeting.

Second, an April 2024 report set a 2026 commercialization target for the atmospheric SEM, and at ECTC 2026 in June 2026 the company presented its TGV inspection performance. Third, its ion gun technology received NET certification, and supply of ion beam modules to AP System has continued.

The company also announced NET certification for its graphene-based atmospheric electron microscope technology. In its 2026 forecast, Kiwoom Securities projected sales of KRW 14.5 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.3 billion, reflecting a conservative view of industrial-segment results in the second half.

On February 27, 2026, the company entered a KRW 2 billion treasury share acquisition trust agreement with Kiwoom Securities, which was terminated on August 27, 2026, and it decided to cancel shares on June 16, 2026. Whether the 2026 atmospheric SEM target turns into actual sales will be clarified by later filings. The key question for future results is whether industrial sales grow enough to offset weaker research demand.

Bull factors

Early signs of an industrial pivot

Supplying ion beam modules to AP System is cited as an early case of moving into industrial sales. Kiwoom Securities expects the company to increase the number of ion beam modules it places in next-generation semiconductor equipment.

The atmospheric SEM's TGV inspection performance, presented at ECTC 2026 in June 2026, connects to demand for semiconductor back-end inspection.

Wider new-product lineup

With the FE-SEM launched in May 2026, the company extended its lineup into the high-resolution segment. It also said its high-performance ion gun and graphene-based atmospheric electron microscope technologies received NET certification. The company expects the new equipment to compete with overseas equipment makers.

Overseas network and growth market

About 30 distributor representatives from Europe, the Americas and Asia attended the June 2026 global distributor meeting. A summary of Kiwoom's investment information puts the global SEM market's expected annual growth at 8%. The company says it aims to expand global share centered on semiconductor and new-materials applications.

Bear factors

Continued losses and falling sales

The company posted operating losses in both 1Q and 2Q 2026, and 2Q 2026 sales fell 6.8% year on year. Kiwoom Securities forecasts continued losses in 2026, with an operating loss of KRW 1.3 billion. Operating losses also occurred in 2024 and 2025, and the timing of a return to profit is still uncertain.

Weak research demand and tougher competition

Because the core tabletop SEM is used mostly for research, it is directly exposed to weakness in research markets. Kiwoom Securities says growth has slowed in Europe and North America as well as in China. Intensifying competition with large global players could delay a sales recovery.

Share-price volatility and disclosure issues

The share was designated an investment-caution stock on March 13, 2026, and investment-caution notices were issued again on September 29, 2026.

In April 2026 the company was flagged for possible designation as an unfaithful-disclosure company over a delayed shareholder meeting resolution, but it was not designated on May 20, 2026. Share-price swings combined with disclosure issues can quickly shift investor sentiment.

Risk factors

Delayed earnings recovery

The combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters was KRW 925 million. Operating losses continued in 1Q and 2Q 2026, and if industrial sales do not grow as expected, the loss period could lengthen.

Operating cash flow has also been negative since 2023, so the burden of new-equipment development spending may persist.

Concentration in product and customers

Most sales are concentrated in the tabletop SEM, so demand shifts in a single product line drive overall results. Overseas sales depend on a distributor network. Industrial transactions confirmed so far are with a limited number of customers, including AP System, so customer diversification remains limited.

Trading and disclosure-management risk

In March 2026 the share was designated an investment-caution stock after a short-term surge, and in September 2026 investment-caution notices cited excessive trading by specific accounts and concentrated trading by a few accounts.

The company also has a history of being flagged for possible designation as an unfaithful-disclosure company over a delayed shareholder meeting resolution. Trading concentration and disclosure issues can raise volatility regardless of the share's direction.

Overall view

Coxem's sales rose from KRW 12.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 14.7 billion in 2024 but fell to KRW 13.4 billion in 2025, and its operating margin moved from -1.6% in 2024 to -11.4% in 2025. Operating losses continued in both 1Q and 2Q 2026, and 2Q 2026 sales declined year on year.

Bullish factors include the early signs of an industrial pivot, such as ion beam module supply to AP System, the May 2026 FE-SEM launch, and the atmospheric SEM presentation at ECTC 2026.

Bearish factors include weaker research demand for tabletop SEMs, slower growth in China, Europe and North America, and intensifying competition with large global players.

Kiwoom Securities forecasts continued losses in 2026 with an operating loss of KRW 1.3 billion, and in its June 2026 report it did not present a separate target price or investment rating.

On the balance sheet, total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 18.4 billion and total liabilities were KRW 4.78 billion, with a debt ratio of 26.1% in 2025. Operating cash flow has been negative since 2023.

The timing of any return to profit depends on whether industrial sales grow enough to offset weaker research demand, and the next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and the 1Q 2027 quarterly report.
