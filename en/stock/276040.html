02

Business structure

Skonec Entertainment is an XR (extended reality) content company founded in 2002 that has built its business around game development and XR-based training solutions. As of the first half of 2023, segment revenue mix was XR training 81.8%, metaverse 15.1% and XR games 3.1%, so training accounted for most revenue.

In games, Mortal Blitz for PlayStation VR reached the top of global downloads in 2017. The MR title TRAVELER, which received development investment from Meta, has its investment amount withheld from disclosure until February 2027.

In training, a February 2026 disclosed contract with a Samsung-group counterparty for XR and Gen AI interview training content was worth KRW 800 million. The company is also seeking to expand XR training content supply to public-sector customers such as the defense ministry and police agency.

Analysts say the business environment deteriorated after the metaverse boom faded. Skonec pursued energy acquisitions, including BSS, but that deal was effectively abandoned, and the return of a KRW 4.0 billion deposit remains uncertain.

A real estate lease business was also pursued through a KRW 8.2 billion property purchase, where the balance payment was reported as postponed. In December 2025, a co-CEO with Japanese game-industry experience joined, and the company shifted to a dual co-CEO structure.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 3.64 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.25 billion in 2023 (up 71.5% year on year), fell to KRW 4.44 billion in 2024 (down 29.0%), and recovered to KRW 6.76 billion in 2025 (up 52.3%), making 2025 the largest year of the 2022–2025 period.

Over the same years, operating losses were KRW 4.63 billion, KRW 6.05 billion, KRW 7.46 billion and KRW 12.2 billion, a streak of four consecutive loss years from 2022 through 2025. One media outlet described a longer run of consecutive losses, but the disclosed figures here cover only 2022 onward.

The operating margin was -127.2% in 2022, -96.9% in 2023, -168.2% in 2024 and -181.0% in 2025, the lowest of the four years. Cost growth, including higher wages and commission fees in the cumulative first nine months of 2025, was reported as the reason losses widened even as revenue grew.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.45 billion, KRW 5.25 billion, KRW 6.65 billion and KRW 21.9 billion for 2022 through 2025, and total net loss including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts in each year.

The company attributed the 2025 first-quarter net loss of about KRW 2.2 billion to one-off items, including M&A advisory fees, restructuring costs and intangible asset amortization.

In the quarterly figures, 2025 Q4 was the revenue peak at KRW 2.89 billion, up 35.4% from the prior quarter, but the operating loss widened to KRW 5.53 billion and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 15.7 billion.

The item-level reasons for the gap between operating and net losses need to be confirmed in the footnotes of the periodic report.

Operating losses narrowed each quarter in 2026, to KRW 1.74 billion in Q1, KRW 868 million in Q2 and KRW 513 million in Q3, while revenue fell for three consecutive quarters to KRW 704 million, KRW 658 million and KRW 177 million.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 19.2 billion. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 13.2 billion and total liabilities were KRW 7.38 billion.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 4.32 billion in 2022, -KRW 2.99 billion in 2023, -KRW 4.94 billion in 2024 and -KRW 11.2 billion in 2025, the largest outflow of the period.

05

Industry analysis

Skonec operates where XR content, metaverse and games overlap, so investment trends in all three flow directly into its results. Analysts attribute the weakness to slower growth in the XR content market after the metaverse boom faded.

A broader contraction in game-industry investment is also cited as a reason for weakness in the XR game business. Industry indicators showing when demand for XR content might recover are not yet clear.

On the platform side, Meta's Quest Store is a key distribution channel, and earlier reports said a VR game, Strike Rush, was being co-developed with Meta. In public-sector training, the VR police training system PoliceOne was reported to have been deployed to police stations nationwide.

The company has said it is shifting toward B2G and B2B business, where revenue follows contract awards rather than game sales. Contract-based revenue has seen repeated extensions of contract end dates, which makes the timing of revenue hard to predict.

Skonec's differentiation lies in XR training and public-sector project experience, but the recent contract sizes are limited relative to quarterly revenue.

06

Outlook

Reporting from February 2026 said the company targets 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 12.5 billion and an operating profit of KRW 1.0 billion or more, based on restructuring effects.

The company described this as a conservative guidance built mainly on confirmed revenue already secured in its existing XR, game and public SI businesses. The company said it adjusted about 37% of its total workforce and secured annual fixed-cost savings of roughly KRW 2.0 billion.

However, quarterly revenue in 2026 Q1 to Q3 was KRW 704 million, KRW 658 million and KRW 177 million, which is far below the annual target. The company said it will use raised funds first to improve its financial structure and liquidity, and will stage strategic investments and small M&A in adjacent XR areas.

Its contract with a Samsung-group counterparty has had its end date amended four times, which raises concerns that revenue timing could slip. Investment amounts for the Meta-linked TRAVELER title are withheld from disclosure until February 2027. The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual revenue and operating profit in the 2026 annual report.

Risk factors

Game development and distribution risk

The MR title TRAVELER is in preparation with Meta development investment, and related investment amounts are withheld from disclosure until February 2027. If launch and monetization timing slips or sales fall short of expectations, the game segment's contribution to revenue could remain limited.

The company also relies on Meta's Quest Store as a key distribution channel, so changes to platform policies or distribution terms could affect its sales structure.

Public procurement timing risk

The company is seeking to widen XR training content supply to public customers such as the defense ministry and police agency, and the VR police training system PoliceOne was reported to have been deployed to police stations nationwide.

Public contracts follow budget allocation and bidding or procurement procedures, so the timing of revenue recognition can vary from quarter to quarter. If budgets shrink or procurement schedules slip, the pace of public-sector revenue recovery could be slower than expected.

Annual target achievement risk

The company set a 2026 consolidated revenue target of KRW 12.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.0 billion or more, describing it as conservative guidance built on confirmed revenue.

The target assumes restructuring effects and revenue already secured in existing businesses, so if contract timing slips, the gap between the annual target and actual results could widen.

If results fall short, the difference will appear in the 2026 annual report due in late March 2027, which could weigh on confidence in the business plan.

Overall view

Skonec's 2025 revenue rose 52.3% to KRW 6.76 billion, marking an annual rebound, but the operating loss widened to KRW 12.2 billion that year and operating cash flow was -KRW 11.2 billion.

Operating losses narrowed through 2026, to KRW 1.74 billion in Q1, KRW 868 million in Q2 and KRW 513 million in Q3, but quarterly revenue fell for three straight quarters to KRW 704 million, KRW 658 million and KRW 177 million.

The company targeted 2026 annual revenue of KRW 12.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.0 billion or more, and the gap with quarterly results will be visible in the 2026 annual report. Failed M&A, capital tied up in new ventures and repeated contract amendments have raised questions about the core business.

On the other side, the Meta-linked game and public and training contracts remain the basis for a possible revenue recovery. The debt ratio rose to 55.9% in 2025, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 19.2 billion.

The current pattern is shrinking losses alongside falling revenue, and whether this reverses will be shown in the 2027 annual and quarterly reports.
