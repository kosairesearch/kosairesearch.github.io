Pemtron: Shifting to Chip Inspection, Losses Persist

Pemtron is shifting from SMT inspection equipment toward semiconductor inspection tools, and it has recorded a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders for three consecutive quarters from 1Q26 through 3Q26.

02

Business structure

Pemtron, founded in 2002, is a specialist in 3D inspection equipment. Its core products are SPI, MOI and AOI systems for surface-mount technology (SMT) lines, and the company has extended the same inspection technology into semiconductor back-end and secondary-battery lead-tab inspection.

In 2022 the revenue mix was 79.5% SMT, 18.4% semiconductor and 2.1% secondary battery. In 2025 the mix shifted to 66.7% SMT and 33.3% semiconductor, and the semiconductor share was 25.5% in 3Q25 versus about 14% in the first nine months of 2024.

By end-market, the 1Q23 revenue split was 33% automotive electronics, 32% IoT, 21% servers and 12% mobile. In its 2026 IR materials, the company said it had secured inspection equipment orders from SK hynix, Micron and GlobalFoundries.

Pemtron spends more than 17% of revenue on R&D, and 143 researchers make up 54.3% of its workforce. In SMT inspection, a 2023 broker report placed it roughly fifth globally but with annual sales still below KRW 100 billion. Its semiconductor tools target inspection demand tied to HBM and other advanced packaging.

04

Earnings analysis

Pemtron's annual results peaked in 2023. Revenue was KRW 73.7 billion, up 20.5% year on year, operating profit was KRW 8.06 billion, up 26.9%, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7.12 billion, up 35.8%.

In 2024 revenue fell 22.7% to KRW 57 billion, and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 3.62 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.91 billion.

Hana Securities, in a December 2024 report, cited a sharp drop in SMT orders from European and American EMS customers and geopolitical risk affecting Russian and Israeli customers as reasons for the 2024 weakness.

In 2025 revenue recovered to KRW 69.9 billion, up 22.6%, and operating profit returned to KRW 3.18 billion, but the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 3.79 billion. The operating margin moved from 10.4% in 2022 and 10.9% in 2023 to -6.4% in 2024 and 4.6% in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 155.6% in 2024 to 316.2% in 2025, when total liabilities were KRW 81 billion and total equity was KRW 25.6 billion. Quarterly, revenue was KRW 17.9 billion in 3Q25 with operating profit of KRW 1.08 billion, and it rose to KRW 24.2 billion in 4Q25 with operating profit of KRW 4.87 billion.

In 1Q26 revenue was KRW 16.9 billion with an operating loss of KRW 1.04 billion, and in 2Q26 revenue reached KRW 24 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.39 billion, a return to profit.

In 3Q26 revenue was KRW 21.8 billion, up 22.2% year on year, but operating profit fell 48.0% year on year to KRW 558 million and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 4.32 billion.

The four-quarter net loss attributable to controlling shareholders totals KRW 4.92 billion, and the reason operating profit stayed positive while the net loss widened in 3Q26 is a point to check in the quarterly filing.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for semiconductor inspection equipment is tied to investment in next-generation memory such as HBM, driven by AI servers and high-performance computing. Pemtron has said it expects demand for its semiconductor inspection tools to rise sharply with the growth of AI and HPC markets.

In an April 2026 report, the Korea IR Council projected that the semiconductor share of revenue would surpass the SMT share for the first time in 2026. For the semiconductor business, supply expansion to overseas IDMs and equipment validation at domestic IDMs have been cited as growth conditions.

SMT inspection equipment, by contrast, is sensitive to demand from automotive electronics, servers and mobile. A 2023 report pointed to economic slowdown weighing on server and mobile demand, and in 2024 orders from European and American EMS customers dropped sharply.

On competition, Pemtron ranks among the global leaders in SMT inspection but its annual sales remain small in scale. In semiconductor inspection, revenue depends on whether customers pass production qualification. The position in the cycle therefore depends on whether SMT demand recovers while semiconductor orders expand.

06

Outlook

In its 2Q26 results announcement, the company said global customers' investment in AI server memory and advanced packaging continues, and that the start of new semiconductor equipment supply should improve revenue and profitability in the second half.

A supply contract with Micron's Malaysian subsidiary, disclosed on February 5, 2026, is worth KRW 3.15 billion. On March 30, 2026, the company disclosed an SMT inspection equipment supply contract totaling about KRW 5.27 billion.

The company said it is strengthening high-value product lines such as 3D semiconductor inspection tools and X-ray automated inspection (AXI) equipment for semiconductor packaging. It also plans to expand into flip-chip ball grid array (FC-BGA) applications to diversify customers and processes.

The Korea IR Council projected that the semiconductor share would exceed half of revenue in 2026. Points to confirm include whether the semiconductor revenue share actually passed 50% in the 2026 annual report and the scale of 4Q26 operating profit.

The latest quarterly results disclosed run through 3Q26, and the next periodic report is the 2026 annual report. How new equipment deliveries are recognized as revenue each quarter will be a key variable for 2027 results.

Bull factors

Rising semiconductor revenue share

The semiconductor share rose from 18.4% in 2022 to 33.3% in 2025, and reached 25.5% in 3Q25. The company said in its 3Q25 results that growth accelerated as high-value semiconductor equipment began shipping in earnest. The Korea IR Council projected that the semiconductor share would surpass SMT in 2026.

Expanding orders from large customers

A supply contract with Micron's Malaysian subsidiary, disclosed on February 5, 2026, is worth KRW 3.15 billion. The company said customer investment in AI server memory and advanced packaging is continuing.

Return to quarterly profit

Operating profit was KRW 4.87 billion in 4Q25, and it turned positive again at KRW 1.39 billion in 2Q26. In its 2Q26 results, the company said new semiconductor equipment supply should improve second-half revenue and profitability. Whether operating profit stays positive is the point to confirm for this bullish case.

Bear factors

Persistent losses and wider net loss

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened from KRW 1.91 billion in 2024 to KRW 3.79 billion in 2025. It has been in the red for three consecutive quarters from 1Q26 through 3Q26, and in 3Q26 it turned from a year-earlier profit to a KRW 4.32 billion loss. The four-quarter total of such losses is KRW 4.92 billion.

Cash outflow and rising debt

Operating cash flow was -KRW 4.98 billion in 2024 and -KRW 5.72 billion in 2025, negative in both years. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 81 billion, above total equity of KRW 25.6 billion. The debt ratio rose from 178.3% in 2022 to 316.2% in 2025.

SMT demand and quarterly volatility

Hana Securities, in a December 2024 report, cited a sharp drop in orders from European and American EMS customers as a reason for weak results. The SMT share was still 74.5% in 3Q25, so SMT demand swings pass through to earnings while the shift to semiconductors is incomplete. Operating profit fell to KRW 558 million in 3Q26 from KRW 1.39 billion in 2Q26.

10

Risk factors

Customers and delivery timing

Semiconductor revenue depends on equipment deliveries and acceptance inspections at large customers such as SK hynix, Micron and GlobalFoundries. If delivery or acceptance slips from one quarter to the next, revenue recognition shifts and quarterly results can swing more widely. A cut in customers' capital spending plans would also affect the order flow.

Funding and financial capacity

With limited financial headroom, production of new equipment and R&D must continue, which makes additional fundraising a variable. Depending on the terms and timing of external financing, the pace of capacity expansion and new product investment could change. Any capital increase could also affect the value of existing shareholders' stakes.

Technical qualification and new segments

In a February 2025 report, Hana Securities said the company's technical edge depends on passing qualification after equipment validation at domestic IDMs. If the expansion into new segments such as FC-BGA and AXI equipment is delayed, the pace of high-value equipment sales could slow. Longer validation would push back the timing of revenue recognition for new equipment.

Overall view

Pemtron is moving from SMT inspection equipment toward semiconductor inspection tools. The semiconductor share rose from 18.4% in 2022 to 33.3% in 2025, and the 2026 IR materials list orders from SK hynix, Micron and GlobalFoundries.

Annual revenue peaked in 2023 at KRW 73.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 8.06 billion. After a loss in 2024, operating profit returned to KRW 3.18 billion in 2025.

However, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 3.79 billion in 2025, and losses have continued for three consecutive quarters from 1Q26 through 3Q26. Operating cash flow was negative in both 2024 and 2025, and the debt ratio stood at 316.2% at the end of 2025.

Bullish factors are the growing semiconductor revenue and large customer orders, while bearish factors are continued net losses and cash outflows with heavy debt.

The next points to confirm are the semiconductor share and earnings composition in the 2026 annual report and the preliminary 2026 annual results in early 2027.
