02

Business structure

Pemtron builds 3D inspection equipment based on machine-vision optical inspection, spanning SMT (surface-mount technology) inspection tools, semiconductor inspection tools and secondary-battery inspection tools.

Cumulative through 3Q 2024, SMT equipment accounted for 86.1% of sales and semiconductor inspection equipment for about 14%, according to a Hana Securities report from December 2024.

The core semiconductor product was the ZEUS wire-bonding inspection tool, and the lineup has since expanded to the 8800WIR HBM wafer-pattern inspection tool and the MARS memory-module inspection tool.

SMT made up 73% of KRW 40.9 billion in first-half 2026 sales, so semiconductors have grown in weight but SMT remains the larger business.

Semiconductor customers now include SK hynix (HBM inspection equipment, confirmed September 2025), Micron (SOCAMM2 inspection equipment, confirmed February 2026), GlobalFoundries, and a domestic foundry (confirmed December 2025).

In its August 2026 results announcement, the company said it had won equipment orders within the supply chain of a Chinese memory maker, Company C, which added a new customer. It also agreed to supply 3D inspection equipment for AI data-center servers to a global EMS company.

The company has built a structure of direct shipments to overseas IDMs. One analysis notes that customers limit suppliers to two or three vendors per process because of security and technical difficulty, forming an oligopoly. The largest shareholder and related parties hold 44.61%, as of the last change recorded in September 2024.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue rose from KRW 61.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 73.7 billion in 2023, up 20.5%, making 2023 the largest year of 2022–2025. Revenue fell 22.7% to KRW 57 billion in 2024, the smallest year in the range, and operating profit swung to an operating loss of KRW 3.62 billion.

In 2025 revenue recovered to KRW 69.9 billion, up 22.6%, and operating profit turned positive at KRW 3.18 billion. Operating margin moved from 10.4% in 2022 and 10.9% in 2023 to -6.4% in 2024 and 4.6% in 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net income went from KRW 7.12 billion in 2023 to a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.91 billion in 2024, and the loss widened to KRW 3.79 billion in 2025. The company attributed the 2025 revenue change mainly to rising demand for 3D semiconductor inspection equipment.

Operating cash flow was KRW 8.17 billion in 2023 but turned negative at -KRW 4.98 billion in 2024 and -KRW 5.72 billion in 2025. At end-2025 total liabilities were KRW 81 billion against total equity of KRW 25.6 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose from 178.3% in 2022 to 316.2%.

By quarter, 4Q 2025 was the strongest, with revenue of KRW 24.2 billion, operating profit of KRW 4.87 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 3.04 billion. 1Q 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 1.04 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.42 billion. 2Q 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 1.39 billion, but the controlling-shareholder net loss widened to KRW 2.23 billion.

In 3Q 2026 revenue was KRW 21.8 billion, up 22.2% year on year, yet operating profit fell to KRW 558 million, down 48.0% year on year, and the controlling-shareholder net loss reached KRW 4.32 billion, a swing from profit a year earlier.

Operating profit in the black alongside a much larger net loss indicates that items below operating profit, such as non-operating items or taxes, weighed heavily. The four quarters from 4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026 total a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 4.92 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand is being led by the spread of AI servers. At its 3Q 2025 results announcement, the company said rising demand for high-value chips such as HBM, SoCAMM and ASICs was raising inspection difficulty and the variety of equipment required.

A corporate monitor summary projects semiconductor packaging to grow at an annual rate of 10.6% through 2028, and demand for wafer-bump and wire-bonding inspection equipment is also expanding.

SMT was weaker, however: in 2024 orders from European and North American EMS customers fell sharply, and geopolitical risk at Russian and Israeli customers compounded the slowdown, according to a Hana Securities report from December 2024. That period showed that an SMT-heavy mix is sensitive to the cycle.

Inspection equipment customers tend to narrow suppliers to two or three per process, so once a supplier passes qualification, follow-on orders can be more likely.

Compared with listed peers such as HANMI Semiconductor and EO Technics, which are centered on semiconductor equipment sales, Pemtron still has a higher SMT share. The semiconductor share rising from about 14% in 2024 to about 27% in the first half of 2026 is the clearest sign of the mix shift.

06

Outlook

At its 2Q 2026 results announcement in August 2026, the company said it expected sales and profitability to improve in the second half, citing the full-scale start of new semiconductor equipment deliveries. The same announcement said the company expected a record annual result for 2026.

However, 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 21.8 billion fell 9.1% from the prior quarter and operating profit was KRW 558 million, so the second-half improvement outlook and the actual quarterly trend diverged. The next checkpoints are the 4Q 2026 provisional results and the 2026 annual report.

With controlling-shareholder net losses totaling KRW 4.92 billion over the last four quarters, whether losses continue after 4Q 2026 is the central question. On orders, the items to track are whether SK hynix HBM inspection equipment moves into volume production and how large the Micron SOCAMM2 supply becomes.

The company said it is pursuing additional orders tied to capacity expansion and new process investment at Company C, a Chinese memory maker.

On products, it plans to extend into 3D semiconductor inspection equipment, X-ray automated inspection (AXI) equipment for semiconductor packaging, and flip-chip ball grid array (FC-BGA) applications.

In March 2026 it resolved to dispose of 260,100 treasury shares, with the stated purpose of employee welfare and securing R&D funds.

08

Bull factors

Wider customer base

Beyond SK hynix HBM inspection equipment (confirmed September 2025) and Micron SOCAMM2 inspection equipment (confirmed February 2026), customers now include GlobalFoundries, a domestic foundry and Company C, a Chinese memory maker.

The company said it is pursuing additional orders tied to Company C's capacity expansion. Broader customer diversification reduces dependence on any single customer.

Rising semiconductor share

The semiconductor share of sales rose from about 14% cumulative through 3Q 2024 to about 27% in the first half of 2026. The company said rising HBM and ASIC demand is increasing inspection difficulty and equipment needs. A Hana Securities report from 2025 projected margin improvement as the semiconductor equipment share grows.

Record of quarterly profit turns

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 3.18 billion in 2025, and 4Q 2025 operating profit of KRW 4.87 billion rose 352.9% from the prior quarter. 2Q 2026 also returned to operating profit of KRW 1.39 billion. Several quarterly recovery points are the basis for the bullish case.

Bear factors

Persistent quarterly net losses

Controlling-shareholder net losses have run for three consecutive quarters from 1Q 2026 through 3Q 2026, with a four-quarter total of KRW 4.92 billion. 3Q 2026 operating profit of KRW 558 million fell 48.0% year on year, and the controlling-shareholder net loss widened to KRW 4.32 billion.

Operating recovery has not carried through to controlling-shareholder earnings, which is the main bearish point.

Balance-sheet strain and cash flow

Total liabilities were KRW 81 billion against total equity of KRW 25.6 billion at end-2025, lifting the debt-to-equity ratio to 316.2% from 178.3% in 2022. Operating cash flow was -KRW 4.98 billion in 2024 and -KRW 5.72 billion in 2025.

With debt high relative to equity and operating cash generation negative, the burden of raising additional funds could grow.

Order volatility and sales swings

Revenue fell 22.7% to KRW 57 billion in 2024, which Hana Securities attributed mainly to a sharp drop in orders from European and North American EMS customers in the SMT business. 3Q 2026 revenue also fell 9.1% from the prior quarter.

Because order announcements do not translate immediately into quarterly sales, the predictability of quarterly results is limited.

Risk factors

Customer concentration and timing of volume production

Semiconductor sales appear to depend on a few large customers, including SK hynix and Micron. Even large supply contracts can see revenue recognition delayed by the timing of volume production and performance evaluation. In 2025 reporting, Micron's first and second units were awaiting performance-evaluation results.

Balance sheet and pace of earnings recovery

The debt-to-equity ratio stood at 316.2% at end-2025, above 178.3% in 2022, and operating cash flow was negative in both 2024 and 2025. With controlling-shareholder net losses totaling KRW 4.92 billion over the last four quarters, deleveraging through earnings is slow. Changes in the financial structure will be visible again in the 2026 annual report.

Ownership structure and share supply

The largest shareholder and related parties hold 44.61%. In March 2026 the company resolved to dispose of 260,100 treasury shares at a 4.8% discount to the regular-session closing price, to fund employee welfare and R&D. Additional tradable supply could add to share-price volatility.

Overall view

Pemtron is a 3D inspection equipment company that started in SMT inspection and is expanding into semiconductor inspection equipment.

On a disclosed basis, 2023 revenue of KRW 73.7 billion was the largest year of 2022–2025, and 2025 brought a recovery to KRW 69.9 billion in revenue and KRW 3.18 billion in operating profit.

Meanwhile, the 2025 controlling-shareholder net loss widened to KRW 3.79 billion, and controlling-shareholder net losses have continued for three consecutive quarters from 1Q 2026 through 3Q 2026.

Customers have broadened to SK hynix, Micron, GlobalFoundries and Company C, a Chinese memory maker, and the semiconductor share of first-half 2026 sales was about 27%, up from about 14% cumulative through 3Q 2024.

On the financial side, the debt-to-equity ratio of 316.2% at end-2025 and negative operating cash flow remain burdens. Because the last four quarters show net losses, PER cannot be calculated, and market capitalization sits well above net asset value.

The next checkpoints are the 4Q 2026 provisional results, the 2026 annual report and the 1Q 2027 results, which will show whether profitability holds and whether orders move into volume production.
