02

Business structure

S&KPOLYTECCO makes materials and parts across mobile parts (SMT), automotive wipers, information electronic materials and polyurethane (PU) foam.

A MarketScreener aggregation of 2025 segment data puts mobile parts at about KRW 286 billion, the largest segment, wipers at about KRW 144 billion and information electronic materials at about KRW 19.4 billion, before about KRW 139 billion of intersegment sales is removed to reach consolidated revenue of about KRW 310 billion.

The mobile parts business centers on flexible printed circuit assemblies used in OLED panels, so its results track display panel demand.

The wiper business sits in subsidiary CAP, and the company said strong wiper exports and cost savings from better production efficiency lifted the operating margin in the first half of 2026. PU foam is the legacy business, and press coverage points to stagnant results from intensified competition.

The company said its PU foam for EV batteries passed a qualification test at a leading Chinese battery maker and that it is negotiating deliveries with customers.

It bought 100% of Sunjin Engineering in June 2026, which makes EV busbars and terminals through precision press processing, and completed an absorption merger in October. Because mobile parts are the largest segment, order changes from display customers pass quickly into revenue.

Unlike large petrochemical groups tied to commodity prices, the company's revenue depends more on customer orders and export volumes.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 336.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 251.1 billion in 2023 (down 25.3% year on year), recovered to KRW 330.1 billion in 2024 (up 31.5%), and was KRW 310.2 billion in 2025 (down 6.0%). The largest year in 2022-2025 was 2022 and the smallest was 2023.

Operating profit rose from KRW 194 million in 2022 to KRW 2.84 billion in 2023 (up 1,367.3%), KRW 15.2 billion in 2024 (up 436.0%) and KRW 15.7 billion in 2025 (up 3.2%), increasing for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, while the operating margin moved from 0.1% to 5.1%.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 1.21 billion in 2022 and a loss of KRW 5.88 billion in 2023, a continued loss that widened, before turning to a profit of KRW 8.87 billion in 2024 and falling 54.9% to KRW 4 billion in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 11.5 billion in 2025, below KRW 15.7 billion in 2024, and the company attributed the drop to lower non-operating income such as foreign exchange gains. The gap between the two profit measures is the share belonging to non-controlling interests.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 118.2% in 2022 to 80.7% in 2025, with year-end 2025 total equity of KRW 141 billion, of which KRW 92.4 billion belongs to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities of KRW 113.8 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 12.8 billion in 2022, KRW 8.03 billion in 2023, KRW 21.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.42 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue peaked at KRW 85.7 billion in 4Q 2025 and then fell over three consecutive quarters to KRW 70.5 billion, KRW 68.4 billion and KRW 57.7 billion in 3Q 2026, with 3Q down 16.8% year on year.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 6.72 billion in 1Q 2026, then fell for two consecutive quarters to KRW 4.95 billion and KRW 1.31 billion, with 3Q down 50.4% year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 21.8 billion in 3Q 2026, up 804.6% from the prior quarter, which coincides with the timing of the gain of about KRW 30 billion on the KRW 48 billion sale of the Ansan factory in June.

Preliminary results announced on November 17, 2026 showed cumulative third-quarter operating profit of KRW 13 billion, up 33% year on year, and cumulative net profit of KRW 32.1 billion, consistent with the sum of the disclosed first-to-third-quarter operating profits.

Of the KRW 30.1 billion total for the last four quarters, KRW 21.8 billion came from 3Q 2026, so the earnings base leans heavily on one quarter's one-off items.

05

Industry analysis

S&KPOLYTECCO's main markets are mobile display parts, automotive wipers and PU foam, each with its own cycle. Mobile parts move with OLED panel demand, so display customers' production plans are the largest external variable for results.

Wiper earnings rose with export volumes and cost improvements, and the company cites strong CAP exports as the center of its first-half 2026 profit recovery. PU foam is widening its applications, but press coverage describes intensified competition that limits growth.

The EV electrical parts business, built on Sunjin's precision press processing for busbars and terminals, needs capacity expansion and facility investment before it can contribute meaningfully.

Large chemical companies are exposed to petrochemical commodity and battery material cycles, whereas S&KPOLYTECCO is smaller and driven more by customer orders.

Revenue fell in 2026 while margins improved through company-specific factors such as CAP cost savings and the asset sale, which matters for separating company effects from industry trends. Whether the industry itself is turning cannot be judged from company disclosures alone.

06

Outlook

The main guidance from the company is that Sunjin Engineering's results will be reflected from the fourth quarter of 2026, allowing meaningful separate-basis revenue growth.

The company said that after using proceeds from the June factory sale to repay all KRW 26 billion of separate-basis short-term borrowings as of the end of 1Q 2026, it can still invest more than KRW 20 billion.

It plans to use these funds to expand existing businesses or enter new ones and to pursue M&A with companies that have stable growth potential. Expectations for a broader shareholder return policy have followed the completion of a governance change in March 2026.

The PU foam for EV batteries has passed a qualification test at a leading Chinese battery maker, but the company has not disclosed delivery timing or volumes. The EV parts business is pursuing facility investment and quality control capability, and its contribution depends on capacity expansion and order wins.

Whether wiper export strength continues depends on automotive demand and CAP's production efficiency. Whether the asset sale gain is one-off or repeatable will show up in operating profit in quarters after 2026.

The next confirmation point is the fourth-quarter 2026 result and the scale of Sunjin's contribution in the 2026 annual report.

Bull factors

Wiper exports and cost gains

The company said strong wiper exports and cost savings at CAP lifted first-half 2026 consolidated operating profit 65% year on year, in a preliminary release dated August 18, 2026. Combining the disclosed first- and second-quarter operating profits gives about KRW 11.7 billion, with CAP's margin gains at the center.

The wiper segment was the second-largest in the 2025 aggregation, so it offers a base tied to automotive export flows.

Asset sale creates investment capacity

In June 2026 the company sold its idle Ansan factory for KRW 48 billion, with a gain of about KRW 30 billion reported. The company said it can still invest more than KRW 20 billion after repaying all KRW 26 billion of separate-basis short-term borrowings at the end of 1Q 2026. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 118.2% in 2022 to 80.7% at year-end 2025, reducing financial burden.

New EV parts and battery foam lines

The Sunjin merger brought EV busbar and terminal operations in-house, and the company said those results will be reflected from 4Q 2026, enabling meaningful separate-basis sales growth. Its battery-use PU foam passed a qualification test at a leading Chinese battery maker, and deliveries are under discussion.

The company said its share price is excessively low relative to fundamentals and hinted at broader shareholder returns, a view that belongs to the company.

Bear factors

Consecutive declines in revenue and operating profit

Revenue has fallen for three consecutive quarters since 1Q 2026, and 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 57.7 billion was down 16.8% year on year. Operating profit peaked at KRW 6.72 billion in 1Q 2026 and has fallen for two quarters since, with 3Q at KRW 1.31 billion down 50.4% year on year.

With both sales and core profit declining, how far the wiper recovery can carry group results is the open question.

One-off character of 3Q net profit

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 21.8 billion in 3Q 2026 far exceeds operating profit of KRW 1.31 billion, indicating the asset sale gain lifted net profit sharply.

That quarter makes up most of the last four quarters' KRW 30.1 billion total, so excluding it would change the earnings picture considerably. The 54.9% decline in 2025 controlling-shareholder profit also shows that earnings power has not yet stabilized.

Stalled PU foam and insider share sale

Press coverage describes stagnant PU foam results from intensified competition, while the mobile parts business depends on display demand. On March 12, 2026, executive director Park Hyung-shin sold 1,538 common shares on the market, reducing his holding to zero according to the filing. The disclosure alone does not establish whether this sale signals anything about the outlook.

Risk factors

One-off earnings items

Third-quarter net profit leaned heavily on the factory sale gain, which will not recur. Whether operating profit holds up after the third quarter will determine earnings quality. The larger the share of non-operating items, the more quarterly net profit swings.

Execution risk in new businesses

The Sunjin merger and EV parts facility investment only contribute to results if capacity expansion and order wins follow. The battery PU foam is still at the negotiation stage, with no disclosed volumes or timing. Until the new businesses grow, stagnation in the legacy business could persist.

Customer demand and currency swings

Mobile parts follow display customers' demand, and wipers depend on automotive exports. The 2025 net profit decline was attributed partly to lower foreign exchange gains in non-operating income, so currency moves can swing net profit through non-operating items.

Overall view

S&KPOLYTECCO's 2026 results show a split between top-line trends and core profit. Revenue has fallen for three consecutive quarters since 1Q 2026, and operating profit has declined for two quarters since its 1Q 2026 peak.

On the other side, CAP's wiper exports and cost savings lifted first-half 2026 operating profit, and preliminary cumulative third-quarter operating profit of KRW 13 billion was up 33% year on year, announced November 17, 2026.

The KRW 21.8 billion third-quarter net profit attributable to controlling shareholders appears to lean heavily on the factory sale gain, so earnings quality needs to be confirmed.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 80.7% at year-end 2025 from 118.2% in 2022, and the June sale proceeds have created room for new investment. The Sunjin merger and battery PU foam talks are presented as growth lines, but their contribution must show up in capacity and order wins.

PU foam remains stagnant amid intensified competition, and an insider share sale was disclosed in March 2026. The next confirmation points are the 2026 annual report, due by the end of March 2027, and the 1Q 2027 quarterly report, due by May 15, 2027.
