02

Business structure

G&E Healthcare was founded in February 2014 and is a manufacturer of other textile products that listed on the KONEX market on June 29, 2018. Its flagship brand is Hannah Pad, a reusable organic cotton pad. In 2018, Hannah Pad accounted for 91.4% of KRW 9.25 billion in revenue.

The range has since widened to menstrual cups, period underwear, incontinence pads and eco-friendly laundry products. In January 2019 the company absorbed the third-ranked domestic cotton pad maker as a subsidiary, and Hanwha Research put its domestic cotton pad share at about 90%.

That share applies only to the narrow reusable pad segment. As of 2017, alternative products held less than 2% of the domestic sanitary pad market, which was led by Yuhan-Kimberly, LG Unicharm and P&G Korea in disposables.

Sanitary pads are quasi-drugs overseen by the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety, so sales require an approval process. This regulation acts as a modest barrier to new entrants. Hanwha Research said the export share had risen to around 20% as of 2020. The stock is classified under textiles, fashion and household goods.

Its business and scale differ completely from apparel majors such as Youngone and F&F, so there are few listed peers for a direct comparison.

Recent trends

At the end of 2025, standalone total assets were KRW 6,974 million, total liabilities KRW 2,275 million and total equity KRW 4,699 million. For the year, revenue was KRW 4,897 million, operating profit KRW 372 million and net profit KRW 377 million, with net profit slightly above operating profit.

The auditor's standalone opinion was unqualified. The Company Monitor attributed the improvement to continued preference for organic cotton products and demand for the company's eco-friendly lines.

FnGuide's consolidated figures differ in scope: revenue of KRW 3.6 billion with an operating loss of KRW 0.4 billion in 2023, KRW 3.1 billion with a loss of KRW 0.2 billion in 2024, and KRW 5.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 0.1 billion in 2025.

Those consolidated figures are rounded to the nearest KRW 100 million, so they do not reconcile directly with the standalone numbers. The share price was KRW 405 on March 31, 2026 and rose 222% during July 2026. News on July 16, 2026 reported a trading halt and investment warning designation.

The September 30, 2026 close was KRW 590, against a 52-week high of KRW 1,000 and a low of KRW 178. On October 16, 2026 the stock rose 11.60% to KRW 779, yet only 22 shares changed hands. On October 27, 2026 it fell 14.16% to KRW 600, giving back a large part of that move. Quarterly results after the first half of 2026 could not be confirmed in this research.

Outlook

Future earnings depend on the stability of domestic online sales centered on Hannah Pad, along with new products and export growth. Standalone operating profit of KRW 372 million in 2025 implies an operating margin in the high single digits, so keeping revenue large enough to cover fixed costs is the key.

The operating margin has room to improve with higher sales, but the 2019 loss shows how easily the cost structure can erode it. In 2019, revenue was KRW 5,000 million and the operating loss was KRW 1,536 million. No broker consensus target price is compiled for the stock, so external estimates offer little guidance.

On the regulatory side, reports described a rule change that would add a share price below KRW 1,000 as a delisting criterion, and Daily Pharm included G&E Healthcare (KRW 599) among stocks priced under KRW 1,000. How the rule applies to the KONEX market needs separate confirmation.

The treasury share purchases continued in small lots on each trading day from October 1 to 12, 2026. The total still fell short of the planned KRW 100 million.

We leave the judgment on price direction to readers; the indicators to watch are half-year and annual results, trading liquidity and market warning designations.

Bull factors

Return to profit in 2025

According to the Company Monitor, standalone revenue grew 73.8% in 2025 and operating and net profit turned positive. Operating profit of KRW 372 million and net profit of KRW 377 million were both positive.

The change follows consolidated operating losses in 2023 and 2024, giving a basis to test whether the business has stabilized.

Leading brand in a narrow segment

Hanwha Research cited a domestic cotton pad market share of about 90%. The quasi-drug approval process makes it hard for small new entrants to catch up quickly. If eco-friendly consumption and the focus on product safety persist, Hannah Pad's brand recognition could serve as a demand base.

Balance sheet and buybacks

Standalone total assets were KRW 6,974 million and total liabilities KRW 2,275 million in 2025. Equity exceeds liabilities, so financial stability looks adequate for a company of this size. The company held 5.26% of its shares as treasury stock as of October 12, 2026.

This signals an intent toward shareholder returns or price stability, but the small scale of purchases limits what can be inferred.

Bear factors

Small scale and single-brand reliance

Standalone 2025 revenue of KRW 4,897 million is small. In 2018, 91.4% of revenue came from Hannah Pad. The range has widened since, but we could not confirm how much the single-brand dependence has eased. A small workforce and limited resources may make it hard to respond to promotional competition from large players.

Earnings volatility and past losses

In 2019 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1,536 million and a net loss of KRW 1,571 million. On FnGuide's consolidated basis, operating losses also continued in 2023 and 2024. The durability of the 2025 turnaround rests on a single year of results so far. Quarterly results for 2026 could not be confirmed in this research.

Extremely thin trading

On October 16, 2026 volume was 22 shares, and the intraday range ran from KRW 600 to KRW 781. On October 27, 2026 trading value was only KRW 46,190. Even small orders can move quotes sharply, so the price is hard to read as a reflection of business value. Executing at a desired price when buying or selling may also be difficult.

Risk factors

Market and regulatory

In July 2026 G&E Healthcare was newly designated an investment warning stock. A rule change adding a share price below KRW 1,000 as a delisting criterion was also reported. How the criterion applies on the KONEX market needs confirmation, and market warning designations can lead to trading restrictions.

Business and competition

The domestic sanitary pad market is led by large disposable-product makers, and alternative products hold a small share. Cotton pads carry the structural drawback of laundering and upkeep, which may cap demand growth.

Demand gained from safety concerns over disposables can fade as the issue subsides, as the 2019 revenue decline showed.

Financial and governance

As of 2020, the chief executive held a 75.1% stake. A high major-shareholder stake leaves a small free float, which tends to keep trading thin. This is a structural factor that amplifies price volatility. The latest shareholding structure could not be confirmed here, so regulatory filings should be checked directly.

Overall view

A single year of results is too little to judge how durable that earning power is. The share price swung widely after July 2026 and fell 14.16% to KRW 600 on October 27, 2026. With a KRW 6.7 billion market cap and trading value of only KRW 46,190, it is hard to confirm that the quote accurately reflects business value.
