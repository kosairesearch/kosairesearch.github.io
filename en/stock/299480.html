Business structure

G&E Healthcare was founded in February 2014 as a maker and seller of women's hygiene products and is headquartered in Hanam, Gyeonggi Province. Its reusable organic cotton menstrual pads under the Hannah Pad brand form the core of revenue, supplemented by menstrual cups, period underwear and disposable pads.

As of 2025, domestic sales account for 93.02% of reusable pad revenue and exports for 6.98%, so most revenue is generated through domestic channels. Menstrual cups, period underwear and disposable pads were recorded as 100% domestic sales in the same period.

Overseas, the company set up a Vietnam sales subsidiary in September 2019 and a U.S. sales subsidiary, HANNAH HEALTH ONE Inc., in February 2021, in which it holds a 90% stake.

Vietnam OEM production began in March 2019 to restructure cost, and the domestic pad maker Triplelife, acquired in January 2019, was absorbed through a small-scale merger in December 2021.

On the competitive side, consumer preference for safety intensified after the disposable pad contaminant controversy of 2017 and 2018, which lifted demand for alternative products such as cloth pads.

However, Hannah Pad accounted for 91.4% of revenue in 2018, showing heavy reliance on a single brand, so the pace of product diversification remains central to earnings stability.
