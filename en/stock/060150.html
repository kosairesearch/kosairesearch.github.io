Insun Environmental NEW Technology: Profit Rebound Amid Shrinking Sales

Insun Environmental NEW Technology posted losses attributable to controlling shareholders in 2024 and 2025, but returned to profit in each quarter of 2026 through the third quarter. Revenue and operating profit have both declined for three straight years since 2023, so whether the profit recovery is sustained remains the key question.

02

Business structure

Insun Environmental NEW Technology was founded in 1997 to collect, transport and intermediately treat construction waste, and listed on KOSDAQ in 2002.

Starting from construction waste intermediate treatment, the group has expanded into recycled aggregate production and sales, landfill, incineration, automotive dismantling and shredding recycling, and waste battery recycling, covering most of the waste treatment chain within one corporate group.

At the end of 2025, construction waste accounted for 84.66% of consolidated revenue, landfill and asbestos waste for 11.52%, rental income for 1.98%, and recycled aggregate and related sales for 1.84%, according to fnguide.

In that same table, automotive recycling, shown separately through 2024, does not appear for 2025, so segment shares across years are hard to compare directly.

A news report on the third-quarter 2026 disclosure put cumulative segment revenue through Q3 at KRW 87.9 billion for incineration and construction waste intermediate treatment, KRW 45.3 billion for end-of-life vehicle recycling, and KRW 7.9 billion for landfill.

Automotive recycling is handled by the subsidiary Insun Motors, and the incineration business widened through the acquisition of an incineration specialist.

The company describes itself as holding the top market share in construction waste collection and transport and end-of-life vehicle dismantling, while an industry-ranking site lists it second among peers in its sector.

Incineration requires high technical capability, safety performance and regulatory compliance, which raises the barriers to entry.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 237.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 221 billion in 2023 (down 7.0% year on year), KRW 208.8 billion in 2024 (down 5.5%) and KRW 187.2 billion in 2025 (down 10.4%), declining for three consecutive years from 2023.

Operating profit also dropped for three straight years, from KRW 33.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 25.8 billion in 2023 (down 22.3%), KRW 23.2 billion in 2024 (down 10.2%) and KRW 18.2 billion in 2025 (down 21.4%), and the operating margin narrowed from 14.0% to 11.7%, 11.1% and 9.7% over the same period.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 21.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.71 billion in 2023 (down 92.2%), then turned into a loss of KRW 18.9 billion in 2024. The loss narrowed to KRW 10.9 billion in 2025, and net profit including non-controlling interests followed the same path.

The company said that although revenue and operating profit declined in 2025, the impairment charges on intangible assets such as goodwill were smaller than the prior year, which improved the profit structure indicators (based on news reports).

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 46.2 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 47.2 billion in Q4 2025 (up 2.1% quarter on quarter) to KRW 42.5 billion in Q1 2026 (down 9.9%), before rising to KRW 50.3 billion in Q2 2026 (up 18.2%) and settling at KRW 48.3 billion in Q3 2026 (down 3.8% quarter on quarter, up 4.6% year on year).

Operating profit fell from KRW 5.38 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 5.9 billion in Q4 2025 (up 9.7%) to KRW 2.79 billion in Q1 2026 (down 52.8%).

In Q2 2026 revenue rose but operating profit slipped to KRW 2.32 billion (down 16.9% quarter on quarter), and in Q3 it recovered to KRW 4.39 billion (up 89.5% quarter on quarter) yet remained 18.4% below the year-earlier quarter.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a loss of KRW 1.2 billion in Q3 2025 and a larger loss of KRW 5.36 billion in Q4 2025 to a profit of KRW 1.72 billion in Q1 2026, then KRW 1.24 billion in Q2 (down 28.2%) and KRW 2.5 billion in Q3 (up 101.7%).

The sum for the last four quarters was KRW 96.88 million. In Q4 2025 operating profit was positive while the net loss widened, so non-operating items and income tax effects need to be checked against the detailed annual report.

Quarterly revenue has stayed within a fairly narrow range, but operating profit and net profit swing widely, and whether the 2026 profit recovery holds will be confirmed in the annual report.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for construction waste treatment is tied to construction activity. In its Q3 2026 disclosure, the company said construction waste revenue declined somewhat year on year amid a prolonged construction slowdown (based on news reports).

By contrast, a December 2025 report noted that steady remodeling and urban redevelopment projects keep construction waste demand at a certain level. The same coverage described the waste industry as having low correlation with the business cycle, with tighter regulation supporting stable demand.

Stricter incineration rules from the Ministry of Environment and carbon reduction policy are cited as factors favoring companies with eco-friendly facilities and technical capability. Automotive recycling is described as having become an important environmental indicator and a growth pillar for the company.

In the Q3 2026 disclosure, landfill revenue rose 5% year on year and end-of-life vehicle recycling revenue rose 24% (based on news reports). On a peer ranking basis, the company is listed second in its sector, with Hankyung Paper's counterpart Hangang E&P in third.

The company is focusing on nearby orders and maintaining appropriate unit prices with a profitability-first strategy. Because construction waste volumes directly drive results, the timing of a construction recovery is the central variable in this industry cycle.

06

Outlook

A company representative explained that the business environment was still unfavorable at the time of the Q3 2026 disclosure, but said that, as a result of focusing selectively on improving profitability, profit improved significantly compared with the previous quarter (according to news reports).

The same representative said that revenue was holding steady and that this was translating into improved profit. There was also a remark that once the economy enters a full-fledged recovery, the improvement in earnings will be even greater.

08

Bull factors

Profit in every quarter of 2026

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.72 billion in Q1 2026, turning from a loss to a profit, followed by KRW 1.24 billion in Q2 and KRW 2.5 billion in Q3. Compared with the KRW 5.36 billion net loss in Q4 2025, earnings capacity is moving back toward profitability.

Q3 operating profit of KRW 4.39 billion rose 89.5% from the prior quarter, showing an operating-level improvement as well.

Improving balance sheet

The debt-to-equity ratio declined from 61.0% in 2022 to 50.3% in 2025. Total liabilities were KRW 155.5 billion and total equity KRW 308.9 billion at end-2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 27.8 billion in 2025, below KRW 33.9 billion in 2022, yet cash generation continued through the loss years, which leaves a financial buffer.

Expansion in automotive recycling and incineration

In its Q3 2026 disclosure, the company said end-of-life vehicle recycling revenue rose 24% year on year, with cumulative revenue of KRW 45.3 billion for that segment. Cumulative incineration and construction waste intermediate treatment revenue was KRW 87.9 billion.

Incineration is viewed as a business with high entry barriers because it demands technical capability and regulatory compliance, and industry commentary holds that tighter environmental rules support stable demand.

Bear factors

Three straight years of falling revenue and operating profit

Revenue declined for three consecutive years, from KRW 221 billion in 2023 to KRW 208.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 187.2 billion in 2025, with operating profit falling in the same period. The operating margin narrowed from 14.0% in 2022 to 9.7% in 2025.

The company attributed the drop mainly to a prolonged construction slowdown that reduced construction waste revenue.

Operating profit still below the prior year

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 48.3 billion rose 4.6% year on year, yet operating profit of KRW 4.39 billion fell 18.4% from the year-earlier quarter. In Q2 2026 revenue rose to KRW 50.3 billion while operating profit declined to KRW 2.32 billion. Revenue growth has not yet translated into higher operating profit.

Small base and volatile quarterly profit

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 96.88 million, a small base. In Q4 2025 operating profit was KRW 5.9 billion, yet the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 5.36 billion.

Profitable quarters have swung widely, so whether annual profitability holds needs confirmation in the annual report.

10

Risk factors

Construction demand risk

Construction waste accounted for 84.66% of revenue at end-2025, so sales are highly sensitive to construction slowdowns. A prolonged downturn could reduce volumes and pull down both revenue and operating profit.

The company says it is responding with nearby orders and steady unit prices, but volume recovery remains the key factor.

Regulatory and capital expenditure risk

Tighter incineration rules from the Ministry of Environment are seen as favorable for companies with eco-friendly facilities. However, meeting these rules requires capital spending and compliance costs. As requirements tighten, facility burdens could grow and slow the pace of margin improvement.

Asset impairment and shareholder structure risk

The company explained that impairment charges on intangible assets such as goodwill were smaller in 2025 than in the prior year, improving profit indicators. That also means past impairments could have materially swung earnings.

The controlling shareholder holds a large stake and the free float is around 50%, which may raise share price volatility.

Overall view

Insun Environmental NEW Technology is an environmental company that has expanded from construction waste treatment into recycled aggregate, landfill, incineration and automotive recycling.

On a disclosed basis, revenue and operating profit both declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and the operating margin narrowed from 14.0% to 9.7%. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was negative in both 2024 and 2025.

Quarterly profit was positive from Q1 through Q3 2026, and Q3 operating profit rose sharply from the prior quarter but fell year on year. The debt-to-equity ratio declined, while total equity also fell over the same period.

Strengths include the return to profit, the improved financial structure, and growth in automotive recycling and incineration. Weaknesses include dependence on construction activity, three years of falling sales and margins, and quarterly volatility from a small profit base.

The next checkpoints are the full-year results in the 2026 annual report and whether profitability persists in Q1 2027.
