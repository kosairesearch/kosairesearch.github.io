02

Business structure

KT Genie Music operates three business lines: a music business centered on the 'genie' digital streaming platform covering both music services and distribution; a book content business run through subsidiary KT Millie's Library's e-book subscription platform; and other businesses spanning concerts, merchandise (MD/goods), and IT development services.

As of Q1 2026, music accounted for the largest share of revenue at 65.2%, but book content contributed 70.6% of operating profit, reflecting growing reliance on the subsidiary for earnings.

The music business is pursuing B2B revenue growth through telecom-partnered services with KT and LG Uplus, and connected-car cooperation with Hyundai Motor Group.

Major shareholders include KT Studio Genie (35.97%), CJ ENM (15.35%), and LG Uplus (9.99%), forming a structure that links the KT group and CJ content value chains.

In the domestic music distribution market, Kakao Entertainment's Melon retains the lead while global platforms such as YouTube Music and Spotify have pushed KT Genie Music's monthly active user ranking down to around fourth place.

KT Millie's Library, which runs the book content business, provides e-book subscriptions across B2C, B2B, and B2B2C channels and expanded into web novels and webtoons in 2025.

Other businesses—concert planning and ticketing, merchandise sales, and the online concert platform 'STAYG'—have actually seen revenue decline over the past three years.

The success of the diversification strategy hinges on how much the subsidiary and new businesses can offset structural stagnation in the core music business.

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated revenue rose from KRW 286.1bn in 2022 to KRW 307.0bn in 2023, then stayed range-bound at KRW 301.6bn in 2024 and KRW 302.9bn in 2025.

Operating profit moved from KRW 13.82bn (2022) to KRW 15.36bn (2023) to KRW 12.19bn (2024), then recovered to KRW 16.17bn in 2025, lifting the operating margin from 4.0% back to 5.3%.

Net income told a very different story: after posting owner net profit of KRW 17.54bn in 2022 and KRW 13.81bn in 2023, the company swung to a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 20.10bn in 2024 and KRW 27.52bn in 2025, marking two consecutive years of losses.

This was driven largely by substantial goodwill impairments recognized in 2024 and 2025 related to the 2018 acquisition of CJ Digital Music. On a quarterly basis, owner net profit remained solid at KRW 2.28bn through Q3 2025, but a sharp loss of KRW 32.36bn in Q4 2025 significantly damaged the full-year result.

Q1 2026 showed signs of normalization with revenue of KRW 75.18bn, operating profit of KRW 4.96bn, and owner net profit of KRW 1.39bn, but Q2 2026 saw operating profit shrink to KRW 1.89bn and owner net income revert to a loss of KRW 1.99bn.

Over the trailing four quarters (Q3 2025 to Q2 2026), cumulative owner net loss stood at KRW 30.68bn, reflecting a recurring pattern in which one-off impairments and bad-debt charges weigh on results despite operational stability.

On the balance sheet side, the debt ratio has steadily declined from 87.7% in 2022 to 55.7% in 2025, indicating improving capital soundness even as profitability has been volatile.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic music streaming market has reached subscriber saturation, and competition has intensified as global platforms such as YouTube Music and Spotify have accelerated their entry, even as Kakao Entertainment's Melon retains the top position.

As of February 2026, monthly active users ranked YouTube Music first at 9.02 million, Melon second at 6.62 million, Spotify third at 3.20 million, and KT Genie Music fourth at 2.61 million—a decline from its former second-place standing behind Melon.

Amid this competitive backdrop, music segment revenue has contracted every year since 2022, and its share of total revenue has continued to shrink.

In contrast, the e-book and audiobook subscription market has maintained growth momentum centered on B2B and B2B2C channels, and KT Millie's Library has been expanding share through telecom bundling partnerships and publisher collaborations.

The concert and artist-IP-linked market is also considered to have growth potential, but the company's other business segment (concerts/merchandise) has actually seen revenue decline over the past three years.

Overall, KT Genie Music can be seen as undergoing a portfolio shift between a maturing, increasingly competitive core music business and a growing subsidiary business in book content.

06

Outlook

The company launched an AI DJ service in 2025 to advance AI capabilities on its music platform, and has positioned expansion of in-vehicle music services with Hyundai Motor Group and improved B2B structure for telecom-partnered music services as key pillars for earnings recovery.

In its Q1 2026 earnings release, the company outlined further AI service enhancement, expansion of vehicle music services, and strengthened music distribution partnerships as forward direction.

On the cost side, the company is implementing an accounting policy change starting 2026 to smooth content costs—previously concentrated in Q4—across quarters, which could reduce seasonal earnings volatility. Subsidiary KT Millie's Library continues its own growth narrative by expanding into web novels and webtoons.

The 5-for-1 share consolidation resolved in July 2026 will see trading halted from October 2, take effect October 7, and see new shares relisted October 30, with the company describing the purpose as stabilizing share price and enhancing corporate value by maintaining an appropriate float.

The company has clarified that this consolidation is not a capital reduction aimed at decreasing paid-in capital.

With the large goodwill impairment recognized in 2025, the book value of goodwill related to CJ Digital Music has effectively been exhausted, and some assessments suggest the likelihood of further impairment has decreased from prior years.

Overall, the key watch points going forward are whether structural stagnation in the music business can ease and how much book content and new businesses can expand their profit contribution.

08

Bull factors

Growing Profit Contribution From the Book Content Subsidiary

As of Q1 2026, the book content business accounted for only 30.2% of revenue but 70.6% of operating profit, emerging as the core of the group's earnings structure. KT Millie's Library is expanding its content footprint through diversified B2C, B2B, and B2B2C channels and new web novel and webtoon businesses.

If subsidiary growth continues to offset stagnation in the core music business, this could contribute to overall earnings stability.

Improving Balance Sheet and Debt-Free Stance

The debt ratio has steadily fallen from 87.7% in 2022 to 55.7% in 2025, reflecting an improving capital structure. One IR research note stated that the company generates stable annual financial income based on abundant cash holdings and a debt-free structure.

This cash cushion could serve as a resource for future new business investment or the resumption of shareholder return policies.

Potential Volatility Reduction From Cost Recognition Policy Change

The accounting policy for content-related costs, previously concentrated in Q4, is being adjusted starting 2026 to smooth recognition across quarters. This could ease the pattern of sharp Q4 earnings declines driven by seasonality.

With the large 2025 goodwill impairment having exhausted the book value tied to CJ Digital Music, the risk of further impairment from the same source is assessed as lower than before.

Bear factors

Structural Continuation of Weakening Music Business Competitiveness

With the domestic entry of global platforms like YouTube Music and Spotify, KT Genie Music's monthly active user ranking has fallen to around fourth place. Music segment revenue has declined every year since 2022, and its share of total revenue continues to shrink.

If structural contraction in the core business persists, subsidiary growth alone may face limits in defending overall performance.

Recurring One-Off Losses and Earnings Volatility

The KRW 32.36bn net loss attributable to owners in Q4 2025 stemmed from a one-off goodwill impairment related to CJ Digital Music.

In Q2 2026, on a standalone basis, the company posted its first quarterly operating loss in 11 years since Q2 2015, due to bad-debt allowance recognition tied to concert business receivables. This recurring pattern of non-operating, one-off factors weighing on results makes quarterly performance difficult to predict.

Demotion From the Blue-Chip Tier and Small-Cap Liquidity Constraints

In May 2026, the company was demoted from KOSDAQ's blue-chip tier to the mid-cap tier due to failing to meet net income and market capitalization criteria over the trailing three years. This shows that recent earnings weakness and reduced capital soundness have affected exchange index eligibility requirements.

Combined with the October trading halt tied to the 5-for-1 share consolidation, small-cap-specific liquidity variability factors are present.

Risk factors

Business Competition Risk

Intensifying competition from overseas platforms such as YouTube Music and Spotify could continue eroding music business revenue and user base. Whether new B2B channels such as telecom partnerships and connected cars can offset this remains a key question. Further deterioration in the competitive landscape could weigh on overall revenue growth.

Further Impairment and Earnings Volatility Risk

While goodwill related to CJ Digital Music was fully impaired across 2024–2025, the possibility of further impairment on other intangible assets or subsidiary-related assets cannot be ruled out.

If segment-specific one-off costs such as bad-debt charges tied to the concert business recur, quarterly earnings volatility could persist. This volatility also warrants caution when reading annual performance trends.

Share Consolidation and Market Structure Risk

During the 5-for-1 share consolidation scheduled for October 2026, procedural events such as trading halts and cash settlement of fractional shares could create temporary liquidity constraints. The company has stated the schedule could change during discussions with relevant authorities and procedural processes.

Continued status in the mid-cap tier rather than the blue-chip tier could affect institutional flows or index-related capital movement.

12

Overall view

KT Genie Music is in a phase of earnings structure realignment between a maturing, increasingly competitive music business and a subsidiary book content business that continues to grow.

The consecutive net losses in 2024–2025 were driven primarily by a one-off factor—the large goodwill impairment related to CJ Digital Music—whose book value has now effectively been exhausted.

However, given the recurrence of a standalone operating loss and consolidated net loss attributable to owners in Q2 2026, the pattern of segment-specific one-off costs disrupting results, beyond just impairment, continues.

Declining debt ratios and a debt-free cash structure are positive signals for financial stability, but the direction of net income remains unstable, alternating between recovery and renewed deterioration.

The 5-for-1 share consolidation scheduled for October is a procedural event that changes the outstanding share count and per-share metric basis, and does not by itself alter business fundamentals.

Going forward, the key watch points are whether structural stagnation in the music business eases and whether the profit contribution from book content and new businesses continues to expand.
