02

Business structure

coocon is a business data platform company built on two pillars: financial data relay and payment infrastructure. The business is split into a Data segment and a Payment segment. Based on the third-quarter 2026 release, Data segment revenue was KRW 9.61 billion and Payment segment revenue was KRW 10.66 billion.

Revenue in the two segments rose 12.3% and 21.4% year on year, respectively. The Data segment grew on revenue from financial MyData construction projects and is widening its product lineup, including a market quote API and an insurance agent qualification verification API.

The Payment segment, which serves big tech companies and large electronic payment gateway (PG) operators, passed KRW 10 billion in quarterly revenue for the first time as transaction volumes rose and new clients were added.

The customer base is also expanding through real-name verified virtual accounts and RegTech packages such as eKYC and AML. Profitability differs by segment: in the first quarter of 2026 the Data segment operating margin was 32.5% and the Payment segment margin was 23.6%.

In the third quarter of 2026, Data segment operating profit fell 9.6% year on year to KRW 2.4 billion as a higher share of build-type projects changed the revenue mix, while Payment segment operating profit rose 26.7% to KRW 2.71 billion.

The company presents its fee-based relay structure and payment access points of 2 million QR merchants, 100,000 franchise outlets and 40,000 ATMs as strengths. Several operators compete in financial data and payment authentication, so market share cannot be calculated from segment disclosures alone.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 64.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 68.4 billion in 2023 (up 6.0% year on year) and KRW 73 billion in 2024 (up 6.8%), then fell 4.9% to KRW 69.5 billion in 2025. Within 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 20 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.6 billion in 2023 (down 17.2%), held flat at KRW 16.6 billion in 2024, and recovered to KRW 18.9 billion in 2025 (up 13.7%).

The operating margin declined from 31.0% in 2022 to 24.3% in 2023 and 22.7% in 2024 before rising to 27.2% in 2025, and the margin recovery in a year of falling revenue is the defining feature of this period.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders grew from KRW 3.65 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.17 billion in 2023 (up 96.4%), KRW 15.8 billion in 2024 (up 119.9%) and KRW 21.5 billion in 2025 (up 36.2%), increasing for three consecutive years from 2023.

That growth far outpaced operating profit movements, so the gap may stem from items outside operating profit, and its composition should be checked in the income statement and notes of the periodic report.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 17.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 18.1 billion in the fourth quarter (up 4.3% quarter on quarter), KRW 16 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 11.5%), KRW 18 billion in the second quarter (up 12.2%) and KRW 20.3 billion in the third quarter (up 12.9%), rising for two consecutive quarters from the second quarter of 2026.

The company attributed the first-quarter decline to lower transaction volumes after financial lending regulations. Operating profit ran between KRW 4.47 billion and KRW 5.11 billion across the last five quarters.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 580 million in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 782 million in the fourth quarter to KRW 7.43 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 13.2 billion in the second quarter, then swung to a net loss of KRW 896 million in the third quarter.

The sum for the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, is KRW 20.5 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 10.2 billion in 2022, KRW 29.6 billion in 2023, KRW 52.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 45 billion in 2025, exceeding profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 21.5 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

coocon's business sits across two downstream demand sources: financial data connectivity and electronic payment transaction volumes.

Data segment revenue moves with financial MyData construction projects and demand for financial APIs, and in the first quarter of 2026 the seasonal slow period and financial lending regulations were cited as headwinds.

The Payment segment is tied to transaction volumes at large tech companies and major PG operators, and in the third quarter of 2026 higher volumes and new clients drove revenue growth. Because the model is fee-based, changes in volume and policy flow directly into revenue, which is a defining feature of this industry.

The company's strategy assumes that wider adoption of structures in which AI agents call external data directly would expand demand for data APIs. In payments, stablecoin settlement is cited as a new source of demand, and the pace of commercialization depends on the timing of related regulatory work.

Competition overlaps among financial data relay and identity and payment authentication providers, with peers in the same IT and software sector such as NICE Information Service, Daou Technology and Ahnlab serving as reference points.

Comparing revenue scale or profitability with these companies in numbers requires reviewing each company's periodic reports.

06

Outlook

The company set a target of 10% annual revenue growth, and third-quarter 2026 company-wide revenue growth of 16.9% exceeded that level, according to the release.

From the fourth quarter the company plans to expand MCP-based data API products to about 100 and push full-scope MyData business in earnest, which would let AI agents call data directly.

In payments, it plans to broaden the reach of the KSQR mobile payment infrastructure and commercialize stablecoin payment, top-up and withdrawal services in line with related regulatory work.

In its first-quarter 2026 release in May, the company said it would expand collaboration with global payment providers such as Alipay, WeChat Pay and UnionPay and speed up commercialization of global stablecoin payments.

On November 6, 2026, it signed a business agreement with blockchain infrastructure firm DSRV covering on-chain financial data APIs and AI agent payments, and the two plan to jointly verify stablecoin settlement data and automatic API fee payment technology.

On November 9, 2026, news reported the launch of a card holder name verification service before payment. Management set a goal of making global payments a core strategic business within two to three years and transforming into an AI-based data company.

At the first-quarter release, a recovery after the second quarter was expected, and second-quarter revenue indeed rose 12.2% quarter on quarter to KRW 18 billion. The next checkpoints are how much revenue the new businesses show in segment disclosures and whether the Data segment operating margin recovers.

08

Bull factors

Payment volume growth

Payment segment revenue reached KRW 10.66 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 21.4% year on year, and quarterly revenue passed KRW 10 billion for the first time. The company attributed the increase to higher volumes at big tech companies and major PG operators plus new client wins.

The segment operating margin rose 1.0 percentage point to 25.4%, showing revenue growth and margin improvement together.

Data products and MyData

The Data segment recorded third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 9.61 billion, up 12.3% year on year, with financial MyData construction projects contributing. The company plans to expand MCP-based API products that AI agents can call directly to about 100 and to push MyData business across all sectors.

Demand for AI-related data could be added on top of existing financial data demand, which is cited as a supporting argument.

Profit recovery and cash generation

Annual operating profit rose 13.7% to KRW 18.9 billion in 2025, and the operating margin recovered from 22.7% in 2024 to 27.2%. Operating cash flow of KRW 45 billion in 2025 exceeded profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 21.5 billion.

Company-wide revenue growth of 16.9% in the third quarter of 2026 surpassed the 10% annual growth target the company had set.

09

Bear factors

Quarterly earnings volatility

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 896 million in the third quarter of 2026. Compared with KRW 7.43 billion in the first quarter and KRW 13.2 billion in the second quarter of the same year, the quarter-to-quarter swing is very large.

Operating profit stayed in the black at KRW 5.11 billion, so the effect of items outside operating profit needs to be confirmed.

Lower Data segment profit

Data segment operating profit fell 9.6% year on year to KRW 2.4 billion in the third quarter of 2026. The company attributed the decline to a change in revenue mix as build-type projects grew as a share of the business.

The segment margin fell from 32.5% in the first quarter to 25.0% in the third, suggesting that profitability could be diluted as build-type projects expand.

Rising leverage and regulation

The debt-to-equity ratio rose every year from 40.8% in 2022 to 83.9% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 143 billion at the end of 2025. In the first quarter of 2026, revenue fell 7.5% year on year as financial lending regulations reduced transaction volumes. Volumes can be affected by policy changes, which is a bearish factor.

Risk factors

Policy and regulation

Financial lending regulations and MyData-related rules could change transaction volumes and the scope of the business. In the first quarter of 2026 the company described a partial volume decline from financial lending regulations.

The timing of stablecoin payment commercialization depends on regulatory work, so external variables weigh heavily.

New business execution

MCP-based data APIs, stablecoin payments and global payment partnerships are areas whose revenue scale is not yet separately disclosed by segment. Partnerships with overseas payment providers such as Alipay and WeChat Pay and the planned joining of the Agentic AI Foundation are still closer to the planning stage. Early investment costs and delayed monetization could weigh on margins.

Financial and earnings volatility

If the debt-to-equity ratio keeps rising, changes in interest rates and borrowing terms could weigh on earnings. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders moves sharply from quarter to quarter, which lowers earnings predictability.

Total liabilities of KRW 143 billion against total equity of KRW 170.5 billion at the end of 2025 shape the financial room available.

Overall view

coocon's revenue fell in 2025, yet operating profit rose and profit attributable to controlling shareholders grew for three consecutive years to KRW 21.5 billion.

In 2026 the Payment segment passed KRW 10 billion in quarterly revenue for the first time, and third-quarter company-wide revenue growth of 16.9% exceeded the company's 10% annual target.

At the same time, the third quarter brought a net loss of KRW 896 million, making quarterly earnings volatility the most visible challenge. Build-type projects in the Data segment lifted revenue but reduced segment operating profit, and the debt-to-equity ratio climbed from 40.8% in 2022 to 83.9% in 2025.

MCP-based data APIs, stablecoin payments and global payment partnerships are due to ramp up from the fourth quarter, but their revenue contribution must be verified in periodic reports.

Bullish factors include payment volume growth, wider data product lines and cash generation, while bearish factors include earnings swings, financial regulation and leverage.

This report offers no investment opinion or target price, and the next checkpoints are the operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report and profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
