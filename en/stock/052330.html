02

Business structure

Kortek is an industrial monitor maker founded in 1987, with its head office and factory in Songdo, Incheon, and a production subsidiary, KORTEK VINA, in Vietnam. The business has two lines: gaming monitors that display game screens and handle touch input in casino slot machines, and other industrial monitors.

According to a securities analysis report from April 2024, gaming monitors made up 82.1% of 2023 revenue and other monitors 17.9%, with exports at 96.2% of total sales.

A company information service describes Kortek as the world's No. 1 by global share in casino gaming monitors for 23 consecutive years, a figure based on external tallies rather than company disclosure.

Its core TFT-LCD monitors saw higher demand in Q1 2026, which a company data service attributed to expanding global integrated resort investment and policy support in emerging casino markets. New content launches and new models tied to larger display sizes also lifted sales and profitability.

Other monitors fell sharply in 2023, which the same report cited as the main driver of that year's revenue decline. With exports dominating sales, quarterly results depend heavily on overseas casino investment flows and the timing of new model launches.

In Q3 2026, a customer settlement of KRW 20.56 billion linked to tariff refunds after a US Supreme Court IEEPA ruling was deducted from revenue.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 428.6 billion in 2022, the largest year of 2022 to 2025, and KRW 308 billion in 2024, the smallest. Revenue fell 23.7% to KRW 327.1 billion in 2023 and fell a further 5.8% to KRW 308 billion in 2024.

It rose 28.8% to KRW 396.8 billion in 2025, and the company partly attributed the increase to a 33.3% rise in gaming monitor sales.

Operating profit dropped 82.5% to KRW 4.8 billion in 2023, when the operating margin fell to 1.5%, then rose 391.0% to KRW 23.5 billion in 2024 and 60.5% to KRW 37.8 billion in 2025, a two-year rise after the 2023 low, with a 2025 operating margin of 9.5%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 28.2 billion in 2022, KRW 7.93 billion in 2023 (down 71.9%), KRW 27.3 billion in 2024 (up 244.2%) and KRW 28.3 billion in 2025 (up 3.8%).

Total net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 28.3 billion in 2025, essentially the same as the controlling-shareholder figure. Quarterly operating profit improved from KRW 8.24 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 16 billion in Q2 2026, when revenue reached KRW 129.6 billion.

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 15.6 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 15.7 billion in Q2 2026. Q3 2026 revenue fell to KRW 88.5 billion, down 31.8% from the prior quarter and 16.3% year on year, and operating profit fell 54.2% from the prior quarter to KRW 7.35 billion.

Controlling-shareholder net income turned into a loss of KRW 1.83 billion.

A provisional release dated November 9, 2026 showed a tariff-refund customer settlement of KRW 20.56 billion deducted from revenue and FX-related net losses of KRW 11.82 billion, made up of valuation losses of KRW 5.25 billion and FX translation losses of KRW 6.57 billion.

Because these are provisional figures, the company said they may change after external audit review. Operating cash flow went from KRW 93 billion in 2023 to KRW 8.58 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.95 billion in 2025, well below the 2025 operating profit of KRW 37.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Kortek's core end market is casino gaming equipment, and demand moves with global casino investment flows. A company information service cited expanding global integrated resort investment and policy support in emerging casino markets as demand drivers in its Q1 2026 commentary.

The same commentary said new models tied to new content launches and larger display sizes significantly improved sales and profitability. Its market position is presented through an external tally that ranks Kortek as the world's No. 1 by global share in gaming monitors for 23 consecutive years.

Because its product line is concentrated in casino gaming equipment, the investment cycle in that end market feeds directly into company demand. On FX, export sales at 96.2% of 2023 revenue mean currency moves flow directly into profit and loss, as the KRW 11.82 billion FX-related net loss in Q3 2026 shows.

On tariffs, refunds of previously paid duties are under way after a US Supreme Court IEEPA ruling, and a customer settlement was deducted from Q3 2026 revenue. Its scale and product mix differ from larger electronics and components makers in the same sector, so direct comparison with sector averages is limited.

Whether Q3 results carry this trend forward must be checked against Q4 figures. Quarterly operating profit fell from KRW 16 billion in Q2 2026 to KRW 7.35 billion in Q3, so whether the Q3 level is temporary or persistent is the key question. Bull factors

Profit recovery and margin improvement in 2025

Operating profit rose from KRW 4.8 billion in 2023 to KRW 37.8 billion in 2025, and the operating margin climbed from 1.5% to 9.5%. Operating profit was KRW 15.4 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 16 billion in Q2 2026. The company pointed to a 33.3% rise in gaming monitor sales as a driver of 2025 revenue growth.

Lower debt burden

The debt ratio fell from 38.1% in 2022 to 18.4% in 2025. Total liabilities stood at KRW 71.6 billion and total equity at KRW 388.3 billion at the end of 2025. Controlling-shareholder equity of KRW 368 billion makes up most of that equity base.

Earnings momentum in the first half of 2026

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 15.6 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 15.7 billion in Q2 2026. Cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 rose 17.8% year on year, according to the provisional release.

A company information service attributed first-half demand to policy support in emerging casino markets and new content launches.

Bear factors

Swing to a loss in Q3 2026

Q3 2026 revenue fell 31.8% from the prior quarter to KRW 88.5 billion, and controlling-shareholder net income turned into a loss of KRW 1.83 billion. Operating profit fell 54.2% from the prior quarter to KRW 7.35 billion. The quarter showed that quarterly results can shift sharply within one period.

Volatility from FX results and settlement items

FX-related net losses of KRW 11.82 billion, made up of valuation losses of KRW 5.25 billion and FX translation losses of KRW 6.57 billion, were recorded in Q3. A customer settlement of KRW 20.56 billion reduced revenue directly. Because the figures are provisional and subject to external audit review, they add uncertainty.

Weak cash flow and market concentration

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 93 billion in 2023 to KRW 3.95 billion in 2025. With most sales coming from gaming monitors and exports at 96.2% of revenue in 2023, demand exposure to casino investment cycles is high.

Risk factors

Tariffs and FX

Tariff refund procedures are under way after a US Supreme Court IEEPA ruling, and customer settlements have reduced quarterly revenue. High export exposure means currency moves show up directly in valuation and FX translation losses. Q3 2026 FX-related net losses were KRW 11.82 billion.

Concentration in casino end markets

Revenue is concentrated in casino gaming equipment, so a downturn in that end market would hit results hard. In 2023, a sharp drop in other monitor sales pulled down total revenue. Changes in emerging-market policy or the pace of integrated resort investment could shift demand direction.

Quarterly earnings and cash flow volatility

The operating margin recovered from 1.5% in 2023 to 9.5% in 2025, but quarterly operating profit fell from KRW 16 billion in Q2 2026 to KRW 7.35 billion in Q3. Operating cash flow of KRW 3.95 billion in 2025 shows a gap between profit and cash. If both persist, they could weigh on dividend and investment capacity.

Overall view

Kortek is an export-oriented company whose core product is casino gaming monitors, and it recovered from the 2023 low with 2025 operating profit of KRW 37.8 billion and a 9.5% operating margin.

Revenue and operating profit rose in Q1 and Q2 2026, but in Q3 2026 a tariff settlement of KRW 20.56 billion and FX-related net losses of KRW 11.82 billion together pushed controlling-shareholder net income into a loss of KRW 1.83 billion.

The debt ratio of 18.4% and total equity of KRW 388.3 billion at the end of 2025 point to a low debt burden. Operating cash flow of only KRW 3.95 billion in 2025 means whether profit has fully converted into cash needs further checking.

Bullish factors include expanding casino investment, new model launches and a low debt burden, while bearish factors include concentration in a single industry, quarterly volatility from FX and tariffs, and the Q3 2026 swing to a loss.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 provisional results and the 2026 annual business report, which will show whether the tariff settlement was one-off and whether operating cash flow improves.
