02

Business structure

HyosungONBCo is an organic fertilizer and growing-medium manufacturer founded in 1984, classified under chemicals and operating as a small-to-mid-sized company.

Its main products are mixed organic meal and mixed organic pellets, and a 2025 fiscal-year company summary attributed the decline in sales and profitability to weaker demand for these products together with higher raw material prices. Because of farming seasonality, sales concentrate in spring and autumn.

The company views the traditional organic fertilizer market as structurally shrinking due to rural depopulation and aging, and is building growbags and nutrient solutions for smart farms as a new growth engine.

Growbag sales rose about 32% from KRW 741 million in the 42nd fiscal year to KRW 978 million in the 43rd, and nutrient solution sales increased from KRW 147 million to KRW 207 million over the same period, according to a press report.

Growbags are produced at the company's Sri Lankan subsidiary, which handles raw material sourcing, production, and domestic sales. The company holds patents on a solid medium composition for hydroponics and on growbag and hydroponic methods.

On competition, the company faces intensifying rivalry and a rising number of small manufacturers, which makes it harder to secure market share.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 29.7 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 28.1 billion in the year ended June 2024 (down 5.4% year on year), KRW 24.4 billion in the year ended June 2025 (down 13.1%), and KRW 22 billion in the year ended June 2026 (down 9.9%).

Sales have declined for three straight years from the year ended June 2024 through the year ended June 2026, and the year ended June 2023 was the largest year in the period.

Operating profit rose from KRW 720 million in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 4.26 billion in the year ended June 2024 (up 491.7%), which marks the peak of the period.

It then fell to KRW 3.47 billion in the year ended June 2025 (down 18.5%) and KRW 2.42 billion in the year ended June 2026 (down 30.3%), a two-year decline. Operating margin moved from 2.4% in the year ended June 2023 to 15.2%, 14.2%, and 11.0% in the following three years.

A press report on the year ended June 2024 cited higher selling prices and lower selling and administrative expenses as the drivers of profit growth.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 1.27 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 4.49 billion in the year ended June 2024 (up 254.9%), then fell to KRW 2.81 billion in the year ended June 2025 (down 37.4%) and KRW 846 million in the year ended June 2026 (down 69.9%), a two-year decline.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 857 million in the year ended June 2026, a different figure from net income attributable to controlling shareholders.

For the year ended June 2025, the company attributed the drop in net income to the recognition of valuation losses on financial assets, according to a press report.

Operating cash flow was KRW 9.31 billion in the year ended June 2025 and KRW 6.37 billion in the year ended June 2026, both above operating profit in the same years. On a quarterly basis, Q1 2026 was the peak, with sales of KRW 10.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.22 billion (up 1,535.5% from the prior quarter).

Q2 2026 swung to an operating loss of KRW 726 million. Q3 2026 sales were KRW 3.94 billion, down 3.6% from the prior quarter and up 29.2% year on year, and operating profit turned to KRW 49.8 million.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 182 million, narrower than in Q2 but a swing into loss from a year earlier. The sum of net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters was KRW 656 million.

05

Industry analysis

The company's own assessment is that the traditional organic fertilizer market is structurally shrinking as rural population declines and farmers age. The shift to automated farming and smart farms is cited as the backdrop for demand for growbags and nutrient solutions.

On the input side, unstable supply and rising raw material prices were cited as causes of weaker results in 2025. On competition, rising rivalry and a growing number of small manufacturers make it harder to hold market share.

In an industry comparison of 121 chemical-sector stocks as of June 2026, the company's 11.00% operating margin was far above the sector average of 1.00% and ranked 19th. In the same comparison, its sales ranked 112th, placing it near the bottom, so the profile is a small company with comparatively high margins.

Because of seasonality, quarterly results swing sharply with spring and autumn selling periods. The quarterly record is still short, so the spring selling season is the next checkpoint for the cycle.

Outlook

Expansion of the smart farm business is the most concrete growth leg in the company's disclosures and reporting. A late-September 2026 press report said the growbag production base at the Sri Lankan subsidiary is operating and that expanding exports in Southeast Asia is a core growth leg.

Growbag and nutrient solution sales rose in the 43rd fiscal year while total sales fell over the same period, so the new business has not yet offset the decline in the core business. The traditional mixed organic meal and pellet business was reported to face weaker demand combined with higher raw material prices.

Q3 2026 sales rose 29.2% year on year, a sign of top-line recovery, but the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 182 million in the same quarter continued. Losses attributable to controlling shareholders occurred in two consecutive quarters, Q2 and Q3 2026.

The next checkpoint is whether spring 2027 selling-season sales can return to the KRW 10.6 billion level recorded in Q1 2026. Whether quarterly operating profit stays positive is also a point to watch. Annual results for the year ending June 2027 are due in the business report around late September 2027.

Bull factors

Growth in the smart farm leg

Growbag sales rose about 32% from KRW 741 million in the 42nd fiscal year to KRW 978 million in the 43rd, and nutrient solution sales also grew over the same period. Growth in the new business while core fertilizer sales shrink reads as an attempt to change the business mix. The Sri Lankan production base and the patents give the company a production foundation.

Balance sheet strength and cash generation

Total equity was KRW 80.2 billion and total liabilities were KRW 12.1 billion as of June 2026, a debt ratio of 15.1%. Operating cash flow was KRW 9.31 billion in the year ended June 2025 and KRW 6.37 billion in the year ended June 2026, both larger than operating profit in those years. A low debt load can act as a buffer during a period of shrinking sales.

Operating margin above the sector

The 11.0% operating margin for the year ended June 2026 was far above the 1.00% chemical sector average in an Alphasquare comparison as of June 2026, and ranked 19th in the sector.

Operating margin rose from 2.4% in the year ended June 2023 to 15.2% in the year ended June 2024, and stayed in double digits in the two years after that.

Shrinking profit and quarterly losses

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders appeared in two consecutive quarters, Q2 and Q3 2026.

Competition and cost pressure

Risk factors

Southeast Asia export shift

Southeast Asian export expansion has been presented in press coverage as a core growth leg of the smart farm business.

If growbag output from the Sri Lankan subsidiary converts into export sales more slowly than planned, the point at which new business sales offset the decline in core fertilizer could also be pushed back.

Whether export channels and buyers have been secured has not yet been confirmed in disclosed results, so the shift to overseas sales needs to be verified in upcoming periodic reports.

Reliance on overseas production

Growbag production depends on the Sri Lankan subsidiary, which handles everything from raw material sourcing to domestic sales. Any disruption at this overseas base would affect the entire smart farm business.

Expansion of Southeast Asian exports is presented as a growth leg in press coverage, and its contribution to sales will appear in the periodic reports.

Small size and liquidity

At a market capitalization of KRW 32 billion, the company is a small-cap stock, and a small trading base can amplify share price moves unrelated to earnings. When quarterly results swing between profit and loss, the share price reaction around report releases tends to be larger.

At annual sales of KRW 22 billion, a disruption in a single selling season has a large effect on full-year results.

Overall view

HyosungONBCo's core organic fertilizer business has shrunk since the year ended June 2024, and the company is moving to change its business mix toward growbags and nutrient solutions for smart farms.

Annual results show sales down for three straight years and both operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders down for two straight years, while the 11.0% operating margin for the year ended June 2026 is above the sector average.

Quarterly results have swung widely, with a peak in Q1 2026, an operating loss in Q2, and a return to operating profit in Q3.

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 49.8 million turned positive, but a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 182 million continued, so the durability of the earnings recovery is still at an early stage.

On the balance sheet, total equity of KRW 80.2 billion and a debt ratio of 15.1% indicate a comparatively low debt burden.

The bullish factors are smart farm sales growth, low debt, and an operating margin above the sector, while the bearish factors are falling core fertilizer sales, raw material and competitive pressure, and continued quarterly losses.

The next checkpoints are spring selling-season results in the quarterly report of mid-May 2027 and the annual operating margin in the business report around late September 2027.
