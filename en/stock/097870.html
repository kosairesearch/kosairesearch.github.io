02

Business structure

HyosungONBCo was founded in August 1984 and makes and sells organic fertilizers, presented as a fertilizer specialist with 43 years of operating history. Its main products are mixed oilcake and mixed organic fertilizer pellets.

Because of the farming calendar, sales concentrate in spring and autumn, and unstable raw material supply can weigh on results. A market information summary cites weaker demand for the main products and higher raw material prices as causes of the recent decline in sales and profitability.

Growing numbers of smaller competitors make it hard to secure market share. To offset this, the company is developing smart-farm grow bags and nutrient solutions as new growth engines.

Grow bag sales rose from KRW 741 million in the 42nd fiscal period to KRW 978 million in the 43rd, and nutrient solution sales rose from KRW 147 million to KRW 207 million.

Total company sales fell to KRW 22 billion in the June 2026 fiscal year, so smart-farm growth and a shrinking core business are moving in opposite directions. Production runs through a Sri Lankan subsidiary used as a grow bag plant, covering raw material sourcing, manufacturing and domestic sales.

The company also holds patents related to a solid medium composition for hydroponics. Segment revenue shares were not confirmed in the sources reviewed, so no segment figures are shown.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue continuing to fall, while operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked in the June 2024 fiscal year and declined after that.

Revenue went from KRW 29.7 billion in the June 2023 fiscal year to KRW 28.1 billion in June 2024 (down 5.4% year on year), KRW 24.4 billion in June 2025 (down 13.1%) and KRW 22 billion in June 2026 (down 9.9%).

Operating profit rose from KRW 720 million in the June 2023 fiscal year to KRW 4.26 billion in June 2024 (up 491.7%), then fell to KRW 3.47 billion in June 2025 (down 18.5%) and KRW 2.42 billion in June 2026 (down 30.3%), a two-year decline.

A report on the June 2024 fiscal year attributed the profit increase mainly to higher selling prices and lower selling and administrative expenses. The operating margin went from 2.4% in the June 2023 fiscal year to 15.2% in June 2024, then 14.2% in June 2025 and 11.0% in June 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.81 billion in June 2025 (down 37.4%) and KRW 846 million in June 2026 (down 69.9%). A report on the June 2025 decline linked the drop in net income to the recognition of losses on financial asset valuation.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 857 million in the June 2026 fiscal year. Quarterly results jumped in Q1 2026, when revenue reached KRW 10.6 billion and operating profit KRW 3.22 billion, a figure larger than the full-year June 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.42 billion.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 4.08 billion, down 61.6% from the prior quarter and up 36.7% year on year, and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 726 million. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (Q3 2025 to Q2 2026) totaled KRW 846 million.

The source of the Q1 2026 jump was not explained in the material reviewed. Operating cash flow was KRW 4.67 billion in the June 2023 fiscal year, KRW 1.19 billion in June 2024, KRW 9.31 billion in June 2025 and KRW 6.37 billion in June 2026, not moving in the same direction as profit.

05

Industry analysis

The company's own view is that the organic fertilizer market is structurally shrinking as rural population declines and farmers age. At the same time, farming is shifting toward smart farms, and the company sees grow bags and nutrient solutions as growth areas.

Intensifying competition and a growing number of smaller players that make market share hard to secure are weak points. Higher raw material prices have also pressed on profitability. Strong farming seasonality causes sharp quarter-to-quarter swings in sales.

In an information service ranking for the June 2026 fiscal year, HyosungONBCo's operating margin ranked 19th among 121 chemicals companies, while its sales ranked 112th. Average operating margin across the sector is low, so the ranking needs careful reading.

Its position is better read as whether it can sustain profitability in a fertilizer niche rather than as one of scale.

Bull factors

Smart-farm sales growth

Grow bag sales rose from KRW 741 million in the 42nd fiscal period to KRW 978 million in the 43rd, and nutrient solution sales also increased. A Sri Lankan subsidiary runs grow bag production and the company handles sourcing through domestic sales itself. As the core business shrinks, the new segment is changing the revenue mix.

Double-digit operating margin

The operating margin was 11.0% in the June 2026 fiscal year, well above the 2.4% recorded in the June 2023 fiscal year. For the June 2024 fiscal year, higher selling prices and lower selling and administrative expenses were cited as the drivers of the profit gain. An information service ranks its operating margin in the upper tier among chemicals companies.

Low leverage and cash generation

Total liabilities at the end of June 2026 were KRW 12.1 billion against total equity of KRW 80.2 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio was 15.1% in the June 2026 fiscal year, a low level. Operating cash flow of KRW 6.37 billion in that year was well above net income of KRW 857 million.

Bear factors

Three straight years of revenue decline

Revenue fell from KRW 29.7 billion in the June 2023 fiscal year to KRW 22 billion in June 2026 and has declined for three consecutive years since June 2024. The company itself sees the traditional fertilizer market as structurally shrinking with rural population decline and aging. How far new businesses can offset the core decline remains uncertain.

Quarterly swings and a return to loss

Q2 2026 swung to an operating loss of KRW 726 million, in sharp contrast with KRW 3.22 billion of operating profit in Q1 2026. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 69.9% to KRW 846 million in the June 2026 fiscal year. Over the last four quarters that figure totaled only KRW 846 million.

Raw material pressure and rising competition

The main products, mixed oilcake and mixed organic fertilizer pellets, faced weaker demand and higher raw material prices that pressured profitability. A growing number of smaller companies makes market share difficult to hold. Seasonal concentration of sales in spring and autumn widens the spread in quarterly results.

Risk factors

Raw materials and selling prices

Raw material cost increases and selling price adjustments can move the operating margin quickly. The profit gain for the June 2024 fiscal year was linked to higher selling prices. If price increases reduce demand, sales and profit could both be affected. Unstable raw material supply could also disrupt production.

Financial asset valuation gains and losses

The decline in net income for the June 2025 fiscal year was reported to reflect losses on financial asset valuation. Non-operating valuation gains and losses can move net income regardless of the core business. Both net income and net income attributable to controlling shareholders are affected. When operating profit and net income move in different directions, the two need to be read separately.

Small-cap liquidity and share price volatility

Market capitalization ranks near the bottom among listed chemicals companies, so share price moves can be larger depending on trading volume. Individual stock trading swings can widen regardless of overall market direction. This risk affects the share price separately from earnings. As an informational report, this document does not state a direction for the risk.

Overall view

HyosungONBCo is a fertilizer company founded in 1984, and its revenue fell to KRW 22 billion in the June 2026 fiscal year as the core market shrinks with rural population decline and aging.

Over the same period the operating margin held in double digits at 11.0%, and a debt-to-equity ratio of 15.1% with total equity of KRW 80.2 billion points to a low debt burden.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 846 million in the June 2026 fiscal year, and Q2 2026 turned to an operating loss of KRW 726 million. Q1 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.22 billion shows how volatile quarterly results can be.

Growth in smart-farm grow bags and nutrient solutions is both a bullish factor and an item still to be proven. Profit defense and financial capacity are the bullish points, while revenue contraction and quarterly swings are the bearish points, and both should be weighed equally.
