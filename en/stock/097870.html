02

Business structure

HyosungONBCo was founded in August 1984 and manufactures and sells organic fertilizers, with mixed organic meal and mixed organic pellets as its main products. Its revenue base remains traditional fertilizer, sold into agricultural production. Smart-farm products are taking shape as a second business line.

Sales of grow-bags for smart farms rose about 32%, from KRW 741 million in the 42nd term to KRW 978 million in the 43rd term, and sales of smart-farm nutrient solutions rose from KRW 147 million to KRW 207 million, according to a company official's remarks reported in late September 2026.

Total revenue over the same span fell from KRW 24.4 billion to KRW 22 billion, so the smart-farm products are growing fast from a small base. The company runs its Sri Lankan subsidiary as the grow-bag production base, handling sourcing, manufacturing and domestic sales itself.

The traditional fertilizer market is crowded, with rising competition and a growing number of smaller producers making it hard to hold market share. Demand follows an agricultural calendar concentrated in spring and autumn, and results can swing when raw material supply is unstable.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue went from KRW 29.7 billion in the June 2023 fiscal year to KRW 28.1 billion in the June 2024 fiscal year (down 5.4% year on year), KRW 24.4 billion in the June 2025 fiscal year (down 13.1%) and KRW 22 billion in the June 2026 fiscal year (down 9.9%), a three-year run of declines.

The June 2023 fiscal year was the largest revenue year in this span. Operating profit rose from KRW 720 million in the June 2023 fiscal year to KRW 4.26 billion in the June 2024 fiscal year (up 491.7%), a gain the company linked to higher selling prices and lower selling and administrative expenses.

The June 2024 fiscal year is the operating profit peak. Operating profit then fell to KRW 3.47 billion in the June 2025 fiscal year (down 18.5%) and KRW 2.42 billion in the June 2026 fiscal year (down 30.3%). The operating margin ran at 2.4%, 15.2%, 14.2% and 11.0% across the four fiscal years.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 4.49 billion in the June 2024 fiscal year (up 254.9%), then fell to KRW 2.81 billion in the June 2025 fiscal year (down 37.4%) and KRW 846 million in the June 2026 fiscal year (down 69.9%).

The company attributed the June 2025 drop in net income to recognized losses on financial assets. The wider swings in net income than in operating profit show that non-operating valuation items can weigh heavily on the bottom line.

Quarterly, operating profit went from KRW 249 million in the second quarter of 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 277 million in the third quarter, then back to KRW 197 million in the fourth quarter.

The first quarter of 2026 brought revenue of KRW 10.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.22 billion, up 1,535.5% from the prior quarter, a result that may partly reflect the spring-weighted sales calendar. The second quarter of 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 726 million on revenue of KRW 4.08 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 9.31 billion in the June 2025 fiscal year and KRW 6.37 billion in the June 2026 fiscal year, which stayed at a meaningful level even as profit declined.

Industry analysis

The organic fertilizer market faces structural pressure from a shrinking rural population and aging farmers. A company official has said the traditional organic fertilizer market is shrinking structurally, and the company is pitching the shift to smart farming as its response.

If farming moves toward smart-farm methods, demand for materials tied to growing equipment, such as grow-bags and nutrient solutions, could rise. Whether that demand can offset the decline in the core fertilizer market is not yet visible in the reported numbers.

On the competitive side, a growing number of smaller producers and intensifying rivalry are cited as the main pressure on profitability. Rising raw material prices are hard to pass through to selling prices, so cost stability largely determines margins.

The company said its nine-month 2025 results held up on lower costs from steadier raw material prices and better production efficiency.

Peers in the broader chemicals sector, such as LG Chem, Lotte Chemical and Hanwha Solutions, are built around petrochemicals and battery materials, with different demand cycles and raw material sensitivity.

HyosungONBCo is a small agricultural materials company, so its results follow farming seasons and farm-level demand more closely than sector averages.

Overall view

HyosungONBCo is building a second business line in smart-farm products inside a shrinking core market for organic fertilizer. On a reported basis, revenue has declined for three straight fiscal years, and operating profit has fallen for two straight years since the June 2024 fiscal year peak.

The operating margin has eased to 11.0%, but it remains well above 2.4% in the June 2023 fiscal year. The first-quarter 2026 profit and the second-quarter 2026 loss both occurred within one year, so quarterly results depend heavily on seasonality and one-off items.

On valuation, the share price sits well below net asset value, while the small base of recent earnings makes earnings-based multiples read high.

Strengths and weaknesses are both present. The next checkpoints are whether the loss streak ends in the third quarter of 2026 and how far smart-farm growth offsets the decline in traditional fertilizer during the spring season of 2027.
