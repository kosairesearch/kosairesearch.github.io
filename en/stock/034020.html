Report overview

Backlog Above KRW 26 Trillion, Earnings Recovery Still in Progress

02

Business structure

Doosan Enerbility is centered on its Enerbility segment, which builds power generation equipment including nuclear main components, turbines, gas turbines, small modular reactors (SMRs) and offshore wind equipment.

Consolidated results also include subsidiaries such as Doosan Bobcat, so group profit is affected by conditions in those businesses. New orders in the Enerbility segment reached KRW 14.73 trillion in 2025, more than double the prior year, according to press reports in early 2026.

New orders in the first half of 2026 rose 89.6% year on year to KRW 7.12 trillion, and the backlog at the end of the first half stood at KRW 26.35 trillion, according to The Tracker. Gas turbines have become a core order category as AI data center expansion lifts demand for power generation in North America.

Reports said the company won 10 gas turbine orders in the first quarter of 2026, including 7 for North American data centers. The same report noted that the company took 7 of the 18 large H-class gas turbines ordered in the United States this year.

In nuclear, the Team Korea consortium's Dukovany units 7 and 8 in the Czech Republic began to be recognized as orders, and Doosan is expected to supply main components, turbines and construction work. In SMRs, a 16-unit main equipment contract with X-energy of the United States was reported in February 2026.

Competition in gas turbines is concentrated among a few global leaders, and a Yuanta-linked analyst cited in May 2026 said the top three players' backlogs have been stuck for more than five years.

Hana Securities said in a May 2026 analysis that first-quarter results at consolidated subsidiaries improved and the Enerbility segment returned to profit.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 15.4 trillion in 2022 to KRW 17.6 trillion in 2023, up 14.1% year on year, fell 7.7% to KRW 16.2 trillion in 2024, and recovered to KRW 17.1 trillion in 2025, up 5.1%.

Operating profit was KRW 1.11 trillion in 2022, peaked at KRW 1.47 trillion in 2023 (up 32.7%), then declined 30.6% to KRW 1.02 trillion in 2024 and 25.0% to KRW 762.7 billion in 2025, a two-year decline.

Operating margin went from 7.2% in 2022 and 8.3% in 2023 to 6.3% in 2024 and 4.5% in 2025, so margins narrowed even as revenue grew.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a net loss of KRW 772.5 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 55.6 billion in 2023, rose to KRW 111.4 billion in 2024 (up 100.3%), and fell 23.9% to KRW 84.8 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, a different measure, was KRW 205.2 billion in 2025, and the gap between the two reflects the share held by non-controlling interests.

At the end of 2025, the debt-to-equity ratio was 129.1%, up from 125.7% in 2024, with total equity of KRW 12 trillion, of which KRW 7.79 trillion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities of KRW 15.5 trillion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 625.1 billion in 2022, KRW 2.07 trillion in 2023, KRW 242.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 751.8 billion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 137.1 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 212.1 billion in the fourth quarter (up 54.7% from the prior quarter), KRW 233.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 314.3 billion in the second quarter (up 34.6%), and KRW 261.2 billion in the third quarter (down 16.9% from the prior quarter but up 90.6% year on year).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a net loss of KRW 50.1 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to a profit of KRW 72.8 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 758 million in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 157.6 billion in the second quarter, and a net loss of KRW 13.7 billion in the third quarter, where the loss is continuing but narrower than a year earlier.

The sum over the last four quarters was KRW 217.5 billion. LS Securities said third-quarter operating profit beat consensus in its October 2026 report.

Because quarterly net income attributable to controlling shareholders swings sharply, the treatment of subsidiary results and non-controlling interests in the next annual report is the key point to check.

05

Industry analysis

The gas turbine market is in a phase where AI data center expansion and Korea's shift toward combined-cycle power plants are lifting demand at the same time.

IBK Investment & Securities said in a May 2026 analysis that demand for large gas turbines in the 380MW class is rising quickly with Korea's combined-cycle policy and expanding AI data center investment in North America.

In February 2026, market views pointed to annual orders of more than 12 units domestically and overseas, constrained by supply bottlenecks. A Yuanta-linked analyst said in May 2026 that Doosan Enerbility would benefit because the top three global players have backlogs stuck for more than five years.

In nuclear, large reactors take at least 10 years from licensing to completion, and many analysts expect procurement to pick up from 2026.

Daishin Securities said in February 2026 that overseas orders for AP1000 reactor vessels and steam generators in Poland, Natrium SMR equipment for TerraPower, and VOYGR SMR main equipment for NuScale were expected to accelerate.

SK Securities said in a February 2026 report that at least two new large reactors could be reflected in the 12th Basic Electricity Plan. iM Securities assessed in February 2026 that 2025 new orders were a record.

In cycle terms, the company sits in the phase where orders are converting into revenue and profit, and the speed of that conversion is a key variable in its competitive position. Few domestic companies supply both gas turbines and nuclear main equipment, which is cited as a background to the expansion in orders.

06

Outlook

The company's 2026 order target for the Enerbility segment is KRW 13.3 trillion. After first-half new orders of KRW 7.12 trillion, about KRW 6.2 trillion more would need to be secured in the second half.

The second-half plan presented includes KRW 2.7 trillion of large nuclear orders, KRW 1 trillion of SMR orders, KRW 100 billion of nuclear services, KRW 1.1 trillion of overseas combined-cycle EPC work, and KRW 1.2 trillion of renewables such as offshore wind.

The Czech new reactor project is cited as a main order source in nuclear. The company's expected 2026 Enerbility segment revenue was reported as KRW 8 trillion, and because the backlog exceeds three times that level, the period over which it converts into revenue could be long.

For SMRs, a dedicated factory was planned to start construction during 2026, according to February 2026 reports. For gas turbines, a medium-size 90MW-class model is planned for launch in 2027, according to IBK Investment & Securities' May 2026 report.

NH Investment & Securities said in its June 2026 report that meaningful news, including Korea's US investment announcements, was expected to continue after the US nuclear supply chain loan announcement.

LS Securities said in its October 2026 report that the order upcycle is likely to continue for some time given the projects awaiting orders. The next checkpoints are whether the second-half order target is reached by the end of December 2026 and how profit is split across segments in the 2026 annual report.

Bull factors

Backlog Expanded to the KRW 26 Trillion Range

The backlog rose to KRW 26.35 trillion at the end of the second quarter of 2026, from KRW 24.13 trillion at the end of the first quarter. First-half 2026 new orders rose 89.6% year on year to KRW 7.12 trillion.

Since the backlog exceeds three times the segment revenue the company expects, a large volume is available to convert into future revenue.

Gas Turbine Demand Structure

Demand for gas turbines in North America is rising as AI data centers expand. Reports said the company won 10 gas turbine orders in the first quarter of 2026, including 7 for North American data centers.

Winning 7 of the 18 large H-class turbines ordered in the United States this year points to a growing share in this business.

Nuclear and SMR Pipeline

Recognition of orders for the Dukovany units 7 and 8 in the Czech Republic has begun, and a 16-unit SMR main equipment contract with X-energy was reported in February 2026. The company's second-half 2026 plan includes KRW 2.7 trillion of large nuclear orders and KRW 1 trillion of SMR orders. If procurement in nuclear continues, it would underpin further order growth.

Bear factors

Declining Operating Margin

Operating margin fell from 8.3% in 2023 to 6.3% in 2024 and 4.5% in 2025. Operating profit declined for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025. Even though 2025 revenue rose 5.1% to KRW 17.1 trillion, operating profit fell to KRW 762.7 billion, showing that revenue growth did not translate into margin improvement.

Volatility in Quarterly Earnings

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 758 million in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 157.6 billion in the second and a net loss of KRW 13.7 billion in the third. Third-quarter operating profit of KRW 261.2 billion fell 16.9% from the prior quarter. Given these swings, the four-quarter total of KRW 217.5 billion may be read as an unstable earnings base.

Divergent Broker Target Prices

LS Securities said in an October 30, 2026 report that it set a target price of KRW 95,000, lowering it from KRW 120,000. NH Investment & Securities set KRW 145,000 in a June 30, 2026 report. The gap between the two targets points to different views among brokers on how quickly orders will convert into earnings.

10

Risk factors

Delay in Converting Orders to Revenue

Large nuclear and gas turbine projects take time from contract signing to revenue recognition. Even with a backlog in the KRW 26 trillion range, delays in design, manufacturing or delivery could slow the translation into quarterly profit.

Whether the operating profit decline in 2024 and 2025 is linked to this timing gap should be checked in the next annual report.

Consolidated Subsidiaries and Non-Controlling Interests

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders is the figure after subsidiary results and the share held by non-controlling interests.

The gap between 2025 net income of KRW 205.2 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 84.8 billion shows that the choice of measure changes the size of profit.

The third-quarter 2026 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 13.7 billion also came from this consolidated structure.

Policy and Procurement Timing

Contracts for the new Czech reactors and SMRs may shift in timing depending on policy decisions and customers' investment decisions. In analyst views in the first half of 2026, SMR equipment procurement was linked to whether customers sign power purchase agreements (PPAs). US policy and Korea-US cooperation schedules are also variables that affect when orders are secured.

12

Overall view

Doosan Enerbility is showing a clear expansion in orders. New orders of KRW 14.7 trillion in 2025 and KRW 7.1 trillion in the first half of 2026 pushed the backlog to KRW 26.4 trillion at the end of the second quarter.

In the reported results, operating profit peaked at KRW 1.47 trillion in 2023 and then declined for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025, while operating margin fell from 8.3% in 2023 to 4.5% in 2025.

Operating profit rose to KRW 314.3 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and then eased to KRW 261.2 billion in the third quarter, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from KRW 758 million in the first quarter to a net loss of KRW 13.7 billion in the third.

How quickly orders convert into revenue and profit is the central variable in reading this company's results. Target prices differ, with LS Securities at KRW 95,000 (October 30, 2026) and NH Investment & Securities at KRW 145,000 (June 30, 2026).

The current multiple is high relative to earnings over the last four quarters, so the pace of earnings recovery matters. The next checkpoints are whether the 2026 year-end order target is reached, the segment profit mix in the 2026 annual report, and first-quarter 2027 results.
