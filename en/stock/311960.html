Business structure

InterRoid is a software developer and supplier listed on KONEX, having been listed on November 29, 2022. Press coverage describes it as a voice call automation platform company. Its core offering appears to automate phone customer service across speech recognition, dialogue processing and speech synthesis.

Company profile material describes it as a developer of AI speech recognition and synthesis technology and also lists rapid molecular diagnostic kits among its main products, so the emphasis of its business description differs by source.

Demand for voice call automation is likely to come from financial, retail and public institutions that run call centers, and corporate customers seeking to cut agent costs and response times are the adoption driver.

Listed companies in the same IT and software sector include large platform operators such as NAVER, Kakao and NHN, and system integrators such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever.

Large platforms have deep AI capabilities and customer reach, so the scale gap is wide, while system integrators capture broad IT contracts from large clients, which keeps voice automation a niche area that is hard to compare directly with them.

InterRoid's competitiveness depends on specific customer deployments and platform expertise, but the 2023-onward periodic report results that would substantiate this have not yet been disclosed. The product mix, revenue split by segment and customer concentration are items to check in the next periodic report.

Earnings analysis

The only annual results disclosed in the periodic reports are for 2022. Revenue was KRW 7.28 billion, the operating loss was KRW 1.34 billion, and the operating margin was -18.4%.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 930 million, while the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 980 million. The two figures measure the same year's loss on different bases and should be read separately.

Because the operating loss of KRW 1.34 billion is larger than the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, non-operating items and taxes appear to have narrowed the loss somewhat.

Total equity at end-2022 was KRW 16.1 billion, of which KRW 16 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 10.8 billion. The debt ratio of 66.8% reflects liabilities that were smaller than total equity.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 1.01 billion, meaning cash left the business at the operating level. The operating loss and the negative operating cash flow moved in the same direction, so the earnings deficit also appeared in cash flow.

Because 2022 was the listing year, whether one-off listing-related costs were included should be checked in the detailed items of the periodic report. No annual results from 2023 onward have been disclosed in periodic reports, so the direction of change cannot be compared on disclosed figures.

The company said in a November 12, 2025 filing that a delayed disclosure of litigation filings led to a notice of a planned designation as an unfaithful-disclosure entity. The triggering event occurred on September 24, 2025, and the disclosure was made on October 13, 2025. The decision on designation was scheduled for December 3, 2025.

Industry analysis

Voice call automation applies artificial intelligence to answering corporate customer service calls, and demand centers on cutting agent costs and shortening response times.

As conversational AI based on large language models spreads, accuracy and the ability to control wrong answers have become key competitive factors. In this segment, large platform operators' in-house AI businesses compete with smaller specialist firms.

Large platforms hold advantages in data and infrastructure, while specialists typically differentiate by designing around the customer service workflows of particular industries.

Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, system integrators in the same sector, handle broad IT work for large clients, so their demand overlaps only limitedly with voice automation.

Small IT companies listed on KONEX have limited capital and trading liquidity, which narrows room for rights offerings or large investments. Neither the disclosures nor the press coverage provide a basis for quantifying the market size or InterRoid's share.

Speech recognition and synthesis technology could extend into areas beyond customer service, but that is an expectation about technical capability rather than something proven in reported results.

Compared with listed peers, InterRoid's disclosed annual results remain at 2022, which makes the gap in business maturity hard to express in numbers.
