KG Dongbusteel: Sales Recovery Diverges From Profit Decline

Quarterly revenue has risen for three straight quarters in 2026, yet third-quarter operating profit fell by more than half from a year earlier, so whether top-line growth is turning into margin recovery is the key question.

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

KG Dongbusteel produces and sells cold-rolled steel and various surface-treated steel sheets as its main products, according to the business overview in the semi-annual DART report as of June 2026. Its consolidated subsidiaries engage in port operations, property leasing, and the manufacture and sale of metal oxides.

The core business is premium colour steel, produced at two plants in Dangjin and Incheon. According to company materials, a two-line expansion at Dangjin required investment of about KRW 95.5 billion, and including Incheon output the company says it can produce roughly 800,000 tons of colour steel a year.

A steel trade publication reported that 2024 colour steel sales reached a record 625,000 tons, with exports of 350,000 tons and the highest export share on record.

In tin plate, the company says it holds the leading position in domestic and overseas markets, and a surface treatment facility completed at Dangjin in May 2024 allows output of tin plate base material to rise by more than 50,000 tons a year.

At Incheon, the company rationalised its galvanised line by shifting products toward galvalume steel, which has higher corrosion resistance. Main demand comes from building materials such as sandwich panels, and overseas the company has supplied colour steel to insulated panel maker Kingspan since 2008.

The ternary-alloy colour steel KS certification announced in September 2026 covers both Dangjin and Incheon, so one plant can supply certified product when the other undergoes maintenance or schedule changes. In colour steel, KG Dongbusteel is compared with two other major producers as one of three leading companies.

In building exteriors, the company has expanded its range through use at the Ulsan Gotjeolgot visitor centre and a fire-resistant three-coat PCM colour steel for public facilities such as subway stations.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results have declined for three consecutive years since 2023. Revenue fell from KRW 3.82 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.43 trillion in 2023 (down 10.2% year on year), KRW 3.3 trillion in 2024 (down 3.8%), and KRW 3.19 trillion in 2025 (down 3.3%).

Operating profit dropped from KRW 340.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 280.4 billion in 2023 (down 17.6%), KRW 206 billion in 2024 (down 26.5%), and KRW 150.7 billion in 2025 (down 26.8%).

In 2024 and 2025 revenue fell only in the low single digits, while operating profit fell in the 20 percent range, which points to margin pressure. The operating margin declined from 8.9% in 2022 to 8.2% in 2023, 6.2% in 2024, and 4.7% in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent fell from KRW 536 billion in 2022 to KRW 234.4 billion in 2023 (down 56.3%) and KRW 133.4 billion in 2024 (down 43.1%), then edged up 1.3% to KRW 135.1 billion in 2025.

The 2023 drop in parent-attributable profit was far steeper than the 17.6% fall in operating profit, which suggests non-operating items and taxes played a large role. The debt-to-equity ratio improved from 80.5% in 2022 to 50.9% in 2025.

Operating cash flow, however, declined from KRW 478.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 184 billion in 2025. Quarterly results turned at the fourth quarter of 2025.

Revenue fell from KRW 830.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 745.2 billion in the fourth quarter (down 10.3% from the prior quarter), then rose to KRW 805.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 896.4 billion in the second, and KRW 1 trillion in the third, marking three consecutive quarterly increases.

Operating profit moved from KRW 53.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 5.18 billion in the fourth quarter, which was the switch to a loss.

It returned to a profit of KRW 21.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026, rose to KRW 43.1 billion in the second quarter (up 100.4% from the prior quarter), and then fell to KRW 24.8 billion in the third quarter (down 42.4% from the prior quarter and down 53.9% year on year).

Revenue grew in the third quarter, yet operating profit declined, so higher sales did not translate into profit.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent reached KRW 93.4 billion in the second quarter, more than double that quarter's operating profit of KRW 43.1 billion, and was KRW 23.1 billion in the third quarter (up 54.2% year on year).

The sum of the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, is KRW 193.8 billion, which exceeds the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 135.1 billion and shows how much quarterly earnings swing from non-operating items.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic steel demand remains weak. In its first-quarter 2026 results announcement, the company cited a slump in domestic construction and a deteriorating global steel market as a difficult external environment.

Demand for building colour steel is closely tied to construction activity, so starts and construction investment feed directly into sales volumes. In early 2025, the company judged that trade conditions would worsen amid the possibility of a 25% blanket US tariff.

It said US-bound exports make up roughly 10 to 15% of export volume and are covered by annual contracts, so volume changes would be limited.

Around the same period, a domestic anti-dumping investigation into hot-rolled steel was advancing, and the company voiced concern that POSCO and Hyundai Steel Company could gain a dominant position. The company also expected raw material and selling prices to stay weak amid demand contraction.

All three colour steel producers reported higher third-quarter 2026 revenue from a year earlier, but profitability worsened. POSCO Steeleon swung from profit in the prior quarter to a loss, while KG Dongbusteel's operating profit fell by more than half year on year, according to a steel trade publication.

The same publication noted that sales growth has not translated into higher profit, making high value-added products and market-by-market profitability management more important.

On the competitive side, the company points to its leading position in tin plate, a rising export share in colour steel, and KS certification across all ternary-alloy colour steel lines as points of differentiation.

Whether these advantages are large enough to offset weak demand is still something to confirm through quarterly operating margins.

06

Outlook

In its first-quarter 2026 results announcement, the company said the benefits of its production efficiency improvements would begin to show in its indicators from the second quarter. Second-quarter operating profit came in at KRW 43.1 billion, up 100.4% from the prior quarter.

In the same announcement, the company said line efficiency gains and product mix optimisation were expected to produce a payback period of about 1.8 years and annual profit improvement of roughly KRW 1.8 billion.

Among the new products developed in 2026, the DART semi-annual report lists trivalent chromium post-treatment material for European and North American markets, a PCM product for livestock buildings in Ireland, and a PCM product for Samsung washing machines.

After the KS certification, the company identified battery and chemical plants, modular buildings, renewable energy equipment, and railway and transport facilities as priority markets. These segments value corrosion resistance, product life, and lower maintenance costs.

In overseas sales, new pattern approvals and a rising export share continue, and the third-quarter 2026 export share was reported as the highest on record. On shareholder returns, the company set a medium-term target of a 30% payout ratio when it announced its plan earlier.

The next checkpoint is whether the fourth quarter of 2026 preliminary results keep operating profit in positive territory. After that, the 2026 annual report will show whether the full-year operating margin exceeds the 4.7% recorded in 2025.

The pace of recovery in domestic construction activity remains an external variable that will determine demand for building colour steel.

Bull factors

Differentiation through higher-value colour steel

All colour steel lines at Dangjin and Incheon obtained KS D 3034 certification, establishing quality control from raw material to finished product.

The company said the certification will help it differentiate from low-priced imports and target corrosion-sensitive markets such as battery and chemical plants and railway facilities. A steel trade publication reported that the third-quarter 2026 export share was the highest on record.

Three straight quarters of revenue growth and a return to profit

Quarterly revenue rose in each of the first three quarters of 2026, reaching KRW 805.3 billion, KRW 896.4 billion, and KRW 1 trillion.

Operating profit swung from a loss of KRW 5.18 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to a profit of KRW 21.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then rose to KRW 43.1 billion in the second quarter. The sum of profit attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters is KRW 193.8 billion.

Low market valuation against book value and improved balance sheet

The price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x, so the market values the company low relative to its book equity. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 80.5% in 2022 to 50.9% in 2025, and total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 1.05 trillion. The company has set a medium-term target of a 30% shareholder payout ratio.

Risk factors

New market entry risk

Following the KS certification, the company identified battery and chemical plants, modular buildings, renewable energy equipment, and railway and transport facilities as priority markets.

These segments value corrosion resistance and lower maintenance costs, which fit the certified product, but they require new customer approvals. If entry into these markets is delayed, sales may remain concentrated in existing building materials.

The pace at which new markets contribute to revenue will determine how much the product mix improvement can deliver.

Trade and tariff risk

In early 2025, the possibility of a 25% blanket US tariff was raised, and US-bound exports account for about 10 to 15% of export volume. Changes in import and trade policy, including the domestic hot-rolled anti-dumping investigation, could affect prices and market share. The company said US-bound volumes are covered by annual contracts, limiting fluctuations.

Earnings mix volatility risk

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 93.4 billion in the second quarter of 2026, more than double operating profit of KRW 43.1 billion. It then fell to KRW 23.1 billion in the third quarter.

This indicates that non-operating items varied greatly from quarter to quarter, so the durability of net profit should be assessed separately from operating profit trends.

12

Overall view

KG Dongbusteel's 2026 results show sales and profit moving in different directions. Quarterly revenue rose in each of the first three quarters of 2026, and third-quarter revenue reached KRW 1 trillion, up 20.5% year on year.

Third-quarter operating profit, however, fell 53.9% to KRW 24.8 billion, and nine-month operating profit of KRW 89.5 billion was 42.6% below the prior-year nine-month figure.

Over the same period, nine-month profit attributable to owners of the parent rose to KRW 161.7 billion, so non-operating items contributed substantially.

Positive factors include KS certification across all ternary-alloy colour steel lines at Dangjin and Incheon, a record-high export share, and a debt-to-equity ratio that fell to 50.9%.

Negative factors include the operating margin decline from 8.9% in 2022 to 4.7% in 2025, weak domestic construction and steel demand, and a margin structure in which higher sales have not lifted profit.

A price-to-book ratio well below 1x shows that the market values the company low against its book equity, but this alone does not establish the direction of the share price. The next checkpoints are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results, the 2026 annual report, and first-quarter 2027 results.
