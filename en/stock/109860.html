Dongil Metal: Quarterly Profit Recovery Alongside Softer Sales

Dongil Metal recorded operating profit in each quarter from the first quarter of 2026 through the third, but sales and profit both fell from the prior quarter in the third quarter.

  2. 2

02

Business structure

Dongil Metal is a KOSDAQ-listed company in the steel and metals sector. Its results are disclosed on a consolidated basis that includes subsidiaries, with total assets of KRW 1.809 billion, total equity of KRW 156.1 billion and total liabilities of KRW 24.7 billion at the end of 2025.

According to fnguide’s financial summary, standalone sales in 2025 were KRW 614 billion, below consolidated sales of KRW 91.7 billion, so the gap appears to come from revenue at consolidated subsidiaries.

Consolidated sales fell from KRW 111.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 86 billion in 2024 before rising 6.7% to KRW 91.7 billion in 2025, which shows a revenue base that moves with market conditions.

In its summary of the first quarter of 2026, fnguide cited cost competitiveness and business diversification as drivers of improved results. Sales mix by segment is found in the business-segment disclosures of the annual report.

Companies such as Korea Zinc, Hyundai Steel Company, Poongsan and SeAH Steel are classified in the same sector, but their main products and scale differ. The balance sheet is conservative: the debt ratio was 15.4% in 2022, 7.6% in 2023, 13.8% in 2024 and 15.8% in 2025. Operating cash flow declined from KRW 15.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.07 billion in 2025.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales were KRW 111.4 billion in 2022, KRW 110.4 billion in 2023 (down 0.9% year on year), KRW 86 billion in 2024 (down 22.1%) and KRW 91.7 billion in 2025 (up 6.7%), with 2022 the largest year and 2024 the smallest in the period.

Operating profit fell from KRW 13.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.4 billion in 2023 (down 11.2%) and then to KRW 618 million in 2024 (down 95.0%) before recovering to KRW 1.43 billion in 2025 (up 131.8%).

The operating margin was 12.5% in 2022, 11.2% in 2023, 0.7% in 2024 and 1.6% in 2025, so the margin was hit hardest in 2024 when sales declined.

In 2024, net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 7.25 billion (up 2,173.1%) despite operating profit of only KRW 618 million, which suggests non-operating or one-off items weighed heavily on the result.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 14 billion in 2022, KRW 319 million in 2023 (down 97.7%), KRW 7.25 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.05 billion in 2025 (down 30.3%), with 2023 the smallest year. Net income for the same years followed the same pattern.

Quarterly results started with sales of KRW 22.6 billion, an operating loss of KRW 225 million and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 441 million in the third quarter of 2025.

The fourth quarter of 2025 posted sales of KRW 23.2 billion (up 2.5% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 272 million, a swing to profit, while controlling-shareholder net income fell to KRW 93.67 million (down 78.8%).

In the first quarter of 2026, sales were KRW 28.6 billion (up 23.4%), operating profit KRW 1.55 billion (up 468.2%) and controlling-shareholder net income KRW 1.38 billion (up 1,375.7%).

In the second quarter of 2026, sales were KRW 30.3 billion (up 6.0%), operating profit KRW 2.36 billion (up 52.8%) and controlling-shareholder net income KRW 2.4 billion (up 73.9%), the largest operating profit among the last five quarters.

In the third quarter of 2026, sales were KRW 28.9 billion (down 4.7% from the prior quarter and up 27.8% year on year), operating profit KRW 1.06 billion (down 55.2% from the prior quarter, a swing to profit from a year earlier) and controlling-shareholder net income KRW 875 million (down 63.6% from the prior quarter, up 98.3% year on year).

Combined operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 exceeded the full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 1.43 billion. Controlling-shareholder net income over the most recent four quarters totaled KRW 4.76 billion, and operating cash flow was KRW 6.07 billion in 2025, down from KRW 15.2 billion in 2022.

Industry analysis

The steel and metals sector is cyclical, with results driven by raw material prices and capital spending in downstream industries. Dongil Metal’s consolidated sales fell from KRW 111.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 86 billion in 2024 under a sector downturn, then recovered modestly to KRW 91.7 billion in 2025.

In 2026, sales were KRW 28.6 billion in the first quarter, KRW 30.3 billion in the second and KRW 28.9 billion in the third, all above KRW 28 billion. Profit has moved from an operating loss in the third quarter of 2025 to operating profit in each quarter of 2026.

Korea Zinc, Hyundai Steel Company, Poongsan, SeAH Steel and KG Dongbusteel are listed in the same sector.

Dongil Metal’s disclosed sales are small next to Hyundai Steel Company and Korea Zinc, so it does not compete with them on scale. fnguide cited cost competitiveness as a driver of first-quarter 2026 results, and whether this shows up in quarterly margins will be visible in future operating margins.

The third-quarter 2026 sales decline from the prior quarter may signal changes in market conditions or product mix, but the disclosed figures alone do not pin down the cause. Sales were up 27.8% from a year earlier, so the revenue base is larger than in 2025.

On disclosed figures, the company’s cycle reads as a peak in 2022, a trough in 2024, a sales rebound in 2025 and a profit recovery in 2026.
