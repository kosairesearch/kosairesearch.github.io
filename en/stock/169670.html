HBM Equipment Qualification Amid Losses

Kostek Systems swung to an operating loss in 2025, and the outcome of mass-production qualification for its HBM temporary bonding and debonding equipment remains the key variable.

02

Business structure

Kostek Systems was founded on April 11, 2000, makes semiconductor and display manufacturing equipment, and is headquartered in Pyeongtaek, Gyeonggi Province. It listed on the Konex market on January 21, 2022.

Its core product is wafer transfer equipment, and it makes EFEM, vacuum cluster systems and LPM units for semiconductor fabrication lines. The company says it was the first in Korea to localize wafer transfer systems and sells them to Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and others.

In early 2024 coverage, CEO Bae Jun-ho said wafer transfer equipment made up roughly 80% of sales. The new growth leg is temporary wafer bonding systems used in HBM TSV processes and advanced packaging such as FOWLP and FOPLP.

In addition, it develops and sells sinter bonders for power semiconductors and transfer and bonding equipment for micro LED displays. In 2024 reporting, customers named included Samsung Electronics, Wonik IPS, Eugene Technology and others.

With a market capitalization of KRW 16.0 billion, the company is far smaller than listed equipment peers such as HANMI Semiconductor and Wonik IPS, and temporary bonding and debonding is an area that has relied on imported equipment, making localization the objective.

03

Recent trends

For fiscal 2025 on a standalone basis, Kostek Systems reported revenue of KRW 19.234 billion, an operating loss of KRW 742 million and a net loss of KRW 2.136 billion. Revenue fell 19.38% from the prior year.

In 2024, it posted revenue of KRW 23.858 billion and net income of KRW 517 million, and operating profit was KRW 84.87 million. In 2025, non-operating expenses of about KRW 1.3 billion and income tax expense of about KRW 740 million made the net loss larger than the operating loss.

At the end of 2025, total assets were KRW 36.359 billion, total liabilities KRW 24.455 billion and total equity KRW 11.904 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio was 205.43%, up from 176.99% at the end of 2024, and operating cash flow was negative. Loss per share was KRW 763.

On October 27, 2026, the stock closed at KRW 5,590, up 3.71% from the previous session, for a market capitalization of KRW 16.0 billion. As of October 1, 2026, the 52-week range was KRW 2,660 to KRW 7,460, and the low of KRW 2,660 was recorded on June 30, 2026.

According to an investing-data site, the three-month average volume was only about 1,370 shares, so liquidity is very low. No confirmed filings on results after the first half of 2026 were found, so they are not covered here.

04

Outlook

The key variable is the outcome of Samsung Electronics' mass-production evaluation of the company's HBM TBDB and peel-off tools. The ELEC reported that the company plans to begin supplying TBDB equipment after passing Samsung Electronics' quality tests.

A company official said it would first prove reliability through an IDM's mass-production verification and then supply outsourced assembly and test providers. Candidate follow-on customers include outsourced assembly and test firms such as Amkor, Nepes and LB Semicon.

The company is also improving and developing an HBM laser debonding tool that links with the peel-off equipment. On power-semiconductor tools, early-2024 remarks said orders for carrier-wafer bonding and debonding equipment for SiC and GaN power chips were within sight, yet 2025 revenue declined.

FnGuide summarizes that growth in AI semiconductors and micro LED displays is expected to lift demand for related equipment. However, the FnGuide consensus fields for 2025 to 2027 are blank, so no verifiable brokerage earnings forecast exists.

As for a KOSDAQ transfer, a plan to pursue a listing in 2026 was confirmed at a November 2024 investor briefing, but as of October 27, 2026 the shares remain on the Konex market.

05

Bull factors

Localization opening in HBM back-end tools

Temporary bonding and debonding is reported to be a field with heavy reliance on imported tools. According to The ELEC, Samsung Electronics' mass-production evaluation is in its final stage, and TBDB supply would begin upon passing.

The ability to offer laser debonding together with peel-off equipment is a plus for the product mix. Whether the evaluation result translates into orders must be confirmed through future filings.

Track record with major customers and recognition

The company says it was the first to localize wafer transfer systems in Korea and has supplied Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. It was designated a materials, parts and equipment champion company in 2023. Experience passing large manufacturers' qualification can serve as a reference in evaluating new products.

Product diversification and market cap versus book equity

Sinter bonders for power semiconductors and transfer and bonding tools for micro LED widen the product range and could reduce reliance on a single process. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 11.904 billion against a market capitalization of KRW 16.0 billion.

In operating terms, there is room for improvement in utilization and operating margin if new-product sales materialize.

06

Bear factors

Falling revenue and a swing to losses

Revenue in 2025 was down 19.38% from the prior year. The company recorded an operating loss of KRW 742 million, reversing from an operating profit of KRW 84.87 million in 2024. The net loss of KRW 2.136 billion was larger than the operating loss. The fact that new products have not yet translated into improved results is a drag.

Weaker balance sheet

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 176.99% at the end of 2024 to 205.43% at the end of 2025. Total equity declined from the KRW 14 billion range to the KRW 11 billion range. Operating cash flow was negative and cash also flowed out through investing activities. For a small company, this structure can increase the burden of borrowings.

Thin liquidity and price volatility

As a Konex micro-cap, its three-month average volume is about 1,370 shares per an investing-data site. The 52-week price range is wide, at KRW 2,660 to KRW 7,460. Prices can move sharply on small trades, which can raise entry and exit costs.

Risk factors

Macro

Equipment order timing can shift with the capital-spending schedules of customers such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. Changes in the memory cycle and in the pace of HBM investment would also affect demand for new tools. No verified figures exist on export share or currency effects, so these can only be assessed qualitatively.

Business

If mass-production evaluation is delayed or falls short of standards, revenue recognition from the TBDB business could be pushed back. Overseas equipment makers have an established position, so competition on price and reliability is unavoidable.

While legacy revenue centered on wafer transfer equipment has declined, a rising share from new products has not yet been confirmed.

Financial and Market

With the debt ratio above 200% and operating cash flow negative, financing conditions matter. A KOSDAQ transfer plan was mentioned in 2024 but has not been completed.

Bae Jun-ho was re-elected as an inside director at the March shareholders' meeting, yet the company website lists Bae Geon-woo as CEO, so changes in the management lineup should be verified through filings.

08

Overall view

Kostek Systems is a Konex-listed equipment maker that has dealt with major semiconductor manufacturers through its wafer transfer equipment. In 2025, revenue declined, the company recorded an operating loss and a net loss, and its debt ratio rose.

On the other hand, its HBM TBDB and peel-off tools are reported to be in the final stage of Samsung Electronics' mass-production evaluation, which puts its business transition to the test. Whether passing the evaluation leads to orders and revenue has not yet been confirmed in filings.

Results after the first half of 2026 and brokerage estimates were not found, so available information is limited. As a micro-cap, trading volume is thin and prices can swing widely, which is also worth considering.

Any judgment is left to the reader after reviewing future order disclosures, quarterly results and changes in financial indicators.
