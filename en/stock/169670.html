Business structure

Kostek Systems, Inc. was founded in 2000 and is headquartered in Pyeongtaek, South Korea, where it makes equipment for semiconductor and display manufacturing. Its core lineup covers temporary wafer bonding and de-bonding systems, plus chip transfer and chip bonding equipment.

Wafer bonding attaches thin wafers to carriers for processing and then separates them, a step used before back-end work. The semiconductor line also includes plasma processing, light etching, encapsulation, and vacuum cluster tools.

Its applications extend into micro LED displays, with wafer bonders, thermal and laser eutectic systems, AR/VR laser eutectic systems, aligners, and pre-bonders.

On the display side, it supplies micro OLEDoS process equipment such as OCA and OCR bonding systems, polarizer lamination, and autoclave systems, along with EFEM products.

The business splits into two axes, semiconductor process equipment and display equipment, and its end markets span semiconductor packaging and displays. Peers for comparison include HANMI Semiconductor, EO Technics, and TSE among other listed back-end and packaging equipment makers. Kostek Systems has been listed on KONEX since January 21, 2022.
