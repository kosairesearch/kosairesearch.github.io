Wiper Profits Hold as Quarterly Sales Decline

02

Business structure

CAP was founded in February 2010 to manufacture and sell electronic products and components, and listed on KOSDAQ in March 2020. Its business has two segments: the SMT segment, which uses surface mount technology to make FPCAs for smartphone OLED panels, and the wiper segment, which makes automotive wiper blades.

According to StockAnalysis aggregated data, 2025 annual revenue of the cell phone parts (SMT) segment was about KRW 286.5 billion and that of automotive parts (wiper) about KRW 143.6 billion, totaling about KRW 430.1 billion before intersegment adjustments.

On that pre-adjustment total, wipers account for roughly 33%, and consolidated revenue after adjustment was KRW 295.2 billion. The SMT segment's results improved on expanded production of modules for Samsung Display and the Galaxy A series.

However, intensifying smartphone competition and shifting downstream demand have left its profitability limited. The wiper business focuses on the aftermarket, producing conventional, flat and hybrid types, and supplies ITW's Rain-X brand, a National Brand in North America.

In Europe, CAP began supplying flat and rear wipers to Swedish distributor Biltema in June 2026 and signed a supply contract with Halfords, the largest automotive and bicycle products distributor in the UK and Ireland, with estimated annual sales of about KRW 1.6 billion.

The company serves more than 200 customers in over 40 countries and has overseas subsidiaries in China, Vietnam, the United States and Japan. The two segments face different demand drivers, so weakness in one can partly offset the other.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 316.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 235.7 billion in 2023 (down 25.5% year on year), the smallest year in the period, then rose to KRW 314.9 billion in 2024 (up 33.6%) and fell to KRW 295.2 billion in 2025 (down 6.2%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for revenue.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.55 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.92 billion in 2023 (up 280.8%), KRW 20 billion in 2024 (up 237.2%) and KRW 24.3 billion in 2025 (up 21.6%), rising three years in a row from 2023 through 2025.

Over the same period the operating margin moved from 0.5% to 2.5%, 6.3% and 8.2%, so in 2025 the margin was higher than in 2024 even though revenue fell 6.2%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss of KRW 1.43 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 3.3 billion in 2023, a turn to profitability, then rose to KRW 19.1 billion in 2024 (up 477.3%) and KRW 19.5 billion in 2025 (up 2.0%), growing for two years in a row from 2024 to 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 19.5 billion in 2025, the same figure. The balance sheet strengthened: the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 124.3% in 2022 to 68.1% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 131.6 billion and total liabilities KRW 89.7 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 10.6 billion in 2022, KRW 8.89 billion in 2023, KRW 26.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.3 billion in 2025, and did not move in step with operating profit. The quarterly trend has turned.

The fourth quarter of 2025 marked the peak, with revenue of KRW 82.6 billion (up 26.5% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 8.39 billion (up 96.0%). Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 49 billion (down 25.0% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 2.71 billion (down 36.6%).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 6.62 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then fell to KRW 3.39 billion in the second quarter and KRW 32.54 million in the third quarter, down 99.3% year on year.

Over the most recent four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 16.2 billion. The size of one-off items cannot be separated from quarterly disclosures alone and should be traced in the notes to the annual report.

05

Industry analysis

Although CAP is classified under semiconductors, its actual demand comes from smartphone OLED modules and the automotive aftermarket. Semiconductor industry indicators are broadly strong.

According to the Semiconductor Industry Association, global semiconductor sales in February 2026 were USD 88.8 billion, up 61.8% from a year earlier. Korea's semiconductor exports in September 2026 reached USD 60.3 billion, up 262.8% from the same month a year earlier.

However, these indicators reflect a cycle led by memory and AI servers, which does not directly map onto demand for CAP's SMT products. SMT demand is tied to smartphone OLED panel output and module orders for models such as the Galaxy A series.

Intensifying competition in smartphones is cited as a constraint on the SMT segment's profitability. The wiper market centers on the North American aftermarket, and the Rain-X brand's recognition underpins CAP's supplier position.

Valueline describes CAP as holding a high market share by supplying ITW's Rain-X brand, a National Brand in North America. Expanding European distribution points toward reducing reliance on North America.

Volatility in the SMT segment tends to track OLED module shipments, while the wiper segment tends to track order volumes from overseas distributors.

06

Outlook

The company has outlined three plans. First, it is expanding wiper capacity at its Vietnam plant in phases across the first and second halves, raising annual capacity from 45 million to about 55 million units, to meet rising orders from global customers and improve profitability through greater scale.

Second, it plans mass production of QD display components. The Vietnam subsidiary has completed a QD component line and passed a quality audit by a global display maker, and reliability testing is underway, with mass production to begin within 2026 once testing is complete.

Third, it is considering additional QD line capacity depending on customer demand. The company has also said it is reviewing mergers and acquisitions centered on its existing wiper and SMT businesses.

In Europe, the Biltema supply that began in June 2026 should be reflected in second-half revenue, and the Halfords supply was indicated at about KRW 1.6 billion in annual sales.

Management says wiper capacity expansion and QD component production in the SMT segment are aimed at improving results and strengthening growth. However, third-quarter 2026 revenue and operating profit both declined, so the key question is how far the expansion and new businesses can reverse the downturn.

The checkpoints are whether QD mass production starts, when the wiper expansion is completed, and the segment revenue breakdown in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Wipers Anchor Profitability

First-half 2026 operating profit rose year on year, and the wiper segment is assessed as having driven most of it. Supply to the North American aftermarket built on the Rain-X brand forms the base of earnings. The ability to hold profit while revenue falls is the core bullish argument.

Expanding European Distribution

CAP began supplying Swedish distributor Biltema in June 2026 and signed a supply contract with Halfords in the UK and Ireland. The company serves more than 200 customers across over 40 countries. Broadening sales channels from a North American base into Europe is a bullish factor.

Capacity Expansion and QD Business

A plan to raise Vietnam wiper capacity from 45 million to 55 million units a year is underway. QD display components have a completed production line, have passed a quality audit, and are preparing for mass production within 2026. Extending into electronic components beyond the existing businesses is a bullish factor.

Bear factors

Three Straight Quarters of Declines

Revenue fell for three consecutive quarters in 2026, to KRW 67.8 billion in the first quarter, KRW 65.3 billion in the second and KRW 49 billion in the third. Operating profit fell from a KRW 8.39 billion peak in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.71 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 36.6% year on year.

Sharp Drop in Net Income

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 6.62 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 32.54 million in the third quarter, down 99.3% year on year. Against third-quarter operating profit of KRW 2.71 billion, profit shrank sharply further down the income statement. The cause of this gap cannot be explained from quarterly disclosures alone.

Reliance on Smartphone OLED

On an aggregated basis, the cell phone parts (SMT) segment accounted for about 67% of pre-adjustment 2025 segment revenue, a high reliance on smartphones. Intensifying smartphone competition and shifting downstream demand have left SMT profitability limited. Swings in this segment could drive overall results.

10

Risk factors

Downstream Demand Risk

SMT revenue moves with smartphone OLED panel and module shipments. If demand for mid-range models such as the Galaxy A series weakens or customer orders shift, quarterly revenue could swing again. When revenue and operating profit fall together, as in the third quarter of 2026, fixed costs can weigh further on margins.

Execution Risk in Expansion and New Businesses

QD display components can enter mass production within 2026 only after reliability testing is complete. The Vietnam wiper expansion will lift utilization only if demand keeps pace, and investment takes time to show up in results. Mergers and acquisitions have so far been described as under review.

Overseas Distribution and Trade Risk

Wiper revenue relies heavily on orders from North American and European distributors. With overseas subsidiaries in China, Vietnam, the United States and Japan, shifts in exchange rates and trade conditions could affect export profitability and price competitiveness. Order changes from a small number of distributors could feed quickly into quarterly results.

12

Overall view

CAP has recovered from a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 24.3 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 19.5 billion in 2025. The operating margin rose from 0.5% in 2022 to 8.2% in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell.

However, revenue has declined for three consecutive quarters in 2026, to KRW 67.8 billion in the first quarter, KRW 65.3 billion in the second and KRW 49 billion in the third, and operating profit fell to KRW 2.71 billion in the third quarter, with net income attributable to controlling shareholders at KRW 32.54 million.

The wiper segment supported first-half profit as it widened supply to North American and European aftermarket channels. The SMT segment is tied to smartphone competition and OLED demand swings, so its results are volatile.

Results from the Vietnam wiper expansion and QD component production should become visible around the end of 2026. Bullish and bearish factors coexist, and the 2026 annual report will be the key checkpoint for the full-year operating margin and non-operating items.
