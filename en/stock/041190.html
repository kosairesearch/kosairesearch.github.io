02

Business structure

Woori Technology Investment is a new technology business finance company (venture capital) that invests in biotech, AI and materials companies, so gains and losses from valuing and disposing of its investment portfolio drive its earnings.

A July 2025 and an August 2025 press report both said the company has been expanding its investments in biotech, AI and materials, which it regards as high-growth fields.

The portfolio includes a significant stake held as an early investor in Dunamu, the operator of crypto exchange Upbit, according to the August 2025 report.

The value of the Dunamu stake moves with the crypto market and with Dunamu's industry environment and earnings, so it is read as an asset indirectly linked to crypto prices. The same report noted that the timing and scenario for realizing gains on the Dunamu stake were not yet concrete.

Because venture capital portfolios are illiquid, investments take considerable time to recover and cannot be converted to cash quickly. Quarterly revenue ranged widely, from KRW 1.82 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 85.4 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 6 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Compared with bank holding companies and large brokerages, the business is better understood against specialized venture capital and new technology finance firms. The company is smaller than large financial groups, and its profitability is judged by the exit results of its investment funds.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results swung from an operating loss of KRW 430.9 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 153.9 billion in 2023, a turnaround to profit, then rose further in 2024 before declining in 2025.

Revenue grew from KRW 35.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 170.7 billion in 2023 (up 383.0% year on year) and KRW 192.3 billion in 2024 (up 12.6%), making 2024 the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

In 2025, revenue fell 15.3% to KRW 162.9 billion, operating profit fell 21.4% to KRW 138.1 billion, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 25.2% to KRW 110.5 billion, so all three measures declined.

Operating margin was 90.2% in 2023, 91.4% in 2024 and 84.8% in 2025, and from 2023 onward net profit including non-controlling interests and profit attributable to controlling shareholders were reported at the same value.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 19.8 billion in 2022 to -KRW 10.2 billion in 2023, KRW 2.86 billion in 2024 and -KRW 1.3 billion in 2025, so cash flow and profit often moved in opposite directions.

The debt ratio stayed in the low 20% range, from 20.7% in 2022 to 21.2% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 776.4 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 164.5 billion.

Quarterly, a third-quarter 2025 operating loss of KRW 945 million and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 964 million widened to an operating loss of KRW 39.4 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 34 billion in the fourth quarter.

The first quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 15.4 billion and controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 14.4 billion, and the second quarter brought revenue of KRW 85.4 billion (up 363.7% quarter on quarter), operating profit of KRW 75.8 billion (up 390.9%) and controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 60.5 billion (up 321.1%).

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue fell to KRW 6 billion (down 93.0% quarter on quarter, up 230.1% year on year), an operating loss of KRW 3.31 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 2.7 billion were recorded, and losses widened year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 38.1 billion, a little over a third of the KRW 110.5 billion recorded for full-year 2025.

Given the large swings between quarters, whether the second-quarter profit and third-quarter loss are one-off or recurring has to be separated using the detailed items in the annual report.

05

Industry analysis

Results at new technology finance (venture capital) firms depend on the growth of portfolio companies and on the recovery of the IPO market, which together determine the scale of exits.

Industry observers say growth in portfolio companies and an IPO market recovery could benefit the sector, but they also note that recovering investments can take considerable time and asset values can shift with market conditions.

Because of this, quarterly profit at venture capital firms tends to swing sharply in quarters when exits cluster. The gap between KRW 85.4 billion of revenue in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 6 billion in the third quarter can be read as an example of this pattern.

The company's end markets overlap with the funding environment for AI, biotech and materials companies, and a 2025 press report cited the expansion of investment in these fields as a growth factor.

Another external cycle is the crypto market, because the value of the Dunamu stake moves with crypto market sentiment, so two outside forces act on earnings at the same time.

Within financial services, bank holding companies and large brokerages have comparatively recurring revenue bases, whereas this company's profit depends more heavily on valuing and disposing of investment assets.

Peer comparison is therefore less distorted when made within the group of specialized new technology finance firms than across the whole financial sector. Whether the cycle is in an exit phase or a phase of accumulating valuation losses cannot be settled from quarterly profit alone.

Outlook

The outlook comes down to three checkpoints. The first is the timing of fund exits and exit results at year-end. A 2025 press report cited year-end portfolio exit results and the visibility of venture fund redemption plans as key variables.

The second is whether portfolio companies' value increases, since valuation of the biotech, AI and materials holdings will determine how the portfolio feeds into quarterly profit. The third is the Dunamu stake, whose valuation can change with industry conditions and Dunamu's earnings trend.

The profits of the first and second quarters of 2026 read as the result of exit and valuation gains supporting results, while the third-quarter loss shows that this trend can reverse within a single quarter.

Whether foreign and institutional investors keep buying was also raised in a 2025 press report as a factor for the share price. The next periodic filing is the 2026 annual report, which will disclose fourth-quarter 2026 results and full-year figures.

As a rule, the annual report must be filed within 90 days after the fiscal year ends, which means by March 31, 2027. Operating cash flow was -KRW 1.3 billion in 2025, so whether annual cash flow moves in the same direction as profit is the next checkpoint.

Year-end 2026 total equity and debt ratio compared with the 2025 levels (total equity of KRW 776.4 billion and a debt ratio of 21.2%) will serve as the benchmark for changes in the financial structure.

Bull factors

Return to Quarterly Profit

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 38.1 billion, which is cited as evidence of recovery from losses. The detailed items behind these profits still need to be checked in the filings.

Expanding New Technology Investments

According to 2025 press reports, the company has been expanding its investments in biotech, AI and materials, which have high growth potential, within its core new technology business finance activities.

The core of the bullish case is that if this portfolio moves into the exit phase, valuation gains could turn into realized profits. As exit results accumulate, quarterly profit could also become more predictable.

Link to the Dunamu Stake

A 2025 press report said that, as an early investor holding a significant stake in Dunamu, the company could indirectly benefit when the crypto market is strong. Being linked to a leading domestic crypto exchange is cited as a feature that distinguishes it from other venture capital firms. This bullish argument, however, depends on crypto price trends.

Bear factors

Third-Quarter Loss Returns

In the third quarter of 2026, an operating loss of KRW 3.31 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 2.7 billion reversed the second-quarter profit within one quarter. Revenue fell 93.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 6 billion, and losses widened year on year. The bearish argument starts from the point that quarterly profits do not guarantee durable earnings.

Declines in Full-Year 2025

In 2025, revenue of KRW 162.9 billion, operating profit of KRW 138.1 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 110.5 billion all declined. In the fourth quarter of 2025, an operating loss of KRW 39.4 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 34 billion widened the losses.

Profitability stayed positive for the year, but the decline in earnings capacity appeared first and is a bearish factor.

Exit Uncertainty

Venture capital investments are illiquid, so recovery takes a long time and asset values can change with market conditions. The value of the Dunamu stake also depends on the industry environment and earnings trends, and the timing and scenario for realizing gains have been identified as not yet concrete. Until exits happen, valuation gains and losses that move quarterly results are a bearish factor.

Risk factors

Quarterly Earnings Volatility

Profit swings between quarters are very large, such as operating profit of KRW 75.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and an operating loss of KRW 3.31 billion in the third quarter. Because profit concentrates around the timing of valuations and disposals, trends are hard to judge from one or two quarters.

The fact that the last four quarters of controlling-shareholder net profit total less than the 2025 full-year figure also shows the effect of quarterly variation.

Crypto and Dunamu Stake

The value of the Dunamu stake depends on crypto market trends and on Dunamu's industry environment and earnings. When crypto investor sentiment weakens, related stocks have fallen together, so the share price can be affected by theme-driven flows apart from earnings. How changes in the stake's value flow into equity and profit is detailed in the notes to the annual report.

Delayed Exits and Cash Flow

If fund exits are delayed, cash inflows are postponed and operating cash flow can diverge from profit. Operating cash flow was -KRW 1.3 billion in 2025, a negative figure. Because recovery of venture investments can take considerable time, uncertainty over exit timing is a risk factor.

Overall view

Woori Technology Investment's disclosed results show a turnaround to profit in 2023, a peak in revenue and operating profit in 2024, and declines in all three measures in 2025.

In 2026, profit in the first and second quarters was followed by an operating loss of KRW 3.31 billion in the third quarter, so quarterly results have swung sharply. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 38.1 billion, below the 2025 full-year figure.

On the business side, the biotech, AI and materials portfolio and the Dunamu stake act as both strengths and sources of volatility.

Bullish factors are the return to quarterly profit and a stake whose value is linked to the crypto market, while bearish factors are the 2025 annual declines, the third-quarter loss, and uncertainty over exits.

Valuation reads as a price below net asset value attributable to controlling shareholders, but given the illiquid portfolio structure typical of venture capital firms, interpreting net asset figures depends on how investment assets are valued.

The next checkpoints are exit results at the end of 2026 and fourth-quarter and full-year profit in the 2026 annual report, which will show whether the quarterly profit trend is structural or one-off.
