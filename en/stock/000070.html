Samyang Holdings: Conditions for Earnings Recovery After Losses

After a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in 2025, profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose sharply in the third quarter of 2026, but operating profit fell to KRW 12.4 billion, so whether the core business recovers is the next checkpoint.

02

Business structure

Samyang Holdings is the business holding company of the Samyang Group, and its main income comes from dividends, trademark royalties and rental income from its food and chemical subsidiaries. Its diversified subsidiary businesses in food and chemicals provide a stable source of earnings for the holding company.

The segment mix in the second quarter of 2026 by sales was chemicals 53%, food 37% and other 11%. The core subsidiary is Samyang, which handles sugar, starch sweeteners and flour, and Samyang Holdings held a 61.83% stake as of year-end 2023. Samyang accounted for 75.2% of consolidated sales in 2024.

In food, Samyang ranks second in sugar and starch sweeteners and fourth in flour, according to a credit rating report. The chemical business covers specialty chemical materials, engineering plastics and PET. The BPA maker is wholly owned, while the TPA producer Samyang Petrochemical is 40% owned.

The other segment includes a restaurant subsidiary and an IT subsidiary. Beyond subsidiary dividends, the holding company earns trademark, shared service center and rental income. The absorption merger of Samyang Biopharm in April 2021 added pharmaceuticals to its own business.

Food competes with established domestic sugar and flour players, while chemicals are a commodity and engineering materials business whose earnings move with raw material prices and demand cycles, so its results swing differently from food.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual sales fell from KRW 3.32 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.21 trillion in 2023, a 3.2% decrease, then rose to KRW 3.55 trillion in 2024, a 10.7% increase, which was the largest year among 2022 to 2025. Sales were KRW 3.35 trillion in 2025, a 5.8% decrease.

Operating profit was KRW 132.3 billion in 2022, the largest year among 2022 to 2025, while KRW 94.8 billion in 2023, a 28.4% decrease, was the smallest year. Operating profit recovered to KRW 127.5 billion in 2024, a 34.5% increase, and then came in at KRW 108.8 billion in 2025, a 14.7% decrease.

The operating margin was 4.0% in 2022, 3.0% in 2023, 3.6% in 2024 and 3.2% in 2025, staying in a band of roughly 3 to 4%.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 172.4 billion in 2023, a 142.7% increase, then fell to KRW 28.3 billion in 2024, an 83.6% decrease, and turned into a net loss of KRW 200 billion in 2025. Net loss for the year was KRW 298.1 billion.

The sharp gap between a positive operating profit and a larger net loss in 2025 suggests that non-operating items or one-off costs were large.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 76.5% in 2024 to 98.8% in 2025, and controlling shareholders' equity was KRW 1.82 trillion out of total equity of KRW 2.65 trillion at year-end 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 95.7 billion in 2022, KRW 305.8 billion in 2023, KRW 258.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 253.8 billion in 2025, remaining positive even in loss years.

By quarter, the operating loss of KRW 12.3 billion and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 266.7 billion in Q4 2025 were the weakest points.

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 29.2 billion in Q1 2026, rose to KRW 60.5 billion in Q2 2026, a 107.6% increase quarter on quarter, and fell to KRW 12.4 billion in Q3 2026, a 79.5% decrease quarter on quarter.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for two consecutive quarters from Q2 2026 through Q3 2026, reaching KRW 118 billion in Q3 2026.

Because operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved in opposite directions, the effect of non-operating items should be checked in the quarterly report notes.

05

Industry analysis

Samyang Holdings' earnings depend heavily on the food margins of its subsidiary Samyang and on conditions in the chemical business. In Q1 2025, a fall in food raw material prices led to lower selling prices and a smaller top line, and Samyang's operating profit fell 16.0% year on year in the same period.

In chemicals, weaker results cut the dividend income that flows directly to the holding company. A credit rating agency expects stable cash inflow to the holding company if Samyang restores profitability.

Food raw materials are heavily imported and sensitive to exchange rates and grain prices, while chemical feedstock margins move with crude oil. Among peers, Samyang Holdings is compared with food and chemical holding companies such as CJ, Lotte and OCI Holdings Company.

Because holding company income follows subsidiary results, earnings swing more widely when the food and chemical businesses move in different directions. The large swings in quarterly operating profit in 2026 show that this structure is still at work.

06

Outlook

The next checkpoint is whether core operating profit stabilizes quarter by quarter. Operating profit fell to KRW 12.4 billion in Q3 2026, while profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 118 billion, so current earnings are strongly affected by non-operating items.

Operating profit turned positive from Q1 2026 after the Q4 2025 operating loss, which reads as an early sign of recovery. However, sales fell 3.4% quarter on quarter in Q3 2026, so top-line recovery remains gradual.

On the subsidiary side, Samyang's food margins and the dividend capacity of the chemical business drive holding company income. The company does not present annual targets or new capacity schedules in this report.

On the financial side, with the debt-to-equity ratio at 98.8%, the relationship between borrowing and cash flow is worth tracking. Operating cash flow of KRW 253.8 billion in 2025 is a cushion, and the full-year 2026 figures will confirm the trend.

Bull factors

Subsidiary cash generation

Samyang's 2024 results improved on wider food margins and a recovery in packaging, according to a credit rating report.

Turn in quarterly profit direction

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 29.2 billion in Q1 2026 and rose to KRW 60.5 billion in Q2 2026. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for two consecutive quarters from Q2 2026 through Q3 2026. Whether this improvement spreads into core operations is the central point of the bullish case.

Low price-to-book level

In a first-half 2026 PBR comparison, the company ranked in the lower range alongside several holding and industrial names. A price below book value is what the bullish side cites as an asset-based argument. However, the quality of the net assets and the valuation of subsidiary stakes must be examined separately.

Bear factors

Quarterly volatility in operating profit

Operating profit was KRW 12.4 billion in Q3 2026, down 79.5% quarter on quarter and 73.3% year on year. Sales of KRW 930.7 billion rose 4.6% year on year, but profitability fell sharply. Following the operating loss of KRW 12.3 billion in Q4 2025, quarterly swings in core profitability form the bearish argument.

Rising financial burden

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 76.5% in 2024 to 98.8% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 2.62 trillion at year-end 2025. Total equity was KRW 2.65 trillion, of which controlling shareholders' equity was KRW 1.82 trillion. Accumulated losses reduced capital headroom, and borrowing could limit the pace of earnings recovery.

Chemical weakness and smaller dividends

Weaker chemical results reduced dividend income, which is a factor cutting holding company revenue. In Q1 2025 Samyang's sales fell 6.0% year on year and operating profit fell 16.0% after food selling prices were lowered. Because subsidiary weakness passes directly into holding company cash inflow, it supports the bearish case.

Risk factors

Opacity of non-operating items

The gap between 2025 operating profit of KRW 108.8 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 200 billion, as well as the gap in Q3 2026 between operating profit of KRW 12.4 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 118 billion, depends on the size of non-operating or one-off items.

Until the nature and persistence of these items are clear, net profit figures alone make it hard to judge the core business. The notes to the quarterly and annual reports are where these items should be checked.

Dependence on subsidiary results

As the 2024 share of 75.2% of consolidated sales from Samyang shows, holding company results are concentrated in one subsidiary. Food raw materials are heavily imported and exposed to exchange rates and commodity prices, while chemical margins depend on feedstock prices and demand. If both businesses weaken at the same time, the downside for holding company earnings widens.

Higher financial leverage

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 76.5% in 2024 to 98.8% in 2025. Total liabilities of KRW 2.62 trillion at year-end 2025 are close in size to total equity of KRW 2.65 trillion.

Interest costs and the maturity profile of borrowings could weigh during a recovery and reduce the cash room of a holding company that depends on subsidiary dividends.
