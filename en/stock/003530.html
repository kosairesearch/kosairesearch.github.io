Report overview

Wealth Management Growth Amid Volatile Quarterly Profit

Annual operating profit recovered to KRW 147.7 billion in 2025, but the wealth management-led earnings base swung sharply in the third quarter of 2026, and the rebound in IB and trading remains limited.

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Hanwha INVESTMENT&SECURITIES divides its revenue into wealth management (WM), wholesale, trading, investment banking (IB) and other segments.

On a standalone basis for January to September 2026, WM net operating revenue rose 108.2% year on year to KRW 273.6 billion, making it the fastest-growing segment, according to News Pim. Wholesale net operating revenue over the same period rose 78.4% to KRW 55.3 billion.

Trading net operating revenue fell 46.4% to KRW 35.2 billion, and IB net operating revenue fell 50.5% to KRW 29.1 billion.

On a quarterly basis, WM operating profit dropped 71.9% from KRW 69.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 19.5 billion in the third quarter, while commission income fell 42.4% from KRW 96.7 billion to KRW 55.7 billion, according to Bizwatch.

In the third quarter, the IB and trading segments swung from second-quarter operating losses to profits of KRW 11 billion and KRW 8.5 billion, but these gains did not offset the decline in WM.

Operating losses in the other segment widened from KRW 8.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 23.6 billion in the third quarter of 2026. Revenue weight is shifting toward WM, which is built on brokerage and commissions, and corporate client business.

Still, as the third quarter showed, commission income can move sharply from quarter to quarter depending on trading conditions. On the competitive side, Korea Economic Daily reported in October 2026 that the gap with larger brokers has widened. The company has said it aims to become a specialized digital asset securities firm.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show a clear recovery since the 2023 low. Revenue fell from KRW 2.11 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.97 trillion in 2023 (down 6.9% year on year), then rose to KRW 2.5 trillion in 2024 (up 26.8%) and KRW 3.09 trillion in 2025 (up 24.0%), with 2025 the largest year of 2022–2025.

Operating profit was KRW 34.4 billion in 2022 and KRW 31.5 billion in 2023 (down 8.6%), fell to KRW 3.97 billion in 2024 (down 87.4%), the smallest year, and then jumped to KRW 147.7 billion in 2025 (up 3,624.2%). The operating margin was 1.6% in 2022 and 1.6% in 2023, dropped to 0.2% in 2024, and rose to 4.8% in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a net loss of KRW 54.9 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 9.3 billion in 2023, turning to growth with KRW 38.9 billion in 2024 (up 318.2%) and KRW 102 billion in 2025 (up 162.3%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was disclosed at the same amounts in each year. Operating cash flow was KRW 412.6 billion in 2022, then -KRW 1.1 trillion in 2023, -KRW 912.3 billion in 2024 and -KRW 728.4 billion in 2025, so cash flow stayed negative from 2023 even as profit recovered.

At the end of 2025, the debt ratio was 739.3%, with total equity of KRW 2.07 trillion and total liabilities of KRW 15.3 trillion.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 469.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 3.23 trillion in the second quarter of 2026 before falling to KRW 1.62 trillion in the third quarter, while operating profit stayed in a range from the KRW 200 billion level to the KRW 500 billion level.

First-quarter operating profit of KRW 29.6 billion and second-quarter operating profit of KRW 55.6 billion drove the recovery, while third-quarter operating profit of KRW 21 billion was down 44.3% year on year.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 to third quarter of 2026) totaled KRW 71.8 billion.

According to Korea Economic Daily, first-half 2026 net operating revenue rose 35.6%, but general and administrative expenses rose 41.8%, so the gain did not carry through to net profit.

05

Industry analysis

The brokerage sector benefited from the strong equity market in the first half of 2026. According to Korea Economic Daily, the combined first-half net profit of the top ten brokerages exceeded KRW 10 trillion.

Commission income from brokerage rose on higher trading activity, and the company's WM growth follows the same trend. Investment banking activity, however, has been weak. The company's equity underwriting as lead manager was nearly absent from early 2026, after the E8 deal, through the third quarter.

Its debt underwriting volume peaked near KRW 1 trillion in 2019 but reached only KRW 146.6 billion through the third quarter of 2026, raising concern that the full-year figure could be a record low, according to Korea Economic Daily in October 2026.

The company's IB net operating revenue also fell 68.1% year on year to KRW 10.7 billion in the first half of 2026, according to The Fact. On competitive position, Korea Economic Daily said the gap with larger brokerages has widened.

On the KRW 3 trillion equity threshold for a full-service investment business, the same report calculated that first-half standalone equity of KRW 2.27 trillion plus the KRW 500 billion capital raise would reach about KRW 2.77 trillion, still below the requirement.

Brokerage earnings move with trading value and the equity capital markets environment, so WM fee income and IB income are currently moving in different directions.

Outlook

The direction the company has set out centers on strengthening its core business, managing risk, and becoming a specialized digital asset securities firm. In the third-quarter 2026 results release, a company official said the firm has maintained a profit trend even in a highly volatile market, according to News Pim.

On the capital side, a KRW 500 billion third-party share issuance was decided in October 2026, according to Korea Economic Daily. If the issuance is completed, equity will increase and narrow the gap with the KRW 3 trillion equity requirement for a full-service investment business.

However, the reported calculation shows that even combined with first-half standalone equity the total would be KRW 2.77 trillion.

WM net operating revenue on a standalone basis for the first half of 2026 rose 156.9% year on year to KRW 210.1 billion, but in the third quarter its operating profit dropped sharply as commission income declined.

The IB segment swung to a KRW 11 billion operating profit in the third quarter of 2026, and trading turned to an KRW 8.5 billion profit, according to Bizwatch. Whether the other segment's losses keep widening will be visible at the annual level in the 2026 annual report. The timing of when digital asset operations will contribute to earnings has not been specified by the company.

08

Bull factors

Expanding Wealth Management Earnings Base

First-half 2026 standalone WM net operating revenue rose 156.9% year on year to KRW 210.1 billion, according to The Fact. Wholesale net operating revenue rose 96.7% to KRW 36.1 billion over the same period, which points to a broader client base.

For January to September 2026, WM net operating revenue was up 108.2%, making WM the center of overall revenue growth. The company has stressed strengthening its core business.

From Losses to Profit: Earnings Recovery

Operating profit recovered from KRW 3.97 billion in 2024 to KRW 147.7 billion in 2025, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a net loss of KRW 54.9 billion in 2022 to profit in 2023 and reached KRW 102 billion in 2025.

Consolidated operating profit for the first half of 2026 was KRW 85.3 billion, a slight year-on-year increase, according to The Fact. First-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 29.6 billion and second-quarter operating profit of KRW 55.6 billion were both above the KRW 25.2 billion of the fourth quarter of 2025.

Capital Raise and Bid for Full-Service Investment Business Status

In October 2026, the company decided on a KRW 500 billion third-party share issuance to strengthen its capital base, according to Korea Economic Daily. The capital strengthening is presented as groundwork for entering the full-service investment business and expanding digital asset operations.

Consolidated equity at the end of September 2026 was KRW 2.23 trillion, up 7.7% from the end of 2025, according to News Pim. On the bullish side, a larger capital base could widen room for corporate business and IB underwriting.

Bear factors

High Quarterly Earnings Volatility

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 21 billion was down 62.2% from the prior quarter, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 11.6 billion fell 57.2%. WM operating profit dropped 71.9% from KRW 69.2 billion to KRW 19.5 billion, according to Bizwatch.

Over the same period, commission income fell from KRW 96.7 billion to KRW 55.7 billion, moving alongside the WM decline. Quarterly revenue has ranged from the KRW 1 trillion level to the KRW 3 trillion level, which makes profit harder to predict.

Weak IB, Equity and Debt Underwriting

IB net operating revenue fell 68.1% year on year to KRW 10.7 billion in the first half of 2026, according to The Fact.

Korea Economic Daily reported that the company's equity underwriting as lead manager was nearly absent from early 2026 through the third quarter, and its debt underwriting volume reached only KRW 146.6 billion through the third quarter.

Third-quarter IB operating profit swung to KRW 11 billion but remained below the KRW 17.1 billion of the same quarter a year earlier, according to Bizwatch.

Leverage and Operating Cash Outflows

The debt ratio was 739.3% at the end of 2025, and total liabilities reached KRW 15.3 trillion. Operating cash flow was disclosed at -KRW 1.1 trillion in 2023, -KRW 912.3 billion in 2024 and -KRW 728.4 billion in 2025.

According to the reported calculation, equity would still fall short of the KRW 3 trillion requirement even after the capital raise. Completing the share issuance would dilute the stakes of existing shareholders.

10

Risk factors

Market and Trading Dependence

Commission income moves sharply from quarter to quarter with trading value and investor sentiment. Third-quarter 2026 commission income was down 42.4% from the second quarter, according to Bizwatch. If the market corrects, WM revenue could also be disrupted, which makes quarterly results hard to forecast. The trading segment, too, can see its results diverge in volatile markets.

Capital Requirements and Execution of the Share Issuance

Entry into the full-service investment business requires equity of KRW 3 trillion. Whether and when the KRW 500 billion capital raise decided in October 2026 is completed remains an open question. The reported calculation suggests the raise alone would not close the gap with the requirement.

Leverage and Recovery of Corporate Finance Revenue

The debt ratio of 739.3% at the end of 2025 means the company is sensitive to funding costs and capital capacity. Underwriting and arrangement fees shrank to KRW 4.2 billion in the first half of 2026, according to Korea Economic Daily. If IPO and rights offering activity stays thin, the recovery of IB income could be delayed.

12

Overall view

Hanwha INVESTMENT&SECURITIES's results show two sides. Annual operating profit for 2025 recovered sharply to KRW 147.7 billion from KRW 3.97 billion in 2024, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 102 billion. This recovery relies on growth in the wealth management (WM) segment.

Cumulative WM net operating revenue for January to September 2026 rose 108.2% to KRW 273.6 billion. In the same period, trading and IB net operating revenue declined, and third-quarter WM operating profit fell 71.9% from the prior quarter, so quarterly profit volatility remains high.

On the financial side, the debt ratio was 739.3% at the end of 2025, and operating cash flow has been disclosed as negative since 2023. The company is pursuing entry into the full-service investment business and a wider digital asset business by increasing capital through a KRW 500 billion share issuance.

However, whether equity reaches the KRW 3 trillion requirement and whether IB, equity underwriting and debt underwriting recover remain issues to watch. The next checkpoints are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results and the 2026 annual report.
