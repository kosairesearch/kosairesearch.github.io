A Profit Turnaround Tied to MSD's Late-Stage Trials

INGENIA Therapeutics (Reg.S) has earnings and a share price that depend largely on how far MSD's late-stage MK-8748 program advances.

  1. 1

    First-half 2026 cumulative operating profit was KRW 0.36 million and net profit was KRW 0.47 million, a turnaround from the same period a year earlier.

  2. 2

    MSD is running four late-stage trials of MK-8748 (IGT-427) in wet macular degeneration and diabetic macular edema, and MSD bears the development cost.

  3. 3

    A further USD 5 million milestone for the start of the diabetic macular edema Phase 3 was disclosed on September 28, 2026.

  4. 4

    The company had essentially no revenue in 2025, so its income is concentrated in clinical milestones.

  5. 5

    A large block of locked-up shares was released one month after listing, and the market capitalization of KRW 1,147.1 billion is well above the offer-price market cap at listing.

02

Business structure

INGENIA Therapeutics is an antibody drug developer founded in Boston in March 2018 by CEO Sang-Yeol Han. It develops antibody drugs that repair and stabilize damaged microvessels, based on core technology from KAIST and the Institute for Basic Science (IBS).

Because it is a US corporation, it listed in Korea by issuing depositary receipts (KDR) backed by shares issued in the United States.

Its core asset is the retinal disease candidate IGT-427. The company licensed the compound to EyeBio in October 2022, and it became part of MSD's pipeline as MK-8748 when MSD acquired EyeBio in 2024.

Since MSD runs the global trials itself, INGENIA receives stage-based milestones and future royalties without carrying development costs.

MSD is running two Phase 2b/3 trials in wet macular degeneration and two Phase 3 trials in diabetic macular edema, covering about 4,000 patients in total with direct comparison against Eylea.

The in-house track is IGT-303 for chronic kidney disease (CKD). A global Phase 2a trial is under way in Australia, New Zealand and Korea, and the company is targeting a global licensing deal in 2027.

The company has also filed an IND for a Phase 1/2a trial with the US FDA and is in licensing talks with several global pharmaceutical companies. Revenue is not broken out by segment, and current income essentially comes from the IGT-427 agreement.

In the competitive landscape, the task is to show an advantage over the standard of care, Eylea. In Korea, MMT-205, co-developed by Curacle and MapTix, has been licensed to Memento Medicine in the US and is preparing to enter clinical trials.

Among candidates combining direct TIE2 activation with VEGF inhibition in a single bispecific antibody, MK-8748 is described as the most advanced.

03

Recent trends

Per DART filings, first-half 2026 cumulative operating profit was KRW 0.36 million and net profit was KRW 0.47 million, both a turnaround from the same period a year earlier.

Money Today reported on August 21, 2026 that second-quarter consolidated revenue was USD 5 million (about KRW 6.9 billion), driven by the MK-8748 milestone.

That article presented second-quarter operating and net profit in US dollars as USD 0.36 million and USD 0.47 million, so the currency presentation should be checked against the original filing. In 2025 the company had no consolidated revenue and an operating loss of USD 23,338,334.

In its securities registration statement, the company projected 2026 revenue of KRW 47.7 billion and operating and net profit of KRW 14.5 billion each.

The share price has been volatile since listing. On August 18, 2026 it opened at KRW 10,960, below the KRW 12,000 offer price, and fell as low as KRW 9,900. It closed that day at KRW 17,990, up 49.92% from the offer price.

On September 2, 2026, trading value was KRW 175.97 billion, among the highest on KOSDAQ, while the shares fell 4.77% to KRW 18,370.

On September 28, 2026 the company disclosed that it had received notice from an MSD subsidiary that the diabetic macular edema Phase 3 milestone was achieved, and had invoiced USD 5 million. On September 29 the shares traded as high as KRW 24,700 intraday.

At the time of writing on October 27, 2026, the share price was KRW 23,350, up 7.85% on the day, with a market capitalization of KRW 1,147.1 billion. MTN reported that private equity and investment trust funds bought 110,000 shares on October 23 and 26.

04

Outlook

Future earnings depend on how fast MSD advances the trials and when milestones are recognized. The USD 5 million diabetic macular edema milestone disclosed on September 28 should be checked for its accounting treatment in reports for the third quarter of 2026 and later.

The diabetic macular edema Phase 3 trial MK-8748-005 enrolls 1,104 patients, compares MK-8748 with aflibercept on change in best-corrected visual acuity at one year, and has an estimated primary completion in November 2028.

CEO Han said the company is targeting FDA approval in 2029 and commercialization in 2030 after Phase 3. These are company goals and depend on trial results and regulatory review.

In the in-house pipeline, the IGT-303 Phase 2a efficacy data and the 2027 licensing target are the variables. A deal would add an income source beyond milestones, whereas delays would draw more attention to the pace of cash use.

A clinical trial application for MK-8748 has also been accepted in China, so regional expansion procedures have begun. The company said it will invest the listing proceeds in research and development of follow-on pipelines.

On supply and demand, the lock-up release schedule continues. For second-largest shareholder Interbest Open Innovation, 78.7% of its holding was released one month after listing, with 3.3% to follow at three months and 15.0% at one year. Brokerages have viewed the late-stage progress favorably.

Daol Investment & Securities said MK-8748 could become best in class if the trials succeed. This is the brokerage's opinion, and trial results will not be known until 2028 or later.

05

Bull factors

A big pharma partner funds development

MSD runs the global late-stage trials of MK-8748 itself, so INGENIA's development cost burden is small. MSD is running four late-stage trials across wet macular degeneration and diabetic macular edema. At the J.P.

Morgan Healthcare Conference in early 2026, MSD introduced MK-8748 as a program looking toward a next-generation Keytruda. The partner's visible commitment is a basis for judging business continuity.

Milestones are reaching the income statement

Operating profit of KRW 0.36 million and net profit of KRW 0.47 million in the first half of 2026 show the turnaround. In the second quarter the company received USD 5 million as the wet macular degeneration trials entered late-stage development.

On September 28 it invoiced a further USD 5 million for the start of the diabetic macular edema Phase 3. The contract structure, in which cash arrives as clinical stages advance, has now been demonstrated.

Indication expansion and follow-on pipeline

MK-8748's late-stage program has widened to two eye diseases, enlarging the target patient pool. Total enrollment has grown to about 4,000 patients. The company is expanding its pipeline beyond ophthalmology into kidney disease, glaucoma and oncology. A successful licensing of IGT-303 would reduce reliance on a single contract.

06

Bear factors

Income concentrated in one contract

All income depends on conditional milestones under the MSD licensing contract, which raises financial risk if trials are delayed. Revenue in 2025 was essentially nil once upfront payment recognition ended.

Operating profit of KRW 0.36 million for the first half could easily change depending on whether a milestone is booked.

A market cap far above the offer-price level

The offering was KRW 60 billion and the market capitalization at the offer price was about KRW 593.3 billion. The current market capitalization of KRW 1,147.1 billion is nearly double that.

One reading is that expectations of clinical success and commercialization are already largely priced in, and disappointing trial data could widen price swings.

Weak offering demand and free-float supply

The offer price was set at KRW 12,000, the bottom of the target range. In the institutional book-building, the share of commitments to hold for at least 15 days was only 2.23%. One month after listing, the number of tradable shares rose to 28,871,128. The larger volume of shares that early investors can sell is cited as a supply-demand burden.

07

Risk factors

Macro

The biotech sector is sensitive to interest rates and risk appetite. DB Securities explained that weak healthcare sentiment at the time of book-building led to a low offer price. A change in sector sentiment can amplify volatility in newly listed biotech shares. Because revenue and costs are incurred in US dollars, the KRW/USD rate also affects won-denominated results.

Late-stage trials can fail or slip. The diabetic macular edema Phase 3 compares MK-8748 with aflibercept, and the estimated primary completion date is November 2028. If the head-to-head trials do not show the expected advantage, the outlook for later milestones and royalties would change. MSD, as the partner, holds the decisions on development direction and schedule.

Interbest Open Innovation's locked-up shares are also released in tranches at three months and one year after listing. Each release date increases the volume of shares available for sale and may amplify price volatility.

In addition, because the issuer is a US corporation listed through KDR, the transfer of offering proceeds and the way shareholder rights are exercised differ from Korean domestic issuers. Investors should read the relevant explanations in the securities registration statement.

08

Overall view

INGENIA Therapeutics (Reg.S) is a development-stage company whose milestone income arises as MSD advances late-stage trials of the licensed MK-8748. First-half 2026 cumulative operating profit of KRW 0.36 million and net profit of KRW 0.47 million are meaningful as a turnaround, but the amounts are small.

The bullish side points to the partner bearing development costs, four late-stage trials running in parallel, and further milestone inflows. The bearish side points to dependence on a single contract, a market capitalization well above the offer-price level, and supply pressure from lock-up releases.

Trial results due from 2028 onward and the 2027 licensing target for IGT-303 are the variables that will shape the business structure. This report is for information only, and any directional judgment is left to the reader.
