SK REIT: Earnings Recovery Meets Asset Expansion

SK REIT's operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for two consecutive fiscal years after the June 2024 fiscal-year trough. The next test is whether asset additions keep translating into earnings growth under a sizable debt load.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

SK REIT is a hybrid REIT listed on KOSPI in September 2021, holding mainly office assets in central Seoul and the Pangyo area of the capital region.

Its portfolio includes Seorin Building, SK U-Tower, Jongno Tower and SK-C Tower alongside industrial assets such as SK Energy gas stations and an SK hynix water treatment center.

Most assets are leased under a triple-net structure, with affiliates including SK Inc., SK Energy and SK hynix signing long-term master leases. NH Investment & Securities assessed that this structure lowers vacancy risk and limits earnings volatility.

SK-P Tower in Pangyo was added through a sale-and-leaseback with affiliates, funded with the company's own cash and borrowings without a separate rights offering. A KRW 216.4 billion loan disclosed in November 2025 carries a three-year term at the three-month CD rate plus 90 basis points.

Compared with sponsor-linked Lotte REIT, analysts have pointed to a different way in which major shareholders participated in asset additions. Based on a foreign data service, SK Inc. held 29.09% as of year-end 2024, and Mirae Asset Global Investments held 20.41% as of a July 2026 filing. Revenue depends heavily on long-term leases with group tenants, so tenant concentration is high.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed-results basis, revenue was KRW 52.6 billion in the fiscal year ended June 2023, KRW 52.4 billion in the year ended June 2024 (down 0.4% year on year), KRW 70.4 billion in the year ended June 2025 (up 34.2%), and KRW 80.2 billion in the year ended June 2026 (up 14.0%).

Revenue has risen for two consecutive years since the June 2024 trough, and the June 2026 year is the largest of the four years.

Operating profit moved from KRW 35.8 billion to KRW 35.4 billion (down 1.1%) before rising to KRW 64.5 billion in the year ended June 2025 (up 82.2%) and KRW 71.7 billion in the year ended June 2026 (up 11.2%).

Operating margin went from 68.0% and 67.6% in the first two years to 91.7% in the year ended June 2025 and 89.5% in the year ended June 2026. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.59 billion, then KRW 1.15 billion (down 68.0%), then KRW 32.4 billion (up 2,716.0%), then KRW 37.2 billion (up 15.0%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 38.4 billion in the year ended June 2026, and the gap from the controlling-shareholder figure reflects the non-controlling share. Past press reports linked the weak year ended June 2024 to rising interest costs that reduced distributable earnings.

NH Investment & Securities said earnings capacity improved after the refinancing. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 122.5 billion to KRW 15.3 billion and then KRW 4.93 billion before recovering to KRW 23.5 billion in the year ended June 2026.

The debt ratio went from 225.1% and 250.5% in the first two years to 168.9% and 165.5% in the two most recent years. At June 2026 year-end, total equity was KRW 2.04 trillion, equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.96 trillion, and total liabilities were KRW 3.38 trillion.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the four most recent quarters (third quarter 2025 to second quarter 2026) totaled KRW 37.2 billion.

The preliminary first-quarter 2026 result, disclosed on May 6, 2026 on a separate basis, showed revenue of KRW 422.2 million... in full terms KRW 42.221 billion (up 16.3% year on year) and operating profit of KRW 39.209 billion (up 27.5%).

05

Industry analysis

Industry commentary generally holds that larger REITs have advantages in raising funds and buying new assets. When press reported on SK REIT in 2022, it had overtaken Lotte REIT to become the largest by assets under management.

Against Lotte REIT, another sponsor-linked vehicle, the points of comparison are the tenant behind each asset and how major shareholders joined asset additions. Koramco Life Infra REIT has been described as following a different operating strategy, including on gas station disposals.

On demand, NH Investment & Securities viewed the Pangyo location as capturing demand that would otherwise flow to Gangnam. Press reports from late 2025 said REIT-focused ETF inflows into SK REIT had affected its share price.

On rates, a policy easing stance was read as supportive of inflows to the sector, but the rate path affects both funding costs and dividend appeal at the same time. The 2023 addition of a water treatment center, an industrial asset that was a first for a Korean listed REIT, drew mixed market reactions.

NH Investment & Securities argued that long-term lease contracts make the company relatively less exposed to short-term swings in the sector. The variables that will indicate where the cycle stands are the interest-rate path and demand for office space.

06

Outlook

According to the company's annual report published in 2024, the sectors under consideration for addition include group semiconductor and advanced materials assets, data centers, renewable energy and telecom infrastructure.

Commentary articles noted, however, that the direction, timing and size have not been specified and that these may be examples rather than a plan. NH Investment & Securities said the 2025 refinancing cut funding costs by 75 basis points and that capital-reserve dividends were expected to end in 2026.

The same analysts reported that the company plans to reduce excess dividends to minimize the size of rights offerings needed for asset additions. Its expected dividend yield for 2026 was estimated at around 5%, and the estimate could rise if a special dividend tied to gas station disposals is reflected.

The KRW 216.4 billion loan from November 2025 is priced off the three-month CD rate, so the rate path will drive future interest costs. NH Investment & Securities said the convertible bond conversion period ended on February 15, 2026, which removed overhang pressure.

Which sector the next acquisitions will target remains uncertain until the company discloses them. The items to watch are the borrowing rate level, whether a special dividend is paid, and the disclosure of any new asset additions.

08

Bull factors

Earnings recovery and high operating margin

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from the June 2024 trough to KRW 32.4 billion in the year ended June 2025 and KRW 37.2 billion in the year ended June 2026. The operating margin stayed at a high level of 89.5% in the year ended June 2026. The lease-based model, with limited cost burden, is cited as the basis for this profitability.

Lower funding costs and a stronger balance sheet

NH Investment & Securities analyzed that the 2025 refinancing cut funding costs by 75 basis points. The debt ratio fell from 250.5% in the year ended June 2024 to 165.5% in the year ended June 2026. Press reports said LTV fell after the asset revaluation, which is read as widening financial room.

Long-term leases and a sponsor-linked pipeline

The triple-net structure, under which affiliates including SK Inc., SK Energy and SK hynix hold long-term master leases, is seen as lowering vacancy risk. SK-P Tower in Pangyo was added without a separate rights offering.

NH Investment & Securities assessed that the end of the convertible bond conversion period in February 2026 removed overhang pressure.

09

Bear factors

Reliance on borrowing and interest burden

Total liabilities of KRW 3.38 trillion exceed total equity of KRW 2.04 trillion. The KRW 216.4 billion loan disclosed in November 2025 is priced at the three-month CD rate plus 90 basis points. Press reports said interest costs rose quickly in the past, reducing distributable earnings.

Dilution concerns and a history of rights offerings

Around 2023, a rights offering equal to about 37.4% of the existing share count drew dilution criticism. The convertible bond conversion period ended in February 2026, but the rights offering history keeps shareholder burden concerns alive for future asset additions. The company has said it will reduce excess dividends to minimize the size of any rights offering.

Asset versatility and earnings volatility

When the water treatment center was added in 2023, a Samsung Securities analyst said the asset had low versatility and limited room for value appreciation.

As the 68.0% drop in profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the year ended June 2024 showed, earnings were sensitive to interest rates and interest costs.

10

Risk factors

Interest rates and funding costs

Borrowings are large relative to equity and loan rates track the CD rate, so a rate rise flows directly into interest costs. The 2025 refinancing lowered funding costs, but the rate on new borrowing could offset that gain.

Rising interest costs are a driver of swings in distributable earnings and profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

Sponsor concentration and conflicts of interest

Rental income is concentrated among SK group tenants, so affiliate performance and lease terms largely determine the company's profit. Because asset additions have been structured as sale-and-leasebacks with affiliates, questions about conflicts of interest in deal terms have been raised. Changes in the tenant mix are a variable for vacancy and rent adjustments.

Asset valuation and shifting share demand

Book asset values and LTV depend on the revaluation outcome, and a smaller revaluation would change how financial room is read. If ETF-driven inflows affected the share price, as reported in late 2025, changes in those flows could increase price volatility. Supply-and-demand shifts can occur independently of earnings.

Overall view

SK REIT's operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for two consecutive fiscal years after the June 2024 trough, and the operating margin was 89.5% in the year ended June 2026.

The debt ratio fell from 250.5% to 165.5% over the same period, and NH Investment & Securities said the 2025 refinancing cut funding costs by 75 basis points. Strengths include the earnings recovery, the long-term affiliate lease structure and the removal of convertible bond overhang.

Weaknesses include a borrowing structure in which total liabilities exceed total equity, interest costs sensitive to rate moves, and questions about past rights offerings and asset versatility. Market capitalization sits below the equity attributable to controlling shareholders.

The next points to check are the consolidated figures in the third-quarter 2026 periodic report, the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and dividend decision, and the first-quarter 2027 preliminary results.
