02

Business structure

SeoSanCo was founded in 1974 to produce and sell concrete products and listed on KOSDAQ in 2005. It now operates through two divisions, concrete products manufacturing and energy solutions. The concrete business covers ready-mix concrete and secondary products such as piles, hume pipes and utility poles.

In the first quarter of 2026, piles accounted for 50.50% of sales according to a news report, while ready-mix concrete contributed KRW 4.42 billion or 38.49%.

Pile sales reached KRW 5.8 billion, more than double the KRW 2.5 billion of the prior-year quarter, which points to a clearer contribution from the secondary product line. Ready-mix sales are concentrated in Gwangju and nearby areas, where the company reported a market share of 5.5% in the first half of 2025.

The same period also saw price competition described by the company as a sales price war. The energy solutions division focuses on EV chargers, a fast-changing field where one industry commentary says the company has not yet shown clear results.

Demand appears to come mainly from local construction sites, which differs from the order structure of large contractors.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 49.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 41.3 billion in 2023, a 16.1% decline, and then to KRW 35.7 billion in 2024, a 13.5% decline, before rebounding to KRW 45.1 billion in 2025, a 26.4% increase.

Over the same period the operating loss went from KRW 491 million in 2022 to KRW 1.58 billion in 2023, KRW 2.49 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.33 billion in 2025, a four-year run of losses with the loss widening each year. Operating margin moved from -1.0% in 2022 to -3.8% in 2023, -7.0% in 2024 and -7.4% in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.19 billion in 2022 and KRW 1.56 billion in 2023, a 31.5% increase, before turning into a net loss of KRW 476 million in 2024 and widening to a net loss of KRW 985 million in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests is a different figure, and the net losses of KRW 2.46 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.85 billion in 2025 were larger than the losses attributable to controlling shareholders.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 1.66 billion in 2022, KRW 4.17 billion in 2023, KRW 1.94 billion in 2024 and -KRW 2.05 billion in 2025.

In quarterly terms, the operating loss narrowed from KRW 387 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 331 million in the fourth quarter, and the first quarter of 2026 produced operating profit of KRW 308 million on revenue of KRW 11.5 billion.

The company attributed the improvement to a higher Gwangju-area ready-mix share and growth in secondary products such as piles.

However, the second quarter of 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 326 million on revenue of KRW 13 billion, and the third quarter showed an operating loss of KRW 376 million on revenue of KRW 12.6 billion, down 6.4% year on year. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totals only KRW 107 million.

05

Industry analysis

The ready-mix and secondary concrete product market served by SeoSanCo moves with the volume of construction work. In its first-half 2025 business commentary, the company cited a construction slowdown as a cause of weaker results.

The same commentary also pointed to a sales price war, higher raw material prices and shorter production working hours. These points show a regional market where pricing power is limited.

On the other side, the company's Gwangju-area ready-mix share was 5.5% in the first half of 2025, and a rise in that share was cited as a driver of revenue growth in the first quarter of 2026.

Among secondary products, pile sales more than doubled year on year in the first quarter of 2026, which makes piles the main axis of portfolio diversification.

Large construction groups such as Samsung C&T, Hyundai Engineering & Construction and DL E&C have overseas and plant-related order structures, so direct comparison with SeoSanCo is limited. Against regional ready-mix peers, changes in local share have a relatively larger effect on SeoSanCo's earnings. The EV charger market is changing fast, and the company has said it is working to maintain competitiveness.

06

Outlook

The next checkpoint for SeoSanCo is the 2026 annual report, and the key question is whether the annual operating result ends the run of losses that began in 2022. It is still unclear whether the first-quarter 2026 profit turn is temporary, given the operating losses in the second and third quarters.

Second-quarter revenue of KRW 13 billion was up 12.9% from the prior quarter, yet an operating loss of KRW 326 million continued, and third-quarter revenue fell to KRW 12.6 billion while the operating loss widened to KRW 376 million.

Whether pile sales growth and the higher Gwangju-area share are sustained is the most direct variable for operating margin. The direction of ready-mix selling prices and raw material costs should be read alongside that.

Total liabilities were KRW 5.5 billion at the end of 2025 with a debt-to-equity ratio of 5.4%, so near-term financial constraints are limited even if losses continue. The EV charger business has not yet contributed visible earnings, so whether its profit and loss improves is a separate line to follow.

In the 2026 annual report, the key comparisons are the full-year operating margin against the -7.4% recorded in 2025 and how fourth-quarter results follow the first-quarter profit. The first-quarter 2027 quarterly report will show whether the first-quarter 2026 profit turn repeats a year later.

08

Bull factors

Signs of narrowing losses and a profit turn

Operating loss narrowed to KRW 331 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 from KRW 387 million in the third quarter, and the first quarter of 2026 produced operating profit of KRW 308 million. The higher Gwangju-area ready-mix share was cited as a driver of revenue growth. Whether the quarterly results have passed a trough can be checked in later quarters.

Broader secondary product mix led by piles

First-quarter 2026 pile sales reached KRW 5.8 billion, more than double the prior-year level, and piles made up 50.50% of sales. A larger share from secondary products reduces dependence on ready-mix. Whether this growth holds across several quarters remains to be seen from a short data series.

Low leverage and a sizeable equity base

At the end of 2025 the debt-to-equity ratio was 5.4%, total liabilities were KRW 5.5 billion and total equity was KRW 101.9 billion. Operating cash flow was positive in 2023 and 2024. Low borrowing during the loss period gives the business room during a period of adjustment.

Bear factors

Four straight years of operating losses and margin erosion

SeoSanCo has posted operating losses in each year from 2022 to 2025, with the loss widening every year. Operating margin fell from -1.0% in 2022 to -7.4% in 2025. Losses widened even though revenue rose 26.4% in 2025, meaning sales growth did not carry through to profitability.

Losses returned in the second and third quarters of 2026

Operating losses were KRW 326 million in the second quarter and KRW 376 million in the third quarter of 2026, two consecutive loss quarters. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 12.6 billion fell 6.4% from a year earlier. The two loss quarters leave open whether the first-quarter profit reflected temporary factors.

Regional price competition and an unproven new business

In the first half of 2025, the company cited sales price competition and a construction slowdown as causes of weaker results. The EV charger business operates in a fast-changing field and one commentary says it has not yet shown clear results.

If price competition in the core business coincides with the slow new business, the pace of profit recovery could be limited.

Risk factors

Construction cycle and regional demand

Demand for ready-mix and secondary concrete products follows construction volumes. The company's main market is concentrated in Gwangju and nearby areas, so changes in local construction activity can feed directly into results. A construction slowdown was cited as a cause of weaker results in the first half of 2025.

Cost pressure and pricing competition

In the first half of 2025, the company pointed to higher raw material prices and shorter production working hours as cost pressures. When sales price competition is also intense, it is harder to pass cost increases through to customers. With margins already negative, small price changes can move earnings considerably.

New business and technology change

The EV charger business in the energy solutions segment operates in a field where technology changes quickly. One commentary says it has not yet produced clear results, so the timing of any return on investment is uncertain.

How much this segment weighs on consolidated results should be checked in the segment information of the annual report.

Overall view

SeoSanCo is a regionally focused building materials company whose main products are ready-mix concrete and concrete products such as piles. Revenue rose 26.4% in 2025, but the operating loss widened to KRW 3.33 billion. The first-quarter 2026 operating profit and the growth in pile sales are positive changes.

However, consecutive operating losses in the second and third quarters mean a profit trend has not yet taken hold. Strengths include a debt-to-equity ratio of 5.4%, a more diversified secondary product mix and quarters in which losses narrowed.

Weaknesses include four straight years of operating losses from 2022, a declining operating margin trend, a construction slowdown and regional price competition, and the lack of visible results in the EV charger business.

Valuation is limited on earnings-based measures and the share price sits at a premium to net assets, so whether profit improvement lasts is the starting point. The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, followed by the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report to see whether the profit turn repeats.
