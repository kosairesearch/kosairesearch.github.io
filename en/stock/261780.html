02

Business structure

Aribiolab is the vaccine and immunology specialist of the Aribio group, and it changed its name from Cha Vaccine Institute and listed under the current name in May 2026. Its most advanced pipeline is CVI-VZV-001, a recombinant shingles preventive vaccine.

In Phase 1 it confirmed safety and a 100% seroprotection rate, and in a Phase 2 study of 135 adults aged 50 and older it completed the second dose. The company plans to release topline results in the first quarter of 2027 before entering Phase 3.

The hepatitis B therapeutic vaccine CVI-HBV-002 significantly raised HBV-specific T-cell responses versus placebo in a Phase 2b study, and combination with siRNA therapies and expansion into preventive vaccines are under review.

The mRNA-based immuno-oncology candidate CTRNA-001 is a two-year national drug development project targeting head and neck squamous cell carcinoma first. A government-funded project is also developing the first domestic recombinant Japanese encephalitis vaccine.

The technology base is the in-house adjuvant platforms L-pampo and Lipo-pam, and Lipo-pam is the only domestic company technology listed in the CEPI vaccine adjuvant library. Commercialization centers on joint research with global vaccine developers, adjuvant supply and licensing.

Domestically, the company signed an MOU with Samjin Pharm on joint development and commercialization. A July 2026 report said the articles of incorporation were amended to add cosmetics manufacturing and sales to the business purpose, as a way to broaden revenue.

However, disclosed revenue is only in the KRW 100 million range annually, so the vaccine-focused business has not yet translated into meaningful sales.

04

Earnings analysis

Across the annual disclosed results, revenue rose from KRW 179 million in 2022 to KRW 294 million in 2023 and KRW 371 million in 2024, then fell to KRW 159 million in 2025. Of the 2022 to 2025 period, 2024 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest, and 2025 revenue fell 57.2% year on year.

The operating loss was KRW 6.47 billion in 2022, KRW 6.42 billion in 2023, KRW 7.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.3 billion in 2025, so the loss narrowed in 2023 and widened in 2024 and 2025. The company has posted losses for four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025.

The operating margin was -8,997.2% in 2025, showing that revenue does little to offset the operating loss.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.72 billion, KRW 1.75 billion, KRW 10.1 billion and KRW 16 billion for 2022 through 2025, and net loss including non-controlling interests was disclosed separately at KRW 6.72 billion, KRW 1.75 billion, KRW 10.1 billion and KRW 16 billion for the same years.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 83.0% in 2022 and 27.4% in 2023 to 70.4% in 2024 and 151.1% in 2025. At end-2025, total equity was KRW 11.9 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 17.9 billion.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 5.19 billion in 2022 and widened to -KRW 11 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, the operating loss was KRW 3.42 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 5.39 billion in the fourth quarter, narrowed to KRW 1.71 billion in the first quarter of 2026, rose to KRW 2.46 billion in the second quarter and narrowed to KRW 1.67 billion in the third quarter.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.76 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 1.96 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and totaled KRW 12.6 billion over the most recent four quarters.

Apart from KRW 173 million in the first quarter of 2026, no quarterly revenue appears in the disclosed results. Funding has filled part of the gap: Aribio agreed in September 2026 to invest KRW 15 billion in Aribiolab's KRW 18 billion third-party rights offering. The cost items behind the widening losses can be traced in the periodic report's expense breakdown.

05

Industry analysis

The company says domestic recombinant shingles vaccines are entirely imported, which gives localization significant meaning. Recent epidemiological studies linking shingles vaccination to a lower risk of dementia have drawn attention to the relationship between immunity and neurodegenerative disease.

That link, however, does not directly prove a preventive effect against Alzheimer's disease. Within the same group, Aribio completed the global Phase 3 dosing of its oral Alzheimer's drug AR1001 in 1,535 patients across 13 countries.

Topline results for AR1001 are slated for disclosure at CTAD 2026 in Boston on November 16, 2026. Among vaccine developers, Aribiolab's differentiator is its adjuvant platform, and its listing in the CEPI library works as an external technology credential.

SK Bioscience (SK Bioscience), a listed company focused on vaccines in the same sector, can serve as a comparison point. The gap in revenue scale and clinical stage is wide, so the competitive position will depend on clinical results and whether licensing deals are completed. The room for domestic supply of shingles vaccines is one reason the clinical outcome carries weight.

06

Outlook

The nearest checkpoint is the topline result of the shingles vaccine's Phase 2 study. The company plans to release topline results in the first quarter of 2027 and then enter Phase 3. The hepatitis B therapeutic vaccine is under review for combination with siRNA therapies and for expansion into preventive use.

The mRNA immuno-oncology candidate research runs over two years and incorporates AI-based drug design. On commercialization, the company is pursuing joint development and licensing with global companies based on its adjuvant platform.

On revenue, the company has said the need to expand sales grows from 2027, and it is reviewing businesses beyond vaccine R&D that could generate sales. On governance, Aribio agreed to acquire a convertible bond from Aribiolab, and if the conversion right is exercised the two companies would hold shares in each other.

Once the rights offering payment is completed, the largest shareholder is set to change to Aribio. Some observers are also watching for a possible merger between the two companies.

However, reports note that after the largest-shareholder change there are constraints on a near-term merger, and an absorption merger would take eight to nine months or more. Depending on the group's AR1001 Phase 3 results, valuation and future merger and listing strategies could change.

Overall view

Aribiolab is broadening its pipeline with the completed Phase 2 dosing of its shingles vaccine, an mRNA immuno-oncology program and a Japanese encephalitis vaccine, and its platform has external validation through its CEPI library listing.

However, disclosed revenue in 2025 was only KRW 159 million while the operating loss widened to KRW 14.3 billion, and five consecutive quarters of losses plus KRW 12.6 billion in net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters highlight financial strain.

The third-quarter 2026 operating loss narrowed from a year earlier, a sign of easing, but the pace at which the business converts into sales remains a challenge.

Financially, operating cash outflows widened each year and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 151.1% in 2025, so the company continues to rely on external funding such as rights offerings and convertible bonds.

On governance, the group is reshaping into a cross-shareholding structure through Aribio's bond purchase and rights offering participation, and a merger has been raised as a possibility.

Once the group's AR1001 Phase 3 results are released, governance and merger or listing strategies could shift, which is another variable. The next checkpoints are the first-quarter 2027 topline for the shingles vaccine and the revenue and operating loss structure in the 2026 annual report.
