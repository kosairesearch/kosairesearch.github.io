Exports Grow, but Q3 Swings to an Operating Loss

KG Mobility restored profitability in 2025, but its Q3 2026 operating loss has made the durability of that recovery the next question to track.

02

Business structure

KG Mobility is a vehicle maker centered on its Pyeongtaek headquarters, with SUVs and pickup trucks as its core products. Its main lineup includes the Torres, REXTON, KORANDO and MUSSO, and it has recently been reshaping the range to expand eco-friendly models including hybrids and EVs.

Cumulative sales for January to September 2026 were 81,033 units (preliminary), down 1.6% from a year earlier. Over the same period, domestic sales fell 6.8% to 28,816 units, while exports rose 1.5% to 52,217 units. As a result, the export share of total sales rose from 62.5% to 64.4%.

Türkiye is the core export market, with sales of 15,964 units in January to September 2026, up 41.2%. Eco-friendly vehicle exports reached 25,975 units, up 130.5%, and their share of exports widened from 21.9% to 49.7%.

In September 2026 the company started semi-knocked-down (SKD) kit business in Ethiopia and is expanding it to Saudi Arabia, Vietnam and Cambodia. In competition, the MUSSO has been positioned against KIA's Tasman pickup.

As a mid-sized maker smaller than Hyundai Motor and KIA, the company's results are heavily affected by the sales trend of each new model.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 3.42 trillion in 2022 to KRW 3.74 trillion in 2023 (up 9.1% year on year), KRW 3.91 trillion in 2024 (up 4.5%) and KRW 4.3 trillion in 2025 (up 10.2%), and it has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. Among the years from 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest.

Operating profit swung from an operating loss of KRW 112 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.5 billion in 2023, fell to KRW 1.43 billion in 2024 (down 88.6%), and recovered to KRW 36.2 billion in 2025 (up 2,423.2%). The operating margin was -3.3% in 2022, 0.3% in 2023, 0.0% in 2024 and 0.8% in 2025, staying around 1% or below.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a loss of KRW 60.1 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 8.92 billion in 2023, then KRW 33.8 billion in 2024 (up 279.2%) and KRW 46.6 billion in 2025 (up 37.6%), growing for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

At end-2025, total equity was KRW 1.48 trillion and total liabilities were KRW 1.88 trillion, and the debt ratio fell from 143.4% in 2023 to 127.0% in 2025. Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 251.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.1 billion in 2023, KRW 131.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 117.3 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit of KRW 21 billion in Q4 2025 was followed by KRW 9.59 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 5.22 billion in Q2 2026, before Q3 2026 swung to an operating loss of KRW 50.4 billion. Revenue in Q3 2026 was KRW 1.01 trillion, down 17.2% from a year earlier.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 43 billion in Q4 2025 to a loss of KRW 154 billion in Q3 2026, and the last four quarters total a loss of KRW 68.8 billion.

For the same quarter, the preliminary results released on November 4, 2026 (separate financial statements basis) showed an operating loss of KRW 29.8 billion and revenue of KRW 994.1 billion, which differ from the regular report figures used here.

The company attributed the weakness mainly to fixed cost burdens from production line maintenance ahead of the SE10 launch and foreign currency translation losses from the weaker won.

05

Industry analysis

In the domestic vehicle market, KG Mobility is a mid-sized brand competing with large makers such as Hyundai Motor and KIA. Domestic sales in Q3 2026 fell 44.4% year on year to 7,010 units (preliminary), and the company cited weaker domestic demand as a factor behind its results.

Exports in the same quarter rose 4.6% to 17,264 units, accounting for more than 70% of total sales (preliminary). In this structure, a stronger won lowers both won-converted revenue and the won value of foreign-currency assets, so exchange rate moves have a large effect.

The expansion of foreign currency translation losses in Q3 is linked to this point. In the pickup segment, KIA's Tasman is cited as the competing model for the MUSSO.

In electrification, KG Mobility is building its eco-friendly lineup around technology partnerships with Chinese manufacturers; as of a June 2025 announcement, it was pursuing ties with BYD and Chery Automobile.

As domestic makers roll out multiple new models in 2026, the performance of KG Mobility's new models is seen as key to regaining share. The demand outlook reflects a mix of weaker domestic sales and rising overseas exports.

06

Outlook

The company's stated direction is the launch of the SE10, expanded exports and a stronger domestic response, and a return to profitability through cost structure improvements (based on the preliminary results released on November 4, 2026).

The SE10 was introduced in June 2025 as a mid-to-large SUV to be launched in 2026, reportedly developed on Chery's T2X platform. The Q3 2026 filing linked production line maintenance and lower output to higher fixed costs, so whether utilization recovers after new model production begins is the next point to watch.

The KD business started in Ethiopia in September 2026 and is being expanded to Saudi Arabia, Vietnam and Cambodia.

In September 2023 the company set a 2026 global sales target of 320,000 units, including 220,000 finished vehicles and 100,000 KD units, but cumulative sales for January to September 2026 were 81,033 units (preliminary), well short of that goal.

In June 2025 the company said it would roll out seven eco-friendly models sequentially from 2026 through 2030. The Q300 pickup was described in a December 2025 report as targeting a launch in Q1 2026.

The effect of cost structure improvements will show up in future regular reports as changes in operating margin relative to revenue. The next checkpoints are the annual operating margin in the 2026 annual report and the operating profit trend in the Q1 2027 quarterly report.

08

Bull factors

Export and eco-friendly vehicle growth

The export share rose to 64.4% in January to September 2026, and eco-friendly vehicle exports grew 130.5% to 25,975 units (preliminary). Türkiye sales rose 41.2% to 15,964 units. Export growth partly offset weaker domestic sales, lifting cumulative revenue 5.8% to KRW 3.31 trillion (preliminary).

Recovery in annual earnings capacity

Operating profit improved from an operating loss of KRW 112 billion in 2022 to KRW 36.2 billion in 2025, and operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 117.3 billion. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders grew in both 2024 and 2025. The debt ratio fell from 143.4% in 2023 to 127.0% in 2025.

The SE10 and cost structure improvements

The company has said it aims to restore profitability through the SE10 launch and cost structure improvements. The SE10 is reported to be a mid-to-large SUV built on Chery's T2X platform. Once production line maintenance is complete, the impact of fixed cost burdens on results could change.

Bear factors

Q3 2026 swing to losses

The Q3 2026 regular report shows an operating loss of KRW 50.4 billion and a loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 154 billion. The same quarter a year earlier had an operating profit. The last four quarters total a loss of KRW 68.8 billion, a sharp change in the earnings trend since the Q4 2025 profit.

Domestic sales decline and lower overall volume

Q3 2026 sales fell 16.6% to 24,274 units, and domestic sales dropped 44.4% to 7,010 units (preliminary). Cumulative sales for January to September also fell 1.6% to 81,033 units. As domestic demand weakens, the company relies more heavily on exports.

Fixed cost and currency pressure

The company explained that fixed costs from production line maintenance and foreign currency translation losses from a weaker dollar weighed on Q3 results (based on preliminary figures). With a high export share, a stronger won reduces won-converted revenue. If won strength continues, the won-converted benefit of export growth could weaken.

Risk factors

New model launch and production timing

If the SE10 launch or the completion of production line maintenance is delayed, fixed cost pressure could persist. Lower Q3 production volume is already reflected in results, and additional costs during the shift to mass production are possible. If new model sales fall short of expectations, the recovery in domestic sales could be slower.

Export concentration and currency exposure

The export share rose to 64.4% in January to September 2026 (preliminary), and the weight of Türkiye sales has grown. Concentration in a single market could amplify the effect of local demand or policy changes. Won exchange rate moves affect both revenue and the valuation of foreign-currency assets at the same time.

Financial burden and accumulated losses

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 1.88 trillion, above total equity of KRW 1.48 trillion, with a debt ratio of 127.0%. As losses of KRW 68.8 billion accumulated over the last four quarters, capital headroom is narrowing. If losses continue, room for financial restructuring could be limited.

Overall view

KG Mobility restored its earnings capacity from an operating loss of KRW 112 billion in 2022 to an operating profit of KRW 36.2 billion in 2025, but Q3 2026 returned it to losses, with an operating loss of KRW 50.4 billion and a loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 154 billion.

On the positive side, the export share is rising, eco-friendly vehicle exports grew 130.5% (preliminary), and the debt ratio fell from 143.4% to 127.0%. On the negative side, domestic sales fell 44.4% (preliminary), fixed costs rose with production line maintenance, and currency translation losses weighed on results.

The last four quarters total a loss of KRW 68.8 billion, showing that the earnings direction has changed since the Q4 2025 profit. With the PER not calculable, valuation is being compared mainly on asset and shareholder return metrics such as the PBR and dividend level.

The SE10 launch and cost structure improvements the company has outlined have not yet appeared in reported results. In short, the picture is one of recovery attempts set against renewed losses, and the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report will indicate which way it moves.
