Report overview

Narrowing Losses at an MVNO Under Capital Restructuring

FreeT (formerly Inscobee) is an MVNO operator whose operating profit turned positive in 2025 but has posted operating losses for three straight quarters through Q3 2026, and a capital reduction to offset accumulated deficits passed a shareholder vote in November 2026.

  1. 1

    Revenue concentration: MVNO services account for 97.03% of sales, so results are closely tied to subscriber competition and network leasing terms.

  2. 2

    Annual trend: revenue rose from KRW 76.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 105.6 billion in 2025, and operating profit returned to KRW 4.08 billion in 2025, while the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 41.6 billion.

  3. 3

    Quarterly flow: operating losses have continued for three consecutive quarters since Q1 2026, narrowing to KRW 42.76 million in Q3 2026.

  4. 4

    Balance sheet: the debt-to-equity ratio reached 276.3% at end-2025, up from 60.5% in 2022; total equity was KRW 16.1 billion, of which KRW 15.2 billion is attributable to controlling shareholders.

  5. 5

    Capital restructuring: a 5-to-1 reverse stock merger to offset accumulated deficits passed at the November 9, 2026 extraordinary shareholder meeting, alongside a change in the largest shareholder and fundraising plans during 2026.

02

Business structure

FreeT was founded in 1970 and listed on the KOSPI in 1985; it changed its corporate name from Inscobee to FreeT effective June 19, 2026. Its core business is the MVNO service, which leases wireless networks from the three Korean mobile network operators.

Company data compiled as of March 2026 shows revenue mix of MVNO 97.03%, smart grid 1.46%, cosmetics and other distribution 1.17%, and watches 0.34%. Most sales therefore come from telecom services, while the other segments are small.

Its domestic MVNO subsidiary is a 100%-owned telecom reseller and the core consolidated entity. The smart grid business has quarterly revenue that moves with public procurement and delivery schedules. Cosmetics and other distribution sales were split 27.10% domestic and 72.90% export in 2025.

Overseas, the company signed an MVNO agreement with Telus of Canada in 2025 and established its Canadian subsidiary in May 2025. Competitively, the MVNO business is exposed to pricing and marketing competition from the subsidiaries of the three major carriers.

The CEO changed from Yoo In-soo to Song Byung-kwon in May 2026, and the company had 37 employees as of June 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 76.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 99.8 billion in 2023 (up 30.2% year on year), KRW 100.5 billion in 2024 (up 0.7%), and KRW 105.6 billion in 2025 (up 5.0%), marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025. 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating results swung each year: an operating loss of KRW 1.39 billion in 2022, operating profit of KRW 447 million in 2023 (a turn to profit), an operating loss of KRW 3.79 billion in 2024 (a turn to loss), and operating profit of KRW 4.08 billion in 2025 (a turn to profit).

The operating margin was -1.8% in 2022, 0.4% in 2023, -3.8% in 2024, and 3.9% in 2025, the highest of the four years.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 7.23 billion in 2022, a loss of KRW 3.02 billion in 2023 (losses continued and widened), a profit of KRW 298 million in 2024 (a turn to profit), and a loss of KRW 4.16 billion in 2025 (a turn to loss).

The gap between 2025 operating profit of KRW 4.08 billion and the controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 41.6 billion arises below the operating line, and the breakdown is a point to reconcile in the next regular report.

Operating cash flow turned positive at KRW 4.75 billion in 2025 after a outflow of KRW 6.33 billion in 2024.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 26.1 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 26.5 billion in Q4 2025, dropped to KRW 24.2 billion in Q1 2026 (down 8.5% quarter on quarter), and then rose to KRW 24.8 billion in Q2 and KRW 26.2 billion in Q3 2026, marking two consecutive quarters of growth.

Operating profit fell from KRW 2.72 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 942 million in Q4 2025 (down 65.4% quarter on quarter) to operating losses of KRW 1.51 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 1.31 billion in Q2, and KRW 42.76 million in Q3 2026, three straight loss quarters.

In Q1 2026 the company posted a controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 1.1 billion despite the operating loss, and the controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters totaled KRW 18.7 billion.

A company data site attributed the first-half 2026 deterioration to aggressive marketing by the three carriers' subsidiaries and intensifying competition.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's MVNO market sells lower-priced mobile services on networks leased from the three national mobile network operators. FreeT operates an integrated MVNO service on the wireless networks of SK Telecom, KT and LG Uplus, so leasing terms and wholesale costs directly affect profitability.

A company data site summarized the first-half 2026 results as showing that MVNO demand has defensive characteristics, but that aggressive marketing by the carriers' subsidiaries and intensifying competition weighed on results.

Demand may be defensive, yet rising selling costs make it hard to convert that demand into operating profit. In scale, the company is far smaller than the three carriers, which are both network suppliers and competitors.

Diversification is limited: smart grid at 1.46%, cosmetics and other distribution at 1.17%, and watches at 0.34% of sales mean the MVNO competitive environment dominates results. The smart grid business adds quarterly volatility because it depends on public procurement and delivery timing.

The overseas MVNO push in Canada is under way, but the Canadian subsidiary's revenue contribution should be checked in the regular report's segment disclosures. Judging where the cycle stands would require subscriber trends to be disclosed alongside the financial statements.

06

Outlook

The nearest items to track are the capital restructuring steps. On September 17, 2026 the company decided on a 5-to-1 reverse stock merger to offset accumulated deficits, and the proposal passed at the extraordinary shareholder meeting on November 9, 2026, after which paid-in capital declines.

Third-party share issuances were carried out in April and May 2026.

A Seoul Economic Daily report said the payment date for a KRW 4 billion rights offering had been changed and the size reduced from KRW 5 billion, and that a convertible bond plan was scaled down from an earlier announced KRW 10 billion to about KRW 5.1 billion.

The same report noted that a change of 50% or more from the original announcement could lead to designation as an insincere-disclosure company. On the growth side, the 2025 MVNO agreement with Telus in Canada and an AI-based customer service rollout are the highlighted initiatives.

In June 2026 the largest shareholder disclosed on-market purchases of 915,000 shares, on a basis before the merger. The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report, whether operating profit can sustain a turn to profit, and the outcome of the rights offering and convertible bond issuance.

Bull factors

Return to operating profit and narrower losses

Operating profit returned to KRW 4.08 billion in 2025, and the 3.9% operating margin was the highest of 2022 to 2025. The operating loss narrowed from KRW 1.31 billion in Q2 2026 to KRW 42.76 million in Q3 2026. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 26.2 billion was flat, up 0.2% year on year.

Three consecutive years of revenue growth

Revenue grew for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025 to KRW 105.6 billion, the largest of 2022 to 2025. It has risen for two straight quarters since the Q1 2026 low. The revenue base is supported as long as the MVNO subscriber base holds.

Overseas expansion and diversification attempts

In 2025 the company signed an MVNO agreement with Telus and opened a Canadian subsidiary to pursue overseas MVNO growth. It is also introducing an AI-based customer service to improve operating efficiency. In 2025, 72.90% of cosmetics and other distribution sales were exports, indicating some overseas channels.

Bear factors

Three consecutive quarters of operating losses

The company posted operating losses of KRW 1.51 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 1.31 billion in Q2 and KRW 42.76 million in Q3, three straight loss quarters. Operating profit had fallen 65.4% quarter on quarter to KRW 942 million in Q4 2025. Controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four quarters totaled KRW 18.7 billion.

Accumulated deficits and a capital reduction

The controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 41.6 billion in 2025 marked a sharp swing from a KRW 2.98 billion profit in 2024. The debt-to-equity ratio reached 276.3% at end-2025, up from 60.5% in 2022. A 5-to-1 reverse stock merger to offset deficits passed at the November 9, 2026 shareholder meeting.

Financing and governance uncertainty

A Seoul Economic Daily report said the payment date of a rights offering was changed and its size reduced from KRW 5 billion to KRW 4 billion, while a convertible bond was scaled down from an announced KRW 10 billion. An extraordinary shareholder meeting once failed to reach a quorum and its agenda was left pending. A company data site counted two largest-shareholder changes within 180 days.

Risk factors

Competition and wholesale cost risk

The MVNO business is exposed to aggressive marketing and pricing competition from the three carriers' subsidiaries. Higher promotional spending can leave operating profit flat even as subscribers grow. Changes to network leasing terms or wholesale fees would directly affect margins.

Financial and funding risk

Total liabilities of KRW 44.4 billion at end-2025 exceed total equity of KRW 16.1 billion. The rights offering and convertible bond were scaled down and their payment timing changed. Conversion of the bonds into shares would add to the share count and dilute existing holders.

Concentration and governance risk

MVNO accounts for 97.03% of revenue, so changes in one competitive environment drive overall results. Management and board changes, along with a change in the largest shareholder, occurred in 2026. The contribution from smart grid and overseas operations needs to be confirmed in regular reports.

Overall view

FreeT is an MVNO operator, with MVNO services accounting for 97.03% of revenue. Annual revenue grew for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and operating profit returned to KRW 4.08 billion in 2025.

However, operating losses have continued for three straight quarters from Q1 through Q3 2026, and the controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters totaled KRW 18.7 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio was high at 276.3% at end-2025, and a reverse stock merger to offset deficits has passed.

Funding uncertainty remains, as the rights offering and convertible bond were scaled down and their timing changed. Bullish factors include the return to operating profit, narrower operating losses, and overseas MVNO agreements such as Canada.

Bearish factors include marketing competition from the carriers' subsidiaries, accumulated net losses, and potential dilution during the capital restructuring.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report, where the continuation of operating losses and the drivers below operating profit will be central.
