Report overview

Subsidiary-Driven Holding Company: Operating Profit Down Three Straight Quarters

SNT Holdings grew operating profit to KRW 300.7 billion in 2025, but operating profit has fallen for three straight quarters from the first quarter of 2026, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders has declined for four straight quarters, making subsidiary earnings the key variable.

  1. 1

    Annual revenue for 2025 rose 22.8% to KRW 2.24 trillion, and operating profit rose 30.1% to KRW 300.7 billion, lifting the operating margin to 13.4%.

  2. 2

    Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 142.9 billion in 2024 and fell 9.1% to KRW 129.9 billion in 2025.

  3. 3

    Third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 31.6% year on year to KRW 45.1 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 80.8% to KRW 7.29 billion.

  4. 4

    Cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 was KRW 1.46 trillion and cumulative operating profit was KRW 173.7 billion, both below the same period of 2025, based on the November 2, 2026 disclosure as reported.

  5. 5

02

Business structure

SNT Holdings was founded in 1979 as Samyoung Machinery Industry and became a pure holding company in 2008.

As of March 31, 2026, its main subsidiaries are SNT Dynamics, SNT Motiv and a plant-equipment unit that makes heat exchangers, with SNT Gulf for Industry as a key sub-subsidiary, and the group has 20 consolidated subsidiaries in total.

Operations are split into three segments: vehicle parts, industrial equipment and other. SNT Dynamics covers automotive parts such as axles and transmission products, defense powertrains, guns and artillery systems, and machine tools. Customers named in reports include commercial-vehicle and auto-parts makers.

In March 2026 SNT Dynamics signed a KRW 510.48 billion transmission assembly supply contract with Hanwha Aerospace, running from March 23, 2026 to December 31, 2028, for domestic supply.

The holding company's stakes are 56.41% of SNT Dynamics (as of June 30, 2026), 51.11% of SNT Motiv (as of November 6, 2026) and 51.20% of the plant-equipment unit (as of November 9, 2026).

In April 2026 it transferred a 13.65% stake in Smac and its stake in SNT Robotics to SNT Motiv, aiming to build an integrated structure around robotics and smart-factory businesses. Some media have noted that the mix of defense, auto parts and energy resembles that of Hanwha Group.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results from 2022 to 2025 show operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders rising substantially.

Revenue went from KRW 1.63 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.9 trillion in 2023 (up 16.3%), fell to KRW 1.82 trillion in 2024 (down 4.0%), and then rose to KRW 2.24 trillion in 2025 (up 22.8%), the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit went from KRW 144.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 179 billion in 2023 (up 23.7%) and KRW 231.2 billion in 2024 (up 29.1%), reaching KRW 300.7 billion in 2025 (up 30.1%), and it has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

The operating margin rose from 8.9% in 2022 to 9.4% in 2023, 12.7% in 2024 and 13.4% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 51.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 87.3 billion in 2023 (up 69.9%) and KRW 142.9 billion in 2024 (up 63.6%), peaking that year, before falling 9.1% to KRW 129.9 billion in 2025.

The fact that net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell in 2025 while operating profit rose 30.1% is a point to examine.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 219.7 billion in 2025, above the KRW 129.9 billion attributable to controlling shareholders, and the gap reflects the share held by outside shareholders of the subsidiaries.

In 2024, one-off provision reversals at subsidiary SNT Dynamics were reported as part of its operating profit. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 160.5 billion in 2022 and KRW 210.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 131.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 120.1 billion in 2025, declining for two straight years.

At the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 2.39 trillion, equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.38 trillion, and total liabilities were KRW 869.1 billion, with the debt ratio at 36.3% against 32.8% in 2024.

Quarterly results peaked in the fourth quarter of 2025, with revenue of KRW 691 billion and operating profit of KRW 97.4 billion, and operating profit then fell for three straight quarters, to KRW 64.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 64 billion in the second and KRW 45.1 billion in the third.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 454.6 billion was down 12.1% year on year.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 38 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 31.8 billion, KRW 16.1 billion, KRW 13.5 billion and KRW 7.29 billion in the third quarter of 2026, declining for four straight quarters, and the last four quarters total KRW 68.8 billion.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, controlling shareholders' net income was KRW 31.8 billion against operating profit of KRW 97.4 billion, while in the third quarter of 2026 it was KRW 7.29 billion against operating profit of KRW 45.1 billion.

Subsidiary SNT Dynamics reported third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 6.07 billion, down 69.5% year on year, and cumulative operating profit of KRW 44.5 billion for January to September, down 33.7%.

Industry analysis

The group's core businesses are defense, auto parts and energy equipment. A domestic brokerage noted in a recent report that the share price correction at SNT Motiv came from a decline in valuation tied to a slowdown in physical AI momentum across the auto sector, rather than from a deterioration in earnings capacity.

SNT Motiv cancelled all of its treasury shares on September 28, 2026, equal to 10.2% of its free float. In defense, the transmission assembly contract with Hanwha Aerospace runs through December 31, 2028 and serves as a reference point for supply volume.

A financial news outlet has said the group's mix of defense, machinery, energy and finance resembles that of Hanwha Group.

SNT Dynamics' cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 rose 6.4% to KRW 519.8 billion, while its cumulative operating profit fell 33.7% to KRW 44.5 billion, so margins narrowed as sales grew. Its third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 143.6 billion was down 5.4% year on year.

The combination of defense contract wins and a slowdown in auto-parts demand is pulling the subsidiary's results in different directions.

Outlook

SNT Holdings published its voluntary corporate value enhancement plan on March 3, 2026. The direction it has set out centers on SNT Motiv, linking Smac, the machine-tool affiliate and SNT Robotics into one integrated structure and widening its reach into robotics and smart-factory businesses.

SNT Dynamics' contract with Hanwha Aerospace runs through the end of 2028, which sets the supply base for that period. On the shareholder-return side, SNT Dynamics disclosed plans to cancel 8,334,579 treasury shares, equal to 25.1% of its issued shares and about KRW 248.4 billion.

From November 6 to 10, 2026 the holding company bought 207,000 SNT Motiv shares on the open market, and from November 10 to 11, 2026 it bought 106,000 shares of the plant-equipment unit, lifting its stake there to 51.20% as of November 9, 2026.

In January 2026 the company issued convertible bonds and exchangeable bonds, with the exchangeable bonds referencing its stakes in SNT Dynamics and the plant-equipment unit, a move described as strengthening the balance sheet and securing funds for new technology investment.

The 2026 annual report will show the full-year operating margin and net income attributable to controlling shareholders. Fourth-quarter 2026 results have not yet been filed, and whether operating profit extends its three-quarter decline into the fourth quarter is the next point to check.

Risk factors

Dependence on Subsidiary Earnings

The holding company's results depend on SNT Dynamics, SNT Motiv and the plant-equipment unit. Auto-parts results move with sector sentiment and vehicle demand, while the defense business depends on performance under the Hanwha Aerospace contract.

In the third quarter of 2026, a decline in SNT Dynamics' operating profit and a decline in controlling shareholders' net income appeared in the same period.

Governance and Stake Transactions

In the April 2026 transfer of Smac shares, reports said 5,197,192 shares held by a major shareholder were included in the transaction, differing from the earlier disclosure. Transfers of affiliate stakes and exchangeable bond structures can affect minority shareholder interests and governance.

Open-market purchases at SNT Motiv and the plant-equipment unit are proceeding alongside these ownership changes.

Balance Sheet and Funding Flows

At the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 2.39 trillion and total liabilities were KRW 869.1 billion. The January 2026 bond issuance, subsidiary treasury share cancellations and open-market purchases at the holding company have run in the same period, so the funding flows need to be read together. Operating cash flow's two-year decline also bears on the funding base for dividends and share repurchases.

Overall view

SNT Holdings is a holding company whose operating profit grew for three consecutive years from 2022 through 2025. Revenue fell 4.0% in 2024 before rising 22.8% to KRW 2.24 trillion in 2025, and the 2025 operating margin of 13.4% was the highest of the period.

However, net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 9.1% in 2025, and in 2026 operating profit has declined for three straight quarters while that net income has declined for four. Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 173.7 billion, down 14.57% from the same period of 2025.

Positive factors include the KRW 510.48 billion Hanwha Aerospace contract at SNT Dynamics, subsidiary treasury share cancellations and open-market purchases by the holding company.

Negative factors include lower operating profit at SNT Dynamics, two straight years of declining operating cash flow, and the convertible and exchangeable bond issuance in January 2026. The price-to-book ratio sits below 1x, and whether subsidiary earnings recover will decide the direction of profit. The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report and the preliminary first-quarter 2027 results.
