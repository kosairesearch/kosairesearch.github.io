02

Business structure

Incar Financial Service is a corporate insurance agency (GA) founded in 2007 that compares and brokers policies from multiple insurers and earns sales commissions.

As of June 2026 it had 942 branches nationwide and 24,724 financial advisors (FA), and it has been pursuing a shift from insurance sales toward a comprehensive asset-management model since its 2022 KOSDAQ listing.

The core asset is the advisor network, which stood at 25,291 at the end of September 2026, up 4,640 or 22.5% from the end of 2025. iM Securities describes the structure as a mix of direct-employment and entrepreneurial-agent models, which it contrasts with affiliate-type GAs favoring in-house products and alliance-type GAs with weaker control.

The operating base includes the in-house sales-support system IIMS, advisor training programs and a prospect database supply. In May 2026 the company completed the absorption merger of a smaller GA affiliate and declared a single operating structure.

Its subsidiaries cover online sales support, financial consulting and mortgage brokerage. On a standalone basis for the first quarter of 2026, the company cited wider sales of long-term non-life and life policies as a driver of revenue growth. In terms of position, iM Securities estimated the company's advisor count to be roughly second among all GAs.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, sales rose from KRW 401.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 556.8 billion in 2023, KRW 832.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.02 trillion in 2025, increasing for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. Growth was 49.5% in 2024, the largest jump in the period, and 22.8% in 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 27.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 94.8 billion in 2025, also increasing for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, but growth slowed to 9.9% in 2025.

The operating margin climbed from 6.8% in 2022 to 8.4% in 2023 and 10.4% in 2024 before easing to 9.3% in 2025, because sales growth of 22.8% ran well ahead of operating profit growth of 9.9% that year.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 20.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 72.3 billion in 2025, with a 110.2% jump in 2024 marking the strongest recovery in the period.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 71 billion in 2025 and should be read separately from the controlling-shareholder figure. Operating cash flow moved from -KRW 895 million in 2022 to KRW 75.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 56.2 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 414.0% in 2023 to 367.0% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 214.4 billion and total liabilities were KRW 787 billion. Quarterly sales increased for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, reaching KRW 347.8 billion.

Operating profit dipped to KRW 24 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and recovered to KRW 32.3 billion in both the second and third quarters of 2026, while profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 82.7 billion.

Preliminary cumulative results for the first three quarters of 2026, announced by the company on November 16, 2026, show sales of KRW 987.8 billion, operating profit of KRW 90.5 billion and net profit of KRW 65.5 billion; these are unaudited preliminary figures. The company said the cumulative sales figure was a record for the first three quarters.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic GA market is structured so that earnings depend heavily on insurance sales commission rules. iM Securities noted that when the 1200% commission rule was first introduced, revenue at other GAs fell while Incar Financial Service's sales and profit rose (report dated September 10, 2026).

The same broker expects the company to defend earnings reasonably well even if commission rules are tightened further.

The company said in its April 2026 management strategy announcement that it runs a dedicated task force to prepare for a commission reform plan. iM Securities expects no major issues for earnings or cash flow through 2027, but it sees possible partial system-related shocks around 2028, when commissions currently paid in the second contract year shift to the third and fourth years.

The advisor count rose from 7,185 at the end of 2016 to 23,602 in the first half of 2026, according to iM Securities. On the product side, wider sales of long-term non-life and life policies were cited as supporting first-quarter 2026 results.

Competitively, the company combines direct-employment and entrepreneurial-agent models, which iM Securities contrasts with affiliate-type GAs concentrated in in-house products and alliance-type GAs with weaker control.

In cycle terms, the company is in a phase of advisor network expansion, and changes to commission rules are widely seen as the next inflection point.

06

Outlook

The company set 2026 management targets of 30,000 financial advisors, KRW 1.2 trillion in sales, KRW 100 billion in net profit and KRW 100 billion in new long-term contracts, according to an April 2026 announcement.

Preliminary cumulative sales of KRW 987.8 billion for the first three quarters of 2026 were reported as 82.3% of the company's annual sales target. Financial advisors stood at 25,291 at the end of September 2026, still short of the 30,000 goal.

The company plans to expand training for newly recruited advisors and the supply of prospect databases. On internal controls, it said it will add compliance specialists to strengthen field monitoring and proactive risk response.

Total equity at the end of the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 257.5 billion, up 20.1% from KRW 214.4 billion at the end of 2025, according to the company. iM Securities projected standalone net profit of KRW 83.9 billion for 2026, up 22.1% from the prior year (report dated September 10, 2026).

The 2026 annual report, the next regular filing, will show how close full-year sales came to the target and whether year-end advisor numbers reached the goal. Responses to the commission reform and the shift of deferred commissions around 2028 remain medium-term variables.

08

Bull factors

Advisor network underpins earnings

Financial advisors reached 25,291 at the end of September 2026, up 22.5% from the end of 2025, according to the company's November 16, 2026 announcement.

Preliminary cumulative sales for the first three quarters of KRW 987.8 billion equaled about 97% of 2025 full-year sales, and the company called this a record for that period. The link between advisor growth and sales growth also shows up in reported figures.

Earnings defended through rule changes

iM Securities noted that when the 1200% commission rule was first introduced, other GAs saw revenue fall while Incar Financial Service's revenue and profit rose (report dated September 10, 2026).

It cites the bargaining power of the direct-employment and entrepreneurial-agent model as the basis for expecting earnings to hold steady even if commission rules are tightened further.

Room for improvement in operating metrics

The operating margin rose from 6.8% in 2022 to 10.4% in 2024. iM Securities cited the company's top-tier operating margin as a source of premium. After easing to 9.3% in 2025, the metric has room to improve if profitability recovers.

09

Bear factors

Medium-term commission-system shock

iM Securities sees no major earnings or cash-flow issues through 2027. It does expect partial system-related shocks around 2028, when commissions shift from the second year to the third and fourth years. Whether the bargaining power of its large network holds in that window is the key test.

Margin decline and quarterly profit swings

Sales rose 22.8% in 2025, while operating profit grew only 9.9%, which lowered the margin.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders dropped 19.6% quarter on quarter to KRW 17.4 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 before recovering to KRW 22.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026, showing large quarterly swings.

Ownership concentration and internal-control load

Insiders and related parties held 44.02% as of August 31, 2026, according to WiseReport. As the advisor base grew to 25,291, the internal-control burden to prevent mis-selling has grown with it. The company said it will add compliance specialists to strengthen field monitoring.

10

Risk factors

Regulation and commission structure

If commission rules tighten, revenue per advisor could change materially. The company said in its April 2026 strategy announcement that it runs a dedicated task force for the commission reform plan. The timing and detailed rules of implementation still need to be confirmed.

Organization and internal controls

As the advisor base grows, the management load for maintaining fair-sales practices rises. The company said it merged its consumer-protection and risk units into a new internal-control division in the second half of 2025.

If advisor retention and training results weaken, the size of the organization itself could come under pressure.

Shareholding structure and earnings volatility

With the largest shareholder group holding 44.02%, the share of stock in general circulation is limited. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 19.6% quarter on quarter in the fourth quarter of 2025, showing quarterly volatility. If quarterly earnings fluctuate, the earnings-based reading can shift as well.

Overall view

Incar Financial Service is a GA that grew sales from KRW 401.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.02 trillion in 2025 by expanding its advisor network. On a reported basis, operating profit rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025 to KRW 94.8 billion in 2025.

The operating margin eased from 10.4% in 2024 to 9.3% in 2025. Preliminary cumulative sales of KRW 987.8 billion for the first three quarters of 2026 equal about 82.3% of the company's annual target, and it had 25,291 financial advisors at the end of September.

Bullish factors include scale, bargaining power and past resilience during rule changes. Bearish factors include possible commission-system shocks around 2028, concentrated shareholding and the internal-control burden of a growing network.

The shares trade at a premium to book value, and iM Securities argued that this premium is not yet reflected in the price. The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report, due by late March 2027, and the first-quarter 2027 report.
