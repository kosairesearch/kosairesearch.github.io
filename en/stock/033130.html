02

Business structure

DigitalChosun is a digital content media company belonging to the Chosun Ilbo group. Its core businesses are digital content services and outdoor electronic billboard operations, supported by newspaper publishing system and SI (system integration) work that strengthens its media IT infrastructure capability.

Outdoor billboards are advertising media installed across cities, so the scale of this business depends on advertising demand and available installation sites.

In its corporate value enhancement plan disclosed in March 2026, the company said it would expand billboard installations and other core businesses to steadily grow net profit. The plan shows that billboard expansion is positioned as a central pillar of future profit growth.

The 2025 revenue of KRW 39.9 billion represents the combined scale of the whole business mix. In outdoor and digital advertising, the company competes with large media and advertising operators.

Compared with peers in the media and entertainment sector such as SBS and kt nasmedia, its business scope and scale differ considerably.

Data providers classify the company under different industries, including internet information intermediary services and IT services, which also reflects the mixed nature of its business. Its revenue does not swing sharply with content hits the way it does for broadcasters or music agencies.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 34.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 36.6 billion in 2023 (up 5.3%), KRW 37 billion in 2024 (up 1.0%), and KRW 39.9 billion in 2025 (up 8.0%), and it has grown for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Growth slowed to 1.0% in 2024 before picking up to 8.0% in 2025, and 2025 was the largest revenue year in the 2022–2025 span. Operating profit grew steadily from KRW 2.06 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.17 billion in 2023 (up 5.5%) and KRW 2.36 billion in 2024 (up 8.7%), then jumped to KRW 5.51 billion in 2025 (up 132.9%).

The operating margin edged up from 5.9% in 2022 and 5.9% in 2023 to 6.4% in 2024, then reached 13.8% in 2025. The 2025 profit increase far exceeded the 8.0% revenue growth, which means cost burdens did not rise in line with sales.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders grew from KRW 2.79 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.45 billion in 2025 (up 44.0%), while net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 5.41 billion in 2025, a different figure.

Quarterly revenue climbed from KRW 10 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 11.6 billion in the fourth quarter (up 15.5% from the prior quarter), then moved to KRW 10.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 11.3%), KRW 11.2 billion in the second quarter (up 9.4%), and KRW 10.5 billion in the third quarter (down 6.4%).

Third-quarter revenue growth of 4.9% year on year stayed in single digits.

Quarterly operating profit was stable at KRW 2.2 billion, KRW 2.24 billion and KRW 2.38 billion from the third quarter of 2025 through the first quarter of 2026, then rose to KRW 3.75 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (up 57.4% from the prior quarter).

Because that profit increase far exceeded the 9.4% revenue growth, cost-side factors may have played a part.

Third-quarter operating profit of KRW 3.06 billion fell 18.3% from the prior quarter but rose 39.5% year on year, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.71 billion rose 38.5% year on year.

Over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 10.4 billion. Operating cash flow increased from KRW 5.62 billion in 2022 and KRW 5.98 billion in 2024 to KRW 8.51 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 16.6%, up from 11.4% in 2024, with total liabilities of KRW 15.3 billion, total equity of KRW 92.2 billion, and controlling shareholders' equity of KRW 92 billion at that date.

Industry analysis

Data providers classify DigitalChosun under different industries, including internet information intermediary services and KOSDAQ IT services, so the interpretation changes with the peer group chosen.

Within media and entertainment, outdoor advertising media operators are distinct from advertising agencies such as Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide and from broadcasting and platform businesses such as SBS and kt nasmedia.

Outdoor and digital advertising are sensitive to corporate advertising budgets and the economic cycle. Billboard installation is constrained by site availability and permit conditions, and the company presented it as a means of growing its core business in its March 2026 plan.

After the profit increase in 2025, quarterly operating profit has stayed at KRW 2.2 billion or more into 2026, so whether the annual improvement is a one-off rebound or a sustained trend is still being shaped quarter by quarter.

Competitively, the company shares advertising budgets with large advertising agencies and broadcasters. In scale, it is small compared with the large listed companies in the media and entertainment sector.

The pace of recovery in advertising demand and the permit conditions for billboards together will determine how fast the business can expand.

The fourth-quarter 2026 result will set the full-year 2026 figures, which will be confirmed in the annual business report expected around late March 2027. The timing and scale of billboard expansion and the content of new-business investments will become clearer as disclosures are published.

For shareholder returns, the key checkpoint is whether actual dividend execution follows the payout target in the same direction.

Bull factors

Higher operating margin level

The 2025 operating margin rose to 13.8% from 6.4% in 2024, and quarterly operating profit stayed at KRW 2.2 billion or more from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026. The 132.9% profit increase in 2025 outpaced the 8.0% revenue growth, showing a change in the earnings structure. Whether the billboard expansion plan can sustain this trend is the next checkpoint.

Explicit shareholder return policy

The company set a target of keeping its consolidated dividend payout ratio at roughly 25%. The 2025 payout ratio was 27.25%, the dividend amount rose 33.3% from the prior year, and the company disclosed that it qualifies as a high-dividend company. The dividend yield is above the KOSDAQ average.

Financial capacity and cash generation

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 92.2 billion against total liabilities of KRW 15.3 billion. Operating cash flow reached KRW 8.51 billion in 2025, up from KRW 5.62 billion in 2022. The 16.6% debt ratio indicates that liabilities remain modest relative to equity.

Bear factors

Slowing revenue growth

Revenue growth was only 1.0% in 2024, and third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 10.5 billion fell 6.4% from the prior quarter. Year-on-year growth of 4.9% remained in single digits. Slower advertising demand or slower billboard installation could extend the slowdown.

Volatile quarterly profit

Operating profit jumped 57.4% to KRW 3.75 billion in the second quarter of 2026, then fell 18.3% to KRW 3.06 billion in the third quarter. Swings of this size make it hard to judge how durable annual profit is. Whether the 13.8% margin in 2025 reflects one-off factors cannot be settled from the reported figures alone.

Rising debt ratio trend

The debt ratio climbed from 10.7% in 2022 to 16.6% in 2025. Total liabilities of KRW 15.3 billion at the end of 2025 are low relative to equity but rising. Capital capacity remains ample, but a continued rise in liabilities could change the direction of financial stability indicators.

Demand for outdoor billboards and digital content advertising moves with the economic cycle and advertisers' budgets. Third-quarter 2026 revenue growth of 4.9% year on year suggests softer ad demand could quickly show up in sales. A delay in billboard expansion could also push back the growth targets in the value enhancement plan.

Quarterly volatility and earnings durability

Operating profit surged in the second quarter of 2026 and then declined in the third, so quarterly swings have been large.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 10.4 billion over the last four quarters, forming the base of annual profit, but whether that base can absorb quarterly swings will be shown in upcoming results.

The interpretation of the earnings base depends on whether the second-quarter jump came from cost factors or one-off items.

Small-cap liquidity and shareholder structure

Overall view

DigitalChosun is a digital content and outdoor billboard company within the Chosun Ilbo group, and its profit base has clearly grown, with 2025 operating profit of KRW 5.51 billion (up 132.9%) and a 13.8% operating margin.

Quarterly operating profit has stayed at KRW 2.2 billion or more through 2026, but revenue growth has remained in single digits, so the growth momentum still rests more on margins. The 16.6% debt ratio, KRW 92.2 billion in total equity and KRW 8.51 billion in 2025 operating cash flow point to financial stability.

The target of a consolidated payout ratio of roughly 25% and the 27.25% payout ratio in 2025 show a consistent shareholder return stance. On the other side, profit swings between the second and third quarters of 2026, dependence on the advertising cycle and small-cap liquidity constraints are the weak points.

On valuation, the PER and PBR sit at low levels relative to the company's own history and the sector average, so confirming earnings durability is the central issue. The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 result and the 2026 annual business report, followed by the first-quarter 2027 trend.
