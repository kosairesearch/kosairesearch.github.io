02

Business structure

Dhautonex is an auto parts manufacturer founded in 1967 that joined the DH Group in 2024 and changed its corporate name in 2025. Following the 2024 acquisition, the company relocated its headquarters and production base to Gimje, Jeonbuk, according to press reports.

The company cites growth in its core automotive parts business as the main driver of 2025 sales growth.

On the product side, FnGuide reports that the company continues to win new-model LPG fuel tank orders from major domestic automakers, and that stable mass production of small truck fuel tanks has supported its quality and delivery reputation.

In future mobility, the company is developing cryogenic liquid hydrogen storage vessels and is pursuing a carbon-nano heating element product line. Some media outlets describe the company as a transmission and network equipment specialist, so its business definition varies across reports.

Its customer base centers on domestic automakers, so revenue appears tied to new model launch schedules and order volumes. On capital structure, a share consolidation was carried out as part of a capital reduction, cutting common shares from 82,874,110 to 41,437,055, with the changed listing effective January 14, 2026.

On February 6, 2026, the company agreed to sell land and buildings in Chungju, Chungbuk for KRW 7.6 billion, which it said would fund investment in future flagship businesses. Against large parts makers such as Hyundai Mobis, Hyundai WIA, Hanon Systems and HL Mando, Dhautonex sits among smaller parts suppliers.

04

Earnings analysis

In the annual figures from regular filings, sales fell 93.9% from KRW 536.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 32.8 billion in 2023, edged down 1.3% to KRW 32.4 billion in 2024, and rose 24.1% to KRW 40.2 billion in 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit of KRW 2.22 billion in 2022, with a 4.1% margin, turned into an operating loss of KRW 29.9 billion in 2023, a margin of -91.3%. The operating loss narrowed to KRW 8.56 billion in 2024, a margin of -26.4%, and operating profit returned to KRW 3.23 billion in 2025, an 8.0% margin.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from losses of KRW 24.3 billion in 2022 and KRW 177.2 billion in 2023 to a profit of KRW 113.5 billion in 2024, then fell 94.9% to KRW 5.76 billion in 2025. In 2025 that attributable profit exceeded operating profit, so non-operating items need separate review.

The company cited higher sales in its core auto parts business and a reversal of bad-debt provisions from loan collections as drivers of the 2025 improvement.

Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 2.46 billion in 2024 to positive KRW 6.03 billion in 2025, and year-end 2025 total liabilities were KRW 1.91 billion, total equity KRW 3.21 billion, and controlling shareholders' equity KRW 3.62 billion.

Quarterly, the third quarter of 2025 showed sales of KRW 10.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.25 billion, while the fourth quarter brought sales of KRW 13.2 billion but operating profit of only KRW 531 million, down 87.5%.

Sales reached KRW 14.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026, with operating profit falling 47.0% to KRW 282 million. The second quarter of 2026 saw sales drop 52.4% to KRW 6.84 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.92 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 7.5 billion, down 27.2% year on year, with an operating loss of KRW 402 million. The four-quarter cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.58 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Dhautonex's end market is the new-model production plans of domestic automakers, and fuel tank parts whose revenue depends on which models win orders. Third-quarter 2026 sales fell 27.2% year on year, a pattern that should be read alongside any change in that order flow.

The auto parts sector is in a transition where combustion-engine and electrified components coexist, and LPG fuel tanks appear closer to parts tied to existing models.

In future mobility, the company is developing cryogenic liquid hydrogen storage vessels and carbon-nano heating products, so whether these move into mass production and orders will define its direction.

Compared with large parts makers such as Hyundai Mobis, Hyundai WIA, Hanon Systems and HL Mando, its revenue scale and customer diversification are much narrower.

Against smaller peers such as KG Mobility, Myoung Shin Industry, Inhwa Precision and Hallacast, the key benchmarks are product overlap in fuel tanks and body parts and the mix of automaker customers.

The KOSPI transport equipment and parts group includes many dividend-paying companies, so Dhautonex also differs on shareholder returns. On the balance sheet, the debt ratio fell to 59.6% at the end of 2025, but operating profit remains small relative to sales. Its position within the sector will likely depend on whether profitability recovery holds across quarters.

Outlook

The nearest checkpoint is the absorption merger of its wholly owned subsidiary, an asset management and debt recovery company. The board approved the merger on October 19, 2026, on a 1-for-0 basis with no new shares issued, with the merger date set for December 22, 2026.

The merger registration is scheduled for December 23, 2026, and Dhautonex will remain the surviving company with no change in its largest shareholder.

The company described the merger as a follow-up to implementing the approved rehabilitation plan and closing the rehabilitation proceedings, and it expects lower administrative costs and better efficiency.

On orders, the company disclosed on September 18, 2026 a KRW 2.2 billion solar power plant construction contract in Gwangmyeong, running through December 31, 2026, equal to 5.48% of sales as stated in the disclosure.

The KRW 7.6 billion sale of the Chungju property was presented as funding for investment in future flagship businesses. The carbon-nano heating products and cryogenic liquid hydrogen storage vessels are described as still in development, so the timing of mass production and their revenue contribution remain uncertain.

The next regular report is the 2026 annual report, and because the audit delay in March 2026 pushed back the submission and disclosure of the audit report, the filing schedule and audit opinion are key items to watch. The outcome of the listing review could also affect whether and when these business plans proceed.
