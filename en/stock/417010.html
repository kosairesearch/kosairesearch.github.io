02

Business structure

NanoTIM was founded in December 2016 and listed on KOSDAQ in March 2023 as a maker of thermal management materials for EV batteries.

Its core products are the gap filler and gap pad heat-dissipation materials, and it began supplying thermal runaway barrier pads, a flame-resistant material, for models launched in 2025, adding a new revenue stream.

By product, the mix for the second quarter of 2026 was gap filler 78%, gap pad 13% and other products 9%, according to the Butler company data site. Hyundai Motor Group accounted for about 90% of its customers and thermal management parts suppliers for about 10%, according to a Samsung Securities report from May 2024.

The same report said deliveries to overseas makers such as BMW began in 2024, and that six EV models on Hyundai Motor Group's eM platform were secured for delivery.

Its competitive edge lies in proprietary material formulation and structural design, and the same report said it was selected among 32 competitors and cited price competitiveness against global rivals. A 2023 securities filing said the company was pursuing plants in the Czech Republic and the United States.

As of 2024 data, it had overseas customers such as GM, Ford and Volkswagen, but their share of total revenue was small.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual sales rose from KRW 38.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 47.2 billion in 2023 (up 22.7% year on year), fell to KRW 32.4 billion in 2024 (down 31.4%), and recovered to KRW 40.3 billion in 2025 (up 24.5%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit grew from KRW 3.03 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.44 billion in 2023 (up 79.7%), then turned into an operating loss of KRW 3.76 billion in 2024. The 2025 operating loss narrowed to KRW 501 million, and operating margin moved from 11.5% in 2023 to -11.6% in 2024 and -1.2% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss of KRW 3.71 billion in 2024 to a profit of KRW 998 million in 2025, and net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 998 million.

A March 2026 disclosure cited higher volumes from key customers and lower fixed costs for 2025, and also listed non-cash financial income from fair-value measurement of convertible bonds as a factor in the results.

Operating cash flow was KRW 8.24 billion positive in 2023 but turned negative at minus KRW 3.72 billion in 2024 and minus KRW 390 million in 2025.

Quarterly sales were KRW 10.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 10.1 billion in the fourth quarter (down 2.1% from the prior quarter), with the first quarter of 2026 at the low point of KRW 9.84 billion (down 2.5%).

Sales then rose to KRW 14.2 billion in the second quarter (up 44.1%) and KRW 22.2 billion in the third quarter (up 56.8% from the prior quarter and 115.7% from a year earlier), an increase over two consecutive quarters.

Operating profit turned to KRW 1.41 billion in the second quarter but fell to KRW 583 million in the third quarter, down 58.8% from the prior quarter.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 727 million in the second quarter and a loss of KRW 911 million in the third quarter, with the four-quarter total at KRW 698 million.

05

Industry analysis

The core end market is the EV battery market. Stricter EV safety demands and tighter fire regulations in several countries are cited as drivers of growth in the thermal runaway prevention materials market.

On the cycle, a January 2024 Korea Investor Relations Association report said BEV demand softness continued into the second half of 2023, and an October 2023 Samsung Securities report said second-half 2023 results fell short of the first half because Hyundai Motor Group delayed EV production.

Earnings therefore react sharply to customer production schedules. On competitive position, the company holds proprietary material formulation and structural design know-how, was selected among 32 competitors, and is credited with price competitiveness against global rivals.

Customer diversification remains at an early stage. A 2023 report also expected battery pack designs shifting to cell-to-pack and cell-to-body structures to require bonding functions, which could move materials toward polyurethane and acrylic chemistries.

With gap filler at 78% of the mix in the second quarter of 2026, how far this product shift has reached revenue should be checked in the detailed items of the annual report.

06

Outlook

Based on confirmed facts, the central question for the earnings trend is how far the sales recovery since the second quarter of 2026 carries through to profit.

Thermal runaway barrier pads began supplying models launched in 2025, and the shift from heat-dissipation to flame-resistant materials has been cited as a driver of higher content value per vehicle.

A 2023 report projected the value per vehicle could rise from USD 100 to USD 200 to 300, but that is a 2023 estimate, so actual realization should be checked against disclosed revenue mix.

The same report expected new products to exceed 50% of total sales from 2026, yet gap filler still made up 78% of the mix in the second quarter of 2026. On funding, the results of a KRW 20 billion second private placement of convertible bonds were disclosed in October 2026.

A 2023 report saw a need to raise funds for overseas plant construction, and whether this issuance links to overseas capacity can be checked in the use-of-proceeds section of the annual report.

Fourth-quarter 2026 results have not yet been filed in a periodic report, so whether third-quarter sales growth continues is the next checkpoint. Whether operating margin stays stably positive is the other item to watch.

08

Bull factors

Product shift could lift content value

Thermal runaway barrier pads began supplying models launched in 2025. A 2023 Samsung Securities report saw value per vehicle rising from USD 100 to USD 200 to 300. If this mix shift materializes, room for operating margin improvement would widen. The figure is a 2023 estimate, so the annual report's product breakdown is needed to verify it.

Quarterly direction has turned

After the first-quarter 2026 low, sales rose to KRW 14.2 billion in the second quarter and KRW 22.2 billion in the third, an increase over two consecutive quarters. Operating profit turned to KRW 1.41 billion in the second quarter, and third-quarter sales were up 115.7% from a year earlier.

Higher volume creates room to absorb fixed costs, which is one reading of the trend. However, third-quarter operating profit fell to KRW 583 million from the prior quarter.

Technology, price competitiveness and regulatory demand

The company holds proprietary formulation and structural design know-how and was selected among 32 competitors, according to a 2024 report. Price competitiveness against global rivals was also cited as a strength.

Tighter EV fire regulation and growth in the thermal runaway prevention materials market form the structural backdrop for demand.

Bear factors

Customer concentration

A May 2024 Samsung Securities report put Hyundai Motor Group's share of customers at about 90%. At that level, the customer's EV production schedule and model changes flow directly into sales. Second-half 2023 results fell short of the first half amid softer BEV demand and production delays. Overseas customer growth has so far been small as a share of revenue.

Thin earnings base

After an operating loss of KRW 3.76 billion in 2024, 2025 also posted an operating loss of KRW 501 million, making two consecutive loss years. The third-quarter 2026 loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 911 million, a wider loss than a year earlier. The four-quarter total is KRW 698 million. Because part of the 2025 swing came from non-cash financial income, the nature of earnings also needs scrutiny.

Financial burden

At the end of 2025, the debt-to-equity ratio was 140.7%, with total liabilities of KRW 68.8 billion exceeding total equity of KRW 48.9 billion. Operating cash flow was negative at minus KRW 3.72 billion in 2024 and minus KRW 390 million in 2025. The results of a KRW 20 billion second private placement of convertible bonds were disclosed in October 2026.

Risk factors

Demand and policy risk

Softer EV demand is recorded as a factor behind weaker second-half 2023 results. Fire regulation supports product demand, but slower EV sales could reduce gap filler and gap pad volumes. If the pace of the product shift and the customer's model plans diverge, sales volatility could increase.

Funding and dilution risk

The results of a KRW 20 billion second private placement of convertible bonds were disclosed in October 2026. If the bonds convert into shares, the share count would rise and affect per-share metrics. Net income also remains exposed to fair-value swings in the convertible bonds.

Governance and shareholder meeting risk

At the March 2026 annual general meeting, the financial statements were approved, but the proposed limit on director compensation was voted down. The auditor issued unqualified opinions on both separate and consolidated statements.

In a structure with high controlling-shareholder ownership, how minority shareholder views are reflected should be watched at the next meeting.

12

Overall view

NanoTIM is focused on thermal management materials for EV batteries, with customer concentration in Hyundai Motor Group. Annual disclosed results rose to a peak in 2023, fell in 2024 and recovered in 2025, with operating losses in both 2024 and 2025.

The 2025 swing to net profit included non-cash financial income and should be viewed apart from operating strength.

In 2026, sales rose over two consecutive quarters and operating profit turned positive in the second quarter, but in the third quarter operating profit fell from the prior quarter and net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a loss.

Bullish factors include product expansion into thermal runaway barrier pads, demand driven by EV fire regulation, and technology and price competitiveness. Bearish factors include customer concentration, a thin earnings base, and financial strain from a 140.7% debt-to-equity ratio and convertible bonds.

With the two sets of factors pulling in opposite directions, whether profitability holds in the next periodic reports is the most important item to confirm.
