02

Business structure

BioInfra Life Science was founded in January 2001. It listed on the KONEX market on July 22, 2020. Its main activities are in-vitro diagnostics research and the development, manufacturing and sale of beauty and medical devices, and it runs diagnostic testing services, beauty devices and cosmetics.

The flagship diagnostic service is i-FINDER, a blood multi-marker test used to assess cancer risk. The company says it provides diagnostic testing services through a diagnostic specialty hospital. On the treatment side, it developed a targeted drug-delivery platform that uses ultrasound and micelles. The platform induces cavitation with ultrasound energy to raise drug-delivery efficiency.

Sonocube, which applies this core technology, helps active cosmetic ingredients penetrate deeper into the skin. FnGuide described a plan to widen the beauty and medical device market through an ultrasound beauty device launch in 2025.

According to a late-2024 report, the company intends to expand into consumer products such as cosmetics, health functional foods and beauty devices.

The diagnostics business has a regulatory history. In May 2023 the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety ordered a sales halt and disposal of an in-vitro diagnostic device, and the penalty amount equaled 31.36% of the latest revenue. Segment revenue shares after that episode could not be confirmed in this research.

Headcount is also small. JobKorea lists the number of employees as 5. Large listed peers in the same industry, such as Samsung Biologics and Celltrion, differ greatly in business scope and scale. The competitive set is closer to small diagnostics and genomic analysis firms such as 3Billion and Genoplan Korea. Note that the KOSDAQ-listed clinical research organization with a similar name is a separate company.

03

Recent trends

In the 2024 results, standalone revenue fell 85.5% from the previous year. FnGuide's consolidated table shows 2024 revenue of about KRW 0.2 billion, an operating loss of about KRW 2.0 billion and a net loss of about KRW 3.1 billion.

On the 2025 standalone filing, revenue was KRW 1,270 million, operating profit was KRW -1,388 million and net income was KRW -2,597 million, and earnings per share were also negative. Revenue recovered from 2024 and the operating loss narrowed. Even so, the operating loss remained larger than revenue.

At the end of 2025, total assets were KRW 2,603 million, total liabilities KRW 2,064 million, total equity KRW 539 million and capital stock KRW 13,933 million. Equity is very small relative to capital stock, which reflects large accumulated deficits.

At the annual general meeting on March 31, 2026, the fiscal year 25 financial statements were approved, with approval equal to 52.6% of total voting shares outstanding. On the same day, chief executive Lee Deok-yoon resigned and Kim Seung-jun was appointed. After the change there are 4 registered directors, of whom 1 is an outside director.

On March 13, 2026, the company decided to transfer 1,011,805 Foodnamu shares for KRW 2,225,971,000. A down payment of KRW 600 million was due on March 13, and the balance of KRW 1,625,971,000 was due on April 17. Whether the balance was received and the deal closed could not be confirmed in this research.

The shares hit an all-time high of KRW 4,770 on July 22, 2020, and a low of KRW 79 on August 4, 2026. The price on October 27 was KRW 95, and trading value of KRW 361,185 shows thin activity. First-half 2026 results could not be confirmed in this research.

Outlook

The near-term items to watch are whether the operating loss narrows further from the 2025 level and whether revenue avoids shrinking again. How the proceeds from the Foodnamu share transfer actually showed up in liquidity must be confirmed in later periodic reports.

The medium-term business direction under the new chief executive was not spelled out in the filings reviewed. The company's IR page carries a record-date notice for an extraordinary general meeting dated June 15, 2026, but the agenda could not be confirmed.

In late 2024 the company put forward an expansion into consumer products such as cosmetics, health functional foods and beauty devices. Without segment disclosure, it is hard to establish whether this strategy translated into revenue. The diagnostics business also leaves regulatory compliance as a variable given its approval history.

On capital structure, equity is small, so further accumulated losses could weaken financial indicators quickly. Depending on how funding is raised, share count and governance could also change, which is worth monitoring.

FnGuide leaves the 2025 to 2027 consensus estimates blank, so broker forecasts offer little guidance either. Operating loss size, total equity, debt levels and actual revenue mix are the key items to check in future filings.

05

Bull factors

Narrower losses and a revenue rebound

On FnGuide figures, the 2024 operating loss was around KRW 2.0 billion. The 2025 standalone filing shows operating profit of KRW -1,388 million. Revenue also rose from the low 2024 level to KRW 1,270 million. On operating metrics alone, one reading is that room remains to improve the operating margin.

Long-accumulated research assets

The company's website highlights more than 15 years of research results. It developed a blood multi-marker test on the basis of basic research into the tumor microenvironment. The ultrasound drug-delivery platform is an enabling technology that could apply to both beauty devices and cancer therapy. If these technology assets are commercialized, the business structure could change.

Asset disposal to raise funds

The company decided to transfer its Foodnamu shares to secure liquidity and improve management efficiency. The transfer amount of KRW 2,225,971,000 is sizeable against year-end 2025 total equity of KRW 539 million. A down payment of KRW 600 million was due on the filing date. If the deal closed as planned, it would be read as adding to operating funds.

06

Bear factors

Persistent losses and thin equity

FnGuide's table shows operating losses in every year from 2020 through 2024. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 539 million, far below capital stock of KRW 13,933 million. Total liabilities at the same date were KRW 2,064 million. If losses keep accumulating, the financial structure could weaken quickly.

Licensing and regulatory history

In 2023 the ministry took action against a cancer diagnostic test software on the grounds of selling an unapproved in-vitro diagnostic device. That product accounted for roughly 32% of the prior year's revenue at the time.

The company said it would object to the administrative disposition and respond through litigation. Regulatory uncertainty in diagnostics may have affected the later revenue structure.

Reports questioning funding and governance

In 2024, In The News reported criticism that a capital-impaired KONEX-listed company was used as a funding vehicle in the Foodnamu rights offering. The same report described one of the participants in the offering as effectively a paper company. The company's rebuttal was not found in that report. In March 2026 the chief executive and one outside director also resigned, adding to management turnover.

07

Risk factors

Financial and liquidity

With operating losses continuing and equity small, securing operating funds is the key variable. Receipt and use of the Foodnamu share-transfer proceeds must be confirmed through later filings. If additional equity or convertible bonds are needed, existing shareholders could be diluted. A third-party allotment of new shares was also carried out in 2024.

Regulatory and business

In-vitro diagnostic devices face strict approval requirements, and the company has a precedent of a 2023 sales-halt order. Results and segment shares of new businesses such as beauty devices and cosmetics are not sufficiently confirmed in filings.

With a small workforce, pursuing research and sales at the same time may be constrained. The business priorities under the new chief executive are also unclear.

Market and macro

KONEX small caps trade thinly, and the share price can move sharply on small flows. Trading value on October 27 was only KRW 361,185. If investor sentiment and the funding environment weaken, raising external capital could become harder. The stock is also exposed to shifts in sentiment across the broader biotech and pharma sector.

08

Overall view

BioInfra Life Science is a small KONEX company with in-vitro diagnostics and ultrasound drug-delivery technology. On the 2025 standalone filing, revenue recovered to KRW 1,270 million and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 1,388 million.

However, an operating loss larger than revenue and total equity of only KRW 539 million are burdens. In March 2026 the company decided to transfer Foodnamu shares and changed its chief executive, and the follow-through on the transfer could not be confirmed.

The revenue contribution of its expansion strategy, its funding methods and its regulatory handling are items to check in future filings. This report is for information only, and any investment judgment is left to the reader.
