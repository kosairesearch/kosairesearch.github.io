Report overview

Smart-home leader; earnings recovery is the test

Hyundai HT ranks among the leaders in private apartment smart-home supply, but net losses attributable to controlling shareholders ran through three consecutive quarters from Q1 2026 to Q3 2026, leaving earnings recovery and the payoff from Vietnam investments as the key tests.

02

Business structure

Hyundai HT is a smart-home specialist that makes intercom and home network systems and digital door locks for apartment complexes. Its core is B2B supply of smart-home devices and services to about ten leading construction companies in Korea.

The company said its share of private apartment sales was 44% in 2024 and that it has built smart-home solutions for 1.8 million households. Based on Q2 2026 sales, the business mix was Smart Home 61%, Others 20%, digital door locks 9%, unmanned security systems 8%, and kitchen TV 2%.

On the B2C side, the company is expanding its subscription maintenance service HT PLUS and its electronic bulletin board HT POST, and it has launched the residential integrated service app HT HOME LIFE.

In product terms, it released the Diffolock digital door lock in May 2026, and its digital door lock category won the 2026 Consumer-Choice Brand Award for a second straight year. Overseas, it set up a sales unit in Vietnam in September 2025 and a production unit in November 2025.

The company positions custom models for leading builders and cost reduction as its competitive edge. Because its customer base is concentrated in large builders, demand is sensitive to changes in private apartment sales.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 104.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 131.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 168.9 billion in 2024, then fell 9.2% to KRW 153.3 billion in 2025. Of 2022 to 2025, the largest year was 2024, and the company itself described its 2024 sales as a record.

Operating margin moved from 1.2% in 2022 to -0.6% in 2023, 6.5% in 2024 and 4.6% in 2025. Operating profit of KRW 1.28 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 820 million in 2023, which the company attributed in part to a construction slowdown and higher raw material costs.

In 2024 operating profit of KRW 11.1 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 13.9 billion turned the company back to profit. In 2025 operating profit fell 36.6% to KRW 7.01 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 23.8% to KRW 10.6 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 8.66 billion in 2022, KRW 4.31 billion in 2023, KRW 8.26 billion in 2024 and KRW 3.09 billion in 2025, the lowest of the four years.

By quarter, Q3 2025 sales were KRW 39 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.2 billion, and Q4 2025 sales fell to KRW 35 billion with operating profit of KRW 750 million.

Q1 2026 sales were KRW 31 billion with an operating loss of KRW 1.33 billion, and Q2 2026 sales rose to KRW 36.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 201 million. Q3 2026 sales were KRW 36.5 billion, up 0.1% from the prior quarter, while operating profit was KRW 44.71 million, down 77.7%.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders were KRW 1.55 billion, KRW 1.49 billion and KRW 1.12 billion in Q1 to Q3 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 2.2 billion. Press reports cite the weaker won and higher memory chip prices as cost pressures.

Start-up costs for the Vietnam units and plant build-out mean the timing of a return to sustained profitability is still unclear.

05

Industry analysis

The company's main market is tied to new apartment supply in Korea. In 2024 it cited a construction slowdown, higher raw material prices, a rise in unsold apartments and project financing defaults as headwinds. Press reports in 2026 said the domestic housing market slump was weighing directly on results.

Sales can swing with construction starts and builders' ordering patterns. Its subscription maintenance and digital door lock businesses for existing apartments, by contrast, can widen demand regardless of new sales, the company says.

In cycle terms, a recovery in new apartment sales remains a precondition for earnings improvement. On competition, the company points to custom models for leading builders and cost reduction as its differentiators. Supplying mainly leading builders also means high dependence on a small group of large customers.

Overseas, it is prioritizing Southeast Asia, starting with Vietnam and Indonesia, and plans to extend to Latin America and the Middle East. Press reports put the Vietnam sales unit's H1 2026 sales at KRW 765 million with a net loss of about KRW 99 million. Overseas sales remain very small relative to the domestic business.

06

Outlook

The company's stated direction rests on three pillars: overseas unit performance, local partnerships and new products. The company expects its Vietnam sales unit to produce visible results from the first half of 2027.

On production, it set up the Vietnam production unit HYUNDAI HT GLOBAL in November 2025, and on March 31, 2026 it decided to acquire 100% of that unit for KRW 12 billion in cash.

On partnerships, it announced a strategic partnership framework in Vietnam in March 2026, agreed in June 2026 to work with Sao Viet to expand in the south, and in July 2026 announced expanded cooperation with HANDICO and others to push into northern Vietnam's smart-home market.

On products, it launched the Diffolock digital door lock in May 2026, and in February 2026 its product won a winner award at the Asia Design Prize. Domestic apartment sales and cost trends remain the variables that shape the earnings direction.

In the 2026 annual business report, the annual operating margin and the profit or loss of the overseas units are the main points to verify. How the overseas units are separated in quarterly disclosures is another item to watch.

08

Bull factors

Domestic supply base and market position

The company said its share of private apartment sales was 44% in 2024, and it supplies devices and services to about ten leading builders. Its track record of solutions in 1.8 million households supports new complex wins. A second straight consumer brand award in digital door locks is a plus for B2C expansion. The share figure is the company's own estimate.

Quarterly sales recovery

Sales bottomed at KRW 31 billion in Q1 2026 and then rose to KRW 36.4 billion in Q2 and KRW 36.5 billion in Q3, two consecutive quarterly increases. Operating profit moved from a KRW 1.33 billion loss in Q1 2026 to KRW 201 million in Q2. The quarterly sales trend points to room for the revenue base to recover. Operating profit remains small in absolute terms.

Financial capacity and overseas footprint

At end-2025 the debt-to-equity ratio was 32.9% and total equity was KRW 106.8 billion. The ratio fell from 42.4% in 2023 to 39.9% in 2024 and 32.9% in 2025. Vietnam sales and production units and local partners such as HANDICO give the company a Southeast Asian base that could support medium-term growth. Results from the overseas push are likely to become visible from the first half of 2027.

Bear factors

Sharp erosion of earnings power

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 44.71 million, down 96.3% year on year. Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders were KRW 1.55 billion, KRW 1.49 billion and KRW 1.12 billion across Q1 to Q3 2026, three straight loss-making quarters.

The last four quarters total KRW 2.2 billion in net losses attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating profit already fell 36.6% in 2025, so the decline has run since the 2025 results.

Dependence on the domestic housing market

Sales were KRW 153.3 billion in 2025, down 9.2% year on year, and Q3 2026 sales fell 6.5% year on year. Press coverage describes the domestic housing slump as hitting the company hard. Revenue is tied to new apartment sales and builder orders, so a prolonged downturn could translate into lower volumes. In 2024 unsold apartments and project financing defaults were also cited as headwinds.

Overseas investment burden and delayed payoff

Press reports say the Vietnam sales unit posted a net loss of about KRW 99 million in H1 2026, while the production unit, still in plant build-out, posted a net loss of about KRW 1.215 billion. Over the same period KRW 4.555 billion was lent to the production unit.

A cash acquisition of the production unit for KRW 12 billion was decided in March 2026. With results expected from the first half of 2027, the investment load could delay the earnings recovery.

10

Risk factors

Cost and currency

Press reports cite a weaker won and higher memory chip prices as cost pressures. Smart-home devices rely on semiconductors and LCD panels as main inputs, which makes them sensitive to input cost swings. If higher costs cannot be passed through to selling prices, operating margin could fall further. The operating margin was 4.6% in 2025, below 6.5% in 2024.

Customer concentration and apartment sales

The company supplies devices to about ten leading builders, so customer concentration is high. If builders delay sales schedules or unsold units rise and orders shrink, revenue is affected quickly. The shift from the record-sales year of 2024 to a decline in 2025 illustrates this exposure.

Governance and related-party dealings

The Q3 2023 quarterly report shows large purchase transactions with related companies, including HT Access and HTBond. The size and terms of related-party dealings are a point for minority shareholders to examine. The current status of these dealings should be checked in the notes to the latest periodic reports.

12

Overall view

Hyundai HT holds a leading position in private apartment smart-home supply, backed by its builder supply base and a consumer award for its digital door locks. Its balance sheet is relatively stable, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 32.9% and total equity of KRW 106.8 billion at end-2025.

Profitability, however, fell in 2025 after the 2024 return to profit, and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders ran from Q1 through Q3 2026. The swing to operating profit in Q2 2026 and two straight quarters of sales growth can be read as recovery signals.

Still, the drop in Q3 2026 operating profit to KRW 44.71 million shows that the recovery is still narrow. The overseas business carries both unit losses and loan burdens, and the company expects results from the first half of 2027. On costs, the won and memory chip prices remain variables.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary Q4 2026 results, the annual operating margin in the 2026 annual business report, and overseas unit performance in the first half of 2027.
