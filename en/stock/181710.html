Report overview

Record quarterly profit as cloud and payments drive growth

NHN reported record quarterly revenue of KRW 758.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 58.8 billion for Q3 2026, with payments and cloud driving the gain, while game-segment softness and the absence of formal earnings guidance remain open questions.

02

Business structure

NHN runs three core businesses: games, payments, and technology led by NHN Cloud.

According to the company's Q3 2026 results release of November 10, 2026, payment revenue was KRW 397.6 billion (up 21.5% year on year), technology revenue was KRW 171.7 billion (up 53.6%), game revenue was KRW 131.0 billion (up 10.7%), and other revenue was KRW 78.5 billion (down 12.4%).

The payment segment includes NHN KCP and NHN Payco. NHN KCP's Q3 2026 transaction volume reached KRW 17.3 trillion, up 30.5% year on year, and overseas merchants accounted for a record 25.3% of that volume. NHN Payco opened online payments for Baemin at the end of August 2026.

NHN Cloud, the center of the technology segment, grew revenue 75.5% year on year on AI GPU demand while staying profitable. Japanese technology subsidiary NHN Techorus grew revenue 20.8% on AWS resale.

The game segment centers on domestic web-board games, including Hangame poker and the Hangame Poker Tour (HPT) offline tournament series, plus live operation of Japanese mobile games, and the company is restructuring some low-profit businesses.

Consolidated subsidiaries stood at 56 at the end of Q3 2026, nine fewer than a year earlier. Payment customers are mainly online merchants, while cloud customers include government projects and private-sector GPU users. In cloud, the company is compared with Samsung SDS and LG CNS, and in payments with domestic PG operators.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 2.11 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.52 trillion in 2025. It reached KRW 2.27 trillion in 2023 (up 7.3%), KRW 2.46 trillion in 2024 (up 8.2%), and KRW 2.52 trillion in 2025 (up 2.4%), so revenue grew for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Growth slowed from 8.2% in 2024 to 2.4% in 2025. Operating profit rose from KRW 39.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 55.6 billion in 2023 (up 42.4%), then turned to an operating loss of KRW 32.6 billion in 2024, and returned to operating profit of KRW 132.4 billion in 2025.

The operating margin moved from 1.8% in 2022 to 2.4% in 2023, -1.3% in 2024, and 5.3% in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was a loss of KRW 32.1 billion in 2022, a loss of KRW 8.54 billion in 2023 (losses narrowed), a loss of KRW 132.5 billion in 2024 (losses widened), and a profit of KRW 32.1 billion in 2025 (a swing to profit).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 51.9 billion in 2025, a different figure from profit attributable to owners of the parent. Over the same period the debt-to-equity ratio rose from 51.8% in 2022 to 117.5% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 1.77 trillion, of which equity attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 1.46 trillion, and total liabilities were KRW 2.08 trillion. Operating cash flow was KRW 25.9 billion in 2022, KRW 165.3 billion in 2023, KRW 47 billion in 2024, and KRW 374 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 625.6 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 685.6 billion in Q4 2025 (up 9.6% quarter on quarter), KRW 671.4 billion in Q1 2026 (down 2.1%), KRW 757.9 billion in Q2 2026 (up 12.9%), and KRW 758.3 billion in Q3 2026 (up 0.1% quarter on quarter and 21.2% year on year).

Operating profit went from KRW 27.6 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 55.2 billion in Q4 2025, fell to KRW 26.3 billion in Q1 2026 (down 52.5%), then rose to KRW 57.9 billion in Q2 2026 (up 120.6%) and KRW 58.8 billion in Q3 2026 (up 1.5%).

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 12.1 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 14.7 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 23.6 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 20 billion in Q2 2026 (down 15.0%), and KRW 29.2 billion in Q3 2026 (up 45.7%).

The company said Q3 net profit partly reflected a one-time debt forgiveness gain from a subsidiary being liquidated. Depreciation was KRW 37.8 billion, up 2.0% from the prior quarter.

05

Industry analysis

In AI infrastructure, GPU demand is supporting growth at NHN Cloud. The company said on its earnings call that it expects a government project of roughly KRW 4 trillion in 2027 using the latest Vera Rubin-class and newer GPUs, and that it is preparing to participate.

New data centers are described as equipped with liquid cooling. The CFO said the GPU business earns a direct-cost profitability of about 30% and that there is room for improvement as GPU generations advance, though this is the company's own figure and not an independently verified industry benchmark.

In payments, overseas merchant volume grew on higher automotive merchant volume, and the link to food delivery payments added a new growth channel.

In games, the company said the regulatory change in web-board games raised average revenue per user, and web-board revenue rose 16.2% year on year on the Chuseok holiday and offline tournament effects. Mobile game revenue fell from the prior quarter, so growth across the game business has been uneven.

In cloud, the company is compared with NAVER, Kakao, Samsung SDS, and LG CNS, and in payments with established domestic PG operators. Payments and technology together made up about 75% of Q3 2026 revenue, which shows the business mix shifting from games toward payments and cloud.

06

Outlook

The core of the company's plan is expanding AI data center capacity. NHN Cloud targets operating about 100MW of AI data centers in total, including existing infrastructure, by the end of 2027.

It has leased data centers in the Seoul metropolitan area, including Gasan in Seoul and Gajwa in Incheon, to deploy more than 1,700 B300 GPUs, and plans to open services in stages from January 2027 and recognize revenue from that point.

Full-scale service is indicated to start between December 2026 and late January 2027. It also plans to lease a 26MW AI data center in Jangseong, Jeonnam, and to lease a 20MW Pohang AI data center slated for completion in Q4 2027, while building 10MW of its own infrastructure in the Gwangju R&D special zone.

In payments, NHN Payco is focused on profitability-driven expansion with the target of a full-year profit in 2027, and is accelerating work on stablecoin and AI agent payment technology.

In games, the Japan-Korea co-developed title Puchi Pocket Yong, which was shown at the Tokyo Game Show, is targeted for launch in the first half of 2027. The company is preparing to participate in a government project of roughly KRW 4 trillion.

The company has not provided specific quarterly or annual revenue and profit forecasts. The 2027 checkpoints are the completion and operating schedule of leased and owned facilities, the start of revenue recognition after January, and whether NHN Payco reaches full-year profitability.

08

Bull factors

Profit base lifted by payments and technology

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 58.8 billion rose 112.6% year on year, and the operating margin of 7.8% was 3.3 percentage points higher than a year earlier. Payment revenue of KRW 397.6 billion and technology revenue of KRW 171.7 billion together made up about 75% of revenue.

Quarterly operating profit rose for two consecutive quarters, from KRW 26.3 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 57.9 billion in Q2 and KRW 58.8 billion in Q3.

AI data center expansion and GPU demand

NHN Cloud targets about 100MW of AI data center operations by the end of 2027, with more than 1,700 B300 GPUs deployed and staged service openings planned from January 2027. The CFO said the GPU business earns a direct-cost profitability of about 30%.

The company is also preparing for a government project of roughly KRW 4 trillion expected in 2027, which it presents as a demand-side positive.

Payment volume growth and overseas merchants

NHN KCP's Q3 2026 transaction volume of KRW 17.3 trillion rose 30.5% year on year, and overseas merchant volume grew 75.4%. The overseas merchant share of 25.3% is the highest on record. NHN Payco opened online payments for Baemin at the end of August 2026 and targets full-year profitability in 2027.

09

Bear factors

Uneven trend in the game segment

Game revenue was KRW 131.0 billion in Q3 2026, up 10.7% year on year but down 6.2% from the prior quarter. Mobile game revenue of KRW 80.8 billion fell 10.7% from the prior quarter.

Web-board revenue growth partly reflects higher revenue per user after a regulatory change and holiday and tournament effects, so whether those effects persist needs to be confirmed.

Capital spending and data center execution load

The AI data center expansion is a large program combining leased and self-built facilities. Depreciation was KRW 37.8 billion in Q3 2026, up 2.0% from the prior quarter, and depreciation on B300 GPUs has begun to be reflected.

If construction timing, customer demand, and investment execution do not align, margins could come under pressure.

Rising debt and capital structure

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 51.8% in 2022 to 117.5% in 2025. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 2.08 trillion, above total equity of KRW 1.77 trillion.

Equity attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 1.46 trillion, so if investment continues to be funded with debt, the room in the balance sheet will need monitoring.

10

Risk factors

Regulation and policy

Web-board games are a business whose revenue structure can shift with the regulatory environment. The company said the regulatory change raised revenue per user, but the direction of regulation is outside its control.

Participation and scale in government AI projects depend on public procurement timing, so the timing of revenue is uncertain.

Execution

The 100MW target depends on timely construction, securing customer demand, and efficient investment execution. The company has not issued specific near-term earnings guidance, which makes the near-term earnings path hard to gauge from outside. Revenue will be recognized in stages as services open.

One-time items and earnings volatility

Q3 2026 net profit partly reflected a one-time debt forgiveness gain at a subsidiary being liquidated. Q1 2026 operating profit of KRW 26.3 billion fell 52.5% from the prior quarter, and profit attributable to owners of the parent fell 15.0% in Q2 2026. Quarterly swings are large, so trends need to be read across several quarters.

12

Overall view

NHN reported Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 758.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 58.8 billion, which the company described as record quarterly results.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 32.6 billion in 2024 to KRW 132.4 billion in 2025, and quarterly operating profit rose in both Q2 and Q3 2026, which points to a change in the earnings base.

The growth came mainly from transaction growth in payments and AI GPU cloud demand in technology, which together made up about 75% of revenue. On the other side, the game segment declined from the prior quarter, and web-board revenue partly depends on the regulatory environment and tournament effects.

Expanded capital spending and the rise in the debt-to-equity ratio remain items to monitor.

Because the company has not issued specific earnings guidance, the next checkpoints are the start of cloud service and revenue recognition after January 2027, NHN Payco's full-year profitability target for 2027, and the 100MW target for end-2027. When the 2026 annual report is filed, full-year and Q4 2026 results will be confirmed in the regulatory filing.
