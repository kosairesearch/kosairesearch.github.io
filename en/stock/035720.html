Ad and commerce growth; AI monetization is the test

02

Business structure

Kakao operates in two reporting segments, Platform and Content. Platform revenue in the third quarter of 2026 rose 17% year on year to KRW 1.235 trillion, while Content revenue fell 6% to KRW 851.7 billion.

Talk Biz, the core of the platform, grew revenue 13% to KRW 632.9 billion, and its advertising and subscription revenue rose 11% to KRW 391.5 billion. Talk Display ads grew 35%, the fastest line in the group, and Business Message revenue still rose 7% despite a base effect from last year's consumer coupon program.

In commerce, Seonmulhagi and Talk Deal gross transaction value rose 13% to KRW 2.3 trillion, and self-purchase reached about 30% of commerce transaction value.

Platform other revenue, which includes Mobility and kakaopay, grew 21% to KRW 602.1 billion, and kakaopay recorded double-digit operating margins for three straight quarters. In Content, Music revenue fell 10% to KRW 508.3 billion and Story revenue fell 17%, while Media revenue rose 49% to KRW 142.8 billion.

KakaoTalk monthly active users in Korea reached 49.677 million, up about 209,000 from a year earlier, which underpins the ad and commerce base. In AI, the KakaoTalk-based assistant Kanana was officially launched on March 19, 2026, and a Kanana Space pilot is set to start in December.

On the structure side, Kakao plans a spin-off into the surviving company Kakao X, which handles investments and subsidiary management, and a newly formed Kakao AI, which runs the KakaoTalk-based AI business.

04

Earnings analysis

In annual results, revenue rose from KRW 6.8 trillion in 2022 to KRW 7.56 trillion in 2023 (up 11.1%), KRW 7.87 trillion in 2024 (up 4.2%) and KRW 8.1 trillion in 2025 (up 2.9%). Revenue has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022–2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 569.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 460.9 billion in 2023 (down 19.1%) and KRW 460.2 billion in 2024 (down 0.1%), then recovered to KRW 732 billion in 2025 (up 59.1%).

The operating margin moved from 8.4% in 2022 to 6.1% in 2023 and 5.8% in 2024, then rose to 9.0% in 2025, a margin gain that outpaced the 2.9% revenue growth.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 1.36 trillion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 1.01 trillion in 2023, returned to a profit of KRW 55.3 billion in 2024, and rose to KRW 491.5 billion in 2025 (up 789.1%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 518 billion in 2025, after a net loss of KRW 161.9 billion in 2024.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 15.2 trillion, of which controlling shareholders held KRW 11.3 trillion, and total liabilities were KRW 12.6 trillion; the debt ratio went from 69.9% in 2022 to 84.8% in 2024 and 82.5% in 2025.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 678.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.4 trillion in 2025, the largest of 2022–2025. Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 232.7 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 211.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 277 billion in the second quarter.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 2.09 trillion, down 0.6% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 240.3 billion fell 13.3% from the prior quarter but rose 15.5% from a year earlier.

The company's own release, which reflects discontinued operations (the Kakao Games sale and Kakao Healthcare), showed revenue up 6% and operating profit up 7% year on year, a different basis from the filed figures.

Third-quarter controlling-shareholder profit was KRW 159.2 billion, and the four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026 total KRW 380.7 billion.

Because controlling-shareholder profit swung from KRW 171.7 billion in the first quarter to KRW 16.3 billion in the second, non-operating factors should be checked alongside operating profit.

05

Industry analysis

In advertising, Talk Display ads grew 35% in the third quarter of 2026 and led growth, while Business Message still rose 7% despite the base effect of last year's consumer coupon program.

Commerce is in a phase where holiday demand and rising self-purchase overlap; self-purchase customers and transaction value rose 23% and 33%, respectively. Content depends heavily on album releases and box-office performance, and some observers see recovery depending on the lineup after the fourth quarter.

In AI, the appeal of invoking an assistant inside the messenger is seen as a Kakao strength because it eases user acquisition compared with separate apps.

Still, Meritz Securities said in a January 2026 report that the reaction to ChatGPT and the onboarding of kakaopay and Mobility inside KakaoTalk was smaller than expected. BNK Securities said in a July 2026 report that more evidence of AI and commerce monetization is needed before the market assigns a higher multiple.

Competition overlaps with domestic platform companies such as NAVER in parts of advertising and search. Structurally, Kakao is trimming non-core subsidiary stakes to focus resources on KakaoTalk and AI.

Daol Investment & Securities also noted that deconsolidating loss-making subsidiaries could lift the operating margin. Overall, advertising and commerce are in a growth phase, content faces headwinds, and AI is in an investment phase.

06

Outlook

The nearest checkpoint is the start of the Kanana Space pilot in December 2026. The company plans to roll out new services such as Modu AI and Kanvas during 2026 and set a target of 10 million monthly active AI users.

Daol Investment & Securities said in an April 2026 report that 2026 is a period of feature expansion and that fee revenue would become meaningful from 2027. The same firm said in a May 2026 report that revenue, operating profit and operating margin would all improve in 2026.

The spin-off will split Kakao into the surviving company Kakao X and the new Kakao AI, and the timing differs across reports: Korea Investment & Securities cited January 2027 in October 2026, while Hankyung reported February 2027 in November 2026.

Kakao also moved forward half of a KRW 300 billion plan that Kakao X had intended to execute after the split, through the KRW 150 billion share buyback and cancellation announced in November 2026.

The next regular report will cover the fourth quarter of 2026 and the 2026 annual report, and the key question is whether the 2025 profit recovery continues. Alongside that, investors can track whether the AI service loss widens again next quarter and whether Talk Biz growth holds. Content recovery depends on the artist schedule and the lineup of works.

08

Bull factors

Joint growth in Talk Biz ads and commerce

Talk Biz revenue rose 13% to KRW 632.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and advertising and subscription revenue grew 11% to KRW 391.5 billion. Talk Display ads rose 35%, suggesting that a wider product mix is working.

Seonmulhagi and Talk Deal transaction value reached KRW 2.3 trillion, up 13%, while self-purchase customers and value grew 23% and 33%, widening the commerce user base.

Profit recovery and the cleanup of loss-making units

Operating profit rose 59.1% to KRW 732 billion in 2025, lifting the operating margin to 9.0%. Daol Investment & Securities projected in a May 2026 report that the 2026 margin would improve from 9.0% to 11.8%.

The same firm saw additional support from deconsolidating loss-making subsidiaries. kakaopay posted double-digit operating margins for three consecutive quarters.

Governance restructuring and wider shareholder returns

Kakao decided in November 2026 to buy back KRW 150 billion of shares and cancel all of them. The move brings forward half of a KRW 300 billion plan that Kakao X had intended to execute after the split. IBK Securities said in a September 2026 report that combined market value could grow once the split stabilizes. That view depends on how the new structure settles in practice.

09

Bear factors

Risk of a prolonged content slump

Content revenue fell 6% to KRW 851.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026, with Music down 10% and Story down 17%. Media rose 49%, but that was not enough to offset the decline across Content.

Music and Story results swing with artist activity and box-office performance, so the timing of recovery depends on the lineup after the fourth quarter.

AI service losses and the timing of monetization

The AI service unit's operating loss widened to about KRW 64 billion in the third quarter of 2026 from the prior quarter. Outsourcing and infrastructure costs, driven by AI model training and operations, rose 8% from the prior quarter.

Daol Investment & Securities said in a May 2026 report that 2026 is a transition period for improving AI agent quality and that monetization will take time. BNK Securities cited limited buzz around AI services as a near-term burden.

Quarterly profit volatility and lower target prices

Controlling-shareholder profit moved sharply, at KRW 171.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 16.3 billion in the second and KRW 159.2 billion in the third. Third-quarter operating profit fell 13.3% from the prior quarter.

Hankyung reported in early November 2026 that ten securities firms lowered their target prices on Kakao. The FnGuide consensus also fell from its level at the fourth-quarter 2025 outlook.

10

Risk factors

Timing of the governance restructuring

The spin-off splits Kakao into the surviving Kakao X and the new Kakao AI, and reports differ on whether it happens in January or February 2027. How each business is valued after the split could change how shareholders read the company. One-off accounting tied to Kakao Games and the business restructuring has been cited as a near-term burden.

AI monetization and competition

The Kanana Space pilot and new AI services are concentrated in the rest of 2026, so any delay could push back monetization. Reactions to external AI services inside KakaoTalk were seen as smaller than expected, so AI usage in the messenger is still being tested. If the 10 million monthly AI user target slips, the payback period for AI spending could also lengthen.

Content results and labor issues

Music and Story revenue declines are limiting overall revenue growth in the Content segment. BNK Securities listed labor issues among near-term burdens. Content revenue is sensitive to artist activity and box-office results, so quarterly results can swing widely.

What to watch next

  1. December 2026

    Start of the Kanana Space pilot inside KakaoTalk. This is the first point at which AI usage in the messenger and its monetization approach become visible.

  2. By year-end 2026

    Whether Modu AI and Kanvas launch as planned and whether monthly active AI users reach 10 million. These should be read alongside the AI service unit's operating loss in the next quarter.

  3. January–February 2027 (reports differ)

    The spin-off date and related filings for Kakao X and Kakao AI. This is when the financial structure of each business first becomes visible.

  4. Early 2027 (date not confirmed)

    The fourth-quarter 2026 results and the 2026 annual report. The fourth quarter is not yet in the filed record, and the key check is whether the operating margin holds near the 9.0% reached in 2025.

12

Overall view

In the third quarter of 2026, Talk Biz advertising, commerce and kakaopay drove growth, and operating profit rose 15.5% year on year. On the filed annual figures, 2025 operating profit rose 59.1% to KRW 732 billion, and the 9.0% margin was the highest of 2022–2025.

Content revenue fell 6%, and the AI service unit posted an operating loss of about KRW 64 billion in the third quarter. Controlling-shareholder profit swings sharply quarter to quarter, and the last four quarters total KRW 380.7 billion.

On governance, the KRW 150 billion buyback and cancellation and the early-2027 spin-off are cited as key variables for shareholder returns and restructuring. Several securities firms lowered target prices after September 2026.

The bull case rests on advertising and commerce indicators and profit recovery, while the bear case rests on the content slump and delayed AI monetization. The next checkpoints are the Kanana Space pilot in December 2026, the year-end AI user target, the early-2027 spin-off, and the fourth-quarter 2026 results.
