Earnings analysis

Annual sales were KRW 20.1 billion in 2022, KRW 20.2 billion in 2023, KRW 33.5 billion in 2024, and KRW 25.3 billion in 2025, with 2024 the largest year and 2022 the smallest in the period. Sales rose 65.8 percent in 2024 and then fell 24.6 percent in 2025.

Operating losses were KRW 9.02 billion in 2022, KRW 10.4 billion in 2023, KRW 3.71 billion in 2024, and KRW 831 million in 2025, which means four consecutive years of operating losses from 2022 through 2025.

The operating margin improved from -44.9 percent in 2022 and -51.5 percent in 2023 to -11.1 percent in 2024 and -3.3 percent in 2025, but the improvement came while sales were shrinking, so cost of sales and selling and administrative expense lines should be checked against the full-year filing.

The preliminary consolidated results released on February 23, 2026 showed an operating loss of about KRW 200 million and a net loss of KRW 9.9 billion for 2025, which differ from the audited periodic report figures of KRW 831 million and KRW 15.7 billion.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from losses of KRW 14.1 billion in 2022, KRW 7.98 billion in 2023, and KRW 3.48 billion in 2024 to a profit of KRW 24.3 billion in 2025.

The gap with the 2025 net loss of KRW 15.7 billion reflects non-controlling interests moving in the opposite direction, and because the controlling profit is far larger than the KRW 831 million operating loss, non-operating items appear to have weighed heavily.

Quarterly sales were KRW 6.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 5.12 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 5.21 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 5.99 billion in the second quarter, and KRW 4.05 billion in the third quarter of 2026, while operating losses were KRW 162 million, KRW 932 million, KRW 3.91 billion, KRW 1.07 billion, and KRW 1.19 billion in the same order, marking five straight quarters of operating losses.

WiseReport attributes the weakness in the first half of 2026 to slower demand in home appliance and electric vehicle markets, higher fixed costs, and a higher cost ratio.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 27.1 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 accounts for most of the KRW 23.6 billion total over the last four quarters, and the net loss of KRW 2.99 billion in the third quarter of 2026 is the largest loss since the first quarter of 2026.
