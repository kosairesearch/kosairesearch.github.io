02

Business structure

PC Direct was founded in 1998 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2002. It is an authorized Intel retail partner in Korea and imports and distributes products from overseas brands, including Gigabyte motherboards and graphics cards, Seagate storage, and SteelSeries and CHERRY peripherals.

According to a third-party aggregator (Butler), the 2026 Q2 sales mix was 41% motherboards and graphics cards, 21% processors, 20% storage and 17% other products such as drones.

Its customers include makers of DVRs, PVRs, storage devices, set-top boxes and PC manufacturers, and a 2023 company analysis described it as an intermediary between overseas component suppliers and domestic finished-goods makers. It runs its own online mall (OFF&ON), a nationwide dealer network and retail channels.

In recent years it has widened its range into drones, robot vacuum cleaners and home appliances, and its corporate history lists distribution agreements with Dreame, DJI, Check Point, SteelSeries and AKKO.

A 2023 analysis noted that two other Korean dealers also handle Intel products, so the Intel line has multiple distributors. Because its core assets are contracts with overseas brands, continuity of those agreements and changes in the product lineup are the main structural variables.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 312.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 304.7 billion in 2023 (down 2.4% year on year), then rose to KRW 333.8 billion in 2024 (up 9.5%) and KRW 373 billion in 2025 (up 11.8%), growing for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 806 million in 2022 to KRW 3.28 billion in 2023 (up 307.3%), fell to KRW 1.85 billion in 2024 (down 43.6%), and reached KRW 4.25 billion in 2025 (up 129.7%), the largest year in 2022–2025.

The operating margin was 0.3% in 2022, 1.1% in 2023, 0.6% in 2024 and 1.1% in 2025, which remains thin. The company attributed the 2025 improvement to business expansion and a better profit structure, as reported in March 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung between losses and profits, with a net loss of KRW 4.14 billion in 2022, a return to profit of KRW 766 million in 2023, a loss of KRW 665 million in 2024, and a return to profit of KRW 2.52 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amount as controlling-shareholder net income in each of these four years. The quarterly trend is clearer.

Revenue went from KRW 87.9 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 101.7 billion in Q4 2025 (up 15.7% quarter on quarter), KRW 105.4 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 101.4 billion in Q2 2026 (down 3.8%), and KRW 104 billion in Q3 2026 (up 2.5% quarter on quarter and up 18.3% year on year).

Operating profit has risen for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, and in Q2 2026 it grew 2.7% even though revenue declined. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.18 billion in Q3 2026, and the four quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026 total KRW 6.16 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW -8.25 billion in 2024 and turned to KRW 5.32 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

A FnGuide company overview said hardware demand is being reshaped around AI PCs, edge devices and cloud storage. According to the same source, PC Direct's standalone revenue for the first nine months of 2025 rose 10.9% year on year, and its operating and net income turned positive.

However, a 2023 company analysis judged that PC distribution is directly affected by macroeconomic conditions and corporate capital spending.

The same analysis noted that replacement demand from the end of Windows 10 support was expected, but high interest rates and corporate cost cutting made a recovery harder to forecast. On competition, two other Korean dealers also handle Intel products, so the Intel line has several distributors.

The 2023 analysis also said Intel holds more than 90% share in server and workstation processors. Because motherboards, graphics cards and storage depend on overseas brand supply rights, keeping those supplier contracts is central to competitiveness.

The cycle position is mixed: Q3 2026 revenue rose 18.3% year on year, while Q2 2026 revenue fell 3.8% quarter on quarter, so the data do not point in one direction.

The next checkpoints are whether quarterly revenue stays around KRW 100 billion after Q4 2026 and whether the operating margin holds near the Q3 2026 level.

06

Outlook

In a corporate value-up plan disclosed on March 27, 2026, the company said it will expand the share of high value-added products to establish a more stable profit structure.

Execution tasks include maximizing profitability through efficient inventory management and stronger foreign exchange risk response, expanding high-margin lineups through global partnerships, and developing new business models.

On shareholder returns, it said it will run a dividend policy that shares operating results within a range that maintains financial soundness, and will keep an appropriate dividend level.

At the general meeting held the same day, the business purpose was expanded to include sales and distribution of artificial intelligence and robot-related products, which the company cited as part of broadening its business scope.

Management also changed: on March 27, 2026, the board appointed Lee Seong-soo as CEO after the term of Seo Dae-sik expired.

In terms of product exposure, PC Direct will take part in G-Star 2026 at BEXCO in Busan from November 19 to 22, 2026, with Intel as main sponsor and SteelSeries and CHERRY as sub-sponsors, setting up a 114-unit PC demo zone. The plan is directional, and whether it is being carried out will show up in figures in the 2026 annual report.

Bull factors

Consecutive quarterly profit growth

Operating profit rose for four straight quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, and Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.61 billion was up 375.6% year on year. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 2.18 billion in Q3 2026, compared with KRW 77.1 million in Q3 2025.

Operating profit grew in Q2 2026 even as revenue declined, which allows an interpretation that cost and margin management is improving.

Formalized shareholder return policy

On March 27, 2026, the company disclosed a corporate value-up plan and stated a dividend policy that shares operating results within a range that maintains financial soundness. The fiscal 2025 dividend was approved at the general meeting, with a total payout of about KRW 1 billion and a payout ratio of 39.98%.

The company stated that it meets the high-dividend company criteria, so shareholder returns are now written into its stated policy.

Insider open-market purchases

Director Seo Jae-woong bought 6,424 shares on the open market on February 4 and 10, 2026, and continued purchases on March 6 and 9. As of the March 13, 2026 filing, holdings stood at 184,488 shares, or 1.20%.

These are factual transactions, though a purchase alone does not establish a directional signal for the share price.

Bear factors

Thin operating margin and loss history

The operating margin was 0.3% in 2022, 1.1% in 2023, 0.6% in 2024 and 1.1% in 2025, which is low relative to revenue. Controlling-shareholder net income was negative in 2022 and 2024, and operating profit fell 43.6% in 2024. Revenue declined 3.8% quarter on quarter in Q2 2026. Quarterly profits have held up, but the year-to-year swings remain large.

Rising financial leverage

The debt ratio rose from 102.1% in 2022 to 113.5% in 2023, 135.5% in 2024 and 142.0% in 2025. Total liabilities of KRW 56 billion at year-end 2025 exceeded total equity of KRW 39.5 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW -8.25 billion in 2024.

A March 2026 news report referred to KRW 5.7 billion in short-term borrowings used to secure operating and liquidity funds.

Sensitivity to exchange rates and demand

In its 2026 value-up plan, the company listed stronger foreign exchange risk management as an execution task. Because a large share of its products is imported, exchange rate moves can affect costs and margins.

A 2023 analysis also judged that PC distribution is directly exposed to macroeconomic conditions, pointing in the same direction.

10

Risk factors

Financial and liquidity

The debt ratio rose to 142.0%, with liabilities exceeding total equity. Operating cash flow was negative in 2024, and a news report linked short-term borrowings to operating funding. If borrowing terms or interest rates change, cash capacity may stay limited even as profits grow. The next annual report will show whether debt and cash flow are moving in the same direction.

Exchange rates and inventory

Because the company imports and distributes overseas-brand products, exchange rate movements feed directly into purchase costs. Inventory management and foreign exchange risk response are central tasks in its 2026 plan. If inventory builds or exchange rates move unfavorably, margin gains like those in Q3 2026 could reverse.

Supplier contracts and management changes

The business relies on distribution agreements with overseas brands such as Intel and Gigabyte, so changes in contract terms or supply policy could alter the product lineup. Two other Korean dealers also handle Intel products, which adds competitive pressure.

The CEO changed in March 2026, and corporate records show a change in the largest shareholder, so the direction of governance changes deserves attention.

Overall view

PC Direct posted controlling-shareholder net losses in 2022 and 2024 and profits in 2023 and 2025, and operating profit has risen for four straight quarters through Q3 2026, indicating a recovery in earnings capacity. However, the 2025 annual operating margin of 1.1% is thin relative to revenue.

The debt ratio rose from 102.1% in 2022 to 142.0% in 2025, which leaves the financial burden as a bearish factor. On shareholder returns, the March 2026 value-up plan, the dividend approval and insider open-market purchases are all on record.

The share price sits at a deep discount to book value, but the small size of earnings makes the multiple sensitive to results. The business depends on distribution agreements with overseas brands, so maintaining those contracts and managing exchange rates and inventory are key variables.

The next checkpoint is the operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report, due around late March 2027.
