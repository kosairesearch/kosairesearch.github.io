YBM NET: Profit Recovery Amid Shrinking Revenue

Revenue has fallen for three straight years through 2025, but operating profit has stayed in the black in each quarter from Q1 to Q3 2026, following an operating loss in Q4 2025.

02

Business structure

YBM NET is an online education company offering foreign-language, lifelong, IT and job-skills courses, and it has expanded on the basis of online classes and digital content sales.

Its flagship brands are the adult online course YBM Inkang, the primary and secondary English academy YBM Engle, and the early-childhood and elementary English academy YBMECC. The company history records 2026 Korea Education Enterprise Award wins of 13 for YBM Inkang, 4 for YBM Engle and 1 for YBMECC.

Korea Management Association Consultants ranked the company first in the online foreign-language academy category of its 2026 K-BPI survey for the 19th consecutive year, which is cited as evidence of brand recognition.

The company says its AI tutor and Q&A systems raise learning engagement, while an AI assessment system drives its digital transformation, and overseas expansion is also under way. The exchange classifies it under IT·software, but revenue depends more on individual learners' study demand than on corporate IT spending.

Revenue fell from KRW 62.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 51.8 billion in 2025. In its Q3 2026 results release, the company said it reshaped its portfolio around higher value-added businesses. On a cumulative 2026 basis, gross profit held near the prior-year level while selling and administrative expenses declined. Quarterly sales tend to be seasonal, shifting with school holidays, exam periods and public holidays.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 62.6 billion in 2022, KRW 60.5 billion in 2023 (down 3.3% year on year), KRW 58.3 billion in 2024 (down 3.6%) and KRW 51.8 billion in 2025 (down 11.1%), declining for three straight years from 2023 to 2025. Within 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for revenue and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 4.78 billion in 2022 to a peak of KRW 5.12 billion in 2023 (up 7.1%), then fell to KRW 2.79 billion in 2024 (down 45.5%) and KRW 950 million in 2025 (down 66.0%), a two-year decline. The operating margin moved from 7.6% in 2022 to 8.5% in 2023, 4.8% in 2024 and 1.8% in 2025.

In 2025, revenue fell 11.1% while operating profit fell 66.0%, so profitability eroded much faster than the sales base.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders dropped from KRW 7.63 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.6 billion in 2025, and total net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 13.7 billion in Q3 2025 with operating profit of KRW 662 million, then KRW 12.6 billion in Q4 2025 (down 8.5% quarter on quarter) with an operating loss of KRW 70.86 million, a swing into loss.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 12.9 billion (up 2.2% quarter on quarter) with operating profit of KRW 483 million, a return to profit, and Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 12.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 298 million (down 38.3%).

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 13.3 billion (up 9.0% quarter on quarter, down 3.4% year on year), with operating profit of KRW 964 million (up 45.8% year on year). Press reports put the operating margin at 7.3% in Q3 2026, up from 4.8% a year earlier.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 837 million in Q3 2026, up 20.2% year on year. Press reports on the Q3 2026 filing, dated November 13, 2026, put cumulative January to September 2026 operating profit at KRW 1.75 billion, up 71.0% year on year.

Over the last four quarters, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 1.99 billion, and quarterly swings are large enough that one or two quarters do not establish a trend.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 15.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.84 billion in 2023, KRW 5.98 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.07 billion in 2025. The debt ratio declined from 79.0% in 2022 to 52.8% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 27.7 billion and total equity of KRW 52.4 billion at year-end 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The e-learning market has pronounced seasonality tied to school holidays, exam periods and public holidays, and demand shifts with new course launches and promotional events.

According to company overview data, consolidated revenue in the first half of 2025 fell 10.0% year on year, operating profit fell 59.2% and net profit fell 21.4%.

In online foreign-language education in Korea, adult online courses and children's English academies compete side by side, and the company says it has ranked first in the online foreign-language academy category of the K-BPI survey for 19 consecutive years.

Upgrading services with AI learning features is the company's main response. Peers in the same IT·software classification, such as Samsung SDS and NHN, rely mainly on corporate IT service demand, so their industry exposure differs from YBM NET's.

The revenue decline since 2023 reflects both sector factors and company-specific shrinkage, and whether sector recovery signals translate into company sales will show up in future quarterly revenue.

In Q3 2026, operating profit rose even as revenue fell year on year, which points to cost restructuring rather than sales growth as the main driver. Quarterly operating profit has swung widely, from an operating loss in Q4 2025 to a decline in Q2 2026 and KRW 964 million in Q3 2026, indicating high volatility.

Outlook

In its Q3 2026 results release, the company said it is pairing a reshaping of its portfolio around higher value-added businesses with strict cost control. It also said it will focus on stable profit generation and enhancing shareholder value, based on the competitiveness of its advanced edtech content.

According to press reports, cumulative revenue for January to September 2026 fell 2.4% year on year, while cumulative operating profit rose 71.0%. At the regular shareholders' meeting in March 2026, amendments to the articles of incorporation and the director compensation limit were approved.

The revenue contribution from the AI tutor and AI assessment systems does not appear as a separate metric, so the effect of diversification will need to be confirmed through changes in the revenue mix.

Revenue of KRW 51.8 billion in 2025 is the base, and Q3 2026 revenue was still below the prior-year level, so a return to revenue growth is an important checkpoint.

The operating margin moved from 1.8% in 2025 to 7.3% in Q3 2026 according to press reports, and the 2026 annual report, the next regular filing, will show whether the full-year margin reflects this recovery. Because an operating loss was recorded in Q4 2025, the sign of Q4 2026 operating profit is the next checkpoint.

Bull factors

Operating profit back in the black

After an operating loss of KRW 70.86 million in Q4 2025, operating profit has been positive for three straight quarters from Q1 to Q3 2026. Q3 2026 operating profit rose 45.8% year on year to KRW 964 million, and press reports put the operating margin at 7.3%, up from 4.8%. The company attributes the increase to lower selling and administrative expenses.

Stronger balance sheet

The debt ratio fell from 79.0% in 2022 to 52.8% in 2025, and total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 52.4 billion. Total liabilities of KRW 27.7 billion are smaller than equity. Maintaining the financial base through a period of declining profit is cited as a bullish factor.

AI transition and brand base

The effect of adopting the AI learning and assessment systems can be seen in future learning engagement and changes in the revenue mix. Company history records first place in the online foreign-language academy category of the 2026 K-BPI survey for 19 consecutive years. The brand base is cited as a factor supporting the restructuring of its revenue model.

Bear factors

Revenue down three years running

Revenue fell from KRW 62.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 51.8 billion in 2025, declining for three straight years from 2023. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 13.3 billion was still down 3.4% year on year, so signs of a sales recovery are not yet clear. The company itself frames its strategy around profitability given the smaller revenue base.

Lower margins and shrinking cash flow

The operating margin fell from 7.6% in 2022 to 1.8% in 2025, and operating profit declined for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025. Operating cash flow dropped from KRW 15.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.07 billion in 2025. Several more quarters of profit would be needed before 2026 can be read as a full-year trend reversal.

Quarterly volatility and seasonality

Q4 2025 swung to an operating loss of KRW 70.86 million, and Q2 2026 operating profit fell 38.3% quarter on quarter to KRW 298 million. E-learning demand follows school holidays, exam periods and public holidays, so quarterly results can swing sharply.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 72.36 million in Q4 2025, so the stability of recent quarterly results still needs more evidence.

Risk factors

Seasonal demand and shrinking sales

E-learning demand moves with seasonal factors such as school holidays, exam periods and public holidays, as well as new course launches and events. With revenue down for three straight years, quarterly swings could push profit back into an operating loss. The Q4 2025 operating loss illustrates that possibility.

AI investment cost and payoff

The investment scale of the AI tutor and AI assessment systems and their revenue contribution do not appear separately in disclosures. If profit is being defended through cuts in selling and administrative expenses, expanding AI investment could add cost pressure. The payoff from diversification will need to be confirmed through future changes in the revenue mix.

Thin cash flow and earnings base

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 15.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.07 billion in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 1.99 billion over the last four quarters, a small base in which one-off costs or profit swings could change the earnings trend.

The debt ratio has declined, but the direction of cash generation needs to be confirmed in the next regular report.

Overall view

YBM NET's revenue fell from KRW 62.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 51.8 billion in 2025, a shrinking sales base. Over the same period, the operating margin dropped from 7.6% to 1.8%, and Q4 2025 recorded an operating loss.

In 2026, operating profit has stayed positive from Q1 through Q3, and Q3 operating profit rose 45.8% year on year to KRW 964 million. The company cites lower selling and administrative expenses and a portfolio reshaped around higher value-added businesses as drivers of the recovery.

The debt ratio fell from 79.0% in 2022 to 52.8% in 2025, while operating cash flow declined from KRW 15.7 billion to KRW 5.07 billion.

Bullish factors are the return to profit, the financial base and brand indicators, while bearish factors are three straight years of revenue decline, a low operating margin and quarterly volatility.

The weight of these factors will likely depend on how long operating profit stays positive through Q4 2026 and in the 2026 annual report.
