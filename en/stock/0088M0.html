Early Profitability, Testing Expansion Amid Rising Competition

02

Business structure

Mezoo is a digital healthcare company established in 2018 by a team of biomedical engineering PhDs from Yonsei University's Wonju campus who had been researching wearable ECG technology since 2007.

The company provides a B2B SaaS-style ambulatory remote patient monitoring (aRPM) solution that combines biomedical telemetry technology with AI analysis.

Its flagship product, HiCardi, is an integrated monitoring system consisting of a SmartPatch, a mobile application (SmartView), and central monitoring software (LiveStudio).

The next-generation HiCardi M350, which received manufacturing approval from Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety in 2026, continuously tracks multi-channel ECG signals and heart rate, detects suspected arrhythmias, and monitors continuous body temperature, with up to 48 hours of battery life on a single charge.

Domestic sales have run since 2022 through an exclusive distribution agreement with strategic investor Dong-A ST, and that contract was extended in 2026 with an annual contracted volume of 12,000 units.

Dong-A ST received exclusive sales rights to the HiCardi lineup from Mezoo in 2022, and the product family has been adopted at about 53% of tertiary general hospitals nationwide, along with more than 700 hospitals and clinics.

The company has secured major global approvals including CE, FDA, and ANVISA, completing medical device registration in more than 19 countries across Europe, South America, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.

In Korea's patient-monitoring market, Seers Technology is regarded as the industry's largest company by market capitalization, and alongside existing rivals such as ATsensor and Wellysis, Huinno's renewed KOSDAQ listing attempt has reshaped the ECG and in-hospital monitoring market into a three-way contest.

Mezoo listed on KOSDAQ on March 26, 2026 under the technology growth special listing track, with Shinhan Investment Corp as lead underwriter.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose 211.8% from KRW 2.37 billion in 2024 to KRW 7.38 billion in 2025. Over the same period the operating loss narrowed from KRW 5.95 billion to KRW 2.83 billion, remaining in loss territory but with the loss shrinking, while the operating margin improved from -251.5% in 2024 to -38.4% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling interests turned from a net loss of KRW 7.07 billion in 2024 to net income of KRW 2.76 billion in 2025, and total net income including non-controlling interests followed the same pattern at KRW 2.76 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow, however, moved in the opposite direction, with outflows widening from -KRW 4.92 billion in 2024 to -KRW 5.39 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, 1Q 2026 revenue of KRW 3.91 billion was accompanied by operating profit of KRW 957 million and controlling-interest net income of KRW 955 million, marking a quarterly swing to profit.

In 2Q 2026, revenue grew 45.0% quarter over quarter to KRW 5.67 billion, but operating profit fell 8.6% to KRW 875 million, indicating profitability did not scale at the same pace as revenue.

By contrast, 2Q controlling-interest net income rose 14.9% quarter over quarter to KRW 1.1 billion, diverging from the operating profit trend. Across the four quarters of 2025, cumulative controlling-interest net income reached KRW 2.76 billion on full-year revenue of KRW 7.38 billion.

According to the securities registration statement, the company posted modest profits even before listing, in December 2025 (about KRW 180 million) and January 2026 (about KRW 140 million), suggesting the approach to breakeven began prior to the IPO.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's patient-monitoring market is in a phase of growing wearable device adoption driven by nursing staff shortages and demand to streamline ward workflows.

Seers Technology, which counts Daewoong Pharmaceutical as a strategic investor, holds the top market-cap position in the industry, and leveraging its ECG screening solution MobiCare and inpatient monitoring platform Think, it reported adoption at roughly 12,000 hospital beds last year—about 1.7% of Korea's roughly 700,000 total beds—indicating overall market penetration remains at an early stage.

Competitors ATsensor, Wellysis, and SkyLabs have also entered the market with patch- and ring-type form factors, and Huinno's renewed KOSDAQ listing push has further reshaped the competitive landscape into a three-way contest.

Within tertiary general hospitals, Mezoo holds a relatively leading position with about 53% adoption, but expansion into general and specialty hospitals as well as overseas markets remains comparatively early-stage versus peers. Overseas, the company has secured U.S.

FDA 510(k) clearance along with European CE, Japanese PMDA, and Saudi SFDA approvals, but its U.S. business remains at the proof-of-concept stage with medical institutions.

Industry observers note that, separate from rising competition, hospitals' adoption of patient-care products tailored to staffing shortages and workflow simplification is supporting growth in the underlying market.

06

Outlook

The company set a 2026 full-year revenue target of KRW 15.4 billion, which it has described as a conservative figure based on an assumed supply of about 8,500 hospital beds. Management has stated that, factoring in actual sales momentum, bed supply could expand to a range of 10,000 to 15,000 beds.

Hana Securities forecast that, considering expansion into tertiary hospitals including Severance Hospital and revenue from the central monitoring software LiveStudio, Mezoo was likely to exceed its own revenue guidance, as reported around May 28, 2026.

On the product side, the company received manufacturing approval for its next-generation HiCardi M350 from Korea's drug safety ministry in May 2026, and plans to add oxygen saturation (SpO2) and blood pressure functions in stages through separate clinical trials and approval amendments.

The exclusive domestic distribution contract with Dong-A ST sets an annual contracted volume of 12,000 units for 2026, supporting visibility into domestic revenue.

Overseas, the company is running proof-of-concept programs across roughly 30 medical institutions in six U.S. states and has stated a goal of securing an independent CMS reimbursement code in the United States within the year.

The consumer wellness platform HiCardi Wellness was listed in the securities registration statement for a 2027 launch, but management has said the underlying technology is complete and an earlier launch could follow validation.

08

Bull factors

Continuity of the Profit Turnaround

Mezoo posted operating profit for two consecutive quarters in 1Q and 2Q 2026, signaling a move away from its earlier loss-making profile. Controlling-interest net income also rose 14.9% quarter over quarter in 2Q, reinforcing the direction of the improvement.

On an annual basis, 2025 revenue grew 211.8% year over year while the operating loss shrank to less than half its prior size, a favorable underlying trend.

Domestic Distribution Network and Reimbursement-Backed Revenue

The exclusive distribution contract with Dong-A ST allows Mezoo to use a nationwide sales network, with a contracted volume of 12,000 units set for 2026. The company has already secured health insurance reimbursement codes such as ECG bedside monitoring, giving it a recurring rather than one-off revenue base.

Its roughly 53% adoption rate among tertiary general hospitals can also serve as a reference point when selling into additional hospitals.

Expanding Product Lineup and Overseas Approvals

Approval of the multi-parameter HiCardi M350 by Korea's drug safety authority is expanding the product line from an ECG-centric device to a multi-vital-sign platform. The company has secured medical device approvals in more than 19 countries, including U.S.

FDA clearance, building a regulatory foundation for overseas expansion. Longer-term growth options also remain open through the μBIC chip semiconductor strategy and a consumer wellness platform.

09

Bear factors

Signs of Slowing Operating Leverage

2Q 2026 revenue grew 45.0% quarter over quarter, yet operating profit fell 8.6%, meaning profitability did not keep pace with revenue growth. On an annual basis, the 2025 operating loss of KRW 2.83 billion still leaves the company in loss territory.

Operating cash flow outflows actually widened from -KRW 4.92 billion in 2024 to -KRW 5.39 billion in 2025, a divergence from the improvement seen in reported earnings.

Intensifying Competition

While Seers Technology retains the top market-cap position in the industry, the number of competitors is growing as ATsensor, Wellysis, and SkyLabs are now joined by Huinno's renewed listing effort.

Adoption at tertiary general hospitals is already fairly advanced, meaning further growth must come from general and specialty hospitals, segments with different pricing and sales dynamics.

Whether multi-parameter and on-device AI technology, Mezoo's key points of differentiation, will translate into actual market share gains has not yet been confirmed.

Supply-Demand Overhang Factors

Analysts have attributed some share price pressure to profit-taking by early financial investors and the release of lock-up shares. While the lock-up period for the largest shareholder and related parties runs three years, lock-ups for some venture capital investors were set at a relatively short one to twelve months.

Because the volume of freely tradable shares increases in stages after listing, supply-demand variables could persist.

10

Risk factors

Policy and Reimbursement Risk

Because the core of Mezoo's revenue comes from domestic hospital sales tied to health insurance reimbursement codes, any change in reimbursement rates or coverage criteria could directly affect its revenue structure.

The company's revenue contracted in 2024 due to supply-side disruptions in the medical sector, illustrating its exposure to similar external variables.

Distribution Channel Concentration Risk

Domestic revenue is heavily dependent on a single exclusive distributor, Dong-A ST, so any change in contract terms or the partner's sales strategy could have an outsized effect on results. Because the contract is renewed on a multi-year basis, the terms at the next renewal point will also warrant monitoring.

Overseas Execution Risk

The U.S. business remains at the proof-of-concept stage across roughly 30 medical institutions in six states, and the timing of securing a CMS reimbursement code or generating meaningful revenue has not been confirmed.

There may be a lag between having obtained numerous overseas approvals and actually contributing to revenue, warranting close monitoring of execution speed.

12

Overall view

Mezoo grew 2025 revenue 211.8% year over year and narrowed its operating loss, and has extended that improvement into 2026 with consecutive quarterly operating profits in 1Q and 2Q.

At the same time, 2Q operating profit declined quarter over quarter despite higher revenue, showing that profitability improvement has not kept pace with top-line growth.

Domestically, the company has built a base through exclusive distribution with Dong-A ST and roughly 53% adoption among tertiary general hospitals, but the competitive landscape has grown more complex with Seers Technology and other incumbents now joined by new entrants.

Overseas, Mezoo has established a regulatory foundation including U.S. FDA clearance, but its business remains at the proof-of-concept stage, meaning meaningful revenue contribution will take more time.

Investors should weigh the quarterly operating margin trend, the pace of execution under the Dong-A ST distribution contract, and progress in the U.S. market together when forming a view.
