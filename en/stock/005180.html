02

Business structure

Binggrae runs its dairy business as a single segment and manages its products in a chilled category and a frozen and other category.

The chilled category centers on Banana Flavored Milk and includes yogurt and similar drinkable dairy products, while the frozen category includes ice cream brands such as Melona, Bungeo Ssamanko and Together. Domestic sales are highly sensitive to household consumption.

In the third quarter of 2026, lower chilled sales and a high base in frozen products both weighed on revenue. Overseas, the company operates subsidiaries in the United States, China, Vietnam and Australia and is widening direct-sales channels there.

Overseas sales rose 17.7% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, with steady growth in major markets including the United States, China and Vietnam.

Standalone export sales grew from KRW 104.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 172.2 billion in 2025, and DS Investment & Securities projects exports will pass KRW 200 billion in 2027. Export items are expanding from Melona to Bungeo Ssamanko and other ice cream products.

After establishing an Australian subsidiary, the company has also begun producing Banana Flavored Milk through a local contract manufacturer under OEM arrangements.

A corporate value-up plan announced in early March 2026 includes merging its ice cream subsidiary into Binggrae to streamline operations and expand in key markets such as the United States.

In domestic operations, the pace of volume recovery is the key variable, while in overseas operations, securing distribution networks is central.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 1.27 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.39 trillion in 2023 (up 10.0% year on year), KRW 1.46 trillion in 2024 (up 4.9%) and KRW 1.49 trillion in 2025 (up 1.8%). Sales increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit jumped from KRW 39.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 112.2 billion in 2023 (up 184.8%), and reached KRW 131.3 billion in 2024 (up 17.0%), the largest year in the period. Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 88.4 billion, down 32.7%, and the operating margin fell from 9.0% in 2024 to 5.9%.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 103.2 billion in 2024, the largest of the period, and fell 46.2% to KRW 55.6 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was also reported at KRW 55.6 billion for 2025.

In April 2024, IBK Securities analyzed that amortization of intangible assets rose to about KRW 9.1 billion a year in 2021 to 2023 after the acquisition of its ice cream subsidiary, and expected this burden to ease from the fourth quarter of 2025.

An April 2025 first-quarter preview from IBK Securities cited weaker domestic consumption, higher prices for cocoa and coffee, and rising labor and logistics costs as margin headwinds.

Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 58.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 turned into an operating loss of KRW 10.9 billion in the fourth quarter, then returned to operating profit of KRW 13.8 billion in the first quarter of 2026.

Operating profit reached KRW 69.7 billion in the second quarter of 2026, up 406.8% from the prior quarter. The third-quarter figure of KRW 65.2 billion was down 6.5% from the prior quarter but up 10.7% year on year.

Press reports said third-quarter selling, general and administrative expenses were KRW 81.8 billion, down 12.1% from KRW 93.2 billion a year earlier. Profit attributable to owners of the parent totaled KRW 8.72 billion over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

Operating cash flow stayed positive, moving from negative KRW 1.12 billion in 2022 to KRW 156.7 billion in 2023, KRW 147.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 114.3 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic beverage market is facing weak household consumption and intensifying competition at the same time. Lotte Chilsung Beverage's beverage segment reported third-quarter 2026 standalone sales of KRW 513.3 billion, down 4.7% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 29.0 billion, down 47.4%.

Lotte Chilsung cited higher raw and packaging material prices linked to Middle East-related issues in the first half of 2026 as a cost burden. In the same quarter, its beverage exports rose 6.1%, supported by demand for brands such as Milkis and Lecsbee, which partly offset domestic weakness.

Binggrae's third-quarter 2026 sales were also reported to be hurt by unfavorable weather during the peak season and a high base. Ice cream sales are highly weather-dependent, and in April 2024 IBK Securities pointed to early heat and summer weather patterns as key drivers of peak-season sales.

Overseas, based on IBK Securities analysis in January 2025, sales of Melona in the United States were strong, and Canadian exports recovered after tariff issues. Expansion of plant-based Melona into Europe, including the United Kingdom and France, was cited as a medium-term export opportunity.

On competitive positioning, Binggrae's overseas business has shown growth in the United States, China and Vietnam, while its domestic chilled category appears exposed to intensifying competition in the beverage market.

06

Outlook

A Binggrae representative said in the third-quarter 2026 results release that the company will continue to focus on profitability through cost reduction, management of low-margin products and efficiency in selling, general and administrative expenses.

The same statement said the company plans measures to stabilize its domestic market and lift sales. The merger of the ice cream subsidiary aims to streamline operations and eliminate duplicate costs.

In a March 2026 report, IBK Securities analyst Kim Tae-hyun said merger-related costs would limit near-term earnings expectations through the second quarter, while profitability improvement from removing duplicate costs was expected to take hold in the second half.

In its November 16, 2026 report, DS Investment & Securities projected 2027 consolidated sales of KRW 1.6 trillion and operating profit of KRW 154.9 billion, representing increases of 3% and 6% year on year.

In the same report, the firm highlighted the expansion of ice cream export items beyond Melona to products including Bungeo Ssamanko and Ppangttoa.

In its October 21, 2026 report, Yuanta Securities projected the operating margin would improve to 9.7% in 2026 and 9.8% in 2027, citing cost efficiency and expanded overseas sales. These are broker estimates rather than company guidance.

The next checkpoint is the fourth-quarter operating margin that will appear in the 2026 annual business report, along with whether the decline in domestic chilled sales narrows.

Bull factors

Wider export lineup

Standalone export sales grew from KRW 104.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 172.2 billion in 2025. Overseas sales rose 17.7% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, with steady growth in the United States, China and Vietnam.

DS Investment & Securities highlighted export items expanding beyond Melona and projects exports will pass KRW 200 billion in 2027.

Margin recovery outlook

The operating margin fell from 9.0% in 2024 to 5.9% in 2025, but operating profit reached KRW 69.7 billion in the second quarter of 2026. Press reports said third-quarter selling, general and administrative expenses fell 12.1% year on year.

Yuanta Securities projected margins of 9.7% in 2026 and 9.8% in 2027, citing cost efficiency and overseas sales growth.

Shareholder returns maintained

Despite a 46.2% drop in profit attributable to owners of the parent in 2025, Binggrae maintained its cash dividend and recently retired treasury shares. IBK Securities viewed this as a positive sign of a strengthened shareholder return policy. The value-up plan announced in March 2026 also included a dividend policy.

Shareholder returns have continued independently of earnings swings, and their durability will depend on how fast profits recover.

Bear factors

Continued domestic sales decline

Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 456.3 billion, down 4.8% year on year. Weak household consumption reduced chilled product sales, and a high base from the prior year pushed frozen sales lower. Sales have risen for three consecutive quarters since the first quarter of 2026, but remain below the year-earlier levels. Without a clear recovery in domestic operations, total sales growth depends largely on overseas markets.

Earnings volatility and cost pressure

Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 88.4 billion, down 32.7%, and the fourth quarter of 2025 recorded an operating loss of KRW 10.9 billion. An April 2025 IBK Securities analysis cited higher cocoa and coffee prices and rising labor and logistics costs as margin headwinds.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 6.5% from the prior quarter. Profit attributable to owners of the parent totaled KRW 8.72 billion over the last four quarters.

Weather and industry competition

Ice cream sales are heavily weather-dependent, and third-quarter 2026 peak-season sales were reported to be hurt by unfavorable weather and a high base.

Lotte Chilsung Beverage's beverage segment saw third-quarter operating profit fall 47.4%, and it attributed the decline to weak domestic consumption and intensifying competition in the domestic beverage market. Binggrae's domestic chilled category is exposed to the same pressures.

Risk factors

Input and labor costs

Higher prices for key inputs such as cocoa and coffee, along with rising labor and logistics costs, were cited as factors weighing on 2025 results. Lotte Chilsung Beverage cited higher raw and packaging material prices linked to Middle East issues in the first half of 2026. The pace of margin recovery may depend on how much of the cost burden can be passed on in pricing.

Merger and integration

The merger of the ice cream subsidiary aims to streamline operations, but integration may bring temporary costs. In a March 2026 report, IBK Securities said merger-related costs would limit earnings expectations through the second quarter.

If the removal of duplicate costs and business synergies fall short, the timing of margin improvement could be delayed.

Tariffs and export dependence

Overseas growth relies on sales trends in specific markets, including the United States, China and Vietnam. An IBK Securities analysis from January 2025 noted that Canadian exports recovered after tariff issues, which shows export results are sensitive to trade conditions. The outlook for exports to pass KRW 200 billion in 2027 could also change with trade developments.

Overall view

Binggrae's third-quarter 2026 sales fell 4.8% on weak domestic demand, but operating profit rose 10.7% on a 17.7% increase in overseas sales and a 12.1% decline in selling, general and administrative expenses.

After profit fell in 2025, quarterly results in 2026 moved back into profit, although third-quarter operating profit declined from the prior quarter.

Strengths include standalone export sales growing from KRW 104.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 172.2 billion in 2025, broker forecasts of margin recovery in 2027, and the maintained cash dividend alongside share retirement.

Weaknesses include weak sales in both chilled and frozen categories domestically, the drop in the operating margin to 5.9% in 2025, the swing to an operating loss in the fourth quarter of 2025, and cost pressure from raw materials and labor.

Broker target prices were reported at KRW 120,000 by Yuanta Securities in its October 21, 2026 report, and DS Investment & Securities maintained KRW 120,000 in its November 16, 2026 report.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter results in the 2026 annual business report, the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report, and the effect of duplicate-cost removal after the merger. This report is for information only and does not express an investment opinion, rating or target price.
