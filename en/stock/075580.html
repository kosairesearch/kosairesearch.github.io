Report overview

Sejin Heavy Industries: Subcontract Volume and Overseas Expansion

Sejin Heavy Industries depends on subcontracted tank and deck house work from HD Hyundai shipbuilders. Its operating margin reached 18.2% in 2025, but quarterly profit has moved up and down through the first three quarters of 2026.

  1. 1

    Revenue in 2025 was KRW 402.7 billion, up 14.3% from the prior year, and operating profit was KRW 73.4 billion, up 104.0%. The operating margin rose from 10.2% in 2024 to 18.2%.

  2. 2

    Quarterly operating profit in the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 13.6 billion, KRW 20.4 billion and KRW 15.1 billion, a combined KRW 49.1 billion. Third-quarter operating profit rose 24.8% year on year but fell 25.6% from the prior quarter.

  3. 3

    The order backlog stood at KRW 360.5 billion at the end of 2024. Because most work is subcontracted, the build schedules of HD Hyundai shipbuilders shape earnings visibility.

  4. 4

    Overseas capacity expansion in Vietnam, the Philippines and India, along with LNG and LCO2 tanks and offshore wind substructures, is cited as a next growth driver, though delivery timing and scale are not yet fixed.

  5. 5

    Brokerage views differ. IBK Securities set a target price of KRW 21,000 in a May 2026 report, and Shinhan Investment's counterpart Shin Young Securities raised its target price in April 2026 on overseas expansion.

02

Business structure

Sejin Heavy Industries, founded in 1999, makes ship superstructures such as deck houses and tanks for LPG and LCO2 carriers and supplies them to shipbuilders. Revenue in the 2024 annual report was KRW 352.4 billion.

Domestic deck house sales were KRW 195.0 billion, export deck house sales were KRW 27.5 billion, and domestic hull sales were KRW 124.2 billion. The company recorded no plant revenue in 2023 or 2024, after KRW 63.4 billion in 2022.

Its customers are centered on HD Hyundai shipbuilders, and brokerage reports say it handles most of the subcontracted LPG tank work for HD Hyundai Heavy Industries and HD Hyundai Mipo.

Hanwha Ocean (deck houses and engine casings, ordered June 2024) and Samsung Heavy Industries (88K LPG cargo tank, ordered October 2023) also appear on its customer list. The company is described as the domestic leader in deck houses and LPG tanks.

Its 2024 production capacity was 92 deck house units and 30 hull units, with utilization of 76.1% and 66.7%. The order backlog was KRW 360.5 billion at the end of 2024.

Its Vietnamese subsidiary SEJIN VIETNAM makes superstructures, and in December 2025 the company signed a contract to occupy space in the Ninh Thuan industrial park in Vietnam. It also cites offshore wind substructures, FLNG construction and FPSO work as business areas.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 410.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 384.8 billion in 2023 (down 6.2%) and KRW 352.4 billion in 2024 (down 8.4%), then rose to KRW 402.7 billion in 2025 (up 14.3%). 2022 was the largest revenue year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 24.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 33.6 billion in 2023 (up 34.8%), KRW 36 billion in 2024 (up 7.2%) and KRW 73.4 billion in 2025 (up 104.0%), and it has risen three consecutive years since 2023. The operating margin climbed from 6.1% in 2022 to 18.2% in 2025.

In May 2025, IBK Securities said fewer deck house deliveries would be offset by favorable tank price negotiations that would improve profitability.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent went from KRW 13.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 22.7 billion in 2023 (up 70.2%), KRW 11.4 billion in 2024 (down 50.1%) and KRW 50.7 billion in 2025 (up 346.5%), with 2025 the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 59.8 billion in 2025, above the parent-attributable figure. Operating cash flow was KRW 19.5 billion in 2022, KRW 69.1 billion in 2023, KRW 31.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 57.6 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 90.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 115.7 billion in the fourth quarter (up 28.0% quarter on quarter), and operating profit jumped from KRW 12.1 billion to KRW 34.7 billion (up 185.6%).

Shinhan Investment attributed this seasonality to year-end and early-year delivery concentration and fewer working days in the third quarter.

In the first quarter of 2026 revenue was KRW 87.2 billion (down 24.6% quarter on quarter) and operating profit KRW 13.6 billion (down 60.7%), then the second quarter recovered to KRW 93.5 billion in revenue (up 7.2%) and KRW 20.4 billion in operating profit (up 49.3%).

The third quarter of 2026 posted revenue of KRW 83.5 billion (down 10.7% quarter on quarter and down 7.6% year on year) and operating profit of KRW 15.1 billion (down 25.6% quarter on quarter and up 24.8% year on year).

Combined revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 264.2 billion and combined operating profit KRW 49.1 billion.

Quarterly profit attributable to the parent was KRW 7.19 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 26.2 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 14.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 16.5 billion in the second quarter and KRW 7.14 billion in the third quarter, for a four-quarter total of KRW 64 billion. Quarterly earnings appear to swing with delivery timing and settlement items.

05

Industry analysis

Korean shipyards are holding roughly three years of order backlog, Shinhan Investment said in early 2026. Backlog at HD Hyundai group shipbuilders is reported to fill deliveries through 2027 and part of 2028, which the brokerage cited as support for Sejin's volume visibility.

Analysts note the company handles all of the subcontracted LPG tank work for HD Hyundai group yards, which lowers order uncertainty. In 2025, press coverage pointed to rising demand for high-value equipment such as LNG and LPG tanks and deck houses, driven by global shipping and eco-friendly vessel demand.

IBK Securities summarized in May 2026 that HD Korea Shipbuilding & Offshore Engineering plans to raise Vietnam capacity from 15 ships to 23 by 2030 and the Philippines from 4 to 10, and to build a new yard in Tamil Nadu, India.

In April 2026, HD Hyundai and the Indian government signed a shipbuilding cooperation MOU that includes support for equipment makers such as block and engine suppliers to enter locally. The 2024 annual report shows utilization of 76.1% for deck houses and 66.7% for hulls, leaving room to absorb higher volume.

In May 2025, IBK Securities said the company's first-quarter standalone operating margin of 19% was higher than HD Hyundai Mipo's profitability benchmark, HD HHI Samho.

Press coverage in 2025 raised concern about lower tank volume after HD Hyundai Heavy Industries acquired the Vietnamese unit of Doosan Enerbility, but brokers said the target of 40 tank deliveries a year through 2027 remains valid.

06

Outlook

In March 2026, Shinhan Investment estimated Sejin's 2026 revenue at KRW 451.8 billion and operating profit at KRW 92.2 billion. Cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 49.1 billion, a large gap from that estimate, so the full-year path depends on fourth-quarter deliveries.

In a 2025 report, IBK Securities projected that the operating margin could reach 20.5% in 2027 once LCO2 carrier and LNG bunkering vessel tank deliveries ramp up. In May 2025, the same brokerage said LNG storage tank deliveries starting in 2027 would push the operating margin above 20%.

The plan to expand Vietnam output to more than three times current levels is moving ahead alongside the Ninh Thuan industrial park contract, and the company is also pursuing new orders in the Philippines and India.

Press coverage in 2025 reported that the company is preparing to enter eco-friendly equipment markets such as offshore wind substructures. No plant revenue appeared in the 2023 or 2024 annual reports, so the timing of any resumed plant orders is the next variable.

The next items to check in filings are tank delivery timing, the pace of Vietnam capacity expansion and any new overseas orders.

Bull factors

Visibility from subcontracted volume

Shinhan Investment said in early 2026 that Korean yards hold about three years of backlog and that Sejin handles all of the subcontracted volume for HD Hyundai group yards. The same analysis saw HD Hyundai group backlog filling deliveries through 2027 and part of 2028. The order backlog stood at KRW 360.5 billion at the end of 2024, per the annual report.

Margin gains and a quarterly rebound

The operating margin rose to 18.2% in 2025 from 10.2% in 2024, and the fourth-quarter margin was in the 30% range. Second-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 20.4 billion, up 49.3% from the prior quarter and the largest of the 2026 quarters so far.

Overseas expansion alongside key customers

In a May 2026 report, IBK Securities described Sejin as the largest supplier among HD Korea Shipbuilding & Offshore Engineering's partners and set a target price of KRW 21,000.

The same report mentioned HD Korea Shipbuilding & Offshore Engineering's capacity expansion in Vietnam and the Philippines and its planned new yard in India. Sejin also plans to raise its Vietnam output to more than three times current levels.

Bear factors

High quarterly earnings volatility

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 83.5 billion, down 10.7% from the prior quarter, and profit attributable to the parent was KRW 7.14 billion, down 0.6% year on year. First-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 13.6 billion was down 60.7% from KRW 34.7 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025.

Shinhan Investment described a seasonal pattern, with deliveries concentrated at year-end and early in the year and fewer working days in the third quarter.

Customer concentration and subcontract structure

Sejin supplies subcontracted production volume in full, with its main customers concentrated in HD Hyundai group shipbuilders. Press coverage in 2025 raised concern about lower tank production volume after HD Hyundai Heavy Industries acquired the Vietnamese unit of Doosan Enerbility.

Brokers called that concern premature and said the target of 40 tank deliveries a year through 2027 remains valid.

Plant and new-business uncertainty

Plant revenue was KRW 63.4 billion in 2022 and did not appear in the 2023 or 2024 annual reports. Entry into eco-friendly equipment markets such as offshore wind substructures is described as still in preparation.

Profit attributable to the parent fell 50.1% to KRW 11.4 billion in 2024, so the time needed for diversification to show up in earnings is a bearish factor.

Risk factors

Customer concentration

Because the business is subcontract-based, any change in build schedules or order direction at HD Hyundai group shipbuilders moves Sejin's volume and delivery timing.

The order backlog stood at KRW 360.5 billion at the end of 2024, but a change in a major customer's plans could directly affect that backlog and actual deliveries.

Execution of overseas expansion

Expanding Vietnam output to more than three times current levels and pursuing new orders in the Philippines and India are still plans. Running overseas operations and securing local workers must happen together, and if production expansion slips, tank delivery schedules and the timing of margin gains could slip too.

Balance sheet and cash flow

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 338.3 billion, above total equity of KRW 299.5 billion. The debt ratio fell from 163.6% in 2022 to 112.9% in 2025, but rose again to 149.0% in 2024. Operating cash flow dropped to KRW 31.7 billion in 2024 before recovering to KRW 57.6 billion in 2025.

Overall view

Sejin's DART-based results improved clearly in 2025, with revenue of KRW 402.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 73.4 billion, and the operating margin reached 18.2%.

Combined operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 49.1 billion, and quarterly figures of KRW 13.6 billion, KRW 20.4 billion and KRW 15.1 billion show large swings against KRW 34.7 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025.

The business depends on subcontracted volume from HD Hyundai group shipbuilders, and the KRW 360.5 billion backlog at the end of 2024 along with delivery schedules through 2028 underpin visibility.

Overseas capacity expansion and LCO2 and LNG tank deliveries are growth drivers, but their timing and scale are not yet fixed. No plant revenue appeared in filings from 2024 onward, and offshore wind and other new businesses are still described as preparatory.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio fell to 112.9% at the end of 2025 from 2022, yet total liabilities of KRW 338.3 billion still exceed total equity of KRW 299.5 billion. Brokerage views differ.

IBK Securities set a target price of KRW 21,000 in May 2026 and highlighted the benefit from key customers' overseas expansion, while Shin Young Securities raised its target price in April 2026.

Strengths in order visibility and margin gains coexist with weaknesses in quarterly volatility and customer concentration, and the next checkpoints are preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results and the 2026 annual report.
