Report overview

KT: AI Infrastructure Expansion and Core Telecom Profit Recovery

KT's operating profit recovered in 2025 from 2024, but quarterly earnings have moved unevenly through Q3 2026 while the company positions AI data centers and AX as its next growth engine.

  1. 1

    Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 2.47 trillion, recovering from KRW 809.5 billion in 2024. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in 2025 was KRW 1.73 trillion.

  2. 2

    Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 7 trillion, down 1.8% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 505.3 billion, down 6.1% year on year.

  3. 3

    The company says wireless business has begun recovering since August, but expects full-year wireless revenue to remain below the 2025 level.

  4. 4

    KT has secured 911 megawatts across 20 primary sites and 500 megawatts across 10 backup sites for AI data centers, with 152 megawatts already operating, and targets more than 1 GW by 2031.

  5. 5

    Cumulative B2B orders through Q3 2026 rose more than 20% year on year, and the company expects them to convert into revenue through 2027.

02

Business structure

KT combines its core fixed and mobile telecom business with AX (AI transformation), cloud and AI data centers under an 'AX Platform Company' strategy. In a July 2026 press briefing, the company said it would invest KRW 12 trillion over three years in core telecom competitiveness and KRW 6 trillion in AI infrastructure.

About KRW 5 trillion of the AI infrastructure spending is planned for securing a 1 GW-scale AI data center and building AI edge capacity. The core AX platforms are Agentic On, an enterprise AI agent platform, and Token Factory, launched at the end of October 2026 with more than 500 AI models.

In customer service, the company upgraded its AI contact center into an agentic AICC, which banks have already adopted. Among subsidiaries, IBK Securities estimated that KT Estate's revenue, which covers hotels and property sales, rose 39.1% in Q3 2026, while content subsidiaries and KT Cloud grew 12.1% and 7.7%.

In the same quarter, KT's standalone operating profit was KRW 2.993 billion, down 12.2% year on year. Customers are split between B2C fixed, internet and TV households and B2B clients in enterprise, finance, public sector and defense. Domestic mobile is an oligopoly of three operators, with SK Telecom and LG Uplus as the main rivals.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 25.7 trillion in 2022 to KRW 26.4 trillion in 2023 (up 2.8%), KRW 26.4 trillion in 2024 (up 0.2%) and KRW 28.2 trillion in 2025 (up 6.9%). Revenue has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit followed a different path. It fell from KRW 1.69 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.65 trillion in 2023 (down 2.4%) and to KRW 809.5 billion in 2024 (down 50.9%), the smallest year in the period. It then rebounded to KRW 2.47 trillion in 2025 (up 205.0%).

Operating margin was 6.6% in 2022, 6.3% in 2023, 3.1% in 2024 and 8.7% in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 1.26 trillion in 2022 to KRW 470.3 billion in 2024 before reaching KRW 1.73 trillion in 2025 (up 268.1%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests, at KRW 1.84 trillion in 2025, is reported separately and is a different figure. The debt-to-equity ratio at the end of 2025 was 120.7%, lower than 132.7% in 2024. Operating cash flow was KRW 5.5 trillion in 2023, KRW 5.07 trillion in 2024 and KRW 4.94 trillion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit fell from KRW 538.2 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 227.4 billion in Q4 2025 (down 57.8% quarter on quarter), then recovered to KRW 482.7 billion in Q1 2026 (up 112.3%) and KRW 648.3 billion in Q2 2026 (up 34.3%). Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 505.3 billion, down 22.1% from Q2.

For Q3, IBK Securities attributed the weakness partly to security-incident follow-up costs, including SIM replacement and penalty waivers, and said standalone profit had declined for three consecutive quarters. The company said that excluding one-time gains from 2025, Q3 operating profit rose 3.5%.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, totaled KRW 1.23 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

In domestic telecom, competition for wireless subscribers and the cost of customer incentives have become key drivers of earnings swings.

Shinhan Investment Corp. estimated that about 230,000 subscribers left during a penalty-waiver period in Q1 2026, and that a six-month 100 GB data offer temporarily lowered ARPU, or average revenue per user. The company said mobile business has been gradually recovering since its customer appreciation program ended.

Management said it is shifting toward raising profitability across mobile, internet and TV rather than chasing subscriber growth in a mature market. Demand for AI data centers, by contrast, is in an expansion phase.

Mirae Asset Securities said in a September 2026 report that roughly 80% of KT's new 1 GW pipeline appears to be secured. The same report said orders are expanding beyond existing domestic conglomerates to neocloud providers and global CSPs.

The company said cumulative B2B order pipeline through Q3 grew more than 20% year on year and that related orders should convert into revenue through 2027. A fair comparison with SK Telecom and LG Uplus will require their AI data center capacity and order disclosures for the same period.

For now, the company and brokers both describe AI infrastructure demand as moving from order intake toward revenue.

06

Outlook

The company's near-term guidance centers on how quickly wireless revenue recovers. KT expects full-year wireless revenue to stay below the 2025 level. In a November 2026 report, Korea Investment & Securities said B2C ARPU has improved monthly since August and forecast B2C earnings recovery from Q4.

IBK Securities also expects earnings to enter a recovery phase from Q4. On AI infrastructure, the company said it plans to add 60 megawatts in Q4 2026. KT aims to supply more than 1 GW by 2031 and plans to invest about KRW 6 trillion over five years in AIDC, submarine cables and related AI infrastructure.

Daishin Securities projects AI-related revenue rising from KRW 900 billion in 2026 (KRW 700 billion from AIDC and KRW 200 billion from submarine cables) to KRW 4.8 trillion in 2031 (KRW 4.4 trillion from AIDC and KRW 400 billion from submarine cables).

On AX, the company said it could publicly quantify cost-saving effects from AX adoption in 2027. On shareholder returns, KT maintains an annual minimum dividend and a progressive dividend policy.

Daishin Securities said cumulative buybacks reached KRW 500 billion, with the remaining KRW 500 billion possibly executed early. Korea Investment & Securities mentioned year-end dividend expectations and a possible resumption of buybacks in 1H 2027.

08

Bull factors

AI data center secured capacity and operating progress

KT said it has secured 911 megawatts across 20 primary sites and 500 megawatts across 10 backup sites, with 152 megawatts already operating. Daishin Securities projects AI-related revenue reaching KRW 4.8 trillion by 2031. The planned addition of 60 megawatts in Q4 2026 is another marker of expansion pace.

Shareholder return policy and buyback execution

KT maintains an annual minimum dividend and has stated a progressive dividend policy. Daishin Securities said cumulative buybacks reached KRW 500 billion and the remaining KRW 500 billion could be executed early.

The year-end dividend decision and any disclosure of a buyback restart are the next checkpoints for shareholder returns.

Early signs of core profitability recovery

The company said wireless business began recovering after August. Korea Investment & Securities saw monthly ARPU improving since August. Q2 2026 operating profit rose 34.3% quarter on quarter to KRW 648.3 billion, and the company said Q3 operating profit excluding 2025 one-time gains rose 3.5%.

09

Bear factors

Delayed wireless recovery and lower quarterly profit

KT expects full-year wireless revenue to remain below the 2025 level. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 7 trillion, down 1.8% year on year, and operating profit fell 6.1%. IBK Securities said standalone profit declined for three consecutive quarters.

Security-incident follow-up costs and subscriber churn

IBK Securities attributed part of the earnings pressure to follow-up costs from the security incident, including SIM replacement and penalty waivers. Shinhan Investment Corp. estimated that about 230,000 subscribers left during the Q1 2026 penalty-waiver period. The temporary ARPU decline from a six-month data offer also remains a near-term margin burden.

AI investment payback expected after 2027

KT frames AX as a growth driver after 2027 and expects to disclose quantified cost savings only in 2027. Non-metropolitan data center projects will proceed only after tenant demand is secured. The debt-to-equity ratio stood at 120.7% at the end of 2025, so financial burden could grow as investment scales up.

10

Risk factors

Security and customer churn

If security-incident follow-up costs keep recurring, the pace of core profit recovery could slow. Customer-retention costs such as penalty waivers and data offers have acted as a drag on ARPU. Standalone profit declining for three straight quarters shows the impact is still in short-term results.

AI investment execution and financial burden

The plan to invest about KRW 6 trillion over five years in AIDC and submarine cables represents large capital spending. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 23.5 trillion, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 120.7%. Non-metropolitan projects may move at varying speeds depending on tenant demand.

Pace of AX monetization

The materials for the AI Festa 2026 presentation did not specifically quantify productivity gains or cost reductions. Token Factory launched at the end of October 2026, so its revenue contribution will take time to show.

How quickly the order pipeline converts into actual revenue and profit will determine the payback on AX investment.

What to watch next

  1. December 2026

    Year-end dividend disclosures and progress on the additional 60 megawatts of AIDC planned for Q4 are the next checkpoints. They show whether year-end dividend expectations are confirmed by formal disclosure.

  2. End of March 2027 (statutory deadline)

    The 2026 annual report is the next periodic filing. It will confirm full-year 2026 operating margin and debt-to-equity ratio on a DART-filed basis.

  3. Early 2027

    The Q4 2026 earnings release is the next quarterly checkpoint. IBK Securities expects earnings to enter a recovery phase from this quarter.

  4. 1H 2027

    Whether buybacks resume and whether the remaining KRW 500 billion is executed early are the items to watch. Korea Investment & Securities and Daishin Securities each mentioned these points.

  5. 2027

    Quantified cost savings from AX adoption are the item to watch. The company has said it will disclose these more concretely in 2027.

12

Overall view

KT's operating profit recovered to KRW 2.47 trillion in 2025 from 2024, and operating cash flow stayed between KRW 4.94 trillion and KRW 5.5 trillion from 2023 through 2025.

However, quarterly operating profit in 2026 has moved unevenly, at KRW 482.7 billion in Q1, KRW 648.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 505.3 billion in Q3, with Q3 down 6.1% year on year.

In core telecom, wireless revenue remains below the 2025 level, and security-incident follow-up costs and subscriber churn from penalty waivers remain burdens. On the other hand, AI data centers have a concrete path, with 911 megawatts secured, 152 megawatts operating and more than 1 GW targeted by 2031.

Cumulative B2B order pipeline through Q3 2026 grew more than 20% year on year. On shareholder returns, the annual minimum dividend policy and buyback execution continue. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 earnings release, the 2026 annual report and progress on additional AIDC capacity.
