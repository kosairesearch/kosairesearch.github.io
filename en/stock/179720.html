Losses and Capital Impairment at a KONEX-Listed P2P Lender

MoneyMove, which relies mainly on platform fees from P2P lending, posted an operating loss in 2025 and turned to negative total equity.

02

Business structure

MoneyMove is a registered online investment-linked finance operator (registration No. 2021-34) that runs a platform connecting borrowers needing funds with investors.

According to the company website, it offers small, diversified investment products starting at KRW 10,000 units and states that investor assets are held separately at Nonghyup Bank. Revenue comes from platform fees and advertising, and the company began collecting loan platform fees in March 2023.

According to FnGuide, loan platform fees made up 88% of total revenue on a 2024 full-year basis. The average yield the company disclosed was 11.82% as of February 2026, and a 12-month product offering a 15.58% annual yield is also listed.

The company emphasizes its proprietary AI credit rating system as its core technology for loan screening and repayment prediction. Press reports said the company began full-scale linked investing with a savings bank. In competitive terms, the company presents itself as the only P2P lending operator listed on KONEX.

Compared with large financial holding companies and securities firms, its revenue scale is very small and concentrated in intermediary fees. According to JobKorea corporate data, the company had 23 employees at the end of 2025, and it changed its name from Yellopay to MoneyMove in 2024.

03

Recent trends

Taken together, the disclosed figures and aggregated corporate data show a recent financial trend defined by continued losses and capital impairment. According to JobKorea corporate data, 2025 revenue was KRW 566.58 million, up 185.93% from KRW 198.16 million in 2024.

Over the same period the operating loss was KRW 3.26 billion, slightly narrower than KRW 3.34 billion in 2024. The 2025 net loss was recorded at KRW 3,237,576 thousand, and the operating margin was -575.82%.

Total equity turned negative to KRW -1,712,215 thousand at the end of 2025 from KRW 852,248 thousand at the end of 2024. Total liabilities of KRW 2,401,690 thousand exceeded total assets of KRW 689,475 thousand at the same date.

A third-party capital raise worth KRW 3.0 billion was decided on February 12, 2026, with 289,214 new shares issued at KRW 10,919 per share. According to recent press reports, trading resumed on March 12, 2026 after the reason for the suspension was resolved.

The share price was KRW 12,500 as of March 11, 2026, while the current price is KRW 6,200 and trading value is zero.

04

Outlook

The outlook can be split into two axes. On one side, the fee-based revenue model is cited as a potential source of growth if linked investing with savings banks and system integration with financial institutions expand. Whether those partnerships translate into actual transaction volume is the key checkpoint.

On the other side, capital impairment and operating losses at the end of 2025 remain, and how total equity changes is the central variable. The February 2026 capital raise increased shares outstanding from 2,460,804 to 2,750,018, which implies dilution on a per-share basis.

Whether revenue growth in the 2026 fiscal year translates into a narrower operating loss is another key checkpoint. The largest shareholder group's stake rose to 33.41% as of October 27, 2026, and off-market purchases on the same date were reported.

Interest rate conditions and regulatory changes for online investment-linked finance are external variables that shape intermediary volume. The average yield the company presents is calculated by the company itself and should be read on that basis.

05

Bull factors

Registered operator and KONEX listing as trust signals

MoneyMove completed its online investment-linked finance registration on November 12, 2021 (registration No. 2021-34). The company cites its status as the only P2P lending operator listed on KONEX as a trust factor. Its description also includes a structure that holds investor assets separately at Nonghyup Bank.

Since its KONEX listing on July 1, 2013, being both a registered operator and a listed company has been central to its public positioning.

Savings bank linked investing widens intermediary scope

According to press reports, MoneyMove began full-scale linked investing with a savings bank. As linked investing expands, platform transaction volume could grow and fee-based revenue could rise with it.

After starting loan platform fee collection in March 2023, that fee accounted for 88% of revenue on a 2024 full-year basis. The clarity of its fee-centered business axis is also cited as a positive factor.

Capital raise funds AI credit rating upgrades

In the February 12, 2026 capital raise disclosure, the proceeds were stated as going to operating costs and stabilization costs for upgrading the AI credit rating system. The raise totals KRW 3.0 billion, with 289,214 new shares issued at KRW 10,919 per share.

Part of the funds is also directed to system integration for linked investing with financial institutions. The company presents its AI credit rating capability as its core competitive strength.

06

Bear factors

Persistent operating losses

According to JobKorea corporate data, operating losses were KRW 2.16 billion in 2022, KRW 2.53 billion in 2023, and KRW 3.34 billion in 2024. The 2025 operating loss narrowed slightly to KRW 3.26 billion, but the loss structure did not change.

Although 2025 revenue rose to KRW 566.58 million, the operating loss remains large relative to sales. The 2025 operating margin was recorded at -575.82%.

Capital impairment and liabilities exceeding assets

Total equity at the end of 2025 was recorded at negative KRW 1,712,215 thousand, indicating capital impairment. Because liabilities exceed assets, total equity is negative. Compared with total equity of KRW 852,248 thousand at the end of 2024, the capital structure deteriorated within a single year. The effect of the February 2026 capital raise on this structure has not yet appeared in the reported figures.

Thin trading liquidity and lower share price

Trading resumed on March 12, 2026, but trading value at the time of writing is zero. The share price was KRW 12,500 as of March 11, 2026, and the current price is KRW 6,200. A September 5, 2025 price snapshot showed a 52-week range of KRW 7,000 to KRW 15,000. Market capitalization is about KRW 17.1 billion.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Changes in interest rate levels and household loan demand can affect demand for P2P linked investing and the scale of intermediation. The 11.82% average yield and the 15.58% annual yield on a 12-month product are calculated on the company's own basis.

If financial market conditions shift, the pace of investor fund inflows and product absorption could also change.

Regulatory

Online investment-linked finance is a business operated under a registration system administered by the Financial Services Commission, and changes in registration requirements or linked-investing rules could alter its scope.

The company states that segregated custody of investor assets and information disclosure are its operating principles. As savings bank linked structures expand, the range of applicable regulation could also widen.

Financing and dilution

If reliance on external funding such as capital raises grows while capital is impaired, per-share dilution could increase. Shares outstanding rose from 2,460,804 before the February 2026 capital raise to 2,750,018 now.

The largest shareholder group's stake increased to 33.41% as of October 27, 2026, and off-market purchases on the same date were reported.

08

Overall view

MoneyMove is a small KONEX-listed company whose main revenue source is platform fees from P2P lending. Revenue rose sharply in 2025 from the prior year, but operating and net losses continued.

Total equity was negative at the end of 2025, and how the capital structure changes after the February 2026 capital raise is the next checkpoint.

On the positive side, its status as both a registered operator and a listed company, the expansion of savings bank linked investing, and a fee-centered revenue structure are cited.

On the negative side, persistent losses, capital impairment, thin trading value and liquidity, and dilution from the capital raise are cited.

The largest shareholder group's stake rose to 33.41% as of October 27, 2026, and the reported off-market purchase price of KRW 63 is far below the current market price, with the reason for the gap not explained in the reports. This summary is a qualitative overview based on disclosures, press reports, and aggregated corporate data.
