SK Square: A Holding Company Tied to SK hynix

SK Square's earnings and share price rest largely on its SK hynix stake and shareholder return policy, and reported profit has grown quickly since it returned to profit in 2024.

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

SK Square is an investment company whose core asset is its stake in SK hynix, and although it is a holding company, its asset mix differs clearly from general group holding companies. After SK hynix retired 2.1% of its treasury shares in February 2026, SK Square's stake in SK hynix rose slightly from 20.1% to 20.5%.

Daishin Securities estimated in a report dated November 17, 2026 that SK hynix accounts for 93.8% of the company's net asset value. Its other unlisted assets include TMAP Mobility, SK Shieldus, SK Planet and ONE store, and Yuanta Securities Korea assessed the weight of unlisted subsidiaries as limited.

According to Daishin Securities, TMAP Mobility turned a quarterly operating profit after its mobility data revenue increased.

One brokerage industry official described SK hynix's dividends as effectively the core source of income, and SK hynix paid SK Square about KRW 438.3 billion in dividends for the prior year in January 2026.

The company adopted a policy of using at least 30% of its recurring dividend income, plus part of its investment gains, to buy back shares for cancellation or pay cash dividends. Daishin Securities said actual shareholder return since 2023 has been about 50 to 60% of recurring dividend income.

In terms of competition, general holding companies such as SK and LG hold subsidiaries across several business areas, which dilutes the effect of any single industry cycle, whereas most of SK Square's assets are tied to semiconductors and so industry changes pass through more directly.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual figures, revenue fell from KRW 2.37 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.28 trillion in 2023 (down 4.0%), KRW 1.91 trillion in 2024 (down 16.2%) and KRW 1.41 trillion in 2025 (down 26.0%), a decline in each of the three years from 2023 to 2025.

Within 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest revenue year and 2025 the smallest. Profit moved in the opposite direction.

Operating profit went from KRW 16.1 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 2.34 trillion in 2023, turned to operating profit of KRW 3.91 trillion in 2024, and reached KRW 8.8 trillion in 2025, up 124.8%.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders followed the same path, from a loss of KRW 1.29 trillion in 2023 to a profit of KRW 3.71 trillion in 2024 and KRW 8.82 trillion in 2025, up 137.6%.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 8.82 trillion in 2025, a separate figure from profit attributable to controlling shareholders. The operating margin rose from 0.7% in 2022 to 205.2% in 2024 and 623.3% in 2025.

Articles on the 2026 second quarter said the contribution from equity-method gains on subsidiaries was large, which helps explain margins rising while revenue fell.

The debt ratio fell from 29.1% in 2022 to 9.0% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 27.99 trillion, of which KRW 27.64 trillion belonged to controlling shareholders, with total liabilities of KRW 2.52 trillion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 342.4 billion in 2022, KRW 265.7 billion in 2023, KRW 190 billion in 2024 and KRW 387.2 billion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit was KRW 2.65 trillion in Q3 2025, KRW 3.1 trillion in Q4 2025 (up 17.1% from the prior quarter), KRW 8.28 trillion in Q1 2026 (up 167.2%), KRW 19.2 trillion in Q2 2026 (up 132.4%) and KRW 7.94 trillion in Q3 2026 (down 58.7% quarter on quarter, up 200.2% year on year).

SK Securities described Q1 2026 operating profit as a quarterly record in a May 2026 report. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 18.7 trillion in Q2 2026 and fell 32.9% to KRW 12.5 trillion in Q3 2026.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 304.8 billion, down 7.2% from the prior quarter and 25.3% from a year earlier. Over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026), profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 42.9 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

SK Square's effective end market is the memory semiconductor cycle in which SK hynix operates. In a July 2026 report, Yuanta Securities said continued AI-related memory demand has raised the likelihood that SK hynix sets another annual record profit.

The same report noted that SK hynix's consensus profit attributable to controlling shareholders was raised sharply for both 2026 and 2027 compared with the start of the year.

The report also flagged that weaker AI-related memory demand, falling memory prices or intensified global chip competition could weaken the case for re-rating the stake. Some market analysts put the correlation between SK Square's and SK hynix's share prices at about 98%.

On the institutional side, one investor official described SK Square as a proxy for SK hynix, with funds seeking semiconductor exposure moving to it because Samsung Electronics and SK hynix already carry large weights.

Because single-stock inclusion limits of 10% apply to equity funds, institutions that can no longer add SK hynix after its market value rose have been turning to SK Square as an alternative.

Analysts have also said SK Square's NAV discount has narrowed more than those of general holding companies, given its concentration in semiconductor assets.

Daishin Securities lowered its target price from KRW 2.1 million to KRW 1.5 million on November 17, 2026 after SK hynix's market value declined, showing how swings in the end market flow directly into target price estimates.

06

Outlook

The company's confirmed plans centre on shareholder returns and a discount target. SK Square has set a target of bringing its NAV discount down to about 30% by 2028 as part of its corporate value enhancement plan.

A dividend of KRW 1,500 per share, totaling KRW 204.3 billion, was paid in June 2026, and the company plans to buy back KRW 70 billion of shares during 2027 according to NH Investment & Securities.

Daishin Securities assumed SK hynix's annual dividend would be about KRW 30 trillion, which implies roughly KRW 6.2 trillion of dividend income for SK Square, and estimated shareholder returns of KRW 4.2 to 4.8 trillion after March 2027, rising to about KRW 5.6 trillion in 2028.

That estimate assumes at least 60% of recurring dividend income plus all special dividends from Kioxia are returned to shareholders. Yuanta Securities pointed to the prospect of a SK hynix ADR listing and further shareholder returns as share price momentum in a July 2026 report.

SK hynix is pursuing a listing of overseas depositary receipts, and SK Securities expected SK Square to keep its stake at 20% or more to maintain control, as stated in a May 2026 report. Among unlisted subsidiaries, earnings improvement at TMAP Mobility and SK Shieldus is cited as a secondary driver.

The next checkpoints are the scale of shareholder returns after March 2027 and the trend in operating margin and equity-method gains in the 2026 annual report.

SK Square is more commonly read through its discount to net asset value (NAV) than through earnings multiples.

The company said its NAV discount has narrowed to about 38%, and Daishin Securities noted that since the company's November 2021 spin-off and listing, the discount has ranged from 36% to 77%, averaging about 64%. The current discount sits near the lower end of that historical range.

Market commentary attributes the rapid narrowing in 2026 to the re-rating of its SK hynix stake. NH Investment & Securities raised its target price to KRW 2.7 million in June 2026 and said it applied a 25% discount. Yuanta Securities kept its KRW 2.1 million target in July 2026 using a 30% discount.

Daishin Securities lowered its target from KRW 2.1 million to KRW 1.5 million on November 17, 2026 and kept the 30% discount. The gap among the three targets stems from differences in SK hynix valuation and discount assumptions.

Because the company posted an operating loss in 2023, earnings-based multiples have varied widely across periods.

08

Bull factors

Upward revisions to SK hynix earnings

When SK hynix's earnings outlook rises, SK Square's NAV rises with it. Yuanta Securities noted in a July 2026 report that consensus profit attributable to SK hynix shareholders was sharply raised for both 2026 and 2027 compared with the start of the year. The rise in SK Square's stake to 20.5% after SK hynix's share retirement points in the same direction.

Projected expansion of shareholder returns

The company said it would fund shareholder returns with at least 30% of recurring dividend income plus part of its investment gains. Daishin Securities estimated returns of KRW 4.2 to 4.8 trillion after March 2027, rising to about KRW 5.6 trillion in 2028. The company has set a goal of a NAV discount of about 30% by 2028.

Brokers raising target prices in 2026

NH Investment & Securities raised its target from KRW 1.1 million to KRW 2.7 million in June 2026 and kept its Buy rating. Yuanta Securities maintained its KRW 2.1 million target in July 2026. Daishin Securities lowered its target to KRW 1.5 million on November 17, 2026 while keeping its Buy rating.

09

Bear factors

Concentration on SK hynix's cycle

Because 93.8% of NAV rests on SK hynix, a downturn in the semiconductor cycle would sharply affect the holding company's value. Daishin Securities lowered its target price to reflect a decline in SK hynix's market value. A brokerage industry official also named volatility tied to the chip cycle as a risk factor.

Earnings volatility and past losses

In 2023 the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 2.34 trillion and a loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.29 trillion, so profit has not moved in one direction.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 18.7 trillion in Q2 2026 was attributed largely to equity-method gains from subsidiaries. It fell 32.9% to KRW 12.5 trillion in Q3 2026.

Conditions attached to further discount narrowing

The company said its NAV discount has narrowed to about 38%, but the discount has ranged from 36% to 77% since listing. Yuanta Securities noted that a lower discount rests on shareholder returns expanding and SK hynix's value rising at the same time.

Shareholder return scale also depends on recurring dividend income, and therefore on SK hynix's dividend payouts.

10

Risk factors

Unlisted subsidiary earnings

TMAP Mobility turned to a quarterly operating profit after its mobility data revenue increased, and the earnings improvement at SK Shieldus is also cited as a secondary driver.

If profit gains at these unlisted subsidiaries do not continue from quarter to quarter, the contribution that assets other than SK hynix make to the holding company's earnings could fall short of expectations. TMAP Mobility's turn to profit came relatively recently, so its continuity needs to be confirmed.

Flow-driven risk

Institutional inclusion of SK Square relies on single-stock limit rules, so a change in those rules would alter the demand base. One brokerage has said its 52-week beta is larger than SK hynix's. If demand for it as an alternative vehicle weakens, the same share price elasticity could work in reverse.

Dependence on special dividends

The company's shareholder return funding estimate assumes that all special dividends from Kioxia are returned to shareholders. Special dividends do not arise on a regular basis like recurring dividends, so if their timing or size differs, the funding for returns could shrink as well.

Together with the assumption of returning at least 60% of recurring dividend income, a shortfall in either source would lower the estimated return scale.

Overall view

On the disclosed figures, SK Square's revenue fell in each of the three years from 2023 to 2025, while operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a 2023 loss to a return to profit in 2024 and grew sharply through 2025.

Quarterly operating profit peaked at KRW 8.28 trillion in Q1 2026 and KRW 19.2 trillion in Q2 2026 before falling to KRW 7.94 trillion in Q3 2026.

These figures are tied to equity-method gains from SK hynix, and the fact that most of the stake's value sits in SK hynix is the backdrop for earnings and share prices moving together.

Bullish factors cited include upward revisions to SK hynix earnings, expanding shareholder returns and the goal of narrowing the NAV discount.

Bearish factors include SK hynix's industry cycle and market value swings, large volatility in quarterly profit, and the dependence of shareholder return scale on dividend income assumptions.

Target prices given by brokers were KRW 2.7 million from NH Investment & Securities in June 2026, KRW 2.1 million from Yuanta Securities in July 2026 and KRW 1.5 million from Daishin Securities on November 17, 2026, with differences stemming from SK hynix value and discount assumptions.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results in early 2027, the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027, and the scale of shareholder returns after March 2027.
