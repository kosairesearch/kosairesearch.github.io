02

Business structure

Jeongmoon Information manufactures household goods on an OEM and ODM basis and automotive interior parts.

Butler, a data aggregator, put the sales mix for the second quarter of 2026 at 44% automotive parts manufacturing, 31% household goods manufacturing, 19% recording media manufacturing and 6% LED parts manufacturing, so automotive interiors are the largest share.

The company says it streamlined its legacy media business and restructured around household goods and automotive interior parts. The MS AR business ended in April 2025, and in September 2025 a new OEM customer, Henkel, was added to the household goods division.

In the domestic and export breakdown, automotive interior parts were recorded as 100% exports as of December 2025, so overseas vehicle supply is central to this segment.

The household goods segment relies mainly on domestic OEM and ODM volumes, and the company says investment made since 2025 and stabilization of that business are producing restructuring effects. In automotive interiors, entry by new vehicle makers is cited as a factor widening the revenue base.

The headcount at the end of June 2026 was 81, and R&D spending in 2025 was KRW 293 million, or 0.33% of sales. Customer concentration and pricing terms are not detailed in the disclosures and are items to review in the notes to the business report.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 64.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 74.6 billion in 2023 (up 14.8% year on year), KRW 80.4 billion in 2024 (up 7.9%) and KRW 89.8 billion in 2025 (up 11.6%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit moved from KRW 4.73 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.45 billion in 2023 (down 6.0%), KRW 4.05 billion in 2024 (down 9.0%) and KRW 4.07 billion in 2025 (up 0.6%), so profit fell while sales grew. The operating margin declined from 7.3% in 2022 to 6.0% in 2023, 5.0% in 2024 and 4.5% in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.48 billion in 2022, KRW 2.45 billion in 2023 (down 1.3%), KRW 3.77 billion in 2024 (up 53.9%) and KRW 3.43 billion in 2025 (down 9.0%).

The 2024 rise came in a year when operating profit fell, so one-off items outside operations should be checked in the notes to the business report.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 21.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 26.8 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 31.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 30.6 billion in the second quarter and KRW 37.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 1.03 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then rose to KRW 1.78 billion and KRW 3.01 billion. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 6.19 billion.

The third-quarter 2026 figures match the preliminary release of November 9, 2026, and as preliminary closing figures before external review they could still change. Operating cash flow was KRW 6.72 billion in 2022, KRW 8.08 billion in 2023, KRW 5.98 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.38 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Household goods OEM and ODM depend on volumes that customers place for contract manufacturing, so securing volume and cost control determine profitability. The company added a new OEM customer to its household goods division in 2025 and says its domestic household goods business is stabilizing.

For automotive interiors in North America, an earnings comment carried by WiseReport says share in Foam Pad and Door Trim could expand to as much as 70%. The same comment says entry by new vehicle makers and quality upgrades are strengthening links with global OEMs.

Demand for automotive interior parts tends to track U.S. vehicle production and trade policy, so growth in this segment depends on the production plans of vehicle customers. The company is classified under chemicals, but its structure is closer to contract manufacturing of OEM and parts than to materials production.

Segment-level margin data for the U.S. business is limited, so it should be read alongside the consolidated trend. With 81 employees, the company is small, so volume changes at one or two customers can show up clearly in results.

Pricing power and customer concentration are not detailed in the disclosures, so the business report's customer information should be read alongside.

Outlook

The company plans to expand supply volumes in the second half and keep management focused on profitability.

At an extraordinary shareholder meeting on September 2, 2026, the company offset its accumulated deficit by reducing statutory reserves and capital surplus, which the company says lays the groundwork for dividends if earnings keep improving.

Trading resumed on November 10, 2026, following the 5-to-1 share consolidation. Related parties, including the CEO, have continued buying shares on the open market this year, though the amounts and share counts were not given in the press release.

The third is whether year-end disclosures show receivable aging and changes in the allowance for doubtful accounts.

Bull factors

Growth in U.S. automotive interior parts

Cumulative January to September 2026 sales in the U.S. automotive interior parts segment reached KRW 44.9 billion, up 214.7%, while segment operating profit rose 131.3% to KRW 2.2 billion. The company says the restructuring effect is showing up in results. A WiseReport comment also points to expanding share in Foam Pad and Door Trim in North America.

Consecutive quarterly rebound in operating profit

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 1.03 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then rose to KRW 1.78 billion in the second quarter and KRW 3.01 billion in the third. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 37.5 billion was up 71.8% year on year. The same third-quarter figures appeared in the preliminary release of November 9, 2026.

Wider base for shareholder returns

The September 2, 2026 extraordinary shareholder meeting approved the deficit write-off, which lays the groundwork for future dividends. In May 2026 the company cancelled 740,014 treasury shares with a book value of KRW 2 billion. Related parties, including the CEO, have continued buying shares on the open market.

Bear factors

Long-term decline in operating margin

The operating margin fell from 7.3% in 2022 to 6.0% in 2023, 5.0% in 2024 and 4.5% in 2025. Margins declined even as sales grew, so growth has not carried through to profitability. Whether the third-quarter 2026 profit increase reversed this trend must be confirmed on an annual basis in the 2026 annual report.

Quarterly decline in profit attributable to controlling shareholders

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.01 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 48.8% from the prior quarter and down 32.4% from a year earlier. The reason profit fell while operating profit rose needs to be traced through the detailed line items.

Over the last four quarters the total was KRW 6.19 billion, so whether the operating rebound fully reached net profit is not yet settled.

Burden of receivables

According to a Daily Union report, receivables reached KRW 22.87 billion, equal to 25.5% of sales, with receivables growth of 35.5% well ahead of 11.6% sales growth. If collections slow, cash generation may lag reported profit. The adequacy of the allowance for doubtful accounts becomes a key item in the next disclosure.

Risk factors

Liquidity and cash flow

Year-end cash and cash equivalents fell 37% from the prior period to KRW 6.55 billion. The net cash outflow from investing activities widened to KRW 8.85 billion from KRW 4.18 billion.

Operating cash flow rose to KRW 7.38 billion, but cash balances still declined, so external funding through short-term borrowing or equity issuance has been raised as a possibility.

Audit flags and governance

The auditor issued an unqualified opinion but flagged the adequacy of the allowance for doubtful accounts and the adequacy of disclosures on related-party transactions and balances as key audit matters. Ownership reports from executives and major shareholders have been filed repeatedly. The level of related-party disclosure is an item to confirm in the next filing.

Business concentration and float

Because the U.S. automotive interior parts segment, with cumulative sales of KRW 44.9 billion through September 2026, drove much of the gain, changes in U.S. vehicle production or trade policy could affect results directly.

The largest shareholder group holds 47.40% and the free float ratio is 52.58% as of October 7, 2026, so changes in effective float after the consolidation should be tracked.

Overall view

From 2022 to 2025 Jeongmoon Information grew sales while its operating margin fell from 7.3% to 4.5%. Sales rose for three straight years through 2025, reaching KRW 89.8 billion.

In 2026 the U.S. automotive interior parts segment lifted results, with third-quarter operating profit rising to KRW 3.01 billion and operating profit increasing for two consecutive quarters. On shareholder returns, the deficit write-off and treasury share cancellation have built a base for dividends.

Against that, third-quarter profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.01 billion fell from a year earlier, and receivables of KRW 22.87 billion, equal to 25.5% of sales, along with lower year-end cash remain burdens. Strengths and weaknesses coexist, so the direction is hard to call.

The next decision points are whether the 2026 annual report meets the sales target, the trend in operating margin, and the collection of receivables and changes in the allowance. This report does not offer an investment opinion or target price.
