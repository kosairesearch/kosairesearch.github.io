02

Business structure

Suntech is a manufacturer of electric motors, generators, and power conversion, supply, and control equipment, and it was listed on KONEX on April 29, 2015.

The business report explains that the company began developing generators after incorporating in 1996, aiming to build domestic technology in a market that had relied heavily on imports. Permanent-magnet generator technology is presented as a central pillar of the business.

Its subsidiary Withps makes emergency generators for the defense industry. Suntech is Withps's largest shareholder with a 92.22% stake. Suntech's largest shareholder, Lee Seon-hyu, serves as Withps's chief executive. Consolidated revenue for 2025 was KRW 21,533 million and standalone revenue was KRW 16,401 million.

Withps's standalone revenue for 2025 was KRW 5,131 million. Revenue splits by product or segment did not appear in the materials reviewed, so they are described only qualitatively.

In the competitive landscape, Suntech is small. It had 35 employees as of March 25, 2026. Press reports indicate that one listed competitor holds more than 70% of the domestic emergency generator market.

Within the broader machinery and equipment classification, Suntech differs sharply in scale and business nature from large manufacturers such as Doosan Enerbility or Doosan Bobcat.

03

Recent trends

Standalone revenue for 2025 was KRW 16,401 million, up 5.83% from KRW 15,497 million in the prior year. Gross profit rose 33.85% to KRW 2,764 million, while selling and administrative expenses fell 4.9% to KRW 2,735 million.

Operating profit was therefore KRW 29 million, a turnaround from an operating loss of KRW 811 million a year earlier. However, the net loss widened to KRW 1,757 million from KRW 1,288 million.

The gap between operating profit and the net loss appears to reflect losses in non-operating items, whose details could not be confirmed.

Consolidated revenue declined from KRW 30,442 million in 2023 to KRW 25,779 million in 2024 and KRW 21,533 million in 2025. Consolidated operating results worsened from an operating profit of KRW 393 million in 2023 to an operating loss of KRW 519 million in 2024 and KRW 1,894 million in 2025.

Withps posted a standalone net loss of KRW 1,936 million for 2025. The subsidiary's weakness appears to have contributed to the consolidated deficit.

On March 26, 2026, the day of the shareholders' meeting, the share price rose 15.00% to KRW 8,280. A price of KRW 11,000 was shown for September 16, 2026. The price at the time of writing is KRW 7,650, with a market capitalization of KRW 8.0 billion.

The 52-week range was KRW 600 to KRW 21,950, indicating very large price swings. Quarterly results for 2026 were not found in the materials reviewed.

04

Outlook

Near-term events are concentrated on the financing schedule. November 4, 2026 is both the payment date of the Withps rights offering and Suntech's scheduled acquisition date. Suntech will acquire 41,200 shares in cash, lifting its stake to 93.09% after the acquisition.

Payment for Suntech's own third-party allotment offering is due November 3, 2026, with proceeds earmarked as KRW 799 million for acquiring another company's securities, KRW 500 million for other purposes, and KRW 201 million for operating funds.

For the KRW 2.0 billion redeemable convertible preferred shares disclosed on December 19, 2025, the conversion request period runs from December 30, 2026 to December 29, 2035. If conversion is requested, the number of issued shares could rise and affect the shareholder mix. The detailed conversion terms were not confirmed.

On the industry side, data centers and AI infrastructure are cited as one pillar of emergency generator demand. MarketsandMarkets forecasts the global data center generator market to grow from USD 8.57 billion in 2026 to USD 9.79 billion by 2031, a 2.7% compound annual rate.

Another report, from GII, expects growth from USD 7.92 billion in 2026 to USD 9.89 billion in 2031 at 4.56% annually. The same report notes that diesel sets accounted for 81.25% of 2025 demand but that their headroom is capped by carbon liabilities. These are global market forecasts, and how much Suntech benefits must be verified separately.

Among business indicators, the room for improvement lies in the operating margin. The standalone operating margin was only 0.18% in 2025, and eliminating the consolidated operating loss is the key issue. A recovery in Withps's profitability is the main swing factor for consolidated results.

05

Bull factors

Standalone operating result turned positive

Standalone operating results improved from an operating loss of KRW 811 million in 2024 to an operating profit of KRW 29 million in 2025. Gross profit grew 33.85% while selling and administrative expenses fell 4.9%. This can be read as an improvement in cost structure and expense control. The profit is very small, however, so its durability remains to be seen.

Generator demand backdrop

In Korea, analysts have argued that the emergency and commercial generator industry has structural momentum as data center and AI infrastructure power demand expands. MarketsandMarkets forecasts the global data center generator market to grow 2.7% annually through 2031.

This backdrop could form a demand base for smaller manufacturers in the same market. Suntech's actual order status, however, is not visible in its filings.

Continued capital support for the subsidiary

In December 2025, Suntech acquired 203,750 Withps shares for KRW 2.5 billion, lifting its stake to 92.21%. It is set to join the October 2026 offering as well, taking its stake to 93.09%. The stated purpose is to pursue growth by improving the subsidiary's financial structure.

The move is a way to ease the subsidiary's financial burden, but the outcome has to be confirmed in future earnings.

06

Bear factors

Widening consolidated losses

The consolidated operating loss grew from KRW 519 million in 2024 to KRW 1,894 million in 2025. The standalone net loss also increased from KRW 1,288 million to KRW 1,757 million. Per FnGuide, the standalone ROE for 2025 was -63.76%. Operating profit sits at roughly breakeven and cannot absorb the net loss.

High debt and subsidiary balance sheet strain

Standalone total assets for 2025 were KRW 14,131 million, total liabilities KRW 10,897 million, and total equity KRW 3,234 million. Withps reported total assets of KRW 11,756 million, total liabilities of KRW 8,550 million, and total equity of KRW 3,206 million. Both companies carry heavy debt relative to equity. The need for repeated capital raising reflects the funding burden.

Small scale and weak share position

With only 35 employees, the organization has limited capacity to handle large orders. Press reports say one listed competitor holds more than 70% of the domestic emergency generator market. Standalone revenue fell from KRW 25,425 million in 2023 to KRW 15,497 million in 2024. Revenue can swing widely with order size, which is a burden.

Risk factors

Industry and regulation

GII projects that the diesel generator market could plateau after 2028 once stricter quotas lock in. The California Air Resources Board has proposed cutting the NOx emission limit for large generators by half. Tighter emission rules could raise development and certification costs for diesel-centered products. Suntech's product mix is unconfirmed, so the degree of impact is uncertain.

Financial and dilution

The standalone debt ratio was 337.02% in 2025. The conversion request period for the KRW 2.0 billion redeemable convertible preferred shares begins on December 30, 2026. Conversion would increase the share count and could change existing shareholders' ownership percentages. The detailed redemption terms were not confirmed, so the extent of the burden cannot be stated.

Market and liquidity

KONEX-listed stocks trade thinly, so gaps in the order book can arise easily. Trading value at the time of writing was zero. The 52-week price range runs from KRW 600 to KRW 21,950. Even small trades can move the price sharply, which limits the reliability of executions and quoted prices.

08

Overall view

Suntech is a small manufacturer that promotes permanent-magnet generator technology and has broadened its scope through a subsidiary making emergency generators for defense. Standalone operating profit returned to KRW 29 million in 2025, but the consolidated operating loss grew to KRW 1,894 million.

The standalone debt ratio of 337.02% signals a heavy financial burden. The capital-raising filings of December 2025 and October 2026 show continued funding aimed at improving the subsidiary's finances.

On the bullish side, the standalone earnings improvement and the generator demand backdrop, including data centers, are cited. On the bearish side, consolidated losses, high debt, potential dilution from convertible securities, and low liquidity are flagged.

Quarterly results for 2026 and any recovery in Withps's profitability are the next checkpoints. This report is for information only, and any view on the share price is left to the reader.
