02

Business structure

Suntech was founded on May 29, 1996 and is engaged mainly in manufacturing and selling generators and rotary converters. It changed its corporate name to Suntech Co., Ltd. in 2008 and listed its shares on the KONEX market in April 2015.

Its core product is the diesel-engine emergency generator, and permanent magnet generators are also included in its production lineup. The sales structure leans heavily on public procurement, with the public-sector market reported to account for more than half of revenue.

Major customers include KEPCO, power plants, government offices, and first-tier construction companies. The company holds two items certified as Excellent Public Procurement Products, which gives it eligibility in government tenders.

According to the 2025 business report, the company has one consolidated subsidiary, WithPS, which manufactures generators for the defense industry. In December 2025, Suntech took part in a rights issue at WithPS and disclosed that its stake reached 92.21%.

Consolidated 2025 revenue was KRW 21,533 million, higher than standalone revenue of KRW 16,401 million, reflecting the partial inclusion of subsidiary sales.

Revenue is concentrated in generator manufacturing, and compared with larger listed machinery companies in the same sector, its product range and sales network are narrow. Results tend to swing quarter to quarter depending on the timing of public orders and the outcome of major tenders.

03

Recent trends

Standalone revenue for fiscal 2025 was KRW 16,401 million, up from KRW 15,497 million in 2024. Standalone operating profit was KRW 29 million, turning positive from an operating loss of KRW 811 million in 2024. The standalone net loss widened to KRW 1,757 million from KRW 1,288 million in 2024.

Equity-method losses rose sharply, to KRW 1,530 million in 2025 from KRW 147 million in 2024, and this is the main factor behind the larger net loss. Consolidated revenue fell to KRW 21,533 million from KRW 25,779 million in 2024. The consolidated operating loss grew to KRW 1,894 million from KRW 519 million in 2024.

The consolidated net loss widened to KRW 2,164 million from KRW 938 million, and non-controlling interests recorded a loss of KRW 407 million. At the annual general meeting on March 26, 2026, the 30th fiscal year financial statements were approved, with 59.3% of voting shares in favor.

The share price was KRW 8,280 on March 26, 2026, up 15.00% from the previous day, and KRW 7,650 on October 26, 2026. The current price is KRW 7,480, and trading value is recorded at zero, indicating thin trading. A low of KRW 600 was recorded on October 21, 2025, showing a wide range of price swings.

04

Outlook

Future results are likely to depend on whether public-sector orders continue and whether subsidiary earnings improve. Standalone operating profit turned positive, but the operating margin was only 0.18%.

Selling and administrative expenses of KRW 2,735 million nearly equal gross profit of KRW 2,764 million, leaving considerable room for improvement in cost structure.

Because equity-method losses drove the standalone net loss, the recovery of affiliate and subsidiary results, including WithPS, is the key variable for earnings. WithPS is pursuing a balance sheet repair based on demand for defense-grade generators.

As of the November 2026 disclosures, the CEO's stake fell from 49.29% to 41.02%, and the holdings of the largest shareholder group including related parties were reported at 53.66%.

Participation in a third-party allotment rights issue is cited as the reason for the change, so share dilution is progressing alongside the increase in share count. The next checkpoint is whether standalone and consolidated earnings move in a clear direction in the third-quarter 2026 report.

Demand for emergency power equipment and the public procurement cycle remain external factors that will shape revenue scale.

Bull factors

Standalone operating profit turns positive

Suntech's standalone operating profit was KRW 29 million in 2025, shifting from an operating loss of KRW 811 million in 2024. Standalone gross profit rose to KRW 2,764 million from KRW 2,065 million, showing some easing of cost pressure. The move out of operating loss can be cited as evidence of a normalizing business.

Public-sector base and certified products

The public-sector market accounts for more than half of sales, with KEPCO, power plants, government offices, and first-tier builders cited as key customers. Two items hold Excellent Public Procurement Product status, which secures eligibility in public tenders. This base gives a small manufacturer a foundation for keeping its customer relationships stable.

WithPS consolidation and defense demand

In December 2025, Suntech took part in a rights issue at WithPS and raised its stake to 92.21%. WithPS mainly manufactures generators for the defense industry, and the stated purpose of the capital increase is to improve its financial structure. Adding defense generator demand to the consolidated business is read as a positive factor.

Bear factors

Consolidated losses widen

Consolidated revenue declined to KRW 21,533 million from KRW 25,779 million in 2024. The consolidated operating loss grew to KRW 1,894 million from KRW 519 million, and the consolidated net loss reached KRW 2,164 million.

Falling revenue and wider losses appear together, which limits the evidence for an improving earnings structure.

Equity-method losses and thin operating leverage

Standalone equity-method losses jumped to KRW 1,530 million in 2025 from KRW 147 million in 2024. Standalone selling and administrative expenses of KRW 2,735 million nearly absorbed gross profit of KRW 2,764 million, leaving an operating margin of only 0.18%. The structure makes it hard for modest revenue gains to translate into profit.

Share dilution and KONEX liquidity

As of the November 2026 disclosures, the CEO's stake declined from 49.29% to 41.02%. Participation in a third-party allotment rights issue is cited as the reason for the change in the largest shareholder group's holdings. Trading value is recorded at zero, so liquidity in the shares is low.

Risk factors

Macro

Changes in interest rates, raw material costs, and the capital expenditure cycle could affect both generator demand and cost at the same time. Because Suntech's gross profit and selling and administrative expenses are roughly equal, a small revenue decline can quickly move operating results. Margin pressure could intensify in periods when cost increases are hard to pass through to selling prices.

Public procurement

With more than half of sales from the public sector, results depend on the timing of public orders and budget execution. Order volume could change depending on whether Excellent Public Procurement Product status is maintained and on tender outcomes. A delay in large power-generation orders would increase quarter-to-quarter revenue swings.

Liquidity and governance

Trading value of zero and KONEX listing characteristics point to low liquidity. Holdings of the largest shareholder group fell to 53.66% after a third-party allotment rights issue, and the CEO's stake is 41.02%.

In December 2025, a rights issue was decided to raise KRW 2,000 million for acquiring securities of another company, showing that capital raising is continuing.

08

Overall view

Suntech is a small manufacturer centered on diesel generators, with strengths in its public-sector base and certified product status. In fiscal 2025, standalone operating profit turned positive, but the consolidated operating loss and net loss widened, leaving profitability as an open challenge.

Higher equity-method losses and lower consolidated revenue are the main factors behind the deterioration in earnings. The earnings direction of the consolidated group continues to depend on the results of subsidiary WithPS.

On the ownership side, the CEO's stake has declined and participation in rights issues continues, so both the share count and governance structure are changing. Trading value is zero and the KONEX listing limits liquidity, with price swings also remaining wide. Public-sector order flow and subsidiary earnings are the key variables for the next phase of results.
