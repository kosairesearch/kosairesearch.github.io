02

Business structure

Ecocab makes automotive electronic components, including wiring and electrical parts, and supplies them to vehicle makers and parts suppliers. Its core products are automotive electric wire, wiring harnesses (bundled in-vehicle wiring assemblies) and bulb sockets.

Since the first half of 2017 it has also produced LED modules, including daytime running lights (DRL) and rear combination lights (RCL). In 2010 it reportedly became the first in Korea to commercialize flexible automotive wire using eco-friendly halogen-free insulation.

Recent press coverage describes its supply of lighting and power-control modules to automakers and parts makers. On production, Ecocab has split its Vietnam manufacturing into two sites and set up a sales subsidiary in Poland to broaden its overseas supply chain.

FnGuide, which publishes company summaries, cites the expansion of North American LED modules as a driver of the earnings recovery. The same summary mentions wider use of HBM-related applications, but it does not give a revenue scale for that business.

Although the company is classified under chemicals, its business is automotive parts, and FnGuide benchmarks it against the KOSDAQ transport equipment and parts sector. Revenue depends on vehicle production volumes and on whether parts are adopted in customer programs, so customers' production plans are a key variable.

04

Earnings analysis

On the basis of filed results, annual revenue rose from KRW 121.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 135.9 billion in 2023 (up 11.7% year on year), KRW 138.5 billion in 2024 (up 1.9%) and KRW 151.8 billion in 2025 (up 9.6%).

Revenue has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 1.92 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 3.95 billion in 2023, a swing to profit, and then rose to KRW 19.6 billion in 2024 (up 396.0%).

The operating margin went from -1.6% in 2022 to 2.9% in 2023 and 14.1% in 2024, then fell to -2.1% in 2025, when an operating loss of KRW 3.16 billion turned the year into a loss.

Controlling-shareholder net profit was KRW 20.3 billion in 2024 (up 876.7%), and in 2025 it became a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 2.46 billion. Net income including non-controlling interests was a loss of the same KRW 2.46 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow improved from -KRW 6.47 billion in 2022 and -KRW 4.9 billion in 2023 to KRW 2.37 billion in 2024 and KRW 30.6 billion in 2025, so cash generation stayed positive in a loss year.

Quarterly, the third quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 34.5 billion, operating profit of KRW 8.28 billion and controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 8.06 billion, before the fourth quarter swung to an operating loss of KRW 4.15 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 5.78 billion.

The first quarter of 2026 had revenue of KRW 34.3 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.56 billion, yet controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 1.63 billion.

The second quarter turned to operating profit of KRW 5.72 billion on revenue of KRW 37 billion (up 7.9% quarter on quarter), with controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 5.96 billion.

In the third quarter revenue was KRW 36.7 billion (down 0.9% quarter on quarter, up 6.2% year on year), operating profit was KRW 1.88 billion (down 77.3% year on year), and controlling-shareholder net profit rose to KRW 10.8 billion (up 33.8% year on year), well above operating profit.

The non-operating items behind that gap can be traced in the third-quarter 2026 quarterly report. Controlling-shareholder net profit for the latest four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, totals KRW 12.6 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Ecocab's market is tied to the growing electrification of vehicles, which increases the wiring and lighting and power-control module content per car.

Wiring harnesses and electric wire tend to grow with the number of electrical systems in a vehicle, so demand follows vehicle production volumes and the expansion of electronic specifications.

Competition in automotive wire and harness involves large electronics suppliers and mid-sized parts makers, and cost competitiveness and a record of adoption in automaker platforms determine awards.

Ecocab's combination of wire and LED modules is a distinguishing feature, and FnGuide's summary points to North American LED module growth as a recent earnings driver.

Expanding overseas production and sales bases can help it stay close to customers in an automotive supply chain where local production requirements matter more.

Because the company is classified under chemicals, direct comparisons with chemical peers are of limited use, and automotive parts makers are the closer competitive reference. The sharp short-term price moves in November also show that trading dynamics diverged from reported results over that period.

The capital measure the company has disclosed is a treasury share acquisition trust contract. Press reports put the contract at about KRW 1.5 billion, roughly 7% of market capitalization as of the November 6 close.

The same reports said the share price move that day coincided with the trust contract disclosure rather than an earnings release.

The reports also said pledged shares held by largest shareholder and representative director Kim Chang-kyu rose to 2.81 million, while the stake of major shareholder Choi Young-cheon fell from 9.99% to 3.24%. Those two items need to be read alongside governance and share supply.

On the business side, the split Vietnam manufacturing and the Polish sales subsidiary are the main pillars of supply chain diversification, and FnGuide's summary cites North American LED module growth as a driver.

After the 2025 swing to losses, the company posted profitable quarters in the second and third quarters of 2026, so the next filed reports will show whether operating profit can keep quarterly profits going.

The exchange's review window for an investment warning designation runs through November 25, 2026, and trading conditions could change within it, so exchange notices should be checked. The 2026 annual report will show full-year operating profit together with fourth-quarter results.

Bull factors

Quarterly profit has turned up

After an operating loss of KRW 1.56 billion in the first quarter of 2026, operating profit turned to KRW 5.72 billion in the second quarter, and controlling-shareholder net profit rose to KRW 10.8 billion in the third.

That is two consecutive quarterly increases in controlling-shareholder net profit, following the fourth-quarter 2025 loss. Operating profit in the third quarter fell year on year, so whether the recovery is fully established will depend on the next reported results.

Overseas supply chain and LED module growth

Ecocab has split its Vietnam manufacturing across two sites and set up a sales subsidiary in Poland to broaden its overseas supply chain. FnGuide's summary cites North American LED module expansion as a driver of recent earnings.

Widening the customer geography in auto parts can add award opportunities where local production requirements are tightening.

Earnings capacity and shareholder return signal

In 2024 the company recorded an operating margin of 14.1% and operating profit of KRW 19.6 billion, showing a year of high profitability. Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 30.6 billion, so cash generation held up in a loss year.

Press reports of a treasury share trust contract of about KRW 1.5 billion are a factual sign of a shareholder return measure.

Bear factors

The 2025 swing to losses and earnings volatility

The operating margin swung to -2.1% in 2025, with an operating loss of KRW 3.16 billion, against operating profit of KRW 19.6 billion in 2024. Net results also moved from a KRW 20.3 billion controlling-shareholder profit in 2024 to a KRW 2.46 billion controlling-shareholder loss in 2025. Quarterly results have alternated between losses and profits, which points to low earnings stability.

Flat revenue and a sharp drop in quarterly operating profit

First-quarter 2026 revenue fell 12.3% quarter on quarter, and third-quarter revenue fell 0.9% quarter on quarter. Third-quarter operating profit of KRW 1.88 billion fell 77.3% year on year. Revenue growth slowed to 1.9% in 2024, so top-line growth has been uneven.

Because operating profit fell while controlling-shareholder net profit rose, the quality of earnings also needs close review.

Supply-demand strain and caution designations

The stock was designated for caution on November 4 because of concentrated trading in a small number of accounts, and again on November 10 and November 12 after sharp price gains. Exchange data also showed a large net sale from a single account on November 5. Press reports of pledged shares rising to 2.81 million add a governance element to the trading strain.
