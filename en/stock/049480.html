02

Business structure

Openbase is an IT services company focused on IT infrastructure construction and security and cloud services for corporations and public institutions.

As of the second quarter of 2026, the computer and communications equipment sales and software development business accounted for 97% of revenue, with other businesses at 2% and real estate leasing at 1%.

The company is introduced as a server and storage solutions provider, so equipment supply and software development make up most of its revenue.

Its proprietary platform is the TARGOS threat intelligence platform, and the company said it upgraded its security monitoring service by integrating a machine-learning-based web attack detection module.

A final evaluation of the 2024 AI voucher support program rated the company as excellent, a result announced on January 3, 2025.

On March 3, 2026, the company announced it had obtained VMware's ANS PSO certification for specialist services in application networking and security, describing itself as a verified partner able to deliver services based on VCF 9. The largest shareholder, Jeong Jin-seop and related parties, holds a 27.6% stake.

The company has domestic and overseas affiliates including Nanobase and Data Solution. Its competitive field sits between large IT services firms such as LG CNS, Samsung SDS and Hyundai Autoever and security specialists such as Ahnlab, and its revenue scale is small relative to the larger players.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 193.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 204 billion in 2023 (up 5.7% year on year), KRW 226.3 billion in 2024 (up 10.9%), and KRW 233.1 billion in 2025 (up 3.0%). Sales grew for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 7.98 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.61 billion in 2023 (down 17.2% year on year), then rose to KRW 6.66 billion in 2024 (up 0.9%) and KRW 7.92 billion in 2025 (up 18.9%), growing for two consecutive years. The largest year for operating profit was 2022, and the smallest was 2023.

The operating margin was 4.1% in 2022, 3.2% in 2023, 2.9% in 2024 and 3.4% in 2025, recovering from the 2024 low but still below the 2022 level.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6 billion in 2022, KRW 5.21 billion in 2023 (down 13.3%), KRW 7.58 billion in 2024 (up 45.7%) and KRW 7.26 billion in 2025 (down 4.3%).

Profit for the year including non-controlling interests was KRW 7.67 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.62 billion in 2025, and the gap between the two measures reflects non-controlling interests.

Quarterly results were concentrated in the fourth quarter of 2025, when sales reached KRW 94.2 billion and operating profit reached KRW 6.12 billion, up 268.2% from the prior quarter. In the first quarter of 2026, sales fell 38.2% quarter on quarter to KRW 58.2 billion and operating profit dropped to KRW 309 million.

Sales then rose to KRW 60.7 billion in the second quarter and KRW 97.8 billion in the third quarter, and operating profit rose to KRW 3.16 billion and KRW 3.72 billion over the same two quarters.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 3.95 billion, down 6.3% quarter on quarter but up 164.5% year on year. The total for the last four quarters was KRW 13.9 billion.

Operating cash flow moved from KRW 13.6 billion in 2024 to -KRW 1.2 billion in 2025, so profit recovery and cash generation did not move in the same direction.

05

Industry analysis

The market the company serves is one where corporate cloud migration and cybersecurity demand are growing together. Digital transformation, cloud market growth and the spread of AI-based security technology were cited as drivers of the earnings improvement in the first half of 2025.

A September 2025 news report said the industry expected the government to expand budget and staff for cloud-based disaster recovery (Cloud DR) after the National Information Resources Service fire, and it said Openbase could benefit given its experience in related infrastructure.

That is an industry expectation and not a confirmed contract size. The stock is also grouped with information security theme shares when that theme strengthens, which is another factor in its price movement. The competitive landscape has two sides.

On one side are large IT services companies such as LG CNS, Samsung SDS and Hyundai Autoever, which have large-scale infrastructure capabilities. On the other side are security specialists such as Ahnlab. Openbase sits between the two, handling both infrastructure construction and security solutions.

Because public and large corporate IT budgets shape order flow, government cloud policy and the pace of corporate AI infrastructure investment are the main industry variables.

Bear factors

Low operating margin

The operating margin fell from 4.1% in 2022 to 3.2% in 2023 and 2.9% in 2024, then recovered partially to 3.4% in 2025. Sales rose from KRW 193.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 233.1 billion in 2025, but the margin did not return to the 2022 level. Limited pass-through from sales growth to profit growth is a bearish factor.

Large swings in quarterly results

Sales in the fourth quarter of 2025 rose 85.6% quarter on quarter to KRW 94.2 billion, then fell 38.2% to KRW 58.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026. Fourth-quarter 2025 operating profit of KRW 6.12 billion accounted for most of the full-year operating profit of KRW 7.92 billion. A pattern in which results cluster in particular quarters reduces the predictability of quarterly profit.

Cash generation and ownership concentration

Operating cash flow turned from KRW 13.6 billion in 2024 to -KRW 1.2 billion in 2025. Operating profit also fell 17.2% in 2023. The largest shareholder and related parties hold a 27.6% stake, which limits the free float.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly sales move sharply with project timing. In the first quarter of 2026, sales and operating profit both fell substantially from the prior quarter. If such swings recur, the stability of annual profit weakens, and whether quarterly flows smooth out in future periodic reports is the point to check.

Cash flow and financial position

Operating cash flow turned negative in 2025, which diverges from the profit recovery. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 70.0% in 2022 to 88.6% in 2024 before falling to 81.9% in 2025. If cash generation fails to keep up with profit, working capital pressure could increase.

Share price flows and theme dependence

Reports show the share price tends to move with related stocks when the information security theme strengthens. With the largest shareholder holding 27.6%, the free float is limited. Share price swings driven by theme or market flows could grow regardless of the company's earnings trend.
