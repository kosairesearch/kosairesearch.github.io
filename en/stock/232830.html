02

Business structure

Itcenpns is a security affiliate of a Korean IT services group. Its business runs on two axes: the company's own security business, and GPU server and memory-related sales at a subsidiary.

According to the company, standalone revenue in Q3 2026 was KRW 8.45 billion, only a fraction of consolidated revenue of KRW 120.9 billion. Consolidated growth was led by the subsidiary. The core security business rests on mobile security, biometric authentication, e-signature and security consulting.

The company is building global security, AI security and post-quantum cryptography (PQC) into new growth engines. In global security, it works with Palo Alto Networks to extend from solution supply into AI-driven vulnerability analysis, security consulting, and managed operating services such as XDR and MDR.

Its revenue model is shifting away from one-time solution sales toward subscription security solutions and security monitoring that generate recurring revenue. Its customers are in finance, the public sector and enterprises, and the plan is an integrated service spanning diagnosis, solution deployment and operation. In the domestic security market, it competes with listed security peers such as Ahnlab and Axgate.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell 59.0% from KRW 39.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.2 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 104.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 269.6 billion in 2025, growing for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025. Revenue in 2025 rose 158.1% year on year and was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit of KRW 2.17 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 1.28 billion in 2023, and the loss widened to KRW 1.57 billion in 2024. The 2025 operating loss narrowed to KRW 303 million, but the company has posted operating losses for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Operating margin moved from 5.5% in 2022 to -7.9% in 2023, -1.5% in 2024 and -0.1% in 2025, so the loss margin was smallest in 2025, the year revenue was largest. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 959 million in 2023, KRW 0.99 million in 2024 and KRW 4.55 billion in 2025.

The net loss grew in 2025 even as the operating loss narrowed, which makes non-operating items a point to check in the full annual report. The debt-to-equity ratio reached 1,214.2% in 2024 and stood at 681.2% at end-2025, when total liabilities of KRW 177.1 billion far exceeded total equity of KRW 26 billion.

Quarterly revenue fell from KRW 75.6 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 61.2 billion in Q4 and KRW 53.9 billion in Q1 2026, then rose to KRW 64.7 billion in Q2 and KRW 120.9 billion in Q3, extending growth for two consecutive quarters.

Operating losses of KRW 310 million in Q3 2025, KRW 388 million in Q4, KRW 836 million in Q1 2026 and KRW 918 million in Q2 gave way to an operating profit of KRW 1.66 billion in Q3 2026.

In Q2 2026 net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.81 billion despite that operating loss, and press reports said cumulative nine-month net income turned positive partly on convertible bond valuation gains.

05

Industry analysis

GPU server demand that lifted the subsidiary's results comes from expanding AI infrastructure investment. Memory-related product sales also contributed to revenue growth over the same period. This demand is sensitive to the AI infrastructure investment cycle, so revenue could swing widely if capital spending slows.

In security, the arrival of the quantum computing era has exposed the limits of existing cryptography, and demand for PQC adoption is growing quickly in finance and the public sector.

A PQC transition typically starts with identifying the cryptographic assets that enterprises and public agencies rely on, then migrating them to quantum-resistant schemes.

Among listed domestic security companies, the company competes with Ahnlab and Axgate, and it points to its global security partnerships and PQC authentication demonstrations as differentiators. Gross margin improved in the first half of 2026, from 13.5% to 15.1%.

Yet higher selling and administrative costs from new businesses widened the standalone operating loss from KRW 800 million to KRW 1.1 billion. Accordingly, the key point to watch is whether the growth in GPU server sales or the new PQC and AI security businesses ends up as the earnings base.

06

Outlook

The company attributed the first-half revenue decline to some major projects being pushed into the second half, and it plans to focus on recognizing that deferred project revenue alongside business-level profitability management.

It also plans to manage headcount and personnel costs for new businesses while running cost-control measures in parallel. It intends to scale up PQC certification and cryptographic transition projects, centered on global security, AI security and edge CS.

The plan also includes expanding diagnosis and PoC work with financial and public-sector clients to connect them to actual build-and-operate projects. The company also plans to strengthen recurring revenue through license renewals, maintenance and operating services.

It invested in a semiconductor IP firm to widen its reach from PQC-based security chips to security infrastructure. The company said it validated the commercial potential of PQC-based mutual authentication in a zero-trust overlay financial network demonstration.

Cumulative operating results through Q3 2026 show a loss of about KRW 100 million, so Q4 2026 results will determine the direction of full-year operating profit. The next checkpoints are how much deferred project revenue is recognized in Q4 2026 and the full-year operating result in the 2026 annual report.

Bear factors

Three straight years of operating losses

The company recorded operating losses from 2023 to 2025, and the 2025 loss narrowed to KRW 303 million. Among the disclosed quarters, Q3 2026 is the only one with an operating profit.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a KRW 4.55 billion loss in 2025, so the losses carried down to the net income line.

New-business costs and wider standalone losses

Standalone operating loss widened from KRW 800 million to KRW 1.1 billion in the first half of 2026 as selling and administrative costs for new businesses rose. The company attributes the pressure to headcount additions and personnel costs. If new businesses take longer to generate revenue, this cost structure could keep weighing on results.

Revenue recognition timing and non-operating swings

First-half consolidated revenue fell 10.7% year on year because some major projects were pushed into the second half. The Q3 net profit was supported partly by convertible bond valuation gains. Operating trends and net income direction can diverge, which is a weakness to monitor.

Risk factors

Revenue recognition timing

Project-based revenue shifts between quarters, as seen in the first-half decline and the Q3 surge. Deferred second-half revenue must be recognized in Q4 for the full-year picture to improve. Quarterly results can swing sharply on the timing of recognition.

Dependence on subsidiary demand

GPU server sales at the subsidiary depend on AI infrastructure investment flows. A slowdown in demand could sharply reduce consolidated growth. The standalone operating loss widened in the first half, so the company's own security business offers a limited cushion if subsidiary demand weakens.

Leverage and convertible bonds

Total liabilities of KRW 177.1 billion far exceed total equity of KRW 26 billion at end-2025. The debt-to-equity ratio was 1,214.2% in 2024 and 681.2% in 2025. Convertible bond valuation gains and losses can move net income, so the headline profit is not a clean read on operating strength.

Overall view

The third quarter of 2026 was the first quarter with an operating profit in the disclosed series, with consolidated revenue of KRW 120.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.66 billion.

However, the company posted operating losses for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, and the last four quarters total a KRW 567 million net loss attributable to controlling shareholders.

Most of the growth came from GPU server and memory sales at a subsidiary, while the company's own security business widened its standalone operating loss in the first half.

PQC and AI security are areas where demand in finance and the public sector is rising, but how far they convert into recurring revenue remains a task for the next annual report. On valuation, the recent net losses leave the price-to-earnings ratio uncalculated, and the price-to-book ratio sits above book value.

The debt-to-equity ratio of 681.2% at end-2025 calls for close attention to financial leverage. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results at end-December 2026 and the 2026 annual report in late March 2027.
