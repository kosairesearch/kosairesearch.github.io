Gridwiz: Testing Whether the Turn to Profit Holds

Gridwiz, a leading Korean demand response operator, returned to operating profit in 2025 and again in the third quarter of 2026, but a cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.39 billion over the last four quarters keeps the durability of earnings as the next point to verify.

02

Business structure

Gridwiz, founded in March 2013, is an energy solutions company built around demand response (DR), with electric vehicle charging, energy storage systems (ESS) and solar power businesses alongside it. The company participates in the demand resource market operated by the Korea Power Exchange.

Under the DR mechanism, companies cut electricity use when demand surges and receive compensation, and Gridwiz brokers and manages that process for a fee. Pre-listing materials cited a domestic DR operating share of 47.0%, and the company is described as the number one DR operator in Korea.

Its customers are mainly industrial firms and data centers. By segment, DR made up 83.9% of 2023 revenue, EV charging modems and chargers (EM) 9.8%, ESS 1.1%, solar (PV) 3.2%, and other businesses 3.1%.

According to Valueline's company profile, the ESS business offers a one-stop consulting, EPC and O&M model and operates 904 MWh of capacity under contract. The solar business runs a 10 MW plant through a subsidiary and also performs EPC work.

The EV charging unit supplies modems, bidirectional chargers and energy service applications that link charging infrastructure into distributed resources.

The company cites its DR operating know-how and customer power data as a way to lower participation costs and raise incentive revenue, and points to a higher customer retention rate than peers as a strength. It is also expanding into ESS, VPP and V2G technologies for efficient power management and trading.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell 5.5% year over year, from KRW 1,319 hundred million in 2023 to KRW 1,247 hundred million in 2024, and then rose 0.7% year over year to KRW 1,256 hundred million in 2025. Among 2023-2025, the largest year was 2023 and the smallest was 2024.

Operating profit of KRW 15.9 hundred million in 2023 turned into an operating loss of KRW 43.4 hundred million in 2024, and then returned to an operating profit of KRW 8.2 hundred million in 2025. The operating margin was 1.2% in 2023, -3.5% in 2024, and 0.6% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 43.1 hundred million in 2023, a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 30.1 hundred million in 2024, and a net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 21.4 hundred million in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 41.6 hundred million in 2023, a net loss of KRW 27.9 hundred million in 2024, and a net profit of KRW 20.2 hundred million in 2025, which differs from net profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

The press cited cost efficiency and business structure improvement as the background to the 2025 profitability recovery. Cash flow from operating activities was KRW 25.9 hundred million in 2023, KRW 25.3 hundred million in 2024, and KRW 54 hundred million in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 427 hundred million, operating profit was KRW 33.4 hundred million, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 78.3 hundred million, but in Q4 the company swung to revenue of KRW 347 hundred million (down 18.8% quarter over quarter), an operating loss of KRW 6 hundred million, and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 44 hundred million.

After Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 206 hundred million (down 40.5% quarter over quarter), an operating loss of KRW 22 hundred million, and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 13 hundred million, the losses narrowed in Q2 to revenue of KRW 209 hundred million, an operating loss of KRW 16.5 hundred million, and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 9.5 hundred million.

Q3 2026 revenue is KRW 382 hundred million (up 83.4% quarter over quarter and down 10.4% year over year), operating profit is KRW 30.9 hundred million (a return to profit from the previous quarter and down 7.5% year over year), and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders is KRW 22.5 hundred million (down 71.3% year over year).

The key to the profit recovery is that operating profit returned to positive as revenue rose for two consecutive quarters from the Q1 low.

05

Industry analysis

Demand response is gaining a role as an alternative to adding generation capacity when electricity demand peaks. Incheon Ilbo has pointed out that AI expansion and tighter carbon-neutrality policies are raising the importance of DR.

Rising power demand from data centers aligns with the company's base of industrial and data center customers. On renewables, RE100 and PPA projects continue to expand, and the company is described as having a platform that integrates DR, ESS and EV charging.

V2G technology links electric vehicles to the grid in both directions, and the company's supply of bidirectional chargers places it in that segment. In the domestic DR market, the company holds a 47.0% operating share according to pre-listing materials and ranks as the number one operator.

The concentration of the market in one operator is both a strength in execution and customer base and a risk, since losses from a policy change or customer attrition would fall heavily on results.

DR incentive revenue depends on the design of the system and the scale of participating resources, so policy direction feeds directly into the revenue structure. On the demand side, data center investment and peak power management needs are supportive factors.

ESS and solar accounted for just 1.1% and 3.2% of 2023 revenue, so DR remains the axis that drives company-wide results.

Outlook

The company has decided to absorb its wholly owned subsidiary IDR Service through a merger, with Gridwiz as the surviving company and IDR Service as the dissolving company. The merger is structured as a small-scale merger.

The effective date is January 1, 2027, the shareholder meeting for the closing report is January 4, 2027, and the scheduled merger registration date is January 5, 2027.

After the merger, the subsidiary's profit and loss will be consolidated into the parent's results, so filings from 2027 onward will need to be compared against the current structure. On the business side, the company plans to expand power management and trading using ESS, VPP and V2G technologies.

On renewables, an integrated platform riding the RE100 and PPA project growth is presented as a growth axis. Disclosures provide few figures on the revenue scale or order wins of these businesses, so the outlook is best read in directional terms. Fourth-quarter 2026 results have not yet been filed in a periodic report.

Whether full-year 2026 operating profit ends in the black depends on how far third-quarter operating profit offsets the operating losses of the first and second quarters, along with fourth-quarter results.

Once the 2026 annual report confirms the full-year operating margin and debt ratio, those figures can be compared with the 2025 disclosed values.

Bull factors

Domestic DR leadership and rising demand

According to pre-listing materials, the domestic DR operating share is 47.0%, placing the company at the top, with industrial firms and data centers as customers. AI expansion and carbon-neutrality policy are raising the profile of DR as an alternative to new generation capacity, a supportive demand factor.

The share figure dates from pre-listing materials, so any change in the current share needs to be checked in later disclosures.

Return to profit and quarterly revenue recovery

Operating profit turned positive for 2025 and returned to profit in the third quarter of 2026 at KRW 3.09 billion. Revenue rose for two consecutive quarters after the first-quarter 2026 low, which reads as a recovery signal.

Expansion into ESS, V2G and renewables

New business lines include 904 MWh of ESS capacity under contract, a 10 MW solar plant, and expansion into ESS, VPP and V2G technologies. The growing RE100 and PPA project flow is also supportive.

Given 2023 revenue shares of 1.1% for ESS and 3.2% for solar, these businesses still contribute only modestly to company-wide results.

09

Bear factors

Cumulative losses and limited track record of profit

The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 4.39 billion. Operating losses continued in the first and second quarters of 2026, and the third-quarter return to profit represents a single quarter. Whether profits persist across consecutive quarters remains to be seen.

Quarterly revenue volatility

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 38.2 billion rose 83.4% from the prior quarter but fell 10.4% year on year. Revenue declined 5.5% in 2024, and the first quarter of 2026 recorded a 40.5% quarter-on-quarter drop to a low. Such large quarterly swings make earnings harder to forecast.

Concentration in DR and dependence on policy

DR accounted for 83.9% of 2023 revenue. Because DR incentive income depends on system design and the scale of participating resources, a policy change would affect the entire revenue structure. Diversification is under way, but non-DR businesses still account for a small share of revenue.

Risk factors

Policy and regulatory risk

DR operators earn brokerage fees within the demand resource market, so changes to compensation rules or participation conditions would alter revenue. The direction of renewable energy and EV charging policy also affects business opportunities. The direction and pace of policy change lie outside the company's control.

Earnings volatility

The operating margin has moved in a low range, at 1.2% in 2023, -3.5% in 2024 and 0.6% in 2025. Quarterly results alternate between profit and loss, which makes the earnings trend harder to read. With margins in the low single digits, revenue swings feed directly into profit.

Execution risk in merger and restructuring

The absorption merger of wholly owned subsidiary IDR Service was decided as a small-scale merger with an effective date of January 1, 2027. Integrating the subsidiary's earnings, staff and operations into the parent creates execution risk. The financial effect of the merger must be verified in later periodic reports.

Overall view

Gridwiz is the number one DR operator with a 47.0% domestic operating share, and its demand management business, serving industrial firms and data centers, accounts for most revenue.

Annual operating profit turned positive at KRW 816 million in 2025, and third-quarter 2026 operating profit returned to profit at KRW 3.09 billion.

However, the cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 4.39 billion, and operating losses continued in the first and second quarters of 2026.

Revenue rose for two consecutive quarters after the first-quarter 2026 low, but fell 10.4% year on year in the third quarter. The debt ratio rose to 41.7% in 2025 from 29.4% in 2024.

The business is highly concentrated in DR and sensitive to policy changes, while ESS, solar and V2G expansion still represents a small share of revenue. The absorption merger of IDR Service has an effective date of January 1, 2027 and should be tracked alongside the structural change.

The next points to verify are whether profitability holds in fourth-quarter 2026 results and the 2026 annual report.
