Laser Equipment Mix Shift: Can the Margin Recovery Hold?

02

Business structure

EO Technics, founded in 1989 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2000, is a specialist in laser application equipment. Its core business is the manufacture and sale of semiconductor laser markers and other laser applications, supplied to the semiconductor, display, PCB and mobile phone industries.

Its equipment lines are broadly divided into Marker, Annealing and Cutting. Markers engrave permanent information such as manufacturing dates, serial numbers and QR codes for production traceability, and remain the established revenue base.

Annealing equipment is laser heat treatment that heats only specific areas, and its adoption is discussed in connection with Samsung Electronics' 1C nano DRAM investment and NAND production at 400 layers or more.

Cutting equipment is used for Grooving and Full Cutting in HBM processes and is cited as a growth axis alongside the largest customer's HBM output expansion.

Segment revenue shares are not presented in the disclosure summary, and the semiconductor equipment share figure appears only as an iM Securities estimate from September 2025.

Customer-level revenue shares also do not appear in the disclosure summary, while broker reports mention possible initial shipments of high-priced equipment to chipmakers in Korea, the United States and Japan beyond the largest customer.

On competition, a company official said the low-price marker segment dominated by Chinese vendors is not treated as a direct competitor. The ownership structure shows Sung Gyu-dong and 10 related persons holding 31.09% as of October 7, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual figures, 2022 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025, with revenue of KRW 447.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 92.8 billion. In 2023, revenue fell 29.2% year on year to KRW 316.4 billion, and operating profit fell 69.5% to KRW 28.3 billion, making 2023 the smallest year of the period.

The operating margin declined from 20.8% to 9.0% over the same span. After the 2023 low, revenue, operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

Revenue was KRW 320.9 billion in 2024, up 1.4%, and operating profit was KRW 31.2 billion, up 10.1%. In 2025, revenue reached KRW 380.9 billion, up 18.7%, and operating profit reached KRW 80.8 billion, up 158.8%, lifting the operating margin to 21.2%.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders climbed from KRW 36.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 57.2 billion in 2025, while net income including non-controlling interests moved from KRW 36.4 billion to KRW 57.6 billion over the same span.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 92.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 31.6 billion in 2023 and then rose to KRW 100.3 billion in 2025. The debt ratio was 13.1% at the end of 2025, compared with 14.2% in 2022.

On a quarterly basis, revenue rose from KRW 100.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 101.3 billion in the fourth quarter, up 0.8% from the prior quarter, and then to KRW 132.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026, with four consecutive quarterly increases starting in the fourth quarter of 2025.

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 14.4 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, down 44.5% from the prior quarter, and rose for three consecutive quarters from the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 47.1 billion in the third quarter, up 81.0% year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders, however, dropped 41.3% from KRW 37 billion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 21.7 billion in the third quarter, and was also down 6.4% year on year.

The gap between rising operating profit and falling controlling-shareholder profit should be reconciled against non-operating items and income tax in the quarterly report notes.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from the front end is centered on memory capital spending and expanding HBM output. iM Securities said in a September 2025 report that expanded 1C nano DRAM investment at Samsung Electronics and NAND production at 400 layers or more would increase annealing equipment orders.

The same report expected the largest customer's HBM output to rise sharply in 2026, lifting shipments of ultra short pulse equipment for HBM4. It also included an estimate that full cutting laser equipment would be adopted in HBM4E processes from the fourth quarter of 2026.

This estimate dates from September 2025, so actual adoption should be checked against the annual report and customer-related disclosures. Heungkuk Securities assessed in August 2025 that AI demand is feeding a DRAM expansion cycle centered on leading-edge processes.

Shinhan Investment Corp. explained in a February 2026 report that fab investment is expanding amid a shortage of semiconductor supply, which should sustain growth in laser markers.

By contrast, a company official said in March 2026 that the low-price segment dominated by Chinese vendors is not a competitive arena for the company.

The company is compared with semiconductor equipment listed peers such as HANMI Semiconductor and Wonik IPS, but few peers operate in the same laser application processes. Current materials offer limited basis for pinning down where the cycle stands.

06

Outlook

Management comments point more to direction than to numerical targets. At the regular shareholder meeting on March 31, 2026, Chairman Sung Gyu-dong said he expects this year's results to rise sharply from the prior year and to reach a record or higher.

At the same meeting, a company official said major customers are placing sufficient orders for laser markers and that a good performance is still expected for much of the year. On facilities, the company said construction of its Anseong plant was targeted to start in May or June 2026 and finish before year-end.

Once completed, total production capacity would double, according to the company. Quarterly revenue in 2026 was KRW 115.1 billion in the first quarter, KRW 127.8 billion in the second and KRW 132.2 billion in the third, so fourth-quarter revenue will decide whether the record claim holds for the full year.

The disclosed record benchmarks are 2022 revenue of KRW 447.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 92.8 billion, and whether 2026 full-year results exceed them is the key check on the chairman's statement.

In September 2025, iM Securities estimated 2026 revenue at KRW 480 billion and operating profit at KRW 123.8 billion, but that estimate is a year old and should be compared with the 2026 disclosed figures.

A March 2026 iM Securities report flagged the possibility of additional orders in 2026 for HBM4 and OSAT-related equipment from the largest customer. The 2026 annual report, the next regular filing, will finalize full-year and fourth-quarter results.

Bull factors

Wider adoption of annealing and cutting equipment

iM Securities projected in September 2025 that annealing equipment adoption would grow with 1C nano DRAM investment at Samsung Electronics and NAND production at 400 layers or more. Cutting equipment is cited for adoption in HBM4 and HBM4E processes. The bull case assumes that a larger semiconductor equipment share changes the profitability structure.

Margin recovery and rising quarterly operating profit

The 2025 operating margin of 21.2% exceeded the 20.8% recorded in 2022. Operating profit rose for three consecutive quarters from the first quarter of 2026, and third-quarter operating profit of KRW 47.1 billion was up 81.0% year on year. On an operating profit basis, the recovery is visible quarter by quarter.

Capacity expansion plans

Chairman Sung Gyu-dong said at the March 2026 shareholder meeting that this year's results would be at or above a record. The company also disclosed a plan for total production capacity to double once the Anseong plant is completed. A company official described major customers' laser marker orders as sufficient.

Bear factors

Quality of third-quarter earnings

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose to KRW 47.1 billion, but net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 41.3% from the prior quarter to KRW 21.7 billion.

Until the reasons why operating gains did not carry through to net profit are confirmed in the quarterly report, earnings quality remains an open question. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totals KRW 107 billion.

High valuation and multiple pressure

The price-to-book ratio sits above the three-year peak average that iM Securities used in its valuation. Shinhan Investment Corp. also noted in February 2026 that the valuation was high relative to the industry average.

With a low dividend yield, returns depend on growth expectations, so a shortfall against those expectations could increase pressure on the multiples.

Low-price competition and customer concentration

A company official described the low-price laser marker segment as dominated by Chinese vendors. Earnings depend heavily on the largest customer's HBM and memory investment flows. Customer-level revenue shares do not appear in the disclosure summary, so concentration cannot be measured numerically.

Risk factors

Legal dispute

The company filed criminal complaints against DIT for alleged violations including the Industrial Technology Protection Act and the Unfair Competition Prevention Act. At the defendant's request, the case was transferred from the Daejeon District Court to the Suwon District Court and is under review.

The company said in March 2026 that the trial could take one and a half to two years, though it could be shortened.

Capex cycle and adoption timing

The timing of annealing and cutting equipment adoption rests on broker estimates from September 2025. If memory capital spending adjusts, equipment orders could decline and adoption could slip. Whether the leading-edge DRAM expansion flagged by Heungkuk Securities continues is the key question.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Quarterly operating profit fell 44.5% in the fourth quarter of 2025 and then rose 106.3% in the first quarter of 2026, showing large swings. In the third quarter of 2026, operating profit rose while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders declined. When quarterly profit moves sharply, the range of interpretation widens until annual results are confirmed.

12

Overall view

EO Technics has seen revenue, operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders recover through 2025 from the 2023 low. The 2025 operating margin of 21.2% moved above the 2022 level. Quarterly revenue and operating profit in 2026 have also trended higher.

However, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 41.3% from the prior quarter in the third quarter of 2026, so operating gains and bottom-line trends diverge.

On the business side, the key question is whether annealing and cutting equipment, alongside laser markers, expand the semiconductor equipment share of revenue.

If that shift is confirmed, the profitability structure would change, but the current outlook leans heavily on broker estimates and needs verification through the annual report.

On valuation, the price-to-book and price-to-earnings ratios sit above the industry average and the company's past levels, so whether earnings developments meet market expectations is the variable for interpreting the share price.

The bull side rests on HBM and NAND equipment adoption and capacity expansion, while the bear side is set by quarterly earnings swings, litigation and customer concentration. The next checkpoints are the Anseong plant completion target in late December 2026 and the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027.
