02

Business structure

Moatech was founded in 1989 and listed on KOSDAQ in 1997 as a maker of small precision motors. Built on its in-house development of Korea's first PM (permanent magnet) type stepping motor, the company built a position in the PC and home appliance optical disk drive (ODD) market, which was once its core business.

It has since widened its lineup to actuators for automotive headlamps and HVAC units, along with BLDC motors and fan motors. According to the company's business commentary, its airflow control actuators for vehicles sell steadily in line with carbon emission regulations.

The business model is B2B, supplying in-house designed and manufactured precision drive components to large domestic and overseas electronics and automotive parts makers.

IB Tomato reported that domestic sales account for 84.5% of total revenue, and that sales of items other than stepping motors, which are purchased for resale, more than doubled from KRW 1.3 billion to KRW 2.9 billion.

The company has said it aims to shift from single motor supply to a total solution provider combining motors, gears and control circuits. Its largest shareholder is the Japan-based Minebea Mitsumi group, and the company is expanding sales promotion through that group's global sales network.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales moved from KRW 32 billion in the fiscal year ended March 2023 to KRW 30.6 billion in the year ended March 2024 (down 4.3% year on year), then rose two years in a row to KRW 31.5 billion in the year ended March 2025 (up 3.0%) and KRW 42.6 billion in the year ended March 2026 (up 35.4%).

Operating losses were KRW 2.26 billion, KRW 2.17 billion, KRW 3.16 billion and KRW 1.69 billion across the four fiscal years from March 2023 to March 2026, and the operating margins were -7.1%, -7.1%, -10.0% and -4.0%.

IB Tomato reported that the cost of sales ratio reached 89.7% in the year ended March 2026, so gross profit of KRW 4.4 billion did not absorb SG&A expenses of KRW 6.1 billion. Gross profit itself rose from KRW 3.2 billion to KRW 4.4 billion, which points to a better profit base.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a KRW 1.52 billion loss in the year ended March 2023 to a KRW 56.48 million profit in the year ended March 2024, a turnaround to profit, then back to a KRW 1.98 billion loss in the year ended March 2025, and then a KRW 666 million loss in the year ended March 2026, which was a narrower loss.

The year ended March 2024 had a profit attributable to controlling shareholders despite an operating loss, so non-operating items should be checked in the periodic report.

Quarterly sales fell from KRW 10.5 billion in the second quarter of 2025 to KRW 10.1 billion and KRW 9.93 billion in the third and fourth quarters, then rose to KRW 12.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 14.4 billion in the second quarter, up 37.1% year on year.

The second-quarter 2026 operating loss of KRW 130 million was smaller than in the prior quarter and the year-earlier quarter, and the company has posted operating losses for five straight quarters from the second quarter of 2025.

Cash flow is a burden: operating cash flow was KRW -2.84 billion in the year ended March 2026 versus KRW -753 million a year earlier, and IB Tomato reported that receivables rose from KRW 4.9 billion to KRW 7.9 billion and inventory from KRW 3.4 billion to KRW 5.5 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Moatech's end markets are shifting from PC and home appliance optical disk drives toward automotive electronics and premium home appliances. The company, once a core supplier in the ODD market, is described as expanding its supply chain into higher-value markets such as automotive electronics and premium appliances.

In automotive, the spread of eco-friendly vehicle demand is cited as a background to the recent earnings improvement, according to FnGuide's company overview. After mass production of airflow control components for hybrid and electric vehicles, EV thermal management actuators are presented as the next growth driver.

Demand for auto parts is tied to vehicle production volumes and new model development cycles, so production swings are likely to pass through to sales.

On competition, Moatech emphasizes its in-house design and long history of stepping motor manufacturing, but it competes with large domestic and overseas motor and actuator suppliers.

It is compared with larger listed electronics and components companies that are expanding their auto parts exposure, while its sales scale remains small. With a high cost of sales ratio, growing scale while improving the cost structure is the central industry challenge.

06

Outlook

The company's confirmed plans center on growth through automotive parts. According to IB Tomato, Moatech plans full-scale mass production of EV thermal management actuators from 2027, and airflow control components for hybrid and electric vehicles are already in production.

The company has also presented a plan to shift from single motor supply to a total solution provider in order to raise profitability. Sales promotion through the global sales network of its largest shareholder, the Minebea Mitsumi group, is also expanding.

The key question is whether the second-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 14.4 billion, which rose for two straight quarters, will carry into these plans. Investment industry analysts have, however, taken a cautious view on whether the sales growth recorded in the year ended March 2026 can be sustained.

How much rising receivables and inventory from sales expansion weigh on cash flow is another point to watch.

The next checkpoints are the operating loss and cost of sales ratio in the third-quarter 2026 periodic report, followed by how far the operating margin narrows in the annual report for the year ending March 2027.

08

Bull factors

Sales growth has run for two years

Annual sales rose two years in a row across the years ended March 2025 and March 2026, reaching KRW 42.6 billion in the latter, up 35.4%. Second-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 14.4 billion were up 37.1% year on year. According to the company's business commentary, automotive output is rising as its customer base diversifies.

Narrowing losses and a better profit base

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 3.16 billion in the year ended March 2025 to KRW 1.69 billion in the year ended March 2026, and the second-quarter 2026 loss of KRW 130 million was smaller than a year earlier. Gross profit rose from KRW 3.2 billion to KRW 4.4 billion.

The operating margin of -4.0% is the smallest loss margin across the fiscal years from March 2023 to March 2026.

Electrification parts and a global sales network

Airflow control components for hybrid and EV applications are already in production, and EV thermal management actuators are planned for full-scale production from 2027, according to IB Tomato. Sales promotion through the global network of the largest shareholder, Minebea Mitsumi, is also growing. The company's view is that these two areas could help absorb fixed costs if they broaden the sales base.

09

Bear factors

Four straight years of operating losses, five straight quarters of losses

Operating losses have run for four straight fiscal years from March 2023 to March 2026, and the company has posted losses for five straight quarters from the second quarter of 2025. IB Tomato reported that SG&A expenses of KRW 6.1 billion exceeded gross profit of KRW 4.4 billion in the year ended March 2026. The 89.7% cost of sales ratio is cited as the largest constraint on profit improvement.

Cash outflow and working capital strain

Operating cash flow was KRW -2.84 billion in the year ended March 2026, a larger outflow than KRW -753 million a year earlier. Rising receivables and inventory alongside sales growth mean the core business has not yet generated cash, according to IB Tomato. Investment analysts have also taken a cautious view on whether sales growth can be sustained.

Ownership concentration and a small free float

Minebea Mitsumi holds a 50.8% stake, and the free float ratio was 20.83% as of September 14, 2026, per WiseReport. The company was designated as an investment caution stock in June 2024, according to IB Tomato. A limited free float can amplify share price volatility.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and auto demand

Because automotive actuator sales are the main growth engine, the company is sensitive to vehicle production swings and customer order flows. IB Tomato noted that the sustainability of sales growth in the year ended March 2026 needs to be watched. Production adjustments by a specific customer or model could feed directly into sales.

Profitability and cost structure

With a cost of sales ratio of 89.7%, the company has not been able to absorb SG&A costs even as sales grow. Without both cost ratio improvement and SG&A reduction, the shift to operating profit could be delayed. The operating margin for the year ended March 2026 was -4.0%.

Working capital and liquidity

Operating cash outflows and rising working capital could increase the need for external funding. The debt ratio rose to 12.7% in the year ended March 2026 from 8.9% in the year ended March 2025. With a small free float and a past caution designation, changes in trading conditions could drive share price swings.

12

Overall view

Moatech is a maker of small precision motors and automotive actuators, and its sales reached KRW 42.6 billion in the year ended March 2026, rising two years in a row, which confirms top-line growth.

However, operating losses have continued for four straight fiscal years, and losses have persisted for five straight quarters through the second quarter of 2026. Gross profit rising from KRW 3.2 billion to KRW 4.4 billion and the operating margin falling to -4.0% read as signs of a stronger profit base.

On the other side, operating cash outflow widened to KRW -2.84 billion, and rising receivables and inventory leave a working capital burden. On valuation, the price-to-book level is below book net assets, but net losses over the last four quarters make earnings-based comparison difficult.

Minebea Mitsumi's sales network and the EV thermal management actuator plan are growth routes, but their timing and scale need to be confirmed through future disclosures.

The next checkpoints are the operating loss in the third-quarter 2026 periodic report and the operating margin in the annual report for the year ending March 2027.
