AI Substrate Equipment Orders Rise; Losses Widen Again in Q3 2026

Taesung returned to operating profit in the first half of 2026 on AI-related equipment orders, but its operating loss and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened again in the third quarter of 2026, leaving earnings durability as the next point to watch.

  1. 1

    Consolidated first-half 2026 revenue of KRW 50.4 billion, operating profit of KRW 2.2 billion and half-year net profit of KRW 3.0 billion were reported from a filing on August 12, 2026, with first-half revenue reaching about 133% of full-year 2025 revenue.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

    New businesses center on TGV copper-plating equipment for glass substrates, composite copper foil materials and the new Cheonan plant, and how far these translate into quarterly profit is the next point to check.

02

Business structure

Taesung, founded in 2006, develops, manufactures and sells automated equipment used in PCB, glass substrate and composite copper foil production. It listed on KOSDAQ in June 2022 and holds subsidiaries in Vietnam and China.

Its core product is wet-process equipment, the key equipment in automated PCB production, supplemented by surface-treatment and etching equipment. Customers often cited include Pengding (formerly ZDT), a Foxconn affiliate, Samsung Electro-Mechanics, LG Innotek and Daeduck Electronics.

Supply of surface-treatment and etching equipment has expanded as demand for high-performance semiconductor package substrates has grown.

One new business is glass substrate process equipment: in January 2026 the company disclosed supply of an electroplating tool that fills TGV through-holes with copper from the bottom up, and an order for chemical process equipment for TGV glass back-end processing.

The other is composite copper foil material for secondary batteries, where the company said it signed an initial supply contract with a large domestic company and received purchase orders from three companies.

For China, the company's CEO has said direct material supply is more profitable over the long term than equipment sales. In October 2025 the company announced etching equipment supply worth KRW 5.8 billion in total to affiliates of a Chinese semiconductor substrate maker.

The business is broadening from traditional PCB equipment toward glass substrates and composite copper foil materials.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 61.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 33.3 billion in 2023 (down 45.6% year on year), recovered to KRW 59.2 billion in 2024 (up 77.6%), and fell again to KRW 37.9 billion in 2025 (down 36.1%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit moved from KRW 2.31 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 686 million in 2023 (a swing into loss), operating profit of KRW 6.04 billion in 2024 (a swing into profit) and an operating loss of KRW 2.51 billion in 2025 (a swing into loss), with operating margins of 3.8%, -2.1%, 10.2% and -6.6%.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to KRW 5.97 billion in 2024 from a loss, then to a net loss of KRW 2.54 billion in 2025. In 2024, net income and net income attributable to controlling shareholders were both KRW 5.97 billion.

Quarterly, the fourth quarter of 2025 posted revenue of KRW 12.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.41 billion, a swing into profit.

The first quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 19.9 billion and an operating loss of KRW 883 million, and the second quarter revenue of KRW 30.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 3.12 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 23.3 billion fell 23.6% from the prior quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 1.96 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.72 billion, a continued loss that widened from a year earlier.

Over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 2 billion. Operating cash flow deteriorated from KRW 4.49 billion in 2024 to negative KRW 9.75 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 128.1 billion and total liabilities KRW 44.5 billion, with the debt ratio falling from 94.3% in 2024 to 34.7% in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for PCB equipment such as Taesung's tracks investment in AI servers and high-performance semiconductors.

The industry expects demand for high-performance substrates such as FC-BGA and high-layer-count PCBs to keep rising as AI server and data center investment expands, and continued capital spending by global PCB makers is cited as a supportive factor for equipment suppliers.

According to the company, capital spending by major domestic and overseas PCB makers has been gradually recovering in 2026. By contrast, in 2025 inventory adjustments and economic uncertainty in the global PCB market weighed on downstream demand and reduced orders.

Demand for automotive PCBs is also described as growing on investment related to electric vehicles and advanced driver assistance systems. On competitive position, a corporate monitoring summary describes the company as holding a dominant domestic position while expanding into the Chinese market.

Order volumes remain sensitive to customers' capital spending timing. Glass substrate equipment depends on how quickly TGV processes are adopted, while composite copper foil materials follow a separate cycle tied to the shift in secondary battery copper foil processes. On the cycle, the pattern reads as a downturn in 2025 followed by an order recovery in 2026.

06

Outlook

The company said cumulative first-half 2026 orders were about KRW 91.3 billion and expected them to approach about KRW 100 billion once deals under negotiation in June were reflected.

Because revenue typically follows orders by three to four months, it said the backlog would be recognized progressively from the second quarter.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose to KRW 23.3 billion but the company returned to an operating loss, so conversion of orders into revenue and recovery of margins both need to be confirmed. In China, the company said investment is expected to expand from 2026 and additional orders are anticipated.

For glass substrates, it said some customers brought forward mass-production preparation, so shipment and installation of equipment are being pulled into the current year, and it expected concrete results to become visible from the fourth quarter.

Follow-on orders for glass substrate equipment have reportedly continued after initial deliveries. For composite copper foil materials, the company has been preparing mass production through construction at its Ansan material plant and operations at the new Cheonan plant.

The next checkpoints are how fast the order backlog converts into fourth-quarter 2026 revenue, how much glass substrate equipment shipments contribute, and whether composite copper foil purchase orders become mass-production sales.

Bull factors

Demand from AI and high-end substrate equipment

Rising demand for AI servers and high-performance substrates such as FC-BGA keeps PCB makers investing in equipment. Taesung supplies wet-process, surface-treatment and etching equipment and sits directly in this demand flow. The company reported cumulative first-half 2026 orders of about KRW 91.3 billion.

Return to profit in the first half of 2026

First-half 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 50.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.2 billion were reported from the filing. The company moved from an operating loss in 2025 to profit, and the second-quarter operating margin was 10.2%. First-half revenue reached about 133% of full-year 2025 revenue, indicating a fast top-line recovery.

New glass substrate and composite foil businesses

Orders for TGV copper-plating and chemical process equipment for glass substrates were disclosed in January 2026, with follow-on orders reported after initial supply. Composite copper foil material secured an initial supply contract with a large domestic company and purchase orders from three companies. If these businesses monetize, the company's equipment-centered structure could broaden.

Bear factors

Losses widened again in the third quarter

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 23.3 billion rose 165.1% year on year, but the operating loss of KRW 1.96 billion and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.72 billion widened. The first-half turn to profit did not carry through at the quarterly level. Over the last four quarters, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totals only KRW 2 billion.

Cyclical swings and customer concentration

Revenue swung widely, falling 45.6% in 2023 and 36.1% in 2025. Reports attributed the 2025 decline to PCB inventory adjustments that reduced orders for surface-treatment and etching equipment. A concentrated customer base among large PCB makers means results can move with customers' investment timing.

High price-to-book and market alert

A March 2026 report noted that the price-to-book ratio was high relative to the industry average and that further profit improvement would be needed to validate growth. The same period saw attention on whether a market alert measure would be lifted. With a low dividend yield, share price support rests heavily on earnings.

10

Risk factors

Customer and cycle concentration

Revenue depends heavily on PCB makers' equipment investment schedules. Orders fell during the 2025 inventory adjustment and revenue dropped 36.1%, and a similar pattern could recur. Expected Chinese investment is a supportive factor but deepens dependence on specific customers and regions.

Scaling and monetizing new businesses

Glass substrate equipment and composite copper foil materials require time for mass-production preparation and customer qualification. Whether the Cheonan plant operations and the Ansan material plant preparations proceed as planned is critical. As these businesses grow, initial investment and cost burdens could weigh on margins.

Cash flow and market alert

If cash continues to flow out of operations, funds available for operations could shrink. A March 2026 report raised whether a market alert measure would be lifted. With quarterly profit volatile, a larger working capital burden could reduce financial flexibility.
