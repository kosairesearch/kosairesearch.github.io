02

Business structure

SR biotek is a cosmetics manufacturer founded in May 2007 and listed on KONEX in June 2017. Although the market tags it as bio-pharma, it develops and manufactures the full range of basic and functional cosmetics.

Its core technology is Altum Peptide, a material that combines microneedles with peptides. The company developed it in 2020 and launched it on the market in 2021. Altum Peptide underpins the company's own brand, Idenel.

On the 2025 sales mix, OEM/ODM represents 97.19% and the Idenel brand 2.72%. Three customers account for the bulk of OEM/ODM sales. A KRX technical analysis report described the company as continuing related-party ODM work while strengthening marketing of its own brand.

Since 2023 the company has pursued a strategy of moving away from OEM/ODM toward its own brand for the professional market. The current sales mix shows that this strategy has not yet translated into large brand revenue.

The largest shareholder is Crayver Corporation, which held 58.88% according to the KRX report. Goodai Global has grown through serial acquisitions that include Tirtir, Skinfood, Seorin Company and Crayver Corporation.

Compared with large ODM players such as Cosmax and Kolmar Korea, SR biotek is small, and its microneedle-peptide material is its main point of differentiation.

Recent trends

On reported figures, 2023 revenue was KRW 8.2 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 1.3 billion and a net loss of KRW 1.5 billion. In 2024 the company turned profitable, with revenue of KRW 29.4 billion, operating profit of KRW 2.0 billion and net income of KRW 1.6 billion. Equity turned positive, ending the full capital impairment.

For 2025 the company reported revenue of KRW 37.4 billion, operating profit of KRW 2.6 billion and net income of KRW 3.3 billion. On a standalone basis, revenue rose 27.1%, operating profit 27.3% and net income 98.4%. Net income exceeded operating profit.

The operating margin was 6.91% in 2024 and 6.92% in 2025, almost unchanged. Revenue grew while profitability stayed flat.

At end-2025, total assets were KRW 9.7 billion, total liabilities KRW 4.2 billion and equity KRW 5.5 billion. The debt ratio fell from 194.03% in 2024 to 75.46% in 2025. R&D spending in 2025 was about KRW 1.09 billion, or 2.92% of sales.

According to data checked in early September 2026, the 52-week high was KRW 33,950 and the low KRW 8,010. The same data showed returns of -50.04% over six months and -34.12% over one year. The 2026-10-27 close of KRW 9,210 sits near the bottom of the 52-week range. Interim 2026 results could not be verified through search.

Outlook

FnGuide shows no broker consensus for 2026 annual results, so no official market forecast could be confirmed. The company's own 2026 guidance also could not be found through search. The outlook can therefore only be framed qualitatively from the trend through 2025 and the business structure.

The first variable is the change in sales mix. A larger share for the Idenel brand would create room for operating-margin improvement, but it was only 2.72% on the 2025 mix. Conversely, a drop in OEM/ODM orders from key customers could raise revenue volatility.

The second variable is activity at the controlling-shareholder level. A report dated 2026-07-20 said Crayver Holdings has signed a lead-manager agreement and is preparing for a Tokyo Stock Exchange listing targeted for the third quarter of 2027.

The same report said Goodai Global is also pursuing an IPO, with an expected value of around KRW 10 trillion being discussed in the market. How such ownership changes would affect SR biotek's business and share structure has not been confirmed in filings.

The third variable is the payoff from materials and R&D spending. R&D spending in 2025 was higher than in 2024, and the company says it has completed overseas trademark and patent registrations. Whether this converts into new customers or brand revenue will need to be checked in future quarterly filings.

Bull factors

Sustained profitability and a stronger balance sheet

The company went from an operating loss of KRW 1.3 billion in 2023 to operating profit of KRW 2.0 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.6 billion in 2025. Equity turned positive and the company exited capital impairment. The debt ratio fell from 194.03% in 2024 to 75.46% in 2025. The improvement in financial stability versus earlier years is a verified fact.

Proprietary material and intellectual property

Altum Peptide is a proprietary material that combines microneedles with peptides. The company says it has secured a platform technology that can extend to various active ingredients and has completed overseas trademark and patent registrations.

R&D spending rose from about KRW 548 million in 2024 to about KRW 1.09 billion in 2025. The material's competitiveness can serve as a base for customer orders.

Controlling shareholder's K-beauty footprint

Goodai Global has grown by acquiring several K-beauty companies, including Crayver Corporation. Goodai Global's 2025 consolidated revenue was KRW 1,471.8 billion. The group's distribution and brand network could help SR biotek win orders and expand overseas. No specific cooperation contract has been confirmed, however.

06

Bear factors

OEM/ODM and customer concentration

On the 2025 mix, 97.19% of sales came from OEM/ODM for three customers. The KRX report said related-party ODM work continues. In a structure that depends on a few customers and related-party dealings, order changes flow straight into results. The independence of transaction terms needs to be checked separately in filings.

Slow brand transition and flat margins

Since 2023 the company has pursued a higher-margin strategy centered on its own brand. Yet the Idenel brand accounts for only 2.72% of sales. The operating margin did not improve either, at 6.91% in 2024 and 6.92% in 2025. More time is needed before the strategy's results show up in the numbers.

Thin liquidity and wide price swings

Turnover on 2026-10-27 was just KRW 454,710 against a market capitalization of KRW 15.8 billion. Data from early September 2026 showed a free-float ratio of 41.96% and a 52-week range of KRW 8,010 to KRW 33,950. The six-month return was -50.04%. Small trades alone can move the price sharply, which makes execution difficult.

Risk factors

Macro

Cosmetics demand is affected by the consumer cycle, exchange rates and the export environment. Because SR biotek's manufacturing business centers on a few customers, weak sales at those customers can quickly reduce orders. Small KONEX stocks tend to swing more in risk-off markets. This is a general market characteristic, and no company-specific figures were confirmed.

Industry & competition

Large manufacturers such as Cosmax and Kolmar Korea are established in the domestic cosmetics ODM market. SR biotek seeks differentiation through microneedle and peptide materials, but how long that edge lasts depends on competitors' technical catch-up.

Its own brand, which is entering the professional market, must also compete with established dermo-cosmetics brands. Results of that competition have not yet appeared in sales.

Company-specific

Crayver Corporation holds 58.88%, so changes in the ownership structure carry significant weight. Press reports covered Crayver Holdings' Tokyo listing plans, but their link to SR biotek has not been confirmed.

On 2026-05-20, 45,705 preferred shares were converted into common shares, and the share count also rose through stock option exercises. The absence of verified results after the first half of 2026 adds uncertainty.

08

Overall view

SR biotek is classified as bio-pharma, but its actual business is microneedle-and-peptide cosmetics manufacturing. Two straight profitable years in 2024 and 2025 and a lower debt ratio are verified financial improvements.

On the other hand, 97.19% of sales come from OEM/ODM and the operating margin has stalled in the 6% range. The own-brand strategy has yet to show clear results, with the brand at 2.72% of sales on the 2025 mix.

Press reports of listing plans at the controlling shareholder's group are a governance factor to watch, but their effect on this company has not been confirmed in filings. As a thinly traded KONEX small cap, it also carries price-volatility and execution risk. This report is for information only, and the judgment is left to the reader.
