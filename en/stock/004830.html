Dcksung swings to a Q3 2026 loss; profit recovery is the test

02

Business structure

Dcksung was founded in 1966 to make and sell synthetic leather and was listed on the Korea Exchange in 1987. Its business is divided into synthetic leather, synthetic resin and materials segments.

The 2025 annual report shows that shoe-use synthetic leather generated KRW 100 billion or more, or 68.3% of revenue, making it the core of the business. Its main brand is Dual Max, and the material is also used in bags, apparel, sports balls and gloves.

Production is centered on the Suwon headquarters, with plants in Incheon, Osan, Pyeongtaek and Busan, and the synthetic leather is made on dry and wet laminator lines. Overseas, the company operates consolidated subsidiaries in China and Vietnam, combining offshore production with sales.

The synthetic leather is also supplied to many global companies as IT device accessory material, and the company has recently started supplying powder puff material for cosmetics.

Its major customers are footwear and sporting-goods makers, and management has pointed to demand swings in the sporting-goods material market and a slowdown in the global economy as reasons for lower sales.

Against large chemical peers such as LG Chem and Hanwha Solutions, which center on petrochemicals and battery materials, Dcksung is a small-cap company focused on synthetic leather processing. Its synthetic resin and materials segments are disclosed separately as part of the business structure.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 133.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 107.9 billion in 2023 (down 19.0% year on year), then rose to KRW 125.5 billion in 2024 (up 16.2%) and KRW 146.5 billion in 2025 (up 16.8%), so revenue has grown for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

Among the 2022-2025 years, 2025 was the largest and 2023 the smallest. Operating profit was KRW 5.25 billion in 2022, KRW 5.09 billion in 2023 (down 3.0%), KRW 8.91 billion in 2024 (up 75.1%) and KRW 6.24 billion in 2025 (down 30.0%), with 2024 the largest year.

The operating margin rose from 3.9% in 2022 to 7.1% in 2024 before easing to 4.3% in 2025, and the company cited higher fixed costs as the main reason for the 2025 operating profit decline.

Attributable net income went from KRW 2.52 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.63 billion in 2023 (up 124.0%) and KRW 6.8 billion in 2024 (up 20.7%), then fell to KRW 5.49 billion in 2025 (down 19.3%). In 2025, revenue grew while margins compressed.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 9.75 billion in 2022, KRW 12.5 billion in 2023 and KRW 8.02 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.08 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 50.1% in 2022, 47.9% in 2023 and 47.4% in 2024 to 56.7% in 2025, and at year-end 2025 total equity was KRW 97.4 billion (all attributable to shareholders) against total liabilities of KRW 55.2 billion.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 42 billion, operating profit of KRW 2.41 billion and attributable net income of KRW 2.86 billion turned into an operating loss of KRW 1.22 billion and an attributable net loss of KRW 514 million in Q4 2025, when revenue was KRW 30.6 billion (down 27.2% from the prior quarter).

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 34.3 billion (up 12.1% quarter on quarter), with operating profit of KRW 910 million and attributable net income of KRW 447 million, returning to profit; Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 32.8 billion, operating profit KRW 809 million and attributable net income KRW 820 million.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 29.3 billion (down 10.9% quarter on quarter and down 30.3% year on year), operating profit was KRW 128 million (down 94.7% year on year), and the attributable net loss was KRW 2.75 billion.

Q1 2026 was the peak quarter, followed by two consecutive quarters of decline through Q3, and the attributable net loss over the last four quarters totals KRW 2 billion. In our reading, the drop in operating profit to about KRW 100 million-level in Q3 suggests that fixed costs weighed on profit as sales fell.

Industry analysis

Dcksung's results are directly tied to footwear and sporting-goods demand, so order flow from its customers quickly shows up in its revenue. Management attributed the decline in first-half 2026 sales to demand swings in the sporting-goods material market and a slowdown in the global economy.

Over the same period, higher cost pressure was cited as weakening profitability. First-half 2026 consolidated revenue fell 9.2% year on year, operating profit fell 65.9% and net income fell 59.6%, according to a company results comment dated September 3, 2026.

In cycle terms, sales have been contracting since the peak in Q1 2026. Competition in synthetic leather combines price and cost competition with differentiation through functional materials, so keeping supply relationships with shoe brand customers matters.

The newer demand areas, IT device accessories and cosmetic powder puff material, are described as complements to the shoe-centered structure.

Large chemical companies often follow petrochemical and battery material cycles, whereas Dcksung depends on consumer demand for shoes and bags, so its share price may move differently from the sector index. Its overseas production base in China and Vietnam can serve as a cost-response tool.

The drop in Q3 2026 operating profit to KRW 128 million suggests that its ability to absorb fixed costs in a demand slowdown has weakened.
