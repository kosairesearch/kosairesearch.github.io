Report overview

From LED to Semiconductor Equipment: Earnings Recovery Meets Funding Test

Hmnex's revenue and operating profit rose sharply after it consolidated its subsidiary SMI in 2025, but the swing to negative operating cash flow and the funding of the Yongin expansion remain the next points to watch.

  1. 1

    Revenue reached KRW 31.7 billion in 2025, up 109.1% from the prior year, and operating profit reached KRW 2.37 billion, up 644.1%. The company cited the consolidation of its subsidiary and higher profitability in its vehicle LED business as the main drivers.

  2. 2

    The operating margin rose from 2.1% in 2024 to 7.5% in 2025. Net income of KRW 10.9 billion, well above operating profit, reflects a large contribution from non-operating items such as subsidiary and associate results and interest income.

  3. 3

    Revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 14.8 billion, up 10.6% from the prior quarter, and operating profit was KRW 1.92 billion, up 38.7%, extending the two-quarter gains that began after the first quarter of 2026.

  4. 4

    Quarterly net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung widely, falling 53.5% to KRW 1.25 billion in the second quarter of 2026 before rising 218.1% to KRW 3.99 billion in the third quarter.

  5. 5

    The debt ratio rose to 20.7% at the end of 2025 from 0.9% at the end of 2024. The funding plan centers on KRW 14.8 billion for the Yongin site and future capex, which is structured to be raised through a capital increase.

02

Business structure

Hmnex began as a maker of vehicle LED packages. In May 2025 it acquired a 91.15% stake in SMI, a maker of liquid delivery systems used in thin-film deposition, for KRW 26.5 billion, expanding into semiconductor equipment.

SMI says it localized the optical temperature sensor used in semiconductor etch processes for the first time in Korea, and the company's history records the start of ozone-water supply equipment sales in March 2025.

The 2025 annual report still lists LED production and sales as the main business, so the reported business mix and the revenue structure are still shifting. Press reports said all 36 headquarters employees as of end-2025 belong to the LED division.

By inventory at end-2025, the LED division held about KRW 2.9 billion and the semiconductor equipment division about KRW 2.8 billion; these are inventory figures, not revenue shares.

The company said the optical temperature sensor passed qualification at Micron's Singapore plant on January 8, 2026, after about five months of wafer-yield testing that began in August 2025. The company says its durability is more than four times longer than the one-to-1.5-month replacement cycle of existing products.

The market has been dominated by overseas players such as US-based Luxtron, and potential customers named include SK hynix, Samsung Electronics, TSMC and Micron's US and Taiwan fabs. According to the company, revenue growth reflects both the consolidation of SMI and higher sales in its vehicle LED business.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 13.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 14.5 billion in 2023, up 8.2%, and to KRW 15.2 billion in 2024, up 4.4%, and the company recorded three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025. Revenue reached KRW 31.7 billion in 2025, up 109.1%, the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

The company attributed the increase to higher vehicle LED sales and profitability, the consolidation of subsidiary investment results, equity-method gains from associates, and higher interest income from other receivables.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 1.07 billion in 2022 to a profit of KRW 14.42 million in 2023, then KRW 318 million in 2024, up 2,106.5%, and KRW 2.37 billion in 2025, up 644.1%, with growth in both 2024 and 2025.

The operating margin went from -8.0% in 2022 to 0.1% in 2023, 2.1% in 2024 and 7.5% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.18 billion in 2022, KRW 8.2 billion in 2023, KRW 5.51 billion in 2024, down 32.8%, and KRW 11 billion in 2025, up 98.8%.

In 2023, operating profit was only KRW 14.42 million while controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 8.2 billion, which shows how much non-operating income mattered that year.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 11.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 11.8 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 11.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 13.4 billion in the second quarter and KRW 14.8 billion in the third quarter.

Operating profit followed a different path, with a dip to KRW 902 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 868 million in the first quarter of 2026 before reaching KRW 1.92 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved more sharply, at KRW 2.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 1.25 billion in the second quarter and KRW 3.99 billion in the third quarter, and the last four quarters total KRW 11.8 billion.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 249 million in 2022 to KRW 1.91 billion in 2024 before turning to -KRW 517 million in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 0.9% in 2024 to 20.7% in 2025, and the annual report states that funds for the SMI acquisition came from cash on hand and borrowings.

05

Industry analysis

In the semiconductor cycle, the growth of the memory market on AI and big-data demand is the main theme. WiseReport's company monitor reads that capital spending on chips is expanding mainly in the United States and China, and that process equipment matters more as miniaturization and 3D stacking advance.

Press reports described the HBM capacity expansion at chipmakers as a favorable backdrop for the subsidiary's results. However, the optical temperature sensor is a component that must pass process qualification at large chipmakers, so its customer qualification cycle differs from the order cycle for complete equipment.

Listed peers in semiconductor equipment and materials include HANMI Semiconductor, EO Technics, Wonik IPS, Jusung Engineering and PSK, but Hmnex sits in in-process sensors and components rather than complete tools. In competition, the overseas monopoly structure is the largest variable.

Whether localization turns into meaningful revenue depends on qualification results at customers beyond Micron. Vehicle LEDs remain the legacy business, so auto-parts demand and LED pricing still form one pillar of earnings. How memory capex translates into demand for the equipment division is also a point to confirm.

The industry backdrop shows many positive signals, but the pace of customer wins at the company itself appears to be the more decisive variable.

06

Outlook

Several facts disclosed by the company and its subsidiary have dated milestones. First, the optical temperature sensor passed qualification at Micron's Singapore plant, as announced by the company on January 8, 2026. The company said it plans to expand supply in stages.

Second, press reports said additional qualification tests at Micron's Japan site and at China's CXMT are under way. Third, SMI passed the move-in review for remaining plots in the Yongin Semiconductor Cluster industrial complex on December 17, 2025, and is in the preparation stage for the facility.

Fourth, the KRW 14.8 billion payment for the Yongin site and future capex are structured to be funded through a capital increase, so the specifics of the funding plan remain a parallel task. Fifth, the first fab at SK hynix, a potential customer, has a target start in 2027.

Sixth, the company said it plans to extend its customized optical sensor systems to other industries that require high-temperature and high-risk environments.

The next regular checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, where the consolidated revenue mix, operating cash flow and debt ratio will show how the business has been organized. The report also shows whether the stated main business shifts from LED toward semiconductor equipment.

08

Bull factors

Earnings base shift after consolidation

The operating margin rose from 2.1% in 2024 to 7.5% in 2025, and operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 1.92 billion, up 38.7% from the prior quarter. Operating profit has grown for two consecutive quarters since the first quarter of 2026.

This trend is cited as evidence of a changed earnings base, and the annual report will show whether it holds on a full-year basis.

Customer qualification progress

The company said the optical temperature sensor passed qualification at Micron's Singapore plant on January 8, 2026. It presents durability more than four times longer than the one-to-1.5-month replacement cycle of existing products as a strength. Potential customers include SK hynix, Samsung Electronics and TSMC.

Expanding memory capital spending

WiseReport's company monitor cites expanding chip capex centered on the United States and China, and the rising importance of process equipment, as growth momentum for the semiconductor equipment division.

This is the monitor's interpretation, and order backlog or revenue contribution must be confirmed through separate disclosures.

09

Bear factors

Earnings quality and non-operating weight

Net income of KRW 10.9 billion in 2025 far exceeded operating profit of KRW 2.37 billion, and the company cited consolidated subsidiary results, equity-method gains and interest income as the causes. Operating cash flow turned to -KRW 517 million in 2025 from KRW 1.91 billion in 2024.

Whether core operations generate the cash to back up reported earnings is the central point of the bearish case.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 2.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 1.25 billion in the second and KRW 3.99 billion in the third. Operating profit rose more steadily over the same period, which suggests non-operating items drove much of that swing. The stability of quarterly earnings still needs more time to confirm.

Gap between business mix and market perception

The 2025 annual report lists LED production and sales as the main business, and press reports said all 36 headquarters employees belong to the LED division. After a surge on the CCUS theme in March 2026, the Korea Exchange designated the stock as an investment warning issue on March 11. The gap between the reported business shift and the share price movement is a bearish factor.

10

Risk factors

Customer qualification and concentration

Sales growth in the optical temperature sensor depends on qualification results at customers beyond Micron. In a market long dominated by overseas firms, revenue follows only after customer approval is completed. If dependence on a few customers rises, changes in orders could pass straight through to earnings.

Funding and balance sheet

The debt ratio climbed sharply within a year, increasing the burden on the balance sheet. Because the Yongin site payment and expansion costs are structured to be raised through a capital increase, a larger share count could dilute existing holders. The swing of operating cash flow to -KRW 517 million in 2025 adds to funding pressure.

Share price volatility and theme dependence

In March 2026 the stock jumped 23.72% in a single day on the CCUS theme, and the Korea Exchange designated it an investment warning issue. Press reports noted that the 2025 annual report contains no description of CCUS business. Theme-driven price moves may diverge from the company's fundamentals.

12

Overall view

Hmnex is in the process of shifting from a vehicle LED maker to a company that holds a semiconductor equipment subsidiary. Revenue reached KRW 31.7 billion in 2025 and operating profit reached KRW 2.37 billion, both up sharply from the prior year, and the operating margin rose from 2.1% in 2024 to 7.5% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue and operating profit grew for two consecutive quarters in 2026, but net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung widely from quarter to quarter.

On the bullish side, the Micron qualification of the optical temperature sensor and the expansion of memory capex are cited as supporting points.

On the bearish side, the large gap between net income and operating profit, the turn to -KRW 517 million in operating cash flow in 2025, and the 20.7% debt ratio with a capital-increase-based funding plan are the central issues.

The 2025 annual report still lists LED as the main business, so the pace of the business shift and the gap with disclosures also remain to be confirmed. The next checkpoints are how operating cash flow and funding are presented in the 2026 annual report, and the results of qualification tests at other sensor customers.
