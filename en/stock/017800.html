Hyundai Elevator: Profit Recovery Meets Quarterly Swings

Hyundai Elevator's operating margin rose to 8.5% in 2025, but in Q3 2026 operating profit fell sharply from the prior quarter and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, raising quarterly volatility.

  1. 1

    Sales peaked at KRW 2.89 trillion in 2024, the largest year in 2022–2025, before falling 14.5% year on year to KRW 2.47 trillion in 2025.

  2. 2

    Operating margin climbed from 2.0% in 2022 to 8.5% in 2025, rising even in the year sales declined.

  3. 3

    Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 262 billion in 2025, up 43.0% year on year, reversing the 42.6% decline of 2024.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Hyundai Elevator was founded in 1984 and is headquartered in Chungju, North Chungcheong Province, operating in the general machinery and equipment manufacturing industry. Its business is divided into new installation, maintenance, remodeling, and leasing.

In the first three quarters of 2025 on a cumulative basis, revenue mix was 56.6% new installation, 24.4% maintenance, 18.0% remodeling, and 1.0% leasing. New installation covers supplying and installing elevators in newly built buildings, so it depends directly on construction activity and order flow.

Remodeling and maintenance serve elevators already in place, and demand builds as the stock of older units grows. Over the same first three quarters of 2025, maintenance booked orders of KRW 345.7 billion and revenue of KRW 298.9 billion, and it was the only segment that grew in scale.

Customers are centered on construction firms running redevelopment and new-build sites, and on building operators.

In June 2023, the company won 314 units of elevators and escalators, worth KRW 43.4 billion, for the Dunchon Jugong redevelopment in Seoul, which it described as the largest in its history by both unit count and order value.

The company said it commercialized the Innoblock high-rise modular elevator, with more than 90% of key components pre-assembled in the factory, as the first of its kind in the world. It says Innoblock cuts installation time by up to 80%, and it is targeting overseas high-rise and large projects with the product.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 2.13 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.6 trillion in 2023, up 22.2% year on year, and to KRW 2.89 trillion in 2024, up 10.9%. It then fell to KRW 2.47 trillion in 2025, down 14.5%.

Operating profit grew from KRW 43 billion in 2022 to KRW 82.6 billion in 2023, up 92.1%, and to KRW 225.7 billion in 2024, up 173.2%. It eased 7.3% to KRW 209.2 billion in 2025. Operating margin moved from 2.0% in 2022 to 3.2% in 2023, 7.8% in 2024, and 8.5% in 2025, so margin rose in 2025 even as sales declined.

Related reports attributed the 2025 sales decline to weak domestic construction activity. In the first nine months of 2025, new installation revenue fell from KRW 852.9 billion to KRW 692.7 billion, and remodeling revenue fell from KRW 343.7 billion to KRW 220.1 billion.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 78.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 318.9 billion in 2023, up 307.0%. It then fell 42.6% to KRW 183.2 billion in 2024 and recovered 43.0% to KRW 262 billion in 2025.

In 2023, that profit figure ran well ahead of operating profit, which points to a large non-operating contribution. The specific items are identified in that year's annual report notes.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 78.2 billion in 2022, KRW 309.9 billion in 2023, KRW 193.9 billion in 2024, and KRW 269.3 billion in 2025, and the gap from controlling-shareholder profit reflects non-controlling interests.

Quarterly sales went from KRW 600.8 billion in Q3 2025 and KRW 634.7 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 545.8 billion in Q1 2026, then jumped to KRW 692.8 billion in Q2 2026.

Q2 2026 operating profit rose 108.7% from the prior quarter to KRW 84.5 billion, and both sales and operating profit were the highest of the last five quarters.

In Q3 2026, sales were KRW 604.2 billion, down 12.8% quarter on quarter and up 0.6% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 33.9 billion, down 59.9% quarter on quarter and 33.6% year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 147.1 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 30.5 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 28 billion in Q1 2026, and KRW 161.9 billion in Q2 2026, then turned into a net loss of KRW 9.68 billion in Q3 2026.

Q3 2026 posted operating profit but a loss attributable to controlling shareholders, so the gap between operating and bottom-line results is the defining feature of that quarter.

Cumulative operating profit through Q3 2026 in the quarterly report filed on November 16, 2026 was KRW 158.9 billion, up 2.5% year on year, while cumulative net income fell 23.3%.

05

Industry analysis

Elevator revenue is sensitive to domestic construction activity. Related reports cited weak domestic construction as the main reason for the 2025 sales decline, with lower demand for new installation and remodeling.

Over the same period, only maintenance grew in scale, and reports said the company is protecting earnings by focusing on replacing aging elevators and on maintenance. At the end of 2025, brokerage commentary pointed to an earnings recovery in 2026 and continued high dividends.

Cumulative 2026 revenue through the first three quarters was up 0.6% year on year and operating profit up 2.5%, so the top line has been roughly flat. In cycle terms, new installation is in a downturn, while replacement of aging equipment and maintenance provide a floor.

In high-rise, super-tall, and industrial facilities, modular products that shorten on-site installation are being presented as a differentiator. The company says Innoblock cuts installation time by up to 80%.

The elevator business is tied to construction cycles and aging-stock demand, so its demand structure differs from other listed companies in the machinery and equipment sector. Whether overseas super-tall project orders continue is the key test for moving beyond a domestic-centered model.

Outlook

Hyundai Elevator has secured orders for 51 Innoblock units, which are scheduled to be produced and delivered in sequence through the end of 2027. Reports expect this volume to support new installation revenue as it is delivered.

The company plans to build on domestic reference projects to widen its order base for overseas high-rise and large projects. On shareholder returns, the company introduced quarterly dividends for the first time at its regular shareholder meeting in March 2025 after amending its articles of incorporation.

Reports from the same period said a special dividend was also paid, expanding shareholder returns considerably. The next full-year disclosed results will come with the 2026 annual report, and until then quarterly results are confirmed only through the earnings announcements and quarterly reports.

Whether Q4 2026 operating margin recovers from the Q3 2026 level is the next checkpoint. The full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report, compared with 8.5% in 2025, will set the reference for the profitability trend.

The debt ratio stood at 157.8% at the end of 2025, below 166.2% in 2024, and whether capital strengthening and dividend capacity move together is the medium-term point to watch.

08

Bull factors

Improved profitability

Operating margin rose from 2.0% in 2022 to 8.5% in 2025. Operating profit stayed far above the 2022 level of KRW 43 billion, at KRW 225.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 209.2 billion in 2025. Cumulative operating profit through Q3 2026 was KRW 158.9 billion, up 2.5% year on year, showing core earnings are holding up.

A maintenance-based earnings floor

In the first three quarters of 2025, maintenance orders were KRW 345.7 billion and revenue was KRW 298.9 billion, up 7.1% and 6.3% year on year. Reports said demand for replacement and upkeep builds as the stock of older elevators grows, making the segment less exposed to construction cycles. Maintenance accounted for 24.4% of revenue over the same period.

Modular orders and shareholder returns

Innoblock orders for 51 units will be delivered in sequence through the end of 2027, and the company plans to use them as a base for entry into overseas high-rise markets. At its March 2025 regular shareholder meeting, the company introduced quarterly dividends for the first time and also paid a special dividend. If paired with an earnings recovery, the read on dividend capacity could change.

Bear factors

Slowing new installation and remodeling demand

Sales in 2025 were KRW 2.47 trillion, down 14.5% year on year. In the first three quarters of 2025, new installation revenue fell from KRW 852.9 billion to KRW 692.7 billion, and remodeling revenue fell from KRW 343.7 billion to KRW 220.1 billion.

Reports attributed both declines to weak domestic construction, and if new installation does not recover, top-line growth may stay limited.

Swing to a loss in Q3 2026

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 33.9 billion, down 59.9% quarter on quarter and 33.6% year on year. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned into a net loss of KRW 9.68 billion.

Cumulative net income through the first three quarters of 2026 fell 23.3% from a year earlier, so operating gains have not carried through to the bottom line.

Earnings volatility and balance-sheet load

In 2023, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 318.9 billion far exceeded operating profit of KRW 82.6 billion, and in 2025 it was also above operating profit of KRW 209.2 billion. That indicates bottom-line results can depend heavily on non-operating items. Total liabilities were KRW 2.28 trillion at the end of 2025, larger than total equity of KRW 1.44 trillion.

Risk factors

Construction cycle and order flow

New installation and remodeling demand is tied to domestic construction, so a slowdown in redevelopment and new-build orders would reduce sales. The 14.5% sales decline in 2025 is an example. The timing of large-site orders can swing quarterly revenue significantly.

Quarterly earnings swings

Q2 2026 sales and operating profit jumped before falling sharply in Q3, and Q3 produced a loss attributable to controlling shareholders. Quarterly results can vary widely with non-operating items and non-controlling interests. The last four quarters total KRW 210.7 billion, so quarter-to-quarter swings can shape the annual picture.

Modular order execution and financial capacity

The 51 Innoblock units are scheduled for sequential production and delivery through the end of 2027, so whether manufacturing and installation proceed on plan is the key test. The debt ratio was a high 157.8% at the end of 2025, and how financial capacity is allocated if dividend expansion continues will matter.

Overall view

Based on reported results, Hyundai Elevator's operating profit grew from KRW 43 billion in 2022 to KRW 209.2 billion in 2025, and operating margin rose to 8.5%.

Sales peaked at KRW 2.89 trillion in 2024 before falling 14.5% in 2025, and because new installation and remodeling are tied to construction activity, the pace of top-line recovery is the variable to watch.

Q2 2026 sales and operating profit were the highest of the last five quarters, but in Q3 operating profit fell to KRW 33.9 billion and the company posted a net loss of KRW 9.68 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

Operating profit and a bottom-line loss appearing in the same quarter has amplified quarterly swings. Growth in maintenance revenue, 51 Innoblock orders, and the introduction of quarterly dividends are cited as supporting factors, while slowing new installation and quarterly earnings swings are the counterpoints.

With these forces coexisting at comparable weight, the direction is not yet clear. The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 results in the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report, along with Innoblock delivery progress.
