Revenue Growth Amid Three Years of Operating Losses

Revenue rose to KRW 66.6 billion in 2025 after two straight years of growth, but operating losses have continued for three consecutive years since 2023, leaving the gap between expansion and profitability as the central issue.

02

Business structure

DigiCap was founded in April 2000 as a developer and supplier of system software and listed on KOSDAQ in September 2018. Its core product is the CAS conditional access system that limits viewing to paying pay-TV subscribers, alongside an N-screen service that supports content use across connected platforms and devices.

The company has also developed an ATSC 3.0 terrestrial UHD dynamic targeted advertising platform, a connected-car app security solution, and a blockchain-based security solution as new businesses.

Based on second-quarter 2026 revenue, the segment mix is content software 44%, digital twin software 23%, metaverse software 17%, video security and security software 8%, and other solutions 4% (per the corporate information aggregator Butler).

Beyond broadcast reception, the company has expanded its customer base to more than 3,000 clients in games, film and animation, OTT content production, and architecture and design, according to FnGuide.

Management attributed revenue growth in the first nine months of 2025 to a wider base of digital content creators and studios, while it also said the difficulty of securing high-performance GPUs for AI training and the cost of acquiring data limited profitability.

Overseas, a consortium including DigiCap reported in January 2026 an exclusive supply contract with US network operator C5G worth about KRW 24 billion through 2028.

Compared with large platform and IT services companies in the same sector such as NAVER and Samsung SDS, the scale and customer structure differ widely, and DigiCap's business rests on a narrow base of broadcast reception and UHD transmission solutions.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell 4.5% from KRW 25.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 24.6 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 32 billion in 2024 (up 30.0%) and KRW 66.6 billion in 2025 (up 108.1%). Revenue grew for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025, 2025 was the largest year of the 2022–2025 period, and 2023 was the smallest.

Operating income moved from KRW 682 million in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 665 million in 2023, then to an operating loss of KRW 1.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.53 billion in 2025. The company has posted operating losses for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

The operating margin was 2.6% in 2022, -2.7% in 2023, -5.0% in 2024, and -3.8% in 2025, so the loss rate narrowed slightly in 2025 even as revenue scale grew sharply over two years without reaching breakeven.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 957 million in 2022, a loss of KRW 3.29 billion in 2023, KRW 1.15 billion in 2024 (a return to profit), and a loss of KRW 529 million in 2025.

The 2024 return to profit came in a year of operating losses, so it should be read separately from any improvement in operating profitability.

The company explained the wider 2025 loss as reflecting the acquisition and loss of control of subsidiaries and a merger, along with higher costs for new businesses that reduced operating income, as reported by Edaily on January 26, 2026.

On a quarterly basis, revenue moved from KRW 17.2 billion and operating income of KRW 239 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 15.4 billion and operating income of KRW 85.32 million in the fourth quarter, down 10.6% and 64.3% quarter on quarter.

The first quarter of 2026 revenue of KRW 14.9 billion (down 3.2%) came with an operating loss of KRW 1.09 billion, and the second quarter revenue of KRW 17.6 billion (up 18.0%) came with a wider operating loss of KRW 1.23 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 14.2 billion was down 19.0% quarter on quarter and down 17.3% year on year, with an operating loss of KRW 242 million that narrowed from the second quarter but reversed a year-earlier operating profit.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, totaled KRW 2.05 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 4.06 billion in 2022, KRW 4.77 billion in 2023, and KRW 1.23 billion in 2024, then turned negative at -KRW 1.09 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The company's core market is pay-TV reception control and broadcast transmission technology, with ATSC 3.0-based UHD solutions for the next-generation terrestrial broadcasting standard at the center.

According to a 2020 report, DigiCap supplied ATSC 3.0 next-generation broadcasting solutions to Sinclair Broadcast Group in October 2019 and entered the United States for the first time in May 2020.

Demand for broadcast standard transitions tends to concentrate around equipment and solution replacement cycles, so a slower transition could also delay demand.

In Korea, the Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT) started in January 2026 an export support program for small and mid-sized broadcast and media companies seeking overseas supply chains, and DigiCap was cited as a result of that program.

On the content side, demand from digital content creators and studios has broadened, and management said the company's revenue growth moved in line with that trend.

However, the cost of AI training data and high-performance GPUs limits profitability, so whether scale growth translates into earnings improvement is the key issue for reading the sector.

Compared with large platform and cloud companies in the same sector such as NAVER, Kakao, and Samsung SDS, DigiCap differs in business scale and customer mix and is building its business within the narrow field of broadcast reception and UHD solutions.

The position of the industry cycle depends on the broadcast transition investment timeline and advertising and content production demand, so it is more useful to track upstream investment flows than to read it from a single figure.

Outlook

Three factors currently frame the outlook: export contracts, share buybacks, and expansion of new businesses.

First, the exclusive supply contract with C5G, worth about KRW 24 billion through 2028, was reported in January 2026, and the scale and timing of its execution should be confirmed in subsequent quarterly reports.

Second, on June 10, 2026, the company decided to enter a KRW 3 billion share repurchase trust agreement, which it said was aimed at stabilizing the share price and enhancing shareholder value, according to Investing.com.

Third, at the annual general meeting on March 24, 2026, shareholders approved the 2025 standalone financial statements and an amendment to the articles of incorporation and re-elected one outside director.

Because management attributed the wider 2025 loss to higher costs for new businesses, whether those costs persist in 2026 is the first checkpoint for any recovery in profitability.

The third-quarter 2026 operating loss of KRW 242 million was smaller than the KRW 1.23 billion loss in the second quarter, but losses have continued for three straight quarters, so the timing of a return to operating breakeven is not yet visible.

A slot at the NAB Show that had been scheduled for April 2026 was reported as offered to companies selected under the MSIT export support program, and whether trade show results convert into contract execution is another point to watch. No quantitative guidance that could serve as a baseline has been disclosed.

The 2026 annual report, the next regular filing, will show the full-year operating margin and any change in new-business costs.

Risk factors

New business costs and earnings volatility

Management attributed the 2025 decline in operating income to higher costs for new businesses. Until these investments generate revenue, quarterly earnings volatility could persist. Operating losses widened in the second quarter of 2026 even as revenue rose, showing that the cost structure offset revenue growth.

Execution of overseas contracts

The C5G exclusive supply contract was reported as running through 2028, but the revenue recognition scale and timing must be confirmed through quarterly reports. Overseas supply can be affected by regulation, exchange rates, and local operating conditions. If the contract is executed later than expected, the timing of this growth driver would also shift.

Changes in group structure and business structure

The company said its 2025 results reflected the acquisition and loss of control of subsidiaries and a merger. When the business structure and consolidation scope change, year-on-year comparisons become harder and the interpretation of profit attributable to controlling shareholders becomes more complex. Because the market capitalization is relatively small, share price volatility also remains a risk factor.

Overall view

DigiCap's 2025 revenue of KRW 66.6 billion was up 108.1% from the prior year, the largest year of the 2022–2025 period, yet an operating loss of KRW 2.53 billion has extended a three-year run of losses starting in 2023.

Operating losses have continued for three straight quarters from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026, and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 2.05 billion.

Bullish factors include revenue growth, a customer base expanded to more than 3,000 clients, an overseas supply contract worth about KRW 24 billion through 2028, and a KRW 3 billion share repurchase decision.

Bearish factors include the limited improvement in operating margin, the shift of operating cash flow to negative, and structural costs from new businesses and the cost of GPUs and data.

The price-to-book ratio sits below 1x and equity of KRW 56.5 billion indicates reasonable financial capacity, but whether the earnings structure returns to profitability remains the central unresolved question.

The next checkpoint is the operating margin and new-business cost trend in the 2026 annual report, followed by whether losses persist in the first-quarter 2027 report. The scale of revenue recognized from the overseas contract should also be confirmed through quarterly filings.
