Report overview

Revenue Surges, Profit Turn Still Pending

Seojin System returned to operating profit in Q3 2026 on ESS and semiconductor equipment growth, but attributable net losses persist, so the durability of earnings is the next checkpoint.

  1. 1

    Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 529.6 billion and operating profit was KRW 55.9 billion, turning to operating profit from a year-earlier loss.

  2. 2

    Attributable net loss in Q3 2026 was KRW 203 billion, and attributable losses have run for three straight quarters since Q1 2026. The combined attributable net loss over the last four quarters is KRW 238 billion.

  3. 3

    Semiconductor equipment revenue reached KRW 308.6 billion in the first half of 2026, already above the 2025 full-year semiconductor revenue of KRW 291.3 billion, according to a Korean business daily.

  4. 4

    Q2 2026 results were hit by delayed ESS finished-product revenue, tied to automation setup delays at the US plant and grid connection delays at a customer site. The Q3 turn to operating profit was supported by recognition of deferred shipments.

  5. 5

    On September 30, 2026 the company announced it would halt non-core production lines at its Vietnam unit and redeploy equipment to core ESS and semiconductor lines without separate capacity investment.

02

Business structure

Seojin System emphasizes a vertically integrated structure that covers metal processing through final assembly as its core competitive edge.

Eugene Investment & Securities, after a March 2026 visit to the Vietnam plant, described in-house ingot production, hot-rolled plate fabrication, extrusion and casting, PCB, SMT and cable processes, and final assembly as points that differentiate it from conventional EMS providers.

The business is divided into ESS, semiconductors, automotive, heavy industry, telecommunications and PCB. Semiconductor equipment is led by its subsidiary Texon, and according to a Korean business daily, semiconductor revenue in Q2 2026 rose 73.6% quarter on quarter to KRW 195.8 billion.

ESS supply centers on customer Fluence Energy, and the same report put Fluence's order backlog at about USD 5.6 billion as of Q1 2026. Expansion of Bloom Energy box contract manufacturing and SOFC component cooperation is also under way.

On September 30, 2026 the company said it would halt production of home appliances, machine tools, heavy industry and wheel products at its Vietnam unit; those segments generated about KRW 94.2 billion in 2025, roughly 9% of consolidated revenue.

Freed equipment and staff will move to ESS, semiconductor equipment, SOFC and data center lines. On the competitive side, analysts argue that the push to exclude China-linked supply chains raises the profile of Seojin's non-Chinese production capacity.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results swung widely in both sales and profit. Revenue fell from KRW 787.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 778.7 billion in 2023, a decline of 1.1%, then rose to KRW 1.21 trillion in 2024, up 55.9%, before dropping to KRW 1.07 trillion in 2025, down 12.1%.

Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest. Operating profit went from KRW 44.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 49 billion in 2023, up 10.4%, and KRW 108.7 billion in 2024, up 121.8%, then fell to KRW 1.15 billion in 2025, down 98.9%.

The operating margin moved from 5.6% in 2022 to 6.3% in 2023 and 9.0% in 2024, then slipped to 0.1% in 2025. Attributable net income turned to a loss of KRW 22.6 billion in 2023, returned to profit of KRW 84.3 billion in 2024, and swung back to an attributable net loss of KRW 101.2 billion in 2025.

Eugene Investment & Securities data show ESS revenue falling from KRW 636.0 billion in 2024 to KRW 397.8 billion in 2025, which points to ESS as the main driver of the 2025 profit collapse.

Quarterly figures moved from a Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 215.5 billion with an operating loss of KRW 37.3 billion to a Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 311 billion with operating profit of KRW 23.5 billion. Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 280.2 billion, down 9.9% quarter on quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 33 billion.

Q2 revenue rose 46.4% to KRW 410.2 billion while the operating loss narrowed to KRW 19.4 billion. Q3 revenue was KRW 529.6 billion and operating profit KRW 55.9 billion. That Q3 revenue was below the KRW 559 billion and operating profit of KRW 88 billion that iM Securities projected in an August 2026 report.

Operating profit was positive, but the attributable net loss of KRW 203 billion was far larger, so non-operating items and income tax effects deserve attention alongside the operating trend.

05

Industry analysis

Seojin System's industry backdrop depends on demand in two areas, ESS and semiconductor equipment. ESS demand is supported by the expansion of renewable energy and rising data center power needs.

Texas's ERCOT grid is under heavy strain from heat waves and the growth of AI data centers, and delayed customer installations weighed on Q2 2026 results.

Eugene Investment & Securities argued that chips and power are the core of North American AI infrastructure and that de-Sinicization of supply chains is becoming a necessary option, a view under which Seojin could benefit in both chips and power.

ESS projects, however, recognize revenue on uneven schedules, so quarterly swings are structurally large and a single quarter is a weak guide to trend.

In semiconductors, growth drivers cited include higher utilization at the Vietnam plant and wider lineup of high-value process modules, and Eugene said it confirmed the rising utilization during its plant visit.

Against conventional EMS peers, Seojin's in-house processing from metal work to assembly is seen as an advantage, while heavy dependence on a few customers and specific projects is a weakness critics point to.

Whether non-Chinese production bases gain value will depend largely on the pace of North American infrastructure spending.

06

Outlook

In its September 30, 2026 announcement, the company said the business restructuring and the start of its US plant would combine to make revenue growth and margin improvement clearer in 2027.

In an August 2026 report, iM Securities pointed to an ESS order backlog of roughly KRW 1 trillion and more than KRW 500 billion in confirmed semiconductor equipment orders, and projected 2026 revenue of KRW 1.817 trillion and operating profit of KRW 132 billion.

Measured against the operating results disclosed through Q3, that projection would require a meaningful operating profit in Q4. Because operating profit alternated between losses and profits from Q1 to Q3 2026, reaching the full-year figure will depend on Q4 results and how fast deferred ESS shipments are recognized.

A new Vietnam plant, including a semiconductor-dedicated cleanroom, is scheduled for completion in the second half of 2026, which would add capacity for global customer orders. The Fluence order backlog was reported at about USD 5.6 billion as of Q1 2026, which is cited as support for medium-term revenue visibility.

SOFC component supply and contract manufacturing with Bloom Energy are cited as expansion lanes for higher-value products. The 2026 annual report, the next scheduled periodic filing, will confirm Q4 and full-year results, and the operating margin in that filing is the key test of whether profitability holds. The earnings effect of halting non-core production and redeploying equipment also needs to be tracked.

08

Bull factors

Rapid growth in semiconductor equipment

Semiconductor revenue in the first half of 2026 reached KRW 308.6 billion, already above the 2025 full-year figure of KRW 291.3 billion, according to a Korean business daily. iM Securities said the company holds more than KRW 500 billion in confirmed semiconductor equipment orders. Utilization at the Vietnam plant and the expansion of higher-value modules are continuing.

ESS backlog and Q3 turn to profit

The Fluence order backlog was reported at about USD 5.6 billion as of Q1 2026. Deferred ESS shipments from Q2 were reflected in Q3, and operating profit turned positive at KRW 55.9 billion. iM Securities cited an ESS order backlog of roughly KRW 1 trillion as a basis for its 2026 full-year projection.

Portfolio reshaping and capacity plan

On September 30, 2026 the company announced the halt of non-core production at its Vietnam unit. Those segments generated about KRW 94.2 billion in 2025, roughly 9% of consolidated revenue.

The company plans to expand ESS and semiconductor capacity without separate capacity investment by moving equipment and staff to core lines.

09

Bear factors

Persistent attributable net losses

Attributable losses have run for three straight quarters from Q1 to Q3 2026. Q3 2026 showed operating profit of KRW 55.9 billion, yet the attributable net loss was KRW 203 billion, which shows profit not yet reaching shareholders.

Uncertain ESS revenue timing

In Q2 2026 delayed automation setup at the US plant pushed finished-product revenue into later periods while semi-finished production costs were booked first, leading to an operating loss of KRW 19.4 billion. Overloaded Texas grid conditions also delayed customer installations. ESS revenue fell from KRW 636.0 billion in 2024 to KRW 397.8 billion in 2025.

High leverage and cash flow

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 1.57 trillion and the debt ratio was 212.6%. Operating cash flow was negative at KRW -113.5 billion in 2024 and KRW -39.8 billion in 2025. Whether the Q3 operating profit converts into cash will need to be confirmed in the next periodic report.

10

Risk factors

Production and plant ramp-up risk

A delay in automation setup at the US plant previously pushed finished-product revenue into later quarters in Q2 2026. The Vietnam plant completion is scheduled for the second half, so slippage remains possible.

With production spread across several sites, the effect of equipment redeployment may show up in results with a lag.

Customer concentration and project dependence

ESS revenue depends heavily on supply to specific customers such as Fluence Energy. Quarterly revenue swings widen with project recognition timing. If customers' grid connections or project completions slip, the company's revenue recognition can slip with them.

Policy and trade environment

Demand timing for ESS and semiconductor equipment may shift with the pace and direction of North American infrastructure policy. The push to exclude China-linked supply chains favors non-Chinese capacity, but related policy changes are hard to predict. Overseas production also carries local tax and customs administration factors.

What to watch next

  1. During Q4 2026

    Completion and start of operation of the new Vietnam plant, including the semiconductor cleanroom. This is where the expansion of semiconductor capacity is confirmed in practice.

  2. Around March 31, 2027 (annual report filing deadline)

    The 2026 annual report will confirm Q4 and full-year results. Key items are the full-year operating margin, whether attributable losses continue, and the cause of the gap between operating profit and the net loss.

  3. Around May 15, 2027 (Q1 2027 quarterly report filing deadline)

    Q1 2027 results will show whether the Q3 2026 turn to operating profit holds on a quarterly basis and how much of the deferred ESS volume and US plant ramp-up is reflected.

  4. Second half 2026 onward (timing not fixed)

    The pace at which the Fluence ESS order backlog and Bloom Energy-related SOFC supply convert into quarterly revenue. This conversion speed is the checkpoint for medium-term revenue visibility.

12

Overall view

Seojin System is in a phase where ESS and semiconductor equipment revenue are growing together, and operating profit turned positive at KRW 55.9 billion in Q3 2026.

Semiconductor revenue in the first half already exceeded the 2025 full-year figure, and the Fluence backlog and confirmed orders are cited as support for medium-term visibility.

On the other side, the attributable net loss for Q3 was KRW 203 billion, and the combined attributable net loss over the last four quarters was KRW 238 billion, so profit has not yet reached shareholders. A debt ratio of 212.6% and negative operating cash flow in 2025 remain financial burdens.

Broker forecasts from August 2026 assume a full-year operating profit, but compared with the disclosed trend through Q3, the size of Q4 profit is the key variable. How the business restructuring, the new Vietnam plant and the US plant ramp-up feed into results is the next point to watch.

The 2026 annual report, the next periodic filing, will fix Q4 results, the full-year operating margin and the direction of attributable losses, and that outcome will be the test of whether the shift to profitability is sustained.
