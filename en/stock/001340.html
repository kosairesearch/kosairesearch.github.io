PKC: Semiconductor Materials Expand, Leverage Remains a Test

PKC's semiconductor specialty materials unit is growing its revenue share and lifted operating profit in 2026, while the 171.6% debt ratio at end-2025 and swings in quarterly earnings remain items to watch.

  1. 1

    Cumulative revenue for January–September 2026 reached KRW 215.2 billion, up 7.3% year on year, and cumulative operating profit rose 57.1% to KRW 13.7 billion (Asia Economy report, November 17, 2026).

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

PKC is a chemical company whose core is chlor-alkali (CA) products such as chlorine and caustic soda, and it also runs specialty gas for semiconductors and a food additives business.

In 2024 CA products accounted for the largest share of sales at 71.22%, followed by food additives at 15.15%, materials (specialty gases) at 10.64%, and other items at 2.99% (FnGuide, December 2024 basis).

The food additives line includes products such as sorbitol and maltitol, while the materials line covers high-purity hydrogen chloride and nitrous oxide used in semiconductor processes (JobKorea company profile product examples).

Domestic sales made up 89.4% of revenue that year and exports 10.6%, so the business is centered on the domestic market (FnGuide). The materials unit's share of revenue widened from 16.1% in Q3 2025 to 21.0% in Q3 2026 (Asia Economy report).

CA revenue moves with utilization rates in the petrochemical industry, and on a cumulative basis through Q3 2025 the company said lower industry utilization had held back sales in its CA division (FnGuide). The company is also replacing aging facilities to raise production efficiency and cut costs (FnGuide).

In materials, PKC competes with other specialty suppliers of high-purity gases, and its capacity depends on the Gunsan and Saemangeum plants. On a scale basis, the gap with large petrochemical companies in the same sector is wide.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual figures, revenue went from KRW 232.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 233.5 billion in 2023 (up 0.3% year on year), KRW 245.2 billion in 2024 (up 5.0%), and KRW 272.1 billion in 2025 (up 11.0%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year of 2022–2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 28.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.7 billion in 2023 (down 37.3%) and KRW 9.75 billion in 2024 (down 45.0%), then rose to KRW 12.2 billion in 2025 (up 24.6%).

The operating margin was 12.1% in 2022, 7.6% in 2023, 4.0% in 2024 and 4.5% in 2025, so margins had not returned to the 2022 level even in the years when sales grew.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 22.7 billion in 2022, KRW 7.73 billion in 2023 (down 66.0%) and KRW 3.6 billion in 2024 (down 53.4%), before recovering to KRW 10.1 billion in 2025 (up 180.5%).

The company attributed the 2025 improvement to higher CA and semiconductor materials sales (Digital Today AI disclosure team, based on the February 5, 2026 filing).

By quarter, operating profit rose from KRW 3.24 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 3.41 billion in Q4 (up 5.0% from the prior quarter), and reached KRW 5 billion in Q1 2026 (up 46.7%), yet net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 2.47 billion.

Financial data aggregators show a large non-operating loss in that quarter. Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 5.45 billion was the largest of the five quarters on the disclosed record, while Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.29 billion fell 39.6% from the prior quarter but rose 1.4% from a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 9.57 billion. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 42.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 25.2 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

PKC's end-market demand spans petrochemicals, semiconductors, secondary batteries and food. On the petrochemical side, lower industry utilization held back CA division sales on a cumulative basis through Q3 2025 (FnGuide).

In Q1 2025 the company cited higher electricity costs as a cost pressure and said it would pass the increase through to product prices (Sedaily report).

On the semiconductor side, Heungkuk Securities' May 26, 2026 report says specialty gas materials such as high-purity chlorine and hydrochloric acid are entering a full-scale upcycle (Thinkpool reprint).

How long that upcycle lasts cannot be judged from this material alone, and it will show up in the materials unit's sales trend in coming quarters.

In secondary batteries, quality certification for phosphorus trichloride and phosphorus oxychloride is under way at the Saemangeum No. 1 plant, so the end markets are widening from semiconductors toward battery materials (Thinkpool reprint).

In competition, large petrochemical companies in the same sector such as LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions and Lotte Chemical are far bigger, so PKC's scale gap with them is wide. The specialty gas business competes with other suppliers of high-purity gases.

Petrochemical earnings move with utilization, while specialty gas earnings depend on expansion speed and customer qualification. The materials unit's rise to 21.0% of Q3 2026 sales shows the mix shifting between the two businesses (Asia Economy report).

06

Outlook

In a May 2026 report, the company's CEO said the company aims for sales growth of more than 10% year on year and a continued quarterly improvement in operating profit (Thinkpool reprint). Cumulative sales for January–September 2026 rose 7.3% year on year, which is below the 10% target (Asia Economy report).

Cumulative operating profit rose 57.1% to KRW 13.7 billion, but on a quarterly basis operating profit in Q3 2026 fell from the prior quarter.

The Saemangeum No. 1 plant was completed in April 2026, and it is now going through quality certification for key battery materials such as phosphorus trichloride and phosphorus oxychloride.

The company has set a target of passing certification in the second half of 2026 and said it plans to start full-scale supply to battery materials customers after certification (Thinkpool reprint).

At the Gunsan plant, the company plans capacity expansion to add production of high-purity gases for semiconductors (same report). Heungkuk Securities' May 2026 report projects that earnings will grow in 2027 on the back of ongoing capacity expansion.

Bullish factors are higher specialty gas volumes and new materials qualifications, while bearish factors are quarterly earnings volatility and utilization after the investment. The timing of capacity completion and the certification outcome are items for upcoming disclosures.

08

Bull factors

Semiconductor specialty gas demand expands

Materials unit revenue in Q3 2026 was KRW 15.4 billion, up 37.2% year on year, and cumulative revenue for the same period reached KRW 39.6 billion, up 44.2% (Asia Economy report, November 17, 2026). The unit's share of total sales widened from 16.1% to 21.0%. The company said it plans to add high-purity semiconductor gas capacity at the Gunsan plant.

Operating profit recovery

Cumulative operating profit for January–September 2026 reached KRW 13.7 billion, up 57.1% year on year (Asia Economy report).

Operating profit of KRW 5.45 billion in Q2 2026 was the largest of the five quarters on the disclosed record, and the 7.1% operating margin in Q1 2026 was the highest for a first quarter since 2023 (Thinkpool reprint). The 2025 operating margin edged up to 4.5% from 4.0% in 2024.

New plant and certification timeline

The Saemangeum No. 1 plant was completed in April 2026 and is going through quality certification for key battery materials such as phosphorus trichloride and phosphorus oxychloride. The company is targeting certification in the second half of 2026 (Thinkpool reprint, May 2026). Heungkuk Securities' May 2026 report projected earnings growth in 2027 supported by the expansion.

Bear factors

Earnings scale remains small

Operating profit fell from KRW 28.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.75 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 12.2 billion in 2025, still below the 2022 level. The operating margin declined from 12.1% in 2022 to 4.5% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 10.1 billion in 2025 but remains below KRW 22.7 billion in 2022.

Rising financial leverage

The debt ratio climbed from 92.8% in 2022 to 171.6% in 2025, and total liabilities at end-2025 of KRW 383.2 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 223.3 billion. Net cash outflow from investing activities widened substantially in 2025 (JobKorea financial data).

Volatile quarterly earnings

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 2.47 billion in Q1 2026, and Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.29 billion fell 39.6% from the prior quarter.

Over the last four quarters, net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 9.57 billion, with large swings from quarter to quarter. Financial data aggregators show a large non-operating loss in Q1 2026.

Risk factors

Plant and certification timing

If battery materials certification slips past the second-half 2026 target, the start of new supply will be delayed. The utilization of the Saemangeum plant and the completion timing of the Gunsan expansion will also determine how fast earnings reflect new capacity. If capacity arrives ahead of demand, utilization could fall.

Costs and industry cycle

Lower petrochemical industry utilization has previously held back CA product sales (cumulative through Q3 2025, FnGuide). Higher electricity costs were flagged as a cost burden, and the company said it would pass the increase through to prices (Sedaily report, based on the Q1 2025 results). Specialty gas demand could change direction depending on how long the semiconductor upcycle lasts.

Financial structure

A debt ratio of 171.6% and total liabilities of KRW 383.2 billion would become a heavier burden if borrowing conditions tighten. Operating cash flow declining from KRW 42.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 25.2 billion in 2025 points in the same direction.

If borrowing for facility investment continues, interest costs and repayment obligations will weigh on earnings.

12

Overall view

PKC is a company in the middle of a business mix shift, building its semiconductor specialty materials alongside its core CA product line. On disclosed annual figures, revenue rose from KRW 232.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 272.1 billion in 2025, while the operating margin fell from 12.1% to 4.5% over the same period.

Cumulative operating profit for January–September 2026 rose 57.1% to KRW 13.7 billion, and the materials unit's share of sales widened to 21.0%, showing signs of improvement in the earnings mix.

On the other side, the debt ratio stood at 171.6% at end-2025, net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss in Q1 2026, and quarterly swings remain large.

Specialty gas demand and capacity expansion are the bullish factors, while financial burden and quarterly volatility are the bearish factors, and both remain in place with similar weight.

The next checkpoints are the battery materials certification outcome, the operating pace of the Gunsan and Saemangeum plants, and preliminary 2026 annual results followed by the 2026 annual report. Heungkuk Securities presented a KRW 7,700 target price in a May 2026 report, which is one brokerage's view. This report does not take a directional view on the share price.
