02

Business structure

ESTec is a specialist audio-equipment maker that produces car speakers, TV speakers and related components, supplying mainly overseas customers.

The sales mix in Alphasquare's company overview is car speakers at 74.50%, LCD/OLED (TV) at 21.30%, and HP and other parts at 4.20%, though that source does not state a reference date. Exports were 93.4% of sales in December 2025, against 6.6% domestic.

A 2019 securities research report put car speakers at 50%, TV speakers at 27% and mobile headphones at 20%, showing the product mix has shifted since. Major customers are domestic and overseas car and TV appliance makers.

Six consolidated subsidiaries operate in Vietnam (ESTec VINA, ESTec Phu Tho), China (ESTec Electronics JIAXING), Japan, the United States and India, split between production and sales. The India subsidiary was established on November 1, 2025.

The 3Q 2025 company summary cites rising demand for premium car speakers as electric and autonomous vehicles spread, while appliance sales declined on weak economic conditions. Major shareholders and affiliates hold 49.43%, and treasury shares account for 22.91% (as of June 13, 2026).

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 468.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 414.6 billion in 2023 (down 11.5% year on year) before recovering to KRW 509.4 billion in 2024 (up 22.9%). Revenue then declined to KRW 468.2 billion in 2025 (down 8.1%), with 2024 the largest year and 2023 the smallest among 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 8.72 billion in 2022 to KRW 24.6 billion in 2023 (up 181.5%), KRW 44 billion in 2024 (up 79.1%) and KRW 45.8 billion in 2025 (up 4.2%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. Operating profit in 2025 was the largest of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating margin rose from 1.9% in 2022 to 5.9% in 2023, 8.6% in 2024 and 9.8% in 2025. Over the same span, the cost of sales ratio fell from 85.63% in 2022 to 80.26% in 2025, and the 3Q 2025 company summary attributed the gross profit improvement to productivity gains and cost cuts.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 9.54 billion in 2022, KRW 21.7 billion in 2023 (up 127.1%), KRW 46.3 billion in 2024 (up 113.6%) and KRW 41.6 billion in 2025 (down 10.0%), with 2024 the largest year.

Operating cash flow was KRW 9.04 billion in 2022, KRW 47.7 billion in 2023, KRW 58.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 42.1 billion in 2025, exceeding attributable net profit in each year from 2023 to 2025.

Quarterly revenue bottomed at KRW 105.2 billion in 1Q 2026 (down 9.5% from the prior quarter), with operating profit of KRW 5.32 billion (down 44.4%). In 2Q 2026 revenue rebounded to KRW 127.1 billion (up 20.8%) and operating profit to KRW 12.5 billion (up 135.7%).

In 3Q 2026 revenue was KRW 118.6 billion (down 6.7% quarter on quarter, up 3.8% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 12.3 billion (down 2.2% quarter on quarter, up 32.1% year on year).

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in the same quarter was KRW 4.23 billion, down 66.4% from the prior quarter and down 64.0% year on year. The reason operating profit and attributable net profit diverged should be checked in the quarterly report detail.

05

Industry analysis

Car speakers are tied to the electrification and autonomy trend in automobiles, which is the core message of ESTec's 3Q 2025 company summary. The summary said the automotive business maintains stable sales.

By contrast, the TV and appliance business is sensitive to economic conditions, and its sales declined on a 3Q 2025 year-to-date basis.

In Alphasquare's annual ranking of 96 companies in the electronic equipment and devices sector, ESTec ranks 11th in operating profit at KRW 45.8 billion, 10th in net profit at KRW 41.6 billion, and 13th in operating margin at 9.79%.

The same dataset shows the sector's average operating margin as negative, so the sector's average profitability is low. ESTec ranks 17th by revenue.

Peers in the electronic components sector include Samsung Electro-Mechanics, LG Innotek and Seojin System, but ESTec's audio-component focus gives it a different business structure. Production sits mainly in Vietnam and China, which exposes the company to local labor costs, exchange rates and trade policy.

With exports at 93.4% of sales, overseas demand and currency movements carry significant weight. Within its sector, ESTec ranks high on profitability metrics but sits in the middle on revenue scale.

06

Outlook

In the automotive segment, rising demand for premium speakers used in electric and autonomous vehicles is cited as a growth driver. The company says it is adding circuit-product and software staff to develop composite circuit products as a new business.

R&D spending fell from KRW 11,946 million in 2023 (2.88% of sales) to KRW 3,663 million in 2025 (0.78% of sales). How the new-business investment fits with lower R&D spending should be checked in the R&D note of the next periodic report.

On the sales side, the India subsidiary established in November 2025 adds one more overseas sales base. The company's history records the appointment of Lee Seok-jeong as CEO on November 18, 2025, and the expiration or dismissal of former CEO Noh Kwang-seok.

Whether the new-business direction continues under new management is a second item to track. After the rebound in 2Q 2026 revenue and operating profit, whether 3Q levels hold is the near-term test. Whether automotive sales remain stable and whether appliance demand recovers will determine the revenue trajectory. The next checkpoints are the 4Q 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual business report.

Bull factors

Sustained margin improvement

Operating margin rose from 1.9% in 2022 to 9.8% in 2025, while the cost of sales ratio fell from 85.63% to 80.26% over the same period. Operating profit still rose to KRW 45.8 billion in 2025 even though revenue declined, which points to cost-structure gains. Margin expansion continuing from 2023 through 2025 is the core bullish evidence.

Stronger balance sheet

The debt ratio fell from 90.6% in 2024 to 46.9% in 2025, and total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 235 billion. Operating cash flow in 2025 was lower than in 2024 but still exceeded attributable net profit. Lower debt and cash generation that backs earnings form the financial base.

Rebound in 2Q 2026

Operating profit recovered from a KRW 5.32 billion low in 1Q 2026 to KRW 12.5 billion in 2Q and KRW 12.3 billion in 3Q. Third-quarter operating profit rose 32.1% year on year. Quarterly operating profit sitting well above the 1Q 2026 low indicates a recovery direction in operations.

Bear factors

Weak conversion to net profit

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.23 billion in 3Q 2026, down 64.0% year on year, while operating profit rose 32.1%. The gap between operating profit and attributable net profit is large. The cause should be checked in the detailed items of the quarterly report.

Weak appliance and TV segment

The 3Q 2025 year-to-date company summary attributes appliance sales declines to weak economic conditions. Annual revenue fell 8.1% to KRW 468.2 billion in 2025. If the appliance segment does not recover, the revenue base remains constrained.

Quarterly swings and export concentration

Revenue fell 9.5% quarter on quarter in 1Q 2026 and then rose 20.8% in 2Q, showing wide quarterly swings. With exports at 93.4% of sales, the company is exposed to overseas demand and currency moves. Large quarterly swings also make earnings harder to project.

Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

Alphasquare's company overview puts car speakers at 74.50% of sales, so changes in automotive customer production plans weigh heavily on results. Exports at 93.4% of 2025 sales add exposure to overseas customers and trade conditions. With product mix concentrated in one segment, alternative revenue sources are an important challenge.

Earnings volatility

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.23 billion in 3Q 2026, down 66.4% from the prior quarter, and KRW 7.91 billion in 1Q 2026, down 27.8%. The four-quarter total is KRW 35.7 billion. Sharp quarterly swings make the annual earnings trend harder to read.

Governance and free float

The CEO was replaced in November 2025, and major shareholders and affiliates (49.43%) plus treasury shares (22.91%) leave a limited share of stock available for trading. Continuity of new-business execution under new management matters. A small free float can also affect trading liquidity and share-price volatility.

Overall view

ESTec's reported results since 2022 show a clear rise in operating margin and a fall in the debt ratio. Operating profit reached KRW 45.8 billion in 2025, well above 2022, and the debt ratio fell to 46.9%.

However, revenue fell 8.1% in 2025, and in 3Q 2026 attributable net profit fell 64.0% to KRW 4.23 billion even as operating profit rose. The four-quarter total of attributable net profit is KRW 35.7 billion.

Strengths include the lower cost ratio, the improved balance sheet and stable operating cash flow, while weaknesses include the appliance slowdown, concentration in automotive and exports, and large quarterly swings in profit.

Valuation shows a multiple below the sector average and a share price below book net asset value, but because earnings change quarter to quarter, the read on the multiple changes too. The next checkpoints are the 4Q 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual business report and the 1Q 2027 quarterly report.
