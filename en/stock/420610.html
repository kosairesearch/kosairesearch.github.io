Report overview

13-Year Profit Streak, Weak After Listing

IntelliVIX listed on KOSDAQ on October 30, 2026 with a reported 13-year operating profit streak, but a cumulative 2026 third-quarter operating loss has put the durability of its profitability at the center of the debate.

02

Business structure

IntelliVIX is a video analytics AI company founded in 2000 that supplies safety control solutions for public safety, industrial safety, transportation and disaster management.

According to the company's IPO briefing, public-sector customers (B2G), mainly local governments and public agencies, account for about 60% of domestic sales, while private-sector customers (B2B) in manufacturing, construction and logistics make up about 40%.

Its core product is GenAMS, an integrated safety control platform based on generative AI, which combines the in-house VIX 2.0 analytics engine with the VIXA AI agent to understand site conditions and generate situation reports automatically.

In hardware, the VIXallcam AI camera can identify objects up to 200 meters away in bad weather and at night, and it received a CES 2026 Innovation Award. The robotics business centers on the ARGOS quadruped patrol robot, extending fixed camera monitoring into mobile operations.

The company cites a 68.5% share of the domestic public intelligent video analytics market and deployment in about 70% of local governments nationwide. That share is limited to the public segment, so competitive intensity in private-sector and overseas markets needs separate assessment.

Brokerage reports have named video security and AI algorithm companies as possible valuation peers. In Japan, the company supplies solutions to SoftBank through local distributor TB-eye, and it has secured partners in Indonesia, Bangladesh and the Philippines.

However, 2025 exports were about KRW 280 million, only 0.6% of total revenue. The company has set a target of raising exports to more than 20% of total revenue by 2028.

03

Recent trends

IntelliVIX reported 2025 revenue of KRW 46.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 4.9 billion (a 10.5% operating margin) and net profit of KRW 5.4 billion, roughly 2.5 times the 2023 revenue of KRW 18.3 billion.

Citing its audit report, media reported 2024 revenue of KRW 34.0 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.3 billion. The cumulative 2026 third-quarter filing shows revenue of KRW 10.5 billion, up 190.0% year on year.

Over the same period the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 816 million and a net loss of KRW 781 million, with losses narrower than a year earlier.

Because cumulative revenue is only a fraction of 2025 full-year revenue, the seasonality of sales concentrated in the second half and especially the fourth quarter appears to shape the earnings pattern.

The company has said that, given the budget execution cycle of public agencies, revenue tends to cluster in the fourth quarter.

Media reports state that provisional results showed operating losses of KRW 580 million in July and KRW 444 million in August 2026, reducing cumulative operating profit through August to about KRW 200 million.

As of 14:23 KST on November 18, 2026, the share price was KRW 7,880, down 12.93% from the previous close, with a market capitalization of KRW 89.3 billion and trading value of KRW 6.6 billion.

Media reports said the offering price was set at KRW 9,000, the top of the KRW 7,600 to 9,000 indicative range, after retail subscriptions on October 21 and 22, 2026 drew a competition ratio of 1,410.9 to 1. The current price of KRW 7,880 sits below that offering price.

04

Outlook

At its year-end event on December 24, 2025, the company set a 2026 target of KRW 70.0 billion in revenue and KRW 9.0 billion in operating profit.

Cumulative third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 10.5 billion represents only part of that target, and whether the full-year goal is met depends heavily on fourth-quarter results, given how sales concentrate in that quarter.

Of the net proceeds of KRW 18.57 billion at the bottom of the offering range, the company plans to allocate KRW 12.0 billion to R&D, KRW 5.0 billion to facilities and KRW 1.574 billion to overseas bases. In defense, the company plans to finish developing its AI sentry system in 2028 and conduct field trials in 2029.

Media reported a defense AI project contract worth KRW 6.2 billion, and the company cites its selection as a Defense Innovation Company 100 as a foundation for this business.

It is also pursuing a subscription model that extends its installed systems into services such as video search and automated report generation, with recurring revenue as the stated aim.

Overseas, the company cites a partnership with LG CNS for joint expansion of overseas factories and a distribution network covering eight Southeast Asian countries.

Shares available for trading after listing represent 23.95% of total listed shares, while 60.12% held by the largest shareholder and core executives is locked up for three years.

05

Bull factors

13 Years of Profit and Financial Buffer

The company announced 2025 revenue of KRW 46.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 4.9 billion and net profit of KRW 5.4 billion. Accumulated retained earnings stood at KRW 16.4 billion.

Media reports based on the audit report put year-end 2025 total equity at KRW 12.9 billion, total liabilities at KRW 8.3 billion and the net debt ratio at 35.09%. The company is cited as an example of a domestic AI firm that generates its own profits, in an industry where many peers remain loss-making.

Dominance in Public-Sector Market

Media reports say its solutions are installed at more than 5,000 sites with more than 600,000 licensed channels in operation. The model builds subscription services on top of a base of installed public-sector systems.

Emerging Business Pipeline

Selection as a Defense Innovation Company 100 and a CES 2026 Innovation Award are cited as external validation of its technology. Media reported a defense AI project contract worth KRW 6.2 billion, and its physical AI lineup, including the ARGOS quadruped robot, is expanding. Defense and robotics are presented as growth options that extend its existing video control business.

06

Bear factors

Cumulative Losses Through Third Quarter 2026

The cumulative third-quarter 2026 filing shows an operating loss of KRW 816 million and a net loss of KRW 781 million. The losses narrowed from a year earlier, but the company did not return to profit over the period.

Media reports on provisional results indicate operating losses of KRW 580 million in July and KRW 444 million in August 2026, which cut cumulative operating profit through August to about KRW 200 million.

Seasonality and Quarterly Volatility

The company says that the budget execution cycle of public agencies concentrates sales in the second half, especially the fourth quarter. Under this pattern, quarterly results alone make it hard to read the underlying trend, and the timing of year-end revenue recognition largely determines full-year performance.

Cumulative third-quarter revenue of KRW 10.5 billion compared with the KRW 70.0 billion full-year target means the year depends heavily on fourth-quarter results.

Limited Overseas Revenue Contribution

Exports in 2025 totaled about KRW 280 million, only 0.6% of total revenue. Media reports say the company is expanding into Japan, Southeast Asia and the Middle East, but converting its overseas sales network into revenue remains a challenge.

The gap between current overseas contribution and the target of more than 20% of revenue by 2028 is cited as a weakness.

07

Risk factors

Public Demand

About 60% of domestic sales depend on local governments and public agencies, so results are sensitive to public budget allocation and execution timing. Because budget execution tends to concentrate in the fourth quarter, delays can widen quarterly swings. Reduced public demand or budget adjustments would directly affect the revenue base.

Industry and Competition

Media reports note that many domestic AI companies struggle to secure profitability. In video analytics, hardware makers and AI algorithm firms compete in public and industrial safety markets. Private-sector and overseas markets may present a different competitive landscape from the public segment.

Execution

The defense AI sentry system is scheduled for technology development completion in 2028 and field trials in 2029, so visible results will take time. The shift to subscriptions and overseas expansion remain execution variables until they contribute measurable revenue.

Whether the pace of offering proceeds deployment translates into later financial results is the key open question.

08

Overall view

IntelliVIX listed on KOSDAQ on October 30, 2026, backed by 13 consecutive years of reported operating profit and a 68.5% share of the public video analytics market. Its 2025 results, with revenue of KRW 46.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.9 billion, showed both growth and profitability.

By contrast, the cumulative 2026 third-quarter filing shows an operating loss of KRW 816 million and a net loss of KRW 781 million, and media reports also cite operating losses in July and August 2026.

Because revenue clusters in the fourth quarter, quarterly results alone limit how far the underlying trend can be read, and this is the central point of debate. Defense, physical AI and exports are presented as growth options, but exports were only 0.6% of 2025 revenue.

The share price of KRW 7,880 is below the KRW 9,000 offering price after a 12.93% decline in the latest session. The bullish factors center on financial stability and public-sector dominance, while the bearish factors center on loss-making quarters and weak overseas revenue.
