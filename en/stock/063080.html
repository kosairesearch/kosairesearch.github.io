02

Business structure

Com2uS Holdings is a holding company with a game publishing business and a platform business. Its platform subsidiary runs the game back-end and cloud operations. The flagship title Soul Strike introduced seasonal content on a three-month cycle to extend its live service.

Q3 2026 revenue reflected the global launch of the action RPG Ares: The Iron Vanguard alongside continued sales from existing titles. Hive Axyl, launched in September 2026, targets demand for AI-based game development and supports titles from launch through live operations.

Cloud revenue grew 117% year on year in Q3 2026, and cumulative cloud revenue for the first three quarters passed the full-year 2025 cloud revenue scale. Cumulative contracted clients stood at 56 as of end-September 2026, with 11 added in the third quarter alone.

External game publishers account for a growing share of back-end revenue, and collaboration with Tencent Cloud is expanding the AI-based B2B business. Holding-company results include equity-method investment income, which according to Smartbiz rose from KRW 300 million to KRW 6 billion year on year in Q3 2026.

New games are being prepared for broader platforms and genres including PC and console. The publishing business depends heavily on a small number of hit titles, so the long-term operation of existing IP and new-release performance both matter.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 116.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 153.1 billion in 2023, up 31.7% year on year, before declining 2.5% in 2024 to KRW 149.3 billion and 33.5% in 2025 to KRW 99.2 billion, a two-year decline running from 2024 through 2025.

Within 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest revenue year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating losses were KRW 26.4 billion in 2022, KRW 3.35 billion in 2023, KRW 50.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.82 billion in 2025, a four-year run of operating losses in which the loss narrowed in 2023, widened in 2024 and narrowed again in 2025.

Operating margin was -22.7% in 2022, -2.2% in 2023, -33.9% in 2024 and -6.9% in 2025, with 2024 the lowest. In 2024 the operating loss widened even as revenue slipped 2.5 percent.

In 2025 the operating loss narrowed to KRW 6.82 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 11 billion. Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 37.3 billion in 2025, a different figure that should not be read as controlling-shareholder profit.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 54.9 billion in 2022, KRW 9.34 billion in 2023 and KRW 36.3 billion in 2024.

Operating cash flow was negative KRW 5.91 billion in 2022, negative KRW 3.02 billion in 2023, negative KRW 8.64 billion in 2024 and negative KRW 24.1 billion in 2025, the largest outflow of the period.

On a quarterly basis, revenue went from KRW 19.6 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 28 billion in Q4 2025, up 42.6% quarter on quarter, then fell to KRW 17.1 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 15.6 billion in Q2 2026, before rising to KRW 25.1 billion in Q3 2026.

Operating results moved from a KRW 3.95 billion loss in Q3 2025 to a KRW 2.52 billion profit in Q4 2025, back to a KRW 9.71 billion loss in Q1 2026, a KRW 5.28 billion loss in Q2 2026 and a KRW 4.8 billion profit in Q3 2026.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was positive in Q4 2025 at KRW 24.6 billion and in Q2 2026 at KRW 9.75 billion, and negative in Q1 2026 at KRW 11.5 billion and in Q3 2026 at KRW 173 million.

The sum for the last four quarters was KRW 22.7 billion, so the quarter-to-quarter swings call for reading the cumulative trend. The company credited platform growth and equity-method investment income for the Q3 2026 turn, and consolidated operating expenses fell 13.8% year on year to KRW 20.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The game industry is structured so that a handful of hit titles can swing results sharply, so Com2uS Holdings' earnings are usually read through both the long-term operation of existing titles and the performance of new releases.

Beyond its own game publishing, the company has broadened into the Hive game back-end service and cloud operations. The main customers of the back-end business are game developers, and securing overseas clients in line with demand for AI-based game development is the key variable.

The company has described plans to extend Hive into a B2B2B platform linking game companies and publishers worldwide. It says its AI-based B2B business is gaining traction through its collaboration with Tencent Cloud. In puzzle games, Colorsweeper and Pop Pop Match were prepared for soft launches targeting Q1 2026.

New titles built on external IP and projects sourced through a game challenge contest are also being used to diversify genres. Because back-end and cloud demand depends on game developers' spending, the number of clients and the share of external revenue serve as useful indicators.

As a holding company, the company is also exposed to volatility in equity-method investment income. Game publishing results depend on new-release hit rates and the lifespan of existing titles, so whether both businesses grow together is the key question.

Zenonia 1: Memories of Thread and Ron Chef are being expanded to PC and console lineups. The company plans to enter the FMV segment in partnership with SOOP and Monster Guide. On the platform side, the company intends to grow Hive Axyl and cloud into core growth pillars.

The next checkpoints are how far the Hive Axyl client count rises from 56 at end-September 2026 and whether cloud growth holds near 117 percent. The next periodic report, the 2026 annual report, will cover Q4 2026, so whether operating profit stays positive after the Q3 turn is the next test.

Reports also say that Q3 operating revenue fell slightly year on year, so sustaining the improvement is seen as requiring continued IP revenue, new-title hits and higher platform income. Because equity-method investment income contributed meaningfully to the Q3 turn, whether it recurs in later quarters also matters.

Whether new releases hit their schedules and whether revenue holds for more than three months after launch will show up in upcoming quarterly results.

Contracted clients reached 56 at end-September, with 11 added in the quarter. Hive Axyl targets AI-driven game development, so further client wins could raise the share of external revenue.

A deep pipeline of titles

The company set a target of eight publishing titles for 2026 and said it would launch roughly ten by year-end. Ares: The Iron Vanguard broadened the lineup with its global launch in Q3, while Zenonia 2 and Ron Chef are being prepared for 2027. If long-running live titles and new hits combine, the earnings base could broaden.

Bear factors

Volatile quarterly results

After operating losses of KRW 9.71 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 5.28 billion in Q2 2026, operating profit returned at KRW 4.8 billion in Q3. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from KRW 9.75 billion in Q2 to a net loss of KRW 173 million in Q3. Whether quarterly profits will carry into a full-year profit remains to be seen.

Reliance on affiliate income

According to Smartbiz, affiliate investment income rose from KRW 300 million to KRW 6 billion year on year in Q3 2026, helping drive the turn to profit. On a standalone basis, the company still recorded an operating loss of KRW 3 billion in the same quarter. It is too early to say whether the core business has fully recovered.

Cash flow and a long loss record

Operating losses ran for four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025, and operating cash outflow widened to KRW 24.1 billion in 2025 from KRW 8.64 billion in 2024. Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 266.4 billion, above total equity of KRW 252.5 billion. Whether cash generation improves remains an open issue.

Overall view

Com2uS Holdings returned to operating profit in Q3 2026, with revenue of KRW 25.1 billion, up 28.0% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 4.8 billion, after operating losses in Q1 and Q2 2026.

The direction has shifted from a run of operating losses in every year from 2022 through 2025 toward a 2025 turn to net profit attributable to controlling shareholders and a Q3 2026 operating profit. Strengths include 117 percent cloud growth, 56 contracted clients for Hive Axyl and a deep new-title pipeline.

Weaknesses include volatile quarterly results, reliance on affiliate investment income and the KRW 24.1 billion operating cash outflow in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 22.7 billion, with wide quarter-to-quarter variation.

Whether new hits and higher platform income arrive together will largely determine how durable the profit turn is. The next checkpoints are Q4 2026 results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, followed by the 2027 launch performance of Zenonia 2 and Ron Chef.
