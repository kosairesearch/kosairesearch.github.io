Report overview

Fee Growth, but a Weaker Q3 Profit

After returning to an annual profit in 2025, Daol Investment & Securities saw third-quarter 2026 operating profit fall 68.9% from the prior quarter as market trading slowed, even as fee income and revenue expanded.

  1. 1

    The 2025 annual result of KRW 33.7 billion operating profit and KRW 40.5 billion controlling-shareholder net income reversed the KRW 74.9 billion operating loss and KRW 48.6 billion controlling-shareholder net loss of 2024.

  2. 2

    Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 649 billion rose 115.6% year on year, while operating profit of KRW 5.32 billion fell 53.8% year on year, leaving a gap between activity and earnings.

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Daol Investment & Securities is the securities arm of the Daol financial group. On a consolidated basis its subsidiaries include Daol Savings Bank, Daol Asset Management and the Thailand-based brokerage Daol Thailand PCL.

Its business is organized into corporate sales, retail and platform, the trading division, equity derivatives, fixed income sales and investment banking. In 2025 the newly created strategic sales division, which handles acquisition finance and structured finance, and the global markets division began operations.

According to Joongang Economy, for January to September 2026 self-trading generated KRW 11.5 billion, up 80% year on year, and asset management generated KRW 1.7 billion, also up 80%. Over the same period brokerage generated KRW 17.7 billion, down 9.03%, and equity investment generated KRW 6.8 billion, down 40.80%.

Underwriting arrangement swung to a KRW 10.3 billion net loss. Cumulative fee net revenue rose 56.9% to KRW 126.5 billion, led by brokerage fees and fund-related fees, according to The Value News. The retail business widened its base on higher market trading activity and stronger digital sales capacity.

The company cites the sales strength of its corporate, retail and fixed income teams as the foundation of its results. Overseas, it has a strategic partnership with the US firm IBKR to expand its global investment platform.

The savings bank subsidiary recorded cumulative net income of KRW 13.98 billion for January to September 2026, according to The Value News.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 1.84 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.51 trillion in 2023, down 18.1%, and to KRW 1.44 trillion in 2024, down 4.4%, before rising 18.8% to KRW 1.71 trillion in 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit of KRW 107 billion in 2022, a 5.8% margin, turned into an operating loss of KRW 62 billion in 2023, a margin of -4.1%. In 2024 the operating loss widened to KRW 74.9 billion, a margin of -5.2%, before the company returned to operating profit of KRW 33.7 billion in 2025, a 2.0% margin.

Controlling-shareholder net income went from KRW 61.7 billion in 2022 to a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 10.9 billion in 2023 and KRW 48.6 billion in 2024, then to KRW 40.5 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 43.9 billion in 2025, a different figure from controlling-shareholder net income. The company attributes the 2025 turnaround to the corporate, retail and fixed income sales teams and to the trading division's response to volatility.

In investment banking, a reduction in real estate project finance exposure, lower provisions and partial provision reversals supported the improvement. Provision reversals do not recur every quarter, so they should be separated from operating earnings when judging durability.

Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 11.5 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 10.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025 were followed by an operating loss of KRW 3.38 billion in the fourth quarter.

Operating profit recovered to KRW 9.18 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 17.1 billion in the second quarter. In the third quarter of 2026 revenue fell 38.8% from the prior quarter to KRW 649 billion, and operating profit fell 68.9% to KRW 5.32 billion.

According to press reports, cumulative selling, general and administrative expenses for January to September 2026 rose to KRW 231.1 billion from KRW 199.9 billion a year earlier, which is cited as one reason profit fell despite the larger top line.

05

Industry analysis

Brokerage earnings are sensitive to trading volumes, market interest rates and investment income.

In the first half of 2026 a strong stock market lifted profitability, but in the third quarter a market correction, lower trading value and rising market rates squeezed both brokerage commissions and financial product management, according to SR Times.

In the same quarter Korea Investment Securities' net income fell 70.2% from the prior quarter to KRW 281.8 billion, and KIWOOM Securities' fell 65.0% to KRW 238.6 billion, the same report said. The slowdown is not confined to Daol Investment & Securities and is spreading to smaller brokerages.

Daol's third-quarter operating profit fell 68.9% from the prior quarter, a decline that combines the industry-wide correction with swings in trading income and the swing of underwriting arrangement into a loss.

Viewed as a cycle, the first half of 2026 benefited from a trading boom and the third quarter reflected a pullback. On fees, cumulative brokerage fees rose 138.1% to KRW 55.8 billion and fund sales fees rose to KRW 81.9 billion, widening the base of trading and fund distribution income, according to The Value News.

Reducing real estate project finance exposure is viewed as a change in investment banking risk management. Still, the pace at which market trading recovers will determine how volatile earnings remain.

08

Bull factors

Expanding fee base

According to The Value News, cumulative fee net revenue for January to September 2026 rose 56.9% year on year. Because brokerage and fund-related fees grew together, the result could reduce reliance on market trading swings. The company also said a more diversified revenue structure has been the foundation of its earnings recovery.

Smaller investment banking risk

In 2025 the investment banking division was supported by lower real estate project finance exposure, reduced provisions and partial provision reversals. With less real estate project finance burden, the view that credit loss risk is lower than before is possible.

Provision reversals do not recur, however, so underlying earnings strength needs to be checked in the operating businesses.

Capital base versus share price

Because the share price trades below book value per share, the argument that the capital base is not fully reflected in the price is one bull case. Controlling-shareholder equity of KRW 626 billion at end-2025 is also cited as asset-side support.

Controlling-shareholder net income in the second quarter of 2026 was KRW 14 billion, up 12.1% from the first quarter.

09

Bear factors

Earnings sensitive to trading and rates

A market correction, lower trading value and higher market rates hit brokerage commissions and investment income at the same time. The speed with which the first-half boom gave way to a second-half slowdown shows earnings depend heavily on market conditions.

Quarterly operating profit swung from a KRW 3.38 billion loss in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 17.1 billion in the second quarter of 2026, which underlines the volatility.

Underwriting loss and lower brokerage income

According to Joongang Economy, cumulative underwriting arrangement for January to September 2026 swung to a KRW 10.3 billion net loss. Over the same period brokerage fell 9.03% to KRW 17.7 billion, and equity investment fell 40.80% to KRW 6.8 billion.

If these segments do not recover even as fee income grows, the overall earnings base could weaken, which is the core of the bear case.

High leverage and negative operating cash flow

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 863.5% in 2022 to 1,195.6% in 2025. Operating cash flow was disclosed as -KRW 683.2 billion in 2023, -KRW 270.9 billion in 2024 and -KRW 265.6 billion in 2025. With total liabilities of KRW 9.65 trillion and a thin equity cushion, the financial structure is cited as a bear point.

10

Risk factors

Internal controls and legal matters

According to Edaily, two former employees were referred to prosecutors on November 2, 2026, over leaking a whistleblower report received in November 2021 on an apartment developer. At that time the company was arranging a KRW 300 billion real estate project finance loan for the project.

Police judged that the company itself had not clearly breached its duty of care, so the corporation was excluded from punishment.

Real estate project finance credit risk

The company says it has reduced its real estate project finance exposure, but 2025 investment banking earnings were partly supported by lower provisions and partial reversals. If provisions have to be rebuilt, investment banking earnings could deteriorate quickly. The pace at which the property market and PF projects are resolved is one source of future earnings volatility.

Market trading and interest rate volatility

When stock trading value falls or market rates rise, brokerage commissions and investment income both come under pressure. In the third quarter of 2026 both factors operated at once.

Because the slowdown in brokerage earnings is spreading to smaller firms, a delayed industry recovery could push back the timing of an earnings rebound.

Overall view

Daol Investment & Securities returned to an annual profit in 2025, with operating profit of KRW 33.7 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 40.5 billion, and quarterly operating profit recovered in the first and second quarters of 2026.

In the third quarter of 2026, however, revenue of KRW 649 billion rose 115.6% year on year while operating profit fell to KRW 5.32 billion, so top-line growth did not carry through to profit.

The 56.9% rise in cumulative fee net revenue is presented as evidence of a more diversified revenue structure, but deterioration in equity investment and underwriting arrangement partly offset it.

On the financial side, the debt-to-equity ratio of 1,195.6% at end-2025 and negative operating cash flow from 2023 to 2025 remain items to monitor. The share price trades below book value per share, so asset-based arguments and concerns about earnings volatility coexist.

Legal matters and real estate project finance risk should be read alongside the exclusion of the company from punishment and the reduced exposure. The next points to confirm are the 2026 annual business report, the preliminary 2026 full-year results and first-quarter 2027 results.
