Report overview

A Konex Optical Parts Maker in a Loss Phase

NWC, a Konex-listed optical communications parts maker, posted lower sales and an operating loss for fiscal 2025, so the open question is whether sector improvement reaches its own results.

  1. 1

    For fiscal 2025 (the 27th term), standalone sales were KRW 18,782 million, with an operating loss of KRW 485 million and a net loss of KRW 1,370 million.

  2. 2

    On a consolidated basis, sales were KRW 19,051 million, the operating loss was KRW 963 million and the net loss was KRW 1,734 million, with an unqualified audit opinion.

  3. 3

    The core products are passive optical communications parts such as FTTH drop cables, FTTH connectors, 5G CPRI products and optical closures, and NWC newly listed on Konex on December 22, 2025.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

NWC is an optical communications parts maker. It was founded on January 29, 1999, manufactures optical devices (FTTH drop, FTTH connectors, 5G CPRI, MDU and optical closures), and also trades telecom equipment. Its English corporate name is NWC CO.,LTD in the KIND preliminary review file.

It newly listed on the Konex market on December 22, 2025, under the telecom and broadcasting equipment manufacturing classification. Its field-assembly optical connector was selected as a World-Class Product by the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy in November 2017.

According to the company profile, it has moved beyond parts to produce optical cables and 5G base-station cables, supplied to domestic carriers and others.

Overseas, it set up a U.S. subsidiary in 2009, a China subsidiary in 2010 and a Vietnam subsidiary in 2019.

In 2024, media reported that it teamed up with VMC, a Viettel affiliate in Vietnam, on optical fiber cable production and supply, targeting annual revenue of USD 4 million to 7 million from North American and European customers. No filing confirms how much sales this cooperation has actually produced.

Domestically, NWC received KT's FTTH partner certification in 2007 and supplied field-assembly connectors to KT in 2008, which suggests a heavy reliance on domestic carriers. Segment and customer sales shares could not be confirmed, so this is described qualitatively only.

Among sector peers, TAIHAN Fiberoptics is described by Leading Investment & Securities as vertically integrated from fiber preform to optical cable, with sales split about 60% telecom and 40% power. NWC, by contrast, has 37 employees on the National Pension basis, so the two differ widely in product scope and scale.

03

Recent trends

At its annual general meeting on March 31, 2026, NWC approved the 27th-term financial statements and the election of directors and an auditor. On a standalone basis, 27th-term sales were KRW 18,782 million, with an operating loss of KRW 485 million and a net loss of KRW 1,370 million.

On a consolidated basis, sales were KRW 19,051 million, the operating loss was KRW 963 million, the net loss was KRW 1,734 million, and the loss per share was KRW 326.

The consolidated balance sheet shows total assets of KRW 16,692 million, total liabilities of KRW 12,723 million and total equity of KRW 3,969 million, so liabilities exceed three times equity.

According to financial data compiled by Saramin, 2024 sales were KRW 26.76 billion, with operating profit of KRW 1.04 billion and net income of KRW 1.08 billion. In 2025, by comparison, sales fell and the company slipped back into an operating loss.

The same source shows an operating loss of KRW 2.60 billion in 2023 and KRW 3.94 billion in 2022. Across the last four fiscal years, 2024 was the only year with an operating profit.

We could not confirm any 2026 quarterly or half-year earnings figures. The company did, however, file investor relations (IR) notices or results on June 10, June 26, September 17 and October 6, 2026. The stock closed at KRW 4,835 on March 31, 2026, and it closed at KRW 4,095 on October 27, 2026. Market capitalization is KRW 21.8 billion, and turnover that day was KRW 1,023,750.

04

Outlook

The sector backdrop is showing more favorable signals. According to recent reports, the spread of generative AI and larger data center investment are likely to expand demand for optical cables and transceivers structurally.

Brokerage reports say an AI data center needs 16 to 36 times more optical fiber than a conventional cloud data center, and data center fiber prices reportedly rose by as much as 70% to 90% from the third quarter of 2025.

Leading Investment & Securities assessed TAIHAN Fiberoptics as achieving qualitative growth on the strength of orders for ultra-high-density optical cables for AI data centers.

However, this demand is concentrated mainly in optical fiber, high-density cable and transceivers. NWC's core products are passive FTTH access and drop parts, so it appears more directly tied to carriers' FTTH investment cycles.

No filing confirms whether it has any AI data center sales or how large they are, so sector improvement cannot be assumed to translate directly into better results.

On operating metrics, the key question is where room exists to narrow the operating loss and lift the operating margin from the fiscal 2025 level. With small sales, the fixed-cost burden is heavy, so the size of any sales recovery could change profit and loss substantially.

The balance sheet improved once when convertible bonds worth KRW 8.05 billion were fully converted into common shares in May 2024, but 2025 consolidated liabilities still exceed equity.

Future earnings filings and IR materials should be checked for revenue from overseas partnerships, order status and any improvement in profitability.

05

Bull factors

Improving optical communications sector

As AI data volumes grow, optical communications gain importance, and optical cable demand is said to be likely to expand structurally. If rising fiber prices coincide with a revival in carrier investment, demand conditions for parts makers could also improve. NWC has long handled optical parts and sits within the same industry current.

Certified products with a long track record

Its field-assembly optical connector was named a World-Class Product in 2017, and the company holds KT FTTH partner certification and a supply record. Dealing with domestic carriers for 27 years since its founding can act as an entry barrier in terms of quality certification and delivery experience. Subsidiaries in the United States, China and Vietnam also give it an overseas sales base.

Overseas partnership and balance sheet cleanup

In 2024, NWC announced optical fiber cable cooperation with VMC, a Viettel affiliate, and set a goal of supplying North American and European defense and civilian customers. If the cooperation shows up in sales, it could help reduce reliance on domestic carriers.

In May 2024, convertible bonds of KRW 8.05 billion were fully converted into common shares, which lightened the repayment burden.

06

Bear factors

Recurring operating losses

According to Saramin data, operating losses occurred in 2022, 2023 and 2025, and only 2024 showed an operating profit. In 2025, the standalone operating loss was KRW 485 million and the consolidated operating loss was KRW 963 million. There is not yet confirmed evidence that a profitable footing has been established.

High leverage on thin equity

Consolidated total liabilities are KRW 12,723 million against total equity of KRW 3,969 million. On a standalone basis too, total liabilities of KRW 10,793 million far exceed total equity of KRW 4,883 million. If losses continue, equity could shrink further and weigh on financial stability.

Indirect AI exposure and limited scale

Demand tied to AI data centers is showing up first at fiber and high-density cable makers such as TAIHAN Fiberoptics. NWC is a small company with 37 employees, and its core line is passive FTTH parts, so its path to benefit is unclear. It also remains unconfirmed whether the overseas cooperation the company announced has flowed into sales.

07

Risk factors

Liquidity

Turnover on October 27, 2026 was only KRW 1,023,750. Small Konex stocks can see wide quote gaps and large price swings even on small orders. At a market capitalization of KRW 21.8 billion, investors must allow for the chance that trades do not fill at the desired price or size.

Earnings and financial

NWC posted net losses on both a standalone and a consolidated basis for fiscal 2025. If losses persist, equity could shrink and borrowing and funding conditions could worsen. If funding comes through new shares or convertible bonds, existing shareholders could be diluted.

Industry and customer

Demand for FTTH and 5G parts depends heavily on carriers' capital spending schedules. The company appears to rely heavily on domestic carriers, so order changes by a single customer could show up directly in results.

Overseas subsidiaries and foreign-currency transactions also make exchange rates and local policy changes variables.

08

Overall view

NWC is a Konex-listed company with a 27-year history that makes passive optical communications parts for FTTH and 5G. For fiscal 2025, it posted standalone sales of KRW 18,782 million with an operating loss of KRW 485 million, and a consolidated operating loss of KRW 963 million.

The sector is drawing attention for AI data center investment and rising fiber prices, but no confirmed data show how much of this reaches NWC directly. On the bull side, sector improvement, product certification history and possible overseas cooperation are cited.

On the bear side, the recurring operating losses, liabilities large relative to equity and very thin trading liquidity are cited. Confirmed 2026 quarterly results and evidence that overseas cooperation has reached sales would give a clearer basis for assessment, and the final judgment rests with the reader.
