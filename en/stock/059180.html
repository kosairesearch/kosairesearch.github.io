02

Business structure

NWC was founded on January 29, 1999 as a maker of optical communications components and related materials. In 2000 it developed optical closures and obtained domestic quality certification from customers including Korea Telecom and Dacom, followed by the EM mark in May 2001.

Its product lines span optical closures, multi-function enclosures for indoor and outdoor use, terminal boxes, distribution boxes, and optical cables.

The component side includes passive products such as optical patch cords, optical attenuators, and optical terminators, along with newer products such as optical couplers, WDM devices, variable optical attenuators, and optical-device connectors.

The company also produces optical cables and drop and alarm cables used in 5G base station construction and supplies domestic telecom operators. NWC says it is developing optical sensors and intends to grow them into a future growth engine. Overseas, it is pursuing marketing mainly in the United States and China.

Among listed peers in the same sector, TAIHAN Fiberoptics is the closest comparison in optical communications components. Because domestic telecom operators make up a significant share of its customers, telecom capital spending trends can affect its revenue.

03

Recent trends

At the annual general meeting on March 31, 2026, the financial statements for the 27th fiscal term (fiscal 2025) were approved. Separate-basis revenue for 2025 was KRW 18.78 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 485 million and a net loss of KRW 1.37 billion.

On a consolidated basis, revenue was KRW 19,051 million, operating loss KRW 963 million, and net loss KRW 1,734 million, and the external auditor issued unqualified opinions on both separate and consolidated statements.

Separate-basis revenue for 2024 was KRW 26.76 billion, with an operating profit of KRW 1.04 billion and net income of KRW 1.08 billion. The company posted operating losses of KRW 2.60 billion in 2023 and KRW 3.94 billion in 2022. Revenue fell sharply in 2025, and operating results moved from profit to loss.

At the end of 2025, separate-basis total assets were KRW 15,676 million, total liabilities KRW 10,793 million, and total equity KRW 4,883 million. Investor relations briefing results were disclosed on June 26 and October 6, 2026.

The share price was KRW 4,835 at 4:10 p.m. on March 31, 2026, and the reference price for this report is KRW 4,210 (+0.24% versus the previous close).

Outlook

The key variables for future results are whether revenue recovers and whether the swing back to operating profit proves sustainable. Because revenue fell sharply in 2025 and operating profit turned to a loss, a revenue rebound is a precondition for better profitability.

It is not yet clear from disclosed figures how much optical sensors and newer products such as optical couplers and WDM devices will offset core optical materials sales. Telecom operators' capital spending plans and demand for FTTH and 5G-related materials act as external variables for revenue.

The revenue and profit trends of TAIHAN Fiberoptics, a peer in optical communications components, serve as an in-sector benchmark. Consolidated total equity of KRW 3,969 million is below the separate-basis figure, so changes in the capital structure also warrant attention.

The company held investor relations briefings on June 26 and October 6, 2026, where it presented its business direction. As a KONEX-listed company, its disclosure frequency and information density are more limited than those of large KOSPI or KOSDAQ companies, which shapes how its figures should be interpreted.

Bull factors

Optical infrastructure product range and certification record

NWC offers optical closures, FTTH drop products, and 5G CPRI-related products, which align with demand for telecom infrastructure materials. Its record of developing optical closures in 2000 and obtaining domestic quality certification and the EM mark is cited as the basis for its telecom supply relationships.

Direct production of optical cables, drop cables, and alarm cables shows a structure that covers both components and materials. However, viewed alongside the 2025 revenue decline, the extent to which this strength has been reflected in sales remains limited.

A profitable year in 2024

Separate-basis operating profit in 2024 was KRW 1.04 billion, and net income was KRW 1.08 billion. Coming out of operating losses in 2022 and 2023, the year can be read as an example of an earnings turnaround. Separate-basis revenue that year was KRW 26.76 billion.

Whether this profit reflected a one-off factor or a repeatable pattern is reflected in the earnings trend after 2025.

Optical sensor and product expansion with overseas marketing

NWC says it is developing optical sensors and plans to grow them into a future growth engine. It is also developing new products including optical couplers, WDM devices, variable optical attenuators, and optical-device connectors.

Its global marketing effort, centered on the United States and China, is presented as another expansion axis. However, the revenue contribution from these new areas is not yet visible in disclosed figures.

Bear factors

Sharp revenue decline in 2025

Separate-basis revenue for fiscal 2025 was KRW 18.78 billion, down sharply from KRW 26.76 billion in 2024. The revenue decline fed into weaker profitability, and operating results moved from profit to loss. Consolidated revenue of KRW 19,051 million is close to the separate-basis figure. The detailed causes of the revenue drop are not broken out by business segment in current disclosures.

Earnings volatility and repeated losses

Separate-basis operating losses were KRW 3.94 billion in 2022, KRW 2.60 billion in 2023, and KRW 485 million in 2025, so loss years recur. The 2024 profit was a single-year event. Consolidated operating loss of KRW 963 million and net loss of KRW 1,734 million show that the loss structure has not been fully resolved. Because annual earnings swing widely, the continuity of results is limited.

High leverage and a small trading base

At the end of 2025, separate-basis total liabilities of KRW 10,793 million exceeded total equity of KRW 4,883 million, indicating a high debt ratio. Consolidated total equity was KRW 3,969 million, below the separate-basis figure.

With a trading value of KRW 1,052,500, the market's depth to absorb price moves is limited. The combination of financial burden and a thin trading base is a bearish factor.

Risk factors

Macro

Interest rate levels and economic conditions can affect telecom operators' capacity for capital spending. A prolonged high-rate environment raises financing costs for companies with heavy debt. As overseas marketing expands, currency movements may also feed into export profitability. Changes in the macro backdrop could slow the pace of any earnings recovery.

Industry

Because domestic telecom operators make up a large share of customers, demand moves with the investment cycles for 5G and FTTH. If new areas such as optical sensors fail to grow enough to complement the core business, concentration risk remains.

Price competition with peers in optical communications components, including TAIHAN Fiberoptics, could intensify. If industry demand and the competitive landscape shift together, margin pressure could increase.

Liquidity

As a KONEX-listed company with a trading value of KRW 1,052,500 and a market capitalization of KRW 22.4 billion, its market is small. In a thin trading structure, short-term supply and demand can move the share price considerably. Disclosure frequency is also more limited than at large KOSPI or KOSDAQ companies. When earnings disclosures come out in a thinly traded market, the market reaction can be larger.

08

Overall view

NWC is a KONEX-listed company operating in the telecom infrastructure materials market, centered on optical closures and optical communications components. Its 2024 profit and its push into optical sensors and new products are cited as bull factors.

On the other hand, separate-basis revenue for fiscal 2025 fell sharply and the company swung to operating and net losses, while its debt burden relative to equity is high. Because earnings have fluctuated repeatedly since 2022, bear factors weigh relatively heavier on the continuity of results.

Still, the modest size of the consolidated loss and the unqualified audit opinions are neutral considerations. The share price has moved from KRW 4,835 on March 31, 2026 to KRW 4,210 as the reference price.

The contribution of new businesses to revenue and whether quarterly earnings recover after 2026 are the indicators to track. This verdict is a qualitative summary provided for information only.
