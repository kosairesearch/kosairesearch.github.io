02

Business structure

PSK develops, manufactures and supplies dry strip and dry cleaning equipment that removes photoresist from wafers, and is listed on KOSDAQ as a front-end process equipment company.

It was spun off from PSK Holdings in 2019 to run the front-end equipment business, while PSK Holdings, an affiliate, handles back-end equipment.

According to a report by Daily Invest, PR strip equipment accounts for about 70% of revenue, and the company operates subsidiaries in the United States, Taiwan, China and Japan.

PSK has commercialized Bevel Etcher equipment, which improves yield by removing wafer edges, and is widening its etching lineup with Etch back equipment that etches without lithography. Daishin Securities, in an April 2026 report, named rising global share in dry strip as the core growth driver.

The same report said new dry cleaning equipment could partly support higher average selling prices. Customers include Samsung Electronics and SK hynix as well as Intel and Micron.

In competition, Chinese-linked rivals are losing share outside China after being placed on a US trade restriction list, and analysts argue PSK can absorb that vacancy.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, 2023 was the trough. Revenue fell from KRW 460.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 351.9 billion in 2023, down 23.7% year on year, and operating profit fell from KRW 91.8 billion to KRW 54.1 billion, down 41.1%.

Revenue then rose for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025, reaching KRW 398.1 billion (up 13.1%) and KRW 457.2 billion (up 14.9%). Operating profit recovered to KRW 83.9 billion in 2024 (up 55.1%) and reached KRW 88.5 billion in 2025 (up 5.4%). Operating profit was largest in 2022 across 2022 to 2025.

The operating margin moved from 19.9% in 2022 to 15.4% in 2023, 21.1% in 2024 and 19.4% in 2025. In 2025, operating profit rose while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 0.7% to KRW 78.5 billion, and that figure was largest in 2024 at KRW 79.1 billion.

The debt-to-equity ratio, calculated as total liabilities divided by total equity, fell from 33.8% in 2022 to 19.8% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 539 billion and total liabilities of KRW 107 billion at year-end 2025.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 46.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 61.7 billion in 2025, peaking at KRW 67.2 billion in 2024. Quarterly revenue moved from KRW 104.1 billion in 2025 Q3 to KRW 142.7 billion in 2025 Q4, up 37.0% quarter on quarter, while operating profit slipped to KRW 21.9 billion, down 6.4%.

Operating profit then rose three consecutive quarters, to KRW 47.2 billion in 2026 Q1, KRW 50.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 67.7 billion in Q3, and revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from 2025 Q4 through 2026 Q3.

Korea Investment & Securities said in an April 2026 report that customers were pulling investment forward. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 148.9 billion.

Hana Securities had projected 2026 Q2 operating profit at KRW 50.2 billion, and the reported figure was KRW 50.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The common premise in broker research is that the semiconductor investment cycle is still in an upswing. In an April 2026 report, Daishin Securities projected that the semiconductor capex upcycle would extend into 2027.

The same report expected PSK's growth to exceed the growth rate of domestic memory front-end capex over the next two years. Daishin also expected the domestic semiconductor front-end equipment market to grow at an annual average of 22% over the same period.

Korea Investment & Securities noted that 2026 memory investment is centered on front-end processes, with conversion investment exceeding new investment in volume. DS Investment & Securities said NAND conversion investment is rising from the prior year and equipment spending is expected to continue through 2027.

By contrast, demand from China fell by more than 50% as local chip localization advanced. Chinese-linked rivals losing share outside China after being placed on a US trade restriction list is cited as a favorable factor for PSK. Analysts broadly view expanding front-end investment as the driver of equipment stock strength.

Outlook

Forward earnings reference points are broker estimates. Hana Securities projected 2026 revenue of KRW 660.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 183.6 billion in a June 5, 2026 report.

KB Securities, in a July 7, 2026 report, estimated 2026 revenue of KRW 678.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 184.6 billion, and projected record annual results.

KB Securities said new equipment revenue from capacity expansion, in addition to conversion investment by memory customers, would drive earnings, and expected a stepwise rise in quarterly profitability. Analysts argue that medium-term growth depends on applying new processes and diversifying overseas customers.

Daishin Securities expected growth to accelerate as NAND customer diversification emerges by late 2026. It also projected that Bevel Etcher share within existing customers could rise through 2030 on localization benefits. New dry cleaning equipment is cited as a possible driver of higher average selling prices. The next checkpoint is whether 2026 annual results reach the level of broker estimates.

08

Bull factors

Dry strip share and customer diversification

PSK is described as the global leader in dry strip equipment. In an April 2026 report, Daishin Securities said its global leadership position would become clearer. Growth could accelerate if NAND customer diversification emerges by late 2026. Its customer base spans Samsung Electronics, SK hynix, Intel and Micron, which analysts cite as a strength.

Conversion and capacity investment expansion

Expanding conversion and capacity investment at memory customers is the basis of the bullish case. KB Securities said in a July 7, 2026 report that new equipment revenue from capacity expansion would drive growth. Korea Investment & Securities noted that conversion volume exceeds new investment in 2026 memory spending. The same firm expected PSK to benefit as US-invested fabs exclude Chinese equipment.

Quarterly profit leverage

In Q3 2026, operating profit growth outpaced revenue growth. Operating profit has risen for three consecutive quarters since 2026 Q1. Hana Securities projected 2026 operating profit at KRW 183.6 billion, and KB Securities estimated KRW 184.6 billion. If these estimates hold, 2026 operating profit would be well above the KRW 88.5 billion reported for 2025.

Bear factors

Decline in China-bound demand

Demand from China fell by more than 50% as local chip localization advanced. Conversion investment from domestic customers offset that drop, but a structural shift in regional demand still weighs on revenue. The benefit from absorbing rivals' vacated share could change if US regulations shift. The lower China exposure is a variable to track separately from share gains.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Customer capex and the timing of equipment delivery and acceptance drive quarterly results. In 2025 Q4, revenue rose 37.0% quarter on quarter while operating profit fell 6.4%. Operating profit has risen three consecutive quarters since 2026 Q1, but whether that continues depends on the timing of capex execution.

Valuation burden and share price volatility

Intraday shares rose more than 24% on July 10, 2026 in a move reported by the press, reflecting large swings. Market capitalization far exceeds total equity, so a premium to net asset value is large, and a miss on earnings estimates could weigh on the multiple. Korea Investment & Securities said the valuation would need higher EPS growth to expand.

10

Risk factors

Customer capex cycle

If memory customers delay capex, equipment deliveries and acceptance could slip and quarterly results could swing. In 2025 Q4, revenue rose while operating profit fell, showing that quarterly profitability is not steady.

Because spending is concentrated in front-end processes, the business is sensitive to any signal of investment cuts.

Regional regulation and China demand

Demand from China fell by more than 50% because of localization. The benefit from excluding Chinese-linked rivals depends on the direction of US chip restrictions. If restrictions ease, Chinese-linked rivals could recover share.

Target price dispersion and share price volatility

Shares moved sharply, rising more than 24% intraday on July 10, 2026. Broker target prices range from KRW 160,000 at Hana Securities to KRW 280,000 at KB Securities, so a downward revision to earnings estimates could widen valuation gaps. Target prices are reference values based on future earnings estimates, not guaranteed levels.

Overall view

PSK is a front-end process equipment company described as holding the global lead in dry strip equipment. On a reported basis, revenue and operating profit rose for two consecutive years after the 2023 trough.

Revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from 2025 Q4 through 2026 Q3, and operating profit rose for three consecutive quarters from 2026 Q1. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 67.7 billion rose 189.4% year on year.

Bullish factors include conversion and capacity investment by memory customers, customer diversification, and target price increases by brokers. Bearish factors include weaker China-bound demand, quarterly swings tied to capex timing, and share price volatility alongside a large premium to net asset value.

Broker estimates put 2026 operating profit around KRW 183.6 billion to KRW 184.6 billion, but these are estimates rather than confirmed results. The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, due by the end of March 2027.
