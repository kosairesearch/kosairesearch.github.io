02

Business structure

ESteem was founded in 2004 as one of Korea's first model management agencies, changed its name to ESteem in 2017, and absorbed its subsidiary ESteem Entertainment through a merger in 2024. The business consists of branding content, general content and artist management, with the other segment being small.

Branding content means producing content for client companies that aims to strengthen brand identity or rebrand, built on intellectual property (IP) such as models and influencers.

By segment, 2024 sales were 61.6% branding content, 12.6% general content, 24.5% management and 1.3% other, according to a February 2026 IPO research note. Management operates on a pool of more than 300 artists, and artist IP is linked to content production.

Key references include the Seoul Fashion Week and Catwalk Festa productions and a number of global luxury brand projects. The IPO research note described this structure as a one-stop solution unique in the industry, a phrasing that reflects the issuer's own positioning.

New businesses include brand incubation: as of February 2026 the company had management contracts with 10 brands and was investing in two more (per a Newsis report).

At the regular shareholders meeting on March 31, 2026, the company added brand incubation and investment, and domestic and overseas distribution and import-export of fashion, beauty and lifestyle products to its business purpose.

Other listed companies in the same miscellaneous category run very different businesses, such as paper and education, so direct comparison with a content-and-management model is difficult.

04

Earnings analysis

Full-year 2025 revenue was KRW 35.2 billion, down 1.2% year on year, while operating profit rose 22.5% to KRW 2.46 billion, lifting the operating margin from 5.7% in 2024 to 7.0% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 51.6% from KRW 1.37 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.08 billion in 2025, and net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 2.08 billion. Revenue declined while profit increased, so profitability moved in a favorable direction.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 3.42 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.67 billion in 2025, so profit gains did not translate fully into cash generation.

The balance sheet improved, with the debt ratio falling from 76.6% in 2024 to 62.8% in 2025; at year-end 2025, total liabilities were KRW 9.67 billion and total equity was KRW 15.4 billion, of which equity attributable to controlling shareholders was also KRW 15.4 billion.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (2025 Q1 to 2025 Q4) totaled KRW 2.08 billion. In 2026 Q1, revenue was KRW 5.9 billion, the operating loss was KRW 568 million and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 452 million.

In 2026 Q2, revenue rose 46.6% quarter on quarter to KRW 8.64 billion, and the operating loss was KRW 100 million, a continued loss but a smaller one.

In 2026 Q3, revenue fell 23.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 6.65 billion, and the operating loss widened to KRW 1.77 billion and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders to KRW 1.84 billion.

A media report from October 2026 attributed the first-half operating loss largely to new business investment amid a weaker advertising and marketing market.

Separately, branding content revenue is recognized according to campaign awards and production schedules, and rising production and labor costs can limit the improvement in operating profit even when project revenue grows, which has been cited as a driver of quarterly volatility (per a Japost report).

05

Industry analysis

Demand from the downstream market is highly sensitive to brand marketing budgets, and an October 2026 report described the advertising and marketing market as weakening.

A February 2026 IPO research note argued that rising importance of brand value could expand demand for branding content, but this reflects a brokerage's industry view rather than a confirmed trend.

In fashion, there is demand for incubating domestic designer brands, and the company positions its model as a platform that combines brand sales, branding, content collaboration and cultural events (per an Edaily report).

Content production is won project by project, so changes in the industry show up in quarterly results quickly. Management revenue depends on how active the agency's talent is.

Where the industry sits in its cycle is hard to pin down: the weakening advertising market is a headwind, while the greater focus on brand value and the growing designer brand ecosystem are tailwinds.

The domestic listed peer group used as a valuation reference comes from the February 2026 IPO research note, and because those companies have very different business mixes, direct comparison is limited.

Overseas, the company signed a memorandum with a digital solutions firm that runs WeChat mini-shop commerce, aiming to spread K-fashion and K-content into China (October 2026 report).

Outlook

The outlook elements confirmed so far are business plans, including the planned use of IPO proceeds and overseas partnerships.

The company said in February 2026 that IPO funds would go to expanding investment in domestic designer brand incubation, opening a flagship store, taking Catwalk Festa overseas and setting up an E Studio office in New York (per a Newsis report).

An October 2026 report said the China memorandum covers cooperation in education, advertising and marketing, and commerce.

A February 2026 IPO research note projected 2026 revenue of KRW 39.0 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.5 billion; these are estimates from the time of the offering, and the direction of 2026 quarterly results disclosed so far differs from them.

The next regular report, the 2026 annual business report, will show the full-year 2026 operating margin and the fourth-quarter trend. Around March 2027, one year after the listing, the number of tradable shares is scheduled to rise to about 4.96 million under the schedule in the securities registration statement.

The CEO bought 42,389 shares on the market on November 6, 2026, raising the stake to 41.66% as of November 10, 2026, which is also relevant to share supply. The contribution of the China agreement and overseas expansion to earnings has not yet been disclosed.

The timing of when new-business investment costs fall amid a weaker advertising market will be a key variable for the timing of any profit recovery.

08

Bull factors

Profit growth and a stronger balance sheet in 2025

Operating profit rose 22.5% and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 51.6% in 2025, with the operating margin rising from 5.7% to 7.0%. The debt ratio fell from 76.6% to 62.8%. Profit rose even as revenue slipped 1.2%, pointing to improved profitability.

A branding content and artist IP structure

Branding content accounted for 61.6% of 2024 sales, and the management artist pool exceeds 300. The company holds IP-based references such as Seoul Fashion Week and Catwalk Festa. Its core is a structure in which content and artists grow together.

New business expansion and a higher CEO stake

Brand incubation is under way with 10 brands under management contracts and investments in two more. The China memorandum reported in October 2026 widens an overseas growth path. The CEO increased his stake to 41.66% through on-market purchases on November 6, 2026.

09

Bear factors

Three straight loss quarters and a wider loss in Q3

Losses have continued across the three quarters from 2026 Q1 to Q3. The operating loss in Q3 was KRW 1.77 billion, a wider loss than the prior quarter, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.84 billion. The fact that 2025 profit gains have not carried into 2026 quarterly results is a weakness.

Quarterly revenue volatility and a weaker advertising market

Revenue fell 23.0% quarter on quarter, from KRW 8.64 billion in Q2 to KRW 6.65 billion in Q3. Revenue recognition timing on projects causes large quarterly swings. A weaker advertising and marketing market adds uncertainty about the steadiness of project awards.

Share supply pressure and price volatility

On July 16, 2026, the stock was designated a one-day caution stock after a sharp closing-price rise. Tradable shares are set to increase through the one-year mark after listing, and media reports flagged the possibility of large share sales. Price swings are large, and supply factors weigh heavily.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility and cost structure

Project revenue is recognized according to awards and production timelines, which makes quarterly results volatile. If production and labor costs rise, profit improvement can be limited even when revenue grows. Continued new-business investment costs could extend the loss period.

Shareholder structure and tradable supply

Major shareholders are subject to lengthy lock-up periods, and tradable shares rise around the one-year mark after listing. The CEO's 41.66% stake means the free float is limited. Changes in share supply can act as a source of price swings.

Overseas expansion and new business execution

The contribution of the China agreement, the New York office and the overseas push for Catwalk Festa has not been disclosed. The timing of returns on brand incubation investments is uncertain. If the businesses fail to grow as expected, the investment burden could remain in the results.

Overall view

ESteem's disclosed 2025 results show higher operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders, a higher operating margin and a lower debt ratio, but losses have continued across the three quarters from 2026 Q1 to Q3.

The operating loss widened to KRW 1.77 billion in 2026 Q3, and revenue fell 23.0% quarter on quarter. The branding-content-led structure and artist IP are strengths, but the timing of project revenue recognition and the burden of labor and production costs make quarterly results volatile.

New businesses, including the China partnership and brand incubation, are expanding, but their contribution to earnings has not yet been disclosed.

On the supply side, the CEO's on-market purchase and the increase in tradable shares around the one-year mark after listing sit alongside a past caution-stock designation, which points to high volatility.

With strengths and weaknesses both present, the 2026 annual operating margin and 2027 Q1 results are the key points to confirm.
