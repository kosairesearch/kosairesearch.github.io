02

Business structure

Booster (008470) is a company focused on industrial boilers, listed on KOSDAQ on February 23, 2011. Its product lineup includes once-through boilers, vacuum boilers and non-pressure boilers, and according to WiseReport's 2025 data, domestic sales accounted for 96.21% of product revenue and exports for 3.79%.

Its service business, covering maintenance and parts, showed a 99.81% domestic share in the same year. Valueline puts the company's overall domestic share at 97.8%, so figures differ by data provider.

Main customers include construction companies, public institutions, hotels and commercial buildings across a wide range of industries. Valueline also says Booster holds about 30.3% of the industrial boiler market and remains the industry leader, though the source does not show the date of that estimate.

New businesses include heat pumps, AERCO boilers and air pollution control facilities, and a subsidiary is extending the company into environmental equipment.

At the March 23, 2026 annual general meeting, the articles of incorporation were amended to add constant temperature and humidity units and air handling units, with diversification given as the reason. According to WiseReport, R&D spending in 2025 equaled 0.71% of sales.

The company's competitive strengths rest on its leadership in industrial boilers and its base of construction and public-sector demand.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 91.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 101.1 billion in 2023, KRW 106.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 120.8 billion in 2025, increasing for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. Revenue in 2025 grew 13.7% from the prior year, making it the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

Operating loss was KRW 3.31 billion in 2022, and operating profit of KRW 1.54 billion in 2023 marked a swing back to profit. Operating profit fell 85.0% to KRW 232 million in 2024 and then rose 682.9% to KRW 1.81 billion in 2025.

Operating margin went from -3.6% in 2022 to 1.5% in 2023, dropped to 0.2% in 2024 and returned to 1.5% in 2025. Revenue kept growing in 2024 and 2025, yet margins stayed between 0.2% and 1.5%, so top-line growth has not translated directly into margin gains.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.45 billion in 2023 and KRW 1.69 billion in 2024, then fell 7.7% to KRW 1.56 billion in 2025. Annual net income equals the figure attributable to controlling shareholders in every year reported.

On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 posted revenue of KRW 31.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 995 million, but the fourth quarter turned to an operating loss of KRW 452 million.

The first quarter of 2026 showed an operating loss of KRW 1.03 billion, and the second quarter narrowed it to KRW 425 million. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 1.61 billion in the second quarter, larger than the operating loss, which points to non-operating items weighing on results.

The third quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 30.4 billion, operating profit of KRW 936 million and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.39 billion, all improving from the prior quarter, though revenue and operating profit were down 4.1% and 5.9% year on year.

At the end of 2025 the debt ratio was 25.4%, down from 33.6% in 2024, and total equity of KRW 80.1 billion was entirely attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow was KRW 4.48 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.98 billion in 2025. The disclosed figures alone do not pin down the specific causes of the quarterly swings.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for industrial boilers is described as being supported by replacement cycles in construction, public and commercial buildings. Valueline views the market as sensitive to economic cycles but stable because replacement demand is steady.

Valueline also says Booster holds about 30.3% of the industrial boiler market and ranks first in the sector. The business is domestically focused, and exports were only 3.79% of 2025 product revenue according to WiseReport, so the company is more exposed to domestic construction and facility investment.

That also means its growth path from overseas demand is relatively narrow. WiseReport classifies the company under metals rather than chemicals, which differs from the chemical classification used in this report.

The heat pump, AERCO boiler and air pollution control initiatives link to building energy and environmental equipment demand, widening the company's market scope.

The addition of constant temperature and humidity units and air handling units to the articles of incorporation fits the same direction, as the company explained it. The first-place position applies to industrial boilers only and cannot be used to describe its standing in other equipment fields.

A slowdown in construction reduces new installations, and how much replacement demand offsets that decline will show up in the quarterly revenue trend.

The public summary shows no numerical targets for new businesses or orders, so the company's goals read as directional.

On results, operating profit turned positive in the third quarter of 2026 after operating losses in the first and second quarters, so the operating trend from the fourth quarter onward is the next checkpoint.

The 2026 annual report will show the full-year operating margin and whether the new businesses contribute to revenue. Regular filings will also show from which period new business revenue begins to appear in results.

Bull factors

Operating profit recovery

Operating profit rose 682.9% to KRW 1.81 billion in 2025 from KRW 232 million in 2024. In the third quarter of 2026 operating profit of KRW 936 million reversed the prior quarter's loss, and revenue of KRW 30.4 billion rose 13.0% from the prior quarter. The combination of revenue recovery and earnings improvement is cited as the main bullish point.

Domestic base and replacement demand

Main customers include construction companies, public institutions, hotels and commercial buildings, and the business is predominantly domestic. Valueline says steady replacement demand provides market stability.

The roughly 30.3% share of the industrial boiler market, which Valueline places at first, is also cited as a business foundation.

Shareholder returns and disclosed plan

The company said its 2025 payout ratio was 41.44% and that it qualifies as a high-dividend company. The 2026 value enhancement plan includes maintaining an appropriate dividend level. The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 25.4%, lower than 33.6% in 2024.

Bear factors

Volatility in quarterly results

Booster posted operating losses of KRW 1.03 billion in the first quarter and KRW 425 million in the second quarter of 2026. The second-quarter net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.61 billion was larger than the operating loss, showing non-operating items moved results. The cumulative net loss over the last four quarters is KRW 265 million.

Thin margins and slowing growth

Operating margin was 0.2% in 2024 and 1.5% in 2025, both at low levels. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 7.7% in 2025 from the prior year. In the third quarter of 2026, revenue was down 4.1% and operating profit down 5.9% from a year earlier.

Dependence on domestic cycles

Exports are a small share of sales and demand relies on construction and public-sector orders, which makes the business exposed to slowdowns. Valueline also notes the market is sensitive to economic cycles. How far the new businesses can offset a slowdown in the core business is not yet visible in figures.

Risk factors

Construction and facility investment cycle

Main customers are construction companies, public institutions, hotels and commercial buildings, so construction activity and public orders can drive new installation volumes. Valueline highlights the market's sensitivity to economic cycles. Whether replacement demand cushions these swings will show in the quarterly revenue trend.

Execution of new businesses

It remains to be seen whether new businesses such as heat pumps, AERCO boilers and air pollution control facilities will convert into orders and profit, as few numerical disclosures are available. Recruiting sales talent and strengthening cost competitiveness are also execution tasks. Early-stage costs for staffing and investment could weigh on results first.

Earnings volatility and small market capitalization

Operating and net profit have changed sign from quarter to quarter, which makes it hard to judge how long annual profitability will last. In quarters when non-operating items weigh more, the net loss can exceed the operating loss. With a small market capitalization, share price volatility can also be large.

Overall view

Booster focuses on industrial boilers, and in 2025 revenue reached KRW 120.8 billion and operating profit KRW 1.81 billion, both up, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 7.7%. The operating margin of 1.5% in 2025 is low, after falling to 0.2% in 2024.

With a cumulative net loss of KRW 265 million over the last four quarters, the direction of quarterly results has not yet settled. Bullish factors include replacement demand in the domestic market, the first-place position in industrial boilers, the high-dividend policy and the 2026 value enhancement plan.

Bearish factors include dependence on construction and public-sector demand, thin margins, quarterly swings from non-operating items and execution risk in new businesses.

The debt ratio was 25.4% at the end of 2025, and total equity of KRW 80.1 billion is entirely attributable to controlling shareholders, so financial burden is modest. Valuation is framed by the fact that PER cannot be calculated while market capitalization sits well below the book value of total equity.

The next checkpoints are the full-year operating margin and fourth-quarter results in the 2026 annual report, and whether new business orders materialize.
