Revenue recovery meets a Q3 2026 loss and a trading halt

02

Business structure

Korea Computer Terminal was spun off from Korea Computer Holdings in 2002 and is mainly engaged in manufacturing and selling financial terminal systems and special-purpose terminal systems.

In automated equipment, it supplies smart ATMs, mini CD units and kiosks, while in finance it supplies integrated teller terminals for branch counters, integrated terminal solutions and card issuing machines.

In special-purpose terminals, airport systems and public-sector special applications are cited as the main end uses. The company's 2024 first-half earnings commentary said sales of product lines such as automated equipment and terminal systems rose sharply.

Some public disclosure coverage describes the company as an integrated logistics business, so the product category classification should be cross-checked against the business description in the periodic report.

In the December 2025 sales mix, product sales were reported as 100% domestic, while merchandise sales were 81.57% domestic and 18.43% export. The domestic share of service revenue was 98.97%. R&D spending was KRW 623 million in 2024, equal to 5.34% of sales, and KRW 685 million in 2023, equal to 4.24% of sales. Quarterly revenue has been uneven because it depends on order volume.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was reported at KRW 14.6 billion in 2022, KRW 16.1 billion in 2023 (up 10.9% year on year), KRW 11.7 billion in 2024 (down 27.7% year on year), and KRW 23.1 billion in 2025 (up 97.9% year on year). 2025 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest within 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.17 billion in 2023 (up 97.9%), fell to KRW 1.61 billion in 2024 (down 25.9%), and recovered to KRW 4.03 billion in 2025 (up 151.0%). The operating margin moved from 7.5% in 2022 to 13.4% in 2023, 13.8% in 2024 and 17.5% in 2025.

In 2024, when revenue declined, the operating margin still edged up from the prior year. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.77 billion in 2025, up 216.2% year on year.

The company cited higher order volume for product sales and an improved revenue structure as the main reasons for the 2025 change. By quarter, fourth-quarter 2025 revenue reached KRW 13.3 billion, up 334.3% from the prior quarter, so more than half of annual revenue fell into one quarter.

That quarter's operating profit was KRW 2.72 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.09 billion.

First-quarter 2026 revenue fell 80.1% quarter on quarter to KRW 2.64 billion, then rose to KRW 2.75 billion in the second quarter and KRW 3.63 billion in the third, marking two straight quarters of growth.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 750 million, up 318.3% from the prior quarter, but profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 345 million. The reason for the divergence between operating profit and shareholder net income is a point to confirm in the quarterly report notes.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (fourth quarter 2025 to third quarter 2026) totaled KRW 1.79 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 2.48 billion in 2022, KRW 1.83 billion in 2023 and KRW 484 million in 2024, before rising to KRW 3.84 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Korea Computer Terminal's core business is terminal equipment for bank branches and special-purpose public-sector terminals, so demand depends on branch equipment replacement and public-sector digital investment.

The company's 2024 first-half earnings commentary said industry in special-purpose terminals, including airport systems and public-sector applications, was expected to expand as technology innovation accelerated in public administration.

That line comes from an information service's outlook, and verified market size and share data are limited. The market classification (WI26) is IT hardware, so despite the IT and software sector label, its revenue and profit structure differ from software and service-led listed companies.

The company cited higher order volume as the reason for the 2025 improvement. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 3.63 billion was up 18.8% year on year, which indicates that orders are continuing to convert into sales.

Quarterly revenue has nevertheless swung sharply, from KRW 13.3 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.64 billion in the first quarter of 2026, reflecting timing effects in orders. R&D intensity rose from 4.24% of sales in 2023 to 5.34% in 2024.

A portfolio that supplies automated equipment, integrated terminal solutions and card issuing machines together can support repeat orders tied to equipment replacement cycles. Market size and competitor share data should be checked against the business section of the periodic report.

06

Outlook

The company's disclosed shareholder return plan calls for strengthening profit-focused management to raise earnings capacity and maintaining a dividend payout ratio of at least 40%. The payout ratio for fiscal 2025 was 49.52%, and the dividend amount for that year rose 33.3% from the year before.

The company said it qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act. On the earnings side, revenue rose in both the second and third quarters of 2026, but the third quarter showed a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders.

The exchange said it would decide whether the company is subject to a listing qualification review over the embezzlement complaint, and the outcome will determine whether the trading halt is lifted and what procedures follow. Whether the company is delisted has not been decided.

The fiscal 2026 annual report is generally due within 90 days after year end, so it is expected around late March 2027. The fourth-quarter 2025 results were announced on March 18, 2026, so the fourth-quarter 2026 results may follow a similar schedule.

First-quarter and second-quarter 2026 operating profit were each about KRW 180 million, so whether the third-quarter recovery to KRW 750 million continues is the next checkpoint. The dividend record date for fiscal 2025 was December 31.

08

Bull factors

Earnings recovery backed by orders

The company cited higher order volume for product sales and an improved revenue structure as the main reasons for the 2025 earnings change. Operating cash flow rose to KRW 3.84 billion in 2025 from KRW 484 million in 2024, showing earnings converting into cash. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was up 18.8% year on year.

Even with volatile orders, the earnings recovery appeared alongside stronger cash flow, which is the core bullish point.

Structural rise in operating margin

The operating margin rose from 7.5% in 2022 to 17.5% in 2025. In 2024, when revenue fell 27.7%, the margin still stood at 13.8%, above the prior year. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 750 million, up 240.5% year on year, suggests the margin improvement also showed up at the quarterly level.

A written shareholder return policy

In a disclosure dated March 26, 2026, the company said it would pursue profit-focused management and maintain a dividend payout ratio of at least 40%. The payout ratio for fiscal 2025 was 49.52%, and the dividend amount rose 33.3% from the year before. The company said it qualifies as a high-dividend company.

Because the policy is tied to profit rather than a fixed amount, it is a bullish feature on the shareholder return side.

Bear factors

Swing to a shareholder net loss in Q3 2026

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 345 million in the third quarter of 2026, reversing a profit in the same quarter of 2025. Operating profit in the same quarter rose to KRW 750 million, so the gap between the operating and shareholder levels is wide.

The cause is a point to confirm in the quarterly report notes. If the gap persists into later quarters, questions about earnings quality could grow.

Large swings in quarterly results

Fourth-quarter 2025 revenue of KRW 13.3 billion was up 334.3% from the prior quarter, while first-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 2.64 billion fell 80.1% quarter on quarter.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 1.79 billion, a large part of the KRW 2.77 billion reported for full-year 2025. When results concentrate in one quarter, the quarterly numbers alone make the trend difficult to read.

Operating profit base in the first half of 2026

Operating profit in the first and second quarters of 2026 was about KRW 180 million each, low compared with KRW 2.72 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025. It recovered to KRW 750 million in the third quarter, but whether that recovery holds beyond one quarter remains to be seen.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 2.75 billion was far below the fourth quarter of 2025, so whether order volume returns in concentrated batches is the key question.

Risk factors

Listing qualification and trading halt

The exchange said a listing qualification review trigger had occurred following the company's complaint, and the halt was set to last until the decision on whether the company is subject to review. If it is subject to review, deliberation by the corporate review committee would follow.

The exchange said delisting had not been decided. The timing and conditions for resuming trading remain uncertain, which is a trading risk.

Internal controls and fund management

The company disclosed that it had filed a complaint against a former team leader in its management support department on suspicion of embezzlement. The complaint concerns allegations and does not imply a final finding of guilt.

Even so, because the matter arose in a management support function, questions about internal controls and fund management remain. Whether recurrence-prevention measures and internal review results are disclosed is the next point to watch.

FX and export merchandise sales

In the December 2025 sales mix, merchandise sales were 81.57% domestic and 18.43% export. Export sales are translated into won, so the same shipment volume can produce different won-denominated revenue and profit as exchange rates move.

Given the export share within merchandise sales, the direction of the exchange rate is a variable to examine when interpreting profitability in the merchandise segment. How currency movements affect export margins should be checked against the cost structure in the periodic report.

Overall view

Korea Computer Terminal recovered sharply in 2025, with annual revenue of KRW 23.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.03 billion, and its operating margin rose from 7.5% in 2022 to 17.5% in 2025.

Revenue bottomed in the first quarter of 2026 and then grew in the second and third quarters, and third-quarter operating profit of KRW 750 million was up 240.5% year on year, which is a bullish signal.

On the other side, profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 345 million in the same quarter, and the concentration of revenue in the fourth quarter of 2025 adds quarterly volatility.

The trading halt and the listing qualification review over the embezzlement complaint create trading-side uncertainty separate from operating results. The target of a dividend payout ratio of at least 40% is a bullish feature for shareholder returns, but dividend amounts move with earnings.

The next checkpoints are the exchange's review decision, the reasons behind the divergence between annual and quarterly profit in the 2026 annual report, and the December 31, 2026 dividend record date. This text is for information only and does not contain an investment opinion.
