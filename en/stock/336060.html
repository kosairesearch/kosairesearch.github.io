02

Business structure

Wavus (336060) is a KOSDAQ-listed company that has built and operated national geospatial information systems. Its revenue falls into two groups: service revenue recognized over time from public system construction (SI) and maintenance (SM), and product revenue recognized at a point in time, such as equipment supply.

Independent Research R put 2025 over-time service revenue at KRW 54.23 billion, the core of the business. In the first half of 2026 service revenue rose 9.9% year on year to KRW 25.24 billion, while product revenue fell to KRW 640 million from KRW 8.87 billion a year earlier.

Major customers are public bodies including the National Health Insurance Service, Korea Land and Housing Corporation and Korea Airports Corporation. A maintenance contract with the National Health Insurance Service disclosed in May 2026 has a confirmed value of KRW 7.19 billion and runs from June 2026 to May 2028.

The product line now includes GeOnAI, a natural-language geospatial analysis agent; GeOnDT, a 3D digital twin platform; GeOnAirX, an urban air mobility control platform; GeOnCity, a smart-city administration package; and GeOnRoadX, an AI precision road map tool.

The company points to about two decades of experience in national geospatial projects as its strength. Heavy reliance on public-sector orders, however, remains a structural feature of the business.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 40.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 48.8 billion in 2023, KRW 56.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 70.2 billion in 2025. Revenue grew for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025 at 24.6% growth.

Operating profit was KRW 1.78 billion in 2022, KRW 5.06 billion in 2023 (up 183.6%), KRW 1.68 billion in 2024 (down 66.8%) and KRW 1.99 billion in 2025 (up 18.7%), with 2023 the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Revenue expanded, but the operating margin fell from 10.4% in 2023 to 3.0% in 2024 and 2.8% in 2025, so top-line growth did not carry through to profitability. Independent Research R attributed part of the first-half 2026 cost increase to hiring specialists for new businesses.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a net loss of KRW 8.09 billion in 2022 to a net profit of KRW 7.07 billion in 2023, then fell 53.9% to KRW 3.26 billion in 2024 and 15.8% to KRW 2.74 billion in 2025.

Net profit in 2023 exceeded operating profit, so non-operating items deserve attention in that year. Net profit including non-controlling interests is reported separately, and its annual amounts sit at similar levels to the controlling-shareholder figures.

Operating cash flow grew from KRW 4.64 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.64 billion in 2025, a faster rise than profit. The debt-to-equity ratio declined from 47.2% in 2022 to 32.8% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 14 billion against total equity of KRW 42.8 billion at the end of 2025.

Quarterly, revenue reached KRW 22.4 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (up 44.2% from the prior quarter) with operating profit of KRW 3.23 billion. In the first quarter of 2026 revenue fell 49.9% to KRW 11.2 billion and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 2.13 billion.

The second quarter brought revenue of KRW 15.2 billion and an operating loss of KRW 886 million, which narrowed. The third quarter returned to operating profit of KRW 1.87 billion on revenue of KRW 19.2 billion, up 23.3% from a year earlier.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totals KRW 1.99 billion, so quarterly swings remain large.

05

Industry analysis

Wavus's main market is public-sector geospatial construction and maintenance. Independent Research R notes that public SI work is seasonal, with orders and revenue recognition concentrated in the second half of the year.

The National Geospatial Information Integrated Platform (K-GeoP) provides 1,242 standardized spatial datasets for administrative work and opens 686 of them to the private sector, which may widen demand for spatial data among government and private users.

The company is adding AI to automate precision road map production, urban administration analysis and conversational spatial analysis. In urban air mobility, analysts have described its GeOnAirX platform as an extension of 2D cadastral GIS work into real-time management of 3D urban airspace.

Autonomous-driving precision road maps are also cited as a possible new demand source. The company's stated edge combines hands-on experience in national geospatial systems with specialized technologies in digital twins, UAM and drone response.

Because public references matter in bidding, public procurement flows largely determine its competitive position.

06

Outlook

The company has not issued formal annual revenue guidance, so the outlook rests on contract wins and product timelines.

Independent Research R's report dated October 1, 2026 estimated 2026 core revenue of about KRW 57.9 billion or more, based on the existing backlog, recurring revenue and the seasonality of second-half public SI work. The same report said KRW 17.71 billion of backlog is expected to be recognized in the second half.

The company disclosed a July 2026 win for a lift-type digital vertiport platform from Korea Airports Corporation and a September 2026 win for a regional industry information system from the Korea Institute for Industrial Economics and Trade.

In November 2026 it unveiled GeOnRoadX, an AI precision road map solution, and said it plans to add automatic detection of road facilities, potholes and surface cracks. A city safety AI model is being co-developed with KAIST and Datamaker.

The company also showed an integrated anti-drone control platform in October 2026 and is pursuing navigation-signal shadow-zone technology for urban air mobility with DGIST. After overseas buyer talks at a September 2026 smart city expo, the company said it will expand into global smart city and spatial AI platforms.

It has also described a staged shareholder return policy linked to profitability improvements. Whether operating margin recovers in the next annual report is the next checkpoint for this outlook.

08

Bull factors

Backlog surge and revenue visibility

Disclosed backlog rose 107% from KRW 16.94 billion at the end of 2025 to KRW 35.12 billion at the end of June 2026. Independent Research R estimated 2026 core revenue at about KRW 57.9 billion or more based on that backlog and existing contracts. The KRW 17.71 billion expected to be recognized in the second half is cited as a basis for revenue visibility.

Gross margin improvement from higher service mix

Gross margin in the first half of 2026 rose 5.7 percentage points to 26.7% from 21.0%, as lower-margin equipment sales shrank and over-time service revenue expanded. Service revenue reached KRW 25.24 billion, up 9.9% year on year. Improved earnings structure despite a lower top line in the period is cited as a positive factor.

AI transition strategy and debt-free balance sheet

In August 2026 the company set out a strategy to move from an SI-centered model to an AI platform business, with GeOnAI, GeOnDT, GeOnAirX, precision road maps and anti-drone technology as expansion lines. As of June 2026 it reported no bank borrowings and net financial assets of KRW 39.43 billion. Total liabilities stood at KRW 14 billion against total equity of KRW 42.8 billion at the end of 2025.

Bear factors

Downward trend in operating margin

The operating margin fell from 10.4% in 2023 to 3.0% in 2024 and 2.8% in 2025. Operating profit stayed at KRW 1.99 billion in 2025 even though revenue rose 24.6%. Independent Research R attributed part of the first-half cost increase to specialist hiring. Whether top-line growth converts into margin recovery is the central question on the bearish side.

Volatility in quarterly results

In the first quarter of 2026 revenue was KRW 11.2 billion with an operating loss of KRW 2.13 billion, and the second quarter still posted an operating loss of KRW 886 million. The third quarter's operating profit of KRW 1.87 billion is a single quarter's result.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders totals KRW 1.99 billion over the latest four quarters. Quarterly results appear sensitive to contract recognition timing and equipment sales.

Dependence on public orders and execution risk of transition

Revenue centers on orders from public bodies such as the National Health Insurance Service and Korea Land and Housing Corporation. Public SI work is seasonal and concentrated in the second half, which widens quarterly variation.

The AI platform shift and overseas expansion have been outlined, but their revenue contribution is still limited. No public timeline has been set for commercializing UAM and precision road map businesses.

10

Risk factors

Public budget and procurement risk

Most of the business depends on public procurement, so changes in budgets or tender schedules could delay revenue recognition. The National Health Insurance Service contract runs to May 2028, while renewal terms beyond that are not yet known. Even a large backlog offers less visibility if execution or recognition slips.

Execution risk of the business transition

The AI platform transition announced in August 2026 requires hiring specialists and investing in product development. As Independent Research R noted, these costs can weigh on near-term operating margin. Whether new products convert into orders and revenue quickly enough to offset the spending is the key test.

Quarterly earnings swings and no dividend

After operating losses in the first and second quarters of 2026, the third quarter returned to profit, but whether that direction will persist is not yet established. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders declined for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025. No dividend is currently paid, and the shareholder return policy is under staged review tied to profitability.

Overall view

Wavus is a geospatial company whose revenue rests on public-sector SI and maintenance work, and revenue grew for three consecutive years to KRW 70.2 billion in 2025.

On the positive side, disclosed backlog rose 107% to KRW 35.12 billion by the end of June 2026, the first-half gross margin improved to 26.7%, and the company reported no bank borrowings with net financial assets of KRW 39.43 billion.

On the negative side, the operating margin fell from 10.4% in 2023 to 2.8% in 2025, and although the third quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit, profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totals only KRW 1.99 billion.

Dependence on public procurement and the execution burden of the AI platform shift deserve equal weight. On valuation, the price-to-book ratio below 1x points to asset backing, while the small earnings base makes earnings multiples volatile, so the two lenses point in different directions.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, which will show whether order growth is reaching profit.
