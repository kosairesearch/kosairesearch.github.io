02

Business structure

Tokai Carbon Korea makes consumable parts for semiconductor processes based on high-purity graphite and SiC (silicon carbide) coating technology.

The company was founded in 1996 as a joint venture in which Japan's Tokai Carbon and Korea's KC Tech each held 50%, and Tokai Carbon is now the largest shareholder with a 52.63% stake as of August 31, 2026.

Based on Q2 2026 sales, the Solid SiC segment accounted for about 90%, semiconductor-use products about 7%, and Susceptor products for LED and semiconductor parts about 3%. Its core SiC focus ring is used in etching, and a September 2025 Shinyoung Securities report estimated it at about 80% of revenue.

The same report put memory-related sales at about 99% of revenue, with NAND at about 70%. A 2020 IBK Securities report named Lam Research and Applied Materials as main customers, which points to a structure heavily tied to etcher makers.

A August 2025 news report put China's share of sales below 30%, and in March 2026 Kiwoom Securities said new Chinese customers and entry into the HBM market had begun to show up in its outlook.

On the production side, utilization stayed high at 96.2% in the first half of 2026, and Meritz Securities said the company has reportedly ordered equipment to expand existing plants.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results peaked in 2022, fell sharply in 2023, and then rose for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025. Revenue dropped from KRW 319.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 226.7 billion in 2023 (down 29.1% year on year), then recovered to KRW 275.7 billion in 2024 (up 21.7%) and KRW 301.3 billion in 2025 (up 9.3%).

Operating profit fell from KRW 127 billion in 2022 to KRW 66.7 billion in 2023 (down 47.5%), then rose to KRW 80.7 billion in 2024 (up 21.1%) and KRW 83.9 billion in 2025 (up 3.9%).

Operating margin, however, declined every year, from 39.8% in 2022 to 29.4% in 2023, 29.3% in 2024 and 27.8% in 2025, so revenue recovery did not translate into margin recovery.

Attributable net income was KRW 94.1 billion in 2022, KRW 61.2 billion in 2023 (down 34.9%), KRW 72 billion in 2024 (up 17.6%) and KRW 69.9 billion in 2025 (down 2.9%), so profit slipped in 2025 even as sales and operating profit grew. Net income including non-controlling interests was filed at the same yearly values.

Operating cash flow was KRW 87.3 billion in 2022, KRW 21 billion in 2023, KRW 74.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 54.1 billion in 2025. At the end of 2025 the debt-to-equity ratio stood at 9.3%, with total liabilities of KRW 48.3 billion against total equity of KRW 519.3 billion.

On a quarterly basis, Q4 2025 revenue fell 10.3% from the prior quarter to KRW 71.1 billion, then rebounded to KRW 95.4 billion in Q1 2026 (up 34.2%). Q3 2026 revenue reached KRW 110.1 billion, with operating profit of KRW 30.2 billion and attributable net income of KRW 23 billion.

Attributable net income peaked at KRW 25.1 billion in Q1 2026, then declined for two consecutive quarters to KRW 23.7 billion in Q2 and KRW 23 billion in Q3. The sum of attributable net income over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, was KRW 89 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The company's main demand source is NAND flash memory. In a November 11, 2026 report, Meritz Securities expected the share of NAND produced at 300 layers or more to rise from 6% at the end of 2026 to 15% at the end of 2027, as domestic and Japanese NAND makers convert investment to higher layer counts.

The same report said the company's NAND exposure is about 70%. It also argued that the full operation of new DRAM fabs in 2027 and 2028 would add to SiC demand and contribute to consolidated earnings growth.

In March 2026, Kiwoom Securities said the effect of entering the HBM market had started to be reflected in its numbers. Exposure to front-end equipment makers is another variable.

In June 2025, Kiwoom Securities judged that Lam Research's market share would expand as its cryogenic etch equipment supplier changed from TEL to Lam Research. This shows that demand shifts with the equipment share of key customers.

On the cycle, the main focus is on the recovery of the NAND market and the resumption of investment. Unlike Soulbrain and Dongjin Semichem, which center on chemical materials, the company focuses on graphite and SiC parts, so it sits directly in the path of memory investment cycles.

06

Outlook

In its April 2025 corporate value-up plan, the company set targets of average annual sales growth of at least 6% over 2025 to 2027, return on invested capital of at least 25%, and a dividend payout ratio of at least 20%.

According to the first-half 2026 semiannual report, the new facility investment had been scheduled for completion by October 2026, but the schedule was revised because of permit delays at the developer. A October 30, 2026 news report said the company expects land-related investment to be finished within this year.

Meritz Securities expects production from the additional capacity to begin contributing in mid-2027. The same report estimated 2027 revenue of KRW 522.3 billion, up 26%, operating profit of KRW 163.5 billion, up 39%, and an operating margin of 31%. Meritz put the Q3 2026 operating margin at 27%.

At the March 2026 annual shareholders meeting, the 31st fiscal-year financial statements and a cash dividend were approved. Both the company's targets and broker estimates assume profit expansion after capacity is completed.

If expansion slips or demand recovery is delayed, the timing of this outlook could also move back. If utilization stays high, the effect of added capacity could be larger. Progress on this outlook can be checked step by step through quarterly results and investment-related disclosures.

08

Bull factors

Rising demand from NAND layer upgrades

In a November 11, 2026 report, Meritz Securities projected the share of NAND at 300 layers or more to rise from 6% at the end of 2026 to 15% at the end of 2027. With NAND exposure at about 70%, the company sits directly in this shift. The same report expects new capacity to start contributing in mid-2027.

High utilization and expansion need

First-half 2026 utilization was 96.2%, above 95% in the first half of 2025. Meritz Securities said strong front-end demand has made capacity expansion more urgent, and that the company has reportedly ordered equipment to expand existing plants. Q3 2026 operating profit rose 35.0% year on year to KRW 30.2 billion.

Expansion of demand into DRAM and HBM

Meritz Securities analyzed that the full operation of new DRAM fabs in 2027 and 2028 would raise SiC usage and contribute to earnings growth. In March 2026, Kiwoom Securities said the effect of entering the HBM market had started to appear in its estimates. Bulls point to this as a broadening of memory demand beyond a NAND-centered structure.

09

Bear factors

Concentration in memory and NAND

According to a September 2025 Shinyoung Securities report, memory-related sales account for about 99% of revenue and NAND for about 70%. Shifts in market share at major customers such as Lam Research also affect demand.

In June 2025, Kiwoom Securities said concerns over Lam Research's share decline had long weighed on the company's valuation.

Downward trend in operating margin

Operating margin fell every year from 39.8% in 2022 to 29.4% in 2023 and 27.8% in 2025. Attributable net income declined 2.9% in 2025. After peaking at KRW 25.1 billion in Q1 2026, it fell for two consecutive quarters, so sales growth has not yet translated into higher net profit.

Repeated delays to expansion timing

The investment period for the new site was extended again because of permit delays at the developer. In its August 2026 semiannual report, the company had said the investment would be completed by October 2026, but the schedule was revised.

Meritz Securities expects new capacity to contribute only from mid-2027, so supply could come later than demand.

10

Risk factors

Industry cycle and customer concentration risk

Most sales depend on the memory cycle, so a cut in NAND investment could shift demand quickly. Parts demand also changes with the share of the etch equipment makers that use the company's products.

China's sales share is below 30%, and expanding Chinese customers is a growth factor, but demand volatility in that region also needs to be weighed.

Execution risk on expansion and investment

Land acquisition and permit timing have been adjusted twice, pushing back the point at which new capacity comes online. With utilization at 96.2% and little spare capacity, delays could limit the company's ability to take on additional orders. Conversely, overinvestment could raise fixed-cost burdens if demand weakens.

Shareholder structure and trading volatility

Tokai Carbon Co., Ltd. held 52.63% as of August 31, 2026, which leaves a free float of about 47%. Foreign ownership stood at 66.54%. The 52-week beta of 1.58 indicates larger price swings than the market, and shifts in foreign flows can have a strong effect on the share price.

Overall view

Tokai Carbon Korea is a materials and parts supplier whose SiC focus rings are tied to NAND-centered memory demand. Its 2025 results showed revenue of KRW 301.3 billion, operating profit of KRW 83.9 billion and attributable net income of KRW 69.9 billion, recovering from the 2023 low.

Q3 2026 revenue reached KRW 110.1 billion, up 38.9% year on year, with operating profit of KRW 30.2 billion. Against that, operating margin fell from 39.8% in 2022 to 27.8% in 2025, and attributable net income has declined for two consecutive quarters since Q1 2026.

Utilization of 96.2% and the mid-2027 capacity contribution cited by Meritz Securities are the main bullish factors. Memory concentration, extended expansion timelines and margin decline are the main bearish factors.

The next checkpoints are completion of the new-site investment, preliminary Q4 2026 results, and the operating margin trend in the 2026 annual report.
