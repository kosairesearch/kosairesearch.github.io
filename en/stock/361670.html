02

Business structure

Samyoung S&C was founded in 2000 as a sensor specialist and listed on KOSDAQ in May 2021. It began as a spin-off from Samyoung Electronics, a separate listed company.

The company says it performs the full process from material synthesis and MEMS semiconductor processing to mass production of chip-type temperature and humidity sensors. Its product line covers chip-type temperature and humidity sensors, pressure sensors, chilled-mirror dew-point meters and relative humidity sensors.

A JobKorea product-mix aggregate shows chip-type temperature and humidity sensors at about 36%, pressure sensors at about 31%, dew-point meters at about 16% and relative humidity sensors at about 15%, but the aggregate does not state its period.

A 2025 press report put dew-point meters at about 10% of 2024 revenue, a gap that likely reflects different measurement bases. The company says it has dew-point meter references at all three major Korean battery makers.

Its 2026 disclosure of September describes continued supply of high-precision automotive components and pressure sensors, and a global ODM contract for HumiChip. Before listing, the company counted global firms such as Honeywell, Philips and Ford among its customers, according to a 2022 report.

Against larger component makers such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek, the company is a small specialist focused on environmental sensing. As of September 2026, it had 52 National Pension subscribers.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 13.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.6 billion in 2023, a 14.6% decline, recovered to KRW 12.4 billion in 2024 with a 6.4% increase, and then dropped to KRW 11 billion in 2025, down 11.4%. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year by revenue and 2025 the smallest.

The operating loss ran at KRW 1.27 billion in 2022, KRW 3.54 billion in 2023, KRW 3.12 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.7 billion in 2025, a continuous loss streak across the four years from 2022 to 2025. The operating margin moved from -9.3% in 2022 to -15.5% in 2025.

In its 2025 annual disclosure, the company cited a higher share of new products, a lower R&D spend (down from 19% to 14% of revenue) and cost-improvement activities as the main drivers.

Gross profit rose from KRW 1.06 billion in 2024 to KRW 2.88 billion in 2025, suggesting cost improvement was central to the narrower operating loss. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed from KRW 3.36 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.13 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow went from KRW -82.93 million in 2022 to KRW -2.15 billion in 2025, a larger outflow. On a quarterly basis, the operating loss was KRW 585 million in Q3 2025, KRW 299 million in Q4 2025 and KRW 0.57 million in Q1 2026, then widened again to KRW 407 million in Q2 2026 and KRW 404 million in Q3 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned positive at KRW 237 million in Q1 2026, then posted losses of KRW 264 million in Q2 and KRW 713 million in Q3. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 3.14 billion rose 18.7% year on year, yet the higher sales did not translate into a smaller net loss.

The debt ratio of 15.0% at end-2025 is low, so the key question is profit recovery rather than balance sheet strain.

05

Industry analysis

End demand comes from battery plants, automotive electronics and industrial measurement markets. In a 2022 company analysis, the firm said it would focus on high-value industrial segments and target medical, industrial and electric vehicle markets to drive revenue growth.

In the battery segment, chilled-mirror dew-point meters are used to manage extremely dry plant environments. A 2025 press report said a wireless sensor project for North American battery plants had moved into full-scale rollout.

The same report said the company planned to set up a U.S. subsidiary by the second half of 2026 to handle supply and maintenance in North America. A 2026 press report, however, said the company was adjusting its investment timing because investment by downstream industries had been delayed.

In sensors, the company stresses in-house materials and MEMS processing as a differentiator, since these largely determine cost and quality. Peers such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek are much larger component makers, so they are of limited use as direct benchmarks.

The company is a small-cap on KOSDAQ, so changes in demand from its customers tend to reach its results quickly.

06

Outlook

In a 2025 press report, the company projected the dew-point meter business at about 10% of revenue in 2024, about 20% in 2025 and 30% to 40% in 2026. These are management targets that show room for improvement in the business mix, not guaranteed results.

The company said it entered a ramp-up phase after securing references at all three major Korean battery makers. In North America, a wireless sensor project and a local subsidiary are being pursued together.

IPO proceeds were earmarked for production equipment, factory land and R&D, but only KRW 2.28 billion had been spent by end-2025. The company has said it is adjusting investment timing because downstream investment was delayed.

Q3 2026 revenue rose to KRW 3.14 billion, which may reflect dew-point meters, pressure sensors and HumiChip ODM supply together, but quarterly revenue has also declined, as in Q2 2026. The next checkpoints are whether the dew-point meter share and the operating margin move in the annual reports. No formal company guidance has been confirmed.

Bull factors

Dew-point meter references at battery makers

The company said in a 2025 press report that it has dew-point meter references at all three major Korean battery makers. The same report projected the dew-point meter share rising from about 10% in 2024 to 30% to 40% in 2026. A wireless sensor project for North American battery plants has also moved forward. Whether this translates into actual revenue share is a question for the business section of the annual report.

Narrowing operating loss and higher gross profit

The operating loss narrowed from KRW 3.12 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.7 billion in 2025. Gross profit rose from KRW 1.06 billion to KRW 2.88 billion over the same period, and the company cited a higher share of new products and cost improvement. The Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 404 million was smaller than a year earlier.

Low debt burden

At end-2025, total liabilities were KRW 2.85 billion and the debt ratio was 15.0%, small relative to total equity of KRW 19 billion. Since 2022, the debt ratio has ranged from 12.6% to 15.9%. The financial capacity is there, but whether it supports profit recovery depends on the next few quarters of earnings.

Bear factors

Continued operating losses and wider net loss

Operating losses have run for five consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 through Q3 2026. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 713 million in Q3 2026, a wider loss than a year earlier.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders have continued for two quarters since Q2 2026, and the four-quarter total is KRW 868 million.

Revenue decline

Annual revenue fell from KRW 13.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 11 billion in 2025. A rebound to KRW 12.4 billion in 2024 was reversed in 2025. A 2025 press report described revenue as stagnant with continued operating losses. Quarterly revenue fell to KRW 2.76 billion in Q2 2026 from the prior quarter.

Investment delays and cash outflow

Against the facility investment plan in the IPO registration statement, actual spending reached only KRW 2.28 billion by end-2025. The next checkpoint is whether the operating cash flow deficit narrows.

Risk factors

Downstream investment delay

If investment by battery and automotive customers slows, the ramp-up of new businesses such as dew-point meters could be delayed. The company has said it is adjusting investment timing because downstream investment has been delayed.

Because revenue depends on the capital spending of a concentrated customer base, order gaps can quickly show up in results.

Cash flow and fund deployment

The operating cash outflow widened to KRW 2.15 billion in 2025 from KRW 82.93 million in 2022. With IPO fund deployment running behind plan, the pace of executing the capital plan is a challenge.

If profitability does not turn positive for longer, funding equipment and R&D from internal cash flow becomes a heavier burden.

Ownership structure and free float

Largest shareholder-related parties hold 52.38% and the free float ratio is 45.15% (as of October 14, 2026). With a limited float, price moves can be larger depending on trading conditions. Because market capitalization is small, the volatility typical of small-caps also needs to be considered.

Overall view

Samyoung S&C is a sensor specialist that narrowed its 2025 operating loss to KRW 1.7 billion and lifted gross profit to KRW 2.88 billion, showing improvement on the cost side.

However, it recorded losses for four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025, and Q3 2026 still showed an operating loss of KRW 404 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 713 million. The four-quarter total of that net loss is KRW 868 million.

Positives include dew-point meter references at all three major Korean battery makers and a low debt ratio of 15.0% at end-2025. Negatives include the revenue decline, slow IPO fund deployment and a wider operating cash outflow.

The share price trades above book net asset value in a range where the price-to-earnings ratio cannot be calculated, so whether operating results turn to profit is the key open question.
