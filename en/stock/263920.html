02

Business structure

HuM&C is a healthcare packaging and materials specialist within the Huons Group. It reports two segments, glass and cosmetics. The glass segment is led by ampoules and cartridges used in anesthetics, and higher output efficiency at its Vietnamese production base, HuM&C Vina, has lifted sales.

In Q3 2026 glass sales were KRW 9.0 billion, up 10% year on year, out of total revenue of KRW 15.0 billion. The cosmetics segment reflects overseas customers' finished-product orders as K-beauty spreads, and its Q3 2026 sales were KRW 6.0 billion, up 15% year on year.

Export sales in Q3 2026 rose about 30% year on year. The Vietnam plant is in Hung Yen Province, covers 14,876 square meters, and can produce 60 million vials and 90 million cartridges a year. The plant was completed in December 2024, and its production lines began full operation in the second quarter of 2025.

The company is described as a small but strong firm with a one-stop line that compounds and produces NBR-based materials in-house. Although it sits in the same machinery and equipment sector, its customers and cost structure differ from listed peers that make robots or construction machinery. It competes with domestic and foreign makers of medical and cosmetic packaging components.

04

Earnings analysis

HuM&C's reported results peaked in 2023 and then declined in 2024 and 2025. Revenue rose from KRW 35.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 48.4 billion in 2023 (up 35.9% year on year), eased to KRW 47.2 billion in 2024 (down 2.5%), and reached KRW 52.3 billion in 2025 (up 10.8%), the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 window.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.77 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.84 billion in 2023 (up 174.1%) and KRW 4.95 billion in 2024 (up 2.2%), then fell to KRW 2.48 billion in 2025 (down 49.8%).

The operating margin moved from 5.0% in 2022 to 10.0% in 2023 and 10.5% in 2024 before dropping to 4.7% in 2025, so the margin fell in the year revenue grew most.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 798 million in 2022 to KRW 10.3 billion in 2023 (up 1,191.3%), the peak, then fell to KRW 3.4 billion in 2024 (down 67.0%) and KRW 1.57 billion in 2025 (down 53.8%), declining for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts in each of those years. Net profit in 2023 well exceeded operating profit of KRW 4.84 billion, which points to non-operating items that the next annual report's income statement should clarify.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 45.5 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, total liabilities were KRW 16.3 billion, and the debt ratio was 35.8%. Operating cash flow was KRW 2.07 billion in 2022, KRW 6.14 billion in 2023, KRW 4.91 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.96 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 13.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 730 million, and Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 13 billion (down 2.9% quarter on quarter) with operating profit of KRW 464 million (down 36.3%) marked the low point.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 14.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 823 million (up 77.2%), and Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 15.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.24 billion, the highest quarterly operating profit in the reported window.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 15 billion (down 4.4% quarter on quarter, up 11.9% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 1.02 billion (down 18.0% quarter on quarter, up 39.8% year on year).

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 94.17 million, down 90.4% quarter on quarter and 85.8% year on year, which the company attributed to foreign-currency translation losses as the won weakened.

Over the last four reported quarters, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 2.62 billion. The company described Q1 2026 as its best quarterly result on record, citing better cost ratios, demand for vials used in health functional foods, and the start of Vietnam production.

05

Industry analysis

HuM&C's end demand comes from three areas: pharmaceutical packaging, health functional foods and cosmetics. In June 2026 the company said higher sales of health functional foods had lifted demand for vial containers.

The same explanation pointed to rising demand for prefilled syringes as the cosmetics and beauty market expands. Anesthetic demand has also grown at home and abroad, supporting ampoule and cartridge sales in the glass segment.

K-beauty's reach has widened from Asia to the United States and Europe, lifting finished-product orders from overseas customers. However, demand rests on three drivers, health functional foods, anesthetics and K-beauty, so a slowdown in any one could weigh on total volume.

Public industry indicators that would show whether the cycle is near a peak or a trough are not easily linked to this company's results. On competition, the company has stressed price competitiveness from its Vietnamese base.

Export sales rising about 30% in Q3 2026 shows a growing overseas share of revenue, but it also means overseas receivables are exposed to currency moves. The company has said it will keep looking for cooperation opportunities with global pharmaceutical and biotech companies.

Among listed firms in the same machinery and equipment sector, companies that make robots or construction machinery serve different demand bases than HuM&C, which supplies medical and cosmetic components.

06

Outlook

The near-term items to watch are the Vietnam expansion and how its funding is used. On November 12, 2026, the company disclosed that it will extend the maturity of the existing USD 5.1 million loan to its subsidiary HuM&C Vina and make a new USD 700,000 loan.

The funds are planned for facility upgrades and expansion, to meet supply demand from new customers. The Vietnam plant already has annual capacity of 60 million vials and 90 million cartridges and is now moving toward further expansion.

Under a June 2026 board resolution, the company will buy back KRW 1.0 billion of its shares through a trust agreement with Samsung Securities, over six months through December 11, 2026. Reports say the planned purchase is 315,493 shares, which would lift treasury stock from 0.01% to 3.6% of shares outstanding.

In February 2026 the company paid a cash dividend for the first time since its founding, and the dividend decision at the next annual general meeting will show whether the return policy continues.

The chief executive has said the company aims to strengthen its market position through balanced growth across businesses and to develop new products. In the next annual report, the key item to watch is how much of the Vietnam capacity shows up in glass segment sales and operating margin.

The cosmetics segment has stayed profitable since turning a profit in Q2 2026, but its cumulative profit record is still short. Q4 2026 results will be disclosed in the 2026 annual report.

Bull factors

Vietnam production base and glass demand

The Vietnam plant, HuM&C Vina, was completed in December 2024 and has annual capacity of 60 million vials and 90 million cartridges. Q3 2026 glass sales were KRW 9.0 billion, up 10% year on year, supported by higher anesthetic demand for ampoules and cartridges.

In November 2026 the company extended a loan to the Vietnam subsidiary and approved a new one, signaling its expansion plans.

Recovery in quarterly operating profit

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 464 million in Q4 2025 and then rose to KRW 823 million in Q1 2026, KRW 1.24 billion in Q2 and KRW 1.02 billion in Q3. Q3 2026 operating profit was up 39.8% year on year, with revenue and operating profit growing together.

The company attributed the Q1 2026 result to better cost ratios and higher demand for vials used in health functional foods.

Start of shareholder returns

In June 2026 its board approved a KRW 1.0 billion share buyback, to be executed through a trust agreement with Samsung Securities until December 11, 2026. The company describes the buyback as a sign of its commitment to shareholder value.

09

Bear factors

Profit decline and low margin in 2025

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders declined for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025. Profit fell even though revenue reached KRW 52.3 billion, the largest year in the window, and that is the starting point of the bearish case.

Sharp drop in Q3 net profit

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 94.17 million in Q3 2026, down 85.8% year on year. The company cited foreign-currency translation losses as the won weakened. Operating improvement and net profit moved in different directions, so the quality of earnings needs closer scrutiny.

Short profitability record in cosmetics

In Q1 2026 cosmetics sales were KRW 4.6 billion, down 10% year on year, and the loss narrowed by 43%. The segment turned profitable in Q2 2026 and stayed profitable in Q3, but its cumulative profit record is short. Because it relies on overseas finished-product orders, profit may swing with order volumes.

10

Risk factors

Currency and translation losses

Loans to the Vietnam subsidiary are denominated in dollars, so currency moves can create translation losses. In Q3 2026 a sharp fall in the won increased such losses and sharply reduced net profit attributable to controlling shareholders. If loan balances grow with expansion, the same volatility could increase.

Demand concentration and utilization of new capacity

The glass segment depends on anesthetic and health functional food demand, and the cosmetics segment on overseas finished-product orders. If demand in either area weakens or new customers are slow to arrive, utilization of the expanded capacity could fall. How far utilization feeds into results after the investment is a key point to monitor.

Small-cap characteristics and trading conditions

With a small market capitalization, trading volume can be thin and share price volatility can be large. As a member of the Huons Group, group-level transactions and support could affect results and the share price. The buyback period and the share price trend should be reviewed together.

Overall view

HuM&C's reported net profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked in 2023 and declined in 2024 and 2025, and the 4.7% operating margin in 2025 was the lowest in the reported window.

Quarterly operating profit recovered through 2026, reaching KRW 823 million in Q1, KRW 1.24 billion in Q2 and KRW 1.02 billion in Q3. Both the glass and cosmetics segments grew and overseas exports rose, but Q3 2026 net profit fell sharply on currency translation losses.

The Vietnam plant has production capacity and its expansion funding is moving forward, which broadens the operating base. Shareholder returns have also begun, with the first dividend in 2026 and the share buyback. However, the cosmetics segment's profit record is short, and net profit is sensitive to currency moves.

The next checkpoints are the end of the buyback on December 11, 2026, the annual operating margin in the 2026 annual report, and the effect of the expansion in the Q1 2027 report.
