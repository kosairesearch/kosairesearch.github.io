Public-Sector IT Profitability Recovers, Nine-Month Profit Tops Full-Year 2025

Itcenentec's cumulative operating profit through the third quarter of 2026 has already surpassed its full-year 2025 level, but quarterly net profit attributable to controlling shareholders has swung widely, leaving the durability of earnings as the open question.

  1. 1

    Sales rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, reaching KRW 572 billion in 2025, the largest year in the 2022-2025 span.

  2. 2

    Operating profit reached KRW 20.9 billion in 2025, up 121.2% year on year and up for two consecutive years from the 2023 low, with the operating margin rising to 3.6%.

  3. 3

    According to the company's announcement dated November 12, 2026, cumulative consolidated operating profit through the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 27.6 billion, up 157.9% year on year, with a cumulative operating margin of 6.7%.

  4. 4

    Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 11.1 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 4.98 billion in Q2 before recovering to KRW 11.3 billion in Q3, with the last four quarters totaling KRW 46.4 billion.

  5. 5

    Subsidiary ITCEN Cloit moved from an operating loss to profit, becoming one pillar of the consolidated improvement.

02

Business structure

Itcenentec builds and operates IT systems mainly for public-sector and financial clients, and joined the Itcen group in June 2020.

Of the separate-basis sales of KRW 244.2 billion in the first nine months of 2026, IT outsourcing (ITO) accounted for KRW 145.2 billion (59.5%), digital transformation (DX) for KRW 52.2 billion (21.4%), and AI transformation (AX) for KRW 45.3 billion (18.5%).

Legacy system operation remains the largest revenue base, with AI-related work following. The in-house sLLM-based development support solution IntelliCEN CODE has been applied to more than ten public and financial projects.

Its external products include NExQ.cen, an AI solution for insurers' actuarial and risk analysis launched in August, and the AI Essay Coach for university applicants launched in October, which marks its entry into education SaaS.

In defense, the company said in its first-quarter 2025 results announcement that it has long worked on C4I systems, tactical data links such as Link-22, and Army KCTC system development.

Its main clients are public bodies including KERIS, the National Intelligence Service, HUG and K-water, and it also has a financial-sector client base. In distribution, it entered digital services tied to the Hyundai Department Store Group H.Point platform.

Its subsidiary ITCEN Cloit focuses on enterprise AI through the AgentGo AI agent platform, and posted nine-month 2026 sales of KRW 158.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.8 billion, turning from an operating loss of KRW 8.6 billion a year earlier.

Listed IT and software peers that compete in public and large-scale SI markets include Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results from the regular filings show sales rising from KRW 325.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 392.5 billion in 2023 (up 20.7% year on year), KRW 441.8 billion in 2024 (up 12.6%) and KRW 572 billion in 2025 (up 29.5%), three consecutive years of growth.

Operating profit fell to KRW 8.41 billion in 2023 (down 13.1%), then rose to KRW 9.44 billion in 2024 and KRW 20.9 billion in 2025 (up 121.2%), two consecutive years of increase.

The operating margin was 3.0% in 2022, 2.1% in 2023, 2.1% in 2024 and 3.6% in 2025, so the margin stayed in the low single digits even as sales grew in 2023 and 2024 before crossing 3% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 12 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.43 billion in 2023 (down 54.8%), then recovered to KRW 11.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 23 billion in 2025.

In 2025, attributable profit exceeded net profit of KRW 22 billion, which means the non-controlling interests recorded a loss. Quarterly sales peaked at KRW 195.1 billion in Q4 2025, up 42.2% from the prior quarter, with operating profit of KRW 10.2 billion.

Sales then fell 30.3% to KRW 135.9 billion in Q1 2026, with operating profit of KRW 7.04 billion, and operating profit rose for two straight quarters to KRW 8.31 billion in Q2 and KRW 12.2 billion in Q3.

Q3 2026 sales of KRW 133.8 billion were down 2.5% year on year, while operating profit rose 25.1%, so profitability rather than volume drove results.

Attributable net profit went from KRW 19.1 billion in Q4 2025 (up 160.5% from the prior quarter) to KRW 11.1 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 4.98 billion in Q2 (down 54.9%), before recovering to KRW 11.3 billion in Q3 (up 53.9% year on year).

According to the company's announcement, cumulative consolidated selling, general and administrative expenses fell from KRW 41.3 billion to KRW 37.8 billion over the first nine months, while gross profit rose from KRW 52 billion to KRW 65.4 billion.

Because some quarters show a wide gap between operating profit and attributable net profit, the breakdown of non-operating items and one-off factors in the next regular report is the item to check.

05

Industry analysis

Public and financial IT services are procured as large individual projects, so the size and timing of each contract can move quarterly sales sharply.

In its first-quarter 2025 results announcement, the company said it had won a series of large projects worth more than KRW 10 billion from clients including KERIS and the National Intelligence Service.

In its first-half 2026 announcement, it said a larger order backlog allowed it to optimize staffing and control project execution cost ratios. That reads more as profitability gains from its existing client base than as market-driven growth.

The fall from the Q4 2025 sales peak of KRW 195.1 billion to KRW 135.9 billion in Q1 2026 shows the quarterly swings typical of project-based revenue.

Demand for AI transformation among public and financial institutions is expanding, and the company is positioning its in-house sLLM development support solution as its main differentiator.

Listed peers such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever have greater scale and reference projects in large SI and operations markets, so Itcenentec is positioned as a mid-sized public and financial IT specialist.

Its consolidated operating margin reached 6.7% and its separate-basis margin 9.9% over the first nine months of 2026, which narrows the margin gap with larger peers.

06

Outlook

The company said it would continue upgrading IntelliCEN CODE in the second half of 2026, specifically strengthening its RAG WhiteBox technology and Thinking reasoning features to expand AX work in public and financial sectors. The same direction was presented in its first-quarter and first-half 2026 announcements.

Subsidiary ITCEN Cloit is expanding enterprise AI around the AgentGo platform and launched AgentGo Guard in September 2026 to block prompt attacks and sensitive data leakage. Itcenentec plans to connect this capability to its public and financial client base to pursue business automation.

NExQ.cen and the AI Essay Coach are described as attempts to add in-house solution and service revenue to a sales base centered on public-sector SI. Because the education SaaS business only launched in October 2026, its revenue contribution will need to be confirmed in upcoming regular reports.

In distribution, the company is building data-driven services through its work with the Hyundai Department Store Group H.Point platform and describes this as a new growth axis. The company has also said it expects continued sales growth and profitability improvement in 2026.

The next items to check are whether the full-year 2026 operating margin exceeds the 3.6% of 2025 and whether Q4 2026 sales hold near the Q3 level of KRW 133.8 billion, both of which will appear in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Clear Recovery in Earnings Power

Operating profit reached KRW 20.9 billion in 2025, up 121.2%, and cumulative operating profit through Q3 2026 reached KRW 27.6 billion, up 157.9% year on year. The nine-month figure already exceeds the full-year 2025 level, a fact confirmed by both the company announcement and the regular filings. Over the same period, the consolidated operating margin rose from 2.8% to 6.7%.

Subsidiary Turnaround and Cost Control

Subsidiary ITCEN Cloit posted nine-month 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.8 billion, turning from an operating loss of KRW 8.6 billion a year earlier. The subsidiary's gross margin improved from 5.0% to 9.7% and its selling, general and administrative expenses fell 23.6%. Consolidated SG&A also declined from KRW 41.3 billion to KRW 37.8 billion, supporting the profit gain.

Progress in Proprietary AI Solutions

IntelliCEN CODE has been applied to more than ten public and financial projects, while NExQ.cen launched in August and the AI Essay Coach in October. The company says it will grow proprietary AI services from its public-sector know-how to raise profitability. Adding own-solution revenue could reduce the volatility of a public-sector SI-centered revenue base.

09

Bear factors

Signs of Slowing Sales Growth

Q3 2026 sales of KRW 133.8 billion fell 2.5% year on year and 3.6% from the prior quarter. Cumulative sales through Q3 rose 8.4%, but the turn to a quarterly decline raises questions about the durability of top-line growth. Compared with the KRW 195.1 billion peak in Q4 2025, quarterly sales have been volatile.

Track Record of Thin Margins

The operating margin was 3.0% in 2022, 2.1% in 2023 and 2.1% in 2024, staying in the low single digits, and only reached 3.6% in 2025. The 2026 cumulative improvement is clear, but the company has a history of thin margins even as sales grew. Whether the gain is durable needs to be confirmed in upcoming regular reports.

High Volatility in Quarterly Profit

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 19.1 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 11.1 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 4.98 billion in Q2, before recovering to KRW 11.3 billion in Q3. The last four quarters total KRW 46.4 billion, but the large quarter-to-quarter swings limit how stable those earnings are.

Several quarters show a wide gap between operating profit and net profit, pointing to a meaningful role for non-operating items.

10

Risk factors

Project and Procurement Concentration

Sales depend heavily on large projects won from public institutions. If the timing of major awards slips or contracts end, quarterly sales and profit can move together. The company's own 2025 explanation that low-margin projects ended before efficiency improved shows that project mix drives profit.

Leverage and Cash Flow

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 220.5 billion against total equity of KRW 99.1 billion, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 222.4%. Operating cash flow swung from KRW 53.9 billion in 2022 to negative KRW 19.7 billion in 2023, then KRW 11.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 15.7 billion in 2025. The 2023 outflow shows that working capital management remains a key variable.

Uncertain Monetization of New AI Businesses

NExQ.cen and the AI Essay Coach launched in August and October 2026, so their revenue contribution is not yet visible in the regular filings. The education SaaS and distribution digital services are at an early stage.

The subsidiary's return to profit is based on a single year of results so far, and whether these businesses reach margins comparable to public-sector SI work remains to be tested.

12

Overall view

Itcenentec's cumulative consolidated operating profit through Q3 2026 of KRW 27.6 billion has already exceeded the KRW 20.9 billion recorded for full-year 2025, and the cumulative operating margin rose to 6.7% from 2.8% a year earlier.

The gains come from lower SG&A, the return to profit at subsidiary ITCEN Cloit, and better project execution. On the other side, sales declined year on year in Q3 2026, and quarterly attributable net profit has swung from KRW 4.98 billion to KRW 11.3 billion.

The debt-to-equity ratio stood at 222.4% at the end of 2025, so financial leverage remains high, and operating cash flow has varied widely from year to year. The new AI products and the education and distribution businesses are still early, and their revenue contribution is not yet visible in the filings.

The next checkpoints are the full-year 2026 operating margin and Q4 sales in the 2026 annual report, and whether quarterly profitability holds near the Q3 2026 level in 2027. This report is for information only and contains no buy or sell opinion or target price.
