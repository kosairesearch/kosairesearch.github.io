02

Business structure

Dongjin Semichem makes materials for semiconductors and displays, with photoresist (PR) used in semiconductor lithography as its core product. Beyond photoresists, its electronic materials business supplies stripping agents, cleaning agents and etchants for semiconductor processes.

A first-half 2026 report tied double-digit growth to wider sales of photoresists and wet chemicals. Its main customers include Samsung Electronics, SK hynix, LG Display and Samsung Display. A first-half 2026 article reported that Samsung Electronics accounts for 62% of sales, indicating high customer concentration.

The company is restructuring, including a split of its foaming agent business and a withdrawal from China. In semiconductors, it is described as a domestic company that supplies EUV photoresist in volume.

In packaging, it is preparing redistribution-layer insulation materials and build-up films for high-performance chips such as HBM.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue was largest in 2022 at KRW 1.46 trillion and smallest in 2025 at KRW 1.19 trillion. After KRW 1.31 trillion in 2023 (down 10.1% year on year), revenue recovered to KRW 1.41 trillion in 2024 (up 7.5%), before falling 15.2% in 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 216.3 billion in 2022, KRW 176.2 billion in 2023 (down 18.6%), KRW 208.2 billion in 2024 (up 18.2%) and KRW 172.4 billion in 2025 (down 17.2%). The operating margin stayed around 14%, at 14.8% in 2022, 13.4% in 2023, 14.8% in 2024 and 14.4% in 2025.

The 2025 operating profit decline therefore appears to have come mainly from lower revenue rather than margin erosion. Controlling-shareholder net profit was KRW 159.5 billion in 2022, KRW 127.3 billion in 2023 (down 20.2%), KRW 154.8 billion in 2024 (up 21.6%) and KRW 99.1 billion in 2025 (down 36.0%).

In 2025 the decline in controlling-shareholder net profit was much larger than the decline in operating profit, so items below operating profit may have weighed heavily.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 94.1 billion in 2025, below the controlling-shareholder figure, which reflects a non-controlling loss. Quarterly results have improved since the fourth quarter of 2025.

Revenue rose 120.9% quarter on quarter to KRW 309.2 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, then reached KRW 328.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 346.9 billion in the second and KRW 370.8 billion in the third, increasing for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

Operating profit went from KRW 18.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 41.6 billion in the fourth quarter (up 124.5% quarter on quarter) and KRW 66.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 60.0%).

It fell 11.0% to KRW 59.3 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and reached KRW 61.1 billion in the third (up 3.0% quarter on quarter). Combined operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 exceeds the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 172.4 billion.

Controlling-shareholder net profit reached KRW 114.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 383.8% year on year and far faster than operating profit.

The preliminary third-quarter results released on November 3, 2026 said pre-tax income from continuing operations was a loss and that net profit reflected discontinued-operations gains and losses. Those discontinued items may account for a substantial part of the net profit increase.

05

Industry analysis

Photoresist is a core material that forms circuit patterns in lithography, and it is a market with high technical barriers. In Korea, Dongjin Semichem is reported as a supplier of EUV photoresist in volume production.

A 2024 etnews report described it as the only domestic company to have succeeded in mass-supplying EUV PR. In early 2025, a securities firm described it as a domestic producer of ArF, KrF and EUV photoresists and estimated that TOK, JSR and Shin-Etsu Chemical held a combined 60% to 70% share of the photoresist market.

On the demand side, a first-half 2026 article said demand for repeatedly consumed semiconductor materials such as photoresists, cleaning agents and etchants has been rising. EUV generational change is another variable.

High-NA EUV tools would require material changes, and the company said it is preparing materials for that transition. Environmental regulation also shapes the sector.

The company set a target in October 2024 to remove perfluorinated compounds from all PR products by 2030, while it said semiconductor PFAS rules are less strict than in other industries such as displays.

Packaging materials are a field where Japanese companies have strong market dominance, which makes localization harder.

06

Outlook

The company has outlined three confirmed directions. The first is wider supply of EUV photoresist, the second is preparation of materials for High-NA EUV, and the third is advanced packaging materials for HBM and similar chips.

In packaging, it is developing photosensitive insulation materials (PID) for redistribution layers and build-up films first, while also evaluating underfill and other packaging materials.

As of October 2026, however, it had not yet supplied advanced packaging materials to any customer and was working closely with customers on evaluation. Overseas, a report at the end of 2025 said the Texas thinner subsidiary planned to ship evaluation samples in the second quarter of 2026.

The report also said the thinner can cut photoresist usage by up to 50% compared with general thinners. The same report said a sulfuric acid joint venture in the US would enter full-scale production from 2026 and begin generating sales. That joint venture, DSM Semichem, was founded in September 2022.

Supply timed to the start of Samsung Electronics' Taylor fab is another point to watch for customer expansion. The next checkpoints are whether the Texas thinner moves into mass production and whether advanced packaging materials reach their first supply.

Bull factors

Record of EUV photoresist supply

The company is described as a domestic supplier of EUV photoresist in volume production. In October 2026 it said it supplies EUV photoresist to customers. Its share of advanced products remaining below 30% indicates room for customer expansion.

Earnings recovery trend

Revenue increased for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. The operating margin trend will be visible in the next annual report.

Preparation to expand into packaging materials

The company is developing photosensitive insulation materials for redistribution layers and build-up films first, preparing to enter the HBM and high-performance packaging market. Underfill and other materials are also under review. It has no supply record yet, but customer evaluations are under way.

Bear factors

Quarterly operating profit volatility

Operating profit was KRW 172.4 billion in 2025, below KRW 216.3 billion in 2022. In the second quarter of 2026, operating profit fell 11.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 59.3 billion. Quarterly profit has not moved in a single direction, which is a weak point.

Net profit shaped by discontinued operations

In preliminary third-quarter results released on November 3, 2026, pre-tax income from continuing operations was a loss, and net profit reflected discontinued-operations items. This is why controlling-shareholder net profit growth far exceeded operating profit growth. It is a factor in assessing earnings quality.

Japanese oligopoly and restructuring burden

One analysis estimates that Japanese firms such as TOK, JSR and Shin-Etsu Chemical hold 60% to 70% of the photoresist market. Restructuring, including the split of the foaming agent business and the withdrawal from China, adds to the burden of changing the business structure. Competition and restructuring are intertwined.

Risk factors

Customer concentration

A first-half 2026 article reported Samsung Electronics accounts for 62% of sales. The structure links the pace of one customer's equipment investment and production adjustments directly to results. Adjustments in semiconductor capital spending could quickly affect photoresist orders.

Technology transition

High-NA EUV tools would require material changes, and the company said it is preparing materials for that shift. The timing of the transition between existing EUV materials and new materials, and the pace of customer qualification, are risks. Delays in new materials could extend reliance on existing products.

Environmental rules and portfolio exits

The company set a 2030 goal to remove perfluorinated compounds from all PR products. It said Huizhou Dongjin Semichem Electronic Materials was excluded from the current disposal and is undergoing a separate sale process. The timing and terms of that sale will be disclosed.

Overall view

Dongjin Semichem is a materials company that supplies EUV photoresist in volume production in Korea. Revenue and operating profit declined in 2025, but quarterly results have recovered sharply since the fourth quarter of 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 114.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026 rose 383.8% year on year, yet pre-tax income from continuing operations was a loss and discontinued-operations items were reflected in net profit.

Strengths include the EUV photoresist supply record, the quarterly earnings recovery and preparation for packaging materials. Weaknesses include quarterly volatility in operating profit, reliance on discontinued-operations items, Japanese oligopoly competition and customer concentration.

Its share of advanced products remains below 30%, leaving room for customer expansion. The next checkpoints are preliminary Q4 and full-year 2026 results, the 2026 annual report, the outcome of the Huizhou subsidiary sale and customer evaluation of advanced packaging materials.
