Report overview

Revenue Growth, Thinner Margins at Soosan INT

Soosan INT's revenue rose in both 2024 and 2025, but operating profit has declined for two straight years since its 2023 peak, pulling the operating margin from 27.1% to 16.6%.

  1. 1

    Revenue of KRW 30.7 billion in 2025 was the largest year of 2022-2025. It followed a low of KRW 23.9 billion in 2023 and rose in both 2024 and 2025.

  2. 2

    Operating profit peaked at KRW 6.47 billion in 2023 and fell to KRW 5.1 billion in 2025, declining for a second straight year. The operating margin over the same period dropped from 27.1% to 16.6%.

  3. 3

    Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 7.32 billion rose 19.7% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 598 million rose 31.0%. Revenue fell 42.2% quarter on quarter in Q1 2026, so quarterly swings are large.

  4. 4

    At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 91.2 billion and total liabilities were KRW 7.43 billion, a debt ratio of 8.1%. The balance sheet carries very little borrowing.

  5. 5

    The business leans on public-sector security references. In 2026 the company has moved forward with the eSafe AI launch, ISO/IEC 27001 certification, and overseas proof-of-concept projects.

02

Business structure

Soosan INT runs two businesses: a security business that supplies network security solutions based on traffic analysis, and a service business that provides internet value-added services together with telecom operators.

By 2025, security solutions accounted for 68.5% of revenue and the shared-terminal access management service for 28.8%. These ratios come from a press report whose amount base differs from the DART revenue figures, so only the ratios are shown.

The flagship products are the ePrism SSL VA SSL visibility solution and the eWalker SWG harmful-site blocking solution, which the company says hold first place in the market. In data protection, the company unveiled an image character recognition function for eWalker DLP.

In 2025 it launched the eWalker SSG SaaS control solution. The customer base was about 1,700 companies at the end of 2025, and the company emphasizes its focus on the public sector, where security policies change often.

Competition comes from domestic security firms such as AhnLab, competing for public-sector references. Overseas, the company signed an ODM contract with Germany's NEOX NETWORKS.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 25.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 23.9 billion in 2023, a 6.6% decline, and then rose to KRW 26.7 billion in 2024 (up 11.7%) and KRW 30.7 billion in 2025 (up 15.1%).

Operating profit rose from KRW 3.48 billion in 2022 to a peak of KRW 6.47 billion in 2023 (up 85.8%), then fell to KRW 5.67 billion in 2024 (down 12.3%) and KRW 5.1 billion in 2025 (down 10.1%). Revenue grew while profit did not, and the operating margin moved from 27.1% in 2023 to 21.3% in 2024 and 16.6% in 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 2.49 billion in 2022, KRW 5.57 billion in 2023, KRW 4.43 billion in 2024, and KRW 5.22 billion in 2025. The 2023 bottom line was helped by a negative income tax expense. Non-operating income increased in 2024 and 2025 and supported profit.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.18 billion in 2022, KRW 4.71 billion in 2023, KRW 4.43 billion in 2024, and KRW 5.22 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow went from KRW 4.18 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.46 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.56 billion in 2025.

Among the quarters, Q4 2025 had the highest revenue at KRW 11.5 billion (up 87.3% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 2.3 billion (up 402.9%), about 45% of full-year 2025 operating profit. Q1 2026 revenue fell 42.2% to KRW 6.63 billion and operating profit fell 79.9% to KRW 462 million.

Revenue recovered to KRW 7.72 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 7.32 billion in Q3 2026, when operating profit was KRW 598 million. Controlling-shareholder net income over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 6.61 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In Korea's security market, network security appliances and SaaS control tools are sensitive to shifts in public-sector policy.

The introduction of the National Network Security Framework (N2SF) is moving the market away from network-separation-centered security toward a more flexible model based on business importance and data sensitivity. The spread of generative AI has raised data leakage and misuse of access rights as new threats.

In its 2026 outlook, the company pointed to AI-driven attacks becoming routine and an environment without clear perimeters as the key themes. Market growth is described as gradual given the characteristics of the domestic security industry.

The company says it invests roughly one-third of operating profit in R&D each year. R&D spending was KRW 1.4 billion in 2021, KRW 1.9 billion in 2022, and KRW 2.1 billion in 2023.

In competition, the company claims first place in SSL visibility, but its product scope differs from that of integrated peers such as AhnLab. Supply-chain security consulting is slated to become mandatory from 2027, a potential demand driver for the consulting business.

06

Outlook

The company set out customer focus, in-house AI capability, and sustainable growth as its 2026 strategy. The eSafe AI product, which controls prompts and attachments to prevent leaks during generative AI use, was launched in January 2026.

At the April 2026 partners day, the company presented eWalker SSG and eSafe AI and disclosed an OCR function for eWalker DLP. Overseas, testing of the German ODM product is nearly complete, and proof-of-concept tests are running in Indonesia and Singapore, according to the company.

The company said 2026 is meant to convert overseas efforts into real revenue rather than one-off sales. In October 2026 the company announced a partnership with cPacket Networks that combines encrypted traffic decryption with packet-level AI analysis. The company obtained ISO/IEC 27001 certification in January 2026.

On a separate-financials basis, first-half 2026 revenue rose 9.2% while operating profit fell 52.7%. Whether new products and overseas sales add meaningful revenue will show up in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Room for Margin Recovery

The operating margin fell from 27.1% in 2023 to 16.6% in 2025. Q3 2026 operating profit rose 31.0% year on year, the first clear break from the decline. If the cost structure is adjusted, the margin has room to recover, though first-half 2026 operating profit on a cumulative basis still declined.

Public-Sector References and Product Line-Up

The company had about 1,700 customers at the end of 2025. Its SaaS control and generative AI security products could align with public-sector demand under the N2SF framework. The company said it won more than 300 SSL visibility solution orders in 2024 alone.

Early Stage of Overseas Partner Expansion

The October 2026 partnership with cPacket Networks pairs encrypted-traffic visibility with AI-based analysis. About 40 major partners attended the April 2026 partners day. If overseas sales grow in practice, the business mix could diversify.

09

Bear factors

Two Straight Years of Profit Decline

Operating profit fell to KRW 5.67 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.1 billion in 2025, a second straight annual decline. Q1 2026 operating profit dropped 79.9% quarter on quarter to KRW 462 million. First-half 2026 separate-basis operating profit fell 52.7%, so profit has not kept pace with revenue.

Quarterly Revenue Volatility

Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 11.5 billion was the highest of the disclosed quarters, but Q1 2026 revenue fell 42.2% to KRW 6.63 billion. If revenue concentrates in particular quarters, quarterly results become harder to forecast. Q3 2026 revenue rose year on year but fell 5.2% quarter on quarter.

Cost Burden from New Businesses

Analysts note that development costs for new security products constrain profitability. The company spends roughly one-third of operating profit on R&D each year. Expanding overseas and new services could weigh on near-term margins.

10

Risk factors

Dependence on Policy and Public Procurement

Frequent changes in public-sector security policy can shift procurement timing and scale. Delays in adapting products to frameworks such as N2SF could mean missed orders. Broadening private-sector demand is the challenge.

Cost and Profitability Pressure

Commentary notes that the cost of developing new security products limits profitability. With the operating margin at 16.6% in 2025, continued cost growth could prolong the profit decline.

Event-Driven Share Price Swings

In December 2024 the share price was affected after the stock was grouped with political theme stocks. The Korea Exchange designated it as an investment caution stock for one day in the same month. Event-driven moves unrelated to business results can amplify share price volatility.

12

Overall view

Soosan INT recorded its largest revenue year of 2022-2025 at KRW 30.7 billion in 2025, but operating profit has declined for two straight years since its KRW 6.47 billion peak in 2023, pulling the margin down to 16.6%.

Q3 2026 revenue and operating profit rose year on year, while Q1 2026 saw a sharp drop, so quarterly variation is pronounced. The public-sector security references, about 1,700 customers, the eSafe AI launch, and ISO/IEC 27001 certification form the business base.

The lasting profit decline, new-product development costs, and procurement swings tied to public policy remain weak points. Financially, the 2025 debt ratio of 8.1% and equity of KRW 91.2 billion indicate a light debt burden.

Valuation metrics show the stock below net asset value and below the industry multiple, which reflects the market's view on earnings durability. The next checkpoints are whether the operating margin recovers in the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
