After the Turnaround: Utilization and Capacity Set the Next Step

02

Business structure

Nepes Ark was spun off in April 2019 from the semiconductor testing division of Nepes, and it listed on KOSDAQ in November 2020. Its main test targets include power management ICs (PMIC), application processors, display driver ICs (DDI), system ICs, RF chips and AI semiconductors.

Wafer testing accounted for 99.54% of sales in the first quarter of 2026, with package testing at 0.21% and other items at 0.25%. The company is positioned as a core back-end test partner for chips produced by Samsung Electronics’ foundry.

Under its turnkey model, Nepes handles packaging while Nepes Ark handles testing. A dedicated sales unit set up in September 2025 gave the company capacity to win test orders independently of its parent.

Applications remain centered on mobile, and one analyst cited in a Daily Invest report expects mobile to make up 84% of 2026 expected sales, with automotive and AI server exposure close to nil. The same report cited a 2030 target mix of 35% mobile, 43% automotive and 14% AI server.

Like other non-memory OSAT companies in Korea, results depend heavily on whether flagship phones adopt Korean-designed mobile APs. Growing test volumes for PMIC and DDI are seen as strengthening earnings stability.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 153.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 126.1 billion in 2023 (down 18.0% year on year), KRW 119.3 billion in 2024 (down 5.4%) and KRW 114.1 billion in 2025 (down 4.3%), a decline that has run for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Of the 2022 to 2025 period, 2022 was the largest year by revenue and 2025 the smallest. Operating profit moved from KRW 22.5 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 6.92 billion in 2023, which turned the year into a loss, and then an operating loss of KRW 2.63 billion in 2024, where losses continued but narrowed.

Operating profit returned to the black at KRW 4.76 billion in 2025. The operating margin went from 14.6% in 2022 to -5.5% in 2023, -2.2% in 2024 and 4.2% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 25.7 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 30.3 billion in 2023 and a net loss of KRW 28.7 billion in 2024, before returning to a profit of KRW 3.42 billion in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests matched the controlling-shareholder figure in each of the four years as disclosed.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 89.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 55.3 billion in 2023, KRW 55.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 46.6 billion in 2025, so cash generation remained well below 2022 levels even after the return to profit. The debt-to-equity ratio was 78.6% in 2022, 75.3% in 2023, 88.8% in 2024 and 72.9% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity stood at KRW 168.6 billion and total liabilities at KRW 122.9 billion.

Quarterly revenue fell from KRW 27.7 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 24.8 billion in the fourth quarter (down 10.5% quarter on quarter), then rose to KRW 29.9 billion, KRW 30.9 billion and KRW 31.2 billion across the first three quarters of 2026.

Operating results swung to a loss of KRW 42.53 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 before returning to profit of KRW 5.01 billion, KRW 5.1 billion and KRW 4.44 billion in the first three quarters of 2026. The third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 13.1% quarter on quarter but rose 320.3% year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss of KRW 605 million in the third quarter of 2025 to a profit of KRW 3.57 billion in the fourth quarter, KRW 2.63 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 9.35 billion in the second and KRW 8.97 billion in the third.

The last four quarters total KRW 24.5 billion. In the second and third quarters of 2026, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders exceeded operating profit, suggesting non-operating items added to earnings, while the first quarter showed the reverse.

05

Industry analysis

End-market demand is broadening from mobile toward AI servers and power semiconductors. A Korea Investment & Securities report from April 2026 noted that demand for PMIC in downstream industries is strong.

Demand for power chips has surged across AI servers, electric vehicles and industrial equipment, and global leaders including Texas Instruments and Infineon have raised prices in succession.

In the same report, major North American power chip makers reportedly had an order-to-shipment ratio above 1 in Q4 2025 and secured order backlogs. Outsourced test demand is reportedly rising as foundry customers’ operating conditions gradually improve.

LS Securities pointed to rising line utilization, citing a production bottleneck at leading-edge foundries and a split of volumes between Nepes Ark and rival-won items. On the AI side, qualification expectations for HBM4-related memory controllers were cited as a driver of the latest capacity expansion.

Mobile remains a variable: if phones adopt Korean-designed APs more widely, the surrounding PMIC test volumes rise, according to LS Securities. Its competitive position rests on wafer testing for PMIC, DDI, SoC and RF products, combined with the parent’s packaging under a turnkey structure.

On the cycle, back-end companies face earnings volatility when customers change production or investment plans, and a slowdown in chip demand can weigh on both packaging and test volumes.

06

Outlook

Nepes Ark disclosed two facility investments in 2026, KRW 37.5 billion running from April 2026 to March 2027 and KRW 66.7 billion running from June 2026 to May 2027, for a combined KRW 104.2 billion.

LS Securities described the spending as the first large-scale expansion in four years, following the 2020 to 2022 expansion wave. The same report estimated the CAPEX payback period at five to six years, depending on test item. Further expansion within the remaining space at the Cheongju plant is also expected.

On products, the report pointed to additional Exynos 2600 volumes and initial Exynos 2700 production expected to concentrate from the fourth quarter of 2026. LS Securities estimated 2026 revenue at KRW 136.9 billion and operating profit at KRW 29.2 billion in its May 28, 2026 report.

Adding up the operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 from the disclosed figures gives about KRW 14.55 billion, roughly half of that estimate, which would require about KRW 14.65 billion in the fourth quarter to reach it.

A Daily Invest report cited expectations that a further investment decision could be announced in the second half of 2026 for meaningful growth in new product lines.

The parent Nepes signed a technology and business cooperation memorandum with Hana Micron on November 16, 2026, and wider turnkey orders would feed test volumes to Nepes Ark. The next checkpoints are the outcome of HBM4 memory controller qualification and the timing at which Exynos 2700 volumes show up in revenue.

08

Bull factors

Power chip demand and utilization recovery

Order-to-shipment ratios above 1 at major North American power chip makers in Q4 2025 point to upstream demand that can feed test volumes. LS Securities cited rising line utilization at Nepes Ark. Higher utilization spreads fixed costs and could show up in the operating margin.

Expansion and new product order signals

Two facility investments totaling KRW 104.2 billion were disclosed in 2026. Analysts cited order signals in silicon capacitors and automotive SoCs as evidence of business improvement. Expectations for HBM4-related quality approval are also cited as a reason for the expansion.

Turnkey collaboration and earnings recovery

Operating profit returned to the black in 2025, and all three quarters of 2026 showed operating profit. Because packaging sits with the parent and testing with Nepes Ark, turnkey orders from the parent translate into test volumes. The November 2026 cooperation deal between the parent and Hana Micron is also part of this turnkey expansion.

09

Bear factors

Dependence on mobile AP

A Daily Invest report pointed to mobile making up 84% of expected 2026 sales, with automotive and AI server exposure near nil. If mobile unit sales or flagship adoption shifts, test volumes move with them. Expanding the automotive and AI server mix is framed as a 2030 target, so short-term change looks limited.

Thin margins and earnings volatility

Operating losses and net losses continued in 2023 and 2024, with operating margins of -5.5% and -2.2%. The 2025 margin of 4.2% is a modest return to profit. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 89.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 46.6 billion in 2025, so converting the earnings recovery into cash has been slow.

Expansion burden and priced-in expectations

The combined KRW 104.2 billion of 2026 investments equals about 62% of total equity of KRW 168.6 billion at the end of 2025. The share price set a 52-week high in late May 2026, so if earnings do not catch up with expectations, that gap could weigh on the stock.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and foundry cycle

Test volumes are closely tied to Samsung Electronics foundry chips and to whether mobile APs adopt them. A shift in customer production could reduce volumes quickly.

Expansion payback and utilization risk

The CAPEX payback is estimated at five to six years. If utilization does not rise as expected, the depreciation burden from new capacity could weigh on profit. Both expansion projects run through May 2027, so confirming utilization will take time.

Technology competition and product transition

LS Securities noted that the Exynos 2700 sample’s GPU trails the competing product by about 30%. If a customer’s product competitiveness slips, test volumes can be affected too. Because new product revenue is still small, the pace of transition will shape results.

12

Overall view

Nepes Ark moved from operating and net losses in 2023 and 2024 to an annual profit in 2025, and through the third quarter of 2026 it has posted three straight quarters of revenue growth and quarterly operating profit.

Annual revenue, however, declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025 before the recent rebound, so the earnings base is still short. Positive factors include power chip demand, higher test utilization, KRW 104.2 billion in combined 2026 expansion and signals of new product orders.

Negative factors include reliance on mobile APs, a history of thin operating margins and a CAPEX payback estimated at five to six years. Brokerages tie the current valuation to growth expectations at foundry customers, but the pace at which those expectations show up in reported results remains the key issue.

The next checkpoints are whether a further investment decision is announced in the second half of 2026, whether Exynos 2700 volumes appear in the fourth quarter and what the 2026 annual report confirms.

How close the first-three-quarter 2026 operating profit of about KRW 14.55 billion gets to the annual estimate will be a key gauge of the earnings path.
