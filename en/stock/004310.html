02

Business structure

Founded in 1965, Hyundai Pharmaceutical is a finished-dose drug manufacturer that runs a pharmaceutical business centered on prescription and over-the-counter drugs alongside a food business built around the dietary fiber drink 'Myero Fiber,' plus cosmetics and other smaller operations.

Finished pharmaceuticals remain the core of the revenue mix, with the pharmaceutical segment reportedly accounting for more than 80% of total sales.

The company manufactures its own prescription drugs such as the dementia treatments DM Duo, Hypezil, and Tamirin and the hyperlipidemia drug Ezepeno, while also distributing in-licensed products including the hemostatic agent TachoSil and the breakthrough-pain treatment Actiq.

In the OTC space, the hair-loss treatment 'Mynoxyl' has long been a flagship product, while in food, 'Myero Fiber,' launched in 1989, has surpassed 2.3 billion cumulative units sold and continues to expand its lineup, including a new candy-format product, 'V-Candy,' introduced in 2026.

The company also holds exclusive domestic rights to the abortion medication 'Mifegymiso' (mifepristone), which draws market attention whenever related policy discussions surface.

On research and development, Hyundai Pharmaceutical pursues improved new drugs, new formulations, and drug delivery system research, with some cardiovascular improved new drug candidates reportedly in domestic Phase 3 trials and the diabetes candidate HDNO-1605 in Phase 2b.

In 2023 it signed an exclusive license agreement with Cassiopea, a subsidiary of Italy's Cosmo Pharmaceuticals, for domestic development and commercialization of the acne treatment 'Winlevi' (clascoterone), and interest has grown in whether the same active ingredient's hair-loss efficacy data could lead to an additional indication license.

Competitively, the company faces numerous mid-sized domestic pharmaceutical peers in finished dosage drugs while leveraging its long-standing brand position in the functional beverage market.

04

Earnings analysis

Hyundai Pharmaceutical's annual revenue rose from KRW 162.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 180.8 billion in 2023 (up 11.1% year on year), dipped to KRW 175.7 billion in 2024 (down 2.8%), then climbed to KRW 191.8 billion in 2025 (up 9.1%), the largest figure in the 2022-2025 span.

Operating profit fell from KRW 7.97 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.93 billion in 2023 (down 13.1%) and to just KRW 181 million in 2024 (down 97.4%), the smallest figure in the period, before rebounding sharply to KRW 4.16 billion in 2025 (up 2,200.3%).

Net profit attributable to owners swung from a loss of KRW 165 million in 2022 to a profit of KRW 6.12 billion in 2023, back to a loss of KRW 575 million in 2024, and then to a profit of KRW 2.52 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue fell from KRW 51.6 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 48.7 billion in Q4 2025 (down 5.5% quarter on quarter) and KRW 46.5 billion in Q1 2026 (down 4.7%), before rising for two consecutive quarters to KRW 55.6 billion in Q2 2026 (up 19.7%) and KRW 56.3 billion in Q3 2026 (up 1.2% quarter on quarter and up 9.1% year on year).

Operating profit shrank to KRW 42.67 million in Q4 2025, rebounded sharply to KRW 2.52 billion in Q1 2026, fell again to KRW 976 million in Q2 2026 (down 61.3%), and recovered to KRW 2.44 billion in Q3 2026 (up 149.9% quarter on quarter but down 29.4% year on year).

Net profit attributable to owners rose for three straight quarters after bottoming at KRW 268 million in Q4 2025, reaching KRW 420 million in Q1 2026, KRW 1.21 billion in Q2 2026, and KRW 1.71 billion in Q3 2026, with the trailing four quarters (Q4 2025-Q3 2026) totaling KRW 3.61 billion.

The 2025 turnaround has been attributed to higher sales of key drugs including the dementia treatments DM Duo, Hypezil, and Tamirin and the hyperlipidemia drug Ezepeno, with both in-house manufactured products and in-licensed products posting year-on-year growth.

The operating margin fell from 4.9% in 2022 to just 0.1% in 2024 before recovering to 2.2% in 2025, still a low single-digit level, and the quarter-to-quarter swings suggest earnings quality has not yet fully stabilized.

05

Industry analysis

South Korea's pharmaceutical industry has entered a mature phase for generic and improved-new-drug finished dosage products, while competition intensifies around long-acting injectable (LAI) and drug delivery system (DDS) technologies for obesity, diabetes, schizophrenia, and dementia treatments.

Companies such as G2GBio, Peptron, and InventageLab have won recognition for their technology through joint development agreements with global pharmaceutical firms, and large players such as Samsung Epis Holdings have also invested in G2GBio to co-develop obesity treatments, reflecting active open-innovation moves among bigger firms.

Hyundai Pharmaceutical is less a direct competitor to these platform companies and more a mid-sized pharmaceutical firm pursuing a niche strategy of improved new drugs and new formulations through its own pipeline, including the diabetes candidate HDNO-1605 and the obesity candidate HD-6277.

Meanwhile, the hair-loss treatment market has seen repeated government discussions about national health insurance coverage, which has heightened share-price volatility for related stocks, and Cosmo Pharmaceuticals' successful clascoterone Phase 3 results also moved the share price of its domestic partner, Hyundai Pharmaceutical.

The abortion drug Mifegymiso has likewise seen periodic policy discussions about legalization and formal market introduction, with Hyundai Pharmaceutical frequently cited as a prime beneficiary candidate given its exclusive domestic rights.

In the functional beverage market, 'Myero Fiber' has maintained long-term brand strength, even as competition continues through new product variations such as zero-sugar and carbonated formats aligned with health trends.

Overall, Hyundai Pharmaceutical occupies a position that mixes a stable cash-generating core business with pipeline and licensing assets exposed to policy themes, and it differs markedly in scale and R&D spending from the industry's larger biosimilar and new-drug developers.

06

Outlook

Building on its 2025 annual rebound and the earnings recovery that has continued into 2026, Hyundai Pharmaceutical is pursuing a strategy of parallel growth in its finished-dose pharmaceutical and food businesses.

In the food segment, the company continues to expand its Myero Fiber lineup, including the 2026 launch of a candy-format product, 'V-Candy,' to reinforce the brand.

On the pipeline side, the diabetes candidate HDNO-1605 is in domestic Phase 2b trials, while the obesity candidate HD-6277 is reportedly being developed with a mechanism aimed at curbing the muscle-loss side effect seen with existing obesity treatments.

Partner Cosmo Pharmaceuticals' clascoterone 5% solution has received Korean regulatory approval as the acne treatment 'Winlevi,' and global Phase 3 data demonstrating hair-loss efficacy has drawn attention to whether an additional indication license will follow, though as of October 2026 no separate hair-loss license agreement has been formally announced.

Regarding Mifegymiso, discussions about formal market introduction and national health insurance coverage have repeatedly resurfaced whenever related policy statements are made, and whether concrete steps such as coverage decisions or price negotiations actually proceed will require confirmation through future health authority announcements.

The company has shown a continuous rise in net profit attributable to owners from Q1 through Q3 2026, signaling continuity in its profitability recovery, but given the sizable quarter-to-quarter swings in operating profit, whether this trend persists into subsequent quarters will need to be confirmed through future disclosures.

Bull factors

Continuity of the Earnings Turnaround

Operating profit surged 2,200.3% year on year in 2025, and net profit attributable to owners then rose for three consecutive quarters through Q1-Q3 2026. Revenue also grew quarter on quarter in both Q2 and Q3 2026, showing top-line growth alongside the profit recovery.

Still, given the large swings in quarterly operating profit, whether this trend holds steady will require confirmation through upcoming quarterly disclosures.

A Diversified Product Portfolio

A revenue mix spanning finished pharmaceuticals, food (Myero Fiber), and in-licensed products (such as TachoSil) helps reduce reliance on any single item. Growth in prescription drugs such as dementia treatments and a hyperlipidemia drug was cited as a key driver of the 2025 earnings rebound.

The long-standing Myero Fiber brand continues to contribute to the food segment through steady new product launches.

Exposure to Policy Themes

Holding exclusive domestic rights to the hair-loss treatment Mynoxyl and the drug Mifegymiso means the company draws market attention whenever related policy discussions surface. Partner Cosmo Pharmaceuticals' successful clascoterone Phase 3 results have raised expectations for an additional indication.

It should also be considered that such thematic events carry timing lags and uncertainty before translating into actual revenue contribution.

Bear factors

Profitability Remains Thin

The operating margin fell from 4.9% in 2022 to just 0.1% in 2024 and recovered only to 2.2% in 2025, still a low single-digit level. Quarterly swings have also been large, with operating profit shrinking to KRW 42.67 million in Q4 2025 before jumping to KRW 2.52 billion in Q1 2026. The observation period is still too short to conclude that earnings quality has stabilized.

Balance-Sheet Strain

The debt ratio rose from 82.6% in 2023 to 98.8% in 2025. Operating cash flow was negative for three straight years, at -KRW 4.55 billion in 2023, -KRW 2.37 billion in 2024, and -KRW 123 million in 2025. Even as earnings turned positive, cash generation has yet to catch up.

Pipeline and Licensing Uncertainty

The diabetes candidate HDNO-1605 remains in Phase 2b and the obesity candidate HD-6277 is still at an early development stage, meaning commercialization is likely years away. Clascoterone's hair-loss indication also has no formally announced separate domestic license agreement as of October 2026.

Formal introduction of Mifegymiso into the health-insurance system likewise has not been confirmed to be moving through concrete procedural steps following policy statements.

Risk factors

Share Price Volatility

Hyundai Pharmaceutical's share price has repeatedly spiked and retreated sharply on policy statements or partner clinical news. Short-term volatility can be amplified by thematic news unrelated to actual business performance. Investors should bear in mind the possibility of a gap between such swings and underlying fundamentals.

Regulatory and Policy Risk

Both national health insurance coverage for hair-loss treatments and formal introduction of Mifegymiso depend on government policy decisions, with timing and realization both uncertain. If policy discussions stall or are delayed, related expectations could recede.

The profitability impact could also vary depending on detailed procedures such as price negotiations and coverage criteria.

Financial and Working-Capital Risk

With the debt ratio trending higher and operating cash flow negative for three consecutive years, financial soundness indicators warrant continued monitoring. Rising working-capital burdens, such as higher accounts receivable, could add further pressure on cash flow.

Whether the earnings recovery translates into improved cash generation will need to be confirmed through disclosures in subsequent quarters.

Overall view

Hyundai Pharmaceutical has shown a profitability recovery, with both operating profit and net profit attributable to owners turning positive in 2025 and net profit attributable to owners rising for three consecutive quarters into 2026.

At the same time, the operating margin remains in the low single digits and operating cash flow has been negative for three straight years, pointing to ongoing financial challenges.

The company's mix of stable cash-generating products such as Mynoxyl and Myero Fiber alongside policy- and pipeline-linked themes such as exclusive Mifegymiso rights, clascoterone, HD-6277, and HDNO-1605 means its share price has reacted sharply to both earnings releases and policy news.

Ahead of any investment decision, it is worth tracking the continuity of the earnings recovery through the upcoming Q3 report and annual filing, as well as whether the policy- and licensing-related events actually progress.
