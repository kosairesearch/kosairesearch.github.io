Business structure

Hyosung ITX is a KOSPI-listed company centered on call center and IT services.

Its largest business is contact center services, which accounted for KRW 101.27 billion of revenue in the third quarter of 2026 according to the preliminary results released on October 30, 2026, the bulk of that quarter's total revenue of KRW 137.9 billion.

In the same release, contact center revenue rose 2.12% year on year. IT solutions and services revenue reached KRW 13.95 billion, up 115.27% year on year, and ITO revenue reached KRW 9.18 billion, up 57.72%. IDC revenue, by contrast, fell 26.50% to KRW 2.8 billion, so segment trends diverged.

On the customer side, a colocation service contract with SK Telecom signed on July 20, 2026 stands out. The contract value is KRW 252.72 billion and runs from January 1, 2027 through December 31, 2033, about 6 years and 11 months.

Based on the disclosure, the amount equals about 48.7% of 2025 revenue of KRW 519.01 billion. Largest shareholder Cho Hyun-jun and one other person hold 68.30%, and treasury shares account for 4.50%, as of October 6, 2026.

In competition, the contact center and IT services segments are compared with system integrators such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, while the IDC segment is compared with data center operators such as Kinx and Gabia. Contact center services remain the center of revenue, with IT services and IDC following.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on annual results in the filings, revenue fell from KRW 511.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 503.8 billion in 2023, down 1.5% year on year, then rose to KRW 506.4 billion in 2024, up 0.5%, and to KRW 519 billion in 2025, up 2.5%, marking two consecutive years of growth from 2024 to 2025.

Of the 2022-2025 years, 2025 was the largest and 2023 the smallest. Operating profit fell from KRW 22.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 19.2 billion in 2023, down 14.0%, and to KRW 18.7 billion in 2024, down 2.3%, before recovering slightly to KRW 19 billion in 2025, up 1.1%.

Operating profit was largest in 2022 and smallest in 2024. As a result, the operating margin declined from 4.4% in 2022 to 3.8% in 2023, 3.7% in 2024 and 3.7% in 2025, then flattened.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 14.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 14.6 billion in 2023, down 2.3%, and KRW 11.8 billion in 2024, down 19.3%, before rebounding to KRW 15.5 billion in 2025, up 31.3%. Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 15.5 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 168.9% in 2022 to 135.6% in 2024, then rose to 160.0% at the end of 2025, when total liabilities were KRW 120.2 billion against total equity of KRW 75.1 billion.

On a quarterly basis, revenue peaked at KRW 145.7 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, up 17.3% from the prior quarter, then fell to KRW 130.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026, down 10.6% quarter on quarter.

Revenue then rose to KRW 134 billion in the second quarter and KRW 137.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, marking two consecutive quarters of growth from the second quarter through the third quarter of 2026.

Operating profit was highest in the fourth quarter of 2025 at KRW 6.56 billion, up 52.6% from the prior quarter, while the third quarter of 2026 recorded KRW 3.75 billion, down 12.6% year on year.

In the preliminary results released on October 30, 2026, cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 10.59 billion, down 14.61% year on year. A company commentary covering the first half of 2026 noted that contact center revenue grew but rising costs lowered profitability.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 through third quarter of 2026) totals KRW 14.1 billion.

Industry analysis

The call center and IT services market is shifting toward cloud-based infrastructure.

Hyosung ITX has been expanding its cloud-based AICC infrastructure, and a company commentary covering the first half of 2026 linked customized services and this infrastructure build-out to revenue growth, while noting that higher costs weighed on profitability.

The company is also expanding its generative AI-based VOC analytics solution, xtrmVOB, and its smart factory solutions are extending into petrochemicals, semiconductors and shipbuilding. This direction matches the 115.27% year-on-year rise in IT solutions and services revenue in the third quarter of 2026.

By contrast, IDC revenue fell 26.50% in the third quarter of 2026, showing clear divergence across segments. The IDC segment is compared with Kinx and Gabia. In system integration and IT services, large companies such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS, Hyundai Autoever and POSCO DX are the main reference points.

Hyosung ITX sits in a small-to-mid-cap range, with a market capitalization far smaller than those peers. On the customer side, its long-term contract with a telecom operator is a feature that sets it apart from many IT service companies.

Overall view

Hyosung ITX's third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 137.9 billion, up 11.0% year on year, extending its top-line growth. However, operating profit for the same quarter was KRW 3.75 billion, down 12.6%, and the operating margin fell from 3.7% for full-year 2025 to about 2.7% in the third quarter of 2026.

On a full-year basis for 2025, revenue of KRW 519 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 15.5 billion indicate an earnings recovery. The colocation contract with SK Telecom, signed in July 2026, was disclosed as a long-term driver that could widen the revenue base from 2027 through 2033.

On shareholder returns, the company has set a target to keep the 2026 payout ratio at 50% or higher. On the other side, the debt ratio rose to 160.0% at the end of 2025 from 135.6% in 2024, and the largest shareholder's 68.30% stake leaves a limited share of stock available for trading.

Growth, long-term contracts and shareholder returns coexist with margin stagnation and financial burden. The next checkpoints are the 2026 fiscal-year close and dividend decision, the start of the contract on January 1, 2027, and first-quarter 2027 results.
