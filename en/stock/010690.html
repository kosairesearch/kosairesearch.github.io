Hwashin: Annual Recovery in 2025, Quarterly Profits Slowing Through 2026

Hwashin's 2025 annual revenue and operating profit were the highest of 2022 to 2025, but revenue and operating profit have declined for three straight quarters since Q1 2026, and Q3 2026 swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders.

  1. 1

    Revenue reached KRW 1.96 trillion in 2025, up 14.6% year on year, and operating profit reached KRW 102.4 billion, up 56.9%, making 2025 the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

  2. 2

    Operating profit for Q3 2026 was KRW 2.09 billion, down 81.9% year on year, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 51.3 billion.

  3. 3

    Samsung Securities projected 2026 operating profit of KRW 119.2 billion in a February 2026 report, but disclosed operating profit fell in each of the first three quarters of 2026, widening the gap with that estimate.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Hwashin was founded in 1975 as an auto parts maker whose main customers are Hyundai Motor Group affiliates, supplying major automotive parts as modules or individual components.

Based on Q2 2026 revenue, the business mix is Chassis Parts 61%, Other Products 20%, Body Parts 10%, Trading Goods 6% and Electrification Parts 3%, according to the Butler company data page.

Because chassis parts make up a majority, the chassis volumes that Hyundai Motor and KIA assign by model are the core of earnings. A Samsung Securities report from February 2026 noted that Hwashin is the exclusive chassis supplier to Hyundai Motor and KIA in the United States and India.

The production footprint consists of local subsidiaries in the United States, China and other regions, a newly established subsidiary in Pune, India, and an electric vehicle plant in Georgia, United States that has been completed and is delivering chassis products in stages.

The core of the electrification business is the battery pack case. The same report put battery case revenue at KRW 20 billion in 2024 (1.2% of sales), KRW 80 billion in 2025 (4.1%), and forecast KRW 170 billion for 2026 (8% of sales).

The same report also mentioned the possibility of extending chassis and materials technology into new areas such as robot body modules.

In competition, a securities analysis article summarized that SL, a peer in the same industry, emphasizes global growth centered on automotive lamps, while SNT Motiv emphasizes diversification into HEV and EV motors, defense and robot parts.

Hwashin carries high customer concentration, and it responds by expanding its production footprint to match where customers build vehicles.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 1,690.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 1,802.8 billion in 2023 (up 6.7% year on year), fell to KRW 1,712.3 billion in 2024 (down 5.0% year on year), and then reached its highest level at KRW 1,962.5 billion in 2025 (up 14.6% year on year).

Operating profit declined from KRW 87.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 83.7 billion in 2023 (down 4.2% year on year) and KRW 65.3 billion in 2024 (down 22.0% year on year), then recovered to KRW 102.4 billion in 2025 (up 56.9% year on year).

The operating margin fell from 5.2% in 2022 to 4.6% in 2023 and 3.8% in 2024, then returned to 5.2% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was highest in 2023 at KRW 77.3 billion and lowest in 2024 at KRW 53.6 billion (down 30.7% year on year), then increased again to KRW 66.7 billion in 2025 (up 24.5% year on year).

Over the same period, net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts as net profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

One-off factors in 2025 included an impact of about KRW 20 billion from U.S. tariffs, which, according to a Samsung Securities report, was compensated by the automakers in the fourth quarter.

On a quarterly basis, the peak came in the fourth quarter of 2025, with revenue of KRW 512.4 billion (up 5.5% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 33.9 billion (up 193.3% quarter on quarter), compared with revenue of KRW 485.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 11.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025.

After that, results declined for three consecutive quarters: revenue of KRW 505.6 billion (down 1.3% quarter on quarter) and operating profit of KRW 17.0 billion (down 49.9% quarter on quarter) in the first quarter of 2026, revenue of KRW 498.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026, and revenue of KRW 491.9 billion (up 1.2% year on year) and operating profit of KRW 2.09 billion (down 81.9% year on year) in the third quarter of 2026.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 28.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and was KRW 7.03 billion in the second quarter, but turned to a loss with a net loss of KRW 51.3 billion in the third quarter.

Because the loss was large relative to operating profit, non-operating items and other factors may have had a large effect, but the cause should be determined from the body of the third-quarter report.

As for cash flow, operating cash flow decreased from KRW 136.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 42.0 billion in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose from 155.4% in 2024 to 172.9% in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Hwashin's end market depends heavily on production and sales at Hyundai Motor and KIA.

Industry summaries note that Hyundai Motor Group expanded market share by putting its full SUV lineup into mass production, and that sales of hybrid and electric vehicles, SUVs and the Genesis brand in North America and small SUVs in India were strong.

Samsung Securities said in its February 2026 report that new plants and new models at Hyundai Motor and KIA would lift Hwashin's 2026 revenue. However, quarterly revenue turned down through Q3 2026, so the key question is how quickly vehicle volume gains reach quarterly results.

In electrification, battery case growth sits at the center of the assumptions, and a securities analysis article pointed out that EV market growth could be slower than previously expected.

Among peers, SL is cited for lamp-led growth and margin improvement, while SNT Motiv is cited for diversification into HEV and EV motors, defense and robot parts. Samsung Securities views Hwashin as capable of moving from battery cases into robot body modules, building on chassis and materials technology.

With the 2025 tariff impact already offset through automaker compensation, the tariff question depends on whether that compensation structure continues.

Outlook

In its February 2026 report, Samsung Securities projected 2026 revenue of KRW 2.1 trillion and operating profit of KRW 119.2 billion, with an operating margin of 5.6%. The same report expected battery case revenue to rise to KRW 170 billion in 2026, or 8% of sales.

However, disclosed quarterly operating profit in the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 17 billion, KRW 5.8 billion and KRW 2.09 billion, declining each quarter.

A securities analysis article from September 2026 cited a report outlook in which 2026 operating profit falls to about KRW 54 billion before recovering to KRW 74 billion in 2027 and KRW 89 billion in 2028. Because the two estimates differ widely, it matters which direction Q4 2026 results follow.

Overseas production relies on the Pune subsidiary in India and the Georgia electric vehicle plant in the United States, which deliver chassis in stages and depend on new plant ramps at Hyundai Motor and KIA.

The company decided to cancel 171,969 treasury shares, carried at about KRW 364 million, on November 25, 2026, stating the aim of enhancing shareholder value. After cancellation, the common shares outstanding will change from 34,920,410 to 34,748,441.

The 2026 annual report, the next periodic filing, will fix full-year 2026 revenue and operating profit, and will show battery case sales and overseas subsidiary contributions in disclosed figures.

Bull factors

2025 earnings recovery and overseas plant ramp

Revenue and operating profit in 2025 were the highest of 2022 to 2025, and the operating margin rose from 3.8% in 2024 to 5.2% in 2025. The new Pune subsidiary in India and the completed Georgia electric vehicle plant in the United States are widening the overseas chassis supply base.

Samsung Securities explained that the roughly KRW 20 billion 2025 tariff impact was offset by automaker compensation in the fourth quarter.

Rising battery case share of sales

Battery case revenue rose from KRW 20 billion in 2024 to KRW 80 billion in 2025, and a February 2026 Samsung Securities report projected KRW 170 billion for 2026. Its share of sales was expected to rise from 4.1% in 2025 to 8% in 2026.

The same report also mentioned possible entry into new areas such as robot body modules, building on chassis and materials technology.

Shareholder return steps under way

The company decided to cancel 171,969 treasury common shares on November 25, 2026, stating the aim of enhancing shareholder value. The scheduled cancellation amount is KRW 364 million, and common shares outstanding will fall to 34,748,441 after cancellation.

The cancellation is taking place while the share price sits well below book value per share, which is a fact shareholders may weigh.

Bear factors

Three straight quarters of profit decline

Operating profit fell from KRW 17 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 5.8 billion in Q2 and KRW 2.09 billion in Q3, with Q3 down 81.9% year on year. Revenue also declined for three straight quarters from Q1 through Q3 2026.

Over the same period, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 51.3 billion in Q3.

Widening gap between earnings estimates

The 2026 operating profit of KRW 119.2 billion in the Samsung Securities February 2026 report differs sharply from the roughly KRW 54 billion cited in a September 2026 analysis article. Disclosed operating profit falling in each quarter of 2026 so far follows the direction of the lower estimate. Whether estimates are being cut will be clearer from the Q4 2026 preliminary results.

Rising financial burden

The debt ratio rose from 155.4% in 2024 to 172.9% in 2025, and total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 987.8 billion. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 136.9 billion in 2024 to KRW 42 billion in 2025. If higher debt coincides with shrinking profits, room for new investment could be constrained.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration

Hwashin's main customers are Hyundai Motor Group affiliates, and chassis parts accounted for 61% of Q2 2026 revenue. New model launches or weaker sales at Hyundai Motor and KIA translate directly into volume changes.

Even with more overseas plants, the high customer concentration remains, so earnings are exposed to automaker sales trends.

Tariffs and overseas subsidiary profitability

The 2025 U.S. tariff impact was about KRW 20 billion, and automakers compensated for it in the fourth quarter. If that compensation structure does not continue, the profitability of expanded overseas production could be affected. The new Pune and Georgia plants are at an early operating stage, so initial costs could weigh on earnings.

EV demand and battery cases

The battery case growth case assumes that EV production continues. Some analysts have noted that EV market growth could be slower than previously expected. If the KRW 170 billion projection for 2026 falls short, the scenario of a rising electrification share of sales would be delayed.
