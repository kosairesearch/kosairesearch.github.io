02

Business structure

BASKHANBIOPharma was founded in March 1986 and runs its headquarters and plant in Anseong, Gyeonggi Province. Its business centers on medicines and medical devices used before, during and after surgery, with pain-related products at the core.

Based on 2024 results, prescription drugs made up 72.1% of standalone sales and medical devices 13.8%. The company builds its lineup around products that can lift operating profit, such as non-reimbursed drugs, injectables and hemostatic agents. It also handles over-the-counter drugs and health functional food, though these are a smaller share.

The company started as Hanbul Pharm, was acquired by LigaChem Biosciences in 2015, and took its current name after merging with Medizone in 2020. It listed on the KONEX market on July 20, 2022.

The Anseong plant completed a government-backed smart factory build-out and upgrade in 2020, and its production management system is used to control raw material usage and defect rates. Two of its five directors are outside directors, which is a relatively developed governance setup for a KONEX-listed company. Headcount stood at 114 as of December 2025.

In the competitive landscape, large drugmakers such as Yuhan and Hanmi Pharm bring in-house new drugs and large sales networks. BASKHANBIOPharma instead relies on in-licensed surgical products and its manufacturing base.

Its R&D expense declined from KRW 0.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 0.3 billion in 2023 and KRW 0.1 billion in 2024, which also sets it apart from new-drug-driven companies. Its business model differs from that of large biotech names such as Samsung Biologics and Celltrion, so direct comparison is of limited use.

Recent trends

According to the 40th-term standalone financial statements approved at the March 26, 2026 shareholder meeting, 2025 sales were KRW 33,520 million, operating profit was KRW 2,895 million, and net income was KRW 4,766 million. Earnings per share are roughly one-fifth of the current share price.

Standalone sales for 2024 were KRW 28.9 billion and operating profit was KRW 1.2 billion, so operating profit rose 141.67% in a year. The operating margin climbed from 4.31% to 8.64%, and ROE was reported at 14.37%.

Because 2025 net income exceeded operating profit, the composition of non-operating items needs separate confirmation.

On the balance sheet, total assets at the end of 2025 were KRW 40,980 million, total liabilities were KRW 4,840 million and total equity was KRW 36,140 million. Total equity is larger than the market capitalization of KRW 24.9 billion.

In 2024, the accumulated deficit turned into retained earnings as capital surplus declined, and other income included a KRW 1.0 billion gain on asset disposal. Cash and cash equivalents stood at KRW 5.3 billion at the end of 2024, up from the prior year. The company has not paid dividends to date.

The share price was KRW 4,700 on March 26, 2026, and is KRW 3,550 as of October 27, 2026. Data from Investing.com show a 52-week range of KRW 2,860 to KRW 6,090 and a one-year change of about -34% as of the October 15, 2026 close.

The three-month average volume is about 140 shares, and trading value for the day was only KRW 6,855, so trading is extremely quiet.

Outlook

The sector backdrop cuts both ways. Medipharm Health News, using half-year reports for 2026, found that 45 of 75 major pharma and biotech companies posted operating profit, with a clear skew toward large biotechs and high-margin firms.

In an analysis of 82 listed companies by the Korea Biopharmaceutical Association, first-half sales growth was 7.2 percentage points lower than a year earlier and drug profitability declined.

Kiwoom Securities estimated that the combined operating profit of major pharma and biotech companies would reach KRW 5.7 trillion in 2026, up 25% from the prior year. That growth is led by large names such as Alteogen, SK biopharmaceuticals and Celltrion, so it does not translate directly to BASKHANBIOPharma.

At the company level, the key question is whether the operating margin lifted in 2025 holds. If higher-margin in-licensed items such as non-reimbursed drugs, injectables and hemostatic agents grow as a share of sales, the operating margin has room to improve further.

On the other hand, R&D spending remains low, so new products must largely come from external in-licensing. The 2026 half-year and third-quarter disclosures will be the first checkpoints for the earnings trend.

There is no clear factor that would quickly change trading conditions on KONEX, so the link between earnings and the share price may stay loose.

Bull factors

Improving operating margin

Standalone operating profit for 2025 rose sharply to KRW 2,895 million from KRW 1.2 billion the year before. The operating margin roughly doubled from 4.31% to 8.64%. This is read as the effect of spreading fixed costs as sales grew from KRW 28.9 billion to KRW 33,520 million. Further margin improvement depends on the mix of in-licensed products.

Light financial burden

Total liabilities of KRW 4,840 million at the end of 2025 are only a fraction of total equity of KRW 36,140 million. The debt ratio is 13.39% and the reserve ratio is 105.98%. With little borrowing, the company is less exposed to interest rate swings. That total equity exceeds the market capitalization is a reference point on book value.

Focused niche and governance

Its focus on surgery-related medicines and devices lifts the prescription drug share of sales to 72.1%. The smart factory gives it a system for managing defect rates. Two of five directors are outside directors, and the company won the Best ESG Award at the KONEX Awards in May 2026. The specialized focus and management framework distinguish it among small drugmakers.

Bear factors

Small scale, low R&D

Sales of KRW 33,520 million in 2025 are small next to large drugmakers. R&D expense declined from KRW 0.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 0.1 billion in 2024. With no prominent in-house new-drug pipeline, growth depends on in-licensed items and sales of existing products. The company may face disadvantages in pricing and sales networks against larger rivals.

Low KONEX liquidity

The three-month average volume is about 140 shares, and trading value on October 27, 2026 was KRW 6,855. Wide bid-ask spreads can make executing trades difficult. A market capitalization of KRW 24.9 billion also limits institutional participation. The speed and extent to which earnings improvement shows up in the share price are hard to gauge.

Uncertain earnings durability

In 2024, a KRW 1.0 billion gain on asset disposal was included in other income, and in 2025 net income exceeded operating profit. Whether non-operating items will keep contributing has not been confirmed. With no dividend history, the path from earnings to shareholder returns is unclear. The share price has moved down from the 52-week high of KRW 6,090 to the current level.

Risk factors

Macro

Sentiment toward pharma and biotech shifts materially with interest rates and risk appetite. BASKHANBIOPharma carries little debt, so the direct effect of rates is limited. Even so, small KONEX stocks can see trading shrink further when broader market sentiment weakens.

Domestic drug consumption and hospital surgical demand are also affected by the economy and the health environment.

Industry and regulatory

The Korea Biopharmaceutical Association's first-half analysis showed slower sector growth and lower drug profitability. Pharmaceuticals are subject to drug pricing policy, approval standards and manufacturing and quality control rules.

In a business built around in-licensed products, changes in contract terms with suppliers can affect earnings. As polarization within the sector deepens, a small company's standing depends on product competitiveness.

Company-specific

CEO Beoppyo Lee held 22.23% as of the March 30, 2026 disclosure, and the change in his holding stemmed from option expiry and an officer's retirement. In a structure centered on a small group of shareholders, transactions by major holders can have a relatively large market impact.

Dependence on key products and the renewal of in-licensing contracts could not be confirmed in the disclosures reviewed. Until results after the 2026 half-year are confirmed, the earnings trend remains uncertain.

08

Overall view

BASKHANBIOPharma is a small drugmaker specializing in medicines and devices for surgery, and its standalone operating profit rose to KRW 2,895 million in 2025 with the operating margin reaching 8.64%. Its light debt burden and total equity above its market capitalization are reference facts on the financial side.

On the other hand, R&D spending has declined and KONEX trading is extremely thin, so the link between earnings and the share price is weak. For the sector as a whole, first-half 2026 showed polarization centered on large companies, which is hard to describe as a favorable backdrop for small names.

It needs to be confirmed whether the 2025 profit improvement came from operating factors or from non-operating items. The 2026 half-year and third-quarter disclosures are the next materials for judging earnings durability, and this report is provided for information only, leaving judgment to the reader.
