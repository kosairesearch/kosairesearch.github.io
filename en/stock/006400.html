Samsung SDI: ESS orders drive return to profit, pace of recovery in focus

Samsung SDI returned to operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 with KRW 407.8 billion, and its recovery now rests on US ESS orders and local LFP production.

02

Business structure

Samsung SDI operates a battery segment and an electronic materials segment.

According to the preliminary results disclosure of October 29, 2026, battery segment operating profit was KRW 361.9 billion and electronic materials operating profit was KRW 45.9 billion, which together match third-quarter operating profit of KRW 407.8 billion.

The battery segment's main products are prismatic and cylindrical cells, ESS batteries, and battery backup units (BBU) for data centers. The electronic materials segment sells semiconductor patterning materials and display film materials for foldable smartphones. The center of the ESS business is the United States.

The SPE plant in Indiana began LFP mass production in early October 2026, and reports said three of four EV battery lines in the United States were converted to ESS LFP lines. Jupiter Power, a US energy developer, will receive 16GWh of supply over three years starting in 2029.

The Hungary Göd plant, with about 40GWh of capacity, saw utilization rise sharply on higher orders from BMW and the Volkswagen Group. In a market long led by Chinese suppliers, the company says it is the only non-Chinese maker producing prismatic ESS cells locally in North America.

LFP cathode material is sourced from domestic suppliers including L&F and POSCO Future M. The company also bought the entire stake in the GM joint venture SynergyCells, and it is shipping all-solid-state battery samples for humanoid robots while expanding cylindrical cell customers in aerospace.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue peaked at KRW 22.7 trillion in 2023 (up 12.8% year on year), then fell to KRW 16.6 trillion in 2024 (down 26.9%) and KRW 13.3 trillion in 2025 (down 20.0%), so revenue declined for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025.

The largest year among 2022 to 2025 was 2023, and the smallest was 2025. Operating profit fell from KRW 1.81 trillion in 2022 (margin 9.0%) to KRW 1.63 trillion in 2023 (down 9.7%) and KRW 363.3 billion in 2024 (down 77.8%, margin 2.2%), before swinging to an operating loss of KRW 1.72 trillion in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent followed the same path, moving from KRW 2.01 trillion in 2023 (up 2.9%) to KRW 599.3 billion in 2024 (down 70.2%) and a net loss of KRW 649.5 billion in 2025.

The 2025 net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 584.9 billion, which is a different figure from the parent-attributable loss. The debt-to-equity ratio at the end of 2025 was 79.3%, down from 88.2% in 2024. The quarterly trend turned in the second quarter of 2026.

Operating losses of KRW 591.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 299.2 billion in the fourth quarter, and KRW 155.6 billion in the first quarter of 2026 narrowed, and the second quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 203.8 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 407.8 billion rose 100.1% from the prior quarter and was reported as the highest since the third quarter of 2023.

Third-quarter revenue of KRW 4.87 trillion was up 59.7% from a year earlier, and parent-attributable net profit of KRW 600.4 billion was affected by non-operating items such as a corporate tax reversal, according to the preliminary disclosure.

Third-quarter revenue and operating profit include AMPC tax credits of KRW 122.5 billion, and the company cited the reflection of customer compensation as another factor in the improvement.

05

Industry analysis

Demand is shifting from electric vehicles toward ESS and AI data centers. Reports have described a prolonged slowdown in EV battery demand, and Samsung SDI has responded by converting EV lines to ESS LFP lines.

One outlook projects North American ESS demand rising from more than 130GWh in 2026 to more than 190GWh in 2030 and more than 240GWh in 2035. Drivers cited include renewable expansion, the replacement of aging grids, and surging power demand from AI data centers.

Higher BBU sales for data centers also contributed to third-quarter battery segment results. LFP is a price-competitive market long led by Chinese producers. Samsung SDI is combining local US production with a domestic cathode supply chain to reduce reliance on Chinese materials.

In the third domestic ESS central contract auction, reports said LG Energy Solution did not take part, leaving Samsung SDI competing with another domestic battery maker for the main supplier slots. Samsung SDI secured a total of 629MW in the previous two rounds.

According to the company, European EV demand is gradually improving on high oil prices and subsidies. In industry terms, the sector is in a transition in which EVs slow and ESS drives growth.

06

Outlook

The following schedule items have been stated by the company. For the fourth quarter of 2026, the company expects ESS sales to rise sharply from the prior quarter on SPE LFP output and higher volumes on existing NCA lines, and it expects to stay in profit.

LFP cells at the SPE plant have been shipping since mid-October 2026, and completed Samsung Battery Box (SBB) units are scheduled for customer delivery beginning in December 2026. The company is targeting additional orders by year-end that exceed its cumulative October figure.

Two additional LFP lines at SPE will be phased in starting in the first half of 2027. SynergyCells targets the start of high-capacity LFP ESS mass production between the second half of 2028 and early 2029. Based on orders already secured, the SPE and Ulsan lines can run at full capacity through 2028.

Supply to Jupiter Power runs for three years from 2029, and the company is also working to secure volumes for the period after 2029. For next-generation technology, reports said the company targets commercialization of all-solid-state batteries in the second half of 2027.

Utilization at the Hungary plant continues to rise on higher automaker orders. However, sustaining profitability after 2030, when US AMPC benefits phase down, remains a challenge.

The company plans to negotiate long-term supply prices with customers and respond through higher cell energy density, lower manufacturing costs, and supply chain optimization.

08

Bull factors

US ESS order backlog

Samsung SDI said it secured more than 60GWh of ESS orders through October 2026, mainly for US utility projects. The company said the secured volume alone can keep the SPE and Ulsan lines at full capacity through 2028.

A 16GWh contract with Jupiter Power announced on November 16, 2026 lifts the cumulative supply volume with that customer to about 27GWh. Greater visibility on production utilization is cited as the main bullish argument.

Quarterly profit recovery

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 203.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and then rose to KRW 407.8 billion in the third quarter, up 100.1% from the prior quarter. Reports said the third-quarter figure was the highest since the third quarter of 2023.

Parent-attributable net profit of KRW 600.4 billion was up 1,154.2% from a year earlier. The company expects profitability to continue in the fourth quarter of 2026.

Local US production and non-China supply chain

The SPE plant began ESS LFP mass production in early October 2026, and completed SBB units are scheduled for delivery from December. The company says it is the only non-Chinese maker producing prismatic ESS cells locally in North America.

LFP cathode material comes from domestic suppliers including L&F and POSCO Future M. A supply chain less dependent on Chinese materials could be a competitive factor in US local demand.

09

Bear factors

Reliance on tax credits and the post-2030 challenge

The preliminary third-quarter results included AMPC tax credits of KRW 122.5 billion in revenue and other income. The company itself said sustaining profitability after 2030, when US AMPC benefits phase down, is a challenge.

The operating profit level without the credit is the starting point for judging earnings quality. The challenge remains until cost reductions and long-term price agreements show results.

2025 loss and two years of falling revenue

The 2025 operating loss was KRW 1.72 trillion, and the net loss attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 649.5 billion, so the full year remained in deficit. Revenue fell for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025.

Quarterly profitability appeared from the second quarter of 2026, but a full-year return to profit is not yet visible in the disclosed annual figures. The first quarter of 2026 still recorded an operating loss of KRW 155.6 billion.

EV demand slowdown and LFP price competition

Reports said the company converted EV lines to ESS LFP lines as the slowdown in EV battery demand persisted. LFP is a market led by Chinese producers, so price competition is intense. The Hungary plant, whose utilization rose on automaker orders, still depends on EV demand. If price competition intensifies, the pace of margin improvement could slow.

10

Risk factors

Execution and revenue recognition timing

ESS contracts are not recognized as revenue immediately after an order announcement, and recognition can be delayed by construction, delivery, and inspection schedules. Securing volume for 2029 and later is still in progress.

The new LFP lines are scheduled to start in phases from the first half of 2027, so any delay would push back gains in utilization and operating margin.

Policy and tax credit variables

AMPC is structured to phase down after 2030 under US policy. Because KRW 122.5 billion of it was included in third-quarter results, a policy change could directly affect quarterly profit. How expanded local production links to the credit requirements needs to be confirmed in future disclosures and company remarks.

Non-operating items and equity stake changes

The sale of the Samsung Display (SDC) stake reduced the value of the associate holding, and the preliminary disclosure said a equity-method loss of KRW 35.8 billion was recognized in the third quarter.

Non-operating items such as a corporate tax reversal contributed to parent-attributable profit being well above operating profit. Such items may not recur, so they should be separated from the operating profit trend.

Overall view

Samsung SDI's third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 407.8 billion marked a swing to profit from a year-earlier operating loss of KRW 591.3 billion, and it rose 100.1% from the second quarter. US ESS is the center of the recovery.

The company said it secured more than 60GWh of orders through October 2026, and the new 16GWh contract with Jupiter Power lifts the cumulative supply volume to about 27GWh.

On the other hand, revenue fell for two consecutive years in 2024 and 2025, and the 2025 operating loss of KRW 1.72 trillion remains part of the track record.

Third-quarter operating profit includes AMPC tax credits of KRW 122.5 billion, so sustaining profitability after 2030, when the credits phase down, is the next test. ESS and local LFP production are becoming the growth axis as EV demand slows, a shift that appears consistently in disclosures and announcements.

Valuation will be read differently depending on how fast earnings recover. The next checkpoints are the result of the third domestic ESS auction in November 2026, the start of SBB customer deliveries in December 2026, and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, due by the end of March 2027.
