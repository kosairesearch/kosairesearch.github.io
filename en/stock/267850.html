02

Business structure

Asiana IDT is an IT services company whose main businesses are system building and operation for airline and airport systems, IT solutions for transport and logistics, and financial and external SI (system integration) work.

The company describes its business areas as system integration, IT outsourcing, consulting and solutions, and it had 427 employees as of December 31, 2025.

The business traces its roots to the IT division of parent Asiana Airlines, and running the IT systems of Asiana Airlines and its affiliated airlines is cited as the foundation of the business. Revenue is weighted toward recurring income.

According to press reports, maintenance and operation revenue rose to KRW 132.9 billion in 2025 from KRW 121.7 billion in 2024, and accounted for 68.5% of total sales. Over the same period, consulting and SI revenue fell from KRW 50 billion to KRW 34.3 billion.

Long-running maintenance contracts form the base of sales, while new build project revenue fluctuates widely from year to year. Sales of computing products also rose, but reports said this was not enough to lift total sales.

On the technology side, the company is developing its own AI model performance management solution, ModelOps.AI, along with generative AI linked solutions and a ground-handling safety AI. The order backlog at end-2025 was reported at KRW 76.8 billion.

Competitively, the company operates a captive structure centered on affiliated-group demand, which differs from large SI providers that serve a broad set of outside customers. Listed peers in the same industry include Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, and the company's revenue scale is far smaller than theirs.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 181.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 188.4 billion in 2023 (up 3.9% year on year), KRW 194 billion in 2024 (up 3.0%), and KRW 194.1 billion in 2025 (no change year on year).

Revenue has increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Operating profit fell from KRW 10.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.11 billion in 2023 (down 11.0%), which marked the low point, and then rose two straight years to KRW 9.23 billion in 2024 (up 1.2%) and KRW 12.1 billion in 2025 (up 31.5%). The company attributed the 2025 improvement mainly to winning higher-margin business.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 8.6 billion in 2022, KRW 11 billion in 2023 (up 27.5%), KRW 11.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 12.6 billion in 2025 (up 7.4%), extending growth to three consecutive years from 2023.

Net income including non-controlling interests is reported separately and was shown at the same amounts in each year. The operating margin was 5.6% in 2022, 4.8% in 2023, 4.8% in 2024 and 6.2% in 2025, the highest of the period.

Operating cash flow was KRW 7.74 billion in 2022, KRW 12.2 billion in 2023, KRW 9.84 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.52 billion in 2025, so cash generation fell in 2025 even as profit rose.

On a quarterly basis, revenue peaked at KRW 45.8 billion in Q1 2026 after KRW 43.4 billion in Q4 2025 (down 2.3% quarter on quarter), then fell to KRW 45 billion in Q2 2026 (down 1.7%) and KRW 41.6 billion in Q3 2026 (down 7.6% quarter on quarter and down 6.3% year on year), a two-quarter decline.

Operating profit in Q3 2026 was KRW 1.49 billion, down 34.1% quarter on quarter and down 46.3% year on year. In Q2 2026 operating profit rose 11.3% to KRW 2.26 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 81.0% to KRW 4.96 billion, so the two measures moved by different amounts.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 13.9 billion, above the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 12.6 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The market Asiana IDT serves is enterprise IT services, and within it, system integration and operation tailored to specific industries such as airlines, airports, logistics and finance.

In aviation, operational systems such as flight operations, airport check-in and baggage handling run for long periods, so maintenance demand tends to be steady. However, airline IT procurement can be affected by the parent's operating environment.

Reports said Asiana Airlines posted an operating loss of KRW 295.1 billion in Q2 2026, and its cargo revenue fell KRW 256.5 billion year on year to KRW 114.7 billion in the same quarter. In such conditions, the possibility of affiliated IT budgets being adjusted remains a structural risk.

On the other side, the merger of the two airlines scheduled for December 2026 could serve as an occasion to reorganize group IT systems, which may create system demand during integration.

In competition, Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever are companies with large SI capabilities and customer bases across many industries. Asiana IDT is a small to mid-sized company by revenue, but it emphasizes its understanding of aviation and airport operations and its operating experience as a differentiator.

Other companies in the same industry, including NICE Information Service and Gabia, compete in different areas. In short, the company's position depends more on affiliated-group demand and industry-specific expertise than on overall industry growth.

06

Outlook

The first variable shaping the company's direction is its ownership structure. A July 2026 report said Korean Air is considering acquiring directly the 76.22% stake in Asiana IDT held by Asiana Airlines, in order to resolve holding-company conduct restrictions under the Monopoly Regulation and Fair Trade Act.

Another report said Asiana IDT sits at a great-grandchild level rather than as a direct subsidiary of Asiana Airlines, and that Asiana Airlines' less-than-100% ownership is preventing it from meeting the legal requirement. The second variable is the group restructuring timetable.

The merger of Korean Air and Asiana Airlines is scheduled for December 2026, and until then Asiana Airlines continues to operate independently with a focus on restoring profitability in the second half. How affiliated-group IT procurement is organized after the merger is the next point to watch.

The third variable is the business pipeline. The company attributed the 2025 operating profit increase to winning higher-margin projects, and the order backlog at end-2025 was KRW 76.8 billion.

Whether its in-house AI products, including ModelOps.AI and generative AI-linked solutions, turn into actual orders is the key question. Because revenue and operating profit both declined in Q3 2026, the speed at which the pipeline converts into reported earnings should be checked in upcoming quarterly reports.

On an annual basis, the 2026 annual report will show whether the operating margin holds near the 6.2% recorded in 2025.

08

Bull factors

Revenue weighted to recurring contracts

Maintenance and operation revenue was reported at 68.5% of 2025 sales, rising from KRW 121.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 132.9 billion in 2025. Because the company runs IT for affiliated airlines, existing contracts can sustain part of revenue even when new orders weaken. The order backlog of KRW 76.8 billion at end-2025 also gives some visibility into future sales.

Profitability recovery in 2025

Operating profit in 2025 rose 31.5% to KRW 12.1 billion, and the operating margin of 6.2% was the highest of the 2022 to 2025 period. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders also rose to KRW 12.6 billion, extending growth to three consecutive years from 2023. The company attributed the improvement to winning higher-margin business.

Financial capacity

At end-2025 the debt-to-equity ratio was 24.1%, with total equity of KRW 174.3 billion and total liabilities of KRW 42 billion. The ratio moved from 28.5% in 2022 and 35.2% in 2023 to 30.3% in 2024 before falling further. Ample financial capacity widens the options available during group restructuring or business investment.

09

Bear factors

Weaker quarterly results in 2026

Revenue fell for two consecutive quarters through Q3 2026, and Q3 was down 6.3% year on year. Q3 operating profit of KRW 1.49 billion was down 34.1% quarter on quarter and 46.3% year on year.

Even though net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 13.9 billion, the weaker operating trend is a bearish factor that needs monitoring.

Shrinking consulting and SI revenue

Consulting and SI revenue fell from KRW 50 billion in 2024 to KRW 34.3 billion in 2025. Higher maintenance revenue cushioned total sales, but the decline can be read as a sign of weaker demand for new builds. Computing product sales also rose without lifting total revenue meaningfully. Recovering new orders is needed to widen growth potential.

Parent weakness and restructuring burden

Asiana Airlines was reported to have posted a Q2 2026 loss of KRW 295.1 billion. Its cargo revenue in the same quarter fell KRW 256.5 billion year on year to KRW 114.7 billion.

Weaker parent profitability could reduce or reshape affiliated IT orders, and the merger and stake reorganization could change the company's business structure.

10

Risk factors

Ownership and group restructuring

Korean Air's reported consideration of a direct stake purchase and the December 2026 merger timetable overlap, which could change Asiana IDT's ownership and its role within the group.

Reports say the aim is to resolve holding-company rules under the Fair Trade Act, but the method and timing need to be confirmed through disclosures. Changes in how affiliated IT work is allocated during restructuring are also a risk factor.

Customer concentration

Because operation of affiliated airline IT is the core demand source, the customer base is effectively concentrated within the group. The parent's business conditions can directly affect order volumes, and broadening external clients is a condition for stable earnings. The decline in consulting and SI revenue in 2025 shows that expanding new external orders remains a task.

Order volatility and cash flow

Project-based revenue swings sharply from quarter to quarter, and operating cash flow fell to KRW 7.52 billion in 2025 from KRW 9.84 billion in 2024. Profit rose while cash generation declined, so the divergence between earnings and cash is a point to check. How quickly the KRW 76.8 billion backlog at end-2025 converts into revenue also needs monitoring.

12

Overall view

Asiana IDT's revenue and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders have grown since 2022, supported by a revenue base weighted toward operation and maintenance contracts, and its operating margin reached 6.2% in 2025, the highest of the period.

The debt-to-equity ratio of 24.1% indicates a relatively stable financial structure. On the other hand, revenue fell for two consecutive quarters in 2026, and Q3 operating profit declined 46.3% year on year. The shrinking consulting and SI revenue and the weaker results at the parent are bearish factors.

Reported consideration of a direct stake purchase by Korean Air and the December 2026 merger timetable are variables that could change the business structure and procurement system, so strengths and weaknesses coexist. In summary, profitability recovered in 2025, while the quarterly trend weakened in 2026.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, quarterly results for Q1 2027, and disclosures on the ownership restructuring. This report is for information purposes only and does not offer an investment opinion.
