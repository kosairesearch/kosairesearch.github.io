Report overview

Defense exports drive profit rebound; ammunition restructuring is the swing factor

Poongsan's third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose sharply on defense exports and demand for copper materials used in AI data centers, but the company is also weighing a possible sale of its ammunition unit while a new entrant emerges in domestic ammunition.

02

Business structure

Poongsan runs two businesses: a copper processing unit (Copper Materials) and a defense unit that makes ammunition. Press reports put the revenue mix at roughly 70% copper materials and 30% defense, while defense accounts for roughly 70% of operating profit.

The defense unit produces a full range of domestic military ammunition, from 5.56mm small-caliber rounds to 155mm artillery shells, and reporting describes Poongsan as virtually the only domestic supplier across that full range.

It has also widened its drone-specific munitions lineup, including small high-explosive bombs dropped from drones and anti-armor rounds. Of the KRW 331.6 billion in third-quarter 2026 standalone defense revenue, exports were KRW 188.1 billion, with strong sports-ammunition shipments to North America among the drivers.

Its main customers are defense companies that build self-propelled howitzers and rocket launchers; Hanwha Aerospace, reportedly, receives 155mm rounds from Poongsan for K9 howitzers exported to Poland and other countries.

The copper materials unit makes copper plate, rod and wire mainly for automotive and construction materials; third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 883.7 billion, up 37.1% year on year, while shipment volume fell 3% to 44,000 tons.

Weaker auto production, a strike and a slow construction recovery weighed on volume, while demand for materials used in AI data centers and energy storage systems (ESS) rose.

In ammunition, SNT Motiv, a domestic firearms maker, is reportedly going through permit procedures to build an ammunition production facility at its Busan site, which could erode Poongsan's long-held position.

Hanwha is also reportedly studying its own ammunition production bases in Arkansas in the United States and in Estonia.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue went from KRW 4.37 trillion in 2022 to KRW 4.13 trillion in 2023 (down 5.7% year on year), then rose to KRW 4.55 trillion in 2024 (up 10.4%) and KRW 5.05 trillion in 2025 (up 10.9%), making 2025 the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit was largest in 2024 at KRW 323.8 billion (up 41.6%) and fell to KRW 297.4 billion in 2025 (down 8.1%). The operating margin climbed from 5.3% in 2022 to 7.1% in 2024 before easing to 5.9% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 236 billion in 2024 and fell to KRW 147.2 billion in 2025 (down 37.7%). Operating cash flow dropped from KRW 696.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 39.9 billion in 2024 and turned to -KRW 168.5 billion in 2025, moving from positive to negative.

Over the same period the debt-to-equity ratio rose from 84.3% in 2022 to 88.4% in 2025. Quarterly revenue in the fourth quarter of 2025 was KRW 1.42 trillion, up 21.3% from the prior quarter, and operating profit was KRW 91.5 billion, up 115.0%.

Revenue then fell 10.8% to KRW 1.27 trillion in the first quarter of 2026 and rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 1.47 trillion in the second quarter and KRW 1.64 trillion in the third. Operating profit peaked at KRW 125.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and was KRW 108.8 billion in the third quarter.

The standalone preliminary operating profit of KRW 77.3 billion for the third quarter includes equity-method gains from subsidiaries and affiliates of KRW 23.4 billion, which should be read apart from core operating earnings.

Preliminary cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 324.2 billion, announced on November 3, 2026, which is above the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 297.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In defense, the prolonged Russia-Ukraine war has driven a sharp rise in demand for 155mm artillery shells since 2022, and analysts describe secure ammunition supply as a key variable for defense exports.

In the third quarter of 2026, partial shipments were pushed back by the Middle East conflict, but sports-ammunition demand held up and revenue recognition on previously booked orders gained momentum.

Poongsan's near-sole position in domestic ammunition supply is a core strength, yet Hanwha Group is moving to secure its own ammunition supply chain. Hanwha Aerospace produces the K9 self-propelled howitzer and the Chunmoo rocket launcher and has expanded exports to Poland, Norway and Australia.

Against this backdrop, SNT Motiv's entry into ammunition is being read as a sign that a division of labor that has held for more than six decades could change.

Copper materials demand split by end market: lower auto production, a strike and a slow domestic construction recovery reduced volumes, while demand for materials used in AI data centers and ESS increased.

Fluctuations in electrolytic copper prices, the main raw material, are cited as a factor that could raise working capital needs. Expanded capital spending in copper materials and new production lines for shell casings are expected to raise short-term funding needs.

On defense profitability, one analysis notes that domestic defense sales are priced on a cost-based structure with limited margins, while export volumes can earn comparatively higher profitability.

Goodkyung Economy reported that the defense unit has generated operating profit of roughly KRW 200 billion per year since 2023.

06

Outlook

The company has forecast fourth-quarter 2026 standalone revenue of KRW 1.40 trillion and pre-tax profit of KRW 20.0 billion. On that basis, cumulative 2026 revenue would reach KRW 4.70 trillion and pre-tax profit KRW 303.7 billion.

These are company projections, to be compared with the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results when they are released. In defense, the company plans to focus on timely shipments of export contracts and on expanding new orders.

In domestic business, however, the company expects delays in large-caliber shell production and delivery because of supply disruptions for government-supplied materials.

On the ammunition unit, a Korea Economic Daily report on March 4, 2026 first described a planned sale, with a value of around KRW 1.5 trillion including a control premium cited in the market. In the same report, a Poongsan official said the company had never pursued a sale of its ammunition business.

In April 2026, reports said Hanwha Aerospace had submitted a final bid in a private auction for the unit, while major competitors were reportedly not bidding. In its disclosure, the company said it is reviewing various options, including restructuring its business, but nothing specific has been finalized.

If a sale proceeds, it would need approval from the Minister of Trade, Industry and Energy under the Defense Industry Act, a merger review by the Fair Trade Commission, and a special resolution at a shareholders' meeting requiring two-thirds of all shareholders. Reports differ on whether and when a sale will proceed, and no confirmed timetable has been identified.

08

Bull factors

Defense exports and profit contribution

Third-quarter 2026 standalone defense revenue rose 46% year on year to KRW 331.6 billion, of which exports were KRW 188.1 billion. Partial shipments were delayed by the Middle East conflict, but sports-ammunition demand stayed firm.

Press reports say defense makes up about 30% of revenue but about 70% of operating profit. Some analysts argue export volumes can carry higher margins than domestic cost-based sales.

Demand for AI data center materials

Copper materials revenue reached KRW 883.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 37.1% year on year and slightly lower than the prior quarter. Reports say demand for materials used in AI data centers and ESS partly offset weakness in autos and construction. Whether higher-value material mix can grow while volumes are falling is the key question for this unit.

Profit recovery and credit rating

Preliminary cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 324.2 billion, announced on November 3, 2026, up 57.5% year on year. That is above the full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 297.4 billion.

Reports say the company's credit rating was raised by one notch, citing better profitability and cash generation from defense exports.

Bear factors

Uncertainty over restructuring of the ammunition unit

Press reports describe a possible sale of the ammunition unit, but the company says nothing specific has been finalized. A sale would materially change the profit structure of a defense unit that is reported to generate about 70% of operating profit.

Restrictions under the Defense Industry Act on foreign nationals holding control of defense companies are cited as a background factor. Whether and when a sale happens remains open.

Weak volumes in copper materials

Copper materials shipments were 44,000 tons in the third quarter of 2026, down 3% year on year. Lower auto output, a strike and a slow construction recovery were cited as causes. Revenue grew while volume shrank, which points to reliance on price and mix effects. A timeline for a volume recovery has not been confirmed.

Cash flow and financial burden

Operating cash flow moved from KRW 39.9 billion in 2024 to -KRW 168.5 billion in 2025, turning negative. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 84.3% in 2022 to 88.4% in 2025. Swings in copper prices could raise working capital needs. A new entrant in domestic ammunition adds to the possibility of more volatile profitability.

Risk factors

Policy and regulation

A sale of the ammunition unit requires approval from the Minister of Trade, Industry and Energy under the Defense Industry Act and a merger review by the Fair Trade Commission. A split would require a special resolution at a shareholders' meeting, needing two-thirds of all shareholders.

Some observers expect it to be hard to win minority shareholder support for selling the most profitable unit. Longer procedures would delay any restructuring.

Raw materials and cash flow

Fluctuations in electrolytic copper prices, the main raw material, could increase working capital needs. Capital spending on copper materials and new shell-casing lines are expected to raise short-term funding requirements. Operating cash flow was -KRW 168.5 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio of 88.4% in 2025 is higher than in 2022.

Geopolitics and delivery timing

Partial third-quarter shipments were pushed back by the Middle East conflict. In the domestic market, supply disruptions for government-supplied materials are expected to delay large-caliber production and delivery. Defense results could swing from quarter to quarter depending on export shipment timing. Even with strong demand, revenue recognition shifts later when delivery slips.

Overall view

Poongsan posted third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 1.64 trillion and operating profit of KRW 108.8 billion, reflecting both higher defense exports and demand for copper materials. In reported annual results, operating profit was largest in 2024 at KRW 323.8 billion and was KRW 297.4 billion in 2025.

Preliminary cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 was KRW 324.2 billion, above the 2025 full-year figure. Defense accounts for about 30% of revenue but, according to press reports, about 70% of operating profit, so its share of earnings is large.

Copper materials volumes fell, and operating cash flow turned negative at -KRW 168.5 billion in 2025. A sale of the ammunition unit has not been finalized, but if it proceeds, the business structure and the basis for valuing the company could both change.

In domestic ammunition, the entry of SNT Motiv has raised questions about the end of a near-monopoly. The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual report, and any disclosure on the ammunition unit sale.
