Report overview

Synthetic mica maker adds AI unit as quarterly profit recovers

China Crystal New Material Holdings is adding an AI infrastructure unit to its synthetic mica business. Operating profit has risen for two straight quarters after a 2025 net loss, while revenue and operating cash flow remain below earlier years.

  1. 1

    The company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.83 billion in 2025, after a KRW 9.03 billion profit in 2024. The most recent four quarters (4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026) total a KRW 7.83 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders.

  2. 2

    Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 20.3 billion, up 11.3% from the prior quarter but down 29.1% from a year earlier. Operating profit was KRW 3.38 billion, rising for two consecutive quarters after the first-quarter 2026 low.

  3. 3

    On November 18, 2026, the company said its AI subsidiary GeneVision AI Technology Holdings could exceed RMB 40 million in 2026 annual revenue, and that AI could account for about 10% of group revenue.

  4. 4

02

Business structure

China Crystal New Material Holdings is a China-based specialty materials company listed on KOSDAQ in January 2016.

It covers the chain from synthetic mica raw materials to high-value application materials, with main product lines in synthetic mica flakes and powders, pearlescent pigments, graphene powder, and natural and synthetic mica tapes.

An equity information site lists pearl pigments, insulation materials and thermal insulation materials as the main revenue sources. Synthetic mica offers strong insulation, high-temperature and corrosion resistance, allowing it to replace natural mica in automotive coatings, cosmetics and power equipment.

Pearlescent pigments are functional materials used in cosmetics, automotive paints, premium packaging and plastics. Mica tape demand is tied mainly to growth in China's home appliance industry.

Customers are centered on Chinese appliance, automotive and cosmetics companies, and the company says it has cooperation ties with major Chinese telecom operators.

In November 2025 it acquired 100% of AI technology company GeneVision AI Technology Holdings, extending its business from synthetic mica and composites into an integrated 'AI technology plus advanced materials' model.

The subsidiary handles AI infrastructure contracts such as computing resource operation and predictive equipment maintenance. The company said five contracts signed in the third quarter of 2026 total more than RMB 10 million and have all been delivered and accepted.

The structure of handling everything from synthetic mica raw materials to finished application products within one company is presented as its differentiator.

04

Earnings analysis

The company reports in renminbi, and the amounts below are translated into won. Revenue went from KRW 81.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 90 billion in 2023 (up 9.8% year on year) and KRW 104.5 billion in 2024 (up 16.1%), then fell to KRW 71.1 billion in 2025 (down 31.9%).

Across 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit rose from KRW 20.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 25.4 billion in 2023 (up 25.4%), which was the peak, then fell to KRW 20 billion in 2024 (down 21.1%) and KRW 10.9 billion in 2025 (down 45.6%), declining two years in a row from 2024 through 2025.

The operating margin was 24.7% in 2022, 28.2% in 2023, 19.2% in 2024 and 15.3% in 2025, lower since 2023. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 15.1 billion in 2022, KRW 8.24 billion in 2023 (down 45.4%) and KRW 9.03 billion in 2024 (up 9.6%), then turned to a net loss of KRW 1.83 billion in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 16.2 billion in 2022, KRW 10.1 billion in 2023, KRW 9.03 billion in 2024, and a net loss of KRW 1.83 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 changes to the disposal of subsidiaries, with the related business excluded from continuing operations from 2025, and the missing fourth-quarter 2025 revenue reflects the classification of that subsidiary's results as discontinued operations after its sale.

By quarter, third-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 28.6 billion with operating profit of KRW 4.54 billion, and the fourth quarter of 2025 swung to an operating loss of KRW 2.26 billion, a loss after the prior quarter.

In the first quarter of 2026 revenue was KRW 11 billion with operating profit of KRW 523 million, a return to profit, and the second quarter brought revenue of KRW 18.2 billion (up 65.0% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 2.4 billion (up 358.9%).

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 20.3 billion (up 11.3% from the prior quarter, down 29.1% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 3.38 billion (up 40.7% from the prior quarter, down 25.6% year on year).

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 73.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 56.8 billion in 2023, KRW 23.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 21.8 billion in 2025, while the debt ratio edged up from 6.7% in 2022 to 7.1% in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Synthetic mica is used as a substitute for natural mica in automotive coatings, cosmetics and power equipment, and demand differs by product line.

An equity information site, Company Monitor, states that the global synthetic mica market has grown more than 20% a year recently, but this is that site's description and not a figure confirmed by independent market research.

The same source notes that low heavy-metal content is raising demand for synthetic mica in cosmetics. Demand for mica tape is linked to growth in China's home appliance industry, so the appliance cycle in China affects the revenue direction.

At a China-Korea summit on November 18, 2026, the two countries announced a substantive conclusion of second-phase FTA negotiations on services and investment, raising expectations for industrial exchange.

The company plans to expand supply of synthetic mica for cosmetics and automotive uses together with Korean companies. Compared with broader-portfolio listed chemical companies such as LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions and SKC, the company's portfolio is concentrated in synthetic mica and pearlescent pigments.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was 29.1% lower year on year, so whether demand recovery is reaching reported earnings is a point to confirm in the next periodic report. The AI infrastructure business sits on a separate demand driver tied to computing resources, so its drivers differ from those of the mica business.

06

Outlook

On November 18, 2026, the company said its AI subsidiary GeneVision AI Technology Holdings is expected to exceed RMB 40 million in 2026 annual revenue.

At the same briefing it said about 80% of the annual revenue target was estimated to be achieved as of the end of the third quarter, which is the company's own estimate and not a figure confirmed in a periodic report. It also expects AI to account for about 10% of group revenue.

Planned projects include building a computing power network linking major regions of China, a Central Asia project of 2000P scale, and a cooperation network across 20 countries.

In September 2026, reports covered a computing-power and electricity convergence contract with China Mobile and participation in a Guangzhou data international cooperation pilot.

On November 23, 2026, the company said it would expand supply of synthetic mica materials for cosmetics and automotive uses in Korea and Asia-Pacific, and strengthen joint material development, quality certification and supply chain cooperation with Korean companies.

Pearlescent pigments are included among the high-value products targeted for expansion. Whether the next periodic report separately discloses AI business revenue is the point that will test the company's outlook.

For the existing synthetic mica business, whether quarterly operating profit keeps rising after the second quarter of 2026 is likely to shape the earnings direction.

Bull factors

AI infrastructure subsidiary added

In November 2025 the company acquired 100% of AI technology firm GeneVision AI Technology Holdings, adding AI infrastructure work to its synthetic mica core.

In the third quarter of 2026 the subsidiary signed five contracts covering computing resource operation and predictive equipment maintenance, with cumulative value above RMB 10 million, all delivered and accepted, according to the company.

If the forecast of more than RMB 40 million in 2026 annual revenue is realized, a new revenue stream outside the materials business would add to reported results, which is the main bullish factor.

Quarterly operating profit recovery

After a fourth-quarter 2025 operating loss of KRW 2.26 billion, operating profit returned to KRW 523 million in the first quarter of 2026, then rose to KRW 2.4 billion in the second quarter and KRW 3.38 billion in the third, increasing for two consecutive quarters.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was up 40.7% from the prior quarter. Revenue also rose for two consecutive quarters, from KRW 11 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 20.3 billion in the third, showing a recovery from the quarterly low.

Balance sheet room and Korean market push

Total liabilities were KRW 35.2 billion at the end of 2025 against total equity of KRW 493.5 billion, and the debt ratio was 7.1%.

On November 23, 2026 the company announced plans to expand supply of synthetic mica for cosmetics and automotive uses in Korea and Asia-Pacific, citing the substantive conclusion of second-phase FTA services and investment talks announced at the China-Korea summit on November 18. With a light debt load, widening overseas channels for high-value materials is cited as a bullish factor.

Bear factors

Swing to loss and continuing losses

The 2025 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.83 billion, reversing from a KRW 9.03 billion profit in 2024, and the most recent four quarters total a KRW 7.83 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, including a KRW 2.26 billion operating loss in the fourth quarter of 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 swing to the disposal of subsidiaries and their exclusion from continuing operations. Whether 2026 quarterly profits will translate into a full-year profit has not yet been confirmed in a periodic report.

Revenue decline and margin compression

Revenue fell 31.9% from KRW 104.5 billion in 2024 to KRW 71.1 billion in 2025, and third-quarter 2026 revenue was down 29.1% year on year. The operating margin declined from 28.2% in 2023 to 19.2% in 2024 and 15.3% in 2025.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was 25.6% lower than a year earlier, so the quarterly recovery has not yet brought profit back to prior-year levels.

Weaker cash generation

Operating cash flow declined from KRW 73.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 56.8 billion in 2023, KRW 23.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 21.8 billion in 2025. Total liabilities of KRW 35.2 billion at the end of 2025 leave the debt burden small, but the sharp drop in cash generated from operations alongside lower profit is a weakness.

10

Risk factors

Exchange rate

The company reports in renminbi, so reported figures in won move with the won-yuan exchange rate. The 2025 disclosure translated income items at the average rate and balance sheet items at the year-end rate, so the gap between the two rates feeds into the won figures. A move in the exchange rate changes both won-translated revenue and profit and the won value of net assets.

AI business execution and contract delivery

The 2026 revenue forecast of more than RMB 40 million for the AI subsidiary, and its estimated progress toward the target, are management plans.

The five contracts have been delivered and accepted, but many projects such as the Central Asia 2000P project and the 20-country cooperation network are still in the pursuit stage. If new contracts or capital recovery from AI projects are delayed, weakness in the materials business would remain in reported results.

Policy and geopolitics

Improved China-Korea relations are presented as the premise for the planned expansion into the Korean market, so a change in bilateral ties could delay supply expansion for cosmetics and automotive uses.

The AI business, which handles cross-border data transfer verification and computing resource allocation, may be affected by changes in data regulation in China. Both are external conditions the company cannot fully control.

12

Overall view

China Crystal New Material Holdings is built on synthetic mica materials and has widened its scope with the November 2025 addition of an AI subsidiary.

On reported figures, revenue peaked at KRW 104.5 billion in 2024 before falling to KRW 71.1 billion in 2025, and the operating margin declined from 28.2% in 2023 to 15.3% in 2025.

The company swung to a KRW 1.83 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in 2025, and the most recent four quarters total a KRW 7.83 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders.

On the other side, operating profit turned positive at KRW 523 million in the first quarter of 2026 and rose over the second and third quarters, and the debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 7.1%, a light financial load.

The forecast of more than RMB 40 million in 2026 annual revenue for the AI subsidiary and the roughly 10% share of group revenue are company plans that have not yet been confirmed in a periodic report.

With both strengths and weaknesses present, the 2026 annual report and whether AI revenue is disclosed separately are the next checkpoints. The discount to net assets and the divergence between profit recovery and valuation shape how the valuation reads.
