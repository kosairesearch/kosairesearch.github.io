Reliability testing expands as quarterly profit swings

02

Business structure

Quality Reliability Technology (QRT) is a specialist in semiconductor reliability testing and comprehensive analysis services. Reliability testing subjects prototype chips under development to heat and radiation stress to predict their long-term lifespan and suitability for mass production.

The comprehensive analysis service helps customers trace the causes of defects when they occur, and is bundled with burn-in testing as a total solution. A February 2026 report said reliability testing and comprehensive analysis together account for close to 95% of revenue.

First-quarter 2026 reliability testing revenue reached KRW 11.5 billion, up 32.7% from KRW 8.7 billion a year earlier. In the first half of 2025, comprehensive analysis revenue rose 50% year on year and drove top-line growth.

The equipment business started when existing external tools could not meet test specifications, so the company began building its own; its main lineup includes SEE analysis systems and RF HTOL lifetime evaluation systems.

Equipment revenue was KRW 500 million for full-year 2025 and KRW 1.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026. According to the 2023 first-half investment prospectus, the application mix was AI at 53.4%, IoT and 5G at 16.5%, and memory at 7.1%.

The company serves about 500 customers, including major domestic and overseas semiconductor firms, and KB Securities described QRT as the only reliability verification company for semiconductors in Korea.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 59.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 53.3 billion in 2023 (down 10.7% year on year) before recovering to KRW 65.3 billion in 2024 (up 22.5%) and KRW 68.9 billion in 2025 (up 5.5%). 2023 was the low point, revenue rose for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022–2025 span.

Operating profit fell from KRW 10.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 752 million in 2023 (down 92.7%), then rose to KRW 4.77 billion in 2024 (up 533.8%) and KRW 4.78 billion in 2025 (up 0.3%), increasing for two consecutive years. 2022 was the largest operating profit year of the span.

The operating margin was 17.2% in 2022, 1.4% in 2023, 7.3% in 2024 and 6.9% in 2025, staying around 7% since 2024.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 6.43 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.62 billion in 2023 (down 59.2%), then rose to KRW 2.86 billion in 2024 (up 8.9%) and KRW 2.89 billion in 2025 (up 1.3%), increasing for two consecutive years.

Net income including non-controlling interests was also reported at KRW 2.89 billion for 2025. Revenue in 2025 exceeded the 2022 level, but operating profit remains less than half of 2022, which suggests that changes in the cost structure are weighing on margins.

Operating cash flow was KRW 19.1 billion in 2022, KRW 8.97 billion in 2023, KRW 16 billion in 2024 and KRW 13.8 billion in 2025, broadly moving in the same direction as earnings.

The debt ratio went from 43.4% in 2022 and 41.7% in 2023 to 50.9% in 2024 and 74.2% in 2025, when total liabilities were KRW 66.3 billion and total equity was KRW 89.3 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders.

On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 had revenue of KRW 19 billion, operating profit of KRW 2.04 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 1.36 billion; the fourth quarter fell to revenue of KRW 17.1 billion (down 10.2% quarter on quarter), operating profit of KRW 995 million (down 51.4%) and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 309 million (down 77.3%).

The first quarter of 2026 reached revenue of KRW 20.4 billion (up 19.5%), operating profit of KRW 2.98 billion (up 199.5%) and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 2.68 billion (up 766.7%), the peak quarter, and media reported it as the company's largest quarterly result on record.

The second quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 18.5 billion, operating profit of KRW 1.23 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 1.11 billion, all below the prior quarter.

The third quarter of 2026 had revenue of KRW 18.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.66 billion (up 35.6% quarter on quarter), but controlling-shareholder net income was only KRW 62.9 million (down 94.3% quarter on quarter and down 95.4% year on year), marking a second consecutive quarterly decline.

The gap between operating profit of KRW 1.66 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 62.9 million should be checked against non-operating items and income tax in the regular report.

Controlling-shareholder net income for the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 through third quarter of 2026) totals KRW 4.15 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for reliability testing grows as semiconductors become more miniaturized and more highly integrated. In a September 2025 report, KB Securities said that thinner, smaller and more feature-rich chips raise inspection difficulty, which leads to longer test times and higher unit prices.

A March 2026 KB Securities report argued that next-generation memory technologies such as HBM4 and SOCAMM2 trigger demand for reliability inspection.

Applications are widening into AI data centers and autonomous driving, and the company has built a response system for ISO 26262 in line with Hyundai Motor and its suppliers adopting the standard from 2024.

Demand from space and defense is rising, led by government agencies and private firms, and QRT positions its SEE analysis systems as the basis for entering that market. Media reported that the business scope also covers evaluation of next-generation semiconductor materials such as glass substrates.

On competition, the company says its self-developed evaluation equipment cut Korea's dependence on overseas equipment by 70%, presenting localization as a strength in areas with heavy import reliance.

The cycle position of the upstream memory market is less direct a driver than the development schedules of customers' new products, which determine testing volume.

Consequently, timing of new product development and customer quality approval, rather than the industry cycle alone, is likely to determine quarterly results.

06

Outlook

On November 13, 2026, the company said it plans to launch reliability evaluation and comprehensive analysis services for servers used in AI data centers, along with a new space and aerospace business, soon.

Its strategy is to go beyond simple verification requests and strengthen the revenue structure through self-developed equipment and customized test and evaluation services. As a near-term task, the company cited delivering overseas equipment ordered in the fourth quarter without disruption.

In the first half of 2026 it delivered RF HTOL equipment to a European defense customer, and it said equipment orders from global customers continued into the second half.

In May 2026, media reported that RF HTOL revenue from a European order placed early in the year would be reflected in results from the second half, and the amount recognized can be checked in segment revenue in the annual report.

For reliability testing, a Yuanta Securities analysis dated May 2026 expects new products such as SOCAMM, PIM and HBF to follow HBM and CXL. In May 2026, media also reported that the company internally expects annual revenue in the KRW 80 billion range, with delays in end-customer quality approval cited as a variable.

Adding the disclosed quarterly revenue for the first through third quarters of 2026 gives KRW 57.8 billion, so reaching at least KRW 80 billion for the year would require fourth-quarter revenue of at least KRW 22.2 billion.

Whether that threshold is met can be checked against fourth-quarter results and segment revenue in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Rising reliability testing demand

Next-generation memory such as HBM4 and SOCAMM2 is cited as raising inspection difficulty and therefore testing volume. First-quarter 2026 reliability testing revenue of KRW 11.5 billion rose 32.7% from a year earlier. Comprehensive analysis revenue grew 50% year on year in the first half of 2025. Growth has shown up first in the testing service line.

Equipment business begins to generate revenue

Equipment revenue in the first quarter of 2026 alone exceeded the full-year 2025 figure. The company says its self-developed evaluation equipment cut Korea's dependence on overseas equipment by 70%. RF HTOL systems were supplied to a European defense customer, and North America and Taiwan are being pursued in parallel. Whether equipment sales build up as an additional line on top of testing services is the point to watch.

Wider applications in space, defense and AI data centers

Space, aerospace and defense demand is rising, led by government agencies and private firms, and QRT leads with its SEE analysis systems. On November 13, 2026, the company said it would soon launch server evaluation for AI data centers and a new space and aerospace business. Media also reported that the first quarter of 2026 was the company's record quarterly result.

09

Bear factors

High volatility in quarterly profit

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 2.68 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then fell to KRW 1.11 billion in the second quarter and KRW 62.9 million in the third, a second consecutive decline. The third quarter was down 95.4% year on year. The last four quarters total KRW 4.15 billion, so annual figures depend heavily on individual quarters.

Operating margin still below its earlier level

The operating margin fell from 17.2% in 2022 to 1.4% in 2023, then reached 7.3% in 2024 and 6.9% in 2025. Operating profit of KRW 4.78 billion in 2025 is still below the KRW 10.3 billion of 2022. Earnings are recovering in direction but remain some distance from earlier levels. How far equipment and new businesses can lift the margin is the next item to track.

Financial burden and ongoing capex needs

The debt ratio is higher in 2025 than in 2022, and that burden sits alongside the need for facility investment. KB Securities cited the need for continuous facility investment as a risk factor. Expanding the equipment business comes with capital spending, so debt trends should be read together with cash flow.

Risk factors

Customer quality approval and delivery timing

Delays in end-customer quality approval have been cited as a variable for internal revenue expectations. Equipment revenue depends on delivery timing and can swing quarterly results sharply.

In the third quarter of 2026, operating profit rose from the prior quarter, but net income attributable to controlling shareholders was only KRW 62.9 million.

Business concentration

Reliability testing and comprehensive analysis account for close to 95% of revenue, so demand in these two businesses drives results. Media reported that new businesses such as equipment and space and defense still make up a small share of revenue. If the new businesses do not take hold, dependence on the core business will persist.

Shift in financial structure

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 74.2%, well above 43.4% in 2022. Because the business needs ongoing facility investment, the relationship between borrowing and operating cash flow must be watched. Operating cash flow was KRW 13.8 billion in 2025.

12

Overall view

QRT has shown a recovery in revenue and earnings since the 2023 low, centered on semiconductor reliability testing. Revenue of KRW 68.9 billion in 2025 was the largest year of the 2022–2025 span. However, the operating margin fell to 6.9% in 2025 from 17.2% in 2022, so earnings remain some distance from earlier levels.

The first quarter of 2026 peaked at revenue of KRW 20.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.98 billion, but controlling-shareholder net income declined in the second and third quarters.

Equipment revenue of KRW 1.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026 shows early traction, but its share of total revenue is still small. The debt ratio rose to 74.2% at the end of 2025, increasing the financial burden from facility investment and the equipment expansion.

Bullish factors are demand for next-generation chip reliability testing and wider applications in space, defense and AI data centers, while bearish factors are high quarterly profit volatility, a low operating margin and a changing financial structure.

The next points to confirm are overseas equipment deliveries at the end of December 2026 and the fourth-quarter results and non-operating items in the 2026 annual report.
