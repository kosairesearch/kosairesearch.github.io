02

Business structure

Bioten was founded in October 2010 and manufactures and sells health foods and animal feed supplements in Jeongeup, North Jeolla Province. It was newly listed on the KONEX market on October 13, 2023.

The company develops materials based on its water-solubilization technology for poorly soluble natural compounds (WIN-T), under joint research and technology transfer agreements with the Korea Research Institute of Bioscience and Biotechnology.

According to reports at the time of its 2023 listing, it produced nine health functional food products and 26 feed supplement products.

Its core material, TENQMIN S Plus (TSP), is registered with the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety as a food ingredient and is a water-soluble curcumin-based material designed to raise the bioavailability of curcuminoid compounds.

The company has also pursued overseas expansion in the United States, China, Vietnam and Central Asia under its own TENQMIN brand. Revenue shares by segment could not be confirmed, so no figures are given.

On competition, the top 15 companies accounted for 67.8% of market production in 2024, so concentration is pronounced. Bioten is far smaller than these large manufacturers, which makes material differentiation and research partnerships the center of its business.

03

Recent trends

In the standalone filing for fiscal 2025 (the 15th term), revenue was KRW 3,749 million, with an operating loss of KRW 905 million and a net loss of KRW 831 million. The same filing shows total assets of KRW 7,187 million, total liabilities of KRW 5,184 million and total equity of KRW 2,003 million.

The loss per share was KRW 232. According to financial data compiled by Saramin, revenue in 2022 was KRW 10.70585 billion with an operating profit of about KRW 1.777 billion. In 2023, revenue was about KRW 5.40184 billion with an operating loss of about KRW 452 million.

In 2024, revenue was about KRW 5.48792 billion, with an operating loss of about KRW 916 million and a net loss of about KRW 1.204 billion. Revenue has therefore declined since 2022, and operating results have been negative for three consecutive fiscal years from 2023 through 2025.

As of October 1, 2026, Hankyung Markets showed a share price of KRW 2,670, market capitalization of KRW 9.5 billion and 3,576,689 listed shares, with changes of -17.85% over three months and -32.49% over twelve months.

On October 27, 2026 the shares closed at KRW 2,800 (-1.75% day on day), with market capitalization of KRW 10 billion and a 52-week range of KRW 2,000 to KRW 4,885.

04

Outlook

Bioten's future business centers on commercializing TSP in adjunct oncology materials and health functional foods. At ASCO in June 2026, the company presented results from a roughly 24-month study of 34 colorectal cancer patients undergoing chemotherapy.

The study observed increased cellular immune activity in more patients at week 12 than at week 4. The researchers noted that the small sample and lack of a control group mean follow-up studies are needed to confirm any actual therapeutic effect.

The company also said it secured a patent registration decision for a composition for preventing or treating colorectal cancer. According to the Korea Health Functional Food Association, however, the domestic market in 2025 was KRW 5.9626 trillion, up just 0.2% from the prior year.

The same survey put the purchase experience rate at 83.6%, a five-year high, with internet malls accounting for 71% of channels.

Whether the material milestones translate into higher revenue and profitability will be visible only in filings for fiscal 2026 and later, and any room for operating margin improvement depends on whether revenue scale recovers.

05

Bull factors

Technology and patent base

Bioten has developed its solubilized materials using technology transferred from the Korea Research Institute of Bioscience and Biotechnology. TSP is registered as a food ingredient, and a 2024 report said a US patent application had been filed.

In 2026 the company also secured a patent registration decision for a colorectal cancer composition. This intellectual property base leaves open the possibility of material supply and technology partnerships.

Clinical data from an international conference

The final results of the Konyang University Hospital study of TSP were presented at ASCO in June 2026. The company said increased activity of natural killer cells and cytotoxic T cells was observed. The final results followed an interim analysis released in April 2025, showing continued data accumulation. These findings can serve as a reference when follow-up studies are designed.

Narrowing net loss and broader consumer base

By Saramin's compilation, the net loss narrowed from about KRW 1.204 billion in 2024 to about KRW 831 million in 2025. The operating loss in 2025, about KRW 905 million, was similar to about KRW 916 million in 2024.

In the health functional food association survey, the purchase experience rate among senior households rose 2.6 percentage points, and consumption broadened across generations. Market size has stalled, but the consumer base is widening.

06

Bear factors

Falling revenue and three straight operating losses

Revenue fell from KRW 10.70585 billion in 2022 to KRW 3,749 million in 2025. After an operating profit in 2022, results turned negative from 2023 through 2025. The 2025 operating loss of KRW 905 million is substantial relative to revenue. Filings do not confirm when the loss structure might be resolved.

Thin equity and heavy liabilities

Total liabilities of KRW 5,184 million at the end of fiscal 2025 exceeded total equity of KRW 2,003 million. The current market capitalization of KRW 10 billion is far larger than year-end equity. If losses continue, equity could shrink further. Funding gaps filled through borrowing or equity issuance may bring financial burden or shareholder dilution.

Stagnant market and top-heavy structure

The domestic health functional food market grew just 0.2% in 2025, and the rapid growth of 2021 to 2022 has faded. The three most recognized brands hold a firm lead in consumer awareness and purchases. The top 15 companies also held a high 67.8% share of production in 2024. This is a difficult environment for a small materials company to expand through its own brand.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Health functional foods are sensitive to household spending, and a survey noted a stronger tilt toward value-oriented consumption. With internet malls accounting for 71% of sales, price competition could intensify. Overseas expansion depends on exchange rates, local regulation and securing distribution. A slowdown or consumption pullback could amplify revenue volatility for a small company.

Regulatory and clinical

The TSP study was an exploratory observation with few patients and no control group, so it does not establish a therapeutic effect. Expanding into health functional foods or pharmaceuticals would require further clinical work and food and drug regulatory procedures.

The results and timing of follow-up studies have not been confirmed. Changes in rules on efficacy claims and advertising could also affect the pace of commercialization.

Liquidity and financial

As a KONEX-listed stock with trading value of only KRW 12,355, trading is extremely thin. Even small trades can move the price sharply, and executing at a desired time can be difficult. With liabilities above equity at the end of fiscal 2025, a tougher funding environment would raise the burden. Financial changes will be hard to verify until fiscal 2026 results are filed.

08

Overall view

Bioten is a small materials company building clinical data and patents around TSP, a water-soluble curcumin material based on technology from the Korea Research Institute of Bioscience and Biotechnology.

Its reported fiscal 2025 results, with revenue of KRW 3,749 million, an operating loss of KRW 905 million and a net loss of KRW 831 million, show that business progress is not yet reflected in the financials.

A balance sheet in which total liabilities exceed total equity, together with thin trading, is a separate consideration. The domestic health functional food market grew only 0.2% in 2025 and is dominated by large players.

On the other hand, a broadening consumer base and follow-up progress on TSP research could support improvement in business metrics. However, the study presented in June 2026 was exploratory, which limits how far its results can be interpreted.

Whether material milestones translate into revenue and profitability should be confirmed through future filings, and the judgment is left to the reader.
