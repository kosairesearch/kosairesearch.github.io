Report overview

Glass-substrate pivot as losses persist

Revenue has declined for three straight quarters as the core cover-glass business weakens, while TGV glass substrates are presented as a new growth axis. JNTC has posted losses for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

  1. 1

    Revenue fell to KRW 185.7 billion in 2025, the second straight annual decline, and operating loss widened to KRW 78 billion.

  2. 2

    Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 35.5 billion, down 8.5% from the prior quarter, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 45.1 billion.

  3. 3

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

JNTC was founded in 1996 and makes cover glass (tempered glass) and connectors. Mobile cover glass is the core business, covering mobile, automotive and wearable uses. Its integrated automotive cover glass was developed and commercialized in 2020.

Connector sales are centered on supply to domestic set makers, with waterproof C-Type connectors supporting revenue. Customers include Chinese smartphone makers and domestic set and panel makers, and reliance on Chinese customers has been cited as one driver of earnings swings.

In the first half of 2026, cover-glass revenue declined on chip supply issues at a Chinese customer. The new TGV (through-glass via) glass-substrate business entered semiconductor glass substrates in 2024 and built its TGV production process within about two years.

The company says it internalizes the full process from laser processing and chemical etching to plating and CMP polishing, and it absorbed its plating and etching subsidiary COMET by merger. Production bases are a Hwaseong plant in Gyeonggi and plants in Vietnam, where production lines are being expanded.

HDD glass platters are also cited as a new business. A May 2026 report by Small Insight Research said JNTC passed initial quality tests at 16 global customers and is supplying paid samples to seven of them.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 161.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 323.4 billion in 2023, a 100.5% increase and the largest year in the 2022–2025 period. Revenue then fell 15.5% to KRW 273.2 billion in 2024 and 32.1% to KRW 185.7 billion in 2025, two consecutive annual declines.

For 2024, the decline was attributed to weaker cover-glass sales amid China's domestic slowdown, premium-segment competition and chip shortages. In 2025, weaker demand from Chinese customers and automotive applications was cited for the second-quarter shortfall.

Operating margin moved from -27.5% in 2022 to 8.8% in 2023, then to -16.9% in 2024 and -42.0% in 2025. The 2023 operating profit of KRW 28.5 billion marked a return to profit, but operating losses of KRW 46.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 78 billion in 2025 followed, two consecutive loss years.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 17.7 billion in 2023 to net losses of KRW 33.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 91.6 billion in 2025. Yuanta-type analysis attributed the wider 2025 loss to higher investment in new businesses.

Quarterly revenue peaked at KRW 42.4 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and then declined for three straight quarters to KRW 35.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Operating losses ran for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025, reaching KRW 19.4 billion in the third quarter of 2026 after KRW 25 billion in the second quarter.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 45.1 billion in the third quarter of 2026, the largest among the five quarters shown. The four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 total a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 96.4 billion.

A securities information site noted that first-half 2026 revenue fell year on year while operating losses narrowed slightly.

05

Industry analysis

TGV glass substrates are drawing attention as a next-generation packaging material that could complement conventional package substrates as AI and high-performance computing chips spread. Analysts have cited lower heat and structural warpage compared with plastic-based substrates as an advantage.

The company says its product reduces thickness by 25% and cuts power consumption by more than 30% versus plastic-based substrates; these are company figures. Glass interposers are used in niche products such as some medical devices.

JNTC states that it does not perform packaging itself and therefore does not compete with packaging companies, which are its customers. The company's view is that many players have not yet brought TGV glass into mass production at the front-end stage.

The core cover-glass market is volatile, driven by Chinese smartphone and automotive demand, and its recovery affects the funding base and earnings support for the new business. On the supply side, the company highlights internalizing plating and etching technology through subsidiary consolidation.

Relative to peers, the TGV business remains at an early stage because mass-production results have not yet appeared in disclosures.

06

Outlook

JNTC's TGV glass-substrate plan is presented in stages. In July 2026 it signed a commercialization agreement with TOPPAN of Japan, and the two companies will jointly review product performance and mass-production applicability.

The company says its 2.0mm-thick product is made from a single glass sheet and that mass-production yield has reached about 94%.

CEO Cho Nam-hyuk said the company is running projects and product evaluations with leading global semiconductor firms and packaging companies in China, Japan, Europe and Korea, targeting mass production in 2027. He also said the company plans to pursue a strategic domestic mass-production line expansion.

Timing and scale of that expansion have not been disclosed. Small Insight Research projected in a May 2026 report that 2026 revenue would reach KRW 401.1 billion, more than double the prior year, with operating profit of KRW 68.9 billion turning positive.

That is a third-party estimate and differs widely from the quarterly revenue trend disclosed for the first three quarters of 2026. The company also presented a 2028 revenue target of KRW 1 trillion in 2025 reporting.

Items to confirm next are whether mass production begins in 2027, the timing and size of domestic expansion, and when TGV revenue becomes visible in reported results.

08

Bull factors

Progress toward TGV commercialization

JNTC signed a TGV glass-substrate commercialization agreement with TOPPAN in July 2026. The company puts mass-production yield for its 2.0mm single-sheet product at about 94%. Evaluations are under way with customers across several regions, according to company statements and a third-party report.

Production base and in-house process

The company operates a Hwaseong plant in Gyeonggi and production lines in Vietnam, where capacity is being expanded. It says it holds the process from laser processing through etching, plating and CMP polishing in-house. Merging its plating and etching subsidiary is presented as a supply-side strength.

Profitability record in past profitable years

In 2023 the company returned to profit with an operating margin of 8.8%. Revenue that year of KRW 323.4 billion was the largest in the 2022–2025 period. The disclosed record shows that the 2023 profitable year can serve as a reference point if business indicators recover.

Bear factors

Continuing quarterly losses

JNTC has posted operating losses for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. The third-quarter 2026 operating loss was KRW 19.4 billion and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 45.1 billion, with losses wider than a year earlier. Over the last four quarters the controlling-shareholder net loss totals KRW 96.4 billion.

Shrinking core revenue

Revenue fell in both 2024 and 2025 and has declined for three straight quarters since the first quarter of 2026. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 35.5 billion was down 9.1% from a year earlier. Chip supply issues at a Chinese customer and weaker automotive demand are cited as drivers of the core business weakness.

Uncertain timing of new-business revenue

The TGV glass-substrate mass-production target is 2027, and the timing and scale of expansion have not been disclosed. A third-party forecast assumes much higher 2026 revenue, a wide gap from the disclosed quarterly trend. If revenue recognition from the new business is delayed, the loss trend could persist for longer.

10

Risk factors

Financial structure

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 300.5 billion and total equity was KRW 247.7 billion, with a debt ratio of 121.3%, up from 56.1% in 2022. Operating cash flow was negative KRW 2.33 billion in 2025. If additional funding is needed, the capital structure could come under pressure.

Customer and demand concentration

Revenue depends heavily on Chinese smartphone customers and automotive demand. In 2024, cover-glass sales fell amid China's domestic slowdown and premium-segment competition. If customer inventory adjustments or chip supply disruptions persist, a recovery in the core business could be delayed.

Technology and mass-production execution

TGV glass-substrate yield and quality are company figures, and specifications and pricing are not public because of nondisclosure agreements with customers. If the 2027 mass-production target slips or customer qualification is delayed, new-business revenue will move later. Verification of mass-production technology, such as plating uniformity over large panels, is a key checkpoint.

Overall view

JNTC is restructuring from cover glass and connectors, where revenue has declined since 2024, toward TGV glass substrates. On a disclosed basis, it posted operating losses in 2024 and 2025, and has posted operating losses for five consecutive quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

The July 2026 commercialization agreement with TOPPAN and the 2027 mass-production target show progress in the new business. However, yield and customer qualification details rest on company statements, and a third-party 2026 forecast differs widely from the disclosed quarterly trend.

Valuation shows a large premium to net asset value, with no earnings-based multiple calculable. The next checkpoints are the full-year figures in the 2026 annual report, whether mass production begins in 2027, and the timing of domestic expansion.
