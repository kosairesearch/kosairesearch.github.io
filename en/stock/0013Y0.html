MS Bio's High-Margin Platform Moves Into ECM and Amniotic Lenses

MS Bio, a regenerative medicine device maker generating approximately 36% operating margins, debuted on the KOSDAQ on October 22, 2026, and is now pivoting toward ECM skin boosters and a suture-free amniotic lens as its next growth engines.

02

Business structure

MS Bio is a human tissue and biomaterial-based regenerative medicine platform company founded in 2015, headquartered in Seongnam, Gyeonggi Province, and listed on the KOSDAQ on October 22, 2026.

The company develops and manufactures products that aid in the reconstruction and regeneration of damaged tissue, drawing on four core proprietary technologies: decellularization and ECM preservation, customized collagen purification and crosslinking, precision splitting and standardization, and amniotic membrane application.

By revenue in FY2025, soft tissue reconstruction (primarily plastic surgery and breast reconstruction) accounted for approximately 62% and bone regeneration (orthopedics and dental) approximately 26%, together representing roughly 88% of total revenue.

The full in-house integration of R&D, manufacturing, and quality control underpins the company's high operating margins, with precision processing technology providing a structural cost advantage.

New growth segments include AM Lens, a contact-lens-shaped amniotic membrane product for ophthalmic use, and GCELLE REBORNNE, a human-derived ECM skin booster launched in March 2026, extending the business into ophthalmology, dermatology, and aesthetics.

GC Wellbeing serves as the exclusive domestic and overseas distributor for GCELLE REBORNNE under a production-and-sales partnership. Several other KOSDAQ-listed companies compete in the domestic ECM skin booster segment, and peers that entered the market earlier have seen meaningful market re-rating.

The Seongnam facility expanded its tissue bank capacity from approximately KRW 24 billion to over KRW 40 billion per year following a late-2025 capacity addition that received Ministry of Food and Drug Safety approval in July 2026.

A new dedicated medical device factory in Ochang, North Chungcheong Province, funded by approximately KRW 19 billion from IPO proceeds, will complete a dual-site structure—Seongnam for human tissue and Ochang for medical devices.

03

Recent trends

In FY2025, MS Bio posted revenue of KRW 23.787 billion, operating profit of KRW 8.558 billion, and net profit of KRW 7.288 billion, with an operating margin of approximately 36%. Operating profit grew 42.9% from FY2024's KRW 5.99 billion, and revenue expanded 32.4% year-on-year.

According to company disclosures, the compound annual revenue growth rate since 2020 has been approximately 48%. In the first half of 2026, revenue reached KRW 13.9 billion (+26.5% year-on-year) and operating profit came in at KRW 4.5 billion (+21.3%), sustaining the growth trajectory.

Institutional book-building for the IPO drew 2,269 institutions at a demand ratio of 1,096.56-to-1, while the general subscription round attracted a ratio of 1,538.51-to-1 with subscription deposits of approximately KRW 6.0152 trillion.

The final IPO price was set at the top of the indicative range at KRW 7,800, putting total proceeds at KRW 31.2 billion, with the underwriting managed by KB Securities.

On listing day, October 22, 2026, the stock opened above KRW 16,000—more than double the IPO price—and surged to an intraday high of KRW 19,650 before selling pressure reduced the gain through the afternoon session.

The stock closed at KRW 12,660, up 62.31% from the IPO price, with market capitalization of KRW 362.3 billion and exceptionally high single-day trading value of KRW 1.0 trillion.

04

Outlook

Management has set a full-year 2026 target of KRW 35.6 billion in revenue and KRW 12.6 billion in operating profit, with a medium-term goal of KRW 68.3 billion in revenue and an operating margin above 33% by 2028.

The strategy layers new-product contributions from ECM skin boosters, AM Lens, and a 3D-printed collagen scaffold on top of core businesses that management expects to grow at 20% or more annually.

For the ECM skin booster, the company has disclosed targets of KRW 4.2 billion in 2026, KRW 11 billion in 2027, and KRW 35 billion by 2030.

AM Lens was submitted for non-reimbursement listing in August 2026, and a potential first-half 2027 approval could extend its domestic use to moderate-to-severe dry eye patients, representing a meaningful inflection point for that product line.

Once the Ochang facility is completed, total production capacity is expected to exceed KRW 66 billion for the tissue bank and KRW 44 billion for medical devices, supporting longer-term volume growth.

The current overseas revenue share of approximately 6% is targeted to reach 12% by 2028, driven by a potential U.S. tissue bank acquisition, the Vietnam AM Lens pilot, and planned clinical trials in China (estimated at approximately KRW 4 billion per product).

The quarterly sales trajectory of GCELLE REBORNNE, the AM Lens non-reimbursement decision, and the Ochang factory completion schedule will be the primary metrics for tracking progress against the 2028 targets.

05

Bull factors

Proven Earning Power in a Sector That Typically Lacks It

An operating margin of approximately 36% is exceptionally high for a domestic medical device or biotech-adjacent company, underpinned by full in-house process integration and proprietary precision processing technology.

Revenue has grown at a compound annual rate of approximately 48% since 2020, and operating profit rose 42.9% year-on-year in FY2025. Cash generated from the core business can fund new-product investments, reducing reliance on external financing.

A Hana Securities analyst, as reported on October 22, 2026, stated that the company "is expected to sustain top-line growth driven by new product launches and expanded production capacity."

Positioned to Benefit From a Fast-Growing ECM Skin Booster Market

The domestic market for tissue-derived ECM skin boosters has demonstrated rapid growth and re-rating potential, as evidenced by the performance of earlier-entering KOSDAQ-listed peers.

MS Bio combines the differentiating attribute of human-derived ECM raw material with GC Wellbeing's existing clinic distribution network, facilitating early market penetration.

If ECM skin booster revenue grows as targeted—from KRW 4.2 billion in 2026 to KRW 35 billion by 2030—the aesthetics segment would become a major revenue pillar, reshaping the overall business mix.

Pipeline products including SETTAFILL (porcine dermis-derived ECM) and P-AMEX (amniotic membrane-derived) are under development, offering further portfolio diversification in aesthetics.

AM Lens Indication Expansion and Global Market Groundwork

If AM Lens receives a non-reimbursement code in Korea—targeted for H1 2027—its addressable domestic patient population would expand well beyond the current seven covered indications to include moderate-to-severe dry eye sufferers.

The product's suture-free, under-one-minute outpatient application differentiates it meaningfully from conventional amniotic membrane therapy, potentially driving clinical adoption.

In the United States, the company's strategy of acquiring a local tissue bank to manufacture with domestically sourced amniotic membrane is designed to sidestep export restrictions and establish a foothold in the world's largest medical device market.

The MOU with a Hanoi tissue bank and an ongoing AM Lens pilot study in Vietnam represent the first steps in building a Southeast Asian commercial platform.

06

Bear factors

Post-IPO Liquidity Overhang and Elevated Listing Valuation

The stock surged to an intraday high of KRW 19,650 before closing at KRW 12,660 on listing day, illustrating the sharp price swings inherent in newly listed small-cap medical device companies.

Approximately 30.6% of institutionally allocated shares carry a minimum 15-day lock-up, and potential selling pressure after expiry warrants close monitoring.

The listing-day market cap of KRW 362.3 billion is roughly 1.7 times the KRW 212.2 billion expected at the time of IPO pricing, implying the current market level is already pricing in substantial execution on all new growth initiatives.

Single-day trading value of KRW 1.0 trillion indicates concentrated short-term speculative demand, a dynamic that may contribute to above-average price volatility in the early trading period.

Uncertain Revenue Ramp Timeline for New Products

GCELLE REBORNNE was launched in March 2026 with a full-year 2026 target of KRW 4.2 billion, but the product is still early stage and actual market uptake has yet to be confirmed.

If the AM Lens non-reimbursement listing is delayed or the qualifying patient population is narrower than anticipated, the revenue ramp for this product could underperform.

Overseas clinical trials for two animal-derived ECM skin boosters and AM Lens in the U.S. and China are estimated at approximately KRW 4 billion per product, and commercialization timelines depend on clinical outcomes and each country's regulatory schedules.

Should growth momentum in the core soft tissue and bone regeneration businesses moderate, new products may not fully offset any resulting revenue gap in the near term.

Heavy Capital Expenditure Exceeding IPO Proceeds

Total capital requirements—including approximately KRW 19 billion for the Ochang plant and approximately KRW 12 billion for overseas clinical programs—exceed KRW 52 billion, substantially surpassing the KRW 31.2 billion raised in the IPO.

The funding gap must be met through operating cash flows or additional financing, and the form of that financing could affect existing shareholders depending on the instruments chosen.

Around the time of the new factory's completion, rising depreciation charges and below-optimal initial utilization rates are likely to exert some pressure on near-term operating margin metrics.

07

Risk factors

Macro and Policy Risk

In a global economic slowdown or domestic consumption contraction, the aesthetics segment faces above-average demand sensitivity given its discretionary nature.

Changes in Korea's national health insurance and non-reimbursement policies will directly affect the pace of AM Lens market adoption and the revenue structure of the ECM skin booster business.

Exchange rate movements between the Korean won and the U.S. dollar affect both the won cost of overseas clinical programs and the converted value of future international sales.

Regulatory and Licensing Risk

Delays in the domestic non-reimbursement listing review for AM Lens, or in medical device approvals in the U.S., Vietnam, and China, could push back the start of overseas revenue and slow return on invested capital.

The raw material for human tissue implants is donation-dependent; tighter donation regulations or unexpected supply constraints could disrupt production schedules.

The U.S. market entry via tissue bank acquisition is at an exploratory stage, and the time required to close a deal and obtain the necessary regulatory approvals may exceed current assumptions.

Competition and Technology Risk

In the ECM skin booster market, competing products from earlier-entrant domestic peers have already established distribution channels in clinics and hospitals, requiring MS Bio to invest heavily in marketing and differentiation to build share as a late entrant.

Given the rapid pace of innovation in regenerative medicine, existing products face long-term substitution risk from next-generation technology-based alternatives.

Entry by large global medical device or biotech companies with comparable platform technologies into the same indications could also significantly reshape the competitive environment.

08

Overall view

MS Bio enters the KOSDAQ on a foundation of proven, high-margin businesses in soft tissue reconstruction and bone regeneration, with an approximately 36% operating margin that stands out in the domestic medical device and biotech sector.

The new GCELLE REBORNNE ECM skin booster and AM Lens both build on existing core technologies, providing technological continuity, but the pace of market penetration and overseas regulatory timelines are the central variables for achieving medium-term growth targets.

With the listing-day market cap of KRW 362.3 billion already more than 1.7 times the IPO-implied level of approximately KRW 212.2 billion, semi-annual earnings delivery relative to market expectations will be closely watched.

Key milestones to monitor include the Ochang factory completion schedule and utilization ramp, the outcome of the AM Lens non-reimbursement application, and the quarterly revenue trajectory for GCELLE REBORNNE.

Given that total planned capital expenditures exceed IPO proceeds, the manner in which the funding gap is addressed and whether current profitability levels are maintained will also be important factors to track.
