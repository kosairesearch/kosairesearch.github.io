02

Business structure

Msbio was founded in 2015 and develops products from human tissue and biomaterials for tissue reconstruction and regeneration. Its proprietary processes cover decellularization and ECM preservation, precision splitting, collagen purification and crosslinking, and amniotic membrane applications.

Based on 2025 figures, soft-tissue reconstruction products accounted for 60.9% of revenue (KRW 14.5 billion) and bone regeneration for 26.4% (KRW 6.3 billion), according to inews24, while Straight News put the split at roughly 66% and 26%, reflecting different classification methods.

Flagship items include an acellular dermal matrix for breast reconstruction, a collagen soft-tissue matrix, and dental bone graft and barrier membranes.

The company points to its Ministry of Food and Drug Safety tissue bank license, medical device manufacturing approval, and ISO 13485 certification as evidence of technical competitiveness. Production is planned to split between the Seongnam plant for human-tissue products and a new Ochang plant for medical devices.

In aesthetics, an ECM skinbooster launched in March 2026 is distributed domestically by a partner to roughly 200 clinics.

The AM Lens ophthalmic amniotic lens has a non-reimbursed treatment application pending, and the company is pursuing a partnership with a local tissue bank in Vietnam while reviewing a US tissue bank acquisition or establishment. The company emphasizes its 21 commercialized products as its main competitive differentiator.

03

Recent trends

Third-quarter 2026 results disclosed on November 17, 2026 show revenue of KRW 7.97 billion, up 35.0% year on year, and operating profit of KRW 2.73 billion, up 36.4%. Net income was KRW 1.66 billion, down 4.2% from the same period a year earlier, so operating growth did not carry through to net income.

Reported third-quarter operating margin was about 34%. For the first half of 2026, revenue rose 26.5% to KRW 13.85 billion and operating profit rose 21.3% to KRW 4.51 billion, according to IB Tomato's analysis of the filing. First-half operating margin therefore slipped to 32.6% from 34% a year earlier.

Full-year 2025 revenue was KRW 23.78 billion with operating profit of KRW 8.6 billion, a 36.0% margin, and the company says revenue grew at a 26.7% compound annual rate over 2023 to 2025.

Shares listed on October 22, 2026 and traded 80.4% above the KRW 7,800 offering price in early trading at 9:39 a.m., reaching KRW 14,070 according to Seoul Economic Daily.

As of the morning of November 18, 2026, the stock stood at KRW 5,010, down 4.93% on the day and below the offering price, with a market capitalization of KRW 136.3 billion and trading value of KRW 1.8 billion. The debt ratio fell to 3.8% at the end of the first half of 2026 from 94.3% at the end of 2025.

04

Outlook

Management set a 2026 revenue target of KRW 35.6 billion and a 2028 revenue target of KRW 68.3 billion, and says it will keep the operating margin at 33% or higher through 2028.

The KRW 35.6 billion figure is an annual target rather than a confirmed result, so second-half performance against the first-half revenue of KRW 13.85 billion and third-quarter revenue of KRW 7.97 billion will determine how close the company gets.

The ECM skinbooster is the main growth axis, with revenue targets of KRW 4.2 billion in 2026, KRW 11.0 billion in 2027, and KRW 35.0 billion in 2030. The AM Lens targets a new non-reimbursed billing code in the first half of 2027, which the company views as a key inflection point for sales.

The new Ochang plant is scheduled to start construction in January 2027 and begin commercial production in the second half of 2028, with KRW 18.4 billion of offering proceeds allocated to construction. The overseas revenue share is targeted to rise from about 6% in 2026 to 10.5% in 2027 and 12% in 2028.

Overseas clinical trials and local approvals take a long time, so a lag before investment translates into sales is expected. Higher R&D, depreciation, and personnel costs could weigh on profitability as new products and overseas operations expand.

The first-quarter 2027 manufacturing approval for the antibiotic-containing soft-tissue product and the creation of the AM Lens billing code are the main checkpoints for future results.

05

Bull factors

A 30%-plus operating margin structure

The company reports a 36.0% operating margin for 2025 and says margins have stayed in the 30% range since 2020. Third-quarter 2026 operating margin was reported at about 34%. Management says it runs R&D, manufacturing, and quality control in-house, which supports its cost competitiveness. The company argues that the platform becomes more economical as its product portfolio and customer base grow.

Room to expand through new products and overseas sales

Approved and launched products rose from 11 in 2021 to 21 in 2025, and the company is pursuing four more approvals and launches in 2026. Revenue targets for the ECM skinbooster are KRW 4.2 billion in 2026, KRW 11.0 billion in 2027, and KRW 35.0 billion in 2030. The company aims to lift its overseas revenue share from about 6% in 2026 to 12% in 2028.

Capacity expansion funded by the IPO

KRW 18.4 billion of offering proceeds is earmarked for constructing the new Ochang plant, which the company plans to bring to tissue-product capacity of more than KRW 66.0 billion and medical-device capacity of more than KRW 44.0 billion. Liquid assets at the end of the first half totaled about KRW 38.2 billion.

06

Bear factors

Slowing profit growth

First-half 2026 revenue rose 26.5%, but operating profit rose only 21.3%, pushing the operating margin down to 32.6% from 34% a year earlier. Third-quarter operating profit grew 36.4%, yet net income fell 4.2%.

IB Tomato's analysis holds that higher R&D, depreciation, and personnel costs from new products and overseas expansion are likely to weigh on profitability.

Targets not yet validated by reported results

The 2026 revenue target of KRW 35.6 billion is a management estimate rather than a confirmed result. The 2028 target of KRW 68.3 billion assumes existing businesses keep growing above 20% a year, with new products and overseas sales added on top. The 2026 ECM skinbooster target of KRW 4.2 billion has not yet been confirmed in filed results.

Share price volatility and thin trading

Shares that traded as high as KRW 14,070 in early trading on the first day now stand at KRW 5,010, below the KRW 7,800 offering price. Trading value on the same morning was KRW 1.8 billion, against a market capitalization of KRW 136.3 billion. Large price swings since listing mean the share price could move independently of reported earnings.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Exchange rates and interest rates can affect the won value of overseas revenue and the cost of funding capacity investment. As the plan to lift the overseas revenue share from about 6% toward 12% advances, exposure to currency moves rises alongside it. A domestic slowdown could also affect clinic and hospital procedure demand.

Regulatory and approvals

The non-reimbursed billing code for AM Lens is targeted for the first half of 2027, and the antibiotic-containing soft-tissue product targets manufacturing approval in the first quarter of 2027. If approvals or billing codes slip, the timing of new-product revenue slips with them. Supplementary requests from the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety could add further delays.

Overseas expansion

The US tissue bank is still at the stage of acquisition or establishment review, and clinical trials and approvals in China are reported to take a long time. A lag exists between overseas investment and recognized sales. Facilities and staffing costs could rise before the overseas revenue share reaches its target.

08

Overall view

Msbio's revenue and operating profit both grew year on year in the third quarter of 2026 on the back of its soft-tissue reconstruction and bone regeneration products, and its operating margin has remained in the 30% range.

Its in-house process control, 21 commercialized products, and a debt ratio of 3.8% are presented as business strengths. On the other side, first-half operating profit grew more slowly than revenue and third-quarter net income declined, and both remain weak points.

The 2026 revenue target of KRW 35.6 billion and the 2028 target of KRW 68.3 billion are management targets whose outcome depends on approval timing and overseas progress. IB Tomato's analysis holds that rising costs from new products and overseas expansion are likely to weigh on profitability.

The share price rose sharply on its first day of trading and now trades below the offering price, which shows a gap between market sentiment and reported results. This text is a qualitative summary for information purposes only and does not contain an investment opinion or price forecast.
