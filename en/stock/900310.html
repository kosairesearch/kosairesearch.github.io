02

Business structure

Coloray International Investment produces pearlescent pigments, a cosmetic raw material, and its operations center on production subsidiaries in China. The company's related-party list covers Zhejiang-based and other Chinese subsidiaries as well as the Korean unit Coloray Korea, each held at 100%.

The exchange classifies the company under finance, but the WI26 industry classification places it in cosmetics, apparel and toys, so its business is closer to cosmetic raw material manufacturing.

Products are grouped into series such as interference effect, golden, color-changing, silver white (I and II) and mica powder. As of December 2025, the golden series had the highest export share among the series at 25.26%.

Based on Q2 2026 sales compiled by Butler, the other series accounted for 36%, the interference effect series for 19%, the color-changing series for 14%, the silver white series for 12% and silver white II for 10%.

Major customers include global cosmetics companies such as L'Oréal and Estée Lauder, and a company profile service notes that the company is widening overseas sales on price competitiveness relative to quality.

Production capacity expanded with a new plant completed in March 2021, and a new synthetic mica production base was established in June 2024. Expansion in pet products and domestic agency sales of cosmetic raw materials are cited as new growth areas. Reported figures are converted from CNY into KRW, so exchange-rate moves flow into the KRW-translated results.

04

Earnings analysis

Coloray reports in CNY, so the KRW amounts in this section are translated from CNY.

Annual sales were KRW 40.8 billion in 2022, rose to KRW 50.7 billion in 2023 (up 24.3% year on year) as the largest year of the period, and then fell to KRW 48.6 billion in 2024 (down 4.3%) and KRW 44.4 billion in 2025 (down 8.5%), a two-year decline from 2024 through 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 11.6 billion in 2022, the largest year of the period, and dropped to KRW 6.22 billion in 2023 (down 46.4%) and KRW 199 million in 2024 (down 96.8%) before recovering to KRW 236 million in 2025 (up 18.7%).

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 12.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.81 billion in 2023 (down 52.3%) and KRW 885 million in 2024 (down 84.8%), then turned into a net loss of KRW 817 million in 2025 as the company moved into the red.

Net profit, which includes non-controlling interests, was reported at the same level as controlling-shareholder profit in each year. The operating margin declined from 28.4% in 2022 to 12.3% in 2023, 0.4% in 2024 and 0.5% in 2025.

For 2025, the company cited a slight decline in sales tied to market conditions together with higher operating profit from lower cost of sales, as reported in March 2026. Operating cash flow was KRW 20.5 billion in 2022, KRW 4.51 billion in 2023, KRW 8.76 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.73 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 sales were KRW 14.1 billion with operating profit of KRW 529 million and controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 255 million.

Q4 2025 sales fell to KRW 6.8 billion, down 51.9% from the prior quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 1.53 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 2.08 billion. Q1 2026 sales recovered to KRW 12.3 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.4 billion and controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 628 million.

Q2 2026 sales were KRW 9.55 billion, and operating profit was KRW 652 million, down 53.5% from Q1.

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 11.9 billion (up 25.0% from Q2 but down 15.5% year on year), operating profit was KRW 2.01 billion (up 279.0% year on year), and controlling-shareholder net profit was KRW 879 million (up 245.0% year on year).

Over the last four quarters, the controlling-shareholder net loss totaled KRW 370 million, and quarterly margins have not moved in a steady direction.

Industry analysis

Coloray's end market is demand for pearlescent pigments used mainly in color cosmetics. Because pigments drive shade and texture, results depend on orders from global cosmetics brands and the pace of color-cosmetic launches.

The company maintains transactions with global firms such as L'Oréal and Estée Lauder and emphasizes price competitiveness relative to quality. On competition, the company appears to pitch quality and price together as its position.

Production capacity has widened through the new plant added in 2021 and the synthetic mica production base established in 2024. Sales, however, peaked at KRW 50.7 billion in 2023 and declined in 2024 and 2025, and Q3 2026 sales were still down 15.5% year on year, which suggests demand recovery has been gradual.

At the same time, Q3 2026 operating profit rose sharply year on year, pointing to better pricing or cost conditions. On the cycle, the key test is whether sales recover from current levels or whether profit gains hold without a sales rebound.

The company has also begun domestic agency sales of cosmetic raw materials, an attempt to widen its contact with Korean customers. Because the exchange classifies it under finance while WI26 places it in cosmetics, the choice of peer group changes how multiples are read.

Bear factors

Sharp decline in operating margin

Operating profit fell from KRW 11.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 199 million in 2024, and the company moved into a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 817 million in 2025. As sales declined, profit cushions thinned, which shows weaker margin defense. The change in the margin from 28.4% in 2022 to 0.5% in 2025 shows how much the earnings structure has shifted.

Large swings in quarterly results

Q4 2025 sales fell to KRW 6.8 billion, down 51.9% from the prior quarter, with an operating loss of KRW 1.53 billion and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 2.08 billion. Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 652 million dropped 53.5% from Q1, so results have moved up and down even after the return to profit.

The combined controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 370 million over the last four quarters reflects alternating profit and loss quarters.

Sales decline and smaller cash generation

Sales peaked at KRW 50.7 billion in 2023 and then declined to KRW 48.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 44.4 billion in 2025. Q3 2026 sales were still down 15.5% year on year, so a recovery in the top line has not yet appeared.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 20.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.73 billion in 2025, a much smaller cash generation from core operations.

Risk factors

FX and currency translation

Filings are in CNY and are translated into KRW in this report. A change in the CNY-KRW exchange rate alters the KRW-translated results even when local sales are unchanged. Because the Chinese subsidiaries anchor the business, CNY movements feed directly into how reported scale should be read.

Customer orders and concentration

A company profile service says the company maintains dealings with global cosmetics firms such as L'Oréal and Estée Lauder. Order changes or product substitutions by a few large customers pass directly into sales. If color-cosmetic demand slows or customers reduce inventory, order volumes could fall quickly.

New business execution and share consolidation

The speed at which the synthetic mica base, pet products and domestic raw-material agency sales convert into revenue and profit is not yet visible in reported results. The share consolidation approved at the 2026 shareholders' meeting would change the share count and trading structure once executed.

Both the contribution of new businesses and the timing of the consolidation will need to be confirmed through later disclosures.
