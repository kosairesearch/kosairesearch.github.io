02

Business structure

Winners Elec is centered on manufacturing smart wiring devices such as switches and sockets. The company changed its corporate name from Winners Co., Ltd. to Winners Elec Co., Ltd. through a filing on March 31, 2026, citing value enhancement and business diversification as the reasons.

Alongside wiring devices, it signed a supply contract for water meter materials in September 2025. Sales run through two channels: deliveries against orders from construction companies, and online retail through platforms such as Coupang, which the company has expanded since 2025.

The company said first-half 2026 sales growth came mainly from expanded sales through online retail channels. In construction, deliveries for large builders including GS Construction and Lotte Construction are scheduled for the fourth quarter of 2026.

According to the independent research firm CMIR's analysis in November 2026, the company's main plant produced 19.21 million wiring devices in 2025 against a production capacity of 27.8 million units.

The same analysis described the in-house handling of design, tooling, production, quality inspection, sales and after-sales service as a core capability. Wiring-device revenue moves with construction volumes and distribution channels, so the company is widening both channels at once.

The new business is wearable-robot contract manufacturing.

On October 12, 2026, Winners Elec signed an exclusive OEM contract with a wearable-robot developer, Hexa Robotics, covering production of the H40A walking-assist robot from building the mass-production process through core module production, final assembly and shipment inspection.

The company plans to extend its output from wiring devices and water meter materials into mechatronics products that incorporate motors, reducers and sensors.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales fell from KRW 27.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 26.4 billion in 2024 (down 4.6% year on year) and then rose to KRW 31.2 billion in 2025 (up 18.5%). Within 2023 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 3.46 billion in 2023, KRW 2.93 billion in 2024 (down 15.4%) and KRW 5.27 billion in 2025 (up 80.2%). The operating margin was 12.5% in 2023, 11.1% in 2024 and 16.9% in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.02 billion in 2023, KRW 2.47 billion in 2024 (down 18.1%) and KRW 4.65 billion in 2025 (up 88.2%), while net profit including non-controlling interests was reported as KRW 3.02 billion, KRW 2.47 billion and KRW 4.65 billion for the same years.

Operating cash flow was KRW 2.77 billion in 2023, KRW 4.88 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.61 billion in 2025, falling again in 2025. On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 showed sales of KRW 8.69 billion and operating profit of KRW 744 million.

The fourth quarter of 2025 recorded sales of KRW 8.01 billion (down 7.9% quarter on quarter) but operating profit jumped to KRW 2.33 billion (up 213.9%). The first quarter of 2026 marked the low point, with sales of KRW 7.33 billion (down 8.5%) and operating profit of KRW 619 million (down 73.5%).

The second quarter of 2026 recovered to sales of KRW 8.16 billion (up 11.4%) and operating profit of KRW 1.15 billion (up 85.3%), and the third quarter brought sales of KRW 8.24 billion and operating profit of KRW 991 million (up 33.2% year on year).

First-half 2026 sales of KRW 15.5 billion rose 6.4% year on year, but operating profit declined year on year as higher copper and polycarbonate prices squeezed costs. The cause of the fourth-quarter 2025 operating profit jump and whether it was one-off should be confirmed in the full annual report text.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 4.32 billion, with the fourth quarter of 2025 the largest at KRW 1.91 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for wiring devices depends on both construction volumes and distribution channels. The company said it plans to deliver orders from GS Construction and Lotte Construction in the fourth quarter of 2026. On the cost side, higher copper and polycarbonate prices weighed on profitability in the first half of 2026.

As background for robot demand, CMIR noted that people aged 65 and over passed 20% of the population at the end of 2024, marking a super-aged society. The same analysis cited figures showing that work-related musculoskeletal disease cases rose from 6,715 in 2018 to 11,945 in 2022.

CMIR also said that rental-only walking-assist robots have launched domestically, widening demand beyond hospitals and industrial sites to general consumers.

In the industry, a division of labor is spreading in which developers focus on design, certification and sales while specialized manufacturers handle mass production and quality control.

Winners Elec's position, as described in the CMIR analysis, rests on quality-control and certification experience built in wiring devices together with in-house mold technology that can be applied to robot production.

Output of 19.21 million wiring devices at the main plant in 2025 was above two-thirds of the 27.8 million unit capacity. Growth room in the wiring-device business therefore depends on that spare capacity and on expansion of online channels.

06

Outlook

The company said deliveries for G-customer and L-customer volumes, already contracted, will begin in earnest from the second half of 2026. In the fourth quarter of 2026, it plans to strengthen its construction revenue base through deliveries tied to orders from GS Construction and Lotte Construction.

A company official said the company aims to raise growth and profitability together in the second half. The next checkpoints are how much of these volumes shows up in fourth-quarter 2026 sales and how much of the raw-material burden remains in the operating margin.

In robotics, the mold for the Hexa Robotics H40A is under production, and the prototype is scheduled for the first quarter of 2027. Next come finalizing injection parts, assembly processes and inspection standards, then passing durability and safety tests to win mass-production approval.

CMIR outlined public milestones for the robot business: completion of dedicated lines and molds, prototype shipment, certification, first mass-production orders with a fixed annual minimum order, and the H40A consumer launch and rental partnerships.

The robot developer plans to start consumer sales and subscription or rental operations from 2027. As output extends to mechatronics products that include motors, reducers and sensors, process complexity and quality standards rise as well.

The company filed a voluntary disclosure of a corporate value-up plan on April 21, 2026, and its detailed targets should be checked in the original document.

Bull factors

Exclusive contract manufacturing with the robot developer

On October 12, 2026, Winners Elec signed an exclusive OEM contract covering the full mass-production process for the H40A. The contract includes an annual minimum order guarantee and an automatic renewal clause, which are cited as factors improving revenue predictability.

Industrial, medical and logistics robot volumes won by the developer are also covered by exclusive production. The core bull case is that this could broaden a revenue base built mainly on wiring devices.

Second-half order deliveries and sales recovery

The company said deliveries for G-customer and L-customer volumes already contracted will begin in earnest in the second half. Deliveries tied to construction orders from GS Construction and Lotte Construction are also scheduled for the fourth quarter of 2026.

Sales rose in the second and third quarters of 2026, and third-quarter operating profit grew 33.2% year on year.

Profit recovery and a low debt burden

Operating profit rose 80.2% to KRW 5.27 billion in 2025, and the operating margin improved to 16.9%. The debt ratio fell to 22.5% at the end of 2025 from 37.2% in 2023, with total liabilities of KRW 9.17 billion. The low financial burden suggests room to absorb swings in costs.

Bear factors

Raw-material prices and lower first-half profit

First-half 2026 sales of KRW 15.5 billion rose 6.4%, but operating profit declined year on year. The cause cited was cost pressure from higher copper and polycarbonate prices. First-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 619 million fell 73.5% from the prior quarter, showing large quarterly swings. If cost increases persist, the improvement in operating margin could weaken.

Third-quarter sales down year on year

Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 8.24 billion rose 1.0% from the prior quarter but fell 5.2% year on year. Sales declined quarter on quarter in both the fourth quarter of 2025 and the first quarter of 2026. Because growth leans on online retail channels, changes in those channels could affect revenue.

Timing of earnings contribution from robotics

Robot contract manufacturing starts with the first-quarter 2027 prototype shipment, so revenue contribution comes only after that point. Certification, first mass-production orders and a fixed annual minimum order remain outstanding, leaving open the possibility of schedule slippage.

The developer's consumer sales are also planned from 2027, so finished-product demand needs time to be confirmed. Operating cash flow of KRW 2.61 billion in 2025 was below KRW 4.88 billion in 2024, which also warrants attention.

Risk factors

Raw-material prices

Copper and polycarbonate (PC) are key raw materials for wiring devices, so rising prices put immediate pressure on operating margin. The decline in first-half 2026 operating profit has been attributed to this effect. The extent and timing of any pass-through to customers should be checked quarter by quarter in the filings.

Robot execution and counterparty dependence

Contract volumes depend on the developer's product performance and sales. The contract includes a minimum order, but if mass-production approval or certification is delayed, the investment burden could arrive before revenue.

The company plans to build dedicated lines, inspection equipment and molds with its own capital, so upfront costs will be incurred.

Channel concentration and small-cap characteristics

Online sales center on major platforms such as Coupang, so the company is exposed to policy changes at those channels. Exercise of stock options in May 2026 issued 275,000 new common shares, increasing the share count. As a small-cap stock on KOSDAQ, its share price can be volatile.

Overall view

Winners Elec's disclosed results showed a recovery in 2025, with sales of KRW 31.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.27 billion, after a decline in 2024. Quarterly results became more volatile in 2026.

Sales rose in the second and third quarters after the first-quarter low, and third-quarter operating profit grew 33.2% year on year, but first-half operating profit fell under raw-material cost pressure from copper and polycarbonate.

On the business side, construction orders and online channels were added to the core wiring-device business, and robot contract manufacturing was added as a new line.

The robot business carries a minimum order and an automatic renewal clause, but its revenue contribution can only be confirmed after the first-quarter 2027 prototype.

The financial structure is relatively light, with a debt ratio of 22.5% at the end of 2025, yet operating cash flow of KRW 2.61 billion in 2025 was below 2024. Valuation sits in a range below net asset value, while earnings sustainability and cost trends remain the key variables. The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and the robot prototype schedule.
