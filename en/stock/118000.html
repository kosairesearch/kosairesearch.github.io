Metacare: Quarterly Profit Recovery Meets Balance-Sheet Tests

Metacare's operating profit has risen for two straight quarters since its first-quarter 2026 low, but weaker 2025 profitability, a cumulative net loss over the last four quarters and a KRX managed-issue designation remain open items.

  1. 1

    Operating profit for the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 1.14 billion, up 863.8% year on year, on revenue of KRW 19.4 billion, up 122.0% from the prior quarter.

  2. 2

    Full-year 2025 revenue rose 62.4% to KRW 32.8 billion, but operating profit fell 12.0% to KRW 1.32 billion, leaving an operating margin of 4.0%.

  3. 3

    The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 2.31 billion, shaped largely by a KRW 3.72 billion loss in the fourth quarter of 2025.

  4. 4

    Because listed market capitalization stayed below KRW 30 billion, KRX designated the stock as a managed issue; trading was halted on October 29, 2026 and resumed on October 30.

  5. 5

    A correction filing on November 16, 2026 cut third-quarter 2026 consolidated net income to KRW 449 million from KRW 1.34 billion, according to press reports.

02

Business structure

Metacare was split off from the medical division of a pharmaceutical company in 2009 and operates from its Hwaseong headquarters across pharmaceutical distribution, hair-transplant clinic management consulting, real estate development and advertising agency services.

According to a Money Today report dated October 6, 2026, first-half 2026 revenue mix was 46.4% consulting, 37.0% pharmaceuticals, 8.5% medical and 8.1% real estate development.

The pharmaceutical arm supplies prescription drugs, medical devices and medical consumables through a proprietary distribution network built through M&A.

The consulting arm rests mainly on management consulting contracts with hair-transplant specialty hospitals, while the company's earnings commentary credits a pharmaceutical distributor acquisition with strengthening purchasing power. The subsidiary base has also widened.

Metacare bought all shares of a real estate developer in 2024 and in 2026 consolidated an ad-tech subsidiary, extending into advertising agency and ad-tech services.

A wholly owned distribution subsidiary was acquired for KRW 360 million on July 27, 2026 and merged into Metacare with an effective date of October 1, 2026.

In hair care, the company is preparing a low-dose dutasteride 0.2mg product, with contract manufacturing from a pharmaceutical partner and sales through distribution channels it already holds.

On the customer side, reports describe plans to use more than 200,000 customer records accumulated through clinic consulting and hair-care solutions across a platform linking patient intake to aftercare. The largest shareholder is Metalabs.

A June 2026 filing on a KRW 6 billion third-party allotment rights issue said its stake would rise from 37.28% to 51.78%, while an October 2026 report cited 29.04%, so the reference dates should be checked.

Against larger drug distributors, direct scale comparison is difficult, and the hospital management-support segment is closer to a niche business.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 19.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 55.8 billion in 2023, and 2023 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span. Revenue fell 63.8% year on year to KRW 20.2 billion in 2024 and then rose 62.4% to KRW 32.8 billion in 2025.

Operating losses of KRW 300 million in 2022 and KRW 8.9 billion in 2023 reflected continued losses with a widening deficit in 2023, before operating profit of KRW 1.5 billion in 2024 marked a turn to profit.

Operating profit fell 12.0% to KRW 1.32 billion in 2025, and the operating margin slid from 7.4% in 2024 to 4.0%. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to KRW 9.28 billion in 2024 and fell 94.6% to KRW 498 million in 2025.

The 2025 net loss of KRW 212 million differs from that figure because non-controlling interests posted a loss, as FnGuide's financial summary shows.

Debt-to-equity rose to 63.6% at the end of 2025 from 35.5% in 2024, with total liabilities of KRW 77 billion against total equity of KRW 121 billion, of which KRW 119.4 billion belongs to controlling shareholders.

Operating cash flow moved from negative KRW 12.9 billion in 2023 to positive KRW 4.48 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.42 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 119 million in the third quarter of 2025 was followed by an operating loss of KRW 143 million in the fourth quarter, when net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 3.72 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 10.1 billion and operating profit KRW 577 million, a turn back to profit. Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 8.76 billion fell 13.4% from the prior quarter, yet operating profit rose 38.1% to KRW 796 million.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 19.4 billion, up 122.0% from the prior quarter and 80.9% year on year, with operating profit of KRW 1.14 billion, up 43.7% from the prior quarter and 863.8% year on year. Operating profit has risen for two straight quarters from the first-quarter 2026 low.

The 2026 consolidation of an ad-tech subsidiary is cited as part of the revenue jump, but its quarterly contribution needs to be checked in filing detail.

A November 16, 2026 correction cut third-quarter consolidated net income from KRW 1.34 billion to KRW 449 million, which press reports attributed to accounting adjustments for newly consolidated and liquidated entities.

05

Industry analysis

In hair-loss treatment, dutasteride has been used mainly at the 0.5mg standard approved dose, while the 0.2mg low-dose product is presented as keeping effect at a set level while lowering drug exposure, according to a Money Today report dated October 6, 2026.

Whether demand for low-dose options converts into actual prescriptions is hard to gauge from press reports alone, and the market size cannot be quantified from the available sources.

The pharmaceutical distribution arm is exposed to hospital demand and drug pricing policy, and the plan to link general-hospital GPO business with hair-care drugs is presented as its growth path.

Hospital management consulting rests on contracts with hair-transplant specialty hospitals, so results can swing with the number and terms of those counterparties. Advertising agency and ad-tech operations are sensitive to advertising spend.

Unlike consumer rental businesses such as Coway, Metacare's weight sits on hospital-facing B2B services and pharmaceutical distribution.

A cycle position cannot be shown with a quantitative indicator, and within its sector the company's position rests more on the combination of hospital services and hair-care drugs than on scale.

Outlook

On October 21, 2026, the board approved a 2026 corporate value-up plan.

Its targets are improving the profit structure, restoring financial stability and laying the groundwork for shareholder returns, with execution tasks covering liquidity stabilization, profit-structure improvement with growth foundations, and shareholder value.

The plan includes a PBR improvement measure, and its details may change with market and business conditions. The first business test is sales of the low-dose dutasteride 0.2mg product, whose contribution to results can first be gauged in the provisional fourth-quarter 2026 results expected in early 2027.

The plan also links general-hospital GPO business with hair-care drugs to expand supply of pharmaceuticals and medical consumables. The merger of the wholly owned distribution subsidiary, effective October 1, 2026, simplifies the distribution structure.

The company said proceeds from the KRW 6 billion rights issue announced in June 2026 would be used for acquiring shares of other companies, and the target and size will become clear when individual disclosures are filed.

Whether the first-half 2026 run-rate of KRW 18.9 billion in revenue and KRW 1.4 billion in operating profit continued through the third quarter can be checked against the quarterly figures already filed.

The 2026 annual report, due by March 31, 2027, is the next benchmark for annual operating margin, net income attributable to controlling shareholders and progress on the value-up plan.

Bull factors

The company intends to pair GPO business at general hospitals with hair-care drugs and to link more than 200,000 customer records to patient care. The revenue contribution of the new product will be gauged from quarterly filings after launch.

Largest-shareholder support and funding

On October 21, 2026, a value-up plan covering liquidity stabilization and profit-structure improvement was approved.

Overall view

Metacare grew 2025 revenue to KRW 32.8 billion, but operating profit fell to KRW 1.32 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders to KRW 498 million. In 2026, operating profit has risen for two straight quarters since the first-quarter turn to profit, showing a recovery in quarterly earnings.

How much the consolidation of a new entity contributed to third-quarter revenue of KRW 19.4 billion, and the level of corrected net income, need to be confirmed in upcoming filings.

The business spans consulting, pharmaceuticals, medical and real estate development, with plans for a low-dose hair-loss drug and GPO linkage at general hospitals.

On the balance sheet, debt-to-equity rose to 63.6% at the end of 2025, and a cumulative four-quarter loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.31 billion remains.

On the listing side, the company went through a managed-issue designation and trading halt in October 2026, and its value-up plan names liquidity stabilization and profit-structure improvement as tasks.

Positive factors are the quarterly profit recovery, business diversification and support from the largest shareholder. Negative factors are weaker annual profitability, restated quarterly results and an unmet listing requirement.

The 2026 annual report, with full-year operating margin and net income attributable to controlling shareholders, will show more clearly how these factors balance.
