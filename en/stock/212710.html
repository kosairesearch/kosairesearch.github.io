02

Business structure

Iste is a Korean company that operates both semiconductor manufacturing equipment and energy facilities, and it listed on KOSDAQ on February 12, 2025.

Its core semiconductor equipment is the FOUP Cleaner, which cleans the FOUP pods that hold wafers, and a November 2025 securities report estimated this product at about 52% of revenue.

The same report put the 2024 revenue mix at 56% semiconductor equipment, 26% other equipment such as OLED, LCD and automation systems, and 18% energy business. SK hynix was the core customer, but the company said supply to Samsung Electronics is being added in 2026, broadening its customer base.

The company said it has secured 13 domestic customers and is expanding sales channels into China, Taiwan and Europe. Its SiCN PECVD equipment began supply to SK hynix at the end of 2023 and went through quality testing.

That market has long been dominated by Applied Materials and Lam Research, which makes the domestic localization notable.

On the energy side, the company won an oxygen-enrichment facility construction contract worth KRW 7.648 billion in August 2025 and disclosed a plastic thermal liquefaction facility contract worth KRW 23.5 billion in April 2026 with a counterparty established in 2023.

That contract runs through October 31, 2027, and progress payments are made after quarterly completion inspections. Because the company runs two businesses, quarterly revenue depends heavily on the timing of large orders and equipment deliveries.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 27.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 41.1 billion in 2024, an increase of 51.1%, and then fell to KRW 32.2 billion in 2025, a decrease of 21.6%. Among 2023 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2023 was the smallest.

The company attributed the 2025 revenue decline to delays in supply and sales schedules for major semiconductor customers and delayed OLED orders.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 710 million in 2023 to an operating profit of KRW 638 million in 2024, which was a turn to profit, and then to an operating loss of KRW 5.84 billion in 2025, which was a turn to loss.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a net loss of KRW 129 million in 2023, net income of KRW 558 million in 2024 (a turn to profit), and a net loss of KRW 4.53 billion in 2025 (a turn to loss). Net income including non-controlling interests moved in the same direction in each of those years.

The operating margin was -2.6% in 2023, 1.6% in 2024 and -18.1% in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 891.8% in 2023 to 494.7% in 2024 and 198.7% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 20.5 billion, of which KRW 20.5 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 40.8 billion. Operating cash flow was -KRW 4.68 billion in 2023 and -KRW 4.35 billion in 2024, then turned positive at KRW 6.31 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 5.26 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 13.3 billion in the fourth quarter (up 152.4% from the prior quarter), then KRW 6.38 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 51.9%), KRW 16.8 billion in the second quarter (up 164.0%), and KRW 8.18 billion in the third quarter (down 51.5% from the prior quarter, up 55.5% from a year earlier).

Operating results went from an operating loss of KRW 1.07 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and an operating loss of KRW 4.17 billion in the fourth quarter, when the loss widened, to an operating profit of KRW 535 million in the first quarter of 2026 (a turn to profit), KRW 2.07 billion in the second quarter (up 287.6%), and KRW 905 million in the third quarter (down 56.4% from the prior quarter, a turn to profit from a year earlier).

Operating profit grew alongside revenue in the quarters when sales rose and fell as sales fell, so quarterly profit has been sensitive to the revenue level. For the third quarter, the company said that higher utilization from increased orders for higher-value products and equipment spread fixed costs.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from the downstream market comes mainly from memory investment centered on HBM (high-bandwidth memory). The company said it expects continued HBM competitiveness investment at SK hynix and tech-migration and capacity investment at another domestic integrated chipmaker.

In the same context, the company said demand from AI data centers and advanced packaging is increasing the importance of deposition and cleaning equipment. The SiCN PECVD market has long been dominated by US suppliers, so domestically localized products are seen as reducing dependence on foreign equipment.

The global FOUP Cleaner market was estimated at about KRW 200 billion in a November 2025 securities report. In that market, the company was assessed as holding a high share at one domestic customer.

Its business partly overlaps with listed peers in the same semiconductor equipment sector such as HANMI Semiconductor and Wonik IPS, but Iste is specialized in cleaning and transport automation equipment and PECVD.

Its cleaning equipment has reference deliveries in domestic and overseas processes, which the company cites as a strength. PECVD is still in an early localization stage, and the timing when mass-production volumes reach reported results is the key variable in comparing it with peers. Whether memory-market improvement translates into actual equipment orders will shape order flow after 2027.

06

Outlook

The company said its order backlog stood at KRW 37.6 billion at the end of the third quarter of 2026, and it expects stable results to continue after the fourth quarter (announced November 12, 2026). Compared with the KRW 41.4 billion backlog at the end of the second quarter, the third-quarter balance declined.

In August 2026, the company said it plans to diversify its portfolio through AI data center business and new equipment development such as PECVD. PECVD is going through mass-production verification with SK hynix, and a 2025 report said mass production was approaching.

The timing and volume of mass-production contribution to revenue can be confirmed in the next periodic report. The company said supply to Samsung Electronics began to be added in 2026.

The plastic thermal liquefaction facility construction contract with a counterparty is worth KRW 23.5 billion, disclosed on April 21, 2026, with a structure of 30% advance payment, 60% progress payments and 10% final payment.

Progress payments are made after quarterly completion inspections, and the expected end date, taking into account the expected overall completion date, is October 31, 2027. The company was selected as the lead research institution for a national project worth KRW 1.767 billion that runs through June 30, 2028.

The securities registration statement at the 2025 listing presented a neutral scenario of KRW 70.6 billion in 2026 revenue and KRW 10.5 billion in operating profit. Adding up the 2026 first to third quarter disclosed results shows a large gap from that projection.

Whether order conversion and quarterly revenue levels narrow this gap after the fourth quarter is the next checkpoint.

Bull factors

Quarterly rebound and order backlog

Operating profit turned positive in the first quarter of 2026, and profit remained positive in the second and third quarters, at KRW 2.07 billion and KRW 905 million respectively.

The company put its third-quarter 2026 order backlog at KRW 37.6 billion, down from KRW 41.4 billion at the end of the second quarter, but still a sizable balance by its account.

According to the same company announcement, equipment division revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 7.3 billion, up 142% from a year earlier.

Customer diversification and PECVD localization

The company said supply to Samsung Electronics is being added in 2026, broadening its customer structure. Reports said PECVD began supply to SK hynix at the end of 2023, went through quality verification and then entered mass-production verification.

It is drawing attention as a localization case in a market long held by foreign suppliers, and the company cited 13 domestic customers and expanding overseas sales channels.

Diversification through energy facility orders

In August 2025 the company won an oxygen-enrichment facility construction contract worth KRW 7.648 billion for a carbon-neutral technology project, and in April 2026 it disclosed a plastic thermal liquefaction facility contract worth KRW 23.5 billion.

The company was selected as the lead research institution for a national project worth KRW 1.767 billion. As energy orders accumulate alongside semiconductor equipment, the revenue base is broadening.

Bear factors

Loss record and financial leverage

The company turned to an operating loss of KRW 5.84 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.53 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow turned positive in 2025 but was negative in 2023 and 2024.

At the end of 2025, total liabilities of KRW 40.8 billion exceeded total equity of KRW 20.5 billion, leaving a debt-to-equity ratio of 198.7%. Net losses over the last four quarters mean a PER cannot be calculated.

Volatility in quarterly results

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 13.3 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 6.38 billion in the first quarter of 2026, rose to KRW 16.8 billion in the second quarter, and fell again to KRW 8.18 billion in the third quarter.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 4.17 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to an operating profit of KRW 2.07 billion in the second quarter of 2026. The company attributed the 2025 revenue decline to delayed supply and sales schedules for major customers and delayed OLED orders.

Customer concentration and reliance on one product

Heavy reliance on specific customers, including SK hynix, is seen as a weakness. A November 2025 securities report said FOUP Cleaner holds a high share at one domestic customer. Supply to Samsung Electronics is being added, but revenue still depends heavily on the investment timing of a few customers.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and investment cycle

Semiconductor equipment revenue is sensitive to the investment schedules of SK hynix and a small number of customers. The company said delayed supply and sales schedules for major customers weighed on 2025 revenue. A cut in memory investment or a change in customer timing could reduce quarterly revenue again.

Order gaps and contract terms

The order backlog fell from KRW 41.4 billion at the end of the second quarter to KRW 37.6 billion at the end of the third quarter. Payments on the counterparty contract depend on progress and inspections, and the disclosure states that its main terms may change as work proceeds. A delay in that contract could push back the timing of energy revenue.

Financial burden and execution of new businesses

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 198.7% at the end of 2025, but the debt burden remains significant. Whether PECVD moves into mass production and whether hydrogen and energy businesses execute as planned remains uncertain. Whether R&D projects and equipment orders translate into profit is a matter for quarterly results.

Overall view

Iste is a semiconductor equipment company centered on the FOUP Cleaner and PECVD, and energy facility orders have been added as a pillar of diversification.

In 2025, revenue fell to KRW 32.2 billion and the company turned to an operating loss of KRW 5.84 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.53 billion.

In 2026, operating profit has been positive in the second and third quarters after a turn to profit in the first quarter, and second-quarter revenue of KRW 16.8 billion is at the level the company described as its largest quarter since founding.

However, third-quarter revenue of KRW 8.18 billion fell from the prior quarter, and quarterly swings are large, so profit flow depends heavily on order timing. Customer dependence on SK hynix is high, and supply to Samsung Electronics is being added in 2026.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 198.7% at the end of 2025, but liabilities still exceed equity. Because the last four quarters were loss-making and no PER can be calculated, the valuation is described in terms of a premium over net assets.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results expected in early February 2027 and the March 31, 2027 filing deadline for the 2026 annual report. The conversion of PECVD into mass-production volumes and the quarterly progress of the counterparty contract are also items to follow.
