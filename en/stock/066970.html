LFP Orders Expand as Losses Persist; Recovery Still to Be Confirmed

L&F swung to a KRW 26.5 billion operating loss in Q3 2026, but cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters stayed positive as LFP cathode orders became a second growth pillar.

  1. 1

    On a filed basis, L&F posted operating losses for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. The 2025 operating loss of KRW 156.8 billion was smaller than the KRW 558.7 billion loss in 2024.

  2. 2

    The Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 26.5 billion followed a profit a year earlier. The company attributed it mainly to raw-material inventory valuation losses of about KRW 42.6 billion and an exchange-rate-related loss of about KRW 22 billion.

  3. 3

    The company expects 2026 NCM cathode shipments to rise about 40% from the prior year, which would be a record.

  4. 4

    L&F has medium-to-long-term LFP supply contracts with four customers, including all three major Korean cell makers and one overseas customer, covering 250,000 tons (about 100 GWh of ESS batteries).

  5. 5

02

Business structure

L&F makes cathode materials for secondary batteries. Its core product is nickel-cobalt-manganese (NCM) cathode material, centered on ultra-high-nickel grades.

In Q3 2026, high-nickel products accounted for 90% of sales and mid-nickel products for 10%, and the company said first-half raw-material price increases were reflected in that quarter's selling prices.

Materials for 46-series cylindrical cells, which are 46 mm in diameter, are growing with new North American customers and reached about 10% of NCM shipments in Q3. The company expects that share to reach 40% in 2027.

The second pillar, LFP cathode material, is run through a dedicated subsidiary, and the Daegu plant started mass production at 30,000 tons per year at the end of September 2026.

The LFP market has been led by Chinese companies, but US power developers tightening their China-exit stance have increased demand for Korean-made materials.

Domestic rival POSCO Future M has also signed long-term LFP supply deals worth about KRW 1.1 trillion with SK On and about KRW 6 trillion with Samsung SDI, which intensifies competition.

The customer base has widened to the three major Korean cell makers and an overseas customer, but because L&F supplies ultra-high-nickel products on a sole-source basis to a core NCM customer, dependence on specific customers remains high.

04

Earnings analysis

On a filed basis, revenue peaked at KRW 4.64 trillion in 2023, up 19.5% from the prior year, which was the largest year in the 2022-2025 window. It fell to KRW 1.91 trillion in 2024, down 58.9%, the smallest year in the window, then recovered to KRW 2.15 trillion in 2025, up 13.0%. Profitability moved more sharply.

Operating profit of KRW 266.3 billion in 2022 (operating margin 6.9%) turned into an operating loss of KRW 222.3 billion in 2023, and the loss widened to KRW 558.7 billion in 2024 (operating margin -29.3%). The 2025 operating loss of KRW 156.8 billion (operating margin -7.3%) was smaller.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders were KRW 194.3 billion in 2023, KRW 377.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 533.5 billion in 2025. Including non-controlling interests, the 2025 net loss was KRW 534.7 billion.

Operating cash flow went from -KRW 864.3 billion in 2022 and -KRW 374.6 billion in 2023 to a positive KRW 280.5 billion in 2024, then -KRW 29.2 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 22.1 billion in Q3 2025 rose to KRW 82.5 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 117.3 billion in Q1 2026, then fell 82.2% to KRW 20.8 billion in Q2 2026 before an operating loss of KRW 26.5 billion in Q3 2026.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 874.4 billion was up 34.0% from a year earlier, so sales growth and profit moved in opposite directions.

The company said on November 5, 2026 that cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 was about KRW 111.6 billion, a turnaround from a loss of about KRW 239.3 billion in the same period of 2025.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) total KRW 238.6 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from downstream markets is split in two directions. In electric vehicles, global EV market uncertainty persists, but the adoption of 46-series cylindrical cells is picking up and pushing ultra-high-nickel NCM shipments higher.

In energy storage systems (ESS), demand is rising with the spread of AI data centers and renewable energy, and the spread of lower-priced EVs is cited as a factor that increases demand for LFP lines.

On the raw-material side, falling prices for key metals such as lithium carbonate and nickel created inventory valuation losses that weighed on the income statement. A sharp drop in the won-dollar rate also reduced the won-equivalent value of dollar-denominated payments.

The company said inventory valuation losses should ease gradually as LFP production expansion consumes existing raw-material stock faster. The LFP market has been led by Chinese companies, and domestic cell makers are moving to secure Korean-made materials.

In NCM, L&F holds a position centered on high-performance products, with high-nickel grades accounting for 90% of Q3 2026 sales. Market-share figures or competitors' NCM shipment volumes were not available as verified public data, so the company's position is not compared numerically.

06

Outlook

The company's near-term guidance calls for Q4 2026 NCM shipments to rise 6% to 9% from the prior quarter. Over the same period, average NCM selling prices were expected to fall about 3% from the prior quarter.

Sole supply of ultra-high-nickel products is expected to continue into Q4, and rising EV sales by a North American end customer are driving new 46-series supply. For the full year, the company expects 2026 NCM shipments to rise about 40% from the prior year.

For 2027, its plan calls for NCM shipments to grow at least 10% and the 46-series share to reach 40%. In LFP, the plan is to produce about 3,000 tons in 2026, ship 40,000 tons in 2027 and more than 60,000 tons in 2028.

The Daegu LFP plant is scheduled to expand capacity to 60,000 tons per year in the first half of 2027, and the company plans to raise LFP capacity to 120,000 tons per year by 2028.

Under the LG Energy Solution contract, supply runs for four years from 2028 through 2031, and the company says it has also secured additional volume of more than 30,000 tons per year from 2028 onward.

Capital expenditure for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 160.5 billion, more than double the KRW 74.5 billion in the same period of 2025. The company expects that as LFP shipments rise, raw-material stock will be consumed faster and profit volatility will ease.

08

Bull factors

Record NCM Shipments Projected

The company projected that 2026 NCM shipments would rise about 40% from the prior year to a record. Sole supply of ultra-high-nickel products is expected to continue into Q4, with a target of a new quarterly sales high. Its 2027 plan calls for the 46-series share to reach 40% and NCM shipments to grow at least 10%. Whether this volume growth converts into operating profit recovery is the next point to confirm.

Medium-to-Long-Term LFP Orders Secured

L&F says it has secured medium-to-long-term supply contracts totaling 250,000 tons with four customers, including the three major Korean cell makers and one overseas customer.

The Samsung SDI contract of about KRW 1.6 trillion was signed in March 2026, and the SK On contract of about KRW 161.7 billion in September 2026. The LG Energy Solution contract of about KRW 1.035 trillion was signed on November 2, 2026. Mass production has started at the Daegu plant, establishing the production base.

Improving Cash Flow and Debt Ratio

Operating cash flow turned positive at KRW 280.5 billion in 2024. According to the company's November 2026 release, operating cash flow for the first three quarters of 2026 was an inflow of KRW 85.8 billion, improved from an outflow of KRW 8.6 billion a year earlier.

The debt ratio at the end of Q3 2026 was 372%, lower than 692% at the same point a year earlier. The company expects equity to strengthen if the remaining warrants are exercised.

09

Bear factors

Three Straight Years of Operating Losses

On a filed basis, operating losses persisted from 2023 through 2025. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 533.5 billion in 2025 from KRW 377.9 billion in 2024. The operating margin was -29.3% in 2024 and -7.3% in 2025, so the loss narrowed but a return to profit is still some way off. Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters total KRW 238.6 billion.

Earnings Sensitive to Raw Materials and FX

The Q3 2026 operating loss reflected raw-material inventory valuation losses of about KRW 42.6 billion and exchange-rate-related losses of about KRW 22 billion.

The company says profitability was solid excluding these external factors, but critics note that earnings still swing sharply with raw-material prices and exchange rates. The company expects average NCM selling prices to fall about 3% in Q4 from the prior quarter.

LFP Competition and the Ramp-Up Lag

The LFP market has been led by Chinese companies, and competition is intense, with POSCO Future M signing long-term deals worth about KRW 1.1 trillion with SK On and about KRW 6 trillion with Samsung SDI. L&F's full-scale LFP shipments are planned from 2027 onward, with about 3,000 tons produced in 2026. Converting order volume into profit depends on the pace of capacity expansion and on price competitiveness.

10

Risk factors

US China-Exit Policy Shift

Part of LFP demand has emerged as US power developers have strengthened their stance of exiting Chinese supply chains. This demand may shift with US trade and energy policy and with grid market conditions.

If the policy direction changes or ESS procurement timing is delayed, the LFP shipment plan from 2027 and utilization at the Daegu plant could be affected. Whether the medium-to-long-term contracts absorb such demand shifts is the key issue.

Balance Sheet and Funding

Total liabilities were KRW 2.46 trillion and total equity was KRW 676.8 billion at the end of 2025, giving a debt ratio of 363.1%. Capital expenditure rose to KRW 160.5 billion in the first three quarters of 2026, and the company is using policy financing such as the National Growth Fund for LFP expansion.

The scale of equity capital added could vary depending on whether and when the remaining warrants are exercised.

Customer Concentration and Contract Values

Because L&F supplies a core NCM customer on a sole-source basis, results depend on that customer's EV sales and production plans. LFP contract values are calculated from minimum committed volumes and recent prices, so actual amounts may vary with supply volumes and selling prices.

With global EV market uncertainty continuing, the possibility of adjustments to volume plans also needs to be watched.

Overall view

L&F recorded operating losses for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025 and carries a balance sheet with a debt ratio of 363.1% at the end of 2025.

For 2026, the projected record NCM shipments and the securing of 250,000 tons of medium-to-long-term LFP orders are presented as factors that could reshape the business.

However, Q3 2026 showed an operating loss of KRW 26.5 billion driven by inventory valuation and exchange-rate losses, reconfirming that earnings are sensitive to raw-material prices and exchange rates.

The cumulative turn to operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 can be read as a positive sign, but quarter-to-quarter swings remain large. On the strength side are volume growth and the LFP customer base. On the weakness side are repeated losses, a high debt ratio and intensifying LFP competition.

The next points to confirm are the Q4 2026 NCM shipment growth and selling price decline, progress toward 60,000-ton LFP capacity at Daegu, and the operating and cash flow figures in the 2026 annual report.
