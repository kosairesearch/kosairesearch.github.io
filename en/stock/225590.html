Report overview

Profit recovery at a women's apparel maker, market value below book equity

Fashion Platform, a women's apparel brand operator, lifted its operating margin from 1.6% in 2024 to 7.9% in 2025, but sales have moved within a narrow range, and its market value trails book equity, after which it was designated an administrative issue in August 2026.

02

Business structure

Fashion Platform was founded in 2009 after taking over the women's apparel division of Renoma, and it manufactures and sells women's apparel.

A 2020 technical analysis report introduced its main brands, and a 2026 Dealsite report said its women's apparel brands are sold mainly through department stores, outlets and street shops. Sales by segment in the company profile data show apparel sales at 99.17% and rental income at 0.83%.

According to Dealsite, apparel accounted for KRW 105.4 billion, or 99.1%, of 2025 sales. Despite the word 'platform' in its name, the business is not a marketplace earning commissions on third-party sellers; it designs and sells its own products in a traditional apparel model.

Rental income comes from its investment property and is under 1% of total sales. The same report cited cash and equivalents of KRW 16 billion and inventory of KRW 31 billion.

Competition comes from mid-to-low-priced women's apparel brands and online select shops; a 2020 report said the entry of select shops such as W Concept, Musinsa and 29CM intensified competition. The company's competitiveness therefore rests on brand positioning and its department-store-centered offline distribution.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales went from KRW 109.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 106 billion in 2023 (down 3.6% year on year) and KRW 104.6 billion in 2024 (down 1.3%), before reaching KRW 106.3 billion in 2025 (up 1.6%). 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest among 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit declined from KRW 12 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.1 billion in 2023 (down 32.3%) and KRW 1.66 billion in 2024 (down 79.6%), then rose to KRW 8.36 billion in 2025 (up 404.9%). Operating margin was 10.9% in 2022, 7.6% in 2023, 1.6% in 2024 and 7.9% in 2025.

Margins moved far more than sales did, which suggests that the cost structure, rather than sales volume, drove profit; the line-item causes should be checked in the annual report's income statement details.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 9.67 billion in 2022, KRW 6.35 billion in 2023 (down 34.3%), KRW 1.29 billion in 2024 (down 79.7%) and KRW 2.74 billion in 2025 (up 112.4%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 9.72 billion, KRW 6.37 billion, KRW 1.11 billion and KRW 2.68 billion for the same years, so the two figures differ. Operating cash flow was KRW 7.03 billion in 2022, KRW 4.64 billion in 2023, KRW 16.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.97 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales were KRW 26.7 billion in the second quarter of 2025, KRW 21.9 billion in the third (down 18.0% from the prior quarter), KRW 34.6 billion in the fourth (up 58.3%), KRW 27.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 21.8%) and KRW 30.2 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (up 11.7% from the prior quarter and up 13.3% year on year).

Quarterly operating profit rose from KRW 921 million in the second quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.79 billion in the third and KRW 4.04 billion in the fourth, then came to KRW 3.16 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 21.6%) and KRW 4.17 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (up 31.7% from the prior quarter and up 352.5% year on year).

Profit attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters (third quarter of 2025 to second quarter of 2026) totaled KRW 11.2 billion. According to Dealsite, first-half 2026 operating margin improved from 5.1% to 12.8%.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's fashion platform sector is shifting its focus from sales expansion to profitability. According to an Edaily-type industry report cited by E-Today, the sector is strengthening revenue models through commissions, service sales and category diversification.

Musinsa reportedly posted 2025 consolidated sales of KRW 1,467.9 billion, up 18.1%, with operating profit up 36.7%. W Concept, by contrast, swung to an operating loss of KRW 3.1 billion as marketing costs rose. Profitability is therefore diverging even among platforms.

Fashion Platform is a women's apparel maker and seller rather than a marketplace, so it is not directly exposed to this shift; demand for women's apparel and the department-store distribution environment drive its results instead.

A 2019 technical analysis report described the apparel market as facing low growth and intense competition, and a 2020 report described women's apparel as sensitive to the economic cycle.

First-half 2026 sales growth of 14.9%, compared with 1.6% growth for full-year 2025, is a data point to confirm against quarterly trends to tell whether it reflects a sector recovery or a one-off factor.

Outlook

The next periodic filing is the third-quarter 2026 quarterly report, due 45 days after quarter-end, on 14 November 2026. The first checkpoint is whether third-quarter sales exceed the KRW 21.9 billion recorded in the third quarter of 2025, and whether operating profit exceeds KRW 1.79 billion.

Second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.17 billion shows improvement, but the fourth quarter of 2025 at KRW 4.04 billion and the first quarter of 2026 at KRW 3.16 billion show that quarterly swings remain large.

According to Dealsite, the KOSDAQ market-capitalization threshold is set to rise from KRW 20 billion to KRW 30 billion from 2027, which raises the bar for lifting market value above the line. Exit from administrative-issue status depends on whether the reason for designation has been resolved.

The 2026 annual report is due by 31 March 2027, 90 days after year-end, and the key test is whether operating profit holds near the KRW 8.36 billion of 2025. The trend in inventory, reported at KRW 31 billion by Dealsite, and whether operating cash flow stays near the KRW 7.97 billion of 2025 are also items to confirm. On the sales side, the central question is whether first-half growth continues into the second half.

08

Bull factors

Earnings base has recovered

Operating profit rose to KRW 8.36 billion in 2025 from KRW 1.66 billion in 2024, and operating margin climbed to 7.9%. Second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.17 billion, up 352.5% year on year, shows the recovery at the quarterly level as well.

According to Dealsite, first-half 2026 operating cash flow reached KRW 6.9 billion, above net income of KRW 6.3 billion for the same period.

A steadier balance sheet

The debt-to-equity ratio was 33.5% at the end of 2025, down from 44.5% in 2022. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 77.8 billion, of which KRW 77.5 billion belonged to owners of the parent. Operating cash flow in 2024 was KRW 16.4 billion, well above that year's operating profit of KRW 1.66 billion.

A gap between assets and market value

Dealsite reported that market value has fallen to roughly the level of the company's cash holdings. The same report listed its investment property, property and equipment, and inventory. If this gap narrows alongside sustained earnings, it would support the bull case, which is the central question for the stock.

09

Bear factors

Stagnant top line

Annual sales fell from KRW 109.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 106.3 billion in 2025. First-half 2026 sales rose 14.9%, but one half-year is too short to judge a long-term trend. With the top line flat, profit improvement depends more heavily on the cost structure.

Volatile profits

Operating margin fell to 1.6% in 2024, and profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 1.29 billion. Quarterly sales dropped 21.8% from the prior quarter, from KRW 34.6 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 27.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026. Such swings in quarterly results make the durability of the recovery a point to verify.

Listing rules and market perception

The company was designated an administrative issue in August 2026 because its market value fell below the threshold. Dealsite reported that the KOSDAQ market-capitalization threshold will rise to KRW 30 billion from 2027. If market value does not return above the threshold, the regulatory pressure could persist.

10

Risk factors

Listing-rule risk

If the market-capitalization threshold for administrative-issue designation rises, the current market value would make it harder to meet the requirement. A prolonged designation can weigh on trading and on investment decisions. If market value recovers slowly, the regulatory uncertainty lasts longer.

Sector cycle and concentration risk

Apparel sales account for 99% of revenue, so the company is directly exposed to any slowdown in consumer spending. A 2019 technical analysis report pointed to low growth and intense competition in the apparel market, and a 2020 report noted that women's apparel is sensitive to the economic cycle. Competition from select shops in the mid-to-low-priced segment also continues.

Inventory and earnings-swing risk

Inventory stood at KRW 31 billion according to a Dealsite report. Heavier inventory could amplify swings in gross profit and operating profit. Operating profit fell to KRW 1.66 billion in 2024, and quarterly sales have moved between KRW 27.1 billion and KRW 34.6 billion in recent quarters.

12

Overall view

Fashion Platform is a maker and seller of women's apparel, and despite its name, its business is centered on apparel sales. On a disclosed basis, operating profit recovered from KRW 1.66 billion in 2024 to KRW 8.36 billion in 2025, and reached KRW 4.17 billion in the second quarter of 2026.

Sales, by contrast, have stayed between KRW 104.6 billion and KRW 109.9 billion since 2022, so top-line growth has been limited. Market value sits well below book equity, and the company was designated an administrative issue in August 2026.

On the bull side, the earnings recovery, a steadier balance sheet and the gap between assets and market value are the supporting arguments. On the bear side, a flat top line, volatile quarterly profits and listing-rule requirements are the main concerns.

The next checkpoints are the third-quarter 2026 quarterly report, due 14 November 2026, and the 2026 annual report. The gap between market value and the company's assets could narrow or persist, depending on whether earnings are sustained and whether the market-value threshold is met.
