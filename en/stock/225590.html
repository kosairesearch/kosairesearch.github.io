02

Business structure

Fashion Platform was established after acquiring the Renoma women's apparel division in 2009 and listed on KOSDAQ through a SPAC merger in 2018.

The company operates five women's apparel brands—The Renoma, Bonispax, Le Chap, Liete and DECO—supplying differentiated products through offline distribution channels and online malls.

Through market research, design planning, brand launches and item development, it targets women in their 20s to late 40s across varied styles and price points. In the first half of 2026, out of revenue of KRW 57.3 billion, 99.1% came from the fashion segment, with the remainder from smaller businesses such as leasing.

The largest shareholder is Maven FC, whose stake rose to 30.17% after the 2026 stock consolidation. CEO Park Won-hee also serves as representative of the controlling shareholder entity, tying management closely to the governance structure.

In the domestic women's apparel market, the company competes against larger players such as LF, Handsome and Shinsegae International that have expanded into broader lifestyle businesses.

With planning, production and distribution handled in-house, the company carries a business model typical of small- and mid-cap apparel firms, directly exposed to inventory burden and consumption swings.

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated revenue fell from KRW 109.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 106 billion in 2023 (down 3.6% year on year) and KRW 104.6 billion in 2024 (down 1.3%), before rebounding to KRW 106.3 billion in 2025 (up 1.6%). Among 2022-2025, revenue was highest in 2022 and lowest in 2024.

Operating profit plunged from KRW 12 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.1 billion in 2023 (down 32.3%) and KRW 1.66 billion in 2024 (down 79.6%), before surging to KRW 8.36 billion in 2025 (up 404.9%).

The company disclosed that the 2025 operating profit increase was mainly driven by valuation reversals from the sale of multi-year aged inventory. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 9.67 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.29 billion in 2024, then recovered 112.4% to KRW 2.74 billion in 2025.

Quarterly results showed a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 3.36 billion in the second quarter of 2025, a swing to profit of KRW 839 million in the third quarter, KRW 4.08 billion in the fourth quarter, followed by KRW 2.71 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 3.54 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (a swing to profit from a year earlier).

Revenue rose to KRW 34.6 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, fell to KRW 27.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then rose again to KRW 30.2 billion in the second quarter (up 11.7% quarter on quarter and up 13.3% year on year).

The sum of controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters (Q3 2025 through Q2 2026) came to KRW 11.2 billion. Operating margin fell to 1.6% in 2024 before recovering to 7.9% in 2025, and second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.17 billion rose 352.5% year on year.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic women's apparel market is sensitive to consumption cycles and fashion trends, with a mix of department store/outlet-centered offline distribution and online channels.

Total revenue for the KOSDAQ textile and apparel sector came to KRW 3,179 billion with operating profit of KRW 24.9 billion in 2025, placing Fashion Platform (revenue of KRW 106.3 billion, operating profit of KRW 8.4 billion) among the smaller players in the sector.

The industry includes large firms such as LF, Handsome and Shinsegae International that have expanded into broader lifestyle businesses, alongside small and mid-sized firms like Fashion Platform that focus on the mid-price segment through brand portfolios.

Domestic apparel consumption, amid the expansion of online and resale platforms, has become a structural pressure point for smaller brands reliant on offline channels.

The Korea Exchange raised the market-cap threshold for administrative issue designation twice, in January and July 2026, triggering a wave of new administrative issue designations among small-cap KOSDAQ stocks.

Fashion Platform was included among the stocks designated as administrative issues on August 13, 2026 for falling below the KRW 20 billion market-cap threshold.

This wave of designations tied to the raised market-cap threshold appears to be a structural factor affecting small-cap KOSDAQ stocks broadly rather than a risk unique to this company alone.

06

Outlook

In June 2026, Fashion Platform disclosed a mid-term policy to allocate at least 10% of adjusted standalone net income for 2026-2028 to shareholder returns including dividends and treasury share buybacks or retirements.

Since the company had paid no cash dividends for the previous five years, whether this policy translates into actual dividends or buybacks will be a test of market confidence going forward.

In March 2026, the company also carried out a 5-for-1 common stock consolidation and retired 1,029,831 treasury shares, working in parallel to reduce per-share dilution.

A company official stated that sales of its women's apparel brands remain decent but that the share price has not kept pace with performance, adding that no new business ventures are currently under review.

Starting January 1, 2027, the KOSDAQ market-cap threshold for administrative issue designation is set to rise further from KRW 20 billion to KRW 30 billion, adding to the burden of meeting the requirement.

Even if the earnings recovery continues, escaping administrative issue status requires the company to meet the exchange's market-cap requirement continuously for a specified period.

08

Bull factors

Earnings Recovery Trend

Operating profit rose 404.9% year on year in 2025, and the recovery continued with operating profit of KRW 3.16 billion and KRW 4.17 billion in the first and second quarters of 2026, respectively.

The sum of controlling-shareholder net income over the last four quarters came to KRW 11.2 billion, a clear move away from the net loss recorded in the second quarter of 2025. Operating margin also climbed from 1.6% in 2024 to 7.9% in 2025.

Financial Soundness

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 44.5% in 2022 to 33.5% in 2025, and year-end 2025 total equity of KRW 77.8 billion far exceeds total liabilities of KRW 26.1 billion. Operating cash flow also stayed consistently positive at KRW 16.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.97 billion in 2025. Despite topline fluctuations, the balance sheet has been managed relatively conservatively.

Capital Policy and Shareholder Return Efforts

The company decided on a 5-for-1 common stock consolidation and the retirement of 1,029,831 treasury shares in March 2026, and in June 2026 disclosed a mid-term shareholder return policy to allocate at least 10% of adjusted net income for 2026-2028 to dividends and treasury buybacks or retirements. Given the prior history of no dividends, whether this policy is actually implemented is a key point to watch.

Bear factors

Revenue Growth Stagnation

Consolidated revenue fell from KRW 109.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 104.6 billion in 2024 and remained at just KRW 106.3 billion in 2025, staying in the low-to-mid KRW 100 billion range for four straight years.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 30.2 billion rose 13.3% year on year, but quarter-to-quarter swings have been large.

Earnings Volatility

Operating profit swung sharply year to year, falling 79.6% from KRW 12 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.66 billion in 2024 before surging 404.9% in 2025. The company disclosed that the 2025 improvement was influenced by one-off factors including valuation reversals from the sale of multi-year aged inventory. This volatility adds uncertainty to forecasting future performance.

Administrative Issue Status and Valuation Gap

Fashion Platform was designated an administrative issue stock on August 13, 2026 after its market capitalization remained below KRW 20 billion.

Even though consolidated total equity reached KRW 84.1 billion at the end of June 2026, market capitalization stayed below half that amount, leaving a large gap between book value and market value. From 2027, the threshold rises further to KRW 30 billion, adding to the burden.

Risk factors

Listing Maintenance Risk

If market capitalization fails to continuously meet the exchange's requirement following the August 2026 administrative issue designation, it could lead to further procedures such as a listing eligibility review. The threshold rising from KRW 20 billion to KRW 30 billion starting January 2027 adds to the burden.

Business Concentration Risk

With most revenue concentrated in the single category of domestic women's apparel, performance can be sensitive to domestic consumption cycles or shifts in demand for specific brands. No new business plans beyond its existing brands have been confirmed at this time.

Shareholder Return Execution Uncertainty

The mid-term shareholder return policy disclosed in June 2026 has not yet been finalized with specific details such as payout ratio or buyback size. Given the absence of cash dividends over the past five years, whether the policy is actually executed needs to be confirmed through future disclosures.

12

Overall view

Fashion Platform's performance, which saw operating margin fall to as low as 1.6% in 2024, has clearly recovered through 2025 and the first half of 2026.

In contrast, its market capitalization fell below the exchange's requirement, leading to its designation as an administrative issue stock on August 13, 2026, moving the market's assessment in a different direction from the earnings trend.

The company has continued efforts to lift per-share value through capital measures including a 5-for-1 stock consolidation, treasury share retirement, and the disclosure of a mid-term shareholder return policy.

Still, revenue has not broken out of the low-to-mid KRW 100 billion range for four straight years, and the wide year-to-year swings in profit make it premature to draw firm conclusions about the earnings trajectory.

The further rise in the administrative issue threshold starting in 2027 remains an additional challenge the company must clear.

The next quarterly filing, the actual execution of the shareholder return policy, and whether the requirements for removal from administrative issue status are met appear to be the key variables to monitor going forward.
