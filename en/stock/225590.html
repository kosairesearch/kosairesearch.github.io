02

Business structure

Fashion Platform was founded in 2009 to manufacture and sell apparel and is a women's wear focused fashion company. It listed on KOSDAQ in 2018 through a merger with a special purpose acquisition company, after which the listed entity has run the business in substance.

Despite the word 'platform' in its name, it is not an online intermediary; it plans and sells its own brands. According to the company profile, it operates the Renoma Lady, Bonispax and Herad Dresscode brands through department stores, discount stores, dealers and directly operated roadshops.

In July 2021 it acquired the Lesop brand from a related company through an asset transfer agreement, widening its brand lineup. Fashion segment revenue accounted for 99.1% of consolidated sales in the first half of 2026, so the business is effectively concentrated in one segment.

Under this structure, the performance of specific seasons and brands weighs heavily on results, and the seasonality shows in the 2025 quarterly figures, where fourth-quarter sales far exceeded third-quarter sales.

Competition comes from listed women's wear and casual brand companies and from department-store-centered retail rivalry; listed peers in the sector include LF, Handsome, F&F and Shinsegae International.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show sales and profits both declining after 2022, followed by a recovery in profit in 2025. Consolidated sales fell from KRW 109.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 106 billion in 2023 (down 3.6% year on year) and KRW 104.6 billion in 2024 (down 1.3%), then rose slightly to KRW 106.3 billion in 2025 (up 1.6%).

Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year by sales and 2024 the smallest. Operating profit went from KRW 12 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.1 billion in 2023 (down 32.3%) and KRW 1.66 billion in 2024 (down 79.6%), then recovered to KRW 8.36 billion in 2025 (up 404.9%).

The operating margin fell from 10.9% in 2022 to 1.6% in 2024 and returned to 7.9% in 2025. Attributable net income dropped from KRW 9.67 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.29 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 2.74 billion in 2025 (up 112.4%); net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 2.68 billion in 2025.

The gap between 2025 operating profit of KRW 8.36 billion and attributable net income of KRW 2.74 billion reflects items below the operating line whose detail should be checked in the full annual report. A financial information provider attributed the 2025 improvement to brand diversification.

Operating cash flow was KRW 16.4 billion in 2024, far above that year's operating profit of KRW 1.66 billion, so working capital and other drivers need to be checked.

On a quarterly basis, the fourth quarter of 2025 was the peak with sales of KRW 34.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.04 billion, followed by KRW 27.1 billion and KRW 3.16 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 30.2 billion and KRW 4.17 billion in the second quarter, before the third quarter of 2026 fell to KRW 21.5 billion in sales and KRW 735 million in operating profit.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 58.8% year on year, and attributable net income swung to a net loss of KRW 39.73 million from a profit of KRW 839 million in the third quarter of 2025.

On a cumulative basis from the quarterly report, operating profit for January to September 2026 rose 86.5% year on year to about KRW 8.07 billion, so the year-to-date recovery trend still holds.

05

Industry analysis

Industry reporting on the third quarter of 2026 describes a broadly improved performance across domestic fashion companies. Samsung C&T's fashion division recorded third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 498 billion (up 11.9% year on year) and operating profit of KRW 20 billion (up 66.7%).

LF reported third-quarter 2026 consolidated operating profit of KRW 51.1 billion, up 216.6% year on year, with lower cost of sales cited as the main driver of margin improvement. Results were uneven across peers, with F&F reported as weak and Kolon FnC as loss-making.

Industry players point to overseas expansion and fast-growing imported brands as the main sources of earnings improvement. Fashion Platform's third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 21.5 billion (down 1.7% year on year) moved against this trend, and its operating profit fell 58.8%.

In scale, Fashion Platform's 2025 annual sales of KRW 106.3 billion are small next to LF's first-half 2026 consolidated sales of KRW 927.3 billion, placing it at the smaller end of the sector.

Unlike peers whose growth has been led by overseas and imported brands, Fashion Platform leans on expanding its own women's wear brand lineup, so its growth path differs.

Seasonality is visible in the 2025 quarterly figures, with sales of KRW 21.9 billion in the third quarter and KRW 34.6 billion in the fourth, which suggests that winter product mix drives quarterly results.

Bull factors

Profit recovery still in place

Operating profit recovered from KRW 1.66 billion in 2024 (operating margin 1.6%) to KRW 8.36 billion in 2025 (operating margin 7.9%). Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 rose 86.5% year on year to about KRW 8.07 billion. Attributable net income in 2025 was more than double the 2024 level. On a cumulative basis, the profit recovery trend remains intact.

Value-enhancement plan disclosed

The plan disclosed in October 2026 sets a 2030 price-to-book target of 0.50x. The company identified insufficient liquidity and communication, along with excess non-earning assets, as causes of weaker capital efficiency. Publishing a numerical target gives shareholders a public benchmark. The implementation measures and timing should be checked in the original disclosure.

Stable balance sheet

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 44.5% in 2022 to 33.5% in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 77.8 billion, of which KRW 77.5 billion was attributable to shareholders. Total liabilities were KRW 26.1 billion. Operating cash flow stayed positive at KRW 7.97 billion in 2025.

Bear factors

Stalled sales

Annual sales fell from KRW 109.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 106.3 billion in 2025, and 2025 growth was only 1.6%. Third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 21.5 billion, down 1.7% year on year. Without sales growth, profit margins remain sensitive to small changes.

Third-quarter profit deterioration

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 58.8% year on year to KRW 735 million. Attributable net income swung to a net loss of KRW 39.73 million. The 2024 operating margin of 1.6% shows how vulnerable profitability has been to weaker periods.

Administrative designation and thin float

The stock was designated as an administrative issue in August 2026 after market capitalization remained below KRW 20 billion. Largest shareholders hold 40.95%, leaving a free float of 59.05%. With a relatively small tradable share base, the designation adds a burden on trading and confidence.

Risk factors

Listing maintenance

The August 2026 administrative designation is based on market capitalization. If the value stays below the threshold for longer, the designation can continue. Whether the threshold is met and any change in status will be confirmed through exchange disclosures. This risk operates separately from operating results.

Seasonality and quarterly volatility

Fourth-quarter 2025 sales of KRW 34.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.04 billion were well above the third quarter. By contrast, third-quarter 2026 sales fell to KRW 21.5 billion and operating profit to KRW 735 million.

Product mix and seasonal performance in one or two quarters can shift quarterly results sharply, so several quarters need to be read together.

Execution of the value plan

The 2030 price-to-book target of 0.50x depends on improving capital efficiency and liquidity. If the excess non-earning assets and communication gaps the company identified are not resolved, reaching the target becomes uncertain. Whether concrete implementation steps and timing are disclosed will determine the size of this risk.

Overall view

Fashion Platform's sales have stalled since 2022 and its profit recovered in 2025, but in the third quarter of 2026 operating profit fell 58.8% and attributable net income turned into a loss, leaving earnings volatile.

Annual operating profit for 2025 was KRW 8.36 billion (up 404.9%), with an operating margin of 7.9%, recovering from the 2024 low.

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 rose 86.5% to about KRW 8.07 billion, but the third quarter alone shows operating profit of KRW 735 million and a net loss of KRW 39.73 million.

Strengths include the profit recovery, a debt-to-equity ratio of 33.5%, and the value-enhancement plan disclosed in October 2026. Weaknesses include flat sales, the weaker third quarter, the administrative designation in August 2026 and a low free float.

The gap between market capitalization and book value and recent earnings is a matter of fact, but how much it narrows depends on results and execution of the plan. The next checkpoint is the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and the fourth-quarter 2026 results, expected around late March 2027.
