02

Business structure

Hanwha Galleria operates five Galleria department stores, including the Apgujeong Galleria Luxury Hall, Gwanggyo, Centercity in Cheonan, Timeworld in Daejeon and Jinju, making it a department-store-centered retailer.

The department store segment accounted for 97.7% of sales in 2023 and 88.5% in 2024, according to NICE Credit Rating. In the first half of 2026 the department store share fell to 84.1% as the food and beverage business expanded. The F&B business has been built around store expansion for Five Guys and Benson.

Segment sales before consolidation adjustments for January to September 2026 were KRW 350.6 billion for department stores and KRW 75.2 billion for F&B, as reported by Newspim.

Profit contribution is far more concentrated: in the first half of 2026 gross profit was KRW 190.1 billion for department stores and KRW 4.2 billion for F&B. Key customers are VIP clients with strong demand for luxury brands and foreign visitors.

The Seoul Luxury Hall recorded sales of KRW 1.17 trillion in 2024, down 1.5% year on year, and it is the only Galleria store to exceed KRW 1 trillion in sales.

In competition, NICE Credit Rating analyzed that the Shinsegae Gyeonggi renovation and the opening of Shinsegae Art & Science in Daejeon shifted demand away from the Gwanggyo and Timeworld stores.

Based on the National Statistics Portal department store sales data, Galleria's market share fell from 8.0% in 2024 to 6.4% in 2025 and 6.1% in the first half of 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 434.5 billion in 2023 to KRW 538.3 billion in 2024 (up 23.9% year on year) and KRW 575.2 billion in 2025 (up 6.8%), increasing two years in a row from 2024 to 2025. The largest year among 2023 to 2025 was 2025, and the smallest was 2023.

Operating profit was KRW 9.82 billion in 2023, KRW 3.13 billion in 2024 (down 68.1%) and KRW 9.41 billion in 2025 (up 200.8%). The largest operating profit year among 2023 to 2025 was 2023, and the smallest was 2024.

The operating margin was 2.3% in 2023, 0.6% in 2024 and 1.6% in 2025, so the margin stayed low even as sales grew. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 30.1 billion in 2023 and a loss of KRW 18.8 billion in 2024, then turned to a profit of KRW 3.31 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests showed the same direction, at KRW 3.31 billion in 2025.

In quarterly terms, Q4 2025 sales were KRW 193.4 billion (up 54.1% from the prior quarter) with operating profit of KRW 9.09 billion, and attributable net income of KRW 25 billion far exceeded that operating profit, so non-operating items should be read alongside it.

Q1 2026 sales were KRW 126.1 billion (down 34.8% from the prior quarter) with operating profit of KRW 12.3 billion, compared with about KRW 1.8 billion in the same quarter of 2025 according to news reports. Q2 2026 sales were KRW 150.9 billion with operating profit of KRW 15.1 billion.

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 122.9 billion (down 2.1% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 5.99 billion (up 76.5% year on year), but the attributable net loss widened to KRW 3.05 billion.

According to news reports, Q3 selling and administrative expenses fell by KRW 2.6 billion year on year, and cumulative January to September 2026 operating profit was KRW 33.5 billion. Attributable net income over the most recent four quarters totaled KRW 26.2 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 127.7 billion in 2023, KRW 63.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 78.4 billion in 2025. The consolidated debt ratio at end-Q3 2026 was 192.2% according to news reports, up from 144.6% at end-2025, and short-term borrowings rose from KRW 153.0 billion to KRW 511.0 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Department store sales are recovering in luxury and fashion, yet polarization across the sector is deepening. Hanwha Galleria cited luxury demand and higher foreign-customer sales as drivers in its Q1 2026 earnings explanation.

Foreign visitors to Korea topped 10 million in the first half of 2026, supporting luxury spending, according to analysis in the press. NICE Credit Rating, however, pointed to weaker domestic consumption amid high prices and deeper polarization in the department store market as factors behind lower profitability.

The same assessment attributed the sales slowdown at the Gwanggyo and Timeworld stores to demand shifting after the Shinsegae Gyeonggi renovation and the opening of Shinsegae in Daejeon. The operating margin fell from 2.3% in 2023 to 0.6% in 2024 and recovered to 1.6% in 2025, still below the 2023 level.

Among nine companies in the department store and general retail industry on an annual basis, Hanwha Galleria ranked fourth in 2025 sales and sixth in operating profit, according to AlphaSquare. The Seoul Luxury Hall fell to 12th in national department store sales rankings in 2024, its second consecutive annual decline.

Because one store accounts for a large share of results, luxury demand directly shapes the company's earnings direction. In competition with Shinsegae, Hyundai Department Store and Lotte Shopping, luxury brand access and VIP customer loyalty are cited as differentiators.

06

Outlook

The company said it will build medium- to long-term growth by strengthening its department store competitiveness and diversifying its business portfolio. On the customer side, it said it will keep developing new content and strengthening services.

The largest variable is the rebuild of the Apgujeong Galleria Luxury Hall. The east and west buildings are to be rebuilt in sequence from 2027 to 2033, with expected investment of about KRW 900 billion.

NICE Credit Rating expected that sales would inevitably decline during the rebuild because of store closures, and it judged that how well existing stores defend profitability would be the key factor for results.

In the F&B business, the company took part in two rights offerings, in December 2024 and May 2025, investing KRW 7.0 billion in total to support store expansion for Five Guys.

In June 2026 it bought the Sunhwa Building and its site in Jung-gu, Seoul for KRW 213.5 billion, and acquired land in Sinsa-dong, Gangnam-gu for KRW 236.7 billion.

According to news reports, short-term borrowings at end-Q3 2026 were KRW 511.0 billion and long-term borrowings were KRW 270.0 billion, while cash and equivalents stood at KRW 39.6 billion.

Whether core luxury brands and customers are retained during the rebuild, and how the rebuild is financed, are the next items to check. Whether the F&B business reduces dependence on department stores will also be visible in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Profit recovery centered on the flagship

Q3 sales fell 2.1% year on year, while operating profit rose 76.5%. According to news reports, selling and administrative expenses fell by KRW 2.6 billion in the same quarter, and the company attributed the overall profit gain to better department store profitability.

Expansion of the F&B business

F&B segment sales were reported at KRW 75.2 billion for January to September 2026, before consolidation adjustments. The department store share of sales fell from 97.8% in 2023 to 84.1% in the first half of 2026. Store expansion for Five Guys and Benson is tied to the strategy of reducing dependence on department stores.

Wider real estate asset base

In June 2026 the company bought the Sunhwa Building and its site in Jung-gu, Seoul for KRW 213.5 billion, and investment property rose to KRW 369.6 billion at end-Q3 2026. Total assets grew from KRW 2.02 trillion at end-2025 to KRW 2.50 trillion at end-Q3 2026. The larger property base can be read as a broader foundation for the business, which is cited as a bull factor.

09

Bear factors

Shrinking department store footprint and share

Seoul Luxury Hall sales were KRW 1.17 trillion in 2024, down 1.5% year on year. Galleria's market share fell from 8.0% in 2024 to 6.4% in 2025 and 6.1% in the first half of 2026. In 2024, sales declined at all five Galleria stores.

Rising financial leverage

According to news reports, the consolidated debt ratio at end-Q3 2026 was 192.2%, up from 144.6% at end-2025. Over the same period, short-term borrowings rose to KRW 511.0 billion and long-term borrowings to KRW 270.0 billion. BizWatch examined the financial burden in light of loans and guarantees extended to a PFV.

Sales gap during the rebuild

The Apgujeong Galleria Luxury Hall east and west buildings are to be rebuilt in sequence from 2027 to 2033, with expected investment of about KRW 900 billion. NICE Credit Rating judged that sales would inevitably decline during the rebuild as stores stop operating.

Because this flagship is central to Galleria's results, how well it defends operations during the rebuild is the key issue.

10

Risk factors

Financing and funding

Rebuild investment and property purchases overlap, raising reliance on borrowing. According to news reports, cash and equivalents fell from KRW 56.5 billion at end-2025 to KRW 39.6 billion at end-Q3 2026. If financial pressure grows, the pace of store investment and F&B expansion may need to be adjusted.

Demand and foreign visitor dependence

Luxury demand and higher foreign-customer sales are cited as the main drivers of the recent earnings recovery. If foreign visitor spending slows or luxury consumption turns, department store sales would be affected quickly.

In the first half of 2026, the department store segment accounted for 97.8% of gross profit, so this risk translates directly into earnings swings.

Business portfolio

The F&B business generated only KRW 4.2 billion of gross profit in the first half of 2026. In Q1 2026, the liquidation of a social enterprise affiliate and lower sales at some F&B subsidiaries reduced consolidated sales. A November 2025 report said store expansion and a sale review for Five Guys were proceeding at the same time.

What to watch next

  1. End of March 2027 (annual report filing deadline)

    Check how the operating margin (1.6% in 2025) and the debt ratio (144.6% in 2025) changed in the 2026 annual report. Whether the debt ratio, at 192.2% in the Q3 2026 news reports, persisted to year-end is the key point.

  2. Around May 2027 (based on the Q1 disclosure timing)

    Check whether Q1 2027 sales and operating profit exceed Q1 2026 (sales of KRW 126.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 12.3 billion). Also check whether luxury demand and foreign-customer sales momentum hold.

  3. 2027 (start of the Apgujeong Galleria Luxury Hall rebuild)

    After the east building rebuild begins, check the scope of store operations, the size of the sales decline and the funding plan. The rebuild runs through 2033, so the impact of each phase on results is the focus.

  4. During 2027

    Check F&B subsidiary sales and the progress of Five Guys store expansion and sale review. The key question is whether the strategy to reduce department store dependence translates into actual profit contribution.

12

Overall view

Hanwha Galleria is advancing a recovery in its core department store profitability while expanding its F&B business at the same time. Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 9.41 billion, up 200.8% year on year, and quarterly operating profit in January to September 2026 was positive in every quarter.

However, sales growth is constrained by a falling department store share and lower Seoul Luxury Hall sales. The financial structure has become more leveraged, with the debt ratio rising from 144.6% at end-2025 to 192.2% at end-Q3 2026 according to news reports, and short-term borrowings increasing sharply.

The Apgujeong Galleria Luxury Hall rebuild from 2027 to 2033, with expected investment of about KRW 900 billion, is the largest variable for both earnings and finances.

Bullish factors are the earnings recovery supported by luxury and foreign demand and a larger property and F&B asset base, while bearish factors are the falling share, higher borrowing and the sales gap during the rebuild.

The next items to check are the operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report and the store operations' resilience after the rebuild begins in 2027.
