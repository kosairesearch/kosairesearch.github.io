Report overview

BBT Technology Assets Meet Business Overhaul: Revenue Recovery Is the Critical Test

NPX, Korea's only BBT equipment maker in the midst of a multi-pronged business overhaul, has seen revenue fall 84% from its 2022 peak and suffered massive 2025 losses that severely eroded its equity base; AI-server-driven FC-BGA substrate capex revival and a sharply narrower Q1 2026 operating deficit now represent the decisive inflection test for a recovery trajectory.

02

Business structure

NPX (formerly Baiopeutro) was founded in 2000 as a PCB electrical testing equipment manufacturer and remains Korea's only dedicated BBT (Bare Board Tester) equipment company.

BBT systems inspect the electrical continuity of bare PCBs — detecting open circuits and shorts — before component assembly, covering semiconductor substrates, FPCBs, and HDI boards.

In 2023 it achieved the world's second domestic FC-BGA BBT development, and subsequently became the world's first to commercialize a Hybrid Package BBT, entering the package-level BBT market long dominated by Japan's Nidec-Read. Screw automatic fastening systems constitute a secondary manufacturing line.

In December 2023, private equity group NPX Holdings acquired control and renamed the company, adding artist management, IT materials, and investment activities to its corporate charter.

By 2025, NPX had entered the beauty/fashion space under the 'Olio' brand and launched a YouTube-based new-media operation as part of its diversification strategy. The core customer base remains semiconductor substrate and PCB manufacturers, while global competition is led by Nidec-Read of Japan.

NPX is the only KOSDAQ-listed company producing comparable BBT equipment domestically, giving it a degree of technological differentiation, but reference-customer accumulation and revenue scale recovery remain the key operational hurdles.

04

Earnings analysis

NPX recorded its peak profitability in 2022 with revenue of ~KRW 22.3bn and operating income of ~KRW 4.8bn (OPM +21.6%), reflecting a strong semiconductor substrate upcycle.

A sustained upstream downturn and reduced customer capex thereafter drove a rapid deterioration: revenue fell to ~KRW 12.5bn in 2023 with an operating loss of ~KRW 538mn (OPM –4.3%), and further to ~KRW 8.3bn in 2024 with operating losses widening to ~KRW 3.2bn (OPM –38.6%).

The 2025 full year saw revenue collapse to ~KRW 3.6bn — down 56% year-on-year — while operating losses ballooned to ~KRW 14.6bn (OPM –401.2%), exceeding revenue by a factor of four. On a quarterly basis, Q1–Q3 2025 generated ~KRW 952mn, ~KRW 1.6bn, and ~KRW 435mn in revenue, respectively.

Q4 2025 was particularly severe: just ~KRW 633mn of revenue against ~KRW 8.5bn in operating losses, concentrating approximately 58% of the annual operating deficit into a single quarter — strongly implying material asset impairments or one-time write-downs.

The full-year net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached ~KRW 15.7bn, cutting equity from ~KRW 20.6bn (end-2024) to ~KRW 4.5bn (end-2025). Operating cash flow has been consistently negative since 2023: –KRW 1.3bn (2023), –KRW 4.0bn (2024), –KRW 3.4bn (2025).

Q1 2026 showed a sharp improvement, with operating loss narrowing to ~KRW 499mn on revenue of ~KRW 567mn, though the company remains in operating deficit.

05

Industry analysis

The global BBT equipment market is a concentrated oligopoly dominated by Japan's Nidec-Read, with no comparable listed domestic competitor in Korea.

PCB substrate demand cycles closely with semiconductor capex, and the structural expansion of AI servers and data-center infrastructure is driving secular growth in high-end FC-BGA substrate requirements.

Domestic Korean substrate manufacturers have already entered an FC-BGA capacity expansion phase, with select players expected to reach full-line FC-BGA utilization in H2 2026. Rising electrical testing requirements for GPU/CPU FC-BGA packages represent the critical catalyst for a new BBT equipment order cycle.

However, the 2023–2025 period was characterized by sluggish PC/smartphone demand and semiconductor inventory overhangs that severely compressed substrate capex, directly causing NPX's revenue collapse.

The gradual loosening of Japanese dominance in Hybrid Package and FC-BGA BBT markets is creating localization opportunities, but technology qualification and reference customer accumulation typically require extended timelines.

Optical inspection equipment makers such as GIGABIS occupy an indirect competitive position using a different inspection methodology; no directly comparable domestic listed BBT equipment maker exists.

06

Outlook

NPX faces a dual near-term challenge: stabilizing finances on a substantially reduced equity base while reversing the BBT order book decline.

Based on public disclosures, the company's strategy centers on accelerating penetration of Japanese-dominated FC-BGA and Hybrid Package BBT markets using its proprietary technology stack, bolstered by the completion of in-house Controller mass-production verification.

A 2024 supply contract with CNB Electronics (~KRW 3.7bn for FC-BGA BBT) established the first customer reference; converting that into additional wins is critical for a meaningful revenue recovery.

The beauty/fashion brand 'Olio's' planned online channel expansion and overseas export preparation, together with YouTube-based new-media monetization, are being pursued as supplementary revenue streams, though their financial materiality remains unverified.

Full FC-BGA line utilization at major domestic substrate manufacturers expected in H2 2026 represents a favorable external shift for BBT equipment procurement. However, if operating cash outflows persist and order recovery lags, the company may need to execute external financing via CBs, BWs, or rights offerings.

Whether the multi-dimensional diversification strategy — equipment, content, and investment — translates into genuine earnings contribution will be validated by results over the next several quarters.

08

Bull factors

Direct Beneficiary of Structural AI/FC-BGA Demand Recovery

The structural expansion of AI servers and data-center infrastructure is driving sustained FC-BGA substrate demand growth, with major Korean substrate makers expected to reach full FC-BGA line utilization in H2 2026.

NPX holds a unique domestic-supplier position through its FC-BGA BBT localization and world-first Hybrid Package BBT. Building on the first FC-BGA BBT reference delivery to CNB Electronics, NPX is positioned to capture meaningful upside when substrate customers restart equipment procurement in earnest.

Import-Substitution Opportunity and Rising Technology Self-Sufficiency

Successful entry into the package-level BBT market with the world-first Hybrid Package BBT has opened a structural opportunity to benefit from domestic customers' import-substitution preferences over incumbent Japanese suppliers.

Completion of in-house Controller mass-production verification further improves technology self-sufficiency. Global supply-chain restructuring and Korea's equipment localization policy trends could create a progressively more favorable procurement environment.

Q1 2026 Loss Contracts Sharply, Potential Normalization of Run-Rate Losses

Operating losses contracted dramatically from ~KRW 8.5bn in Q4 2025 to ~KRW 499mn in Q1 2026, suggesting that the bulk of one-time impairment charges were absorbed in Q4 and that underlying run-rate losses are normalizing.

Even a modest revenue uptick could accelerate operating loss reduction given the leaner cost base. If upstream demand materializes, the embedded operating leverage could amplify the speed of profit recovery.

09

Bear factors

Deepening Equity Erosion and Going-Concern Concerns

Equity ended 2025 at ~KRW 4.5bn, down 81% from 2022, with cumulative operating cash outflows of ~KRW 7.8bn over 2022–2025. Continued operating losses could push the company into full capital deficit.

This would necessitate external financing via rights offerings or CB/BW issuances — a risk made concrete by the company's prior expansion of its CB issuance limit from KRW 10bn to KRW 100bn, meaning dilutive capital-raising capacity is readily available.

Strategic Dilution and Core Competency Erosion Risk

Simultaneously pursuing beauty/fashion brands, artist management, new media, and semiconductor equipment within a severely capital-constrained entity risks inefficient resource allocation and diluted R&D/sales investment in the core BBT franchise.

New business financial contributions remain unproven, and if costs outpace revenues, operating losses could re-widen. There is insufficient public evidence that BBT order momentum has structurally improved since the management transition.

Upstream Recovery Timing Uncertainty and Market Penetration Constraints

The precise timing of a full FC-BGA substrate industry recovery remains highly uncertain, contingent on AI server investment cadence and substrate manufacturers' capex schedules.

NPX is still in early market penetration relative to Nidec-Read, with limited installed-base references in the FC-BGA and Hybrid Package segments.

Rebuilding customer confidence after four consecutive years of revenue decline is a multi-quarter process, and ongoing technology improvements by incumbent suppliers represent a persistent competitive threat.

10

Risk factors

Liquidity and Capital Structure Risk

With equity of ~KRW 4.5bn at end-2025 against annual operating cash outflows of several billion won in recent years, a prolonged order drought could precipitate full capital deficit in 2026.

The company's prior expansion of its CB issuance limit to KRW 100bn provides broad dilutive financing headroom, meaning any capital raise — whether via rights offerings or convertible instruments — would materially dilute existing shareholders.

Prolonged Order-Book Drought Risk

BBT equipment is fundamentally an order-driven business dependent on substrate manufacturers' capex decisions, where one or two large contracts can be decisive for full-year revenue, and order cycles are inherently irregular.

If FC-BGA industry conditions recover more slowly than expected, or if substrate customers satisfy needs with incumbent Japanese equipment, NPX could face a sustained order drought. Q3 2025's ~KRW 435mn in revenue illustrates the magnitude of downside exposure during order gaps.

Diversification Execution Failure and Management Credibility Risk

Simultaneously operating beauty/fashion brands and new-media ventures alongside a semiconductor equipment business in a capital-constrained environment risks inefficient resource allocation and may undermine investor confidence in management focus and domain expertise.

If new businesses incur costs without proportionate revenues, losses could widen again.

The management change and the addition of artist management and investment activities to the corporate charter represent ongoing market credibility risks for a company that needs to rebuild trust after four years of declining revenue.

12

Overall view

NPX possesses genuine technology assets — Korea's only BBT equipment franchise, FC-BGA localization, and the world-first Hybrid Package BBT — but faces a serious financial crisis following an 84% revenue decline since 2022 and massive 2025 losses that have eroded equity to approximately KRW 4.5bn.

If the Q4 2025 loss concentration was predominantly one-time in nature, the underlying run-rate loss trajectory in 2026 should be materially lower — a reading supported by the sharply narrower Q1 2026 operating deficit.

However, with persistent operating cash outflows and a dramatically thinned equity buffer, a delayed order recovery could necessitate external capital raises, bringing real dilution risk to existing shareholders.

Acceleration of AI and data-center infrastructure investment driving FC-BGA substrate capex revival is the clearest potential catalyst for a meaningful BBT order recovery, but limited installed-base references and a strategically dispersed business focus constrain the speed of any such conversion.

With new-business financial contributions unproven, BBT order momentum and capital sustainability remain the dominant determinants of NPX's fundamental trajectory going forward.
