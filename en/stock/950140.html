02

Business structure

Englewood Lab is an OEM and ODM company for cosmetics, developing and manufacturing products for client brands. Its sales are heavily concentrated in the United States, which accounted for 95% of sales as of Q3 2025 according to press coverage of the filing.

Product mix in the same quarter was basic cosmetics 74%, functional cosmetics and over-the-counter (OTC) products 17%, color cosmetics 8%, and services and other 1%. The functional and OTC share fell from 26.0% in Q3 2024 to 17% in Q3 2025, moving the mix toward basic cosmetics.

Brokerage commentary around 2025 said the company was offering clients a fast-track development tool aimed at launches within six months, and that OTC and functional products would lead growth. Whether that projection translated into shipments is not confirmed in the materials reviewed.

On the customer side, online retail clients have been a key order source, and a 2021 press report described a shift from turnkey orders to OEM as improving profitability.

In the domestic ODM market, large players such as Cosmax and Kolmar Korea compete, and Englewood Lab positions itself on a US-based development and manufacturing base. Peer sales-mix data is not included in the materials, so the comparison is qualitative only.

04

Earnings analysis

On the regular-report basis, annual sales rose from KRW 157.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 213 billion in 2023 (up 35.1% year on year), fell to KRW 179.7 billion in 2024 (down 15.7%), and recovered to KRW 205 billion in 2025 (up 14.1%). The largest year in 2022–2025 was 2023.

Operating profit rose from KRW 10.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 29.7 billion in 2023 (up 189.0%), fell to KRW 18.5 billion in 2024 (down 37.7%), and recovered to KRW 35.3 billion in 2025 (up 90.7%).

The operating margin moved from 6.5% in 2022 to 14.0% in 2023, 10.3% in 2024 and 17.2% in 2025, and in 2024 it stayed in double digits even as sales declined.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7.56 billion in 2022, KRW 21.4 billion in 2023 (up 182.6%), KRW 19.4 billion in 2024 (down 9.4%) and KRW 23.5 billion in 2025 (up 21.2%).

Net income including non-controlling interests is reported as KRW 7.56 billion, KRW 21.4 billion, KRW 19.4 billion and KRW 23.5 billion for the same years. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 154.2 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 58.5 billion.

The debt-to-equity ratio moved from 45.5% in 2022 to 51.1% in 2023, 38.8% in 2024 and 37.9% in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 10.1 billion in 2022, KRW 21.5 billion in 2023, KRW 12.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 21 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 started with sales of KRW 60 billion, operating profit of KRW 12.8 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 10.7 billion. Sales then fell to KRW 50.5 billion in Q4 2025 (down 15.8% from the prior quarter) and operating profit to KRW 8.61 billion (down 32.9%).

Sales were KRW 45.7 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 46.5 billion in Q2 2026 (up 1.8% quarter on quarter), and KRW 43.1 billion in Q3 2026 (down 7.2% quarter on quarter and down 28.1% year on year).

Operating profit in Q3 2026 was KRW 4.87 billion (down 62.1% year on year), and net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a net loss of KRW 1.35 billion. Over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026), net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 18.1 billion.

A provisional announcement dated November 6, 2026 reported Q3 sales of KRW 456.6 million... more precisely KRW 45.66 billion, operating profit of KRW 5.157 billion and a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 1.433 billion.

This report uses the regular-report figures, and the gap between the two sets should be reconciled when the quarterly report is finalized. The amount of any one-off items is not confirmed in the materials reviewed.

Bear factors

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 43.1 billion, down 28.1% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 4.87 billion, down 62.1%. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a net loss of KRW 1.35 billion.

Concentration in the United States

The US accounted for 95% of sales as of Q3 2025. Because sales depend so heavily on US client orders, a drop in those orders would move total sales directly, and geographic diversification is limited. Press coverage of the first nine months of 2025 reported that sales outside Korea and the US had roughly halved.

Product mix shift and margin pressure

The functional and OTC share of the product mix declined over one year. How much that shift affected the operating margin cannot be separated cleanly using the disclosed figures alone. The Q3 2026 operating margin is lower than in Q3 2025, so defending margins during a sales decline remains a challenge.

Overall view

Englewood Lab's sales rose from KRW 157.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 205 billion in 2025, and operating profit rose from KRW 10.3 billion to KRW 35.3 billion over the same period. The 17.2% operating margin in 2025 is the main indicator of the profit recovery.

However, Q3 2026 sales were KRW 43.1 billion, down 28.1% year on year, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a net loss of KRW 1.35 billion. Over the last four quarters that figure totals KRW 18.1 billion.

US sales at 95% as of Q3 2025 are both the foundation of the business and a client concentration risk. On financial capacity, the debt-to-equity ratio was 37.9% and operating cash flow was KRW 21 billion at the end of 2025.

Bullish and bearish factors carry comparable weight, so the next checks are the confirmed Q3 2026 figures in the quarterly report, provisional Q4 2026 results, and the 2026 operating margin and US order flow in the annual report.
