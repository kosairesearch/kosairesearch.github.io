02

Business structure

Englewood Lab is an OEM and ODM contract manufacturer for cosmetics, handling product planning, formulation development, production and launch. Production sites are in the United States and Korea, and the company's Korean plant is located in Chungbuk.

Its Q3 2026 category mix was basic cosmetics at 69.2%, functional cosmetics and OTC products at 21.5%, makeup at 5.9% and other at 3.3%. In the US, the company promotes a one-stop OGM offering and targets independent brands in North America.

Its RTG OTC service supports sunscreen products classified as OTC drugs in the US, covering FDA guideline compliance through launch. A press report described Englewood Lab as a US subsidiary of Cosmecca Korea. The stock has been listed on KOSDAQ since October 14, 2016.

Peers in the domestic ODM market include Cosmax and Kolmar Korea, and Englewood Lab differentiates itself through its US production base and North American independent-brand focus.

Earnings analysis

On a KRW-converted basis (the company reports in USD), revenue rose from KRW 157.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 213 billion in 2023, up 35.1%, fell to KRW 179.7 billion in 2024, down 15.7%, and reached KRW 205 billion in 2025, up 14.1%. 2023 was the largest revenue year.

Operating profit moved from KRW 10.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 29.7 billion in 2023 (up 189.0%), KRW 18.5 billion in 2024 (down 37.7%) and KRW 35.3 billion in 2025 (up 90.7%), with 2025 the largest year. The operating margin was 6.5% in 2022, 14.0% in 2023, 10.3% in 2024 and 17.2% in 2025, the highest of the period.

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 7.56 billion in 2022, KRW 21.4 billion in 2023 (up 182.6%), KRW 19.4 billion in 2024 (down 9.4%) and KRW 23.5 billion in 2025 (up 21.2%).

Net income including non-controlling interests is reported at KRW 7.56 billion, KRW 21.4 billion, KRW 19.4 billion and KRW 23.5 billion for the same years. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 45.5% in 2022 to 51.1% in 2023, then fell to 38.8% in 2024 and 37.9% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 10.1 billion, KRW 21.5 billion, KRW 12.7 billion and KRW 21 billion for 2022 through 2025, below operating profit in each of those years.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 60 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 50.5 billion in Q4 2025 (down 15.8% quarter on quarter), KRW 45.7 billion in Q1 2026 (down 9.7%), KRW 46.5 billion in Q2 2026 (up 1.8%) and KRW 43.1 billion in Q3 2026 (down 7.2%, down 28.1% year on year).

Operating profit went from KRW 12.8 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 8.61 billion, KRW 5.14 billion and KRW 7.82 billion across the next three quarters, and then to KRW 4.87 billion in Q3 2026 (down 62.1% year on year). The weakness in Q3 2026 has been attributed to the Korean subsidiary EWLK.

In Q2 2025, EWLK reported operating profit of KRW 8.2 billion, showing that the subsidiary carries a large share of group profit. Average exchange rates applied in Q3 2026 were KRW 1,423.38 per dollar, above KRW 1,385.28 a year earlier.

Industry analysis

Englewood Lab manufactures products on behalf of brand owners as a contract manufacturer. In the same industry, Cosmax and Kolmar Korea are regarded as large domestic ODM companies, while Englewood Lab's focus is on US production and North American clients.

At the MakeUp in NewYork 2026 trade show held in September 2026, the company highlighted a custom strategy for North American independent brands and local manufacturing capacity that spans small-batch to large-volume runs.

The skincare-makeup hybrid formulations shown at the event combine high-functionality basic care with makeup expressiveness. The growing influence of independent brands in North America is a factor to watch for the company's client base.

Its OTC sunscreen entry support demonstrates regulatory capability in North America. In an earlier first-quarter results briefing from the period of expanding COVID-19 vaccinations, the company said that a shift from turnkey orders to OEM orders improved profitability.

Demand for consumer goods reaches the company through client order volumes. With basic cosmetics at 69.2% of Q3 2026 revenue, exposure to changes in basic-product demand is high. Competitive differentiation rests on the US production base and the OTC sunscreen service.

US production base and OTC service

The company emphasizes US infrastructure that covers small-batch to large-volume production. Its RTG OTC sunscreen service drew interest from global buyers at Cosmoprof Las Vegas in July 2026 and at the New York show in September 2026. The Q3 2026 decline has been attributed to weakness at the subsidiary EWLK. In Q2 2025, EWLK's operating profit of KRW 8.2 billion showed how large a share of group profit the subsidiary carries. Swings at the subsidiary pass directly into consolidated earnings, which raises volatility.

Overall view

Englewood Lab posted the strongest profitability of the 2022–2025 period in 2025, with operating profit of KRW 35.3 billion and a 17.2% operating margin. However, Q3 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 4.87 billion and the company posted a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.35 billion.

The planned dividend of up to KRW 3 billion signals a shareholder-return direction, but the final amount is not yet set.
