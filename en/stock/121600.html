02

Business structure

Advanced Nano Products, founded in 2000, is an advanced materials maker that synthesizes metals into ultrafine nanopowders and commercializes them as solid targets or liquid forms.

Its main products include CNT (carbon nanotube) conductive agents for secondary batteries, hollow silica and TCO targets for displays, CMP slurry for semiconductors, and silver paste for solar cells.

Based on 2024 data, the segment mix was secondary-battery materials such as CNT at 45.8 percent, display materials at 8.4 percent, semiconductor materials at 11.7 percent, solar materials at 5.8 percent, and other items at 28.3 percent.

In the second quarter of 2025, the secondary-battery share rose to 58.5 percent and the display share was 6.8 percent, which increased dependence on battery conductive agents. Production has expanded to the United States, Poland, and China, and higher shipments from overseas plants have eased fixed-cost pressure.

Key customers include domestic battery cell makers and overseas customers, and semiconductor materials are supplied to SK hynix through Entegris.

Brokerage analysis notes that CNT conductive agents account for roughly 1 to 1.5 percent of cost, so price burden on customers is limited, and performance gains support adoption.

Utilization of the TCO target plant was 26 percent in 2023, 15 percent in 2024, 21 percent in 2025, and 14 percent in the first half of 2026, making it a weak link in the portfolio. The largest shareholder and related parties hold 21.63 percent as of February 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue was KRW 79.9 billion in 2022, KRW 83.2 billion in 2023 (up 4.1 percent), KRW 87.8 billion in 2024 (up 5.5 percent), and KRW 111.7 billion in 2025 (up 27.3 percent).

Revenue has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period. Operating profit moved in the opposite direction over the same span.

It fell from KRW 16.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.9 billion in 2023 (down 29.3 percent) and KRW 2.99 billion in 2024 (down 74.9 percent), making 2024 the smallest year. Operating profit recovered to KRW 4.68 billion in 2025, up 56.7 percent, but remains well below the 2022 level.

The operating margin was 21.1 percent in 2022, 14.3 percent in 2023, 3.4 percent in 2024, and 4.2 percent in 2025. The company attributed the 2024 decline to operating costs at its newly built secondary-battery plants in the United States and Poland, along with costs related to convertible and warrant bonds.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 19.2 billion in 2022 and KRW 16.8 billion in 2023 (down 12.5 percent), swung to a net loss of KRW 1.85 billion in 2024, and returned to a profit of KRW 1.63 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests is reported at the same level for each year, but it is a different measure from net income attributable to controlling shareholders.

On a quarterly basis, revenue moved from KRW 31.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 903 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 42.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 6.62 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 35.7 percent and 632.5 percent year on year.

Revenue rose for three consecutive quarters after the fourth-quarter 2025 low, reaching KRW 32.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 36.5 billion in the second, and KRW 42.4 billion in the third.

Operating profit has increased for four consecutive quarters since the fourth quarter of 2025, when it was KRW 1.64 billion.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 6.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then fell to KRW 6.05 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.94 billion in the third, a decline over two consecutive quarters.

Because operating profit rose while net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell over the same period, non-operating items and income tax lines are a point to check in the next periodic report.

05

Industry analysis

In battery materials, EV demand has slowed, but new applications for CNT conductive agents are driving growth. Domestic battery makers have weak EV-related demand, yet analysts note that rising ESS demand is increasing the role of conductive agents.

Mass production of dry electrodes at North American automakers was projected in an October 2025 brokerage article to become significant in the first half of 2026, representing a next-generation source of demand.

In semiconductor materials, an improving memory chip market supports demand for CMP slurry, which carries relatively higher margins. The company is expanding its end-customer base on the back of supply to SK hynix through Entegris.

In solar, Chinese low-priced material oversupply has weakened the industry, and press reports say solar-material sales fell from KRW 7.4 billion in 2023 to KRW 0.1 billion in the first half of 2026. On the industry cycle, iM Securities views 2024 as the earnings trough, with a recovery trend expected to continue.

On competitive position, the same brokerage expects the most distinct growth among domestic secondary-battery materials companies. With the secondary-battery share at 58.5 percent as of the second quarter of 2025, earnings are highly sensitive to changes in the battery market.

Outlook

In a November 5, 2026 report, iM Securities projected Advanced Nano Products revenue of KRW 210 billion and operating profit of KRW 31.8 billion for 2027, representing increases of 40 percent and 56 percent from the prior year.

The same report expects CNT conductive agent supply to expand simultaneously from 2027 to three domestic battery cell makers and overseas customers. In its November 5, 2026 report, iM Securities raised its target price from KRW 80,000 to KRW 115,000 and gave a buy rating.

These are the brokerage's own projections and not company disclosures. A October 2025 article projected that dry-electrode mass production at North American automakers would lift dry conductive agent orders, and when those orders begin to appear in revenue is a point to check in upcoming quarterly data.

TCO target utilization fell to 14 percent in the first half of 2026 from 21 percent in 2025. iM Securities cites TCO targets and silver paste as new growth axes, but solar-material sales have not yet shown up clearly in results.

The brokerage attributes reduced fixed-cost pressure to higher shipments from overseas plants, so whether utilization holds could determine the direction of the operating margin. On financing, the possibility of early redemption of convertible and warrant bonds remains, making cash flow management a task.

The next checkpoints are the annual operating margin in the 2026 annual report and quarterly results for the first quarter of 2027.

Bear factors

Weak utilization at the solar TCO plant

Utilization of the TCO target plant was 26 percent in 2023, 15 percent in 2024, 21 percent in 2025, and 14 percent in the first half of 2026, all at low levels. Solar-material sales fell from KRW 7.4 billion in 2023 to KRW 0.1 billion in the first half of 2026.

Fixed costs continue to weigh on the plant, and Chinese low-priced material oversupply is cited as the reason for the industry downturn.

Early-redemption pressure from bonds and shrinking cash

Press reports say put options on the remaining KRW 95.5 billion of convertible and warrant bonds became exercisable from March 21, 2026. Cash and cash equivalents, including short-term financial instruments, were KRW 159 billion at the end of the first half of 2026, down 30.1 percent from a year earlier.

Cash had been KRW 268.2 billion in 2023, a reduction of more than 40 percent. Analysts say conversion prices well above the share price increase the incentive to request early redemption.

Earnings volatility and a lower operating margin

The operating margin fell from 21.1 percent in 2022 to 3.4 percent in 2024 and was only 4.2 percent in 2025. In 2024, net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 1.85 billion. It peaked at KRW 6.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then declined for two consecutive quarters. Because quarterly profit direction has changed, sustainability needs to be confirmed over additional quarters.

Risk factors

Demand and adoption-timing risk

Domestic battery makers' EV-related demand is weak, while CNT conductive agent growth depends on the adoption schedule of dry electrodes at North American automakers. If mass production slips, it may take longer for order expectations to flow through to results.

With the secondary-battery share at 58.5 percent as of the second quarter of 2025, the impact of battery market changes is large.

Financial and convertible bond risk

About KRW 95.5 billion of convertible and warrant bonds remain subject to put-option pressure. Cash and cash equivalents were KRW 159 billion at the end of the first half of 2026, down 30.1 percent from a year earlier.

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 208 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio of 84.1 percent is higher than 7.5 percent in 2022. Conversions would increase the share count, so changes in the capital structure also matter.

Overseas plant operations and external-factor risk

Operating costs at the new secondary-battery plants in the United States and Poland weighed on 2024 results. If overseas plant utilization falls, fixed costs could again weigh on profit.

The company's higher overseas shipments in the third quarter of 2026 eased fixed-cost pressure, but whether utilization holds needs to be checked quarter by quarter. Solar materials remain exposed to external pressure from low-priced oversupply out of China.

Overall view

Advanced Nano Products is showing a profit recovery since 2025, centered on CNT conductive agents and CMP slurry for semiconductors. Revenue in 2025 was KRW 111.7 billion and operating profit was KRW 4.68 billion, and third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 6.62 billion rose 632.5 percent year on year.

Operating profit, however, fell from KRW 16.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.99 billion in 2024 and is still recovering, so it has not returned to the 2022 level. TCO plant utilization of 14 percent and solar-material sales of about KRW 0.1 billion are weak points in the portfolio.

Roughly KRW 95.5 billion of remaining convertible and warrant bonds and the decline in cash remain financial challenges. iM Securities estimates 2027 operating profit of KRW 31.8 billion and sees the recovery continuing, but that is one brokerage's view.

Strengths and weaknesses should be weighed equally, and the next checkpoints are the annual operating margin in the 2026 annual report and quarterly results for the first quarter of 2027.
