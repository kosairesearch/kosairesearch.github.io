Report overview

Losses narrowing, but funding and business pivot remain the test

Mgen Solutions has reported operating losses for five consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, yet its KRW 2.67 billion operating loss in that quarter was the smallest of the last five. The next tests are the KRW 10 billion convertible bond funding announced on November 17, 2026 and whether its new businesses in recycled fuel and data centers progress.

02

Business structure

Mgen Solutions has been listed on KOSDAQ since June 1997, and its business now centers on ICT (information and communication technology). According to a Fieldnews report, ICT accounted for 84% of revenue in the first half of 2026.

The ICT unit builds and maintains municipal broadband self-owned networks and supplies intelligent AI video analytics solutions.

The same report said optical network and AI solution revenue fell from KRW 24.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 17.3 billion in 2025, and to KRW 6.0 billion in the first half of 2026, down 39% year on year.

The former core printer and photo-processing business was halted in September 2025 after its China production unit was sold in August 2024, and the company cited that discontinued operation as the main reason for the 2025 revenue decline.

It acquired fire-safety firm Hyundai Infracore in 2023, but the same report said its electrical fire-safety unit posted an operating loss of KRW 0.9 billion in the first half of 2026.

New projects include recycled fuel equipment based on a September 2026 MOU with circular-resource technology firm Jigentech, with the company adding its AlphaShot AI fire response system to equipment safety management.

A hydrogen business purpose added in March 2026 was removed in an October 13, 2026 charter amendment, which added 22 new business purposes including a data center.

According to the company results commentary, demand for AI video control is expanding into projects for local governments and public institutions, so procurement timing shapes quarterly revenue.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 109.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 90.5 billion in 2023 (down 17.5% year on year), KRW 42.7 billion in 2024 (down 52.9%) and KRW 28.6 billion in 2025 (down 33.0%). 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit of KRW 2.91 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 6.31 billion in 2023, then an operating loss of KRW 3.98 billion in 2024 (a narrower loss), and KRW 10.3 billion in 2025 (a wider loss). The operating margin moved from 2.7% in 2022 to -7.0% in 2023, -9.3% in 2024 and -35.9% in 2025.

The company attributed its 2025 net loss of KRW 16.3 billion to lower sales from the discontinued printer business, a larger operating loss and higher non-operating costs such as valuation losses on convertible bonds.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 435 million in 2022, a loss of KRW 21.3 billion in 2023, a profit of KRW 3.09 billion in 2024 (a swing to profit) and a loss of KRW 16.3 billion in 2025 (a swing back to loss).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 2.86 billion in 2024, a year with an operating loss of KRW 3.98 billion, which suggests non-operating items weighed heavily that year.

Operating cash flow was KRW 1.25 billion positive in 2022 and negative in each of 2023 (KRW -2.07 billion), 2024 (KRW -6.13 billion) and 2025 (KRW -2.78 billion).

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 33.7 billion, equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 33.8 billion and total liabilities were KRW 26.2 billion, a debt ratio of 77.7%.

Quarterly revenue ran KRW 3.08 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 5.58 billion in the fourth quarter (up 81.4% from the prior quarter), KRW 4.02 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 4.95 billion in the second quarter and KRW 3.71 billion in the third quarter.

Quarterly operating losses were KRW 3.53 billion, KRW 3.19 billion, KRW 3.79 billion, KRW 2.95 billion and KRW 2.67 billion over the same five quarters.

Over the same period, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 4.97 billion in the third quarter of 2026, wider than the prior quarter, so operating and net trends moved in different directions. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 16.8 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for Mgen Solutions core products comes from broadband self-owned networks and video control systems ordered by local governments and public institutions.

As of the company results commentary dated December 2025, AI demand was expanding into crime prevention and security markets, lifting demand for intelligent video control systems. This demand is tied to budget execution and procurement timing, so quarterly revenue moves sharply.

Quarterly revenue in 2026 ran KRW 4.02 billion, KRW 4.95 billion and KRW 3.71 billion across the first three quarters. Annual revenue of KRW 28.6 billion in 2025 is small, so the presence or absence of a single large project has a visible effect on quarterly results.

In the new-business area, a company official said carbon neutrality and the shift to a circular economy have raised interest in recycled feedstock and alternative energy markets.

Recycled fuel equipment handles flammable materials and gases, so safety capability matters, and the company plans to supply that through its own AI safety technology. The hydrogen business was added in March 2026 and removed in October 2026, a sign of how unsettled the portfolio has been.

Competitive dynamics in the ICT core depend on how public buyers select vendors. Recycled fuel and data center projects require both technical validation and capital, which raises the entry barrier.

06

Outlook

On November 17, 2026 the company decided to issue KRW 10 billion in convertible bonds to a renewable-energy private equity investor, with payment due on November 25, 2026. The company said the proceeds will be used as operating funds to improve its capital structure.

According to the company, the investor manages roughly KRW 500 billion in assets as of July 2026. The recycled fuel project follows a September 2026 MOU with Jigentech under which Mgen would manufacture and supply equipment and co-develop technology.

In March 2026 a company official said partnerships and project awards would be turned into actual revenue, though no timing was given.

The data center concept would use a former mine site in Jangseong County, Jeollanam-do, with a reported total project cost of KRW 3.2 trillion and a first phase estimated at KRW 2.4 trillion. Investment decisions and a financing plan for it remain at the concept stage.

An external audit opinion of unqualified was disclosed on March 20, 2026, and the company framed this as removing financial uncertainty.

On the share side, 3,843,197 new common shares from a third-party allocation rights offering were listed on November 2, 2026, and they are subject to a lock-up through November 1, 2027. After the listing, common shares outstanding total 30,987,431.

08

Bull factors

Narrowing operating losses

The third-quarter 2026 operating loss of KRW 2.67 billion was the smallest of the last five quarters. The loss narrowed in both the second and third quarters of 2026. Third-quarter revenue rose 20.6% year on year to KRW 3.71 billion.

Recycled fuel and AI safety pipeline

Under a MOU with Jigentech, the company plans to manufacture and supply equipment that converts waste plastics, tires and oil into recycled fuel. It intends to add its AI video analytics and fire-response technology to equipment safety management. A company official said adding circular-resource projects to its ICT capability is meant to diversify revenue.

Efforts to ease financial risk

The company disclosed an unqualified external audit opinion on March 20, 2026. The KRW 10 billion convertible bond announced on November 17, 2026 is to be used as operating funds to improve the capital structure. The company cited the audit opinion as evidence that financial uncertainty had been reduced.

Bear factors

Five straight quarters of operating losses and thin margins

The 2025 operating margin was -35.9%, with the operating loss widening to KRW 10.3 billion. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 4.97 billion in the third quarter of 2026 from the prior quarter.

Weak cash generation and thinning capital

Operating cash flow was negative in each year from 2023 to 2025. According to Fieldnews, cash stood at KRW 4.4 billion and accumulated deficit at KRW 29.5 billion at the end of June 2026. The same report said the gap between consolidated equity and paid-in capital had narrowed to KRW 1.9 billion.

Frequent business-purpose changes and dilution

The hydrogen business purpose was added in March 2026 and removed in October 2026. According to press reports, the first-phase data center cost of KRW 2.4 trillion is 545 times the cash on hand. Shares issuable on conversion of the 24th convertible bond amounted to 8.29% of total shares.

10

Risk factors

Funding and dilution

The KRW 10 billion convertible bond is scheduled for payment on November 25, 2026. The 3,843,197 rights offering shares listed on November 2, 2026 are locked up until November 1, 2027. Convertible bonds and the rights offering together increase the share count and dilute per-share measures.

Delayed revenue from partnerships

The September 2026 MOU with Jigentech covers manufacturing and supply of recycled fuel equipment along with joint technology development. The company intends to turn the partnership into actual revenue, but no timing has been set for project awards.

If the first contract is delayed, the revenue contribution from the new business will slip and may not offset the quarterly swings in core procurement. Because core revenue moves with local government and public institution procurement timing, a gap in monetizing the new business can reach quarterly earnings directly.

Core revenue decline and goodwill impairment

Optical network and AI solution revenue fell from KRW 24.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 17.3 billion in 2025. Goodwill from the Unicomnet acquisition was impaired in the 2023 and 2024 closings, leaving KRW 1.2 billion at the end of June 2026. The discontinued printer business reduced 2025 revenue.

Overall view

Mgen Solutions reported falling revenue from 2022 onward and operating losses from 2023. In the third quarter of 2026 revenue rose year on year and the operating loss was the smallest of the last five quarters, which shows losses narrowing.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened in the same quarter, so operating and net trends diverge. On funding, operating cash flow was negative from 2023 through 2025, and the November 2026 convertible bond and rights offering shares affect both cash and share count.

On the business side, the key questions are whether recycled fuel and AI safety equipment reach revenue and whether the data center concept becomes an investment decision. The record of adding and then removing a hydrogen business purpose raises questions about strategic consistency.

The next checkpoints are the November 25, 2026 bond payment, the first contract disclosure for the Jigentech-linked equipment and the annual figures in the 2026 annual report.
