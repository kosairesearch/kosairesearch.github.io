Report overview

Exem: Q4-Weighted Revenue Drives Uneven Quarterly Profits

Exem's software revenue is growing, but because sales concentrate in the fourth quarter, its quarterly profit keeps swinging between profit and loss.

  1. 1

    Cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 34.8 billion, up 17% year on year, as announced on November 13, 2026.

  2. 2

    In the third quarter of 2026 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 611 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.05 billion, swinging from profit a year earlier.

  3. 3

    The operating margin fell from 22.7% in 2022 to 7.6% in 2025, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

  4. 4

    The fourth quarter of 2025 delivered an operating profit of KRW 5.7 billion, and the company expects full-year 2026 operating profit to improve on the same seasonal pattern.

  5. 5

    The debt-to-equity ratio at the end of 2025 was 12.8%, and of total equity of KRW 141 billion, KRW 122.7 billion belongs to controlling shareholders.

02

Business structure

Exem develops AI and IT infrastructure performance management software and supplies it to financial, public-sector and manufacturing customers.

Its core lines are the database performance management (DBPM) and application performance management (APM) segments, centered on MaxGauge and InterMax, while the AI-based observability solution exemONE is driving growth.

License revenue from exemONE roughly doubled in the first three quarters of 2026, and the company says it has signed more than 80 domestic and overseas customers across finance, manufacturing and the public sector.

The AI and big-data segment comprises EBIGs for big-data operations, Woodpecker for AI model development, and eXemble for generative LLM and AI agent platforms, and EBIGs license revenue more than doubled over the same period.

Revenue splits into software (licenses and maintenance) and services, with software up 20% and services up 16% in the first three quarters of 2026. Overseas subsidiaries in Japan, China and the United States grew revenue 46%, 10% and 99% respectively in the first half of 2026.

The Japanese unit supplied exemONE to large banks and energy companies, and the US unit added MaxGauge and InterMax at a Samsung Electronics semiconductor plant in Taylor, Texas.

In January 2026 the company sold its stake in a former major subsidiary, so year-on-year comparisons for 2026 exclude that business's results from the 2025 base.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 55.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 53.9 billion in 2023, a 2.3% decline, then peaked at KRW 61.2 billion in 2024 after a 13.6% increase. It dropped 22.0% to KRW 47.8 billion in 2025, the smallest year in the 2022 to 2025 window.

Operating profit fell from KRW 12.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.91 billion in 2023, a 60.8% decline, recovered to KRW 8.83 billion in 2024 (up 79.8%), and then dropped 58.7% to KRW 3.65 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the 2024 recovery to a wider set of product license contracts with financial firms and public agencies. The operating margin moved from 22.7% in 2022 to 9.1% in 2023, 14.4% in 2024 and 7.6% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 10.4 billion in 2023 and then declined for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025, reaching KRW 6.61 billion in 2025. Total net income including non-controlling interests, a different measure, was KRW 8.69 billion in 2025.

Quarterly results were concentrated at year-end: in the fourth quarter of 2025 revenue reached KRW 10 billion and operating profit KRW 5.7 billion, after a 26.1% quarter-on-quarter revenue decline from the third quarter.

In 2026 the first quarter posted an operating loss of KRW 2.23 billion, the second quarter an operating profit of KRW 2.59 billion, and the third quarter an operating loss of KRW 606 million.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 11 billion was down 18.8% year on year, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.05 billion compares with a net profit of KRW 1.06 billion a year earlier.

The company cited higher R&D spending and AI infrastructure costs such as GPU servers as the reasons for the third-quarter operating loss.

Cumulative net income of KRW 11.27 billion through the third quarter was supported by gains on the disposal of a former subsidiary's stake and of listed shares, according to the company.

05

Industry analysis

The IT performance management and observability market in which Exem operates is seeing demand grow as hybrid cloud operations expand.

In March 2026 the company pointed to a government plan to move public-sector IT systems fully to the cloud by 2030 and said exemONE's public-sector revenue growth is assured, though this is the company's own view and the timing and size of public orders vary by project.

In AI transformation work, the company stresses that it develops and supplies both performance monitoring for AI infrastructure and AX solutions in-house. It also said it planned to develop GPU and NPU monitoring features in the first half of 2026.

InterMax, specialized in end-to-end monitoring, has many financial-sector customers, so demand from banks is an important variable for the revenue mix. Overseas, the Japanese, Chinese and US units are expanding by widening their local customer bases.

Because public and financial IT budgets tend to be executed in the second half and the fourth quarter, quarterly profit volatility is likely to remain high.

06

Outlook

The company expects steady revenue growth in the fourth quarter of 2026 across its IT infrastructure performance management and AI and big-data platform segments.

Contracts for XAIOps have been signed with a bank, a major public agency and a core government ministry, and the company said one more large public agency and one more government ministry plan to adopt it. License revenue from InterMax is expected to concentrate in the second half of 2026.

A large manufacturer using Woodpecker signed an agreement to add eXemble and the OntoMax ontology solution, and OntoMax will also be supplied to a major government ministry.

In AX work, the company is carrying out an AI transformation project for a shipping company and plans to bid on several AX projects from large public institutions in the second half. The company expects full-year operating profit to improve because revenue is concentrated in the fourth quarter every year.

The key variable for the outlook is whether R&D and AI infrastructure costs, which rose in the third quarter of 2026, continue after the fourth quarter. Public AX projects can be signed at different times from when revenue is recognized, so the speed at which signed contracts turn into revenue should be checked.

Whether the announced additional XAIOps adoptions and the InterMax license concentration show up in reported revenue is the next checkpoint.

08

Bull factors

Q4-concentrated revenue and the full-year improvement outlook

Fourth-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 10 billion and operating profit KRW 5.7 billion, showing profit concentrated at year-end. The company cites this seasonality in expecting full-year operating profit to improve.

It also expects steady growth in the fourth quarter of 2026, and whether that translates into full-year operating profit is the point to check.

Fast license growth in exemONE and EBIGs

Cumulative license revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 roughly doubled for exemONE and more than doubled for EBIGs. The company says exemONE has more than 80 customers across finance, manufacturing and the public sector. New products Woodpecker and eXemble have also started generating revenue, which widens the product portfolio.

Overseas subsidiaries and public AX supply contracts

In the first half of 2026, revenue rose 46% in Japan, 99% in the United States and 10% in China. The Chinese unit added two local manufacturers as new customers. The company said XAIOps supply contracts have been signed with a bank, public institutions and a government ministry.

09

Bear factors

Repeated swings into quarterly losses

An operating loss was recorded in the first and third quarters of 2026, and the third-quarter net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.05 billion compares with a net profit of KRW 1.06 billion a year earlier. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 11 billion fell 18.8% year on year.

Because of the fourth-quarter concentration, how much of the quarterly losses the year-end profit can absorb is the key question on the bearish side.

Lower profitability and two years of declining profit

The operating margin fell from 22.7% in 2022 to 7.6% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025. Operating profit in 2025 was KRW 3.65 billion, down 58.7% from KRW 8.83 billion in 2024, and revenue fell 22.0% to KRW 47.8 billion.

Rising costs and reliance on disposal gains

The company cited higher R&D spending and GPU server and other AI infrastructure costs as the reasons for the third-quarter operating loss. Gains on the disposal of a former subsidiary's stake and of listed shares contributed significantly to cumulative net income growth.

Because costs rose at the operating level first, the reliance on one-off disposal gains for net income is a bearish factor.

10

Risk factors

Seasonality and quarterly volatility

Because revenue is concentrated in the fourth quarter, any delay in recognizing year-end contracts could undermine the full-year profit outlook. The gap between fourth-quarter 2025 operating profit of KRW 5.7 billion and first-quarter 2026 operating loss of KRW 2.23 billion shows how wide quarterly swings can be. Judging trends from single quarters can be misleading.

AI investment costs and the lag before monetization

R&D and AI infrastructure investments such as GPU servers are booked as costs first, while sales of newer products have only just begun. If payback is slower, operating losses could continue. The company expects AI product revenue to expand, but its scale remains to be confirmed.

Dependence on public and financial contracts and timing risk

Public-sector and financial contracts are an important revenue pillar, so results can move significantly with budget execution and contract timing. The company's outlook assumes the government's cloud transition plan and the outcome of AX bids. If contracts are delayed, the concentration of revenue in the second half and fourth quarter could weaken.

  2. By end of December 2026

  3. By end of March 2027

    This is the deadline for the 2026 annual report. Full-year 2026 operating profit and operating margin will be fixed in the disclosed figures and can be compared with the 7.6% operating margin of 2025. This is where the effect of fourth-quarter results on the annual trend will be confirmed.

  4. By May 15, 2027

    This is the deadline for the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report. It is the point to check whether a swing into operating losses, as in the first quarter of 2026, repeats and whether the cost structure has been adjusted since the third quarter.

12

Overall view

Exem's cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 rose 17%, showing continued top-line growth. However, the operating margin fell from 22.7% in 2022 to 7.6% in 2025, and the third quarter of 2026 produced an operating loss and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders.

Because revenue is concentrated in the fourth quarter, year-end results keep determining the full-year profit, as the KRW 5.7 billion operating profit in the fourth quarter of 2025 showed.

The company expects steady fourth-quarter growth and better full-year operating profit, but how quickly higher R&D and AI infrastructure costs convert into revenue is the key question. Bullish factors include strong license growth in exemONE and EBIGs, expanding overseas subsidiaries and the XAIOps supply contracts.

Bearish factors include quarterly losses, two consecutive years of lower net income attributable to controlling shareholders, and net income that relies on disposal gains. Valuation should be compared factually against the size of net assets and the earnings level of the last four quarters. The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and the first-quarter 2027 results.
