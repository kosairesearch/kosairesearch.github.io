Report overview

Standalone software grows, consolidated profit slips

Standalone software operating profit rose 123% in the first nine months of 2026, but consolidated operating profit fell 27.0% and the third quarter posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders.

  1. 1

    Standalone operating profit for the first nine months of 2026 reached KRW 4.2 billion, up 123% year on year, and the standalone operating margin rose from 10.1% to 19.7% (company announcement, November 17, 2026).

  2. 2

    Consolidated revenue for the same period rose 3.2% to KRW 246.7 billion, but consolidated operating profit fell 27.0% to KRW 7.6 billion, with press reports attributing the drop to losses in the groupware unit and lower profit at the API and auto parts units.

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Polaris Office was founded in 1997 as a document software developer and listed on KOSDAQ in 2005, changing its name in 2021. It added subsidiary Sewon in June 2023 and Polaris AI Pharma in April 2024, extending the portfolio into automotive HVAC parts, chemicals, and API and CDMO businesses.

According to a September 25, 2026 report citing a Korean IR research body, at the end of the first half of 2026 automotive HVAC parts accounted for 26.1% of consolidated revenue, synthetic and chemical businesses 25.9%, API and CDMO 17.7%, the software platform 16.9%, and the F&C and AI convergence business 13.4%.

Businesses outside software therefore generate more than 80% of consolidated revenue. The software segment covers office apps and enterprise products such as SDKs, document converters, and web office.

The mobile service OfficeDoumi, offered through a mobile carrier's PASS app, is being grown into a recurring revenue source, and the company said related revenue in the first half of 2026 rose 295% year on year. Subscription revenue from existing office services rose 10% in the first nine months of 2026.

In AI, the company launched Polaris Office AI in September 2023 and the NOVA AI agent in August 2024, and an on-device AI product for Windows received GS certification in December 2025.

The group is pursuing a vertical AI strategy that combines industry-specific data with AI, working with groupware affiliate Handysoft in document, approval workflow, and security areas. Groupware posted an operating loss of KRW 2.6 billion in the first nine months of 2026, offsetting part of the software growth.

04

Earnings analysis

In the audited annual results, revenue rose from KRW 24.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 107.9 billion in 2023 (up 346.1%), KRW 276.3 billion in 2024 (up 156.1%), and KRW 324.2 billion in 2025 (up 17.4%), growing three years in a row from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

In a May 2024 report, the company attributed the top-line growth partly to subsidiary Sewon being consolidated for a full period from the third quarter of 2023.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 1.66 billion in 2022 to KRW 6.25 billion in 2023 (up 277.1%), fell to KRW 4.8 billion in 2024 (down 23.1%), and recovered to KRW 9.12 billion in 2025 (up 89.9%). The operating margin declined from 6.9% in 2022 to 5.8%, 1.7%, and 2.8% in 2025, narrowing as revenue expanded.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 2.13 billion in 2023 and then fell two years in a row, to KRW 707 million in 2024 (down 66.8%) and KRW 486 million in 2025 (down 31.2%).

Total net income was KRW 1.28 billion in 2022, KRW 2.24 billion in 2023, KRW 1.86 billion in 2024, and KRW 1.35 billion in 2025; in 2022 the controlling-shareholder figure of KRW 1.31 billion exceeded total net income, reflecting the non-controlling share.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 502.9 billion, of which KRW 89.3 billion belonged to controlling shareholders.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 84.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 85.2 billion in the fourth quarter (up 0.5%), KRW 75.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 11.4%), KRW 84.1 billion in the second quarter (up 11.4%), and KRW 87 billion in the third quarter (up 3.5%, and up 2.7% year on year).

Operating results swung from a loss of KRW 1.24 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to a profit of KRW 2.39 billion in the first quarter of 2026, rose to KRW 3.59 billion in the second quarter (up 50.5%), and fell to KRW 1.58 billion in the third quarter (down 56.1%, and down 66.9% year on year).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 1.27 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and the sum for the latest four quarters was KRW 451 million.

Groupware recorded an operating loss of KRW 2.6 billion for the first nine months of 2026, while the API unit's operating profit fell from KRW 3.4 billion to KRW 1.3 billion and the auto parts unit's from KRW 1.9 billion to about KRW 0.3 billion (per the quarterly report).

05

Industry analysis

Polaris Office has two main demand sources. The first is enterprise and public-sector AI transformation spending, which the company targets with AI across document creation, approval workflows, security, and data analysis. The second is the user base of mobile carriers.

Security demand is also rising: the company says generative AI use is raising the risk that customer personal data and unreleased financial materials flow into outside AI services, and in late October 2026 it launched a generative AI security product built on a data loss prevention solution from the global security firm Netwrix.

On the technology side, on-device AI is gaining attention. The company launched an on-device AI office product for Mac using the Upstage Solar model in May 2024 and said it is developing a document AI agent based on a lightweight language model optimized for Intel CPUs.

Cloud dependence is also material: in an October 2024 report the company said more than 60% of user revenue came from cloud products. On the ecosystem side, it has signed memoranda with KT Cloud, Saltlux, Naver Cloud, LG AI Research and Upstage to pursue technical cooperation and market expansion.

The company is classified as IT and software, but its revenue exposure spans the automotive parts, chemicals and pharmaceutical industries. Direct comparison with other listed companies in the same sector is limited because their business structures differ widely.

06

Outlook

At its third-quarter results announcement on November 17, 2026, the company said it would continue growing its core business, citing expanded enterprise product sales and a stronger earnings base from new services.

On November 12, 2026, it announced that OfficeDoumi would add storage and management functions through a partnership with a cloud service provider, and said it expects the link to help attract new subscribers. It is also reviewing certificate-based document sharing in cloud environments.

Under its vertical AI strategy, it is pursuing document creation, approval workflow, security and data analysis together with Handysoft.

In healthcare, on March 13, 2026 it signed a business associate agreement with OpenAI to build a medical data processing environment meeting HIPAA standards, and plans to grow AskDoc for Pharma, co-developed with Polaris AI Pharma, into a pharmaceutical AI solution.

In security, it plans to prioritize B2B markets such as financial institutions and edtech with Polaris WebOffice, which applies post-quantum cryptography. Variables remain on the consolidated side.

Whether the KRW 2.6 billion cumulative operating loss in groupware narrows, and whether profit at the API and auto parts units recovers, will drive consolidated results. The next checkpoint is the consolidated operating margin and groupware segment results in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Standalone core profitability recovers

Standalone operating profit for the first nine months of 2026 was KRW 4.2 billion, up 123% year on year, and the standalone operating margin rose from 10.1% to 19.7%. The company said cumulative standalone operating profit had already passed the full-year 2025 standalone figure. Enterprise SDK, converter and web office sales, along with carrier-linked services, drove the increase.

Recurring revenue from carrier partnerships expands

The company said revenue related to OfficeDoumi rose 295% year on year in the first half of 2026. Subscription revenue from existing office services rose 10% in the first nine months of 2026. The cloud link announced in November 2026 adds storage and management features that could extend usage among existing users.

Operating cash flow expands and healthcare AI entry

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 1.58 billion in 2022 to KRW 25 billion in 2025. In March 2026 the company signed a business associate agreement with OpenAI to establish a U.S. medical data processing environment, and plans to expand AskDoc for Pharma, a pharma-focused AI solution developed with an affiliate.

09

Bear factors

Weak consolidated earnings

Consolidated operating profit for the first nine months of 2026 fell 27.0% to KRW 7.6 billion, and consolidated net income fell 15.8% to KRW 10.5 billion.

The third quarter swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.27 billion, and third-quarter consolidated operating profit fell 66.9% year on year. Gains on a standalone basis have not fully carried through to consolidated profit.

Weak profit at subsidiary units

The groupware unit posted an operating loss of KRW 2.6 billion for the first nine months of 2026. Operating profit at the API unit fell from KRW 3.4 billion to KRW 1.3 billion, and at the auto parts unit from KRW 1.9 billion to about KRW 0.3 billion (per the quarterly report). Lower earnings at subsidiaries that account for most revenue have widened swings in consolidated results.

Long-term margin decline and rising debt

The operating margin fell from 6.9% in 2022 to 2.8% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders has declined two years in a row since the 2023 peak. The debt ratio rose from 17.5% in 2022 to 28.2% in 2025, and controlling shareholders' equity stood at KRW 89.3 billion at the end of 2025.

10

Risk factors

Subsidiary earnings volatility

Most consolidated revenue comes from subsidiaries in automotive HVAC parts, chemicals and API and CDMO. If profits in these businesses swing, improvement in the core software business may not show up in consolidated results.

Through the first nine months of 2026, profit at the API and auto parts units fell sharply, and groupware remained loss-making.

Ownership structure and non-controlling interests

Of KRW 502.9 billion in total equity at the end of 2025, KRW 89.3 billion belongs to controlling shareholders. In 2022 net income attributable to controlling shareholders exceeded total net income, and since then the gap between the two reflects the non-controlling share. Shareholders' earnings should be read on the controlling-shareholder basis, which makes this gap important.

Platform dependence and AI competition

OfficeDoumi depends on external platform channels such as the mobile carrier's PASS app. In 2024 the company said cloud products accounted for more than 60% of user revenue. In the document AI market, feature competition and platform bargaining power could affect profitability.

12

Overall view

Despite its name, Polaris Office generates most of its consolidated revenue from automotive parts, chemicals and pharmaceutical subsidiaries.

On a standalone basis for the first nine months of 2026, software core operating profit reached KRW 4.2 billion with a 19.7% margin, improved from the prior year, and carrier-linked recurring revenue from OfficeDoumi and B2B sales both grew.

Consolidated operating profit for the same period fell 27.0% to KRW 7.6 billion, and the third quarter turned to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.27 billion.

On an annual basis, net income attributable to controlling shareholders has declined two years in a row since the 2023 peak, and the operating margin fell from 6.9% in 2022 to 2.8% in 2025. The debt ratio rose to 28.2% in 2025, while operating cash flow expanded to KRW 25 billion.

Strengths and weaknesses coexist with comparable weight. The next checkpoints are how groupware, API and auto parts results are settled in the 2026 annual report, due by March 31, 2027, and whether the standalone core improvement carries through to consolidated profit.
