AI packaging demand lifts quarterly profit; annual losses remain

02

Business structure

DUKSAN HI METAL CO. was founded in 1999 as a specialist in bonding materials for semiconductor packaging. Its core products are solder balls and solder paste, and it is reported to be the exclusive supplier of ultra-fine micro solder balls below 130 micrometers.

Micro solder balls are used in high-performance chip packages such as FC-BGA substrates, so demand moves with back-end investment by chipmakers.

Its first-quarter 2026 results commentary said solder ball sales rose on demand for high-performance chips used in AI, data centers and HBM, while defense and navigation solutions and high-pressure gas containers contributed more to sales.

Those businesses come from two subsidiaries: Duksan Navcours, in which it took a stake in 2021, and a high-pressure gas container maker acquired in 2023 that entered the hydrogen vessel market. In 2019 it set up a local subsidiary in Myanmar to strengthen its tin smelting capacity.

Revenue on the disclosed basis was KRW 236.4 billion in 2025, and preliminary nine-month 2026 revenue of KRW 259.0 billion, announced November 5, 2026, already exceeded that full-year figure.

One brokerage estimated the company's share of the micro solder ball market at about 70% in an April 2026 report; this is a broker estimate, not a company disclosure. Because shipments go to domestic and overseas substrate and chipmakers, customers' investment schedules feed directly into results.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 171.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 144.5 billion in 2023, a 15.8% decline that marked the low point, before rising to KRW 235.9 billion in 2024 (up 63.3%) and KRW 236.4 billion in 2025 (up 0.2%), two straight years of growth from 2024 through 2025.

The largest year of 2022 through 2025 was 2025 and the smallest was 2023, and the 0.2% gain in 2025 means most of the sales growth came in 2024.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 2.74 billion in 2022 and an operating loss of KRW 11 billion in 2023, a year of continued losses that widened, to an operating profit of KRW 18.6 billion in 2024, a swing to profit.

The operating margin went from -1.6% in 2022 and -7.6% in 2023 to 7.9% in 2024, then fell to 1.2% in 2025 as operating profit dropped 84.9% to KRW 2.81 billion. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 20.4 billion in 2024, up 512.9%, before turning into a loss of KRW 115.7 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 31.3 billion in 2024, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder number, and a net loss of KRW 115.7 billion in 2025.

The fourth-quarter 2025 loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 120.8 billion was larger than the full-year 2025 net loss, so the annual swing into loss was concentrated in that quarter.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 61.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 73.4 billion in the fourth quarter (up 19.4% quarter on quarter), dipped to KRW 68.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 6.4%), and reached KRW 90.8 billion in the second quarter (up 32.1%).

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 668 million in the first quarter of 2026, a swing from profit, to KRW 10.1 billion in the second quarter, a swing back to profit, and KRW 10.2 billion in the third quarter (up 1.5% quarter on quarter).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.92 billion in the third quarter, down 44.0% from the prior quarter, so the operating gain did not carry through to the bottom line at the same rate.

Preliminary nine-month 2026 operating profit of KRW 19.6 billion, announced on November 5, 2026, is far above full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 2.81 billion, and DT Elec reported that the third-quarter sales and operating profit were record quarterly results.

05

Industry analysis

The back-end materials market has entered a growth phase as AI server chips lift demand for flip-chip ball grid array (FC-BGA) substrates, a view many analysts share. Brokerage reports from the first half of 2026 described continued supply shortages as global FC-BGA substrate makers expand capacity.

Meritz Securities said in a May 2026 report that the FC-BGA market had entered an upcycle and that shipments of micro solder balls to Taiwanese FC-BGA makers had increased sharply. The same report estimated that the company's share within a Taiwanese customer supplying mainly GPU and ASIC products roughly doubled.

Hana Securities said in an April 2026 report that rising AI server demand had put the company into a structural growth phase, and that micro solder ball content per package was climbing fast as AI chip performance rose.

Surging silver and tin prices were cited as a factor that pressured first-quarter 2026 profitability. Because the share estimates reported are high, whether the company keeps that share will matter alongside the upcycle.

06

Outlook

On capacity, Hana Securities said in an April 2026 report that the company had raised solder ball capacity by 30% from April 2026 and added 10% to micro solder ball capacity.

Meritz Securities said in a May 2026 report that higher selling prices, added capacity, better utilization and wider raw material price pass-through clauses would bring a full profit recovery.

Hyundai Motor Securities reported that price increases implemented in March 2026 were reflected in selling prices from then on, improving results from the second quarter. Confirmed company figures so far are the third-quarter 2026 preliminary results and nine-month totals.

The company's first-quarter 2026 results commentary said contributions from high-pressure gas containers and defense and navigation solutions had expanded.

Brokers see wider global FC-BGA capacity additions supporting long-term material demand, but that view depends on the timing of capacity additions and on customer share. The next confirmation points are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual business report.

08

Bull factors

AI packaging demand and quarterly recovery

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 10.2 billion, up 552.9% year on year, and preliminary nine-month 2026 revenue of KRW 259.0 billion, announced November 5, 2026, already exceeds full-year 2025 revenue.

Revenue has risen for two straight quarters from the second quarter of 2026 through the third, a trend attributed to demand for high-performance AI server chips. Meritz Securities described the FC-BGA market as entering an upcycle.

Micro solder ball share and capacity expansion

Hana Securities estimated in an April 2026 report that the company holds about 70% of the micro solder ball market. The same report said solder ball capacity was raised 30% from April 2026 and micro solder ball capacity was added by 10%. Meritz Securities estimated that its share within a Taiwanese FC-BGA customer roughly doubled.

Price increases and wider raw material pass-through

Hyundai Motor Securities said that price increases implemented in March 2026 have been reflected in selling prices, normalizing results. Meritz Securities expected wider raw material price pass-through and higher utilization to drive the profit recovery.

Hyundai Motor Securities also described the company's exposure to raw material price swings as relatively limited.

09

Bear factors

Accumulated losses and rising debt ratio

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders swung to KRW 115.7 billion in 2025, and the last four quarters total KRW 96 billion in net losses attributable to controlling shareholders.

The debt-to-equity ratio climbed from 19.5% in 2022 to 94.5% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 320.2 billion at the end of 2025. The effect of accumulated losses on equity will be visible in the 2026 annual business report.

Raw material prices and quarterly margin swings

The first-quarter 2026 operating result swung to a loss of KRW 668 million from the prior quarter, and Meritz Securities attributed the margin pressure to surging silver and tin prices. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 44.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 6.92 billion in the third quarter of 2026. If the lag before price increases takes effect grows longer, quarterly profit could swing again.

Quarterly volatility and customer concentration

Fourth-quarter 2025 operating profit was KRW 57.61 million, down 96.3% from the prior quarter, and the same quarter recorded a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 120.8 billion.

If shipments depend more heavily on specific customers such as a Taiwanese client, changes in those customers' investment plans pass through to quarterly results quickly. Whether the fourth quarter holds profitability will be visible in the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results.

10

Risk factors

Raw material prices

If silver and tin prices rise and selling prices lag behind, quarterly margins can weaken. Meritz Securities cited surging silver and tin prices as a reason for the margin pressure in the first quarter of 2026. Whether wider pass-through clauses reduce this risk will be visible in 2027 quarterly results.

Industry cycle and customer concentration

Because the FC-BGA market has moved from supply shortage into an upcycle, excess capacity additions could quickly pass a demand slowdown through to material demand. Shipments concentrated in a few customers are highly exposed to those customers' inventory adjustments and shifts in competition.

Balance sheet and preliminary results

The debt-to-equity ratio reached 94.5% at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 320.2 billion close to total equity of KRW 338.8 billion. Third-quarter 2026 results are unaudited preliminary figures, and the company said final results may differ.

The confirmed results and capital changes in the 2026 annual business report will be the final reference for balance sheet risk.

12

Overall view

The recent trend at DUKSAN HI METAL CO. is one in which demand for AI and high-performance chip back-end materials is lifting results.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 99.5 billion and operating profit KRW 10.2 billion, up 61.9% and 552.9% year on year, and preliminary nine-month 2026 revenue has already exceeded full-year 2025 revenue.

However, the full-year 2025 net loss of KRW 115.7 billion and the KRW 96 billion four-quarter cumulative net loss are still visible in the equity and balance sheet. The debt-to-equity ratio was 94.5% at the end of 2025, and operating profit stayed positive in the second and third quarters of 2026.

Strengths include share gains and capacity expansion in micro solder balls and the FC-BGA upcycle, while weaknesses include accumulated losses, debt, customer concentration and raw material price swings.

The next confirmation points are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual business report and first-quarter 2027 results. This report does not offer an investment opinion or a target price.
