Report overview

Revenue Down for Three Years, Quarterly Profit Rebounding

NPC's revenue has declined for three straight years since 2023, yet operating profit in each of the first three quarters of 2026 stayed well above the fourth quarter of 2025, which marked the low point.

02

Business structure

NPC runs a single business that is mostly plastic injection molding and sales, including the mold division. Its main segments are containers, pallets, chairs and desks, environmental products, sheet products and container leasing.

Leasing's share of revenue rose from 16.83% in 2022 to 23.87% in 2024, while the injection-molded product share fell from 83.17% to 76.13% over the same period. In 2025, injection products were 77.72% domestic and 22.28% export, and container leasing was 71.94% domestic and 28.06% export.

The consolidated group includes a subsidiary that recycles waste plastic into non-metal raw materials. In February 2025 NPC bought an additional 51% stake in NPC Molltech and consolidated it as a subsidiary, and its corporate history lists an acquisition-merger of NSC effective January 1, 2026.

At the March 31, 2026 general shareholders meeting, a charter amendment adding warehousing and logistics-center operation to the business purpose was approved. The Pyeongtaek plant began new operation in April 2026. R&D spending was 2,619 million won in 2025, or 0.58% of revenue.

Unlike large petrochemical-feedstock producers such as LG Chem and Lotte Chemical, NPC sits at the finished-goods processing stage, so its results depend on how much container and pallet demand it captures.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue shrinking while operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung sharply from year to year.

Revenue was KRW 544.6 billion in 2022, KRW 508.7 billion in 2023 (down 6.6%), KRW 458.3 billion in 2024 (down 9.9%) and KRW 450.3 billion in 2025 (down 1.7%), falling for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025; 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 23.5 billion in 2022, KRW 29.6 billion in 2023 (up 25.9%), KRW 10.5 billion in 2024 (down 64.5%) and KRW 23.6 billion in 2025 (up 124.6%); 2023 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest. The operating margin was 4.3% in 2022, 5.8% in 2023, 2.3% in 2024 and 5.2% in 2025, lowest in 2024.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 28.1 billion in 2022, KRW 26.7 billion in 2023 (down 4.9%), KRW 75.8 billion in 2024 (up 183.6%) and KRW 12.9 billion in 2025 (down 82.9%), with 2024 the largest year.

In 2024 that figure far exceeded operating profit of KRW 10.5 billion, and total net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 75.8 billion; the non-operating items behind that gap need to be traced through the business report's income statement.

Operating cash flow was KRW 64 billion in 2022, KRW 51.2 billion in 2023, KRW 69.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 23.7 billion in 2025, a sharp drop in the latest year.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit of KRW 7.52 billion in the third quarter of 2025 fell 65.7% to KRW 2.58 billion in the fourth quarter, then recovered to KRW 6.23 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 7.99 billion in the second quarter before dropping to KRW 5.69 billion in the third quarter.

Revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 109.3 billion, down 5.4% year on year.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 478 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, rose to KRW 6.76 billion in the second quarter of 2026 and was KRW 3.08 billion in the third quarter, totaling KRW 14.9 billion over the last four quarters.

The 2025 third-quarter cumulative remarks linked the injection segment's sales decline to weak domestic demand and lower shipping volumes.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for finished plastic injection products tracks domestic consumption and logistics volumes. The company's earnings remarks attributed the injection segment's sales decline to weak domestic demand and lower shipping volumes.

By contrast, the expansion of online trade has raised demand for containers for fresh food and insulated thermal boxes, and the company is developing reusable boxes to replace single-use EPS boxes.

Within the chemicals sector, LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions and SKC carry larger shares of battery-materials, petrochemical and film businesses and face different cycles, while NPC's consumer logistics container focus makes direct comparison limited.

Its competitive base rests on injection capability including molds and a container leasing model. With revenue down for three straight years, the direction of the cycle is currently read mainly through quarterly revenue trends.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly profit recovery in 2026

Operating profit fell to KRW 2.58 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 before recovering to KRW 6.23 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 7.99 billion in the second quarter.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 478 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 6.76 billion in the second quarter of 2026. The third quarter dropped to KRW 5.69 billion in operating profit and KRW 3.08 billion in net profit, still above the fourth-quarter 2025 level.

Stronger balance sheet

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 449.4 billion and total liabilities were KRW 175.9 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 53.1% in 2022 to 39.1% in 2025, and reached 31.8% in 2024. The lighter debt load can be read as room for capital spending or business expansion.

Expanding business base

The Pyeongtaek plant began new operation in April 2026, and a March 2026 charter amendment added warehousing and logistics-center operation to the business purpose. In February 2025 NPC acquired an additional 51% of NPC Molltech and consolidated it. The rise in the leasing share to 23.87% in 2024 indicates a broader leasing base.

Bear factors

Three years of falling revenue

Revenue fell to KRW 508.7 billion in 2023, KRW 458.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 450.3 billion in 2025, and 2025 was the smallest year of the 2022 to 2025 span. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 109.3 billion was down 5.4% year on year. The third-quarter decline suggests the injection-segment recovery is not yet visible.

Earnings volatility and weaker cash flow

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 82.9% to KRW 12.9 billion in 2025, and operating cash flow dropped to KRW 23.7 billion from KRW 69.6 billion in 2024. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 5.69 billion was down 24.3% year on year. The divergence between operating profit and net profit has recurred across years.

Weak domestic demand

According to the company's earnings remarks, injection-product sales declined in the first nine months of 2025 on a weak domestic economy and lower shipping volumes. Over the same period, net profit fell 87.2%. If domestic logistics demand does not recover, the revenue decline could continue.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

The operating margin moved between 2.3% in 2024 and 5.2% in 2025. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 75.8 billion in 2024 to KRW 12.9 billion in 2025, a wide year-to-year swing. With last-four-quarter profit of KRW 14.9 billion, small changes can move the profit metrics substantially.

Execution risk from acquisitions and expansion

A 51% stake increase in NPC Molltech in February 2025, the acquisition-merger of NSC recorded as of January 1, 2026, the Pyeongtaek plant start in April 2026 and the logistics business added in March 2026 all fall within a short period.

Running several projects at once makes cost burden and the timing of investment returns less certain. How much the consolidations contribute to consolidated profit is the key question.

Small-cap liquidity and share price volatility

Given its small market capitalization, share prices can move sharply when trading volume is thin. Stocks with large earnings swings tend to see larger market reactions. Changes in the shareholder base appear in the shareholder section of the annual business report.

Overall view

NPC's disclosed results for 2022 to 2025 show revenue down for three straight years while operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders diverged sharply from year to year.

Operating profit rose 124.6% to KRW 23.6 billion in 2025, but net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 82.9% to KRW 12.9 billion and operating cash flow dropped to KRW 23.7 billion.

Quarterly operating profit recovered to KRW 6.23 billion and KRW 7.99 billion in the first two quarters of 2026 after the fourth-quarter 2025 low, then fell to KRW 5.69 billion in the third quarter. The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 39.1% in 2025 from 53.1% in 2022, easing financial burden.

Bullish factors include the quarterly profit recovery, a stronger balance sheet, and business expansion through the Pyeongtaek plant and the added business purpose. Bearish factors include continued revenue decline, earnings volatility and weak domestic demand.

The valuation sits at a low multiple of book value, but because profit over the last four quarters is small, the multiples are volatile. The next checkpoints are the 2026 preliminary annual results around late February 2027 and the 2026 annual business report around late March 2027.
