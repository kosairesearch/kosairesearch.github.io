Report overview

Earnings Up, Shares Below IPO Price

Madup's reported half-year profit grew faster than its revenue, yet the current price of KRW 5,750 sits below the KRW 8,000 IPO price, leaving a gap between results and share performance.

  1. 1

    Reported half-year (cumulative) 2026 revenue was KRW 18.3 billion, up 58.6% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 3.79 billion, up 126.6%.

  2. 2

    The IPO price was set at KRW 8,000, the top of the indicated range. The current price of KRW 5,750 on October 27 is below that level.

  3. 3

    In 2025, revenue was split 76% marketing, 9.8% solutions and 14.1% brand build-up. The growth of the AI solution share is the key gauge of the business shift.

  4. 4

    Madup paid a quarterly dividend of KRW 54 per share, totaling about KRW 1.016 billion. The company set a goal of keeping its shareholder return ratio at 40% or higher.

  5. 5

    Madup is on the October lock-up release list, with about 10% of issued shares affected. Trading value of KRW 1.1 billion points to thin liquidity.

02

Business structure

Madup was founded in 2015 and is a marketing technology company that applies AI to digital advertising operations. It operates three segments, marketing, solutions and brand build-up, which accounted for 76%, 9.8% and 14.1% of 2025 revenue respectively.

The marketing segment provides a managed ad-operations service that uses the company's own AI tools to automate and streamline campaign execution. Campaigns run on performance data from domestic and global platforms such as NAVER, Kakao, Google, Meta, Amazon, TikTok and Reddit.

The solutions segment centers on LEVER Xpert, an AI advertising agent that combines more than KRW 1 trillion of ad-execution data accumulated over ten years with marketers' decision logic. The company said it invested about KRW 27.7 billion in developing and upgrading the solution over the past three years.

It says it serves more than 200 advertisers, including Samsung Electronics, Olive Young and Musinsa, with annual ad billings above KRW 500 billion. Meta provided technology development funding of USD 400,000 in 2023 and USD 50,000 in 2025.

By the company's own analysis, its five largest advertisers accounted for roughly 17% to 24% of revenue over the last three years.

Unlike NAVER and Kakao, which are also classified in the IT and software sector, Madup is an agency and solutions provider that buys media on such platforms, and no verified market-share data for competitors was found, so the competitive picture is described only qualitatively.

03

Recent trends

Madup listed on KOSDAQ on July 1, 2026, with the IPO priced at KRW 8,000, the top of the range. The book-building drew 2,392 institutions and a subscription ratio of about 1,396 to 1. The shares jumped about 180% intraday on the first day but then fell quickly, trading roughly 25% below the IPO price on July 7.

After the earnings disclosure on July 29, the stock reached the daily upper limit at KRW 5,920. On a reported basis, half-year (cumulative) 2026 revenue was KRW 18.3 billion, up 58.6% from the same period a year earlier. Operating profit was KRW 3.79 billion, up 126.6%, and net income was KRW 4.24 billion, up 505.2%.

Profit grew faster than revenue, which points to better profitability, while the reason net income exceeds operating profit should be checked against non-operating items and taxes in the periodic report. Some media outlets reported the same figures as second-quarter consolidated results.

In 2024 Madup posted revenue of KRW 35.0 billion and an operating loss of KRW 0.4 billion, and in 2025 it turned profitable with revenue of KRW 50.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 8.5 billion. First-quarter 2026 consolidated revenue was KRW 13.7 billion and operating profit was KRW 0.3 billion.

On September 1 the shares traded as high as KRW 9,290 amid reports that SK Securities saw the company entering a monetization phase. As of October 27 the price is KRW 5,750, market capitalization is KRW 109.9 billion and trading value is KRW 1.1 billion.

04

Outlook

The company earmarked about KRW 13.7 billion of IPO proceeds for AI equipment, personnel and operating costs, working capital, and the setup and operation of a U.S. entity. The North American subsidiary was established in November 2025.

According to the company, global ad billings grew at an average annual rate of 85% from 2023 to 2025. Its plans also include expanding an AI-managed service for small and mid-sized advertisers.

Park Jong-sun of Eugene Investment & Securities assessed Meta's direct technical validation and capital commitment as positive for the medium-to-long-term growth strategy.

Indicators to watch are the change in the solution revenue share, the revenue contribution of the North American unit, and whether the higher operating profit level seen in the second quarter is sustained.

Shareholder returns began with a quarterly dividend and a share buyback decision, and the buyback window was disclosed as running to September 20, 2026, so the execution result should be checked separately.

05

Bull factors

Profit growing faster than revenue

The 126.6% rise in reported half-year operating profit outpaced the 58.6% rise in revenue. Revenue has grown at an average annual rate of about 33% over the last three years. In 2025 the company turned profitable with operating profit of KRW 8.5 billion.

As labor-heavy ad operations shift toward automation, there is room for the operating margin to improve further.

AI agent built on execution data

The company stresses that real ad-execution data, which general-purpose large language models cannot easily access, is its core edge. It says LEVER Xpert has automated more than 90% of data-related tasks. In the same disclosure, ad billings per marketer rose about 51%. These figures come from the company, and no independent verification was found.

Early shareholder returns and overseas push

Madup said it would pay a quarterly dividend one month after listing, the first among KOSDAQ-listed companies to do so. It also decided to buy back 210,000 shares worth KRW 1.5645 billion. Overseas, it aims to link demand from Korean consumer brands and content companies to its North American unit. If the return policy and overseas revenue progress together, earnings visibility could improve.

06

Bear factors

Heavy reliance on ad agency revenue

The solution segment made up only 9.8% of 2025 revenue. Reports have noted that more than 70% of earnings is concentrated in the ad-agency-based marketing segment. IB Tomato flagged that the AI solution business is still in an investment phase. The pace of any shift to a software-like earnings structure has yet to be confirmed.

Quarterly profit volatility

Operating profit in the first quarter of 2026 was KRW 0.3 billion. Second-quarter consolidated operating profit was reported at KRW 3.79 billion, a wide gap between quarters. The company also recorded an operating loss in 2024. Quarterly profit can swing with ad spend volumes and the timing of costs.

Price below IPO level and thin trading

The current price of KRW 5,750 is below the KRW 8,000 IPO price. Money Today reported that the early buyback and dividend announcements were interpreted as a response to weak share performance. Trading value of about KRW 1.1 billion means prices can react sharply to shifts in supply and demand.

The market capitalization of KRW 109.9 billion is below the roughly KRW 150 billion expected at the time of the offering.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Advertising is discretionary spending that is sensitive to the economic cycle, so advertisers may cut budgets in a slowdown. Because Madup's revenue is tied to ad billings, changes in the ad market can flow directly into results.

The overall supply-and-demand climate on KOSDAQ also affects volatility in newly listed shares. Exchange rates and overseas ad market conditions could also influence North American results.

Industry and technology

Madup places ads through global platforms such as Google, Meta, Amazon and TikTok. Changes in those platforms' ad policies or algorithms could alter operating methods and results.

If platforms or general-purpose AI providers strengthen their own ad automation features, the differentiation of a proprietary solution could weaken. No verified statistics were found to size this threat.

Supply and demand

More than 10% of Madup's issued shares were listed for release from lock-up in October. Long-term commitments of three months or more were only 5.7% of the book-building requests. Whether released shares actually reach the market needs to be checked date by date. With small trading value, the impact of selling can be relatively large.

08

Overall view

On a reported basis, Madup's revenue and profit both rose, and half-year operating profit growth outpaced revenue growth. The business is still centered on ad agency work, with the solution segment at only 9.8% of 2025 revenue.

The bull side points to an AI agent built on execution data, shareholder returns through dividends and buybacks, and North American expansion. The bear side points to reliance on ad agency revenue, quarterly profit volatility, lock-up releases and thin trading.

The current price of KRW 5,750 is below the IPO price of KRW 8,000, and market capitalization is KRW 109.9 billion. Separately, the fact that some media outlets described the half-year figures as second-quarter results should be checked against the periodic report. This material is for information only, and any view on direction is left to the reader.
