02

Business structure

Madup is an AI-based digital marketing company founded in 2015, with its core business automating advertising operations with AI using ad execution data. The company reports three segments.

The marketing segment is AI-driven advertising agency work and generated KRW 10.0 billion in first-quarter 2026 revenue, or 72.8% of the total. The solutions segment sells LEVER Xpert, the company's in-house AI marketing agent, and its revenue rose 120% year on year to KRW 900 million in the same quarter.

The brand build-up segment expanded 96% to KRW 2.9 billion over the same period. Solutions revenue grew from KRW 1.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 4.9 billion in 2025, the fastest growth among the segments, yet it remains a smaller share of total revenue than agency work.

The company says LEVER Xpert combines more than KRW 1 trillion of ad execution data accumulated over ten years with marketers' decision logic, and that cumulative development spending reached KRW 28 billion.

The product collects and processes media and tracker data and supports creative analysis and campaign operations, and the company says it has automated more than 90% of data collection and processing work. Its client base includes Samsung Electronics and more than 200 advertisers in total.

On competition, Madup emphasizes integrated optimization across multiple media and its data from actual ad spending, a contrast that press coverage drew against traditional agencies that spend 1% to 2% of revenue on R&D; this comparison reflects the company's own account.

03

Recent trends

Madup's cumulative first-half 2026 results show revenue of KRW 18.3 billion, up 58.6% year on year. Operating profit rose 126.6% to KRW 3.79 billion, and net income increased 505.2% to KRW 4.24 billion.

These results were announced on July 29, 2026, and on that day the stock traded near KRW 5,920 in early trading after rising to the daily limit. Consolidated 2025 revenue reached KRW 50.2 billion, up 43.6%, and operating profit of KRW 8.5 billion turned a 2024 operating loss of KRW 400 million into a profit.

Reported 2025 net income ranged from KRW 7.7 billion to KRW 7.8 billion depending on the report, after net losses in 2023 and 2024. First-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 13.7 billion, up 41.5% year on year, with operating profit of KRW 332.07 million and net income of KRW 532.82 million.

The balance sheet had been in capital impairment in 2023 and 2024, but conversion of redeemable convertible preferred shares into common stock in 2025 restored capital, leaving a debt ratio of 156.2% as of 2025.

The stock listed on July 1, 2026 at the KRW 8,000 offer price, and the current price of KRW 5,160 is 35.5% below that level. The market capitalization of KRW 97.1 billion is below the KRW 131.2 billion to KRW 150.0 billion range the company projected before listing.

04

Outlook

The company set a target of linking its AI operational recommendations directly to ad execution in the fourth quarter of 2026. Its AI managed service for small and mid-sized advertisers reduces staffing needs and targets customers that traditional agencies found hard to serve profitably.

Offering proceeds were allocated to AI infrastructure of KRW 2.2 billion and to personnel and operating costs of KRW 5.7 billion, with the remainder going to working capital and the US business.

The North American subsidiary was established in November 2025, and the company said it is running global ad campaigns through local US media connections. The company stated that its global ad volume grew at an annual average of 85% from 2023 to 2025.

Meta provided technology development funding of USD 400,000 in 2023 and USD 50,000 in 2025, which the company cites as an external collaboration. Operating more than 30% of global digital marketing through LEVER Xpert is a long-term target the company has presented.

The items to track in future results are the shift in solutions revenue mix, agency-segment profitability, and the scale of North American revenue, which will appear in disclosed figures starting with the third-quarter 2026 periodic report.

05

Bull factors

Profitability visible in reported results

Madup moved from a KRW 400 million operating loss in 2024 to KRW 8.5 billion in operating profit in 2025, and cumulative first-half 2026 operating profit rose 126.6% to KRW 3.79 billion. Net income over the same period grew 505.2% to KRW 4.24 billion, so the improvement also appeared below the operating line.

Press coverage noted that the company was assessed under the pre-profit listing requirement during its preliminary review because of its past losses, a status that changed after late 2025. Its record of profitability before listing is presented as a backdrop for absorbing the cost of AI solution investment.

Data assets and AI solution growth

The company says LEVER Xpert combines more than KRW 1 trillion of ad execution data accumulated over ten years with its marketers' decision logic. Solutions revenue rose 285% to KRW 4.9 billion in 2025 and grew another 120% year on year in the first quarter of 2026.

The company says advertising volume per marketer rose about 51%, though this is an internal figure it compiled. Ad creative analysis and operations automation features are also being expanded.

Expansion overseas and into smaller advertisers

The North American subsidiary was established in November 2025, and the company says its global ad volume grew at an annual average of 85% from 2023 to 2025. Its AI managed service for smaller advertisers cuts staffing needs and targets customer groups that traditional agencies found hard to serve.

Meta's technology development funding is cited as evidence of external collaboration. Part of the offering proceeds was also allocated to US operations.

06

Bear factors

Agency-centered revenue mix

The marketing segment still accounted for 72.8% of first-quarter 2026 revenue. Agency income moves with the advertising cycle and the spending scale of large advertisers, so earnings could become more volatile if the economy slows.

According to the company's internal analysis, the top five clients accounted for 17% to 24% of revenue from 2023 to 2025, reflecting a structure built around large advertisers.

Small solutions share and investment burden

Cumulative development spending on LEVER Xpert reached KRW 28 billion, yet solutions revenue remains a small share of the total. The company was in capital impairment in 2023 and 2024, and its debt ratio was 156.2% as of 2025.

Offering proceeds are also allocated to AI infrastructure and staffing, so related costs continue after the listing. Press coverage described the AI solutions business as still in an investment phase.

Share price path and liquidity

The current price of KRW 5,160 is 35.5% below the KRW 8,000 offer price. Even after reaching the daily limit on July 29, 2026, the shares trade below the offer price. The market capitalization of KRW 97.1 billion is below the lower end of the pre-listing projected range.

Trading value at the time of this report was about KRW 800 million, so price moves could be sharp given thin liquidity.

07

Risk factors

Macro

A slowdown in ad spending and cuts to corporate marketing budgets directly affect agency revenue. With more than 70% of revenue concentrated in agency work, a downturn could slow growth or erode profitability. Expanding overseas ad execution also brings local competition and currency exposure.

Platform dependence

Ad execution depends on external media platforms such as Google, Meta, Amazon and TikTok. Changes in platform policy or data access could affect data collection and the operation of the solution.

Because the company also uses data from domestic platforms including NAVER and Kakao, policy shifts across these platforms can matter materially.

Liquidity and supply

With a market capitalization of KRW 97.1 billion and trading value of about KRW 800 million, the stock's trading volume is thin, so price swings can be large. Newly listed shares often show wide swings in supply and demand. Changes in major shareholdings can be tracked through disclosures.

08

Overall view

Madup's profits have improved since its 2025 consolidated turnaround, with both operating profit and net income rising through the first half of 2026.

Still, 72.8% of first-quarter 2026 revenue came from the agency segment, so the company remains heavily exposed to the advertising cycle and to the spending of large advertisers.

Solutions revenue is growing quickly but remains a small share of the total, and how far LEVER Xpert can change the revenue mix will show up in later disclosures. A debt ratio of 156.2% as of 2025 and cumulative development spending of KRW 28 billion point to a heavy technology investment burden.

The current price of KRW 5,160 is below the KRW 8,000 offer price, and thin trading means the share price could move sharply on supply and demand factors.

Overall, the earnings improvement is supported by disclosed figures, while the pace of the business shift and the share price direction lack a firm basis at this point.

Results from the overseas subsidiary and the push into smaller advertisers should become visible in disclosed figures from the third quarter of 2026 onward.
