Report overview

Agency-led profits, AI shift still the test

Madup has grown revenue and profit together since its 2025 turnaround, yet advertising agency work still generates most of its sales.

02

Business structure

Madup, founded in 2015, is a digital marketing technology company that runs three business lines: solution-based agency work for large advertisers, the AI ad agent LEVER Xpert, and an AI managed service for small and mid-sized advertisers.

In 1Q 2026 the marketing segment generated KRW 10.0 billion, or 72.8% of revenue, down from a 76.0% share in 2025. Solution segment revenue rose 120% year on year to KRW 0.9 billion in 1Q 2026, while the brand build-up segment expanded 96% to KRW 2.9 billion in the same period.

The company says it serves more than 200 advertisers, including Samsung Electronics, Olive Young, and Musinsa, and manages annual ad spending above KRW 500 billion.

LEVER Xpert is built on more than KRW 1 trillion of ad execution data accumulated over ten years, and the company says it automates over 90% of data tasks such as collection and report writing.

AMS, an AI operations service for smaller advertisers, launched at the end of 2025 and has been reported to support revenue diversification.

On the media side, Madup works with data from domestic and global platforms including NAVER, Kakao, Google, Meta, and TikTok, and in July 2026 it was named a Meta Collaborative Ads partner agency for commerce platforms such as Musinsa, 29CM, Kurly, and Olive Young.

The value of its data asset will become visible through the pace of advertiser growth and the changing mix of solution revenue.

03

Recent trends

Based on 3Q 2026 DART filings, revenue reached KRW 18.3 billion, up 42.0% year on year. Operating profit rose 31.5% to KRW 2.37 billion, and net income grew 18.5% to KRW 2.06 billion. The company said these results reflect one-off costs tied to the KOSDAQ listing and regular performance bonuses.

Consolidated revenue for 2025 was KRW 50.2 billion, up 43.6%, and operating profit of KRW 8.5 billion marked a turnaround from an operating loss of KRW 400 million in 2024. Net income in 2025 was KRW 7.8 billion, ending the prior year's loss.

Revenue in 1Q 2026 rose 41.5% to KRW 13.7 billion, but operating profit stayed at around KRW 300 million. After the November 12, 2026 release of 3Q results and a shareholder return plan that included share buybacks, the stock was reported to have come close to its daily upper limit.

On November 17, 2026 the stock traded as high as KRW 7,580 intraday, and the current price of KRW 7,410 is below the KRW 8,000 IPO price set on July 1, 2026. The company also resolved a quarterly cash dividend of KRW 54 per common share, extending its payouts to a second consecutive quarter since listing.

04

Outlook

Management said it expects 2026 revenue to grow 30% to 40%, building on 41.5% growth in 1Q 2026. This is the company's own outlook rather than a formal guidance figure. The company said it plans to deploy IPO proceeds into AI infrastructure, R&D staffing, and global expansion.

It also expects growth in the fourth quarter, when advertising demand typically rises, to be supported by existing client spending and expanded services.

Overseas, the North American subsidiary established in November 2025 supports advertising for Korean brands, and global ad volume has been reported to have grown at an average annual rate of 85% from 2023 to 2025.

In retail media, the company is widening its operations on commerce platforms based on its Meta Collaborative Ads partner status. Points to monitor include how far the solution revenue share rises, whether North American revenue becomes visible, and whether the service for smaller advertisers diversifies revenue. Changes in the advertising market and in large platform policies are the main variables for this outlook.

05

Bull factors

Continuity of the profit turnaround

Consolidated revenue reached KRW 50.2 billion in 2025, up 43.6%, and operating profit of KRW 8.5 billion marked the turn to profit. Revenue rose 41.5% in 1Q 2026, and 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 18.3 billion was up 42.0%. Proponents cite the continuation of profits across several quarters as the core support for the bull case.

Acceleration in AI solution revenue

Solution revenue tied to LEVER Xpert rose from KRW 1.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 4.9 billion in 2025, a 188.2% increase. Solution segment revenue in 1Q 2026 reached KRW 0.9 billion, up 120% from the prior-year period.

Reports say AMS, an AI operations service for smaller advertisers, has supported revenue diversification, and securities analysts have viewed favorably the link between AI technology and sales and profit.

Data assets and overseas expansion

The company says it holds more than KRW 1 trillion of ad execution data accumulated over a decade as an asset for training its AI. It received technology development funding of USD 400,000 in 2023 and USD 50,000 in 2025 from Meta.

Through its North American subsidiary set up in November 2025, the company says global ad volume grew at an average annual rate of 85% from 2023 to 2025.

06

Bear factors

Revenue still concentrated in agency work

The marketing segment generated KRW 10.0 billion in 1Q 2026, or 72.8% of revenue. Solution revenue of KRW 4.9 billion in 2025 was roughly 10% of annual revenue of KRW 50.2 billion. Because agency work drives most of the growth, the pace of the shift toward solutions determines how much the revenue structure changes.

Short profit track record and thin quarterly margins

Reports say Madup posted cumulative net losses of about KRW 35 billion from 2016 onward. Its first full-year profit came in 2025, so the record of sustained profitability is short. Operating profit in 1Q 2026 was roughly KRW 300 million, a margin of about 2% on revenue for the quarter.

Dependence on ad cycles and platform policy

Reports note that demand for agency work depends on advertisers' budget spending, and that large platform policies and rising personnel costs can shift profit margins. Madup's ad operations are affected by changes in ad products and policies at external platforms such as NAVER, Kakao, Google, and Meta.

The current price of KRW 7,410 is below the KRW 8,000 IPO price, a gap that some observers cite as a counterweight to the earnings improvement.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Companies often cut marketing budgets first when uncertainty rises, which can make agency revenue more volatile. A prolonged slowdown could lower growth expectations, and a larger share of overseas ad spending would increase exposure to currency movements.

Platform and policy

Madup's ad operations depend on the ad products and policies of external platforms such as NAVER, Kakao, Google, Meta, and TikTok.

Changes in platform policies or fee structures could alter how it operates and how profitable it is, and maintaining partner status such as Meta Collaborative Ads affects the scope of its business.

Market and supply

The stock traded as high as KRW 7,580 intraday on November 17, 2026, and the current price of KRW 7,410 is below the KRW 8,000 IPO price. Reports indicate the share price has reacted sharply to earnings and shareholder return announcements. The timing of lock-up expirations and the increase in tradable shares remain factors for supply and demand.

08

Overall view

Madup has shown revenue and operating profit rising together since its 2025 turnaround, through 3Q 2026. Its 3Q 2026 results of KRW 18.3 billion in revenue (up 42.0%), KRW 2.37 billion in operating profit (up 31.5%), and KRW 2.06 billion in net income (up 18.5%) support this trend.

However, the marketing segment still generates more than 70% of revenue, so the expansion of the LEVER Xpert AI solution is the key indicator of the business shift. Because profits have only been recorded since 2025, the durability of profitability will need to be confirmed through additional quarterly results.

The company presents its more than KRW 1 trillion of ad execution data and its collaboration with platforms such as Meta as points of differentiation. Reports indicate that securities analysts viewed favorably the link between AI technology and sales and profit.

The stock surged after the November 12, 2026 results release, and the current price of KRW 7,410 is below the KRW 8,000 IPO price.
