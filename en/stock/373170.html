02

Business structure

Micube Solution is a smart factory software company that collects and integrates manufacturing shop-floor data and makes production processes more intelligent.

Its core products are the Manufacturing Execution System (MES) and the Equipment Engineering System (EES), and it has extended into autonomous manufacturing solutions built on AI and digital twin technology.

In 2025 it launched MICUBE.AI, an AI manufacturing solution with four services: quality prediction, anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, and process optimization. A report from early 2026 said MES products account for 42% of revenue, with 91% of that sold domestically.

Major customers named in coverage include Samsung Electro-Mechanics, Samsung SDI, Dongkuk Steel, Pulmuone, and Ottogi. The company says it has about 100 cumulative customers and more than 700 project deliveries. It says roughly 95% of its customers are domestic companies.

Per a 2025 report, the autonomous manufacturing business was about KRW 5 billion in 2024. Government projects include the Daegu Manufacturing AI Center and the Ulsan Mipo AX demonstration industrial complex.

Listed peers in the same sector such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS, Hyundai Autoever, and POSCO DX are much larger in scale and product range, so the company is better viewed as a smaller manufacturing-focused operator.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 22.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 24.2 billion in 2023 (up 8.3%) and KRW 29.6 billion in 2024 (up 22.7%), before falling to KRW 25.6 billion in 2025 (down 13.6%); 2024 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 period. The company attributed the 2025 revenue decline to delayed customer orders.

Operating margin moved from 12.7% in 2022 to 7.3% in 2023, -3.6% in 2024, and 3.5% in 2025. Operating profit of KRW 1.76 billion in 2023 fell 37.9% year on year, and 2024 turned into an operating loss of KRW 1.07 billion. The company cited the failure to complete one or two projects as the cause of the 2024 loss.

In 2025 operating profit was KRW 886 million and profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.63 billion, both swinging back to profit; fourth-quarter 2025 operating profit of KRW 1.58 billion exceeded the full-year figure, suggesting profit was concentrated in the fourth quarter.

First-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 4.44 billion fell 26.7% from the prior quarter and swung to an operating loss of KRW 838 million. Second-quarter revenue recovered to KRW 5.8 billion (up 30.7% from the prior quarter), but an operating loss of KRW 319 million persisted, with the loss narrowing.

In the third quarter revenue reached KRW 7.21 billion (up 18.1% year on year) and operating profit of KRW 149 million turned positive, though that operating profit fell 7.0% year on year and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 120 million fell 67.8%.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters (fourth quarter 2025 to third quarter 2026) totals KRW 1.07 billion.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio fell from 119.2% in 2022 to 36.0% in 2025; year-end 2025 total equity was KRW 23.7 billion and total liabilities KRW 8.54 billion, and operating cash flow was KRW 2.92 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Fiscal spending on domestic AI policy is frequently cited as a key driver of demand for manufacturing AI.

A 2025 Korea Investment & Securities report noted that the new government set a target of KRW 100 trillion in investment over five years and expected demand for solutions that integrate MES and EES with digital twin and AI analytics to grow.

The company defined 2026 as the first year of full-scale spread of autonomous manufacturing AI.

In September 2026, it was reported that the company was selected as a joint R&D institution for a large government-led physical AI national project, receiving KRW 2.5 billion and KRW 3.6 billion for its two tasks, KRW 6.1 billion in total.

Demand from the front end depends on manufacturers building new factories, and the CEO said revenue comes through a rollout effect only when new plants are built. Results can therefore be linked to capital spending cycles at manufacturers such as in semiconductors and batteries.

A Sisajournal report said the growth rate of new order value has averaged more than 10% a year. Government-supported projects are also cited as one pillar of the revenue base. On competition, the company says it differentiates itself by combining AI and digital twin capabilities with manufacturing-specific MES and EES. Still, the scale gap with larger listed peers in the same sector remains wide.

06

Outlook

The company has set revenue of KRW 30 billion or more in 2026 as its management target. First-three-quarter 2026 revenue totals about KRW 17.45 billion, so reaching the target requires roughly KRW 12.5 billion or more in the fourth quarter.

Revenue rose in both the second and third quarters of 2026, which serves as one reference point for judging the target. In a 2025 report the company cited a target of KRW 50 billion in revenue for 2027.

Its 2026 New Year message said it would launch an autonomous manufacturing AI package and apply it first to predictive maintenance of equipment. Business plans call for expanding the Daegu Manufacturing AI Center and Ulsan AX demonstration complex projects, with a focus on government-supported and private projects.

The company named food and logistics as markets to watch, saying humanoid robots and autonomous mobile robots could create automation demand in logistics centers. It said it is preparing for overseas expansion, including Vietnam, through an in-house marketing team.

In a June 2026 report the company said it expected earnings to reverse this year. The next checkpoint is whether 2026 revenue reaches the annual target and what the full-year operating margin is in the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Third-Quarter 2026 Turnaround Signal

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 7.21 billion rose 18.1% year on year and has increased for two straight quarters since the first-quarter low. Operating profit of KRW 149 million turned positive from the prior quarter's loss.

Fourth-quarter 2025 operating profit of KRW 1.58 billion shows that quarterly profitability moves sharply with project completion timing.

Policy Demand and National Project Wins

A 2025 Korea Investment & Securities report said the new government's five-year KRW 100 trillion AI investment target could expand demand for solutions integrating MES, EES, and AI analytics.

In September 2026 the company was reported as a joint R&D institution for a physical AI national project, receiving KRW 6.1 billion in research funding across two tasks.

Financial Capacity and Cash Generation

The debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 36.0%, down from 119.2% in 2022, with total equity of KRW 23.7 billion and total liabilities of KRW 8.54 billion. Operating cash flow rose to KRW 2.92 billion in 2025 from KRW 1.07 billion in 2024.

Cash and cash equivalents including short-term financial instruments were reported at KRW 21.2 billion at the end of the first quarter of 2026.

09

Bear factors

Losses in the First Half of 2026

First-quarter 2026 operating loss was KRW 838 million and loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 660 million, and the second quarter still showed an operating loss of KRW 319 million and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 161 million.

First-quarter revenue of KRW 4.44 billion was the low point after a 26.7% drop from the prior quarter. Third-quarter operating profit of KRW 149 million turned positive but was 7.0% lower than a year earlier.

Revenue Volatility and Dependence on Order Timing

Revenue fell 13.6% to KRW 25.6 billion in 2025, which the company attributed to delayed customer orders. Reaching the KRW 30 billion target for 2026 would require roughly KRW 12.5 billion or more in the fourth quarter.

The 2024 loss was attributed to one or two projects not being completed, so quarterly results can shift significantly with revenue recognition timing.

Market-Cap Requirement and Investor Sentiment

A June 2026 report said authorities would raise the market-cap requirement for continued listing to KRW 20 billion in the second half, with designation as a managed stock after 30 consecutive trading days below it. The same report said investor sentiment had not improved despite the bonus issue and dividend. With market capitalization near the threshold, share price moves alone could create a compliance issue.

10

Risk factors

Listing Requirement and Market Cap

Whether market capitalization meets the threshold is a direct variable for continued listing. The reported threshold is KRW 20 billion, and 30 consecutive trading days below it triggers designation as a managed stock.

Because market cap sits near the threshold, a large swing in the share price would immediately make compliance the issue.

Customer and Capex Dependence

With roughly 95% of customers being domestic companies, results depend on domestic manufacturers' capital spending. Major customers include Samsung affiliates, so the timing of specific group projects can have an outsized effect. A slowdown in manufacturer investment could delay new orders.

Project Timing and Revenue Recognition

The 2024 loss and the first-quarter 2026 revenue decline were both explained by project completion and order timing. The company said delivery typically falls within four to twelve months.

When revenue dips against a largely fixed cost base, the result can quickly become an operating loss, so quarterly results are volatile.

12

Overall view

Micube Solution's revenue peaked at KRW 29.6 billion in 2024 and fell to KRW 25.6 billion in 2025, while operating results swung from a KRW 1.07 billion loss in 2024 to KRW 886 million of operating profit in 2025.

In 2026 operating losses in the first and second quarters were followed by a third-quarter operating profit of KRW 149 million, and revenue has risen for two consecutive quarters.

Supporting factors include government AI policy and a national physical AI project, a debt ratio of 36.0% at the end of 2025, and the KRW 30 billion revenue target for 2026.

Negative factors include revenue dependence on order timing, operating losses in the first half of 2026, a year-on-year decline in third-quarter profit attributable to controlling shareholders, and the KRW 20 billion market-cap requirement for continued listing.

The customer base is concentrated among domestic manufacturers, which makes results sensitive to their capital spending.

Valuation can be summarized as a share price slightly below book value per share and a multiple near the lower end of its past earnings-based band, with the durability of the earnings recovery as the key variable for interpreting it.

The next checkpoints are whether 2026 revenue reaches the annual target in the 2026 annual report, the first-quarter 2027 report, and whether the market-cap requirement is met.
