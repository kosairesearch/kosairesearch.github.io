02

Business structure

JK Synapse was founded in 1999 and built its business around display materials, and it now runs three main lines. The first is display materials, including OCR, UV resin, silver (Ag) paste and etchants used in OLED and LCD production.

The second is semiconductor process materials, with cleaning, developing, etching and stripping chemicals supplied to chip manufacturing, and the company is developing a stripper for high-bandwidth memory (HBM).

The third is telecom-device distribution through a subsidiary that sells via an online platform and nationwide offline stores; it came into the group in 2025, alongside a battery-recycling subsidiary.

The distribution subsidiary reported first-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 14.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 6.6 million, according to the company, and that contribution was cited as a driver of the group's first-quarter profit.

Distribution adds scale while materials are meant to carry margins, but the revenue share and segment profitability of each business should be checked against segment disclosures in the periodic reports.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 76.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 49.5 billion in 2023 (down 35.6% year on year) and KRW 48.9 billion in 2024 (down 1.3%), before rising to KRW 61.9 billion in 2025 (up 26.8%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

The company attributed the 2025 revenue increase to a newly acquired subsidiary being consolidated. Operating losses were KRW 1.2 billion in 2022, KRW 9.32 billion in 2023 and KRW 11 billion in 2024, before narrowing to KRW 8.25 billion in 2025, so operating margin moved from -1.6% to -18.8%, -22.6% and -13.3%.

The company has reported losses in each year from 2022 through 2025. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened from KRW 41 billion in 2023 to KRW 78.3 billion in 2024 and narrowed to KRW 18.6 billion in 2025. The company described the 2024 loss as arising under the former management.

In 2025, cost cuts and headcount reductions were pursued. The debt-to-equity ratio was 91.2% in 2022, 60.3% in 2023, 178.4% in 2024 and 136.2% in 2025; at the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 64.1 billion, equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 67 billion and total liabilities were KRW 87.3 billion.

Operating cash flow was negative in every year, at -KRW 5.17 billion, -KRW 6.03 billion, -KRW 11.1 billion and -KRW 2.97 billion from 2022 to 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the third quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 20 billion and an operating loss of KRW 2.33 billion, improving to KRW 22 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.52 billion in the fourth quarter.

The first quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 24 billion and operating profit of KRW 220 million, which the company described as the first quarterly operating profit in 15 quarters.

The second quarter of 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 790 million on revenue of KRW 23.3 billion, down 3.0% from the prior quarter. The third quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 14.7 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.63 billion.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a KRW 2.65 billion profit in the second quarter of 2026 and a KRW 7.73 billion loss in the third quarter. The sum of net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters is KRW 11.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The materials business is sensitive to utilization rates in display and semiconductor end markets. On the chip side, the company says a rebound in the global semiconductor cycle has restored fab utilization at major manufacturers and lifted orders for consumable process chemicals.

The company also links localization of supply chains to stronger demand for etchants and strippers. For this market, the key question raised in analyst commentary is whether new materials such as HBM stripper translate into higher supply volumes and better profitability.

Nice Investors and Credit Bureau cited HBM stripper and anti-moisture coatings for in-vehicle displays as possible beneficiaries of more advanced chip processes.

In telecom-device distribution, demand fell in 2025 amid economic uncertainty, price increases and delayed AI feature launches, according to company information compiled from disclosures.

Among chemical peers, LG Chem and Hanwha Solutions are much larger in scale and product range, so a closer comparison is with specialists that compete on custom formulation in narrow process-chemical niches.

06

Outlook

In a November 2026 report, Nice Investors and Credit Bureau projected consolidated revenue of KRW 86.7 billion for 2026, up 40% from KRW 61.9 billion in 2025.

By segment, it expected telecom-device distribution at KRW 45.4 billion, electronic materials at KRW 16.2 billion, battery recycling at KRW 15 billion and electronic components including RFID at KRW 7 billion.

The same report said whether higher sales turn into operating profit depends on segment profitability and cost control. The company has set a target of record results for its semiconductor process materials business in 2026.

It said in October 2026 that second-quarter 2026 semiconductor materials revenue reached KRW 3.00820 billion, up about 90% year on year. At the end of the first half of 2026, the current ratio stood at 37.9% and operating cash flow was negative.

Because third-quarter revenue fell from the prior quarter, reaching the KRW 86.7 billion projection would require a sharp fourth-quarter rise, so the annual 2026 figure in the business report is the first check. Whether HBM stripper supply feeds into consolidated revenue is the other point to watch.

08

Bull factors

Larger revenue base

Revenue of KRW 61.9 billion in 2025 rose 26.8%, and KRW 24 billion in the first quarter of 2026 was the highest of the last five quarters. Consolidating the distribution subsidiary raised the sales base. The company has targeted record results for its semiconductor process materials business in 2026.

Balance sheet repair

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 178.4% in 2024 to 136.2% in 2025, and the company put it at 99.7% at the end of the third quarter of 2026. Rights offerings and convertible bond conversions raised equity and reduced borrowings. A lower financial risk after the capital raise is a positive factor.

Quarterly profit track record

Operating profit of KRW 220 million in the first quarter of 2026 was, according to the company, the first quarterly operating profit in 15 quarters. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 2.65 billion in the second quarter of 2026.

Selling and administrative expenses fell to KRW 3.36 billion in the third quarter, which supports the cost-efficiency case.

09

Bear factors

Third-quarter revenue decline

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 14.7 billion fell 36.9% from the prior quarter and 26.5% from a year earlier. The same quarter posted an operating loss of KRW 1.63 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.73 billion. Whether the first-quarter profit was a single-quarter result or a trend is not yet clear.

Persistent losses and cash flow

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters total KRW 11.3 billion. Operating cash flow was negative in every year from 2022 to 2025. A analysis also noted that the current ratio was 37.9% at the end of the first half of 2026, leaving short-term liquidity pressure.

Dilution from capital raises

The balance-sheet repair relied on rights offerings and conversion of convertible bonds into shares. A higher share count dilutes existing holders' stake. If capital raises continue while the share price sits below book value, per-share value recovery could be slower.

10

Risk factors

Business mix risk

Much of the revenue growth depends on the telecom-device distribution subsidiary. Company information compiled from disclosures recorded weaker demand in 2025 amid economic uncertainty, price increases and delayed AI feature launches. If distribution sales fall or margins thin, consolidated results could be hit. Without better materials profitability, higher sales may not translate into profit.

Financing and capital risk

The debt-to-equity ratio reached 178.4% in 2024, and total liabilities were KRW 87.3 billion at the end of 2025. Rights offerings and CB conversions were repeated during the capital raise. With operating cash flow negative since 2022, the need for further funding remains.

Listing and governance risk

The company said it received an unqualified audit opinion for fiscal 2025. Large losses and debt built up under the previous management, and the capital-impairment ratio fell from 107.8% to 33.3%. If that ratio rises again, concerns over management-issue designation could return.

12

Overall view

JK Synapse's revenue base expanded after its telecom-device distribution subsidiary joined the group, with 2025 revenue at KRW 61.9 billion, up 26.8%.

It posted an operating profit of KRW 220 million in the first quarter of 2026, but operating losses in the second and third quarters put it in two straight loss-making quarters. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 178.4% in 2024 to 136.2% in 2025, and capital raises eased financial burden.

However, net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters total KRW 11.3 billion, and operating cash flow was negative every year from 2022 to 2025. The share price sits below book value, but without a steadier earnings record that gap alone is a weak basis for a valuation view.

The next checkpoints are full-year 2026 operating profit in the annual business report and whether profitability holds in the first-quarter 2027 report. The business combines distribution-driven sales with materials-driven margins, so whether those two move in the same direction is the central question.
