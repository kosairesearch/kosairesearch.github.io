02

Business structure

i-Components develops and manufactures high-function barrier films for display and solar applications and is listed on KOSDAQ.

Its product lines are barrier films for electronic shelf labels (ESL) and QD TVs, and optical films for notebooks and tablets, according to an independent research report dated November 12, 2026.

The company cited higher sales of its optical film products and a higher exchange rate as the drivers of 2025 operating profit growth. In the second quarter of 2026 it pointed to strong sales of high value-added products, cost savings from production efficiency, and the effect of customer diversification.

Barrier film demand is tied to wider ESL adoption by large retailers and to QD TV products being applied in ESL and monitors. Its medium-term growth area is barrier films for film-type perovskite solar cells, which need protection from moisture and oxygen.

The company supplied such a film to a European flexible solar cell maker in 2024 and currently provides samples to about 30 companies at home and abroad. Mass-production tests with Japanese firms are under way, and development of films for solar and space uses is also being pursued.

In materials, it overlaps with listed peers such as Soulbrain, Dongjin Semichem and Hansol Chemical, but product mix and revenue scale differ, so direct comparison is limited. CEO Kim Yang-guk held 15.42% as of the May 22, 2026 filing and is described as the de facto controlling shareholder.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results started from 2022 sales of KRW 35.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.93 billion. In 2023 sales fell 22.5% year on year to KRW 27.3 billion and the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 2.15 billion.

In 2024 sales rose 35.6% to KRW 37.1 billion and operating profit returned to KRW 2.6 billion, making 2024 the largest sales year in the 2022 to 2025 span. 2025 sales fell 6.6% to KRW 34.6 billion, while operating profit rose 132.6% to KRW 6.04 billion.

Profit grew while sales shrank, so the margin improvement mattered more than top-line size. The company cited higher optical film sales and a higher exchange rate as the drivers of 2025 operating profit. The operating margin went from 8.3% in 2022 to -7.9% in 2023, 7.0% in 2024 and 17.4% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.25 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 2.69 billion in 2023, KRW 3.29 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.01 billion in 2025, up 82.4%. Net income including non-controlling interests for 2025 was also KRW 6.01 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 9.62 billion in 2022, KRW 665 million in 2023, KRW 5.87 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.51 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 60.8% in 2022 to 34.2% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 44.3 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities of KRW 15.2 billion at year-end 2025. On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 sales were KRW 10.3 billion with operating profit of KRW 2.61 billion.

Q4 2025 sales fell 34.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 6.82 billion and operating profit fell 81.8% to KRW 474 million. Q1 2026 sales were KRW 9.74 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.66 billion, up 249.4% quarter on quarter.

Q2 2026 sales reached KRW 11.9 billion and operating profit KRW 3.6 billion, up 117.3% quarter on quarter.

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 7.56 billion, down 36.3% quarter on quarter and 26.9% year on year, with operating profit of KRW 473 million, down 81.9% year on year, and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 882 million.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, totaled KRW 6.56 billion. The reasons behind the simultaneous drop in Q3 sales and operating profit are a matter to be confirmed in the next periodic report's business discussion.

05

Industry analysis

End demand has two legs, display and solar. In display, wider ESL adoption by large retailers is cited by an independent research report as a driver of higher barrier film sales. Extension of QD TV products into monitors and ESL is also cited as broadening the demand base.

In solar, film-type perovskite cells are light and flexible but sensitive to moisture and oxygen, which is the demand case for the company's barrier films. Solar and space films under development are presented as a way to widen medium-term customers.

On the cycle, existing display products move with TV and monitor set demand. Solar barrier films remain at sample and mass-production testing, so their revenue contribution has not yet become material.

On production, coating line utilization in the first half of 2026 was 81.7%, and in June 2026 the company decided on a KRW 5.5 billion expansion to meet display and solar demand. Against same-sector materials peers such as Soulbrain and Dongjin Semichem, the gap in revenue scale and product range is wide. The supply of samples to about 30 companies shows a broadening customer base in barrier films.

06

Outlook

When it announced Q2 2026 results on August 11, 2026, the company said it would sustain growth through a higher mix of high value-added products and customer diversification. It also said it would accelerate mass-production preparation for barrier films for perovskite solar cells.

An independent research report dated November 12, 2026 expects earnings growth to continue on higher barrier coating film sales and capacity expansion.

The same report puts first-half 2026 sales at KRW 21.6 billion, up 23.6% year on year, and operating profit at about KRW 5.3 billion, up 77.9%, which matches the sum of Q1 and Q2 quarterly results. The report cites wider ESL adoption by large retailers as the basis for higher sales.

The coating line expansion is targeted for completion in February 2027, so how utilization changes afterward is the next point to watch. Mass-production timing for perovskite barrier films is not yet specified, and it will determine the medium-term revenue mix.

On earnings, the Q3 2026 sales decline and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders will be explained in the next periodic report's business discussion. Whether Q4 sales and operating profit recover toward Q2 levels is a related point to confirm.

Signs of wider applications

Wider ESL adoption by large retailers and QD TV products moving into monitors are cited as drivers of barrier film sales. The company supplied a European flexible solar cell maker in 2024 and currently provides samples to about 30 companies at home and abroad. Whether sample customers convert into revenue is the next point to confirm.

Financial and production capacity

The debt-to-equity ratio was 34.2% at the end of 2025, with total equity of KRW 44.3 billion and total liabilities of KRW 15.2 billion. Operating cash flow recovered to KRW 8.51 billion in 2025 from KRW 665 million in 2023.

Coating line utilization in the first half of 2026 was 81.7%, and production capacity will change once the KRW 5.5 billion expansion is completed.

Bear factors

Volatile quarterly earnings

Q3 2026 sales fell 26.9% year on year to KRW 7.56 billion, and operating profit fell 81.9% to KRW 473 million. The company swung to a KRW 882 million net loss attributable to controlling shareholders. Q4 2025 operating profit also fell 81.8% quarter on quarter to KRW 474 million.

Sales declines

2025 sales fell 6.6% to KRW 34.6 billion after the 2024 rebound, and 2023 sales had fallen 22.5% to KRW 27.3 billion. The decline in 2025 after 2024's recovery shows top-line growth is not yet stable. Because profit improved while sales shrank, whether revenue recovers matters.

Uncertain timing of monetization

Perovskite barrier films remain at sample and testing stages, so the timing of revenue contribution is not specified. The CEO's stake reduction was reported in filings in September 2025 and May 2026. The timing of new business profits and the stake changes remain bearish considerations.

Risk factors

Display application concentration

Barrier films and optical films are both tied to display applications, so they are sensitive to changes in TV, monitor and ESL demand. The company cited optical film sales and a higher exchange rate together as drivers of 2025 profit, so exchange rates and set demand can move results.

Earnings swings and renewed losses

Operating losses appeared in 2023, and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders appeared in Q3 2026. With quarterly margins swinging sharply, an annual profit does not prevent quarterly losses. The four-quarter total of KRW 6.56 billion gives limited cushion.

Disclosure and governance

In April 2025 the exchange announced an intention to designate the company as an unfaithful disclosure entity over changes to a single sales contract disclosure. The CEO's stake reduction was disclosed in September 2025 and May 2026. Both items are tracked through the company's disclosure record.

Overall view

i-Components moved from a 2023 operating loss to a 2024 return to profit and a 2025 operating profit of KRW 6.04 billion, up 132.6%, restoring its earnings base. The operating margin reached 17.4% in 2025, above 8.3% in 2022, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell to 34.2%.

Total equity of KRW 44.3 billion and total liabilities of KRW 15.2 billion at year-end 2025 support its capacity to invest.

On the other hand, 2025 sales fell 6.6% to KRW 34.6 billion, and Q3 2026 sales of KRW 7.56 billion and a KRW 882 million net loss attributable to controlling shareholders show quarterly results weakening again.

Barrier film growth spans ESL and QD TV applications and perovskite solar films, where about 30 sample customers and Japanese mass-production tests are under way, but the revenue contribution timing is not specified.

First-half utilization of 81.7% and the expansion targeted for February 2027 are variables for production capacity.

On valuation, market capitalization sits below year-end 2025 total equity, which reads as a discount to net asset value, but wide quarterly swings in earnings also widen the range of how multiples can be interpreted.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 and full-year 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual report, and utilization after the expansion is completed.
