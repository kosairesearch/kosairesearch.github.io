02

Business structure

TNE Korea, founded in 2014, is a high-speed turbomachinery specialist that develops and manufactures blowers and compressors based on air bearing and high-speed motor technology.

The business began with turbo blowers for water treatment and industrial processes and has expanded into hydrogen fuel cell power air blowers, compressors for hydrogen vehicles, and on-site power turbomachinery for AI data centers.

According to the securities registration statement, 2025 revenue was about KRW 22.3 billion, with eco-friendly industrial turbomachinery at roughly 38.1% and on-site power turbomachinery at roughly 37.2%. Eco-friendly automotive turbomachinery accounted for about 14.9%, aviation about 7.1%, and marine about 1.7%.

Press reports put the same year's revenue at KRW 23.4 to 23.5 billion, so the figures differ by reporting basis. The registration statement projected on-site power revenue at about KRW 30.9 billion in 2026 and about KRW 73.3 billion in 2027, positioning this segment as the growth axis.

On competition, the company's compact high-speed turbomachinery platform received A and BBB ratings from two specialized evaluation agencies, which the company presents as a differentiator.

Because the portfolio concentrates on hydrogen and AI data center power equipment, a direct comparison with large power equipment makers is limited. Five major external customers accounted for more than half of 2025 revenue, indicating high customer concentration.

Overseas revenue rose to KRW 8.2 billion in 2025, and the company supplies high-speed turbo air blowers to a US SOFC power company.

03

Recent trends

Based on the cumulative figures disclosed through the third quarter of 2026, revenue reached KRW 10.9 billion, up 35.8% from the same period a year earlier. Operating profit for the same period was KRW 1.15 billion, up 439.0% year on year, which points to improved profitability at the core business.

The net loss of KRW 389 million, however, widened from the prior-year period and shows that the company has not yet reached net profitability.

Press reports state that first-half 2026 revenue was about KRW 21.1 billion with operating profit of about KRW 3.5 billion, turning the company profitable, and that first-half revenue rose 180% year on year.

The gap between the first-half revenue reported in the press and the KRW 10.9 billion cumulative figure in the third-quarter disclosure is large, so the reporting scope difference should be reconciled against the quarterly report.

For full-year 2025, press reports put revenue at about KRW 23.4 billion and an operating loss of about KRW 1.8 billion, with revenue up 45.8% year on year. The 2025 audit report reportedly showed a net loss of KRW 7.1 billion, negative equity of KRW 5.1 billion, and a material uncertainty regarding going concern.

The stock opened on October 29, 2026 more than doubled from the IPO price of KRW 15,300 at its intraday high of KRW 34,500. As of November 18, 2026, the share price of KRW 15,280 sits near the IPO price, with market capitalization of KRW 173.4 billion and trading value of KRW 10.4 billion.

04

Outlook

The company has set a 2026 annual revenue target of KRW 55.3 billion and a 2027 target of KRW 96.6 billion. Production capacity, currently about KRW 50 billion on a revenue basis, is targeted to reach KRW 171.0 billion in 2027 and more than KRW 210.0 billion by 2030.

The KRW 36.72 billion raised in the listing is planned for a second plant expansion, production automation, and new air blower and recirculation blower lines.

In hydrogen power, the company is pursuing supply of a four-part balance-of-plant package consisting of air blowers, recirculation blowers, inverters, and start-up blowers. It already supplies major domestic fuel cell companies and is in talks with European SOFC companies.

However, reaching the 2026 annual target of KRW 55.3 billion from cumulative third-quarter revenue of KRW 10.9 billion would require about KRW 44.4 billion in the fourth quarter, more than twice the first-half revenue of about KRW 21.1 billion.

On the ownership side, the largest shareholder and key shareholders holding 60.12% are subject to a three-year sales restriction, and the free float is scheduled to rise from 31.27% at listing to 46.99% one month later and 68.91% three months later.

Fourth-quarter results and the lock-up release schedule will show both the degree of progress toward the company's targets and share price volatility.

05

Bull factors

Scale of the cumulative profit turnaround

Cumulative operating profit through the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 1.15 billion, up 439.0% from a year earlier. First-half revenue grew 180% year on year, so sales expansion and profit improvement appeared together. The registration statement estimated 2027 operating profit at about KRW 19.5 billion.

Demand from SOFC and AI data center power

Solid oxide fuel cell power generation for AI data centers is presented as the company's main growth axis. It co-developed power air blowers with a US SOFC company, signed a supply contract, and entered mass production in 2026.

Hydrogen power air blower revenue reached KRW 761.6 million in the first half of 2026, accounting for 36.1% of total revenue.

Capacity expansion and funding secured

The listing raised KRW 36.72 billion, which is planned for a second plant, production automation, and related investment. The company targets production capacity of KRW 171.0 billion in 2027 and more than KRW 210.0 billion by 2030.

Given current capacity of roughly KRW 50 billion on a revenue basis, the pace of facility expansion is the key to scaling sales.

06

Bear factors

Persistent net losses and capital erosion concerns

The cumulative net loss through the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 389 million, and it widened from the prior-year period. The 2025 audit report included a material uncertainty about going concern, and total equity was negative at KRW 5.1 billion. Press reports put the 2025 net loss at KRW 7.1 billion.

Customer concentration and earnings volatility

In 2025, five major external customers accounted for more than half of total revenue. Because the business is project-based equipment and component supply, revenue tied to specific customers and projects can amplify earnings swings. Overseas revenue rose to KRW 8.2 billion, but customer concentration remains a weakness.

Gap between sales targets and disclosed results

Cumulative third-quarter revenue of KRW 10.9 billion is roughly 20% of the 2026 annual target of KRW 55.3 billion. The 2027 target of KRW 96.6 billion is more than four times 2025 revenue and depends on large order wins and facility ramp-up. Free float is rising in stages after the listing, which adds a supply-side pressure.

07

Risk factors

Macro

If US AI data center investment slows, orders for on-site power equipment could be delayed. As overseas sales grow, currency movements can affect profitability. The interest rate environment also links to the cost of funding facility investment.

Policy and industry

Changes in hydrogen economy and fuel cell deployment policy directly affect demand for hydrogen power air blowers. Hydrogen-related products accounted for 45.7% of revenue in the first half of 2026, so exposure to policy shifts is substantial. Intensifying technological competition in high-speed turbomachinery could also pressure pricing and order wins.

Supply and liquidity

Free float at listing was 31.27% of total shares, and the schedule raises it to 46.99% after one month and 68.91% after three months. When institutional holdings become eligible for sale, share price volatility could increase. The share price swung widely around the IPO price in the first days of trading.

08

Overall view

TNE Korea's cumulative operating profit through the third quarter of 2026 rose 439.0% year on year, showing a profitable trend at the operating level, but the net loss of KRW 389 million means the turnaround is not complete at the net profit level.

On the business side, SOFC-based on-site power and hydrogen air blower demand have taken shape as growth drivers, and funding for capacity expansion has been secured.

Conversely, the going concern uncertainty in the 2025 audit report, negative equity, and customer concentration remain weaknesses in financial stability and earnings volatility.

The 2026 annual revenue target of KRW 55.3 billion requires a large fourth-quarter revenue build compared with cumulative third-quarter revenue of KRW 10.9 billion. The share price moved widely relative to the IPO price after listing and currently sits near that level.

During the period of rising free float and quarterly disclosures, profitability indicators and progress toward targets are the key metrics to track.
