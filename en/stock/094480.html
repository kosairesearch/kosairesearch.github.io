02

Business structure

GalaxiaMoneytree is a digital finance company with two core businesses: electronic payment (PG) and online-to-offline (O2O) services.

According to a March 30, 2026 report by Korea IR Association, electronic payment accounted for 74.7% of 2025 revenue of KRW 129.7 billion, and O2O, including convenience store payments, accounted for 25.3%. The electronic payment unit offers mobile phone payments as well as credit card and account transfer payments.

On a preliminary cumulative basis announced on November 16, 2026, credit card payment revenue rose 7.3% to KRW 39.1 billion, which the company attributed to higher payment volume for delivery service insurance premiums and a wider merchant base led by hospitals and clinics.

Mobile phone payment revenue on the same preliminary basis rose 2.1% to KRW 34.0 billion. The O2O unit consists of mobile gift vouchers and coupons, convenience store prepaid payments and the Moneytree Card prepaid card.

Preliminary cumulative O2O revenue rose 96.5% to KRW 30.1 billion, with voucher and coupon sales up 51.2% to KRW 11.1 billion. The Moneytree Card grew on the back of an expanded corporate welfare partner network, and the company said its revenue rose about 38% in the first quarter of 2026.

On the customer side, direct sales of department store vouchers through Naver and vouchers sold via large online fashion platforms increased.

In competition, IB Tomato reported on November 11, 2026 that the company is one of three major players in mobile phone payments and holds the top sales share in mobile vouchers, with a convenience store prepaid card share above 90%.

The company belongs to the Hyosung group, and related parties including chairman Cho Hyun-jun held about 54.7% of shares as of the end of the first half of 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 112.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 132.4 billion in 2023 (up 17.9%), making 2023 the largest year of 2022–2025, before easing to KRW 128.8 billion in 2024 (down 2.7%) and recovering to KRW 129.7 billion in 2025 (up 0.7%).

Operating profit moved differently, going from KRW 8.6 billion to KRW 9.24 billion, KRW 13.2 billion and KRW 18.4 billion, rising for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year of the period.

The operating margin fell from 7.7% in 2022 to 7.0% in 2023 before rising to 10.3% in 2024 and 14.2% in 2025, so margin improvement drove profit growth even when revenue barely moved.

Korea IR Association said the company entered a profitability rebound after 2023, which it attributed to trimming low-margin merchants and focusing on higher-quality ones.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from KRW 5.42 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 47.09 million in 2023, returned to profit at KRW 811 million in 2024, and reached KRW 8.3 billion in 2025, up 923.0%.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 351 million in 2023, KRW 557 million in 2024 and KRW 8.2 billion in 2025.

Cash flow moved in the opposite direction: operating cash flow was KRW 26.6 billion in 2022 and KRW 20.7 billion in 2023, then turned negative at -KRW 26.6 billion in 2024 and -KRW 1.52 billion in 2025.

IB Tomato said that paying merchants in advance for mobile microtransactions and Moneytree Card top-ups raises working capital needs, and it pointed to repeated one-off costs, including tax audit assessments, a impairment on virtual assets and a KRW 6.6 billion impairment on receivables tied to Ticket Monster and WeMakePrice.

Quarterly revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, but operating profit swung quarter to quarter, falling 15.7% to KRW 4.05 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and 15.1% to KRW 4.76 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders dropped 94.8% to KRW 136 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 before recovering to KRW 2.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 3.63 billion in the second quarter, and the four-quarter total from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 8.62 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026, the company said selling and administrative expenses rose on STO license preparation and hiring, and it booked loan loss provisions early as mobile payment volume grew.

05

Industry analysis

The market GalaxiaMoneytree serves combines mobile phone payments, credit card payments, mobile gift vouchers and convenience store prepaid payments.

In the mobile voucher segment, sales fell after the 2025 Ticket Monster and WeMakePrice incident, but IB Tomato reported that the Moneytree Card helped keep annual revenue at about KRW 130 billion.

On regulation, an amendment to the Electronic Financial Transactions Act requires settlement funds to be placed in external trusts at a 100% ratio by 2028, which will reduce available cash.

The corporate welfare channel is linked to the Moneytree Card's partner expansion, so welfare demand feeds directly into card top-up volume.

In digital assets, stablecoin-based payments are at an early stage; the company completed a stablecoin payment proof of concept with Sebitsum in July 2026 and announced further tests with Cedeck and Mewju.

For cross-border payments, the company said in July 2026 that it would work with Heypay to connect Alipay and WeChat Pay. In investment brokerage for beneficiary certificates, the license is the key variable, and the company applied for it in September 2025.

Because consumer demand and corporate welfare spending move together, transaction volume and card top-up volume are the main indicators of the business cycle for this company.

A clear industry-level cycle measure is not available, so the company's disclosed revenue trend and policy timelines are the more practical reference.

06

Outlook

In its November 16, 2026 announcement, the company said it would sustain growth in its existing business by securing new merchants and expanding voucher sales. It is also pursuing its own Moneytree voucher, foreign-customer simple payments and stablecoin-linked overseas remittance.

On June 1, 2026, the Moneytree app added overseas use and Zero Pay payments, and the company said it plans to link global payments to the Moneytree Card in the second half of 2026 to support overseas offline payments.

The STO business was moved to the affiliate Galaxia FN, as announced on August 4, 2026, and that affiliate's license application for investment brokerage of beneficiary certificates has been completed. After approval, the company plans to resume STO on aircraft lease assets that had been delayed.

In a March 2026 Korea IR Association report, the target was to issue the first aircraft engine STO within the year. On stablecoins, the company completed a proof of concept with Sebitsum and is pursuing Alipay and WeChat Pay linkage through Heypay.

The Korea IR Association's March 2026 report expected the Moneytree Card's economics and STO-related revenue to support continued improvement. Selling and administrative expenses are rising on STO license preparation and hiring, so how these new-business costs appear in results is the next checkpoint.

The timing of the license and the revenue contribution from new payment services are not yet separated in disclosed results, so they will need to be checked against the periodic reports.

08

Bull factors

Shift in earnings structure

The operating margin climbed from 7.7% in 2022 to 14.2% in 2025, reflecting fewer low-margin merchants and more higher-margin business such as the Moneytree Card. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 8.3 billion in 2025 after returning to profit in 2024. Korea IR Association cited this shift as the basis for a profitability rebound from a 2023 low.

Moneytree Card and O2O growth

The company said Moneytree Card revenue grew about 38% in the first quarter of 2026. IB Tomato reported that monthly top-up volume rose from about KRW 3 billion at launch to KRW 24 billion in June 2026.

The number one position in mobile voucher sales and a convenience store prepaid card share above 90% underpin this business.

New business options

The STO business applied for a beneficiary certificate investment brokerage license in September 2025 and plans to resume STO on aircraft lease assets after approval.

The Moneytree app added overseas use and Zero Pay payments in June 2026, and a stablecoin payment proof of concept with Sebitsum was completed in July 2026. Revenue from these businesses is not separately broken out in the disclosed results.

Bear factors

Persistent operating cash outflow

Operating cash flow was -KRW 26.6 billion in 2024 and -KRW 1.52 billion in 2025. IB Tomato noted that advance settlement balances rose to KRW 263.2 billion by the end of 2025. Because merchants are paid in advance, earnings growth does not always convert into cash.

Higher borrowings and short-term repayment burden

The debt ratio rose from 193.7% in 2022 to 236.9% in 2025, and IB Tomato reported 240.1% with total borrowings of KRW 138.6 billion at the end of June 2026. Short-term borrowings of KRW 135.3 billion were more than three times cash equivalents of KRW 44.1 billion and made up 97.6% of total borrowings.

IB Tomato also noted that KRW 92.2 billion of short-term borrowings are advance settlement loans backed by financial advances, so the effective repayment burden is lower than the ratio suggests.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 94.8% to KRW 136 million in the fourth quarter of 2025. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.76 billion was down 1.1% year on year.

In the first quarter of 2026, hiring for STO preparation raised selling and administrative expenses and loan loss provisions were booked early, so revenue growth did not translate directly into profit growth.

10

Risk factors

Regulation and settlement funds

An amendment to the Electronic Financial Transactions Act requires settlement funds to be held in external trusts at a 100% ratio by 2028, which will reduce the cash available to the company.

Of cash equivalents of KRW 44.1 billion at the end of June 2026, KRW 34.1 billion was entrusted to external financial institutions. STO business launch could slip depending on license approval and timing.

Counterparty credit risk

As mobile payment volume expanded, the company booked loan loss provisions early in the first quarter of 2026. It has previously recorded a KRW 6.6 billion impairment on receivables tied to Ticket Monster and WeMakePrice. Expanding large merchants raises revenue but also increases exposure to collecting settlement payments.

Controlling shareholder group and rating structure

Related parties of the Hyosung group hold about 54.7% of shares, so control is concentrated in one group.

IB Tomato reported on November 11, 2026 that Nice Information Service kept the commercial paper rating at A3 and placed it one notch above the standalone credit profile, reflecting possible extraordinary support from the Hyosung group. The rating assumes such support, which is worth keeping in view.

Overall view

GalaxiaMoneytree's disclosed results show the operating margin rising from 7.7% in 2022 to 14.2% in 2025, a change in its earnings base.

Operating profit reached KRW 18.4 billion in 2025 and net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 8.3 billion, a larger earnings base after the return to profit in 2024.

However, operating cash flow was negative at -KRW 26.6 billion in 2024 and -KRW 1.52 billion in 2025, and the debt ratio rose to 236.9% in 2025, so the balance between cash and borrowing remains an open issue.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 36 billion rose 9.5% year on year while operating profit fell 1.1%, and revenue has grown for four consecutive quarters even as quarterly profit has moved up and down.

Strengths include O2O growth led by the Moneytree Card, the number one position in mobile vouchers, and progress in overseas use and stablecoin businesses. Weaknesses include the settlement fund structure and borrowing concentration, repeated one-off costs, and concentration of control in one group.

These factors deserve equal weight, and the current share price sits at a premium to book net assets. The next checkpoints are whether operating cash flow turns positive in the 2026 annual report, whether the STO license is granted, and whether global payment linkage to the Moneytree Card goes live.
