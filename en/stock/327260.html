02

Business structure

RF Materials makes packages and heat-dissipation parts that protect compound semiconductor devices. Revenue falls into two main lines, communications packages and military equipment parts.

According to a Daily Invest report, the first-half 2026 revenue mix was 45% communications packages, 43% military equipment parts, 6% military packages, 1% laser packages and 5% other. Military equipment parts revenue reached KRW 33.6 billion in 2025, up 22% from the prior year, according to Bloter.

Its key communications customer is US optical component maker Lumentum, and Shinyoung Securities analysts describe RF Materials as the sole supplier of Lumentum's pump laser packages.

A supply contract for Lumentum-linked pump laser packages with Metallife-USA, worth KRW 7.29 billion, was disclosed in April 2026 and runs to October 23, 2026. The company positions pump laser packages for AI data center links and co-packaged optics (CPO) as its core growth axes.

Results of its subsidiary RF Systems are consolidated into group earnings, and Hana Securities said subsidiary performance modestly improved in the second quarter of 2026 and supported the group. The largest shareholder is RFHIC, with a 41.54% stake as of March 31, 2026.

Against domestic optical packaging peers, brokers cite inclusion in Nvidia's CPO value chain as a differentiator.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue was KRW 50.4 billion in 2022, KRW 47.9 billion in 2023 (down 5.0% year on year), KRW 44.5 billion in 2024 (down 7.1%) and KRW 64.1 billion in 2025 (up 44.0%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 2.73 billion in 2022, turned into an operating loss of KRW 34.63 million in 2023, and the loss continued in 2024 at KRW 1.45 billion before the company returned to an operating profit of KRW 7.37 billion in 2025. Operating margin was 5.4% in 2022, -0.1% in 2023, -3.3% in 2024 and 11.5% in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.34 billion in 2022, followed by losses of KRW 669 million in 2023 and KRW 4.79 billion in 2024, before a KRW 7.06 billion profit in 2025. Net income of KRW 10.4 billion in 2025 includes non-controlling interests and should be read separately from that figure.

Operating cash flow rose to KRW 7.16 billion in 2025 from KRW 2.01 billion in 2024, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell to 48.4% at the end of 2025 from 81.5% at the end of 2022.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 14.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 21.1 billion in the fourth quarter (up 44.8% quarter on quarter), KRW 20 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 5.2%), KRW 25.1 billion in the second quarter (up 25.2%) and KRW 24.8 billion in the third quarter (down 1.2%, up 69.7% year on year).

Operating profit moved from KRW 1.89 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.77 billion, KRW 3.2 billion and KRW 5.29 billion (up 65.5% quarter on quarter) in the second quarter of 2026, then eased to KRW 4.87 billion (down 7.9%) in the third quarter.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.08 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (up 134.7% quarter on quarter), then KRW 2.45 billion, KRW 3.8 billion and KRW 2.74 billion through the third quarter of 2026, and the four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026 total KRW 13.1 billion.

Analysts attributed the second-quarter revenue increase to an 80% year-on-year rise in Lumentum's pump laser shipments in that quarter, according to Daily Invest.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for optical packaging is tied to the spread of AI data centers. According to Daily Invest, demand for pump lasers used in long-distance links between AI data centers is surging, and brokers view RF Materials as a direct beneficiary.

Shinyoung Securities analysts said strong growth in downstream industries is lifting volumes and optical component prices at the same time.

Korea Investment & Securities described RF Materials as a pure-play optical company and said the 2027 start of CPO mass production, combined with rising Lumentum volumes, brightens its medium- to long-term earnings outlook.

Some analysts also argue that expanded US telecom network investment will lift RF demand from the second half onward, according to PinPoint News. On the supply side, the exit of Chinese telecom equipment and the withdrawal of global chipmakers from telecom create gaps that some expect RFHIC and RF Materials could fill.

That view remains an expectation, and the only publicly disclosed single supply contract in this set is the April 2026 deal worth KRW 7.29 billion. Because military equipment parts account for 43% of revenue, defense demand is also a source of earnings variability.

In the optical competitive landscape, brokers point to inclusion in Nvidia's CPO value chain and sole supply of pump laser packages as differentiators from domestic peers.

06

Outlook

The capacity plan starts with the shareholder rights offering decided in April 2026. The offering, initially sized at about KRW 28.2 billion, shrank in proceeds after the final issue price was set at KRW 52,500 (according to BusinessPost).

Of the proceeds, KRW 25 billion for facilities goes to purchasing land in Ansan, Gyeonggi and building a new plant, which is targeted for completion in the fourth quarter of 2027. Hana Securities noted that once the new plant is completed, production capacity would be roughly double the level before the expansion.

Production of Raman packages and EDFA packages is to be increased in sequence from the second half of 2026. Lumentum has said it plans to expand pump laser shipments fourfold over several quarters, according to Shinyoung Securities.

In a July 28, 2026 report, Hana Securities projected operating profit above KRW 20 billion for 2026 and about KRW 30 billion for 2027, and presented a target price of KRW 75,000 adjusted for the bonus issue. These are broker estimates and should be read separately from company disclosures.

The next regular filing is the 2026 annual report, where full-year operating margin and facility investment spending are the next checkpoints.

08

Bull factors

Lumentum shipment growth

Shinyoung Securities reported that Lumentum's pump laser shipments rose 80% year on year in the second quarter of 2026 and that Lumentum plans to expand shipments fourfold over several quarters. Revenue in that quarter was KRW 25.1 billion, up 25.2% quarter on quarter. Demand for pump lasers in long-distance data center links is cited as the basis for growth.

Earnings base after the turnaround

Operating margin improved from -3.3% in 2024 to 11.5% in 2025. Operating profit in the second quarter of 2026 was KRW 5.29 billion, up 65.5% quarter on quarter and the highest of the last five quarters. Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 7.16 billion, up from KRW 2.01 billion in 2024.

Capacity expansion and product breadth

The new plant targeted for completion in the fourth quarter of 2027 is planned to roughly double capacity versus the pre-expansion level. Raman and EDFA package output is also set to rise in sequence from the second half of 2026.

Korea Investment & Securities views the 2027 start of CPO mass production, combined with rising volumes, as supportive of medium- to long-term earnings.

09

Bear factors

Customer concentration and contract renewal

Communications packages are reported to be a sole-supply arrangement for Lumentum's pump laser packages, so order swings from one customer flow straight into results. The Metallife-USA supply contract disclosed in April 2026 ran to October 23, 2026. The terms and size of any follow-on contract must be confirmed through future disclosures.

Dilution and share price volatility

The April 2026 rights offering was announced at a planned issue price 20% below the reference price, and the final issue price was set at KRW 52,500. A one-for-one bonus issue then raised the listed share count to 17,988,839 shares. The stock was also designated an investment caution stock for one day in March 2026.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Third-quarter 2026 revenue fell 1.2% quarter on quarter, operating profit fell 7.9%, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 27.9%. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders also fell 39.9% in the first quarter of 2026, showing wide swings between quarters. Because military equipment parts make up 43% of revenue, quarterly results can move with defense demand.

10

Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

A large share of revenue depends on pump laser packages tied to Lumentum, so order changes flow directly into results. The pace of the customer's CPO transition and whether alternative suppliers expand are also variables to watch. The more dependent revenue is on a single product line, the larger the impact of this risk.

Capacity execution

The new plant's completion target is the fourth quarter of 2027, and any delay would push back the capacity gains. The execution of the KRW 25 billion facility budget and the trend in utilization must be checked in regular filings. If demand slows after expansion, fixed cost burden could rise.

Ownership and group dependence

The largest shareholder, RFHIC, held a 41.54% stake as of March 31, 2026. Its plan to take up 100% of the rights offering is a stabilizing factor, but dependence on the parent's RF package sales and on subsidiary RF Systems results also needs to be considered.

What to watch next

  1. January to March 2027

    The 2026 fourth-quarter results and the 2026 annual report will fix full-year revenue and operating margin. The key checks are whether fourth-quarter revenue holds near the third-quarter 2026 level of KRW 24.8 billion and whether operating margin stays near the 11.5% of 2025.

  2. End of December 2026

    This is the point to confirm whether Raman and EDFA package output is rising in sequence from the second half of 2026. The next question is whether the output increase translates into higher quarterly revenue.

  3. Fourth quarter of 2027

    This is the target completion date for the new plant. Whether it is completed on schedule and whether capacity reaches roughly double the pre-expansion level will determine the payoff from the expansion.

  4. During 2027

    Two items need confirmation: the timing of CPO mass production and whether the planned fourfold expansion of Lumentum pump laser shipments materializes in actual volumes. These two are the core assumptions behind the medium- to long-term earnings outlook.

12

Overall view

Since swinging to operating profit in 2025, RF Materials' quarterly revenue has stayed between KRW 14.6 billion and KRW 25.1 billion through the third quarter of 2026.

Revenue in 2025 was KRW 64.1 billion with operating profit of KRW 7.37 billion, and third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 24.8 billion with operating profit of KRW 4.87 billion.

Growth rests on Lumentum-linked pump laser packages, where second-quarter shipment gains and the planned fourfold expansion are cited as strengths. Customer concentration, dilution from a discounted rights offering and a bonus issue, and swings in quarterly results remain the main weaknesses.

The new plant is targeted for completion in the fourth quarter of 2027, so the full effect of the expansion is expected to show from 2027. Broker forecasts assume continued earnings growth, and valuation views diverge depending on how fast that growth comes.

The next checkpoints are the full-year operating margin in the 2026 annual report, the new plant's completion schedule, and the Lumentum shipment trend.
