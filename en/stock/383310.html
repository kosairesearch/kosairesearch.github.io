02

Business structure

Ecopro HN was spun off in May 2021 from the environmental business of Ecopro and listed on KOSDAQ the same month. The company reports three business lines: clean room chemical filters, greenhouse gas reduction solutions, and fine dust reduction solutions.

For 2026, the company expects greenhouse gas reduction solutions to account for 40% of sales, with clean room chemical filters and fine dust reduction solutions at 26% each. By end market, semiconductors are the largest at 62%, followed by secondary batteries at 12%, power generation at 11%, and displays at 3%.

Greenhouse gas reduction equipment treats perfluorinated compounds (PFCs) generated in semiconductor production, and the company says its low-temperature catalytic decomposition technology cuts energy use by more than 90% compared with conventional thermal decomposition.

Because the equipment runs for long periods inside customer plants, it requires periodic inspection, catalyst replacement, and consumable supply, and the company targets operating revenue above half of total sales by 2030.

Its customer base has moved from semiconductor fabs in Cheongju, Korea, to semiconductor plants in the United States and Singapore and a power plant in Taiwan. Overseas accounted for 41% of cumulative 2026 orders, up from 22% in 2023.

Demand for semiconductor process environmental equipment moves with customers' fab expansion schedules, so order timing and revenue recognition can diverge from quarter to quarter.

The company says it is pursuing entry into semiconductor process materials, advanced packaging and HBM materials, and eco-friendly marine equipment.

04

Earnings analysis

The disclosed results show a reversal in both sales and profit between 2022 and 2025. Sales rose from KRW 218.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 228.9 billion in 2023 (up 4.9% year on year) and KRW 234.5 billion in 2024 (up 2.4%), then fell to KRW 141.1 billion in 2025 (down 39.8%).

Operating profit was KRW 41.4 billion in 2022 and KRW 41.8 billion in 2023 (up 0.9%), making 2023 the largest year in the period; it then fell to KRW 24.2 billion in 2024 (down 42.0%) and KRW 11.7 billion in 2025 (down 51.7%), declining for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

The operating margin narrowed from 19.0% in 2022 to 18.2% in 2023, 10.3% in 2024, and 8.3% in 2025. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 33.5 billion in 2023 (up 3.7%) to KRW 21.5 billion in 2024 (down 35.8%) and KRW 13.2 billion in 2025 (down 38.6%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests is disclosed at the same yearly amounts as the controlling-shareholder figure from 2023 to 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 58.2 billion in 2024 and turned negative at -KRW 4.87 billion in 2025.

Over the same period the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 112.8% in 2022 to 50.6% in 2025, lowering financial leverage.

Quarterly sales rose 38.1% in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 39.2 billion, after which the first quarter of 2026 (KRW 34.7 billion, down 11.6% from the prior quarter) was the low point; sales then rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 51.5 billion and KRW 52.8 billion.

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 2.31 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and rose for three consecutive quarters to KRW 5.03 billion, KRW 5.76 billion, and KRW 11.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 12.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 387.6% year on year, and the sum for the last four quarters is KRW 25.4 billion. The company attributed the third-quarter improvement to order growth tied to a strong semiconductor cycle and better product mix.

Whether order-backed projects convert into revenue from the fourth quarter onward is the next point to check for earnings durability.

05

Industry analysis

The semiconductor market that anchors demand for this company is being driven by new fab investment linked to the spread of AI. One press report said Korean chipmakers posted record exports in August 2026.

The company argues this investment expansion is raising demand for greenhouse gas reduction equipment that treats PFCs. PFCs are greenhouse gases with a global warming potential thousands to tens of thousands of times that of carbon dioxide, so demand for treatment equipment can rise as chip plants expand.

The group's secondary battery materials business, by contrast, saw sales decline in the third quarter of 2026 as North American EV demand slowed, which shows that end-market demand moves in different directions across the group.

Semiconductors make up 62% of this company's expected sales, so the extent to which results follow the chip investment cycle matters. On competition, the company says it commercialized the world's first high-capacity greenhouse gas treatment technology outside semiconductor fabs.

That is the company's own claim, and its technical position will show up in order wins and customer renewals. If chipmakers revise their facility investment plans, order pace and the timing of revenue recognition could shift together.

06

Outlook

The company expects order-backed environmental plant projects to begin converting into revenue in the fourth quarter of 2026, which it says should accelerate the earnings recovery.

As of the end of the third quarter of 2026, the greenhouse gas reduction segment held about 80% of the order backlog, and the company described project revenue recognition as the central driver of fourth-quarter results.

Two Micron-related orders, one in Singapore worth KRW 93.6 billion and one in Idaho in the United States worth KRW 19.8 billion, had reached about 13% revenue progress by the end of the third quarter of 2026.

That suggests conversion of these contracts into revenue is still early, and the quarter in which the remaining volume is recognized is the next point to check.

Reports differ on the 2030 target for operating revenue share, with one citing more than half of total sales and another citing 55%, so the company's own disclosure should be checked for the exact figure.

New business efforts include entry into HBM-related advanced packaging materials, preparation for HBM4E materials, next-generation battery materials, and eco-friendly marine equipment. Company IR material showed four orders totaling KRW 171.6 billion between March and August 2026. The company said it targets a dividend payout ratio of at least 30% and has prepared an interim dividend plan.

08

Bull factors

Cumulative orders exceed annual sales

Cumulative orders disclosed for 2026 reached KRW 191.4 billion, about 136% of 2025 sales. The overseas share rose to 41% from 22% in 2023, with customers extending to US semiconductor plants, a Singapore fab, and a Taiwan power plant. The order volume can be read as widening the revenue base.

Quarterly profit has risen for consecutive quarters

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 2.31 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and then rose for three consecutive quarters to KRW 11.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders has also risen for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. Whether this quarterly recovery continues will indicate how durable the earnings trend is.

Target to raise recurring operating revenue

The company says it aims to lift recurring operating revenue, from inspection, catalyst replacement, and consumable supply after equipment delivery, to more than half of total sales by 2030. If achieved, that could reduce earnings swings tied to equipment delivery volumes. Because the equipment runs for long periods, maintenance demand can grow with the installed base.

Bear factors

Profit fell for two consecutive years in 2024–2025

Operating profit fell from KRW 41.8 billion in 2023 to KRW 24.2 billion in 2024 (down 42.0%) and KRW 11.7 billion in 2025 (down 51.7%). The operating margin declined from 19.0% in 2022 to 8.3% in 2025. Sales in 2025 were KRW 141.1 billion, down 39.8%, so both top line and profit contracted.

Operating cash flow turned negative in 2025

Operating cash flow was KRW 12.8 billion in 2022 and KRW 5.44 billion in 2023, and the swing has been large across years. The negative reading coincides with falling sales and profit, confirming weaker cash generation.

Concentration with semiconductors at 62%

Semiconductors account for an expected 62% of sales by end market, followed by secondary batteries at 12%, power generation at 11%, and displays at 3%. With heavy reliance on one end market, slower chip capital spending could slow order intake. Smaller exposure to other end markets limits the diversification effect.

Risk factors

Project execution and revenue recognition timing

Equipment projects are installed to match customer plant operating schedules, so delays on site could push quarterly revenue into later periods. The company expects order-backed volume to convert from the fourth quarter, but schedule changes by customers are largely outside its control. Overseas sites may face timing differences tied to local permits and installation conditions.

FX and local regulation in overseas business

With overseas orders rising to 41%, exchange rate moves and local regulation in the United States, Singapore, and Taiwan carry more weight in contract execution and profitability. How cost and currency changes flow into overseas contract pricing needs to be confirmed from disclosures. Whether overseas expansion improves margins depends on local cost structures.

Liquidity at the group holding company

Reports put the largest shareholder's stake at 31.09%. The group holding company Ecopro reported standalone cash and equivalents falling from KRW 604.8 billion at the end of the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 392.2 billion at the end of the third quarter.

The holding company says it is paying in rights offering proceeds and carrying out investments. Changes in group cash flows could affect transaction terms and financing conditions among affiliates.

Overall view

Ecopro HN's operating profit peaked at KRW 41.8 billion in 2023, fell to KRW 11.7 billion in 2025, and recovered quarter by quarter to KRW 11.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026. The nine-month operating profit of KRW 22.1 billion is above full-year 2025 operating profit.

Cumulative orders disclosed for 2026 of KRW 191.4 billion equal about 136% of 2025 sales, so conversion of those orders into revenue is central to the next results. Strengths include order growth, a lower debt-to-equity ratio of 50.6% in 2025, and the target to expand recurring operating revenue.

Weaknesses include the profit decline in 2024–2025, the negative operating cash flow in 2025, and the 62% semiconductor concentration. The greenhouse gas reduction segment holds about 80% of the backlog, which makes quarterly results sensitive to project delivery timing.

The next points to check are fourth-quarter 2026 results, the revenue recognized in the 2026 annual report, and whether operating cash flow recovers. Overseas share expansion and group liquidity also warrant monitoring.
