Semiconductor Abatement Backlog Underpins Profit Recovery

02

Business structure

Ecopro HN was founded in May 2021 through a spin-off of the environmental business of Ecopro.

Its core lines are clean-room chemical filters that remove harmful gases from semiconductor processes, greenhouse-gas abatement solutions for perfluorinated compounds, and fine-dust reduction solutions for power plants and industrial sites.

In the sales mix the company presented at its September 2026 investor briefing, greenhouse-gas abatement accounts for 40% of expected 2026 revenue, clean-room chemical filters for 26%, and fine-dust reduction for 26%.

By end market, semiconductors make up 62%, secondary batteries 12%, power generation 11%, and displays 3%. Customers in 2026 orders include Samsung E&A, a U.S. customer, and Micron, and supply has expanded to semiconductor fabs in the United States and Singapore and to a power plant in Taiwan.

Abatement systems require catalyst replacement, routine inspection, and consumables for years after delivery, which builds recurring operating revenue.

The company says it commercialized large-capacity greenhouse-gas treatment outside semiconductor fabs for the first time in the world and offers integrated design, construction, and operation management based on its own catalyst technology.

Its materials business is expanding into advanced packaging and HBM materials, as well as CCUS, eco-friendly water treatment, and by-product recycling. Unlike battery-material affiliates in the group, its earnings depend mainly on equipment and maintenance revenue rather than metal prices.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 218.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 228.9 billion in 2023 (up 4.9% year on year) and KRW 234.5 billion in 2024 (up 2.4%), then fell to KRW 141.1 billion in 2025 (down 39.8%).

Operating profit reached KRW 41.8 billion in 2023, the peak of the period, after KRW 41.4 billion in 2022, and then declined two years in a row to KRW 24.2 billion in 2024 (down 42.0%) and KRW 11.7 billion in 2025 (down 51.7%).

The operating margin narrowed from 19.0% in 2022 to 18.2% in 2023, 10.3% in 2024, and 8.3% in 2025. In 2025 the drop in operating profit was larger than the drop in revenue, so falling sales and a lower margin occurred together.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 32.4 billion in 2022 and KRW 33.5 billion in 2023 to KRW 21.5 billion in 2024 (down 35.8%) and KRW 13.2 billion in 2025 (down 38.6%), again declining for two years.

The debt-to-equity ratio improved from 112.8% in 2022 to 50.6% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 304.7 billion and total liabilities of KRW 154.2 billion at the end of 2025. Operating cash flow swung from KRW 58.2 billion in 2024 to -KRW 4.87 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 28.4 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 39.2 billion in Q4 2025 (up 38.1% quarter on quarter), dipped to KRW 34.7 billion in Q1 2026 (down 11.6%), and then rose to KRW 51.5 billion in Q2 2026 (up 48.6%) and KRW 52.8 billion in Q3 2026 (up 2.5%), marking two straight quarters of growth.

Operating profit rose for three straight quarters from the Q4 2025 low of KRW 2.31 billion to KRW 5.03 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 5.76 billion in Q2 2026, and KRW 11.3 billion in Q3 2026.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders has grown for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, and the KRW 12.2 billion posted in Q3 2026 is close to the full-year 2025 figure of KRW 13.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The main end market is semiconductors. According to a September 2026 report, Korean chipmakers' August exports hit a record high, and global chipmakers are accelerating capacity expansion. The company argues that new fab investment driven by the spread of AI is feeding demand for greenhouse-gas abatement equipment.

Perfluorinated compounds emitted from semiconductor processes have a global warming potential thousands to tens of thousands of times higher than carbon dioxide, so regulatory-driven demand is rising alongside investment.

A Wisereport company summary cites AI infrastructure investment, the adoption of sub-3-nanometer processes, and tighter global carbon-emission rules as growth drivers.

In terms of cycle position, the company is in a phase of expanding chip investment, but equipment orders in semiconductors can fall quickly when the industry slows, which is a bear point. Overseas, supply has widened to chip plants in the United States and Singapore and to a power plant in Taiwan.

On competition, the company points to its commercialization of large-capacity greenhouse-gas treatment outside fabs and its proprietary catalyst technology. If chip-industry conditions weaken and new equipment orders slow, the key question is whether maintenance revenue from installed systems can cushion the impact.

06

Outlook

The orders the company has disclosed in 2026 include KRW 24.0 billion on March 30, KRW 33.0 billion on July 27, KRW 21.0 billion on August 21 for a North American market entry, and KRW 93.6 billion from a new customer on August 29, totaling KRW 171.6 billion over four orders from March to August.

Cumulative disclosed orders for 2026 stood at KRW 191.4 billion as of September. Micron orders consist of KRW 93.6 billion for Singapore and KRW 19.8 billion for Idaho in the United States, with about 13% revenue progress at the end of Q3.

The greenhouse-gas abatement segment accounted for roughly 80% of backlog at the end of Q3, and the company expects revenue recognition to pick up from Q4.

The company has set a target of lifting its operating revenue share to more than half of sales by 2030, although some reports cite 55%, so the figure should be checked against the company's original materials. New businesses include entry into HBM4E materials and development of materials for generations beyond HBM5.

A semi-mass-production line for advanced semiconductor packaging materials has been completed at the Ochang campus, with final customer approval targeted for 2027. In energy, the company is preparing exhaust after-treatment systems for eco-friendly ships and an ammonia-to-hydrogen conversion system. The profit contribution from these new businesses has not yet appeared in disclosed figures.

08

Bull factors

Room for backlog to convert into revenue

Cumulative disclosed orders reached KRW 191.4 billion as of September 2026, about 136% of 2025 revenue of KRW 141.1 billion. The greenhouse-gas abatement segment made up roughly 80% of backlog at the end of Q3. The company said environmental plant orders will start to be booked as revenue from Q4.

Broader overseas customer base

The overseas share of orders rose to 41% on a cumulative basis, up 19 percentage points from 22% in 2023. Supply now reaches semiconductor plants in the United States and Singapore and a power plant in Taiwan.

Among four orders booked from March to August 2026, one was a North American market entry and another came from a new customer.

Consecutive quarterly profit recovery

Operating profit has risen for three straight quarters since the Q4 2025 level of KRW 2.31 billion, reaching KRW 11.3 billion in Q3 2026. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders has grown for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026.

Cumulative operating profit for January to September 2026 of KRW 22.1 billion exceeds full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 11.7 billion.

09

Bear factors

Annual profit and margin decline

Operating profit peaked at KRW 41.8 billion in 2023 and then fell to KRW 24.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 11.7 billion in 2025, a two-year decline. The operating margin dropped from 19.0% in 2022 to 8.3% in 2025. Operating cash flow was -KRW 4.87 billion in 2025.

Pace of backlog conversion to revenue

Revenue progress on Micron's new orders was about 13% at the end of Q3 2026. Even as disclosed orders accumulate, quarterly results can swing widely depending on when revenue is recognized. Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 34.7 billion fell 11.6% from the prior quarter, showing quarter-to-quarter variation.

Semiconductor cycle and customer concentration

Semiconductors account for 62% of the sales mix, the largest share. If chipmakers cut equipment investment, new orders could slow quickly. The group holding company is in the midst of receiving proceeds from a rights offering and executing investments, and the group has said liquidity risk is not large.

10

Risk factors

Delay in converting orders to revenue

Even with cumulative disclosed orders of KRW 191.4 billion, revenue recognition follows project progress. Micron's new orders stood at about 13% progress at the end of Q3 2026. If conversion slows, revenue and operating profit growth after Q4 could also slow.

Industry cycle and customer concentration

Semiconductors make up 62% of the sales mix, tying earnings to one industry's investment cycle. As 2025 showed with a 39.8% revenue decline, gaps in orders can produce sharp swings in sales.

Although overseas orders have grown, the customers still belong to the same chip industry, so they would be exposed to the same downturn.

Group and audit factors

Parent Ecopro posted a net loss of KRW 135.1 billion in Q3 2026 after turning to a loss, and its precursor affiliate Ecopro Materials posted an operating loss of KRW 29.0 billion. Group funding needs and affiliate results could weigh on how the market views the group as a whole. Results are also subject to external audit, which could lead to changes.

12

Overall view

Ecopro HN is a company centered on semiconductor environmental equipment and greenhouse-gas abatement solutions. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 52.8 billion and operating profit was KRW 11.3 billion, both up sharply year on year, and operating profit has risen for three straight quarters since Q4 2025.

Cumulative disclosed orders of KRW 191.4 billion and an overseas order share of 41% provide the basis for revenue conversion. However, annual operating profit peaked in 2023 at KRW 41.8 billion and then declined for two years, with the 2025 operating margin at 8.3%.

Micron orders at about 13% revenue progress at the end of Q3 show that conversion is still at an early stage. Dependence on the semiconductor cycle and customer concentration support both the bull and bear arguments.

The next checkpoints are the Q4 revenue recognized in the 2026 annual report, whether the operating margin improves from 8.3%, and whether packaging-material customer approval arrives during 2027.
