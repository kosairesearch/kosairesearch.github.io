02

Business structure

Lotte Tour Development was founded in 1971 as a travel company, but its earnings now center on the casino. It secured its casino license through the 2018 acquisition of the Jeju Lotte Casino and moved operations to Jeju Dream Tower in 2021, expanding the scale more than fourfold.

At the end of 2025 on a consolidated basis, casino sales accounted for 72.9% of total revenue and hotel sales for 12.5%. The casino is run by a subsidiary, and the company said it plans to receive KRW 110.9 billion in dividends from that subsidiary and make financial adjustments to reduce accumulated deficits.

The travel segment accounted for 14.5% of total revenue in the first three quarters of 2025 on a cumulative basis, and the company operates seven business divisions. Travel segment revenue in Q1 2026 fell 16% to KRW 18.3 billion, according to Hana Securities, reflecting the timing of cruise revenue recognition.

Jeju Dream Tower's casino is a foreign-only casino, and visitors rose 61.8% to 590,332 in 2025. Members reached 138,000 at the end of April 2026, up 71% year on year. Kiwoom Securities estimated that Chinese visitors make up about 80% of foreign tourists in Jeju. Hotel occupancy in April 2026 was reported at 87.6%.

In competition, the company trails the industry leader in absolute sales but is reported to have among the fastest growth rates in the sector.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue rose from KRW 183.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 313.5 billion in 2023 (up 70.7% year on year), KRW 471.5 billion in 2024 (up 50.4%), and KRW 653.4 billion in 2025 (up 38.6%), marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025. 2025 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 period, and 2022 the smallest.

Operating loss was KRW 118.7 billion in 2022 and KRW 60.6 billion in 2023, a continued deficit with a narrower loss, before operating profit of KRW 39 billion in 2024 marked a turn to profit.

Operating profit reached KRW 143.3 billion in 2025, up 267.4%, and the operating margin climbed from 8.3% in 2024 to 21.9% in 2025.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders were KRW 224.7 billion in 2022, KRW 202.2 billion in 2023, and KRW 116.6 billion in 2024, before a KRW 27.8 billion profit in 2025 turned the result positive.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 27.6 billion in 2025, a different figure from profit attributable to controlling shareholders. Visitors to the Jeju Dream Tower casino rose 61.8% in 2025 and were cited as the main driver of the improvement.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 2,590.9% in 2023 to 592.1% in 2024 and 488.7% in 2025. Operating cash flow moved from KRW -62.9 billion in 2022 and KRW -13.1 billion in 2023 to KRW 121.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 100.1 billion in 2025.

Among the quarters, Q3 2025 operating profit of KRW 53 billion was the highest in the period, followed by KRW 44.2 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 28.8 billion in Q1 2026, and a recovery to KRW 51.9 billion in Q2 2026. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 179.6 billion, down 3.8% year on year.

Meritz Securities estimated Q3 operating profit at KRW 36.1 billion in a November 9, 2026 report, and attributed the shortfall to a drop in table hold rates from 22.1% in Q2 to 15.8% in Q3. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 44.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for Korean casinos moves with inbound foreign tourism. The company's cumulative casino sales for January to October 2026 reached KRW 455.6 billion, up 18.5% year on year (preliminary), and table drop rose 12.3% to KRW 2.3 trillion over the same period.

Chinese visitors make up a large share of foreign tourists in Jeju, so the pace of inbound recovery is the key variable. Kiwoom Securities judged that demand growth in Jeju is faster than on the mainland, and cited a benefit from restrictions on Chinese group travel to Japan.

The company has been reducing KRW 50,000–100,000 tables and expanding KRW 200,000–300,000 tables, in line with the Macau average (Hana Securities, May 2026). Hana Securities argued that a tax-rate gap between Macau and Korea allows the company to offer higher rolling commissions, which strengthens competitiveness.

In competition, the company trails the industry leader in absolute sales but is viewed as having the highest growth rate in the sector. A company official cited geopolitical uncertainty in the Middle East and high oil prices as external variables, while noting record-level visitors and results for May.

In the cycle, foreign-focused casino demand is in a recovery phase, but volatility from hold rates and customer mix remains high.

06

Outlook

The company said its growth potential remains ample given expanding direct flight routes and rising foreign tourist arrivals. It described a virtuous cycle in which growing membership and drop reinforce each other.

Hana Securities said in May 2026 that expanding rolling business could add more than KRW 100 billion in annual casino sales as a further growth driver. In the same period, the company planned to resume the Macau–Jeju route from June and to add three poker tables and about 80 slot and electronic table game units.

Membership growth of 71% as of April 2026 is cited as the base for this plan. On the financial side, refinancing is the key checkpoint. Kiwoom Securities said in a January 2026 report that cutting financing costs by KRW 20–30 billion a year would lift net profit.

Hana Securities said in its May 2026 report that early refinancing would be possible from the second half. On shareholder returns, the company announced in March 2026 that its articles would add an interim dividend clause.

Under the Commercial Act, however, dividends can only be paid from retained earnings, and the company plans to reduce accumulated deficits to about KRW 525.5 billion through financial adjustments. The speed of deficit reduction therefore determines when dividends can resume.

October 2026 preliminary data showed casino sales rebounding 24.1%, while hotel sales fell 9.2%. The next checkpoints are the November preliminary indicators, whether refinancing is executed, and the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Continued Casino Volume Growth

Meritz Securities said in a November 9, 2026 report that October drop and casino net sales reached record highs. Cumulative casino sales for January to October 2026 rose 18.5% year on year, and table drop rose 12.3%. Membership grew 71% year on year as of April 2026, which the company cites as the base for drop growth.

Potential Reduction in Financing Costs

Kiwoom Securities said in a January 2026 report that refinancing could cut financing costs by KRW 20–30 billion a year and improve net profit. Whether early refinancing is actually carried out will determine when financing costs fall. Lower financing costs would widen the share of operating profit that reaches net profit.

Rolling Business and Table Mix Shift

Expanded rolling business could lead to additional growth in casino sales. The company is cutting KRW 50,000–100,000 tables and expanding KRW 200,000–300,000 tables, which the broker said could lift hold rates.

Bear factors

Quarterly Earnings Volatility from Hold Rates

Meritz Securities analyzed that table hold rates fell from 22.1% in Q2 2026 to 15.8% in Q3, limiting the improvement in casino profit. Preliminary September 2026 casino sales fell 45.1% year on year. Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 30.8 billion, down 41.8% year on year.

High Leverage and Accumulated Deficits

The debt-to-equity ratio stood at 488.7% at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 1.8 trillion. Dividends are possible only after accumulated deficits are cleared, and the company plans to reduce them to about KRW 525.5 billion. Press coverage in August 2026 also said the financing-cost burden remains significant.

Reliance on Inbound Visitors and External Variables

Chinese visitors make up about 80% of foreign tourists in Jeju, so results depend on a specific customer group and inbound flows. A company official also cited Middle East instability and high oil prices as external variables. Preliminary October 2026 hotel sales fell 9.2% year on year, so recovery outside the casino has been limited.

Risk factors

Earnings Volatility (Hold Rate)

Casino revenue depends on the combination of drop and hold rate, so it swings sharply from quarter to quarter. Q3 2026 operating profit fell 41.8% year on year and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders. October indicators rebounded, but later months may vary with customer mix and events.

External Demand and Policy Environment

Foreign-only casino demand is sensitive to inbound tourist flows, air routes, geopolitics, and oil prices. A company official also cited Middle East instability and high oil prices as external variables.

With Chinese visitors making up about 80% of foreign tourists in Jeju, shifts in demand from one country have a large effect.

Financial Structure and Timing of Dividend Resumption

The debt-to-equity ratio was 488.7% at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 1.8 trillion. Financing-cost savings depend on whether refinancing is executed. Dividend resumption remains limited until accumulated deficits are cleared.

12

Overall view

Lotte Tour Development's earnings depend largely on casino revenue, and operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders both turned positive in 2025. Cumulative casino sales for January to October 2026 rose 18.5% year on year, and preliminary October indicators rebounded.

By contrast, Q3 2026 operating profit fell 41.8% year on year to KRW 30.8 billion, and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.83 billion. Meritz Securities attributed the decline mainly to lower table hold rates.

On the financial side, a debt-to-equity ratio of 488.7%, total liabilities of KRW 1.8 trillion, and remaining accumulated deficits leave financing-cost reduction and the timing of dividend resumption as open issues.

Bullish factors include foreign-focused casino growth, rolling expansion, and refinancing potential, while bearish factors include hold-rate volatility, concentration on inbound visitors, and high leverage.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary November indicators in early December 2026, refinancing execution, and the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027.
