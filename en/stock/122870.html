Report overview

Tour Costs Weigh on Q3; New Debuts Are the Next Variable

YG Entertainment's Q3 2026 operating profit fell 56.8% year on year to KRW 13.5 billion as early tour costs and an album gap weighed on results, leaving the first-half 2027 debuts as the next checkpoint.

  1. 1

    Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 156.7 billion, down 9.4% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 13.5 billion, down 56.8%. Both rose 22.7% from the prior quarter.

  2. 2

    Album sales fell 73.0% to KRW 3.2 billion as no major comeback landed, while merchandise sales rose 44.4% to KRW 40.6 billion.

  3. 3

    Operating profit reached KRW 71.3 billion in 2025, turning positive from an operating loss of KRW 20.6 billion in 2024, with an operating margin of 13.1%.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

YG Entertainment is an agency that earns revenue from artist albums, music services, concerts, merchandise (MD), and royalties. In Q3 2026, concerts and merchandise led the results. Concert revenue was KRW 43.9 billion, down 13.7% year on year, reflecting a high base from the prior-year BLACKPINK shows.

The quarter included 41 shows in total: 8 by BIGBANG, 14 by BABYMONSTER, and 19 by TREASURE. Merchandise revenue rose 44.4% to KRW 40.6 billion, helped by global tour venues and planned product sales.

Album revenue fell 73.0% to KRW 3.2 billion, reflecting a base effect from the prior quarter's releases by BABYMONSTER and TREASURE and the absence of major comebacks.

Royalty revenue declined 6.4% to KRW 15.3 billion, and music service revenue fell 15% to about KRW 18.1 billion excluding the Naver VIBE operating agency business.

Concerts and merchandise are increasingly run directly by the subsidiary YG PLUS, which raised tour-related sales commissions by about KRW 5.7 billion from the prior quarter.

On the customer side, overseas fans of BLACKPINK are the core demand for concerts and merchandise, while BABYMONSTER and TREASURE are widening demand as younger IP. Among large peers such as Hybe, SM Entertainment, and JYP Ent., the comparison centers on artist line-up breadth and tour scale.

Because earnings swing heavily with the tour cycle, album sales and the share of new artists are cited as keys to earnings stability.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 391.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 569.2 billion in 2023, a 45.5% increase, fell to KRW 364.9 billion in 2024, a 35.9% decrease, and recovered to KRW 545.4 billion in 2025, a 49.4% increase. Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit grew from KRW 46.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 86.9 billion in 2023, an 86.4% increase, but swung to an operating loss of KRW 20.6 billion in 2024. It returned to operating profit of KRW 71.3 billion in 2025, with the operating margin at 13.1%.

The operating margin was 11.9% in 2022, 15.3% in 2023, -5.6% in 2024, and 13.1% in 2025. The 2024 loss came before the BLACKPINK world tour began in mid-2025, which points to the tour cycle as a major driver.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 33.7 billion in 2022, KRW 61.3 billion in 2023, KRW 18.5 billion in 2024, and KRW 36.9 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests, which is a separate measure, was KRW 53.7 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 operating profit of KRW 31.1 billion was the highest, followed by KRW 22.3 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 19.4 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 11 billion in Q2 2026, and KRW 13.5 billion in Q3 2026.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent in Q3 2026 was KRW 4.57 billion, down 73.1% year on year, and the total for the last four quarters was KRW 21.5 billion.

Analysts cited a KRW 2 billion bad-debt write-down on BIGBANG merchandise receivables, early world-tour operating costs, and a higher share of low-margin regional shows as Q3 2026 margin pressures.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 650.8 billion, equity attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 513.5 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 187.9 billion, with a debt ratio of 28.9%. Operating cash flow recovered from KRW 4.881 million in 2024 to KRW 90.9 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The downstream market relies on overseas tours and merchandise spending by K-pop artists. In years when a major group runs a world tour, concerts and merchandise rise together, and once the tour ends, revenue shifts back toward albums and royalties in a repeating cycle.

BLACKPINK's Deadline World Tour started in Goyang in July 2025 and ended in Hong Kong in January 2026, with 33 shows across 16 cities. The year-on-year decline in Q3 2026 concert revenue after that tour shows where the company sits in the cycle.

The company, however, said its younger IP is growing its tours faster than those of other agencies. BABYMONSTER held two shows at a dome-scale venue just two years after its debut.

Compared with peers such as Hybe, SM Entertainment, and JYP Ent., which have multiple artist pipelines, YG Entertainment's reliance on one major group is the central point of debate. That dependence amplifies earnings swings depending on when BLACKPINK is active.

K-pop concert business also tends to concentrate early costs such as overseas visas, freight, and localization at the start of tours. Analysts pointed to localization costs in Western markets and a higher share of low-margin regional shows as drags on Q3 profitability. Album and music sales are volatile from quarter to quarter depending on release timing.

Outlook

The company said it is preparing to debut one male and one female group in the first half of 2027, narrowing the gap between the two debuts so both launch within the first half. Some analysts expect these new artists to rebuild growth expectations for the first half of 2027.

BABYMONSTER held two shows at a dome-scale venue just two years after debut, showing the scalability of its younger IP, and TREASURE also supported Q3 concert revenue with fan concerts and shows.

Q3 2026 concert revenue of KRW 43.9 billion came without the BLACKPINK tour base, so how far new artists and younger IP can fill that gap will shape the revenue mix in 2027. If the debuts go ahead, production and marketing costs may rise early, so quarterly cost structure after debut will need to be tracked.

With Q3 album revenue at KRW 3.2 billion, first-week album sales from the new artists will be an early signal for album recovery. The company's fourth-quarter 2026 results and its 2026 annual report are the next reporting items, and the quarterly trend in concert and merchandise costs will become clearer then.

Because the Q3 operating margin was thin, how the cost structure changes after Q4 is the item to check in the next regular report.

08

Bull factors

New Artist Pipeline

The company said it is preparing two new groups, one male and one female, to debut within the first half of 2027. If the debuts translate into album sales and tour dates, they could fill the album gap seen in Q3. The fast tour expansion of younger IP is cited as support for this path.

Merchandise and IP Expansion

Q3 2026 merchandise revenue rose 44.4% year on year to KRW 40.6 billion. Higher sales at tour venues and planned products are strengthening merchandise's role in cushioning swings in concert revenue. The swing to KRW 71.3 billion in operating profit in 2025 also shows that tour-linked income can restore earnings.

Scaling Younger IP Tours

Q3 2026 also included shows by TREASURE and BIGBANG, so several IPs are sharing concert revenue. A lower reliance on a single group is cited as a supporting factor on the bullish side.

09

Bear factors

Q3 Profit Missed Consensus

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 13.5 billion fell short of the market consensus of KRW 19.9 billion. It fell 56.8% year on year, and profit attributable to owners of the parent fell 73.1% to KRW 4.57 billion. Analysts saw the size of the miss as a burden on confidence in the results.

Album Gap and Rising Costs

Album revenue fell 73.0% to KRW 3.2 billion. Early tour operating costs, a rise of about KRW 5.7 billion in sales commissions from direct operation by the subsidiary, and a bad-debt write-down of about KRW 2 billion on BIGBANG merchandise receivables overlapped. Costs concentrated in a quarter without new albums pressured the margin.

Quarterly Earnings Volatility

After KRW 31.1 billion in Q3 2025, operating profit was KRW 22.3 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 19.4 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 11 billion in Q2 2026, and KRW 13.5 billion in Q3 2026. Q2 2026 operating profit fell 43.5% from the prior quarter.

Even after the swing to profit, large quarter-to-quarter swings have led to criticism that earnings are hard to predict.

10

Risk factors

Artist Activity Cycle

Earnings depend heavily on the timing of BLACKPINK's major tours and album releases, so a long activity gap by key groups could reduce concert and album revenue together. The 73.0% drop in Q3 2026 album revenue is a representative case. If the new debuts slip, the options for filling the gap are limited.

Tour Profitability Structure

Direct operation of concerts and merchandise by a subsidiary has raised sales commissions and operating costs, and the share of low-margin regional shows has grown. This structure could lower the operating margin as shows increase. Overseas localization costs tend to concentrate early in tours, which amplifies quarterly profit swings.

Receivables and Accounting Swings

A bad-debt write-down arose on merchandise receivables, and costs can vary by quarter depending on settlement terms with outside partners. The Q3 write-down was about KRW 2 billion. Whether it is a one-off or a recurring factor will become clearer in upcoming quarterly disclosures.

Overall view

YG Entertainment's 2025 results showed operating profit of KRW 71.3 billion and an operating margin of 13.1%, a swing from an operating loss in 2024 back to profit. In Q3 2026, the concert base effect, the album gap, and early tour costs overlapped, leaving operating profit at KRW 13.5 billion, down 56.8% year on year.

The direction of earnings depends heavily on artist activity schedules. Bullish factors include a 44.4% rise in merchandise revenue, the fast tour expansion of younger IP, and the planned first-half 2027 debuts.

Bearish factors include the miss against market consensus, the 73.0% drop in album revenue, and rising costs and bad-debt write-downs tied to direct operation by the subsidiary. As of November 10, 2026, KB Securities lowered its target price to KRW 50,000, and Heungkuk Securities said it keeps its KRW 58,000 target.

Both brokers described Q3 results as falling short of market expectations. The next checkpoints for this report are the Q4 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual report, and the timing of the first-half 2027 debuts.
