Itoxi: Delisting Review Under Way Amid Continued Losses

Itoxi posted an operating loss of KRW 4.93 billion in 2025, and its third-quarter 2026 revenue fell to KRW 82.13 million while the company has filed an objection to a KRX delisting decision.

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Itoxi was founded in 1997 for software consulting, development and supply, listed on KOSDAQ in 2002, merged with game developer Preston in 2005, and changed its name to Itoxi in 2020.

The business is split into mobile games (publishing and in-house development), overseas distribution, and domestic product distribution and services.

The game unit rests on publishing deals for Cheonsang-bi K (developer Playworks) and La Salas (developer Lepor Games), plus an IP revenue-sharing contract for God of High School.

According to an IB Tomato report dated January 26, 2026, game revenue fell from KRW 16.1 billion for 2024 to KRW 3.8 billion for the first three quarters of 2025.

In the same report, overseas distribution revenue was KRW 4.9 billion over that period, equal to 49% of total sales, and centered on pickup trucks tied to Ukraine's reconstruction projects.

FnGuide's company overview also cites KG Mobility pickup truck deliveries through a Ukrainian joint venture as a key overseas distribution example. Several titles, including Super Girls Battle and Magic Cat Raising, have ended service.

Because revenue depends heavily on the performance of new releases, the success of the publishing pipeline largely determines the direction of the business. Compared with larger IT and software companies such as NAVER, Kakao and Samsung SDS, the gap in business scale and financial capacity is wide.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 28.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 21.6 billion in 2023 (down 23.1% year on year), KRW 17.2 billion in 2024 (down 20.7% year on year), and KRW 5.7 billion in 2025 (down 66.8% year on year).

Revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025; 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest within the 2022-2025 period. Operating income went from a profit of KRW 718 million in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 4.4 billion in 2023, a turn into deficit versus the prior year.

The loss widened to KRW 11.8 billion in 2024, which the company recorded as continued losses with a larger deficit, before narrowing to KRW 4.93 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 2.6% in 2022 to -20.3% in 2023, -69.0% in 2024 and -86.4% in 2025.

Falling revenue combined with a larger operating loss drove the margin deterioration. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 407 million in 2022, KRW 4.43 billion in 2023, KRW 16.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.28 billion in 2025, which makes four consecutive years of losses from 2022 through 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests shows the same annual loss amounts in each of those years.

In the audit process for fiscal 2025, the company cited heavier competition in the mobile game market, reduced sales of existing games, weak sales of newly launched games, impairment of unlaunched game assets, weak distribution sales tied to Ukraine's reconstruction in the second half, and impairment of an equity-method investment (as reported by Topstar News).

Whether the impairment charges were one-off non-cash items is hard to judge, since no amounts were presented as disclosed figures.

By quarter, third-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 709 million and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.44 billion, a profit, while fourth-quarter 2025 data is not available. Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 964 million with an operating loss of KRW 463 million.

Q2 2026 revenue dropped to KRW 71.72 million (down 92.6% from the prior quarter) with an operating loss of KRW 552 million. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 82.13 million (up 14.5% from the prior quarter, down 88.4% year on year) with an operating loss of KRW 286 million, which narrowed the deficit.

The company attributed the revenue shortfall to lower game sales. The company has reported losses for three consecutive quarters from Q1 to Q3 2026, and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (Q1 2025 to Q4 2025) totals KRW 7.28 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The mobile game market releases new titles constantly, and hit-or-miss outcomes make revenue for publishers and developers highly volatile. In its fiscal 2025 audit process, the company itself cited heavier competition in the mobile game market as a cause of lower sales from existing games.

FnGuide's company overview attributes the decline in results to delayed new game launches and user attrition. The overseas distribution unit is tied to demand from Ukraine's reconstruction, so it is sensitive to the political situation and the rebuilding timetable there.

IB Tomato noted that geopolitical uncertainty has grown around Ukraine's reconstruction projects. Peers in the same IT and software sector, such as NAVER, Kakao and Samsung SDS, rely on platform, cloud and system integration businesses, so their structures differ from Itoxi's.

Measured by profitability, Itoxi's disclosed operating margin sits in loss territory, which separates it from profitable companies in the sector. The game industry's results depend on release cycles, so the company's own new-title performance matters more than the broader market.

On funding, the withdrawal of a convertible bond issuance and unfaithful-disclosure penalty points have constrained the company's financing options. Currently, the largest external variable is the outcome of the exchange's listing review rather than the industry cycle.

Outlook

The nearest checkpoint is the exchange process. KRX's listing committee resolved on October 26, 2026 to delist the stock, and the company filed an objection on November 16, 2026. The KOSDAQ Market Committee is scheduled to decide by December 14, 2026 whether to delist and whether to grant an improvement period.

A quarterly report submitted on November 16, 2026 showed recent-quarter revenue below KRW 300 million, and the exchange added a listing-qualification review on grounds that the main business had stopped (as reported by Topstar News).

Trading in the shares has been suspended since March 23, 2026, and an October 26, 2026 disclosure changed the end of the suspension to the date of the delisting decision.

The delisting ground tied to the fiscal 2025 audit opinion leads to a delisting decision after the improvement period ends, which runs until 10 days after the day following the statutory filing deadline for the next annual report.

On management, a disclosure dated March 30, 2026 reported that Choi Chi-kyung became sole representative director after Jeon Bong-kyu's term expired.

In December 2025 the company carried out a 75% free capital reduction to ease the burden of capital impairment, and its capital impairment ratio for fiscal 2025 was 34.1%, down from 77.5% in the prior fiscal year (as reported by Topstar News).

The most important point to check on the results side is when the 2026 annual report shows full-year revenue, operating loss and the audit opinion.

Bull factors

Publishing pipeline

Publishing deals for Cheonsang-bi K and La Salas remain in place, and a separate IP revenue-sharing contract covers God of High School. Game revenue fell from KRW 16.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 3.8 billion in the first three quarters of 2025, but bulls argue that new releases could be a route back to growth. Whether that happens should be checked against disclosed game revenue trends.

Reconstruction-linked overseas distribution

Overseas distribution reached KRW 4.9 billion in the first three quarters of 2025, 49% of total sales, and the company is pursuing a glass plant investment and expanded construction material supply for Ukraine's reconstruction. If reconstruction demand turns into real volume, distribution sales could recover. The same business was also the cause of weakness in the second half of 2025, so the effect cuts both ways.

Narrowing quarterly losses

Operating loss narrowed from KRW 552 million in Q2 2026 to KRW 286 million in Q3 2026, and the Q3 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders also shrank from the prior quarter. Cost reduction has contributed to the smaller loss.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 94.2% in 2024 to 82.2% in 2025, a sign that financial pressure eased somewhat. Whether cost control continues is the next point to check.

Bear factors

Delisting process under way

KRX's listing committee resolved on October 26, 2026 to delist the stock. Although the company filed an objection on November 16, a listing-qualification review was also added because quarterly revenue fell below KRW 300 million.

The outcome of the KOSDAQ Market Committee's decision by December 14, 2026 will determine whether trading can resume.

Sharp contraction in the revenue base

Annual revenue fell from KRW 28.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.7 billion in 2025, and Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 82.13 million was down 88.4% year on year. Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 71.72 million was 92.6% lower than the prior quarter. Bears argue that a revenue base at this level is too small to cover the operating losses.

Capital and disclosure credibility concerns

The capital impairment ratio for fiscal 2025 was 34.1%, and it remained a ground under the listing rules. After withdrawing an 18th convertible bond issuance, the company was designated for unfaithful disclosure and received 7.5 penalty points, added to 5.5 points from August 2024. Accumulated penalty points could weigh on financing and on the discussion of maintaining the listing.

Risk factors

Listing continuity risk

Delisting review and listing-qualification review are running at the same time, so the trading suspension could last longer depending on the outcome. A final delisting decision follows after the improvement period tied to the fiscal 2025 audit opinion ends. If the objection is rejected, the company would lose its status as a listed stock.

Concentration risk

Overseas distribution sales are tied to Ukraine's reconstruction work, so they are directly exposed to geopolitical uncertainty and delays in the rebuilding timetable. According to the company, sales in this unit were weak in the second half of 2025. The game unit's revenue moves sharply with delayed launches and user attrition.

Financial and disclosure risk

The capital impairment ratio improved, but the 75% free capital reduction in December 2025 sharply cut the share count. Withdrawing a convertible bond issuance and accumulating unfaithful-disclosure penalty points could limit financing options. Disclosure credibility is an element raised alongside the exchange's review.
