02

Business structure

Device (formerly Device ENG Co., Ltd.) was founded in 2002, listed on KOSDAQ in 2017, and is headquartered in Cheonan, South Chungcheong Province. It changed its name to Device in April 2025.

Its main products are cleaning equipment for OLED deposition, optical inspection equipment, and contamination-removal equipment for FOUP wafer carriers.

The display segment centers on fine metal mask (FMM) cleaning equipment, and one estimate puts the company's share of mask cleaners above 90% during the 8.6-generation IT OLED investment wave. Exports accounted for KRW 47.5 billion, or 57% of 2025 revenue.

In semiconductors, the company supplies FOUP cleaners to Samsung Electronics and has also disclosed contracts with SK hynix. Its display dependence was high while Samsung Electronics held back capital spending, and its semiconductor orders are disclosed as individual contracts.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual disclosed results show revenue of KRW 72.7 billion in 2022, KRW 57.2 billion in 2023 (down 21.4%), and KRW 47.2 billion in 2024 (down 17.5%), before rising to KRW 84.1 billion in 2025 (up 78.3%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 13.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.31 billion in 2023 (down 67.7%), was KRW 4.22 billion in 2024 (down 2.0%), and recovered to KRW 17.3 billion in 2025 (up 310.5%). Operating margin was 18.3% in 2022, 7.5% in 2023, 9.0% in 2024 and 20.6% in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders bottomed at KRW 7.2 billion in 2023 (down 33.4%), then rose two years in a row to KRW 7.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 16.9 billion in 2025. Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts in each year.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 161 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 57.2 billion. The debt ratio rose from 25.2% in 2022 to 41.8% in 2024 and eased to 35.5% in 2025. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 23.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 6.07 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 39.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 13.3 billion, followed by a sharp drop in Q4 2025 to KRW 27 billion in revenue (down 31.0% quarter on quarter) and KRW 2.58 billion in operating profit (down 80.6%).

Q1 2026 marked the peak, with revenue of KRW 31.1 billion, operating profit of KRW 12.3 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 11.1 billion.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 23.3 billion and Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 32.9 billion, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders has declined for two consecutive quarters. Over the last four quarters, profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 29.5 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Display demand is tied to renewed investment competition among Chinese panel makers and to the 8.6-generation IT OLED build-out. Commentary suggests the company could benefit as Chinese panel makers renew spending.

Display equipment demand has been volatile, and capital spending adjustments by Chinese panel makers caused a sharp earnings drop in 2023 and 2024. On the Korean side, Samsung Electronics said its mobile display business saw weaker customer demand in Q1 2026 due to seasonality and memory price effects.

In semiconductors, the memory upcycle is lifting equipment orders, and Samsung's DS division reported a record quarterly result in Q1 2026. A securities research note cited growth rates in revenue and operating profit that trailed peers in Q4 2025. The two end markets are in different cycles, so which one recovers first will shape the direction of results.

The next checkpoints are new supply contracts disclosed through the end of 2026, the actual scale of BOE orders, and operating margin and operating cash flow in the 2026 annual report compared with 20.6% and KRW 6.07 billion in 2025.

Bull factors

Earnings power recovered in 2025

Operating margin reached 20.6% in 2025, up from 9.0% in 2024, and operating profit was KRW 17.3 billion. Revenue from the BOE contract began to be recognized in Q4 2024, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose two years in a row to KRW 16.9 billion in 2025.

Accumulating semiconductor equipment orders

After a KRW 2.97 billion Samsung Electronics order in December 2025, disclosures followed for a KRW 10.93 billion contract with Samsung Austin Semiconductor in February 2026, a KRW 3.77 billion SK hynix contract in March, and a KRW 6.95 billion Samsung Electronics contract in April.

One press report expected growth momentum to strengthen as memory-driven demand lifts semiconductor equipment contracts.

Mask cleaner share and export mix

One estimate puts the company's share of FMM mask cleaners above 90%. Exports reached KRW 47.5 billion in 2025, or 57% of revenue, and renewed overseas OLED and semiconductor investment has been cited as the driver of recovery.

Bear factors

Quarterly profit softening in 2026

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 8.05 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 6.17 billion in Q3 2026, two consecutive quarterly declines. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 9.74 billion was down 26.9% year on year. A Q4 2025 earnings note said revenue and operating profit growth trailed peers.

End of BOE supply and base effect

A February 2026 report noted that BOE equipment supply ended with 2025 and that earnings would decline on a base effect without new orders. Earnings also fell sharply in 2023 when Chinese display makers adjusted capital spending.

Dependence on customer capex and contract size

In September 2025, a KRW 27.0 billion display equipment contract with Xiamen Tianma Display Technology was disclosed, running through May 30, 2026. Individual semiconductor contracts in 2026 have been in the tens of billions of won, so quarterly revenue can swing with the timing of a few customers' orders.

Risk factors

Customer capex cycle

Display equipment demand depends heavily on customer capex decisions. The 2023 earnings drop was tied to capital spending adjustments by Chinese display makers. Samsung Electronics said small and mid-sized display demand weakened in Q1 2026 due to seasonality and memory prices.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Q4 2025 revenue fell 31.0% quarter on quarter and operating profit fell 80.6%. After the Q1 2026 peak, profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell in Q2 and Q3. Because individual contracts are sized in tens of billions of won, an order gap in a single quarter can show up in results quickly.

Durability of the 2025 recovery and cash flow

Operating cash flow in 2025 was lower than in the prior year, so the earnings recovery did not translate into higher cash inflow. Operating margin rose from 7.5% in 2023 to 20.6% in 2025, but because the recovery leaned heavily on BOE contract revenue, sustaining that level would be difficult if order gaps emerge.

Overall view

Device's 2025 recovery reflected BOE contract revenue, with revenue of KRW 84.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 17.3 billion, and operating margin rose to 20.6%. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 161 billion and the debt ratio was 35.5%.

Quarterly revenue in 2026 moved from KRW 31.1 billion in Q1 to KRW 23.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 32.9 billion in Q3, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell in Q2 and Q3.

Positive factors include continued semiconductor equipment contracts with Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, a high share in mask cleaners, and expected demand recovery in semiconductors from the memory upcycle.

Negative factors include the base effect after the BOE supply ended, a history of customer capex adjustments, and the limited size of individual contracts. Past multiples sat in a low range relative to book value, but that alone does not establish where the current share price stands.

The next checkpoints are new supply contracts disclosed through the end of 2026 and the operating margin and operating cash flow in the 2026 annual report.
