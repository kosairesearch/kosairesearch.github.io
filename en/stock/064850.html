FnGuide: Operating Margin Climbs Into the 30% Range

FnGuide posted a 30.1% operating margin in 2025, and its owner-attributable net profit rose for three consecutive quarters from Q1 through Q3 2026. Dependence on ETF-linked index fees and the securities market cycle remain the key variables.

02

Business structure

FnGuide is a Korean financial information provider founded in 2000, operating financial information services, indices, and fund evaluation. Its main revenue comes from financial information services, indices, and solution development and maintenance, and it listed on KOSDAQ in December 2020.

The 2025 revenue mix was 57.0% financial information services, 30.4% indices, 5.3% fund evaluation, and 7.3% other, according to a May 2026 broker report. The index business earns fees linked to the net assets of ETFs that track FnGuide indices, so revenue scales with ETF market growth.

That broker said index revenue rose by KRW 3 billion from 2024 and led 2025 growth. The company is shifting its focus from B2B to B2C and is developing services that combine unstructured data such as news and ESG information with AI and alternative data.

WiseReport's company monitor describes it as holding a leading position in private index business through an independent industry classification system. Overseas comparison names include MSCI, S&P Global, and Morningstar, and the May 2026 broker report included a peer table against them. Comparable data on domestic competitors' business scale was not sufficiently confirmed in this review.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue was KRW 28.4 billion in 2022, KRW 29.6 billion in 2023 (up 4.1% year on year), KRW 31.6 billion in 2024 (up 6.8%), and KRW 35.4 billion in 2025 (up 12.0%). Revenue rose for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 is the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit was KRW 6.07 billion in 2022 and KRW 6.07 billion in 2023 (down 0.1%), then rose to KRW 7.65 billion in 2024 and KRW 10.7 billion in 2025, a second straight annual gain. The 39.4% rise in operating profit in 2025 far outpaced the 12.0% revenue growth.

A Daily Invest report explained the gap by pointing to a cost base in which about 80% of expenses are fixed, and described 2025 results as a record. Operating margin moved from 21.4% in 2022 to 20.5% in 2023, 24.2% in 2024, and 30.1% in 2025.

Owner-attributable net profit was KRW 7.94 billion in 2022, KRW 7.76 billion in 2023 (down 2.3%), KRW 4.07 billion in 2024 (down 47.6%), and KRW 9.89 billion in 2025 (up 143.1%). Net profit including non-controlling interests, which is a different figure, was KRW 4.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 10 billion in 2025.

The reason owner-attributable profit fell in 2024 despite higher operating profit was not identified in this review. A November 2025 GL Research report noted that net profit was improving as loss-making affiliates were cleaned up.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 9.51 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 11.5 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 14.9 billion in Q2 2026. According to a Thinkpool earnings flash, Q1 2026 revenue rose 16% year on year, operating profit rose 124%, and both beat market forecasts.

Owner-attributable net profit bottomed at KRW 2.52 billion in Q4 2025 and rose for three consecutive quarters to KRW 4.85 billion, KRW 6.16 billion, and KRW 6.26 billion in Q3 2026.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 14.8 billion fell 0.5% from Q2, and operating profit of KRW 7.73 billion fell 1.7%, both slightly below the Q2 peak.

05

Industry analysis

FnGuide's end market is the Korean securities market. Earlier analysis pointed to securities market conditions as a key checkpoint because they affect demand for financial information. The index business is directly exposed to ETF market growth.

WiseReport's company monitor noted that index segment results improved on ETF market growth and rising demand for indices. Growth in low-cost and diversified investing is cited as a backdrop for index-based products.

Because index revenue is linked to total ETF net assets, fee income grows when markets rise and inflows continue, and shrinks in the opposite conditions. Data sufficient to determine whether the market is in an upswing or a downturn was not confirmed in this review.

Overseas comparison names such as MSCI, S&P Global, and Morningstar are much larger index and data businesses, and the broker peer table also showed operating margins. The company presents AI-based data interpretation as a new market, so whether end demand broadens beyond securities firms is the next point to watch.

Outlook

No annual guidance formally disclosed by the company was confirmed in this review. A May 2026 broker report projected 2026 revenue of KRW 55 billion (up 55.4% year on year) and operating profit of KRW 27 billion (up 144.9%).

In the same period, a researcher quoted in a Daily Invest report projected 2026 revenue of KRW 41.4 billion (up 17%) and operating profit of KRW 13.3 billion. The gap between the two estimates is wide, so the baseline for full-year 2026 results differs across brokers.

Disclosed quarterly revenue for Q1 through Q3 2026 was KRW 11.5 billion, KRW 14.9 billion, and KRW 14.8 billion. That Q1 2026 results beat market forecasts is a fact that supports upward revisions. In the index business, ETF net asset flows and index demand determine revenue direction.

No disclosed figures were found for the revenue scale of the new B2C services or AI-based data products. The next checkpoint is therefore whether Q4 and full-year 2026 revenue land closer to one of the two estimates. Whether the company has announced capacity expansion or new product timing was not confirmed in this search.

08

Bull factors

Index fee structure

The index business earns fees linked to total net assets of ETFs tracking FnGuide indices. A May 2026 broker report said index revenue rose by KRW 3 billion from 2024 and led 2025 growth. Rising demand for low-cost and diversified investing is cited as a medium- to long-term growth driver.

Margin expansion and fixed-cost structure

Operating margin rose from 21.4% in 2022 to 30.1% in 2025. A Daily Invest report said that, with about 80% of costs fixed, operating profit grew 39% while revenue grew 12%. The 2025 results show a structure in which margins expand as revenue grows.

Quarterly momentum in 2026

According to a Thinkpool earnings flash, Q1 2026 revenue rose 16% year on year and operating profit rose 124%, both above market forecasts. Owner-attributable net profit has risen for three consecutive quarters since its Q4 2025 low through Q3 2026. Q3 2026 results eased slightly from the Q2 peak.

Bear factors

Dependence on the securities market

Earlier analysis flagged the securities market as the end market that affects demand for financial information. Index fees track ETF net assets, so the fee base shrinks in market downturns. Q3 2026 revenue and operating profit both declined slightly from Q2.

Earnings volatility in 2024

Owner-attributable net profit fell 47.6% to KRW 4.07 billion in 2024 even as operating profit rose to KRW 7.65 billion. The cause of the divergence was not identified in this review. Whether this was a one-off factor or a recurring one remains unconfirmed.

Wide spread in 2026 estimates

The 2026 revenue estimates differ widely, at KRW 55 billion in a May 2026 broker report and KRW 41.4 billion in a Daily Invest report. Disclosed revenue for Q1 through Q3 2026 was KRW 11.5 billion, KRW 14.9 billion, and KRW 14.8 billion. The wide gap makes it unclear which baseline should be used for full-year results.

Risk factors

Market cycle risk

If trading activity in the securities market weakens, demand for financial information and index fund flows could weaken together. Earlier analysis highlighted the securities market cycle as a factor affecting the business. This is an external factor the company cannot control.

Concentration in fee structure

Index revenue is linked to total ETF net assets, making it sensitive to fund flows and market price moves. Because the index business led growth, outflows in that segment would affect overall revenue. Year-by-year trends in segment mix were not confirmed in this review.

Quarterly volatility and estimate dispersion

In 2024 operating profit and owner-attributable net profit diverged, showing earnings volatility. A largely fixed cost base lifts margins when revenue rises but works in reverse when revenue falls. Because 2026 revenue estimates differ widely across brokers, the baseline for results can shift.

Overall view

FnGuide recorded a 30.1% operating margin in 2025 on its financial information services and index businesses, and its debt-to-equity ratio fell from 75.6% in 2022 to 28.3% in 2025. In 2025, revenue rose 12.0%, operating profit rose 39.4%, and owner-attributable net profit rose 143.1%, recovering from the 2024 low.

From Q1 through Q3 2026, quarterly owner-attributable net profit rose for three consecutive quarters. However, index fees are linked to ETF net assets and are exposed to securities market conditions and fund flows.

Q3 2026 revenue and operating profit eased slightly from the Q2 peak. 2026 revenue estimates diverge sharply across brokers, at KRW 55 billion and KRW 41.4 billion. The cause of the 2024 drop in owner-attributable net profit was not identified in this review.

Strengths such as index growth and the fixed-cost structure and weaknesses such as market dependence and estimate dispersion are weighed equally here, and the next checkpoints are the Q4 and full-year 2026 disclosures.
