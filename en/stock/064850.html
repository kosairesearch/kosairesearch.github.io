02

Business structure

FnGuide is a financial information provider founded in 2000, operating financial information services, indexes and fund evaluation as its main businesses. Its 2025 annual revenue mix was 57% financial information services, 30.4% indexes, 5.3% fund evaluation and 7.3% other.

Financial information services, the core business, bundle sell-side research reports, corporate financial data and earnings consensus, with the FnGuide research platform and WiseReport as flagship services.

The index business develops and calculates the benchmark indexes underlying ETFs and ETNs, and generated KRW 10.8 billion of revenue in 2025. Of the 173 ETFs listed in 2025, 15 used FnGuide indexes, accounting for 9% of total listings.

Key customers are financial institutions such as securities firms, asset managers and investment advisers, along with universities and research institutes. Industry analysis points to data acquisition, switching costs and network effects that make new entry difficult in financial data.

In May 2024 the company set up Findxx, a joint venture with global index provider Indxx, to pursue overseas listings of Korean indexes. Fund evaluation is a small share at around 5% of revenue.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 28.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 29.6 billion in 2023, KRW 31.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 35.4 billion in 2025. Growth accelerated from 4.1% in 2023 to 6.8% in 2024 and 12.0% in 2025, and revenue has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit was flat at KRW 6.07 billion in 2022 and KRW 6.07 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 7.65 billion in 2024 (up 26.0%) and KRW 10.7 billion in 2025 (up 39.4%), extending growth for two years.

In 2025 revenue grew 12.0% while operating profit grew 39.4%, lifting the operating margin from 24.2% in 2024 to 30.1%. One securities analyst noted that about 80% of total costs are fixed in nature, so incremental revenue flows heavily into profit.

Controlling-shareholder net profit, however, fell from KRW 7.94 billion in 2022 and KRW 7.76 billion in 2023 to KRW 4.07 billion in 2024 (down 47.6%), before recovering to KRW 9.89 billion in 2025 (up 143.1%).

A financial statement table compiled by Hana Securities shows negative financial income and negative associate and subsidiary income in 2024, which appears to explain the gap between higher operating profit and lower net profit.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 8.41 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.69 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 14.8 billion and KRW 7.73 billion in Q3 2026. Operating profit peaked at KRW 7.86 billion in Q2 2026, and Q3 2026 was 1.7% lower quarter on quarter.

Controlling-shareholder net profit bottomed at KRW 2.52 billion in Q4 2025 and then rose to KRW 4.85 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 6.16 billion in Q2 and KRW 6.26 billion in Q3, increasing for three consecutive quarters.

05

Industry analysis

The Korean ETF market reached about KRW 348 trillion in net assets in early 2026, and its product mix is broadening from passive funds toward thematic and active products.

ETFs tracking FnGuide indexes passed KRW 45 trillion in February 2026, and nine of the ten largest Korean equity theme index products use FnGuide indexes. The Korea IR Council research center estimated that index revenue growth from 2021 to 2025 averaged 20 percentage points above KOSPI index gains.

The same report assumed more conservative growth than in the past given the larger revenue base. Index fees scale with ETF assets, so the business is exposed to ETF fund flows and broader market swings. Analysts have described the company as having a near-monopoly position in Korean financial data.

Its index competitiveness rests on proprietary data infrastructure and automation of index development, and it has more than 160 ETF listings to date.

06

Outlook

In February 2026 the Korea IR Council research center projected 2026 revenue of KRW 41.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 13.3 billion.

On disclosed figures, operating profit for January to September 2026 totals KRW 20.27 billion (KRW 4.68 billion in Q1, KRW 7.86 billion in Q2 and KRW 7.73 billion in Q3), already above that projection. The projection dates from February 2026, so it does not reflect later quarters.

Index revenue rose 184% year on year to KRW 5.44 billion in Q1 2026, and January to September index revenue was reported at KRW 22.4 billion. The company is pursuing overseas listings of Korean indexes through Findxx, its joint venture with Indxx.

The same report noted plans to extend into bond indexes, overseas indexes and digital asset indexes. On capital use, the sale of idle assets with a book value of KRW 26.8 billion has been delayed, and that was flagged as an open task. Achieving substantive results in the B2C business also remains unfinished.

The next checkpoints are the full-year results to be confirmed in the 2026 annual report, the index revenue share, and progress on the idle asset sale.

08

Bull factors

Expansion of the index business

Index revenue reached KRW 10.8 billion in 2025, up 39.2%, and rose 184% year on year to KRW 5.44 billion in Q1 2026. Financial information services revenue also grew 15% in the same quarter, according to press reports.

As of February 2026, ETFs tracking FnGuide indexes exceeded KRW 45 trillion, and nine of the ten largest Korean equity theme products use its indexes.

Improving operating margin structure

The operating margin rose from 24.2% in 2024 to 30.1% in 2025. One securities analyst said about 80% of costs are fixed, so revenue growth flows heavily into profit. The operating margin for Q3 2026 was reported at 52.1%.

Balance sheet and profit recovery

Equity has built up well beyond liabilities, which has eased the balance-sheet burden since 2022. Operating profit for January to September 2026 totals KRW 20.27 billion, above the KRW 13.3 billion full-year figure projected in February 2026. Controlling-shareholder net profit has also risen for three consecutive quarters since Q1 2026.

09

Bear factors

Profit disconnect risk, as in 2024

Operating profit rose to KRW 7.65 billion in 2024, yet controlling-shareholder net profit fell 47.6% to KRW 4.07 billion. Hana Securities' financial table shows negative financial income and negative associate and subsidiary income in 2024. Non-operating items can swing earnings, so operating performance alone does not explain net profit.

Idle assets and the B2C challenge

The sale of idle assets with a KRW 26.8 billion book value has been delayed, which is flagged as a challenge for capital use. The Korea IR Council research center also noted that tangible results from the B2C business remain unfinished. Progress on both items can be checked in the 2026 annual report.

Market linkage of index fees

Index fees scale with ETF asset size, so a shift in ETF flows would affect index revenue. Indexes made up 30.4% of revenue in 2025, a growing share. Financial information services remain the largest business at 57%.

10

Risk factors

ETF and theme-market swings

Index revenue moves with ETF product design and asset size. A cooling of thematic products or a change in asset managers' strategies could affect index usage and fees. The high concentration of top products in the domestic theme index market is also a source of volatility.

Non-operating items and net profit volatility

As in 2024, net profit can fall even when operating profit rises if financial or associate income worsens. Controlling-shareholder net profit and total net profit differ depending on non-controlling interests, so the two should be tracked separately. The timing of idle asset disposals also affects earnings and capital structure.

Preliminary disclosure versus final figures

The third-quarter 2026 results were disclosed as preliminary figures based on internal accounts, with external audit review not yet complete. The filing states that final results may differ. Final numbers should be rechecked in the periodic report.

12

Overall view

FnGuide's disclosed revenue has risen every year since 2022, and its operating margin climbed to 30.1% in 2025, making the improvement in its earnings structure visible in the numbers.

The index business has become a growth pillar alongside the expansion of ETF net assets, and operating profit for January to September 2026 exceeded the February 2026 research projection. Net profit, however, fell sharply in 2024 because of non-operating items, so the quality of earnings matters.

Total equity of KRW 68.9 billion and a 28.3% debt ratio show a relatively stable balance sheet, while the delayed idle asset sale and the B2C challenge remain open. Valuation can be compared against the historical price-to-book band and against peers, but the interpretation changes as earnings estimates are revised.

Bullish and bearish factors coexist. The next checkpoints are the confirmed results in the 2026 annual report and Q1 2027 results, along with how non-operating items appear in the next periodic report.
