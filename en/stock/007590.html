Quarterly swings widen at pesticide maker, back in loss in Q3 2026

02

Business structure

Dongbang Agro was founded in 1971 as a manufacturer and seller of agricultural chemicals and listed on the KOSPI in 1977. Its main product line is emulsifiable concentrates, followed by granules, wettable powders and dusts.

By segment, 2026 Q2 sales were 60% emulsifiable concentrates, 23% granules, 15% wettable powders, 1% other and 1% dusts. The company has a subsidiary, Dongbang Green Farm.

It continues to invest in automated production lines to make mixed formulations and is broadening its product mix with multifunctional eco-friendly fertilizers.

Active ingredients are sourced through a strategic partnership with leading global technical-grade manufacturers, which underpins its product development and supply to farms. The company says it will pursue new substances by drawing on its product development capability and marketing strategy.

Its labor-saving formulation development targets products that reduce farmers' workload, and the company credits it with supporting sales. On the customer side, a February 2026 pesticide supply contract with a domestic agricultural cooperative's economic holding company is disclosed, equal to 50.48% of recent revenue.

According to a foreign financial information site, Sumitomo Corporation (14.32%) and Sumitomo Chemical (5.79%) were listed as holders as of March 31, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 146.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 164.3 billion in 2023 (up 12.0% year on year), KRW 170.2 billion in 2024 (up 3.6%) and KRW 179 billion in 2025 (up 5.1%), growing for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 9.34 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.6 billion in 2023 (up 45.5%) and KRW 14.6 billion in 2024 (up 7.5%), then fell to KRW 12.7 billion in 2025 (down 13.4%). The operating margin was 6.4% in 2022, 8.3% in 2023, 8.6% in 2024 and 7.1% in 2025, easing after the 2024 peak.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 6.78 billion in 2022 to KRW 12.1 billion in 2023 (up 79.0%), then declined to KRW 9.76 billion in 2024 (down 19.5%) and KRW 9.75 billion in 2025 (down 0.1%), a two-year decline.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 6.79 billion, KRW 12.2 billion, KRW 9.79 billion and KRW 9.77 billion in the same years, so the gap with controlling-shareholder income is about KRW 0.1 billion to KRW 1 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 8.73 billion in 2025, up from KRW 327 million in 2024, and was negative in 2023. The debt ratio stayed in the low-40% range, at 43.6% in 2022, 40.3% in 2023, 43.7% in 2024 and 43.8% in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 18.9 billion with an operating loss of KRW 5.59 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.71 billion. Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 15.1 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 2.4 billion, so the loss narrowed but persisted.

Q1 2026 revenue jumped to KRW 104.4 billion (up 589.9% from the prior quarter) and operating profit reached KRW 17.2 billion, a swing to profit, with net income attributable to controlling shareholders at KRW 12.7 billion. The disclosed table alone does not pin down the cause of the Q1 revenue surge.

Q2 2026 revenue fell to KRW 48.9 billion (down 53.2% quarter on quarter), operating profit to KRW 2.14 billion (down 87.6%) and net income attributable to controlling shareholders to KRW 1.81 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 19.8 billion (up 4.5% year on year), but the company posted an operating loss of KRW 4.95 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.59 billion. The operating loss was smaller than a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 9.26 billion.

One external aggregator reports Q1 2026 revenue up 0.3%. Compared with the chemical sector as a whole, Dongbang Agro's 2025 annual operating margin of 7.07% sits well above the sector average of 1.00%, based on 121 chemical-sector stocks in an Alphasquare aggregation.

In the same aggregation, its market capitalization ranks 81st within the sector, in the lower-middle range. Active ingredients are sourced through partnerships with leading global technical-grade manufacturers, so the supply side relies on those alliances.

In domestic sales, the contract with the agricultural cooperative's economic holding company equals 50.48% of recent revenue, which makes dependence on a single customer a point to watch.

Direct comparison of industry-wide supply and demand indicators with disclosed data is limited, so the position is best read through company disclosures and sector-level aggregates.

The contract runs through December 31, 2026. Whether it drove the quarterly swing will be checked against future periodic reports.

Balance sheet stability

Bear factors

Sharp quarterly volatility

Q2 2026 revenue fell to KRW 48.9 billion, down 53.2% from the prior quarter, and operating profit dropped to KRW 2.14 billion, down 87.6%. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 19.8 billion, and the company returned to an operating loss of KRW 4.95 billion. The Q3 operating loss was narrower than a year earlier. The thinner quarterly earnings base after the Q1 peak is the starting point of the bear case.

Slowing earnings momentum

Operating profit fell 13.4% from KRW 14.6 billion in 2024 to KRW 12.7 billion in 2025, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined in both 2024 and 2025. The operating margin eased from 8.6% in 2024 to 7.1% in 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net income over the latest four quarters totals KRW 9.26 billion. Whether the post-peak decline in annual earnings continues is the central question for the bear case.

Structural change in pesticide use

Westernized diets and climate change are cited as factors reducing pesticide consumption. The company says it is responding through labor-saving formulations and marketing. Whether these efforts have offset weaker demand cannot be judged from disclosures alone.

In a period of shifting demand structure, the pace of revenue recovery could be limited, which is the core bearish point.

Overall view

Dongbang Agro is a pesticide maker centered on emulsifiable concentrates. Revenue grew for three consecutive years through 2025, but operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders have declined since 2024.

Q1 2026 brought both revenue and a profit, but revenue and earnings fell sharply after that, and Q3 returned to an operating loss. Bullish factors include the payout target of at least 40%, a debt ratio that has stayed in the 40% range, and a share price below book value per share.
