Report overview

Polaris AI: Physical AI Pivot Amid Shrinking Sales

Polaris AI's sales have fallen for three straight years since 2023 and its 2025 operating loss widened, while it builds physical AI businesses such as humanoid robots and industrial safety AI on the cash flow of its fashion-led F&C division.

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Polaris AI, listed on KOSDAQ in 2002, has expanded from wireless communications and system integration (SI) into AI convergence businesses. According to its company profile, it is preparing to reorganize its IT division into an AI convergence division.

Its operations span the fashion and culture (F&C) division, which includes the Kipling brand, and the AI convergence division covering SI, AI solutions and wireless communications. Based on a Butler data summary of 2026 second-quarter sales, the mix was F&C 60%, ITS 26% and ICT 14%.

The F&C division handles domestic distribution and licensing for Kipling and is pushing brand renewal and online channels to reach Gen Z consumers. The AI convergence division has built its base on infrastructure clients such as public agencies, subways and railways.

Physical AI has been added more recently as a new growth axis. As the domestic official distributor of UBTECH Robotics, the company sells industrial robots such as Walker S2 and the U1 series, and is developing models that combine its vision AI with the 'Polaris Edge' on-site AI device.

It also cites the use of group manufacturing sites as demonstration bases as a differentiator. In the same IT sector, Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever compete in the SI and AI solutions market.

04

Earnings analysis

On DART-filed results, annual sales were KRW 80.9 billion in 2022, KRW 59.4 billion in 2023, KRW 53.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 43.6 billion in 2025. Sales fell 26.5% year on year in 2023, 10.7% in 2024 and 17.8% in 2025, declining for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit of KRW 1.46 billion in 2023 turned the business profitable from an operating loss of KRW 2.36 billion in 2022, but it swung back to an operating loss of KRW 1.31 billion in 2024, and the 2025 operating loss of KRW 4.69 billion continued the deficit with a wider loss.

The company has posted operating losses for two consecutive years, from 2024 to 2025. Operating margins were -2.9% in 2022, 2.5% in 2023, -2.5% in 2024 and -10.7% in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 920 million in 2023 and KRW 1.42 billion in 2024, but it turned to a net loss of KRW 783 million in 2025, and net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 783 million in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 operating loss mainly to one-off losses from inventory valuation in its fashion business. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 10.7 billion in 2022 and KRW 13.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 2.31 billion in 2024, and was -KRW 4.49 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, sales of KRW 9.78 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 fell 14.4% from the prior quarter, and the operating loss widened to KRW 3.29 billion.

The first quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 345 million on sales of KRW 10.3 billion, and the second quarter recorded sales of KRW 11.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 499 million.

Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 8.23 billion fell 25.6% from the prior quarter and 27.9% from a year earlier, with an operating loss of KRW 177 million.

Over the last four quarters, from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, the cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.58 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for AI in Korean industry is shifting toward on-device AI chips with low power and low latency, as physical AI and agentic AI spread. Polaris AI has said it is upgrading edge-device vision AI for industrial sites using NPUs from the domestic AI chip company DEEPX.

DEEPX began mass production of its 'DX-M1' on-device AI chip in 2025. On the robot side, UBTECH said in its June 2026 global launch event that cumulative orders for the U1 series exceeded 13,361 units. That figure comes from UBTECH rather than from Polaris AI, so it should be separated from domestic deployment volume.

In industrial safety, the company demonstrated an industrial robot performing logistics tasks at the 2026 Korea Safety Industry Expo in Busan in September 2026. In SI and AI solutions, the competitive field is led by large IT services firms such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever.

Polaris AI is smaller than these players but emphasizes its wireless communications SI experience with public and infrastructure clients and manufacturing references from group affiliates.

Because the robot hardware comes from an overseas manufacturer, supply stability and margin structure may depend on UBTECH's schedule. Capital expenditure trends in the manufacturing and logistics sites it serves are difficult to read from company filings alone.

06

Outlook

The company's stated plans put weight on developing physical AI as a medium- to long-term growth axis.

Polaris AI took part in the 2026 Robot World exhibition at KINTEX from November 4 to 7, 2026, with UBTECH headquarters staff, and said it would hold robot adoption consultations with manufacturing, logistics and service companies in Korea.

At the group level, it plans to combine generative AI software and manufacturing process know-how from affiliates such as Polaris Office, Handysoft and Polaris Sewon with robot hardware to build a physical AI ecosystem. Demonstrations were first carried out at an affiliate's pharmaceutical production site.

Polaris AI completed a 16-channel installation of Polaris EYE at the Baln plant of Polaris AI Pharma and started operations as the first commercial case, and it plans to extend supply to manufacturing, construction, logistics, railways and public safety.

Over the medium to long term, it is considering combining its vision and edge AI with UBTECH robots so that robots patrol sites and detect hazards. It also plans to expand trials applying domestic NPUs to UBTECH robots.

However, the scale and timing with which these plans translate into revenue have not yet appeared in filed figures. The 2026 annual results will be confirmed in the annual business report due by the end of March 2027. The next checkpoint is therefore the revenue mix and operating margin in that 2026 business report.

08

Bull factors

Early Position in Physical AI Distribution

As the domestic official distributor of UBTECH Robotics, the company is introducing industrial and consumer humanoid lineups in Korea. UBTECH said cumulative U1 series orders exceeded 13,361 units at its June 2026 global launch event. At the September 2026 safety expo, the company demonstrated an industrial robot performing logistics tasks.

Quarterly Operating Profit Recovery

Operating profit of KRW 345 million in the first quarter of 2026 turned the business profitable from the prior quarter's loss, and the second quarter's operating profit of KRW 499 million rose 44.8% from the prior quarter.

Compared with the operating losses of KRW 635 million in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 3.29 billion in the fourth quarter, the size of losses has narrowed. Whether this trend continues is the checkpoint that will shape the direction of full-year profit.

Balance Sheet Headroom and Group Test Sites

Total liabilities were KRW 12 billion at the end of 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell to 12.6% from 41.0% in 2022. Total equity stood at KRW 95 billion.

The company plans to use manufacturing sites within the group as test beds, gather data, and then expand into manufacturing, construction, logistics, railways and public safety.

09

Bear factors

Sustained Revenue Decline

Annual sales declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, reaching KRW 43.6 billion in 2025. Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 8.23 billion fell 27.9% from a year earlier. WiseReport's company monitor recorded a 4.6% year-on-year decline in standalone sales for the first half of 2026.

Renewed Operating Losses and Accumulated Deficits

The 2025 operating loss widened to KRW 4.69 billion, and the fourth-quarter 2025 operating loss of KRW 3.29 billion added heavily to that full-year deficit. The third quarter of 2026 swung back to an operating loss of KRW 177 million.

The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 3.58 billion.

Uncertain Monetization of Physical AI

Revenue from physical AI has not yet appeared in filed figures. The robot hardware depends on distribution of products from an overseas manufacturer. Operating cash flow turned negative at -KRW 4.49 billion in 2025, so how far internal cash can fund new-business investment is a point to watch.

10

Risk factors

Fashion Dependence and Inventory Risk

The Kipling-led F&C division relies mainly on domestic distribution and licensing, so it is strongly affected by consumer demand and online channel strategy. As the 2025 operating loss showed, inventory valuation losses in the fashion business can hit earnings as one-off items and increase volatility. Inventory management and channel efficiency are variables for profit in coming quarters.

Delays in Physical AI Commercialization

Robot hardware and domestic adoption contracts depend on the schedules of an overseas manufacturer and on capital spending decisions by domestic companies. The first commercial case for industrial safety vision AI is a 16-channel deployment at a pharmaceutical plant, so the reference base remains limited. If commercialization slows, investment costs could hit earnings before revenue arrives.

Governance and Affiliate Linkages

According to fnguide, related parties including the largest shareholder hold 41.07%, and many business links among group affiliates make the transaction structure complex. The company said the share consolidation decided in March 2026 aims at share price stability and enhancing corporate value.

With net losses attributable to controlling shareholders continuing over the last four quarters, related-party transaction disclosures are the next item to watch.

12

Overall view

Polaris AI's sales have fallen for three straight years since 2023, its 2025 operating loss widened to KRW 4.69 billion, and it is now in a transition phase, building physical AI as a new growth axis.

Strengths in its filed results include the debt-to-equity ratio falling to 12.6% in 2025 from 41.0% in 2022 and operating profit in the first and second quarters of 2026.

On the other side, the third quarter of 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 177 million, and the cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters of KRW 3.58 billion is a weakness.

Physical AI has entered the commercialization stage through UBTECH robot distribution, the first commercial deployment of Polaris EYE and trials of domestic NPUs, but its revenue scale has not yet appeared in filed figures.

The business still relies on cash flow from the F&C division, so fashion consumption and inventory management are major variables for results.

On valuation, price-to-earnings cannot be calculated from earnings, and the share price sits in a low range relative to net assets, so whether profitability persists and whether asset value converts into business value are central to interpretation.

The bull case is operating profit continuing quarter after quarter alongside visible physical AI revenue. The bear case is operating losses widening again and new-business investment hitting earnings first.

The next checkpoints are full-year operating profit or loss in the 2026 annual business report and the reflection of physical AI revenue in the first-quarter 2027 report. This report does not present an investment opinion or a target price.
