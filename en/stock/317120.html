Ranix: Narrower Losses, Quantum-Secure Chip Test

Ranix's operating loss narrowed to KRW 668 million in the third quarter of 2026, but the four straight years of losses from 2022 through 2025 and two years of falling sales since 2024 have not yet reversed.

02

Business structure

Ranix designs automotive wireless communication and security semiconductors. Its core is the Automotive MCU segment, which the company's profile says holds a share of more than 91% in the domestic ETCS-related market (Before Market).

On the communication side it has focused on commercializing integrated C-V2X and 5G-V2X solutions, and at a 2025 investor briefing it stressed that it is the only domestic company with an integrated 5G V2X solution.

In security, the quantum-secure chip RQ2622 was supplied as its first mass-production product in early Q3 2025. A post-quantum cryptography (PQC) security chip is in development with customer sample (CS) completion targeted for late 2026.

In September 2025 the company unveiled a prototype applying Korean PQC algorithms on an FPGA, and said product inquiries and technology development requests followed. Production is outsourced to foundries, so the model is closer to a design-centric fabless structure.

According to 2022 IR material, 97 to 98 percent of domestic automotive MCUs were imported, and the company pursued MCU localization. It began developing a China-specific DSRC chip in 2016 and set up a Shanghai subsidiary in 2021 to pursue China's ETCS automotive market. Because sales rely heavily on automotive electronics demand, shifts in that market flow directly into results.

04

Earnings analysis

On a consolidated basis, Ranix's sales rose from KRW 11 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.5 billion in 2023, the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period, and then fell 33.2% to KRW 7.71 billion in 2024 and 3.2% to KRW 7.47 billion in 2025.

The operating loss widened every year, from KRW 1.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.96 billion in 2023, KRW 5.42 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.05 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from -16.4% in 2022 to -25.6%, -70.3% and -81.0% in 2025.

In 2024 and 2025 the loss grew faster than sales shrank, which points to a higher fixed-cost burden relative to revenue.

The loss attributable to controlling shareholders grew from KRW 4.93 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.77 billion in 2025, and the net loss including non-controlling interests was also KRW 7.77 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 193.1% in 2022 to 103.9% in 2025, with year-end equity of KRW 21.3 billion and total liabilities of KRW 22.1 billion.

Quarterly sales peaked at KRW 2.11 billion in the third quarter of 2025, fell to KRW 1.79 billion and KRW 1.56 billion, rebounded to KRW 1.94 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (up 23.9% from the prior quarter), and dropped to KRW 1.49 billion in the third quarter (down 23.0% quarter on quarter and 29.3% year on year).

The operating loss was largest at KRW 1.75 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and narrowed to KRW 1.57 billion, KRW 1.55 billion and KRW 668 million in the next three quarters.

The narrowing in the third quarter came with lower sales, so whether it reflects cost improvement or timing of spending needs to be separated in the notes to the 2026 annual report.

The controlling-shareholder net loss for the latest four quarters totals KRW 7.11 billion, and operating cash outflows were KRW -5.68 billion in 2024 and KRW -5.12 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Ranix operates in automotive MCUs, V2X communication chips and automotive and defense security chips. A 2022 IR document said 97 to 98 percent of domestic automotive MCUs are imported, and the company treated localization as a target.

Successful localization could absorb part of that import-substitution demand, but the timing of commercialization and its revenue contribution do not appear separately in the reported results. In V2X, C-V2X and 5G-V2X approaches are developing side by side as autonomous driving spreads.

Government support for 5G-based next-generation vehicle communication technology has also been reported. Interest in post-quantum cryptography rose after the prototype was unveiled in September 2025, and press reports said the share price hit its daily limit up at that time.

On the cycle, Ranix's sales have been in a decline since the 2023 peak, so whether recovery in automotive parts demand translates into a sales rebound is the key question.

Among listed peers, memory-focused large caps such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix have a different business structure, while Ranix concentrates on designing automotive communication and security chips.

Compared with equipment and materials peers, Ranix has a small revenue base, and its security-chip business is still at an early revenue stage.

Outlook

The nearest milestone the company has set for its PQC security chip is customer sample (CS) completion in late 2026. A September 2025 disclosure outlined outsourcing production to a foundry in the second half of 2025, with mass-production sample chips targeted for completion in the first half of 2026.

Whether this plan converts into revenue can be judged from the business section and revenue breakdown of the annual report. On March 3, 2026, the company decided to dispose of 25,710 treasury shares, citing operating funds, funding resources and shareholder value.

The planned disposal equals about 0.17% of total issued shares, and the company disclosed that the dilution impact would be minor. At the regular shareholders' meeting on March 26, 2026, the agenda including approval of the 23rd fiscal year financial statements passed as proposed.

Sales rebounded in the second quarter of 2026 but fell again in the third, so it is not yet separable in disclosures whether the recovery is temporary or lasting. The MCU localization and the third phase of 5G V2X, the CCU reference module validation, were presented in a 2022 IR document as starting from 2024.

How those timelines fed into revenue cannot be read directly from the reported results alone. The 2026 annual report's full-year sales and operating loss will therefore be the first regular indicator of how much visibility the security-chip business offers.

08

Bull factors

Post-quantum security chip progress

The RQ2622 quantum-secure chip was supplied as its first mass-production product in early Q3 2025, and the PQC security chip is in development with customer sample completion targeted for late 2026. After the September 2025 prototype, the company said product inquiries and development requests followed. Its security solutions extending into automotive and defense are cited as a bull argument.

Automotive MCU share base

The company profile puts its share of the domestic ETCS-related market above 91%. The 2022 IR material cited a 97 to 98 percent import dependence for domestic automotive MCUs, so localization progress could absorb import-substitution demand. An existing share base may serve as the starting point for the security-chip business.

Quarterly loss narrowing

The third-quarter 2026 operating loss of KRW 668 million was smaller than KRW 1.75 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 1.55 billion in the second quarter of 2026. The controlling-shareholder net loss also narrowed to KRW 961 million from KRW 2.14 billion in the prior quarter.

Sales rose 23.9% in the second quarter of 2026 but fell back to KRW 1.49 billion in the third, so the weight of this argument depends on whether sales recovery follows.

Bear factors

Shrinking revenue base

Sales fell from KRW 11.5 billion in 2023 to KRW 7.47 billion in 2025, down two years in a row. Third-quarter 2026 sales of KRW 1.49 billion were 29.3% lower year on year. With a small revenue base, the operating leverage from any loss narrowing is limited, which is the bear argument.

Four straight years of losses

Operating and controlling-shareholder net losses persisted for four straight years from 2022 through 2025, with a 2025 operating margin of -81.0%. The 2025 controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 7.77 billion was larger than in 2022.

The latest four quarters total KRW 7.11 billion in losses, and five consecutive loss-making quarters are reported.

Cash outflows and funding needs

Operating cash flow was KRW -5.68 billion in 2024 and KRW -5.12 billion in 2025, so cash outflows persisted. Total liabilities of KRW 22.1 billion at the end of 2025 are close to shareholders' equity of KRW 21.3 billion.

The March 2026 treasury share disposal disclosure cites securing operating funds as one purpose, which points to funding needs.

10

Risk factors

Commercialization risk

The PQC chip's customer sample completion is targeted for late 2026, and mass-production sample chips were planned for the first half of 2026. Because production is outsourced to foundries, schedules and yields depend on external factors.

If interest from the prototype stage does not turn into mass-production orders, development spending may not be recovered through sales.

Financing risk

At the end of 2025, shareholders' equity was KRW 21.3 billion against total liabilities of KRW 22.1 billion, and operating cash flow remained negative. The treasury share disposal was disclosed partly to secure operating funds. If additional funding takes the form of new shares, shareholder dilution could follow. Even with a lower debt ratio, continued cash outflows would reduce financial flexibility.

Concentration and timing risk

Sales and the core business concentrate on automotive MCUs and ETCS-related markets, so demand shifts in that market pass through directly. The company has pursued China's ETCS automotive market, and overseas entry is shaped by local regulation and competition.

How the 2024 target timelines presented in the 2022 IR material fed into business results cannot be read directly from the reported figures.

Overall view

Ranix's reported results show falling sales since the 2023 peak and widening losses through 2025. Full-year 2025 sales were KRW 7.47 billion, the operating loss was KRW 6.05 billion, and the operating margin was -81.0%.

In the third quarter of 2026 the operating loss narrowed to KRW 668 million and the controlling-shareholder net loss to KRW 961 million, but sales of KRW 1.49 billion were 29.3% lower year on year.

Strengths include the domestic MCU share and mass-production progress on the PQC security chip, while weaknesses include the shrinking revenue base, persistent cash outflows and funding needs. Whether quarterly loss narrowing comes with a sales recovery is the key variable separating the bull and bear cases.

The next checkpoints are completion of PQC customer samples and the full-year operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report. The share price sits in a net-loss period over the latest four quarters, so no P/E ratio is calculated, and the price is near 1x net assets.
