T3 Entertainment: Solutions Growth Joins Audition Royalties

T3 Entertainment's solutions business is growing alongside Audition royalty income, and nine-month 2026 preliminary revenue rose 28% year on year, while quarterly operating profit has declined for two straight quarters since its first-quarter 2026 peak.

02

Business structure

T3 Entertainment runs two consolidated business lines, game services and solutions. The core of the game services segment is Audition, a rhythm action dance game the company developed in-house in 2004, and royalty and license income from long-running overseas service is its main revenue source.

More than 90% of Audition IP revenue is generated overseas. The solutions segment focuses on digital twin and geospatial software, spectral imaging and AI-based data analytics, and its customer base is widening from private firms such as construction and telecom companies to local governments and research institutes.

The other segment covers drone-related businesses, including drone distribution and drone-based industrial solutions.

Cumulative preliminary segment revenue through September 2026 was KRW 38.5 billion for game services, KRW 27.1 billion for solutions and KRW 1.3 billion for other, with solutions up 118% and game services up 4% year on year (announced November 16, 2026).

In competition, game royalties depend on how long an IP keeps its commercial life, while solutions compete for digital twin and geospatial demand with other listed IT and software companies.

As of June 5, 2026, the controlling shareholder group led by Kim Ki-young held 35.84%, VIP Asset Management held 18.30%, and treasury shares accounted for 9.17%.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 76.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 51 billion in 2023 (down 33.1% year on year), then rose to KRW 59.3 billion in 2024 (up 16.4%) and KRW 69.5 billion in 2025 (up 17.2%), growing for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 12.8 billion in 2022, KRW 7.56 billion in 2023 (down 41.0%), KRW 10.5 billion in 2024 (up 39.2%) and KRW 17 billion in 2025 (up 61.7%), and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022–2025 period. The operating margin moved from 16.8% in 2022 to 14.8% in 2023, 17.7% in 2024 and 24.5% in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 improvement to higher sales of its main game and a broad improvement across subsidiaries. Net income attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 11.1 billion in 2022, KRW 5.6 billion in 2023 (down 49.5%), KRW 11.5 billion in 2024 (up 104.6%) and KRW 16.9 billion in 2025 (up 47.8%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 12.5 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 929 million in 2023, KRW 12.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 20.6 billion in 2025, and the gap between that figure and owners' net income in 2025 reflects the non-controlling share.

Operating cash flow was KRW 13.2 billion in 2022, KRW 1.5 billion in 2023, KRW 10.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.69 billion in 2025, so cash generation in 2025 was small relative to operating profit of KRW 17 billion.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 110.5 billion, of which KRW 95.4 billion belonged to owners of the parent, and the debt ratio fell from 27.0% in 2022 to 12.9% in 2025. On a quarterly basis, third-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 16.7 billion, operating profit KRW 2.97 billion and owners' net income KRW 3 billion.

Fourth-quarter 2025 revenue rose to KRW 17.1 billion (up 2.6% from the prior quarter), operating profit to KRW 4.65 billion (up 56.5%) and owners' net income to KRW 5.5 billion (up 83.3%).

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue reached KRW 22.1 billion (up 29.1% from the prior quarter), operating profit KRW 5.84 billion (up 25.5%) and owners' net income KRW 4.26 billion, the operating profit peak.

Second-quarter revenue of KRW 25.8 billion was the highest quarterly figure in the disclosed series, but operating profit fell 11.4% to KRW 5.17 billion.

In the third quarter, revenue was KRW 19 billion and operating profit KRW 4.16 billion (up 39.8% year on year), while owners' net income fell to KRW 1.92 billion (down 36.2% year on year). Owners' net income for the last four quarters totaled KRW 16.1 billion.

The reason operating profit rose while owners' net income fell in the third quarter should be checked against the line items in the next periodic report.

05

Industry analysis

The game segment rests on a single franchise, Audition, and its overseas royalty structure. Long-running overseas service is a stable source of cash, but growth stays limited unless new IP launches succeed.

Cumulative game services revenue rose only 4% year on year through September 2026, which illustrates the structure. The solutions segment is riding a period of rising digital transformation demand.

In its first-quarter 2026 results announcement the company said it would make solutions a core medium-term growth pillar, and as of June 2026 it cited digital twin, spectral imaging and drone-based industrial solutions as its focus areas.

Broadening the customer base from private companies to local governments and research institutes is consistent with reducing reliance on any single buyer.

In industry classification, T3 Entertainment sits in KOSDAQ IT services and software, where peers include NAVER, Kakao and NHN, which combine games and platforms, and Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, which are centered on IT services.

Game royalties follow the lifecycle of an IP, while solutions follow public and private IT investment cycles. Because the two businesses run on different cycles, a shift in revenue mix can change the character of earnings volatility.

06

Outlook

At its nine-month 2026 results announcement, the company said it would keep raising profitability in both its game IP and solutions businesses in the fourth quarter to sustain annual growth.

The corporate value enhancement plan it disclosed on March 20, 2026 targets global revenue expansion anchored in core IP and steadier royalty growth, and it pairs this with stronger shareholder returns.

On the return side, the company keeps its four-times-a-year quarterly dividend system and is executing a KRW 4 billion share buyback, with partial cancellation also under way. As of the first-half 2026 results announcement, cumulative shareholder returns since listing were about KRW 58.6 billion.

In solutions, the plan as of June 2026 was to strengthen technology competitiveness and expand in drone-based industrial solutions and spectral imaging. Royalty income depends on sustaining the long-term overseas service of the Audition IP, so keeping that service running matters while no new game IP is in market.

The next confirmation points are whether the year-end quarterly dividend and treasury share cancellation are executed, and whether solutions keep a share of roughly 40% of consolidated revenue in the 2026 annual results.

08

Bull factors

Solutions growth diversifies the business

Cumulative preliminary solutions revenue through September 2026 was KRW 27.1 billion, up 118% year on year and about 40% of consolidated sales. According to the company, the customer base is widening from a game-centered base to construction and telecom firms, local governments and research institutes. The company has said solutions will be a core medium-term growth pillar.

High profitability from overseas royalties

More than 90% of Audition IP revenue comes from overseas, and the cumulative separate operating margin through September 2026 was 60% (preliminary, announced November 16, 2026). The consolidated operating margin rose from 16.8% in 2022 to 24.5% in 2025. Game services revenue grew only 4%, but the company describes its earnings base as stable.

Shareholder returns formalized

Quarterly dividends were introduced in 2026 and paid in each of the first three quarters, with a stated policy of using at least 50% of separate operating profit for dividends and share cancellation. A KRW 4 billion share buyback is also under way.

The company's preliminary announcement put consolidated cash and cash equivalents at KRW 65.6 billion at the end of September 2026.

Bear factors

Two straight quarters of lower operating profit

Operating profit peaked at KRW 5.84 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then declined to KRW 5.17 billion in the second quarter and KRW 4.16 billion in the third.

Third-quarter owners' net income fell 36.2% year on year to KRW 1.92 billion, while operating profit in the same quarter rose 39.8%, so the two measures moved in opposite directions.

Slowing growth in the game segment

Cumulative game services revenue through September 2026 was KRW 38.5 billion, up only 4% year on year. Because more than 90% of Audition IP revenue comes from overseas, the segment is sensitive to conditions in overseas service markets. Royalties can move with the life of an IP, so growth may stay limited until a new IP gains traction.

Volatile operating cash flow

Operating cash flow in 2025 was KRW 2.69 billion, far below operating profit of KRW 17 billion in the same year. The annual series also swung widely, from KRW 13.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.5 billion in 2023 and KRW 10.9 billion in 2024.

Because the conversion of profit into cash has varied year to year, the stability of dividend funding needs to be checked.

10

Risk factors

IP concentration

Revenue rests heavily on the Audition IP, so aging of the franchise or changes in the overseas service environment could affect the entire game segment. New IP launches and their results reach reported earnings with a lag, which adds short-term volatility. Given the high overseas share, changes in overseas policy and platform conditions are also items to monitor.

Durability of solutions growth

Solutions revenue grew 118%, but project-based sales can fluctuate from quarter to quarter. Backlog and customer-level revenue mix are disclosed only in limited form, so the durability of growth has to be tracked through cumulative quarterly results.

Because the technology overlaps with other listed companies, the degree of differentiation is also a point to monitor.

Earnings swings and dividend funding

Quarterly dividends and buybacks are tied to a policy of using at least 50% of separate operating profit, so a decline in quarterly operating profit reduces the funding available.

When operating profit and net income diverge, as in the third quarter of 2026 when owners' net income fell to KRW 1.92 billion, the size of shareholder returns becomes harder to project. Even with the policy unchanged, funding moves with quarterly results.

12

Overall view

The disclosed results show a recovery: after the 2023 low, revenue, operating profit and owners' net income all grew in 2024 and 2025. Operating profit reached KRW 17 billion in 2025, with a 24.5% margin, the highest of the 2022–2025 period.

In 2026 the solutions business grew 118% and is reshaping the revenue mix, and cumulative preliminary revenue through the third quarter reached KRW 66.9 billion, up 28% year on year.

On the other hand, quarterly operating profit has declined for two straight quarters since its first-quarter 2026 peak, and game services revenue grew only 4% over the same period.

Operating cash flow of KRW 2.69 billion in 2025, well below operating profit, also needs to be checked for the stability of dividend funding.

Strengths include solutions expansion, royalty-based profitability and the quarterly dividend system, while weaknesses include dependence on the Audition IP, slowing quarterly profit and uneven cash flow.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 full-year preliminary results and the 2026 annual business report, and in particular whether fourth-quarter operating profit recovers and whether the solutions share stays near 40% of revenue.
