02

Business structure

Sungwoo Techron Company was founded in 1997 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2001 as a maker of semiconductor components and inspection equipment.

Its business is split into a component division that handles processing for semiconductor parts, an equipment division that builds inspection systems, and a PCB division that performs final PCB inspection. Segment weights differ by data provider.

Butler put the mix at 43% equipment, 40% components and 16% PCB based on second-quarter 2026 revenue, while JobKorea showed 43% components, 38% equipment and 19% PCB as of March 2026. Both sources put components and equipment together above 80% of sales. Major customers include Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek.

In the December 2025 product mix, inspection equipment and related products were 53.05% domestic and 46.95% exports, according to WiseReport. R&D spending in 2025 was KRW 1.393 billion, or 3.40% of revenue.

Component processing depends on customers' production volumes, while equipment demand follows semiconductor capital spending. Headcount stood at 272 as of June 30, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 44.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 33.8 billion in 2023 (down 23.9% year on year), recovered to KRW 41.7 billion in 2024 (up 23.4%), and reached KRW 40.9 billion in 2025 (down 1.8%). 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest among the four years.

Operating profit moved from KRW 4.83 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 808 million in 2023, turned to a profit of KRW 1.18 billion in 2024, and reached KRW 1.95 billion in 2025, up 64.9%. Operating margins were 10.9%, -2.4%, 2.8% and 4.8%.

Operating profit rose in 2025 even as revenue fell, and the company cited improved cost of inspection equipment as the main driver of the year's earnings change.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent went from KRW 4.77 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 1.04 billion in 2023, KRW 2.24 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.74 billion in 2025, up 111.6%.

Net income including non-controlling interests, a separate figure, was KRW 4.81 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 1.05 billion in 2023, KRW 2.25 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.75 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 9.55 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 14.4 billion in the second quarter of 2026, then came to KRW 13.6 billion in the third quarter (down 5.8% from the prior quarter, up 42.4% from a year earlier).

Operating profit was KRW 1.37 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 2.54 billion in the second and KRW 1.53 billion in the third, and the first-half total was KRW 3.91 billion. Profit attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters totaled KRW 7.15 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 4.76 billion in 2022, KRW 2.77 billion in 2023, KRW 5.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.04 billion in 2025, the largest of the four years. Equity stood at KRW 75.4 billion at end-2025, of which KRW 74.3 billion belonged to owners of the parent, with total liabilities of KRW 25 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Sungwoo Techron's results are tied to semiconductor cycles and demand from downstream electronic components.

WiseReport notes that the component division is focusing on profitability while the semiconductor oversupply is being adjusted, and that demand rose on higher prices and stronger economic activity in the United States and Europe.

The equipment division's results improved sharply as investor sentiment recovered with the semiconductor market, according to the same source, which also cites new patent acquisitions that strengthen technology competitiveness.

On inspection technology, some observers point to vision algorithms and AI deep-learning-based inspection as strengths. A Buffett Lab article expected demand for AI-based inspection to grow as factory automation and smart factories spread, but that is an outside view and not a figure the company has given.

In terms of cycle position, the company has been recovering since its 2023 operating loss, yet whether the oversupply adjustment has ended cannot be judged from filings alone.

Against peers, AlphaSquare's data put its operating margin above the semiconductor and semiconductor equipment sector average and its debt ratio lower. The sector average includes large companies, so direct comparison has limits. The next checkpoint is how component-division sales move as the oversupply adjustment plays out.

Timing of revenue recognition depends on equipment delivery and acceptance schedules, so how much of this contract appears in later quarterly sales is the next thing to track. On September 15, 2026, an extraordinary shareholders' meeting appointed one new independent director.

On demand, WiseReport explains that higher prices and stronger economic activity in the United States and Europe support component-division demand. In equipment, a recovery in chip-industry investment sentiment could support order flow, but that depends on whether the sector upturn continues.

The extent to which new patents contribute to revenue has not been quantified. WiseReport reports first-half 2026 consolidated revenue up 23.1% year on year, and whether that pace holds in the second half is worth watching. The March 2026 shareholders' meeting approved stock and cash dividends.

The next checkpoint is whether the operating margin in the 2026 annual report exceeds the 4.8% recorded for 2025.

08

Bull factors

Continuity of profit recovery

After returning to profit in 2024, profit attributable to owners of the parent reached KRW 4.74 billion in 2025, up 111.6%. Quarterly operating profit ran KRW 1.37 billion, KRW 2.54 billion and KRW 1.53 billion across the first three quarters of 2026, with no loss quarter.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent over the last four quarters totaled KRW 7.15 billion. Profit has been visible quarter after quarter, which is the main bullish point.

Improving demand in equipment and components

WiseReport describes the equipment division's results as improving sharply as investor sentiment recovered with the semiconductor market. Component demand is described as rising on higher prices and stronger economic activity in the United States and Europe.

First-half 2026 consolidated revenue is reported up 23.1% year on year. Improvement in both divisions at the same time is a bullish factor.

Contract backlog and financial capacity

The single sales contract disclosed in September 2026 is a double-digit share of 2025 revenue, so the timing of its revenue recognition is the next point to check. A debt ratio lower than the prior year's eases the financial burden as orders grow.

Operating cash flow reached KRW 7.04 billion in 2025, the largest of the four years. Improvement in both order size and cash generation is a bullish point.

09

Bear factors

Stagnant revenue scale

Revenue for 2025 was KRW 40.9 billion, down 1.8% from the prior year and below KRW 44.4 billion in 2022. It fell to KRW 33.8 billion in 2023, the smallest of the four years. Operating profit can rise while the sales base stays weak.

Whether first-half 2026 revenue growth carries into a full-year recovery is the central question for the bearish view.

High volatility in quarterly results

Operating profit fell 39.6% from KRW 2.54 billion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 1.53 billion in the third. It moved from KRW 674 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 523 million in the fourth, then rebounded to KRW 1.37 billion in the first quarter of 2026. The 2025 operating margin of 4.8% remains below 10.9% in 2022. Uneven quarterly profit is a bearish point.

Oversupply adjustment and customer concentration

WiseReport notes that the component division is focused on maintaining profitability while the semiconductor oversupply is being adjusted. Production plans at key customers such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek directly affect volumes. Equipment orders can also swing with chip capital spending. Dependence on one industry cycle and a small set of customers is a bearish factor.

10

Risk factors

Customer and sector concentration

Component processing revenue depends on production plans at a small number of customers, including Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek. The equipment division is sensitive to chip capital-spending sentiment. As the third-quarter revenue decline showed, the trend can reverse within a single quarter. Lower customer volumes or a sector slowdown could widen swings in results.

Filing amendments and figure revisions

An amended half-year report for 2026 was filed on September 29, 2026. Because an amendment has been made, quarterly figures already published could be revised in later filings. Any citation of first-half 2026 figures should reference the post-amendment filing. Market aggregators and filed figures can also differ.

Low liquidity and concentrated ownership

According to WiseReport, the largest shareholder group holds 42.14% and the free-float ratio is 54.37%. Foreign ownership is 2.35%, which is low. With limited tradable shares, price moves can be more sensitive to changes in trading volume. Ownership structure and supply-demand conditions are items to check separately from earnings.

12

Overall view

Sungwoo Techron has moved from a 2023 operating loss to a return to profit in 2024, a further gain in 2025, and a widening of quarterly operating profit in 2026. Revenue, however, remains below KRW 44.4 billion in 2022, and the 2025 operating margin of 4.8% is below 10.9% in 2022.

Quarterly results also remain volatile, with operating profit falling in the third quarter of 2026 after the second-quarter peak. Bullish factors include improving equipment and component demand, a contract worth KRW 7.115 billion, and a lower debt ratio.

Bearish factors include stagnant revenue, uncertain component demand during the oversupply adjustment, customer concentration and a low free-float ratio.

Valuation multiples and dividend yield sit at the low end relative to the sector, which can be read as reflecting both earnings volatility and the ownership structure. The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results and first-quarter 2027 results.

How much of the contract revenue shows up in quarterly results is also worth tracking. This report is for information only and does not include a buy or sell opinion.
