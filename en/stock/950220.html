02

Business structure

NeoImmuneTech, founded in 2014 in Rockville, Maryland, develops T-cell-based immunotherapies, with its core candidate being NT-I7 (efineptakin alfa), a long-acting interleukin-7 (IL-7) based T-cell amplifier.

The molecule was licensed from Genexine, the company's largest shareholder, which originally developed it as GX-I7. The company's directly controlled trials have been narrowed to a U.S.

Phase 1b/2a study across five solid tumor types (NIT-110) and an acute radiation syndrome (ARS) program (NIT-A01), while a Phase 2 non-small-cell lung cancer trial (NIT-119) and a COVID-19 trial (NIT-118) were reportedly voluntarily withdrawn.

Its remaining key growth area, CAR-T combination therapy, is being pursued through investigator-initiated trials (IITs), including a repeat-dosing study (NIT-126) in patients treated with Breyanzi or Yescarta that dosed its first patient in the first half of 2026 and has since added enrollment.

At the end of 2025, the company acquired exclusive North American sales rights to Endari, a sickle cell disease treatment, from Emmaus Life Sciences, giving it an immediately revenue-generating commercial asset without clinical or regulatory risk.

In August 2026, it licensed exclusive development and commercialization rights for NT-I7 in thymic dysfunction indications, including immune reconstitution in HIV patients, across North America, Latin America, and Europe to Tolerance Bio, while retaining rights in its core immuno-oncology areas.

A joint research collaboration with Immunocore evaluating combination potential with its TCR therapy KIMMTRAK was presented at ASCO 2026.

In the competitive landscape, the absence of an FDA-approved CAR-T therapy for solid tumors is cited as a potential differentiator for combination strategies aimed at extending T-cell persistence.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue fell 46.6% year over year, from KRW 173 million in 2024 to KRW 92.31 million in 2025. The operating loss narrowed each year across four consecutive years of losses, from KRW 62.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 59 billion in 2023, KRW 40.6 billion in 2024, and KRW 24.6 billion in 2025.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders followed the same pattern, narrowing from KRW 64.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 31 billion in 2025. The 2025 operating margin stood at -26,596.6%, a figure distorted by the extremely small revenue base.

On a quarterly basis, revenue bottomed at KRW 17.97 million in Q3 2025 before rising for three consecutive quarters to KRW 37.39 million in Q4 2025, KRW 498 million in Q1 2026, and KRW 1.09 billion in Q2 2026, a pattern that coincides with the effective date of the Endari U.S. sales license on May 15, 2026 and the start of North American sales.

Over the same period, the operating loss continued but narrowed year over year, from KRW 6.6 billion in Q2 2025 to KRW 3.93 billion in Q2 2026.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed sharply to KRW 416 million in Q2 2026 from KRW 7.13 billion in the prior quarter, though the trailing four-quarter total loss (Q3 2025 through Q2 2026) still reached KRW 23.5 billion, underscoring that losses continue.

At the end of 2025, total equity stood at KRW 57.1 billion and total liabilities at KRW 22 billion, for a debt ratio of 38.6%, while operating cash outflow shrank from KRW 46.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 24.4 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The T-cell-based immunotherapy and cytokine adjuvant market is increasingly driven by demand for combination therapies that extend CAR-T response durability and address its limitations in solid tumors.

While CAR-T therapies such as Carvykti, Breyanzi, and Yescarta have been commercialized for multiple myeloma and lymphoma, no CAR-T therapy has yet received FDA approval for solid tumors, creating interest in adjuvants that enhance T-cell survival and functional persistence.

The acute radiation syndrome (ARS) treatment market is a specialized one centered on U.S. government and BARDA procurement rather than general consumers, with a distinct regulatory feature allowing approval based on animal study data under the FDA's Animal Rule instead of human trials.

Kosdaq-listed biotech firms that entered under the technology special listing track are reportedly facing growing pressure to demonstrate revenue generation and financial stability as listing requirement grace periods expire.

Endari, a rare-disease treatment approved by the FDA in 2017, has seen its patent expire, intensifying generic competition and contributing to a declining trend in U.S. sales.

Relative to peers, with directly controlled trials narrowed to a single Phase 1b/2a study and the ARS program, the company's competitive position is shaped more by execution on a small number of programs and its ability to secure external partnerships than by pipeline breadth.

06

Outlook

The company has framed 2026 as the year revenue becomes visible, prioritizing the commercial ramp of Endari and securing a development partner for NT-I7.

The ARS program has completed rodent studies and entered the final primate study stage, with a company representative indicating final data could be available as early as the end of 2026, though this depends on the outcome of the BARDA BAA award process.

The CAR-T combination trial (NIT-126), designed as a repeat-dosing study, began dosing its first patient in the first half of 2026 and continues to add enrollment, with the company considering a transition to Phase 2 upon safety confirmation.

The August 2026 agreement with Tolerance Bio structured the upfront payment as a 4% equity stake in the partner, with future milestones tied to clinical development and sales achievement totaling up to USD 260 million (about KRW 3,669 billion), meaning actual cash inflow depends on execution at each development stage.

Endari was targeted to generate around USD 10 million in annual sales, but U.S. sales have trended lower amid generic competition following patent expiration, leaving the minimum annual sales threshold a variable for maintaining exclusivity.

The potential conversion of roughly KRW 10 billion in outstanding convertible bonds into equity is also cited as a factor that could affect the capital structure. The company has stated that licensing discussions in the immuno-oncology area continue based on CAR-T combination trial data.

08

Bull factors

Global Licensing Deal Secured

In August 2026, through a contract with Tolerance Bio, the company transferred the North America, Latin America, and Europe rights for NT-I7's thymic dysfunction indication and secured a milestone structure of up to $260 million (approximately KRW 366.9 billion).

The company retains the rights to core areas such as immuno-oncology, leaving room for additional partnerships. This can be interpreted as the first tangible outcome of the partnership discussions the company has continued to pursue with multiple global pharmaceutical companies.

Commercial Asset via Endari

Endari, in-licensed from Emmaus Life Sciences, is an asset that can recognize sales immediately without clinical or approval risk, and contributed to quarterly sales increasing for three consecutive quarters since the license became effective in May 2026.

It is also positive that the company was able to reduce the initial sales gap by inheriting existing distribution infrastructure, including major U.S. wholesaler networks and listings with the three major PBMs.

Multi-Year Loss Narrowing

Both operating losses and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders showed a trend of annual reduction from 2022 to 2025, and in Q2 2026, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders continued to decrease compared to the same period of the previous year.

Favorable response rates and safety reported in early data from the CAR-T combination trial are also serving as a basis for further partnership discussions.

09

Bear factors

Sharp Reduction in Company-Led Trials

With multiple company-led clinical trials, including the Phase 2 trial for non-small cell lung cancer (NIT-119), being voluntarily withdrawn, the clinical trials currently directly controlled by the company have been narrowed down to the solid tumor Phase 1b/2a trial and the ARS program.

It is also pointed out that the CAR-T combination therapy, the core growth axis, is being conducted as an investigator-initiated trial (IIT), limiting the company's direct control.

Revenue Listing Requirement Shortfall

With 2025 sales amounting to only KRW 92.31 million, the company continues to fall significantly short of the annual sales requirement of KRW 3 billion for maintaining listing.

Caution from an investor protection perspective is needed, as new regulations designating penny stocks as administrative issues will take effect from July 2026, and the company was also designated as an investment alert stock in August 2026 due to a sharp stock price surge.

Declining Endari Sales and Conditional Exclusivity

Endari's U.S. sales have shown a declining trend due to generic competition following patent expiration, and the license agreement includes a condition that exclusive rights convert to non-exclusive if the annual minimum sales requirement is not met.

Royalty and cost margin payment obligations amounting to double-digit percentages of sales could also be a burden on profitability.

10

Risk factors

Listing Maintenance and Liquidity Risk

Amid the continued failure to meet the annual sales requirement of KRW 3 billion, a regulation effective from July 2026 designates stocks as administrative issues if the closing price remains below KRW 1,000 for 30 consecutive trading days.

Attention should be paid to institutional risks related to trading, as the company was also designated as an investment alert stock in August 2026 due to a stock price surge.

Pipeline and Clinical Execution Risk

There is a history of multiple company-led clinical trials being voluntarily withdrawn, and the CAR-T combination therapy, the core growth axis, is being conducted as an investigator-initiated trial, meaning the data acquisition schedule may be dependent on the schedules of external researchers and institutions.

The expected approval timing for the ARS treatment is mentioned as 2028 at the earliest, indicating that considerable time will be needed before commercialization.

Financial Structure Risk

Approximately KRW 10 billion in convertible bonds remain outstanding, raising the possibility of dilution upon conversion to shares, and the company has a history of raising capital through a large-scale rights offering in 2025.

The clause converting Endari's license agreement to non-exclusive if the minimum sales requirement is not met could also serve as a variable in financial performance.

Overall view

NeoImmuneTech reached a turning point in 2026 with quarterly sales increasing for three consecutive quarters due to the recognition of Endari sales and the technology transfer agreement with Tolerance Bio, but 2025 annual sales amounted to only KRW 92.31 million, still far from the listing maintenance requirement.

Operating losses and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders showed a trend of annual reduction from 2022 to 2025, and this trend continued in Q2 2026, but the cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the most recent four quarters was KRW 23.5 billion, indicating that the deficit structure itself persists.

For the core pipeline NT-I7, company-led clinical trials have been narrowed down to one solid tumor trial and the ARS program, while the CAR-T combination therapy relies on an investigator-initiated trial structure.

Endari is an asset that can recognize sales immediately, but it carries conditional risks including a declining U.S. sales trend after patent expiration and a minimum sales requirement.

Institutional variables related to trading, such as the penny stock administrative issue designation regulation and the history of investment alert stock designation, also need to be examined.

In summary, this is judged to be a phase where positive signals from technology transfer and securing a commercialization asset coexist with the listing maintenance sales requirement and pipeline concentration risk.
