02

Business structure

Caregen is a biotech company that builds cosmetic ingredients, fillers, health supplements and drug candidates on peptide technology. The company says it holds roughly 960 substance patents at home and abroad, as stated after its CPHI Milan appearance in October 2026.

The company attributed the 2025 revenue decline to a renewal of its core filler product line and a reallocation of supply regions. Its health-supplement lineup centers on Korglutide, Myoki and ProGsterol.

Korglutide is a GLP-1-based peptide presented as a product that can help offset muscle loss alongside weight reduction, and it has entered the U.S. market after FDA NDI registration. Press reports say Myoki completed registration with Mexican health authorities.

On the platform side, the proprietary delivery technology Luxidase is central, and a topical siRNA hair product built on it was shown at CPHI Milan. Representatives of global cosmetics companies including Estée Lauder visited the booth to discuss technology licensing.

On the drug side, press reports in August 2026 described the eye-drop wet AMD candidate CG-P5 as having entered Phase 2, while the next-generation CG-T1 is being prepared for global clinical entry.

Sales run through overseas distribution partners; in May 2026, a supply contract worth USD 5.7 million for ProGsterol with a Polish company was reported. Within the sector, peptide-based listed companies such as Peptron and SAM Chun Dang Pharm serve as reference points.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue rose from KRW 69.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 79.2 billion in 2023 and KRW 82.6 billion in 2024, then fell 11.8% to KRW 72.8 billion in 2025. Within 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 40.4 billion in 2023, then fell to KRW 34.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 20.4 billion in 2025, two consecutive years of decline, and the operating margin slid from 48.7% in 2022 and 51.0% in 2023 to 41.5% in 2024 and 28.0% in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 revenue decline to the renewal of its core product lines and a reallocation of supply regions to improve profitability.

The same year, a conservative bad-debt provision reflecting Middle East tensions and foreign-currency valuation gains and losses tied to a weaker exchange rate weighed further on earnings.

As a result, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 37.8% to KRW 20.1 billion in 2025, and total net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 20.1 billion. For the 2024 operating profit decline, the company pointed mainly to higher R&D spending.

Quarterly, Q4 2025 swung to an operating loss of KRW 7.07 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.28 billion.

Q1 2026 returned to profit with KRW 22.7 billion in revenue, KRW 10.2 billion in operating profit and KRW 11.7 billion in controlling-shareholder net profit, the peak of the period shown.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 18.8 billion with operating profit of KRW 5.24 billion, and Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 18.4 billion with operating profit of KRW 7.19 billion. Q3 operating profit rose 37.3% from the prior quarter but fell 27.1% year on year.

Controlling-shareholder net profit was KRW 648 million in Q3, down 93.6% year on year, and the last four quarters combined total KRW 13.4 billion. In its Q2 announcement, the company had flagged deferred European sales and a bad-debt provision reversal for Q3, yet Q3 revenue was 2.2% lower than in the prior quarter.

05

Industry analysis

Peptides are a technology trend with expanding uses across cosmetics, medical devices, health supplements and drugs, and CEO Jeong Yong-ji said the trend was confirmed firsthand at CPHI Milan.

In health supplements, GLP-1-type peptide ingredients are drawing attention for demand around weight management and muscle support. European entry depends on novel-food approval, and a May 2026 report said Deglusterol is in the final stage of evaluation by the European Food Safety Authority.

In the United States, FDA NDI registration serves as the entry base, and the company says it has secured this. On competition, Thinkpool analysis of Q1 2026 said Caregen's revenue growth trailed peers while its operating profit growth ran relatively high.

Geopolitics also shapes the backdrop: in Q2 2025, supply chains for ProGsterol were temporarily constrained by the spillover from the Iran-Israel war. Trade shows and conferences serve as channels for overseas demand, and Caregen participated as an emerald sponsor at IMCAS, the large aesthetic medicine congress.

In fillers, the company competes with global medical aesthetics firms, so whether the product line renewal translates into market share is the key question.

06

Outlook

The company's medium-term targets appear in its 2026 value-enhancement plan, which calls for annual operating profit growth of at least 5% and a dividend payout ratio of at least 30%; it was disclosed on March 24, 2026.

CEO Jeong Yong-ji described 2026 as the first year of a shift to global growth and said that from the second quarter, the U.S. launch of Korglutide and sales expansion of Luxidase-based products would take hold.

A capacity expansion is also planned: in April 2026, the CEO told Paxetv that production capacity, now around 10 tons a year, would be raised to 30 to 50 tons. In Europe, EFSA approval of Deglusterol is the gate, and the company said it is preparing a supply system for immediate entry after approval.

In the pipeline, press reports from August 2026 said the eye-drop candidate CG-P5 entered Phase 2, while CG-T1 is preparing for global clinical entry.

At its September 2026 global partners meeting, the company said it would pursue commercialization of new products linking functional peptides with RNA delivery technology, along with management of its export supply chain.

However, on disclosed figures revenue and controlling-shareholder net profit have trended lower through Q3, so the speed at which overseas sales growth shows up in quarterly results is the next checkpoint.

Comparison of the 2026 fiscal-year outcome against the plan will be possible with the annual report around late March 2027.

Korglutide entered the U.S. market stage after FDA NDI registration, and Myoki's registration with Mexican health authorities was reported. Bear factors

Declining operating profit trend

Operating profit fell from KRW 40.4 billion in 2023 to KRW 20.4 billion in 2025, with declines in both 2024 and 2025. The 2025 operating margin of 28.0% is well below the 48.7% of 2022. Controlling-shareholder net profit in Q3 2026 was KRW 648 million, down 93.6% year on year.

Revenue volatility and past quarterly losses

Revenue fell 11.8% to KRW 72.8 billion in 2025, and Q4 2025 swung to an operating loss of KRW 7.07 billion. Revenue declined in both Q2 and Q3 2026. Q2 operating profit fell 48.7% from the prior quarter, reversing much of the Q1 peak.

Large market value versus earnings and concentrated ownership

Market capitalization exceeds 100 times the last four quarters of controlling-shareholder net profit and more than seven times year-end 2025 total equity. The largest shareholder and related parties hold 64.47%, and the free float ratio is 26.47%. The bear case holds that if profit recovery is delayed, the earnings-multiple burden could persist.

Risk factors

Supply chain and geopolitics

In Q2 2025, ProGsterol supply was disrupted by the spillover from the Iran-Israel war. In 2025, a conservative bad-debt provision reflecting Middle East instability weighed on earnings. Middle East variables affect revenue recognition, receivable collection and supply timing together.

Regulatory and clinical timing uncertainty

European entry for Deglusterol depends on EFSA novel-food approval, and CG-P5 is in Phase 2. Neither the approval timing nor the clinical readout timing is fixed, which leaves earnings visibility uncertain.

The speed at which overseas supply contracts flow into quarterly results remains to be seen in the reported figures.

Ownership structure and liquidity

Jeong Yong-ji and five related parties hold 64.47% of issued shares as of September 2026. The free float ratio is 26.47% and the 52-week beta is recorded at 1.31, a relatively wide price swing against the market. A concentrated ownership structure can affect trading volume and price formation.

12

Overall view

On disclosed figures, Caregen's operating profit and controlling-shareholder net profit have declined since their 2023 peak, and quarterly results have been volatile in 2026.

Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 22.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 10.2 billion marked the high point of the period shown, and revenue and controlling-shareholder net profit then declined in both Q2 and Q3.

The bullish factors are the historically high-margin profile, the expansion of overseas channels for health supplements, the patent base and delivery platform, and partner contracts already signed.

The bearish factors are the downward operating margin trend, the record of quarterly losses, Middle East and other geopolitical variables, and a market value that is large relative to earnings. Both sets of factors currently carry comparable weight, so the disclosed figures alone do not settle the direction.

The 2026 value-enhancement plan targets at least 5% annual operating profit growth, and the gap between that plan and actual results will serve as the medium-term benchmark. The next checkpoints are the 2026 fiscal-year results and EFSA approval for Deglusterol.
