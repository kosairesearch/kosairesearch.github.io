02

Business structure

Cosmo Advanced Materials & Technology makes cathode active materials for secondary batteries and release film for MLCCs (multilayer ceramic capacitors), positioning itself as an advanced IT materials company.

The company reports its business in two segments, film and battery materials, and said the film segment led the profit improvement in 3Q 2026. It said stable orders for MLCC release film continued on the back of a recovery in AI and semiconductor demand.

The battery materials segment, by contrast, was affected by weak downstream demand and customer production schedule changes. Cathode sales are concentrated among a few customers.

In 3Q 2024 LG accounted for 100 percent of sales, but as of 3Q 2025 LG Energy Solution held 20 percent and Samsung SDI 80 percent, according to an IBK Securities report. In 1Q 2026 ESS supply was halted, and the company supplied mainly IT, power tools and small batteries, according to IBK Securities.

Press reports cite single-crystal cathode and ESS as medium-term recovery levers for cathode. On raw materials, a Chungcheong press report said the company completed a three-stage vertical chain, covering battery recycling, precursor and cathode, through collaboration with its parent COSMO CHEMICAL CO.

Another report says the company has the world's largest release-film production capacity. In cathode, it competes with listed peers such as POSCO Future M, Ecopro BM and L&F.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 485.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 629.6 billion in 2023 (up 29.6% year on year), then fell 9.5% to KRW 569.7 billion in 2024 and 19.9% to KRW 456.3 billion in 2025, a two-year decline after the 2023 peak. The largest year in 2022-2025 was 2023 and the smallest was 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 32.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 32.3 billion in 2023 (down 0.5%), KRW 25 billion in 2024 (down 22.7%) and KRW 2.38 billion in 2025 (down 90.5%), a three-year decline from 2023 to 2025. The operating margin fell from 6.7 percent in 2022 to 0.5 percent in 2025.

EBN Industry Economy attributed the weakness to a prolonged EV slowdown, customer inventory adjustments in cathode shipments, and fixed-cost pressure after aggressive capacity expansion.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 27.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 27 billion in 2023 and KRW 17.6 billion in 2024, then turned to a loss of KRW 523 million in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests shows the same 2025 loss of KRW 523 million.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 496.8 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 224.3 billion. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 9.07 billion in 2024 to KRW 138.2 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue rose for four consecutive quarters from KRW 117 billion in 4Q 2025 to KRW 144.1 billion in 3Q 2026. Operating profit rose from KRW 135 million in 3Q 2025 to KRW 4.65 billion in 2Q 2026, then fell 60.8 percent to KRW 1.83 billion in 3Q 2026.

The company attributed the 3Q decline mainly to the translation effect of lower exchange rates on export sales. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from a loss of KRW 2.6 billion in 3Q 2025 to a profit of KRW 1.24 billion in 2Q 2026 and KRW 7.16 billion in 3Q 2026. The 3Q figure includes a valuation gain on an investee, so it should be read apart from operating performance.

05

Industry analysis

The cathode market remains in a phase of prolonged EV demand weakness. A prolonged EV slowdown and customer inventory adjustments slowed cathode shipments, a common view in press coverage (EBN Industry Economy).

In November 2025 IBK Securities lowered its rating on the company to Trading Buy, citing a cut in its earnings estimates due to weaker downstream demand. In the 3Q 2026 disclosure, the company again pointed to weak downstream demand in its battery materials segment.

On the film side, growth in AI servers and automotive electronics is lifting demand for MLCC release film, which is cited as a new growth driver. ESS is drawing attention as an alternative demand source for cathode (EBN Industry Economy).

The cathode market is competitive with listed domestic peers including POSCO Future M, Ecopro BM and L&F, and volumes from large cell makers largely determine earnings. The 3Q 2025 and 2Q 2026 results show that plant utilization has swung sharply with customer volumes.

Vertical integration and in-house precursor production are presented as cost advantages. The timing of the sector recovery should be confirmed through cathode utilization and customer shipment data.

06

Outlook

In its 3Q 2026 results statement, the company said it would expand its growth base through a recovery in cathode utilization and stronger product competitiveness.

In its 2Q 2026 results, announced on July 22, 2026, it said cathode utilization would return to normal levels in the second half and that it would keep pushing market expansion.

In its 1Q 2026 results, disclosed on April 28, 2026, the company said recovery in sales to key customers had slowed, limiting cathode volume growth, while it was rebuilding its supply chain to set up a rebound in the second half.

Management also said its single-crystal patented technology would help it become a core partner in high-performance materials supply chains.

The KRW 120 billion convertible bond disclosed on January 16, 2026 was described by the company as financing for an overseas production base and a response to the global secondary battery market.

A October 2024 press report said a 100,000-ton cathode capacity investment was underway and had reached the trial production stage after equipment installation, but that report predates the current period, so current plant operations should be confirmed from later disclosures.

The company presents expanded MLCC release-film volume tied to AI and semiconductor demand as a medium-term growth driver. Whether cathode utilization returns to normal levels is the key variable for second-half earnings.

08

Bull factors

Film business anchors earnings

The film business, which supplies MLCC release film, led the 3Q 2026 profit improvement as orders held steady amid AI and semiconductor demand. Press coverage says the company has the world's largest release-film production capacity. If MLCC demand keeps rising with AI servers and automotive electronics, film volumes could grow as well.

Cathode utilization is recovering

Cathode plant utilization recovered from the prior quarter in 2Q 2026, and operating profit rose to KRW 4.65 billion. The company said it would normalize cathode utilization in the second half and keep expanding in the market.

If utilization returns to normal levels, fixed-cost pressure could ease and leave room for the operating margin to improve.

Vertical integration and new demand

Press coverage says a three-stage chain covering battery recycling, precursor and cathode, built with parent COSMO CHEMICAL CO, strengthens raw material sourcing and cost competitiveness. Single-crystal cathode and ESS are cited as medium-term recovery levers.

The company says the KRW 120 billion convertible bond disclosed in January 2026 is meant to fund an overseas production base.

Bear factors

Downstream demand and customer concentration

As of 3Q 2025, LG Energy Solution accounted for 20 percent of cathode sales and Samsung SDI for 80 percent, according to IBK Securities. ESS supply was halted in 1Q 2026, and a prolonged EV slowdown reduced cathode shipments.

In 3Q 2026 the company again said its battery materials segment was hit by weak demand and customer production schedule changes.

Annual profitability has weakened

Operating profit fell for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, reaching KRW 2.38 billion in 2025 (down 90.5%). Press coverage attributes part of the pressure to higher fixed costs after aggressive capacity expansion.

Thin earnings base and FX exposure

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders totals KRW 2.7 billion over the last four quarters. Operating profit fell 60.8 percent from the prior quarter in 3Q 2026, which the company attributed to the translation effect of lower exchange rates on export sales. Because the 3Q net income includes an investee valuation gain, it does not fully reflect operating performance.

Risk factors

Downstream demand and customer concentration

If EV demand stays weak for an extended period, cathode shipments and utilization could fall again. The experience of 2025 and 1Q 2026, when customer inventory adjustments and the halt in ESS supply overlapped, illustrates this.

With sales concentrated among two customers, a change in one customer's production schedule feeds directly into quarterly results.

Exchange rates and export sales

Operating profit in 3Q 2026 declined partly because lower exchange rates reduced the won value of export sales. Because the company has export sales, exchange-rate moves can widen quarterly profit swings. Since it runs film and cathode businesses together, the sensitivity of each segment to exchange rates also matters.

Financial structure and funding

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 224.3 billion, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 45.1 percent. In January 2026 the company raised funds for an overseas base through a KRW 120 billion convertible bond.

With net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaling KRW 2.7 billion over the last four quarters, balancing investment funding against earnings generation matters.

Overall view

Cosmo Advanced Materials & Technology posted 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 144.1 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 7.16 billion, a swing back to profit year on year.

The improvement was driven by MLCC release film, while the cathode business remains exposed to customer demand and utilization. Full-year 2025 operating profit was KRW 2.38 billion, down sharply from KRW 32.5 billion in 2022, and the operating margin fell from 6.7 percent to 0.5 percent over the same period.

Quarterly profit remains volatile, as operating profit fell from KRW 4.65 billion in 2Q 2026 to KRW 1.83 billion in 3Q 2026. Bullish factors include film demand growth, cathode utilization recovery, new demand from single-crystal cathode and ESS, and vertical integration.

Bearish factors include weak EV-driven cathode shipments, concentration among two customers, exchange-rate effects and a thin recent earnings base. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 224.3 billion, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 45.1 percent.

The next checkpoints are the 4Q and full-year 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual business report and the 1Q 2027 results, and the key question is whether cathode utilization recovery shows up in these figures.
