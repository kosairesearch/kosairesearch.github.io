Report overview

Release Film Holds Profits as Cathode Recovery Is Tested

Cosmo Advanced Materials & Technology saw operating profit shrink to KRW 2.38 billion in 2025 on weak cathode shipments, then posted KRW 4.65 billion in Q2 2026 on strong MLCC release-film sales and higher utilization; the next tests are a second-half restart of cathode shipments and execution of its European expansion.

    Annual revenue peaked at KRW 629.6 billion in 2023, then fell for two straight years (2024–2025) to KRW 456.3 billion in 2025, while operating margin slid from 6.7% in 2022 to 0.5% in 2025.

  2. 2

    Quarterly revenue and operating profit have both risen for three straight quarters since the Q3 2025 trough, and Q2 2026 operating profit reached KRW 4.65 billion, up 779.9% year on year.

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Cosmo Advanced Materials & Technology runs on two pillars, cathode active materials for rechargeable batteries and release film for MLCCs, and also makes chemical materials such as toner.

It began in functional films and toner materials, then reshaped itself into a battery-materials company by expanding its cathode business.

Its cathode customers are Samsung SDI and the LG group; according to IBK Investment & Securities, Samsung SDI accounted for 80% and LG Energy Solution for 20% of cathode sales in Q3 2025.

On products, it is seen as trying to set itself apart from larger players such as Ecopro BM, POSCO Future M and L&F by focusing on single-crystal high-nickel cathodes.

Release film is a process film used to form ceramic layers during MLCC manufacturing; the company makes it at its Chungju plant and supplies Samsung Electro-Mechanics, among others.

As of July 2026 the company said it had about 70 million square meters per month of release-film capacity and was raising the share of ultra-smooth film used in high-spec industrial and automotive MLCCs.

Within the group, parent COSMO CHEMICAL CO handles raw materials and battery recycling while this company makes cathodes, forming a vertically integrated chain. Up-to-date segment revenue shares could not be confirmed from filings, so we note only that the business leans heavily on cathodes.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose 29.6% from KRW 485.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 629.6 billion in 2023, the largest year in the 2022–2025 period. It then declined for two straight years, to KRW 569.7 billion in 2024 (down 9.5%) and KRW 456.3 billion in 2025 (down 19.9%).

Operating profit went from KRW 32.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 32.3 billion in 2023, KRW 25 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.38 billion in 2025, falling for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, while operating margin dropped from 6.7% to 0.5%.

IBK Investment & Securities attributed continued losses in the cathode segment in Q3 2025 to lower utilization, and net income attributable to owners swung to a loss of KRW 523 million in 2025.

Quarterly revenue bottomed at KRW 105.3 billion in Q3 2025 and has risen for three straight quarters to KRW 137.7 billion in Q2 2026 (up 14.6% year on year). Operating profit also rose for three straight quarters, reaching KRW 4.65 billion in Q2 2026, up 432.4% quarter on quarter.

Media reports credited higher MLCC release-film sales and lower fixed-cost burden from better utilization, with a low prior-year base also playing a part.

Net income attributable to owners turned negative in Q3 2025 and stayed negative for two more quarters before turning to a profit of KRW 1.24 billion in Q2 2026, but the last four quarters add up to a net loss of KRW 7.06 billion, meaning non-operating items ate into earnings.

Operating cash flow of KRW 138.2 billion in 2025 was far larger than operating profit, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 58.3% in 2024 to 45.1% in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The cathode market has slowed since 2023 as the EV chasm, a temporary stall in demand, has dragged on. In a May 2026 report, IBK Investment & Securities cited weak US EV demand and a halt in ESS-bound deliveries as the company's main headwinds.

Industry observers point to ESS growth and single-crystal cathode competitiveness as key variables for a medium-term recovery. In Europe, the Critical Raw Materials Act and the EU-UK Trade and Cooperation Agreement are pushing for locally produced materials.

Competitor Ecopro BM moved first on local production, making its first shipment to a European automaker from its plant in Debrecen, Hungary in June 2026. The company is smaller than the large cathode makers, so swings in customer volumes have a relatively larger impact on its results.

On the MLCC side, demand is rising with AI server and data-center investment, letting release film partly offset weakness in cathodes.

06

Outlook

In a May 2026 report, IBK Investment & Securities projected that supply to LG would resume from Q2 and the first EV-grade cathode shipments to Samsung SDI would begin in Q3. The same report expected new lines to come online one at a time in Q3 2026, Q4 2026 and Q1 2027.

When reporting Q2 2026 results, the company said cathode utilization was getting back on track and signaled an aggressive market push in the second half.

Expansion funding came from a KRW 120 billion convertible bond in January 2026 and a KRW 35 billion rights offering in March 2026, with all new shares taken up by a holding company on the largest shareholder's side.

The company plans to invest in a European cathode base from Q2 2026 through end-2027, and is reportedly considering covering additional needs with cash on hand and borrowing.

FnGuide consensus (two institutions) puts 2026 revenue at KRW 611.5 billion and operating profit at KRW 16 billion; given first-half operating profit, second-half earnings would need to rise sharply to reach that figure. Q3 2026 results had not been released as of October 19, 2026.

08

Bull factors

Release film props up the earnings floor

Higher MLCC release-film sales drove the Q2 2026 improvement in operating profit. With AI server and data-center investment lifting MLCC demand, the release-film lines moved to full utilization. A growing share of high-spec ultra-smooth products could sustain an earnings base regardless of the cathode cycle.

Cathode shipment restart and new lines

IBK Investment & Securities cited resumed supply to LG and first EV-grade shipments to Samsung SDI as second-half drivers. If lines come online in stages from H2 2026 through Q1 2027, higher utilization could spread fixed costs further. Three straight quarters of operating profit growth since Q3 2025 point in that direction.

Balance-sheet room and group backing

Operating cash flow reached KRW 138.2 billion in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell to 45.1%. The company raised more than KRW 150 billion in Q1 2026 alone, with the rights offering fully taken up by the largest shareholder's side.

Vertical integration with COSMO CHEMICAL CO in raw materials and recycling is cited as an advantage for cost and supply stability.

09

Bear factors

Losses at the bottom line

Even as operating profit stayed positive, net income attributable to owners was negative for three quarters in a row. The last four quarters show a combined net loss of KRW 7.06 billion, confirming that non-operating costs offset operating earnings. The convertible bond issue could add to financing costs and valuation-related swings.

EV demand and customer concentration

Cathode revenue depends heavily on EV volumes from a small number of battery customers. In a November 2025 report, IBK Investment & Securities noted repeated delays in shipment timelines, and weak US EV demand persists. Another delay in the restart could undermine expectations for second-half earnings improvement.

Expansion burden and dilution

European investment runs through end-2027, and borrowing is also being considered for additional funding. FnGuide consensus expects the debt-to-equity ratio at end-2026 to be higher than at end-2025. Conversion of the convertible bond would dilute existing shareholders.

10

Risk factors

Demand and customers

Cathode results hinge on EV and ESS volumes at Samsung SDI and the LG group. Any production cut or delivery halt at customers quickly lowers utilization. As in Q3 2025, falling utilization has previously led to losses in the cathode segment.

Overseas execution

The European base is at an early stage, and its site, customer contracts and start-up timing have not been confirmed in detail. Ecopro BM has already begun mass production in Hungary, so competition for local customers is under way. Delays or cost overruns could add to the financial burden.

Raw materials and IT demand

Cathode selling prices track metals such as nickel and lithium, so raw-material swings affect both revenue and inventory gains or losses. Release-film results are tied to utilization at MLCC makers such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics. A slowdown in AI server investment could hit the release-film demand that has supported recent earnings.

12

Overall view

Earnings at Cosmo Advanced Materials & Technology fell for three straight years as the cathode cycle weakened after 2023, with operating margin reaching 0.5% in 2025. Quarterly revenue and operating profit have since risen for three straight quarters, but release film for MLCCs, not cathodes, did most of the lifting.

Net income attributable to owners turned positive in Q2 2026, yet the last four quarters still add up to a net loss. In the second half, the restart of cathode shipments to LG and Samsung SDI, new line start-ups and European investment will test both earnings and the balance sheet.

The stock trades at a large premium to book value, so the key question is whether recovery hopes show up in actual results. Readers should weigh the bull and bear factors themselves by watching Q3 2026 results and convertible-bond conversion.
