LG Chem: Batteries Hold Up as Petrochemicals Drag

LG Chem's third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 561.2 billion held up as battery materials and LG Energy Solution offset a petrochemical swing to loss, but controlling-shareholder losses keep the earnings recovery path open.

  1. 1

    Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 38.4% year on year to KRW 15.5 trillion, while operating profit fell 17.4% to KRW 561.2 billion. The company put the consolidated operating margin at 3.6%. LG Chem announced consolidated third-quarter figures, with company-wide operating margin at 3.6%.

  2. 2

    The petrochemical segment swung to an operating loss of KRW 175.6 billion in the third quarter from a profit in the prior quarter. Lagged pass-through of raw material costs narrowed product spreads, while higher freight costs and a weaker won added pressure.

  3. 3

    LG Energy Solution posted third-quarter revenue of KRW 9.6 trillion and operating profit of KRW 756.0 billion. ESS shipment growth and EV cylindrical and mid-nickel pouch supply lifted sales, while better European utilization and stabilized North American ESS production improved margins.

  4. 4

    The company expects annual cathode shipments in 2027 to more than double from 2026 levels. However, it also said 2026 shipments would fall short of early-year expectations because of a shrinking North American EV market and some delayed projects.

  5. 5

    The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 81.4% in 2022 to 114.5% in 2025, and controlling-shareholder net losses over the latest four quarters total KRW 2.28 trillion. Balance-sheet capacity against the loss run is the key variable for upcoming results.

02

Business structure

LG Chem is organized around its Petrochemicals, Advanced Materials and Life Sciences segments, plus its consolidated subsidiary LG Energy Solution. A 2024 report put petrochemical revenue at KRW 18.6 trillion and advanced materials revenue at KRW 2.6 trillion.

Still, LG Energy Solution accounted for more than half of revenue on a cumulative basis through the third quarter of 2025.

By segment in the third quarter of 2026, petrochemical revenue was KRW 4.8 trillion, advanced materials revenue KRW 1.1 trillion and life sciences revenue KRW 337.3 billion, per the November 5, 2026 earnings release.

Advanced Materials covers cathode and other battery materials plus electronic materials for semiconductors and automotive uses, and higher battery material volumes and a one-time gain, along with steady electronic materials results, improved quarterly sales and profitability.

Battery dependence also shows up in intra-group trade. Intercompany sales among subsidiaries, LG-affiliated companies and associates were about KRW 6 trillion in 2024. The company is also working to diversify its customer base.

The plan is to widen supply beyond LG Energy Solution to customers such as GM, Toyota and Stellantis. In petrochemicals, LG Chem competes with domestic peers such as Lotte Chemical and Kumho Petro Chemical, and it is shifting toward higher-value products such as semiconductor-grade IPA and SSBR for EV tires.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 51.9 trillion in 2022 to KRW 55.2 trillion in 2023, then fell to KRW 48.9 trillion in 2024 and KRW 45.9 trillion in 2025, a decline in each of the last two years. The largest revenue year in the period was 2023 and the smallest was 2025.

Operating profit was largest in 2022 at KRW 3 trillion and smallest in 2024 at KRW 916.8 billion. The 2025 figure of KRW 1.18 trillion rose 28.8% from 2024, showing a recovery. The operating margin slid from 5.8% in 2022 to 4.6% in 2023 and 1.9% in 2024, then edged up to 2.6% in 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net income turned into a loss of KRW 690.9 billion in 2024, and the loss widened to KRW 1.82 trillion in 2025, the second consecutive loss year. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 515 billion in 2024 and a net loss of KRW 977.1 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 569.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.23 trillion in 2025. Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 679.7 billion in the third quarter of 2025 gave way to an operating loss of KRW 413.3 billion in the fourth quarter, and the first-quarter 2026 operating loss narrowed to KRW 49.7 billion.

The second quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 599.6 billion. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 561.2 billion and the controlling-shareholder net loss was KRW 648.2 billion.

The petrochemical segment swung to a KRW 175.6 billion operating loss in the third quarter, reflecting narrower product spreads, higher freight costs and a weaker won.

By contrast, LG Energy Solution's third-quarter operating profit of KRW 756.0 billion included KRW 416.9 billion in North American production subsidies, and was KRW 339.1 billion without them. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 81.4% in 2022 to 114.5% in 2025.

Year-end 2025 total equity was KRW 47.1 trillion, of which controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 32.8 trillion.

05

Industry analysis

Supply pressure weighs heavily on petrochemicals. Lagged pass-through of raw material costs narrowed key product spreads.

The company sees oversupply persisting even after some global plant closures, and expects the start-up of the Shaheen project in 2027 to deepen oversupply of some domestic base oils and polyethylene.

Geopolitical risk in the Middle East is another factor, as the weakness in petrochemicals continues amid Middle East-related geopolitical risks and oversupply. Peers such as Lotte Chemical and Kumho Petro Chemical face the same commodity cycle, so differentiation depends largely on the share of higher-value products.

Battery demand is mixed. A shrinking North American EV market and some delayed projects have left 2026 shipments below early-year expectations. On the other side, LG Energy Solution cited rising ESS shipments and wider EV battery sales as its main growth drivers. Metal prices are also a variable.

The company said that if metal price declines continue into 2027, sales growth may be limited relative to volume growth. For the medium term, the company expects high-density LFP demand to ramp up in 2028 to 2029 and is finalizing supply contracts of about 40,000 tons. The pace of a recovery in end-market demand is, in the company's view, gradual.

06

Outlook

The most concrete direction the company has given is battery materials volume growth. Growth in battery materials is expected to come through in 2027, when shipments from projects that began in the third quarter of 2026 scale up and annual volume more than doubles.

The company expected this to be supported by new customers in the second half and expanded supply to LG Energy Solution, with second-half cathode shipments expected to exceed the first half. The Tennessee cathode plant is slated to begin production in the first quarter of 2027.

A 2025 report cited a target of KRW 30 trillion in battery materials revenue by 2030. The company aims for battery materials to account for more than 30% of total revenue from 2028. The fourth-quarter outlook is mixed by segment.

Battery material volume expansion is expected to lift sales in the fourth quarter, but profitability is seen flat because of the base effect of a one-time gain. Petrochemicals face continued high oil prices, customers holding off on purchases, and lost opportunities from the Yeosu naphtha cracker turnaround.

Life Sciences is expected to improve in the fourth quarter. Structural change in petrochemicals will proceed through a restructuring plan that is due by the end of 2026 and a shift toward higher-value products such as IPA and SSBR. The next checkpoints are the scope of that restructuring and the 2027 cathode shipment trajectory.

08

Bull factors

Planned Expansion in Battery Materials Shipments

The company expects annual cathode shipments in 2027 to more than double from 2026 levels. The Tennessee cathode plant is slated to start production in the first quarter of 2027, and the company expects high-density LFP demand to build from 2028 to 2029. The company also noted that continued metal price declines could keep sales growth below volume growth.

Profit Contribution from LG Energy Solution

LG Energy Solution posted third-quarter revenue of KRW 9.6 trillion and operating profit of KRW 756.0 billion. Excluding production subsidies, its operating profit was KRW 339.1 billion, so it stayed profitable without them. That contribution partly absorbs the petrochemical loss at the consolidated level.

Restructuring and Shift to Higher-Value Products

The company plans to submit a petrochemical restructuring plan by the end of 2026 and to shift the business toward higher-value products such as semiconductor-grade IPA and EV-grade SSBR.

It has said proceeds from the LG Energy Solution stake sale are being used for growth investment, financial soundness and shareholder returns. When this shift shows up in earnings still needs to be confirmed.

Bear factors

Petrochemical Losses and Oversupply

The petrochemical segment swung from a profit in the prior quarter to a loss in the third quarter, adding to earnings pressure. The company expects oversupply to persist into 2027, with the Shaheen project deepening oversupply in some domestic base oils and polyethylene. Turnaround-related lost opportunities in the fourth quarter add to the pressure.

Battery Dependence and Intra-Group Trade

A 2024 report put LG Energy Solution at 55% of total revenue. Intra-group sales that year were about KRW 6 trillion across subsidiaries, LG-affiliated companies and associates. Battery sales are directly exposed to industry cycles, policy shifts and changes in key customers' inventory strategy.

Financial Burden and Investment Scale

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 81.4% in 2022 to 114.5% in 2025. The controlling-shareholder net loss in 2025 was KRW 1.82 trillion. A press report noted that capital expenditure rose to KRW 15.3 trillion in 2024 from KRW 9.2 trillion in 2022.

Controlling-shareholder net losses over the latest four quarters total KRW 2.28 trillion, so balance-sheet capacity needs ongoing monitoring.

Risk factors

Raw Materials and Metal Prices

The company has said that continued metal price declines could limit sales growth relative to volume growth. In petrochemicals, lagged pass-through of raw material costs is another variable that narrows spreads.

A weaker won produced valuation losses on LG Energy Solution's foreign-currency assets and liabilities, which weighed on net income.

North American EV Demand and Policy

The company said that a shrinking North American EV market and project delays left 2026 shipments below early-year expectations. Third-quarter operating profit at LG Energy Solution included KRW 416.9 billion in North American production subsidies under US tax credit rules. Policy changes and customer timing therefore flow directly into reported earnings.

Petrochemical Oversupply and Geopolitics

Geopolitical risk in the Middle East and oversupply are keeping petrochemical performance weak. High oil prices and customers holding off on purchases weigh on fourth-quarter profitability. If the restructuring is delayed, the floor for petrochemical earnings may keep shifting.

12

Overall view

LG Chem's third-quarter 2026 results show a structure in which a petrochemical swing to loss is offset by better profitability at its battery subsidiary.

Revenue of KRW 15.5 trillion rose from a year earlier, but operating profit of KRW 561.2 billion fell, and the controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 648.2 billion extended the loss run. Across 2022 to 2025, the operating margin fell from 5.8% to 2.6%, with a recovery direction in 2025.

On the battery side, ESS shipments and better European utilization at LG Energy Solution support results, and the company expects 2027 cathode shipments to more than double from 2026.

Petrochemicals, however, face oversupply that the company expects to persist into 2027, so execution of the restructuring is the variable that will shape the earnings structure.

The debt-to-equity ratio at 114.5% and controlling-shareholder net losses of KRW 2.28 trillion over the latest four quarters are points to monitor for balance-sheet capacity.

The next checkpoints are the restructuring plan due by the end of 2026, the start of Tennessee plant production in the first quarter of 2027, and the 2027 cathode shipment trend.
