Report overview

Supercapacitor Demand: Can Profit Gains Last?

Korchip reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 21.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.43 billion, extending a four-quarter run of increases, yet annual operating profit fell for two straight years through 2025, so whether the recent improvement lasts is the central question.

02

Business structure

Korchip manufactures and sells carbon-based small and ultra-small secondary batteries under the Chipcell Carbon brand and lithium-based ones under Chipcell Lithium, and it also sells MLCCs made by Samsung Electro-Mechanics. The company listed on KOSDAQ on May 7, 2024.

It acquired the DLC division of Samsung Electro-Mechanics in 2002 and secured ultra-small supercapacitor technology through that deal. The supercapacitor share of revenue, the manufacturing side, rose from 30% in 2020 to 67.4% in the third quarter of 2023.

Pre-listing coverage reported gross margins of 46.8% in 2022 and 46.3% in the third quarter of 2023 for supercapacitor manufacturing, against operating margins of 4% to 9% for MLCC distribution in 2020 to 2022.

Korea Investment & Securities said that, in connection with 2025 results, ultra-small Chipcell Carbon products grew 26.0% on a rebound in China sales even as the distribution share declined. Chipcell Carbon is used in IP cameras, smart meters and solar inverters.

Reported customers include Samsung Electronics, LG Electronics, Amazon, Epson, Brother and DJI. One news report described Korchip as holding a distinctive position in Korea after more than 20 years in small and ultra-small supercapacitors.

Current segment-level revenue figures could not be confirmed in this research, so changes in the revenue mix are described qualitatively only.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 32.9 billion in 2023 to KRW 33.4 billion in 2024 (up 1.4%) and KRW 38.3 billion in 2025 (up 14.7%), an increase for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

Operating profit, by contrast, went from KRW 4.19 billion in 2023 to KRW 2.9 billion in 2024 (down 30.8%) and KRW 1.14 billion in 2025 (down 60.7%), a decline for two consecutive years, while the operating margin slid from 12.7% to 8.7% and then 3.0%.

Korchip attributed the weak 2025 profit to a higher manufacturing cost ratio from rising raw material prices and labor costs, plus higher costs from R&D and facility investment.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders also declined for two consecutive years, from KRW 4.8 billion in 2023 to KRW 3.23 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.85 billion in 2025, but the 11.8% drop in 2025 was far smaller than the 60.7% drop in operating profit, which appears to reflect support from non-operating items.

Quarterly revenue climbed from KRW 9.73 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 11.4 billion, KRW 12.1 billion, KRW 18 billion and KRW 21.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026, an increase for four consecutive quarters.

Operating profit also rose for four consecutive quarters over the same span, from KRW 66.05 million to KRW 148 million, KRW 1.07 billion, KRW 4.41 billion and KRW 4.43 billion, and the second-quarter operating margin of 24.5% was 19.0 percentage points higher than a year earlier.

In the third quarter, however, revenue grew 21.3% quarter on quarter while operating profit grew 0.5%, so the operating margin was lower than in the prior quarter.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung widely, from KRW 482 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 2.13 billion, KRW 3.49 billion and KRW 1.68 billion in the following three quarters, and the sum for the last four quarters is KRW 7.78 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 7.49 billion in 2023, KRW 4.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.61 billion in 2025, and the reason operating profit and net income diverged in the third quarter of 2026 can only be established from the non-operating items in the quarterly report notes.

05

Industry analysis

Korchip is classified in the semiconductor sector, but its actual business centers on supercapacitors, small secondary batteries and MLCC distribution, which places it in electronic components and energy storage.

Supercapacitors are used for power stability in data centers, including server power backup and instantaneous output response, and their range of applications is widening.

With AI data center investment continuing mainly in the United States and Europe, Korchip plans to win global IT infrastructure companies as new customers through its local subsidiaries.

On the MLCC side, reports said rising demand for high-spec AI server MLCCs is leading top makers to reorganize capacity toward higher value-added products.

A May 2026 report cited MLCC supply shortages tied to AI semiconductor growth and a large silicon capacitor order at Samsung Electro-Mechanics as background to the rise in Korchip's share price. The same report noted that brokerages were not publishing investment opinions or target prices on Korchip.

The company says it has reached technical specifications on par with global competitors. On industry position, in an Alphasquare comparison of the semiconductor and semiconductor equipment sector, Korchip ranked 118th of 153 companies by 2025 annual revenue, and its debt ratio was below the sector average.

The contrast with large semiconductor makers is therefore a small revenue base paired with a light balance sheet.

06

Outlook

Korchip said it plans to strengthen its medium- to long-term growth base by moving beyond single-component supply toward integrated solutions tailored to each customer.

According to the company, the prismatic Chipcell LTO battery has had its specifications fixed with a customer, and full-scale order wins are expected once mass-production equipment is completed in the second half.

Target applications are widening to data centers, automated guided vehicles and defense, and medium and large supercapacitors have entered the stage just before mass production.

On capacity, the company said in early 2024 that the second plant would lift ultra-small cell capacity from 14 million to 17 million cells a month.

In a January 2025 report, the Korea IR Service said Chipcell Lithium capacity was scheduled to expand from 1 million cells a month in 2024 to about 3 million by the end of 2025. No revenue or operating profit guidance for full-year 2026 from the company was found in this research.

Stunning Value Research said on September 1, 2026 that the investment question has shifted from whether sales will grow to how long profit growth can continue, and that the company could be revalued as a power backup solutions provider if the improvement proves sustainable.

That assessment is the firm's own view, and the timing of new product mass production and the size of orders need to be checked through filings and quarterly results.

08

Bull factors

Order growth tied to AI data centers

Korchip said on May 6, 2026 that supercapacitor orders had risen sharply on new demand centered on AI data centers. Revenue of KRW 18 billion in the second quarter and KRW 21.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026 show that order growth is flowing into reported sales. Because server power backup is a widening application, the customer base has room to expand.

Revenue growth flowing into profit

Operating profit rose from KRW 66.05 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 4.43 billion in the third quarter of 2026, an increase for four consecutive quarters. In the second quarter, revenue grew 109.3% from a year earlier while operating profit grew 824.5%, lifting the operating margin to 24.5%.

If a larger manufacturing share is what lifted the margin, room for further operating margin improvement could persist as revenue scales.

Light balance sheet and funding for new businesses

At the end of 2025 the debt ratio was 20.4%, with total equity of KRW 55.8 billion and total liabilities of KRW 11.4 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 4.61 billion in 2025, up from KRW 4.4 billion in 2024.

The company has relatively ample financial capacity to absorb spending on Chipcell LTO mass-production equipment and medium and large supercapacitors.

09

Bear factors

Operating profit growth stalled in the third quarter

Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 4.43 billion, only 0.5% above KRW 4.41 billion in the second quarter. Revenue rose 21.3% over the same period, so the operating margin declined.

Because annual operating profit fell for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025, it needs to be determined whether the improvement is temporary or structural.

Volatile net income and non-operating items

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 51.9% quarter on quarter, from KRW 3.49 billion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 1.68 billion in the third. Operating profit stayed at a similar level, so swings in non-operating items may have played a role.

Financial data from Hankyung Markets show non-operating income larger than operating profit in 2025, so net income can move differently from core profit.

High share price volatility

FnGuide data show Korchip's one-year beta at 1.57, above the KOSDAQ benchmark of 1.00. A brokerage industry source said in May 2026 that Korchip's share price volatility had increased.

Because the share price can move sharply on earnings expectations and theme-related reports, swings may widen around earnings confirmations.

10

Risk factors

Execution

The company's own explanation is that full-scale orders for the prismatic Chipcell LTO battery depend on completing mass-production equipment. A delay in construction or customer approval could push back the timing of new-business revenue.

Medium and large supercapacitors are also just short of mass production, so it may take time before they contribute to revenue.

Cost and profitability

Korchip cited a higher manufacturing cost ratio from rising raw material prices and labor costs, along with higher R&D and facility investment costs, as reasons for the 2025 decline in operating profit. If capital spending and hiring continue, depreciation and labor costs could weigh on the operating margin again. The lower third-quarter operating margin versus the prior quarter is also something to monitor.

Share count

The total number of issued shares rose to 8,770,126 at the end of the first quarter of 2026, including conversions of convertible bonds. Treasury shares totaled 557,237, equal to 6.35% of shares outstanding.

Further conversions or disposals of treasury shares would change the share count and could dilute earnings per share.

What to watch next

  1. Q4 2026 to Q1 2027

    Watch whether mass-production equipment for the prismatic Chipcell LTO battery is completed and whether a first order is disclosed. The company said it expects orders after the second-half equipment build, so schedule delivery is the benchmark for judging the new business.

  2. Mid-February 2027

    Fourth-quarter 2026 and full-year results are due to be disclosed. Full-year 2025 results were disclosed on February 11, 2026. The key points are whether fourth-quarter operating profit holds near the third-quarter level of KRW 4.43 billion and how the operating margin moves.

  3. March 2027

    The 2026 annual report should show the revenue mix by segment, the composition of non-operating items and any dividend decision. The notes may also explain why operating profit and net income diverged in the third quarter.

  4. Mid-May 2027

    The first-quarter 2027 report will show whether revenue growth continues. Whether operating profit growth, which slowed in the third quarter of 2026, catches up with revenue growth again is a clue to the durability of profit.

12

Overall view

Through the third quarter of 2026, Korchip's filings show revenue and operating profit rising for four consecutive quarters, a move away from the weak 2025.

Third-quarter revenue of KRW 21.8 billion was up 124.4% from a year earlier, and operating profit of KRW 4.43 billion compares with KRW 66.05 million in the same quarter a year before.

Yet operating profit grew only 0.5% quarter on quarter and net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 1.68 billion, so whether the pace of improvement is slowing is now the open question.

The bullish side points to larger AI data center orders, new products such as LTO batteries and medium and large supercapacitors, and a low debt ratio of 20.4%.

The bearish side points to the two consecutive years of declining annual operating profit from 2024 to 2025, net income volatility from non-operating items, and high share price volatility.

Current multiples show lower price-to-earnings and price-to-book ratios and a higher dividend yield than semiconductor sector averages, but the sector classification differs from the actual business, which limits interpretation.

The next points to check are the completion of LTO mass-production equipment with orders, and the direction of the operating margin in fourth-quarter 2026 results.
