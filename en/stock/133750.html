02

Business structure

MegaMD was founded in 2004 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2015. It runs two business segments: professional exam preparation and certification and lifelong education.

The professional exam segment sells lecture services under brands such as MegaMD, Mega Law School, Mega Lawyers and Mega Patent Attorney, while the certification segment operates remote and lifelong education institutes.

In the first half of 2025, professional exam products and services accounted for roughly 75% of sales and certification for roughly 25%. The same mix held in the third quarter of 2024, when the professional exam segment generated KRW 21.2 billion (72.34%).

Lectures are the largest revenue source: a third-party aggregator puts lecture revenue at about 85% of sales in the second quarter of 2026, with book sales at about 9%.

Core customers are university students and adult candidates preparing for professional licenses and certifications, and demand depends on exam system changes and recruitment numbers. The company also runs offline campuses, including a PEET prep school in Gwangju.

In 2025 it went through a small merger absorbing its subsidiary MegaLand, and it has said no further restructuring is planned after the merger. The 2025 annual report lists one inside director as the representative director of the largest shareholder, so the relationship with that shareholder's group is worth tracking.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 64.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 36 billion in 2023, a 44.1% decline, then rose to KRW 36.5 billion in 2024 (up 1.2%) and fell to KRW 34.6 billion in 2025 (down 5.2%). 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest in the 2022 to 2025 range.

The company attributed the weak 2023 to the end of the PEET pharmacist exam. Operating profit went from KRW 2 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 3.94 billion in 2023, then back to an operating profit of KRW 2.24 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.81 billion in 2025 (down 18.9%).

Operating margin was 3.1% in 2022, -10.9% in 2023, 6.1% in 2024 and 5.2% in 2025. The company linked the 2024 return to profit to cost reductions from a restructured business.

Controlling shareholders' net profit went from a net loss of KRW 3.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 3.78 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.79 billion in 2025 (down 26.3%). Net profit including non-controlling interests is disclosed at the same yearly amounts.

By quarter, the third quarter of 2025 posted revenue of KRW 9.32 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.67 billion; the fourth quarter swung to revenue of KRW 7.13 billion (down 23.5% from the prior quarter) and an operating loss of KRW 570 million.

The first quarter of 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 150 million on revenue of KRW 7.78 billion. Second-quarter revenue reached KRW 9.01 billion (up 15.8% quarter on quarter) with operating profit of KRW 995 million.

Third-quarter revenue of KRW 8.75 billion was down 2.9% quarter on quarter and down 6.2% year on year, while operating profit of KRW 1.05 billion was up 5.3% quarter on quarter but down 37.2% year on year.

Controlling shareholders' net profit peaked at KRW 1.89 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then fell to KRW 1.59 billion and KRW 1.35 billion. Operating cash flow stayed positive from 2023 to 2025, at KRW 1.05 billion, KRW 8.39 billion and KRW 5.1 billion.

The reason for the fourth-quarter 2025 loss cannot be pinned down from the disclosed figures alone, so the notes in the next annual report are the place to check it.

05

Industry analysis

MegaMD's end market is professional licensing exams and lifelong education, where exam system changes and candidate numbers drive demand. Changes in the examination system, recruitment numbers and lecture content competitiveness have been cited as factors affecting results.

The 2023 slump after the PEET exam ended shows how a single exam's change can swing total sales. Industry commentary cites rising demand for online education, but company revenue fell over the same period. Demand growth in the sector and the company's own sales trend did not move together.

Quarterly revenue in 2026 has ranged between KRW 7.78 billion and KRW 9.01 billion after the 2022 peak. Competition comes from online lecture providers and offline academies in the same exam market.

Direct comparison with listed IT and software peers is limited by very different business mixes, so the difference is better read as a difference in business structure.

Industry exposure therefore rests on exam system and candidate numbers, and the next annual report's revenue trend will show how those variables play out.

06

Outlook

In a value-up plan disclosed on March 27, 2026, the company said it will pursue maintaining an operating profit structure based on expanding market leadership. The same plan presented sustainable dividends as part of shareholder returns and value enhancement. The company stated that its 2025 payout ratio was 47.48%.

It also said it qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act. A cash dividend decision was disclosed on March 12, 2026, with the total amount calculated on shares outstanding excluding treasury shares.

The annual general meeting was held on March 27, 2026, approving director re-elections and articles changes. The board after the meeting has five directors, two of them outside directors, a 40% outside director ratio.

No quantified guidance, new contract wins, capacity expansion or new product timelines were found in the disclosures reviewed. The next checkpoints are whether the operating profit structure holds and whether dividend sustainability is maintained.

Operating profit recovered in recent quarters while controlling shareholders' net profit declined, so whether these two lines move in the same direction is worth watching.

With the dividend policy set out in disclosures, dividend sustainability can be checked against the size of earnings.

Overall view

MegaMD's disclosed results swung from an operating loss in 2023 back to operating profit in 2024 and 2025, but revenue fell from KRW 64.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 34.6 billion in 2025.

Quarterly results in 2026 recovered from a first-quarter operating profit of KRW 150 million, yet third-quarter operating profit and revenue were both below the prior-year quarter.

The balance sheet looks relatively stable, with the debt ratio down from 42.3% in 2022 to 25.4% in 2025 and total equity well above total liabilities. On shareholder returns, the company disclosed a 2025 payout ratio of 47.48% and high-dividend company status.

Revenue volatility tied to exam systems and recruitment numbers, and sensitivity of quarterly results to a small earnings base, remain. On valuation, the price-to-book ratio sits below 1x, and earnings-based multiples are affected by the recent decline in profit.

The next checkpoints are 2026 annual revenue and operating margin in the annual report, the fiscal 2026 dividend decision, and whether operating profit stays in the black in the first quarter of 2027.
