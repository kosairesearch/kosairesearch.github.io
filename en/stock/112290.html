Ycchem: Revenue Up Four Straight Quarters After Losses Narrowed

Ycchem's revenue has risen for four straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026, and quarterly operating profit has stayed positive since the fourth quarter of 2025, while quarterly net income remains volatile and leverage is high.

  1. 1

    Revenue reached KRW 83.1 billion in 2025, up 18.2% year on year, the largest year in the 2022–2025 period and the second consecutive year of growth.

  2. 2

    The 2025 operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.77 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a profit of KRW 4.63 billion.

  3. 3

    Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 29.1 billion, up 38.7% from a year earlier, and operating profit of KRW 1.85 billion compares with an operating loss of KRW 569 million a year earlier.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Ycchem is a semiconductor process materials specialist founded in 2001 that listed on KOSDAQ in July 2022. Before its listing it operated under the name Yeongchang Chemical.

Its core business is photo materials, centered on photoresist-related electronic materials used to form circuit patterns in advanced chip processes. According to Kiwoom Securities, photo materials accounted for 54% of revenue in the first quarter of 2026, wet chemicals for 27%, and PR rinse and other products for 18%.

At the same time, semiconductor electronic materials made up 97% of revenue and general chemicals 3%. The company said that sales of photoresist-related electronic materials led the revenue growth in the first quarter of 2026.

Among next-generation products, EUV metal-oxide photoresist (MOR) and EUV rinse have been formally supplied to customers since the second half of 2025.

Glass-substrate coatings also expanded supply in the same period, and the product is reported to be the first successfully developed and commercialized by a domestic materials company.

A CMP slurry for hybrid bonding has entered performance verification, and a material for HBM processes is reportedly being developed for SK hynix. The company is also expanding sales channels in Asia, including Japan and Taiwan.

In a 2025 report, Daol Investment & Securities said Ycchem absorbed volume previously held by competitor Merck from the 1b nm node onward and widened its share at the customer. Other semiconductor materials companies in the same industry include Dongjin Semichem, Soulbrain, and Hansol Chemical.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell 24.4% from KRW 82.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 62.3 billion in 2023, then recovered to KRW 70.3 billion in 2024 (up 12.9%) and KRW 83.1 billion in 2025 (up 18.2%). Revenue grew in 2024 and 2025, two consecutive years of growth, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

Operating profit of KRW 5.31 billion in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 7.83 billion in 2023, and the loss widened to KRW 8.16 billion in 2024. The 2025 operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.77 billion, and the company has posted losses in three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

The operating margin improved from -12.6% in 2023 and -11.6% in 2024 to -2.1% in 2025. The company attributed the narrower 2025 operating loss to higher revenue and said higher non-operating income, including fire insurance proceeds, also contributed.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a profit of KRW 4.63 billion in 2025, and operating cash flow moved from negative KRW 4.43 billion in 2023 and negative KRW 6.93 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.6 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, the operating loss of KRW 569 million in the third quarter of 2025 (net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.03 billion) turned into operating profit of KRW 1.16 billion in the fourth quarter.

First-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 26 billion, up 7.4% quarter on quarter, and operating profit was KRW 1.6 billion, up 37.5%.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 27.1 billion, up 4.0%, and operating profit was KRW 2.12 billion, up 32.8%, but the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 466 million.

In its semi-annual report, the company said this reflected a valuation loss of KRW 1.566 billion on derivatives linked to its warrant and convertible bonds.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 29.1 billion, up 7.5% quarter on quarter and 38.7% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 1.85 billion, down 12.8% quarter on quarter, with net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 220 million.

Revenue has risen for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totals KRW 4.46 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The company, explaining its first-half 2026 results, cited an improving semiconductor end market and higher sales of photo materials as key drivers. Expansion of EUV processes and rising HBM demand are mentioned as sources of additional sales for related materials.

Kiwoom Securities said rising investment by major chip customers would lift demand for general-purpose products, and that growth would become more pronounced in 2027 as the high-value product lineup expands.

GROWTH Research was reported in May 2026 to expect the company to benefit uniquely from being the first to supply high-margin glass-substrate materials in the industry. On the packaging side, glass-substrate and hybrid-bonding materials are presented as new businesses tied to next-generation semiconductor packaging.

The company says its next-generation materials have entered a market-settling stage and points to new product mass production and plant expansion effects. In EUV rinse, the company is expanding share by absorbing volume previously supplied by global incumbents.

Because its business is centered on photo materials, results may react closely to investment trends in leading memory makers. Whether HBM materials reach mass production will be a key variable for sales scale.

Domestic peers in the same industry include Dongjin Semichem, Soulbrain, and Hansol Chemical, which allows comparison of product portfolios and customer mix.

06

Outlook

The company expects that sales of new products, now fully reflected in annual results from 2026, will further strengthen profitability. A company official said new materials are expected to account for roughly 20% of 2026 sales.

In its first-half 2026 announcement, the company said profitability gains would widen as new product mass production and plant expansion effects take hold in the second half.

It is expanding capacity through its fifth plant and preparing mass production of new products including glue cleaner and glass-substrate materials. A CMP slurry for hybrid bonding has entered performance verification, and a company official said it could be monetized within 2026.

The company plans to expand next-generation materials in the second half, centered on dedicated materials for HBM and EUV processes. According to the company, supply of glass-substrate coatings is rising in step with its customer's glass-substrate mass production pace.

The company's plan is to expand overseas sales centered on Asia, including Japan and Taiwan. The next checkpoint is whether the 2026 annual report shows the share of new product sales and the effect of the fifth plant in annual figures.

Bull factors

Revenue growth and a rising share of new products

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 29.1 billion, up 38.7% from a year earlier, and revenue has risen for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. EUV rinse, MOR, and glass-substrate coatings have been formally supplied to customers since the second half of 2025.

The company's expectation that new materials will be about 20% of 2026 sales is cited as support for this trend.

Sustained operating profit and margin improvement

The operating margin narrowed from -12.6% in 2023 and -11.6% in 2024 to -2.1% in 2025. Quarterly operating profit has been positive in every quarter from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and first-quarter 2026 operating profit rose 37.5% quarter on quarter. The company said the operating margin improved substantially as new product sales took effect.

Expanding next-generation materials portfolio

Dedicated materials for HBM and EUV processes, a CMP slurry for hybrid bonding, and glue cleaner are presented as commercialization targets. Kiwoom Securities analyzed that growth would become more pronounced in 2027, citing the profitability of glass-substrate materials.

In a 2025 report, Daol Investment & Securities said the company was absorbing volume previously held by competitor Merck.

09

Bear factors

Net income volatility and derivative valuation effects

The second-quarter 2026 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 466 million reflected a derivative valuation loss of KRW 1.566 billion linked to warrant and convertible bonds, according to the company.

Because derivative liabilities are revalued with share price movements, they affect quarterly net income. Third-quarter 2026 net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 220 million, below operating profit of KRW 1.85 billion.

Financial burden

The debt ratio rose from 71.9% in 2022 to 97.7% in 2023, 161.3% in 2024, and 252.4% in 2025. Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 124.1 billion, larger than total equity of KRW 49.2 billion. Operating cash flow turned positive at KRW 1.6 billion in 2025, after negative results in 2023 and 2024.

Customer concentration and competition

EUV rinse volume for a leading customer is cited as a variable for results. Daol Investment & Securities said that securing additional customers could expand 2026 revenue further. Whether the competitive position against global players such as Merck holds is a key variable.

Risk factors

Customer mass-production timing

Growth in new materials depends on mass-production schedules at a small number of customers and on the verification progress of HBM and hybrid-bonding materials. The company said its hybrid-bonding slurry is in performance verification, but the timing of the shift to mass production depends on customer decisions. Delays could change the outlook for the share of new materials.

Balance sheet and derivatives

The debt ratio rose to 252.4% at year-end 2025, and derivatives linked to warrant and convertible bonds mean share price moves flow into valuation gains and losses. In the second quarter of 2026, a KRW 1.566 billion derivative valuation loss produced a net loss. Changes in financing conditions could add to earnings volatility.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 87.0% quarter on quarter in the first quarter of 2026 and turned to a net loss in the second quarter. Net income moved sharply from quarter to quarter even as operating profit stayed positive.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totals KRW 4.46 billion, a small base.

Overall view

Ycchem's revenue fell in 2023, then rose in 2024 and 2025, two consecutive years of growth, and 2025 brought a narrower operating loss and a turn to profit in net income attributable to controlling shareholders.

Quarterly operating profit has been positive in every quarter from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 29.1 billion was up 38.7% year on year.

Strengths include photo-material-led sales growth, the start of supply for next-generation EUV and glass-substrate materials, and an improving operating margin.

Weaknesses include quarterly net income volatility, derivative valuation effects, a financial burden reflected in a debt ratio of 252.4%, and possible dependence on a few customers. Both sets of factors currently coexist, and whether operating profit translates into stable net income is the central checkpoint.

On the business side, the share of new materials, the effect of the fifth plant, and customer diversification are the items to confirm.

The price-to-book ratio sits in a range with a large premium over net assets, and earnings-based multiples depend on how far net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters, totaling KRW 4.46 billion, can grow. The next regular report is the 2026 annual report, with a filing deadline in late March 2027.
