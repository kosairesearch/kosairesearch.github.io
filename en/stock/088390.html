INNOX Corporation: Turnaround Profit Amid Quarterly Swings

INNOX Corporation is a holding company whose profit attributable to shareholders turned positive in 2025, yet the third quarter of 2026 brought an operating loss and a net loss attributable to shareholders, leaving quarterly results highly volatile.

  3. 3

02

Business structure

INNOX Corporation functions more as a holding company that owns stakes in subsidiaries and divides business areas among them than as an operating manufacturer. Consolidated revenue in 2025 was KRW 59.1 billion, and the consolidated business spans three areas: battery materials, leisure and bicycles, and investments.

The company is also the largest shareholder of INNOX Advanced Materials, which was spun off through a split.

In battery materials, a lithium hydroxide subsidiary completed a first plant with annual capacity of 20,000 tons in Ochang, Chungbuk, and a second plant on the remaining site would bring the total to 50,000 tons. The company said the first plant's output equals batteries for about 400,000 electric vehicles.

That subsidiary is also exploring cooperation with domestic battery makers and global automakers. In anode materials, a silicon anode subsidiary produces silicon powder used as the raw material for silicon anodes, which offer higher capacity and output than graphite anodes.

Anodes account for roughly 14% of battery material cost. The leisure segment is the bicycle business under Alton, and the investment segment is I-Best.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 80.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 61.7 billion in 2023 (down 23.3% year on year) and KRW 56.2 billion in 2024 (down 9.0%), before rising to KRW 59.1 billion in 2025 (up 5.2%). The largest year among 2022 to 2025 was 2022, and the smallest was 2024.

Operating profit dropped from KRW 21.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.14 billion in 2023 (down 76.3%), then rose to KRW 6.48 billion in 2024 (up 26.1%) and KRW 6.53 billion in 2025 (up 0.7%), increasing in each year after the 2023 low.

The operating margin, however, fell from 26.9% in 2022 to 8.3% in 2023 and has since stayed at 11.5% in 2024 and 11.0% in 2025, not returning to the 2022 level.

Profit attributable to shareholders moved from KRW 14.9 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 13.8 billion in 2023, and the loss widened to KRW 19.9 billion in 2024. It turned to a profit of KRW 23.2 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 20.7 billion in 2025, below the shareholder portion, which suggests losses at non-controlling interests. A March 2026 disclosure tied the net income turnaround to equity-method gains from associates and income from investment companies.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 5.96 billion in 2023 to -KRW 2.64 billion in 2024 and -KRW 4.51 billion in 2025. Quarterly, revenue in Q4 2025 was KRW 10.7 billion (down 19.8% from the prior quarter) with an operating loss of KRW 1.92 billion, yet profit attributable to shareholders was KRW 12.9 billion.

Q2 2026 revenue rose to KRW 26.2 billion (up 81.6% quarter on quarter) with operating profit of KRW 5.27 billion, then Q3 2026 revenue fell to KRW 6.85 billion (down 73.8%) and the quarter swung to an operating loss of KRW 6.77 billion.

05

Industry analysis

INNOX Corporation's center of gravity is shifting toward battery materials. Anodes are a core material accounting for roughly 14% of battery material cost, and silicon anodes offer higher capacity and output than graphite anodes.

One brokerage analyst noted that battery-related shares move with lithium carbonate and lithium hydroxide prices. Another analysis said demand for de-Chinanization is rising under the US Inflation Reduction Act, which could benefit anode subsidiaries.

That view assumes the policy environment at the time of reporting, so demand assumptions could change if policy shifts. The position of the lithium price cycle is a key variable for the margins of the lithium hydroxide business.

Electric vehicles and energy storage systems are the main end markets for lithium hydroxide, and the lithium subsidiary is pursuing the ESS market with customers. In competition, scale of supply and the scope of customer qualification are the key variables for silicon anodes.

Within the group, INNOX Advanced Materials, a display and semiconductor materials company, was described as maintaining stable profitability even as the OLED market slowed. INNOX Corporation itself combines materials with a consumer bicycle business, so exposure to any single industry cycle is relatively spread out.

06

Outlook

The concrete plans the company has announced focus on expanding production capacity. The lithium hydroxide subsidiary plans a second plant on the remaining site that would bring total capacity to 50,000 tons.

The silicon anode subsidiary raised silicon powder output from about 30 tons per month in 2020 to about 300 tons per month in 2025, and plans to expand to about 1,000 tons per month in 2027. The start dates and customer demand for these projects are not yet reflected in reported results.

Separately, INNOX Corporation was reported to have bought KRW 2.0 billion of shares in its leisure subsidiary Alton on the open market, which some coverage interpreted as a signal of responsible management and a move into new businesses. Whether these purchases lead to better results is not yet visible.

On the earnings side, the decline from KRW 26.2 billion in Q2 2026 revenue to KRW 6.85 billion in Q3 is the next point to watch. The key questions are whether quarterly revenue recovers from Q4 onward and whether the operating margin stabilizes around 11%.

Because the 2025 turnaround leaned on equity-method and other non-operating items, the share of such items in the next annual report is also important.

Bull factors

Battery materials expansion axis

The silicon anode subsidiary raised monthly silicon powder output from about 30 tons in 2020 to about 300 tons and plans about 1,000 tons per month in 2027. These gains depend on start-up timing and secured demand.

2025 return to profit and operating recovery

Operating profit rose to KRW 6.48 billion in 2024 and KRW 6.53 billion in 2025 after the 2023 low. Profit attributable to shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 13.4 billion.

Subsidiary share purchases as a signal

INNOX Corporation was reported to have bought KRW 2.0 billion of shares in its subsidiary Alton on the open market. Coverage read the purchase as a commitment to enhancing corporate value and as a move toward responsible management and new businesses.

The company is also the largest shareholder of INNOX Advanced Materials, so its ownership structure matters for control of subsidiaries.

Bear factors

Q3 2026 swing into loss

The Q3 revenue drop was steep, and operating profit turned into a loss, pushing net income attributable to shareholders into a loss as well. Compared with Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 26.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.27 billion, the quarter-to-quarter gap is wide. Whether the cause is temporary or structural is a point to check in upcoming disclosures.

Negative cash flow and rising leverage

Operating cash flow was -KRW 2.64 billion in 2024 and -KRW 4.51 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 31.0% in 2022 to 55.8% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 116.1 billion and total equity of KRW 207.9 billion at the end of 2025. With operating cash generation weak, the higher debt share is a bearish factor.

Earnings recovery leaning on non-operating items

The 2025 net income turnaround was tied in a disclosure to equity-method gains from associates and income from investment companies. Operating profit rose only 0.7% in 2025 to KRW 6.53 billion. Against the 26.9% operating margin of 2022, the recovery in core profitability has been limited.

Risk factors

Raw material and lithium price swings

Profitability in lithium hydroxide and battery materials is sensitive to the direction of lithium prices. A brokerage analyst noted that battery-related shares move with lithium carbonate and hydroxide prices. A decline in lithium prices could weigh on utilization and margins at the hydroxide plant. A delay in the second plant would also lengthen the payback period on invested capital.

Quarterly volatility and reliance on non-operating items

In Q4 2025, the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.92 billion, yet profit attributable to shareholders was KRW 12.9 billion. Non-operating items were large enough to shape the reported quarter. In Q3 2026, operating and net losses appeared together. Because equity-method results depend on investee performance, the durability of such profit is limited.

Leverage and funding for expansion

The debt-to-equity ratio stood at 55.8% at the end of 2025, up from 31.0% in 2022. Operating cash flow was negative in 2024 and 2025, so funding expansion and share purchases from operating cash alone is difficult. If battery materials capacity expansion proceeds, additional borrowing or equity raising remains possible.

Overall view

INNOX Corporation is a holding company with battery-materials subsidiaries, and the biggest change is that profit attributable to shareholders turned positive in 2025.

That turnaround, however, was tied in disclosures to equity-method gains from associates and income from investment companies, and operating profit grew only 0.7% in the same year. The operating margin has fallen from 26.9% in 2022 to 11.0% in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 55.8%.

In Q3 2026, revenue was KRW 6.85 billion and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 6.77 billion, a sharp reversal in quarterly results. Profit attributable to shareholders over the most recent four quarters totaled KRW 13.4 billion, which is positive, but quarterly results have alternated between profit and loss.

Battery-materials expansion is visible through concrete plans for a second lithium hydroxide plant and higher silicon anode output, and whether start-up and demand translate into revenue is the next point to check.

The share price sits well below the book value attributable to shareholders, but that does not settle whether earnings will persist. The operating margin and the weight of non-operating items in the next annual business report are the central points for reading this stock.
