02

Business structure

ISC is a semiconductor test-component company whose core products are test sockets that electrically connect chips to final test equipment.

Its main product lines are silicone rubber (elastomer) sockets, pogo-pin sockets and burn-in sockets, and the company says elastomer sockets account for 80% of sales with an estimated global share of about 90%.

Businesses acquired in Q2 2025 added module testers, high-speed burn-in testers, EFEM, chemicals and PCBs to the portfolio. Of Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 71.8 billion, AI-related revenue was KRW 62.6 billion, up 46% year on year, and its share rose to 87%.

Revenue from AI data center applications exceeded KRW 50 billion in a quarter for the first time, with growth extending from AI accelerator sockets to server CPU products. The company says its share within one North American CPU customer is 100%.

The mobile business, long centered on R&D sockets for a global smartphone customer, began supplying mass-production test products for mobile AP and CPU in Q3 2026, and orders for SLT sockets for a flagship smartphone AP increased.

In memory, ISC is expanding development and supply of test solutions for HBM and SOCAMM2 alongside legacy product sales. Overseas sales account for 75% of revenue, and most transactions are denominated in US dollars.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 178.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 140.2 billion in 2023, a 21.6% decline, then rose 24.4% to KRW 174.5 billion in 2024 and 26.2% to KRW 220.2 billion in 2025, so revenue grew two years in a row from 2024 to 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 55.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.7 billion in 2023, an 80.8% decline, then rose 317.1% to KRW 44.8 billion in 2024 and 34.1% to KRW 60.1 billion in 2025, and press reports described 2025 as a record annual result.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 44 billion in 2022, KRW 13.2 billion in 2023, KRW 54.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 56.1 billion in 2025, while net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 56.3 billion in 2025, a small gap.

The operating margin moved from 31.2% in 2022 to 7.7% in 2023 and then recovered to 25.7% in 2024 and 27.3% in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 65.4 billion in 2022, KRW 22.4 billion in 2023, KRW 50.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 41.7 billion in 2025, declining in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 64.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 17.4 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 72.3 billion and KRW 21.9 billion in Q4 2025, a 12.1% quarter-on-quarter gain.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 68.3 billion with operating profit of KRW 23.6 billion, which press reports called a record quarterly operating profit. Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 72.9 billion was described by the company as a quarterly record.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 71.8 billion, down 1.6% quarter on quarter, and operating profit was KRW 21.8 billion, up 2.5% quarter on quarter, with about KRW 1.1 billion of revenue deferred to Q4 by the capacity bottleneck.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 21.7 billion in Q4 2025 and then declined for three straight quarters to KRW 20.5 billion, KRW 19.3 billion and KRW 14.8 billion. The four quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026 total KRW 76.3 billion in profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for test sockets is growing as AI accelerator, server CPU and ASIC product lines expand. The company says faster, higher-power AI chips, rising pin counts and larger packages have raised test difficulty and expanded demand for high-specification sockets.

Press coverage has described ISC as entering a structural growth phase tied to the AI semiconductor boom. Brokers have focused on system-level test (SLT), which is gaining weight as chips grow more complex and testing shifts from individual chips to whole systems.

The company says its SLT sockets, which it describes as earning 30–40% better profitability than general sockets, reached 70% of its mass-production mix in Q1 2026. In memory, demand is concentrated in high-end products such as HBM while legacy memory is weaker.

Some analysts say the recovery in consumer IT demand from smartphones and PCs has been slow. The company argues that large global customers now treat the ability to supply volume on time, alongside technology, as a key competitive factor.

Listed peers in the same industry include TSE, LEENO Industrial and HanaMicron, and the roughly 90% global share cited by the company is its own estimate.

06

Outlook

On October 27, 2026, the company disclosed under fair disclosure rules that it expects 2026 consolidated revenue of KRW 305.0 billion and operating profit of KRW 100.0 billion.

The same disclosure put the Q4 estimate at about KRW 92.0 billion in revenue (range KRW 87.0–97.0 billion) and about KRW 33.3 billion in operating profit (range KRW 28.3–38.3 billion).

Adding that estimate to preliminary nine-month operating profit of KRW 66.8 billion brings annual operating profit to roughly KRW 100 billion, according to press reports. The company says Q4 is a seasonally weak quarter but expects its largest quarterly revenue of the year.

On capacity, the Vietnam Fab 1 line began operating in October 2026, and Fab 2 is targeted to start in Q2 2027. Once the KRW 100 billion investment across Vietnam and Songdo is complete, annual socket capacity is expected to rise from about 2 million units to as many as 5 million.

The Songdo fab broke ground on November 12, 2026, with construction due to finish by the end of 2027 and commercial operation planned from 2028. A summary of the earnings call says management targets growth of 30% or more in 2027, and that automation is to rise from about 45% in October 2026 to 85% by October 2027.

The company also flags that delays in capacity expansion or customer product approvals could push these targets back.

08

Bull factors

Rising AI revenue share

AI-related revenue reached KRW 62.6 billion in Q3 2026, up 46% year on year and 87% of total revenue. AI data center revenue passed KRW 50 billion in a quarter for the first time. The company says it is broadening its lineup to server CPUs and ASICs.

Capacity expansion timeline

Vietnam Fab 1 began operating in October 2026, and Fab 2 is targeted for Q2 2027. The Songdo fab is planned for commercial operation from 2028, with capacity expected to reach as many as 5 million units by 2029.

In a market where timely volume supply has become a competitive factor, the expansion is the company's response.

Higher-value mix and profitability

The company says SLT sockets reached 70% of mass-production mix in Q1 2026 and earn 30–40% higher profitability than general sockets. Operating margin held at about 30% in Q3 2026. A summary of the earnings call says the CPU segment's operating margin already exceeded 55%.

09

Bear factors

Production bottleneck

Q3 revenue came in somewhat below market expectations. A production-capacity constraint in post-processing inspection pushed roughly KRW 1.1 billion of orders into Q4. If capacity lags orders, quarterly revenue may not rise in step with bookings.

Three straight quarters of lower controlling-shareholder profit

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 21.7 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 14.8 billion in Q3 2026 over three consecutive quarterly declines. Operating profit rose over the same period, so the two measures are moving in different directions.

Q3 2026 profit attributable to controlling shareholders was down 7.9% year on year. The company said the weaker dollar against the won weighed on Q3 results.

Weak consumer IT and legacy memory

Some analysts say the recovery in consumer IT demand from smartphones and PCs has been slow. Consumer IT-related test socket revenue was reported to be somewhat weak in Q1 2026. In memory, legacy product sales are soft, so growth in high-end products such as HBM has to carry the segment.

10

Risk factors

Capacity execution and customer approvals

Delays in capacity expansion or customer product approvals could push back the timeline for 2027 growth targets. A summary of the earnings call said the 30% growth target is conservative, with upside to 40% if permitting and ramp-ups proceed smoothly.

The company says the KRW 100 billion investment will be executed in stages tied to customer demand and approval schedules.

Exchange rates and dollar-based transactions

Most transactions are dollar-denominated, so won-dollar moves feed directly into won-based revenue and profit. Currency weakness weighed on Q3 2026 results. With overseas sales at 75% of revenue, the exchange-rate direction remains a source of quarterly earnings swings.

Automation and labor costs

Post-processing inspection is labor-intensive, and labor was described as about 30% of total cost. As of October 2026 the company put combined automation at about 45% and targets 85% by October 2027. Whether that target is met will feed directly into labor costs and operating margin.

12

Overall view

ISC's disclosed results show revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders rising two years in a row after the 2023 low, with the operating margin recovering from 7.7% in 2023 to 27.3% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue held around KRW 68–73 billion through 2026, and Q3 operating profit of KRW 21.8 billion was up 25.4% year on year. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders, however, has fallen for three straight quarters from its Q4 2025 peak of KRW 21.7 billion, moving against operating profit.

Part of Q3 revenue slipped into Q4 because of a capacity bottleneck, which makes quarterly results sensitive until new capacity comes online. The bullish points are an AI share of 87% of revenue, an estimated global share of about 90% in silicone rubber sockets, and the profitability profile of SLT sockets.

The bearish points are the capacity bottleneck, exposure to the dollar in most transactions, and weakness in consumer IT and legacy memory. The next checkpoints are Q4 2026 results, the preliminary full-year 2026 results in early 2027, and the 2026 annual report.
