Report overview

Shrinking Sales, Narrowing Losses, and Recapitalization in Focus

Foodnamoo's sales have declined for three straight years through 2025, yet restructuring and asset disposals turned attributable net income positive in 2025 and lifted total equity to KRW 35 billion, while operating losses have continued through the third quarter of 2026.

02

Business structure

Foodnamoo, founded in 2013, centers its business on selling chicken-breast products and convenience health foods (HMR) through online platforms.

Its flagship channel is a chicken-breast specialty platform, and as of a 2024 press report its platform business was split into seven platforms including its diet-food and chicken-product brands. Company profile data cites roughly 3.19 million members built through brand management and marketing.

Products are developed quickly using outsourced manufacturing. The company said its 'Matisseun' chicken brand, known in Korean as Masitdak, passed 263.31 million cumulative packs sold in December 2025.

Per the December 2025 disclosure, domestic sales account for the bulk of revenue, with revenue types including product sales, logistics, ERP and content services, and chicken sales.

Overseas, a 2024 press report referenced business in China and Vietnam, and in October 2025 the company set up a U.S. overseas subsidiary, RANKING GLOBAL USA. At the end of 2024 it had seven subsidiaries, and only two were reported as profitable. In November 2025 it sold its fulfillment subsidiary.

Competitively, it overlaps with online and home-shopping distributors such as Coupang and Hyundai Home Shopping, and its scale is far smaller than large retailers such as E-Mart and Lotte Shopping.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 217.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 190.7 billion in 2023 (down 12.2% year on year), KRW 124.1 billion in 2024 (down 34.9%) and KRW 86.8 billion in 2025 (down 30.1%), with declines in each year from 2023 through 2025.

Operating losses were KRW 2.09 billion in 2022, KRW 17.8 billion in 2023, KRW 17.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.15 billion in 2025, and operating margins were -1.0%, -9.3%, -14.0% and -9.4% over the same years.

For 2023, a company official pointed to weak consumer spending and initial investment costs at newly established subsidiaries. For 2025, the company said weaker consumer sentiment and sluggish marketing reduced sales, while restructuring and asset disposals cut fixed costs and raised disposal gains.

As a result, attributable net income was KRW 2.02 billion in 2025, a swing to profit, while net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 2.03 billion. Attributable net losses widened to KRW 19.9 billion in 2023 and KRW 45.1 billion in 2024 before turning positive in 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 4,913.1% in 2024 to 146.9% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 21.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 15.3 billion in the fourth quarter (down 29.9% from the prior quarter), and KRW 16.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 10.6%), after which it fell to KRW 12.9 billion and KRW 11.4 billion, a two-quarter decline.

Operating losses widened from KRW 2.17 billion and KRW 1.52 billion in the third and fourth quarters of 2025 to KRW 7.02 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then narrowed to KRW 3.64 billion and KRW 3.01 billion.

The fourth-quarter 2025 attributable net income of KRW 12.9 billion came in the same quarter as the November 2025 subsidiary sale. Attributable net losses have followed for three quarters since the first quarter of 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 4.05 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The convenience health food and HMR market is presented as growing, supported by rising single-person households and greater interest in health. An information service reports that low-calorie, low-sodium and high-protein product lines are expanding.

Foodnamoo's sales, however, declined over the same period, a company official cited weak consumer spending in 2024, and the 2025 disclosure again pointed to weaker consumer sentiment. The gap between category growth and the company's shrinking top line is therefore the central issue.

In online distribution and platform competition, the capital strength and logistics scale of large operators matter. Large retailers such as E-Mart and Lotte Shopping are far bigger and more diversified, while Foodnamoo is concentrated in chicken-breast and diet foods.

Its strengths cited include a member base of about 3.19 million and fast product development through outsourced manufacturing. In cycle terms, the company's own sales contraction is appearing before any sign of sector recovery.

Whether product strength can translate into revenue recovery is the next test, given that quarterly sales have declined since the first quarter of 2026.

06

Outlook

When it submitted its audit report in March 2026, the company said it would focus on profitability-driven operations and strengthening its core competitiveness.

At the same time it said it was pursuing workforce restructuring, the sale of non-core subsidiaries and logistics efficiency, and that it was preparing capital-raising and funding plans.

On the major stake, the company said it received a proposal from a private investment firm, that due diligence is complete, and that specific terms have not been fixed. On products, the company announced that cumulative sales of its chicken brand passed 263.31 million packs in December 2025.

Overseas, it set up a U.S. subsidiary in October 2025, and a 2024 press report said it planned to start sales in China in the second half of that year. No official revenue or profit targets were found in the sources.

The next check is whether revenue declines stop in the 2026 full-year results, and whether operating losses narrow beyond the KRW 3.01 billion seen in the third quarter of 2026. The concrete form of the capital plan and the terms of the stake transaction would change how the balance sheet and ownership should be read.

08

Bull factors

Balance sheet normalization under way

Total equity rose to KRW 35 billion at the end of 2025 and liabilities fell to KRW 51.5 billion, a sharp recovery from roughly KRW 2.2 billion of equity at the end of 2024.

The 2025 consolidated statements received a clean external audit opinion, and the company moved off the administrative issue and investor-caution designations. Press coverage described the recovery in financial credibility and transparency.

Narrowing operating losses

Operating losses narrowed from KRW 17.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 8.15 billion in 2025, and the operating margin improved from -14.0% to -9.4%. Quarterly operating losses also narrowed from KRW 3.64 billion in the second quarter of 2026 to KRW 3.01 billion in the third quarter. Attributable net income moved from a loss to a profit in 2025.

Brand volume and category growth

The company said cumulative sales of its chicken brand passed 263.31 million packs in December 2025. An information service attributes category growth in convenience health foods and HMR to rising single-person households and health awareness. The company is expanding its low-calorie, low-sodium and high-protein lines.

Bear factors

Persistent revenue decline

Revenue has fallen for three straight years through 2025, and after peaking in the first quarter of 2026 it declined in the second and third quarters. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 11.4 billion was down 47.7% year on year. The company itself cites weaker consumer sentiment and sluggish marketing as drags on recovery.

Continuing quarterly losses

Operating losses have continued for five straight quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. The first-quarter 2026 operating loss widened to KRW 7.02 billion.

Attributable net losses have followed for three straight quarters since the first quarter of 2026, and the last four quarters total KRW 4.05 billion.

Ownership and funding variables

In June 2026 CEO Kim Woo-ju obtained a 28.63% stake, changing the largest shareholder. The company said it received a proposal related to the major stake, but its terms are not fixed.

Two third-party share issuances of KRW 10 billion each took place in July and September 2026, and the balance-sheet repair has relied on outside capital and preferred-share conversion.

10

Risk factors

Overseas subsidiary returns

Foodnamoo set up a U.S. overseas subsidiary, RANKING GLOBAL USA, in October 2025, and at the end of 2024 only two of its seven subsidiaries were reported as profitable. Overseas operations typically carry initial investment costs before returns appear, so the expansion could add to the burden on domestic earnings.

If the U.S. unit does not convert into sales, the recovery of overseas investment could be delayed and related costs could keep accumulating.

Durability of restructuring gains

Lower fixed costs and asset disposal gains both contributed to the improvement in 2025 earnings. Workforce restructuring, the sale of non-core subsidiaries and logistics efficiency are under way, but if these measures do not reduce fixed costs, the earnings structure of 2025 will be hard to sustain. Disposal gains are largely one-time in nature, so their contribution to later quarterly results may be limited.

Audit opinion and administrative designation

The 2025 consolidated statements received a clean audit opinion, which removed the company from the administrative issue and investor-caution designations. The 2026 annual report is due by late March 2027, and if attributable net losses and operating losses continue through that point, the audit result could change. A qualified or adverse opinion could become grounds for re-designation as an administrative issue.

12

Overall view

Foodnamoo's sales have fallen for three straight years, yet in 2025 it achieved both a swing to attributable net profit and a recovery in equity.

Operating losses narrowed from KRW 8.15 billion in 2025 to KRW 3.01 billion in the third quarter of 2026, but operating losses have continued for five straight quarters since the third quarter of 2025.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 11.4 billion was down 47.7% year on year, so a sales recovery is not yet visible in the numbers. The debt ratio fell from 4,913.1% in 2024 to 146.9% in 2025, and the clean audit opinion has led press coverage to describe a restored credibility of the financials.

On the other side, the last four quarters total an attributable net loss of KRW 4.05 billion, and the fourth-quarter 2025 profit came in the same quarter as a subsidiary sale, so the durability of earnings needs more quarters to confirm.

The proposed sale of the major stake and further capital plans are variables that could change ownership and funding direction. The next check is the revenue trend and operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
