02

Business structure

Hyundai Autoever was founded in 2000 as the IT services and automotive software arm of Hyundai Motor Group. Its 2021 merger with Hyundai AutoEver's affiliates Hyundai Autron and Hyundai MNsoft widened the business into embedded automotive software, high-precision mapping, in-vehicle OS and cloud control systems.

Revenue is split between the Enterprise IT segment, which combines system integration (SI) and IT outsourcing (ITO), and the Automotive SW segment. In third-quarter 2026, Enterprise IT revenue rose 19.7% year on year to KRW 1.02 trillion, while Automotive SW revenue rose only 0.8% to KRW 199.9 billion.

In the first half of 2026, SI revenue grew 26.2% to KRW 867.4 billion and ITO revenue grew 18.8% to KRW 908.8 billion. The core automotive product is the mobilgene standard vehicle OS, split into a Classic version for chassis and powertrain and an Adaptive version for high-performance processors.

High-precision maps and cloud links sit in the same value chain. Customer concentration is high: affiliates generated KRW 2.10 trillion, or 96.1% of first-half 2026 revenue, and overseas revenue rose to 35.6% of the total.

In IT services the company competes for group demand with Samsung SDS and LG CNS, but its automotive software role, supplying the group's standard middleware, is different in nature. Winning third-party orders from outside the group remains an unproven task.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual figures, revenue rose from KRW 2.75 trillion in 2022 to KRW 4.25 trillion in 2025. Revenue grew for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Growth was 11.3% in 2023, 21.2% in 2024 and 14.5% in 2025.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 142.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 181.4 billion in 2023, KRW 224.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 255.3 billion in 2025. The operating margin improved from 5.2% in 2022 to 5.9% in 2023, 6.0% in 2024 and 6.0% in 2025, and has barely moved since 2024.

Controlling-shareholder net income rose from KRW 113.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 182.5 billion in 2025, but the 2025 gain of 6.8% trailed the 13.8% operating profit gain. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 186.8 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 78.5% in 2023 to 93.6% in 2024 before easing to 92.3% in 2025. At year-end 2025, total equity was KRW 1.87 trillion, controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 1.85 trillion and total liabilities were KRW 1.73 trillion.

Operating cash flow grew from KRW 201.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 423.6 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, revenue peaked at KRW 1.32 trillion in the fourth quarter of 2025, then fell to KRW 935.7 billion in the first quarter of 2026 with operating profit of KRW 21.2 billion, which market commentary attributed to slower SW business revenue.

Revenue recovered to KRW 1.25 trillion in the second quarter of 2026, with operating profit of KRW 90.5 billion. The third-quarter operating margin of 5.6% was below the 6.7% of a year earlier, according to press reports of the 30 October 2026 preliminary results. Controlling-shareholder net income over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 183.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for Hyundai Autoever is closely tied to the investment cycle of Hyundai Motor Group. The group plans to invest about KRW 125 trillion through 2030 to move its factories toward software-defined factories, which is expected to lift demand for system build-outs and IT services.

In robotics, the company is expected to handle system integration and robot control systems if Boston Dynamics robots are deployed in production lines. On the vehicle side, the shift to SDV architectures is moving cars from distributed ECUs toward central high-performance computers and zone controllers.

LS Securities has argued that this makes software integration more valuable, since consolidated controllers need more complex operating systems and middleware.

In IT services the company is compared with Samsung SDS and LG CNS, while its automotive software role differs because it supplies the group's standard platform. However, Automotive SW revenue grew only 0.8% in the third quarter of 2026, so the effect of wider mass production has not yet shown fully in quarterly sales.

The company has not disclosed separate revenue figures for its high-precision map and cloud businesses, so those contributions can only be discussed qualitatively. Whether third-party customers outside the group grow will be the main yardstick for its position in the sector.

Outlook

In late January 2026 the company disclosed through a fair disclosure filing a 2026 consolidated revenue outlook of KRW 4.51 trillion, and it did not provide profit guidance, citing high external uncertainty.

According to press reports, revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 totaled KRW 3.41 trillion, up 16.4% year on year, with cumulative operating profit of KRW 180.6 billion, up 1.0%. The FnGuide consensus compiled for 2026 shows revenue of KRW 4.77 trillion and operating profit of KRW 277 billion.

Samsung Securities, in a report carried by the press on 30 October 2026, projected operating profit of KRW 277 billion for 2026, KRW 355 billion for 2027 and KRW 536 billion for 2028.

The same brokerage estimated that GPU purchasing agency work in data center builds from 2027 through 2029 could generate revenue of about KRW 5 trillion. The roughly KRW 365 billion GPU contract signed in July 2026 can be read as the starting point of this flow.

In a September 2026 report, LS Securities said mass-production timing and revenue per vehicle, along with the profitability of integration, verification and OTA operations, would be key variables for future earnings and valuation.

The company has said it plans to extend automated verification to actual customer controllers from late 2026 into 2027. Other new businesses, including robot control and the RMAC center, have no disclosed revenue timing yet.

08

Bull factors

Wider role in the group's SDV shift

LS Securities said in a September 2026 report that the company's role now extends from architecture design and mass-production verification to OTA operations. The group plans about KRW 125 trillion of investment through 2030 toward software-defined factories. Bulls cite wider per-vehicle software scope as a source of development fees and per-controller license revenue.

Large GPU contract and data center revenue potential

In July 2026 the company signed a roughly KRW 365 billion GPU server bulk purchase and supply deal covering Hyundai Motor and KIA. Samsung Securities estimated that GPU purchasing work from 2027 through 2029 could bring about KRW 5 trillion of revenue. If that estimate materializes, the top line would rise well beyond its current scale.

A rising operating profit estimate path

Second-quarter 2026 operating profit reached KRW 90.5 billion, and first-half SI revenue rose 26.2%. Samsung Securities projects operating profit rising from KRW 277 billion in 2026 to KRW 355 billion in 2027 and KRW 536 billion in 2028.

Bulls point to high-value projects such as next-generation ERP and cloud services as drivers of margin improvement.

09

Bear factors

Stalled operating margin

The operating margin held at 6.0% in both 2024 and 2025. The first-half 2026 margin fell to 5.1% from 5.8% a year earlier. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 2.6% to KRW 68.9 billion. Bears argue that top-line growth has not translated into margin gains.

Group dependence and third-party wins

Affiliates accounted for KRW 2.10 trillion, or 96.1%, of first-half 2026 revenue. Commentators note that the company must reduce its reliance on the group market and widen orders from overseas automakers and parts suppliers. Bears note that a slowdown in group investment could disrupt the growth path.

Slowing Automotive SW business

Automotive SW revenue rose only 0.8% to KRW 199.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026. The first-quarter 2026 profit decline was linked to delayed SW business revenue. Bears argue that SDV volume gains will take time to show up in quarterly sales.

10

Risk factors

Balance sheet

The debt ratio jumped to 93.6% in 2024 and eased to 92.3% in 2025. Total liabilities of KRW 1.73 trillion are close to total equity of KRW 1.87 trillion at year-end 2025.

Operating cash flow rose to KRW 423.6 billion in 2025, which cushions the balance sheet, but the working capital needs of large contracts should be watched.

Talent and R&D costs

Competition for software talent in SDV development is raising personnel costs. First-half 2026 R&D spending fell 9.8% to KRW 34.3 billion, and its share of revenue dropped from 2.03% to 1.57%. The capitalized and expensed split also shifted, so the effect of accounting treatment on reported profit needs to be checked.

Margins on large contracts

GPU server purchasing and supply contracts can carry equipment costs within revenue, so margins may be thin relative to sales size. Whether operating margin gets diluted as revenue grows is a key check. The timing and profit margin of the post-2027 revenue estimates remain limited in what the company has disclosed.

12

Overall view

Hyundai Autoever provides IT services and automotive software for Hyundai Motor Group, and disclosed revenue grew from KRW 2.75 trillion in 2022 to KRW 4.25 trillion in 2025. Operating profit reached KRW 255.3 billion in 2025, but the operating margin has stayed around 6.0%.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 16.2%, while operating profit fell 2.6% and Automotive SW revenue grew only 0.8%. Bull factors include a wider role in the group's SDV shift, a roughly KRW 365 billion GPU contract and potential large data center revenue, and brokerage estimates of rising operating profit.

Bear factors include the 96.1% affiliate revenue share in the first half of 2026, a stalled margin and slow Automotive SW growth. On the balance sheet, the debt ratio is 92.3% and operating cash flow has risen, and both sit alongside each other.

The price multiple is high relative to recent earnings, so the pace of margin recovery is the central question. The next checkpoints are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report.
