02

Business structure

Lake Materials is a specialty company that designs organometallic compounds and manufactures and supplies them as ultra-high-purity products. It listed on KOSDAQ through a merger with the SPAC Dongbu No.5 in 2020 and holds three unlisted subsidiaries.

Its core technology is TMA (trimethylaluminum) manufacturing, which media reports describe as the only such capability in Korea and one of only four in the world.

Its products fall into semiconductor deposition precursors for ALD and CVD, solar materials, LED materials and petrochemical catalysts such as MAO for metallocene catalysts.

By segment, semiconductor materials accounted for 59.71%, solar materials 25.75%, petrochemical catalysts 8.04% and LED materials 5.98% of sales in the third quarter of 2024. Solar materials generated KRW 17.4 billion, or about 17.5%, of the KRW 99.3 billion in revenue for January to September 2025.

Past reports said TiCl4 was supplied to Samsung Electronics in mass production, and TSMC, KIOXIA, EPISTAR and LG Chem were also named as customers. Petrochemical catalysts are presented as a new business with strengths in high-value materials such as MAO for metallocene catalysts.

Sulfide lithium for all-solid-state batteries was developed through a subsidiary in which the company holds a 70.6% stake, and as of early 2024 it was at the stage of supplying samples to customers. The broad product mix spans markets with different cycles, so weakness in one segment can weigh on overall margins.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show diverging trends in scale and profitability. Revenue fell 10.4% from KRW 131.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 117.8 billion in 2023, then recovered to KRW 138.7 billion in 2024, up 17.8%, and KRW 141.2 billion in 2025, up 1.8%, making 2025 the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit fell from KRW 35.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 29.5 billion, KRW 22.2 billion and KRW 13.3 billion, declining for three straight years, with 2025 the smallest year. The operating margin over the same period fell from 26.9% to 25.1%, 16.0% and 9.4%.

Profit declined even in 2024 and 2025, when revenue grew, so higher sales did not translate into higher earnings.

The company cited higher costs during the evaluation period for new items, lower utilization from slower growth in the solar market, and expenses from new businesses including all-solid-state batteries and a US subsidiary.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 27 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.69 billion in 2025, while total net income including non-controlling interests fell from KRW 27.2 billion to KRW 7.8 billion.

Operating cash flow bottomed at KRW 896 million in 2024 before recovering to KRW 15.5 billion in 2025. On a quarterly basis, revenue in the fourth quarter of 2025 was KRW 41.8 billion with operating profit of KRW 4.78 billion, up sharply from the prior quarter.

Revenue dipped to KRW 33.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 before rising to KRW 37.2 billion in the second quarter and KRW 39.8 billion in the third quarter. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 5.27 billion, down 13.3% from the prior quarter but up 282.1% from a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 6.35 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then fell to KRW 2.53 billion in the second quarter and KRW 797 million in the third quarter.

05

Industry analysis

Demand from downstream markets falls into three areas: semiconductors, solar and petrochemicals. In semiconductors, a February 2026 report cited a strong industry backdrop, and the company's IR representative said semiconductor materials would support earnings.

Past reports showed that reduced wafer input by memory makers pressured sales, so semiconductor revenue also follows industry cycles. Solar materials are sensitive to the utilization of Chinese solar manufacturers.

US tariffs on Chinese solar products and tighter measures against indirect exports led to inventory adjustments and delayed investment by customers, which the company and media said contributed to weakness in the solar segment in 2025.

Petrochemical catalysts consist of high-value materials such as MAO for metallocene catalysts, and the company said profitability improvements would become visible as this business scales.

Because the company is understood to be one of only four global holders of TMA manufacturing technology, barriers to entry on the supply side are relatively high. However, if the demand cycles of the three areas move in different directions, a recovery in one segment can be offset by weakness in another.

Competitively, the scarcity of TMA technology is a strength, but in revenue scale the company is a small to mid-sized player that is hard to compare directly with large chemical groups such as LG Chem or Hanwha Solutions.

06

Outlook

The company's guidance came from a February 2026 IR event. Its representative said 2026 revenue would grow by more than 10% and that profitability would improve from the prior year. Based on disclosed results, third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 39.8 billion was up 10.1% year on year, consistent with that direction.

Profitability, however, varies widely by quarter. Operating profit held at KRW 6.08 billion in the second quarter and KRW 5.27 billion in the third quarter, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined over the same period.

Whether the profitability gains the company has pointed to in petrochemical catalysts translate into a recovery in the operating margin is the next checkpoint.

On new businesses, sulfide lithium for all-solid-state batteries has moved to sample supply and expansion of mass-production equipment, and the timing of mass production and customer contracts will need to be confirmed through future disclosures.

The KRW 50 billion convertible bond issued in May 2026 was disclosed as financing for facilities and operations, and the scale of spending and conversion terms will be clarified in later filings.

The 2025 annual preliminary results were disclosed in early February 2026, so the 2026 annual preliminary results may follow in early February 2027. The 2026 business report and the annual general meeting are expected around March 2027, when segment sales and dividend decisions will also be confirmed.

08

Bull factors

Room for margin recovery

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit reached KRW 5.27 billion, up 282.1% year on year, while revenue rose 10.1% to KRW 39.8 billion. With the operating margin having fallen to 9.4% in 2025, bulls argue there is room for margins to recover as sales grow. The company has said semiconductor materials and petrochemical catalysts would offset weakness in solar.

Technological scarcity and high-value products

TMA manufacturing technology is reported to be the only such capability in Korea and one of only four in the world. High-value materials such as MAO in petrochemical catalysts are presented as a strength in metallocene catalysts.

Bulls point out that technological scarcity can support pricing power and reduce the risk of customer defection.

Optionality in all-solid-state battery materials

The company completed development of sulfide lithium for all-solid-state batteries through a subsidiary, and past reports highlighted possible cooperation with existing customer Samsung SDI. The timing of mass production and revenue contribution has not been confirmed in public materials. If commercialized, it would add a new pillar to the business and is cited as a key option in bullish scenarios.

Bear factors

Long-term decline in operating margin

The operating margin fell from 26.9% in 2022 to 9.4% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, falling to KRW 7.69 billion in 2025. Bears center their argument on the multi-year pattern of revenue growing without corresponding profit growth.

Financial burden and convertible bonds

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 220 billion against total equity of KRW 135.8 billion, a debt-to-equity ratio of 161.9%. A KRW 50 billion private convertible bond was issued in May 2026.

Operating cash flow also dropped to KRW 896 million in 2024, so bears argue debt and convertible obligations could constrain the pace of profit recovery.

Volatility in quarterly earnings

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 6.35 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then fell to KRW 2.53 billion and KRW 797 million in the following two quarters. Operating profit also fell 13.3% from the prior quarter in the third quarter.

Because solar results depend on Chinese customer utilization and US policy, bears argue the durability of the recovery needs to be confirmed over several more quarters.

10

Risk factors

Policy and trade risk

US tariffs on Chinese solar products and tighter measures against indirect exports led to inventory adjustments and delayed investment among Chinese customers. Solar was cited as a main cause of the company's weaker 2025 results. Further changes to these policies could again disrupt demand and pricing in the solar segment.

Financing and dilution risk

A KRW 50 billion second-round private convertible bond was issued in May 2026 to fund facilities and operations. With a debt-to-equity ratio of 161.9%, further financing could limit financial flexibility. Conversion of the bonds would increase the share count and dilute existing shareholders.

Customer concentration and new-business validation risk

Semiconductor and solar revenue depends on a limited number of customers, so inventory or investment decisions by a single customer can directly affect results.

If customer qualification and the shift to mass production for new businesses such as solid-state battery materials and petrochemical catalysts are delayed, cost burdens may persist.

The company cited new-business expenses among the causes of its weaker 2025 results, so cost control during validation periods is important.

12

Overall view

Lake Materials' revenue rose from KRW 131.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 141.2 billion in 2025, while operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined over the same period, showing diverging trends.

The operating margin fell sharply from 26.9% in 2022 to 9.4% in 2025, and the company cited costs from new item evaluations, lower solar utilization and new-business expenses.

Quarterly operating profit in the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 4.25 billion, KRW 6.08 billion and KRW 5.27 billion, above the KRW 1.38 billion of the third quarter of 2025, and third-quarter operating profit rose 282.1% year on year.

However, net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell for two straight quarters after peaking in the first quarter of 2026, and the four-quarter total is about KRW 14 billion.

Bullish factors include the only-in-Korea TMA technology, growth in semiconductor materials and petrochemical catalysts, and optionality in solid-state battery materials.

Bearish factors include solar market conditions, US and Chinese policy variables, and financial strain from a debt-to-equity ratio of 161.9% and convertible bond issuance. Valuation sits at a high multiple relative to recent earnings, so the durability of quarterly profits is central to interpretation.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual preliminary results and the 2026 business report, and whether the operating margin recovers to a meaningful level in those documents is the key item to track.
