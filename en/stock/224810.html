02

Business structure

Eomjihouse was founded in 1986 to carry out interior construction and finishing work, and listed on the KONEX market in 2015. It handles interior design planning and engineering, construction and management, and building projects across residential, commercial and office spaces.

One company covers the whole chain from planning and design to construction, supervision and raw material sourcing. It is described as a contractor with a track record on landmark projects such as hotels, offices and public facilities.

A revenue breakdown by segment could not be confirmed in this research, and given its industry classification, contract construction revenue appears to be the core.

Its cost structure leans heavily on subcontracting. The cost-of-sales ratio was 91.01% in 2023, and subcontracting costs were reported at KRW 62.91 billion in the same year. This shows that interior construction runs on thin margins, where order volume and cost control largely determine profitability.

On ownership, Jung Jae-ho and seven related parties hold 52.75%, Lee Seon-gu holds 9.31%, and treasury shares account for 3.36% (as of 2026-10-12). The company counts Yogi Corporation as an affiliate. Market data lists the sector as distribution and consumer goods, but FnGuide classifies the company under construction.

Peer comparison therefore fits better with interior construction contractors than with furniture and interior retailers such as Hanssem.

03

Recent trends

FY2025 revenue was KRW 104.44 billion, up 28.1% from the prior year. Operating profit rose 346.0% to KRW 5.66 billion. Net profit was KRW 6.26 billion, a swing from a net loss of KRW 3.42 billion in 2024. Pre-tax profit was KRW 3.84 billion, and income tax expense was negative.

This means a meaningful part of the net profit gain came from a tax effect, so it should be read separately from operating performance.

The recovery is clear against earlier years. The operating loss was KRW 1.92 billion in 2023. In 2024, revenue fell 3% while operating profit turned positive. The balance sheet also changed. The debt ratio dropped from 703.63% in 2024 to 353.88% in 2025. Total equity rose from KRW 8.27 billion to KRW 14.53 billion. By contrast, operating cash flow was KRW 1.18 billion in 2025, down from KRW 25.22 billion in 2024.

The share price has been volatile. The close on 2026-03-31 was KRW 1,789. As of 2026-10-13 the price was KRW 1,290, with a 52-week high of KRW 2,745 and a low of KRW 788. The one-year return was -48.19%, and trading volume at that time was only 829 shares.

Based on FY2025 results, PER was shown as 0.87x and PBR as 0.36x. On 2026-10-27 the price is KRW 1,309, up 14.42% from the previous close, with a market capitalization of KRW 5.5 billion.

No separate disclosure or news explaining this move was found, and quarterly or half-year results for 2026 could not be confirmed in this research.

04

Outlook

Future results will depend on three factors: order volume, the cost ratio and interest burden. A market research service attributed the earnings improvement to rising interest in interior design, remodeling market expansion and growing investment in space value.

This is a general industry comment, however, and Eomjihouse's order backlog and new contracts could not be confirmed in this research. The operating margin improved to 5.42% in 2025 from 1.56% in 2024. Whether that level holds needs to be checked in the 2026 quarterly and half-year reports.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio has declined but remains high in absolute terms, so financing costs and borrowing structure stay under scrutiny. It is also worth noting that non-operating expenses weighed heavily on the net losses of 2023 and 2024.

Because FY2025 net profit included a tax effect, the underlying earnings power without it will become visible in later years. Given the thin trading on KONEX, the share price can move sharply on earnings releases or disclosures.

The direction of the market environment, order flow and financial indicators should be confirmed through later filings.

05

Bull factors

Recovery in operating profitability

The operating margin moved from -2.29% in 2023 to 1.56% in 2024 and 5.42% in 2025. Gross profit also grew from about KRW 7.6 billion in 2023 to KRW 9.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.7 billion in 2025. Larger profit alongside higher revenue shows scale and profitability improving together. Room for further margin improvement depends on cost ratio management.

Improving balance sheet

The debt ratio fell from 703.63% to 353.88%. Total equity rose from KRW 8.27 billion to KRW 14.53 billion. The capital increase is a result of the return to net profit, and the financial buffer is larger than before. The absolute level is still high, so whether the trend continues needs to be monitored.

Long operating history and project record

The company has built nearly four decades of operating history since its founding in 1986. It is described as having delivered large projects such as hotels, offices and public facilities. Remodeling market expansion and rising investment in space value are favorable industry factors cited by a market research service. This track record and these industry factors can be read as supportive for maintaining its order base.

06

Bear factors

Earnings quality and cash flow

Income tax expense was negative in 2025, so net profit exceeded pre-tax profit. Operating cash flow was KRW 1.18 billion, sharply below KRW 25.22 billion in 2024, and investing cash flow was an outflow of about KRW 6.4 billion.

Whether the rise in operating profit translated into cash generation needs further confirmation. In construction, working capital swings can heavily distort cash flow.

Leverage still high

Standalone figures for 2025 show total assets of KRW 65.96 billion, total liabilities of KRW 51.43 billion and total equity of KRW 14.53 billion. The debt ratio is 353.88%. A credit information service assessed the company as strong on profitability but relatively weak on stability. A tougher rate or liquidity environment could raise the financing cost burden.

Thin liquidity and large price swings

The 52-week range is KRW 788 to KRW 2,745, and the 12-month change was -48.19%. Trading volume as of 2026-10-13 was just 829 shares. With 4.2 million shares outstanding and a market capitalization of KRW 5.5 billion, even small trades can move the price substantially. A daily move such as 14.42% reflects this market structure.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Interior construction demand is tied to the construction cycle, interest rates and corporate capacity to invest in office space. Rising material and labor costs can squeeze margins if they cannot be passed through to contract prices. With a high debt ratio, rate changes can directly affect financing costs.

Official statistics on the size and growth of the domestic interior market could not be confirmed in this research.

Business

As an order-driven business, results swing considerably from year to year. Revenue in 2022 was KRW 36.51 billion, down 59.52% from the prior year. The cost-of-sales ratio was high at 91.01% in 2023. Delays on large projects, cost overruns or changes in subcontracting costs can erode profit quickly.

Market and governance

KONEX trading is thin, so prices can diverge depending on timing. The largest shareholder group holds 52.75%, above half of the shares. At the 2026-03-31 annual meeting, the KRW 2 billion director compensation cap passed, with approval at 49.4% of total issued shares. Minority shareholders may want to consider ownership concentration and the decision-making structure together.

08

Overall view

In its FY2025 results, Eomjihouse recorded higher revenue, expanded operating profit and a return to net profit, a clear improvement in profitability. The debt ratio also fell from 703.63% to 353.88%, giving it a larger financial buffer than before.

On the other hand, net profit included a tax effect, operating cash flow declined, and the absolute debt level remains high, all of which call for a cautious reading. On the market side, a KRW 5.5 billion market capitalization, thin volume and a -48.19% one-year change make price interpretation difficult.

Quarterly and half-year results for 2026 and order data were not confirmed in this research, so whether the 2025 improvement continues must be judged from future filings. This document is a qualitative summary for information only, and judgment is left to the reader.
