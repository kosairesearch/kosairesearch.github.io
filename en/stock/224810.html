02

Business structure

Eomjihouse is an interior construction and finishing contractor founded in 1986 and listed on KONEX in 2015. It handles interior design planning and design, construction and management, and construction projects across residential spaces, commercial facilities and offices.

The company describes a vertically integrated system that covers planning and design, construction, construction supervision and raw material procurement. It cites landmark projects such as hotels, offices and public facilities as its core track record.

Managing design, construction cost and materials within one structure links to project-level profitability control. The business centers on spatial design and construction services rather than finished product sales, so revenue appears tied to project awards and construction progress.

Among listed peers in the same sector, Hanssem focuses on furniture and interior products, a different business model. The interior construction market is competitive between conglomerate-affiliated interior firms and smaller contractors.

As of December 2025, the company had 116 employees, a small scale relative to large builders, so revenue can swing with large project awards.

03

Recent trends

Based on the 2025 business report, standalone revenue reached KRW 104.44 billion, up 28.1% from the prior year. Operating profit rose 346.0% to KRW 5.66 billion, and the operating margin improved to 5.42% from 1.56% in 2024. Net income of KRW 6.26 billion turned positive from a net loss of KRW 3.42 billion in 2024.

Pre-tax profit was KRW 3.84 billion, and the income tax line was a benefit, so part of net income reflects tax effects. Operating losses of KRW 8.53 billion in 2022 and KRW 1.92 billion in 2023 were followed by an operating profit in 2024, and profit continued in 2025.

Cash generation weakened: operating cash flow from ordinary activities fell to KRW 1.18 billion from KRW 25.22 billion in 2024, and cash operating profit dropped to KRW 1.75 billion from KRW 28.47 billion. The share price was volatile.

On April 23, 2026 it fell 14.92% to KRW 2,110, and on November 3, 2026 it rose 14.33% to KRW 1,428. According to wisereport, the 52-week high was KRW 2,745 and the low was KRW 788. CEO Jung Seok-hwan disclosed a 6.16% stake on April 23, 2026, up 0.12 percentage points from 6.04% on September 29, 2025. The 2025 business report, filed March 23, 2026, was flagged by the Korea Exchange for a correction request.

Outlook

In the near term, an extraordinary shareholders meeting will be held on December 18, 2026 at 8:30 a.m. in the basement meeting room of the head office, with the re-election of two inside directors, Jung Seok-hwan and Son Gi-geun, on the agenda. If both are re-elected, each term will be two years.

Given that the 2025 business report was filed on March 23, 2026, disclosure of 2026 full-year results is customary around March 2027. The key operating variable is whether revenue growth can be sustained at an operating margin in the mid-single digits.

On cash flow, the sharp decline in operating cash flow in 2025 makes project collections and working capital the items to watch in upcoming results. On the balance sheet, the 353.88% debt-to-equity ratio and 22.03% equity ratio (2025) are the key constraints to monitor.

Whether tax benefits again add materially to net income will also affect the quality of earnings. On governance, the meeting outcome and any further insider share disclosures will be the points to track.

05

Bull factors

Top-line growth and margin recovery

Standalone revenue rose 28.1% to KRW 104.44 billion in 2025. The operating margin improved to 5.42% from 1.56%, extending operating profit to a second consecutive year.

Wisereport cites rising interest in interior design, a growing remodeling market and increased investment in spatial value as drivers of the improvement.

Vertically integrated design and construction

The company describes an integrated system spanning planning, design, construction, supervision and raw material sourcing. Handling the full project cycle in-house links to the ability to offer design and construction together. Landmark projects in hotels, offices and public facilities are cited as evidence of this capability.

Higher insider ownership

CEO Jung Seok-hwan disclosed a 6.16% stake as of April 23, 2026. This is up 0.12 percentage points from 6.04% on September 29, 2025. Wisereport lists the largest shareholder group at 52.75%.

06

Bear factors

Heavy balance sheet leverage

The 2025 debt-to-equity ratio was 353.88% and the equity ratio 22.03%, so liabilities are large relative to capital. Borrowing dependence was 31.57% and the quick ratio 23.44%. The equity base is small, so the capital cushion is thin.

Weaker operating cash generation

Operating cash flow from ordinary activities fell to KRW 1.18 billion in 2025 from KRW 25.22 billion in 2024. Cash operating profit declined to KRW 1.75 billion from KRW 28.47 billion. Cash inflows did not keep pace with the rise in reported profit.

Earnings quality and filing history

Net income of KRW 6.26 billion exceeded pre-tax profit of KRW 3.84 billion in 2025 because of a tax benefit. Operating losses continued in 2022 and 2023, when the cost of sales ratio reached 91.01% in 2023. The 2025 business report was flagged by the Korea Exchange for a correction request.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Construction and interior demand is sensitive to interest rates and the property cycle. A drop in new residential and commercial orders could reduce construction volume. Because revenue leans on remodeling and spatial design demand, an economic slowdown could translate into earnings volatility.

Cost and operations

Reports indicate outsourcing costs roughly doubled to KRW 62.91 billion in 2023. In a model that relies heavily on subcontracting, subcontract costs and project schedule control drive profitability. The cost of sales ratio of 91.01% in 2023 left little margin for cost overruns.

Market and governance

As a KONEX-listed company with a small market capitalization, its share price swings sharply. It fell 14.92% on April 23, 2026 and rose 14.33% on November 3, 2026. The December 18, 2026 vote on inside directors and any further CEO share disclosures are the next items to track.

08

Overall view

Eomjihouse showed revenue growth, a recovery in operating profit and a return to net profit in its 2025 business report. The 5.42% operating margin remains low, and whether top-line growth translates into margin gains will be shown in subsequent results.

Net income was helped by a tax benefit, and operating cash flow fell sharply from 2024. The 353.88% debt-to-equity ratio and 22.03% equity ratio remain constraints on financial stability.

Supporting factors include two consecutive years of operating profit, an integrated design-and-construction model and a higher CEO stake. Offsetting factors include weaker cash generation, earnings quality questions and share price volatility.
