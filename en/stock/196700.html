Business structure

Waps (KOSDAQ: 196700) is a KOSDAQ-listed chemical company centered on functional materials. Its 2025 consolidated results disclosure attributed higher sales in major business segments, including functional materials, to the expansion of operating profit and net income.

Annual revenue moved from KRW 34 billion in 2022 to KRW 36.2 billion in 2023 and KRW 34.1 billion in 2024 before reaching KRW 43.2 billion in 2025, a 26.7% increase.

Chemical products tend to see profit swings driven by raw material prices and plant utilization, which is consistent with the quarterly volatility in the recent filings. Compared with large listed chemical peers, Waps is much smaller in business scale and market capitalization.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 40.4 billion and total liabilities were KRW 32.8 billion, and the debt ratio fell from 97.7% in 2023 to 81.4% in 2025. The largest shareholder group led by Lee Jae-chun held 39.24% as of November 4, 2026, a concentrated ownership structure.

On November 3, 2026, two related parties bought shares on the market. The company acquired 657,680 common shares under a trust contract between July 15 and September 18, 2026.

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, Waps started 2022 in the red, with an operating loss of KRW 1.1 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.5 billion.

The company turned profitable in 2023, with operating profit of KRW 2.57 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1 billion.

In 2024, operating profit rose to KRW 3.09 billion (up 20.4% year on year) and net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 1.2 billion (up 19.5%).

In 2025, revenue reached KRW 43.2 billion, operating profit reached KRW 5.55 billion (up 79.7%), and net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 4.1 billion (up 243.1%). The operating margin widened from -3.2% in 2022 to 7.1% in 2023, 9.1% in 2024 and 12.9% in 2025.

Revenue fell 6.0% in 2024 to KRW 34.1 billion, yet operating profit still grew, which means margins improved even as sales declined. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1 billion in 2023, KRW 1.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.1 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 2.32 billion in 2022, -KRW 5.41 billion in 2023, KRW 5.94 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.37 billion in 2025, so 2023 was the only negative year. The debt ratio rose from 83.5% in 2022 to 97.7% in 2023 before falling to 90.8% in 2024 and 81.4% in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 12.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.92 billion gave way to Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 10.1 billion, down 22.1% from the prior quarter, and Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 10.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 767 million.

Q2 2026 recovered to revenue of KRW 10.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.45 billion, but Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 9.79 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.1 billion gave way to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 191 million.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 3.29 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The chemical sector has a capital-intensive profile, with earnings swinging widely on raw material prices and plant utilization. Demand for functional materials tends to track capital spending and new product adoption cycles in downstream industries such as electronics, automotive and construction.

Commodity petrochemicals have faced price pressure from overseas capacity additions, while functional materials are shaped by product specialization and close customer relationships, with smaller specialty producers coexisting alongside large groups.

Whether Waps' 2025 improvement came from a sector upturn or from its own product mix and cost gains cannot be separated cleanly using disclosed figures alone. The year-on-year declines in Q3 2026 revenue and operating profit suggest both sector and company-specific factors may have played a role.

Against large listed chemical peers, Waps is smaller in both business scale and market capitalization. The Korea Exchange noted that Waps' market capitalization had been below KRW 20 billion for 25 consecutive trading days as of October 29, 2026.

Its position within the sector is better gauged through profitability metrics than by size, and whether its margin gains are above or below the sector average is a point to check against peer filings over the same period.
