02

Business structure

Bohae Brewery is a Honam-based liquor maker founded in 1952 that produces and sells soju, fruit wine and other alcoholic beverages. Fruit wine made up 48.71% of 2024 sales, followed by soju at 30.08%, plum beverages at 17.53% and other liquor at 4.76%.

Its core fruit wine lines are Maechwisun and Bohae Bokbunja, and the company is reported to rank first in traditional liquor sales on online platforms. Fruit wine revenue grew from KRW 34.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 39.5 billion in 2023 and KRW 42.7 billion in 2024, equal to 48.7% of 2024 sales.

Soju revenue fell every year, from KRW 33.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 30.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 26.4 billion in 2024. Ipsaeju once held about 80% of its regional market but lost share after failing to win younger consumers and losing ground to large liquor makers.

The company says soju is concentrated in regional markets, which has pushed its share down. The food and OEM segment declined from KRW 18.5 billion in 2022 and KRW 18.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 15.4 billion in 2024.

Domestic sales accounted for 94.30% of 2024 total sales and exports for 5.70%, and export revenue fell from KRW 6.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.0 billion in 2024.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales moved from KRW 90.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 93.1 billion in 2023 (up 2.4% year on year), fell to KRW 87.6 billion in 2024 (down 5.9%), and recovered to KRW 89.9 billion in 2025 (up 2.6%). 2023 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest among 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 61.88 million in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 2.8 billion in 2023, a swing into deficit, then returned to operating profit of KRW 2.3 billion in 2024, and reached KRW 3.81 billion in 2025, up 65.7%. The operating margin was 0.1% in 2022, -3.0% in 2023, 2.6% in 2024 and 4.2% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was a loss of KRW 2.83 billion in 2022 and a loss of KRW 3.56 billion in 2023, then turned to a profit of KRW 6.69 billion in 2024 and fell 47.8% to KRW 3.49 billion in 2025.

Operating profit rose in 2025 while net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell, so the two moved in different directions. Net income including non-controlling interests matched the controlling-shareholder figure in 2025 at KRW 3.49 billion.

The debt ratio was 78.8% in 2022 and 79.1% in 2023, and then 59.5% in 2024 and 62.4% in 2025. Total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 90.2 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 56.3 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 3.14 billion in 2022, KRW 942 million in 2023, KRW 3.29 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.02 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, sales went from KRW 24.6 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 22.1 billion in Q4 2025 (down 10.4% quarter on quarter), the low point, then rose to KRW 23.5 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 25.6 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 26.7 billion in Q3 2026, up 8.5% year on year.

Operating profit was lowest in Q4 2025 at KRW 237 million (down 78.8% quarter on quarter), rose to KRW 1.21 billion in Q1 2026 (up 411.4%), then KRW 1.12 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 1.76 billion in Q3 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 1.51 billion in Q3 2026, up 165.7% year on year, and the last four quarters total KRW 4.52 billion. The cost of sales ratio was 72.00% in 2023 and 69.17% in 2024, which may have contributed to the margin recovery.

05

Industry analysis

In soju, Bohae's position is defined by a shrinking share in its regional base. The decline in Ipsaeju's share coincides with intensifying competition from large liquor makers such as Hite Jinro, and the gap with nationwide brands remains a challenge.

In fruit wine, the brand is described as growing into a healthy-drinking brand in line with the healthy-pleasure trend, which signals a relative shift in the mix away from soju. The plum beverage segment's share of sales fell from 20.42% in 2022 to 17.53% in 2024.

In exports, the company has pursued expansion on the back of the Korean food boom, but this has not yet produced visible results. On costs, the cost of sales ratio fell to 69.17% in 2024 from 72.00% in 2023, a sign of defended profitability amid weak demand.

Whether fruit wine growth can offset the shrinking soju and export businesses is the central variable for its competitive position.

08

Bull factors

Fruit wine growth engine

Fruit wine revenue grew steadily from KRW 34.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 39.5 billion in 2023 and KRW 42.7 billion in 2024. Its share of sales rose to 48.71% in 2024, concentrating the revenue base in one product line.

The company is reported to rank first in online traditional liquor sales, which points to a strong position in distribution channels.

Profitability recovery

The operating margin rose from 0.1% in 2022 to 4.2% in 2025, and operating profit grew 65.7% to KRW 3.81 billion in 2025. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.76 billion was up 58.2% year on year, and the last four quarters of net income attributable to controlling shareholders total KRW 4.52 billion. The earnings base has moved from deficit toward profit and that movement continues on a quarterly basis.

Shareholder signals and insider buying

The company decided to acquire treasury shares to stabilize the share price and enhance shareholder value. An executive bought 18,000 shares on the open market on November 6, 2026. Related parties of the largest shareholder held 6,896,346 shares, or 24.96%, up 0.06 percentage points from the prior report. These are factual insider and shareholder movements that can be read alongside the earnings recovery.

09

Bear factors

Shrinking soju business

Soju revenue fell every year, from KRW 33.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 30.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 26.4 billion in 2024. The company itself has acknowledged that soju share has declined somewhat. Ipsaeju is viewed as having lost share sharply after failing to reach younger consumers.

Limited growth drivers beyond Bokbunja

The company has not secured a clear growth driver beyond Bokbunja wine, which is cited as a weakness. Food and OEM sales also fell to KRW 15.4 billion in 2024. With segments outside fruit wine shrinking, business diversification has not advanced.

Weak exports and net income

Export revenue declined from KRW 6.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.8 billion in 2023 and KRW 5.0 billion in 2024. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 47.8% to KRW 3.49 billion in 2025. Net income declined even as operating profit rose, which is a factor that requires attention to earnings quality.

Risk factors

Cost ratio reversal risk

The cost of sales ratio fell from 72.00% in 2023 to 69.17% in 2024, and this easing of cost pressure may have been one factor behind the margin recovery. If cost pressure rises again, the improvement in the operating margin, which reached 4.2% in 2025, could narrow.

Operating profit fell from KRW 1.21 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 1.12 billion in Q2 2026, so cost changes can quickly appear in quarterly earnings. In a structure where fruit wine is a large share, how far cost increases can be passed on to prices will shape this trend.

Trading and market-alert risk

Bohae Brewery was designated as an investment-caution issue on June 23, 2026. Under the market alert system, trading can be suspended at the investment-warning and investment-risk stages. Because a market alert was triggered by a sharp price rise, price swings can affect trading conditions.

Governance and share structure risk

Related parties of the largest shareholder hold 24.96%, a high concentration. The outside director ratio has stayed at 25% before and after the March 2026 change, with four registered directors in total.

The 5-to-1 share consolidation in April 2026 changed the share count structure, and such changes need to be tracked through disclosures.

12

Overall view

Bohae Brewery has raised operating profit and operating margin since returning to profit in 2024, anchored in a fruit wine-centered business, and sales rose for three straight quarters through Q3 2026 with Q3 operating profit up 58.2% year on year.

Still, fruit wine at 48.71% of 2024 sales makes results sensitive to one product line. Soju revenue has declined every year since 2022 and export revenue fell from KRW 6.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.0 billion in 2024, so diversification has not yet produced clear results.

Operating profit rose in 2025, but net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 47.8% to KRW 3.49 billion, so operating gains did not fully carry through to net income.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio was 62.4% in 2025, lower than in 2022, and all KRW 90.2 billion of equity belongs to controlling shareholders.

On governance, related parties of the largest shareholder hold 24.96%, and disclosures in 2026 covered a treasury share purchase, a share consolidation and an executive open-market purchase.

Earnings recovery is the main strength and the shrinking soju and export businesses are the main weakness, and the next checkpoint is the operating margin in the 2026 annual report alongside soju and export revenue trends.
