02

Business structure

Imagis is a fabless semiconductor design company listed on KOSDAQ in 2010. Its main products are Touch Controller ICs, Haptic Driver ICs, MST ICs and SAR (grip) Sensor ICs, centered on touch and haptic solutions for smartphones and other IT devices. Production is outsourced to foundry partners.

According to past press coverage, the company has supplied Samsung Electronics and major smartphone and notebook makers in volume, and in notebooks it won adoption in Samsung's Galaxy Book series and expanded business with overseas notebook brands.

Revenue shares by product or segment could not be confirmed from reliable sources in this research, so no figures are given.

The strategy is to widen applications from mobile to notebooks, automotive and robotics, and the company has previously made an initial shipment of automotive chips to an overseas semiconductor firm. In June 2026 it signed a supply contract of about KRW 5.78 billion with an overseas automotive chip distributor.

The contract equals 43.71% of its most recent annual revenue, with deliveries scheduled from July to December 2026.

In July 2026 the company said it would develop tactile sensors for robot hands under a government R&D project with the Korea Research Institute of Standards and Science (KRISS), and expand its AI Edge business using the on-device AI platform of Sima.ai.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 21.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.1 billion in 2023 (down 26.5%) and KRW 13 billion in 2024 (down 19.3%), then rose to KRW 13.2 billion in 2025 (up 1.8%). Among 2022 to 2025, the largest year was 2022 and the smallest was 2024.

Operating results went from an operating profit of KRW 1.55 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 2.31 billion in 2023, widened to an operating loss of KRW 5.21 billion in 2024, and narrowed to an operating loss of KRW 1.99 billion in 2025.

That is three consecutive years of operating losses from 2023 to 2025, and the operating margin was 7.1% in 2022, -14.4% in 2023, -40.2% in 2024 and -15.0% in 2025.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed from KRW 4.61 billion in 2023 to KRW 3.75 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.84 billion in 2025, while operating cash flow was -KRW 3.33 billion in 2024 and -KRW 964 million in 2025.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 3.16 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 3.46 billion in the fourth (up 9.5% quarter on quarter), KRW 3.61 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 4.5%), KRW 5.07 billion in the second (up 40.3%) and KRW 5.64 billion in the third (up 11.2%), four consecutive quarterly increases.

The operating loss was KRW 597 million in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 164 million in the fourth, then KRW 489 million in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 464 million in the second, before turning to an operating profit of KRW 79.36 million in the third quarter of 2026.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders also moved from a loss of KRW 344 million in the second quarter to a profit of KRW 17.99 million in the third, yet the combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (fourth quarter of 2025 to third quarter of 2026) is KRW 853 million.

The company attributed the 2025 improvement to higher product sales and lower selling and administrative expenses, and the third-quarter 2026 result to higher semiconductor sales and a better cost structure.

The operating losses of the first three quarters of 2026 combined exceed the full-year operating loss guidance, so meeting that guidance depends on the size of a fourth-quarter profit.

05

Industry analysis

Imagis sells into touch and haptic components for smartphones and notebooks, with automotive chips and robotic tactile sensing added on top. Touch ICs for smartphones and notebooks are a mature market, so quarterly revenue can swing sharply depending on whether customers adopt a given model.

The decline in revenue in 2023 and 2024 and the recovery from the fourth quarter of 2025 appear to reflect customer volumes and new design wins to a large extent. However, no reliable source was found in this research to quantify the end-market cycle.

In automotive, the company says it will expand its automotive chip business, citing medium- to long-term growth from vehicle electrification and electric vehicles.

In robotics, the company and press reports alike say that wider use of humanoid and collaborative robots is raising the importance of tactile perception and on-device AI.

Imagis is a small fabless company ranked in the lower tier of its industry by market capitalization, and its scale and cycle position differ greatly from the large listed names in the semiconductor sector.

As a result, individual customer orders and new product adoption matter more for earnings than sector-wide upswings.

06

Outlook

In June 2026 the company disclosed guidance of KRW 21 billion in revenue and an operating loss of KRW 700 million for the full year, which assumes revenue growth of roughly 60% from 2025 and a smaller operating loss.

The guidance reflects the KRW 5.78 billion automotive chip contract with deliveries from July to December 2026. On November 2, 2026 it announced the third-quarter return to an operating profit and said it aims to keep quarterly profitability in the second half.

The stated means are larger supply to existing customers, profitability-focused product management and cost efficiency. For 2027 it aims to extend revenue growth in the existing semiconductor business and settle into profitability.

In new businesses, it is developing a tactile sensor platform for AI robot hands based on a single-layer mutual-capacitance patent registered in June 2026, and plans to combine AI inference functions and application software to build a solutions business.

However, no order or revenue size has been disclosed for the robotics and AI Edge business, and whether it will contribute to near-term earnings has not been confirmed.

08

Bull factors

Revenue recovery and quarterly profit

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 5.64 billion was up 78.6% from a year earlier, with an operating profit of KRW 79.36 million. Revenue growth and fixed-cost efficiency coincided in the same quarter and lifted operating results. Four consecutive quarterly revenue increases suggest the recovery was not limited to a single quarter.

Automotive contract adds revenue visibility

The KRW 5.78 billion contract for deliveries from July to December 2026 equals 43.71% of the most recent annual revenue. Because part of second-half revenue is backed by a contract, it supports the KRW 21 billion full-year guidance. Automotive chips also broaden a customer mix concentrated in smartphones and notebooks.

Stronger balance sheet and Physical AI options

The debt ratio fell from 255.5% in 2023 and 198.0% in 2024 to 27.5% in 2025, with total equity of KRW 10.6 billion. Lower leverage gives the company room to operate even during loss-making periods.

The tactile sensor patent, the joint research with KRISS and the partnership with Sima.ai provide a technical base for entering the robotics market.

09

Bear factors

The profit is still very small

The third-quarter operating profit of KRW 79.36 million is around 1% of KRW 5.64 billion in revenue, a level that small cost swings could push back into a loss. The first and second quarters of 2026 had operating losses of KRW 489 million and KRW 464 million.

The combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 853 million, so earning power has not been fully proven.

Uncertainty over meeting guidance

The combined operating loss of the first three quarters of 2026 exceeds the full-year operating loss guidance, so a meaningful profit is needed in the fourth quarter. The automotive contract's delivery window ends in December, which makes the sustaining of 2027 revenue a key question.

The company's earlier long-term revenue target, set when it issued convertible bonds, was not met, which is worth keeping in mind.

New business contribution unconfirmed

The robotic tactile sensor and AI Edge businesses are at the stage of patent registration, joint research and partnership announcements, with no disclosed orders or revenue. Businesses where expectations form first can disappoint if commercialization is delayed. It also matters that the existing touch IC business remains the core of revenue.

10

Risk factors

Customer and product concentration

Touch and haptic ICs for smartphones and notebooks are the core business, so revenue depends heavily on model adoption and volumes at specific customers. The revenue decline in 2023 and 2024 may be related to this structure. If the customer mix does not broaden, quarterly volatility could persist.

Funding and dilution

In May 2025 the company decided on a KRW 6.5 billion rights offering with public subscription for unsubscribed shares, and the 6.3 million new shares exceeded one third of the shares outstanding at the time. In April 2026 trading was suspended for a share consolidation.

Since operating cash flow was negative in both 2024 and 2025, the possibility of further fundraising remains something to monitor.

Execution and new business

Revenue timing in automotive chips can shift with reliability qualification and customer production schedules. In robotics and AI Edge, R&D spending comes first while the timing of revenue recognition is uncertain. A profit built on cost efficiency could be eroded again if R&D spending expands.

12

Overall view

Imagis turned to a quarterly operating profit in the third quarter of 2026, with revenue of KRW 5.64 billion and an operating profit of KRW 79.36 million, and four consecutive quarterly revenue increases and a lower debt ratio point to an improving trend.

On an annual basis, however, it posted operating losses for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and the combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters is KRW 853 million.

The bull case rests on the automotive contract, cost efficiency and Physical AI options, while the bear case rests on an operating margin of around 1%, uncertainty over meeting guidance and the absence of new-business revenue.

Company guidance is KRW 21 billion in revenue and an operating loss of KRW 700 million for 2026, and since the combined operating loss of the first three quarters already exceeds that guided loss, the fourth quarter matters.

The price-to-book ratio is above the industry average, meaning the stock trades at a premium to net assets, and whether profit scale supports it will be settled by later filings.

The next points to check are the fourth-quarter revenue recognition, the full-year results around February 2027 and whether profitability holds in the first quarter of 2027. This report is for information only and contains no buy or sell opinion or target price.
