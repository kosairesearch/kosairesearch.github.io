02

Business structure

Wing Yip Food Holdings Group is a food company listed on KOSDAQ whose main operating stage is China. The company said in June 2026 that it would put funds into production facilities and marketing to respond to growth in China's health food market.

Its product focus appears to be dried meat snacks, and a new dried meat jerky product was unveiled at a Chinese trade fair in November 2026. Expansion is centered on Guangdong Province, where the company plans to widen its footprint from major cities to county-level areas and secure 100 franchisees within one year.

Because franchisee recruitment is the main channel for reaching more outlets, the pace of franchise additions is a key driver of sales volume. A Hankyung consensus page framed the outlook around the launch of a Sino-Korean joint brand.

The company reports in Chinese yuan (CNY), and the KRW amounts in this report are translated into won. Since operating profit has declined since its 2023 peak, the key question is which product mix and channel can restore earnings.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show sales and profit moving in different directions. Revenue rose from KRW 176.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 189.9 billion in 2023 (up 7.9% year on year) and KRW 208.2 billion in 2024 (up 9.6%), then fell to KRW 194.4 billion in 2025 (down 6.6%).

Operating profit peaked at KRW 26.4 billion in 2023 (up 18.4%) and then fell to KRW 21.5 billion in 2024 (down 18.3%) and KRW 15.1 billion in 2025 (down 29.9%), declining for two consecutive years. The operating margin moved from 12.6% in 2022 and 13.9% in 2023 to 10.3% in 2024 and 7.8% in 2025.

In 2025, operating profit fell far more than sales, so the margin compressed sharply.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders declined from KRW 17.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 17 billion in 2023 (down 0.5%), KRW 15.1 billion in 2024 (down 11.0%), and KRW 11.2 billion in 2025 (down 25.9%), a three-year run of declines from 2023.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was reported at the same levels in each year. Operating cash flow was KRW 18 billion in 2022, KRW 36 billion in 2023, KRW 23.5 billion in 2024, and KRW 4.6 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 240 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and the debt ratio stood at 25.8%.

Quarterly results were volatile: operating profit was KRW 4.355 million in the third quarter of 2025, when controlling shareholders posted a net loss of KRW 163 million, then rose to KRW 5.46 billion in the fourth quarter with a KRW 4.93 billion net profit.

Sales fell in the first and second quarters of 2026 to KRW 43.9 billion and KRW 43.5 billion, while operating profit reached KRW 5.45 billion in the second quarter. The sum of controlling-shareholder net profit over the last four quarters was KRW 8.18 billion.

Industry analysis

The downstream market for Wing Yip Food is consumer demand for health foods and snacks in China. The company said it is raising funds to respond to growth in China's health food market. Whether market growth translates into sales depends on how fast franchise outlets are added and on product pricing.

The dried meat snack segment appears competitive on price and distribution, and the company's response centers on new products and a franchise model. In cycle terms, earnings have been in a downturn since the 2023 peak.

Sales peaked in 2024 and fell in 2025, and quarterly sales declined in both the first and second quarters of 2026. In the second quarter of 2026, however, operating profit rose year on year even as sales fell, so the margin improved in that quarter.

One quarter is not enough to call a trend reversal, and quarterly swings are large, so annual trends should be read alongside it. Compared with other listed companies targeting Chinese consumers, Wing Yip Food's strategy relies on franchise expansion and new products.

Whether the Sino-Korean joint brand raises local brand awareness will show up in franchisee counts and sales trends.

The remaining shares are to be subscribed by WONG SIO CHAN and RUI XING INTERNATIONAL HOLDINGS LIMITED, each taking 3.5 million shares. The proceeds are planned for production facilities and marketing.

Controlling-shareholder net profit fell 7.8% year on year in the second quarter of 2026, so whether the operating profit recovery carries through to net profit is a point to confirm in the next quarterly results.

Profit recovery during shrinking sales depends on cost structure changes. Without a sales recovery, the durability of the profit rebound remains uncertain.

Downward trend in margins and net profit

Controlling-shareholder net profit declined for three consecutive years to KRW 11.2 billion in 2025. In the first quarter of 2026 it was KRW 662 million, down 86.6% from the prior quarter.

In the second quarter it was KRW 2.75 billion, down 7.8% year on year, so the operating recovery did not fully carry through to net profit in that quarter.

Dilution and share structure changes

New shares issued in the rights offering dilute existing holders' stakes. The issue price carries an 11.10% premium to the reference price, while the subscription by parties other than the largest shareholder is limited. Trading was halted for a share consolidation that took effect on May 6, 2026. Changes in share count and per-share units make per-share comparisons across periods harder.

Risk factors

Concentration risk

The business is concentrated in Guangdong Province, so it is highly exposed to local consumer conditions and regulatory changes. Under the franchise model, sales scale depends on how fast franchisees are added. Delays in reaching the franchise target could disrupt the sales recovery plan. Response to new products is also a variable for whether profit recovery lasts.

Funding and dilution risk

The KRW 22.4 billion rights issue is large relative to total equity of KRW 240 billion at the end of 2025. Once the new shares are issued, the share count rises and per-share measures could fall. If the proceeds used for facilities and marketing do not translate into earnings, the financial burden could grow. The use of proceeds can be tracked in future quarterly and annual reports.

Earnings volatility and currency factors

Quarterly operating profit swung from KRW 4.355 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 5.46 billion in the fourth quarter. Such volatility makes quarter-by-quarter reading difficult. Because the company reports in yuan, KRW-translated figures are affected by exchange rate movements. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 4.6 billion in 2025 from KRW 18 billion in 2022.
