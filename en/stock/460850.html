Export Margins Under Pressure: Dongkuk Coated Metal Back in Loss in Q3

02

Business structure

Dongkuk Coated Metal was spun off from Dongkuk Steel Mill's cold-rolling business in June 2023 and produces cold-rolled, galvanized and color-coated steel.

Its core products are color-coated steel and galvanized cold-rolled steel, and press reports put color-coated steel sales at KRW 1,186.0 billion in 2024 and KRW 1,086.9 billion in 2025.

Premium architectural color steel under the Luxteel brand and appliance color steel under the Appsteel brand form its higher-value lines, produced at Busan, Gimcheon and Gumi.

The company has no hot-rolling facilities, so it sourced KRW 936.7 billion of hot-rolled coil in 2025 from domestic integrated mills and overseas suppliers including Japan's JFE. Exports account for roughly 70% of sales, which makes results highly sensitive to exchange rates.

Overseas, it has expanded Latin American and Southeast Asian sales through a Mexico coil center and a Thai subsidiary, and a 2024 report said Europe accounted for about 40% of its construction-material color steel exports.

In 2024 it decided to acquire a 56.6% stake in the fourth-ranked color steel maker for KRW 128.5 billion. In the domestic color-coated steel market it competes with KG Dongbusteel and POSCO Steeleon. Press reports said color-coated steel made up 54.5% of total sales in January to September 2023.

04

Earnings analysis

Dongkuk Coated Metal's reported results show sales growth alongside deteriorating profitability. Sales were KRW 1.27 trillion in 2023 with operating profit of KRW 26.7 billion, then rose to KRW 2.16 trillion in 2024, up 70.9% year on year.

Sales reached KRW 2.77 trillion in 2025, up 28.0%, and rose for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025 after starting in 2023; 2025 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest in the 2023–2025 span.

Operating profit climbed to KRW 77.3 billion in 2024, up 189.7%, but the company then recorded an operating loss of KRW 38.8 billion in 2025, turning to a loss. Operating margin moved from 2.1% in 2023 to 3.6% in 2024 and -1.4% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 63.8 billion in 2024, up 530.7%, before a net loss of KRW 52.9 billion in 2025; total net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 69.9 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 80.7% in 2024 to 159.1% in 2025, when total equity was KRW 1.05 trillion, of which controlling shareholders' equity was KRW 982 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 1.66 trillion.

Operating cash flow was negative KRW 60.2 billion in 2023, then KRW 175.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 137.1 billion in 2025. Quarterly, operating profit of KRW 1.73 billion in Q3 2025 was followed by an operating loss of KRW 25.4 billion in Q4 2025, a swing to loss from the prior quarter.

Q1 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 7.98 billion, and Q2 2026 operating profit reached KRW 22 billion, up 175.9% from the prior quarter. Q3 2026 swung back to an operating loss of KRW 3.92 billion, even though sales of KRW 821.9 billion rose 23.1% year on year.

The company attributed the Q3 pressure to currency swings, rising protectionism, higher raw material costs and cheap Chinese imports in its results announcement on October 30, 2026. Press reports credited the Q2 return to profit to expanded exports of premium products during a period of high exchange rates.

05

Industry analysis

Color-coated steel is a higher-value material used in white goods and architectural exterior and interior products, so demand tracks construction activity and appliance output.

The company cited 2024 domestic demand for construction-use cold-rolled and coated steel at 2.8 million tons a year, of which imports were 1 million tons, about 90% of them from China. In February 2025 the company announced it would pursue anti-dumping complaints against Chinese galvanized and color-coated steel.

Press reports said its color-coated market share was expected to rise from 29.7% to 34.4% after the 2024 acquisition. Commentary in 2024 said demand for color-coated steel had cushioned the company against a downturn in end markets and low-priced Chinese supply.

Because it has no hot-rolling capacity, its margins depend on hot-coil prices and currency moves. Press reports cited an average utilization rate of 76.7% in the first half of 2024, up from 75.2% a year earlier.

Its scale and export mix are cited as differentiators against domestic rivals such as KG Dongbusteel and POSCO Steeleon, yet overseas trade measures affect it directly. Import controls help its domestic sales, while raw material sourcing and export prices remain separate variables.

06

Outlook

In its Q3 2026 results announcement on October 30, 2026, the company said it would partly adjust its export mix and focus on raising the share of higher-value products in Q4. Q4 operating margin will be the first quarter to show whether that adjustment works.

Its medium-term target, DK Color Vision 2030, was announced in 2021 and calls for 1 million tons of annual color-steel output, KRW 2 trillion in color-steel sales, a 65% export share and eight overseas bases.

Press reports from 2024 added a goal of consolidating 1 million tons of color capacity within five years and reaching KRW 3.2 trillion in sales after the acquisition.

Integration work on the acquired company is under way, with a new management team drawn partly from Dongkuk Coated Metal and additional financial support approved, according to press reports. Given sales of KRW 2.77 trillion in 2025 and a Q3 2026 operating loss, reaching these targets depends on margin recovery.

Anti-dumping action against Chinese imports has been announced as a plan, and its outcome and effect on domestic pricing must be confirmed through future disclosures. Utilization at the overseas coil centers in Mexico and Thailand will indicate how stable the export channels are.

Hot-rolled coil purchases of KRW 936.7 billion in 2025 mean that coil prices and exchange rates will continue to affect margins. The next disclosure checkpoints are the Q4 2026 provisional results and the 2026 annual report.

08

Bull factors

Sales growth has continued

Sales have risen for three consecutive quarters from Q1 to Q3 2026, and Q3 sales of KRW 821.9 billion were up 23.1% year on year. Volume recovery has followed the Q4 2025 low. The company links the top-line recovery to higher export volumes and a larger share of premium products.

Premium products and overseas bases

The company is raising the share of premium color steel under the Luxteel and Appsteel brands, and it has widened its overseas network through a Mexico coil center, a Thai subsidiary and a European office in Germany. A 2024 report said Europe accounted for about 40% of construction-material exports. These overseas bases give it channels that can offset currency and trade-policy shocks.

Scale gained through acquisition

The 2024 acquisition of a 56.6% stake in the fourth-ranked color steel maker expanded production scale, and a 2024 report expected the company's domestic share to rise from 29.7% to 34.4%.

Integration work has been under way since, and some press reports describe the company as the world's largest color-steel producer by volume.

09

Bear factors

Return to loss in Q3

Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 3.92 billion marked a swing back to loss from the prior quarter, and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 43.3 billion. Over the last four quarters that total came to KRW 50.7 billion in net losses.

Currency, raw material and import pressure

With about 70% of sales from exports, currency swings feed straight into results. The company cites rising protectionism, higher raw material costs and cheap Chinese imports as burdens. Because it buys all hot-rolled coil externally, it is highly exposed to input cost swings.

Rising financial burden

The debt ratio rose from 80.7% in 2024 to 159.1% in 2025, and total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 1.66 trillion. The 2025 total net loss was KRW 69.9 billion, with KRW 52.9 billion attributable to controlling shareholders. Continued losses could constrain borrowing capacity and dividend room.

10

Risk factors

Currency risk

With roughly 70% of sales from exports, currency moves hit revenue and operating profit directly. The company named exchange-rate swings as a burden in Q3 2026. The gains from high exchange rates seen in Q2 can reverse when the currency moves the other way.

Trade and import risk

Low-priced Chinese cold-rolled and color-coated imports pressure domestic prices. The company announced in February 2025 that it would pursue anti-dumping complaints, and the outcome and subsequent import trends still need to be confirmed. Wider protectionism abroad directly affects its export channels.

Raw material and balance sheet risk

Without hot-rolling capacity, the company bought KRW 936.7 billion of hot-rolled coil in 2025, so input prices drive margins. With the debt ratio at 159.1%, continued losses could increase financial strain. The pace at which integration of the acquired business shows up in earnings is another variable.

Overall view

Dongkuk Coated Metal grew sales from KRW 1.27 trillion in 2023 to KRW 2.77 trillion in 2025, but profitability swung from a profit in 2024 to an operating loss of KRW 38.8 billion in 2025.

In 2026 the company posted operating profits in Q1 and Q2 but returned to an operating loss of KRW 3.92 billion in Q3, while sales of KRW 821.9 billion rose 23.1% year on year.
