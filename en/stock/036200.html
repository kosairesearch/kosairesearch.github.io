02

Business structure

Union Semiconductor Equipment Co.,ltd makes gas scrubbers and chillers used in semiconductor fab facilities and runs a maintenance and after-service business alongside them.

A product sales breakdown compiled by WiseReport lists chillers at 40.03%, maintenance and other services at 30.85%, gas scrubbers at 27.29%, and vacuum line works at 1.80%. The same compilation shows domestic market shares of 40.00% for gas scrubbers and 30.00% for chillers as of March 2026.

Export shares at the end of 2025 were 47.55% for chillers, 36.37% for gas scrubbers, and 22.78% for maintenance and other services. Customers are centered on semiconductor makers such as Samsung Electronics, and the company says customer capex expansion translates into higher equipment supply.

Because chillers and scrubbers are ordered in line with fab investment schedules, the timing of customer capex flows directly into quarterly results. The maintenance business provides a cushion when equipment orders slow. R&D spending was 4.22% of revenue in 2025, down from 4.83% in 2024.

Peers sharing the same investment cycle include Jusung Engineering and Wonik IPS, while the chiller and scrubber-focused product mix is the company's defining feature.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 253.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 232.1 billion in 2023 (down 8.3% year on year) and KRW 218.2 billion in 2024 (down 6.0%), then rose to KRW 273.3 billion in 2025 (up 25.3%). Within 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year for revenue and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit dropped from KRW 29 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.4 billion in 2023 (down 40.1%) and KRW 9.97 billion in 2024 (down 42.6%), before edging up to KRW 10.9 billion in 2025 (up 9.3%).

The operating margin declined from 11.5% in 2022 to 7.5%, 4.6% and 4.0% in the following years, and in 2025 revenue grew 25.3% while operating profit grew only 9.3%, so top-line recovery did not translate directly into margin recovery.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 19.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 18.2 billion in 2023 (down 6.9%), KRW 15.5 billion in 2024 (down 14.9%) and KRW 8.98 billion in 2025 (down 42.1%), declining for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 8.96 billion in 2025, reported separately from the figure attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow ran at KRW 16.9 billion in 2022, -KRW 10.3 billion in 2023, KRW 36.2 billion in 2024 and -KRW 6.49 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit moved from KRW 1.75 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 2.27 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 5.12 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 5.35 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 12.8 billion in Q3 2026, rising for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026.

Revenue of KRW 74.8 billion in Q1 2026 (down 1.5% quarter on quarter) was the low point, and revenue then rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 90.8 billion in Q3 2026 (up 13.3% quarter on quarter).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 5.73 billion in Q3 2026 rose 73.9% year on year, and the company reported a Q3 operating margin of 14.09% versus 3.07% a year earlier.

The company credited customer capex expansion, which raised equipment supply, together with better profitability for the quarterly improvement.

Industry analysis

The semiconductor equipment cycle moves with the capex budgets of memory and foundry customers. The company attributed its Q3 2026 improvement to expanded investment at Samsung Electronics' Pyeongtaek P4 DRAM line and the Taylor foundry plant in the United States.

A November 2026 news report said Samsung Electronics' DS division posted record results, which it linked to a recovery in HBM competitiveness, rising AI infrastructure investment and higher memory prices amid tight supply. The company expects memory customers to expand capex in 2027 beyond the 2026 level.

It says equipment introduction into new fabs will drive revenue growth and profitability gains. The key variable for the cycle is whether the 2027 memory investment increase converts into actual equipment orders, since order timing shifts when customer investment plans change.

Domestic sales made up 52.45% of chiller revenue and 63.63% of gas scrubber revenue at the end of 2025, which makes the company sensitive to domestic chip investment schedules.

WiseReport puts its domestic market share at 40.00% for gas scrubbers and 30.00% for chillers as of March 2026, so its scrubber position is relatively stronger.

Compared with listed equipment peers such as Jusung Engineering, Wonik IPS and PSK, the company pairs chiller and scrubber supply with a maintenance revenue base.

Risk factors

Dependence on customer investment cycle

Revenue depends on the capex schedules of major customers such as Samsung Electronics. If investment is delayed or scaled back, equipment shipment volumes could fall. The company itself cites this investment as the driver of its recent improvement, which underlines the level of dependence.

Margin volatility

The operating margin has fallen from 11.5% in 2022 to 4.0% in 2025. Changes in cost structure and product mix could push margins down again. Whether the 14.09% Q3 2026 margin persists will become clear only through subsequent quarterly results.

Domestic concentration and overseas expansion

At the end of 2025, domestic sales exceeded half of both chiller and gas scrubber revenue. Expanding the overseas customer base remains a medium- to long-term task. A shift in domestic chip investment direction could affect the revenue mix directly.
