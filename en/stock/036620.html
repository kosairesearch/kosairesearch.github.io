Apparel growth continues as quarterly profit eases from its peak

02

Business structure

Gamsung is a lifestyle outdoor company whose results are driven by its apparel business, centered on the Snow Peak Apparel brand. Apparel accounted for about 95.8% of total revenue in the third quarter of 2026, which shows how concentrated the business is in one brand.

In the first quarter of 2026 the apparel segment posted revenue of KRW 56.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 9.4 billion, up 27.0% and 36.9% year on year, and its operating margin rose 1.2 percentage points to 16.5%. Apparel revenue reached KRW 56.7 billion in the second quarter of 2026.

In the third quarter the segment recorded revenue of KRW 52.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 7.2 billion, up 20.5% and 21.7% year on year. Distribution combines domestic online and offline channels with direct exports to China.

In China the company began with two Guangzhou stores in early 2026 and opened large stores in Beijing and Shanghai in the second half, and it says the network has grown to four stores. It also expands direct-to-consumer sales and runs ESG management built on eco-friendly materials.

A May 2026 report set July 1, 2026 as the split date for its non-core mobile business, which would let management focus on outdoor apparel.

Among domestic outdoor brands, most saw sales decline or stall in the second quarter of 2026, while the company highlights its in-house fabric development and waterproof, windproof and breathable products as differentiators.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue rose from KRW 117.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 177.9 billion in 2023 (up 51.6%), KRW 220.4 billion in 2024 (up 23.9%) and KRW 250.2 billion in 2025 (up 13.5%), growing three years in a row from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 16.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 32.2 billion in 2023 (up 98.5%), KRW 36.1 billion in 2024 (up 11.8%) and KRW 44.6 billion in 2025 (up 23.8%), also rising three years in a row from 2023.

Controlling-shareholder net income climbed from KRW 15.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 24.2 billion, KRW 29.3 billion and KRW 35.4 billion over 2023 to 2025, and net income including non-controlling interests was the same amount in each year.

The operating margin moved from 13.8% in 2022 to 18.1% in 2023, fell to 16.4% in 2024 and recovered to 17.8% in 2025. In 2024 margin compressed because revenue grew 23.9% while operating profit grew only 11.8%.

The debt ratio fell from 64.9% in 2022 to 38.0% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 134 billion against total liabilities of KRW 50.9 billion.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 11.44 million in 2022, KRW 26.7 billion in 2023, KRW 5.82 billion in 2024 and KRW 39 billion in 2025, declining in 2024 before rising again in 2025.

The fourth quarter of 2025 was the quarterly peak, with revenue of KRW 104 billion (up 126.5% from the prior quarter), operating profit of KRW 22.8 billion (up 268.4%) and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 18.2 billion (up 288.5%).

The company describes the fourth quarter as the seasonal peak, so profit is concentrated in the outerwear selling season.

Revenue was KRW 58.5 billion with operating profit of KRW 9.47 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 58.5 billion and KRW 9.1 billion in the second quarter, and KRW 54.6 billion and KRW 7.38 billion in the third quarter, so operating profit declined for three consecutive quarters from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026.

Revenue rose 0.1% in the second quarter and then fell 6.7% in the third quarter. Controlling-shareholder net income also fell for three consecutive quarters, to KRW 8.5 billion, KRW 7.23 billion and KRW 4.43 billion, and the last four quarters total KRW 38.3 billion.

In the third quarter of 2026 revenue and operating profit were each up 19.0% year on year, while controlling-shareholder net income was down 5.3%, so profit at the operating and net levels moved in different directions.

At the apparel-segment level, the operating margin in the first quarter of 2026 was 16.5%, up 1.2 percentage points from 15.3% a year earlier.

05

Industry analysis

Domestic fashion demand faces concerns about slowing consumption, yet Gamsung's apparel segment still grew third-quarter 2026 revenue 20.5% year on year.

Outdoor demand is reported to have risen since the end of COVID-19 restrictions, and the company points to in-house fabric development and waterproof, windproof and breathable functions as its differentiators.

In the second quarter of 2026, however, most major domestic outdoor brands were reported to have seen sales decline or stall. Against that backdrop, the segment's KRW 56.7 billion of second-quarter revenue drew press attention as a contrasting result.

Where domestic demand has stalled, China is emerging as the axis of growth. In China the company is building offline footprints centered on first-tier cities and positioning its products as premium brands.

As direct exports rise, exposure to Chinese consumer and distribution conditions becomes a larger variable for the cycle. Seasonality is also a feature of this industry, and profit concentrated in the fourth-quarter outerwear season has been reflected in the quarterly pattern.

Its position within domestic competition will be gauged by whether quarterly growth rates hold after the second quarter of 2026.

06

Outlook

For the fourth-quarter season the company plans to lead with new heavy down outerwear collections, building on steady demand for lightweight padding. It intends to pair a revamped official online store with co-marketing on major fashion platforms to link its online and offline channels.

Management has said it expects full-year 2026 results to set a new record. In China the company has signaled that store openings will accelerate in the second half. On shareholder returns, it plans to keep expanding dividends and buying back and cancelling shares under its three-year value-up plan.

Separately, consensus estimates compiled by fnguide as of June 2026 put full-year 2026 operating profit on a standalone basis at KRW 58.2 billion and third-quarter 2026 operating profit at KRW 11.3 billion. Disclosed third-quarter operating profit was KRW 7.38 billion, below that estimate.

Reaching the full-year estimate would require fourth-quarter operating profit well above the KRW 22.8 billion posted in the fourth quarter of 2025, given that the first three quarters of 2026 already total roughly KRW 26 billion. Full-year 2026 results will be fixed through the 2026 annual report, due by March 31, 2027.

08

Bull factors

Double-digit apparel growth

Apparel revenue in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 52.3 billion, up 20.5% year on year, and segment operating profit was KRW 7.2 billion, up 21.7%. In the first quarter the apparel operating margin rose 1.2 percentage points to 16.5%.

The company said cumulative January–September 2026 revenue of KRW 171.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 25.9 billion were the highest for a first-three-quarter period.

China store expansion and direct exports

The China business began with two Guangzhou stores and has expanded to large stores in Beijing and Shanghai, with openings set to accelerate in the second half of 2026. Growing direct-export volume could reduce reliance on the domestic market. The company said its premium positioning in China has won strong local consumer response.

Expanding shareholder returns

Under its three-year value-up plan the company has pledged to return at least 50% of prior-year net income to shareholders. In 2026 it carried out a KRW 18 billion cash dividend and a KRW 4.5 billion share buyback. The debt ratio was 38.0% at the end of 2025, a relatively low level of financial burden.

Bear factors

Consecutive quarterly profit declines

Operating profit fell from KRW 22.8 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 9.47 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 9.1 billion in the second and KRW 7.38 billion in the third, a three-quarter decline.

Controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 4.43 billion in the third quarter was down 38.7% from the prior quarter and 5.3% from a year earlier. Revenue and operating profit rose year on year, but the diverging trends across profit lines form the bearish case.

Single-brand and apparel concentration

The apparel segment makes up about 95.8% of third-quarter 2026 revenue, so sales of Snow Peak Apparel effectively determine results. A shortfall in fourth-quarter selling season would weigh on the full-year outcome. Strong seasonality, with profit concentrated in the fourth quarter, also amplifies quarterly swings.

Domestic cycle and missed estimates

Press reports said most major domestic outdoor brands saw sales decline or stall in the second quarter of 2026. The fnguide consensus compiled as of June 2026 put third-quarter operating profit near KRW 11.3 billion, but the disclosed figure was KRW 7.38 billion. Broader concerns about slowing fashion consumption remain a demand-side burden.

10

Risk factors

Single-brand dependence

Most earnings come from Snow Peak Apparel, so weaker brand appeal or product response could hit revenue and profit together. Apparel's share of about 95.8% of revenue shows how large this exposure is. Sell-through of new collections and inventory management feed directly into quarterly results.

China expansion execution risk

Chinese stores have spread from Guangzhou to Beijing and Shanghai and openings are accelerating, so store performance will drive overseas revenue. If the cost of large-format stores or shifts in local demand exceed expectations, the pace of direct-export growth could slow. Exchange rates and logistics conditions also shape the export structure.

Seasonality and consumption slowdown

Profit concentrates in the fourth-quarter outerwear season, so shifts in winter temperatures or selling timing can move full-year results sharply. Margin maintenance is a challenge while concerns about slowing fashion consumption persist.

Whether the operating profit decline seen from the first to the third quarter of 2026 reverses in the fourth quarter is the central variable.

Overall view

Gamsung's results are led by its apparel business centered on Snow Peak Apparel, and revenue, operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income each rose three years in a row from 2023 through 2025.

The operating margin was 17.8% in 2025, up from 13.8% in 2022, and the debt ratio fell from 64.9% in 2022 to 38.0% in 2025.

The fourth quarter of 2025 marked the peak at KRW 104 billion in revenue and KRW 22.8 billion in operating profit, and operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income then declined for three consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026.

In the third quarter of 2026 revenue and operating profit each rose 19.0% year on year, while controlling-shareholder net income fell 5.3%, so the profit lines moved in different directions.

The bullish factors cited are double-digit apparel growth, expanding China stores and direct exports, and higher shareholder returns under the three-year plan.

The bearish factors cited are consecutive quarterly profit declines, the concentration of about 95.8% of revenue in one brand, a stalled domestic outdoor cycle and missed estimates. With both trends visible at once, the key question is whether fourth-quarter seasonal results change the quarterly pattern.
