Report overview

AI pivot: revenue rebounds, losses persist

Uracle swung to an annual operating loss in 2025. Quarterly revenue has risen for three straight quarters from the first quarter of 2026, but operating losses have continued for five consecutive quarters since the third quarter of 2025.

  1. 1

    Revenue fell 8.0% year on year to KRW 43.8 billion in 2025, and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 6.77 billion after a profit in the prior year.

  2. 2

    Third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 14.4 billion, up 43.9% from the prior quarter, while the operating loss narrowed to KRW 482 million.

  3. 3

    Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 5.89 billion over the last four quarters, and the full-year 2025 figure was a loss of KRW 6.83 billion.

  4. 4

    AI platform revenue was reported at KRW 800 million in the first quarter of 2026, and whether it grows enough to offset rising costs is the central question.

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Uracle was founded in 2001 and develops and supplies software, mainly mobile app development platforms. It launched the Mopius mobile platform in 2011 and has since built its mobile technology base, and it is now pushing an AI-driven business transition centered on its Athena AI platform.

The business center of gravity is moving from mobile platforms toward AI platforms. AI platform revenue was reported at KRW 800 million in the first quarter of 2026. Its customers were reported at listing as major corporate groups including Hyundai Motor, LG and SK.

The AI business is anchored on a partnership with LG AI Research: a April 2025 report said Uracle agreed to combine EXAONE with Athena and supply it to enterprises.

An analyst at Kiwoom Securities in the same period described a model in which build costs are booked as revenue and maintenance revenue follows each year at 10 to 15 percent of license revenue.

The company said it was running proof-of-concept projects with ten organizations, including government agencies, financial firms and general enterprises.

Peers in the same IT and software sector include Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, all of which are larger than Uracle, whose 2025 revenue was KRW 43.8 billion. The company targets KRW 5 trillion in revenue by 2030.

04

Earnings analysis

Uracle's consolidated revenue rose from KRW 45.7 billion in 2023 to KRW 47.6 billion in 2024, up 4.0% year on year, and then fell to KRW 43.8 billion in 2025, down 8.0%. Of 2023 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 3.1 billion in 2023 to KRW 1.97 billion in 2024, down 36.6%, and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 6.77 billion in 2025, turning from profit to loss.

For the 2024 decline, the company attributed the increase in cost of sales and selling and administrative expenses to investment in AI solution R&D, hiring of business staff and purchases of research software assets. The operating margin moved from 6.8% in 2023 to 4.1% in 2024 and to -15.5% in 2025.

The 2025 annual operating loss was reported as the first annual loss since 2012. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.5 billion in 2023 and KRW 2.2 billion in 2024, down 51.2%, and turned into a net loss of KRW 6.83 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was also a net loss of KRW 6.83 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 59.5% in 2023 and 44.4% in 2024 to 89.7% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 17.4 billion and total equity of KRW 19.3 billion at the end of 2025.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 3.91 billion in 2023 and KRW 3.19 billion in 2024 to -KRW 3.55 billion in 2025. By quarter, the fourth quarter of 2025 was the weakest, with revenue of KRW 7.81 billion, down 38.4% from the prior quarter, and an operating loss of KRW 3.31 billion.

Revenue then rose for three straight quarters from the first quarter of 2026 to the third quarter, reaching KRW 8.68 billion, KRW 9.98 billion and KRW 14.4 billion.

The operating loss narrowed to KRW 799 million in the first quarter and KRW 482 million in the third quarter, but widened to KRW 1.13 billion in the second quarter. Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 5.89 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Uracle's industry backdrop is a transition from mobile-platform software toward AI-platform software. The company says it is pushing AI-driven innovation centered on its high-performance Athena AI platform.

Because the core of the strategy is combining with large language models, the partnership with an AI model developer is seen as a differentiating factor, but it also means the scope of the business depends partly on the LG AI ecosystem.

A Kiwoom Securities analyst said in April 2025 that Athena-based projects could range from a few hundred million won to several billion won in size, which can make project-based revenue uneven from quarter to quarter.

The same analyst said performance could be expected from the second half if proof-of-concept projects turned into full orders, but that was an April 2025 view and later order flow needs to be checked. Among peers, Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever are larger in scale and customer base than Uracle.

Uracle emphasizes the combination of its mobile platform experience with AI, but at KRW 43.8 billion in 2025 revenue, a single project carries a large weight in its results.

06

Outlook

Uracle has not published a formal full-year 2026 guidance that this report can rely on, so the reference point is the brokerage outlook. A June 2026 report said Kiwoom Securities expected 2026 revenue of KRW 46.6 billion and an operating loss of KRW 3.5 billion.

The same report put the quarterly breakeven point in the fourth quarter of 2026. The same analyst, however, said that rising AI revenue would struggle to fully offset the added costs in the near term.

Since the operating loss widened in the second quarter of 2026 compared with the first, whether quarterly profitability improves as expected is the key checkpoint. Reaching the 2030 target of KRW 5 trillion in revenue would require AI revenue to grow well beyond the KRW 43.8 billion base of 2025.

Whether build revenue and recurring maintenance revenue accumulate over time is the medium-term question. Whether the KRW 14.4 billion of third-quarter 2026 revenue reflects one-off builds or recurring revenue can be checked against the disclosures. The 2026 annual report will show operating margin and cash flow as formal figures.

08

Bull factors

AI platform and corporate partnerships

Uracle partnered with LG AI Research to combine the EXAONE model with Athena and supply it to enterprises. It said in April 2025 it had POC projects running with ten organizations, and AI platform revenue was KRW 800 million in the first quarter of 2026.

Analysts describe a structure in which maintenance revenue follows build projects, which is the bull case for AI revenue accumulating over time.

Three straight quarters of revenue growth

From a low of KRW 7.81 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, quarterly revenue rose to KRW 8.68 billion, KRW 9.98 billion and KRW 14.4 billion across the first three quarters of 2026.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was up 13.2% from the prior-year quarter, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 482 million from KRW 3.31 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025. The recovery in revenue and narrower losses moving in the same direction is cited as a bull factor.

Founder share purchases

Founder Cho Jun-hee was reported to have started buying company shares from March 2026. The purchases did not stop the share price from falling, but they may be read as a signal of management's confidence.

The market is focused on whether AI revenue expands and profitability improves, rather than on the purchases themselves.

Bear factors

Consecutive losses and weaker cash flow

The 2025 annual loss was reported as the first annual loss since 2012. Operating losses have continued for five consecutive quarters since the third quarter of 2025, and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 5.89 billion.

Operating cash flow also turned to -KRW 3.55 billion in 2025. Bears argue that cash generation remains weak until earnings recover.

Risk of a delayed breakeven

Kiwoom Securities projected an operating loss of KRW 3.5 billion for 2026. The same analyst said rising AI revenue would not fully offset the added costs in the near term. The operating loss widened to KRW 1.13 billion in the second quarter of 2026 from the first quarter, which supports the bear case.

Higher leverage and weak share price

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 44.4% in 2024 to 89.7% in 2025. A June 2026 report said the share price had fallen to its lowest level since listing, and market capitalization dropped below KRW 30 billion. Bears argue that rising debt against a thinner equity base could weaken the capacity to raise funds.

Risk factors

Treasury share overhang

As of a February 2026 report, the largest shareholder and related parties held 31.17%. If treasury shares are disposed of on the market, concerns about potential sell-side supply and dilution of control have been raised.

Reporting also said the handling of treasury shares became an issue under discussions on amending the Commercial Act.

AI investment costs and monetization timing

A June 2026 report said the analyst expected improvement from the second quarter but also that rising AI revenue would not fully offset higher costs in the near term. The actual second-quarter 2026 operating loss of KRW 1.13 billion was wider than in the first quarter. If investment outlays keep running ahead of revenue, the timing of the profit recovery could be pushed back.

Dependence on corporate group clients

At the time of listing, reporting said its key customers included major corporate groups such as Hyundai Motor, LG and SK. If the customer base is centered on group affiliates, quarterly revenue could swing with project timing within those groups. The rise in the debt-to-equity ratio to 89.7% could also add financial pressure.

Overall view

Uracle, founded in 2001 as a mobile and AI software company, swung to an operating loss in 2025 with revenue of KRW 43.8 billion and an operating loss of KRW 6.77 billion.

Quarterly revenue has risen for three straight quarters through 2026, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 799 million in the first quarter and KRW 482 million in the third quarter.

However, the second-quarter loss widened to KRW 1.13 billion, and operating losses have continued for five consecutive quarters since the third quarter of 2025, so the earnings recovery is not complete.

Bull factors include the Athena supply structure built on the LG AI Research partnership and the founder's share purchases. Bear factors include the swing to an annual loss in 2025, the rise in the debt-to-equity ratio to 89.7%, and overhang concerns from treasury share disposals.

Brokerage outlooks project a full-year 2026 operating loss and place the quarterly breakeven in the fourth quarter of 2026. On valuation, no PER can be calculated from the last four quarters of net losses, and the price-to-book ratio sits near net asset value.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 operating result, the 2026 annual report, and the AI revenue and treasury share treatment in the first-quarter 2027 report.
