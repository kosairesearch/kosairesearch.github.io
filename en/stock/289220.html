02

Business structure

Giantstep is a company producing visual effects (VFX) and real-time 3D content. According to a Dailyinvest report, its revenue mix as of Q1 2025 was 81.64% content production, 14.97% video planning and production, 0.77% broadcasting service revenue and 2.62% broadcasting equipment sales.

Its core business is advertising VFX and video VFX, where results move with clients' marketing budgets. In May 2026 press reports, the company said it had narrowed its portfolio to four strategic businesses: AI content, hardware infrastructure, mobility solutions and space solutions.

The hardware infrastructure unit supplies equipment to AI data centers through a subsidiary, which press reports said generates cash flow.

In the mobility unit, a real-time engine-based HMI graphics solution reportedly passed mass-production standards at a global automaker and was slated for models launched from May 2026.

According to a June 2026 company IR release, real-time 3D content was applied to Hyundai Motor Group's Pleos Connect infotainment system. In space solutions, a immersive exhibition based on IP from the 3D animated film The King of Kings is operating, and an April 2026 IR release said it was extended through August.

In advertising VFX the company competes with agencies and video producers, and advertising agencies such as Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide are useful industry reference points because their activity tracks ad spending.

Because the model is project-based, the timing of revenue recognition for individual projects can move quarterly results considerably.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on filed results, annual revenue was KRW 40.6 billion in 2022, KRW 42.3 billion in 2023 (up 4.2% year on year), and KRW 50.1 billion in 2024 (up 18.4%) before falling to KRW 36.7 billion in 2025 (down 26.7%). The largest year in 2022 through 2025 was 2024, and the smallest was 2025.

The operating loss was KRW 17.9 billion in 2022, KRW 26.1 billion in 2023, KRW 17.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 19 billion in 2025, and the company recorded losses in all four years, with operating margins of -44.0%, -61.6%, -34.5% and -51.7%.

In 2024 the loss narrowed as revenue rose, and in 2025 the loss widened as revenue fell. Press coverage tied the 2024 revenue increase to a 23.8% rise in content production sales.

A first-half 2025 report cited major advertisers cutting or delaying marketing budgets amid economic uncertainty, and the company booked impairments and project losses as one-off charges in that period.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 12.7 billion in 2022, KRW 24.6 billion in 2023, KRW 27.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 17 billion in 2025.

The net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 12.7 billion, KRW 25.2 billion, KRW 28.3 billion and KRW 17.6 billion for the same years, a different measure that should be read separately.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 53.6 billion, of which KRW 52.1 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 16.4 billion. The debt ratio rose from 19.0% in 2022 to 30.6% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was negative in all four years, at KRW -7.47 billion, KRW -13.3 billion, KRW -8.18 billion and KRW -12.4 billion. Quarterly, revenue was KRW 8.99 billion in Q3 2025 with an operating loss of KRW 4.86 billion, then KRW 9.88 billion and an operating loss of KRW 1.49 billion in Q4 2025.

In Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 9.3 billion with an operating loss of KRW 5.55 billion, and in Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 6.55 billion with an operating loss of KRW 2.84 billion. In Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 10.3 billion and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 182 million, though it was still a loss.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 706 million. Press reports put the operating loss ratio at 59.7% in Q1 2026, 43.3% in Q2 and 1.8% in Q3. The company said it had reflected R&D asset impairments, investment valuation losses and one-off severance in its 2025 and Q1 2026 results.

It announced on October 1, 2026 that it posted a monthly operating profit for August 2026, while noting that monthly results vary with the timing of revenue recognition on projects.

Press reports said the subsidiary Bigin Square recorded an operating profit of KRW 890 million on a standalone basis for January to September 2026, which is not a consolidated figure.

05

Industry analysis

Advertising VFX and video VFX demand depends on both advertising cycles and investment in broadcast and film production.

The company's Q1 2026 earnings commentary said revenue from its core advertising VFX, video VFX and real-time content businesses declined, citing the cyclical nature of advertising and lower ad spending.

A first-half 2025 report also described major advertisers cutting or delaying marketing budgets amid economic uncertainty.

By contrast, film and drama VFX is a cost-heavy area, and a November 2026 press report said the company was adjusting its order volume there while pursuing process efficiency aimed at profitability.

The company has said it will avoid low-priced outsourcing and focus on premium AI video production that combines its in-house VFX capabilities. Press reports also described introducing generative AI tools into production pipelines to cut working time.

Demand for real-time 3D and extended reality (XR) is broadening into space content such as exhibitions and performances, as well as automotive HMI.

In the cycle, advertising VFX is in a phase where costs have been reduced while the company waits for demand to recover, and the company said it expected a second-half recovery on the assumption that the advertising market normalizes.

For peer comparison, the advertising agencies Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide are the reference points for the advertising cycle, although their scale and revenue structure differ greatly from Giantstep.

Because Giantstep's revenue base is small and the model is project-based, the timing of revenue recognition on individual projects feeds directly into quarterly results.

Outlook

In a May 2026 press report, the company said it expected a return to profit from Q3 2026, once one-off costs were removed and a controlled low-cost structure was in place. Filed results for Q3 2026 show the operating loss narrowed to KRW 182 million, but the company has not yet returned to operating profit.

In an October 1, 2026 announcement, the company said it recorded a monthly operating profit for August 2026 and would disclose its next large project lineup once confirmed.

In new businesses, the exhibition based on The King of Kings IP was extended through August 2026, and an April 2026 IR release showcased content produced for the MSG Sphere in Las Vegas. A June 2026 IR release also described content work for a BTS-related MSG Sphere video.

In July 2026 the company held its own AI conference and said it was applying its in-house AI creative production OS, TabTo, to its production pipeline.

In space solutions, following an immersive exhibition at Lotte Mall Gimpo Airport, a subsidiary's Seongsuyul venue was reported in May 2026 as planned for an XR performance proof of concept within the year.

The company also said it would reveal major undisclosed projects and resume briefings and face-to-face meetings with institutional investors. On shareholder returns, it said it would link distributable profits, if any arise, to buybacks and cancellation of treasury shares.

At an extraordinary shareholders' meeting on October 20, 2026, amendments to the articles of incorporation and the election of an outside director and an auditor were all approved.

The next checkpoints are whether operating results move into profit and whether revenue from new exhibition and XR businesses shows up in quarterly results.

Bull factors

Clear narrowing of losses

The Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 182 million compares with KRW 4.86 billion in Q3 2025, while revenue rose 14.6% year on year to KRW 10.3 billion. Press reports put the operating loss ratio at 59.7% in Q1 2026 and 1.8% in Q3, with revenue recovery and cost reduction both contributing. The company also disclosed a monthly operating profit for August 2026.

Restructured fixed-cost base

According to press reports, headcount was cut from 360 to 190, a reduction of about 47%, which lowered personnel and operating overhead costs. The company said that with one-off costs cleared from Q3 2026, its controlled low-cost structure should support a return to profit. How much of this cost base reduction shows up in future filings is the point to watch.

IP and new-business expansion

The King of Kings IP exhibition was extended through August 2026, and a June 2026 company IR release said real-time 3D content was applied to Hyundai Motor Group's Pleos Connect. Press reports say the hardware infrastructure unit supplies equipment to AI data centers through a subsidiary and generates cash flow. Filed quarterly results do not yet break out how much revenue these new businesses contribute.

Losses at both the operating and controlling-shareholder net level have been recorded for five consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026.

Demand tied to advertising cycle

In its Q1 2026 earnings commentary, the company said revenue from its core advertising VFX, video VFX and real-time content businesses declined. Q1 2026 revenue fell 7.6% year on year, while the operating loss rose 30.9% and the net loss rose 33.9%. If the advertising market does not recover, core demand could remain constrained.

Share price volatility and concentrated trading

Overall view

Giantstep is a company whose core businesses are visual effects and real-time content. Operating losses were recorded every year from 2022 through 2025, and the Q3 2026 operating loss narrowed sharply to KRW 182 million.

Q3 2026 revenue rose 14.6% year on year to KRW 10.3 billion, so revenue recovery and loss reduction occurred together.

On the other side, losses have run for five consecutive quarters, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 9.56 billion over the latest four quarters, and operating cash flow was negative from 2022 through 2025.

Strengths include lower fixed costs after the headcount reduction and progress in new businesses such as the The King of Kings IP exhibition and mobility applications.

Weaknesses include demand tied to the advertising cycle, volatility from project revenue timing, and the concentrated-trading designation in August 2026.

The August 2026 monthly operating profit and the narrower Q3 loss are positive signals, but the target of a Q3 return to profit was not reached at the operating level. The next checkpoints are the provisional Q4 2026 results and the full-year operating profit and operating cash flow in the 2026 annual report.
