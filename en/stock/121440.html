Report overview

Profit slowdown and holding-structure shake-up overlap

Golfzon Holdings faces a double squeeze: earnings at its key investee Golfzon are weakening while a planned sale of Golfzon County would remove a large share of its operating profit.

  1. 1

    On a disclosed basis, 2025 revenue was KRW 354.3 billion (down 8.3% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 28 billion (down 29.0%). Both revenue and operating profit have declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

  2. 2

    The operating margin fell from 19.3% in 2022 to 12.4% in 2023, 10.2% in 2024 and 7.9% in 2025.

  3. 3

  4. 4

    Press reports said Golfzon County-related income accounted for KRW 7.9 billion, or 46%, of first-half 2026 operating profit of KRW 17 billion. A proposed sale of 100% of Golfzon County could leave a gap in the company's earnings structure.

02

Business structure

Golfzon Holdings was founded in 2000, listed on KOSDAQ in 2011 and converted into a holding company structure in 2015. Its businesses are holding operations, golf course operation and leasing, golf equipment distribution and screen golf.

By segment share of 2Q 2026 revenue, distribution accounted for 84%, holding and other operations for 14% and golf course operation for 2%. The distribution core is Golfzon Commerce, which accounted for about 85.3% of 2024 consolidated revenue of KRW 386.2 billion.

Golfzon Commerce recorded 2024 revenue of KRW 329.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 2.4 billion and net profit of KRW 0.7 billion.

The golf equipment business struggled with slow economic growth, high prices and price competition among rivals, and improved profitability in 2025 by securing price competitiveness through overseas brand distribution rights and private-label products.

Golfzon County runs the golf course business, and Golfzon Holdings leases four golf courses to it. Golfzon County is a joint venture rather than a consolidated subsidiary, so its revenue does not enter consolidated sales directly.

Screen golf operations sit with Golfzon (215000), which is not a consolidated subsidiary, so its sales are excluded from Golfzon Holdings' consolidated revenue.

Press reports put first-half 2026 Golfzon County-related income at KRW 1.8 billion in royalties, KRW 3.4 billion in equity-method income and KRW 2.7 billion in leasing operating profit, or KRW 7.9 billion in total.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue went from KRW 488.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 427.1 billion in 2023 (down 12.6%), KRW 386.2 billion in 2024 (down 9.6%) and KRW 354.3 billion in 2025 (down 8.3%), declining for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 94.4 billion in 2022, KRW 53.1 billion in 2023 (down 43.8%), KRW 39.4 billion in 2024 (down 25.7%) and KRW 28 billion in 2025 (down 29.0%), also falling for three consecutive years.

The operating margin dropped from 19.3% in 2022 to 7.9% in 2025, and because operating profit fell faster than revenue, the margin compression reflects both lower sales and weaker profitability.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 86.9 billion in 2022, KRW 37.6 billion in 2023 (down 56.7%), KRW 43.6 billion in 2024 (up 15.8%) and KRW 24 billion in 2025 (down 44.9%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 24.1 billion in 2025, reported separately from profit attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow was KRW 18 billion in 2022, KRW 14.5 billion in 2023, KRW 25.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 10 billion in 2025, the lowest of the four years.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 750.1 billion, of which equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 744.8 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 309.5 billion. The debt ratio fell from 47.2% in 2022 to 41.3% in 2025, though it edged up from 40.7% in 2024.

On a quarterly basis, 3Q 2025 showed revenue of KRW 100.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 9.73 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.03 billion.

In 4Q 2025, revenue was KRW 78.7 billion (down 21.8% from the prior quarter), operating profit was KRW 616 million (down 93.7%), and the company posted a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.16 billion, turning to a loss.

In 1Q 2026, revenue was KRW 69.5 billion, operating profit was KRW 2.61 billion (up 323.7% from the prior quarter) and profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.63 billion, turning to a profit.

In 2Q 2026, revenue was KRW 105.8 billion (up 52.2% from the prior quarter), operating profit was KRW 14.4 billion (up 450.8%) and profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 9.15 billion.

In 3Q 2026, revenue was KRW 91.6 billion (down 13.4% from the prior quarter and down 9.0% year on year), operating profit was KRW 6.68 billion (down 53.6% from the prior quarter and down 31.4% year on year) and profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 6.75 billion (down 16.0% year on year).

Revenue and operating profit both rose in 2Q 2026 but both fell in 3Q 2026. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the most recent four quarters (4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026) totaled KRW 14.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The Korean screen golf market is viewed by industry participants as approaching saturation. One press report said Korean screen golf stores numbered 9,880 at the end of 2024, close to 10,000.

Golfzon (215000) said its 1Q 2026 screen golf sales fell as new store growth slowed and demand for replacement of new products was exhausted. The same company said new golfer inflows slowed amid rising competition and diversified leisure demand, which reduced its GDR business.

Golfzon (215000) reported 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 126.0 billion (up 4.3% year on year) but operating profit of KRW 10.0 billion (down 34.4%). Its filing attributed the profit decline to higher cost of sales from domestic product sales promotion and higher sales and marketing costs in the United States and China.

Over the same period, its overseas store count rose 20.3%, from 3,857 to 4,641. In a report dated November 2, 2026, Yoojin Securities analyst Park Jong-seon projected Golfzon (215000) 2026 revenue of KRW 461.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 41.6 billion, down 4.5% and 38.8% respectively.

Golf equipment distribution is highly seasonal and linked to field golf demand, and price competition among rivals weighs on profitability. Because Golfzon Holdings holds its screen golf exposure indirectly through an investment, the earnings trend of Golfzon feeds directly into holding-company profit.

06

Outlook

The largest variable is the sale of Golfzon County. MBK Partners is pushing to sell 100% of Golfzon County, and press reports said Golfzon Holdings opposes the sale and is considering legal action.

Golfzon County-related income was KRW 7.9 billion in first-half 2026, equal to 46% of operating profit, so a completed sale would materially change the holding company's operating profit structure.

Press reports said Golfzon's first-half operating profit fell 55.6% year on year, which could weaken the investment income base of the holding company. Overseas operations are seen as the main path to recovery at Golfzon.

Its overseas store count reached 4,641 in 3Q 2026, and Yoojin Securities expects overseas sales of new products and store operations to rise with the fourth-quarter peak season. Domestic operations still face cost-of-sales pressure and higher operating costs.

On governance, the largest shareholder side secured more than 85% of shares and, according to reports, decided not to pursue a delisting for the time being, so the minority shareholder structure may persist for now.

The next scheduled confirmation point is the 2026 annual report, which will show whether the operating margin moves from 7.9% in 2025 and what accounting effect the Golfzon County sale has. Trends for 4Q 2026 and 1Q 2027 will be visible indirectly through Golfzon's own filings.

Valuation reads differently on earnings and on assets. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters totals KRW 14.4 billion, less than the KRW 24 billion recorded for 2025 as a full year.

Because earnings have shrunk, earnings-based multiples are mechanically higher than they would be on a stronger profit base. By contrast, the price-to-book ratio sits well below 1x, meaning the share price is low relative to net assets.

A large part of net assets, however, depends on the valuation of investments such as Golfzon, so the asset-based reading moves with the value of those holdings. Reports that the tender offer price fell well short of net asset value also point to a gap between the share price and asset value.

If the Golfzon County sale is completed, the earnings base changes and the premise for reading earnings-based multiples changes with it. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 10 billion in 2025, which is one of the items to review alongside dividend capacity.

08

Bull factors

Operating profit recovery since 4Q 2025

Operating profit fell to KRW 616 million in 4Q 2025 and then recovered to KRW 2.61 billion in 1Q 2026 and KRW 14.4 billion in 2Q 2026. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders also returned to a profit in 1Q 2026 after the 4Q net loss.

Operating profit fell again to KRW 6.68 billion in 3Q 2026, so whether the recovery is sustained needs confirmation in further quarters.

Overseas store expansion at Golfzon

Golfzon (215000) had 4,641 overseas stores in 3Q 2026, up 20.3% from 3,857 a year earlier. Yoojin Securities expects overseas new-product sales and store operating revenue to rise in the fourth-quarter peak season.

A broader overseas base could widen the earnings foundation of the key investee, but how higher overseas operating costs flow through to profit remains the key question.

Asset base and debt management

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 750.1 billion, of which equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 744.8 billion. Total liabilities were KRW 309.5 billion, and the debt ratio declined from 47.2% in 2022 to 41.3% in 2025. Even as earnings shrank, the financial burden is lighter than in 2022.

Bear factors

Lower earnings at the key investee

Press reports said Golfzon's first-half 2026 revenue fell 15.1% and operating profit fell 55.6% year on year. Golfzon Holdings receives income from Golfzon indirectly, so weaker results at the investee pass through to holding-company profit. Golfzon (215000) also reported a 34.4% drop in 3Q 2026 operating profit.

Earnings gap if Golfzon County is sold

Press reports put Golfzon County-related income at KRW 7.9 billion in first-half 2026, or 46% of operating profit. A completed sale would remove or reduce that income. Because Golfzon Holdings opposes the sale and is considering legal action, timing and outcome remain uncertain.

Three years of decline and margin compression

The operating margin fell from 19.3% in 2022 to 7.9% in 2025. Operating cash flow of KRW 10 billion in 2025 was the lowest of the four years. Operating profit fell again in 3Q 2026, which leaves the continuity of the quarterly recovery uncertain.

Risk factors

Governance and legal disputes

Reports said the largest shareholder side secured more than 85% of shares through two tender offers, while the delisting plan did not proceed. Golfzon Holdings is considering legal action over the Golfzon County sale, so litigation could add decision-making and cost burdens. Diverging interests between minority shareholders and the largest shareholder are another variable.

Market saturation and weaker consumption

Korean screen golf stores numbered 9,880 at the end of 2024, close to 10,000. Golfzon (215000) said new golfer inflows have slowed and replacement demand for new products has been used up. Golf equipment distribution is highly seasonal and faces intense price competition, which makes quarterly results volatile.

Overseas costs and litigation at the key investee

Golfzon (215000) reported that higher sales and marketing costs in the United States and China reduced its 3Q 2026 operating profit. If overseas sales growth does not offset cost increases, the investee's profit could keep weakening. Press reports also said damages litigation tied to a past personal data leak is ongoing at Golfzon.

12

Overall view

Golfzon Holdings' disclosed results show revenue and operating profit declining for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, with the operating margin falling from 19.3% in 2022 to 7.9% in 2025. Operating profit recovered from the 4Q 2025 loss through 2Q 2026 but fell again in 3Q 2026.

A large part of holding-company profit relies on Golfzon County-related income, so whether that stake is sold is the variable most likely to change the earnings structure.

The key investee Golfzon (215000) has an overseas store expansion as a recovery driver, but market saturation in Korea and higher overseas costs offset it. The balance sheet at the end of 2025 showed total equity of KRW 750.1 billion and a debt ratio of 41.3%, which does not signal heavy financial strain.

On governance, the largest shareholder side holds more than 85% of shares, but minority shareholder structure and litigation possibilities remain. The next checkpoints are the operating margin in the 2026 annual report and the outcome of the Golfzon County sale.
