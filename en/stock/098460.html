Report overview

Koh Young: AI server inspection demand drives profit recovery, Q3 net loss a watch point

Koh Young Technology posted record quarterly revenue and operating profit in Q3 2026 on AI server inspection demand, but swung to a attributable net loss of KRW 6.81 billion in the same quarter, making the quality of earnings and FX effects the next points to watch.

  1. 1

    Q3 2026 revenue reached KRW 106.6 billion, up 76.8% from a year earlier, and operating income reached KRW 19.3 billion, up 312.0%. The company said the results were a quarterly record.

  2. 2

    Server-related revenue rose 195% year on year to KRW 49.5 billion in Q3, and server customers accounted for 43% of quarterly revenue, ahead of automotive at 24%.

  3. 3

    Attributable net income swung to a loss of KRW 6.81 billion in Q3. The company attributed the loss mainly to foreign currency valuation losses from the weaker won against the dollar.

  4. 4

    The Geniant Cranial brain surgery robot had shipped seven units abroad through Q3 2026 (five to the US, two to Japan), leaving three units to reach the annual minimum target of ten.

  5. 5

    Mirae Asset Securities, in a report dated November 12, 2026, presented a maintained target price of KRW 52,500 and a Buy rating.

02

Business structure

Koh Young Technology's core business is 3D inspection equipment used in the surface-mount technology (SMT) process for printed circuit boards. Its main products are 3D solder paste inspection and component placement inspection equipment, and its share of the SMT inspection equipment market was 38% as of 2023.

According to Blotter, which cited company data, 3D component placement inspection equipment accounted for KRW 142.1 billion (53.0%) of cumulative January–September 2026 revenue of KRW 268.1 billion, and 3D solder paste inspection equipment for KRW 103.4 billion (38.6%).

Together the two product lines made up 91.6% of revenue. The remaining KRW 22.6 billion (8.4%) was reported as other revenue, which includes new businesses such as the Geniant Cranial brain surgery robot and parts sales.

By customer type, server customers made up 43% of Q3 2026 revenue, ahead of automotive at 24%, and the cumulative 2026 server share was 41%.

In memory, the company began supplying SOCAMM2 inspection equipment to a domestic memory maker in June 2026 and said it secured an additional order in September, without disclosing the size.

In North America, it hired a West Coast sales head in September 2026 to strengthen its sales network in the western United States and Canada.

In medical robotics, the Geniant Cranial has been shipped mainly abroad, and Medtronic and Zimmer Biomet are cited as competitors, both of which have built platforms and clinical data through acquisitions.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 275.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 225.6 billion in 2023 (down 18.1% year on year) and KRW 202.5 billion in 2024 (down 10.2%), then rose to KRW 232.6 billion in 2025 (up 14.9%). Among 2022–2025, 2022 was the largest year for revenue and 2024 the smallest.

Operating income fell from KRW 44.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 20.4 billion in 2023 (down 54.0%) and KRW 3.32 billion in 2024 (down 83.7%), then recovered to KRW 17.3 billion in 2025 (up 422.0%). The operating margin moved from 16.1% in 2022 to 9.0% in 2023 and 1.6% in 2024, before rising to 7.5% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, from KRW 39.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 21.9 billion in 2023 (down 44.2%), KRW 21 billion in 2024 (down 4.2%) and KRW 14.8 billion in 2025 (down 29.7%).

In 2025, operating income recovered strongly while attributable net income fell, so the two measures moved in opposite directions. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 335.2 billion, total liabilities were KRW 75.2 billion, and the debt ratio was 22.4%, up from 16.9% in 2024.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 36.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 18.1 billion in 2025. The quarterly trend is steeper.

Revenue went from KRW 60.3 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 69.1 billion in Q4 2025 (up 14.7% from the prior quarter), KRW 72.7 billion in Q1 2026 (up 5.2%), KRW 88.8 billion in Q2 2026 (up 22.0%) and KRW 106.6 billion in Q3 2026 (up 20.1%), rising for four consecutive quarters.

Operating income moved from KRW 4.7 billion to KRW 6.95 billion, KRW 9.85 billion, KRW 14.6 billion and KRW 19.3 billion over the same period, also rising for four consecutive quarters.

Attributable net income was KRW 8.45 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 7.8 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 15.7 billion in each of Q1 and Q2 2026, before turning into a net loss of KRW 6.81 billion in Q3 2026. The company attributed the loss mainly to foreign currency valuation losses from the weaker won against the dollar. Attributable net income over the last four quarters totaled KRW 32.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Koh Young's demand base is widening from AI servers to optical communication modules, high-speed network equipment and power modules. Mirae Asset Securities noted that production expansion in these areas is lifting demand for inspection equipment and solutions.

Higher component density and more complex production processes raise the importance of precision inspection. In high-bandwidth memory, the company described its HBM inspection equipment as in the testing stage as of July 2024.

On November 17, 2026, inspection and metrology equipment stocks among HBM-related shares declined on profit-taking, and Koh Young was among them. In automotive, Mirae Asset's breakdown showed automotive revenue of KRW 24.1 billion in Q3 2026, up 28%.

On the competitive side, a global slowdown could lead customers to cut capital spending or adopt rival inspection equipment, which would limit the benefit of a stronger sales network.

On profitability, Mirae Asset flagged a rising share of Asian revenue, inflation and logistics costs as limits on margins, while revenue growth in AI solutions, servers and optical modules was seen as a margin support. The company's short lead times also make it hard to infer future earnings from inventory alone.

Its position rests on a 38% share of the SMT inspection equipment market, with 3D inspection technology being extended into AI memory and optical communication.

06

Outlook

The company said expanding its Yeoju production center would secure production capacity ahead of demand. It also said order volumes had grown enough to require a substantial increase in production capacity.

SOCAMM2 inspection equipment supply began in June 2026, with additional orders since then, though the size was not disclosed. For the brain surgery robot, seven units had shipped abroad through Q3 2026, leaving three units to reach the annual minimum target of ten.

Mirae Asset Securities presented 2027 revenue of about KRW 450 billion, up 19% from the prior year, on the back of expanding global AI investment. The same report noted that Q3 2026 marked a second consecutive quarter of earnings beating expectations, and it leaned toward growth continuing next year.

On profitability, however, it expected limited operating leverage despite top-line growth. In North America, the key question is whether the appointment of a West Coast sales head turns into new orders. Supply contracts and delivery volumes in the region are expected to be verified through future quarterly disclosures.

The company also cited rising investment from Chinese AI server and semiconductor customers as a new growth opportunity. Brokers generally tie the durability of earnings growth to whether AI infrastructure spending converts into equipment orders and revenue.

Bull factors

Expanding AI server inspection demand

Server-related revenue reached KRW 49.5 billion in Q3 2026, up 195% year on year, and server customers made up 43% of quarterly revenue. Production growth in optical communication and power modules is widening demand for 3D inspection.

Rising investment from Chinese AI server and semiconductor customers is another factor the company cited as a growth opportunity.

Four straight quarters of operating profit growth

Operating income rose for four consecutive quarters, from KRW 4.7 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 19.3 billion in Q3 2026. The operating margin recovered from 1.6% in 2024 to 7.5% in 2025, and the company reported 18.2% for Q3 2026. The shift away from the margin slowdown seen through early 2024 is visible in the data.

New products and overseas sales expansion

The company said it secured an additional SOCAMM2 inspection equipment order in September 2026, after supply began in June. In September 2026 it hired a sales head to lead operations in the western United States and Canada. The brain surgery robot had seven overseas shipments by Q3 2026, close to the annual target.

Bear factors

Q3 net loss and FX sensitivity

Attributable net income turned into a loss of KRW 6.81 billion in Q3 2026, which the company attributed mainly to foreign currency valuation losses from the weaker won. Annual results for 2025 also showed attributable net income falling while operating income rose. Whether the operating profit recovery carries through to attributable net income remains to be confirmed.

Concentration in server customers and cycle risk

Server customers accounted for 43% of Q3 2026 revenue and 41% on a cumulative 2026 basis. A high share from one industry can make results more volatile if global AI investment slows. Mirae Asset Securities also flagged the growing share of Asian revenue and logistics costs as factors that could limit profitability.

Small robot revenue and competition

According to Blotter, about 80% of the KRW 18.9 billion in Q3 other revenue came from semiconductor inspection equipment, and robots and software together contributed roughly KRW 3.8 billion. The brain surgery robot's contribution to results is not yet visible in the numbers. Competitors Medtronic and Zimmer Biomet have built platforms and clinical data through acquisitions.

10

Risk factors

FX risk

The company attributed the Q3 2026 net loss mainly to foreign currency valuation losses from the weaker won. Exchange-rate swings can move quarterly net income sharply, so gaps between operating income and attributable net income could recur. Whether this sensitivity eases will show in the income statement composition of upcoming periodic reports.

Customer concentration and capex cycle

If server and optical communication customers cut capital spending, revenue growth could slow quickly. A global slowdown that leads local manufacturers to cut capex would also limit the benefit of the stronger North American sales network. Changes in customer production plans can also reach orders and revenue with a lag.

New business commercialization and capacity

With seven of the minimum ten annual units shipped, reaching the robot target is not yet assured. Until the Yeoju production center expansion is completed, rising orders may be hard to meet fully with production.

A slower pace of commercialization for new businesses would delay verification of the growth story the company has presented.

12

Overall view

Koh Young Technology recorded revenue of KRW 106.6 billion and operating income of KRW 19.3 billion in Q3 2026 on demand for 3D inspection equipment used in AI servers, and operating income has risen for four consecutive quarters since Q4 2025.

The annual operating margin recovered from 1.6% in 2024 to 7.5% in 2025 over the same period. Attributable net income, however, fell for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and in Q3 2026 it swung to a loss of KRW 6.81 billion, which the company attributed mainly to FX valuation losses.

Server customers account for 43% of quarterly revenue, so spending cycles in one industry weigh heavily on results. The brain surgery robot is at an early stage, with seven units shipped abroad and a still-small revenue contribution.

Among brokers, Mirae Asset Securities maintained a target price of KRW 52,500 and presented a view of continued growth into 2027, while also noting that operating leverage is likely to be limited.

On valuation, the earnings multiple is high when read on the last four quarters, and the price-to-book ratio reflects a large premium over net asset value.

The next checkpoints are whether the annual robot shipment target is reached at the end of December 2026, Q4 2026 results in Q1 2027, and the final figures in the 2026 annual business report due by the end of March 2027.
