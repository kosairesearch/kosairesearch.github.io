KBG: Q3 2026 Profit Turn Under Review as Silicone Materials Push Into Packaging

KBG is a small functional silicone materials maker whose annual operating profit fell for two straight years through 2025, while operating profit turned positive again in Q3 2026 after an operating loss in Q2 2026.

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

KBG was founded in 2001 as a functional silicone materials company and listed on KOSDAQ in 2019. Building on fine chemical and active pharmaceutical ingredient manufacturing, it synthesizes products in-house from silane monomers through silicone polymers and resins.

Its core lines are silane monomers, silicone resins and polymers, and reactive silicone fluids, supplied to display, electronic materials, automotive and industrial customers.

In July 2024 it acquired an aerogel company and entered silica aerogel insulation materials, and its 2024 consolidated revenue of KRW 24.9 billion was higher than the KRW 23.1 billion reported on a standalone basis.

New projects include silicone for automotive TIM and TRB applications, silicone for LED light protection, and personal care silicone.

In January 2026 the company said it had established a TFE mass-production process for removing low-molecular-weight siloxanes and that it aims to move from synthesis toward refinery-based, higher-value business.

In March 2026 it said it would expand silicone materials for AI semiconductor thermal management and packaging, and in October 2026 it announced expanded high-purity silicone products for semiconductor packaging.

Measured by 2025 annual revenue, the company ranks 111th among 121 listed chemical companies, placing it among the smaller specialty players in the sector.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, revenue fell from KRW 24.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 21.6 billion in 2023 (down 11.3%), then rose to KRW 24.9 billion in 2024 (up 15.4%), the largest year of 2022 to 2025. Revenue was KRW 23.9 billion in 2025 (down 4.0%).

Operating profit peaked at KRW 4.31 billion in 2023 (up 5.0%) and then fell to KRW 3.16 billion in 2024 (down 26.7%) and KRW 2.33 billion in 2025 (down 26.3%), declining for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025. The operating margin moved from 16.9% in 2022 and 20.0% in 2023 to 12.7% in 2024 and 9.7% in 2025.

In 2024 revenue grew while operating profit fell, meaning the top-line gain did not carry through to earnings.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 4.58 billion in 2023 (up 40.9%) to KRW 3.77 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.59 billion in 2025, and total net income was reported at the same amounts for each year.

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 46.9 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, with total liabilities of KRW 5.42 billion and a debt ratio of 11.6%. Operating cash flow was KRW 1.67 billion in 2022, KRW 9.47 billion in 2023, KRW 4.29 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.34 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 4.71 billion with operating profit of KRW 338 million, and Q4 2025 revenue rose to KRW 6.89 billion (up 46.3% from the prior quarter) with operating profit of KRW 343 million.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 5.36 billion (down 22.2% from the prior quarter), operating profit was KRW 720 million (up 109.7%) and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 762 million.

Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 6.31 billion (up 17.7% from the prior quarter), but the company posted an operating loss of KRW 279 million, turning from profit to loss.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 5.36 billion (down 15.0% from the prior quarter and up 13.9% year on year), operating profit was KRW 503 million, turning positive from the prior quarter and up 48.8% year on year, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 840 million (up 114.5% year on year).

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, totals KRW 2.04 billion. The specific cause of the Q2 2026 loss is not explained in the disclosed material reviewed, so it is a point to check in the segment discussion of the next annual report.

05

Industry analysis

Silicone demand is diverging by end market. According to a WiseReport company summary as of December 2025, demand for general-purpose sealants fell amid a construction slowdown, while demand for high-performance silicone in electric vehicles, electrical and electronics, and K-beauty cosmetics increased.

The company is positioning solutions for tightening low-molecular-weight siloxane regulations in automotive electronics and electronics as a new business.

In AI semiconductors, the company cites growing demand for thermal management and packaging materials for GPUs and HBM, but this comes from its own press releases and has not been checked against external market-size forecasts.

On a cycle view, construction-linked general demand is weak while high-performance and electronics-linked demand is comparatively stronger.

Within its sector, KBG ranks low on revenue, 111th of 121 listed chemical companies on 2025 annual figures per AlphaSquare, but its 9.72% operating margin ranks 25th on the same basis. That suggests a specialty product mix is supporting profitability more than scale does. Whether the margin decline from 20.0% in 2023 to 9.7% in 2025 will persist has not yet been confirmed.
