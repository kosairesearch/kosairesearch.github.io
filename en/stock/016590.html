02

Business structure

Shindaeyang Paper produces corrugated base paper, including medium and liner paper, which serves as the core raw material for corrugated boxes.

According to the company's own description, it runs an integrated structure that covers the process from used-paper sourcing to base-paper production, which it cites as a source of cost competitiveness.

Its production sites are the Banwol and Siheung plants in Ansan, Gyeonggi, and the 2026 Q1 quarterly report's facility table shows a 2025 corrugated base paper total of 694,260 tons.

Base paper is supplied to group affiliates and outside packaging makers, and products are made to order in a range of specifications to customer requirements.

Several listed group subsidiaries, including Kwangsin Paperboard, Daeyoung Packaging, Daeyang Paperboard and the newly formed Shindaeyang Packaging, are disclosed as consolidated subsidiaries that make corrugated sheets and boxes.

In February 2026, Shindaeyang Packaging was established in Cheongju, North Chungcheong Province, and consolidated as a subsidiary. According to an investment information site, most sales come from corrugated base paper and box operations.

Most paper makers in Korea depend on a single business line, and the company has said it will keep its focus on corrugated paper.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 676.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 645.4 billion in 2023 (down 4.6% year on year), recovered to KRW 658.5 billion in 2024 (up 2.0%), and reached KRW 639.5 billion in 2025 (down 2.9%), the smallest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit was KRW 58.5 billion in 2023 (up 15.0%), the largest year of 2022 to 2025, before falling to KRW 29.3 billion in 2024 (down 50.0%) and KRW 22.2 billion in 2025 (down 24.1%), a two-year decline from 2024 through 2025.

The operating margin moved from 7.5% in 2022 and 9.1% in 2023 to 4.4% in 2024 and 3.5% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose to KRW 47.9 billion in 2023 (up 0.6%) and then fell to KRW 35.1 billion in 2024 (down 26.6%) and KRW 22.8 billion in 2025 (down 35.1%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 22.3 billion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder amount, with the gap reflecting non-controlling interests. For 2025, the company attributed the decline to lower sales, higher costs and lower financial income.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 92.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 59.6 billion in 2025, moving in the same direction as profit. The debt-to-equity ratio was 34.6% in 2022, 27.6% in 2023, 26.6% in 2024 and 28.0% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 721.1 billion, of which controlling-shareholder equity was KRW 601.6 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 201.8 billion.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit slid from KRW 5.49 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 561 million in Q4 2025 (down 89.8% quarter on quarter), then recovered to KRW 3.78 billion in Q1 2026 (up 572.9%), KRW 14.8 billion in Q2 2026 (up 290.8%) and KRW 12.7 billion in Q3 2026 (down 14.2% quarter on quarter, up 131.1% year on year).

Q3 2026 sales of KRW 168.6 billion rose 3.1% year on year, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.87 billion fell 2.1%, so sales and profit moved in different directions.

An investment information site summarized first-half 2026 figures as sales up 4.1%, operating profit up 14.5% and net income up 79.5%. Because the Q2 2026 increase was large, the next annual report's income statement items will show whether cost or price drove it.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for corrugated base paper is supported by secondary packaging linked to parcel delivery and home-shopping volumes, according to an investment information site. The shift toward corrugated packaging for farm produce and food products is also cited as widening the demand base.

On the other side, used-paper prices and fixed costs such as electricity drive unit costs, and this cost structure is named as a main reason for the margin pressure.

A press report noted that annual production fell from 1.08 million tons in 2018 to about 670,000 tons, which points to both softer demand and adjusted plant utilization.

Listed paper and board peers in Korea include Hansol Paper, Moorim P&P, Sambo Corrugated Board and PaperCorea, and many of them also depend on a narrow product base, so cost pressure is a shared industry theme.

Shindaeyang Paper differs from pure base-paper makers because it covers both base paper and boxes within one vertically linked group. Judging the position in the cycle requires tracking corrugated base-paper selling prices and plant utilization over time. The report does not present a market-share comparison because no comparable figure is available.

Outlook

The corporate value-up plan the company disclosed on March 27, 2026 sets out a goal of securing target earnings through growth investment and stable profit generation to achieve shareholder-return objectives.

The company said dividend levels will be decided by considering shareholder value and the operating environment together. In the same filing, it stated that it qualifies as a high-dividend company under the Restriction of Special Taxation Act.

The plan also states that it will maintain a conservative financial policy to manage financial soundness. At the March 2026 general shareholders meeting, three outside directors were newly elected and two resigned, raising the outside-director ratio to 33.3%.

The operating start date and investment scale of Shindaeyang Packaging, established in February 2026, have not yet been disclosed. On the ownership side, Shin Daehan Paper, a shareholder holding 10% or more, bought 2.07 million additional shares through off-market trades in November 2025, raising its stake to 18.91%.

The next checkpoint is whether quarterly operating profit stays near the level seen in Q2 and Q3 2026. On an annual basis, the 2026 annual report, which includes Q4 2026, will show whether the full-year operating margin exceeds the 3.5% recorded in 2025.

Whether the listed subsidiary clears its administrative-issue designation also remains a marker of group-level standing in the capital market.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in quarterly profit

Operating profit reached KRW 14.8 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 12.7 billion in Q3 2026, up sharply from KRW 561 million in Q4 2025. A first-half 2026 summary put operating profit up 14.5% year on year. Whether the recovery holds beyond the current quarters is the central checkpoint for the bullish case.

Wider demand for secondary packaging

An investment information site notes that secondary packaging demand is growing with parcel delivery and home-shopping expansion, and that corrugated packaging for farm produce is taking hold. The company says it holds a high share of the Seoul metropolitan market. A structure linking base paper to boxes within the group can support supply stability.

Shareholder-return plan and ownership

The value-up plan disclosed in March 2026 sets a goal of securing target earnings for shareholder returns, and the company said it qualifies as a high-dividend company. In November 2025, Shin Daehan Paper, a shareholder holding 10% or more, raised its stake to 18.91% through off-market trades. Treasury shares account for more than a quarter of issued shares.

Bear factors

Lower annual profitability

The operating margin fell from 7.5% in 2022 to 3.5% in 2025, and operating profit has declined for two straight years from its 2023 peak of KRW 58.5 billion. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders also fell to KRW 22.8 billion in 2025 from KRW 47.9 billion in 2023. Lower sales and operating cash flow together are cited as grounds that earnings recovery may be slow.

Raw-material and fixed-cost pressure

The company attributed the 2025 decline to lower sales, higher costs and lower financial income. Industry coverage has pointed to used-paper prices and electricity costs as weighing on profitability. A press report also said annual production fell from about 1.08 million tons in 2018 to about 670,000 tons.

Group-level listing risk

A listed subsidiary was designated as an administrative-issue stock in August 2026 under the low-price stock rule, and the company holds a 45.97% stake as its largest shareholder.

In November 2026, the exchange designated the stock as an investment-caution stock because of concentrated trading in a small number of accounts. A research head at a sustainable management institute warned that listing-requirement issues at group companies can damage the group's reputation.

Risk factors

Raw-material and cost risk

Higher used-paper prices or rising fixed costs such as electricity can lift the cost ratio and press the operating margin. The company itself cited higher costs as a reason for the 2025 profit decline. How much cost increases can be passed through to selling prices will determine the direction of margins.

Group listing risk

Under the rules, a subsidiary designated as an administrative-issue stock faces delisting procedures if the cause is not resolved within 90 trading days. The company holds 45.97% of that subsidiary, so the outcome could affect group reputation and consolidated results. Governance questions raised about the wider group add to the burden.

Trading and price volatility risk

In November 2026, a sharp three-day price move led to an investment-caution designation. The exchange's filing recorded concentrated buying by a small number of accounts as the trigger. Prices can move with trading flows regardless of the earnings trend, which makes trading volatility a risk factor in its own right.

Overall view

Shindaeyang Paper's quarterly operating profit has recovered from KRW 561 million in Q4 2025 to KRW 14.8 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 12.7 billion in Q3 2026.

On an annual basis, however, operating profit fell from KRW 58.5 billion in 2023 to KRW 22.2 billion in 2025, a two-year decline, and the operating margin dropped from 7.5% in 2022 to 3.5% in 2025.

Corrugated secondary-packaging demand and the vertically linked structure are cited as strengths, yet raw-material and electricity costs continue to weigh on margins.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 32.5 billion over the last four quarters, which supports the recovery view, but because the Q2 2026 gain was large, whether it is temporary or structural is not yet settled.

On shareholder returns, the company has disclosed a value-up plan and high-dividend status, and treasury shares are a large share of the total.

Against that, the administrative-issue designation of a listed subsidiary and the investment-caution designation over concentrated trading point to group-level risk and price volatility.

The next checkpoints are the subsidiary's designation deadline in December 2026 and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report due by late March 2027.
