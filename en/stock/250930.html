02

Business structure

YeSUN Tech is a specialist maker of functional adhesive materials used in displays and electronic products. Its main products are foam and gasket pads for LCD TVs and monitors, foam and COF pads and encapsulation materials for OLED TVs and monitors, and bonding tapes for automotive and energy applications.

As of the first quarter of 2026, LCD accounted for the largest share of revenue at 44.8%, OLED for about 31%, and automotive for 15%. The company says it reduced the share of low-margin products and reorganized its portfolio around higher-value products.

As a result, the SG&A ratio fell from 25.0% in 2025 to 19.9% in the first quarter of 2026, according to an interview with the CEO. The automotive business supplies adhesive materials for digital clusters and HVAC displays and is widening its customer base to LG Electronics and global automotive electronics suppliers.

The LCD business is regarded as a mature industry but serves as a stable source of cash generation, the company says.

The energy business is still at an early stage: the company developed flame-retardant adhesive materials for large secondary batteries, filed a patent in 2025, and registered a patent for fireproof sheets for EV batteries in 2026, with customer testing under way.

Among chemical peers, YeSUN Tech is a small company specialized in adhesives, unlike diversified chemical groups such as LG Chem and Hanwha Solutions.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on the disclosed annual figures, revenue was KRW 53.2 billion in 2022, KRW 44.1 billion in 2023 (down 17.0% year on year), KRW 43.6 billion in 2024 (down 1.1%), and KRW 37.9 billion in 2025 (down 13.2%), falling for three consecutive years from 2023. 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest in the 2022–2025 period.

The operating loss was KRW 8.01 billion in 2022 and KRW 8.8 billion in 2023, widening in the latter year, then narrowed to KRW 3.24 billion in 2024 and KRW 813 million in 2025. The company posted operating losses for four consecutive years from 2022 through 2025.

The operating margin improved from -15.1% in 2022 and -19.9% in 2023 to -7.4% in 2024 and -2.1% in 2025. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed from KRW 9.12 billion in 2022 and KRW 10.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 5.97 billion in 2024 and KRW 755 million in 2025.

The quarterly turning point came in the third quarter of 2025, when operating profit of KRW 124 million marked a return to profit, followed by KRW 272 million in the fourth quarter and KRW 830 million in the first quarter of 2026.

Operating profit fell to KRW 10.12 million in the second quarter of 2026 before recovering to KRW 301 million in the third quarter.

The KRW 1.82 billion net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter of 2026 came despite operating profit, and the company attributed it to a temporary valuation loss. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters totaled KRW 627 million.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 5.34 billion in 2022 to KRW 27.56 million in 2025, and the debt ratio rose from 94.0% in 2022 to 232.8% in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The automotive display market is growing alongside the industry's electrification trend. One outlet reported that the market for automotive displays is expected to grow from USD 9.3 billion in 2023 to USD 15.9 billion in 2030.

The automotive share of the company's revenue rising to about 15% in the first quarter of 2026 reflects this trend. By contrast, LCD is regarded as a mature industry that is susceptible to demand swings and price competition.

Because LCD accounts for 44.8% of revenue, the company's earnings volatility is directly tied to the cycle in this segment. In OLED, the company's strategy is to grow higher-value products such as foam and COF pads.

The secondary battery safety materials market is a new market that starts from demand for materials that slow thermal runaway in EV batteries. YeSUN Tech has registered a patent and is in customer testing, and the company itself describes its energy business as early-stage in terms of revenue contribution.

In display adhesives, it competes with domestic and foreign specialty materials makers, and it is responding by shifting its product mix toward high-growth markets such as automotive and secondary batteries.

Large diversified chemical companies have broader business scopes, which makes direct comparison with an adhesives specialist difficult.

Outlook

The company attributed the rise in third-quarter 2026 revenue to stable supply of LCD and OLED adhesive materials and a larger automotive share.

The CEO said YeSUN Tech expects to regain the number one position in foam pad share at LG Electronics and that the Chinese customer share is growing, which he said should support steady earnings growth in the LCD business.

Supply expansion of adhesive materials for automotive digital clusters and HVAC displays is also under way. In secondary batteries, the company aims for commercialization after patent registration and customer testing.

On shareholder-value measures, a 5-for-1 share consolidation took effect on July 22, 2026, and 48,989 treasury shares, about 0.7% of issued shares, were cancelled on September 10, 2026. An executive bought 8,806 shares on the open market, disclosed on September 9, 2026, lifting his stake from 0.70% to 0.84%.

Convertible bonds worth KRW 2.5 billion were fully redeemed early in 2026, and the company said it has no plans for additional rights offerings or convertible bond issuance.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results in the 2026 annual report and progress in customer testing for secondary battery materials.
