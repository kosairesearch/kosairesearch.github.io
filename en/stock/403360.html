Infrastructure Deal Drives a Step-Change in H1 Results

After absorbing its network-infrastructure subsidiary, Rapeech posted H1 2026 revenue of KRW 15 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.29 billion, and the question is whether that pace holds in the second half.

02

Business structure

Rapeech, founded in 2005 and listed on KONEX in 2021, is a conversational AI technology company offering AI consultation, AI assistant and voice authentication services. Its predecessor was a speech-recognition solutions firm, and it changed its name in March 2022.

Built around its in-house ConvAI platform, it supplies offerings ranging from NLU-based callbots to LLM-based AI agents to financial, public-sector and telecom clients. The company says it built the first voice-based identity verification service in Korean finance and has worked with LG Electronics and SK Telecom. Its website also features an AI contact-center project for LG Uplus.

The acquired subsidiary had about 30 years of experience in network infrastructure and data center construction, and Rapeech said it would use it to expand AI data centers and cloud-based AICC. Its website describes a structured cabling project built to the TIA-942 international data center standard. After the merger, the business rests on two pillars: AI software and network infrastructure (NI).

The subsidiary's revenue in its latest fiscal year, per the merger filing, was roughly KRW 21.3 billion. Rapeech's 2025 consolidated revenue was KRW 16.1 billion, so the merger changed the scale of the business materially. We could not confirm the revenue split by segment in the half-year report.

On the competitive side, large IT service groups such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS hold scale advantages in contact-center and AI infrastructure markets.

03

Recent trends

FnGuide data show consolidated revenue of KRW 17.1 billion in 2023, KRW 15.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 16.1 billion in 2025, a largely flat trend. In the same data, operating profit was nil in 2023, an operating loss of KRW 1.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 0.6 billion in 2025. The 2025 operating margin was a thin 3.66%.

Cumulative H1 2026 filed results depart sharply from that pattern. Revenue was KRW 15 billion, up 299.9% year on year. Operating profit was KRW 1.29 billion, up 657.7%, and net income was KRW 1.25 billion, up 347.3%.

The acquisition of the network-infrastructure subsidiary closed in January 2026. The wider consolidation scope appears to be the main driver of the revenue jump, but segment-level contributions are not confirmed.

H1 operating profit of KRW 1.29 billion is more than twice the full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 0.6 billion.

The share price was KRW 8,400 intraday on February 20, 2026. A report dated April 29, 2026 put it at KRW 6,790, up 9.52% on the day. As of October 2, 2026, the 52-week range ran from KRW 5,000 to KRW 10,070. On October 27, 2026 the stock closed at KRW 8,200, up 11.26% on the day.

Capstone Partners acquired 363,635 new shares on March 21, 2026 and disclosed a 10.14% holding. This is best read simply as an inflow of third-party capital. The stake change does not indicate the future direction of the share price.

04

Outlook

The key question is whether the revenue and profit levels seen in the first half hold on a full-year basis. H1 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.29 billion already exceeds full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 0.6 billion, so quarterly disclosures from the third quarter onward are the first checkpoint.

On business metrics, there is room for the operating margin to improve. The 2025 operating margin was 3.66%, and a larger AI software share could change the margin structure. Segment margins are not disclosed, however, so this can only be assessed qualitatively.

In its three mega-projects announced in June 2026, the government outlined a plan for data centers totaling 18.4GW. Observers also note that order growth turns into earnings only if actual groundbreaking, project scope and cost control are considered together.

Rapeech is a network-infrastructure and AI company rather than a general contractor, so how directly this demand reaches it is unclear.

After the merger, the company said it would unify decision-making and combine its AI and NI capabilities. Results of this plan should be checked through cross-selling examples and new-order disclosures. No specific order size or timetable has been confirmed.

On the share price, the thin trading conditions of KONEX are a variable. Low trading value means small volumes can move the price materially. Price volatility can widen regardless of the earnings trend.

05

Bull factors

Clear improvement in H1 results

Per filed results, cumulative H1 2026 revenue was KRW 15 billion, up 299.9% year on year. Operating profit rose 657.7% to KRW 1.29 billion and net income rose 347.3% to KRW 1.25 billion, so profit growth outpaced revenue growth.

Compared with full-year 2025 operating profit of KRW 0.6 billion, the half-year alone exceeded that level. If profitability persists, it supports the view that the company has moved past the 2024 operating-loss phase.

AI software combined with infrastructure

The merger's core is a portfolio spanning AI software and NI construction and operation, offering customers everything from infrastructure build-out to AI adoption. Rapeech has callbot and voice-authentication references built up with financial and telecom clients.

The company's explanation is that the infrastructure business generates ongoing operating demand after construction, which may partly cushion swings in the software business. Whether this synergy shows up in actual orders remains to be verified.

AI infrastructure investment backdrop

Global Economic reported on October 27, 2026 that major contractors' data center order totals in the first half of 2026 exceeded KRW 6.2 trillion. The government's large-scale AI infrastructure plan and private data center investment form the backdrop for network-construction demand.

Rapeech publishes data center network projects on its website, which indicates an existing business base in the field. How much of that market growth converts into Rapeech orders still needs separate confirmation.

06

Bear factors

Earnings durability is unproven

FnGuide data show consolidated operating profit swinging from nil in 2023 to an operating loss of KRW 1.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 0.6 billion in 2025. Even with a stronger first half, the change is recent and needs full-year confirmation. Project-type revenue can vary widely by quarter depending on order timing. If the same level is not sustained in the second half, the first half could be viewed as closer to a one-off.

Growth largely reflects the acquisition

The 299.9% revenue increase appears to reflect, to a large degree, the wider consolidation scope from the subsidiary's inclusion. That is acquired growth rather than organic growth, so it should be separated from the growth rate of the legacy AI business. The acquisition price was KRW 11.6 billion.

That exceeds the KRW 9.2 billion of equity at end-2025, so recovery of the investment and the funding structure warrant a check.

Small scale against larger rivals

Large IT service groups such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS participate in contact-center and AI infrastructure markets. Rapeech's 2025 revenue of KRW 16.1 billion is not comparable in scale. In large-customer bids, references and financial stability matter, which can disadvantage small suppliers. The spread of generative AI could also reshape competition in conversational AI quickly.

07

Risk factors

Macro

IT investment budgets at financial, public-sector and telecom clients are affected by the economic cycle and interest rates. Newspim reported on October 27, 2026 that concern over high rates constraining AI customers' investment capacity was reflected in semiconductor shares.

Higher borrowing costs for data center investors can delay capital spending, which may also affect network-construction demand.

Business and integration

The company said it completed the merger after roughly six months of organizational integration following the acquisition. A software-centered organization and an infrastructure-construction organization differ in earnings structure and order patterns, so management complexity can rise after integration.

Segment revenue and profit are not visible in the filings, making it hard for outsiders to tell which business is driving results.

Market and liquidity

Investing.com data show average volume over the past three months of about 2,260 shares. Trading value on October 27, 2026 was KRW 47,361,670, very small relative to the KRW 30.9 billion market capitalization. Access for general investors to KONEX is limited, which can make execution and bid-ask spreads unfavorable. Small volumes can move the price substantially, so volatility needs to be managed.

08

Overall view

Rapeech has moved to a structure that adds network-infrastructure construction to conversational AI software, and its scale changed markedly, with H1 2026 revenue of KRW 15 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.29 billion per filed results.

The bull case rests on the size of the earnings increase, the AI-plus-infrastructure strategy and the data center investment backdrop from government and private players. The bear case rests on the 2024 operating loss, the share of growth attributable to the acquisition and its small scale against larger rivals.

A further limit is that segment results are not disclosed, so the quality of growth is hard to judge from outside. Given KONEX's thin trading and the stock's small-cap character, price volatility can widen independently of earnings. This report is for information only, and any judgment about direction is left to the reader.
