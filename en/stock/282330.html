Report overview

Foreign-shopper sales rise while operating margin slips

BGF retail's sales and operating profit both rose in the third quarter of 2026, helped by foreign-shopper sales and differentiated products, while its annual operating margin has slipped from 3.3% in 2022 to 2.8% in 2025.

02

Business structure

BGF retail operates the CU convenience store chain. Its product mix is shifting away from tobacco toward processed foods and general merchandise.

According to the company, tobacco's share of sales in the third quarter of 2026 fell 0.4 percentage points from a year earlier, while processed foods rose 0.7 percentage points. This shift kept the average merchandise margin on an upward trend.

Differentiated products include items using popular intellectual property (IP), coffee candy and truffle ham snacks, and snack category sales of these products grew 61.1% year on year in the same quarter.

Other categories also expanded, with food ingredients up 15.7% and ready-to-eat meals up 9.5%, and grocery merchandise sales rose 7.8%. On the customer side, foreign visitors have become a new pillar. Foreign-shopper share of sales rose from 1.5% in the third quarter of 2025 to 2.5% in the third quarter of 2026.

The company is pursuing a strategy to widen its customer base, including women and foreign shoppers. New openings focus on profitable stores, and the company is raising the share of mid-to-large stores of 25 to 40 pyeong. The company plans to extend IP-based products into multiple categories rather than relying on single hit items.

04

Earnings analysis

On disclosed figures, annual revenue rose from KRW 7.62 trillion in 2022 to KRW 8.19 trillion in 2023 and KRW 8.7 trillion in 2024, reaching KRW 9.06 trillion in 2025. Revenue has risen for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 span.

Over the same period, operating profit was KRW 252.4 billion in 2022, KRW 253.2 billion in 2023, KRW 251.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 253.9 billion in 2025, with 2024 the smallest year.

Because revenue grew while operating profit stayed flat, the operating margin fell from 3.3% in 2022 to 3.1% in 2023, 2.9% in 2024 and 2.8% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 193.5 billion in 2022 and KRW 195.3 billion in 2025, with no change in 2025 from 2024.

The balance sheet improved, with the debt ratio falling from 206.1% in 2022 to 170.8% in 2025. At year-end 2025, total liabilities were KRW 2.24 trillion and total equity was KRW 1.31 trillion. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 630.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 770.8 billion in 2025. Quarterly results were volatile.

Operating profit of KRW 97.6 billion in the third quarter of 2025 fell to KRW 64.3 billion in the fourth quarter, down 34.2% from the prior quarter, and reached its lowest level at KRW 38.1 billion in the first quarter of 2026, down 40.7% from the prior quarter.

It then rose to KRW 84.9 billion in the second quarter of 2026, up 122.7%, and KRW 100.8 billion in the third quarter, up 18.8%, marking two consecutive quarterly increases. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 2.52 trillion rose 2.2% from a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 74.1 billion fell 6.5% from a year earlier, and press reports tied this to pre-tax income of KRW 95.3 billion, down 3.9%.

The company said that foreign-shopper sales and processed-food demand supported revenue despite a base effect from the 2025 consumption coupons and a hot summer.

05

Industry analysis

The convenience store sector is in a phase of continued store restructuring. In a March 9, 2026 report, Sangsangin Securities said restructuring over the past year of roughly a year had centered on smaller operators, and that BGF retail's market share had instead risen.

The same report said investor interest, which had concentrated on department stores, would gradually broaden toward convenience stores.

Heungkuk Securities noted in a September 16, 2026 report that BGF retail has moved away from growth driven mainly by new openings since 2023 and is focusing on improving the quality of each store.

On the demand side, one analysis says the rise of single- and two-person households is a structural tailwind for convenience stores. Domestic consumption is also described as recovering, and Korea Investment & Securities pointed to a recovery in domestic spending in its first-quarter 2026 preview.

Looking at the cycle, the base effect from the 2025 consumption coupons constrained third-quarter 2026 revenue growth, according to the company. Foreign-shopper sales growth should be analyzed separately from domestic consumption indicators.

06

Outlook

The company has outlined three directions. First, it plans to extend IP-based differentiated products into more categories and strengthen products and services aimed at foreign customers.

Second, it is opening new stores centered on profitable locations and expanding the share of mid-to-large stores of 25 to 40 pyeong to build a medium-term profit base. Third, it disclosed a 40% shareholder return ratio goal in its corporate value-up plan. Broker estimates are mixed.

In a September 16, 2026 report, Heungkuk Securities raised its 2026 to 2027 earnings estimates, lifting its 2026 annual revenue forecast to KRW 9.5 trillion and operating profit forecast to KRW 306.7 billion.

Operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 totals KRW 223.8 billion, so whether the full-year estimate is reached will become clear with fourth-quarter results.

Hanwha Investment & Securities had projected third-quarter operating profit of KRW 103.4 billion, and the reported KRW 100.8 billion came in below that estimate. Hanwha described the slowdown in same-store sales as a temporary base effect. Year-to-date operating profit grew 18.0% according to the company.

The next checkpoints are whether foreign-shopper sales growth holds at third-quarter levels in the fourth quarter of 2026, and whether the full-year 2026 operating margin falls below the 2.8% recorded in 2025.

08

Bull factors

Foreign demand growing every quarter

Foreign-shopper sales growth ran at 53.3% in the first quarter of 2026, 60.9% in the second and 66.8% in the third, according to the company's November 5, 2026 release. The foreign-shopper share of sales rose from 1.5% in the third quarter of 2025 to 2.5% in the third quarter of 2026. Heungkuk Securities said expanding spending by visitors to Korea would keep second-half operating results firm.

Product mix shift lifts merchandise margin

Tobacco's share of sales fell 0.4 percentage points from a year earlier while processed foods rose 0.7 percentage points, per the company. The average merchandise margin continued to rise.

Heungkuk Securities said expanding high-margin products and operating efficiency could support stable profit growth, in a September 16, 2026 report.

Balance sheet improvement and return plan

In its April 30, 2026 corporate value-up plan, the company stated a goal of a 40% shareholder return ratio. The debt ratio fell from 206.1% in 2022 to 170.8% in 2025.

Bear factors

Falling margin and flat profit base

The operating margin fell from 3.3% in 2022 to 2.8% in 2025. Over the same period operating profit stayed in the KRW 250 billion range, so revenue growth did not translate into profit growth.

Operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 rose 18.0% from a year earlier, but whether the annual margin trend has changed will need to be confirmed in the 2026 annual report.

Quarterly volatility and lower net profit

Operating profit in the first quarter of 2026 fell 40.7% from the prior quarter to KRW 38.1 billion. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter of 2026 fell 6.5% from a year earlier to KRW 74.1 billion. Press reports said pre-tax income of KRW 95.3 billion, down 3.9% from a year earlier, weighed on the result.

Valuation burden and target cut

In an August 7, 2026 report, Korea Investment & Securities said convenience store operators carry the highest valuations among retail channels, which could prompt profit-taking.

In an October 7, 2026 report, Hanwha Investment & Securities expected third-quarter operating profit to fall short of consensus and lowered its target from KRW 210,000 to KRW 190,000. The same firm described the same-store slowdown as a temporary base effect.

10

Risk factors

Foreign demand volatility

Foreign-shopper growth depends on visitors to Korea and is sensitive to travel flows, exchange rates and tourism demand. Foreign shoppers accounted for 35.5% of the third quarter's store sales growth, so the dependence has grown. A slowdown in this demand could also weaken the benefit from the product mix shift.

Weather and base effects

The company cited the base effect from the 2025 consumption coupons and a hot summer as unfavorable conditions in the third quarter of 2026. Because the base changes each quarter, growth rates can fluctuate.

External factors such as weather and government consumption support add to the volatility of quarterly operating profit.

Store restructuring and opening strategy

Sangsangin Securities said in a March 9, 2026 report that store restructuring in the convenience store sector has continued mainly among smaller operators. The company is reshaping its profit structure through openings focused on profitable locations and a larger share of mid-to-large stores.

Slower openings could lower the pace of revenue growth, and converting stores to larger formats carries cost burdens.

Overall view

BGF retail's disclosed results show revenue growth alongside flat profit. Revenue rose to KRW 9.06 trillion by 2025, but operating profit stayed in the KRW 250 billion range and the operating margin fell to 2.8%.

In 2026, foreign-shopper sales, differentiated products and the product mix shift supported third-quarter operating profit of KRW 100.8 billion.
