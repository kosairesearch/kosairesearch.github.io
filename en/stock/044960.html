02

Business structure

Eagle Veterinary Technology was founded in December 1983 and its core business is manufacturing and importing veterinary medicines.

It holds KVGMP certification for injectables, liquids and additives, and an investment information service describes that certification and market trust as the basis of its export capability.

In November 2017 it became the first domestic veterinary drug maker to obtain EU GMP certification for a sterile injectable production facility, according to a March 2021 brokerage report. The company also runs a pet business and is pushing new ventures in pet medicines, pet feed and non-drug products.

A 2018 equity research report estimated that pet feed and feed additives made up more than 70% of sales at that time, so the current segment mix may differ. On distribution, the 2021 report described new direct channels with Coupang and GS SHOP and a push for mid- to low-priced private-label products.

In competition, large corporates such as Lotte were launching pet brands as of 2018. Headcount was 112 as of June 30, 2026, according to JobKorea. The largest shareholder group held 38.40% as of September 2025. Updated segment sales mix will be reflected in the next annual report.

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue declined from 435 hundred million won in 2022 to 428 hundred million won in 2023 (down 1.7% year over year) and 417 hundred million won in 2024 (down 2.5% year over year), then recovered to 433 hundred million won in 2025 (up 3.9% year over year).

Over 2022-2025, the year with the highest revenue was 2022 and the year with the lowest was 2024.

Operating profit bottomed at 33.3 hundred million won in 2023 (down 6.5% year over year), then rose to 34.7 hundred million won in 2024 (up 4.3% year over year) and 42.6 hundred million won in 2025 (up 22.9% year over year), increasing for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

Over 2022-2025, the year with the highest operating profit was 2025. The operating margin was 8.2% in 2022, 7.8% in 2023, 8.3% in 2024, and 9.8% in 2025, with 2025 the highest. While revenue grew 3.9%, operating profit grew 22.9%, so margin improvement drove the profit increase more strongly.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from 26.4 hundred million won in 2022 to 29.8 hundred million won in 2023 (up 12.6% year over year), 37.1 hundred million won in 2024 (up 24.5% year over year), and 39.5 hundred million won in 2025 (up 6.5% year over year), increasing for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was also reported as 26.4 hundred million won, 29.8 hundred million won, 37.1 hundred million won, and 39.5 hundred million won for the same years, the same values as net profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

Operating cash flow was -3.2 hundred million won in 2022, then 48.9 hundred million won in 2023, 43.4 hundred million won in 2024, and 55.1 hundred million won in 2025, positive in all three years since 2023.

On a quarterly basis, results improved from revenue of 105 hundred million won and operating profit of 2.9 hundred million won in the third quarter of 2025 to revenue of 111 hundred million won and operating profit of 10.8 hundred million won in the fourth quarter of 2025 (up 278.3% quarter over quarter).

The profit trend continued with revenue of 110 hundred million won and operating profit of 13.7 hundred million won in the first quarter of 2026, and revenue of 121 hundred million won and operating profit of 17.4 hundred million won in the second quarter of 2026.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue was only 107 hundred million won (down 12.3% quarter over quarter, up 1.8% year over year) and operating profit was only 8.5 hundred million won (down 51.0% quarter over quarter, up 198.7% year over year), and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was 3.2 hundred million won, a swing to a loss from a year earlier.

That operating profit stayed positive while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a loss means items below operating profit pulled down the period's profit significantly.

The sum of net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters (the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026) is 47.1 hundred million won.

Industry analysis

A 2021 research report described the domestic pet market as growing rapidly, and the company's pet-related sales growth is read as tied to that expansion. Growth in livestock environments and small-animal markets is another growth driver the company cites.

However, livestock breeding supply and disease outbreaks drive demand for veterinary medicines, so the cycle position is hard to describe with figures.

The company's strength is its KVGMP certification for injectables, liquids and additives, together with an EU GMP-certified production facility, which the 2021 report cited as a basis for its competitiveness as a contract manufacturer.

On competition, a 2018 report pointed to large corporates such as Lotte launching pet brands. Compared with the distribution scale of large companies, the 2021 report said Eagle Veterinary Technology chose to widen its sales network through direct channels and mid- to low-priced private-label products.

Its pet business relied heavily on distribution of imported premium pet feed as of 2018, which exposes it to raw material prices, exchange rates and import conditions. Certification-backed export competitiveness is the central variable in the veterinary medicine segment.

Bull factors

Earnings recovery

Controlling-shareholder net income rose three years in a row from 2023 through 2025, and operating profit rose two years in a row from 2024 through 2025. The operating margin of 9.8% in 2025 was the highest of 2022 to 2025. Operating profit of KRW 4.26 billion in 2025 was above the KRW 3.56 billion of 2022. The fact that profit improvement outpaced sales recovery is the bullish point here.

Financial headroom

The debt ratio fell from 27.7% in 2022 to 23.3% in 2025. Total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 51.9 billion and total liabilities were KRW 12.1 billion. The controlling-shareholder stake accounts for all of the KRW 51.9 billion of equity. A balance sheet with a light debt burden reads as headroom to absorb business swings.

Positive operating cash flow

Operating cash flow swung from a KRW 318 million deficit in 2022 to KRW 4.89 billion in 2023, then stayed positive at KRW 4.34 billion in 2024 and KRW 5.51 billion in 2025. The 2025 operating cash flow exceeded that year's operating profit of KRW 4.26 billion.

Cash generation that holds alongside profit can serve as a funding source for dividends and business investment.

Bear factors

Q3 2026 swing to loss

The controlling-shareholder result in Q3 2026 swung to a net loss of KRW 323 million from a year earlier. Operating profit in the same quarter of KRW 854 million fell 51.0% from the prior quarter. Compared with Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.74 billion, the quarterly peak was short-lived, which is the bearish point. The Q3 loss is also included in the four-quarter total of KRW 4.71 billion, which weighs on that figure.

Stalled sales

Sales went from KRW 43.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 43.3 billion in 2025, roughly flat over the period. Sales declined in 2023 and 2024, and the 3.9% rise in 2025 was a recovery after those years. Q3 2026 sales of KRW 10.7 billion rose only 1.8% from a year earlier. Limited top-line growth means profit gains have come mainly from profitability.

Small-cap size and concentrated ownership

The company is a small cap with a market value of about KRW 36.2 billion. The largest shareholder group's stake accounts for a sizable part of the shares, which affects the float. A high concentration like this can limit the float available for trading. Smaller companies can see sharp share-price reactions to earnings swings, which is another bearish factor.

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly operating profit moved sharply, from KRW 286 million in Q3 2025 to KRW 1.08 billion in Q4 2025 and KRW 1.74 billion in Q2 2026. It fell to KRW 854 million in Q3 2026 and the controlling-shareholder result turned to a loss. Large quarterly swings reduce confidence in full-year outlooks. Items below operating profit also move the quarterly result.

Business mix and competition

A 2018 report estimated that pet feed and feed additives made up more than 70% of sales at that time. A business built on imported premium pet feed is sensitive to raw material prices, exchange rates and import conditions. Entry by large corporates such as Lotte intensifies distribution competition. How far the new ventures will change this mix is not yet visible in disclosed figures.

Certification and production regulation

KVGMP certification for injectables, liquids and additives, together with the EU GMP-certified facility, underpins exports and contract manufacturing. Any problem in maintaining or renewing certification could directly affect production and exports.

Pharmaceutical manufacturing regulation also raises quality-control costs. Changes to this base feed directly into export sales and margins.
