02

Business structure

BGFecomaterials was founded in 1997 as a materials specialist, and the company groups its business into five areas: engineering materials, white bioplastics, plastic recycling, specialty chemicals centered on fluorine materials, and automotive and electronic materials.

Its engineering-materials line centers on compounds and alloys built on PA6/66, PP, PBT, mPPO and PC/ABS, and it also produces long-fiber reinforced thermoplastics.

Listed end markets include automotive interiors and exteriors, secondary batteries and ESS, electrical components, consumer electronics, and furniture and tools. The company says its electronics and semiconductor materials supply process materials for semiconductors, display panels, secondary batteries and solar power.

Its fluorine business produces fluorine-based core materials such as specialty gases used in semiconductors. FnGuide data for December 2024 show combined domestic sales at 68.5% and exports at 31.5%, with exports of semiconductor specialty gases at 35.4%.

According to the company, capacity is about 140,000 tons, and its overseas footprint began with a U.S. plant in 2015. As of September 2025 the consolidated group comprised 11 companies, including automotive-materials, plastic-recycling, industrial-gas and chip-manufacturing subsidiaries.

Peers in the same chemicals sector include LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions, SKC, Kumho Petro Chemical and Lotte Chemical, though their business scope and scale differ widely, which limits direct comparison.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 263.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 285.7 billion in 2023 (up 8.5%), KRW 364.3 billion in 2024 (up 27.5%) and KRW 397.9 billion in 2025 (up 9.2%). Revenue has grown for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 is the largest year of 2022–2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 15.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.2 billion in 2023 (up 8.0%), KRW 14 billion in 2024 (down 18.5%) and KRW 17.3 billion in 2025 (up 23.8%), with 2024 the smallest year of the period.

Operating margin was 6.0% in 2022, 6.0% in 2023, 3.8% in 2024 and 4.4% in 2025, so margin fell in 2024 even as sales grew, and partly recovered in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 28.9 billion in 2022, turned to a net loss of KRW 9.81 billion in 2023, returned to a profit of KRW 15.5 billion in 2024, and fell 9.8% to KRW 14 billion in 2025.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 10 billion in 2025, and the gap from the controlling-shareholder figure reflects non-controlling interests.

Operating cash flow was negative KRW 10.1 billion in 2022, then KRW 24.3 billion in 2023, KRW 12.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 26.9 billion in 2025, staying positive since 2023.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 100.8 billion in Q3 2025 with operating profit of KRW 5.79 billion, then fell to KRW 94.3 billion in Q4 2025 (down 6.5% quarter on quarter) with operating profit of KRW 615 million (down 89.4%).

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 92.8 billion (down 1.5%) and operating profit recovered to KRW 3.23 billion (up 424.9%). Revenue then rose to KRW 102.7 billion in Q2 2026 (up 10.6%) and KRW 104.4 billion in Q3 2026 (up 1.7% quarter on quarter, up 3.6% year on year), marking two consecutive quarters of growth.

Operating profit, however, was KRW 1.2 billion in Q2 2026 (down 62.8%) and KRW 4.14 billion in Q3 2026 (up 244.3% quarter on quarter, down 28.5% year on year). Q3 2026 also recorded a net loss of KRW 2.25 billion attributable to controlling shareholders, moving in the opposite direction from the operating profit.

Over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 11 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The company's demand comes from automotive, electrical and electronics, secondary batteries and ESS, and semiconductors and displays. Demand for battery and ESS materials and for electrical components is tied to the flow of automotive electrification and energy-storage investment.

The company presents semiconductors, display panels, secondary batteries and solar power as the core process-material areas of future industries.

In engineering plastics, competition turns on developing compounds tailored to customer requirements on the base of commodity and engineering resins such as PA6/66, PP and PBT. The company presents its formulation technology as a differentiator in automotive and electronics materials.

Its fluorine materials sit close to semiconductor and battery process demand, so volumes can shift with investment cycles in those industries.

By revenue scale, the company is smaller than large chemical groups such as LG Chem, Hanwha Solutions and Lotte Chemical, and is positioned as a mid-sized materials company focused on high-performance materials and specific customer groups.

Among listed peers, SKC, Kumho Petro Chemical, ISU Specialty Chemical, Foosung and Hankuk Carbon partly overlap or sit adjacent in business lines, but product ranges and revenue mixes differ. Domestic sales at 68.5% in 2024 indicate reliance on Korean customers, while exports at 31.5% show exposure to overseas demand. Where the sector stands in its cycle requires separate confirmation from industry supply and demand indicators.

Outlook

In August 2024 the company announced a semiconductor-materials plant in the Onsan Industrial Complex in Ulsan.

In September 2024 it decided to acquire a chip-manufacturing subsidiary, and FnGuide's history records the purchase of all of that company's shares in October 2024; the subsidiary is now part of the consolidated group.

In May 2023 the company announced the acquisition of two materials companies, including an automotive-materials firm. In July 2024 a third-party private placement issued 8,733,624 common shares, and the purpose of the issuance should be checked in the original disclosure.

Recycling and white bioplastics are areas the company describes as new, and whether they expand can be checked in the business section of the annual report. The start date of the Ulsan plant and its contribution to semiconductor-materials sales should be confirmed through company investor-relations materials.

Revenue rose for two consecutive quarters through Q3 2026, but operating profit fell year on year in Q3, so whether the acquisitions and new capacity translate into profit still needs confirmation.

The next regular filing, the 2026 annual report, will show the operating margin and debt-to-equity ratio as the main checkpoints.

Bull factors

Diversified end markets

The company lists automotive, electrical and electronics, secondary batteries and ESS, semiconductors and displays, and solar power as its application areas. A customer base not concentrated in one industry can spread the impact of a slowdown in any single sector. Fluorine products such as semiconductor specialty gases are tied to demand from advanced industries.

Earnings recovery

Operating profit rose 23.8% to KRW 17.3 billion in 2025, and operating margin improved from 3.8% in 2024 to 4.4%. Revenue grew for two consecutive quarters in Q2 and Q3 2026. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders also swung from a net loss in 2023 to a profit in 2024.

Cash generation and equity base

Cash has kept coming in from core operations since 2023 without interruption, which supports the company's cash-generating capacity. Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 499.7 billion, above total liabilities of KRW 282.1 billion. Equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 410.8 billion.

Bear factors

Volatile quarterly profit

Operating profit fell 89.4% quarter on quarter to KRW 615 million in Q4 2025. It dropped again to KRW 1.2 billion in Q2 2026 before reaching KRW 4.14 billion in Q3. Q3 2026 operating profit was down 28.5% from the year-earlier quarter.

Thin earnings power and net result

The Q3 2026 net loss of KRW 2.25 billion attributable to controlling shareholders ran against the operating profit in the same quarter. Controlling-shareholder profit fell 9.8% to KRW 14 billion in 2025. Operating margin of 4.4% in 2025 remained below the 6.0% of 2022 and 2023.

Rising debt burden

The debt-to-equity ratio rose from 30.6% in 2023 to 56.4% in 2025. Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 282.1 billion. If debt grows faster than profit recovers, the financial burden could amplify swings in results.

Risk factors

Acquisitions and subsidiary management

Acquisitions followed in 2023 and 2024, and the consolidated group comprised 11 companies as of September 2025. Profitability recovery at acquired businesses and the workload of managing subsidiaries could drive earnings swings. Profit contribution by subsidiary should be checked in the notes to the annual report.

Exports, currency and trade

Combined exports were 31.5% of sales in December 2024. Semiconductor specialty gases had a 35.4% export share, so they are exposed to overseas demand and trade conditions. Currency movements affect the won value of overseas revenue.

Major shareholder stake and free float

Major shareholders and related parties held 71.65% as of March 19, 2026, and treasury shares were 1.34%. A limited free float can amplify share-price volatility. A third-party private placement in 2024 increased the number of shares outstanding.

Overall view

BGFecomaterials has shown revenue growth for three straight years from 2023 and a 23.8% rise in operating profit in 2025, signaling a recovery in both scale and profit. Still, operating margin of 4.4% in 2025 remains below the 6.0% of 2022 and 2023, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders declined in 2025.

Revenue rose for two consecutive quarters through Q3 2026, but operating profit fell year on year and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders appeared in that quarter. The debt-to-equity ratio climbed from 30.6% in 2023 to 56.4% in 2025, raising the financial burden.

Operating cash flow has stayed positive since 2023, which confirms cash generation. The share price sits below book value per share, but profit volatility widens the range of interpretation for earnings-based measures.

The key variables are whether the acquired businesses recover profitability and whether the Ulsan semiconductor-materials plant adds to profit. The next checkpoint is the operating margin and debt-to-equity ratio in the 2026 annual report.
