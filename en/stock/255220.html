SG: Profit Quarters Return, Cumulative Losses Remain the Test

SG returned to an operating profit for 2025 and posted operating profits again in the second and third quarters of 2026, yet attributable net losses ran for three straight years and totaled KRW 15 billion over the last four quarters. The gap between operating profit and cumulative net results is the next point to track.

02

Business structure

SG was founded in 2009 and is headquartered in Incheon, South Korea. Its core product is asphalt concrete used in road pavement and parking lots, and the company also produces and sells ready-mixed concrete. Its products are supplied throughout Korea.

Asphalt concrete and ready-mixed concrete are materials whose demand moves with road works and residential and civil project schedules, so public civil engineering and building construction timing feed directly into revenue.

On the disclosed figures, revenue was KRW 89.9 billion in 2022, KRW 80.9 billion in 2023, KRW 118.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 102.2 billion in 2025, a wide swing across years.

Quarterly revenue also varied sharply, falling from KRW 30.9 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 8.95 billion in Q1 2026 before recovering to KRW 23 billion in Q2 and KRW 24.4 billion in Q3 2026. Peers in the same sector include SAMPYO Cement, HANIL CEMENTCO and IS Dongseo, which run cement or ready-mixed concrete operations.

Raw material price movements and how quickly they pass into contract costs are the key variables for profitability.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual figures, revenue fell from KRW 89.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 80.9 billion in 2023, a 9.9% decline, then rose to KRW 118.9 billion in 2024, a 47.0% increase, which was the largest year in the period, before dropping to KRW 102.2 billion in 2025, a 14.0% decline.

Operating profit moved from KRW 566 million in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 13.5 billion in 2023, which was a swing into loss, then narrowed to an operating loss of KRW 6.07 billion in 2024 and turned to an operating profit of KRW 752 million in 2025.

The operating margin was 0.6% in 2022, negative 16.6% in 2023, negative 5.1% in 2024 and 0.7% in 2025. Attributable net income was KRW 3.01 billion in 2022, then an attributable net loss of KRW 29.7 billion in 2023 and KRW 35.7 billion in 2024, before narrowing to an attributable net loss of KRW 2.15 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests is a separate figure and was a net loss of KRW 2.45 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow was positive at KRW 1.43 billion in 2022, KRW 13.1 billion in 2023 and KRW 18.6 billion in 2024, then turned to negative KRW 28.5 billion in 2025.

By quarter, Q4 2025 showed revenue of KRW 30.9 billion and an operating profit of KRW 3.36 billion, but the attributable net loss widened to KRW 6.26 billion. Q1 2026 marked the low point, with revenue of KRW 8.95 billion, an operating loss of KRW 6.04 billion and an attributable net loss of KRW 9.92 billion.

Q2 2026 showed revenue of KRW 23 billion, an operating profit of KRW 687 million and an attributable net income of KRW 1.34 billion, a swing to profit from the prior quarter. Q3 2026 showed revenue of KRW 24.4 billion, an operating profit of KRW 1.37 billion and an attributable net loss of KRW 200 million.

Where operating profit is positive but attributable net results are negative, items outside operations are part of the gap, and their breakdown should be checked in the periodic report. Attributable net losses over the last four quarters totaled KRW 15 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The construction sector is diverging by segment.

GS Engineering & Construction disclosed provisional third-quarter 2026 results on October 30, 2026, showing revenue in its building and housing division down 18.7% year on year amid a downturn in the real estate market, while its plant division revenue rose 34.6% as large domestic and overseas projects moved into full swing.

Samsung C&T's construction division disclosed provisional third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 277 billion on October 28, 2026, up 149.5% year on year, with the company citing the start of high-tech construction work as a driver.

The gap in profitability between firms weighted toward high-value plant and high-tech work and housing-focused firms appears to be widening.

SG is a construction materials company centered on asphalt concrete and ready-mixed concrete, so it connects indirectly to general contractors' project volumes, private housing starts and public civil works orders.

Compared with peers such as SAMPYO Cement, HANIL CEMENTCO and IS Dongseo, SG has a smaller scale and a narrower product mix. A prolonged housing slump could weigh on material demand, while a rise in civil and plant orders would change the demand path.

How quickly the sector trend reaches SG's revenue can be checked against its quarterly revenue trend in periodic reports.

Outlook

The fourth quarter of 2026 will be compared against Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 30.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.36 billion. In the 2026 annual report, the first checkpoint is whether the full-year operating margin improves from 0.7% in 2025.

The debt ratio fell to 119.6% in 2025 from 266.9% in 2023, and whether that trend holds will be checked in the next periodic report. The Q1 2027 results will show whether the Q1 2026 low point recurs.

Bull factors

Operating profit back in positive territory

Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 752 million, a turn to profit, and both the second and third quarters of 2026 were profitable at KRW 687 million and KRW 1.37 billion. Third-quarter operating profit rose 99.3% from the prior quarter. The operating margin improved from negative 5.1% in 2024 to 0.7% in 2025, in the same direction.

Revenue recovering from the Q1 2026 low

After a low of KRW 8.95 billion in Q1 2026, revenue rose to KRW 23 billion in Q2 and KRW 24.4 billion in Q3. Third-quarter revenue was up 10.9% year on year. Revenue increased in both the second and third quarters of 2026.

Lower debt ratio

The debt ratio fell from 266.9% in 2023 to 168.3% in 2024 and 119.6% in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 102.2 billion and total liabilities were KRW 122.2 billion. The ratio remains above the 158.0% recorded in 2022, but it has declined since the 2023 peak.

Bear factors

Cumulative net losses over four quarters

The attributable net loss of KRW 9.92 billion in Q1 2026 was the largest within that period. Q3 2026 narrowed the loss to KRW 200 million but did not produce a profit.

Operating cash flow turned negative

Operating cash flow moved from KRW 18.6 billion in 2024 to negative KRW 28.5 billion in 2025. Over the same period, revenue declined from KRW 118.9 billion to KRW 102.2 billion. Even with an operating profit, whether cash generation follows remains a weak point.

Large swings in quarterly revenue

Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 8.95 billion fell 71.0% from the prior quarter. In Q4 2025, revenue had risen 40.3% to KRW 30.9 billion. With quarterly swings this large, the data series is still short for judging how long profitable quarters will last.

Risk factors

Quarterly earnings volatility

Revenue and operating profit moved sharply by quarter with project volumes and site schedules. Q1 2026 posted an operating loss of KRW 6.04 billion before returning to profit in Q2. A gap in project volume in any single quarter could shorten the profitable stretch.

Raw material and cost pressure

Asphalt concrete and ready-mixed concrete are affected by raw material prices and transport costs. If price increases cannot be passed through to contract prices quickly, the operating margin can come under pressure. An operating margin of 0.7% in 2025 leaves a relatively thin cushion against such pressure.

Capital base and cash flow

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 102.2 billion, below the KRW 105.5 billion attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 122.2 billion. Operating cash flow was negative KRW 28.5 billion in 2025.

During periods of accumulated losses, borrowing capacity and working capital management can matter more than reported profit.

Overall view

SG returned to an operating profit for 2025 and posted operating profits in the second and third quarters of 2026, while revenue increased in both the second and third quarters.

However, the company recorded losses for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and attributable net losses totaled KRW 15 billion over the last four quarters. Operating cash flow turned negative at KRW 28.5 billion in 2025.

Positive factors are the return to operating profit and the revenue recovery, while negative factors are cumulative net losses, weaker cash flow and quarterly volatility.
