02

Business structure

Deutsch Motors is an import car dealer centered on new BMW and MINI sales, certified pre-owned car sales and after-sales service. According to the third-quarter 2025 quarterly report, the parent company Deutsch Motors Co., Ltd. accounted for 66.39% of consolidated revenue.

In the same report, vehicle sales in the BMW business unit were listed at 74.33% of the parent's main product revenue. The parent sold 15,268 new cars and 2,770 certified pre-owned cars, equal to 20.70% and 36.22% of total domestic sales in those categories.

The parent operates 12 BMW, 6 MINI and 3 used-car showrooms along with 15 service centers. As of 2020, revenue by segment was 80% BMW and MINI, 12% Porsche dealer Deutsch Auto, 2% Jaguar Land Rover dealer British Auto, 5% used-car complex Deutsch Autoworld and 1% auto finance Deutsch Financial.

British Auto, which ran the Jaguar Land Rover business, ended operations at the end of June 2026 as the company refocused on profitability.

The BYD business, run by the subsidiary DT Networks, reported preliminary first-half 2026 sales of KRW 144.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 4.3 billion (announced July 27, 2026).

As of 2020, the company held a 22% share of the domestic BMW market and 16% of the Porsche market, which was viewed as establishing it as a leading dealer for both brands.

Press coverage has noted that most operating profit still comes from the existing dealerships, including the BMW and MINI parent business and the Porsche dealer Deutsch Auto.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results filed with DART show revenue rising from KRW 1.96 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.2 trillion in 2023 (up 12.2%), easing to KRW 2.17 trillion in 2024 (down 1.3%) and reaching KRW 2.55 trillion in 2025 (up 17.6%), the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 range. Profit moved in the opposite direction.

Operating profit fell from KRW 64.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 42.8 billion in 2023 (down 34.0%) and KRW 25 billion in 2024 (down 41.5%), then recovered to KRW 40.8 billion in 2025 (up 62.9%), still below the 2022 level. Operating margin was 3.3% in 2022, 1.9% in 2023, 1.2% in 2024 and 1.6% in 2025, staying at low levels.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 36.4 billion in 2022 and fell to KRW 11.5 billion in 2023 (down 68.3%). It turned into a net loss of KRW 4.89 billion in 2024, a swing into deficit, and returned to a profit of KRW 3.42 billion in 2025, a swing back to surplus.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 7.66 billion in 2024 and net income of KRW 1.71 billion in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 221.4% in 2022 to 336.3% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 1.27 trillion and total equity was KRW 376.3 billion, of which KRW 371.2 billion belonged to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow was KRW 34.6 billion in 2022, -KRW 784 million in 2023, -KRW 82 billion in 2024 and KRW 181.5 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating profit was KRW 6.33 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 12.8 billion in the fourth quarter (up 102.0% from the prior quarter), KRW 10.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 18.3%), KRW 12.9 billion in the second quarter and KRW 11.8 billion in the third quarter.

The preliminary first-quarter 2026 results, announced April 27, 2026, fully expensed additional tax payable from a combined tax audit covering 2020 to 2024, which the company described as a one-off cost.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.71 billion in the second quarter and KRW 280 million in the third quarter of 2026, and the four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026 total KRW 2.67 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The domestic import car market is growing, led by electric vehicles, according to an October 2026 press report. In the same report, strong BYD sales were cited as one driver of the company's improved results.

By contrast, the company said in its July 27, 2026 announcement that competition among import brands intensified in the second quarter of 2026 and economic uncertainty grew. The company attributes the slowdown in combustion-engine car demand to the fast growth of the BYD business, which offset it.

As of 2020, a 22% share of the domestic BMW market and 16% of the Porsche market were cited in a securities firm report as evidence that the company had become a leading dealer. A 2019 technical analysis report also placed the company among the top BMW dealers, with a 24.1% share.

These figures are old, so they are limited as a guide to current share. Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 rose from a year earlier, which reads as the company holding profit despite a weaker industry backdrop.

06

Outlook

In its corporate value-up plan disclosed on March 27, 2026, the company said it will continue a three-year medium-term shareholder return policy covering fiscal years 2024 to 2026.

Under the plan, the company aims to keep annual dividends per share at or above the prior year's level even if profit declines, and to continue buying back and cancelling shares until the price-to-book ratio reaches about 1x.

The plan also sets a target of buying additional shares and cancelling them if the shareholder return ratio through dividends falls short of 50% of consolidated net income.

The board's October 26, 2026 decision to cancel 1,030,223 treasury shares is an example of the plan being carried out, and the change in listed shares took effect on November 17, 2026. On the BYD business, the chief executive said 2026 would be the year its results become clearly visible.

Operations at British Auto, which ran the Jaguar Land Rover business, ended at the end of June 2026, and the company described this as a refocus on profitability. In July 2026, the chief executive said the company would complete a stable and sustainable business structure within 2026.

The next checkpoint is whether a successor policy is disclosed when the 2024 to 2026 medium-term policy ends. The scale of buybacks, cancellations and dividends will depend on how earnings develop.

Industry slowdown and weaker third-quarter profit

Risk factors

Tax and one-off costs

In the first quarter of 2026, additional tax payable from a combined tax audit covering 2020 to 2024 was fully expensed. The company described this as resolving past tax risk. Because the charge was one-off, quarterly profit comparisons can be distorted.

New business profitability and guarantee burden

The BYD business's turn to profit is still small, with preliminary first-half 2026 operating profit of KRW 4.3 billion. Press coverage has pointed to the guarantee exposure on BYD dealerships as a financial burden. If the new business does not grow its profit, the recovery in overall margins will be limited.

Funding and continuity of shareholder returns

The company set a target to keep dividends per share at or above the prior year's level even if profit declines. The 2024 to 2026 medium-term policy ends with fiscal year 2026. Dividends and share cancellations rely on operating cash flow and profit, so weaker cash flow could change the scale of the policy.

Overall view

Deutsch Motors combines a BMW and MINI-centered import car dealership business with the new BYD business. Annual revenue reached KRW 2.55 trillion in 2025, the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 range.

Operating profit, however, was KRW 40.8 billion, below the KRW 64.8 billion of 2022, and the operating margin stayed at 1.6%. The debt ratio was 336.3% in 2025, higher than in 2022.

Quarterly operating profit in 2026 ranged between KRW 10.4 billion and KRW 12.9 billion, and third-quarter profit rose 85.6% from a year earlier. The BYD business posted its first quarterly profit since its founding in the first quarter of 2026, with preliminary first-half operating profit of KRW 4.3 billion.

On the other side, profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled only KRW 2.67 billion over the last four quarters. On shareholder returns, the company has disclosed share cancellations of 1,030,223 shares and a target of about 1x price-to-book. The next filing will show whether the operating margin improves and whether the BYD business stays profitable.
