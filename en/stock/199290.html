Business structure

BIO Protech listed on the KONEX market on December 8, 2016 and is classified as a medical device manufacturer. Its headquarters are in Wonju, in Korea's Gangwon Special Self-Governing Province. The product range covers electrosurgical smoke evacuators, surgical pencils, ECG electrodes and SpO2 sensors.

In pain management, it offers electrodes for transcutaneous nerve stimulators as well as muscle-stimulation devices. Most of these products are single-use electrodes and consumables that touch the patient's skin, so demand tends to follow hospital usage.

A segment-by-segment revenue breakdown could not be confirmed in our research, so no figures are given. In 2024 the company carried out national R&D projects on medical hydrogel, electrically conductive adhesives and conductive polymers.

Handling the adhesive and hydrogel materials in-house is one pillar of its product competitiveness. As of December 31, 2024, the largest reported holder was Metistone Growth PEF, with a 28.83% stake.

The competitive landscape appears to include global medical-consumables majors and domestic small caps, but no market-share data could be verified.

Although the company is tagged as electrical equipment in our classification, its filings describe a medical device business, so direct comparison with power-equipment peers is of limited use.

Products are spread across ECG electrodes, nerve-stimulator electrodes, electrosurgical consumables and pain-management devices, so reliance on any single product is relatively lower. The company has a record of national R&D projects on core materials such as hydrogel and conductive adhesives. Patent publications continued into 2026.

Investor outreach and governance process

Filings show an investor presentation announced on October 20, 2025 and its results reported on November 6. Operating profit of KRW 1.29951 billion against revenue of KRW 31.1 billion is small in absolute terms. The 501.5% growth rate reflects a low base year, so sustainability must be confirmed by later results. Price competition with larger players is a standing burden in medical consumables.

Company Monitor shows both book value per share and price-to-book as negative. The conversion of redeemable preferred shares created 507,935 new common shares. Amended convertible-bond decision filings have also recurred. A larger stock of convertible securities can dilute existing holders.

Thin trading and sharp price swings

Overall view

BIO Protech is a KONEX-listed maker of medical consumables such as ECG electrodes and nerve-stimulator electrodes.
