Report overview

Insurance Margin Recovery Meets Investment Headwinds

Long-term insurance underwriting has rebounded through the third quarter of 2026, but annual net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked in 2024, fell in 2025, and the debt ratio has risen every year since 2022.

  1. 1

    Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 99.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 177.0% from a year earlier but down 16.3% from the prior quarter.

  2. 2

    The preliminary results released on November 11, 2026 pointed to improved long-term insurance claims experience as the main driver of higher insurance service profit.

  3. 3

    Investment income fell 38.3% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, with rate-driven valuation losses cited as the main cause.

  4. 4

    On an annual basis, 2024 was the largest year for net income attributable to controlling shareholders at KRW 343 billion, while 2025 fell 12.8% to KRW 299 billion.

  5. 5

    The debt ratio climbed from 431.5% in 2022 to 656.9% in 2025, and dividends resumed for fiscal 2023 were halted within a year.

02

Business structure

Hanwha General Insurance is a non-life insurer whose operations centre on long-term, auto and general insurance. Long-term insurance profit rose 103% to KRW 136.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026 and accounted for most of the increase in insurance service profit.

Auto insurance still posted a loss of KRW 9.7 billion, though it narrowed, while general insurance returned to a profit of KRW 5.1 billion. Higher-value products such as women's insurance were reported as supporting new contract growth in long-term lines.

The company completed the absorption merger of the auto-focused insurer Carrot at the end of 2025. Contractual service margin (CSM) held on the balance sheet reached KRW 4.62 trillion at the end of September 2026, up 8.5% year on year.

NH Investment & Securities analyzed that 2025 new CSM from guaranteed products grew 38.9%, well ahead of the top-tier peers Samsung, DB and Hyundai Marine.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual operating profit was KRW 204.8 billion in 2022, KRW 306.5 billion in 2023 (up 49.6% year on year) and KRW 435.7 billion in 2024 (up 42.2%), before easing 1.4% to KRW 429.6 billion in 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 222.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 247.2 billion in 2023 (up 11.3%) and KRW 343 billion in 2024 (up 38.7%), then fell 12.8% to KRW 299 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests, which is a different figure, was KRW 187.3 billion, KRW 212.8 billion, KRW 316 billion and KRW 292.3 billion over the same years.

In October 2025, BNK Investment & Securities had pointed to a wider auto insurance loss and lower general insurance profit as reasons to trim its net income estimate.

Quarterly operating profit rose from KRW 63.9 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 86.9 billion in the fourth quarter (up 36.1% quarter on quarter) and KRW 135.9 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 56.4%), then reached KRW 140.8 billion in the second quarter and KRW 120.7 billion in the third quarter (down 14.2% quarter on quarter, up 89.1% year on year).

Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 36 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 99.7 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and the last four quarters totalled KRW 382.9 billion.

Per the preliminary third-quarter release, insurance service profit rose on better long-term claims experience, adding KRW 69.4 billion year on year, while investment income fell 38.3% to KRW 96.2 billion on rate-driven valuation losses.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 1.58 trillion in 2023 and KRW 1.49 trillion in 2024 to KRW 668.3 billion in 2025. At end-2025, total liabilities were KRW 17.8 trillion against total equity of KRW 2.71 trillion, so the debt ratio rising from 431.5% to 656.9% means liabilities grew faster than equity.

05

Industry analysis

Third-quarter 2026 results across insurers diverged, as investment income weakened amid financial market volatility while insurance underwriting profit determined the gap. Samsung Life Insurance's third-quarter consolidated net income rose 8.3% year on year, while Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance's fell 8.3%.

In auto insurance, the view from NH Investment & Securities is that improvement would show from 2027 once premium rates normalize and scale benefits materialize.

For long-term insurance, BNK Investment & Securities said in April 2026 that a recovery is hard to come by, but that market share gains since 2022, backed by capital raising, are a positive.

On capital, the K-ICS ratio at end-September 2026 was estimated at 186% before transitional measures and 231% after them, well above the regulatory guideline of 130%. SK Securities said in May 2025 that a conservative approach is needed until the discount-rate reform concludes in 2027.

NH Investment & Securities noted that distributable profit is still absent, but that dividends could follow if the surrender-refund reserve rules are eased.

06

Outlook

NH Investment & Securities expects CSM growth in 2026 to exceed the industry average. New CSM in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 306.6 billion, up 8.0%, and the nine-month total reached KRW 936.2 billion, up 27.4%, per the November 11, 2026 preliminary release.

Average monthly guaranteed new business fell 8.9% to KRW 7.0 billion in the same quarter, while the new business value multiple rose from 12.4x to 14.7x. Auto insurance is expected to stay weak for a time after the Carrot merger, with improvement from 2027 in NH's view.

Dividend resumption is conditional on the surrender-refund reserve rules being eased, so regulatory change is the key confirmation point for shareholder returns.

The 2026 annual report will confirm full-year operating profit, controlling-shareholder net income, the debt ratio and the dividend decision on an audited basis.

On a quarterly basis, the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results will show whether improved long-term claims experience persists and whether investment income recovers.

08

Bull factors

Recovery in insurance underwriting

Insurance service profit in the third quarter of 2026 reached KRW 132.1 billion, up 196.7% year on year on a preliminary basis. Improved long-term claims experience added KRW 69.4 billion to the figure. Long-term insurance profit rose 103% to KRW 136.7 billion, and auto and general insurance also improved.

Expanding CSM base

Contractual service margin held at the end of September 2026 reached KRW 4.62 trillion, up 8.5% year on year. New CSM in the third quarter was KRW 306.6 billion, up 8.0%. NH Investment & Securities analyzed that 2025 new guaranteed-product CSM grew 38.9%.

Capital adequacy headroom

The K-ICS ratio at end-September 2026 was estimated at 186% before transitional measures and 231% after them. Both figures are well above the 130% regulatory guideline. The end-2025 ratio before transitional measures was 174.4%.

09

Bear factors

Investment income headwind

Investment income in the third quarter of 2026 fell 38.3% to KRW 96.2 billion. Interest income rose, but disposal and valuation gains and losses worsened by KRW 60.7 billion on rising rates. If this drag continues, gains in underwriting could be offset.

Persistent auto insurance losses

Auto insurance cumulative underwriting losses widened from KRW 26.3 billion to KRW 53.5 billion year on year through the third quarter. The line remained in loss in the third quarter of 2026. Controlling-shareholder net income fell 12.8% in 2025.

Dividend gap and leverage

The debt ratio rose from 431.5% in 2022 to 656.9% in 2025. NH Investment & Securities noted that distributable profit is still absent. Dividends were halted within a year of resumption, so the timing of any return to payouts depends on regulatory change.

10

Risk factors

Interest rates and investment income

Rising rates can worsen disposal and valuation results on invested assets and swing quarterly earnings. Even with better underwriting, a fall in investment income limits the gain in total net income. Earnings volatility rises with the direction of interest rates.

Regulation

SK Securities argued in May 2025 that a conservative approach is needed until the discount-rate reform concludes in 2027. The surrender-refund reserve rules also determine distributable profit. Changes to these rules would directly affect capital ratios and the timing of dividend resumption.

Auto insurance and loss ratio

Auto insurance losses narrowed but continued in the third quarter of 2026. Following the Carrot merger, auto insurance earnings are expected to stay weak for now. If premium normalization is delayed, the expected improvement from 2027 could also slip.

Overall view

Hanwha General Insurance's third-quarter 2026 disclosed results show a clear recovery in long-term insurance underwriting. Controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 99.7 billion, up 177.0% year on year, and the last four quarters total KRW 382.9 billion.

Annually, however, 2024 was the largest year at KRW 343 billion, and 2025 fell 12.8% to KRW 299 billion. Investment income declined on higher rates, and auto insurance remains in loss. On the balance sheet, the debt ratio rose to 656.9% at end-2025, and dividends were halted within a year of resumption.

Strengths cited include CSM growth and a K-ICS ratio of 186% before transitional measures at end-September 2026. The next confirmation points are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results, the 2026 annual report, and regulatory changes affecting auto insurance and dividends.
