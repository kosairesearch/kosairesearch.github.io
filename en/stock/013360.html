02

Business structure

Ilsung Construction, listed on KOSPI on December 2, 1989, is a housing and civil engineering company. It is a mid-sized builder that constructs apartments and public buildings for redevelopment associations, Korea Land and Housing Corporation (LH) and educational institutions.

The Hwarang Housing small-scale redevelopment contract in Guro-gu, Seoul, worth KRW 54.7 billion, was disclosed on October 26, 2026, with no advance payment and payments made as construction progresses. A KRW 32.7 billion main building project at Eulji University's Daejeon campus was disclosed on August 10, 2026.

A KRW 99.4 billion LH contract for the Namyangju Wangsuk 2 A-3BL apartment project, Lot 1, was signed on December 8, 2025. The three disclosed contracts equal 12.07%, 7.21% and 19.87% of the latest annual revenue, so individual projects carry a large weight in annual sales.

On August 12, 2026, a Sosa-bon-dong street-home redevelopment contract signed in March 2024, worth about KRW 47.9 billion, was terminated by the counterparty, and the company said the contract caused no financial loss. Segment revenue shares are a point to confirm in the business report.

In competition, the company bids in the same markets as large builders such as Samsung C&T, Hyundai Engineering & Construction and DL E&C, holding a mid-sized position. As of the March 31, 2026 general meeting, the board had four directors, one of whom was an outside director, a 25% ratio.

04

Earnings analysis

In the annual results disclosed to regulators, revenue rose from KRW 462.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 607.7 billion in 2023 (up 31.4%), then fell two years in a row to KRW 500.4 billion in 2024 (down 17.7%) and KRW 452.7 billion in 2025 (down 9.5%). 2023 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025, and 2025 was the smallest.

Operating profit grew from KRW 2.72 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.27 billion in 2023 (up 166.9%), but turned into an operating loss of KRW 43.6 billion in 2024. It returned to operating profit of KRW 10.4 billion in 2025, and operating margins were 0.6%, 1.2%, -8.7% and 2.3% from 2022 to 2025.

The company cited improved business profitability as the main driver of the 2025 turnaround.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 7.23 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.08 billion in 2023 (down 43.6%), then posted a net loss of KRW 57.5 billion in 2024 before returning to a profit of KRW 3.74 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 3.75 billion in 2025, a different figure from the controlling-shareholder amount.

In quarterly results, revenue went from KRW 110.9 billion in 3Q 2025 and KRW 112.5 billion in 4Q 2025 (up 1.5% quarter on quarter) to KRW 84.6 billion in 1Q 2026 (down 24.8%), then KRW 107.4 billion in 2Q 2026 (up 26.8%) and KRW 92 billion in 3Q 2026 (down 14.3% quarter on quarter and down 17.1% year on year).

Operating profit reached KRW 3.72 billion in 2Q 2026 (up 127.0% quarter on quarter) and KRW 3.46 billion in 3Q 2026 (up 97.2% year on year), both above KRW 1.85 billion in 4Q 2025.

Yet the company posted a net loss of KRW 540 million attributable to controlling shareholders in 3Q 2026, a swing into loss after a profit in 2Q 2026, so the gap between operating and net results stands out. The source of that gap is a point to confirm in the non-operating items of the quarterly report.

Controlling-shareholder net profit for the last four quarters, from 4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026, totals KRW 3.41 billion. The debt ratio at year-end 2025 was 415.7%, with total liabilities of KRW 305.6 billion and total equity of KRW 73.5 billion. That is lower than 454.4% in 2024 but above 243.0% in 2022.

Operating cash flow was -KRW 25.6 billion in 2022, KRW 18 billion in 2023, -KRW 26.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.8 billion in 2025, changing direction almost every year.

05

Industry analysis

The construction cycle is driven by two forces: the timing of housing supply and public and industrial infrastructure orders.

In a July 2026 report, Shinhan Investment Corp. analyst Kim Seon-mi forecast that the government's semiconductor mega-project would lift orders for chip plants, power infrastructure and data centers, and increase new contract wins across the construction sector.

The same report said the housing segment's recovery could be slow in the short term, while advanced-industry infrastructure orders could become a medium- to long-term growth driver.

Ilsung Construction rose nearly 30% intraday in early July 2026 alongside other small and mid-sized builders on this expectation, but at that time the company had not directly won or confirmed participation in the project.

On the housing side, a April 2026 report described the 686-unit A3 block of the Namyangju Wangsuk 2 district as a complex ahead of sale, with Ilsung reported as the builder. The same report noted that higher construction costs were being reflected in sale prices.

Disclosed contracts ranging from KRW 32.7 billion to KRW 99.4 billion place the company in a mid-sized position relative to large builders.

A financial information site counted 11 sharp price-surge events in the small and mid-sized construction theme over the past year, reflecting how sensitive the stock has been to policy announcements, contract disclosures and theme rallies.

Outlook

Among the disclosed contracts, the following are expected to generate revenue: the Hwarang Housing small-scale redevelopment (KRW 54.7 billion, 27 months from actual construction start), the Eulji University Daejeon main building (KRW 32.7 billion, contract period through January 9, 2029), and Namyangju Wangsuk 2 A-3BL Lot 1 (KRW 99.4 billion, signed on December 8, 2025).

The Hwarang contract has no confirmed actual start date, so no end date is specified, and it carries no advance or down payment, so cash comes in as work progresses. The company noted that contract amounts and construction periods may change under the terms of the construction contract.

The Sosa-bon-dong street-home project terminated in August 2026, worth about KRW 47.9 billion, is a factor reducing the order backlog.

The company received a notice of designation as an unfaithful disclosure filer on November 4, 2026 over the late termination disclosure, and the final designation decision is the next checkpoint.

The 2025 annual preliminary results were disclosed on February 27, 2026, so the 2026 annual results are likely to be disclosed around a similar time. The next periodic report is the 2026 business report, where whether the annual operating margin exceeds 2.3% from 2025 is the key checkpoint.

Whether the debt ratio declines from 415.7% in 2025 and whether operating cash flow holds near the 2025 level of KRW 14.8 billion are also indicators to track.

On the semiconductor and AI projects, reporting said that once government plans take shape and orders for industrial complexes and infrastructure gather pace, attention would turn to the company's success in winning additional contracts.

In housing, the timing of sales in the third-generation new towns and how construction cost increases are reflected in prices are the points to track.

Whether the improved business profitability cited as the driver of the earnings improvement holds in later quarters is the next point to check.

Consecutive Large Public and Redevelopment Contracts

The company signed a KRW 99.4 billion LH contract in December 2025, then disclosed a KRW 32.7 billion contract in August 2026 and a KRW 54.7 billion contract in October 2026. Each disclosed contract equals roughly 7% to 20% of annual revenue, so individual awards add visibility to future revenue.

Expectations From Semiconductor and AI Infrastructure Orders

Shinhan Investment Corp. analyst Kim Seon-mi expects investment in chip plants, power infrastructure and data centers to lift new construction orders. The same report views these orders as a medium- to long-term growth driver. The timing of concrete project orders has not been set.

Revenue peaked at KRW 607.7 billion in 2023 and then fell to KRW 500.4 billion in 2024 (down 17.7%) and KRW 452.7 billion in 2025 (down 9.5%). Revenue of KRW 84.6 billion in 1Q 2026 was down 24.8% quarter on quarter, and KRW 92 billion in 3Q 2026 was down 17.1% year on year. Operating cash flow was -KRW 26.4 billion in 2024 and improved to KRW 14.8 billion in 2025, but its direction changed from year to year.

Gap Between Operating and Net Results

Operating profit was KRW 3.46 billion in 3Q 2026, yet net profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a loss of KRW 540 million. Controlling-shareholder net profit for the last four quarters totals KRW 3.41 billion, a small amount on a quarterly basis. Why operating gains are not reaching net profit is a point to check in the non-operating and cost items.

Risk factors

Disclosure and Governance Risk

The Korea Exchange gave notice of designation as an unfaithful disclosure filer over a contract termination that occurred on August 12, 2026 and was disclosed late on November 3, 2026. The cumulative penalty points for the past year are zero.

If penalty points reach 10 or more, a listing qualification review could be triggered, although this is conditional and not a current review. The deadline for objections was November 13, 2026, and the final decision will be disclosed by the exchange. The outside director ratio is 25%.

Balance Sheet Risk

Total liabilities at year-end 2025 were KRW 305.6 billion, more than four times total equity of KRW 73.5 billion. The debt ratio of 415.7% is well above 243.0% in 2022.

Operating cash flow swung from -KRW 26.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 14.8 billion in 2025, so funding needs can vary with the timing of payments on projects.

Order Concentration and Market Risk

Recent disclosed contracts are concentrated in LH-ordered apartments and redevelopment projects, so client and project types may be concentrated. Shinhan Investment Corp. noted that the housing segment's recovery could be slow in the short term.

Orders from semiconductor and AI infrastructure depend on when government plans take concrete shape, so order flow carries continued uncertainty.

Overall view

Ilsung Construction swung from an operating loss of KRW 43.6 billion in 2024 to operating profit of KRW 10.4 billion in 2025, and quarterly operating profit stayed at KRW 3.72 billion in 2Q 2026 and KRW 3.46 billion in 3Q 2026.

Revenue, however, has declined for two straight years since peaking at KRW 607.7 billion in 2023, and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 540 million in 3Q 2026.

On the financial side, the debt ratio at year-end 2025 was 415.7%, with total liabilities of KRW 305.6 billion, so the debt burden remains heavy.

On orders, contracts of KRW 99.4 billion in December 2025, KRW 32.7 billion in August 2026 and KRW 54.7 billion in October 2026 were disclosed, but a termination of about KRW 47.9 billion in August 2026 and a late disclosure led to a notice of designation as an unfaithful disclosure filer, raising disclosure-process risk.

On the industry side, expectations for medium- to long-term orders from semiconductor and AI infrastructure contrast with concerns that the housing recovery could be slow in the short term.

On valuation, the price-to-earnings multiple is high relative to controlling-shareholder net profit of KRW 3.41 billion over the last four quarters, and the price sits below the value of book net assets.

The next checkpoints are the final designation decision by the Korea Exchange, the 2026 annual preliminary results, and the operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 business report.
