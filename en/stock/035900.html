02

Business structure

JYP Ent. earns revenue from records and streaming, concerts, merchandise (MD), and advertising. According to press reports of the provisional disclosure on November 11, 2026, music streaming revenue in 3Q rose 48% to KRW 19.8 billion, reflecting higher overseas streaming use and a reclassification of YouTube revenue.

Concert revenue in the same quarter fell 31.1% to KRW 43.6 billion, mainly because the prior-year quarter included many large overseas shows. Advertising revenue rose 25% to KRW 16 billion, a record.

Domestic revenue increased 29.5% to KRW 131.1 billion. 3Q concert revenue reflected 36 shows, including Stray Kids' concerts in Korea and Japan and shows by DAY6, TWICE, ITZY and NMIXX.

NH Investment & Securities said in a November 12, 2026 report that the new Stray Kids album drove record-level record sales growth, with 3Q album sales topping 4 million copies.

The artist roster includes Stray Kids, TWICE, DAY6, ITZY, NMIXX, KickFlip and NEXZ, so the success of a single group heavily shapes quarterly results. In the same sector, JYP Ent. competes with Hybe, SM Entertainment and YG Entertainment for artist lineups and global fandom.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue rising for three straight years while profitability margins declined. Revenue went from KRW 345.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 566.5 billion in 2023 (up 63.8% year on year), KRW 601.8 billion in 2024 (up 6.2%), and KRW 821.9 billion in 2025 (up 36.6%).

Revenue rose in each year from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 96.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 169.4 billion in 2023 (up 75.4%), fell to KRW 128.3 billion in 2024 (down 24.3%), and recovered to KRW 155.2 billion in 2025 (up 21.0%). 2023 was the largest operating profit year of the period.

The operating margin moved from 27.9% in 2022 and 29.9% in 2023 to 21.3% in 2024 and 18.9% in 2025. In 2025 revenue grew 36.6% but operating profit grew only 21.0%, so profit did not keep pace with sales.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 67.5 billion in 2022, KRW 105.5 billion in 2023 (up 56.3%), KRW 97.8 billion in 2024 (down 7.3%), and KRW 160.6 billion in 2025 (up 64.2%). Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 160.6 billion in 2025, as disclosed.

Quarterly results are volatile. Operating profit was KRW 40.8 billion in 3Q 2025, KRW 41.9 billion in 4Q 2025, KRW 33.4 billion in 1Q 2026 and KRW 31 billion in 2Q 2026, before reaching KRW 48 billion in 3Q 2026. Revenue of KRW 267.5 billion in 3Q 2026 rose 46.0% from the prior quarter, and operating profit rose 54.8%.

Per provisional reports, 3Q operating profit beat a market estimate of KRW 40.2 billion by 19.2%, and the operating margin was 17.9%.

NH Investment & Securities said that a quarter with large low-margin domestic concert revenue still held company-wide profitability because high-margin sales from the core IP performed well.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 32 billion in 1Q 2026, KRW 21.8 billion in 2Q 2026 and KRW 24.5 billion in 3Q 2026, down 9.7% year on year in 3Q.

Operating profit rose while controlling-shareholder net income fell, so the gap between the operating and net profit lines is a point to check in the next periodic report. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 106 billion over the last four quarters, from 4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026.

05

Industry analysis

The entertainment sector's record and concert demand moves sharply from quarter to quarter with artists' activity schedules. Reports around the time of the 3Q release said investor sentiment across the entertainment sector had weakened.

In a November 12, 2026 report, Entermedia analyzed that core artists' future activities had become more uncertain and that the growth of next-generation IPs had fallen short of expectations.

NH Investment & Securities noted that four major domestic entertainment companies plan to debut new boy groups in the first half of 2027, and said expectations can translate into growth only if rookie results are proven in numbers. The industry as a whole has entered a rookie IP competition.

The same report pointed to rising Western-centered fandom indicators despite the group's ninth year since debut. Against Hybe, SM Entertainment and YG Entertainment, securities analysts cite expanding global fandom as JYP Ent.'s distinguishing factor.

06

Outlook

The most concrete near-term checkpoint is Stray Kids' tour in 2Q 2027. NH Investment & Securities said a stadium tour across Japan, North America and Europe begins in 2Q 2027, with 46 shows in total, a larger scale than the previous tour. The same report expects growth in both revenue and profitability from the tour.

On member enlistment, the report noted that a full-group tour schedule has been confirmed after member Lino's enlistment, and said the earnings gap could be limited even if the remaining members enlist in sequence. In new IPs, the boy group oftheblue makes its official debut on January 11, 2027.

Pre-orders for the limited first-press edition of its debut album run from November 12 to 25, 2026, and a pre-release track was released on November 11. NH Investment & Securities cited the addition of new fifth-generation IPs as a growth factor.

Whether the new IPs convert into album sales and tour revenue is the key variable for earnings after 2027. When the 2026 annual report discloses full-year 2026 revenue and operating margin, the figures can be compared with the 18.9% margin of 2025.

08

Bull factors

Stray Kids global tour

NH Investment & Securities noted that a Japan, North America and Europe stadium tour starting in 2Q 2027 will be larger than the previous tour, with 46 shows. The same report cited growth expectations for both revenue and profitability from the tour. It also pointed to 3Q album sales topping 4 million copies.

Growth in non-concert revenue

Per press reports of the November 11 provisional disclosure, 3Q advertising revenue rose 25% to KRW 16 billion, a record, and music streaming revenue rose 48% to KRW 19.8 billion. Higher overseas streaming use was cited as a driver. In a quarter when concert revenue fell, records, merchandise and advertising supported total sales.

Rookie and new IP pipeline

Boy group oftheblue makes its official debut on January 11, 2027, with first-press limited album pre-orders running from November 12 to 25, 2026. In January 2026, KickFlip was named to a list of artists to watch for 2026 in the K-pop category by Apple Music and Shazam.

Adding new IPs could spread out an earnings structure concentrated in specific IPs, so performance after the debut is the next checkpoint.

09

Bear factors

Dependence on core IPs

In a November 12, 2026 report, Entermedia pointed to a revenue structure concentrated on specific artists. The future activities of core artists have become more uncertain, and next-generation IPs have not yet grown fast enough to fill the gap, according to the same coverage.

Declining operating margin

The operating margin fell from 29.9% in 2023 to 21.3% in 2024 and 18.9% in 2025. In 2025 revenue grew 36.6% but operating profit grew only 21.0%. NH Investment & Securities said 3Q also reflected a large amount of low-margin domestic concert revenue.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 31.9% from KRW 32 billion in 1Q 2026 to KRW 21.8 billion in 2Q 2026. 3Q operating profit rose 17.6% year on year, while controlling-shareholder net income fell 9.7%.

Securities firms cut target prices before the results and mostly kept their outlooks afterward, which reflects uncertainty in earnings estimates.

10

Risk factors

Artist activity and member changes

Gaps in the activities of core IPs directly affect earnings. NH Investment & Securities referred to a scenario in which members enlist in sequence. A full-group tour schedule has been confirmed, which could limit any gap, but changes in individual members' activities can still affect album sales and concert plans.

Sector sentiment

Reports said investor sentiment across the entertainment sector had weakened around the time of the 3Q results. A weaker sector-wide view can affect share price movements regardless of an individual company's earnings. Rookie debut competition among peers is also cited as a burden.

Concert demand and overseas tour scale

3Q concert revenue fell 31.1%, mainly because the prior-year quarter included many large overseas shows. Concert revenue swings widely from quarter to quarter depending on schedule and scale. Once the scale of the 2027 major tour is set, its execution costs and revenue structure will need to be read together.

12

Overall view

JYP Ent.'s 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 267.5 billion and operating profit of KRW 48 billion rose year on year, and media reported the revenue as a quarterly record. However, the 2025 operating margin of 18.9% was below the 29.9% of 2023, and 3Q concert revenue fell 31.1%.

A large part of earnings relies on albums and concerts from specific IPs such as Stray Kids, so both strengths and weaknesses exist. On the strength side, a 46-show stadium tour in 2Q 2027 and the January 11, 2027 debut of oftheblue are scheduled.

On the weakness side, quarterly controlling-shareholder net income has moved sharply, falling 31.9% in 2Q 2026, and some analysts say next-generation IPs are growing more slowly than expected. Securities firms' target prices differ from firm to firm.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 full-year operating margin in the annual report, provisional 4Q 2026 results in early 2027, and the performance of the new boy group after its debut.
