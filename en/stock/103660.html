02

Business structure

Ciaat was founded in April 2006 to produce digital photo printing and printing materials. It entered the dye-sublimation film business later that year after acquiring the information materials division of Hansol Chemical.

Press reports state that the company holds Korea's only dye-sublimation technology along with its own production facilities. Its core products are ink ribbons and photo paper, with dye-sublimation film for ID cards and photo applications at the center.

The scope extends beyond materials: in 2016 the company launched a new game from Japanese publisher SEGA locally, and in 2018 it supplied mobile printers to LG Electronics on an ODM basis. Based on the 2025 product-level sales mix, the game card line was 18.59% domestic and 81.41% export.

All A-series printer media were exported, while the A product line was 81.78% domestic. This mix shows that revenue exposure to overseas demand and currency movements differs by product line.

Against large petrochemical and battery-materials producers such as LG Chem and Lotte Chemical, Ciaat is a small company concentrated in a niche sublimation segment. Its earnings are therefore likely to be driven more by printing demand and individual customer orders than by sector-wide cycles.

03

Recent trends

At the annual general meeting disclosed on March 31, 2026, the articles amendment and financial statements were approved. Standalone figures for the 20th fiscal term (2025) were reported as revenue of 31,194 million won, operating profit of 3,146 million won and net profit of 3,054 million won.

The same disclosure presented consolidated revenue of 31,187 million won, operating profit of 3,393 million won and net profit of 3,054 million won. For 2024, consolidated revenue was KRW 27.9 billion and operating profit was KRW 2.47 billion, while net loss was KRW 310 million.

On this basis, 2025 net profit moved from a loss to a profit. Standalone equity rose from about KRW 0.9 billion in 2024 to 3,801 million won in 2025, but the standalone debt ratio for 2024 stood at 2,656.95%, indicating a heavy financial burden.

A February 20, 2026 disclosure reported that Company K Partners sold its entire stake, which was reported at 3.97%. The share price moved from about 750 won as of February 20, 2026 to 1,290 won on April 1 and 1,500 won on October 13, then closed at 1,275 won on October 28.

The current price is 1,150 won, down 3.36% on the day, with a market capitalization of about KRW 6.9 billion. The 52-week range of 638 won to 2,100 won indicates wide price swings.

04

Outlook

Whether the 2025 return to profit lasts will first show up in the 2026 quarterly financial statements. On the positive side, demand for digital printing and a high export share support earnings.

Standalone revenue in 2024 rose 16.4% year on year and operating profit rose 48.8%, which is cited as evidence of business expansion. On the capital side, equity turned positive after the full capital impairment seen from 2020 through 2022.

The shareholder structure can be read in two ways: the sale of Company K Partners' stake reduces uncertainty over sell-down supply from fund maturity, while the concentration of stake in the largest shareholder raises dependence on one person's management.

A move to KOSDAQ was pursued after the KONEX listing in 2015, but as of the 2025 reporting it had not been realized due to earnings and other factors. Press reports from Seoul Economic Daily have previously pointed to thin free float as a structural challenge for the KONEX market.

Over the medium term, the outlook depends on whether profitability continues, capital structure improves and trust in financial disclosure recovers together.

05

Bull factors

Return to profit in 2025

Standalone 2025 net profit was reported at 3,054 million won, turning the 2024 consolidated net loss into a profit. Standalone operating profit was 3,146 million won, above the 2024 consolidated operating profit of KRW 2.47 billion.

Revenue rose from KRW 27.9 billion on a 2024 consolidated basis to 31,194 million won on a 2025 standalone basis.

Export-led product mix

Game card products accounted for 81.41% exports in 2025, reflecting high reliance on overseas demand. A-series printer media were fully exported, which shows a clear link to overseas printing markets.

The reported domestic-only dye-sublimation technology and in-house production are cited as points of technical differentiation for these export lines.

Progress in stake clean-up

As of August 2025, Company K Partners held 6.46% and said it was reviewing off-market sales as its fund neared maturity. A February 20, 2026 disclosure reported that the holder sold its entire stake, which was reported at 3.97%. This reduces uncertainty over large-block sell-downs, which is one reading of the change.

06

Bear factors

Accounting violations and sanctions

The Securities and Futures Commission found that Ciaat overstated net profit and equity over six fiscal years from 2014 to 2019 through early revenue recognition and fictitious sales. Omission of related-party disclosures and interference with the external audit were also identified.

The resolution included a fine, a three-year auditor designation, a recommendation to dismiss the CEO with a six-month suspension, and referral to prosecutors. Press reports said the company was excluded from a substantive review for listing eligibility in August 2024, and normal trading resumed.

Management change and governance

A disclosure dated April 1, 2026 reported that the CEO changed from Lee Young-sun to Kim Hwan-ki, as Lee resigned for personal reasons. Kim previously served as CEO from January 15, 2007 to August 22, 2024 before returning.

Kim holds 1,168,708 shares, or 14.58%, as the largest shareholder, which raises questions about concentration of decision-making. At the same board meeting, one outside director and the audit committee member were reported as absent.

Small-cap volatility and debt burden

The share price was about 750 won as of February 20, 2026, rose to 1,500 won on October 13, and currently stands at 1,150 won. A 52-week range of 638 won to 2,100 won shows wide swings. The standalone debt ratio for 2024 was 2,656.95%, indicating debt was large relative to equity. The thin free float typical of the KONEX market is cited as a background for price distortion.

07

Risk factors

Macro

A large share of revenue comes from export lines, so currency swings and softer overseas printing demand can directly affect sales. Customer inventory adjustments and shifts in order timing can move quarterly results. Changes in raw material costs and logistics expenses could also pressure margins.

Business concentration

Revenue is concentrated in specific materials such as dye-sublimation film and ink ribbons, as well as game cards and printer consumables. A shift in demand toward digital capture and mobile printing could reduce demand for existing product lines. Whether new product lines can offset such declines is the key variable.

Regulatory and trust

Past accounting violations have raised the market's bar for audit quality and disclosure reliability. The pace at which trust in financial disclosure recovers is a precondition for any discussion of corporate value. Because of thin free float on KONEX, prices can move sharply even on small trades.

08

Overall view

On 2025 financial statements, Ciaat showed improvement signals in the form of a return to net profit and better capital structure. Its export-heavy dye-sublimation product lines and reported domestic-only technology are positive factors.

On the other hand, accounting violations covering fiscal 2014 through 2019 and the regulatory sanctions raise the market's bar for disclosure reliability. The April 2026 CEO change and concentration of the largest stake leave governance and management continuity open for discussion.

The exit of Company K Partners' stake reduced uncertainty over large-block sales. With a market capitalization of about KRW 6.9 billion and thin free float, the share price remains volatile even around 1,150 won.

Whether quarterly profits continue and whether audit controls stabilize are the reference points for the next round of factual review.
