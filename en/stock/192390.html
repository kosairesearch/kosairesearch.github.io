Losses Deepen as Deck Plate Sales Fall; Affiliate Order in Focus

02

Business structure

Winhitech was spun off in September 2011 and listed on KOSDAQ in July 2014, and it manufactures deck plates, the steel formwork that supports concrete floors during building construction.

Its flagship product is the integrated EXTRA DECK, and the line-up also includes the removable NOVA Deck and the hollow-core Void Deck. The company says it runs fully automated production lines across its product range.

A company profile with no stated date attributes 99.07% of sales to the deck plate segment, including construction work, and 0.93% to general construction. The 2024 half-year report shows construction-type sales of KRW 36.4 billion (54.4%) and product sales of KRW 30.5 billion (45.6%).

Under its general construction license, the company is pursuing property development as a general developer. 2025 materials describe an Anseong logistics center under development at roughly KRW 25.5 billion and a planned knowledge industry center on a Dobong-dong site in Seoul.

In 2026 it disclosed a KRW 38 billion LH rental-housing construction contract with an affiliate in Incheon, the first large case of its diversification. Several producers compete in deck plates, and a 2020 equity research report pointed to excess capacity and intensifying competition.

The company cites product variety, automated production and a Japanese building-center evaluation certificate as its strengths.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results peaked in 2023 and turned down afterward. Revenue rose from KRW 116.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 153.8 billion in 2023 (up 31.7% year on year), then fell two years in a row to KRW 112.2 billion in 2024 (down 27.1%) and KRW 75.1 billion in 2025 (down 33.0%).

Operating profit climbed from KRW 9.73 billion in 2022 to KRW 18.4 billion in 2023 (up 88.7%), fell to KRW 11 billion in 2024 (down 39.9%), and swung to an operating loss of KRW 3.93 billion in 2025. The operating margin went from 8.3% in 2022 and 11.9% in 2023 to 9.8% in 2024 and -5.2% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 6.29 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.77 billion in 2024, then turned into a loss of KRW 6.69 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was also a loss of KRW 6.69 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 revenue decline mainly to a weaker construction market. The debt ratio fell from 134.2% in 2022 to 83.5% in 2024 and then rose to 110.9% in 2025, when total liabilities were KRW 80.4 billion.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 9.36 billion in 2022 and KRW 17.3 billion in 2023 to KRW 11.1 billion in 2024 and -KRW 1.99 billion in 2025. The quarterly pattern runs in the same direction.

Revenue fell from KRW 20.6 billion in Q3 2025, with an operating loss of KRW 1.33 billion, to KRW 13.2 billion in Q4 2025 (down 35.9% quarter on quarter).

Revenue recovered to KRW 14.3 billion in Q1 2026 (up 8.3%) and KRW 17.4 billion in Q2 2026 (up 21.9%), yet operating losses were KRW 3.1 billion and KRW 2.47 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 15.5 billion (down 24.7% year on year), with an operating loss of KRW 3.39 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 5.37 billion.

Losses persisting in quarters when sales recovered suggest that cost and expense burdens are offsetting the sales rebound, although the filed figures alone do not isolate the cause. The company has reported losses for five consecutive quarters from Q3 2025 through Q3 2026.

05

Industry analysis

Winhitech's core end market is domestic construction activity. The company itself cited a weaker construction market as the main reason for the 2025 revenue decline, which suggests deck plate demand is tied to construction starts.

A 2020 equity research technical report pointed to alternative construction methods replacing deck plates in residential work and to excess capacity from producers adding production.

Whether the supply conditions described then still hold today cannot be confirmed from that report alone, but it shows the basic character of competition in the segment. In the 2025 annual ranking of 34 building materials companies, Winhitech ranked 29th on revenue and 26th on operating profit.

The company's stated differentiators are fully automated production and product variety. Results since 2025 overlap with a construction downturn. Whether the property development business can offset the cyclical swings in its core deck plate demand is a key variable for its position within the sector.

06

Outlook

The largest identified variable is the LH rental-housing construction contract with an affiliate in Incheon. The contract is worth KRW 38 billion and runs from the signing date to September 30, 2028, roughly two years and one month.

Disclosure-based reports state that the amount equals 50.57% of the KRW 75.1 billion revenue recorded at the end of 2025. Payment terms include a 10% advance on the contract price, followed by monthly cash progress payments tied to construction completion.

As work progresses, the project should be reflected in quarterly revenue from 2027. Because the counterparty is an affiliate, pricing terms and project profitability need to be checked against the related-party transaction disclosures in periodic reports.

The third plant in Icheon was announced in September 2024 as a roughly KRW 12.5 billion investment, with construction planned to start in early 2025 and completion in the first quarter of 2026.

Whether it was completed and is operating, and what capacity it adds, can be checked in the facility disclosures of the 2026 annual report. Earlier 2025 materials said the Anseong logistics center was being marketed for sale, and the timing and size of sale proceeds will appear in ad hoc disclosures. The knowledge industry center planned on the Dobong-dong site was described as still at the planning stage.

Large affiliate construction order

A 10% advance and monthly cash progress payments are cited as support for cash flow. Because the counterparty is an affiliate, pricing terms still need to be checked.

Low multiple to net assets

The price-to-book ratio sits below the sector average, and total equity at end-2025 was KRW 72.5 billion. The argument that book value can anchor valuation during a loss-making phase is cited. The company is also developing real estate assets, including the Anseong logistics center and a site in Dobong-dong, Seoul. The market value of those assets is not in the filed results, so the analysis stays on a book-value basis.

Deck product line-up and certifications

The company describes a varied product line-up across Extra Deck, NOVA Deck and Void Deck, supported by fully automated production.

A company interview reported that NOVA Deck received a Public Procurement Service excellent product certification, and the Japanese building-center evaluation certificate is the basic requirement for entering Japan. A 2020 technical report cited patents and a new construction technology certification as strengths. Those strengths have not prevented the sales decline seen in the filed results.

Losses widen even as sales recover

Operating cash flow turning to -KRW 1.99 billion in 2025 also limits financial room. Because quarterly profit moved against the sales trend, the cost structure needs scrutiny.

Construction cycle and debt burden

The company itself cited a weaker construction market for the 2025 revenue decline. Deck plate demand is tied to construction starts, so order volumes can be volatile until conditions recover. The debt ratio rose from 83.5% in 2024 to 110.9% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 80.4 billion. The excess capacity competition flagged in a 2020 report could translate into pricing pressure.

Risk factors

Dependence on an affiliate project

The KRW 38 billion contract is an order from an affiliate, so its pricing and profitability are not fully visible. As a long-running project, delays or cost increases could hit quarterly results. Because the order is large relative to revenue, progress on this single project could drive the earnings trend. Transaction terms can be checked in the related-party section of periodic reports.

Financial capacity and cash flow

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 80.4 billion, and the debt ratio rose above its 2024 level. Operating cash flow turned to -KRW 1.99 billion, which could raise reliance on borrowing for development projects. A 2024 disclosure said the third plant would be funded with own capital and bank borrowings. Against total equity of KRW 72.5 billion, the debt load becomes a burden if results weaken further.

Industry conditions and development volatility

A prolonged construction downturn could reduce both construction starts and deck plate orders. The sector has seen capacity expansion and alternative methods intensify price competition.

Property development results depend heavily on the timing of sales or disposals, so diversification may not show up in the short term. With quarterly losses continuing through Q3 2026, the pace of the business restructuring is the key variable.
