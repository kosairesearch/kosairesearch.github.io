02

Business structure

Synergy Innovation is a mid-sized bio company that runs medical, in-vitro diagnostics, environmental monitoring and health functional food businesses on a bio-technology base. Its operations split into two main segments: medical and bio-technology.

The medical segment sells non-vascular and vascular stents, and the non-vascular stent market is described as growing on the back of a preference for minimally invasive procedures (FnGuide). According to Wisereport, 92.29% of non-vascular stent sales in 2025 were exports.

The bio-technology segment centers on culture media for diagnostics and environmental monitoring products, produced under ISO 13485 and KGMP certified conditions. Its main customers are hospitals, research institutes and bio companies.

R&D spending in 2025 was KRW 7.64 billion, or 6.76% of revenue (Wisereport, consolidated basis). Overseas sales expansion at key subsidiaries was disclosed as the main driver of the 2025 revenue and operating profit increase (Nate News, March 12, 2026).

In new drugs, a subsidiary develops drugs for brain and central nervous system diseases and remains in R&D. Since 2024 the company has invested in production facility expansion and automation.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 30.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 83.2 billion in 2023, up 174.5% year on year, and then grew for three consecutive years to KRW 90.6 billion in 2024 (up 8.9%) and KRW 113.1 billion in 2025 (up 24.8%).

Operating profit jumped from KRW 1.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 15 billion in 2023, up 1,144.7%, and then rose for three consecutive years to KRW 16.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 19.2 billion in 2025. The operating margin climbed from 4.0% in 2022 to 18.0% in 2023 and then held near 17% at 17.8% in 2024 and 17.0% in 2025.

In 2025 revenue grew 24.8% while operating profit grew 19.0%, so the margin edged lower.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 16.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.3 billion in 2023 (down 85.7%), KRW 10.4 billion in 2024 (up 350.0%) and KRW 3.01 billion in 2025 (down 71.0%), with 2022 the largest year in the period.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 22.8 billion in 2022, KRW 7.55 billion in 2023, KRW 18.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 11.2 billion in 2025. The 2025 decline in net income was attributed to losses from valuation of derivatives and investment assets (Nate News).

Operating cash flow was KRW 12.9 billion in 2022, KRW 35.4 billion in 2023, KRW 26.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.8 billion in 2025. The debt ratio went from 32.5% in 2022 to 28.4% in 2023, 33.6% in 2024 and 23.7% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue peaked at KRW 30.4 billion in the third quarter of 2025, fell to KRW 27.8 billion in the fourth quarter (down 8.6% quarter on quarter) and KRW 27 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then rebounded to KRW 31.4 billion in the second quarter (up 16.2%) before reaching KRW 29.3 billion in the third quarter, down 3.7% year on year.

Operating profit went from KRW 6.37 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 3.31 billion in the fourth quarter, recovered to KRW 5.38 billion in the second quarter of 2026, and in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 4.32 billion, down 32.2% year on year, a steeper decline than the 3.7% revenue decline.

Controlling-shareholder net income moved to a KRW 2.9 billion loss in the fourth quarter of 2025, back to a KRW 3.85 billion profit in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 5.23 billion in the second quarter, and a KRW 8.95 billion loss in the third quarter of 2026.

With operating profit still positive while controlling-shareholder earnings turned to a loss, the composition of non-operating items is the point to watch in coming quarters.

Industry analysis

Market data providers classify Synergy Innovation under the KOSDAQ medical and precision instruments sector (Wisereport). The medical equipment and supplies industry is used as the comparison group, in line with the business mix.

In a 2025 year-end comparison compiled by AlphaSquare, the company ranked 13th of 97 companies in the medical equipment and supplies industry on operating profit and 14th on operating margin. On demand, a shift toward minimally invasive procedures and an aging population are cited as drivers for non-vascular stents.

In diagnostics and environmental monitoring, the prevailing brokerage view is that demand for these products has remained steady. Securities analysts generally describe the company as being in a phase of structural improvement rather than judging it on near-term earnings (Pinpoint News).

On supply, the company has invested since 2024 in facility expansion and production automation, with capacity expected to rise about 13%, and productivity gains were expected after the first half of 2025 (Wisereport).

Because exports are a large share of stent sales, the business is exposed to overseas demand and changes in certification and regulation across countries.

On competition, ISO 13485 and KGMP certification is described as a trust factor in expanding deals with domestic and foreign diagnostics, pharmaceutical and bio companies. In new drugs, an aging population is expanding the market for degenerative brain diseases, but the pipeline remains in R&D.

The buyer missed the balance due by November 12, 2026, which gave rise to a contract termination ground. The final closing deadline is November 30, 2026, and the company said it will disclose promptly once the termination decision is made (Global Economic, November 13, 2026).

The result will change the subsidiary's ownership structure and the timing of cash inflows. The largest shareholder pledged 2,132,764 shares (2.42%) under a share pledge contract running through November 18, 2026 (Digital Today, report dated May 21, 2026).

On capital policy, the company said it would cancel in full bonds worth KRW 4.55 billion acquired through a call option on its 14th convertible bond issue, with cancellation scheduled for June 9, 2026 (News1). The company described this as eliminating a potential dilution factor and enhancing shareholder value.

The same bond's conversion price was lowered on April 22, 2026, following a decline in the share price (GoInsider). The new-drug pipeline is at a stage where technology transfer or clinical progress is discussed, with no dated milestone confirmed.

The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, which based on last year's filing date of March 19, 2026, is likely around March 2027, when the fourth-quarter 2026 results and full-year operating margin and controlling-shareholder net income trend will be confirmed.

Overall view

Synergy Innovation's operating profit has risen for three consecutive years since 2023, and the operating margin has held in the 17% range, showing a pattern of expanding core revenue and margins.

On the other side, net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 71.0% to KRW 3.01 billion in 2025 and turned into a KRW 8.95 billion loss in the third quarter of 2026.

The cumulative controlling-shareholder net loss over the last four quarters is KRW 2.77 billion, while operating profit stayed positive over the same period. Financially, the debt ratio at the end of 2025 was 23.7% with total equity of KRW 272.4 billion, so leverage is relatively light.

On valuation, the P/E ratio cannot be calculated and the price-to-book ratio sits below 1x. The next checkpoints are the November 18, 2026 end of the share pledge, the November 30, 2026 closing deadline for the subsidiary stake sale, and the 2026 full-year results and annual report in March 2027.

Operating capacity has been maintained, but the direction of controlling-shareholder earnings and the outcome of the subsidiary stake sale are the most important variables.
