NPK: Back in the Black, Share Price Below 1,000 Won

NPK returned to operating profit in 2025 and posted KRW 579 million in operating profit in the third quarter of 2026, yet its share price has stayed below 1,000 won for 25 straight trading days, close to the administrative-issue designation threshold.

  1. 1

    Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 1.01 billion, turning from an operating loss of KRW 630 million in 2024. Operating margin for the year was 1.2%.

  2. 2

    Sales in 2025 reached KRW 83.8 billion, up 29.8% from the prior year and the largest year in the 2022–2025 span.

  3. 3

    Operating profit for the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 579 million, turning from a year-earlier operating loss, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 958 million.

  4. 4

    The first quarter of 2026 posted an operating loss of KRW 282 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 486 million.

  5. 5

Business structure

NPK listed on KOSDAQ in October 2000 as a manufacturer of synthetic rubber and plastic materials. Media coverage describes the company as a synthetic resin maker, with resin production at the center of the business.

A corporate information site summarizes a technology licensing agreement with Nippon Pigment Holdings for advanced synthetic resin manufacturing technology. That technology base is read as underpinning stable material properties and productivity.

An older media report described an investment of KRW 5.5 billion in a new building and production equipment for special materials used in optical films.

Standalone sales for 2025 were about KRW 57.8 billion, against KRW 83.8 billion on a consolidated basis, which suggests a large contribution from subsidiaries to the top line.

The company attributed its 2025 improvement to higher yields and profitability from productivity gains, along with growing sales of its own products at its Chinese subsidiary.

Standalone operating profit for 2025 was about KRW 2.05 billion, above consolidated operating profit of KRW 1.01 billion, which suggests subsidiary-level operations weighed on consolidated profit.

Large listed chemical companies such as Kumho Petro Chemical, Lotte Chemical and Hanwha Solutions operate at far larger production and raw material procurement scale.

Synthetic resins are intermediate materials whose demand follows production in downstream industries such as automotive, electronics and packaging, so NPK is exposed to the same demand swings.

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 65.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 63 billion in 2023 (down 3.4% year on year), then rose to KRW 64.6 billion in 2024 (up 2.5%) and KRW 83.8 billion in 2025 (up 29.8%).

Sales have increased for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025. 2025 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 span, and 2023 was the smallest.

Operating profit rose from KRW 1.44 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.87 billion in 2023 (up 30.1%), then turned to an operating loss of KRW 630 million in 2024 before returning to operating profit of KRW 1.01 billion in 2025. Operating margin was 2.2% in 2022, 3.0% in 2023, -1.0% in 2024 and 1.2% in 2025.

Sales grew in 2025, but the margin stayed below the 3.0% of 2023, so higher sales did not translate directly into better profitability. The company attributed the return to profit to higher yields and profitability from productivity gains, along with growing sales of its own products at its Chinese subsidiary.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.48 billion in 2022 and KRW 1.71 billion in 2023 (up 15.8%), turned to a net loss of KRW 842 million in 2024, and returned to KRW 1.13 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.48 billion in 2022, KRW 1.71 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 842 million in 2024, and KRW 1.13 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 7.93 billion in 2022 and KRW 6.22 billion in 2023 to KRW 532 million in 2024, then recovered to KRW 2.95 billion in 2025. Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 46.2 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 23.3 billion.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 65.3% in 2022 to 50.5% in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, the company moved from an operating loss of KRW 609 million on sales of KRW 20.7 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to operating profit of KRW 1.34 billion on sales of KRW 22.3 billion in the fourth quarter (sales up 7.9% quarter on quarter).

The first quarter of 2026 had sales of KRW 22.2 billion (down 0.3%) but an operating loss of KRW 282 million and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 486 million.

The second quarter of 2026 returned to profit, with sales of KRW 24.9 billion (up 11.9%), operating profit of KRW 1.06 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 801 million.

The third quarter of 2026 had sales of KRW 22.7 billion (down 8.6% quarter on quarter but up 10.0% year on year), operating profit of KRW 579 million (down 45.2% quarter on quarter, turning from a year-earlier operating loss), and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 958 million (up 19.6% quarter on quarter).

In quarters such as the first quarter of 2026, where the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders exceeded the operating loss, items below operating profit likely played a role. Over the last four quarters, cumulative net income attributable to controlling shareholders totals KRW 2.55 billion.

Industry analysis

Synthetic resins and specialty plastic materials are intermediate goods whose orders shift with production and inventory cycles in downstream industries such as automotive, electronics and packaging.

Profit depends largely on the spread between raw material costs and product prices, so profitability varies widely even at similar sales levels. NPK's sales rose 29.8% in 2025, but operating margin was only 1.2%, so higher sales did not translate directly into a better margin.

The company cited higher yields and productivity gains as the drivers, which points to process efficiency supporting profit more than a market upturn. Whether the sales increase reflected market recovery or company-specific factors cannot be separated from the disclosed figures alone.

Among listed peers, Kumho Petro Chemical, Lotte Chemical, Hanwha Solutions and LG Chem are large petrochemical and materials companies with very different production and raw material procurement scale.

Within the smaller specialty chemical group, ISU Specialty Chemical, Miwon Specialty Chemical, Advanced Nano Products and Foosung are common comparison names.

NPK is a small chemical company built on technology-licensed synthetic resin and sales through its Chinese subsidiary, and its market capitalization is far smaller than that of the large chemical groups.
