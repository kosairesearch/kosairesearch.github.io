DB Insurance: Insurance Profit Recovery Drives Earnings

DB Insurance's insurance profit rose sharply in the third quarter of 2026 and lifted cumulative net income, while lower investment income and wide swings in quarterly profit remain the main issues.

  1. 1

    Insurance profit in the third quarter of 2026 reached KRW 467.3 billion, up 302.6% year on year, and accounted for about 75.6% of that quarter's operating profit of KRW 618.3 billion.

  2. 2

    A recovery in long-term insurance and a swing of auto insurance into profit drove the rebound. Auto insurance profit moved from a loss of KRW 55.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to a profit of KRW 15.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026.

  3. 3

    Operating profit swung widely from quarter to quarter, with KRW 1.17 trillion in the second quarter of 2026 and KRW 618.3 billion in the third quarter, down 47.3% from the prior quarter.

  4. 4

    Contractual service margin (CSM) on a direct-insurance basis reached KRW 13.4 trillion at the end of the third quarter of 2026, up KRW 836.6 billion from year-end 2025, and the K-ICS solvency ratio stood at 212.0%.

  5. 5

    Operating cash flow turned negative at -KRW 282.4 billion in 2025 from KRW 3.34 trillion in 2024, and the debt-to-equity ratio (total liabilities divided by total equity) stood at 576.9%.

02

Business structure

DB Insurance is a core affiliate of the DB Group and one of the largest non-life insurers in Korea, holding a top-tier share of direct premiums written. Its business is organized around three pillars: long-term insurance, auto insurance and general insurance.

In the third quarter of 2026, long-term insurance profit on a standalone basis rose 35.5% year on year to KRW 309.0 billion, with the company citing lower claims experience variance losses, premium adjustments and better retention.

Auto insurance swung to a profit of KRW 15.5 billion, helped by fewer seasonal factors such as heavy rain that had lifted the loss ratio in the prior-year period. The cumulative auto loss ratio was 84.3% for the first three quarters of 2026, down 6.2 percentage points from a year earlier.

General insurance turned to a profit of KRW 6.2 billion in the same quarter, and its cumulative loss ratio improved to 71.9%, which the company attributed to a revised reinsurance strategy and tighter underwriting.

Overseas, the company acquired the US specialty insurer Fortegra, and about KRW 48.9 billion of its net income was reflected for the first time in third-quarter 2026 results. Investment profit fell 53.6% to KRW 151.0 billion in the third quarter as weaker equity markets weighed on returns.

New protection-type policy sales declined, which reduced new-business CSM, but better retention and policy management increased the in-force CSM. Competition comes mainly from large non-life peers such as Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance and HYUNDAI MARINE&FIRE INSURANCE CO in long-term and auto lines.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, insurance revenue rose from KRW 13.7 trillion in 2022 to KRW 14.6 trillion in 2023 (up 6.4%), KRW 15.7 trillion in 2024 (up 7.8%) and KRW 16.4 trillion in 2025 (up 4.7%), marking three consecutive years of growth from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit moved in the opposite direction in 2023, falling 18.0% to KRW 2.23 trillion from KRW 2.73 trillion in 2022, before recovering to KRW 2.42 trillion in 2024 (up 8.5%) and easing to KRW 2.38 trillion in 2025 (down 1.7%).

Insurance revenue grew while operating profit stayed below its 2022 level, and 2022 was the largest year for operating profit.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.03 trillion in 2022, KRW 1.74 trillion in 2023 (down 14.5%), KRW 1.85 trillion in 2024 (up 6.5%) and KRW 1.79 trillion in 2025 (down 3.4%), while net income including non-controlling interests is reported separately.

Operating cash flow was KRW 2.74 trillion in 2022, KRW 4.46 trillion in 2023 and KRW 3.34 trillion in 2024, before turning to -KRW 282.4 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio (total liabilities divided by total equity) rose from 375.4% in 2022 to 608.2% in 2024 and eased to 576.9% in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, insurance revenue bottomed at KRW 4.21 trillion in the first quarter of 2026 and then rose to KRW 4.27 trillion in the second quarter and KRW 5.27 trillion in the third quarter, two consecutive quarters of growth.

Operating profit of KRW 1.17 trillion in the second quarter of 2026 was the largest of the five disclosed quarters, but the disclosed tables do not break down the drivers of that spike.

Cumulative operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 reached KRW 2.21 trillion, up 18.6%, and cumulative insurance profit rose 55.8% to KRW 1.30 trillion, while cumulative investment profit fell 11.7% to KRW 904.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The Korean non-life insurance market is growing more slowly, and Hana Securities said in an April 2026 report that overseas market penetration and stronger underwriting capability were positive developments.

Auto insurance faces both loss-ratio pressure and premium-cut pressure, and the same report expected the narrowing of auto losses to be difficult amid pressure from premium reductions linked to the vehicle five-day driving restriction.

Auto insurance nonetheless returned to profit in the third quarter of 2026, which differed from that outlook.

Hana Securities also said that institutional changes, including a revised pricing structure for managed medical fees and an eight-week limit on treatment for minor auto injuries, would improve visibility of medium-term earnings recovery.

Mirae Asset Securities said in a July 2026 report that premium increases, a smaller book of business and institutional changes together should sustain a profitability recovery.

The same report pointed to the inclusion of physical therapy in managed medical fees from July as a factor that could further reduce claims experience variance losses in the second half. In long-term insurance, lower claims experience variance losses and better policy retention have been reflected in disclosed results.

On the investment side, unstable financial market conditions, including the equity market decline, weighed on investment profit. DB Insurance keeps a top-tier share of direct premiums written and competes with Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance and HYUNDAI MARINE&FIRE INSURANCE CO in long-term and auto lines.

06

Outlook

DB Insurance said on November 12, 2026, when it released third-quarter results, that stable contractual service margin (CSM) build-up and efficient loss-ratio management should support steady earnings through year-end.

LS Securities said in a November 13, 2026 report that third-quarter net income was in line with market expectations and that insurance profit was solid, supported by lower claims experience variance losses and a continuing auto insurance profit trend, and it expected the insurance profit recovery to continue on premium increases and institutional changes.

Mirae Asset Securities said in a July 2026 report that both insurance profit and investment profit would rise from the prior quarter, although investment profit has been highly sensitive to equity market conditions.

On shareholder returns, NH Investment & Securities said in a June 2026 report that the company planned to begin communicating on a consolidated basis in the second half and to announce a corporate value enhancement plan covering a capital ratio target, a surplus capital allocation policy and a medium-term payout ratio target.

The same report expected the company to raise dividend per share by at least 10% a year until the payout ratio reaches 40%, from 29.3% in 2025. The accumulation of Fortegra's contribution in consolidated results is the next item to track overseas. Full-year 2026 figures will be fixed when the annual report is disclosed.

08

Bull factors

Insurance profit recovery

Insurance profit reached KRW 467.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 302.6% year on year, and the cumulative figure rose 55.8% to KRW 1.3 trillion. Long-term insurance profit rose 35.5% to KRW 309.0 billion on lower claims experience variance losses and a steadier loss ratio. Auto and general insurance both swung to profit, broadening the base of underwriting earnings.

Capital buffer and shareholder return outlook

The K-ICS solvency ratio stood at 212.0% at the end of the third quarter of 2026, and in-force CSM on a direct basis reached KRW 13.4 trillion, up KRW 836.6 billion from year-end 2025.

NH Investment & Securities expected dividend per share to rise by at least 10% a year as the payout ratio climbs from 29.3% in 2025 toward 40%. The same report flagged a corporate value enhancement plan on a consolidated basis for the second half.

Overseas portfolio expansion

With the acquisition of the US specialty insurer Fortegra reflected, about KRW 48.9 billion of net income was included in third-quarter 2026 results for the first time.

Hana Securities said in an April 2026 report that overseas market penetration and stronger underwriting capability were positive as domestic insurance market growth slowed. The company said profitability of its overseas insurance business remained stable in the third quarter.

Bear factors

Lower investment income

Investment profit fell 53.6% to KRW 151.0 billion in the third quarter of 2026, and cumulative investment profit declined 11.7% to KRW 904.4 billion. The company said unstable financial market conditions, including the equity market decline, weighed on the result.

Because insurance profit accounts for most of the operating profit increase, swings in the core insurance business pass directly through to reported earnings.

Large swings in quarterly profit

Operating profit moved sharply by quarter, at KRW 413.0 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 1.17 trillion in the second quarter and KRW 618.3 billion in the third quarter. Second-quarter operating profit rose 184.2% from the prior quarter, but the disclosed tables do not break down the reasons.

NH Investment & Securities expected first-quarter 2026 results to weaken on higher IBNR reserve build-up and a deterioration in general insurance in an April 2026 report. Given the wide quarterly swings, the trend is hard to read from quarterly figures alone.

Leverage and cash flow

The debt-to-equity ratio (total liabilities divided by total equity) was 576.9% at the end of 2025, above 375.4% in 2022, with total liabilities of KRW 63.1 trillion and total equity of KRW 10.9 trillion. Operating cash flow turned from KRW 3.34 trillion in 2024 to -KRW 282.4 billion in 2025.

The decline in new protection-type policy sales, which reduced new-business CSM, remains a burden on future insurance earnings.

Risk factors

Auto insurance loss ratio

Auto insurance results swing with seasonal factors such as heavy rain, premium adjustments and premium-cut pressure linked to the vehicle five-day driving restriction.

Hana Securities said in an April 2026 report that persistent loss-ratio deterioration and premium-cut pressure would make it hard to narrow auto losses, although auto insurance returned to profit in the third quarter. If institutional reforms fall short of expectations, the pace of the earnings recovery could change.

Financial markets and investment income

Investment profit is sensitive to market conditions including equity prices and interest rates, and the equity decline was a factor behind the 53.6% drop in third-quarter investment profit. If investment income stays weak for longer, gains in insurance profit could be offset. The direction of investment profit changes with market conditions.

Acquisition integration and capital

The Fortegra acquisition broadens the company's scale and portfolio, but post-deal integration and overseas loss-ratio management remain variables for earnings. With the debt-to-equity ratio at 576.9% at the end of 2025, the effect of overseas investment burdens on capital headroom is the next item to track. The decline in new protection-type policy sales remains a burden on medium-term insurance earnings.

Overall view

DB Insurance's third-quarter 2026 results show that the recovery in insurance profit has become the central pillar of earnings. Long-term insurance profit rose 35.5% to KRW 309.0 billion, and auto insurance swung to a profit of KRW 15.5 billion.

Investment profit, however, fell 53.6% to KRW 151.0 billion, limiting the increase in operating profit. Annual insurance revenue grew for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, while operating profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved up and down after 2022.

Cumulative net income attributable to controlling shareholders for 2026 reached KRW 1.56 trillion, up 10.5% year on year, pointing to a recovery in earnings.

On capital, the K-ICS ratio of 212.0% and in-force CSM of KRW 13.4 trillion provide support, while the debt-to-equity ratio of 576.9% at the end of 2025 and the swing of operating cash flow to negative in 2025 are items to monitor.

Quarterly operating profit of KRW 413.0 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 1.17 trillion in the second and KRW 618.3 billion in the third makes the trend hard to read from quarterly figures alone.

LS Securities proposed a target price of KRW 230,000 in a November 13, 2026 report, and Mirae Asset Securities proposed KRW 210,000 in a July 10, 2026 report; these reflect each firm's own judgment. The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report and the fourth-quarter 2026 results announcement.
