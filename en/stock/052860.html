02

Business structure

I&C Technology was founded in 1996 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2009. It is a fabless-style communication chip designer that focuses on chip design rather than owning fabrication.

Its main products are power line communication (PLC) chips, AMI (automated metering infrastructure) modems, data concentrator units (DCUs) and electricity meters, and it also supplies Wi-Fi chips and modules and digital audio broadcasting (DAB) chips and modules.

Its business spans both selling chips alone and delivering equipment such as modems, DCUs and meters. Its main customer is the AMI business of Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO), followed by private-sector AMI, smart gas meters, IoT devices and vehicle or broadcast reception modules.

The company cited higher sales from the KEPCO AMI business and other business revenue as the main drivers of 2025 results. In electrical safety, it also handles ultra-small arc fault breakers and electric fire detection products.

Several companies supply PLC modems and DCUs for KEPCO AMI projects, so bidding competition and chip adoption matter. Comparable peers are small and mid-sized smart meter, power communication and IoT module companies rather than large memory chipmakers.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 39.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 64.3 billion in 2023, up 63.2% year on year, making 2023 the largest year of 2022 to 2025. It fell to KRW 23.4 billion in 2024, down 63.6%, the smallest year in that span, before recovering to KRW 36.9 billion in 2025, up 57.8%.

Operating profit was KRW 2.45 billion in 2022 and KRW 1.43 billion in 2023, down 41.5%. The company then swung to an operating loss of KRW 10.7 billion in 2024 and returned to operating profit of KRW 4.05 billion in 2025. Operating margin was 6.2% in 2022, 2.2% in 2023, -45.7% in 2024 and 11.0% in 2025.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 963 million in 2022, turned to a loss of KRW 297 million in 2023, widened to a net loss of KRW 11.9 billion in 2024, and returned to a profit of KRW 4.57 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 4.28 billion in 2025, a different figure from profit attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow was KRW -2.23 billion in 2022, KRW 5.65 billion in 2023, KRW 1.07 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.44 billion in 2025.

In quarterly terms, the trough was the fourth quarter of 2025, with revenue of KRW 4.91 billion, down 40.2% from the prior quarter, and an operating loss of KRW 2.26 billion.

Revenue then reached KRW 8.99 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 8.98 billion in the second quarter, before rising to KRW 12.7 billion in the third quarter.

Third-quarter operating profit was KRW 844 million, down 63.0% year on year, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 810 million, down 66.0%. Revenue growth did not carry through to profit, and the cause should be checked in the next annual report's explanation of costs.

05

Industry analysis

AMI (automated metering infrastructure) is the infrastructure that remotely reads and manages electricity, gas and water usage, and it is the core demand base for I&C Technology. When KEPCO's AMI orders expand replacement and rollout volumes, opportunities for PLC chip and module supply grow.

When orders are delayed or unit-price competition intensifies, utilization and margins come under pressure. A 2020 technical analysis report described how KEPCO AMI orders from 2016 lifted sales from below KRW 10 billion into the KRW 30 to 40 billion range.

That description dates from 2020, so it offers limited evidence about where the current procurement cycle stands. Several suppliers of PLC modems and DCUs compete for KEPCO AMI work, so bidding competition and chip adoption shape results.

The more natural peers are small and mid-sized companies in smart meters, power communication, IoT modules and electrical safety equipment, rather than memory chipmakers such as Samsung Electronics or SK hynix. The company is classified as a designer of communication chips within non-memory semiconductors.

AMI applications have widened in 2026 into water meters, yet the company's own explanation for 2025 sales growth still centers on KEPCO AMI business.

Outlook

On February 26, 2026, it signed an MOU with Namdong-gu Urban Management Corporation on preventing electrical fires in local facilities.

On May 8, 2026, it announced an MOU with the Korea Fire Safety Education Culture Association to expand arc fault detection device (AFDD) deployment and fire safety education.

These projects widen AMI and electrical safety applications, but their revenue scale has not been separated out in the disclosed quarterly figures. On the results side, third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 12.7 billion is the largest of the five disclosed quarters from the third quarter of 2025 onward.

However, third-quarter operating profit of KRW 844 million was below the KRW 2.28 billion of the third quarter of 2025, so it is too early to say that the revenue recovery has translated into profit recovery.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results and the revenue breakdown in the 2026 annual report. Disclosures on KEPCO AMI orders and deliveries come at irregular times and should be tracked as they arrive.

With profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaling KRW 657 million over the last four quarters, whether quarterly profit stays positive is the central item to watch.

Bull factors

Recovery in public-sector AMI revenue

Revenue reached KRW 36.9 billion in 2025, up 57.8% year on year, which the company attributed to higher KEPCO AMI business sales and other business revenue. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 12.7 billion rose 55.1% year on year, continuing the broader base recovery. The rebound from KRW 23.4 billion in 2024 shows that AMI orders remain the central driver of results.

Signs of wider application reach

In March 2026 the company's IR site announced an AMI board for Seoul's integrated water meters and the commercial application of a domestic NB-IoT one-chip platform. In February and May of 2026 it announced MOUs covering electrical fire prevention in local facilities and expanded arc fault breaker deployment.

Moving from electricity metering into water metering and safety equipment creates room for revenue sources beyond AMI.

Improved financial structure

The debt ratio fell from 68.3% in 2024 to 48.3% in 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 17.2 billion and total equity of KRW 35.7 billion at end-2025. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 1.07 billion in 2024 to KRW 8.44 billion in 2025, alongside the return to profit. Maintaining an equity base after the loss-making period leaves room for future investment.

Bear factors

Unstable return to profit

The 2024 operating loss of KRW 10.7 billion and net loss of KRW 11.9 billion attributable to controlling shareholders were large, and quarterly profit remained volatile after the 2025 turnaround.

The fourth quarter of 2025 posted an operating loss of KRW 2.26 billion and a loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.74 billion. Second-quarter 2026 operating profit fell to KRW 349 million, down 66.0% from the prior quarter.

Profit lagging revenue growth

Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 55.1% year on year, yet operating profit fell 63.0% and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 66.0%. If higher revenue does not carry into profit through costs or product mix, margin recovery could be delayed. Until operating margin recovers, the profit base remains thin.

Bidding competition and earnings volatility

Several suppliers of PLC modems and DCUs compete for KEPCO AMI work, making bidding intense. Annual revenue fell 63.6% in a single year, from KRW 64.3 billion in 2023 to KRW 23.4 billion in 2024. Because delayed orders or price competition pressure both utilization and margins, earnings are harder to forecast.

Risk factors

Dependence on procurement

Results hinge largely on the timing and volume of KEPCO AMI orders. The 63.6% revenue decline in 2024 shows how much a procurement gap can hit results. If expansion into new applications has not yet shown up clearly in revenue, concentration remains high.

Market volatility and investment alerts

On April 24, 2026, the company disclosed that it would be designated an investment-caution stock for one day on April 27, 2026. The designation followed a top-10 account buying share of 41.44% over three days and a 16.76% price change. In December 2025, director Ahn Chang-kwon disclosed an increase of 5,000 shares in his holdings.

Thin profit base

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 657 million over the last four quarters, after a KRW 11.9 billion net loss in 2024. When quarterly profit swings between profit and loss, the durability of the annual turnaround is hard to judge.

Controlling shareholders' equity of KRW 35.3 billion out of total equity of KRW 35.7 billion means the equity base is largely attributable to the parent.

Overall view

I&C Technology's revenue base rests on KEPCO's AMI business, and its annual results moved from losses in 2024 to operating profit of KRW 4.05 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 4.57 billion in 2025.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 12.7 billion, up 55.1% year on year, was the largest of the disclosed quarters, yet operating profit of KRW 844 million fell 63.0%, so revenue and profit diverged. On the financial side, the debt ratio of 48.3% at end-2025 and operating cash flow of KRW 8.44 billion show improvement.

On the other side, profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled only KRW 657 million over the last four quarters, a thin base, and dependence on KEPCO procurement adds volatility.

Expansion into water metering and electrical safety was announced in 2026 through IR, but its revenue contribution is not yet clear. Current multiples are high relative to earnings, so interpretation will depend heavily on the direction of quarterly profit.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results, the revenue breakdown in the 2026 annual report, and first-quarter 2027 results.
