Kakao Games: New Launches Test the Earnings Recovery

Kakao Games has seen revenue fall for three straight years and operating losses for five consecutive quarters, so the launches of Dokkaebi-ui Segye and Odin Q are the next test of recovery.

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Kakao Games is a Korean game company that runs both in-house IP development and publishing, with mobile and PC online games as its core businesses. According to company announcements, mobile games generated KRW 50.6 billion and PC online games KRW 18.5 billion of third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 69.1 billion.

In the second quarter of 2026, PC online revenue was KRW 22.3 billion and mobile revenue KRW 52.7 billion. PC online revenue fell about 57% year on year in the third quarter, and mobile revenue fell about 40%, so both segments declined.

The company attributes the decline to softening revenue from existing live titles and a gap in new releases. Its new-title lineup centers on Dokkaebi-ui Segye, a MMORPG developed by Supercat and launched on October 8, 2026, based on the webtoon and web novel IP Myeolgwisudojeon.

Path of Exile 2, developed by Grinding Gear Games, is scheduled for full release on December 12, 2026. ArcheAge Chronicles and The Cube, Save Us from subsidiary XL Games are also on the PC and console roadmap. Odin Q from Lionheart Studio is planned for a simultaneous domestic and overseas launch in January 2027.

In 2026 the management team changed, the company exited non-core businesses, acquired global casual game company ME2ON, and made a strategic investment in AI backend firm Backendx. Listed industry peers include Krafton, Netmarble, PearlAbyss, and NEXON Games.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART filing basis, revenue fell from KRW 1.15 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.03 trillion in 2023 (down 10.7%), KRW 627.2 billion in 2024 (down 38.8%), and KRW 465 billion in 2025 (down 25.9%). Of the 2022 to 2025 period, 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 175.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 74.5 billion in 2023 (down 57.6%) and KRW 19.1 billion in 2024 (down 74.3%), and in 2025 the company recorded an operating loss of KRW 39.6 billion, a swing into loss from the prior year.

The operating margin moved from 15.3% in 2022 to 7.3% in 2023 and 3.1% in 2024, and reached -8.5% in 2025.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 233.6 billion in 2022, KRW 228.7 billion in 2023, KRW 108.9 billion in 2024, and KRW 100 billion in 2025, a loss in each of four years with the size narrowing after 2024.

Net loss including non-controlling interests moved differently, at KRW 196.1 billion, KRW 322.4 billion, KRW 128.1 billion, and KRW 143.1 billion over the same years. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 133.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 27.4 billion in 2024 and was -KRW 44.3 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 92.3% in 2022 to 123.4% in 2025. Quarterly revenue went from KRW 127.5 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 98.9 billion, KRW 82.9 billion, KRW 75 billion, and KRW 69.1 billion, declining for four consecutive quarters.

Operating losses widened from KRW 5.45 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 13.1 billion and KRW 25.5 billion, narrowed to KRW 23 billion in the second quarter of 2026, and widened again to KRW 26.3 billion in the third quarter.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 34 billion in the third quarter of 2025, then turned to a KRW 84.9 billion loss in the fourth quarter, and the four quarters through the third quarter of 2026 total a loss of KRW 186.3 billion.

The company put third-quarter 2026 costs at KRW 95.4 billion, down 28.2% year on year, with personnel costs of KRW 36.7 billion and commission fees of KRW 35 billion as the main items.

05

Industry analysis

The game industry moves on mobile and PC/console markets in parallel, and the company has said it is expanding from a mobile-heavy mix into PC, console and global markets. Industry coverage notes that the game market is saturated, so the question is whether new titles can stand out.

The company chose a January 2027 launch for Odin Q, citing the competitive environment in the MMORPG market. It also believes a global PvP MMORPG market on Steam still exists. Raising the weight of in-house IP and large projects marks a shift from the earlier publishing-heavy model.

Compared with listed peers such as Krafton, Netmarble, NC, PearlAbyss, NEXON Games, Wemade, Neowiz and Com2us, Kakao Games has been slower to recover because of its recent gap in new releases. Some media describe the third quarter as the earnings trough, though that reading draws on company commentary.

In terms of the cycle, the company says the second half of 2026 has entered a period when major new titles are concentrated.

06

Outlook

In its August 5, 2026 conference call, the company said it views the second and third quarters of 2026 as the revenue trough and expects new-release effects to build gradually from the fourth quarter.

Three new titles, including Dokkaebi-ui Segye, are slated for the fourth quarter, and ArcheAge Chronicles is targeting early access within 2026. The company has also started preparing a global rollout of Dokkaebi-ui Segye. Odin Q, God Save Birmingham and others are planned for the first quarter of 2027.

The company has set a target of returning to operating profit in the first quarter of 2027 and plans to release at least five new titles across the fourth quarter and the first quarter.

At its February 2026 conference call, the company said marketing could temporarily reach about 15% of revenue in a quarter during major launches but would be kept at around 10% of revenue on an annual basis. It also said it is pursuing share buybacks as part of shareholder returns.

Some earlier launch dates were adjusted in early 2026, so whether the schedule holds is the next point to watch. Samsung Securities analyst Oh Dong-hwan expects revenue to rebound from the fourth quarter, but said profitability could come only from 2027 given marketing spending.

08

Bull factors

Early traction of Dokkaebi-ui Segye

Dokkaebi-ui Segye launched on October 8, 2026, and according to the company reached the top spot on Google Play sales within five days. The company says its original IP and monetization structure have drawn positive player reception.

Revenue from the title is expected to enter fourth-quarter results, in line with the company's view that the third quarter was the low point.

Large new-title pipeline and overseas operating capability

The company plans to release at least five new titles across the fourth quarter of 2026 and the first quarter of 2027. Through its acquisition of ME2ON, a global casual game company, it says it has gained overseas live-service and marketing capability.

The lineup includes expansions of existing IPs such as Odin Q and Path of Exile 2, which is cited as a strength.

Cost structure efficiency

The company put third-quarter 2026 costs at KRW 95.4 billion, down 28.2% year on year. It says personnel costs of KRW 36.7 billion reflect the effect of operating efficiency measures. Management also says exiting non-core businesses has stabilized cash flow and increased flexibility in deploying capital.

Bear factors

Extended losses and accumulated deficit

The third quarter of 2026 recorded an operating loss of KRW 26.3 billion, extending losses to five straight quarters. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 186.3 billion.

Controlling-shareholder losses ran for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, and both the company target and analyst views place a return to profit in 2027 or later.

Declines in existing live titles

Third-quarter 2026 PC online revenue fell about 57% year on year and mobile revenue about 40%. Revenue has declined for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

The company cites a stabilization at lower levels for existing live titles, but whether new releases can offset those declines remains to be shown.

Schedule changes and marketing burden

In early 2026 the company adjusted some launch dates, citing the sequencing of major titles. It has said larger marketing spend is unavoidable ahead of major launches, so costs may rise alongside revenue. A Samsung Securities analyst expects profitability to come only from 2027 once marketing spending is considered.

10

Risk factors

New-title performance and schedule

It remains to be seen whether the early success of Dokkaebi-ui Segye holds in subsequent quarters after launch. If launch dates for early-2027 titles such as Odin Q shift again, the target of returning to profit could be delayed. Given the schedule adjustments made in early 2026, keeping to launch dates is a key risk.

Financial structure and cash flow

With the debt burden already larger, operating cash flow turned to -KRW 44.3 billion in 2025. While losses continue, the balance between borrowing and cash use will determine financial stability.

M&A integration and competition

The integration results of the ME2ON acquisition and the Backendx investment have not yet appeared in reported earnings. Industry coverage points to a saturated game market where differentiation is difficult. In competition with listed peers, a shortfall in new-title performance could slow the recovery further.

12

Overall view

Kakao Games' DART-based results show a sustained decline in revenue and operating losses since 2022, with a KRW 39.6 billion operating loss in 2025 and a KRW 26.3 billion operating loss in the third quarter of 2026 as the latest markers.

Third-quarter revenue of KRW 69.1 billion declined in both mobile and PC online compared with a year earlier, and the company attributes this to a gap in new releases and stabilization of existing titles.

On the other side, early performance of Dokkaebi-ui Segye and a slate of major launches from the fourth quarter are cited as grounds for a rebound. Analysts see revenue recovery as possible but place a return to profit in 2027 or later.

With no price-to-earnings ratio available and the price-to-book ratio in a discount range, valuation reading depends on new-title results and the direction of financial indicators until profit recovery is confirmed.

The next checkpoints are the launch of Path of Exile 2 in December 2026, Odin Q in January 2027, whether the first-quarter 2027 profitability target is met, and the operating margin in the 2026 annual business report due around early 2027.
