02

Business structure

Daesung Private Equity (Daesung Venture Capital) is a venture capital company established in 1987, whose main businesses are investing in venture companies and forming and managing venture investment partnerships.

It was listed on KOSDAQ in 1999, and in 2009 Daesung Holdings became the largest shareholder, bringing it into the Daesung Group. As of November 4, 2026, Daesung Holdings and one other party held 54.48% of the shares.

Revenue comes mainly from fund-related income, including the share of equity-method gains from partnerships and management fees, so earnings move with the investment performance of the funds it manages.

Management fees provide a steadier income stream; in one financial data series, fund management fees accounted for about 59% of revenue. Performance fees arise when a fund succeeds in exiting an investment, and the company said performance fees rose more than 20-fold year on year when it explained its 2024 results.

Key portfolio companies frequently cited include game developer Shift Up, AI and machine learning platform developer Makina Rocks and DALBA Global. Reports said Makina Rocks was exited at about 13 times the entry multiple after the fund sold its stake right after the company listed, following an investment in 2020.

Assets under management were reported at about KRW 330 billion, and the Daesung Secondary Investment Fund No. 2, with a size of KRW 30 billion, was formed at the end of 2025.

On competition, the scale of assets under management and the ability to raise new funds are the key variables relative to other listed venture capital firms.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed figures, the peak of earnings was 2024. Revenue that year was KRW 20.1 billion, up 62.5% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 10.2 billion, up 398.8%. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose 535.6% to KRW 13.8 billion, and the operating margin was 51.0%.

Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year for revenue, operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders. In 2025, revenue fell 53.9% to KRW 9.25 billion and operating profit fell 92.2% to KRW 797 million.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 97.7% to KRW 314 million, making 2025 the smallest year of the period, and net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 314 million. The operating margin dropped to 8.6%, after rising from 7.5% in 2022 to 16.6% in 2023.

A report on cumulative results through the third quarter of 2025 cited the decline in the value of portfolio stakes and delays in forming new funds as factors behind the weaker earnings.

Operating cash flow was KRW -6.05 billion in 2022, KRW -5.97 billion in 2023, KRW 18.8 billion in 2024 and KRW -11.9 billion in 2025, changing direction sharply from year to year.

Quarterly, operating profit rose from KRW 28.65 million in Q3 2025 to KRW 237 million in Q4 2025, then turned to an operating loss of KRW 349 million in Q1 2026.

Operating profit returned to KRW 2.99 billion in Q2 2026, and Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 967 million, down 67.7% from the prior quarter but up 3,276.6% from a year earlier.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 763 million, down 37.1% year on year, so operating profit and attributable net profit moved in opposite directions.

Over the last four quarters, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 4.92 billion, and Q2 2026's KRW 2.34 billion accounted for nearly half of that.

05

Industry analysis

Venture investment returns depend on the exit market, meaning IPOs and M&A transactions. When exits are delayed, gains on fund holdings remain unrealized and performance fees are pushed back.

Analysts attributed much of the 2024 result to exit proceeds from the listing of game developer Shift Up on the KOSPI market. 2025 looked different. A report on cumulative results through the third quarter of 2025 identified Daesung Private Equity as the only loss-making company among listed venture capital firms.

The same report cited delays in forming new funds as the cause. On the policy side, the company was selected as general partner for a secondary fund jointly financed by the Korea Fund of Funds and the pension fund investment pool, opening a link to government-backed capital.

However, in 2024 the company failed to secure limited partners for Korea Fund of Funds and growth finance programs, returned its general partner status and was excluded from those programs for a set period. That history shows that the ability to raise funds is a precondition for taking part in policy-backed programs.

In deep tech, reports said the company plans to expand AI investments following the Makina Rocks exit. A company official also said it would raise its GP commitment ratio to improve profitability. The pace of exits is closely tied to the listing schedules of portfolio companies.

06

Outlook

The company's long-term goal is to expand assets under management to about KRW 1 trillion. A 2023 report cited a target of KRW 1 trillion in assets under management by 2028 along with a goal of entering the top ten venture capital firms, and any revised target can be checked in the company's disclosures.

The end-2025 formation of the Daesung Secondary Investment Fund No. 2, at KRW 30 billion, restarted new fund formation. That fund was formed after the company was selected as general partner in a program jointly financed by the Korea Fund of Funds and the pension fund investment pool.

Reports said the company plans to expand deep-tech investments and form a larger fund. The company is also expecting additional exits and higher performance fees from the KRW 110 billion Daesung Metaverse Scale-up Investment Partnership.

On shareholder value, reports said the company decided on a five-for-one share consolidation that raises the par value per share, and that it is considering share purchases and treasury share cancellation. The company has said it will focus on investment and fund raising rather than dividends.

If listing schedules for portfolio companies such as Lablup are set, the size of exits and the timing of performance fee recognition could change with them. The next confirmation point in the disclosed results is the fourth-quarter profit and loss included in the 2026 annual report.

Valuation is best read through two lenses that match the disclosed figures: earnings scale and net assets.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 4.92 billion over the last four quarters, and it fell to KRW 314 million in 2025, so a single quarter's swing has a large effect on earnings multiples.

The historical price-to-book ratio was above 1 times in 2021 and 2022, and it fell below 1 times around the end of 2024. Equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 99.1 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, so the ownership structure of net assets is straightforward.

Market capitalization currently sits below that level of net assets. The carrying value of investments within net assets can differ from actual exit proceeds at each sale, however. The company does not pay dividends, so comparisons on a dividend basis are difficult.

A share consolidation would change the per-share figures, so historical multiples should be compared on a consistent share count. Because earnings swing widely, a historical PER band is not a stable reference point.

08

Bull factors

Leverage to portfolio exits

The company said the residual stake value of its Shift Up investment rose at least tenfold versus the invested principal. Reports said Makina Rocks was exited at a 13 times multiple. Exit proceeds from Shift Up were cited as a main reason operating profit rose 398.8% in 2024. Portfolio companies with listings ahead remain, leaving room for further exits.

Quarterly earnings recovery in 2026

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 2.99 billion in Q2 2026, and Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 967 million was up 3,276.6% year on year. Q3 2026 revenue rose 114.0% year on year to KRW 36.4 billion, showing top-line recovery. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 4.92 billion.

Shareholder-value measures and a light balance sheet

Reports said the company decided on a five-for-one share consolidation and is considering share purchases and treasury share cancellation. Daesung Holdings injected KRW 14.1 billion in 2022 and KRW 16.1 billion in 2023, continuing parent-company support. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 533 million, a light debt burden.

09

Bear factors

Earnings dependent on exit timing

Revenue depends heavily on fund-related gains and performance fees tied to investment outcomes. Revenue fell 53.9% in 2025 and operating profit fell 92.2%. Without exits in a given year, results can shrink sharply, leaving annual swings large.

Cash-flow deficits and divergence in quarterly profit

Operating cash flow was KRW -11.9 billion in 2025, and it was also negative in 2022 and 2023. Q3 2026 operating profit fell 67.7% from the prior quarter. Operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders moved in opposite directions, which makes the earnings structure harder to read.

Fund-raising history and reliance on policy programs

In 2024 the company failed to secure limited partners for Korea Fund of Funds and growth finance programs, returned its general partner status and was excluded from support for one year and three years respectively.

Delays in forming new funds were cited as a cause of weaker results in cumulative 2025 figures through Q3. Plans to expand assets under management carry uncertainty until the funds are actually formed.

10

Risk factors

AI investment expansion and valuation

Reports said the company plans to expand AI investments in deep tech following the Makina Rocks exit. Most of these stakes have not yet been realized, so their carrying values depend heavily on assumptions made before any listing.

The gap between assessed value and actual sale price could be reflected in earnings at each valuation point. As the portfolio shifts toward new deep-tech holdings, earnings volatility could increase.

Share liquidity and ownership structure

Daesung Holdings and one other party hold 54.48% of the shares, which limits the number of shares available for trading. Market capitalization is in the small-cap range. A share consolidation could change trading units and price patterns.

Higher GP commitment burden

The company said it would raise its GP commitment ratio to improve profitability. Because the GP commitment is the portion the manager invests directly alongside fund investors, losses in the partnerships can flow straight into the company's own results.

A larger commitment could widen swings in operating results in years when fund performance is weak. Whether asset growth and a higher commitment ratio advance together could shape the earnings structure.

12

Overall view

Daesung Private Equity's earnings moved sharply by year with the exit performance of the funds it manages.

After peaking in 2024 with revenue of KRW 20.1 billion, operating profit of KRW 10.2 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 13.8 billion, earnings fell in 2025 to operating profit of KRW 797 million and attributable net profit of KRW 314 million.

In 2026, operating profit followed an operating loss of KRW 349 million in Q1 with KRW 2.99 billion in Q2 and KRW 967 million in Q3, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 4.92 billion.

Positive factors include the Shift Up and Makina Rocks exits, the formation of secondary funds, plans to expand assets under management, and shareholder-value measures such as the share consolidation and treasury share actions.

Negative factors include a revenue model that depends heavily on exit timing, negative operating cash flow in 2025, the decline in operating profit in Q3 2026 from the prior quarter, and the absence of a set dividend plan.

Valuation sits at a low level relative to net assets, but with a small earnings base, multiples respond sharply to changes in profit. The next confirmation points are the fourth-quarter results in the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
