02

Business structure

Opasnet is a network and systems integration specialist founded in 2004. It listed on KOSDAQ in 2018 and was classified as a mid-sized company in 2025. In Q1 2025, network build-out accounted for 51% of revenue, maintenance for 37.5%, and other business for 11.5%.

The company is a Cisco Preferred Partner, and supplying and building Cisco network equipment is the core of its business.

Its customers include large corporations such as SK Telecom and Samsung Electronics, IT companies such as Samsung SDS and LG CNS, and government ministries including the Ministry of National Defense.

On the solutions side, the company said it assigned skilled technical staff to Splunk, a security and data analytics platform, and won large projects from major customers.

AI-based network optimization and cloud transition services are described as areas the company is growing, and it is also pursuing expansion into a digital healthcare platform. The KRW 10 billion large-scale AI IT infrastructure project completed recently was cited as the driver of Q3 2026 revenue growth.

By contrast, the network build-out segment saw sales and operating profit decline in H1 2026 due to reduced IT investment and economic uncertainty. The company is responding by expanding its solutions-focused portfolio and diversifying sales channels.

Samsung SDS and LG CNS are listed among the large IT services companies in the same industry, but their scale and product mix differ, so direct comparison is limited.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 170.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 225 billion in 2023, up 31.9% year on year. It fell to KRW 192.9 billion in 2024, down 14.3%, before recovering to KRW 233.3 billion in 2025, up 20.9%. Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 8.52 billion in 2022, KRW 14 billion in 2023 (up 63.7%), KRW 10.7 billion in 2024 (down 23.6%), and KRW 14 billion in 2025 (up 31.9%). The operating margin was 5.0% in 2022, 6.2% in 2023, 5.5% in 2024, and 6.0% in 2025, staying in a 5 to 6% range since 2023.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders moved from KRW 6.07 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.5 billion in 2023, KRW 9.54 billion in 2024, and KRW 12.7 billion in 2025. Total net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 12 billion in 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 increase to expanded orders from major customers and other sales channels, along with efficient cost management. Quarterly results were volatile.

Revenue rose from KRW 42.8 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 87.9 billion in Q4 2025, up 105.4% quarter on quarter, then fell to KRW 38.1 billion in Q1 2026, down 56.7%. It then rose to KRW 47.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 51 billion in Q3 2026, increasing for two consecutive quarters.

Operating profit fell from KRW 4.54 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 1.41 billion in Q1 2026, and Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 1.84 billion was down 45.2% year on year. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 562 million, down 81.6% year on year.

A press report described Q3 results as stable, but on reported operating profit, the sales recovery did not carry through to earnings. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders for the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 to Q3 2026, totals KRW 10.6 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The network equipment market is described as entering a phase where AI data center investment and corporate cloud transitions are lifting demand. As of 2025, brokerage analysts viewed rising demand for AI network equipment as the start of proactive investment by customers.

Opasnet supplies and builds Cisco network equipment in Korea, so it is directly affected by Cisco's AI technology strategy and product cycles. In its Q3 2026 press release, the company said its business is synergizing with Cisco's latest AI strategy.

By contrast, in H1 2026 the network build-out segment saw sales and operating profit decline amid reduced corporate IT investment and economic uncertainty. Areas of growing AI infrastructure demand and areas where corporate IT spending has slowed therefore exist at the same time.

Among listed IT services companies in the same industry, Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever are comparable names, but their scale and contract structures differ, which limits direct comparison.

Opasnet's competitive position rests on its build-out and maintenance capability for specific vendor equipment and its customer base, so changes in partner vendor product strategy translate directly into changes in its operating environment.

06

Outlook

In its Q3 2026 press coverage, the company said it aims to build its AI infrastructure business into a core growth engine.

Reported cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was KRW 136.5 billion, and cumulative operating profit was KRW 5.2 billion, figures consistent with the quarterly results summed from Q1 to Q3.

Full-year 2026 results will depend on revenue recognition from the large AI project and on whether year-end projects are completed. Because the company works on a project-based order structure, quarterly revenue has tended to swing with the timing of large project completions.

Reported backlog at the end of Q1 2025 was KRW 151 billion, up 26.9% year on year. The company says it plans to shift its profit structure by expanding its solutions-focused portfolio and diversifying sales channels.

A 2025 report said the company was internally reviewing shareholder return measures such as higher dividends and share buybacks, but the company said at the time that nothing specific had been finalized.

The next checkpoints are whether the operating margin holds near the 2025 level of 6.0% in the 2026 annual report, and whether a shareholder return policy is formally confirmed.

08

Bull factors

AI network demand and project wins

As investment in AI data centers and enterprise networks grows, demand for building Cisco network equipment, the company's core business, is also rising. A KRW 10 billion AI IT infrastructure project was completed in Q3 2026, and revenue of KRW 51 billion was up 19.2% year on year. Revenue has increased for two consecutive quarters, from Q2 to Q3 2026.

Balance sheet improvement and cash generation

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 237.7% in 2022 to 129.7% in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 31.7 billion in 2025, after a KRW 35.2 billion outflow in 2022. Total equity was KRW 64.9 billion and total liabilities KRW 84.2 billion at end-2025.

Shareholder return review and dividend record

A 2025 press report said the company was internally reviewing shareholder return measures including higher dividends and share buybacks. An official said discussions on share buybacks were under review. The report also noted that the company had paid year-end dividends for the past four years. The company said nothing specific had been finalized.

Bear factors

Falling operating profit and margin pressure

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 1.84 billion, down 45.2% year on year, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 562 million, down 81.6%. Operating profit in H1 2026 also fell 45.6% year on year. Revenue has grown while profit has not kept pace.

Quarterly revenue volatility

Revenue was KRW 87.9 billion in Q4 2025, then fell to KRW 38.1 billion in Q1 2026, down 56.7% quarter on quarter. The company has described revenue concentrating in the third and fourth quarters. Quarterly results therefore swing with the timing of large project completions.

Weaker corporate IT investment

The network build-out segment saw sales and operating profit decline in H1 2026 amid reduced IT investment and economic uncertainty. This segment has been the core area, accounting for more than half of revenue in Q1 2025. If corporate IT spending remains weak, a recovery in the build-out segment could be delayed.

10

Risk factors

Vendor dependence

As a Cisco Preferred Partner, the company's business centers on Cisco equipment. Changes in Cisco's product strategy or partner policies could directly affect orders and revenue. Its Splunk solutions business also relies on the technology of a specific vendor.

Project concentration and revenue deferral

Quarterly revenue moves sharply depending on when one or two large projects are completed. The drop from KRW 87.9 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 38.1 billion in Q1 2026 illustrates this. When large orders slip into the next quarter, quarterly operating profit also fluctuates.

Shareholder return policy not finalized

At the time of the 2025 press report, shareholder return measures were under review, and the company said nothing specific had been finalized. Whether dividend increases or share buybacks take place will depend on later disclosures. When profits decline, market interpretations of return capacity can change.

12

Overall view

Opasnet's full-year 2025 results showed revenue of KRW 233.3 billion, operating profit of KRW 14 billion, and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 12.7 billion, all up year on year, and the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 237.7% in 2022 to 129.7% in 2025.

In 2026, revenue increased for two consecutive quarters, from Q2 to Q3, but Q3 operating profit of KRW 1.84 billion was down 45.2% year on year. A KRW 10 billion AI IT infrastructure project was cited as the driver of Q3 revenue growth, yet revenue recovery has not carried through to profit at the same rate.

Strengths include AI network demand, the Cisco partner structure, the improved balance sheet, and the shareholder return review. Weaknesses include reduced IT investment in the network build-out segment, large quarterly revenue swings, and a smaller quarterly operating profit.

The shareholder return measures were still under review as of the 2025 report, and their finalization has not yet been confirmed in disclosures. The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, and the key question is whether the operating margin in Q4 and for the full year holds near the 2025 level of 6.0%.
