Report overview

AI passes 30% of standalone sales as margins absorb investment

Hancom's AI business now exceeds 30% of standalone sales and has become its growth engine, while heavier investment ahead of the U.S. launch cut third-quarter 2026 operating profit from a year earlier.

  1. 1

    AI revenue reached KRW 28.3 billion in January–September 2026, more than three times the KRW 8.9 billion booked for full-year 2025.

  2. 2

    AI's share of standalone revenue more than doubled to 30.7% in the third quarter of 2026 from 13.7% in the first half.

  3. 3

    Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 9.2 billion, down 25.5% year on year, with the company attributing the decline to expanded AI investment including the Nomadian platform.

  4. 4

    The Nomadian AI workforce platform is scheduled for a U.S. launch in December 2026 and a global launch in March 2027, and the value-up plan is slated for release within the fourth quarter of 2026.

  5. 5

    Attributable net income for the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totaled KRW 30.4 billion, and quarterly results have swung widely.

02

Business structure

Hancom is shifting from a document software company, best known for the Hangul word processor, toward an AI-centered business. The company changed its corporate and listing name from Hanguel & Computer to Hancom in July 2026.

Its business divides into the traditional office software franchise and AI-inclusive new businesses. In the third quarter of 2026, new-business revenue including AI reached KRW 30.9 billion, up 67.2% from a year earlier, equal to 64.1% of standalone sales.

AI's share of standalone sales rose from 13.7% in the first half to 30.7% in the third quarter. The installed base is large, with more than 200,000 existing customers supporting AI product supply.

In the public sector, Hancom supplies its Hancom Assistant AI product to Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power and Korea Western Power, and provides AI agents for LG's ChatEXAONE. In the private sector it has an AX partnership with BGF Group.

Overseas, it is preparing a U.S. launch of Nomadian, whose first target is 5.21 million U.S. small businesses and sole proprietors that already use or pay for AI. In Europe it is preparing local operations with 7Bulls and Algomine of Poland.

The company has also restructured its portfolio, divesting stakes in Hancom InSpace, ThinkFree and MadXcompany, which had limited synergy with AI. Management expects AI results to show more clearly in consolidated earnings from 2027, as non-core affiliates leave the consolidation scope.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 242.0 billion in 2022 to KRW 326.7 billion in 2025, increasing for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. The growth rate slowed from 12.0% in 2023 and 12.4% in 2024 to 7.2% in 2025.

Operating profit grew from KRW 25.0 billion in 2022 to KRW 40.4 billion in 2024, then fell 10.0% year over year to KRW 36.4 billion in 2025. The operating margin rose from 10.3% in 2022 to 12.6% in 2023 and 13.3% in 2024, then fell to 11.1% in 2025.

In 2025, revenue grew while the margin fell, which suggests it was a year of heavier cost burdens.

Net profit attributable to owners of the parent fell from KRW 46.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.1 billion in 2023 (down 67.7% year over year) and KRW 13.9 billion in 2024 (down 8.0%), then recovered to KRW 34.4 billion in 2025 (up 147.7%).

Because net profit attributable to owners of the parent rose sharply in 2025 even as operating profit declined, non-operating gains and losses appear to have had a large effect. Net profit including non-controlling interests recorded a net loss of KRW 27.5 billion in 2023 and was KRW 24.6 billion in 2025.

Net profit attributable to owners of the parent and net profit including non-controlling interests are different figures, so they must be interpreted separately. Cash flow from operating activities increased every year, from KRW 23.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 69.7 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 44.5% in 2022 to 34.3% in 2023, then rose to 46.7% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 496.5 billion, of which equity attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 366.7 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 232.0 billion.

Quarterly revenue fluctuated widely: KRW 84.1 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 95.4 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 63.6 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 120.0 billion in Q2 2026 and KRW 92.1 billion in Q3 2026. Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 120.0 billion was the largest of the five quarters reviewed.

The first-half trend, which the company described as 'the largest ever' with standalone first-half revenue of KRW 98.6 billion, was reflected in this quarter.

Operating profit swung to an operating loss of KRW 750 million in Q4 2025, then returned to profit at KRW 8.56 billion in Q1 2026, followed by KRW 14.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 9.2 billion in Q3.

Net profit attributable to owners of the parent rose from a net loss of KRW 3.7 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 22.1 billion in Q2 2026, then fell to KRW 2.91 billion in Q3.

However, operating profit showed a downward trend, owing to factors such as expanded investment for the U.S. launch of the AI workforce platform 'Nomadian' and the commercialization of an agentic OS.

The standalone operating margin for the same quarter stayed in the 20% range on a standalone basis even as spending on R&D and hiring increased. One-off costs incurred in the process of disposing of the related stake were reflected in third-quarter results.

05

Industry analysis

Rising government AI adoption budgets in Korea are cited as a near-term tailwind. The public-sector AI opportunity is widening from government agencies into nuclear and power utilities.

In the enterprise market, the transition of existing office software customers to AI products is meeting demand for AI transformation across public and private organizations. Overseas, the focus is on the U.S. small-business market and Japan eKYC expansion.

AI revenue grew from KRW 8.9 billion for full-year 2025 to KRW 28.3 billion in January–September 2026, which reads as a phase where revenue scale is building in an early-stage business.

Public-sector wins have so far run largely through proof-of-concept projects, so the speed of conversion into full contracts will determine the pace of revenue. Domestically, Hancom competes for public and enterprise AX projects against Samsung SDS, LG CNS, Hyundai Autoever and POSCO DX Company.

Its differentiation rests on document and office technology and its installed base. Its approach is to move office users directly onto AI products, while large IT service providers compete mainly on systems integration capability. Overseas competition will hinge on the U.S. small-business market and its European local partnerships.

06

Outlook

In an April 20, 2026 disclosure, the company set a standalone 2026 target of KRW 210 billion in revenue and KRW 60 billion in operating profit, maintaining a roughly 30% operating margin. Those targets are about 20% and 18% above 2025 standalone revenue of KRW 175.3 billion and operating profit of KRW 50.9 billion.

The company also targets lifting non-office revenue to 50% of the total. In the first half of 2026, standalone revenue reached KRW 98.6 billion, which the company described as a record. Third-quarter standalone revenue was KRW 48.3 billion with operating profit of KRW 9.7 billion.

Adding first-half and third-quarter standalone revenue against the full-year target implies that fourth-quarter standalone revenue must rise sharply to reach it. Nomadian is slated for a U.S. launch in December 2026 and a global launch in March 2027.

Parallel commercialization of the sovereign agentic OS in Korea and Europe is targeted for 2027. Conversion of Japan eKYC revenue is another open task for the 50% non-office target. The value-up plan is slated for a formal disclosure within the fourth quarter of 2026 after advisory work with a major accounting firm. Proceeds from non-core divestments will be concentrated on Nomadian.

08

Bull factors

AI revenue at three times the full-year 2025 level

AI revenue for January–September 2026 reached KRW 28.3 billion, more than three times the KRW 8.9 billion booked for full-year 2025. Third-quarter AI revenue of KRW 14.8 billion rose more than 80% from the second quarter. AI's share of standalone sales also rose from 13.7% in the first half to 30.7% in the third quarter.

Defending margins while investing

Standalone operating margin stayed at 20.1% in the third quarter despite higher R&D and staffing spending. Cost of sales fell 4.4% while gross profit rose 14.7%, lifting the gross margin to 76.6%. The company says it is funding its global push from core-business earnings.

Balance sheet room and portfolio cleanup

Standalone cash and cash equivalents at the end of the third quarter reached KRW 121.9 billion, up 69.4% from year-end 2025. Stakes in affiliates with limited AI synergy, including Hancom InSpace, have been sold to concentrate resources on AI. The value-up plan is expected within the fourth quarter.

09

Bear factors

Operating profit decline and investment burden

Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 36.4 billion, down 10.0% year on year. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 9.2 billion fell 25.5% from a year earlier, and the consolidated operating margin was 10.0%.

Heavier investment ahead of the Nomadian U.S. launch and agentic OS commercialization is cited as the main drag.

Quarterly swings in attributable net income

Attributable net income in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 2.91 billion, down 75.5% from a year earlier. Q4 2025 posted an attributable net loss of KRW 3.7 billion.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 63.6 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 120 billion in Q2 and KRW 92.1 billion in Q3, a volatility that keeps results hard to read.

Target cut and unproven overseas revenue

Shinhan Investment Corp. lowered its target price in its August 25, 2026 report. Overseas AI revenue is planned to scale from 2027, so it has not yet been proven in reported results. The debt ratio rose from 34.3% in 2023 to 46.7% in 2025.

10

Risk factors

Overseas execution

If the Nomadian U.S. launch in December 2026 or the global launch in March 2027 slips, the payback on investment could also be delayed. European operations are at the local preparation stage with Polish partners, and Japan eKYC revenue still needs to become visible. Overseas revenue is not yet large in reported results.

Public budgets and PoC conversion

Public-sector AI revenue depends on how quickly government AI budgets are set and spent. Because many public projects have been run as proof-of-concept trials, the rate of conversion into full contracts could determine results. Concentration on a few public buyers could pass timing changes straight through to revenue.

Balance sheet and quarterly earnings volatility

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 232 billion, and the debt ratio of 46.7% is higher than in 2023. Quarterly operating profit has moved between an operating loss of KRW 749 million in Q4 2025 and a profit in Q2 2026. One-time costs from stake disposals hitting quarterly results add another variable.

Overall view

Hancom's AI revenue reached KRW 28.3 billion in January–September 2026, more than three times the full-year 2025 AI revenue, as the company shifts its growth engine toward AI.

Annual revenue rose three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and operating profit peaked at KRW 40.4 billion in 2024 before falling to KRW 36.4 billion in 2025.

In the third quarter of 2026, consolidated operating profit fell 25.5% year on year to KRW 9.2 billion as Nomadian investment expanded, while standalone operating margin stayed in the 20% range. Attributable net income swings sharply from quarter to quarter, with KRW 30.4 billion over the last four quarters.

Bullish factors include the rising AI share of revenue, divestment of non-core affiliates and higher standalone cash. Bearish factors include lower operating profit, quarterly volatility, unproven overseas revenue and a higher debt ratio.

Shinhan Investment Corp. lowered its target price in August 2026 while keeping a Buy rating. The next checkpoints are the December 2026 Nomadian U.S. launch, the value-up plan within Q4 2026 and the Q4 2026 results.
