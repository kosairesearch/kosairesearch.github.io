Hecto Innovation: Revenue Growth, Margin Still the Question

Revenue reached KRW 375.8 billion in 2025 after three straight years of growth, but the operating margin fell from 15.3% in 2024 to 13.4%, and the debt ratio rose to 122.3%.

02

Business structure

Hecto Innovation is the holding company of the Hecto Group, with three business segments: IT information services, fintech, and healthcare.

The IT information services segment posted Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 35.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 9.0 billion, up 20.2% year on year, and the company said it set a record quarterly revenue for the sixth straight quarter.

The fintech segment booked Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 46.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.4 billion, driven mainly by merchant payment processing (PG) and simple cash payment services.

The healthcare segment recorded revenue of KRW 23.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.3 billion, while expanding its product line with the pet probiotic Dr. Simone Pet and the skincare line Only Ritual. First-half 2026 revenue grew 16.8% in IT information services, 18.3% in fintech, and 17.3% in healthcare.

Building on its security and authentication technology, the group is pursuing a stablecoin payment ecosystem with digital asset wallet infrastructure, and has presented a plan to link wallet, payment, and platform into one settlement rail.

New services include the sports prediction platform Polyball launched in March 2026, the participatory commerce platform Round, and the weather app Nalssi-dol. Among the same IT and software sector peers, Samsung SDS, NHN, and Ahnlab run different business mixes. As a multi-business holding structure, direct comparison with a single software company is limited.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue rose from KRW 263 billion in 2022 to KRW 288.5 billion in 2023, KRW 319.5 billion in 2024, and KRW 375.8 billion in 2025, and grew for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025. The 17.6% increase in 2025 was faster than the 10.7% gain in 2024.

Operating profit went from KRW 35.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 37.3 billion in 2023 (up 4.5%) and KRW 48.9 billion in 2024 (up 31.1%), then reached KRW 50.2 billion in 2025, up only 2.7%. The gap between revenue growth and operating profit growth was widest in 2025.

The operating margin was 13.6% in 2022, 12.9% in 2023, 15.3% in 2024, and 13.4% in 2025, peaking in 2024 before declining.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 29.5 billion in 2022, KRW 22.6 billion in 2023 (down 23.4%), KRW 27.9 billion in 2024 (up 23.7%), and KRW 30.5 billion in 2025 (up 9.4%), rising for two consecutive years after the 2023 low.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 36.8 billion in 2025, above the KRW 30.5 billion attributable to controlling shareholders. On a quarterly basis, Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 112.3 billion, up 13.6% from the prior quarter, while Q2 2026 revenue fell 8.4% to KRW 102.8 billion.

Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 12.9 billion was down 4.0% year on year, which the company attributed to investment in new service launches. Q4 2025 operating profit of KRW 10.4 billion was the lowest of the last five quarters in the disclosed figures, and it recovered to KRW 15.3 billion in Q3 2026.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 26 billion in 2022 to KRW 106 billion in 2024 and eased slightly to KRW 100.4 billion in 2025. The debt ratio climbed from 86.4% in 2022 to 122.3% in 2025, and total liabilities of KRW 439.8 billion at the end of 2025 exceeded total equity of KRW 359.5 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Hecto Innovation's core areas, IT information services and payments, depend on corporate digital transformation spending and online payment volumes. Within this, stablecoins are drawing attention as a new axis that could change how payments and settlement work.

The company said it is supporting domestic firms in claiming positions in the ecosystem around Circle’s stablecoin-dedicated blockchain mainnet, Arc, and that it provides digital asset wallet technology based on API.

Domestic stablecoin businesses may move at different speeds depending on licensing requirements and the scope of issuance and payments. Large players in the same sector include NAVER, Kakao, Samsung SDS, and NHN, which center on large-scale businesses such as platforms and cloud.

Hecto Innovation is more accurately described as a portfolio combining IT services, payments, and healthcare. The company highlighted that cumulative Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 324 billion surpassed KRW 300 billion for the first time, based on the preliminary disclosure dated November 10, 2026.

Three segments growing at double-digit rates at the same time in 2026 widens the scope of expansion. Whether growth in healthcare and fintech can persist should be checked against segment figures in the next regular report.

The key variable for the outlook is how much new-service investment weighs on the margin. The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual results and the 2026 annual report on operating margin.

08

Bull factors

Revenue growth spread across segments

Revenue rose for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, up 17.6% in 2025 to KRW 375.8 billion. Q3 2026 revenue rose 13.8% year on year to KRW 108.8 billion. The company said IT information services, fintech and healthcare all posted double-digit cumulative growth. Growth not dependent on one segment can be read as a bullish factor.

Recovery in quarterly profit

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders climbed from KRW 4.12 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 6.31 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 7.02 billion in Q2, and KRW 10 billion in Q3. Q3 operating profit of KRW 15.3 billion rose 4.0% year on year, and the company and media described it as a quarterly record.

Healthcare operating profit rising 77.8% from the prior quarter is also part of the improvement. Whether this momentum carries into 2027 is the next point to check.

Completion of the shareholder return plan

Under the three-year shareholder return plan announced in 2024, the company cancelled 1% of issued shares each year. On November 3, 2026, it decided to cancel 129,934 treasury shares, completing the three-year commitment. Returns that reduce the number of issued shares can be viewed as shareholder-friendly. The direction of returns after the plan ends should be confirmed through company disclosures.

09

Bear factors

Lower operating margin and slower profit growth

The operating margin fell from 15.3% in 2024 to 13.4% in 2025. Revenue rose 17.6% in 2025, but operating profit rose only 2.7%. Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 12.9 billion was down 4.0% year on year, which the company attributed to investment in new service launches. If the effect of these investments on margins comes later, it remains a bearish factor.

Rising debt and financial burden

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 439.8 billion, above total equity of KRW 359.5 billion. Operating cash flow eased from KRW 106 billion in 2024 to KRW 100.4 billion in 2025. With new investment and higher debt advancing together, the key question is how well cash generation supports them.

Uncertain timing of new business monetization

Stablecoin payment ecosystem efforts, the participatory commerce platform Round, and the sports prediction platform Polyball are at an early stage, making the timing of their revenue contribution hard to gauge.

The healthcare segment’s Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.3 billion means its contribution to earnings is limited. If new business investment is prolonged, quarterly operating profit may swing more widely. The unclear timing of monetization is a bearish factor.

10

Risk factors

Investment burden and earnings volatility

When investment in new service launches is executed, quarterly operating profit can move differently from revenue. In Q2 2026, operating profit actually declined year on year. Wide swings between quarters, such as KRW 10.4 billion in Q4 2025, also make earnings harder to judge. Margin management is a key variable.

Changes in the regulatory environment

Stablecoin and digital asset wallet businesses are affected by financial regulation and issuance and payment requirements. A change in requirements could alter launch schedules and business scope.

The company promotes a regulation-compliant digital asset model, but shifts in regulatory interpretation are hard for it to control. Cross-border payment models may move at different speeds under different national systems.

Segment concentration and financial leverage

In Q3 2026, IT information services operating profit of KRW 9.0 billion and fintech operating profit of KRW 5.4 billion made up a large share of the three segments’ combined operating profit. With the debt ratio at 122.3%, a dip in profit from either segment could reduce financial flexibility.

The relationship between controlling shareholders’ equity of KRW 222.3 billion and total liabilities of KRW 439.8 billion should be considered together.

Overall view

Recent trends at Hecto Innovation show revenue growth and profit recovery arriving together. Revenue reached KRW 375.8 billion in 2025 after three consecutive years of growth, and rose 13.8% year on year to KRW 108.8 billion in Q3 2026.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders has risen for three quarters since the Q4 2025 low, totaling KRW 27.5 billion over the latest four quarters.

By contrast, the operating margin fell from 15.3% in 2024 to 13.4% in 2025, and Q2 2026 operating profit declined year on year as new-service investment weighed. The debt ratio climbed to 122.3% in 2025, which also needs to be read as a financial burden.

The three-year shareholder return plan has been completed through annual 1% share cancellations, and brokers have pointed to both improved results and the recent share price decline when describing valuation appeal.

Bullish factors are balanced growth across segments and the quarterly profit recovery, while bearish factors are the stalled margin, rising debt, and uncertain timing of new-business monetization.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 preliminary annual results and the operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report.
