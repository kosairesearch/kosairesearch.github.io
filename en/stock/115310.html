02

Business structure

INFOvine, founded in February 2000, is a software company whose main business is a mobile certificate storage service used for online transactions that require public certification.

According to company materials, it commercialized UBIKey, a service linking wired and wireless networks based on its CTI (computer telephony integration) technology, which expanded from banking to public agencies and corporations. Its business is described around identity verification, payments, and content.

In 2023, the mobile certificate storage service accounted for 73.49% of revenue, followed by other mobile services at 12.71%, game services at 8.93%, and other businesses at 4.87%. In 2022, the certificate service was 79.89% and game services only 1.30%, so the game share rose sharply in 2023.

The game business started by supplying the Vinepay micropayment service to major game companies. A 2010 IPO-era report cited a 98% market share in mobile certificate storage, but that figure dates from the listing period and should not be read as the current share.

Peers often cited include AhnLab and NICE Information Service, though the narrow focus limits direct comparison.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue rose to KRW 22.8 billion in 2023 (up 9.0%), KRW 25.2 billion in 2024 (up 10.5%), and KRW 28.7 billion in 2025 (up 14.0%), growing three years in a row from 2023 with 2025 the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 7.87 billion in 2024 (up 1.5%) before falling 18.0% to KRW 6.46 billion in 2025, the smallest year of the period. Operating profit fell in 2025 even as revenue grew, which means costs rose faster than sales, and the operating margin dropped from 31.3% in 2024 to 22.5% in 2025.

Causes of cost increases and any one-off items should be checked in the notes to the annual report. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 8.97 billion in 2024 (up 25.3%) and fell 21.5% to KRW 7.04 billion in 2025.

In both 2024 and 2025, this figure exceeded operating profit, suggesting a meaningful contribution from non-operating income, which is consistent with a financial income line of about KRW 3.3 billion reported for 2024.

Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts as controlling-shareholder net income in each year.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 7.2 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 7.06 billion, KRW 6.87 billion, KRW 6.66 billion, and KRW 6.65 billion through the third quarter of 2026, a fourth straight quarterly decline, with the latest quarter down 7.7% year on year.

Quarterly operating profit fell from KRW 2.39 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.92 billion in the first quarter of 2026, rebounded to KRW 2.16 billion in the second quarter (up 12.7% quarter on quarter), and was KRW 2.06 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 13.8% year on year.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue was nearly flat on the prior quarter (down 0.2%) while operating profit fell 4.8%, which points to cost growth as the driver.

Quarterly net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.67 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 17.5% quarter on quarter and 29.5% year on year, and totaled KRW 7.88 billion over the last four quarters.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 7.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.7 billion in 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose modestly from 8.0% to 9.8% over the same period.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for mobile certificate storage depends on how identity verification is handled in online financial and public transactions.

The end of mandatory use of public certificates in December 2020 changed the structure of demand for certificate-based authentication, and as more authentication methods emerge, the importance of any single method can shift. This change lines up with the company's recent revenue trend.

When the certificate business accounts for most revenue, a slowdown in authentication demand flows directly into total sales. Game services rose from 1.30% of revenue in 2022 to 8.93% in 2023, and because they respond to different drivers than authentication, the mix shift can add volatility to earnings.

NAVER and Kakao, which operate in the same IT and software sector, keep identity verification and payments inside their own ecosystems, so their business structure differs from INFOvine's.

System integrators such as Samsung SDS, Hyundai Autoever, and LG CNS depend on corporate IT spending cycles, which makes their demand profile different from consumer-facing authentication.

Position against the industry average is therefore hard to judge from a single metric, and earnings are easier to explain when authentication demand and the game share are read together.

The 7.7% year-on-year revenue decline in the third quarter of 2026 is consistent with these demand-side changes feeding into reported results.

08

Bull factors

Low debt burden

Total liabilities were KRW 9.31 billion at the end of 2025 against KRW 94.9 billion of equity, with the debt-to-equity ratio at 9.8%. The ratio edged up from 8.0% in 2022, but low reliance on borrowing leaves room to absorb weaker earnings. Controlling-shareholder equity and total equity are both reported at KRW 94.9 billion.

Continued shareholder-return disclosures

The March 2026 stock split cut par value from KRW 500 to KRW 100 to widen the tradable share base. In September, the board resolved to cancel 1,132,866 treasury shares, about 7.6% of issued shares, with a book value of about KRW 6.4 billion. A cash dividend was approved at the March annual general meeting.

A quarterly operating profit rebound

Operating profit rose 12.7% quarter on quarter to KRW 2.16 billion in the second quarter of 2026 from KRW 1.92 billion in the first quarter, even as revenue slipped slightly. Cost management may have contributed.

Operating profit fell back to KRW 2.06 billion in the third quarter, so whether the rebound is a trend remains to be confirmed.

Bear factors

Four straight quarters of falling revenue

Revenue declined from KRW 7.06 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 6.65 billion in the third quarter of 2026 across four consecutive quarters. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was down 7.7% year on year.

Because the certificate business is the core, demand changes feed quickly into quarterly sales, which is a bearish factor.

Margin compression and lower operating cash flow

The operating margin fell from 31.3% in 2024 to 22.5% in 2025, and operating profit dropped 18.0% to KRW 6.46 billion. Operating cash flow declined from KRW 7.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.7 billion in 2025.

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was down 13.8% year on year, so the earnings decline is visible on a quarterly basis as well.

Shifting authentication demand and game-mix volatility

Since mandatory use of public certificates ended in December 2020, authentication methods have diversified, and the mobile certificate storage service accounted for 73.49% of 2023 revenue. The game share jumped from 1.30% in 2022 to 8.93% in 2023, so mix swings can become a source of earnings volatility. If both pressures persist, the revenue decline could be slow to stop.

10

Risk factors

Regulatory and demand risk

Diversifying authentication methods and regulatory changes could directly affect demand for the mobile certificate storage service. The company has extended its reach from banks to public agencies and corporations, but if competition among authentication methods intensifies, both usage and unit pricing could weaken. Trends should be tracked in the segment revenue tables.

Concentration and earnings volatility

The certificate service made up 73.49% of revenue in 2023, while the game share jumped from 1.30% in 2022 to 8.93% in 2023. When a few businesses carry most of the revenue, demand shifts in one segment move total earnings. The 18.0% drop in 2025 operating profit should be broken down by segment.

Governance and shareholder structure

At the March 2026 annual general meeting, the director pay limit passed with 32.9% approval of issued voting shares, with interested parties' votes excluded from the calculation per the disclosure.

In a structure with a concentrated largest shareholder, minority shareholders' views may have limited channels to influence management. This should be watched alongside the next meeting's agenda.

Overall view

INFOvine's revenue, centered on its mobile certificate storage service, grew three years in a row from 2023 to 2025, but operating profit fell in 2025 and quarterly revenue has declined for four straight quarters through the third quarter of 2026.

The operating margin fell from 31.3% in 2024 to 22.5% in 2025, and operating cash flow dropped to KRW 4.7 billion in 2025.

The balance sheet is conservative, with a debt-to-equity ratio of 9.8% and equity of KRW 94.9 billion. 2026 brought a stock split, a cash dividend decision, and the cancellation of treasury shares with a book value of about KRW 6.4 billion.

Bullish factors include the low debt burden, the shareholder-return steps, and the operating profit rebound in the second quarter of 2026. Bearish factors include the run of quarterly revenue declines, the lower margin, and a business still concentrated in certificate services.

Both sets of factors come from the same disclosures, and which one weighs more will show up in upcoming periodic reports. The next checkpoints are revenue trends and the operating margin in the 2026 annual report, due around late March 2027.
