02

Business structure

Ji-Tech makes high-purity chemical materials and precision system solutions used in semiconductor processes. Its core product is precursors used in semiconductor deposition, alongside photomask cases, intermediates for OLED organic materials, and distribution of specialty gases for semiconductor processes.

An outside information summary says most revenue comes from the precursor and specialty gas businesses. Within precursors, the company has reportedly been expanding higher-value products such as Si precursors and High-K precursors.

Its main customers are identified as domestic memory chip makers and overseas chipmakers including those in China.

Its subsidiary DaeHeung CCU began commercial operation of a CCUS plant in December 2023, which captures carbon dioxide from power plant exhaust in Saemangeum to produce liquefied carbon dioxide, and the company has targeted annual greenhouse gas reduction of about 30,000 tons.

The company's business page says a lithium carbonate production facility is planned for the third Saemangeum plant. Under the listing classification the company is categorized as a chemical products manufacturer, so its actual business structure differs from the biopharma category in the requested sector label.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue went from KRW 68.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 43.4 billion in 2023, down 36.5%, then rose to KRW 57.3 billion in 2024, up 32.1%, and fell to KRW 53.6 billion in 2025, down 6.5%. 2022 was the largest year in the period and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit bottomed at KRW 4.09 billion in 2023, down 64.1%, then rose to KRW 8.71 billion in 2024, up 113.1%, and to KRW 10.4 billion in 2025, up 19.4%, marking two consecutive years of growth from 2024 through 2025.

An outside brokerage analysis attributed the 2023 decline to a downturn in semiconductor demand that cut sales in the precursor and specialty gas units.

The operating margin fell from 16.7% in 2022 to 9.4% in 2023, then recovered to 15.2% in 2024 and 19.4% in 2025, and the 2025 improvement is described as cost reduction offsetting lower revenue.

Net income attributable to owners was KRW 10.9 billion in 2022, KRW 3.75 billion in 2023, down 65.7%, KRW 10.7 billion in 2024, up 186.5%, and KRW 6.74 billion in 2025, down 37.2%. In 2024 this figure exceeded operating profit of KRW 8.71 billion, which suggests non-operating items had a large effect.

Net income including non-controlling interests, a different measure, was KRW 6.67 billion in 2025. Quarterly, Q4 2025 revenue was KRW 13.5 billion and operating profit KRW 2.96 billion, yet the company posted a net loss attributable to owners of KRW 393 million, a swing into loss.

In Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 15.4 billion, operating profit KRW 3.9 billion and net income attributable to owners KRW 3.87 billion, a return to profit.

Q2 2026 revenue reached KRW 21.9 billion, up 41.7% from the prior quarter, operating profit KRW 6.33 billion, up 62.4%, and net income attributable to owners KRW 3.94 billion, up 1.8%.

In Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 17.7 billion, down 19.0% from the prior quarter but up 11.3% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 5.11 billion, up 61.9% year on year, while net income attributable to owners was KRW 1.58 billion, down 54.8% year on year, so operating profit and owner net income moved in opposite directions.

Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 7.12 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.09 billion in 2023, then KRW 16.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.32 billion in 2025. The sum of net income attributable to owners over the last four quarters was KRW 8.99 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The semiconductor and display markets move with electronics demand, and one summary states that in 2025 lower revenue was offset by cost reductions that improved profitability.

Precursors are essential materials in semiconductor deposition, and a securities information report noted that in Q3 2024, precursors still made up 79% of revenue despite a slowdown in memory demand.

Order volumes shift with memory chip utilization and demand from Chinese customers, so earnings swing considerably with the chip cycle. The 36.5% drop in 2023 revenue illustrates this structure. On the display side, the company plans to run an OLED organic material synthesis facility from 2026.

India is described as a market where the government actively supports semiconductor self-sufficiency, and the company is using its Indian subsidiary as an overseas production base.

In its March 2026 corporate value enhancement plan, the company listed expanding semiconductor materials sales and diversifying its global customer base as execution priorities. Alongside these, it is pursuing CCUS under carbon neutrality policy, which adds businesses outside semiconductors.

Outlook

In its corporate value enhancement plan disclosed on March 31, 2026, the company listed expanding semiconductor materials sales, diversifying its global customer base, and adding capacity and reorganizing production lines as execution priorities.

The same plan said it would maintain a sustainable dividend policy based on stable cash flow, and a news report put the 2025 payout ratio at 46.74%.

The Indian subsidiary completed its first production building and began local production in September 2025, and it targets completing all seven buildings and establishing full operations in 2026. This target appears on the company's business page, and whether it is met has not yet been confirmed.

The OLED organic material synthesis facility is planned to run in 2026. A lithium carbonate production facility is planned for the third plant, which points to a possible expansion into secondary battery materials.

At the March 2026 annual general meeting, shareholders approved adding business purposes covering waste recycling and renewable energy such as solar power to the articles of incorporation.

In the first half of 2026, cash of KRW 9.1 billion came in from a capital reduction payment of KRW 5 billion from an affiliate and KRW 4.1 billion from treasury share disposal, and this cash was reported to have funded facility investment and a KRW 3 billion CB repayment, leaving KRW 7 billion of CB outstanding. Further facility investment for new businesses is reported to continue.

Bull factors

Operating profit recovery

Operating profit grew in 2024 and 2025 after bottoming in 2023, and the 2025 operating margin of 19.4% is the highest of the 2022 to 2025 years. Q2 2026 operating profit was KRW 6.33 billion, up 62.4% from the prior quarter.

Q3 2026 operating profit rose 61.9% year on year, so the operating improvement is visible across several quarters.

New businesses and overseas production base

The Indian subsidiary targets completing seven buildings in 2026, and an OLED organic material synthesis facility is planned to run that year. Subsidiary DaeHeung CCU began commercial CCUS operation in December 2023, with planned liquefied carbon dioxide output of 35,000 tons a year.

A planned lithium carbonate facility at the third plant is another element that could broaden the business scope.

Balance sheet and shareholder returns

The 2025 year-end debt ratio of 65.7% was lower than 73.2% in 2024, and the first half of 2026 brought KRW 9.1 billion in cash from an affiliate's capital reduction and treasury share disposal. Operating cash flow turned positive in 2023 and reached KRW 16.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 9.32 billion in 2025.

In March 2026 the company disclosed a corporate value enhancement plan and said it would strengthen shareholder return policies.

Bear factors

Sensitivity to the chip cycle

In 2023 revenue fell 36.5% and operating profit fell 64.1%, and outside analysis attributed this to a weaker chip cycle. Q3 2026 revenue was down 19.0% from the prior quarter. Because precursor demand depends on memory utilization, earnings can move quickly when the cycle turns.

Volatile net income

Net income attributable to owners fell 37.2% in 2025, and Q4 2025 swung into a net loss. In Q3 2026 operating profit rose 61.9% year on year, but net income attributable to owners fell 54.8%, so the operating improvement did not carry through to the bottom line. The causes of non-operating items need to be checked in the periodic reports.

Funding pressure

KRW 7 billion of CB remains outstanding, and an August 2026 report noted the share price was below the conversion price, which makes early repayment a variable. Operating cash flow of KRW 9.32 billion in 2025 was lower than KRW 16.8 billion in 2024. Facility investment for new businesses is set to continue, so cash uses are increasing.

10

Risk factors

Chip cycle and customer concentration

The precursor and specialty gas businesses depend on the chip cycle and orders from specific customers. Around 2024 a securities analysis pointed to supply centered on one major customer in the precursor unit. As in 2023, a downturn can cut revenue and operating profit sharply at the same time.

Financing and funding

The remaining KRW 7 billion of CB and planned facility investment add to cash uses. The KRW 9.1 billion raised in the first half of 2026 came from an affiliate's capital reduction and treasury share disposal, which differs in nature from cash generated by operations. If net income attributable to owners keeps declining, assessments of financial capacity could change.

Execution of new businesses and overseas plant

Several projects run at once, including completing seven buildings at the Indian subsidiary, running the OLED material facility, introducing lithium carbonate equipment, and CCUS liquefied carbon dioxide production. For many of these, target dates and the timing of revenue generation have not been confirmed. Until new businesses become larger, semiconductor materials remain the center of earnings.

Overall view

Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 21.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 6.33 billion rose sharply from the prior quarter, and Q3 2026 operating profit was still up 61.9% year on year.

Net income attributable to owners, however, fell 37.2% in 2025 and 54.8% year on year in Q3 2026, so operating gains have not carried through to the bottom line.

On the business side, completion of seven buildings in India, operation of the OLED material facility, and the lithium carbonate plan are checkpoints around the end of 2026. Revenue volatility from the chip cycle and reliance on specific customers remain structural issues.
