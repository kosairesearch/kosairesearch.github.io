02

Business structure

SEMI Total Solution designs and supplies automated material handling systems (AMHS) that move wafers and components between process steps in semiconductor front-end fabs. The company was founded in 2014 and listed on KOSDAQ in June 2026 through a merger with NH SPAC 29.

Its core products are clean conveyor systems that transfer wafers and nitrogen purge systems that fill tools with nitrogen to support yield. The company says its purge system is the only one globally that can be added to existing equipment without modifying it.

Projects run on a per-customer basis, and revenue is recognized as design, fabrication, installation, and inspection steps are completed, so timing shifts from quarter to quarter. By region, overseas customers accounted for 55.9% of 2025 revenue.

In the first half of 2026, however, one domestic customer generated KRW 12.7 billion, or 83.5% of the total. The overseas subsidiary won a project worth about KRW 11 billion from a global chipmaker's new fab in August 2026, and the parent signed a KRW 7.3 billion clean conveyor supply contract with that subsidiary.

Compared with process equipment makers such as Wonik IPS and Jusung Engineering, which focus on tools that perform deposition or etching, SEMI Total Solution specializes in the logistics and gas-management layer that links those tools.

The company is also developing a third-generation aerial transfer robot (AMR) to extend its technology to secondary batteries, biotech, and other advanced manufacturing.

03

Recent trends

Based on the Q3 2026 filing, revenue was KRW 10.1 billion, up 400.8% year on year. Operating profit was KRW 3.42 billion, a swing to profit from a year earlier, and net income was KRW 2.12 billion, up 674.5% year on year.

According to Newspim, standalone Q3 revenue was KRW 10.1 billion, and cumulative 2026 revenue reached KRW 25.3 billion, surpassing 2025 full-year revenue of KRW 23.0 billion within three quarters. The same report put cumulative operating profit at KRW 7.5 billion, with an operating margin of about 30%.

The company attributed the improvement to revenue recognition on major semiconductor customer projects, which ramped up in Q3 after design and fabrication work limited recognition in Q2.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 12.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 4.5 billion, but Q2 revenue fell to KRW 2.5 billion and posted an operating loss of KRW 0.4 billion. First-half revenue was KRW 15.2 billion and operating profit KRW 4.0 billion, down 13.3% and 42.2% year on year.

First-half net loss of KRW 12.1 billion reflected a one-time KRW 16.9 billion listing cost tied to the SPAC merger. The shares closed at KRW 2,665 on September 11, 2026, 40.9% below the merger reference price of KRW 4,512. As of the writing time, the share price was KRW 1,890 and market capitalization was KRW 57.0 billion.

04

Outlook

The company has set a 2026 revenue target of KRW 30.0 billion. With first-half revenue at KRW 15.2 billion, reaching the target requires recognizing KRW 14.8 billion in the second half. The company expects additional fourth-quarter revenue from projects scheduled for completion within the year.

On the order side, the backlog of about KRW 27.3 billion that the company disclosed in April 2026 is now supplemented by the KRW 11 billion overseas project won in August.

Because the pace at which backlog converts into revenue drives quarterly results, any slippage in project completion would widen quarterly swings again.

On technology, the nitrogen purge system is undergoing qualification tests at semiconductor fabs in Korea and abroad, and a demonstration test at a Japan-based fab aims at application on an existing production line. The company presents these results as support for second-half growth.

It is also pursuing a stocker purge system that extends nitrogen purge technology to the wafer storage section, along with next-generation logistics equipment.

Three items mark the next checkpoints: the fourth-quarter revenue recognized and whether projects finish within the year; the qualification results for the nitrogen purge system and progress in winning overseas customers; and how far dependence on the single domestic customer declines.

05

Bull factors

Sustained high-margin structure

Operating margin stayed at a high level after the 2024 return to profit, at 28.4% in 2024 and 27.8% in 2025. The Q3 2026 operating margin was about 34%. The company attributes the high profitability to a business structure centered on high-value solutions.

Margins rose in the quarter when project revenue concentrated, which the company cites as evidence that the structure works alongside revenue growth.

Expanding order pipeline

The overseas subsidiary won a new project worth about KRW 11 billion in August 2026. The parent signed a KRW 7.3 billion clean conveyor supply contract with the subsidiary, equal to 31.7% of 2025 consolidated revenue. In April 2026 the company said its backlog was about KRW 27.3 billion, above 2025 revenue. Broadening the order base overseas aligns with the goal of reducing reliance on one domestic customer.

Debt-free balance sheet

As of end-June 2026, consolidated total assets were KRW 76.8 billion, liabilities KRW 5.9 billion, and equity KRW 70.9 billion. The debt ratio fell from 38.4% at the end of 2025 to 8.3%.

Cash and equivalents of KRW 27.8 billion plus other current financial assets of KRW 32.5 billion total KRW 60.3 billion, about 79% of total assets. The company says it has kept a debt-free approach since its founding and plans to use this capacity for R&D and production expansion.

Bear factors

Large quarterly swings

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 12.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 4.5 billion, but Q2 revenue dropped to KRW 2.5 billion and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 0.4 billion. The company explains that revenue recognition timing shifted with project stages.

Q3 revenue recovered to KRW 10.1 billion, yet quarterly results still depend on the progress of specific projects. The volatility may appear larger externally as reporting becomes more frequent.

Single-customer dependence

One domestic customer generated KRW 12.7 billion in first-half 2026 revenue, or 83.5% of the total. Overseas customers accounted for 55.9% of 2025 revenue, so the main customer base has changed. Even so, a few customers' project schedules still have an outsized effect on results. Diversification of the customer base remains a structural weakness still in progress.

First-half decline and target pressure

First-half revenue was KRW 15.2 billion, down 13.3% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 4.0 billion, down 42.2%. Reaching the company's 2026 revenue target of KRW 30.0 billion requires recognizing KRW 14.8 billion in the second half.

The first-half net loss of KRW 12.1 billion includes a one-time KRW 16.9 billion merger-related cost. Shares trading below the KRW 4,512 merger reference price since listing indicate that market valuation remains low.

07

Risk factors

Macro

Semiconductor capital spending is cyclical and moves with memory and foundry demand. If customers delay fab expansion, the timing of AMHS orders and installations can slip as well. As overseas projects grow, exchange-rate movements also affect revenue and profit more. These external factors lie largely outside the company's control.

Customer and project

One domestic customer accounted for 83.5% of first-half 2026 revenue. Because revenue is deferred while projects move through design, fabrication, installation, and inspection, a schedule slip of even one quarter can sharply reduce quarterly results. The company expects fourth-quarter revenue, but realization depends on completing those projects.

Technology and expansion

The nitrogen purge system is in qualification testing at fabs in Korea and abroad, but it has not yet been confirmed as a volume revenue source.

The third-generation aerial transfer robot and expansion into non-semiconductor areas such as secondary batteries and biotech still require development schedules and customer validation. Entering those industries may bring different qualification standards from the current business. Maintaining profitability while R&D spending rises remains a challenge.

08

Overall view

SEMI Total Solution swung to profit in its Q3 2026 filing, with revenue of KRW 10.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 3.42 billion, which points to improving profitability. An operating margin above 30% reflects a business structure centered on high-value equipment.

However, Q2 2026 posted an operating loss of KRW 0.4 billion, and first-half revenue declined year on year. Because results depend on when projects are recognized, the gap between quarters is the central variable.

On customers, one domestic customer represented 83.5% of first-half revenue, a high level of concentration. On finances, the debt-free approach and KRW 60.3 billion in financial assets at end-June form a stable base.

The company presents overseas orders and qualification results for the nitrogen purge system as grounds for second-half growth.

The current share price of KRW 1,890 and market capitalization of KRW 57.0 billion sit below the KRW 4,512 merger reference price, and how the market assesses the company will become clearer through future quarterly results and order disclosures.
