Report overview

Lithium in name only, losses persist as control is restructured

Despite a name that points to lithium, the company reported almost no lithium sales in 2025, and it is reorganizing around subsidiary LMK and semiconductor cleaning technology while sales and operating losses continue.

  1. 1

    Sales in 2025 were KRW 10.7 billion, roughly half the KRW 20.7 billion recorded in 2022, and have declined for three straight years from 2023 to 2025.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    The company's cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 10.8 billion. It swung to a profit of KRW 972 million in the second quarter of 2026, but that was a single quarter.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Lithium-for-earth has built its core business on IP-based content products and mobile phone accessories. In the first quarter of 2025, sales were made up of phone accessories at 80.9% and fragrance products at 10.1%, with no lithium-related sales.

A filing showed that lithium business sales in 2025 were KRW 8 million, or 0.1% of total sales.

The company changed its name to Lithium-for-earth in 2022 while announcing entry into lithium materials for secondary batteries, and in April 2023 it bought a site in the Saemangeum national industrial complex for about KRW 17 billion.

However, it said in its business report that external funding for the plant's project costs has been difficult, so it is revising the business plan, and it booked impairments of KRW 9.1 billion on land and equipment and KRW 1.1 billion in bad-debt write-offs on loans at the end of 2025.

Subsidiary LMK is developing a 'PFAS-Free' technology that uses plasma to clean and regenerate core semiconductor components. Its cell-culture substrate technology received a US patent grant decision.

On the content side, the company signed a global distribution and operating deal with game developer Igimob and secured exclusive operating rights to the 'Geongeurebeu' IP game on Roblox.

In the secondary battery sector, it is classified alongside large cathode and cell makers such as LG Energy Solution, POSCO Future M and Ecopro BM, but its disclosed business scale is far smaller than theirs.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 20.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.6 billion in 2023 (down 24.6% year on year), KRW 15.2 billion in 2024 (down 3.0%), and KRW 10.7 billion in 2025 (down 29.7%), declining three years in a row from 2023 to 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year for sales and 2025 the smallest.

Operating losses were KRW 3.75 billion in 2022, KRW 6.81 billion in 2023, KRW 5.22 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.48 billion in 2025, an operating loss in each of the four years from 2022 to 2025. Asia Economy reported in April 2026 that losses have now continued for five years.

Operating margins were -18.1% in 2022, -43.6% in 2023, -34.4% in 2024 and -42.1% in 2025. The 2025 operating loss amount was smaller than in 2024, but because sales shrank, the operating margin deteriorated.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 89.6 billion in 2022, a profit despite an operating loss of KRW 3.75 billion, which points to non-operating items as the source of the gap.

In 2023 it turned to a net loss of KRW 182.8 billion, and the loss narrowed to KRW 102.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 13.4 billion in 2025.

Quarterly sales were KRW 2.67 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 3.06 billion in the fourth quarter (up 14.6% from the prior quarter), KRW 2.93 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 4.88 billion in the second quarter (up 66.6%), and KRW 3.2 billion in the third quarter of 2026 (down 34.4% from the prior quarter but up 19.7% year on year).

Over the same span, operating losses were KRW 841 million, KRW 1.1 billion, KRW 1.9 billion, KRW 1.33 billion and KRW 1.78 billion, a loss in each of the five quarters from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

The second-quarter sales jump did not reduce the operating loss, which narrowed only modestly from the first quarter, so top-line recovery did not translate into better profitability.

The fourth quarter of 2025 saw a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 10.3 billion, a period that coincides with the year-end impairment of KRW 9.1 billion and bad-debt write-offs of KRW 1.1 billion.

The second quarter of 2026 turned to a profit of KRW 972 million despite continued operating losses, a result that reads as driven by non-operating items. The third quarter of 2026 returned to a net loss of KRW 428 million.

The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 10.8 billion.

Balance sheet figures at the end of 2025 show total equity of KRW 55.9 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, total liabilities of KRW 3.25 billion and a debt ratio of 5.8%, so the debt burden is low. Equity, however, fell from KRW 117.3 billion at the end of 2023 to KRW 55.9 billion at the end of 2025.

Operating cash flow was negative in each year, at -KRW 3.2 billion in 2022, -KRW 4.28 billion in 2023, -KRW 3.64 billion in 2024 and -KRW 4.89 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Lithium-for-earth is classified in the secondary battery materials sector, but lithium-related revenue accounts for only 0.1% of disclosed sales, so company-specific business transition matters more than sector trends for its results.

The stock has been traded as a lithium-related name whenever lithium issues surface, according to press reports. Asia Economy reported that the share price has swung sharply on lithium news even though lithium business sales have been virtually nil, a gap between business substance and the share price.

In a report from October 2026, Business Post listed whether lithium production facilities are operating, product sales, subsidiary results and the capital and additional funding plan after the capital reduction as the key items to watch.

In the competitive landscape, cathode makers POSCO Future M and Ecopro BM and cell makers LG Energy Solution and Samsung SDI operate at a very different scale in production and customer base. In battery recycling and materials, SungEel HiTech is a comparable name in the same sector.

LMK's plasma cleaning and regeneration technology for semiconductors is still at the development stage, and no sales contribution has been disclosed yet. The game IP business operates on Roblox, a platform with more than 100 million daily active users, and sits within the same content business the company already runs.

The company's industry position can therefore be summarized as a mix of lithium materials pre-commercialization, semiconductor component cleaning, and IP licensing.

06

Outlook

On governance, the company said on November 10, 2026 that it had secured 99.9% of LMK, and it plans to acquire the remaining shares to make LMK a wholly owned subsidiary before pursuing restructuring, including a merger.

The deal was structured so that payments for the existing shares were recycled into a rights offering, and the company said no cash left the business. In a third-party allotment in November 2026, director Byun Jae-seok's holdings were reported to have increased to 2,204,534 shares, a 16.85% stake.

Executive director Myung Hyun-sik was also reported to have acquired 106,288 shares through a third-party allotment on November 11, 2026. On shareholder returns, the company decided to cancel treasury shares in September 2026.

On financial matters, the company said it is focused on resolving financial uncertainty and exiting administrative-issue stock status in early 2027. On legal matters, a first-instance win in a suit over a fraudulent transfer of assets, worth around KRW 40 billion, was reported as progress toward recovering receivables.

New business lines include global distribution and operating rights for the 'Geongeurebeu' IP, a US patent decision on its cell-culture substrate technology, and semiconductor component cleaning and regeneration. The key variable for the lithium business is the outcome of the plan revision at the Saemangeum plant.

In its business report, the company said the likelihood of a termination event under the move-in contract has risen, and it recognized part of the assets under construction as an impairment. The next point to verify is whether lithium sales actually appear in disclosed results.

08

Bull factors

Ownership consolidation under way

Following an announcement on November 10, 2026, the company said it holds 99.9% of LMK and plans to make it a wholly owned subsidiary and pursue a merger. Payments for the existing shares were recycled into a rights offering, which the company described as a transaction without cash outflow. The stated goal is a simpler decision-making structure.

Low debt burden

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 3.25 billion, with a debt ratio of 5.8%, below the 33.1% recorded in 2022. All KRW 55.9 billion of total equity is attributable to controlling shareholders. The debt ratio has stayed in single digits since 2024, which means limited reliance on borrowing.

Diversifying business options

The company is pursuing the 'Geongeurebeu' IP distribution and operating rights, a US patent decision on cell-culture substrates, and semiconductor component cleaning technology in parallel. A first-instance win in a roughly KRW 40 billion asset-transfer suit was reported as progress in recovering receivables. Still, these businesses have not yet shown a contribution in disclosed results.

Bear factors

Sales down three years in a row

Sales in 2025 were KRW 10.7 billion, roughly half the KRW 20.7 billion of 2022. They have fallen for three consecutive years since 2023, with a 29.7% drop in 2025 alone. The operating margin worsened from -34.4% in 2024 to -42.1% in 2025.

Gap in the lithium business

Lithium business sales in 2025 were KRW 8 million, or 0.1% of the total. The Saemangeum plant faces difficulty raising external funds, is undergoing a business plan revision, and carries a KRW 9.1 billion impairment. The business report also says the likelihood of a contract termination event has increased.

Recent quarterly losses and governance questions

The operating loss for the third quarter of 2026 widened to KRW 1.78 billion from a year earlier. One press report, citing securities industry analysis, described the 2025 rights offering as closer to a circulation of owner-family funds than outside capital.

Risk factors

Funding and dilution

Reports said total equity was KRW 52.3 billion in the first quarter of 2025, less than half the KRW 117.3 billion at the end of 2023, and that cash and equivalents fell from KRW 22.9 billion to KRW 19.1 billion. A third-party allotment for operating funds was corrected several times in its disclosures. If additional funding is needed, dilution for existing shareholders could continue.

Administrative-issue designation and listing status

The company was designated as an administrative-issue stock in 2025, and it says it is working to resolve financial uncertainty and exit that status in early 2027.

Operating losses have continued for four consecutive years from 2022, and net losses for three from 2023, so whether the status is lifted depends on how operating and net results move.

Related-party-centered control

Related parties including Byun Hee-jo and five others were reported to hold 56.02% as of October 26, 2026. Press coverage citing market analysts said internal disputes, including a control dispute, have damaged market trust. Fund flows among related parties and changes in their holdings remain items to monitor.

12

Overall view

Despite its name, Lithium-for-earth reports lithium business at just 0.1% of disclosed sales. Sales fell from KRW 20.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.7 billion in 2025, and operating and net losses have continued.

The balance sheet is lightly indebted, with total equity of KRW 55.9 billion and a debt ratio of 5.8% at the end of 2025, but equity has fallen by more than half from KRW 117.3 billion at the end of 2023.

On the business side, the 99.9% stake in LMK, semiconductor cleaning technology and game IP licensing are presented as new pillars. On the other side, the Saemangeum lithium plant is undergoing a business plan revision because of funding difficulties, and its impairment has already been booked.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters total KRW 10.8 billion, and the swing to profit in the second quarter of 2026 was a single quarter.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual business report due by late March 2027, any disclosure on the LMK merger, and the progress toward exiting administrative-issue status.
