Construction starts rise, but profit recovery is still the test

02

Business structure

Xi S&D is a subsidiary of GS Construction and runs three segments: construction, housing, and Home Solutions (HS).

The construction segment handles cleanroom and fab facilities for semiconductor, display and secondary battery processes, along with data center work, and accounted for about 72% of consolidated revenue in Q3 2026 at KRW 302.8 billion.

Its operating profit in the same quarter was KRW 1.8 billion, an operating margin of 0.6%. The housing segment has been shifting toward mid-sized redevelopment projects, urban mixed-use buildings and self-developed projects rather than large reconstruction schemes.

The company's 2026 order target of KRW 3.2 trillion is split into construction KRW 1.4 trillion, housing KRW 1.5 trillion and HS KRW 0.3 trillion, against a revenue target of KRW 1.6 trillion.

The HS segment provides residential ancillary services and property operations and posted Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 65.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.8 billion, an 8.8% margin. On a cumulative basis through Q3 2026, HS generated about 21% of consolidated revenue but about 82% of consolidated operating profit.

The company exited general apartment management at the end of 2025 and is concentrating on senior housing and care. Its construction-capability ranking rose from 211th in 2019 to 54th in 2024.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 2.48 trillion in 2022 to KRW 2.37 trillion in 2023 (down 4.2%), KRW 1.58 trillion in 2024 (down 33.5%) and KRW 1.39 trillion in 2025 (down 12.1%), a three-year decline from 2023 through 2025.

Media reports tied the 2022 jump to the acquisition of an LG Group-affiliated construction company that year, when revenue had been KRW 435.7 billion in 2021. The operating margin fell from 8.0% in 2022 to 5.3% in 2023 and 0.2% in 2024 before edging up to 0.9% in 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 2.38 billion in 2024 to KRW 12.5 billion in 2025, up 426.3%, which the company attributed to cost structure improvement, fixed cost reduction and profit-focused selective bidding.

In 2025, profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 35.6 billion, up 2,058.2%, while net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 37.1 billion.

Operating cash flow turned from KRW -890 billion... more precisely, from -KRW 89 billion in 2024 to KRW 63.3 billion in 2025, and the debt ratio fell from 170.4% in 2022 to 71.0% in 2025.

Quarterly revenue fell to KRW 335 billion in Q4 2025 and bottomed at KRW 270.3 billion in Q1 2026, then rose for two consecutive quarters to KRW 303.3 billion in Q2 and KRW 419.4 billion in Q3 2026.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 2.53 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 11.3 billion in Q1 2026, then fell for two consecutive quarters to KRW 9.04 billion in Q2 and KRW 7.75 billion in Q3.

Shinhan Investment Corp. attributed the Q3 shortfall to a delay in the execution cost-rate adjustment for a data center project until Q4 and KRW 2.8 billion of cost tied to housing site subcontractors.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders in Q3 2026 was KRW 6.97 billion, down 77.5% year on year, and the four-quarter total from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026 was KRW 20.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The company is expanding data center construction to meet AI infrastructure demand. Hanwha Investment & Securities said in an August 2026 report that data center expansion at its construction subsidiary could serve as a new growth momentum.

Shinhan Investment Corp. said in a November 2026 report that construction volume already started, rather than new data center orders, is supporting earnings. Analysts also noted that accounting rules require conservative early-stage cost-rate recognition, so profit from these projects lags revenue.

The housing market remains weak, and the company has been favoring mid-sized projects and limiting project financing risk through public auction land acquisitions and blind fund structures.

The company expects the urban redevelopment market to expand significantly, but this is management's view rather than a third-party estimate. In construction, the company highlights its cleanroom design and build capabilities for semiconductor, display and battery facilities.

Its construction profitability remains low, so the evidence for a margin edge over peers is limited. Media reports attribute its housing bidding to a higher construction-capability ranking and brand recognition.

06

Outlook

The company set 2026 targets of KRW 3.2 trillion in orders and KRW 1.6 trillion in revenue. Cumulative orders through Q3 2026 reached KRW 2.67 trillion, up 37.3% year on year and equal to 83.4% of the annual target.

Media reports citing company data said the construction segment had reached 99.5% of its annual order target, led by an AI data center project in Paju.

Hanwha Investment & Securities said in an August 26, 2026 report that four projects worth KRW 700 billion had started in the first half, with four to five more planned for the second half.

The same report said the company plans 12 construction starts in 2027 and nine in 2028, which it cited as a driver of revenue growth. Shinhan Investment Corp. estimated in a November 2, 2026 report that 2027 revenue and operating profit would rise 32% and 72% respectively.

Shinhan Investment Corp. maintained a Buy rating and presented a target price of KRW 11,000 in its November 2, 2026 report, down 8.3% from the earlier figure. Hanwha Investment & Securities raised its target price from KRW 10,000 to KRW 14,000 in its August 2026 report.

Open points include whether the data center cost-rate adjustment is recognized in Q4 2026 and whether the planned construction starts proceed on schedule.

08

Bull factors

Earnings visibility from the order backlog

The consolidated order backlog at end-September 2026 was KRW 6.2 trillion, up 37.0% from KRW 4.52 trillion at end-2025. Cumulative orders through Q3 2026 reached KRW 2.67 trillion, up 37.3% year on year.

Shinhan Investment Corp.'s November 2026 report said construction already started supports the 2027 earnings recovery.

Data center projects now flowing into revenue

Revenue grew 4.1% year on year in Q3 2026 as the Sejong data center construction began to flow into sales. A Paju AI data center project was also secured. Hanwha Investment & Securities' August 2026 report described data centers as new growth momentum added to existing construction and housing earnings.

Balance sheet improvement and asset sale

The debt ratio fell from 170.4% in 2022 to 71.0% in 2025, and operating cash flow was KRW 63.3 billion in 2025. The KRW 140 billion Seocho-dong site sale equals 11.77% of consolidated total assets at end-2025. The company said the proceeds are intended to fund new and core business investment.

09

Bear factors

Q3 profit gap and thin construction margins

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 7.75 billion fell 72.7% year on year, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 77.5%. The construction segment's operating margin was only 0.6%, and a cost-rate adjustment for a data center project was pushed into Q4. Revenue growth has not yet translated into profit.

Slowdown in HS and concentrated profit

HS segment revenue fell 8.0% quarter on quarter to KRW 65.9 billion in Q3 2026, and operating profit dropped to KRW 5.8 billion. The margin declined from 14.0% to 8.8%. Because HS accounts for about 82% of cumulative consolidated operating profit, a slowdown there directly affects total earnings.

Three years of falling revenue and volatile profit

Annual revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and the operating margin fell from 8.0% in 2022 to 0.2% in 2024. Operating profit recovered in 2025, but it was only KRW 2.38 billion in 2024. With margins near 1%, small cost changes can swing profit sharply.

10

Risk factors

Share price volatility and trading controls

After a sharp 15-day share price rise, the stock was designated as an investment-caution stock in August 2026 under Korea Exchange criteria tied to closing-price gains and top-account buying concentration.

Media reports said further market alert procedures could follow, including additional designations or trading suspensions.

Construction cost rates and concentration

The construction segment accounted for about 72% of consolidated revenue in Q3 2026, indicating high concentration. A one-quarter delay in the data center cost-rate adjustment added to earnings swings. Because early-stage construction costs are recognized conservatively, the timing of margin improvement is uncertain.

Housing market and project financing risk

With the housing market weak, housing profitability remains limited, and the segment's operating margin was only 0.4% in Q3 2026. The company has limited project financing risk through public auction land purchases, but housing market volatility remains. The company has not disclosed how the Seocho-dong sale proceeds will be allocated across businesses.

12

Overall view

Xi S&D's financial trends improved in 2025, with operating profit recovering to KRW 12.5 billion and the debt ratio falling to 71.0%. The KRW 6.2 trillion order backlog at end-September 2026 and the flow of data center construction into revenue are cited as growth grounds.

On the other side, Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 7.75 billion fell 72.7% year on year, and the construction segment's operating margin was only 0.6%. HS accounting for about 82% of cumulative operating profit is also cited as a source of earnings volatility.

Brokers expect earnings recovery in 2027, but their target prices differ. Shinhan Investment Corp. presented KRW 11,000 in a November 2026 report, and Hanwha Investment & Securities presented KRW 14,000 in an August 2026 report.

The next checkpoints are the Seocho-dong site balance payment on December 24, 2026 and the preliminary fiscal 2026 results expected in late January 2027.
