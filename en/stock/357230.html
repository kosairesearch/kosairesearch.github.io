Revenue Keeps Growing, but Third-Quarter Profit Fell Sharply

H.pio's nine-month revenue rose to KRW 213.5 billion on company figures, but third-quarter operating profit fell to KRW 730 million and the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders, so whether the profit recovery holds is the next item to confirm.

  1. 1

    Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 11.5% year on year to KRW 71.7 billion, but operating profit fell 65.4% to KRW 730 million.

  2. 2

    Profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned to a net loss of KRW 216 million in the third quarter of 2026, and the last four quarters together totaled KRW 7.26 billion.

  3. 3

    Annual revenue rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, reaching KRW 258.2 billion in 2025, the largest year in the 2022-2025 span, while operating profit fell to KRW 7.99 billion in 2024 before recovering to KRW 13.5 billion in 2025.

  4. 4

02

Business structure

H.pio was founded in 2012 to develop, manufacture and sell health functional foods and listed on KOSDAQ in 2021. In the first half of 2026, health functional foods accounted for 73.4% of consolidated revenue and kids products for 25.5%, according to the Korea IR Council's report dated November 12, 2026.

Its flagship brand Denps sells the Danish Lactobacillus Story product, which the same report says holds a 44% share of the domestic imported probiotics market and ranks first. The Chinese-market product High&GoGo, a milk powder for child growth, is described as the number one product in its category.

Manufacturing sits with a subsidiary acquired in 2019, which brings production in-house, and that subsidiary secured an OEM order from a leading Amazon health-supplement brand, per company comments on May 14, 2026.

The kids segment is run by a kids-lifestyle subsidiary formed by merging a kickboard distributor with a children's apparel company acquired in 2023; its first-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 12.1 billion, up 20.2% year on year, per Korea Investment & Securities (May 19, 2026).

The dermocosmetic brand Judo, acquired in 2025, is the new growth driver, and the company said Judo's revenue rose 55% in the first nine months of 2026.

On channels, the Korea IR Council noted that home shopping accounts for about 38% of Denps sales and has the lowest contribution margin, and highlighted a shift toward offline channels.

Kiwoom Securities said in an April 2024 report that the health functional food market is highly competitive, while citing strain selection and product diversification as the company's strengths.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, annual revenue was KRW 196 billion in 2022, KRW 232 billion in 2023 (up 18.4%), KRW 242.8 billion in 2024 (up 4.7%) and KRW 258.2 billion in 2025 (up 6.3%). Revenue rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, with 2025 the largest year and 2022 the smallest in the 2022-2025 span.

Operating profit was KRW 17.5 billion in 2022, KRW 22.1 billion in 2023 (up 26.2%), KRW 7.99 billion in 2024 (down 63.8%) and KRW 13.5 billion in 2025 (up 69.5%), with 2023 the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

The operating margin moved from 8.9% in 2022 to 9.5% in 2023, 3.3% in 2024 and 5.2% in 2025, so the direction changed after the 2024 low.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 11.5 billion in 2022, KRW 15.2 billion in 2023 (up 32.1%), KRW 6.39 billion in 2024 (down 57.8%) and KRW 8.42 billion in 2025 (up 31.8%).

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 9.01 billion in 2025 and should be read separately from the KRW 8.42 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

Operating cash flow was KRW -3.26 billion in 2022, KRW 24.1 billion in 2023, KRW 6.72 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.95 billion in 2025, declining after 2023.

The debt ratio rose from 12.8% in 2022 to 29.6% in 2023, 35.2% in 2024 and 33.8% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 192.2 billion, of which KRW 152 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 64.9 billion.

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 4.26 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (up 101.7% from the prior quarter) to KRW 3.28 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 23.1%) and KRW 3.9 billion in the second quarter (up 18.9%).

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 71.7 billion (up 11.5% year on year), but operating profit fell to KRW 730 million (down 81.3% from the prior quarter and 65.4% year on year), and profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 216 million.

The company pointed to exchange-rate-driven cost pressure as the reason for the margin squeeze, while revenue had risen to KRW 78.5 billion in the second quarter before easing in the third.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for health functional foods is being shaped by a domestic consumption recovery and rising inbound visitor spending.

Korea Investment & Securities said in its May 14, 2026 report that pharmacy spending by foreign visitors to Korea reached KRW 80.9 billion in January to April 2026, a 10.9% share, and read this as positive for the sector.

On costs, the euro's strength is a key variable, and the Korea IR Council said in its November 12, 2026 report that cost pressure from a strong euro would be a major driver of lower operating profit.

On production, the Korea IR Council argued that both subsidiaries face supply shortfalls against demand, so capacity expansion translates directly into revenue. In China, High&GoGo is presented as the number one product in child-growth milk powder, and the Danish plant's break-even is planned within 2026.

In domestic probiotics, the Denps product's 44% share of the imported probiotics market is cited as a strength. On channels, the high share of home shopping is flagged as a margin weakness, and expansion into offline stores and Olive Young is presented as the alternative.

Reports describe the dermocosmetic market as growing quickly, and Judo is expanding sales within it. The health functional food market is nonetheless described as highly competitive, so balancing share retention against marketing costs is central to how the sector reads.

06

Outlook

The company presented 2026 guidance of KRW 300 billion in revenue and KRW 20 billion in operating profit, as reported by Korea Investment & Securities on May 14, 2026. In its November 17, 2026 release, the company said nine-month revenue of KRW 213.5 billion was a record high.

However, third-quarter operating profit was KRW 730 million, so reaching the KRW 20 billion full-year guidance would require a large improvement in the fourth quarter. Analyst estimates vary.

The Korea IR Council expects 2026 revenue of KRW 282 billion and operating profit of KRW 12 billion, while Shinhan Investment Corp. forecast KRW 286.2 billion and KRW 17 billion in its July 1, 2026 report. Shinhan also projected KRW 321.7 billion in revenue and KRW 26.4 billion in operating profit for 2027.

Sales channel conversion and in-house production were cited as structural sources of profit improvement.

The Korea IR Council expects about KRW 5 billion a year from moving sales from home shopping to offline channels and about KRW 1 billion from installing a capsule line at the Danish plant in 2027, together roughly KRW 6 billion a year, with full effects visible from 2027.

Overseas sales are another driver; Korea Investment & Securities expects a new Amazon top-selling brand's revenue to be recognized from the second quarter of 2026 and utilization at the Danish plant to keep rising.

The company said exchange rates and costs have been stabilizing since the second half, so whether cost relief shows up in fourth-quarter results is the central question for the outlook.

Bull factors

Revenue Growth and Profit Recovery in 2025

The company said first-half 2026 consolidated revenue rose 8.8% year on year to KRW 141.8 billion and was a record for the period, announced August 14, 2026. Annual operating profit for 2025 was KRW 13.5 billion, up 69.5%, recovering from the 2024 low.

Overseas OEM orders at the manufacturing subsidiary and channel expansion for Judo are cited as sources of top-line growth.

Overseas Production Expansion and OEM Demand

Korea Investment & Securities said overseas revenue in the first quarter of 2026 reached KRW 16.5 billion, up 184.0% year on year, in its May 19, 2026 report. It attributed the gain to higher High&GoGo output, which also contributed to profitability.

The Korea IR Council said both subsidiaries face supply shortfalls, so added capacity converts directly into revenue.

Dividend Policy and Net Cash

The Korea IR Council said in its November 12, 2026 report that the consolidated payout ratio for 2025 rose from 23% to 29%.

The same report cited the high-dividend and preferential-dividend policy for general shareholders, retention of separate-taxation eligibility and net cash of about KRW 25 billion as support for sustainability. At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 64.9 billion and total equity was KRW 192.2 billion.

09

Bear factors

Sharp Third-Quarter Profit Drop and Swing to Loss

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 730 million, down 65.4% year on year and down 81.3% from the prior quarter. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 216 million in the same quarter.

Cumulative profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters was only KRW 7.26 billion, and the size of quarterly swings is a bearish factor.

Persistent Exchange-Rate and Cost Pressure

The company said that rising exchange rates from the second half of 2025 through the first half of 2026 increased its cost burden. The Korea IR Council also expected euro-driven cost pressure to be a major factor behind lower operating profit. Whether that pressure eases will become visible only in results from the fourth quarter of 2026 onward.

Divergent 2026 Profit Estimates

Full-year 2026 operating profit estimates differ: KRW 12 billion from the Korea IR Council, KRW 17 billion from Shinhan Investment Corp., and the company's KRW 20 billion guidance. Based on disclosed quarterly figures, reaching the guidance requires a large fourth-quarter improvement. Shinhan also pointed to aggressive marketing spending concentrated in the cosmetics segment.

10

Risk factors

Plant Expansion Execution

Installing a capsule line at the Danish plant is planned for 2027, and the Korea IR Council expects in-house production to add roughly KRW 1 billion in profit improvement. A delay in installation or a slower startup could push back when the cost benefit shows up in results.

If capacity does not keep pace with demand while plant utilization is rising, the speed at which added production turns into revenue could also slow. This schedule is a point to verify through results during 2027.

Sales Channel Shift

Home shopping accounts for about 38% of Denps sales and has the lowest contribution margin among channels. The company and the Korea IR Council cite a shift to offline channels and Olive Young as a route to margin improvement.

If sales moved out of home shopping are not replaced, or offline listings are delayed, both revenue and profit could fall short of expectations. The change in channel mix may become visible in quarterly results during 2027.

Ownership Concentration and Free Float

Shareholders classified as largest holders hold 73.12% as of August 14, 2026, and the free-float ratio is 26.20%. Foreign ownership is 1.25%. Shinhan Investment Corp. noted that concerns over the high controlling-shareholder stake persist. A share cancellation decision was disclosed on August 24, 2026.

Overall view

H.pio recorded revenue growth for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and operating profit rose 69.5% to KRW 13.5 billion in 2025, showing both top-line growth and profit recovery.

The company said nine-month 2026 revenue of KRW 213.5 billion was a record, with its manufacturing subsidiary, kids brand and overseas High&GoGo production driving growth.

However, third-quarter operating profit fell 65.4% year on year to KRW 730 million, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders swung to a net loss of KRW 216 million.

The company cited exchange-rate-driven cost pressure, and when that pressure eases will become visible in results from the fourth quarter of 2026 onward.

Full-year 2026 operating profit estimates range from KRW 12 billion to KRW 17 billion across brokers, against the company's KRW 20 billion guidance, so readings depend on the earnings base used.

On valuation, the Korea IR Council said the PBR sits at the bottom of its band, and Shinhan noted room for normalization if profits recover. The 73.12% controlling stake and 26.20% free float are structural variables for trading.

The next checks are the fourth-quarter 2026 results and whether the operating margin recovers in the 2026 annual report.
