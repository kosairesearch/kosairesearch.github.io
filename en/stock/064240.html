Business structure

homecast designs and manufactures set-top boxes (STBs) and home gateways for the broadcasting and telecom equipment market. Its core products are digital set-top boxes tailored to different broadcast environments, including IPTV, satellite broadcasting and cable TV.

Recently the product scope has been widening toward AI home gateways that combine AI speaker and IoT hub functions. Its customers are mainly broadcast operators, so sales tend to concentrate in particular quarters depending on equipment order timing.

Quarterly sales in the filed results were KRW 15.5 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 74.6 billion in Q4 2025, KRW 5.06 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 867 million in Q2 2026 and KRW 35.6 billion in Q3 2026, a wide spread.

This pattern suggests large order-driven sales may have clustered in a few quarters, but confirming it requires product-level revenue mix. Product and regional revenue shares and customer concentration are items to check in the business and customer sections of the annual report.

The company had 21 employees as of June 2026 according to a job-listing site aggregate, and it is classified as a small and mid-sized enterprise. Competition broadly pits large overseas equipment makers against domestic small and mid-sized manufacturers on price, delivery and feature differentiation.

On the balance sheet, total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 4.87 billion against total equity of KRW 84.5 billion, indicating limited reliance on borrowing.

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell 13.1% from KRW 73.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 63.7 billion in 2023, then rose to KRW 97.9 billion in 2024 (up 53.6% year on year) and KRW 98 billion in 2025 (up 0.1%), growing two years in a row from 2024 through 2025. The largest year in the 2022–2025 span was 2025, and the smallest was 2023.

Operating profit of KRW 380 million in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 10.2 billion in 2023, followed by an operating loss of KRW 2.44 billion in 2024 (losses continued, narrower) and KRW 2.88 billion in 2025 (losses continued, wider), three consecutive loss years from 2023 through 2025.

The operating margin moved from -16.0% in 2023 to -2.5% in 2024 and -2.9% in 2025, so improvement has been limited since 2024. Attributable net income turned positive at KRW 8.8 million... precisely KRW 880 million in 2024, then fell 97.3% to KRW 23.38 million in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 3.78 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.17 billion in 2025, a different figure from attributable net income.

In 2024 attributable net income was positive despite an operating loss, which points to a contribution from non-operating items, and the detail should be checked in the annual report income statement.

Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 84.5 billion, all of it attributable to shareholders, and the debt ratio fell from 28.4% in 2023 to 5.8% in 2025. Operating cash flow was positive at KRW 7.69 billion in 2022, KRW 4.44 billion in 2023 and KRW 17.3 billion in 2024, then turned to -KRW 29.2 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, sales jumped from KRW 15.5 billion in Q3 2025 (operating loss of KRW 1.28 billion) to KRW 74.6 billion in Q4 2025 (up 382.0% from the prior quarter), when operating profit of KRW 2.07 billion marked a swing to profit.

Sales then fell to KRW 5.06 billion in Q1 2026 (down 93.2% quarter on quarter, operating loss of KRW 2.33 billion) and KRW 867 million in Q2 2026 (down 82.9%, operating loss of KRW 2.01 billion), before reaching KRW 35.6 billion in Q3 2026 (up 4,004.7% quarter on quarter and up 130.2% year on year, operating loss of KRW 2.6 billion).

Attributable net losses over the last four quarters totaled KRW 689 million, as the KRW 4.32 billion attributable profit in Q4 2025 offset much of the losses in the three quarters of 2026. Whether the Q4 2025 sales surge was one-off or recurring will be clearer from the composition of sales in the annual report.

05

Industry analysis

The set-top box and home gateway market that homecast serves moves with the capital investment cycles of broadcast platform operators. Orders tend to cluster when cable and IPTV operators replace or expand equipment, which can make quarterly sales swing sharply.

The jump to KRW 74.6 billion in Q4 2025 followed by steep declines in Q1 and Q2 2026 can be read against this ordering pattern. Meanwhile, the spread of OTT services is structurally changing demand for traditional broadcast set-top boxes, and this shift directly affects how much broadcast operators spend on equipment.

AI home gateways that combine functions may carry higher added value than simple receiving devices, so product mix changes could alter the margin structure.

On competition, large overseas equipment makers hold scale and supply chain advantages, while domestic small and mid-sized manufacturers try to differentiate through custom design and quick response.

With the operating margin negative from 2023 through 2025, the ability to absorb cost of sales and selling expenses as sales grow is the yardstick for its position against the industry.

The electronics and components sector groups large component makers with equipment makers, so business structure and revenue scale differ sharply even within the same classification.

Rather than comparing against the sector average, it is more appropriate to set the company's quarterly trend beside broadcast operators' investment signals.

Demonstrated profit capacity

Q4 2025 swung to an operating profit of KRW 2.07 billion, and attributable net income reached KRW 4.32 billion. Attributable net income also turned positive in 2024 at KRW 880 million. The fact that profit was achieved in a quarter of higher sales points to the potential of the business structure.

Low leverage and equity base

Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 84.5 billion, against total liabilities of KRW 4.87 billion. Operating cash flow of KRW 17.3 billion in 2024 is also cited as evidence of financial capacity.

Attributable net losses over the last four quarters totaled KRW 689 million, and the profitable quarter was a single instance, which is the basis of the bearish case.

Deteriorating operating cash flow

Operating cash flow swung from KRW 17.3 billion in 2024 to -KRW 29.2 billion in 2025. This means losses and cash outflows appeared together in the same year. Whether working capital growth is the cause should be checked in the cash flow statement of the annual report.

Q1 2026 sales of KRW 5.06 billion fell 93.2% from the prior quarter, and Q2 sales of KRW 867 million fell a further 82.9%. Q3 sales recovered to KRW 35.6 billion, but the quarter still posted an operating loss. Such sharp quarterly swings reduce predictability, which is a bearish factor.
