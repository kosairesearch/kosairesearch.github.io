02

Business structure

Mobicontents Tech, founded in June 2023, distributes K-pop merchandise and related goods. It runs the global K-content commerce platform COKODIVE, selling albums and related products to overseas fans. Its website says it exports more than 70,000 products from over 530 artists to 230 countries.

One sales channel is live selling through TikTok and other social media. Through exclusive licensing deals with entertainment companies, it plans and produces merchandise and also runs fan events such as fan-signing sessions and pop-up stores.

The product range has widened from albums and merchandise to K-beauty, fashion and stationery, and a partnership with the design retail brand Artbox supports overseas distribution of K-lifestyle goods. Its subsidiary is a wholly owned US entity, Mobicontents Tech US. It had 36 employees as of September 30, 2026.

No verified segment revenue breakdown is available, so none is quoted. Competition appears to come from K-pop merchandise online shops such as Idol Store.

04

Earnings analysis

Per filings, 2025 revenue was KRW 28.6 billion, up 30.5% from KRW 22 billion in 2024, so top-line growth continued. Operating profit, however, fell 59.3% from KRW 1.01 billion in 2024 to KRW 412 million in 2025.

The operating margin dropped from 4.6% to 1.4%, meaning the additional revenue did not translate into profit. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 73.0% from KRW 1.49 billion to KRW 401 million.

Net income including non-controlling interests moved the same way, from KRW 1.49 billion to KRW 401 million, so the two measures do not differ. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 274 million in 2024 to KRW 527 million in 2025, so cash generation grew even in a year of lower profit.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio rose from 14.9% to 25.3% at end-2025 but remains low. Total equity is KRW 32.2 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities are KRW 8.13 billion.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the four quarters of 2025 sums to KRW 401 million, equal to the full-year figure.

No periodic report has been filed for any 2026 quarter and no preliminary results have been confirmed, so the specific causes of the margin decline need to be checked in the annual report notes and later filings.

05

Industry analysis

K-pop album export value topped USD 300 million for the first time in 2025, but total sales volume fell for a second straight year amid weak domestic demand. According to the Korea Customs Service, first-quarter 2026 album exports were USD 120 million, up 159% year on year.

Album sales from January to September 2026 reached 79 million copies, 7.6 million more than a year earlier. September sales, however, fell 38% from August, showing how strongly sales depend on the comeback calendar.

A music data journalist described this as a swing tied to artists' comeback cycles rather than a market turn. Data from Hankyung Aicel show album and DVD exports to Japan fell 61%, from USD 280.07 million in 2022 to USD 108.92 million in 2025.

Because Mobicontents Tech focuses on overseas fans, shifts in demand by export region can affect it directly. Unlike large listed peers such as Hybe and SM Entertainment, it is a small operator specialised in album and merchandise distribution and commerce, and its profit is small relative to its revenue.

Broader products and IP businesses

The company is widening its range from albums and merchandise to K-beauty, fashion and stationery. Mobidays said the video IP business is preparing six to seven titles. Combining fan purchase data with IP licensing could help spread the swings in album sales. The profit contribution of the new businesses has not yet been confirmed.

Low leverage and cash generation

The debt ratio was 25.3% at end-2025 and total equity was KRW 32.2 billion. Operating cash flow grew from KRW 274 million to KRW 527 million. The light financial burden leaves room for investment and business expansion. That cash flow rose in a year of lower profit is a useful reference when assessing earnings quality.

Bear factors

Operating margin of about 1%

The 2025 operating margin was 1.4%, down from 4.6% in 2024. Operating profit fell 59.3% to KRW 412 million. Profit declined even as revenue rose 30.5%, so evidence that revenue growth leads to better profitability is still lacking. Pinpoint News also noted that whether revenue growth turns into profit growth is what matters.

Small and volatile earnings

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 401 million in 2025, down 73.0% from KRW 1.49 billion in 2024. The absolute profit is small for a company of this size, so even minor factors such as costs or exchange rates can move earnings sharply. This limits earnings-based assessment. Until 2026 quarterly results are available, it is hard to judge whether profit is recovering.

Liquidity and concentrated ownership

The five major shareholders together hold 95.65%. No shares traded in the regular session on the first day. On KONEX, thin trading can widen the gap between quotes and executed prices. Whether price formation stabilises needs to be checked through trading after listing.

Risk factors

Industry and demand

Album sales swing sharply month to month with the comeback schedule, and September sales were 38% below August. Exports to Japan fell 61% over three years. Sales can vary with demand from particular regions or a few artists, so product mix matters for a commerce operator. Revenue shares by artist have not been disclosed.

Competition and channel dependence

Similar companies mentioned include Code Crayon, Sujip and Idol Store. The company sells through social media channels, including TikTok Live. Changes in platform policy or fees could affect sales and margins. The terms of licensing contracts with entertainment companies also bear on profitability.

Governance and parent dependence

Mobidays is the largest shareholder with a 66.96% stake. Business strategy and fund management may be tied to the parent group's direction. Minority shareholders need to check intra-group transaction terms and dividend policy in filings. With ownership concentrated, the number of tradable shares is also limited.
