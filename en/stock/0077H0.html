Mobicontents Tech Lists on KONEX; Revenue Growing, Margins Still Thin

Mobicontents Tech reported standalone revenue of KRW 28.53 billion in 2025, up 29.5% year-on-year, yet an operating margin of roughly 1.4% underscores the challenge of translating top-line expansion into meaningful profit improvement.

    FY2025 standalone revenue of KRW 28.53 billion (+29.5% YoY), operating profit of KRW 396 million, and net profit of KRW 384 million, yielding an operating margin of approximately 1.4%.

  2. 2

02

Business structure

Mobicontents Tech was incorporated in June 2023 through Mobidays' acquisition of COKODIVE, an operation that had originated as a sole-proprietor business in 2018.

The core business is the distribution of K-pop albums and official merchandise (MD) covering over 130 artists including BTS, BLACKPINK, TWICE, Stray Kids, and SEVENTEEN.

Revenue is 99% overseas-derived, with North America as the primary market; COKODIVE has 1.65 million registered users and a broader global fanbase of over 5 million across more than 200 countries.

Beyond its proprietary website, the company became the first domestic K-content commerce platform to open a global TikTok Shop, launched in November 2024, and the platform's TikTok account has since surpassed one million followers.

Product categories are broadening from K-pop albums and merchandise into K-beauty, K-fashion, and K-lifestyle goods, supported by a strategic partnership with domestic design retailer Artbox for global distribution of K-lifestyle products.

Operations are backed by a dedicated international fulfillment center in Goyang, Gyeonggi-do, an overseas-only customer service center, and an enhanced logistics partnership with FedEx. A wholly-owned U.S. subsidiary, Mobicontents Tech US, manages North American operations directly.

Key competitors include Ktown4U, which has raised a cumulative reported KRW 50 billion in funding, and Hybe's Weverse Shop, among other fan-commerce platforms; COKODIVE claims the largest social media follower count among domestic K-content commerce platforms.

A reported repeat-purchase rate of 70% in the first half of 2024 indicates a relatively loyal and engaged customer base.

03

Recent trends

The Korea Exchange approved Mobicontents Tech's listing on the KONEX market on October 21, 2026, with trading commencing on October 23, 2026. The KRX set an evaluation price of KRW 55,000 per share; the closing price of KRW 60,000 on listing day represented a premium of approximately 9.1% to that evaluation price.

Not a single share changed hands in the regular session on listing day, reaffirming the characteristically thin liquidity of the KONEX market. On a standalone basis, FY2025 revenue was KRW 28.53 billion, a 29.5% increase from KRW 21.95 billion in FY2024.

Standalone operating profit contracted to KRW 396 million from approximately KRW 1.002 billion in FY2024—a decline of roughly 60%—despite double-digit revenue growth, signaling a divergence between top-line and bottom-line trajectories.

On a consolidated basis, the cost-of-goods-sold ratio was approximately 63.7% and SG&A expenses were KRW 9.98 billion (approximately 34.8% of revenue), with cost growth outpacing revenue expansion and driving the margin deterioration.

The standalone balance sheet remained sound as of year-end 2025: total assets KRW 40.33 billion, total liabilities KRW 8.18 billion, equity KRW 32.15 billion, and a debt-to-equity ratio of 25.25%.

The company received the '7 Million USD Export Tower' at the 62nd Korea Trade Day in December 2025, and in January 2026 closed a Pre-IPO round with Studio Mir and CRIT Ventures in preparation for the KONEX listing.

Listed shares consist of 600,000 common shares at a face value of KRW 5,000 per share, with Daishin Securities serving as designated advisor.

04

Outlook

K-content export growth remains the primary macro driver for Mobicontents Tech; the Korea Creative Content Agency reported that K-content exports reached USD 14.91 billion in 2025, up 5.9% year-on-year.

The company is pursuing category expansion from K-pop albums and merchandise into K-beauty, K-fashion, and K-lifestyle goods, aiming to increase average order value and diversify the revenue base.

Geographic diversification is underway, with plans to enter South American and Middle Eastern markets; the November 2024 launch of COKODIVE CHINA via Tencent's Weidian platform represented a concrete entry into the Chinese market.

COKODIVE's TikTok following of over one million provides a potential channel for live-commerce-driven customer acquisition. Parent Mobidays is simultaneously developing video IP and AI-driven marketing solutions, which could generate group-level synergies for COKODIVE's product planning and promotional capabilities.

The KONEX listing serves as an intermediate milestone on the path to an eventual KOSDAQ listing, which the company has publicly identified as a medium-to-long-term goal.

However, concurrent category and geographic expansion initiatives, if accompanied by additional SG&A investment, risk narrowing the room for operating margin improvement.

05

Bull factors

Direct Beneficiary of Structural K-Content Export Growth

K-content exports reached USD 14.91 billion in 2025, growing 5.9% year-on-year according to the Korea Creative Content Agency, reflecting structural rather than cyclical expansion.

With 99% of COKODIVE's revenue derived from overseas markets, the platform is directly positioned to capture rising global fan demand for K-content merchandise, making export growth a near-direct revenue driver.

A registered user base of 1.65 million and a reported repeat-purchase rate of 70% in the first half of 2024 provide a foundation for recurring revenues.

Social Commerce Diversification and Expanding Fan Touchpoints

COKODIVE was the first domestic K-content commerce platform to surpass one million TikTok followers, and leveraged this to launch a global TikTok Shop in November 2024, adding a live-commerce sales channel.

The simultaneous launch of COKODIVE CHINA via Tencent's Weidian platform marked a concrete step in diversifying geographically from the core North America and Europe base toward Asian markets.

Short-form video-driven social commerce can serve as a cost-effective customer conversion channel relative to conventional digital advertising.

Sound Balance Sheet and Mobidays Group Infrastructure

A debt-to-equity ratio of 25.25% and cash and cash equivalents of KRW 4.69 billion as of year-end 2025 provide a stable financial foundation, reducing near-term funding pressure as the company pursues expansion.

Access to parent Mobidays' group-level infrastructure—including AI-driven marketing, digital advertising capabilities, and global assets—provides operational advantages over independent startups building these capabilities from scratch.

The January 2026 Pre-IPO investment by animation studio Studio Mir and content-focused venture capital firm CRIT Ventures represents external validation from specialists in the content industry.

06

Bear factors

Sub-2% Operating Margin and Accelerating Cost Growth

The FY2025 standalone operating margin of approximately 1.4% translates to just KRW 396 million in operating profit against KRW 28.53 billion in revenue.

Operating profit contracted approximately 60% from the prior year's reported figure of approximately KRW 1.002 billion, even as revenue grew 29.5%, indicating that top-line growth has not produced commensurate improvement in profitability.

SG&A of KRW 9.98 billion growing faster than revenue is the primary driver, raising uncertainty about whether further scale will generate operating leverage.

Negligible Free Float and Structurally Constrained Price Discovery

Parent Mobidays (66.96%) and the top five shareholders collectively hold 95.65% of total shares, leaving virtually no shares available for open-market trading. This was starkly illustrated on listing day—October 23, 2026—when not a single share changed hands in the regular session.

Combined with the characteristically thin trading volumes of the KONEX market, this structure impairs fair price discovery and creates the potential for outsized price swings even on minimal transaction volumes.

Revenue Concentration in K-Pop Trends and Intensifying Competition

The bulk of revenue is generated from K-pop artist merchandise, making demand directly sensitive to individual artist activity cycles, group disbandments, and shifts in fan engagement.

Competitors include Hybe's Weverse Shop and other entertainment-label direct merchandise channels, as well as Ktown4U with its reported cumulative funding of approximately KRW 50 billion; sustained market-share competition persistently pressures SG&A.

With only 36 employees, Mobicontents Tech's organizational scale constrains its capacity for the large-scale marketing and logistics investment deployed by better-resourced rivals.

07

Risk factors

FX and Trade Policy Risk

With 99% of revenue denominated in foreign currencies, a sustained appreciation of the Korean won would reduce KRW-equivalent revenue and further compress already-thin operating margins.

Changes in North American tariff or import regulations could directly affect the cost structure of cross-border e-commerce operations.

Stricter government regulation of TikTok—COKODIVE's key social commerce channel across multiple markets—could increase channel maintenance costs and necessitate additional investment in alternative sales channels.

IP Contract and K-Pop Trend Risk

Revenue concentration in K-pop artist IP means that non-renewal of or materially worsened conditions on IP licensing agreements with entertainment companies could directly impair the product assortment. A hiatus or disbandment of major artist groups would cause sharp near-term revenue declines.

A structural paradigm shift in K-pop consumption patterns would require a significant rebuild of the existing product planning and distribution capabilities that currently underpin the business model.

Profitability Deterioration and Growth Deceleration Risk

With standalone operating profit already down approximately 60% year-on-year in 2025, additional investment required for category and geographic expansion could impose further near-term pressure on profitability.

If the deceleration in revenue growth from the high-growth rates of 2024 to 29.5% in 2025 continues, the ability to generate meaningful operating profit under the current SG&A structure becomes increasingly constrained.

National Pension Service employment data indicating 31 employees as of August 2026, down from a higher recent level, represents an operational dynamic worth monitoring in the context of business execution capacity.

08

Overall view

Mobicontents Tech is a direct-play e-commerce platform on K-content export growth, with FY2025 revenue exceeding KRW 28.5 billion and a debt-to-equity ratio of 25.25% providing a sound financial foundation.

At the same time, an operating margin of approximately 1.4% and an approximately 60% year-on-year decline in operating profit signal that revenue growth has not yet translated into improved profitability—the most critical observable following the listing.

The near-zero free float resulting from a 95.65% ownership concentration among the top five shareholders, combined with KONEX's characteristically thin trading volumes, structurally constrains fair market price formation.

Category and geographic diversification alongside potential Mobidays group synergies outline a medium-to-long-term growth trajectory, but the near-term priority is whether SG&A discipline can create room for operating margin improvement.
