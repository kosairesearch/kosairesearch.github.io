02

Business structure

Heerim is a architectural design firm that performs architectural design, construction management (CM) and supervision together, and it has been listed on KOSDAQ since February 2000.

The domestic design segment accounts for 55%, domestic supervision for 36%, overseas supervision for 5% and overseas design for 4%, according to the stock information site Alphasquare, which does not state the aggregation date.

In September 2026 reports said the company was involved in redevelopment projects including Apgujeong 3-district, Banpo Midopa 1-cha, Hannam Heights and Mokdong 1, 12 and 14-danji.

A September 9, 2026 report said Heerim moved from designing the Eunma apartment redevelopment to also serving as its CM firm, linking design-stage project knowledge to cost and schedule control under a DCM model.

An architecture information platform's tally shows the Sejong District Court design competition, commissioned by the Multifunctional Administrative City Construction Agency, had a winning entry announced on August 31, 2026.

A February 27, 2026 disclosure showed a KRW 10 billion loan for acquiring project rights in a residential development, with planned joint equity investment, equal to 12.18% of equity capital. Competition takes the form of bids against other specialist firms, and three companies took part in the Eunma CM bidding.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 221.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 228.7 billion in 2023 (up 3.4% year on year) and KRW 241 billion in 2024 (up 5.4%), then fell to KRW 231.9 billion in 2025 (down 3.8%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 10.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 7.38 billion in 2023 (down 27.2%), recovered to KRW 15.3 billion in 2024 (up 107.1%), and dropped to KRW 9.14 billion in 2025 (down 40.2%). The operating margin was 4.6% in 2022, 3.2% in 2023, 6.3% in 2024 and 3.9% in 2025, with 2024 the highest.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7.15 billion in 2022, KRW 6.18 billion in 2023, KRW 12.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.82 billion in 2025. Net profit including non-controlling interests, a separate figure, was KRW 13.4 billion in 2024.

In a January 23, 2026 profit-change disclosure, the company attributed the 2025 decline to a weaker construction market. Operating cash flow was KRW 23.2 billion in 2022, KRW 12.7 billion in 2023, KRW 25 billion in 2024 and KRW 14 billion in 2025, moving broadly with earnings.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 87.5 billion, all of it attributable to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 124.3 billion.

Quarterly revenue ran from KRW 55.8 billion in 3Q 2025 to KRW 58.8 billion in 4Q 2025, KRW 59.7 billion in 1Q 2026, KRW 56.7 billion in 2Q 2026 and KRW 59.2 billion in 3Q 2026.

Quarterly operating profit moved from KRW 73.4 million in 3Q 2025 to KRW 3.91 billion in 4Q 2025, then KRW 1.16 billion in 1Q 2026, KRW 2.25 billion in 2Q 2026 and KRW 1.39 billion in 3Q 2026.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was largest in 4Q 2025 at KRW 4.84 billion among the last five quarters, and the factors behind that concentration can be checked in the notes of that quarter's report.

05

Industry analysis

Heerim's earnings are directly tied to domestic construction activity and procurement systems.

A corporate summary on a securities information site says the domestic construction market has been pressured by saturation and economic difficulties, with cumulative 3Q 2025 consolidated revenue down 1.4% year on year, operating profit down 46.4% and net profit down 59.8%.

The same summary says a shift toward competitive design contests, turnkey and CM-type evaluation favors large firms with proven track records and technical staff, and expects the industry to reorganize around stronger firms. Heerim is responding with a DCM model that combines design with CM and supervision.

In redevelopment, a May 2026 report said it received 802 votes in the Mokdong 1-danji designer selection, ahead of two rivals with 370 and 122 votes. The Eunma CM role was won in a three-firm bidding contest through a consortium with a partner.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose 6.2% year on year, showing top-line recovery, but operating profit swings from quarter to quarter, so profitability recovery needs more confirmation in quarterly figures.

Because the company cited the weaker construction market for the 2025 decline, the construction cycle and redevelopment order flow remain the main variables for earnings.

Outlook

The confirmed order pipeline is concentrated in redevelopment design and CM. A May 12, 2026 report said Mokdong 1-danji is set to be rebuilt from 15 floors and 1,882 households into a complex of up to 49 floors and about 3,500 households.

The company has said it plans to pursue the three Olympic redevelopment projects in Songpa-gu (Olympic Family Town, Olympic Athletes Village and Asian Athletes Village), with no timing disclosed.

As of the September 2026 report, Eunma apartment was approaching follow-on steps including a main contract with a builder, the management and disposal plan, and relocation and demolition, so Heerim's CM work will track those stages.

For the Sejong District Court design contest, the next steps are the design contract and permitting. The joint equity investment in a residential development disclosed in February 2026 is still at the planned stage, and its size and timing will be confirmed through later disclosures.

The next checkpoints are whether year-on-year revenue growth seen in 3Q 2026 continues, and whether the operating margin moves back above the 3.9% recorded in 2025. On shareholder returns, the board approved a year-end cash dividend on February 27, 2026, and the shareholders' meeting approved it on March 24, 2026.

No company revenue or profit target was found in the public material, so the outlook rests on the trend of reported results and order progress.

08

Bull factors

Continued redevelopment design wins

A May 2026 report said Heerim was selected as designer for Mokdong 1-danji, adding to Mokdong 12-danji and 14-danji. It received 802 votes, ahead of two rivals with 370 and 122 votes. The project is reported to be rebuilt as a complex of up to 49 floors and about 3,500 households. Repeated design wins in redevelopment are cited as the core strength of its order base.

Expansion from design into construction management

A September 9, 2026 report said Heerim was chosen as CM firm for Eunma apartment redevelopment after serving as its designer, in a three-firm bidding contest. The company plans to use knowledge gained in design and permitting for construction cost and schedule control.

The design-plus-CM model widens its role in each redevelopment project. Similar roles are reported at Apgujeong 3-district and Banpo Midopa 1-cha.

Stronger balance sheet and sustained quarterly profits

At the end of 2025, all of the KRW 87.5 billion in total equity was attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders stayed positive in every quarter from 1Q 2026 through 3Q 2026. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 59.2 billion, up 6.2% year on year.

Bear factors

Earnings tied to the construction cycle

The company attributed its 2025 earnings decline to a weaker construction market, as stated in a January 23, 2026 profit-change disclosure. Revenue for 2025 was KRW 231.9 billion, down 3.8%, and operating profit was KRW 9.14 billion, down 40.2%.

Second-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 56.7 billion, down 4.9% from the prior quarter. Dependence on external construction conditions for both sales and order flow is the main bearish factor.

Thin margins and volatile quarters

The operating margin was 4.6% in 2022, 3.2% in 2023, 6.3% in 2024 and 3.9% in 2025, mostly in the low single digits. Third-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 1.39 billion, down 38.1% from the prior quarter. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 93.3% to KRW 323 million in 1Q 2026.

Of the KRW 8.3 billion total for the last four quarters, 4Q 2025 contributed KRW 4.84 billion, more than half, so profit is concentrated in a few quarters.

Procurement shifts that favor large firms

A corporate summary on a securities information site says the shift toward design contests, turnkey and CM-type procurement favors large firms with strong track records and technical staff. The same summary expects the industry to reorganize around stronger firms.

Whether Heerim keeps its win rate against larger competitors will be seen in later bid results. On new business, the KRW 10 billion loan disclosed on February 27, 2026 equals 12.18% of equity capital, which could become a financial burden.

10

Risk factors

Construction cycle and procurement policy

Revenue and operating profit both fell in 2025, and the company cited the weaker construction market. A decline in construction orders or a policy shift in procurement could move design and CM award volumes. A shift toward comprehensive evaluation that favors large firms is a medium-term risk. These variables feed directly into operating margin and order volume.

Redevelopment timeline delays

Design and CM revenue from projects such as Mokdong 1-danji and Eunma depends on each association's schedule. Eunma is still ahead of its main builder contract, management and disposal planning, and relocation and demolition. Delays in those stages could push back CM revenue and follow-on orders together. Because profit is concentrated in a few quarters, such delays can amplify quarterly swings.

Balance sheet and new business exposure

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 124.3 billion, larger than total equity of KRW 87.5 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 14 billion in 2025, down from KRW 25 billion in 2024.

The KRW 10 billion loan and planned joint equity investment, equal to 12.18% of equity capital, could take longer to recover if the project is delayed. Its size and timing need to be checked in later disclosures.

Overall view

Heerim performs architectural design and CM together, and its earnings move sharply with the construction cycle, as seen in 2025 revenue of KRW 231.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 9.14 billion.

Operating profit peaked at KRW 15.3 billion in 2024 before declining in 2025, while the debt ratio fell from 192.5% in 2022 to 142.1% in 2025. Redevelopment-centered wins continued in 2026, including the Mokdong 1-danji design and the Eunma CM role, and that is the main strength.

On the other side, the 2025 operating margin was only 3.9%, and profit in 2026 has been concentrated in a few quarters, so earnings stability is still to be confirmed.

Market capitalization sits below total equity at the end of 2025, so the stock trades at a low multiple of net assets, but that does not guarantee an earnings recovery. The balance of strengths and weaknesses will be confirmed by the next annual report and follow-on order results.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin and debt ratio in the 2026 annual report, the progress of Eunma CM work, and whether the company wins the Olympic redevelopment projects.
