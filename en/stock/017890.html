02

Business structure

KoreaAlcoholIndustrial, founded in 1984, produces refined and anhydrous alcohol products as well as chemicals such as ethyl acetate and butyl acetate.

In a securities report from August 2023, the product mix was 40.8% ethyl and butyl acetate, 26.8% high-purity organic solvents from its consolidated subsidiary Furit, 25.9% alcohol products, 3.9% real estate leasing, and 2.7% color paste. Because this mix dates from 2023, the current composition may differ.

Paxetv reported in the first half of 2026 that the company holds more than a 50% share of the industrial ethanol market and is the only domestic producer of ethyl acetate, supplying more than half of domestic demand.

Ethyl acetate is an industrial solvent used in paints, printing inks, adhesives and pharmaceutical raw materials. The same report noted that low-priced Chinese products are increasing, but that the company maintains its market position through quality and a long-standing sales network.

In electronic materials, affiliate ENF Technology (stake of about 26%) supplies process chemicals such as thinners and etchants for semiconductor and display manufacturing, while consolidated subsidiary Furit (stake of about 49%) produces raw materials for high-purity organic solvents.

A company official said the parent's semiconductor materials share is not large. Domestic alcohol production operates under a National Tax Service license system, and as of early 2025 nine producers were active.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue went from KRW 512.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 438.3 billion in 2023 (down 14.5% year on year), KRW 421 billion in 2024 (down 3.9%), and KRW 423.9 billion in 2025 (up 0.7%). 2022 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit was KRW 25.2 billion in 2022, KRW 21.9 billion in 2023 (down 13.2%), KRW 27.4 billion in 2024 (up 25.4%), and KRW 31.8 billion in 2025 (up 16.0%). Operating profit rose in 2024 and 2025 after the 2023 low, and 2025 was the largest year of the period.

The operating margin moved from 4.9% in 2022 to 5.0% in 2023, 6.5% in 2024 and 7.5% in 2025, improving while revenue was broadly flat. Attributable net income was KRW 30.3 billion in 2022, KRW 10.1 billion in 2023 (down 66.8%), KRW 24.5 billion in 2024 (up 143.8%) and KRW 30.6 billion in 2025 (up 24.9%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 33.8 billion, KRW 14.3 billion, KRW 30.4 billion and KRW 37.8 billion over the same years, a different series from attributable net income. The disclosures alone do not establish the causes of the 2023 decline.

On a quarterly basis, revenue was KRW 106.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025, with operating profit of KRW 10.5 billion and attributable net income of KRW 10.5 billion. In the fourth quarter, revenue was KRW 103.3 billion, operating profit KRW 5.43 billion and attributable net income KRW 2.9 billion.

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 109 billion, operating profit KRW 10.7 billion and attributable net income KRW 10.8 billion.

The second quarter of 2026 recorded revenue of KRW 128.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 17.2 billion and attributable net income of KRW 15.2 billion, the highest of the disclosed quarters on all three measures.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 126.5 billion, down 1.6% from the prior quarter but up 19.0% year on year. Operating profit was KRW 10.5 billion, unchanged from a year earlier, and attributable net income was KRW 8.82 billion, down 16.1% year on year.

Attributable net income over the latest four quarters totaled KRW 37.8 billion. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 594.8 billion, attributable equity was KRW 535.4 billion and total liabilities were KRW 108.8 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 38.4 billion in 2022, KRW 36.1 billion in 2023, KRW 26.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 54.4 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

The core industrial alcohol and ethyl acetate businesses sit in a narrow domestic production base. A company official said few firms in Korea produce industrial ethanol at all. A 2026 report, however, indicates that low-priced Chinese imports are growing, which adds pricing pressure.

On the beverage-grade side, an analyst at Research Alum said in early 2025 that declining domestic soju consumption is a concern, but rising overseas exports and the 2023 increase in alcohol prices, the first in ten years, should keep the segment stable.

In semiconductors, expectations for a recovery are rising on AI data center expansion and higher demand for high-bandwidth memory, and the same report said the high-purity organic solvent market is growing. Domestic alcohol production is licensed, which limits new entrants.

In competition, the company holds a large share of domestic industrial alcohol and acetate output. In semiconductor materials, ENF Technology supplies process chemicals and the subsidiary Furit supplies raw materials, so their results should be read alongside the consolidated figures. The company argues its alcohol business is less exposed to petrochemical cycles than other chemical makers.

Bull factors

Operating profit recovery and margin gains

Operating profit rose from KRW 21.9 billion in 2023 to KRW 31.8 billion in 2025, and the operating margin climbed from 4.9% in 2022 to 7.5% in 2025. Profit rose two years in a row from 2024 through 2025.

Second-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 17.2 billion was the highest of the disclosed quarters, and the improvement in profitability came without a large revenue increase.

Leading position in industrial ethanol and ethyl acetate

Paxetv reported in the first half of 2026 that the company holds more than a 50% share of the industrial ethanol market and is the sole domestic producer of ethyl acetate. Despite rising low-priced Chinese products, it is said to maintain its position through quality and its sales network. The narrow domestic production base makes entry difficult.

Financial capacity and cash generation

The debt-to-equity ratio was 18.3% at the end of 2025 and total liabilities were KRW 108.8 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 54.4 billion in 2025, the largest of the 2022–2025 period. With retained earnings accumulating, the company has room for shareholder returns, a reading some observers draw.

Bear factors

Quarterly earnings volatility

Attributable net income fell to KRW 2.9 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, down 72.4% from the prior quarter. In the third quarter of 2026, revenue rose 19.0% year on year, yet attributable net income fell 16.1% to KRW 8.82 billion. Periods in which revenue growth does not reach profit show that quarterly stability still needs confirmation.

Stagnant revenue base

Annual revenue declined from KRW 512.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 423.9 billion in 2025, and the 2025 increase was only 0.7%. Because the profit gains have come mainly from margins, absolute profit growth may be hard to sustain without a renewed rise in revenue.

Low-priced Chinese products and domestic demand

Paxetv reported in the first half of 2026 that low-priced Chinese products are growing in share. In early 2025, Research Alum cited declining domestic soju consumption as a concern. Domestic demand and price competition in core products could both weigh on results.

Risk factors

Business structure risk

Much of the semiconductor materials exposure sits in affiliate ENF Technology (stake of about 26%) and consolidated subsidiary Furit (stake of about 49%). Changes in their results or investment plans would feed through to consolidated figures, and shifts in ownership stakes are also worth tracking.

Earnings volatility

Attributable net income fell 66.8% to KRW 10.1 billion in 2023 before recovering to KRW 30.6 billion in 2025. Swings of that size make it hard to judge the earnings trend from a single year, and the cause of the third-quarter 2026 decline cannot be pinned down from the disclosures alone.

Shareholder returns and capital allocation

Press reports put retained earnings at KRW 472.2 billion at the end of the third quarter of 2025. Some market commentary views shareholder returns as modest relative to the capital base.

A cash dividend was confirmed at the March 2026 shareholders' meeting, but whether payouts or buybacks will expand has not been confirmed.

Overall view

KoreaAlcoholIndustrial is a chemical company with a large domestic production share in industrial alcohol and ethyl acetate, and its semiconductor materials exposure runs indirectly through an affiliate and a subsidiary.

On disclosed figures, operating profit and operating margin improved from the 2023 low through 2025, and the debt-to-equity ratio is a low 18.3%. Against that, annual revenue has stayed in the KRW 400 billion range since 2022, and in the third quarter of 2026 revenue rose 19.0% while operating profit was flat.

The reason attributable net income fell 16.1% cannot be confirmed from the disclosures alone. Strengths include market position, margin gains and financial capacity, while weaknesses include quarterly volatility, stagnant revenue, low-priced Chinese imports and domestic demand.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio and dividend yield, read as relative measures, suggest the share price is low against book value, and whether shareholder return policy changes is a key variable.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual business report, the 2027 regular shareholders' meeting and first-quarter 2027 results. This report provides information only and does not offer an investment opinion.
