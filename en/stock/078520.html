02

Business structure

Able C&C operates a beauty portfolio centered on Missha and Apieu. Overseas sales made up 73% of revenue in the first three quarters of 2026, and the company is shifting its weight from domestic offline retail toward overseas distribution. Able C&C says it has reached 61 countries and about 50,000 stores.

In Japan, the M cushion line accounts for 63% of total Japanese sales, and the Japanese subsidiary posted profits in both the second and third quarters of 2026. By region, third-quarter 2026 sales rose 49% in Europe, 10% in the United States and 87% in the Middle East.

Domestically, the company completed the closure of its company-run stores in August 2026, while online platforms and the Daiso channel expanded. The flagship M Perfect Cover BB Cream held the number one spot in the Amazon US BB cream category as of February 2026.

As of December 2025, offline stores numbered about 250, of which roughly 70 were franchise outlets. Competitively, Able C&C is compared with domestic beauty groups such as Amorepacific, LG H&H and CLIO Cosmetics, and entry into overseas retail chains is the key variable.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 247.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 273.6 billion in 2023, up 10.4%, which was the largest year in the 2022–2025 window. It then fell 3.5% to KRW 264 billion in 2024 and 8.3% to KRW 242 billion in 2025, two consecutive years of decline.

Operating profit moved from KRW 9.98 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.4 billion in 2023 (up 14.5%) and KRW 19.7 billion in 2024 (up 72.7%), the largest year of the window, before falling 9.0% to KRW 18 billion in 2025.

Operating margin rose from 4.0% in 2022 and 4.2% in 2023 to 7.5% in 2024 and 7.4% in 2025, even as revenue declined. The company attributes this to the restructuring that began in 2025, which cut low-profit domestic offline company-run stores and duty-free channels.

Attributable net income rose from KRW 935 million in 2022 to KRW 6.14 billion in 2023 (up 555.9%) and KRW 14.5 billion in 2024 (up 137.1%), then eased 2.9% to KRW 14.1 billion in 2025. Because the 2024 net income growth outpaced operating profit growth, non-operating items appear to have contributed.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.07 billion in 2022 and KRW 6.15 billion in 2023, slightly above attributable net income, and matched it in 2024 and 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio went from 68.4% in 2022 to 98.3% in 2023, fell to 74.3% in 2024 and reached 89.1% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 22.9 billion in 2022 and KRW 24.2 billion in 2023, dropped to KRW 5.3 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 35.6 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, an operating loss of KRW 955 million in Q3 2025 turned into operating profit of KRW 6.48 billion in Q4 2025, then KRW 9.42 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 10.4 billion in Q2 2026, the largest of the last five quarters. The operating margin was 15.4% in Q1 2026.

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 6.46 billion fell 37.9% from the prior quarter but swung to profit from a loss a year earlier.

05

Industry analysis

The company says it is accelerating growth beyond its domestic offline-centered structure. In the United States, Able C&C sells through Amazon, TikTok Shop and Ulta Beauty, building consumer contact online before expanding offline.

Emerging regions are gaining weight: Latin American sales grew twelvefold in the first quarter of 2026, and China sales rose 31% year on year in the second quarter. In Europe the brand entered the UK through TikTok Shop in March, Boots in June and Superdrug in July 2026.

In Japan, drugstore and variety-store distribution, anchored by the M cushion line, supports demand. Domestically, offline company-run stores have shrunk while online platforms and Daiso now carry the channel role.

The CEO cited Missha as the only first-generation K-beauty brand that redesigned its channels, marketing, products and organization together; this is the company's own claim.

US growth decelerated in steps, from 230% in the first quarter of 2026 to 154% in the second and 10% in the third, showing that channel expansion is not translating at a constant pace.

06

Outlook

The CEO said that in the second half of 2026 the company will keep extending its global distribution and brand competitiveness to maintain sustainable growth.

The company plans to expand online and offline distribution in the United States and Europe, strengthen profitability at overseas subsidiaries including Japan, and look for new opportunities in the Middle East and other emerging markets.

In the US, Able C&C has entered about 150 Costco stores and about 60 Costco stores in Canada. In Europe, it has added UK retail partners and is widening its offline network, including dm in Germany. In Japan, it launched the M Pro Black Mesh Cushion in July 2026.

In Latin America, it has made its first entry into Brazil and is pursuing expansion across South America. Domestically, the company said it will keep its roughly 70 franchise outlets for now.

Points to watch are whether US growth, which slowed to 10% in the third quarter, stays subdued in the fourth quarter, and whether Daiso and online channel growth holds. The 2026 annual report will present full-year revenue and operating profit against 2025 on a formal disclosure basis.

08

Bull factors

A higher overseas mix and growing regions

Overseas sales were 73% of revenue in the first three quarters of 2026, and overseas sales rose 24.2% year on year in the third quarter. European sales kept growing at high rates, with 43% in the first quarter, 48% in the second and 49% in the third. The Middle East rose 87% and other Asian regions rose 46.4%, showing emerging markets becoming a growth axis.

Sustained profitability recovery

Operating margin rose to 7.5% in 2024 and 7.4% in 2025 from 4.0% in 2022. The third-quarter 2026 margin of 11.2% kept the three-quarter run of double-digit margins intact. Cumulative operating profit for January–September 2026 of KRW 26.3 billion is about 1.5 times the full-year 2025 figure.

Effects of the domestic channel overhaul

Even after the company-run store closures, revenue growth excluding those stores was 20% year on year in the third quarter. The Daiso channel rose 92% to a record quarterly level, and online platforms such as Naver and 11st grew 32.3%. The flagship BB cream held the top spot in its Amazon category.

09

Bear factors

Third-quarter declines in both revenue and profit

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 57.9 billion fell 11.3% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 6.46 billion fell 37.9%. The operating margin declined from about 16% in the second quarter to 11.2% in the third. US growth slowed from 154% in the second quarter to 10% in the third.

Two consecutive years of annual revenue decline

Annual revenue peaked at KRW 273.6 billion in 2023, then fell to KRW 264 billion in 2024 and KRW 242 billion in 2025. Operating profit also fell in 2025 to KRW 18 billion from KRW 19.7 billion in 2024. Margins held up despite the revenue drop, but revenue scale has not returned to the 2023 level.

Financial burden and volatile cash flow

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 98.3% in 2023 to 74.3% in 2024 before rising to 89.1% in 2025. Operating cash flow swung from KRW 5.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 35.6 billion in 2025.

With total equity of KRW 86.6 billion and liabilities of KRW 77.2 billion, changes in debt burden need to be read alongside the earnings trend.

10

Risk factors

Currency and overseas dependence

Overseas sales account for 73% of revenue, so the company is exposed to currency moves and local distribution policies. Japanese sales rose 28% in won terms and 33% in local-currency terms in the third quarter, so currency effects are mixed into the reported growth rate.

Concentration in overseas markets also makes local regulatory and retailer policy changes a source of earnings volatility.

Concentrated ownership and free float

Major shareholders hold 64.20% of shares, and the free float ratio is 28.77%, both as of September 30, 2026. A low free float can limit trading volume and widen the swings that supply and demand can produce. The concentrated structure also means major shareholders have large influence over decisions.

Dependence on platforms and channels

US sales depend on a small set of platforms and retailers including Amazon, TikTok Shop and Costco, while Daiso and online platforms also depend on channel policies and fee structures. The sharp slowdown in US growth in the third quarter may point to rising dependence on specific channels. Changes in channel mix feed directly into the revenue growth rate.

  3. End of March 2027 (statutory deadline for the annual report)

    The annual report will confirm full-year results, the debt-to-equity ratio and operating cash flow. How the 89.1% debt-to-equity ratio of 2025 changes is the financial point to check.

  4. Mid-May 2027 (typical timing of first-quarter results)

    Whether first-quarter 2027 profitability holds against the first-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 9.42 billion and 15.4% margin, and whether the US subsidiary stays profitable.

12

Overall view

Since 2022, Able C&C has grown operating profit and attributable net income while lifting overseas sales to 73% of revenue and reshaping its business structure. Annual revenue fell in both 2024 and 2025, yet operating margin stayed in the 7% range.

Cumulative operating profit for January–September 2026 of KRW 26.3 billion is about 1.5 times the full-year 2025 figure. However, third-quarter 2026 revenue and operating profit both fell from the prior quarter, and US growth slowed from 154% in the second quarter to 10% in the third.

Strengths include overseas distribution expansion, growth in emerging regions and the expansion of online and Daiso channels. Weaknesses include the quarterly slowdown, two years of annual revenue decline, currency and channel dependence, and the higher debt-to-equity ratio.

The next checkpoints are US sales in the fourth quarter of 2026 and full-year results in the 2026 annual report.
