After the Profit Rebound, New Titles Face the Test

02

Business structure

NC builds PC and mobile games anchored by the Lineage franchise and AION IP. In its February 2026 earnings call, the company said it expects three revenue pillars in 2026: MMORPG, shooter, and mobile casual. Third-quarter 2026 revenue by platform was KRW 289.4 billion for PC and KRW 162.2 billion for mobile.

Lineage Classic generated KRW 158.7 billion in Q3, and its cumulative revenue about eight months after launch reached KRW 427.8 billion. AION 2 began global early access on September 30, and its cumulative revenue through November 10 was KRW 110.3 billion.

Mobile casual revenue reached KRW 171.9 billion in Q3, or 25% of the total, up from 22% in Q2. Revenue by region was 46% Korea, 34% North America and Europe, and 20% Asia, which puts overseas at 54%.

Mirae Asset Securities said legacy titles are stabilizing at a lower level, with Lineage M and Lineage W revenue declining. On competition, NC points to its large MMORPG IP and in-house global publishing experience as strengths, and it is pursuing genre diversification through mobile casual.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, revenue fell from KRW 2.57 trillion in 2022 to KRW 1.78 trillion in 2023 (down 30.8% year on year), KRW 1.58 trillion in 2024 (down 11.3%), and KRW 1.51 trillion in 2025 (down 4.5%).

Revenue has declined for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, and 2022 was the largest year in the 2022-2025 range while 2025 was the smallest. The operating margin moved from 21.7% in 2022 to 7.7% in 2023 and -6.9% in 2024.

Operating profit was KRW 137.3 billion in 2023, turned into an operating loss of KRW 109.2 billion in 2024, and returned to a profit of KRW 16.1 billion in 2025.

Attributable net income was KRW 436 billion in 2022, KRW 212.1 billion in 2023 (down 51.3%), and KRW 94.2 billion in 2024 (down 55.6%), before rising to KRW 346.7 billion in 2025 (up 268.0%).

Attributable net income in 2025 far exceeded operating profit, so the non-operating income structure needs to be checked alongside it. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 347.4 billion in 2025, a different figure from attributable net income.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 736 billion in 2022 to KRW 162 billion in 2025, but stayed positive every year. Quarterly, an operating loss of KRW 7.47 billion in Q3 2025 turned into an operating profit of KRW 3.25 billion in Q4 2025, and Q1 2026 operating profit jumped to KRW 113.3 billion.

Q2 2026 operating profit was KRW 173.9 billion, and Q3 fell 31.2% to KRW 119.6 billion. Revenue was KRW 557.4 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 770.5 billion in Q2, and KRW 686.5 billion in Q3, a 10.9% quarterly decline.

Mirae Asset Securities said Q3 revenue came in 4% below market consensus, while operating profit beat expectations by 12% on lower marketing costs. Attributable net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 286.9 billion, and Q4 2025 included an attributable net loss of KRW 2.38 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The MMORPG market is crowded with new releases, and early launch momentum often shapes long-term revenue. Whether AION 2's global performance can exceed that pattern is a central variable for the company's earnings.

Gaps in new releases amplify earnings swings, and Shift UP drew press attention after its third-quarter operating profit was roughly halved during a lull in new titles. NC has long been tied to the Lineage series, and it is now widening its lineup into subculture, shooter, and mobile casual titles.

At gamescom 2026, NC unveiled three global titles: AION 2, Cinder City, and Project Bonfire. Mobile casual is expanding its reach through a publishing deal for the next title from a Chinese developer. Overseas revenue at 54% means results are now more sensitive to demand shifts in North America and Europe.

Still, Korea accounts for 46% of revenue, so the domestic PC MMORPG competitive landscape also matters. The stabilization of Lineage-related revenue at a lower level suggests that the legacy franchise has moved into a more mature phase.

06

Outlook

On November 11, 2026, the company disclosed that it had raised its 2026 revenue target from KRW 2 to 2.5 trillion to KRW 2.8 trillion. Cumulative revenue through Q3 2026 was already KRW 2.01 trillion, above the low end of the earlier range.

The 2027 revenue target is KRW 3.2 trillion to KRW 3.4 trillion, and management described these figures as conservative. The company plans to release 10 new titles globally by 2027 across subculture, MMORPG, and shooter genres.

Cinder City, an open-world shooter shown at gamescom 2026, is in development, and the company said launch timing for Cinder City, Brave Breakers, and Time Takers depends on global CBT results. Astra Ora Tio and Mystbound are also in the lineup, with release dates not yet set.

For legacy IP, the company is preparing a global launch and a China launch for AION Mobile, along with regional expansion for Lineage titles. AION 2 global revenue is expected to be fully reflected from Q4 2026, making it a key driver of that quarter.

Mirae Asset Securities said short-term earnings momentum is limited, but noted that the company is preparing new growth drivers including mobile casual expansion.

08

Bull factors

Revenue recovery and a higher target

Cumulative revenue through Q3 2026 reached KRW 2.01 trillion, up 83% year on year. The company raised its 2026 revenue target to KRW 2.8 trillion, and Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 770.5 billion was the highest quarterly level. Management emphasized that Q3 results no longer depend on a single IP's success.

Mobile casual diversifies the portfolio

Mobile casual revenue reached KRW 171.9 billion in Q3, or 25% of the total, up from 22% in Q2. The overseas revenue share rose to 54%, extending a five-quarter climb. Lineage Classic reached cumulative revenue of KRW 427.8 billion about eight months after launch.

Cost efficiency supports profit

Mirae Asset Securities said that although Q3 revenue fell short of expectations, operating profit beat its estimate by 12% thanks to lower marketing spending in the core games segment. Operating profit has stayed positive since the Q3 2025 loss. That suggests profitability is being managed separately from revenue swings.

09

Bear factors

Consecutive quarterly profit declines

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 686.5 billion declined 10.9% from the prior quarter. Attributable net income fell to KRW 15.9 billion in Q3, down 95.4% year on year, and has declined for two straight quarters after peaking in Q1 2026.

Attributable net income over the last four quarters totals KRW 286.9 billion, with large swings from quarter to quarter.

Legacy Lineage IP stabilizing at a lower level

Mirae Asset Securities said both PC and mobile revenue fell short of market expectations. AION 2 Q3 revenue dropped 27% from the prior quarter to KRW 52.6 billion, and mobile segment revenue fell 23% year on year as Lineage M and Lineage W declined. Reducing reliance on the Lineage series remains an open task.

New titles unproven ahead of the 2027 target

The 2027 revenue target of KRW 3.2 trillion to KRW 3.4 trillion is above the raised 2026 target. Mirae Asset Securities said short-term earnings momentum is limited because legacy titles are stabilizing.

Reaching the target would require several new titles to perform at once, and so far only AION 2 has a disclosed global revenue record.

10

Risk factors

New-title performance and launch timing

The company said launch timing for Cinder City, Brave Breakers, and Time Takers will depend on global CBT results. If those schedules slip, the basis for the 2027 revenue target weakens.

AION 2 recorded KRW 110.3 billion in cumulative revenue over its first 42 days of global early access, but its longer-term revenue trend is not yet visible.

Currency and foreign-exchange gains and losses

The company said Q3 2026 net income fell 85% from the prior quarter because foreign-exchange-related gains and losses declined. With overseas revenue at 54%, exchange-rate moves can have a large effect on quarterly net income. The swings in attributable net income from quarter to quarter also reflect this volatility.

A thin profit base

NC posted an operating loss of KRW 109.2 billion in 2024 and returned to a profit of only KRW 16.1 billion in 2025. The operating margin was -6.9% in 2024 and 1.1% in 2025, so a revenue decline or higher marketing spending could quickly move profit again. Whether the 2026 operating recovery holds is the open question.

    Progress on the global and China launches of AION Mobile and regional expansion for Lineage titles. These show how fast legacy IP revenue can expand.

12

Overall view

NC has stayed profitable at the operating line since its 2025 turnaround, and the earnings recovery is clear. Lineage Classic, AION 2, and mobile casual together support revenue, and the 2026 revenue target was raised to KRW 2.8 trillion.

However, attributable net income peaked in Q1 2026 and declined in both Q2 and Q3, and Q3 revenue fell short of market expectations. Legacy Lineage revenue is stabilizing at a lower level, and the 2027 target of KRW 3.2–3.4 trillion depends on new-title results.

The bullish case rests on a more diversified revenue base, a rising overseas share, and marketing efficiency, while the bearish case rests on quarterly earnings volatility and reliance on legacy IP.

The next checkpoints are how much AION 2 global revenue appears in Q4 2026, whether full-year 2026 results in the annual report reach the target, and whether new titles launch as their CBT results indicate.
