02

Business structure

Best Bristle Company builds its business around monofilament material used in toothbrush bristles, with healthcare, fashion accessories, cosmetics and air filter materials alongside. Its quarterly filing describes three business segments: monofilament materials, healthcare and fashion accessories.

According to AlphaSquare's breakdown, monofilament accounts for 59.73% of sales, healthcare 18.30%, B2B fashion accessories 11.72%, cosmetics 3.90%, air filter materials 2.22% and other items 4.13%.

A securities firm's 2023 analysis put the materials segment at 77% of 2022 sales and beauty and healthcare at 23%, though classification bases differ, so direct comparison with today's mix is limited. Key subsidiaries include K&K, L&C Corporation and Common Plus.

Customers include global clients, toothbrush OEM makers, machinery producers and raw material suppliers, and the company works with them through joint development rather than solely proprietary development. The company says it holds core proprietary technology for producing toothbrush bristles.

Fashion accessories sell on a B2B basis, and the company said market diversification narrowed losses in that segment. Within oral care, graphene toothbrushes and electric toothbrushes drove the improvement in 2024.

The exchange classifies the stock under secondary batteries, but the disclosed business mix shows no direct link to battery materials.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 45.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 53.6 billion in 2023 (up 18.4% year on year) and KRW 60.7 billion in 2024 (up 13.2%), then fell to KRW 51.6 billion in 2025 (down 14.9%). Across 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2022 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 1.91 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.46 billion in 2023 (up 133.8%), KRW 5.73 billion in 2024 (up 28.4%) and KRW 6.19 billion in 2025 (up 8.1%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023. The operating margin moved from 4.2% to 8.3%, 9.4% and 12.0% over the same period.

In 2025 the margin rose even as revenue declined, which points to cost structure rather than scale as the main source of improvement.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.56 billion in 2022, KRW 5.94 billion in 2023 (up 281.4%), KRW 4.09 billion in 2024 (down 31.2%) and KRW 5.51 billion in 2025 (up 34.9%).

Operating profit rose in 2024 while controlling-shareholder net income fell, and the non-operating items behind that gap need to be checked in the annual report detail.

In 2023, controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 5.94 billion exceeded total net income including non-controlling interests of KRW 5.36 billion, which suggests non-controlling interests absorbed a loss.

In quarterly terms, Q4 2025 revenue rose to KRW 15.5 billion (up 46.6% from the prior quarter), but operating profit fell to KRW 467 million (down 37.2%) and controlling-shareholder net income to KRW 108 million (down 92.6%). Operating profit recovered to KRW 2.12 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 3.79 billion in Q2 2026.

In Q3 2026, revenue was KRW 12.2 billion (down 20.5% from the prior quarter and up 14.9% year on year), operating profit was KRW 1.89 billion (down 50.3% from the prior quarter), and the company recorded a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 2.03 billion, turning from profit to loss.

05

Industry analysis

The exchange classifies the stock under secondary batteries, but the disclosed business is centered on oral-care materials such as toothbrush bristles. For that reason, battery-materials cycle indicators are not a direct guide to this company's earnings.

Toothbrush bristle demand is linked to finished toothbrush demand and OEM orders, and the company says it co-develops with global clients, toothbrush OEM makers, machinery producers and raw material suppliers. In that structure, client product development schedules and order flow determine the timing of revenue.

The company points to its proprietary bristle production technology as the basis of its competitiveness. In healthcare, graphene and electric toothbrushes were cited as growth drivers, and fashion accessories are expanding through B2B sales.

Share or sales comparisons with specific competitors cannot be drawn from the disclosed figures, so the peer position is best read qualitatively.

The cycle position in oral care depends on finished toothbrush demand and overseas client orders, so client-level revenue trends in the coming quarters will be the main input for judging the industry backdrop.

Profit rebound in the first half of 2026

Operating profit rose to KRW 2.12 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 3.79 billion in Q2 2026. Controlling-shareholder net income stayed positive at KRW 3.13 billion and KRW 5.34 billion in those quarters. Compared with the KRW 108 million recorded in Q4 2025, the recovery is pronounced.

Low-burden capital structure

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 12.1 billion and total equity was KRW 80 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 17.6% in 2024 to 15.2% in 2025. Operating cash flow rose from KRW 3.73 billion in 2024 to KRW 11.3 billion in 2025, improving alongside earnings.

Quarterly operating margins also vary, so annual figures alone do not show the trend. Whether non-operating items and tax costs drive quarterly profit is the question to examine.

Gap between sector classification and business

The exchange classifies the stock under secondary batteries, but the disclosed business is oral-care materials. Even if battery-materials conditions improve, the disclosed business mix offers weak grounds for a direct effect on this company's earnings. Sector index movements and this company's earnings trend should not be treated as the same thing.
