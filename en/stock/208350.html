02

Business structure

Jiransecurity is a business-to-business software company whose core offering is security as a service (SECaaS), covering threats across email, documents, mobile and malware. It was spun off from the security division of Jiran Jiransoft in 2014 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2016.

Its flagship email security product SpamSniper is described on a financial information site as holding a share of more than 50 percent of the domestic spam-blocking market. The business has two main lines: security products sold by the parent, and security solutions and consulting led by its consulting subsidiary.

Media reports of the first-half 2026 filings show security product sales of KRW 13 billion, up 22.4% year on year, lifting their share of consolidated sales to 75.6% from 65.4%. Over the same period, security solutions sales fell 18.4% to KRW 3.2 billion and consulting sales fell 45.6% to KRW 800 million.

Product lines include MailScreen for email DLP, Jvault Plus for email archiving, SaniTOX for attachment disarmament, DocuONE for document centralization and MobileKeeper for enterprise mobile management.

Customers span public, financial and corporate sectors, and MailScreen was supplied to Woori Bank and Woori Card in June 2026. In a New Year interview the company cited a 50,000-unit tablet management deployment for Daekyo. In March 2026 the company merged Trinity Soft into its consulting subsidiary.

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue fell from KRW 65.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 35.3 billion in 2023 (down 46.0% year on year), dipped to KRW 34.1 billion in 2024 (down 3.3%), and recovered to KRW 38.9 billion in 2025 (up 14.1%). Among 2022 to 2025, revenue was largest in 2022 and smallest in 2024.

Operating profit moved from KRW 1.86 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.84 billion in 2023 (down 1.5%) and KRW 1.38 billion in 2024 (down 25.0%), before rising to KRW 2.48 billion in 2025 (up 80.3%), the largest of the four years. Operating margin was 2.9% in 2022, 5.2% in 2023, 4.0% in 2024 and 6.4% in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent rose from KRW 1.45 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.8 billion in 2023 (up 713.3%), a gap far above that year's operating profit of KRW 1.84 billion, so non-operating items deserve attention.

The parent swung to a net loss of KRW 1.33 billion in 2024 and back to a profit of KRW 1.84 billion in 2025. Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 884 million in 2025, below the parent figure, which fits a subsidiary loss absorbed by minority holders.

Quarterly, an operating loss of KRW 1.21 billion in Q3 2025 was followed by an operating profit of KRW 4.3 billion in Q4 2025, an operating loss of KRW 1.37 billion in Q1 2026, an operating profit of KRW 978 million in Q2 2026 and an operating profit of KRW 21.49 million in Q3 2026.

Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 14.4 billion was the highest of the last five quarters, while Q1 2026 revenue fell 50.0% from the prior quarter. Parent-owner net profit for the last four quarters totals KRW 2.81 billion.

Media reports of the first-half 2026 filings put the consolidated operating loss at KRW 390 million, down from KRW 610 million a year earlier.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for security software is shifting toward more advanced defenses as AI-driven attacks and document-disguised malware spread. The company's CTO said it uses AI-based vulnerability analysis from the development stage to respond to threats driven by AI.

The company said demand for content disarm and reconstruction, which removes malicious elements from files, has grown across public and private customers as document-disguised malware attacks rose.

In email security, the company describes its SpamSniper product as holding more than 50 percent of the spam-blocking market. In public-sector mobile security, procurement channels such as the Public Procurement Service's digital service mall serve as an entry point for demand.

In education, a revised Elementary and Secondary Education Act that took effect in March 2026 provides a legal basis for limiting smart device use during class, drawing attention to device management needs.

Post-quantum cryptography migration is an emerging demand area that starts with diagnosing the encryption systems a company currently uses, and the company is considering a business in it.

Competition in email, document and endpoint security includes specialist security firms such as Ahnlab and large IT affiliates pursuing the same public and financial customers. Compared with large platform companies, Jiransecurity is small, so customer acquisition and product differentiation are central to growth.

06

Outlook

The company's plans for 2026 center on product launches and new businesses. MailScreen V4 was released in August 2026, and the company announced in the same month that it would apply NIST-standard post-quantum cryptography to its core security product lines.

In September 2026 it launched Project Q-BRIDGE to start its quantum-safe transition business. In October 2026 it released MudScope, which scores email threat exposure for employees.

In a July 2026 interview the CTO said the company plans to launch a human risk management product in the second half of 2026, combining AI-based user behavior analysis with risk scoring. In a New Year interview the company said it would apply AI across its email, document and mobile product lines during 2026.

Uncertainty is higher at the subsidiary: its first-half 2026 sales were KRW 4.1 billion, down from KRW 5.5 billion a year earlier, and its operating loss widened from KRW 700 million to KRW 1.9 billion, according to media reports of the filings.

The company has said that cancelling treasury shares would reduce the number of shares outstanding and improve per-share metrics. Checkpoints are whether security product sales growth continues in the preliminary Q4 2026 results and whether the subsidiary's losses narrow.

08

Bull factors

Stronger earnings from core security products

First-half 2026 security product sales rose 22.4% year on year to KRW 13 billion, lifting their share of consolidated sales to 75.6%. Media reports say standalone operating profit in the same period rose to about 18 times the year-earlier level. Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 21.49 million turned positive from a year-earlier loss.

Public and education mobile security demand

MobileKeeper V5 was listed on the Public Procurement Service's digital service mall in August 2026, and the company says it holds the top share of Korea's MDM procurement market.

The company points to a revised education law effective March 2026 that created a legal basis for limiting smart device use in class as a driver of school device management demand. It also cites a 50,000-unit tablet management deployment as a reference case.

Shareholder returns and lower leverage

In March 2026 the company decided to cancel all 400,000 of its treasury shares, and its consulting subsidiary also cancelled its treasury shares and signed a trust agreement for buybacks. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 61.9% in 2022 to 32.5% in 2025. Operating cash flow was positive in both 2024 and 2025, at KRW 3.34 billion and KRW 6.64 billion.

Bear factors

Widening losses at the subsidiary

Media reports of the first-half 2026 filings show the subsidiary's sales fell to KRW 4.1 billion from KRW 5.5 billion a year earlier, while its operating loss widened from KRW 700 million to KRW 1.9 billion.

The parent's higher standalone operating profit was offset by this loss, leaving the consolidated operating result in deficit. The timing of any improvement at the subsidiary will show up in upcoming periodic reports.

Slowing and volatile revenue

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 8.33 billion rose only 0.5% year on year. Q1 2026 revenue fell 50.0% from the prior quarter, and annual revenue declined 3.3% in 2024 after a 46.0% drop in 2023. The 2025 recovery leaned heavily on the KRW 14.4 billion Q4 2025 quarter, so revenue varies widely from quarter to quarter.

Quarterly profit swings and cash flow

Parent-owner net profit was a profit of KRW 1.75 billion in Q4 2025 but a loss in both Q1 and Q3 2026. Media reports say first-half 2026 net profit rose while liquidity fell sharply, as collections of receivables slowed and payments to suppliers increased. Operating cash flow was negative at KRW 1.65 billion in 2023, so the record of steady cash generation is short.

Risk factors

Subsidiary results and merger integration

The merger of Trinity Soft into the consulting subsidiary in March 2026 is in the integration stage. With losses widening in solutions and consulting, the effects of integration may take time to show up in profit and loss.

The effect of personnel and business adjustments after the merger on results is still limited in the disclosures available.

Technology shifts and new-business investment

The CTO said the company plans AI-based vulnerability analysis and a human risk management product to respond to AI-driven threats. Its quantum-safe transition business and PQC rollout are at an early stage, and investment scale and timing of demand are not fully visible in disclosures. New-business costs could weigh on quarterly operating profit.

Small-cap liquidity and reporting structure

As of September 2026, the free float ratio was 36.26% and foreign ownership was 5.59%, according to a financial information site. With a small market capitalization, the pool of trading participants may be limited.

Consolidated results are affected by subsidiaries and non-controlling interests, so standalone and consolidated figures should be read separately.

Overall view

Jiransecurity's disclosed 2025 results show revenue of KRW 38.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 2.48 billion, recovering from the 2024 low. In first-half 2026, security product sales rose 22.4%, which strengthens the core earnings structure.

However, losses at the subsidiary's security solutions and consulting units widened over the same period, leaving a consolidated operating loss, and quarterly parent-owner net profit alternated between profit and loss.

Q3 2026 operating profit turned positive, but revenue growth of 0.5% means top-line expansion is not yet clear. Positive factors include the email, document and mobile product lines, public and education mobile security demand, and financial measures such as treasury share cancellation and a lower debt ratio.

Negative factors include subsidiary results, quarterly earnings swings and the 2023 operating cash flow deficit. The next checkpoints are the preliminary Q4 2026 results and the subsidiary's profit and loss structure in the 2026 annual report.
