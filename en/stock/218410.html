02

Business structure

RFHIC is a radio-frequency semiconductor specialist built on gallium nitride (GaN) devices, serving telecom, defense and RF energy markets. Its core products are GaN power amplifiers and GaN transistors, which together accounted for more than 90% of revenue according to a 2024 press report.

Product-mix figures vary by data source: a Jobkorea company profile shows GaN power amplifiers at 66% and GaN transistors at 33% as of March 2026, while another view of the same site shows 64% and 35%.

In defense, the main product is the GaN power amplifier that boosts radar signals; an August 2024 DART disclosure described a KRW 11.5 billion high-power GaN supply contract with a local defense contractor signed through the U.S. subsidiary.

A press report says the subsidiary Kyunion supplies receiver front-ends for the K2 tank, and the company's subsidiaries are credited with helping its defense expansion. Backlog stood at KRW 148.0 billion in the first half of 2025, according to reporting.

Overseas, the U.S. unit RFHIC US Corporation was founded in 2012, and RFHIC Europe was newly established on August 13, 2025. The company rose on a 2018 GaN supply deal with Huawei, then lost that customer and has been broadening its client base with new products.

It targets defense customers in Korea, the United States and Europe and does not supply radar GaN amplifiers to China or Russia. Headcount was 335 at the end of December 2025.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed basis, annual revenue went from KRW 108 billion in 2022 to KRW 111.4 billion in 2023 (up 3.1% year on year), KRW 114.9 billion in 2024 (up 3.1%), and KRW 185.8 billion in 2025 (up 61.7%).

Revenue has risen for three straight years from 2023 through 2025, and 2025 is the largest year in the 2022–2025 span.

Operating profit fell from KRW 803 million in 2022 to KRW 303 million in 2023 (down 62.3%), the low point, then rose to KRW 1.53 billion in 2024 (up 404.8%) and KRW 30.9 billion in 2025 (up 1,919.2%), extending gains for two straight years.

Operating margin went from 0.7% in 2022 and 0.3% in 2023 to 1.3% in 2024 and 16.6% in 2025, so the 2025 story is margin expansion rather than revenue growth alone.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 2.79 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.4 billion in 2023 (up 526.0%), KRW 25.7 billion in 2024 (up 47.2%) and KRW 28.7 billion in 2025 (up 11.7%), rising for three straight years from 2023.

Total net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 35.5 billion in 2025, above the controlling-shareholder figure. In 2024 total net income of KRW 19.7 billion was below the KRW 25.7 billion attributable to controlling shareholders, which implies non-controlling interests absorbed a loss.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 40.5 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 68.8 billion in Q4 (up 69.9% quarter on quarter), dropped to KRW 43.1 billion in Q1 2026 (down 37.4%), then recovered to KRW 51.5 billion in Q2 and KRW 71.1 billion in Q3 2026 (up 75.5% year on year).

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 21.7 billion (up 194.3% year on year) and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 14.8 billion (up 160.7%). The last four quarters of controlling-shareholder net income total KRW 42.3 billion.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 3.97 billion in 2022 to KRW 32.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 29.5 billion in 2025, moving with earnings. The size of one-off items by quarter is a matter to confirm in the annual report notes.

05

Industry analysis

GaN offers high power density and heat tolerance, allowing high output from a small package and stable operation in high-temperature environments. In military radar, GaN radar power amplifiers raise output signal strength, which extends detection range and improves target accuracy.

Press coverage describes the company as shifting its business from telecom power amplifiers, which long served as its cash cow, toward defense power amplifiers.

On November 16, 2026, defense stocks rose on expanding global arms demand and persistent geopolitical tension, and RFHIC was mentioned as a company holding GaN-based technology for wireless communications and defense power amplifiers.

On the telecom side, a Hana Securities analyst, Kim Hong-sik, said in a March 31, 2026 report that a supply shortage of GaN transistors in 2027 was highly likely. Press reports describe few Korean companies that produce both GaN-based transistors and telecom and radar power amplifiers.

A defense customer base concentrated in the United States, Europe and Korea offers supply-chain stability but also regional concentration risk.

Within the electronic components sector, large component makers such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek are much bigger, while RFHIC is classified as a small or mid-sized company specializing in GaN power amplifiers.

A March 2026 report said RFHIC had won large defense orders and that more were expected as large projects continued. That report also cited a high likelihood of additional supply contracts with an existing customer.

On the telecom side, expectations at the time of a March 2026 report centered on equipment orders after a U.S. spectrum auction then scheduled for June.

At KIMES 2026 in March 2026, the company showcased its 2.45GHz microwave module as its main product, and said it aims to expand partnerships with medical device makers in Korea and abroad. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 71.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 21.7 billion form the base for Q4 trends.

Q4 results will be confirmed in the 2026 annual report, which is the next regular checkpoint. The AOC 2026 event, scheduled for December 8–10, 2026 in National Harbor, Maryland, appears on the company's 2026 event calendar. The items to watch next are backlog updates and whether new overseas defense supply contracts are disclosed.

08

Bull factors

Defense mix and margin recovery

The operating margin rose to 16.6% in 2025 from 1.3% in 2024. Operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income have risen in succession since the 2023 low, showing earnings recovery. Press coverage links this trend to a shift in the business mix from telecom to defense power amplifiers.

Consecutive quarterly recovery

After a Q1 2026 low of KRW 43.1 billion, revenue rose to KRW 51.5 billion in Q2 and KRW 71.1 billion in Q3. Q3 operating profit of KRW 21.7 billion was up 194.3% year on year. Controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 14.8 billion was up 160.7%.

Backlog and overseas footprint

The backlog underpins revenue to be recognized going forward. A March 2026 report described large defense orders and further contract possibilities. The company is widening its approach to overseas defense customers through its U.S. unit and its European subsidiary established in 2025.

09

Bear factors

Large quarterly revenue swings

Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 43.1 billion fell 37.4% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 7.74 billion fell 32.6%. The drop came one quarter after revenue of KRW 68.8 billion in Q4 2025. Whether the Q2 and Q3 recovery persists as an annual trend needs to be confirmed with the audited Q4 figures.

High multiple and expectation burden

The current multiple is well above the level implied by 2024 earnings. The premium to net assets is large, so any gap between results and expectations could draw attention to the multiple. The current valuation depends on 2025 earnings strength carrying forward.

Concentration in products and customers

According to a 2024 press report, two GaN product lines accounted for more than 90% of revenue. The company lost its Huawei business after a 2018 supply agreement and does not supply radar products to China or Russia. Concentration across products and regions is a bearish factor.

10

Risk factors

Geopolitics and export controls

Press reports say the company does not supply radar GaN amplifiers to countries opposed to the United States. A shift in geopolitical tensions could change which regions and customers are open to defense exports. Rising defense demand may create orders, but the high dependence on specific regions remains a risk.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 43.1 billion was down 37.4% from the prior quarter. Because revenue fell within one quarter from KRW 68.8 billion in Q4 2025, quarterly results can swing sharply depending on contract timing and revenue recognition. The size of one-off items should be checked in the annual report notes.

Non-controlling interests and subsidiary structure

In 2025 total net income was KRW 35.5 billion, while controlling-shareholder net income was KRW 28.7 billion, and the gap reflects non-controlling interests. In 2024 non-controlling interests absorbed a loss.

As subsidiary results weigh more heavily, controlling-shareholder earnings depend more on subsidiary performance, so the detail should be checked in the annual report.

Overall view

RFHIC is a gallium nitride-based RF semiconductor company whose filed results show earnings recovering since the 2023 low. Revenue reached KRW 185.8 billion in 2025 and operating profit reached KRW 30.9 billion, lifting the operating margin to 16.6%.

Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 43.1 billion was a dip, but Q2 and Q3 recovered, with Q3 operating profit of KRW 21.7 billion. Defense GaN power amplifier backlog and the expansion of overseas subsidiaries are reported as factors widening the business base.

On the other side, quarterly volatility, customer and regional concentration after the loss of Huawei, and the burden of a high multiple remain bearish factors. Both sets of factors appear in the same filed data.

The next checkpoint is whether the confirmed Q4 results and operating margin in the 2026 annual report are sustained.
