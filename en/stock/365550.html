02

Business structure

ESR Kendall Square REIT was founded in February 2020 and listed on the KOSPI market of the Korea Exchange in December of the same year as a logistics-focused REIT.

The company describes itself as Korea's first pure-play logistics REIT and says it pursues stable dividend income and sustainable growth through a premium logistics asset portfolio. Its sponsor, a logistics developer within the ESR group, is one source of its asset supply.

Income depends heavily on leasing; according to AlphaSquare's breakdown, rental income is 97.07% of revenue, management income 1.92%, and other income 1.01%. Lease terms and tenant mix therefore drive results directly.

Tenants are mainly e-commerce and third-party logistics companies, and one e-commerce tenant holds a high share of leased area, which is the most notable feature of the tenant base. Tenant-level detail is in the tenant disclosures of the annual report.

In June 2023 the company sold the Icheon 1 logistics center and recognized a KRW 15 billion disposal gain, showing that asset rotation is part of its operations. In October 2023 it said it became the first listed company in Korea to earn the top GRESB 5-Star rating in two consecutive years.

Other listed REITs in the same sector, such as Macquarie Korea Infrastructure Fund, SK REIT and Lotte REIT, hold other asset classes, so logistics specialization is this company's point of difference. New logistics supply and rent levels, however, are variables the whole sub-sector shares.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported basis, revenue rose from KRW 53 billion in the fiscal year ended May 2023 to KRW 56.9 billion in the year ended May 2024 (up 7.4% year on year), KRW 59.6 billion in the year ended May 2025 (up 4.7%), and KRW 65.6 billion in the year ended May 2026 (up 10.2%).

Revenue has grown for three consecutive years from the year ended May 2024 through the year ended May 2026, and the year ended May 2026 was the largest of the four fiscal years from May 2023 to May 2026.

Operating profit moved from KRW 24.2 billion to KRW 26.6 billion (up 9.7%), then KRW 25.9 billion (down 2.6%), then KRW 34.1 billion (up 32.1%). It dipped slightly in the year ended May 2025 and rose most sharply in the year ended May 2026.

The operating margin was 45.7%, 46.7%, 43.4% and 52.0%, with the highest level in the year ended May 2026. Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 11.9 billion to KRW 3.6 billion (down 69.6%) and then turned to a net loss of KRW 3.44 billion in the year ended May 2025.

It returned to a profit of KRW 6.38 billion in the year ended May 2026. Reported net profit equals net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in each year. The gap between operating profit and net profit is generally read as the burden of financing costs on borrowings, which connects to the debt-ratio trend.

The debt ratio was 109.9%, 132.8%, 143.4% and 142.7%, staying above 130% from the year ended May 2024 onward. Operating cash flow went from KRW 36.5 billion to KRW 7.71 billion and KRW 8.84 billion, then recovered to KRW 21.9 billion in the year ended May 2026.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (from the third quarter of 2025 through the second quarter of 2026) totals KRW 6.38 billion, the same as the year ended May 2026. The size of one-off items should be checked in the income statement notes of the annual report.

05

Industry analysis

On the demand side, expansion of e-commerce and demand for logistics facilities are cited as medium-term growth drivers. On the supply side, rising logistics center supply and lease renewal terms are seen as variables that can affect profitability. REIT share prices are sensitive to the interest rate path.

Expectations of rate cuts, which are read as lower funding costs, have been reported to move sub-sector stocks unevenly depending on individual earnings and financial burdens. Two benchmarks help gauge the cycle position. The first is the pace of new supply relative to leased area.

The second is tenant volume and the distribution of lease expiries. Expiry distribution and rent renewal spreads are reported in the leasing section of the annual report. Competitive positioning breaks into two axes. Specialization in logistics is the asset-side point of difference.

Compared with REITs in other asset classes such as Macquarie Korea Infrastructure Fund, SK REIT and Lotte REIT, tenant concentration and leverage are the items to weigh together.

When rates and supply move at the same time, rents and funding costs may not move in the same direction, so the combination of the two variables needs to be read together.

06

Outlook

The company's medium-term direction is asset rotation. It has adjusted its portfolio by buying and selling assets in parallel, and the sale of the Icheon 1 logistics center in June 2023, which produced a KRW 15 billion disposal gain, is one example.

If sales and acquisitions continue, both the rental income structure and the borrowing base will change, so asset count and debt trends should be read together in each disclosure. On rates, the refinancing cost of debt coming due determines interest expense.

With total liabilities of KRW 1.62 trillion, rate changes have a large effect on net profit. On leasing, renewal terms with major tenants are the key variable for next-period results. Rent adjustments and vacancy occurrence connect directly to a structure where rental income is about 97% of revenue.

Operating cash flow recovered to KRW 21.9 billion in the year ended May 2026, so whether this continues in the year ending May 2027 is the next check. Whether the net profit turn to positive continues and whether the 52.0% operating margin holds will also be tested in upcoming disclosures. If asset sales fill part of profit, it is necessary to distinguish whether such gains recur.

Operating profit of KRW 34.1 billion was the largest in the four-year span, and the 52.0% operating margin was the highest. Operating cash flow rose to KRW 21.9 billion from KRW 8.84 billion in the year ended May 2025.

Logistics Specialization and ESG Rating

The company said in October 2023 that it became the first listed company in Korea to earn the top GRESB 5-Star rating for two consecutive years. A logistics-focused portfolio with a clearly defined asset profile is seen as an advantage of a specialist REIT.

The company's history also records that Moody's Korea assigned an A- rating with a stable outlook in August 2022.

Trading Below Book Value

The price-to-book ratio sits below 1x, so the shares trade under book equity of KRW 1.14 trillion. This is a reference for the share price against asset value, but it depends on appraised asset values holding and rental income being sustained. All of book equity belongs to controlling shareholders, so the shareholder claim is clearly defined.

Even with operating profit of KRW 34.1 billion, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was only KRW 6.38 billion, a wide gap between the two. Volatility in Earnings and Cash Flow

Operating cash flow fell sharply from KRW 36.5 billion to KRW 7.71 billion over the same period. Whether the profit turn holds over several fiscal years remains to be seen.

Tenant Concentration and Lease Renewals

Leased area is concentrated in one e-commerce tenant, so a change in that tenant's lease terms has a large effect on total rental income. Rising logistics center supply and lease renewal terms are cited as variables that can affect profitability. Rental income makes up 97.07% of revenue.

Risk factors

Interest Rates and Borrowing

Total liabilities of KRW 1.62 trillion mean rate changes flow directly into net profit through interest expense. Expectations of lower funding costs from rate declines have been moving REIT share prices. Refinancing terms for maturing debt are the key variable for the next fiscal year.

Leased area is concentrated in one e-commerce tenant, so a change in that company's logistics strategy could directly affect rental income. Supply and Reliance on Asset Sales

Overall view

The fiscal year ended May 2026 is summarized by revenue of KRW 65.6 billion, operating profit of KRW 34.1 billion and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 6.38 billion.

Revenue and operating profit were the largest of the four fiscal years from May 2023 to May 2026, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders turned from a net loss in the year ended May 2025 to a profit.

However, the 142.7% debt ratio and the gap between operating profit and net profit show that earnings can swing again with rates and borrowing terms. With rental income at 97.07% of revenue, tenant concentration and lease renewal terms are the central variables for results.

The P/B sits below 1x while the P/E is high relative to earnings, so asset value and the pace of earnings recovery send different signals. Strengths include the earnings recovery, the 52.0% operating margin and the top GRESB rating, while weaknesses include leverage, tenant concentration and cash flow volatility.

The next checkpoints are the operating margin and debt ratio in the half-year report in mid-January 2027, and whether the annual profit holds in the annual report due in late August 2027.
