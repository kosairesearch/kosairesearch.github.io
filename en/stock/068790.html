02

Business structure

DMS is a company whose core business is wet process equipment used in display panel manufacturing. Its product line covers cleaning, developing, etching and stripping equipment, which the company presents as a full lineup. A January 2026 Edaily report described the company as the world's No. 1 in cleaning equipment.

Major customers include LG Display and Chinese display makers BOE, CSOT and Visionox. In the first half of 2026 the company won about KRW 140 billion of equipment orders from major Chinese customers such as COST and HKC, according to its August 14, 2026 announcement.

News reports said 2026 equipment orders exceeded KRW 170 billion as of end-September 2026. The company is developing wet equipment for semiconductor glass substrates to enter chip-related areas, and is diversifying into wind power and aesthetic medical devices.

Display equipment remains the center of revenue, and the sharp drop in Q1 2026 revenue was attributed to a steep decline in FPD equipment sales. As display makers shift from LCD to OLED, customer investment direction shapes order flow.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 317 billion in 2022 to KRW 164.5 billion in 2023, a 48.1% decline, then rose 2.0% to KRW 167.8 billion in 2024 before falling 7.3% to KRW 155.5 billion in 2025. Within 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 53.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 28.9 billion in 2023, a 46.0% decline, recovered 6.0% to KRW 30.6 billion in 2024, and then dropped 86.2% to KRW 4.24 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 16.9% in 2022, 17.6% in 2023 and 18.3% in 2024 to 2.7% in 2025.

According to fnguide's compilation, the cost of sales ratio rose from 62.09% in 2023 to 72.55% in 2024 and 72.20% in 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 25.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 16.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 15.2 billion in 2024, then rose 763.6% to KRW 131.4 billion in 2025.

Full-year 2025 net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 142.2 billion, a different figure, and the company cited discontinued-operations income and loss tied to the sale of a subsidiary stake as the main cause.

Quarterly revenue fell from KRW 61.6 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 41.1 billion in Q4 (down 33.3%), bottomed at KRW 13.8 billion in Q1 2026 (down 66.5%), then rose to KRW 28.8 billion in Q2 (up 109.5%) and KRW 29.7 billion in Q3 (up 3.2%).

In its August 2026 announcement the company said it switched from recognizing revenue by production progress to recognizing it on equipment delivery, so quarterly sales now move with delivery timing.

Operating profit of KRW 10.4 billion in Q3 2025 turned into an operating loss of KRW 1.5 billion in Q4 2025, followed by operating losses of KRW 8.57 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 5.63 billion in Q2 and KRW 6.11 billion in Q3. A report based on the Q1 2026 quarterly filing said the gross margin fell to 21.0% from 33.8% a year earlier.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for display wet equipment is directly tied to the capital spending cycle of panel makers. The company attributed the weak Q1 2026 result partly to panel makers shifting to selective investment focused on higher-value products, which reduced their equipment spending.

The display industry is in a structural transition as the LCD market shrinks and production moves toward OLED, and in the first half of 2025 operating profit turned to a loss as investment and orders declined.

In 2026, equipment orders have been active mainly from Chinese and Taiwanese display makers such as CSOT and HKC, according to a company official. The company has said continued OLED investment in China supports expectations of additional orders in the second half of 2026.

Because LCD equipment demand is contracting while OLED and China investment expands, equipment makers' results are sensitive to the pace of customers' technology transitions. The company's position as the world's leading cleaning equipment supplier is cited as the basis of its order competitiveness.

Its wet equipment for semiconductor glass substrates is an attempt to extend into the semiconductor equipment market, and whether that brings a customer base different from its display-centered order book is a point to watch.

06

Outlook

In its August 2026 announcement the company said it expects the order backlog and new orders to be reflected in earnings in sequence from the second half of 2026. Equipment ordered in late 2025 and the first half of 2026 is set to be delivered in sequence during the second half of 2026, according to the company.

The order backlog stood at about KRW 175.8 billion at the end of Q2 2026. Q3 2026 revenue growth of 3.2% was slower than the 109.5% rise in Q2, which matters for judging how fast orders are converting. The company also decided to cancel 386,100 treasury shares, about 1.71% of total issued shares.

Among brokers, Hanyang Securities projected 2027 revenue of KRW 235 billion, up 53% from 2026, and operating profit of KRW 23.2 billion with a return to profitability, according to a November 2026 report.

The timing at which new businesses such as semiconductor glass substrate equipment, wind power and medical devices contribute revenue is a point to confirm in upcoming regular filings.

Q4 2026 is a period in which orders from late 2025 and the first half of 2026 are delivered in sequence, and recognition on delivery will set the quarterly revenue level. The 2026 annual report, the next regular filing, will show how the full-year operating margin compares with 2.7% in 2025.

Bull factors

Order backlog of KRW 175.8 billion

The order backlog stood at about KRW 175.8 billion at the end of Q2 2026, according to the company's August 14, 2026 announcement. First-half orders from major Chinese customers were roughly KRW 140 billion, and deliveries of late-2025 and first-half 2026 orders are set to proceed in sequence during the second half. A news report also said 2026 orders exceeded KRW 170 billion as of end-September 2026.

Revenue up two straight quarters

Revenue rose from KRW 13.8 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 28.8 billion in Q2 and KRW 29.7 billion in Q3. The 3.2% increase in Q3 was milder than the 109.5% jump in Q2, but the direction is up. Compared with KRW 61.6 billion in Q3 2025, the recovery is still at a low level.

Cleaning equipment position and diversification

The company has a wet process lineup spanning cleaning, developing, etching and stripping equipment, and a January 2026 Edaily report described it as the world's No. 1 in cleaning equipment. It is widening its scope into wet equipment for semiconductor glass substrates, wind power and aesthetic medical devices. How much this expansion reduces reliance on display equipment is a point to check in upcoming regular filings.

Bear factors

Four consecutive quarters of operating losses

The company has posted losses for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026, and the Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 6.11 billion turned a loss from a year-earlier profit. A report said the Q1 2026 gross margin fell to 21.0% from 33.8% a year earlier.

Annual revenue, which was KRW 317 billion in 2022, fell to KRW 155.5 billion in 2025, a trend that remains a burden.

Panel makers cutting equipment investment

A report based on electronic filings said Q1 2026 revenue fell 58.7% from a year earlier, as panel makers shifted to selective investment focused on higher-value products and FPD equipment sales dropped sharply. Q3 2026 revenue was also down 51.8% from the prior-year quarter.

Because orders are concentrated among Chinese customers, a slowdown in Chinese investment could directly affect order flow.

Cash outflow and higher short-term borrowings

A report based on the Q1 2026 filing said operating cash flow turned to an outflow of KRW 16.4 billion from an inflow a year earlier. Short-term borrowings rose to KRW 55.9 billion at the end of March 2026 from KRW 31.7 billion at the end of 2025, and the current ratio fell to 109.5%.

The fiscal 2025 dividend total rose about 150%, but the same report said no cash payment had been made as of the Q1 statement of changes in equity.

Risk factors

Customer and regional concentration

Reports say first-half 2026 orders were concentrated among Chinese customers such as COST and HKC. If Chinese panel investment speed or customers' equipment plans change, order flow and delivery recognition in the second half could be directly affected. The pace at which the order backlog turns into revenue is the indicator for gauging this risk.

Volatility from delivery-based revenue recognition

In its August 2026 announcement the company said it changed revenue recognition from production progress to equipment delivery. As a result quarterly sales move sharply with delivery schedules, as the gap between KRW 13.8 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 28.8 billion in Q2 shows. The difficulty of reading the trend from quarterly results alone is itself a risk.

Non-operating items and earnings quality

Full-year 2025 net income of KRW 142.2 billion and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 131.4 billion are far above operating profit of KRW 4.24 billion, and the company cited discontinued-operations effects as the main cause. Operating profitability should be read together with operating cash flow. Operating cash flow for 2025 was KRW 36 billion.

Overall view

DMS's operating margin fell from 16.9% in 2022 to 2.7% in 2025, and the company has posted losses for four consecutive quarters from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 13.8 billion in Q1 2026 to KRW 28.8 billion in Q2 and KRW 29.7 billion in Q3, but it remains below the KRW 61.6 billion of Q3 2025.

Whether the order backlog of about KRW 175.8 billion at the end of Q2 2026 and roughly KRW 140 billion of Chinese orders convert into revenue from the second half onward is the key point to confirm.

The sharp rise in 2025 net income and controlling-shareholder net income was attributed by the company to non-operating items, including discontinued-operations effects from a subsidiary stake sale, so they should be separated from operating profitability.

Positive factors include the potential conversion of orders into revenue, the technology position in cleaning equipment and shareholder returns such as treasury share cancellation.

Negative factors include reduced panel-maker equipment investment, quarterly swings from delivery-based recognition, and operating cash outflow with higher short-term borrowings. Hanyang Securities projected 2027 revenue of KRW 235 billion and operating profit of KRW 23.2 billion, but this is a brokerage estimate.

The most direct checkpoints are how the operating margin and cash flow change in the 2026 annual report and the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
