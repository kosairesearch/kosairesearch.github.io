02

Business structure

DaishinInformation, founded in 1987 and listed on KOSDAQ in 1995, is an IT system integration (SI) company that earns revenue by winning information system projects ordered by government and public institutions.

Press reports on the March 2026 fiscal year put system construction revenue at KRW 133 billion, or 51.3% of total revenue, and other IT services, including operation and maintenance, at KRW 80.9 billion, or 31.2%.

A JobKorea business breakdown as of March 2026 showed system construction at 51%, other IT services at 31%, hardware at 12%, and PDA and terminals at 4%.

The company offers its own product lines, including financial software, OKnet, DS-NET, and output solutions, and has previously operated industrial PDA and logistics VAN businesses.

Its main customers are public institutions such as the Ministry of the Interior and Safety's National Information Resources Service, which orders through the Public Procurement Service.

Recent work reportedly includes a customer-service AI platform for the National Health Insurance Service and, in defense, GPU servers for an AI data center and an integrated platform for the Ministry of Government Legislation.

Large projects are often awarded jointly; a late-2025 computing equipment procurement at the National Information Resources Service was a KRW 122.4 billion joint award by five companies, with DaishinInformation's 28% share applied.

In the same industry, large SI players such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever compete, while DaishinInformation operates at mid-sized scale with a public-procurement focus. Backlog at the end of March 2026 was reported at KRW 78.9 billion.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results are most visible in the March 2026 fiscal year. Revenue was KRW 259.5 billion, up 27.9% year on year, reversing the decline to KRW 202.9 billion in the March 2025 fiscal year (down 21.0%).

Revenue ran from KRW 228.5 billion in the March 2023 fiscal year to KRW 256.8 billion in the March 2024 fiscal year (up 12.4%), and the March 2026 fiscal year is the largest year in the period.

Operating profit moved from KRW 4.18 billion in the March 2023 fiscal year to KRW 8.84 billion in the March 2024 fiscal year (up 111.7%), KRW 9.1 billion in the March 2025 fiscal year (up 2.9%) and KRW 16 billion in the March 2026 fiscal year (up 75.6%), rising for three consecutive fiscal years from the March 2024 fiscal year.

The operating margin climbed every year, from 1.8% in the March 2023 fiscal year to 3.4%, 4.5% and 6.2%.

In the March 2025 fiscal year operating profit rose, yet net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell 12.6% to KRW 5.74 billion, and JobKorea's financial table shows non-operating expenses rising to roughly KRW 2.6 billion from about KRW 1.3 billion.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 12.6 billion in the March 2026 fiscal year, up 119.3%, after turning from a net loss of KRW 48.36 million in the March 2023 fiscal year to a profit of KRW 6.57 billion in the March 2024 fiscal year.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 1.76 billion in the March 2023 fiscal year to -KRW 6.33 billion and -KRW 8.63 billion in the next two fiscal years, before turning to KRW 6.2 billion in the March 2026 fiscal year. Quarterly results are uneven.

Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 132 billion and operating profit of KRW 16.5 billion were close in size to the full March 2026 fiscal year operating profit of KRW 16 billion, so profit was concentrated in one quarter.

The national computing center contract period (November 25, 2025 to January 13, 2026) overlaps that quarter and the next, which ties public delivery timing to quarterly revenue.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 40.5 billion with an operating loss of KRW 2.51 billion, and Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 42.1 billion rose 9.1% year on year, but the operating loss of KRW 828 million continued.

05

Industry analysis

Public IT services move with government and public-institution IT budgets and procurement schedules. Recent orders connect to demand for AI transformation (AX).

Press reports say the company is extending its business by layering AI functions such as customer consultation, task processing and data use on top of public networks. A 2026 report said a contract for an AI-based work platform for Korea Airports Corporation was also near.

Public procurement tends to cluster revenue in particular quarters, which the Q4 2025 results illustrate, and reconstruction demand after the fire at the Daejeon main center appeared as an urgent equipment procurement.

On competition, Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever have larger private-sector client bases and scale, while DaishinInformation concentrates on public procurement.

The company said special amnesty in August 2023 lifted all related administrative sanctions on its past bidding ban, and press reports say no similar disposition has occurred since. The CEO described a review process covering the bidding department, compliance department and CEO from the bid-preparation stage. Reports also note that bidding restrictions are a general risk for any company in public procurement.

06

Outlook

Backlog at the end of March 2026 was reported at KRW 78.9 billion, with about KRW 19 billion expected to be recognized as revenue. The storage DR integration project disclosed on November 3, 2026 runs from November 2, 2026 to April 22, 2027, with a delivery deadline of March 31, 2027.

The KRW 42.31 billion contract value equals the company's 50% share of a KRW 84.62 billion joint award by four companies. The scheduled inspection date is April 14, 2027, so revenue timing is tied to delivery and inspection.

The company reportedly won a GPU server project for AI data centers in defense and an integrated platform project for the Ministry of Government Legislation, and was near an AI-based work platform contract with Korea Airports Corporation.

On capital actions, the board approved a 5-for-1 par value consolidation and a KRW 2 billion treasury share purchase for cancellation. The CEO said the company plans to increase dividends from the prior year given retained earnings above KRW 30 billion.

Next items to track are how quickly public AX projects convert from backlog into revenue, and whether the operating losses seen in Q1 and Q2 2026, which followed a large-delivery quarter in Q4 2025, recur.

08

Bull factors

Profit recovery on public-sector orders

Operating profit for the March 2026 fiscal year rose 75.6% to KRW 16 billion, and the operating margin climbed to 6.2% from 1.8% in the March 2023 fiscal year. Press reports describe the KRW 259.5 billion revenue as the largest on record.

Backlog of KRW 78.9 billion at March 2026 and the KRW 42.31 billion DR contract disclosed in November 2026 are cited as support for revenue continuity.

Balance sheet and shareholder returns improve

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 185.4% in the March 2026 fiscal year from 248.2% a year earlier. Operating cash flow turned to KRW 6.2 billion in the March 2026 fiscal year. The CEO said retained earnings above KRW 30 billion support a larger dividend than the prior year. The board approved a 5-for-1 par value consolidation and a treasury share purchase for cancellation.

Expansion into AX projects

Press reports say the company is extending into AX projects that add AI functions to public networks.

It reportedly built an AI customer-consultation platform for the National Health Insurance Service and won defense-sector GPU servers for an AI data center and an integrated platform for the Ministry of Government Legislation.

A contract for an AI-based work platform with Korea Airports Corporation was reported as near, making public-sector AX a potential next order area.

Bear factors

Quarterly profits swing to losses

Operating losses were KRW 2.51 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 828 million in Q2 2026, two straight quarters of losses. The Q2 2026 loss was wider than the KRW 183 million loss in Q2 2025. Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 40.5 billion fell 69.3% from KRW 132 billion in Q4 2025. Revenue concentrated in particular quarters makes the earnings trend less continuous.

Cash flow and debt burden

Operating cash flow was -KRW 6.33 billion in the March 2024 fiscal year and -KRW 8.63 billion in the March 2025 fiscal year. It turned to KRW 6.2 billion in the March 2026 fiscal year, but that is a single year, so a trend needs more periods to confirm.

Total liabilities of KRW 93.4 billion at March 2026 exceed total equity of KRW 50.4 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio is 185.4%.

Bidding history and public-sector dependence

Press reports say special amnesty in August 2023 lifted all related administrative sanctions, with no similar disposition since. Some observers, however, argue the past bidding restriction may have weighed on investor sentiment. System construction accounts for 51.3% of revenue, so public order timing and size largely determine results.

10

Risk factors

Dependence on public orders

Most revenue comes from government and public-institution orders. A change in budget execution or procurement method can move both order volume and the timing of revenue recognition. Under joint awards through the Public Procurement Service, revenue depends on the company's share percentage.

Concentration of quarterly results

Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 132 billion and operating profit of KRW 16.5 billion concentrated in one quarter, followed by operating losses in Q1 and Q2 2026. When delivery and inspection schedules straddle quarter boundaries, results can shift sharply between quarters. The high share of subcontracted contracts also makes cost and process management a variable.

Recurrence of bidding restrictions

Any company in public procurement carries the potential risk of a bidding restriction. The company says the past sanction did not concern its technical or execution capability, and that review by the relevant departments and the CEO from the bid-preparation stage aims to prevent recurrence. A recurrence could limit order opportunities, so related disclosures and reports bear watching.

Overall view

DaishinInformation restored profitability in the March 2026 fiscal year on public-sector SI orders, with revenue of KRW 259.5 billion, operating profit of KRW 16 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 12.6 billion.

Operating profit has risen for three consecutive fiscal years from the March 2024 fiscal year, and the operating margin climbed from 1.8% to 6.2%. The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 185.4%, and operating cash flow turned positive.

On the other side, revenue and profit concentrated in Q4 2025 were followed by operating losses in Q1 and Q2 2026, so quarterly results remain volatile.

Backlog of KRW 78.9 billion and the KRW 42.31 billion DR contract disclosed in November 2026 are cited as revenue support, but recognition depends on delivery and inspection schedules.

The past bidding sanction was lifted and the company describes a recurrence-prevention process, though some observers see the history as weighing on sentiment.

On valuation, the price-to-book ratio is below 1x and market capitalization is below total equity, while large quarterly profit swings mean earnings multiples vary with the period used. The next checkpoints are the Q3 and Q4 2026 quarterly reports and the annual report for the March 2027 fiscal year.
