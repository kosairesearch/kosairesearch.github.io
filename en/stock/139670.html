Revenue Down Three Straight Years, Profit Stays in the Black

02

Business structure

KineMaster is a mobile multimedia software company founded in 2002, with its core products being a mobile video editor (Editor) and a video player (Player) that ship on or are installed in mobile devices.

The K5 company profile places the Editor product at 47.87% of revenue, the Player product at 38.87%, and other items at 13.26%, based on the March 2026 quarterly report that page lists as its most recent filing.

Because that mix predates the latest quarters, segment revenue should be re-checked against the next periodic filing.

On the user side, the company's official blog describes a free editing toolkit with 4K export as of June 2026, and Dribbble named KineMaster among the top workflow tools used by freelancers and agencies in May 2026.

The competitive field overlaps with basic smartphone video features and free editing tools from platform companies, so differentiation and retention of users are the central tasks for a stand-alone app business. The company had 40 employees as of June 2026 and is headquartered in Seocho-gu, Seoul.

Its balance sheet at the end of 2025 showed total equity of KRW 25.4 billion and total liabilities of KRW 2.22 billion. Operating cash flow was KRW 3.97 billion in 2023, KRW 3.48 billion in 2024, and KRW 2.93 billion in 2025, remaining positive in each year.

04

Earnings analysis

The biggest annual change was the move from an operating loss in 2022 to a profit in 2023. In 2022 revenue was KRW 19.6 billion, the operating loss was KRW 16.4 billion, the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 15.3 billion, and the operating margin was -83.5%.

Revenue in 2023 was KRW 16.8 billion, down 14.4% year on year, but operating profit turned positive at KRW 1.86 billion with a margin of 11.1%. In 2024 revenue fell 20.5% to KRW 13.3 billion and operating profit dropped 68.2% to KRW 592 million, lowering the margin to 4.4%.

In 2025 revenue fell 8.1% to KRW 12.3 billion while operating profit rose 56.3% to KRW 925 million, lifting the margin to 7.5%. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.62 billion in 2023, KRW 4.4 billion in 2024 (up 67.6%), and KRW 3.41 billion in 2025 (down 22.3%).

Operating profit rose in 2025 while controlling shareholder net income fell, so the two measures moved in opposite directions, and items below the operating line or income tax are worth checking line by line in the periodic report. The quarterly pattern is similar.

Revenue peaked at KRW 3.01 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and then fell to KRW 2.83 billion, KRW 2.72 billion, and KRW 2.55 billion across the first three quarters of 2026.

Operating profit rose from KRW 228 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 324 million and KRW 506 million in the first two quarters of 2026, then eased to KRW 454 million in the third quarter.

Controlling shareholder net income was KRW 2.63 billion in the first quarter of 2026, well above that quarter's operating profit of KRW 324 million, and the fourth quarter of 2025 posted a net loss of KRW 47.8 million on operating profit of KRW 228 million.

Such gaps suggest non-operating or one-off items affected some quarters. Controlling shareholder net income over the last four quarters totaled KRW 5.96 billion.

Industry analysis

Demand in the mobile video editing market comes from independent creators, social media content production, and freelancers handling short video jobs.

In June 2026 the company's official blog described a free editing toolkit that includes 4K export, and in October 2026 version 8.2 added AI templates, multi-select, advanced replace, and tablet interface upgrades. That direction widens free functionality while using AI to speed up editing work.

Dribbble's May 2026 listing of KineMaster among the top tools relied on by freelancers and agencies points to continued recognition among professional users.

Whether that recognition has translated into revenue should be checked against the filed revenue trend, which declined in each quarter of 2026 through the third quarter.

In cycle terms, revenue fell for three years through 2025 and continued to decline in 2026, so whether a demand recovery is showing up in results will be visible only in upcoming quarterly revenue.

Against larger peers, platform companies can bundle video editing into their services, which means a stand-alone app has to differentiate through feature quality and user loyalty. Because the editor product is the largest revenue line, the pace of feature upgrades is the main operating driver to track.

Bull factors

Turnaround from losses to profits

Operating profit moved from a KRW 16.4 billion loss in 2022 to KRW 1.86 billion in 2023, and rose 56.3% to KRW 925 million in 2025. Controlling shareholder net income has stayed positive since 2023.

Product updates and professional recognition

Version 8.2, announced in October 2026, includes AI templates and cross-device handoff. Dribbble listed KineMaster among top tools relied on by freelancers and agencies in May 2026. Keeping professional users while widening free functionality is a point in the company's favor.

Bear factors

Revenue decline persists

Revenue fell for three straight years through 2025, and the 2025 figure of KRW 12.3 billion is only a little above half of the KRW 19.6 billion recorded in 2022. Revenue then declined in the first, second, and third quarters of 2026, to KRW 2.55 billion in the third quarter, down 15.2% from a year earlier.

Margin and earnings volatility

The operating margin fell from 11.1% in 2023 to 4.4% in 2024 and recovered to 7.5% in 2025, still below the 2023 level. Controlling shareholder net income dropped from KRW 2.63 billion in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 862 million in the third quarter, and the fourth quarter of 2025 posted a loss. Large quarterly swings mean the durability of profit still needs confirmation.

Shrinking operating scale and cash generation

Operating profit fell from KRW 1.86 billion in 2023 to KRW 925 million in 2025, roughly half. Operating cash flow also declined each year, from KRW 3.97 billion in 2023 to KRW 3.48 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.93 billion in 2025. A shrinking revenue base alongside lower cash generation is a weakness.

10

Risk factors

Competition from platform companies

If built-in smartphone video tools and free editors from platform companies expand, the user base of a stand-alone app could shrink. Because KineMaster is widening its free feature set, profitability will depend more on feature differentiation than on pricing. If the feature gap narrows, the revenue decline could last longer.

Product transition and development cost

New features such as AI templates carry development and operating costs. If the 8.2 features do not lead to more users or paid conversion, costs could rise without matching revenue. As the 2024 operating margin of 4.4% showed, cost increases feed straight into profitability.

Earnings volatility and small-cap profile

With 40 employees, the company is a small business whose small market value can bring sharp share price moves. Controlling shareholder net income has at times diverged widely from operating profit, so one-off items can heavily influence reported results. These items need to be rechecked each time a periodic report is released.

Overall view

KineMaster has stayed profitable on an annual basis since 2023, but revenue fell for three straight years through 2025 and declined for three consecutive quarters in 2026. Operating profit was KRW 925 million in 2025, below KRW 1.86 billion in 2023, and the operating margin slipped from 11.1% in 2023 to 7.5% in 2025.
