Profit Expansion Continues as Probe Card PCB Demand Rises

02

Business structure

Tiger Elec was founded in 2000 to manufacture printed circuit boards (PCBs), became a subsidiary of TSE in 2011, and listed on KOSDAQ in 2015. Its core products are high-multilayer, fine-pitch rigid PCBs used in semiconductor test processes, divided into Probe Card, Load Board, Socket Board and general boards.

According to Meritz Securities research, 2025 segment revenue was KRW 40.7 billion for Probe Card, KRW 21.4 billion for Load Board, KRW 10.5 billion for general boards, KRW 7.6 billion for Socket Board and KRW 0.4 billion for other products, so Probe Card accounts for roughly half of total sales.

Probe card PCBs connect semiconductor test equipment to chips, and demand comes from both new process changes and replacement as existing processes age. Customers are probe card makers, and analysts have mentioned project participation through a Taiwanese probe card customer.

The products are technically demanding, so supply runs through customer qualification and only a small number of suppliers serve the market. Mirae Asset Securities has argued that the high share of customized products allows raw material cost increases to be passed on to selling prices.

Production includes a Vietnam plant, where capacity was added in 2020 and yields were stabilized over several years. Listed PCB peers in the same industry include Korea Circuit and Isupetasys.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell 16.4% from KRW 58.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 48.6 billion in 2023, then rose 27.3% to KRW 61.8 billion in 2024 and 30.5% to KRW 80.7 billion in 2025, increasing for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025. 2025 was the largest revenue year in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Operating profit moved from KRW 3.11 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 3.08 billion in 2023, which marked a swing into loss. In 2024 the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.92 billion, so losses continued but narrowed.

In 2025 operating profit of KRW 7.22 billion turned the business back to profit, and the operating margin of 8.9% was the highest level in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.27 billion in 2025, up 1,817.4% from the prior year, while net profit including non-controlling interests was also KRW 5.27 billion.

In 2023 the company recorded a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 2.56 billion, and in 2024 it returned to a profit of KRW 275 million.

Quarterly revenue rose from KRW 20.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 1.04 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 34.7 billion and KRW 8.52 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 70.3% and 723.0% year on year.

Operating profit has increased for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026.

The fourth quarter of 2025 reached KRW 2.28 billion, up 120.6% from the prior quarter, the first quarter of 2026 reached KRW 3.52 billion, up 54.0%, and the second quarter of 2026 reached KRW 6.88 billion, up 95.8%.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders also rose for four consecutive quarters over the same period, and the four-quarter total from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 15.6 billion.

The increase is explained by stronger demand for high-multilayer PCBs used in semiconductor test processes and higher sales of Probe Card and Load Board PCBs. Meritz Securities said the operating margin moved into the 20% range in the second quarter of 2026.

According to the broker, the 2023 loss came as revenue fell, since probe cards track semiconductor production. From the fourth quarter of 2025 onward, analysts say higher selling prices, product mix improvement and yield gains were reflected in results.

05

Industry analysis

Tiger Elec's end market is the semiconductor test process. Analysts broadly note that finer semiconductor processes are raising demand for higher-density test PCBs. Probe card PCBs face both new demand from process changes and replacement demand as older processes are retired.

Mirae Asset Securities analyst Park Jun-seo said order intensity has risen since the fourth quarter of 2025, centered on memory test PCBs, and that the market has entered a phase of capacity shortage.

Meritz Securities said in its September 2026 report that full utilization continues and a supply-tight environment allows selective orders focused on higher-margin products.

Kiwoom Securities said in 2024 that probe card PCBs are technically difficult to make, supply sources are limited, and existing suppliers face limits in aggressively expanding capacity. Valuefinder said memory is a cyclical industry, so the outlook for probe card PCB sales depends on memory market conditions.

On the non-memory side, a Kiwoom Securities analysis described participation in CPO test-related projects through a Taiwanese probe card customer. The number of upstream projects rose from five in 2024 to 25 in 2025, which analysts cite as evidence of growing demand for advanced products.

Listed PCB peers in the same industry include Korea Circuit and Isupetasys, but analysts describe Tiger Elec's position as one of a few suppliers in the probe card PCB segment.

06

Outlook

Broker estimates have moved higher during 2026. Mirae Asset Securities projected 2026 revenue of KRW 94.1 billion and operating profit of KRW 18.5 billion in an early 2026 report, representing increases of about 20% and 94%.

Meritz Securities raised its estimates in a September 2026 report to 2026 revenue of KRW 133.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 28.7 billion, and 2027 revenue of KRW 179.0 billion and operating profit of KRW 46.6 billion.

Kiwoom Securities estimated 2026 operating profit at KRW 26.3 billion and 2027 operating profit at KRW 41.9 billion in an October 2026 report. Because the two brokers' 2026 operating profit estimates differ, the reported full-year figure will show which estimate is closer.

Capacity plans cited include stabilizing the Vietnam plant and adding equipment worth KRW 7.0 billion to expand capacity. Meritz Securities said a cycle linking utilization, delivery times and quality is expected to form.

On the non-memory side, STO localization and entry into the DRAM probe card PCB market were cited as growth drivers in a 2024 report. Market confidence in additional volume from Chinese memory customers was described as limited.

Meritz Securities said sustained operating margins in the 20% range in the second and third quarters of 2026 would ease concerns about earnings volatility. The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report, which will confirm full-year 2026 operating margin and the quarterly trend on a disclosed basis.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly profit expansion after the 2025 turnaround

Operating profit of KRW 7.22 billion in 2025 turned the business back to profit from an operating loss of KRW 1.92 billion in 2024. Operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders each increased for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. Analysts cite the continued quarterly earnings power as a bullish point.

Supply tightness and selling price gains

Meritz Securities said in a September 2026 report that full utilization continues and the supply shortage persists. Analysts say higher selling prices, product mix improvement and yield gains were reflected in third-quarter results.

Mirae Asset Securities argued that a high share of customized products makes it easier to pass raw material cost increases on to customers.

Expansion into CPO and non-memory projects

Kiwoom Securities described participation in CPO test-related projects through a Taiwanese probe card customer. Mass production plans for STO products for non-memory applications were mentioned in a 2024 report.

The increase in upstream projects from five in 2024 to 25 in 2025 is cited as evidence of demand for more demanding products.

09

Bear factors

Customer concentration and Chinese memory volume uncertainty

Meritz Securities noted that market confidence in additional growth from Chinese memory customers in the second half was limited. Probe card PCBs depend on a qualified customer base, so order changes from a single customer can weigh heavily on quarterly sales. Analysts cite this as a source of earnings variability.

Dependence on the memory cycle

Valuefinder said memory is a cyclical industry, so the outlook for probe card PCB sales depends heavily on memory market conditions. With probe card revenue of KRW 40.7 billion in 2025, about half of sales, changes in memory conditions have a large effect. A slowdown in memory capital spending could reduce test demand and orders.

Loss history and earnings volatility

The company swung to an operating loss of KRW 3.08 billion in 2023 as revenue declined, and recorded a further operating loss of KRW 1.92 billion in 2024. Meritz Securities said concerns over past earnings volatility constrained the valuation.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and demand swings

Probe card customers are concentrated among a small number of makers, and confidence in additional volume from Chinese memory customers is limited. Orders for memory test PCBs could weaken if memory capital spending slows.

The 2023 revenue drop to KRW 48.6 billion, which came with an operating loss, shows how directly demand swings can reach profit.

Capacity and delivery

Analysts at Mirae Asset Securities said delivery capability determines the upper bound of earnings. Whether the stabilization of the Vietnam plant and the additional equipment investment of KRW 7.0 billion proceed as planned is a key point. A drop in production proficiency or yield could disrupt delivery and quality.

Estimate divergence and balance sheet strain

The 2026 operating profit estimates differ across brokers, at KRW 26.3 billion from Kiwoom Securities and KRW 28.7 billion from Meritz Securities. The debt ratio rose from 31.1% in 2022 to 54.9% in 2025.

Because the profitable run covers only four quarters, the durability of earnings still needs to be confirmed in later quarterly results.

Overall view

Tiger Elec's results moved from a loss in 2023 and continued losses in 2024 to a profit in 2025. Revenue rose for two consecutive years from 2024 through 2025, and the 2025 operating margin of 8.9% was the highest level in the 2022 to 2025 period.

Revenue and operating profit both rose from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026, and operating profit has increased for four consecutive quarters since the fourth quarter of 2025. Bullish points include tight supply for probe card PCBs, selling price gains and participation in non-memory projects.

Bearish points include concentration on a few customers, uncertainty over volume from Chinese memory customers, the memory cycle and the losses recorded in 2023 and 2024. Broker estimates for 2026 operating profit also diverge, at KRW 26.3 billion and KRW 28.7 billion.

The 2026 annual report will confirm full-year operating margin and the debt ratio, and the Vietnam capacity expansion and quarterly trend will be the variables that test the durability of profit.
