02

Business structure

Hansung Enterprise, founded in 1963, operates in marine product processing, seafood manufacturing and processed meat production. Its business is split into a Food segment and an Overseas segment.

According to Alphasquare's company profile, revenue mix is 43.20% processed marine products including crab sticks, 30.80% tuna and pollack in the Overseas segment, 18.10% processed meat such as ham, 7.30% other food, and 0.60% facility-use income.

The information site wisereport tallies the Food segment at 64.19%, Overseas merchandise and products at 28.87%, and Overseas catch-related sales at 6.30%, so the percentages shift with the classification basis.

Its flagship brand is the surimi line Kraemi, and wisereport put its share of the crab stick category at 38.00% as of June 2026. The company is also pushing product differentiation, including a pollack-based diet functional food.

As of year-end 2025, exports accounted for 71.32% of the non-crab-stick merchandise and product sales, and domestic sales for 28.68%.

In competition, its surimi and marine processed food lines overlap with large food companies' processed food ranges, and on market capitalization it is far smaller than the large food and beverage groups.

The company says it prioritizes domestic raw materials but also uses ingredients from several countries to supply several hundred products, as it stated on its website in July 2026. Because the Food segment is the larger share, raw material and logistics cost swings feed directly into profitability.

04

Earnings analysis

Hansung Enterprise's audited results peaked in 2024 and declined in 2025. Revenue rose from KRW 292.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 320.6 billion in 2023 (up 9.7% year on year) and KRW 332.5 billion in 2024 (up 3.7%), then fell to KRW 318.4 billion in 2025 (down 4.2%).

Operating profit was largest in 2024 at KRW 11 billion, up 36.4% year on year, and smallest in 2025 at KRW 5.83 billion, down 47.0%. The operating margin was 2.4% in 2022, 2.5% in 2023 and 3.3% in 2024 before falling to 1.8% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was largest in 2024 at KRW 2.84 billion and fell to KRW 167 million in 2025, down 94.1%. Net income including non-controlling interests was also KRW 167 million in 2025.

Kyeonggi Daily reported that 2025 operating profit was roughly halved and cited surging raw material and logistics costs amid a global slowdown as a factor in weaker profitability.

Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 8.16 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.22 billion in 2023, KRW 18.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 8.68 billion in 2025, staying positive after 2023.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 349.3% in 2022 to 262.0% in 2025, but year-end 2025 total liabilities of KRW 176.4 billion still exceed total equity of KRW 67.3 billion, so leverage remains high.

In quarterly terms, after an operating profit of KRW 409 million and a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.19 billion in Q3 2025, Q4 2025 swung to an operating loss of KRW 282 million. Q1 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 3.03 billion, and Q2 2026 rose to KRW 3.69 billion, up 21.6% quarter on quarter.

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 1.57 billion, down 57.5% from the prior quarter but up 282.8% year on year. In the same quarter, a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 1.3 billion emerged despite the operating profit, so non-operating items and other details should be checked in the 2026 annual report.

Controlling-shareholder net income summed to KRW 3.41 billion over the last four quarters, from Q4 2025 through Q3 2026.

Industry analysis

In Korea's crab stick and marine processed food market, Hansung Enterprise held a 38.00% share of the crab stick category as of June 2026 according to wisereport, placing it near the top of that product category. Costs are shaped by the availability of pollack and tuna raw materials and overseas catch conditions.

The company prioritizes domestic raw materials while also disclosing that it uses ingredients from several countries. Among nine seafood-sector companies, Alphasquare ranks Hansung sixth on operating margin and first on debt-to-equity ratio.

In the same tally, the sector average debt-to-equity ratio is 126.68%, well below Hansung's 262.00%. Across the food industry, rising raw material and logistics costs have been reported as pressure on profitability.

The Overseas segment's tuna and pollack trading accounts for 30.80% of revenue, which exposes it to exchange rates and overseas demand. A credit-rating analysis noted that price increases on key products and higher sales volumes of brands such as Kraemi and Luncheon Meat lifted Food segment revenue.

Compared with large food and beverage groups, however, Hansung is far smaller by market capitalization and revenue, which may affect its negotiating power with distribution channels and its ability to absorb costs.

Whether Food segment growth rests on pricing or on volume can be judged from the revenue mix in coming quarters. Peers such as Dongwon Industries are a useful reference for seafood-linked margins.
