Report overview

Data-rich AI analytics, with profit recovery still the test

Newen AI is expanding abroad on the back of a data asset of more than 200 billion records and a government R&D award, but an operating loss in 2025 and a wider operating loss in the third quarter of 2026 leave profit recovery as the key test.

  1. 1

    Revenue on a DART-filed basis fell 4.5% to KRW 18.8 billion in 2025, and the company swung from an operating profit of KRW 429 million in 2024 to an operating loss of KRW 1.11 billion in 2025.

  2. 2

    Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 4.84 billion, up 0.4% year on year, while the operating loss widened to KRW 367 million from KRW 149 million a year earlier.

  3. 3

    In July 2026 Newen AI was selected, from a field with 18.4-to-1 competition, for a Ministry of SMEs and Startups project to build a K-beauty AI platform with a total R&D budget of KRW 3.5 billion over two years.

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Newen AI is an industry-specific AI analytics company that collects and analyzes conversational and unstructured data from online platforms and sells the results to enterprises, having been founded in 2004.

Its core product line is the general-purpose trend analytics platform Quettai, and the company says it operates five vertical platforms in Korea covering global trends, consumers, commerce and beauty.

Before its listing, an investor briefing presented revenue shares of 32.9% for Quetta Data and 40.5% for Quetta Enterprise. Its client base spans telecom and electronics, retail and commerce, broadcasting and advertising, automotive and manufacturing, finance and insurance, and cosmetics.

More than 88% of revenue comes from private companies, 77% of clients have stayed for three years or more, more than 200 clients are in active projects, and cumulative projects exceed 550. Clients cited include L'Oréal, Amorepacific, CJ Olive Young and Cosmax.

The company says its data assets exceed 200 billion records, about seven times the industry average, and it adds more than 900 industry-specific AI models. On competition, the company points to data collection across 150 countries and 30 language areas, and to its in-house Quetta LLMs as differentiators.

The business model is subscription-based AI analytics, which one assessment has compared to Salesforce's cloud subscription model. Quettai MCP, released on November 16, 2026, lets AI agents such as ChatGPT and Claude call the company's social data analytics directly, and it uses usage-based credit billing.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results describe a year in which revenue declined and the company slipped into operating loss. Revenue fell 4.5% from KRW 19.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 18.8 billion in 2025.

Operating profit went from KRW 429 million in 2024 to an operating loss of KRW 1.11 billion in 2025, and the operating margin fell from 2.2% to -5.9%. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed from KRW 5.62 billion in 2024 to KRW 1.05 billion in 2025, but both years were loss-making.

Net income including non-controlling interests showed the same direction, with a net loss of KRW 5.62 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.05 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 40.9% in 2024 to 7.7% in 2025, and total equity of KRW 53.5 billion at end-2025 was all attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 4.14 billion.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 56 million in 2024 to KRW 972 million in 2025, so cash generation stayed positive even in a loss-making operating year.

Quarterly revenue moved in both directions: KRW 4.82 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 5.34 billion in the fourth quarter (up 10.7% quarter on quarter), KRW 4.34 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 18.7%), KRW 5.31 billion in the second quarter (up 22.3%), and KRW 4.84 billion in the third quarter (down 8.9%).

Operating profit turned positive in the fourth quarter of 2025 at KRW 574 million and in the second quarter of 2026 at KRW 227 million, but operating losses of KRW 149 million, KRW 745 million and KRW 367 million were recorded in the third quarter of 2025, the first quarter of 2026 and the third quarter of 2026, so loss quarters outnumbered profit quarters.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was positive in the fourth quarter of 2025 at KRW 553 million and in the second quarter of 2026 at KRW 412 million, and the four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026 sum to KRW 544 million.

In the pre-listing explanation, rising personnel costs and solution development expenses were described as entering a stabilization phase. Against the 2025 revenue target of KRW 25.0 billion presented during the IPO, the filed 2025 revenue of KRW 18.8 billion was lower.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for AI analytics is growing as companies use social and commerce data in decision-making. The company says that as more businesses adopt generative AI in day-to-day work, customers increasingly ask to connect social data into their internal AI environments.

Among end markets, K-beauty is growing in the United States through major retail channels such as Sephora and Amazon, which reporting cites as raising demand for local consumer trend data. In media and advertising, a brand partnership performance analysis with Netflix Korea has been added as a reference.

In a government project evaluation, a data base of more than 30 billion records and GPU cluster infrastructure were cited as evaluation points. On competition, the company presents the difficulty of securing unstructured conversational data as a barrier to entry.

Conversely, if general-purpose large language model providers strengthen their own data analysis features, the role of external data suppliers could narrow, which is a structural variable.

Quettai MCP, released on November 16, 2026, means general-purpose AI draws on the company's data, so the relationship could partly shift from competition toward supply.

Overseas, the company overlaps with global beauty data analytics providers and says it aims to differentiate through 22 years of accumulated unstructured conversational data analysis and its in-house language model.

Domestically, an official at the listing lead underwriter praised the fact that revenue comes from private companies rather than public institutions.

06

Outlook

The 2027 targets presented during the 2025 IPO process were revenue of KRW 44.5 billion, operating profit of KRW 17.8 billion and net income of KRW 14.0 billion.

Compared with the filed 2025 revenue of KRW 18.8 billion, the revenue target is more than double, and reporting noted that reaching it would require annual revenue growth of more than 30%. The company said it would use KRW 33.3 billion of IPO proceeds for R&D on new products and for overseas expansion costs.

Within that, KRW 6.4 billion was allocated to multilingual translation of the Quetta service, whose launch was indicated for 2026. Full-scale overseas expansion has been described as starting in 2027.

In North America, the plan is to build sales hubs in the western, southern and midwestern regions starting from the New Jersey subsidiary, and then expand to Japan, Europe, Southeast Asia and the Middle East.

VUSSENS was shown at K-Beauty Expo Korea in October 2026, and a North American commercial launch was planned for the second half of 2026. The Netflix Korea data collaboration was presented as a basis for expanding in entertainment, with the company also seeking related opportunities in global markets.

The government project runs for two years with a budget of KRW 3.5 billion and follows a three-stage rollout that moves from pilot deployments to paid subscriptions. Quettai MCP will add analysis functions for individual enterprise workflows in sequence.

The next checkpoints are how fourth-quarter 2026 and full-year revenue and operating profit appear in the 2026 annual report, and the actual revenue growth rate against the 2027 target.

08

Bull factors

Government R&D award and data assets

In July 2026 Newen AI was selected for a Ministry of SMEs and Startups domain-specific AI project, won from 18.4-to-1 competition, and it will run R&D worth KRW 3.5 billion over two years.

Evaluators cited a data base of more than 30 billion records, GPU infrastructure, and collaboration cases with L'Oréal and Amorepacific. The data asset of more than 200 billion records is presented as underpinning its position as a data supplier.

Long-tenured clients and a private revenue base

The company reports that 77% of clients have been with it for three years or more, and private companies account for more than 88% of revenue. More than 200 clients are in active projects, and cumulative projects exceed 550.

An official at the listing lead underwriter said the revenue from private companies rather than public institutions was highly rated.

Overseas and product expansion pipeline

Expansion axes include the North American subsidiary, the planned North American launch of VUSSENS, and the data collaboration with Netflix Korea. Quettai MCP, released on November 16, 2026, extends distribution into AI agent environments. The company says it plans to strengthen partnerships within the agentic AI ecosystem.

09

Bear factors

Weakening earnings capacity

Operating profit moved from KRW 429 million in 2024 to an operating loss of KRW 1.11 billion in 2025, and the operating margin fell to -5.9%. The third-quarter 2026 operating loss of KRW 367 million was larger than the KRW 149 million loss a year earlier.

The four quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026 sum to only KRW 544 million of net income attributable to controlling shareholders.

Stalled revenue growth and a gap to targets

Revenue fell 4.5% to KRW 18.8 billion in 2025 and fell short of the KRW 25.0 billion target for 2025 presented during the IPO. Third-quarter 2026 revenue rose only 0.4% year on year. The 2027 revenue target of KRW 44.5 billion is more than double the 2025 result, which places a heavy demand on growth speed.

General-purpose AI and overseas competition

If general-purpose large language models strengthen their own data analysis, the differentiation of external analytics platforms could weaken. Overseas, the company also competes with global beauty data analytics providers.

No separate figures have been disclosed for the revenue contribution of the North American subsidiary or VUSSENS.

10

Risk factors

Earnings volatility

Quarterly operating profit has alternated between profit and loss, with swings between turning positive and turning negative repeated from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. With a small four-quarter total, earnings metrics move sharply on modest changes. If personnel and solution development costs rise again, the operating loss could widen.

Gap to targets

The 2027 targets of KRW 44.5 billion in revenue and KRW 17.8 billion in operating profit are far above the 2025 results. Reporting said reaching them requires annual growth of more than 30%. Whether the company has revised these targets will become clear through later disclosures.

Platform structure and billing transition

If general-purpose AI such as ChatGPT or Claude broadens its own search and analysis, the differentiation of external data analytics services could weaken. The company chose to supply data to general AI through Quettai MCP, but no separate figures have been disclosed for its revenue contribution. Data acquisition costs and changes in personal data regulation could also become cost factors.

12

Overall view

Founded in 2004, Newen AI has built a base of more than 200 billion data records, more than 200 clients and more than 88% of revenue from private companies.

Expansion news has followed, including the government R&D award in July 2026, the release of Quettai MCP on November 16, 2026, and preparations for a North American subsidiary and VUSSENS.

However, the filed results show a swing from an operating profit of KRW 429 million in 2024 to an operating loss of KRW 1.11 billion in 2025, and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders in both 2024 and 2025.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 4.84 billion rose only 0.4% year on year, and the operating loss widened to KRW 367 million.

Strengths include the data assets, the long-tenured client base, and the government R&D and overseas expansion pipeline, while weaknesses include thin earnings capacity, the gap between revenue and targets, and competition from general-purpose AI.

On valuation, market capitalization sits below end-2025 total equity, but earnings-based multiples will read differently depending on when profit turns sustainably positive. The next checkpoints are full-year operating results in the 2026 annual report and revenue progress against the 2027 target.
