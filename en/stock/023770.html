02

Business structure

Playwith Korea operates online and mobile MMORPGs built on its own Seal and Lohan IP. According to a company overview, about 87% of revenue comes from mobile games and 13% from online games.

Seal Online is the PC flagship and Seal M is its mobile version, a cartoon-style action MMORPG that carries the original's comic tone to phones. The original Seal title is described as having a user base in Greater China and Southeast Asia.

First-quarter 2026 domestic revenue was KRW 2.55 billion, down about 56% year on year, and overseas revenue for core titles such as Seal M and Lohan 2 fell about 33%. Public filings and news do not separate how much of this decline came from IP aging versus a gap in new releases.

In the first half of 2026 the company signed a Seal IP license deal with a Thai developer and is expanding licensing through its IP hub site. Its second-half 2026 pipeline includes the mobile MMORPG Seal M2 and Lohan Classic, a Lohan-based project.

The company said Seal M2 passed 1 million pre-registrations at the end of September 2026. Developer interviews for Seal M2 involved staff from Playwith Games and Bluefish Studio.

Among listed game companies, Playwith is small by disclosed revenue (KRW 34.7 billion in 2025), so a single title's performance can move quarterly results sharply.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 14.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 28.6 billion in 2023 (up 96.8% year on year), KRW 32.3 billion in 2024 (up 13.2%), and KRW 34.7 billion in 2025 (up 7.3%). Revenue grew for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of 2022–2025.

Operating losses were KRW 7.19 billion in 2022, KRW 6.65 billion in 2023, KRW 8.53 billion in 2024, and KRW 4.68 billion in 2025, which means four consecutive years of operating losses from 2022 to 2025. The loss widened in 2024 and narrowed in 2025.

The operating margin was -49.5% in 2022, -23.3% in 2023, -26.4% in 2024, and -13.5% in 2025, the smallest loss margin of the period. Owner-attributable net losses were KRW 3.01 billion, KRW 5.99 billion, KRW 6.9 billion, and KRW 2.01 billion for 2022 through 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests is disclosed with the same amounts. Operating cash flow was negative in 2022 to 2024 (KRW -3.22 billion, KRW -3.96 billion, KRW -1.37 billion) and turned to KRW 4.27 billion in 2025. The debt ratio went from 25.0% in 2022 to 94.1% in 2024 and 68.1% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 17.9 billion, all attributable to owners of the parent, and total liabilities were KRW 12.2 billion.

Quarterly revenue was KRW 8.05 billion in the third quarter of 2025, KRW 5.47 billion in the fourth quarter (down 32.0% from the prior quarter), KRW 3.69 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (down 32.6%), KRW 8.31 billion in the second quarter (up 125.1%), and KRW 3.82 billion in the third quarter (down 54.0% sequentially and 52.5% year on year).

Operating losses ran from KRW 236 million in the third quarter of 2025 through KRW 2.84 billion, KRW 1.81 billion, KRW 183 million, and KRW 2.23 billion in the third quarter of 2026, five consecutive loss-making quarters.

Standalone revenue fell 51.0% in the first quarter of 2026, but cost cuts reduced the operating loss by 23.1%. Owner-attributable net income turned positive at KRW 607 million in the first quarter of 2026 and was KRW 56.08 million in the second quarter, then turned into a KRW 2.29 billion loss in the third quarter. The owner-attributable net loss for the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totals KRW 2.22 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In Korea's game industry, mobile MMORPG earnings vary widely depending on new title performance and how well existing IP is used. Playwith's past Lohan M success pushed its shares up sharply, after which the Korea Exchange designated the stock for investment warning and halted trading for a day.

That episode shows how new-title results can move the share price and investor attention for a small-cap company. Seal M2 developers have emphasized keeping the Seal series' tone while adding current MMORPG features. The key variable for Seal M2 is whether it can retain legacy fans while attracting new players.

Lohan Classic is presented as a reinterpretation of the original Lohan's feel and core systems for today's service environment. Among listed peers such as Krafton, Netmarble, Pearl Abyss, Wemade and Webzen, larger IP portfolios or more titles are common.

Playwith is small in revenue, so its results fall sharply during gaps between launches, and the third-quarter 2026 revenue decline reflects that pattern.

In overseas markets, securing China approval and Southeast Asian licensing are the main expansion routes, though revenue from those routes is not yet separately visible in the filings.

Lohan Classic, a Lohan IP project, is in preparation as a major second-half business. In a September 2026 press release, the company said a mobile game based on the Seal Online IP obtained a China publishing approval.

Intangible assets at the end of the first quarter of 2026 were KRW 14.07 billion, up KRW 2.59 billion from year-end 2025, and assets under construction rose by KRW 3.85 billion. This points to continued investment in new projects, but the filings alone do not show when these assets will convert into revenue.

Seal M2's revenue contribution has not yet appeared in the disclosed results. The first full quarter with Seal M2 is the fourth quarter of 2026. Based on the timing of last year's preliminary results (February 2, 2026), the fourth-quarter preliminary figures would likely come out in early 2027.

The next checkpoints are whether fourth-quarter revenue recovers from the KRW 3.82 billion of the third quarter and whether the five-quarter operating loss streak ends. The 2026 annual report must be filed by the end of March 2027.

Domestic and overseas revenue declines

Small capital base and concentrated earnings

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 17.9 billion, a small base. The debt ratio fell from 94.1% in 2024 to 68.1% in 2025 but remains elevated. Operating cash flow was negative from 2022 to 2024, and the 2025 positive result reflects a single year. Because quarterly results swing with one or two titles, earnings visibility is low.

Overall view

Playwith posted operating losses every year from 2022 to 2025, but its loss margin narrowed from -49.5% in 2022 to -13.5% in 2025. Revenue rose three straight years to KRW 34.7 billion in 2025, and operating cash flow turned positive at KRW 4.27 billion.

Yet in the third quarter of 2026 revenue fell to KRW 3.82 billion and the operating loss widened to KRW 2.23 billion, extending the losing streak to five quarters.

More than 1 million pre-registrations for Seal M2 and the scheduled October 22, 2026 launch are the basis for expectations around new titles, while the Thai license and China approval are the IP expansion routes.

However, Seal M2's revenue contribution has not yet appeared in the filings, and domestic revenue fell about 56% in the first quarter of 2026. The next checkpoints are the 2026 fourth-quarter preliminary results and the 2026 annual report, and whether new titles end the quarterly losses is the central question.
