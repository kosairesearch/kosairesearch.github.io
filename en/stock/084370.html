02

Business structure

Eugene Technology makes thin-film deposition equipment used in the front-end semiconductor process. Its core products are low-pressure chemical vapor deposition (LPCVD) and atomic layer deposition (ALD) tools, and in ALD it is regarded as one of the leading domestic players alongside Jusung Engineering.

Its main customers are memory makers including Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, where finer process nodes require more ALD steps. According to industry sources, an ALD furnace tool sells for roughly KRW 6-7 billion each, compared with KRW 3-4 billion for a conventional CVD tool.

Reports from the early 2020s said Samsung Electronics, which had relied almost entirely on Kokusai Electric for ALD tools, added Eugene Technology equipment to its process and diversified its supply.

That led to a patent dispute with Kokusai Electric, and in December 2024 the Korean Intellectual Property Tribunal was reported to have invalidated part of a Kokusai patent. The dispute was reported to be continuing.

The breakdown of revenue by product line is a point to check in the business-segment disclosures of the periodic reports. Customer concentration is structurally tied to the investment decisions of memory makers.

04

Earnings analysis

On the basis of the periodic reports, annual revenue fell from KRW 310.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 276.5 billion in 2023 (down 11.0% year on year), then rose to KRW 338.1 billion in 2024 (up 22.3%) and KRW 350.3 billion in 2025 (up 3.6%), growing for two consecutive years from 2024 to 2025.

Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest. Operating profit dropped from KRW 53.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 24.3 billion in 2023 (down 54.7%), recovered to KRW 61.2 billion in 2024 (up 151.5%), and fell again to KRW 51.7 billion in 2025 (down 15.5%), with 2024 the largest year.

Operating margin was 17.3%, 8.8%, 18.1% and 14.7% from 2022 to 2025. In 2023 the drop in operating profit was far steeper than the drop in revenue, which points to cost-structure swings amplifying the earnings change.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 37.9 billion in 2022, KRW 24.4 billion in 2023 (down 35.6%), KRW 63.3 billion in 2024 (up 158.9%) and KRW 42.5 billion in 2025 (down 32.9%).

Consolidated net profit, which includes non-controlling interests, was a different figure: KRW 65.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 44.5 billion in 2025. At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 491.4 billion, of which KRW 459.5 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 89.2 billion.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 38.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 66.5 billion in 2025.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 62.3 billion in 3Q25 to KRW 99.9 billion in 4Q25 (up 60.2% from the prior quarter), then KRW 101.9 billion in 1Q26, KRW 122.1 billion in 2Q26, and KRW 105.6 billion in 3Q26 (down 13.5% from the prior quarter).

Operating profit was KRW 5.18 billion in 3Q25, KRW 17.5 billion in 4Q25, KRW 18.8 billion in 1Q26, KRW 18.2 billion in 2Q26 and KRW 24.9 billion in 3Q26, so every quarter from 4Q25 to 3Q26 stayed at or above KRW 17.5 billion.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was far more uneven, at KRW 7.88 billion in 3Q25, KRW 16.4 billion in 4Q25, KRW 21.8 billion in 1Q26, KRW 65.2 billion in 2Q26 and KRW 10.9 billion in 3Q26. The sum for the last four quarters was KRW 114.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

ALD deposits films one atomic layer at a time, so its use is growing in advanced memory nodes where the number of process steps keeps rising.

Industry sources view ALD tools as essential for Samsung Electronics and SK hynix as they push finer geometries, and this trend has been cited as a backdrop for the share-price moves of Eugene Technology and Jusung Engineering. The ALD supply landscape, however, has remained centered on Japanese vendors.

Reports from the early 2020s said Samsung Electronics had relied heavily on Kokusai Electric tools and also dealt with Tokyo Electron and other Japanese suppliers. Analysts at the time argued that price negotiations on early volume orders were unavoidable and could push prices below the average.

The memory investment cycle is another variable. In early 2024, brokerages expected Samsung Electronics to trim NAND and DRAM investment but judged that resumed orders from SK hynix and Micron would offset the impact.

In the same period, analysts argued that customers' finer-geometry investment would add process steps and expand order intake. Those analyses are dated and should not be carried straight into a judgment of current conditions.

Domestically, the company's competitive position in ALD batch equipment is comparable to that of Jusung Engineering on a qualitative basis. Where the industry sits in its cycle will depend on how much the 2026 recovery in quarterly results relies on customers expanding their capital spending.

06

Outlook

The direction of future earnings depends on the scale of memory customers' investment and the share of ALD in new orders. Quarterly revenue held near the KRW 100 billion level, at KRW 99.9 billion in 4Q25, KRW 101.9 billion in 1Q26, KRW 122.1 billion in 2Q26 and KRW 105.6 billion in 3Q26.

Revenue fell from the prior quarter in 3Q26 while operating profit rose to KRW 24.9 billion, so the effect of product mix on margins needs to be checked in the coming quarters.

The first checkpoint in the next periodic report, the FY2026 annual report, is how annual revenue compares with KRW 350.3 billion in 2025 and whether operating margin moves from the 14.7% recorded in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit has stayed at or above KRW 17.5 billion since 4Q25, so whether that level holds on an annual basis matters. A 2024 analysis argued that customers' shift to finer geometries would lift tool orders, but that was an outlook at the time and should be compared with the current order flow.

The patent litigation with Kokusai Electric has no fixed ruling date, so its effect on the ALD supply landscape is hard to gauge until a decision is issued. The 1Q27 earnings release is likely around May 2027, based on the 1Q26 release date of May 13, 2026.

Customers' investment direction often becomes visible first in their early-year earnings releases and capital plans.

08

Bull factors

Quarterly earnings power recovering

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 24.9 billion rose 380.3% from a year earlier, well ahead of the 69.4% revenue growth. Every quarter from 4Q25 to 3Q26 posted operating profit of at least KRW 17.5 billion.

Bulls point to room for operating margin to climb back toward the 18.1% recorded in 2024 if the improvement continues.

ALD demand and tool pricing

Finer process nodes that add ALD steps are cited as the basis for medium-term demand. Industry sources put an ALD furnace tool at KRW 6-7 billion each, above the KRW 3-4 billion for a conventional CVD tool, which makes the shift to advanced nodes favorable in order value. Samsung's move to diversify its ALD suppliers is also cited as an opening for domestic vendors.

Customer diversification

Earlier reports said Samsung Electronics diversified away from a single Japanese ALD supplier by adding Eugene Technology tools. That was described as raising purchasing leverage and widening the adoption of domestic equipment. Bulls cite the potential to move beyond reliance on a single customer.

09

Bear factors

Large earnings swings

Operating profit fell 54.7% to KRW 24.3 billion in 2023, pushing operating margin down to 8.8%. In 2025 it fell a further 15.5% to KRW 51.7 billion. Bears cite a record of sharp earnings swings tied to memory customers' investment cycles.

Gap between quarterly net and operating profit

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 65.2 billion in 2Q26, well above operating profit of KRW 18.2 billion, then fell to KRW 10.9 billion in 3Q26, down 83.3% from the prior quarter.

Because quarterly net profit has diverged from operating results, bears argue the durability of earnings is hard to judge until one-off items are identified.

Patent dispute and pricing pressure

The patent dispute with Kokusai Electric was reported to be continuing even after the Korean Intellectual Property Tribunal invalidated part of a Kokusai patent in December 2024. A prolonged dispute could weigh on new supply contracts and customer confidence. Earlier analyses also argued that price negotiations on early volume orders would be unavoidable.

10

Risk factors

Patent litigation

The patent dispute with Kokusai Electric has been flagged as potentially long-running. Depending on the ruling, the ALD sales landscape and royalty burden could change. Progress will become visible through court decisions and disclosures.

Customer investment cycle

If memory customers such as Samsung Electronics and SK hynix cut capital spending, tool orders could slip. An early 2024 analysis also raised the possibility of Samsung trimming NAND and DRAM investment.

Earlier reports pointed to dependence on particular customers, so a single client's investment adjustment could show up directly in quarterly results.

Earnings volatility and one-off items

Operating profit fell 15.5% in 2025, and in 2Q26 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was far above operating profit. If non-operating items or tax effects lifted that figure, it could diverge from underlying operating strength. The detailed income-statement items in the annual report are the reference for separating the two.

12

Overall view

Eugene Technology's third quarter of 2026 showed a quarterly profit recovery, with revenue of KRW 105.6 billion and operating profit of KRW 24.9 billion. On an annual basis, operating profit has swung widely, with 2024 (KRW 61.2 billion) the largest year and 2023 (KRW 24.3 billion) the smallest.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 65.2 billion in 2Q26 was far above operating profit of KRW 18.2 billion, so whether one-off items drove that gap remains the key point to confirm.

On the bull side, the cited factors are ALD demand from finer process nodes, tool pricing structure and customer diversification. On the bear side, the cited factors are earnings volatility, the patent dispute with Kokusai Electric and concentration on memory customers' investment cycles.

On valuation, the facts are that quarter-end price-to-book ratio stayed between the mid-1x and low-3x range in a business with large earnings swings. Once the FY2026 annual report confirms full-year operating margin and the composition of 2Q26 earnings, the durability of this quarter's recovery can be assessed.
