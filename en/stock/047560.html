Report overview

AI Revenue Grows as Losses Narrow, Profit Still Elusive

ESTsoft posted 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 27.3 billion, which the company said was a third-quarter record, but losses persist with an operating loss of KRW 1.93 billion and a net loss attributable to owners of the parent of KRW 2.94 billion.

  1. 1

    Annual revenue rose from KRW 88.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 106.8 billion in 2025, with three straight years of growth from 2023. Over the same period the operating loss widened, reaching KRW 19 billion in 2025 with an operating margin of -17.7%.

  2. 2

    3Q 2026 revenue rose 9.5% year on year, and the operating loss narrowed from KRW 4.48 billion a year earlier to KRW 1.93 billion. AI·SW and games drove growth, while the portal and commerce businesses declined.

  3. 3

    Profit attributable to owners of the parent turned positive at KRW 175 million in 1Q 2026, then returned to losses of KRW 889 million in 2Q and KRW 2.94 billion in 3Q. The net loss attributable to owners of the parent totals KRW 12.4 billion over the last four quarters.

  4. 4

    On a cumulative basis for the first nine months of 2026, AI·SW accounted for 52.3% of revenue, confirming a shift in the business mix toward AI. The asset management unit, however, remains a variable that moves quarterly results with stock market weakness.

  5. 5

    The debt-to-equity ratio reached 196.3% at year-end 2025, up sharply from 124.9% in 2024, and operating cash flow was -KRW 17.7 billion in 2025.

02

Business structure

ESTsoft runs a business mix spanning AI and software (AI·SW), games, portal, commerce and asset management. In the first nine months of 2026, AI·SW generated KRW 46.0 billion of cumulative revenue, or 52.3% of the total of KRW 88.0 billion.

The AI·SW segment includes the real-time AI avatar service PERSO Interactive, the AI search and agent service Alan, and established software products such as Altools and ALYac.

PERSO Interactive is expanding from public, education, mobility and tourism into entertainment and retail, and proof-of-concept projects with Olive Young and 7-Eleven are under way.

The game business reached cumulative revenue of KRW 23.9 billion, up 12.2% year on year, on the launch of the new title Kabal RED, although its operating profit fell from KRW 5 billion to KRW 2.7 billion over the same period.

The portal business, which runs Zum.com, saw cumulative revenue fall about 48% to KRW 1.9 billion, with an operating loss of KRW 2.8 billion. Commerce revenue was KRW 5.2 billion, down 17.9%, and asset management revenue was KRW 11 billion, slightly lower.

Asset management assets grew, but weak stock markets produced an operating loss of about KRW 1.9 billion in 3Q 2026.

Competitively, ESTsoft contends with domestic AI companies for public-sector AI transformation (AX) and enterprise AI adoption demand, and it is using its role as a supplier under the Ministry of Science and ICT's AX One-Stop Voucher program to build demand among small and mid-sized companies.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 88.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 92.5 billion in 2023 (up 4.2%), KRW 102.5 billion in 2024 (up 10.8%) and KRW 106.8 billion in 2025 (up 4.2%). Revenue grew for three consecutive years from 2023, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022 to 2025 period.

Over the same span the operating loss widened from KRW 5.67 billion in 2022 to KRW 8.91 billion, KRW 13.5 billion and KRW 19 billion in 2025, with operating margins of -6.4%, -9.6%, -13.1% and -17.7%. Operating losses continued for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025.

The company attributed the 2025 widening to accelerated infrastructure spending, including building a GPU farm for AI service and product development.

The net loss attributable to owners of the parent grew from KRW 6.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.7 billion in 2025, and the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 20.6 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow swung from KRW 199 million in 2022 to a negative position in later years, reaching -KRW 17.7 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 196.3% at year-end 2025 from 124.9% in 2024, with total liabilities of KRW 130.2 billion against total equity of KRW 66.3 billion, of which KRW 50.2 billion belongs to owners of the parent.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 24.9 billion in 3Q 2025 to KRW 25.5 billion in 4Q 2025 and KRW 31 billion in 1Q 2026, which was the peak, before falling to KRW 29.7 billion in 2Q and KRW 27.3 billion in 3Q 2026, the second straight quarterly decline.

The company described 1Q 2026 revenue as its largest first-quarter figure on record. Operating loss widened to KRW 10.2 billion in 4Q 2025 and then narrowed to KRW 1.67 billion, KRW 332 million and KRW 1.93 billion across 1Q to 3Q 2026.

The company said non-operating gains from share disposals and financial asset valuation helped the 1Q 2026 net result turn positive. Since 2Q 2026, losses attributable to owners of the parent have widened again, and the four-quarter total from 4Q 2025 to 3Q 2026 is a net loss of KRW 12.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand in the domestic AI software market centers on public-sector AI transformation (AX) and enterprise AI adoption. ESTsoft participates as a supplier under the Ministry of Science and ICT's AX One-Stop Voucher program to build demand from small and mid-sized companies.

It is also a participating organization in the LG AI Research elite team within the government's sovereign AI foundation model project.

Demand for AI avatars is broadening, as PERSO Interactive expands from public, education, mobility and tourism into entertainment and retail, and the company said it identified new customers at domestic and overseas exhibitions.

In security, ESTsoft launched ALYac Agentic EDR for the AI security market, and its affiliate ESTsecurity is developing a security-specialized AI model with Naver Cloud. The game business depends on the launch effect of the new title Kabal RED.

The portal business faces pressure from continued declines in advertising revenue. The company says its own GPU infrastructure offers room to lower cloud costs.

Its position combines software, security products and AI services in one structure, though its scale and investment capacity are clearly smaller than those of large platform companies.

06

Outlook

The company plans to keep growing its AI·SW and game businesses while continuing cost efficiency across all businesses to improve profitability.

In its 1H 2026 results, the company cited synergies with national AX projects, expansion of PERSO Interactive into retail and mobility, and ALYac Agentic EDR as drivers of second-half revenue growth.

Earlier in 2026, it said the year would be a turning point when revenue growth and profitability improvement appear together. In its 1Q 2026 results, it expected the expansion of PERSO Interactive at home and abroad, AI education growth and steady game revenue to keep improving results.

Revenue fell in 2Q and 3Q 2026, however, and losses attributable to owners of the parent widened again, so there is a gap between the company's outlook and reported results. The next checkpoints are how much the operating loss narrows in 4Q 2026 and whether PERSO Interactive supply expansion sustains AI·SW growth.

Whether the AX One-Stop Voucher supplier role converts into revenue from small and mid-sized companies is also a point to watch, along with how game revenue holds up as the new title's launch effect fades. Whether cost efficiency extends beyond advertising and operating expenses also matters.

08

Bull factors

AI·SW revenue growth

AI·SW revenue reached KRW 15.7 billion in 3Q 2026, up 19.2% year on year, and its cumulative share of revenue for the first nine months is 52.3%. The company cites the expansion of PERSO Interactive from public and education into entertainment and retail as the basis for growth. The company said it will continue to grow AI·SW.

Cost efficiency and narrowing losses

The operating loss of KRW 1.93 billion in 3Q 2026 was smaller than the KRW 4.48 billion a year earlier. Over the same period, advertising costs fell 43.1% and operating expenses fell 10.3%. The company attributes this to stabilized new AI services and internal AI adoption.

A profitable 1Q 2026

Profit attributable to owners of the parent turned positive at KRW 175 million in 1Q 2026, and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.67 billion from KRW 4.37 billion a year earlier. The company said results began to reflect earlier AI infrastructure investment. Non-operating gains also contributed to that profit.

09

Bear factors

Weak portal and commerce

Cumulative portal revenue fell about 48% year on year to KRW 1.9 billion, and the portal business recorded an operating loss of KRW 2.8 billion. Commerce revenue fell 17.9% to KRW 5.2 billion. Portal advertising revenue kept declining, and commerce sales fell in online and direct-store channels.

Quarterly losses widen again

Profit attributable to owners of the parent slipped back to a net loss of KRW 889 million in 2Q 2026 and widened to KRW 2.94 billion in 3Q. The asset management unit posted an operating loss of about KRW 1.9 billion in 3Q as stock markets weakened.

Rising financial burden

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 196.3% at year-end 2025 from 124.9% in 2024. Total liabilities stood at KRW 130.2 billion and total equity at KRW 66.3 billion. Operating cash flow reached -KRW 17.7 billion in 2025, a wider deficit.

10

Risk factors

Business concentration risk

Revenue growth in 3Q relied on AI·SW and the launch effect of a new game title. Game revenue may decline as the Kabal RED launch effect fades, and the pace of PERSO Interactive expansion will determine whether overall growth can continue.

Financing and cash flow risk

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 196.3% at year-end 2025, and operating cash flow remains negative. If AI infrastructure investment continues, additional funding may be needed. Of total equity of KRW 66.3 billion, KRW 50.2 billion belongs to owners of the parent.

Market exposure

The asset management unit posted losses despite growth in assets under management because of weak stock markets. Its results depend on market conditions the company cannot control. The portal business continues to lose revenue as advertising markets shift.

12

Overall view

ESTsoft posted 3Q 2026 revenue of KRW 27.3 billion, up 9.5% year on year, and its operating loss narrowed to KRW 1.93 billion. Revenue rose from KRW 88.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 106.8 billion in 2025, but the operating loss widened over the same period and the operating margin fell to -17.7%.

AI·SW made up 52.3% of cumulative revenue for the first nine months of 2026, so the business mix is shifting toward AI. On costs, lower advertising and operating expenses are a positive factor.

Against that, losses attributable to owners of the parent widened again from 2Q 2026, and the four-quarter total is a net loss of KRW 12.4 billion. Weakness in portal and commerce, a debt-to-equity ratio of 196.3% and negative operating cash flow remain financial burdens.

The next points to confirm are the 4Q 2026 operating loss, the full-year results and leverage in the 2026 annual report, and whether the 1Q 2027 profit can be repeated.
