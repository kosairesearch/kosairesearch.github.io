02

Business structure

E Kocref CR-REIT is a corporate restructuring real estate investment company that has been listed on KOSPI since 2018 and generates rental income from the properties it holds.

Its operating margin of 78.5% in the year ended June 2026, on revenue of KRW 23.3 billion and operating income of KRW 18.3 billion, reflects the high-margin profile typical of asset-holding REITs. A major shareholder, Mirae Asset, reported a 16.57% stake as of March 24, 2026.

A March 19, 2026 disclosure appointed Kim Hong-jun, formerly CFO at E-Land Retail, as CEO; he is a related party of the largest shareholder and was scheduled to take office on March 22.

In the listed REIT market, the company competes for investor capital with Macquarie Korea Infrastructure Fund, SK REIT, Lotte REIT and Hanwha REIT. Its distinguishing feature is a dividend guidance of around 7% a year based on the IPO price.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue rose from KRW 22.3 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 22.8 billion in the year ended June 2024 (up 2.1% year on year), KRW 23.2 billion in the year ended June 2025 (up 2.0%), and KRW 23.3 billion in the year ended June 2026 (up 0.5%).

Growth slowed each year, with three consecutive annual increases from the year ended June 2024, and the year ended June 2026 was the largest year in the period. Operating income rose from KRW 17.3 billion to KRW 18.3 billion over the same span, with three consecutive annual increases from the year ended June 2024.

The operating margin was 77.5%, 77.4%, 78.3% and 78.5% across the four fiscal years, so it dipped only in the year ended June 2024 and then widened, meaning operating costs grew more slowly than revenue.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 9.91 billion in the year ended June 2023, the highest in the period, and KRW 5.03 billion in the year ended June 2024 (down 49.2%), the lowest.

It recovered to KRW 6.95 billion in the year ended June 2025 (up 38.2%) before slipping to KRW 6.75 billion in the year ended June 2026 (down 2.9%).

In the year ended June 2024 operating income increased to KRW 17.6 billion while attributable profit nearly halved, which shows that items below operating income drove the swings in net profit. Net profit including non-controlling interests is reported at the same amounts as attributable profit in each year.

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 15 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 10.4 billion in the year ended June 2024, then recovered to KRW 12.2 billion in the year ended June 2025 and KRW 11.9 billion in the year ended June 2026. The debt ratio rose from 193.6% to 216.4%.

Over the last four calendar quarters (Q3 2025 to Q2 2026), attributable profit totaled KRW 6.75 billion, matching the year ended June 2026 figure.

05

Industry analysis

The listed REIT market is highly sensitive to the rate environment. Reports say concern over higher financing costs after further policy rate hikes has weighed on investor sentiment across listed REITs. Within that trend, share price pressure appeared alongside moves across the sector.

The asset manager emphasized that lease terms and cash flow structures have not changed significantly. Peers in the same sector include Macquarie Korea Infrastructure Fund, SK REIT, Lotte REIT, Hanwha REIT, Shinhan Alpha REIT and Koramco Life Infra REIT.

E Kocref CR-REIT is distinct from general REITs because it is classified as a corporate restructuring REIT. Its 78.5% operating margin reflects rental-based profitability, while a debt ratio of 216.4% means interest costs could weigh on earnings in a rising-rate environment.

Its position within the sector therefore depends more on borrowing terms and the durability of its dividend guidance than on operating margin alone.

Outlook

The company has pointed to keeping its dividend guidance of around 7% a year, based on the IPO price. Reports say it is also reviewing shareholder return measures, including a shift to quarterly dividends.

The weighted average rate on the KRW 430 billion loan due in 2027 is 4.95%, and a stress test showed the guidance could be maintained even if rates rose by one percentage point to 5.95%.

On the sell-side side, KB Securities gave a buy rating in a report dated September 30, 2026, and also expressed a buy view in a March 25, 2026 report. The actual terms of the refinancing are the key test of the assumptions behind the dividend guidance.

Dividend policy and shareholder communication after the management change are the next items to track. The stake held by major shareholder Mirae Asset rose from 13.52% as of January 6, 2026 to 16.57% as of March 24, 2026, based on disclosures whose interpretation should be read alongside the filing trend.

The year ended June 2024 marked the low point at KRW 10.4 billion, and cash flow recovered to KRW 12.2 billion in the year ended June 2025. Cash generation held a steady level even in years when profit swung.

Bear factors

Earnings volatility at the net level

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 49.2% from KRW 9.91 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 5.03 billion in the year ended June 2024. It recovered 38.2% to KRW 6.95 billion in the year ended June 2025, then declined 2.9% to KRW 6.75 billion in the year ended June 2026.

Unlike operating income, which rose for three consecutive years, net profit has not returned to the year ended June 2023 level.

Rising leverage

The debt ratio climbed from 193.6% in the year ended June 2023 to 216.4% in the year ended June 2026. Total liabilities of KRW 455.4 billion as of June 2026 are more than twice total equity of KRW 210.5 billion. The wide gap between operating income and net profit may be related to this borrowing structure.

Rate and refinancing pressure

The weighted average rate on the loan due in 2027 is 4.95%. Reports say concern over higher financing costs after further policy rate hikes has weighed on sentiment across listed REITs. If rates move toward the 5.95% stress scenario, room for dividends could narrow.

The gap between attributable profit of KRW 6.75 billion and operating cash flow of KRW 11.9 billion is a reference point for judging dividend capacity.

Governance and shareholder changes

A March 19, 2026 disclosure appointed Kim Hong-jun as CEO, with a scheduled start on March 22. He is reported to be a related party of the largest shareholder.

Overall view

E Kocref CR-REIT has a rental-based structure in which the operating margin rose from 77.5% to 78.5%, and operating cash flow of KRW 11.9 billion in the year ended June 2026 exceeded attributable profit for the same year.

On the other side, profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 9.91 billion in the year ended June 2023 to KRW 6.75 billion in the year ended June 2026, and the debt ratio rose to 216.4%.

Revenue and operating income rose for three consecutive years, but the gains have not carried through to net profit, which is the central observation. On dividends, the guidance of around 7% on the IPO price and the review of quarterly payouts were reported as positive factors.

The actual rate terms on the KRW 430 billion loan due in 2027 are needed before the guidance assumptions can be tested. Buy ratings from KB Securities coexist with reports of weaker sentiment across listed REITs.

The next checkpoints are attributable profit in the quarterly report for October to December 2026 and the 2027 refinancing terms.
