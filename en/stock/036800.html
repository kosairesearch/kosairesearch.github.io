Report overview

Leading VAN Franchise: Checking Earnings After the KIS Deal

Nice Information & Telecommunication is combining its leading VAN share, its fast-growing TRS business and the KIS acquisition, which lifted third-quarter 2026 revenue sharply, while attributable net income fell in the same quarter, so earnings quality and integration gains are the next checkpoints.

  1. 1

    Operating profit for 2025 was KRW 55.3 billion, up 38.7% year on year, and has risen for two straight years since the 2023 low.

  2. 2

    Third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 399.8 billion, up 39.6% from a year earlier, and LS Securities says the KIS subsidiary was fully consolidated from that quarter.

  3. 3

    Attributable net income for the same quarter was KRW 12.2 billion, down 34.7% from the prior quarter, moving in the opposite direction to operating profit.

  4. 4

    TRS (tax refund) revenue rose from KRW 8.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 36.0 billion in 2025, and 2026 forecasts differ by broker, at KRW 42.7 billion (LS Securities) and KRW 45.9 billion (Shinhan Investment Corp.).

  5. 5

    The 2026–2028 return plan targets a standalone payout ratio of at least 25% and annual cancellation of at least 1% of issued shares, and 9.0% of treasury-linked shares were already cancelled in June 2026.

02

Business structure

Nice Information & Telecommunication runs its payment infrastructure on two pillars, VAN (value-added network) and PG (payment gateway). The VAN business connects merchant terminals with card authorization transactions, while the PG business handles online and mobile payment processing.

According to Dealsite, PG (computerized sales) accounted for 62.1% of first-quarter 2026 revenue at KRW 177.5 billion and VAN (service sales) for 29.2% at KRW 29.2 billion.

By contrast, VAN generated 88.8% of the KRW 55.3 billion operating profit in 2025, while PG contributed only 3.4%, so the revenue and profit contribution structures differ. Dealsite reported that the company absorbed its PG business division through a merger in October 2025.

FnGuide presents a VAN market share of 28.6%, ranking the company first in the industry. In June 2026 the company agreed to acquire a 96.1% stake in KIS Information & Telecommunication from NICE Holdings for about KRW 112.3 billion and brought it in as a subsidiary.

That VAN company was described as holding a 10–15% domestic VAN share, and the combined share of the two companies was presented at 42.7% (Alpha Economy). TRS, the tax refund business for foreign visitors, grew from KRW 8.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 36.0 billion in 2025, helped by the merchant network.

R&D spending rose 72% to KRW 11.5 billion in 2025, and the company is expanding its payment device lineup, including a generative AI voice-order kiosk (Dealsite).

04

Earnings analysis

On an annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 804.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 891.6 billion in 2023 (up 10.8% year on year), KRW 975.1 billion in 2024 (up 9.4%) and KRW 1.09 trillion in 2025 (up 12.3%), rising for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with 2025 the largest year in the period.

Operating profit fell from KRW 48.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 36.7 billion in 2023 (down 23.6%), then rose to KRW 39.9 billion in 2024 and KRW 55.3 billion in 2025, increasing for two straight years.

The operating margin dropped from 6.0% in 2022 to 4.1% in both 2023 and 2024 and recovered to 5.1% in 2025, so 2023 and 2024 were years when revenue grew while margins compressed.

Attributable net income moved from KRW 40.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 30.3 billion in 2023 (down 25.7%), KRW 38.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 42.9 billion in 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 45.2 billion in 2025, a different figure from attributable net income, and the two should be read separately. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 13.7 billion in 2024 before reaching KRW 163.6 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio declined from 230.7% in 2023 to 173.3% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 437.7 billion, of which the attributable portion was KRW 396 billion, with total liabilities of KRW 758.4 billion.

Quarterly revenue bottomed at KRW 284.2 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and then grew for three consecutive quarters to KRW 285.7 billion, KRW 316.2 billion and KRW 399.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 26.4% from the prior quarter.

Operating profit reached KRW 23 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up 29.3% year on year, but attributable net income was KRW 12.2 billion, down 34.7% from the prior quarter, so the gap between the two needs to be traced in the full-year report.

Attributable net income for the last four quarters (fourth quarter 2025 through third quarter 2026) totaled KRW 54.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Nice Information & Telecommunication's domestic VAN share rose steadily from 17.8% in 2018 to 27.8% in the first quarter of 2023 and 28.3% in the fourth quarter of 2024 according to LS Securities, and FnGuide's company summary presents a 28.6% share. The increase is read as the result of an accumulated merchant base.

A 2023 company analysis flagged that the domestic card payment market is exposed to economic conditions. On the other side, per-transaction fees linked to transaction values were seen as benefiting from consumer price inflation.

In PG, Samsung Securities' March 2026 analysis noted that transaction value growth was only around 1% in the fourth quarter of 2025 and that PG revenue declined.

The rise in foreign tourist visits to Korea is a favorable backdrop for TRS, and LS Securities cited an 18.3% standalone operating margin for a domestic TRS peer in the first half of 2026 to highlight profitability.

The biggest change in competitive positioning is the combined VAN share of 42.7% after the KIS acquisition. LS Securities noted that revenue has not declined over the past ten years.

In cycle terms, the operating margin bottomed at 4.1% in 2023 and recovered to 5.1% in 2025, but it remains below the 6.0% recorded in 2022. The pace of recovery depends on card transaction volumes and PG transaction value growth together.

06

Outlook

Broker estimates diverge. In a report dated August 21, 2026, LS Securities projected that operating profit would grow 30–60% year on year from the third quarter of 2026 through the second quarter of 2027, and estimated 2026 TRS revenue at KRW 42.7 billion.

In its August 21, 2026 report, LS Securities raised its target price from KRW 8,000 to KRW 10,000. Shinhan Investment Corp. estimated in an October 12, 2026 note that 2026 consolidated revenue would reach KRW 1.36 trillion, up 24.3% year on year, and operating profit KRW 78.0 billion, up 41.0%.

The same note expected VAN revenue of KRW 343.7 billion, PG revenue of KRW 744.8 billion, TRS revenue of KRW 45.9 billion and KIS revenue of KRW 127.0 billion. The gap between the two brokers' TRS estimates is about KRW 3.2 billion, which full-year 2026 results will resolve.

On shareholder returns, the company has proposed a standalone payout ratio of at least 25% for 2026–2028, annual increases of at least 10% in dividend per share, and annual cancellation of at least 1% of issued shares.

It also outlined using at least 5% of standalone net income for treasury share cancellation and plans to buy back KRW 3.0 billion of shares. In new business, a proof-of-concept for an LLM-based voice-order kiosk is underway, and a March 2026 articles amendment added digital-asset-related business purposes.

An extraordinary shareholders' meeting on November 27, 2026 will vote on appointing an inside director and a non-standing director. The next checkpoints are how far the operating margin rises above 5.1% in 2025 after the KIS consolidation, and which TRS estimate the full-year figure approaches.

08

Bull factors

VAN leadership and share gains

VAN share rose from 17.8% in 2018 to 28.3% in the fourth quarter of 2024 according to LS Securities, and FnGuide presents 28.6% as industry-leading. The merchant base built up over time is the core of the bullish case.

Combined with KIS, the share was presented at 42.7%, and the VAN business's stability underpins the revenue scale.

TRS growth and KIS integration synergies

TRS revenue rose from KRW 8.2 billion in 2023 to KRW 36.0 billion in 2025, and LS Securities estimated KRW 42.7 billion for 2026. LS Securities projected 30–60% year-on-year operating profit growth from the third quarter of 2026 through the second quarter of 2027, citing cost synergies from the KIS deal. Shinhan Investment Corp. also estimated 2026 operating profit at KRW 78.0 billion, pointing the same way.

Specific shareholder return commitments

The company said it will pursue a standalone payout ratio of at least 25% for 2026–2028 and increase dividend per share by at least 10% each year. It also presented a plan to cancel at least 1% of issued shares annually, and it cancelled 9.0% of shares in June 2026. An additional KRW 3.0 billion treasury share purchase has been disclosed as well.

09

Bear factors

Slowing PG growth and limited profit contribution

PG generated 62.1% of first-quarter 2026 revenue, yet only 3.4% of 2025 operating profit, according to Dealsite. Samsung Securities noted that PG transaction value growth was about 1% in the fourth quarter of 2025 and PG revenue declined.

A business with a large revenue share but low profit contribution is the main bearish point, and slower transaction growth could limit the earnings recovery.

Thin margins and divergent quarterly profit

The operating margin recovered to 5.1% in 2025 but remains below the 6.0% of 2022. Operating profit rose to KRW 23 billion in the third quarter of 2026, while attributable net income fell 34.7% from the prior quarter to KRW 12.2 billion.

When operating profit and net income move in different directions, questions about earnings quality arise, and whether the gap is temporary or structural still needs confirmation.

Uncertainty over the KIS consolidation effect

LS Securities stressed that how much the operating margin improves as fixed-cost ratios fall after the KIS deal will be the key variable. Broker estimates also diverge, with 2026 TRS forecasts of KRW 42.7 billion and KRW 45.9 billion, and Shinhan Investment Corp. expecting KIS revenue of KRW 127.0 billion.

If integration results fall short of expectations on the roughly KRW 112.3 billion acquisition, much of the bullish case would weaken.

10

Risk factors

Shareholder returns vs. investment funding

The company plans a standalone payout ratio of at least 25% and at least 10% annual growth in dividend per share for 2026–2028, and the total dividend approved at the March 2026 shareholders' meeting was about KRW 9.6 billion.

As attributable net income swings sharply from quarter to quarter, keeping this commitment would increase the cash used for dividends and treasury share cancellation. If that cash overlaps with funds needed for KIS integration and new-business R&D, room for investment could be constrained.

Sensitivity to payment volumes and tourism demand

A 2023 analysis flagged that the domestic card payment market is exposed to economic conditions. TRS depends on rising inbound tourism, so a slowdown in visitor demand could slow its growth.

The spread in broker TRS forecasts may partly reflect this demand variable, and the revenue outlook could shift with changes in the external environment.

Investment burden and delayed monetization of new businesses

R&D spending rose from KRW 5.2 billion in 2023 and KRW 6.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 11.5 billion in 2025, and KRW 3.5 billion was invested in the first quarter of 2026 (Dealsite). Dealsite noted that most profit still comes from VAN and that turning new businesses into a separate profit source is the open task. If costs rise before monetization, margin recovery could be constrained.

12

Overall view

Nice Information & Telecommunication holds a leading position in domestic VAN, and operating profit rose for two consecutive years to KRW 55.3 billion in 2025, marking a recovery phase. In the third quarter of 2026, consolidation of KIS lifted revenue sharply to KRW 399.8 billion.

However, attributable net income fell to KRW 12.2 billion in the same quarter, and PG's limited profit contribution relative to its revenue share remains a bearish factor. On the bullish side, TRS revenue growth and a shareholder return plan that includes a payout ratio of at least 25% stand out.

Broker estimates also diverge: LS Securities expects operating profit to grow 30–60% year on year from the third quarter of 2026 through the second quarter of 2027, while Shinhan Investment Corp. projects 2026 operating profit of KRW 78.0 billion.

The stock trades with a price-to-book ratio below 1x and a low price-to-earnings range, but earnings swings are large, so the multiples should be read alongside the quarterly trend.

The next checkpoints are how far the operating margin rises above the 5.1% of 2025 after the KIS consolidation, and whether attributable net income keeps pace with operating profit growth in the annual report around March 2027.
