Ryuk-Il C&S: Restructuring Amid Losses and Listing Pressure

Ryuk-Il C&S is reshaping from tempered glass and fine chemicals toward an edutech-centered structure, while net losses attributable to controlling shareholders total KRW 10.2 billion over the last four quarters and the company remains under management-issue designation with listing requirements still to be met.

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

Ryuk-Il C&S started as a maker of tempered glass for displays, but in 2025 it sold its Vietnam tempered glass production unit, which had accounted for about half of the glass business's sales, for KRW 1.42 billion, giving up much of its manufacturing base.

In 2022 it acquired a fine-chemicals subsidiary for KRW 1.79 billion as a diversification step. That subsidiary makes PVC stabilizers and generated sales of KRW 1.75 billion and operating profit of KRW 240 million in 2025, making it the company's core subsidiary.

Press reports stated that the fine-chemicals segment accounted for KRW 1.12 billion, or 60.3%, of first-half 2026 consolidated sales of KRW 1.86 billion.

In July 2026 the company sold its entire stake in that subsidiary to a special purpose company for KRW 14.5 billion, and its business focus moved to an edutech subsidiary that makes audiovisual teaching equipment and reportedly posted a first-half 2026 operating loss of KRW 900 million.

In October 2026 the company agreed to buy land and buildings in Songdo-dong, Incheon, for KRW 25.0 billion and completed the final payment on November 10, 2026, citing new business development and investment purposes.

Compared with larger listed electronic components makers such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek, the scale and product mix differ widely.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 32.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 29 billion in 2023, an 11.8% decline year on year, then rose to KRW 37.5 billion in 2024 (up 29.4%) and KRW 44.8 billion in 2025 (up 19.4%), growing for two years after the 2023 low. 2025 was the largest year of the 2022–2025 period.

Operating results moved from an operating loss of KRW 2.19 billion in 2022 to operating profit of KRW 914 million in 2023, a swing back to profit, before turning to an operating loss of KRW 476 million in 2024 and widening to KRW 1.51 billion in 2025, two consecutive loss-making years.

Operating margins were -6.7% in 2022, 3.2% in 2023, -1.3% in 2024 and -3.4% in 2025, so revenue growth in 2024 and 2025 did not lift margins into positive territory.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to profit at KRW 1.76 billion in 2025 after a KRW 412 million loss in 2024, while net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.62 billion.

Positive net income alongside an operating loss in 2025 points to non-operating items, whose breakdown is in the annual report notes. Operating cash flow stayed positive every year, at KRW 1.48 billion, KRW 1.98 billion, KRW 617 million and KRW 2.18 billion from 2022 to 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 155.5% in 2024 to 135.9% in 2025. Quarterly revenue went from KRW 9.75 billion in Q4 2025 (up 18.9% from the prior quarter) to KRW 10.8 billion in Q1 2026 (up 10.3%) and KRW 7.79 billion in Q2 2026 (down 27.6%).

Operating profit of KRW 514 million in Q1 2026 gave way to an operating loss of KRW 482 million in Q2 and KRW 3.05 billion in Q3 2026, a wider loss than in Q3 2025.

Controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 13.1 billion in Q3 2025 was followed by a KRW 9.56 billion loss in Q4 2025, KRW 589 million of profit in Q1 2026, a KRW 728 million loss in Q2 and a KRW 548 million loss in Q3 2026, which turned from profit a year earlier.

The Q4 2025 net loss was far larger than its operating loss of KRW 454 million, pointing to non-operating items. Controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 10.2 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Historically, tempered glass was the core of Ryuk-Il C&S, but after selling its Vietnam production unit in 2025, the weight of its manufacturing business shrank considerably.

The company explained that the chemicals sector has a strong sunset-industry character, citing it as the reason for selling the fine-chemicals business.

A January 2026 press report noted that the stock diverged from a weak display sector on a political-theme rally and expectations for business restructuring, and interpreted the move as a short-term supply-and-demand overheating.

The edutech business is being reshaped around audiovisual teaching equipment and posted an operating loss in the first half of 2026, so its earnings base is not yet stable. Press coverage raised concerns that the proposed robotics, AI and real estate development businesses have limited connection to the core business.

Compared with larger listed peers in the same electronic components category, such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek, the gap in scale and product mix is wide, so company-specific restructuring factors rather than industry cycle positioning drive earnings. Within its industry, the company is best described as a business-transforming firm.

Outlook

At the December 16, 2026 extraordinary shareholders' meeting, five business purposes are on the agenda, including robotics system and AI-related industries and real estate development, investment and trading.

At the October 1, 2026 extraordinary meeting, 25 business purposes were added to the articles of incorporation, and three inside directors, one independent director and one non-standing auditor were elected for three-year terms.

A company official said the specific direction of the biotech-related business restructuring would become clearer after the extraordinary meeting and the completion of management changes.

The company plans to use the KRW 14.5 billion from the fine-chemicals stake sale for new business investment, but the specific investment targets and business plans have not been disclosed.

From October 1 to October 30, 2026, the company bought 587,935 treasury shares for KRW 1.0 billion over 18 trading days, and as of November 2, 2026 it held 1,922,751 treasury shares, or 10.89%.

The Songdo property was described as acquired for new business and investment purposes, and the company stated it has no plans for corporate restructuring.

Under the management-issue rules, the company must keep market capitalization at KRW 20 billion or more for 45 consecutive trading days within a 90-trading-day period, while delisting procedures are suspended after a court accepted an injunction.

In the 2026 annual report, the next regular filing, the annual operating results and how the Songdo property transaction is reflected in the financial statements will be the key checks. Whether the operating margin moves from -3.4% in 2025 into positive territory will be a central gauge of the restructuring's results.

08

Bull factors

Operating cash generation held up

Operating cash flow was positive every year from 2022 to 2025, and the KRW 2.18 billion in 2025 was the largest amount in that period. Cash inflows continued in 2024 and 2025, when operating losses were recorded. Proponents cite this as evidence that the core business's cash base has not fully collapsed.

Easing financial burden

Total equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 23.7 billion against total liabilities of KRW 32.3 billion. The July 2026 sale of the fine-chemicals stake brought in KRW 14.5 billion, reducing reliance on borrowing.

Wider range of business options

Twenty-five business purposes were added at the October 2026 extraordinary meeting, and five more are on the December 2026 agenda, including robotics, AI-related and real estate development businesses. The sale proceeds and the Songdo property are described as funding for new business investment.

With reliance on the legacy manufacturing and chemicals businesses lower, the room to rebuild the portfolio has widened.

Bear factors

Recurring core-business losses

Operating losses continued in 2024 and 2025 and have now extended into two consecutive loss-making quarters from Q2 2026. The KRW 3.05 billion operating loss in Q3 2026 was the largest loss among the last five quarters. The operating margin remained negative at -3.4% in 2025.

Shrinking revenue base

Press reports said the sales of the Vietnam unit and the fine-chemicals subsidiary removed more than half of consolidated revenue. Fine chemicals accounted for KRW 1.12 billion, or 60.3%, of first-half 2026 consolidated sales of KRW 1.86 billion.

Analysts cited in press reports argued that the remaining edutech subsidiary, which reportedly posted a KRW 900 million operating loss in the first half of 2026, makes continued core-business losses likely.

Listing maintenance and trading volatility

Market capitalization fell below the KRW 20 billion listing threshold, leading to a management-issue designation in July 2026. In January 2026, short-term trading overheated on a combination of a political theme and restructuring expectations.

The share price has previously moved more with thematic money than with earnings, so volatility driven by trading flows could remain large.

Risk factors

Listing maintenance requirements

If market capitalization does not stay at KRW 20 billion or more for 45 consecutive trading days within 90 trading days after the designation, delisting procedures can proceed. The injunction suspended these procedures, but a change in the legal status would bring the compliance question back.

Because market capitalization moves with the share price and trading flows, the distance from the threshold is the key variable.

Financial and liquidity pressure

The KRW 25.0 billion Songdo transaction closed with the final payment on November 10, 2026, and total liabilities increased as a result. Controlling-shareholder net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 10.2 billion.

Quarterly net results have swung sharply on non-operating items, so cash capacity and borrowing burden remain key concerns.

New-business and governance uncertainty

The board was largely replaced in October 2026, and an individual tied to a KONEX-listed biotech company was nominated as an inside director. A Seoul Economic TV report raised concern that a wave of business additions unrelated to the core business might be aimed at supporting the share price. Until specific plans are disclosed, uncertainty over business direction and use of funds remains.

Overall view

Ryuk-Il C&S's revenue rose for two straight years to KRW 44.8 billion in 2025, yet it still posted an operating loss of KRW 1.51 billion, and the Q3 2026 operating loss widened to KRW 3.05 billion.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders turned to profit at KRW 1.76 billion in 2025, but controlling-shareholder net losses total KRW 10.2 billion over the last four quarters.

After selling the Vietnam glass unit and the fine-chemicals subsidiary, the revenue structure has shifted toward edutech, with more capital and cash but a smaller operating base.

The Songdo property purchase and added business purposes read as signs of a change in direction, but specific business plans have not been disclosed and no results tied to them have appeared yet.

The listing-maintenance question under the management-issue designation and the injunction is a separate variable that determines listing status regardless of business assessment.

Factors on the positive side include continued positive operating cash flow, a lower debt ratio and inflows from asset sales, while factors on the negative side include consecutive losses, a shrinking revenue base and uncertainty over new businesses.

The next checkpoints are whether market capitalization meets the threshold during the review period, the outcome of the December 16, 2026 extraordinary meeting, and the annual operating results in the 2026 annual report due by March 31, 2027.
