KC: Quarterly profit recovery, construction-heavy mix

KC's operating profit has risen for four consecutive quarters through the third quarter of 2026, but net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, and that decline has not yet reversed on an annual basis.

  1. 1

    Operating profit in 2025 rose 66.1% year on year to KRW 46.5 billion, recovering from the 2024 low of KRW 28 billion.

  3. 3

  4. 4

02

Business structure

KC is a Korean company that makes gas and chemical supply equipment used in semiconductor and display manufacturing, and it operates a consolidated structure that pairs facility construction with gas-related subsidiaries.

In the December 2024 fiscal year, the revenue mix was construction 49.59%, equipment 27.71%, materials and parts 21.21% and other 2.59% according to FnGuide.

Based on second-quarter 2026 revenue, the mix was construction 46%, equipment 30%, materials and parts 22% and other 2% according to Butler, so the equipment share is slightly higher than in 2024. Construction is handled mainly by a subsidiary that designs and builds industrial facilities.

The equipment segment centers on gas and chemical supply systems, and a subsidiary makes gas scrubbers (Gas Scrubber). The materials and parts line links to a subsidiary that handles specialty gas manufacturing and sales as well as gas supply and trading.

Another subsidiary makes semiconductor manufacturing machinery, and a further one makes machinery and parts for display manufacturing, so the product range spans equipment-centered lines. Among Korean semiconductor equipment and materials peers, Wonik IPS, PSK and Soulbrain are the usual comparison points. The customer-by-customer revenue split is best checked in the major customer section of the business report.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 858 billion in 2022 to KRW 727 billion in 2023, down 15.3% year on year, then rose to KRW 738.5 billion in 2024 (up 1.6%) and KRW 797.3 billion in 2025 (up 8.0%), with growth in both 2024 and 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2022 was the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 101.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 66.4 billion in 2023 (down 34.4%) and KRW 28 billion in 2024 (down 57.9%), then recovered to KRW 46.5 billion in 2025 (up 66.1%).

The operating margin was 11.8% in 2022, 9.1% in 2023, 3.8% in 2024 and 5.8% in 2025, so 2024 was the low point and 2025 moved up one step.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell from KRW 99.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 47.5 billion in 2023 (down 52.2%), KRW 46.5 billion in 2024 (down 2.2%) and KRW 43.9 billion in 2025 (down 5.6%), a decline for three straight years from 2023 to 2025.

In 2025 operating profit rose while controlling-shareholder profit fell, so the operating line and the bottom line moved in different directions.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 50.4 billion in 2025, above the KRW 43.9 billion attributable to controlling shareholders, and the gap reflects the non-controlling share.

By quarter, operating profit went from KRW 3.56 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 (up 30.6% from the prior quarter) to KRW 14 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 292.7%), KRW 22.2 billion in the second quarter (up 58.7%) and KRW 25.5 billion in the third quarter (up 14.8%).

However, first-quarter 2026 operating profit was down 45.3% from KRW 25.5 billion a year earlier according to FnGuide, so the sequential recovery and the year-on-year decline fall in the same quarter.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders rose from KRW 3.37 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 22.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and then held near that level at KRW 22.1 billion in the second quarter and KRW 22.6 billion in the third quarter.

The sum of controlling-shareholder net profit over the latest four quarters is KRW 70.5 billion. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 108 billion in 2022 to KRW 36.8 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 51 billion in 2025.

05

Industry analysis

Gas and chemical supply equipment and facility construction are utility systems that a semiconductor fab needs in order to run, so demand moves with new fab construction and line expansion. KC's order flow is therefore tied directly to the direction of capital spending (CAPEX) at memory and foundry customers.

In phases when investment concentrates, construction and equipment volumes tend to rise together, and when investment slows, construction volumes tend to fall first.

The move from a 3.8% operating margin in 2024 to 5.8% in 2025, with quarterly operating profit reaching KRW 25.5 billion in the third quarter of 2026, can be read as consistent with this demand recovery. Where the company sits in the cycle is best checked through customers' capex announcements and equipment order flow.

On competition, supplying gas and chemicals depends on design and construction experience and safety management, which is generally seen as a moderately high entry barrier. Domestic comparison names Wonik IPS, PSK and Soulbrain focus on deposition or etch equipment and materials, so their product mix differs from KC's.

The materials and parts line is tied to demand for consumable materials such as specialty gases and moves with a lag relative to equipment investment. If display investment diverges from memory investment, segment results could diverge as well, which makes the equipment segment's share a key variable. Overseas revenue share is best checked in the regional disclosures of the business report.

Sequential operating profit growth also narrowed, from 292.7% in the first quarter of 2026 to 58.7% in the second quarter and 14.8% in the third. The first quarter of 2026, however, started with revenue down 10.2% and operating profit down 45.3% year on year according to FnGuide.

Operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 alone already exceeds the full-year 2025 operating profit. Changes in the contribution from each subsidiary can be checked in the consolidated-entity information of the business report. The first-quarter 2027 quarterly report is due by mid-May 2027 and will be compared with first-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 14 billion.

08

Bull factors

Continuity of quarterly profit recovery

Operating profit has risen for four consecutive quarters from the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, and third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 25.5 billion is close to the KRW 28 billion posted for all of 2024. Third-quarter operating profit was up 833.7% year on year.

The operating margin rose from 3.8% in 2024 to 5.8% in 2025, which is read as a sign of margin recovery. The bullish view holds that if this pattern continues, it would support a broader recovery in annual earnings.

Expansion of the revenue base

Third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 251.5 billion, up 43.7% from KRW 175 billion a year earlier. Cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 rose 15.0% year on year, which indicates a broader revenue base. Annual revenue in 2025 grew 8.0% to KRW 797.3 billion, extending the growth that began in 2024. The bullish view argues that a larger revenue base improves the capacity to respond to new orders.

Financial headroom

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 184.3 billion, total equity was KRW 968.7 billion and the debt ratio was 19.0%. The debt ratio declined steadily from 27.0% in 2022 to 22.8% in 2023, 22.1% in 2024 and 19.0% in 2025.

The bullish view holds that this balance sheet headroom gives the company room to respond during periods of expanded capital spending by customers. How it is deployed in investment and shareholder returns is best checked in the business report.

Bear factors

Three straight years of lower controlling-shareholder profit

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 99.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 47.5 billion in 2023, KRW 46.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 43.9 billion in 2025, a decline for three straight years from 2023 to 2025.

Operating profit rose 66.1% in 2025, yet controlling-shareholder net profit fell 5.6%, so the operating recovery did not carry through to the bottom line. First-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 14 billion, down 45.3% year on year. The bearish view treats this weak link as the central weakness.

Weak link in cash flow

Operating cash flow fell from KRW 108 billion in 2022 to KRW 36.8 billion in 2024 and recovered to KRW 51 billion in 2025. Operating cash flow was negative KRW 2.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026 according to FnGuide.

The bearish view points out that when working capital ties up cash, profit gains may not translate into cash generation. Whether quarterly operating cash flow turns positive again is the test of this argument.

Slowing quarterly revenue growth and business mix

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 251.5 billion rose only 0.4% from the prior quarter, a sharp slowdown from the 17.8% increase in the second quarter.

Construction accounted for the largest share at 46% of second-quarter 2026 revenue according to Butler, so swings in customer capital spending could pass through to construction volumes. The bearish view holds that this structure increases revenue volatility. Whether the construction share declines is a point to check in the business report.

Risk factors

Industry and capex cycle

KC's construction and equipment volumes move with semiconductor capital spending. If investment announcements slow or customers adjust their operating plans, quarterly revenue growth could stall. Third-quarter 2026 revenue growth slowed to 0.4% sequentially, which shows this sensitivity in the reported numbers.

Volatility of quarterly profit

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 47.8% sequentially to KRW 3.37 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and then rose 566.6% to KRW 22.5 billion in the first quarter of 2026. Because quarterly swings are this large, one or two quarters are not enough to judge the trend.

The volatility may depend on cost structure and the timing of project recognition, so the quarterly sequence needs to be tracked continuously.

Cash generation and financial management

Negative operating cash flow of KRW 2.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026 shows a timing gap between profit and cash. Annual operating cash flow fell well below the KRW 108 billion recorded in 2022 to KRW 36.8 billion in 2024.

It recovered to KRW 51 billion in 2025, but whether this matches the pace of profit recovery will become clearer as quarterly cash flows accumulate.

Overall view

KC's profit has recovered in 2025 and 2026 from a 3.8% operating margin low in 2024.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue reached KRW 251.5 billion and operating profit KRW 25.5 billion, with quarterly figures rising in succession, and cumulative revenue for the first three quarters of 2026 was up 15.0% year on year according to a Digital Today report.

On the other hand, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, and whether the operating recovery carries through to the bottom line will become visible in the 2026 annual business report.

The first quarter of 2026 started with revenue down 10.2% and operating profit down 45.3% year on year, and operating cash flow was negative.

Bullish factors are the consecutive quarterly profit recovery and a debt ratio of 19.0%, while bearish factors are the decline in controlling-shareholder profit, quarterly cash flow and earnings volatility tied to a construction-heavy mix.

The key question for valuation is how the price-to-book level below 1x interacts with the speed of earnings recovery. The next checkpoints are the provisional fourth-quarter 2026 results, the segment and cash flow data in the 2026 annual business report, and the first-quarter 2027 quarterly report.
