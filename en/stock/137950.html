02

Business structure

JC Chemical, which began its biodiesel business in 2006, is a biofuel specialist listed on KOSDAQ since 2011. The business is split into a domestic biofuel segment and an Indonesian palm plantation segment.

As of the first half of 2024, sales mix by segment was 83% biofuel and 17% palm plantation (Hana Securities report, September 2024). The biofuel segment produces biodiesel, bio-heavy oil and bio-marine fuel, and has added PTU, a feedstock for sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), to widen its product range.

The palm segment acquired local plantation companies in Indonesia in 2012 and 2013 and added the PT.PPP farm in 2024, strengthening its raw material base. Its customers are mainly refiners such as S-Oil and SK Energy, and the company supplies feedstock it produces directly.

In the first half of 2026, biofuel segment operating profit was KRW 3.7 billion and palm plantation operating profit was KRW 9.3 billion, so palm accounted for 72% of group operating profit (Kyobo Securities report, reported September 2026).

Green Economy, a Korean daily, described the chain from palm feedstock to biofuel production as unique among domestic biofuel companies. Intensifying competition in bio-heavy oil remains a variable for the domestic business.

04

Earnings analysis

On a DART-filed annual basis, sales fell from KRW 511.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 439.2 billion in 2023 (down 14.2% year on year) and KRW 367.3 billion in 2024 (down 16.4%), then were flat at KRW 367.5 billion in 2025 (up 0.1%). The largest sales year in 2022 to 2025 was 2022, and the smallest was 2024.

Operating profit went from KRW 42.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 32.3 billion in 2023 (down 24.3%), KRW 10.7 billion in 2024 (down 66.9%) and KRW 7.72 billion in 2025 (down 27.8%), declining for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025. The operating margin narrowed from 8.3% in 2022 to 7.4%, 2.9% and 2.1% in 2025.

A 2024 securities report attributed the decline partly to intensifying bio-heavy oil competition and a narrower spread as soybean prices fell.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 20.4 billion in 2022 and KRW 20.8 billion in 2023 (up 1.8%) to KRW 6.07 billion in 2024 (down 70.8%), and turned into a net loss of KRW 3.96 billion in 2025. Net income including non-controlling interests was a net loss of KRW 5.15 billion in 2025.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 22.5 billion in 2022 and KRW 46.8 billion in 2023 to KRW 13.6 billion in 2024, then to -KRW 41.4 billion in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, sales went from KRW 110.8 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 86.2 billion in the fourth quarter (down 22.2% from the prior quarter), then to KRW 121.2 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (up 40.5%), KRW 142.8 billion in the second quarter (up 17.9%) and KRW 186.2 billion in the third quarter (up 30.4%), rising for three consecutive quarters.

Operating profit moved from an operating loss of KRW 379 million in the fourth quarter of 2025 (a swing from profit to loss versus the prior quarter) to KRW 1.59 billion in the first quarter of 2026 (a swing back to profit), KRW 11.5 billion in the second quarter (up 622.0% from the prior quarter) and KRW 9.89 billion in the third quarter (down 13.7%).

Because third-quarter operating profit was below the second quarter while sales rose, the operating margin also fell from the second quarter.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 3.12 billion in the third quarter of 2026, down 64.4% from the prior quarter, while the sum over the latest four quarters is KRW 10.1 billion.

The debt ratio rose from 115.2% in 2022 and 86.7% in 2023 to 109.3% in 2024 and 164.7% in 2025, when total liabilities were KRW 237.9 billion against total equity of KRW 144.4 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Biofuel demand is largely shaped by policy. In Korea, the biodiesel blend mandate for diesel was raised from 3.0% to 3.5% in July 2021, widening the demand base. A research analysis reported at the end of 2023 noted plans to introduce bio-marine fuel and bio-aviation fuel in Korea in 2025 and 2026.

Indonesia decided to bring forward its B50 policy, which raises the palm oil share in biodiesel to 50%, and a Korean daily estimated that annual local palm oil demand could rise about 28.6% once the policy takes full effect.

Some market commentary holds that domestic biofuel companies with their own palm plantations could benefit directly from higher palm oil prices. A 2024 securities report cited the spread of aviation fuel blending mandates as the backdrop for the SAF feedstock business.

Compared with large petrochemical or battery-materials companies such as LG Chem and Lotte Chemical, JC Chemical is a small specialist that links palm farms and biofuel production in one chain, so it is more exposed to policy and raw material prices.

Its customers are concentrated among refiners, which is another feature of its sales structure. Kyobo Securities cited the product range, spanning biodiesel, bio-marine fuel and PTU, as a strength that allows sales channels to diversify.

On the industry side, the actual start date of B50 and the path of palm oil prices are the next points to watch.

06

Outlook

The company disclosed a KRW 15.8 billion investment in pretreatment equipment at its Onsan plant, with about 12 months of construction and a target of commercial operation in March 2027.

The company said pretreatment feedstock capacity has been expanded from 60,000 tons to 120,000 tons a year, and first-half 2026 PTU sales reached 35,000 tons, up 21,000 tons from a year earlier.

The company said the Onsan upgrade would raise the use of lower-cost feedstock and that it would pursue entry into next-generation biofuel markets such as SAF and HVO. It has signed a technology license and basic design agreement for HVO and SAF with Topsoe, targeting commercial production around 2030.

The SAF finished-product business, however, is still before an investment decision. In palm, the company operates existing farms of about 7.3 thousand hectares and added the PT.PPP farm in 2024, and it plans to expand to 15,000 hectares within five years.

The company also said it would pursue higher productivity at its Indonesian plantations. Kyobo Securities viewed PTU exports as raising earnings visibility.

The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 preliminary results, the cash flow and debt figures in the 2026 annual report, and whether the Onsan equipment starts commercial operation in March 2027.

08

Bull factors

Palm plantation contribution is growing

Palm farms in Indonesia have entered their peak production period, and plantation operating profit is rising, which is cited as a strength.

According to a Korean daily, the local palm company posted sales of KRW 22.4 billion, operating profit of KRW 2.8 billion and an operating margin of 12.5% in the first quarter of 2026. Indonesia's early adoption of the B50 policy is also cited as a favorable factor for palm demand.

Domestic biofuel profit improves

The company said domestic biofuel operating profit rose about 276% year on year, driven by higher biodiesel exports, PTU sales growth and the use of lower-cost feedstock.

First-half 2026 PTU sales reached 35,000 tons, up 21,000 tons from a year earlier, and PTU export sales of KRW 104.2 billion already exceeded the full-year 2025 export sales of KRW 32.6 billion. Kyobo Securities viewed this as a factor raising earnings visibility.

Next-generation fuel feedstock business expanding

The company disclosed a KRW 15.8 billion investment in pretreatment equipment at Onsan, with about 12 months of construction and a target of commercial operation in March 2027. It has signed a technology license and basic design agreement for HVO and SAF with Topsoe, targeting commercial production around 2030. Securities analysts view the business as a mid- to long-term growth option.

09

Bear factors

Third-quarter operating profit fell from the prior quarter

Third-quarter 2026 sales rose to KRW 186.2 billion, but operating profit fell 13.7% from the prior quarter to KRW 9.89 billion. It was below the second quarter's KRW 11.5 billion, so profitability relative to sales declined.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders also dropped 64.4% from the prior quarter to KRW 3.12 billion.

Balance sheet and cash flow pressure

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 237.9 billion, exceeding total equity of KRW 144.4 billion, and the 164.7% debt ratio is the highest of 2022 to 2025. Operating cash flow turned into an outflow of KRW 41.4 billion in 2025.

The 2025 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 3.96 billion also shows weaker earnings capacity.

Profit structure leaning on policy and feedstock prices

Palm plantations and biofuels form the core of profit, so earnings can swing sharply with palm oil prices, Indonesian policy and feedstock procurement terms. As of the first half of 2024, the sales mix was 83% biofuel and 17% palm plantation. Annual operating profit of KRW 7.72 billion in 2025 was the smallest of 2022 to 2025.

Risk factors

Financial leverage

With the debt ratio up to 164.7%, borrowing costs could rise if equipment investment and farm development overlap. Operating cash flow of -KRW 41.4 billion in 2025 points in the same direction. The funding flow for the Onsan equipment and farm development is the next point to check.

Policy and feedstock prices

The start date of Indonesia's B50 policy and palm oil prices are variables the company cannot control. Kyobo Securities cited diversified sales channels as a strength, but margins could come under pressure if feedstock costs and product prices diverge. Intensifying bio-heavy oil competition was also named as a profit drag in a 2024 report.

Concentration and construction execution

The SAF finished-product business is still before its investment decision, and the Onsan pretreatment equipment targets commercial operation in March 2027 after about 12 months of construction. Construction delays or changes in feedstock quality would leave uncertainty in the 2027 earnings outlook.

Because customers are mainly refiners, the business is also sensitive to demand changes from specific customers.

12

Overall view

JC Chemical's annual operating profit declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and it swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in 2025, but sales and operating profit have recovered from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026.

The recovery rests on two segments, the Indonesian palm plantation and domestic biofuels including PTU and biodiesel, and palm accounted for 72% of first-half 2026 operating profit. Strengths include three consecutive quarters of sales growth, PTU capacity of 120,000 tons a year and plans to expand palm farms.

Weaknesses include the 164.7% debt ratio at end-2025, operating cash flow of -KRW 41.4 billion in 2025, the quarterly decline in third-quarter operating profit, and dependence on policy and feedstock prices.

The next checkpoints are whether the Onsan pretreatment equipment starts commercial operation in March 2027, the cash flow and debt figures in the 2026 annual report, and first-quarter 2027 results.

The SAF finished-product business, targeting commercial output around 2030, is still before its investment decision and is viewed as a mid- to long-term option.
