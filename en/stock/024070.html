02

Business structure

Wiscom was founded in May 1978 and makes and sells plastic products, with PVC compounds at the core. In an Alphasquare breakdown, PVC compound products account for 68.76% of sales, colored processing contract work for 23.06%, PVC compounds sold in China for 8.09%, and pigments and other trading goods for 0.11%.

The company profile describes a focus on high-value compounds for electric cable and a plan to expand compound sales to overseas cable makers. Its two main subsidiaries are Wuxi and Jiangmen-based entities, which are named in Korean filings. ABS compounds follow PVC compounds as the next-largest product line.

The PVC compound industry requires heavy investment per facility, depends on compounding know-how that blends plasticizers and stabilizers, and runs through a distribution chain more complex than other synthetic resins.

A 2017 record cited the company as a leading PVC compound maker with about a 20.7% market share, and no later share figure was confirmed. Customers include plastic processors and cable makers. Profit contribution by segment was not confirmed in this review.

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 125.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 112.3 billion in 2023, a 10.6% decline, and then to KRW 98.5 billion in 2024, a 12.3% decline, before rebounding to KRW 103.3 billion in 2025, up 4.8%. Among the years from 2022 through 2025, 2022 was the largest and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit of KRW 744 million in 2022 turned into an operating loss of KRW 2.85 billion in 2023, and the loss widened to KRW 8.86 billion in 2024. The 2025 operating loss narrowed to KRW 698 million, and the operating margin was -0.7%.

The operating margin moved from 0.6% in 2022 to -2.5% in 2023, -9.0% in 2024 and -0.7% in 2025. Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 7.43 billion in 2022, KRW 476 million in 2023 and KRW 23.9 billion in 2024 before turning into a KRW 1.34 billion profit in 2025.

Net profit including noncontrolling interests was also KRW 1.34 billion in 2025, and the two figures matched. Operating cash flow was KRW 2.59 billion in 2022, KRW 5.85 billion in 2023, KRW 2.69 billion in 2024 and KRW 975 million in 2025, a declining trend since 2023.

On a quarterly basis, an operating loss of KRW 728 million in the third quarter of 2025 gave way to operating profit of KRW 1.1 billion in the fourth quarter, which turned positive, and the first quarter of 2026 returned to an operating loss of KRW 1.23 billion.

Operating profit was KRW 924 million in the second quarter of 2026 and fell to KRW 373 million in the third quarter, down 59.6% from the prior quarter. Third-quarter revenue of KRW 29.2 billion rose 7.7% from a year earlier, but operating profit only moved from a year-earlier loss to a profit.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.38 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025 and KRW 2.56 billion in the second quarter of 2026, while the third-quarter 2026 net loss of KRW 568 million widened from a year earlier. Downstream demand comes from electric cable and plastic processing, and with colored processing contract work at 23.06% of sales, changes in processing demand also flow into results. Pricing or supply statistics that would show where the cycle stands were not found.

Overall view
