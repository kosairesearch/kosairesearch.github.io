02

Business structure

GST, founded in 2001 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2006, makes process equipment for semiconductor and display fabs. Its core products are scrubbers, which treat harmful gases generated in production, and chillers, which hold process equipment at stable temperatures, and scrubbers account for the largest share of revenue.

The company has grown by riding fab capex increases at domestic and overseas chipmakers while diversifying customers and regions. Its customer base reportedly includes Samsung Electronics, Samsung Display, Micron, YMTC and CXMT.

A subsidiary, EST, produces semiconductor equipment parts, supporting vertical integration of key components. Overseas, GST operates subsidiaries in the United States, China, Taiwan and Singapore, and added European and Japanese units in 2024.

Maintenance parts and technical support after equipment sales generate recurring service revenue. Its new business, liquid immersion cooling for data centers, began operating in July 2026 when GST installed an AI server carrying NVIDIA H200 GPUs and ran its self-developed 6U immersion cooling unit on it.

GST is also pursuing demonstrations with S-OIL and Sungkyunkwan University's supercomputing center in high-heat GPU server environments. In equipment markets, once a supplier enters a fab's supply chain it is hard to replace, so share within existing customers is seen as the key to competition.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, revenue went from KRW 312.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 279.2 billion in 2023 (down 10.8% year on year), then rose to KRW 346.2 billion in 2024 (up 24.0%) and KRW 347.2 billion in 2025 (up 0.3%), increasing for two straight years from 2024 through 2025. 2025 was the largest year of 2022 to 2025 for revenue, but growth of 0.3% means the expansion was concentrated in 2024.

Operating profit was KRW 56.9 billion in 2022, KRW 42.5 billion in 2023 (down 25.3%), KRW 59.1 billion in 2024 (up 38.9%) and KRW 59.2 billion in 2025 (up 0.2%), with 2025 the largest year and 2023 the smallest.

Operating margin fell from 18.2% in 2022 to 15.2% in 2023 and recovered to 17.1% in 2024 and 17.0% in 2025, holding near 17% while revenue grew.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 46.8 billion in 2022, KRW 35.7 billion in 2023 (down 23.8%), KRW 45.4 billion in 2024 (up 27.2%) and KRW 45.3 billion in 2025 (down 0.2%), a flat stretch after 2024.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 45.6 billion in 2025, a different figure from profit attributable to controlling shareholders. Operating cash flow was KRW 31.5 billion in 2022, KRW 47.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 54.4 billion in 2024 before falling to KRW 41.6 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio (total liabilities divided by total equity) moved from 25.1% in 2022 to 20.0% in 2023, 24.8% in 2024 and 26.8% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total equity was KRW 304.7 billion, of which KRW 301.2 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, with total liabilities of KRW 81.7 billion.

By quarter, revenue was KRW 80.8 billion in Q3 2025, KRW 92.2 billion in Q4 2025 (up 14.1% from the prior quarter) and KRW 115.9 billion in Q1 2026 (up 25.7%), then eased to KRW 99.2 billion in Q2 2026 (down 14.4%).

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 137.9 billion rose 39.0% from the prior quarter and 70.7% from a year earlier, operating profit of KRW 27 billion rose 61.8% year on year, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 19.8 billion rose 46.9% year on year.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026) totals KRW 61.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for scrubbers and chillers tracks semiconductor capex directly.

WiseReport attributed the sharp improvement in first-half 2026 results to rising scrubber and chiller demand as AI data center and HBM demand drove capex higher. iM Securities said in an April 13, 2026 report that capex plans at major memory customers had been raised and were accelerating.

The same report tied the outlook to capex expansion at memory customers including Samsung Electronics and Micron. In terms of cycle position, the company sits in a phase where new fab investment and process expansion are turning into equipment orders.

Environmental regulation is another demand driver: an analyst cited tighter rules on fluorocarbon refrigerants as lifting demand for eco-friendly equipment such as CO2 chillers. In foundry, news in late 2025 reported that GST passed a TSMC process test, which was read as a boost to technology credibility.

The scrubber and chiller markets are contested by domestic and global equipment makers, so technical validation and customer references are central to winning orders.

Liquid immersion cooling is a new market linked to data center cooling demand but is still at the demonstration stage, so its revenue contribution is likely small compared with the chiller business.

At the industry level, memory investment is expanding, while China-related investment volatility and export controls remain variables.

Bull factors

Direct exposure to the capex cycle

Analysts say demand for scrubbers and chillers is rising with AI data center and HBM investment. A corporate data provider reported first-half 2026 revenue up 26.7% and operating profit up 27.3% year on year. Capex expansion at memory customers was cited as the basis for the 2026 outlook.

High operating margin and cash generation

Operating margin held at 17.1% in 2024 and 17.0% in 2025. Operating cash flow was KRW 41.6 billion in 2025. The debt ratio stood at 26.8% at the end of 2025.

Chiller mix upgrade and new business options

An analyst cited tighter fluorocarbon refrigerant rules as lifting demand for eco-friendly chillers such as CO2 units. Liquid immersion cooling demonstrations have been running since the H200-equipped server was introduced in July 2026. If these translate into orders, they could add revenue outside the core equipment business.

Bear factors

Flat growth and earnings in 2025

In 2025, revenue grew only 0.3%, operating profit 0.2%, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 0.2%. Operating cash flow dropped from KRW 54.4 billion in 2024 to KRW 41.6 billion in 2025. Growth slowed after the sharp increase in 2024.

Quarterly volatility

Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 99.2 billion fell 14.4% from the prior quarter, and operating profit of KRW 15.5 billion fell 23.4%. After sharp gains in Q1 and Q3 2026, quarterly swings remain large. Whether the Q3 jump continues into later quarters is not yet established.

Customer concentration and new business uncertainty

Mass production orders for electric chillers from TSMC were not yet confirmed as of an April 2026 securities report. Liquid immersion cooling is still in demonstration, so its revenue contribution is likely small. The business is directly exposed to order timing if memory customers change their investment plans.

10

Risk factors

Capex cycle

Demand for scrubbers and chillers can shift quickly with semiconductor capex. If memory customers adjust CAPEX, order timing and size could move with it. The large quarterly swings in 2026 can be read partly as reflecting order timing.

Customer and regional concentration

Key customers include Samsung Electronics, Micron and Chinese chipmakers, so the company is sensitive to investment changes at a few large customers. With subsidiaries in several countries, exchange rates and export controls can affect results. Volatility in Chinese chip investment is a particularly important variable given its China operations.

New business demonstration and profitability

Liquid immersion cooling is in demonstration with S-OIL and Sungkyunkwan University and is not yet commercialized. Demonstration costs and staffing could weigh on core margins. Operating margin fell to 15.6% in Q2 2026 from 17.4% in Q1.

12

Overall view

GST makes scrubbers and chillers for semiconductor process equipment. On a disclosed basis, revenue and operating profit have stayed in roughly the KRW 340 billion to 350 billion and KRW 59 billion ranges since 2024.

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 137.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 27 billion were the largest of any disclosed quarter. Securities firms and corporate data providers see AI data center and memory investment as lifting equipment demand.

On the other side, 2025 revenue growth was only 0.3%, and quarterly results fell back in Q2 2026. The liquid immersion cooling business and the TSMC chiller order are not yet confirmed, so their contribution remains to be verified.

With market capitalization well above total equity, the share price reflects expectations for earnings durability. The next checkpoints are the preliminary Q4 2026 results and the 2026 annual report, which will show whether quarterly volatility carries into the full-year trend.
