02

Business structure

SM C&C operates three segments: advertising, entertainment (management and content production), and travel. The advertising segment is an agency business covering ad strategy, production and media buying, and it is described as the core revenue source.

According to a Wisereport breakdown of revenue as of December 2025, advertising agency revenue was 93.85 percent domestic and 6.15 percent export.

Management and content production was 96.97 percent domestic and 3.03 percent export, and travel products such as air tickets were 100 percent domestic, so all three segments are above 90 percent domestic.

Advertising revenue in the first half of 2025 was KRW 21.3 billion, down 36 percent year on year, and press reports attributed the later recovery to an improving ad market and PT-won projects entering execution.

The entertainment segment expanded into management with exclusive contracts signed with broadcasters and actors in April and September 2025. The company is a co-producer of the SBS audition program Our Ballad, now in its second season, and it manages the artists discovered through the show.

In October 2026 the company announced an MOU with Netflix for the K-pop audition project 'We Go High', in which it will handle trainee development and management of the debut group.

Competitors include Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide in advertising, and Hybe, SM Entertainment, JYP Entertainment and YG Entertainment in music management. SM C&C is an affiliate of SM Entertainment within the group.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 157.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 127.3 billion in 2023 (down 19.4% year over year), KRW 109.9 billion in 2024 (down 13.6%), and KRW 100.5 billion in 2025 (down 8.6%).

Revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and across 2022-2025 the largest year was 2022 and the smallest was 2025. Operating profit rose from KRW 2.0 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.09 billion in 2023 (up 4.0% year over year), then fell to KRW 1.59 billion in 2024 (down 24.0%).

In 2025, the company swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.29 billion from the previous year's profit. The operating margin declined from 1.3% in 2022, 1.6% in 2023, and 1.4% in 2024 to -1.3% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.36 billion in 2022, but the company swung to a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 9.84 billion in 2023. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 14.1 billion in 2024 and narrowed to KRW 2.08 billion in 2025.

The company has posted losses for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025.

Net income including non-controlling interests moved in the same direction, from a profit of KRW 5.33 billion in 2022 to a net loss of KRW 9.92 billion in 2023, a net loss of KRW 14.2 billion in 2024, and a net loss of KRW 1.93 billion in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 81.8 billion, of which equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 80.9 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 124.9 billion. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 212.8% in 2022 to 225.6% in 2023, 180.0% in 2024, and 152.8% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 7.79 billion in 2022, KRW -10.3 billion in 2023, KRW 5.56 billion in 2024, and KRW -23.4 billion in 2025, with the 2025 outflow being the largest.

On a quarterly basis, the company moved from revenue of KRW 25.4 billion and an operating loss of KRW 0.19 billion in the third quarter of 2025 to revenue of KRW 36.3 billion (up 43.0% quarter over quarter) and an operating profit of KRW 3.09 billion in the fourth quarter, returning to profitability.

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue fell to KRW 21.5 billion (down 40.8% quarter over quarter), and the company swung back to an operating loss of KRW 2.24 billion.

In the second quarter of 2026, revenue was KRW 27.2 billion (up 26.8%) with an operating loss of KRW 1.56 billion, and in the third quarter, revenue was KRW 27.6 billion (up 1.4% quarter over quarter and up 8.8% year over year) with an operating loss of KRW 1.69 billion.

Revenue grew for two consecutive quarters, but operating losses continued for three consecutive quarters, and the third-quarter operating loss was larger than the KRW 0.19 billion loss in the same period a year earlier.

The third-quarter net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.59 billion, a swing from a net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 0.19 billion in the same period a year earlier.

The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the most recent four quarters (the fourth quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026) is KRW 6.66 billion.

In explaining its third-quarter 2026 results, the parent company SM Entertainment stated that the loss widened because of higher music content production costs at SM C&C.

05

Industry analysis

Advertising is a cyclical industry that moves with corporate marketing budgets. Wisereport attributes the growth in SM C&C's advertising revenue to growth in the new-media ad market. In the second half of 2025, press reports said the ad market was gradually recovering and agency volume was rising.

Agency revenue follows a lag, since PT wins move into execution before being recognized as revenue, and the company's first-half 2025 PT projects entering execution illustrate that pattern. In advertising the company competes for accounts with Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide.

On the entertainment side, the spread of Korean content overseas and rising OTT viewing are cited as demand drivers. In music management it competes with Hybe, SM Entertainment, JYP Entertainment and YG Entertainment.

Its full push into music management began only in the second half of 2025, so the business is at an early stage compared with the established large agencies.

Because the ad recovery and the music IP expansion are happening at the same time, the results of the structural shift will show up in quarterly results from 2026 onward.

06

Outlook

The company has said it will pair the share consolidation with a reset of its overall business to lift its operating margin. A 5-to-1 consolidation is slated for the November 26, 2026 general meeting, and if approved the total number of issued shares would fall from 96,714,620 to 19,342,924.

The par value would rise from KRW 500 to KRW 2,500. The company said the step is meant to keep an appropriate float, stabilize the share price and raise corporate value.

In the music IP business, the second season of the SBS program Our Ballad was on air as of press reports in October 2026, and through 'We Go High' with Netflix the company plans to handle development, management and global activities of the debut group.

The parent, SM Entertainment, has flagged comebacks for the first quarter of 2027, but the filings do not state how those comebacks link to SM C&C's results.

In advertising, revenue is rising but operating losses have widened alongside it, so whether higher ad volume can offset production costs is the next point to track. The next filings are the preliminary results for the fourth quarter of 2026, expected in early 2027, and the 2026 annual report, due by late March 2027.

Bull factors

Ad market recovery and a rebound in quarterly revenue

Revenue reached KRW 36.3 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, up 43.0 percent quarter on quarter, and rose again in the second and third quarters of 2026. Third-quarter 2026 revenue was up 8.8 percent year on year.

Wisereport data show that first-quarter 2026 revenue also rose year on year while the operating loss narrowed, and press coverage of the 2025 third-quarter recovery pointed to higher agency volume.

Expansion of the music IP business

The company co-produced Our Ballad for both seasons and built a model in which it manages the artists found through the show. In October 2026 it announced an MOU with Netflix for a K-pop audition project.

Since the second half of 2025 it has pushed into music management in earnest, expanding the share of IP-based business.

Profitability target and the share consolidation

Alongside the share consolidation, the company has said it will reset its operations to raise the operating margin. The fourth quarter of 2025 showed an operating profit of KRW 3.09 billion, an example of a quarter in which profitability turned positive.

If the 5-to-1 consolidation passes at the November 26, 2026 general meeting, the number of issued shares will decline.

Bear factors

Three straight quarters of operating losses and a wider deficit

Operating losses have continued from the first quarter through the third quarter of 2026, and the third-quarter loss of KRW 1.69 billion was larger than the KRW 195 million loss a year earlier. The sum of net losses attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters was KRW 6.66 billion. Because net losses persist across the last four quarters, no earnings-based multiple is calculated.

Burden of music content production costs

In its third-quarter 2026 disclosure, the parent SM Entertainment said that rising costs in SM C&C's music content production widened the company's losses. While IP expansion broadens the revenue base, production and artist development costs weigh on short-term profit. Operating cash flow of -KRW 23.4 billion in 2025 also stands out.

Balance sheet and cash flow pressure

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 124.9 billion, above total equity of KRW 81.8 billion. The debt ratio fell from 212.8 percent in 2022 to 152.8 percent in 2025, but liabilities still exceed equity. Operating cash flow was negative in two years, -KRW 10.3 billion in 2023 and -KRW 23.4 billion in 2025.

10

Risk factors

Ad market and cyclicality

Advertising is cyclical, so a drop in corporate ad spending quickly hits revenue. The 36 percent fall in first-half 2025 advertising revenue shows this. Because the 2026 quarterly rebound was tied to an ad market recovery, a downturn could reverse that momentum.

Content production costs and hit-or-miss outcomes

The music IP business spends on production before results arrive, so earnings are volatile. Higher music content production costs were cited as a reason for the wider loss in the third quarter of 2026. If artist contracts or programs underperform, cost recovery could be delayed.

Group structure and share price swings after the consolidation

Because business direction and resource allocation are set within the SM Entertainment group, group strategy changes could affect the company. A 2024 report on a review of organizational efficiency in advertising was met by the company's statement that nothing had been decided.

After the 5-to-1 consolidation, the per-share price and the float will change, which could add volatility to the share price.

12

Overall view

SM C&C is in a phase where the recovery in the advertising agency market and the expansion of the music IP business are happening at the same time. Quarterly revenue rebounded from the fourth quarter of 2025 and rose in the second and third quarters of 2026.

Operating losses, however, have continued for three straight quarters from the first quarter of 2026, and the third-quarter loss was larger than a year earlier. The parent, SM Entertainment, attributed the wider loss to higher music content production costs.

On the financial side, total liabilities exceeded total equity at the end of 2025, and operating cash flow was a KRW 23.4 billion outflow, which weighs on the company.

The company has said the 5-to-1 consolidation and a business reset are aimed at lifting the operating margin, and the proposal is on the agenda for the November 26, 2026 general meeting.

The next checkpoints are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results and the 2026 annual report, and the key items are the trend in operating losses and whether cash flow improves.
