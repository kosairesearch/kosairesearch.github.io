02

Business structure

Wise birds runs digital advertising built on real-time bidding (RTB) and operates on its proprietary ad-optimization platform, Adwitt. The business is split between an advertising agency unit and a Technology Business Division that runs the AI-driven ad automation platform.

The company said its agency unit's Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 53 hundred million, up 113.0% year on year, with operating profit of about KRW 1.9 billion, announced on May 13, 2026.

Its search-ads subsidiary, Adefficiency-style search operations, also added more than KRW 1.5 billion in operating profit in the same quarter, according to the company.

Nest Ads Manager works on a revenue-share model: publishers install an SDK and run ads without separate infrastructure, and Wise birds earns more as publisher ad revenue grows.

Technology Business Division revenue in H1 2026 was KRW 1.25 billion, up 135.9% year on year, equal to roughly 5% of H1 consolidated revenue of KRW 24.5 billion.

A data aggregator's split for Q2 2026 shows 98% advertising agency revenue and 2% platform development, so the weight of each unit depends on the classification used. Clients include advertisers such as Zigzag and annual-contract customers such as Naver Financial and Naver Webtoon.

Large agencies such as Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide anchor the competitive landscape, while Wise birds positions itself on RTB publisher expertise and automation tools.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue went from KRW 15.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.9 billion in 2023 (down 12.1% year on year), then rose to KRW 30.8 billion in 2024 (up 122.2%) and KRW 45.5 billion in 2025 (up 47.9%), increasing in both 2024 and 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 2.57 billion in 2022 to KRW 219 million in 2023 (down 91.5%), then recovered to KRW 2.31 billion in 2024 (up 953.7%) and KRW 7.58 billion in 2025 (up 228.6%), with 2025 the largest year of 2022 to 2025.

Operating margin moved from 16.3% in 2022 to 1.6% in 2023, 7.5% in 2024 and 16.6% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 2.24 billion in 2022 to KRW 875 million in 2023 (down 60.9%), KRW 2.17 billion in 2024 (up 148.5%) and KRW 1.88 billion in 2025 (down 13.4%), with 2022 the largest year.

In 2025 operating profit rose sharply while controlling-shareholder net income fell, so non-operating items and financing costs are the next items to check. Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 1.86 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 64.6% in 2022 to 113.0% in 2023, 281.4% in 2024 and 280.1% in 2025, and at end-2025 total liabilities were KRW 139.8 billion against total equity of KRW 49.9 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 4.17 billion in 2022, -KRW 1.27 billion in 2023, KRW 6.09 billion in 2024 and -KRW 277 million in 2025.

Quarterly revenue went from KRW 10.4 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 15.5 billion in Q4 2025 (up 48.4% quarter on quarter), and operating profit from KRW 2.09 billion to KRW 2.92 billion (up 39.4%).

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 11.8 billion (down 23.7% quarter on quarter) while operating profit held at KRW 2.91 billion (down 0.4%). Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 12.7 billion with operating profit of KRW 2.86 billion.

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 13.5 billion (up 29.6% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 3.69 billion (up 76.0% year on year). Operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 totaled KRW 9.46 billion, above the full-year 2025 figure.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 7.81 billion, with Q4 2025's KRW 3.7 billion the largest contributor. Because Q4 2025 controlling-shareholder net income exceeded that quarter's operating profit, the contribution of non-operating items is a point to verify.

05

Industry analysis

The digital advertising market in which Wise birds operates is described by research summaries as continuing to grow. One securities research summary expected that AI-native ad operations entering full swing in 2026 would expand demand for automated bidding and wider targeting.

Ad demand follows a seasonal pattern with Q1 as the slow quarter and H2 concentrated, and the company said it turned a KRW 640 million H1 2024 operating loss into a KRW 2.94 billion H2 2024 profit, and grew H2 2025 operating profit to KRW 5.02 billion from KRW 2.56 billion in H1 2025.

Because of this seasonality, quarterly results differ widely between the first and second halves. Some securities analysts note that its earnings structure resembles that of the Nasdaq-listed ad platform AppLovin.

The company claims it was the only top-10 Korean ad agency to post 20%-plus growth in a survey by the Korea Advertising Council, but this is the company's own statement and should be cross-checked against third-party data.

On scale, the company reported 2025 advertising transaction volume of KRW 579.2 billion and a record quarterly consolidated transaction volume of KRW 174.2 billion in Q4 2025.

Large full-service agencies such as Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide are the reference points for the sector, and Wise birds differentiates through RTB publisher specialization and automation tools, occupying a niche position.

In media, the model of growing publisher revenue alongside ad revenue is presented as its core competitive feature.

06

Outlook

On August 7, 2026, the company said that H1 results were a record for a half-year and that growth would continue in H2, with the year expected to set a new all-time high. This is management's expectation, and no detailed full-year numeric guidance appeared in the announcement.

On the product side, the company plans to roll out the creative AI tool Ad-Visor across H2, targeting a 97% cut in ad content production time. Nest Ads Manager has expanded to 16 linked publishers with Viber and SmartScore added, and June net revenue doubled from January, according to the company.

The company also said it is pursuing global expansion of Nest Ads Manager. On shareholder returns, it is pursuing share buybacks and cancellations along with a share consolidation, and the CEO said the policy would be made regular.

Given the seasonal pattern, the next checkpoint is full-year 2026 preliminary results, and whether the record-year claim holds on annual operating profit is the key test.

The 2026 annual business report is due by March 31, 2027, when full-year operating profit, operating cash flow and the debt ratio can be re-checked against the audited filing.

Q1 2027 is a slow quarter, so whether operating profit holds near the KRW 2.91 billion of Q1 2026 is a measure of underlying strength, and the growing weight of the Technology Business Division is another item to watch.

08

Bull factors

Margin recovery

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 3.69 billion was up 76.0% year on year. The company said H1 2026 operating profit was its best half-year on record. Profitability has now held for four straight half-years since H2 2024.

Technology unit growth and publisher expansion

Technology Business Division revenue in H1 2026 was KRW 1.25 billion, up 135.9% year on year. Nest Ads Manager now links 16 publishers, and June net revenue doubled from January, according to the company.

Because the company earns a share of publisher ad revenue, adding publishers carries little incremental cost, as the company explains.

Expanded shareholder returns

On August 7, 2026, the company said it was strengthening shareholder returns through share buybacks, cancellations and a share consolidation. The CEO said the company plans to make the shareholder return policy regular in line with earnings growth.

The company framed sharing growth with shareholders as responsible management. The specific criteria and timing of that policy will appear in later disclosures.

Bear factors

High debt ratio

Total liabilities at end-2025 were KRW 139.8 billion, about 2.8 times total equity of KRW 49.9 billion. The debt ratio jumped from 64.6% in 2022 to 281.4% in 2024 and stood at 280.1% in 2025. A market data aggregator shows interest expense rising from KRW 1.34 billion in Q4 2025 to KRW 2.69 billion in Q1 2026.

Volatile quarterly earnings

Controlling-shareholder net income swung from KRW 389 million in Q3 2025 to KRW 3.7 billion in Q4 2025, then KRW 1.23 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 995 million in Q2 2026 and KRW 1.89 billion in Q3 2026. Operating profit held near KRW 2.9 billion in Q1 and Q2 2026 before reaching KRW 3.69 billion in Q3. In 2025 operating profit rose 228.6% while controlling-shareholder net income fell 13.4%.

Client concentration and cash flow

Most revenue comes from the advertising agency unit, which depends partly on annual contracts with customers such as Naver affiliates. Operating cash flow was negative in 2025 at -KRW 277 million and in 2023 at -KRW 1.27 billion. A slowdown in ad spending or changes in publisher policies could affect bookings and settlements.

10

Risk factors

Balance sheet

The debt ratio has stayed near 280%, so changes in interest rates or borrowing terms could pressure the earnings structure. Total liabilities of KRW 139.8 billion far exceed total equity of KRW 49.9 billion. Funding structure and repayment schedules are items to confirm in periodic filings.

Ad cycle and seasonality

Ad demand is seasonal, with a slow Q1 and an H2 concentration, which produces large quarterly swings. Q1 2026 revenue fell 23.7% quarter on quarter. Cuts in advertiser budgets or lower digital ad prices would hit agency revenue directly.

Platform and publisher dependence

Growth at Nest Ads Manager depends on adding publishers and on their ad operations. Changes in large platforms' ad policies or data regulation could affect how the automation tools operate. Reliance on specific publishers or advertiser contracts remains a risk.

12

Overall view

Wise birds recovered from an operating profit low of KRW 220 million in 2023 to KRW 7.58 billion in 2025, and operating profit for the first three quarters of 2026 totaled KRW 9.46 billion, above the full-year 2025 figure.

The recovery rests mainly on the advertising agency unit's return to profit and on the expansion of publishers on the AI ad platform Nest Ads Manager. Technology Business Division revenue is still only about 5% of the total, so growth continues to rest mainly on the agency business.

Against that, the debt ratio near 280% and negative operating cash flow in 2025 mean that cash conversion of profits and the debt burden both need checking.

Quarterly net income did not move in step with operating profit, and the reason controlling-shareholder net income exceeded operating profit in Q4 2025 is a point to confirm in periodic filings.

The company said it expects a record full-year result in 2026 and has expanded shareholder returns through buybacks, cancellations and a share consolidation.

The next checkpoints are full-year 2026 preliminary results in early 2027 and the 2026 annual business report, where operating profit trends, the debt ratio and cash flow will be assessed together.
