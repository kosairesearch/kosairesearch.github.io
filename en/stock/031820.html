02

Business structure

Itcencts is a comprehensive IT services company that builds and operates IT infrastructure, with network infrastructure and AI infrastructure as its two pillars.

Based on the Butler company data aggregator, the second-quarter 2026 revenue mix was 33% cloud segment excluding others, 23% distribution, 20% SI, 17% cloud and 5% DI, and this split may differ from the company's own disclosure categories. Segment mix is shown only as a share.

In its first-quarter 2026 results release, the company said its AI and high-performance computing (HPC) solutions generated visible revenue while its existing network infrastructure business grew steadily.

In the third quarter of 2026, growth in AI infrastructure and higher revenue at major affiliates lifted the top line, and one affiliate returned to profit, adding to consolidated operating profit.

On a standalone basis, the company posted a third-quarter 2026 operating profit of KRW 0.4 billion, and its cumulative standalone operating loss narrowed from KRW 5.4 billion to KRW 2.7 billion.

Equity-method income also matters: investment gains of KRW 37.0 billion from associates, including KGE Holdings, the parent of a Korean gold-trading business, added to third-quarter net income.

Confirmed contract examples include an internet data center project for a securities firm, reported in April 2025, and an MEP system role in a data center project. Competitors include Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever, which overlap with its large SI and cloud business. The company has said it aims to transform into a specialized AI infrastructure company.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show continued revenue growth but a thin profit margin. Revenue rose from KRW 709.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 892.6 billion in 2023 (up 25.7% year on year), KRW 995.3 billion in 2024 (up 11.5%) and KRW 1.1 trillion in 2025 (up 10.0%), and has increased for three straight years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 15.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 15.5 billion in 2023 (down 2.2%), then rose to KRW 21 billion in 2024 (up 35.3%) and KRW 23.6 billion in 2025 (up 12.1%). Operating margin ran at 2.2%, 1.7%, 2.1% and 2.2% from 2022 to 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders dropped from KRW 33.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 3.32 billion in 2023 (down 90.1%), a far steeper fall than the 2.2% dip in operating profit, which suggests non-operating and equity-method swings were large in that year.

It then rose to KRW 24.6 billion in 2024 and KRW 52.8 billion in 2025, a second straight year of growth.

The gap between 2025 net income of KRW 92.6 billion and the controlling-shareholder figure reflects the share attributable to non-controlling interests, and the company cited stronger subsidiary business and a sharp rise in associates' net income, which lifted equity-method gains.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 41.8 billion in 2022 to -KRW 47.1 billion in 2023, then recovered to KRW 20.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 64.3 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio fell from 179.4% in 2022 to 145.4% in 2025, with year-end 2025 total equity of KRW 339.7 billion, controlling-shareholder equity of KRW 229 billion and total liabilities of KRW 493.9 billion.

Quarterly results jumped in the fourth quarter of 2025, with revenue of KRW 376.7 billion (up 45.5% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 19.4 billion (up 75.6%), then bottomed in the first quarter of 2026 at KRW 248.8 billion in revenue and KRW 3.47 billion in operating profit.

Operating profit then rose for two straight quarters, and the company said first-quarter net income was lifted by equity-method gains from investee companies.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for Itcencts is driven by AI infrastructure spending, particularly data centers and high-performance computing (HPC) facilities. The company has long named the growth of the AI data center industry as a key tailwind.

According to a 2025 report, it joined as the MEP (mechanical, electrical and plumbing) system contractor for a hyperscale data center in Ansan's Chogi-dong, with 40MW of contracted power capacity and a target completion in the second half of 2027.

That project is a channel through which AI infrastructure orders turn into company revenue. Data center work runs over long construction periods, so revenue recognition timing can vary from quarter to quarter.

Where the company sits in the investment cycle cannot be fixed from its disclosures alone, and the scale of large AI infrastructure orders in Korea is the next point to watch.

In competition, it faces large SI and cloud providers such as Samsung SDS, LG CNS and Hyundai Autoever in the same market, and Itcencts is positioned by adding AI infrastructure on top of its network infrastructure and distribution base.

In its first-quarter 2026 results release, the company said growth in AI-related business was the main driver of improved profitability. How far this shift changes an operating margin in the 2% range will show up in the next annual report.

A November 2025 report also described plans to launch an AI contact center solution with a large language model and to develop an HPC resource allocation and monitoring solution.

Outlook

The company's chief executive said in the third-quarter 2026 release that the fourth quarter would focus on the early construction of projects already won and on winning large AI infrastructure contracts. Fourth-quarter revenue and operating profit will depend on how those two efforts turn out.

In the first-quarter 2026 release, the company said its key investee companies' performance continues to improve and expects a stable contribution to earnings over the medium to long term.

A November 2025 report described plans to launch an AI contact center solution with a large language model, and it set a 2026 prototype target for the HPC resource allocation solution. The Ansan data center was given a target completion in the second half of 2027.

The securities firm data center contract and the expansion of AI infrastructure business support top-line growth. On the other side, equity-method income tracks the results of outside companies, so it remains a source of quarter-to-quarter swings in net profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

Whether net profit attributable to controlling shareholders keeps falling after the second and third quarters of 2026 is the next point to check. The 2026 annual report is due around the end of March 2027 and will set out confirmed full-year results and segment detail.

Bull factors

Quarterly profit recovery

The cumulative standalone operating loss also narrowed from KRW 5.4 billion to KRW 2.7 billion over the same period.

Expansion of AI infrastructure business

The company said its AI and HPC solutions began producing visible revenue in the first quarter of 2026. It joined the Ansan data center project as MEP system contractor, widening its data center capabilities.

Its chief executive said the company would keep focusing on large AI infrastructure orders in the fourth quarter.

Improvement at affiliates and associates

The company said one affiliate returned to profit and that revenue rose at other key affiliates. Investment gains of KRW 37.0 billion from associates added to third-quarter 2026 net income. Net income for 2025 of KRW 92.6 billion compares with KRW 24.6 billion in 2024.

Bear factors

Thin operating margin

Operating margin was 2.2% in 2022, 1.7% in 2023, 2.1% in 2024 and 2.2% in 2025, staying around the low 2% range. Operating profit of KRW 23.6 billion on KRW 1.1 trillion of revenue in 2025 shows a small profit pool relative to the top line. Revenue growth has not yet flowed into margin expansion.

Volatility in net profit attributable to controlling shareholders

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 3.32 billion in 2023, and after peaking at KRW 19.3 billion in the first quarter of 2026 it dropped to KRW 5.26 billion and KRW 4.96 billion in the next two quarters.

The company attributed the first-quarter spike partly to equity-method gains from investee companies. Profit that leans on equity-method results can swing sharply from quarter to quarter.

Balance sheet burden and cash flow

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 493.9 billion, above total equity of KRW 339.7 billion. Operating cash flow turned negative at -KRW 47.1 billion in 2023 and recovered in 2024 and 2025, but that recovery is still short. With the debt ratio at 145.4%, expanding projects could raise working capital needs.

Risk factors

Project execution and schedule risk

Data center and AI infrastructure projects run over long construction periods, and schedule changes shift when revenue is recognized. The Ansan data center was targeted for completion in the second half of 2027, and early delivery of projects already won will affect fourth-quarter results. Delays in large orders could slow top-line growth.

Volatility in associate results

Third-quarter 2026 net income benefited from investment gains of KRW 37.0 billion from associates including KGE Holdings. Such gains depend on outside companies' results and market conditions, which the company cannot control.

A decline in associate income could lower both net income and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders.

Financial structure and cash flow

Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 493.9 billion, and the debt ratio stood at 145.4%. Operating cash flow was negative at -KRW 47.1 billion in 2023. With an operating margin in the 2% range, higher financing costs or working capital needs could weaken cash conversion of profits.

Overall view

Itcencts' revenue rose for three straight years from 2022 through 2025 to KRW 1.1 trillion, and operating profit has grown for two straight years since the 2023 low. Operating margin, however, stayed between 1.7% and 2.2%, so the profit pool is small relative to sales.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders recovered to KRW 52.8 billion in 2025, but in 2026 it peaked at KRW 19.3 billion in the first quarter and then fell for two straight quarters.

Quarterly operating profit has risen for two straight quarters since the first-quarter 2026 low, reflecting the AI infrastructure business and improvement at affiliates. Because associate investment gains weigh heavily on net income, the quality and durability of earnings should be read together.

On the balance sheet, the debt ratio fell to 145.4% in 2025, but total liabilities still exceed total equity. The next checkpoints are the confirmed full-year results in the 2026 annual report, first-quarter 2027 profit attributable to controlling shareholders, and the Ansan data center completion schedule.
