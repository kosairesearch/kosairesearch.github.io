02

Business structure

BMTCo was founded in 1988 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2007. It makes precision fittings, valves and electrical distribution panels for industrial use.

The Fitting and Valve division supplies piping materials for plants, while the Electrical division produces distribution panels, switchboards and intelligent power monitoring systems.

The core revenue driver is ultra-high-purity fittings and valves used in semiconductor equipment, and the company set a 2026 semiconductor revenue target of KRW 75 billion, about 50 percent above the roughly KRW 50 billion of the prior year. The second pillar is eco-friendly ship fuel supply units.

The company said it developed and completed application and commissioning of a Fuel Valve Unit (FVU) for WinGD's ammonia engine, and it has since won additional orders from a major Korean shipbuilder.

Before dedicated production facilities were built, the SKID product had already built a backlog of about KRW 30 billion. On the customer side, BMTCo is registered as a supplier to Samsung Electronics and SK hynix and appears on the standard maker lists of several major Korean shipbuilders.

In piping, customer approval status largely determines competitiveness in bids. Its High-Integration Gas Box (HGB) and High-Integration Gas System (HGS) have been delivered as test equipment, and the company is pursuing their application at new customers.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 142.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 149.6 billion in 2023, fell 11.2% to KRW 132.8 billion in 2024, and recovered 10.7% to KRW 147 billion in 2025. Among 2022 to 2025, 2023 was the largest year for revenue and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 23 billion in 2022 to KRW 17.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 6.47 billion in 2024, then rose 136.8% to KRW 15.3 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 16.2% in 2022 to 11.7% in 2023, 4.9% in 2024 and 10.4% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 19 billion in 2025, the largest of the four years, while 2024 at KRW 4.12 billion was the smallest. The company attributed the 2025 improvement to a recovery in the semiconductor industry and strong demand in LNG and eco-friendly fuel-powered vessels.

Quarterly revenue bottomed at KRW 33.7 billion in 4Q 2025, down 10.1% from the prior quarter, and then increased for three consecutive quarters through 3Q 2026 to KRW 50.2 billion. Operating profit of KRW 8.93 billion in 3Q 2026 is the highest of the last five quarters.

However, net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 12.6 billion in 3Q 2025 against operating profit of KRW 5.81 billion in the same quarter, a gap that suggests non-operating items or tax effects should be checked in the periodic report notes.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in 3Q 2026 was KRW 4.52 billion, down 64.0% year on year, and the sum over the last four quarters is KRW 14.8 billion.

Industry analysis

BMTCo's main end market is semiconductor capital spending. The company says the resumption of memory chip investment and high-bandwidth memory (HBM) capacity expansion driven by AI data centers are increasing demand for piping and fluid control parts.

Press coverage indicates the semiconductor recovery began to show up in results from March 2026. Tighter environmental rules for ships are keeping orders for eco-friendly fuel vessels flowing, and the company is positioning its ammonia-engine fuel supply units as a new growth pillar.

Demand from shipbuilding, offshore plant, power generation and semiconductor customers is cyclical, and one tally shows revenue declining year on year in 1Q 2026. In competition, customer approval history acts as an entry barrier.

BMTCo is registered as a supplier to Samsung Electronics and SK hynix and appears on standard maker lists in shipbuilding. Its revenue scale is smaller than large equipment makers, so the timing of large project orders can move quarterly results significantly.

06

Outlook

The company set a 2026 semiconductor revenue target of KRW 75 billion. Its CEO said related orders have grown significantly since the second quarter as chip capital spending resumed. The company also expects new ship-related orders to be reflected in results starting in the second quarter of 2026.

Revenue in 2Q 2026 was KRW 48.6 billion, up 36.8% from the prior quarter, and 3Q 2026 revenue reached KRW 50.2 billion. Production capacity is a key variable: the company says the first plant alone is barely enough to handle incoming orders, and it expects the second Jangan plant to relieve that bottleneck.

The fuel supply unit (SKID) will move into mass production for the first time, and a backlog of about KRW 30 billion had already built up before the dedicated facility started.

If new customer applications for the gas box and gas system convert into mass-production orders, the second plant could serve as the production base.

The next confirmation point is the 2026 annual report, which should show whether the KRW 75 billion semiconductor target was met, how much of the new shipbuilding orders reached results, and how non-operating items and tax lines changed.

Based on last year's timing, the preliminary 4Q 2026 results are likely around early February 2027, and whether revenue and operating profit extend the three-quarter trend will be the next check.

Bull factors

Semiconductor capex resumption and order growth

In July 2026 press coverage, the CEO said related orders had grown significantly since the second quarter as chip capital spending resumed. The company set a 2026 semiconductor revenue target of KRW 75 billion, about 50 percent above the prior year's roughly KRW 50 billion. Its registration as a supplier to Samsung Electronics and SK hynix is cited as a foundation for these orders.

Eco-friendly ship fuel supply orders

In June 2026, the company announced that its FVU for the WinGD ammonia engine had completed application and commissioning. It said it secured eco-friendly fuel supply orders worth about KRW 13 billion in the first half from major shipbuilders and engine makers.

Press coverage also reports a SKID backlog of about KRW 30 billion built before dedicated production facilities came online.

Capacity expansion from the Jangan 2nd plant

A July 2026 press report says completion of the Jangan 2nd plant would lift domestic production capacity to KRW 250 billion to KRW 350 billion in annual sales. Compared with 2025 revenue of KRW 147 billion, that is a substantial expansion of capacity. The company says it can meet rising orders for some time without another large construction investment.

Bear factors

Operating gains not reaching net profit

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 4.52 billion in 3Q 2026, down 64.0% year on year. Operating profit in the same quarter was KRW 8.93 billion, up 53.6%, so the gap between operating and net results is wide.

Press coverage reports that pre-tax income fell sharply from a year earlier, but a detailed explanation must be checked in the periodic report.

Earnings volatility from industry cycles

Operating profit fell to KRW 6.47 billion in 2024, with the operating margin dropping to 4.9%. In 1Q 2026, operating profit was KRW 1.77 billion, down 46.4% from the prior quarter.

Demand depends on cycles in shipbuilding, offshore plants, power generation and semiconductors, so quarterly results react sharply to conditions in those industries.

New businesses still being verified and customer concentration

The High-Integration Gas Box and Gas System have been delivered as test equipment, and applications at new customers are still in progress. The semiconductor business depends on the investment schedules of a small number of large customers including Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. How much of any major bid reaches quarterly results must be verified through each quarter's disclosures.

Risk factors

Industry cycle risk

Semiconductor capital spending and shipbuilding orders can shift quickly with economic conditions and policy. In 2024, revenue fell 11.2% and the operating margin dropped to 4.9%, showing that a demand slowdown flows directly into results. Concentration in specific customers and industries is another factor to weigh.

Earnings volatility and non-operating items

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 12.6 billion in 3Q 2025, far above that quarter's operating profit of KRW 5.81 billion, so a single quarter can weigh heavily on annual totals. In 3Q 2026, operating profit rose while net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell. The quality and composition of earnings must be checked against the detailed notes in the periodic reports.

Utilization and investment burden after expansion

If production capacity rises sharply after the Jangan 2nd plant is completed, fixed costs could weigh heavily should orders fail to keep pace. The company has mentioned possible mass production of new products, but whether these convert into orders must be confirmed through future disclosures.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 75.8% in 2025 from 123.7% in 2022, but how the expansion is funded should be reviewed alongside future financial statements.

Overall view

BMTCo has built its recovery on two pillars, the resumption of semiconductor capital spending and eco-friendly ship fuel supply orders, with revenue and operating profit both rising for consecutive quarters in 2026.

Annual operating profit for 2025 was KRW 15.3 billion, up 136.8% from the prior year, recovering from the low point of 2024.

That trend continued into 3Q 2026 with operating profit of KRW 8.93 billion, but net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 64.0% to KRW 4.52 billion, so whether operating gains reach the bottom line remains to be confirmed.

Bullish factors include the KRW 75 billion semiconductor revenue target, capacity expansion from the Jangan 2nd plant and the shipbuilding backlog. Bearish factors include earnings swings tied to industry cycles and customer concentration, plus uncertainty around non-operating items.

Valuation is harder to read given quarterly swings in the last four quarters, and the price-to-book ratio sits near net asset value.

The next checkpoints are the December 2026 fiscal year-end, the preliminary 4Q 2026 results around early February 2027 and the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027, where the revenue target and the conversion of operating profit into net profit should be reviewed together.
