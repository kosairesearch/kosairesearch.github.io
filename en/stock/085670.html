02

Business structure

Newflextechnolgyco (085670) manufactures flexible printed circuit boards (FPCBs) and is listed on KOSDAQ. A March 2022 technical analysis report described the business as starting with FPCBs for camera modules and wireless charging modules and expanding into VR and AR devices, EV batteries and 5G antenna cables.

The same report said the company was investing KRW 50 billion in new facilities to grow its new businesses. Demand for its main products moves closely with smartphone shipments from its main customer, a point repeated in brokerage research summaries.

Production is based in Vietnam, where Vina Newflex Co., Ltd and Newflex Technology Vina Co., Ltd are consolidated subsidiaries making flexible circuit boards, with Vietnamese units set up in 2015 and 2018.

A domestic subsidiary, Newflex Technology's domestic unit NFG, was established in June 2024 and is also consolidated. Competition is intensifying as Chinese and Southeast Asian manufacturers expand on price competitiveness and rigid PCB makers enter the FPCB market.

Existing smartphone demand and new applications such as XR and EV batteries now run side by side as business pillars, and one research summary says the company is developing XR-optimized FPCBs in cooperation with Meta.

04

Earnings analysis

On the basis of filed results, annual revenue went from KRW 260.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 195.1 billion in 2023 (down 25.0% year on year), KRW 175.9 billion in 2024 (down 9.8%) and KRW 154.1 billion in 2025 (down 12.4%), declining for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

The company cited a slowdown in downstream industries as the main cause of the 2025 revenue decline. Operating profit fell from KRW 17.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 10.3 billion in 2023 (down 42.3%), recovered to KRW 14.6 billion in 2024 (up 41.8%), and dropped again to KRW 9.23 billion in 2025 (down 36.9%).

The operating margin was 6.9% in 2022, 5.3% in 2023, 8.3% in 2024 and 6.0% in 2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 14.4 billion in 2022, KRW 6.79 billion in 2023 (down 52.9%), KRW 16.1 billion in 2024 (up 137.1%) and KRW 8.8 billion in 2025 (down 45.3%), with 2024 the largest year in the period.

Net income including non-controlling interests was reported at the same levels for each year. Operating cash flow was KRW 23.7 billion in 2022, KRW 5.89 billion in 2023, KRW 42.7 billion in 2024 and KRW 14.7 billion in 2025, swinging more widely than earnings.

Quarterly revenue reached KRW 39.6 billion in Q4 2025 (up 4.3% from the prior quarter) with operating profit of KRW 3.2 billion (up 50.8%). Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 35 billion, with operating profit of KRW 291 million (down 90.9% from the prior quarter).

Q2 2026 revenue recovered to KRW 37.1 billion with operating profit of KRW 1.42 billion. Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 31.7 billion (down 16.4% year on year) and the company posted an operating loss of KRW 1.51 billion, a swing to loss from a year earlier.

Net loss attributable to controlling shareholders in the same quarter was KRW 6.37 billion, against net income of KRW 3.08 billion in Q3 2025. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders totaled KRW 600 million over the last four quarters.

05

Industry analysis

Newflextechnolgyco's business is sensitive to the smartphone-centered downstream demand cycle. The slowdown in downstream industries that the company cited for the 2025 revenue decline sits within this cycle.

Because FPCB volumes move with the shipments of its main customer's smartphones, where the industry stands in the shipment cycle largely determines revenue. On the competitive side, price competition from Chinese and Southeast Asian manufacturers is the largest pressure point.

Rigid PCB makers entering the FPCB market are also blurring technology boundaries and affecting pricing power.

On the other side, uses are broadening beyond camera and wireless charging modules into EV batteries and automotive electronics, and development of FPCBs for XR devices is ongoing, which is cited as widening the demand base.

Large component makers in the same electronics and components sector, Samsung Electro-Mechanics and LG Innotek, run camera module and substrate businesses at far larger scale, and Newflextechnolgyco differs from them considerably in both revenue and market capitalization. Its focus on a specific FPCB product group is therefore a defining feature of its position.

Q2 2026 rebound and wider applications

Risk factors

Customer and demand concentration

Core demand is linked to the smartphone shipments of its main customer, so volume adjustments by that customer flow directly into revenue. The downstream slowdown is the factor the company cited for the 2025 revenue decline.

Revenue shares of new applications such as XR and EV batteries are not separately reported in the filed results, making the pace of change hard to measure.

Pricing and margin pressure

Price competition from Chinese and Southeast Asian manufacturers, combined with entry by rigid PCB makers, puts pressure on both selling prices and margins. The operating margin ranged between 5.3% and 8.3% from 2022 to 2025 and turned into an operating loss in Q3 2026. Cost competitiveness depends heavily on the operating efficiency of the Vietnamese production base.

Cash flow and dividend capacity
