HS Ad: Profit Rebounds After a Loss Quarter, Revenue Still Shrinking

HS Ad returned to operating profit in the second and third quarters of 2026 after an operating loss in the first quarter, while full-year 2025 revenue fell to KRW 483.9 billion, so profit recovery and a smaller top line are appearing together.

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

HS Ad is an advertising agency that handles ad planning, production and media buying, and its consolidated revenue divides into production revenue and agency commission revenue.

According to the 2025 business report, production revenue was KRW 404.7 billion, or 83.6% of 2025 consolidated revenue of KRW 483.9 billion, and agency commission revenue was KRW 79.1 billion, or 16.4%.

Production revenue is recognized on a gross basis, while media buying is recognized net, with only the commission booked as agency commission revenue. Because of this accounting, revenue moves closely with production volume.

In the same report's gross profit breakdown, production accounted for KRW 156.0 billion (66.3%) and agency commissions for KRW 79.1 billion (33.7%) of the KRW 235.0 billion consolidated gross profit.

On a parent-only basis, production of advertising materials was the largest item at KRW 78.6 billion (33%), up from KRW 67.5 billion in 2024. In the same breakdown, parent-only media buying fell from KRW 69.7 billion in 2024 to KRW 59.7 billion in 2025.

On the customer side, affiliates of the LG Group account for a large share of revenue. A June 2020 news report put the LG Group affiliate share at 73% of revenue in 2019.

A corporate profile service's earnings comment describes demand shifting from traditional four major media to digital advertising, with broadcast ad and production revenue declining, and it names expanding the digital business as a core task. Competition overlaps with large agencies such as Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide.

04

Earnings analysis

On a reported annual basis, revenue rose from KRW 530.4 billion in 2022 to KRW 548.3 billion in 2023 (up 3.4%) and KRW 554.7 billion in 2024 (up 1.2%), then fell to KRW 483.9 billion in 2025 (down 12.8%). Among 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest year and 2025 the smallest.

Operating profit went from KRW 24.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 26.4 billion in 2023 (up 6.7%) and KRW 29.6 billion in 2024 (up 11.8%), then declined to KRW 27.5 billion in 2025 (down 7.0%). The operating margin rose every year, from 4.7% in 2022 to 4.8% in 2023, 5.3% in 2024 and 5.7% in 2025.

The margin improved in 2025 even as revenue fell, and the cause of the revenue decline can only be separated by reading the segment-level disclosures together.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 17.5 billion in 2022, KRW 16.2 billion in 2023 (down 7.2%), KRW 22.7 billion in 2024 (up 40.0%) and KRW 17.4 billion in 2025 (down 23.4%).

Its swings were larger than operating profit's, which means non-operating items and income tax also need to be read alongside it. Operating cash flow was KRW 15.7 billion in 2022 and KRW 58.2 billion in 2023, then turned negative at -KRW 4.64 billion in 2024 and -KRW 10.9 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 208.5% in 2022 to 139.6% in 2025, and at year-end 2025 total equity of KRW 212 billion was all attributable to controlling shareholders. Quarterly results swung widely.

Operating profit rose from KRW 5.21 billion in Q3 2025 to KRW 31.3 billion in Q4 2025 (up 501.7% quarter on quarter), and Q4 2025 revenue of KRW 211.9 billion was the largest of the last five quarters.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 72.1 billion with an operating loss of KRW 12.1 billion, a swing into loss from the prior quarter. Q2 operating profit of KRW 3.77 billion marked a return to profit, and Q3 operating profit of KRW 7.11 billion was up 88.7% from the prior quarter and up 36.6% from a year earlier.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the most recent four quarters totaled KRW 20.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

The advertising industry is undergoing a structural shift as demand moves from traditional four major media to digital. A corporate profile service's comment on 2025 results said broadcast advertising and production revenue declined but the overall earnings decline was limited.

An fnguide analysis of Q1 2026 likewise framed digital transition as accelerating amid a shrinking advertising market. HS Ad's high production revenue share means it is directly exposed to clients cutting production budgets.

Production revenue made up 84.5% of consolidated revenue in 2024 and 83.6% in 2025, so the mix changed little. Agency commission revenue fell from KRW 86.2 billion in 2024 to KRW 79.1 billion in 2025. The position of the industry cycle can be gauged by how the digital share of media and the client industry mix change.

A high share of revenue from group affiliates means results may depend more on group advertising plans than on the broader cycle. Cheil Worldwide and Innocean Worldwide are the listed peers in the same industry.

Comparing scale and profitability on the same basis requires lining up the consolidated figures in each company's periodic reports.

Bull factors

Recovery in quarterly profit

Improving financial structure

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 208.5% in 2022 to 139.6% in 2025. Total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 212 billion and total liabilities were KRW 295.9 billion. The operating margin rose from 4.7% in 2022 to 5.7% in 2025 over the same period.

Steady dividends and a production-led model

Annual dividends were held at the same level in 2024 and 2025. With production revenue at 83.6% of the total, the revenue base could stay stable if production orders continue. The same structure also means volumes can change with advertising cycles.

Bear factors

Shrinking top line

Revenue in 2025 fell 12.8% from the prior year to KRW 483.9 billion. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 87.5 billion was also down 5.1% from a year earlier. An fnguide analysis put Q1 2026 revenue down 10.7% from a year earlier.

Negative operating cash flow

Operating cash flow was -KRW 4.64 billion in 2024 and -KRW 10.9 billion in 2025. Cash generation did not follow operating profit even in years when operating profit was positive, which calls for a breakdown of working capital changes. The gap is an item to check in the cash flow statement of the next periodic report.

Group dependence and quarterly swings

Because the group affiliate share of revenue has been high, changes in affiliate order volumes can affect revenue. The Q1 2026 operating loss of KRW 12.1 billion and net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 10 billion show how sharply quarterly results can swing. Whether the return to profit becomes a change in the earnings trend needs confirmation over more quarters.

Risk factors

Shift in the advertising market structure

If the shift from traditional broadcast and print advertising to digital continues, media mix and production pricing may change. Digital growth needs to be fast enough to offset revenue declines, yet 2025 revenue fell 12.8%. Pace of digital transition and profit maintenance remain joint challenges.

Client concentration

In a structure with a high share of group affiliate business, changes in group advertising plans translate directly into revenue changes. The 2019 news-reported share was 73%. How far non-affiliate revenue expands is the key variable in changing this concentration.

Quarterly volatility and cash generation

Quarterly operating profit ranged from KRW 31.3 billion in Q4 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 12.1 billion in Q1 2026. Operating cash flow was also negative in 2024 and 2025. Whether the year-end concentration repeats and whether cash flow moves in the same direction as earnings are the points to check.

HS Ad's operating margin rose every year from 2022, and in 2025 operating profit held at KRW 27.5 billion even as revenue fell 12.8%. In 2026, operating profit followed an operating loss in Q1 with profit in Q2 and Q3, turning results toward recovery.

Still, Q3 2026 revenue was down from a year earlier, and operating cash flow was negative in 2024 and 2025. On the financial side, the debt-to-equity ratio fell from 208.5% in 2022 to 139.6% in 2025, and total equity at year-end 2025 was KRW 212 billion.
