02

Business structure

LTC was founded in 2007 and listed on KOSDAQ in 2013 as a specialist in display and semiconductor process materials, with eight affiliated companies.

Its core products are wet chemicals such as PR strippers for TFT processes and cleaning solutions, complemented by cleaning solutions and etchants for DRAM and NAND and wafer cleaning equipment.

Subsidiary LTCAM produces wet chemicals used in DRAM and NAND manufacturing, and C.C.S.S, a high-purity chemical supply system, is supplied in Korea and overseas. Wafer cleaning equipment is handled by equipment affiliate LSE.

By segment, wafer cleaning equipment accounted for KRW 40.8 billion and semiconductor process materials for KRW 14.6 billion of KRW 75.9 billion in first-quarter 2025 sales, according to a securities analysis published in July 2025.

In the same analysis, display process material sales fell 3.5% in 2024, while semiconductor process materials and wafer cleaning equipment rose 95.2% and 574.4%.

Customers include major Korean display and semiconductor companies, and a July 2025 report described the company as a key materials and equipment supplier to SK hynix.

The first-quarter 2026 earnings commentary cited continued PR stripper deliveries as a major customer shifted lines to QD-OLED, and higher wafer cleaning equipment sales from large chipmaker investments. Exports were 23.71% of product sales in 2025, so the structure leans toward domestic sales.

Competitors in semiconductor and display chemical materials include Foosung, Lake Materials and ENF Technology, and wafer cleaning equipment revenue moves with customer fab investment schedules.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results moved from revenue of KRW 219.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 10.8 billion in 2022 to revenue of KRW 113.4 billion, down 48.3%, and an operating loss of KRW 16 billion in 2023. In 2024, revenue rose 144.3% to KRW 277 billion and operating profit turned to KRW 24.2 billion.

In 2025, revenue increased 12.1% to KRW 310.4 billion and operating profit rose 22.5% to KRW 29.6 billion, the largest revenue year in the 2022–2025 window, and the operating margin of 9.5% was higher than 8.7% in 2024.

The 2023 decline was linked to a 73.8% drop in wafer cleaning equipment sales, and the 2024 recovery was attributed to the easing of delayed SK hynix fab equipment investment, which normalized the equipment business.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.12 billion in 2022, a net loss of KRW 25.6 billion in 2023, KRW 10.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 4.81 billion in 2025, down 52.3%.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 18.2 billion in 2025, up from KRW 14.4 billion in 2024, so the gap between the two figures widened as the non-controlling share grew.

At the end of 2025, total equity was KRW 223.7 billion, of which KRW 129.3 billion belonged to controlling shareholders, and total liabilities were KRW 159.6 billion; the debt ratio fell from 191.0% in 2022 to 71.4% in 2025.

Operating cash flow was KRW 23.2 billion in 2022, -KRW 12 billion in 2023, KRW 20.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 25.1 billion in 2025.

Quarterly, Q3 2025 revenue was KRW 67.9 billion with operating profit of KRW 8.23 billion; Q4 2025 revenue reached KRW 105.2 billion, up 54.9% quarter on quarter, while operating profit was KRW 7.07 billion, down 14.1%.

Q1 2026 revenue was KRW 98.8 billion (down 6.1%) and operating profit KRW 18.1 billion (up 156.1%); Q2 2026 revenue was KRW 140.9 billion (up 42.5%) and operating profit KRW 23.2 billion (up 28.2%); Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 114.3 billion (down 18.9%) and operating profit KRW 21.3 billion (down 8.4%, but up 158.4% year on year).

Q3 2026 net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 5.49 billion, down 43.5% quarter on quarter and up 1,477.8% year on year, and the last four quarters total KRW 26.4 billion. Because operating profit fell less than revenue in Q3, profitability improved relative to sales.

05

Industry analysis

Demand on the semiconductor side is directly tied to memory capex. Korea Investment & Securities said, after the second-quarter 2026 results, that LTC is widening its business into high-purity chemical supply systems and wafer cleaning equipment, and flagged the importance of expanding memory chip investment.

The 2024 recovery in cleaning equipment was linked to the easing of delayed SK hynix fab equipment investment, which suggests equipment demand is sensitive to customer fab timing. Cleaning equipment sales fell 73.8% in 2023, which was a backdrop to the revenue decline that year.

On the display side, PR stripper deliveries tied to the QD-OLED process transition supported first-quarter 2026 results.

In cycle terms, the business has been in a recovery phase from the 2023 trough through 2026, but if chipmaker investment slows, equipment revenue could fall as sharply as it did in 2023, so the durability of the recovery depends on customer investment plans.

Among peers, the company is compared with Foosung, Lake Materials and ENF Technology in semiconductor and display chemical materials. A securities analyst cited the combination of materials and equipment as a strength.

Display process material sales fell 3.5% in 2024, showing little growth, while semiconductor materials and equipment led growth.

Outlook

The expansion plan confirmed in company disclosures is a KRW 10 billion new facility investment announced on July 24, 2025, for semiconductor materials R&D and production facilities. The disclosed investment period ran through March 31, 2026, and the amount equaled 5.27% of equity.

A securities analyst, citing a July 2025 report, described the company as having secured medium- to long-term growth drivers through materials and equipment, and said a stable growth path through 2027 was likely.

In coverage of pre-release estimates for third-quarter 2026, one securities firm's forecast cited expanding new fab equipment as the basis for what the article called record results next year.

On shareholder returns, the company said in 2025 that it had preemptively carried out share cancellation and a bonus issue, and it referred to expanded in-kind dividends, a longer special cash dividend period and possible further share cancellation.

The company also described shareholder protection options tied to a planned listing of its subsidiary LSE. Whether memory investment expansion and the QD-OLED transition continue will be the key variables for orders and sales.

The next periodic report, the 2026 annual report, will settle full-year operating profit and year-end capital structure. The next checkpoint after that is whether first-quarter 2027 results continue the recent quarterly trend.

Bull factors

Memory capex and expanding cleaning equipment

The first-quarter 2026 earnings commentary said wafer cleaning equipment sales rose on large investments by chipmakers. Korea Investment & Securities said, after the second-quarter 2026 results, that memory investment expansion and new growth businesses deserve attention. Wafer cleaning equipment sales rose 574.4% in 2024, the largest recovery among the segments.

Quarterly profit recovery

Operating profit was KRW 18.1 billion in Q1 2026, KRW 23.2 billion in Q2 and KRW 21.3 billion in Q3, all above KRW 18 billion. Operating profit moved from a KRW 16 billion loss in 2023 to KRW 29.6 billion in 2025. Q2 2026 revenue of KRW 140.9 billion rose 129.7% from a year earlier, according to a securities analysis.

Business expansion and shareholder returns

The company supplies the C.C.S.S high-purity chemical supply system in Korea and overseas, widening its materials and equipment mix. In 2025 the company said it had carried out share cancellation and a bonus issue and was considering further share cancellation. Subsequent implementation can be confirmed through DART disclosures.

Bear factors

Volatility in quarterly results

Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 114.3 billion fell 18.9% quarter on quarter, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 5.49 billion fell 43.5%. That net profit for 2025 was KRW 4.81 billion, down 52.3% year on year. In 2023 revenue fell 48.3% and the company posted an operating loss, showing how sharply results can swing.

Concentration and customer dependence

Growth in 2024 was concentrated in equipment and semiconductor materials, while display process materials fell 3.5%. In the first quarter of 2025, wafer cleaning equipment accounted for KRW 40.8 billion of KRW 75.9 billion in sales, more than half. A delay in customer fab investment could cause a sharp revenue drop like the one in 2023.

Non-controlling interests and multiple interpretation

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 18.2 billion in 2025, but net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was only KRW 4.81 billion. Total liabilities were KRW 159.6 billion at year-end 2025 against total equity of KRW 223.7 billion.

The debt ratio fell from 191.0% in 2022 to 71.4% in 2025, but how the multiples read depends on how far the earnings recovery reaches controlling shareholders.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and capex cycle

Sales of cleaning equipment and semiconductor materials move with the capex schedules of major Korean chipmakers. As the 73.8% drop in equipment sales in 2023 showed, investment delays can translate quickly into lower revenue. Heavy reliance on a few customers makes changes in their investment plans a major risk factor.

Export exposure, FX and regulation

Exports accounted for 23.71% of product sales in 2025, so the company is partly exposed to overseas demand and currency moves. Because the business handles chemicals, tighter environmental rules could affect production and raw material costs. The size of any regulatory impact would depend on the business mix.

Governance and subsidiary listing

The wider gap between net profit including non-controlling interests and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders in 2025 reflects a larger non-controlling share.

Because shareholder protection measures were discussed in connection with a planned LSE listing, changes in the subsidiary's ownership structure could affect the share of profit going to controlling shareholders. The continuity of shareholder return policy depends on company decisions.

Overall view

LTC combines display and semiconductor process chemicals with wafer cleaning equipment, and after an operating loss in 2023 its operating profit held in the KRW 18–23 billion range through the first three quarters of 2026, broadening its earnings base.

Revenue of KRW 310.4 billion and operating profit of KRW 29.6 billion in 2025 made it the largest revenue year in the 2022–2025 window, with an operating margin of 9.5%.

Strengths include demand for cleaning equipment tied to memory investment, PR stripper deliveries tied to the QD-OLED transition, and business expansion into high-purity chemical supply systems.

The main weakness is volatility in quarterly sales: Q3 2026 revenue fell 18.9% quarter on quarter, and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell 52.3% in 2025.

Revenue could drop sharply as in 2023 if customer capex slows, and exports at 23.71% of product sales leave the company exposed to overseas demand and currency moves.

The gap between net profit including non-controlling interests and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders shows a larger non-controlling share, so how far the recovery reaches controlling shareholders is the key variable.

Valuation reads against the last four quarters of earnings and a premium to net assets, and its interpretation depends on whether the earnings recovery lasts. The next checkpoints are the Q4 and full-year 2026 results, the 2026 annual report, and the Q1 2027 results.
