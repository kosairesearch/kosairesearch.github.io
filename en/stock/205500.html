02

Business structure

Nexus (NEXUS) develops and supplies game software and runs a blockchain- and AI-based global platform business.

On June 29, 2026, it acquired an 89.03% stake in app marketplace ONEstore for KRW 62.6 billion and consolidated it as a subsidiary, with ONEstore's three months of results first appearing in the Q3 2026 consolidated financials.

ONEstore centers on game distribution and payments, and in August 2026 it launched 'ONEstore Global' in 122 countries. At the end of September it opened its AI game section to all developers, supporting in-app payments and ad monetization.

Nexus is also pushing a game item trading service, ONEitem, and short-drama, webtoon and web-novel content. Its blockchain infrastructure, ONEchain, includes ONEstore, DSRV and HashKey Cloud among its validators, along with other domestic and overseas firms.

A Dubai government media agency signed an MOU with the company on game industry development and global cooperation. As of September 14, 2026, the largest shareholder group held 16.69%, SK Square 14.49%, CEO Jang Hyun-guk 9.70% and NAVER 5.55%.

Segment revenue shares are not publicly available, and the company has not separately disclosed ONEstore's contribution to Q3 revenue. In domestic app distribution, the company competes for developers and users with global platform operators such as Google and Apple.

04

Earnings analysis

In the annual results, revenue was KRW 5.76 billion in 2022 and KRW 5.46 billion in 2023, down 5.2%, before rising to KRW 7.55 billion in 2024, up 38.3%, and KRW 36.7 billion in 2025, up 386.3%. Revenue has grown for two consecutive years since the 2023 low, and 2025 was the largest year in the 2022–2025 period.

Operating losses widened from KRW 1.51 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.62 billion in 2023, narrowed to KRW 0.894 billion in 2024, and turned into an operating profit of KRW 1.39 billion in 2025, a 3.8% operating margin.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders shrank from KRW 1.85 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.2 billion in 2023, KRW 0.822 billion in 2024 and KRW 0.333 billion in 2025, but the company has recorded losses for four consecutive years.

Total equity at end-2025 was KRW 30.1 billion and total liabilities KRW 43.9 billion, with the debt ratio rising to 145.9% from 30.8% at end-2024. Operating cash flow was negative in each year, at KRW -7.84 billion, KRW -13.7 billion, KRW -11.2 billion and KRW -8.79 billion.

Quarterly revenue fell to KRW 5.79 billion in Q2 2026 and then jumped to KRW 32.9 billion in Q3 2026, which the company attributed mainly to the ONEstore consolidation. Operating losses, however, were KRW 5.15 billion in Q2 and KRW 4.76 billion in Q3, two consecutive quarters of losses.

The Q3 net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 12.9 billion followed a KRW 8.59 billion profit in Q2, which included a KRW 15.7 billion bargain purchase gain from the ONEstore acquisition. The last four quarters' net losses attributable to controlling shareholders total KRW 941 million. Press reports put platform revenue at KRW 9.45 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 3.57 billion in Q2 2026.

05

Industry analysis

Nexus's business combines three pillars: a game distribution and payment platform, AI game distribution and blockchain infrastructure. Game distribution and payments are a market in which transaction volume depends on how many developers and users an app marketplace operator can attract.

Domestic app distribution competes with global platform operators, and ONEstore has chosen to extend its overseas distribution network from a base of domestic developers. The company is trying to expand content supply by distributing AI-made games through a dedicated tab and section.

This approach lowers entry barriers for developers, which can be read as a change on the platform supply side. In blockchain infrastructure, validators are spread across domestic and overseas firms, so cooperation with network operators becomes central to the business.

The cooperation with a Dubai government agency can be read as an effort to link local developers to a distribution network in overseas game markets.

Among IT and software companies listed in Korea, NAVER and Kakao, which run platforms and content together, are natural reference points, but Nexus's focus on game distribution, payments and blockchain infrastructure limits direct comparison.

06

Outlook

In its Q2 2026 results announcement in August, the company said revenue growth and operating margin improvement would appear from Q3. Revenue did jump in Q3, but operating losses continued, so whether this outlook materializes should be checked against operating results after Q4.

The CEO also presented the possibility of ONEstore reaching a monthly breakeven in September and turning profitable in Q4, which can be checked in the Q4 2026 results.

Since launching ONEstore Global, the company has been widening its service scope step by step, and it is pushing to expand ONEitem to global and iOS service. It also plans to merge its short-drama service with ONEstore's content app into a single entertainment app covering games, webtoons, web novels and short dramas.

The cooperation with the Dubai agency covers support for local developers entering overseas markets, studio development and talent training. At the extraordinary shareholders' meeting on November 16, 2026, the company will consider an amendment to rename itself ONE Corp.

The CEO said the company will keep pursuing growth through M&A while strengthening organizational capability through AI transformation, and the financial impact of further acquisitions will need to be watched.

The next scheduled check point is how the 2026 annual report sets out full-year operating results and the effect of the ONEstore consolidation.

08

Bull factors

Revenue expansion and platform reach

Q3 2026 revenue was KRW 32.9 billion, up 257.4% year over year, and cumulative revenue for January–September reached KRW 50.27 billion, up 100.1%, according to the November 3, 2026 release. The ONEstore consolidation drove the step-up in the consolidated scale.

ONEstore launched global service in 122 countries in August 2026 and opened an AI game section that broadens developers' monetization routes.

Narrower quarterly operating loss

The Q3 2026 operating loss was KRW 4.76 billion, narrower than the KRW 5.15 billion loss in Q2. Press reports put the operating margin at -88.9% in Q2 and -14.5% in Q3. In August 2026 the CEO had expected revenue growth and operating improvement from Q3, and the Q3 revenue increase moved in that direction.

Blockchain infrastructure and overseas partnerships

ONEchain has domestic and overseas firms as validators, including ONEstore, HashKey Cloud and DSRV. Nexus also signed an MOU with a Dubai government media agency on game industry development and global business cooperation.

The company presents these partnerships as a base for widening content supply and network operations on its platform.

09

Bear factors

Continued operating losses

The Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 4.76 billion compares with an operating profit of KRW 311 million a year earlier. Cumulative January–September operating results swung from a KRW 844 million profit to a KRW 9.77 billion loss.

Higher personnel and publishing costs tied to the ONEstore acquisition have been cited as a driver of the operating loss.

Fading one-off gain and wider net losses

Q2 2026's net profit attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 8.59 billion included a KRW 15.7 billion bargain purchase gain from the ONEstore acquisition. That one-off gain did not recur in Q3, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 12.9 billion.

Cash flow and balance sheet burden

The debt ratio at end-2025 was 145.9%, higher than 30.8% at end-2024. The KRW 62.6 billion ONEstore stake purchase and the CEO's stated focus on M&A-driven growth make the financial burden a point to watch.

10

Risk factors

Earnings and profitability

Operating losses have continued for two consecutive quarters, Q2 and Q3 2026. The operating improvement the CEO projected from Q3 still sits in loss territory. If the possible Q4 turnaround at ONEstore does not materialize, the loss period could extend.

Financing and balance sheet

The debt ratio was 145.9% at end-2025, and operating cash flow has been negative for four consecutive years. Continued acquisitions and M&A would increase funding needs. Depending on how the funding is raised, the shareholding structure could also be affected.

Disclosure and divergent interpretations

The ONEstore contribution to Q3 revenue and the revenue and profitability of the new businesses have not been separately disclosed. Press accounts of Q2 platform revenue diverge.

Bloter reported a fall from KRW 9.45 billion in Q1 to KRW 3.57 billion in Q2, while Economist reported a large increase in platform revenue after the ONEchain 2.0 upgrade.

What to watch next

  1. November 16, 2026

    Whether the extraordinary shareholders' meeting approves the articles amendment to rename the company ONE Corp. This is the formal step in the plan to tie the business and brand more closely to ONEstore.

  2. End of March 2027

    The filing deadline for the 2026 annual report. Check how full-year operating results, a full year of ONEstore results and one-off items such as the bargain purchase gain are presented.

  3. Early 2027 (confirm the release date on DART)

    Whether the Q4 2026 results show the monthly breakeven at ONEstore and the possible Q4 turnaround that the company had pointed to. Operating profit and net profit attributable to controlling shareholders are the reference points.

  4. Mid-May 2027 (around the quarterly report deadline)

    Q1 2027 is the first quarter in which ONEstore appears in both the current and prior-year periods, making it the first like-for-like year-over-year comparison. Check whether revenue and operating results improve on that basis.

12

Overall view

Nexus's revenue rose to KRW 32.9 billion in Q3 2026 after the ONEstore acquisition, a large step-up in scale. Operating loss of KRW 4.76 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 12.9 billion continued, so profitability improvement remains limited.

On an annual basis, 2025 turned to an operating profit of KRW 1.39 billion, but losses have continued for two consecutive quarters since Q2 2026. Operating cash flow was negative every year from 2022 to 2025, and the debt ratio rose to 145.9% at end-2025.

Bullish factors include the larger revenue base, the ONEstore Global launch, blockchain validator participation and overseas partnerships. Bearish factors include continued operating losses, the disappearance of the one-off acquisition gain in Q2, and undisclosed profitability of the new businesses.

The company is pursuing operating improvement after Q4 and a rename to strengthen the link between its businesses. Both sets of factors coexist, so the direction of operating profit in the next disclosures is the key checkpoint.
