Samwha Electric: Checking the High-Value Shift Amid a Profit Rebound

Samwha Electric's operating profit peaked at KRW 24.8 billion in 2024 and fell to KRW 12 billion in 2025, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders rebounded in the third quarter of 2026 even as revenue stayed flat in the mid-KRW 50 billion range.

    Revenue in 2025 was KRW 230.1 billion, down 1.3% year on year, and operating profit was KRW 12 billion, down 51.4%, with the company citing higher raw material prices that lifted cost of sales and expenses.

  2. 2

  3. 3

02

Business structure

Samwha Electric is an electronic components maker whose core products are electrolytic capacitors and GREEN-CAP capacitors. According to the production data in the 2025 annual business report, the Cheongju plant produced 233,111 thousand pieces of electrolytic capacitors and 1,405 thousand pieces of GREEN-CAP.

Overseas, the company consolidates Tianjin Samwha Electric Co., Ltd. (55.00% stake) and lists PT.SAMWHA INDONESIA as a consolidated subsidiary. In the same report, the average utilization rate was 54.64% for electrolytic capacitors and 61.18% for GREEN-CAP, with a combined rate of 54.68%.

The company has said it is strengthening competitiveness in 5G communications and automotive applications through quality and technology leadership. A summary from Wisereport, however, cited intensifying global competition and low-priced imports from Southeast Asia and China as reasons for sales declines.

The company has presented a higher share of high-value products as its strategic direction.

In a May 2025 report, Heungkuk Securities cited the S-CAP product line, co-developed with Samsung Electronics, as an example of high-value items, and said the company was reportedly developing a next-generation S-CAP with the same customer.

The customer mix can be framed as commodity appliance parts on one side and higher-value products for automotive and communications on the other.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed basis, annual revenue fell from KRW 239.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 202.5 billion in 2023, down 15.3% year on year, then recovered to KRW 233.1 billion in 2024, up 15.1%, before slipping to KRW 230.1 billion in 2025, down 1.3%. Revenue was largest in 2022 and smallest in 2023.

Operating profit was KRW 13.6 billion in 2022, KRW 7.83 billion in 2023 (down 42.6%), KRW 24.8 billion in 2024 (up 216.6%) and KRW 12 billion in 2025 (down 51.4%).

Operating margin moved through 5.7%, 3.9%, 10.6% and 5.2%, with 2024 the highest of the four years, when a 15.1% revenue increase came with a margin improvement.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 10.7 billion in 2022, KRW 5.56 billion in 2023 (down 47.8%), KRW 19.6 billion in 2024 (up 253.1%) and KRW 9.44 billion in 2025 (down 51.9%).

Net income including non-controlling interests was KRW 11.1 billion in 2025, a different figure, with the gap reflecting non-controlling interests. For 2025, the company attributed the decline to higher raw material prices that raised cost of sales and expenses.

By quarter, the third quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 54 billion, operating profit of KRW 3.09 billion and net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 1.8 billion.

The fourth quarter of 2025 showed revenue of KRW 55.1 billion, operating profit of KRW 2.44 billion (down 21.0% quarter on quarter) and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 1.85 billion (up 2.7%).

In the first quarter of 2026, revenue reached KRW 57.4 billion (up 4.2%) and operating profit KRW 3.38 billion (up 38.5%), but the second quarter fell to revenue of KRW 53.6 billion (down 6.7%), operating profit of KRW 1.37 billion (down 59.6%) and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 1.14 billion (down 17.6%).

The third quarter of 2026 showed revenue of KRW 55.2 billion (up 2.2% year on year), operating profit of KRW 1.82 billion (down 41.0% year on year) and controlling-shareholder net income of KRW 3 billion (up 66.8% year on year).

The composition of the gap between operating profit and controlling-shareholder net income is a point to verify in the notes to the next annual report.

05

Industry analysis

The capacitor market in which Samwha Electric operates is shifting its weight toward demand for high-performance electronics. The 2026 annual report expects structural change to accelerate amid supply chain restructuring, intensifying technology competition and policy shifts in major economies.

In an October 2026 report, Hana Securities said MLCC, FCBGA and BGA substrates at Samsung Electro-Mechanics were entering a supply-shortage cycle. Daishin Securities also noted that shortages of AI-related components were intensifying at Samsung Electro-Mechanics over the same period.

These trends concern high-performance components and should be distinguished from demand for the electrolytic capacitors that Samwha Electric mainly produces. In a May 2025 report, Heungkuk Securities said commodity products in domestic appliances contributed to sales through pull-in demand ahead of tariff uncertainty.

Wisereport's summary, by contrast, noted that low-priced imports from Southeast Asia and China had intensified price competition in commodity segments. Samwha Electric sits between these two areas, needing to defend commodity volume while raising the share of high-value products.

Direct utilization comparisons against listed peers such as Samsung Electro-Mechanics, LG Innotek and Korea Circuit are not included in this report.

06

Outlook

In its 2026 annual report, the company said it would pursue quality growth through preemptive investment and a higher share of high-value products. Actual capital expenditure for the capacitor segment in 2025 was 3,186 million won, below the planned 3,848 million won, and machinery investment was 2,634 million won.

The gap versus plan may reflect timing adjustments, though the disclosure does not state the cause. With utilization at 54.68% in 2025, the company has positioned a shift in product mix ahead of capacity expansion.

Operating profit of KRW 1.82 billion in the third quarter of 2026 recovered from the prior quarter but remained below the year-earlier level, so whether the rebound continues needs confirmation.

Whether the next-generation S-CAP developed with a major customer turns into mass-production revenue has not yet appeared in disclosed figures. The next checkpoint is the 2026 annual report, where full-year revenue and operating margin can be compared with KRW 230.1 billion and 5.2% in 2025. Whether revenue from high-value products becomes a separately disclosed item should also be watched.

Bull factors

Shift toward high-value products

In its 2026 annual report, the company said it would pursue quality growth through preemptive investment and a higher share of high-value products. In May 2025, Heungkuk Securities cited the S-CAP line, co-developed with Samsung Electronics, as an example of high-value products.

If sales of these products grow, operating margin could recover even while total revenue stays flat, which is the business logic behind this strategy.

Third-quarter 2026 profit rebound

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders in the third quarter of 2026 was KRW 3 billion, with a larger increase than operating profit in the same quarter. Operating profit also rose 33.2% from the prior quarter to KRW 1.82 billion, recovering from the KRW 1.37 billion low in the second quarter. Whether this quarterly momentum carries forward is the starting point for judging the earnings recovery.

Quality competitiveness in 5G and automotive

The company has said it is strengthening competitiveness in 5G communications and automotive applications through quality and technology leadership. These fields require high quality and performance characteristics, where technical capability tends to weigh more than price. The company's position is that this technical strength acts as a defense even as 2025 revenue slipped 1.3%.

Bear factors

Flat revenue and low-price competition

Revenue declined from KRW 239.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 230.1 billion in 2025, and fell 15.3% in 2023. A Wisereport summary cited intensifying global competition and low-priced imports from Southeast Asia and China as reasons for the decline. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 55.2 billion rose only 2.2% year on year.

Cost pressure and quarterly earnings swings

Operating profit fell 51.4% to KRW 12 billion in 2025, and the company attributed this to higher cost of sales and expenses from raw material price increases. Operating profit in the second quarter of 2026 fell 59.6% from the prior quarter to KRW 1.37 billion, showing large quarterly swings. If cost increases cannot be passed through to prices, margin recovery could be limited.

Low utilization and diverging profit measures

The average utilization rate in the business report stood at 54.68%, indicating spare capacity. Operating profit in the third quarter of 2026 fell 41.0% year on year, while net income attributable to controlling shareholders rose 66.8%, so the two measures diverged.

Because operating-level profitability is still recovering, the rebound in controlling-shareholder net income alone is not enough to establish a trend.

10

Risk factors

Raw materials and cost structure

The company itself cited higher raw material prices as a main cause of the 2025 earnings decline. If cost of sales and expenses rise without a matching increase in revenue, operating margin could repeatedly settle around 5%. The 3.9% margin in 2023 and 5.2% in 2025 fall within that range.

Reliance on overseas production subsidiaries

Tianjin Samwha Electric Co., Ltd. is a consolidated subsidiary with a 55.00% stake, and PT.SAMWHA INDONESIA is also disclosed as a subsidiary. Operating conditions at overseas plants and changes in local regulation directly affect consolidated results.

If low-priced import competition intensifies, price pressure on overseas-produced volumes could flow through to consolidated revenue.

Earnings volatility and a thin profit base

Operating profit of KRW 24.8 billion in 2024 fell to KRW 12 billion in 2025, less than half within a year. Second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 53.6 billion dropped 6.7% from the prior quarter, reflecting frequent reversals in quarterly results.

With net income attributable to controlling shareholders at KRW 7.37 billion over the last four quarters, one-off factors could move reported results materially.

Overall view

Samwha Electric's operating profit peaked at KRW 24.8 billion in 2024 and fell to KRW 12 billion in 2025, with revenue flat around KRW 230 billion. Raw material cost pressure and low-priced import competition are the main backdrop to the decline.

On the other hand, net income attributable to controlling shareholders reached KRW 3 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up sharply from the prior quarter, which signals an earnings rebound. Operating profit fell year on year in the same quarter, so the two measures diverged and a clear trend is not yet established.

The shift toward high-value products and technical strength in 5G and automotive are presented as strengths, but revenue growth and higher utilization are not yet visible in disclosed figures.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totals KRW 7.37 billion, below the 2024 full-year level, which places the earnings base in the middle of a recovery. The next checkpoints are the preliminary fourth-quarter 2026 results and the 2026 annual report.
