02

Business structure

GH Advanced Materials is mainly engaged in manufacturing and selling felt and nonwoven fabrics for automotive interiors, with production plants in Gumi, Gyeongju, Dunpo, and Pocheon.

Its segments are nonwovens, PU Foam, yarn, and overseas pallet operations, and key products include Floor Carpet, ISD Dash, PU Foam, and PET S/F. In January 2025 the company transferred its PU Foam business, which changed its business structure.

The company said in its 2026 Q3 results announcement that the Gumi division improved profitability by expanding the sales share of CFM, a functional material for video appliances, and raising yields.

The Pocheon division is pursuing revenue growth and cost reduction together by producing WSS filters for semiconductor cleanrooms in-house.

In the first half of 2026 the Pocheon division's revenue rose 25.3% year on year to KRW 718.6 million, and its operating profit rose 129.7% to KRW 90.1 million, according to a Hankyung report dated August 14, 2026.

The company said the Gumi division's automotive nonwovens and fiber materials maintained solid performance. Its filings include a cleanroom VOC adsorption filter developed as a substitute for Japanese materials.

Overseas, the U.S. subsidiary GH AMERICA INCORPORATION (GHA) is preparing local production of hybrid pallets made of wood pallets coated with polyurea, using a coating technology co-developed with BASF. Company profile materials also describe expansion into the Life segment through non-automotive nonwoven development.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue moved from KRW 86.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 88.8 billion in 2023, KRW 83.7 billion in 2024, and KRW 87.6 billion in 2025. 2023 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest within 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit rose from KRW 3.22 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.7 billion in 2023 (up 45.8%) and KRW 7.67 billion in 2024 (up 63.3%), making 2024 the largest year in the period. In 2025 operating profit fell 10.7% to KRW 6.84 billion even as revenue rose 4.7%.

The operating margin went from 3.7% in 2022 to 5.3% in 2023, 9.2% in 2024, and 7.8% in 2025, peaking in 2024.

Controlling-shareholder net income reached KRW 5.17 billion in 2024 (up 140.8%), but in 2025 it swung to a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 4.96 billion, and net income including non-controlling interests swung to a net loss of KRW 3.98 billion.

The controlling-shareholder loss being larger than the total net loss means non-controlling interests contributed positively to the bottom line. The reason for the gap between positive operating profit and net losses should be examined in the 2025 annual report notes and non-operating items.

The debt ratio jumped from 58.1% in 2022 to 103.7% in 2023 and stood at 105.9% at the end of 2025, with total liabilities of KRW 99.6 billion against total equity of KRW 94.1 billion.

Operating cash flow was KRW 584 million in 2022, a negative KRW 2.07 billion in 2023, KRW 1.3 billion in 2024, and KRW 3.67 billion in 2025.

Quarterly results show that in 2025 Q4 revenue rose 4.4% quarter on quarter to KRW 22.7 billion, yet operating profit fell 65.0% to KRW 794 million and the company posted a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 7.31 billion.

From a low of KRW 21.1 billion in 2026 Q1 (down 7.2%), revenue rose to KRW 22.7 billion in Q2 and KRW 25 billion in Q3, two consecutive quarterly gains. Operating profit increased for three consecutive quarters, from KRW 2.03 billion in 2026 Q1 to KRW 3.43 billion in Q3.

However, controlling-shareholder net income in 2026 Q3 was KRW 1.44 billion, down 37.7% year on year, and the combined controlling-shareholder net loss for the last four quarters is KRW 3.35 billion.

Some media reported 2026 Q3 revenue of KRW 68.7 billion and operating profit of KRW 8.1 billion; these figures closely match the sum of the first three quarters of 2026 and appear to be cumulative.

05

Industry analysis

Automotive interior materials, the core market, see orders move with vehicle production volumes and model specifications. The company's own comments on 2025 results cited strong domestic and overseas auto markets as a driver of sales growth.

The company profile cited domestic and overseas auto market growth and a possible recovery in non-automotive demand after the end of the war in Ukraine, as of the 2025 Q3 profile.

The R&D list in its filings focuses on lightweighting, sound absorption, and cost reduction, so performance and cost are the main variables among automotive parts suppliers.

The semiconductor cleanroom filter line began as a development to replace Japanese materials and has moved into in-house production, linking it to semiconductor equipment investment cycles.

On the U.S. pallet market, company management said wood pallets account for more than 90% of the market and that it aims to expand share with hybrid pallets that emphasize eco-friendliness and durability.

Auto demand and semiconductor capital spending can move at different times, which reduces the portfolio's dependence on one cycle. A comparison of operating margins with listed peers becomes meaningful only after peer filings are aligned on the same basis.

06

Outlook

The company's stated medium-term direction is to strengthen profitability by expanding the share of high-value products and improving production efficiency.

For its U.S. subsidiary GHA, the board on August 19, 2026 approved participation in a rights offering, with an acquisition amount of KRW 8.57 billion and a 60.71% stake after the purchase.

The stated purpose is to fund local production facilities and operating capital, with financing from new borrowings and existing credit lines. The total GHA capital raise is USD 10 million, and GHA's paid-in capital will rise from USD 19 million to USD 29 million once completed.

The CEO said the company will expand its market position in the U.S. wood pallet market, where wood accounts for more than 90% of pallets, emphasizing the eco-friendliness and durability of hybrid pallets.

On September 15, 2026 the board approved a loan guarantee of KRW 24.2 billion for the extension of a Korea Development Bank loan to the U.S. affiliate 3E Tech LLC, with the guarantee period running from September 23, 2026 to September 23, 2027.

The borrower, 3E Tech, is a U.S. pallet business, and as of the end of 2025 its total liabilities were KRW 60.7 billion and total equity KRW 12.8 billion on a consolidated basis. In the domestic business, expansion of in-house WSS production was cited as a driver of first-half 2026 growth.

The next regular checkpoint is how the 2026 annual report records the U.S. subsidiary's profit contribution and the outstanding guarantee balance.

08

Bull factors

Operating profit up for three straight quarters

Operating profit rose from KRW 2.03 billion in 2026 Q1 to KRW 2.6 billion in Q2 and KRW 3.43 billion in Q3, three consecutive quarterly gains. Q3 operating profit was up 51.6% year on year. Revenue also rose for two consecutive quarters beginning in Q2, reaching KRW 25 billion in Q3. The company attributes the gains to a higher share of high-value products and production efficiency.

In-house WSS production lifts profitability

According to a Hankyung report, the Pocheon division's operating profit in the first half of 2026 rose 129.7% to KRW 90.1 million, with its operating margin improving from 6.9% to 12.6%. WSS filters for cleanrooms have moved from external sourcing toward in-house production, changing the cost structure. The company presents this as a base for scaling the business.

U.S. hybrid pallet production preparations advance

GH Advanced Materials invested KRW 8.57 billion to secure a 60.71% stake in its U.S. subsidiary GHA, with the proceeds going to local plant facilities and operating funds. The hybrid pallet uses a coating technology co-developed with BASF. The company has said it aims to enter a U.S. wood pallet market it describes as large.

09

Bear factors

2025 swing to loss and recent net losses

The controlling-shareholder net loss in 2025 was KRW 4.96 billion, and the net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 3.98 billion, a swing into loss. The combined controlling-shareholder net loss for the last four quarters is KRW 3.35 billion, and in 2025 Q4 it reached KRW 7.31 billion.

Controlling-shareholder net income in 2026 Q3 fell 37.7% year on year to KRW 1.44 billion, so operating gains have not carried through to the bottom line.

Leverage and overseas loan guarantee burden

The debt ratio was 103.7% in 2023 and stood at 105.9% at the end of 2025, staying above 100%. The guarantee approved in September 2026 totals KRW 24.2 billion, equal to 25.74% of equity.

The GHA capital raise is to be financed with new borrowings and existing credit lines, which could increase reliance on external funding.

Revenue swings and limited cash generation

Revenue in 2026 Q1 fell 7.2% quarter on quarter to KRW 21.1 billion, and operating profit fell 65.0% in 2025 Q4. Operating cash flow was negative KRW 2.07 billion in 2023 and only KRW 3.67 billion in 2025.

Because revenue follows automotive demand and cash generation is modest, room to absorb borrowing costs is limited.

10

Risk factors

Financial and guarantees

With the debt ratio above 100%, the KRW 24.2 billion guarantee for the U.S. affiliate could raise consolidated debt burden. The guarantee runs to September 23, 2027, and its size will depend on whether the underlying borrowing is extended. Continued net losses could reduce financial flexibility.

Overseas new business execution

The payback period and scale of the U.S. production investment are difficult to gauge from currently disclosed figures. Because the hybrid pallet targets replacement of wood pallets in the U.S., customer wins and price competitiveness will need to be proven. Larger overseas losses or additional capital injections could offset domestic profit gains.

Auto demand and business concentration

Because automotive interiors are the core business, results are sensitive to changes in vehicle production. Quarterly swings can be sizable, as seen in the revenue decline in 2026 Q1. How far non-automotive nonwovens and WSS filters reduce this concentration is the key point to monitor.

Overall view

Based on the 2026 Q3 filing, revenue was KRW 25 billion and operating profit KRW 3.43 billion, with operating profit improving for three consecutive quarters.

On an annual basis, however, 2025 brought a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 4.96 billion, and the last four quarters combined show a controlling-shareholder net loss of KRW 3.35 billion.

The debt ratio stood at 105.9% at the end of 2025, and a KRW 24.2 billion guarantee for a U.S. affiliate adds to leverage, so financial flexibility depends on how overseas investments play out.

On the business side, a higher share of high-value products such as CFM and WSS and preparation for U.S. hybrid pallet production are presented as medium-term growth drivers.

Bullish factors are the operating profit recovery and progress in new businesses; bearish factors are net losses, leverage, and uncertainty about when overseas investments pay back.

The price-to-book ratio sits well below 1 times, but with no earnings-based multiple available, the valuation reading depends on whether net income recovers.

The next checkpoints are the 2026 annual report's full-year net results and non-operating items, the WSS and CFM revenue trend in the 2027 Q1 quarterly report, and the end of the 3E Tech guarantee period on September 23, 2027.
