Report overview

Union Korea Pharm Restructures Under Bukwang After Rehabilitation

Union Korea Pharm, which entered court-led corporate rehabilitation in September 2025, was acquired by Bukwang Pharmaceutical and renamed Bukwang Unipharma in October 2026, and while revenue has declined and operating losses have persisted, net income attributable to controlling interests turned positive for the first time in the second quarter of 2026.

02

Business structure

Union Korea Pharm has been listed on KOSDAQ since July 26, 2018, and has manufactured antibiotics, injectables, and solid dosage products at its No. 1 and No. 2 plants in Munmak Industrial Complex, Wonju, Gangwon Province.

The company's cephalosporin antibiotic production facility, which requires dedicated processes due to strict cross-contamination controls, has been regarded as a source of stable revenue in that segment.

After entering a loss-making phase in 2020, the company shifted its sales organization to a contract sales organization (CSO) model to cut fixed costs, but structural problems emerged as commission expenses kept rising even as top-line growth continued.

Following financial deterioration triggered by embezzlement and breach-of-trust allegations against former management, the company filed for court-led rehabilitation with the Seoul Rehabilitation Court in September 2025 and received an opening decision the same month.

Through a stalking-horse public sale process, Bukwang Pharmaceutical was selected as the final acquirer in January 2026, with the KRW 30 billion acquisition price paid in full through a third-party capital increase.

Bukwang cited the acquisition of Union Korea Pharm's antibiotic and liquid injectable facilities as a way to expand its total manufacturing capacity by roughly 30% and more than double its liquid injectable capacity, as its existing Ansan plant had reached capacity limits.

Through the deal, Union Korea Pharm became a grandchild company of OCI Holdings and a consolidated subsidiary of Bukwang Pharmaceutical.

After the rehabilitation proceeding formally concluded in late August 2026 and a sequence of debt-to-equity conversion, a 3-for-1 reverse merger capital reduction, and a capital increase were completed, shareholders approved a corporate name change to Bukwang Unipharma Co. at an extraordinary general meeting on October 8, 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 61.3 billion in 2022 to a peak of KRW 63.2 billion in 2023 (up 3.1% year on year), then declined for two straight years to KRW 51.3 billion in 2024 (down 18.8%) and KRW 36.4 billion in 2025 (down 29.1%).

Operating profit swung from KRW 1.27 billion in 2022 to an operating loss of KRW 5.23 billion in 2023, before losses widened further to KRW 13.5 billion in 2024 and KRW 17.2 billion in 2025, marking three consecutive years of operating losses.

The operating margin deteriorated from 2.1% in 2022 to -8.3% in 2023, -26.2% in 2024, and -47.4% in 2025, worsening faster than the pace of revenue decline.

Net income attributable to controlling interests posted losses for four consecutive years, from a KRW 932 million loss in 2022 to a KRW 30.8 billion loss in 2025, reflecting both widening operating losses and growing non-operating expense burdens.

On a quarterly basis, revenue slid from KRW 10.2 billion in the second quarter of 2025 to KRW 5.94 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (down 41.4% year on year), and the operating loss peaked at KRW 9.91 billion in the third quarter of 2025, immediately after the rehabilitation filing, the largest quarterly loss in the series.

The operating loss then narrowed for four consecutive quarters to KRW 2.65 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, KRW 1.57 billion in the first quarter of 2026, and KRW 1.25 billion in the second quarter of 2026.

In the second quarter of 2026, net income attributable to controlling interests turned positive at KRW 2.14 billion, swinging from loss both sequentially and year on year, but given that the operating loss of KRW 1.25 billion persisted in the same quarter, it is worth confirming whether non-operating factors tied to the rehabilitation and restructuring process contributed to the net profit swing.

The cumulative net loss attributable to controlling interests over the last four quarters (third quarter of 2025 through second quarter of 2026) was KRW 24.3 billion, meaning the company remains in a cumulative loss position on a trailing basis.

Operating cash flow improved gradually from -KRW 5.06 billion in 2022 to KRW 1.36 billion in 2023 and KRW 415 million in 2024, before turning negative again at -KRW 1.13 billion in 2025, indicating operating cash generation has not yet stabilized.

05

Industry analysis

South Korea's prescription drug manufacturing sector is populated by numerous small and mid-sized generic and contract manufacturing (CMO) players competing on price and plant utilization, and industry consolidation frequently occurs when larger pharmaceutical companies hit capacity limits and acquire or outsource to smaller manufacturers.

Union Korea Pharm's solid dosage facility utilization fell from 79% in 2022 to 63% in 2023 and 66% in 2024, then dropped further to 31% in 2025 and remained around 35% in the first quarter of 2026, well below installed capacity.

By contrast, acquirer Bukwang Pharmaceutical's Ansan plant has reportedly run above 100% utilization for several consecutive years, making the two companies' facility profiles complementary.

In this context, Bukwang is reportedly considering using Union Korea Pharm's idle capacity to take on additional third-party contract manufacturing volume, suggesting some room to absorb CMO demand within the industry.

However, the CSO-based sales structure the company previously adopted has at times been the subject of discussion around introducing a registration requirement for CSOs, and any future regulatory change in this area could affect the cost structure of smaller pharmaceutical companies.

Parent Bukwang Pharmaceutical, after coming under the OCI Holdings group, restructured its portfolio around central nervous system (CNS) products and achieved a consolidated operating profit turnaround in 2024, and whether that management approach can be applied successfully to Union Korea Pharm is a point of industry interest.

06

Outlook

At its March 2026 annual general meeting, Bukwang Pharmaceutical's management stated that regarding the Union Korea Pharm acquisition, the company was aiming for an operating profit turnaround within the year and, over the medium term, to restore revenue to the roughly KRW 50 billion level the company had historically achieved.

That target implies a substantial recovery from the KRW 36.4 billion in disclosed 2025 revenue, and whether it is achieved will need to be confirmed through upcoming quarterly results.

The company has outlined plans to link Bukwang's prescription drug sales network with Union Korea Pharm's production infrastructure, including internal transfer of existing outsourced production volume, expansion of third-party contract manufacturing orders, and broadening of the product portfolio.

After the rehabilitation proceeding concluded in late August 2026 and debt repayment through debt-to-equity conversion was completed, reports indicated the debt-to-equity ratio improved to around the 40% level.

At the extraordinary general meeting on October 8, 2026, shareholders approved the corporate name change along with governance-related agenda items including auditor appointment and director/auditor compensation limits, continuing the follow-up steps toward management normalization.

Even so, time will be needed before capacity utilization recovery and cost structure improvements are reflected in actual earnings, and since operating losses persisted through the second quarter of 2026, an operating-level turnaround has not yet been confirmed.

While the injunction suspending the delisting decision remains in effect, the outcome of the underlying lawsuit could still change the company's listing status and whether normal trading resumes, leaving this as an important variable.

Bull factors

Facility and Sales Synergies from Bukwang Integration

Bukwang Pharmaceutical has said the acquisition secures cephalosporin antibiotic and liquid injectable production facilities, targeting roughly a 30% increase in total manufacturing capacity and more than double the liquid injectable capacity.

Given that Bukwang's Ansan plant has run above 100% utilization for several years, leveraging Union Korea Pharm's underutilized facilities could help relieve that production bottleneck.

There is also potential for revenue recovery through expanded third-party contract manufacturing volume and linkage with Bukwang's existing sales network. However, confirming these synergies in actual earnings will require utilization gains and cost savings to show up in quarterly results.

Balance Sheet Cleanup Following the End of Rehabilitation

Reports indicate that after the rehabilitation proceeding formally concluded in late August 2026 and debt-to-equity conversion, reverse merger capital reduction, and capital increase were completed in sequence, the company's prior debt burden was substantially reduced.

Media reports also cited an improvement in the debt-to-equity ratio to around the 40% level. Bukwang Pharmaceutical has stated it completed the acquisition after all debts were settled under the rehabilitation plan.

However, it should also be considered that existing shareholders' stakes were significantly diluted during the capital restructuring process.

First Controlling-Interest Net Profit in Five Quarters

Net income attributable to controlling interests reached KRW 2.14 billion in the second quarter of 2026, breaking a streak of five consecutive quarterly losses dating back to the second quarter of 2025.

The operating loss also narrowed for four consecutive quarters after peaking at KRW 9.91 billion in the third quarter of 2025, shrinking to KRW 1.25 billion by the second quarter of 2026. The continuation of a loss-narrowing trend despite declining revenue points to a direction of cost structure improvement.

However, it is worth noting that the net profit turnaround was not accompanied by an operating profit turnaround.

Bear factors

Revenue Decline for Multiple Consecutive Years

Revenue peaked at KRW 63.2 billion in 2023, then fell for two straight years to KRW 51.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 36.4 billion in 2025, and in the second quarter of 2026 came in at only KRW 5.94 billion, down 41.4% year on year.

This is attributable to the contraction of sales networks and production activity during the prolonged ownership dispute and rehabilitation process. While a rebound has been targeted following the Bukwang acquisition, disclosed results through the second quarter of 2026 have not yet shown the revenue decline reversing. Additional quarters will be needed before a revenue recovery becomes visible.

Three Straight Years of Operating Losses and Weak Utilization

Operating results posted losses for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025, with the 2025 operating loss widening to KRW 17.2 billion from the prior year.

Solid dosage facility utilization was only 31% in 2025 and 35% in the first quarter of 2026, reflecting persistently underused capacity relative to installed facilities. Operating cash flow also turned negative again at -KRW 1.13 billion in 2025, indicating cash generation has not yet stabilized. If the recovery in utilization is delayed, fixed cost burdens could continue to pressure profitability.

Unresolved Delisting Risk

The KOSDAQ Market Committee resolved to delist the company on April 3, 2026, and the delisting and liquidating-trade procedures are currently suspended due to an injunction the company requested.

It has also been reported that if the court were to dismiss the injunction request, the company could be delisted immediately without a liquidating-trade period.

While management normalization steps such as the conclusion of rehabilitation and the corporate name change are proceeding, uncertainty over the listing status remains until the underlying lawsuit is resolved. Investors need to continue monitoring the progress of both the injunction and the underlying litigation.

Risk factors

Legal and Listing Risk

The injunction suspending the delisting decision remains in place only for now, and the exchange has indicated that if the company loses the underlying lawsuit, it could be delisted immediately without a liquidating-trade period. In that scenario, investors' ability to trade the shares could be effectively constrained. The timing of the court's final ruling has not yet been determined.

Capital Structure and Governance Risk

Existing shareholders' stakes were significantly diluted through the sequential debt-to-equity conversion, 3-for-1 reverse merger capital reduction, and third-party capital increase under the rehabilitation plan.

With Bukwang Pharmaceutical now the controlling shareholder holding a 75.14% stake, issues of alignment with minority shareholder interests could arise. Given that the capital structure has changed multiple times within a short period, care is also needed regarding the comparability of financial statements.

Business Integration Execution Risk

Bukwang Pharmaceutical's stated targets of an operating profit turnaround within the year and a medium-term revenue recovery to roughly KRW 50 billion are predicated on production transfers and utilization normalization proceeding as planned.

Given that operating losses persisted through the second quarter of 2026, achieving these targets may require additional time. Unexpected delays or costs could also arise during the transfer of contract manufacturing volume and the integration of quality management systems.

Overall view

Union Korea Pharm has undergone sweeping structural change through its 2025 rehabilitation filing, the 2026 acquisition by Bukwang Pharmaceutical, the conclusion of rehabilitation, and the corporate name change to Bukwang Unipharma.

Based on DART-disclosed figures, revenue declined for two consecutive years after peaking in 2023, operating results posted losses for three straight years from 2023 to 2025, and net income attributable to controlling interests recorded losses for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025.

On a quarterly basis, operating losses continued for five consecutive quarters from the second quarter of 2025 through the second quarter of 2026, though the loss size narrowed after peaking in the third quarter of 2025, and net income attributable to controlling interests turned positive in the second quarter of 2026.

However, since the operating loss persisted in the same quarter, whether this net profit swing reflects an underlying operating improvement requires confirmation over further quarters.

Bukwang Pharmaceutical has set targets of an early operating profit turnaround through facility integration and a medium-term revenue recovery to roughly KRW 50 billion, but achievement of these targets has not yet been demonstrated in actual results.

At the same time, legal uncertainty remains, as the listing status could change again depending on the outcome of the underlying lawsuit even while the injunction against the delisting decision remains in effect.

Overall, the company is in the midst of completing its financial restructuring and governance overhaul, at a stage where both the actual progress of business normalization and the resolution of its listing status need to be monitored together.
