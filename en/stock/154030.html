02

Business structure

Asia Seed breeds, produces and sells vegetable seeds. The company converted into an agricultural corporation in 2010 and moved its listing to KOSDAQ in 2018 (per the business report).

A company profile cited by FnGuide describes it as producing and selling vegetable seeds across 243 crops and 1,444 varieties, with particular strength in male-sterile cabbage and broccoli breeding and colored baby-leaf and sprout vegetables.

Seeds made up 86% of sales and products 14% in Q2 2026, according to Butler's compilation. Exports are also a core pillar. A 2023 report by Youngnong Jaejae News said export seeds accounted for 38% of sales, and cabbage seed is sold in Southeast Asia, Europe, Africa and the Americas.

In 2018 the company set up a subsidiary in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, and was described by Edaily that year as the second-largest vegetable seed exporter in Korea.

Its research base includes the Biotechnology Breeding Institute in Icheon, Gyeonggi, and in 2025 it was designated a collaborative farm with the National Institute of Agricultural Sciences. A report also says a domestic Chinese cabbage (pak choi) variety rose to the top grade in that seed market. In a 2023 report, the company said Japanese varieties hold a high share of the domestic vegetable seed market.

04

Earnings analysis

On a disclosed annual basis, sales rose from KRW 24 billion in 2022 to KRW 26.4 billion in 2023 (up 9.8% year on year), the peak year. Sales then fell to KRW 24.6 billion in 2024 (down 6.8%) and to KRW 3.52 billion in 2025 (down 85.7%), two consecutive years of decline after 2023. The 2025 figure needs a caveat.

The company changed its fiscal year-end from September to December at a shareholder meeting on December 30, 2025, and the 2025 annual amounts match the three-month financial statements for October to December 2025 shown in its March 2026 shareholder meeting materials.

The 85.7% decline therefore compares twelve months with three months and should not be read as a straight trend. Operating losses were KRW 1.6 billion in 2022, KRW 546 million in 2023, KRW 1.12 billion in 2024 and KRW 989 million in 2025, four consecutive years of losses.

The operating margin was -6.6%, -2.1%, -4.6% and -28.1%, with 2025 the largest loss relative to sales. Controlling shareholder net losses were KRW 605 million, KRW 2.45 billion, KRW 634 million and KRW 1.37 billion from 2022 to 2025, and in 2023 the net loss was much larger than the operating loss.

Net loss including non-controlling interests was reported at the same amounts. Operating cash flow was -KRW 2.42 billion in 2022, -KRW 258 million in 2023, KRW 754 million in 2024 and -KRW 535 million in 2025. The debt ratio rose from 75.8% in 2022 to 90.4% in 2023, then fell to 80.8% in 2024 and 72.2% in 2025.

Equity at the end of 2025 was KRW 24.1 billion, all attributable to controlling shareholders, against total liabilities of KRW 17.4 billion. Quarterly, the company posted an operating loss of KRW 201 million in Q3 2025 and KRW 898 million in Q4 2025, then swung to operating profit of KRW 1.22 billion in Q1 2026.

Q2 2026 sales were KRW 6.75 billion (down 23.6% quarter on quarter) with an operating loss of KRW 263 million. In Q3 2026 sales rose to KRW 7.5 billion (up 11.1% quarter on quarter) and the operating loss narrowed to KRW 6.5 million.

Controlling shareholder net profit moved from KRW 1.5 billion in Q1 2026 to net losses of KRW 293 million in Q2 and KRW 685 million in Q3, and the Q3 loss widened year on year.

05

Industry analysis

In vegetable seeds, varietal competitiveness largely determines sales. A 2023 report said Japanese varieties hold a high share of the domestic vegetable seed market, and the company views this as room for new domestic varieties to gain ground.

The same report described Asia Seed as the second-largest seed company in Korea. In the same year the CEO described the domestic and overseas seed industry as broadly in a difficult phase, while arguing that companies must keep developing new varieties to capture opportunities when the market recovers.

The company is also developing new varieties of tomato and onion, crops that are among the most widely grown worldwide. A report said a domestic pak choi variety reached top grade in a seed market that Japanese varieties had dominated.

Cabbage seed is sold in Southeast Asia, Europe, Africa and the Americas, so the export variety base spans several continents. For market position, the most useful indicators going forward are the number of variety applications and registrations and the expansion of overseas buyers.

Outlook

A report from autumn 2026 said the company completed variety protection applications for five early-maturing, marketable Chinese cabbage varieties (CC2503 to CC2507). Variety protection still goes through examination and registration, so the timing of any sales contribution is not specified in the sources.

The company said it is developing new tomato and onion varieties aimed at export markets (2023 report). The same report said the company aims to raise the share of overseas seed sales, with its Vietnam and India subsidiaries leading market development.

In 2025 it was designated a collaborative farm with the National Institute of Agricultural Sciences, which covers joint research projects and trial sites for new technologies. At the March 27, 2026 shareholder meeting, all three agenda items were approved, including director and auditor pay limits.

Following the change to a December year-end, the fiscal year now runs from January 1 to December 31, so 2026 is the first full twelve-month year on the new basis. This makes the 2026 business report the first clean annual comparison with 2024 and 2025.

The direction of quarterly profit will depend on how fast new varieties feed into sales and whether sharp quarterly sales drops such as Q2 2026 recur.

Bull factors

Seed-centered variety assets

Asia Seed is described as producing and selling 1,444 varieties across 243 crops, with strength in male-sterile cabbage and broccoli. Seeds generated 86% of Q2 2026 sales. Variety protection applications for five new Chinese cabbage varieties extend the breeding pipeline.

Improving direction in quarterly profit

Operating profit reached KRW 1.22 billion in Q1 2026 and controlling shareholder net profit reached KRW 1.5 billion. The Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 6.5 million was smaller than the Q2 loss of KRW 263 million, and it narrowed year on year.

Compared with the Q4 2025 operating loss of KRW 898 million, the operating trend has moved in a better direction.

Export base and overseas subsidiaries

A 2023 report said export seeds accounted for 38% of sales, and the company set up a Vietnam subsidiary in 2018. Cabbage seed is reported to sell across Southeast Asia, Europe, Africa and the Americas. A higher overseas share can partly cushion swings in domestic sales.

Bear factors

Shrinking sales base

Annual sales fell from KRW 24.6 billion in 2024 to KRW 3.52 billion in 2025, two consecutive years of decline. The 2025 figure is hard to compare directly because it covers three months, but the decline in sales is still visible. Q2 2026 sales of KRW 6.75 billion were 23.6% lower than Q1 2026's KRW 8.84 billion.

Persistent losses

Operating losses ran for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025, and controlling shareholder net losses did the same. Q2 and Q3 2026 were two consecutive quarters of losses. Controlling shareholder net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 1.43 billion, so profitable periods have not yet been sustained.

Audit history and financial buffer

In 2022 the company received a disclaimer of opinion from its auditor due to a limitation on audit scope, which led to an designation-as-managed-issue trigger. The debt ratio rose to 90.4% in 2023 before falling to 72.2% in 2025, while operating cash flow still showed a KRW 535 million outflow in 2025. Equity of KRW 24.1 billion serves as a buffer against the losses.

Risk factors

Audit and disclosure risk

In 2022 the auditor issued a disclaimer of opinion over a scope limitation, and the Korea Exchange disclosed that a managed-issue designation trigger had occurred. Audit results must be re-checked each time the annual business report is filed. This history is a fact to weigh when judging the reliability of the company's financial reporting.

Comparability risk

Following the change to a December year-end at a shareholder meeting on December 30, 2025, the 2025 annual figures equal the October to December three-month amounts. As a result, the 85.7% sales decline for 2025 is hard to compare directly with 2024. Only the 2026 business report will allow the first full twelve-month annual comparison.

Competition and export dependence

The company itself described Japanese varieties as holding a high share of the domestic vegetable seed market (2023 report). Because overseas sales are a large share, the business is exposed to import-country seed regulations and local selling conditions. If new varieties fail to gain a foothold, both export and domestic sales could weaken together.

Overall view

Asia Seed is a vegetable seed company with a variety development base and an export network. However, annual sales fell in both 2024 and 2025, and operating losses have continued for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025.

The 2025 annual figures equal three-month amounts after the fiscal year-end change, which limits annual comparison. Quarterly results in 2026 alternated between a Q1 profit and losses in Q2 and Q3, with the Q3 operating loss narrower.

Controlling shareholder net losses over the last four quarters total KRW 1.43 billion. On the balance sheet, the debt ratio was 72.2% at the end of 2025, below the 2023 peak, and equity was KRW 24.1 billion. Valuation sits with the price-to-book ratio below 1x, and no earnings-based multiple can be calculated.

Bullish factors include the variety assets, the export base and the improving direction of quarterly profit. Bearish factors include falling sales, persistent losses, the audit history and the gap in comparability.

The next checkpoints are the full-year results for the December 2026 year-end and the operating margin and sales trend in the 2026 business report.
