02

Business structure

Korea Plasma Technology U was listed on KOSDAQ in April 2002 and specializes in metal surface treatment. Its core business is metal surface coating based on its proprietary eco-friendly process, and the company lists vacuum heat treatment, surface treatment, PVD coating and plasma coating as its main businesses.

It was founded in 1988 and converted into a corporation in 1995, and it describes its shift from heat treatment toward surface treatment. The company says it holds several patents and has operated an in-house research institute since 1995.

Its plasma coating process, which can replace plating, is the technical center of the business. The industry classification is consumer distribution, but in substance the business is closer to processing that adds surface functions to parts and materials.

Revenue therefore tends to track the production volumes of manufacturing customers. The largest shareholder held 61.85% as of an August 7, 2026 disclosure, up from 56.2% at the time of a September 2023 disclosure.

The company also disclosed a loan of about KRW 5.8 billion at 4.6% per year to its Vietnamese affiliate ALK VINA, with the loan period set to end on September 13, 2023.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue moved from KRW 36.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 34.9 billion in 2023, down 5.4% year on year, and to KRW 33.3 billion in 2024, down 4.5%, before rebounding to KRW 39.9 billion in 2025, up 19.9%. Among 2022 to 2025, 2025 was the largest year and 2024 the smallest.

Operating profit fell from KRW 1.39 billion in 2022 to KRW 847 million in 2023, down 39.0%, and to KRW 757 million in 2024, down 10.6%, then returned to KRW 1.39 billion in 2025, up 83.3%. The operating margin recovered from 3.8% in 2022 and 2.4% in 2023 and 2.3% in 2024 to 3.5% in 2025.

Operating profit grew much faster than revenue, so the margin improvement was larger than the sales growth. Net income attributable to owners rose from KRW 2.02 billion in 2022 to KRW 2.86 billion in 2023, up 41.6%, and KRW 3.16 billion in 2024, up 10.6%, before falling to KRW 2.21 billion in 2025, down 30.1%.

In 2025 operating profit rose while profit attributable to owners fell, which suggests that items outside operating profit drove the bottom line. Operating cash flow went from KRW 25.07 million in 2022 to KRW 1.33 billion in 2023 and KRW 1.16 billion in 2024, then fell to KRW 229 million in 2025.

On a quarterly basis, revenue rose from KRW 9.32 billion in the third quarter of 2025 and KRW 9.46 billion in the fourth quarter to KRW 11.4 billion in the first quarter of 2026 and KRW 12.9 billion in the second quarter, then came to KRW 11.8 billion in the third quarter, down 8.2% from the prior quarter and up 27.1% from a year earlier.

Operating profit rose from KRW 246 million in the third quarter of 2025 to KRW 1.02 billion in the second quarter of 2026, then fell to KRW 311 million in the third quarter, down 69.5% from the prior quarter and up 26.2% from a year earlier.

Net income attributable to owners jumped to KRW 2.93 billion in the first quarter of 2026, fell to KRW 613 million in the second quarter, and turned into a net loss of KRW 3.45 billion in the third quarter.

The gap between operating profit and profit attributable to owners is wide, which points to non-operating items driving quarterly results. Net income attributable to owners for the latest four quarters totaled KRW 472 million.

05

Industry analysis

KPTU's business applies surface functions to metal parts, so demand moves with the production cycles of downstream manufacturers. Because the company is classified under consumer distribution, a direct comparison with distribution peers is of limited use given the different nature of the business.

Plasma coating, which can replace plating, becomes more meaningful as environmental rules on plating processes tighten, but linking it to earnings requires data on regulatory timing and replacement demand.

On the market side, the exchange designated the stock as an investment caution issue for one day on July 16, 2026, citing a 15.48% price change over three days and a 46.94% buying share held by the top ten accounts. Foreign accounts accounted for the largest share, and the largest single account held 11.20%.

With the largest shareholder holding 61.85%, the free float traded in the market is relatively small, so price moves on heavy-trading days can be large.
