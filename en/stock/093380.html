Pungkangco: Operating profit recovers as sales decline

02

Business structure

Pungkangco was founded in 1974 to make and sell nuts, bolts and screws and listed on KOSDAQ in 2007. Its core products are cold-forged nuts, with WELD NUT and HEX NUT cited as representative items. Customers are domestic automakers including Hyundai Motor and KIA, together with their tier-one suppliers.

Automotive nuts are supplied under a no-inspection direct delivery system with multi-variety, small-lot orders. As of 2020 data, its EV battery components were supplied through a tier-one supplier of LG Chem.

Overseas, it operates SPM AUTO PARTS in Mexico and holds PUNGKANG AMERICA INC, a U.S. real estate investment entity. Revenue moves with automaker production plans and tier-one order flow. Sales declined from KRW 97.3 billion in the fiscal year ended August 2023 to KRW 88.2 billion in the fiscal year ended August 2026.

As a fastener maker with many product items, its profitability is sensitive to pricing pressure and cost conditions.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales fell from KRW 97.3 billion in the fiscal year ended August 2023 to KRW 93 billion in the year ended August 2024 (down 4.4%), KRW 90.3 billion in the year ended August 2025 (down 3.0%) and KRW 88.2 billion in the year ended August 2026 (down 2.3%).

The decline in the year ended August 2024 was tied to lower sales of key products such as WELD NUT and HEX NUT, alongside higher labor, entertainment and warranty costs.

Operating profit fell from KRW 5.24 billion in the year ended August 2023 to KRW 1.15 billion in 2024 (down 78.1%) and KRW 728 million in 2025 (down 36.6%). The operating margin followed the same path at 5.4%, 1.2% and 0.8%.

Operating profit recovered to KRW 3.23 billion in the year ended August 2026 (up 343.4%), with an operating margin of 3.7%, which the company attributed to higher product gross profit and lower selling and administrative expenses.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders went from KRW 5.58 billion in 2023 to a loss of KRW 981 million in 2024, a profit of KRW 802 million in 2025 and KRW 4.86 billion in 2026 (up 506.0%).

Net income in the year ended August 2026 also reflected a swing to equity-method income from lower losses at affiliates and higher foreign currency translation gains, so non-operating items matter for reading the bottom line.

By quarter, operating profit was KRW 474 million in the quarter ended August 2025, KRW 277 million in the quarter ended November 2025, KRW 1.83 billion in the quarter ended February 2026 and KRW 1.54 billion in the quarter ended May 2026.

The quarter ended August 2026 swung to an operating loss of KRW 411 million, and the loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 661 million year on year.

Operating cash flow rose from KRW 4.78 billion in 2023 to KRW 8.66 billion in the year ended August 2026, while the debt-to-equity ratio moved from 24.0% to 27.1%.

05

Industry analysis

Pungkangco's sales are directly linked to automaker production volumes. A corporate monitor report dated June 2026 described a slight sales decline attributed to lower automaker output, while cost reductions and selling and administrative expense efficiency drove the swing to operating profit.

Auto parts makers move through cycles tied to vehicle utilization and overseas production plans. In 2020, COVID-19 sharply reduced overseas plant operations of automakers and tier-one suppliers, and the company's sales shrank accordingly. That episode shows how quickly revenue can fall when end demand weakens.

Because domestic automakers are a large share of its customer base, the company is strongly exposed to domestic production trends. Its Mexican subsidiary links it indirectly to North American vehicle output. In fastener markets, price, quality and delivery are evaluated together, and many suppliers compete on cost.

Lower selling and administrative expenses were part of the fiscal 2026 profit recovery, which illustrates how cost control shapes margins. Exchange rate moves also feed into equity-method results through foreign currency translation gains.
