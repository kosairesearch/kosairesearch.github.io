02

Business structure

JB Financial Group is a holding company centered on Jeonbuk Bank and Gwangju Bank. Its group companies include the capital firm JB Woori Capital, the asset manager JB Asset Management, the investment firm JB Investment, and Phnom Penh Commercial Bank (PPCBank) in Cambodia, a second-tier overseas subsidiary.

Net income of group companies in Q3 2026 was KRW 72.5 billion for Jeonbuk Bank, KRW 82.4 billion for Gwangju Bank, KRW 66.3 billion for JB Woori Capital, KRW 6.6 billion for JB Asset Management, KRW 2.8 billion for JB Investment and KRW 14.3 billion for PPCBank.

The two banks together earned KRW 154.9 billion in Q3, a large share of the group's Q3 attributable net income of KRW 210 billion. Group revenue splits into interest and non-interest income.

Cumulative interest income for January–September 2026 was KRW 1.65 trillion, up 9.5%, while non-interest income fell 27.6% to KRW 147.9 billion, tilting the income mix toward interest.

Foreign-currency-linked lending outstanding reached KRW 1.18 trillion at end-Q3, up 9.3% from the prior quarter, and auto-secured loans reached KRW 2.93 trillion, up 11.4%. Combined, the two loan books totaled KRW 4.11 trillion, up 10.8%, and form the core of a portfolio rebalancing toward higher-yield assets.

The company said it manages risk-weighted assets below the growth rate of won-denominated loans to raise asset efficiency. A 2026 April brokerage report described expansion into Indonesia, beyond Cambodia, as an overseas growth driver.

Compared with commercial financial holding companies such as Shinhan Financial Group, Hana Financial Group and Woori Financial Group, the group has a larger weight of regional bank subsidiaries.

04

Earnings analysis

On the disclosed annual figures, operating profit was KRW 825.8 billion in 2022, KRW 800 billion in 2023 (down 3.1% year on year), KRW 906.1 billion in 2024 (up 13.3%) and KRW 952.3 billion in 2025 (up 5.1%), so it rose for two consecutive years after 2023. 2025 was the largest year of 2022–2025.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders fell to KRW 586 billion in 2023 (down 2.5%), then rose to KRW 677.5 billion in 2024 (up 15.6%) and KRW 710.4 billion in 2025 (up 4.9%), also two consecutive years of growth.

Net income including non-controlling interests, which is a separate measure, was KRW 730 billion in 2025, and the gap between the two reflects the non-controlling share. The debt-to-equity ratio (total liabilities divided by total equity) fell from 1,156.1% in 2022 to 1,065.5% in 2024 before rising to 1,084.2% in 2025.

At end-2025, total equity was KRW 6.18 trillion, of which controlling shareholders' equity was KRW 5.91 trillion, and total liabilities were KRW 66.9 trillion.

Operating cash flow was negative in all four years, at -KRW 1.15 trillion in 2022 and -KRW 1.14 trillion in 2025, and loan growth at a bank typically flows through this line.

Quarterly operating profit went from KRW 186.9 billion in Q4 2025 (down 33.5% from the prior quarter) to KRW 305.6 billion in Q2 2026, and was KRW 290.9 billion in Q3 2026 (down 4.8% from the prior quarter, up 3.5% year on year).

Attributable net income was KRW 131.7 billion in Q4 2025, the lowest of the last five quarters, then KRW 166.1 billion in Q1 2026 and KRW 219.6 billion in Q2 2026, and KRW 210 billion in Q3 2026.

The Q3 decline in non-interest income was attributed to a base effect from JB Woori Capital's securities-related gains in the prior quarter and lower fee income. Q3 provisions were KRW 99.4 billion, down 4.6% year on year, and selling and administrative expenses fell 9.4% from the prior quarter.

An NH Investment & Securities researcher estimated that about KRW 23 billion of provision reversals were reflected in Q3. Attributable net income for the most recent four quarters totaled KRW 727.3 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Interest rates are weighing on both funding costs and margins. Time deposit funding grew and rates rose, raising funding cost pressure, yet asset growth in corporate and household lending partly offset it, according to one analysis.

Higher rates on wholesale funding raised the possibility of a tougher operating environment ahead. On asset quality, a weaker market for selling non-performing loans reduced sales volumes, which was cited as a reason for the rise in the Q3 NPL ratio.

Kyobo Securities attributed the Q3 drop in non-interest income to a base effect from strong capital-market activity in the prior quarter and lower fee income, but judged that earnings generation remains intact given solid interest income and lower provisions.

In competition, JB Financial Group differs from commercial financial holding companies such as Shinhan Financial Group, Hana Financial Group and Woori Financial Group in that regional bank subsidiaries carry a larger weight.

BNK Financial Group is the most typical comparison among holding companies with regional bank subsidiaries. A margin improvement in the capital business has supported group interest income, which also differentiates it within the sector.

When loan growth concentrates in specific asset classes such as foreign-currency-linked loans and auto-secured loans, asset quality management becomes more demanding, a point to weigh in the sector context.

06

Outlook

The company said it will strengthen profitability on a stable capital base while continuing to expand its shareholder return policy. Its target for the 2026 total shareholder return ratio is 50%, as reported on October 28, 2026. Quarterly dividends have been paid under an equal quarterly dividend policy since Q1 2026.

On annual earnings, an Insight Korea analysis said the company had achieved about 79.4% of its annual target through Q3, and that Q4 net income of at least KRW 154.3 billion would be needed to meet it.

Attributable net income forecasts for 2026 reported on October 28, 2026 were KRW 720 billion from NH Investment & Securities, KRW 734.6 billion from Kyobo Securities and KRW 738 billion from KIWOOM Securities. The chairman, Mr.

Kim, explained that as the portfolio is rebalanced toward higher-margin assets with relatively higher delinquency, the delinquency or NPL ratio could rise slightly in 2027, which leaves room for asset quality indicators to move higher.

Expansion of auto-secured and foreign-currency-linked lending is likely to continue as a strategic focus. Overseas, besides PPCBank in Cambodia, a April 2026 brokerage report referred to a push into Indonesia.

On capital, the CET1 ratio at end-Q3 was 12.39%, down 0.14 percentage points from the prior quarter, and managing risk-weighted asset growth below loan growth is presented as the key tool for preserving capital capacity.

08

Bull factors

Interest-led growth

Cumulative interest income for January–September 2026 was KRW 1.65 trillion, up 9.5%, and Q3 net interest income was KRW 566.5 billion, up 9.4% year on year. The group net interest margin fell 5bp to 3.07%, but won-denominated loans rose 5.3% from the prior quarter, offsetting the margin decline. Loan expansion as a direct driver of earnings growth is cited as the bullish case.

Loan growth with capital efficiency

Cumulative won-denominated loans grew 10.0% over January–September 2026, while risk-weighted assets grew only 5.1%. The company said it achieved this by raising the weight of higher-profit businesses through asset rebalancing. Yuanta Securities Korea assessed that this growth model contributes to higher returns per unit of risk.

Expanding shareholder returns

The company targets a 50% total shareholder return ratio for 2026, and its board approved a quarterly cash dividend per common share on October 27, 2026. The equal quarterly dividend policy has applied since Q1 2026. The company said it will keep expanding shareholder returns on a stable capital base.

09

Bear factors

Sharp drop in non-interest income

Cumulative non-interest income for January–September 2026 was KRW 147.9 billion, down 27.6%, and Q3 alone was KRW 21.9 billion, down 61.6%. JB Woori Capital's securities-related gains showed a base effect from a strong prior quarter, and fee income also declined. As reliance on interest income rises, whether fee-based income recovers remains a bearish factor.

Weaker asset quality indicators

The NPL ratio at end-Q3 2026 was 1.57%, up 0.36 percentage points year on year and up 0.14 points from the prior quarter. Growth in interim-payment mortgage loans reached 67.1%. Mr. Kim said delinquency or NPL ratios could edge up in 2027 as the portfolio is reshaped.

Durability of lower provisions

According to an NH Investment & Securities researcher, Q3 included about KRW 23 billion of provision reversals, and the underlying recurring cost of credit excluding that was 0.79%. The analysis says the sustainability of lower provisions needs monitoring.

Growth in risk-weighted assets could add to capital management pressure, and the CET1 ratio fell 0.14 percentage points from the prior quarter to 12.39%.

10

Risk factors

Credit risk

Interim-payment mortgage loans grew 67.1%, while individual business loans fell 2.5%, showing concentration in loan composition. Foreign-currency-linked and auto-secured loans combined reached KRW 4.11 trillion, up 10.8%, raising portfolio concentration. With the NPL ratio at 1.57%, a rise in delinquent assets could push credit costs higher again.

Rate and funding risk

Higher time deposit funding and rising rates have increased funding cost pressure. The group net interest margin fell 5bp to 3.07% in Q3. Higher rates on wholesale funding were flagged as a possible headwind for the operating environment.

Capital and return capacity

The provisional CET1 ratio fell 0.14 percentage points from the prior quarter to 12.39%. Risk-weighted asset growth is being managed below loan growth, but larger loan books still require more capital. Meeting the 50% total shareholder return target will depend on both earnings scale and capital headroom.

Overall view

JB Financial Group recorded cumulative attributable net income of KRW 595.7 billion for January–September 2026, a record by the company's announcement. Interest income rose 9.5% to KRW 1.65 trillion and drove growth, while risk-weighted asset growth was held to 5.1% against 10.0% growth in won-denominated loans.

Non-interest income fell 27.6%, and the NPL ratio at end-Q3 rose from a year earlier to 1.57%. The provisional CET1 ratio was 12.39%, and the company targets a 50% total shareholder return ratio for 2026.

Broker target prices reported include KRW 38,000 from Yuanta Securities Korea (reported October 31, 2026) and KRW 35,000 maintained by NH Investment & Securities (reported October 28, 2026).

The next checkpoints are the Q4 2026 preliminary results in early 2027 and the 2026 annual report due by end-March 2027, where non-interest income recovery and the direction of the NPL ratio will be read together.
