02

Business structure

Welcron Company combines defense materials, living goods such as bedding, and construction and plant work, built on functional fiber and ballistic materials.

In defense, the core products are armor plates and protection materials, and the company signed an armor plate supply contract with the Defense Acquisition Program Administration in 2021.

Its quarterly report says an acquisition of a defense and composite materials maker gave the company a defense division and a composite materials center. According to the same report, the composite materials center began supplying Level 4 armor plates to the Ministry of National Defense shortly after launch.

The living segment centers on cooling bedding under the Sesa and Sesa Living brands, with the Ice bedding line launched in 2023, an upgraded Air Tunnel Ice bedding in 2024, and the premium hotel bedding brand The White Gallery in 2025.

Stunning Value Research describes Welcron as the top domestic maker of ballistic composite materials and estimates its domestic share at about 70%.

On exports, the research house says that after a landmine protection kit shipment to Saudi Arabia, supply contracts for vehicle add-on armor to Poland, Romania, Indonesia and the Philippines are lined up.

An August 2026 report said export customers, now including Malaysia, the Philippines and Australia, are expanding to Saudi Arabia, Poland, Romania, Indonesia and Taiwan.

In construction and plant work, WiseReport's commentary says order declines and project delays reduced revenue in Q1 2026, and the company attributed the 2025 revenue decline to lower new orders at a subsidiary.

Because defense materials, bedding, and construction and plant work sit inside one company, its structure is hard to benchmark against a single-industry peer group.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 445.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 425.9 billion in 2023 (down 4.4% year on year), KRW 329.1 billion in 2024 (down 22.7%) and KRW 269 billion in 2025 (down 18.3%), a decline in each of the three years from 2023 to 2025. Revenue was largest in 2022 and smallest in 2025 across 2022 to 2025.

Operating profit fell from KRW 6.3 billion in 2022 to KRW 4.85 billion in 2023 (down 23.0%) and KRW 2.17 billion in 2024 (down 55.2%), then turned into an operating loss of KRW 11.1 billion in 2025. The operating margin moved from 1.4% in 2022 to 1.1% in 2023 and 0.7% in 2024, then reached -4.1% in 2025.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 644 million in 2022, turned into a loss of KRW 4.61 billion in 2023, and then widened to a loss of KRW 4.71 billion in 2024 and KRW 18.1 billion in 2025.

Total net loss including noncontrolling interests was KRW 49.2 billion in 2025, larger than the KRW 18.1 billion attributable to controlling shareholders.

The company attributed the 2025 operating loss to lower new orders at a subsidiary, which reduced revenue, and to impairments on uncollected receivables, while impairments on investments in associates in renewable energy and on loans added to the net loss.

By quarter, the loss narrowed from an operating loss of KRW 9.18 billion on revenue of KRW 62.7 billion in Q3 2025 to an operating loss of KRW 1.82 billion on revenue of KRW 67.9 billion in Q4 2025.

Q1 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 569 million on revenue of KRW 59.1 billion, followed by KRW 2.18 billion in Q2 on revenue of KRW 72 billion and KRW 1.62 billion in Q3 on revenue of KRW 65.6 billion.

Net profit attributable to controlling shareholders was positive only in Q2 2026, at KRW 5.87 billion, while Q1 and Q3 2026 showed losses of KRW 2.39 billion and KRW 1.94 billion. The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 12.7 billion.

Operating cash flow went from KRW 29.6 billion in 2022 to outflows of KRW 27.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 5.79 billion in 2024, then turned to an inflow of KRW 21.7 billion in 2025.

The debt ratio rose from 163.1% in 2022 to 444.9% in 2025, and at year-end 2025 total equity was KRW 60.4 billion, equity attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 40.5 billion, and total liabilities were KRW 268.7 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand in defense depends on the expansion of Korean defense exports. Stunning Value Research said that when major Korean defense makers export armored vehicles or weapon systems, Welcron's add-on armor and armor plates are installed and exported together.

The 70% domestic share in ballistic composite materials is the research house's own estimate, and it would need separate verification before being treated as market statistics. Defense demand also tends to move with the timing of government procurement and export contract signings, which makes quarterly revenue lumpy.

The bedding business depends on export growth for functional bedding. The research house named global bedding export growth and higher defense capacity utilization as the two drivers of 2026 earnings improvement. In construction and plant work, order declines and project delays appear in the Q1 2026 results.

WiseReport's commentary also pointed to rising demand for cathode material equipment for secondary batteries and energy-saving equipment as medium-term growth factors in plant work.

As a result, defense and bedding are linked to expanding export contracts, while construction and plant show reported order declines, so the segments are moving in different directions.

Welcron's position in defense materials rests on the research house's share estimate and export contract reports, and no published source in this review supports a share comparison with specific competitors.

Outlook

In an August 2026 report, Stunning Value Research forecast that Welcron would return to profit on both a consolidated and standalone basis in 2026, citing higher defense capacity utilization and bedding export growth.

The same research house set a defense revenue target of KRW 50 billion for 2029, and that figure is the research house's projection.

On the export pipeline, supply contracts for vehicle add-on armor to Poland, Romania, Indonesia and the Philippines are reported as pending, and the landmine protection kit shipment to Saudi Arabia is reported as already completed.

WiseReport's Q1 2026 commentary mentioned development of lightweight armor plates and an expansion of the ballistics business. The next periodic report is the 2026 annual report.

Since the 2025 annual results were disclosed on February 25, 2026, the 2026 preliminary annual results are expected around late February 2027.

The 2026 annual report will show whether the operating margin moved from -4.1% in 2025 into profit, whether the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed, and whether the 444.9% debt ratio declined.

Only one quarter, Q2 2026, showed a profit attributable to controlling shareholders through Q3, so whether defense export contracts feed into results after Q4 2026 remains the main variable.

In construction and plant work, the reversal of the order declines and project delays flagged by WiseReport also needs to be tracked.

Bull factors

Defense export expansion

Stunning Value Research pointed to defense revenue growth and set a 2029 defense revenue target of KRW 50 billion. The research house called Welcron the top domestic maker of ballistic composite materials and estimated its share at about 70%.

It also said vehicle add-on armor contracts for Poland, Romania, Indonesia and the Philippines are pending after the Saudi landmine kit shipment.

Operating profit turnaround

Operating profit turned positive at KRW 569 million in Q1 2026, then reached KRW 2.18 billion in Q2 and KRW 1.62 billion in Q3. Q3 2026 operating profit also swung from a year-earlier loss to a profit. WiseReport attributed the turnaround to cost reduction and expense efficiency.

Operating cash flow turned positive

Operating cash flow turned to an inflow of KRW 21.7 billion in 2025. This contrasts with outflows of KRW 27.4 billion in 2023 and KRW 5.79 billion in 2024. It offers a benchmark for checking whether earnings recovery is converting into cash.

Bear factors

Continued net losses to controlling shareholders

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 18.1 billion in 2025, a loss in each of the three years from 2023 to 2025. Over the last four quarters that loss totaled KRW 12.7 billion, and Q4 2025 alone posted a loss of KRW 14.3 billion. Apart from Q2 2026, Q1 and Q3 2026 also showed losses attributable to controlling shareholders.

Heavier balance sheet burden

The debt ratio rose from 163.1% in 2022 to 243.8% in 2024 and 444.9% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 268.7 billion and total equity was KRW 60.4 billion. With the equity cushion thinner, the pace of earnings recovery will determine how much of the financial burden eases.

Revenue decline and weak orders

The company explained the 2025 operating loss through lower new orders and impairments on uncollected receivables. In Q1 2026, lower construction and plant orders and project delays were again cited as reasons for the revenue decline.

Risk factors

Timing of export contracts

Export supply contracts are reported as pending, and the timing of signing and revenue recognition may not line up. The KRW 50 billion defense revenue target for 2029 is a figure set by Stunning Value Research. A prolonged delay in contracts could push back the timing of the earnings recovery.

Financial burden and receivable recovery

The debt ratio stood at 444.9% at the end of 2025, above the 163.1% recorded in 2022. Operating cash flow was negative in 2023 and 2024 before turning positive in 2025.

Because impairments on uncollected receivables and on associate investments added to the 2025 loss, receivable recovery is central to the financial risk.

Construction and plant order gap

WiseReport's Q1 2026 commentary named lower construction and plant orders and project delays as reasons for the revenue decline. The company also cited lower new orders as a cause of the 2025 revenue decline. Whether the cost reduction effect continues will depend on order flow in coming quarters.

Overall view

Welcron Company combines defense materials, bedding, and construction and plant work, and recorded an operating loss of KRW 11.1 billion and a net loss attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 18.1 billion in 2025. Revenue declined in each year from 2023 to 2025, falling to KRW 269 billion in 2025.

Operating profit continued in 2026, at KRW 569 million in Q1, KRW 2.18 billion in Q2 and KRW 1.62 billion in Q3, but profit attributable to controlling shareholders was positive only in Q2. Stunning Value Research cited a KRW 50 billion defense revenue target for 2029 and pending export contracts as growth drivers.

On the other side, the debt ratio was 444.9% at the end of 2025, and the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the last four quarters totaled KRW 12.7 billion. Lower construction and plant orders and project delays remained reasons for the revenue decline as of Q1 2026.

Defense exports and operating profit on the positive side, and net losses and financial burden on the negative side, are placed side by side at equal weight. The next checkpoints are the 2026 preliminary annual results and the 2026 annual report.
