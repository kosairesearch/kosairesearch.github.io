02

Business structure

Welcron, founded in 1992, is an industrial textile specialist whose parent company produces microfiber cleaners, functional bedding (Living), nanofiber filters and defense materials, while subsidiary Welcron Hantech handles plant engineering and construction.

According to data provider FnGuide, the consolidated revenue mix runs roughly 37.53% textiles, 22.63% construction, 26.60% plant and 10.69% renewable energy.

The bedding business operates under the 'Sesa' and 'Sesa Living' brands, with ice bedding using the patented 'Air Tunnel' technology as its flagship product, having sold out for seven consecutive years while expanding global exports.

The defense business entered the market in 2011 and accelerated after the 2018 acquisition of Smart Composite, broadening its lineup from personal bulletproof plates and vests to add-on armor for armored vehicles and tactical vehicles.

Demand is anchored by domestic government procurement from the Ministry of National Defense, the Defense Acquisition Program Administration and the National Police Agency, while the company is expanding its export footprint beyond Malaysia, the Philippines and Australia into Saudi Arabia, Poland, Romania, Indonesia and Taiwan.

Nanofiber technology is applied to high-efficiency MB filters, masks and cleanroom wipers, and the company's microfiber cleaners once held the top global market share.

Subsidiary Welcron Hantech operates industrial plants (membrane filtration, evaporation-concentration, secondary battery cathode material equipment), power-plant water and wastewater treatment EPC, and specialized construction such as senior housing.

Competitively, the bedding and cleaner businesses face numerous household-goods brands, the defense unit operates in a concentrated domestic bulletproof-plate procurement market where it holds a leading share, and the plant unit competes on its track record in power-generation and senior-housing niches within the small-to-mid EPC market.

04

Earnings analysis

Consolidated annual revenue contracted for four straight years, from KRW 445.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 425.9 billion in 2023, KRW 329.1 billion in 2024 and KRW 269.0 billion in 2025.

Operating profit also eroded each year—KRW 6.30 billion in 2022, KRW 4.85 billion in 2023, KRW 2.17 billion in 2024—before turning into an operating loss of KRW 11.12 billion in 2025.

Owner net income was positive at KRW 0.64 billion in 2022 but swung to losses of KRW 4.61 billion in 2023, KRW 4.71 billion in 2024 and KRW 18.14 billion in 2025, with the loss widening each year.

On a quarterly basis, an operating loss of KRW 9.18 billion in Q3 2025 and an owner net loss of KRW 14.26 billion in Q4 2025 were the key drags on the full year, coinciding with a period when one-off subsidiary-related provisions and bad-debt write-offs were recorded.

Momentum then shifted: Q1 2026 revenue fell year-over-year to KRW 59.12 billion but operating profit turned positive at KRW 0.57 billion, and Q2 2026 showed a clearer improvement with revenue of KRW 72.02 billion, operating profit of KRW 2.18 billion and owner net income of KRW 5.87 billion.

Still, the trailing four-quarter owner net income (Q3 2025 through Q2 2026) remained negative at KRW 12.83 billion, indicating the large losses of the second half of 2025 have not yet been fully offset.

On the balance sheet, owner's equity fell from KRW 76.11 billion in 2022 to KRW 40.54 billion in 2025, while the debt ratio rose sharply from 163.1% to 444.9% over the same period, signaling weaker capital buffers.

In contrast, operating cash flow was positive at KRW 21.73 billion in 2025 despite the net loss, highlighting a gap between reported earnings and cash generation that period.

Industry analysis

South Korea's defense export industry sees rear-supply-chain demand for add-on armor and ballistic materials grow alongside expanding exports of complete armored vehicles and tactical vehicles; Stunning Value Research analyst noted the company holds roughly a 70% share of the domestic bulletproof-plate procurement and bidding market.

Rising geopolitical tension and expanding defense budgets globally are cited as medium-term tailwinds for personal protective gear and vehicle armor demand.

The functional bedding and household-goods industry is a fairly fragmented market shaped by seasonal demand tied to heatwaves and the expansion of overseas distribution, competing against numerous domestic and international brands.

The plant and construction segment is driven by a mix of secondary-battery cathode and separator equipment investment, power-plant water and wastewater treatment demand, and specialized construction such as senior housing, with the capital spending cycle of Chinese battery material makers directly affecting order flow at the Chinese subsidiary of Welcron Hantech.

That subsidiary reported cumulative battery-equipment orders of KRW 24.2 billion through Q3 2026, roughly 5.8 times the prior full year's KRW 4.2 billion.

While overall domestic construction activity remains sluggish, Welcron Hantech is pursuing a niche strategy of building a continuous order track record in specialized construction such as senior housing.

Relative to peers, the company's position can be summarized as a relative leader in defense, a niche small-to-mid EPC player in plant and construction, and a brand-backed stable revenue base in textiles and household goods.

06

Outlook

Both the company and outside analysts point to a strengthened, high-value-added portfolio centered on defense and bedding as the core direction going forward.

The defense division launched an official website with an English interface in September 2026 to expand contact points with overseas defense buyers and consolidate order and technical inquiry channels.

The company has set a target of KRW 50 billion in defense segment revenue by 2029, expanding add-on armor supply in step with rising exports of complete armored vehicles.

The bedding business plans to continue expanding exports, building on seven consecutive years of sold-out ice bedding and higher factory utilization during heatwave-driven demand spikes.

Welcron Hantech secured a backlog with the KRW 79.9 billion Bukwirye Village construction contract and stated it has demonstrated power-plant EPC capability through wins at Korea Southern Power's Andong Complex Unit 2 and Korea Midland Power's Haman Complex water and wastewater treatment facilities.

Its Chinese subsidiary disclosed it is in discussions for an additional roughly KRW 14 billion in battery equipment projects.

Stunning Value Research forecast improved consolidated and standalone results in 2026 on maximized defense utilization and expanded global bedding exports, while stating it did not present an investment rating or target price.

Whether these positive developments fully resolve the risk of a recurrence of the large one-off losses seen in the second half of 2025, and the financial strain from the elevated debt ratio, will require confirmation in upcoming quarterly results.

Bull factors

Structural Growth in the Defense Business

Defense segment revenue grew 132% from KRW 11.1 billion in 2024 to KRW 25.8 billion in 2025, and the company is assessed to hold roughly a 70% share of the domestic bulletproof-plate procurement market.

Beyond existing export destinations such as Malaysia, the Philippines and Australia, it is expanding into Saudi Arabia, Poland, Romania, Indonesia and Taiwan. In step with rising exports of complete armored vehicles, it is increasing add-on armor supply and has set a 2029 revenue target of KRW 50 billion.

Export Expansion of the High-Margin Bedding Business

Ice bedding using the patented 'Air Tunnel' technology has sold out completely for seven consecutive years, generating reported revenue of more than KRW 11 billion last year. Related factory utilization reportedly reached 100% amid heatwave-driven demand.

Expanding global bedding exports is cited as another pillar of the high-value-added portfolio alongside defense.

Resolution of Welcron Hantech's Financial Risk and New Order Wins

Subsidiary Welcron Hantech confirmed a KRW 42.45 billion debt forgiveness and provision reversal, followed by securing the KRW 79.9 billion Bukwirye Village construction contract.

In the power sector, it won orders for Korea Southern Power's Andong Complex Unit 2 and Korea Midland Power's Haman Complex water and wastewater treatment facilities, demonstrating EPC capability.

Its Chinese subsidiary's cumulative battery-equipment orders reached KRW 24.2 billion through Q3 2026, about 5.8 times the prior full year.

Bear factors

Four Consecutive Years of Revenue Contraction

Consolidated revenue fell for four straight years, from KRW 445.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 269.0 billion in 2025. Operating profit also deteriorated each year over the same period before turning into an operating loss of KRW 11.12 billion in 2025. The overall top line has clearly contracted amid the business restructuring process.

Shrinking Equity and a Sharply Rising Debt Ratio

Owner's equity fell from KRW 76.11 billion in 2022 to KRW 40.54 billion in 2025, while the debt ratio surged from 163.1% to 444.9%. Large losses concentrated in Q3-Q4 2025 are cited as the main driver of the equity decline. The weakened capital buffer increases sensitivity to future earnings volatility.

Trailing Four Quarters Still Show a Net Loss

Despite consecutive operating profits in Q1-Q2 2026, the trailing four-quarter owner net income from Q3 2025 through Q2 2026 remains negative at KRW 12.83 billion. This can be interpreted as meaning the impact of the large losses in the second half of 2025 has not been fully offset. The pattern of high quarter-to-quarter earnings volatility continues.

Risk factors

Balance Sheet Risk

With the debt ratio rising to 444.9% at the end of 2025, owner's equity has been continuously shrinking. If one-off subsidiary-related provisions or bad-debt expenses recur, the capital buffer could weaken further. Whether additional capital raising or debt restructuring occurs could be key to future financial stability.

Construction and Plant Segment Volatility

As noted in the Q1 2026 earnings commentary, Welcron Hantech's construction and plant revenue can fluctuate significantly on a quarterly basis due to project delays or order gaps. Even though financial risk was recently resolved, results can swing heavily depending on the progress of a single large project. Orders for secondary-battery equipment in China also depend on the local capital investment cycle.

Export and Geopolitical Risk

Defense exports are expanding into new regions such as Saudi Arabia, Poland, Romania, Indonesia and Taiwan, but order timing could be delayed by changes in each country's defense budget execution or political conditions.

Because add-on armor supply is linked to complete armored vehicle exports, any change in the vehicle export pipeline could directly affect add-on armor revenue. Currency fluctuations are also a variable that can affect the profitability of export-heavy businesses.

12

Overall view

Welcron is showing positive signals with revenue growth in high-margin businesses such as defense and functional bedding, along with two consecutive quarters of operating profit in Q1-Q2 2026.

At the same time, subsidiary Welcron Hantech stated it has resolved part of its financial risk through debt forgiveness, provision reversal, and a large new contract win.

However, on a full-year 2025 basis, the company posted an operating loss and a large net loss, and financial strains remain in the form of shrinking owner's equity and a 444.9% debt ratio.

The fact that the trailing four-quarter figure still shows a net loss indicates the aftereffects of the second-half 2025 losses have not fully dissipated.

Revenue has contracted for four straight years since 2022, while the profit structure has shown sensitivity to shifts in the weighting of higher-value-added businesses like defense and bedding.

Upcoming quarterly results, the execution status of Welcron Hantech's large order, and progress in expanding defense export regions will likely be key variables in assessing whether the turnaround trend is sustained.
