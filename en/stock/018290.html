Overseas Sales at 70%, Marketing Spend Weighs on Profit

VT's overseas share of sales reached 70% in Q3 2026, but heavier promotional spending cut first-half 2026 operating profit by 51.8%, so sales and profit are moving in different directions.

  3. 3

02

Business structure

VT is a diversified beauty company centered on cosmetics. Its consolidated sales in 2025 were 94.06% cosmetics and 5.89% laminating, while in 2024 cosmetics accounted for 79.80% and record production for 12.48%. The decline in consolidated sales in Q1 2025 was attributed to the deconsolidation of Cube Entertainment.

Its flagship product is the Lidshot line, which took off first in Japan and Korea and is now expanding into the United States, Europe, Southeast Asia and the Middle East.

Domestic channels centre on Daiso and Olive Young, and the company said Daiso sales rose about 65% year on year and Olive Young sales rose about 47% quarter on quarter in Q2 2026.

Overseas, it is widening business-to-consumer sales through Amazon in the US and Europe and Shopee in Southeast Asia, while building business-to-business distribution locally.

Q2 2026 sales rose 454% year on year in Europe and 546% in the Middle East, with B2B sales through DERMA SPACE and NOTINO in Europe and growth in Turkey led by GRATIS. Overseas sales reached a quarterly record in Q3 2026 and made up 70% of total sales.

In October 2026 the company held a masterclass with Ulta Beauty in New York to build brand awareness in North America. Among Korean cosmetics peers, VT competes for overseas channel access, using Lidshot's popularity in Japan as a base for expansion.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 240.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 295.5 billion in 2023 (up 23.0% year on year), KRW 431.7 billion in 2024 (up 46.1%) and KRW 437.2 billion in 2025 (up 1.3%), and have increased for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Operating profit climbed from KRW 23.6 billion in 2022 to KRW 110.9 billion in 2024 (up 143.5%) before falling to KRW 82.9 billion in 2025 (down 25.3%). The operating margin was 9.8% in 2022, 15.4% in 2023, 25.7% in 2024 and 19.0% in 2025, peaking in 2024 and easing in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 98 billion in 2024 and KRW 60.9 billion in 2025 (down 37.9%), while net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 104 billion in 2024 and KRW 64.2 billion in 2025, and the two measures differ. Sales grew only 1.3% in 2025 while operating profit fell 25.3%.

In the Q3 2025 results coverage, higher personnel and marketing costs tied to global store expansion were cited as the reason operating profit declined.

On a quarterly basis, Q3 2025 sales were KRW 110.2 billion with operating profit of KRW 12.1 billion; Q4 2025 reached KRW 115 billion and KRW 12.7 billion; Q1 2026 sales were KRW 114.3 billion (down 0.6% quarter on quarter) with operating profit of KRW 17.3 billion (up 36.9%).

Q2 2026 sales rose to KRW 131.7 billion (up 15.2%) while operating profit fell to KRW 10.7 billion (down 38.4%), so sales and profit moved in opposite directions.

Q3 2026 sales were KRW 137.8 billion (up 4.6% quarter on quarter and 25.0% year on year) and operating profit was KRW 14.9 billion (up 39.1% quarter on quarter and 22.5% year on year).

First-half 2026 operating profit fell 51.8% to KRW 28.0 billion, and advertising and promotion expenses reached KRW 36.1 billion, more than double the KRW 16.4 billion of a year earlier, lifting their share of sales from 7.8% to 14.7%.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent of KRW 18.2 billion in Q1 2026 exceeded operating profit of KRW 17.3 billion, which may reflect non-operating items, but the disclosed figures alone do not separate the causes.

Over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 to Q3 2026), profit attributable to owners of the parent totalled KRW 37.7 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Korea's cosmetics sector is riding a record run of K-beauty exports, but the won's strength is a counterweight. The won-dollar rate fell into the 1,300 range, its lowest level in two years, which reduces the won value of dollar earnings and has weighed on export-heavy cosmetics stocks.

VT's overseas share of standalone sales was 66% in Q2 2026, so its FX exposure is large. According to a KB Securities analysis reported in October 2026, a 1% rise in the won-dollar rate would lift VT's 2026 earnings per share by 0.9%.

Demand for Lidshot in Japan has slowed, and the company has turned to the United States, Europe, Russia and the Middle East after Japan, which once accounted for 40% of sales, lost momentum.

IBK Securities analysis reported that Lidshot sales at Daiso fell from roughly KRW 10.0 billion per quarter at launch to an average of about KRW 4.5 billion. Brokers assess that growth is not as steep as when Lidshot first took off in Japan.

In product-cycle terms, the company is looking for a follow-up hit after Lidshot. Compared with peers, many fast-growing K-beauty companies rent offices in Gangnam or Songpa, whereas VT agreed in October 2026 to buy a building, moving from leasing toward ownership. Some in the industry have described the move as an aggressive bet.

06

Outlook

A company spokesperson said in a November 2026 report that business-to-consumer sales through Amazon in the United States and Europe and Shopee in Southeast Asia are rising steadily, and that overseas B2B sales are gaining pace.

The spokesperson added that the company expects global sales to keep growing, since major promotions are concentrated in Q4. A KB Securities projection reported in October 2026 put 2026 operating profit at KRW 61.0 billion, a further decline from KRW 82.9 billion in 2025.

If that projection holds, operating profit would fall further this year. The next checkpoint is how Q4 2026 promotional results show up in the overseas sales mix and in the advertising and promotion ratio.

In products, VT is extending its PDRN (salmon-DNA-derived ingredient) line and is reportedly running clinical trials and patent work on dermatology-based new products. On the balance sheet, VT disclosed in October 2026 that it will acquire land and a building in Seongsu-dong, Seoul for KRW 79.0 billion.

A deposit of KRW 15.8 billion was paid on signing, the KRW 63.2 billion balance is due on December 24, 2026, and funding will come from cash on hand and bank borrowing. The timing and payment schedule may change depending on negotiations with the counterparty.

Shareholder returns are also continuing: in October 2026 the company resolved to retire 1,525,358 treasury shares, about 4.4% of issued shares, and after the change listing on November 5, 2026, listed common shares fell to 33,416,480.

08

Bull factors

Wider overseas sales base

Overseas sales reached a quarterly record in Q3 2026 and made up 70% of total sales. Europe, the Middle East and North America each grew more than 200% year on year, and South America rose 913%. Partnerships with Amazon, Shopee and Ulta Beauty are widening the sales base across regions.

Domestic channel recovery

Domestic sales began recovering in Q1 2026 and have kept growing. In Q2 2026 coverage, Daiso sales rose about 65% year on year and Olive Young sales rose about 47% quarter on quarter. The company paired this with marketing activity such as a Pokemon IP collaboration at Olive Young.

Balance-sheet capacity and shareholder returns

The debt ratio fell from 114.8% in 2022 to 39.6% in 2025. Treasury shares of 1,525,358, about 4.4% of issued shares, were retired after the change listing on November 5, 2026.

Bear factors

Marketing costs and profit decline

First-half 2026 operating profit fell 51.8% to KRW 28.0 billion. Advertising and promotion expenses reached KRW 36.1 billion in the same period, more than double the KRW 16.4 billion of a year earlier, and their share of sales rose from 7.8% to 14.7%. Q2 2026 sales rose, but operating profit fell 38.4% quarter on quarter to KRW 10.7 billion.

FX exposure and a stronger won

The won-dollar rate fell into the 1,300 range and the won strengthened. Overseas sales were 66% of standalone sales in Q2 2026, so the company is directly exposed to a stronger won. KB Securities estimated, in an October 2026 report, that a 1% rise in the rate would lift 2026 earnings per share by 0.9%.

Reliance on Lidshot and slower growth

Japan, which once made up 40% of Lidshot-driven sales, has lost momentum. According to an IBK Securities analysis, Daiso's Lidshot sales fell from about KRW 10.0 billion per quarter at launch to roughly KRW 4.5 billion on average. Brokers say growth is not as steep as in Japan during the product's early launch.

10

Risk factors

FX and overseas channel dependence

With overseas sales making up most of revenue, earnings are sensitive to currency moves and to promotion policies at overseas retail channels. A sustained stronger won reduces the won value of overseas profit. Heavy reliance on a few online platforms or regions would magnify the effect of any policy change there.

Marketing efficiency and earnings volatility

Advertising and promotion costs rose to 14.7% of sales, and quarterly profit swung widely. In Q2 2026 operating profit fell even as sales rose. Whether the Q4 promotional push converts into operating profit is the key point to confirm.

Large investment and borrowing

The KRW 79.0 billion Seongsu-dong purchase equals 22.35% of total assets at end-2025. The KRW 63.2 billion balance will be funded from cash on hand and borrowing, and the schedule may change. Some in the industry describe the purchase as an aggressive bet.

12

Overall view

VT is in a phase where its growth engine, overseas expansion, and its cost pressure, rising marketing spend, are moving at the same time. On disclosed figures, sales grew from KRW 240.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 437.2 billion in 2025 and have risen for three consecutive years from 2023.

Operating profit, however, peaked at KRW 110.9 billion in 2024, fell to KRW 82.9 billion in 2025, and dropped 51.8% in the first half of 2026 to KRW 28.0 billion. Q3 2026 sales of KRW 137.8 billion and operating profit of KRW 14.9 billion both rose, an early sign of profit recovery.

Strengths include overseas sales at 70% of the total, fast growth in Europe, the Middle East and North America, the rebound at Daiso and Olive Young in Korea, and a debt ratio that fell to 39.6%.

Weaknesses include the surge in advertising and promotion spending, FX exposure to a stronger won, and reliance on Lidshot with a slowing Japan. The next checkpoints are the Seongsu-dong balance payment in December 2026, Q4 2026 promotional results, and the 2026 annual report due by the end of March 2027. Whether earnings recovery catches up with sales growth is the central variable for this stock.
