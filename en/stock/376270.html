02

Business structure

HEM Pharma runs a microbiome health business that combines analysis services, probiotic product manufacturing, drug candidate development and cosmetics, all built on gut microbiome data.

The company says it holds more than 120,000 microbiome data records, and its flagship analysis service my LAB estimates gut microbiome and metabolite profiles from stool samples to predict individual health status and disease risk.

In Japan, the my LAB PLUS service launched in May 2026, and Japanese customers accounted for 73.3% of service revenue in the second quarter of 2026.

Japanese analysis contracts are not booked as revenue when the purchase order is received; they are recognized gradually as HEM Pharma performs the analysis at its Korean headquarters and delivers result reports.

On the product side, a new probiotic co-developed with the supply arm of Amway, Access Business Group, is fully manufactured at the Sejong plant, and a formal purchase order worth KRW 4.63 billion was received in June 2026.

Its pipeline includes HEMP-001, a depression candidate that received FDA Phase 2 IND clearance, and HEMP-002, a candidate for long-gut-motility disorders that received Australian Phase 2 approval. The data subsidiary LogMe continues to report a stable profit.

In March 2026 the board approved a physical spin-off that separates R&D, pharmaceuticals and health functional food, and cosmetics into independent companies, with a split date of May 1, 2026.

Competition overlaps with other listed and private firms offering gut microbiome analysis and customized functional foods, and the company points to the scale of its data and its combined analysis-and-solution model as differentiators.

04

Earnings analysis

Revenue on a filed basis rose from KRW 3.67 billion in 2022 to KRW 5.34 billion in 2023, a 45.7% increase, and then to KRW 15.1 billion in 2024, up 181.9%. It fell 13.9% to KRW 13 billion in 2025. Within 2022 to 2025, 2024 was the largest revenue year and 2022 the smallest.

The company attributed the 2025 decline to a strategic reduction in its key raw-material supply business and a timing shift in product supply to a major customer.

Operating losses narrowed from KRW 13.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 11.8 billion in 2023 and KRW 7.68 billion in 2024, then widened to KRW 15.6 billion in 2025, and the company has recorded operating losses in each year from 2022 to 2025.

The company said the 2025 widening reflected higher routine R&D expenses tied to ongoing research projects, advertising for the global launch of its new cosmetics business, and fees for outside advisers supporting overseas expansion.

The 2025 net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 23.7 billion, and the loss attributable to owners was KRW 23.8 billion, both larger than in 2024; the company said derivative valuation losses on convertible bonds issued in October 2025 were included.

The operating margin improved from -361.0% in 2022 to -51.0% in 2024 and then worsened to -120.1% in 2025. Gross profit in the first half of 2026 rose from KRW 900 million to KRW 2.35 billion, and the gross margin rose from 14.1% to 24.7%.

Quarterly revenue in 2026 was KRW 4.94 billion in the first quarter, KRW 4.57 billion in the second and KRW 10.9 billion in the third, while operating losses over the same quarters were KRW 3.84 billion, KRW 2.86 billion and KRW 1.05 billion.

The cost of sales ratio in the second quarter of 2026 fell from 88.5% a year earlier to 57.0%, with the service segment's cost-efficiency gains outweighing a one-time loss in the product segment.

Profit attributable to owners swung from a KRW 14.7 billion loss in the fourth quarter of 2025 to a KRW 1.5 billion profit in the second quarter of 2026 and a KRW 3.86 billion profit in the third quarter.

Because operating losses remain while owners' net profit turned positive, the composition of non-operating items is a point to check in the detailed notes of the periodic reports.

05

Industry analysis

Gut microbiome analysis, the core of HEM Pharma's business, has a different earnings structure from large-cap bio and pharma names such as Samsung Biologics and Celltrion, which focus on contract manufacturing or antibody drugs.

Because the model turns analysis data into products and drug candidates, revenue moves sharply from quarter to quarter with service contracts, the conversion of advance payments and OEM orders.

The company emphasizes a structure that covers analysis, strain discovery and finished-product manufacturing on the strength of more than 120,000 data records. It has said it plans to expand its data to 1 million records by 2028, and it describes that scale as more than three times the U.S.

National Institutes of Health's 10-year human microbiome project. Japan is the core of overseas expansion, and the company plans to use Amway's sales network in 80 countries to extend sequentially to Thailand, Taiwan, Malaysia and the United States.

Domestically, it plans to expand through health screening centers, hospitals and pharmacies nationwide. In competition, it overlaps with companies that combine gut microbiome analysis and customized health functional foods, and its revenue scale is small compared with large bio and pharma companies.

Some analysts argue that the post-spin-off structure, with independent companies for each business, can make growth strategies and fundraising clearer. Because both the analysis and product businesses rely on the Japanese channel, order flow through that channel is the largest variable in earnings swings.

The Sejong No. 2 plant, funded with KRW 27 billion, is targeted to begin operating in the second half of 2027, and automation equipment with KRW 7.5 billion of proceeds is intended to more than double analysis processing capacity by the end of 2027.

Its Japan revenue estimate rises from KRW 14.93 billion in 2026 to KRW 28.06 billion in 2027. These estimates came alongside a target-price cut that reflected a one-quarter delay in recognizing Japanese revenue and volatility in the KOSDAQ market.

Bull factors

Growing Weight of Japanese Analysis Services

Japanese customers made up 73.3% of service revenue in the second quarter of 2026. The my LAB PLUS service secured cumulative pre-orders of 90,000 cases, worth about KRW 9.3 billion.

The cost of sales ratio fell to 57.0% in the second quarter of 2026 from 88.5% a year earlier, and the Japanese subsidiary turned operating profit positive as of the second-quarter announcement.

Narrowing Quarterly Operating Losses and Profit Swings

Operating losses narrowed to KRW 3.84 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 2.86 billion in the second quarter and KRW 1.05 billion in the third quarter.

Profit attributable to owners was KRW 1.5 billion in the second quarter and KRW 3.86 billion in the third, and the third quarter was a swing to profit from a year earlier. Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 10.9 billion rose 269.1% from the same quarter a year earlier.

Broker Estimates and New Business Pipeline

In an August 2026 report, Leading Investment & Securities estimated 2026 consolidated revenue at KRW 45.6 billion and projected 2027 revenue of KRW 86.59 billion and operating profit of KRW 16.37 billion.

The same report cited a possible 2026 launch of the Vignal device and OEM revenue from Amway's global operations as drivers. In June 2026 the company received a formal purchase order worth KRW 4.63 billion from Amway's supply arm.

Bear factors

Continued Operating Losses and a Wider 2025 Loss

The company has recorded operating losses in each year from 2022 to 2025, and the 2025 operating loss of KRW 15.6 billion was the largest in that span. Revenue fell 13.9% to KRW 13 billion in 2025.

Profit attributable to owners over the last four quarters totaled a KRW 13.5 billion loss, and the third quarter of 2026 still carried an operating loss of KRW 1.05 billion.

Balance Sheet and Convertible Bond Burden

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 46.6 billion and total equity was KRW 5.21 billion, and the debt-to-equity ratio rose to 894.0% from 41.1% in 2024.

The company completed payment for a KRW 54 billion second-series convertible bond in August 2026, and KRW 27 billion of it is earmarked for the Sejong No. 2 plant. The 2025 net loss widened partly because of derivative valuation losses on convertible bonds issued in October 2025.

Concentration in Japan and Amway Channels, and Recognition Lag

Japanese customers accounted for 73.3% of service revenue in the second quarter of 2026, so results depend heavily on the flow of Japanese analysis volume. Revenue from Japan is booked as advance payments convert into actual sales, so quarterly results move in steps.

Leading Investment & Securities also noted that the timing of Japanese revenue recognition was delayed by one quarter on an accounting basis. The company said in September 2026 that Vignal revenue is expected to begin in 2027.

Risk factors

Clinical Development Progress

HEMP-001 received FDA Phase 2 IND clearance and HEMP-002 received Australian Phase 2 approval, but clinical results have not been determined. If the Phase 2 trials fail to show the expected results or fall behind schedule, recovery of development spending in the drug pipeline could be delayed. Added costs or schedule changes during the trials could also increase the company's R&D burden.

Data Scale and Competition

The company has said it plans to expand its data to 1 million records by 2028. If this target is not met on schedule, or domestic and overseas gut microbiome analysis firms expand their data scale quickly, the differentiation built on more than 120,000 records could weaken.

If data scale does not translate into analysis accuracy and product development, securing revenue in a competitive market could become harder.

Investment Spending and Timing of Profitability

KRW 27 billion is being put into the Sejong No. 2 plant and KRW 7.5 billion into analysis automation, so fixed-cost pressure is likely to persist. The company attributed higher selling and administrative expenses to up-front investment for new businesses launching in the second half. The company has said it is difficult to specify when operating profit will turn positive.

Overall view

HEM Pharma's filed results show two trends running side by side. Revenue has risen to KRW 10.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, driven by Japanese analysis services, and operating losses narrowed to KRW 3.84 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 2.86 billion in the second and KRW 1.05 billion in the third.

Profit attributable to owners also turned positive, at KRW 1.5 billion in the second quarter and KRW 3.86 billion in the third. On the other side, the 2025 operating loss of KRW 15.6 billion was the largest in the 2022 to 2025 span, and the company has recorded losses in each year from 2022 to 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio was 894.0% at the end of 2025 with total equity of KRW 5.21 billion, so how the balance sheet changes after the KRW 54 billion convertible bond and the Sejong plant investment is a point to watch.

Because quarterly results move with the timing of advance payments converting into revenue, fourth-quarter profit and loss in the 2026 annual report is the next checkpoint.

The timing of the Vignal launch, any additional Amway orders and the start of the Sejong No. 2 plant in the second half of 2027 will also indicate how far the expansion has progressed.

Broker estimates assume profit in the fourth quarter of 2026, so how filed results compare with those estimates is the benchmark to check. Strengths and weaknesses coexist in this stage.
