02

Business structure

Coway is a living-appliance rental company that earns by renting water purifiers, air purifiers, bidets, beds and massage chairs and bundling them with care services.

In the third quarter of 2026, overseas subsidiaries' revenue rose 26.2% year on year to KRW 591.8 billion, which press reports put at about 41.5% of consolidated sales of KRW 1.43 trillion.

Domestic revenue reached KRW 754.9 billion, up 2.0%, supported by ice water purifiers, beds, massage chairs and seasonal promotions. Newer products such as food waste disposers and wall-mounted air conditioners are being sold alongside the core environmental appliances.

Domestic rental net additions reached 214,000 units in the third quarter, which expands the account base behind recurring service revenue. Malaysia is the anchor overseas market, with 2025 revenue of KRW 1.41 trillion, and the company says it holds the leading share of the local water purifier market.

The United States sells Mega Series air purifiers, bidets and water purifiers under a regular-delivery filter management model, with 2025 revenue of KRW 236.7 billion. Thailand reported second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 66 billion, up 53.9%, and is often called a second Malaysia.

Overseas revenue reached KRW 1.89 trillion in 2025, about 38% of group sales. Coway competes with Cuckoo Homesys and others in domestic rental, but it is seen as the most active rental company in expanding into new overseas countries.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual results show revenue, operating profit and profit attributable to controlling shareholders all higher in 2025 than in 2022. Revenue rose from KRW 3.86 trillion in 2022 to KRW 4.96 trillion in 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the 2022–2025 period after a 15.2% increase.

Over the same span, operating profit went from KRW 677.4 billion to KRW 878.7 billion, and profit attributable to controlling shareholders from KRW 458.2 billion to KRW 617.7 billion. Operating profit and controlling-shareholder profit both rose for three consecutive years from 2023 through 2025.

Growth in controlling-shareholder profit was strongest in 2024 at 20.0% and slowed to 9.2% in 2025. Operating margin went from 17.6% in 2022 to 18.4% in 2023 and 18.5% in 2024 before easing to 17.7% in 2025. Operating profit grew 10.5% in 2025, slower than revenue, which diluted the margin.

At the end of 2025, the debt-to-equity ratio stood at 93.7%, up from 80.3% a year earlier, with total liabilities of KRW 3.39 trillion and equity of KRW 3.61 trillion. Operating cash flow fell from KRW 330.3 billion in 2024 to KRW 35.5 billion in 2025.

Quarterly operating profit dropped 25.3% to KRW 181.6 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, then rose to KRW 250.9 billion, KRW 253.2 billion and KRW 260.8 billion over the next three quarters.

Profit attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 182 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then slipped to KRW 178.3 billion and KRW 111.5 billion.

According to press analysis, third-quarter pre-tax profit was about KRW 149 billion, well below operating profit, because non-operating expenses exceeded KRW 110 billion. The sum of controlling-shareholder profit over the last four quarters is KRW 619.1 billion.

05

Industry analysis

In rental, the account base drives recurring revenue, so net additions are the key indicator. Third-quarter 2026 domestic rental net additions reached 214,000 units, up 26.7%, yet domestic revenue grew only 2.0%.

Trade coverage linked the slower domestic revenue growth to fewer accounts reaching ownership transfer and fewer operating days. The company says demand for environmental appliances such as water and air purifiers remains firm, and newer living appliances are adding demand.

Press coverage reported that overseas subsidiary revenue grew at an average annual rate of about 20% over the past five years. In Malaysia, Coway introduced rental and care services in 2007 and says it has held the top water purifier share since.

In the United States, regular-delivery filter management and a subscription service developed with Amazon are used in place of in-home visit care. Cuckoo Homesys was reported to have all its overseas subsidiaries outside Malaysia in full capital impairment. The same report flagged Coway's Singapore, India and US subsidiaries as in full capital impairment.

06

Outlook

The company said it will pursue both top-line growth and profitability in the fourth quarter of 2026. According to the company, cumulative third-quarter revenue reached 77% of the top of its full-year guidance range, and operating profit reached 80%.

Reports from March and April 2026 said the company set a 2026 revenue target of about KRW 5.2 trillion. In Korea, the key questions are whether rental net additions and sales of newer living appliances keep pace.

Overseas, the plan is to hold the leading position in Malaysia while expanding growth markets such as Thailand and Indonesia. Thailand's second-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 66 billion, up 53.9%.

Press coverage said the Indonesian subsidiary entered annual operating profit territory for the first time since its founding. The company also plans to speed up the establishment of newer markets such as Vietnam and Indonesia.

Singapore and India were still in early stages and loss-making as of the first half of 2026, so whether they reach breakeven is a point to track.

Guidance progress is measured through the third quarter, so the fourth-quarter results and the 2026 annual report will determine where the company stands against its full-year targets.

08

Bull factors

Fast overseas growth

Third-quarter 2026 overseas subsidiary revenue rose 26.2% year on year to KRW 591.8 billion, with the United States (up 45.8%) and Thailand (up 38.0%) growing fastest. Press reports said the overseas share of consolidated sales widened to about 41.5%.

That share was about 38% in 2025, up from roughly 8% a decade earlier. Malaysia's leading local market position is cited as the foundation.

Domestic rental account growth

Domestic rental net additions reached 214,000 units in the third quarter of 2026, up 26.7% year on year. New rental unit sales rose 7.3%, widening the account base. The company credits newer living appliances such as food waste disposers and wall-mounted air conditioners for supporting sales. Rental accounts are the starting point for recurring revenue from care services and follow-on products.

Consecutive quarterly operating profit gains

Operating profit rose for three straight quarters after bottoming at KRW 181.6 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, reaching KRW 250.9 billion, KRW 253.2 billion and KRW 260.8 billion in 2026. Third-quarter operating profit was up 7.3% year on year.

The company said cumulative third-quarter operating profit reached 80% of the top of its full-year guidance range. Press coverage described third-quarter operating profit as the highest quarterly figure on record.

09

Bear factors

Lower net profit and non-operating losses

Third-quarter 2026 profit attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 111.5 billion, down 36.6% year on year and 37.5% from the prior quarter. Press analysis said operating profit of KRW 260.8 billion turned into pre-tax profit of only about KRW 149 billion because non-operating expenses exceeded KRW 110 billion. The same coverage said cumulative net profit for the first three quarters rose only 0.3% year on year.

Slower domestic growth

Domestic revenue reached KRW 754.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026, up only 2.0%, far behind the 26.2% growth of overseas subsidiaries. The slower domestic growth is attributed to fewer accounts reaching ownership transfer and fewer operating days, and whether these two factors persist is the next point to check. The full-year 2025 operating margin of 17.7% was below 18.5% in 2024.

Balance sheet and cash flow pressure

The debt-to-equity ratio rose to 93.7% at the end of 2025 from 80.3% in 2024. Operating cash flow fell to KRW 35.5 billion in 2025 from KRW 330.3 billion in 2024.

Reports said three overseas subsidiaries, including those in Singapore, India and the United States, are in full capital impairment, so the financial burden of overseas expansion is also being discussed.

10

Risk factors

Overseas concentration and currency

As the overseas share rises, the impact of specific countries such as Malaysia and local currency moves grows. Malaysia accounts for most overseas subsidiary revenue, so local market conditions feed directly into reported results. The effect of exchange rates on won-translated revenue and profit should be checked in each quarterly report.

Loss-making new subsidiaries and capital impairment

Press reports said the Singapore subsidiary posted KRW 400 million in first-half 2026 revenue with a KRW 100 million net loss, while the India subsidiary had no revenue and a KRW 300 million net loss. Three overseas subsidiaries, including the United States unit, are reported to be in full capital impairment. As new markets become a bigger growth driver, their path to profitability could affect consolidated earnings.

Gap between earnings and cash flow

Third-quarter 2026 operating profit rose while profit attributable to controlling shareholders fell, widening the gap between operating and final profit. Operating cash flow of KRW 35.5 billion in 2025 sat far below controlling-shareholder profit of KRW 617.7 billion. Swings in non-operating items remain a variable in interpreting results.

12

Overall view

Coway's recent trend is one in which overseas subsidiaries and domestic rental accounts are growing the business together. Full-year 2025 revenue reached KRW 4.96 trillion, operating profit KRW 878.7 billion and profit attributable to controlling shareholders KRW 617.7 billion, with three consecutive years of growth.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 1.43 trillion and operating profit of KRW 260.8 billion were also higher year on year. Profit attributable to controlling shareholders, however, fell 36.6% to KRW 111.5 billion, and press coverage tied this to higher non-operating expenses.

Strengths include the overseas share of about 41.5%, domestic account net additions and consecutive quarterly operating profit gains.

Weaknesses include the gap between operating and net profit, slower domestic revenue, a 93.7% debt-to-equity ratio alongside lower operating cash flow, and losses and capital impairment at newer overseas subsidiaries.

With both sets of factors present, the next checkpoint is how far operating profit converts into net profit in the fourth-quarter 2026 results and the 2026 annual report.
