Report overview

Losses Narrowing as Rehabilitation Reshapes Capital

Samyung ENC is reshaping its capital under a court-supervised rehabilitation plan. Its operating profit has returned to the black in recent quarters, while net results remain uneven.

  1. 1

    The company is in rehabilitation proceedings. The Busan Rehabilitation Court began the process on January 26, 2026 and approved the rehabilitation plan on September 29, 2026.

  2. 2

    Based on DART annual reports, the operating loss narrowed to KRW 195 million in 2025 from KRW 5.93 billion in 2024, and controlling shareholders' net income turned to a profit of KRW 840 million.

  3. 3

  4. 4

  5. 5

02

Business structure

Samyung ENC manufactures marine communication equipment and defense equipment, according to Edaily's reporting. The company divides its business into national defense projects and civilian marine applications.

The company said its national defense business posted notable sales growth in 2024, and it has put forward plans for exports in the K-shipbuilding defense field and communication systems built on AI big data. In civilian applications, it said it is building a foundation for autonomous navigation technology.

The company has set a goal of going beyond the top spot in domestic marine electronics to enter global markets. The revenue base shrank over a long stretch.

Citing business reports, Edaily reported that revenue fell from KRW 42.8 billion in 2014 to KRW 33.3 billion in 2023, while operating profit swung from KRW 7.9 billion to an operating loss of KRW 6.1 billion over the same period. The company once recorded an operating margin above 20%.

Management has acknowledged that an overly aggressive expansion strategy weakened its financial structure. The company has not disclosed a revenue split by segment in the figures used here.

The key question for the business structure is how much the defense and civilian segments each contribute to revenue, which can be checked against filings.

04

Earnings analysis

Based on DART periodic reports, annual revenue rose from KRW 31.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 33.3 billion in 2023 (up 4.5% year on year) and KRW 36.1 billion in 2024 (up 8.3% year on year), then fell back to KRW 33.3 billion in 2025 (down 7.9% year on year).

Among 2022 to 2025, the largest year was 2024 and the smallest was 2022.

The operating loss was KRW 20.1 billion in 2022, KRW 6.13 billion in 2023, KRW 5.93 billion in 2024 and KRW 190 million in 2025, so the company kept posting losses but the size of the loss narrowed, and it was loss-making for four straight years from 2022 to 2025.

The operating margin improved from -63.2% in 2022 to -0.6% in 2025.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders narrowed to KRW 41.8 billion in 2022 and KRW 16.2 billion in 2023, widened to KRW 26.8 billion in 2024, and turned to a profit in 2025 with net income attributable to controlling shareholders of KRW 840 million.

In 2025 the operating loss was KRW 190 million while net income was KRW 840 million, so non-operating items worked in favor of profit that year.

Cash flow from operating activities was negative at KRW -7.22 billion in 2022 and KRW -4.53 billion in 2023, turned positive at KRW 3.72 billion in 2024, and then went negative again at KRW -6.6 billion in 2025.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 234.2% in 2024 to 122.3% in 2025, and at the end of 2025 total liabilities were KRW 22.1 billion and total equity was KRW 18.1 billion.

On a quarterly basis, fourth-quarter 2025 revenue was KRW 11.4 billion, up 25.5% from the previous quarter, and operating profit of KRW 190 million marked a return to profit. The first quarter of 2026 was the low point, with revenue of KRW 5.08 billion and an operating loss of KRW 320 million.

Second-quarter 2026 operating profit was KRW 870 million, but the net loss attributable to controlling shareholders widened to KRW 3.35 billion.

Third-quarter 2026 revenue was KRW 8.01 billion, down 11.6% from the same period a year earlier, but operating profit of KRW 1.07 billion marked a return to profit and net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 710 million.

The combined net loss attributable to controlling shareholders over the latest four quarters is KRW 3.25 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Samyung ENC operates in a market tied to demand for marine electronics and defense equipment. Marine equipment depends on conditions in shipbuilding and shipping, while defense equipment volumes move with national defense budgets and export contracts.

The company has named exports in the K-shipbuilding defense field as a medium-term priority, which ties its strategy to Korea's defense export flow. In civilian applications, it has presented autonomous navigation technology as a core future business.

Autonomous navigation depends on more advanced navigation and communication equipment, which links to equipment supply opportunities. On competition, the company has stated a goal of moving beyond the top domestic position in marine electronics into global markets.

The number of consecutive profitable quarters is still short, so it is too early to read where the company sits in its cycle. Whether the recovery reflects an industry upturn or a change in the cost structure during rehabilitation will become clearer in the segment explanations of the next annual report.

06

Outlook

The company's rehabilitation schedule is proceeding in several stages. It signed an M&A investment agreement with Five Ocean on May 20, 2026, with a purchase price of KRW 10 billion carried out through a 100% share issuance.

The Busan Rehabilitation Court approved the rehabilitation plan on September 29, 2026, and a capital reduction that cancels 80% of the shares held by the largest shareholder and related parties was decided.

The first capital reduction took effect on October 13, 2026, the debt-to-equity conversion followed on October 16, 2026, and the second reduction through share consolidation took effect on November 10, 2026.

The third-party share issuance was scheduled with a payment date of November 16, 2026 and an effective date of November 17, 2026. New shares are scheduled to be listed on December 4, 2026, and the largest shareholder is expected to change to Five Ocean after the issuance.

The company has said the schedule may change depending on court decisions and procedures. The proceeds are for improving the financial structure through repayment of secured and unsecured rehabilitation claims.

The company's earlier long-term view on national defense sales growth was stated in its statements around 2024, and no quantitative target appears in the recent filings reviewed. The next checkpoints are the shareholder structure after the new shares are listed and the operating trend in the 2026 annual report.

Bear factors

Mixed quarterly results

Operating profit was KRW 869 million in the second quarter of 2026, yet the controlling net loss widened to KRW 3.35 billion. Some quarters showed operating profit without net profit, leaving the quality of earnings as an open question.

Cash generation and revenue swings

Operating cash flow turned negative again at -KRW 6.6 billion in 2025. Revenue fell 7.9% to KRW 33.3 billion that year. Quarterly revenue dropped 55.4% in the first quarter of 2026 to KRW 5.08 billion before recovering to KRW 8.01 billion in the third quarter, showing large swings.

Risk factors

Rehabilitation plan execution risk

Capital reduction, debt-to-equity conversion, the second share consolidation and the share issuance are sequential, so a delay in one step can affect later steps. Whether the schedule shifts around the scheduled December 4, 2026 listing of new shares is a point to watch.

Governance and existing shareholder rights

A capital reduction cancelling 80% of the shares held by the largest shareholder and related parties was decided, which heavily dilutes the value of existing holdings. The largest shareholder is expected to change to Five Ocean after the third-party issuance.

Press reports linked management disputes and weak internal controls to the financial deterioration, so whether governance normalizes matters.

Medium-term business execution

The company has presented exports in the K-shipbuilding defense field, an AI big data-based communication system and autonomous navigation technology for civilian use as medium-term tasks.

However, recent filings give no quantitative target for defense sales, and its long-term defense growth view remains the one stated before 2024. If these businesses do not convert into orders and revenue, the basis for financial recovery could weaken.

If investment capacity is limited during rehabilitation, the pace of technology development and export preparation could also slow.
