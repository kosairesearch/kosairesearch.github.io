AICC drives operating profit recovery; net income gap is the key question

02

Business structure

KTis was spun off from KT's 114 directory assistance business in 2001 and listed on the KOSPI market in 2010.

Based on 2024 revenue, the KT customer service business accounted for 39%, the general contact center business for 27%, the distribution business for 18%, directory assistance for 3%, and other businesses for 13%.

The revenue mix is shifting toward contact centers: the combined share of the KT customer service and general contact center businesses rose from 66.5% in 2023 to 72.9% in 2024 and to about 77% in 2025.

In 2025 the company sold its digital advertising business for about KRW 53.8 billion and acquired a contact center specialist for about KRW 9.6 billion. Service delivery depends on client size: large clients receive KT's build-type AICC, while smaller clients are served with KTis's proprietary platform.

The company positions its combination of contact center build-and-operate capability with AI solutions as its differentiator for corporate AI transformation. Client reach has widened from finance and telecom into manufacturing, distribution, construction and the public sector.

In August 2026 the company became the first general hospital in Korea's top tier to offer an integrated AICC, extending into healthcare. In competition, it faces large IT services players such as LG CNS and Samsung SDS in AI solutions, as well as specialized contact center operators. KT holds 29.30%, so the business leans heavily on KT group volume.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue rose from KRW 535.8 billion in 2022 to KRW 593 billion in 2023 (up 10.7% year on year) and KRW 603.9 billion in 2024 (up 1.8%), then fell to KRW 588.8 billion in 2025 (down 2.5%). Of the 2022 to 2025 period, 2024 was the largest year.

Operating profit went from KRW 19.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 20.5 billion in 2023 (up 5.0%), KRW 21 billion in 2024 (up 2.3%) and KRW 24.7 billion in 2025 (up 17.9%). It rose for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2025 was the largest year of the period.

In 2025 operating profit grew even as revenue fell, lifting the operating margin from 3.5% in 2024 to 4.2%. Attributable net income moved from KRW 15.9 billion in 2022 to KRW 13.9 billion in 2023 (down 12.5%) and KRW 11.9 billion in 2024 (down 14.8%), then jumped to KRW 36.4 billion in 2025 (up 206.6%).

Because 2025 attributable net income exceeded operating profit, how much non-operating or one-off items contributed should be checked in the annual report notes. Quarterly results in 2026 started with a low of KRW 155.4 billion revenue?

No: first-quarter revenue of KRW 154.9 billion and operating profit of KRW 5.22 billion, followed by second-quarter revenue of KRW 155.4 billion (up 0.3% from the prior quarter) and operating profit of KRW 7.13 billion (up 36.6%).

Third-quarter revenue reached KRW 165.2 billion (up 6.3% from the prior quarter) and operating profit KRW 9.19 billion (up 28.9%). Operating profit has risen for two consecutive quarters since the first quarter of 2026.

The company said in its disclosure on November 11, 2026 that third-quarter operating profit was the highest quarterly level in the last five years.

Attributable net income was KRW 4.77 billion, KRW 5.79 billion and KRW 7.66 billion across the first three quarters of 2026, and totaled KRW 20.4 billion over the latest four quarters. Press reports said third-quarter 2026 net income fell year on year, and cumulative January-September net income declined 46.6%.

The same reports said cumulative January-September revenue of the general contact center business rose 20.6% to KRW 165.4 billion, and KT customer service revenue rose 6.4% to KRW 207.9 billion.

05

Industry analysis

Demand for contact center outsourcing is spreading from finance and telecom into manufacturing, distribution, construction and the public sector, according to one industry analysis, driven by corporate hiring policy changes, the need for specialized customer service and the burden of IT system investment.

Beyond customer response functions, AI adoption is extending into agent support, training and quality control. A person in charge at the company's AICC business division said inquiries and demand for AICC-related services rose more than 100% year on year.

According to the company, inquiries from external clients about AICC adoption more than doubled from 2025. The company is expanding industry-specific services, including after-hours civil complaint intake for public bodies, appointment and checkup guidance for hospitals, and product fault intake for manufacturers.

How quickly AI transition demand spreads depends on clients' budget execution, so contract disclosures and the pace of client additions are the key indicators for reading the industry.

On competition, large clients receive KT's build-type AICC, so the link with the KT group serves as a strength, while smaller clients are served with the company's proprietary platform.

Because large IT services players such as LG CNS and Samsung SDS are competing in AI transformation, differentiation through combining contact center operating experience with AI solutions is the central issue.

The 2025 sale of the advertising business reshaped the company around contact center and customer service operations.

06

Outlook

In its medium-term shareholder return policy announced in September 2026, the company said it will allocate 40% of adjusted net income to shareholder returns from 2026 through 2028. It plans to keep an annual minimum dividend per share and to combine cash dividends with share buybacks and cancellations.

A September 23, 2026 disclosure decided to cancel 3,397,048 treasury shares, equal to 9.76% of total issued shares, with the scheduled cancellation date given as October 30, 2026. Share capital does not change from the cancellation.

Because shareholder return funds are tied to adjusted net income, swings in net income flow directly into the size of returns. On business, the company began extending into healthcare in August 2026 by offering an integrated AICC at a top-tier general hospital, the first in Korea.

The company says inquiries about AICC adoption from external clients more than doubled, which points to the direction of contract growth, but contract size and duration must be confirmed in disclosures.

The next point to check is whether fourth-quarter 2026 results continue the operating profit improvement seen in the third quarter. In the 2026 annual report, how the gap between 2025 attributable net income and operating profit is explained will also matter.

The debt ratio fell to 88.6% at the end of 2025 from 125.8% in 2024. Whether this decline is linked to the advertising business sale should be checked in the financial statement notes.

Bull factors

Quarterly operating profit recovery

The company said third-quarter operating profit was the highest quarterly level in five years. Revenue growth and margin improvement moving together are cited as a strength.

AICC demand and healthcare expansion

The company said inquiries and demand for AICC-related services rose more than 100% year on year. Inquiries from external clients about AICC adoption also more than doubled from 2025. Its integrated AICC at a top-tier general hospital in August 2026 is building healthcare references.

Shareholder return policy and treasury share cancellation

A policy to use 40% of adjusted net income for shareholder returns from 2026 through 2028 was announced. A September 23, 2026 disclosure decided to cancel treasury shares equal to 9.76% of total issued shares. Dividend yield was above the KOSPI average in a domestic financial data aggregation.

Bear factors

Gap between net income and operating profit

Attributable net income of KRW 36.4 billion in 2025 exceeded operating profit of KRW 24.7 billion. Press reports said third-quarter 2026 net income fell year on year, and cumulative January-September net income declined 46.6%. Whether operating profit gains carry through to net income depends on identifying one-off items.

Slowing revenue growth

Revenue fell 2.5% in 2025, and second-quarter 2026 revenue rose only 0.3% from the prior quarter. Press reports said cumulative January-September KT customer service revenue grew 6.4%, below the 20.6% growth of the general contact center business. Much of top-line growth relies on expansion in general contact centers.

Thin margins and group concentration

The operating margin rose to 4.2% in 2025 from 3.6% in 2022 but remains thin. The KT customer service business was the largest segment at 39% of 2024 revenue. KT holds 29.30%, so results are sensitive to changes in group volume.

10

Risk factors

Customer concentration and group dependence

The KT customer service business accounts for a large share of revenue, and KT holds 29.30%, so changes in the group's outsourcing plans could directly affect results. The use of KT's build-type AICC for large clients also raises group dependence.

Earnings volatility and one-off items

The 2025 rise in attributable net income was far larger than the rise in operating profit, so the presence of one-off items needs to be checked. Because shareholder return funds are set at 40% of adjusted net income, swings in net income pass directly into the size of returns.

Business transition and technology competition

The 2025 sale of the advertising business and the acquisition of a contact center operator carry integration risk during the transition. Technology competition in AI solutions and intensifying competition from large IT services players are variables for medium-term profitability.

Overall view

KTis recorded revenue of KRW 165.2 billion and operating profit of KRW 9.19 billion in the third quarter of 2026, driven by AICC and general contact center expansion. Operating profit has risen for two consecutive quarters since the first-quarter 2026 low.

Operating profit rose for three straight years through 2025, while attributable net income fell in 2024 and then jumped to KRW 36.4 billion in 2025, above operating profit of KRW 24.7 billion.

Press reports that third-quarter 2026 net income declined year on year make the structure of net income and one-off items the next item to verify. Shareholder returns are set at 40% of adjusted net income from 2026 through 2028, and treasury shares were cancelled in a decision made in September 2026.

On the client side, dependence on KT group volume is high, which creates both growth and risk. Valuation indicators sit at levels below the industry average and below the company's own history, but without confirming earnings durability their meaning cannot be pinned down.

The next checkpoints are fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results to be finalized in the annual report, and first-quarter 2027 results.
