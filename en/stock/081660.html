A Golf-Driven Holding Company Slows in Q3

Misto Holdings is a holding company whose earnings depend heavily on its golf subsidiary Acushnet. Operating profit recovered from the 2023 low through 2025, but fell from the prior quarter in Q3 2026.

02

Business structure

Misto Holdings moved to a holding company structure through a 2020 physical spin-off and changed its name in 2025. Before the rename, as Fila Holdings, the company was centered on the FILA brand, but its golf business at subsidiary Acushnet now accounts for the bulk of revenue.

A March 2026 press report said the Acushnet segment, which runs the golf business, represents more than 80% of total revenue. Acushnet sells Titleist golf balls, irons and wedges, and FootJoy golf shoes.

The Misto segment covers FILA footwear and apparel in Korea, online premium channels, Malaysia operations and Greater China operations.

Misto segment revenue for 2025 was KRW 829.6 billion, down 9.6% year on year, which the company attributed to the U.S. subsidiary restructuring and a reduced business scale after inventory sell-through. That year, the Misto segment's operating profit was KRW 74.7 billion, a swing from an operating loss to a profit.

In Q3 2026, the Misto segment posted revenue of KRW 207.8 billion (up 15.1% year on year) and operating profit of KRW 9.4 billion (up 8.6%). In the same quarter, Acushnet posted revenue of KRW 917.6 billion (up 1.1%) and operating profit of KRW 118.5 billion (down 3.8%).

The company cited golf ball sales and a higher average selling price for golf equipment as drivers of Acushnet's growth. The largest shareholder, Piemonte and three others, held 40.69% as of early June 2026.

04

Earnings analysis

On the filed figures, annual revenue fell from KRW 4.22 trillion in 2022 to KRW 4.01 trillion in 2023 (down 5.1% year on year), then rose to KRW 4.27 trillion in 2024 (up 6.5%) and KRW 4.47 trillion in 2025 (up 4.7%), two straight years of growth. 2025 was the largest revenue year in the 2022–2025 period.

Operating profit fell from KRW 435.1 billion in 2022 to KRW 303.5 billion in 2023 (down 30.2%), the smallest year, then rose to KRW 360.8 billion in 2024 and KRW 474.8 billion in 2025, two straight years of growth.

The operating margin was 10.3% in 2022, 7.6% in 2023, 8.5% in 2024 and 10.6% in 2025, so 2025 finished above the 2022 level. The company attributed the 2025 operating profit gain to profit-focused management, the effect of the U.S. subsidiary restructuring and Acushnet's steady growth.

Profit attributable to owners of the parent fell from KRW 343.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 42.6 billion in 2023 (down 87.6%), then rose to KRW 84.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 224.3 billion in 2025, two straight years of growth. 2022 remains the largest year for that line.

Net profit including non-controlling interests was KRW 348.6 billion in 2025, a different figure, and the gap reflects the non-controlling share. The debt ratio rose every year, from 87.3% in 2022 to 112.7% in 2025.

At the end of 2025, total liabilities were KRW 2.92 trillion and total equity was KRW 2.59 trillion, of which equity attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 1.93 trillion.

Operating cash flow went from negative KRW 138.7 billion in 2022 to KRW 577.8 billion in 2023, KRW 494 billion in 2024 and KRW 567.4 billion in 2025. Quarterly, Q4 2025 posted an operating loss of KRW 1.65 billion, a swing from profit, before Q1 2026 returned to operating profit of KRW 193.7 billion.

Q2 2026 operating profit of KRW 295.2 billion, up 52.4% from the prior quarter, is the largest quarter in the filed period, and the company said it included a one-time tariff refund benefit.

Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 128 billion, down 56.7% from the prior quarter and down 3.0% year on year, and profit attributable to owners of the parent was KRW 43.8 billion, down 30.7% year on year.

In Q3, higher IT system transition costs and weaker FootJoy sales at Acushnet weighed on results, while the Misto segment's gains were limited by a high Greater China base effect and upfront costs for new brands.

05

Industry analysis

The golf equipment market is the core end market that drives Misto Holdings' results. A Q1 2026 press report said Acushnet grew revenue to KRW 1.10 trillion (up 8.0% year on year), helped by stronger sales of Titleist T-Series irons and Vokey SM11 wedges and a higher average selling price for Pro V1 golf balls.

In Q3 2026, the company said golf ball sales were steady, but the timing of revenue recognition for the early launch of the GTS series and weaker FootJoy sales weighed on results. Tariffs are a cost variable: the company cited higher tariff and new-product marketing costs in Q1 2026. Currency works in both directions.

In an October 2026 report, KB Securities said it lowered its estimate of Acushnet's KRW-based earnings to reflect a stronger won. The apparel industry is in a downturn, and a Q3 2025 press report said the Misto segment grew both revenue and operating profit in that quarter.

The company said Greater China operations posted triple-digit revenue growth for the full year 2025. Product momentum in Titleist Pro V1 balls and Vokey wedges is cited as a strength by brokerage reports and press.

As Acushnet's share of the group grows, golf demand and currency movements pass through to the holding company's results more directly.

06

Outlook

As reported in November 2026, the company kept its full-year 2026 revenue guidance and raised its operating profit growth guidance to 15–20% from the previous 5–15%. The upgrade was attributed to Acushnet's solid performance and a one-time tariff refund benefit.

In an August 2026 announcement, the company said it is preparing additional new brand launches targeting the 2027 S/S season. The same announcement listed strengthening the competitiveness of FILA products in Korea, expanding the Greater China brand portfolio and business efficiency as second-half priorities.

Shareholder returns operate under a policy of up to KRW 500 billion for 2025–2027. The company returned about KRW 285.4 billion in 2025, equal to 57.1% of the three-year target. As of a May 2026 announcement, cumulative shareholder returns were about KRW 305.4 billion, a 61.1% execution rate.

For the medium term, the company proposed keeping the total shareholder return ratio at 40% or more on average for 2026–2027 and making share cancellations routine.

For January through September 2026, cumulative revenue was KRW 3.85 trillion, up 8.2% year on year, and cumulative operating profit was KRW 616.9 billion, up 29.5%.

The next checkpoints are whether annual operating profit growth in the 2026 annual report reaches the raised 15–20% range, and whether Q4 operating results move out of loss territory. The revenue contribution and payback timing of the 2027 S/S new brands are also worth tracking.

Bear factors

Dependence on Acushnet and quarterly swings

Because Acushnet accounts for more than 80% of revenue, the subsidiary's results largely determine the holding company's. Acushnet's Q3 2026 operating profit was KRW 118.5 billion, down 3.8% year on year. Consolidated operating profit fell 56.7% from KRW 295.2 billion in Q2 to KRW 128 billion in Q3. Q4 2025 also swung to an operating loss of KRW 1.65 billion.

Rising financial leverage

The debt ratio rose every year from 87.3% in 2022 to 112.7% in 2025. At the end of 2025, total liabilities of KRW 2.92 trillion exceeded total equity of KRW 2.59 trillion. Profit attributable to owners of the parent reached KRW 224.3 billion in 2025, well above 2024, but still below the KRW 343.7 billion of 2022.

Currency, tariffs and lower estimates

In an October 2026 report, KB Securities said it lowered Acushnet's KRW-based earnings estimates on the stronger won and cut its target price to KRW 57,000 from KRW 60,000. The same report estimated Q3 2026 operating profit at KRW 141.8 billion, above the filed KRW 128 billion. Part of the Q2 operating profit reflected a one-time tariff refund benefit.

Risk factors

FX and tariffs

A stronger won lowered Acushnet's KRW-based earnings and was part of the rationale behind KB Securities' target cut. In Q1 2026, operating profit rose despite higher tariff and new-product marketing costs, helped by currency effects and revenue growth. One-time items such as tariff refunds can distort quarterly figures.

Greater China brand portfolio

In Q1 2026, the end of some Greater China brand contracts caused a short-term portfolio adjustment. Greater China grew triple digits in 2025, but in Q3 2026 a high base effect and upfront costs for new brands limited operating profit gains. Whether new Greater China brands can replace the lost contracts is the key variable for the Misto segment.

Leverage and shareholder returns

The debt ratio stood at 112.7% at the end of 2025, above 87.3% in 2022. Total liabilities at the end of 2025 were KRW 2.92 trillion. Shareholder returns of about KRW 285.4 billion in 2025 increased capital outflows, while operating cash flow of KRW 567.4 billion in 2025 stayed positive after 2023.

Overall view

Misto Holdings recorded its largest year in the filed period in 2025, with revenue of KRW 4.47 trillion and operating profit of KRW 474.8 billion, and quarterly profit recovered through Q2 2026. Profit attributable to owners of the parent has risen two straight years since its 2023 low but remains below the 2022 level.

Q3 2026 operating profit of KRW 128 billion fell from the prior quarter, with Acushnet's system transition costs and weaker FootJoy sales cited as causes. The Misto segment has kept improving since returning to profit in 2025, with Q3 2026 revenue up 15.1% year on year.

Shareholder returns are a strength, with about KRW 285.4 billion distributed in 2025 and a plan to keep the total return ratio at 40% or more for 2026–2027. On the other side, the debt ratio rose to 112.7%, with total liabilities above total equity, and quarterly profit attributable to owners swings widely.

Brokers differ in their views: KB Securities lowered its target on the stronger won, while Hanwha Investment & Securities presented KRW 68,000 in a June 2026 report. The next checkpoints are annual operating profit growth in the 2026 annual report and the performance of new brands for the 2027 S/S season.
