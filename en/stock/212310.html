02

Business structure

Organ Eco Tech was established on November 10, 2006, and it is a medical device maker listed on KONEX on July 26, 2016. Its main products are spinal implants and related items, centered on spinal implants and surgical instruments used in orthopedics and neurosurgery. The English DART company profile lists its industry as manufacture of dental prosthesis.

The company's website describes it as a bio and polymer materials specialist and an export-oriented medical device company. The business highlights on the website list bio materials, polymer business and medical cosmetics. A revenue breakdown by product line could not be verified from disclosures, so none is given here.

A business transfer decision was disclosed on January 10, 2025, and cancellation of the transfer agreement was disclosed on April 11, 2025. This means one restructuring attempt did not go through.

Electrical-equipment peers such as LS Electric, HD Hyundai Electric and Hyosung Heavy Industries make power equipment and do not overlap with its business. Direct comparison with them therefore carries little meaning.

Recent trends

For fiscal 2024, revenue was KRW 369 million and net loss was KRW 92 million, and the company was in full capital impairment. On March 27, 2025, a rights offering moved the largest shareholder group from T-Life and eight others (4,593,197 shares, 43.33%) to HM Networks and eight others. The stated purpose of the stake acquisition was a debt-to-equity conversion.

On April 8, 2025, share trading was suspended because the auditor's delayed report left the annual report unfiled. The April 17, 2025 audit report disclosure confirmed a disclaimer of opinion citing a scope limitation and uncertainty over going-concern ability.

The company filed an appeal on May 12, 2025 and was granted an improvement period. Unfaithful-disclosure designations followed on May 27, 2025 (reversal of disclosures) and June 19, 2025 (failure to disclose), the first including a prior notice.

On March 23, 2026, the company disclosed a delay in the audit report because the auditor had not obtained sufficient audit evidence amid late submission of accounting materials.

The annual and audit reports were filed on March 31, 2026, followed by a notice on April 10 about the delisting decision after the improvement period, an appeal filing on April 21, a notice on May 4 about submission of the improvement-plan implementation report, and a June 2 notice that the Listing Disclosure Committee would resume deliberation.

Overall view
