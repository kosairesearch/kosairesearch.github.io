Kodi: Sales Rebound, but Margins Remain Thin

Kodi's quarterly sales have risen for three consecutive quarters since the fourth-quarter 2025 low, and operating profit was positive in each quarter from the first quarter to the third quarter of 2026. 02

Business structure

Kodi is an ODM (original design manufacturer) specializing in color cosmetics, led by shadows and lip products. According to a Bloter report, cosmetics segment sales in the first half of 2026 were KRW 35.8 billion, or 82.2% of consolidated sales of KRW 43.6 billion after intragroup eliminations.

The container business, run by its subsidiary Inocostech, generated KRW 9.2 billion in the same period, about 21.1%, and annual container sales were KRW 15.2 billion in 2025. Segment figures are shown before intragroup eliminations, so the two segments add up to more than consolidated sales.

As of the third quarter of 2025, product sales mix was 82.26% cosmetics and 18.98% containers. Customers are major domestic brands and indie brands, and Japan is reported to account for more than 30% of end demand.

Domestic sales in the first half of 2026 were KRW 39 billion, and overseas sales were KRW 4.5 billion, up slightly from KRW 4.2 billion a year earlier. The company is expanding base makeup such as foundation and says it has strengthened R&D to soften the seasonality of point makeup sales.

Against large domestic ODM players such as Cosmax and Kolmar Korea, Kodi's scale is relatively small. Its ability to supply both cosmetic contents and containers is cited as a differentiator.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual sales rose from KRW 55.2 billion in 2022 to KRW 79.2 billion in 2023, up 43.6%, making 2023 the largest year in the 2022 to 2025 period. Sales then fell to KRW 78.4 billion in 2024, down 1.0%, and to KRW 76.5 billion in 2025, down 2.4%, marking two consecutive annual declines from 2024 through 2025.

Operating losses of KRW 5.2 billion in 2022 and KRW 772 million in 2023 narrowed, and the company returned to operating profit of KRW 1.54 billion in 2024. Operating profit then rose to KRW 3.75 billion in 2025, up 143.2%. The operating margin moved from -9.4% in 2022 to -1.0% in 2023, 2.0% in 2024 and 4.9% in 2025.

Profit improved while sales were shrinking in 2024 and 2025, so margin recovery has come before revenue growth.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders swung from a loss of KRW 13 billion in 2022 and KRW 6.22 billion in 2023 to a profit of KRW 605 million in 2024, then rose to KRW 5.25 billion in 2025, up 767.9%. The debt ratio fell from 148.3% in 2022 and 150.4% in 2023 to 41.4% in 2025.

Operating cash flow moved from negative in 2022 and 2023 to KRW 4.2 billion in 2024 and KRW 12.8 billion in 2025. Quarterly sales bottomed at KRW 14.5 billion in the fourth quarter of 2025, after KRW 16.8 billion in the third quarter, down 13.5% quarter on quarter.

They then rose to KRW 20 billion in the first quarter of 2026, up 37.7%, and KRW 23.6 billion in both the second and third quarters, with the third quarter up 40.7% year on year.

Operating profit turned to a loss of KRW 571 million in the fourth quarter of 2025, returned to a profit of KRW 1.07 billion in the first quarter of 2026, then came in at KRW 1.03 billion in the second quarter, down 3.8%, and KRW 1.24 billion in the third quarter, up 20.5%.

In the fourth quarter of 2025, net income attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 1.95 billion despite an operating loss, which points to non-operating items playing a large role.

Net income attributable to controlling shareholders then ran at KRW 1.01 billion, KRW 1.92 billion and KRW 922 million across the first three quarters of 2026, showing wide quarter-to-quarter swings.

05

Industry analysis

Color cosmetics OEM and ODM work moves with the launch cycles of indie and emerging beauty brands. In a February 2026 report, Korea IR Association described Kodi as a direct beneficiary of indie brand growth, noting that most of its sales come from indie brands.

The same report said color cosmetics OEM and ODM is structurally a business that struggles to sustain high margins for long. Given Kodi's color-ODM-centered structure, it argued that the structural ceiling for the operating margin is reasonably seen in the mid-to-high single digits.

Measured against that range, the 2025 operating margin of 4.9% sits within it. Seasonality is another feature of the sector, and the company has cited the dependence of its sales on point makeup as a challenge.

Among listed peers in the same industry, Cosmax, Kolmar Korea and CLIO Cosmetics are the more prominent comparisons, and Kodi is smaller in scale. In May 2026, the company said it was working with new customers in the United States and Russia, indicating an effort to broaden its export channels.

Outlook

In June 2026 a company official said results were expected to improve in the second half, led by base makeup products. The same month, the company decided to cancel all treasury shares, said it would strengthen communication with the market, and said it is reviewing additional shareholder return policies.

On the reported figures, third-quarter 2026 sales were KRW 23.6 billion, up 40.7% year on year, while first-half 2026 operating profit of KRW 2.1 billion was below the prior year.

Overseas, the company said in May 2026 that it had completed product development and a contract with a large Russian beauty retail chain and was preparing to start trading.

At the same time, it said it had finished developing a foundation with a US vegan beauty brand and planned to pursue listings at Walmart and CVS, but it has not given a timetable for those listings.

In a February 2026 report, Korea IR Association projected 2026 sales of KRW 87.2 billion, up about 14%, and operating profit of KRW 4.7 billion, up about 25%, and said the cosmetics ODM operating margin could recover to 6.5% to 7%. That estimate dates from February 2026 and may differ from recent quarterly trends.

A stock consolidation at a par value of KRW 2,500 per share, up from KRW 500, took effect in April 2026. The next checkpoints are the fourth-quarter 2026 preliminary results and the 2026 annual report, and the key question is whether the full-year operating margin reaches the level implied by that outlook.

Bull factors

Quarterly sales recovery

Sales rose from the KRW 14.5 billion low in the fourth quarter of 2025 to KRW 20 billion in the first quarter of 2026, KRW 23.6 billion in the second quarter and KRW 23.6 billion in the third, described as three consecutive quarterly increases. Third-quarter sales were up 40.7% year on year.

Operating profit was positive in every quarter from the first quarter to the third quarter of 2026, so the recovery shows up in both sales and profit.

Improving profitability structure

The operating margin improved from -9.4% in 2022 to 4.9% in 2025, and operating profit rose 143.2% in 2025. Operating cash flow grew to KRW 12.8 billion in 2025 from KRW 4.2 billion in 2024. The debt ratio fell from 148.3% in 2022 to 41.4% in 2025, lowering financial burden.

Shareholder structure and return moves

Zipclub has been the largest shareholder since March 2024, with a 36.78% stake at the end of June 2026. The company decided in June 2026 to cancel all of its treasury shares and said it is reviewing additional shareholder return policies. It also presented plans to add overseas customers and expand distribution.

Bear factors

First-half 2026 profit decline

First-half 2026 operating profit was KRW 2.1 billion, roughly half the KRW 4 billion of a year earlier (Bloter report). Sales in the same period fell from KRW 45.2 billion to KRW 43.6 billion. Annual sales declined in both 2024 and 2025, showing that the top-line recovery is not yet complete.

Structural margin ceiling in the sector

Korea IR Association said color cosmetics OEM and ODM is structurally hard to sustain high margins for long, and that a mid-to-high single-digit ceiling is reasonable for Kodi's operating margin. Kodi's 2025 operating margin was 4.9%. Overseas sales are only about 10% of the total, so export expansion has so far contributed little to profit.

Quarterly earnings volatility

Operating profit turned to a KRW 571 million loss in the fourth quarter of 2025, and second-quarter 2026 operating profit fell 3.8% from the prior quarter. Net income attributable to controlling shareholders dropped 48.3% quarter on quarter in the first quarter of 2026 and 51.9% in the third quarter. Because quarterly profit swings are large, a trend is hard to read from one or two quarters.

Risk factors

Customer and market concentration

Sales depend on domestic indie brand customers, and Japan accounts for more than 30% of end demand. A shift in a particular brand or in Japanese demand could move quarterly sales sharply. The seasonality of point makeup sales also remains a source of quarterly volatility.

Ownership and free float

The largest shareholder's stake is 36.78%, or 41.69% including related parties, which is a high concentration. After the April 2026 consolidation and the trading halt from April 14 to May 15, 2026, the free float structure changed. Shifts in the stake and in tradable shares can affect trading conditions.

Timing of overseas plans

Trading with a Russian beauty retail chain and listings at US retailers are still plans that the company has described. Without a set timetable, whether overseas sales turn into reported results remains to be confirmed.

With overseas sales at about 10% of the total, the outcome of these plans could determine how much profit improves.
