02

Business structure

Woosung Materials processes and sells polyester fabric and exports part of its output, and its business is described as fabric processing and export sales alongside real estate leasing. The company is described as a textile maker for apparel.

In December 2024, its affiliate W.S. acquired management control through an affiliate of Woosung Metal, and the CEO has since focused on cutting SG&A. A new engine generator business unit was set up as the growth arm.

The CEO describes the generators as eco-friendly products using waste plastic as feedstock, with waste heat recovery that supplies energy to industrial complexes, smart farms, greenhouses and factories, and the plan covers installation through maintenance.

Target customers include facilities that need heating and temperature control, such as smart farms and large glass greenhouses, along with manufacturing sites needing industrial washing or process heat.

Sales run through specialized dealers, with the first contract volume to be delivered through five dealers within the year, including installation and commissioning. The generator segment's revenue contribution is still at an early stage, while fabric remains the core business.

04

Earnings analysis

Annual revenue fell from KRW 72.5 billion in 2022 to KRW 33.8 billion in 2023 (down 53.4% year on year), KRW 25.5 billion in 2024 (down 24.6%) and KRW 24.7 billion in 2025 (down 3.1%). Revenue declined for three consecutive years from 2023 to 2025, and 2022 was the largest year in the period.

The operating loss widened from KRW 1.52 billion in 2022 to KRW 9.33 billion in 2023, peaked at KRW 10.1 billion in 2024, and narrowed to KRW 2.33 billion in 2025. The operating margin was -2.1% in 2022, -27.6% in 2023, -39.5% in 2024 and -9.5% in 2025, with 2024 the weakest year.

The net loss attributable to controlling shareholders was KRW 2.9 billion in 2022, KRW 42.1 billion in 2023, KRW 32.4 billion in 2024 and KRW 7.67 billion in 2025, a loss in each of the four years from 2022 to 2025. The net loss including non-controlling interests was KRW 7.67 billion in 2025.

Lower SG&A is the cited factor behind the narrower loss. Operating cash flow was KRW 2.49 billion in 2022, -KRW 10.4 billion in 2023, KRW 1.14 billion in 2024 and KRW 1.13 billion in 2025. The debt-to-equity ratio fell from 457.6% in 2022 to 224.4% in 2025.

Quarterly, Q3 2025 revenue of KRW 8.06 billion and an operating profit of KRW 236 million turned into an operating loss of KRW 4.15 billion in Q4 2025 on revenue of KRW 7.47 billion.

Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 2.68 billion fell 64.0% from the prior quarter, then rose to KRW 2.98 billion in Q2 and KRW 4.4 billion in Q3, up for two consecutive quarters. Yet the Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 508 million reversed the operating profit recorded in the year-earlier quarter.

Net losses attributable to controlling shareholders peaked at KRW 4.57 billion in Q4 2025, then came in at KRW 1.78 billion, KRW 1.54 billion and KRW 1.76 billion over the next three quarters. The four quarters to Q3 2026 total KRW 9.66 billion in net losses attributable to controlling shareholders.

Revenue recovery has not yet translated into a smaller operating loss, which is the point to check in the next periodic report.

05

Industry analysis

The fabric manufacturing sector that Woosung Materials belongs to depends on demand for finished goods such as apparel and outdoor wear, as well as raw material prices. Its core polyester fabric business centers on demand for functional and recycled materials and is sensitive to consumer spending.

Revenue has declined for three consecutive years from 2023, which makes it hard to judge whether core demand has passed its weakest phase. Most listed peers in textiles and fashion are larger, so the company has limited cushion against cost swings.

The generator market that the new business targets is tied to growing demand for distributed energy and combined heat and power. One market report expects the global diesel generator market to reach USD 38.09 billion by 2034.

That forecast covers diesel generators broadly, so the company's share can only be gauged from the KRW 8 billion first contract. In competition, the generators compete with combined heat and power equipment, and regional sales networks and after-sales service are the key factors. Lower SG&A is an area management controls regardless of the textile cycle.

06

Outlook

The most specific company plan is delivering the first contract of ten generators, worth KRW 8 billion, within the year and completing installation and commissioning. The contract was presented as the first official result of the engine generator business.

The company said it will use the first supply as a start to pursue distributed energy and combined heat and power markets. The CEO said there were no orders for the Anglo American project in 2025 and that he expects further contract talks.

Discussions on applying affiliate Woosung Metal's products in defense and aerospace were also reported. On funding, a third-party rights offering was paid in on 28 September 2026, raising KRW 4.1 billion.

However, a 2026 report said the first payment date for a KRW 10 billion offering in October 2022 was pushed back many times and went unpaid for nearly four years. The same report said a merger with Woosung Metal was under review. Timing for the merger and the remaining payments is not yet fixed.

Beyond whether the new business shows up in revenue, the 2026 annual report will show the generator segment's revenue scale and operating margin.

08

Bull factors

First contract in the new generator business

The company announced on 26 October 2026 a first supply contract for ten continuous high-efficiency generators worth KRW 8 billion. The volume will be delivered through five specialized dealers within the year, with installation and commissioning planned.

It is presented as the first official result of the engine generator business. The company said it will use this as a base to expand into distributed energy and combined heat and power markets. The contract is close to one-third of 2025 revenue of KRW 24.7 billion.

SG&A cuts and narrower losses

The CEO said SG&A, which exceeded KRW 10 billion in 2024, fell to the KRW 4 billion range in 2025. Over the same period the operating loss narrowed from KRW 10.1 billion to KRW 2.33 billion, and the operating margin improved from -39.5% to -9.5%. Revenue rose for two consecutive quarters in 2026 after the Q1 low. Operating losses continue, so how far cost efficiency carries into revenue recovery is the point to watch.

Larger stakes from the largest shareholder and CEO

The largest shareholder, W.S., reported on 13 November 2026 that its stake rose 0.27 percentage points to 25.23% through open-market purchases. The CEO's disclosure shows 13 open-market purchases between 18 September and 12 November 2026, lifting his holding to 255,577 shares, or 1.25%. Both filings simply report higher holdings.

Bear factors

Accumulated losses and thin liquidity

A press report said the accumulated deficit reached KRW 70.4 billion at the end of 2025. Current assets were KRW 11 billion and cash and cash equivalents KRW 1.4 billion, with a current ratio of 21%. Operating losses have continued for four consecutive quarters through Q3 2026.

With the deficit large relative to total equity of KRW 31.2 billion, narrower losses alone would not quickly rebuild the capital structure.

Convertible bonds and dilution

Reports say the company issued convertible bonds eight times in recent years, with a total face value of KRW 54.45 billion. The second round carried a potential dilution rate of 43.2% at issuance. Several conversion price downward adjustments have been disclosed.

Large funding plans have repeatedly slipped, and a KRW 10 billion offering scheduled for October 2022 went unpaid for nearly four years.

Shrinking sales and renewed quarterly losses

Revenue of KRW 24.7 billion in 2025 is roughly one-third of KRW 72.5 billion in 2022. Q1 2026 revenue of KRW 2.68 billion fell 64.0% from the prior quarter. Q3 2026 revenue of KRW 4.4 billion recovered from the Q1 low but was still down 45.4% year on year. The Q3 2026 operating loss of KRW 508 million reversed the year-earlier operating profit.

Risk factors

Liquidity and capital structure

Reports say cash and equivalents were about KRW 1.4 billion at the end of 2025 and the current ratio was 21%. With total liabilities of KRW 70.1 billion exceeding total equity of KRW 31.2 billion, short-term repayment pressure could build.

Delays in capital raising and convertible bond conversion price adjustments make the timing of funding uncertain.

Governance and related parties

The largest shareholder is W.S., an affiliate linked to Woosung Metal. An April 2026 report said a merger with Woosung Metal was under review. The same report questioned whether the major shareholder's funding would materialize.

If a merger proceeds, the capital structure and business mix could change, and the uncertainty lasts until details are disclosed.

Market capitalization thresholds and listing requirements

Reports say a market capitalization threshold of KRW 30 billion applies from 2027 and KRW 50 billion from 2028. At the time of one report, market capitalization was above the KRW 30 billion threshold. If corporate value does not hold, the KRW 50 billion threshold could become a medium-term burden.

Market capitalization moves with the share price and share count, so meeting the thresholds depends on both price and capital raising.

Overall view

Woosung Materials has seen revenue decline for three consecutive years from 2023 and recorded losses for four consecutive years from 2022 to 2025. In 2025, the operating loss narrowed to KRW 2.33 billion and the operating margin improved to -9.5%.

Revenue rose for two straight quarters in 2026, yet operating and net losses attributable to controlling shareholders continue. The generator business has a starting point in a KRW 8 billion first contract, and on-time delivery within the year is the first check.

The debt-to-equity ratio fell to 224.4%, but total liabilities of KRW 70.1 billion, convertible bonds with a face value of KRW 54.45 billion and delayed capital raising remain funding variables.

The price-to-book ratio is below 1x, and with net losses over the last four quarters, the price-earnings ratio is not calculated. The next checks are the generator segment's results and operating margin in the 2026 annual report, followed by the Q1 2027 quarterly report.
